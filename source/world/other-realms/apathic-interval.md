## summary
### LITE
The Apathic Interval is the first stage of being "uplifted" into the Temple of Space, the floating hall above Windrest Peak. Individuals chosen from vanished eras are cast here and must reach a state of complete emptiness, giving up every desire through Maranasati meditation, before they may enter the temple proper. Depending on the person this can take a few years or several centuries. The change is deep: it alters personality and leaves a person unable to obtain a Vision.
### STANDARD +
Asmoday made the Interval with the rest of the temple, and her Watchers Nihilita and Melanta could move through it freely when they brought people in. Its loading-screen text reads: existence is not, yet the world manifests; emptiness is full, yet cannot be stayed.

## geography
### STANDARD
A set of platforms floating above a sea of red cubes called the Unbroken Sea. The cubes can be crossed only with the help of the juvenile Watcher Dex. The Interval was unlocked by the quest Lion's Celerity and can be revisited through a Teleport Waypoint.
### LARGE +
Maranasati Caskets, cube-like guardians, occupy the platforms and attack any approaching life, imprisoning them in cages. The Interval is a separate stage in the Temple of Space and is not part of any of the exhibition halls.

## people
### STANDARD
Those who endured the Interval include the temple's Uplifted residents. Istoria spent centuries here, giving up his pursuit of truth and his longing for his loved ones. Calcerius of Remuria lost his faith in Phobos there. Venipi of King Deshret's time spent perhaps under a hundred years. Daozi says that after the Interval, none of them could receive a Vision. Watchers brought people in; after Asmoday vanished Ronova's Caskets took over its guard.
### LARGE +
Yu Huangyi, speaking from the Mahavaipulya Chamber, says the Interval's torment and tempering eroded will and desire, which suited an ancient art of cutting away the "Three Deadly Selves", though a single good idea could revive a creative spark.

## culture
### STANDARD
The Interval is the temple's rite of passage, a purely mental discipline: relinquish every desire by meditating and mastering your own thoughts. Success makes a person quiet and receptive, and those who passed treat it as a shared hardship.

## history
### STANDARD
Created by Asmoday. After she disappeared, Ronova installed the Maranasati Caskets to guard the area. The temple later announced that its uplifting had ended, so no new arrivals are expected. In 6.5, during Lion's Celerity, the Melanta-driven crisis cast the Traveler, Istoria, Zeitlind and Dex into the Interval; they escaped by destroying the Caskets with Dex and collecting Deathmuse Shards, without forgoing their desires.
### LARGE +
In the Caskets' weak-spot fights Dex can seize the exposed core and strike the Casket down. After returning to the temple the group were puzzled that Paimon had not been pulled in with them.
### ULTRA +
The juvenile Watcher Dex, whom Nihilita entrusted first to Raumhilda and then to the Traveler, was cast into the Interval with the party. Istoria spent centuries here by the wiki's account, Venipi under a hundred years by his own.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Maranasati Caskets and the Domain Keepers they summon; the Unbroken Sea, which cannot be crossed without Dex; and the Interval's pressure on identity.
### LARGE +
Gameplay note: the Caskets cannot be damaged directly until their weak spot is exposed.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Blank, silent and weightless: no landscape worth the name, only platforms over a red sea, the sense that wanting anything is the only thing that costs you here.

## status
### LITE
As of 6.5 (and unchanged through 7.1) the Interval is still guarded by Caskets but no new people are being uplifted.
### STANDARD +
The Traveler's party escaped without sacrificing their desires; the system itself is intact, since Asmoday remains absent and Ronova's Caskets were not repealed.

## entry: Maranasati Caskets
@keys: Maranasati Casket, Maranasati Caskets, Divine Barrier of Transcendent Enlightenment
@keys2: Apathic Interval, Temple of Space
@version: 6.5
### LARGE
Ronova created the Maranasati Caskets to guard the Apathic Interval. They attack approaching life forms and lock them in cages. They come in several forms; some summon Domain Keepers, some slam the ground or roll along a fixed path. Dex can grip an exposed weak spot and tear them apart, and each defeated Casket yields a Deathmuse Shard that he absorbs. The name echoes the Buddhist practice of mindfulness of death.
