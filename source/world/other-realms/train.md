## summary
### LITE
The Train is the Traveler's personal base in Snezhnaya: a large train summoned by the Eye of Graeae and made to run on the iron rails crossing the frozen tundra. It lets the Traveler reach the region's stations and carries a shooting range, crafting bench and firearm loadout display.
### STANDARD +
It is made of the first two carriages of the defunct train in which the Traveler originally found the Eye of Graeae, renovated and upgraded by the Eye after the quest Gunfire in the Silent Lands. Calling it from the Eye's gadget near any track brings it in with the line "After some time, the train arrived...", and it displaces any public train on that stretch.

## places
### STANDARD
The first carriage holds the controls and most of the equipment: the Eye of Graeae terminal with Navigation FoV (a station map), a pyramid-shaped Shadow Realm Analyzer, a diamond-shaped portal into the Shooting Range, a display wall for firearms and tactical gear, a crafting bench and a front window for watching the tundra. In-game, a Prime Ice Communicator also connects to Katheryne of the Adventurers' Guild once the Snezhnaya guild quest is done.
### LARGE +
The second carriage is more homely. A window carries a drawing by Paimon, and a table gathers keepsakes from Snezhnaya as the Traveler collects them. Paimon comments on several: matryoshka dolls modelled on Odette and Vodyanitsa, a poster for Odette's final performance, a Jack Frost figurine, a doll that Ikhor turned into, a model locomotive and a picture of Nastya. Character stickers appear on the wall as Meeting Points rise in level. The interior is a warm zone, extending just outside the rear door and between carriages.

### ULTRA +
From the front window the player sees only a top view; from the side window in the second carriage the view can switch between side and top, and the horn can be sounded in top view. The Jack Frost figurine looks icy by day and glows gold at night. If a public train is called while the personal train is on the tracks, the personal train is moved to an adjacent station.

## travel
### STANDARD
The Navigation FoV lists stations the Traveler can ride to: Snezhnograd, Morepesok, Rankova Zorya, Svetloledovka and Sretomorozsk, with Pale Crown Palace listed until the Traveler has started the related quest. The train's position is marked on the map and the player can teleport to it or to the Shooting Range.
### LARGE +
The station blurbs give flavour. Snezhnograd's central station is the nation's largest, with inspections by the Royal Railway Transport Bureau before the elevator up to the city. Morepesok is a fishing coast station. Rankova Zorya was a temporary stop nearly abandoned. Svetloledovka serves Prime Ice miners who live in Snezhnograd and commute. Sretomorozsk, in the southwest, mostly serves people travelling into and out of Nod-Krai. Pale Crown Palace is described as a stop where no one arrives, a snow-covered grave where the Belyi Tsar is buried.

## history
### LARGE
The train began as a wrecked, defunct train where the Traveler found the Eye of Graeae. After Gunfire in the Silent Lands, the Eye restored two of its carriages into the current train, which was introduced with Snezhnaya in Version 7.0. Its in-game description calls it a massive train built to traverse the iron rails over the frozen tundra, to be used well on expeditions.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
A cosy, slightly cluttered refuge from the cold: steady motion over the rails, snow sweeping past the windows, Paimon's drawing and small souvenirs of past adventures on the table.
