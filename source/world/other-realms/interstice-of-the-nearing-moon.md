## summary
### LITE
The Interstice of the Nearing Moon is a hidden instance reached by riding a Moonlane, a path of moonlight, from a kuuhenki behind the Idol of the New Moon on Hiisi Island. In the World Quest Echoes of an Unfinished Past (6.0) it appears as the strange route Verna leads the Traveler and Paimon down while trying to break the seal on the Court of the First Edict. It is in fact an old orbital positioning facility of the dragons, which they call Engraving.
### STANDARD +
When the visitors approach, a draconic voice wakes it, checks their identity and matches them against known parameters.

## geography
### STANDARD
An instance of floating pillars over concentric tracks, with Moonlane-like paths. Moving the pillars onto the right tracks lifts the seal, after which golden light runs down to a crevice at the base of the central pillar.
### LARGE +
The only way down from the first platform is to slide the three floating pillars over the innermost, middle and outermost tracks; Verna compares the arrangement to lining up the grooves of a lock.

## history
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
The Moongazer Ibni-Belum built it long ago in what was then called Nephilheim as Engraving, an advanced orbital adjustment and payload guidance system. A storage log by the dragon recorder Nu-ur-Gestug records the second phase of retrofitting: a data storage array was mounted, and downlink tests from the lunar terminal to the surface node exceeded expectations. The node was meant as a backup so that research could be recovered if the primary array on the moon were ever destroyed.
### LARGE +
The log appears in the Ia-Ilimmu Storage Array Index at the Nuur Stone Circle (available after the quest Fragments of Ia-Ilimmu), and says the original Engraving was reinforced as well.

## people
### STANDARD
Verna, a self-proclaimed priestess of the Frostmoon Scions, leads the Traveler and Paimon in, believing they are inside a sanctum. Other Scions (Ehrnrooth, Krodo and Rahetar) hunt the intruders after the seal breaks.

## quests
@version: 6.0
### STANDARD
In Echoes of an Unfinished Past the Traveler throws special fragments at a floating rock to open the way, follows the Moonlane, and enters the Interstice. After the pillars are set, the sealed Court of the First Edict opens; the quest goes on in the Sanctum of the Oathbearer, where a lingering image of the first Moonchanter Aila appears, and the sword Moonweaver's Dawn can be found.
### LARGE +
The voice's authorisation message changes with the player's progress in Natlan: it welcomes an unnamed Master if the quests were not completed, the sovereign ruler of Natlantea after one Natlan quest, and King Xiuhcoatl after a later one. Before that the voice reports only a moderate match with the parameters of the Dragonlord of Flame. These are lines of the 6.0 quest as the wiki records them.
### ULTRA +
Why a Nod-Krai facility reports a Dragonlord of Flame match for the Traveler is not explained in anything read for this entry.

## status
### STANDARD
As of 7.1 the Interstice is reachable and dormant after the quest. Its connection to the later Moongazer story is by name and builder only.

## entry: Engraving
@keys: Engraving, Interstice of the Nearing Moon
@keys2: Ibni-Belum, Nephilheim, orbital
@version: 6.7
### STANDARD
Engraving is the dragon term for the orbital positioning facility, an Advanced Orbital Adjustment and Payload Management Guidance System by Ibni-Belum, today called the Interstice of the Nearing Moon. A backup data node was installed in it by the recorder Nu-ur-Gestug.

## entry: Moonlane
@keys: Moonlane
@keys2: kuuhenki, Idol of the New Moon, Hiisi Island
@version: 6.0
### STANDARD
A path of moonlight formed by a kuuhenki. Riding the Moonlane behind the Idol of the New Moon leads into the Interstice and, in Echoes of an Unfinished Past, onward to the Court of the First Edict.
