## summary
### LITE
Genesis of a Certain Micro-Universe is a stretch of the Primordial Sea, the ancient water beneath Fontaine, reached only during the world quest Waking from the Great Dream (4.2). It is the arena where the Traveler, Paimon and Ann face Narzissenkreuz, the failed leader of the Narzissenkreuz Ordo.
### STANDARD +
The quest places the Traveler in an ocean current that leads to a giant Hydro figure. Its in-game viewpoint is titled "The World's Amniotic Fluid", with the line that this was once meant to be the cradle of all life again.

## geography
@version: 4.2
### STANDARD
The place is water and current rather than land. The Traveler enters an ocean current, keeps moving ahead, and reaches a giant being, Narzissenkreuz, in a Hydro-filled arena. During the fight, rift-like cracks and a bubble the Holy Blade points toward appear in the water. Healing orbs occasionally rise in the arena.
### LARGE +
Narzissenkreuz warns that anyone caught in the current faces a "cracking and leaking" of the self within the amniotic sea. The Traveler can break his hold on the arena by riding the current and using the quest-only Sword of Narzissenkreuz against him. Gameplay note: the quest teleport waypoint starts at the Tower of Ipsissimus and moves to the arena partway through.

## history
@version: 4.2
### STANDARD
Narzissenkreuz sought to bring about a new world and so avert the apocalypse of the Prophecy of Fontaine, and took control of the Primordial Sea for that purpose. In the quest he claims to be saving the world and demands the Holy Blade. After he falls, he says he has lost all control of the Primordial Sea and that the new world cannot be born.
### LARGE +
Defeated, he admits that his plan has harmed many people and apologises when Ann presses him, though he still says his aims were lofty. He says that he wishes to go back to the time before the Narzissenkreuz heroes set out. Ann asks him to come back with them, and says the primordial waters are then to be resealed. He later vanishes in the quest Rowboat's Wake, according to the wiki.
### ULTRA +
The dialogue changes with the player's progress. If Masquerade of the Guilty is done, Narzissenkreuz says a "variable" has descended on the world and calls the Traveler its witness and recorder, and that the world has been saved nonetheless. If not, he speaks of the whale being put to flight and of a world still fated to be saved. He says Director Lyris, unable to grasp the present, holds onto the still-living Mary-Ann; Ann answers that her name is Ann.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Vast, dreamlike and ominous: deep water without a shore, a voice announcing that the day of return has come, and a being that speaks of salvation while attacking. The mood is one of a half-formed world, grief and a failed dream rather than ordinary menace.

## dangers
@version: 4.2
### STANDARD
Narzissenkreuz fights with Hydro attacks in the form of water-made fists, spears, hammers and swords, and calls down a "Rain of Blades". For the quest the party is limited to a trial Traveler. Fontaine-born people dissolve in pure Primordial Seawater, while non-Fontainians only gain greater sensitivity to Hydro, according to the Primordial Sea page.

## travel
### STANDARD
There is no ordinary route. The quest sends the party into the Primordial Sea by current. The viewpoint is only available by entering the Narzissenkreuz Ordo's hidden chamber through a book in a set spot, and its Archive entry stays hidden until collected.

## quests
@version: 4.2
### STANDARD
Waking from the Great Dream, the single world quest of the third act of In the Wake of Narcissus (after Savior's Wake and before Rowboat's Wake). Completing it grants the achievement An Immortal Emperor in a Mundane Universe.

## status
### STANDARD
As of 7.1 the place is known only from this quest. Narzissenkreuz was defeated there, and the story afterwards moves on to the resealing of the water and his later disappearance.

## entry: Narzissenkreuz's Defeat in the Primordial Sea
@keys: Genesis of a Certain Micro-Universe, Waking from the Great Dream, amniotic sea
@version: 4.2
### STANDARD
In the Primordial Sea, Narzissenkreuz called the Traveler an equal to a world, only after the Holy Blade had cut through the will around him. Beaten, he said the Holy Blade was not on his side and that his ordinary self had returned to its body. Ann, whose soul he said was made of two colours, forgave him in her own right.
