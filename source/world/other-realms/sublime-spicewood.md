## summary
### LITE
Sublime Spicewood is the Sumeru-themed Realm Layout, added in Version 3.2. The game pictures the branches and leaves of a towering tree hanging high above a sea of clouds, immense and sturdy, with a wondrous floral fragrance lingering evermore.
### STANDARD +
It unlocks by completing the Archon Quest Secret of the Scorching Desert and buying Nature's True Fruit from Tubby at the Realm Depot. The fruit is a fragrant memento Chubby brought back from Sumeru. The mansion is the Sumeru Mansion: Meditative Retreat; the four areas are Reverie Realm, Seat of Clarity, In Wit's Corner and Dais of Wisdom.
### LARGE +
The area names lean towards thought and learning, which suits Sumeru's scholarly image, but the wiki gives no canon reading beyond the names.

## geography
### STANDARD
A canopy-top landscape of branches and leaves above cloud. Elevation falls from the Reverie Realm, the highest area, to In Wit's Corner, the lowest.
### LARGE +
There are small ponds in Reverie Realm and Dais of Wisdom, and a built-in Ornamental Fish pond at In Wit's Corner that works like a Pool of Sapphire Grace without counting against load. Seat of Clarity is surrounded by vines.

## places
### STANDARD
Reverie Realm (Trust Rank 1): the highest area, with a small pond to the east and a Sub-Space Waypoint in the south. Seat of Clarity (Rank 5): a round area surrounded by vines. In Wit's Corner (Rank 7): the lowest area, with the built-in fish pond along the path from Seat of Clarity. Dais of Wisdom (Rank 9): a round area with a small pond on its west side.
### ULTRA +
Each area allows up to 16,000 load.

## travel
### STANDARD
Reached via the Serenitea Pot gadget after buying and using Nature's True Fruit; switched via Tubby. Chubby appears at one unlocked area on Fridays to Sundays.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Fragrant, lofty and hushed, a treetop retreat suited to study, meditation or a Sumeru-flavoured tea party.

## status
### STANDARD
As of Version 7.1 unchanged since 3.2; it is still unlocked by the quest-gated fruit rather than by default.
