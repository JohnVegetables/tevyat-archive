## summary
### LITE
The Moongazer is the spaceship the dragon researcher Ungien built to chase a mysterious deep-space signal. He flew it out across the sea of stars and vanished; years later it crashed, cloaked, into the Space Lifeform Capture Station on the Frost Moon, carrying a round black spiky creature that was all that remained of him. Its wreck still lies beside the station.
### STANDARD +
The ship is not the Moongazers (the dragon researchers) nor Project Moongazer, though it is named for their ideals. It is where Ungien's fusion with an alien creature took place and where that creature, speaking through him, delivered its message to the dragons.

## geography
### STANDARD
The wreck lies outside the Space Lifeform Capture Station in space above Ungien's Circle, among debris from the collision. Columbina notes that the station looks seriously rammed and that a ship is more likely the cause than a creature. In the quest, a mechanism inside the wreck holds the Moongazer Log.
### LARGE +
A viewpoint named "The Fallen Moongazer" overlooks the wreck from the station. The ship sits next to the damaged Space Capture Arm, the corridor that was meant to bring captured alien creatures aboard.

## history
### STANDARD
The Eye of Ibni-Belum, the transceiver tower built after the first Moongazer received a strange signal from deep space, later recorded a second signal and its coordinates. Ungien, a life scientist, who had made the Moongazer, piloted it to find its sender and send word back, even though the coordinates were too far for the ship to return from. He disappeared into the sea of stars and no messages came back.
### LARGE +
During the journey the ship and Ungien met an unknown creature. The ship's log shows that it did not attack him; he chose to fuse with it so that it could overcome the language barrier and speak to the dragons. Years later the station's Space Capture Arm was struck by an unknown, cloaked object, and after emergency repairs the researchers found it was the Moongazer. Ungien's partner Maagees-Geti entered first and found only a round, black, spiky creature that matched his data signature.
### ULTRA +
After recovering, the creature delivered the message and Ungien died soon after. Maagees used an experimental technique that converts a living thing into pure Archaeolune Energy, producing the kuuhenki later called Ungie, the first artificially-born kuuhenki on the moon; Ungien's consciousness was lost in the process. This message, translated by their student Lusalim, became the heart of Project Moongazer.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Drifting, weightless wreckage, silent against a view of the moon and Teyvat. The mood is of a quiet monument to a scientist's conviction rather than a battlefield.

## dangers
### STANDARD
The station and wreck are in weightless space; the party is limited to the Traveler and, if owned, Columbina while inside the station. The surrounding lunar sea can be harmful to non-kuuhenki during Archaeolune storms.

## travel
### STANDARD
Reached via the Skymount elevator from Ungien's Circle to the Space Lifeform Capture Station, then outward from the station.

## quests
### STANDARD
Visitors From the Stars (Part 2) reveals the ship's story; the hidden exploration quest A Journey's End makes its log readable.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the Moongazer is a derelict wreck beside the Space Lifeform Capture Station; no one is shown repairing or flying it.

@spoiler: major

### STANDARD
A mechanism inside the wreck, readable after the quest A Journey's End, replays fragmentary lines in a haunting voice: the fall of a star, the soul of a star that calls like broth to a table, an address to "Ysna'qurel, Ruler of Throgg" who is said to be swallowed by darkness, a request to carry a message onward, and the string "Bad-Uru-Namu-Ungien-Blackbody". The last line states that the speaker and the addressee are one. Canon does not explain Ysna'qurel or Throgg.
