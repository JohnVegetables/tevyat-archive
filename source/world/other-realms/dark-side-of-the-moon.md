## summary
### LITE
The Dark Side of the Moon is the half of the Frost Moon that never faces Teyvat. Dragon researchers built a hidden base here as part of Project Moongazer. Later the Dragon Lord of Darkness, Nibelung, returned from the stars and reforged the moon into a weapon: the far side became a cannon drawing on the lunar core, aimed at Teyvat. The Heavenly Principles defeated him, and the weapon is dormant. Visitors reach it through the Project Moongazer quests (6.7).
### STANDARD +
At the bottom of the launch silo lies Moonheart Gap, the moon's core, where Columbina and the Traveler learned what Project Moongazer was.

## geography
@version: 6.7
### STANDARD
A fortress-like landscape of launch silos and transport levels sunk into the moon's far side. Gravity is unreliable: in the silo the Traveler can walk along its curved wall, then drift weightless when the guardian draws power from the core. Archaeolune Energy barriers, Pioneer Source Structures (including exploding Annihilation Modules and Transmission Modules that teleport intruders away), terminals and security control rooms guard the way down.
### LARGE +
The base is entered from the surface and descends through security zones, the lower base level that holds Lusalim's Laboratory, a sealed zone, and finally the silo and Moonheart Gap. The viewpoint named The Dark Fortress looks out over it; its caption describes the Dragon Lord of Darkness returning from the starry firmament to shatter the throne of the winged usurper.

## places
@version: 6.7
### STANDARD
Lusalim's Laboratory sits on a lower transport level; its terminals hold the dragon researcher's farewell to his pupil Nin-Kiggia. Beneath the silo's door, which opens with the Ancient Moon's Fount, lies Lusalim's final facility. His earlier work, the Lunar Veil Laboratory, became the lunar sea. At the very bottom is Moonheart Gap, the core and source of the moon's power.
### LARGE +
The silo's guardian is a boss machine, the Warden of the Flame, which borrows power from the core and removes gravity as it fights. It must be destroyed by turning Archaeolune Energy against its subnodes while dodging the high-energy blasts fired down the silo. Columbina then disables the weapon, and she and the Traveler pass into the core.

## history
@spoiler: major
@version: 6.7
### STANDARD
Ibni-Belum led dragon researchers to the Frost Moon, and they built research facilities in the Lunar Highlands and, on the far side, a base serving Project Moongazer. Lusalim, a pupil of Ungien and Maagees, later worked here and inherited their data; his Lunar Veil project failed and became the lunar sea. His terminal records that he yielded to the Heavenly Principles and gave up the project's results, and that he entrusted Project Moongazer to Nin-Kiggia. Nibelung came back from the Sea of Stars, corrupted by the Abyss, and rebuilt the moon into a space weapon powered by Moonheart Gap, after imprisoning the Frost Moon Goddess Canon; the Heavenly Principles defeated him and the weapon went silent.
### LARGE +
Canon's imprisonment and the plan to turn all three moons against the Heavenly Principles come from the Frost Moon lore; Columbina's own account in Project Moongazer (Part 2) states only that the primordial ruler reforged the moon into a weapon, that the core's power became artillery fire aimed at Teyvat, and that he was defeated. The moon was the only one of the three to survive the war of funerary flame whole, and it was banished beyond the false sky.

## quests
@spoiler: major
@version: 6.7
### STANDARD
Project Moongazer (Part 1) takes the Traveler and Columbina into the base; Part 2 takes them down the silo to the core. There they meet a recording of Nin-Kiggia, who explains that Eggie, the black kuuhenki, was born from the data sea in the core, and that Project Moongazer means sending the message the dragons once received back out into space. The pair then return to the Eye of Ibni-Belum, where Eggie sends the message with Columbina shielding it using her moon-goddess power.

## status
### STANDARD
As of 7.1 the weapon is silent and the base is a ruin that can be explored. Nothing there shows any Abyssal presence, as with the rest of the moon.
### ULTRA +
In Project Moongazer (Part 2), Nin-Kiggia's recording and Columbina describe a repeating cycle: researchers' data merges into the core's sea and they are reborn, probably as black kuuhenki like Eggie, to walk the same path and resend the message, and this may already have happened more than once. The Traveler and Columbina agreed to let this cycle run once more, with Columbina's goddess power shielding the signal and able to erase it if danger appears.

## dangers
@version: 6.7
### STANDARD
The Dark Side is hazardous more for its machinery than for monsters: security systems that reset the intruder, gravity shifts that can drop a careless visitor, and the silo's cannon. When the silo's door opens and a huge mechanism appears, Columbina wonders aloud whether the system has detected them, and says it can do nothing to stop them.

## entry: Moonheart Gap
@keys: Moonheart Gap, moon's core, lunar core
@keys2: Frost Moon, Dark Side of the Moon
@version: 6.7
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The core of the Frost Moon and the source of its power, lying at the bottom of the launch silo. Nibelung drew on it to feed his space weapon. Inside it is the data sea where Nin-Kiggia gathered the researchers' information and where Eggie was born.

## entry: Lusalim's Final Terminal
@keys: Lusalim, Lusalim's Laboratory, Lusalim's final facility
@keys2: Frost Moon, Dark Side of the Moon, Nin-Kiggia
@version: 6.7
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Lusalim, a dragon researcher and pupil of Ungien and Maagees, left terminal records on the Dark Side thanking his own good fortune: being born after Ibni-Belum, being taken in by his teachers, and having Nin-Kiggia, whom he calls a true child of the stars. He entrusts Project Moongazer to her, says he will not live to see it done because he has sinned against dragonkind by yielding to the Heavenly Principles, and hopes to drift among the stars afterwards.
