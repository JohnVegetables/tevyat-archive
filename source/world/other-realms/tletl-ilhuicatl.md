## summary
### LITE
Tletl Ilhuicatl is the Natlan-themed Realm Layout, added in Version 5.3. The game places it high above a sea of clouds, where towering peaks bask in scorching sunlight and the air is full of endless heat. The name is Nahuatl, from words for 'fire' and 'sky'.
### STANDARD +
It is unlocked by buying the Scroll of the Fiery Firmament from Tubby at the Realm Depot after finishing Flowers Resplendent on the Sun-Scorched Sojourn (the layout's own page names Home of the Hot Springs instead). Its mansion is the Natlan Dwelling: "Lofty Tower"; the four areas are Firecage, Blazebelt, Flamecrest and Sunridge.
### LARGE +
The signature feature is travel by hot air balloon. A balloon stop is described in-game as a resting place formed by Adeptal Energy and a landing ground for Phlogiston Hot Air Balloons, the favoured transport of Natlan's tribes.

## geography
### STANDARD
Sunlit peaks and ridges over clouds, with long cliff-side terraces. Firecage and Blazebelt share an island at different heights; Flamecrest sits at the lowest elevation; Sunridge is a long strip overlooked by a cliff face.
### LARGE +
Halberd-Crest Birds fly around the layout; they are scenery and cannot be attacked.

## places
### STANDARD
Firecage (Trust Rank 1): the lower starting area, with a Sub-Space Waypoint on the cliff above it and a seat beside a lantern on the westernmost cliff. Blazebelt (Rank 5): overlooks Firecage and has a balloon platform to Flamecrest. Flamecrest (Rank 7): a long, thin area; balloons run to Blazebelt (through a short canyon) and to Sunridge. Sunridge (Rank 9): long and thin, reached by balloon from Flamecrest.
### ULTRA +
Each area has the same 16,000 load cap as the other layouts.

## travel
### STANDARD
Entered through the Serenitea Pot gadget once the scroll is used; inside, the hot air balloons run between areas. Chubby, who brought the scroll as a souvenir from Natlan, appears at one unlocked area on Fridays to Sundays.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Hot, bright and open, with balloons and birds drifting overhead. It evokes Natlan's tribes and Phlogiston travel, but no war or Archon trial exists here.

## status
### STANDARD
As of Version 7.1 unchanged since 5.3; it remains quest-gated through the scroll.
