## summary
### LITE
Silken Courtyard is an Inazuma-themed Realm Layout added to the Serenitea Pot in Version 2.2. The game says its charm comes from overlapping patchworks of mountains and waters and the elegant scent of tea in the air. It is unlocked with the Silken Print, a prayer plaque of Inazuman scenery carved on Yumemiru wood.
### STANDARD +
The print is a Level 40 reward of Sacred Sakura's Favor, the offering system at the Sacred Sakura of Grand Narukami Shrine. The layout's mansion is the Inazuman Walled House: Refined Estate. Its four areas are Kasumi Shoal, Madoromi Island, Sakurazaki Island and Nodoka Terrace.
### LARGE +
Its water has a distinctive colour and wave animation, and it has its own sun and moon unlike those of the open world. The wiki records both as visual facts, which scenes can treat as the realm's private sky.

## geography
### STANDARD
Four islands of beach, grass and cliff linked by trails, a wooden bridge and a bridge of sakura petals, with sakura trees.
### LARGE +
The water cannot be frozen or Electro-Charged by character skills, though it still applies Wet.

## places
### STANDARD
Kasumi Shoal (Trust Rank 1): a sandy starting beach with a sakura tree and a Sub-Space Waypoint in the west; a non-buildable island behind it has a trail to Madoromi Island. Madoromi Island (Rank 5): round and flat, with cliffs facing Kasumi Shoal; a sakura petal bridge leads to Sakurazaki Island. Sakurazaki Island (Rank 7): lower than Madoromi, with a small beach and steep cliffs; a wooden bridge leads on to Nodoka Terrace. Nodoka Terrace (Rank 9): a cliff-top building area above a southern beach.
### ULTRA +
All four areas hold up to 16,000 load.

## travel
### STANDARD
Reached through the Serenitea Pot gadget once the Silken Print has been obtained; the layout is then switched via Tubby. Chubby visits one unlocked area on Fridays to Sundays.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Elegant and unhurried, scented with tea and sakura. A natural stage for a refined Inazuman tea gathering, in a realm with no Shogunate and no Vision hunts.

## status
### STANDARD
As of Version 7.1 unchanged since 2.2. It stays unlocked via the Silken Print rather than being a default layout.
