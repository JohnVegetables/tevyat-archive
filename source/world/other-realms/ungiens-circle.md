## summary
### LITE
Ungien's Circle is a living biosphere built on the Frost Moon by the dragon life scientist Bad-Uru-Namu-Ungien. Seen from space it is a yellowed crater, and inside it plants, creatures and the kuuhenki thrive while the rest of the moon is barren and storm-lashed. It was a home for living beings on the moon, and it is where the Traveler and Eggie learn what became of Ungien (Visitors From the Stars, 6.7).
### STANDARD +
Its loading-screen description calls it a place holding Ungien's conviction, later passed on to the mistress of the greenhouse. A giant tree grows at its heart, and above it floats the Space Lifeform Capture Station.

## geography
@version: 6.7
### STANDARD
The only region of the Frost Moon not subject to the low-gravity effects of turbulent Archaeolune Energy. Its landscape includes a large tree, vine-choked turbines, a Power Engine that must be restarted, a Transit Station with a waypoint, and a Skymount lift to the Space Lifeform Capture Station in orbit overhead. Moonlit Vines and Knotted Moonlit Vines grow thickly; kuuhenki, including Wondrous Black Kuuhenki, live there.
### LARGE +
Local specialties and hazards match the rest of the moon: Teardrop of the Moon, Archaeolune Energy surges, Pioneer Source Structures, and Precursor's Secret Source Matrix guardians, along with Geo Slimes, Cryo Whopperflowers and Stretchy Geo Fungi.

## places
@version: 6.7
### STANDARD
Ungien's Circle: Power Engine (the turbines whose vines the Traveler clears), Ungien's Circle: Transit Station (waypoint), the Skymount (elevator) and the Space Lifeform Capture Station above. The last station contains a main observation room with a model showing Teyvat and its three moons, a gravity control room, the capture arm and the wreck of the Moongazer, the dragons' ship.
### LARGE +
A terminal inside the Circle holds a note from a teacher who calls his pupil Ungien a rebellious, obdurate mule who respects neither teacher nor king, saved only by talent; it credits Ibni-Belum with design and engineering support.

## history
@spoiler: major
@version: 6.7
### STANDARD
Ungien, a dragon life scientist and the pupil of Ibni-Belum, built the biosphere. He and his partner Maagees-Geti worked on the Frost Moon. After the Eye of Ibni-Belum recorded a new deep-space signal, Ungien flew the Moongazer to find its source. He returned fused with a creature from space, a data-based lifeform carrying a message of hope from a dying civilisation. He delivered the message and died. Maagees used an experimental method to convert him into an enormous kuuhenki of pure Archaeolune Energy, called Ungie.
### LARGE +
Maagees's study of that visitor is credited as the foundation on which Eggie was later born. The Space Lifeform Capture Station was Ungien's, built to catch creatures from space.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
A sheltered pocket of life in an otherwise stormy, low-gravity moon: overgrown vines, great dragon machines and kuuhenki, with normal gravity underfoot.

## quests
@version: 6.7
### STANDARD
Visitors From the Stars (Part 1) leads the Traveler and Eggie here by transit lane, helps kuuhenki fend off monsters, restores the power engine and ends at the Skymount. Part 2 reveals Ungien's fate. The Circle also appears in Project Moongazer (Part 2), where Columbina names it as one of the stops on the path of the project.

## status
### STANDARD
As of 7.1 the Circle still stands, its machinery working, vines cleared from the turbines, and kuuhenki living in it.
### ULTRA +
Nin-Kiggia's recording credits Ungien with sacrificing his life to relay the final message sent by the visitor's civilisation, and Maagees with uncovering that the visitor was a data-based lifeform. The terminals here and at the Eye of Ibni-Belum show the three-way tension of the founders: Ibni-Belum expelled Ungien from his team as an oath-breaker, yet still valued the biosphere's findings.

## entry: Ungie and Maagees
@keys: Ungie, Ungien, Bad-Uru-Namu-Ungien, Maagees, Maagees-Geti
@keys2: Frost Moon, Ungien's Circle, kuuhenki
@version: 6.7
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Ungien was a dragon life scientist of the Frost Moon; Maagees-Geti was his partner and fellow researcher. After his death delivering a message from a dying civilisation, Maagees transformed what remained of him into Ungie, a huge artificial kuuhenki. Their pupil Lusalim later collated the group's research. Both are named as benefactors by Nin-Kiggia's recording in Project Moongazer (Part 2).

## entry: Space Lifeform Capture Station
@keys: Space Lifeform Capture Station, Skymount
@keys2: Ungien's Circle, Moongazer
@version: 6.7
### STANDARD
An orbital station above Ungien's Circle where Ungien intended to hold creatures from space. The Traveler reaches it by the Skymount and is weightless inside; the Moongazer lies wrecked there.
