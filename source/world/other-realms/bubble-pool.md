## summary
### LITE
The Bubble Pool is a lake subarea in the Lunar Highlands of the Frost Moon, in Nod-Krai. It was added with the Frost Moon in version 6.7 (Luna VIII). Canon says almost nothing about it beyond its name and an in-game viewpoint titled "The Iridescent Lake".
### STANDARD +
Treat it as a quiet, water-filled stretch of the Frost Moon's highlands. Its in-game map label places it in the Frost Moon region alongside Ungien's Circle and the other highland subareas, and the wiki lists a handful of exploration quests tied to it.

## geography
@version: 6.7
### STANDARD
The Bubble Pool sits within the Lunar Highlands subregion of the Frost Moon. The only descriptive handle canon provides is the viewpoint title "The Iridescent Lake", which suggests a shimmering body of water; no further physical description is established in the sources consulted.
### LARGE +
The name is rendered in other languages as roughly "floating bubble lake" (Chinese) and "land of bubbles" (Japanese). The location is a subarea rather than a single point of interest, so it is a region to wander rather than one building or structure.
