## summary
### LITE
The Realm of Khab is a quiet, damp, plant-grown place reached from Chatrakam Cave in the Lokapala Jungle of Sumeru's Dharma Forest. Aranara lore calls it the convergence of the memories of Nara Varuna and the Aranara, and the dream where the Barsam Flower sleeps. The Traveler visits it in the Risen Moon Chapter of the Agnihotra Sutra quests to fetch that flower.
### STANDARD +
It was once the home of "Nara" (the Aranara word for humans), who left stone houses and runes; after they fell asleep, plants grew from their dreams and took the place over. Fungi have since crept in and the Realm is weakened.
### LARGE +
Its function in the story is protective: the Barsam Flower can sleep there, floating on water, safe from the Withering (called Marana by the Aranara).
### ULTRA +
The Realm is therefore less a settlement than a refuge and a memory: Aranara memories live in tubers, seeds and leaf veins, human memories in carved stone, and here the two flow together.

## geography
@version: 3.0
### STANDARD
The route runs through Chatrakam Cave, a fungus-choked cave system that humans rarely enter, then out of the cave's far side, down a drop past vines that Aragaru persuades to part, into the Realm. Inside, the Barsam Flower grows beneath a seal of plant-eroded stones; water hangs overhead. The area is humid, silent and overgrown, and is guarded by Fungi and ruin machines.
### LARGE +
Gameplay note: after both Starry Night Chapter and Risen Moon Chapter are done, lighting its Dendro monuments unlocks an unmarked waypoint.

## places
@version: 3.0
### STANDARD
Chatrakam Cave, the approach, holds Aranara and Nara runes and a series of cracked, vine-bound stones that Aragaru breaks with Kusava. Inside the Realm are sealed side chambers reached by following three Seelie, and the chamber where the Barsam Flower grows.
### LARGE +
Three runes in the cave tell a story in pictures: a river of light with star-blooming stems that sends brave Nara; the Grandmaster of Verdure lifting vines to let them pass to the Realm; and the Nara and Aranara driving off the dark Marana together with the Barsam Flower.

## history
@version: 3.0
### STANDARD
According to Aragaru, the Nara left the stones, but not their memories. Nara Varuna came and wept for them, and the place where the tears fell became the forest. The memories of Nara Varuna and the Aranara flowed into it and became the Realm of Khab.
### LARGE +
Aragaru says the three Aranara used to come to the cave whenever the moon was high, to recall old stories as the Nara did. None of this is dated, and canon does not say who the Nara of the stone homes were.

## quests
@version: 3.0
### STANDARD
Risen Moon Chapter (Agnihotra Sutra, 3.0). Aragaru needs the Barsam Flower for Arahaoma, the potion that lets the Aranara reach Mawtiyima and fight Marana. He and the Traveler break seals with Kusava, defeat Fungi and Winged Dendroshrooms, and reach the flower. When it is woken the dream disperses in a flood of water that sweeps them away. Aragaru then leaves for Mawtiyima Forest to prepare the ritual.
### LARGE +
The quest can be taken before or after Starry Night Chapter; Aragaru's closing dialogue changes depending on whether the Zohrah Mushroom has been found. The chain ends with The Final Chapter.

## dangers
@version: 3.0
### STANDARD
Fungi infest the cave and the Realm; Aragaru fears they will multiply and compete for nutrients. Ruin machines guard the sealed chambers. The Realm is weak, and once the flower is woken its stored "dream" collapses into a sudden flood.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
A hushed, humid, slightly uncanny place: quiet enough to give Paimon goosebumps, heavy with overhead water, soft with plants and old carved stone. The Aranara treat it with tenderness, as a sleeping place not to be disturbed lightly.

## status
@version: 3.0
### STANDARD
As of 7.1 it is only known to have appeared in the Risen Moon Chapter. The flower was taken and the dream dispersed; the Realm can still be revisited in game. Nothing in 6.x or 7.0-7.1 content mentions it.

## entry: Barsam Flower
@keys: Barsam Flower, Barsam
@version: 3.0
### STANDARD
A plant spirit's flower used as an ingredient of the Arahaoma elixir. Aragaru calls it a good friend of the Aranara who sleeps in the Realm of Khab. When woken it was angry but agreed to join the Aranara for Mawtiyima's revival. Its item text says "Barsam" is said to be a sacred tree long vanished from history.
