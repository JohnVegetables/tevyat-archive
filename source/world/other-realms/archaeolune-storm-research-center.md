## summary
### LITE
The Archaeolune Storm Research Center is an ancient dragon facility on the Frost Moon whose job was to watch Archaeolune storms, surges of moon energy that sweep the surface. Today it lies in an Archaeolune Energy disturbance zone, and a terminal there holds a researcher's anxious notes about where the storms came from.
### STANDARD +
Its map viewpoint is called Land of Raging Storms. It has an upper and a lower level.

## geography
@version: 6.7
### STANDARD
A multi-level dragon complex set among Archaeolune Energy barriers and Storm Occlusion Fields, patches of shelter where a traveller can rest from the storm. It uses Ancient Moon Pearls, Edict Modules, terminals and a High-Speed Transit Lane.
### LARGE +
The disturbance zone around it fills a gauge on anyone caught outside shelter; once full, the traveller is carried back to the last safe place without harm. Elsewhere on the moon, storms and the lunar sea work the same way.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Archaeolune storms and disturbance zones; Precursor's Secret Source Matrix of Overseer Network guardians; and puzzle barriers that must be broken with the traveller's own absorbed Archaeolune Energy.

## history
@spoiler: major
@version: 6.7
### STANDARD
The record on its terminal shows dragon researchers troubled by a disturbance in Archaeolune Energy that grew faster over time and could become a storm, a phenomenon not seen when they first arrived on the moon. Someone named Kalag insisted the cause was blackbody contamination from Ungien's Circle, brought by the mysterious visitor Ungien had returned with from space. The author dismissed this as political: it would let Kalag remove Maagees's people from the biosphere, and Kalag could not be acting without backing, since King Xiuhcoatl favoured Ungien and Maagees.
### LARGE +
The author's priority was finishing an inhibitory field device quickly so it would not delay every other project. As a half-doubted guess, they wondered whether the disturbance could come from the Moon Maiden at the Lunar Palace, since the palace sits above the Moonheart like a spring above its source, and noted that one of Maagees's students had been visiting the palace often. They could not imagine why anyone would do that, or how the Moon Maiden could agree.

## people
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
Named in the record: Kalag, who wants Maagees's people out of the biosphere according to the author; Maagees, a life scientist and Ungien's partner; Ungien, who had returned with a mysterious visitor; King Xiuhcoatl; the Moon Maiden at the Lunar Palace; and one of Maagees's students, taken to be Lusalim. The author is not identified.

## status
### STANDARD
As of 7.1 the center is derelict, still dangerous, and open to exploration.
### ULTRA +
The record also reflects the dragons' politics during Project Moongazer: factions around Kalag, Maagees and King Xiuhcoatl, and an unspoken worry that research and rulers' favour were bound together. Whether the inhibitory field device was ever finished is not shown.

## entry: Archaeolune Storm
@keys: Archaeolune storm, Archaeolune Storm, Archaeolune disturbance
@keys2: Frost Moon, dragon, moon energy
@version: 6.7
### STANDARD
A storm of Archaeolune Energy, the moon energy that is very similar to kuuvahki. Gravity, safety and travel on the Frost Moon are shaped by it. Dragon researchers recorded a rising disturbance that could produce such storms and built field devices to calm it; their record says the cause was never settled.
