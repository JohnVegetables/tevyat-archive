## summary
### LITE
The Cage of Suchness is the highest point of the Temple of Space, the floating hall above Windrest Peak in Mondstadt. It was the home of the god Asmoday and her two Watchers, Nihilita and Melanta, and from here they oversaw the temple. It lies beyond the Corridor of Nothingness and can be reached only by laying down all of one's memories. After Asmoday's disappearance it became the seat of the Watcher Melanta, who ruled the temple's inhabitants harshly until the Traveler defeated him there.
### STANDARD +
The room is cut off from the rest of the temple, so nothing short of the Path to the Heavens leads in or out. Wiki shorthand for the Temple of Space as a whole is "Segment-Domain Tau"; the Cage is its innermost, most private part.

## geography
### STANDARD
The Cage sits at the very top of the temple, separated from the exhibition halls and the Pillar Hall Central Zone by the Corridor of Nothingness. The map shows it with a side chamber as a second level. The only approach is the Path to the Heavens, which runs through the Corridor.
### LARGE +
Quest text sometimes calls the room the Atrium of Suchness. In the room the Traveler can find a damaged coffin and a note left by a visitor who claims to have escaped a containment vessel (see uncertainties).

## people
### STANDARD
Asmoday, Ruler of Space, lived here with her Watchers in the temple's early age; she is gone. Nihilita opened his eye after her disappearance, abandoned his duties and left, so only Melanta remained. Melanta used the Cage as his seat while he condemned Nihilita as a traitor and enforced the founding rules; his longing for Asmoday and his fear of her laws being corrupted drove him to cruelty.
### LARGE +
Raumhilda, a traveller who had met Nihilita at Windrest Peak, reached the Cage once by sacrificing her memories to build the Path to the Heavens, and confronted Melanta there. They did not reach an accord. She promised that her younger sister Zeitlind would one day return and defeat him, and Melanta in a rage destroyed half the Corridor and severed the path.

## governance
### STANDARD
Under Asmoday the temple's purpose was preservation alone: nothing new, no innovation, nothing taken out into the world. Under Melanta that became rigid enforcement, with punishment for anyone who blurred the lines. Those who wished to leave had to win an audience with Asmoday by offering up every memory and every scrap of wisdom, so that no knowledge of the temple could return to the mortal world.

## history
### STANDARD
The Cage was created with the rest of the temple by Asmoday. After she vanished and Nihilita left, Melanta took control, punished the Mahavaipulya Chamber, Luyang Academy and Desert Pavilion for changes that other Shades had allowed, and exiled a resident called "Maukie" for defiling the laws of space. In 6.5, during The Final Judgment, the Traveler and Zeitlind reached the Cage by offering Zeitlind's memories on top of Raumhilda's remnants, and the Traveler defeated Melanta in battle.
### LARGE +
Melanta admitted afterwards that every creation became free the moment Asmoday disappeared. He also said his own actions, including severing the Path to the Heavens, set off his self-destruction; he confessed envy of Nihilita's freedom and a wish for Asmoday's approval, and his final words were directed at Nihilita. On the way, the Traveler relives the shared fantasies of the two sisters while drifting through the Corridor.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
A scene here is quiet, bare and heavily symbolic: a place that holds the weight of an absent god, with no wind, no sense of time and no bustle. It should feel like a stage after the play has ended.

## status
### LITE
As of 6.5 and still at 7.1, Melanta is gone and the Cage is empty.
### STANDARD +
Melanta self-destructed after the battle with the Traveler, freeing the temple from his influence. Asmoday remains absent, and Nihilita was last seen outside the temple, at Windrest Peak. The Cage holds a damaged coffin whose text links it to the Traveler's sibling. No later canon through 7.1 has revisited the Cage.

## entry: Melanta's Fall

@keys2: Cage of Suchness, Temple of Space, Nihilita
@version: 6.5
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
Melanta, the Watcher who stayed, became the temple's tyrant in Asmoday's absence. He punished the inhabitants of the exhibition halls: those in the Mahavaipulya Chamber were pressed between pages, those of Luyang Academy turned to ink blots in paintings, and those of the Desert Pavilion turned to sand. When Domain Keepers began to fall onto Windrest Peak the temple's laws were visibly failing. The Traveler fought him in the Cage and he destroyed himself afterwards.

## entry: The Damaged Coffin
@keys: Damaged Coffin
@keys2: Cage of Suchness, Traveler's sibling
@version: 6.5
@spoiler: major
### ULTRA
Inside the Cage the Traveler can interact with a damaged coffin. The text says time has washed away every trace of whoever rested there, and tells the Traveler it was the person most important to them, urging them to keep travelling until they meet again. The coffin names no one and gives no cause; it is best treated as a deliberately open detail.
