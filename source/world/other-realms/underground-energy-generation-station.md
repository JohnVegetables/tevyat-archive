## summary
### LITE
The Underground Energy Generation Station is one of three stations that generate energy for the Eye of Ibni-Belum on the Frost Moon. It makes Ancient Moon Pearls, spheres that store and transport Archaeolune Energy.
### STANDARD +
After Pioneers (Part 2), its terminal shows a dedication to Ibni-Belum, the pioneer who went ahead of everyone, "the first Moongazer", for his contributions to Project Moongazer.

## geography
@version: 6.7
### STANDARD
It lies below the ground in the Eye of Ibni-Belum area. The Ancient Moon Pearls can be rolled around and pushed into power stations, which then pass the energy along Archaeolune Energy Pathways to run machinery.
### LARGE +
In-game, a pearl cannot be moved after it is packed in a container, and a launcher can fire pearls (or characters standing in it) into the air to reach otherwise inaccessible places. These mechanics are exploration gameplay, not lore.

## history
@version: 6.7
### STANDARD
The station is part of the power supply of the Eye of Ibni-Belum, the facility from which the Moongazers' message is later sent into space. Beyond the terminal dedication, canon gives no building date or builder for this station.
### ULTRA +
The loading-screen text for the pearls calls them a wondrous spherical device whose true function is to store energy. The wiki credits the Pioneers quest chain for unlocking the dedication, so the station is a story-adjacent site tied to the first Moongazer Ibni-Belum's legacy rather than to a named engineer.

## status
### STANDARD
Ancient and explorable as of 7.1.
