## summary
### LITE
The Lunar Highlands is the main stretch of the Frost Moon open to explore: a pale, low-gravity upland scattered with ruined facilities left by the ancient dragon researchers of Project Moongazer. It includes the Eye of Ibni-Belum, Ungien's Circle (a living biosphere), the Archaeolune Storm Research Center, the Nuur Stone Circle, the Golden Hall and Traveler's Crater, where the Traveler's spaceship rests. The moon opened to travellers in 6.7 through the Moon Gazing quests.
### STANDARD +
Teyvat hangs in the sky above it, and travel between its sites is by High-Speed Transit Lanes, which dragon terminals bring back to life.

## geography
@version: 6.7
### STANDARD
Outside Ungien's Circle the moon is blanketed by turbulent Archaeolune Energy, which weakens gravity: standing jumps are much higher and falls much slower. Stormy zones lie across the moon, and the Moontide Sea, a lunar mare, covers a large part of the near side and is the failed product of the dragon scientist Lusalim's Lunar Veil project.
### LARGE +
In-game, the Archaeolune storms and the lunar sea tire the traveller until they are carried back to the last safe place; safe refuge is found on floating stones and inside Storm Occlusion Fields. Dragon-built machines in the highlands run on Archaeolune Energy: Ancient Moon Pearls, Pioneer Source Structures, Backup Power Generators, Gravimeters that change local gravity, and terminals that unlock transit lanes. The Teardrop of the Moon is a local specialty.

## places
@version: 6.7
### STANDARD
The Eye of Ibni-Belum is the dragon antenna and monitoring station and the first stop in the Moon Gazing story. Ungien's Circle is a biosphere built by the dragon life scientist Ungien and the one place where normal gravity holds. The Archaeolune Storm Research Center observed the moon's energy storms. The Nuur Stone Circle stored the dragons' research data. The Golden Hall is a quieter stop marked on the map with a line about a silver ship and a great golden house. Traveler's Crater holds the Traveler's spaceship.
### LARGE +
The Dark Side of the Moon is a separate area behind the highlands, reached through the Project Moongazer quests. Dragon facilities across the moon use the Secret Source technology of the ancient dragon civilisation, and elite guardians of the Overseer Network patrol them.

## history
@spoiler: major
@version: 6.7
### STANDARD
Before the Heavenly Principles first invaded Teyvat, the dragon scientist Ibni-Belum led a group of researchers to the Frost Moon and set up the facilities that begin Project Moongazer. The project dealt with signals from deep space, which a later dragon, Ungien, followed; his return with a message of hope from a dying civilisation shaped the project's final mission. Lusalim, Ungien and Maagees's pupil, collected the group's research.
### LARGE +
Much later the Frost Moon was moved beyond the false sky. After Canon's death the Frostmoon Scions foretold a new moon goddess, and Columbina took that place. During Song of the Welkin Moon the moon was drawn back inside the false sky. In the Moon Gazing quests (6.7) the Traveler, Paimon and Eggie crash-land on the moon, and Columbina guides the Traveler to the dark side.

## dangers
@version: 6.7
### STANDARD
Archaeolune storms, disturbance zones, the lunar sea, and dragon guardian machines, chiefly the Precursor's Secret Source Matrix of Overseer Network. Local legends include Prism Slime and Mushteshir.

## status
### STANDARD
As of 7.1 the highlands are open, uninhabited by anyone but kuuhenki and the occasional visitor, and show no trace of Abyssal presence even after thousands of years outside the false sky.
### ULTRA +
The map description for the whole Frost Moon recalls that three silver lights once rose above the earth, called Dilimbabbar by the ancient spirits of the frostbound reaches, and that only one of the three remains. In the Moon Gazing quests Eggie's name and the dragons' own names (Ibni-Belum, Ungien, Maagees, Lusalim, Nin-Kiggia) form the cast of this landscape's story.

## entry: Moon Gazing quest series
@keys: Moon Gazing, Pioneers, Visitors From the Stars
@keys2: Frost Moon, Lunar Highlands
@version: 6.7
### STANDARD
A three-act World Quest series that unlocks the Frost Moon: Pioneers, Visitors From the Stars and Project Moongazer. It is the first exploration World Quest series with full voice-over and animated cutscenes. It begins when the Traveler follows Columbina and ends with the message sent from the Eye of Ibni-Belum.

## entry: High-Speed Transit Lanes
@keys: High-Speed Transit Lane, Transit Station
@keys2: Frost Moon, Lunar Highlands
@version: 6.7
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
Fast routes between moon sites, active once the nearby Transit Station is switched on. Each station then doubles as a waypoint. The lanes connect the Eye of Ibni-Belum, Ungien's Circle, the Nuur Stone Circle, Traveler's Crater and other sites.
