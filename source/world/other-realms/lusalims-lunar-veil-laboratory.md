## summary
### LITE
Lusalim's Lunar Veil Laboratory is what is left of a hidden laboratory in the Moontide Sea, where the dragon researcher Lusalim carried on Project Lunar Veil after his teacher Maagees fell ill and Kalag's security force took control of the biosphere. It is unlocked in the Moontide Sea by repairing the High-Speed Transit Station.
### STANDARD +
Two terminal logs are kept here. The first speaks of Containment Object-1, a black creature fused from Archaeolune Energy and blackbody matter, which keeps growing. The second is Lusalim's farewell to Maagees before he performs the operation she asked for.

## places
@version: 6.7
### STANDARD
A ruined laboratory with a terminal holding the two logs. It lies in the Moontide Sea and is a point of interest rather than a district.

## history
@version: 6.7
### STANDARD
Log I: Maagees operated on Containment Object-1 with an experimental technique, turning it into a new living being; Lusalim notes it is bigger every day and that "it will never be the Professor Ungien we knew". He hopes Kalag's security force does not find out, as it could endanger Project Lunar Veil, and observes that they have no access to his laboratory yet.
### LARGE +
Log II: Lusalim will perform on Maagees the same creative operation she once did when she created Ungie, as she wished; the experience will serve the upcoming Project Moongazer. He admits he took her insistence on sending the message for grief turned obsession until she showed him the whole message brought by the Stellar Blackbody, and he now understands why the Eye of Ibni-Belum team stood with her against Kalag's force, even though the first Moongazers considered her and Ungien traitors.
### ULTRA +
He quotes the pioneer Ibni-Belum that the starry sky was not born of the King's dreams but has always been there, waiting for its children to look up. He calls his teachers children of the stars, thanks them for their time on the moon, and vows to carry out their will, signing as their student Lusalim. He mentions that the operation fits with King Xiuhcoatl's counsel.
The wiki's Project Lunar Veil page states that the laboratory and the large Veil generator were later destroyed, forming the Moontide Sea, after which Lusalim moved to a laboratory on the dark side of the moon.

## entry: Lusalim
@keys: Lusalim
@version: 6.7
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Lusalim is a dragon researcher, student of Maagees and the other pioneers (he calls them his teachers), and the designer of the full-scale Lunar Veil. His own entry carries his biography; this lab holds his logs.

## status
### STANDARD
Destroyed and ruined as of 7.1. Only terminals remain for exploration.
