## summary
### LITE
The Desert Pavilion is the Temple of Space's exhibition area for sculpture and architecture, built in the style of the desert temples of Sumeru. Its loading-screen text says that though every grand tower in the world will crumble into sand, towers built upon sand remain here forever. It was reconstructed from memory by the temple's Uplifted residents. Under the Watcher Melanta's rule its people were punished by being turned into sand statues, until the Traveler freed them in 6.5.
### STANDARD +
Death does not exist in the Temple of Space, so the sand statues are alive and can be restored. A mirror version of the area exists on the map as "Desert Pavilion: Mirage", an upside-down copy.

## geography
### STANDARD
A multi-level complex of Sumeru-style architecture with pillars, torches, rock walls and elevators between levels, set around a central area. Parts of it are built on quicksand: with Dex's help the player can raise and remove sand structures to build cover or reach new places. The area is opened by the quest Unofficial Chronicle of the Desert Pavilion.
### LARGE +
Sumeru-style features here include Dendrograna, Primal Beams, Primal Embers and Torches, Tri-Yana Seeds and Valaya. Enemies include Suppression and Patrol Type Segment-Domain Keepers, Domain Keepers, and a local legend, the Punisher of Gomorrah.

## people
### STANDARD
Venipi, who comes from the time of King Deshret's divine form, once worked on the king's mausoleum; he was plucked from the desert and spent less than a hundred years in the Apathic Interval. Vitruvius designed the main layout and pathways and was the first the party saw turned to sand. Kshama is another resident freed from sand, from the Dendro Archon's age. Makani Khagan, Mardava and Nagahvaya are also listed there.
### LARGE +
Venipi supplied the patternwork and motifs from King Deshret's time; he keeps a coin the king gave him as a lucky charm and a reminder of his old life, and refused to hand it to Paimon as a reward. Vitruvius, in his own account, had spent millennia working out an epitaph, asking that his love in Patraipolis be told something. Residents say that everyone in the temple is friends, regardless of status in their former lives.
### ULTRA +
After the rescue Venipi joked that the coin could be had once he had finished chanting ten thousand prayers of penance to King Deshret; Vitruvius says Venipi cried out that Kshama was lying when she described the king's fall. Vitruvius had asked "Maukie" to carry a note in secret, and suspects the higher authority in the temple means to deal with him in violation of the rules.

## history
### STANDARD
After Asmoday vanished, the other Shades let minor changes into the temple. Venipi says the deity's sisters asked him to rebuild desert buildings from memory, and with everyone's help the Pavilion was made. Later Melanta, mad with longing for Asmoday, sought to restore the temple to its original order and punished the Pavilion's people by turning them to sand, exiling the resident "Maukie" for defiling the laws of space.
### LARGE +
In 6.5 the Traveler, Paimon, Zeitlind and Dex explored the Pavilion in Unofficial Chronicle of the Desert Pavilion, fighting Suppression Type Keepers, rescuing Venipi, Vitruvius and Kshama, and defeating the Punisher of Gomorrah (Afar's Avian), a boss that had hidden its form. Defeating it collapsed the upside-down mirage of the Pavilion and brought the real one, and all its inhabitants, back to life. Afterwards Zeitlind found an amethyst brooch of her sister's displayed there, one of the items lost from her suitcase.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Suppression Type Keepers, which can turn people into sand, Patrol Type Keepers, Domain Keepers and the Punisher of Gomorrah; quicksand that must be handled with Dex's help.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Sun-bleached stone and heat, with eerie lifelike sand figures frozen in flight from an unseen disaster, together with the friendly bickering of the people who built it.

## status
### LITE
As of 6.5 (unchanged through 7.1) the sand curse is lifted and the residents are restored.
### STANDARD +
No later release through 7.1 was found to revisit the Pavilion. Its inhabitants remain in the Temple of Space, but 'Maukie', exiled by Melanta, was not among those restored here.

## entry: The Punisher of Gomorrah

@keys2: Desert Pavilion, Temple of Space

### LARGE
The only known way to stop people being turned to sand was to defeat the Punisher of Gomorrah, which had hidden its form. The Traveler found and defeated it, in two waves of Domain Keepers and then the Punisher itself. After that the Pavilion's sand statues could be restored.

## entry: Venipi and Vitruvius
@keys: Venipi, Vitruvius, Kshama
@keys2: Desert Pavilion, Temple of Space
@version: 6.5
### LARGE
Venipi (of King Deshret's era) and Vitruvius (the Pavilion's layout designer) are friends and tease each other. Vitruvius turned to sand mid-sentence while stating his planned epitaph. Kshama, from the Dendro Archon's age, was rescued from three Suppression Keepers.
