## summary
### LITE
Cloud Retainer's Abode is the hidden home of the adeptus Cloud Retainer (later called Xianyun) at the top of Mt. Aocang in Liyue. It is a space of rotating platforms, bridges, wind currents and elemental mechanisms that she says she built and designed herself, and visitors reach it only by laying out a food offering.
### STANDARD +
It appears twice: in the Archon Quest Custodian of Clouds (Version 1.0), where the Traveler and Paimon come to tell her of the death of Rex Lapis, and in the world quest An Adeptal Summons, where she sends them to fetch her old blueprints. Outside those quests the entrance only notes that the adeptus does not want guests.
### LARGE +
The place is also a showcase of her mechanical craft. She says Guizhong and Rex Lapis commended her work, and in the second quest she dismisses the Jade Chamber as dull beside her own inventions.

## geography
### STANDARD
The abode lies at the top of Mt. Aocang in the Minlin area of Liyue. Visitors find a barrier the Traveler cannot pass at first, a pond with a round stone table at its centre, and a door that opens once an offering has been accepted.
### LARGE +
Inside, the domain is a chain of floating stone platforms joined by bridges that rotate when a device is activated. The way alternates between slimes and hilichurls, timed Pyro Monument puzzles, and wind currents that lift the party to the next platform. At the end Cloud Retainer waits on an archway in crane form.
### ULTRA +
Gameplay detail: it is a quest domain with five chests (two common, two exquisite, one precious) and a recommended Pyro element. Characters who can gain height (Geo constructs, wind or flight abilities, blink skills) can glide past the platform puzzles. In An Adeptal Summons the same space becomes a time trial in which the party carries her blueprints across the platforms.

## places
### STANDARD
Notable spots are the stone table in the middle of Mt. Aocang's pond, the abode's door, the moving platforms and the final archway.
### LARGE +
The table has three stone chairs with carved names: Retainer (with a wine jar before it), Guizhong (a bowl and chopsticks) and Rex (an empty place). Canon does not explain them; they sit beside her remark that Guizhong and Rex Lapis admired her work. The pots around the pond hold cooking traces and recipe notes used in the offering quest. A stone tablet up the stairs reads that the Free Adeptus, the Unfettered, performs no action and sets no rule.

## people
### STANDARD
The occupant is the adeptus Cloud Retainer, whose human-form name is Xianyun. She is a crane adeptus who values quiet, mechanical invention and good food, and deals with mortals strictly on her own terms. Her disciple Shenhe is listed by the wiki as affiliated with the abode.
### LARGE +
Xianyun says mortal pilgrims once left food at the Mt. Aocang altar and some turned up asking to be disciples. To disperse them she posted a sign demanding ninety-nine different dishes over ninety-nine days, each with Qingxin as the main ingredient, and the crowd left. Moon Carver, an old adeptus acquaintance, is the one who sent the Traveler to her in the first quest.
### ULTRA +
She also recalls building a Snowdrift Mechanism on the mountain for skiing with Guizhong and another adeptus, until Moon Carver sent it off the rails into a snow bank; she later added safety features at the cost of speed. These anecdotes come from her voice-overs and show the mountain as a place of shared play before it became a place of seclusion.

## history
### STANDARD
Xianyun's profile and the Mt. Aocang page place her isolation on the mountain after the Archon War and until A Thousand Moonlit Miles. She says she built the abode herself.
### LARGE +
In Custodian of Clouds she meets the news of Rex Lapis' assassination with anger and speaks of quashing Liyue first and then consulting the other adepti. Because the Traveler arrived with a Sigil of Permission and an accepted offering, which she treats as a contract, she holds back; she says the sigil's power has mostly faded and that she must investigate the claims herself. She says the adepti guarded Liyue Harbor under Rex Lapis for 37 centuries, and that Moon Carver's summons is the reason for what follows.
### ULTRA +
In An Adeptal Summons she asks the Traveler to bring blueprints she left, behind a seal, at her Dwelling in the Clouds, a separate floating island near Qingyun Peak. She uses them to make a point against the Jade Chamber. Per the Dwelling's own text, that island began as her private sanctuary, was later used by other adepti for a trial, and is again in her care. In Version 4.4's A Thousand Moonlit Miles she leaves in human form to check on her disciples in Liyue Harbor, and afterwards takes up residence in the city.

## culture
### STANDARD
Offerings are the key. A dish laid at the table earns a hearing; in the world quest a single dish buys only a short audience. The recipes the Traveler finds by the pots are Jewelry Soup, Matsutake Meat Rolls and Mora Meat.
### LARGE +
Her speech sets the tone: she refers to herself as "one" or "this adeptus", addresses visitors formally, and turns sharp once the business is finished.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
A calm mountain pond under thin cloud, a laid table with nobody at it, and a closed door; inside, creaking mechanisms, drifting platforms and sudden wind currents, all under the eye of an unseen and faintly irritable adeptus.

## dangers
### STANDARD
The main hazard is falling into the void and the occupant's temper, not guards. Slimes and a few hilichurls patrol the platforms. Failing the timed challenge in An Adeptal Summons, or falling off, sends the party out of the domain.

## travel
### STANDARD
Climb Mt. Aocang to the pond and table, offer food, and enter through the abode's door. Outside the two quests the domain cannot be entered.

## quests
### STANDARD
Custodian of Clouds (Archon Quest, Chapter I Act I, Version 1.0) and An Adeptal Summons (world quest, requires Farewell, Archaic Lord). Xianyun's A Thousand Moonlit Miles (4.4) opens at Mt. Aocang.

## status
### STANDARD
As of Version 7.1 the abode is available only as a quest domain, and Xianyun lives in Liyue Harbor. Mt. Aocang is still used by the adepti in later content: Zhongli, Xianyun, Madame Ping, Mountain Shaper and Moon Carver gather there at a Lantern Rite, and seal it off during a later quest.
