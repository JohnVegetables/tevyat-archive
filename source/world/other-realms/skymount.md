## summary
### LITE
Skymount is a huge elevator that connects Ungien's Circle, the Frost Moon's biosphere, to the Space Lifeform Capture Station in space above it.
### STANDARD +
It opens in the world quest Visitors From the Stars, during which the Traveler rides it up to the station. Afterwards it can be used in both directions, and a cutscene plays on each ride.

## geography
@version: 6.7
### STANDARD
It rises from Ungien's Circle to the station, which hangs in space over the biosphere. Once at the station, travellers enter a weightless state and only the Traveler (plus Columbina if owned) can take part.
### LARGE +
The station was designed and built by the dragon researcher Ungien as part of Project Moongazer, to capture or contact the creature he thought sent the deep-space signal. Its capture arm was later damaged by a cloaked object that proved to be the Moongazer, the station's own spaceship.

## travel
@version: 6.7
### STANDARD
Canon describes it as the link between the biosphere and the station; the ride is brief and plays as a cutscene.
### ULTRA +
It unlocks only after Visitors From the Stars (Part 1), so characters who have not made that journey cannot be assumed to know the station exists. The station then holds a main observation room with a miniature showing Teyvat's link to the three moons, a gravity control room with a gravimeter, the damaged capture arm and the Moongazer wreckage.
