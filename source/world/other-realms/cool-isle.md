## summary
### LITE
Cool Isle is one of the three Realm Layouts offered when the Serenitea Pot is first unlocked: a cluster of islands surrounded by water inside the teapot. The game's own blurb wonders how many cups of tea such a vast ocean could brew. It came with the Serenitea Pot in Version 1.5.
### STANDARD +
Its starting plateau is Pearl Isle; Conch-Court Isle, Mirage Isle and Letter Isle open as Trust Rank with Tubby rises. The mansion supplied is Mondstadt Mansion: Windward Manor. The Teapot Spirit Tubby and the travelling salesman Chubby appear here as in every layout.
### LARGE +
The Serenitea Pot is a pocket realm made by an adeptus's Sub-Space Creation, so Cool Isle is not a place in Teyvat: it has no inhabitants, history or politics of its own, only the companions, furnishings and visitors the player brings.

## geography
### STANDARD
Four islands set in open water, running from the starting island north and then northeast. Pearl Isle and Conch-Court Isle share the first island; Mirage Isle is the second island to the north; Letter Isle is the last island, in the northeast.
### LARGE +
Each area has the same 16,000 furnishing load cap, so the islands differ in shape rather than capacity: a plateau and beaches on the first island, a long narrow grassy strip on Mirage Isle, and a large flat top on Letter Isle.

## places
### STANDARD
Pearl Isle (Trust Rank 1): the starting area, a large plateau with a built-in Sub-Space Waypoint in its northwest. Conch-Court Isle (Rank 5): below Pearl Isle on the same island, with a large sandy area in the south and a smaller beach in the west. Mirage Isle (Rank 7): a long, narrow grassy building area. Letter Isle (Rank 9): the final island, with a large flat expanse.
### LARGE +
On Letter Isle the buildable ground is a big T-shaped area plus a small circular one at the northeastern end.

## travel
### STANDARD
Reached only by entering the Serenitea Pot gadget; once inside, the Sub-Space Waypoint and Mansion appear on the teapot map tab. Layouts are switched by speaking to Tubby, and furnishings in an inactive layout are kept.
### ULTRA +
Trust Rank, Adeptal Mirror progress, Realm Currency, the Jar of Riches and Chubby's weekly stock are shared by all layouts, whereas Adeptal Energy and load depend on what is placed in the active one. Chubby visits on Fridays to Sundays, appearing at one unlocked area each week.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
A bright, open island-and-sea setting, a neutral stage for tea, picnics and quiet conversation with companions, with Tubby fussing in the background.
### LARGE +
Scenes work best when they remember it is a teapot: wonder at the scale, a faintly cosy absurdity (the ocean is a very large teacup), and no true weather or danger.

## status
### STANDARD
As of Version 7.1 Cool Isle is unchanged since 1.5. Per the wiki it is available by default, or through the Trust Rank 8 and 10 layout unlocks.
