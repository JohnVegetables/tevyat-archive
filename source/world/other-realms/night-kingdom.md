## summary
@version: 5.0
### LITE
The Night Kingdom is a realm of souls deep beneath Natlan, described as "a liminal space between the body and mind, life and death". Yohualtecuhtin, the Lord of the Night, built it from the fragments of Natlan's ancient ley lines, which were shattered in the war of funerary flame. Since then it has worked as Natlan's ley lines. The tribes' ancestral spirits, the Wayob, live there, and so do the souls of Natlan's dead. Natlanese warriors draw their "Nightsoul" power from those souls. For five hundred years after the cataclysm the Abyss occupied the realm. The Traveler and Mavuika purged it in 5.3.
### STANDARD +
The dead of Natlan go there and slowly lose themselves in a "sea of souls". In time they are reborn in Natlan in a different form. Ancient Name bearers who fell in the Night Warden Wars could be brought back out through the Ode of Resurrection, provided their team won. Because the realm is not like the ley lines elsewhere in Teyvat, it holds only memories made in Natlan, and for millennia it accepted only Natlanese souls.

## geography
### LARGE
The Night Kingdom is not a place on the map. It lies "deep underground", and visitors reach it as souls or through ritual rather than on foot. Inside, Chasca warns, real-world knowledge no longer applies: the group falls a huge distance unharmed, and to leave they only need to stand under a shaft of light from above and pray. It is misty and easy to get lost in. Obsidian totem poles stand throughout it, and a Celestial Nail can be seen hanging far above.
### ULTRA +
Mavuika calls it "a realm of souls and memories, where mental fortitude outweighs physical strength". Six main totem poles act as the bodies of the six tribes' Wayob, and countless smaller proxy totems let the Wayob spread their awareness across the whole realm. The deepest part, where the Abyss had dug in, was the most contaminated. Gosoythoth retreated there, and the final battle of the war was fought there. When Mavuika's divine power washed through it during the war, the souls and memories inside took on the forms of their past selves for a while.

The Weaver of Secrets, one of the ancient Thirteen Sovereign Lords, merged her consciousness into the realm long ago and wove a "dream labyrinth" in it. It was meant to give the souls of dead dragons and humans peace and comfort. The Masters of the Night-Wind count the realm as familiar ground. Ororon tells the Traveler that he could never have caught them off guard anywhere else.

## people
### LARGE
- **The Lord of the Night (Yohualtecuhtin)**: the realm's creator and ruler. She is an angel, a kind of being whose devolved descendants are the Seelies. Natlan's people worship her as a god.
- **The Wayob**: formless ancestral spirits, "will without form or thought". Each tribe has its own Wayob, which protects it, chooses who inherits Ancient Names and speaks through the chief's dreams.
- **The dead**: Natlanese souls, also called Nightsouls, who gradually merge into the sea of souls.
### ULTRA +
During the war the Traveler met many souls there. Mallko, a fallen Ancient Name bearer of the Scions of the Canopy, guided the rescuers through the mist. Tupac, a hero of five hundred years ago, spoke to them through Mualani's Ancient Name. In the final descent, the spirits of Atea, Mallko, Vichama, Chuychu and Guthred helped, along with the heroes of the old war. Mavuika says the heroes they saw were not true souls but "snapshots" left by Ancient Name engravers; their real souls had long since been reborn. Abyssal servants such as the Abyss Lector Comonqui also hunted there while the realm was corrupted.

## governance
### LARGE
The Lord of the Night keeps the realm and its rules. Mavuika says Natlan's rules "are founded in the Night Kingdom", so if the Lord of the Night vanished, they would vanish too. The rules that make the Ode of Resurrection possible come from Ronova, the Ruler of Death. She wrote them into the realm for the first Pyro Archon, Xbalanque, and lent him her power. Mavuika later used that power in the war against the Abyss, and its price under Ronova's rules was her own death.
### ULTRA +
The tribes' bond to their Wayob is the link between the surface and the realm. A tribe's strength showed in its Wayob's strength, and the Wayob chose a hero only when the tribe was ready for war. Before the war ended, the Wayob protected Natlanese everywhere inside Natlan, visitors included. They were very strict about letting anyone leave, because protecting people beyond the border used power they needed for the fight against the Abyss. Leaving needed the chief to ask the Wayob for approval.

## history
@version: 5.3
### STANDARD
In the war of funerary flame between the Descender and the dragons, Natlan's ley lines took the worst damage, and that opened a way for the Abyss. Guided by Ronova, the Lord of the Night rebuilt a similar network from what was left, and that network is the Night Kingdom. Xbalanque later strengthened it. In the cataclysm five hundred years ago, the Abyss was driven off the surface but rooted itself in the realm. That began the Night Warden Wars, which lasted five centuries and steadily wore the Wayob down. In 5.3 Mavuika and the Traveler destroyed Gosoythoth in its depths, and the Abyss was gone.
### LARGE +
Ancient legend credits the first Wayob to Chaac, the first human to receive Phlogiston. At the end of his life, alone, he lit a flame on a mountain and called to his lost companions, and the god of the night realm answered. The tribes then worshipped their ancestors, the Wayob and the Night Kingdom. They spoke to it through obsidian and, in ancient times, through living sacrifices, often prisoners of war. Och-Kan, tyrant of Ochkanatlan, banned that worship. After his overthrow the tribes reconnected with the realm.
### ULTRA +
The Lord of the Night says the Heavenly Principles altered ley lines everywhere to counter the Abyss, and that stability came first. After Natlan's network was rebuilt, the rules needed for the Ode of Resurrection meant it could not be reconnected to other nations' ley lines. Mavuika adds that the Ruler of Death probably limited it to Natlan's souls so that the Ode could not be abused. Damage to the realm scatters souls that cannot find their way back, and those losses damage it further. Citlali gives this as the reason the Masters of the Night-Wind once planned to use Ororon as a vessel to carry lost souls home.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Visitors describe mist, endless dark, glowing totems and voices. Under the Abyss's corruption, black manifestations of its power drift everywhere. Kachina kept hearing a voice tell her to give up on the Wayob and leave. In the depths, the Traveler sensed souls that had suffered for a very long time. After the purge, the Lord of the Night describes it as a place of rest, where souls lose themselves peacefully in the sea of souls.

## dangers
### LARGE
While the Abyss held it, visitors were contaminated even by a short stay, and a soul could be lost if a nearby proxy totem was badly corrupted. That is how Kachina went missing in 5.0. The Abyss could also seal the exits. Anyone whose soul is damaged or incomplete is especially vulnerable: the Lord of the Night's waking cry nearly shattered Ororon's soul.
### ULTRA +
Natlanese who left the realm's protection paid for it. Mavuika links memory loss, stress and even bad luck to life without the Wayob's protection. Souls that cannot reach the realm drift on the surface as ghosts and slowly, painfully disperse, unless a Night-Wind shaman such as Citlali "opens the gate" for them.

## travel
### STANDARD
There is no road in. Known ways include the Pyro Archon stepping into the Sacred Flame during the Ode of Resurrection, and the Masters of the Night-Wind's rites (the Spiritspeaker Stone sent Kachina's rescuers). Capitano and Ororon also used a Masters' ritual to draw the Traveler's soul there. Obsidian totems scattered over Natlan act as contact points, and the Wayob can manifest on the surface through them and through Nightsouls.
### ULTRA +
Gameplay (not in-world lore): the Night Kingdom is not part of the open world. Touching Nightsoul Totems sends the player there for Local Legend trials, and Archon Quest scenes use the quest domain "Realm of the Night Kingdom" and the Chamber of Night's Trial. Nightsoul's Blessing, Nightsoul Bursts and Nightsoul Transmission are the combat and movement mechanics named after its souls.

## quests
### STANDARD
Archon Quest Chapter V: Black Stone Under a White Stone (5.0, Into Eternal Night), Beyond the Smoke and Mirrors (5.1, To Whence the Smoke Drifts) and Incandescent Ode of Resurrection (5.3, the final descent). World quests include Peace to the Slumbering, The Mystery of Tecoloapan Beach and To the Night, What is the Night's. Act VI, A Space and Time for You (5.7), reveals what the Traveler's sibling did there.

## status
@version: 5.7
### LITE
As of 7.1 the Night Kingdom is free of the Abyss and is being restored. The Lord of the Night is back inside it, maintaining Natlan's ley lines, and the Night Warden Wars are over.
### STANDARD +
Capitano gave his life in Mavuika's place and merged with the Lord of the Night. That made her undying and changed the realm's rules: the Night Kingdom now also takes in the souls of outlanders who die in Natlan, and Capitano led the souls he carried into it. Natlanese can now leave the country as often as they like, as long as their chief gives permission.
### LARGE +
The Traveler's sibling later used part of the realm to weave New Khaenri'ah's ley lines (5.7). Citlali sensed only some "noise" and no real effect on Natlan. In 6.6, Mavuika's link to the Night Kingdom meant she could not be recorded in the Irminsul-linked ley lines that Dottore was watching while she was in Natlan.

## entry: Wayob Totems and Proxies
@keys: Wayob totem, totem poles, Wayob proxy, Wayob Proxies, Nightsoul Totem
@keys2: Night Kingdom, Wayob, Mallko, Kachina
@version: 5.0
### STANDARD
Mallko explained the realm's layout to Kachina's rescuers. Six main totem poles in the Night Kingdom are the "bodies" of the six tribes' Wayob, and countless proxy totems let each Wayob extend its awareness over the realm and track souls wherever they wander. A badly contaminated proxy can let a soul go missing. In 5.0 the rescuers cleansed one corrupted totem, and Mavuika said that otherwise that part of the realm would have been lost to the Abyss for good.

## entry: Ode of Resurrection and the Night Kingdom
@keys: Ode of Resurrection, Rite of Resurrection
@keys2: Night Kingdom, Sacred Flame, Mavuika, Pyro Archon
@version: 5.0
### STANDARD
During the Ode, the Pyro Archon steps into the Sacred Flame and searches the Night Kingdom for the fallen warrior and their Ancient Name. If at least one of the warrior's team came back victorious, they could be brought back. A whole team that fell could not be. Souls left behind stay in the realm, and their Names are eventually lost. The Ode heals only the fatal wound. In 5.0 Mavuika came back without Kachina, finding neither her nor her Name, because the Abyss had cut the Wayob off.
### LARGE +
With all six heroes gathered, Mavuika briefly extended the Ode's protection to every Natlanese warrior, Name or no Name, which brought casualties in the war to effectively nothing. After the war Mavuika declared the Night Warden Wars over and said she hoped the Ode would never again be needed to bring back the fallen.

## entry: The Abyss in the Night Kingdom
@keys: Night Warden Wars, Gosoythoth, Reaper of the Abyss, Lord of Eroded Primal Fire
@keys2: Night Kingdom
@version: 5.3
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
After the cataclysm the Abyss took root in the Night Kingdom, and for five centuries Natlan sent its best Ancient Name bearers into the realm to fight it. The Wayob grew weaker and their tie to the tribes nearly broke. In 5.0 Mavuika judged that the realm would be lost in under a year. In 5.3, Mavuika and the Traveler descended to the deepest, most corrupted layer, where Gosoythoth, the "heart of the Abyss", had fled. ## entry: Capitano's Sacrifice and the New Rules
@keys: Capitano, The Captain, Thrain
@keys2: Night Kingdom, Lord of the Night, Yohualtecuhtin, outlander souls
@version: 5.3
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Mavuika had borrowed Ronova's power, and the price was her death. She meant to give her life to restore the Night Kingdom. The Lord of the Night waited for another who wished to take part: Capitano. He merged his existence with her, which turned his curse of undying life into her immortality and let him rewrite the realm's rules. Ronova withdrew her demand for Mavuika's death. Capitano led the souls he had carried, Khaenri'ahn and Natlanese alike, into the realm. From then on it also accepts outlanders who die in Natlan.
### LARGE +
The Lord of the Night admitted that she had once thought little of how long a life lasts. She thanked humanity for her new endless life and called her responsibility the purpose of her existence, not a burden. Before Capitano's sacrifice, Mavuika had told the Traveler that the realm accepted only Natlan's souls. Capitano had come to Natlan because it was the one land where the souls he bore might find rest.

## entry: The Sibling's Ley Line Weaving
@keys: Loom of Fate, New Khaenri'ah
@keys2: Night Kingdom, Lord of the Night, Citlali, Abyss Order
@version: 5.7
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The Traveler's sibling believed the Heavenly Principles had locked Teyvat's ley lines, but saw the Night Kingdom as an exception. After the war they had the Abyss Order fake an attack on Natlan's tribes to buy time. Meanwhile they entered the realm and negotiated with the Lord of the Night. Even the Night Kingdom could not host new ley lines, but she granted them room to run the Loom of Fate. Through it they wove the ley lines of New Khaenri'ah, which they now hold.
### LARGE +
Citlali later learned the story from the Lord of the Night in a dream. The Lord of the Night was intrigued by a third path besides Mavuika's defence of memory and Capitano's preference for the ley lines, and agreed once she was satisfied that the Loom would have no real effect on Natlan. She set a condition that Citlali was not told. The weaving briefly overlaid parts of Khaenri'ah onto Natlan, which caused a time loop.

## entry: Origins of the Wayob and the Dream Labyrinth
@keys: Chaac, Weaver of Secrets, dream labyrinth, Holy Sovereign
@keys2: Night Kingdom, Wayob, Lord of the Night
@version: 5.0
### LARGE
Two legends describe how the realm became bound to its people. The artifact Myths of the Night Realm says that Chaac, the first holder of Phlogiston, lived to be the last survivor of his band. On his final night he lit a flame on a mountain and called out to his lost companions. The god of the night realm answered, and that was the birth-cry of the first Wayob. Weapon texts and a Dragonlord priestess's records tell of the Weaver of Secrets, the Third of the Thirteen Sovereign Lords. She pitied her devolving dragons and made a pact with the human ancestors of the Masters of the Night-Wind, then merged her consciousness into the Night Kingdom to weave a "dream labyrinth" where dragon and human souls could rest.
### ULTRA +
Her pact demanded a "Holy Sovereign" with a soul approved by the Lord of the Night, and the role was a cruel one. Kukulkan tried to wreck the arrangement by leading the last Holy Sovereign, the girl later called Maghan's master, astray. The Weaver foresaw it and guided her into the realm. There she showed the girl a future of peace between humans and dragons and told her to record memories on tapestries so that the labyrinth would endure. The labyrinth's pact with the Masters of the Night-Wind is said to hold to this day.
