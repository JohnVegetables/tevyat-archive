## summary
### LITE
Maagees's Greenhouse is a greenhouse in Ungien's Circle, the biosphere of the Frost Moon, reached by an elevator. It belonged to the dragon researcher Maagees and held Containment Object-1, a black specimen that began sending a message once shown starfield footage. Its terminal logs tell how the research broke down.
### STANDARD +
Three research logs and a terminal describe the specimen's early days, the discovery of blackbody matter, Maagees's collapse after feeding the specimen with her own body, and the biosphere's takeover by Kalag. Lusalim finishes the record, which ends with the specimen being moved elsewhere.

## places
@version: 6.7
### STANDARD
At the bottom of an elevator there is an entrance and a Cultivation Area. The logs and terminal are found there. The in-game loading text elsewhere calls Ungien's resolve something later passed on to the "mistress of the greenhouse".

## history
@version: 6.7
### STANDARD
Log I (by Maagees): Day 1 the specimen's glossy black surface showed no response to stimuli. Day 30 it vibrated at a high frequency when shown starfield footage. Day 37 the Eye of Ibni-Belum team decoded the vibrations as a message in the researchers' own language, roughly "I'm glad this message reached you", and Maagees asks whether it is Ungie.
### LARGE +
Log II: by Day 89 it deteriorated rapidly after almost a month of decodable output, and medullary tissue was extracted. On Day 92 she named the black filamentous ejecta blackbody matter, the substance that drives the transmissions. On Day 98 she found that pure energy raises its activity, especially the Archaeolune Energy in living bodies, and fears that this is why Ungie ended as he did.
Log III (by Lusalim): on Day 107 Maagees was in a coma after feeding the specimen with her own body; Lusalim takes over, insisting the thing is no longer Ungien. Day 137 he fumes that Kalag has taken control of the biosphere and blames Maagees's research for the lost control over the Moonlit Vines. Day 138 he writes that the biosphere is no longer safe and that Containment Object-1 will be moved that day.
### ULTRA +
The terminal reports that the low-frequency waves the body emits are no longer Ungie's groans but a warning signal, and that its messenger is the Stellar Blackbody fused within Ungie.

## entry: Containment Object-1
@keys: Containment Object-1
@version: 6.7
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Containment Object-1 is the specimen logged here and later in Lusalim's laboratory, treated by the logs as the transformed Ungien. It fuses Archaeolune Energy with blackbody matter and keeps growing.

## status
### STANDARD
Ancient and derelict as of 7.1; the greenhouse is used for exploration and lore.
