## summary
### LITE
Emerald Peak is a mountain Realm Layout in the Serenitea Pot, one of the three offered by default. The game describes a cloud-piercing summit, then jokes that inside a teapot the tallest mountain probably reaches no higher than the stalk of a tea leaf. It was added in Version 1.5 with the Serenitea Pot.
### STANDARD +
Its four building areas are Spring Peak, Summerchase Peak, Autumnhold Peak and Wintersweep Peak, unlocked in that order through Trust Rank. The mansion supplied is the Liyue Estate: Exquisite Mansion, the same one Floating Abode uses.
### LARGE +
Like every layout it is a manufactured pocket space, not a mountain in Teyvat. The Chinese name 翠黛峰 (Cuidai-feng) is glossed on the wiki as 'Dark Green Peak'.

## geography
### STANDARD
A cluster of peaks with flat, buildable shoulders. Spring Peak is the largest flat area; Summerchase Peak is a pair of peaks beside it; Autumnhold Peak lies beyond a small intervening peak; Wintersweep Peak is the highest buildable ground.
### LARGE +
Height changes the view rather than the rules, since every area has the same 16,000 load cap. Some parts, such as a bump on Summerchase's southern peak and an inclined section of Wintersweep, cannot be built on.

## places
### STANDARD
Spring Peak (Trust Rank 1): starting area and the largest flat space, with a Sub-Space Waypoint in the southwest. Summerchase Peak (Rank 5): two peaks beside Spring Peak; the northern one connects to Wintersweep. Autumnhold Peak (Rank 7): a large flat area with a smaller raised one above it. Wintersweep Peak (Rank 9): large, flat and the highest of the four.
### ULTRA +
The wiki records no seasonal change in the realm itself; the season names are only labels.

## travel
### STANDARD
Entered through the Serenitea Pot gadget and chosen via Tubby. Its Sub-Space Waypoint and Mansion appear on the teapot map tab. Chubby the salesman visits on Fridays to Sundays at one unlocked area.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
High and open, a summit setting with a Liyue-style estate. Good for contemplative or ceremonial scenes, with the running joke that the 'mountain' is tiny.
### LARGE +
Nothing dangerous lives here; it is a built space with only the guests the player invites.

## status
### STANDARD
As of Version 7.1 Emerald Peak is unchanged since 1.5 and remains available by default or through the Trust Rank 8 and 10 layout unlocks.
