## summary
### LITE
The Veluriyam Mirage, nicknamed Bottleland, is a pocket world inside a wondrous glass bottle, said to lie hidden in Sumeru's desert. It appears only in the limited-time 3.8 event Secret Summer Paradise. Its caretaker is Idyia, an Oceanid who fled Fontaine, and the place is built to grant visitors a quiet respite and their heart's desires.
### STANDARD +
The invitation came through Klee's mother Alice, who sent Klee a letter and a "Magic Invite". Klee, Kaeya, the Traveler and Paimon arrive, meet Collei, Eula and Sangonomiya Kokomi already inside, and find the Mirage's central hub broken. The party gathers three missing parts to start it again; the deeper reveal is that the town's residents are Hydro Eidolon copies of earlier guests.
### LARGE +
The place runs on "Preprints," two-dimensional blueprint spaces entered through Streaming Projectors, with light reflected through the bottle turning them into the world. Idyia states that this kind of magic works only inside the bottle.

## geography
### STANDARD
Inside the bottle is a humid, green and watery pocket world, a striking contrast with the desert outside. The official loading text calls it a beautiful, quiet domain where special Hydro life-forms live. Game-wise it is a limited-time area rather than an actual Domain, although the story calls it one. The main hub is the Silver Bottle Courtyard, with a giant wheel, the Central Hub, above it; the Overgrown Valley holds a cavern with a painter's hidden mural.
### LARGE +
Travel is helped by the Choo-Choo Cart, a cart ride in the area run by three caretakers (Ferdinand, Jeroney, Haddo). Exploration features include Hydro Eidolons, Streaming Projectors, Gleaming Lamps, Four-Leaf Sigils, Valaya (the Sumeru ring puzzle) and Bloatty Floatty, plus Nilotpala Lotus and Kalpalata Lotus as local specialties.

## places
### STANDARD
Key places: the Silver Bottle Courtyard (arrival point); the fairground and its big tent run by Maymunah, a Spantamad scholar; Zosimos's treehouse theatre; the cart area; the Overgrown Valley; and the Central Hub with the Ferris-wheel ride that works again at the end.
### LARGE +
The fairground tent is itself a domain-like challenge area. The blueprint core of the Mirage is a spindle, set in a Preprint, where the three repaired components are fitted together.

## people
### STANDARD
Idyia is the Oceanid caretaker, called "Aunty Desert" by Klee and "Mud Fairy" by Paimon, a wish-granting fairy of local legend. Maymunah runs the fairground; Zosimos is a stage director; Lessig, a visitor from the Lawrence Clan, came to the Mirage to try to restore his family's honour and caused the breakdown by accident. Eula came after hearing a Lawrence Clan cousin had gone to Sumeru, Collei helped guide the group, and Kokomi came looking for the lost Shinrou Casket.
### LARGE +
The three caretakers of the cart area were rescued by Idyia from a sandstorm after an adventure. A painter's diary found around the Overgrown Valley describes someone who asked Idyia to improve his skill and was declined, since she does not force changes on others and thinks shortcuts are not good.

## history
### STANDARD
Idyia fled Fontaine after the old waters grew full of pain and hatred (she speaks of Oceanids fleeing, and mentions Rhodeia, who has since settled in Qingce Village in Liyue), and collapsed in the desert. According to the wiki's profile, Alice met her and granted her wish for a place away from everyone by giving her this bottle. Over time travelers found it, their wishes were granted, and they left gifts that powered the Mirage and were copied by Hydro Eidolons.
### LARGE +
Preprints, Idyia says, are like the blueprints seeds and eggs carry, and light through the bottle turns them into the Mirage; the diary fragments say a painter visited for months, and treasure ciphers called Returning Curios (an event quest) were left by guests who hid mementos and stories. Idyia explains that some guests became upset when they could not bring creations outside; others asked for Hydro Eidolon copies of themselves to carry on their dreams.
### ULTRA +
According to the wiki, many buildings were redesigned by Kaveh (Idyia says so in a quest). The finale of Act III has Kokomi explain that every resident has an elemental signature despite lacking a Vision, so Hydro Eidolons can take any form including human. Idyia reveals herself as an Oceanid; the components are the gifts guests left her; and Kokomi borrows the Shinrou Casket, a relic that gathers shinki from the prayers made to it, from the core and gives Idyia the wish: that everyone who came to the Mirage achieve what they truly wish in reality. The Central Hub starts again.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
A fairground summer: warm lamplight, water droplets that guide visitors, painted flats, a Ferris wheel and little trains, and a hush beneath. Visitors feel like guests of a mild, slightly shy host who regrets her first greeting going wrong. Cheerful nostalgia and loneliness sit side by side, since Idyia says she often rides the wheel alone after someone leaves.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Minor: the Silver Bottle Courtyard has slimes, Cryoshrooms and mitachurls as ordinary exploration enemies. The real trouble is the broken core, which stalled the central hub and trapped Hydro Eidolons in the fairground tent.

## travel
### STANDARD
Entry is by an invitation. In the story the party uses Klee's Magic Invite to teleport to the desert and Klee calls for help; a bottle appears and they enter it. Items cannot be carried out, Idyia says.

## status
@availability: limited
### LITE
The Mirage was available only during Version 3.8's event and is not a permanent part of the map. No later events or quests revisit it that were found as of 7.1.

## entry: Idyia
@keys: Idyia
@version: 3.8
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
Idyia is an Oceanid and the caretaker of the Veluriyam Mirage. She fled Fontaine when the waters changed and, per the wiki's profile, is a friend of Alice. She hopes visitors will find some respite from their journeys and looks after the wishes guests have left in her care. In the finale she reveals her true form as an Oceanid and wishes on the Shinrou Casket.

## entry: Hydro Eidolons and Preprints
@keys: Hydro Eidolon, Hydro Eidolons, Preprint, Preprints, Streaming Projector
@version: 3.8
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
Hydro Eidolons are the water-made residents of the Mirage and can take any form, including copies of earlier guests. Preprints are two-dimensional spaces on murals reached through Streaming Projectors, which turn the exploration into a platformer; the official description says they are accessible 2D spaces akin to murals.

## entry: Shinrou Casket
@keys: Shinrou Casket
@version: 3.8
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
A relic lost from Watatsumi Island that listens to the wishes of those who pray to it, building up shinki, the source of its power. Kokomi came to the Mirage because all clues pointed here. During the finale she asks Idyia to use it to make a wish that restores the Central Hub.
