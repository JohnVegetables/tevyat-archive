## summary
### LITE
The Moonheart Gap is the fissure at the bottom of the Frost Moon's Dark Side base that opens onto the moon's core, the source of its power. The dragon king Nibelung, returned from the stars and corrupted by the Abyss, tapped it to power a weapon that would fire the moon at Teyvat; earlier, the dragon researcher Lusalim used the core to gather the data of the Moongazers for rebirth.
### STANDARD +
Columbina and the Traveler descended through the weapon's launch silo to reach it. The core is also where the black kuuhenki Eggie was born, and where an imprint of Nin-Kiggia waits to explain Project Moongazer.

## geography
### STANDARD
At the very bottom of the Dark Side base, a carved fissure leads into the moon's core, from which an endless stream of ancient-moon energy surges and powers the base. Above it lies a circular launch silo with shifting gravity, entered by a door sealed with a key.
### LARGE +
The silo's walls are walkable, with gravity that changes along the way; the core is a dark floating space with a very high concentration of Archaeolune Energy. Columbina says the core is the origin of the moon's life and the seat of its will.

## places
### STANDARD
The launch silo (with the weapon's core and an engineered guardian, the Warden of the Flame). Below it, the moon's core as a sea of gathered data. The door key is the Ancient Moon's Fount, the energy entity Lusalim made at the Lunar Veil Laboratory.
### LARGE +
The weapon is guarded by Pioneer Source Structures, Annihilation Modules and a blast cannon that fires repeatedly; the party hides in alcoves in the silo wall to avoid the shots.

## history
### STANDARD
The moon's core was the origin of its life and power. Lusalim gathered the researchers' ancient-moon energy data there and pleaded with the Moon Goddesses to lend the primordial power of creation. With it, the data of past Moongazers could be reborn as data-based lifeforms, so that the Stellar Blackbody's message could be sent again and again. Nin-Kiggia's recording says a cute black kuuhenki, the Traveler's Eggie, was born in this sea when that power was triggered.
### LARGE +
After the Heavenly Principles arrived, the dragon king Nibelung returned from the stars as the "Lord of Darkness", building the Dark Side base to turn the moon into a space-based weapon, a launch silo whose artillery would use the core's energy. Columbina says the primordial ruler was defeated by the Heavenly Principles, so the weapon is no longer a threat.
### ULTRA +
The researcher data merged into this ocean is meant to rebirth in forms akin to Eggie, who instinctively absorb each other's data until the mission is clear, then follow the same journey to the Eye of Ibni-Belum to resend the message. Columbina says the cycle may have happened more than once.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Dark, weightless and beautiful: Columbina calls the core beautiful, while the silo above it is cold, mechanical and echoing with warning announcements.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Gravity shifts, engineered mechanisms that explode, the silo's repeating energy blasts, and the Warden of the Flame guardian that borrows power from the core, making gravity vanish. High Archaeolune Energy concentration is harmful if you are not shielded by Columbina's power.

## quests
### STANDARD
Project Moongazer (Part 2), Moon Gazing chapter Act III (6.7).

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the weapon is stopped and the core is quiet; the gathered researcher data is preserved.
### STANDARD +
Columbina and the Traveler stopped the weapon and entered the core together, then returned to the Eye of Ibni-Belum for the transmission.

### STANDARD
At the core waits a recorded imprint of Nin-Kiggia, addressed to the one who inherits the mission. She tells the visitor they are a new life created by the will of the moon, thanks the Moon Goddesses for allowing the transgression of borrowing the power of creation, and names those who came before: Ibni-Belum, Ungien, Maagees and Lusalim. She says the visitor's mission is to continue sending the message into the universe, and signs off with a salute to all fellow Moongazers.
