## summary
### LITE
The Dark Sea is Teyvat's name for everything beyond the continent and the Seven's dominion: unknown lands and seas, not only water. After the Archon War ended about 2,000 years ago, many defeated gods who refused to live under the Seven fled there and came to be called evil gods.
### STANDARD +
The name is a misnomer, since it covers landmasses as well as sea. Coastal peoples use it as a vague, ominous label for what lies past the edge of the known world; it is a term of folklore and item text rather than a charted place.
### ULTRA +
In the Chinese and Japanese versions the name reads as the Dark Outer Sea, which is why it is sometimes rendered as the Outer Sea or the other-shore. The English term 'Dark Sea' therefore points at the outside of the known world, not at a body of water that is itself dark.

## geography
### STANDARD
No map, boundary or list of regions exists. Canon describes the Dark Sea only as the regions outside the Seven Nations and outside the Seven's jurisdiction, including remote islands. Enkanomiya is the one place explicitly called part of it, even though it sits beneath Watatsumi Island and so within Teyvat's borders: the label is about who holds dominion, not distance.
### LARGE +
Places that lie outside the Seven's rule and could fit, such as Khaenri'ah or the Haar Islands (the Golden Apple Archipelago), are never directly called part of the Dark Sea. Treat them as separate named places; do not assert the connection.

## people
### STANDARD
The only inhabitants canon ties to the Dark Sea are the fled gods and, through Enkanomiya, the people who once lived in Byakuyakoku. Only one fled god is shown in detail: Orobashi, the serpent god, who met the abandoned people of Enkanomiya and chose to stay as their Orobashi no Mikoto, later returning to Teyvat to become the god of Watatsumi Island.
### LARGE +
Item text about Orobashi says it broke off the coral branches on its body to give light to children huddled in darkness, and used them to build a great ladder back to the surface. Orobashi was later slain by Ei, and Celestia is said to have sentenced it for obtaining forbidden knowledge from a book in Enkanomiya (see the Enkanomiya entry for the details).

## history
### STANDARD
At the end of the Archon War, only the Seven remained and Teyvat took the form of the seven nations. Gods who would not live under the new order fled to islands in the Dark Sea. Other defeated gods took a different road and cooperated with the Seven, such as Boreas and Marchosius.
### LARGE +
Item text states that the fled gods' power came from the same source as Rex Lapis' power and is separate from the all-devouring darkness; they became 'evil gods' in the eyes of Teyvat, not because they were servants of that darkness. The Dark Sea is therefore not the Abyss, and the two should not be merged in a scene.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
In Teyvat the Dark Sea is a place of rumour: sea-monster legends in Liyue (where the term is rendered as the other-shore), warnings on the coasts, and dread of what refused the Seven. Characters speak of it as distant, dangerous and mostly unvisited.
### LARGE +
Inazuma's Shogunate is said in an official video to have been drawn into many wars because its islands lie close to the Dark Sea. Liyue's ancient sailors mounted sea-monster bones on their ship prows to scare away beasts from beyond the shore.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Canon gives no specific hazards beyond the old tales: sea monsters, hostile evil gods, and the unknown. Do not invent particular creatures.

## travel
### STANDARD
Not accessible in the game and not visited in any quest, apart from Enkanomiya which is reached through a pool near Sangonomiya Shrine. Crossing to the Dark Sea is not described anywhere.

## status
### STANDARD
As of 7.1 the Dark Sea remains an undefined term. Nothing in Nod-Krai (6.x) or Snezhnaya (7.0-7.1) content found for this entry redefines it.

## entry: Enkanomiya and the Dark Sea
@keys: Dark Sea, Dark Outer Sea
@keys2: Enkanomiya, Orobashi
@version: 2.4
### STANDARD
Enkanomiya is called part of the Dark Sea in item text because it lay outside the Seven's jurisdiction. Its people, the lightless realm's inhabitants, were saved from the Dragonheirs of the Depths by the serpent god Orobashi, whom they worshipped. See the Enkanomiya entry for its history and the Orobashi entry for the god.
