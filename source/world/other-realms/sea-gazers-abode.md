## summary
### LITE
Sea Gazer's Abode is the hidden treasure house of Sea Gazer, an adeptus of Liyue who died long ago, located in the Lisha area. Abandoned and forgotten, it still runs on adeptal energy, which keeps its guardian mechanisms and the monsters inside active. It appears only in the Interlude quest Seagaze Sunset (Version 2.4), when the Traveler, Paimon and Shenhe enter it to fetch a huge piece of Sunset Vermillionite for the rebuilding of the Jade Chamber.
### STANDARD +
The abode is a quest domain, a pocket space reached through a waterfall once Shenhe lifts the adepti art that conceals it. Inside, false clouds hide the lower levels and the party must break the mechanisms keeping the illusion up in order to descend.

## geography
### STANDARD
The entrance is hidden around Lisha and is revealed when Shenhe performs an adepti art that turns the weather rainy and removes the concealment; a Seelie then leads the party to a waterfall, which is the way in. Inside, the abode is three levels: floating islands above a sea of clouds, a lower cloud-wrapped level, and a deepest level with a pool of water, a maze of movable stone walls and Seelie courts.
### LARGE +
The clouds are not real: Shenhe explains they are an adepti art used for spatial partitioning, held in place by mechanisms called Ancient Array Cores. Once a core's guardians are beaten it can be destroyed and the party drops to the next level, the last fall ending in a pool of water. The rocks and trees look incomplete from above, which is what first makes Paimon suspect something lies beneath the clouds.

## places
### LARGE
The first level has east and west Geo Lamps, each tied to a Geogranum, and a central island; activating them opens a wind current to glide on and raises Plaustrite platforms. The second level is another core arena. The third holds the water pool, a deactivated Ruin Guard, a maze of four stone walls that must be pushed in the right order, a final core, and the Seelie courts, beyond which a bridge leads to the Sunset Vermillionite.
### ULTRA +
The wiki lists Shenhe as the domain's trial character and recommends Cryo, Geo and Pyro elements; these are game-mechanic notes, not lore.

## people
### STANDARD
Nobody lives in the abode and its builder, Sea Gazer, is long dead. The party inside consists of Paimon and Shenhe, who knows adepti arts and whose master is Cloud Retainer.
### LARGE +
Sea Gazer was an adeptus, a skilled explorer and collector who kept his rarest finds here. He had been very close to Mountain Shaper. Cloud Retainer later recalls that he was usually mild-mannered but quite a braggart when showing off a new discovery at Guizhong's gatherings at the Guili Assembly.

## history
### STANDARD
According to Xingqiu, citing the book "Records of Drifting Clouds", Sea Gazer built the abode to store his rarest and most exquisite treasures, among them Sunset Vermillionite. After he died the abode was abandoned and its location was lost to time. Xingqiu worked out that it lay in Lisha by cross-referencing a stack of folk-history books that make oblique references to it.
### LARGE +
The Jade Chamber is being rebuilt, and Ningguang has opened a contest to gather the materials, of which Sunset Vermillionite (the Plaustrite that lifts the structure) is the hardest to find. The Feiyun Commerce Guild holds one piece for auction, which Xingqiu expected to sell for over 500 million Mora, so he pointed the party to the abode instead. Paimon worries that taking a dead adeptus' treasure is disrespectful; Shenhe says Sea Gazer would not have minded, on the strength of a rumour only.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Because the abode is abandoned, its adepti defences treat visitors as intruders. Ancient Array Cores release pulses that deal Physical damage, and each is protected by waves of monsters (slimes of several elements, hilichurls, Mitachurls, a Lawachurl, a Geovishap and Ruin Guards, per the domain's enemy lists). An inactive Ruin Guard on the lower levels awakens and smashes the ground during the descent.
### LARGE +
The falls between levels are part of the way down; the party survives the last drop only because a pool of water breaks it.

## travel
### STANDARD
Entry requires removing the concealing adepti art at the marked spot in Lisha and then following the Seelie to the waterfall. Within the abode movement is by gliding on wind currents, using Plaustrite platforms and pushing stone walls.

## quests
@version: 2.4
### STANDARD
Seagaze Sunset, part 2 of Act I (The Crane Returns on the Wind) in the Interlude Chapter, is the only quest set here. After Xingqiu's briefing at the Feiyun Commerce Guild, the party reaches the abode, descends through its levels and finds the huge Sunset Vermillionite. Shenhe lifts it alone and carries it to the Jade Chamber worksite, where Ningguang's secretary Baiwen rates it the finest Plaustrite received so far and says it will go into the Jade Chamber's foundation.
### LARGE +
Afterwards Shenhe admits she is fatigued from the abode, accepts a rest at a makeshift hotel run by Ju'an, and Cloud Retainer arrives the next day.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
A cloud-bound pocket world: floating weathered stone, ancient mechanisms humming with leftover adeptal power, the sound of water, and a small Seelie drifting ahead as a guide. It feels grand and lonely, the private treasure room of a collector left to rust and monsters.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the abode is a finished quest location. Its treasure, the Sunset Vermillionite, was removed in 2.4 and is meant for the Jade Chamber's foundation.
### STANDARD +
The wiki lists no later appearance, and nothing in canon through 7.1 says what became of the remaining defences or the second Seelie in the court. Its builder remains dead and only sparsely described.

## entry: Sea Gazer's Abode (Seagaze Sunset)
@keys: Sea Gazer's Abode, Abode of Sea Gazer, Sea Gazer, Seagaze Sunset
@version: 2.4
### STANDARD
The abode is where the Traveler and Shenhe find the Sunset Vermillionite for the Jade Chamber: a hidden treasure house of the dead adeptus Sea Gazer in Lisha, with cloud-veiled levels, Ancient Array Cores, movable stone walls and a guiding Seelie.
### LARGE +
Sea Gazer was a close friend of Mountain Shaper, and Cloud Retainer and Guizhong would answer his boasts about new finds with mechanical creations of their own. A Mingxiao Lantern in the Lantern Rite event The Exquisite Night Chimes was modelled after him, which is limited-event material.
