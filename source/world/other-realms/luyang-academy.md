## summary
### LITE
Luyang Academy is the part of the Temple of Space devoted to painting, built in the style of Liyue architecture. Though it is a gallery of paintings from many eras, the sages who first named it believed that books and paintings are one and the same, hence "academy". Under the Watcher Melanta's rule its artists were punished by being turned into ink blots or sealed in paintings. In 6.5 the Traveler, Paimon, Zeitlind and Dex freed them by restoring paintings into real space.
### STANDARD +
Death does not exist in the temple, so the sealed artists were alive in their paintings and could be released.

## geography
### STANDARD
A Liyue-styled compound reached across a small island, with halls such as the Abode of Resplendent Talent, the Pavilion of Paintings and the Tower of Radiant Light. Its defining mechanic is the painting world: with Dex, the player can re-project paintings, entering them to complete missing parts of space, or fight ink blots in a top-down view. The area opens with the quest Long Live Life.
### LARGE +
A dim star in ink hangs above the entrance. One door cannot be forced open, since Daozi explains it was manifested from a painting through the mind and is not physical matter as we know it; a painter must open it. Elite enemies are Domain Keepers and the Xuanwen Beast.

## people
### STANDARD
Daozi, a painter who coached Kanou in Liyue landscape painting, is freed first and guides the party. Kanou, Yoshitoshi and Bosch are painters later freed (Kanou says he never wants to paint another screen); Castiglione, Jinggui and Zhangsun Jijing are also listed there. Bosch and Daozi once taught Raumhilda to draw, and she named a painting "Long Live Life" before leaving it in the Academy.
### LARGE +
Daozi says that realism makes the best cage for a spirit because its essence is read at a glance, so realistic paintings hide secrets. They admire the feat attributed to Rex Lapis of bringing stone to life, but Yu Huangyi told them a dragon like Azhdaha already existed in his era.

## culture
### STANDARD
Artists sign commissions with secret signatures. They treat the mind as stronger than matter: Istoria's teacher Aristocles said so, and Daozi uses it to explain why a painted door resists a kick. The painters were somewhat unsettled by "Maukie"'s space-bending paintings but like him and use his nickname; Daozi says Melanta regards names as numbers assigned on arrival.

## history
### STANDARD
Created by Asmoday as a gallery for paintings. When she disappeared, Melanta sought to restore the temple to its original order and punished the Academy by turning its people into ink blots and sealing them in paintings; he also had "Maukie" exiled for defiling the laws of space. In 6.5, in Long Live Life, the party freed Daozi, then Kanou, Yoshitoshi and Bosch, and unrolled the great scroll by plucking the star in the Pavilion of Paintings.
### LARGE +
Along the way the party meets a lone figure who says he is the painter Escher and offers to take them somewhere safe; he proves a fake, and ink floods in as the party flees through re-projected paintings, until a bridge painting collapses and drops them near the Tower of Radiant Light. Daozi blames Melanta, who works behind the scenes. In one painting Zeitlind finds photographs from her sister's Kamera, which she takes. Afterwards Bosch shows Zeitlind a painting her sister left, which Zeitlind chooses to leave in the Academy. Istoria later chats with the party outside about philosophy.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Domain Keepers, ink blots (harmful to touch), the Xuanwen Beast, and the disorienting rules of painting-space.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Mist and ink, silent halls in a Liyue style; paintings that could hide doors, cages or whole landscapes.

## status
### LITE
As of 6.5 (and still at 7.1) the Academy is freed and its painters live there again.
### STANDARD +
The painters remain in the Temple of Space; Raumhilda's painting "Long Live Life" stays on display.

## entry: Raumhilda's Painting
@keys: Long Live Life, Raumhilda's painting
@keys2: Luyang Academy, Zeitlind, Bosch
@version: 6.5
@spoiler: minor
### LARGE
Raumhilda, who had spent time in the Academy, made a painting that Bosch thought neither mastery nor childlike innocence, only sadness. She said the canvas, once coloured, cannot become white again, and signed it with a secret signature. Her younger sister Zeitlind finds it, learns it is hers, and decides it should remain here in peace instead of being taken outside, where it might be lost within a hundred years.
### ULTRA +
The painting names the quest. The conversations and the philosophy that Istoria shared with Raumhilda are not in this entry.

## entry: "Maukie"
@keys: "Maukie", Maukie
@keys2: Luyang Academy, Desert Pavilion, Temple of Space
@version: 6.5
### ULTRA
A resident nicknamed "Maukie", a painter whose art bent space, was exiled by Melanta for defiling the laws of space. The Academy's painters worry he was lured into the Desert Pavilion's construction site; the Pavilion's survivors say he was not among those restored. Daozi says "Maukie" was the nickname Escher's grandfather and father gave him as a child. A false "Maukie" appears in the Academy's paintings and leads the party into an ink trap.
