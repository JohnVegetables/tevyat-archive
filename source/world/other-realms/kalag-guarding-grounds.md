## summary
### LITE
The Kalag Guarding Grounds is a security area in Ungien's Circle, the biosphere on the Frost Moon. It is named for Kalag, the moon's Security Chief. A Local Legend automaton, Mushteshir, can be fought here after two combat challenges.
### STANDARD +
A terminal at the back of the room carries an order concerning the Stellar Blackbody: all seven Kings have been informed, and to protect the biosphere, the administrative authority of the researchers Ungien and Maagees is temporarily suspended. Objections are to go to Kalag, who holds supreme clearance.

## places
@version: 6.7
### STANDARD
The area has a combat arena and, at the very back of the room, the terminal. Mushteshir, "The Pioneer That Gazes at the Stars", is a Hydro automaton of the Secret Source family; after the first visit it enters the arena immediately on respawn.

## people
@version: 6.7
### STANDARD
Kalag (Security Chief, supreme clearance) is the authority named here. Lusalim's research log in Maagees's Greenhouse complains that Kalag took control of the biosphere and blamed the loss of control over the Moonlit Vines on Blackbody contamination caused by Maagees's research.
### LARGE +
In his own lab logs Lusalim hopes Kalag's force does not learn of the research and notes it has no access to his laboratory yet; he adds that the Eye of Ibni-Belum team chose to stand with Maagees even against Kalag's force.

## history
@version: 6.7
### STANDARD
The suspension order shows the Kings were told of the Stellar Blackbody and that the biosphere's safety was the stated reason. Kalag's security force then took control of Ungien's Circle, according to Lusalim's log, and the Project Lunar Veil wiki page says this forced Lusalim to continue Project Lunar Veil elsewhere, in the Moontide Sea.

## status
### STANDARD
Abandoned as of 7.1, with the Local Legend as its only active occupant.
### ULTRA +
The wiki notes that the name Kalag matches a word meaning strong in Akkadian and Sumerian, which is a real-world etymology and not canon lore. Mushteshir's Local Legend form is a combat encounter and need not be treated as an in-world 'guard'.
