## summary
### LITE
The Space Lifeform Capture Station is a dragon-built space station floating above Ungien's Circle on the Frost Moon. The researcher Ungien made it to capture or contact the alien creatures he believed lived among the stars, so as to prove his belief that the deep-space signal came from such a being. A huge elevator called the Skymount carries visitors up from the moon to reach it.
### STANDARD +
It forms the upper half of a single complex with the Ungien Biosphere below. The Traveler, Paimon and Eggie rode up in the first Visitors From the Stars quest and found Columbina floating at a window inside, watching Maggie, the Mega Kuuhenki.

## geography
### STANDARD
The station is in space proper, far enough above the moon that the party sees Teyvat from it. Inside, everything is weightless; the party is limited to the Traveler plus Columbina if owned. Rooms: the Main Observation Room, the Gravity Control Room, the Space Capture Arm and, outside, the wrecked Moongazer.
### LARGE +
The Main Observation Room is an observatory where Teyvat can be viewed with a miniature showing its link to the three moons. The Gravity Control Room contains a Gravimeter for controlling gravity artificially. The Space Capture Arm is the damaged corridor where the Moongazer struck.

## places
### STANDARD
Main Observation Room (a view of Teyvat and a model of the three moons). Gravity Control Room (a Gravimeter). Space Capture Arm (presumed capture corridor, damaged). Moongazer Wreckage (see location.moongazer).

## people
### STANDARD
Ungien, life scientist, designer; Maagees-Geti, his partner, who investigated the Moongazer and later became the Mega Kuuhenki Maggie; Lusalim, their student. In the story only Columbina, Maggie and the Traveler's party are present.
### LARGE +
Columbina uses the station as a vantage point to monitor Ungie, the other Mega Kuuhenki, and to explain Eggie's origins to the party.

## history
### STANDARD
Ungien built the station as part of Project Moongazer to prove his theory that the deep-space signal received by the dragon pioneers came from an alien lifeform, not from the dragon king as Ibni-Belum had held. His own voyage in the Moongazer ended when the ship returned cloaked years later and struck the station's Space Capture Arm.
### LARGE +
After the collision and emergency repairs, Maagees-Geti entered the ship and found the creature that matched Ungien's data signature; she later converted him into the kuuhenki Ungie. The damaged arm and the ship were left as they were. The facility was one of the dragon sites left untouched when the Heavenly Principles arrived; Columbina reasons that Lusalim's bargain may have spared it.
### ULTRA +
The station's loading-screen description calls it a place that holds Ungien's conviction, later passed on to the greenhouse mistress. It stands as one of four sites Columbina names as parts of Project Moongazer: the Eye of Ibni-Belum, Ungien's Circle, this station and Lusalim's Lunar Veil Laboratory.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Silent, weightless and quiet, with Teyvat hanging in the window. The mood is of a half-forgotten dream of contact with something else in the sky.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Inside, nothing hostile is shown; the risks are the lift (the Skymount fails when power is out), weightlessness, and the energy storms that rise from the lunar sea below.

## travel
### STANDARD
Reached by the Skymount elevator inside the great tree of Ungien's Circle. The lift needed the Ungien power engine repaired, which the party did by clearing Knotted Moonlight Vines from its turbines and escaping before they restarted.

## quests
### STANDARD
Visitors From the Stars (Part 1 and Part 2), Moon Gazing chapter Act II (6.7). The hidden exploration quest A Journey's End also starts at the Space Capture Arm.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the station is damaged but intact and open to visitors via the Skymount.
### STANDARD +
Columbina treats it as part of her home to be tidied. Ungie and Maggie, the two Mega Kuuhenki, live in the lunar sea below, not in the station.

### STANDARD
The Skymount is a huge lift set against the great tree of Ungien's Circle. It failed at first for lack of energy. A blueprint terminal showed that the complex has two parts, the Ungien Biosphere on the ground and the capture station in orbit, and that its power hub sits underground fed by a sea of dense ancient-moon energy beneath Ungien's Circle. The party restored four turbines to reboot it.
