## summary
### LITE
Traveler's Crater is the huge crater on the Frost Moon in whose centre the Traveler's spaceship rests. Columbina led the Traveler here in Welkin Moon's Homecoming (6.3), when it was a quest-only location, and from the Moon Gazing quests (6.7) it can be visited freely. Teyvat hangs in the sky above it.
### STANDARD +
The viewpoint here is called The Next Journey. The crater can be reached by the High-Speed Transit Lane from the Nuur Stone Circle.

## geography
### STANDARD
A wide lunar crater under low gravity, with the star-shaped, white-and-gold spaceship standing at its centre. Columbina first reached it on foot across the lunar plain, after crossing a path of moonlight and a boat on a lake of Moon-Prayer Blossoms from Teyvat.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Open lunar ground under a sky where the world of Teyvat is visible. Columbina remarks that she could spend a whole day looking at Teyvat from here, and that she took off her eye mask to see it.

## history
@spoiler: major
@version: 6.3
### STANDARD
The Heavenly Principles hid the spaceship on the Frost Moon after the Cataclysm. Columbina sensed it after she became the Trilune Goddess and took the Traveler there. The Traveler read the logs aboard, collected an old outfit, and learned that the ship had flown over Teyvat once, much earlier, without landing. See the spaceship entry for details.

## quests
@version: 6.7
### STANDARD
Visited during Welkin Moon's Homecoming (6.3), then made permanently available in the Moon Gazing quests. A post-quest conversation with Columbina here follows Project Moongazer (Part 2).

## status
### STANDARD
As of 7.1 the crater is open and the spaceship remains where the Heavenly Principles placed it.
### LARGE +
Columbina told the Traveler she was unsure why it was hidden here and urged them to go in alone while she waited, humming a tune. When the Traveler came out, they said the truth was neither good nor bad, and that they considered Teyvat their home.
### ULTRA +
The crater is reached afterwards through the Nuur Stone Circle's transit lane. Columbina said she hoped to bring kuuhenki and friends to the moon in future once she had tidied it, since the place had had no guests for a very long time.
