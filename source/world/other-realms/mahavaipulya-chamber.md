## summary
### LITE
The Mahavaipulya Chamber is the Temple of Space's library, devoted to ancient texts and to the new novels written by some of its Uplifted residents. Its loading-screen text says it stores books from various eras. Because the residents' stories blurred history and fiction, the Watcher Melanta pressed them between the pages; in 6.5 the Traveler, Paimon, Zeitlind and Dex restored the library and freed them.
### STANDARD +
The name comes from the Sanskrit "Mahavaipulya", the great expansive sutras, an allusion the wiki ties to the Buddhist Avatamsaka tradition of infinite realms. The place can be called the Mahavaipulya Hall or Archives.

## geography
### STANDARD
A library whose pages are doorways: with Dex the player can flip pages to open passages, fold them and ride floating pages to other places, revealing rooms in different styles (one is an Inazuma-style room). Restoration Cores hidden in these spaces must be gathered and inserted to restore the building. The area opens with the quest Counterfeit Classics.
### LARGE +
Seelie wander the reading rooms. A large book called "Tale of the Lochknight Aerinny" is a retelling of the Remuria-era story "Erinnyes", and the party can enter it as a trial of rewriting its plot.

## people
### STANDARD
Yu Huangyi comes from the era when Shanhui Fort stood below Mt. Tianheng, before the Guili Assembly; Calcerius is a Remurian; Okikuni comes from the youkai age before the Narukami unified the archipelago. Istoria spoke highly of Calcerius's rewrite of "Erinnyes", saying it captured the moment realism collapsed into nominalism.
### LARGE +
Yu Huangyi, who guides the party through the library, says besides the borderland newcomers all Remurians are made of rock; Calcerius, who lost his faith in Phobos in the Apathic Interval, corrects this as "ichorification". Okikuni was fascinated by Liyue novels and wished to join their adventures. The three joke that together they have the strength of an entry-level rookie soldier.
### ULTRA +
Raumhilda chatted with the residents even after they had been pressed into paper; Yu Huangyi says her arrival, when the selection process was in disarray and everyone was low in spirit, brought a spark of life.

## culture
### STANDARD
The Uplifted swapped stories and kept writing novels even after the Apathic Interval, and enjoyed Liyue novels written by later generations. Zeitlind says her favourite among the Yaksha was Alatus. Naming things is a game: the Restoration Core was named by Paimon after the residents' own jokes.

## history
### STANDARD
Created by Asmoday as an archive of books. After she vanished the Shades allowed small changes, and the Uplifted began writing novels that mingled fact and fiction. Melanta classified this as pollution and punished them by pressing them between pages. In 6.5, during Counterfeit Classics, the party freed the paper people, collected Restoration Cores, and restored the library, which broke Melanta's control over the area.
### LARGE +
To retrieve the last core the Traveler and Paimon entered the retold "Aerinny", guided by Zeitlind's imagination to steer a tragedy toward a happy ending. Zeitlind also finds a chest of letters she and her sister exchanged.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Domain Keepers, traps hidden in pages, and trap rooms. Floors may fall away when a Restoration Core is touched.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Quiet rooms of shelves and oversized pages, with the chatter and jokes of the freed paper people.

## status
### LITE
As of 6.5 (unchanged through 7.1) the library is restored and its residents free.
### STANDARD +
The residents remain in the Temple of Space and are friendly with visitors.

### LARGE
The Restoration Core is the device Paimon named for the objects Dex uses to unfold manipulated space back to its original form. Yu Huangyi says space manipulated leaves a residual trail of its reversal. Cores must be carried and inserted; some are in pieces hidden within pages.

### ULTRA
Three Uplifted writers from different eras who were turned into paper. They are good-natured, argue constantly about the origin of their trouble, and promise to tell the others that the party is safe.
