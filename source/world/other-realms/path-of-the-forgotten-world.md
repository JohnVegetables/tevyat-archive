## summary
### LITE
The Path of the Forgotten World is the entrance area of the Temple of Space, the floating hall above Windrest Peak in Mondstadt. The wiki calls it the entrance area, leading toward the central Pillar Hall. Creatures from lost ages are exhibited along it. The area has very little recorded lore of its own.
### STANDARD +
The wiki describes it as Asmoday's adaptation of the Login Screen environment. Its map levels are labelled the Pillar Hall of Space Front Hall and Rear Hall.

## geography
### STANDARD
A pair of halls running from the entrance to the central Pillar Hall, with a Teleport Waypoint, Harpasta (which turn the player into a Plume Moth) and Organization Type Segment-Domain Keepers. Dex helps the player move architecture along the way.
### LARGE +
The Organization Type Keepers usually do not harm people unless they try to steal relics, according to Istoria.

## people
### STANDARD
No residents are listed. Exhibits include two Fontemer Aberrants: an Armored Crab and a Blubberbeast, which speak when interacted with.
### LARGE +
The Armored Crab recalls seeing creatures like it in the southern territories when the apostle of the Golden City (Hyperborea) still walked the world, wonders what the Great Mother who made them would think of one of her creations confined here, and adds that perhaps every place in the world is a cage. The Blubberbeast notes that this "lazy and degenerate lower creature", as Remurians called it, has wandered the seas for longer than Remuria endured, and so may be closer to eternity than any empire.

## history
### STANDARD
Part of Asmoday's temple. The temple selects not only people but landscapes, relics and ecosystems from vanished ages, which is why animals from lost civilisations are kept here.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Organization Type Keepers (avoid stealing relics) and Domain Keepers elsewhere in the temple.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Hushed and museum-like: exhibits left standing in a space with no wind and no passage of time.

## status
### LITE
As of 6.5 (unchanged through 7.1) the Path is open and uneventful.
### STANDARD +
No quest is listed as centred on it.

### ULTRA
Two speaking Fontemer Aberrants stand along the Path. The Armored Crab refers to the apostle of the Golden City and the Great Mother, giving a Hyperborean perspective; the Blubberbeast makes a wry point about Remuria's contempt for it. Both are best used as flavour for a wandering scene rather than as proof of wider lore.
