## summary
### LITE
Simulanka is a storybook world created by the Hexenzirkel witches Alice, Barbeloth and Andersdotter, whom its people call the Goddesses of Creation, Prophecy and Fate. In the limited 4.8 event Summertide Scales and Tales, a strange book sent by Alice pulled the Traveler, Paimon and several friends into it to save the world from an evil dragon. Visions do not work there.
### STANDARD +
The dragon turned out to be Durin, a gentle storybook creation of the Goddess of Fate whose touch turns things into toy blocks. Nilou, Navia, Kirara and the Wanderer gave him their blessings, and he became a small, harmless dragon. Alice then told the party how Simulanka was founded and how they could leave.
### LARGE +
The event ran from the 4.8 update (2024-07-17) to the end of 4.8 and is canon but limited: not every Teyvat character knows of it.
### ULTRA +
Simulanka is also the root of the later story of Durin, who was built from Andersdotter's tale and, in the Draco Rubedo Chapter (Version 6.2), faced the danger that the real Durin's fate would overtake him.

## geography
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
Three subareas make up the world. The Forest of Blessings is where visitors first arrive, a woodland whose creatures are made of folded paper. Constellation Metropole is a city of tracks, gears and toy figures, reached by train. The Broken Sea is at the far end of the world, where a mining village of stranded workers and guards lives near the dragon's waterfall lair.
### LARGE +
A railway called the Maritime Express runs between the Forest, the Metropole and the end of the world. The Metropole is climbed by a lift called the Gear Sky Ladder, and the Goddess of Prophecy's statue stands at its highest point, near the Astral Garden. At the Broken Sea there is a boat that can carry travellers to another world, and a giant bookmark at the Cliff of Prophecy does the same. A study belonging to the Goddess of Fate lies beneath the dragon's lair.
### ULTRA +
Page III has the party fall through the dragon's lair into a world that works like the next page of the book: the mine's lamp resembles the star on the page before, and the old book records Simulanka's history page by page.

## people
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
The Forest of Blessings is inhabited by origami animals such as paper frogs, hamsters, alpacas and flying squirrels. Nilou is cast as the Fairy of the Forest in the story's prophecy, and the villagers include Almond, Ginkgo and Chestnut. Navia is cast as the King of Constellation Metropole, with ministers Montagne and Bizerte. The Maritime Express is driven by Wheel. Kirara and the Wanderer ('Hat Guy') arrive separately, the Wanderer bearing the title Hero of Simulanka.
### LARGE +
Metropole residents are toy figures and guards who accept the Goddess of Prophecy's oracles; the Forest residents say all new arrivals thank the Goddess of Creation in the Forest, hear oracles in the Metropole, and finally tell the Goddess of Fate their dearest wish at the end of the world. Most stranded workers and guards at the Broken Sea are people who dug toytanium mines or came to claim the dragon's head and could not leave.

## governance
### STANDARD
No fixed government is shown. Navia is told by a voice that she is the king of Constellation Metropole and plays the role; Nilou's role is Fairy of the Forest. These are story roles given to visitors, not permanent offices.

## culture
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
Life in Simulanka follows the three goddesses. The Goddess of Creation gave life to matter and magic and folded storybook pages into the Forest's animals. The Goddess of Prophecy rules the stars and the natural course of things, and the Metropole's machinery is described as her gift for keeping people on track, though residents keep personal freedom. The Goddess of Fate presides over cherished stories and wishes, and visitors tell her their wishes at the end of the world.
### LARGE +
Words and feelings carry real power in this fairytale world: a blessing spoken sincerely can change what a thing is. The Forest's residents tell the story of how the goddesses made them, with some local argument over who actually saw the Goddess of Creation.
### ULTRA +
The people of Simulanka have predestined lives. Navia notes that these lives were always going to disappear, and Alice replies that Andersdotter wished the people of Simulanka could one day choose their own path and see the wider world beyond.

## history
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
Alice, Barbeloth and Andersdotter created Simulanka together. Alice (Goddess of Creation) proposed stepping into the worlds in Andersdotter's stories; Barbeloth (Goddess of Prophecy) mapped the fate of all living things; and Andersdotter, 'M', the Goddess of Fate, wrote the tales. Andersdotter wrote a story set among origami animals, and also the story of a young dragon.
### LARGE +
Barbeloth used her powers to map out the fates of everything in Simulanka, which Andersdotter felt spoiled the ending. Andersdotter, who did not expect to live long, bet that the people would one day carve their own destiny. She wrote the dragon's story knowing his fate would echo the real-world Durin's if given the name; she gave him the name anyway and wished that he could have a happier ending.
### ULTRA +
After Andersdotter's death she became a star in Simulanka, and the dragon, grieving, flew up and took the stars; people feared and hunted him, and he hid in a lair. Years of would-be dragon-slayers had made him wary. The party's blessings broke the curse and shrank him.

## dangers
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
Near the dragon his breath makes full power impossible (the party could not use Elemental Skills and Bursts in the final fight). The Wanderer says the fog around can cause hallucinations; the dragon's attack also made the party witness his memories. Hilichurls seized one train platform. Visions do not work anywhere in Simulanka; the story's own 'magic' replaces them.

## travel
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
Visitors arrive by opening a book Alice sent them, not by any road. Within the world one travels by the Maritime Express. To leave, one takes the boat at the Broken Sea or touches the giant bookmark at the Cliff of Prophecy and concentrates on the destination; Alice noted the way home was available from the beginning, though no one told the party.

## quests
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
The story of Summertide Scales and Tales has three pages and an afterword: the Forest of Blessings and the closed tavern, the trip to the Metropole to ask the Goddess of Prophecy, and the train to the end of the world to face the evil dragon. Four mini-games supplied the gameplay: Boreal Flurry, Flying Hatter's Trick, Metropole Trials and Figurine Fabrication.
### LARGE +
The event's reward character was Kirara, and Kirara's outfit Phantom in Boots was an event prize.

## status
### STANDARD
As of 7.1 Simulanka is not accessible. Durin lives in Mondstadt as a playable character (6.2). Alice has since appeared in person elsewhere in the story, but not in Simulanka.

## entry: Goddesses of Simulanka
@keys: Goddess of Creation, Goddess of Prophecy, Goddess of Fate
@keys2: Simulanka
@version: 4.8
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
Simulanka's people call Alice the Goddess of Creation, Barbeloth the Goddess of Prophecy and Andersdotter ('M') the Goddess of Fate. Alice tells the party that the three contributed equally to founding the world. The Goddess of Fate is described as M's reflection who grants wishes and loves cliffhangers. The Goddess of Prophecy is statued at the highest point of Constellation Metropole.

## entry: Simulanka's evil dragon
@keys: Simulanka's Evil Dragon, evil dragon, Mini Durin
@keys2: Simulanka
@version: 4.8
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
The 'evil dragon' of Simulanka is Durin, a gentle storybook creation of the Goddess of Fate who was shunned and hunted by Simulanka's people. He took the stars after M's death and could turn things to toy blocks. The Wanderer reached him in the dragon's memories and told him his name. The party's blessings freed him from the curse and he became Mini Durin. His later story is in Durin's own entry.

### STANDARD
Simulanka's origami woodland, where the Hut of Blessings stands and where the Goddess of Creation first made the townspeople. Its tavern was closed until Nilou learned the magic of the Goddess of Creation. Its animals are made of paper and fear the dragon.

## entry: Constellation Metropole
@keys: Constellation Metropole, Maritime Express
@keys2: Simulanka
@version: 4.8
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
Simulanka's gear-driven toy city, ruled in the story by Navia as king, with a Goddess of Prophecy statue at its summit and the Astral Garden. Its stars were scattered when the dragon took them. Routes include the Gear Sky Ladder and the Maritime Express station.
