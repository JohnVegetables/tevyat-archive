## summary
### LITE
The Frost Moon is the second of Teyvat's Three Moons, which the dragon king Nibelung created. It is the brightest of them and the only one that survived the war of funerary flame. The Heavenly Principles banished it beyond the false sky, where its goddess Canon faded and died. For thousands of years Teyvat saw only a fake "shadowmoon" in its place. In True Moon (6.3) the real Frost Moon was pulled back inside the false sky, and Columbina, its new Moon Goddess, became the Trilune Goddess. It is now a reachable lunar world, full of ruins left by ancient dragon researchers.
### STANDARD +
The game counts it as a subregion of Nod-Krai even though it lies off Teyvat's surface. Its three areas are the Lunar Highlands, the Moontide Sea and the Dark Side of the Moon. The Heavenly Principles hid the Traveler's own spaceship there.

## geography
### STANDARD
The Frost Moon is a cratered world of grey highlands with much lower gravity than Teyvat: jumps carry far and falls are slow. Teyvat hangs huge in its sky. Turbulent Archaeolune energy shapes it. This is an ancient lunar power related to kuuvahki, which Columbina calls "blue kuuvahki". The near side is divided between the Lunar Highlands and the Moontide Sea, a glowing lunar mare that covers much of that face. The far side, facing away from Teyvat, was rebuilt into an enormous weapon. The moon's core, the Moonheart Gap, powers it.
### LARGE +
The Lunar Highlands hold the Eye of Ibni-Belum, Traveler's Crater, Ungien's Circle (seen from space as a yellowed crater) and the Golden Hall. The Moontide Sea is a sea of concentrated Archaeolune energy. It was born when the Heavenly Principles destroyed a dragon laboratory, and floating stones rise from it. The far side holds the "Dark Fortress" of launch tubes, where gravity is strange enough to walk on the walls. Some zones are weightless, others under crushing hypergravity, and the dragons' gravimeters can change the local pull.
### ULTRA +
Nibelung raised all three moons out of Teyvat's crust, at what is now Nod-Krai. According to the moon lore, the Eternal Moon was the largest and caused the tides, and the Iridescent Moon was the smallest. The Frost Moon was the brightest, the "cold, undying lantern" of night. A local specialty, the Teardrop of the Moon, grows only here. It is an azure crystal holding unusually pure kuuvahki, with near-perfect affinity for every kind of elemental energy. Its item text links it to the old Mondstadt bards' name for night dew, "tears of the moon".

## places
### LARGE
- **Lunar Highlands**: the dragon pioneers' first settlement zone. It holds the Eye of Ibni-Belum (a deep-space transceiver later turned into a lunar monitoring station), Ungien's Circle (a biosphere for living things, with the Space Lifeform Capture Station) and Traveler's Crater, where the Traveler's spaceship rests.
- **Moontide Sea**: the lunar sea, with the Curtain-Form Blackbody Containment Lab beneath it and the ruins of Lusalim's Lunar Veil Laboratory.
- **Dark Side of the Moon**: a high-security dragon base and launch silo. Lusalim's last laboratory is here, and the Moonheart Gap lies below.
### ULTRA +
- **Golden Hall**: a site in the Highlands tied to the Arcadian boatman Euthyphro and the vessel of the moon goddesses (see its sub-entry).
- **Dunanna Forward Station** (on Teyvat, in Nod-Krai's Dunanna Pit): an old dragon base whose "Celestial Orbit" terminal can find the Frost Moon's coordinates. It held a lunar gravity simulator and a moon-bound spaceship built by Ibni-Belum. This ship carried the Traveler and Paimon to the moon in Moon Gazing, and its escape pods brought them down.

## people
### LARGE
No people live on the Frost Moon. Its inhabitants are kuuhenki, wildlife and machines. Small schools of kuuhenki live in the lunar sea. There are two "Mega Kuuhenki": Maggie, who is gentle and helps Columbina, and Ungie, who went berserk while overgrown with moonlit vines. Moonglow jellyfish and Moonglow Frostfin Whales also live there. The dragons' security machines still patrol. Columbina is its goddess but lives on Hiisi Island. She visits often, says she has to tidy the place up before she can receive guests, and hopes to bring willing kuuhenki there.
### ULTRA +
Both Mega Kuuhenki were once dragons. Maggie is what became of Professor Maagees-Geti, and Ungie came from the creature her husband Ungien turned into. Each was transformed into a being of Archaeolune energy and Blackbody Matter, and neither kept its original consciousness. Eggie, a black kuuhenki born from the Moongazers' stored data, was found by Columbina in Maagees's Greenhouse. She later carried the Moongazers' message (see Project Moongazer).

## history
### STANDARD
Canon, the second-born Moon Sister, ruled the Frost Moon. She and her sisters took turns over the night sky. Before the Heavenly Principles came, dragon researchers led by the pioneer Ibni-Belum settled the moon and built research facilities there. When Nibelung came back from the Abyss and made war on the Heavenly Principles, he imprisoned the hesitating Moon Sisters in the "moon's reflection" and began turning their moons into weapons. In return the Heavenly Principles shattered the Eternal and Iridescent Moons and threw the Frost Moon, still intact, beyond the false sky. Cut off from Teyvat, Canon lasted for a time, long enough to lead Hyperborea's survivors to Hiisi Island, and then faded. Her worshippers, the Frostmoon Scions, prophesied that a new goddess would come. Columbina was born on Hiisi Island in Nod-Krai some 400 to 500 years ago.
### LARGE +
The Frost Moon is not dead. Skirk passed through the false sky centuries ago and saw two moons in lifeless pieces and a third that was whole and showed no life, though its will had not completely faded. While Columbina was trapped in the moon's reflection during the Archon Quest, she split herself into three parts across time. Her Trilune authority was hidden inside the Frost Moon. In Descending Moon (6.3) Sandrone's world-formula connected Columbina again. Arlecchino, Varka and Nicole dragged the real moon into Teyvat through the opening Dottore had torn, breaking the false sky there. The scattered parts of Columbina reunited and she descended as the Trilune Goddess. The shadowmoon became unnecessary and broke apart.
### ULTRA +
The dragons' records describe a long settled age. Ibni-Belum explored the moon and the space around it, built the Eye after receiving a deep-space signal, and was honoured after his death as "the first Moongazer". During Nibelung's absence the dragon Xiuhcoatl visited the moons, looking for his trail, and got only silence. Lusalim, the last Moongazer, capitulated to the Heavenly Principles and surrendered his research to them, which his final records call a sin against dragonkind. Columbina says the Dark Side weapon was "reforged" by a Dragon Lord of Darkness and that the Heavenly Principles defeated him. She notes the moon carries no trace of Abyssal corruption despite millennia outside the false sky.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Silent grey plains under a black sky, with Teyvat shining overhead. Bounding steps in low gravity. Pale-blue Archaeolune light runs through old dragon conduits, and huge machine-like structures that still work stand among the craters. The Moontide Sea glows and churns, and the far side looks like an armoured fortress. It feels lonely and very old, but not dead: kuuhenki drift by, terminals still hold the dragons' voices, and Columbina speaks of it as a second home.

## dangers
### LARGE
Archaeolune storms and the lunar sea cause exhaustion that can knock out anyone who stays in them; floating stones and storm-occlusion fields are the safe places. Gravity can change suddenly between weightless and crushing. The dragons' security systems still guard their bases: Pioneer Source Structures (self-destructing, patrolling and teleporting modules) and the elite Precursor's Secret Source Matrix of Overseer Network. The moon's local legends include a Prism Slime and the creature Mushteshir.

## travel
### STANDARD
There are two known ways to reach the moon. Since True Moon, Columbina can open a path of blue moonlight from the Idol of the New Moon. It leads through murals to a crescent-shaped boat that sails to the moon, and she can teleport herself and others there. The second is the dragons' moon-bound ship at Dunanna Forward Station (Moon Gazing). Once there, travelers use the dragons' High-Speed Transit Lanes, which run on the ancient moon's power and double as waypoints.

## quests
### LARGE
- **True Moon** (Archon Quest, Song of the Welkin Moon Act VIII, 6.3): the moon is pulled into the false sky. In Welkin Moon's Homecoming, Columbina takes the Traveler to their spaceship on the moon.
- **Moon Gazing** (World Quest series, 6.7: Pioneers, Visitors From the Stars, Project Moongazer): unlocks the Frost Moon map and tells the Moongazers' story.
- **The Unmanned Vessel** (World Quest series, 6.7): three quests about the moon goddesses' vessel, with flashbacks of the Three Moon Goddesses.
- **Beneath the Lunar Sea** (World Quest, 6.7): leads into the Curtain-Form Blackbody Containment Lab.
### ULTRA +
The moon has two Ancient Moon's Remnant reputation shrines, at Nuur Stone Circle and at the Curtain-Form Blackbody Containment Lab, which take Luna Sigils. In a later Selenic Chronicles quest (The Artist By the Moon's Side (IV)), Columbina told the kuuhenki painter Maalaus about her visit to the moon with the Traveler and gave her moon soil; Maalaus painted the two of them gazing at Teyvat from the moon, and Columbina said she would like to take the kuuhenki to the moon more often.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the Frost Moon is back inside the false sky and visible over Teyvat again. Columbina reigns over it as the Trilune Goddess. She lives on Hiisi Island and travels freely between Nod-Krai and the moon. The shadowmoon is gone. The Moongazers' message was transmitted again in Moon Gazing. The Snezhnaya Archon Quest (7.0-7.1) adds nothing new about the moon.
### STANDARD +
After True Moon, Columbina gave back part of her power to Nod-Krai as kuuvahki. She said she could do so safely because the Frost Moon now lies inside the false sky and she is the Trilune Goddess. The Eternal Moon Marrow went to Lauma and the Iridescent Moon Marrow to Arlecchino. In Nasha Town, children now draw the "new" moon, which looks different from the old false one.
### ULTRA +
After Project Moongazer, Columbina waited for the Traveler at Traveler's Crater. She promised to go with them and help if they ever decide to return to their birthplace. She counts both Teyvat and the Frost Moon as home.

## entry: Project Moongazer and the Moongazers
@keys: Project Moongazer, Moongazer, Moongazers, Ibni-Belum, Ungien, Maagees-Geti, Lusalim, Nin-Kiggia, Ninnin, Eggie, Stellar Blackbody
@version: 6.7
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The Moongazers were dragon researchers on the Frost Moon. Ibni-Belum caught a mysterious deep-space signal and thought it came from Nibelung. Years after his death, Ungien flew the ship *Moongazer* to the signal's source and came back fused with an alien "Stellar Blackbody" that carried a warning and message of hope from a dying civilisation facing pitch-black annihilation. His wife Maagees-Geti began Project Moongazer to send that message on into space. Their student Lusalim stored the researchers' data in the moon's core, using creative power he borrowed from the Moon Goddesses, so that data-born messengers would be reborn again and again to repeat the transmission. In Moon Gazing (6.7) the black kuuhenki Eggie, the latest of these messengers, carried the transmission out from the Eye of Ibni-Belum with the Traveler, Paimon and Columbina. Columbina shielded the signal as it passed the false sky. Eggie's form dissolved afterwards.
### LARGE +
One earlier transmission reached the Traveler and their sibling in deep space. Inspired, they replied with thanks and promised to visit once their own troubles were over. That reply sat sealed in the Eye's data until Eggie's broadcast unlocked it. Ibni-Belum himself disapproved of the later Moongazers and expelled Ungien as an oath-breaker; the title "first Moongazer" was given to him after his death. The messages are recorded in *Moongazer's Engraving*.

## entry: The Lunar Veil and the Moontide Sea
@keys: Moontide Sea, Lunar Veil, Project Lunar Veil, lunar sea, Blackbody Matter, Curtain-Form Blackbody Containment
@version: 6.7
### STANDARD
Lusalim feared that broadcasting into space might draw hostile attention, so he and Maagees-Geti began Project Lunar Veil, a screen meant to hide the moon. It drew on Blackbody Matter, which reflects light and interacts with Archaeolune energy. A small test veil hid Ungien's Circle. Lusalim then built a generator large enough to cover the whole moon in his laboratory, but when the Heavenly Principles arrived, the laboratory and generator exploded. The blast left the Moontide Sea, a lunar sea dense with Archaeolune energy.
### LARGE +
Lusalim then moved to a last laboratory on the Dark Side and finally handed his research to the Heavenly Principles. Columbina noticed how much a moon-hiding veil resembles the false sky. Canon does not say whether one led to the other.

## entry: The Dark Side Weapon and the Moonheart Gap
@keys: Dark Side of the Moon, Moonheart Gap, Dark Fortress, Dragon Lord of Darkness
@keys2: Frost Moon, Nibelung, weapon, silo, Columbina
@version: 6.7
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The hemisphere facing away from Teyvat was turned into a giant cannon powered by the moon's core, the Moonheart Gap. Nibelung built it after returning from the Abyss, as part of his war on the Heavenly Principles. Columbina calls it the masterpiece of a "Dragon Lord of Darkness" and says the Heavenly Principles defeated it, so it is no longer a threat. In Project Moongazer the Traveler and Columbina passed through its launch silo, disabled its still-active weapon core, beat its guardian (the "Warden of the Flame"), and reached the core. The core is the seat of the moon's will and the origin of Project Moongazer.

## entry: The Traveler's Spaceship on the Moon
@keys: Traveler's Crater, Aether and Lumine's Spaceship, spaceship
@keys2: Frost Moon, moon, Columbina, Traveler
@version: 6.3
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The twins' spaceship crash-landed on Starsand Shoal in Nod-Krai long ago. After the Cataclysm of five hundred years ago, the Heavenly Principles moved it to the Frost Moon, which was then beyond the false sky. With her Trilune authority, Columbina sensed it there. In Welkin Moon's Homecoming she took the Traveler to it in a great crater, since named Traveler's Crater. The flight logs show the ship had flown past Teyvat once thousands of years earlier without landing, travelled on for a very long time, and only later came back and crash-landed. The Traveler wonders whether their sibling left the ship during that first pass. They also took a matching outfit from a cabinet aboard.

## entry: Golden Hall and Prusmene
@keys: Golden Hall, Prusmene, Euthyphro, Unmanned Vessel, silver shining ship
@version: 6.7
@spoiler: minor
### LARGE
The Golden Hall in the Lunar Highlands keeps a song by Euthyphro, a boatman from Arcadia. He prays to Prusmene, Arcadia's name for the Frost Moon's goddess (Canon), and calls her the mistress of the Golden Hall. He says he steered her silver ship to carry pure souls up to the lunar palace above the clouds. He laments that the Primordial One on its eternal throne seeks to shroud the dreams of mortals with its own dream, and begs her to deliver him from the hands of his "dread master". The Unmanned Vessel world quests (6.7) feature the vessel of the Welkin Moon and include flashbacks of the Three Moon Goddesses; whether it is the same ship as Euthyphro's is not stated.

## entry: Archaeolune Energy
@keys: Archaeolune Energy, Archaeolune, blue kuuvahki, Moonvein Suffusion, Ancient Moon Pearl
@version: 6.7
### STANDARD
Archaeolune energy is the Frost Moon's ancient power, which the game describes as the primordial form of kuuvahki. Besides kuuvahki's own properties, it can act like phlogiston (programmable matter, an energy source and an information store), and like the Primordial Sea it has creative power, including the ability to make life. The dragons ran their machines on it. A body full of it becomes lighter and can leap, release bursts that change gravity on objects, or briefly dash in the form of a black kuuhenki. Columbina calls it "blue kuuvahki".
