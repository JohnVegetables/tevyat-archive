## summary
### LITE
Swirling Isles is the Fontaine-themed Realm Layout, added in Version 4.3: a wondrous deepwater archipelago, silent and serene, with a multitude of lights swirling about. Inside the realm it is always night, whatever hour the clock shows.
### STANDARD +
It unlocks after the Archon Quest Lies Cast Shadows Under Gathered Lights, by buying the Swirling Scene in a Bottle from Tubby at the Realm Depot. The bottle is a terrarium of landscapes Chubby brought back from Fontaine. The mansion is the Fontaine Villa: Illusory Annex, and the four areas are Luminescent Islet, Seafoam Shoal, Brumerose Reef and Clearbreak Surf.
### LARGE +
Invited companions greet the player only with their night lines here, and the time-adjust function is blocked inside the Realm Within; the wiki records both as evidence the layout is fixed at night.

## geography
### STANDARD
Islands scattered across deep, still water with lights drifting around them. Brumerose Reef, the third area, has a built-in Ornamental Fish pond at the far end of its island that functions like a Pool of Sapphire Grace without counting against load.
### LARGE +
The wiki gives few other geographic details, because the other area notes are still blank.

## places
### STANDARD
Luminescent Islet (Trust Rank 1): the starting area. Seafoam Shoal (Rank 5). Brumerose Reef (Rank 7): has the built-in fish pond. Clearbreak Surf (Rank 9). Each takes up to 16,000 load.
### ULTRA +
Only the names, rank order, load and the Brumerose Reef pond are documented; do not invent features for the other isles.

## travel
### STANDARD
Reached through the Serenitea Pot gadget once the bottle has been used, then selected through Tubby. Chubby, whose trip to Fontaine the item text mentions, appears at one unlocked area on Fridays to Sundays.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Hushed night-time water with drifting lights: a dreamy setting suited to quiet, intimate or reflective scenes. It borrows Fontaine's look, but canon states no courtroom, Oratrice or Hydro Archon presence here.

## status
### STANDARD
As of Version 7.1 unchanged since 4.3 and still unlocked through the quest-gated bottle.
