## summary
### LITE
The Eye of Ibni-Belum is a tower-like dragon station on the Frost Moon. The dragon pioneer Ibni-Belum, called the First Moongazer, built it after receiving a mysterious signal from deep space that he took to be from Nibelung, hoping to hear it again. When it never came, the researchers turned it into a lunar surface monitoring station. In 6.7 the Traveler and Eggie reactivate it, and in the end Eggie uses it as the antenna to send the dragons' message back out into the stars.
### STANDARD +
It is the first stop of the Moon Gazing quests and the base for the Traveler and Columbina's journey to the dark side.

## geography
@version: 6.7
### STANDARD
Located in the southern Lunar Highlands, with a teleport waypoint, a viewpoint called Silent Barrens and another called A Gaze Toward the Stars. It has terminals, Backup Power Generators, Archaeolune Energy pathways, Pioneer Source Structures and a High-Speed Transit Lane, and an underground energy generation station below.
### LARGE +
Reactivating the Eye requires finding other energy generation stations on the surface, restoring power underground and adjusting the signal locator at the top of the tower.

## history
@spoiler: major
@version: 6.7
### STANDARD
During his exploration of the moon and the space around it, Ibni-Belum received a signal he could not decipher. He believed it was a message from Nibelung and built the Eye as a transceiver to catch it again. He died, still waiting. Many years later the signal returned, the Eye recorded its coordinates, and the life scientist Ungien flew the Moongazer to find its source. He came back fused with a creature from space who carried a message of hope from a dying civilisation, delivered the message, and died.
### LARGE +
The Eye then became central to Project Moongazer: a mission to send similar messages out so that other civilisations would know they were not alone in the dark. The Eye is the antenna for these messages. In one of its terminals, the First Moongazer's final text says that, although Ungien had been expelled from the team as an oath-breaker, his lunar biosphere work and test sites might still help landing on the distant star, and ends with praise to the Creator and the goal of journeying to the stars.
### ULTRA +
Nin-Kiggia's recording at the moon's core says each reborn Moongazer, once informed, uses themselves as a key to open the Moongazer module inside the Eye and resend the message. In 6.7 Eggie, a black kuuhenki born from that data, did so with the Traveler, Paimon and Columbina's support: the Traveler and Columbina agreed that the project could run one more time, Columbina promising to use her moon-goddess power to shield the signal and erase it if danger appeared.

## quests
@version: 6.7
### STANDARD
Pioneers (Part 2) activates the Eye. Project Moongazer (Part 2) returns here with the key; Eggie reads the project's purpose, gives up her mission briefly out of concern for her friends, then, supported by Columbina and the Traveler, sends the message as a toast to those who came before. A bundle of Saurus Crackers left near a terminal with a note in slime reading "They're all for you, Eggie" is a small post-quest detail.

## status
### STANDARD
As of 7.1 the Eye has sent the message and remains operational.

## entry: Ibni-Belum the First Moongazer
@keys: Ibni-Belum, First Moongazer
@keys2: Frost Moon, dragon, Moongazer
@version: 6.7
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
A dragon pioneer, called the First Moongazer, who led dragon researchers to the Frost Moon and began Project Moongazer. He died long before the Traveler's time, and the record in his name shows his faith in Nibelung. His name resembles an Akkadian phrase meaning "the Lord has created". The wiki holds no character profile for him.

## entry: Eggie

@keys2: Frost Moon, Eye of Ibni-Belum, Moongazer
@version: 6.7
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
A black kuuhenki met on the moon who travels with the Traveler and Paimon. She is revealed as a new life born of the data sea in the moon's core and as the project's inheritor, sometimes called by the Nin-Kiggia recording's nickname Ninnin; she chooses to send the Moongazers' message from the Eye at the end of Project Moongazer (Part 2).
