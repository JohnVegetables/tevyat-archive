## summary
### LITE
Lusalim's Laboratory is the dragon researcher Lusalim's final workplace, tucked deep inside the vast hidden base on the Dark Side of the Moon in the Frost Moon (Nod-Krai). Its terminals hold his farewell: he confesses that he sinned against dragonkind by bowing to the Heavenly Principles, and he entrusts the last step of Project Moongazer to Nin-Kiggia (Ninnin), the data-born "daughter" he created.
### STANDARD +
The laboratory is a place of records, not machinery: three terminals and a book-like engraving preserve what Lusalim wanted remembered. It was reached by the Traveler, Paimon and Eggie (who is Ninnin's successor in the story) in the second act of the Project Moongazer world quests, when Columbina had mysteriously gone missing.

## geography
### STANDARD
The laboratory lies inside the Dark Side of the Moon base, past the front hall and its security systems. In-game it sits on the base's transport level. A left corridor, a central terminal and a right corridor each hold one readable record. The base around it was built much later than the other dragon facilities and is lit and powered by a fissure that reaches down to the Moonheart Gap.
### LARGE +
To get there the party had to disable the base's security by shutting down two control rooms in quick succession, slip past patrolling searchlight machines by hiding beside storage chests, and open a door sealed by an Archaeolune Energy barrier.

## places
### STANDARD
Left corridor terminal: Lusalim's farewell record, written to Ninnin. Central terminal: a dedication in Ninnin's voice, addressed to "my father, Lusalim", crediting him as chief architect of Project Lunar Veil, designer of Nin-Kiggia's Game of Life and executor of Project Moongazer. Right corridor terminal: the Moongazer's Engraving (volume I), a digest of the message carried by the Stellar Blackbody.
### LARGE +
Volume II of the Moongazer's Engraving is given to the player automatically during the quest and is addressed by Lusalim to "my daughter, Ninnin". The engravings are text only; they record the alien message in a condensed translation, with Lusalim's note that terms he could not understand were replaced by his own equivalents.

## people
### STANDARD
Lusalim: a dragon researcher, student of Ungien and Maagees-Geti, and one of the Moongazers who inherited their work. Nin-Kiggia ("Ninnin"): the data-based life he made and raised as a daughter; her record is the one that addresses him as father. Neither is present: the laboratory is empty and only their words remain.
### LARGE +
Lusalim's record names three pieces of good fortune: living in the age after Ibni-Belum so that he could reach the moon, being taken in by Ungien and Maagees, and having Ninnin. He expects to die before the project is completed and hopes to leave a line of stars that says "The dragons were here." Eggie, the black kuuhenki who travelled with the Traveler, was born from the moon's data sea as part of the same project and carries the mission.

## history
### STANDARD
After the Heavenly Principles arrived, the Lunar Veil Laboratory in the Moontide Sea was destroyed and Lusalim moved his work to this laboratory on the Dark Side. He tells Ninnin he surrendered the precious results of their research to the "terrifying sovereign who was not of this world", and does not expect to escape judgment for it. The quests suggest this bargain kept the moon's other facilities from being destroyed.
### LARGE +
The laboratory therefore stands inside a base that, according to data the party found at the entrance, serves the will of the "Lord of Darkness who returned from the Sea of Stars". The Traveler identified that figure as the returning dragon king Nibelung; that base was built to turn the moon into a space-based weapon. Lusalim's rooms are tucked inside that later installation rather than being part of it.
### ULTRA +
The timeline is therefore layered: Lusalim's work comes first, the arrival of the Heavenly Principles breaks it, and Nibelung's weapon is built around it afterwards. Lusalim's final record is the only place where the project's last step (a way for life, information and memory to be inherited and to circulate) is spelled out as being entrusted to Ninnin and to the moon's primordial power of creation lent by the Moon Goddesses.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Cold, sterile and quiet. After the pursuit through searchlights, the laboratory is a hush of old terminals whose records are addressed to someone who never came; the contrast between the machine-hardness of Nibelung's base and the intimate, grateful tone of Lusalim's words is the point of the place.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Approaches to the laboratory are defended by security systems: patrolling machines with searchlight-like Transmission Modules, annihilation modules, a base-wide alarm and guard constructs. The laboratory itself is not shown to be hostile.

## travel
### STANDARD
Reached through the Dark Side of the Moon base: in the quest the party, led by Columbina, flew across the edge of the lunar sea to the base's gate and then worked inward through the front hall.

## quests
### STANDARD
Project Moongazer (Part 1), Moon Gazing chapter Act III (6.7): the party find the laboratory while searching for Columbina and read Lusalim's records. Project Moongazer (Part 2) continues down to the Moonheart Gap and the moon's core.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the laboratory is intact and unattended inside the Dark Side base on the Frost Moon; its records stand as Lusalim's last testament.
### STANDARD +
Project Moongazer was carried through to its conclusion in the quest by Ninnin's successor, with Columbina shielding the transmission. Nothing in the laboratory itself is shown to change afterwards.

## entry: Lusalim's Farewell Record
@keys: Lusalim's farewell, Records Left in the Terminal, three pieces of good fortune
@keys2: Lusalim, Ninnin, Nin-Kiggia, Project Moongazer
@spoiler: major
@version: 6.7
### STANDARD
In the record left in the left corridor, Lusalim looks back on three pieces of good fortune and then entrusts Project Moongazer, "now only one step away from completion", to Ninnin, whom he calls a true child of the stars. He says the missing step is a way for life, information and memory to be inherited and to circulate, and that the power of the moon's origin, granted by the Moon Goddesses, is enough to do it.
### LARGE +
He confesses that he will not live to see it finished because he sinned against dragonkind: he capitulated to the sovereign who was not of this world and surrendered the fruits of their research, and he does not intend to escape judgment. He adds that Ninnin knows why he chose this, because he had his own mission. His last wish is to journey into the sea of stars, shed a few tears there in tribute to his teachers, and set stars in a line saying "The dragons were here."

## entry: The Moongazer's Engraving
@keys: Moongazer's Engraving, Stellar Blackbody
@keys2: Lusalim, Moongazers, message
@spoiler: major
@version: 6.7
### LARGE
The engraving in the right corridor is Lusalim's translated digest of the message carried by the Stellar Blackbody, the alien lifeform with which Ungien fused. As quoted in the quest, it is an address to a friend in a distant world, hoping the voices of the Moongazers will at least drive away a little cold silence from a lonely universe and that the listener's civilisation will know it is not alone. Lusalim notes that unintelligible terms were rendered with the researchers' own concepts. The message is the content Project Moongazer was designed to keep transmitting.
