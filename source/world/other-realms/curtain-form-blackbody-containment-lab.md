## summary
### LITE
The Curtain-Form Blackbody Containment Lab is an ancient research site in the Moontide Sea on the Frost Moon. Here Professor Maagees's team learned that Moonshade Trees fused with "Blackbody Matter" and stimulated with Archaeolune Energy could throw off reflective waves that formed a curtain-like barrier. That was the first stage of Project Lunar Veil, a plan to hide the moon.
### STANDARD +
The site keeps terminal records describing four stages of the work: the discovery of Blackbody Matter, the curtain-form containment itself, Project Lunar Veil Phase I, and Phase II. An Ancient Moon's Remnant of the same name holds a Moonshade Tree.
### LARGE +
The records are written by someone close to the project who worries about the plan and later identifies as the person who named it. Treat the author as unnamed in this lab; other sites on the moon identify Lusalim as the Veil's designer.

## places
@version: 6.7
### STANDARD
Features found here include an Ancient Moon's Remnant with a Moonshade Tree, a terminal that plays the records, Archaeolune Energy machinery (barriers, an Ancient Moon Pearl system and a backup power generator), material storage, and a "Pioneer Source Structure". The elite machine "Precursor's Secret Source Matrix of Overseer Network" guards the area.

## history
@version: 6.7
### STANDARD
After the researcher Ungien fused with the Stellar Blackbody, his body began exuding a black filament-like substance; Maagees named it Blackbody Matter. The terminal author notes that the same substance coated the outer hull of the Moongazer and explains why the ship stayed hidden. Experiments showed that it reacts with Archaeolune Energy and can fuse with plants and animals, sometimes shifting their colour from blue to black.
### LARGE +
The author fears sending signals into deep space, since nothing proves that whoever receives them is friendly, and sets out to build a safeguard based on Blackbody Matter. The ecological team had grown thousands of Moonshade Trees; Maagees's team picked specimens highly compatible with the matter. Under enough Archaeolune stimulation they emitted reflective diffusive waves, and with greater intensity the waves formed a stable curtain with a radius beyond the whole laboratory. The author wanted to extend that range next.
### ULTRA +
Phase I: the researcher Nu-ur-Gestug proposed larger trials in the biosphere (Ungien's Circle); Maagees agreed. The author suggested the name Project Lunar Veil, a veil that could obscure the moon, though the phrase seemed to come from the child Ninnin, who could barely speak yet seemed to grasp the aim. A miniature Veil was cast over the biosphere. Phase II: Maagees's health kept declining after a traumatic incident, and the Eye of Ibni-Belum team carried out the signal transmission as her final wish. The author could not stop it, prayed to King Nibelung and the Moon Goddesses, and resolved to finish the full-scale Veil at once, though it needed far more energy.

## entry: Project Lunar Veil
@keys: Project Lunar Veil, Lunar Veil, Curtain-Form Blackbody Containment
@version: 6.7
### STANDARD
Project Lunar Veil was a safeguard for Project Moongazer: a veil of Blackbody-fused Moonshade Trees meant to obscure the moon from whatever might answer its signal. The lab here produced the first curtain; Ungien's Circle hosted the Phase I trial.

## entry: Blackbody Matter
@keys: Blackbody Matter
@version: 6.7
### STANDARD
Blackbody Matter is the black filamentous substance first secreted by Ungien after he merged with the Stellar Blackbody, and which also coats the Moongazer's hull. It reacts to Archaeolune Energy, bonds with plants and animals, and carries information in some specimens.

## status
### STANDARD
Ruined and ancient as of 7.1; it can be explored and read for lore but is not inhabited.
