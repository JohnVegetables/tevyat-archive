## summary
### LITE
Floating Abode is one of the three default Realm Layouts of the Serenitea Pot: a cluster of islands suspended in mid-air. The game calls this a typical feature of many adepti realms, a boundless world with nothing but a cluster of islands. It arrived in Version 1.5 with the Serenitea Pot.
### STANDARD +
The four isles are Windchill Isle, Cloudrest Isle, Smoke-Sailing Isle and Evermist Isle, linked in part by Adeptus Bridges. Its mansion is the Liyue Estate: Exquisite Mansion.
### LARGE +
The Serenitea Pot is itself an adeptal creation. The layout echoes adepti retreats such as Cloud Retainer's Abode as a style only; it is not a copy of any specific realm.

## geography
### STANDARD
Four round, grassy islands floating in open sky, with trees on each. Windchill Isle has a large tree on its east side; Evermist Isle is the highest of the four and gives a view of the other three.
### LARGE +
Outdoors there is no visible difference between day and night, so outdoor lighting furnishings do not light up; the Mansion interior does keep a day and night cycle.

## places
### STANDARD
Windchill Isle (Trust Rank 1): the starting isle, with an Adeptus Bridge to Cloudrest Isle on the west and a Sub-Space Waypoint in its northwest. Cloudrest Isle (Rank 5): joins Windchill to the east and Smoke-Sailing to the west. Smoke-Sailing Isle (Rank 7): joins Cloudrest to the north and Evermist to the south, with a rock pile and large tree in the northwest. Evermist Isle (Rank 9): the highest, linked to Smoke-Sailing by an Adeptus Bridge.
### ULTRA +
Each isle has the same 16,000 load cap as in the other layouts.

## travel
### STANDARD
Entered via the Serenitea Pot gadget; layouts are changed by talking to Tubby. Chubby the Teapot Traveling Salesman appears at an unlocked isle on Fridays to Sundays.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Calm, airy and a little otherworldly: a retreat held out of time, where the light outdoors never changes. It suits quiet talk, contemplation and tea.

## status
### STANDARD
As of Version 7.1 Floating Abode is unchanged since 1.5 and remains available by default or through Trust Rank 8 and 10 unlocks.
