## summary
### LITE
The Golden Hall is a small site in the Lunar Highlands of the Frost Moon. Its map marker reads like an invitation to board a silver ship and return to the great golden house, and its readable text, the Chanson of the Boatman, is a prayer written by a boatman who served a night goddess.
### STANDARD +
The viewpoint is called The Boatman's Song-House. An elite Tangled Vines enemy, Frostnight Scion, guards the area.

## places
@version: 6.7
### STANDARD
A subarea of the Lunar Highlands. A High-Speed Transit Lane leads there, apparently unlocked by an exploration objective called To Quell the Storm.

## culture
@version: 6.7
### STANDARD
The Chanson of the Boatman is a hymn from a man named Euthyphro of Arcadia, the eternal holy city. He prays to the ever-blessed goddess Prusmene, mother of the silver shuttle, to preserve him and bring him inspiration. He calls her ancient mother of the night, mistress of the Golden Hall, and says she guided his vessel many times as he carried unsullied souls to the lunar palace above the clouds, letting mortals sleep and dream dreams their own.
### LARGE +
Yet it is a final night: he fears the eternal throne in the heavens, the Primordial One, a world-shaking lord who forges the bones of the earth and means to cover human dreams with its own, so that mortals live without beginning or end in dreamless rest. He begs deliverance from his dread master, saying he ushered the souls and steered the silver ship for her sake, and asks that he not sink beneath the silver tree to be sullied by dust.
### ULTRA +
The text is a prayer rather than a record of events, so it should be read as one believer's account. It associates the Golden Hall with a goddess of night and with the passage of the dead, and its silver tree matches the Irminsul, but the entry does not explain what became of the boatman.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
The site's mood comes from its text: a night-goddess hymn, a silver ship and a promise of rest set against fear of a dreamless end.

## status

As of 7.1 the area is open. The wiki lists a Golden Hall quest category but names no quest in what was read.
