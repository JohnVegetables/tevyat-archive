## summary
### LITE
The twins' spaceship is the vessel in which the Traveler and their sibling crossed the sea of stars before ever reaching Teyvat. It is a star-shaped craft of white and gold with a blue cockpit dome, made of a material nothing in Teyvat could scratch. It once lay overgrown at Starsand Shoal in Nod-Krai; after the Cataclysm the Heavenly Principles removed it and hid it on the Frost Moon, where Columbina brought the Traveler to find it (Welkin Moon's Homecoming, 6.3).
### STANDARD +
The ship holds the Traveler's flight logs, a cabinet of old outfits, and a hibernation chamber that opens only with each twin's own key. It sits at the heart of Traveler's Crater and has been freely visitable since the Moon Gazing quests (6.7).

## appearance
@spoiler: none
### STANDARD
Shaped like a four-pointed star, with the front and rear points each split into two sharp prongs. The hull is mostly white with gold lines and a blue cockpit dome in the middle. Its look echoes Celestial architecture, in particular the Nails and Teleport Waypoints. The hull is described as impervious: when the ship lay in Nod-Krai, generations of locals tried to strip it for parts and could not dent it.
### LARGE +
While it lay at Starsand Shoal it became overgrown with plants. In the Frost Moon's low gravity it rests inside a vast crater, with Teyvat shining overhead.

## function
@spoiler: major
@version: 6.3
### STANDARD
Built for interstellar travel, it carries a genetic vault and a hibernation chamber reachable only with the two special keys, one belonging to each twin. A navigational AI-like guide handled maintenance, course and destinations while the twins slept. As a pioneer vessel it could terraform a planet: reshape its lithosphere, atmosphere and biosphere so a civilisation could take root. It also made protective outfits for the sleeping twins; the Traveler's As Heaven and Earth Are Made Anew outfit is one of them, found in a cabinet aboard.
### LARGE +
By the time the twins reached Teyvat they no longer had a clear memory of the guiding presence aboard. Item text (Wings of Descension) and the outfit description both mention a long journey, protective clothing and a speaker warning of danger, and the wiki reads them as the same entity; the games do not confirm this.

## history
@spoiler: major
@version: 6.7
### STANDARD
After the twins' home world was destroyed, they set out in the ship to look for a world with the potential to survive and rebuild civilisation, surveying countless planets across thousands of years. Along the way they received a goodwill signal sent from Teyvat by the dragons' Project Moongazer and replied that they would visit once their present affairs were settled. The flight log shows the ship passed Teyvat once, long before, without landing; the Traveler guesses the sibling may have left then. Aeons later Teyvat's gravity captured it, and it fell to Starsand Shoal in Nod-Krai.
### LARGE +
The fall was observed in Khaenri'ah by the Universitas Magistrorum, who reported predicted orbit and re-entry to King Irmin (per the Orbits namecard). The Traveler remembers feeling kuuvahki for the first time as they entered Teyvat. During the Cataclysm, five hundred years ago, the sibling appeared outside the ship and woke the Traveler; they tried to flee together but were beaten and separated by Asmoday. The Heavenly Principles then took the ship from Nod-Krai and hid it on the Frost Moon, then outside the false sky.
### ULTRA +
Wiki summaries of the Natlan quests (A Space and Time for You and A Space and Time Without You) say the sibling decided to abandon Khaenri'ah and go to the ship after a Khaenri'ahn, Direidyth, brought a key as proof of having met the Traveler. Asmoday and Istaroth are said to have arranged this to keep the Cataclysm's loop intact. The key came from either the Traveler of the future or the sibling, depending on the player's choice, but the Traveler still carries their own key and later finds the sibling's on a hilichurl near the Collective of Plenty, so canon appears to favour the sibling having given theirs. In the Nod-Krai Archon Quests the Traveler saw a kuuvahki afterimage of the ship and, with Lauma, Columbina and the Eternal Moon's Marrow, watched its landing and the sibling approaching it.

## places
@spoiler: minor
@version: 6.7
### LARGE
The ship stands in Traveler's Crater on the Frost Moon, in the Lunar Highlands. The High-Speed Transit Lane at the Nuur Stone Circle leads there, and the viewpoint at the crater is named The Next Journey.

## status
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
As of 7.1 the ship is on the Frost Moon and the Traveler knows where it is. In Welkin Moon's Homecoming (6.3) Columbina took the Traveler there; they read the flight logs, took a spare outfit from the cabinet, and left the ship in place. Since the Moon Gazing quests (6.7) it is permanently accessible. Why the Heavenly Principles hid it remains unexplained.

### STANDARD
The ship's hibernation chamber opens only with two special keys, one for each twin. The keys later matter in Natlan, where the sibling's choice of whether to seek the Traveler is tied to a key given to Direidyth; see the main entry for the wiki's reading and its player-choice variant.

## entry: Starsand Shoal Landing
@keys: Starsand Shoal landing, ship fell, spaceship crash
@keys2: spaceship, Nod-Krai
@version: 6.3
### STANDARD
The ship fell to Starsand Shoal in Nod-Krai long before the Cataclysm and stayed there long enough to be overgrown and to be seen by several generations of locals, none of whom could damage it. The Traveler first sensed kuuvahki as they entered Teyvat and, during Song of the Welkin Moon, glimpsed the landing again through the Eternal Moon's Marrow.
