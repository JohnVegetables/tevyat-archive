## summary
### LITE
The Wingalet is a very large ship that Wriothesley, administrator of the Fortress of Meropide, built in secret in the Forbidden Zone beneath the prison. He began it once he suspected the Primordial Sea lay just beyond the Fortress's sluice gate, meaning it as an escape vessel and something for Fontainians to hold on to. It surfaced during the Prophecy of Fontaine to rescue people from the flood, then went back into storage. In Version 6.7 it was leased out during the Fontinalia Film Festival and fitted with an aquarium.
### STANDARD +
Wriothesley modelled the project on the legendary Fortuna, the ship of Remuria's first king, though he did not expect it to match that legend.

## geography
### STANDARD
Built in a factory-scale production zone of the Fortress of Meropide, below the Duke's Office and near the isolation gates of the Forbidden Zone. Wriothesley shows it to the Traveler and Paimon as a gargantuan silhouette seen through glass in the distance. When it rises, its sides open and cranes extend with hooks to haul people from the water.
### LARGE +
The 6.7 version has spacious cabins, a long deck with a high balcony at the stern, and captain's quarters. A deck area is a marked point of interest on the map near the Court of Fontaine, and a waypoint on the ship unlocks with the story quest, even before the player can dive.

## history
### STANDARD
Some years before the Traveler reached the Fortress, Wriothesley began to suspect that the Primordial Sea lay under the sluice gate he had found. He launched the project in secret so that Fontainians would have a way to reach safety and something to cling to, drawing on a whole factory's labour, materials and technology, with technical advice from Jurieu and Lourvine of the Fontaine Research Institute. The Traveler learns of it in "Secret Keepers and Forbidden Zones" (Cataclysm's Quickening). Wriothesley warns that if the Fortress's workers learned the truth behind the ship, riots could destroy the Fortress faster than the catastrophe. When the Prophecy of Fontaine came to pass and the nation flooded, he commanded the Wingalet, which rose from the water to rescue the stranded. After the Prophecy was averted, it returned to the Fortress's factory and sat idle.
### LARGE +
Wriothesley's character story "The Wingalet" frames the project as a response to unsettling signs, a historian-inmate theory that the sea that swallowed Remuria could return, and his wish to prepare even if the effort proved futile; it calls the Wingalet a ship built to flee disaster and says an unbridgeable gulf lay between it and the golden Fortuna. The story ends by repeating the quoted line that oceans rise, empires fall and change alone endures.
### ULTRA +
The 6.7 story quest "Sunny Summer Fontinalia" is a limited-time event story. Wriothesley, unable to afford the ship's heavy maintenance, rented it out during the festival to raise Mora and to give the inmates who keep it running a visible reward. See the entries below.

## people
### STANDARD
Wriothesley commands and controls it. Jurieu and Lourvine, former Fontaine Research Institute researchers working for him as technical consultants, advised on the design. Inmates of the Fortress clean and maintain it. In 6.7 Sandrone leases it under the surname Guillotin and is called its current captain.
### LARGE +
Citlali is shown at the aquarium's front desk in the 6.7 story, the person Sandrone hired. Furina, Columbina and the Traveler's other companions pass through during the film shoots.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the Wingalet belongs to the Fortress of Meropide and, since 6.7, doubles as an aquarium that Wriothesley agreed to keep running in part.
### STANDARD +
At the end of "Those We Have Kept" Wriothesley asks Sandrone, the current captain, whether some installations may stay so Fortress inmates can enjoy the aquarium, and she agrees. No later use of the ship is shown in canon through 7.1.

## entry: The Wingalet Project
@keys: Wingalet, Wingalet project, the ship Wriothesley built
@version: 4.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The Wingalet was built beneath the Fortress of Meropide as insurance against the Primordial Sea breaking through the sluice gate of the Forbidden Zone. Wriothesley hid it from the Fortress's own population so that panic would not spread. He admits it is no match for the legendary Fortuna, the ship on which King Remus founded Remuria with the seer Sybilla, but it gave Fontaine something to hold on to. During the Prophecy of Fontaine it was used for rescue work under his command.
### LARGE +
Jurieu, once assistant to Edwin Eastinghouse and versed in Arkhium and gravimeters, and Lourvine, his former assistant, helped design it. The same quest where the Traveler is shown the ship also reveals the Fortress's history and the rising Primordial Seawater around it.

## entry: Sunny Summer Fontinalia and the Wingalet Fisharium
@keys: Wingalet Fisharium, Gloop-Gloop Funderwater Fisharium, Fontinalia Film Festival, Sunny Summer Fontinalia
@keys2: Wingalet, Sandrone
@version: 6.7
@availability: limited
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
During the limited Sunny Summer Fontinalia event (Version 6.7), Wriothesley rents the Wingalet out for the Fontinalia Film Festival to cover its upkeep and boost morale among the inmates who maintain it. The renter is a deep-pocketed woman with a fishing permit and business licence who sets up an aquarium; she turns out to be Sandrone, who uses the cover in the hope of finding the underwater home she shared with Alain Guillotin. Xavier and Lepine-Pauline's two film crews share the ship as a location. In the event the player runs the aquarium by collecting creatures and curios, and event rewards include a banner and a rules sign from the Fisharium.
### LARGE +
Sandrone applied for the fishing permit under the name Guillotin, then keeps the aquarium open only to look legitimate if the Gestionnaires check. At the end of the story Wriothesley, thankful for the ship's good condition, asks that some installations stay so the Fortress's inmates can enjoy the aquarium.
