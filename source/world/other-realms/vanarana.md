## summary
### LITE
Vanarana is the secret home of the Aranara, the small plant-like forest spirits of Sumeru's Dharma Forest. It exists twice: as a quiet clearing in the real forest, and as Mahavanaranapna ("Vanarana of Great Dreams"), a dream version where the Aranara actually live, with houses, a stage, a stall and gardens. Adults normally never find it; children and trusted friends can.
### STANDARD +
The two versions are linked by the Tree of Dreams, the elder Araja, who anchors the dream to the real world. A visitor switches between them by playing the Aranara's "Rhythm of the Great Dream" on a lyre beside a Silapna by the central pond. The village is warm, a little childlike and sentimental, and built around memories, songs and shared meals.
### LARGE +
Vanarana is also the setting of most of the Aranyaka quest series (3.0): the Festival Utsava, the gathering of flowers from every Aranara, and the preparations for the Bija that wakes the sleeping girl Rana. Behind the village's calm lies the history of the Cataclysm, which drove the Aranara from Old Vanarana, and the lingering threat of Marana, the Withering.

## geography
### STANDARD
Vanarana lies in the Dharma Forest of Sumeru, in the rainforest north of the desert. The wiki treats an "area" (the surroundings, including a southern stretch not touched by the dream) and a "village" proper, centred on a pond. A small garden cave at the west end grows Viparyas. In the dream version the lighting is dreamlike, a Statue of The Seven stands there, and the Tree of Dreams glows violet instead of green.
### LARGE +
The Statue of The Seven and the cave holding the Tree of Dreams are reachable only after the opening quest, The World of Aranara; before that, the way is blocked by rock. Local plants differ between versions: Viparyas grow in the dream, while Padisarah and Nilotpala Lotus grow in the real clearing. Near the central lake the music swells and a voice joins in.

## places
### STANDARD
Notable spots: the central pond; the Silapna east of it that toggles dream and reality; Araja's house; a festival stage; the stall of Aramani, the Aranara vendor; the nursery garden at the west end; and the Tree of Dreams in its cave.
### LARGE +
Arama's house holds a messily written note that grows as Aranyaka advances (the writer notes new events, then Festival Utsava, then the plan to read the Aranyaka stories). The Aranara who were helped elsewhere in Sumeru (the confused, material-collecting, music-loving and reckless ones, though not the mischievous ones) later turn up in the dream village.

## people
### STANDARD
The residents are Aranara, each with a name beginning "Ara-" and each with a personal tune. Araja is the elder and village chief; Arama is the first villager to befriend the Traveler and sets them tasks to win the others' trust; Arana is a gentle Aranara tied to Rana in Vimara Village. Others include Arakavi, Arapacati, Aramani, Arakara, Aranakula, Arapandu, Aranakin and Aragaru. In total more than two dozen are named in the village.
### LARGE +
Distinct small groups exist: song gatherers (such as Arakavi and Aradasa) who collect the tunes of other Aranara; a band that repaired the Varuna Contraption (Arapandu, Aranakula, Arabalika, Arakunti); and cooks in training around Arapacati. Aramuhukunda, the first Aranara "born", appears at the festival and later in Old Vanarana.

## governance
### LARGE
There is no formal government. Araja serves as chief and elder, and the Aranara decide most things together. They speak of humans as "Nara" and use "Nara" before the Traveler's name as a form of address.

## culture
### LARGE
Aranara cherish memories, songs and gifts of flowers (a flower given to a friend helps them be remembered). They copy human customs with no practical use: cooking although they do not eat and fear fire, houses although they do not sleep, a market although they do not trade, and the idea of siblings although they do not reproduce. Festival Utsava is their big gathering.
### ULTRA +
Their writing is the Aranara language (see its own entry). The Aranyaka book, once belonging to the earlier human friend Nara Varuna, records their stories with humans, so a person who forgets how to dream can still read of the Aranara.

## history
### STANDARD
Greater Lord Rukkhadevata created the Aranara after she left the desert to make the Dharma Forest. They first lived in Old Vanarana and befriended humans. When the Cataclysm struck they fought the invaders from the Abyss, then withdrew, set up a new Vanarana and hid it within a dream anchored by the Tree of Dreams.
### LARGE +
Not long after the Cataclysm, Nara Varuna (named by the wiki as the Traveler's Sibling) befriended the Aranara and helped them repair the Varuna Contraption; the Aranara keep murals and monuments of this friend. After Rukkhadevata's disappearance, they remembered her as "Queen Aranyani". When Nahida (the Lesser Lord Kusanali) edited the Irminsul during the Archon Quest, the Aranara forgot Rukkhadevata in that role and now think of themselves as children of the current Dendro Archon, whom they call the Lord of Verdure.
### ULTRA +
In Aranyaka (3.0), the Traveler and Paimon reach Vanarana through the quest The World of Aranara, are tested by Arama, and gain the village's trust. Festival Utsava follows, ending with a garland from all the collected flowers. Afterwards, the Traveler and Arama take Vasoma Fruits to the weakened Fane of Ashvattha and then enter Old Vanarana to destroy Marana's Avatar; Arama becomes the new Ashvattha Tree. In the final part, Arana gives up her memory to form the Bija, which wakes Rana, who receives a Dendro Vision.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Soft, dreamy and homely. Light is gentle, music drifts from the pond, and small plant-like beings fuss over cooking, flowers and festival preparations. Visitors are treated as honoured friends but the Aranara are easily frightened by humans, fire and salt. Beneath the cheer is a faint sense of what has been lost.

## dangers
### LARGE
The dream village itself is safe. The perils near Vanarana are Vanagni (the Aranara name for Whopperflowers, which Arama says answer only with rage and flames) and, in the past, the Withering of Marana, which still marks Old Vanarana until Aranyaka Part III. Fire and salt are feared by the Aranara.
### ULTRA +
Marana is, in Araja's telling, the name of death: it urges everything toward death and chooses "avatars", tall dark warped beings. Araja says these are stories and not his own memories.

## travel
### STANDARD
Humans cannot normally find the way. In the world's story, a visitor plays the five-note Rhythm of the Great Dream on a lyre in front of the strange arch to enter, and again at the Silapna to move between real and dream Vanarana. The Traveler borrowed a Mondstadt-style lyre from Amadhiah in Vimara Village.
### LARGE +
Only children, and a few adults whom the Aranara trust (such as Rana and, in the wiki's note, Yoimiya), can see the Aranara.

## quests
### LARGE
The key quest is Aranyaka (World Quest series, 3.0), especially Part II "Dream Nursery" (The World of Aranara, Children of the Forest, Encounter in the Woods, An Unwavering Culinary Dream, Varuna Gatha, For a Better Reunion, Festival Utsava). Parts III and IV concern Old Vanarana and Rana's awakening. Smaller quests include Trees and Dreams (unlocks the Tree of Dreams) and The Viparyas of Vanarana.
### ULTRA +
No Archon Quest is required for Aranyaka. In gameplay, offering Dendro Sigils to the Tree of Dreams increases Vanarana's Favor and unlocks rewards.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 Vanarana is at peace, with the Aranara safe in the dream village and the threat from Marana's Avatar ended.
### STANDARD +
Old Vanarana began to recover after Marana's Avatar was destroyed; Arama is now an Ashvattha Tree. Arana and Rana have set off on a journey together.
### LARGE +
No later events (6.x, 7.0-7.1) were found that change Vanarana.

## entry: Tree of Dreams and Araja
@keys: Tree of Dreams, Araja, Mahavanaranapna, Vanarana's Favor
@version: 3.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The Tree of Dreams grows in a cave at the heart of Vanarana and seems sentient. Per the wiki, Araja turned his original body into the tree after the Cataclysm so he could sustain a dream large enough for the other Aranara. His body cannot move, but his mind can manifest a body inside the dream. At Festival Utsava he reveals that he cannot leave the dream because he is the Tree of Dreams. Over about 500 years he has lost many memories.
### LARGE +
Araja's friends in the past were Araji, Arapacati and Aramuhukunda, and he took part in the last Festival Utsava before the dream. In play, the tree is where Dendro Sigils are offered (Vanarana's Favor, gameplay). Arama also mentions "the very first Ashvattha's Tree of Dreams" rooted in Sarva; whether it is the same tree is unclear.

## entry: Festival Utsava
@keys: Festival Utsava, Utsava
@version: 3.0
### STANDARD
The Aranara's festival, held in the dream Vanarana during Aranyaka. The Traveler and Paimon collect a flower from each Aranara as a token of friendship, everyone plays the Rhythm of the Great Dream on the stage, and Arama weaves the flowers into a garland for the Traveler. It is a permanent quest and not a limited-time event.

## entry: Old Vanarana and Marana's Avatar
@keys: Old Vanarana, Marana's Avatar, Door of Vana, Land of Grounded Dreams
@version: 3.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Old Vanarana was the Aranara's home for hundreds of years before the Cataclysm. Afterwards they sealed Marana's Avatar deep inside with four layers of seals, including three Inscriptions of Remembrance by Aramaha, Arayama and Araji, who became Vasara Trees. Corruption still leaked, until the Traveler and Arama broke the seals, entered the Land of Grounded Dreams and destroyed the Avatar in Aranyaka Part III. Old Vanarana then began to heal.

## entry: Vanarana Nursery
@keys: Vanarana Nursery, Viparyas, Vasmrti
@version: 3.0
### STANDARD
A small garden in a cave at the west end, tended by Aranakula and Arakara, who grow "happy Viparyas". The name is unofficial wiki usage. After the quest The Viparyas of Vanarana the 13 Viparyas can be harvested; in the real clearing the plants are Sweet Flowers instead.
