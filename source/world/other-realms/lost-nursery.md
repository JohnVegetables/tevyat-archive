## summary
### LITE
The Lost Nursery is a small area in Sumeru's Dharma Forest that contains Old Vanarana, the Aranara's home for hundreds of years before the Cataclysm. It is unlocked in the Aranyaka world quest series, Part III ("Nursery of Lost Dreams"), when the Traveler, Paimon and the Aranara Arama enter to destroy Marana's Avatar. Before that the area is choked by the Withering.
### STANDARD +
Old Vanarana is where the Aranara fought the invaders of the Cataclysm and afterwards sealed away a piece of Marana, the force of death. The seals weakened with time, so the dying Fane of Ashvattha, the Aranara's tree-shrine, could no longer bear Bija, the fruit. Reaching the avatar in the heart of Old Vanarana, defeating it, and planting a new Ashvattha there is the entire story of the area.
### LARGE +
The ending costs the Aranara dearly: Arama becomes the new Ashvattha Tree, and Arana gives away its memories to form the Bija, so afterwards Arana does not remember the Traveler.

## geography
### STANDARD
The Lost Nursery lies in the Dharma Forest and, as of 3.0, was the smallest area in the game. Old Vanarana is its subarea; below it lie the underground chambers where the seals were kept. Before the quest "For Fruits, Seeds, and Trees" is finished, stepping into the area is treated as out of bounds, and Paimon pulls the Traveler back.
### LARGE +
Notable spots are Sand-Embraced Home (reached by going down from the north teleport waypoint of Old Vanarana) and the Land of Grounded Dreams at the bottom of the tunnel, where Marana's Avatar lies. A viewpoint in the Archive for Old Vanarana is hidden until a later Aranyaka quest is complete.

## places
### STANDARD
Old Vanarana is the main settlement ruin; the tunnel beneath it was drained of sand by Arama; Sand-Embraced Home is the last chamber, guarded by Aramuhukunda himself; the Land of Grounded Dreams is where Marana's Avatar is destroyed and where Arama is later planted.
### LARGE +
According to the wiki's lore page, seals included an entrance called the Door of Vana linking the Fane of Ashvattha to Old Vanarana; three Inscriptions of Remembrance made by Aramaha, Arayama and Araji, who later became Vasara Trees; several Ararakalari (Aranara mystic arts) such as Healthy Dendrograna, Claustroflora and Stone Pillar Seals; and the final one at Sand-Embraced Home.

## people
### STANDARD
Arama, the Aranara who befriended the Traveler in Vanarana, leads the descent and eventually becomes the new Ashvattha Tree. Aramuhukunda, the first Aranara "born" and the one the Traveler met at the festival, guards the final seal and opens it when he sees the quest is to destroy Marana's Avatar. Arana appears with Arama at the end.
### LARGE +
Aramaha, Arayama, Araji and Aradasha are named as creators of earlier seals. Marana's Avatar itself is a quest-exclusive boss; the Aranara regard Marana as the voice that says all good things must vanish.

## history
### STANDARD
The Aranara lived in Old Vanarana, created by the Greater Lord Rukkhadevata, for hundreds of years before the Cataclysm. When invaders from the Abyss came, they fought them there, and ruin machines still litter the old tunnels. Afterwards the Aranara moved to a new Vanarana, hid it in a dream, and sealed Marana's Avatar deep under Old Vanarana with four layers of seals.
### LARGE +
Over time Marana's corrosion leaked out regardless, producing the Withering that gradually weakened the Fane of Ashvattha and made it unable to bear Bija. In Aranyaka Part III the Traveler and Arama broke the seals and reached the Land of Grounded Dreams.
### ULTRA +
In "For Fruits, Seeds, and Trees," Arama guides the Traveler and Paimon to the Fane of Ashvattha, which is weakened, and they enter its dream and remove a Withering Zone; after they give it Vasoma Fruits the Fane tells Arama to make the ultimate sacrifice. In "For the Children of the Past" Arama shields the party from the Withering, they break seals by finding three Inscriptions of Remembrance and burning seven Stone Pillar Seals, then Arama drains the sand from a huge tunnel; a Ruin Grader attacks at the bottom and Arama binds it. In "For All Children Who Long for Life" Marana's Avatar moves its soul into Withering Zones three times; each is destroyed, and the core is destroyed for good. Arama then dives into the ground to become a sapling, and Arana, who is giving up its memories, asks the Traveler who they are once the Bija forms. The rhythm of the Great Dream is played again to make the Bija grow.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Before it is cleared, the nursery is a withered, silent, dangerous hollow; afterwards the old Vanarana begins to rejuvenate, slowly and quietly, with a young tree and an echo of a village that once rang with Aranara songs.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Before Part III the Withering here is among the strongest in the game (tied with Mawtiyima, starting at four bars of its gauge). Enemies in Old Vanarana include hilichurls, shooters and berserkers, slimes, Stretchy Pyro Fungus, Rockfond and Thundercraven Rifthounds, Blazing Axe Mitachurl and Ruin Guards, plus Ruin Machines left from the Cataclysm.

## travel
### STANDARD
The route in is through the Dharma Forest; before the quest series the area is blocked by the game's out-of-bounds rule. The Aranara protect travelers with a shield during the descent.

## quests
### STANDARD
Aranyaka Part III, "Nursery of Lost Dreams": "For Fruits, Seeds, and Trees," "For the Children of the Past," and "For All Children Who Long for Life" (3.0). The wiki shows a later Archive viewpoint for Old Vanarana that appears only after a later Aranyaka quest.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 Marana's Avatar is gone, Old Vanarana is slowly renewing, and Arama grows as the new Ashvattha Tree, though traces of the Withering remain.
### STANDARD +
Arana lost memories of the Traveler and of Rana and the children of Vimara Village in order to make the Bija.

## entry: Marana's Avatar
@keys: Marana's Avatar
@version: 3.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Marana's Avatar is the physical form of Marana (death, in the Aranara's telling) that the Aranara imprisoned in Old Vanarana after the Cataclysm. It attacks with Withering waves and lasers and moves its soul into Withering Zones; the Traveler and Arama destroy those zones and then the core. Its destruction means the Withering cannot form significantly again, though traces remain.

## entry: Sand-Embraced Home
@keys: Sand-Embraced Home, Aramuhukunda
@version: 3.0
### STANDARD
Sand-Embraced Home is the deepest chamber of Old Vanarana, where Aramuhukunda guarded the final seal. He opens it for the Traveler and Arama so they can face Marana's Avatar.
