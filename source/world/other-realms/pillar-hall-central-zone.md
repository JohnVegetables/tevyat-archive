## summary
### LITE
The Pillar Hall Central Zone is the centre of the Temple of Space, the floating hall above Windrest Peak. It houses the Memory Core, a storage device holding information about unknown civilisations. The hall is lined with display boards presenting relics of many eras, and it is the safest place in the temple: suppression-type guards cannot enter it. The philosopher Istoria and the shopkeeper Josephus keep to it, and it is the hub from which the three themed exhibition areas are reached.
### STANDARD +
Its loading-screen title is "Hall of a Thousand Pillars". The Memory Core's description says countless replicas of civilisations drift here, suspended in the windless instant before their extinction.

## geography
### STANDARD
A cold, columned hall with a rotating platform at its heart. Dex-assisted puzzles use malleable platforms, path bridging (vertical and horizontal) and spatial dragging. From it the party reaches the Luyang Academy, Mahavaipulya Chamber and Desert Pavilion. The Memory Core stands at its centre.
### LARGE +
Elite enemies in the zone are Domain Keepers and the Ruin Grader. Display cases hold relics, and the nearby Path of the Forgotten World and the Apathic Interval are separate parts of the temple.

## people
### STANDARD
Istoria, a philosopher of the unified civilisation, gathers the party's allies here. Josephus, called the Master of Anti-Self-Termination, is a friend of Istoria's and runs the souvenir shop Emberic Reconstruction, open after The Wind Has Ceased. The wiki also lists Amoxcihuatl, Cirsteen and Soga Sumidanoryu Sakon Shigehira here, without detail.
### LARGE +
Josephus says everyone in the temple is everyone else's friend, since otherwise one would drown the world in sorrow, and that no one dies in the Temple of Space. Istoria notes the Organization-type Keepers do not harm people unless they try to steal relics.

## culture
### STANDARD
A cooperative and dryly funny society of long-lived scholars and artists. They treat mortality as a joke and philosophy as daily conversation.

## history
### STANDARD
Once the sacred hall of the God of Space, who is nowhere to be found. After Asmoday vanished, her Watchers Nihilita and Melanta remained; Nihilita departed, and Melanta grew severe. When the laws began to fail, Istoria left the safety of the centre to rescue friends, and met the Traveler there during The Wind Has Ceased in 6.5. The party freed the three exhibition areas and then reached the Cage of Suchness; after Melanta's fall Istoria is found here.
### LARGE +
The temple selects people and objects from a vanished age as "segments" and keeps them as living memories. Istoria thinks it was not designed as an eternal paradise. Early on, the God of Space's sisters occasionally brought interesting things in and allowed small pardons for change; Melanta reversed this. Zeitlind's lost belongings, classed as relics, were scattered through the temple's areas.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Domain Keepers, Patrol Type Keepers (which send people to the Apathic Interval) and the Ruin Grader. The centre itself is protected from Suppression Type Keepers.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Echoing and cold, with towering pillars and silent exhibits, broken by Istoria's wry quips and Josephus's grumbling.

## status
### LITE
As of 6.5 (unchanged through 7.1) the hall is a safe hub for the freed residents and for visitors.
### STANDARD +
The Memory Core remains an offering system for Memory Factors; offerings raise its level and give rewards, and surplus factors can be traded to Josephus once it is maxed.

## entry: Memory Core
@keys: Memory Core, Memory Factor, Memory Factors, Emberic Reconstruction
@keys2: Temple of Space, Pillar Hall
@version: 6.5
### STANDARD
The Memory Core is a storage device at the Temple of Space's heart holding information about unknown civilisations. Memory Factors found in chests or floating clusters are offered to it for rewards; Josephus's shop trades excess factors for items or Mora once the core reaches its maximum. Its lore text says the Pillar Hall stands in silent vigil, no less taciturn than its master.

## entry: Pillar Hall Display Boards
@keys: Display Board, Arcadia, the Entombed Capital, From Swords to Plowshares, Musica Instrumentalis, Three Strategies Scroll
@keys2: Pillar Hall, Temple of Space
@version: 6.5
### LARGE
Exhibits include "A Corner of Valivija", a hymn to Nabu Malikata; "Arcadia, the Entombed Capital", a verse about the Knight of the Golden Bough, with Istoria remarking that Arcadian doors seem to open only from within; "From Swords to Plowshares", a Khaenri'ah-made Field Tiller displayed as a farm machine; "Lord Trubetskoy's Treasure", a corner of the Nod-Krai governor's vault now overrun by weasels; "Musica Instrumentalis", a conversation among Calcerius and others about palaces razed; "The First Scroll", about the Masters of the Night-Wind; "Three Strategies Scroll", a tengu sword-training formation; and "The Very First Print of The Steambird". "Deathly Silence" appears after The Starry Night.
### ULTRA +
Some boards are puzzling: no one can say why a plain "Holy Sword" is exhibited, a point the Remurian and Fontainian residents raise; the Three Strategies Scroll carries a name, Teruyo, that Okikuni does not recognise as a tengu.

## entry: Istoria and Josephus
@keys: Istoria, Josephus, Master of Anti-Self-Termination
@keys2: Pillar Hall, Temple of Space, Memory Core
@version: 6.5
### LARGE
Istoria is a tall philosopher of the war of funerary flame era with a layered white and dark blue toga; Josephus is his friend, dressed similarly with a laurel wreath, and the pair banter. Istoria called Raumhilda "Hilda" and she called him "Grandpa Istoria". Josephus says nobody dies in the Temple of Space.
