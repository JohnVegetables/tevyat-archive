## summary
### LITE
The Chamber of Night's Trial is a hidden arena in Natlan, in the Night Kingdom, resembling the Stadium of the Sacred Flame. It is entered by a visitor holding a Night Jade. A Nightsoul Spirit there invites the visitor to quiet the restless Nightsouls through battle.
### STANDARD +
Its in-game viewpoint is titled "Trial Grounds of Restive Nightsouls" and declares that only the blaze of battle can still these unquiet souls to slumber.

## geography
### STANDARD
In the Basin of Unnumbered Flames, the entrance lies to the south of the Stadium of the Sacred Flame. The chamber is a self-contained arena echoing the Stadium's layout rather than an open region.
### LARGE +
Natlan legend, as the Night Jade's item text says, holds that offering the jade at a specific altar may open the path to the kingdom ruled by the night. The jade is a nearly weightless black stone wrapped in chilling flames.

## people
### STANDARD
The only inhabitant shown is the Nightsoul Spirit, a quest-only NPC who speaks in archaic, courtly phrasing. It says the Nightsouls here are like dreams of its mistress, the sleeping goddess of the night (Yohualtecuhtin, Lord of the Night), and that when she wakes now and then they are single strains of her mind.
### LARGE +
The spirit laments that no one living under the sun remembers the Nightsouls' names or deeds, and says it has been long since anyone found this entrance. It addresses the visitor as a beloved outlander warrior and presents the fight as a way to calm souls overcome by battle-craving.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
A quiet, ceremonial and slightly melancholy arena: forgotten warriors, an archaic-speaking host, and a sense of old battles that were never settled. The tone is respectful of the dead rather than menacing.

## dangers
### STANDARD
The trial pits the visitor against eight Nightsoul warriors. Players have sixty seconds to defeat them all; success opens a set of reward chests and earns the achievement "By Battle Be Settled...". The challenge can be retried after failing (gameplay detail).
### ULTRA +
The Nightsoul Spirit reacts to the visitor's choice in character: it is amused by a bold acceptance, regretful but patient if asked to wait, and encourages another attempt after a defeat. After a win it hands over the rewards. The related achievement is hidden until completed.

## travel
### STANDARD
Access requires the Night Jade, a quest item formed automatically once three Shattered Night Jades are gathered. The shards come from the world quests "Revelations from the Past" and "To the Night, What is the Night's", and from a chest at the Phlogiston Extraction Research Center (a hidden exploration reward).

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the chamber is still accessible as a permanent hidden challenge; no 6.x or 7.0-7.1 source found mentions it.
