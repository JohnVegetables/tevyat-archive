## summary
### LITE
Shadowflit Archipelago is the Nod-Krai-themed Realm Layout of the Serenitea Pot, added in Version Luna V (6.4, released 2026-02-25). The game describes a complicated group of islands hovering high in the sky, drawn together by some mysterious mechanism.
### STANDARD +
It is unlocked with the Sphere of Fleeting Shadow, bought from Tubby at the Realm Depot. The sphere is a Nod-Krai kuuvahki mechanism that plays music and produces dazzling light, a souvenir Chubby brought from Nod-Krai. The mansion is the Kerplunk-a-tron Windmill Base, and the four areas are Vassen Isle, Troki Isle, Ettu Isle and Vietje Isle.
### LARGE +
The item text says a glimpse of the sphere's shifting lights brings up faint images that Sub-Space Creation can recreate as Nod-Krai landscapes. The Realm Layout list gives A Dance of Snowy Tides and Hoarfrost Groves as the requirement, though the layout's own page leaves it blank.

## geography
### STANDARD
Islands hovering high in the sky and held together by an unexplained mechanism. The wiki has no further area descriptions.
### LARGE +
Each of the four areas has the standard 16,000 load cap.

## places
### STANDARD
Vassen Isle (Trust Rank 1), Troki Isle (Rank 5), Ettu Isle (Rank 7) and Vietje Isle (Rank 9). The wiki has not yet described them.
### ULTRA +
Only the names, rank order and load are recorded; do not invent features for the individual isles.

## travel
### STANDARD
Entered through the Serenitea Pot gadget after the sphere is used and then chosen with Tubby. Chubby, the sphere's supplier, appears at an unlocked area on Fridays to Sundays.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Airy, gleaming and faintly mechanical, with soft shifting light. It suits scenes of wonder and curiosity about Nod-Krai's kuuvahki, in a place with no Fatui or Abyss threat.

## status
### STANDARD
As of Version 7.1 it is the most recently added Realm Layout and unchanged since Luna V.
