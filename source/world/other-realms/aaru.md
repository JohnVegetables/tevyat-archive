## summary
### LITE
Aaru is the unfinished afterlife King Deshret designed for the people of the desert, a "land of dreams" built from celestial technology. For thousands of years it held three of his Seven Pillars (Thoth, Sobek and Heryshaf). Since the destruction of Irminsul in 6.6 it has been reconfigured to store and collect the Ley Lines' information in Irminsul's place. It is not a place a traveler walks into; the entrance is the top of the Mausoleum of King Deshret, Aaru's Shut.
### STANDARD +
Deshret's aim, as the wiki summarises it, was a new kind of afterlife that would free humans from Teyvat's gods and dangers. It was never completed. The three Pillars sealed inside could leave only by having humans answer three questions to Deshret's satisfaction; that was finally achieved in the Archon Quest, after which Aaru was rebuilt for a very different job. Do not confuse Aaru with Aaru Village, the human settlement built later in the rainforest edge of the desert.
### LARGE +
Because Aaru works on a principle close to the Akasha System, Kaveh and Alhaitham could use its mainframe, with the Seven Pillars' help and a chessboard-trial override, as the heart of the plan to overload and shut down Irminsul while Dottore's experiment was growing inside it.

## geography
### STANDARD
Aaru is not a place on the map. It is a constructed realm reached through the Mausoleum of King Deshret, whose highest point is called Aaru's Shut. A cyclone that guarded the mausoleum top was permanently switched off in the world quest Dual Evidence (3.1), and Aaru's Shut can be entered afterwards through the Great Hall of Truths. A map description of it says it should have been paradise on earth, the crown jewel of the desert, but now looks very different.
### LARGE +
Souls can enter without a body; the wiki says the Golden Slumber quest shows Samail and Jebrael doing so as disembodied souls. The spirits of the sealed Pillars could leave to a limited degree, borrowing the bodies of nearby animals such as cats, but their own bodies could not be freed. The mausoleum scenes of the 6.6 Archon Quest show Aaru's mainframe as a structure with obelisks carrying inscriptions that Kaveh and Alhaitham read with Thoth's help.

## people
### STANDARD
Thoth, Sobek (the Crocodile King) and Heryshaf (the Goat King), three of King Deshret's Seven Pillars, were sealed in Aaru by Deshret. Thoth says only he can hear the other two, since all three are inside. Bennu, another Pillar, handed Aaru's mainframe to Kaveh with a warning to use it with great caution. The earlier human visitors who answered Thoth's questions were Goudarz, Makhaira and Nefer.
### LARGE +
Thoth passes the time with chess and, as he tells it, often took the form of a cat to talk to people. He says Sobek and Heryshaf played badly, with Heryshaf giving back-seat advice and Sobek taking it. Apep, the Dendro Dragon Sovereign that Deshret counted as a Pillar although she herself rejected it, appears in the 6.6 events, ferrying Nahida to the mausoleum.

## history
### STANDARD
King Deshret created Aaru with celestial technology that the angel Nabu Malikata gave him against Celestia's rules; she was reportedly executed by the Heavenly Principles for this. Because the project was extremely complex, Aaru was never completed. Deshret later locked Thoth, Sobek and Heryshaf inside, and set Thoth three questions (supplied, Thoth says, by the Mistress of Flowers): what causes our rise and fall, what obliterates love and hate, what determines truth and falsehood.
### LARGE +
Thoth chose three humans over the ages and granted each a gift in exchange for their answer. Goudarz said conspiracy and betrayal and received the power to deceive. Makhaira said vengeance alone can quench both love and hate and received the power to be loved. A third person, whom Thoth refused to name in Headlong Into the Sands, answered falsely, since he was not freed. In 6.6 Nefer gave the answer Deshret intended, "time" for all three, and the Pillars were released to the Temple of Silence.
### ULTRA +
In the Song of the Welkin Moon chapter's tenth act (6.6, "Truth Amongst the Pages of Purana"), the allies decided to overload Irminsul with the Akasha System to force it to shut down, using Aaru as the shared base because Irminsul itself was compromised by Dottore's experiment. Thoth revealed the purpose of Aaru, and Kaveh and Alhaitham mapped the mainframe. When the assault on Irminsul came, the Traveler and Wanderer fought their way in, the world's information was extracted from Irminsul and redirected to Aaru, and Nahida burned the corrupted tree with the Pyro Gnosis that Mauvika had entrusted to her. The Pyro Gnosis burned for seven days and nights. Afterwards Nahida reported that the reconfigured Aaru had safely taken over collecting and storing information, and that Nicole's reliance on Irminsul had been transplanted to Aaru so she suffered no ill effects.

## travel
### LARGE
Players reach Aaru's Shut at the top of the Mausoleum after Dual Evidence. Aaru's interior can only be entered as a soul.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Still, cold, scholarly and faintly sad: a hall of obelisks and a chessboard where three ancient minds have waited for millennia, their voices audible to one another and to a few visitors, with the sense of a promised paradise left half-built.

## quests
### STANDARD
Quests that touch Aaru: Golden Slumber (3.0) and Dual Evidence (3.1) in the Great Red Sand; Headlong Into the Sands, where Thoth tells the story of the three questions; and the Song of the Welkin Moon Archon Quest act "Truth Amongst the Pages of Purana" (6.6).

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 Aaru is the active repository of the Ley Lines' information, replacing Irminsul. The sealed Pillars are free and living at the Temple of Silence.
### STANDARD +
The Heavenly Principles no longer hold direct control over fate through Irminsul, as the wiki reads the 6.6 outcome. Whether Thoth, Sobek and Heryshaf will ever leave the temple is not stated; Thoth and Heryshaf choose to stay for now, and they decline to move their statues into the temple out of respect for Hermanubis's memory.

## entry: Aaru Village
@keys: Aaru Village
@version: 3.0
### STANDARD
Aaru Village is a different place from Aaru: the largest human settlement in the Sumeru desert, home to the followers and descendants of King Deshret and to scholars exiled from the Akademiya. It is built around an old fortress from the Gurabad period, and the wiki notes its name was chosen from the "ancient dream of the gods," the settlers swearing off kings and preserving the embers of their civilisation. Use Aaru only for the afterlife system.

## entry: Aaru's Shut
@keys: Aaru's Shut
@version: 3.1
### STANDARD
Aaru's Shut is the highest point of the Mausoleum of King Deshret and the way into Aaru. After Dual Evidence it is reachable through the Great Hall of Truths. The wiki notes that "shut" derives from an ancient Egyptian word for shadow.

## entry: Thoth's Three Questions
@keys: Thoth's three questions, three questions of Deshret
@keys2: Thoth, Aaru, Goudarz, Makhaira, Nefer
@version: 6.6
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Thoth, Sobek and Heryshaf could be released from Aaru only if humans gave the answer King Deshret intended to three questions: what causes our rise and fall, what obliterates love and hate, what determines truth and falsehood. Goudarz, Makhaira and Nefer each answered one of them over the centuries; the first two answers were rejected, the third person, Nefer, eventually found the intended one, "time", and the Pillars were freed in 6.6.

## entry: Aaru and the Irminsul
@keys: Irminsul, Ley Lines, Akasha System
@keys2: Aaru
@version: 6.6
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
After Irminsul was burned in the 6.6 Archon Quest, Aaru took over its role of collecting and storing the Ley Lines' information, shifting from a dead king's unfinished afterlife into the world's record-keeper. The wiki links this to the Akasha System, which resembles Aaru's architecture, and reports that the Heavenly Principles no longer have direct control over fate.
