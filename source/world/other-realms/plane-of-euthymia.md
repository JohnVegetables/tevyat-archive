## summary
### LITE
The Plane of Euthymia is the inner world of Ei, the Raiden Shogun. Over 400 years ago, after building the Shogun puppet to rule in her place, Ei withdrew her consciousness into it to meditate on eternity and protect her spirit from decay. It is a space of mind, not a place one walks to, and it lies within the sword Musou Isshin kept inside the Shogun. For centuries Ei admitted no one.
### STANDARD +
The Traveler was drawn in three times: first as an "exception" to be judged, then to shake Ei's resolve with the light of the confiscated Visions, and later to persuade her to leave and see modern Inazuma. In Chinese the name is Yixin Jingtu, "Pure Land of One Mind".

## geography
### STANDARD
It has no map as such. In the first judgment the Traveler finds bare gravel ground with torii rising from it in a dark void. Later it appears as a quiet, dark-skied space where Ei meditates, with Ei standing some distance away. It reflects her inner world, so its look follows her state of mind.
### LARGE +
In The Omnipresent God the people's ambitions shine into the Plane and light it. Yae Miko remarks that the sky, once so dark, is glowing again and takes it as a sign that Ei is happy to see her. Ei herself later says she saw human aspirations light up the sky as countless stars when the Traveler charged in. The clearest guide for use in a scene: its weather and light are emotional, not physical.

## people
### STANDARD
Ei is the only permanent occupant. The Shogun puppet does not live there, though she can confront Ei in consciousness-spaces. Outsiders enter only because Ei pulls them in or, on the Traveler's second visit, because Yae Miko appears through the Grand Narukami Shrine omamori she had given them.

## history
### STANDARD
Yae Miko once taught Ei how to place consciousness in objects. Ei used this to create the Plane and set it in the blade Musou Isshin, a sword made from her sister Makoto's divine might that passed to Ei after Makoto died in the cataclysm about 500 years ago. Over 400 years ago she retired into it, leaving the Shogun to pursue Inazuma's eternity. She could watch the world and take over the puppet at will, but cared only about matters touching eternity, which is why she tacitly accepted the Vision Hunt Decree.
### LARGE +
The Traveler, able to use elements without a Vision, disrupted the Vision Hunt Ceremony at the Statue of the Omnipresent God. The Shogun called them an exception and an enemy of eternity and drew them into the Plane to judge their strength; the Traveler was struck down and woke outside (Amidst Stormy Judgment).

In The Omnipresent God the Traveler returned, this time carrying the people's ambitions, with Yae Miko joining through the omamori. Ei was defeated, agreed to end the Vision Hunt Decree, and admitted she was glad to see Yae again, though she still held to eternity and asked for time to think about whether Inazuma should move forward.
### ULTRA +
After the main quests Ei put the Shogun into a rest state and announced she was resting. The thunderstorm at sea, which Inazuma reads as her will, grew wilder and the Tri-Commission lacked orders. In The Lonesome Euthymia the Traveler and Paimon found the Shogun refusing visitors, were let into the Plane, and learned Ei had simply disabled many of the puppet's functions so she would not repeat mistakes under old rules while Ei thought. They invited her on a walk through Inazuma. Ei agreed, asked to be called just Ei, and left the Plane for the first time in over 400 years; the storm turned out to be a minor error in the puppet she could fix.

Afterwards Ei involved herself more in Inazuma's affairs and controlled the Shogun's body more often. This led to conflict with the Shogun, who suspected erosion. The dispute carried on in Makoto's realm of consciousness (Farewell to the Past), a separate space that resembles the Plane.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Quiet, dark and lonely at first, with a pressure the Traveler feels as static and headaches before entering. It is a place for slow, unhurried thought, where Ei refuses to reach a verdict just to have one. It brightens when someone she cares about arrives.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the Plane remains Ei's inner retreat, but she is no longer sealed in it.
### STANDARD +
Ei has ended the Vision Hunt Decree, and spends time in the outside world. No Plane of Euthymia content was found for the 6.x Nod-Krai or 7.0-7.1 releases.

## entry: Musou Isshin
@keys: Musou Isshin
@version: 2.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The legendary sword made from Makoto's divine might. In her hands it was kept unsharpened as a symbol of true peace; after her death Ei took it into battle. Ei placed the Plane of Euthymia inside it, and the Shogun summons it from her chest. Makoto left a faint trace of her will in it, which was released after Ei's change of heart.

## entry: The Vision Hunt Ceremony Trial
@keys: Vision Hunt Ceremony, Amidst Stormy Judgment
@version: 2.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
At the ceremony marking the 100th seized Vision, held at the Statue of the Omnipresent God, Thoma was to lose his. The Traveler intervened, was marked as an enemy of eternity and drawn into the Plane. In the fight the Shogun invoked the decree to stop other characters' Elemental Skills and Bursts, then delivered divine punishment. Thoma escaped with the unconscious Traveler.
