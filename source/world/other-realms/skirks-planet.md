## summary
@spoiler: major
@version: 5.7
### LITE
Skirk's planet is the world in the sea of stars where the swordswoman Skirk was born. It began as a hostile world whose extreme temperatures could not support life, yet humans emerged there and evolved the ability to adapt to any environment or energy source. A merchant ship from a neighbouring planet was welcomed, discovered that ability and judged the people a threat, and an army then destroyed them. The planet is unnamed and cannot be visited; it is known only from Skirk's memories in her Crystallina Chapter (5.7).
### STANDARD +
Skirk survived only because Surtalogi, one of the Five Sinners of Khaenri'ah, took her in and trained her. She later travelled to Teyvat, and she still carries her homeland's tunes and instruments.
### LARGE +
Her people lived alongside the Abyss without using it; the destruction came from outsiders who feared what they were. This is the background to her stoic outlook and to her remark in Heart and Mind that a penchant for goodness over reason doomed her homeland.

## geography
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
The planet once lacked the conditions for life because of extreme temperatures. By Skirk's childhood it carried grasses and forests, a sea with large fish and treasures at the bottom, a northern prairie, and a giant bird said to spit fire.
### LARGE +
The attack also exterminated the natural flora and fauna, so the world is now barren of the life Skirk remembers.

## people
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Humans who, over generations, evolved the ability to adapt to nearly any environment or energy source. The wiki reads this as letting them touch the Abyss without grave danger, though none ever used it as a power. They lived in villages, sent children to school, and welcomed visitors with hospitality.
### LARGE +
From Skirk's memories they appear peaceful and non-violent; most could not defend themselves, though a man and his wife are said to have put up a real fight. Skirk herself was a curious child who skipped school to explore and dreamed of travelling the stars.

## culture
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Her people made music, and Skirk keeps a tune her parents taught her. The item "Lingering Euphonia" is a guitar Skirk made from the instruments of her homeland, with an ancient melody that seems to hum of a history nobody recalls.
### ULTRA +
The guitar is a reward of the story quest Unbounded. No wider customs, language or religion of the planet are given in canon.

## history
@spoiler: major
@version: 5.7
### STANDARD
A merchant ship from a neighbouring planet arrived while Skirk was a child. The locals welcomed the crew, who explored the planet and learned that the people could adapt to any environment or energy source. The outsiders judged them a potential threat. Months later an army of unknown origin landed without warning and set about destroying everything and everyone. Skirk's village was burned; her wounded parents told her to flee, and soldiers left her under burning rubble. She survived only through Surtalogi's intervention.
### LARGE +
Surtalogi's later reading, voiced in Special Training, was that fear forces a people to adapt, and that her homeland was destroyed because of its potential. Skirk afterwards left the planet, trained, and travelled to Teyvat.

## status
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
As of Version 7.1 the planet is destroyed and is seen only in Skirk's memories. Skirk has not returned, and canon gives no way to travel there.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Treat it as a memory, tender and grieving: grass, sea and stars seen through a child's eyes, ending in smoke. Skirk speaks of it rarely and without drama.

@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
In Skirk's memories a spaceship of outsiders who spoke of friendship arrived and was welcomed. Their investigation showed the planet's people could adapt to any environment or energy source, so they were treated as a potential threat. An army came months later and destroyed the people. Soldiers spoke of burning the civilisation down; most villagers were unable to resist.

### STANDARD
Her people's adaptability allowed contact with the Abyss without grave danger, though they never used it directly. Skirk's own power came later from Surtalogi's training, and she hides it in Teyvat as Cryo through the Seven-Shifting Serpent technique.
