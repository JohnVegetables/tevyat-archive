## summary
### LITE
The Sea of Flowers at the End is a place where Inteyvat flowers, the national flower of Khaenri'ah, bloom in full. The Traveler's sibling found it at the end of their long journey. The Traveler had once said they wished to find such a place in the universe, so its discovery moved the sibling to stop travelling with the Traveler and take up Teyvat's affairs.
### STANDARD +
It is not a place the player can visit. Everything known comes from the sibling's own words to the Traveler during the Sumeru-set Archon Quest World-Order Narration.
### LARGE +
The sibling frames the place as something they cannot quite explain: they offer it to the Traveler as proof that they still remember the old wish, while admitting they do not know how to face the Traveler as the conflict goes on.

## description
### STANDARD
The sea is made of Inteyvat flowers: five-petalled blooms, four white petals and one pale blue. The name is the sibling's, repeated in quotation marks as a proper name for the place.
### LARGE +
Inteyvat once bloomed all over Khaenri'ah and lasts only about two weeks before wilting. Taken out of Khaenri'ah, the petals reportedly harden and soften again only when the flower returns to its home soil, so it is a symbol of a wanderer far from home. This is Dainsleif's account in Memories of Inteyvat. The flower is also what Lumine wears in her hair.

## history
@version: 4.7
### STANDARD
During their travels between worlds, the Traveler told their sibling they wanted to find a place in the universe where Inteyvat flowers were in full bloom. At the end of the sibling's journey across Teyvat, the sibling found such a place: the Sea of Flowers at the End.
### LARGE +
The sibling tells this to the Traveler in the Sumeru Archon Quest, after the Traveler asks why the two cannot keep travelling together. The sibling asks whether the Traveler really thinks it a coincidence that such a place suddenly appeared before them, and says they miss the Traveler too.
### ULTRA +
The sibling places the conversation in the middle of an ongoing war and their search for a final answer, and says that in this state they do not always know how to face themselves, let alone their own sibling. The Sea of Flowers is thus offered as a memory of shared wishes, not as a refuge the Traveler can be taken to. The place's names in other languages (Chinese Zhongjing de Huahai, Japanese Hate no Hana no Umi) both mean roughly 'the final / farthest sea of flowers'.

## status
### STANDARD
Not accessible and not visited by anyone shown in game as of 7.1. The Traveler has heard of it only through the sibling.

### STANDARD
In World-Order Narration the Traveler's sibling names the Sea of Flowers at the End as the place where they stopped their journey, and links it to the Traveler's old wish to find a world where the Inteyvat flower bloomed in full. The sibling does not say where it is or what is there beyond the flowers.

## entry: Inteyvat
@keys: Inteyvat
@keys2: flower, Khaenri'ah
@version: 1.0
### STANDARD
Inteyvat is the national flower of Khaenri'ah, with four white petals and one pale blue petal. Dainsleif tells of it wilting in two weeks and hardening when taken away from Khaenri'ah. The Traveler says the same flowers grow in their homeland; Lumine wears one in her hair. Emilie, a modern Teyvat resident, had never heard of it before being told by the Traveler.
