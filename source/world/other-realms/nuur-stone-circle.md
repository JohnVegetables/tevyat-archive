## summary
### LITE
Nuur Stone Circle is an ancient dragon facility on the Frost Moon, in the Lunar Highlands, that records and stores the dragon researchers' data. Its tablets, once repaired, preserve notes about Project Moongazer, the Moongazers' backup research, and the plan to engrave a researcher's hopes into a data-born life that became Nin-Kiggia.
### STANDARD +
It was maintained by a dragon recorder, Nu-ur-Gestug ("Nuur"), who described himself as a recorder, not a researcher. The site also holds an Ancient Moon's Remnant, the Frost Moon's reputation shrine shared with Dunanna Pit, which unlocks after the quest Origin of the Sequence.

## geography
### STANDARD
A subarea of the Lunar Highlands on the Frost Moon. It has a high-speed transport station, a teleport waypoint, underground halls and ruins, and viewpoints named "Silent Stone Circle". The Ia-Ilimmu Storage Array Index tablets stand in three places: inside an underground hall entered from a crater south of the transit station, near the waypoint, and in ruins north of the station.
### LARGE +
Features include Archaeolune Energy Nodes and Surges, Ancient Moon Pearls, a Gravimeter, terminals, Secret Source Dragon Idols and high-speed transit lanes. Elite enemies include the Precursor's Secret Source Matrix of Overseer Network and Surging Currents: Frostnight Scion. The player repairs four data-storage areas in the objective Fragments of Ia-Ilimmu to read the tablets.

## places
### STANDARD
The Ancient Moon's Remnant hub; the three Ia-Ilimmu Storage Array Index tablets; the crater hall; a transit station connecting to other Lunar Highlands sites.
### LARGE +
Index I is titled "Researches Concerning Life", Index II "Concerning the Engraving" and Index III "Researches Concerning Cognition". Each shows an expired index and asks the reader to contact Nu-ur-Gestug or Nin-Kiggia for the main text.

## people
### STANDARD
Nu-ur-Gestug, recorder of the system log; Lusalim and Maagees-Geti, authors of the research on self-sustaining informational processes; the dragon king Xiuhcoatl, who offered suggestions; Ibni-Belum, author of the "Engraving" orbital system. Nin-Kiggia refers to Nu-ur-Gestug as "Uncle", though not in a genetic sense.
### LARGE +
Nu-ur-Gestug was not affiliated with Project Moongazer and opposed Project Lunar Veil, according to the wiki; he researched cognitive hazards as an alternative defence against the Abyss.

## history
### STANDARD
Index I's log records Nu-ur-Gestug's conclusion that Maagees-Geti was right: any system of sufficient complexity is computationally equivalent, so a simulated self-awareness is no different from organic consciousness. He writes that Lusalim proposed engraving a researcher's affections, desires and ideals into data so that an "immortal life" could re-emerge infinitely, even if no longer a dragon, to broadcast the Moongazers' ideals across the ages. Lusalim planned to name that life Nin-Kiggia, "messenger".
### LARGE +
King Xiuhcoatl visited and showed interest, noting parallels with his own work to manifest life through phlogiston (computronium), hoping to bring the surface-dwelling dragons some relief. When Nu-ur-Gestug let slip Lusalim's plan, the king offered practical suggestions, which Nu-ur-Gestug forwarded to Lusalim despite regretting the lapse. Index II is Nu-ur-Gestug's public log about retrofitting the "Engraving", the orbital positioning facility in Nephilheim built by Ibni-Belum, as a backup storage array, ending with a wish that "the Creator", Nibelung, be crowned with supreme glory.
### ULTRA +
The Engraving's backup lets later researchers recover core results if the lunar array is destroyed or contaminated; the log notes it can serve as an emergency bunker after weapon-system countermeasure tests. The index text is thus a snapshot of the dragons' work before the Heavenly Principles arrived.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Silent standing stones glowing with faint inscriptions, dim halls and ruined arrays; the feel is of an archive nobody has visited in an age.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Frostnight Scions and the Precursor's Secret Source Matrix guardians, and Archaeolune Energy Surges.

## travel
### STANDARD
Reached by the high-speed transit lane from other Lunar Highlands sites; the transit station is the site's anchor.

## quests
### STANDARD
Origin of the Sequence unlocks the Ancient Moon's Remnant; Nuur Stone Circle quests add the Fragments of Ia-Ilimmu exploration objective.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the circle is a quiet ruin whose array can be repaired and read.

### STANDARD
The Ia-Ilimmu Storage Array Index is a set of three tablets that list the dragons' research and backup data. The listed items include research by Maagees-Geti and Lusalim, a completeness analysis based on computronium by Xiuhcoatl, and joint work by Lusalim and Nu-ur-Gestug. Their system logs are the source of how Nin-Kiggia came to be named.
