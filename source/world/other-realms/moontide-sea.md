## summary
### LITE
The Moontide Sea is a lunar sea on the Frost Moon, a body of liquid-like Archaeolune Energy so dense that it can harm any living being that is not a kuuhenki. It formed after a terrible calamity wrecked Lusalim's Lunar Veil Laboratory; it is home to the Mega Kuuhenki Maggie and Ungie and to shoals of small kuuhenki that can mimic jellyfish and whales.
### STANDARD +
Its centre holds the Ancient Moon's Fount, an energy entity made by Lusalim. When the Mega Kuuhenki fought, the sea stirred into an Archaeolune Storm that spread toward Ungien's Circle; the Traveler fetched the Fount while Columbina tended the wounded Maggie.

## geography
### STANDARD
A sea on the Frost Moon, reached in the quest by floating down from the Space Lifeform Capture Station beside Maggie; the Dark Side of the Moon lies beyond its edge. Blue areas of the sea hold high concentrations of ancient-moon energy and incapacitate anyone who touches them; floating islands and stones rise from it. A "Sea of Broken Skies" viewpoint overlooks it.
### LARGE +
Features include Archaeolune Energy Barriers, Nodes and Surges, Ancient Moon Pearls, high-speed transit lanes, material storage chests, Pioneer Source Structures, shoaling kuuhenki and wondrous black kuuhenki, and elite enemies such as the Precursor's Secret Source Matrix of Overseer Network. The local specialty is the Teardrop of the Moon.

## places
### STANDARD
The Ancient Moon's Fount at the sea's centre. The Curtain-Form Blackbody Containment Lab, a site tied to Lusalim's research. Floating islets used as footholds.
### LARGE +
The sea is the site of Lusalim's Lunar Veil Laboratory, his second-to-last facility; the last was the one on the Dark Side.

## people
### STANDARD
Maggie (the Mega Kuuhenki who was Maagees-Geti) and Ungie (the Mega Kuuhenki born from Ungien) live here, with schools of shoaling kuuhenki. Columbina guides visitors.
### LARGE +
Ungie was irritable, his body tangled in Knotted Moonlight Vines that held data from Ungien's Circle, until the party cleared them. Eggie absorbed Ungie's stored information to learn what had happened at the Capture Station.

## history
### STANDARD
The sea was once Lusalim's Lunar Veil Laboratory. After the miniature Veil on Ungien's Circle succeeded, the researchers built a massive Veil generator here to hide the entire moon. Before it could take shape, a being from beyond the stars pierced it; the device went haywire, the laboratory exploded and all came to nothing. Only deep underground might traces remain.
### LARGE +
Lusalim and Maagees designed the Lunar Veil to protect Project Moongazer's transmission from hostile listeners, using the reflective properties of Blackbody Matter. Columbina notes the Veil resembles the false sky in miniature and suspects that Lusalim bargained with the Heavenly Principles with his research, which would explain why other dragon facilities survived.
### ULTRA +
Lusalim also created the Ancient Moon's Fount here. Columbina intended to extract it because it lies at the heart of the storm, expecting this to calm the storm and prevent further trouble if Maggie and Ungie fought again.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
A glimmering, weightless sea where you float by kuuhenki, with distant whales drifting overhead. It is beautiful but hazardous, like a storm of moonlight.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Contact with the blue energy zones incapacitates non-kuuhenki; Archaeolune Storms stirred by Ungie's attacks; Frostnight Scions; Ungie's sticky bombs; and the Precursor's Secret Source Matrix guardians.

## travel
### STANDARD
Crossed with the help of shoaling kuuhenki, which can mimic jellyfish to bounce travellers across or whales to ferry them. Columbina or Maggie can keep the party weightless and shielded nearby.

## quests
### STANDARD
Visitors From the Stars (Part 2) (Moon Gazing Act II, 6.7). The related Beneath the Lunar Sea quest opens the Curtain-Form Blackbody Containment Lab Ancient Moon's Remnant.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the sea is calm after the Fount's removal and the Mega Kuuhenki's reconciliation, though its energy remains dangerous.
