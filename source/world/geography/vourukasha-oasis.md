## summary
### LITE
Vourukasha Oasis is the birthplace and homeland of the Pari, avian flower-like beings who guard it. It holds a sea of pure dew called Amrita, formed from the remains of the original Hydro Archon, Egeria, and at its centre grows the Harvisptokhm, a giant tree that keeps what is left of her consciousness. After an Abyss attack the Pari elder Zurvan sealed the Oasis with the Great Songs of Khvarena, admitting only heroes who have bonded with Pari and pilgrims who come to worship Egeria.
### STANDARD +
The Oasis lies in the Girdle of the Sands within the Realm of Farakhkert and is the setting of the Khvarena of Good and Evil quest series (3.6). Its name echoes the Vourukasha, the cosmic sea of Zoroastrian belief, with the Harvisptokhm as its tree of all seeds.

## geography
### STANDARD
A sacred oasis built around the central Harvisptokhm. The Amrita Pool holds the primordial Amrita; the tree's interior, with the great lotus Gaokerena inside, is entered through a hollow at Tunigi Hollow or over the stump's top. A thorn-filled canyon holds Asipattravana Swamp just to the north.
### LARGE +
Before the Traveler cleanses it, the Harvisptokhm looks like a withered stump under the Sign of Apaosha, a defiled mark that hangs in the sky. Afterwards it sprouts a glowing blue canopy, and after The Hymn of Tir Yazad (Part 2) a tower of pure Amrita grows on top.

## people
### STANDARD
The Pari live here: Zurvan, the First Pari and elder; Mihir, a familiar of thorns; Rashnu; and Sorush, a young Pari who joins the Traveler. The wiki also lists Sefana and Fedhri among the Oasis's NPCs. Visitors are rare. Zurvan limits entry to heroes who have bonded with Pari and to pilgrims who come to worship Egeria.
### LARGE +
Pari are the sentient form of Khvarena. Each has a fated responsibility they must carry out alone. At the end of their life they turn into Plumes of Purifying Light that return to the Amrita Pool; a Pari tainted by Abyssal corruption is rejected by the Oasis and becomes a Fravashi Tree, and their bonded human, called Yasnapati, brings lost Pari home.

## culture
### STANDARD
Pari consider Egeria their god. They exist to avert catastrophe and cleanse defilement, and the Great Songs of Khvarena are their ritual power. 

## history
### STANDARD
In the cataclysm 500 years ago Egeria, the previous Hydro Archon, was slain; Greater Lord Rukkhadevata grew the Harvisptokhm from the Amrita, her remains, anchoring Egeria's consciousness in the mortal world. The tree absorbs the defilement leaking from the Abyssal rift at Tunigi Hollow and keeps it sealed. The divine bird Simurgh sacrificed herself into thousands of Khvarena, drank the Amrita and mixed her Khvarena with it, creating the Pari. The first Pari, Zurvan, woke during the cataclysm, fought Abyssal creatures, and found Dainsleif shortly after Khaenri'ah's fall.
### LARGE +
Zurvan, Dainsleif and the one-armed sage Nagarjuna found the Harvisptokhm with the help of Khaenri'ahn ruin constructs and held the first Rite of Ab-Zohr, which cleansed sky and earth and created the other Pari, who bonded with the Skeptics. Sometime later, after an Abyss attack, Zurvan sealed the Oasis with the Great Songs of Khvarena. The Oceanids, created by Egeria, came to Tunigi Hollow looking for their old master after rejecting the new Hydro Archon Focalors, saw the tree was not what they hoped, and scattered; later people followed their pilgrimage trail.
### ULTRA +
In the quest series the Traveler and Sorush answer a growing build-up of defilement, shown as the Sign of Apaosha. Zurvan tells the Traveler that if the filth overwhelms the tree, the rift at Tunigi Hollow could reopen. The events of The Hymn of Tir Yazad (Part 2) extinguish the Sign; Zurvan says the power of defilement can never be fully removed, only delayed, and that the Pari will rest and recover.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Abyssal defilement, which can twist Pari and cloud areas like Asipattravana Swamp with purple mist; Abyss monsters tied to the Tunigi Hollow rift; and any visitor the Pari judge unworthy. Zurvan opens her sealed home only to bonded heroes and pilgrims.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
A sacred, ancient and guarded place. The wiki's intro speaks of a sea of pure dew with a tree at its heart, and the Pari talk of duty and of a cycle that returns them to the water. Zurvan greets trespassers sternly, while Sorush's talk is bluntly tough.

## quests
### STANDARD
Khvarena of Good and Evil (3.6): The Splendorous Sky That Day, Asipattravana Itihasa, The Hymn of Tir Yazad, Pale Fire and related quests. The Traveler gains Sorush, helps the Pari awaken Khvarena, cleanses the Harvisptokhm and extinguishes the Sign of Apaosha.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the Oasis is calm: the Sign of Apaosha is gone and the Pari rest.
### STANDARD +
Zurvan keeps the seal in place and the Harvisptokhm is whole, crowned with Amrita. Nothing from 6.x or 7.0-7.1 has changed this.

## entry: The Pari
@keys: Pari, Zurvan, Sorush, Mihir
@keys2: Vourukasha Oasis, Harvisptokhm, Khvarena
@version: 3.6
### STANDARD
The Pari are avian, flower-like beings descended from the divine bird Simurgh who live in the Vourukasha Oasis. They are the only sentient form of Khvarena. Zurvan is the first; Mihir is a familiar of thorns; Sorush, young and untested, stays with the Traveler. Their Plumes of Purifying Light return to the Amrita Pool.
### LARGE +
Zurvan says they exist to avert catastrophe and cleanse defilement. Sorush was born after the previous catastrophe and has not seen the old war, and Zurvan calls her tough talk a cover for a good heart.

## entry: Harvisptokhm
@keys: Harvisptokhm, Amrita, Tunigi Hollow, Gaokerena, Sign of Apaosha
@version: 3.6
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The giant tree at the Oasis's centre. Greater Lord Rukkhadevata grew it from the Amrita, the remains of Egeria. It binds Egeria's remaining consciousness to the mortal world, seals the Abyssal rift at Tunigi Hollow and absorbs its defilement. Inside is the giant lotus Gaokerena where the god of the Pari slumbers.
### LARGE +
Its Zoroastrian namesake is the tree of all seeds in the cosmic sea Vourukasha, within which grows the Gaokerena and on which the Simurgh perches.
