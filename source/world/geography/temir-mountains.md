## summary
### LITE
The Temir Mountains are a range in Gavireh Lajavard, in Sumeru's Girdle of the Sands. They are the main passage between the Desert of Hadramaveth and the rest of the Girdle. Legend says the range was formed from molten metal, and the desert folk treat it as the border of the red desert.
### STANDARD +
The wiki lists the mountains as the base of the Order of Skeptics, the scholars who split from the Akademiya and made friends with the Pari. They were added in version 3.6.
### LARGE +
"Temir" means iron in several Turkic languages. Two minerals are gathered here: Mourning Flower, a crimson flower said to be watered by the blood of martyrs of the cataclysm, and Trishiraite, a stone formed from congealed elemental energy.

## geography
### STANDARD
A mountain belt that serves as the primary passage between the Desert of Hadramaveth and the rest of the Girdle of the Sands.
### LARGE +
The western range holds Fravashi Trees that can be restored by waking the Pari spirits sleeping in them. A Skeptic outpost stands behind a Statue of the Seven in Gavireh Lajavard, where a note insists on strict control over entry and exit of all mountain passes so that a ceremony and plan can be completed.

## history
### STANDARD
Trishiraite's description recalls the old Sumeru legend of Dahri, an underground kingdom said to have fallen to the dark disaster of the cataclysm after straying from the true path. Mourning Flowers are said to bloom on ancient battlefields, watered by the blood of heroes who died in that war.
### LARGE +
In "Monumental Study" (3.6) the Traveler and Sorush meet Sosi in the mountains and restore a Khvarena Monument by finding four inscription fragments, after fighting Fatui led by Markozov, the "Leader of Those Pursuing Knowledge". Once complete, the inscription records that Amrita and Khvarena extinguished the phantoms mirroring the bedevilled sky, gives praise to the spirits of light and the protection of Dahri, warns that the Khvarena has no beginning or end and the Amrita must be kept pure by ritual prayer, and urges later visitors to be diligent rather than neglectful.
### ULTRA +
Trishiraite is described as the deep-red ore left after the flames of the sun-swallowing battles died; hardest stones were set alight then, and listeners are told they may still hear echoes of the fighting. The tribes of the plains add a second tradition about Mourning Flowers: the innocents stained by the war's filth are barred from eternal samsara and can only weep among the flowers. Trishiraite was first introduced in 3.6 and its name echoes Trishira, a three-headed rakshasa of the Ramayana (out-of-universe note).

## dangers
### STANDARD
Fatui camps, marked by Sosi in Monumental Study, and the wider hazards of Gavireh Lajavard, whose boss is the Iniquitous Baptist.

## quests
### STANDARD
"Monumental Study" (3.6) starts here once the first step of "As the Khvarena's Light Shows" is done, and Fravashi Tree quests in the western mountains let the Traveler wake residual Pari. Both belong to the Girdle of the Sands and Khvarena of Good and Evil content.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
A border range at the edge of the red desert, known in legend as molten metal turned to stone.

## status

As of 7.1 nothing is recorded as changing here beyond the events of the 3.6 quests.
