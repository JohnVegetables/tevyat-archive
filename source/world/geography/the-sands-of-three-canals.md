## summary
### LITE
The Sands of Three Canals are an elevated part of the Desert of Hadramaveth in Sumeru, north of Mt. Damavand. Desert folk still keep the name of a lost river and the green gardens that once lay here. The Fane of Panjvahe, a one-time domain, stands in it.
### STANDARD +
The area was added in version 3.4. Today the only thing the old river carries is grinding gravel.

## geography
### STANDARD
High ground in the Great Red Sand, north of Mt. Damavand, around which sandstorms never cease.
### LARGE +
During The Dirge of Bilqis world quest series the large Ruin Golem of the Hadramaveth is moved to a spot north of the Sands of Three Canals on its way across the desert, before it comes to rest in Wounded Shin Valley.
### ULTRA +
The golem's whole route is Dunes of Steel (where it first stands), then north of the Sands of Three Canals, then Wounded Shin Valley, where it sinks and loses its mobility.

## places
### STANDARD
The Fane of Panjvahe, a lonesome hall where ancient mechanisms (Primal Constructs) and desert bandits (Eremites) patrol. It is a one-time domain with a single level, Fading Rumination.
### LARGE +
The domain recommends Dendro and Electro. Its ley line disorder is a game rule that sets off Electro shockwaves when Spread or Aggravate reactions occur.

## history
### STANDARD
Loading-screen text recalls a beautiful river, navy as a moonlit night, that once flowed here in an age of songsters and flowers, marble colonnades and glazed domes. Desert people still cherish the green and blue memory and the name Three Canals; the river now carries only grinding gravel.
### LARGE +
The name Panjvahe in the domain recalls Panjvahe, which Gurabad records describe as a long-impoverished district of that city; the wiki glosses the word as 'five oases'. Treat any link to this site as unconfirmed.

## dangers
### LARGE
Eremites and Primal Constructs in the fane; the open desert's sandstorms.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the sands are an open, permanent subarea of the desert.
### STANDARD +
No change to the area is recorded after version 3.4.

## entry: Fane of Panjvahe
@keys: Fane of Panjvahe, Fading Rumination
@version: 3.4
### STANDARD
One-time domain in the Sands of Three Canals. The game says that when travellers step into this lonesome hall, the ancient mechanisms and desert bandits patrolling it emerge from the shadows to punish the reckless. Enemies are Primal Constructs and Eremites (Desert Clearwater, Daythunder, Crossbow and Galehunter types).
