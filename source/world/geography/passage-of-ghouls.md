## summary
### LITE
The Passage of Ghouls is a passage sunk between two cliffs in the Desert of Hadramaveth, in Sumeru's Great Red Sand. It links Wadi Al-Majuj, a canyon area south of Mt. Damavand, with the flatter Sands of Al-Azif.
### STANDARD +
It is a corridor rather than a destination, and the most memorable thing in it is the pair of damaged records left by an investigation team that never made it to the Eternal Oasis.

## geography
### STANDARD
A narrow, depressed pass between two cliffs. Wadi Al-Majuj, with its large canyon, lies to one end, and the Sands of Al-Azif, one of the flatter areas of the desert with roaming Wenut and the tunnels beneath, lies to the other.
### LARGE +
The pass sits within the web of canyons that surround Mt. Damavand, where sandstorms make travel dangerous. The Desert of Hadramaveth is the area with the most subareas in the game, eleven in the wiki's count.

## history
### STANDARD
Damaged Records found here are the notes of a team that was ordered to find the path to the Eternal Oasis, the Orchard of Pairidaeza. They mention serious losses, supply lines cut by storms, and food running out so that they thought of catching nearby Wenut to eat. A hunting party was lost to the sandstorm and no more were sent.
### LARGE +
By the end the team had run out of water and had bled their sumpter beasts, scouts were still hunting for a way back, and the writer left their fate to "the sleeping mistress of the Oasis" if she exists at all. A second record is a prayer to the Goddess of Flowers to carry the abandoned's souls to the Eternal Oasis.
### ULTRA +
The name is an out-of-universe nod to ghouls, shapeshifting, flesh-eating creatures of pre-Islamic Arabian folklore said to haunt remote desolate places, often near graveyards; the game text read gives no in-world reason, and the Chinese name translates roughly as Narrow Passage of the Deceased.

## dangers
### STANDARD
The hazards of the Desert of Hadramaveth in general: sandstorms, scarce water and food, and roaming Wenut on the neighbouring sands. No enemy list is recorded for the passage itself.
### LARGE +
In gameplay a Ruin Grader can be fought near the passage, and the Remarkable Chest it guards holds the blueprint for the Serenitea Pot furnishing "Iron, Sand, Treasure". That is a game mechanic, not story.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
A cliff-walled corridor in a storm-ridden desert, made grim by the notes of people who did not get through.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 nothing is recorded as changing here.

## entry: Damaged Records of the Passage
@keys: Damaged Records
@keys2: Eternal Oasis, Passage of Ghouls, Hadramaveth
### STANDARD
Two fragmentary notes. The first describes an expedition ordered to find the path to the Eternal Oasis that has lost people, supplies and its hunting parties to the storms and now has little hope. The second prays to the Goddess of Flowers to bring the abandoned to the Oasis.
