## summary
### LITE
Wakukau Shoal is a shoreline subarea of Tsurumi Island in Inazuma. It has little lore of its own, but in the world quest The Sun-Wheel and Mt. Kanna it is the first place where the Traveler uses the Thunderbird feather on a Mysterious Carving and is drawn into a memory of Kanna Kapatcir.
### STANDARD +
It is part of the search for the boy Ruu, who is not found here.

## geography
### STANDARD
A rocky shoreline with a rock arch, a large camp site and cliffs above. Mysterious Carvings and a Thunder Sakura Bough stand here, and Sea Ganoderma grows along the shore.
### LARGE +
Hilichurl camps, including one led by an Electro Samachurl, and Ruin Guards occupy the area. They rearrange themselves over the three days of Tsurumi's cycle: a Thunderhelm Lawachurl, Hydro slimes and Samachurls on day one; Thundercraven Rifthound whelps and a Rifthound with the Ruin Guards nearby on day two; and several Rifthounds, with the Ruin Guards active, on day three.

## history
### STANDARD
Using the feather here summons two waves of monsters, ending with a Thunderhelm Lawachurl. A voice asks where someone is, and the feather that drops shows the Traveler a vision from the Thunderbird's view of Tsurumi Island under fog. She hears the voices of boars, humans, squirrels, lizards and Wen Kamuy, and then a clear song in the rain, unlike the war songs of the boars and the choruses of the fish.
### LARGE +
The feather flies up to Mt. Kanna. Kama and Paimon agree the voice sounded as if the Thunderbird were searching for someone, and the party moves on to Oina Beach.

## quests
### STANDARD
The Sun-Wheel and Mt. Kanna (2.2): the first of three feather sites, followed by Oina Beach and the Autake Plains.
### ULTRA +
The first wave here is one each of a Cryo Hilichurl Shooter, an Electro Hilichurl Shooter, a Wooden Shield Hilichurl Guard and a Hilichurl Fighter. Afterwards Kama explains that, in the legends his grandfather taught him, the Thunderbird's feathers were the basis for the Maushiro, which can summon spirits.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Hilichurls, Electro Samachurls, Hydro slimes, Ruin Guards and Thundercraven Rifthounds, depending on the day.
