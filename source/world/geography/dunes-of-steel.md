## summary
### LITE
The Dunes of Steel are a subarea of the Desert of Hadramaveth in Sumeru's Great Red Sand. Canon describes them only as the stretch of the desert that connects it to Old Vanarana, the former home of the Aranara.
### STANDARD +
They were added with the Desert of Hadramaveth in version 3.4. Nothing else about their history, inhabitants or purpose is stated.

## geography
### STANDARD
Sand dunes at the edge of the Desert of Hadramaveth, on the side that leads toward Old Vanarana in the Dharma Forest's Lost Nursery. The wiki lists no points of interest, enemies or specialties for the subarea itself.
### LARGE +
The Desert of Hadramaveth around it is a wide sandy basin cut by many ravines (its game description speaks of scimitar-shaped ravines hiding a lost city), crossed by sandstorms, and holds Setekh Wenut as its boss and a large number of interactable records and tablets.

## places
### LARGE
A Ruin Golem stands in the dunes, and beneath its original position lies the Radiant Spincrystal 88, an item that unlocks the "Kingdom of Dust" soundtrack for the Serenitea Pot.

## history
### STANDARD
Canon gives the dunes no history of their own. Their only stated role is as the link to Old Vanarana, which the Aranara called home for hundreds of years before the cataclysm and which stayed engulfed in the Withering until the Nursery of Lost Dreams quest.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Open, wind-swept sand with no settlement in sight, where the desert gives way toward the old Aranara nursery. Treat details beyond that as scene-setting, not canon.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the dunes are an open part of the Desert of Hadramaveth.
### STANDARD +
Nothing after their 3.4 introduction has changed them.
