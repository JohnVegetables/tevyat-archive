## summary
### LITE
The Hills of Barsom lie just north of Asipattravana Swamp in the Realm of Farakhkert, in Sumeru's Girdle of the Sands. They are home to the Barsom, a huge pine with pink foliage, and to its familiar, the Pari Rashnu, who sleeps there.
### STANDARD +
The hills were the setting of the Order of Skeptics' old musical ritual with the Pari and are the start of the world quest "Awakening's Real Sound" (3.6), part of the Khvarena of Good and Evil series.
### LARGE +
The name echoes the barsom, a ritual implement used by Zoroastrian priests in sacred ceremonies, according to the wiki.

## geography
### STANDARD
A rise of ground dominated by the Barsom tree, a towering pine with pink foliage. The area sits just north of Asipattravana Swamp, and the Samudra Coast and a cave by the water lie close by.
### LARGE +
The quest "Awakening's Real Sound" also has the party search a cavern whose walls carry the drum scores, with Thundercraven Rifthound Whelps inside. The five Kory Drums, one for each of five elements, are items found around the Girdle of the Sands.

## people
### STANDARD
Rashnu, a Pari called the Familiar of the Barsom, lives in and around the tree, usually asleep. Nasejuna, the Order of Skeptics' Nasuraia (Vijnanapati), brings the Traveler to the hills and cares about the Order's traditions; Sorush, the young Pari who calls the Traveler her Yasnapati, comes to wake Rashnu and take her to the Vourukasha Oasis.
### LARGE +
Rashnu is sleepy and blunt. Before she is woken she says Barsom is a peaceful place for good sleep, that Pari sleep differently from humans, and that sleep lets her focus on what the roots and branches say. She calls the Kory Drums a human instrument that is extremely noisy.

## history
### STANDARD
By the Order of Skeptics' account, their predecessors, after helping the Pari quell the disaster of the cataclysm, befriended them. Barsom's familiar liked music, so the Skeptics made the Kory Drums; to make the bond ("Laupa") official, five drums had to be gathered and played to scores. The drums were later lost and Barsom's familiar stopped answering them.
### LARGE +
The truth, revealed in "Awakening's Real Sound", is different: Rashnu found the human drumming too noisy and threw the drums to different places so she could sleep. Nasejuna is dismayed that his records were wrong; Sorush says the scattering may have been meant to test who was fit to be a Yasnapati. Rashnu agrees to travel with Sorush to the Vourukasha Oasis, and after The Hymn of Tir Yazad (Part 2) she greets the Traveler there as one of Sorush's companions and gives credit for extinguishing the Sign of Apaosha to Sorush and her helpers.
### ULTRA +
The Barsom viewpoint ("Elder Pine Atop the Wastes", unlocked after the quest) says the spirit within the pine has sheltered a great many warriors returning from their trials, though nobody remembers the details of the ancient compact. The Kory Drums are named after the five skandhas of Buddhist thought (form, perception, formation, sensation, consciousness) in the game's naming, and the wiki ties the whole ritual to the union between the Order and the Pari.

## quests
### STANDARD
"Awakening's Real Sound" begins here (3.6). Julien's "An Artist Adrift" also starts nearby, at a cave between the Samudra Coast and Asipattravana Swamp, once "The Splendorous Sky That Day" is complete.

## dangers
### LARGE
Thundercraven Rifthound Whelps in the drum-score cavern; the wider Girdle of the Sands is not safe. The tree and its familiar are not hostile.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Quiet and sleepy. The giant pink pine towers over the hills, and the Pari who dwells in it dislikes noise; her only stated complaint is the drumming.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 Rashnu has left her post to join Sorush at the Vourukasha Oasis, though the Barsom remains.
### STANDARD +
The Kory Drums have been recovered and played once; the Order of Skeptics' version of the ritual has been corrected, but Nasejuna's unanswered questions about the Skeptics' traditions are left for Rashnu to answer "later, perhaps".

## entry: Kory Drums and the Skeptics' Ritual
@keys: Kory Drums, Korybantes, Kory Drum, Laupa
@keys2: Barsom, Order of Skeptics, Rashnu, Pari
### STANDARD
Mysterious drums said to symbolise the union between the Order of Skeptics and the Pari. There are five, one per element (Rupa, Samjna, Sankhara, Vedana, Vijnana), each with a score that must be played in order. The scores are carved in stone and survived the sandstorms; the drums themselves were lost until the Traveler found them. In truth Rashnu scattered them because they were noisy.

## entry: Rashnu
@keys: Rashnu, Familiar of the Barsom, Barsom's familiar
@keys2: Pari, Barsom, Sorush
### STANDARD
A Pari known as the Familiar of the Barsom. Sleepy, honest and uninterested in "human business". She answers Sorush, whom she recognises, agrees to go with her to the Vourukasha Oasis, and says that sleeping is also a duty. Her Zoroastrian-derived name refers to the yazata of justice, according to the wiki.
