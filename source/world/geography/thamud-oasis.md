## summary
### LITE
Thamud Oasis is an oasis in the Land of Lower Setekh in Sumeru's Great Red Sand. It is where Saleh, the Electrum City of Warriors, once stood. Its Viewpoint text calls oases like it gems adorning the sea of sand.
### STANDARD +
Saleh was home to the golden-eyed Thamudians and was known for seven walls. The kingdom was short-lived, was drawn into the wars between desert kingdoms and was destroyed; today there is little sign of it. The oasis still gives caravans and wanderers springs and plants.

## geography
### STANDARD
A green oasis of springs and plants amid the sand of Lower Setekh. Exploration objects here include Dendroculi, Dendrogranum puzzles and a Four-Leaf Sigil.

## history
### STANDARD
Saleh existed from the time of King Deshret's civilisation and became known as one of the Cities of the Seven Towers. By Parvezravan's reign inscriptions record it paying annual tribute to Gurabad as King Deshret's primary vassal kingdom.
### LARGE +
After King Deshret's death Saleh was one of the many desert kingdoms in the era of warring city-states. Inscriptions name a Khagan, Turan, as its first named leader; he became regent for the young Artaxir and was later ousted and executed as a traitor with his three sons. King Humayun of Tulaytullah, who claimed Shah Ramshahr's throne by marriage, was beheaded at Saleh during an event called the Jinn Uprising. Later the tyrant Badanah ruled it before being assassinated by Faramarz and Makhaira, and after Faramarz's third ascension the people rebelled again, only to be pacified by Makhaira as queen regent of Tulaytullah.
### ULTRA +
The wiki records that some Eremite tribes descend from the Thamudians, among them the Iab and the Unut, who destroyed each other in a plot arranged by Nefer in revenge for the massacre of the Shimti tribe (Dance of Death: Serpent and Scorpion). These details come from inscriptions and quests, not from the oasis page.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Hilichurls of several kinds, a Wooden Shieldwall Mitachurl, fungi and Winged Dendroshrooms, and desert wildlife such as Desert Sumpter Beasts, Red Vultures and Spinocrocodiles.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
A calm green refuge in red sand that carries a vanished city's memory without any visible walls.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 it is a quiet oasis with no city; Saleh survives only in texts and inscriptions.
