## summary
### LITE
Xalac Vale is a river valley in Natlan. It extends from the Stadium of the Sacred Flame to the Flower-Feather Clan's lands, and on its north side it runs past the Masters of the Night-Wind. It is a travel corridor between the tribes rather than a settlement, and canon says little more about it.
### STANDARD +
It is a minor point of interest, added with Natlan in Version 5.2, within the Tezcatepetonco Range area.

## geography
### STANDARD
The in-game description calls it a river valley. Its stretch links three known points: the Stadium of the Sacred Flame at one end, the Flower-Feather Clan at the other, and the Masters of the Night-Wind alongside its northern side. The name Xalac is of Nahuatl-style origin: roughly "sand" plus "water" with a locative ending, so something like "place of sand and water" (a wiki etymology, not an in-game explanation).
### LARGE +
Its location between the Stadium and the Flower-Feather Clan makes it a natural line of travel between two of Natlan's tribal territories. Exact boundaries, the river's name and its course are not given in canon read.

## travel
### LARGE
A notice posted in the vale advertises the Pakpaka Flying School and invites passers-by to sign up for flying lessons now rather than waiting for the next flying contest, which are held from time to time. This suggests gliding and flight training are part of local life, but the notice is the only detail given.

## atmosphere
### ULTRA
For a scene, treat it as open Natlan river country: a waterway winding between tribal territories, with travellers heading to or from the Stadium and the Flower-Feather Clan, and the Night-Wind lands visible to the north.
