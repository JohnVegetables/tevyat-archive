## summary
### LITE
Amakumo Peak is the fragmented, storm-wrapped heart of Seirai Island in Inazuma, a place of constant Electro tempests. Long ago the huge Thunderbird Kanna Kapatcir was killed over the island and her fury was held down by wards kept at the Asase Shrine. About 500 years ago the shrine's head priestess Asase Hibiki broke those wards, and the Thunderbird's lingering regret came back as the Thunder Manifestation, which still haunts the summit.
### STANDARD +
The Traveler reaches the summit in the world quest series Seirai Stormchasers, resealing four Warding Stones to calm the storm and defeating the Thunder Manifestation, and takes away a strange feather, the Peculiar Pinion, that later matters on Tsurumi Island.

## geography
### STANDARD
The summit is broken into fragments of stone ringed by storm cloud and is unlocked as part of Seirai Stormchasers, Part IV. At the foot of the peak lies a lake of electrified water patrolled by Ruin Sentinels, with a light-up tile puzzle on its shore. Sea Ganoderma, Onikabuto, Naku Weed and Amakumo Fruit are local specialties.
### LARGE +
Solving the tile puzzle drains the lake and exposes a large hole in the ground leading to flooded, maze-like underground ruins on several levels, which hold dormant Ruin Sentinels, Hydro slimes, treasure and Seelie that guide visitors through gated passages. The in-game viewpoint text says the great battle in which sea and sky changed colour is lost to time, but the vast storms called up then remain.

## places
### STANDARD
The Summit: the shattered crown of the mountain, home of the Thunder Manifestation. The Lake: electrified water that must be drained to be crossed safely. The Underground Ruins: a sunken settlement of passages and gates beneath the lake, revealed once the water is gone.

## history
### STANDARD
In the lore told by the Asase Shrine and Inazuma's weapon and artifact texts, the Thunderbird Kanna Kapatcir was a monster once revered on Tsurumi Island. Killed over Seirai Island by the Raiden Shogun (Ei), her leftover hatred and regret were suppressed by wards that the Asase Shrine maintained. Around 500 years ago, during the Seirai Rebellion, Asase Hibiki used what she had learned from the tengu to unseal them in support of Ako Domeki's crew against the Shogunate. The released power raised a vast Electro storm in the middle of the island and called up the Thunder Manifestation.
### LARGE +
The storm devastated Seirai and the villagers of Koseki Village were evacuated to Inazuma City. The wiki links the release to ships being swept far out to sea and to Domeki's fleet being stranded at the Golden Apple Archipelago. The Asase Shrine itself was left largely unaffected apart from losing its visitors. A second Thunder Manifestation later appeared on Mt. Kanna on Tsurumi Island (see that entry), and Ruu later sang to the Thunderbird in the Seirai storm, so that his old promise to her was at last kept.
### ULTRA +
The Kapatcir page records that the Thunderbird's feather appeared before Ruu when he sang, a sign that she heard him again and that the curse on the island was lifted.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Strong Electro storms and electrified water, Ruin Sentinels around the lake, hilichurls, Mitachurls, Treasure Hoarders, Nobushi and Kairagi, and above all the Thunder Manifestation on the summit.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
A permanent vortex of storm cloud and rumbling thunder, floating rock and crackling Electro: the aftermath of a battle between sky and sea that never fully ended.

## travel
### STANDARD
The island has Electro Seelie and an Electro Elemental Monument; the summit and its boss open up with Seirai Stormchasers.

## quests
### STANDARD
Seirai Stormchasers (Parts I-IV, 2.1) is the world quest series set here. The Traveler, with the Adventurers' Guild members Etsuko and Taisuke, learns about the Warding Stones from Neko, the talking cat who calls herself the Asase Shrine's provisional head priestess, seals all four stones and defeats the Thunder Manifestation.
### ULTRA +
The guild's exploration was blocked by the Balethunder storm at the island's core. Two of the four Warding Stones are on the ground and two are in the sky, reached through a Phase Gate; sealing the last pair raises a platform of floating stones on which the Thunder Manifestation waits. Once all four are sealed the storm settles, and Taisuke, excited at the thought of meeting a talking cat, insists the guild pair visit the shrine before returning to report.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the peak remains the stormy centre of Seirai Island; no later canon read for this entry changes it.
### STANDARD +
The Thunder Manifestation can still be fought there; the storm has been calmed by the sealed Warding Stones.

## entry: Thunder Manifestation and Kanna Kapatcir
@keys: Thunder Manifestation, Kanna Kapatcir, Kapatcir, Great Thunderbird, Peculiar Pinion
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Kanna Kapatcir ("Great Eagle of the Storm"), the Thunderbird, was a huge, violet-feathered bird of prey regarded as a divine guardian by the tribe of Tsurumi Island, though she paid them little attention until a boy named Ruu sang to her. His sacrifice by his own tribe drove her to destroy the mountain and everyone on it; she was later hunted down and killed by the Raiden Shogun. The Thunder Manifestation is the coagulated regret of that death, a lightning-wielding elemental being.
### LARGE +
Two Thunder Manifestations appear in canon, on Amakumo Peak and later on Mt. Kanna; the Peculiar Pinion obtained from the first lets the Traveler awaken the Thunderbird's memories on Tsurumi Island. The wiki notes that Kapatcir's appearance is never actually shown.
