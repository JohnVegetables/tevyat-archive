## summary
### LITE
The Dune of Magma is a subarea of the Hypostyle Desert in Sumeru, a remnant of King Deshret's civilisation. It connects to the underground Rock Cavern Area at the southwest of the Mausoleum of King Deshret.
### STANDARD +
Its ruins are full of puzzle machinery: Scarlet Sand Slate puzzles (full clearance needs Clearance Level 3), a Plinth of the Secret Rites, Primal Barriers, Primal Embers, Sconces and Torches, Large Fan Blades, Verity Cells, Forged Primal Light with a Replicator Keystone, a Sacred Seal, a torch puzzle and a Time Trial Challenge.

## geography
### STANDARD
Ruins in the red sands of the Hypostyle Desert, linked by passage to the Mausoleum's southwest rock cavern.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Only fungi are listed as common enemies; the main hazards are the ruins' mechanisms.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Ancient glowing machinery and torch-lit puzzles in sand-buried halls.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 it is an explorable ruin, with no later changes recorded in the sources.
### ULTRA +
The Chinese name translates as Hill of Melted Stone. The wiki lists the area as a stub, and no lore text, NPCs or quests are recorded for it.
