## summary
### LITE
Oina Beach is a subarea on the coast of Tsurumi Island in Inazuma. It has little recorded lore of its own, but in the world quest The Sun-Wheel and Mt. Kanna the Traveler uses a Thunderbird feather on a Mysterious Carving here and sees part of Kanna Kapatcir's memories.
### STANDARD +
It is the second of three places, after Wakukau Shoal and before the Autake Plains, where the feather is used.

## geography
### STANDARD
A stretch of shore on Tsurumi Island with Mysterious Carvings, Thunderstones, Thunder Barriers, Seelie and a Thunder Sakura Bough. Fluorescent Fungus grows here.
### LARGE +
Hostile groups include Ruin Cruisers, Ruin Destroyers and a Ruin Guard, plus Hilichurl Berserkers, Cryo Hilichurl Shooters, Pyro Whopperflowers, Crackling Axe Mitachurls and Electro Abyss Mages.

## history
### STANDARD
In the quest the feather draws out a vision from the Thunderbird's point of view: strange objects fell from the heavens and one landed on Tsurumi Island, after which her sky cleared and fog began to emerge. She had the power to disperse it and did not. The vision also shows the islanders gathering the feathers she shed at set intervals, a habit she found puzzling.
### LARGE +
The memory contradicts the islanders' belief that the Thunderbird made the fog to protect them. Kama remarks that his ancestors may simply have been wrong. The feather then flies off towards Mt. Kanna, which leads the party on to the Autake Plains.
### ULTRA +
Afterwards the party talks about the ancient Kama and Sayo glimpsed in earlier visions; the present-day Kama says the records do not tell whether his ancestor ever met Sayo after leaving Tsurumi.

## quests
### STANDARD
The Sun-Wheel and Mt. Kanna (2.2). Ruin Cruiser and Ruin Destroyer must be defeated, then a Ruin Guard, before the feather memory is revealed. Ruu is not found here.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Ruin Machines, hilichurls and Electro Abyss Mages; elites include Mitachurls.
