## summary
### LITE
Thunderclap Reef is a rocky reef in the southwest of Hiisi Island, Nod-Krai. Its kuuvahki has been unstable since at least 500 years ago, giving it a strong kuuvahki environment, and the Frostmoon Scions treat it as a forbidden place. Two large Moon Fragments fell there recently and made things worse.
### STANDARD +
In the world quest For a Green Island..., the Traveler and Paimon help the self-styled priestess Verna remove the fragments and partly stabilise the kuuvahki. Afterwards the weather there is perpetually rainy.

## geography
### STANDARD
A rocky reef on the southwest of Hiisi Island, with caves, a high summit and a Moon Mirror at the top. Before the quest the area is a red strong kuuvahki environment.
### LARGE +
During the quest the environment turns into a stormy one with lightning, which can be deflected with the Airspace Data Collector 2.0. After the quest it becomes a blue strong kuuvahki environment.

## people
### STANDARD
Verna, a young woman in Frostmoon robes who calls herself a priestess and the finest mechanic on Hiisi Island, works here with small helper robots named Kraka (models III, V and VII appear). She says she is a direct apprentice of the mechanic known as Papa Wrench.
### LARGE +
She describes her work as a secret mission to restore the reef's kuuvahki balance, and says she cannot bring others because they lack her resistance; her robes ward off kuuvahki, and she says she gets nosebleeds without them. A second robed woman, who speaks in cryptic prayers, waits at the Moon Mirror; Verna accuses her of impersonating a Moonchanter.

## history
### STANDARD
Verna says the kuuvahki at the reef has been unstable since the time of the first Moonchanter, Lady Aila, and that the trouble grew worse after two large rocks fell from the sky.
### LARGE +
The quest plan is to throw kuuvahki-charged fragments at the two boulders to cancel the disturbance. The first attempts flip the kuuvahki into what Verna calls a reversed chaotic state, and a third fragment, held by the robed woman at the Moon Mirror, is needed to calm it. A Frostnight Scion awakens at the mirror and must be defeated.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Strong, unstable kuuvahki, and (during the quest) lightning storms. Cave collapses also occur; one blocks the original exit while the Traveler and Paimon search for fragments.

## quests
### STANDARD
For a Green Island... (world quest, chapter Polkka Beneath the Moon's Oracle): begins automatically when approaching the reef. The wiki also lists Animal Research: Chic Badgers and Everlasting As the Moon as touching the area; they are not summarised here.
### ULTRA +
Quest rewards include Adventure EXP, Primogems, Mora, Hero's Wit and Mystic Enhancement Ore, and progress unlocks several chests, among them a Luxurious Chest behind a barrier. The wiki notes the quest can block, or be blocked by, the Archon Quest A Nation That Doesn't Exist because their locations conflict.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the reef's kuuvahki is partly stabilised but still strong, and rain is constant.

### STANDARD
In this world quest Verna recruits the Traveler and Paimon to calm the reef by throwing fragments at two boulders. The plan briefly makes the kuuvahki worse; then a robed woman holding the final fragment at the Moon Mirror lets them have it, cryptically speaking of a newborn "god" and saying she will await them at the Court of the First Edict. The kuuvahki is afterwards somewhat steadier and the weather turns rainy.
