## summary
### LITE
Chemin Oublie is a tunnel that connects the Girdle of the Sands in Sumeru to Romaritime Harbor in Fontaine. Its French name means "Forgotten Path". It is a point of interest, not a region.
### STANDARD +
It was added with Fontaine in version 4.0.

## geography
### STANDARD
A cave-like tunnel running from Sumeru's Realm of Farakhkert to the harbor in Fontaine, a large coastal elevator and observation deck beside a waterfall that also provides the passage into Sumeru. Along it are seelie to follow, a Time Trial Challenge, Trishiraite and Mourning Flower to gather, and many sea shells scattered about.
### LARGE +
Romaritime Harbor, at the Fontaine end, is a coastal elevator and observation deck on a waterfall vista, connected to the Clementine Line aquabus rail to the Court of Fontaine.

## history
### STANDARD
Canon does not say who made the tunnel. The wiki infers from the name and the many shells that Oceanids may have used it as a pilgrimage route.
### LARGE +
In Sumeru's own story, the Oceanids rejected the ideals of the new Hydro Archon Focalors, travelled to Tunigi Hollow in search of their former master Egeria, found the Harvisptokhm was not what they hoped, and scattered; people later followed their pilgrimage trail, which eventually led to the Purification Spring domain. Whether they passed through Chemin Oublie is not stated.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Hydro slimes and Hydro hilichurl rogues.

## travel
### STANDARD
The tunnel is the walking link between Sumeru and Fontaine at Romaritime Harbor.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Quiet and half-forgotten, in keeping with its name, with sea shells strewn through the cave giving a hint of the water-people who may once have passed this way.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the tunnel is an open connection between Sumeru and Fontaine.
### STANDARD +
Nothing after its 4.0 introduction has changed it.
