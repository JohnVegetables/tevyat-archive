## summary
### LITE
Wadi Al-Majuj is a canyon region south of Mt. Damavand in Sumeru's Desert of Hadramaveth. In its dark rift lie the ruins of ancient Gurabad's great gate, and a canyon, the Pairidaeza Canyon, cuts through it with Eremite camps beside it.
### STANDARD +
It was added in version 3.4. Its name combines the Arabic wadi (river valley) with 'Majuj', the desert folk's word for hilichurls.

## geography
### STANDARD
South of Mt. Damavand, with a large canyon running through it. Parts of the Pairidaeza Canyon are open to the sky and hold Eremite camps; other parts run underground over a basin of shallow water, and the canyon leads into Liloupar's Cell.
### LARGE +
Features include a cascade pool, Four-Leaf Sigil, Primal Obelisk and Ruins Torch, and a Time Trial Challenge.

## places
### STANDARD
The great gate of Gurabad, fallen into the dark valley rift, and the mansions and estates that now serve as bandit nests; the Pairidaeza Canyon; and Liloupar's Cell at the end of that canyon.
### LARGE +
The Remnants of the Ancient Orchard viewpoint describes the gate as once a place where poetry and beauty gathered, where only the cream of the crop could stand and behold their noble mortal lord. The Gap of the Hidden Ruins viewpoint says mighty Gurabad's monuments could not escape the rift, and that their mansions and estates became nests for roving bandits, adding humiliation to destruction.

## history
### STANDARD
Gurabad was a city founded by Ormazd, King Deshret's favoured vassal; the wiki says it fell after Liloupar used Abyssal power in revenge, with much of the city swallowed by a chasm and a plague after. These are the ruins at the canyon's edge.
### LARGE +
Akademiya logs from elsewhere in the desert say 'Majuj' is the desert folk's name for the creatures that Sumeru calls hilichurls, and the team came to think they were survivors of an ancient country, transformed.

## people
### STANDARD
Eremite camps lie in the canyon. At some earlier time an Eremite expedition including Jebrael, Samail and a man named Tarafa searched for Gurabad's hidden treasure.
### LARGE +
Pathfinders' Logs, written by Tarafa, mourn the party's dead: Umur lost to a cyclone near Gurabad's ruins, Bahaddin blinded by a Red Vulture, Rabeed killed in his sleep by a scorpion, and Jebrael fallen from the ruins in the gorge. Tarafa found a keystone at a third place of worship and believed the treasure must lie behind Gurabad's gate.
### ULTRA +
A sixth log, available after the Dual Evidence quest, reverses this account (see the entry on the logs).

## dangers
### STANDARD
Eremite camps, bandits among the ruins, hazards of the rift and cyclones.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the canyon is open, its Eremite camps in place and Gurabad's gate still lost in the rift.
### STANDARD +
No change to the area is recorded after version 3.4.

## entry: The Pathfinders' Logs
@keys: Pathfinders' Log, Pathfinders' Logs, Tarafa, Samail
@version: 3.4
### STANDARD
Six notes in the canyon tell of an Eremite expedition to Gurabad in which several members died. Tarafa wrote the first five, asking the reader to turn back from a cursed journey; Jebrael and Samail are named as the last survivors alongside him. Collecting all six grants the hidden achievement Genesis of the Rift.

## entry: Pathfinders' Log VI
@keys: Genesis of the Rift, Dual Evidence
@keys2: Tarafa, Samail, Jebrael
@version: 3.4
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
The sixth log, written by a member who says he faked his own death, apparently Jebrael whose fall Tarafa recorded, accuses Tarafa of betraying the team and causing the deaths of Umur, Bahaddin and Rabeed, and of nearly attacking Samail. It also calls the stone slate Babel's ploy and fears Gurabad holds no treasure. It is available after the Dual Evidence quest.
