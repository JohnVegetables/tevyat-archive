## summary
### LITE
Mt. Kanna is the mountain at the heart of Tsurumi Island, a hollow cavern from which, in the account Kama gives, the great Thunderbird Kanna Kapatcir would launch into the sky like a violet meteor visible even through the fog. An altar to her stands inside. It is where the Traveler finishes the Tsurumi story by fighting a Thunder Manifestation and helping the boy Ruu keep an old promise.
### STANDARD +
The mountain is the last stop of the world quest The Sun-Wheel and Mt. Kanna (2.2), the end of the series Through the Mists.

## geography
### STANDARD
A mountain in the middle of Tsurumi Island with a vast hollow cavern inside, holding an altar and a tree. A viewpoint near its entrance appears after the quest. Fluorescent Fungus grows on the island; the area is patrolled by Electro Hilichurl Shooters, Geo slimes, Pyro Whopperflowers, Crackling Axe Mitachurls and Electro Abyss Mages.
### LARGE +
Tsurumi Island is otherwise swathed in fog and, in the game, repeats a three-day cycle that changes enemy and chest placement until the story is finished; the mountain is the island's focal point.

## history
### STANDARD
The tribe of the Thunderbird Civilization revered Kapatcir and held a ceremony for her. According to the memories shown in the quest, Kapatcir paid them little heed until a boy named Ruu sang to her and gave her the name Kanna Kapatcir. When the tribe sacrificed him, she razed the mountain in anger and swore that the land would suffer until she heard his song again.
### LARGE +
Millennia later, when the Traveler uses the Peculiar Pinion at the altar, a Thunder Manifestation, the Thunderbird's coagulated regret, emerges from the cavern and is defeated. Ruu, who has waited on Tsurumi Island all that time, learns the truth about the Thunderbird, sings to her at Seirai Island with the Traveler and Paimon, and returns with a feather of his own that revives the altar's tree.

## quests
### STANDARD
The Sun-Wheel and Mt. Kanna (2.2), the fourth part of Through the Mists. The Traveler first touches Thunderbird feathers at Wakukau Shoal, Oina Beach and the Autake Plains, each one showing a piece of her memories, before the feather leads to the altar here.
### LARGE +
After Ruu sings, the Thunderbird's storm over Tsurumi ends and the fog is permanently cleared. Ruu chooses to stay and rest on the island for now, asking the Traveler to take the Thunderbird's feather along on their travels. The quest ends with the Traveler reporting to Sumida and Kama.
### ULTRA +
On the first visit here the Thunder Manifestation is called Coagulated Millennial Regret in the quest, and as it fights it speaks of Ruu's song. When the Traveler later plays back the Pinion, the Thunderbird's own words are heard: because the one song was allowed to flow in blood into the earth, the land would remain under catastrophe until she heard Ruu's song again.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Electro enemies and lightning during the quest, hilichurl camps, Electro Abyss Mages and Mitachurls, and the Thunder Manifestation itself. Before the quest the fog sends travellers back to the Statue of The Seven.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
A hush inside a hollow mountain, like a temple for something that has long since left. Fog thins at the entrance; the tree at the altar is the sign of what was lost and restored.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the fog and thunderstorm are gone and the altar's tree has leaves again; no later canon read for this entry changes this.
### STANDARD +
Ruu remains resting on Tsurumi Island at the end of the story.
