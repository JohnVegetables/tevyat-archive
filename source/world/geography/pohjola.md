## summary
### LITE
Pohjola is the large northern island of Nod-Krai, lying north of the Southern Isles. It is made up of Voidsea Outlook in the south, Wavechaser Plain and Ashveil Peak in the north and, by the wiki's grouping, Dunanna Pit. The Lightkeepers' city Piramida stands on it, and the frontier's abyssal and Wild Hunt dangers are felt here. Its name is borrowed from the cold northern land of Finnish mythology.
### STANDARD +
Voidsea Outlook, Wavechaser Plain and Ashveil Peak opened with Version 6.3 (Luna IV); Dunanna Pit came in 6.7. The old Hymns of the Far North place a passage into the Shadow Realm on Pohjola, but that book is an unreliable source.

## geography
### STANDARD
Voidsea Outlook lies at the southern end of the island, just north of Paha Isle in the Southern Isles, and holds Piramida and Dreadshade Mire. Wavechaser Plain lies to its north and contains Lake Amsvartnir, Favonius Keep and the Pillar of Embla. Ashveil Peak is the northernmost part of the island and includes Kipumaki Cliff. Dunanna Pit is listed with Pohjola but is described elsewhere as east of it and close to Fontaine.
### LARGE +
Dunanna Pit has no Meeting Point of its own; it shares the Ancient Moon's Remnant at the Nuur Stone Circle with the Lunar Highlands to unlock the Luna Treasure Compass. Local wildlife and enemies differ by area: Wavechaser Plain has Pale-Furred Wolves and a Radiant Moongecko, while Ashveil Peak has Wilderness Exiles, Wilderness Hunters and Fishers of Hidden Depths.

## places
### STANDARD
Piramida, the Lightkeepers' headquarters, is a city built around a giant golden-age lighthouse, itself raised on the ruins of an older castle. North of it, Lake Amsvartnir hides the Royal Court of the Seelie. Favonius Keep in Wavechaser Plain is a Meeting Point; the Pillar of Embla also stands in this area. In Ashveil Peak, Lightkeeper allies appear and a boss called the Lord of the Hidden Depths: Whisperer of Nightmares is found.
### LARGE +
Beryna's oven in Voidsea Outlook can be used to bake a mushroom dish, a small touch of frontier daily life. Ashveil Peak's Kipumaki Cliff is a separate map section of the area.

## history
### STANDARD
Pohjola is named in the Hymns of the Far North. Its Prayer Song XVI says a young man named Saarelainen crossed the isle of Hiisi and the mist-shrouded lands of Pohjola to court Anastasya, and that the old man Pakkaisukko (the hymn's name for the Belyi Tsar) would grant her hand only if Saarelainen opened the gates of Pohjola, which the wiki links to the Shadow Realm. The prayer says a Lord of the Seven Calamities carved Saarelainen into seven pieces there.
### LARGE +
The Black Feathers Scattered at Parting Archon Quest in 7.1 draws on this tradition. The wiki cautions that the book's details about Anastasya and Saarelainen are of dubious veracity, because the Heavenly Principles altered Irminsul, and that some claims, such as Anastasya being the Belyi Tsar's daughter, are demonstrably wrong.
### ULTRA +
Wavechaser Plain also keeps the memory of the Mondstadt knight Roland's campaign: four Lost Notes written by one of his followers record that the party marched west after Natlan, met the Lightkeepers (whose offer to join Roland refused) and were later sheltered in a lighthouse they offered. The writer ends alone, holding the lighthouse after a brutal fight, and asks the wolf that came with them from Mondstadt to see him out. The notes give no dates.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Cold, wind-swept and haunted by old war: ruins, lighthouses and camps of people who keep watch against abyssal monsters. The name itself recalls the hostile northern land of Finnish epic.

## dangers
### LARGE
Abyssal creatures, the Wild Hunt and bandits threaten the frontier, which is why the Lightkeepers keep camps and outposts. Wolves, exiles and hunters roam the plain and the peak, and Ashveil Peak holds a boss-level horror from the hidden depths.

## travel
### LARGE
Travellers reach Pohjola from the Southern Isles, with Voidsea Outlook the nearest stretch. Piramida is the main waypoint; Favonius Keep and Cliffwatch Camp are Meeting Points added in 6.3. Dunanna Pit is reached via the Nuur Stone Circle shared with the Lunar Highlands.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 Pohjola is open for travel and exploration, and the Lightkeepers hold it against the dark.
### STANDARD +
The island's Shadow Realm gate legend resurfaced in the 7.1 Archon Quest, but its truth is still contested by the sources.

## entry: Voidsea Outlook and Piramida
@keys: Voidsea Outlook, Piramida, Dreadshade Mire, Torchforger Statue
@version: 6.3
### STANDARD
Voidsea Outlook sits at Pohjola's southern end, north of Paha Isle, and contains Piramida, the Lightkeepers' headquarters city, and Dreadshade Mire. The Lightkeepers revere a statue of their founding Starshyna, Solovei the Torchforger. Piramida was built on the ruins of the castle of Prokhor Trubetskoy, around a golden-age lighthouse.
### LARGE +
The Fatui once marched to the gates as reinforcements when the Lightkeepers were about to face the Wild Hunt.

## entry: Wavechaser Plain and Roland's Follower
@keys: Wavechaser Plain, Pillar of Embla, Roland's follower, Lost Notes
@keys2: Nod-Krai, Pohjola
@version: 6.3
### STANDARD
Wavechaser Plain holds Favonius Keep, the Pillar of Embla and Lake Amsvartnir. Local specialties are Winter Icelea and Pine Amber, and Pale-Furred Wolves roam the plain. Lost Notes found here record a Mondstadt follower of the knight Roland.
### LARGE +
The notes show Roland's campaign continued west after Natlan, that he refused the Lightkeepers' invitation to join them, and that the follower died after refusing local aid.

## entry: Ashveil Peak
@keys: Ashveil Peak, Kipumaki Cliff, Whisperer of Nightmares
@version: 6.3
### STANDARD
The northernmost part of Pohjola, with Kipumaki Cliff. Wilderness Exiles, Wilderness Hunters and Fishers of Hidden Depths are common, Lightkeeper NPCs appear, and the Lord of the Hidden Depths: Whisperer of Nightmares is the area's boss.

## entry: Dunanna Pit
@keys: Dunanna Pit, Dunanna Forward Station
@version: 6.7
### STANDARD
Dunanna Pit opened in 6.7 (Luna VIII) and is close to Fontaine, east of Pohjola. It shares the Ancient Moon's Remnant at the Nuur Stone Circle with the Lunar Highlands rather than having its own Meeting Point. The name likely combines Sumerian roots for building and the moon god Nanna.

## entry: Gates of Pohjola
@keys: Gates of Pohjola, Pakkaisukko, Prayer Song XVI
@keys2: Saarelainen, Anastasya, Shadow Realm
@version: 7.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
According to the Hymns of the Far North, Pohjola was where a passage into the Shadow Realm was opened by Anastasya and Saarelainen. In the prayer, the bride's father Pakkaisukko, the Belyi Tsar in the hymn, set opening the gates as the price of her hand, and Saarelainen went knowing the scheme. The book is unreliable, so its account is tradition, not fact.
