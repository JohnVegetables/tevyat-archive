## summary
### LITE
Loch Urania is a lake in Erinnyes Forest, Fontaine, containing a large water vortex that forms a big bubble at the top. It starts out tainted by contaminated bacterial mats; clearing them calms the water and the sky.
### STANDARD +
It is the starting place of the world quest Furious Mouth of the Spring, part of The Wild Fairy of Erinnyes, where an enraged vishap and a small Melusine called Pahsiv guard a "key".

## geography
### STANDARD
A lake in Erinnyes Forest with a giant vortex whose top forms a bubble. An altar sits near the "mouth of the spring" in the water, with a vishap lying beside it.
### LARGE +
While tainted, strong high-speed winds push travellers away and the sky is dark even in daytime. The contaminated cores can only be cleansed with the abilities of Xenochromatic Creatures; after the lake is cleared the winds fade and the sky brightens.
### ULTRA +
Cleansing the two Contaminated Bacterial Mats in the water is one way to start Furious Mouth of the Spring; completing the nearby quests Foggy Forest Path or Wilting Weeping Willow also starts it.

## dangers
### STANDARD
The enraged Primordial Bathysmal Vishap by the altar attacks anyone who disturbs the altar. Before cleansing, the winds make approaching difficult.
### LARGE +
The vishap is a guardian of the key rather than a villain: Pahsiv says a "purple thing" stole a key and left it fainted, and she calms it with a touch.

## quests
@version: 4.2
### STANDARD
Furious Mouth of the Spring (World Quest, 4.2) begins here. The Traveler subdues the vishap and meets Pahsiv, who speaks in a few repeated words. She asks for help gathering three keys (misty path to the south, the spring to the north, willows to the east) and taking them to the square in the west, to protect Erinnyes. The quest has variants depending on how many Teardrop Keys the player already has.
### LARGE +
Pahsiv calls Erinnyes her home, though Melusine are normally associated with the Court of Fontaine and Merusea Village. She mentions a "visitor" who teaches her language and wisdom; the visitor's identity belongs to the later Erinnyes questline.

## status
### STANDARD
Calm and navigable once cleansed; no later change is recorded as of 7.1.

### STANDARD
Pahsiv is the name Paimon gives a small Melusine met at Loch Urania who speaks in short fragments ("Pahsiv" works as yes and "Melusine" as no in her usage). She guards keys with the vishap and asks the Traveler to help protect Erinnyes. She is a quest character; do not expand her beyond the quest.
