## summary
### LITE
The Dune of Elusion is a subarea of the Hypostyle Desert in Sumeru, one of the ruins of King Deshret's civilisation. It contains the boss Algorithm of Semi-Intransient Matrix of Overseer Network.
### STANDARD +
Exploration here includes a Mysterious Mural and a Four-Leaf Sigil. The ruins south of the dune are one of the places where Primal Constructs must be defeated for the hidden exploration objective The Dead End and the Glinting Architects, which requires the quest Dual Evidence.

## geography
### STANDARD
A desert hill with ruined structures belonging to the vanished civilisation of King Deshret, in the Hypostyle Desert of the Great Red Sand. The southernmost ruins of the Hypostyle Desert lie south of the dune.

## dangers
### STANDARD
The Algorithm of Semi-Intransient Matrix of Overseer Network, a boss, guards the site; ruin machines called Primal Constructs are found in the ruins to its south.

## quests
### STANDARD
The Dead End and the Glinting Architects (an unofficial wiki name for a hidden exploration objective, available after Dual Evidence).

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Wind-worn stone and sand over machinery older than the desert kingdoms.
