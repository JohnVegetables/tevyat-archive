## summary
### LITE
Pairidaeza Canyon is a point of interest in Sumeru's Great Red Sand, a canyon that cuts through Wadi Al-Majuj in the Desert of Hadramaveth. It was added in Version 3.4.
### STANDARD +
Parts of the canyon lie open to the sky and hold some Eremite camps; other parts run underground and contain a basin of shallow water. The canyon leads into Liloupar's Cell.

## geography
### STANDARD
A cut through the desert terrain of Wadi Al-Majuj, with an exposed, sunlit stretch and a darker underground stretch with a shallow water basin. Its inner end connects to Liloupar's Cell.

## people
### STANDARD
Some Eremite camps stand in the open parts of the canyon.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the canyon is an ordinary explorable point of interest.
### LARGE +
Canon says little about it: no history of the canyon is recorded.
### ULTRA +
Its Chinese name means Oasis Canyon; the English name is a transliteration. Apart from Liloupar's Cell beyond it, nothing ties a quest or character to the canyon.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Scenes here can contrast bright, exposed desert rock and Eremite camps with cool, damp underground passages.

## entry: Liloupar's Cell connection
@keys: Pairidaeza Canyon, Liloupar's Cell
### STANDARD
The canyon is the approach to Liloupar's Cell. That site's appearance changes once it is discovered during the quest The Temple Where Sand Flows Like Tears, per the wiki's Liloupar's Cell page.
