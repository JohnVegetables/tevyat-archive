## summary
### LITE
Dryas Wood is a small hollow in Fontaine between the Fontaine Research Institute and Erinnyes Forest. It used to be the Institute's "backyard", where plant nurseries stood, and it is said that the potioneer who devised the "Pure Water" formula once lived here in seclusion. It is now abandoned.
### STANDARD +
Its main role in canon is as the setting of the world quest Villains (4.1), in which a girl named Blanche searches the hollow for her brother, who picked up the Pure Water formula in the wilderness after the Institute's explosion. The visitors find an old cabin that speaks.

## geography
### STANDARD
A small, sheltered hollow nestled between the old Fontaine Research Institute site and the edge of Erinnyes Forest. Rainbow Rose grows here as a local specialty.
### LARGE +
A potioneer's cabin stands in the hollow. Nothing remains of the nurseries that once filled it, and the place looks long unused.

## history
### STANDARD
The hollow served as the Institute's backyard and held assorted plant nurseries. After the Institute's explosion, many things were scattered across the wilderness, which is how Blanche's brother came by the Pure Water formula.
### LARGE +
Tradition names a talented but withdrawn potion and alchemy prodigy at the Institute, sidelined because the Institute's focus lay elsewhere, who moved into seclusion in the mountains and invented several remarkable potions. That tale is told by an antagonist in the quest and is framed as legend. The potioneer is never named.
### ULTRA +
In the quest the cabin itself claims that its previous occupant received Pure Water as a reward from a "World of Purity" and grew special fruit with it; the Traveler's party treats this as a falsehood. The legend told later is of discarded potions forming a pond from which a small flower grew, which some say was the Hydro Archon's doing. Treat both versions as unverified stories.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Quiet, overgrown and forgotten: a secluded hideaway close to ruined research grounds, with an empty cabin and no sign of the nurseries.

## quests
### STANDARD
Villains (World Quest, 4.1, Fontaine Research Institute region) takes place here. Its reward includes the Pure Water formula.
### LARGE +
Characters in the quest include Blanche and the Fruity Order operatives who go by fruit and flower code names ("Guileless Wolfhook", "Ruthless Calla Lily", "Trendy Zaytun Peach", "Tricky Bulle Fruit"). Do not add detail beyond what the quest shows.

## travel
### STANDARD
Lies between the Institute region and the edge of Erinnyes Forest.

## status
### STANDARD
Abandoned as of 7.1; no change is recorded since its introduction in 4.1.

## entry: Pure Water (formula)
@keys: Pure Water, Pure Water formula
@keys2: Dryas Wood, potioneer
@version: 4.1
### STANDARD
"Pure Water" is a potion formula credited to a potioneer of the Fontaine Research Institute who is said to have lived in seclusion at Dryas Wood. Copies were scattered after the Institute exploded; in the quest Villains the formula is deciphered and treated as the Institute's inheritance, to be sent to the new Fontaine Research Institute. The quest also notes the potion sells well in the Fortress of Meropide.
