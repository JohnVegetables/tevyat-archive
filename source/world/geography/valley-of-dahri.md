## summary
### LITE
The Valley of Dahri is a wind-eroded valley in the Land of Upper Setekh, in Sumeru's Great Red Sand. A huge, sealed Ruin Golem, a war machine from Khaenri'ah (called Dahri in old Sumeru), lies in it. Scattered Akademiya research notes tell of the desert's strange hilichurl-like creatures and the Abyss.
### STANDARD +
The Valley and its golem feature in the world quest Afratu's Dilemma, added in version 3.1, in which the Traveler silences the machine's autonomous guns.

## geography
### STANDARD
Open wind-carved country in the Land of Upper Setekh, so named because the land lies toward the sun at noon. A cavern north of the area's Statue of The Seven leads into a Ruin Golem part, and the golem itself towers over the valley.
### LARGE +
Of the three Ruin Golems the wiki counts in Sumeru, this one is in worse repair than the Hadramaveth golem; its eye cannon can be operated freely by hand once the autonomous system is off. The viewpoint, The Giant of the Wind-Eroded Valley, appears after Afratu's Dilemma and treats the swarms from the Abyss as nearly poetic exaggeration beside such a mountain-breaking power.

## places
### STANDARD
The Ruin Golem, and the cavern and arm passages through which one enters it, past a seal of three Suda's Flow clusters. Inside lie the cockpit and old orders written by the machine's former crew.
### LARGE +
Notes left by Akademiya investigation teams survive in the valley. One report tells of a sage-made seal called Suda's Flow and of the hope of imitating the vein-like Vamadha seal; another says the creatures the desert folk call Majuj (hilichurls) show many traces, and that the Temple of Silence ordered every Khaenri'ahn machine sealed according to the Revelatory Monument's format.
### ULTRA +
A torn page hidden here belongs to the scholar Zandik. He writes that the golem's main guns could be repaired from abandoned parts, and that he has kept this from the rest of the team. He dismisses Suda's Flow as mere sediment from disturbed elements, absurd as the core of a seal.

## people
### STANDARD
No settlement. In Afratu's Dilemma the Vahumana Mahamata Afratu, on temporary assignment, asks adventurers to deal with the golem because the Eremites she commissioned from the Citadel of Regzar have been slow to arrive.
### LARGE +
Afratu says Dahri is an old name for Khaenri'ah that Vahumana Darshan scholars use, and that Schwanenritter once piloted these machines against monsters from the Abyss. Three golems survived that war and were sealed by sages.

## history
### STANDARD
The golem's old orders are the main record of its past. They name a Knight Commander Hadura, honoured as Sentinel of the Golden Hall for stalling the beasts, under Marshal Regent Anfortas. Sealed orders tell the crew to abandon the machine for the Marshal's machine and to leave the wounded with supplies in a safe place, with the promise to return for them.
### LARGE +
A badly burned medical report describes an ailment of unknown cause: peeling skin, thick hair, severe facial ulceration, impaired language, and in the first cases lost rational capacity, with patients gathered for central treatment. Nothing places the patients or the date.
### ULTRA +
An inscription at a Primal Obelisk in the valley records that Zumurud was said to have ninety-nine children, among them Badanah and Muzzudin, and that the desert kingdoms entered a golden age under them (the Gurabad wiki cites this obelisk).

## dangers
### STANDARD
Before the quest, the golem fires beams indiscriminately at anything living near it. Inside: Ruin Guards, fungi, Dendro Samachurls, and Wooden Shieldwall Mitachurls.

## travel
### LARGE
The golem's guns can be baited into blasting open the rock that blocks the entrance. The scarab is the valley's local specialty.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the golem lies silent in its valley, its autonomous guns off after Afratu's Dilemma.
### STANDARD +
Afratu reported the anomaly to the Akademiya, and expects the other sealed golems to show problems if ley lines are the cause.

## entry: Afratu's Dilemma
@keys: Afratu's Dilemma, Mahamata Afratu
@version: 3.1
### STANDARD
World quest in the Valley of Dahri. The Traveler and Paimon find the Mahamata Afratu worried about a Ruin Golem that has begun to attack. They bait it into blasting the entrance open, break the three Suda's Flow seals, and switch off its autonomous targeting from the cockpit. Vines inside suggest a ley line cause to Afratu, who passes the report to the Akademiya.
