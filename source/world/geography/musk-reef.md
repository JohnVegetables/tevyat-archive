## summary
### LITE
Musk Reef is a small island off the coast of Galesong Hill in Mondstadt. Its main feature is the portal to the Spiral Abyss, and it is reached through a bluish-purple time tunnel at Cape Oath (or by gliding there from the cape). Long ago, before Barbatos reshaped the land, it was the summit of a great mountain called Pilos Peak.
### STANDARD +
The island is also tied to the adventurer Leonard, who failed to climb Pilos Peak and later invented the wind glider. Long after his time, his lingering will fell on the reef during the Unreconciled Stars event.

## geography
### STANDARD
A rocky islet rising above the sea off Cape Oath, within Galesong Hill. It holds the Spiral Abyss domain entrance, an Anemo elemental monument and Anemograna to collect. Visitors arrive by stepping into the time tunnel (wormhole) at the edge of Cape Oath, which the wiki lists as a one-way link, or by gliding across from the cape.
### LARGE +
The reef is the surviving tip of a vanished mountain: Mona observes that its summit still reaches just above the water. In Leonard's day the peak lay under heavy snow, as his journal describes; today only a modest island stands above the waves.

## places
### STANDARD
The Spiral Abyss portal is the one significant feature on the island. Entry becomes available once a player reaches Adventure Rank 20 (a gameplay requirement; the Abyss itself is described in its own entry).

## history
### STANDARD
Before Barbatos became the Anemo Archon, about 2,600 years ago, the area was part of the continent and the reef was the summit of Pilos Peak. The adventurer Leonard tried to reach the top but never managed it. When Barbatos raised the winds to clear the ice and snow from the land and reshaped Mondstadt, the mountain was swept from its place, the meltwater became sea, and its peak became the island now called Musk Reef.
### LARGE +
Leonard recorded his attempt in a journal, "Of Mountains and Seas" (a copy is in the Mondstadt library), and mentions help from Venerare of the Lawrence Clan in getting across to the peak. He died before achieving his dream. His other legacy is the wind glider, built so that people could go where their legs could not carry them.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Canon describes no ordinary dangers on the island. Its one notable hazard was the meteorite core during the Unreconciled Stars event (see the sub-entry below), which also drew Fatui agents.

## travel
### STANDARD
Cape Oath hosts the time tunnel that leads here. Gliding off the cape is the alternative route. The usual reason to visit is to reach the Spiral Abyss.

## quests
### LARGE
Musk Reef features in the 1.1 Unreconciled Stars event quest "Where Ancient Stars Align" (see the sub-entry below) and as the Spiral Abyss location.

### ULTRA +
The Unreconciled Stars event placed its largest ordinary meteorite, the "heart of darkness" in Fischl's words, at Falcon Coast, not on the reef; the meteorite core that carried Leonard's will is the one destroyed at Musk Reef in the final event quest. Fischl was the event's featured character.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 Musk Reef remains an ordinary accessible island in Mondstadt, still holding the Spiral Abyss portal. The meteorite crisis of 1.1 ended long ago. Nothing in Nod-Krai (6.x) or Snezhnaya (7.0-7.1) content changes it.

## entry: Leonard and Pilos Peak
@keys: Pilos Peak, Of Mountains and Seas
@keys2: Musk Reef, Leonard, mountain, summit
### STANDARD
Pilos Peak was the mountain Leonard could never climb. In his journal he writes that by forty he had conquered every domain, but the peak alone defeated him, and fear of the cold, snow-buried summit cost him his courage as a climber. He turned to building tools, reasoning that where legs fail, wings might carry. That ambition produced the wind glider. The journal also thanks Venerare, of the Lawrence Clan of his day, for help in getting across to the mountain.
### LARGE +
Mona, who reconstructed the missing parts of the book with her astrolabe, dates him roughly two thousand years back; the wiki argues for about 2,600 years. Barbatos' reshaping of the land later turned the mountain into the reef.

## entry: Unreconciled Stars at Musk Reef
@keys: Unreconciled Stars, meteorite core, Where Ancient Stars Align
@keys2: Musk Reef, Mona, Fischl, Leonard
@version: 1.1
@availability: limited
### LARGE
During the 1.1 event Unreconciled Stars (a limited-time event), meteorites fell across Mondstadt and put those who touched them into a deep sleep in which they felt driven to climb a mountain. Mona identified them as the fallen constellation of an ancient adventurer, Leonard, carrying his unfulfilled will. She and the Traveler found his journal, worked out that the largest meteorite, the core, would have fallen on the summit he never reached, and went to Musk Reef. Fatui agents were there; the Fatui Harbinger Scaramouche, who had entered the dream, left shaken, claiming to have discovered something about the sky. The Traveler and Mona fought off the agents and destroyed the core, ending the dreams.
