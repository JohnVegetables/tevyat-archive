## summary
### LITE
Asipattravana Swamp is a thorn-filled canyon swamp just north of Vourukasha Oasis in Sumeru's Realm of Farakhkert. It is guarded by Mihir, a Pari called a familiar of thorns. In the quest Asipattravana Itihasa it is smothered by a purple mist of Abyssal origin until the Traveler, Paimon and the Pari Sorush cleanse it.
### STANDARD +
The name comes from the Sanskrit asipatravana, the forest whose leaves are swords, one of the hells of Naraka.

## geography
### STANDARD
A swamp in a canyon thick with thorns, immediately north of the Oasis. Local life includes consecrated horned crocodiles, and the Mourning Flower is the area's local specialty.
### LARGE +
It sits in the Girdle of the Sands, in the same Realm of Farakhkert area as the Oasis.

## history
### STANDARD
When the Traveler and Paimon arrive with Sorush, the swamp is blanketed in Abyssal mist. To protect it, Mihir had sealed herself and the source of the Abyssal energy behind a Gray Gate. The Traveler and Sorush awaken Khvarena to disperse the mist, cleanse the defilement and free her.
### LARGE +
Their reason for coming is the Chaplet: they need the help of Mihir and Rashnu to obtain it. The quest also touches the Pari principle that every Pari has a fated responsibility which must be carried out alone. Mihir's duty is the swamp.
### ULTRA +
The wiki's etymology note says asipatravana names a hell for those who cut down trees to kill people they held grudges against, where sword-shaped leaves cut off limbs and noses in fierce winds. The in-game place has only the name in common; do not describe it as punishment of the dead.

## people
### STANDARD
Mihir, a Pari described as a familiar of thorns. After the quest she tells the Traveler that the Oasis's sights are as they always were and says Sorush is dedicated though her methods need refining.
### LARGE +
After the Oasis's crisis ends in The Hymn of Tir Yazad (Part 2), Mihir says peace has returned for now and that she will finish resting before she turns to her many tasks.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Abyssal mist and defilement, consecrated horned crocodiles, and a Gray Gate that blocked the source of the energy until the quest cleared it.

## quests
### STANDARD
Asipattravana Itihasa (3.6), part of Khvarena of Good and Evil. Sorush and the Traveler follow floating Khvarena through the swamp, awaken its power to disperse the mist, and free Mihir.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Thorny and enclosed, with a purple haze of Abyssal mist before the quest. The name hints at a place of sword-like leaves and punishment, but the real situation is a guardian shutting herself in to protect others.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the swamp is clear of the purple mist and Mihir is free.
### STANDARD +
Mihir stays to rest. Nothing after 3.6 has changed the area.

### STANDARD
A Pari known as a familiar of thorns who guards Asipattravana Swamp. During the Abyssal mist she locked herself and the source of the energy behind a Gray Gate. Freed, she speaks in a formal, measured way.
### LARGE +
Her post-quest lines speak of Pari responsibilities as things that come naturally, and she treats Sorush as dedicated but rough.
