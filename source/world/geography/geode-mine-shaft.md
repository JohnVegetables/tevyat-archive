## summary
### LITE
The Geode Mine Shaft is an abandoned mine in the Fortress of Meropide's abandoned production zone, filled with Arkhium crystal and patrolled by Flying Energy Thieves. It is the escape route used in the world quest An Eye for an Eye.
### STANDARD +
Mining stopped after the Fontaine Research Institute accident. A locking research terminal at the shaft's front opens only with two people working together, and the way out is rigged with a hazard.

## geography
### STANDARD
Reached from the abandoned production zone level of the Fortress, beyond sluice gates and a pipe, in a setting that looks nothing like the rest of the Fortress: no signs of ordinary buildings, only rock, tunnels and glittering crystal.
### LARGE +
The walls are studded with Arkhium crystals, which Lanoire mistakes at first for gems. Caterpillar identifies them as Arkhium.
### ULTRA +
Arkhium mining here ended with the Fontaine Research Institute accident, and the mine has been left to the Flying Energy Thieves since.

## dangers
### STANDARD
A Flying Energy Thief guards the shaft: a "trial product" that pursues anyone carrying an energy storage device and disables the device. The front research terminal needs such a device to start, so whoever activates it is chased.
### LARGE +
A posted notice warns that the lock needs two people at once, that the energy storage unit has been removed from the terminal, and that the manufacturer is not liable for loss of control or explosions. It urges anyone to turn back, saying production-zone work is hard but not life-threatening. Its tone is bureaucratic and darkly sarcastic.

## history
### STANDARD
Arkhium mining here halted after the Fontaine Research Institute accident. Later, guards had Clynes install a lock like the one at the Rift of Erosion; Clynes mentioned having prepared a "surprise" there, and the notice at the terminal describes the Flying Energy Thief.

## quests
@version: 4.1
### STANDARD
In An Eye for an Eye (4.1, chapter Unfinished Comedy), Caterpillar decides to take Lanoire out of the Fortress and back to her family via this shaft. The Traveler and Paimon help, race the Flying Energy Thief and open the way out. On the other side Lanoire sees the open outdoors for the first time.
### LARGE +
Caterpillar says he can never be truly human but imitates human kindness, and that he believes his sins can never be absolved, which is why he acts. See his own entry rather than repeating his story here.

## status
### STANDARD
Disused as a mine and known only as the route in the quest, as of 7.1.
