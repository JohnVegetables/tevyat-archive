## summary
### LITE
Devantaka Mountain is a mountain in Sumeru's Ardravi Valley, in the Dharma Forest, home to a massive Ruin Golem built in Khaenri'ah's time. The scholar Pir Kavikavus of the Akademiya's Kshahrewar once studied the machine and suffered a terrible accident, and the Akasha System gets no signal on the mountain. The official description calls it the mountain that saw the fall of the demons.
### STANDARD +
The name probably references Devantaka, a Hindu asura whose name means destroyer of gods. The Ruin Golem was the setting of the Vimana Agama world quests, in which a Kshahrewar researcher called Jazari studies it.

## geography
### STANDARD
Valleys and greenery hide a giant machine whose circular holes seem to watch time pass. The Ruin Golem has three levels of rooms and corridors, with energy blocks, large gears and lifts; caves run beneath and behind it, and below it lies Dev's Cavern. Ruin Drakes, Ruin Graders and an Aeonblight Drake are the main hostile machines and beasts.
### LARGE +
Local specialties are Nilotpala Lotus and Rukkhashava Mushrooms. Breaking Dendrogranum-locked rocks can release a Ruin Scout that leads to more ancient automatons and a chest.

## places
### STANDARD
The Ruin Golem: one of three known in Sumeru, the others being at the Valley of Dahri and the Desert of Hadramaveth. Dev's Cavern: a cavern beneath the mountain where the Golem's core was removed and sealed, now also where the Abyss Order seeks power. The Ruin Golem's top floor and side chambers hold notes from the Akademiya investigation team's Zandik.
### LARGE +
Inside, an Ancient Work Log records defects: energy cores stopping from overload, a weapons system melted from overuse, a smart boot system failing and walking ability lost. Mysterious Chronicles record the fates of three knights of the Schwanenritter: Hildric (death from organ failure in a foreign land), Hadura (executed by Knight Marshal Anfortas for a betrayal that caused irreversible damage to a machine; the Marshal lost his left eye) and Ynghildr (missing in battle against the onslaught of dark beasts).

## history
### STANDARD
The Ruin Golem was a Khaenri'ahn war machine whose core was removed and sealed in Dev's Cavern. Hundreds of years on, the Akademiya's Kshahrewar studied it. Pir Kavikavus joined Kshahrewar after the cataclysm of 500 years ago, rose to Herbad before thirty, and became the last person known to enter the Golem for systematic research before an accident erased his work. Kshahrewar's prestige fell, and the Akademiya has not raised its funding since.
### LARGE +
Kavikavus was a friend of the Aranara Aralohita, who helped him and later hid some of his research behind riddles and puzzles. In the Pir Kavikavus Prize, named for him, scholars under forty are honoured. Many in Kshahrewar still admire him, including Jazari, who hopes to follow in his steps.
### ULTRA +
The wiki records that Kavikavus discovered the Akasha System has no signal on Devantaka Mountain; the Bimarstan proverb column quotes him on it.

## dangers
### STANDARD
The Golem's Chaos Core leaked energy and polluted the nearby forest until it was sealed. Monsters gathered at the machine; Abyss Order mages sought the core's power; Eremites, hilichurls, Rishboland tigers and fungi are also present.
### LARGE +
Ynghildr, a Khaenri'ahn knight cursed into an Abyssal creature, blocks the Traveler from the Golem's controls in Vimana Agama: Jazari's Chapter.

## quests
### STANDARD
Vimana Agama: Ararycan, an Aranara, investigates a contamination around Devantaka Mountain with the Traveler and meets the Kshahrewar researcher Jazari. Jazari cannot see Ararycan, who explains that humans lose the ability as they grow older. Jazari's chapter has the Traveler reactivate the Ruin Golem enough for its arm to smash the ground and reopen Dev's Cavern; the Golem then breaks down again. In Until Vana is Healed, the Traveler finds Kavikavus's hidden research paper, brings it to Jazari and helps seal the Golem's Chaos Core, weakening the Abyss Order's threat to the forest.
### LARGE +
The two quests use the mountain as stage and make Jazari the keeper of its research, while Ararycan tends the surrounding wood.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
A green and quiet valley slope with a colossal rusted machine watching over it: the official wording stresses a machine that has lost its light. Sumeru scholars and Aranara regard the place with awe and some sorrow.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the Golem still stands idle, its core sealed, and Jazari researches it.
### STANDARD +
Nothing after 3.2 has changed the mountain, and no later Sumeru or Snezhnaya material in 6.x-7.1 references it.

## entry: Pir Kavikavus

@version: 3.0
### STANDARD
Historical Kshahrewar prodigy who became a Herbad before thirty and was the last known person to research the Ruin Golem on Devantaka Mountain. An accident lost his research and he probably died. He was friends with the Aranara Aralohita, who hid some of his work behind puzzles.
### LARGE +
A prize for scholars under forty is named after him. His paper on sealing the Golem's Chaos Core was found in Until Vana is Healed and tested by Jazari.

## entry: Dev's Cavern and the Chaos Core
@keys: Dev's Cavern, Chaos Core, Amiti Device
@keys2: Devantaka Mountain, Jazari, Ruin Golem
@version: 3.2
### STANDARD
Dev's Cavern lies beneath the mountain, where the Ruin Golem's core was sealed. The core leaked and polluted the forest; entrances were blocked, and the Golem's arm blow in Vimana Agama reopened them. The Traveler sealed the core with Kavikavus's method, which weakened the Abyss mages who tried to stop it.
