## summary
### LITE
Mont Automnequi is one of Fontaine's most iconic peaks, a mountain that straddles two regions: its west slopes lie in the Belleau Region and its east slopes in the Morte Region. Much of Fontaine is covered by water, but this is one of the places where mountain and forest show through. From its heights one can look across to the ridge of Elynas and the lakes beneath which the Melusines' homeland is hidden.
### STANDARD +
A tunnel, the Poisson Tunnel, was dug through the west side so that the Clementine Line of the aquabus system could pass, a feat of the Fontaine Research Institute.

## geography
### STANDARD
Mont Automnequi spans the Belleau Region (the West Slopes of Mont Automnequi, released in 4.0) and the Morte Region (the East Slopes, released in 4.2). The west side faces the coast where half of the wrecked Sponsian lies, with the Elton Trench just offshore and the ridge of Elynas across the water.
### LARGE +
Local specialties on the west slopes are Lumidouce Bell and Rainbow Rose. The east side belongs to the Morte Region, whose name is French for "dead"; that region as a whole offers Condessence Crystal, Spring of the First Dewdrop, Lumidouce Bell, Rainbow Rose and Romaritime Flower. The wiki gives no separate lore for the east slopes.

## places
### STANDARD
Poisson Tunnel on the west slopes; a stretch of coast where wreckage of the Sponsian can be found; and, nearby, the Elton Trench offshore. The viewpoint "View From Mont Automnequi" stands on the west slopes.
### LARGE +
Wooden-and-masonry dwellings called Automnequi's Watch stand on the west slopes; they are a common countryside building in Fontaine, easy to maintain and build, and do not need specialists to repair.

## people
### LARGE
The only NPC the wiki lists on the west slopes is Antoine. Treasure Hoarders also roam there.

## history
### STANDARD
The Fontaine Research Institute brought in huge engineering devices to bore the Poisson Tunnel, which the official map text calls one of the biggest challenges in building the aquabus lines. The wiki records that new machines, including the "Golden Vessel", were developed for the job.
### LARGE +
The tunnel's success pushed a new generation of clockwork technology, including the Construction Specialist Mek, which was modelled on the Golden Vessel. During construction on the Clementine Line, engineers had to stay near the tunnel to repair malfunctioning clockwork meka at any time, so they built houses patterned on local dwellings; the houses, Automnequi's Watch, are said to have withstood intense earthquakes and stand upright years after the engineers left.
### ULTRA +
Long before she became Admiral, Basil Elton (who lived about 500 years ago) is said to have driven off an Emperor of Fire and Iron that was attacking a ship, sending it back to the depths; that creature is the boss of the west slopes. One half of her flagship Sponsian, wrecked in the fleet's last battle against Elynas, lies on a beach nearby.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Broad vistas of lakes, sea and the ridge of Elynas, rugged slopes of mountain and forest unusual in a land mostly covered by water, and clockwork meks and the engineers' tunnel as reminders of Fontaine's technology.

## dangers
### LARGE
Hilichurls (including Hilichurl Berserkers), Large Hydro Slimes, Treasure Hoarders of several kinds, and clockwork Meks (Area Alert, Geological Survey, Suppression Specialist and Recon Log). Elite foes on the west slopes are Blazing Axe Mitachurls, Hydro Hilichurl Rogues and Tainted Water-Splitting Phantasms, and the Emperor of Fire and Iron is the area's boss.

## travel
### STANDARD
The Clementine Line aquabus passes through the Poisson Tunnel under the west slopes. The mountain itself spans the Belleau Region and the Morte Region.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 nothing new has been reported on the mountain; it remains accessible and quiet.

## entry: Poisson Tunnel and the Clementine Line
@keys: Poisson Tunnel, Clementine Line, Golden Vessel, Construction Specialist Mek
@spoiler: none
### STANDARD
The Poisson Tunnel carves through the West Slopes of Mont Automnequi so the Clementine Line of the aquabus network can run. The Fontaine Research Institute bored it with huge engines including the legendary "Golden Vessel", and the project led to the Construction Specialist Mek and a new generation of clockwork technology.

## entry: Automnequi's Watch
@keys: Automnequi's Watch
@keys2: Fontaine, Mont Automnequi
### LARGE
A traditional country dwelling mixing wood and masonry, seen on the west side of Mont Automnequi and in Fontaine's countryside generally. It is far less durable than the large Tearstone buildings or the slag-cement houses of the Court of Fontaine, but is easy to repair. Engineers building the Clementine Line tunnel built these houses patterned on local homes, and they stand to this day.
