## summary
### LITE
Blue Amber Lake is a highland lake on a raised plateau of Lempo Isle in the Southern Isles of Nod-Krai, between the north and south sections of Barrowmoss Barrens. Its unusual climate gives it an ethereal glow at every hour of the day, and it is the only place where Stellafruit grows. Travellers rest at its shoreside bonfires, and the inventor known as Papa Wrench once built a workshop beneath it.
### STANDARD +
The in-game expedition description calls it a highland lake of crystalline blue that cradles the sky's reflection, a place of tranquility amid the world's clamour when the stars come out. It is the setting of the World Quest The Tale-Telling Heart.

## geography
### STANDARD
A lake on a plateau sitting between the two Barrowmoss Barrens plateaus on Lempo Isle. Rippleweeds beside the water, flytrap-like plants opened by throwing Kuuvahki Dewdrops, contain the Stellafruit that grows nowhere else. A teleport waypoint stands on its western side.
### LARGE +
Portable Bearing is a local specialty. Barrowmoss Barrens around the lake hosts the Radiant Moonfly boss and contains the Hidden Laboratory, Windworn Path and Pellucid Cavern map levels.

## places
### STANDARD
The shoreside campfire where the quest begins; the area near the western waypoint; and Papa Wrench's underground workshop, reached by a lift that needs missing parts recovered from Treasure Hoarders.
### LARGE +
Papa Wrench's Messily-Scribbled Notes lie around the lake; the later two are readable only during the quest. An Opened Secret Letter near the western waypoint is a note between two treasure-seekers, mentioning money to be made, possible competition and a debt to be repaid double.

## people
### STANDARD
Papa Wrench, a gentle mechanic who lived and later died here, is the lake's presiding memory. Tolfin, a storyteller who knew him, gathers people at the campfire. Aino, builder of the Clink-Clank Krumkake Craftshop, was Papa Wrench's pupil. The other participants in the quest are Danyusha and Zhorik Shesternin of Novokitezhgrad, and Huginn, who guides the Traveler through the workshop.
### LARGE +
In the quest, Danyusha is Papa Wrench's daughter and Zhorik is her husband. Tinet, a child of the caravan Papa Wrench travelled with, was too ill to travel further, so the caravan made camp here; he stayed behind to look after her and built the workshop's machines to make her happy.

## history
### STANDARD
Papa Wrench's original name was Mr. Shesternin Sr., of Snezhnaya. About ten years before the events of the game he sailed on the ship Fortunate One, disappeared and was presumed lost overboard. He survived but lost his memory, took the name Papa Wrench and travelled between Nod-Krai's islands with a caravan, then settled at Blue Amber Lake.
### LARGE +
There he found an underground warehouse containing the Stratagema-Cognoscenti's Mask, one of the Proofs of the Cognoscenti, and turned it into a workshop. Tinet did not survive her illness, and he later died of chronic illness and grief. He left his workshop and belongings to Tolfin.
### ULTRA +
Papa Wrench taught Aino and listened to her ideas about robots, according to her voice lines, and the Hiisi Island mechanic Verna claims to be his direct apprentice. His real family had a legal claim to the inheritance, so Tolfin lured the Shesternin couple with a promise of a Proof of the Cognoscenti, a test of Zhorik's character.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Stillness lit from within: water the colour of blue amber, a faint glow on the plateau, bonfires at night and the sound of stories being told. Under the lake is an old workshop of gears, lifts and half-finished toys.

## dangers
### LARGE
Treasure Hoarders, hilichurls, Blazing Axe Mitachurls and Hydro Slimes appear in and near the workshop during the quest, and the Radiant Moonfly boss lives in the surrounding Barrens.

## travel
### LARGE
On Lempo Isle. A teleport waypoint stands on its west side, and the campfire is the quest start.

## quests
### STANDARD
World Quest: The Tale-Telling Heart, part of the East of the Moon, West of the Sun chapter. Tolfin's campfire storytelling becomes a treasure hunt for a Proof of the Cognoscenti, guided by Huginn, and ends with Zhorik confronted by a picture and Tolfin's account.
### LARGE +
The quest has the Traveler collect Stellafruit for a game of 'Colonel Iron Whale' and 'Sergeant Octopus', repair the lift, explore the workshop and stop a runaway machine, then ride the 'Aurora' in search of the treasure. Zhorik takes a flashy goblet to be the Proof; the real Proof is a mask he cast aside, which goes to the Traveler. Finishing unlocks the Iron Whale's Dream viewpoint and the achievement When the Aurora Shines.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the lake is a peaceful stop on Lempo Isle, and Papa Wrench's workshop lies open under it.
### STANDARD +
Zhorik's fate after the quest is not shown.

### STANDARD
A World Quest at the lake campfire. Tolfin the storyteller and the Shesternins gather; he proposes a treasure-hunt tale about Papa Wrench's Proof of the Cognoscenti. The Traveler and Paimon collect Stellafruit, fix the lift, explore the workshop with Huginn and find the treasure; Zhorik flees after seeing a picture that implicates him in the Fortunate One incident.

## entry: Papa Wrench
@keys: Papa Wrench, Mr. Shesternin Sr., Fortunate One
@keys2: Blue Amber Lake, Tolfin, Tinet
@version: 6.0
### STANDARD
Mr. Shesternin Sr. lost his memory after the Fortunate One incident, became the roaming mechanic Papa Wrench and later lived at Blue Amber Lake. He charged little for his repairs and sometimes none, told the caravan children endless stories and gave them candy. He built the workshop's machines for Tinet, and passed away from illness and grief afterwards.

## entry: Stellafruit and Rippleweeds
@keys: Stellafruit, Rippleweed, Kuuvahki Dewdrop
@version: 6.0
### STANDARD
Stellafruit is a star-shaped quest-item fruit that grows only here, found in Rippleweeds beside the lake. Rippleweeds open when Kuuvahki Dewdrops are thrown at them. The fruit seems to hold some mysterious power.
