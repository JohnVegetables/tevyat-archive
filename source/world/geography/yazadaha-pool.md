## summary
### LITE
Yazadaha Pool is a subarea of Avidya Forest in Sumeru's Dharma Forest, added in Version 3.0. It is a water area where Eremites and hilichurls are found.
### STANDARD +
Exploration objects include a Ruins Torch, Tri-Yana Seeds and Valaya. The local wood is Adhigama Wood.

## geography
### STANDARD
A pool within the dense rainforest of Avidya Forest. The Chinese name is Tianbi Pool.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Eremite Axe Vanguards, Daythunders, Linebreakers and Sword-Dancers, plus hilichurls, Hydro Samachurls, Pyro Hilichurl Grenadiers and Stretchy Geo Fungi.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
A damp forest clearing around still water, with Eremite patrols and hilichurl camps nearby.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 it is an ordinary explorable subarea.
### ULTRA +
The pool belongs to the Dharma Forest subregion and was added in Version 3.0. The wiki records no quests, NPCs or description text for it.
