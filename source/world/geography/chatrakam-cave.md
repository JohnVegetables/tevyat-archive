## summary
### LITE
Chatrakam Cave is a cave system hidden deep in the Lokapala Jungle of Sumeru's Dharma Forest, best known for its huge number of fungi. Humans seldom enter: the damp air suits mushrooms, and apart from the Vanara, for whom mushroom picking is a duty, few people go in. Underground ruins, Eremites, ruin machines and the Realm of Khab are also found there.
### STANDARD +
The name derives from the Sanskrit chatraka, meaning mushroom. The cave also holds a Battered Notebook tied to the quest Aranakin's Old Friend, and the Realm of Khab opens within it.

## geography
### STANDARD
The cave has two mapped levels, a Northern Underground Ruins and a Southern Cavern, with a Dendro Elemental Monument, Clusterleaf of Cultivation, Four-Leaf Sigil, bouncy mushrooms, poisonous mushrooms, Gloomy Path puzzles and Auspicious Branch plants. Outside lies Lokapala Jungle forest where hilichurl berserkers, Dendro samachurls and Eremite Sunfrost are found.
### LARGE +
Nilotpala Lotus is the local specialty. The Battered Notebook interactable inside the cave is linked by the wiki to the World Quest Aranakin's Old Friend, which begins on the hill northwest of the cave.

## places
### STANDARD
The Realm of Khab, reached from the Northern Underground Ruins, is described as the place where the memories of Nara Varuna and the Aranara converge and where the dreams of the Barsam Flower gather. Khab is a Persian word for sleep.
### ULTRA +
The Realm of Khab is entered during the Risen Moon Chapter of the Archon Quest. The wiki records Nara Varuna as the Traveler's sibling in that context.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Many fungi: floating Hydro and Dendro fungi, stretchy Geo fungi, grounded Hydroshrooms and winged Dendroshrooms. Eremites (Ravenbeak halberdiers, axe vanguards, linebreakers, crossbowmen and sword-dancers) are also inside. Ruin Destroyers, Cruisers, Defenders and Graders form the elite foes.
### LARGE +
Outside the cave there are Ruin Drake Skywatchers; a Fatui agent appears north of the cave during a limited event.

## people
### STANDARD
The Vanara, who treat mushroom picking as a duty, are the only people said to go into the cave in any number. According to the Amurta scholars quoted in the expedition text, the fungi come in a great many varieties.
### LARGE +
The Aranara Aranakin is found on the hill northwest of the cave, singing next to a seelie; his quest ends with a notebook on the second floor of a tree house.

## quests
### STANDARD
Aranakin's Old Friend (3.0) begins at the hill northwest of the cave and ends with a notebook in a tree house; Aranakin has forgotten many of the friends he made. The limited event Fabulous Fungus Frenzy (3.2) placed a Fatui Pyro Agent north of the cave carrying a letter from Second Lieutenant Yurochka that plotted against a rogue Fatui named Elchingen.
### LARGE +
The Fabulous Fungus Frenzy material is limited-event content: the letter tells its recipient to ambush Elchingen as he is transferred to a prison after the Akademiya handles him, to prevent his unauthorised plans from endangering Yurochka's division.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Damp, closed-in and thick with fungi; the official text calls it a damp environment hidden deep in the woods, ideal for fungal growth, and few people willingly go in.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 Chatrakam Cave is still a fungus-ridden cave that few people enter.
### STANDARD +
The Fabulous Fungus Frenzy event is over, but the cave and the Realm of Khab remain accessible.
