## summary
### LITE
The Roots of Erinnyes are a network of subterranean caverns and water veins beneath the island of Erinnyes in Fontaine, in the region of Erinnyes Forest. Its waters work as an information system, and the central part is called Hauteclaire. Many of its channels lead up to the Weeping Willow of the Lake. In the 4.2 world quest series The Wild Fairy of Erinnyes, the Traveler cleanses it of corruption alongside a strange Melusine named Pahsiv.
### STANDARD +
Pahsiv explains, in broken words, that all of Erinnyes is an information system whose centre is the roots: willows collect, fog handles and springs send. A powerful "visitor" who protects Fontaine uses it to sense evil, so its destruction would blind that guardian.

## geography
### STANDARD
Below Erinnyes Forest, a labyrinth of caverns and water veins connects springs, the lake and the great willow. The way in is a hidden passage under the square at Marcotte Station, opened by inserting three Teardrop (Waterdrop) Keys into the statues at the fountain there. By Pahsiv's directions, the keys lie along the foggy path to the south, at the spring to the north and by the willows to the east.
### LARGE +
Deeper down, the passage leads through underwater stretches to the roots themselves, which are the roots of the great willow in the lake; when they are corrupted the tree looks sickly and the lake turns yellow. The roots lie "very far, very deep", as Pahsiv puts it.
### ULTRA +
On the surface, Erinnyes Forest has puzzles that use the Xenochromatic Ball Octopus (Contaminated Bacterial Mats, Dew Bubbles, Harmonious Reed Pipes, and so on). After the cleansing, six non-hostile Primordial Bathysmal Vishap Hatchlings appear along the river between the forest's Statue of the Seven and Loch Urania.

## places
### STANDARD
The Weeping Willow of the Lake stands at the centre of a lake and its roots seem to extend underwater. Its condition is tied to the health of the water veins: before the cleansing the whole lake area appears sickly yellow, afterwards vibrant blue. Marcotte Station, with its fountain and square, hides the entrance.
### LARGE +
Elsewhere on the island of Erinnyes stand the Opera Epiclese, the Fountain of Lucine and the ruins of the Fontaine Research Institute on the north side. The wiki's Erinnyes island page cites the NPC Elphane as saying that Neuvillette considers exploring the depths of Erinnyes ill-advised.

## people
### STANDARD
Pahsiv, listed as a Melusine, speaks only in single words and was named by Paimon; Paimon notes that she acts more like a wild fairy than an ordinary Melusine. She protects the roots from a "purple thing", scratched the writing from the stone tablet, and vanishes after the cleansing without asking for a reward, though she comes back with a chest for Paimon. The brothers Remaudiere and Keratry are among the people in the Marcotte Station square above.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Corruption: dark filth and a Root-Consuming Tumour at the heart of the roots. The tumour's destruction triggers an arcane formation from which Rifthounds (Thundercraven and Rockfond Rifthound Whelps) pour. Late in the fight a Primordial Bathysmal Vishap joins the player's side. Afterwards the Local Legend Mageblade Corrouge appears where the tumour was.
### LARGE +
Before the cleansing an enraged Primordial Bathysmal Vishap has to be subdued at the spring (the quest Furious Mouth of the Spring); Pahsiv says the unnamed purple thing fought a "blue thing", which suggests a vishap, and the blue thing "lost lots".

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Fog, willows and reed pipes above; below, flooded passages and a root chamber that Paimon calls "a whole load of funky stuff", with the party always aware that something evil lurks nearby. After the final fight a sudden rise of water carries the Traveler and Paimon up to the surface.

## quests
### STANDARD
The Wild Fairy of Erinnyes (4.2): Foggy Forest Path, Wilting Weeping Willow and Furious Mouth of the Spring (three concurrent quests, each yielding a key) lead to Within the Depths of Erinnyes. The Traveler also needs to have done The Outlander Who Caught the Wind for Furious Mouth of the Spring.
### LARGE +
In Within the Depths of Erinnyes the Traveler and Paimon insert the three keys, descend, cleanse the filth around the root, defeat the tumour and Rifthounds, and are washed up on the surface, where the willow has recovered.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the roots are cleansed and healthy. The willow is vibrant blue, and no later content has changed the area.
### STANDARD +
Pahsiv vanished at the end of the quest and reappeared briefly with a chest; where she is now is not stated. Paimon wants to visit her often and teach her more words.

## entry: Hauteclaire

@keys2: Erinnyes, Roots of Erinnyes, Fontaine
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
In the official text, Hauteclaire names the central part of the Roots of Erinnyes. The wiki also records Hauteclaire as the name of the sword of the historical Erinnyes, High King of Aremorica, better known as the weapon Splendor of Tranquil Waters; in the book La Chanson d'Erinnyes, a retelling that takes liberties, she throws it into a lake. The link between the two is not explained, and the name echoes the sword of Oliver in the French Song of Roland.
### LARGE +
The weapon's story says the sword sank into the lake beside Egeria's blessings after her judgment, and the knight walked away never to be seen again.

## entry: Pahsiv
@keys: Pahsiv
@keys2: Erinnyes, Melusine, Marcotte Station
### STANDARD
A Melusine who stays in Erinnyes Forest and speaks in single words, using "Pahsiv" for "yes" and "Melusine" for "no", as Paimon works out. She wants the Traveler's help to stop the Erinnyes water veins from failing and gives them a quiet reward at the end. She fears the purple thing but guards the roots.
### LARGE +
She says the water vein carries the "visitor" lots of information, tastes evil and senses it. She scratched the writing off the stone tablet to hinder the purple thing, and when the Traveler is exhausted she touches their stomach to give them warmth and strength.

## entry: Erinnyes the High King
@keys: High King of Aremorica, Erinnyes the Lochknight
@keys2: Roots of Erinnyes, Fontaine
@spoiler: minor
### LARGE
Erinnyes, a historical Lochknight and High King of Aremorica in the last years of Remuria, united the tribes of Fontaine against the Remurian Empire. She wielded the Splendor of Tranquil Waters, which the wiki identifies as the sword Hauteclaire, and swore with her knights to protect the Oceanids. After Egeria told her that Egeria herself had created humans from Oceanids, her faith broke and she left, never seen again. The island and forest are likely named after her; the Roots may share the name.
