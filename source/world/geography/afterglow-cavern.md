## summary
### LITE
Afterglow Cavern is a mountain cave in Wavechaser Plain, Nod-Krai, southeast of Favonius Keep. It was once a tunnel leading north, but it collapsed after a kuuvahki-related anomaly. Travellers know it mostly as the den of a large moonlight-loving gecko.
### STANDARD +
At first the cave was split in two separate parts: a southern cave reached near a waterfall north of Dreadshade Mire, and a northern cave reached near the Teleport Waypoint just east of Favonius Keep.

## geography
### STANDARD
A rocky cave in the hills of Wavechaser Plain. The southern entrance lies by a waterfall north of Dreadshade Mire; the northern one is by the waypoint directly east of Favonius Keep. The collapse cut the old northward tunnel, which is why the cave was at first two separate halves.
### LARGE +
Intricate Bloom plants grow in the cave and can be used to reconnect the blocked path deeper in; slothsheep, creatures that live only where kuuvahki is present, wander in the area.

## people
### STANDARD
No residents. A guide named Vacarao is met at the northern entrance, near a locked chest, and later by the nearby waypoint; he is a local who cannot use Frostmoon Scion magic and lacks kuuvahki devices strong enough to clear the blockage.
### LARGE +
An overseas traveling merchant had befriended a slothsheep that wandered into the cave and became trapped by the collapse, and insisted on rescuing it. Vacarao later reported that when the merchant tried to take the slothsheep out of Nod-Krai it turned into a lifeless seed, since slothsheep cannot live outside kuuvahki regions.

## dangers
### STANDARD
The collapsed tunnel and unstable kuuvahki around it. Vacarao warns that kuuvahki anomalies have been frequent in the area and urges care.
### LARGE +
The cave is the arena of the Radiant Moongecko, an ancient territorial gecko drawn to moonlight. In-game texts say it lives deep in the rock, that kuuvahki has crystallised its body into segments as precise as machine parts, and that on full-moon nights it gnaws on lunar-suffused rock and soil to preserve its bloodline. Some scholars think its ancestors once lived on the surface and were driven underground by sweeping changes in the world.

## quests
### LARGE
Slothsheep in Need (hidden exploration objective, 6.3): talk to Vacarao, solve two Intricate Bloom puzzles, and guide the trapped slothsheep across a bridge.
### ULTRA +
The objective's reward is two chests, one of them a locked Precious Chest next to Vacarao at the northern entrance. Its outcome is bittersweet: the merchant's plan to take the slothsheep home fell through when it became a lifeless seed outside Nod-Krai. Vacarao regrets that the merchant did not listen to his warning.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the northern tunnel remains collapsed and the cave is the Radiant Moongecko's lair.

## entry: Radiant Moongecko
@keys: Radiant Moongecko, Moonseeker Drake
@keys2: Afterglow Cavern, Nod-Krai
### STANDARD
The Radiant Moongecko is a normal boss that dwells in Afterglow Cavern. Official descriptions call it an ancient monster drawn to moonlight that lives deep in the rock; the Archive says kuuvahki has crystallised its body into mechanical-looking segments that harden into stone spikes against intruders. Its fiercely territorial nature is explained in-game by the scarcity of moonlight that reaches the rock walls.
