## summary
### LITE
The Golden Apple Archipelago is a set of mountainous islands in the sea east of Mondstadt. It was a limited-time summer event map: first as the setting of Midsummer Island Adventure (1.6), when Klee and her friends followed a mysterious letter from "Dodo-King" to it, and again in Summertime Odyssey (2.8) with a new look. It was formerly the fog-bound Haar Islands. It is not accessible as of 7.1.
### STANDARD +
Its islands are the tops of Mondstadt's old mountains, flipped upside down into the sea when Barbatos reshaped the land. The archipelago was the first place to introduce the Waverider boat.

## geography
### STANDARD
In 1.6 it began as a handful of small islands in thick fog and, after the quest "Summer Vacation: Proceed With Caution", rose into a group of mountainous islands under a golden sun, with a tropical feel. Four named areas: Pudding Isle (where visitors start), Twinning Isle to the north, Broken Isle to the west and Minacious Isle to the south. Later in 1.6 further islands emerged, among them a Central Platform where the samurai puppet stands.
### LARGE +
The 2.8 version gave each isle a theme. Pudding Isle: a Kaedehara Kazuha-themed island in two halves that switch between Mondstadt, Liyue and Inazuma styles. Broken Isle: Xinyan-themed, with parts that rise and fall at an overgrown drum. Twinning Isle: Fischl-themed and home to Night Raven statues. Minacious Isle: Mona-themed, letting visitors travel between two time periods. Specialties of several nations grow here (dandelions, starconches, sea ganoderma and more), unusual for one area. The sky shows no Celestia, unlike the main map.

## history
### STANDARD
About 2,600 years ago, when Barbatos terraformed Mondstadt, he split the tops off mountains and they landed upside-down in the sea, forming the islands (the Haar Islands; "haar" is a cold sea fog, and the islands were initially covered in it). A group of shipwrecked people settled and lived on the archipelago for generations; many tried to escape and canon does not say whether any succeeded.
### LARGE +
About 500 years ago a pirate fleet under Ako Domeki, thrown off course in a battle with the Shogunate's forces at Seirai Island, was shipwrecked on the islands. It carried a stolen samurai puppet (a Maguu Kenki) that protected the islanders until it was damaged. Pirates and islanders together devised an escape route that used the archipelago's odd features and everyone left; Domeki's fleet returned to a ruined Seirai Island. The wiki's timeline (a fan compilation of conch and quest text) also places Minacious Isle's arrival, with a Seelie, from Mondstadt waters before the settlers.
### ULTRA +
Some years before 1.6, Alice visited the islands, found them beautiful and decided to dress them up for a summer vacation for her daughter Klee, her friends and Diluc. The Echoing Conches in the 1.6 map preserve islander voices; the wiki infers from them that the settlers spanned at least four or five generations, an inference rather than a stated figure.

## people
### STANDARD
No permanent inhabitants in the present. The islands are home to hilichurls, slimes, Mimic creatures and other monsters. In 1.6 Klee, Jean, Barbara, Kaeya, Diluc, Albedo and Razor appear as event NPCs. In 2.8 the event focuses on Fischl, Mona, Kazuha and Xinyan and their islands, with the Night Raven statues on Twinning Isle.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Dense fog once made the sea around the islands extremely dangerous, and the changing winds and tides made escape hard. Present-day threats are hilichurl camps, slimes, Abyss mages, Mimic creatures and a Maguu Kenki on the Central Platform (a gameplay boss).
### LARGE +
In 2.8 an area on the archipelago also features a Fatui dream machine, shown in event art as both broken and repaired.

## travel
### STANDARD
In 1.6 Klee's group were flown to the islands on Dvalin, summoned by Venti. The islands are also reached by the Waverider boat, which is the area's characteristic means of travel. In 1.6 a time tunnel from Minacious Isle back to Falcon Coast could be used once, during the quest "The Winding Homeward Way". The archipelago has its own map tab, separate from Teyvat.

## quests
### LARGE
In 1.6 the Midsummer Island Adventure quests were "Mysterious Islands: Journey to the Unknown", "Summer Vacation: Proceed With Caution", "Dodo-King of the Sea: Lying in Wait" and "The Final Riddle: A Secret Uncovered". World quests included "A Trip Through Fog and Wind", "The Other Side of Isle and Sea", "From Outer Lands", "Who Wields the Wild Wind?", "They Who Hear the Sea" and "The Winding Homeward Way". The 2.8 Summertime Odyssey began with the quest "The Golden Apple Vacation Returns!".

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the archipelago is not accessible: both event runs have ended and the map is removed. Nothing in Nod-Krai (6.x) or Snezhnaya (7.0-7.1) content revisits it. Its events remain canon but are not widely known.
### STANDARD +
Many of its elements, such as the Waverider, reappeared later in Inazuma.

## entry: Midsummer Island Adventure (1.6)

@version: 1.6
@availability: limited
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
This limited-time event (June 2021) began when Klee received a letter from "Dodo-King" threatening to take her plush Dodoco away unless she proved herself. With the Traveler and Paimon she followed clues to the archipelago, accompanied for her safety by Acting Grand Master Jean and Deaconess Barbara; Dvalin flew them there at Venti's call. Kaeya, Diluc, Albedo and Razor, who had received letters of their own, followed and were briefly separated when Razor jumped off Dvalin's back. The mystery ends with the revelation that Klee's mother Alice had arranged it all so her daughter would have a memorable summer.

## entry: Summertime Odyssey (2.8)

@availability: limited
### LARGE
In this limited-time event (July to August 2022), the archipelago returned with a new look after Fischl received an important missive and set out with others for another summer journey. Each isle has the theme of one character: Kazuha (Pudding Isle), Xinyan (Broken Isle), Fischl (Twinning Isle) and Mona (Minacious Isle). Gameplay centred on Waverider challenges and "Phantom Realm" trials. The plot beyond that outline is not summarised here because it was not read in full.
