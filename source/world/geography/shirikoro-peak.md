## summary
### LITE
Shirikoro Peak is a rugged part of Tsurumi Island, the fog-shrouded island of Inazuma that once held two ancient civilisations. A great tree called a perch stands in a cave here, one of the places where the islanders once made offerings to the Thunderbird. Beneath the peak runs a stretch of underground ruins full of Electro puzzles, Seelie courts and ancient Ruin machines.
### STANDARD +
It is one of several perch sites (with Chirai Shrine, Autake Plains and the first perch) in the world quest series Through the Mists, where the Traveler helps the spirit of a boy named Ruu break a curse laid on the island by the Thunderbird. The name is Japanese Shirikoro-yama; "shirikoro" may come from the Ainu for "owning land".

## geography
### STANDARD
The peak lies on Tsurumi Island, and its perch is inside a cave reached from the southeast, with three Seelie Courts nearby. Features of the peak include Mysterious Carvings, Phase Gates, Ruins Torches, Thunder Barriers and Thunder Sakura boughs. Local specialties are Fluorescent Fungus and Sea Ganoderma.
### LARGE +
An underground cavern connects the peak to Chirai Shrine, and seven Stone Slates can be used there; the ruins can be opened and partly solved before Ruu's questline, though some puzzles and the hidden exploration objective need the quest steps. Three Star-Shaped Gems can be placed on the murals to release Electro Seelie. Waverider Waypoints and Lightning Strike Probes are also listed among its features.

## places
### STANDARD
The perch cave. A broken dome holding a remarkable chest. Doors of the buried ruin that open when Electro Current puzzles are solved. Seelie courts around a central mechanism.

## history
### STANDARD
Tsurumi Island is the home of two ancient peoples: an older civilisation buried deep underground, and later the tribe that worshipped the Thunderbird Kanna Kapatcir. The perches were trees where the Thunderbird once alighted, and the islanders believed offerings to them were needed. The story that unfolds in the quests: Ruu, child of the last priest Mata, befriended the Thunderbird by singing and named her Kanna Kapatcir; when the island declined he was chosen as a living sacrifice, which enraged her and she destroyed the village and cursed the island to relive its ruin.
### LARGE +
After the Traveler destroyed the perches in "The Sea of Fog and the Rite of the Trees", the ceremony fell into chaos under a crimson storm. In "The Sun-Wheel and Mt. Kanna" the Peculiar Pinion shows that the curse can only lift when Kapatcir hears Ruu sing again. Because the Raiden Shogun had slain Kapatcir, the Traveler and Paimon bluff that she has moved to Seirai Island, and the Peculiar Pinion carries Ruu there to sing and the curse ends; the time loop closes, and Ruu is expected to pass on.
### ULTRA +
The island's wiki lore notes that the buried older civilisation left a vast underground ruin with Ruin Sentinels and murals depicting Celestia, much like Seirai Island's; Sumida theorises that something fell from Celestia and Kama suspects it disturbed the island's ley lines. Ruu was the only phantom aware of the loop, able to see and hear everything, and in the thousands of years of repeating the ceremony none could see or speak to him.

### STANDARD
In A Particularly Particular Author, the author Sumida, whose commission is posted through the Adventurers' Guild, asks the Traveler to fetch a Maushiro from Tsurumi Island for her novel; Kama takes them across by boat, and the Bantan Sango Detective Agency also takes an interest. The Traveler meets a boy, Ruu, and helps make offerings to perches, including the one at Shirikoro Peak. The locals behave oddly because they are illusions replaying the past. The Maushiro is gone when delivered, as for every adventurer before. The series continues in Octave of the Maushiro, and ends with the curse lifted and the island's fog permanently cleared.
### LARGE +
Shirikoro's perch cave requires guiding three Seelie to their courts to lower the water level and following the Thunderbird feathers through ruined doors, past Thundercraven Rifthound Whelps, Ruin Cruisers and a Ruin Guard. The Tsurumi Island time loop makes spawns change on a three-day cycle.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
A cave of roots and water, glowing fungus and quiet Electro, under a fogged island where the dead still seem to carry on their festival. It feels stilled, sad and a little uncanny.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Hilichurl Fighters and Shooters, Samachurls, Electro Whopperflowers, and elites: Electro Abyss Mages, Wooden Shieldwall Mitachurls, Thundercraven Rifthound Whelps and several Ruin machines. The fog of Tsurumi Island teleports travellers away until cleared by the quests.

## travel
### LARGE
Reached by Kama's boat; the gate in the fog must be walked through during the quest to clear most of the island. A "Boatman" NPC is associated with the peak, and a follow-up hidden objective is called "Boatman's Task".

## quests
### LARGE
Through the Mists (2.2): A Particularly Particular Author includes the Shirikoro perch puzzle; it requires completing Seirai Stormchasers. Follow-up hidden objectives and The Saga of Mr. Forgetful continue the island's stories.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 Tsurumi Island's fog is gone and the curse is lifted, so the peak is a quiet ruin-filled hill.
### STANDARD +
Ruu is expected to pass on. No later canon through 7.1 changes the peak.
