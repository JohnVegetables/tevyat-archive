## summary
### LITE
Abdju Pit is a deep sinkhole at the edge of the Great Red Sand in Sumeru's Land of Lower Setekh. Before the quest Lost in the Sands the spot was an ordinary stretch called Abdju Road; afterwards the ground has opened into a pit leading down into ruins of King Deshret's ancient civilisation. Its name echoes Abydos, an ancient Egyptian city.
### STANDARD +
The pit is where the Traveler finds the archaeology team of the Akademiya researcher Tirzad, trapped with their guides and a merchant's Sumpter Beasts, in the first part of the Golden Slumber quest series.

## geography
### STANDARD
The pit lies where the desert's edge meets the road toward the sands' heart, in the Land of Lower Setekh within the Great Red Sand. Down in the hole are broken temple structures, long corridors, torch-lit chambers, sealed doors and an exit cavern through which a dry wind blows out to the open desert. Official map text says that at the desert's very edge stands a palace whose main form is entombed beneath the sands, and that King Deshret's realm lies ahead.
### LARGE +
Before the quest, the same ground is shown as Abdju Road, with no pit. The Scarlet Sand Slate calls the temple of Abdju Road the entrance to the King's territory, notes that most of its pillars have collapsed, and says the passage buried under it has become a haunt of scorpions and lizards.

## places
### STANDARD
Red Desert Threshold, a one-time domain, lies in the pit. Its description says the wise once debated here in a corner of the ancient city of Gurabad and outlined the desert king's dream; after the dream burst, the place became a secret holding cell, and it is now infested with monsters though old treasures remain.
### LARGE +
The pit's passages and sealed doors respond to the Scarlet Sand Slate, an object tied to King Deshret's civilisation. In the quest the Traveler follows seelie lights, lights torches and defeats ancient guardian constructs to open the way out.
### ULTRA +
In the Scarlet Sand Slate's guide text, called the Supplicant's Steps, Abdju Road is the first stop of a pilgrim's route: from it one walks toward the sunset to Khemenu Temple, whose wall murals depict King Deshret's reign, and from there to the Mausoleum of King Deshret. The text frames the road as the beginning of a great journey and tells the reader to open paths with clearance and document the way.

## history
### STANDARD
The pit belongs to the ruins of King Deshret's civilisation, which the Great Red Sand still hides. Canon gives no date for the collapse of Abdju Road into a pit, and the pit itself is a recent development within the story: it appears only after Lost in the Sands.
### LARGE +
The domain text suggests a progression: a place of scholarly debate in the ancient city, then a secret prison after the king's dream ended, then a monster-ridden ruin. A legend attached to the domain speaks of a prince exiled by his father and imprisoned in a place of neither day nor night, where a bronze revelation first emerged.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Ancient guardian constructs (the quest names Primal Construct Prospectors and Reshapers), scorpions and lizards in the buried passage, sunstroke and thirst for anyone stranded, and the risk of being stuck with no way back to the surface. Treat the pit as a trap that is easy to fall into and hard to climb out of.

## quests
### STANDARD
Lost in the Sands (3.1), the first part of Golden Slumber. The merchant Bonifaz, a Mondstadter in Aaru Village, hires the Traveler to find a missing associate, Nachtigal, and the Sumpter Beasts leased to Tirzad's team. Footprints lead to the pit; below, the Traveler finds the stranded party. The team leaves together, guided by the wind, after the Traveler finds a strange friendly machine that Jeht names Benben.
### LARGE +
The party includes Tirzad, an Akademiya researcher who insists he studies indoor archaeology; the Eremite Jebrael and his daughter Jeht, hired as guides; the merchant Nachtigal, who arrives from another passage with the Sumpter Beasts; and two further party members, Mirsad and Omid. Tirzad's stone slate, the Scarlet Sand Slate, reacts to Benben, and Tirzad asks the Traveler to look after it. Once out, Jebrael says an oasis nearby will serve as a camp.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
A deep hole reached by a long drop from the desert above, with ancient passages, torches to light and seelie to follow, and a dry breeze that hints at the exit. The mood is part trap, part archaeological site; in the quest the strain between the anxious scholar Tirzad and his easygoing guide Jeht supplies much of the humour.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 Abdju Pit is an explorable ruin in the Great Red Sand and nothing later has changed it.
### STANDARD +
The pit remains open in the world after Lost in the Sands, and its domain Red Desert Threshold is available to players who have started Golden Slumber.

## entry: Abdju Pit Expedition
@keys: Abdju Pit, Abdju Road, Red Desert Threshold
@keys2: Tirzad, Jeht, Jebrael, Nachtigal, Benben, Scarlet Sand Slate
@version: 3.1
### STANDARD
Tirzad's archaeology team fell into the pit while working on his research, the Scarlet Sand Slate. They are Tirzad, the Eremite Jebrael and his daughter Jeht, with Mirsad and Omid; the merchant Nachtigal and the leased Sumpter Beasts are in another part of the ruins. Jeht befriends a small guardian machine she names Benben; it reacts to the slate, hinting that the slate commands devices in King Deshret's ruins.
### LARGE +
Tirzad distrusts desert people and hands the slate to the Traveler, believing Jebrael has an ulterior motive; the Traveler and Paimon think Jebrael seems reliable. The slate is later needed to open many chambers in the Great Red Sand.
