## summary
### LITE
Apam Woods is a huge rainforest in Sumeru's Dharma Forest, within the Ashavan Realm. Giant trees reach for the sky and, in the official description, the rain beneath their canopy has never ceased. The rain is not natural: it comes from the Varunastra, a pillar of water raised by an old device, the Varuna Contraption, which the Traveler helps the Aranara repair.
### STANDARD +
The area is home to fungi, ruin machines, Eremites and tigers, and its name comes from the Sanskrit word for "of the waters."

## geography
### STANDARD
A dense, wet jungle of towering trees with an underground layer. The Varunastra, a column of water, stands in the southern part, at The Rain's End, where the main contraption is found. Between 00:00 and 06:00 and between 12:00 and 18:00 (late night or late day) it rains and the water level is low; from 06:00 to 12:00 and 18:00 to 00:00 (early night or early day) it is dry and the water is high, flooding lower ground. After the quest the Traveler can fix either state.
### LARGE +
Two smaller contraptions appear once the first is activated, one next to the Varunastra and one further north. The Grove of Dreams, an underground cavern, connects to the Ruins of Dahri to the west and holds one of the contraption's energy sources, which the Aranara call branches and leaves.

## places
### STANDARD
The Rain's End, where the Varuna Contraption sits. The Grove of Dreams, a cavern with a real and a dream version that the Traveler switches between with the Rhythm of the Great Dream played on a vintage lyre at a Silapna. The Tower of Abject Pride, a weapon-material domain whose description recalls three sage-kings of the desert, one of whom (Hermanubis) stayed wise and lovely to the end, and says the tower was the temporary home of one of them while building an earthly paradise, and earlier a small shrine against wind and sand.
### LARGE +
Local specialties are Kalpalata Lotus and Rukkhashava Mushrooms. Poisonous mushrooms grow in the woods. Underground ruins hold Ragged Records, notes from the Akademiya trainee Zandik.

## history
### STANDARD
Greater Lord Rukkhadevata built the Varuna Contraption to summon timely rains in part of the desert; the forest that now covers the area grew from that rain. Item text adds that after the forest labyrinth was made, the forest's lord Viaghara was born from the moon's reflection on the water. In the present, the original makers are long dead and the Nara Varuna is gone, so the contraption has gone unmaintained.
### LARGE +
The mismanaged rain let fungi grow stronger. In Varuna Gatha the Traveler repairs the device with the help of Aranara named Arapandu, Aranakula, Arabalika and Arakunti, then enters its heart and defeats a Winged Dendroshroom that was damaging it.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Rishboland tigers, spinocrocodiles, shaggy sumpter beasts, floating and stretchy fungi, Eremite axe vanguards and sword-dancers, and Ruin Guards. The rain and the shifting water level change which routes are flooded; the Traveler is warned the air is off before the repair.

## quests
### STANDARD
Varuna Gatha (3.0): after The World of Aranara, the Traveler and Paimon rescue Arapandu from fungi and learn the contraption is failing; three smaller quests (Memory of Stone, Irate Iron Chunk, Slumbering Roots) lead to A Prayer for Rain on the Fecund Land, the climax. Arapandu then teaches the Rhythm of the Source Water for controlling the device, and the Aranara head to Vanarana for a festival.
### ULTRA +
The Zandik records here show an Akademiya trainee Dastur involved in medical experiments who became interested in capturing Aranara.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Towering trees, rain under the canopy and a great pillar of water; official text calls it the forest of a rain without end. Dry spells raise the water and flood the low ground, and the Aranara-tended machinery gives it a sense of something old and half-neglected.

## status
### LITE
Apam Woods is a repaired rainforest as of 7.1, with its rain regulated by the Varuna Contraption.
### STANDARD +
Nothing later than 3.0 has changed the area.

## entry: Varuna Contraption
@keys: Varuna Contraption, Varunastra, The Rain's End, Rhythm of the Source Water
@version: 3.0
### STANDARD
A device made by Greater Lord Rukkhadevata that raises the Varunastra, a giant pillar of water, to bring a constant cycle of rain to a former desert. It runs on a twelve-hour cycle and its water draw lowers the surrounding water level. Its energy sources, the branches and leaves, were misaligned until the Traveler corrected them; the Aranara maintain it and know its tune, played on a vintage lyre.
### LARGE +
Its maintainer, the Nara Varuna, is absent. Item text credits the device with the long chain that produced the forest and its lord.

## entry: Grove of Dreams
@keys: Grove of Dreams, Silapna, Rhythm of the Great Dream
@version: 3.0
### STANDARD
An underground cavern that connects to the Ruins of Dahri to the west. According to Arapandu it is a place where one may enter dreams, and it, like Vanarana, exists in a real and a dream form.
