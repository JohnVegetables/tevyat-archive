## summary
### LITE
The Dune of Carouses is a dune in the Hypostyle Desert of Sumeru's Great Red Sand. It is home to the Tomb of Carouses, a cone-shaped ruin that Soheil, an Akademiya researcher in Aaru Village, calls "a miniature version of the Mausoleum of King Deshret".
### STANDARD +
The tomb is the setting of the 3.1 world quest "Desert's Remembrance", part of "Soheil's Wish" in the quest chain "Old Notes and New Friends".
### LARGE +
The Garden of Endless Pillars domain lies west of the dune. The wiki records Scarabs as the local specialty.

## geography
### STANDARD
A dune area with the Tomb of Carouses, a pyramid-like ruin that echoes the shape of the Mausoleum of King Deshret on a smaller scale. Soheil found the same mysterious barrier keeping him out here as at his earlier ruins.
### LARGE +
To its west is the Garden of Endless Pillars, once part of the sapphire city of Tulaytullah, where thousands of giant pillars once stood and priests and the powerful walked beneath their shade. The domain text says the garden has endured thanks to the protection of the white tree (Irminsul) and the ley lines, and is now a paradise for fungi. The wiki's exploration lists for the area include an Elemental Monument: Dendro and, in the tomb, Seelie, Forged Primal Light, Large Fan Blades, Prisms of Khaj-Nisut, Obscuring Cells and Everlight Cells.

## history
### STANDARD
Soheil's notes (days 32 to 46 of a youthful expedition) recall the day he reached the dune: "We have arrived at The Dune of Carouses." He named the cone-shaped ruin the Tomb of Carouses "for now", found it much smaller than the Mausoleum and tried to imagine how Deshret enslaved his people to build such a structure, and was turned away by the same mysterious power that blocked him elsewhere.
### LARGE +
On the next day, the team was attacked by Eremites who called themselves Thutmose. Their two young leaders disagreed: one wanted to kill everyone, the other let them go. Soheil returned to the Akademiya feeling he had nothing to show for it. He also speculated that the Thutmose hide in rock formations, as the local sheep "disappear" near a place called Lamb-Devourer Rock. Later, in "Soheil's Wish", he sends the Traveler back to the places in his notes.
### ULTRA +
In "Desert's Remembrance" the Traveler searches the tomb; Paimon repeats Soheil's name for it, the Lesser Mausoleum of King Deshret, and notes it is a lot like the main tomb. Activating the Prism of Khaj-Nisut changes a mechanism but seems to do nothing until a switch opens a path. An Exquisite Chest holds the stone slate "Record of the Tomb of Carouses", which the Traveler can give to Soheil. In "Soheil's Wish" he studies a slate with alternating frowns and wonder, since it is his first time seeing writing like it, and he wants to visit the places where such slates were unearthed.

## people
### STANDARD
Soheil Usaibia, an Akademiya researcher found in Aaru Village, sends the Traveler on his behalf; he says he and Hulwani must patrol the area each day because young researchers keep trying to worm their way into the desert. The Eremites of Thutmose are fanatics of Deshret who once attacked his team.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Hilichurls of several kinds (including Dendro Samachurls and shield and shooter variants), Stretchy Geo Fungi, and, inside the tomb, Primal Construct Reshapers and floating Hydro and Anemo fungi.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
A dune of the red desert with a small cone-shaped tomb that once turned scholars away at a barrier.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the tomb can be entered and its slate can be given to Soheil for study; nothing else is recorded as changing.

## entry: Tomb of Carouses
@keys: Tomb of Carouses, Lesser Mausoleum of King Deshret
@keys2: Dune of Carouses, Soheil, Mausoleum of King Deshret
### STANDARD
A cone-shaped ruin on the Dune of Carouses, a smaller echo of the Mausoleum of King Deshret. Soheil named it. A hidden barrier once kept explorers out. Inside are Primal mechanisms and a stone slate recording its history (the Record of the Tomb of Carouses).

## entry: Garden of Endless Pillars
@keys: Garden of Endless Pillars, Tulaytullah, city of sapphire
@keys2: Hypostyle Desert, Dune of Carouses, pillars
### STANDARD
A one-time Dendro domain west of the dune, in the Hypostyle Desert. It is the surviving garden of the "city of sapphire" Tulaytullah, once dominated by thousands of giant pillars. The white tree and ley lines preserved it after the fall of the desert kingdom, and it is now a home for fungi.
