## summary
### LITE
Elton Trench is a deep underwater trench in Fontaine, in the Beryl Region between the beast-mountain of Elynas and Mont Automnequi. It is the final resting place of the old Fontaine Navy, which sank here fighting the great beast Elynas about 500 years ago. Its water is oddly bright, so divers misjudge its depth, yet the shipwrecks littering its bottom warn them off; local legends call the nearby waters haunted, which also makes it a good hiding place for anyone who wants secrecy.
### STANDARD +
The trench is probably named for Admiral Basil Elton, who led that last voyage in the white ironclad flagship Sponsian. It is part of the Beryl Region's map level "Source of All Waters: The Great Fontaine Lake".

## geography
### STANDARD
An underwater subarea of the Beryl Region, lying between the surface remains of Elynas to the north and the West Slopes of Mont Automnequi. The water is bright for its depth. The wrecks of the Navy's fleet are scattered along the trench, which a Fontaine viewpoint text (as cited on the Sponsian page) calls the "Graveyard of Ships". Nearby, on a beach by Mont Automnequi's western slopes, one half of the Sponsian lies wrecked on shore.
### LARGE +
Underwater, features include Pneumousia Blocks and Relays, Accelerated Aquaring and Recovery Orbs, and Xenochromatic creatures; local specialties are Beryl Conch and Romaritime Flower. A ghostly apparition of the Sponsian's front half is seen around the trench and Salacia Plain.

## history
### STANDARD
About 500 years ago, during the cataclysm, the great beast Elynas appeared and fouled Fontaine's land and waters. Fontaine's Armada, with the repaired Sponsian at its head and Admiral Basil Elton aboard, set out in what would be its last battle. The fleet pursued Elynas for roughly 35 days, drawing monsters from the Abyss, and the ship was split in half in the final charge. The rest of the fleet also sank, and the remains are strewn across the trench.
### LARGE +
A damaged diary recovered from the wreckage records the chase day by day: ruins where Elynas had passed, a trail of blood, a battered ship drifting wherever winds and currents took it, and a last fragment reading "Fontaine expects that everyone will do their duty". After the battle the Navy ceased to exist. Basil's First Mate, Nathaniel Pickman, spent years pressing the Court of Fontaine to salvage and rebuild the Sponsian; the families of the fallen suspected he was merely fishing for glory. Later stage plays reconstructed the final battle from Pickman's records.
### ULTRA +
The Navy's flagship was the pride of an age when armoured warships with heavy cannons and ramming prows were the darlings of the Court of Fontaine. The one keepsake left of the Admiral is her blue and white sword, remembered in the weapon Tidal Shadow, a standard-model officer's blade forged for the Navy's flagship captains. Elynas itself, bleeding from unexplained wounds, eventually died; its colossal remains became the mountains of the Beryl Region and the cradle of the Melusines.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Dim, pale water with a deceptive glow, broken masts and hulls looming out of the murk, a sense of a crowd that never left. Divers feel alarm bells go off once the wrecks come into view. Ghostly legends hang over the surrounding waters.

## dangers
### LARGE
Armored Crabs, Sternshield Crabs, Blubberbeasts, and Underwater Patrol and Survey Meks patrol the trench. The Abyssal monsters that harried the fleet are long gone, but the wreck-strewn depths are still easy to misjudge.

## travel
### LARGE
Reached by diving from the Beryl Region shore near the West Slopes of Mont Automnequi or from Salacia Plain. The depth is easy to underestimate because of the light.

## quests
### LARGE
The trench has no quests of its own. In the world quest The Lone Phantom Sail the Melusine Ahes, a self-styled Sponsian archaeologist, uses an old military conch to call the ghostly half-ship and leads the Traveler and Paimon from Salacia Plain along the clues to the wreck on the shore.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the trench is a silent graveyard; no recent event has changed it.
### STANDARD +
Ahes visits the beach wreck daily, and the Sponsian's captain has entrusted the wreckage to her. She means to restore the ship's logbook and piece Basil's story together.

## entry: The Sponsian and the Fontaine Armada
@keys: Sponsian, White Armada, Fontaine Armada, Nathaniel Pickman
@keys2: Elton Trench, Fontaine, Navy
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
The Sponsian was the white ironclad flagship of Fontaine's Navy, an armoured warship with heavy cannons and a ramming prow. Its name came from the First Mate, Nathaniel Pickman, who suggested the name of a usurper; Basil Elton first called it awful and then adopted it, saying she liked the "unreal reality" of it. In the last battle it was split in half: one half lies on the shore near Mont Automnequi, and the Traveler speculates the other was swallowed by Elynas. Of the crew, only Basil, Pickman, the Chief Engineer and volunteers stayed to the end; the Second Mate had taken many crew home. A machine aboard read zero chance of survival. All their fates are dead, missing or unknown.
### LARGE +
Wreckage is also scattered as ballast, crates and a smashed lifeboat along the coast, signs that the ship dumped weight in an emergency manoeuvre to rise from danger. The crew spoke of "boys and girls", which the quest suggests may have been an affectionate nickname.

## entry: Ahes and the Phantom Sail
@keys: Ahes, Lone Phantom Sail, military conch, Sponsian archaeologist
@keys2: Sponsian, Elton Trench, Melusine
### LARGE
Ahes is a Melusine who studies the Sponsian's wreck. She learned from another Melusine, Verenata, that the Traveler had glimpsed the ghost ship while under the effect of a "Truesight Potion". With an old military communications conch found in a sunken wreck, she sends tuned signals that make the phantom front half of the ship appear. At the end of the trail, the Traveler recounts Basil's last moments and hears her voice, described as sorrowful, and Ahes wins the captain's recognition as keeper of the wreckage. Ahes's claim that the ship responds like a whale to song is her own belief.
