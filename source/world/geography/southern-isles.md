## summary
### LITE
The Southern Isles, also called the Lempo Isles, are a group of three islands at the south of Nod-Krai, lying south of Pohjola: Lempo Isle, Hiisi Island and Paha Isle. Together with the city of Piramida they are the most populated part of Nod-Krai. Lempo Isle holds Nasha Town and the inventors' workshops, Hiisi Island is the wild home of the Frostmoon Scions, and Paha Isle is the Fatui's staging ground.
### STANDARD +
The islands became explorable with Version 6.0 (Luna I, 2025-09-10) and are the region's most populated area alongside Piramida. They are named with Finnic mythological words: Lempo, Hiisi and Paha are all figures or terms from the Kalevala tradition.

## geography
### STANDARD
Lempo Isle is the central island of the group, with Nasha Town, the Clink-Clank Krumkake Craftshop and the highland plateaus of Barrowmoss Barrens around Blue Amber Lake. Hiisi Island is mostly untouched forest wilderness. Paha Isle, once known for sunset-coloured salt marshes and colonies of Tassel Jellies, now hosts the Kuuvahki Experimental Design Bureau.
### LARGE +
Frostlamp Flower is a local specialty on Lempo, Hiisi and Paha alike, while Moonfall Silver is found on Hiisi Island. Bosses include Knuckle Duckle and the Radiant Moonfly on Lempo Isle, the Frostnight Herra on Hiisi Island and the Super-Heavy Landrover: Mechanized Fortress on Paha Isle. Small cubical Amazing 'Bots, which can display hints or emit kuuvahki energy, are found mainly on Lempo Isle.

## places
### STANDARD
Lempo Isle: Nasha Town (home of the Speranza restaurant), the Clink-Clank Krumkake Craftshop run by Aino, Barrowmoss Barrens and Blue Amber Lake. Hiisi Island: the Light-Bathed Platform and the forest sanctuaries of the Frostmoon Scions. Paha Isle: the Kuuvahki Experimental Design Bureau.
### LARGE +
The Barrowmoss Barrens are the two plateaus north and south of Blue Amber Lake. Blue Amber Lake is where the inventor Papa Wrench once lived (see its own entry), and the quest The Tale-Telling Heart takes place there.

## people
### STANDARD
Lempo Isle is the home of ordinary Nod-Krai townsfolk and inventors, including Aino, whose craftshop is based there. Hiisi Island belongs to the Frostmoon Scions, a religious sect devoted to the Goddess of the New Moon, and the Fatui personnel of Nod-Krai use Paha Isle as a staging ground.
### LARGE +
Helka describes the Frostmoon Scions as a strange group living in the wilds and forests, whom people generally do not look into or approach. Verna calls herself the best mechanic on Hiisi Island and a direct apprentice of Papa Wrench.

## culture
### LARGE
Hiisi Island's description speaks of legends that descendants of the golden realm (Hyperborea), guided by moonlight, travelled countless miles to find refuge on these shores, and of worship of the Moon Goddess that endures there. Lempo Isle, by contrast, is a place of workshops, small restaurants and tinkering.

## history
### LARGE
Paha Isle was an unremarkable salt-marsh island until the heaviest fragment of the fallen moonstone struck its shores, and it is now the Fatui's staging ground in Nod-Krai. On Hiisi Island Helka reports relics whose designs resemble Secret Source technology, though all appear inactive.
### ULTRA +
Tolfin, in a post-quest line of The Tale-Telling Heart, says that five Proofs of the Cognoscenti are rumoured to exist: one belonged to Papa Wrench, two more are on the three southern islands and two further north, with clues to be found in Piramida. This is a storyteller's claim ("allegedly").

## atmosphere
### LARGE
A cold, quiet archipelago of lakes, forests and salt marshes where small towns, inventors' workshops and old moon-worship coexist with Fatui installations.

## dangers
### LARGE
Fatui personnel and their machines on Paha Isle, hilichurls and treasure hoarders on Lempo, and the creatures of the wild on Hiisi.

## travel
### LARGE
Teleport waypoints on Lempo Isle include one by Blue Amber Lake.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the islands are open and inhabited. Hiisi Island's moon cult and Paha Isle's Fatui bureau are the two forces that most affect events.
### STANDARD +
Columbina was reported near Hiisi Island in 6.0; consult her entry for later developments.

## entry: Lempo Isle and Nasha Town
@keys: Lempo Isle, Nasha Town, Speranza, Amazing 'Bots
@version: 6.0
### STANDARD
Lempo Isle is the main settled island, including Nasha Town (with the Speranza restaurant), Aino's Clink-Clank Krumkake Craftshop, and Barrowmoss Barrens with Blue Amber Lake. The wiki notes Frostlamp Flower and Portable Bearing as local specialties and Knuckle Duckle as a boss. Amazing 'Bots roam the island.
### LARGE +
The name Lempo comes from a demon of Finnish folklore connected with love and fire.

## entry: Hiisi Island
@keys: Hiisi Island, Light-Bathed Platform, Frostnight Herra
@version: 6.0
### STANDARD
A forested island that is the home of the Frostmoon Scions, a sect devoted to the Goddess of the New Moon. Much of it is untouched wilderness. The Light-Bathed Platform and the Frostnight Herra are found here, and Moonfall Silver and Frostlamp Flower can be gathered.
### LARGE +
Hiisi is a Finnic word for a sacred grove or natural place and later a kind of spirit. In 6.0 official material Columbina was said to be seeking refuge near the island.

## entry: Paha Isle and the Kuuvahki Bureau
@keys: Paha Isle, Kuuvahki Experimental Design Bureau, Tassel Jelly
@version: 6.0
### STANDARD
Paha Isle is the Fatui's staging ground in Nod-Krai. Its Kuuvahki Experimental Design Bureau is a high-tech complex. The island's earlier fame came from salt marshes and Tassel Jellies, until the heaviest moonstone fragment fell on it. Paha is a word for bad or evil in Finnic languages and, in the Kalevala, a demon follower of Lempo.
