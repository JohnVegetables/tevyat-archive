## summary
### LITE
The Nameless Island is a small, uninhabited island off the coast northeast of Starfell Valley in Mondstadt, with no name on the map. A weathered sundial stands at its centre, and the island is where the world quest Time and Wind begins.
### STANDARD +
The sundial is carved with a riddle about stories carried on the wind, and a second, matching sundial stands near the Thousand Winds Temple on the mainland. Reaching the island is itself a small discovery for a traveller.

## geography
### STANDARD
The island lies across the water northeast of Starfell Valley, within sight of the mainland coast in clear weather (Henry Morton says he has sometimes seen it). It has a central stone circle with a sundial, some ruined stonework and pillars along its edges. It is not marked on the map. Travellers cross by freezing the water with Cryo, gliding from Starsnatch Cliff, or using a Waverider.
### LARGE +
Wind is the island's character. Near its centre a strange gusting sound can be heard that grows stronger behind the sundial and stops at the very middle. The same sound is heard at the wind barrier around Stormterror's Lair.

## places
### STANDARD
The sundial, with its carved riddle, is the one notable feature. Around it are a stone circle, a few ruins on the eastern coast and a large pillar, all used as places where wind gathers during the quest. A breakable rock on the island's south end hides a ragged notebook.
### ULTRA +
The sundial's gnomon points west, so by day it is of little use as a clock. Its shadow only becomes meaningful at night: it falls straight downward in the small hours, which is the clue the notebook describes.

## history
### STANDARD
Nobody on the island is known. Its makers and purpose are unrecorded. The only human trace is the notebook left by an archaeologist who surveyed the matching sundial site at the Thousand Winds Temple, then vanished; his friend Henry Morton, a scholar, searched for him in Mondstadt.
### LARGE +
The notebook's author reasoned that the island and the Thousand Winds Temple were linked, that together the sundial inscriptions form one message, and that the pair may relate to a forgotten god of Time, possibly connected by ancient scholars to the Anemo Archon. He noted that something odd happens there between about 2 and 5 in the morning, but, lacking a Vision, he could not investigate. This is his theory, not confirmed canon.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Quiet, windblown and slightly uncanny: a lonely islet with old carved stone and sea air, where the wind seems to carry a faint whisper in the early hours.

## dangers
### STANDARD
A Ruin Guard (an elite enemy) sometimes spawns there. During the quest, wind clusters dispersed with Anemo summon Anemo slimes, and an Eye of the Storm (a pure elemental lifeform) appears.

## quests
### STANDARD
Time and Wind (world quest, 1.0). Reaching the island, breaking the rock on its south end and reading the Ragged Notebook starts it. The Traveler uses Elemental Sight in the early morning to find and disperse wind clusters, scatters a deposit of wind, and fights the Eye of the Storm, which flees toward the Thousand Winds Temple. There the Traveler finds the identical sundial and Henry Morton, who learns of the island and plans to visit it with the Traveler.
### LARGE +
In the second half the Traveler must again wait for the small hours, disperse four small wind clusters and scatter another deposit of wind, and face a stronger Eye of the Storm. The faint voices heard along the way speak of an ancient tale carried on the wind and of something that will sprout again in time. Morton concludes the two sundials and places must be linked but the quest ends without explaining the link.

## status
### STANDARD
As of 7.1 the island is unchanged and still uninhabited. No later content revisits the riddle, the sundials or the missing archaeologist.
