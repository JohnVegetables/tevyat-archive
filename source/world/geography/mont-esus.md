## summary
### LITE
Mont Esus is a very large mountain in Fontaine, north-west of the Court of Fontaine. It appears in the in-game expedition menu but cannot be visited. What players can see is its foothills by the sea close to Palais Mermonia, a gentle landscape of wide flower fields, and its eastern foothills, which form the Mont Esus East subarea.
### STANDARD +
Fontaine's official expedition text speaks of a magnificent palace, now gone, that once stood among the flowers.

## geography
### STANDARD
The mountain lies north-west of the Court of Fontaine. Its foothills run down to the sea near Palais Mermonia, where a gentle breeze crosses broad fields of flowers. The eastern foothills are reachable as the Mont Esus East subarea of the Liffey Region. The mountain itself is the only expedition location in the game that is not accessible.
### LARGE +
According to the wiki, the mountain's backdrop is higher than the Celestial Nail on Dragonspine and gives a view across most of Teyvat, from Mondstadt to Nod-Krai, except Inazuma. Treat this as an observation of the in-game scenery rather than a stated fact.

### ULTRA +
Mont Esus East consists of the eastern foothills of the mountain and sits in the Liffey Region; its wiki page keeps "before" and "after" images dated to Version 4.6, so the area was visibly changed then, but the pages read do not say how or why. The wiki lists a fishing point there but no lore text.

## places
### STANDARD
The flowery meadows by the foothills are where the viewpoint "Memories of Mont Esus" stands (a windmill turns lazily in the breeze); it appears after completing the Court of Fontaine Region quest "Good Stuff, but Terrible Taste". The Mont Esus East foothills are a separate subarea.

## history
### STANDARD
According to ancient records cited in the expedition text, a magnificent palace was once built here. It was named after the flowers and meant to sing praises of love and to nurture abandoned orphans above the countless waters. The high tower of old no longer stands, but the flowers remain as fragrant as ever.
### LARGE +
The palace is not named in the text, and no source ties it to a specific institution; do not state which one it was.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Gentle breezes, flower fields stretching to the sea, a lazy windmill, and a sense of peaceful memory. The viewpoint text says the relaxed mood makes people dwell on pleasant memories; it adds that Salsa once painted the scenery here.

## travel
### STANDARD
The mountain cannot be entered. The viewpoint over the foothills is in the Court of Fontaine Region, and the eastern foothills belong to the Liffey Region as Mont Esus East.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 Mont Esus is still a distant, inaccessible peak. Nothing in 6.x or 7.0-7.1 content changed that.

## entry: Memories of Mont Esus (viewpoint)
@keys: Memories of Mont Esus, Mont Esus Foothills
@keys2: Salsa, Fontaine
### STANDARD
A viewpoint in the Court of Fontaine Region among flowery meadows, with a windmill whose arms creak and turn in the breeze. It unlocks after completing the quest "Good Stuff, but Terrible Taste (Court of Fontaine Region)". The text says Salsa once painted the scenery, and that the peaceful atmosphere draws people to their happiest memories.
