## summary
### LITE
Musoujin Gorge is a narrow, majestic rift valley in the east of Yashiori Island. It is no natural feature: legend and official texts say it is the scar left when the Electro Archon killed the great serpent god Orobashi with her sword technique, the Musou no Hitotachi, cleaving both serpent and island. Lightning still echoes in the canyon, and the Tatarigami, the lingering curse of the slain god, broods in its gloom.
### STANDARD +
The name refers to that stroke: Musoujin Hazama is "the gorge of the Musou blade". The in-game explorer's text calls the valley a testament to the Electro Archon's skill with the sword. Electro Seelie, the "thunder elves", are said to chatter about the legendary strike that tore through sky and earth.

## geography
### STANDARD
A deep canyon running through the eastern part of Yashiori Island, in the island's east. The air is thick with the remnants of lightning bolts, and a river of electrified water runs through it. Thunder Sakura boughs grow throughout. Naku Weed and Onikabuto are the local specialties.
### LARGE +
The remains of Orobashi are scattered across Yashiori Island, with its skull at Serpent's Head. A teleport waypoint lies between the gorge and Nazuchi Beach, and a formation of six sword hilts stands south of the gorge.

## history
### STANDARD
Orobashi, a serpent god worshipped as Watatsumi Omikami, led a navy against Yashiori Island, and the Shogunate under the Electro Archon waged a bloody war on Watatsumi. Towards its end Ei slew the serpent with the Musou no Hitotachi; the stroke split the island and made the gorge. The serpent's will and power became the Tatarigami, a curse of storms and madness on the island, which the Archon suppressed with wards. The people of Watatsumi still worship Orobashi.
### LARGE +
The slash is also said to have killed Orobashi's vassal ruler of Yashiori, Touzannou (later called Akuou). Other casualties of the war included Ei's lieutenant Sasayuri. The Kitain Clan later took the duty of guarding the wards as the Yashiori Guardians until the line ended with Kitain Hideie's escape during the cataclysm. During the Vision Hunt Decree, a radical group in the Sangonomiya Resistance destroyed the wards at the Narukami Pillars, believing it would restore Orobashi's dignity; this unleashed the Tatarigami, and a Fatui mole named Nathan had pushed them to it. The Traveler resolved the crisis in the world quest series Orobashi's Legacy.
### ULTRA +
The Shogunate later used the Tatarigami's lingering power to fuel the Mikage Furnace, according to the world quest Tatara Tales. The Sangonomiya people accepted Shogunate rule after Orobashi's death, but the Shogun later said they were allowed to keep worshipping Orobashi so that it could keep receiving prayers.

## entry: Orobashi, the Serpent God
@keys: Orobashi, Orobashi no Mikoto, Orobaxi, Watatsumi Omikami, Great Serpent
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Orobashi was an ancient serpent god, a coral-covered being said to shine with colour, who reigned in Enkanomiya and later on Watatsumi Island as Watatsumi Omikami. Its people were taught farming and smelting by it and revere it as a guardian. It broke off coral from its body to make Watatsumi Island so that the people of Enkanomiya could return to the surface. Watatsumi suffered famine and sickness, and the invasion of Yashiori was meant to relieve it.
### LARGE +
Celestia judged Orobashi and its people guilty of profanity for learning the forbidden history in the book Before Sun and Moon, and Orobashi took all the transgressions on itself. The wiki account of the Sangonomiya folk belief says the god refused many times to attack Yashiori, knowing the Shogun's record of killing rival gods, and eventually relented. The shrine maiden Mouun and her twin sister Ayame led its navy, and Mouun's disciple Touzannou was named vassal ruler. The true reason for its death was meant to stay secret; a rumour says Celestia ordered it.

## entry: Musou no Hitotachi
@keys: Musou no Hitotachi, Musou Isshin
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The Musou no Hitotachi is the legendary sword technique of the Electro Archon, used by Ei and by the Shogun. The wielder dashes through the target, cutting a point in space with the Musou Isshin and briefly freezing time before lightning bolts strike. Inazumans revere it almost as much as the Archon herself, and the Shogun has used it to execute those who lose duels before the throne.
### LARGE +
In Japanese the technique's Musou is written with the kanji for "blank mind", while the Musou Isshin and, after Chapter II, the Elemental Burst's description use the kanji for "dream"; the distinction is lost in English. For another use of the technique, see the Mt. Yougou entry.

## entry: Tatarigami
@keys: Tatarigami, Curse God, Serpent's Head
@version: 2.0
### STANDARD
The Tatarigami is a curse formed from Orobashi's will and power. Unsuppressed, it brings perpetual thunderstorms, withering soil and untreatable illness, from fevers and bleeding to hallucinations of a blood-eyed god called "Him". Vision-bearers are less affected. Higi Village was abandoned after the wards fell, and the Shogunate's investigators began succumbing. Some people, such as Chouji's mother, show unusual resistance.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
A deep, majestic cleft whose walls still seem to hum with spent lightning, under a heavy sky; the island's thunderstorms and wet weather press in. The place feels like the aftermath of a divine verdict rather than a battlefield: quiet, charged and a little holy.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Balethunder in the gorge steadily drains health unless Thunderwood is near or an Electrogranum protects the visitor; the electrified river drains it faster. Hilichurl Berserkers, Electro Hilichurl Grenadiers, Kairagi and Nobushi are the enemies met there. Tatarigami influence lingers on the island.

## travel
### LARGE
Thunder Sakura boughs summon Electrograna that shield travellers from the Balethunder. The gorge lies close to Nazuchi Beach, with a Teleport Waypoint between the two.

## quests
### LARGE
Orobashi's Legacy (Yashiori Island world quests, 2.0) concerns the Tatarigami; the Archon Quest "In the Name of the Resistance" (Chapter II) climaxes on the neighbouring Nazuchi Beach.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the gorge remains a scarred, thunderous canyon with hostile creatures, and Orobashi is still worshipped by the people of Watatsumi.
### STANDARD +
The Vision Hunt Decree has ended, most Fatui have withdrawn from Yashiori, and the abnormal storms of the island last only until Orobashi's Legacy is completed. No post-2.x canon found by 7.1 changes the gorge itself.
