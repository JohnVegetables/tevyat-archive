## summary
### LITE
Mawtiyima Forest is a forest of enormous fluorescent mushrooms at the start of Sumeru's Lokapala Jungle, in the Dharma Forest. Giant mushroom caps form a canopy of light that hides the sun and moon. The forest is guarded by Mawtiyima, an energy form made of its ancient dreams and memories, and it is the home of Aranaga's little garden, where the Aranara tend their friends the flora and fungi.
### STANDARD +
Its monumental plants grow nowhere else. The Expedition text calls it the dense forest that defends northern Sumeru, once tainted by the filth that corroded the entire world and only returned to its giant form after much revivification and purification.
### LARGE +
The Aranara and Mawtiyima appear to communicate. In the world quest "The Final Chapter" (3.0), the last part of the Agnihotra Sutra series, the Traveler helps three Aranara cleanse the forest's heart of the Withering.

## geography
### STANDARD
Lokapala Jungle begins here, close to Minlin and to Chenyu Vale's Southern Mountain, and runs along the north coast of the Dharma Forest toward the west slope of a nameless mountain near Bayda Harbor, where it joins the Desert of Hadramaveth and the Realm of Farakhkert. Inside the forest, under the fungal caps, lies the hidden Mawtiyima point.
### LARGE +
Wiki-listed features include the hovering Valaya rings, Tri-Yana Seeds, Stone Pillar Seals, Seed Mirrors, bouncy mushrooms, poisonous mushrooms and Phantasmal Gates; local specialties are Rukkhashava Mushrooms and Kalpalata Lotus. A viewpoint at Aranaga's little garden is described as a place where "the pomegranate sings and the apples clap."

## people
### STANDARD
Aranaga, Aranakin and Aragaru, three Aranara, tend the forest. Aranaga calls the Mawtiyima Forest his garden and says Mawtiyima protects it and its flora and mushroom friends. Khayyam, an NPC who camps in the forest, cannot recall something he is trying to remember and is thankful that the forest has been revived.
### LARGE +
The Aranara speak in a halting, childlike manner and call the Traveler "Nara"; for them Mawtiyima is a being they protect and are protected by. Memory is central to them: they say a new life is nourished by memory, that death is only a loss of memory, and that what they have lived through will be forgotten but will remain in the soil and roots of the forest.

## history
### STANDARD
The Aranara's tale says that in the first moon the fungi woke, in the second the branches, and in the third the flowers, until Marana crept out of the moons' shadows and devoured Mawtiyima. The forest was later healed with the help of the Traveler and the Aranara's Kusava, a seed-and-fruit carrying the Aranara's memories and power.
### LARGE +
In The Final Chapter the Traveler and Paimon follow Aranaga to find the Yajna Grass, perform the Arahaoma ritual under the moon, and offer the elixir to Jamikayomars, which unlocks the entrance to Mawtiyima. Inside, they destroy three Withering Branches and then a last Tumor of the Withering after fighting fungal beasts, using the Kusava's power of dreams. Afterwards, the three Aranara stay with the awakened Mawtiyima, which has stories to tell them.
### ULTRA +
Nara Varuna, a mysterious person who keeps a place in the memories of many Aranara, is said to have left behind the energy that feeds the forest. The Aranara plan to meet the Traveler again at the Festival Utsava, and Aranakin gives them a song so they will not be forgotten.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Slimes, fungi, hilichurls, Treasure Hoarders and Eremites roam; the elite enemies include Fatui Cicin Mages, Mirror Maidens and Fatui Pyro Agents, and an Electro Regisvine is the area boss. Before the cleansing, the Withering in the forest was among the strongest in the game.

## quests
### STANDARD
"The Final Chapter" (3.0) is the main quest here. After "Memory's Final Chapter" a hidden quest has the Traveler speak to Khayyam the next day at his camp, where he thanks them for the forest's revival and gives them a gift. "Homecoming" (a 3.6 Nahida story quest) also mentions a group of fungi found near Mawtiyima Forest, one of which Nahida revives.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Glowing grass, giant caps that block sun and moon, and a sense that the forest knows you have arrived. Paimon remarks on entering that the grass is glowing and that the place feels aware of them.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the forest is healthy and open. The Withering was driven out in The Final Chapter, though the memory of that struggle is fading for the Aranara.

## entry: Mawtiyima
@keys: Mawtiyima, Morty Arena
@keys2: forest, Aranara, Withering
### STANDARD
An energy form created by the forest's ancient dreams and memories. It keeps the forest alive, fights the Withering and appears to communicate with the Aranara, who treat it as a she. Paimon's "Morty Arena" is a mispronunciation, not a name. In the ancient tongue the name meant "human death." Its inner place is sealed until The Final Chapter, when the Arahaoma is offered to Jamikayomars.
