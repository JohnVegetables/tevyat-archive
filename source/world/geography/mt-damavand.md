## summary
### LITE
Mt. Damavand is the huge hollow mountain at the centre of the Desert of Hadramaveth in Sumeru's Great Red Sand. A massive tornado whirls over its middle, and the sandstorm around it never stops. In the lost language of the desert dwellers its name means "the mountain from which smoke and ash arise". Beneath it lie the Orchard of Pairidaeza, also called the Eternal Oasis, and ancient palaces and pipelines.
### STANDARD +
Wandering desert tribes believe the Eternal Oasis is hidden inside; they are right. The mountain is explored in the world-quest chain "The Dirge of Bilqis" (3.4).
### LARGE +
The Celestial Nail page connects the great crater, the withered Irminsul beside the Eternal Oasis, and the permanent central tornado with a nail that fell here and desertified a once-lush land. In Nahida's story quest "Homecoming" (3.6) the Dendro Dragon Apep is revealed to live in that storm.

## geography
### STANDARD
Wadi Al-Majuj, a canyon area, lies south of the mountain, and the Passage of Ghouls connects that wadi to the Sands of Al-Azif. The loading screen says the mountain is surrounded by countless complex and crumbling gullies and canyons spreading in all directions. Underneath it are ancient palaces and intricate pipelines "where the age-old dreams of the gods and their servants are buried".
### LARGE +
The Orchard of Pairidaeza can be entered only through Deshret's Glass Goblet, a chamber below the mountain whose central platform is the oasis's entrance; Bayt Al-Hayah, the "House of Life", is a cavern network beneath Wounded Shin Valley that leads into it. The Orchard holds a withered Irminsul tree beside its central island and three chairs for the three God-Kings of Sumeru.

## history
### STANDARD
Legend says King Deshret found a divine nail and used its power and the Jinn to build the Eternal Oasis here as a cemetery for the Goddess of Flowers, Nabu Malikata, freezing a single moment of her world in place forever. Water, animals and trees are all held motionless, and the water can be walked on. The oasis and its machines were kept by the great Jinni Ferigees.
### LARGE +
The Records of Mt. Damavand are broken tablets that become readable as the Traveler fixes pipes in the quest "Dune-Entombed Fecundity". They tell of a priesthood worried that the Orchard's water output was falling and the outer oases shrinking, that Lady Ferigees was growing weaker and no longer answered calls, and that sand now clogged the pipes. Jinn fragments implanted in the machinery made irrigation efficient, yet the priests wondered whether the Jinn also age. Ferigees refused large-scale repairs because they would disturb the Goddess of Flowers' tomb.
### ULTRA +
Legend says Ferigees destroyed herself to protect the city of Gurabad. A later loading screen says she has fully dissipated, and that the Orchard is likewise heading for an inevitable end. An older text claims her slumber continues in the depths; the two accounts conflict (see uncertainties).

## dangers
### STANDARD
Caravans vanish in the sandstorms, so few are willing to visit this desert. Damaged Records found in the Passage of Ghouls tell of an investigation team that ran out of food and water while looking for the path to the Eternal Oasis and finally prayed to the Goddess of Flowers to carry their souls there. Inside the mountain are Geo fungi and ancient mechanisms.
### LARGE +
The Traveler's route down the mountain, in "Dune-Entombed Fecundity: Part I", involves an abandoned Eremite camp, mysterious mechanisms and junction puzzles, and fights with Stretchy Geo Fungi and Geoshrooms.

## quests
### STANDARD
"The Dirge of Bilqis" (3.4) leads the Traveler, with Jeht, Azariq, Babel and the Jinn Liloupar, down into the mountain. "The Eternal Dream, Ever Lush" has the Traveler visit the Eternal Oasis with Jeht. In "Homecoming" (3.6), Nahida and the Traveler go to the sandstorm to speak to Apep.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
A permanent storm around a hollow mountain; deep below, the Eternal Oasis is frozen in a single moment, with even its water held still.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the mountain still sits in its storm. Apep, the Dendro Dragon, remains in it.
### STANDARD +
Ferigees is gone, and the Eternal Oasis is no longer hidden from the Traveler.

## entry: Apep in the Storm
@keys: Apep, Dendro Dragon
@keys2: Mt. Damavand, Homecoming, Nahida
@version: 3.6
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
In "Homecoming" (3.6), after Nahida learns that fungi she has been reviving were once elemental lifeforms living inside Apep, she and the Traveler cross the Desert of Hadramaveth to the sandstorm. Apep notices them at once, emerges, and tells them to leave, rejecting Nahida's claim of coming to heal her; she calls both Nahida and Amun no different, being gods who lean on the glow of the heavens. The wiki says one nail fell over this mountain and turned its forests into the Great Red Sand, from which Apep and her children survived.

## entry: The Orchard of Pairidaeza
@keys: Orchard of Pairidaeza, Eternal Oasis, Deshret's Glass Goblet, Goddess of Flowers' tomb
@keys2: Mt. Damavand, King Deshret, Nabu Malikata
### STANDARD
An enclosed garden under Mt. Damavand, built by King Deshret on a divine nail as a memorial to the Goddess of Flowers, with everything in it frozen. Entered only through Deshret's Glass Goblet. Three chairs stand at its centre for Sumeru's three God-Kings, with abbreviated names in Deshret Script; one chair carries the name of Greater Lord Rukkhadevata, which the world has otherwise lost. The name Pairidaeza is Avestan for an enclosed garden.
