## summary
### LITE
Maroon Basin is a wide beach of distinctive pink stones on Paha Isle in Nod-Krai. It was once a thriving habitat for Tassel Jellies, but the Fatui's activity on the island has left it barren.
### STANDARD +
Notes left by Fatui personnel there show a guarded, kuuvahki-controlled installation that struggled with stray jellyfish.

## geography
### STANDARD
A broad shoreline of pink stones on Paha Isle, the island associated with the Frostladen Machinery. The wiki notes that the German name may nod to a real red-sand beach in Finland, which is a naming observation and not lore.
### LARGE +
The stones give the beach its look; the surrounding waters were the Tassel Jellies' home before the Fatui moved in.

## people
### STANDARD
Fatui personnel patrol the area. Two named patrol staff, Riku and Dima, appear in logs found there, taking morning, afternoon and night shifts.
### LARGE +
Their logs are casual and complaining in tone: Dima keeps forgetting his ID card and hides a spare near the door, Riku nods off on night shifts, and both are glad when higher-ups hand the jellyfish problem to recon bots.

## dangers
### STANDARD
The installation sits in a managed kuuvahki environment. A found note describes the open state as a weak kuuvahki environment and the closed state as strong, and warns that equipment must be given kuuvahki through the separator and never the reverse.
### LARGE +
The main hazard is the Fatui presence itself.

## history
### LARGE
Earlier the basin was a thriving Tassel Jelly habitat; Fatui activity on the island then made it barren. The patrol records show that jellyfish kept slipping into the barrier: the guards at first released them by hand, and some that could not leave were disposed of. Management later assigned recon bots to the problem.

## quests
### LARGE
The wiki lists three quests touching the area: Drifting Toward a Promised Sky, Flins: Patrol and Jahoda: Cat! Hey! Cat! Their content is not summarised here.
### ULTRA +
The area was first available in Luna I (6.0), when Nod-Krai opened. The kuuvahki separator note and the patrol logs are the only lore documents recorded for the basin itself.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the basin is described as barren of Tassel Jellies; no later change is recorded in the sources read.

## entry: Maroon Basin Patrol Records
@keys: Maroon Basin, Riku, Dima, Patrol Records
@keys2: Paha Isle, Fatui, Tassel Jelly
### STANDARD
A set of logs written by two Fatui guards, Riku and Dima, alternating shifts around a kuuvahki barrier. Jellyfish repeatedly got inside; they were released or, when unable to leave, disposed of, until higher-ups gave the task to recon bots. The logs repeatedly record "no abnormalities".
