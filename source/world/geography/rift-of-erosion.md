## summary
### LITE
The Rift of Erosion is a point of interest in the Fortress of Meropide, west of the Dormitory Block in the Administrative Area. It is called a "potential danger" and is guarded by Galvana.
### STANDARD +
Little else is stated. Galvana muses about sealing it off, and a locking mechanism like the one used here is later requested at the Geode Mine Shaft.

## geography
### STANDARD
Located to the west of the Dormitory Block, Administrative Area of the Fortress of Meropide.
### LARGE +
Galvana notes that the space is not small and would be good to put to use, while also agreeing it would be safer to seal it off.
### ULTRA +
When the Traveler jokes about sealing every entrance and exit, Galvana finds that extreme, and she blames the frequent breakage of searchlights on their installed energy storage devices or on people ignoring the instructions.

## people
### STANDARD
Galvana stands guard at the rift until the quest Scenes from Life in Meropide: Dead End is completed, after which she moves to outside the elevator to the lower Production Zone. She has a younger twin sister, Galvaryet, and prefers to be serious during work hours.

## history
### STANDARD
Guards had Clynes install a locking mechanism at the Geode Mine Shaft "like the one at the Rift of Erosion", as noted in An Eye for an Eye.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Treated by its guard as a potential danger; the rift's actual hazard is not described.

## status
### STANDARD
Guarded and unchanged as of 7.1 apart from Galvana's relocation after the Dead End quest.
