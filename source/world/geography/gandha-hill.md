## summary
### LITE
Gandha Hill is a hill in the Avidya Forest of Sumeru's Dharma Forest, the lush rainforest of the north. It has no named settlement or landmark of its own and no stated history; it is simply a stretch of rainforest slope that travellers cross.
### STANDARD +
It was added with Sumeru in version 3.0. Treat it as a quiet corner of the forest rather than a destination.

## geography
### STANDARD
Part of Avidya Forest, the green, populous eastern rainforest that also holds Sumeru City and the Forest Watchers' base at Gandharva Ville. The wiki lists no terrain features, points of interest or local specialties for the hill itself.
### LARGE +
An in-game puzzle near the hill has a strange plant ringed by Pyro Slimes; when the puzzle is solved a drawing lights up and points in a certain direction, as Paimon remarks. This sits near the entrance of a domain used for the recurring Fayz Trials event.

## people
### LARGE
No resident NPCs are recorded. The random event "Unscrupulous Dealings" can begin here: a group of Fatui and Eremites, which Paimon reads as an under-the-table deal, can be found haggling; around Gandha Hill and Chinvat Ravine the wiki lists Eremite crossbowmen, linebreakers and sword-dancers, Fatui Skirmishers and a sumpter beast.

## quests
### LARGE
Gandha Hill is one of the places where the "Hammer of the Mushrooms" daily commission (a mushroom-knocking challenge) and the "Unscrupulous Dealings" random event can start. Both are repeatable open-world activities, not story content.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 nothing is recorded as changing here.

## atmosphere
### ULTRA
A rainforest hillside in the green Avidya Forest. The canon disturbances recorded here are Pyro Slimes around the puzzle plant and mercenary or Fatui parties met by chance; no other mood is described.

### ULTRA
The wiki notes that "Gandha" may derive from Gandhamadana, the Fragrant Mountain where celestial gandharvas live in Indian religious tradition; the Chinese name means roughly Mesmerizing Fragrance Slope. This is an out-of-universe naming note, not stated by the game.
