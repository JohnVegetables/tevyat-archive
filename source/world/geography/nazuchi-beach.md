## summary
### LITE
Nazuchi Beach is a stretch of shallow coast joining Kannazuka and Yashiori Island in Inazuma. Its name once meant "to be tenderly caressed by the hands of the gods", but the shoal has seen far too much battle. Torn flags, broken arrows, shattered masts and wrecked decks lie along it, and it has long been a haunt of scavengers, pirates and ronin. In the Archon Quest it is where the Sangonomiya Resistance faced the Shogun's Army.
### STANDARD +
During the Vision Hunt Decree war it was a front line between the Tenryou Commission and the Watatsumi Army, fought over by both land and sea. Few people live there; those who are met are mostly soldiers on missions, Treasure Hoarders and wanderers searching for lost things.

## geography
### STANDARD
A shallow coastal strip joining Kannazuka and Yashiori Island, with a Teleport Waypoint in its western part and another in its northern part. Violet Ibis rest on the sand among the torn banners, arrows and wrecked ships. Dendrobium grows here, a rare flower for such a place.
### LARGE +
A large ship is stranded between the two waypoints, and several wrecks hold pirate diaries. An islet just to the south, between the waypoint here and the one at Musoujin Gorge, is where a retired Shogunate soldier wanders. Sword hilts stuck in the sand and rock mark fallen samurai; with Electrograna they can be energised, which wakes the Ochimusha, spirits of the dead, so that they may be put to rest (a hidden exploration objective).

## people
### STANDARD
The Shogunate soldiers Genzou and a superior, Terada, are on a mission near the waypoint; Genzou is the self-effacing subordinate, and Terada is evasive when asked what his mission is. The Treasure Hoarders Miura and Onoda scavenge the south, with Miura the bold boss and Onoda the nervous underling who fears running into either army; Miura's plan is to say they are with whoever they meet. Utsumi, a retired Shogunate soldier, searches an islet for his late son's belongings.
### LARGE +
Utsumi's son joined the Shogunate as a samurai and died on the battlefield; Utsumi says he was buried in Kannazuka. Pirates sheltered a sick miner at their camp in their diaries, mention caring for the boy Chouji on the instructions of a man called Onitaka, and write of marking their borders with the belongings of defeated Shogunate stragglers. One diary also records a pirate with a stubborn fever and crew waking from nightmares; it does not name a cause.

## history
### STANDARD
The beach has been a war zone for close to a thousand years, according to the in-game texts. In the Vision Hunt Decree conflict the Sangonomiya Resistance's front line was pushed back a couple of times until it settled at Nazuchi Beach, where fighting was chaotic and scattered forces could not tell friend from foe.
### LARGE +
In the Archon Quest "In the Name of the Resistance" (Chapter II, Act II) the Traveler finds the front here. Kujou Sara, commander of the Shogun's Army, offers a deal, handing over the Traveler for a pause in the fighting, which General Gorou refuses, saying the resistance never betrays its own. She then proposes a "fight of honor" of single combat, and the Traveler beats three Shogunate fighters in a row. When Sara orders a full assault, Sangonomiya Kokomi, the Divine Priestess of Watatsumi Island and the resistance's organiser, appears with long-hidden reinforcements, including the mercenaries Beidou and Kaedehara Kazuha, and Sara retreats. Kokomi introduces herself to the Traveler and invites them to Sangonomiya Shrine.
### ULTRA +
The three Shogunate fighters in the fight of honor are Fujita Sanshirou ("Spear Demon Fujita"), Kayama Tadao ("Bamboo-Breaking Tiger") and Takasaka Izumi ("Kujou's Right Hand"). Before the battle, a fleeing man named Jiichirou says he had been recuperating on the beach and that the resistance lacks the manpower for an all-out confrontation and had retreated to it more than once. After the fight Kokomi asks Gorou to stay to watch the Shogun's Army and see to the wounded, promising mercenaries to help, and warns that the war is far from over because the Vision Hunt Decree still stands.

## entry: Kannazuka and Fort Fujitou
@keys: Kannazuka, Fort Fujitou, Mikage Furnace
@spoiler: none
### STANDARD
Kannazuka ("the hill where no gods reside") is the rocky disputed territory beside the beach, claimed by neither the Shogunate nor Sangonomiya, where neither Inazuman nor Sangonomiya faith has influence. Mikage Furnace, Inazuma's largest smelting facility, which makes Jade Steel, lies there. General Gorou of the resistance is found at Fort Fujitou.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Violet Ibis over a shoreline of splintered hulls, pennants half sunk in sand and rusted blades; the smell of salt and smoke. It feels like the field where a long war has ended, but never stopped sounding underfoot.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Hilichurls, Pyro and Electro hilichurl shooters, Hydro slimes, Electro Whopperflowers, Nobushi and Kairagi, plus elites such as the Mirror Maiden and the Crackling Axe Mitachurl.

## travel
### LARGE
Two waypoints (west and north); Musoujin Gorge and Kannazuka lie close by. Thunder Sakura boughs are available for Electrograna.

## quests
### LARGE
The Chapter II Archon Quest "In the Name of the Resistance" (2.0). Hidden objective Put Them To Rest; the pirate diaries are collectible lore.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the beach remains a deserted battlefield of wrecks, stragglers and scavengers, with the Vision Hunt Decree war long over.
### STANDARD +
The decree was ended after the Archon Quest; no later canon through 7.1 changes the beach.
