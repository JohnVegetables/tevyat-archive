## summary
### LITE
Suigetsu Pool, the "Water-Moon Pool", is a subarea of Watatsumi Island in Inazuma. A once-famous mansion belonging to the warrior Umigozen lies beneath it, and the place is covered with ancient ruins and Ruin Machines. The mansion is the setting of the one-time domain Palace in a Pool.
### STANDARD +
The domain's lore text says that long ago seawater had yet to fill the pool and moonlight filled it instead, flowing like quicksilver.

## geography
### STANDARD
A sunken pool and a ruin-strewn stretch of Watatsumi Island, with a hole leading down to the mansion. Electro Seelie, Electro Current puzzles, Thunder Sakura Boughs, Lightning Strike Probes, Thunder Dwellings and a Waverider Waypoint are found here. Sango Pearl and Sea Ganoderma are local specialties.
### LARGE +
The surface ruins and the passages beneath include Ruin Sentinels, an inactive Ruin Guard and a small inactive Ruin Hunter. To open the domain the player energises four Lightning Strike Probes with Electrograna to unlock Electro monuments, lowers the water, and drains it fully with a switch in a room beyond.

## places
### STANDARD
Umigozen's Mansion (Palace in a Pool, the single level "Tutelage: Umigozen's Mansion"): once a grand house with a window for drinking wine and watching blossoms, now empty except for bandits, Nobushi and Treasure Hoarders, with sliding doors that hide amulets needed to open the last gate.

## people
### STANDARD
Umigozen is Yuna Ayame, a diver of the Yuna Clan of Watatsumi Island who lived about 2,000 years ago and was one of the leaders of the Watatsumi forces in the Narukami-Watatsumi War. Her older twin sister was the shrine maiden Mouun. She was the first owner of the weapon Wavebreaker's Fin and disappeared into the ocean at the war's end.

## history
### STANDARD
After the war the mansion stood abandoned; seawater covered it and its entrance was sealed. Today the only occupants are criminals using the place as a hideout.
### LARGE +
Ayame and Mouun taught a nameless orphan who later became the king Touzannou, Akuou. In the war that began when Watatsumi invaded Yashiori Island, Ei ended the fighting by killing Orobashi and Akuou, while Mouun was ambushed and killed by tengu who served under Sasayuri. Ayame's Wavebreaker's Fin was separated from her during the war and taken by the tengu.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Treasure Hoarders (Pugilists and Potioneers), Nobushi including a Mirror Maiden, and ancient Ruin Machines.

## quests
### STANDARD
No world quest is set here in the pages read; the Palace in a Pool domain is the main attraction, and a torn page of the Toki Alley Tales storybook can be found in the ruins.
### ULTRA +
The domain's enemy line-up is a Nobushi Hitsukeban or Mirror Maiden, a Treasure Hoarder Pugilist, two Cryo Potioneers and three Pyro Potioneers. Four artifact investigation points, a common chest and a precious chest are inside. On the name, the wiki notes that umigozen is a folklore term for a sea spirit and also a form of address for female warriors, and that one theory has Ayame sailing into the Dark Sea; neither is confirmed by the game.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the mansion remains abandoned; no later canon read for this entry changes it.
