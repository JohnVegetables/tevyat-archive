## summary
### LITE
Sobek Oasis is an oasis in the Hypostyle Desert, part of Sumeru's Great Red Sand. Adventurers crossing the red desert have been known to rest here. A stone tablet nearby promises that a golden sandstorm and burning sun will exorcise the darkness for those who perished here and return their spirits to the Orchard of Pairidaeza.
### STANDARD +
It was added in version 3.1 with the Hypostyle Desert and is named, in the wiki's account, after Sobek, the Crocodile King.

## geography
### STANDARD
A watered spot in the Hypostyle Desert, the deep desert of the Great Red Sand. A teleport waypoint marks the oasis, and the stone tablet stands on the plateau north-east of it.
### LARGE +
The Dune of Carouses and its Tomb of Carouses lie in the same desert area; other points in the area include the Garden of Endless Pillars, once part of the sapphire city of Tulaytullah.

## history
### STANDARD
The stone tablet's blessing speaks of the Orchard of Pairidaeza, the Eternal Oasis, as the destination for souls: "the golden sandstorm and burning sun will exorcise the darkness on your behalf, guiding you who perished here". It is a funerary hope written for those who died in the desert.
### LARGE +
Sobek was one of the gods that King Deshret defeated and brought into his service. He became one of the Seven Pillars, the Crocodile King and Captain of Captains, a hot-tempered general who nevertheless blesses the land with abundance; his statues are commonly found in oases and water temples, and some mercenaries secretly worship him. These are the statements the wiki draws from the Guiding Notes and from the NPC Asenath.
## dangers
### STANDARD
The general hazards of the Great Red Sand: heat, sandstorms and scarce water. No enemy list is recorded for the oasis itself.

## entry: Sobek, the Crocodile King
@keys: Sobek, Crocodile King, Captain of Captains, Croco
@keys2: Seven Pillars, King Deshret, Hypostyle Desert, Aaru
@version: 6.6
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
One of King Deshret's Seven Pillars, defeated in the Archon War and brought into his service. Hot-tempered, straightforward and warlike, yet a giver of abundance; his statues stand in oases and water temples. He appears as a muscular figure with a crocodile's head, half of it blue, and a miniature statue on his head. He is the namesake of this oasis in the wiki's account.
### ULTRA +
He was later said to be a prisoner in Aaru together with Heryshaf and Thoth, passing the time with tabletop games such as chess, and grew easygoing. Thoth, who calls him "Croco", and the other two were said to have pushed Deshret to defy the Heavenly Principles and use forbidden knowledge, after twice being rebuffed; they were banished to Aaru for what they themselves call their wickedness.
