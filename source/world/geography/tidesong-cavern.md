## summary
### LITE
Tidesong Cavern is a hidden cave on Fellfrost Peak, the snowy mountain south of Snezhnograd in Snezhnaya, and the private practice space of the Water Imp Vodyanitsa. A flooded cave with a giant shell and a few Prime Ice echo pillars, it gives her voice the acoustics she wants and keeps her audience away.
### STANDARD +
The in-game description calls it a secluded cavern rarely visited by others, the private sanctuary of "a certain fae". Its map name is the Cavern of Melodic Tides, and it hosts a Meeting Point of the same name (introduced in 7.0).

## geography
### STANDARD
The cave lies below the slopes of Fellfrost Peak and is entered by climbing down an ice wall from a hole near the surface; the last stretch ends in a freezing puddle. Beyond are pools of water, a huge shell or conch in which Vodyanitsa sits, and the Echo Pillars of Prime Ice that shape how sound carries through the chamber. Local specialties found on the peak include Flockingweed and Frostfairy Flower.
### LARGE +
Vodyanitsa says she thought of flooding the entrance to seal it, but engineers warned that blasting the rock could cause landslides, avalanches and cracks, and that wrecking the cave's structure would ruin its acoustics, so she left it. The pillars glow softly and ring in harmony when correctly tuned; when she sings, cracked ones are marked out by lit notes above them.

## places
### STANDARD
The giant shell is Vodyanitsa's seat, and she blows bubbles from it for fun. The Echo Pillars of Prime Ice are the cave's key feature: a few had cracked while she practised, spoiling the "echo chamber" until the Traveler repaired them in the world quest. A tent and campfire are sometimes found where travelling adventurers have sheltered overnight.

### ULTRA +
Travellers sometimes leave traces: Vodyanitsa once found a tent, a campfire, a tidied camp and a Fonta bottle label, suggesting someone chipped ice from the cave to chill a drink, which amused her. After such overnight stays she notes that at least they cleaned up.

## people
### STANDARD
Vodyanitsa is the cave's occupant. Her fellow troupe members Odette and Alyosha visit and appear in post-quest open-world dialogue: Odette brings mints and stands unmoved by Vodyanitsa's teasing, and Alyosha shelters stray puppies there from the blizzard and offers to lend guard dogs, which Vodyanitsa declines.
### LARGE +
Vodyanitsa tells the Traveler she once practised in The Korolevskiy Theater, but crowds gathered below the open window, and one young fan who had once sent her flowers and letters started reselling the tickets she was given at several times their price. Losing the innocent enthusiast, she moved her practice here. She also practises at Rankova Zorya (rendered "Dawn Station" in the English subtitles).

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Cold, damp and echoing, with dripping water, ice walls and the sound of a powerful voice rolling around the chamber. Small animals that flee when she practises return once the pillars are repaired.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Mostly the climb and the cold. Vodyanitsa gives a freezing visitor a warming pill used by hunters rather than liquor. She is confident the blizzard and entrance keep wild animals and adventurers out, and that she could simply dive into the water and escape anyone who found her.

## travel
### STANDARD
Reached from Fellfrost Peak beyond Snezhnograd's walls by following the singing to the cave entrance and climbing down. The Meeting Point unlocks after completing the world quest "Like a Land Where the Tide's Song Whispers", which itself requires finishing step 6 of "Silence Alone is Disfavored".

## quests
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
The world quest "Like a Land Where the Tide's Song Whispers" has the Traveler follow a singing voice to the cave, meet Vodyanitsa, and repair the broken Prime Ice Echo Pillars guided by her song, ending with her singing a cappella to the restored cave. It leads into "The Tides Echo Themselves". The Meeting Point's rewards and companion quests are gameplay features.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the cavern is Vodyanitsa's working private retreat, with its pillars repaired and the Meeting Point available.

## entry: Vodyanitsa's Private Practice Room
@keys: Tidesong Cavern, Cavern of Melodic Tides, Echo Pillars
### STANDARD
Vodyanitsa's hidden practice room on Fellfrost Peak. She keeps it secret, spares the city from rehearsal noise, and relies on its Prime Ice Echo Pillars for acoustics. After the Traveler repaired the pillars she sang for them and said she would practise there that day and go to Rankova Zorya within days. Odette and Alyosha know the place.
