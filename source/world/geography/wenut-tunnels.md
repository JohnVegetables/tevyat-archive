## summary
### LITE
The Wenut Tunnels are the system of caverns below the Sands of Al-Azif in Sumeru's Desert of Hadramaveth. They are the dangerous home of the Wenut, huge eyeless sand-worms, and of the area boss Setekh Wenut.
### STANDARD +
The in-game viewpoint says the mortal palaces have long fallen and the Wenut now rule here, turning the place into a court and garden to their liking.

## geography
### STANDARD
A network of underground caverns arranged on three map levels (upper, middle and lower), beneath the Sands of Al-Azif. The wiki lists the City of the Deceased domain with them as a point of interest.
### LARGE +
The Sands of Al-Azif above are one of the flatter parts of the desert, with many Wenut roaming the surface; the tunnels below are the cavern system dangerous to enter that is their home.

## people
### STANDARD
No humans live here. The Wenut are eyeless predators that hunt by sound and vibration. The Setekh Wenut, titled The Last Prince of the Sands, is a boss of the Mystical Beasts type that strikes with physical and Anemo attacks.
### LARGE +
Archive text says the creatures once thrived alongside their progenitors and offspring in a land of grass and huge trees, swimming through wet soil like fish; when the gifts of heaven turned that soil to sand, the line that survived adapted to earth-living and became the Wenut. Some desert folk wrongly believe sandworms are the last stage of the quicksand eel.
### ULTRA +
The Adventurer Handbook says Wenut sometimes take plant-like shapes, and some say this is because they were scions of the lord of plants, while others say it lures prey seeking shade or fruit. Loading screens add that some legends make them near kin and descendants of dragons.

## history
### STANDARD
Setekh Wenut's text says the desert was once a land of fragrant flowers, huge trees and countless beasts, and that the only ones who remember it are the blind predators called Wenut, spoken of with respect and fear.
### LARGE +
Desert folk still hunt the Wenut for their valuable spices: fat from their bodies yields costly seasoning, and swindlers grind Sand Grease Pupae and sell them as Wenut spice.

## dangers
### STANDARD
The Wenut themselves, and Setekh Wenut. In a fight it first digs and sweeps, and later floats with Windbite Bullets that can be shattered with non-Anemo elements to paralyse it (gameplay).
### LARGE +
Eyewitnesses in the Fulminating Sandstorm event spoke of Wenut in the double digits at least.

## travel
### LARGE
The tunnels lie below the Sands of Al-Azif. How they are entered is not described in the sources read. Because Wenut hunt by sound and vibration, noisy movement is likely to draw them (an inference, not a stated rule).

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the tunnels are open and the Wenut still rule them.
### STANDARD +
No change to the area is recorded after version 3.4.

## entry: The Last Prince of the Sands
@keys: Last Prince of the Sands, Windbite Bullets, Setekh Wenut boss
@version: 3.4
### STANDARD
The boss of the Wenut Tunnels, an eyeless predator from deep in the Great Red Sand that finds prey by sound and vibration. Its attacks are physical and Anemo. It drops the Pseudo-Stamens. It also carries the title Lord of the Desert in its description.
### LARGE +
In its floating phase, Windbite Bullets orbit it; hitting two in a row with Cryo, Electro, Hydro or Pyro attacks paralyses it for about ten seconds and lowers its resistance to those elements (gameplay detail, not lore).

## entry: Fulminating Sandstorm and the Wenutslayer Cannon
@keys: Wenutslayer Cannon, Fulminating Sandstorm
@keys2: Wenut, Shriya, Vinayak
@version: 3.4
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
Limited-time event (Fulminating Sandstorm). The Kshahrewar researchers Shriya and Vinayak, who bicker over funding and design, build a turret called the Wenutslayer Cannon against the Wenut's violent behaviour; Shriya studies why the Wenut and the environment are behaving abnormally. The event is set in the Desert of Hadramaveth, and Vinayak claims eyewitnesses saw Wenut numbering in the double digits.
