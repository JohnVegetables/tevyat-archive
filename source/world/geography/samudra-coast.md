## summary
### LITE
Samudra Coast is the far northwest edge of Sumeru's desert, a shore of sandy beaches and clear blue water in the Realm of Farakhkert. Under it lies Madinat al-Nuhas, an old underground weapons testing site of the fallen kingdom of Khaenri'ah.
### STANDARD +
It was added with the Girdle of the Sands in version 3.6, and is where the wandering Fontaine artist Julien camps in the world quest "An Artist Adrift".

## geography
### STANDARD
A beach and bay at the northwest limit of the desert, close to Asipattravana Swamp and the Hills of Barsom. The wiki's etymology note says samudra is the Sanskrit word for a large body of water.
### LARGE +
A cave by the water lies between the coast and the swamp. Madinat al-Nuhas can be entered from the west end of Asipattravana Swamp and extends out beneath the coast.

## places
### STANDARD
Madinat al-Nuhas ("City of Brass", a title borrowed from the Thousand and One Nights) was an underground Khaenri'ahn facility for testing weapons, particularly Ruin Machines. It still holds an incomplete prototype Ruin Golem and is powered by Azosite, like Hangeh Afrasiyab.
### LARGE +
The facility was apparently adapted into a fortification from which Abyss monsters could be attacked, but it saw little use for that purpose. It is unlocked during "As the Khvarena's Light Shows: Nirodha".

## people
### STANDARD
Julien, who calls himself a renowned artist from Fontaine (the Traveler and Paimon doubt the title), is camped nearby. He came to Sumeru to gather inspiration, found the desert far drier than he is used to, and was planning to go home by water when his raft was washed away. He cannot swim.
### LARGE +
After the Traveler feeds him a fried egg, Julien invites them to tea at his camp. A dog named Pepper is on the beach. The quest has the party find his luggage in the water and on the beach, check on the dog and investigate a sand pile, and defeat a Blazing Axe Mitachurl and hilichurls.
### ULTRA +
Julien says he passed through Chenyu Vale, where the air was still fairly humid, before the dry sandstorms of Sumeru hit him, and that he had meant to leave by water. He also regrets not buying a "mysterious egg" from a stranger that was said to turn aside a day's misfortune; Paimon suspects a scam. He suits wetter climates and was already planning to return to Fontaine with the inspiration he had gathered.

## quests
### STANDARD
"An Artist Adrift" (3.6) becomes available after "The Splendorous Sky That Day" and runs in several parts. Madinat al-Nuhas appears in "As the Khvarena's Light Shows: Nirodha".

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Clear blue water and sand at the edge of a harsh desert, with an abandoned weapons facility underfoot.

### STANDARD
An underground Khaenri'ahn weapons testing facility under the Samudra Coast, mainly for Ruin Machines, with an unfinished prototype Ruin Golem. Powered by Azosite. Later converted for defence against Abyss monsters. Entered from the west end of Asipattravana Swamp.
