## summary
### LITE
Mt. Yougou (Yougou-zan) is the holy mountain of Narukami Island in Inazuma. At its peak stands the Grand Narukami Shrine, the largest shrine in Inazuma, surrounded by the huge Sacred Sakura, a tree in perpetual bloom that purifies the land and stands for the Electro Archon's eternal watch. The mountain is crossed by long stairways and torii gates, and an electrified pool lies at its base.
### STANDARD +
The Japanese word "yougou" refers to the temporary appearance of gods on earth, so the name roughly means "Mountain of the Gods' Manifestation". The shrine is overseen today by the kitsune Yae Miko, and shrine maidens across Inazuma are trained here. Beneath the mountain lie the Depths, where the roots of the Sacred Sakura are cleansed during the Sacred Sakura Cleansing Ritual.

## geography
### STANDARD
The mountain rises on Narukami Island, with the Grand Narukami Shrine at the top and a large pool of electrified water at the base that drains the health of anyone who wades in. Visitors who have no Electro favour climb one step at a time through many torii gates; those blessed with it can be flown up by Thunder Spheres. The slopes carry Thunder Sakura boughs, Naku Weed, Onikabuto and Sea Ganoderma.
### LARGE +
Ei's old duel with the oni Mikoshi Chiyo is said to have cut away part of the mountain (see the entry on the Mikawa Festival investigation). The nearby Konda Village and Chinju Forest are tied to the cleansing ritual, and an abandoned shrine near the mountain is where one of its wards is lifted. Beneath the roots lies a hidden chamber, the Depths of Mt. Yougou, which can only be entered after the final cleansing quest. Its plants are described as gnarled and unnatural from growing in total darkness.

## places
### STANDARD
Grand Narukami Shrine: the summit shrine, dedicated to the Almighty Narukami Ogosho (the Raiden Shogun), with a fortune slip box where a shrine maiden gives out fortunes. The Sacred Sakura: the giant tree beside it, source of all Sakura Blooms on the island. The Depths of Mt. Yougou: the chamber below the roots where filth gathers.
### LARGE +
The torii gates form a long procession up the mountain. According to the official description the Sacred Sakura is in constant bloom, symbolising the eternity of Narukami, and the shrine sits within an embrace of blossoms.

## people
### STANDARD
Yae Miko, the shrine's chief priestess (Guuji), is a kitsune who also runs the Yae Publishing House. The Kuki Clan has long served here, particularly as shrine maidens. Named shrine maidens seen at the shrine include Inagi Hotomi, Kano Nana, Maki and Gendou Ringo; Yamada is a merchant. The wiki also lists Enyashi Yukari, Kanra and Riku as NPCs there without giving them roles.
### LARGE +
Most shrine maidens in Inazuma, apart from Watatsumi Island, are trained at this shrine, and their tending of personal shrines elsewhere is about keeping those shrines aligned with the Grand Shrine. Inagi Hotomi hands out the Memento Lens in the cleansing quest. The tengu of the Yougou Tengu protected the mountain about five hundred years ago, led by a chieftain called the Great Tengu.

## governance
### STANDARD
The shrine belongs to the Grand Narukami Shrine organisation under Yae Miko and exists to serve the Raiden Shogun. It performs the rites that keep the Sacred Sakura and its Thunder Sakura branches healthy.

## history
### STANDARD
The Grand Narukami Shrine was built before the Archon War, and Hakushin served as its first attendant (New Chronicles of the Six Kitsune). Around 500 years ago the shrine was led by the kitsune Saiguu and the Great Tengu, chieftain of the Yougou Tengu; the priestess Asase Hibiki and the tengu Teruyo also lived there for a time. Asase Hibiki took over after Saiguu's death and later moved back to the Asase Shrine during Ako Domeki's rebellion.
### LARGE +
Hanachirusato, the masked shrine maiden of the cleansing quest, turns out to be filth condensed from Saiguu's memory; she lets herself be purified at the end. Records say the Thunder Sakura almost withered several hundred years ago, monsters raged and the seas around Inazuma turned black, until someone performed the ritual.

## entry: Sacred Sakura Cleansing Ritual
@keys: Sacred Sakura Cleansing Ritual, Sacred Sakura cleansing, Hanachirusato, Miasmic Tumor, Memento Lens, Depths of Mt. Yougou
@version: 2.0
### STANDARD
The Sacred Sakura and its side branches, the Thunder Sakura, absorb filth from the land into their roots, but the roots cannot purify themselves. A routine minor cleansing checks the roots and sets barriers that protect both the roots (from malicious people such as the Fatui) and the people (from leaking filth). A major cleansing must be done every sixty years: the Thunder Sakura roots first, then the central roots beneath the Sacred Sakura, entered through the Depths of Mt. Yougou, where the filth condenses into a Miasmic Tumor. The filth driven out by wards takes physical form, harmless in peaceful times and dangerous in times of strife. Those performing the rite are to ask forgiveness from both the Narukami and Watatsumi shrines first.
### LARGE +
In the world quest series the Traveler meets Hanachirusato in Konda Village; she gives them a ward and has them lift barriers at an abandoned shrine near the mountain, at Araumi, the Kamisato Estate and Chinju Forest (where a stone-sealed bake-danuki named Ioroi supplies his ward after the Traveler plays with several tanuki). She then breaks the barrier before the Depths, and the Traveler defeats the tumor. Inagi Hotomi says the shrine had been preparing the ritual but Yae Miko called the preparations off and told her to keep a Memento Lens for a blonde outsider. After the tumor falls, Hanachirusato reveals she is condensed filth from Saiguu's memory and dissolves, since the ritual must cleanse everything, herself included. Once done, the Sacred Sakura glows soft blue, visible from the mainland, and the electrified pool at the base is drained of its charge.

## entry: The Sacred Sakura
@keys: Sacred Sakura, Giant Thunder Sakura, Primeval Sakura, Crimson Sakura, Thunder Sakura
@spoiler: major
@version: 2.0
### STANDARD
The Sacred Sakura (Shin'ou) is the divine tree that purifies Inazuma's ley lines and keeps Abyssal corruption out. Every Sakura Bloom on Narukami Island fell from it, and its protection is said to reach wherever the petals land. Its roots connect all the Thunder Sakura, which are grafted from its branches. Inazumans traditionally believed it cleansed the land through them, defending earth and water alike; it also bestows Electrograna as envoys to favoured travellers.
### LARGE +
Its design is based on himorogi beliefs and, according to the Version 2.0 special program, symbolises Ei's protection of Inazuma.
### ULTRA +
Story revelation: the tree grew from a seed that Makoto gave Ei in the realm of consciousness stored in the Musou Isshin, after Ei's duel with the Shogun there. The seed took root retroactively, so it began to grow thousands of years ago, before the great flood that split Inazuma's islands. When Ei returned from Khaenri'ah after the cataclysm and found it on Mt. Yougou, everyone she knew said it had always been there, and she could not convince Yae Miko otherwise because Miko's memories had been altered. Makoto, who died in Khaenri'ah during the cataclysm, left the seed so that Ei could ease into the role of Electro Archon while the tree protected Inazuma.

## entry: Ei, Chiyo and the Buried Horn (Mikawa Festival)
@keys: Mikoshi Chiyo, Torachiyo, Chiyo the Tiger-Bite, Mikawa Flower Festival, Mikawa Festival, buried horn
@keys2: Yougou, Ei, Yae Miko
@version: 5.4
@availability: limited
@spoiler: major
### ULTRA
During the Version 5.4 event Enchanted Tales of the Mikawa Festival, odd phenomena and doppelgangers appeared around the festival grounds on Mt. Yougou. Investigation led to a horn buried under the mountain, which three bake-danuki had uncovered. Yae Miko recounts a legend of an oni warrior, tainted by darkness, who raised her sword against the Almighty Shogun. Ei confirms it: Mikoshi Chiyo, once her ally, was swallowed by a beast of sin during the cataclysm and tainted by its darkness, and later attacked Ei on Mt. Yougou. Ei struck with the Musou no Hitotachi and removed Chiyo's arm, one horn and part of the mountain, and she suspects the horn was buried there then. A fragment of Chiyo's nightmare clung to the horn and showed itself only on Mt. Yougou. This episode happened during a limited-time event; the archive treats it as canon but not widely known.

## entry: Yougou Tengu
@keys: Yougou Tengu, Great Tengu, Sasayuri, Reizenbou, Teruyo, Yougou Three
@keys2: Mt. Yougou, tengu, Grand Narukami Shrine
@spoiler: minor
### ULTRA
The Yougou Tengu are an elite group of tengu tied to the mountain, responsible for protecting it and the shrine. Their chieftain bears the title of Great Tengu, which both Sasayuri and Reizenbou are said to have held. Names that they give are said to carry divine blessing; Teruyo gave the name Iwakura to Iwakura Michihiro. They trained the "Yougou Three" and built the Empty Boat of a Thousand Gates at the end of the Araumi labyrinth. The Version 2.0 livestream linked the mountain to tengu legend, but no tengu are met on the mountain in the present.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Red torii climb through falling sakura petals toward a shrine wrapped in blossom; the tree glows soft blue at night after the cleansing. It is the most solemn and the most visited sacred ground in Inazuma, with a shrine maiden always ready to draw a fortune slip for pilgrims.

## dangers
### STANDARD
The electrified pool at the base is harmful, and Electro hilichurls, Electro slimes, Fatui Skirmishers and Agents, Treasure Hoarders, Mirror Maidens and Nobushi patrol the slopes. Electro Cubes and Thunder Barriers block some ways forward.

## travel
### LARGE
The trip is a pilgrimage on foot up the stairs; those with Electro favour can use Thunder Spheres to reach the shrine surrounded by the Sacred Sakura. The Depths can only be entered after the cleansing quest. Local specialties are Naku Weed, Onikabuto, Sakura Bloom and Sea Ganoderma.

## quests
### LARGE
The world quest series Sacred Sakura Cleansing Ritual (2.0) ends under the mountain; its requirement is the Archon quest Ritou Escape Plan. The Version 5.4 Mikawa Festival event revisits the mountain (limited).

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the Grand Narukami Shrine remains the seat of the Raiden Shogun's worship in Inazuma under Yae Miko, and the Sacred Sakura stands in bloom.
### STANDARD +
The last cleansing was done by the Traveler and Hanachirusato. No later canon through 7.1 has been found to change the mountain's state.
