## summary
### LITE
The Great Volcano of Tollan is the enormous active volcano that covers Atocpan in the southwest of Natlan. Ancient scrolls call it the Sacred Mountain. It still erupts from time to time and is said to have been far more active in darker ages. It was once the dwelling of the ancient dragons, and the legends of Natlan keep returning to it.
### STANDARD +
Legend says the dragons' palace lies beneath the magma, that the sage Kukulkan stole the primal fire from it in the form of Pyrophosphorite, and that Xbalanque fought and defeated the Abyss-corrupted Xiuhcoatl here. Inside the volcano lies the Ancient Sacred Mountain, an ancient city that the Traveler enters during the quest chain Path to the Flaming Peaks.
### LARGE +
It is also where the legend of the Golden Entreaties leads: bringing both discs to the volcano is said to lift the Flamelord's Blessing from a Saurian. The 5.5 finale shows that this legend hid a far darker purpose.

## geography
### STANDARD
The volcano encompasses the whole of Atocpan in the southwest of Natlan, the same area that holds the home of the Collective of Plenty. The Skyfire Circlet crowns it, and the way into the mountain opens from there. The tribal legend of the Volcano Lord places a Shadow Pin above the Sulfurous Veins, a locale linked to the Children of Echoes.
### LARGE +
Atocpan's own description names the volcano as the area's defining feature. Spiritways let travellers reach the Skyfire Circlet; the quest Nahuatzin's Leap triggers on arrival there.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Heat, sulfur, ash and the rumble of a mountain that is never fully asleep. Eruptions rain burning rocks across the area and send boulders rolling in places, so people and creatures take cover until it passes and then carry on.
### LARGE +
Documents left around Atocpan speak darkly of people who returned from the volcano changed. Treat these as in-world rumour rather than established fact.

## dangers
### STANDARD
The volcano erupts periodically. During an eruption fireballs of Pyro fall across Atocpan and hurt anything they hit, and enemies seek shelter too. Phlogiston inside the mountain is harmful to those who linger. In story terms, the pilgrimage to the volcano is deadly.
### LARGE +
In Manqu's questline, Pacal of the Children of Echoes at first refuses to allow the pilgrimage because many who attempted it failed the trials and died; he relents after a brief duel with the Traveler.

## history
### STANDARD
In ancient times the volcano was the dwelling of the Pyro dragons, and Xiuhcoatl shaped the city of Tollan inside it. When the dragon civilisation crumbled, the Abyss is said to have taken the mountain. Later, Xbalanque, not yet the Pyro Archon, fought and killed the corrupted Xiuhcoatl here.
### LARGE +
Item and book texts add that Kukulkan, Xiuhcoatl's younger brother, is said to have stolen the primal fire from the palace under the magma as Pyrophosphorite. A fable in the Records of Hanan Pacha, which historians judge unhistorical, calls Xiuhcoatl the great lord of the volcano and has a vain winged dragon claim his treasures while he sleeps.
### ULTRA +
The tribal legend The Volcano Lord and the Shadow Pins describes a time when the great beasts withdrew and a dark Volcano Lord, a black-and-purple salamander-like giant, occupied the empty volcano and tormented the tribes. The hero Traore of the Children of Echoes tricked and pinned it with help from the Wayob and four phlogiston nails. It is a children's story and should not be read as literal chronology.

## lore
@version: 5.0
### STANDARD
Tradition says that bringing both Golden Entreaties to the volcano can wash the Flamelord's Blessing from a Saurian. The blessing is a mark tied to Xiuhcoatl that grows into uncontrollable power and madness. The tribes gave up the custom since no warrior ever succeeded, and now simply care for afflicted Saurians until they die.
### LARGE +
The Golden Entreaties were made when Xbalanque split Xiuhcoatl's eye; one went to Xbalanque and the other to Kukulkan. The Saurian Relics Association researcher Manqu believed they were only legend until the Traveler showed him one. Mposo adds that the mark appears every dozen or several dozen years, and that Saurians who walk into the volcano alone are never heard from again.

## status
@version: 5.5
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
As of 7.1 the volcano is still active, still erupts, and remains an open-world landmark of Atocpan. Its interior can be entered after Nahuatzin's Leap. After the Traveler and Little One declined to activate Huitzilopochtli, the Tollan systems ceased maintenance and the draconic remnants faded; the array is obsolete.
### LARGE +
The wiki pages read show no role for the volcano in Nod-Krai (6.x) or the 7.0-7.1 Snezhnaya content. Do not invent later involvement.

## quests
@version: 5.5
### STANDARD
Key quests: Nahuatzin's Leap (the first part of Path to the Flaming Peaks), where the party reaches the Skyfire Circlet and leaps into the crater, and An Omen of Annihilation and the Final Entreaty (the only part of A Finale Emberforged), set in the Heart of the Sacred Mountain.
### LARGE +
Earlier, Revelations from the Past and The Road Ahead in Tequemecan Valley introduce the Golden Entreaties and the idea of a pilgrimage to the volcano.

## entry: Xbalanque and Xiuhcoatl's battle
@keys: Xbalanque, Xiuhcoatl, Flamelord, Pyro Dragon, Fallen Dragon
@keys2: volcano, Sacred Mountain, Tollan
@version: 5.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Long before the present, Xbalanque, not yet the Pyro Archon, fought the corrupted Xiuhcoatl at the volcano. He pierced the weak spots in the dragon's eyes, one of which split into two disc-like fragments, and both fell from a great height. Xiuhcoatl stayed dead while Xbalanque was wreathed in flame and revived. Whether anything of Xiuhcoatl remains is disputed.
### LARGE +
According to Kukulkan, Xiuhcoatl was beaten because one of his eyes served as the control hub of Huitzilopochtli. The wiki notes that Xiuhcoatl's remains can be seen at the Heart of the Sacred Mountain.

## entry: Golden Entreaties and the Flamelord's Blessing
@keys: Golden Entreaty, Golden Entreaties, Flamelord's Blessing
@keys2: volcano, Saurian, pilgrimage
@version: 5.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The Golden Entreaties are two identical white discs with golden patterns, said to have been made from Xiuhcoatl's eyes. Legend claims that a strongly bonded human and Saurian can bring them to the volcano to cleanse the Saurian of the Flamelord's Blessing.
### LARGE +
Kukulkan, the Sage of the Stolen Flame, reveals in 5.5 that he created the legend so a human would bring both discs and a blessed Saurian to Huitzilopochtli. He and Och-Kan both hoped Little One would make the right choice. Och-Kan had hidden his disc on the Tonatiuh because he feared its power.

## entry: Huitzilopochtli
@keys: Huitzilopochtli, Sunquake, Sweeping Skyfire, Natlan Phlogistonization Array
@version: 5.5
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Huitzilopochtli is a device built by Xiuhcoatl in the Heart of the Sacred Mountain to turn Natlan into phlogiston and so destroy the Abyss, at the cost of nearly everything alive there. It is activated by reuniting the two Golden Entreaties.
### LARGE +
In A Finale Emberforged, Kukulkan urged Little One to activate it. Little One declined and the Primeval Source Dragon Mech guarding it was defeated; the Tollan systems and Kukulkan then faded. The wiki records a non-canonical alternative ending for the other choice; ignore it.

## entry: The Volcano Lord and the Shadow Pins
@keys: Volcano Lord, Shadow Pins, Traore
@version: 5.0
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
A tribal story told to Children of Echoes children. A giant black-and-purple salamander-like lord took the empty volcano, bathed in the springs, at its worst swallowed the Collective of Plenty, and finally coveted the gems of the Children of Echoes' valleys. Traore tricked it with a stone chamber, and the Wayob's four pins turned into phlogiston nails to pin it.
### LARGE +
The tale ties several tribes together: Tatankasaurs help free the Collective of Plenty, the People of the Springs suffer from its smog, and the Scions of the Canopy from its sparks. It is a legend, so keep it as folklore.
