## summary
### LITE
Chinvat Ravine is a ravine below Sumeru City, at the base of the Divine Tree, in the Avidya Forest area of Sumeru's Dharma Forest. Forest travellers use the name for the passage beneath the great tree, as an echo of the ancient literary image of crossing a sea of ignorance to reach the light of truth.
### STANDARD +
The name is a Sumeru-flavoured borrowing of the Chinvat Bridge, the bridge of judgement in Zoroastrian belief.

## geography
### STANDARD
The ravine lies below Sumeru City at the foot of the Divine Tree. The expedition text treats it as the last stage of a forest journey before the city of wisdom. It is part of the Avidya Forest.

## history
### STANDARD
The expedition description explains the name: in Sumeru's ancient literary canon, Chinvat stands for the bitter sea of ignorance that seekers must cross to see the light on the far shore. Those who now navigate the forests apply it to this ravine, perhaps for the elation of finally arriving at the city of wisdom.
### LARGE +
The Chinese name means "Separate-and-Cross Ravine", taken from a rendering of the Chinvat Bridge as the Separating-and-Crossing Bridge. In Zoroastrian belief that bridge sifts the dead, parting the living world from the world of the dead.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Eremites (crossbow users, linebreakers and sword-dancers) and shaggy sumpter beasts roam the area. A random encounter called Unscrupulous Dealings can place the Traveler across from Fatui skirmishers, including Anemoboxer and Pyroslinger types, who are in what Paimon calls a dodgy under-the-table deal with Eremites; the Traveler is expected to break it up.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
The official wording gives it a sense of relief and arrival: the elation of reaching the city of wisdom after a long forest crossing.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 Chinvat Ravine is an ordinary part of Sumeru's open world with no later story change.
### STANDARD +
The Fatui and Eremites that appear there are random encounters rather than any lasting presence.
