## summary
### LITE
Tunigi Hollow is a subarea of Gavireh Lajavard in Sumeru's Girdle of the Sands, long a great breach carved by the Abyss. Five hundred years ago, in the cataclysm, gods fought Abyssal monsters here, and the Archon Egeria was slain. The Harvisptokhm, a tree grown from her remains, seals it.
### STANDARD +
Added in version 3.6, it is the site of the Hymn of Tir Yazad quests (Khvarena of Good and Evil). A path can be opened from the Hollow into the Harvisptokhm by the Rite of Chinvat.

## geography
### STANDARD
A hollow, ringed by trees whose hollows hold the Great Songs of Khvarena, in Gavireh Lajavard. Gray crystal and Fravashi Trees dot it. After the Rite of Chinvat, jumping into the Hollow sends the player to the Harvisptokhm instead.
### LARGE +
The wiki lists the nearby Dendrogranum, Elemental Monument, Farrwick and other exploration objects, and the Valley of the Unsullied viewpoint (unlocked after the Hymn of Tir Yazad, Part 1).

## history
@version: 3.6
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
During the cataclysm, the Abyss spewed monsters and forbidden-knowledge pollution from here. Several gods ventured to suppress it, including Greater Lord Rukkhadevata and Egeria, the old Hydro Archon.
### LARGE +
Egeria was slain and her body became the pure primordial water of the Amrita. Rukkhadevata grew the Harvisptokhm from it to anchor Egeria's consciousness and hold the Abyssal defilement at bay. The Pari fought alongside the Nagarjunites, and gray crystal encased stricken monsters for centuries.
### ULTRA +
In Sorush's vision at the top of the tree during the Hymn of Tir Yazad, Part 2, the Pari fought beside Khaenri'ahn warriors, among them a masked sword-wielder and a one-armed sage, in the cataclysm.

## places
### STANDARD
The Harvisptokhm in the Vourukasha Oasis, reached through a hole at the Hollow or at its stump. Its heart, the Gaokerena, holds the Flower of Sanctity.
### LARGE +
The Sign of Apaosha hangs in the sky above the Harvisptokhm. After the Hymn of Tir Yazad, Part 2 it is sealed and a tree of pure Amrita grows atop the Harvisptokhm.

## culture
### STANDARD
The Pari treat the Harvisptokhm as their god. The Order of Skeptics once kept its seal through ritual, but lost its connection to the Pari and let the defilement build up.
### LARGE +
The rites are the Rite of Chinvat (placing the Great Songs, which opens the path from the Hollow) and the Rite of Ab-Zohr, which purges the defilement in the tree. Mihir and Rashnu help Sorush with the Hollow's songs.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Hilichurls, Fatui Skirmishers, rifthounds (some Thundercraven, some Rockfond), fungi, and a Consecrated Fanged Beast after the Hymn's second part. Abyssal defilement in the Hollow and tree.

## quests
### LARGE
In the Hymn of Tir Yazad (Part 1) the Traveler, Paimon and Sorush meet Mihir and Rashnu at the Hollow, place the Great Songs of Khvarena in the tree hollows and purify three flowers, opening the path. In Part 2 they cleanse the Harvisptokhm, collect three Spenta Hearts and awaken the god's consciousness.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the Hollow is cleansed, with the Sign of Apaosha sealed and a clean path into the Harvisptokhm.
### STANDARD +
Before the quests the Hollow was a great breach, with its pathway blocked and the Harvisptokhm near collapse.

## entry: Rite of Chinvat
@keys: Rite of Chinvat, Great Songs of Khvarena
@keys2: Tunigi Hollow, Sorush, Pari
@version: 3.6
### STANDARD
A rite of the Pari performed at Tunigi Hollow with the Great Songs of Khvarena. In the Hymn of Tir Yazad (Part 1), Sorush, with Mihir and Rashnu, uses it to open a pathway into the Harvisptokhm, linking the Hollow with the Sign in the sky, which loading screens call unexplained.
