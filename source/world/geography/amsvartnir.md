## summary
### LITE
Amsvartnir is a lake in Wavechaser Plain, in northern Nod-Krai, lying north of the city of Piramida. A sacred forest grows beside it, beyond the ruins of an ancient palace and on the edge of the wasteland. Above the water, hidden from mortal eyes by day, stands the Royal Court of the Seelie, where Koitar, the first of the Seelie, was once confined.
### STANDARD +
Visitors uncover the court by melting a Lunar Conflux Barrier and playing a silent lyre, which begins the World Quest Echoes of a Forsaken Song. The name is borrowed from Norse mythology, where Ámsvartnir ("pitch black") is the lake holding the island on which the wolf Fenrir was bound.

## geography
### STANDARD
A lake with a central island and floating islands overhead, surrounded by a sacred forest. Lakeside Prisms and Lunar Conflux Barriers are part of the exploration puzzles. Local life includes Pale-Furred Wolves and Radiant Glacial Wolf elites, and Winter Icelea grows here.
### LARGE +
The lake lies in Wavechaser Plain, itself part of Pohjola. The restored court has aqua-green crystals at its base and on the islands above, and aqua-blue beams that form geometric patterns around the structure, connecting the central island to those overhead.

## places
### STANDARD
The Royal Court of the Seelie lies above the lake: a sacred secluded domain that cannot be perceived by ordinary people. It holds a giant lyre. A viewpoint here is called Palaces of Times Bygone.
### LARGE +
Ancient Handwriting notes are found in chests after the quest. Moonlanes carry the Traveler up through the floating islands, and the lake's central island and the airborne islands are linked once the prisms are reconnected.

## history
### STANDARD
The Etherlight Spindlelute's legend says the lake lies beyond the ruins of an ancient palace and that whoever breaks the golden branch in the sacred forest will find the Royal Court, hidden from mortal sight by day. It calls the court a kingdom of the past, forgotten by time and space, where no heavenly light reaches, whose owner has long gone. A young woman's silent lament still echoes in its hall.
### LARGE +
The quest's mural and the legend speak of a woman who dreamed of a different future than the one the Heavenly Principles had woven: a tower raised to heaven by men, mountain-sized beasts breaking from the earth, three moons, and an ancient dragon. Tradition names her as Koitar, the first Seelie and wife of Anacharsis, confined in the court after losing her memories.
### ULTRA +
In the Ancient Handwriting the writer tells of sitting alone in the temple while passers-by could not see her, and of a beast from the north who came to listen to her songs. She named him Boreas, "North Wind", and he and his kin shared her dwelling. After many days he spoke, called her Mother, and asked to go south to make the poor but fortified cities prosper. She granted this, and the text ends as she begins to remember. Boreas and his kin are therefore said to have once dwelt here before departing for Mondstadt.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Hushed, silvery and mournful: a lake with Seelie circling above, a silent instrument whose music only the player hears, and a song of exiles in a hall stripped bare. The Traveler hears the lament in a language they have never studied yet understand.

## dangers
### LARGE
Wolves, including the Radiant Glacial Wolf, roam the shore. The court itself is not hostile; its challenges are puzzles of light and prisms.

## travel
### LARGE
Lies in Wavechaser Plain, north of Piramida. The court appears after the Lunar Conflux Barrier is melted and the lyre played, and completing the quest unlocks a Teleport Waypoint at the court.

## quests
### STANDARD
World Quest: Echoes of a Forsaken Song (6.3). Follow the Seelie up the Moonlanes, break seals on the floating islands and reconnect Lakeside Prisms to hear fragments of a lament, then examine the mural. The quest unlocks the Palaces of Times Bygone viewpoint.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the Royal Court stands restored and open to visitors, and the Seelie gather there.
### STANDARD +
Koitar herself does not appear in the court in this quest, only her recorded words and song.

## entry: Royal Court of the Seelie
@keys: Royal Court of the Seelie, Seelie court, Palaces of Times Bygone
@version: 6.3
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
A sacred, secluded domain above Amsvartnir, hidden from mortals by day. It was built of mighty stone pillars with golden beams and no windows for outside light. Koitar wept there day and night amid Seelie who no longer knew her. Boreas and his kin dwelt in the area before leaving for Mondstadt. The Traveler broke the barrier, revealed a giant lyre and helped the Seelie rebuild the temple.

## entry: Ancient Handwriting of Koitar
@keys: Ancient Handwriting, Koitar's lament, Etherlight Spindlelute
@keys2: Amsvartnir, Seelie
@version: 6.3
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Three notes recovered from chests by the court, written in the first person by a woman confined in a lonely temple. They tell of a beast named Boreas ("North Wind") who served as her emissary and then left to bring prosperity to the south, and end as she starts to remember. They tie the lake's legend to Mondstadt's wind god tradition.
