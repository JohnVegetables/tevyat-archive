## summary
### LITE
Wounded Shin Valley is a subarea of the Desert of Hadramaveth in Sumeru's Great Red Sand. It bore this name long before the great Ruin Golem of the Hadramaveth sank into the sand here, where it now lies immobile.
### STANDARD +
The valley appears in the world quest series The Dirge of Bilqis, whose party drives the ancient war machine to this spot. The viewpoint, Where a Titan's Shins Were Broken, is unlocked after that quest series' Dune-Entombed Fecundity: Part III.

## geography
### STANDARD
A valley in the open desert, the third and last place the Hadramaveth's Ruin Golem stands. The golem went from the Dunes of Steel, to a spot north of the Sands of Three Canals, to here.
### LARGE +
After it sinks, the golem permanently loses its ability to move or punch, though its eye cannon can still be fired manually, like that of the golem in the Valley of Dahri.

## places
### STANDARD
The Ruin Golem of the Hadramaveth, whose arm generator rooms, control cabin and plates are open to explore. The wiki counts it as one of three known Ruin Golems in Sumeru, with those of Devantaka Mountain and the Valley of Dahri, and it is in far better repair than the others.
### LARGE +
Interactable papers inside tell of two ages. Modern ones: a contract in which the Tanit matriarch Babel assigns the warriors Azariq and others to escort the Kshahrewar researcher Jazira Yassar across the desert, and Azariq's copied blueprints of the machine's power system. Ancient ones: an unsigned dying letter by a Khaenri'ahn soldier who still believes his Marshal will save them and that glory will return, and the discarded research page of the scholar Zandik, who calls the machine the 'Gurabad Specimen' and notes how it tilts with its own motion and would sicken any pilot.
### ULTRA +
Zandik's note guesses that the machine's pilot may have had modified inner ears or wore special adhesive boots and gloves, because a trial of piloting it had to stop after half a minute.

## history
### STANDARD
Before the quests, the golem lay in the Dunes of Steel. It is a Khaenri'ahn machine of the same kind as the one on Devantaka Mountain.
### LARGE +
The valley's name predates the golem's fall: the viewpoint says it has been called Wounded Shin Valley for a long time, and that the name now bears extra weight because an iron giant has lost its footing here.

## dangers
### LARGE
The Eremites (Azariq's subordinates) fought in the quest; the open desert's sandstorms.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the valley is open and the golem lies sunk and immobile.
### STANDARD +
No change to the area is recorded after version 3.4.

## entry: Ruin Golem of the Hadramaveth
@keys: Ruin Golem of the Hadramaveth, Hadramaveth Ruin Golem, Gurabad Specimen
@version: 3.4
### STANDARD
A giant Khaenri'ahn war machine, said to have once been piloted by humans called the Schwanenritter, and the best preserved of the three known golems in Sumeru. During The Dirge of Bilqis the party moves it to cross quicksand; at Wounded Shin Valley it sinks and can no longer move.

## entry: The Golem's Last Journey
@keys: Wounded Shin Valley golem, Dune-Entombed Fecundity, Azariq
@version: 3.4
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
In The Dirge of Bilqis, Jeht, Liloupar and the Traveler use the decommissioned Ruin Golem to cross the dunes toward the Eternal Oasis. At the end of Dune-Entombed Fecundity: Part III, Azariq (Babel's Left Hand in the Tanit tribe, who had learned of the technology from the Fatui) demands Liloupar to restore the desert's oases. Jeht challenges and kills him. Aderfi, outside the machine, reveals Azariq meant to replace Babel with Jeht and wed her; the party decides to tell Babel later and presses on to Deshret's Glass Goblet.
### LARGE +
The golem sinks into the valley at the end of its journey. Babel suspected Fatui involvement in Aderfi's betrayal, but the Fatui's exact role is not detailed.
