## summary
### LITE
The Outsider Shrine is a modest shrine at Serpent's Head on Yashiori Island, next to a man called Washizu. He insists that people who pray there three times in a day will be rewarded by a "he" who listens, and the shrine itself shows no sign of anything unusual. It is the centre of the hidden objective Washizu's Prayers.
### STANDARD +
The objective plays out over four real days. Washizu was once the kind chief of Higi Village; the Tatarigami outbreak on the island unmoored him, and the shrine is where his worship turned dangerous.

## geography
### STANDARD
It stands at Serpent's Head, the bone-strewn coast of Yashiori Island, where Orobashi's remains lie. Flavour text for the prayers mentions sea breeze, gulls, distant whales, fireflies, wildflowers and wind through the great serpent's bones, as if weathered into the saddest instrument of the sea.
### LARGE +
On a given day the Traveler prays three times in a row; each prayer produces a short description of sounds and scents, always ending with the Traveler hearing nothing. Prayers on the first three days cause a chest to appear the next day. By the fourth day the descriptions comment that the shrine seems unresponsive.

## quests
### STANDARD
Washizu's Prayers (requires having done Ritou Escape Plan) is time-gated: pray daily for three days, then speak to Washizu on the fourth. His talk grows more feverish: the "words of the heart" over the "words of the tongue", a woman who refused tribute and fled, and a growing sense that "he" is angry. On the last day he attacks the Traveler as a maddened samurai and is defeated, which opens the hidden world quest Sinister Instruction in Higi Village.
### LARGE +
In Sinister Instruction he leaves behind notes and a village register. The register records villagers who died of illness, others lost to the war or sea, and strangers listed as sacrificed, plus a plan to leave the dead's belongings out as a lure for living sacrifices. It also records his search for Chouji's mother and, at the end, marks the Traveler as a "Nameless Outlander".

## people
### LARGE
Washizu, village chief of Higi Village. According to the wiki's profile he built several shrines around the island to appease Orobashi's soul and protect villagers near Jakotsu Crystal. After Watatsumi fanatics inside the Watatsumi Army broke the seals on the island and the Tatarigami spread, he tried to keep order for refugees from Jakotsu Mine, locked up two villagers who had gone mad, and then fell to the Tatarigami himself. Locals later told Chouji that people who questioned him vanished.
### ULTRA +
Washizu is mentioned in the Teyvat Travel Guide as having feasted the visiting witch Alice when she came to the village, a glimpse of how welcoming he once was. His enemy model matches Dancing Thunder Kairagi.

## dangers
### STANDARD
The shrine itself is harmless. Washizu is hostile only on the final day of the objective, and the following quest touches on human sacrifice (kept off-page here).

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the objective and follow-up quest are unchanged, Washizu is dead, and the shrine's prayers no longer function after completion.
