## summary
### LITE
Vanarana Nursery is an unofficial name for a small garden in a cave at the west end of Vanarana in Sumeru. The Aranara Arakara and Aranakula tend it, working hard to grow happy Viparyas.
### STANDARD +
Arakara is always there; Aranakula returns after the Traveler helps restore the Varuna Contraption in A Prayer for Rain on the Fecund Land (this timing is flagged as unverified). In the real Vanarana, Varsha can be found at the nursery after Giving Flowers.
### LARGE +
After the quest The Viparyas of Vanarana, all 13 Viparyas can be harvested and respawn every three days. In the real Vanarana they are replaced by Sweet Flowers; harvesting one kind does not remove the other.

## geography
### STANDARD
A cave garden on the western edge of Vanarana. It looks different in the dream version, with blooming Viparyas, and the real version, with Sweet Flowers.

## people
@version: 3.0
### STANDARD
Arakara and Aranakula, Aranara gardeners. Varsha visits the nursery in the real Vanarana after Giving Flowers.

## quests
### STANDARD
The Viparyas of Vanarana and Giving Flowers, both world quests.

Small, gentle and tended, a patient corner of a village of dreams.
