## summary
### LITE
The Alcor is the flagship of the Crux fleet, led by Captain Beidou. It sails the open seas and can even push through tempest-torn waters; when at rest it is anchored off Guyun Stone Forest in Liyue. Beidou herself is often ashore on business, leaving the ship to her crew.

## geography
### STANDARD
The ship is anchored just off the southeastern end of the island that holds the Domain of Guyun, in the Sea of Clouds. Visitors can climb aboard. The ship carries chests near the bookkeeper Mora-Grubber and on the crow's nest nearest the stern, and a Geoculus sits at the top of the highest crow's nest.
### LARGE +
The map description reads that it traverses the oceans under Beidou's direction and can penetrate the tempestuous storms.

## places
### STANDARD
The main deck, where the crew each have their own post (the ironworker Suling, the bookkeeper Mora-Grubber, the surgeon Yinxing and others), and the crow's nests above it.
### LARGE +
The Crux is organised as two teams, the Spear (naval warfare and cargo) and the Shield (survival skills, navigation, rescue, cartography, meteorology, even counselling).

## people
### STANDARD
Beidou is captain. Crew on board include Juza (chief mate), Yinxing (surgeon at sea), Huixing (navigator), Suling (ironworker), Mora-Grubber (bookkeeper), and the sailors Furong, Sea Drake and Xu Liushi, plus Little Yue. See their own entries where they exist.
### LARGE +
In the world quest, Juza is gruff toward the Traveler, who is made to help with errands because the crew may not leave the ship without Beidou's orders. Yinxing explains Juza has poor hearing, which makes him loud, and that he is good-hearted. Little Yue asks the Traveler to deliver a present to Changchang.

## culture
### STANDARD
The Crux carries cargo, takes bounties and, as the wiki notes, also trades illegally. The Liyue Qixing more or less approve of its voyages. After each voyage the crew hold a three-day feast; the chef Xiangling was once hired for it and later became a regular guest.
### ULTRA +
The Ministry of Civil Affairs entrusts the Crux with a two-year navigation training programme. The fleet has done business in Inazuma. Beidou once took Kaedehara Kazuha aboard as a temporary crew member while he was being pursued after the Vision Hunt Decree, and she was wary of staying docked at Ritou for long.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
A heavily armed ship at rest, with wary sailors who treat strangers as intruders at first. The ship's name is read as an omen of death.

## travel
### STANDARD
Climb aboard from the shore at Guyun Stone Forest. Once the world quest A Lone Ship in Guyun is done, the ship becomes a permanent teleport waypoint on the map.

## quests
### STANDARD
A Lone Ship in Guyun (World Quest): the Traveler boards and is mistaken for a stowaway by Juza, then helps crew members with errands: Yinxing's letter to Bolai, Little Yue's gift for Changchang, and meat for Juza.
### LARGE +
Life on the High Seas (Hangout Event for Beidou, 2.3) has Beidou explain that the Alcor, a star in the constellation her name denotes, was seen as an omen of death.

## status
### STANDARD
As of 7.1 the Alcor remains the Crux's flagship; the sources read note no change to its location.

## entry: The Crux

### STANDARD
An armed fleet based in Liyue Harbor, led by Captain Beidou, known for daring voyages and bounties. It divides into the Spear (naval warfare, cargo) and the Shield (survival skills, navigation).
