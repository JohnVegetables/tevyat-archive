## summary
### LITE
The Dragon-Queller is a huge tree with glowing blue veins standing near Mt. Hulao at Nantianmen in Liyue. It is the seal that holds the Geo dragon Azhdaha, whom Morax and the adepti imprisoned there long ago after his erosion made him a danger to people. The developers describe the tree as Azhdaha's own tail.

## geography
### STANDARD
The tree rises beside Mt. Hulao in the Nantianmen area of Liyue's Minlin region. Its branches carry a cold, moonlight-coloured glow, and one great blue branch at its tip points straight toward Liyue Harbor and cannot be climbed. Bright yellow flowers grow around its base; the same flowers are found in the lair of the Primo Geovishap. Its roots reach deep into the earth and, according to the dragon's own account, connect to the ley lines. A tunnel beneath it leads to the dragon's prison, which is entered as a domain.
### LARGE +
The domain description calls the prison a bed of rock carved into the very heart of the world, a coffin that has held Azhdaha across the long years.

## places
### STANDARD
Stone Monolith: a tablet set before the tree by the adeptus Mountain Shaper as a warning to passers-by. Its text, paraphrased, thanks the merciful adepti for life enduring in Heaven and Earth and states that an evil dragon lies here and must not be disturbed.

Beneath the Dragon-Queller: the prison itself, reached through the tunnel under the tree. Players fight Azhdaha there as a weekly boss; the story treats the fight as a memory of the encounter, because Azhdaha was re-sealed.
### LARGE +
The domain entrance is marked with the elements Azhdaha will absorb inside, and his Cryo, Pyro, Electro and Hydro attacks mark characters who lack a shield. These are game mechanics, not in-world statements.

## history
### STANDARD
Azhdaha, an ancient earth dragon born of elemental crystallisation, once befriended Morax and humanity. Erosion made him forget Morax and lash out, and miners damaging the ley lines of the Chasm worsened his suffering. Morax and the adepti Mountain Shaper, Moon Carver and a third, unnamed adeptus fought him in a battle that began in the Chasm and ran north to Nantianmen, where, with the willing acceptance of his remaining conscience, they sealed the eroded dragon in the Dragon-Queller. The clash of elemental energy in that battle also created Dragonfall.
### LARGE +
Centuries later the seal weakened. Azhdaha projected a portion of his power as the girl Jiu, and she drew a group of miners from the Chasm to the tree to break the seal with their pickaxes from outside. In Zhongli's story quest No Mere Stone (Version 1.5) the Traveler, Paimon and Zhongli, with an expert named Kun Jun, followed the trail. Kun Jun proved to be Azhdaha's benevolent half. The party was pulled into the prison, and Azhdaha was defeated and re-sealed. His benevolent consciousness thanked Zhongli and left Kun Jun's body; Zhongli assured it Liyue would be ready if the dragon ever woke again.
### ULTRA +
Details of the sealing, per the quest: Azhdaha, once blind, had asked Morax to let him see the surface, and Morax unearthed him, gave him sight and warned that he would be sealed again if he ever endangered Liyue's order. The sealing split the eroded dragon into a crazed body and a benevolent consciousness; the latter helped Morax seal the former. Jiu's anger at Morax's supposed betrayal drove the later attempt to break out. Zhongli noted that Dragonfall had been mined to obscurity centuries ago, so Kun Jun's interest in it was unusual for the present day.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
A still, cold place. The tree glows with a moonlit light, the earth around it feels thick with held-back power, and the monolith's plain warning is the only human sign. During the miners' disappearance, Geovishaps and other creatures nearby were unusually aggressive.

## dangers
### STANDARD
The seal itself is the danger: if it breaks, Azhdaha can draw on the ley lines again and attack Liyue.

## travel
### STANDARD
The tree stands in the Nantianmen area near Mt. Hulao, on the way toward Lingju Pass, and its tip branch points at Liyue Harbor.

## quests
### STANDARD
No Mere Stone (Zhongli Story Quest, 1.5), whose final part is Amidst Chaos, the Rock Is Unmoved, is the main source. The domain unlocks on its completion.

## status
### STANDARD
As of 7.1 the dragon remains resealed after the events of 1.5. No later content found in this research changes the seal's state.

## entry: Dragon-Queller

### STANDARD
The Dragon-Queller is the blue-veined tree beside Mt. Hulao at Nantianmen that seals the crazed Geo dragon Azhdaha. A Mountain Shaper tablet at its foot warns people not to disturb the evil dragon beneath.
### LARGE +
The seal was nearly broken in the Zhongli story quest No Mere Stone, when Jiu led missing miners to dig at it. Azhdaha was beaten and resealed.
