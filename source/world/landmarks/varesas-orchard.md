## summary
### LITE
Varesa's Orchard is a family orchard just outside the "Collective of Plenty" tribe's settlement in Atocpan, Natlan. It belongs to Varesa and is guarded by her Tatankasaur companion, Vanana.
### STANDARD +
It grows apples, cacahuatl, grainfruit and quenepa berries, and is the only place where red berryshrooms can be found.

## places
@version: 5.5
### STANDARD
The orchard lies right outside the Collective of Plenty. Varesa's house is behind a fruit stand in front, near the tribe's main entrance. On the southern side stands a "Fruits Aplenty" machine.
### LARGE +
In the quest "Orchard Repose", Varesa says her family runs a huge orchard and that they live close to the tribe's entrance. The machine prepares a dish when ingredients are submitted and it is struck by a Tatankasaur or a Collective of Plenty character.

## people
@version: 5.5
### STANDARD
Varesa owns the orchard; her father is a skilled cook and her Tatankasaur companion Vanana keeps guard and stops visitors from entering until the quest allows it. Her uncle Acatl is the tribe's chief, as she mentions in the quest.
### LARGE +
For Varesa's own background, see her character entry rather than repeating it here.

## quests
@version: 5.5
### LARGE
In "Orchard Repose" the Traveler goes to the orchard with Varesa and picks fruit and vegetables, including the specialty mushroom, for a meal. The quest is part of the "Mushroom Realm Mystery" chain.
### ULTRA +
The quest follows "Ardent Land of Plenty" and precedes "Sage Supreme". The pick-up objective lists apples, grainfruit, cacahuatl, quenepa berries and the red berryshroom, matching what the orchard grows.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Fragrant and sunny, with fruit-laden trees, a stand by the road and a large, watchful Tatankasaur at the gate; homely and abundant.

## status
@version: 5.5
### STANDARD
As of 7.1 it is a working orchard, open to the player after the relevant step of Orchard Repose.
