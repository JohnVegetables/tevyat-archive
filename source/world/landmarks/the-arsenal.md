## summary
### LITE
The Arsenal is a cave under Tatarasuna in Kannazuka, south of the Mikage Furnace, where the Tatarasuna workers hid a ruined blade. The Daitatara Nagamasa was forged by Inspector Mikoshi Nagamasa and became "Katsuragikiri Nagamasa" after he cut down his yoriki Katsuragi with it.
### STANDARD +
Inside lies a Luxurious Chest holding the diagram for forging a replica of that sword.

## geography
### STANDARD
The cave lies south of the Mikage Furnace; the gate is near the water on its eastern side. The surrounding furnace area is blocked by a Containment Field until part of the Tatara Tales quest is completed.
### LARGE +
Three Keys to Some Place found in Tatarasuna's Common Chests open the gate. On first entry three Ruin Sentinels spawn. The cave also holds an Electroculus and an Enigmatic Page. Gameplay note: the Luxurious Chest has Primogems, Mora and Hero's Wit as well as the Diagram: Katsuragikiri Nagamasa, which gives the Blade of Tatara achievement.

## history
### STANDARD
Per the wiki account, Mikoshi Nagamasa, Tatarasuna's Shogunate inspector, grew fond of bladesmithing and forged the Daitatara Nagamasa with the Vice Armory Officer Miyazaki. His group celebrated: Nozomu painted the blade, while Katsuragi and a nameless wandering eccentric performed a sword dance. Soon afterward the group broke up.
### LARGE +
The notes say Nagamasa flew into a rage over Katsuragi's alleged misdeed, slashed him with the blade (giving it its new name) and threw it into the furnace. Nozomu pulled the melted sword out and died of his burns that night. Kinjiro, with Miyazaki sharing his fear that Nagamasa could not be reasoned with, hid the remains and Nozomu's painting in the Arsenal.
### ULTRA +
One note writer still trusts Nagamasa and hopes he will one day remember the joy of forging by hand. Nagamasa was the foster son of the Mikoshi Clan, was abandoned by the legitimate heir Michihiro, and strove to clear his foster mother Chiyo's stain on the name. The nameless eccentric is the future Scaramouche, now the Wanderer, and his part in the affair is told in the quest The Night-Bird Falls at the Curtain's Call (Archon interlude Inversion of Genesis, 3.3), which also changes the notes once completed.

## quests
### STANDARD
Tatara Tales opens the furnace area; the Rather Aged Notes scattered around the Mikage Furnace recount the incident, and they read as if seized in a raid on the Fatui.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the Arsenal stands unchanged as an explorable cave, and nothing in 6.x or 7.0-7.1 content changes the Tatarasuna story.
