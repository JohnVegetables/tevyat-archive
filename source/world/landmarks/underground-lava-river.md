## summary
### LITE
The Underground Lava River is a cavern under Ameyalco Waters in Toyac Springs, Natlan, filled with liquid Phlogiston and subject to periodic eruptions. It is the only known source of Vulcanite, a rare golden crystal formed when liquid phlogiston cools quickly; this glow is what the People of the Springs' legend of the 'Mysterious Island' points toward. Koholasaurs live here, and an old trial door in its ruins opens a Spiritway to the Upper Sanctum.
### STANDARD +
The cave is the setting for two 5.0 quests: Mualani's Journey to the Mysterious Island and Chikya's Tale of Dreams Plucked From Fire.

## geography
### STANDARD
The cavern has an upper and a lower level and a hot, lava-filled maze. It begins with a cave south of the Ameyalco Waters Statue of the Seven, where a trial ruin sits with its Phlogiston Engravings, a half-opened Spiritdoor and a Spiritway on the edge of reality. Deeper in the lava pools, Spinel Fruit grows, a plant said to flourish near lava.
### LARGE +
In The Mysterious Island's Truth the way down is a giant whirlpool that is switched on by Phlogiston nodes and returns at dawn; underneath the water, Viramdra theorises that the Spiritways affected the lava deep underground, creating temporary holes through which the water's current carried the party into the cave.

## history
### STANDARD
The People of the Springs' legend tells of a first chief who sought a 'mysterious island' that shone like gold. Viramdra, a researcher, recognises the description: the shine is Vulcanite, a mineral formed when large amounts of lava are cooled quickly, which appears only as fierce volcanic activity begins and is promptly swallowed by the flow. The golden glow is a warning of an impending eruption.
### LARGE +
The first chief's hidden message on a statue explains that he chased the 'result' for years and reached the glow with his friends, but turned back at the final peril. He returned empty-handed and realised the treasure he most wanted was his friends' safety; he called this, and the tribe's present home, his real 'mysterious island'.
### ULTRA +
Mualani tells the party the map says: when you see the golden light, go the opposite way. Viramdra ignores that and runs in for the ore just as the lava becomes active again; Mualani crosses the lava on her surfboard to bring him back, and when the flow nears the party she whistles for Koholasaurs that carry everyone out. Mualani's lesson from the trip is to value companions over results, and Nuu says that those who understood the chief's intent have chosen not to boast.

## people
### STANDARD
Mualani, the People of the Springs' young guide, leads the Journey to the Mysterious Island with Viramdra, Karya and the Traveler; her teacher Nuu appears at the end. Chikya enters the lava ruin in Tracer No Tracing. The cavern's only residents are creatures: Koholasaurs and Koholasaur Whelps, with Fluid Avatars of Lava as hazards.

## quests
### STANDARD
Where the Springs Return, Act III (The Mysterious Island's Truth, story quest, 5.0): the party reaches the cave, argues over Vulcanite and the chief's message. Tale of Dreams Plucked From Fire (world quest, 5.0): Tracer No Tracing starts here, Seeker No Finding follows in the Upper Sanctum.
### LARGE +
In Tracer No Tracing the party finds Unveiling Scripts, using an indwelling Koholasaurus and Aquadash to fetch one, and fights a Fluid Avatar of Lava. The Spiritdoor then half-opens and the party rides a Spiritway out of the lava. Chikya remembers an old scroll of ancestors swimming in lava and thinks it might have been a warning to trial-takers.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Hot, gold-lit, humid and unsteady: lava pools and glowing ore, a quiet stone ruin, and the constant risk that the flow is about to rise.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Rising lava and eruptions, Large Pyro Slimes and Fluid Avatars of Lava, a Secret Source Automaton: Hunter-Seeker guarding the Tracer ruin, and falls.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the cavern remains hot and active, with Koholasaurs living in it. Vulcanite still cannot be brought back, and the Spiritway trial is solved.
### STANDARD +
No later quest in 6.x or 7.0-7.1 returns here.

### STANDARD
Vulcanite is a rare, glowing golden crystal formed when liquid Phlogiston cools quickly. It forms at the start of fierce volcanic activity and is almost at once swallowed by the lava, so it appears impossible to obtain. The cavern under Ameyalco Waters is its only known site. Viramdra had hoped the ore would prove his theory, but concluded in the end that it can never be taken out.

## entry: The People of the Springs' Mysterious Island

@keys2: Mualani, People of the Springs, Natlan
@spoiler: minor
@version: 5.0
### LARGE
The legend of the Mysterious Island is the rite of passage of the People of the Springs' guides: a map says the island shines like gold and warns to go the opposite way from the golden light. Mualani's story quest shows that the golden light comes from Vulcanite in a lava cave and that the first chief's true 'island' was the tribe's home, the small island on which it was founded, which later coastlines turned into what it is today. Nuu says guides who understand this choose whether to share it or keep the legend for future guides.
