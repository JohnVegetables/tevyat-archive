## summary
### LITE
The Throne of the Primal Fire is a site in Ochkanatlan where the throne of the first Pyro Archon, Xbalanque, once stood. The map text says he borrowed the Ruler of Death's power here to establish Natlan's rules.
### STANDARD +
A thousand years later, the same place is described as where another human hero has "frozen" life and death, to remain there forever.

## history
@version: 5.3
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
In the map description, the throne belonged to Xbalanque, the first Pyro Archon. At this spot he borrowed the power of the Ruler of Death, understood to be Ronova, to establish Natlan's rules of death and rebirth.
### LARGE +
The text then says a thousand years have passed and another human hero has frozen life and death here. The wiki connects this figure to Capitano. The throne's own voice line says a noble figure coats the sun with frost and that two eras grant salvation to Natlan not once but twice.
### ULTRA +
The wiki also notes the scene resembles a scene from another franchise, a trivia point and not canon.

## gameplay
@version: 5.3
### LARGE
Game mechanics: the Pyro-aligned Traveler can offer Blazing Flint Ore to the throne to raise Primal Flame Resonance. Six ores are the maximum and each unlocks bonus effects at a constellation level. Offering requires the Traveler as the active character and attuned to Pyro. The offering system unlocks after completing "Incandescent Ode of Resurrection".

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Austere and sombre, a seat of power gone cold and frosted, with death and rebirth hanging over it.

## status
@version: 5.3
### STANDARD
As of 7.1 the location is a point of interest in Ochkanatlan; it appears after the quest "When All Becomes a Monument". Before that quest the throne is part of the Cradle of Fleeting Dreams.
