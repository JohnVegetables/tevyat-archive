## summary
### LITE
The Snowmelt Drainage Channel is an underground system beneath Okurov, the strange town in Flamefeather Valley. It was meant to carry snowmelt away so the valley basin would not flood. According to the wiki it was built on the orders of Koshchei, who also used it as a laboratory.
### STANDARD +
The Traveler and Paimon land in it by accident in the quest Down in the Dumps and have to find a way back up.

## geography
### STANDARD
Three map levels: the mouth, an upper level, and a lower level. Okurov lies in a low basin, and the channels were dug beneath it. The tunnels are collapsing and abandoned, with gates, flooded sections, rotary mechanisms, and a control room with tanks. Key terminals control the gates and the water level.
### LARGE +
Three kinds of keys open the way: red, white and blue. Inserting either the white or the blue key into the control console lowers the water level; inserting both drains it completely. All three keys must be inserted at once at a control board to open the gate out, which the guide describes as three rounds of authentication.

## people
### STANDARD
No living residents in the quest beyond Leshy. The Traveler and Paimon are guided by the Eye of Graeae. Zhara, the large bird that attacked them, is what sent them down; the Eye suspects Zhara exists to preserve Okurov's status quo.
### LARGE +
Furiosa's name is on the post-op observation reports found in the control room. The Eye of Graeae recalls Koshchei the Deathless as one of the four who made its body and a leading figure of Snezhnaya's vanished school of bio-alchemy.

## history
### STANDARD
In Down in the Dumps the Traveler falls from Okurov into the channel after Zhara attacks them, while trying to get Goggles' body part back and bring Nastya home. The Eye of Graeae says the town's mutated residents and the half-bird creature remind it of Koshchei's approach to life. The channel's written guide, whose cover bears the name "Doctor Moreau", explains that Okurov sits in a basin where, under the influence of something redacted, temperatures may rise and snowmelt may flood it, so an underground channel system was dug first for drainage and second for something redacted, from which a "tender sprout" emerged.
### LARGE +
The guide closes with a wish that when the floodwaters become nourishment for life and useless memories fade into echoes, the author will make his way to a redacted place. The wording echoes the Furiosa report, where new branches and leaves have covered the old body and unneeded memories and emotions are fading.
### ULTRA +
Along the way the Traveler sees a Leshy preserved in a glass tank, other Leshy lying incapacitated around a chest, and signs that nobody has been down there in a long time. The quest is the third part of the chapter In the Dwelling of Life and is followed by Back on One's Feet.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Dank, dim and half-forgotten: collapsed paths, standing water, glass tanks in a control room, and notes written by someone who clearly expected to come back.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Leshy roam the tunnels and some must be defeated, and the water level changes with the gates. Paimon describes the sewer as crawling with them.

## quests
### STANDARD
Down in the Dumps, part of the series In the Dwelling of Life. The Sewer Entrance/Exit Guide can be read only during that quest.

## naming
### ULTRA
Chinese 融雪引流道 (Rongxue Yinliu-dao, "snowmelt diversion channel"), Japanese 融雪導水路, Korean 하수도 ("sewer"), Spanish Canal de deshielo. In English dialogue Paimon calls it the sewer and "this dump".

## status
### STANDARD
As of 7.1 the channel is a derelict, mostly abandoned network. Leaving takes all three keys at the control board; no later content in the sources changes it.
