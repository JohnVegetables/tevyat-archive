## summary
### LITE
The Field Tiller is an inactive Ruin Guard on the upper deck of the central tower of Stormterror's Lair in the Brightcrown Mountains. Before the quest We Will Be Reunited the game calls it a Weird Statue. It is the original prototype of the war machines now known as Ruin Guards.
### STANDARD +
"Field Tiller" was a Khaenri'ah codename for such machines. This one was far stronger than its later copies and, according to Dainsleif, unrestricted in combat power.

## history
### STANDARD
The first Field Tiller left Khaenri'ah and wandered Teyvat, ending up in Wolvendom, where Boreas took it for a challenger and fought it. Badly damaged, it fled to the ruins of Old Mondstadt and became immobile but still active. Sensing danger, it fired missiles at anyone approaching the old city. Mondstadters, who knew little of Ruin Machines, took this for a last outburst of the dead storm god Decarabian and called it the Tyrant's Final Fury. This lasted about a year before the machine shut down.
### LARGE +
Dainsleif says all current Ruin Guards are replicas of this prototype. He cites a Khaenri'ah saying that land is won with steel and blood rather than farm tools. The Abyss Order planned to place its eye into the Defiled Statue and attach Osial's limbs to it, creating a mechanised god. When the Traveler's elemental sight failed to extract the eye, Dainsleif explained that the machine does not run on elemental energy; he removed it himself and vowed to guard it.

## status
### STANDARD
Inactive. After We Will Be Reunited it is labelled Field Tiller rather than Weird Statue, and says nothing. Its eye is in Dainsleif's keeping as of the quest; no later change is reported through 7.1.

### ULTRA +
Different localisations render the machine's name differently (for example the Chinese and Japanese names mean a tillage or cultivating machine), reflecting that the term was a deliberate euphemism for a weapon. Dainsleif's remarks tie it to the fall of Khaenri'ah five centuries ago.
