## summary
### LITE
The Sanctum of the Oathbearer, also called the Court of the First Edict, is a sealed ceremonial sanctum of the Frostmoon Scions on Hiisi Island. Until five hundred years ago the Scions held their rites here. After Aila learned of Lovia's treachery and killed her, she sealed the order's ancient documents and the evidence of Lovia's corruption inside, to stop any later leader from rising as Lovia did. It is reopened in the world quest Echoes of an Unfinished Past.
### STANDARD +
The loading screen describes it as a sealed sacred sanctum, and says that the dark places moonlight cannot touch do not hide only blessings. It is entered from the base of the Sanctum of the Oathkeeper.

## geography
### STANDARD
Entered from the base of the Sanctum of the Oathkeeper. The interactable documents are on the upper level; the ground level holds the White Spring, where Aila left the ritual sword Moonweaver's Dawn. A Moonlane leads through the Court during the quest.
### LARGE +
In the middle of the Court stands the Verdant Crest, a divine tree said to have appeared with the Moon Maiden Columbina. The Interstice of the Nearing Moon, which Aila's notes call the great pillar raised here by the Dragon-born and the sanctuary where the Scions' ancestors found refuge, is reached from the same outer area.

## history
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Aila sealed the Court after assassinating Lovia, the Archpriestess whose rule had corrupted the Frostmoon Scions. She stored the order's ancient documents and proof of Lovia's corruption here, and left Moonweaver's Dawn, a ritual sword forged by Anacharsis in the Hyperborean era, in the White Spring. The place stayed sealed until Verna and the Traveler opened it in Echoes of an Unfinished Past, as Aila had predicted.
### LARGE +
The surviving documents show the order's inner history. Lovia's decrees speak of a "sacred heir", selected through generations of controlled marriage, who alone could rouse the Sampo Mill and create an Elysium. Rotwang, writing to Lovia as a friend and ally from the Belyi Tsar's court, helped her try to recover the woman destined to bear the heir. That woman died by her own hand. Lovia then turned to the twins Alia and Aila as substitutes. Later letters show Solovei, who proposes founding the Lightkeepers, thanking Aila for the Arrow of Terpikeraunas and mourning Alia.
### ULTRA +
Aila's private notes record her view of the order. She believed the Scions should tear down their walls, towers and fortresses and keep only the sanctuary, so that isolation could not breed new ambition. She hoped future Scions would honour nature and kindness to outlanders, not the pride of the dead, and that a new moon goddess, foreseen to be born decades later, would keep the sole power of choice, never moulded into a "perfect deity" or paid with sacrifices. She admits that she lied to Solovei when he asked whether she felt hatred.

## culture
### LARGE
The Scions' rites placed reason above sentiment according to the teachings of the "apostle" of the Golden City (Anacharsis). The decrees forbid many marriages, require offerings to the Sampo Mill before spring sowing and demand oil rites for the Arrow of Terpikeraunas. A tattered children's fairy tale printed by Kitezhgrad Publishing House is also found here, about a kind girl who befriends a white pigeon.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
A sealed, silent court of old stone and moonlight, with a divine tree at its heart. It feels like a place left deliberately untouched, full of documents that someone chose not to destroy.

## quests
### STANDARD
Echoes of an Unfinished Past (a world quest of the chapter Polkka Beneath the Moon's Oracle) starts at the Light-Bathed Platform: Verna asks the Traveler to throw fragments to open the sanctum, follow the Moonlane, unlock a mechanism seal, enter the Court, and investigate the sword Aila left behind. Completing it earns the achievement The Weaver in the Ossuary.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the Sanctum of the Oathbearer is unsealed and open to exploration.

## entry: Aila's Notes
@keys: Aila's Notes, Court of the First Edict
@keys2: Aila, Solovei, Lovia
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Aila's private notes record that she lied, as Lovia and her sister Alia had, when Solovei asked whether she felt hatred, because she did not want to burden him. She calls her hatred horrifying and does not want it understood. She resolves that everything of the old order must be torn down but the sanctuary, that kindness to outlanders and reverence for nature should set the Scions apart, and that the Frostmoon Scions must leave the future new moon goddess free to choose. She foresees a new moon goddess born decades later, that Lovia's long quest was for nothing, and that the Scions, redeemed in her name, must never demand repayment from her.

## entry: Letters to Lovia and Aila
@keys: Letter for Lovia, Letter for Aila
@keys2: Sanctum of the Oathbearer, Rotwang, Solovei
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The Letter for Lovia is signed by Rotwang. It reports that the "holy lady" his agents were told to bring to Lovia killed herself on the spot, offers sympathy and gifts, and agrees that they must find another way to rouse the Sampo Mill and build an "Elysium" for the people of Nod-Krai. The Letter for Aila is signed by Solovei. He laments that he could not stop Alia, thanks her for her sacrifice against Rotwang, passes on her last wish that Aila live a life of happiness and freedom, and agrees to receive the Arrow of Terpikeraunas, proposing to call his successors the Lightkeepers.

## entry: The Ancient Volume and the Sampo Mill
@keys: Ancient Volume, Sampo Mill, Sacred Heir, Hyperborean decrees
@keys2: Frostmoon Scions, Hiisi Island, Lovia
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
The Ancient Volume is a three-part compilation of Frostmoon Scion decrees. The first part is a decree approved by Lovia, which includes a plan to change how the Sacred Heir is selected (its content is erased). The second part, attributed to the thirteenth Archpriestess Saredne, forbids marriage to many categories of people, describes the Elysium prophecy, orders offerings to the Sampo Mill and gives a formula for anointing the Arrow of Terpikeraunas. The third part, hidden behind the back cover, is a bleaker, more technical document by Spargapiris of Haxathrausa, Acting Archpriestess of Hyperborea, describing the Sacred Heir project as no longer viable after Hyperborea's fall, and the Sampo Mill (the "Subatomic Assembling Mass-Energy Patterning Orb") as sealed because its control module was lost. It also proposes remaking the Arrow of Terpikeraunas.
### ULTRA +
The third part also sets out an artificial-selection programme for the Scions' bloodline as a stopgap, and says that once civilisation is secure the descendants of the far north should be free to decide their own destinies.

## entry: Moonweaver's Dawn and the White Spring
@keys: Moonweaver's Dawn, White Spring
@keys2: Aila, Verna, Anacharsis
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Moonweaver's Dawn is a ritual sword forged by Anacharsis in the Hyperborean era. Aila placed it in the White Spring at the Court's ground level. At the end of Echoes of an Unfinished Past the Traveler investigates the sword she left behind.

## entry: The Verdant Crest
@keys: Verdant Crest, Hymns of the Far North
@keys2: Columbina, Moon Maiden, Frostmoon Scions
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
The Verdant Crest is a divine tree in the middle of the Court, said to have appeared alongside the Moon Maiden Columbina. In the book Hymns of the Far North, an annotator points out that it also appears in hymns of rituals older than the Moon Maiden's birth five centuries ago, which should not be possible.
