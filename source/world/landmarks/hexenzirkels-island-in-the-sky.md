## summary
### LITE
Hexenzirkel's Island in the Sky is a floating island above Falcon Coast where the Hexenzirkel used to hold tea parties. Barbatos looks after access to it, and one has to speak an incantation at the cliff to enter.
### STANDARD +
It was first opened during the limited-time Windblume's Breath event (version 3.5), and became permanently accessible once the Traveler completes the Paralogism archon quest (5.6).

## places
@version: 3.5
### STANDARD
At its centre stands a long tea table with seven chairs: three on one side, two on the other and one at each end. The Lantern of Utmost Joy sits at the table's centre, and a small phonograph like Alice used in "Midsummer Island Adventure" stands near the head. Books, flowers and silverware are scattered around.
### LARGE +
The lantern can be operated by four people sitting around it and saying the words "Please allow me to disturb your happy peace."

## travel
@version: 3.5
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
The incantation is spoken at the cliff above Falcon Coast, and begins "Place the squirrel on the back of the pointy-eared cat..." (the full verse is in the event quest). Venti granted the Traveler access during the Windblume Festival's event quest.

## history
@version: 5.6
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
After Paralogism the island stays reachable. There the Traveler faces "The Game Before the Gate", the trial of Alice and Barbeloth. In the quest "The Gardener" the Hexenzirkel's trial is accepted as part of the story of the Interlude Chapter.
### ULTRA +
Details of what the trial reveals belong in the quest and Alice's entries, not here.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
An airy, whimsical garden party suspended in cloud, with tea things and flowers and the faint music of a phonograph; a place of gentle riddles that can turn serious.

## entry: Hexenzirkel tea party island access
@keys: Island in the Sky, Lantern of Utmost Joy, Hexenzirkel tea party
@version: 3.5
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
The Hexenzirkel's floating island above Falcon Coast is entered by an incantation spoken at the cliff and kept open by Barbatos. During the Windblume's Breath event the Traveler used it to find the Lantern of Utmost Joy.
