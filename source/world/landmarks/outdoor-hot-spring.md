## summary
### LITE
The Outdoor Hot Spring is a facility in Everfrozen Earth built around a hot spring that appeared by accident. Humans and fae alike come here to enjoy a rare bit of warmth. It is kept up by an NPC named Kleyeva and is free for everyone.
### STANDARD +
The notice at the entrance sets house rules by species and tells visitors not to report the spring to the Energy Commission.

## geography
### STANDARD
An open-air pool with a deck beside it, and a building with a first room whose pool also restores health. The spring's southern entrance has a flight of stairs leading up to a notice board. Neither the pool nor the interior counts as a low-temperature zone.
### LARGE +
A second copy of the notice board stands on the deck. The wiki notes the Eye of Graeae's energy does not drain while the Traveler is here, a gameplay effect of the warm area.

## people
### STANDARD
Kleyeva maintains the spring and lets everyone use it for free. Thieving Jack Frost is a Jack Frost who stole her clothes in a hidden objective; the wiki ties their dialogue to the hidden objective "Hot Spring Clothing Caper". In Come Out Now!, Kintutu, a Natlan tourist, visits with her Flowfire Bird Javita.
### LARGE +
The wiki infers that Kleyeva has had several unpleasant Vila guests, since the notice singles out Vila for complaining.

## culture
### STANDARD
The notice says the spring was created by the runoff of pipes, is therefore everyone's common property, and must be looked after, and "under no circumstances" be reported to the Energy Commission. Humans should wash before entering. Volkodlak should remove loose fur. Vila should not come if they are going to complain. Jack Frosts and Snegovik should watch how long they stay to avoid fainting from overexposure.
### LARGE +
After Come Out Now! the notice adds, in a larger and thicker hand, that Flowcurrent Birds and Flowfire Birds are not allowed in.

## history
### STANDARD
In the hidden objective Come Out Now! the Flowfire Bird Javita, a Natlanese tourist's companion, grows livelier in the spring and breaks the railings while running around. The Traveler calms her by creating a Prime Ice cube in the gap. Kintutu then takes her bird home; the Flowcurrent Birds that were swimming vanish with them.
### LARGE +
Kintutu says Javita was gloomy on arrival in Snezhnaya, and that she took the locals' advice to try the hot spring. The bird looks dejected when calmed, as though realising the place is not home. After the visit Kleyeva banned the birds because they had made her Jack Frost guests uncomfortable.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Steamy and unexpectedly homely in a frozen land: a small, slightly shabby public bath with hand-written rules. Being warm is a luxury and everyone treats it as one.

## dangers
### STANDARD
No fighting. Jack Frosts and Snegovik can overheat if they stay too long, according to the notice.

## quests
### STANDARD
No main quest. The hidden objectives Come Out Now! and the one involving Kleyeva's clothing involve it.

## naming
### ULTRA

## status

As of 7.1 the spring is open and Kleyeva still maintains it. Flowcurrent and Flowfire Birds are banned.
