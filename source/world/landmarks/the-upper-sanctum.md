## summary
### LITE
The Upper Sanctum, also called Hanan Pacha, is a ring of floating islands above the lake at Ameyalco Waters in Toyac Springs, Natlan. Legend says the Sage of the Stolen Flame and his companion Chaac raised it into the sky, and that people who passed his trials were taught to use phlogiston there and formed the first human tribe. The islands are said to be held together by Phlogiston. A trial ends at a hidden hot spring in the centre.
### STANDARD +
It is called the Upper Sanctum because neither humans nor saurians can ordinarily reach it. A Spiritway is needed.

## geography
### STANDARD
Today the Upper Sanctum is not one island but a ring of smaller islands, visible from the lake below. It can only be entered by activating a Spiritway, and the ancient trial mechanisms (Spiritways and Iridescent Inscriptions) still work. In Seeker No Finding the Traveler follows a sequence of Spiritways and inscriptions to a hidden centre island with a hot spring full of phlogiston. Phlogiston also supports grass and flowers and warms cold ground, Chikya says.
### LARGE +
Gameplay note: besides the quest route, the islands can also be reached with a geyser pillar south of Toyac Springs, with a flying character followed by a Natlan character such as Kinich. The Phlogiston Chiselith found all over Natlan are said, in the scrolls, to have been modelled on the mechanisms here.

## history
### STANDARD
By the legend in Records of Hanan Pacha, the Sage of the Stolen Flame, Waxaklahun Ubah Kan, brought back the 'kindling' of phlogiston from the red and black lands by tricking a vain winged dragon out of its Pyrophosphorite. Because nobody wanted to learn from him, he first taught the 'fool' Chaac, and the two raised a tract of land into the air: Hanan Pacha. Chaac guarded the lake and helped people ascend; those who passed the Sage's trials learned phlogiston, and they formed the first permanent tribe there. The book says historians regard this as a fable with no historical basis.
### LARGE +
The same fable says the people built a city of gems, feathers and flowers on the islands, and the Sage decided to raise them higher still, forbidding any food produced by the land. The Lord of the Night, fearing humanity's departure, tricked Chaac with an intoxicating drink, and when the Sage returned and found everyone drunk he expelled the tribe, which carried phlogiston across Natlan. The Sage then set out for home, and the fable says he will one day return.
### ULTRA +
In Tracer No Tracing, Chikya says some call the Sage a madman or lunatic, which she takes as a compliment, and that he stole the flaming phlogiston seed from the giant dragon. Some of her tribe doubt a human could do it. Quest 5.5 later reveals that 'Waxaklahun Ubah Kan' is one of many names invented by Kukulkan, the dragon behind the Sage of the Stolen Flame, which complicates the legend's claim that the Sage was human.

## people
### STANDARD
Chikya, listed by the wiki as a member of the Masters of the Night-Wind, who cannot wield phlogiston because of an Abyss-related injury in her youth, is the quest's guide; she looks after her tribe's ancient woven scrolls. The Traveler, Paimon and Little One travel with her.

## quests
### STANDARD
Tale of Dreams Plucked From Fire (5.0): Tracer No Tracing starts in the Underground Lava River, where a Spiritdoor trial asks for Unveiling Scripts and opens a Spiritway to the Upper Sanctum. Seeker No Finding then takes the party up to the islands, where they follow Iridescent Inscriptions along Spiritways to the hot spring.
### LARGE +
Chikya had told the Traveler the spring might be a 'legendary elixir that cures all injuries'. She knew from the start it was a hot spring and confesses she tricked them; her injuries are not cured, but she stays to soak.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
From below it looks grey and empty, but the islands hold grass and flowers and warm ground. The final trial is a fight with Avatars of Lava awakened by a stone tablet, after which the water level rises and the spring fills with phlogiston.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Falling from the floating islands, and the Eroding Avatars of Lava that the final trial's stone tablet awakens.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the Upper Sanctum remains a broken ring of islands whose trials and hot spring still function. Chikya soaks in the spring and returns to her scrolls.
### STANDARD +
No later quest in 6.x or 7.0-7.1 returns here. The Records of Hanan Pacha can be found in Natlan's Scions of the Canopy and the Stadium of the Sacred Flame.

## entry: Hanan Pacha hot spring
@keys: Hanan Pacha hot spring, Sage's gift, Upper Sanctum hot spring
@spoiler: minor
@version: 5.0
### STANDARD
The hot spring at the centre of the Upper Sanctum is the 'gift' the Sage of the Stolen Flame leaves for those who pass his trial. After the final fight its water level rises and the spring becomes full of phlogiston. Chikya says a chosen hero who bathes in it receives the power to wield phlogiston; for the Traveler, who could already use phlogiston, nothing changed.

## entry: Records of Hanan Pacha
@keys: Records of Hanan Pacha, Waxaklahun Ubah Kan, Chaac, winged dragon
@keys2: Natlan, Sage of the Stolen Flame, Hanan Pacha
### LARGE
Records of Hanan Pacha is a three-volume fable in Natlan collected from the Scions of the Canopy and the Stadium of the Sacred Flame. It tells first how the Sage and Chaac stole phlogiston from a vain winged dragon that had claimed Xiuhcoatl's treasures, then how phlogiston was passed to people and Hanan Pacha was raised, and finally (volume 3) how the tribe broke the Sage's taboo and was expelled. The book carries a note that scholars consider it unrelated to historical fact.
