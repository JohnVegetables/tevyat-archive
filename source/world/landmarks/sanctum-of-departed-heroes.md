## summary
### LITE
The Sanctum of Departed Heroes is the final resting place of many Lightkeepers who died in battle, at Kipumaki Cliff in Ashveil Peak, Nod-Krai. It was once a hotspot of Wild Hunt activity. Its viewpoint is named the Hall of Resounding Valor.
### STANDARD +
Two diary entries by a newly selected Lightkeeper recruit are kept here, continuing one found at the Pillar of Embla.

## geography
### STANDARD
In Ashveil Peak, at Kipumaki Cliff, close to Cliffwatch Camp and the Chasm of Endless Decay. An unsigned diary calls Kipumaki Cliff itself the place "often called" the Sanctum of Departed Heroes, the resting ground of soldiers who fell in battle.

## history
@version: 6.3
### STANDARD
Piramida ordered a support unit stationed at Kipumaki Cliff to clear Wild Hunt nests; the Frostgale Steeds were called in as reinforcements during a sudden outbreak that spilled onto the ramparts of the Sanctum. Lightkeepers who died in such fights are laid to rest here.
### LARGE +
The cliff was also where the Abyssal Scourge of Endless Decay had been imprisoned five hundred years ago, in the Chasm of Endless Decay below.

## people
@version: 6.3
### STANDARD
The three diary entries in this set belong to the recruit Filaev, under Captain Asebov in a support unit with Chernov, Shonokovych and Patolichev. The second entry describes the terror of his first mission: he froze as a monster threw itself at him and was saved by Chernov, and afterwards wondered whether he would die or see his mother and Nasha Town again.

### ULTRA +
In the third entry, a roaring Wild Hunt attack about four in the morning shook the camp, with a smell of blood in the air. Captain Asebov shouted orders, and flashes of memory (the training grounds, his first night with the unit, and the smell of ribs his mother used to grill) filled Filaev's mind. It was the first time he fought with the squad to clear a nest; he was injured but less afraid than that earlier night, and hoped he would grow into the person he wished to be.

## dangers
### LARGE
Wild Hunt nests and raids around Kipumaki Cliff; Abyssal corruption from the nearby chasm before the Scourge was defeated.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the Sanctum is open to exploration; the wiki describes it as having once been a Wild Hunt hotspot.

## entry: Filaev's Diary
@keys: Filaev, Neatly-Written Diary
@keys2: Lightkeeper, Ratnik, Kipumaki Cliff, Asebov
@version: 6.3
### STANDARD
Filaev passed the Ratnik selection with the lowest rank, loved the uniform and the lantern with his name on it, and hoped his story would reach Nasha Town. Posted to a support unit at Kipumaki Cliff, he spent months in routine until Piramida ordered the unit to clear Wild Hunt nests, which he took as his first chance to prove himself. His fate is not recorded.
