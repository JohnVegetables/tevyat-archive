## summary
### LITE
The Sand-Embraced Home is a point of interest in Old Vanarana, in the Lost Nursery of Sumeru. It is unlocked during the Aranyaka world quest For the Children of the Past.
### STANDARD +
It can be reached by going down from the northern teleport waypoint in Old Vanarana. A mural there can be revisited after the Aranyaka is complete.
### LARGE +
Revisiting the mural after completing the Aranyaka grants the achievement Though to the Earth I May Return...

## geography
### STANDARD
Located in Old Vanarana, below the northern waypoint. Old Vanarana is part of the Lost Nursery and is affected by the Withering in the quest.

## people
@version: 3.0
### STANDARD
Arama accompanies the Traveler through Old Vanarana in this quest and speaks of the Aranara who were lost. The quest includes a step leaving Aramuhukunda's home, but whether it is this place is not stated.

## history
### STANDARD
The quest has the Traveler gather three Inscriptions of Remembrance, face a seal at the Tumor of the Withering, and uncover a way down.
### LARGE +
Unlike most Withering-affected areas, Decay here builds up on its own over time during the quest, while Arama's aura reduces it.

## quests
### STANDARD
For the Children of the Past, the second part of Nursery of Lost Dreams, leads into For All Children Who Long for Life.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
A quiet place below ground in Old Vanarana, linked to remembrance of the lost Aranara.
