## summary
### LITE
The Witch's Garden is a garden point of interest in Voidsea Outlook, Nod-Krai, introduced in version 6.3. It holds the Witch's Lodge, one of the secret homes of the Hexenzirkel mage Nicole, and has a Meeting Point of the same name beside a statue before the bridge. Its map description stresses that the garden's sculptures are ordinary and hold no time-stopping magic.
### STANDARD +
Visitors reach it through Nicole's Witch's Lodge world quest, which she presents as a tale about a grand mage called Nicola Coco. Over the following versions the lodge became a small treasure-hunt house with rooms that open one at a time. Nicole treats the place as both a hobby and a trap for the curious: help is offered to those who do their own work.
### LARGE +
Within Nod-Krai it works as a quiet, whimsical corner more than a threat. It is tied to Nicole personally, and its theme is a mage's show of power that she keeps deliberately gentle.

## geography
@version: 6.3
### STANDARD
The garden lies in the Voidsea Outlook area of Nod-Krai, which the wiki locates at the southern end of the island Pohjola, north of Paha Isle. A bridge leads toward the lodge; the Meeting Point statue stands before it.
### LARGE +
A statue of a squirrel and several carved animals, including finches and a squirrel carving, appear in the quest storyline. Nicole says the statues are her way of capturing a moment in time. The viewpoint description calls the decorations ordinary sculpture with no power to stop time, and describes the secret garden as a place without secrets, which comments ironically on Fate.

## places
@version: 6.3
### STANDARD
The Witch's Lodge is the only listed point of interest inside the garden. The Meeting Point (level 5 unlocks a final Nicole conversation, Witch's Chatter) sits by the bridge-side statue.
### LARGE +
After The Joyous Floating Island the lodge interior opens: a tea room with oversized teapots and a Mandragora statue, a study with a painting, a studio with an angel statue, a tall study with bookshelves, and a hidden room that overlooks the world. Each new quest unlocks another room, and the hidden room bears a resemblance to structures at the Temple of Space.

## people
@version: 6.3
### STANDARD
Nicole is the only resident, and she is often absent. Post-quest lines have her say she is away (in one set, in Sumeru), leaving tea on the table; during The Lost Palace she says she is running a sculpture stall in Fontaine. Paimon and the Traveler are her usual guests.
### LARGE +
Nicole mentions friends from the Hexenzirkel, among them Alice, Anya and Barbeloth, and says Mr. Nine could help finish her writing. Her mention of the nearby Curatorium of Secrets and its member Nefer is a plain pointer for uninvited greedy visitors.

## culture
@version: 6.3
### LARGE
In the Witch's Homework conversation Nicole serves a tea party where the teapot stays half full and a fresh cake or cookie is always on each plate, which she explains as keeping a mage's elegance. She also says guidance can breed complacency and entitlement if given carelessly.

## history
@version: 6.3
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
The Witch's Garden opened with Luna IV (6.3, released 2026-01-14), together with its Witch's Lodge quest and Meeting Point. The hidden Beyond Silver and Fine Gold quest series, with new rooms, arrived with Luna VII (6.6, 2026-05-20), the same version as Nicole's release.
### LARGE +
In the Witch's Lodge quest the Traveler and Paimon photograph local subjects for statue ideas, drive off an enraged Mandragora, and carve a squirrel statue together with Nicole two days later. Nicole then tells her improvised tale of Nicola Coco, a mage who turns out to be a princess from Planet Meow, and says every creation eventually escapes its creator's control.
### ULTRA +
The lodge quest also tells the Traveler, through the dialogue Nicole gives, that she had meant to stop after one home but now wants several so that someone might wander in by mistake. The achievement Joint Meeting Point Construction: The Witch's Garden is awarded for raising the garden's Meeting Point to level 5.

## quests
@version: 6.3
### STANDARD
Witch's Lodge (6.3) opens the area; Witch's Chatter follows at Meeting Point level 5. Beyond Silver and Fine Gold (6.6) has four weekly parts, The Joyous Floating Island, The Silent Theater, The Lost Palace and The Forgotten Lighthouse. A Window Into the World and Witch's Homework: The Role of a Guide...? add the hidden room and an extra conversation.
### LARGE +
The quest series is spaced out: after finishing a room, the next unlocks at 04:00 the following Monday, and the whole set takes about four weeks. Each quest ends with a treasure chest whose Mora and other rewards Nicole says have been fully handed over, joking that more will take a few hundred years.

## status
@version: 6.3
### STANDARD
As of 7.1 the garden is open. Nicole is a playable character and Hexenzirkel member as of 6.6, and the lodge remains available.
### LARGE +
No canon through 7.1 shows the garden damaged, abandoned or changed in function.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Warm, whimsical and theatrical: tea on the table, carved animals standing in the open, a mage who narrates herself as grand while clearly enjoying the audience. The sense is of a theatre set the owner built for visitors, not a fortress.
### LARGE +
Music tied to the area includes the tracks After Tea Party and Seelieland Nocturne from the album Where Roads Are Pledged to Cross.

## dangers
@version: 6.3
### STANDARD
The only hostile encounters in the lodge storyline are a Mandragora (including a giant Mandragora statue that Nicole animates) and the puzzle statues. Nicole says she does not turn people into statues, though she jokes she might.

## travel
@version: 6.3
### STANDARD
It is reached on foot in Voidsea Outlook, and the Meeting Point acts as a waypoint. Quest rooms are entered through the lodge doors; in the hidden-room storyline the lodge is described as containing more space than its outside shows.

## entry: Nicole's Witch's Lodge quest
@keys: Witch's Lodge, Nicola Coco, Great Mage Everlasting
@version: 6.3
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
The Witch's Lodge world quest begins when the Traveler enters the garden and hears a narrated story about a runaway who falls into a secret home. The voice belongs to Nicole, who calls herself Nicola Coco, the Great Mage Everlasting. She asks for photographs of lively subjects to inspire statues, and later shows the Traveler how to carve blocks using number clues. The finished lodge appears beside the bridge. Her story is improvised, ends with Planet Meow and a cat-turned protagonist, and she says this is a creation that outgrew its author.
### LARGE +
Nicole uses a Mandragora, finches and a squirrel statue as models. Post-quest she gives randomised dialogue about building several homes, hiding in Sumeru, and missing the angels as a people. Treat the Planet Meow tale as in-world fiction told by Nicole, not as lore.

## entry: Beyond Silver and Fine Gold
@keys: Beyond Silver and Fine Gold, Joyous Floating Island, Silent Theater, Lost Palace, Forgotten Lighthouse
@version: 6.6
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
This four-part world series unlocks the lodge's rooms, each with a treasure hunt for three golden feathers. In the tea room Nicole shrinks the party for the Great Mage's Tea Party. The studio frames show her joining the Hexenzirkel after an invitation from Alice and, according to the quest, the first angel Koitar's betrayal beneath an Irminsul. The Lost Palace room is a shrine she says was her home as an angel. The Forgotten Lighthouse uses words that become golden platforms.
### LARGE +
In The Silent Theater Nicole says Alice knew her identity and told her to stay quiet at that first tea; she admits speaking to Alice's mind and says Alice complained of a headache afterward. In The Lost Palace she explains that angels gathered for her stories about humans. In The Forgotten Lighthouse she begins to explain things by going back to the first time the mages challenged the Anemo Archon, and hopes Alice never hears of her embarrassment.

## entry: Witch's Lodge hidden room
@keys: Witch's Lodge hidden room, A Window Into the World
@version: 6.6
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
After A Window Into the World, a bookcase in the lodge opens to a room that Nicole calls one of her fixed observation points for changes in the world, chosen for excellent visibility. She says the scene it shows exists only in her memories. She says she has no regrets about the price she paid for becoming human, yet remains an angel at heart.
### LARGE +
Nicole mentions that if she kept her former form and power she would be bound by many more restrictions from the Heavenly Principles, including limits on what she may speak of. She says Teyvat's fate has been shaken more often recently. Her voice line when the platform is used with her as the active character begins with the words that observation is commencing.

## entry: Nicole and the Curatorium of Secrets
@keys: Curatorium of Secrets, Nefer
@keys2: Witch's Garden, Witch's Lodge, Nicole
@version: 6.3
### STANDARD
In Witch's Chatter Nicole says she would help a visitor who is willing to rely on their own effort. A person who expects magic to do all the work would be guided away to the nearby Curatorium of Secrets, where she says Nefer would know how to deal with them better than she would. She is clear that she would not turn them into a statue.
