## summary
### LITE
Drunkard Gorge is a stretch of road in Windwail Highland that separates Dawn Winery from Wolvendom. It used to be a popular place for travelling merchants, but it now has a reputation for danger and most people have left.
### STANDARD +
It is a minor roadside location, known mostly through two open-world NPCs who frequent it.

## people
### STANDARD
Farrah, a fruit vendor, is found in front of the gorge between Springvale and Dawn Winery. He says he used to set up his stall in the canyon, that monsters had recently driven the vendors off, and that a merchant caravan was stuck with no way through; his chief worry is his fruit rotting. Greg wanders the gorge at night. He claims to work at Dawn Winery and to have been given a bottle by Diluc, but he bears a Ravenwing insignia on his bandana and tries to rob drunks, which makes him a Treasure Hoarder in all but admission.
### LARGE +
Greg's pitch is to invite a visitor to share a bottle and walk them toward the winery, offering to hold up anyone who seems tipsy while eyeing their coin pouch; when caught he drops the friendliness and tells a sober person to go away. Farrah is a worried, polite family man who offers a few apples as thanks for sympathy.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Monsters the vendors cannot manage, and opportunistic thieves like Greg after dark. Occasional monsters were once shrugged off as bad luck; now the route is avoided.

## travel
### STANDARD
Lies on the road between Dawn Winery and Wolvendom. It had been used by merchant caravans.

## status
### STANDARD
As of 7.1 no canon change to the gorge is reported. The merchants' flight and the stalled caravan are the last stated situation.

### ULTRA +
The wiki lists the Italian version as Valle del Beone and the Japanese as Suikan-kyou; in the German version it is the Saeuferschlucht. These are translations of the same name, so a character should not treat them as separate places.
