## summary
### LITE
The Pharos Lighthouse stands at Port Ormos in Ardravi Valley, Sumeru. Kaveh's companion dialogue calls it a paradigm of Sumeru's architecture.
### STANDARD +
Architects like Kaveh are responsible for renovating and restoring it so it keeps its form. The wiki notes its name probably alludes to the Lighthouse of Alexandria, also called the Pharos.
### LARGE +
Port officials and a lighthouse guardian are listed as stationed at this location (see people).

## geography
### STANDARD
Located at Port Ormos in Ardravi Valley, in the Dharma Forest.

## people
@version: 3.0
### STANDARD
Araby is listed as the Lighthouse Guardian. Reza is the Port Master and Masrur the Deputy Port Master; Vasco, an adventurer, is also listed there.
### LARGE +
Kaveh's remark comes from his companion voice-over at Friendship Level 7; see his own entry for his background.
