## summary
### LITE
The Chasm of Endless Decay is a cave beneath Kipumaki Cliff in Ashveil Peak, Nod-Krai. Five hundred years ago Solovei, the Torchforger, and Aila, the first Moonchanter of the Frostmoon Scions, imprisoned the Abyssal Scourge of Endless Decay here. In the quest When War Songs Rise, Illuga and his squad return to finish what they began.
### STANDARD +
Its viewpoint is named Dawn Over the Dark Chasm.

## geography
### STANDARD
A cave under Kipumaki Cliff, in the Ashveil Peak area near Cliffwatch Camp. It is the point of interest the quest When War Songs Rise ends at, after the Lightkeepers reactivate the sealing mechanism above ground.
### LARGE +
The sealing mechanism used a series of Verdant Crests around Kipumaki Cliff, which the Traveler and the Nightmare Orioles squad reactivate in follow-up quests (the Focal Point of Ancient Array trio).

## history
@spoiler: major
@version: 6.3
### STANDARD
After Rotwang's first defeat, the Scourge of Endless Decay, an Abyssal entity that had helped him open the false sky using the Pillar of Embla, was sealed beneath Kipumaki Cliff by a sealing array set by Aila and Solovei.
### LARGE +
In When War Songs Rise the Scourge acts as the antagonist. It first appears in the form of a Wilderness Hunter and later as the Lord of the Hidden Depths: Whisperer of Nightmares. The Traveler and Illuga's Nightmare Orioles squad work to reestablish the seal and defeat it. Bjorn is fatally wounded by it during the effort. After the seal is reactivated and the Evil Eye over Kipumaki Cliff destroyed, Illuga pursues it into the chasm.
### ULTRA +
In its lair the Scourge summons illusions from Illuga's past, including Nikita and Olsson, and tries to use his experiences of loss to subdue him. With the squad's help he resists, and they use the Spiralblade to defeat it, cleansing the area of its influence.

## dangers
@version: 6.3
### LARGE
Before its defeat, Abyssal corruption, the Scourge's illusions of the past, and the Wild Hunt nests around Kipumaki Cliff. In its original form the Scourge resembles a much larger Evil Eye of Blightseep with a special core and large hands.

## quests
@version: 6.3
### STANDARD
When War Songs Rise (Nightingale's Song, Darradarljod) starts at Cliffwatch Camp or at the chasm itself.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the Scourge of Endless Decay is dead and the chasm is cleansed.

## entry: Scourge of Endless Decay
@keys: Scourge of Endless Decay, Whisperer of Nightmares, Dawn Over the Dark Chasm
@keys2: Kipumaki Cliff, Illuga
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
A quest-exclusive Abyssal entity allied with Rotwang. It said that its side had failed ten years ago and three years ago and vowed that this time it would not. It was imprisoned beneath Kipumaki Cliff and was killed by the Traveler and Illuga's squad with the Spiralblade.
