## summary
### LITE
The Vault of Qingce is a cavern behind a waterfall north of Qingce Village in Bishui Plain, Liyue. Rumour says it holds the remains of the Chi, and a floating orb-like mechanism glows golden inside. A secret room at the back, with buildings in the style of Qingce Village, is opened during the world quest The Chi of Yore.
### STANDARD +
The secret room holds treasure. After the quest, the Qingce Cache challenge can be started inside the vault.

## lore
### STANDARD
Local legend, told by Yan'er, says the Chi was a dragon-like monster that held the area until Rex Lapis (Morax) defeated it; its body turned to rock, its blood to water and its scales to the terraced fields. Yan'er adds that in older speech the word "Chi" also meant "Qingce". Locals call the unusually large trees nearby Blood-Fed Trees and say they grew on the Chi's blood.
### LARGE +
A piece of incomplete writing says that after the serpent was driven from Qingce, the formula that subdued it ceased to work; its blood congealed into stone and poisoned the world, so three statues were used to hold it down. The Traveler solves puzzles to collect three fragments; Granny Ruoxin explains they are a drawing rather than writing, which leads to the vault. The vault's large amber crystals are said to be the Chi's congealed blood.

## quests
### STANDARD
In The Chi of Yore, the Traveler defends the Qingce Cache against falling rocks and waves of Ruin Guard and Ruin Hunter before reaching the chests, a Geoculus and a Seelie at the room's end.

## rewards
### ULTRA
The quest rewards the Liyue Shrine of Depths Key. Inside the back room are six chests (one Luxurious, one Precious, two Exquisite, two Common), a Geoculus and a Seelie.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the vault stands open after the quest; whether the Chi's remains truly lie below is still not stated.
