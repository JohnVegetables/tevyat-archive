## summary
### LITE
The Eye of Kratti is a small island off Lempo Isle in Nod-Krai, with a crystalline tree growing at its centre. It was once a whirlpool of the same name that became a forbidden zone because of its turbulent waters, rumours of monsters including the Kratti it is named for, and the many people who vanished in it. The wrecked ship Atlas lies here with many others.
### STANDARD +
Its quest, Whisper Beneath the Waves, belongs to the East of the Moon, West of the Sun series and involves ghosts of the Atlas' crew and the tree's roots.

## geography
### STANDARD
A small island with a crystalline tree at its centre; the shipwreck of the Atlas and many others sit around it. A lower map layer, Beneath the Forsaken Sea, is entered by dropping into a pit near the wrecked ship on the eastern side, after breaking a scrap metal pile while in a kuuvahki-empowered state.
### LARGE +
Below ground are caves with unipolar crystonite blocks and blue fields that switch on and off, and at the bottom the "roots" of the tree.

## history
### STANDARD
Before the island there was a whirlpool, the Eye of Kratti, which Ilmari describes as a forbidden zone with monster sightings and many lost souls. A few years before the present the ship Atlas sank near it, along with the Proof of the Cognoscenti that was aboard. Some time later a crystalline tree sprouted from the whirlpool and made a small island. Ilmari says rocks fell from the sky around that time and "little shiny things" began to appear, and a friend of his kept hearing strange singing nearby.
### LARGE +
In Whisper Beneath the Waves the Traveler and Paimon meet the ghosts of the Atlas' fallen crew, follow them to the tree's roots and revive the tree with kuuvahki, so that it shines. Kuuvahki Dewdrops appear near the tree and the lost Proof of the Cognoscenti is found.
### ULTRA +
Libuse says the Frostmoon Scions believe the Atlas was "cursed by something out at sea"; the sources read give no further account of what happened to it.

## people
### STANDARD
Ilmari is the NPC found here and the source of most of the local lore. Ghosts of the Atlas' crew appear in the quest.

## dangers
### LARGE
Caves with unipolar crystonite barriers and fields that switch on and off. Historically, the whirlpool's turbulent waters were the main danger.

## quests
### STANDARD
Whisper Beneath the Waves (a world quest in East of the Moon, West of the Sun) begins here.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the whirlpool is gone and the island with its restored tree stands in its place.

### ULTRA
The Kratti is the monster Ilmari's account links to the whirlpool; its existence is unconfirmed. The wiki's trivia notes that the name echoes Estonian and Finnish folklore, where a kratt/kratti is a servant creature made of household objects or hay. That is an out-of-universe parallel, not a canon fact.
