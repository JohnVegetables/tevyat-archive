## summary
### LITE
"A Very Warm Place" is the name of the spot where Elynas' heart lies. Elynas is the huge dead creature whose remains fill Fontaine's Beryl Region and shelter the Melusines. The heart is why the air there is warm. When the Traveler first reaches it, it lies dormant under a layer of crystallized stone.
### STANDARD +
A viewpoint here is unlocked after finishing the Ancient Colors quest; its text says the heart that was nearly awakened has gone silent again.

## geography
### STANDARD
Part of the Within Elynas: Northern Zone map level of the Beryl Region. The heart is not a freestanding room so much as a part of Elynas' body: crystallized stone covers it, and the warmth it gives off is the notable feature.
### LARGE +
Elynas' remains, according to Jakob, are far larger than Orobashi's and take up almost the whole of the Beryl Region. The Melusines treat the inside of his body as their home.

## history
### STANDARD
In the Ancient Colors quest "Limner, Dreamer, and Robotic Dog", Jakob (by then an Iniquitous Baptist) used Abyssal power to reverse the crystallization briefly, wanting Elynas' blood. The Traveler caused the heart to crystallize again. Elynas, present as a spirit in the form of a Melusine, thanked them: if he were revived, his organs would move and wreck the Melusines' home inside him.
### LARGE +
Elynas is a quest NPC whom the Melusines call "Father"; they came into being unintentionally through him. The wiki credits Rhinedottir with creating him, and says he came to understand that his happiness harmed others and wanted to die so that he could hurt no one. After death he wanted company though he could not speak to people, and spends his afterlife caring for the Melusines. He thought of Jakob as a friend even after Jakob's transformation.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Warm, quiet and a little strange: crystal and stone around a sleeping heart, with small Melusines nearby, in an interior that is also somebody's body.

## status
### STANDARD
As of 7.1 the heart is dormant and crystallized, and the Melusines' home is intact. No later content is shown to have changed this.

## origin
### ULTRA
According to the wiki's account of Elynas' own words, he was once an unknown form drifting in cold, lonely cosmic darkness, often crying for company, before a "Mother" gave him a name. The Breacher Primus species are his indirect creations. These details come from the wiki's summary of the Ancient Colors quest and are not traced to exact dialogue.
