## summary
### LITE
The Guizhong Ballista is a huge ancient crossbow on Mt. Tianheng in Liyue. The God of Dust, Guizhong, designed it with the adepti, Cloud Retainer among them, to defend Liyue against dangerous enemies. It can fire by itself at anything it sees as a threat.

## geography
### STANDARD
It stands among old ruins at Mt. Tianheng in the Sea of Clouds, at the pass that the Guili Assembly once guarded. A supply post nearby holds spare parts. Structural pieces such as the beams and bow limbs are lacquered and folded away in a store room, and the fine parts (trigger, fasteners, fletchings, bowstring) sit in a separate container.
### LARGE +
The machine fires autonomously at anything nearby it perceives as hostile, including Treasure Hoarders who approach the ruins.

## places
### STANDARD
Supply post and maintenance notice: a notice outside the post explains that the Ballista is a work of divine conception made real by mortal craftsmen, and asks any future repairer to treat each part as their own body, in case an ancient evil returns to threaten the land.

## history
### STANDARD
Guizhong first designed the machine under the name Obscuro Vulpes Mechanism, and Rex Lapis judged it superior to Cloud Retainer's own mechanical creation. Guizhong and Cloud Retainer disagreed about ideals, but after Guizhong fell in the Archon War, Cloud Retainer finished the Ballista in her memory. It was built to defend against invaders from Tianheng Pass; the Guili Assembly moved its people north of the mountain.
### LARGE +
The maintenance notice, left for the future, presumes that after centuries of peace someone with the right skill and character will be summoned to repair it, should the ancient evil return.
### ULTRA +
Guizhong's wider interest in machines is recorded in her biography: she apparently collected Khaenri'ahn machinery such as Ruin Machines for research. The Ballista is the work most linked to her name.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
A weathered siege-weapon slumbering over a quiet ruin. The wood is old and lacquered, and the notices left by its makers speak of duty and long peace.

## dangers
### STANDARD
It can fire on intruders by itself. Treasure Hoarders have broken it before in order to reach treasure hidden in the ruins.

## travel
### STANDARD
The site lies on Mt. Tianheng in the Sea of Clouds, Liyue. After the Cocogoat quest the Traveler returns here while looking for a way to reach the Jade Chamber.

## quests
### STANDARD
Pursuing the legendary adeptibeast Cocogoat for Qiqi, the Traveler, Paimon and Zhongli arrive hoping to use the Ballista as a scanner. They find it broken, Zhongli repairs it with gathered maintenance parts, and the activation draws the ire of the Treasure Hoarders led by Freckle Huang, who had damaged it. The hoarders are driven off. Later the Ballista is found mysteriously repaired in a day, and the Millelith guard it while the Qixing investigate until Keqing intervenes in a dispute with Paimon.
### LARGE +
In the Archon Quest, once Tartaglia has unleashed Osial to draw out Morax, Ningguang summons replica Ballistae at the Jade Chamber with Cloud Retainer's modifications. The Fatui attack them and Osial destroys them. Later, after Beidou warns Ningguang of Beisht, the Liyue Qixing build improved ballistae on a small southern island of Guyun Stone Forest, operated by Millelith under Keqing; they hurt Beisht severely and most survive the fight.

## status
### STANDARD
As of 7.1 the original Ballista remains at Mt. Tianheng. The wiki records no change after Beisht's defeat, other than the presumed dismantling of the temporary copies.

## entry: Guizhong Ballista
@keys: Guizhong Ballista, Obscuro Vulpes Mechanism
### STANDARD
The Guizhong Ballista is the ancient autonomous crossbow on Mt. Tianheng, designed by Guizhong with the adepti and finished by Cloud Retainer after Guizhong's death. It exists to defend Liyue from the worst enemies.
### LARGE +
Replica ballistae fought Osial at the Jade Chamber and Beisht at Guyun Stone Forest. The originals' maintenance notice asks for a skilled, devoted craftsman if war returns.
