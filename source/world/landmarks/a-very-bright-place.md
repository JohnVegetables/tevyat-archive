## summary
### LITE
"A Very Bright Place" is a half-submerged cavern inside the southern zone of Elynas, the vast dead beast whose remains shape Fontaine's Beryl Region. Its walls hold many ore veins and it is filled with a congealed orange substance. The young Melusine Puca likes to collect pretty stones here, which is how the world quest "Strange Stone Chronicle" begins.
### STANDARD +
It is unlocked after step 3 of the Ancient Colors world quest "There Will Come Soft Rains", the point where the earthquakes and Rifthound attacks around Merusea Village begin.

## geography
### STANDARD
On the map level "Within Elynas: Southern Zone" of the Beryl Region, the counterpart of "A Lonely Place" in the north. It is a cavern, part submerged, with veins of ore running through the rock and the orange material pooled through it. Puca, who lives in a village close to the ore veins, walks here to pick stones.
### LARGE +
Puca's own words are that the stones here are pretty but not precious, and that she collects them for their looks. Her friend Glaisti says the two of them had visited the stone-picking spots many times without ever meeting the frightening creatures that appeared during the quest.

## people
### STANDARD
Puca, a small Melusine, comes here to pick rocks; her friend Glaisti sometimes accompanies her. Puca says her sister Sluasi runs a gemstone workshop while she herself does not, preferring to collect unusual rocks for their own sake. Chesil, a gem merchant in the Court of Fontaine, buys the ore the Traveler brings out.

## quests
### STANDARD
"Strange Stone Chronicle" (Parts 1 and 2) starts here: the Traveler helps Puca find her lost token beside a strange ore, then takes a sample to the gem merchant Chesil, who is excited by it and pays an advance for more. In Part 2 Puca marks further spots on the map and the Traveler collects thirty pieces of Strange Ore for Chesil.
### LARGE +
Chesil cannot identify the ore. The Traveler may give it a name of their own, or tell him it comes from near Elynas, which he notes is not a place people normally prospect. Puca says she showed the stone to her sister, who saw nothing precious in it; the quest plays on how a trader and a child value the same rock differently.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Rifthounds attacked the Melusines around Merusea Village during "There Will Come Soft Rains". Puca was found trapped in seagrass at an ore spot during that quest and was freed by the Traveler, with Mamere and Seymour present.
### ULTRA +
In that quest the mercenaries Irus, Kaykaus and Kaykubad admit that Jakob had them dig up red stones for half a day, did not pay them, and then, with a hand-sign and words they did not know, called the monsters that chased them. They say he went toward the place where the giant rock hangs. The sources do not say which ore site they mined, so this cavern should not be assumed to be it.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Glowing, damp and hushed: wet stone, ore glinting in the walls and an orange sheen over everything. The brightness is the point of the name, and it contrasts with the hazed, uneasy route through "A Lonely Place".

## status
### STANDARD
As of 7.1 the cavern is open and quiet. The Rifthound disturbances of the 4.0 quests are over.

## entry: Strange Stone Chronicle
@keys: Strange Stone Chronicle, Strange Ore, Puca's Token
@keys2: Puca, Chesil, Merusea Village
### STANDARD
A short chain of world quests in which Puca loses a token near the ore veins and the Traveler finds it fused to an unfamiliar, shiny ore. Chesil, a gem dealer in the Court of Fontaine, cannot place it but sees a fashion in it and arranges a purchase. The quest is light in tone and has no bearing on the larger Elynas story.
