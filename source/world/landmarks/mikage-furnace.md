## summary
### LITE
The Mikage Furnace is the largest smelting facility in all the islands of Inazuma, a huge furnace at the centre of Tatarasuna in Kannazuka, ringed by jagged rock. It mass-produced Jade Steel, the metal of Inazuma-made swords, by drawing on Tatarigami energy. The war between the Shogunate and the Watatsumi Army halted production, and the plant stands sealed behind a containment dome. The Traveler helps restore it in Tatara Tales.
### STANDARD +
The Tatarigami energy around it shows as the hazard called Balethunder. At the start of Tatara Tales a blue dome blocks the area and Balethunder harms anyone inside; the dome is broken open and the Balethunder cleared during that quest series.

## geography
### STANDARD
The furnace sits in the centre of Tatarasuna, an island with a ring of mountainous crags. A chain of floating rock formations with small houses surrounds it, linked by bridges. Torn records found in the lowest house describe an Arsenal Key that was meant to be split into three pieces, one for the Inspector, one for the Armory Officer and one left in Tatarasuna, but was instead hidden in chests in the haste of withdrawal.
### LARGE +
The Mikage Furnace Emergency Containment Dome, set up by Xavier, rises far above the cliffs, so the rim above the furnace cannot be reached while it holds. Three unused Kamuijima Cannons on the eastern side of Tatarasuna are the means of breaking the tears in the dome.

## history
### STANDARD
The furnace was set up about 400 years ago as a smelting plant for Jade Steel, built with the help of experts from Fontaine, and it ran on Tatarigami energy sourced from Yashiori Island. Before the war between the Shogunate and the Watatsumi Army the witch Alice visited Tatarasuna with Kagami Gozen, then leader of the workers, and modified the furnace, improving the dome and capping output at the cost of a smaller possible explosion. During the war, a high saturation of Tatarigami energy shut the furnace down and defeated even its auto-restart. The Fontaine engineer Xavier, who stayed behind, raised the containment dome to hold the Balethunder in.
### LARGE +
The instability set Shogunate officials, who worried about the lost Jade Steel for the war effort, against Grand Narukami Shrine maidens, who felt the Tatarigami's effects as far away as the Sacred Sakura. In Tatara Tales the Fatui, interested in Tatarigami power, breach the dome; the Traveler and Xavier fire the cannons to widen the breaches and drive them off. Xavier then purifies the furnace from within, leaving the Tatarigami thinned to harmlessness.
### ULTRA +
The older tragedy known as the Tatarasuna Mystery happened here about 400 years ago: workers fell sick from a black gas of Tatarigami power, a Fontaine mechanic called Escher turned out to be the Fatui Harbinger Dottore, and the Armory Officer Niwa Hisahide was killed by him. The kabukimono, who later became the Wanderer, went into the furnace core to cleanse it. See the characters involved for their own accounts.

## quests
### STANDARD
Tatara Tales is an eight-part World Quest series in Tatarasuna. The Traveler meets Xavier, the Shogunate's Toranosuke and the shrine maiden Miyuki, learns the cause of the halt, breaks the dome with the three Kamuijima Cannons and finally enters the furnace with Xavier. After the last part, Hanayama Kaoru appears north of Tatarasuna.
### LARGE +
The quest chain ends with the furnace back in operation. Toranosuke is told of the deed, and Xavier warns that the only way left for the Fatui to reach the energy is to fire on the furnace itself, which he thinks unlikely.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Balethunder, the Tatarigami energy that harms visitors while the quest chain is unfinished; Fatui forces around the furnace; and the dome itself.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
A furnace the size of a mountain at the end of a long bridge of rock, silent and unlit, with the memory of both an industry and a disaster hanging over it.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the Mikage Furnace is running again after Tatara Tales.
### STANDARD +
The dome has been breached and the Balethunder is gone from the area. No 6.x or 7.x events involving it were found on the wiki.

### STANDARD
The World Quest series Tatara Tales sends the Traveler to Tatarasuna where Xavier and the Shogunate's Toranosuke disagree with the shrine maiden Miyuki about how to handle the halted furnace. The Fatui have breached the containment dome; the Traveler fires three Kamuijima Cannons to widen the breaches, watches the energy readings and helps Xavier restore the furnace in the final part.

## entry: The Tatarasuna Mystery
@keys: Tatarasuna Mystery, Escher, kabukimono
@keys2: Mikage Furnace, Niwa Hisahide, Tatarasuna
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
About 400 years ago Fontaine's mechanic Escher worked with the Akame Clan to change how the furnace was run, using Crystal Marrow. Black gas, Tatarigami power, began to spread and workers died; Armory Officer Niwa Hisahide ordered an information blackout and suspected the mechanic of sabotage. Escher revealed himself as Dottore and killed Niwa. The kabukimono, later the Wanderer, asked for help at Tenshukaku but found the Shogun already gone into the Plane of Euthymia, and in despair went into the furnace core to cleanse it. When help from Yae Miko finally arrived, almost no one knew what had happened. This is the account in the Tatarasuna page and a character quest; the full story belongs to the Wanderer's own entry.

## entry: Alice's Modifications
@keys: Alice, Kagami Gozen
@keys2: Mikage Furnace, Tatarasuna
### ULTRA
According to the Teyvat Travel Guide, the witch Alice visited Tatarasuna before the war between the Shogunate and the Watatsumi Army, accompanied by Kagami Gozen, then leader of the workers, and took the liberty of modifying the furnace. She improved the containment dome and limited production, at the price of making the potential explosion smaller.
