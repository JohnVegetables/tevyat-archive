## summary
### LITE
The Concealed Land of Enigmas is a small cave hidden behind a waterfall west of Wavey Bay, in the Easybreeze Holiday Resort (also called Tenochtzitoc) on Natlan's western edge. A woven-scroll mystic arts implement at its end opens a portal to a temple that the wiki calls the Pyro Archon Temple. It is part of the quest The Last Survivor of Tenochtzitoc, added in 5.8.
### STANDARD +
The resort around it is a bright holiday area, but the cave is a quiet, forgotten place with something older behind it.

## geography
### STANDARD
Behind the waterfall is a rock pile; breaking it reveals a short cave whose end holds the old implement, a woven scroll. The tourist beaches and attractions of Wavey Bay lie just east of it. Easybreeze Holiday Resort is a sparsely inhabited island off the westernmost edge of Natlan, and its Tenochtzitoc name is said by a local to mean a 'cavern of stone and prickly pears', though prickly pears no longer grow there.
### LARGE +
Through the portal, a temple with seven stone seats around a central structure opens out: each seat carries an inscription honouring a hero of the lost settlement of Tenochtzitoc (Tupac, Menilek, Sundjatta, Tenoch, Wanjiru, Sanhaj Kompore and Burkina). Stone tablets nearby carry fragments of warnings, including one signed Shulevitz that speaks of cloaking the Enigmatic Land and shrouding the palace so only the legacy of heroes can awaken it. Other tablets and a booklet, signed Gloharry and Cochanina, refer to blocking the Mare Jivari and holding back a Black Tide, and a more recent notebook signed Isaja records a task completed.

## history
### STANDARD
Citlali reasons that the temple was built about five hundred years ago, by the Pyro Archons of that time, to temporarily contain Bakunawa, the Abyssal monster that devoured whole villages. The cave's implement was too old to work: its spirit had long dissipated, and Citlali said every caster is a different lock needing its own key. In the quest it was revived when the Asha, benevolent souls of the Mare Jivari, were drawn to the relic of Sanhaj that the Traveler and Citlali carried.
### LARGE +
Enjou of the Abyss Order, who had been trailing them, arrives in the temple and says he had hoped the woven scroll would take him to the Mare Jivari. Beyond the temple's central seat the quest continues in the Mare Jivari itself, where the Traveler fights a piece of Bakunawa using the Calamity of Eshu, Sanhaj's ritual sword.
### ULTRA +
Inside the Mare Jivari Enjou tells a story, which he admits is partly a distraction, about Tenochtzitoc: a lost settlement of Anemo-wielding warriors, exiled after the old kingdom's civil war, whose last defender was Tenoch; the place where their bones lie, he says, is called the 'Windless Land'. He adds that relatives who had left earlier for the 'true land of wind' became the Muratans. The quest does not confirm his account, and he used the telling to push Citlali's presence out of his body.

## people
### LARGE
Citlali, the Masters of the Night-Wind's elder, and Enjou of the Abyss Order both appear here in the quest, which also concerns Pucli and Papar. See their own entries.

## quests
### STANDARD
The Last Survivor of Tenochtzitoc (World Quest, 5.8) leads here via Enjou's redrawn map. It is a prerequisite that A Curtain Call for a Colorful Summer Night! be completed before it starts. After the quest, the player can return to touch the implement; the temple's central stone then leads to the Night Kingdom and the Mare Jivari.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
After the cheerful noise of the resort the cave is silent: water curtain, damp stone, and an old unsettling hush. On the far side of the portal the light is strange and the Wayob cannot be sensed.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Little in the cave itself. Beyond the temple, in the Mare Jivari, the quest fights a fragment of Bakunawa, whose power Enjou says can corrupt even the passage of time.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the cave and portal are open to visitors; the Pyro Archon Temple can be reached again at will. No new development in 6.x or 7.0-7.1.

## entry: Pyro Archon Temple
@keys: Pyro Archon Temple, temple behind the waterfall, Seven Seats of Tenochtzitoc
@spoiler: minor
@version: 5.8
### STANDARD
The 'Pyro Archon Temple' is the wiki's unofficial name for the sealed temple behind the Concealed Land's portal. It contains seven stone seats honouring heroes, a waterfall with tablets at its back, and two side rooms with a wind-worn booklet and a notebook. According to Citlali it was built about five hundred years ago to contain Bakunawa, and the heroes' legacy 'awakens' it. Its central structure leads to the Night Kingdom and, from there, to the Mare Jivari.
### LARGE +
Notes there speak of sealing the borders and containing the Mare Jivari as a task for more than one generation, and one cheerful line reads 'Task complete. Now I sing!'.

## entry: Bakunawa and Tenoch's sacrifice
@keys: Bakunawa, Tenoch, Sanhaj Kompore, Black Tide
@spoiler: major
@version: 5.8
### LARGE
Per Citlali, Bakunawa was the most terrifying Abyssal monster to attack Natlan five hundred years ago, regrowing from any remains. It was destroyed by Tenoch's sacrifice: Sanhaj Kompore engraved border spells on his body, Tenoch let himself be swallowed, and Sanhaj summoned a miniature Night Kingdom inside the monster. The explosion shattered it, though some believe it never truly died.
