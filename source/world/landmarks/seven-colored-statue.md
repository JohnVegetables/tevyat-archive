## summary
### LITE
The Seven-Colored Statue is a large statue in the Easybreeze Market of Natlan's Easybreeze Holiday Resort. It resembles an octopus spouting seven bubbles.
### STANDARD +
The map description suggests that covering the statue in colour earns a reward from the resort. It was added in version 5.8.

## places
@version: 5.8
### STANDARD
The statue stands on the streets of the Easybreeze Market. Its seven bubbles can each be painted a different colour, and the octopus has an eye that serves as the target.
### LARGE +
A resident named Irqichaykuna Nui is associated with the spot on the wiki's NPC list; her role there is not described.

## gameplay
@version: 5.8
### LARGE
Game mechanics: the player throws coloured paintballs, using the Asha, at the octopus's eye to colour the bubbles. Colouring 3, 5 or 7 bubbles gives progressively better chests; the full seven also gives a furnishing blueprint for a market statue and an achievement. Only four of the seven paint colours are available at first, and the rest unlock through resort quests and objectives.
### ULTRA +
This is a minigame with no story content of its own, so treat it as playful resort entertainment in a scene.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Bright, playful market street, splashes of colour and a whimsical octopus watching shoppers; a holiday feeling.
