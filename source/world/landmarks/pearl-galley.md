## summary
### LITE
The Pearl Galley is a luxurious ship anchored near Liyue Harbor in the Sea of Clouds, meant for the exclusive enjoyment of specially invited guests. Its receptionist is Luoxia and its proprietor is Rouran.
### STANDARD +
Guests are expected to come by invitation; the Traveler's first boarding without one is handled gently by Luoxia. Once The Ocean Pearl is done, the ferryman Pinghai can take the Traveler to and from the ship.

## people
### STANDARD
Aboard are the receptionist Luoxia, the poet Li the Ninth, Cui'er, Guan Hong, Luo Feng, Maksim and Qiu'ge, with Uncle Zhao as daytime security. Pinghai ferries visitors from Liyue Harbor.
### LARGE +
The Ocean Pearl quest has the Traveler gather an invitation letter from guests, deliver letters between Cui'er and Chaoxi, and bring Qiu'ge three Qingxin flowers, before Luoxia and Pinghai return them to the harbor.

## culture
### STANDARD
A scroll on one table, the Connoisseur Catalog, offers the ship's "wares of beauty" in poetic language and lists the names of the "flowers" of the painted boat. The wiki reads these as the names of hostesses who can be hired to keep guests company. The catalogue's line about the beauty of water lying in the pearls it holds echoes a classical Chinese essay on literature.

## naming
### ULTRA
The ship's Chinese name, Zhudian-fang, is rendered on the wiki as "Pearl-Inlaid Boat".

## quests
### STANDARD
Boarding for the first time triggers the world quest The Ocean Pearl. The ship is also the starting point of Zhongli's story quest Shadow of Yore, the first part of Sal Flore, where Zhongli is discussing Rex Lapis and the first Mora with guests at a table and the Traveler meets Wanyan at the stern. If The Ocean Pearl is not yet complete, it starts first and both quests can proceed together.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the Pearl Galley remains moored near Liyue Harbor.
