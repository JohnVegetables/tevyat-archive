## summary
### LITE
The Idol of the New Moon is a large statue on Hiisi Island, in the Light-Bathed Platform area, depicting the Moon Maiden Kuutar. According to the quests Where the Moon Rises and Hidden in the Shadows, it was carved five hundred years ago by Aila, the Moonchanter, from ore rich in kuuvahki.
### STANDARD +
Eveliina describes it as showing Lady Kuutar's original form. During The Homebound Moon the modern Frostmoon Scions add a pair of wings to the statue to represent their goddess's newfound freedom and autonomy.

## geography
### STANDARD
It stands on Hiisi Island's Light-Bathed Platform. It is distinct from the many smaller Statues of the New Moon that unlock the map; this large idol exists only on Hiisi Island.

## history
@version: 6.3
### STANDARD
Aila carved it five hundred years ago from kuuvahki-rich ore, and Eveliina says it shows Lady Kuutar's original form.
### LARGE +
In The Homebound Moon (part of the Silvermoon Hall: Selenic Chronicles), the Traveler meets Columbina in Silvermoon Hall, then Lauma, reports back to Columbina, and the quest's last step is to head to the statue of Kuutar. Afterwards the statue appears with wings.

## culture
### LARGE
For the Frostmoon Scions, the statue stands for the Moon Maiden whom they serve, and the added wings are a deliberate sign that she is free to choose her own path.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
A large idol on the Light-Bathed Platform, bathed in light, with wings in its present form.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the idol stands with wings, if The Homebound Moon has been completed.
