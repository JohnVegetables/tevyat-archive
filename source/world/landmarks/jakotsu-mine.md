## summary
### LITE
Jakotsu Mine is a mining area on Yashiori Island, a stormy island of Inazuma where the remains of the slain serpent god Orobashi lie. Once home to miners and their families, it was abandoned after the Tatarigami began sickening the island's people. Today it is a rain-lashed, lightning-struck site containing a broken ward, the bandit-held Formation Estate and the empty house of a boy named Chouji.
### STANDARD +
The island's constant thunderstorms continue over the mine until the quest Orobashi's Legacy: Part III repairs its ward. Chouji's house is the place where leaflets from both the Shogunate and the Watatsumi resistance still hang on a fence.

## geography
### STANDARD
Jakotsu Mine lies on Yashiori Island next to the Serpent's Head. A Narukami Pillar and a Sacred Stone Mound stand on its southern cliff, near where Chouji first appears. A cave to one side of the ward contains a Thunder Barrier and a Thunder Sakura Bough; a path on the other side leads through slimes and Abyss mages. Chouji's House and the Formation Estate domain entrance are here. Local specialties are Crystal Marrow, Naku Weed and Onikabuto.
### LARGE +
The area has Electro Elemental Monument, Lightning Strike Probe, Narukami Pillars and Seelies. Common enemies are Kairagi: Dancing Thunder, Nobushi: Hitsukeban and Hydro Slimes. One spot is a location for Kid Kujirai's Temari challenge.

## history
### STANDARD
Orobashi's remains and its lingering hatred, the Tatarigami, made Yashiori Island unlivable. The Raiden Shogun's wards held the Tatarigami back until a radical faction of the Watatsumi Army destroyed them, after which the miners and villagers fell ill or were driven mad. A notice found at Chouji's house orders miners and families to evacuate in an orderly way because the Tatarigami and the Sangonomiya rebels had both reached the island, and promises that the Tenryou Commission under Kujou Takayuki will escort them away.
### LARGE +
Leaflets near the house show both sides of the war: the Shogunate offers help at the Kujou troops' forward base, while a resistance leaflet addresses the island's people as fellow victims of the Vision Hunt Decree and asks why the "Ogosho" never answers questions in person. A co-worker's letter to an "Elder Sister" says the writer found no one, only a broken pick, and urges her to rest since Chouji is still a kid and she has taken a job in the mine.
### ULTRA +
The Formation Estate was once a samurai residence belonging, per its description and the Chinese name of its domain, to Akuou, Orobashi's lieutenant, until a flash of lightning split the god, its body and the sky. It is now occupied by bandits. Chouji's mother had been affected by the Tatarigami with fever and bleeding, left for Sumeru with a crew of pirates, and wrote to the pirate Onitaka to look after her son; her Unsealed Letter was opened by someone unknown and left in the mine.

## quests
### STANDARD
Orobashi's Legacy: Part III has the Traveler find the Jakotsu Mine ward, solve its Sacred Stone Puzzle, and retrieve a Rock Pillar Warding Stone and a Rock Pillar Pearl from either side of it before returning them. The quest clears the Tatarigami over the mine and unlocks the Formation Estate domain, whose trial is called Tutelage: King of Thieves.
### LARGE +
Chouji's story quest Gazing Three Thousand Miles Away starts nearby. He is a quest NPC who lives alone and saves Mora from selling Crystal Marrow to leave the island and search for his parents.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Permanent rain and lightning until the ward is repaired, which makes everything Wet and can trigger Electro-Charged; Tatarigami influence; Nobushi, Kairagi and Abyss mages; the bandits of the Formation Estate.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Empty mine shafts and miners' houses in the storm, notices tacked to fences still telling people to evacuate, and a sense of a community that left in a hurry.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 Jakotsu Mine is deserted. The storms stop once its ward is repaired in Orobashi's Legacy: Part III.
### STANDARD +
No 6.x or 7.x content on the wiki involves the mine.

### STANDARD
The Formation Estate is a one-time domain entrance in Jakotsu Mine. The description says it once belonged to a lieutenant of Orobashi, until he, the god's body and the sky were rent open by a flash of lightning; later it became a hideout of bandits. It unlocks after Orobashi's Legacy: Part III and clearing the Seelie puzzle; it holds enemies such as Kairagi, Nobushi and Treasure Hoarders.
