## summary
### LITE
The Big Bad Battle Base is an encampment on Lempo Isle in Nod-Krai that the young inventor Aino set up to run the Clink Clank Clash, a limited-time event of version 6.0. It was the working base for a string of odd jobs: testing a self-defence gadget, chasing runaway recon robots and tracking down lost experimental machines. Thin canon: the wiki gives little more than its map description and a list of three clients.
### STANDARD +
The wiki lists three people tied to the base: Sarin of the Adventurers' Guild, the Fontainian engineer Ginette, and Gaudenty, who describes himself as a representative of the Kuuvahki Experimental Design Bureau. Each commissioned Aino, and the Traveler and Paimon helped carry out the orders.

## geography
### STANDARD
On Lempo Isle, in the Southern Isles of Nod-Krai, filed by the wiki under the Clink-Clank Krumkake Craftshop subarea. The map description calls it an encampment Aino created for the purposes of conducting the Clink Clank Clash. The event quests all start at the nearby Northern Training Ground, but no source states that the two places are the same.
### LARGE +
Lempo Isle is also home to Nasha Town and the Krumkake Craftshop, Aino's workshop, which grew from a tiny workspace into a renowned machine shop. The surrounding country is kuuvahki-rich, which matters because several of the event's problems come from machines built elsewhere failing to cope with it.

## people
### STANDARD
Aino runs the show; Ineffa, her creation, is caught up in the crisis alongside her. Clients: Sarin (Branch Master of the Adventurers' Guild in Nod-Krai) wanted a self-defence device any adventurer could carry; Gaudenty wanted stray recon bots retrieved or destroyed; Ginette wanted help debugging and then finding robots that communicate over distance using kuuvahki.
### LARGE +
Sarin admits his own design failed and that he lacks the talent to fix it. Aino remarks that the design uses technology from elsewhere and cannot adapt to kuuvahki-rich surroundings. Gaudenty is brusque and forgets nothing about his terms: payment is in recon-bot components rather than Mora, and he says he does not take retractions. Ginette, embarrassed, adds a second request after losing several test robots while trying different kuuvahki concentrations.
### ULTRA +
Wiki profiles add background without much detail: Gaudenty is described as a former Adventurers' Guild member and treasure hunter with a record of misconduct (from Nefer's intel, not from the event quests); Sarin is also an informant of the Northern Intelligence Network; Ginette works at the Fontaine Research Institute and says she came to Nod-Krai to see the new kuuvahki-based energy source.

## history
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
Created for the Clink Clank Clash, a Flagship Event of version 6.0 (Luna I) that ran from 2025-09-12 to 2025-09-29 by the wiki's dating.
### LARGE +
The event's four gameplay modes were: chasing and shooting down rogue recon bots along Moonlanes with the help of kuuhenki; a self-defence-device combat test across Nod-Krai; a search for malfunctioning robots using kuuvahki remnants; and a stealth mission with the robot Zip-zap Spy-bot Number 1. Progress was measured in Legion Morale.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
A workshop-on-location feel: gadgets, prototypes and clients with half-finished problems. Aino is cheerful and brisk with them, and in the quests she has to ask more than once which order belongs to whom.

## quests
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
Three event quests supplied the work. An Adventurer's Woes (Sarin) unlocked Boom! Blast! Power Test!, a combat trial of the self-defence device; A Mysterious Outsourced Contract (Gaudenty) unlocked the recon-bot chase; Misplaced Creativity (Ginette) unlocked the search for her lost robots, which continues through The Last Step of Testing.
### LARGE +
In Boom! Blast! Power Test!, the Traveler moves between challenge areas defeating waves of enemies while a self-defence device, charged by kuuvahki energy, emits shockwaves. Its routes were named Villain's Row, Elemental Storm and Heavy Metal. The event's weapon reward was the Etherlight Spindlelute, a Catalyst.

## status
### STANDARD
The event is over. Nothing after 7.1 is recorded here, and the pages read do not say whether the base remains visitable.

## entry: Clink Clank Clash
@keys: Clink Clank Clash, Big Bad Battle Base
@version: 6.0
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
The Clink Clank Clash was a version 6.0 Flagship Event set in Nod-Krai in which the Traveler and Paimon help Aino and Ineffa with a run of commissions: a self-defence gadget for the Adventurers' Guild, runaway recon robots for a Design Bureau representative, and a Fontainian engineer's lost kuuvahki robots. The Big Bad Battle Base is the encampment Aino set up for it.
### LARGE +
It ran 2025-09-12 to 2025-09-29 per the wiki. Requirements were Adventure Rank 20 and the Prologue Act III Archon Quest, with Song of the Welkin Moon Act II recommended for the best experience.

## entry: Aino
@keys: Aino
@keys2: Clink Clank Clash, Big Bad Battle Base, Krumkake Craftshop
### STANDARD
Aino is the child-prodigy inventor who founded the Clink-Clank Krumkake Craftshop and built Ineffa. She treats her creations as family. She accepted the three commissions behind the Clink Clank Clash and asked for no extra pay for Ginette's order because it sounded fun. Her own character entry holds the full detail.
