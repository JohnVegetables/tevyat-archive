## summary
### LITE
Yilong Dais is a lookout in Chenyu Vale: Upper Vale, Liyue, where visitors light Crane Crown Incense for good luck so their wishes might come true. The tea-stall owner Pingshan tends it, and the tourist Wanjing is met nearby.
### STANDARD +
A stone hanging over the cliff resembles a crane's head, and the censer sits on it. Pingshan says the adepti will hear a wish made there. Because many guests lose their nerve looking down, a proxy service exists: for about the price of two cups of tea, Pingshan lights the incense for the visitor.

## people
### STANDARD
Pingshan owns the tea stall and cleans the dais every day, having taken the job over from a master almost twenty years ago. Pingshan laments that crowds are smaller than they used to be and plans to recruit an apprentice and have writers promote the dais. Wanjing, a traveller short of Mora and afraid of heights, treats it as the last stop of a sightseeing trip.
### LARGE +
Asked whether Chenyu Vale's people truly believe in the Crane Adepti, Pingshan repeats the master's view that any adepti who protect the people are good adepti, be they Carp, Crane or Deer, and calls them legends nobody can prove or disprove.

## visitors
### ULTRA
Wanjing says nearby Qiaoying Village and Yilong Wharf are cheap for food and lodging, and that the Upper Vale's famous sights are what drew the trip. The Chinese name is Yilong-tai.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 it remains a small pilgrimage and sightseeing spot in the Upper Vale.
