## summary
### LITE
The Weeping Willow of the Lake is a large willow growing at the centre of a lake in Erinnyes Forest, its roots apparently reaching under the water. Fontaine's lore treats it as a barometer: it flourishes or withers as the water veins run sweet or bitter.
### STANDARD +
In ancient times it was an object of reverence. The viewpoint text laments that omens arrive only in the same month as calamity.

## geography
### STANDARD
A lakeside subarea in Erinnyes Forest. Before the Roots of Erinnyes are cleansed in "The Wild Fairy of Erinnyes", the whole lake area looks a sickly yellow and the willow is tainted; afterwards it is a vibrant blue. The map reflects the change.
### LARGE +
All water veins converge on Erinnyes, and the tree's condition is tied to the veins' health. Its viewpoint is unlocked after completing "Within the Depths of Erinnyes", and its Archive entry stays hidden until collected.

## culture
### STANDARD
The viewpoint calls it revered in ancient times, tying the willow's flourishing or withering to the water being sweet or bitter and treating it as an omen, though one that comes too late.

## status
### STANDARD
As of 7.1 the lake and willow are healthy after the cleansing of the Roots of Erinnyes in 4.2.

## naming
### ULTRA
The Chinese name means roughly "weeping willow in the lake", the French is "Saule pleureur du lac", and the viewpoint title is "Erinnyes's Watery Weeping Willow". The lake's colour on the map changes with the cleansing quest, from yellow to blue.
