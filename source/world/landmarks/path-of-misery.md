## summary
### LITE
The Path of Misery is a large winding cavern under Kipumaki Cliff in Ashveil Peak, in far northern Nod-Krai. It funnels down into the Chasm of Endless Decay, the pit where the Abyssal Scourge of Endless Decay was sealed five hundred years ago and where it was finally destroyed in the world quest When War Songs Rise.
### STANDARD +
The wiki's map shows two levels, Beneath Kipumaki and the Path of Misery itself, and the cavern shares its music with the Chasm. It is thin canon: most of what is known about the place comes from the Chasm, the cliff above, and the quest that ends there.

## geography
### STANDARD
Beneath Kipumaki Cliff, in Ashveil Peak, the northernmost part of Pohjola. It is a winding cave that narrows and descends into the Chasm of Endless Decay. Kipumaki Cliff above it is shrouded in dark clouds and subject to Abyssal Decay, a climate effect that drains health from those in it unless they shelter near Oath Lanterns.
### LARGE +
The wiki lists Wilderness Exiles, Wilderness Hunters and Fishers of Hidden Depths as elite enemies of Ashveil Peak, and Lord of the Hidden Depths: Whisperer of Nightmares as the area's boss. Lightkeepers from the Cliffwatch Camp appear as allied non-player units in the area.

## history
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Five hundred years ago, the Torchforger Solovei and the first Moonchanter of the Frostmoon Scions, Aila, with the help of Alia, subdued the Scourge of Endless Decay and sealed it beneath Kipumaki Cliff with a sealing array. The area afterwards became a hotspot of Wild Hunt activity, and the Lightkeepers set up Cliffwatch Camp to watch it.
### LARGE +
Three years before the present, a surge of Wild Hunt activity caused a catastrophe with many deaths among Lightkeepers and civilians; Cliffwatch Camp was rebuilt afterwards. In When War Songs Rise the Scourge (an Abyssal entity that had helped Rotwang try to open the False Sky with the Pillar of Embla) boasts that it failed ten years ago and again three years ago.
### ULTRA +
The wiki says the Scourge is Abyssal and assisted Rotwang's goal of opening the False Sky to the Abyss. After Rotwang's defeat it was sealed. It speaks of the Professor ascending his tower and opening the path to the Elysium beyond, a thread that belongs to the wider Nightingale's Song story.

## dangers
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Wild Hunt creatures and Abyssal enemies fill the area, and Abyssal Decay accumulates steadily near the Chasm; in the quest the Traveler cannot reduce it for a time and recovers health by dispelling Abyssal shadows. Illusions of fallen Lightkeepers appear in the fog.
### LARGE +
The shadows speak with the voices of people Illuga has lost, including Captain Olsson and Nikita, urging him to run or to give up. They are Abyssal tricks, not the people themselves.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Fog, creeping dark and a sense of being watched. In the quest the air grows thicker as Illuga approaches, and the fallen seem to speak from the fog as illusions. The area's themes on the wiki are The Forlorn Trek of Suffering and The Soul-Devouring Nightmare.

## travel
### STANDARD
Reached from Kipumaki Cliff. Completing When War Songs Rise unlocks a teleport waypoint at the Chasm of Endless Decay.

## quests
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
When War Songs Rise (World Quest, Nightingale's Song, Act II: Darradarljod) sees the Traveler help Illuga and the Nightmare Orioles rebuild the sealing barrier at Kipumaki Cliff and fight off the Scourge, then Illuga goes alone after it into its lair, with the Traveler and his squad joining him. Using the Spiralblade they kill the Scourge and cleanse the area of its influence.
### LARGE +
Bjorn is fatally wounded by the Scourge earlier in the quest series, which is part of what Illuga relives in the illusions. Illuga says the Spiralblade, like the Silver Lampwick, is a relic of extraordinary power that fell into the underground ruins.

## status
### STANDARD
As of 7.1 the Scourge of Endless Decay is destroyed and the Chasm and its approach are explorable. The wiki notes the area is cleansed of its influence. Nothing after 7.1 is recorded.

## entry: Scourge of Endless Decay
@keys: Scourge of Endless Decay
@keys2: Kipumaki Cliff, Chasm of Endless Decay, Illuga
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
The Scourge of Endless Decay is an Abyssal entity that appears as a Wilderness Hunter and later as the Lord of the Hidden Depths: Whisperer of Nightmares, in a larger form resembling an Evil Eye of Blightseep. It plays on its victims' guilt, summoning illusions from Illuga's past to break him, and is destroyed in the Chasm by the Traveler and Illuga's squad with the Spiralblade.
