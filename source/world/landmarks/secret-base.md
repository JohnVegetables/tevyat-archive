## summary
### LITE
The Secret Base is a well-hidden underground meeting place on Lempo Isle in Nod-Krai. Grand Master Varka describes it as the Knights of Favonius's meeting place in Nod-Krai and a contact hub for the Northern Intelligence Network, the continent's black market of information. Only story quests open its interior: the allies of Song of the Welkin Moon gather there to plan against the Wild Hunt and its master, Rerir.
### STANDARD +
Varka brings the party there after rescuing them from a Wild Hunt ambush in Hidden in the Shadows. Nefer, the Network's representative, turns up there because a letter from a 'humble knight of Favonius' asked her to a meeting about a business deal; she knows where it is because she runs the arrangement. The base later hosts several visitors, including Arlecchino, Albedo, Durin and Dainsleif.

## geography
### STANDARD
Underground on Lempo Isle, somewhere reached through the open world but not enterable outside quests. The interior is a hidden gathering space where the group sits and talks; Varka says it is secure, and Paimon remarks how well hidden it is.
### LARGE +
The wiki shows an exterior, an interior and a desk area. In Everlasting As the Moon, leaving a quest mode early teleports the player back to the base.

## people
### STANDARD
Varka, Grand Master of the Knights of Favonius, uses it as his meeting place. In Hidden in the Shadows Aino, Ineffa, Flins, Jahoda, Lauma, Columbina (as the Damselette), Paimon and the Traveler gather there against the Wild Hunt; later scenes feature Varka, Jahoda, Lauma and others returning from their missions. Nefer, who runs the Northern Intelligence Network, knows of it and is paid for helping. Later visitors are the Fourth Harbinger Arlecchino, Albedo, Durin and Dainsleif.
### LARGE +
Varka says the Knights rely on the Network as the most effective way to get reliable local intelligence as an expeditionary force on foreign soil, and that they meet there because it is secure. Nefer says Varka could not have settled in Nod-Krai without her help.
### ULTRA +
Jahoda, who works for Nefer, shares the Network's intelligence at the base in Everlasting As the Moon: Rerir was once a Khaenri'ahn secret agent hunting the Dregs of the Crimson Moon, and had a would-be fiancee named Tholindis. Lauma and Jahoda react to Arlecchino's presence with wariness; Arlecchino says she comes in a personal capacity and not as a Fatui representative.

## history
@version: 6.1
### STANDARD
Introduced in version 6.1 with Song of the Welkin Moon Act III. The wiki gives no origin for the place beyond Varka's description. The Knights' expedition had been working in Nod-Krai well before the Traveler arrived, and Varka says the Network has been its best source of local intelligence.

## quests
@version: 6.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Hidden in the Shadows (Archon Quest, Act III, part 1): after being surrounded by the Wild Hunt, the Traveler's party is led by Varka to the secret base, where Nefer unexpectedly arrives, agrees to help Jahoda for this once, and Varka lays out three priorities: a way to beat Rerir, finding out his end goal, and stalling him while the others act; the group splits into three teams. Everlasting As the Moon (part 3): the party returns to share findings and meets Arlecchino. All Is Foretold (Act IV, part 1): Albedo and Durin, and then Dainsleif, join the planning.
### LARGE +
Columbina suggests seeking the Iridescent Moon's marrow to restore her strength, which requires speaking to Sandrone at the Experimental Design Bureau; Varka also puzzles over the unknown ally who led the group to key files and shielded Hiisi Island, and the group cannot identify them.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
A secure room for conspiring: friendly banter, hard strategy, and a mood that tightens with each new arrival. In All Is Foretold the room fills so much that Varka jokes about running out of chairs and Albedo offers to make more.

## status
### STANDARD
As of 7.1 the base remains the Knights' hidden meeting place in Nod-Krai and is enterable only in the three quests above. Nothing newer is recorded.

## entry: Northern Intelligence Network
@keys: Northern Intelligence Network
@keys2: Nefer, Secret Base, Curatorium of Secrets, Nod-Krai
### STANDARD
The Northern Intelligence Network is an underground information market run by Nefer from the Curatorium of Secrets in Nod-Krai, with contacts in every nation. Varka calls it the black market of intel; the Knights use it because it is reliable. Members and informants include Jahoda, Sarin and Diluc, and clients include Varka.
### LARGE +
The wiki describes it as a loose, volunteer organisation whose members sometimes give up their names. It reportedly has spies in every region except Liyue.

## entry: Varka's meeting place
@keys: Secret Base, secret base, Knights' meeting place
@keys2: Varka, Nefer, Nod-Krai
### STANDARD
Varka holds war councils at the secret base, deciding how to defeat Rerir and the Wild Hunt. He tells the party that the Knights, as an expeditionary force, need the Network to learn what is happening locally.
