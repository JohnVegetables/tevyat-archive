## summary
### LITE
The Frostbearing Tree is a new sprout of an ancient Irminsul tree that the Skyfrost Nail destroyed. It was revived by the blood of the Abyssal dragon Durin and grows in Dragonspine, marked with red rather than the usual blue.
### STANDARD +
The Traveler frees it from a shell of Ancient Rime in the world quest "In the Mountains". Offering Crimson Agates then makes it grow, and at full strength it bears a single fruit, the Frostbearer catalyst.

## history
@version: 1.2
### STANDARD
The tree was once an Irminsul tree revered by the ancient civilisation of Sal Vindagnyr, and the kingdom's last princess is said to have been born beneath it. About six thousand years ago Celestia drove the Skyfrost Nail into Sal Vindagnyr, burying the mountain in ice and snow, and the tree's trunk and canopy shattered and froze. The Crimson Agate's archive text says the trunk and canopy broke into many fragments that were then frozen.
### LARGE +
The princess saved one branch and tried to graft it onto another plant on a small island, but the attempt failed and she is implied to have died. About 500 years ago Dvalin killed Durin over Dragonspine, and his blood seeped into the mountain's ley lines; this revived the tree, which is described as having a "thirst for more power" and seems tainted by the Abyss.
### ULTRA +
Later the tree was sealed in Ancient Rime. In "In the Mountains" the Traveler thaws and breaks that shell and nourishes the tree with more of Durin's blood, in the form of Crimson Agate. The tree then uses "all its might" to bear the Frostbearer.

## gameplay
@version: 1.2
### STANDARD
Game mechanics, not story: Crimson Agates scattered around Dragonspine can be offered to the tree to raise its Gratitude level and earn rewards. At the maximum level crystalline crimson butterflies appear on its branches.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
A great old tree glowing with scarlet markings against the snow, at once hopeful and unsettling because of the Abyss-tainted blood that revived it.

## entry: Crimson Agate
@keys: Crimson Agate, Crimson Agates
@version: 1.2
### STANDARD
Crimson Agate is a crystal formed from concentrated life force and an energy unique to Dragonspine, found across the mountain. It is the ideal nutrient for the Frostbearing Tree.

## entry: Princess of Sal Vindagnyr and the tree
@keys: Sal Vindagnyr princess, last princess of Sal Vindagnyr
@keys2: Frostbearing Tree, Irminsul
@version: 1.2
### STANDARD
The last princess of Sal Vindagnyr is said to have been born beneath the Irminsul tree that became the Frostbearing Tree. She saved a branch after the Skyfrost Nail fell and tried to graft it elsewhere; the attempt failed and she is implied to have died.
