## summary
### LITE
Apep's Resort is a large, mysterious cavern in the far north of the Desert of Hadramaveth, under the Great Red Sand in Sumeru. A glowing tunnel inside it is the entrance to the Realm of Beginnings, the domain tied to the Dendro Dragon Sovereign Apep.
### STANDARD +
In-game viewpoint text says a friend of King Deshret once built a court in a great cavern beneath the dunes, with Wenut as court officials and flying serpents as companions, and that both creatures have since lost their wit and vigour. The wiki links that friend to Apep.
### LARGE +
The place matters mainly because of the dragon it is tied to. Apep once ruled Sumeru and holds a world of primordial elemental lifeforms inside herself; the 3.6 Archon Quest ended her long suffering from forbidden knowledge.

## geography
### STANDARD
A great cavern in the northernmost part of the Desert of Hadramaveth, in the Great Red Sand region. The desert above is the result of a Celestial Nail that fell long ago and turned lush forest to sand. The cavern's glowing tunnel opens onto the Realm of Beginnings.
### LARGE +
The map lists it as a point of interest and viewpoint, titled The Lost Hermitage. The Chinese, Japanese and Korean names read roughly as Apep's royal villa garden, matching the court the viewpoint text describes.

## people
@version: 3.6
### STANDARD
No living community is established here. The viewpoint text recalls Wenut as courtiers and flying serpents as companions in an earlier age. Elsewhere in the 3.x desert story, Liloupar, a jinni from King Deshret's era, calls the Wenut the children of Apep.
### LARGE +
Apep's full body is never shown, only a silhouette in her inner oasis. After the 3.6 quest she is resting and observing the world, and asked not to be disturbed unless it is absolutely necessary.

## history
@version: 3.6
### STANDARD
Apep ruled Sumeru before the Heavenly Principles arrived and bore the first elemental lifeforms inside herself. She survived the war against the Heavenly Principles and the desertification of Sumeru. She later agreed that King Deshret could build his civilisation in her domain, in return for the knowledge and elemental power he accumulated after his death.
### LARGE +
Absorbing Deshret's power also spread his forbidden-knowledge contamination to Apep, who spent thousands of years enduring the pain. The elemental lifeforms inside her mutated or fled; those who fled became fungi and eventually Tumors of the Withering. Greater Lord Rukkhadevata promised them salvation and helped make the Fire Seed. In the Archon Quest, Nahida and the Traveler used it, together with the creatures' own sacrifice, to restore the Heart of Oasis. Apep calmed, set aside thoughts of revenge for now, and chose to observe.
### ULTRA +
Later, when Nahida fell ill after Dottore warped the Irminsul, Apep reluctantly gave some of her life force to revive her, then flew her to the Mausoleum of King Deshret and warned that she would deal with Nahida personally if the changes caused her suffering. Apep also believed the Hydro Dragon would plot revenge, unaware of his friendly relation with the Traveler.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
A hushed, hidden cavern that suggests a grand court of a vanished age whose servants no longer remember its glory, with a glowing tunnel as the sign of the life still stirring in the dragon's world.

## dangers
### LARGE
Ordinary desert hazards apply on the way. The domain behind the tunnel recreates the fight with the Guardian of Apep's Oasis and Proliferating Organisms, the corrupted forms of Apep's creatures. That is a gameplay recreation of past events, not something a visitor simply wanders into.

## travel
@version: 3.6
### STANDARD
Reached overland through the northern Desert of Hadramaveth. The Realm of Beginnings domain unlocks after the Archon Quest chapter What Shape Does the Self Hold.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the cavern is unchanged, and Apep rests in her restored oasis and is not hostile. Nothing found in 6.x or 7.0-7.1 content changes the site.

## entry: Apep's Resort and the Realm of Beginnings
@keys: Apep's Resort, Realm of Beginnings, Lost Hermitage, Heart of Oasis
@version: 3.6
### STANDARD
Apep's Resort is the cavern entrance; the Realm of Beginnings is the domain reached through it, where the player relives the fight with the Guardian of Apep's Oasis. The domain text frames it as memory of the battle, recalled in the land where Apep rests.
### LARGE +
Story background: the Heart of Oasis was Apep's source of power, a world nurturing her elemental children. Forbidden knowledge corrupted it, and the 3.6 quest restored it.
