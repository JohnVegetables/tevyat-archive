## summary
### LITE
Albedo's Campsite is a small laboratory outpost inside a cave in Dragonspine, used by the alchemist Albedo. It lies on the path from the Entombed City's Ancient Palace to its Outskirts.
### STANDARD +
It is a working field lab rather than a home: a place for research, cooking and alchemy, kept warm against Dragonspine's cold.

## places
@version: 1.2
### STANDARD
At the cave mouth two lit torches warm anyone who approaches, and a cooking pot stands beside them. Inside are two workstations, four shelves stacked with research materials, and a crafting bench for alchemy. Barrels and crates are scattered around.
### LARGE +
A record board is covered in his experimental reports. In-game flavour text jokes that the amount of incomprehensible content on it has grown again, and that the Traveler's help to him is what matters.

## people
@version: 1.2
### STANDARD
Albedo is the person tied to the place; his own entry carries his biography. The camp itself has no resident NPC in the sources read.

## history
@version: 1.2
@availability: limited
### LARGE
The wiki's galleries show the camp before the Princeps Cretaceus Chapter and again during the limited-time event quest "The Snowy Past", when an easel and equipment have been knocked over. Treat the event details as limited-time material.
### ULTRA +
The wiki notes the camp lies in a small cave on the route between the Entombed City's Ancient Palace and its Outskirts, so it is best placed as a stop between those two Entombed City areas.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
A cramped, warm cave of notes, glassware and alchemical clutter against the snow outside, with the hum of ongoing research. Good for quiet working conversations.
