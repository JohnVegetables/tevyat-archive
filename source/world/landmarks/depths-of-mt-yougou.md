## summary
### LITE
The Depths of Mt. Yougou is the dark space beneath Mt. Yougou on Narukami Island where the central roots of the Sacred Sakura lie. Its plants have grown gnarled and unnatural in utter darkness, and something twisted was sealed there. It is opened only at the end of the Sacred Sakura Cleansing Ritual, when the barrier is lifted and the Miasmic Tumor made of the roots' accumulated filth is destroyed.
### STANDARD +
"Depths of Mt. Yougou" is an unofficial name; the quest map calls it Mt. Yougou: The Depths.

## geography
### STANDARD
The Depths are a cave reached through an opening at the roots of the Sacred Sakura, beneath the Grand Narukami Shrine's mountain. A large pool of Electrified Water beside the mountain drains health until the ritual is done. The final quest sends the Traveler jumping into the opening beside Hanachirusato.
### LARGE +
The place is the end of the root system that connects the Thunder Sakura to the Sacred Sakura; most of the filth absorbed by the Thunder Sakura has been transmitted here.

## history
### STANDARD
The Sacred Sakura Cleansing Ritual has to clear the central roots in the Depths of Mt. Yougou as its last stage, after the barriers at the Thunder Sakura roots have been broken and the filth purged. The filth here condenses into a Miasmic Tumor. The most recent time this was done was by the Traveler and Hanachirusato.
### LARGE +
Hanachirusato says the Sacred Sakura can tell the person performing the ritual from others and creates openings for them to hit the tumor. The fight has three puzzles involving lanterns, with Ochimusha (Cankered Flame and Ensorcelled Thunder) appearing between stages, and Elemental statuses cannot be applied to the tumor.

### ULTRA +
Before the final step Hanachirusato confesses that when she first gave the Traveler the Konda Village ward and called them the "Destined One", she was not yet confident of their virtue and courage; she also says she wishes someone like the Traveler had stood beside the Kitsune Saiguu. The Depths sit under the tree whose roots, the wiki infers, may have fused with the Irminsul's, though canon does not state this outright.

## quests
### STANDARD
The Depths feature in Yougou Cleansing, the final part of the Sacred Sakura Cleansing Ritual series. After the tumor is purged, Hanachirusato reveals herself to be filth condensed from the memory of the Kitsune Saiguu and must be cleansed too, so she dissipates after saying goodbye, leaving behind a mask. Completing it grants the achievement Spring Cleaning.
### LARGE +
The mask is the Mask of Memories, the quest reward.

## dangers
### STANDARD
The Miasmic Tumor and the Ochimusha that appear during its fight; before the ritual is completed, the Electrified Water around the mountain's foot.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Utter darkness, twisted roots, a sense of something held down for a long time, and after the ritual a quiet that the tree itself seems to share.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the Depths are cleansed. No 6.x or 7.x story content about them was found on the wiki.
### STANDARD +
The Electrified Water pool is de-energised and the Sacred Sakura now glows blue.

## entry: Miasmic Tumor
@keys: Miasmic Tumor, Ochimusha, Mask of Memories
@keys2: Depths of Mt. Yougou, Sacred Sakura, Hanachirusato
@version: 2.0
### STANDARD
The Miasmic Tumor is the physical form the accumulated defilement of the Sacred Sakura's roots takes in the Depths of Mt. Yougou during the major cleansing. Left alone, tainted roots could corrode and kill the tree, so it must be destroyed by whoever performs the ritual.
