## summary
### LITE
The Grand Wrestling Grounds is an elevated arena decorated with massive bull horns in Atocpan, Natlan. It is the central hub of the Collective of Plenty tribe, where its many athletic competitions are held, and is also called the Resonating Arena.
### STANDARD +
Staff listed here include Macacoz, a professional wrestling referee, Muuziyaa of the Flying Squad and Tonaca, the logistics manager. Cuaxilo is also listed among the NPCs, and a Collective of Plenty Bulletin Board stands nearby.
### LARGE +
The arena appeared in version 5.5 with the Collective of Plenty content. The alternate name Resonating Arena is attested in the quest Ardent Land of Plenty.
### ULTRA +
Sources read give no founding history or rules of the contests; keep those unstated in scenes unless a Collective of Plenty entry supplies them.
