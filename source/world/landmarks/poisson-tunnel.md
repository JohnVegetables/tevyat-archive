## summary
### LITE
The Poisson Tunnel is the passage carved through the West Slopes of Mont Automnequi so that the Clementine aquabus line can run beneath the mountain, between the Court of Fontaine and Romaritime Harbor. The Fontaine Research Institute dug it with newly built engineering machines, including the "Golden Vessel".
### STANDARD +
The success of the tunnel project is credited with sparking a new generation of clockwork technology, including the Construction Specialist Mek.

## geography
### STANDARD
Cuts through the West Slopes of Mont Automnequi in the Belleau Region. The aquabus guide Aeval notes that the mountain lies between the port and the Court and is not very steep, but a route round the summit would be too unstable for a small boat, hence the tunnel. It is named for the nearby town of Poisson.
### LARGE +
Passengers see it lit by Fontaine-exclusive night lights powered by Indemnitium, a contrast Aeval draws with the fluorescent materials of Sumeru and the torches of other lands. She adds that when the line first opened the tunnel was badly lit, dark and creepy enough to make some young passengers cry.

## history
### STANDARD
According to the wiki's lore, the Fontaine Research Institute brought in huge engineering devices and developed new machines, among them the "Golden Vessel", to bore the tunnel. The project's success pushed the Institute to develop more advanced clockwork meka for wider use.
### LARGE +
The Construction Specialist Mek's archive text says the mek type is named for an engineer employed by Callas during the Clementine Line's construction; with Callas' backing the engineer built the Golden Vessel, and the mek was later designed with a similar structure at smaller scale. Per Aeval, the Poisson House of Caspar supported the project; when it joined the construction, local fears about the line faded.
### ULTRA +
According to the wiki, Callas' wife Clementine died giving birth to their daughter Navia, after which Callas funded the aquabus system; its lines are the Callas, Clementine and Navia lines. The sources seen do not say outright that the Clementine Line is named for her. Aeval presents the line as a monument to Fontaine's scientific progress, but also remarks that few people use it.

## travel
### STANDARD
Aquabuses of the Clementine Line, operated by Aeval, run from the Court of Fontaine Main Station to Romaritime Station and pass through the tunnel. Aeval's tour commentary changes between trips and often mentions the tunnel.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
A long, glowing bore through rock, lit by Indemnitium lamps and watched over by a cheerful Melusine guide telling stories about the project.

## status
### STANDARD
As of 7.1 the tunnel is in use for the Clementine Line.

## entry: The Golden Vessel
@keys: Golden Vessel, Construction Specialist Mek
@keys2: Poisson Tunnel, Fontaine Research Institute, Callas
### STANDARD
The "Golden Vessel" was a gigantic piece of construction machinery built with Callas' financial backing and used to dig the Poisson Tunnel. The Construction Specialist Mek, a large clockwork meka valued for building and archaeological excavation, was later designed with a similar structure at smaller scale.
