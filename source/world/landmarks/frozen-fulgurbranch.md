## summary
### LITE
The Frozen Fulgurbranch is a bolt of lightning that froze solid mid-fall above the House of Hesperides in Fellfrost Peak. It is a teleport waypoint on the way up to the Cloudborne Altar. The Snegovik Larisa's grief and fury, raised as a blizzard in her last hours, is said to have frozen it.
### STANDARD +
In the quest Bell Ringing at Dusk, Paimon notices the lightning is frozen and recalls that Aglaya told them the blizzard on the day Larisa died could freeze lightning mid-fall. The Eye of Graeae concludes the final site of Pavlina's ritual lies at the summit above the clouds.

## geography
### STANDARD
A formation of frozen lightning shaped like a branch, with a teleport waypoint at its base, above the main mansion of the House of Hesperides. From here the climb continues to the Rumblebranch and then to the Cloudborne Altar.
### LARGE +
The wiki lists the Rumblebranch as one of the area's exploration features. In the quest it is an ice branch that draws lightning down from the sky. Its upper branch is incomplete until the Traveler collects two smaller branches (Rumbletwigs), after which lightning begins to flow.

## history
### STANDARD
According to Pavlina's document in the mansion, Larisa's Prime Ice magic joined with her fury at her betrothed's desertion during the wedding ritual and raised a blizzard that stopped even the clouds and thunder. The wiki's Larisa page credits that blizzard with forming both this branch and the Cloudborne Palace.
### LARGE +
Pavlina raced toward Larisa through the storm, but Larisa's body had become fragile and dissolved into snow-foam. Pavlina later used the lightning-bearing summit for her own ritual, drawing lightning and power from above.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Eerie and sad: a thunderbolt held still in ice, a monument to one instant of rage. It is part of a storm that never ends.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Paimon fears the lightning could fall on the Traveler, and the Eye of Graeae agrees it very well might; the quest warns the Traveler to be careful on the climb.

## naming
### ULTRA
Chinese 冰结的雷枝 (Bingjie de Leizhi, "ice-bound thunder branch"), Japanese 氷結の雷枝, German Gefrorener Blitz-Zweig, French Fulgubranche gelée. The wiki Navigation lists it among Snezhnaya's points of interest.
