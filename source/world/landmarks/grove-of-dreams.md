## summary
### LITE
The Grove of Dreams is an underground cavern in Apam Woods, in the Dharma Forest of Sumeru. Arapandu describes it as a place where one may enter dreams.
### STANDARD +
It connects to the Ruins of Dahri to the west and holds one of the "branch and leaf" energy sources of the Varuna Contraption. Like Vanarana, it exists in a real and a dream version; the Traveler switches between them by playing the Rhythm of the Great Dream on a Vintage Lyre at a Silapna.
### LARGE +
Some enemies do not appear in the dream version.

## geography
### STANDARD
Entered through a stone-littered opening in the ground, reached by following a large tree root north of the Slumbering Roots quest marker. The twisting roots of the great tree make finding anything there hard.
### LARGE +
Local flora include Nilotpala Lotus and Rukkhashava Mushrooms. Hazards are floating and winged fungi, Cryo Whopperflowers, and ruin machines (Ruin Drakes, Cruiser, Destroyer).

## people
@version: 3.0
### STANDARD
Arakunti, one of the Aranara who tend the Varuna Contraption, withdraws here and hides unless Aranara music is played. He speaks cryptically, saying one might be a tree, rain, a dream of Vana or the strings of an instrument.
### LARGE +
Arakunti sends word to Arapandu that he is fine and that the Grove looks after him; Arapandu had asked the Traveler to check on him.

## history
### STANDARD
The Grove shelters one component of the Varuna Contraption, the device Greater Lord Rukkhadevata built to bring timely rains. It had gone unmaintained, and part of its restoration is done here.

## quests
### STANDARD
Slumbering Roots, part of Varuna Gatha: the Traveler follows Arakunti into the Grove, plays the Rhythm of the Great Dream at Silapna, and in the dream turns three leaves toward the branch.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Root-tangled and dim, a place where waking and dreaming sit close together.
