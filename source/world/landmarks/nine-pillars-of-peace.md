## summary
### LITE
The Nine Pillars of Peace are nine ancient stone pillars standing on Cuijue Slope in Minlin, Liyue, around a ruin and a temple at the centre of the area. In-game viewpoint text says nine shackles of stone were said to be laid deep in the valleys there to drive off evil and cleanse the world.
### STANDARD +
Approaching them darkens the sky: when a player nears the pillars, the area turns grey and the land outside Cuijue Slope looks unnaturally dim until they leave the vicinity.

## quests
### STANDARD
The world quest Nine Pillars of Peace is started at the stone tablet in the middle of Cuijue Slope. The Traveler gathers nine Stones of Remembrance by making offerings to Statues of the Seven in Liyue, then climbs each pillar and sets a stone into its face-like tile. Doing so opens the central ruin, where the Traveler must defeat Ruin Guard and other guardians to reach its treasure room.
### LARGE +
The quest ends with a Dull Ring from the ruin, which the Traveler may sell to Bolai in Liyue Harbor or to Linlang. Bolai pays less Mora but adds Adeptus' Temptation, while Linlang pays more Mora. The quest's tablets say the pillars were raised to suppress conflict and warn that breaking them would let war consume the world again; Linlang relays only a legend that Liyue's forebears built them, and no builder is named.

## surroundings
### ULTRA
Cuijue Slope, which holds the pillars, is home to a Pyro Regisvine boss and a Ruins Torch puzzle (three torches lit with Pyro open a chest), and its local specialties include Cor Lapis, Qingxin and Violetgrass.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 nothing further has been revealed about the pillars; they remain a relic of old Liyue, raised to suppress conflict per the quest tablets, with no builder named.
