## summary
### LITE
Tokoyo Reisen, once known as the River Lethe, is a stream in the Evernight Temple area of Enkanomiya. Its water glows faintly in the dark. In the World Quest Lotus Eater the Traveler collects the Waters of Lethe here to grow a Dragonbone Orb, a fruit whose scent calms the mind and makes people forget their worries.
### STANDARD +
The water itself does not erase memory; only the orb made with its help does. The wiki notes the name echoes the River Lethe of Greek myth, whose water made the dead forget.

## geography
### STANDARD
The stream runs near the Evernight Temple in Enkanomiya and is one of the places the Traveler is sent to in Lotus Eater. The water shines weakly in the darkness and looks unremarkable to Paimon on first sight.
### LARGE +
The water is carried to the Dragonbone Flower, atop which it is poured; the orb condenses overnight, taking until the next daily reset. The Evernight Temple's wider neighbourhood is a region of Hilichurls, Abyss mages and Ruin machines.

## history
### STANDARD
Lotus Eater tells how a resistance member in Byakuyakoku, Adonis, and his comrades sold Dragonbone Orbs to fund their cause against the nobles. Soon they found that members of the resistance began to avoid conflict and asked to leave, which Adonis blames on contentment: when oppressed peoples grow satisfied they cease to resist. He suspects the orb's formula may have been leaked by the rulers, though he cannot say. He tells the Traveler that the orbs can only be made with the water of Tokoyo Reisen.
### LARGE +
The Traveler meets the afterimage of a resistance fighter who has forgotten his own name and wants to find his "children", his students, who hide in the Narrows; this leads to Adonis.

### ULTRA +
"Tokoyo" is also tied to the old name of the realm itself: Enkanomiya was once called Byakuyakoku and, before that, Tokoyokoku ("eternal land"), and The Byakuyakoku Collection says its name changed over the years from "Aphotic Earth" to Tokoyokoku to Byakuyakoku. Enkanomiya's ghostly residents who appear during Evernight are called Shades of Tokoyo. The wiki does not say whether the river's name is connected to the realm's.

## quests
### STANDARD
Lotus Eater starts at the Altar at the Serpent's Heart and requires The Subterranean Trials of Drake and Serpent. Its steps include going to Tokoyo Reisen, obtaining the Waters of Lethe, going to the top of the Dragonbone Flower, watering it, and collecting the Dragonbone Orb after a day. Afterwards the orb can be sold in a side objective.
### LARGE +
The quest also yields a Secret Room Key for a separate hidden exploration objective, Adonis' Abandoned Safehouse.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
A dim stream of faintly glowing water in a cave of the temple, quiet and half-lit, with a faint glow that hints at the orb's quiet danger.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 Tokoyo Reisen is unchanged and still the source of the Waters of Lethe. No later story involves it.

## entry: Dragonbone Orb
@keys: Dragonbone Orb, Dragonbone Flower, Waters of Lethe, River Lethe
@keys2: Enkanomiya, Tokoyo Reisen, Lotus Eater
@version: 2.4
### STANDARD
A Dragonbone Orb is a rare fruit a Dragonbone Flower bears when watered with the Waters of Lethe. Its fragrance relaxes the mind and sweeps away worry, so it fetched a high price, and eventually the resistance in Byakuyakoku used its sale for funds. Its effect on the buyer is forgetting. The item text for the Waters of Lethe says the water by itself will not cause forgetting.
