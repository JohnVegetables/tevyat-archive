## summary
### LITE
The Land of Myriad Megaliths is a point of interest in the Sulfurous Veins area of Tequemecan Valley, Natlan. Its in-game viewpoint is titled Stone Chamber of Shifting Crystal Pillars.
### STANDARD +
The name and viewpoint title point to a stone chamber of crystal pillars. No lore, inhabitants or quest are documented for it in the sources read.
### LARGE +
