## summary
### LITE
The Northern Training Ground is a small obstacle-and-target course on Lempo Isle in Nod-Krai, built by Vaino, a thirty-four-year-old former treasure hunter who has retired to run it. It offers light challenges such as shooting targets and dodging, and it has become tangled up with a cave of target-stealing Sniffer Moles the local children call the Hollowhands.
### STANDARD +
The course is on the map as the Northern Training Ground. A Hidden Exploration Objective in its southwest corner has three treasure hunters spar with the Traveler, and the Clink Clank Clash event quests of 6.0 begin here.

## geography
### STANDARD
A landmark on Lempo Isle, with a teleport waypoint close to where Vaino stands; its map area is labelled Tundra Gap and includes two separate caverns. The course has three challenges: a first one near the waypoint, a second whose entrance Vaino marks, and a third he rebuilds during the quest. A cave elsewhere nearby holds the Hollowhands' trading box.
### LARGE +
Some targets fly, and in the second half of the second challenge they move, so the Traveler must shoot on the run. The Hollowhands' cave has a box where Nasha Town's children leave goods for trade and a hidden nest chamber the moles treat as a treasure room.

## people
### STANDARD
Vaino is the owner, a retired treasure hunter who worries he is too old for the trade and hopes the course will bring in customers, especially children and Fatui stationed in Nod-Krai. Sisko, a girl from Nasha Town, tells the Traveler about the Hollowhands; Lalli and Bozena, two other townspeople, are asked about target-making materials. Terho, Bielov and Incali are treasure hunters who spar in the southwest corner.
### LARGE +
Vaino gets the sales pitch he uses from Bozena, whom he credits with teaching it to him in Nasha Town. Terho is the boss of his small crew and, after losing, jokes that he might ask Vaino for a job.

## culture
### STANDARD
Treasure-hunter humour and folklore surface here. A joke Vaino tells says the only place a thirty-five-year-old treasure hunter is called young is at their funeral. The children tell different stories about why the Hollowhands trade by having people count to seventy-seven with eyes closed: Sisko mentions Reed Miller stealing seventy-seven treasures from the governor's palace, while Bozena says it was seventy-seven loyal followers.
### LARGE +
Paimon adds a joke version of her own. No source settles the number's origin.

## history
### STANDARD
Vaino built the course after leaving treasure hunting, using nearly all his savings. Vaino's targets kept vanishing from it; the quest reveals the Hollowhands, a group of Sniffer Moles, were taking them as bedding, and the children had been collecting materials to sell him new ones. In effect, Vaino paid again and again for the same targets.
### LARGE +
After the quest Vaino chooses not to demand money back from the children, and says Sisko's feedback and friends coming to play are worth more than Mora. He has since worked out trading rates with the Hollowhands that leave him a decent profit.

## dangers
### STANDARD
None of note. The sparring with Terho's crew is a friendly challenge, and the Sniffer Moles are harmless.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Grassy and informal, with Vaino cheerfully self-deprecating and signs that spoil their own surprises. A relaxed tune that wiki notes call the training ground's unofficial theme, Loafing on the Grass, accompanies it by day.

## quests
### STANDARD
The Stress of Changing Careers (World Quest) begins when the Traveler is stopped by Vaino near the teleport waypoint. After testing his three challenges and finding that his targets have been stolen, they use Old Portable Bearings from Nasha Town's children to trade with the Hollowhands, discover the moles, and help Vaino improve his final challenge. It unlocks the Hidden Exploration Objective Triple Training Time (the wiki's unofficial name) and an achievement.
### LARGE +
The Clink Clank Clash quests An Adventurer's Woes, A Mysterious Outsourced Contract, Misplaced Creativity and The Last Step of Testing all start here during the event of 6.0.
### ULTRA +
In Triple Training Time, Terho, Bielov and Incali challenge the Traveler one by one and then all together; Terho says afterwards that losing is no cause for brooding and that he may consider early retirement.

## status
### STANDARD
As of 7.1 the training ground is open and Vaino remains its boss. Nothing recorded after 7.1.

## entry: Vaino
@keys: Vaino
@keys2: Northern Training Ground, training ground, Hollowhands
### STANDARD
Vaino is a former treasure hunter, thirty-four, who has started a new career running a training ground in Nod-Krai. He is anxious about age and funerals, but friendly. He originally believed the Hollowhands were old enemies and nearly put his affairs in order before learning they are Sniffer Moles.

## entry: The Hollowhands
@keys: Hollowhands, The Hollowhands
@keys2: Sniffer Mole, Vaino, Sisko, Northern Training Ground
### STANDARD
The Hollowhands are a group of Sniffer Moles that live in a cave near the Northern Training Ground and trade with Nasha Town's children through a small box. The children leave goods, close their eyes and count to seventy-seven, and find a gift in return. The moles took Vaino's targets to line their nests.
### LARGE +
Sisko thinks of them as an unseen, good kind of thief like Reed Miller and kept the trading rules secret. After the quest the moles continue to exchange goods; Vaino says he is still searching for a better nest material than his targets.
