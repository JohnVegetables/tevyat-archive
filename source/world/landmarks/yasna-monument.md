## summary
### LITE
Yasna Monument is a small subarea of the Ashavan Realm in the Dharma Forest of Sumeru. It is the site of the Memory of Stone, a memorial plaque the Aranara set up for Nara Varuna.
### STANDARD +
The Memory of Stone was made so that a visible memory would show that Vana and the Aranara never forget their adventures with the Aranara. The Traveler cleans it in the world quest Memory of Stone, which starts here.
### LARGE +
The quest follows Varuna Gatha, the Aranara story of Nara Varuna, and involves the Aranara Aranakula, spore-covered stones and a hidden cave.

## geography
### STANDARD
Located in the Dharma Forest within the Ashavan Realm, the hilly, wooded and wet southern region of Sumeru. Stones around the monument are covered with spores during the quest, and a cave "branch" lies under a Four-Leaf Sigil at the southwest of a hill.
### LARGE +
The Ashavan Realm is described by the game as a materially rich land of hills, woods and wetland, with ramparts defending its western edge against sandstorms. The name Ashavan is Zoroastrian-derived.
### ULTRA +
The name Yasna is Avestan for a Zoroastrian ceremony and for the main body of Avestan liturgical texts, which suits a place devoted to ritual remembrance, though the game states no such link.

## people
@version: 3.0
### STANDARD
The Aranara Aranakula is the figure the Traveler meets for the Memory of Stone quest. Nara Varuna is the Golden Nara whom the memorial honours; Araja describes Varuna as kind, brave and honest.
### LARGE +
In the quest the Traveler must move forward without being noticed by the Fungi nearby, and Aranakula runs off if they are spotted; defeating the Fungi is pointless because new ones spawn.

## history
@version: 3.0
### STANDARD
The monument stands for the Aranara's remembrance of a Nara who befriended them long ago. It belongs to the same story thread as the Aranyaka, the book of stories between Aranara and Nara.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Quiet and tender: a mossy memorial in the forest, cared for by small creatures who fear forgetting.

## quests
@version: 3.0
### STANDARD
Memory of Stone (part of the Varuna Gatha series) begins here and leads on to A Prayer for Rain on the Fecund Land.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the monument remains a quiet Aranara memorial site; nothing in 6.x or 7.0-7.1 content changes it.

## entry: Memory of Stone
@keys: Memory of Stone, Nara Varuna, Aranakula
@version: 3.0
### STANDARD
The Memory of Stone is the plaque marking Aranara remembrance of Nara Varuna. Its cleaning is the objective of the Memory of Stone quest, in which the Traveler and Aranakula clear spores from the stones and find a "branch" in a cave, following a Seelie to the three "leaves".
