## summary
### LITE
The Final Night Cemetery is a graveyard around a lighthouse on Paha Isle, in Nod-Krai. It was once an active outpost of the Lightkeepers (Ratniki). Several decades ago a fight with the Wild Hunt put out the lighthouse's light and killed most of the garrison, and the survivors buried their dead here. Flins, a Ratnik, now lives alone on the island, together with many ghosts of fallen Lightkeepers.
### STANDARD +
The Meeting Point of the same name stands at the lighthouse. The map description says that a certain Ratnik lives here alone but faithfully fulfils his duties. It is always dark at the cemetery.

## geography
### STANDARD
A small, dark island with a lighthouse that has a telescope for signalling, a cooking stove, a monument bearing the Lightkeepers' oath, and rows of graves. The western part held a tavern long ago. The local specialty is Frostlamp Flower.
### LARGE +
From the lighthouse telescope the player can view distant scenes: the coastal waters, the open sea with Fatui and mechanical constructs, and a far island with the city of Piramida in the north; the Kuuvahki Experimental Design Bureau looms in the distance like a silent behemoth.

## people
### STANDARD
Flins is the island's only living Ratnik. Ghosts of dead Lightkeepers remain: the Loud Phantom (Dietrich), a Food-Obsessed Phantom (Jaakob, after The Chef's Tale) and a Ritual-Conducting Phantom.
### LARGE +
The graves include those of the Lightkeepers Aimo, Dietrich, Eetu and Haapa, the chef Jaakob, the Khorin family (Danuta, Hamalainen and their young daughter Adina) and the Special Support Squad Sumrak. An engraved inscription honours "Aarnivalkea" Chudomir. The epitaphs read differently when Flins is the active character.

## history
### STANDARD
The island was an active outpost whose lighthouse guided merchants and Lightkeeper fleets and reported Wild Hunt movement; its light was never extinguished and became a symbol of hope. A mysterious blue lantern kept there became a Ratnik legend. Several decades ago the Wild Hunt put out the light, and ninety percent of the troops died. In despair the Ratniki turned to the lantern and coincidentally awakened Flins, who helped them fight despite being newly awake and limited in power. The survivors and Flins buried the fallen and made the cemetery; the Lightkeepers then left and only Flins remained.
### LARGE +
The monument records the Lightkeepers' oath: that they foresaw a night sky devoured by deathly silence, vowed to use their bones and blood as fuel, and promised to keep the flame of hope burning in the snowy plains. The full text is shown when Flins or Illuga inspects it after the quest Lightkeepers' Oath.
### ULTRA +
The oath also says: whoever cradles the flame and keeps it burning need not descend into the darkness; the Lightkeepers shall make life greater than death; they will part the snow-curtain of the sky and blast the winter winds across the wasteland, and shine the eternal light of hope into the endless snowy plains. For other characters the monument shows only the opening and closing lines.

## culture
### LARGE
Gravestones are inscribed with short blessings: wishes for dreams of wine, song, flowers, or the flavour of smoked fish. A notice board in the tavern advertised Dandelion Wine from Mondstadt and warned the ghost Loud Phantom's seat was not to be touched. The lighthouse follows a signal system: Distress, Guidance, Summoning, All Safe and Warning, each a pattern of short and long flashes.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Always dark, quiet except for ghosts and sea, with a single steady beam from the lighthouse. Gravestones, a stove that someone relights if it is put out, and a long-closed tavern give it the feel of a place still kept up for the dead.

## quests
### STANDARD
To the Lighthouse (Flins; Aimo and Eetu appear) starts here and unlocks the Meeting Point. It leads to The Special Support Squad's Tale. The Chef's Tale and Lightkeepers' Oath also involve the island.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 Flins lives and keeps watch at the Final Night Cemetery's lighthouse.

### STANDARD
A mysterious blue lantern was kept on Paha Isle. Ratniki made it a local legend: if followed, it would guide them far from the Wild Hunt. The Starshyna of the time ordered offerings to it. When the garrison was being overwhelmed, the survivors turned to it in despair and coincidentally awakened Flins.
