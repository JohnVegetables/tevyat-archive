## summary
### LITE
The Sanctum of the Oathkeeper is an exterior area on Hiisi Island in Nod-Krai. It provides access to two instanced locations: the Sanctum of the Oathbearer and the Interstice of the Nearing Moon.
### STANDARD +
The Sanctum of the Oathbearer (Court of the First Edict) is entered from its base after being unsealed in Echoes of an Unfinished Past.

## geography
### STANDARD
Exterior grounds on Hiisi Island serving as the doorway to the two instances. The wiki gives no further description of its layout.

## history
### STANDARD
No history for the Oathkeeper site itself is stated in the sources read. The inner Sanctum of the Oathbearer was sealed by Aila five hundred years ago.
### LARGE +
The Oathbearer's documents describe the Interstice of the Nearing Moon as the sanctuary where the Scions' ancestors found refuge, raised by the Dragon-born.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the area is open and serves as the entrance to the Oathbearer and the Interstice.

### ULTRA
The Chinese names of the Oathkeeper and Oathbearer sanctums differ by one word: "one who keeps the oath" versus "one who left the oath behind". Other localisations vary in tone: French has a sanctuary of the Proselyte and a sanctuary of the Perjurer; German has Oathkeepers (plural) and Oathbreaker. The English names do not say whom either refers to.
