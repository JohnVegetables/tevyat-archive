## summary
### LITE
The altar at the Serpent's Heart is a circular chamber in Enkanomiya with five blue wall panels, each tied to a different Key Sigil. In Evernight, entering a sigil pattern in the right order makes it spawn a chest, an enemy or a spirit, and two world quests start here.
### STANDARD +
It is where the Traveler first recovers a fragment of the Reins of Revival, and where the afterimage of the resistance leader Supada no Hiko is found, unable to leave.

## geography
### STANDARD
The room sits at the heart of the Serpent's Heart island. A mechanism to the north-east switches the area to Evernight, which is needed to use the altar.
### LARGE +
An Altar Pattern is a set of the five Key Sigil types arranged in a circle, each with one to five dots. The pattern must first be activated elsewhere in Enkanomiya, then entered here by walking into the panels in dot order. Right steps light the sigils and send the player back to the centre; a wrong one leaves the panels dark.

## quests
### STANDARD
The Heart of Ouroboros (part of From Dusk to Dawn in Byakuyakoku) has the Traveler solve the altar's first riddle to receive the first Reins of Revival fragment. In Lotus Eater, an afterimage here, bound by the altar's prison, asks for help recovering his name.
### LARGE +
The tablet near the altar names him as Spartacus the Righteous, now Supada no Hiko, and records a curse in the name of Tokoyo Ookami. He says the prison grinds away his soul and memories and wishes to send a message to his students. The quest leads to Adonis in The Narrows and ultimately the Dragonbone Flower.

## history
### ULTRA
The story told by the afterimage places the altar in the Byakuyakoku era: the resistance leader was captured after an uprising, was imprisoned and tortured in the altar by henchmen of the Sunchild, and his organisation collapsed. The altar appears to have served as a prison, though the original purpose of the room is not stated.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the altar stays as released in 2.4 and has no further role in 6.x or 7.0-7.1 content.
