## summary
### LITE
The Nursery of Nightmares is a space resembling the Night Kingdom in Ochkanatlan, Natlan. It is where the Traveler finished the chase for the Dragon of the City of Flowing Ash in Moment of Awakening, where the dragon's corruption was purged and Och-Kan's soul was freed, and where Och-Kan's remains now lie.
### STANDARD +
The wiki lists it as a Point of Interest unlocked during the quest; the interactable remains describe him as the Python King, the Sacred Lord and the fell dragon, fully dragon and fully human, sleeping in silence until the end of time.

## history
### STANDARD
Cocouik's laser opens a gap in the fleeing dragon's defences, and the Traveler follows it into this space. A voice then introduces itself as Och-Kan, once ruler of Natlan, whose mind was infused into the city and whose soul had been devoured by the Abyss poison and turned into a furious dragon. The voice asks to be helped to purge the poison, and the dragon is defeated. Och-Kan speaks, now with his own mind, as the survivor of the Abyss's taint and as the one who in the past sealed his mind inside the Core of Chu'ulel.
### LARGE +
Och-Kan lets the Traveler walk through his memories, since everything that happened in Ochkanatlan was recorded by his 'two tainted eyes'. They include the arrival of Taika's expedition and a confrontation in which Kukulkan tells Och-Kan he has betrayed Xbalanque's oath, that his dragon blood marks him as the last dragon-heir, and that he must join the two Golden Entreaties to activate Huitzilopochtli. Och-Kan refuses, saying a world without humans has no meaning, and Kukulkan warns that he will come to collect Och-Kan's soul. After the memories, a Golden Entreaty is recovered.
### ULTRA +
Och-Kan says that the Golden Entreaty can cleanse Natlan's Abyssal filth but not in the way the Traveler imagines, which is why he hid it. His memory is fragmented and he cannot recall what the Entreaty is for 'when taken to the sacred mountain', suspecting that Kukulkan is tampering with his thoughts. He also tells the Traveler that Little One carries the same Flamelord's Blessing as he does.

## quests
### STANDARD
Moment of Awakening, the second part of The Tonatiuh Quivers, in the Lost Traveler in the Ashen Realm quest chapter (5.2). The steps are tracking the dragon, defeating it, speaking to the 'evil dragon', looking for answers in Och-Kan's memories, and collecting the Golden Entreaty.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
The place is dream-like and quiet; the quest's black-screen narration and golden text give it the feel of a recollection rather than a location.

## dangers
### STANDARD
In the story, the Dragon of the City of Flowing Ash fights with Golden Flame attacks. Nothing else hostile is listed.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the Nursery is calm. Och-Kan's remains rest there; the dragon body and Cocouik's mind were reunited as Och-Kan, who died as the quest ended.
### STANDARD +
The Golden Entreaty found here was later joined with the other and used in the Heart of the Sacred Mountain (5.5). No development in 6.x or 7.0-7.1.

## entry: Och-Kan's Remains

@spoiler: major

### STANDARD
After Moment of Awakening the Nursery contains Och-Kan's remains as an interactable. Its text calls him the Python King, the Sacred Lord and the fell dragon, fully dragon and fully human, who now slumbers in silence until the end of time.

## entry: Dragon of the City of Flowing Ash
@keys: Dragon of the City of Flowing Ash, evil dragon, Tide-Turning Sacred Lord
@spoiler: major
@version: 5.2
### LARGE
The Dragon of the City of Flowing Ash was the 'evil dragon' that haunted Ochkanatlan for decades and whom Bona's tribe feared. It was Och-Kan's Abyss-corrupted body, the half he had split from his mind. Kukulkan used it, the wiki says, to devour human souls for his masks. A nameless adventurer's notes say it attacked her but that she might owe it thanks for leading her to the Core of Chu'ulel.
