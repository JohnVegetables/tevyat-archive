## summary
### LITE
Jademouth is a stretch of the Bishui River in Chenyu Vale, Liyue, flanked by enormous pieces of jade. Legend says an adeptus cast the jade down to protect the people of the vale from flooding.

## geography
### STANDARD
Huge blocks of jade stand on both sides of the Bishui River in the upper part of Chenyu Vale. It is a subarea of the Upper Vale. The Version 4.4 preview text, in verse, refers to spring cries rippling through the vale, shrines leaving the northeast shores, and a whirlpool that never churns, with beautiful jade concealed within.
### LARGE +
The preview verse is not a map description; it is a poetic lead-in for the new region and should not be used as a literal guide.

## history
### STANDARD
Legend says an adeptus cast the jade down to protect the people from flooding. Chenyu Vale lore, as cited by the wiki, ties this to the Archon War: the mistress of Chenyu Vale was at war with Morax and, near the end, loosed a flood along the Bishui River that would have drowned the vale. Her three adepti, the Herblord, Lingyuan and Fujin, rebelled; Fujin threw the Votive Rainjade into the water and so made Jademouth.
### LARGE +
Afterwards the Herblord was greatly weakened, Fujin lost much of her power and sank into the sea, and the mistress was said to be sealed beneath Chenyu Vale beside the ruins of the lunar chariot. All of this is told through legend and book text, not through a first-hand witness.
### ULTRA +
The wider Chenyu legend of a jade slab that could summon sweet rain and was thrown into a river, probably the source of the name Chenyu ("Jade-Sinking"), is told in several incompatible ways, so Jademouth's origin should be treated as a story with variants.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Cool green stone beside moving water, in a vale of tea terraces and mist. The place feels like a shrine to an old rescue.
