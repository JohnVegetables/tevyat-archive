## summary
### LITE
The Chasm Withered Tree (an unofficial name) is a withered tree in The Chasm's Underground Mines in Liyue, north of the Main Mining Area. It cannot be interacted with.
### STANDARD +
Approaching it makes Paimon remark that it looks just like the strange ancient white tree on Dragonspine.
### LARGE +
The wiki takes her comparison to refer to the white tree where the Princess' Box is found in the Dragonspine quest Dragonspine's Last Trio. The game does not say whether the trees are connected.

## trivia
### ULTRA
Identical withered trees stand on Dragonspine, on Tsurumi Island near the shore where the "Boatman" is found, and beside the central island in The Orchard of Pairidaeza, but the Chasm's tree is the only one that triggers dialogue when approached.
