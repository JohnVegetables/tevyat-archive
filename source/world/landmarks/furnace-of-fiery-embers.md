## summary
### LITE
The Furnace of Fiery Embers names five Source Mechanism sites in Ochkanatlan, Natlan: three in underground caverns (west, southwest and north) and two on small islands (east and southeast).
### STANDARD +
They are the focus of the hidden exploration objective Stoking the Ancient Flames (5.2). Each furnace has a matching Iridescent Inscription near an old sword, and the inscription reports whether the furnace has been rekindled.
### LARGE +
Once all five are rekindled the player can draw the ancient sword, which takes them to a small floating island. Until then the old sword seems bound to the space itself and will not budge.
### ULTRA +
The wiki marks the objective's name as unofficial, and the sources read leave the furnaces' original function unexplained.

## places
### STANDARD
The wiki numbers the sites I to V, though the in-game map labels only some. Furnace I is reached by touching an Iridescent Inscription, II by entering a tower, and III after the achievement Clear the Area, Please! The eastern and southeastern furnaces sit on small islands and are not labelled with numbers.
### LARGE +
Furnaces I and II each have an upper and a lower level; furnace III has an entrance, a cave path and an area of ancient ruins.

## quests
@version: 5.2
### STANDARD
Stoking the Ancient Flames is a quest-like exploration objective: activate all five furnaces, then draw the ancient sword. The first step grants the achievement Embers Rekindled and the second grants a copy of the Flute of Ezpitzal. These rewards are a gameplay detail.

## history
### LARGE
Canon text read does not say who built the Source Mechanisms or when; they belong to the ancient Ochkanatlan civilisation's technology.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Old mechanisms coaxed back to life: glowing inscriptions in caves and on small islands.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the five furnaces remain sites to be rekindled by players. No later changes were found.
