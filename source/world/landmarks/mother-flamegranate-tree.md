## summary
### LITE
The Mother Flamegranate Tree is a great tree in Tequemecan Valley, Natlan, and the source of the Scions of the Canopy tribe's Flamegranates. Its roots, in a cave warmed by lava, grow the Mountain King Flamegranates.
### STANDARD +
Only Scions of the Canopy holding a special entry pass, the Green-Edged Paper Slip, may enter the root cave. Each pass bears its holder's name and cannot be traded. Tisoc has long served as guard at the cave entrance.
### LARGE +
The tree bears two special kinds of fruit: larger Flamegranates on the cliffside, and the Mountain King Flamegranates near the roots. The latter are named for the Mountain King, Kongamato, to whom they are offered.
### ULTRA +
Before the world quest Ripe For Trouble the tree and its fruit are blue and unripe; afterwards they are orange and ripe.

## geography
### STANDARD
A tree on a cliff in Tequemecan Valley, with a cave of roots below. The roots have three map levels: a cool damp cave (before the quest), a warm cave (after it) and a geothermal surge.
### LARGE +
The roots' special fibres thrive when immersed in Liquid Phlogiston rather than burning. That contact is needed for the fruit to mature and acquire its Phlogiston content.

## people
### STANDARD
Tisoc, the cave guard, calls himself an excellent one. The tribe guards the tree because it supplies its Flamegranates.

## culture
### STANDARD
Flamegranate skins can be dried to make bags, such as those carried by couriers. Mountain King Flamegranates are an offering to Kongamato.

## quests
@version: 5.0
### STANDARD
Ripe For Trouble (5.0): the Traveler and Paimon enter the cavern to find Tisoc, who has gone missing, explore the roots, break solidified magma and raise the Liquid Phlogiston levels, then flee before speaking to Tisoc again.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Lava and solidified magma fill the root cave.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Hot, orange and carefully guarded: a tree whose fruit is a tribal treasure.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the tree is ripe and still guarded by the Scions of the Canopy. No later changes were found.
