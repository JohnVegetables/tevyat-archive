## summary
### LITE
"The Real Annapausis" is the hidden heart of Annapausis, reached after Ann's storybook quest to rescue Princess Lyris. It is where the consciousness of Mary-Ann, a hybrid Oceanid, resides. First entered in the world quest "Mary-Ann's Story" (4.0), it then closed until "Rowboat's Wake" (4.2) made it permanently accessible.
### STANDARD +
The place looks like a gentle garden and school hall where Mary-Ann keeps tea, flowers and a "dog" waiting for visitors. It is, in her own words, a tomb rather than a home.

## geography
### STANDARD
Part of the Annapausis subarea in the Court of Fontaine Region. It is reached by escaping the "Convoluted Corridor" of the adventure and has its own map entry. Inside are a flower garden, a tea hall that rings a bell for teatime, and a doghouse; at the end of the first visit the room floods.
### LARGE +
The wiki notes the name Annapausis plays on the Ancient Greek word anapausis, "rest". In Chinese and Japanese the name suggests a resting or sleeping place, also a pun on Ann's name. This is the wiki's reading, not in-game commentary.

## people
### STANDARD
Mary-Ann lives here. She appears as a young woman with fair skin, bright blue eyes, mid-length blue hair and a blue-and-white frilled dress. She is a hybrid Oceanid formed when Lyris absorbed the dying Mary-Ann Guillotin's consciousness, and the wiki says she unintentionally created the Oceanid Ann out of parts of her memories and dreams. Ann, Paimon and the Traveler meet her here.
### LARGE +
In the first meeting she speaks of being long in Fontaine, of "a friend" (Rene) who had been to Sumeru and of Basil, a capable caretaker of children and flowers. Her "dog" speaks with polite court manners and is not Mori; Ann notices this.

## history
### STANDARD
Per the wiki, during a conflict in the remains of Elynas 400-500 years ago between the Marechaussee Hunters and the Narzissenkreuz Ordo, Mary-Ann Guillotin was gravely wounded by an explosion. Lyris dissolved her and absorbed her consciousness, creating Mary-Ann. Mary-Ann presumed her old companions dead and built a dream in Annapausis, hiding inside a fairy tale the human had once told Lyris and waiting for the Institute's people to return.
### LARGE +
In "Mary-Ann's Story" the Traveler deduces that the Narzissenkreuz Kingdom is only a story, and Mary-Ann sends the party away, saying the story is ended and Annapausis is "our tomb". In "Rowboat's Wake" the Narzissenkreuz now defeated, the Traveler and Ann return; the Traveler smashes the "Will to Resist" with the Holy Blade, breaking the illusion. Mary-Ann forgives Rene (now Narzissenkreuz), accepts the past and decides to live as herself.

## quests
### STANDARD
"Mary-Ann's Story" (Ann of the Narzissenkreuz Act III, 4.0) and "Rowboat's Wake" (In the Wake of Narcissus, 4.2). After the latter, returning to the Real Annapausis and approaching the house grants an achievement.
### ULTRA +
In "Mary-Ann's Story" the first visit includes a bell for teatime, tasteless snacks, sunflowers that bloom when watered, and dandelion seeds from Paimon. Mary-Ann tells a parable of water droplets that unite, to ask whether uniting costs identity.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Warm, pretty and tinged with unreality: tea set out for guests who are not coming, flowers waiting to bloom, food that tastes like water, and a temperature that drops sharply when the dream breaks.

## status
### STANDARD
As of 7.1 the place is open and empty of its resident. Mary-Ann set out after "Rowboat's Wake" with Ann, Seymour and Mamere to see the world beyond the glass wall, saying the garden is somewhere they can come back to if afraid.

## entry: Mary-Ann Guillotin and Annapausis

@keys2: Ann, Lyris, Narzissenkreuz
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The human Mary-Ann Guillotin was the sister of Alain Guillotin; the Oceanid Mary-Ann is a different, hybrid being that has her memories fused with Lyris'. The Real Annapausis is her constructed refuge. Ann, a young Oceanid born from the dream, believes her purpose is to rescue Princess Lyris, a quest Mary-Ann gently frees her from.
