## summary
### LITE
The Core of Chu'ulel is a cavern beneath the Vision Serpent's Sanctum in Ochkanatlan, the ruined dragon city of Natlan. Inside is the Chu'ulel Light Core, a glowing orb that pulses like a heart and once powered all of Ochkanatlan. Och-Kan built this core by enslaving his own draconic half, and sealed his mind inside it to fight alongside his people. By 7.1 it has been cleansed of Abyssal energy but is spent, leaving only a piece of Pyrophosphorite.
### STANDARD +
The name echoes the Tzotzil and Tzeltal Maya word 'ch'ulel', the soul or essence centred in the heart (a real-world etymology noted by the wiki). Its maker is the Sacred Lord Och-Kan, who in the present day takes the form of the dragonspirit Cocouik.

## geography
### STANDARD
A cavern below the Vision Serpent's Sanctum, reached after the quest Adventure in the Land of Mists. The map divides it into a Secret Path, with a tower, and an Interior, with tents, pots of scrolls and a transparent barrier gate, around the Light Core. In the quest the Traveler purges Abyssal blight from the ruin, after which the core drops. An adventurer's notes say the Secret Source Automatons here, unlike those elsewhere, seem to understand speech.
### LARGE +
Gameplay note: the Radiant Pillars and Radiant Fragments that the Traveler uses to purge the Abyssal blight are part of the area's mechanisms, introduced in the Palace of the Vision Serpent quest.

## history
### STANDARD
When Och-Kan became king of Ochkanatlan he tried to make the dragon inside the Old Core of Chu'ulel power the city for him, since its mechanisms responded only to draconic energy. She refused, and he trapped the dragon in the Old Core. Writing in the Holy Sovereign's Notes, concluded that the only way to build a core that owed allegiance to humanity alone was to enslave the 'draconic' part of himself, so that it would stay imprisoned beneath human boots even if his human parts perished. The result was the new Core of Chu'ulel, which only he could command.
### LARGE +
At some point he placed his mind inside the core to fight beside his people, then fell inactive, leaving at least one Secret Source Automaton to guard it. Abyssal corruption slowly rotted the Light Core. According to a nameless adventurer's notes, the adventurer (identified with Bona) used Pyrophosphorite on her own body to awaken the dragonspirit slumbering in the heart of the machine, found it small, green as turquoise, and called it Cocouik. In the present day the Traveler and Paimon purge the Abyssal energy; the core falls and loses the last of its vitality, and only a piece of Pyrophosphorite remains inside.
### ULTRA +
Other notes in the interior record Och-Kan's reasoning: the Recording Officers' reports showed Abyssal corrosion in the controllable range; the city must remain permanently sealed so the Abyss cannot break through; and 'hope' alone must never be forsaken. A note in the Secret Path tower laments that its writer could not return to the flames alongside 'him' and calls the writer an unworthy beast; the wiki ties it to Och-Kan.

## quests
### STANDARD
Lost Traveler in the Ashen Realm, Act II: Adventure in the Land of Mists leads to the Sanctum; Palace of the Vision Serpent is set in the Core (cleansing the Abyss, protecting the fallen core from waves of monsters, and finding Pyrophosphorite wrapped in a nameless adventurer's notes). In Moment of Awakening Och-Kan names the Core as the place Bona woke him from.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
The heartbeat-like pulse of the Light Core, Abyssal blight eating at the structure, and tents and pots of scrolls left behind. The notes sound like a ruler reasoning his way toward ever harsher measures to keep his people safe.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Abyssal blight, Abyssal vortices and the Tenebrous Mimesis and Thundercraven Rifthound enemies that attack the fallen core; Secret Source Automatons, though one fights beside the Traveler once the core is protected.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the Core is cleansed but dead: the Light Core lost its last vitality, and what remains is a piece of Pyrophosphorite. Cocouik later regained Och-Kan's body and spoke as him before dying.
### STANDARD +
Cocouik's reawakening as Och-Kan and his death in Moment of Awakening ended his hold on the city. No later development in 6.x or 7.0-7.1.

### LARGE
The Holy Sovereign's Notes are fragmentary writings by Och-Kan scattered through Ochkanatlan. In them he insists that only humans should decide human fate, distrusts every power greater than humanity, and decides to enslave his draconic half; later notes describe the pressure of keeping the city sealed against the Abyss and the hope he clung to. Some pages are smeared and unreadable.

## entry: Cocouik
@keys: Cocouik, Coco
@keys2: Och-Kan, Core of Chu'ulel, Bona
@spoiler: major

### STANDARD
Cocouik is the small, turquoise-green dragonspirit that travels with the Traveler in Lost Traveler in the Ashen Realm. The adventurer's notes describe waking it in the Core of Chu'ulel with Pyrophosphorite. In Moment of Awakening the dragon of the City of Flowing Ash, Och-Kan's body, is defeated; Cocouik's mind rejoins it and speaks as Och-Kan, who dies soon afterwards.
