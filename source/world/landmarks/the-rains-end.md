## summary
### LITE
The Rain's End is a location in Apam Woods, in Sumeru's Dharma Forest. It is where the Traveler first gains access to the Varuna Contraption, the Aranara-tended device that sustains the rain cycle of the forest.
### STANDARD +
It unlocks during the Varuna Gatha world quests, at the end of A Prayer for Rain on the Fecund Land. Its viewpoint text says there is no rain here, yet it is the birthplace of rain, and that the purification of the Vana rains begins and ends here.
### LARGE +
Playing the Rhythm of the Source Water on a Vintage Lyre by the contraption activates it, after which two smaller contraptions appear elsewhere in Apam Woods.

## geography
### STANDARD
Underground in Apam Woods, in the Ashavan Realm. The southernmost contraption is found here, and it unlocks the others.

## people
@version: 3.0
### STANDARD
The Aranara Arapandu, Aranakula, Arabalika and Arakunti repair the contraption in the quest series. Its original makers are long dead, and the Nara Varuna, who once fixed it when it went wrong, is nowhere to be seen.

## history
### STANDARD
The Varuna Contraption was a device made by Greater Lord Rukkhadevata to summon timely rains in a stretch of desert, according to the original Chinese text of an item description. The English text omits this detail.
### LARGE +
After the forest labyrinth was created, the lord of the forest Viaghara was born from the moon's reflection on the water, per Laurel Coronet text.

## quests
### STANDARD
Varuna Gatha, including Memory of Stone, Irate Iron Chunk and Slumbering Roots, then A Prayer for Rain on the Fecund Land. Completing the series lets the Traveler change the weather in Apam Woods; this is a gameplay mechanic.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Quiet, waterlogged and hushed, with the sense of a machine that lets the forest breathe.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the contraption works and Apam Woods keeps its rain cycle; no later changes were found.
### ULTRA +
The location also holds Ragged Records, interactable notes tied to the Zandik's Legacy series; they were not read for this entry.

## entry: Varuna Contraption
@keys: Varuna Contraption, Varunastra
@version: 3.0
### STANDARD
The Varuna Contraption controls the Varunastra, a huge pillar of water that creates the rain cycle nourishing the once-desert forest of Apam Woods. By default the weather alternates between sunny and rainy on a twelve-hour cycle, which the Traveler can override (gameplay mechanic).
