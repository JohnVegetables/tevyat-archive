## summary
### LITE
The Dragonbone Flower is a giant flower in the Serpent's Bowels of Enkanomiya. During the world quest Lotus Eater, the Traveler waters it with the Waters of Lethe from Tokoyo Reisen, and after a wait it condenses into a Dragonbone Orb: a fruit whose scent calms the mind and sweeps worries away. Its story is a warning that comfort can dull the will to resist.
### STANDARD +
The orb is how the last members of a Byakuyakoku resistance tried to fund themselves, and how that resistance lost its fighting spirit. Bards in Mondstadt are said to call the orb the "Fretnaught Fruit".

## quests
### STANDARD
In Lotus Eater (a world quest started near the Serpent's Heart, reached during Evernight) a spirit at the altar has forgotten his own name. The Traveler learns from a stone tablet that he is Supada no Hiko, also called Spartacus, and he sends them to find his surviving students. One of them, Adonis, hides in The Narrows and explains the orbs; as thanks he reveals that they can only be made with the waters of Lethe from Tokoyo Reisen. The Traveler pours the water on the flower from its top, then returns after the next daily reset to take the orb.
### LARGE +
Paimon finds the orb unremarkable to look at and suggests showing it to the shop owners in Mondstadt, Liyue and Inazuma. As a gameplay note (not lore), a follow-up hidden objective lets the Traveler sell the orb to three shopkeepers for Mora and food.

## history
### LARGE
In the item's account, Supada no Hiko led a resistance against the Sunchildren and was captured, after which his organisation collapsed. His remaining students sold Dragonbone Orbs to raise money to regroup. The orbs sold well for their calming effect, but members began to avoid battle and asked to leave, and the movement dissolved. Adonis remembers only that oppressed people who grow content stop resisting.
### ULTRA +
Adonis wonders in hindsight whether the formula, and the orb's popularity, was arranged by Byakuyakoku's rulers. The Dragonbone Orb's own text frames its effect as a question, asking whether giving up objectives and desire to be content is not happiness. Canon does not answer it.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
A strange, hushed bloom in the dim depths of Enkanomiya, tied to Lethe, the water of forgetting. The mood is soporific and bittersweet rather than threatening.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the flower remains in Enkanomiya's Serpent's Bowels, and nothing in 6.x or 7.0-7.1 content changes its story.
