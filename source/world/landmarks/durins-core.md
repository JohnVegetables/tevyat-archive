## summary
### LITE
Durin's Core is the still-beating heart of Durin, the Abyssal dragon killed above Dragonspine, found in Wyrmrest Valley. Its ominous vitality could turn Strange Teeth into Empowered Dragonteeth, and it gives off warmth.
### STANDARD +
During the Interlude Chapter quest "The Gardener" (version 5.6), Albedo drains the blood from the heart, and it goes inert. It no longer makes Empowered Dragonteeth but still warms the surroundings.

## history
@version: 1.2
### STANDARD
Durin was slain over Dragonspine by Dvalin about 500 years before the present, and his blood seeped into the mountain's ley lines. The heart in Wyrmrest Valley is what remains of him there. Its unusual energy is why the Mondstadt adventurers could use it to make Empowered Dragonteeth.
### LARGE +
The same blood is what revived the Frostbearing Tree, which is described as carrying the Abyss's malevolent energy in the newborn tree. The wiki's page for the core links this location to that wider story but canon does not spell out the mechanism.

## gameplay
@version: 1.2
### LARGE
Game mechanics: before the Festering Fang quest the core only shows flavour text. Afterwards, offering four Strange Teeth gives four Empowered Dragonteeth. After The Gardener, interacting with it shows that it has lost its power, and Dragonteeth are obtained at the Alchemist's Crafting Bench near Orban at the Dragonspine Adventurer Camp instead.

## status
@version: 5.6
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
As of 7.1 the heart is inert, still warm, and no longer a source of vitality. The quest text frames the change as a solemn quiet after power left it.
### ULTRA +
Albedo's role in draining it ties the place to the Paralogism arc, in which the small form of Durin helps the Traveler's friends in secret.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
A cave-bound hulking mass that looks like a heart: once warmly glowing and unsettling, now silent and still. Suits tense or melancholy scenes.
