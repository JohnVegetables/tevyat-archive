## summary
### LITE
Dev's Cavern is a cave in Ardravi Valley, in the Dharma Forest of Sumeru. It is a minor point of interest whose name uses the old Sumeru word for Abyssal monsters.
### STANDARD +
It is unlocked during the world quest Vimana Agama: Dev Delver Chapter. Its inhabitants are hilichurls and samachurls, with elite Abyss Mages of the Cryo, Electro, Hydro and Pyro varieties.
### LARGE +
Because it is an investigation site rather than a story location, canon gives it no individual history.

## geography
### STANDARD
A cavern within Ardravi Valley in the Dharma Forest.

## history
### STANDARD
A Sumeru loading-screen tip says the dark beasts from beneath the earth were once called Dev by the people of Sumeru, and the term also appears in the Oracle Parchment, for the End of Death text. This explains the name in general but not the cave itself.
### LARGE +
Dev is a Persian-derived word for monsters; in-game it was applied to Abyssal creatures.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Hilichurls, Electro and Geo samachurls, and elite Abyss Mages (Cryo, Electro, Hydro, Pyro).

## quests
### STANDARD
Vimana Agama: Dev Delver Chapter unlocks the location.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Dim, enclosed and quietly hostile, a hideout of Abyss casters and hilichurls.
