## summary
### LITE
The Chasm's Bed is a mysterious, ancient pocket of space deep beneath the Chasm's Spiritstone. It bends space and time, reads the minds of those inside, and devours the people it traps. During the Cataclysm, Bosacius and Liyue's Millelith used it to seal away the monsters of Khaenri'ah. The Traveler and others entered it in the Version 2.7 Perilous Trail event.

## geography
### STANDARD
A map description calls it a mysterious space beneath the Chasm that would have been exceedingly hard to reach without an accidental fall, with a way out just as unclear. It is a maze of stone caverns with spatial rifts, doors and passages that shift and close behind travellers. Its rock is extremely old Liyue Mountain Rock. In Perilous Trail it was entered through an entrance beneath the Chasm Nail, and became unreachable after the quest ended.
### LARGE +
Breaches in the caverns close on their own, and rifts regenerate after being cut open, so no matter how many were attacked there was no way out.

## places
### STANDARD
The labyrinth of caverns and spatial rifts; the doors that show each person what they fear; and the deeper area where Bosacius and Boyang completed the seal with the Fantastic Compass.
### LARGE +
During the event, the entrance to the event mode Realms of Guile and War was inside the space until Perilous Trail ended, after which it moved to a spot near the Chasm Nail. This is game-structure information.

## history
### STANDARD
When the Cataclysm began, an iron meteorite hit the Chasm and the monsters of Khaenri'ah poured out. Morax sent the Millelith to defend Liyue and evacuate miners. Brothers Boyang and Rongzhao were among those ordered by the Qixing to take the Fantastic Compass, a catalyst that manipulates space, and aid the Millelith. Bosacius, already mad, was drawn by the bloodshed and fought for Liyue, mistaking the Millelith for his Yaksha comrades. The fighting exposed an underground palace, and the defenders found that monsters weakened as they went deeper. The plan was to use the compass to trap the monsters and seal them away, at the cost of a mortal and an adeptus powering it and being sealed in too. Bosacius and Boyang volunteered.
### LARGE +
As the Millelith advanced they became limited too: breaches closed behind them, rifts regenerated, they lost track of time and tired. They used an Orichalceous Time-Dial to mark time between battles. In the end the monsters lost all mobility and were devoured, as were most of the Millelith, leaving only Boyang and Bosacius. Boyang saw illusions of his family and feared he was mad; Bosacius saw his Yaksha comrades, remembered his name and purpose, and together they used the compass to complete the seal. Bosacius died, and Boyang eventually perished too.
### ULTRA +
Centuries later (the wiki says five hundred years), Yelan shot down the Spiritstone, which she says is especially effective against Khaenri'ahn remnants. The impact may have damaged the seal. When Yelan and Itto were about to fight, their power gave the final push to open the seal, and the group fell in. Xiao entered by himself to learn if the nameless yaksha of the records was Bosacius, and later joined them.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Silent, shifting stone, and a sense of being watched. The space seems to know your thoughts and will turn them into doors, voices and faces. Time loses its grip: hunger and tiredness pause, then arrive all at once. Its purpose seems to be to entrap and devour those who enter.
### LARGE +
Characters describe it as a living thing. It targets those inside with changing illusions.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Spatial distortion that returns walkers to the same place (Itto compares it to an Inazuman bake-danuki legend); time distortion (Paimon felt twenty days pass, Itto three to five, though one day had passed); selective fatigue; mind-reading illusions that can become real and attack, including a recreation of Bosacius as he fought; and devouring, in which prolonged stay turns a person into a shadow wandering like a lost soul. The space can also fake voices, such as the Conqueror of Demons, to lure people deeper.
### LARGE +
The party escaped by turning the space's mind-reading against itself. They imagined the Fantastic Compass so that the space produced an illusory copy of it, which they used to open a way out. Shadowy figures pursued them during the escape. Xiao proposed to break out with all his power, but the party refused to leave him behind.

## travel
### STANDARD
Not reachable under normal play. The entrance beneath the Chasm Nail disappeared after Perilous Trail. Whoever enters needs a way to leave, which only the illusory compass provided.

## quests
### STANDARD
Archon Quest Interlude Act II, Perilous Trail (Version 2.7): The Unexpected Guest, Chasmic Maze, Danger All Around, End of the Line, and At Tunnel's End, Light. Realms of Guile and War was the event mode.
### LARGE +
Perilous Trail is a limited-time event, so knowledge of the space is not universal in Liyue. The party was the Traveler, Paimon, Arataki Itto, Kuki Shinobu, Yanfei and Yelan, with Xiao joining.

## status
### STANDARD
As of 7.1 the space is sealed again and inaccessible. The sources read record no later content about it.
