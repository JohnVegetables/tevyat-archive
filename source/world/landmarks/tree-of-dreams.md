## summary
### LITE
The Tree of Dreams is a mystic, seemingly sentient tree growing in the heart of the forest at Vanarana in Sumeru. After the cataclysm, the Aranara chief Araja turned his original body into it, so that only in its dream could the Aranara sustain a Vanarana large enough for them all.
### STANDARD +
Players unlock it with the world quest Trees and Dreams after The World of Aranara. In the Dream State it turns from green to luminous purple. Offering Dendro Sigils here raises Vanarana's Favor for rewards.
### LARGE +
The Aranara hid the real Vanarana in a dream to protect themselves from the devastation of Sumeru; the tree is the anchor of that dream, called Mahavanaranapna by the Aranara.

## geography
### STANDARD
Stands at the centre of Vanarana in the Dharma Forest. It looks different in the real and dream worlds: green and leafy in one, luminescent purple in the other. It is marked on the map only after it is first visited.
### LARGE +
When Vanarana's Favor reaches its maximum, the leaves atop the tree spread open in both the real and dream versions.

## people
@version: 3.0
### STANDARD
Araja, the Aranara village chief, is the tree. His physical body can no longer move, but his mind remains intact and can manifest a body inside the dream. Over about 500 years he has lost many old memories, but he remembers taking part in the last Festival Utsava and being close to Araji, Arapacati and Aramuhukunda.
### LARGE +
Araja tells the Traveler the story of Marana, an Aranara tale of death, and says the Aranara hid the real Vanarana in a dream after Greater Lord Rukkhadevata returned to Sarva and the rampage began. He stresses these are stories, not his memories.

## history
### STANDARD
When the original Vanarana was destroyed by the Withering in the calamity, Araja sacrificed his physical form and became the Tree of Dreams. This created the dream Vanarana, where the Aranara live in peace away from Sumeru's devastation and the harmful Nara.
### LARGE +
The Aranara's tale of the Ashvattha Tree, planted by Rukkhadevata and the origin of the Aranara, is separate from this tree. Arama refers to a first Ashvattha's Tree of Dreams rooted in Sarva; whether it is Araja's is not stated.
### ULTRA +
Araja's story places the tree after the Cataclysm: the real Vanarana was destroyed by the Withering, the Aranara hid it in a dream, and over the last 500 years he has lost many memories. His account of Marana is explicitly told as legend, not memory.

## culture
@availability: permanent
### LARGE
The Aranara honour their stories and names, including those kept in the Aranyaka. Offering Dendro Sigils to the tree is the Traveler's way of earning the forest's favour.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Gentle and strange: a village chief's body quietly holding a whole dream aloft, with the forest's glow shifting between green and violet as the world tips between waking and dreaming.

## quests
### STANDARD
Trees and Dreams, a dialogue-free world quest, only shows the Traveler where the tree is. The quest begins after the Traveler speaks with Araja during The World of Aranara.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the tree is alive and the dream Vanarana is maintained. Nothing found in 6.x or 7.0-7.1 content changes it.

## entry: Vanarana's Favor
@keys: Vanarana's Favor, Dendro Sigil, Dendro Sigils
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
An offering system at the tree, levelled by giving Dendro Sigils in groups of 35. Before version 3.6 the maximum level was capped at 20, 30 and 40 in versions 3.0, 3.1-3.3 and 3.4-3.5. This is a gameplay mechanic.
### LARGE +
Reaching the top level takes 1,750 Dendro Sigils and grants an achievement. Rewards include Mora, Adventure EXP and Mystic Enhancement Ore.
