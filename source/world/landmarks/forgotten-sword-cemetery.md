## summary
### LITE
The Forgotten Sword Cemetery lies in the middle of the large lake in Dadaupa Gorge, Galesong Hill. It is described as having witnessed the battle against the monsters of Khaenri'ah, and it is guarded by a three-layer seal.
### STANDARD +
In the world quest "Break the Sword Cemetery Seal", Dr. Livingstone explains that three hilichurl camps hold the tri-seal. Defeating each camp's guards unlocks an Elemental Monument, and breaking all three opens the cemetery.

## geography
@version: 1.0
### STANDARD
It sits at the centre of the lake in Dadaupa Gorge. The three camps tied to the seal lie south, north and south-east of it, in the gorge among the Meaty, Sleeper and Eclipse hilichurl tribes' territory.
### LARGE +
The three seals can be released in any order. Unlocking them gives access to a treasure at the cemetery's centre.

## history
@version: 1.0
### STANDARD
The only history canon gives is that the place witnessed the battle against the monsters of Khaenri'ah. Who the fallen were, and whose swords lie there, is not stated in the sources read.
### ULTRA +
Do not tie the cemetery to a particular hero, army or year; the link to the Khaenri'ah cataclysm is the only established connection.

## dangers
@version: 1.0
### LARGE
Hilichurls and some Fatui skirmishers are common in the gorge. Mitachurls guard the camps linked to the seal.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
A still, windswept lake island of old blades, quiet apart from the hilichurl camps around the shore. Suits solemn, mysterious scenes about forgotten war.

## entry: Dadaupa Gorge tri-seal
@keys: tri-seal, Sword Cemetery seal, Break the Sword Cemetery Seal
@version: 1.0
### STANDARD
The Forgotten Sword Cemetery is protected by three layers of seal. In the world quest started by Dr. Livingstone, the Traveler breaks them by defeating guards at three hilichurl camps and activating the Elemental Monuments there.
