## summary
### LITE
The Scorchwind Trial Grounds is a training ground in the Tezcatepetonco Range of Natlan, south of the "Masters of the Night-Wind" tribe. It is the base of the Pakpaka Flying School, which teaches flying with Qucusaurs.
### STANDARD +
The school appears to be run by the Flower-Feather Clan. Signboards advertise gliding challenges to passers-by, and the place has coaches, trainees and assistants.

## places
@version: 5.2
### STANDARD
The grounds are laid out as a course for flight practice. Three challenge signboards invite visitors to try different gliding tests: a timed run through obstacles, a ring-piercing route, and a high-speed dive. A flying school advertisement invites anyone to sign up and earn their wings, noting that flying contests are held every so often and that there is no need to wait for one.
### LARGE +
The signboard slogans are cheerful and pushy in tone, urging visitors to keep growing and to plan a route to success. Use them as a flavour of the school's marketing rather than as rules.

## people
@version: 5.2
### LARGE
The wiki lists several residents of the grounds: coaches, a self-proclaimed coach, trainees, an assistant and a few others. The roster mixes serious instructors with enthusiastic amateurs, so a scene here can include both.
### ULTRA +
The listed titles distinguish genuine coaches from one person who only calls themselves a coach. The wiki does not describe the school's internal hierarchy, so do not invent one.

## culture
@version: 5.2
### STANDARD
Flying with Qucusaurs is a skill the school treats as teachable, with levels of challenge and open contests. The name Pakpaka is likely drawn from a word for "wing" in several Philippine languages.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Open, windy training ground with ringed courses, banners and the sound of practice runs; lively, competitive and slightly boastful.
### LARGE +
Beginners wobble, coaches shout encouragement, and Qucusaurs wheel overhead. Good for light-hearted scenes.

## status
@version: 5.2
### STANDARD
As of 7.1 the grounds remain a working flying school with permanent gliding challenges.

## entry: Pakpaka Flying School

The Pakpaka Flying School teaches flying with Qucusaurs and is based at the Scorchwind Trial Grounds in the Tezcatepetonco Range, south of the "Masters of the Night-Wind". It appears to be run by the Flower-Feather Clan and holds contests now and then.
