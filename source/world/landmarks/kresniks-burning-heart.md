## summary
### LITE
Kresnik's Burning Heart is a large chamber under the central plaza of Snezhnograd, the capital of Snezhnaya. It holds the nation's most important Kresnik's Torch, a furnace that burns Kresnik Kristall ore to keep the city habitable. Staff of the Royal Energy Commission work there.
### STANDARD +
It is the central Torch referred to in-world as the most important in all of Snezhnaya. A map viewpoint at the site is titled "Ever-Burning Hearth Core".

## geography
### STANDARD
The chamber lies beneath the central plaza of Snezhnograd, in the Everfrozen Earth area of Snezhnaya. The Torch inside is far larger than the small "civilian model" Torches scattered across the country and the medium ones found in towns, and is described by an attendant as an enormous machine that needs constant human upkeep.
### LARGE +
Kresnik Kristall reaches it by rail from the mines of Novokitezhgrad, according to the attendant Yelagina.

## people
### STANDARD
Five open-world NPCs of the Royal Energy Commission work here as of 7.0 to 7.1:
- Zarechnaya, the Acting Chair (sent temporarily from Zapolyarny Palace);
- Zaretsky, the Vice President, who carries much of the real workload;
- Zavyalov, a staff member who knew the late president well;
- Zybina, an overworked technician-type employee;
- Yelagina, who keeps the day-to-day log of the Torch.
### LARGE +
Mood among them: the previous president, the Domovoy Izmaylov, has died, and staff talk about him fondly (Zavyalov and Zybina) or with unease about what he left behind (Zaretsky, Zarechnaya). Zybina says a former "technical expert" colleague was reassigned elsewhere, leaving her alone with technical problems.

## governance
### STANDARD
Responsibility for the Torches and for the transport and use of Kresnik Kristall lies with the Royal Energy Commission of Snezhnaya. The Commission's leadership is currently an acting chair appointed by the palace rather than a permanent president.

## history
### STANDARD
According to Yelagina, construction of the central Torch began almost immediately after the Belyi Tsar completed his experiments in the Pale Crown Palace; it has run ever since, kept going by generations of workers. Loading-screen text credits the Belyi Tsar with building the Torches to help humans survive Snezhnaya's cold. The Prototype Torches remain in the Pale Crown Palace.
### LARGE +
The viewpoint text records a saying that the Belyi Tsar himself granted ordinary people the right to use Kresnik Kristall, and that to Snezhnayans he was both the biting frost and the dawn that brought warmth.

### ULTRA +
Wider context from other texts: the Eye of Graeae says that before the Torches were built Snezhnaya consisted only of Frigid and Subzero temperature zones, unfit for humans. Kresnik Kristall is said to be crystallised from authority released when the Cryo Dragon Sovereign lost control roughly 6,000 years ago, as recounted in the Archon Quest An Ark That Used to Be a Soul (spoiler context; do not volunteer it in ordinary scenes). The Commission's late president Izmaylov, a Domovoy of great age, used to tell staff stories about Snezhnaya's past (per Zybina).

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
A warm, industrial interior in contrast to the blizzards above, with the steady hum of a furnace and staff completing logs, paperwork and maintenance. The tone is bureaucratic and a little uneasy: grieving colleagues, an acting chair who senses things have been "carefully hidden", and a deputy who sighs about gangs and the High Table.

## dangers
### LARGE
Zavyalov notes that Commission staff travelling to maintain Torches sometimes stumble into the Shadow Realm; the rule is to get out safely and report it to the Druzhna. This is a hazard of the job in general, not a danger inside this chamber.

## status
### STANDARD
As of 7.1 the Torch is running normally and the Commission is under Acting Chair Zarechnaya after the sudden death of its long-serving president. The NPCs say he died of illness; his death is treated differently in quest content, so do not assert a cause from the chamber staff alone.

## entry: Kresnik's Torch
@keys: Kresnik's Torch, Kresnik Torch, Kresnik's Torches
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
Kresnik's Torch is an artificial heat source that burns Kresnik Kristall ore. Most Snezhnayan cities are built around one or more large Torches, which hold a Normal Temperature Zone against the storms; small civilian Torches stand across the land. The most important one is in Kresnik's Burning Heart beneath Snezhnograd. The Royal Energy Commission maintains them. Loading-screen text credits the Belyi Tsar with building them for humans.
