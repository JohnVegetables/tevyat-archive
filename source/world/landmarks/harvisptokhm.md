## summary
### LITE
The Harvisptokhm is the giant tree in the middle of Vourukasha Oasis in Sumeru, the Pari's homeland, and serves as the God of the Pari. It grew from the remains of Egeria, the former Hydro Archon, and keeps her consciousness tied to the mortal world while suppressing an Abyssal rift.
### STANDARD +
Its interior can be entered through a hole at Tunigi Hollow or from the top of its stump. Inside lies a giant lotus, the Gaokerena, in which the God of the Pari's consciousness slumbers.
### LARGE +
Before the Khvarena of Good and Evil quests it looked like a withered stump with the Sign of Apaosha, an Abyss-reflecting phenomenon, looming above; afterwards it has a glowing blue canopy.

## geography
### STANDARD
Stands at the centre of Vourukasha Oasis, in the Realm of Farakhkert of the Girdle of the Sands. Tunigi Hollow, the old site of the Abyssal rift, opens into it. Around it lies the Amrita, a sea of pure dew made from Egeria's remains.
### LARGE +
After the Rite of Chinvat is performed, jumping into Tunigi Hollow takes the player to the Harvisptokhm instead. The name echoes the Zoroastrian tree of all seeds growing in the cosmic sea Vourukasha.

## people
@version: 3.6
### STANDARD
The Pari are the oasis's guardians and regard it as their god. Elder Zurvan sealed the Oasis with the Great Songs of Khvarena after an Abyss attack and admits only heroes bonded with Pari and pilgrims who come to worship Egeria. Sorush, Mihir and Rashnu are Pari in the quest line.
### LARGE +
The Oceanids, created and served by Egeria, rejected the ideals of the new Hydro Archon Focalors and came looking for their master at Tunigi Hollow. Finding not what they hoped, they scattered; people followed their pilgrimage trail, which led to the Purification Spring domain.
### ULTRA +
The name Harvisptokhm echoes the Zoroastrian tree of all seeds, which grows with the white Haoma (Gaokerena) in the sea Vourukasha; the game's naming of the lotus Gaokerena follows that source.

## history
@version: 3.6
### STANDARD
During the cataclysm, gods including Greater Lord Rukkhadevata and Egeria went to Tunigi Hollow to suppress the Abyssal defilement from Khaenri'ah below. Egeria was slain in the battle and her body became Amrita. Rukkhadevata grew the Harvisptokhm from it to anchor her consciousness, so it could absorb the defilement from the rift and keep the seal.
### LARGE +
In Khvarena of Good and Evil the Traveler, with Sorush and the other Pari, gathers the Great Songs of Khvarena and cleanses the defilement through the Rite of Ab-Zohr; the Defiled Chambers, the Gaokerena and the Flower of Sanctity are part of that climax. After The Hymn of Tir Yazad (Part 2) the Sign of Apaosha is sealed.

## religion
@version: 3.6
### LARGE
The Pari treat the tree as their god and Egeria's remnant; pilgrims come to worship her. The Great Songs of Khvarena and the Rite of Chinvat, Ab-Zohr and Tir Yazad are the ritual vocabulary of the quest line.

## dangers
### LARGE
Before the quest, Abyssal defilement and its monsters surrounded the rift; afterwards a Consecrated Fanged Beast guards the former rift and reappears. Interior chambers were defiled during the quest.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Hushed and sacred: pure dew, a god in slumber and a people who keep vigil over a grief older than most living memory.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the defilement has been cleansed and the seal holds; the tree is a living, restored sacred site. Nothing found in 6.x or 7.0-7.1 content changes it.

## entry: Gaokerena
@keys: Gaokerena, Flower of Sanctity, Defiled Chambers
@version: 3.6
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
The giant lotus inside the Harvisptokhm, where the God of the Pari's consciousness is said to slumber. In The Hymn of Tir Yazad the Traveler defends the Flower of Sanctity there against Abyssal creatures while the power of Amrita lends help.

## entry: Egeria's Remains and the Amrita
@keys: Egeria, Amrita, Mistress of Many Waters
### STANDARD
Egeria was the Hydro Archon and God of Justice who ruled Fontaine until 500 years ago, killed at Tunigi Hollow during the cataclysm. Her body became the Amrita, from which the tree was grown; Focalors succeeded her.
### LARGE +
Her consciousness is kept in the mortal world by the tree. The Pari and the Oceanids are among the peoples she created.
