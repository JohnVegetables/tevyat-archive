## summary
### LITE
The Spiritstone is the Celestial Nail at the bottom of the Chasm in Liyue, a vast sky-blue crystal pillar. It was sent from the heavens to purify Abyssal corruption in the ancient civilisation of Lang-Gan. Zibai held it back long enough for many of Lang-Gan's people to flee, and its impact is said to have made the Chasm.

## geography
### STANDARD
The nail hangs high in the Court of Pillars, the deepest point of the Chasm: Underground Mines, a vast cavern of old stone and glowing crystal. A viewpoint note attributed to Zhiqiong calls it a huge sky-blue crystal pillar that could only have come from the heavens, whose shade it shares. A crater surrounds the point of impact. A fragment, called the Wishstone, was found in the Stony Halls.
### LARGE +
A luxurious chest sits on top of the nail. After the events of the Chasm Delvers the nail floats lower than before, and the platform beneath it was struck by its fall.

## places
### STANDARD
Wishstone site (Stony Halls): where miners touched a fragment and began seeing their desires granted.

Court of Pillars: the cavern where the main body hangs, with five crystal mechanisms that feed into the nail.

Space beneath the nail: during the limited-time Perilous Trail event (2.7), the Chasm's Bed lay under it. See the Chasm's Bed entry.

## history
### STANDARD
Celestia sent the nail to purify the Abyssal taint in Lang-Gan. In the Realm of Rocks cinematic (Lantern Rite event, Luna IV) Istaroth tells Zibai that all places tainted by the Abyss must be purified and that this includes Lang-Gan. Zibai, who was a god of Lang-Gan, replies that she will die among its people. She raises a canopy of jade light under the falling nail, buying time for people to escape. Istaroth catches her as she falls and splits her soul into three. The survivors went on to Chenyu Vale, where their descendants live today, according to the wiki.
### LARGE +
Quests and item texts say the nail's impact formed the Chasm. The Zibai quests (The Realm of Clouds, The Realm of Rocks) are part of the limited-time Lantern Rite event A Lanternlit Ode to the Silver Moon.
### ULTRA +
Wishstone: in modern times, a fragment found in the Stony Halls gave miners hallucinations in which their desires were granted. Lumenstone and Lumenspar began appearing in large numbers, the Chasm's mines seemed to shift as if alive, and the Dark Mud of the Abyss emerged. Lumenstone repels Dark Mud. The Liyue Qixing therefore closed off the Chasm and sent investigators.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
A cathedral-like cavern, cold and silent, where a pale blue pillar hangs overhead. The nail seems to belong to another place entirely.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Abyssal power once bound the nail, and the beings guarding it are Abyss Mages, an Abyss Herald and Khaenri'ahn rifthounds. The Khaenri'ahn Ruin Serpent Haftvad the Worm emerged when the Traveler released the nail.

## travel
### STANDARD
Reached only by descending through the Chasm: Underground Mines, beyond the gate Zhiqiong identified near the camp. The Qixing had closed the Chasm after the Wishstone's effects on the miners.

## quests
### STANDARD
Wherefore Did the Spiritstone Descend? (2.6), part six of the Chasm Delvers world quest series. The Traveler purifies five crystal mechanisms with a charged Lumenstone Adjuvant, drawing out Haftvad the Worm. Yelan shoots the nail with a Hydro-infused arrow, dropping it onto the serpent and subduing it, after which it floats back up lower than before.
### LARGE +
Perilous Trail (2.7), a limited-time event with its own Archon Quest Interlude, sent Traveler, Paimon, Itto, Shinobu, Yanfei and Yelan into the space under the nail. Yelan said she had shot the nail because it is highly effective against remnants of Khaenri'ahn civilisation.

## status
### STANDARD
As of 7.1 the nail still hangs in the Chasm. The sources read do not record any Spiritstone-specific change from 6.x or 7.0-7.1.

## entry: Spiritstone
@keys: Spiritstone, Wishstone, Chasm Nail
### STANDARD
The Spiritstone is the Celestial Nail in the Chasm, sent to purify Abyssal corruption in Lang-Gan. Zibai held it back so more of Lang-Gan's people could flee. Miners found a fragment, the Wishstone, which showed them their desires; Lumenstone repels the Abyss's Dark Mud that came with it.
### LARGE +
In the Chasm Delvers the Traveler purified the nail's crystal mechanisms, drew out Haftvad the Worm and, with Yelan, crushed it under the nail.
