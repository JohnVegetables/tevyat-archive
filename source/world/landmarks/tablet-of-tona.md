## summary
### LITE
The Tablet of Tona is a stone wheel enshrined in the Temple of Pax, inside the Stadium of the Sacred Flame in Natlan. It stands as a symbol of unity in Natlan between humans, Saurians, the Sacred Flame and the Pyro Archon.
### STANDARD +
Pilgrims offer Pyro Sigils to it, and its appearance changes as the offerings reach the maximum.

## places
@version: 5.0
### STANDARD
The tablet sits in the Temple of Pax, part of the Stadium of the Sacred Flame in the Basin of Unnumbered Flames. The stone wheel is set against a relief.
### LARGE +
When the offering level is at its maximum, four streams of fire form behind the spikes of the wheel and flow clockwise around it, and the carved details on the relief glow a fiery red.

## culture
@version: 5.0
### STANDARD
The tablet's meaning is shared unity: people, Saurians, the Sacred Flame and the Pyro Archon together. The map text frames offerings as receiving the Sacred Flame's protection and blessings.
### LARGE +
The word "tona" is a Classical Nahuatl verb meaning to shine or be warm, which fits the flame theme.

## gameplay
@version: 5.0
### LARGE
Game mechanics: Pyro Sigils are offered to the tablet to raise the level of Tona's Flame's Restitution and receive rewards. Offerings are made in groups of 30, and 1,500 Pyro Sigils reach the maximum, level 50, which also gives an achievement. Before version 5.8 the maximum level was capped per version.
### ULTRA +
The version caps of 20, 30 and 40 applied during 5.0-5.1, 5.2-5.4 and 5.5-5.7 respectively.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Solemn and warm: firelit stone, a communal shrine at the heart of a festive arena.

## status
