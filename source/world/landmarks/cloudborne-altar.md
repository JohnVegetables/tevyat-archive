## summary
### LITE
The Cloudborne Altar is a summit ritual site above the clouds, high over the House of Hesperides in Fellfrost Peak. Pavlina, the Snegurochka Grand Duchess, used it for the last stage of a rite meant to take the power of the Eye of Graeae. The Traveler reached it in the quest Bell Ringing at Dusk and stopped her there.
### STANDARD +
The altar is reached by climbing past the Frozen Fulgurbranch and the Rumblebranch, ice formations that draw lightning, so a visit means ascending through a frozen storm.

## geography
### STANDARD
Part of the House of Hesperides area (map level "Cloudborne Palace"), above the mansion's chapel and far above the cloud layer. The route up goes through a lift, past a teleport waypoint at the Frozen Fulgurbranch, and then up an incomplete Rumblebranch, which has to be completed by gathering two smaller branches (Rumbletwigs) before the way to the altar opens. The altar itself is described by Paimon as gorgeous.
### LARGE +
The Eye of Graeae explains why Pavlina needed this height: the chapel inside the house is only for the part of the ritual that receives power, while giving power across a wide area needs somewhere much higher. Lightning is drawn down from the clouds toward the site.

## history
### STANDARD
In Bell Ringing at Dusk, the Traveler and Paimon arrive uninvited (the Traveler holds an invitation, which Pavlina says was never meant to be sent). Pavlina fights them. Aksinya then steps out of a portal to the Shadow Realm, saying she has finally broken free of Pavlina's traps, and joins in stopping the rite. After the fight Pavlina and Aksinya speak: Pavlina wants the power to give her people, the Snegovik, a whole soul; Aksinya wants it kept for a different plan. Pavlina disintegrates into icy mist and the union ritual is left complete without the offering having been made.
### LARGE +
The Eye of Graeae says that its fragmented parts re-crystallized after the ritual. Aksinya hands over a part of the Eye's body and explains that the Snegovik can only achieve completion through rituals, which is what Pavlina's endless rites were for. She also says Pavlina's success would have been the greatest defeat for the world, while granting that Pavlina acted out of love for her own people. This dialogue is from a world quest and the details of Aksinya's own plan are covered in Court of Eternal Bliss.
### ULTRA +
Pavlina's last words before dissolving speak of fate and a price to be paid for a "tomorrow", and she calls the door Aksinya seeks "a pageant leading to the executioner's blade". She also says she acted for the sake of a friend who died in pursuit of love, which the wiki connects to Larisa. Pavlina swore on Larisa's sword that her own wedding to Ikhor would succeed (Larisa page, citing Through the Gates of Ivory).

## people
### STANDARD
Pavlina is the central figure here. Aksinya appears at the end of the quest. The Traveler is accompanied by Paimon and by the Eye of Graeae, which supplies the guidance on the route.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
High, bright and cold: an open platform above the cloud layer, with branches of frozen lightning below and a sense that the storm itself has been made part of the ritual. Beautiful, but the Eye of Graeae warns that even the most pleasant places may hide danger.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Pavlina herself is the only threat shown at the altar. On the way up, falling lightning is a concern raised by Paimon and the Eye.

## naming
### ULTRA
Official names in other languages, per the wiki: Chinese 云上的祭台 (Yunshang de Jitai, "altar above the clouds"), Japanese 雲上の祭壇, French Autel des nuages, German Altar über den Wolken (literally "above the clouds"). The Portuguese wiki redirect links the location to "Palácio das Nuvens". English "Cloudborne" is the in-game form.

## status
### STANDARD
As of 7.1 the ritual here has been stopped and no later content in the sources changes that. Pavlina is gone from the House of Hesperides after Bell Ringing at Dusk.
