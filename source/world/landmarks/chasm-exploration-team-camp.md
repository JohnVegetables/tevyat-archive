## summary
### LITE
The Chasm Exploration Team Camps (an unofficial, community name) are two campsites that the Chasm Exploration Team, a group hired by Liyue's Ministry of Civil Affairs, keeps inside The Chasm's Underground Mines in Liyue. They are the team's bases and the places where its members can be found while the Traveler helps survey the mines.
### STANDARD +
The first camp is established during the world quest Chasm Spelunkers, the third part of The Chasm Delvers series, when the Traveler follows the team to it. The second camp appears after the quest Perils in the Dark. Small cages at the camps hold golden finches, which the wiki notes may be a nod to the mine canaries real miners used to detect dangerous gas; the game does not explain them.

## people
### STANDARD
Where NPCs stand depends on how far the player has completed The Chasm Delvers and the quest Valor's Afterglow. At the end of those quests, the first camp is home to Hao (a miner of The Chasm) and You Jun, a Millelith sergeant. The second holds Jinwu (the team's safety technician), Khedive (ecologist) and Taliesin (a bard). Jiaming and Tu can be found near the north-westernmost waypoint in the Chasm rather than at a camp.
### LARGE +
The wider team, per its faction page, includes the adventurer Zhiqiong and the former Treasure Hoarder Clitopho (drafted as demolitions expert) alongside Khedive, Jinwu and Taliesin. After The Chasm Delvers, Muning decides to make the group an official part of Huishan Hall.

## history
### LARGE
The Ministry of Civil Affairs hired the team to investigate anomalies in the Chasm. The Traveler first reaches the team's base in Chasm Spelunkers, after finding clues about Uncle He's whereabouts and meeting Jinwu; at that point the map areas for the Ad-Hoc Main Tunnel and the Main Mining Area are revealed. The second camp comes after Perils in the Dark, which concerns dark fog, ruins and two Abyss Lectors deeper in the Underground Mines.
### ULTRA +
According to Jinwu in The Heavenly Stone's Debris, Klee was meant to join as demolitions expert with Kaeya as her partner but was grounded after blowing up a stove at Good Hunter, so Clitopho took the role.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the camps remain part of the Underground Mines in Liyue, staffed by team members and local miners. No later change to them is recorded.
