## summary
### LITE
The Skyfrost Nail is the Celestial Nail (a "Nail of Retribution") planted at the summit of Dragonspine in Mondstadt, and the summit area takes its name from it. It looks like a colossal broken crystalline pillar. Thousands of years ago it fell from the heavens onto the green mountain of Vindagnyr, burying the kingdom of Sal Vindagnyr under the endless snow that still makes Dragonspine deadly. In the Traveler's time its scattered shards were rejoined, and it now hovers in the air above the peak.
### STANDARD +
It is one of several nails cast down across Teyvat by the Heavenly Principles. Its fall shattered Sal Vindagnyr's sacred white tree (an Irminsul tree, today the Frostbearing Tree), and the kingdom's people perished in the cold. Before the Traveler, no one in modern times had reached the summit: strange ice blocked the paths.

## geography
### LARGE
The nail stands at Dragonspine's summit, above the ritual site where it struck. The summit is reached through a cave southwest of Dragonspine's Statue of The Seven; until the nail's shards were freed, a strange, powerful air current blowing out of that cavern blocked the way. Beneath the nail's impact point a large hollow lies under the ice, where the Peak of Vindagnyr domain entrance now sits.
### ULTRA +
The nail's power changed the mountain's ley lines and climate. Where its fragments lay, the land froze around them in Ancient Rime, glowing ice that fire cannot melt, and unusual blue plants grow thickly around that ice. Its three fragments were frozen in three places: under the Frostbearing Tree on the mountain's outer slopes, in a chamber beneath the Entombed City outskirts, and deep in Starglow Cavern.

## places
### LARGE
- **The nail itself**: a towering pillar with a stone-like shell and a blue crystalline interior visible at its broken end, crowned with the celestial motif shared by all such nails. Since its restoration it floats above the summit, and it can be climbed.
- **Peak of Vindagnyr**: the domain beneath the nail. Its description calls it a ritual site once close to the skies that the frigid nail buried into the mountainside; inside, the cold is so intense that one must fight it as well as the enemies.
### ULTRA +
- **Frostbearing Tree**: Sal Vindagnyr's sacred white tree, shattered when a fragment of the nail struck it, later revived by Durin's blood (see its own entry).
- **Mural Room**: the Sal Vindagnyr fresco chamber near the Entombed City's ancient palace, which holds the princess' murals of the kingdom in its prosperity.

## history
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Thousands of years ago, refugees fleeing a land of snow and strife settled the lush mountain they named Vindagnyr, raised a monument on its peak and founded Sal Vindagnyr, which prospered under the heavens' blessing. Then the Skyfrost Nail descended on the ritual site at the summit. Clouds and blizzards closed over the kingdom, the cold never lifted, and the sacred tree was shattered. The chief priest Varuch climbed to the summit to beg the heavens for guidance and never returned; the princess died trying to graft a branch of the dead tree; the outlander hero Imunlaukr went in search of a way to break the ice and came back to find no one left alive.
### LARGE +
The Ancient Carvings, apparently left by the kingdom's last scribe, record that as snow whipped across the skies "the pillar that fell from the heavens was riven in three". The fragments froze the places where they came to rest, and one struck the white tree. Another fragment records that someone (the surviving text does not say who) attempted to heal the ley lines, but the tree had already withered. Albedo calls the nail's fall a judgment passed down by the Heavenly Principles. In general, canon texts say the heavens sent the nails down after the war of funerary flame to purge corruption and "mend the land", laying waste to mortal realms as they did. No source says why Sal Vindagnyr in particular was struck.
### ULTRA +
Five hundred years ago the Abyssal dragon Durin fell on the mountain, and his blood seeped into its ley lines. Weapon lore says his corrosive blood stained the valley red and the frozen tree reached its roots toward it; the Crimson Agate text says the tree absorbed that "crimson" and has now sprouted anew. About a thousand years ago, at the end of the aristocratic era, a noble expedition to uncover Dragonspine's secrets was lost on the mountain. In modern times the Dragonspine Adventurer Camp's teams still could not reach the summit: they were turned back by unbreakable ice, and the one party that climbed high enough reported only the strange air current pouring from the summit cavern.

## quests
@version: 1.2
### STANDARD
In the world quest **In the Mountains** (1.2), the adventurer Iris at the Dragonspine Adventurer Camp asks the Traveler to look into the strange, fire-proof ice blocking her teams' routes. Using Scarlet Quartz, the Traveler thaws the shard frozen at the Frostbearing Tree and then the ones at the Entombed City outskirts and Starglow Cavern. Each one flies up toward the summit. That opens the cavern to the peak, where Paimon spots a giant pillar with the three shards frozen around it again. Once they are thawed a second time and rejoined to the main body, the ice beneath the pillar shatters, revealing the hollow below, and the restored nail rises to hover over the summit. Iris is astonished to hear of a huge pillar floating there and plans to send explorers up.
### LARGE +
In **Anomaly: Mondstadt** (7.1, part of the world quest series The Anomaly, available after A Rekviem for the Underworld), the Traveler and Paimon find that the Skyfrost Nail has changed in the same way as the other nails. Albedo, waiting at the summit, says he has never seen anything like it in all his time on Dragonspine. He had assumed that once the Heavenly Principles passed judgment and the nail destroyed Sal Vindagnyr, its purpose was complete, but now sees it is not that simple. He stays to observe how the change affects the surrounding ecosystem.

## dangers
### LARGE
The summit is the coldest part of a mountain whose cold kills. Without warmth, Sheer Cold builds up until it drains a person's life, so warmth sources such as braziers, torches, Warming Seelies and the crystals called Scarlet Quartz matter. Cryo hilichurls and slimes roam the summit area, and a Frostarm Lawachurl guarded one of the frozen shards.
### ULTRA +
Defence Mechanisms and dormant Ruin Machines also guard ruins near the shard sites. Inside the Peak of Vindagnyr the floor shifts between warm and subzero zones, and the subzero zones speed up the deadly chill.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Biting wind and snow, bitter cold that even Paimon complains of, and above it all a vast pillar hanging silent in the air; those who climb it find themselves dizzyingly high above the mountain. The ruins of Sal Vindagnyr below and the blue plants around the old ice give the area a mournful, ancient feel. Ordinary visitors have no idea what the pillar is: Paimon could only guess that it fell from the sky.

## status
@version: 7.1
### LITE
As of 7.1 the Skyfrost Nail is whole again and hovers above Dragonspine's summit. After the Ruler of Death Ronova's last act in Snezhnaya, it is giving off the same strange mark of light into the sky as the other Nails of Retribution across Teyvat, and Albedo is studying it.
### STANDARD +
The change began when the dying Ronova fused the Eitr with Snezhnaya's nail, causing that nail to crumble (Archon Quest Chapter VII, 7.1). Albedo's observations focus on how the change affects Dragonspine's ecosystem. What it means has not been revealed.

## entry: Celestial Nails Across Teyvat
@keys: Celestial Nail, Nail of Retribution, Nails of Retribution, divine nail
@keys2: Skyfrost Nail, Dragonspine, Vindagnyr
@spoiler: major
@version: 7.1
### LARGE
The Skyfrost Nail is Mondstadt's Celestial Nail. Others are known in Liyue (the Spiritstone over the Chasm, which destroyed Lang-Gan), on Inazuma's Tsurumi Island, under Sumeru's Eternal Oasis and Mt. Damavand, in Natlan, and at Hyperborea in Snezhnaya. All look like broken crystalline pillars that disturb the ley lines and the weather where they land, and Nahida has said they can purify and stabilise powers that should not exist in Teyvat. Withered trees like Dragonspine's lie near several of them. No nail is known in Fontaine, and no Anomaly mark appeared there in 7.1.
### ULTRA +
In 7.1 Ronova, stripped of her authority and dying, fused the Eitr with Hyperborea's nail and it crumbled. The other nails then threw a strange pillar-like mark into the sky. Snezhnaya's mark can be seen from Windrest Peak in northern Mondstadt, and Liyue's from Mondstadt City. Zhongli and Mavuika reacted with visible alarm, and Albedo kept watch over the Skyfrost Nail.

## entry: The Riven Pillar and Its Shards
@keys: Skyfrost Nail shard, Skyfrost Nail shards, pillar that fell from the heavens
@keys2: Dragonspine, Ancient Rime, Frostbearing Tree, Starglow Cavern
@version: 1.2
### LARGE
After its fall the nail split in three. The fragments, frozen in Ancient Rime, lay at the Frostbearing Tree, beneath the Entombed City outskirts and in Starglow Cavern, each surrounded by blue plants. Fire cannot melt that ice, but the warmth of Dragonspine's Scarlet Quartz crystals can. Each shard freed by the Traveler flew back to the summit and refroze around the main pillar until it was thawed again and rejoined, after which the whole nail rose into the air.
