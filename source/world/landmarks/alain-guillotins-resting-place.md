## summary
### LITE
A small cave in Fontaine, just north of Chemin de L'Espoir and a little above the entrance to Annapausis, holds the grave of Alain Guillotin, the founding director of the Research Institute of Kinetic Energy Engineering and creator of Sandrone. It is reached through the story quest "Those We Have Kept" in the Sunny Summer Fontinalia event, and stays open afterwards.
### STANDARD +
The site was added in Version 6.7 (Luna VIII).

## places
### STANDARD
The grave bears an epitaph in Fontaine script. In paraphrase it reads: here lies Alain Guillotin, Founding Director of the Research Institute of Kinetic Energy Engineering, White Cavalier of Protection, Lord-Artificer; may the waters protect and guide his soul across to the other shore, and "regrettably, there's nothing more to say."
### LARGE +
The transcribed in-world inscription differs slightly: it calls him Maitre-Artificier and Director of the Institute, a White Knight of the Grand Waters, entrusts the keeping of his soul beyond the shores to the waters, and ends with a remark that it is a pity but everything has been said. Wiki translations of the Fontaine script are interpretations.

## people
### STANDARD
Alain Guillotin was a historical figure of the cataclysm era, around 400-500 years ago: a prodigy of the Institute of Natural Philosophy, a Marechaussee Hunter, and in his later years the maker of Sandrone, who became a Fatui Harbinger, and of her attendant Pulonia. His sister Mary-Ann and his adoptive father Emanuel are Guillotins as well. See his own entry for the biography.

## status
### STANDARD
As of 7.1 the resting place is a permanent point of interest in the Court of Fontaine Region.

### LARGE
The grave's unlock belongs to the Sunny Summer Fontinalia event (6.7), so the quest is limited-time content. In its third act, "Those We Have Kept", the party (with Sandrone) deals with the ritual in the Tower of Gestalt and then goes to the Opera Epiclese and to the place Sandrone wishes to head to. The wiki ties the unlocking of the resting place to this quest but does not describe the scene at the grave.

## history
### ULTRA
Wiki accounts place Alain, his sister Mary-Ann and his Seymour automaton in a clash with the Narzissenkreuz Ordo inside the remains of Elynas, which presumably cost Mary-Ann her life; Alain had refused Narzissenkreuz's invitation to join its plan. Later he built Sandrone and Pulonia. The wiki marks this chronology as incomplete pending 6.7 updates, and how he died and came to rest in Fontaine is not stated in the pages read.
