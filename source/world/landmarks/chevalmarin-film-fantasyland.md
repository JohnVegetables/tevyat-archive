## summary
@availability: limited
### LITE
Chevalmarin Film Fantasyland is an amusement park and fan convention for film lovers in the Court of Fontaine Region. It was the idea of the engineer Lepine-Pauline and was built almost overnight by Navia and the Spina di Rosula for the limited-time Whirling Waltz event in Version 5.6. Its theme is Fontaine's film-making, and it is named for Surintendante Chevalmarin, whose image Lepine-Pauline was allowed to use by Furina.
### STANDARD +
During the event the Traveler helps run the park for Navia, choosing attractions, staff and theme dishes to raise its revenue.

## places
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
Three main attractions form the core of the park: Eclectic Escapades, Movie Masterpieces and Mellow Manor, with landscaped areas and booths built around crowd-favourite films. There is also a theme restaurant where the event's dishes are served; Escoffier is seen working at her station there.
### LARGE +
The map description stresses two audiences: film buffs who want a stage to show their passion and promote their favourite films, and visitors who know little about cinema and simply want to enjoy the sights, lights and sounds. The park's attractions are upgraded and expanded during the event, and its facilities grow with revenue.

## people
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
Lepine-Pauline, an engineer and film enthusiast, hosts the convention and gives technical support. Navia, president of the Spina di Rosula, leads the actual construction and operation. Kinich and Ajaw, Escoffier and others are met at the park during the event story.
### LARGE +
Lepine-Pauline obtained Furina's permission to use the image of her close friend Chevalmarin. Navia explains in "Business Acumen From the Spina" that the enthusiasts who proposed the park are not all-round experts and mostly design the booths, so the Spina handles long-term operations and standard strategy guides for the activities.

## governance
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
Run on the ground by the Spina di Rosula under Navia, with Lepine-Pauline as host. Lepine-Pauline says the event and venue only got their approval after long paperwork, whereupon she asked Navia for help at once.

## history
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
The park is the setting for Whirling Waltz (5.6, running 2025-05-09 to 2025-05-26). In the opening story "An Overnight Fantasyland" the Traveler and Paimon hear of it in the Court of Fontaine, learn it was raised in a single night, and are invited to join its crew. Navia then teaches the Traveler her time-slot method of planning, where each operating cycle has limited slots for upgrades, expansion and hiring.
### LARGE +
The event's reward milestones are tied to total revenue earned by the park, culminating in the weapon Sequence of Solitude and other prizes. These are game rewards, not in-world facts.
### ULTRA +
The event ran in phases from 2025-05-09, 2025-05-11 and 2025-05-13. Players needed to have progressed through the Fontaine story to run it, and the three attractions are expanded through levels, staff hires and theme dishes. In-world, Navia describes the park as a sugar-free macaron: several areas were still being built when the Traveler arrived, and the Spina had hired only a limited number of craftsmen.

## atmosphere
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
Festive and slightly chaotic: colourful film sets, crowds of fans, the smells of the theme restaurant and constant small crises of a venue assembled in a night.

## status
### STANDARD
As of 7.1 the park is an event location from 5.6. No later story uses it as a setting, and its permanent status is not documented.

## entry: Whirling Waltz Convention
@keys: Whirling Waltz, Chevalmarin Film Fantasyland, Surintendante Chevalmarin
@keys2: Lepine-Pauline, Navia, Fontaine
@availability: limited
@version: 5.6
### STANDARD
Whirling Waltz was a 5.6 flagship event in which the Traveler and Paimon, strolling the Court of Fontaine, are drawn into a film fan convention hosted at Chevalmarin Film Fantasyland. The same event story also touches on the arrival of visitors from Natlan in Fontaine. In-universe it is a one-off fan festival; it is not a permanent institution of Fontaine.
