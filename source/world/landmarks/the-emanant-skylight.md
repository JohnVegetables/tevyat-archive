## summary
### LITE
The Emanant Skylight names three places in Enkanomiya where a pool of ghostfish waits during Evernight. Gliding into the fish lifts the Traveler to a time tunnel that opens onto one of the three Locus subareas.
### STANDARD +
The fish only appear after the world quest The Three Great Martial Trials has been started, and Bathysmal Vishaps guard each pool.

## places
### STANDARD
The skylight at The Narrows leads to Yachimatahiko's Locus; the one at the Evernight Temple to Yachimatahime's Locus; the one at the Serpent's Heart to Kunado's Locus.
### LARGE +
Each skylight is guarded by a Primordial Bathysmal Vishap and two hatchlings. Ghostfish are schools that let the Traveler keep gliding if they stay inside the group.

## quests
### STANDARD
The Three Great Martial Trials starts with Eboshi (Erebos), who calls herself an auxiliary of Sangonomiya, one of Enkanomiya's shrine maidens. She says the older trial to win the branch was meant to keep the full truth from later generations, whereas she wanted records kept, so Watatsumi Omikami let her add a path leading to the secrets. Starting the quest reveals the three Locus subareas.
### LARGE +
Eboshi says that, because of the Sinshades phenomenon, Enkanomiya's past would persist on its own, so Sangonomiya worked to make the people forget; she disagreed and the two settled the matter with Omikami's mediation. Her clue refers to gazing upon the borders of Byakuyakoku in Evernight.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Dim ceiling openings pouring light into dark cavern waters, with fish that rise like a stairway of ghosts.

## history
@availability: limited
### ULTRA
During the limited-time Three Realms Gateway Offering event (2.5), the three skylight tunnels became available once the event area was unlocked, without needing the trials quest.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the three skylights remain tied to The Three Great Martial Trials, and nothing in 6.x or 7.0-7.1 content changes them.
