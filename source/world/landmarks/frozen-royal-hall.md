## summary
### LITE
The Frozen Royal Hall was the Belyi Tsar's throne room in the Pale Crown Palace. It still holds what the Eye of Graeae calls the very first Kresnik's Torch, a prototype that can be re-lit. The Traveler enters it in the quest The Stone Beasts, Like Ghouls Seated while looking for a fragment of the Eye's power.
### STANDARD +
The room is also guarded: lighting the torch wakes Zver, the Tsar's beast, which sends man-made chimeric monsters against intruders.

## geography
### STANDARD
A hall behind a snow-buried gate, reached by a side route the Eye of Graeae identifies. A door inside the palace leads into the hall, and a statue must be returned to its place to open it. Inside there is a throne with a barrier in front of it, a prototype Kresnik's Torch, and a door behind the throne that stays shut until two stone statues of a two-headed beast, the guardian of the Tsar's corpse, are returned to a mechanism before the throne.
### LARGE +
A tutorial for the "Prototype Kresnik's Torch" appears in this room. The torch's start mechanism no longer works, but a knock on its crystal component triggers an emergency startup. Later parts of the quest lead up a Suspended Tower and an elevator to Zver and on to the ruined Hvergelmir.

## history
### STANDARD
The Eye of Graeae says the torch is the first of its kind. The wiki's Kresnik's Torch page says the Belyi Tsar created the torches to help humans survive Snezhnaya's cold. When the Traveler lights it, a voice swears loyalty in the name of Zver and the guards attack. Once the statues are in position, a flashback plays: Aksinya reports to the Tsar that the first experiments are complete, that test subjects entered a realm of boundless joy, and that none woke up again; the Tsar asks if this is the world she dreams of, and Aksinya answers that his will is hers. He leaves for the front, saying Zver will go with him.
### LARGE +
Notes by a Certain Noble Individual (I), found here, are credited by the wiki to the Tsar. The author writes of a passage that has opened at a heavy price, of the Shadow Realm being the ruins of Hyperborea, a lost homeland he has long sought, and of a long-time acquaintance he found already present there. The note does not name him.
### ULTRA +
After the memory the Traveler faces Zver, who is addressed as one who "swore loyalty to eternity", then goes on to the ruined Hvergelmir and obtains the part of the Eye of Graeae's body there. Later quests use the Court of Eternal Bliss behind the same palace.

## people
### STANDARD
No named residents. Zver, the Tsar's beast, appears at the end of the sequence. Aksinya and the Tsar are seen only in the flashback.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
A cold, grand and long-silent hall: ice over a throne, shut doors and carved beast statues, a torch that once warmed a court. The wiki shows two states, a cold one and a warm one after the torch is lit.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Lighting the torch summons Chimeric Winged Elephants and other man-made monsters, the "stone beasts" of the quest title. The route up involves ice puzzles and more of the same enemies.

## quests
### STANDARD
The Stone Beasts, Like Ghouls Seated.

## naming

## status
### STANDARD
As of 7.1 the hall is still frozen apart from the torch the Traveler lit. No later content in the sources changes it.
