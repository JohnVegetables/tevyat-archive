## summary
### LITE
The Garden Bathed in Radiant Dew is a garden at the House of Hesperides in Fellfrost Peak, kept alive amid the blizzard. It thrived under the care of Larisa, a Snegurochka who died during her own wedding, and its great tree has borne no fruit since. A fragment of the Eye of Graeae's power is hidden in that tree.
### STANDARD +
The Snegovik guard Ladon keeps visitors out, saying the garden is under maintenance and open only to fae helpers.

## geography
### STANDARD
A garden behind a guarded entrance, with a large tree at its centre. Prime Ice constructs stand among the plants and block the line of sight of the Jack Frosts who patrol it, which the Eye of Graeae points out the Traveler can exploit.
### LARGE +
The garden can be viewed from outside, and the guard invites visitors to look from the entrance and tell him stories instead of entering.

## people
### STANDARD
Ladon, a Snegovik, stands guard at the entrance and is friendly and talkative. Jack Frosts work inside as helpers. Larisa, who is dead, used to come and do work there.
### LARGE +
Ladon says that when Larisa was alive the big tree bore the biggest and most beautiful fruit. He has tried everything since her death and nothing makes it bear fruit again.

## history
### STANDARD
In the quest The Sun's Gilded Apple, the Eye of Graeae senses that a fragment of its power lies in the great tree. Paimon keeps Ladon talking while the Traveler sneaks around to the garden, avoids the Jack Frosts and takes the fragment (a Severed Fragment of Ikhor) from the tree.
### LARGE +
Larisa is the Snegurochka who tended the plants and who, according to the wiki's profile, raised a blizzard in her final hours; the garden stands in that blizzard. The wiki gives her as a candidate for Grand Duchess whom her kin considered the most pure-minded among them.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
A hushed, carefully tended place that still carries the mark of the person who loved it; the guard is gentle and nostalgic rather than hostile.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Jack Frosts patrol the grounds, and getting in takes stealth because Ladon will not allow visitors. If the timer in the quest runs out before the fragment is taken, the quest returns to its first step.

## quests
### STANDARD
The Sun's Gilded Apple.

## naming
### ULTRA
Chinese 盛光如露的花园 (Shengguang Rulu de Huayuan, "garden whose abundant light is like dew"), Japanese 光に満ちた庭園, French Jardin baigné de rosée radieuse, German Garten des Tauglanzes.

## status
### STANDARD
As of 7.1 the garden is closed to the public for maintenance and the Traveler has taken the fragment from the tree.
