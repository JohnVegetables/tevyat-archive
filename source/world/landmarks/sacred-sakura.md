## summary
### LITE
The Sacred Sakura is the divine tree that towers over the Grand Narukami Shrine at the top of Mt. Yougou on Narukami Island. It stands for the Electro Archon's protection of Inazuma: its roots purify the land's ley lines and keep Abyssal corruption from invading, and the sakura blossoms across the island have all fallen from it. Because filth builds up in its roots, the Grand Narukami Shrine must perform the Sacred Sakura Cleansing Ritual from time to time.
### STANDARD +
Its offshoots, the Thunder Sakura, are found all over the land, and it is said that its protection reaches wherever its petals come to rest. The people of Inazuma remember it as having always been there, though its origin is stranger than that.

## geography
### STANDARD
The tree rises over the Grand Narukami Shrine at the top of Mt. Yougou on Narukami Island. Its roots spread under the whole land, and the Thunder Sakura grafted from its side branches are connected to the same single root system. Beside the mountain at its foot is a large pool of Electrified Water that drains health until the cleansing is done.
### LARGE +
The Depths of Mt. Yougou beneath the tree is where the tree's accumulated filth gathers into a Miasmic Tumor when the major cleansing lifts the barrier around the central roots. After the Cleansing Ritual of the Traveler and Hanachirusato, the pool is de-energised and the tree glows a soft blue that can be seen from the mainland.

## history
### STANDARD
Inazuman tradition holds that the Sacred Sakura cleanses the land of impurity through the Thunder Sakura, defending earth and water alike. Records say the Thunder Sakura almost withered several hundred years ago, when monsters raged and the seas around Inazuma turned black; someone eventually performed the Cleansing Ritual and the crisis ended. The ritual has a minor form, done often to check the roots and set up protective barriers, and a major form needed every sixty years: first the Thunder Sakura roots are purified, then the central roots of the Sacred Sakura.
### LARGE +
According to the ritual summary, the performers beg forgiveness from both the Narukami and the Watatsumi shrines before beginning, which may suggest that Orobashi's people once shared in the tree's worship. The most recent cleansing was carried out by the Traveler with Hanachirusato; the Grand Narukami Shrine had been preparing the ritual, but Yae Miko called it off and had the Memento Lens ready for a blonde-haired outlander.
### ULTRA +
The Raiden Shogun's story quest reveals the tree's origin. It was planted from a seed that Makoto gave Ei after Ei's duel against the Shogun inside Makoto's realm of consciousness in the Musou Isshin; within that realm the seed took root retroactively in Inazuma's past, so it grew thousands of years ago, even before the flood that divided the islands. Makoto left a portion of her consciousness in the Musou Isshin before dying in Khaenri'ah during the cataclysm 500 years ago. Ei, whose memory was unaltered, came back from Khaenri'ah to find a huge sakura on Mt. Yougou that everyone insisted had always been there, and she failed to convince Yae Miko of any link to Makoto. Yae also says the tree owes its present fox-like shape to centuries of care from kitsune.

## quests
### STANDARD
The Sacred Sakura Cleansing Ritual is a World Quest series that begins at Konda Village. The Traveler meets the masked shrine maiden Hanachirusato, lifts barriers with wards, clears the Thunder Sakura roots at Araumi, the Kamisato Estate and Chinju Forest, then breaks the barrier at the Depths of Mt. Yougou and purges the Miasmic Tumor there. Hanachirusato then reveals she is filth condensed from the memory of the Kitsune Saiguu, and must be cleansed with the rest.
### LARGE +
As a game mechanic, offering Electro Sigils to the Sacred Sakura raises Sacred Sakura's Favor, which unlocks rewards and upgrades Electrograna. In the Raiden Shogun's story quest, the quests Cleansing Light and Radiant Sakura revolve around the tree's connection to Makoto.

### LARGE
A towering tree whose roots run through the whole land, quiet and sacred, glowing blue after the cleansing; shrine bells and falling petals around a place felt to be watching over the islands.

## entry: Sacred Sakura Cleansing Ritual
@keys: Sacred Sakura Cleansing Ritual, Cleansing Ritual, Hanachirusato, Miasmic Tumor
@keys2: Sacred Sakura, Thunder Sakura, Mt. Yougou
@version: 2.0
### STANDARD
The ritual keeps the connected roots of the Sacred Sakura and the Thunder Sakura clean. The Grand Narukami Shrine ordinarily performs it. Minor cleansings check the roots and raise barriers that protect the roots from malicious people such as the Fatui and protect the people from leaking filth. The major cleansing, due every 60 years, breaks those barriers with wards so the filth is driven out and condenses into forms: harmless ones such as the tofu-like figures in peaceful times, dangerous ones in times of strife. At the central roots the filth condenses into a Miasmic Tumor.
### LARGE +
If too much filth accumulates the roots are corroded, the tainted parts form tumors, and left alone the tree withers, which would be catastrophic for Inazuma.

## entry: The Thunder Sakura
@keys: Thunder Sakura, Thunder Sakura Bough, Sakura Bloom
@keys2: Sacred Sakura, Inazuma
### STANDARD
The Thunder Sakura are trees grafted from side branches of the Sacred Sakura; they absorb filth into their roots to purify the land. Their roots connect to the central tree's, so pollution spreads between them. Sakura Bloom, a local specialty of Narukami Island, comes from the Sacred Sakura.

## entry: Origins and Makoto's Seed
@keys: Makoto's seed, Radiant Sakura, Musou Isshin, Realm of Consciousness
@keys2: Sacred Sakura, Ei, Raiden Makoto
@spoiler: major
### ULTRA
The Sacred Sakura is described in the Raiden Shogun's story quest as the product of a seed that Makoto gave to Ei in a realm of consciousness inside the Musou Isshin. Ei speculates that Makoto's plan involved Istaroth. One of the Raiden Shogun's voice-overs says Makoto became the Sacred Sakura as a form of Eternity. The version 2.0 Special Program says the tree was designed on the himorogi (sacred tree) idea and on the quaking aspen, whose many trees are one root system.
