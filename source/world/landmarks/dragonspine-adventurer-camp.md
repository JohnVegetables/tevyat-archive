## summary
### LITE
A small adventurers' camp on the outskirts of Dragonspine, on the path from Dadaupa Gorge up toward the snowbound mountain. The chef Harris built it so adventurers would have somewhere to rest and a hot bowl of soup after a hard day in the cold.
### STANDARD +
Besides Harris's cooking, the camp has a weaponsmith, Orban, and several other residents. It is the practical last stop before the mountain, not a settlement with any government of its own.

## people
### STANDARD
Harris is the chef and founder; he sells hot dishes daily and says he came to help local adventurers. He credits a young Liyue chef he met while travelling (the wiki identifies her as Xiangling) with teaching him that cooking should bring people joy and satisfaction. Orban is the weaponsmith. Other named residents are the adventurer Iris, Joel, Joserf, and the researcher Stevens.
### LARGE +
None of the residents has a detailed canon biography; they are open-world NPCs with short dialogue, so keep their personalities light. Harris is warm and hearty: he greets frozen visitors with an offer of hot food and consoles a losing card-game opponent with soup.

## places
### STANDARD
A cooking stall run by Harris and a forge run by Orban. The Alchemist's Crafting Bench sits beside Orban.
### LARGE +
The bench is tied to the Dragonteeth gathered on Dragonspine: Orban found the material could not be worked with ordinary tools, and after consulting Albedo (through Sucrose) the camp received a Runestone that holds great vitality, in Orban's account a relic left to the Knights of Favonius by a wandering troupe that helped Mondstadt against its old nobility about a thousand years ago. The method as told is to charge one Dragontooth from the Runestone and use it to treat others so they can be forged. This becomes available only after the quests Paralogism (5.6) and The Festering Fang.

## travel
### STANDARD
The camp lies at the foot of Dragonspine, reached from Dadaupa Gorge in Windwail Highland. Beyond it the road climbs into the permanent snow, where warmth and supplies matter.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Cold air, a cooking fire and the smell of soup against the white of the mountain; adventurers in thick coats stamping snow from their boots. Practical and friendly rather than grand.

## status
### STANDARD
As of 7.1 the camp is open and running. Nothing in canon through 7.1 reports it damaged or closed.

### ULTRA +
The camp's roster is as listed on the wiki and no resident is reported to have left. Whether Harris' camp predates the Knights' use of Dragonspine, or how it relates to Albedo's own campsite higher on the mountain, is not stated in canon.
