## summary
### LITE
The Fountain of Lucine stands in front of the Opera Epiclese in the Court of Fontaine. Fontainians say all of the nation's water ultimately gathers there, and it is the origin of the habit of tossing coins into fountains to make wishes. It has listened to Fontainians' wishes for a very long time.
### STANDARD +
In play it is also an offering system: Hydro Sigils gathered across Fontaine can be thrown into it to raise its level and earn rewards, and at the top level extra streams of water rise from its six corners.

## geography
### STANDARD
A large ornamental fountain on the plaza before the Opera Epiclese, in the Court of Fontaine Region. A water-themed inscription is carved on it; in paraphrase it asks that no spring or fountain ever run dry and that the torrent of life wash over the valley. Rainbow Rose and Romaritime Flower grow nearby as local specialties.
### LARGE +
Ordinary Fontainians gather around it: the wiki lists a bard, a light novelist, a reporter, a traveling scholar and a member of the Gardes among the NPCs found here. Fully raised through offerings, the main jet grows stronger and six side streams run from its corners.

## culture
### STANDARD
Fontainians throw coins into fountains to make wishes, a custom a loading-screen text says truly began with tossing Hydro Sigils into this fountain, which it calls the correct way to wish. Newlyweds traditionally wish for children here, according to the Archon Quest chapter set in the city.
### LARGE +
Long ago wishing at the fountain was a ritual for Oceanids who longed for human life: blessed by the spring water, they would become human in the months after. This is told in the Finale of the Fontaine Archon Quest.

## history
### LARGE
The Fontaine Archon Quest treats the fountain as the point where all water in Fontaine converges, even the water of tears. When people dissolved from contact with Primordial Sea water (Vigneire and the victims of the disappearance case), their consciousnesses also gathered here and formed an Oceanid. People sensitive to Hydro can hear it, and drinking Water from the Primordial Sea let the Traveler and Vacher meet it in the fountain. It had enough psychic force to nearly "drown" Vacher in his confrontation with it, after which he lost his mind and soon died of a panic attack.
### ULTRA +
After the quest "As Light Rain Falls Without Reason", Furina's crying voice, wondering how much longer this loneliness must last, can be heard from the fountain. HoYoLAB writing cited by the wiki says this came from Furina's thoughts during "The Opera of Noirceur and Blancheur"; once that quest is complete the voice is gone.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Splashing water, coins and Hydro Sigils glinting at the bottom, the Opera's steps behind it and the constant movement of Fontainians who come to wish, rest, perform or chat.

## status
### STANDARD
As of 7.1 the fountain is intact and in ordinary use before the Opera Epiclese.
