## summary
### LITE
Mary-Ann Memorial Park is a small, once scenic park just south of the Central Laboratory Ruins, in the area of Fontaine given over to the Fontaine Research Institute. Most of its buildings were destroyed by the explosion that levelled the old Institute. Beneath its shallow pond lies Bossuet's Abandoned Laboratory.
### STANDARD +
The park is where researchers pick through surviving papers in the quest "Fontaine Research Institute, Stagnating in the Rubble" and where malfunctioning meka attack in "A Letter".

## geography
### STANDARD
Lies directly south of the Central Laboratory Ruins in the Fontaine Research Institute of Kinetic Energy Engineering Region. It is a park laid out around a shallow pond, with broken machines, pipes, canvas sheets and storage drums strewn across it. Bossuet's Abandoned Laboratory is under the pond.
### LARGE +
Nacker explains that the park was hit by the first shock waves of the explosion, which is why most of its structures are gone. The surviving debris includes wrecked equipment, old copies of the newspaper The Steambird and financial reports that are long out of date.

## people
### STANDARD
Nacker, a former senior researcher who survived the disaster, leads the party through the park in the quest, together with the reporter Chronie and the Traveler. Chronie notes that before "Edwin's big screw-up" Nacker had been second only to Edwin Eastinghouse at the Institute.
### LARGE +
Nacker answers that nothing like his past status remains; he calls himself someone lucky to have survived the project and one of the culprits who turned the Central Laboratory into what it is. His job now is to retrieve assets, and he rates his own worth by that work.

## history
### STANDARD
The park's own origins are not told. It was damaged in the Institute explosion; the quest of 4.1 sends Nacker, Chronie and the Traveler to look for paper assets that may have survived. They find mostly detritus, plus one set of experimental results for a miniaturized personal recording device that Nacker expects will interest Maillardet, who has long wanted tiny gramophones inside Artificed Dancers.
### LARGE +
The wiki suggests the park was dedicated to Mary-Ann Guillotin, sister of the Institute's founder Alain Guillotin. This is an inference, not a stated fact.

## quests
### STANDARD
"Fontaine Research Institute, Stagnating in the Rubble" (Fontaine Research Institute Chronicles, 4.1) passes through the park while recovering assets. "A Letter" (4.1) sends the Traveler here to defeat three malfunctioning Recon Log Mek - Ousia before heading to the Academic Assembly Hall. The Daily Commission "Ardent Calibration" (4.1 to 4.2 per the wiki) is also set here.
### LARGE +
Chronie loses her bag to a strange-looking meka in the park, and the Traveler defeats it; she asks why such meka move at times and stop at others. Nacker says anyone who might answer is indisposed.
### ULTRA +
"A Letter" begins at the New Fontaine Research Institute, where Choiseul refuses to hand a document to Iaune of the Maison Gestion because the letter delivery regulations forbid it. Iaune, whom the Traveler once helped, thanks them; Choiseul notes that the Traveler is an Honorary Senior Researcher of the Institute. The Traveler is sent to the park to clear malfunctioning meka and then photographs the Academic Assembly Hall for Iaune.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Malfunctioning clockwork meka, such as Recon Log Meks, roam the ruined grounds.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Half a park and half a ruin: a pond and plants beside twisted pipes and crates, with a stillness left behind by the blast.

## status
### STANDARD
As of 7.1 the park remains ruined and can be explored in the game. No rebuilding of the park or of the Central Laboratory is documented.
