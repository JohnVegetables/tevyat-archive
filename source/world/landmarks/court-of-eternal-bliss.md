## summary
### LITE
The Court of Eternal Bliss, also called the Court of Bliss, is a chamber in the Pale Crown Palace where the Belyi Tsar's body lies sealed. Aksinya, the Tsar's former court jester, wanted to use it for a rite that would complete the Primordial Human and bring about a "Courtyard of Carefree Light". The Traveler stopped that rite here in the quest An Ark That Used to Be a Soul.
### STANDARD +
Entering it is the climax of the world-quest chain that began at the ruined Hvergelmir, and it contains the Tsar's White Birch, the living remainder of the Belyi Tsar's body.

## geography
### STANDARD
The entrance is a door on the upper level of the ruined Hvergelmir, within the Pale Crown Palace. It is blocked by the Tsar's White Birch and cannot be opened until all three fragments of the Eye of Graeae's power have been recovered, which means finishing the world-quest series Hesperides of Love and Hate, In the Dwelling of Life and On One Side a Palace, On the Other a Tomb. The Traveler then performs a return ceremony, spending Kresnik Energy to open it. Inside is a cold courtyard under the Tsar's White Birch.
### LARGE +
Aksinya describes the White Birch as the ever-growing nerves of the Tsar's husk, a shell that never truly perished. The chamber also holds the Tsar's original body, which the Traveler first sees beneath the birch as a huge mechanical-looking form, and a portal from it leads into a Shadow Realm arena where a train carries the party.

## history
### STANDARD
After the fragments are gathered, the Eye of Graeae vanishes as the door is opened and Aksinya appears. She tells the Traveler that the Eye was built to host the "Proto-Man" and that its body and power are to be assimilated into the Belyi Tsar's body so that he can become the Primordial Human. She teleports away, the party chases her through the Shadow Realm, and in the court the Tsar's body begins to transform. Aksinya returns the Eye as an empty shell and says its consciousness is now the core of the Primordial Human. The Primordial Human wakes, claims to remember everything and asks to be stopped. After a boss fight the door to the new world is shut and the being that remains has the appearance of the Tsar in his youth.
### LARGE +
Before the fight the Primordial Human asks the Traveler what they would do if their sibling had become someone else. The Traveler answers that they would learn to love them again. Afterwards Aksinya and the Primordial Human fall together into the Shadow Realm, and Aksinya says she was freed from it earlier by Izmaylov's help. The Primordial Human admits he incited Koshchei, Pavlina and Alvis to divide him up and betray Aksinya, because he wanted to avoid becoming the Primordial Human.
### ULTRA +
The Primordial Human says he had tried to persuade Koshchei, Pavlina and Alvis to stop Aksinya before her final experiment, since the authority of the Primordial Human could work as poison to beings with mixed desires. Aksinya says she would not herself be allowed into the new world because she lost faith in love. In a flashback at the Frozen Royal Hall she reports to the Tsar that early test subjects entered a realm of boundless joy and never woke up. The Court of Bliss as a world is described in Aksinya's words as one where choice loses meaning and where even the Abyss would become lovable. The boss fight ends with the Eye of Graeae's final protocol, which builds a path of Prime Ice cubes.

## people
### STANDARD
Aksinya, the Tsar's court jester and creator of the Eye of Graeae, is the antagonist. The Primordial Human, the Eye of Graeae, Paimon and the Traveler are the other participants. The Belyi Tsar is present only as a body and as the form the Primordial Human takes.
### LARGE +
Koshchei, Pavlina and Alvis, whom Aksinya counted as allies, have already been stopped or have turned against her before the court is entered.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
A frozen courtyard that feels like a tomb and a temple at once, dominated by the white birch growing out of the Tsar's body. The mood is grief and finality rather than menace: Aksinya speaks sadly and politely, and the conflict is between two ideas of love.

## dangers
### STANDARD
The fight is against Aksinya and then the Primordial Human. During the Shadow Realm chase the Eye of Graeae's gadget is removed from the Traveler, so the Cold accumulation guard is gone and exposure builds quickly (a gameplay detail of the quest).

## quests
### STANDARD
Only one quest uses it, An Ark That Used to Be a Soul, which is the first and only quest of the chapter Her Palace Collapses Into the Blizzard.

## naming
### ULTRA
Chinese 永恒无忧的庭院 (Yongheng Wuyou de Tingyuan, "garden without worry for all time"), Japanese 憂いなき永遠の庭園, French Cour de félicité éternelle, German Garten ohne Sorgen in alle Ewigkeit. The map level is labelled "Once, The Court of Bliss". Aksinya in dialogue calls it the Courtyard of Carefree Light.

## status
### STANDARD
As of 7.1 the rite has been stopped and the Court of Bliss as a world has not been created. The sources do not show it changing in 7.1.
