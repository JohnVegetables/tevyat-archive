## summary
### LITE
The Old Core of Chu'ulel is a set of underground chambers beneath Ochkanatlan in Natlan. A giant dragon statue stands within, where Och-Kan trapped the Dragonlord Ix Uxul Tz'ib Bolon Ch'ule-L after seizing power in Ochkanatlan.
### STANDARD +
In the world quest Open Your Heart to Me (5.2), begun by placing the Ancient Flint Key in a small dragon statue, the Traveler and Paimon descend through the ruins and free the Dragonlord's soul from its imprisonment. It is a different place from the Core of Chu'ulel.
### LARGE +
The levels are named Winding Ancient Road, Buried Underground Ruins, Where the Ancient Race of Yore Ruled, Ancient Ruins Walkway and Chu'ulel Mechanism's Heart. Notices on the walls present the digging as work for an alliance against dragon oppression.
### ULTRA +
After the quest, placing the Ancient Flint Key on a table in the newly opened chamber creates a spiritway out of the Old Core.

## geography
### STANDARD
A multi-level descent: a winding road, a flooded work site with a waterfall, a hall of the ancient race and the mechanism heart around the statue. Abyssal energy blocks the entrance until the Traveler gains Cocouik's power.
### LARGE +
Water pumps, drainage ditches and a control lever show that the builders were trying to drain flooded caverns.

## people
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Texts name Och-Kan with the titles Guardian of the Sacred Flame and Great Peacemaker, and mention the artisans Mahutzintl and Tlanalco and the workers Manqu and Sayani. Ixlel is a figure the Traveler meets in the quest.
### LARGE +
A joint declaration naming Nanatzcayan, Meztli, Huitztlan and Teteocan seals the cursed land from all members of the alliance, who would be recognised as enemies if they entered.

## history
@version: 5.2
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Bulletins show forced labour under Och-Kan: rotating shifts of sixteen hours, extra grain for good teams, rations suspended for slow artisans, and deaths from falls and sickness. The texts frame the effort as being for the alliance against the dragons.
### LARGE +
One artisan's memo records dread: a worker went mad claiming to have seen a swollen black silhouette in the deep water, and strange sounds came from the walls. A later memo says the ditches were completed, a curse against Och-Kan was inscribed by the craftsman Manqu, and the laborers who knew the secrets of the Secret Source Automatons were to be burned alive. One writer survived the flames and recorded meeting a young woman there.
### ULTRA +
The survivor writes that the woman wept golden tears and showed visions of a vast structure ringed by metal birds, of shadows climbing to the moon and of a foe as great as a world. The wiki links her to Ixlel; the memo does not name her, so treat the vision as the narrator's testimony.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Tenebrous Mimiflora, Secret Source Automaton hunter-seekers and Abyssal energy appear during the quest.

## quests
@version: 5.2
### STANDARD
Open Your Heart to Me: reach the depths, deliver heart fragments to Ixlel, free her, evade a Secret Source Sentinel attack and take the Perfect Heart of the Sacred Dragon. Completing it unlocks the achievement Devoted to Eternal Serenity.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Damp, dark and oppressive: fungus-slick walls, grim bulletins and the sense of something huge in the water below.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the chambers are open and the Dragonlord's soul has been freed. No later changes were found.
