## summary
### LITE
"A Lonely Place" is a named subarea in the northern zone of Elynas, the colossal dead beast whose remains form much of the Beryl Region of Fontaine. It is where the Traveler sets out in the Ancient Colors quest "Limner, Dreamer, and Robotic Dog" to follow Jakob toward the heart of Elynas, and it is also the area listed for the world quest "The Lone Phantom Sail".
### STANDARD +
It is a map label rather than a settlement: no one is recorded as living in it, and the Melusine village of Merusea is a separate place.

## geography
### STANDARD
Part of the Beryl Region, on the map level "Within Elynas: Northern Zone". Elynas' skeleton has become one with the mountains and lakes of the region, so this subarea belongs to the creature's body as much as to the landscape. The route onward to "A Very Warm Place", where Elynas' heart lies, runs through water.
### LARGE +
In "Limner, Dreamer, and Robotic Dog" a portal in this area is found blocked, and Seymour cannot bore through the rock wall because it is too thick for his output. Mamere then urges swimming through a water current instead, and the party reaches "A Very Warm Place" that way. Paimon remarks that the currents used to flow in the opposite direction.

## quests
### STANDARD
Two world quests are tied to the subarea: "Limner, Dreamer, and Robotic Dog" (Ancient Colors Act III, starting here; it leads to the confrontation with Jakob and the meeting with Elynas) and "The Lone Phantom Sail" (the enthusiast Ahes searches for the phantom of a white ship, the Sponsian).
### LARGE +
"The Lone Phantom Sail" requires the earlier quest "Hey, This Isn't Pumpkin Soup...". Ahes signals with an old military conch, the party follows a ghostly half-ship, fights Rifthound packs near a wreck and ends by investigating a strange item there. Ahes treats the Sponsian as Admiral Basil Elton's flagship, of which only half the hull was left behind.
### ULTRA +
The Ancient Colors quest ends here in a fight. After Jakob springs a triangular barrier around the Traveler and sets Rifthounds on the party, Seymour, fighting as a Recon Log Mek in Pneuma mode, says nothing he can do will breach the dome; a Shatterstone Breacher Primus then attacks the barrier and breaks it. Mamere seems to hear "Father" at that moment and obeys a request to paint the Traveler and Paimon, which carries the party onward to the meeting with Elynas.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Quiet, sparse and a little eerie: water, rock and the sense of standing inside something enormous. During "Limner, Dreamer, and Robotic Dog" the view is deliberately hazed and pulled back (a gameplay effect), matching the creeping contamination Seymour warns about.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Rifthounds appear in the related quests, together with the "contamination" Seymour reports as the party nears Elynas' heart. Jakob, by then revealed as an Iniquitous Baptist, waits at the end of the route in the quest.

## status
### STANDARD
As of 7.1 the subarea is simply open for exploration. Nothing later than the 4.0 quests is recorded for it.

## entry: The Lone Phantom Sail
@keys: Lone Phantom Sail, Sponsian, Ahes
@keys2: Elynas, Beryl Region, shipwreck, conch
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
Ahes is an enthusiast who haunts the waters near A Lonely Place picking over a shipwreck and a tattered logbook said to belong to Admiral Basil Elton's white ironclad, the Sponsian. She tunes an old military conch to send signals and believes the ship answers her. With Paimon and the Traveler she follows a phantom half-ship to its wreck; the quest closes on a strange item found there. Whether the Sponsian truly survives is left to Ahes' own conviction.
