## summary
### LITE
Silvermoon Hall is a moon-themed cavern on Hiisi Island in Nod-Krai, the hiding place of Columbina after she left the Fatui. The player first enters it during Reunion Beneath the Moonlight, in the Archon Quest chapter Song of the Welkin Moon. While inside, only the Traveler can be controlled and Co-Op Mode is disabled.
### STANDARD +
The map description reads: the new moon always listens to the wishes of the living, laughter or tears, joy or sorrow; one wonders whether the new moon has a wish of its own.

## geography
### STANDARD
A cavern on Hiisi Island whose viewpoint is the Moon-Bathed Courtyard. The local specialty is Moonfall Silver. Columbina and the Record-Keeping Kuuhenki are the resident NPCs.
### LARGE +
If the player enters with Co-Op Mode already on, the party stays unchanged and any playable character can be used.

## history
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Silvermoon Hall appears in the Archon Quest after Columbina leaves the Fatui; it becomes the place the Traveler visits to meet her. Its reputation system, Silvermoon Hall: Selenic Chronicles, is unlocked by completing The Dark Side of Memory.
### LARGE +
Selenic Chronicles is a series of quests (the wiki lists at least thirteen parts), each of which can be taken seven daily resets after the last. Finishing one grants the New Moon's Embrace effect until the seventh reset, setting the party's Moonsign Level to Ascendant Gleam in Nod-Krai. The quests become unavailable once A Nocturne of the Far North starts, which starts automatically after Act IV (An Elegy for Faded Moonlight), and return after True Moon. One of them, The Homebound Moon (6.3), ends with a visit to the Idol of the New Moon.

## people
### STANDARD
Columbina is hiding here. The Record-Keeping Kuuhenki lets the player view the Selenic Chronicles records.

## culture
### LARGE
In The Homebound Moon, Columbina is found on a makeshift bench enjoying a lakkaberry pie and Today's Boon by herself.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Moonlit and calm, with music in which Columbina's own singing seems to be part of the scene; the vocal track goes silent when she is silent.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the Hall remains Columbina's hiding place and is visited through the Selenic Chronicles quests.

## entry: Silvermoon Hall: Selenic Chronicles
@keys: Selenic Chronicles, Record-Keeping Kuuhenki, New Moon's Embrace
@keys2: Silvermoon Hall, Columbina
### ULTRA
The Selenic Chronicles is Columbina's reputation system in Silvermoon Hall: quests named for her (for example The Artist By the Moon's Side in several parts, Moonlight Sonata, A Toast Beneath the Moon, Come Play With the Moon) interleaved with rewards. Quests unlock a week apart. The New Moon's Embrace effect cannot be disabled.
