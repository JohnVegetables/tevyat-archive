## summary
### LITE
Lamb-Devourer Rock is a desert rock formation in the Land of Upper Setekh, in the Great Red Sand of Sumeru. It conceals the secret base of the Thutmose, a group of Eremites.
### STANDARD +
The base is reached in the world quest Hidden Mercenaries, where the Traveler follows Soheil's old notes about a place where Thutmose would appear and vanish.
### LARGE +
The quest has the Traveler learn the base's password by quietly following a patrolling Eremite, open the hidden door and search for valuables to bring back to Soheil.

## geography
### STANDARD
A rock outcrop in the Land of Upper Setekh, with a hidden door that opens to a password-triggered device. The base lies behind it.

## people
@version: 3.1
### STANDARD
The Thutmose are Eremites who are devout believers of King Deshret. Seeking the secret of Al-Ahmar, they have gathered knowledge and collaborators from many sources, from Sumeru researchers to the Fatui. Jebrael and Samail were once called the twin blades of Thutmose. This is background from the Eremites' profile, not from the quest.
### LARGE +
Paimon first assumes the hideout must be long abandoned but finds the Eremites still around, and notes that Soheil's notes say they would appear and disappear suddenly.

## quests
### STANDARD
Hidden Mercenaries, in the Old Notes and New Friends chapter (part of Soheil's Wish), begins with Aaru Village. Steps: approach the rock, enter the secret base, look for valuables, hand slates to Soheil.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Armed Eremites patrol the area; the quest uses stealth rather than direct combat.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Tense and furtive: sun-baked rock hiding a secret base, with Eremites who patrol the surroundings.

## status

No 6.x or 7.0-7.1 content was found that mentions the base. The quest's gameplay opens the hidden door by speaking a password, learned from an Eremite Axe Vanguard on patrol.
