## summary
### LITE
The Tonatiuh, also called the Skyserpent Ship, is a floating, dragon-built vessel high above the clouds over Ochkanatlan in Natlan. Legend made it Och-Kan's treasure ship, full of riches and containing a Jade of Return that could quell the Abyss, and adventurers chased it for generations. In fact it is a bleak, wind-scoured structure of islands and tangled lines, and the 'Jade' is a Golden Entreaty that Och-Kan hid there; Kukulkan invented the legend as a lure. As of 7.1 it is empty.
### STANDARD +
It is reached by placing Pyrophosphorite on the Altar of Primal Flame in Ochkanatlan, which raises a guiding wind. The ship is a spoiler-heavy location for the Lost Traveler in the Ashen Realm quest chapter (5.2).

## geography
### STANDARD
The Tonatiuh hangs above the sea of clouds over Ochkanatlan. From below it looks to Paimon like a vast ball of yarn or tangled snakes rather than a ship; on arrival it is a desolate scatter of floating islands and sand-covered structures, with Pyrophosphorite everywhere and an old mural on the north-western island. A mechanism like an Altar of Primal Flame stands on the ship; special flame-infused Pyrophosphorite ('Firestones') found around it activates the mechanism.
### LARGE +
Gameplay note: before the quest chapter is finished the area can already be approached by characters able to gain great height through Nightsoul Transmission, and its name shows as '???' on approach.

## history
### STANDARD
According to the wiki's reading of An Omen of Annihilation and the Final Entreaty, the dragons built the ship, originally to anchor the sky-cable ('stairway') that was to climb from Chichen Uctokah into space beyond Teyvat. Och-Kan, who took Ochkanatlan, turned it on its side and used it to hide a Golden Entreaty, one half of the control hub for Huitzilopochtli. Over the years countless adventurers from Natlan went looking for the ship, and none proved it existed. Bona's father Taika, with Copan, Titu and Legba, set out on the same search and never returned; the one survivor of that squad, a young mystic called Liriwu, came back from the city alone.
### LARGE +
In Och-Kan's memories Taika's group, the Ochkanatlan Adventure Team, hopes the Jade of Return can quell the Abyss, and is later shown to have fallen victim to the trap. The trap was the work of the Sage of the Stolen Flame, Kukulkan, who made the legend part of an 'experiment'. Years later Bona reached the Tonatiuh herself with Cocouik, saw through the legend, and set out to break the trap.
### ULTRA +
Kukulkan says he donned masks, collecting the souls his son Och-Kan devoured, to test how humans respond to a legend. In Och-Kan's memory Kukulkan presses him to join the Golden Entreaties and activate Huitzilopochtli; Och-Kan answers that the Entreaty is no longer where Kukulkan expects it, and Kukulkan guesses he left it on his 'toy ship'.

## quests
### STANDARD
Lost Traveler in the Ashen Realm (Act II: A Dream of Gazing Upon the Distant Sky; Act III: The Tonatiuh Quivers). In To the Sky-Road, the Traveler, Paimon, Little One, Bona and Cocouik offer two chunks of Pyrophosphorite on the Altar and fly up. In The Other Side of the Sky they use Firestones to open a sealed rock and take out the 'Jade of Return', which turns out to be a Golden Entreaty; Bona becomes agitated and tries to warn them of a trap before the Dragon of the City of Flowing Ash seizes it.
### LARGE +
The Traveler learns the nature of the trap in Och-Kan's memories (Moment of Awakening) and later takes both Entreaties to the Heart of the Sacred Mountain. After the chapter, Bona's goggles and woven notes can be found on the north-western floating island.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Silence, wind and altitude: a lonely, sand-strewn structure among clouds, with the 'desolate' feel of a place that once held a mastermind's dream. A nameless adventurer's notes say it is nothing like they imagined, and that they could understand what the Python King felt at the end.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Falling from the floating islands; the lingering trap, now destroyed; and, in the story, the Dragon of the City of Flowing Ash, which snatches the Entreaty from those who take it.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the Tonatiuh is empty and quiet. The trap set there by the Sage of the Stolen Flame has been destroyed, and the Golden Entreaty has been recovered by the Traveler.
### STANDARD +
The goggles left behind are the only testament to the adventurer who fought the maker of the trap, and an achievement awards the interaction. No later quest in 6.x or 7.0-7.1 returns here.

## entry: The Jade of Return legend
@keys: Jade of Return, Skyserpent Ship, Tonatiuh treasure
@spoiler: major
@version: 5.2
### STANDARD
The Jade of Return is said to be a treasure aboard the Tonatiuh that would 'cleanse filth' and quiet the Abyss in Natlan. Many adventurers, including Bona's father, died or vanished looking for it. It is a fiction: the object is the Golden Entreaty that Kukulkan tells the Traveler he made into a legend, calling it a trap for the 'lions of Natlan', and the inscriptions on it say it is hazardous and should not be touched.

## entry: A Nameless Adventurer's Notes
@keys: Nameless Adventurer's Notes, woven scrolls, Goggles Left Behind by Someone
@spoiler: minor
@version: 5.2
### LARGE
A woven-scroll diary appears in pieces across Ochkanatlan and ends on the Tonatiuh. Its author describes awakening a dragonspirit from the heart of a machine and naming it Cocouik for its turquoise-green colour, following Aunt Liriwu's markings to the Altar, noticing that the legend of the Jade conceals the machine that could save Natlan, and resolving to deny the Sage's plan. Goggles beside it testify that she once existed.
