## summary
### LITE
The Dainichi Mikoshi ("Great Sun Mikoshi") is the artificial sun of Enkanomiya, the hidden realm of Inazuma. The ancient craftsman-sage Aberaku no Mikoto made it to protect the people of Byakuyakoku from the Dragonheirs of the Depths, now called Bathysmal Vishaps. When it burns the realm is in Whitenight, and when it is dark, in Evernight; mechanisms around Enkanomiya can toggle the two. The structure and the ground around it form a subarea of the realm.
### STANDARD +
Aberaku privately called his creation Helios, the solar chariot that drives Hyperion; before Inazuman Watatsumi culture took hold it was known as Hyperion (Byakuya no Hikari). Its history is not purely heroic: nobles later turned the sun into a god to be worshipped and ruled in the name of a child, the Sunchild.

## geography
### STANDARD
The Dainichi Mikoshi stands at the heart of Enkanomiya and works as its main light source, with a central teleport waypoint beneath it and hidden rooms below. Floating rocks above it let a glider reach three nearby loci without falling into the depths. Its surroundings are crowded with Ruin Guards, Hilichurl camps and Abyss mages, and a barrier blocks its approach early in The Subterranean Trials of Drake and Serpent.
### LARGE +
Some of the rocks above the Mikoshi can only be stood upon, not climbed, so reaching the loci takes real mobility. Ruins Torches near its base show as Sunfire Phantasms in Whitenight, so a visitor must switch the realm to Evernight to reveal them and open the way into the hidden room under the structure. Elemental Monuments, Seelies, Phase Gates and Rock Piles are all scattered around it.

## history
### STANDARD
When Byakuyakoku sank into the sea, the Dragonheirs of the Depths tormented its people. The Heavenly Principles and three of their four Shades did not answer the people's pleas; only Istaroth heard and taught Aberaku enough to build the Mikoshi. Its light drove the Dragonheirs away, Whitenight came, Evernight was banished, and the first year of Sun and Moon began. In the second year a cabal of corrupt nobles led the people to worship the Mikoshi as a god and installed a child puppet ruler, the Sunchild. Aberaku opposed this, was accused of treason by the Sunchild and imprisoned, and died in the tenth year of Sun and Moon. His clothes, crown and bones were enshrined in separate places, which split his soul into fragments.
### LARGE +
There were seven Sunchildren in all, the first being Rikoru, and each died young. The nobles feared that a Sunchild who grew up would see how they were being used, so a rite called the Rite of Solar Return took the child into the inner sanctum of the Mikoshi to be burned away by its heat. The Divine Bridle was the badge of the Sunchild's office. Eventually a child exploring Byakuyakoku found the fugitive god Orobashi hiding in a cave and asked it to become their god; Orobashi overthrew the Sunchildren's rule, ended the worship of the Mikoshi, and strengthened the sun's power so the realm could switch between day and night at will. Hyperion's Dirge has the Traveler gather the three scattered relics of Aberaku and reunite his soul so he can open the way to the top.
### ULTRA +
Lotus Eater adds the voice of a resistance member, an afterimage with no name, who denounces the nobles as the real rulers behind the Sunchild. The Byakuyakoku Collection preserves the account of Aberaku's craft and the origin of Whitenight in the book The Serpent and Drakes of Tokoyokoku. Under Orobashi the people worshipped the coral serpent together with Ouroboros, the scaleless imaginary serpent, instead of the sun.

## quests
### STANDARD
The Subterranean Trials of Drake and Serpent is the quest that opens Enkanomiya's deeper story and is required before Hyperion's Dirge and Lotus Eater. Hyperion's Dirge begins with Aberaku beneath the central waypoint and ends at the top of the structure. Three smaller inquiries (Narrow, Temple and Serpent's Heart) gather his relics in The Narrows, the Evernight Temple and the Serpent's Heart.
### LARGE +
Reaching the top of the structure grants the achievement Light and Dark, Dusk and Dawn, and the series rewards a Divine Bridle that is needed for The Phaethons' Syrtos.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Under Whitenight the Mikoshi is a pale, steady brilliance over a sunken realm; under Evernight it is a dim, cold shape in the dark, and the Ruins Torches glow. The place carries the weight of a monument built to save a people and then made into their master.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the Dainichi Mikoshi still serves as Enkanomiya's sun and can be toggled between Whitenight and Evernight.
### STANDARD +
No 6.x or 7.x content found on the wiki touches it. In Hyperion's Dirge the Traveler's task is to reunite the fragments of Aberaku's soul.

## entry: Whitenight and Evernight
@keys: Whitenight, Evernight, Sun and Moon, Of Sun and Moon
@keys2: Enkanomiya, Dainichi Mikoshi, Byakuyakoku
@version: 2.4
### STANDARD
Whitenight is the bright half of Enkanomiya's cycle, kept by the Dainichi Mikoshi; Evernight is its dark opposite. Their rule is that the Mikoshi can be turned on and off to switch them, using mechanisms spread through the realm that unlock as quests are completed. The two states also hide and reveal different things, such as Ruins Torches that show as Sunfire Phantasms by day. The ancients called the era after the sun's creation the years of Sun and Moon.

## entry: Aberaku and the Sunchildren
@keys: Aberaku, Abrax, Helios-Forger, Sunchild, Sunchildren, Phaethon, Rikoru
@keys2: Dainichi Mikoshi, Byakuyakoku, Enkanomiya
@version: 2.4
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
Aberaku no Mikoto, originally Abrax and titled Helios-Forger, built the Mikoshi to save his people from the Vishaps and was imprisoned by the Sunchild Rikoru after the nobles turned the Sunchild against him. His soul fragmented after death. The Sunchildren, also called Phaethons, were seven child rulers who were puppets of the nobles; the people hated them for the nobles' crimes even though they knew the children were manipulated. Aberaku's own character page is the place for his personality and later life as a Sinshade.
