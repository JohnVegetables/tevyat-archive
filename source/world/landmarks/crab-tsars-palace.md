## summary
### LITE
The Crab Tsar's Palace is an underground cavern on Hiisi Island in Nod-Krai, home to the Crab Tsar, an enormous and normally gentle crab. The Traveler reaches it through a deep pit just west of the Frostmoon Enclave while helping Maite Veliu, a self-styled master thief, hunt a cursed stone that had changed the crab's temper. Rock Crabs live in its passages.
### STANDARD +
The palace has an upper and a lower level on the map. Mirrors left behind by the Frostmoon Scions, which only they and the Crab Tsar can pass through, stand in its way. After the quest it is a calm spot with a teleport waypoint, where Maite stays and the Crab Tsar can be fought as a Local Legend.

## geography
### STANDARD
A cave system reached by a pit to the west of the Frostmoon Enclave, on Hiisi Island, the home of the Frostmoon Scions. It descends in two levels (upper and lower). Passages are blocked by Unipolar Crystonite and by crab-built obstructions, and a maze of mirrors hides the way to the crab's lair.
### LARGE +
Rock Crabs live throughout and respond to Lakkaberries, which Maite uses to coax them into opening a blocked path. The mirrors are described by Maite as left behind by the Frostmoon Scions; the Traveler must approach the centre of one while empowered with kuuvahki, which shatters it and drops the party to the crab's lair.

## people
### STANDARD
The Crab Tsar rules the palace. Maite Veliu, who presents herself as heir to the legendary thief Reed Miller, introduces herself to the party here and, later, reveals she belongs to the Conch Gang. Aila, the first Moonchanter, appears only as a name in Maite's story. A priestess in Frostmoon attire guides the party obliquely.
### LARGE +
Maite plays the master thief and insists she is no petty crook, but she admits she is really in the palace to gather intelligence for the Conch Gang, and that she cannot beat the Crab Tsar alone. She says she once nearly fell into the Frostmoon Scions' hands and was freed by a young girl after telling stories.

## history
### STANDARD
Maite says the Crab Tsar was docile until some months earlier, when it found a strange stone and began carrying it on its back; thereafter its temper turned vicious, it crushed other crabs and learned to travel through the mirrors. When the party defeats it the stone flies away; the Tsar hides, which Maite reads as regret for what it did to the little crabs.
### LARGE +
Maite compares the stone to a rock that fell from the sky near the Frostmoon Scions' home and says that if it had reached the market it could have hurt many more people. She also repeats a legend that Reed Miller once took the Frostmoon treasure back from a greedy governor, and that a stone of desire once bewitched the High Priestess Lovia.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Before the quest ends, the Crab Tsar is a hostile Local Legend; in the quest variant it uses different attacks and the player does not directly harm it. Afterwards the crab, stronger than before, can challenge the Traveler; Maite says it is under her protection and listens to her.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Damp stone, echoing chambers and mirrors that hint at hidden routes. Maite's whispered theatrics and the scurrying of small crabs lighten what could otherwise be a dread-filled maze.

## travel
### STANDARD
Approach the pit west of the Frostmoon Enclave and drop in. After completing the quest a teleport waypoint marks the palace and Maite can be spoken to there.

## quests
### STANDARD
The Mirrors, the Maze, and the Tsar (World Quest, chapter Polkka Beneath the Moon's Oracle) starts at the pit. The Traveler and Paimon meet Maite, break the Crystonite barrier, use berries to coax the crabs, shatter a mirror and confront the Crab Tsar. The quest rewards Maite's IOU and Maite's Notes and makes the Crab Tsar a Local Legend.
### LARGE +
Maite tells the party to mention her name at Speranza restaurant in Nasha Town, but admits she has no pull yet and that Katya will still bill them; she gives an IOU and promises to work a few days to pay for a meal herself.

### ULTRA +
Dialogue adapts to progress. If the Traveler has finished For a Green Island..., Maite's account of the stone ties it to the legend of Lovia and Aila, Paimon recognises the priestess from earlier and mentions that she had said she would wait at the Court of the First Edict, and Maite says the Crab Tsar could not use the mirrors until recently. Otherwise Maite says it has carried the stone for about six months. In both versions the priestess says the cursed object lies beneath the mirror and slips away before she can be questioned; after the fight she appears again and speaks of being left behind and of salvation that never comes, then vanishes. Maite's warning about the Frostmoon Scions comes from Katya, and the girl who let her go is linked on the wiki as Kasha.

## status
### STANDARD
As of 7.1 the palace is open. The stone that corrupted the Crab Tsar flew away at the quest's end, and nothing recorded since changes that.

## entry: Crab Tsar

### STANDARD
The Crab Tsar is a giant crab and Local Legend living in the Crab Tsar's Palace. Maite says it used to be gentle but became vicious while carrying a stone of desire on its back, crushing smaller crabs. After the stone left it hid away, and Maite says it is under her protection and obeys her.
### LARGE +
With a Seed Dispensary equipped, its drops yield Lakkaberry and Midsommar Berry seeds.

## entry: Maite Veliu
@keys: Maite, Maite Veliu
@keys2: Crab Tsar's Palace, Conch Gang, Reed Miller
### STANDARD
Maite Veliu, giving that as her real name, presents herself as heir to the legendary thief Reed Miller and later admits she belongs to the Conch Gang, gathering intelligence. She describes the gang as a mutual aid and coordination group for like-minded people of similar age. She sends the Traveler to find her informant Hidimbi in Nasha Town.
