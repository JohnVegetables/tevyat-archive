## summary
### LITE
The Anemo Archon Statue is the large stone likeness of Barbatos, the Anemo Archon, standing in the plaza in front of Favonius Cathedral in Mondstadt City. It shows a hooded figure with two wings and hands cupped together.
### STANDARD +
Words are carved into its pedestal: "The Gateway of Celestia" (in the Teyvat script). It is the city's most recognisable shrine to the god of freedom, and the open plaza around it is a busy public square.

## appearance
@version: 1.0
### STANDARD
The figure is hooded and winged, with its hands held together in a cup. The inscription "The Gateway of Celestia" runs along the base. Canon gives no dimensions, material or sculptor.
### LARGE +
The statue is the visual centrepiece of the plaza, so festival decorations are arranged around it. The wiki's galleries show the plaza dressed for the Windblume Festival and for the Weinlesefest event of version 3.1.

## people
@version: 1.0
### STANDARD
By day the plaza is where a few townsfolk gather, among them a Knight of Favonius on duty (Aramis), the sister Grace, the informant Vile, the bard Six-Fingered Jose and the travelling scholar Sayid. Treat them as ordinary regulars rather than permanent fixtures.
### ULTRA +
Per the wiki's NPC list, Monroe, Nora and Susie are also found there in the daytime. Their exact positions and schedules are gameplay placements, not stories.

## gameplay
@version: 1.0
### LARGE
Game mechanics, not in-world fact: sitting on the statue's cupped hands earns the achievement "Beloved of the Anemo Archon", and a hoop-running minigame around the plaza edge rewards a Luxurious Chest.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
A wide, open stone square with the cathedral behind it and the wind never quite still, matching Mondstadt's image as the city of freedom. Because the statue depicts the god who deliberately does not rule, it works as a place for quiet reflection or a public meeting spot rather than a seat of authority.
### LARGE +
The inscription about the "Gateway of Celestia" is a good hook for a curious character to ask about, but canon gives no explanation, so do not supply one as fact.
