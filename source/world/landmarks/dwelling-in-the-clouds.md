## summary
### LITE
The Dwelling in the Clouds is a small floating island high above Qingyun Peak in Minlin, Liyue, built on a foundation of Plaustrite. It was made by the adeptus Cloud Retainer as a private place to meditate, and is reached by solving the Three Cranes Puzzle on the peak below.
### STANDARD +
Its memorial text, written in Cloud Retainer's voice, says it was meant as a sanctuary away from the world; the adepti later took it over to seek ultimate truth in seclusion and, with Taishan Mansion sealed, returned it to her.

## lore
### STANDARD
Cloud Retainer built it long ago for meditation and reflection. Other adepti, with Rex Lapis' approval, later persuaded her to offer it as the site of the trial of "heaven" for those seeking the adepti's path, and she added a small pavilion. Its Plaustrite foundation and the trial of crossing the void to follow that path gave it the name "Jadevoid", a name that came to cover all places where the adepti reside.
### LARGE +
Cloud Retainer left her blueprints for a manmade floating island at the Dwelling, hoping someone with a matching affinity would find them; when nobody did, she had the Traveler retrieve them in the quest An Adeptal Summons. Because few can now follow the adepti's path, the island is in her care again. Her note adds that she wants to go back into the world for a while, starting with Guili Plains, which she calls the Plains of Returning and Departing.
### ULTRA +
Zhongli visited the Dwelling in the Clouds on the fifth day after completing the quest The Stars Inscribe the Year's Wishes.

## travel
### STANDARD
The island is unlocked by the Three Cranes Puzzle on Qingyun Peak, whose monument speaks of the one who may ascend kowtowing atop three mountains and of auspicious signs sent by three divine birds. Qingyun Peak is also home to the adeptus Moon Carver.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 it is a quiet retreat held by Cloud Retainer; no later events have changed it.
