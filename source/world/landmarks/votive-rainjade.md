## summary
### LITE
The Votive Rainjade is a ritual jade at Carp's Rest on Mt. Laixin in Chenyu Vale, Liyue. Offering Spirit Carp to it raises the offering system Rainjade's Benediction and brings the blessings of soil and water.
### STANDARD +
Its in-game description says the ancestors of Chenyu Vale's people once threw jade into the waters, passing adeptal energy into the spirit veins; this protected their homeland and secured fair weather and good harvests. The rite has long been forgotten, and if the lost energy were gathered into the jade again the blessings might return.

## practice
### STANDARD
Spirit Carp are offered in groups of five. Fifty carp bring the benediction to its maximum level. The offering system's description says it ensures favourable weather in Chenyu Vale for the next year. When maxed, two yellow spirit carp spiral around the jade and its core gives off spirit smoke.
### LARGE +
Access opens by completing the quest The Cloud-Padded Path to the Chiwang Repose, which becomes available after An Ancient Sacrifice of Sacred Brocade. From forty carp onward a Reveal button marks one uncollected carp. Each level pays rewards; the top level grants the achievement One Ring for the Rite of All.

## rewards
### ULTRA
Every level pays Adventure EXP, Mystic Enhancement Ore and Mora; the ten levels together pay out among other things Intertwined and Acquaint Fate, Crown of Insight, a Rainjade Statuette and the character-themed item Chenyu: Rainjade Rite (gameplay rewards).

## history
### STANDARD
Cleaned murals in two caves, the Ancient Cave beneath Mt. Lingmeng's north-west waterfall and Huangcong Grounds near Chizhang Wall, show Chenyu Vale's people performing the Votive Rainjade ritual.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 it is a working offering site; no later change is recorded.
