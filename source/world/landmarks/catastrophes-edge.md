## summary
### LITE
Catastrophe's Edge is an instanced trial area in Natlan's Tequemecan Valley. It lies beyond the Hall of Parting Vows and is entered through a portal during the world quest Revelations from the Past.
### STANDARD +
In that quest the Traveler and the Little One, a saurian companion, follow Manqu's guidance to the ruins to obtain a token left by the Sage of the Stolen Flame. After crossing a lava passage, a portal to Catastrophe's Edge appears, and the party must fight off three waves of foes there.
### LARGE +
The final wave is an Abyss-corroded Golden Wolflord, titled in the quest as a boundary-eroding beast from beyond. During this step the playable character is restricted to the Traveler.
### ULTRA +
Canon text read gives no further lore: the place is known only as a quest instance, and its name and purpose are left unexplained.

## quests
@version: 5.0
### STANDARD
Revelations from the Past (5.0), part of the chapter Between Pledge and Forgettance, follows Rite of the Bold and precedes The Road Ahead. The Edge is the combat climax before the inscription and the token reward.
### LARGE +
The quest steps list three waves: two of Rockfond Rifthound Whelps (Abyss-corroded variants) and a final Abyss-Corroded Golden Wolflord. Canon text read here does not explain the name Catastrophe's Edge.

## travel
### STANDARD
Unlocked through Revelations from the Past. Afterwards it can be re-entered by returning to the Hall of Parting Vows and going down the glowing stairs.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
A threshold trial: a portal reached across lava and a fight against corrupted beasts.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the instance remains accessible from the Hall of Parting Vows. No later developments were found.
