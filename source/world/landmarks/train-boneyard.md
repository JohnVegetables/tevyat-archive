## summary
### LITE
The Train Boneyard is a point of interest in Everfrozen Earth, a graveyard of old trains. In the world quest Disputes Without Honor and Humanity it is where Vasiliy, the Izmaylov family's "Young Sire", made his last stand. Zmei and Sofya say "train boneyard" is just a nickname used by locals.
### STANDARD +
The entrance has a door guarded by an Izmaylov guard who demands a password, and the area ends in a Shadow Realm fight and an exit by mine cart.

## geography
### STANDARD
The wiki places it in Everfrozen Earth and tags the page as a stub, so no further layout is given. The quest has the party approach by train, pass the guarded door, move into the depths, and climb to an upper level beyond where Vasiliy is fought. A mine cart takes the party out afterward.
### LARGE +
A note on the upper level, past Vasiliy's fight, is addressed to the Izmaylov members. The area is also where the Shadow Realm opens in the quest.

## people
### STANDARD
Vasiliy, the Young Sire of the Izmaylov family, and Izmaylov guards hold the place. The Luchkin side, with Zmei, Iryna, Sofya and Gureva, enters to stop him. The password is found out by Gureva, who says it is "Oedipus".
### LARGE +
Zmei speaks for the Fae Gang Federation's High Table when thanking the Traveler afterward.

## culture
### STANDARD
A door guard asks "What's the password?" and turns away guesses such as "Sweet Madame" and "Fire-Water" with an angry "No unauthorized personnel allowed". The locals treat the name as private.

## history
### STANDARD
In Disputes Without Honor and Humanity Vasiliy is working to open the Shadow Realm; Zmei says that if he had figured out how, he could have taken the entire High Table down with him. A note he left, Yet-to-be-Completed "New High Table Rules", says snow buries every story, that the Izmaylov were forced to live in the shadow of "great figures", and that he, Vasiliy, will lead them to stand as equals at the High Table and proudly call themselves Izmaylov.
### LARGE +
Zmei recalls that the three of them (Zmei, Vasiliy and a third person) once played hide-and-seek, and Vasiliy hid in the deepest part of a collapsed tunnel to win. After the quest the tremors stop and the Shadow Realm's influence fades, and Zmei says the device that opened the Shadow Realm has been destroyed.
### ULTRA +
Afterwards, at Zolnyy, Zmei gives the Traveler a block of refined Kresnik Kristall that was the key to activate the device, and a map from a box taken from the Butcher's desk marking the "Legendary Thief's Treasure". The Izmaylovs' fate is left for the High Table to settle.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
A rusting, snow-covered scrapyard of train carriages that doubles as a gang's last hiding place. The note found there is more sorrowful than menacing.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Izmaylov guards at the entrance and fights in the Shadow Realm before the Vasiliy boss fight. The entrance is closed to anyone without the password.

## quests
### STANDARD
Disputes Without Honor and Humanity, in the chapter Mean Streets of the Despicable.

## status
### STANDARD
As of 7.1 the Izmaylov incident is over; the High Table is deciding what happens to the Izmaylovs. The sources do not show it changing in 7.1.
