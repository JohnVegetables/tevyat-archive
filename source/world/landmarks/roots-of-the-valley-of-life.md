## summary
### LITE
The Roots of the Valley of Life (an unofficial name) is a point of interest in The Chasm's Main Mining Area, in Liyue. The hidden exploration objective Seed from the Valley of Life takes place here.
### STANDARD +
Climbing the branches on the tree's southern side lets the player enter a Mine Passageway to Sumeru, near a mushroom growing on the tree. 

## travel
### STANDARD
The passageway is a shortcut between the underground mines of Liyue and Avidya Forest in Sumeru. The pages consulted give no in-world explanation of how it came to exist.
### LARGE +
Its far end opens beside the waterfall under the Statue of The Seven near Gandharva Ville in Avidya Forest. The tree lies in the Main Mining Area of The Chasm: Underground Mines.
### ULTRA +
The wiki lists this crossing as one of several Mine Passageways (it appears as location 4 in its gallery of passageway contexts).

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 it is simply an explorable site and travel link; no lore beyond the hidden objective is given.
