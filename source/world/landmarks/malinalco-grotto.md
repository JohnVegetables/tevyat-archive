## summary
### LITE
Malinalco Grotto is an underground cavern in Quahuacan Cliff, Natlan. It contains a mural depicting the legend of Kuntur and Rumi, and is reached by taking the right-hand tunnel at the entrance of the Tenebrous Papilla boss cave.
### STANDARD +
Notes found there suggest the mural is being repaired by people who use paints produced on the spot. Two viewpoints are named Cavern of Tranquil Light and The Story Recorded. Hued Monetoo and Brilliant Chrysanthemum can be found in the cave.
### LARGE +
The notes ask whether the story could really end with three tribal warriors defeated by Rumi the wicked sorcerer, and wonder whether another mural showing Kuntur lies elsewhere and whether its path has been buried or needs a mechanism.
### ULTRA +
The wiki's etymology note says the name echoes Malinalco in Mexico; this is a real-world reference, not an in-game statement.

## places
### STANDARD
The cavern has several levels and the wiki names the area the Cavern of the Legendary Mural.

## culture
### STANDARD
The notes treat creating murals near where paint is produced as a good idea, since missing colour can be refilled with material gathered on the spot.

## dangers
### STANDARD
The tunnel lies beside the Tenebrous Papilla boss cave.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the grotto remains accessible. No later developments were found.
