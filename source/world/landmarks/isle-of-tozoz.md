## summary
### LITE
The Isle of Tozoz, also called the Isle of Night, is a small island in the northeastern corner of Ochkanatlan in Natlan. It was once guarded by people who watched over saurian hatchlings and is now a quiet ruin of mechanisms and logs.
### STANDARD +
The world quest The Lone Isle Named Night (5.2) begins when the player activates three switches and the main Ancient Inscribed Ring. In a hidden chamber the Traveler and Paimon find a guard's diary and a Saurian egg, which the guard had named Aguara and which becomes a companion.
### LARGE +
Secret Source Sentinels still patrol, warning intruders to leave or be expelled by lethal force.
### ULTRA +
The wiki notes that the name echoes Tozoztli, a Nahuatl word for vigil. This is an etymology note, not stated in game.

## geography
### STANDARD
A small island at the northeast edge of Ochkanatlan, with switches placed high up, a summit the player climbs and a hidden underground chamber.

## people
@version: 5.2
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
An unnamed guard kept the island under Lord Milchi. The diary also mentions Lord Atlatl and warriors from Cinder City who attacked the island.

## history
@version: 5.2
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Maintenance logs show a lax, low-budget watch: security was repeatedly judged too weak, but the approving party ruled that intruders should merely be driven off rather than harmed. A newer guard complained that the Saurians ate better than the staff, citing Lord Och-Kan's view that they were enemies.
### LARGE +
In the diary, messengers from Cinder City describe the island's creatures as pests whose contact brings sickness and personality change, and say Lord Milchi himself was transformed after meeting them. The guard rejects this, saying the Saurians are clean and that attackers kept trying to burn the island. Later entries mention orders to execute the little ones, rumours of tribal rebellions, and Lord Milchi freeing the hatchlings and staying to face Och-Kan.
### ULTRA +
In the last entry the guard writes that the egg could not be moved, hid it in the underground chamber and named it Aguara, while warriors searched the island and food ran out. A stone tablet at the island has its inscription partly chiselled away, and a damaged Sentinel reports failed initialisation.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Secret Source Sentinels guard the island, and a Tenebrous Mimesis variant of an Iktomisaurus Warrior appears in the quest's combat step.

## quests
@version: 5.2
### STANDARD
The Lone Isle Named Night belongs to the chapter In the Footsteps of the Chosen of Dragons. Completing it unlocks an achievement and sends the Unhatched Saurian Egg to the Rest Area.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Quiet and melancholy: tidy logs, grappling hooks and a hidden room that preserve one guard's worries long after the hatchlings left.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the island is abandoned, with Sentinels still active. No later developments were found.

### STANDARD
Logs and the diary on the island record a guard's complaints about budget, ladders and the lax care of the Saurians, as well as the officials' repeated order to avoid harming intruders. They are one guard's account and should be treated as partial.
