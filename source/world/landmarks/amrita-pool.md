## summary
### LITE
The Amrita Pool is a small clear spring in a cave near Vourukasha Oasis in Sumeru. It is an overflow of the Amrita that surrounds the oasis, and the Pari say it is where they were granted life.
### STANDARD +
Fedhri, a Pari, looks after the pool. Memories belonging to the Khvarena are said to flow back through it to the Harvisptokhm, the great structure beneath the oasis. Players unlock it with the world quest Heart of Amrita, available after The Splendorous Sky That Day.
### LARGE +
Its in-game description calls the original Amrita the nourishment of the oasis and of many kinds of Khvarena, and the pool only an overflow of it.

## geography
### STANDARD
A pool in a cave beside Vourukasha Oasis, in the Realm of Farakhkert in the Girdle of the Sands. It is only a spring that overflows from the larger Amrita around the oasis.
### LARGE +
When The Gift of Amrita offering system reaches its maximum level, the pool water glows.

## people
@version: 3.6
### STANDARD
Fedhri, a Pari, says she is in charge of looking after the pool and has not left the place since Lady Zurvan sealed the oasis. She addresses the Traveler as the outlander offerer, a human bonded to a Pari.
### LARGE +
Fedhri recalls a time when Pari walked with humans and both built homes in the oasis, and says the darkness's corrosion never stained it thanks to the Harvisptokhm and the Khvarena.

## history
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Item text records that the Amrita was created in the cataclysm five hundred years ago, when Egeria, the previous Hydro Archon, was killed at Tunigi Hollow fighting the monsters and corruption pouring into Teyvat. Greater Lord Rukkhadevata grew the Harvisptokhm from it, which anchored Egeria's consciousness to the mortal realm and let her act as a seal over the dark hollow.
### LARGE +
The Amrita and the Harvisptokhm later began to be corrupted. To purify it, the Khvarena Simurgh, left in Rukkhadevata's care by Nabu Malikata for this moment, drank from the Amrita and burst into countless Khvarena, which became the Pari.
### ULTRA +
Fedhri describes the pool as the place where Pari receive life from the primordial water of their god. When a Pari exhausts its strength it becomes a Plume of Purifying Light and leaves only pure memories behind. The Amrita is said to wash away the deepest sorrow so a creature regains its pristine form as the Khvarena's familiar.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Hushed and clear: a small bright spring in a cave, with the sense that something very large and old drains through it.

## quests
### STANDARD
Heart of Amrita (3.6): Fedhri explains the pool and introduces the offering system, The Gift of Amrita, in which Plumes of Purifying Light are offered to the pool for rewards. That system is a gameplay mechanic.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the pool remains a spring in Vourukasha Oasis, tended by Fedhri. No later changes were found.

## entry: Fedhri
@keys: Fedhri
@version: 3.6
### STANDARD
Fedhri is the Pari keeper of the Amrita Pool, who has stayed at the pool since Lady Zurvan sealed the oasis. She treasures memories of the age when Pari and humans lived side by side in the oasis.
