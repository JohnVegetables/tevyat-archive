## summary
### LITE
The Heart of the Sacred Mountain is the innermost chamber of the Great Volcano of Tollan, far below the ruined Sacred City in Natlan. It holds the heart of the primal flame (Phlogiston) and the last legacy of the ancient dragons: Huitzilopochtli, the Natlan Phlogistonization Array, a device made to burn Natlan into pure phlogiston and so destroy the Abyss. In 5.5 the Traveler and the saurian Little One reached it, and Little One chose not to run the machine. As of 7.1 it lies dormant and obsolete.
### STANDARD +
Entry requires both halves of the Golden Entreaty and a permit from the dragons' old records, and it is the destination of the Chronicler of the Crumbling City quest chapter. The Sage of the Stolen Flame, who stage-managed the journey, turns out to be the dragon Kukulkan.

## geography
### STANDARD
The Heart lies under the Sacred City of Tollan, inside the volcano that dominates Atocpan. The way down is a fissure in the city centre that the dragon Xiuhcoatl excavated when he rebuilt the city, to give himself a stable place to experiment. A guardian Secret Source Dragon Idol seals the entrance and needs power from three Phlogiston Units. Inside, the story unfolds along a series of platforms, where Kukulkan recounts the history of the dragons, passes the dragons who gave up their bodies, and finally reaches the great array and its activation mechanism.
### LARGE +
Kukulkan describes the place as the home of the blueprints of the old world, and as the spot where the first Pyro Sovereign was born from the primal flame and shaped Tollan and the first Dragonborn. Towers once spread over Natlan were meant to let the array cover the whole land; most were later smashed on Och-Kan's orders, though Kukulkan says their fragments serve the same purpose.

## history
### STANDARD
By Kukulkan's account, Xiuhcoatl was frozen in lava by the Heavenly Principles after their victory, and woke when the Dragon King Nibelung returned from beyond the sky. When the Heavenly Principles killed Nibelung, the Abyss-soaked Xiuhcoatl briefly regained clarity, saw how corrupted the world and his own mind had become, and spent his remaining lucid time building Huitzilopochtli. The device would turn Natlan into a land of only phlogiston, whose burning would be recorded and replicated, and the Abyss would be annihilated on contact. Kukulkan says every Dragonborn but himself accepted the plan; he walked away to look for another path.
### LARGE +
Xiuhcoatl made one of his pupils the control hub of the array, which became his only weakness: Xbalanque exploited it to kill him, and both died, though Xbalanque was reborn as the first Pyro Archon. Xbalanque and Kukulkan then split the hub into two Golden Entreaties and swore that Huitzilopochtli would never be activated. Xbalanque's half passed to Och-Kan, who hid it on the Tonatiuh; Kukulkan hid his in the Hall of Parting Vows. Disappointed in the human tribes after Och-Kan's fall, Kukulkan lost his faith in humanity, spread rumours of the second Entreaty, and waited for someone to join the halves and carry them to the volcano; he says he deliberately sent a dragon (Little One) to walk beside a human.
### ULTRA +
The nameless servant says that sealing the city left the device itself with the deepest Abyssal corrosion, after which the city was taken over; Kukulkan adds that the dragons who had discarded their bodies to become phlogiston lifeforms were held in a dream-like state at great cost in resources. Kukulkan says that by the time of the Traveler's visit the city's reserves were nearly spent and the sleepers would die unless someone took authority over the primal flame.

## quests
### STANDARD
The route is the two final parts of the Natlan wandering-saurian storyline. In Search of a Hidden Heart (Chronicler of the Crumbling City, part 2) sends the Traveler through the Sacred City: a nameless Secret Source Servant, a prototype built to help Xiuhcoatl keep records, explains that entry needs a permit from the Chamber of Deliberation and power for the guardian idol, obtained by clearing Abyssal corruption from several sites. An Omen of Annihilation and the Final Entreaty (A Finale Emberforged) is the ending.
### LARGE +
In the finale the two Golden Entreaties are offered at the central mechanism, and a golden voice (the Sage of the Stolen Flame) invites the Chosen of Dragons in. The Traveler fights the Primeval Source Dragon Mech, is caged by its laser, and Little One, forced to act alone, is guided by an unnamed voice into a temporary mature-dragon form. That voice is Kukulkan, who shows Little One the history of the array and asks it to activate Huitzilopochtli, requiring three rounds of confirmation. Little One, remembering the Traveler and Paimon, cancels, breaks the Traveler out, and the pair defeat the Mech together.
### ULTRA +
Kukulkan admits the removal of the Flamelord's Blessing was a lie of omission: the Blessing would have killed most saurians if mishandled, and he planned to use a human to open the volcano and bring back the second Entreaty. Little One answers his claim that Natlan's people will never forge their own defences with an explicit trust in humanity.

## people
### LARGE
Kukulkan, the dragon behind the Sage of the Stolen Flame and the voice in the Secret Source Servants, appears here in many borrowed forms (including Manqu and Bona's shapes). A nameless Secret Source Servant, a prototype built for Xiuhcoatl's record-keeping, guards the way in. Little One, a young Tepetlisaurus that carried the Flamelord's Blessing, is the protagonist of the choice. Paimon and the Traveler are present throughout.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Vast, quiet and mechanical rather than infernal: white light, golden script appearing as words in the air, and a ceremonial voice that speaks of cleansing creation. The mood shifts between scholarly calm and growing pressure as Kukulkan warns that the energy reserves are running down.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Abyssal corruption in the Sacred City above; the Secret Source Dragon Idol guardian; the Primeval Source Dragon Mech, with a laser cage and charged beam attacks; and the uncontrolled power of the Flamelord's Blessing if a saurian is unguided.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the Heart is dormant. Huitzilopochtli has not been activated and is obsolete; Little One keeps a dimmed mark and has returned to his small saurian size.
### STANDARD +
The Flamelord's Blessing is gone. The volcano did not erupt in response, only releasing a little smoke. Kukulkan and the phlogistonised dragons faded away, and Tollan's maintenance functions ended. No later quest in 6.x or 7.0-7.1 returns here.

## entry: Huitzilopochtli, the Natlan Phlogistonization Array
@keys: Huitzilopochtli, Natlan Phlogistonization Array, Sunquake, Sweeping Skyfire, phlogistonization
@spoiler: major
@version: 5.5
### STANDARD
Huitzilopochtli, also called the Sunquake or Sweeping Skyfire, was the dragons' final weapon, kept in the Heart of the Sacred Mountain. It was built to turn all Natlan, and eventually the world, into phlogiston and matter made only of phlogiston. Everything burned would be transcribed and copied as phlogiston units, and the Abyss would be destroyed when astral assemblages from the primal flame attached to it. Dragons who gave up their bodies would become the rulers of the new world; ordinary life would die or be converted. It needs the two Golden Entreaties and three confirmations to start or stop.
### LARGE +
Kukulkan calls phlogiston an atom on which information can be written, a kind of computronium shaped by Iridescent Inscriptions, which is why it can imitate any form. He says nobody can predict what it would do to the Traveler. The player is allowed to confirm activation; the wiki treats the resulting vision, of a single lonely consciousness in a white world, as a non-canonical branch.

## entry: Golden Entreaties
@keys: Golden Entreaty, Golden Entreaties, Jade of Return
@spoiler: major
@version: 5.5
### STANDARD
The Golden Entreaties are two stone inscriptions that together form the control hub of Huitzilopochtli. They were created when Xbalanque and Kukulkan split the hub to guarantee the array would never run. One was kept by Och-Kan on the Tonatiuh (the legendary 'Jade of Return') and the other in the Hall of Parting Vows. Joined and offered in the Heart, they open the way to the array.

## entry: Kukulkan in the Heart
@keys: Kukulkan, Sage of the Stolen Flame, Waxaklahun Ubah Kan
@keys2: Heart of the Sacred Mountain, Little One, Huitzilopochtli
@spoiler: major
@version: 5.5
### LARGE
Inside the Heart the Sage of the Stolen Flame reveals himself as Kukulkan, saying that 'Kukulkan' was his first name and Waxaklahun Ubah Kan only one of many he invented. He wore the forms of Manqu and Bona to explain things and calls himself Xiuhcoatl's younger brother and Och-Kan's father. He admits that his invented legends, the secret-servant voice and the trial path were all designed to bring a dragon to this room, and that he wanted the Dragonborn to choose the array freely and was still moved when it chose otherwise.
