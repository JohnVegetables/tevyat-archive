## summary
### LITE
The Lofty Clock Tower is a bell tower at the House of Hesperides, the Snegovik mountain manor in Fellfrost Peak. It stands on the manor's upper floors and holds large bells that can be rung. A guardian named Tsvetaev, an "Expedition Returner", defends it.
### STANDARD +
Canon material for it is thin: the wiki describes it only as a bell tower, and its significance comes from a hidden exploration objective, A Secret in the Chimes (an unofficial name).

## geography
### STANDARD
The tower is on the third floor level of the mountain mansion (Mountain Mansion 3F map level). The gate cannot be opened before the objective is done. After the fight the Traveler uses a link mechanism to reach the top, uses the Eye of Graeae to remove a Prime Ice surface, and drops down to ring bells.
### LARGE +
Ringing the bells guides a Seelie, which unlocks Elemental Monuments that are then activated, opening chests below. Ringing one of the bells at the top of the tower for the first time earns an achievement the wiki lists as "Wedding Bell".

## people
### STANDARD
Tsvetaev, a Glacial Warden labelled "Expedition Returner", guards the tower. He says visitors should not enter without leave. When defeated he wonders when he will hear "the true bell" again.

## history
### STANDARD
The sources say nothing of the tower's origin. The wiki says only that it is a bell tower at the House of Hesperides.
### LARGE +
Completing A Secret in the Chimes yields the achievement "For Whom It Tolls" and unlocks one of the five paintings needed for the separate hidden objective Paintings of Hesperides.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
A tall, echoing tower of chimes in a house famous for ceremony; guarded by a polite but firm sentinel.

## dangers
### STANDARD
The Glacial Warden fight at the entrance. Tsvetaev says mid-fight that the clock tower is "the only thing that..." but the line is cut off in the wiki record and flagged there as unverified.

## quests
### STANDARD
No quest in the quest category. Only the hidden objective A Secret in the Chimes uses it.
