## summary
### LITE
The Sacrificial Altar of Enigmatic Land is a point of interest in the Tezcatepetonco Range of Natlan, within the area of the Masters of the Night-Wind. Its viewpoint is called Ceremonial Site Lost in Night's Dim Light.
### STANDARD +
Biram is listed as chief of the Masters of the Night-Wind and Caholom as high priest, with Cip as a guard and Tlapo as the Iktomisaurus elder; Toltec is also listed. Yolotli is found in the area labelled Chamber of Weaving.
### LARGE +
The map level is named Sacrificial Altar of Enigmatic Land and Nightsouls. No rites or history are described in the sources read.
### ULTRA +
The wiki gives no description of the altar itself, so its rites and function stay unknown beyond the viewpoint title.
