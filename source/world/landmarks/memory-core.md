## summary
### LITE
The Memory Core is a huge storage device at the centre of the Temple of Space, in the Pillar Hall Central Zone. It holds information about civilizations that have passed out of human memory. Travelers offer it Memory Factors, the ember-like remains of forgotten creations, and receive rewards in return.
### STANDARD +
The Temple of Space is the sky realm above Windrest Peak, shaped by Asmoday, the Lord of Emptiness. Its in-game description speaks of replicas of civilizations drifting in the instant before extinction, and says nobody can discern the realm's true purpose. The offering system bound to the Core carries its name, and the Core is reached after the quest The Wind Has Ceased.
### LARGE +
The shopkeeper Josephus stands beside it, and his friend Istoria, another preserved person, can be met there after A Long Day in the Mountains.

## geography
### STANDARD
The Core sits in the Pillar Hall Central Zone of the Temple of Space, a cold hall of silent pillars. The temple has its own map and exploration progress, separate from the main map of Teyvat.

## places
### STANDARD
Around the Core are Josephus, who runs the Emberic Reconstruction shop, and (after the later quest) Istoria. Mnemonic Clusters and chests across the temple supply the Memory Factors brought here.

## people
### STANDARD
Two of the temple's "Uplifted" (people taken from past eras to be kept as living memories) are met here: Josephus, a philosopher of the era after the War of Funerary Flame, and Istoria, a philosopher of the unified civilization whose era came earlier. The two are friends.

## history
### LARGE
The Temple of Space was made by Asmoday as an exhibition hall preserving records of fallen civilizations. The Core fits that purpose as a store of information on unknown ones. Josephus explains that a civilization forgotten by humanity loses its meaning, then its form, until only embers called Memory Factors remain, waiting for a new cycle that will never come.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Silent, cold and still. Time stands still in the temple and its inhabitants do not age, tire or die. No wind is allowed into Asmoday's hall. The Core's maximum-level description is gentler: civilizations are fragile, yet remembering them still kindles hope for a slightly brighter tomorrow.

## travel
### STANDARD
The Temple of Space is entered from a map marker at the Old Sanatorium Site after The Wind Has Ceased. Gameplay detail, not lore: Memory Factors are offered in groups of 20, 200 are needed for the maximum level, and some levels also require exploration progress in the temple. Rewards include Primogems, the achievement "Space and Unfree Will" and a namecard. At maximum level Emberic Reconstruction opens and excess Memory Factors can be traded to Josephus.

## status
### STANDARD
As of 7.1 the Core is open and in use. Josephus and Istoria remain in the temple.
### ULTRA +
Wiki trivia of note: Josephus' title references the real-world Josephus problem in mathematics. Istoria's teacher, Aristocles, taught that the mind prevails over matter, and one of Istoria's students was Abrax, who later went to Delphi Pytho (per the Istoria wiki page, medium confidence).

## entry: Memory Factor
@keys: Memory Factor, Memory Factors, Mnemonic Cluster
@version: 6.5
### STANDARD
Memory Factors are the fundamental building blocks of "Civilization". Anything made by a civilization that fades from collective memory eventually disintegrates into them. They are found in the Temple of Space, 3 from each floating Mnemonic Cluster and 1 from every chest except Remarkable Chests, and are offered at the Memory Core to receive blessings from the memories drifting in the interstices. Josephus calls them embers.
### LARGE +
The wiki counts 30 Mnemonic Clusters and 154 qualifying chests, giving 244 Factors against the 200 needed for the Core's top level.

## entry: Josephus and Emberic Reconstruction
@keys: Josephus, Emberic Reconstruction, Master of Anti-Self-Termination
@version: 6.5
### STANDARD
Josephus is a philosopher from the era after the War of Funerary Flame who was uplifted to the Temple of Space. He is titled "Master of Anti-Self-Termination", a joke about a maths problem he posed on who ends up the last man standing among soldiers who refuse to surrender. He runs Emberic Reconstruction, a souvenir shop at the Memory Core that works once the Core is at its top level and takes Memory Factors as currency. He says nobody dies in the temple. He never heard of the dragon that tore open the sky, because his era came after Istoria's, when angels no longer gave humanity true secrets.
### LARGE +
Josephus describes the Core as a place where information on unknown civilizations is stored, and wishes there were enough embers to reproduce a thing or two.

## entry: Istoria
@keys: Istoria
@keys2: Temple of Space, Memory Core, Uplifted
@version: 6.5
### STANDARD
Istoria is a philosopher of the unified civilization who lived around the War of Funerary Flame, when Nibelung returned under the Abyss's influence about 6,000 years ago. He was among the first uplifted to the Temple of Space and spent centuries in the Apathic Interval first. He is the first Uplifted the Traveler and Paimon meet in A Long Day in the Mountains, and he asks for help to free the temple. He is a friend of Josephus and is met at the Memory Core after that quest.
