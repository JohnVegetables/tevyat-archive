## summary
### LITE
The Serpent's Head is the skull of the great serpent god Orobashi, the Watatsumi Omikami, left on Yashiori Island where the Electro Archon killed it. The weathered bones now form a landscape of their own: a subarea of Inazuma battered by rain and thunder. The skull still gazes at the sky, but the god is dead and its name is slowly being forgotten.
### STANDARD +
The Tatarigami left by the god's remains drove the island's people away. The sea wind through the skull's eyes and fangs is said to make a whistling sound, called the requiem the serpent composed for itself.

## geography
### STANDARD
The Serpent's Head is a subarea of Yashiori Island, the bony remains of Orobashi's skull rising over the sea and surrounded by thunderstorms. A viewpoint, Overlooking Serpent Head, looks over it. Inside the serpent's mouth a closed grate blocks the way down to a Simple Tomb under the skull, which is opened by a short puzzle: chase two Electro Seelies to the serpent's eyes, pull a lever beside a Thunder Sakura Bough in the first chamber, defeat a Ruin Sentinel in the second and trip the door switches in order. In the bottom chamber lies Kikuchiyo's Simple Tomb, guarded by a Kairagi.
### LARGE +
Narukami Pillars, Thunder Barriers and Thunder Sakura Boughs stand on and around the skull, and a gate in the bottom chamber leads out to the Serpent's Head Ward. Maguu Kenki, a boss on the island, and Nobushi, Kairagi, Fatui Cryo Cicin Mages and Treasure Hoarders also appear. Local specialties are Crystal Marrow, Dendrobium and Onikabuto.

## history
### STANDARD
After the Raiden Shogun and her twin Makoto united Inazuma, the other gods did not claim land that was not theirs. Orobashi, god of the Watatsumi people, was among them. Its island home was poor soil, and between the cries of its starving people and a decree from Celestia it invaded resource-rich Yashiori Island with a navy led by the shrine maiden Mouun and her sister Ayame. Ei gathered her troops and waged a brutal war, and slew Orobashi with Musou no Hitotachi, the blow splitting the island into two with Musoujin Gorge and leaving the god's remains across the land. The god's lingering hatred became the Tatarigami, which Ei suppressed with wards.
### LARGE +
The Kitain Clan, descended from Kitain Bunsou, became the Yashiori Guardians who suppressed the Tatarigami, until the bloodline ended after Kitain Hideie's escape during the cataclysm. During the war between the Shogunate and Watatsumi that followed the Vision Hunt and Sakoku decrees, a radical faction of the Resistance, at the Fatui mole Nathan's urging and without Kokomi's knowledge, disguised itself as Shogunate troops and destroyed the wards, hoping to restore Orobashi's dignity. The Tatarigami broke free, sickening the villagers of Higi Village and the troops stationed on the island.
### ULTRA +
The Watatsumi people continued to worship Orobashi even after its death, and the Shogun herself says they were allowed to so that it could keep receiving prayers. Some Watatsumi find peace with the Shogunate hard, as they feel it would make Orobashi's sacrifice in vain.

## quests
### STANDARD
In Orobashi's Legacy the Traveler meets Kaji at Fort Fujitou, who is investigating the island's endless rain and lightning and traces it to broken ancient wards. One of the early parts (I to III) has the Traveler find missing Rock Pillar Warding Stones and Pearls and return them to the ward at the Serpent's Head, solving Sacred Stone Puzzles, as is also done at Musoujin Gorge and Jakotsu Mine. After the three wards are mended, Fort Mumei rises from the sea and the final, larger ward is repaired in Parts IV and V; the permanent rain and storm then cease.
### LARGE +
The Simple Tomb puzzle also unlocks the full set of Kid Kujirai's Temari challenge.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Constant rain and lightning until Orobashi's Legacy is completed, which applies Wet and can chain into Electro-Charged; Tatarigami influence; Maguu Kenki; Nobushi, Kairagi and Treasure Hoarders.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
A white skeleton so large that its fangs frame the sky, the wind whistling in its eye sockets, and endless thunderstorms trying to wash away the remains of a past that nobody here wants to repeat.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the Serpent's Head remains a desolate, largely deserted site; the storms cease once Orobashi's Legacy is completed.
### STANDARD +
No 6.x or 7.x events concerning it were found on the wiki. The Fatui who lingered on Yashiori after the war are few.

## entry: Orobashi
@keys: Orobashi, Watatsumi Omikami, Orobashi no Mikoto, great serpent
@keys2: Yashiori Island, Serpent's Head, Watatsumi
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
Orobashi was born some 6,000 years ago, was defeated in the Archon War and fled Teyvat, ending up beyond the seal around Enkanomiya, where a child asked it to become their god. There it overthrew the Sunchildren's rule, learned the forbidden history in the book Before Sun and Moon and, for that knowledge, was judged by Celestia and allowed to sacrifice itself to spare its people. About 2,000 years ago it broke off its coral to make Watatsumi Island. It died fighting the Electro Archon. Its own entry carries its full story.

## entry: The Tatarigami Wards
@keys: Tatarigami, Narukami Pillar, Rock Pillar Pearl, Rock Pillar Warding Stone, Kaji
@keys2: Serpent's Head, Yashiori Island, Orobashi's Legacy
### STANDARD
Ei's wards, tied to Narukami Pillars, suppressed the Tatarigami for centuries, but a group of Watatsumi radicals destroyed them. In Orobashi's Legacy, the Traveler, working with Kaji's investigation, mends them by returning Warding Stones and Pearls. Each repair clears the Tatarigami over its own area; the Serpent's Head, Musoujin Gorge and Jakotsu Mine come first, then Fort Mumei's ward.
