## summary
### LITE
The Symbol of Mondstadt's Hero is the enormous oak tree at Windrise in Galesong Hill. Mondstadt believes it grew after the hero Vennessa ascended to Celestia about a thousand years ago. A Statue of the Seven stands under its branches.
### STANDARD +
It is a landmark of the city's founding-era story of rebellion against the aristocracy, and also somewhere the Anemo Archon Venti is known to come to rest.

## history
@version: 1.0
### STANDARD
Roughly a thousand years ago Vennessa led the revolt that overthrew Mondstadt's corrupt aristocracy and became known as the city's hero. Legend says she ascended to Celestia as a reward at the end of her life, and the towering oak is believed to have risen at the spot where she left. This is the tradition as told in Mondstadt; canon does not independently confirm that the tree grew because of her.
### LARGE +
The wiki's page cites a historian NPC, Dr. Edith, for this account. The tree's name is a title given to it by the people of Mondstadt rather than the name of a person.

## quests
@version: 1.0
### LARGE
In the opening Archon Quest, after the Traveler's presence purifies Dvalin's teardrop, Venti thinks of "a friend so dear" and runs off to this tree. The Traveler finds him sitting in its branches, where he says he can feel the Abyssal poison that passed into him leaving his body. Later, after Signora tears his Gnosis away and Barbara's healing has no effect, he returns to the tree to recover.
### ULTRA +
In a world quest the historian Dr. Edith, researching the power of "wind", wants to examine the tree. The Traveler brings back leaves, which turn out to hold Anemo elemental power.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
A broad plain washed by a constant breeze, with the great oak towering over it and a Statue of the Seven beneath. It suits quiet, reverent or restorative scenes.

## entry: Vennessa and the great oak
@keys: Symbol of Mondstadt's Hero, Vennessa's ascension
@version: 1.0
### STANDARD
Mondstadt tradition holds that the oak at Windrise grew where the hero Vennessa ascended to Celestia about a thousand years ago, after she led the rebellion against the aristocracy. This is presented as belief and legend.
