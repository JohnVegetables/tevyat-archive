## summary
### LITE
"Seiraimaru" is a subarea of Seirai Island named for the flagship of the bandit captain Ako Domeki, which was destroyed there. The rundown atakebune warship lies stranded among thunder-scarred terrain, its wreck still marked by what looks like starlit tears.
### STANDARD +
The viewpoint text recalls a vessel that once sailed proudly, blessed by the shrine maidens and villagers of a bygone age.

## history
### STANDARD
Ako Domeki, called the Deathless Oni of Seirai, was a famed bandit and pirate from Seirai Island who led a rebellion against the Shogunate around 500 years ago and commanded over ten ships. The Seiraimaru was his flagship. The Shogunate's ships, and the island itself, were wrecked when Asase Hibiki unsealed the Thunder Manifestation to help his cause; the storm swept Ako and his crew away.
### LARGE +
In his own words from the weapon text of "The Catch", Ako once called himself master of this ship, then found Seirai ruined on his return, with no home left in the Inazuman islands and even the "naggy" old shrine maiden gone. He sailed off again, vowing to see the edge of the world in her place.

## geography
### LARGE
The wreck sits close to a Thunder Sakura Bough on Seirai Island, whose centre was once swallowed by a perpetual thunderstorm.
### ULTRA +
Common sights nearby include Fatui skirmishers, Nobushi and hilichurls, with Sea Ganoderma and Naku Weed as local specialities.

## quests
### STANDARD
Exploring the ship is a multi-stage puzzle. Four wheels by the mast must be turned so single-pointed "sword" marks face three-pointed "halberd" marks, opening a hatch into the hull. Seelie guide the way inside: a Rust-Worn Key and wheel puzzle unlock an Exquisite Chest, and an Intact Key does the same for a Luxurious Chest (and the Davy Jones' Locker achievement) before another Seelie Court.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the ship remains a wreck on Seirai Island, nothing in 6.x or 7.0-7.1 content changes it.
