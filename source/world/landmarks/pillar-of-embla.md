## summary
### LITE
The Pillar of Embla is a great divine pillar in Wavechaser Plain in northern Nod-Krai, rooted in the earth and rising toward the sky. It was raised about five hundred years ago, during the Cataclysm, by Rauthvangi (known at the Belyi Tsar's court as Rotwang) as a tower to reach the false sky and carry him to his "Elysium", the Abyss. In the centuries since, its presence has permanently altered the surrounding land.
### STANDARD +
In recent events it became the site of the Lightkeepers' effort to stop Rotwang's return. The Frostmoon Scions remember that their first Moonchanter, Aila, travelled to it at the request of the Torchforger Solovei to end the Abyssal catastrophe he caused.

## geography
### STANDARD
The Pillar stands in Wavechaser Plain, in the north of Nod-Krai, within reach of the Lightkeepers' city of Piramida. Wild plants and creatures of the Nod-Krai wilds grow around it, among them mandragora, spirebloom, cottonspring flowers, glimmerfruit and slothsheep; winter icelea is gathered here, and Moonlane is also found in the area. The loading-screen text calls it a divine pillar "firmly rooted in the earth and reaching skyward".
### LARGE +
Around the pillar there are Abyssal fog barriers, Abyssal riftlines, an Evil Eye of Blightseep, a Lunar Conflux barrier and a Solaris Core and Nycalyx puzzle. The pillar can appear in two states: cleansed, and corrupted by Abyssal power.

## history
@version: 6.3
### STANDARD
Rauthvangi was a Khaenri'ahn scholar-monk exiled for his obsession with the Abyss, which he called the pitch-black Elysium. He reached Nod-Krai when it was still a barren borderland, later served the Belyi Tsar as the alchemist Rotwang, and built the Pillar of Embla as a tower to break through the false sky and open the way for the Abyss. Aila and Solovei stopped the first attempt; Rotwang rose again but was sealed at Kipumaki Cliff.
### LARGE +
Rotwang planned to use the stolen Sampo Mill to siphon the land's energy and fuel his ascent. By the time Alia and Solovei understood his aim he was close to success; Alia threw herself into the Sampo Mill, igniting it with her Frostmoon bloodline, and ended the attempt, killing Rotwang the first time (he came back). Solovei's pet nightingale Aedon was carried through time by this event and, five centuries later, became Illuga's messenger bird. After sealing Rotwang, Aila gave Solovei's new order of Lightkeepers the Arrow of Terpikeraunas, a huge crossbow-like Hyperborean device that was set into Piramida's Great Mead Hall, built tall enough to reach the top of the Pillar of Embla.
### ULTRA +
Rotwang's followers' power rose twice in recent memory: ten years ago, when the Starshyna Sigurd and Illuga's parents died, and three years ago, when the Nightmare Orioles squad was nearly wiped out. In the Nightingale's Song chapter, Starshyna Nikita sent Illuga to recover the Silver Lampwick to power the Arrow, and Rotwang corrupted the Pillar of Embla, producing a Calamity Egg and sending Abyssal forces, including the revived Morozov and Raimo, against Piramida. The forces were beaten, the Arrow was fired by Illuga and the Traveler, and the Calamity Egg was destroyed, ending Rotwang for good.

## people
@version: 6.3
### LARGE
Lightkeepers of the support unit stationed at Kipumaki Cliff, near the pillar, left diaries here: a newly selected recruit named Filaev (his first diary entry, "Neatly-Written Diary (I)") and an unsigned, abandoned diary from a Frostgale Steeds unit sent as reinforcements to Kipumaki Cliff. See the sub-entry on the diaries.
### ULTRA +
The quest Tower of Inversion, which begins here, lists Illuga, Vlaicu and the historical Alia among its characters.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
A lonely, wind-scoured plain dominated by a vast pillar; in its corrupted state, the air is thick with Abyssal fog and the pillar is ringed with unnatural barriers. Wild creatures graze and Wild Hunt monsters lurk nearby.

## dangers
@version: 6.3
### LARGE
Abyssal corruption, Wilderness Exiles and Wilderness Hunters, Thundercraven Rifthounds (met inside the tower in Tower of Inversion), Abyssal fog and riftlines, and the Wild Hunt in general.

## quests
@version: 6.3
### STANDARD
Tower of Inversion (Nightingale's Song, Prelude to the Night Vigil, Part 2) starts at the Pillar of Embla: the Traveler and Illuga enter the tower, clear it, purify the Abyssal core and activate a barrier core. It leads into When War Songs Rise.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the Pillar of Embla is open to exploration. Rotwang's threat from it was ended in the Nightingale's Song chapter.
### STANDARD +
The Calamity Egg that formed around the pillar was destroyed. The state of the structure afterwards is not described in the sources read.

## entry: Ratnik Diaries at Kipumaki Cliff
@keys: Filaev, Neatly-Written Diary, Ratnik diary, Frostgale Steeds
@keys2: Pillar of Embla, Kipumaki Cliff, Lightkeepers
@version: 6.3
### STANDARD
At the pillar is the first of three connected diary entries by a new Ratnik named Filaev. He was selected as the last name on the Ratniki list, proud of his uniform and personalised lantern, then assigned to a support unit at Kipumaki Cliff under Captain Asebov with Chernov, Shonokovych (a former poet) and Patolichev. After months of routine he was excited when Piramida ordered the unit to clear Wild Hunt nests around the cliff, expecting his first battle. The continuation appears at the Sanctum of Departed Heroes.
### LARGE +
A separate unsigned diary from a Frostgale Steeds unit records answering Piramida's call for reinforcements during a Wild Hunt outbreak at Kipumaki Cliff, the number of nests to clear (three), and the unit's fear on the third day as the Wild Hunt spilled onto the ramparts.

## entry: The Arrow of Terpikeraunas and the Pillar
@keys: Arrow of Terpikeraunas, Silver Lampwick
@keys2: Pillar of Embla, Piramida, Great Mead Hall
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
The Arrow of Terpikeraunas is a Hyperborean relic that purges an area of Abyssal contamination. Aila gave it to Solovei's Lightkeepers for the day Rotwang rose again. It sits in the Great Mead Hall of Piramida, built tall enough to reach the top of the Pillar of Embla, and needs the Silver Lampwick to power it. Illuga and the Traveler fired it during the Nightingale's Song chapter.
