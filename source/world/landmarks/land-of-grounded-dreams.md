## summary
### LITE
The Land of Grounded Dreams is the deepest part of Old Vanarana in the Lost Nursery, in Sumeru. It is the battlefield where the last war against Marana ended and where the Traveler defeats Marana's Avatar in the Aranyaka.
### STANDARD +
It is unlocked in the quest For All Children Who Long for Life, the third part of Nursery of Lost Dreams. After Marana's Avatar falls, Arama plants themselves here and becomes the new Ashvattha Tree.
### LARGE +
The site is shown in two states, corrupted by the Withering and purified.

## geography
### STANDARD
A cavern reached from the deepest part of Old Vanarana, after the earlier seal and nursery caves. It holds a small pool from which Marana's Avatar rises, and withering growths scattered around it.
### LARGE +
The Withering's decay accumulates here as in the earlier quest, and Arama's protective aura lessens it for the Traveler.

## people
@version: 3.0
### STANDARD
Arama guides the Traveler here, saying the Withering has fully corroded the old place and that they can now resist Marana. Aramuhukunda, according to Arama, had turned into a Vasara Tree on this spot to suppress Marana's Avatar. Arakavi, Arapacati, Arapandu and Arana also appear in the quest.
### LARGE +
Arama says Marana corroded the Vasara Tree that was meant to grow here and took its bodily form, and that if left unchecked Marana would eat away all life.

## history
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Arama says this is where the last war ended: Marana's Avatar was crushed and imprisoned with four seals. The Vasara Tree Aramuhukunda became was meant to hold it down.
### LARGE +
In the Aranyaka, Marana's attempt to rise again meets the Traveler and Arama. Arama explains that destroying the avatar is needed so the new Ashvattha Tree may be born and the Bija formed.

## quests
### STANDARD
For All Children Who Long for Life: follow Arama, destroy the tumors of the Withering in turn, defeat Marana's Avatar and its final form, then play the Rhythm of the Great Dream. Co-op is disabled for it.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Marana's Avatar and rifthounds, with Decay from the Withering.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Heavy and grieving, a site of an ended war, then a quiet place of planting once the avatar is gone.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the avatar is defeated and the new Ashvattha Tree grows from Arama; no later changes found.
### ULTRA +
The quest's trial battle limits the party to a level 80 version of the Traveler, and the site's achievement is The End of Annihilation. Both are gameplay details.

## entry: Marana's Avatar
@keys: Marana's Avatar
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Marana's Avatar is the body Marana took by corroding the Vasara Tree in the Land of Grounded Dreams. The Traveler and Arama destroy the spirit containers (tumors of the Withering) to damage it, and then defeat it so the new Ashvattha Tree can be born.
