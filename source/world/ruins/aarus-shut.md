## summary
### LITE
Aaru's Shut is the highest point of the Mausoleum of King Deshret in the Hypostyle Desert of Sumeru. The Traveler and Paimon first come here in the world quest Dual Evidence, lifted up from the Great Hall of Truths, and it can be visited freely afterwards.
### STANDARD +
Its name plays on Aaru, the dream-afterlife of the desert dwellers; the second word comes from an Egyptian word for shadow. The Scarlet Sand Slate says this place should have been paradise on earth, the jewel of the desert, but that today it looks very different.

## geography
@version: 3.1
### STANDARD
The summit of the whole Mausoleum complex, above Duat Hall and the Great Hall of Truths. After Dual Evidence it is permanently reachable by entering the central section of the Great Hall of Truths. Before the quest, a cyclone turned at the top of the pyramid and could not be approached.
### LARGE +
When the party arrives by elevator it finds sand all around the top level, with a large guardian waiting ahead. The summit changes visibly once the guardian falls.

## history
@version: 3.1
### STANDARD
In Dual Evidence the slate sends the party through Duat Hall and the three trial chambers, then through the Great Hall of Truths. Lighting three primal torches there wakes a device that carries them to the summit. The Primal Construct they defeat, billed in the game as Field Annihilation Array: The Evincing Shadow, is followed by a precious chest holding a stone slate, the Record of Aaru's Shut.
### LARGE +
The party takes the slate to Soheil in Aaru Village. He had refused to believe Tirzad's paper, but the slate and the visible change in the Mausoleum make him apologise on behalf of his family. He says nearly all records of the later period of Al-Ahmar's civilisation are lost or fragmentary and that he has never seen a slate this complete.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Open, windblown and empty after the cyclone is gone: sand to the horizon, the pyramid's stone beneath, a sense of anticlimax. Paimon expects Aaru to be lush, delicious and beautiful and is let down, which captures the scene's tone.

## dangers
### LARGE
The Primal Construct that guards the arrival point is the only enemy in the quest here. Once it is beaten the summit is safe to visit.

## quests
@version: 3.1
### STANDARD
Dual Evidence, the first quest of Old Notes and New Friends (begins after Golden Slumber). Completing it permanently turns off the cyclone over the Mausoleum.
### ULTRA +
The slate's last instruction before the ascent, to burn the embers of the sun at three places with feathers, is carried out by lighting three primal torches in the Great Hall of Truths. Soheil's change of attitude on seeing the slate is played for comedy, and the quest leaves Paimon feeling that there must be more to Aaru than this summit.

## status
### LITE
Open and safe to visit as of Version 7.1.
### STANDARD +
Nothing in the sources up to Version 7.1 changes the summit. The real Aaru is a separate matter handled in its own entry and in the Sumeru Archon Quest.
