## summary
### LITE
Caesareum Palace is a ruin in Machimos, the outer city of Remuria's drowned capital. It served as a training ground for the empire's legions and as the place where the chains meant to bind the vishap prince Scylla were stored. According to the records, the Harmost Boethius ordered Legio VI to hold the Dragonborn back here, and the legion defended it to the end.
### STANDARD +
Engravings in two caves tell the story from the empire's own point of view: orders, a plan to seal the city, and an epitaph by a soldier who died holding the line. The area is reached through the Sea of Bygone Eras after Underwater Nocturne in Canticles of Harmony.

## geography
### STANDARD
A subarea of the Sea of Bygone Eras with a western cave and an eastern cave. A nameless stone statue stands near the western cave and acts as a monument to the fallen. Praetorian Golems patrol the ground.
### LARGE +
It lies among the ruins of Machimos beside the Initium Iani gate, Portus Anticus and Hortus Euergetis. Locked chambers deep in the western cave open only after the monument's challenge has been completed.

## places
### LARGE
Western cave: holds the first engraving, an order about Legio VI, and a hidden chamber with the second, the plan to seal the city.
Eastern cave: holds the soldier's epitaph near two Praetorian Golems.
Nameless Stone Statue: a monument that begins a combat trial against Bathysmal Vishaps when approached.

## people
### STANDARD
Legio VI Rithimi Versi, part of the Legion Athanatoi, under the Harmost Boethius, who took command from Euergetia. The soldier Aetius and his sister Placidia are named in an epitaph. Euergetia and Boethius are the authorities cited in orders.

## history
### LITE
When Scylla marched Dragonborn on the capital, the Harmost Boethius gave Legio VI Rithimi Versi the task of stopping them here. The legion defended the palace until the end, with not a single deserter and no enemy breaking through, according to ancient records.
### STANDARD +
The first engraving records Euergetia entrusting Legio VI to Boethius to guard the palace, and says the Dragonborn should be received with the legion's tradition of "gently uplifting barbarians" without enmity; the next lines are crossed out. The second, an order from Boethius, commands that Portus Anticus and the Initium Iani be sealed at once, seven defensive layers set, and the routes of escape closed. The three chains made for Scylla have been moved to the palace and, if the chieftain of the wicked drakes cannot be slain, are to imprison him there forever. It ends "For the Empire, for [name erased], for human civilization".
### LARGE +
The third engraving, in the eastern cave, is the epitaph of Aetius and his sister Placidia. They did not die of illness but fell resisting the traitorous Dragonborn at the city gates; Legio VI had no deserters, and they followed Euergetia to the northern border to bring civilisation to the tribes. They choose "civilization and death". Other texts show that Scylla's invasion was actually part of Remus's secret plan to destroy Phobos, and that Scylla was lured into a trap and sealed.

## quests
### STANDARD
In The Last Day of Remuria the Traveler, Paimon and Cassiodor in cat form free Scylla by breaking three chains of his seal; the palace's engravings explain what chains of that kind were made for. The optional exploration "Symphony of Yore, Restored Once More" collects six scores for the Ancient Autoharmonic Music Box, and the score Dies Irae is earned by completing the stone statue's trial here.
### LARGE +
The trial has four waves: two Primordial Bathysmal Vishaps, then a swarm of hatchling vishaps while a buff from "ancient heroic spirits" damages nearby enemies, then the Coral Defenders. Completing it unlocks the sealed chamber and an achievement titled after the legion's lines about telling the citizens of the capital.

### ULTRA +
The engravings give a clear chain of command: Euergetia assigns Legio VI, Boethius directs it, and the soldiers are told to receive the Dragonborn as guests until the plan changes. The change is visible in the erasure of the gentle lines and in the second order, which seals the gates and prepares to trap the enemy. Two Praetorian Golems stand guard near the epitaph. The area also offers a time trial challenge of traversing.

## status
### LITE
Quiet after the Requiem. The area is explorable and its dead legionaries are at rest.
### STANDARD +
Nothing after 4.6 changes it.

## dangers
### LARGE
Praetorian Golems, bathysmal vishaps and Primordial Bathysmal Vishaps appear here, and Coral Defenders appear in the monument trial. The deep water and the Remurian machines are hostile to explorers who do not know the area's rules.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Stone caves, scarred rock and the memory of a soldier's pride. The tone of the engravings is dutiful and proud, and then desperate. The stone monument asks passers-by to say the legion did its duty.

## entry: Legio VI Rithimi Versi
@keys: Legio VI, Legio VI Rithimi Versi, Legion Athanatoi
@version: 4.6
### STANDARD
A Remurian legion of the Legion Athanatoi named for rhythm and verse. It served the northern border under Euergetia's direction and later guarded Caesareum Palace under Boethius, where it held back Scylla's Dragonborn. Its vicarius Hirtius wrote a survey of the northern tribes found in the Faded Castle.

## entry: Scylla's Chains
@keys: Scylla's chains, Dragonborn-Suppressing Shackles
@keys2: Caesareum, Remuria, Boethius, Euergetia
@version: 4.6
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
Three chains forged for Scylla, said to be made with Euergetia's blood and illegible bone, were moved here, to be used to imprison him if he could not be slain. In The Last Day of Remuria the Traveler breaks them one by one, helped by a negotiation with Legio IX golems who thought they were still at war. The same quest explains that the invasion was a pretext in Remus's own plan.
