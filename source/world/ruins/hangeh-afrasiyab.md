## summary
### LITE
Hangeh Afrasiyab is an abandoned underground factory of the lost kingdom of Khaenri'ah, hidden under Gavireh Lajavard in Sumeru's Girdle of the Sands. It once processed Azosite, an energy block drawn from ley lines, and deep inside is a sealed gate to the lost nation itself. In the quest As the Khvarena's Light Shows it holds the last Great Song of Khvarena, and Nasejuna reveals he has been using the party.
### STANDARD +
Dahri is an archaic Sumeru name for Khaenri'ah, so the quest calls this an underground Dahrian factory. By Nasejuna's reading of the records it belonged to the Schwanenritter, the knight order that fought the monsters of the cataclysm.

## geography
@version: 3.6
### STANDARD
Two map levels, upper and lower, in Gavireh Lajavard. The main hall is the energy control hub, with relays sealed in Gray Crystals, a furnace for smelting Azosite, an elevator to the deepest level and a control room above. Mourning Flowers and Trishiraite are local specialties; the Gate of Everlasting Mourning viewpoint overlooks the sealed gate.
### LARGE +
The gate bears the eight-pointed star of Khaenri'ah, and a statue in the factory resembles a Black Serpent Knight. The interior has the same industrial, brass feel as the Ruin Golems and the testing facility at Madinat al-Nuhas, and the wiki sets all of them on a grand scale. Inside are a pipe room with logs, a smelting furnace room on an upper floor, and the room at the foot of the elevator.

## history
@version: 3.6
### STANDARD
Azosite was made here by extracting pure elemental energy from ley lines and smelting it into Energy Blocks. A mutilated set of notes says the royal workshop Gnade had gone out of use and this was probably the last workshop with a full Azosite-powered system. Nasejuna, citing records, says it was owned by the Schwanenritter, as was the proving ground at Madinat al-Nuhas, and that they resisted the monsters that came from below in the cataclysm 500 years ago.
### LARGE +
An old journal found here tells of most people fleeing to the surface with the Schwanenritter while some stayed to hide; it reports rumours that those who went above fell sick and turned into monsters, and ends in hopelessness. A bulletin says that because King Irmin was indisposed, Knight Marshal Anfortas had declared himself Regent. An unfinished letter to the Marshal argues that the kingdom's prosperity came from stealing secrets from beyond the skies and that human will, not dogma, should drive technology.
### ULTRA +
According to the wiki, Rene, a researcher from Fontaine, visited around the time of the cataclysm. His investigation notes record that Azosite was stable and safe but lacked any will, since it was pure elemental force, and he compared it to the difference between a Hydro Slime and an Oceanid. A second set of torn pages by him states that Khvarena can reverse Abyssal influence and wonders whether Azosite smelting could be applied to Khvarena or to the potent energy called Amrita. These are the speculations of an outside scholar.

## dangers
### LARGE
Ruin Guards, Ruin Defenders, Ruin Cruisers, Ruin Destroyers and Ruin Graders guard the mechanisms, and hilichurls and mitachurls have wandered in. At the bottom, the Abyss Herald Klingsor and Nasejuna attack the party.

## quests
@version: 3.6
### STANDARD
As the Khvarena's Light Shows (Khvarena of Good and Evil, part 2) leads the Traveler, Paimon, Sorush and Nasejuna here to seek the fifth Great Song of Khvarena. Nasejuna opens doors, links circuits with Farrwicks to power three relays and then the central control device, and rides the elevator down with them.
### LARGE +
At the bottom Nasejuna drops his pose, calls Sorush an idiot of a Pari and says he plans to reverse the Rite of Chinvat using the Songs, releasing the Abyss and restarting the old wars. He claims the Skeptics' history is invention and the Dahri records are the true story, that those who resisted the defilement were the Skeptics' real ancestors, and that the Akademiya erased them. The Traveler and Sorush refuse to hand over the Songs. Nasejuna calls in Klingsor; both are beaten, but Nasejuna slips away, and Paimon later notes that they cannot trust his claims.

## status
### LITE
Abandoned. Some machinery still runs and the main gate toward Khaenri'ah is sealed. Nothing found up to Version 7.1 changes this.

### STANDARD
A grand gate leading to the world beneath the soil, sealed tight. Its viewpoint text adds that the once prosperous kingdom behind it has long since turned to rubble.

## entry: Nasejuna's Revelation
@keys: Nasejuna, Rite of Chinvat, Vijnanapati
@keys2: Hangeh Afrasiyab, Klingsor, Great Song of Khvarena
@version: 3.6
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
Nasejuna, who calls himself Vijnanapati of his Order, lured Sorush into the Rite of Chinvat to get at the Great Songs and had arranged for Klingsor to be waiting at the factory. He wanted to reverse the rite, which would unseal the Abyss, and believed the Dahri records over the Skeptics' own. Both he and the Abyss Herald are defeated, and he escapes.
