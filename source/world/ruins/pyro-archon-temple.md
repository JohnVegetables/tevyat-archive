## summary
### LITE
The Pyro Archon Temple is a hidden temple under the Easybreeze Holiday Resort area of Natlan, first entered in the quest The Last Survivor of Tenochtzitoc (5.8). Citlali guesses it was built about five hundred years ago by Pyro Archons to hold the Bakunawa. Its central stone structure opens onto the Night Kingdom and the Mare Jivari.
### STANDARD +
'Pyro Archon Temple' is an unofficial name used by the community wiki. It is an instance, reached again later through a mystic arts implement in the Concealed Land of Enigmas, a cave behind the waterfall west of Wavey Bay.

## geography
### STANDARD
Beneath the Tenochtzitoc area. A central stone structure sits in the main hall; seven memorial stones stand behind it, five tablets are set near a waterfall at the back, and two side rooms hold a table each. Touching the central structure moves the player into a Night Kingdom-like space, whose portal leads to the Mare Jivari.

## places
### STANDARD
The memorial stones carry short inscriptions, each for a hero of the old coalition: Tupac the giant, Menilek the brave, Sundjatta chief of the mines, Tenoch the everlasting (his remains are buried here), Wanjiru the great runner, Sanhaj Kompore the Border Master (his remains are buried here) and young Burkina.
### LARGE +
A worn tablet by the entrance bears words attributed to Shulevitz about cloaking the Enigmatic Land and shrouding the palace so that only the legacy of heroes may awaken it. Tablets by the waterfall mention Bakunawa's revival, blocking the Mare Jivari and holding back the Black Tide (signed Gloharry), and a hero Kiongozi gone for over a decade, with a shrine built to honour the fallen. A wind-worn booklet in the left room, signed Cochanina, talks of sealing the borders and containing the Mare Jivari as a task for more than one generation. A newer notebook in the right room, signed Isaja, reads only 'Task complete. Now I sing!'

## history
### STANDARD
Citlali's hypothesis is that the temple was raised five hundred years ago by Pyro Archons to temporarily contain the Bakunawa, a calamity left from the Natlan cataclysm. The inscriptions suggest several generations of guardians kept the seal and hid the place.
### LARGE +
The tablets use names of heroes of the cataclysm era; Tenoch and Sanhaj are the two who died destroying the Bakunawa's body at the Mare Jivari. Citlali does not recognise the concealment techniques used.

## dangers
### STANDARD
The Bakunawa, a vast permeated body, can be fought from the Mare Jivari side; after defeat it is only sealed for a time.

## travel
### STANDARD
Enter the Concealed Land of Enigmas, activate the mystic arts implement, then touch the central stone structure to enter the Night Kingdom-like area and the portal to the Mare Jivari.
### ULTRA +
Citlali loses contact with the Wayob on entering but still feels the Night Kingdom's presence; the Traveler suggests this is due to Sanhaj's phlogiston engraving having made a miniature Night Kingdom. The spirit trace Citlali left on Enjou was consumed by a strange power once he entered the true Mare Jivari.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
A silent, weathered memorial hall: worn stone, a hidden waterfall, and tablets whose inscriptions have mostly eroded, so that few names and half-sentences are left.

## status
### STANDARD
Open to visitors in the instance as of 7.1. The Bakunawa stays sealed between fights, and the wider Mare Jivari has not been opened beyond the 5.8 area.

## entry: Memorial Inscriptions of the Temple
@keys: Tupac, Menilek, Sundjatta, Wanjiru, Burkina, Gloharry, Cochanina, Isaja, Shulevitz
@keys2: Pyro Archon Temple, Mare Jivari, Bakunawa
### LARGE
Names carved or signed in the temple: Tupac, Menilek, Sundjatta, Tenoch, Wanjiru, Sanhaj Kompore and Burkina on the memorial stones; Shulevitz (the cloaking words), Gloharry (the warning about the Black Tide), Cochanina (the booklet) and Isaja (the closing notebook). The wiki gives no further information about most of them.
