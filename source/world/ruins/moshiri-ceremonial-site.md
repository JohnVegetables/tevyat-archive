## summary
### LITE
The Moshiri Ceremonial Site is the sacred ground of the Thunderbird Civilization on Tsurumi Island in Inazuma. In the world quest series Through the Mists, illusions of the island's long-dead people gather here to repeat their last ceremony for the Thunderbird Kanna Kapatcir, under a curse that replays the island's destruction.
### STANDARD +
The Traveler visits it repeatedly, speaking to the island's priest and other villagers before learning that they are echoes. The boy Ruu, who died here as a sacrifice, is the one figure on the island who knows the loop is happening. The site has a Statue of The Seven and a Teleport Waypoint, and nearby wildlife includes hilichurls and Abyss Mages.

## geography
### STANDARD
A site on Tsurumi Island. A mural in the Tsurumi Underground Ruins appears to show the mountain on the way to it (the wiki marks this as needing verification). It has an Electro Elemental Monument, a Statue of The Seven, a Lightning Strike Probe, Mysterious Carvings, Electro Cubes and a Thunder Sakura Bough. Fluorescent Fungus and Sea Ganoderma grow nearby.
### LARGE +
Three Pyro Abyss Mages occupy the area on a rotating schedule: one on the first day, two on the second and three on the third.

## places
### STANDARD
The site is a cleared ceremonial ground, which locals prepare in A Particularly Particular Author. Three Perches elsewhere on the island (Chirai Shrine, Shirikoro Peak, the Autake Plains) belong to the same rite. Mt. Kanna, where the Thunderbird once dwelt and where a sacrificial altar stood, lies beyond.

## people
### STANDARD
Illusory villagers: the island's priest Mata, who is Ruu's father; an old man Abe who talks of fungi to bring after the ceremony; and other islanders. Ruu himself, unaffected by the curse, is the only one who understands what is happening. All other islanders are echoes.
### LARGE +
Kama, the last descendant of the Thunderbird people, lives in Ritou, not on the island. Mata hoped the ceremony would finally please the Great Thunderbird. Before the sacrifice, Mata also thought Ruu favoured by the Thunderbird and planned to offer him.

## history
### STANDARD
The Thunderbird Civilization lived on Tsurumi over 1,500 years ago and worshipped Kanna Kapatcir. When the island's situation worsened and some people left into the "sea of darkness", Ruu offered himself as a living sacrifice. He died here, and his blood was set in a goblet at Mt. Kanna. Kapatcir, who had befriended Ruu through his singing, saw this as the murder of an innocent and as the breaking of his promise to sing again; in rage and grief she destroyed the people and cursed the island to repeat its last days until she heard his song once more.
### LARGE +
In The Sea of Fog and the Rite of the Trees the Traveler destroys the Perches to stop the rite; a crimson lightning storm breaks over the site and Ruu, believing the ceremony would please the Thunderbird, flees upset. In The Sun-Wheel and Mt. Kanna, the Traveler shows Ruu Kapatcir's memories, and the group brings him to Seirai Island, where the Raiden Shogun killed the Thunderbird, so that he can sing to her remains. The curse is lifted, the fog disperses and the spirits of the islanders are guided onwards by a Boatman.
### ULTRA +
The Thunderbird Civilization wrote in Ishine Script and had a strict hierarchy with priests and religious officials near the top. It made an instrument called the Maushiro from the Thunderbird's feathers so that its people could communicate through the fog (later a wooden imitation was used) and taught its children to stay within the island, believing those who left were lost in the sea of darkness. Kama, who lives in Ritou, is its only known living descendant.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Before the curse is lifted: a sense of preparation without end, villagers repeating their lines, grandparents anxious that the ceremony go well, and dread beneath the cheerfulness. After: stillness on a quiet hillside, with only monsters and the faint thin mist that still drifts over the island.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Hilichurls, Electro Hilichurl Shooters, Hydro Samachurls and Hydro Slimes; elite Pyro Abyss Mages, Rockfond Rifthound Whelps, Ruin Defenders and Ruin Destroyers. During the quests, fog on the island teleports travellers away.

## travel
### STANDARD
A Teleport Waypoint and a Waverider Waypoint are at the site. The Traveler first goes there to find Ruu in A Particularly Particular Author and returns during Octave of the Maushiro.

## quests
### STANDARD
Through the Mists (2.2): A Particularly Particular Author, Octave of the Maushiro, The Sea of Fog and the Rite of the Trees, The Sun-Wheel and Mt. Kanna. Follow-up hidden objectives, such as "Boatman"'s Task, help the island's spirits find closure. The series requires Seirai Stormchasers first.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the site is empty. The curse over Tsurumi Island has been lifted and the Thunderbird Civilization's spirits have moved on; nothing in later content revisits it.

### STANDARD
Ruu is a young quest NPC and the child of the island's last priest Mata. He loved singing, which drew the interest of the Thunderbird, whom he named Kanna Kapatcir; they promised to meet again. When people began leaving the island, he offered himself as a sacrifice at the Moshiri Ceremonial Site. He alone remains aware of the curse and re-lives the ceremony. See his own entry for his full story.

## entry: The Thunderbird's Curse
@keys: Thunderbird's curse, Kapatcir's curse, Tsurumi time loop, Sea of Fog and the Rite of the Trees
@keys2: Tsurumi, Ruu, Moshiri
@version: 2.2
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
Kapatcir's curse made Tsurumi repeat the last day of its people. Adventurers sent to fetch a Maushiro, a feather-made instrument, never returned with a real one because the Maushiro that the islanders gave them vanished. The curse ended when Ruu sang for Kapatcir's remains on Seirai Island.
