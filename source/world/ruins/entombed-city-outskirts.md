## summary
### LITE
The Entombed City - Outskirts is the outer edge of Sal Vindagnyr's buried city on Dragonspine. The official description says the city has been entombed by snow so deeply that it is nearly indistinguishable from its former self. Cryo hilichurls and the Cryo Hypostasis make it dangerous.
### STANDARD +
A stone monument here is tied to the kingdom's last scribe,.

## geography
### STANDARD
The subarea sits on Dragonspine's slopes with a Teleport Waypoint, a Cryo elemental monument, pressure plates, ruin braziers, Scarlet Quartz, Ancient Rime and Warming Seelie to manage the cold. A weathered stone monument lies to the west of the waypoint.
### LARGE +
The Cryo Hypostasis's arena lies in the outskirts; a cavern below it holds one of the Ancient Carvings and is reached by an entrance near the waypoint that opens during the quest In the Mountains. The wiki also places one of the three ice-bound spheres from the split Skyfrost Nail in an underground chamber near the outskirts.

## history
### STANDARD
The outskirts belonged to Sal Vindagnyr. After the Skyfrost Nail split, its pieces released orbs that froze the places where they stopped: one deep in Starglow Cavern, one in a chamber near the city's outskirts, and one shattered the kingdom's white tree.
### LARGE +
The scribe Ukko, last keeper of the kingdom, tried to heal the withered ley lines, buried the princess, and left a bitter last message: a wish that the world burn, and hope that a "new nation without gods" might stand against it. Offering Cecilia at the weathered monument to the west of the waypoint reveals the scribe's box containing this message.
### ULTRA +
The Ancient Carvings that record the kingdom's story are likely the scribe's work, and one of the eight sits in the cavern below the Cryo Hypostasis arena. Together they are the unlock for the hidden Mural Room.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Ice Shield hilichurl guards, Cryo hilichurl grenadiers and shooters, Cryo samachurls, Ice Shieldwall Mitachurls, Frostarm Lawachurls and the Cryo Hypostasis, plus the cold itself.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Snowdrifts over broken walls, a few blue-glowing plants, and an enormous silence in which a half-buried city seems to wait.

## status
### LITE
Frozen ruins, unchanged as of 7.1.

## entry: Stone monument near the outskirts waypoint
@keys: weathered stone monument, Scribe's Box

### STANDARD
A stone monument west of the outskirts waypoint, its writing worn away by wind. Placing three Cecilia flowers there causes the Scribe's Box to appear. The box carries the scribe Ukko's last wishes for the world to end in flames and for a nation without gods to rise.
