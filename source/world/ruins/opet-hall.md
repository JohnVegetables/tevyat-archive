## summary
### LITE
Opet Hall is an underground chamber just north of the Mausoleum of King Deshret in Sumeru's Hypostyle Desert. The Traveler enters it in the world quest The Secret of Al-Ahmar, part of Golden Slumber.
### STANDARD +
The Scarlet Sand Slate describes it as a place where many blessings gathered that reached King Deshret's throne directly, and where the devout won the right to the throne room. Its name recalls the Opet Festival of ancient Thebes.

## geography
@version: 3.1
### STANDARD
Lies north of the Mausoleum, with a lower level reached through tunnels on either side of the main entrance. Its south side appears to join the Great Hall of Truths, but that way cannot be used and visitors must come through Duat Hall.
### LARGE +
The hall has the usual King Deshret ruin machinery: primal torches and sconces, primal embers, primal obelisks, sacred seals, an Everlight Cell, a Plinth of the Secret Rites, a verity cell and a primal sandglass.

## history
@version: 3.1
### STANDARD
In The Secret of Al-Ahmar, Tirzad's expedition (Jeht, Jebrael, Benben and the Traveler) works through the Mausoleum. After being separated, they learn Benben has been taken. The Traveler fights Samail and the Fatui and recovers it, then goes deeper through the ruins, lighting torches to open the way. The wiki places this last stretch of the quest in Opet Hall.
### LARGE +
A rock wall breaks to reveal a Primal Construct, and a second symbol lights on the Scarlet Sand Slate, projecting a map. Tirzad reads the inscriptions as praise meant to whitewash Deshret's rule, a position that contradicts what he said earlier. The group then agrees to get back to the oasis camp where Nachtigal's merchants were left.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Hot, echoing stone, torchlight and pale sand dust. The mood in the quest is half academic bickering, half unease at being followed by Fatui and Eremites.

## dangers
### LARGE
Listed enemies are Eremites, Fatui Skirmishers, Fatui Cicin Mages and Primal Constructs.

## quests
@version: 3.1
### STANDARD
The Secret of Al-Ahmar is the third part of Golden Slumber. The hall also features in the Chessboard of Safhe Shatranj.
### ULTRA +
During the quest Samail, who calls himself of the Thutmose, speaks to Jebrael as an old comrade, and Jeht learns that her father was once one of the Thutmose; Jebrael says Samail killed Jeht's mother. These conversations belong to the quest and to Jebrael and Samail's entries rather than to the hall.
