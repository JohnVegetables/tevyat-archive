## summary
### LITE
Bravais' Press Works is a hidden underground workshop in the Submerged Stony Path below the Liffey Region of Fontaine. Its big Forging Press refines Arkhium Stock (fused from seven Mysterious Ores) into Arkhium Lumenite. Running the press ends with the whole chamber flooding, so it can be used only once. Its paperwork mentions a Senior Researcher named Bravais, whom nobody else seems to care about.
### STANDARD +
It is the site of the hidden exploration objective that the wiki calls Arkhium Forge (an unofficial name), released with Version 4.1. A passage opened by the finished work leads to a current ending at ruins beneath a Statue of the Seven.

## geography
### STANDARD
Three routes reach it: a waterway from a Millennial Pearl Seahorse's chamber; a hidden tunnel in Kuisel's Clockwork Workshop just before its last room (open after the quest Road to the Singularity); and a water current at the end of the Geode Mine Shaft (open after An Eye for an Eye).
### LARGE +
The chamber has a central Forging Press with its terminal, three steam valves with handwheels, a cooling and drainage terminal on a lower ramp north-west, a blocked water intake at the bottom and drainage pipes later clogged by Red Meanies.

## places
### LARGE
The press and main terminal; the cooling and drainage terminal; the desk with the Maintenance Log; the Emergency Handbook beneath the entrance platform. Beyond the finished press lies a newly opened passage with a precious chest, then a current. The wiki also names a Bravais' Hidden Study that the Lumenite opens, with luxurious and precious chests.

## people
### STANDARD
Senior Researcher Bravais is the only named person. The Emergency Handbook tells operators to contact Bravais about any fault it does not list. Beyond the press, the current ends at the ruins under the Liffey Statue of the Seven where Desnos is found.

## governance
### LARGE
The workshop follows written standards: protective gear, a full equipment inspection, correct stock placement, an energy supply check, and manual operation of the hammers at the right moment when the stock glows orange-red. Faults are handled by the Emergency Response Handbook, third edition.

## history
### LARGE
Canon says little. The Maintenance Log shows an attempt to keep the cooling system and leaking seals under control with stopgaps. Repair requests note leakage near the water intake and say a temporary sealant was applied and the cooling system's use suspended because of backflow. The log notes that no one but Bravais really cares about the devices, and hopes the emergency drain proves useful.
### ULTRA +
The hazards were known: the log warns that removing the seals and running the cooling would fill the room at once and ruin the press. The player eventually triggers exactly that, so the press is destroyed by design at the end of the process.

## status
### LITE
After the Arkhium Lumenite is collected the press is flooded and ruined; the workshop remains submerged. Nothing newer than 4.1 is recorded.

## dangers
### LARGE
Steam valves, overheating, Pyro Slimes that spawn from the heated press, a large Pyro Slime, nimble harvester meks near the handwheel, and rising water. In-game this is a puzzle of pressure, heat, cooling and drainage.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Hissing valves, orange glow from the stock, then a deep flooded hall. The log sounds like a tired technician writing to nobody.

## entry: The Forging Press Process
@keys: Forging Press, Arkhium Stock, Arkhium Lumenite, Mysterious Ore
@keys2: Bravais' Press Works, Arkhium Forge
@version: 4.1
### LARGE
Gameplay-derived process (labelled as game mechanics). The operator closes three steam valves to restore pipe pressure, places the Arkhium Stock, powers and starts the press, and times the hammer strikes when the stock glows orange-red. After overheating, slimes are defeated, the press is cooled with Hydro or Cryo, then flooded by breaking a blocked water intake. Xenochromatic Hunter's Ray and Hat Jellyfish abilities clear the drainage pipes of Red Meanies. A final run ruptures a seal and floods the chamber, after which the Lumenite can be taken.

## entry: The Maintenance Log and Emergency Handbook
@keys: Maintenance Log, Emergency Response Handbook, Senior Researcher Bravais
@keys2: Press Works, Forging Press
@version: 4.1
### STANDARD
The log laments stopgap fixes and that only Bravais cares about the machinery. The Third Edition handbook lists faults: insufficient energy supply from low pipe pressure, system overheated, water entering the system, and a blocked emergency drain, and says to contact Bravais for anything else.
