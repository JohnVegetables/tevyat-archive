## summary
### LITE
The Remnants of Tetenanco are ancient ruins left by the dragons in Atocpan, Natlan. They are thought to be a satellite city destroyed in the war between the dragons and Xbalanque, the first Pyro Archon. Locals now call them the Remnants of the Secret Source excavation site.
### STANDARD +
A warning notice just south of the guard Mposo says the site is temporarily closed to the public because of frequent recent volcanic eruptions. Mposo guards the site.

## geography
### STANDARD
A subarea of Atocpan, a cluster of dragon-made ruins now set up as an excavation site.

## people
### STANDARD
Mposo watches over the site. Excavators and researchers are implied by the site's use, but only Mposo is placed there.

## history
### STANDARD
The ruins are believed to have been a satellite city of the dragon civilisation, destroyed in the war between the dragons and Xbalanque. The name Tetenanco comes from Nahuatl and means 'at the ramparts', per the wiki's etymology note.
### LARGE +
The 'Secret Source' in the local name points to the Secret Source Mechanism technology the Natlantean dragons used for their automata and constructs; the quest A Brief History of Rocks is where the dragon-era link is stated.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Frequent volcanic eruptions, which is why the site is closed.

## quests
### STANDARD
A list of Remnants of Tetenanco quests is attached to the location; A Brief History of Rocks is the one used here as the source of its past.
### ULTRA +
The Chinese name translates roughly as 'Traces of the Lone Remnants' and the Japanese as 'Remnant Ruins'. The wiki records no lore text, books or tablets at the site beyond the warning notice.

## status
### STANDARD
Closed to the public in-world because of eruptions, as of the latest information read (7.1 cutoff).
