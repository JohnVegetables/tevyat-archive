## summary
### LITE
The Entombed City - Ancient Palace is the frozen palace district of Sal Vindagnyr, an ancient kingdom on the mountain now called Dragonspine. The official description calls it an ancient city that lies silent beneath the eternal ice. Cryo hilichurls prowl its ruins.
### STANDARD +
The kingdom was founded by refugees and ruined when the Skyfrost Nail fell, turning a green mountain into a snowfield. Its last inhabitants left painted frescoes and word-boxes that tell what happened.

## geography
### STANDARD
On Dragonspine, with a Statue of The Seven, Warming Seelie, Scarlet Quartz and Ancient Rime to clear. A tower on the leftmost bend of the route above the palace holds the Priest's Box. A sealed cave door near the palace leads to the Mural Room.
### LARGE +
Scarlet Quartz and Warming Seelie help against the cold. The Mural Room door opens after the Hidden Exploration Objective Dragonspine's Glacial Secret is completed. A chest near the palace's cave door holds the first of three investigation journals used in the world quest A Land Entombed.

## history
### STANDARD
Refugees from a land of snow and strife found a lush green mountain, raised a monument on its peak and founded Sal Vindagnyr, building their capital and palace there. They prospered for a time under Celestia's blessings. Then the Skyfrost Nail fell from the sky, clouds and snowstorms swallowed the city, and the kingdom died.
### LARGE +
The chief priest Varuch's daughter, gifted with foresight, painted her visions as frescoes, including a black dragon spreading scarlet poison over the land. Varuch climbed the mountain seeking guidance and wanted a third fresco of thawing ice, which she could not paint. The kingdom's great white tree withered; the princess died and the scribe Ukko buried her. The outlander Imunlaukr, who had left to seek a cure, came back to find everyone dead. Five hundred years ago the princess's dream came true when the dragon Durin fell on the mountain.
### ULTRA +
Centuries later an aristocratic investigation team from before the Knights of Favonius came to the mountain; its journals, found at three sites (one near this palace), suggest that none returned to Mondstadt, though one member, Eberhart, may have escaped. Ruin Guards found on the mountain bear fragmentary records that read, when combined, that the nation could not forgo this skyborne power but failed.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Deadly cold unless warmed by Scarlet Quartz or Seelie, plus Ice Shield hilichurl guards, Cryo hilichurl grenadiers and shooters, Cryo samachurls and the Great Snowboar King.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Buried ruins under permanent snow, a hush broken by wind and the crack of ice, carvings half eroded, and a sense of a people frozen mid-sentence.

## status
### LITE
Still buried in ice as of 7.1; no later change is recorded.

### STANDARD
Sal Vindagnyr was a kingdom on the green mountain Vindagnyr, now Dragonspine, in the unified-civilisation era. The Skyfrost Nail split into three and its fragments froze the land; its great white tree was shattered. Its entire population died. The Imunlaukr Clan descended from the outlander Imunlaukr, who left the kingdom for wars.
