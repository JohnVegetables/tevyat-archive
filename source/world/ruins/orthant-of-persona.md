## summary
### LITE
The Orthant of Persona is the last of the four Seals of Chymical Marriage left by the Narzissenkreuz Ordo in the Morte Region of Fontaine. Its riddle ends with "the first droplet", and the answer that opens it is an Oceanid. It holds the Persona piece, the most important of the four substances for forging the Holy Blade of Narzissenkreuz, which Jakob wants and which Caterpillar gives the Traveler to keep.
### STANDARD +
Added in 4.2. It is the "final seal" of the quest chain, and in Search in the Algae Sea it is where Jakob's trap leads the Traveler to the Tower of Gestalt.

## geography
### STANDARD
An underground area in the Morte Region whose entrance is sealed. Its position is not described beyond that.
### LARGE +
The entrance is shut by the riddle, not by a key; Ann, whom Jakob calls an Oceanid, opens it.

## people
### STANDARD
Ann, Caterpillar and Seymour accompany the Traveler. Jakob Ingold is the rival who set a trap and sealed the space; the Master, Narzissenkreuz, supposedly excised his persona here.
### LARGE +
Ann, whom Jakob calls an Oceanid, is tied to Lyris and Mary-Ann Guillotin; Caterpillar is a hilichurl who knows the Ordo's language and lore; Seymour is a mechanical dog. Their stories are in their own entries.

## culture
### LARGE
The slate quotes the "Third Hymn", Movement 5, Stanza 1, praising Remus as "player of the grand symphony, king of all the world", and begging the Merciful One for eternal life when Sybilla's Golden Bee gives its warning.

## history
### LARGE
Jakob tells the Traveler that the Persona section is the most important, because the persona the Master excised lies there. To keep it, he says he set a trap before activating the Orthant and sealed the space. He says he must forge the Holy Blade and place it in his Master's hand before the "second deluge" of the Prophecy of Fontaine.
### ULTRA +
Jakob says the Blade would let the Master gather the wills of everyone in Fontaine, the continent and the planet unto himself to survive the deluge, which Paimon calls bundling consciousness without consent. After the fight in the Tower of Gestalt he claims to have reversed the Seal of Chymical Marriage, calling it an old Fontainian ritual meant to seal off the source of life; the claim and its results are tied to the 4.2 story, not to this site.

## quests
### STANDARD
Thalia and Melpomene: head to the final seal, solve the Orthant puzzle with the riddle ("Always united... born in an instant, often contradicting, I accompany you in eternal rest... the first droplet"), and obtain the Persona piece. The answers to earlier Orthants were "shadow", "life" and "love". In the world quest Search in the Algae Sea, interacting with the final seal sends the Traveler to Jakob's trap in the Tower of Gestalt.
### LARGE +
Three variant dialogue versions exist depending on whether the Search in the Algae Sea quest was started and in what order the seals were visited. Seymour cannot analyse the Persona, which reads as zero mass.

## status
### LITE
Open; the Persona piece is in the Traveler's hands. Nothing newer than 4.2 is recorded.

## dangers
### LARGE
Jakob's traps and the machinery of the Orthant itself.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
A quiet, tense chamber where the water itself is the key. After the piece is taken, the mood turns from search to confrontation.

## entry: The Orthant Riddle Answers
@keys: Orthant riddles, first droplet, riddle slate
@keys2: Orthant of Persona, Orthant of Souls, Orthant of Wishes, Orthant of Memory
@version: 4.2
### STANDARD
The four slates share a refrain ("Born in an instant, often contradicting, your company in eternal rest") with a changing final line. Answers in the order they appear in the quests: shadow (Memory), life (Wishes), love (Souls), and the first droplet or Oceanid (Persona).
