## summary
### LITE
The Secret Vault is a hidden vault in the Fontaine Research Institute of Kinetic Energy Engineering Region, with its entrance east of the Central Laboratory Ruins. It holds a stone tablet of past Marechaussee Hunters' names and deeds, and some old weapons.
### STANDARD
In the story quest Buried Honor, the group of Clorinde, Navia, Furina, Lyney and the Traveler plays a tabletop adventure whose "treasure" turns out to be real Hunter heritage. The vault's door needs four keys, passed down through generations of Hunters, to form the complete key. What lies inside is no gold: a tablet and worn weapons, which the characters understand to be the Hunters' true legacy. It first appeared in Version 4.7.
### LARGE +
The same cave is tied to a seal on a Devorator kept by a Hunter family, a duty its latest heir neglected.

## geography
### STANDARD
Entrance east of the Central Laboratory Ruins, hidden by a rock that can be blown open; beyond lies a water-filled cave and then a quiet narrow passage ending at a stone door with a pattern mechanism.
### LARGE +
Puzzles in the approach use water-volume detection crystals, and each puzzle yields a key part. A treasure map found in the story marks the way.

## history
### LARGE
In the quest, the door is described as sealed for more than a hundred years. Clorinde recognises the keys as Marechaussee Hunter keys passed from generation to generation; four are needed to form the complete key, and she holds her own, inherited from her Master. Florian, a descendant of Hunters, had acquired three keys by theft and force and wanted the fourth.
### ULTRA +
Florian explains that his family served as Marechaussee Hunters for generations and also passed down a seal, ancient in origin, which they were told to tend. After his father died he neglected it, and when he checked it his memory went hazy. Clorinde suspects the influence of a Devorator, a monster active several centuries ago that amplifies obsessions and desires; some Hunters sealed such creatures hoping for a permanent solution later. Florian recovers after the monster leaves him.

## people
### STANDARD
Clorinde, Navia, Furina, Lyney and the Traveler open it; Florian, a Hunter descendant, intended to seize it.

## dangers
### STANDARD
In the quest, explosives had been planted in the cave; Clorinde dismantled them. The path also includes a fight with a possessed Florian.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Hushed and ancient; the writing describes it as so quiet that the secret there seems to breathe.

## quests
### STANDARD
Buried Honor, part 3 of the Silent Night act in Clorinde's story chapter (Rapperia Chapter).

## status
### LITE
Opened; the tablet and weapons remain.
### STANDARD
As of 7.1 it stays accessible as an exploration point holding the Hunters' tablet and weapons.
