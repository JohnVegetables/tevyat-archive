## summary
### LITE
Chiwang Terrace is an ancient terrace of ritual on Mt. Laixin, the sacred mountain of Chenyu Vale in southern Liyue, where the vale's spirit veins meet. It is above the clouds and open to the sky, and it is where the old people of the vale were said to speak with the heavens. It became central when the adeptus Fujin and the Traveler stopped her old friend Lingyuan from rewriting the spirit veins there.
### STANDARD +
Its design matches an altar remembered from the lost civilisation of Lang-Gan, which suggests the terrace is a keepsake of that people, who fled the Chasm and settled in Chenyu Vale. It is known to the wider world only through the vale's local lore and a few adepti.

## geography
### STANDARD
The terrace stands on Mt. Laixin, called "the sacred mountain" because the spirit veins of Chenyu Vale converge there; whoever controls the mountain can reshape the land of the whole vale. In the world quest it is a separate quest domain reached by a ritual gate, with the sky and cloud sea around it. Nearby are the cave shelters and murals of Chizhang Wall, and the Votive Rainjade at Carp's Rest, whose offerings are unlocked through a follow-up quest.
### LARGE +
The terrace is a raised, open stone platform where the spirit veins can be read as flowing lines of adeptal energy. Mt. Laixin as a whole is a restricted area that opens only through the 4.4 world quest series.

## history
### STANDARD
Lan Yan, whose family comes from Chenyu Vale, says the vale's ancient people used the terrace to commune with the heavens. The wiki ties it to the Lang-Gan altar where Zibai was honoured; see the uncertainties on whether it is a copy or the original. After the Chasm was created by a Celestial Nail, the refugees of Lang-Gan carried their traditions to Chenyu Vale, where later adepti such as Fujin taught the Rainjade Rite of jade offerings.
### LARGE +
In the 4.4 quest "An Ancient Sacrifice of Sacred Brocade", Lingyuan, a suanni adeptus who longs for the untamed vale of the distant past, had been quietly altering the spirit veins for ages, which spread miasma as a side effect. Fujin, a golden carp spirit who loved humans, opened the gate to the terrace with a ritual, and the Traveler destroyed the Miasmic Simulacra Lingyuan used to redirect the flow until she yielded. Fujin then spent all her gathered strength healing the soil and water and reverted to her carp form.
## atmosphere
### LARGE
A quiet height of weathered stone above a sea of cloud; wind, mist and jade-green light over the vale; a place that feels like a stage made for prayer, with the slow hum of spirit veins under the feet. Anything held there is about promises made to heaven and land.

## quests
### STANDARD
"An Ancient Sacrifice of Sacred Brocade" (4.4), the third quest of "Chenyu's Blessings of Sunken Jade", unlocks the terrace. It is also mentioned in Act I of "A Lanternlit Ode to the Silver Moon".

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 Lingyuan has promised not to hinder Fujin's work, the spirit veins are calm, and the terrace is a quiet relic of older rites.
### STANDARD +
Fujin remains in carp form. Remaining miasma in Chenyu Vale is a separate problem for visitors.

## entry: Chiwang Terrace and the Lang-Gan altar

@availability: limited
### STANDARD
Chenyu Vale tradition says the ancient people communed with the heavens at Chiwang Terrace. The wiki and the 6.3 event link it to the jade altar of Lang-Gan, where lanterns and offerings were presented to the Three Moons and to Zibai. Whether the terrace is a rebuilt copy or the original altar carried from the Chasm is left open by canon. The event happened during Lantern Rite 2026.

### ULTRA
During the Lantern Rite event "A Lanternlit Ode to the Silver Moon" (6.3, later kept as a replayable story quest), a photograph of the moon with a bite taken from it, shot above Chiwang Terrace, linked the terrace to the Chasm in the investigators' minds. Zizhi, a remnant of Zibai's soul, said the altar looked just like the one used in Lang-Gan to honour Zibai, and a vision showed the people of Lang-Gan releasing lanterns at such an altar under a Trilunar Syzygy before the Celestial Nail fell.
