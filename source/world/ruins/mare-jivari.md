## summary
### LITE
The Mare Jivari ('Silent Sea of Ashes') was a region at the far west of Tenochtzitoc in Natlan, once a sea of fire and now described as an endless plain of ash where the wind does not blow. It earned its name in a great battle of the cataclysm, when Tenoch and Sanhaj Kompore destroyed the Bakunawa there. Barbatos later blew it off the timeline, so it is cut off from the world.
### STANDARD +
Since version 5.8 a small corner is reachable through the Pyro Archon Temple, as a restricted story-mode area in which the Bakunawa can be fought. The rest is promised for later versions. As of 7.1 no further expansion is recorded on the pages read.

## geography
### STANDARD
The westernmost part of Tenochtzitoc (now the Easybreeze Holiday Resort area). Loading text calls it an endless plain of ash where the wind does not blow. The playable portion is reached by touching the central structure in the Pyro Archon Temple and entering a portal in a Night Kingdom-like area, and is affected by Smoldering Ashes; it can be left only through the portal that appears when the Bakunawa is defeated, or by exiting the story mode.
### LARGE +
An ancient shield near the Bakunawa's arena carries fading etchings about faint outlines of structures in the distance, familiar shapes and a plea to remember everything that happened.

## people
### STANDARD
No living inhabitants. Souls of people who died there, local Monetoo, and Abyssal monsters remain. Traces of earlier visitors include the Mondstadt adventurer Stanley and Tecpatl's Flintblades.
### LARGE +
Because of the temporal disruption, the souls of those who perished there were bound to the land, and foreign souls could not return home. Some of these souls merged with Monetoo; about twenty years ago Asha began to be sighted around Tenochtzitoc.

## history
### STANDARD
Legend says a phoenix lived in the sea of fire, worshipped as a totem and read by kings as a sign of nobility. A sage, the Lavawalker, made a circlet that could withstand the flames; his teacher and peers called this blasphemy and ordered him in, and he survived, wandering in and out for another hundred years until the fire burned him to ash. About a thousand years ago the Wanderer's Troupe came here while performing for foes, and its flautist met the Bakunawa, from which the troupe carved the Runestone.
### LARGE +
In the cataclysm the Abyss invaded Natlan and the Bakunawa, west of Tenochtzitoc, devoured entire villages. The settlement of Tenochtzitoc fought it until only Tenoch remained. He rallied the quarrelling tribes against the black tide and gathered six allies, forming the coalition, and began a campaign at the Mare Jivari. Legend says Sanhaj, a Border Master of the Masters of the Night-Wind, summoned a miniature Night Kingdom to destroy the calamity. In reality he engraved phlogiston onto Tenoch's body, Tenoch let the Bakunawa swallow him, and when Sanhaj triggered the array the clash of the Abyss, the Lord of the Night and Tenoch's spirit fractured the Bakunawa into countless pieces, killing both men.
### ULTRA +
The Bakunawa could still be revived and its power could corrupt the passage of time, so Barbatos, before his long hibernation, blew the entire Mare Jivari off the timeline to stop Abyssal corruption spreading. This explains why the region was created 500 years ago yet recorded for a thousand. Twenty to thirty years before the story, travellers reported a mountain-sized dark creature roaming it, after which the whole region vanished; settlement then increased around it, and the Easybreeze Holiday Resort was set up soon after the Night Warden Wars. Stanley of Mondstadt crossed it with a novice, Hans Archibald, and was killed protecting him; Hans took the name Stanley to keep the man's tales alive. The infant Bennett survived an Abyssal ambush on Tecpatl's Flintblades there.

## dangers
### STANDARD
The Bakunawa, a huge permeated body with Anemo, Cryo and Geo attacks that ignores dash i-frames; Abyssal monsters in the simulated version; and smouldering ash. Many adventurers who tried to prove themselves here fell. After a defeat the Bakunawa is sealed only temporarily.

## quests
### STANDARD
In the Sunspray Summer Resort event (limited-time) Mavuika and Barbatos (now Venti) open a rift between an Abyssal simulation of the Mare Jivari and the real one so that Venti can guide lost Mondstadter souls home; Bennett is targeted by the Abyss for escaping as an infant. In The Last Survivor of Tenochtzitoc the Traveler, Paimon and Citlali stop Enjou and enter the restricted real area, where they restrain the Bakunawa's permeation. Enjou then goes into the true Mare Jivari, which cannot be entered without the Bakunawa's power, looking for a forgotten homeland.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Grey, windless, silent: a plain of ash under a stilled timeline, with the faint outlines of vanished structures at the edge of sight.

## status
### STANDARD
As of 7.1, a small area is open and the rest of the true Mare Jivari remains unexplored. Nahida calls it one of the places beyond her reach, implying Irminsul keeps no record of it.

## entry: The Bakunawa
@keys: Bakunawa, Black Tide
@keys2: Mare Jivari, Pyro Archon Temple, Tenoch, Sanhaj
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
A vast Abyssal calamity of the cataclysm era that devoured villages west of Tenochtzitoc. Its body was fractured by Tenoch, Sanhaj and the Night Kingdom's power, yet it keeps the potential to revive and to corrupt time. The Wanderer's Troupe had earlier carved a Runestone from its body, according to the account in The Festering Fang.

## entry: Enjou and the Mare Jivari
@keys: Enjou, Lavawalker
@keys2: Mare Jivari, Bakunawa, Citlali
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
Enjou researches the region's history and access routes. In Yupanqui's Turnfire he meets Kinich and the Traveler; in the Iktomi Spiritseeking Scrolls event (limited-time) he uses Leyla as bait to make Citlali work out its location. In The Last Survivor of Tenochtzitoc he pacts with Citlali, helps restrain the Bakunawa, and then goes into the true Mare Jivari, because none of the civilisations he had learned about was the forgotten homeland he sought.
