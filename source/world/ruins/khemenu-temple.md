## summary
### LITE
Khemenu Temple is a buried temple in the Hypostyle Desert of Sumeru's Great Red Sand, underground to the east of King Deshret's Mausoleum. It is not the largest of the desert's temples but it is linked to the people: the Scarlet Sand Slate says all would enter and bow their heads to praise Deshret's wisdom. Its murals depict his reign.
### STANDARD +
The temple is explored in An Introduction to Indoor Archaeology, the second part of Golden Slumber, by the scholar Tirzad's team with the Traveler. It appears on the map once the quest is done.

## geography
@version: 3.1
### STANDARD
Four map levels: the Entrance Hall, an Underground Area, the Underground Depths and a Chamber of Secrets. The slate tells a visitor to leave Abdju Road and walk toward the sunset along the axis. People painted images on the shrine walls with plant juice and ore powder, and sand does not cover them as it does footprints.
### LARGE +
Features include a Plinth of the Secret Rites, Sacred Seals, Primal Beams, Everlight and Neverlight Cells, a Primal Sandglass and a Soulferry device. A Time Trial Challenge and Seelie are also here. The doors inside have three corners, which Paimon and the Traveler notice match the shape of the strange machine Benben.

## places
@version: 3.1
### STANDARD
Inside is a mural of a sun and an eye above seven pillars and many small figures. Behind it a passage opens upward when Tirzad bumps into the wall. The temple also holds a vessel, a boat, in a dry room, and mechanisms using fans, torches and an elevator.

## history
@version: 3.1
### STANDARD
The slate calls Khemenu Temple modest in size but close to ordinary people, a place where all entered and praised Deshret. The temple belongs to the realm of King Deshret, whose civilisation is gone; generations of Vahumana researchers have studied the desert's ruins.
### LARGE +
Murals in the temple depict the king's reign. Tirzad reads them as the ordinary people kept small beneath a king over seven pillars of authority. Jeht answers that the picture feels peaceful and orderly, and Jebrael points to the hope that bound the people of the sand to Deshret and their hope of overcoming the calamity. The quarrel is never resolved.

### ULTRA +
Tirzad, who calls himself an Indoorsology researcher, hopes the temple will supply material for his paper. In Dual Evidence his finished paper is named 'The Golden Slumber: A Study on Governance in the Later Period of Al-Ahmar's Civilization and Stages of Survival', which Soheil doubted until the Traveler's report vindicated it.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Dry stone halls and painted walls under the desert, with large fans, torches and sliding doors.

## dangers
### LARGE
A Primal Construct is the temple's elite enemy, and wind from Large Fan Blades blocks passage until it dies down. The slate says the temple on Abdju Road, the approach to Deshret's territory, has collapsed pillars and has become a home for scorpions and lizards.

## quests
@version: 3.1
### STANDARD
In An Introduction to Indoor Archaeology Tirzad's team (Jeht, her father Jebrael and Benben) camps at an oasis and enters the temple. Benben opens the first door by itself. The party lights Primal Torches to open further doors, reaches a mural, and a sliding wall reveals a rising path to the next stage, The Secret of Al-Ahmar.
### LARGE +
Jebrael hands Tirzad's stone slate to the Traveler to use as a guide. Mirsad, Omid and Nachtigal remain behind at the oasis, and Jebrael buries part of the team's supplies as insurance before they go in.

## entry: Benben and the Temple Doors
@keys: Benben
@keys2: Khemenu Temple, Tirzad, Jeht
@version: 3.1
### STANDARD
Benben, a strange three-cornered machine, earlier made the stone slate light up in Golden Slumber, and in the temple opens Khemenu Temple's first door by itself, with Jeht guessing it is drawn to the doors' triangular shape. Tirzad's professional scorn for Benben is a running joke.
