## summary
### LITE
The Place of Swallowing is a small chamber branching off Duat Hall in the Mausoleum of King Deshret, in Sumeru's Hypostyle Desert. Completing its puzzles during Dual Evidence grants Clearance Level 4 of the Scarlet Sand Slate.
### STANDARD +
The slate says that those who ascend to Aaru will forget hunger and thirst, and even if they sup on pomegranate juice they do so for pleasure rather than survival. It names the abstinent as those who pass its trial and never fear hunger or thirst.

## geography
@version: 3.1
### STANDARD
Branches off the east side of Duat Hall. The Fourth Clearance found here allows the Traveler to interact with all chambers in the Great Red Sand.
### LARGE +
The chamber is part of a ladder of permissions: the Ferryman's Clearance opens Duat Hall, and this clearance extends it further.

## history
@version: 3.1
### STANDARD
One of three chambers the slate points to after the Traveler enters Duat Hall. Paimon notes it as they approach and remarks they no longer need Tirzad or Jebrael to read the slate. Clearing it lights a sigil, and the slate's final text says those who finish it will never fear hunger or thirst.
### LARGE +
The pomegranate image is only in the slate's text, and the quest does not explain it.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Close and stony beside the vast void of Duat Hall; the sense of fasting and thirst is only in the slate's words.

## quests
@version: 3.1
### STANDARD
Dual Evidence (Old Notes and New Friends, part 1).
### ULTRA +
With the Place of Rebirth and the Place of Breath it completes the three trials; once all sigils are lit the slate announces the Great Hall of Truths.
