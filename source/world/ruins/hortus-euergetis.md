## summary
### LITE
Hortus Euergetis (Latin, "Garden of Euergetis") is an underwater garden in Machimos, the outer city of Remuria in the Sea of Bygone Eras. It takes its name from Euergetia, a Harmost of Remuria who died in the kingdom's destruction. A short engraving at its waypoint, signed by Ouranides of Ouranopolis, wishes her peace and promises that flowers will always bloom for her here.
### STANDARD +
Introduced in Version 4.6 and visited in the quest The Last Day of Remuria. Hunter's Rays swim over it.

## geography
### STANDARD
A subarea of Machimos, the outer city of the capital, near the Initium Iani, Caesareum Palace and Portus Anticus. The engraving sits on the upper north-east side of the teleport waypoint, at the base of a rock arch. The tall rock formations of Machimos are thought to be remnants of the old city.
### LARGE +
Machimos was a city of temples, arenas, gilded walls and markets before it sank.

## people
### STANDARD
Euergetia, called the benefactrix, was one of the four Harmosts of Remuria and is remembered as kindhearted. Her friend Cassiodor (writing as Ouranides of Ouranopolis) treats her as a sister. Boethius, another Harmost, is said in the Faded Castle's notes to have wished to revive Remuria with her after her death but failed.
### LARGE +
Euergetia ordered an investigation into the beliefs of the northern tribes, and used her power as a Harmost to let conquered people taste their homeland dishes in their imaginations. She entrusted the Legio VI to Boethius and an engraving at Caesareum Palace links her to the three chains forged with blood and bone to bind the dragon Scylla (the text leaves unclear whose blood and bone they were).

## culture
### LARGE
The epitaph is gentle and funerary, unlike the martial engravings elsewhere in Remuria's ruins: it prays that her melody find peace, that she sleep without grieving for the past or fearing the future, and that she laugh as before.

## history
### LARGE
Canon does not describe the garden's origin; the name suggests it commemorates Euergetia. She died when Remuria was destroyed. The epitaph says her name and kindness may be forgotten, but flowers will still bloom for her here.

## quests
### STANDARD
The Last Day of Remuria lists the garden among the quest's subareas. No dialogue is tied to the garden itself.

## status
### LITE
A quiet sunken memorial as of 7.1; nothing in later content changes it.
### ULTRA +
The likely writer of the epitaph, Cassiodor, is present in the 4.6 quests as Monsieur Os (Cassiodor speaking through the cat Osse), so the friend who mourned Euergetia is still alive in the story, though no dialogue ties him to the garden.

## dangers
### LARGE
Hunter's Rays are the common enemy.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Still water over a sunken garden; a calm, mournful place among harsher ruins, with a sense of private grief.

## entry: Epitaph for Euergetia
@keys: Euergetia, benefactrix, Ouranides of Ouranopolis
@keys2: Hortus Euergetis, Remuria
@version: 4.6
### STANDARD
The engraving reads as a short elegy: it asks that the kindhearted Euergetia's melody find peace, calls her colleague, friend and sister, and says that though her beneficence and her name be forgotten, flowers will bloom for her here. It tells her to sleep and to weep no more, and to laugh as she once did.
