## summary
### LITE
The Abandoned Production Zone is a closed-off industrial level at the bottom of the Fortress of Meropide, Fontaine's underwater prison in the Liffey Region. A lift near the waypoint on the Production Zone's lower level leads down to it. Four passages (Supply Warehouse 1, Supply Warehouse 2, the Energy Storage Warehouse and the Geode Mine Shaft) branch from a central chamber and are opened or shut by a large gear-driven drive valve. Notes left lying around show the fortress's daily life: petty complaints, makeshift experiments and boredom.
### STANDARD +
It is a point of interest from Version 4.1. A fortress guard called Camden is posted in it. The zone matters in two world-quest moments: the drive valve is used to reach a locked room in Safe Operation and to open the sealed Geode Mine Shaft in An Eye for an Eye.

## geography
### STANDARD
Reached only by the lift beside the Production Zone's lower-level waypoint, which sits below the Administrative Area. The central chamber holds the Multidirectional Connection Control Drive Valve, with four paths radiating from it: Supply Warehouse 1, the Geode Mine Shaft, the Energy Storage Warehouse and Supply Warehouse 2.
### LARGE +
Leaving and re-entering the area resets the valve to its original position, which keeps the lift route open. A hallway after Supply Warehouse 2 contains a punching bag and training notes, and a room beyond Supply Warehouse 1 holds a desk with a worker's memo. Energy Transfer Terminals let a visitor look into each passage before opening it.

## places
### LARGE
Central drive-valve chamber; Supply Warehouse 1 and Supply Warehouse 2; the Energy Storage Warehouse; the Geode Mine Shaft, a mine filled with Arkhium crystal and patrolled by Flying Energy Thieves; a hallway with a punching bag.
### ULTRA +
At the end of the Geode Mine Shaft a water current (open once An Eye for an Eye is finished) leads on to Bravais' Press Works, so the sealed shaft is also a back way into a separate hidden workshop.

## people
### STANDARD
Camden, a Fortress of Meropide guard, stands watch and is chronically sleepy. Other people appear only through the notes they left: a self-taught drink maker who tests recipes on colleagues, and someone training for the Pankration Ring who keeps a gruff diary.
### LARGE +
The memo names Koblenz (made to taste an early recipe, fell ill for three days) and Delassaux (paid 280 Credit Coupons to try a later batch, and found it invigorating). The training diarist names Roussimoff, an opponent in the Pankration Ring training matches. None of them is otherwise described here.

## governance
### LARGE
The area is run by fortress rules. A posted Notice orders that work on the Geode Mine Shaft is suspended, the paths sealed, and the drive valve operated only with a technical expert present. Unauthorised access by non-essential personnel is prohibited. A Drive Valve Operations and Safety Manual explains the gear rule: when the central Core Gear Drive and a removable Gear Drivetrain point in opposite directions, the path indicated by the Core Gear Drive opens, and the valve halts if all four drivetrains are removed.

## history
### LARGE
Canon gives no history of the zone beyond its present state. The Notice records that Geode Mine Shaft work was suspended, a new locking device was added there, and the research terminal's energy storage device was removed for safety. The zone's name, the Notice and the dusty rooms mark it as a former production area that was shut down.

## quests
### STANDARD
In Scenes from Life in Meropide: Safe Operation the drive valve is used to open the room where Mitul is locked in. In An Eye for an Eye it opens the sealed Geode Mine Shaft so the Traveler can escape with Cater and Lanoire.
### LARGE +
Gameplay note: bringing an Energy Transfer Device from the Energy Storage Warehouse powers the punching bag; striking it releases a common chest holding Credit Coupons.

## status
### LITE
Sealed and quiet as of 7.1, with Camden on watch. No later content changes it.

## dangers
### LARGE
Moving machinery of the drive valve, Flying Energy Thieves in the Geode Mine Shaft, and the isolation of a dead-end level.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Dim metal corridors, hanging pipes and the grind of heavy gears, with the sense that the people who worked here left in a hurry. Their scrawled notes give it a human, grumbling tone: cafeteria complaints, repetitive meals, sore muscles and schemes to earn Credit Coupons.

## entry: Drive Valve Notice and Safety Manual
@keys: Multidirectional Connection Control Drive Valve, Gear Drivetrain, Core Gear Drive
@version: 4.1
### STANDARD
A posted notice at the central chamber. Geode Mine Shaft work is suspended and connecting paths sealed; the valve may be turned only under a technical expert's supervision. The manual explains that a non-removable Core Gear Drive controls the central room while removable Gear Drivetrains set each path's direction, and that the path pointed to by the Core Gear Drive opens when the two directions oppose each other.

## entry: Memo of the Drink Maker
@keys: Koblenz, Delassaux, Credit Coupons drink
@keys2: Abandoned Production Zone, Fortress of Meropide
@version: 4.1
### STANDARD
A desk memo tracks three recipes for a wholesome drink. Early versions of bulle fruit juice, rainbow rose and pluie lotus distillates, cinnamon, honey, tidalga and condessence crystal powder made Koblenz sick for three days. The writer swapped in Sumeru rose and nilotpala lotus, paid Delassaux 280 Credit Coupons to test it (he felt perky), and finally replaced the crystal powder with ground yellowish-green ore. He hopes the last version, which has a rich distinctive flavor, will succeed, and says he is not making Fonta.

## entry: Training Log of a Pankration Ring Hopeful
@keys: Roussimoff, Training Log, Pankration Ring training
@keys2: Fortress of Meropide, Abandoned Production Zone
@version: 4.1
### STANDARD
A diary pinned beside the punching bag. The writer records losses in training matches (once to a rival in a Blubberbeast fighting suit who slipped and flipped him), weighted runs, weightlifting sets and punches, and complains that Credit Coupon prizes barely cover food. He mocks Roussimoff for being laid out by a spicy-sauced Garlic Baguette and for supposed weaknesses to elements, then decides to stay for the title anyway.
