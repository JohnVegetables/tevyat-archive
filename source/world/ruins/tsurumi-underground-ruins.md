## summary
### LITE
The Tsurumi Underground Ruins are a network of caverns beneath Tsurumi Island in Inazuma. They link Shirikoro Peak, whose entrance is at the mountain's base under a Perch, with Chirai Shrine, whose entrance is beneath its ruins. They hold remains of the Thunderbird Civilization and of an even earlier civilization, together with murals.
### STANDARD +
The murals and torch puzzles are the main clues to a human civilization that predates the Thunderbird's worshippers. Ruin machines guard the halls.

## geography
### STANDARD
Long underground passages between the two entrances, with a mural room, a submerged room and several Ruins Torches. Mysterious Carvings can be used to reveal hidden murals.
### LARGE +
The Shirikoro Peak side also contains Seelie courts and seven Stone Slates can be used in the cavern between the peak and Chirai Shrine.

## places
### STANDARD
The Mural Room holds three mountain murals: Shirikoro Peak on the left, the mountain on the road to the Moshiri Ceremonial Site in the middle, and Mt. Kanna on the right. Other murals near the Ruins Torches indicate puzzle solutions, and carvings reveal one beside the submerged room.
### LARGE +
Each mountain mural is marked with a star-shaped slot; the Star-Shaped Gems found during the hidden objective "Opening Old Memories" can be placed in them. The murals are lettered in Ancient Teyvat Script.

## history
### STANDARD
Long ago a unified human civilization existed across Teyvat, including Tsurumi Island, and left murals through the underground ruins. Above it, over 1,500 years ago, the Thunderbird Civilization settled the island and worshipped Kanna Kapatcir; its people were later destroyed after the sacrifice of Ruu.
### LARGE +
The Unified Civilization page, citing a line in The Sun-Wheel and Mt. Kanna, says the earlier Tsurumi civilization worshipped the Three Moons and that, as elsewhere, ships carried the dead to the Golden Hall on the moon. It adds that an object fell from the heavens and landed on the island, after which fog appeared. The wiki Tsurumi Island page describes the earlier civilization as buried deep underground, before the time of the Thunderbird's reign.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Dark halls, glowing Electro ruins and faded murals. A sense of an earlier world, older than the island's ceremonies, that no one now remembers.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Elite Ruin Cruisers, Ruin Destroyers, Ruin Sentinels and Ruin Scouts. Some areas are flooded; Electro current puzzles and torch puzzles block doors.

## travel
### STANDARD
Enter at Shirikoro Peak (base of the mountain beneath the Perch) or from beneath Chirai Shrine's ruins. The A Particularly Particular Author steps show how to open the Shirikoro entrance.

## quests
### STANDARD
Gameplay note: three Ruins Torch puzzles require the Peculiar Pinion gadget from Octave of the Maushiro (2.2). Solving them is a prerequisite for the achievement "Guessing Game". The hidden objective "Tsurumi's Mountain Murals" begins here and needs the Pinion and the three Star-Shaped Gems.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the ruins are abandoned. The Thunderbird's curse was lifted in 2.2, and no later content adds to the site.

## entry: Tsurumi Mountain Murals
@keys: Tsurumi's Mountain Murals, Star-Shaped Gem, Star-Shaped Gems, Ruins Torch
@keys2: Tsurumi, Shirikoro
@version: 2.2
### STANDARD
In the hidden exploration objective, the Traveler places the three Star-Shaped Gems in the mountain murals of the underground ruins. Electro Seelie fly out and mark the locations, at Mt. Kanna, Shirikoro Peak and the mountain on the way to the Moshiri Ceremonial Site, each guiding the player to a spot. Completing it gives an Exquisite Chest and then a Luxurious Chest, and is a prerequisite for the achievement "Guessing Game".

## entry: Pre-Thunderbird Civilization
@keys: Pre-Thunderbird Civilization, Tsurumi civilization
@keys2: Tsurumi, murals, Thunderbird
@spoiler: minor
### ULTRA
The "Pre-Thunderbird Civilization" is the wiki's name for the older people whose ruins lie beneath the island. No canon text names them. They are connected only by inference to the unified human civilization that existed throughout Teyvat, through the murals and the Three Moons reference. Keep claims about their end modest.
