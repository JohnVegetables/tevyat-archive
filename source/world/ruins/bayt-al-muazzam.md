## summary
### LITE
Bayt Al-Muazzam is a cavern temple inside Mt. Damavand in the Desert of Hadramaveth of Sumeru. It lies beneath Qusayr Al-Inkhida' and leads into Deshret's Glass Goblet. The Traveler unlocks it in Dune-Entombed Fecundity: Part I.
### STANDARD +
The name is Arabic for House of the Glorified. The map viewpoint calls it the Temple of Buried Dominion. It has an upper and a lower level and is the first of three similar caverns found in the Dirge of Bilqis quests, the others being Bayt Al-Sukr and Bayt Al-Hayah.

## geography
@version: 3.4
### STANDARD
In the middle of Mt. Damavand's maze of tunnels, under the ruins of Qusayr Al-Inkhida'. The Mt. Damavand map has two levels for this temple, upper and lower. Paths lead onward to the Goblet.
### LARGE +
In Part I, the party enters the mountain through the entrance with the Tanit's help after Azariq relays Matriarch Babel's plan to use dried canals in the dunes to reach the underground foundations of the Eternal Oasis.

## history
@version: 3.4
### STANDARD
Liloupar, a Jinni who is the Traveler's companion in the quest, explains that the mortal kingdoms of the oasis drew their water from within this mountain, hollowing it and weaving a network of tubes through it. The first water-pumping mechanism the party finds now runs on sand instead of water.
### LARGE +
Liloupar finds a fragment of herself here that the party collects, and she says green should flourish near a Jinni's fragments. Further on she names the mechanism built to sustain a dream of eternity for the oasis.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Dry canyon-cavern air, shifting sand where water used to run, doors that open only when sand is drained. The sense is of enormous machinery asleep.

## dangers
### LARGE
Geo fungi wait outside the entrance. Further in, travellers meet Eremites' abandoned camp and various ancient mechanisms.

## quests
@version: 3.4
### STANDARD
Dune-Entombed Fecundity: Part I, third part of The Dirge of Bilqis, with Jeht, Azariq, Babel and Liloupar.
### ULTRA +
The end of Part I shows the Goblet and the Eternal Oasis within sight. Jeht notes her father always hoped to enter the Eternal Oasis and find her mother again, and she never thought she would help make it come true.

## status
### LITE
Open to explore as of Version 7.1.
### STANDARD +
The Eternal Oasis is reached later in the Dirge of Bilqis. No source up to Version 7.1 that I checked changes this temple.
