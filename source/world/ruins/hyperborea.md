## summary
### LITE
Hyperborea, called the Golden City, was a kingdom of Teyvat's ancient unified civilization, located where Snezhnaya lies today. Under the angel Koitar and the voyager Anacharsis it grew into a place of astonishing technology and then rose against the Heavenly Principles of Celestia. A Celestial Nail destroyed it, and its ruins sank into the Shadow Realm. Its survivors became the ancestors of the Frostmoon Scions of Nod-Krai.
### STANDARD +
Hyperborea is not a place the Traveler can simply walk to. Only fragments appear in the game: a ruined sacred structure with murals in a quest domain (Phantom of the Sanctuary, 6.2) and the Shadow Realm ruins that the Belyi Tsar and the Hvergelmir institute later explored.

## geography
### STANDARD
Hyperborea stood in the far north, in the region that is now Snezhnaya. In its prime it was a prosperous city with a holy citadel and a golden tower; accounts hint it may have been a main centre with satellite cities around it. At the end of its history the whole region and its ley lines were frozen by Prime Ice, which is credited as the origin of Snezhnaya's icy landscape. Its ruins now lie in the Shadow Realm as a place of white dust and storms with no true substance in it.
### LARGE +
The Belyi Tsar's notes describe walking through a storm of fine white dust to reach a strange castle among the ruins, and say that the Shadow Realm's interior is the reverse of what happens outside, with none of the Abyss's foul blood found there. Part of Hyperborea can be visited as the quest domain Phantom of the Sanctuary on Hiisi Island, whose ruined sacred place and murals show Nibelung with the weaponised Three Moons and the moons shattered after the War of Funerary Flame, with only the Frost Moon intact.

## places
### STANDARD
Known features are the holy citadel, the golden tower that the Elysium Project tried to extend up to Celestia, and the sanctuary of the moons. The high priest held the sacred weapon Dawnstar of Shahar, given by Koitar. The Hyperborean facility later called the Phantom of the Sanctuary is a surviving structure.
### LARGE +
Hyperborean devices recovered by later ages include the SAMPO orb, which rearranges matter at the subatomic scale to produce limitless food; the Arrow of Terpikeraunas, a weapon that solidifies Light Realm energy and fires it; flying vehicles; and time capsules powered by the primordial forces of Teyvat. One capsule held an infant, Aksinya, who survived the city's fall in suspended animation.

## people
### STANDARD
Koitar, an Archangel of Celestia, and Anacharsis, an interstellar voyager who took possession of a young man there, led the city in its last decades. Other angels such as Nicole lived among the Hyperboreans. The people were human, and they created the Snowland Fae as attendants.
### LARGE +
After the fall, survivors who had been far from the cities fled into a cold wasteland, and a few angels like Nicole guided them to safety. The goddess Canon led them to Nod-Krai, where they became the Frostmoon Scions. Aksinya, the Hyperborean infant later raised by the Belyi Tsar, is the other known survivor of the city itself. Hyperborean names are said to derive from Old Iranian, Avestan or Ancient Greek roots.

## governance
### STANDARD
Originally the city followed the Heavenly Principles and took guidance from their divine envoys, and Koitar was its link to Celestia. Once Anacharsis and Koitar chose defiance, they ruled as its leaders and directed the city's plans together.
### LARGE +
The chief priest was the one who sent Anacharsis, then a young debater who had refuted the city's arguments, to Koitar to plead the case. Koitar's crown, a shackle placed by the Heavenly Principles, shattered when Anacharsis told her the secrets of the world's origins; the two pledged themselves to each other and swore an oath of defiance witnessed by the Three Moons.

## culture
### STANDARD
Hyperboreans prized knowledge and made unprecedented advances after receiving secret knowledge from the angels and from Anacharsis. They built their own scientific vocabulary, such as Tekhneikos processes and Mekhaneikos apparatuses, to record their work. They honoured the moons, and their religion and name later echoed through the Frostmoon Scions.
### LARGE +
Their knowledge covered bioengineering, including the forbidden creation of new life, and genetics (the Sanctum of the Oathbearer notes mention phenotypic stability and inbreeding depression). They also dreamed of breaching the False Sky to explore the sea of stars. A Tower of Ipsissimus description attributed to the Narzissenkreuz Ordo names Hyperborea as the first stage of human spiritual evolution, symbolising the loss of paradise.

## history
### STANDARD
Roughly 6,000 years ago Anacharsis arrived and, with Koitar, started projects meant to save the city from a fate the Heavenly Principles had already decreed: the Elysium Project (a paradise for humanity without divine blessing or judgement), the Shadow Realm, the creation of the Snowland Fae and the Primordial Human project. The last, using the remains of the Cryo Dragon, failed to contain its power; thirty years after Anacharsis' arrival Prime Ice covered the region and the Heavenly Principles launched a Celestial Nail. The Hyperboreans faced it with their arrows aimed at Celestia, joined, according to item texts, by dragons. The city was destroyed and sank into the Shadow Realm.
### LARGE +
Hyperborea's fall led directly into the War of Funerary Flame, when the dragons carried on fighting Celestia and were joined by their returned king Nibelung. Afterwards the Heavenly Principles expelled and cursed the angels for Koitar's rebellion: she lost her memories and the others had to give up wisdom or power. About a thousand years later Monomakh stepped out of the Cryo Dragon's remains and became the Belyi Tsar of the later Pale Star Court. His expeditions into the Shadow Realm found the Hyperborean ruins, which showed him what he was and set his fate toward becoming the Cryo Archon. The Frostmoon Scions built a tower in Nod-Krai to preserve Mekhaneikos devices and attempted the generations-long Sacred Heir Project; five hundred years ago Aila reformed the faith, erasing mentions of Hyperborea in favour of Kuutar and kindness.
### ULTRA +
Spirits of Hyperborean warriors, awakened by Abyssal taint, became Wilderness Hunters. Koitar later returned to Hyperborea, according to Ancient Handwriting texts in the Sanctum of the Oathbearer, intending to wait there for Anacharsis. The Belyi Tsar and Aksinya's colleagues later modified Hyperborean energy-storing devices into firearms.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
A scene in the ruins is white, hushed and strange: fine white dust in motion, great storms, a silence in which every machine seems to be listening. Sight of the golden tower or the moons would feel more sorrowful than grand, because every visible trace belongs to a civilisation that chose to meet its end on its feet.

## dangers
### LARGE
The Shadow Realm, where the ruins now lie, is not threatened by Abyss contamination, but it is a place of storms, trapped memories and isolation, and travellers can be pulled out of time into past scenes. Wilderness Hunters, the warriors' spirits corrupted by Abyssal taint, remain a hazard.

## travel
### STANDARD
Hyperborea is not accessible in the open world. A fragment appears in the Phantom of the Sanctuary domain during the Nod-Krai Archon Quest (6.2), and the Hyperborean ruins in the Shadow Realm are discussed in Pale Crown Palace texts (7.0).

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 Hyperborea remains destroyed. No Hyperboreans live in the ruins and its history is held mainly by the Frostmoon Scions, the Belyi Tsar's notes and fragmentary item text.
### STANDARD +
The Belyi Tsar's Hvergelmir institute, led by his ward Aksinya, once mined the ruins for knowledge. Aksinya remains trapped in the Shadow Realm after the Traveler defeated her in the Snezhnaya world quest series that ends at the Pale Crown Palace (7.0).

## entry: Anacharsis and Koitar's Rebellion
@keys: Anacharsis, Koitar, Elysium Project, Golden Hyperborea
@keys2: Hyperborea, Heavenly Principles, rebellion
@version: 6.2
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
Anacharsis, an interstellar voyager, cast his consciousness into Teyvat about 6,000 years ago and possessed a young Hyperborean man, then won debates against the city's thinkers and was sent to the Archangel Koitar. Learning from him the secrets of the world, Koitar doubted Celestia; her crown of the Heavenly Principles shattered and the two pledged themselves to each other. They launched the Elysium Project, which aimed to turn Teyvat into a paradise without relying on the Heavenly Principles, and prepared for war. When the Celestial Nail fell, Koitar urged Anacharsis to return to his own world, and she declared war on the Heavenly Principles from the steps of the golden tower.

## entry: Fall of Hyperborea
@keys: Celestial Nail, fall of Hyperborea, destruction of Hyperborea
@keys2: Hyperborea, Shadow Realm
@version: 6.2
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
The Primordial Human project, a bid to create a being rivalling the Heavenly Principles from the remains of the Cryo Dragon, failed to contain the dragon's innate power; Prime Ice froze the region and its ley lines. The Heavenly Principles answered with a Celestial Nail. Hyperboreans spent their last hours arming themselves, even the newborn fae, and the dragons joined their rebellion. The Nail killed the vast majority of the population and vaporised most of the fae; the city fell into the Shadow Realm.

## entry: Hyperborean Technology
@keys: Hyperborean technology, Mekhaneikos, Tekhneikos, Arrow of Terpikeraunas, SAMPO
@keys2: Hyperborea
### LARGE
Hyperborean engineers wrote about Tekhneikos processes and Mekhaneikos apparatuses. They created the Snowland Fae by engraving the Frost Moon's light on the bones of giant beasts, tried to engineer elemental augmented humans, built the SAMPO to make food, the Arrow of Terpikeraunas to turn Light Realm energy into projectiles, and sophisticated time capsules. Later explorers from Hvergelmir recovered such devices and adapted them into firearms; the Leshy, a late creation, is noted as a more stable form of life than the fae made with Embryonic-Ichoric-Telluric-Rudiment.

## entry: Survivors and the Frostmoon Scions
@keys: Frostmoon Scions, Frostmoon Scion, antlered crown, Sacred Heir Project
@keys2: Hyperborea, Nod-Krai
@version: 6.2
### LARGE
Those who survived were away from the cities. Canon pitied them and led them to Nod-Krai, according to legend giving them antlered crowns. They named themselves the Frostmoon Scions in her honour and kept their beliefs after her death. They built a tower in Nod-Krai holding Mekhaneikos devices and pursued the Sacred Heir Project, an effort to breed a prophesied heir to Elysium. Its disastrous results five hundred years ago led Aila to reform the faith, replacing Hyperborea with dedications to nature, kindness and the newborn goddess Kuutar.
