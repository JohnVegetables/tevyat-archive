## summary
### LITE
The Inverted City is a ruin deep beneath the Chasm in Liyue: an ancient city whose buildings and a water pool hang upside down from the cavern ceiling. It is what remains of Lang-Gan, a city of Teyvat's unified civilisation governed by Zibai, after the Abyss tainted it and a Celestial Nail fell. The pool seems to soften the curse of immortality laid on the Khaenri'ahns.
### STANDARD +
It was sealed behind a purple barrier until the Traveler and Dainsleif reached it in Archon Quest Chapter II, where hilichurls came to live out their last days, the Black Serpent Knights stood guard, and the Abyss Order tried to turn the pool to its own purpose.

## geography
### STANDARD
The city lies in the Nameless Ruins of the Chasm's Underground Mines, hanging above the cave floor with its terraces facing downward. Its crown is the upside-down water pool, with an altar-like structure and statues around it. Climbing the structure leads to the summit chamber.
### LARGE +
Strange devices stand around the pool; the Abyss Order's amplifier was built into them. Statues near the pool look like those in front of the Dainichi Mikoshi in Inazuma (the wiki notes this without saying whether it is meaningful).

## history
### STANDARD
Before Khaenri'ah, this was a city governed by Zibai under Celestia's gaze. After the war of funerary flame the city was corrupted by the Abyss and a Celestial Nail sent to purify it landed on it, burying Lang-Gan and creating the Chasm. The ruin stayed buried and dark for thousands of years.
### LARGE +
In "Requiem of the Echoing Depths" (2.6) Dainsleif noticed that hilichurls in the Chasm seemed to be gathering to die, because the environment eased the curse; he judged the city to belong to a civilisation older than Khaenri'ah. His former subordinates, now cursed Black Serpent Knights, guarded the dying hilichurls. In a vision at a hilichurl camp the Traveler saw their Sibling and an Abyss Herald discussing a plan to amplify the pool's properties and lift the curse from the gathered hilichurls. The amplifier's activation caused agony before the Traveler broke it, and the Black Serpent Knight Halfdan gave his life to hold it back.
### ULTRA +
The 2.6 quests never name the city. The identification with Lang-Gan comes from the 6.3 Lantern Rite story (see the Lang-Gan entry), and Zibai's role as the city's guide was only revealed then.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Cold stone and inverted architecture, with the unsettling feel of a city seen in a mirror. Light drips down from the pool above; the air is hushed, with the heavy calm that makes tormented people want to lie down and rest.

## dangers
### LARGE
Ruin Guards, Abyss mages, Black Serpent Knights and Shadowy Husks in the surrounding ruins; the area was long sealed by a barrier.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the amplifier is destroyed, Halfdan is dead, and the pool remains a quiet relic. The Chasm itself is being reopened by the Qixing.

## entry: The inverted pool and Dainsleif's curse
@keys: inverted pool, upside-down pool, Inverted Fountain
@keys2: Dainsleif, curse, Khaenri'ah
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
Dainsleif says the pool's cleansing quality eases the curse on Khaenri'ahns, on which he has been suffering for five centuries, but the effect could at most suppress it for a time. A true cleansing would cost the sufferer their life, since a god's curse acts on a deeper level of reality than the person.
