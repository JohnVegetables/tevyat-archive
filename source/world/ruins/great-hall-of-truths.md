## summary
### LITE
The Great Hall of Truths is a chamber inside the Mausoleum of King Deshret in Sumeru's Hypostyle Desert, joined to the bottomless Duat Hall on its south side. In the quest Dual Evidence the Traveler and Paimon reach it by following the Scarlet Sand Slate, and its giant central device carries them up to Aaru's Shut at the summit.
### STANDARD +
The slate describes it as the place where those who bring praises before King Deshret, show wisdom and do not fear the darkness may obtain unique gifts. In practice it is the last stage of a trial laid out in imagery of birds, rivers and sacred flame.

## geography
@version: 3.1
### STANDARD
Part of the Mausoleum complex under the Hypostyle Desert. It is connected to Duat Hall at its south side; Duat Hall is entered through an underground waypoint directly south of the Mausoleum and needs the Ferryman's Clearance from the Scarlet Sand Slate. The central section opens onto the way up to Aaru's Shut once Dual Evidence has been completed.
### LARGE +
Inside is one great device in the middle of the hall, surrounded by four plinths of the secret rites. Beyond a portal that opens when the plinths are operated lies a second chamber with another huge device and three places for sacred torches.

## history
@version: 3.1
### STANDARD
In the quest the Scarlet Sand Slate tells the party to prove itself in three ways: pass the river as a falcon, peck three organs, and burn the embers of the sun at three places with feathers. The trial halls Place of Rebirth, Place of Breath and Place of Swallowing each light a sigil on the slate; once all are lit the Great Hall of Truths appears. The Vahumana researcher Soheil had mocked Tirzad's paper on the Mausoleum, and the party's aim is to prove the paper true.
### LARGE +
Operating the four plinths opens a portal, and the slate declares that the king's consent has been won and Aaru reached. Paimon is unimpressed, since the place holds only a huge device, and a new line appears on the slate: burn the embers of the sun at three places. Lighting three primal torches wakes the device. It lifts the party on an elevator to the top of the Mausoleum, where a guardian waits.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
A cold, vast chamber of sand-worn stone and monumental machinery. The hall feels both ceremonial and mechanical, like a throne room built to weigh visitors rather than welcome them. Paimon compares it to Stormterror's Lair in the desert.

## dangers
### LARGE
At the top of the elevator the party must defeat a Primal Construct, a guardian construct billed as Field Annihilation Array: The Evincing Shadow. A fall into the nearby Duat Hall's void only sends a traveller back to the entrance.

## quests
@version: 3.1
### STANDARD
The hall is the centre of Dual Evidence, the first quest in the world quest series Old Notes and New Friends, begun at Aaru Village after Golden Slumber is done. The quest grants the achievements Walk Like King Deshret's People, The Straight Path and The Path To Enlightenment for the successive stages.
### LARGE +
Afterwards the party returns to Aaru Village with a stone slate recovered from the summit. Soheil, once dismissive, apologises on behalf of his family and wants to study it, noting that records of the later period of Al-Ahmar's civilisation are mostly fragments. Hulwani refuses to escort researchers into the desert because the sage has forbidden entry without his permission.

### ULTRA +
Clearance on the slate is graded: the Ferryman's Clearance opens Duat Hall, and a fourth clearance found in the Place of Swallowing within Duat Hall opens all chambers in the Great Red Sand, so the hall sits at the end of a ladder of permissions. The slate's wording about the river between the mortal plane and Aaru, vulture and falcon forms and the consent of Al-Ahmar is the key to reading the whole sequence. Tirzad, whose paper Soheil had doubted, is not with the party on the climb; the Traveler and Paimon bring back proof for Soheil afterwards.

## status
### LITE
Open to exploration. The cyclone that long crowned the Mausoleum is off for good after Dual Evidence, and Aaru's Shut can be visited through the hall's centre.
### STANDARD +
Nothing found in the sources up to Version 7.1 changes the hall or reveals more of its original purpose.

## entry: Dual Evidence and the Ascent
@keys: Dual Evidence, Aaru's Shut, Scarlet Sand Slate, Ferryman's Clearance
@keys2: Mausoleum, Great Hall of Truths
@version: 3.1
### STANDARD
Dual Evidence has the Traveler follow the Scarlet Sand Slate through Duat Hall and the three trial halls, then use the Great Hall of Truths to climb to Aaru's Shut. The Mausoleum's long-running cyclone stops for good, the summit is changed, and the party brings back a slate as proof for Soheil.
