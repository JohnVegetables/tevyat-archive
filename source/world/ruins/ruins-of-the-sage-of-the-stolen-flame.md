## summary
### LITE
The Ruins of the Sage of the Stolen Flame are an instance in Tequemecan Valley, Natlan, unlocked during the quest Revelations from the Past. After the quest, one reaches them from the Hall of Parting Vows by descending glowing stairs to Catastrophe's Edge and touching an inscription.
### STANDARD +
The Sage of the Stolen Flame is the human-world legend of the dragon prince Kukulkan, who stole phlogiston from the dragons and taught it to humans. The wiki page for these ruins is otherwise very short, so this site is best treated as part of the Hall of Parting Vows trial.

## geography
### STANDARD
A separate instance below the Hall of Parting Vows, entered by an inscription beyond Catastrophe's Edge.

## history
### STANDARD
The Sage, called Waxaklahun Ubah Kan in tribal stories, is said to have taught Natlan's first human tribes to use phlogiston and to have left a token of oath for a worthy human and Saurian pair. He appears in tales from the tribal era, Ochkanatlan and even the cataclysm.
### LARGE +
Later events show the Sage to be Kukulkan, a younger brother of the Pyro Dragon Sovereign Xiuhcoatl and Regent of Natlantea, who in time grew disillusioned and manoeuvred a human and a Saurian into unsealing the Great Volcano of Tollan. Whether these ruins played any part in that scheme is not stated on their wiki page.

## quests
### STANDARD
Revelations from the Past is the only quest tied to the site.
### ULTRA +
Access after the quest: go back to the Hall of Parting Vows, take the glowing stairs down to Catastrophe's Edge, then touch the inscription. The wiki records no enemies, chests or interactables for this instance itself.

## status
### STANDARD
Accessible as of 7.1 once the quest has been completed.
