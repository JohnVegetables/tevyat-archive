## summary
### LITE
The Royal Court of the Seelie is a pale temple hidden above Lake Amsvartnir in Nod-Krai, invisible to ordinary eyes. Legend and ancient notes place the first Seelie, Koitar, wife of Anacharsis, here after the Heavenly Principles wiped her memory. The Traveler and the Seelie of the lake break its barrier and restore it in the world quest Echoes of a Forsaken Song.
### STANDARD +
Reaching it means melting a Lunar Conflux Barrier at the lake, playing a lyre that only the player seems to hear, and riding Moonlanes up through floating islands while reconnecting Lakeside Prisms. Each prism brings back a verse of an old lament, and three Ancient Handwriting notes in the court hold Koitar's own words.

## geography
### STANDARD
The court sits above the lake of Amsvartnir, north of Piramida in Wavechaser Plain, beyond the ruins of an ancient palace on the edge of the wasteland. Its approach is a sacred forest by the water. The old description says it cannot be seen by mortals in daylight, and that whoever breaks the golden branch in the forest will find it.
### LARGE +
Once restored, the court is a group of floating islands joined by Moonlanes and linked to the island in the middle of the lake by aqua-blue beams that form geometric patterns. Aqua-green crystals stand at the base and on the surrounding islands. A gigantic lyre or harp stands within, and a matching instrument by the lakeshore starts the quest. The Traveler can teleport to a waypoint unlocked partway up, and a viewpoint called Palaces of Times Bygone looks over the scene.

## places
### LARGE
- The lakeside lyre: strumming it seems silent to Paimon, but the Traveler hears a mournful tune and a song in an unknown language that is understood perfectly.
- The floating islands: four sets of seals must be broken, each opened by reconnecting Lakeside Prisms along a Moonlane.
- The ruin and its mural: the last prism restores a wall mural that the Seelie fly into, which ends the quest and opens treasure chests.
- The Ancient Handwriting notes: three readable pieces of Koitar's lament, found after the quest.

## history
### STANDARD
Canon casts the court as a place of confinement. After the failed Hyperborean rebellion against the Heavenly Principles, Koitar lost her memories and was held in a temple that, in her own account, she could not leave and no passer-by could see. Her tiny kin, the Seelie, mourned beside her though she no longer knew them. Thousands of years later, the Traveler follows the Seelie to Amsvartnir and uncovers it.
### LARGE +
Koitar's notes describe the building as made of great stones, with pillars sheathed in gold and golden beams and rafters, and with no sun or moon in the sky, only a pale bright light like the morning star. She says a voice told her she must wait inside and not cross the threshold, and she did not know whose command it was. She played a lyre and sang because she could neither remember nor stop mourning.

Her second note records a wolf from the north that burst into her dwelling during a time of thorns and cold. It spoke no human words at first, but listened to her song, took her mockery without resentment and obeyed her. She named it Boreas, meaning North Wind, and let it and its kin stay. Later it gained speech and intelligence, called her Mother, and asked to go south to help a poor but walled people prosper. She let it go. In her third note, she says only that wolf kept her husband's name from fading from her mind, curses the 'false master', and vows to return to the great city of Hyperborea and wait for the star beyond the stars.
### ULTRA +
The Etherlight Spindlelute, a 4-star catalyst whose text was a limited-event reward, places the court in a forgotten kingdom hidden where no heavenly light reaches, with the line that its owner has 'long departed' and that a young woman's silent lament still echoes in the hall. It also describes a dream of a tower raised to the heavens, an apostle warring on the sky, beasts as large as mountains, three moons over the sea and an ancient dragon rising like a tide, and ends with the image of a last singer. The chirping-insects verse that the quest reveals line by line appears in both texts.

## people
### STANDARD
No one lives here now. Koitar, the first Seelie, is the court's only named occupant, and the Seelie of the lake are its guides; both belong to the past of the place. See the character entry for Koitar for her wider story.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Nothing hostile is tied to the court itself. The lake area has its own wildlife and enemies, such as pale-furred wolves and a radiant glacial wolf, and the court is reached by puzzle rather than by combat.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Pale light without sun or moon, mist and mourning music, golden detail on old stone, small glowing Seelie gathering in numbers. It should feel like a place of grief and waiting rather than menace: a song sung to an empty hall.

## quests
### STANDARD
The only quest is the world quest Echoes of a Forsaken Song (6.3, Wavechaser Plain). Its rewards include chests and the Ancient Handwriting notes, and it grants the achievement Farewell Hope, With Hope Farewell Fear.

## status
### LITE
Revealed and restored, and open to explore as of 7.1. No later story has revisited it.

## entry: Koitar's Lament (Ancient Handwriting)
@keys: Ancient Handwriting, Koitar's lament, Koitar's notes
@keys2: Royal Court of the Seelie, Amsvartnir
@version: 6.3
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Three readable notes in the court preserve Koitar's thoughts after her memory was cut away. The first describes the temple, her emptiness, the voice that bids her wait, and her decision to sing. The second tells of the wolf she named Boreas. The third is addressed to Anacharsis and, bitterly, to the Heavenly Principles; she resolves to go back to Hyperborea and wait for him.
### LARGE +
The notes show her as grieving but not broken: she keeps her songs, her dry humour toward the wolf and her faith that her beloved will come. The third note includes the line that she would rather sing in utter ruin than endure servitude under a false heavenly light.

## entry: Echoes of a Forsaken Song
@keys: Echoes of a Forsaken Song, Palaces of Times Bygone
@keys2: Seelie, Amsvartnir
@version: 6.3
### STANDARD
This world quest begins by playing the lyre at Amsvartnir after melting a Lunar Conflux Barrier. The Traveler follows a Seelie up Moonlanes, reconnects Lakeside Prisms to release verses, breaks seals on four floating islands, and restores the court and its mural, ending with chests and the Ancient Handwriting notes.
