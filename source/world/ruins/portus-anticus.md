## summary
### LITE
Portus Anticus was the royal port of Remuria. It has since sunk into an underwater cavern, part of the Sea of Bygone Eras in Fontaine.
### STANDARD
In its prime the port had a lofty bell tower whose bells were said to carry through the ocean's barrier and guide sailors home; the viewpoint text imagines lost souls still hearing them. It once held the emperor Remus' prized imperial ship, the Fortuna. It was added in Version 4.6.
### LARGE +
Its map has two levels, an upper and a lower one, and ancient engravings in the cavern record military orders from the last days of Remuria.

## geography
### STANDARD
A great cavern beneath the waves, the remains of a harbour that once received ships of the Remurian court. The map is divided into an upper and a lower level.

## history
### STANDARD
Remuria's royal port, with a belltower and the Fortuna, Remus' imperial ship, as described in the Remuria history book that the location page cites.
### LARGE +
Engravings in the ruins preserve several documents and one lament. One orders the defence line at the northern harbour withdrawn on the authority of a special envoy named Faunus of Legio V, redirecting troops toward Caesareum Palace, and sets Legio IX to hold the flank against the Dragonborn. A later order, in the name of the Harmost Boethius and in accord with Sebastos Remus' melody, tells Legio V to stop hostilities against the Dragonborn and return to the palace, while Legios VII and IX seal Portus Anticus against northern barbarian attacks so talks are not disturbed. A third engraving is a bitter poem addressed to Faunus about the razing of the city of Lugdunensis and the spoils of war.
### ULTRA +
Taken together, the engravings show the port as a military position in Remuria's war with the Dragonborn and then as a guarded site during peace talks. The speakers (Remurian officers) refer to their enemies in contemptuous terms such as "savages"; treat those as in-world Remurian views, not narrator fact.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Vast, flooded and echoing; a drowned harbour whose only remembered sound is its bell, with the cavern's dark water holding the sense of ships that never came home.

## travel
### STANDARD
Found in the Sea of Bygone Eras of Fontaine, reached from the Remuria ruins. The viewpoint "Where Only the Bell Tolls" is located here.
