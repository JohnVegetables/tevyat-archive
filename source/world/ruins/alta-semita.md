## summary
### LITE
Alta Semita (Latin, roughly "High Pathway") is the golden aqueduct system of Capitolium, the inner city of Remuria, sunk in the Sea of Bygone Eras off Fontaine. It once carried Ichor out from the Domus Aurea toward "the great lake in the far north", and from above it looks like a giant harp. After millennia it had broken apart. In the quest Gradus ad Capitolium the Traveler and the dragon Scylla rebuild it so that the gates of the Domus Aurea will open.
### STANDARD +
It was added in Version 4.6 together with the rest of the Sea of Bygone Eras. Scylla says that in Remuria's heyday the will of Phobos flowed through golden aqueducts to every corner of the kingdom, which is why restoring this section can unlock the palace.

## geography
### STANDARD
A subarea of Capitolium, the inner half of the Remurian capital, lying beside the palace of the Domus Aurea. The aqueduct's pipes and channels run outward from the palace, a design the Domus Aurea's own plan echoes: Monsieur Os says the whole palace was built from a giant ship as an instrument, with pipes extending outward to broadcast the symphony.
### LARGE +
A mural north of the aqueduct was painted with the power of the "symphony", as in the Faded Castle. Collegium Phonascorum lies close by, and the quest reconstruction runs through both places. Reaching Capitolium at all requires passing the Initium Iani and the Passage of Ianus.

## places
### LARGE
The harp-shaped aqueduct and its golden channels; the symphony murals and light-guiding devices that Scylla and the Traveler activate; scattered "scores" and a horn that must be lit; the approach to the Domus Aurea's gate.
### ULTRA +
Ancient Engravings here honour Aurelius, a Harmost, whose tomb the text calls the ocean itself.

## people
### STANDARD
No living inhabitants. Scylla, a Dragon Prince of the vishaps, guides the work; Monsieur Os (the Harmost Cassiodor speaking through the cat Osse) comments on the murals; Boethius, who Os says has lain low in the palace for millennia, waits in the Domus Aurea.
### LARGE +
After the aqueduct is restored the Traveler has a vision in which Scylla addresses Remus, the Sebastos of Remuria, and learns his last plan.

## governance
### LARGE
Remuria's order ran on Phobos, the symphony, and the Harmosts under the Sebastos. The aqueduct was infrastructure of that order: the golden waterways carried the symphony's will, so repairing them lets Phobos's own defences open the palace.

## culture
### LARGE
Remurians recorded music as paintings on stone. Scylla says Remus's musicians once covered every rock and stone wall with their melodies, and that now only golden bees obey the call of the symphony. The Alta Semita's engraving shows a martial, triumphal side of that culture: it praises Aurelius as one who promoted Boethius, pacified tribes "uncivilized for the empire", and led the melodies of ten legions.

## history
### LARGE
The aqueduct fell apart in the centuries after Remuria sank. In Gradus ad Capitolium Scylla, who has been freed from his seal at the Initium Iani and has little strength left, proposes to use the Domus Aurea's own power against it: reconnecting the ruined section will open the great gates "just as they once did". The reconstruction quests (Part I and Part II) have the Traveler follow golden bees to scattered scores, light a horn and return bird phantasms to their places.
### ULTRA +
The engraving beside the aqueduct gives one Remurian view of the war: Aurelius is called a Harmost who promoted Boethius and passed order to his successors, and Phobos is invoked to curse the traitors who colluded with "cruel, reprobate barbarians" and scuttled the flagship that bore him north; the text promises barbarism will be repaid twelve times over. It is a one-sided Remurian account.

## quests
### STANDARD
Gradus ad Capitolium (the first quest of Pharsalia's Rhapsody, part of Canticles of Harmony) leads here. Its child quests, Golden Aqueduct Reconstruction: Part I and Part II, are set on the Alta Semita; Part II also uses Collegium Phonascorum.
### LARGE +
After the aqueduct is restored, Scylla agrees to help against Boethius.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the aqueduct stands rebuilt after the 4.6 quests. No later content revisits it.

## dangers
### LARGE
Praetorian Golems around the Domus Aurea, drowned ruins and strong currents, and Boethius's mastery of Phobos's power.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Gold light along a harp of pipes in the dark water, painted music fading on stone, golden bees drifting past, and a silence broken only by the humming of the symphony.

## entry: Aurelius's Ancient Engraving

@version: 4.6
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
The engraving says nobody knows where Aurelius, a noble Harmost, lies buried; his tomb is the sea, for he sank with his flagship of forty rows of oars. It credits him with promoting Boethius, pacifying tribes for the empire and leading the melodies of ten legions, and curses the traitors who joined the barbarians and scuttled the ship. It ends with a promise that when civilisation crushes savagery, brutality will be repaid twelve times over.
