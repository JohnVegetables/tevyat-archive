## summary
### LITE
Safhe Shatranj is a square of ancient ruins in a canyon in the Desert of Hadramaveth, Sumeru, built around an outlying temple of the cursed city of Gurabad. Before the quest The Temple Where Sand Flows Like Tears it was called The Temples Forsaken and was choked with sand. The Tanit tribe calls the canyon the Great Rift and treats it as bad luck. Its name is Persian for chessboard, and it holds Liloupar's Cell, where the Jinni Liloupar was found.
### STANDARD
The Archive describes the plaza as an abandoned, ruined square that researchers believe was once a training field for ancient war machines. Rune mechanisms, sand piles and a huge central device seal the main temple; Primal Constructs still guard it and Eremites roam the canyon. After the Traveler wakes the central device the sand retreats and a plaza with sides of seemingly equal length appears, which is what the desert tribes call Safhe Shatranj.
### LARGE +
It matters to the wider story because it is where Liloupar, the Mother of the Jinn, is found and bound to the Traveler, and later where chess pieces on the plaza let the Traveler travel to Gurabad itself and to other ruins.

## geography
@version: 3.4
### STANDARD
Safhe Shatranj sits in the Desert of Hadramaveth, part of the Great Red Sand, in a canyon the Tanit call the Great Rift. Gurabad proper is said to lie hidden in the canyon cliffs, so Jeht and the wiki treat these ruins as its outlying temples and fortresses. The temple on the outskirts is the one the Traveler enters first; its halls loop back to one central chamber with a large device. Beyond it lie a river valley and the Pairidaeza Canyon. Azariq guesses that a spring deep in the canyon is all that remains of an old river valley.
### ULTRA +
Thick roots break through the stonework in several rooms; Azariq reads this as a sign that the place was far more humid and fertile when it was abandoned. Sealed doors send visitors round to the main hall by alternative routes. The wiki lists a Cascade Pool, Four-Leaf Sigil, Loose Sand Piles, Primal Obelisk and Weathered Obelisk among its exploration features. One of the Towers of Betrayal stands northwest of it.

## places
### STANDARD
- The outlying temple of Gurabad: the main ruin, with a Teleport Waypoint, sealed gates and a great central device.
- The central plaza: revealed when the sand retreats, square with seemingly equal sides and a new door in the opposite cliff. This is Safhe Shatranj in the narrower sense.
- Liloupar's Cell: a prison turned oasis with a large tree and a bottle; its appearance changes once it is discovered.
- The chess pieces on the plaza and their activation devices in other desert ruins (Border Fort near Aaru Village, Deshret's Glass Goblet, Khaj-Nisut, King Deshret's Pillar near Opet Hall, the Soulferry, the Temple of Gurabad, and the Mausoleum of King Deshret).
### ULTRA +
Several mechanisms blow sand away in one direction once struck so that flowing runes form, which is how piles of sand barring the gates are cleared. The tribespeople avoid touching them out of taboo.

## people
@version: 3.4
### STANDARD
No one lives in the ruins. The quest The Temple Where Sand Flows Like Tears brings in Jeht, her Tanit senior Azariq, who sees the Traveler through the first gates and then turns back, and Matriarch Babel, who sent them. Liloupar is found deeper in the temple. Eremites are met in the Pairidaeza Canyon beyond it.
### ULTRA +
Azariq will not go deeper because the ruins are cursed and taboo for the Tanit. Jeht, who counts as half an outsider, is not bound by the same taboo, and the tribe was willing to send her on that basis.

## history
@version: 3.4
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Tanit tradition, relayed by Azariq, says the temple was first built by a vassal king named Ormazd in remembrance of the god-king Al-Ahmar. A tribal legend adds that in the days of Queen Nabu Malikata the Great Rift was green and ruled by giants, who grew vines to hear news of the heavens and were swallowed by the earth. Jeht's books say Gurabad fell to fire and the anger of the Jinn, while Azariq thinks the damage looks like the work of a mob, soldiers or tribespeople.
### LARGE +
Liloupar's own account is that Ormazd was once her lover and that she brought ruin on his line across three generations. In the vision at Gurabad's gate the city is drowned by a red tide of pomegranate juice and the Jinn pour out of machine husks. These memories are shown through her eyes and reflect her point of view. See location.gurabad and npc.liloupar for the fuller account.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Dry canyon heat and silence, sand pouring down from cracks when something deep in the ruins shakes, giant roots through rock, and an ancient mechanical hum that wakes when the great device starts. The tribespeople call the place bad luck, so entering it feels like trespassing on something the desert has chosen to forget.
### ULTRA +
At the cell the plants wither as the bottle is disturbed.

## dangers
@version: 3.4
### LARGE
Primal Constructs (including a Repulsor-type elite and a forgotten guardian called Anoushbord), Eremite Ravenbeak Halberdiers, Linebreakers, Crossbows and Sword-Dancers, plus Stretchy Geo Fungi and Grounded Geoshrooms. Sand piles block the way until the devices are used, and tremors suggest instability.

## travel
### LARGE
The temple lies on the outskirts of Gurabad and is reached from the Tanit camp by way of the canyon. Once activated, the chess pieces act as teleporters, and the Hidden Exploration Objective needs the Traveler to bring each activation device to its piece.

## quests
@version: 3.4
### STANDARD
- The Temple Where Sand Flows Like Tears (World Quest, the Dirge of Bilqis, part 2): Jeht and Azariq lead the Traveler into the ruins to find the fragment of the Mother of the Jinn, whose aid is needed to enter the Eternal Oasis and restore the tribe's land. The Traveler activates four seal-breaking machines and the central device, reveals the plaza, defeats a guardian, finds Liloupar in her bottle and accepts her true name. Jeht is upset by that choice and is then called back by Babel's messenger.
- Memories of Gurabad (World Quest, Apocalypse Lost): after the Traveler has finished The Fallen Falcon and placed five chess pieces on the board, Liloupar asks to be connected with a piece, shows a vision of Gurabad, and brings the Traveler to Gurabad's gate. By the end she leaves her bottle to rejoin her children in Gurabad, though her gadget keeps working.
- Chessboard of Safhe Shatranj (Hidden Exploration Objective, unofficial name): bring seven activation devices to the seven chess pieces.
### ULTRA +
Gameplay note: completing the Hidden Exploration Objective awards seven Remarkable Chests and an achievement. Finishing Memories of Gurabad makes the Safhe Shatranj viewpoint accessible and makes the stone tablets of the desert readable.

## status
### STANDARD
As of 7.1 the ruin stands open and cleared of sand, with Liloupar having left her bottle for Gurabad. The Tanit still regard it as accursed. No later canon changes it.

## entry: Liloupar's Cell
@keys: Liloupar's Cell, Liloupar's Gaol
@version: 3.4
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Liloupar's Cell is a point of interest inside Safhe Shatranj. The Archive names it Liloupar's Gaol and asks what dire criminal the prison, now an oasis, once held. When the Traveler takes the bottle, the tree and other plants wither and Liloupar speaks, calling herself a Jinni and a former mother. Its look changes after discovery during The Temple Where Sand Flows Like Tears. Jeht notes that Jinn make contracts by giving a human their true name.
### ULTRA +
Liloupar later leaves the bottle to go to Gurabad (Memories of Gurabad), but her Jinni in the Magic Bottle gadget continues to work.

## entry: The Temples Forsaken
@keys: The Temples Forsaken, The Great Rift, Pairidaeza Canyon
@version: 3.4
### STANDARD
The Temples Forsaken is the earlier name of the subarea. Its Archive entry, The Palace Abandoned by the Gods, tells a desert song of long-lived giants thrown into a rift in one night. Part-way through The Temple Where Sand Flows Like Tears the name changes to Safhe Shatranj and the sand is cleared. The Tanit call the canyon the Great Rift, which Jeht explains as the maw of the earth.

## entry: Chess pieces and activation devices
@keys: Chess Piece, Activation Device
@keys2: Safhe Shatranj, Liloupar
@version: 3.4
### STANDARD
Seven chess pieces stand on the plaza. Each is paired with an activation device found in a different desert ruin; once the device is placed, the piece becomes a teleport point to where the device stopped. Liloupar connects to a piece during Memories of Gurabad and the Traveler is taken to Gurabad's gate. The pieces' original purpose is not stated in canon.
