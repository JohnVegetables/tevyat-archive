## summary
### LITE
The Sacellum Requietis is the throne room of Domus Aurea, the royal palace of Remuria. The God King Remus once ruled from it. In Version 4.6 it is where the Traveler silences the Grand Symphony Phobos at the end of the Canticles of Harmony quest chain.
### STANDARD +
Canon says very little about the room itself, so its meaning comes from what happened around it: the palace was the instrument of Phobos, and this chamber is where its sound was ended.

## geography
### STANDARD
It lies on the upper level of Domus Aurea, in the Clivus Capitolinus area of the Sea of Bygone Eras. Players reach the upper level by lift after the final fight, and the quest then points to a podium where the Requiem is played on a harp.

## history
### LITE
The room is where Remus held court. According to the old book on Remuria, the king rested at the heart of the palace, listening to every melody and note from across the empire and correcting any discord with a pluck of his strings. He used that position to guide his people's symphony.
### STANDARD +
In the present, Boethius planned a ritual in the palace to take on flesh. After Boethius and Phobos were defeated and Cassiodor faded, the Traveler went to the upper level and played the final Requiem, Remus's final symphony, an order for the Grand Symphony to end, and the souls bound to it went on towards the sea.
### LARGE +
A viewpoint called "Domus Requiem" opens in the palace after the quest, and its text is a prayer that the dead be granted eternal rest and that light shine on them always. A music box tune named Tears of Days Past, which shares the Requiem motif, is also tied to this place.
### ULTRA +
The Requiem leitmotif is shared by three pieces of music in the game: Whisper of Domus Aurea, which plays while the harp is played in the quest, Tears of Days Past, and Lacrimosa, the sixth score of the Ancient Autoharmonic Music Box, which the player unlocks at the end of the quest.

## status
### LITE
Peaceful since the Requiem. Phobos is gone and the palace is silent.
### STANDARD +
Two viewpoints open in the palace after the story, one of them "Domus Requiem".

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Hush and gold, a stage emptied of its conductor. A scene here should feel like attending a funeral after a long war, with a distant echo of choral music.

## entry: The Silencing of Phobos
@keys: silencing of Phobos, Requiem
@keys2: Sacellum Requietis, Domus Aurea
@version: 4.6
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
After the fight with Boethius, Cassiodor asks the Traveler to complete the final Requiem for his Imperator's wishes. Scylla explains that completing Remus's last symphony will send the souls back to the present and into the great sea, where they will join the current and one day be reborn as mortals. The Traveler plays the harp at the podium; the souls follow a golden bee upward, and the people of Petrichor recover.
