## summary
### LITE
Bayt Al-Sukr is a cavern temple inside Mt. Damavand in Sumeru's Desert of Hadramaveth, beneath the Sands of Three Canals, leading into Deshret's Glass Goblet. It is unlocked in Dune-Entombed Fecundity: Part II.
### STANDARD +
The name is Arabic for House of Drunkenness. The map viewpoint calls it the Court of Dried Springs. It lies on the upper level of the Mt. Damavand ruins.

## geography
@version: 3.4
### STANDARD
Beneath the Sands of Three Canals, on the upper level of the Mt. Damavand map. It is one of the routes into the central cavern with Deshret's Glass Goblet.
### LARGE +
Part II has the party reach the area by driving an ancient machine through the dunes with Azariq's help, then entering a cave through the machine's arm.

## history
@version: 3.4
### STANDARD
During Part II, Liloupar says the oasis honoured the Three Eternal Companion-Gods and that the tunnel was dug in the memory of the slumbering lady of the oasis. She says the Lord charged the mortals' ideal kingdom with guarding his oasis, which is why Gurabad stood on the dune's edge before sinking into the sand.
### LARGE +
The party repairs pipes and a stone slate, opens an ancient gate and collects another fragment of Liloupar. Pipes that can be reconnected control the gate.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Dry canals and pipes, sound of sand, ruined machinery. The viewpoint title evokes springs that no longer flow.

## quests
@version: 3.4
### STANDARD
Dune-Entombed Fecundity: Part II, the fourth part of The Dirge of Bilqis.
### ULTRA +
Part II's last mechanisms lead deeper to the mountain's core. Part III follows with betrayal by Azariq and Aderfi; this temple is unlocked before that turn.
