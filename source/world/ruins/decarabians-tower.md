## summary
### LITE
Decarabian's Tower is the great ruined tower at the middle of Old Mondstadt, from which the God of Storms Decarabian ruled and watched over his people. His death blew away part of it. Centuries later the wounded dragon Dvalin laired in the ruin, and the Traveler climbs it in the Prologue.
### STANDARD +
The tower is also the place where a prototype Ruin Guard, the first Field Tiller, still stands inactive.

## geography
### STANDARD
The tower stands in a very deep valley and reaches far down below the aboveground floors. The bottommost reachable floor is blocked off by an out-of-bounds barrier. The top resembles an observatory. Wind currents lift visitors from floor to floor.
### LARGE +
The second-highest floor holds the inactive Field Tiller; the top floor has a Light Actuator and the entrance to the Confront Stormterror domain, whose text implies this was Decarabian's own residence. Climbing a broken pillar from the roof reaches the tower's very top.

## history
### STANDARD
Decarabian took the tower and built his capital around it after a wicked dragon had been cast down on that spot. From its heights he looked down on subjects who, in his mind, adored him. About 2,600 years ago the rebels killed him, and the blast of his death destroyed part of the tower. His storm barrier dropped with him and his people left for Cider Lake.
### LARGE +
A prototype Field Tiller built in Khaenri'ah, damaged in a fight with Boreas' spirit, took shelter in the tower and fired on any approach for roughly a year, a period called the "Tyrant's Final Fury" until its power ran out. Five hundred years ago the dragon Dvalin, wounded and poisoned by Durin, used the tower as his lair.
### ULTRA +
Alice, researching her travel guide, blew up several arcades inside to make the climb easier; the structure has stayed stable despite this, a sign of how well its stone has lasted.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Gales, vertical drops and the tower's guards. The Confront Stormterror domain at the top is a boss encounter; the Prologue confrontation with Dvalin took place around it, freeing him from the Abyss Order's control.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
A hollow spire in a windswept valley: broken arches, drifting air currents and a sense of old authority gone silent.

## status
### LITE
A ruin, open to climb as of 7.1.
