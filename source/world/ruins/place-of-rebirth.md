## summary
### LITE
The Place of Rebirth is a trial chamber southwest of the Mausoleum of King Deshret in Sumeru's Hypostyle Desert. Solving its puzzles in the quest Dual Evidence lights the second clearance level of the Scarlet Sand Slate.
### STANDARD +
The slate says that those who ascend to Aaru will burst into living flame again like the sun after countless nights, and will no longer know the tragedy of age and decay. In an earlier passage it names the resilient as those who pass the trial of Rebirth and gain an unalterable form.

## geography
@version: 3.1
### STANDARD
Lies southwest of the Mausoleum. Players enter from the Dune of Magma and explore underground until a waypoint appears; a small tunnel inside a ruin near a Dendroculus leads to the chamber.
### LARGE +
It is one of three trial chambers, with the Place of Breath off Sekhem Hall and the Place of Swallowing off Duat Hall.

## history
@version: 3.1
### STANDARD
Dual Evidence has the slate point at three places. Clearing the Place of Rebirth lights one of the three sigils (Paimon comments differently depending on which chambers the Traveler has already cleared). The slate's Clearance Level 2 lets the Traveler operate large fan blades elsewhere in the Great Red Sand.
### LARGE +
Lighting the first primal torch in the chamber makes running water audible, which Paimon takes to be a hidden mechanism responding.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
A hidden, intimate chamber reached by a narrow tunnel, with the sound of water running behind stone.

## quests
@version: 3.1
### STANDARD
Dual Evidence (Old Notes and New Friends, part 1). Some of the slate's clearances are also obtained outside this chamber in Golden Slumber and Old Notes and New Friends.
### ULTRA +
Completing all of the slate's clearances other than the City of Gurabad grants the achievement Walk Like King Deshret's People.
