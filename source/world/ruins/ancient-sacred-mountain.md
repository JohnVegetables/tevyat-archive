## summary
### LITE
The Ancient Sacred Mountain is the huge hollow inside the Great Volcano of Tollan in southwestern Natlan. It holds the Sacred City of Tollan, the capital of Natlantea, the dragon civilisation that ruled Natlan before human tribes. Its creator and master, the Pyro Dragon Sovereign Xiuhcoatl, was killed there when the human hero Xbalanque stormed the city, and Xbalanque died with him before being reborn as the first Pyro Archon. The city was then sealed for millennia and overrun by the Abyss. In 5.5 the Traveler, Paimon and the young saurian Little One leapt into the crater, restarted the city's machinery and reached its Heart. There Little One refused to activate Huitzilopochtli, a dragon device that would have burned all Natlan into phlogiston.
### STANDARD +
Today it is a terraced ruin lit by artificial suns (Astral Assemblages), crossed by rivers of liquid phlogiston and tended only by Secret Source machines. The map calls it the holy city of a proud nobility older than the tribes' woven-scroll histories, of which only a lonely tomb remains.

## history
@version: 5.5
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Xiuhcoatl was born from the primal flame on this mountain. He shaped the Sacred City there and moulded the Dragonborn from the primal flame. After the Heavenly Principles defeated the dragon sovereigns he lay in a false death while his brother Kukulkan ruled as Regent. When Nibelung returned, Xiuhcoatl woke corrupted by the Abyss. In his short spells of clarity he rebuilt the city and created the Astral Assemblages and Huitzilopochtli. Xbalanque's army later broke into Tollan, and he killed Xiuhcoatl by striking the eye that served as the array's control hub. The two Golden Entreaties that could start the array were split between Xbalanque and Kukulkan, and the city was sealed.
### LARGE +
The Chamber of Deliberation's records show the city's last politics. The Thirteen Sovereign Lords voted to impeach Kukulkan for giving phlogiston to humans; Awanyu says the trial collapsed into farce when Xiuhcoatl appeared during a lucid spell. A motion to burn down large human settlements failed. During the war the Lords voted to execute the Eighth Lord, who had raised the human Lianca as her daughter. Xbalanque's forces came in through the Skyfire Circlet, and he and Xiuhcoatl fell together into the volcano's depths. Xbalanque was then reborn as the Pyro Archon.
### ULTRA +
According to Awanyu, the Three Princes died fighting humanity, ten of the Thirteen Lords fell when Tollan collapsed, one remained in Chichen Uctokah, and one survives today in a form it would not have chosen. Memories in the phlogiston pools add that after the war the city went dark. Some dragons abandoned wisdom and form to survive in a land now ruled by humans, and others burned out. A lone silver-white figure kept returning to the pools to meet remembered faces there. By oath, only a human can open the city's seals. Over time the Abyss took over the volcano, and it corroded the Heart worst of all. Kukulkan later admitted that he created the legend of the Chosen of Dragons (he also calls the Jade of Return non-existent). It was meant to draw a human and a saurian with Xiuhcoatl's bloodline here, carrying both Entreaties, since Xbalanque's oath meant a human had to bring back the other Entreaty.

## geography
### LARGE
The city lies inside the active volcano that makes up Atocpan, which ancient scrolls already called the "Sacred Mountain". The only way in is the crater at the Skyfire Circlet, choked with gaseous phlogiston. Below lie an upper city (the map's "Dragons' Residence"), deeper districts reached by the central Celestial Axis lift, and finally the Heart of the Sacred Mountain, a great fissure beneath everything.
### ULTRA +
Liquid phlogiston fills pools and falls everywhere, and in the Heart of Force Inversion it flows upward. Secret Source Servants can lay down patches of solid phlogiston, and a Coagulation Pearl can freeze a whole fall into a pillar. Three Astral Assemblages hang over the upper city and feed beams to the pillars of the Celestial Axis. Below the cloud layer the ruins are thick with Abyssal mud.

## places
### LARGE
Summoning Hall (the entrance, under the crater); Sea of Shifting Sentience (phlogiston pools where dragons absorbed stored knowledge); Flame-Melding Ritual Grounds (birthplace of the first Astral Assemblage); Celestial Axis; Chamber of Deliberation (the Lords' council hall); Heart of the Sacred Mountain (the primal flame's heart and Huitzilopochtli).
### ULTRA +
Also: the Esoteric Arrays, an old calculation array; the Heart of Force Inversion; the Ruined Armament Workshop, a weapons works where dragons seem also to have experimented on humans; and the Pseudostar Pedestal, where a workshop engraving in Xiuhcoatl's voice curses Tollan's enemies and swears the city will survive both the "usurper of the sky" and the earth's "black tides". The viewpoint Court of the Dragons says the Chamber became a place where "dragon cast down dragon" once its master was gone.

## people
### LARGE
No living people or dragons remain. Secret Source Servants maintain the city. A nameless prototype, built to help Xiuhcoatl record information, says no humans had come this far down in at least 500 years. A guiding voice speaks through dormant servants under names of the fallen (Ehecatl, Amaru, Awanyu), and the finale strongly implies it is Kukulkan's. In the Heart, dragons who gave up their bodies for phlogiston form still dream on a dead king's decree.
### ULTRA +
Amaru's namesake was an arrogant dragon who held the threshold of the Celestial Axis until he died, and a servant that once waited on him now stands there. The voice despises the Chamber's lords as power-hungry fools, and hints that they once regarded it as a criminal.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Heat, silence and gold light. Phlogiston pools glow under artificial suns. Golden inscriptions replay old voices when touched, and patient machines whirr awake, speak and fall silent. The city is grand and dead at once. Lower down, black Abyssal mud covers the stone.

## dangers
### LARGE
The Abyss is the main threat. Floating Oozing Cores fire Abyssal bolts, black mud drains health, and Abyss Mages, Rifthounds and Mimifloras roam the ruins. Some of the city's own defences still work, including a golden purifying barrier that consumes friend and foe alike. Liquid and gaseous phlogiston are dangerous in themselves.
### ULTRA +
Oozing Cores break only to phlogiston explosions or a Tatankasaurus charge, and the mud prevents sprinting. The Heart's Astral Assemblages attack anyone without a permit, and in the finale a Source Dragon Mech guards the Heart. An Astral Assemblage that touches the Abyss either destroys itself together with it or is corrupted by it.

## travel
### LARGE
Climb the Skyfire Circlet, usually by riding Atocpan's Spiritways, and jump into the crater; the Traveler learned the way by watching a Qucusaur dive in carrying a stolen statue. Restarting the Celestial Axis opens the depths. The Heart needs a Permit to the Sacred Heart and three Phlogiston Units to wake its guardian Secret Source Dragon Idol.
### ULTRA +
Gameplay (labelled): a separate map whose levels unlock with quest progress. Dropping from an upper level to a lower one turns the player into a fireball that lands on nearby ground, with no loss of HP or Energy.

## quests
@version: 5.5
### LARGE
Path to the Flaming Peaks (the leap into the crater); Chronicler of the Crumbling City (restarting the Assemblages, winning the permit, cleansing the ruins, descending to the Heart); A Finale Emberforged (An Omen of Annihilation and the Final Entreaty, the end of Little One's story; see the finale entry).

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the Sacred City is an open but ruined area. It has been unsealed, much of the Abyss has been cleared, and Huitzilopochtli was not activated. Kukulkan faded away at the Heart, and Little One, freed of the Flamelord's Blessing, is a small Tepetlisaurus again.
### STANDARD +
This research found no 6.x (Nod-Krai) or 7.0-7.1 story content that returns here. Kukulkan warned that the city no longer had the energy to sustain the dragons living on as phlogiston and that they would perish without the primal flame's authority; once the maintenance functions failed, the wiki reads them as fading with him, though no scene shows it directly.

## entry: The finale in the Heart of the Sacred Mountain
@keys: Heart of the Sacred Mountain, Final Entreaty, Chosen of Dragons, Source Dragon Mech
@version: 5.5
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
In An Omen of Annihilation and the Final Entreaty (5.5), the Traveler and Little One offer the two Golden Entreaties in the Heart of the Sacred Mountain. A Source Dragon Mech traps the Traveler and Paimon. Guided by Kukulkan, Little One briefly takes a "mature dragon" form with the power of speech. Kukulkan reveals himself as the Sage of the Stolen Flame and shows him the Heart. He asks Little One to start Huitzilopochtli and rule a phlogiston world as a renewed Pyro Dragon Sovereign. Little One foresees an empty, eternal white world and cancels the array. He will not put the Traveler and Paimon at risk. He breaks back to them, the Mech is defeated and the Flamelord's Blessing is lifted. Kukulkan, alone by the array, admits he made the same choice long ago and fades. The volcano shakes, but only lets out a little smoke.

## entry: Huitzilopochtli
@keys: Huitzilopochtli, Sweeping Skyfire, Sunquake, Natlan Phlogistonization Array
@version: 5.5
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Huitzilopochtli, the "Sweeping Skyfire" or Natlan Phlogistonization Array, is the last great device of the ancient dragons. It stands in the Heart of the Sacred Mountain beneath the Great Volcano of Tollan. Kukulkan explained it to Little One. Xiuhcoatl built it to burn everything in Natlan and copy its information into phlogiston, turning the land into a realm of primal flame. Astral Assemblages would then destroy the Abyss on contact. Living things would either die or give up their bodies and become phlogiston lifeforms, and draconic ones would rule the new world. The array is started by the two Golden Entreaties, made from Xiuhcoatl's pupils, and needs three confirmations to activate or cancel. In 5.5 Little One cancelled it.
### LARGE +
Towers placed across Natlan anchored the array's reach. Och-Kan ordered most of them destroyed, but their fragments still serve, because a single stone can carry Iridescent Inscriptions. Kukulkan describes phlogiston as matter that can store information and imitate anything through those inscriptions, which explains the phantom scenes replayed in the city.

## entry: Astral Assemblages
@keys: Astral Assemblage, Astral Assemblages, Tzitzimimeh
@version: 5.5
### STANDARD
Astral Assemblages (Tzitzimimeh) are floating balls of phlogiston, pressed together into controllable miniature suns. Xiuhcoatl made the first at the Flame-Melding Ritual Grounds of the Sacred City, and the cold voice guiding the Traveler (taken to be Kukulkan) compared the feat to "lunar reconstruction". Their purpose is to fight the Abyss by mutual annihilation, but they cannot tell friend from foe. Three hang over the upper city and power the Celestial Axis. During The Frozen, Rekindling Land the Traveler stabilised the two that had been sealed or drained.

## entry: Chamber of Deliberation (Sacred City)
@keys: Chamber of Deliberation, Court of the Dragons, Sovereign Rulers' Council Records
@version: 5.5
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The Chamber of Deliberation is where the Thirteen Sovereign Lords of Natlantea met, with the First Lord as Speaker, to vote on government and judgment. Its records, written in phlogiston, survive. One is a vote to impeach Regent Kukulkan, with the Second Lord's argument erased and Kukulkan's mocking reply later surfacing over it. Another is a failed motion to burn down large human settlements. A third is the vote that condemned the Eighth Lord for her loyalty to her adopted human daughter Lianca. The Chamber still has working defences and a self-repair system. The Permit to the Sacred Heart was kept above it.

## entry: Secret Source Servants of Tollan
@keys: Ehecatl, Amaru, Awanyu, Secret Source Servant, Secret Source Servants
@keys2: Tollan, Sacred City, Sacred Mountain
@version: 5.5
### STANDARD
In the Sacred City, Secret Source Servants are dragon-made machines that still guard and maintain the ruins. A voice borrowed three of them to guide the Traveler and Little One, naming each after one of the fallen. Ehecatl was a servant a Qucusaur had stolen from the Tephric Ruins. Amaru was named after an arrogant lord who held the Celestial Axis to his death. Awanyu was named after a scribe of the Chamber of Deliberation. After the quest, Ehecatl's dormant body can be returned to the Tephric Ruins. A separate nameless prototype, built to help Xiuhcoatl record information, also speaks to them.

## entry: Och-Kan's origins in Tollan
@keys: Och-Kan, Flamestealer, Nest Experiment Log
@keys2: Tollan, Sacred City, Sacred Mountain, Kukulkan, vishap
@version: 5.5
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
Two texts in the Sacred City concern Kukulkan's son. In the "Flamestealer's" record, Kukulkan rejoices at the birth of a son with dragon blood whose looks would fool even his brother. He refuses to raise the boy as a mere dragon lord, and wonders whether to send him away rather than steer every choice he makes. The Nest Experiment Log in the Ruined Armament Workshop tells of a child-like test subject thrown among vishaps. The child grew strong, shared food with the vishaps and showed fear and empathy. After a "neutralization injection" the subject escaped, and the Regent called off the hunt, saying he had dealt with it personally. Present this as dragon records, not as a scene to dramatise.

## entry: Last words of the Sovereign Lords
@keys: Iridescent Legacy, Iridescent Inscription
@version: 5.5
### LARGE
The Iridescent Legacy is four inscribed messages from Sovereign Lords who died as Tollan fell. The First Lord welcomed Xbalanque as a worthy foe and admitted that the dragons' civilisation had stagnated. The Seventh marvelled that humans kept coming despite their frailty. The Fifth cursed the humans' "crude misuse of phlogiston" and still believed Xiuhcoatl would burn them away. The Twelfth realised too late that a human leader had outwitted her and that her own kind had decayed. The first message is in the Skyfire Circlet, the others in the Heart of Force Inversion, the Flame-Melding Ritual Grounds and the Sea of Shifting Sentience.
