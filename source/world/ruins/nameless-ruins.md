## summary
### LITE
The Nameless Ruins are an area of ancient, unnamed ruins in the Underground Mines under the Chasm in Liyue. Mounds of dark mud cover the floor, and the roof of the cavern bears an upside-down ruin with a water pool: the Inverted City, an old Lang-Gan site. The mines' travellers meet Abyss mages, Ruin Guards and hilichurls here.
### STANDARD +
The Nameless Ruins are where the Archon Quest of Chapter II brings the Traveler and Dainsleif deep below the Chasm.

## geography
### STANDARD
A set of halls, stairs and sealed sections in the Underground Mines. The floor holds solid dark-mud mounds, luminous Seelie wander the dark, and Oozing Concretions and Noctilucous Jade grow here. A purple barrier closed off the Inverted City until "The Grave of the Guarded".
### LARGE +
The architecture is geometric with triangular forms, gold strips and vertical sliding doors. The wiki observes a resemblance to Enkanomiya, which is not stated in canon.

## people
### LARGE
No settled inhabitants. In 2.6 hilichurls drifted here to die, protected by cursed Black Serpent Knights loyal to Dainsleif's old command; Abyss Order cells appear in the same area.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Elite Abyss mages (Electro, Hydro, Pyro), Ruin Guards and Ruin Graders, Black Serpent Knights and Shadowy Husks, plus Hilichurls, Floating Hydro Fungus, Cryo Whopperflowers and Geo Slimes.

## quests
### STANDARD
"Requiem of the Echoing Depths" (Chapter II Act IV, 2.6), especially "The Grave of the Guarded"; it also lies on the way for "Perilous Trail" in the Chasm.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 a dark, mostly empty set of ruins under a partly reopened mine.
