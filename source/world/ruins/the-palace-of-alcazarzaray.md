## summary
### LITE
The Palace of Alcazarzaray is a grand garden palace deep in Sumeru's Lokapala Jungle, north of Sumeru City. It belongs to the merchant Dori, who is known there as Lord Sangemah Bay, and it was designed by the architect Kaveh, who considers it his magnum opus. Building it left Kaveh in debt to Dori.
### STANDARD
Dori asked only that it be large and extravagant, so Kaveh had almost full creative freedom. The Archive shows a purple garden before the palace where flowers dance and warblers sing. Visitors find the palace staffed by a butler, merchants and students, and people praise its architect while many do not know who owns it.
### LARGE +
It is the setting of the start of Kaveh's Hangout Event, and Kaveh became insolvent after building it and was forced to stay with Alhaitham.

## geography
@version: 3.0
### STANDARD
The palace stands in the Dharma Forest area of Lokapala Jungle, in the depths of the woods, just north of Sumeru City according to Dori's wiki page. A purple garden lies before it. The wiki lists Padisarah and Nilotpala Lotus as local specialties and a Four-Leaf Sigil, Auspicious Branch, Dendro Elemental Monument and bouncy mushrooms nearby.
### LARGE +
The project site was beset by the Withering, which is why the palace is a rebuild. The grounds use ornamental plants chosen with advice from Tighnari, whom Kaveh asked for recommendations in the initial planning.

## people
@version: 3.0
### STANDARD
Dori, the owner, appears only intermittently; her butler Goshtasb says the Lord is not in and promises to introduce visitors when she returns. Other open-world figures at the palace include Gaston (a trainee reporter of The Steambird who travelled from Fontaine to interview the owner), the merchants Rajavi and Zhila, the student Sasani, and Ehsan, Mandana, Shaghayeh and Soltani. Kaveh, the architect, does not live there but still owes Dori for it.
### ULTRA +
Goshtasb says the Lord built such a big palace but no waiting place for guests at the gate. Gaston notes that everyone praises the architect but no one knows who owns the estate.

## history
@version: 3.0
### STANDARD
At some point after getting rich, Dori had Kaveh build it. Kaveh gave the project everything. Near the end of construction the Withering spread in the night, destroying the worksite and leaving it beyond saving. Dori did not want to continue, but Kaveh had already invested too much and knew how rare such an opportunity was, so he took a loan from her and rebuilt it, leaving him in massive debt (Kaveh's fourth character story).
### LARGE +
In his voice-overs Kaveh calls it his magnum opus, says he sacrificed much for it and that others thought he had lost his mind. Tighnari says Kaveh ended up heavily in debt after it was finished and that Alhaitham lets him stay in his home. In The Price of a Wish (3.7), Kaveh insists he will repay every coin he still owes.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Perfumed with jungle flowers and Padisarah, the garden is quiet apart from birdsong. A servant at the gate speaks of the Lord in formal terms; visitors wait without chairs. Wealth and a touch of absurdity sit side by side.

## quests
@version: 3.7
### STANDARD
- The Price of a Wish (Hangout Event: Kaveh, Act I, The Pendulum of Weal and Woe, 3.7) begins here: Kaveh and the Traveler meet Dori after failing to find a sponsor for his education-and-culture project. She finally agrees to fund the project, which is aimed at giving the desert's children books, and gets Kaveh to waive his commission and shoulder the project.
### ULTRA +
The same quest reveals that Dori has been buying up land around Aaru Village; she did not tell Kaveh. During Sightseeing With Friends: Back to Sumeru, a limited-time event, Paimon points out the palace as a scenic stop for the Fungi.

## status
### STANDARD
As of 7.1 the palace is still owned by Dori and open to visitors, and no later canon changes it.

## entry: Kaveh and the palace
@keys: Kaveh, Palace of Alcazarzaray, Alcazarzaray
@keys2: debt, loan, architect, magnum opus
@version: 3.0
### STANDARD
Kaveh, the Akademiya-trained architect known as the Light of Kshahrewar, designed the palace for Dori. He calls it his best work. The project cost him far more than it earned him, and he lives with Alhaitham because of it. Character details belong in character.kaveh.
### ULTRA +
In his voice-over about Dori, Kaveh insists he knows full well the palace is not his and does not want to discuss it further.

## entry: Dori's staff and visitors

Goshtasb, the butler, defers everything to the Lord and says Dori will be in later. Gaston, a young reporter from The Steambird, wants a scoop and is certain that the owner must be someone very important. Staff call Dori Lord, and her formal title is Lord Sangemah Bay.
