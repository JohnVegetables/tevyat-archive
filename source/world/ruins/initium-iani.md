## summary
### LITE
Initium Iani (Latin, roughly "the beginning of Ianus") is the great gate between Machimos, Remuria's outer city, and Capitolium, its inner city. It leads to the Passage of Ianus, the only way between the two, and it was where the dragon Scylla was sealed. In The Last Day of Remuria, Scylla, freed after millennia, blasts it open so the Traveler, Paimon and Monsieur Os can reach the Domus Aurea.
### STANDARD +
Introduced in 4.6. The Remurian symphony ran through its golden waterways, which let Boethius's forces shut the gate and trap Scylla as he tried to break in.

## geography
### STANDARD
A subarea at the edge of Machimos. Behind it a water current runs the full length of the Passage of Ianus into Capitolium. The gate is a stone door carved with seals, which fall away one by one when broken.
### LARGE +
Stalactites hang in the corridor beyond and fall or block the way, so passage is a rushed swim. Machimos also includes Hortus Euergetis, Caesareum Palace and Portus Anticus.

## people
### STANDARD
Scylla, the vishap Dragon Prince, is tied to this place by his sealing and his breakout. Cassiodor (as Monsieur Os) explains its history. The Harmosts Boethius and Euergetia are credited with the original sealing.
### LARGE +
Remus, the Sebastos, designed the symphony that flowed here; Scylla says the invasion was a plan with Remus. Legio VI held Caesareum Palace, where the chains were stored.

## governance
### LARGE
The gate was a military control point. Engravings at Caesareum Palace show Boethius ordering Portus Anticus and the Initium Iani sealed at once, seven defensive layers set and all routes of egress closed, and the Dragonborn destroyed on arrival. If the chieftain could not be slain, the chains were to be used to imprison him in the palace.

## history
### LARGE
Remurian history: Scylla led the Dragonborn at the capital, wanting to pass this gate. Monsieur Os says Boethius and his legions used the symphony in the golden waterways to shut the gate and seal the evil dragon. Scylla's version differs: Boethius falsely claimed to have halted resistance on Remus's orders, then rose against him and turned his flesh to stone.
### ULTRA +
After Remuria sank, Scylla remained petrified for millennia. In The Last Day of Remuria the Traveler breaks three chains in Machimos and frees him. In Gradus ad Capitolium Scylla explains that shattering this gate used nearly all his remaining power, so the Domus Aurea must be opened by other means.

## quests
### STANDARD
The Last Day of Remuria: Scylla, freed, calls the gate a glorified stone door and blasts through its seals while the group swims the Passage of Ianus. The Traveler must stay close to avoid falling stalactites.
### LARGE +
Monsieur Os remarks that he once crossed between bodies freely so long as one was prepared in the inner city; with only his small cat body now, the group must take this road.

## status
### LITE
Broken open by Scylla during the 4.6 quests; the door swung shut behind the group. No later content revisits it.

## dangers
### LARGE
Falling stalactites, current and the closing door; no sentinel remains.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
A cold, vaulted entrance, then a tunnel of current and falling stone. The mood is of crossing a threshold between a defeated outer world and a sealed inner one.
