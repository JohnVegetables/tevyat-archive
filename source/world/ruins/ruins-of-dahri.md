## summary
### LITE
The Ruins of Dahri are Khaenri'ahn ruins in the Ashavan Realm of Sumeru's Dharma Forest. In Sumeru Dahri is an archaic name for the lost underground kingdom of Khaenri'ah, and the ruin is full of Ruin Guards, which the Aranara call Big Iron Chunks. In the quest Irate Iron Chunk the Traveler follows the Aranara Arabalika underground to reach a root of the Varuna Contraption.
### STANDARD +
It is a separate place from the Valley of Dahri in the desert. Its enemies include Ruin Hunters and Ruin Cruisers.

## geography
@version: 3.0
### STANDARD
A subarea of the Ashavan Realm, with a Caravan Ribat waypoint; the Traveler first meets Arabalika east of it. Below the surface is a cavern reached by draining water and following Arabalika's burrow.
### LARGE +
A hole opens at the bottom of the drained area into a deep cavern full of inactive Ruin Guards around a stone 'branch'. The Grove of Dreams, another underground cavern holding one of the Varuna Contraption's energy sources, connects to the Ruins of Dahri to the west.

## people
@version: 3.0
### STANDARD
Arabalika, an Aranara with powerful Ararakalari, is exploring alone. He speaks bluntly and without the metaphors other Aranara use, and says he thinks little and only knows how to protect. He is dissatisfied with Paimon and tests the Traveler's strength before trusting them.

## history
@version: 3.0
### STANDARD
Dahri is the archaic term in Vahumana scholarship for Khaenri'ah, a word meaning roughly one who denies a creator, as the wiki's etymology note puts it. The ruins are among the remnants of Khaenri'ah that the wiki says share a grand scale, together with giant Ruin Golems and the Azosite factory under Gavireh Lajavard.
### LARGE +
An Akademiya report at Devantaka Mountain mentions a legend of the Ruins of Dahri that may confirm the Schwanenritter existed. In Irate Iron Chunk Arabalika says the stone and iron here do not belong to Aranara, and he smells iron and calls it a bad smell. The seemingly broken Ruin Guards around the stone branch are quiet until the 'leaves' are turned, after which a huge Ruin Grader starts to move.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Water and fungi on the surface and a deep cavern below that smells of iron to Arabalika. He says the stone and iron chunks here do not belong to the Aranara.

## dangers
### LARGE
Ruin Guards, Ruin Hunters and Ruin Cruisers; Floating Dendro Fungus and Winged Dendroshroom. Arabalika can defeat a Ruin Guard alone, and in the quest he deals Dendro damage at intervals while fighting alongside the Traveler.

## quests
@version: 3.0
### STANDARD
Irate Iron Chunk is a quest in the Varuna Gatha series, begun after Varuna Gatha, with Arapandu asking the Traveler to help heal the Varuna Contraption. The party defeats Ruin Guards, activates a mechanism to drain the water, descends, turns three 'leaves' toward the branch, defeats a Ruin Grader and talks to Arabalika again.
### LARGE +
The Varuna Contraption draws its power from a root system spread throughout Vana. Arabalika tells how Arapandu once protected Vana with Nara Varuna, drove away the black mud and healed the Contraption, and that the story of Nara Varuna is remembered even though Arabalika never met them. Slumbering Roots, a concurrent quest, covers other roots.
### ULTRA +
Arapandu hopes to heal the Contraption again for Festival Utsava, and in the Slumbering Roots quest he is not worried that Arabalika is in the Ruins of Dahri. The wiki ties the Contraption to Greater Lord Rukkhadevata, who created it to call timely rains over a section of the desert.

## status
### LITE
Cleared of the main threat. Nothing found up to Version 7.1 changes the ruin.

## entry: Dahri
@keys: Dahri, Dahrian, Dahrians
@keys2: Khaenri'ah, Sumeru, Ruins of Dahri
@version: 3.0
### STANDARD
Dahri is an archaic Sumeru term for Khaenri'ah, used especially in Vahumana scholarship. The Persian-Arabic word dahri refers to an atheist or one who denies a creator god, and the game uses it for Khaenri'ah. The Ruins of Dahri and the Valley of Dahri carry the name, and the wiki notes the term refers to Khaenri'ah in the game.
