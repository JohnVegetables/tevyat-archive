## summary
### LITE
Old Vanarana is the original home of the Aranara, the small forest spirits of Sumeru, lying below the Fane of Ashvattha in the Lost Nursery of Dharma Forest. It was their home for hundreds of years before the cataclysm. Afterward the Aranara sealed Marana's Avatar in its depths. The Withering engulfs it at first, stronger than in most places, until the Traveler and Arama destroy the Avatar in the Aranyaka quest Nursery of Lost Dreams.
### STANDARD +
The path in is the Door of Vana, the gate from the Fane of Ashvattha, which the quest opens. After the Avatar's destruction Old Vanarana begins to regrow.

## geography
@version: 3.0
### STANDARD
A subarea of the Lost Nursery in Dharma Forest, with a purified and a corrupted version, the latter shown on the map as withered. It is unlocked in For Fruits, Seeds, and Trees. Arama later drains the sand from a chamber, revealing a massive tunnel.
### LARGE +
The ruin has a chamber with seven Stone Pillar Seals, a massive tunnel full of scattered Ruin Machines, and a deep place called the Land of Grounded Dreams where Marana's Avatar waits. Sand-Embraced Home, where the last seal was guarded, lies within.

## people
@version: 3.0
### STANDARD
Old Vanarana belongs to the Aranara. The friendly Aranara Arama is the Traveler's guide. Aramuhukunda, the first Aranara and a Vasara Tree, appears to unlock the last seal.
### LARGE +
Three Aranara, Aramaha, Arayama and Araji, made Inscriptions of Remembrance for the first seal and later became Vasara Trees in Ashvattha's Concourses. Aradasha's Stone Pillar Seals, Aramaha's Healthy Dendrograna and Arayama's Claustroflora help to form the third seal through Ararakalari.

## history
@version: 3.0
### STANDARD
After the cataclysm the Aranara locked Marana's Avatar deep in Old Vanarana with four layers of seals. The first was the Door of Vana, the gate between the Fane of Ashvattha and the ruin. The second was three Inscriptions of Remembrance. The third was a set of Ararakalari, including Healthy Dendrograna, Claustroflora and Stone Pillar Seals. The fourth was at Sand-Embraced Home, guarded by Aramuhukunda himself.
### LARGE +
Over time Marana's corrosion leaked out despite the seals. In Nursery of Lost Dreams the Traveler and Arama undo all four seals and enter the Land of Grounded Dreams, and the Aranara tell how they fiercely fought the cataclysm's invaders. Ruin Machines and riftwolves in the tunnel are remnants of that invasion.
### ULTRA +
Marana's Avatar tries to hide its soul in three Withering Zones, each of which has to be cleared before its core can be destroyed. Afterward Arama dives into the ground as a sapling and becomes the new Ashvattha Tree, removing the Withering from Old Vanarana. The Traveler and Paimon play the Rhythm of the Great Dream again to let a Bija fruit form; Arama has forgotten them. The viewpoint of the Nursery of Lost Dreams appears only after a later quest, 'Hello,' 'Thank You,' and the Final 'Goodbye'.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Before the quest: a withered ruin under sand, choked by the Withering. Afterward: a place that begins to recover, as the viewpoint's text says, 'like a stream purifying itself or a withered tree sprouting once more.'

## dangers
### LARGE
Hilichurls, Mitachurls, slimes, Rockfond Rifthounds and Thundercraven Rifthounds, and Ruin Guards roam. A Ruin Grader activates at the tunnel's bottom and proves unusually resilient until Arama binds it. The Withering is stronger here than in most places, so Arama shields the group from its effects.

## quests
@version: 3.0
### STANDARD
Nursery of Lost Dreams is the third part of the Aranyaka world quest series, in three quests: For Fruits, Seeds, and Trees; For the Children of the Past; and For All Children Who Long for Life. The Traveler and Arama break the seals, defeat Marana's Avatar and plant the new Ashvattha Tree here.
### LARGE +
Arama goes ahead with a shield against the Withering. In the third quest the Avatar moves its soul among three Withering Zones before being destroyed for good.

## status
### LITE
Purified and recovering. Marana is gone for the foreseeable future, though traces of the Withering remain.
### STANDARD +
No later change is recorded up to Version 7.1.

## entry: Marana's Avatar
@keys: Marana's Avatar, Marana
@keys2: Old Vanarana, Aranara, Withering
@version: 3.0
### STANDARD
Marana's Avatar is the embodiment of the Withering that the Aranara sealed in Old Vanarana after the cataclysm. In Nursery of Lost Dreams it attacks the party and relocates its soul to different Withering Zones. When all three are cleared the Traveler destroys its core.
