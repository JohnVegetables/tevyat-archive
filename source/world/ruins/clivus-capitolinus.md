## summary
### LITE
Clivus Capitolinus is the hill at the heart of Capitolium, the inner city of ancient Remuria's capital. Its summit holds the Domus Aurea, the royal palace. The name also covers the cavern under the hill, which contains the Local Legend Cineas, called the Unreturned Envoy. It is named after the road up the Capitoline Hill in Rome.
### STANDARD +
The Traveler comes here in the final stretch of Canticles of Harmony (version 4.6), climbing past the hill's old palace to face Boethius inside Domus Aurea.

## geography
### STANDARD
The hill belongs to the Sea of Bygone Eras. The surface area is divided into Capitolium and its Old Palace, with the Domus Aurea on top and the Sacellum Requietis inside that palace. A cavern runs beneath the hill. Capitolium itself is entered only through the Passage of Ianus from Machimos.
### LARGE +
The approach to the palace is through golden aqueducts (the Alta Semita) and other fairways that once carried the symphony, near Collegium Phonascorum. Gradus ad Capitolium, the name of the quest, means roughly steps to the Capitol.

## places
### LARGE
Domus Aurea: the golden palace on the hill, with its upper-level throne room.
Capitolium: Old Palace: an older palace precinct that appears as part of the same map level.
The cavern beneath: holds an engraving and the sealed-in vishap Cineas.

### ULTRA +
The engraving is found just north of Cineas, inside the cavern.

## people
### STANDARD
In its prime, Capitolium was a haven for artists and musicians where only the most outstanding were admitted, and a smaller number served the God King directly. In the present, the only resident is the vishap Cineas.

## history
### LITE
This was the centre of Remuria: the palace where Remus listened to every note of the empire and corrected any discord with a pluck of his strings, surrounded by theatres and palaces with carved beams and domes. After the great wave, the whole hill sank.
### STANDARD +
An engraving in the cavern records Boethius ordering Dragonborn envoys to be received to discuss cooperation. The rest has been struck out, and below it come scratched lines repeating "Traitor", followed by a line addressed to "xenoproles" telling them to "sink into this immortal prison with us". Cassiodor says an envoy from Fontaine was imprisoned here by Boethius.
### LARGE +
Read with Scylla's account, the engraving suggests Boethius used the language of diplomacy against the Dragonborn side, as Scylla says he did when he claimed to have ordered the legions to stop resisting and then turned on them. This is an inference, since no text says so directly about Cineas.

## status
### LITE
Quiet since the Requiem. The Domus Aurea above is silent and Cineas remains in the cavern, where he can be fought but need not be.
### STANDARD +
The score Confutatis for the Ancient Autoharmonic Music Box lies near Cineas. The hill is part of the explorable area after the 4.6 quests.

## dangers
### LARGE
Praetorian Golems patrol Domus Aurea and its approaches. Cineas is a powerful Primordial Bathysmal Vishap Local Legend; he does not have to be fought to obtain Confutatis.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Hushed grandeur. The hill looks like a processional way that has stopped mid-ceremony, and the cavern below feels like a cell, with scratched words on the wall.

## entry: Cineas, the Unreturned Envoy
@keys: Cineas
@version: 4.6
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
A Primordial Bathysmal Vishap, a Local Legend whose title calls him an envoy who never returned. Osse (Cassiodor) tells the Traveler that an envoy from Fontaine was once imprisoned in this area by Boethius. The cavern engraving is the best surviving hint of how that happened; no source gives Cineas's own words.
