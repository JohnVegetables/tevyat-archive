## summary
### LITE
Qusayr Al-Inkhida' is a ruined palace in the Desert of Hadramaveth in Sumeru's Great Red Sand. It has an Eremite camp and caverns below it that lead toward Deshret's Glass Goblet, but canon tells almost nothing about its history.
### STANDARD +
Its name is Arabic for something like Small Palace of Deception according to the wiki; that is a name, not a story.

## geography
@version: 3.4
### STANDARD
A subarea of the Desert of Hadramaveth with a ruin that has a high platform. Collectibles include a Radiant Spincrystal on that platform and a Remarkable Chest holding the blueprint for the furnishing The Art of Retaining Water. Torn pages of A Thousand Nights lie near Eremite camps.
### LARGE +
The caverns under the palace, called Bayt Al-Muazzam, lead into the cavern holding Deshret's Glass Goblet. They are unlocked during Dune-Entombed Fecundity: Part I of The Dirge of Bilqis.

## people
@version: 3.4
### STANDARD
Eremites camp in the ruin. Some of the pages of A Thousand Nights (II and III) that complete a book are found beside them.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
A weathered palace in open desert, a place with a camp rather than a story.

### ULTRA +
The wiki notes that the Desert of Hadramaveth has more subareas than any other area, eleven in all. The area's event description speaks of a lost city and a wandering tribe sheltering among scimitar-shaped ravines, and of stories erased by sandstorms; this palace is one of the many ruins in that setting.

## quests
@version: 3.4
### LARGE
No quest is based here. The caverns beneath it are unlocked during Dune-Entombed Fecundity: Part I, as the party seeks the Eternal Oasis in Mt. Damavand.

## status
### LITE
Open and unremarkable. Nothing found up to Version 7.1 adds lore.

## entry: Bayt Al-Muazzam
@keys: Bayt Al-Muazzam, Temple of Buried Dominion
@keys2: Qusayr Al-Inkhida', Glass Goblet
@version: 3.4
### STANDARD
Bayt Al-Muazzam is the wiki's name for the caverns under Qusayr Al-Inkhida' that lead into Deshret's Glass Goblet; the name means House of the Glorified. Its viewpoint is called the Temple of Buried Dominion.
