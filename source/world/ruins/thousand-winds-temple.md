## summary
### LITE
The Thousand Winds Temple is a ruined temple and amphitheater in Starfell Valley. The Arcadians built it to worship Istaroth, Ruler of Time; later it served both Istaroth and Barbatos, and in the aristocratic age it became an arena where slaves fought for the nobles' amusement. It is also where the future Anemo Archon, then a small wind spirit, was first answered.
### STANDARD +
The official map text calls it a long-forgotten commemorative site, carved with the maxim that wind brings the seeds of tales and time lets them take root.

## geography
### STANDARD
A collection of temple ruins northeast of Mondstadt. Pillars still bear faint elegant carvings. A large sundial east of the Temple of the Wolf carries an inscription about seeds of stories brought by the wind. Hilichurls, slimes and a Cryo Regisvine roam the site, with Ruin Guards and Abyss mages among its tougher inhabitants.
### LARGE +
Dandelion Seed grows here. NPCs around the ruins include Patchi the Curious (a researcher), Doolan and Henry Morton.

## history
### STANDARD
Arcadians built the temple for Istaroth. When Arcadia fell, it was left in ruins. During the Archon War the Gunnhildr Clan settled among the ruins, seeking help against the blizzards of Decarabian and Boreas' conflict, and raised a small shrine to Istaroth. Their prayers were answered by a small wind spirit, who became Barbatos.
### LARGE +
Venti has said that as a wind spirit he met Istaroth here by chance and she entrusted him with fragments of her power and authority (told during the limited-time Sunspray Summer Resort event, 5.8). Once he ascended as Anemo Archon, the temple was rebuilt for worship of both gods, and the Sacrificial weapons were used as ceremonial implements in its rites. Istaroth was later forgotten, leaving only Barbatos worshipped.
### ULTRA +
In the aristocratic age the temple was turned into an arena where slaves were forced to fight one another. It was eventually abandoned again. The Arcadia page links this temple with the Temple of Space, which was once connected to the temple of the Master of Time and later lifted into the sky, but see the uncertainty about whether the connected temple is this site.

## people
### STANDARD
Patchi the Curious (a researcher), Doolan and Henry Morton can be met around the site.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Weathered stone tiers around a wide floor, wind-bent grass and half-lost carvings: a place that has had three different meanings and kept the marks of all of them.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Hilichurl and mitachurl camps, Ruin Guards, Cryo Abyss mages and a Cryo Regisvine.

## status
### LITE
A ruin as of 7.1, still visited by researchers.
