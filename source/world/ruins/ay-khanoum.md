## summary
### LITE
Ay-Khanoum ("City of the Moon Maiden" in the Jinn's ancient tongue) was an ancient city of Sumeru, jointly ruled by Nabu Malikata and King Deshret and built for the Jinn. It is also called the City of Amphitheaters. It is now ruins, probably in the Hypostyle Desert, and only appears in Sumeru through lore.
### STANDARD +
Later it became one of the desert city-states of the age after Deshret, ruled by a Khagan called Garshasp, who gathered several cities under him before dying; his capital burned afterwards.

## history
@version: 3.0
### STANDARD
Item lore says the "Mistress of Flowers and Oases" (Nabu Malikata) and the desert's king (Deshret) built Ay-Khanoum for the Jinn. In the legend of Nabu Malikata, a Seelie survivor of the War of Funerary Flame, her wounds bled into streams that fed gardens, from which water lilies rose and gave birth to the Jinn; at the Jinn's request she stayed in that garden and padisarah flowers bloomed wherever she walked. The wiki presumes the city was founded there. A few generations after Gurabad fell, Ay-Khanoum was ruled by Garshasp.
### LARGE +
Under Shah Ramshahr, Garshasp (a Khagan) was one of the ministers of the "king of kings"; Ay-Khanoum was among the Cities of the Seven Towers, together with Tulaytullah and Saleh. After the death of Humayun and a period of unrest, Garshasp is credited with restoring peace and taking the throne as "Khagan of Khagans", then moved the capital away from Gurabad. He eventually died, his capital burned, and its survivors fled to Tulaytullah. Inscriptions in the Mausoleum of King Deshret record that he united several desert cities and met a presumably violent end.

### ULTRA +
The seven towers Ramshahr raised near the seven cities may be the Towers of Betrayal, though the wiki notes those towers might also predate Deshret's fall, so the link is unproven. The name "Ay-Khanoum" is given as Jinn language in item lore, which fits a city made for them; nothing says whether the Jinn themselves named it.

## accounts
@version: 3.0
### LARGE
Two accounts of the founding exist. The artifact lore above credits Nabu Malikata and Deshret together, building for the Jinn. The folk-style book The Shepherd and the Magic Bottle says Deshret, curious and wanting help to conquer the world, trapped Jinn in magic bottles, built Ay-Khanoum in their memory, and was given his mausoleum by them in return; it omits Nabu Malikata entirely. The wiki treats the first as better supported, since it matches the Jinni Liloupar, who served both rulers. Nobody in canon settles it.

## status
### LITE
Only ruins remain. The towers built on the sands may have long collapsed, and the Altar of Mirages in the Hypostyle Desert may mark where the city's remains lie. It is not a place the player can visit as a city.
### STANDARD +
When Babel first meets Liloupar in The Temple Where Sand Flows Like Tears, he calls her the "eternal prisoner of Ay-Khanoum", a reference to the city she served. Nothing after 3.x changes what is known of it.

## entry: Garshasp and the Khagan Wars
@keys: Garshasp, Khagan of Khagans, Cities of the Seven Towers
@keys2: Ay-Khanoum, Gurabad, Tulaytullah, Ramshahr
@version: 3.0
### LARGE
Garshasp ruled Ay-Khanoum as Khagan under Shah Ramshahr, rose to become Khagan of Khagans after the chaos that followed Humayun's death, and broke with earlier rulers by leaving Gurabad as capital. He died in unknown circumstances and his capital burned, sending refugees to Tulaytullah. Details come only from obelisk inscriptions in the Mausoleum of King Deshret.
