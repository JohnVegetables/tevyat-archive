## summary
### LITE
The Entrance to Stormterror's Lair is a set of ruins in Decarabian's architectural style in Brightcrown Canyon. It once led straight into the capital of Decarabian, the old God of Storms, and is now the way into the area known as Stormterror's Lair.
### STANDARD +
It is a point of interest, not a settlement. The old capital behind it was later abandoned and, after the dragon Dvalin took refuge there, became known by the name Mondstadt's people gave him.

## geography
### STANDARD
The ruins lie in Brightcrown Canyon, within the Brightcrown Mountains of Mondstadt, at the approach to the old capital of Decarabian's Mondstadt. They are built in the Decarabian style and open directly into that city. The only road shown in-game leads out of the ruined city to a boar habitat to the south.
### LARGE +
Whether further stretches of the road existed and were lost is not stated by canon.

## history
### STANDARD
According to the Teyvat Travel Guide, soldiers were once stationed along the road in Brightcrown Canyon that led into the capital. After Decarabian was killed in an uprising and Barbatos took his place, the people moved their capital to an island in Cider Lake, where Mondstadt stands today, leaving the old city empty.
### LARGE +
Centuries later the Dragon of the East, Dvalin, wounded in his fight with the corrupted dragon Durin, fell into a long sleep in the ruined capital. Mondstadt had forgotten him by the time he woke, and called him Stormterror, which gave the area its present name.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Weathered stone gateworks of an extinct storm-god's kingdom, silent apart from wind in the canyon, with the vast and half-forgotten city beyond.

### ULTRA +
The in-game expedition description for the wider lair calls it a ruined ancient city of magical mechanisms and painful memories, and says daring adventurers still seek the treasures hidden there despite the danger.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Ruin guards and hilichurls are among the enemies in the surrounding Stormterror's Lair area. The area is protected by a storm barrier until the quest Hurdle is completed.

## status
### STANDARD
As of 7.1 the ruins remain abandoned. No Nod-Krai or Snezhnaya content changes them.
