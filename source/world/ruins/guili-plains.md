## summary
### LITE
The Guili Plains are a stretch of ruins and open country in the Qiongji Estuary area of Liyue. This was the centre of the Guili Assembly, an ancient realm ruled jointly by Guizhong, the God of Dust, and Morax, who is now Rex Lapis and lives incognito as Zhongli. About 3,700 years ago, during the Archon War, a great battle laid it waste and Guizhong died. Morax then led the survivors south of Mt. Tianheng to found Liyue Harbor. The plains are now a deserted wilderness of broken stone, frequented by hilichurls, Treasure Hoarders and the occasional scholar.
### STANDARD +
The name comes from the two gods: the "Gui" of Guizhong and a "Li" from the name Morax went by at the time. It is read as "the Plains of Returning and Departing." The ruins still hold stone tablets and jade plates inscribed in the Assembly's era, including Guizhong's Four Commandments.

## geography
### STANDARD
The plains are a subarea of the Qiongji Estuary, the coastal country north of Mt. Tianheng and Liyue Harbor. The main road between Mondstadt and Liyue Harbor runs past them. To the north lie Wangshu Inn and the Dihua Marsh. The Conch Lodge country of Yaoguang Shoal is on the nearby coast, and Mingyun Village is further east. Violetgrass grows here.
### LARGE +
At its height, the Guili Assembly reached far beyond the plains. Stone Tablet Compilations says its farming towns stretched so far that one could walk to Stone Gate without leaving the paths between the fields. The plains themselves are its old centre. During the limited-time Moonlight Merriment event (2.1), Keqing quotes an old text placing the Assembly "60 miles to the northwest", seemingly measured from Liyue Harbor.

## places
### STANDARD
- **Ruins of Guili**: the scattered ruins of the old settlement, with a viewpoint looking over them. Five inscribed stone tablets (north, east, middle-south, southwest and west) and two more ruin inscriptions record the Assembly's founding and fall. Four jade plates in the northern, eastern, southern and western ruins each explain one of Guizhong's Four Commandments.
- **Domain of Forsaken Ruins**: a one-time domain hidden in the area. Its description calls it a cloud-wrapped paradise that one finds only by luck, serene outside and dangerous within.
### LARGE +
- **Karma-Heavy Cavern**: a cavern in the plains that draws monsters corrupted by sinister energy. It is the quest domain of Xiao's story quest (1.3).
- A ruin guarded by a Ruin Guard holds a "wishing bonfire" bait, which leads into the world quest Share Not Your Treasures (see quests).
- A wooden house by the sea, home of Minamoto Kotomi, an elderly mother living alone (see people).

## history
### STANDARD
Stone Tablet Compilations: Vol. I, compiled by the historian Zichang from ancient tablets, tells the story. Guizhong, the Lord of Dust, was Rex Lapis' ally and built the Guizhong Ballista on Mt. Tianheng. She brought her people north of the mountain and taught them farming, and farming towns spread across the land. She told Rex Lapis that her people had left their home yet felt as though they had returned home. So she named the area the Plains of the Returning and Departing, and he praised her for it. In the Archon War the adepti fought to defend the plains, but the war ravaged them and Guizhong was killed. Rex Lapis then led the people south of Mt. Tianheng for good, and the plains became a wilderness.
### LARGE +
The tablets in the ruins tell it differently. In their version the people gathered to farm, then Guizhong descended and gave them four commandments. Afterwards a Geo god descended, brought the people of Liyue to this place and shepherded them together with her. One fragment describes the final battle on the plains, "where black dust choked the heavens and a thousand rocks splintered." Madame Ping's tale of the Stove God, told during a limited-time event, adds a few details. People from the city had built a dwelling on the plains and named it the Guili Assembly. A flood then took it, and the survivors went south to Liyue Harbor. The journey was short, but a terrible storm lasted for a dozen days, and the adepti stayed with the people throughout.
### ULTRA +
The weapon text of Memory of Dust records how Guizhong died. Among the Glaze Lilies she told Morax that their journey together had ended, and asked him to forget the puzzle-dumbbell she had once given him as a pledge and challenge. Then her form dissolved into dust. Over the years the wild Glaze Lilies dwindled until none were left. The viewpoint text alludes to this when it remembers a thriving city "where a certain flower bloomed in abundance."

## people
### LARGE
Nobody lives in the ruins proper. The scholar Soraya, a researcher from the Sumeru Akademiya, studies them and venerates Guizhong's memory. Bao'er poses here as an archaeologist digging on the battlefield where, she says, the Geo Lord won a decisive victory. In fact she is a Treasure Hoarder. Millelith soldiers, among them Fugui and Zhenxiang, guard the Mondstadt–Liyue Harbor road nearby. Fugui sits in the shade drinking tea and offering free face readings to passers-by.
### ULTRA +
Minamoto Kotomi, whose son wrote of the Sakura Blooms of "your homeland" (Inazuma), lives alone in a wooden house by the sea in the plains. Her son's last letter, recovered in Beidou's hangout, explains that he buried a check for all his savings so it would not be stolen from her house. Wiki listings of Guili Plains NPCs also include several people around the coast and the Sea of Clouds edge, such as Granny Chu and Miss Bu at the Conch Lodge and Mr. Zhu of the lonely Emerald Maple Inn. In the game's area divisions some of these are counted under neighbouring areas.

## culture
### STANDARD
Guizhong's Four Commandments are the plains' lasting legacy: "Teach with wisdom, be bound by virtue, fortify the bones, unite in ambition." Their convergence was to be called the Guili Assembly. Soraya reads them as Guizhong's wish for her people to be wise, moral, strong and united in protecting their home.
### LARGE +
The jade plates gloss each commandment. Unity of ambition means being steadfast and immovable. Wisdom is like water, nourishing everyone and reflecting the truth. Strengthened bones keep a body supple when the time comes. Virtue grows like a tree that flourishes even in shade. Someone who refused to leave the ruined land hid a treasure at the point where the four ruins "converge," so that later generations hunting for it would learn the commandments.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Wind over grass and broken masonry, quiet except for hilichurl camps and the scrape of Treasure Hoarders' shovels. The ruins feel like a graveyard of a happier age. Most travellers only pass through on the road between Wangshu Inn and Liyue Harbor. Liyue folk still tell competing stories about what happened here.

## dangers
### LARGE
Hilichurl camps (including Mitachurls and Samachurls), slimes, Treasure Hoarders who dig in the ruins and set traps for treasure-seekers, Cryo Abyss Mages and dormant Ruin Guards around the old structures.
### ULTRA +
Ruin Guards stand watch over some of the old chambers; Treasure Lost, Treasure Found ends with a fight against three of them at the final ruin. (Separately, Guizhong is said to have collected Khaenri'ahn machines for study, though canon does not link those to these guards.) In Xiao's story quest, hilichurls tainted by his karmic debt gathered in the Karma-Heavy Cavern. In 5.7, Rifthounds were found here collecting god remains.

## travel
### LARGE
The plains sit beside the main road from Mondstadt to Liyue Harbor, south of Wangshu Inn and north of the harbour, so they are easy to reach on foot. The Millelith post on the road makes the highway itself relatively safe; the ruins away from it are not.

## quests
### LARGE
- Treasure Lost, Treasure Found (1.0 world quest): Soraya and the Traveler copy the ruin inscriptions, reconstruct the Assembly's story and follow the Four Commandments to a hidden treasure. Soraya concludes that the commandments are the real treasure.
- The Lost Relic / A Little Raid (commissions): Bao'er's "lost relic" errands, which expose her as a Treasure Hoarder.
- Share Not Your Treasures (1.0 world quest): a "wishing bonfire" turns out to be a Treasure Hoarder trap, and their notes point to loot buried on Cuijue Slope.
### ULTRA +
- Butterfly's Dream (Xiao, 1.3): the Traveler tracks corrupted hilichurls to the Karma-Heavy Cavern in the plains and meets Xiao there.
- Guili Plains Treasure Hunt (Beidou hangout, 2.3): Beidou and the Traveler follow a poem about "ruins long forsaken" to a sailor's hidden savings, and deliver his last wishes to his mother in the plains.
- Sinister Undercurrent and Special Training (Skirk, 5.7): after meeting the Traveler at Wanmin Restaurant, Skirk has them come to the plains, investigates there with them and then takes them through an Abyssal rift to train.
- Smaller content: the commission A Novel Idea (Chang the Ninth seeks inspiration here) and the Gliding Challenge event quest Fly High.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the Guili Plains remain uninhabited ruins under Liyue's protection. Nothing of the Assembly survives except stones, inscriptions and memory.
### STANDARD +
Zhongli still lives in Liyue Harbor, and Guoba, the diminished Stove God Marchosius, lives with Xiangling. In 5.7, Skirk found that god remains sealed beneath the plains had been dug up and taken; Rifthounds were found there collecting remains. No later version reports further change here.

## entry: Guizhong's Four Commandments
@keys: Four Commandments, Guizhong's Four Commandments, Teach with wisdom, unite in ambition
@version: 1.0
### STANDARD
Guizhong's Four Commandments survive on the stones of the Guili Plains: teach with wisdom, be bound by virtue, fortify the bones, unite in ambition. Their convergence "shall be known as the Guili Assembly." A jade plate in each of four ruins (north, east, south, west) explains one commandment. Someone who stayed behind after the Assembly fell hid a treasure where the four meet, so that treasure-seekers would carry the words forward. Soraya and the Traveler recovered both in Treasure Lost, Treasure Found.

## entry: The Fall of the Guili Assembly
@keys: fall of the Guili Assembly, Guili Assembly, Plains of Returning and Departing
@keys2: battle, flood, Archon War, Guizhong, destroyed
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
About 3,700 years ago, in the Archon War, a battle on the Guili Plains destroyed the Assembly. The tablets recall black dust choking the heavens and a thousand rocks splintering. The adepti could not hold back the war, and Guizhong was killed. A flood followed, and Morax, Marchosius and the adepti led the survivors south of Mt. Tianheng through more than ten days of storm to found Liyue Harbor. Morax raised the Millelith there. The enemy in that battle is never named.
### LARGE +
Folk explanations differ: a war between gods, a war between humans or a natural catastrophe. Soraya reasoned that, with two gods watching over it, whatever destroyed the Assembly must have been beyond even the gods' power to withstand. Bao'er's patter calls the plains the site of the Geo Lord's decisive victory, but she is a Treasure Hoarder posing as an archaeologist.

## entry: Soraya, Scholar of Guili
@keys: Soraya
### STANDARD
Soraya is a Sumeru Akademiya researcher who studies Liyue's history, especially Guizhong. She leaves the fieldwork in the Guili ruins to the Traveler and admits she tires after a few seconds of running. She despises Treasure Hoarders for selling relics. After the Four Commandments were recovered, she said that if she met Guizhong she would recite them perfectly, to show that the people had not forgotten.

## entry: Rifthounds and God Remains at Guili
@keys: god remains, Rifthounds, Sinister Undercurrent
@keys2: Guili Plains, Guili, Skirk
@version: 5.7
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
In Skirk's story quest (5.7), the Traveler, whose soul had been partly stolen, met Skirk at Wanmin Restaurant and was told to find her on the Guili Plains. There Rifthounds were gathering god remains, the buried, curse-laden remnants of gods fallen thousands of years ago. Skirk found that some of the remains sealed in the plains had been dug up and removed. She linked this to a power-siphoning entity of at least god-level strength, which was also draining god remains in Sumeru's desert and Stella Fortuna in the sky. She opened an Abyssal rift there and took the Traveler to her own domain to train them.

## entry: Beidou's Guili Plains Treasure Hunt
@keys: Guili Plains Treasure Hunt, Sakura Blooms Upon Guili Fell, Minamoto Kotomi, Golden Alligator
@version: 2.3
### LARGE
In one branch of Beidou's hangout (2.3), Beidou bought a hard-to-read treasure map, and she and the Traveler searched the Guili Plains. They drove off digging Treasure Hoarders and recruited the Golden Alligator's gang. Then they decoded a poem about gazing at "ruins long forsaken" beneath a red-leaved tree at dusk. The treasure was a check for a sailor's savings, buried so it would be safe for his mother, Minamoto Kotomi, who lives alone by the sea in the plains; the Traveler calls it "the last will of a faithful son." In the "Sakura Blooms Upon Guili Fell" ending, Beidou sends the Traveler to take her the check and a Sakura Bloom from her homeland.

## entry: Karma-Heavy Cavern
@keys: Karma-Heavy Cavern, Starsnatcher
@keys2: Xiao, Guili, hilichurls
@version: 1.3
### LARGE
In Xiao's story quest Butterfly's Dream (1.3), hilichurls tainted by Xiao's karmic debt troubled the area around Wangshu Inn. A fraud named Starsnatcher failed to drive them off. ## entry: Guili in Later Memory
@keys: Jinggui, Zichang, Stone Tablet Compilations
@keys2: Guili Plains, Guili
### ULTRA
Zichang, the historian who compiled Stone Tablet Compilations, preserved the Guili story for later readers. Jinggui, a Liyue warrioress now preserved among the "Uplifted" in the Temple of Space (seen in 6.5), recalls once driving bandits out of the Guili Plains alongside the swordsman Guhua. In a letter to the Traveler after her 6.3 debut, Zibai writes that she went to see where Liyue Harbor's people came from after the floods and found that the old traces had faded from sight.
