## summary
### LITE
The Knights of Favonius Ruins are the remains of an ancient battlefield beneath Dornman Port, where the Knights of Favonius fought the Abyss and where their dead lie buried. They are reached from the base of the port's old lighthouse. The ruins first appear in version 6.5.
### STANDARD +
In the world quest Winds Beneath the Tower of Silence, the Traveler traces a flashing alarm and rumoured ghosts to this place and finds only a hidden graveyard, old mechanisms and an Eye of the Storm.

## geography
### STANDARD
The ruins sit in three underground levels below the old lighthouse of Dornman Port. Alarm mechanisms flash and doors are wired to them; strong air currents run through the passages. There are two entrances, the lighthouse's main door and a lesser-known one used by locals.
### LARGE +
Moving crates to block the wind opens gates, a main control switch causes a floor collapse down to a cemetery of weathered tombstones, and a final cave opens onto where the Eye of the Storm falls. A teleport waypoint is unlocked in the ruins during the quest.

## places
### STANDARD
The cemetery holds old graves whose inscriptions are unreadable but whose plots have been tended recently; Winslow cleared the weeds and cleaned the stones in recent days.

## people
### STANDARD
Winslow lives in the old lighthouse. He was terrified of the alarm, thought of the Abyss and then of ghosts, and had pushed to move the graves out to fields beyond the port. Odolf, a local who has recently been in Snezhnaya, tried to stop him.
### LARGE +
Lennart and Matthew, drinkers at the Devil's Cut tavern, share the rumours and warn that Winslow was acting strangely. Odolf rigged the mechanism and added a lure for elemental creatures to scare Winslow into leaving the graves alone; it drew an Eye of the Storm instead of harmless Anemo slimes.

## history
### STANDARD
Five hundred years ago, during the Cataclysm, Dornman Port stood on the brink of destruction, and many knights died defending it; their graves lie beneath the city. The ruins are what remains of that fight against the Abyss.
### LARGE +
In the present, the Knights have repeatedly searched the ruins after Winslow's Abyss alarms and found nothing. Honorary Knight status of the Traveler is invoked to persuade Winslow.
### ULTRA +
After the Traveler defeats the Eye of the Storm and the alarm quiets, Odolf tells Winslow it was the cries of fallen knights and credits the Traveler as a "ghost negotiator". Afterwards he admits the whole story was his trick, and says that if Winslow learned it he might smash the tombstones.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Hydro and Electro slimes, and an Eye of the Storm that drops into the final cave. The old floors can give way.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Dusty stone corridors and flashing lights, steady cold draughts, and quiet rows of worn tombstones that someone nonetheless keeps clean.

## quests
### STANDARD
Winds Beneath the Tower of Silence is the world quest set here, which requires finishing Song of the Dragon and Freedom, and it begins at the Devil's Cut tavern in Dornman Port.

## status
### LITE
The alarm is quiet, the graves are tended and the ruins are open as of 7.1.

### STANDARD
Winslow is a nervous resident of the old lighthouse who fears Abyss and ghosts and wants the graves below moved. Odolf is a local who knows a secret route into the ruins and has been quietly protecting the graves. His ghost story is a deliberate fiction.
