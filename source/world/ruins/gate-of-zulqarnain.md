## summary
### LITE
The Gate of Zulqarnain is a gate-like ruin and cavern site in Gavireh Lajavard, part of Sumeru's Girdle of the Sands. An Iniquitous Baptist, a boss of the Abyss, is found here, and the underground Subterranean Defile hides one of the Great Songs of Khvarena sought in a world quest. The wiki ties its eight-pointed star motif to Khaenri'ah.
### STANDARD +
Nothing in canon says who built the Gate or what it was for. Its name echoes the Arabic Dhu al-Qarnayn, "two-horned".

## geography
@version: 3.6
### STANDARD
Located in the Gavireh Lajavard area of the Girdle of the Sands, a region the Adventurers' Guild commission describes as the northern desert. The Gate stands at the surface; the Subterranean Defile is the cave beneath, where the Wenut sleeps.
### LARGE +
It is one of the subareas of Gavireh Lajavard, which also holds the Temir Mountains, Tunigi Hollow and the Realm of Farakhkert, according to the archive's Girdle of the Sands entry.

## dangers
@version: 3.6
### STANDARD
The Iniquitous Baptist, a normal boss of the Abyss family. It uses a combination of Cryo, Electro, Hydro and Pyro, whose mix rotates daily, and has extra limbs from "evolution" through the Abyss's power. Notes elsewhere remark that Abyssal power is little different from elemental items in practice.
### LARGE +
The same enemy type has also appeared as the Fontainian Jakob Ingold and, as Haden, a swordsmanship teacher cursed during the cataclysm; neither is connected to this site in canon.

## quests
@version: 3.6
### STANDARD
In the world quest series Khvarena of Good and Evil, the Order of Skeptics' sage Nasejuna sends the Traveler to find the Great Songs of Khvarena. The quest As the Khvarena's Light Shows: Dukkha starts at the Gate of Zulqarnain: one Great Song is in the cavern where the petrified Setekh Wenut sleeps. Nasejuna remarks that the song has little effect even near the Wenut, and concludes it affects only things related to the Abyss.
### ULTRA +
Nasejuna turns out to have plotted with the Abyss Order and, on obtaining the last Great Song, reveals a hatred of the Pari and tries to take the songs by force with the Abyss Herald Klingsor; the Traveler defeats them and they flee. That betrayal happens at the final ruins and not at the Gate, so a character here would not yet know of it.

## status
### LITE
An open ruin and boss site, with no inhabitants beyond the Abyss creature. As of Version 7.1 nothing found changes it.

## entry: Great Song of Khvarena at the Gate
@keys: Great Song of Khvarena, Great Songs of Khvarena, Subterranean Defile
@keys2: Gate of Zulqarnain, Nasejuna, Wenut
@version: 3.6
### STANDARD
The Great Songs of Khvarena hold great power, but only the Pari can obtain the Khvarena's approval and unleash it through a ritual. Three songs are to be gathered; the last lies in ruins elsewhere. The one at the Gate lies in the cave near the Setekh Wenut and is found in the quest Dukkha.
