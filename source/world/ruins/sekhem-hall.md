## summary
### LITE
Sekhem Hall is a hall in the eastern section of the Mausoleum of King Deshret in Sumeru's Hypostyle Desert, filled with Primal Constructs that do not attack. The Place of Breath branches off it.
### STANDARD +
The name comes from the sekhem, a ritual scepter of ancient Egypt. The Scarlet Sand Slate says Al-Ahmar had no enemies, because all who gave him no homage were destroyed by servants that issue from this place and reduced to a handful of sand.

## geography
@version: 3.1
### STANDARD
A map level labelled for the eastern section of the Mausoleum. It has a waypoint, and a hallway off its right side leads, past a primal sconce, to the Place of Breath.
### LARGE +
The hall holds many Primal Constructs identical to the boss met at the end of Dual Evidence, but they remain still.

## history
@version: 3.1
### STANDARD
It is not the subject of a quest of its own. Players pass through it on the way to the Place of Breath during Dual Evidence. Its role in King Deshret's time is known only from the slate's line about servants that enforced homage.
### LARGE +
The line that enemies were reduced to a handful of sand is a grim picture of the king's protection. Whether this was literal or boasting is unresolved.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
A silent hall of motionless guardians: threatening by implication, harmless in practice. A scene here can use the contrast between the slate's boast and the dust-covered stillness.

## dangers
### STANDARD
The constructs are inert and do not attack the player.
