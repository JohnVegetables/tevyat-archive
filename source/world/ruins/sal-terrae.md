## summary
### LITE
Sal Terrae is a salt-crusted ruin on the Bishui Plain in Liyue, the last sanctuary of Havria, the gentle God of Salt, who refused to fight in the Archon War and gave her people shelter. Her own king killed her with a sword and the city turned to salt. Today a sealed domain holds her remains, a single salt flower, and the broken sword. It is the setting of the first act of Zhongli's story quest.
### STANDARD +
Since 5.7 the surface ruins also host Stygian Onslaught, a recurring ley line disturbance that the Millelith sealed off and the Traveler helps quell.

## geography
### STANDARD
A low plateau of ruined walls and sunken foundations beside the Bishui River. Roald's diary says most of the ancient city sank into the riverbed, leaving the temple's foundation. A sealed ruin lies beneath, guarded by a stele reading that salt seals the ruin within and none uninvited may enter, and opened by Geo, Electro and pressure-plate mechanisms. Local specialties include Cor Lapis, Noctilucous Jade and Violetgrass; the nearby area holds Hilichurl camps and a Stonehide Lawachurl.
### LARGE +
Windmill mechanisms and pressure plates mark puzzle spots. Inside the sealed ruin, statues made of salt line the corridors, and the last chamber ends in an open hall.

## people
### STANDARD
No residents now. Wanyan, of Yinyuan Hall, who in fact reveres Havria, and Kliment, a Fatuus archaeologist, entered the ruin with Zhongli; Treasure Hoarders had already looted much of the surface.

## culture
### STANDARD
Havria's people honoured a goddess of kindness who handed out flowers as a blessing or comfort; Liyue's Flower Ball custom is said to have come from this. Her relics were a chalice that never ran out of salt and a ruler that turned land to salt for harvest, plus the broken sword of her death.
### LARGE +
Yinyuan Hall, the trade hall for salt, descends from survivors who fled to Morax's Liyue Harbor.

## history
### STANDARD
Havria was a weak, gentle god who refused to fight in the Archon War and instead conceded land after land to other gods, finally settling in a tiny holding at Sal Terrae. The city stood for centuries until its first and last king killed her with a sword. Her body dissipated into a salt flower (the "sal flore") and her released power turned everyone nearby into salt. A few outside the blast fled to Liyue Harbor.
### LARGE +
Descendants, afraid of a curse, returned to break the sword and lay it, with two of her relics, on a pedestal in the sealed chamber before resealing the ruin. Over millennia Yinyuan Hall's story drifted until it said Morax murdered Havria out of jealousy; Wanyan's "expedition" with Zhongli was an attempt to prove this. Zhongli, who is Morax, told the truth, took the ruler and later cast Havria's relics into the sea at Guyun Stone Forest, saying he must find a better way to keep the truth of history.
## atmosphere
### LARGE
White crust on stone, the smell of salt, dry wind off the river and a silence broken by gears and the occasional echo in empty halls. Inside, salted statues of people caught in their last gestures make a sad, unsettling gallery.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Hilichurls with Pyro and Electro bows; the Stonehide Lawachurl; a Fatui Pyro Agent (Kliment) in the sealed ruin; Pyro Abyss Mages in the sealed ruin; and, during Stygian Onslaught, powerful bosses spawned by the disturbance.

## quests
### STANDARD
"Sal Flore" (Zhongli's Historia Antiqua Chapter, Act I): "Shadow of Yore", "Vortex's Wake", "A Ruin Under Lock and Key", "Farewell, Sweet Salt" and "A Record of All Things". The domain is "Of Mysteries and Salt". "Tumult Subduer" (5.7) opens Stygian Onslaught.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the sealed ruin is open, the relics are gone, and the surface hosts the recurring Stygian Onslaught disturbance. No new Sal Terrae canon appears in 6.x or 7.0-7.1.

## entry: Havria, God of Salt
@keys: Havria, God of Salt, sal flore
@keys2: Sal Terrae, Zhongli, Liyue
### STANDARD
Havria was a gentle god of the Archon War era, weak in power, who fled the fighting and made countless concessions to protect her followers. Her relics were a self-refilling salt chalice and a ruler that turned land into salt. Her kindness is the reason she could not defend herself against her own king. See her entry for her story.

## entry: Zhongli and the Sal Terrae expedition
@keys: Wanyan, Kliment, Sal Flore, Farewell Sweet Salt, Yinyuan Hall
@keys2: Sal Terrae, Zhongli
@spoiler: minor
### LARGE
Zhongli accompanies the archaeologists Wanyan and Kliment in return for a contract that each may claim one item in turn. Kliment breaks it out of greed over the chalice and Zhongli subdues him; Wanyan, exposed as a devotee of Havria hoping to blame Morax, learns that the sword before her was the murder weapon. Kliment is a Fatuus who claims to be registered with Liyue's Ministry of Civil Affairs.
### ULTRA +
Zhongli believes Havria could not have cursed those she loved, and perhaps accepted her people's plan to end her life as a mercy. He tells the Traveler afterwards that as Rex Lapis he had always written history himself, and that this incident made him want a better way to preserve it.

## entry: The salted ruin and Roald's diary
@keys: Salt Temple, salted statues, Roald's diary
@keys2: Sal Terrae, Bishui River
### LARGE
Roald's diary records the folk version: the God of Salt gave shelter to those displaced by the war; the king, who had once loved her too, killed the lone god to show that gentleness did not suit the times; the temple fell and the city crumbled to salt; the king's end is unknown. Rumours say her body, turned to salt, still holds the posture from the moment she was struck.

## entry: Stygian Onslaught at Sal Terrae
@keys: Stygian Onslaught, Tumult Subduer, ley line disturbance
@keys2: Sal Terrae, Millelith
@version: 5.7
### ULTRA
Since 5.7, a strange ley line disturbance appeared at Sal Terrae. The Adventurers' Guild reported many monsters and the Millelith sealed the area; the world quest "Tumult Subduer" (needs Adventure Rank 20 and the Prologue finished) sends the Traveler in, with Keqing and Yelan involved, and unlocks Stygian Onslaught.
