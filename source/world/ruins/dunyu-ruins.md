## summary
### LITE
The Dunyu Ruins are old ruins sunk in a dead lake in Lisha, a region of Liyue. They are hilltop terraces, stairways and a Statue of the Seven's camp, with a Geo totem that drains the lake, and one of three places where the quest item "Nameless Treasure" is hidden. Little is known of who built them.
### STANDARD +
A Treasure Hoarder's notes suggest the ruins are tied to adepti who died in an ancient war, but this is not confirmed.

## geography
### STANDARD
Ruins on terraces around a lake in Lisha. Walls, stairs, Ruins Torches and rock piles are scattered between buildings. Activating the Geo totem after lighting torches drains the lake to reveal the Geoculus, a barrier guarded by three Seelie courts, and a chest with a Nameless Treasure. The expedition description says the ruins are suspended in time inside the dead lake.
### LARGE +
Local specialty: Cor Lapis. The surrounding Lisha region also holds the Chasm entrance and Qingxu Pool, whose underground ruins hold another Nameless Treasure.

## people
### STANDARD
The astronomer Ziwei and the miner Iron Ingot are the named NPCs here. Treasure Hoarders and the Fatui roam the ruins.

## history
### STANDARD
Little is certain. The Treasure Hoarder Liuliu wrote in a Ruins Investigation Log that research suggested the ruins are linked to adepti who fought and died in an ancient war and that "treasure is hidden within the rocks, concealed beneath the waters". Liuliu also heard mournful groans at night, put down to the north wind blowing past Nantianmen.
### LARGE +
Three Nameless Treasures (from Lingju Pass, Qingxu Pool and Dunyu Ruins) fit together into a single carved object. Linlang, an antiques dealer, reads it as the story of the dragon Azhdaha: three adepti, the Mountain Shaper (a crane) and Moon Carver (a deer) among them, created a cavern realm and sealed him inside. The third adeptus's image has been deliberately ground away. This does not prove the Dunyu Ruins belonged to that era.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Still water over old stone, wind through broken arches, a camp near a statue, and the feeling of being watched that Liuliu wrote about; a place that rewards patience and a cool head.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Fatui Skirmishers (Pyroslinger Bracer, Cryogunner and Hydrogunner Legionnaires, Anemoboxer and Electrohammer Vanguards), Treasure Hoarders, slimes, and elite Ruin Guards and Fatui Pyro Agents.

## quests
### STANDARD
"Hidden in Nameless Treasures", a Hidden Exploration Objective whose items come from Lingju Pass, Qingxu Pool and these ruins.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the ruins are a minor exploration site with no developing story.

### ULTRA
Linlang tells the Traveler the three artefacts are old Liyue work and mentions that Soraya, a researcher, might be interested. She believes the full set tells how Azhdaha was lured into a cavern and sealed, and wonders who ground away the third adeptus's image, and why.
