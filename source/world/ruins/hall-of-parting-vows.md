## summary
### LITE
The Hall of Parting Vows is an underground ruin in Tequemecan Valley, Natlan, said to be where Xbalanque, the first Pyro Archon, and the Sage of the Stolen Flame swore a sacred oath. Players enter it during the world quest Revelations from the Past, seeking the token the Sage left.
### STANDARD +
Below it, glowing stairs lead down to Catastrophe's Edge, and an inscription there transports the player to the Ruins of the Sage of the Stolen Flame. Its map has two levels, an entrance hall and the place of parting vows. A viewpoint inscription in the hall is titled Mural of Sworn Pledges.

## geography
### STANDARD
Underground, with an entrance guarded by a barrier, corridors, a lava crossing and mechanisms. The ruin lies near the Children of Echoes in Tequemecan Valley.

## history
### STANDARD
Within the quest, Manqu explains from ancient tribal scrolls that the Sage believed humans could guide and lead Saurians to a better future than the age of the giant dragons. Feeling guilty for stealing phlogiston from dragonkind, the Sage hoped humans and Saurians could live as equals, and he left a token here; the scrolls say humans and Saurians each have their places.
### LARGE +
The Sage of the Stolen Flame is the human legend of the dragon prince Kukulkan, who once formed an alliance with Xbalanque (the wiki describes it as aimed at preventing the Huitzilopochtli array's activation). The oath sworn here fits that alliance, but the sources read do not spell out its terms.
### ULTRA +
The trial reflects the Sage's design: the barrier opens only when a human and a Saurian stand in their respective positions, a test of understanding between the two. The reward, a Golden Entreaty, later becomes central to the plot at the Great Volcano of Tollan; Kukulkan, speaking as Waxaklahun Ubah Kan, steered the rite for his own aims.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Abyss-corroded Rockfond Rifthounds guard the entrance, and a corroded Golden Wolflord appears in the final fight at Catastrophe's Edge.

## quests
### STANDARD
Revelations from the Past (world quest, Tequemecan Valley, chapter 'Between Pledge and Forgettance'), played with Manqu, Paimon, the Traveler and the Little One Saurian; the player has the Traveler only for part of it.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Sealed, ceremonial and slow: ancient trial machinery, murals and glowing lava, built to be opened only by a pair who cooperate.

## status
### STANDARD
Completed ruin as of 7.1; can be revisited after the quest, with the lower sites reachable by the glowing stairs.
