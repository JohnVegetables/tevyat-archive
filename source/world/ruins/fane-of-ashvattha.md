## summary
### LITE
The Fane of Ashvattha is a sacred grove in Vissudha Field, Dharma Forest, that holds the Ashvattha Tree, the first Vasara Tree and the progenitor of the Aranara. It also acts as the seal over the entrance to Old Vanarana, the older, sealed Vanarana. In the Aranyaka quest series the Traveler and Arama come here to make the Bija that will heal Rana.
### STANDARD +
The tree was weakened by Marana and could not bear the Bija. Arama took its place and planted a new Ashvattha Tree, and the seal at the Fane opened so the party could descend toward Old Vanarana.

## geography
@version: 3.0
### STANDARD
A hidden forest shrine in Vissudha Field, with the Ashvattha Tree at its center and a seal over the passage into Old Vanarana. The body of water over the entrance has to be drained in the quest before the way leads further in. The wiki notes that the area's name is not shown on the map until the quest For Fruits, Seeds, and Trees is done.
### LARGE +
Rukkhashava mushrooms grow nearby as a local specialty. In the quest the Traveler can enter the Ashvattha Tree's dream by reaching toward it, and Arama places the Tree of Dreams within Sarva.

## people
@version: 3.0
### STANDARD
The Aranara hold it sacred. Arama, a friendly Aranara who guides the Traveler, says it is her first visit. The Ashvattha Tree is the oldest of the Vasara Trees, older than any Aranara and their stories, and all Aranara descend from it.

## history
@version: 3.0
### STANDARD
Arama's account is that the first Aranara, Aramuhukunda, sprouted from a seed in a pomegranate the Ashvattha Tree produced after a great singer of the forest called the tree out of Sarva. The tree did this so that the forest's children would grow and befriend the lonely Nara, humans. Later the Aranara spread like leaves on a tree. Aramuhukunda later became a Vasara Tree in Old Vanarana to hold back Marana's avatar, and the tree there withered.
### LARGE +
After the older Vanarana was destroyed and Marana appeared, three Aranara, Araji, Aramaha and Arayama, sealed the three Ashvattha's Concourses and turned into Vasara Trees. In For Fruits, Seeds, and Trees the Traveler carries three Vasoma Fruits from those trees to the Ashvattha Tree to ask for the Bija. Marana had corroded even the Tree's dream, and the Withering was cleared from it. The tree could not bear a Bija. Arama says she will plant a new Ashvattha Tree, the tree giving her its power, and that the seal in the Fane hides Marana's avatar in Old Vanarana, which must be destroyed for the new tree to grow.
### ULTRA +
Arama comes out of the experience with new words, memories and a changed manner of speaking, but insists she is still herself. The new Ashvattha Tree grows inside the withered trunk of Aramuhukunda's Vasara Tree in Old Vanarana's Lost Nursery.

## status
### LITE
The old Ashvattha Tree in the Fane is spent, though the place remains sacred to the Aranara, and the seal over Old Vanarana was opened in the quest.
### STANDARD +
The new Ashvattha Tree is Arama in Old Vanarana, so the Fane's tree is a memory of the first. No later change is recorded as of Version 7.1.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Hushed, mossy and watched over, with the Aranara's quiet reverence. Nara who wear the Aranara's garland are treated as children of the forest while it lasts.

## entry: Vasara Trees
@keys: Vasara Tree, Vasoma Fruit, Bija, Ashvattha Tree
@keys2: Aranara, Fane of Ashvattha
@version: 3.0
### STANDARD
Vasara Trees hold dreams and memories and are usually made when an Aranara chooses to become one. They keep some consciousness, so other Aranara treat a tree as the Aranara it once was. The three Vasara Trees of Araji, Aramaha and Arayama, tended by the concourses, each bear a Vasoma Fruit once the Withering is cleared from their dreams. The Bija is obtained by offering such fruits to the Ashvattha Tree.
