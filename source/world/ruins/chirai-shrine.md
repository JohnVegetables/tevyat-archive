## summary
### LITE
Chirai Shrine is a stretch of ruined shrine buildings on Tsurumi Island in Inazuma. It is one of the three places on the fog-bound island where a Perch must receive offerings in the world quest A Particularly Particular Author, and its ruins hide a door into the Tsurumi Underground Ruins.
### STANDARD +
Hilichurls, Samachurls and ruin machines now roam the stones. The island once belonged to the Thunderbird Civilization, whose people lived under the Thunderbird Kanna Kapatcir, and a Perch on a small islet north-east of the ruins is a relic of their rites.

## geography
### STANDARD
Ruins and hills on Tsurumi Island, with a Perch on a small island to the north-east. Features include Electro Cubes, Electro Current puzzles and Phase Gates, and Fluorescent Fungus grows locally. A door under the ruins leads down to the basement and on into the Tsurumi Underground Ruins.
### LARGE +
The ruins contain Electro mechanisms: Electro Cubes, an Electro Current puzzle and a Phase Gate, which the Perch feather route passes through.

## history
### STANDARD
Chirai Shrine sits on land of the Thunderbird Civilization, an ancient people who worshipped the Thunderbird Kanna Kapatcir. Canon does not describe the shrine itself. Its Perch belongs to the offering ceremony that the island's time loop still replays.
### LARGE +
The Thunderbird people were destroyed after the boy Ruu was sacrificed to Kapatcir, who cursed the island to repeat its last days. In the Through the Mists series, Ruu and the Traveler lift that curse, and the fog is permanently dispelled after The Sun-Wheel and Mt. Kanna. Under the ruins lies an even older layer: the Tsurumi Underground Ruins hold remains of an earlier civilization as well as the Thunderbird people's.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Hilichurls with wooden shields, Electro Hilichurl Shooters, Hydro and Electro Samachurls, Electro Whopperflowers, and elite Blazing Axe Mitachurls, Ruin Cruisers and Ruin Defenders. The Hydro Samachurl group and the Ruin Scouts change positions over a three-day cycle.
### ULTRA +
The cycle runs as follows. Day one: the hilichurls stand at the outskirts of the ruins and the scouts are dormant near the tree. Day two: the hilichurls are near the scouts' earlier spot and the scouts have moved further from the tree. Day three: the hilichurls are up on the large ruins while the scouts are active and run in a triangular formation.

## travel
### STANDARD
The island's fog blocks exploration until the Through the Mists quest series is under way; a gate in the first quest lifts part of it. The Chirai Perch is reached by following feathers through a Phase Gate and across the ruins.

## quests
### STANDARD
A Particularly Particular Author (2.2): the Traveler makes offerings at the Perch here, one of three, as commissioned by the island's locals who are really illusions of the past. Gameplay note: the feather route involves defeating two Electro Whopperflowers and Thundercraven Rifthound Whelps.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the shrine is ruined and populated only by monsters; the island's curse and fog were lifted in Version 2.2 and no later content changes the place.

## entry: Perches of Tsurumi
@keys: Perch, Perches, Tsurumi Perch
@keys2: Tsurumi, Ruu, Thunderbird
@version: 2.2
### STANDARD
The Perches are offering sites used in the Thunderbird Civilization's ceremony. In A Particularly Particular Author Ruu makes a clumsy offering to one and the Traveler completes three: at Chirai Shrine, Shirikoro Peak and the Autake Plains. In later quest steps the Traveler destroys them to stop the ceremony, a spoiler for The Sea of Fog and the Rite of the Trees.
