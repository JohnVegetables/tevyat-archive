## summary
### LITE
The Mural Room is a hidden chamber on Dragonspine that holds frescoes painted by the Princess of Sal Vindagnyr. A claymore, the Snow-Tombed Starsilver, stands stuck in a platform at its centre. It preserves the kingdom's image from before the snow and the outlander Imunlaukr's farewell.
### STANDARD +
The room's name is unofficial. It opens only after the hidden exploration objective Dragonspine's Glacial Secret is completed.

## geography
### STANDARD
A cave southwest of Dragonspine's Statue of The Seven, behind a sealed door near the Entombed City - Ancient Palace. The Starsilver is stuck in the platform in the middle of the room, with the frescoes around it.
### LARGE +
The door opens after Dragonspine's Glacial Secret, a follow-up to the objective Dragonspine's Last Trio. It requires activating numbered Ancient Carvings across the mountain, found for example south of the statue, atop Starglow Cavern, south of Wyrmrest Valley, before the Peak of Vindagnyr domain, under the Ancient Rime in the southwestern ruins, and in a cavern below the Cryo Hypostasis arena. One carving is inside the secret room.

## places
### STANDARD
Two distinct frescoes survive; a third is a copy. The first is divided in two. Its right half shows a green mountain (Vindagnyr), an angel pointing skyward and a floating island above, with a Latin line roughly meaning "Listen and be silent, if you wish to learn". Its left half shows one large figure with a shining symbol over its hand, facing two crowned figures and three small people, beside the line "Fidelis angeli iuvant" (the angels help the faithful). The second fresco shows three green mountains amid wind, the tallest topped by a structure beneath a circle with a golden centre.
### LARGE +
The wiki reads the floating island as Celestia and the crowned figures as Sal Vindagnyr's priest-kings receiving something from an angel, the symbol combining upright and inverted triquetras. In the second fresco's broken top-right corner an inverted triquetra can still be seen.

## history
### STANDARD
The princess had the gift of foresight and painted her visions. Her father Varuch wanted the third fresco to show the thaw of the snow, but she had been in winter so long that she could not remember what colours to use for blue sky and green grass. She also promised Imunlaukr that a fourth fresco would be his likeness. She never finished the third and never painted the fourth.
### LARGE +
Before he left to seek a way to save the kingdom, she gave Imunlaukr a Starsilver greatsword, the Snow-Tombed Starsilver, and said she would wait for him, but the storm drowned out her words. When he returned and found everyone dead he was disillusioned with Celestia, vowed to entertain the heavens with a "song of iron and blood", and left the sword in this room as he went in search of wars to fight. The princess's box laments that she could no longer bear the cold.
### ULTRA +
Imunlaukr's later life is unknown, but his descendants, the Imunlaukr Clan, became one of Mondstadt's prominent clans. The wiki also notes that a sword stands in the ground on the small island where the princess died, and that its owner is unclear.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
A quiet, enclosed cave with faded green hills on its walls and a sword standing at the centre: a mourning room more than a gallery.

## status
### LITE
Unchanged as of 7.1; open to anyone who completes the objective.

The Starsilver greatsword the Princess of Sal Vindagnyr gave to Imunlaukr; its in-game description says it was kept between frescoes and can cleave ice and snow. Imunlaukr left it in the Mural Room.
