## summary
### LITE
The Place of Breath is a small chamber branching off Sekhem Hall in the Mausoleum of King Deshret, in Sumeru's Hypostyle Desert. Solving its puzzles during Dual Evidence grants Clearance Level 3 of the Scarlet Sand Slate.
### STANDARD +
The slate says that those who ascend to Aaru will no longer sigh, their words will come out all joy, and their ears will be filled with Deshret's great deeds and glad bird-like singing. It names the sophophilic as those who pass its trial and win ever-lasting bliss.

## geography
@version: 3.1
### STANDARD
Enter Sekhem Hall, preferably by its waypoint, take the first hallway on the right and turn left where a primal sconce stands; a primal ember shows the way. The wiki places the chamber on Sekhem Hall's east side while another page says west, so the direction is not settled.
### LARGE +
Clearance Level 3 allows the Traveler to use Obscuring Cells and Verity Cells found elsewhere in the desert.

## history
@version: 3.1
### STANDARD
One of the three chambers the slate points to after the party enters Duat Hall. Clearing it lights one of the three sigils; the slate's final text says those who finish it will gain everlasting bliss.
### LARGE +
Its ancient purpose is not described; the slate's wording is poetic. The name pairs with the other two trial places, which are named for Rebirth and Swallowing.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
A quiet side chamber, contrasting with the hall's grandeur; the slate speaks of sighs and birdsong.

## quests
@version: 3.1
### STANDARD
Dual Evidence (Old Notes and New Friends, part 1).
### ULTRA +
Along with the Place of Rebirth and the Place of Swallowing, it forms the three trials the quest requires before the Great Hall of Truths appears.
