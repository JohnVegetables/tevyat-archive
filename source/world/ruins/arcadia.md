## summary
### LITE
Arcadia, also called Great Arcadia, was a civilization in Mondstadt's vast oak forests, between what are now Windrest Peak, Windrise and Windwail Highland. A part of Teyvat's unified civilization, it was a holy city of plenty, favoured by heaven, that worshipped Istaroth and Asmoday and later the Three Moons. It was entombed in endless winter when the War of Funerary Flame came.
### STANDARD +
Its survivors fled south and found shelter under Decarabian.

## geography
### STANDARD
Arcadia was made of several city-states; a holy city was the capital, and one of the city-states, Elysium, was built north of it at Windrest Peak. Vast oakwoods that could weather storms and harsh winters surrounded it.
### LARGE +
The Temple of Space was constructed near Elysium and the Thousand Winds Temple was dedicated to Istaroth.

## government
### STANDARD
A high priest led the city, authorised by a divine envoy who gave them the Golden Bough of Nemorensis. The Gunnhildr Clan controlled Elysium and are described as scions of the hallowed line.

## religion
### STANDARD
The Arcadians worshipped Istaroth, Master of Time, and Asmoday, Master of Space, building the Thousand Winds Temple for the first and the Temple of Space for the second; the two temples were once connected. Divine envoys did not answer people's questions, so the Arcadians turned to the Three Moons, gathering golden boughs from oak groves as moon offerings. They especially revered Canon, original goddess of the Frost Moon, as Prusmene.
### LARGE +
The Golden Bough of Nemorensis was said to restore the city's glory. The wiki links the golden boughs to Moonlit Offering relics; the Frost Moon's worship in Arcadia is referred to in the quest The Unmanned Vessel (II).

## history
### STANDARD
Arcadia was called the eternal holy city, a land without sorrow where hunger was unknown. Prosperity kindled questioning, which stirred the gods' wrath. A disaster lifted the Temple of Space into the heavens, leaving Istaroth's temple behind on the ground. Then the War of Funerary Flame ended Eternity; Arcadia was entombed beneath an endless winter. The Knight of the Golden Bough, Gunnhildr, sought the Golden Bough in the frozen north to save it, found it withered, and led surviving Arcadians south to shelter under Decarabian.
### LARGE +
The knight crossed the wastes to a grove that had survived the calamity, with a white tree no priest or envoy tended. The Jack Frost King, a self-proclaimed fae king bound by an old command, tested visitors with riddles; the knight defeated him in a duel, but the Bough had long withered. The Jack Frost King never grasped the loss and kept guarding the tree. The wiki links the withering to the spread of the Holy Soil of Primordial Ice.
### ULTRA +
Asmoday's gaze fell on the lifted Temple of Space only after its destruction; she made it a watchtower, 'Segment-Domain Tau', which preserves records of fallen civilizations including Arcadia. Other preserved civilizations named alongside it are Hyperborea, Lang-Gan, Delphi Pytho, Ochkanatlan and Remuria.

## culture
### STANDARD
Oak groves were sacred, and golden boughs were gathered for moon offerings. The city saw itself as eternal, and its holy status rested on divine envoys that said nothing when people asked questions.

## people
### STANDARD
The Gunnhildr Clan, scions of Arcadia's hallowed line, ruled Elysium; the Knight of the Golden Bough, Gunnhildr, led its survivors away.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Holy oak groves and the stillness of a snow-buried ruin; the sense of a once-lavish holy city whose promise of eternity failed.

## quests
### STANDARD
The world quest Secret Pirate Treasure has Kaeya send the Traveler into what he calls the 'Lost Arcadian Ruins' to lure Treasure Hoarders; it was a ruse, not a real treasure hunt. Arcadia is also mentioned by Mary-Ann in Rowboat's Wake.

## status
### LITE
Long lost as a civilization; ruins and records only as of 7.1.

### STANDARD
Elysium was an Arcadian city-state north of the holy city, at Windrest Peak, controlled by the Gunnhildr Clan, and hosted the Temple of Space. It is named in the text of the domain Thorny Crown of the Mountain Wind.

## entry: Knight of the Golden Bough
@keys: Knight of the Golden Bough, Golden Bough of Nemorensis, Jack Frost King
@keys2: Arcadia, Gunnhildr
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Gunnhildr, the Knight of the Golden Bough, journeyed across the frozen wastes in search of the legendary Golden Bough to restore Arcadia. The Jack Frost King, guarding a grove that had survived, tested the knight with riddles and trials until a duel decided it. The Bough had withered; the knight gave up on Arcadia, gathered the survivors and led them south to the high tower where a wicked dragon had been cast down, seeking refuge under Decarabian.
