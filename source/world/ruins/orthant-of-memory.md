## summary
### LITE
The Orthant of Memory is one of four Seals of Chymical Marriage in the Morte Region of Fontaine, set by the Narzissenkreuz Ordo. Its riddle slate announces "the Orthant of Memory, the Hall of Memory, the Room of Memory" and the answer to its riddle is a shadow. The Memory piece it once held, one of four substances for forging the Holy Blade of Narzissenkreuz, was already gone when the Traveler arrived; the quest Muse's Mother covers it.
### STANDARD +
Added in 4.2 with the chain In the Wake of Narcissus. The Orthants were meant to be joined, and Caterpillar explains that the "birth of reason" requires all four.

## geography
### STANDARD
Entered through underwater Mysterious Ruins north-west of the Morte Region's Statue of the Seven. A bonfire still burns near the door, and Rifthounds stand near the entrance.
### LARGE +
When the Orthant's puzzle is solved the area floods, which opens areas that could not be reached before and solves others that depended on the water.

## places
### LARGE
The entrance and the riddle slate; the door guarded by the riddle; the inner altar chamber where the Memory piece lay; a mechanism whose top is empty when inspected.

## people
### STANDARD
No residents. The Narzissenkreuz Ordo's remnants, above all Jakob Ingold, are the last to have used it; Caterpillar and Ann search it with the Traveler.

## culture
### LARGE
The slate quotes a verse of "Echoes Hallowed and Humble", Movement 9, Stanza 37: if water loses its incisiveness it cannot become water again, a tree away from soil cannot root, and however hard stone strikes metal, humans cannot live without water or soil.

## history
### LARGE
Canon does not say when the seal was built. The Ordo's four substances (Memory, Wish, Soul and Persona) were "reproduced in the material world" from Fontainian esoteric philosophy and the technology of the underground kingdom, and used in the ritual for forging the Holy Blade. In the Search in the Algae Sea version of the quest, Caterpillar says Jakob had been here first and had solved the riddle.
### ULTRA +
The Ordo's own metaphors explain the purpose: the temple is a microcosm of the universe, its members stand in for gods, and the holy oil is tempered will. The Holy Blade itself stands for reason, which cuts everything open and can be born only of complete human will. Later, in the Tower of Gestalt, Jakob claims to have reversed the Seal of Chymical Marriage, calling it an ancient ritual that sealed off the source of life.

## quests
### STANDARD
Muse's Mother: find the seal, study the riddle slate, solve the Orthant puzzle and obtain the Memory piece. The riddle ("Born in an instant... often contradicting, your company in eternal rest, never separated... fire cannot burn it") is answered "shadows". There are two versions depending on whether Search in the Algae Sea was started first.
### LARGE +
Caterpillar notes the ancient Fontainians and the Ordo both loved riddles; the Traveler can offer silly answers (kidney stones, Oceanid tears) before settling on shadows.

## status
### LITE
Opened and emptied; the Memory piece is gone. Nothing newer than 4.2 is recorded.

## dangers
### LARGE
Rifthounds near the door and puzzle machinery.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Dank stone, lingering firelight and the sense of a place already ransacked.

## entry: The Four Orthants

@version: 4.2
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Four hidden seals in Fontaine: the Orthants of Memory, Wishes, Souls and Persona, each giving a "piece" (Memory, Wish, Soul, Persona) used to forge the Holy Blade of Narzissenkreuz ("reason"). The Traveler finds that Jakob has beaten them to three of them. In the Tower of Gestalt Jakob says only the five most important nodes in Fontaine survive and that he has reversed the Seal. The Collegium Phonascorum's notes in the Sea of Bygone Eras list the same four names as the Quadrant of Qualities, an echo canon does not explain.
