## summary
### LITE
Araumi is a promontory at the northern tip of Narukami Island in Inazuma, cut off from the rest of the island by Mt. Yougou to its south. Beneath it lie the ruins of an ancient city that runs down under the earth and the sea and was abandoned after the Cataclysm. Its sealed passages hide Electro puzzles, Ruin Sentinels and the sunken mansion of the tengu Reizenbou.
### STANDARD +
The upper ground holds Thunder Sakura roots and Electro puzzles; the way underground opens only through the Memento Lens, which the Traveler receives in the world quest Sacrificial Offering. Much of Araumi is underwater, and the flooded lower levels lead to the Perpetual Mechanical Array, a giant Ruin Sentinel automaton, and to the domain Empty Boat of a Thousand Gates.

## geography
### STANDARD
A rocky northern headland of Narukami Island with a Teleport Waypoint, a Waverider Waypoint, Sakura Bloom trees and a small island group to its north-east. The ancient city is not a ruin on the surface but a stack of underground levels: wells and stairwells descend through drowned halls and passages where cube-shaped mechanisms control the water level, down to a large cavern with a wrecked ship.
### LARGE +
Three levels are described. Level 1 begins in a well and has Seelie courts and cube devices that drain water. Level 2 has three rooms, one with a Thunder Sakura Bough, one behind a Thunder Barrier and one with a pair of Ruin Guards. Level 3 opens on a huge cavern with the wreckage of a ship, patrolled by Ruin Scouts and Defenders, and leads on to the boss chamber.

## places
### STANDARD
Notable spots: the Earth Kitsune Statue cave on the western shore; the well with three kitsune statues that opens the way down; the tower ruins at the surface; the cavern of the shipwreck; the arena of the Perpetual Mechanical Array; and the Empty Boat of a Thousand Gates, a tengu annex dwelling now sunk beneath the sea.
### LARGE +
The Empty Boat of a Thousand Gates is a one-time domain; its description calls it the dwelling of a tengu warrior incarnate who forsook the world. English text mentions the "Yougou Three", but the Chinese original points to a single person, which the wiki reads as Reizenbou (see the entry on the Empty Boat).

## people
### STANDARD
Nobody lives in Araumi now. Echoes of past inhabitants are heard only through Earth Kitsune Statues and the Memento Lens. The one living figure met underground is Acting Instructor Mifune Satoshi, an Iwakura Art practitioner hiding from the Shogunate, who fights as a Kairagi.
### LARGE +
Satoshi appears as a Kairagi: Dancing Thunder guarding a chest and asks whether the Shogunate's lackeys have followed him even here. He is a minor character with no known history beyond what the wiki infers (a dissident of the Iwakura clan).
### ULTRA +
After being defeated Satoshi says the fight was like whistling in the wind. The wiki reasons that he belonged to the Iwakura clan around the time Iwakura Mitsunari lost his Vision under the Vision Hunt Decree and joined the Shogunate; many clan members regarded that as a betrayal and either went rogue or fought the Shogunate. How Satoshi got into Araumi is unknown, since the Traveler can enter only with the Memento Lens.

## history
### STANDARD
The city extended underground at the north of Narukami Island and is probably from the Archon War era, judging by its architecture. It stayed active for centuries until the Cataclysm, when its inhabitants fled; the Kitsune Saiguu is said to have led people through its underground passages to safety. The survivors never came back, and Araumi was left empty.
### LARGE +
Sumida, the author in A Particularly Particular Author, recalls the legend that the Kitsune Saiguu ushered people into the underground halls of Araumi to save them from a disaster. In Sacrificial Offering Hanachirusato says she has not been to the Araumi tree root for a long time and wonders whether anyone still lives there. The Reizenbou page places her and the Kitsune Saiguu here during the Cataclysm and says Reizenbou withdrew into the Empty Boat of a Thousand Gates after the Kitsune Saiguu died. Ei's Shogunate era also leaves traces: a voice on a Memento Lens echo, identified as Hiiragi Hiroshi, hopes to build a port here and centralise foreign trade under government control.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Salt wind and stone above; below, still water, dark halls and the hum of Electro mechanisms. Lamps and gates look centuries old but respond to the Memento Lens, giving the sense that the city is asleep rather than dead.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Ruin Guards, Ruin Scouts and Defenders; Kairagi Mifune Satoshi; the Perpetual Mechanical Array, whose attacks include rockets, orbital strikes and cube-form charges; and Ochimusha, the defilement that appears during the Sacred Sakura Cleansing Ritual.
### LARGE +
Deep water, collapsing levels and Thunder Barriers make parts of the city inaccessible without Electrogranum or the Memento Lens.

## travel
### STANDARD
Araumi can be reached by the Waverider and Teleport Waypoints on Narukami Island's northern coast. Its underground passages are sealed until the Memento Lens is used on kitsune statues in a well. The Perpetual Mechanical Array can also be reached through a portal on Jinren Island.

## quests
### STANDARD
Sacrificial Offering (2.0) gives the Memento Lens. Cleansing Defilement (2.0) includes a ritual at Araumi, one of five places where a Thunder Sakura barrier must be broken; its final fight is against Ochimusha. Both belong to the Sacred Sakura Cleansing Ritual series. Araumi's underground also holds Old Stone Slates and a Torn Page from Toki Alley Tales (Prologue).

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 Araumi is abandoned, with no residents and no reported change since its release.
### STANDARD +
The Sacred Sakura Cleansing Ritual at Araumi has been completed in the story. Nothing in 6.x or 7.0-7.1 content revisits the site.

## entry: Empty Boat of a Thousand Gates
@keys: Empty Boat of a Thousand Gates, Tutelage: A Thousand Scrolls
@keys2: Araumi, Reizenbou
@version: 2.0
### STANDARD
The Empty Boat of a Thousand Gates is a tengu annex dwelling that floats on the sea like a lonely boat, deep under Araumi. It was the final abode of a tengu warrior who had abandoned the world, and was once linked with the "Yougou Three". The domain lets the Traveler solve its riddle and fight trespassing bandits: the Kairagi Dancing Thunder, joined by Fiery Might at half health. To unlock it, step on glowing panels to open the gates.
### LARGE +
The wiki reads the Chinese description as saying the boat became the cage where Reizenbou spent her remaining days and then sank into the sea. In the story of the Cataclysm she is said to have withdrawn here after failing to protect the Kitsune Saiguu, leaving her daughter Teruyo behind. This reading is the wiki's, not stated outright in game.

## entry: Perpetual Mechanical Array
@keys: Perpetual Mechanical Array, Perpetual Resonance
@keys2: Araumi, Ruin Sentinels
### STANDARD
A large Ruin Sentinel automaton and world boss that guards the lowest level of Araumi's ruins. It changes between forms, such as a rocket, a rolling cube, a sword and a spinning blade, and sends out cubes that become smaller Ruin Sentinels. It drops the Perpetual Heart.

## entry: Memento Lens and Earth Kitsune Statues
@keys: Memento Lens, Earth Kitsune Statue
@keys2: Araumi, Inazuma
### STANDARD
The Memento Lens, a gadget gained in Sacrificial Offering, reveals hidden mechanisms and echoes of the past at Earth Kitsune Statues. At Araumi it unlocks the sealed passages and shows a moment from the Shogunate era, a commissioner's wish for a port, and other voices from earlier times, including a mother-and-sons exchange tied to Mikoshi Nagamasa and Iwakura Michihiro.
