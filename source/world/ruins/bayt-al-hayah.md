## summary
### LITE
Bayt Al-Hayah is a cavern temple inside Mt. Damavand in Sumeru's Desert of Hadramaveth, beneath Wounded Shin Valley, leading into Deshret's Glass Goblet. It is unlocked in Dune-Entombed Fecundity: Part III.
### STANDARD +
The name is Arabic for House of Life. The map viewpoint calls it the Court of Withered Life. It is on the lower level of the Mt. Damavand ruins.

## geography
@version: 3.4
### STANDARD
Beneath Wounded Shin Valley, on the lower level of the Mt. Damavand map.
### LARGE +
Part III begins on the surface with the ancient machine and ends at the Goblet and the platform leading to the Eternal Oasis.

## history
@version: 3.4
### STANDARD
During Part III, Liloupar describes the tunnels' temple-like stretch as a water supply station and a temple at once, because water was held to be a gift of the Three Gods. She warns that once the sands of time bury this place the Eternal Oasis will inevitably collapse, since even the Master of the Sands could not guarantee its eternity.
### LARGE +
In Part III, Azariq and Aderfi try to kill Jeht and the Traveler; after they are beaten, the party connects the last pipes, collects another fragment and reaches the Goblet, where Ferigees, who guards the oasis, blocks the way. When she falls the path to the Eternal Oasis opens.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Pipes and halls lined with sand, a monumental water station that doubles as a shrine. The viewpoint title evokes withered life.

## dangers
### LARGE
The main danger is the quest's confrontation with Ferigees, an ancient machine in which a Jinni's spirit dwelt, in the central cavern.

## quests
@version: 3.4
### STANDARD
Dune-Entombed Fecundity: Part III, the fifth part of The Dirge of Bilqis.
### ULTRA +
Ferigees calls Liloupar a fallen traitor and accuses her of bringing down Gurabad's walls with honeyed words; Liloupar offers to apologise but is refused. The Dirge of Bilqis continues afterwards in The Eternal Dream, Ever Lush.
