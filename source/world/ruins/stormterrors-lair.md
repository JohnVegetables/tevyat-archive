## summary
### LITE
Stormterror's Lair is the ruin of Old Mondstadt, the capital that the tyrannical God of Storms, Decarabian, built in the Brightcrown Mountains of northwestern Mondstadt. A ring-shaped city surrounds his broken central tower. The city has been empty since his fall about 2,600 years ago. Later the wounded dragon Dvalin came here to rest, and the people of Mondstadt, who had forgotten him and feared him, called him "Stormterror", which gave the ruins their name. In the Prologue, the Traveler, Venti, Jean and Diluc fought their way to the top of the tower to free Dvalin from the Abyss Order's corruption.
### STANDARD +
The ruins remain deserted and wind-scoured. Old storm walls still block the gates, wind currents rise among the ruins, and monsters and Abyss Order agents sometimes gather there. Dvalin still returns at times, though he is not always present.

## geography
### STANDARD
The ruins fill a deep valley in the Brightcrown Mountains, northwest of Mondstadt City. They are approached through Brightcrown Canyon, a pass once held by the King of Gales' soldiers and still guarded by ancient mechanisms. In the centre stands Decarabian's tower, broken but still standing. In 6.5 the area was extended northeast to connect with Millhaven in the newly opened Windrest Peak.
### LARGE +
Alice's Teyvat Travel Guide describes the city as built in rings: every resident was assigned a place between an inner and an outer ring, with the King of Gales' tower in the middle. Alice blew up a few of the tower's arcades to make the climb easier, and remarked that the ruin felt more ancient for it. Calla lilies and windwheel asters grow among the stones.

## places
### STANDARD
- **Decarabian's Tower**: the broken central spire from which the God of Storms ruled. Its uppermost chambers are where the Prologue's battle with Dvalin began.
- **The ring city**: concentric residential rings, ruined arcades and tall bridges in Decarabian's distinctive style.
- **Brightcrown Canyon and the entrance ruins**: the old approach road into the capital.
### LARGE +
- **The first Field Tiller**: a dormant Ruin Guard on one of the tower's upper floors, the prototype from which all later Ruin Guards were copied.
- **Light actuators**: four light mechanisms, one at the top of the tower and three scattered among the ruins, which together opened the seal on the tower in the Prologue.
- **A mysterious camp** on top of a tall rock formation in the southwest, with a tent and a sword planted in front of it, guarded by Anemo slimes and an Eye of the Storm. Who made it is unknown.

## history
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Decarabian raised this city by his own divine power and walled it in storms against the blizzards of the north. He accepted worship from his tower, unaware that his people did not bow out of love. About 2,600 years ago they rose against him, and after his death the new Anemo Archon led them away to found today's Mondstadt City on Cider Lake. The capital was left empty. Five hundred years ago, during the cataclysm, Dvalin was badly wounded killing the Abyssal dragon Durin. He settled in the abandoned tower to recover, and slept there while the Abyssal poison tormented him and the people forgot him.
### LARGE +
In the centuries after the city's fall, a badly damaged machine took refuge in the ruins. It was the first Field Tiller, which had fought Boreas in his proving ground. For about a year it fired on anyone approaching Old Mondstadt, and Mondstadters, who knew nothing of such machines, called it the "Tyrant's Final Fury", the dead storm god's last anger. Then it shut down. In modern times Alice explored the ruins for her travel guide and blew up several arcades.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Grim and windswept: huge empty streets, toppled arcades and a broken tower ringed by howling wind. Visitors find it oddly sad even without knowing why. Its own descriptions call it a desolate city of "magical mechanisms and painful memories", haunted by winds that still tell a story no mortal remembers, of a nameless wind sprite and the chorus that made the tower tremble. For Venti it is also a peaceful lookout; during the 1.4 Windblume event he said that when he has nothing else to do he sometimes comes here just to sit and gaze.

## dangers
### LARGE
Storm walls and ancient seals bar parts of the ruins, and wind currents are needed to reach the upper tower. Hilichurl camps, slimes, Abyss Mages and other Abyss Order forces have occupied the ruins at times. The lowest floor of Decarabian's tower is too dangerous to enter. The expedition description warns that Stormterror made it a dangerous place, though bold adventurers still go there looking for treasure.

## quests
@version: 1.0
### STANDARD
In **Song of the Dragon and Freedom** (Prologue Act III), killing an Abyss Mage frees Venti to sense how to dispel the wind barrier around the ruins. Hilichurls under Abyss Order direction try to stall the party at the entrance. Venti plays the barrier away, and the group climbs the tower to find it sealed. They light four light actuators, then fight Dvalin in the skies above and break the poisoned blood clots on his body. When the battlefield collapses Dvalin saves them, and he flies off reassured that no one is after him.
### LARGE +
In **Bough Keeper: Dainsleif** (1.3), the Traveler and Dainsleif search the lair for an Abyss Herald and check the light actuators. Dainsleif remarks that the ruin has had little to do with the dragon for a long time and that he saw it before Dvalin ever fell. On a cliff above, the Traveler touches a dandelion trampled beside a Ruin Guard's footprint and senses their missing sibling.
### ULTRA +
In **We Will Be Reunited** (1.4), the Traveler and Dainsleif trace the first Field Tiller to the tower, and Dainsleif removes its eye so the Abyss Order cannot use it to build a mechanised god.

## status
@version: 7.1
### LITE
As of 7.1 Stormterror's Lair is a deserted ruin and a known landmark north of Mondstadt City. Dvalin, now reconciled with Mondstadt, comes and goes, and is often absent.
### STANDARD +
Since the opening of Windrest Peak (6.5), the ruins connect northeast to Millhaven, so the old capital now sits on a route between the capital's hinterland and the northern regions.

## entry: Decarabian's Tower
@keys: Decarabian's Tower, tower of Decarabian, Tower of the King of Gales
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Decarabian's Tower is the broken central spire of Old Mondstadt, where the God of Storms lived and received worship. Its upper chambers are filled with howling wind and memories. In the Prologue the Traveler's party climbed it on wind currents to reach the sealed top, from which they went up to fight Dvalin. The first Field Tiller stands dormant on an upper floor.
### LARGE +
Tiles and debris from the tower are said to keep some of the divine power that built the city. Alice deliberately blew up some of its arcades to make it easier to climb.

## entry: Dvalin's Nest in the Ruins
@keys: Stormterror's Lair
@keys2: Dvalin, Stormterror, Dragon of the East, nest, sleep
@spoiler: minor
@availability: limited
@version: 1.4
### STANDARD
Dvalin, the Dragon of the East and one of the Four Winds, settled in the tower after the cataclysm to heal from Durin's poison, and slept there for centuries. When he woke, the people had forgotten him, and the Abyss Order took advantage of his pain and resentment. Mondstadters named his roost Stormterror's Lair. After the Prologue he was freed from the corruption and reconciled with Venti and the city.
### LARGE +
During the Invitation of Windblume event (1.4), Venti brought the Traveler here to clear away monsters as a Windblume gift for Dvalin. He explained that "Windblume" began as a secret code word for resistance in Old Mondstadt, and Dvalin flew overhead.

## entry: The Tyrant's Final Fury
@keys: Tyrant's Final Fury, first Field Tiller
@keys2: Stormterror's Lair, Old Mondstadt, Decarabian, Ruin Guard
@version: 1.4
### LARGE
In the Church's old story, a bombardment once struck anyone approaching Old Mondstadt and was blamed on the dead Decarabian's wrath. It was really the first Field Tiller, the original Khaenri'ahn Ruin Guard. Boreas had badly damaged it, and it lay crippled in the ruins, firing missiles for about a year before it went still. In 1.4 Dainsleif took its eye, which the Abyss Order had planned to use.

## entry: Durin's Visit to Stormterror's Lair
@keys: Stormterror's Lair
@keys2: Durin, story-world, Draco Rubedo, little dragon
@version: 6.2
### LARGE
In Durin's story quest Fate's Accursed Wiles (6.2), a clue from Alice's storybook about a castle where a dragon dwells leads Durin and the Traveler to the lair. Through the old battle site atop the tower, they enter the book's story-world. Durin hoped to meet Dvalin there but did not. As they left, Venti and Dvalin watched from a nearby cliff, and Venti said Durin wanted to be friends but that the moment had not yet come.
