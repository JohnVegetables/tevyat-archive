## summary
### LITE
Tianqiu Valley is a valley of ruined towers and stone in Minlin, Liyue, once a prosperous settlement. A legend says it was ruined by the rampage of an ancient dragon king, whom Rex Lapis and his adepti imprisoned in an adepti realm, while the dragon's servants fled underground to sleep. A cavern under the ruins holds a Primo Geovishap.
### STANDARD +
Tianqiu Valley has two waypoints (east and south) and three ruined towers on the west side. The Adventurers' Guild's concern about earthquakes in the valley is what sends the Traveler to the cavern.

## geography
### STANDARD
A broad valley in Minlin with ruins, a Geo elemental monument and Seelie. Three ruined towers stand on the western side, each hiding a puzzle: a torch puzzle in the north, a time-limited climb in the west and fights against infused monsters in the south. A large cavern revealed by earthquakes holds the Primo Geovishap.
### LARGE +
The towers carry a "Light Actuator" for the main ruins' mechanism. The valley's common enemies include slimes, hilichurls, Fatui skirmishers and Treasure Hoarders; elite foes include Geovishaps, a Geovishap Hatchling, Pyro Abyss Mages and Electro Cicin Mages.

## people
### STANDARD
Treasure Hoarders work the area; their notes complain of too many monsters, of men who came out "acting strange", and of a warning about a dragon-lord. They answer to a "Boss".

## history
### STANDARD
A tablet in the ruins laments the cold trail of the adepti and mourns that, though mighty Azhdaha was defeated long ago, this land of beauty cannot be restored. Zhiping of the Adventurers' Guild says, citing texts, that the valley was once a prosperous settlement, and passes on the legend of an ancient dragon king's rampage ended by Rex Lapis and his adepti, with the dragon's servants hiding underground. She treats it as unverifiable folklore.
### LARGE +
The Primo Geovishap's own description says folktales hold that after the "draconic calamity" that ruined Tianqiu Valley, the Geovishaps and Primo Geovishaps burrowed deep underground to await their overlord, Azhdaha. Hence the legend is the setting's explanation for the creatures' presence.
### ULTRA +
In the world quest the Primo Geovishap lairs in a cavern uncovered by strong earthquakes; the Primo Geovishap boss itself came with the 1.3 promotional event "Even Mountains Tremble".

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Broken towers against a wide sky, dry stone and a lingering sense of an old calamity; tremors underfoot suggest something large sleeping below.

## dangers
### STANDARD
A Primo Geovishap in the cavern (randomly infused with Cryo, Pyro, Electro or Hydro in the quest), Geovishaps, Treasure Hoarders and infused monsters in the towers.

## quests
### STANDARD
"Trails in Tianqiu" (the tablet and the three towers) and "No Restoring This Past Land of Beauty" (Zhiping's request to investigate the earthquakes and cavern).

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 an exploration site with the Primo Geovishap still in its cavern and no developing story.

### ULTRA
The legend, as told by Zhiping and echoed in the Primo Geovishap's description, is the only link in canon between this valley and the dragon Azhdaha, who is otherwise sealed under the Dragon-Queller in Nantianmen and in the Chasm. The tablet's poem names Azhdaha as defeated; the quest title takes its words from it.
