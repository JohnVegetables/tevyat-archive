## summary
### LITE
Natlantea was the civilisation that came before Natlan: an age when Pyro dragons ruled and humans lived in primitive tribes. Its lord was the Pyro Dragon Sovereign Xiuhcoatl, served by the Thirteen Sovereign Lords, and its greatest city was the Sacred City of Tollan. The era ended when Xbalanque united an army, defeated Xiuhcoatl and became the first Pyro Archon.
### STANDARD +
Natlantea is not a place a traveller can visit as such; it is the ancient layer under today's Natlan. Its ruins, mechanisms and records are scattered across the nation, and the dragons of that age eventually devolved into the Saurians.

## geography
### STANDARD
The land that is now Natlan, divided among the Thirteen Sovereign Lords, each of whom was given a territory to rule. Tollan was carved out by Xiuhcoatl himself and was said to be rivalled only by Chichen Uctokah. The wiki lists known sub-civilisations: Chichen Uctokah (the later Ochkanatlan, under the Lord Ix Uxul Tz'ib Bolon), Ch'upikaq Tajin (now the Flower-Feather Clan's land, under Ix Ajkotz'i'j Iq'ismal Waxak), Ek' Pulinik and Q'umqaraq'aj (unplaced), and the Tezcatepetonco Range (under Ix Kemonel Jasjatem Oox).
### LARGE +
The 'cable' in Chichen Uctokah that leads beyond Teyvat's skies was anchored by the construct Tonatiuh. A Celestial Nail struck what is now the Basin of Unnumbered Flames, ruining Natlantea's phlogiston-rich soil.

## people
### STANDARD
Xiuhcoatl, born from the Primal Flame as the Pyro Dragon Sovereign, and his younger brother Kukulkan, whom he made from a portion of that flame on the day of his own birth. The Thirteen Sovereign Lords were artificial intelligences made by Xiuhcoatl, regarded as his descendants and as the peak of Secret Source Mechanism technology. Below them stood the Three Princes. Humans lived in tribes under the dragons' brutal rule.
### LARGE +
The Lords held differing views of humans. The Third Lord made a pact with the humans of her region. The Eighth adopted a human daughter, Lianca, and gave her a bow made by the Second Lord. The Eleventh loathed humans more than any peer. The Fifth recommended culling the human population at a Sovereign Council. The First regarded Xbalanque as an enemy worthy of respect, and the Twelfth recognised his worth just before she died.
### ULTRA +
Kukulkan, who later became the human legend of the Sage of the Stolen Flame, gave a seed of phlogiston to the human Chaac after losing faith in dragonkind, which the sources describe as the re-establishment of human civilisation in Natlantea.

## governance
### STANDARD
Rule by dragons over humans. Xiuhcoatl sat at the top; the Thirteen Sovereign Lords governed territories; councils of the Lords recorded debates, including over how to treat humans. Kukulkan served as Regent while Xiuhcoatl was incapacitated.

## history
### LITE
Natlantea ended in a war. Xbalanque rallied humans under one banner, laid siege to Tollan, killed ten of the Thirteen Sovereign Lords and the Three Princes, and fought Xiuhcoatl; the two fell together and Xbalanque emerged reborn as the first Pyro Archon. A treaty with Kukulkan followed, ending dragon rule.
### STANDARD
Xiuhcoatl was born from the Primal Flame and shaped the Pyro vishaps from it. The Heavenly Principles then arrived in Teyvat and defeated the Seven Sovereigns; Xiuhcoatl was frozen in lava and fell into a false death. Natlantea is thought to have come under Heavenly Principles control, overseen by Angels, with Kukulkan as Regent. When the Nibelung returned bearing the Abyss' power, its call woke Xiuhcoatl, who halted the Sovereign Lords' attempts to explore the stars and replicate moon-forging, retook control and devoured the Angels, an act that drove part of the unified civilisation into savagery.
### LARGE +
As a result of the War of Funerary Flame, the ley lines of Natlantea, which the Heavenly Principles had made, were destroyed, and a Celestial Nail struck the future Basin of Unnumbered Flames. The ruined soil caused the dragonborn of Natlantea to regress. Xiuhcoatl, infested by the Abyss and losing his lucidity, built the Huitzilopochtli array, using his own eye as its control hub, and the Astral Assemblages in a last attempt to banish the Abyss from Teyvat, experimenting on himself in the process. Watching this, Kukulkan gave up on dragonkind and passed phlogiston to humans.
### ULTRA +
Xiuhcoatl's final plan was never completed before Xbalanque's siege. The dragons of Natlan later degenerated into the Saurians.

## status
### STANDARD
Natlantea is long gone. Its legacy survives as ruins, Secret Source machinery, the Great Volcano of Tollan and the Saurians, and the older layers of Natlan's history keep surfacing in the Archon Quests and world quests. Nothing newer than 7.1 changes this.

## entry: Xbalanque's Siege of Tollan

@keys2: Xbalanque, Xiuhcoatl, Natlantea, dragons
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Before Xiuhcoatl could carry out his last plan, Xbalanque and the army he had united invaded Tollan, killing ten of the Thirteen Sovereign Lords and the Three Princes. He then fought Xiuhcoatl; as they fell from a great height Xbalanque came out of Xiuhcoatl's chest, reborn as the first Pyro Archon. His victory and a treaty with Kukulkan ended dragon rule.

## entry: The Thirteen Sovereign Lords
@keys: Thirteen Sovereign Lords, Sovereign Lords, Sovereign Rulers' Council

### LARGE
Xiuhcoatl's artificial 'descendants', each given a territory. Their attitudes to humans ranged from pacts and adoption to a recommendation to cull them. Ten of them died when Tollan fell. Other council records are preserved in the Chamber of Deliberation.
