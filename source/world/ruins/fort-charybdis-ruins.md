## summary
### LITE
Fort Charybdis Ruins are the remains of a fort on an island in the eastern part of the Morte Region. Inside lies a hidden underground complex, the Orthant of Wishes, which holds one of the four Seals of Chymical Marriage placed by the Narzissenkreuz Ordo. The fort's name is also spoken by Remurian golems in 4.6 as a place where their legions were told to fall back. Canon text about the fort itself is thin.
### STANDARD +
The fort was introduced in Version 4.2 with the Morte Region and the World Quest chain In the Wake of Narcissus. The area is linked to the Ordo's diary notes and a hymn that Boethius later quotes.

## geography
### STANDARD
An island ruin on the east of the Morte Region. The visible fort is a ruin on top of a "Wilderness Cavern" level, below which lies the Orthant of Wishes, a puzzle-filled chamber with several floors, mechanisms, energy flow pipes and an underwater section.
### LARGE +
The entrance is called the Mysterious Ruins. A looking glass inside leads to a small side chamber with a chest and a Thundercraven Rifthound.

## places
### LARGE
Orthant of Wishes: one of four seals, in a multi-floored chamber with a riddle slate that begins "Lo, this place is the Orthant of Wishes, the Temple of Wishes, the Room of Wishes".
Second floor: holds the diary and the engraved hymn excerpt.

## people
### STANDARD
No named inhabitants. The Narzissenkreuz Ordo's members left behind a diary; the Ordo's founder Jakob Ingold and their leader, the Master, appear in it. Caterpillar and Ann help the Traveler find the seals.

## history
### LITE
No source describes who built the fort or why. The Ordo later used the site as one of four seals for its Great Ritual; the diary of a member records that the party found "Remuria's ruins" at the indicated location and arranged an official construction project.
### STANDARD +
The diary says the Palais Mermonia confirmed the location and a construction team arrived, with Marechaussee Hunters hired for security. The Master and Mr. Ingold brought an Oceanid to a tower not yet finished; later they said the Master was performing the Great Ritual, tied to celestial bodies and the Primal Fire (Phlogiston). Workers began spreading uneasy rumours, and a faction led by a woman called Zenaida left the Ordo, but the writer later found by divination that those who had left were still inside the tower.
### LARGE +
The excerpt on the second floor comes from a hymn titled "Echoes Hallowed and Humble", Movement 5. It praises will as what humans share with the noblest gods, and says those who command with their will will be obeyed by everyone else. In Gradus ad Capitolium Boethius quotes this verse as his own justification for demanding that the surface dwellers submit.

### ULTRA +
In The Last Day of Remuria the Remurian Este tells the soldiers of Legio IX that they ought to have retreated to Fort Charybdis, and the leader replies that they stayed behind as the rearguard while the main troops went there. A Fort Charybdis is therefore a named military position of the Remurian war, though the wiki does not say it is this ruin. The name matches the sea monster Charybdis, paired with Scylla in Greek myth, as the wiki's trivia notes.

## quests
### STANDARD
In Meteoric Lance, part of Search in the Algae Sea, the Traveler enters the ruins, solves puzzles and reads the riddle slate to collect a wish piece for the seal. In both versions a man lies unconscious at the door and is taken to Lumidouce Harbor for treatment; the Narzissenkreuz Ordo's handiwork is suspected.
### LARGE +
Quest gameplay includes raising a gate to a lift, an energy flow puzzle, a Seelie court, an underwater section and Xenochromatic Hunter's Ray tasks, ending with the obtained wish piece.

## status
### LITE
Unchanged since the 4.2 quest. The seal and the Ordo's apparatus remain.
### STANDARD +
Nothing newer than the 4.2 quest is known about the site.

## dangers
### LARGE
Clockwork mek guards (Underwater Patrol Mek, Recon Log Mek - Ousia), puzzle machinery, strong water currents, and the Ordo's lingering traps.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Salt air and still stone, then a hum of machinery below. The mood is of an old military post made secret and then recycled for ritual.

## entry: Excerpt: Echoes Hallowed and Humble
@keys: Echoes Hallowed and Humble
@version: 4.2
### STANDARD
An engraved hymn in the Mysterious Ruins, quoted as Movement 5, Stanza 3 of "Echoes Hallowed and Humble". It contrasts barbarians and witless water creatures with those whose strength comes from will, which they share with the noblest gods, and says the obedient rest bow down before those who command by will. A line in the Orthant of Memory, a different seal, quotes Movement 9, Stanza 37 of the same work, and Cassiodor quotes that second verse against Boethius in Fortune Plango Vulnera.
