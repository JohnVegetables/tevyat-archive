## summary
### LITE
Gurabad's Ruin - Temple of Deshret is a temple complex on the outskirts of the fallen city of Gurabad, in the canyon country of the Desert of Hadramaveth. It is full of sand-driving machines and sealed rooms, and two hidden chambers on its upper and lower levels hold murals about the shepherd-king Ormazd and the Jinni Liloupar. It is explored in the quest The Temple Where Sand Flows Like Tears.
### STANDARD +
The Tanit tribe regards the ruins as cursed and taboo. Jeht, who counts as half an outsider, goes in with the Traveler to find a fragment of the Mother of the Jinn.

## geography
@version: 3.4
### STANDARD
A temple with an upper and a lower level, and a giant central device. It lies in Safhe Shatranj, a canyon the Tanit call the Great Rift, in the southwest of the Desert of Hadramaveth; before the quest the area is called The Temples Forsaken and drifts of sand block the way.
### LARGE +
Drifts of loose sand can only be cleared by operating the temple's devices: a machine blows sand in a set direction, and a hard blow can turn it to a new direction and make runes flow. Sealing machines, gates and a collapsing section create a chain of linked puzzles across the complex.

## places
@version: 3.4
### STANDARD
The main temple centres on a giant device, which the quest has the party touch more than once as the sand-sealing machines are woken and the sand is made to flow. Opposite the square is a smaller ruin that holds Liloupar's Cell. Two sealed mural rooms, one on each level, are the temple's best known features.
### LARGE +
The upper-level murals show Ormazd, then Ormazd with Liloupar. The lower-level murals show Huvashtra, the tyrant Ormazd overthrew, and Ormazd with Liloupar twice more. The paintings depict Liloupar in a loose pinkish style; apart from that, her appearance is largely unknown.

## history
@version: 3.4
### STANDARD
Tradition among the desert folk says Gurabad was the work of the shepherd Ormazd, who with King Deshret's grace and Liloupar's help overthrew the tyrant Huvashtra and gathered the desert's wandering tribes. Liloupar later brought ruin on the city across three generations in revenge for his corruption, and King Deshret punished her by splitting her into seven fragments hidden in the Great Red Sand.
### LARGE +
A tablet found after the second mural room opens gives Ormazd's own words: once a humble shepherd, he credits Deshret for his forces, says Deshret sent the great Jinni from the City of the Moon Maiden to help build Gurabad, and dedicates half his kingdom to Liloupar and half to the king. He also speaks of a coming sacrifice of a thousand slaves. Jeht remarks that whoever first built the place must have believed their works would last forever.

## culture
@version: 3.4
### STANDARD
For the Tanit tribe the ruins are bad luck. Their guide Azariq stops short of going deeper than the temple's gate. Jeht quotes the Tanit proverb that the closer the home the more reticent the heart, and Matriarch Babel's maxim that true gold has deep roots in soil and spring.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Wind-scoured stone, drifts of sand and ancient machinery that wakes when touched. The viewpoint's name for it is the Palace Abandoned by the Gods, and the desert songs say long-lived giants once lived here before being cast into a rift in one night.

## dangers
### LARGE
Eremites, Primal Constructs and fungi are among the enemies in the surrounding canyon. A forgotten mechanical guardian called Anoushbord confronts the party at the smaller ruin opposite the square, where the bottle holding Liloupar's fragment lies.

## quests
@version: 3.4
### STANDARD
The Temple Where Sand Flows Like Tears is the second part of the world quest series The Dirge of Bilqis. The Murals of Gurabad, an unofficially titled hidden exploration objective, becomes available once The Dirge of Bilqis is finished; it requires finding six Mysterious Stone Slates, three per door, and awards two Precious Chests and the achievement The Nameless City's Past.
### LARGE +
After the quest the Tanit's search continues with Dune-Entombed Fecundity, and the Traveler later learns more of Gurabad's fall in Memories of Gurabad, begun at the chessboard of Safhe Shatranj.

### ULTRA +
The reason for the visit is political. Matriarch Babel wants a Jinni to restore fertility to the tribe's soil and to carry the party through the seals of the Eternal Oasis, and Azariq says this particular Jinni still keeps her intelligence. A Jinni gives a human her true name to form a contract, and Jeht warns that Jinn are said to hate humanity. Liloupar dismisses the Tanit's schemes, but offers her true name to the Traveler alone.

## entry: The Murals of Gurabad
@keys: Murals of Gurabad, Nameless City's Past, Nameless Tablet, Huvashtra
@keys2: Gurabad, Ormazd, Liloupar
@version: 3.4
### STANDARD
After finishing The Dirge of Bilqis, the Traveler can find slates I to III to open the upper-level door and slates IV to VI to open the lower-level door of the temple. The murals show Ormazd, Ormazd with Liloupar and the defeated tyrant Huvashtra. A tablet in the lower room gives Ormazd's account, in which Deshret's grace let him unseat Huvashtra, the Jinni came to help, and he promised his kingdom to Liloupar and the king alike.
