## summary
### LITE
The Ancestral Temple is a sacred site on Coatepec Mountain in Natlan where people once communicated with the Night Kingdom. Today the tribes have a steadier link to the Night Kingdom, so the temple is unused, but it is still holy ground, guarded by the Scions of the Canopy.
### STANDARD +
Legba says only experienced Couriers who have passed three trials may enter. The in-game description evokes ancestors who made sacrifices here and prayed for a revelation that would change everything.

## geography
### STANDARD
A subarea of Coatepec Mountain. Features include Nightsoul Totems, relay balls and ball path converters, and removable grappling hooks, so crossing it involves Nightsoul puzzles.
### LARGE +
Several damaged stone tablets are scattered through the temple; their texts are on the wiki and are not summarised here.

## people
### STANDARD
Legba, the Scions of the Canopy's contact, is the NPC placed here. The Scions of the Canopy keep guard.

## culture
### STANDARD
Natlan's tribes treat the Night Kingdom as a realm tied to their dead and to Nightsoul. The temple is the older way in; the tribes later set up a more stable route. In the expedition text the land is described as one that did not belong entirely to humans, whose primitive elemental forces drained the soil's vitality.

## history
### STANDARD
Natlan's ancestors offered sacrifices here to their god and sought a revelation that would alter everything, according to the expedition description. When the tribes found a steadier connection to the Night Kingdom, the temple lost its practical role.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Austere, stony and ceremonial, with Nightsoul machinery and an air of restriction: a place that is respected rather than visited casually.

## travel
### STANDARD
Entry is restricted to experienced Couriers who have passed three trials, per Legba.
### ULTRA +
The temple's quests are listed on the wiki under Ancestral Temple Quests, and a soundtrack is assigned to the area; the wiki's etymology and lore sections for it are empty, so no more can be said from sources read.

## status
### STANDARD
As of 7.1 the temple is disused but guarded, and remains holy ground.
