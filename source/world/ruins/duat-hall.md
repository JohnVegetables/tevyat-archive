## summary
### LITE
Duat Hall is a vast underground hall in the Mausoleum of King Deshret, in Sumeru's Hypostyle Desert, whose floor falls away into a bottomless void. Its name is the ancient Egyptian realm of the dead, and the Scarlet Sand Slate describes it as the place where the guilty meet their fate.
### STANDARD +
It is the first stop of the trials in Dual Evidence and joins the Great Hall of Truths on its south side. A side chamber off its east side, the Place of Swallowing, holds one of the slate's clearance trials.

## geography
@version: 3.1
### STANDARD
Reached through an underground teleport waypoint directly south of the Mausoleum. A door to it opens only with the Ferryman's Clearance, a permission the slate grants when Dual Evidence begins. The hall is connected to the Great Hall of Truths at its south end and to the Place of Swallowing off its east side.
### LARGE +
Opet Hall to the north looks as if it joins the Great Hall of Truths, but that route cannot be used; travellers must come through Duat Hall instead.

## history
@version: 3.1
### STANDARD
After Soheil's dismissal in Aaru Village, the slate lights up and points to Duat Hall. On entering, the party finds new writing and three points of light. The text describes the mortal plane as a river between the underworld and Aaru that must not be crossed or coveted, and tells the reader to take a vulture's form, peck at three organs, win Al-Ahmar's consent and cross as a falcon. Paimon connects the three organs to the devices Tirzad found earlier.
### LARGE +
The three points of light correspond to the Place of Rebirth, the Place of Breath and the Place of Swallowing; clearing each lights a sigil. Once all are lit the slate announces the Great Hall of Truths.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
A gaping dark hall, stone floors ending at nothing, the sound of shifting sand. Paimon warns the Traveler to stay away from the drop. It feels like a threshold between worlds.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Falling into the void is the main hazard. In play, falling only returns the traveller to the entrance without penalty; the slate's lines about judging the guilty are symbolic.

## quests
@version: 3.1
### STANDARD
Dual Evidence (Old Notes and New Friends, part 1). The Traveler enters here after Soheil's dismissal in Aaru Village.
### ULTRA +
Without Tirzad and Jebrael arguing over the slate's meaning, the party has to read it alone; Paimon remarks that it misses their bickering and wonders how many palaces King Deshret built.
