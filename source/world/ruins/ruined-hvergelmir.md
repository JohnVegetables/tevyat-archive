## summary
### LITE
The Ruined Hvergelmir is what remains of the Hvergelmir research institute in Snezhnaya's White Birch Snowgrave, below the Belyi Tsar's Pale Crown Palace. It was a secret bio-alchemy and Shadow Realm institute founded by the Belyi Tsar and entrusted to his human ward Aksinya, with a goal of engineering a "Proto-Man" who would let the Tsar become the Primordial Human. It holds four laboratories belonging to Aksinya, Alvis, Koshchei and Pavlina.
### STANDARD +
The Traveler first sees it as a Shadow Realm vision on the train to the palace in the world quest To Gaze Upon the Dreaming Pale Crown (7.0). The institute is shown intact about 500 years ago, with its researchers, assistants and a cat.

## geography
### STANDARD
Hvergelmir lies in the hidden chambers of the Belyi Tsar's private palace outside Snezhnograd, reached through the White Birch Snowgrave by hidden train tracks. The Eye of Graeae calls it only a corner of the palace's shadow. The vision places the institute at the edge of the Shadow Realm's "Font", into which Aksinya falls at the end of the flashback.
### LARGE +
Locked gates and concealed doors separate the laboratories; a painting hides a secret passage in Pavlina's lab, and Prime Ice seals some of the notes.

## places
### STANDARD
Four laboratories fill the institute: Koshchei's (alchemy and chimeric beasts), Pavlina's (Prime Ice facilities and preserved Hyperborean relics), Alvis' (potions and the Shadow Realm secret chamber) and Aksinya's (the plan to create the Proto-Man). A hidden room behind a painting in Pavlina's lab holds the starving cat, and a locked gate nearby is guarded by the assistant Vsevolod.
### LARGE +
Koshchei's lab holds notes on the Prime Chimeric Nexus and the Eitr; Alvis' lab has the scales and potion formula used in the quest puzzle, and Alvis' notes about moving Hvergelmir to a new Kitezhgrad; Pavlina's has a Mora chest sealed in Prime Ice with her notes; Aksinya's contains Note VI of the Belyi Tsar's papers and a letter reading "All for a world free of sorrow".

## people
### STANDARD
Aksinya, Court Jester and de facto head, is joined by Alvis (Grand Duke of the Domovoy), Pavlina (Grand Duchess of the Snegurochka) and the alchemist Koshchei. Their assistants Izmaylov (Aksinya's Domovoy pupil), Azazel and Vsevolod appear in the vision.
### LARGE +
Pavlina's cat Lara is left in Koshchei's care. The four leaders have different ideologies yet were close; the Eye of Graeae, reviewing the records, finds it strange that they would betray one another.

## history
### STANDARD
The Belyi Tsar entered the Shadow Realm, found an infant (Aksinya) in a Hyperborean time capsule and, inspired by its life-preserving technology, founded Hvergelmir to seek "the stairway to the vacant seat in the court of the stars" where sorrow would end. The researchers explored Hyperborean ruins in the Shadow Realm, adapted energy-storing devices into firearms and created chimeric monsters; their final creation was the Eye of Graeae. After the Tsar's death about 500 years ago, a vision shows Aksinya pushed into the Shadow Realm Font, an act that later texts attribute to Pavlina, Alvis and Koshchei.
### LARGE +
The institute's work crossed into forbidden territory and survived only through the Tsar's tacit protection. When the new Tsaritsa took the throne, researchers feared a ban on alchemy. Koshchei's notes call Aksinya's idea of eternal shared love a dead end and side with the person in the egg; Alvis plans to take a share of the Eye's power and later to move Hvergelmir to Novokitezhgrad after Kitezhgrad's destruction; Pavlina considers the power rightfully the fae's.

### ULTRA +
Koshchei's notes record a bet with Aksinya over whether a Prime Chimeric Nexus could support five embryo types; her five-embryo nexus held stable for only a few breaths, and she ordered only soft drinks at the Podvorye Restaurant afterwards. The Eye of Graeae's records add that a verdict from 530 years ago accused a Koshchei of using living fae in experiments, and that Pavlina once preserved Hyperborean finds in Prime Ice at the Tsar's request. Hvergelmir also holds an unnamed potion formula involving Kresnik crystal, said to resemble the power that once ruined Kitezhgrad.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
The rooms feel like a laboratory halted mid-experiment, pale, cold, and tense with whispered rivalries. The cat still cries for food, and the notes left on desks read as if addressed to someone no longer there.

## dangers
### LARGE
The Shadow Realm sends visitors into past scenes where they cannot be seen or heard, and the Traveler is trapped until puzzles are solved. Chimeric beasts and winged knights guard the approach, and the cold is deadly without Kresnik Kristall.

## travel
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
Reached via the quest To Gaze Upon the Dreaming Pale Crown: the player summons hidden tracks from a tower in the White Birch Snowgrave, rides the train, and falls into the vision. Co-op and the Serenitea Pot are locked for most of the quest.

## quests
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
To Gaze Upon the Dreaming Pale Crown opens the quest chain On One Side a Palace, On the Other a Tomb. Its sequel, The Stone Beasts, Like Ghouls Seated, and An Ark That Used to Be a Soul continue to the Court of Bliss where the Traveler defeats Aksinya and the Primordial Human.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the institute is a ruin. Aksinya remains trapped in the Shadow Realm after her defeat.
### STANDARD +
The Eye of Graeae's records say Alvis has recently passed away. What became of Koshchei is unclear; Pavlina is met in the present at the House of Hesperides.

## entry: Hvergelmir's Four Laboratories
@keys: Koshchei's lab, Pavlina's lab, Alvis' lab, Aksinya's lab, Hvergelmir laboratories
@keys2: Hvergelmir
@version: 7.0
### LARGE
Koshchei studied the Prime Chimeric Nexus (three, perhaps five, embryo types) and the Eitr, a living bio-furnace that dissolves embryos into one perfect form. Pavlina, a Snegurochka princess, built Prime Ice facilities and kept Hyperborean relics. Alvis, Domovoy Grand Duke, made potions from Kresnik crystal and planned his own advancement. Aksinya's research produced the Eye of Graeae.

## entry: Aksinya's Fall into the Shadow Realm
@keys: Aksinya's fall, Court of Bliss, Proto-Man, Proto Man
@keys2: Hvergelmir, Aksinya
@version: 7.0
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
In a vision, Aksinya, newly bearing the Eye of Graeae, intends to show it to her colleagues in the hope of continuing her plan, believing all differences return to a single path. Someone pushes her into the Font of the Shadow Realm; she says she will return and does not understand why they abandoned the grand undertaking. In the present she leads the Traveler to the Belyi Tsar's tomb and the Court of Bliss.
