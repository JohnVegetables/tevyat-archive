## summary
### LITE
The Faded Castle is a sunken ruin in the Nostoi Region that was once part of ancient Remuria. The Harmosts and scholars of Remuria lived there, maintaining archives and researching music theory and fate. The castle holds the portal to the Sea of Bygone Eras, an even deeper region where the main Remurian ruins lie. The Traveler first enters it during the 4.6 World Quest The Shadow Over Petrichor.
### STANDARD +
In Cassiodor's words it was the "golden castellum", where he and many companions recorded old plays, music scores and every story, real or imagined, in the hope of using them to master fate. Later the castle became the base of Boethius's Golden Troupe. Cassiodor, who sealed it to protect Fontaine, is the one who lets the Traveler in.

## geography
### STANDARD
The castle has several levels: an upper level, a middle level, a lower level with a pool, and an exhibition hall. Paintings of Domus Aurea hang in the middle level. Stages and murals use the power of the symphony, and the way into the Sea of Bygone Eras is a portal in the castle.
### LARGE +
Hidden rooms lie behind Mysterious Bookshelves full of grimoires, which are activated with melodies from the Ancient Autoharmonic Music Box. A library on the middle level and a mural stage play a role in Underwater Nocturne.

## places
### LARGE
Library and bookshelves: grimoires infused with the symphony's power.
Mural stage: a score-shaped mural that must be played with a blank score; this releases Boethius's shadow.
Lower-level pool: next to a bookshelf hiding the Harmost's Notes.
Higher level: where the Vicarius's report is kept.
Portal: leads down to the Sea of Bygone Eras.

## people
### STANDARD
The Harmosts, including Cassiodor, lived and worked here. The cat Osse is Cassiodor's spirit, speaking from the present. Boethius appears as a shadow rising from the mural. Praetorian Golems guard it.

## history
### LITE
In Remuria's golden age the Harmosts kept records of plays, scores and stories here. After the empire fell, Boethius gathered the survivors of Remuria into the castle as the Golden Troupe to resurrect the dead empire. Cassiodor opposed him, because the plan meant destroying the new nation of Fontaine and stealing its people's souls, and sealed the passage to the surface.
### STANDARD +
Cassiodor says he and his colleagues lived here when a new nation had just been born and "the possibilities were yet endless", organising the classics and researching music theory and magic to tame its perilous power. Item text for the Ancient Autoharmonic Music Box, as cited on the wiki, says Boethius's survivors gathered here to research reviving the empire.
### LARGE +
In 4.6, as Boethius reawakens Phobos, Cassiodor removes the seal and the Traveler and Paimon enter. Boethius appears from the mural, announces that he will wait in Domus Aurea, and says the souls of Petrichor's people will be released if Cassiodor stays resolute when the time comes.
### ULTRA +
Hirtius, vicarius of Legio VI Rithimi Versi, wrote a report of the northern tribes under Euergetia's authority. It describes tribes of the north as clan societies, a polis called Lugdunensis ruled by a "holy king", and Aremorica, whose former ruler Cunoricus knelt to the empire some twenty years earlier and whose current ruler Erinnyes lacks any divine gifts, in Hirtius's view. A note by Marius Servius suggests that the name Erinnyes is a mis-transcription of a title meaning "high ruler".

## quests
### STANDARD
The Shadow Over Petrichor leads the Traveler to the castle; Underwater Nocturne is set inside it. The player collects grimoires, a blank score and performs the symphony on the mural, which produces the score Tuba Mirum.
### LARGE +
The first quest also yields the Ancient Autoharmonic Music Box and the score Locus Amoenus. Lost grimoires opened shelves with secrets; three sets of research notes under the name Ruggiero are found in the castle, with no further explanation in the sources read.

## status
### LITE
The castle is now open. The seal is gone, and Phobos has been silenced, so the portal leads to quiet ruins.
### STANDARD +
Unsolved: its role in the Golden Troupe's history is only hinted at.

## dangers
### LARGE
Praetorian Golems (elite), Fontemer Aberrants, and the boss-class "Statue of Marble and Brass".

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Faded gold, drifting water, closed books and music that nobody is playing. It feels like a theatre waiting after the last performance.

## entry: Harmost's Notes (Musica Mundana)
@keys: Musica Mundana, Musica Humana, Musica Instrumentalis, Harmost's Notes
@version: 4.6
### STANDARD
Unsigned notes by a Harmost behind a bookshelf by the lower pool, partly based on the real treatise De institutione musica. They classify music into three kinds: Musica Mundana, the order of everything, Musica Humana, the music of mortals and gods, and Musica Instrumentalis, played by instruments. The writer links the first to Fortuna and says mortals and gods can replace the world's music with their own, and that musicless people cannot be considered human. The notes mention that Euergetia will explain astrological signs.

## entry: Investigation of the Northern Barbarians
@keys: Hirtius, Aremorica, Lugdunensis, Cunoricus, Marius Servius
@version: 4.6
### STANDARD
Report by Hirtius, vicarius of Legio VI Rithimi Versi, on the tribes of the northern border, for the use of future officers. Marius Servius adds notes. It records the "Mistress of All Waters" as a label forced on diverse gods by Aremorica's leader and contains many erased pages.

## entry: The Fall of the Faded Castle
@keys: The Fall of the Faded Castle
@version: 4.2
### STANDARD
Three poems in a book with this title, found in Fontaine from Version 4.2. They tell of a black ship and a dark-robed figure, a golden castle by a lake with a frightened master, and a red fog from which its guests and master fall. They read as legend rather than record, and the wiki ties the master to Boethius and the bees to Phobos.
