## summary
### LITE
Gurabad, called the City of Kings and the city of brass, was a great ancient city in the southwest of Sumeru's Desert of Hadramaveth. It prospered as King Deshret's favoured vassal, then fell: the shepherd-king Ormazd betrayed the Jinni Liloupar, who took revenge over three generations, ending with much of the city swallowed by a chasm and a plague. Later kings kept trying to restore it. Its ruins are now largely abandoned.
### STANDARD +
Its downfall is told in the book The Tale of Shiruyeh and Shirin and in the quest Memories of Gurabad. Because holding Gurabad later became a way to claim the whole desert, the ruins are woven into many other stories: Tulaytullah, Ay-Khanoum, Makhaira, Faramarz and the Eremites.

## geography
### STANDARD
The ruins lie in the south-west of the Desert of Hadramaveth, in the Great Red Sand. A great gate and a large Ruin Golem of Khaenri'ahn make, identical to the one at Devantaka Mountain, stand among the remains. The Debris of Panjvahe, in the same desert, was a poor district served by a river valley that once irrigated it.
### LARGE +
Most of the original city fell into a deep chasm. Surface ruins and underground chambers hold records, such as the notes called Zandik's Legacy (which mention an Akademiya expedition to Gurabad in the distant past and say parts Zandik found near Ardravi Valley could repair Gurabad's machines) and the inscriptions called Records of Gurabad.

## history
@version: 3.0
### STANDARD
A shepherd, Ormazd, won the favour of King Deshret and the Jinni Liloupar and used her blessing to unite the desert peoples, founding Gurabad after Nabu Malikata's death. Liloupar expected him to end slavery; he kept it. Her sense of betrayal made her plot the city's ruin across three generations. She had her daughter by Ormazd, Shirin, bring poisoned honey to a banquet: Ormazd and Shirin's siblings died, Shirin lost her mind, and her husband Kisra (Liloupar's own son, raised as Ormazd's heir and titled Parvez) became king as Parvezravan. He used Jinn-powered machines made by Shirin to replace slave labour, and the resentment of those left jobless fed the "Brass Mask" rebellion of Khorramdin, in truth Parvezravan and Shirin's banished son Shiruyeh. The Great Revolt followed; Liloupar used Abyssal power, turned Parvezravan into a monster and let much of the city fall into the earth. A plague then killed about half the inhabitants.
### LARGE +
Before the fall the city was declining: deserts spread, oases shrank, harvests failed, and areas like Panjvahe stayed poor. The Council of Nobles refused to pay for infrastructure or welfare, nobles ignored lower-ranking officials such as General Hibehrous, and the clergy blamed weak faith in Deshret. The Great Revolt lasted at least thirteen days. The plague became known as Shiruyeh's Plague and Shiruyeh as the Lord of Pestilence; his name came to mean coward or exile.
### ULTRA +
Gurabad fell before King Deshret's forbidden-knowledge incident. After the original city and Deshret were gone, rising desert leaders rebuilt Gurabad to claim its prestige as the former favoured vassal and style themselves "king of kings".

## later_kings
@version: 3.4
### LARGE
Inscriptions in the Mausoleum of King Deshret and artifact lore outline the later reclamations. Kavusbay claimed to be Shiruyeh's heir; Shah Ramshahr, who seems to have usurped him, drained the people to enrich his capital and raised seven towers near seven cities, among them Ay-Khanoum, Tulaytullah and Saleh. After Ramshahr, his son Artaxir died, Turan Khagan of Saleh was branded a traitor and executed with his sons, and Humayun of Tulaytullah ruled briefly until his death in the Jinn Uprising. Garshasp of Ay-Khanoum moved the capital away and later died; the young noble Goudarz of Tulaytullah then won a victory, renamed himself Saleb Dowleh and restored Gurabad as capital. After ten years of peace his family quarrelled; the Dey Muzaffar of Orghana (now Port Ormos), invited as an ally, betrayed him, burned Gurabad and killed him, then built his capital elsewhere in the desert and never went back.
### ULTRA +
To avoid the same end, Muzaffar told his son Zumurud to have many heirs; Zumurud reportedly had 99 children, including Badanah, Muzzudin and Hanzada Sultan, and the desert kingdoms enjoyed something like a golden age. Badanah, a tyrant, ruled Gurabad through violent conquests and made Tulaytullah and Saleh vassals. Tulaytullah's elite gave him the sword-dancer Makhaira, who fell in love with his son Faramarz and plotted with him to kill Badanah in a staged hunting accident. Faramarz later lost his throne to a conspiracy of city-dwellers and slave-warriors and was exiled to the rainforest. See the Tulaytullah entry for the rest.

## people
### LARGE
Ormazd (shepherd-king), Liloupar (Jinni, partner and avenger), Shirin (daughter and unwitting poisoner), Kisra Parvezravan (Liloupar's son, Shirin's husband, then king) and Shiruyeh (leader of the Brass Mask rebels, also known as Khorramdin). The Jinni Ferigees suspected what Liloupar did, but the people never knew.

## status
### LITE
Gurabad is an abandoned ruin. Some Eremites live in parts of it, and others come on expeditions to hunt for the city's treasures.
### STANDARD +
The ruins hold the Ruin Golem and relics of several failed revivals. Liloupar's long imprisonment is part of its legacy and appears in the Sumeru Archon Quest King Deshret and the Three Magi, where Memories of Gurabad also explains the city's fall. Nothing found up to Version 7.1 reports a later change to the site.

## dangers
### LARGE
Eremites; Ruin Guards and Ruin Golems; unstable ground and the chasm that swallowed the city.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
A wind-scoured wreck of cracked stone and brass, a place the desert folk tell cautionary stories about. The city is remembered as proud and wealthy, and its ruins feel like a warning about broken promises and exploited labour.

## entry: Shirin, Parvezravan and Shiruyeh
@keys: Shiruyeh, Shirin, Parvezravan, Kisra, Khorramdin, Brass Mask
@keys2: Gurabad, Liloupar
@version: 3.4
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
Shirin was Liloupar's daughter by Ormazd and married Liloupar's son Kisra, adopted by Ormazd. After the poisoned banquet she went mad and, under the new king Parvezravan, created mindless Jinn to power his machines. Their son Shiruyeh, banished as a threat, led the Brass Mask rebellion as Khorramdin with Liloupar's backing. In the end Liloupar's Abyssal power turned Parvezravan into a monster and brought down the city; Shirin and Shiruyeh were lost with it. In popular memory Shirin is blamed.

## entry: Badanah, Makhaira and Faramarz
@keys: Badanah, Faramarz, Balash, Goraz, Zumurud, Muzaffar
@keys2: Gurabad, Tulaytullah, Makhaira
@version: 3.4
### ULTRA
Badanah, last tyrant of Gurabad in this chain, was killed in a staged hunting accident by his son Faramarz and Makhaira. Faramarz's reign was unstable: he abdicated to his uncle Balash, was exiled by his uncle Goraz, restored after Goraz died, fought rebels and uncles, and finally poisoned Makhaira with viper venom. Dethroned for good, he went to the rainforest.
