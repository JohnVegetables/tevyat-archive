## summary
### LITE
The Qingxu Pool Underground Ruins are old chambers beneath Qingxu Pool in Lisha, Liyue, reached through a workers' entrance left over from an abandoned excavation. Rumour says treasure lies below. In the 2.7 Story quest "Foregone Conclusion" the Liyue investigator Yelan and the Traveler follow the conspirators Zhiyi and the Fatui ambassador Yusupov down here and expose them.
### STANDARD +
The site is the dramatic climax of Yelan's quest chain, not a place anyone visits in ordinary life. Qingxu Pool itself is a monster-infested ruin field that most people avoid, which is exactly why the conspirators chose it.

## geography
### STANDARD
A stone labyrinth under Qingxu Pool: lifts and gates, flooded passages, sliding walls, rooms with grooves for water, and statues that can be pushed onto light spots. Part of it is a construction workers' emergency access route, built when the earlier excavation was attempted. The deepest chamber is a hidden room used as a meeting place.
### LARGE +
The way down is blocked by water and a sealed gate rather than a single door. Lamps fed by fire orbs lower the water, and once water runs into the grooves the statues light up and can be moved to open the final door. The quest domain uses this as a puzzle, but within the story it is simply how Yelan's party gets past the workers' sealed route. Yelan warns that the ruins are a labyrinth and she cannot promise safety underground.

## history
### STANDARD
Legend, as relayed by the Qingxu Pool wiki, says the ruins predate the Archon War: an evil god ruled the city, was defeated, and the city lay underwater until the war ended. The Liyue Qixing once approved a dig for the rumoured treasure, but the ruins were sealed and the technology to blast through the rock did not exist, so the project stopped. Many groups, including the Fatui, never gave up on the rumour.
### LARGE +
Later, the Qingxu Pool Redevelopment Plan, proposed by the Tianshu candidate Zhiyi, was a cover. In Yelan's investigation (Calculated Gambit), Fatui agents had infiltrated the Blackcliff Forge to learn its ruin-grade blasting recipes, so that they could blow through the floor at night and take whatever lay beneath.

## quests
### STANDARD
In Foregone Conclusion (2.7), Yelan announces Zhiyi as her pick for Tianshu to lull him, has him followed, and learns that Yusupov has left the Fatui embassy alone for Qingxu Pool. Yelan leads the Traveler to a workers' entrance. Below, they overhear Zhiyi drinking with Yusupov and admitting he poisoned his teacher, Uncle Tian, using a slow-acting poison delivered in fish soup. Zhiyi then poisons Yusupov's wine, intending to burn the evidence and collapse the ruins with explosives, with Yusupov's undercover agents shut in the next room. Yelan and her Millelith appear before he can light it. Zhiyi tries to talk his way out, then is taken away. The next morning the physician Baizhu confirms Uncle Tian's poison is gone, with recovery expected to take time.
### LARGE +
Zhiyi planned to pin the loss of the Fatui agents and Yusupov on a rogue Fatui operation to steal the Forge's explosives, then cancel the redevelopment plan himself and stand as a clean Tianshu. He also arranged with Yusupov's second-in-command Theofan, who supplied the poison, to tie up loose ends. Yelan photographed the whole scene with a Kamera as evidence. She says she will make Zhiyi an offer rather than let him face the worst outcome, warning that the Fatui will not treat a traitor kindly if they learn what he did.
### ULTRA +
Zhiyi had been given Snezhnayan poison by the Fatui and adapted it into a slow-acting one, hidden in fish soup, to dose Uncle Tian. Yusupov had told him that a commoner could only reach the Tianshu post by shining in its darkest moment. Yelan's rebuke is that Uncle Tian trusted Zhiyi most and would have given him the chance anyway.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Fire-throwing Pyro Abyss Mages, hilichurl fighters and shooters, a Blazing Axe Mitachurl, young Geovishaps and a Stonehide Lawachurl guard the route, according to the domain page. Water and collapse are further dangers; Zhiyi's explosive charge would have buried everything.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Dim stone halls, dripping water and the glow of fire orbs; behind the sealed gate, the clink of cups and a quiet, self-satisfied conversation that the party overhears.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the ruins have not been revisited in story content found for this entry. The explosives were removed by Yelan's people, and the treasure, if any, is still not proven to exist.

## entry: Qingxu Pool Redevelopment Plan and the Blackcliff Forge Plot
@keys: Qingxu Pool Redevelopment Plan, Qingxu Pool treasure
@keys2: Zhiyi, Yusupov, Yelan, Tianshu, Fatui
@version: 2.7
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
The Qingxu Pool Redevelopment Plan was Zhiyi's proposal while competing for the Tianshu post. Yelan judged it a guise: Fatui infiltrators placed inside the Blackcliff Forge could use its explosives to open the sealed ruins and take the rumoured treasure. Yusupov, the Fatui diplomat in Liyue, was irritated that the treasure hunt took attention from his diplomatic work. Zhiyi planned to cancel the plan after eliminating Yusupov, to look innocent.
