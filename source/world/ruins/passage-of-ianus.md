## summary
### LITE
The Passage of Ianus is an underwater corridor in the drowned city of Remuria (Fontaine). It joins the outer city, Machimos, to the inner city, Capitolium.
### STANDARD
A water current runs through its entire length. Its entrance is Initium Iani, which the Initium Iani page calls the sole path between Machimos and Capitolium, so anyone passing between the outer and inner city of sunken Remuria goes through here. It is part of the Sea of Bygone Eras area and was added in Version 4.6.

## geography
### STANDARD
A single long submerged passage, running from the Machimos side to Capitolium, with a continuous water current along it. It has no other known branches in canon.
### LARGE +
Its entrance, Initium Iani, lies at the Machimos end. Interactable engravings there speak of a slave named Decimus who now lies at the sea bottom like the exalted Harmosts, a bleak levelling of rank by the sea.

## history
### LARGE
Canon gives no separate history for the corridor. It belongs to the Remuria of the Remus era; the Initium Iani page records that the Dragon Prince Scylla was long ago sealed at the entrance by the joint efforts of the Harmosts Boethius and Euergetia, which makes the gate the site of one of Remuria's defining acts.
### ULTRA +
That seal claim is drawn from the Initium Iani page, which cites the quest "The Last Day of Remuria" for Boethius and an engraving in Caesareum Palace for Euergetia. Treat the passage's own ancient role beyond "the way between outer and inner city" as unstated.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Dark, flooded and moving: a current tugs along a stone throat between two halves of a dead city, with the silence and weight of the deep above it.

## travel
### STANDARD
Entered at Initium Iani from the Machimos side. The water current carries travellers along the corridor, which is the only route to Capitolium.

## status
### LITE
Submerged and ruined, still the way between Machimos and Capitolium.
### STANDARD
As of 7.1 nothing in canon changes its state; it remains an accessible underwater route in the Sea of Bygone Eras.

### STANDARD
