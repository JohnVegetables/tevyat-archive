## summary
### LITE
Chizhang Wall is a stretch of ruined rampart and cave shelters in southern Chenyu Vale, north of Mt. Xuanlian. The wiki names it the remains of the ancient city of Chizhang, governed by the Mistress of Chenyu Vale. Painted murals and scattered "Records of the Ancestors" tell of villagers saved by a great adeptus, of jade offerings made at Chiwang, and of a people who once left the Chasm.
### STANDARD +
A Solitary Suanni now haunts the ground. The place matters mostly as an archive: what survives on its walls is the best surviving picture of the vale's ancient people.

## geography
### STANDARD
Chizhang Wall lies in the Southern Mountain part of Chenyu Vale, north of Mt. Xuanlian. It is broken stone wall, ruined foundations and a set of cave shelters in the hillside, some with painted murals. A Solitary Suanni is the area's only notable enemy.
### LARGE +
The caves include a mural cave in the northwest (called Huangcong Grounds), a shelter in the northeast whose key panel was removed, and a southeast shelter whose hidden mural is sealed by a jade device. The achievements "The Mural Veil" and "A Ruined Tale" are earned by cleaning and finding them.

## history
### STANDARD
Fujin says the caves were shelters built by the adepti during the Archon War, so ordinary people caught between floods and fighting would have a safe place. The murals show the people of the vale with their adepti and with the Votive Rainjade rite.
### LARGE +
The Records of the Ancestors describe villagers chased by mountain beasts and rescued by a great and beautiful adeptus; a "divine rite" was approaching, the jade was to be divided so that wicked spirits could not take it, and a part was to be offered at Chiwang so the heavens would hear; the courtyard was sealed on the adeptus's orders. A later fragment speaks of someone painting on this wall as lasting testimony.
### ULTRA +
The hidden southeast mural shows the people of Chenyu Vale leaving the Chasm with a Celestial Nail in the background, tying the vale's founders to the destruction of the Lang-Gan region. The most important section of the northeast mural, showing Fujin and the Herblord, was taken to the Adeptus's Repose; Fujin, seeing it for the first time, could only guess why, suspecting the old mountain mistress's anger toward those who defected.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Moss over old paint, shadow in the caves, a feeling of reading a diary left in the open. The wall is quiet and dignified, closer to a memorial than to a fortress.

## dangers
### STANDARD
A Solitary Suanni patrols the area; the sealed mural cave opens only through the jade mechanism.

## quests
### STANDARD
Fujin shows the Traveler these murals in "An Ancient Sacrifice of Sacred Brocade" (4.4), before they move on toward Chiwang Terrace.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 an abandoned, silent ruin with preserved murals and no inhabitants.

## entry: Murals and Records of the Ancestors
@keys: Records of the Ancestors, Huangcong Grounds, Chizhang murals
@keys2: Chenyu Vale, Fujin, Chizhang Wall
### LARGE
The murals show the people of Chenyu Vale with the Votive Rainjade and with their adepti, and the secret mural shows their departure from the Chasm under a Celestial Nail. The Records of the Ancestors are three groups of ancient text fragments: a rescue by a great adeptus, a jade offering at Chiwang, and a mourning writer who paints on the wall. Treat the fragments as partial, damaged accounts, not full chronicles.
### ULTRA +
Fujin, who built these shelters, had never seen the mural herself, so her reading of its damage is speculative.
