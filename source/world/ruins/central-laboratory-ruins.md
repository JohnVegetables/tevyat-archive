## summary
### LITE
The Central Laboratory Ruins are what remains of the heart of the old Fontaine Research Institute, in the Fontaine Research Institute of Kinetic Energy Engineering Region next to the Liffey Region. Years before the Traveler arrived, an experiment built around the crystal Arkhium exploded here and destroyed most of the Institute. The wreckage now hangs in a distorted gravity state beside large cubes of water floating in the air, and the surviving researchers treat it as a hazard that could blow again.
### STANDARD +
The site is tied to three names. Edwin Eastinghouse, the celebrated and reckless senior researcher, led the Arkhium project here. Raimondo and Choiseul now run the Institute from a new site and handle the ruins. Nacker, Edwin's former associate, was the man who knew how to operate Edwin's machine.

## geography
### STANDARD
The ruins sit high above the surrounding lowland, held in a state of abnormal gravity. Large cubes of water, called Allogravity-Condensed Water Bodies, float in the air around them; they are a by-product of the explosion, and the Institute's own people use them as a way to rise to the ruins by "diving" upward through them. The alternative is to glide in from the highest mountain on the opposite bank.
### LARGE +
Inside lie wrecked laboratory rooms, broken houses and rock, the huge disc-like Experimental Field Generator, and a large lump of Arkhium wired to a suppression system. Residual Arkhium energy lingers in the air and water, and it does not fade quickly. Lower in the region are the Executive Office, the Academic Assembly Hall, the Materials Office and Mary-Ann Memorial Park, all of which were battered by the first shock waves.

## places
### LARGE
Central Laboratory (the ruined core): in the Institute's own map text, the laboratory sat at the centre of the campus, held the most advanced instruments in Fontaine and was chiefly occupied with the Experimental Field Generator.
Experimental Field Generator: a huge gravity-altering machine, now powerless except for backup cores.
Large Arkhium mass: the energy source kept dormant by an inhibitor with condensing components.
Allogravity-Condensed Water Bodies: floating water cubes that carry residual energy flow.
### ULTRA +
Nearby, Mary-Ann Memorial Park took the first blast wave and lost most of its buildings; Nacker hoped some paper assets had survived there. A stone tablet in the region lists the deeds of ancient Marechaussee Hunters, an unrelated older layer of Fontaine's hidden history.

## people
### STANDARD
Raimondo and Choiseul lead the rebuilding from the New Fontaine Research Institute; Raimondo is the technician who handled the disaster evaluation, Choiseul the speech-making administrator. Nacker, once Edwin's associate, guides the Traveler to the ruins in the World Quest and then disappears. The reporter Chronie of The Steambird follows the expedition. Edwin himself was dismissed from the Institute for long unauthorised absence after he vanished.

## history
### LITE
The Institute's Arkhium Research Project aimed to use Arkhium, a powerful crystal energy source, to counter gravity and perhaps lift Fontaine into a "city in the sky" so people could survive the prophesied flood. An overload of the project's Arkhium Kinetic Core caused a huge explosion that destroyed most of the Institute and left the Central Laboratory in ruins.
### STANDARD +
Edwin Eastinghouse led the project with collaborators including Nacker and Raimondo. He built the Experimental Field Generator, which makes a Gravity Reduction Field. He and Raimondo created the Arkhium Kinetic Core to power it, together with a suppression device. After the blast, nearly every Arkhium crystal in the region was shattered into fragments that could still react to each other by resonance, so the Institute used the Generator's gravity field to gather and hold them beneath the ruins, and placed Survey Anchors to monitor radiation. Arkhium mining under the Fortress of Meropide was ended.
### LARGE +
Edwin was believed dead, but later quests show him alive and still enthusiastic about Arkhium. By his own account he was inspecting other departments when it blew. He had re-established a lab in the Morte Region, found backers, and was eventually discovered and brought back; he and Nacker were then dismissed and sent to the Fortress of Meropide. The Institute officially kept him on the books for a time and then dismissed him for being absent without permission.
### ULTRA +
Nacker's rival story and Raimondo's are both told within the World Quest. Nacker treats Edwin's fall as a lost chance at the Director post and complains of Choiseul and Raimondo; Raimondo blames him for stripping the Institute's assets and for the later sabotage.

## quests
### STANDARD
The World Quest 'Fontaine Research Institute, Stagnating in the Rubble' (4.1, part of the Fontaine Research Institute Chronicles) takes the Traveler through an absurdly bureaucratic qualification review at the New Fontaine Research Institute, then up to the ruins with Nacker and the reporter Chronie to lower the Generator's gravity field.
### LARGE +
At the ruins Nacker prepares to turn the dormant Arkhium into a three-state form with Raimondo's catalyst. A second explosion rocks the site while the Institute leaders pose for a photo. Raimondo and the Traveler return, retrieve condensing components for the inhibitor, and the Generator, fed temporarily by two Pneumousia cores, moves on its own and must be shut down. The Arkhium is stabilised to an inert state. Raimondo concludes that Nacker stole the Arkhium Kinetic Core and overloaded the big lump as a cover for his escape, and asks the Traveler to keep quiet about it; the Traveler may instead tell Chronie everything. Choiseul declares that the site will be preserved and rebuilt as a safety education centre.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the ruins are stable but dangerous, kept under watch by the Institute, and the Arkhium is inert. No later version is known to have changed them.
### STANDARD +
Nacker's whereabouts were left open at the end of the World Quest; later content has him and Edwin dismissed and held at the Fortress of Meropide. The Institute itself works from a new campus.

## dangers
### LARGE
Out-of-control clockwork meka (Ousia and Pneuma types) keep reactivating when Arkhium energy flows, Hydro Slimes and tainted water-spouting phantasms appear around the condensing components, and the Experimental Field Generator can still move if powered. Direct exposure to active Arkhium causes nausea, vomiting, bleeding under the skin and even coma; the Traveler and Paimon show unusual tolerance. Swimming into the energised water cubes at the wrong moment is lethal, according to Raimondo.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Blue light, cold stone, water hanging in the air, silence broken by humming machinery and the click of aggrieved meka. It feels like a failed ideal left to rot, with a faint smell of scorched metal. The in-game viewpoint text calls it the wreck of shattered dreams and warns that some people see its energy as having another purpose.

## entry: Experimental Field Generator
@keys: Experimental Field Generator, Gravity Reduction Field, Prototype Cal. Breguet
@version: 4.1
### STANDARD
A huge device invented by Edwin Eastinghouse to create a Gravity Reduction Field; early estimates said it could lift a block of buildings. It is powered by the Arkhium Kinetic Core, which Edwin and Raimondo developed along with a suppression system. After the explosion the Institute uses it to gather and hold shattered Arkhium. Nacker was personally trained by Edwin to operate it. Its backup cores are Pneumousia cores; when Raimondo draws on them, the machine moves and attacks the Traveler.

## entry: Allogravity-Condensed Water Bodies
@keys: Allogravity-Condensed Water Body, Allogravity-Condensed Water Bodies, anti-gravity diving
@version: 4.1
### STANDARD
Large cubes of water floating above the ruins, created by the explosion. The Institute treats them as a travel route: one can dive upward through them. After the second blast their energy flow grows turbulent and dangerous.

## entry: Arkhium Kinetic Core
@keys: Arkhium Kinetic Core
@version: 4.1
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
A concentrated power source built around a large piece of Arkhium, used to run the Experimental Field Generator. When it overloaded, the first explosion happened. In the World Quest Raimondo claims that the core was stolen by Nacker, after which a second, smaller blast followed.

## entry: Nacker and the Second Explosion
@keys: Nacker
@keys2: Central Laboratory, Arkhium, Experimental Field Generator, Fontaine Research Institute
@version: 4.1
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
Nacker is Edwin's former associate. He guides the Traveler through the review and ascent, and calls himself the one person who can run the Generator. After he stays behind alone to start it, a blast hits, and Raimondo says Nacker has taken the Arkhium Kinetic Core and overloaded the large Arkhium on purpose as a distraction. Nacker is not seen again in the quest. Raimondo says he would prefer to believe the man died, but his own analysis suggests that someone gathered undepleted Arkhium and piled it there to cover up traces of what had been taken.
