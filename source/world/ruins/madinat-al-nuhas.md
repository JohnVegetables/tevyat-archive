## summary
### LITE
Madinat al-Nuhas is an underground Khaenri'ahn complex beneath the Samudra Coast in the northwest of Sumeru's desert, once used to test weapons, above all Ruin Machines. It is powered by Azosite like Hangeh Afrasiyab and contains an unfinished prototype Ruin Golem. The Traveler reaches it in As the Khvarena's Light Shows: Nirodha.
### STANDARD +
Nasejuna calls it the proving ground of the Dahrians, and an order found inside shows it was used to hold off the monsters of the cataclysm.

## geography
@version: 3.6
### STANDARD
The entrance is at the west end of Asipattravana Swamp, behind vines marked by a Mysterious Amrita Seal and a mechanical door. The facility extends under Samudra Coast, a sandy shore with clear blue water on the far northwest border of the Sumeru desert. Its interior shares the industrial, brass-coloured feel of the Ruin Golems and the Azosite factory.
### LARGE +
The ruin has a control centre with relays, a left-hand door that leads to the proving ground, and a chamber with a Ruin Cannon. The Golem is an incomplete prototype, and the Great Song of Khvarena is said to lie within it.

## history
@version: 3.6
### STANDARD
A testing facility for Ruin Machines in the days of Khaenri'ah. According to Nasejuna's copied records, it was later altered into fortifications from which monsters could be attacked, but this saw little use. A surviving order from Garrison Commander Hadura of the Schwanenritter reads that workshops and similar areas were unsuited to the large-scale operations of the Mechanical Wardens, so he commands the self-defence systems to be activated and survivors evacuated to the surface before resistance continues.
### LARGE +
Azosite energy blocks power the machines; the Traveler must find and install them to bring the facility to life. Nasejuna says the Schwanenritter owned this proving ground and the Azosite factory at Hangeh Afrasiyab alike.

### ULTRA +
Nasejuna points out the oddity that the Dahrians, who set off the defiling disaster, built machines to fight monsters; the quest leaves it unanswered. Engineering notes at Hangeh Afrasiyab add that Azosite alloys were meant to clear the energy-efficiency bottleneck of the large Mechanical Wardens, which links power research there to the machines tested here.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Metal corridors, brass-coloured machinery and old relays: a place built to test weapons rather than to live in.

## dangers
### LARGE
Ruin Guards, Ruin Cruisers, Ruin Destroyers, Ruin Defenders and a Ruin Drake: Skywatch rise in waves when the proving ground's systems are woken. The Golem prototype's core must be destroyed with the Ruin Cannon.

## quests
@version: 3.6
### STANDARD
As the Khvarena's Light Shows: Nirodha is one of the three subquests of the series Khvarena of Good and Evil. Nasejuna and Sorush lead the Traveler and Paimon through the gate, and by powering the circuits, defeating the defence waves and firing the Ruin Cannon the party reaches the Great Song of Khvarena stored in the Golem. The quest earns the achievement Behold My Righteous Strike! at one stage.
### LARGE +
Nasejuna says it is his first time inside a Dahrian ruin; he has the notes called Rene's Investigation Notes as his guide. A Luxurious Chest can be found after the Golem is opened.

## status
### LITE
Abandoned and quiet. Nothing found up to Version 7.1 changes the site.

## entry: Hadura's Order
@keys: Hadura, Soiled Orders, Mechanical Wardens
@keys2: Madinat al-Nuhas, Schwanenritter
@version: 3.6
### STANDARD
A tattered note on the left side of the proving ground chamber is an order from Garrison Commander Hadura of the Schwanenritter. It tells the reader to activate the self-defence systems and quickly evacuate the survivors upward while the Mechanical Wardens, the Khaenri'ahn name for the Ruin Golems, carry on the fight.
