## summary
### LITE
The Orthant of Wishes is one of the four Seals of Chymical Marriage left by the Narzissenkreuz Ordo, hidden in the Mysterious Ruins of the Fort Charybdis Ruins in the Morte Region of Fontaine. Its slate calls it "the Orthant of Wishes, the Temple of Wishes, the Room of Wishes", and the riddle's answer is life. The Wish piece it held was already taken when the Traveler came; the quest is Meteoric Lance. On the second floor an Ordo member's diary records the Great Ritual.
### STANDARD +
Added in 4.2. An unconscious man at the door is taken to Lumidouce Harbor for treatment before the Traveler goes on.

## geography
### STANDARD
Entered through the Mysterious Ruins at Fort Charybdis, a multi-floor complex with a lift, gates, lifting columns and an underwater area beneath the main machine in the central room. A Looking Glass on one floor leads to a side chamber with a Thundercraven Rifthound and a chest; another Looking Glass is on the third floor.
### LARGE +
A Seelie must be led to its court to open access to the underwater section. The riddle slate, the diary and the hymn excerpt lie on or near the second floor.

## places
### LARGE
The entrance with the fallen man; the Riddle Slate; the second-floor excerpt and diary; the central machine room and flooded lower section; the Seelie Court puzzle; two Looking Glass rooms.

## people
### STANDARD
Named in the diary: the Master (Narzissenkreuz), Mr. Ingold (whom the wiki identifies with Jakob Ingold), Ms. Calafia, Mr. Forsamiro, Madam Laveaux, Ms. Lenormand, Professor Griffin L. Randolph and Ms. Zenaida. In the Search in the Algae Sea version the Traveler comes with Ann, Caterpillar and Seymour.
### LARGE +
The diarist calls Ms. Calafia not the sharpest tool but still a disciple of someone (the line is cut off); she questions Mr. Forsamiro about the stone slates and is calmed by Madam Laveaux and Ms. Lenormand, though the diarist expects word to reach the Iudex. Ms. Zenaida led the group who left the Ordo, "disappointed with the future".

## governance
### LARGE
The diary shows an organisation that works through official channels: Mr. Ingold arranges plans for a construction project, the Palais Mermonia confirms the site, and Marechaussee Hunters take charge of security. The Ordo is careful to report its deeds first and to use a cover story, with Professor Randolph's social standing, to buy time.

## history
### LARGE
The diary says the Ordo really did find Remuria's ruins at the indicated location. Workers began spreading uneasy stories, and members of the Ordo suffered mentally and were helped. When the work was done, Mr. Ingold, speaking for the Master, thanked the workers; the Master was said to be performing the Great Ritual, tied to celestial bodies and the Primal Fire. Zenaida's group left after her own divination worried her. The diarist's own divinations show the Master alive within the tower.
### ULTRA +
The final entries turn dark: the writer, curious, divines where those who left went and finds that they are still within the tower, which contradicts their note about leaving. The writer resolves to speak to Mr. Ingold in person.

## quests
### STANDARD
Meteoric Lance: find the seal, study the Riddle Slate, look for a way forward, solve the Orthant puzzle and obtain the Wish piece. The riddle ("Often contradicting... born in an instant, your company in eternal rest, never separated... the tainted fruits of sin") is answered "life": Caterpillar says the Ordo regards Fontainians as sinners bearing original sin.
### LARGE +
The quest's name probably refers to shooting stars, which people have long associated with wishes (the wiki's note).
