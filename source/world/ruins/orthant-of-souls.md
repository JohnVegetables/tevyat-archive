## summary
### LITE
The Orthant of Souls is one of the four Seals of Chymical Marriage set by the Narzissenkreuz Ordo, placed in Mysterious Ruins on the east slopes of Mont Automnequi in the Morte Region of Fontaine. Its riddle slate names it "the Orthant of Souls, the Shrine of Souls, the Room of Souls", and the answer to its riddle is love. By the time the Traveler arrives the Soul piece has already been removed. The quest is Cupid's Lover.
### STANDARD +
Added in 4.2. The ruins have a riddle slate with a pocket watch nearby, an elaborate mechanism puzzle with Looking Glass rooms, a lift and an underwater section.

## geography
### STANDARD
Reached from the east slopes of Mont Automnequi. The ruins have an upper floor and a lower floor, linked by a lift, with a flooded underwater section reached by lowering the lift.
### LARGE +
A Looking Glass room lets the Traveler light torches; a windowsill holds a Special Key that opens a locked door to the last energy-flow device. When energy flow is released in the underwater area, the surrounding ground becomes dry.

## places
### LARGE
The Riddle Slate at the entrance; the mechanism whose top is found empty; three energy-flow devices; the Looking Glass room; the underwater chamber.
### ULTRA +
A 4-star Nymph's Constancy weapon can be picked up beside the Riddle Slate (gameplay note).

## people
### STANDARD
Jakob Ingold and his unseen allies; the Traveler with Ann, Caterpillar and Seymour.

## culture
### LARGE
The hymn excerpt comes from the "Song of Serenity", Movement 2, Stanza 84. It says people share one resilient skin and the same divine essence in their veins, as there is one wisdom and one melody, so their wills are one; they should draw close, "even unto outlanders". The wiki links the verse's "one melody" to Phobos, but the stanza itself names no god.

## history
### LARGE
The Ordo placed the seal in a Mysterious Ruin. Caterpillar notes that "the birth of reason requires the merging of the four orthants". In the Search in the Algae Sea version he notes that someone else has already solved the riddle.
### ULTRA +
The riddle ("I accompany you in eternal rest"... "Born in an instant, often contradicting, always united"... "I am life's most cherished treasure") has the answer love. Paimon's guesses are Mora and then "wealth"; Seymour finds a match in an old novel that ends with love lying nestled in the marrow; Ann connects it to her own pocket watch.

## quests
### STANDARD
Cupid's Lover: find the seal, study the Riddle Slate, look for a way forward, solve the Orthant puzzle and obtain the Soul piece. The quest's title is thought to refer to Psyche, goddess of the soul and consort of Cupid (the wiki's note). It has two versions depending on whether Search in the Algae Sea was started first.
### LARGE +
Enemies met include Recon Log Mek - Ousia and Geological Survey Mek - Ousia in the upper rooms and Recon Log Mek - Pneuma underwater.

## status
### LITE
Opened and emptied. Nothing newer than 4.2 is recorded.

## dangers
### LARGE
Hostile mek constructs and flooded passages.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
A cold stone shrine where a lone pocket watch lies on the ground, with the sense that someone was here just before.
