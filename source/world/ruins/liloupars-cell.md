## summary
### LITE
Liloupar's Cell is a ruined prison in Safhe Shatranj, in the Desert of Hadramaveth, where the most dominant fragment of the Jinni Liloupar was kept. Its viewpoint calls it a prison that has become an oasis: a green spot with a big tree and a river. In The Temple Where Sand Flows Like Tears the Traveler and Jeht find a bottle here, and when it is touched the plants wither and Liloupar's voice speaks from it.
### STANDARD +
Liloupar is the Jinni who destroyed Gurabad in revenge across three generations. King Deshret split her into seven fragments and had divine sages hide them in the Great Red Sand; this cell held the strongest one.

## geography
@version: 3.4
### STANDARD
A small ruin opposite the square of the Gurabad temple, in the canyon the Tanit call the Great Rift. Before the bottle is touched it is a lush spot with a big tree beside a river. A viewpoint here is named Liloupar's Gaol. Afterward its look changes and the oasis is withered.
### LARGE +
It is one of several places in Safhe Shatranj, which also holds a chessboard field the desert tribes call by that name, scattered obelisks with inscriptions, and ruined temples. Researchers think Safhe Shatranj was once a training field for ancient war machines.

## people
@version: 3.4
### STANDARD
Liloupar speaks from the bottle as the Mother of the Jinn, a title she mocks. She is proud, theatrical and sharp-tongued, and calls Jeht a sand-licking lackey before warming to her. She says she could take any shape to please her master if her soul were not sundered. She offers her true name to the Traveler, whom she calls her lord, and declines to serve the Tanit matriarch.
### LARGE +
Jeht recognises the name from tribal books and fears the contract, since a Jinni's true name lets a human command her and the tales say the Jinn eventually take vengeance. Liloupar scoffs that hate is too strong a word, as one does not hate a poisonous insect or toad, and says she likes the Traveler. Her fragment is a pair of orbs in a floating bottle in the present day.

## history
@version: 3.4
### STANDARD
Liloupar was born from water lilies in the streams that followed Nabu Malikata's footsteps, lived in Ay-Khanoum, and served King Deshret after Nabu Malikata died. She aided the shepherd Ormazd in founding Gurabad, fell in love with him, and when he became a tyrant she cursed his house with three generations of ruin. The city finally fell to an abyssal calamity. Deshret punished her by splitting her soul.
### LARGE +
The cell therefore belongs to the aftermath of the fall of Gurabad. Jeht has read in tribal books of a wicked spirit named Liloupar who drowned Ormazd and his household in honey; the fuller account is in the Gurabad entry. Other fragments turn up elsewhere, as at Mt. Damavand and through the chessboard of Safhe Shatranj.

### ULTRA +
Other names for her appear in the texts. She is called the Mother of the Despicable in a quest, Daughter of the Water Lily in the tale of Shiruyeh and Shirin, and Jinn-Mother in a record of the ancient oases. The wiki notes that the spelling Liloufar survives in that book while newer texts use Liloupar.

## dangers
### LARGE
A Primal Construct guardian, Anoushbord - Forgotten Mechanical Guardian, attacks as the party reaches the bottle. Beyond the cell, in Pairidaeza Canyon, the party meets an opposing band of Eremites.

## quests
@version: 3.4
### STANDARD
The Temple Where Sand Flows Like Tears (The Dirge of Bilqis, part 2) takes the Traveler here. After the bottle is claimed, the series continues with Dune-Entombed Fecundity, where more of her soul is collected, and Memories of Gurabad, where Liloupar parts from the party to face the Abyssal power she once released. The bottle stays with the Traveler as a gadget that does what it did when Liloupar was inside.

## status
### LITE
Withered and quiet. Liloupar's fragment no longer lies here, and the bottle that held it stayed with the Traveler.

## entry: Liloupar's Fragments
@keys: Liloupar's fragment, Mother of the Jinn, Liloupar's soul, Jinni in the Magic Bottle
@keys2: Liloupar, Jinni
@version: 3.4
### STANDARD
King Deshret split the Jinni into seven fragments after the fall of Gurabad, and divine sages hid them in the Great Red Sand. The Traveler gathers them across The Dirge of Bilqis and Memories of Gurabad, and her magic bottle becomes a gadget with the benefits she once gave.

## entry: Liloupar's Later Fate
@keys: Liloupar, Your Past Is Like a Garden, A Divergence Leading to Oneself
@keys2: Collei, Nabu Malikata, Jinni
@version: 6.6
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
By the events of A Divergence Leading to Oneself (Luna VII, Version 6.6) Liloupar is awake again and calms the Jinn and sandstorms the Fatui disturbed before travelling with the Traveler's group across Sumeru. In Your Past Is Like a Garden she asks Collei, who carries remnants of Nabu Malikata, to let her absorb them; Collei agrees, and Liloupar fades away to rest content.
