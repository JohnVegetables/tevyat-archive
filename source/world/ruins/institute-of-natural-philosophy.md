## summary
### LITE
The Institute of Natural Philosophy is a dilapidated underwater research facility that once belonged to an organisation of the same name led by Director Dwight Lasker. Much of its research is still preserved in the flooded ruin, including precursors of modern Fontainian technologies and the earliest experiments of the Narzissenkreuz Ordo. The Traveler first reaches it in the Version 4.0 World Quest Aqueous Tidemarks.
### STANDARD +
Its notes show a scientific community that was proud, quarrelsome and worried: young prodigies, a secret group forming among its leaders, and a warning of toxins and tainted Oceanids after a great disaster.

## geography
### STANDARD
Submerged ruins in the Court of Fontaine Region, divided into Outskirts, a Central Quartier and a Northern Quartier, with a deeper underground wing. The Traveler enters by diving and finds sealed doors, ancient logs, glass walls and laboratory rooms.
### LARGE +
Mechanisms in the ruins include high-pressure water vents, current streams and pneumousia blocks and relays. Seelie, Fontemer seagrass and xenochromatic creatures can be found.

## places
### LARGE
Outskirts: where the Traveler and Virgil enter after the Liliacruces Forum site.
Central Quartier: with the underground area holding the Ancient Notes, a Hydroculus and a Ruin Guard.
Northern Quartier: further research areas.
Director's office: a locked place whose key Dwight left in a chest in the Narzissenkreuz Ordo; a few rifthounds were found inside.
### ULTRA +
In the vault, the Traveler meets a Ruin Grader called "Special" Ruin Grader, nicknamed the "Somewhat Uncooperative Research Subject", which regenerates when almost defeated.

## people
### STANDARD
Director Dwight Lasker; his childhood friends Basil Elton, Emanuel Guillotin and Karl Ingold; the young prodigies he recruited, including Coppelius, Alain Guillotin and Alain's assistant Carter Scherbius; and Rene de Petrichor and Jakob Ingold, who joined Alain's lab and later founded the Narzissenkreuz Ordo. In the present, the guide Virgil leads the Traveler.

## governance
### STANDARD
A Director ran the Institute and decided funding and admission. Dwight's notes show him approving research, increasing investment, assigning researchers to labs and receiving reports about members.

## history
### LITE
The Institute was a research body led by Dwight Lasker. During a cataclysm about five hundred years ago Dwight closed it for the safety of its people and kept the young from going to fight, urging post-disaster reconstruction first.
### STANDARD +
After the flood period began, he picked land near the Fountain of Lucine to relocate, only to hear that an opera house would be built there. He later met a newly appointed Iudex and judged him far more reliable than "Leon and those other clowns". Alain introduced Rene and Jakob Ingold, who turned out to be Karl's adopted sons; Dwight enrolled them and assigned them to Alain's lab despite the unrest it caused among other researchers.
### LARGE +
Alain's research on clockwork meka and Khaenri'ah war machines led to the invention of Arkhe energy systems, called Pneumousia energy, which Dwight called the greatest breakthrough in a century. Rene then asked to run a separate study on the Abyss, using Durin's blood, Elynas's remains and Dark Mud, to fight toxins left in Fontaine's water; Dwight approved it, since deformed Oceanids were appearing. Dwight also received a report that the leaders were forming a group around a "Master" and tasked his friend Emanuel to investigate.
### ULTRA +
A line in the Ordo's history says Rene gathered followers from this Institute, with Jakob performing tricks to convince people to join the new Ordo. Alain and Mary-Ann Guillotin refused to join. Dwight's own fate is not recorded.

## culture
### STANDARD
The surviving texts mix science with legend, for example the origin of the Oceanids from Egeria's tear. The Institute valued child prodigies; Dwight says it is its responsibility to support gifted and talented youths.

## quests
### STANDARD
Aqueous Tidemarks (4.0): the Traveler follows Virgil to find the Liliacruces Forum's treasure, discovers the Institute, defeats clockwork meka and a Ruin Guard, solves a key puzzle, and fights a "special" Ruin Grader in a vault before breaking a glass wall and following a current.
### LARGE +
The wiki lists the Institute as the subarea of the 4.2 World Quest Search in the Algae Sea, part of the Narzissenkreuz questline.

## status
### LITE
Still flooded and abandoned. Its documents remain, and its oldest dangers are guarded by meka.
### STANDARD +
Nothing newer than 4.2 is known to have changed it.

## dangers
### LARGE
Hydro Slimes, Area Alert Mek - Ousia, armored and sternshield crabs, Hunter's Rays, a Ruin Guard, a Ruin Grader, and non-respawning Thundercraven Rifthound Whelps.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Cold blue light, silt and ruined laboratory rooms. The viewpoint text calls the place "Sunken Glory". It feels like a proud institution caught mid-argument.

## entry: Ancient Natural History Excerpts (Oceanids)
@keys: Oceanid origin, Helixsplit
@keys2: Institute of Natural Philosophy
@version: 4.0
### STANDARD
Excerpts of a natural-history text say that, by legend, Egeria shed the first tear and from it the first Oceanid was born, with the task of understanding all life. Oceanids differ greatly in intelligence and multiply by a mysterious "Helixsplit" after seeking permission from the Hydro Archon or an elder. A sceptical addendum notes that physical evidence suggests the Oceanids predate both Egeria and Remuria, and asks whether anyone dares explore their true origin.
