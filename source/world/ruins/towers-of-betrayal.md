## summary
### LITE
The Towers of Betrayal are huge ancient obelisks scattered across the Great Red Sand in Sumeru. The desert dwellers call them symbols of shame and say they have stood since the death of King Deshret, a reminder that the desert kings broke their oath to him. Their real history is forgotten.
### STANDARD
Those in the Hypostyle Desert, Land of Lower Setekh and Land of Upper Setekh stand on the surface but must be opened from within through the desert's cave networks. Those in the Desert of Hadramaveth are partly or fully buried and are entered at the main entrance. Each has a ground-level room with a blue crystal, and some have a basement reached by an elevator. A recorded memory of the priest Kasala, shown in Secret of the Scorching Desert, speaks of temples built by King Deshret's priests and filled with Greater Lord Rukkhadevata's power of life.
### LARGE +
The surface towers are tied to the Hidden Exploration Objective The Three Obelisks of Necrovalley.

## geography
@version: 3.1
### STANDARD
Towers stand in the Hypostyle Desert (northwest of the Mausoleum of King Deshret), the Land of Lower Setekh (south of Abdju Pit), the Land of Upper Setekh (west of the Valley of Dahri), and in the Desert of Hadramaveth (northwest of Khaj-Nisut, northwest of Safhe Shatranj, in Debris of Panjvahe, and in Wadi Al-Majuj).
### LARGE +
The three surface towers are reached from underground zones: Abdju Road: Upper Ruin for Lower Setekh, Thutmose's Secret Base for Upper Setekh, and a rock cavern upper level northwest of the Mausoleum for Hypostyle Desert.

## places
### LARGE
- Hypostyle Desert, Lower Setekh and Upper Setekh towers: each needs Clearance level 1 to activate the crystal, open the main entrance and revive the oasis in front. Each basement holds a particle-collecting Time Trial Challenge with invisible platforms.
- Hadramaveth tower northwest of Khaj-Nisut: no accessible basement.
- Tower northwest of Safhe Shatranj: a complex Cascade Pool puzzle and the Records of the Ancient Oases Part II in the basement.
- Tower in Debris of Panjvahe: a Verity Cell puzzle with Ruins Torches on the entrance level and Part VII of the Records; a Ruin Grader in the basement.
- Tower in Wadi Al-Majuj: nothing notable; it is the setting of a fight in the World Quest For Her Judgment Reaches to the Skies... and does not play the special soundtrack of the other towers.

## history
@version: 3.1
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Desert tradition, quoted in the Archive, holds that the obelisks have stood since King Deshret died and shame the desert kings who broke faith with him. The Archive adds that they may be older than those short-lived kingdoms, and the real story is lost.
### LARGE +
In Secret of the Scorching Desert, Kasala's recorded memory says that forbidden knowledge brought into the world by the Scarlet King spread like a plague, that Greater Lord Rukkhadevata summoned the priests to build temples and infused them with the power of life, and that the king finally sacrificed himself. He also says he spent his life guarding one such temple. The wiki reads the towers shown in the cutscene as these temples, which canon text does not itself confirm.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Colossal stone shafts rise out of dunes or sit half-swallowed by sand. Inside is a cold chamber with a glowing blue crystal. Locals regard them with shame rather than awe, and their silence is heavy.

## dangers
### LARGE
Some are guarded by ruin machines or contain puzzles and invisible-platform challenges; the Hadramaveth towers lie among Eremite and Primal Construct country. Tribes consider the ruins ill-omened.

## status
### STANDARD
As of 7.1 the towers still stand, three can be reactivated by the Traveler, and no later canon changes their meaning.

## entry: Kasala
@keys: Kasala
@keys2: priest, temple, Deshret
@version: 3.1
@spoiler: minor
### LARGE
Kasala was one of King Deshret's last priests. In the cutscene Lost Legacies in the Sand, discovered in Secret of the Scorching Desert, his recorded memory describes forbidden knowledge as a plague, the priests building temples under Rukkhadevata's instruction, and the Scarlet King's self-sacrifice. He closes by urging the children of the desert to let go of old grievances and remember this kindness.
### ULTRA +
The wiki says Kasala later guarded the Mysterious Ruins, a quest-only domain, where he was entombed. Alhaitham finds his message there. That makes Kasala's guarded temple a different site from these towers.

## entry: The Three Obelisks of Necrovalley
@keys: Three Obelisks of Necrovalley, Necrovalley
@keys2: obelisk, Towers of Betrayal
@version: 3.1
### LARGE
A Hidden Exploration Objective (unofficial name) about activating the three surface towers in the Hypostyle Desert, Land of Lower Setekh and Land of Upper Setekh, in any order. Each climb involves puzzles with primal light and ends in a Time Trial Challenge. Completing it grants three Precious Chests and an achievement. The towers in Hadramaveth are separate from this objective.
