## summary
### LITE
Downriver is an abandoned fishing village in Minlin, Liyue, a little west of Wangshu Inn. Its remaining houses are empty and falling apart. The village matters in canon as the place that took in the orphaned Beidou as a small child and later drove her out, long before she became captain of the Crux.
### STANDARD +
It is a Point of Interest rather than a settlement anyone lives in. Its name was first spoken in Beidou's hangout quest "Life on the High Seas" (the story event "When the Crux Shines Bright"); before that, the place appeared on the map and through two signboard messages left by former residents.

## geography
### STANDARD
In Minlin, west of Wangshu Inn and visible from the inn. A scatter of old, weathered fishing houses; the village chief's former house is the best-known of them.

## history
### LITE
Fishing families moved here from an older village called Upriver, bringing generations of fishing know-how. Beidou arrived as a homeless child of about five or six and was taken in by the village chief. Roughly a year or two later the chief died of illness during winter, the catch and harvest shrank, and the villagers blamed her and drove her out. Over time everyone else left too.
### STANDARD +
The name Downriver came from this link to Upriver; Beidou says Upriver is long gone and that soon nobody may remember either name. In her telling, the village was never impressive but was lively and home to several families, and she learned a lot about fishing from them.

Her account of her arrival: she had been wandering the streets, found half a Rice Bun and was robbed of it by a stray dog, and chased it into this neighbourhood. Fishermen who saw her stop her and fed her. The dog collapsed and did not get up again, with the bun still in its mouth. The villagers were wary of the dirty child, but the chief took pity on her and brought her to their home.
### LARGE +
When the chief fell ill and died, the harvest was already worsening and the fishermen's catch was dwindling. Without the chief to settle things, neighbours began blaming one another, with rumours that some families had been overfishing. In the end they turned on Beidou, calling her bad luck and pointing to the dog's death on her first day, then the chief's death, then the fish dying out. She told them she was not a curse, only Beidou. They shouted that Nandou controls life and Beidou controls death, and chased her away. She says that was when she learned her name was a constellation, and that Alcor, one of its stars, was an omen of death.
### ULTRA +
Beidou shares this with the Traveler during the hangout, and recounts it with wry humour rather than bitterness. Told the Traveler might be cursed too (the Anemo and Geo Gnoses were both taken soon after arriving), she laughs it off and offers to share the blame, which the hangout presents as the two of them being "scapegoats looking out for each other". This is hangout material: it is canon but was told to the Traveler in private, so ordinary Liyue residents are not assumed to know the story.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Silent and sun-bleached: sagging roofs, empty doorways, water close by and no voices. Beidou calls it a place that has "certainly seen better days".

## places
### STANDARD
Two signboards are the only written traces of the villagers. One, addressed "To Zhong", says fishing has grown harder, sales have dropped, and the villagers have decided to leave as a group and look for work elsewhere. Another message, also to Zhong, complains that the writer's letters were never answered and says the girl has left some things for Zhong in the usual place, by the main house.
### LARGE +
The "girl" in the second message is not identified in the text. Players commonly connect these notes to Beidou's story, but canon does not say that the girl is Beidou, nor who Zhong is; see uncertainties.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Nothing specific in canon beyond decay. Treat it as a quiet, safe, melancholy stop on the road near Wangshu Inn.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 Downriver is still empty and crumbling. No later event is recorded there.

### STANDARD
Beidou, captain of the Crux, was a homeless child of about five or six when she was taken in at Downriver by the village chief. After the chief died and the fish dwindled, the villagers blamed her and drove her out, using the saying that Nandou controls life and Beidou death. She revisits the ruined village with the Traveler in "Life on the High Seas".
