## summary
### LITE
The Old Sanatorium Site is a former mountain sanatorium on Windrest Peak, torn down a few years ago. It stands above the ruins of a temple left behind when the Temple of Space was lifted into the heavens, and its Anemo Archon statue is the gateway up to that temple. Locals say that whoever visits the sanatorium stays in the enchanted hills forever.
### STANDARD +
The site is kept by its doorman Mann, who oversees demolition and heritage preservation. A cemetery of patients' graves lies inside the ruins.

## geography
### STANDARD
The sanatorium lies up the mountain from Dornman Port. Its corridors and rooms are half collapsed and full of dust-covered beds and abandoned things. A cemetery with patients' tombstones lies within, and Dandelion Seed grows on the grounds.
### LARGE +
Weasels burrow in the broken buildings, and a lizard can startle a visitor on an old bed. The statue of the Anemo Archon at the site is a gateway: investigating it, as Nihilita directs, opens a path into the sky above Windrest Peak.

## places
### STANDARD
Rooms with beds and a stack of notes left by patients, and the graveyard at the back with portrait-bearing stones.
### LARGE +
Three patient letters are on a stack of paper. Old Bauer gives his bought burial plot and headstone to Koche on leaving the sanatorium after making peace with his family; Koche, a miner changing careers, then offers it to any other patient who wants it; and Totengrabov, a stonecarver, offers free inscriptions in Liyue, Mondstadt or Inazuman style, recognised by the Wangsheng Funeral Parlor and the Church of Favonius.

## people
### STANDARD
Mann is the elderly doorman who guards the site. He says he oversees demolition work and heritage preservation, worries that the building is being left to rot, and is followed everywhere by a dog named Hauptmann.
### LARGE +
Zeitlind, whose elder sister Raumhilda wrote that the sanatorium was closing and that she was going far away, comes to the site seeking her and to say goodbye. She is startlingly like the portrait on her sister's gravestone. Huffman, who stands with Mann at the Dornman Port waypoint, asks the Traveler to check the site.

## history
### STANDARD
Before the sanatorium there was a temple of the Arcadians dedicated to Istaroth; it was once connected to the Temple of Space. After a disaster, the Temple of Space was lifted into the heavens and hidden, while the earthbound half fell into ruin and was later expanded into the sanatorium. The sanatorium has since been demolished.
### LARGE +
Asmoday's gaze fell on the lifted temple only after its destruction and made it a watchtower called Segment-Domain Tau. In the present, the Watcher Nihilita, having fallen to the mortal world, asks the Traveler to ascend and free the uplifted people of the past.
### ULTRA +
The patients' letters show a community of older residents and people recovering from lung illness who looked after one another with dark humour; Bauer mentions settling his plot with 'the lector here', but the letters do not say what the lector's role was.

## quests
### STANDARD
The Wind Has Ceased, the opening quest of the series A Long Day in the Mountains, begins near the northern waypoint in Dornman Port with Mann's story of ghosts. The Traveler investigates at night, meets Zeitlind, and is taken up through the Anemo statue to the Temple of Space.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Dust, damp and the creak of old beds in a half-collapsed building, where the notes patients left behind keep their jokes and their grief.

## dangers
### STANDARD
No fighting in the ruins themselves. In the first quest, patrol Domain Keepers are visible on the road to the sanatorium.

## status
### LITE
Demolished and decaying as of 7.1; an access point to the Temple of Space.

### STANDARD
Zeitlind came to the site seeking her older sister Raumhilda, whose letters said the sanatorium was closing and that she was going somewhere far away. Her sister's portrait gravestone reads "One lifetime doesn't count". Istoria in the Temple of Space remembers a "Hilda" and says she always looked a little gloomy.

## entry: Mann and the ghost story

@keys2: Dornman Port, sanatorium

### STANDARD
Mann is an old doorman who swears he saw ghosts at night. The figure he took for a ghost was Zeitlind, who arrived late at night to visit her sister's grave and ended up scaring him. He is trying to keep people away from Windrest Peak.
