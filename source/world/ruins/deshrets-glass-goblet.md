## summary
### LITE
Deshret's Glass Goblet is a huge goblet-shaped structure in the depths of Mt. Damavand, in the Desert of Hadramaveth. The platform at its center is the way into the Orchard of Pairidaeza, the Eternal Oasis. It is first seen in the Dirge of Bilqis world quests, and access opens during Dune-Entombed Fecundity: Part III.
### STANDARD +
Desert tales say King Deshret left the cup behind when he bade farewell to joy. The Jinni Liloupar says the visible structure is not the true goblet but a mechanism that holds the oasis in stasis. It was guarded by Ferigees, a Jinni whose spirit lived in a machine on the goblet.

## geography
@version: 3.4
### STANDARD
The Goblet sits inside the cavernous interior of Mt. Damavand, reached by passing ancient pipes and mechanisms, with a platform at its center that leads to the Eternal Oasis. Seen from above it looks like a wide crystal cup. Jeht, a member of the Tanit tribe, described pipes running into the temple inside the mountain.
### LARGE +
Liloupar remembers that her master Al-Ahmar (King Deshret) once shaped an elaborate canal system here to irrigate mortal lands, in a time when the dune was green hills before sandstorms took it.

## history
@version: 3.4
### STANDARD
In the account Liloupar gives in the quest, Deshret left the cup as a receptacle for past traumas and memories. Jinn-built machinery under the mountain, powered by gems under the sands according to artifact lore, keeps the oasis in a frozen moment. When the Traveler's party reached the center, Ferigees blocked them; her fall also ended the old arrangement.
### LARGE +
The party broke the oasis's stabilising device on entering, according to Liloupar, which means the Goblet's mechanism no longer holds the place in perfect stasis and the oasis is eventually doomed to be buried.

## status
### LITE
Ferigees was destroyed in Dune-Entombed Fecundity: Part III, and the Goblet is open to travellers as the way down to the oasis. The quest series continues in The Eternal Dream, Ever Lush.
### STANDARD +
The Goblet's shape is echoed by a chesspiece in Safhe Shatranj, another subarea of the Desert of Hadramaveth, though canon does not explain the link.

## entry: Ferigees at the Goblet
@keys: Ferigees, Guardian of the Eternal Oasis
@keys2: Glass Goblet, Goblet, Mt. Damavand
@version: 3.4
### STANDARD
Ferigees guarded the platform in a machine, bound by what she called shackles, and refused Liloupar entry, calling her a traitor. When the Traveler defeated her mechanical defenders she said the tomb was empty and would crumble, and her spirit faded with the shell.
### ULTRA
Her last words, as quoted in the quest, call the oasis an empty tomb that will crumble like her and like Liloupar, who is not whole. Liloupar had earlier called her sister, in a Chinese term the wiki notes is not necessarily about family.
