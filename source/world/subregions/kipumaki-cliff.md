## summary
### LITE
Kipumaki Cliff is a cliff in northern Nod-Krai, perpetually shrouded in dark clouds. It is said to hold a sealed, undying fiend: the Scourge of Endless Decay, an Abyssal entity that Aila (the first Moonchanter of the Frostmoon Scions) and Solovei (the Torchforger) subdued here 500 years ago. The area has been a hotspot of Wild Hunt activity ever since, and the Lightkeepers watch it from Cliffwatch Camp.
### STANDARD +
Its name echoes Finnish myth: kipumaki means "pain hill", the home of a spirit who takes human pain upon herself. The cliff belongs to Ashveil Peak in the north of Nod-Krai.
### LARGE +
The 6.3 quest When War Songs Rise (Nightingale's Song, Act II) is the cliff's defining story: the seal failed, and Illuga's squad and the Traveler had to rebuild it and kill what it held.

## geography
### STANDARD
A windswept cliff under permanent dark cloud, with a waterfall in its south, a clifftop camp and a cave system beneath it. Local specialties include Winter Icelea, and Oath Lanterns, Mandragora and other kuuvahki sources are scattered across the slopes.
### LARGE +
Beneath the cliff lies the Chasm of Endless Decay, the cave where the Scourge was imprisoned and where Illuga later followed it. The sealing array's focal points are Verdant Crests set on the cliff face; in the quest they respond to the Silver Lampwick the Lightkeepers carry. Cliffwatch Camp, the Lightkeepers' outpost, stands near a lighthouse and keeps a cemetery for fallen Lightkeepers.

## history
### STANDARD
500 years ago the Scourge of Endless Decay, an Abyssal entity that had helped Rotwang try to open the False Sky to the Abyss through the Pillar of Embla, was banished and sealed here by Aila and Solovei, with Alia's help, using a sealing array. The site became a standing source of Wild Hunt activity, so the Lightkeepers founded Cliffwatch Camp to monitor it.
### LARGE +
Three years before the present day a surge of Wild Hunt activity under the cliffs caused a catastrophe: many Lightkeepers and civilians died, among them Captain Olsson, and the monsters spread out beneath the cliffs before they could be cleared. The camp was rebuilt and the fight went on. Illuga, who rushed back to report to Nikita, and Rollon, who answered the emergency order, both recall that day.
### ULTRA +
In When War Songs Rise the Scourge reappears, first as a Wilderness Hunter and then as the Lord of the Hidden Depths: Whisperer of Nightmares. The Nightmare Orioles squad under Illuga, with the Traveler, reactivate the Verdant Crests one by one, and Bjorn is fatally wounded in the process. After the Evil Eye over the cliff is destroyed, Illuga pursues the Scourge into the Chasm of Endless Decay, where it conjures illusions of his past to exploit his losses. His squad helps him resist, and the Spiralblade is used to defeat it.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Wild Hunt creatures, Abyssal Riftlines and Evil Eyes. The cliff has a special climate, The Wild Hunt Lurks...: staying in it builds an Abyssal Decay gauge (a game mechanic) that drains HP once full and narrows vision, unless the traveller shelters near Oath Lanterns or other kuuvahki sources.
### LARGE +
The heavy cloud limits sight, and the standing Abyssal presence keeps the cliff dangerous even after the 6.3 victory.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Dark cloud overhead, lanterns glowing where kuuvahki is strong, and a camp run with the discipline of people who have buried comrades here.

## travel
### LARGE
Reached through Cliffwatch Camp. Outsiders are not casually welcome: when Anleifr meets the Traveler there his first reaction is that this is the front line against the Wild Hunt and they should not be present.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the seal has been restored and the Scourge of Endless Decay defeated, but Wild Hunt pressure on the cliff has not ended and the Lightkeepers still hold the line.
### STANDARD +
Bjorn died in the 6.3 fighting and has a grave at Cliffwatch Camp.
### ULTRA +
The cliff is no longer the prison of a single named horror, yet the Lightkeepers still treat it as a hotspot; nothing in the pages read changes that through 7.1.

## entry: Chasm of Endless Decay
@keys: Chasm of Endless Decay, Scourge of Endless Decay, Lord of the Hidden Depths
@version: 6.3
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The cave beneath Kipumaki Cliff where Aila and Solovei imprisoned the Scourge of Endless Decay. In When War Songs Rise it is the scene of the final battle between the Scourge and Illuga's squad with the Traveler, who destroy it with the Spiralblade.

## entry: Cliffwatch Camp
@keys: Cliffwatch Camp
@version: 6.3
### STANDARD
The Lightkeepers' outpost watching Kipumaki Cliff, near a lighthouse. Its cemetery holds graves for Lightkeepers who fell on the cliff, including Veyde, Olsson, Chernov, Filaev, Pieris Asebov, Shonokovych and, after the 6.3 quest, Bjorn. One grave is an empty tomb prepared in advance for a future Lightkeeper named Patolichev.
