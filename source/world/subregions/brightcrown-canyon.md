## summary
### LITE
Brightcrown Canyon is a rocky canyon in the Brightcrown Mountains, on Mondstadt's north-western side. At its north-eastern end lies the eastern entrance to Stormterror's Lair, the ruined capital of the old storm god Decarabian. Hilichurls roam its ancient mechanical guards and ruins.
### STANDARD +
The canyon is part of the approach to Old Mondstadt. Anemo Rings are found along it, and a bard named Davy sits near the ruins overlooking Cider Lake.

## geography
### STANDARD
A valley at the north-west coast of Cider Lake, flanked by ruins and walled by cliffs. Features include an Anemo Ring, Rock Piles, Ruins Torches and Seelie; calla lilies and small lamp grass grow here. The canyon's north-eastern part opens into Stormterror's Lair.
### LARGE +
A worn path leads between the old city and a boar habitat, which the wiki takes as a sign the city's people hunted here.

## places
### STANDARD
Its main landmark is the eastern gate to the ancient ring-shaped city that held Decarabian's tower. The Travel Guide describes the pass as still guarded by ancient mechanisms, without the soldiers who once held it for the King of Gales.
### LARGE +
Part of the ruins was destroyed in a failed experiment by the witch Alice, who tried to control a Ruin Guard with a hilichurl strapped to it; the guard fell apart.

### ULTRA +
The Travel Guide describes the city beyond as built in a ring, with every resident assigned a spot between inner and outer rings and the tower at the centre. Alice also admits blowing up a few arcades so that people can climb the tower more easily.

## people
### STANDARD
Davy, a bard in the standard Mondstadt bard outfit, stands outside the ruins above Cider Lake for inspiration. He is the ex-husband of the bard Myweiss, once billed with her as Mr. and Mrs. Lovebard, and is bitter about love.
### LARGE +
Davy points travellers to crystal ore he noticed while seeking inspiration, and plans a ballad he calls the Manacles of Matrimony.

## history
### STANDARD
These ruins belong to Decarabian's walled capital, which rebels brought down around 2,600 years ago. The ancient mechanisms guarding the pass are remains of that age.
### LARGE +
In the Prologue the lair beyond was where Dvalin was found; the canyon is the way in from the east.
## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Weathered stone, wind and the creak of old machinery; empty, but not safe.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Hilichurls, Samachurls, Mitachurls (Blazing Axe), Fatui Electro Cicin Mages, slimes of every element, and Ruin Guards.

## travel
### STANDARD
Found on the north-west shore of Cider Lake, with the Brightcrown Mountains beyond.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 nothing new is recorded; still ruined and dangerous.
