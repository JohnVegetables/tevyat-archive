## summary
### LITE
Minacious Isle is a limited-time island in the southwest of the Golden Apple Archipelago. According to Albedo, its main island used to be a mountain of Mondstadt that was ripped away and landed in the sea upside down. It has inverted ruins, dandelions, Anemo enemies and a way back to Mondstadt, and appeared in the 1.6 and 2.8 summer events.
### STANDARD +
Its Mondstadt origin fits the nation of Anemo: the island has Anemo slimes and an Eye of the Storm, and the quest The Winding Homeward Way ends at a portal to Mondstadt.

## geography
@version: 1.6
### STANDARD
A main island with the Inverted Ruins, dandelions, windmill mechanisms and wind currents, a northwest island, and a small stray island far to the west. A whirlpool just west of the main island leads into an underwater ruin cave.
### LARGE +
In 1.6 the northwest island was covered by a giant energy shield until the quest The Final Riddle: A Secret Uncovered was done; afterwards a special crafting bench for harpastum bombs became available. A Seelie on the main island's southwest leads to elemental monuments that light a portal back to Mondstadt.
### ULTRA +
The 2.8 version showed the island in a "past" and a "present" form, switched by layout pools; four torches had to be set two upright and two upside down. Four elemental monuments (Anemo, Cryo, Hydro, Pyro) each changed the weather: wind currents, fog that revealed hidden dream forms, rain that revealed hidden platforms, or clear skies. Old tablets there speak of hidden things showing themselves in fog, rain and wind.

## history
@version: 1.6
### STANDARD
In the event's Echoing Tales conch narration, Albedo tells that Barbatos reshaped Mondstadt, splitting mountaintops that landed in the sea upside down. The wiki's archipelago timeline puts the island's arrival at the Haar Islands, with a Seelie, less than about 2,600 years ago. A shipwrecked group afterwards settled across the archipelago. An eroded tablet in the underwater ruins calls the place an observation point suited to astronomical research.
### LARGE +
After the Dodo-King storyline, Kaeya, Jean and Klee could be found on the northwest island and Albedo near the main island's waypoint until the event ended. Alice, Klee's mother, later revealed that she had visited the islands before and dressed them up for her daughter's summer holiday.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Anemo, Hydro, Pyro, Cryo and Dendro slimes of several sizes, hilichurls and shamans of various elements, Hilichurl Grenadiers and shooters, shield guards, and elite Blazing Axe, Stonehide Lawachurl and Wooden Shieldwall chieftains, along with an Eye of the Storm.

## status
### LITE
Not accessible in the game as of 7.1; it existed only during the 1.6 and 2.8 event runs.
### STANDARD +
Characters may speak of it as the island that came from Mondstadt, but nothing in released canon places the Traveler there after the events ended.

## entry: Minacious Isle Inverted Ruins
@keys: Minacious Isle, Inverted Ruins
@keys2: Golden Apple Archipelago, Mondstadt, Albedo
@version: 1.6

### STANDARD
Minacious Isle's Inverted Ruins stand upside down because, by Albedo's account, the island was a Mondstadt mountain broken off and flipped into the sea. The island has dandelions, Anemo creatures and a portal home to Mondstadt (the quest The Winding Homeward Way).
