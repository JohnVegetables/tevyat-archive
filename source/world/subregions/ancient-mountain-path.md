## summary
### LITE
The Ancient Mountain Path is an old route on the volcano slopes of Atocpan in Natlan. It holds the Flowfire Experimentation Site, an ancient research station where dragon scholars worked out how to turn liquid Phlogiston into stone while the war against the powers of the heavens loomed. The station was abandoned long ago and is kept only by stone constructs.
### STANDARD +
It opens to the Traveler through a hidden exploration objective in Atocpan that begins by waking a giant stone serpent, a Secret Source Dragon Idol.

## geography
### STANDARD
A subarea of Atocpan, on the lava-fed slopes of the Great Volcano of Tollan. The Flowfire Experimentation Site has a surface level and a deeper level, both reached by following the stone serpent into the cavern. A geothermal vent can be found among the exploration features here.
### LARGE +
The lower floor of the site is where the readable records are left. Liquid Phlogiston and lava flow through the workings, which suits experiments on the state of Phlogiston.

## places
### STANDARD
The Flowfire Experimentation Site, with a surface area and a lower level. The records on the lower level are two golden-lit memory traces: an experimental report and a researcher's notes.
### LARGE +
Defence is handled by two kinds of construct named in the notes: the Secret Source Servant and the Secret Source Dragon Idol, set up as periodic defence mechanisms. The stone serpent that guides visitors is a Dragon Idol.

## people
### STANDARD
No living inhabitants are shown. The only presence is the unnamed researcher who wrote the notes, long gone, and the constructs left behind.
### LARGE +
The researcher writes warmly of the constructs, saying they have no true emotion modules yet seem sad to see their keeper leave, and dismisses this as a researcher's folly.

## history
@version: 5.5
### STANDARD
The experimental report records Experiment 150: the Type-01 Phlogiston Converter did turn liquid Phlogiston at the site into a solid. Extracting Phlogiston from it showed that the product was not solid Phlogiston but another sort of rock, the same stone formed when gaseous Phlogiston fuses with stone, as often happens in eruptions of the Tollan volcano. The researchers judged it interesting but not practical for their needs.
### LARGE +
The report goes on to say that time was short. To resist and prevent the descent of enemies from beyond the sky, the 'State Change' device was to turn Phlogiston into sturdier fortifications on top of Tollan Volcano. The writer ends with a line of devotion to the nation.

In the researcher's notes the war has begun and the converter research is finished. Lord Kukulkan had ordered such experiments to be done outside the Sacred Mountain, which the writer thinks may have been meant to protect dissenters like them. They hope that all factions will unite in the face of the real threat and that Kukulkan and the Chamber of Deliberation might reconcile. There was no time to retrieve most materials, so the writer returned to the Sacred City with the data only, trusting in victory and intending to carry everything back later.
### ULTRA +
The notes do not say whether the writer came back. The site still holds its materials and the constructs still keep their post.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Heat and low light from the lava, ancient stone and a faint sadness: a workshop whose keeper never returned in any record, tended only by machines that keep waiting.

## dangers
### STANDARD
The site is guarded by constructs set up as periodic defences. Lava, geothermal heat and Phlogiston currents are the other hazards.
### LARGE +
A Pulverite barrier has to be smashed to proceed in the exploration objective.

## travel
### STANDARD
Reached from Atocpan by completing the first step of the hidden exploration objective; a hidden teleport waypoint is unlocked inside.
### LARGE +
Finishing the objective ends with riding the stone serpent. It also yields two Pyroculi, a furnishing blueprint and the achievement 'A Lonely Sentinel' (game rewards, not lore).

## quests
@version: 5.5
### STANDARD
How Long Must We Wait, My Friend? (an unofficial wiki name for a hidden exploration objective). The Traveler wakes the serpent, follows it into the cavern, runs the converter mechanism, and finally rides it.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the site is still abandoned and open to exploration.
### STANDARD +
Nothing in canon through 7.1 says that the researcher returned.

## entry: Flowfire Experimentation Site
@keys: Flowfire Experimentation Site, Type-01 Phlogiston Converter, Phlogiston Converter
@version: 5.5
### STANDARD
An ancient research station on the Ancient Mountain Path where dragon researchers tested a device that changed liquid Phlogiston into solid form. The first successful run produced a stone rather than solid Phlogiston, a result they put aside as impractical. The researchers intended to use a 'State Change' device to build fortifications from Phlogiston atop Tollan Volcano against attackers from beyond the sky. The writer of the notes left the site guarded by a Secret Source Servant and a Secret Source Dragon Idol.
