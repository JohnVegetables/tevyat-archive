## summary
### LITE
Firethief's Secret Isle is a small island south of Coatepec Mountain in Natlan. It is hidden on the map and under constant cloud until all four of its time-trial challenges are completed. It was an ancient trial site and training ground, and only Saurians live there now.
### STANDARD +
The island is shaped a little like a dragon, and the wiki guesses it was named for the Dragon Prince who stole the flame of Phlogiston.

## geography
### STANDARD
An island in the water south of Coatepec Mountain, with a central spot where the fourth challenge appears after the first three.
### LARGE +
Once the challenges are complete the island is added to the map and the perpetual overcast weather lifts. A teleport waypoint on it earns the achievement 'Only the Training Grounds Are Left?'.

## places
### STANDARD
Four time trial challenges: three that can be done in any order, and a fourth at the island's centre that appears afterwards. Unusually, none of them has a countdown timer.
### LARGE +
Manmade structures, decorations and graffiti on the island suggest humans re-used it at some point.

## people
### STANDARD
Only Saurians live on the isle now.

## history
@version: 5.0
### STANDARD
The island was once an ancient trial site and training ground, presumably set up by Natlan's ancient dragons. Later humans seem to have repurposed it, judging by the buildings and graffiti.
### LARGE +
The guess that its name comes from Kukulkan fits a tale that he stole the flame of Phlogiston from his kin and gave it to humanity, but another Firethief appears in the tribal tale of Turnfire Night, so the name is unresolved.
### ULTRA +
The achievement 'Only the Training Grounds Are Left?' is the main in-game statement of its past as a trial site and training ground. That the island is shaped somewhat like a dragon is a wiki observation, not in-game text.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Before the challenges are cleared, a gloomy, cloud-covered island of old stone. After that it brightens.

## dangers
### STANDARD
The challenges are puzzles, not combat; wild Saurians are the main living creatures.

## travel
### STANDARD
Not on the map at first; the area is found by travelling south of Coatepec Mountain.

## quests
@version: 5.0
### STANDARD
A set of four time trial challenges, with no storyline quest.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the isle is a quiet training ground with only Saurian residents.
