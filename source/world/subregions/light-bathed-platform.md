## summary
### LITE
The Light-Bathed Platform is a subarea of Hiisi Island, the home of the Frostmoon Scions in Nod-Krai. It is the final destination of the pilgrimage route where wishes are offered to Lady Kuutar, the Moon Maiden, before the great Idol of the New Moon. A sealed sanctum, the Court of the First Edict, lies beneath a huge edifice nearby.
### STANDARD +
The Idol is a statue of Kuutar in her original form, said to have been carved by the first Moonchanter, Aila, 500 years ago from kuuvahki-rich ore. Two Scions look after the platform: the acolyte Eveliina and the adherent Kukkia.
### LARGE +
The platform appears in the world quest Echoes of an Unfinished Past, which opens the sealed Court, and later in the 6.3 quest The Homebound Moon, in which wings are added to the Idol.

## geography
### STANDARD
An elevated, open stone platform on Hiisi Island overlooking the Frostmoon Enclave camp below, with the Idol of the New Moon at its heart and Moonfall Silver, a local specialty, gathered nearby. Its map level connects to the Sanctum of the Oathbearer.
### LARGE +
A towering pillar-like edifice beyond the platform marks the entrance to the Court of the First Edict, closed by a Moonchanter's seal. Eveliina says the huge structure fell from the sky and that its fallen fragments mostly landed to the west at Thunderclap Reef, with one in the camp below.

## people
### STANDARD
Eveliina is an acolyte of the Frostmoon Scions who greets visitors with a blessing of moonlight and wonders aloud whether Kuutar would like the statues. Kukkia is an adherent who prays at the Idol and tidies it after prayer, an act she considers equal to praying.
### LARGE +
Kukkia offers pilgrims a freshly picked flower and offers to lead prayer; later her faith looks troubled and Lauma comes to speak with her. Eveliina describes herself as a humble acolyte who will not go into the details of Kuutar's birth or miracles.
### ULTRA +
Verna, a self-declared priestess the Traveler first met at Thunderclap Reef, tries in Echoes of an Unfinished Past to break the Court's seal with kuuvahki fragments her Krakas gathered.

## culture
### STANDARD
The Scions treat the platform as a pilgrimage endpoint. Praying here, cleaning the statue and offering flowers are acts of devotion; Eveliina says even those who did not come to pray should feel Kuutar's blessing.
### LARGE +
Eveliina greets visitors with 'may the moonlight shine upon your path ahead' and Kukkia with a prayer that the Moon Goddess guard their path.

## history
### STANDARD
The Court of the First Edict was an ancient sanctuary that a Moonchanter sealed off 500 years ago. The Scions regard it as forbidden ground. The Idol was carved at about the same time.
### LARGE +
In Echoes of an Unfinished Past the Traveler and Paimon help Verna open the Court by throwing the fragments into the seal, then explore the sanctum, follow a Moonlane, and find the sword Aila left behind.
### ULTRA +
In The Homebound Moon the modern Scions add a pair of wings to the Idol to mark their goddess's newfound freedom and autonomy.

## quests
### STANDARD
Echoes of an Unfinished Past (Polkka Beneath the Moon's Oracle, a world quest that starts at the platform) and The Homebound Moon (Silvermoon Hall: Selenic Chronicles).
### LARGE +
No other quest in the pages read is tied specifically to the platform.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Quiet reverence under open sky: moonlit statues, hushed prayers and a sense of an ending to a long walk.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the Idol stands winged, the Court has been opened, and the platform remains a place of prayer.
### STANDARD +
After In the Shadows of Night, Kukkia keeps praying but appears troubled about the Scions' faith in the Moon Goddess; Lauma comes to talk her round.

A large statue of the Moon Maiden Kuutar, carved by Aila 500 years ago from kuuvahki-rich ore, at the platform's centre. After The Homebound Moon (6.3), the Scions add wings to it for Kuutar's newfound freedom.
