## summary
### LITE
Yachimatahime's Locus is a hidden subarea of Enkanomiya in Inazuma, introduced in Version 2.4. It was the site of one of the three Towers of the Three Realms, built to keep harmony between the human, elemental (vishap) and abyssal realms. It appears on the map only after the world quest Erebos' Secret begins.
### STANDARD +
It is one of three trial grounds opened by the shade Eboshi, an auxiliary of Sangonomiya and a shrine maiden of Enkanomiya. Here the Jibashiri Eki, in charge of the trial of "insight", sets the Traveler a puzzle of four Flames of the High Gate.

## geography
### STANDARD
The Locus is reached by a Time Tunnel at The Emanant Skylight in Evernight Temple once Eboshi has raised it, or can be glided to from above the Dainichi Mikoshi. Most of it is shrouded by a barrier until the trial progresses. It holds a Teleport Waypoint and Phase Gates.
### LARGE +
Before Eboshi is spoken to, the area is only floating textures and smashed stones; her first conversation raises it into the world. The four flames sit on a ruin near the basin, by a pool and on a detached pillar on the northeast landmass, and southeast of a large ruin on the northwest landmass.

## quests
### STANDARD
Yachimatahime's Trial is part of The Three Great Martial Trials and Erebos' Secret. The Traveler passes through the Time Tunnel, speaks to the afterimage before the seal, and releases four Flames of the High Gate. Eki then acknowledges the Traveler's valour, saying it shows enough insight to seek an eel in winter, a reference to the very small unagi fry of the breeding season.
### LARGE +
After all three trials are done, Eboshi tells the Traveler more about the bathysmal vishaps, Watatsumi Omikami and the time before the sun and moon. Those revelations belong to Erebos' Secret, not to this site itself.

## history
### STANDARD
Per The Byakuyakoku Collection (via the wiki), the people of Enkanomiya built the Towers of the Three Realms at the borders of Byakuyakoku to stabilise it, as it sat on the edge of the human, elemental and abyssal realms. The towers are hidden through secret arts and can be summoned only by shrine maidens like Eboshi and the Vassals of Watatsumi. Their ancient names are forgotten.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Anemo and Hydro Specters appear in the area.
### ULTRA +
The wiki observes that the trial grounds are reachable by gliding from the rocks above the Dainichi Mikoshi at any time, though most of the Locus stays barred by a barrier until the trial has progressed. Eki's line about the battlefield being ever-changing frames the insight trial: keep eyes and ears open to master the many variables.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
A floating, ghostly platform of ruins and flame-bearing gates, eerily quiet, with the sense of a rite few have been allowed to see.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1, unchanged: the trial is complete and the tower stays hidden unless raised by Eboshi.
