## summary
### LITE
Stone Gate is the roadside crossroads in Liyue's Bishui Plain where the roads from Liyue Harbor, Qingce Village, Dawn Winery and Dihua Marsh meet. Its bulletin board greets travellers as arriving at "the door of Liyue": south to the bustling port, north to the rustic wine country of Mondstadt. It is a place for tea, gossip and a few stay-behinds rather than a settlement with its own government.
### STANDARD +
The Ridge Watch domain, ancient city ruins that overlook the ridge between Mondstadt and Liyue like a boundary marker, lies in the area.

## geography
### STANDARD
The junction joins the road to Dawn Winery in the northeast, to Wuwang Hill and Qingce Village in the northwest, and to Dihua Marsh and Liyue Harbor in the south. Ruins, a windmill mechanism and hilichurl camps stand among the hills around it.
### LARGE +
Local specialties are Cor Lapis, Dandelion Seed and Jueyun Chili. Ridge Watch's own text describes it as standing over the start of the ridge, looking both north and south, mountain and river.

## places
### STANDARD
The Stone Gate Bulletin Board carries travellers' notices: a welcome as the door of Liyue, a note about a purple-clad young woman from Yujing Terrace asking locals about their daily needs (evidently Keqing), a swordsman's admittedly bad poem, a hypermarket advert for Dihua Marsh, and praise for Pops Zhou's tea. Ridge Watch, a domain of ruined walls, is nearby.

## people
### STANDARD
Pops Zhou sells cheap tea at the gate; a friend of forty years says it has never declined or improved. Aunt Qiao knows the stall holders and is asked to mind their stalls. Huachu and Jianqiu sit drinking his tea. Zhuyu, an herb gatherer, and his wife Qinglian are found south of the gate towards Dihua Marsh.
### LARGE +
Huachu is the second daughter of Gentry De'an of the Minghua Company; her father disapproved of Jianqiu, an arithmetic teacher, so the two faked her death in Liyue Harbor and settled quietly at Stone Gate. Jianqiu claims to be the Minghua Bank's assistant shopkeeper, unaware the bank has been sold. During a Lantern Rite festival (limited-time event) they visited the Harbor's outskirts and ate at the Emerald Maple Inn. Zhuyu was once a sailor and became an herb gatherer to care for Qinglian when she fell ill; he does not know she is only pretending to be ill so that he will stay with her.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Quiet and breezy: travellers resting at the tea stall, the occasional merchant passing, hills with windmills and old ruins.

## dangers
### LARGE
Hilichurls and samachurls, slimes, Whopperflowers and Fatui skirmishers roam the surrounding ground, with Abyss Mages, Geovishap Hatchlings and Mitachurl shield-bearers as tougher threats. The road itself is generally safe.

## travel
### STANDARD
Two Teleport Waypoints lie nearby. It is the natural stop for anyone walking between Mondstadt and Liyue Harbor.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 Stone Gate is an ordinary roadside stop; no later canon changes it.

## entry: Huachu and Jianqiu

### ULTRA
Lovers who eloped and now stay at Stone Gate. Huachu, daughter of the Minghua Company's Gentry De'an, and Jianqiu, an arithmetic teacher from Chihu Rock, are said to have left a corpse dressed as her in the Feiyun Slope well to fake her death, which led De'an to sell off the Minghua Bank to pay for the funeral. Huachu's bulletin note speaks of a hoped-for new home far away under the Geo Archon's protection.
