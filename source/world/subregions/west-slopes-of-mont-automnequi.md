## summary
### LITE
The West Slopes of Mont Automnequi are the western side of one of Fontaine's most iconic peaks, in the Belleau Region; the east side lies in the Morte Region. The slopes are known for the Poisson Tunnel, which carries the Clementine Line through the mountain, and for the clockwork inventor Antoine. Added in Version 4.0.
### STANDARD +
Official exploration text calls Mont Automnequi one of Fontaine's most iconic peaks, noting that although much of the nation is covered by water, mountains and forests exist too.

## geography
### STANDARD
Mountain slopes in the Belleau Region. The Poisson Tunnel passes through them. Local specialties include Lumidouce Bells and Rainbow Roses, and the wiki lists a viewpoint and a fishing point here.
### LARGE +
The name probably derives from French for autumn and equinox (a wiki inference). The official site's exploration description says huge engineering devices from the Fontaine Research Institute dug the tunnel, solving one of the biggest problems in building the aquabus lines.

## places
### STANDARD
Poisson Tunnel: carved through the slopes for the Clementine Line. The Fontaine Research Institute built it with enormous machines, including a bore called the "Golden Vessel". The project's success spurred a new generation of clockwork technology, including the Construction Specialist Mek, which was based on the Golden Vessel.

## people
### STANDARD
Antoine Roger is an open-world NPC engineer here. He is the creator of the Miraculous Antoine Roger Aircraft found around Fontaine, and one stands near him. He believes humans should break free of the earth and soar, and he contrasts his clockwork flight work with Edwin Eastinghouse's research.
### LARGE +
The wiki lists no other named residents, and the explanation Antoine gives of how his propulsion works is not summarised here.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Enemies include Hilichurls and Hydro Hilichurl Rogues, Treasure Hoarders, large Hydro Slimes, Blazing Axe Mitachurls, Tainted Water-Splitting Phantasms and several types of Mek. The boss is the Emperor of Fire and Iron.

## quests
### STANDARD
Quests listed for this subarea include "A Lady's Invitation", "Days Beneath the Waves", "Sea Breeze Rendezvous", "Still Mouthwatering!", "Truly Mouthwatering!", "The Art of the Elegant Blade Dance?", "Blade Dance's Unexpected End!" and "Studies in Light and Shadow: A Fontaine of Enchantment". Their stories are not covered here.
### ULTRA +
The wiki's trivia ties the name to the two seasons' words only as a guess. Antoine's idle line, "break free", sums up his creed that humanity need not be land-bound. The Poisson Tunnel entry states that the tunnel-boring effort pushed the Fontaine Research Institute to produce the Construction Specialist Mek, so the slopes are linked to the nation's mechanical development as much as to its scenery.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the slopes are accessible and unchanged.
