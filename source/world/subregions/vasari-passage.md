## summary
### LITE
Vasari Passage sits at the bottom (south) of the Court of Fontaine. Streets lined with shops lead toward a large fountain plaza, and a cafe and several vendors serve passers-by.
### STANDARD +
It is one of the capital's shopping districts and a common meeting spot because of the statue plaza.

## places
### STANDARD
Statue Plaza, the large fountain square where the shop streets converge; the Eastern Street and the Western Street; and Cafe Lucerne, managed by Arouet. Vendors include Boucicaut (Damoville Purveyor), Estelle (Beaumont Workshop), Eugenie (fresh produce) and Louis (snack shop).
### LARGE +
The plaza is a gathering point for people such as Nour (a member of the Gardes), Paban (a newsy), Talvat (a freelance photographer), Lovi and Potier by day and Ismenor at night. The Eastern Street holds staff of the Confrerie of Cabriere (Calcagni), and Charlene of the Gardes is on the Western Street.

## people
### STANDARD
Listed residents include Sanin, Nolette, Wei Xiaobao, Estella, Manet and Olivier, and Chatelet, Gontharet and Menthe at Cafe Lucerne.
### LARGE +
After the world quest A Certain Stamp, the Maison Gestion gestionnaires Roialte (plaza) and Iaune (Western Street) can be found in the district at night.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Shop windows, a plaza fountain and the steady foot traffic of a capital's commercial heart.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 no change to the district is recorded.
### ULTRA +
The name is unexplained in-world; the wiki only suggests a reference to the Vasari Corridor in Florence.
