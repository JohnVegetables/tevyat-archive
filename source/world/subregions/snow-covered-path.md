## summary
### LITE
The Snow-Covered Path is the trail up the lower slopes of Dragonspine, Mondstadt's permanently frozen mountain. It is coated in ice all the way up, and the Dragonspine Adventurer Camp and the Frostbearing Tree lie along it.
### STANDARD +
It is the usual way into the mountain for adventurers, and cold is its main hazard. An official field report describes the climb as less steep than expected but icy throughout, and so cold that even grass will not burn, so travelers keep hunting for the next heat source.

## geography
### STANDARD
A glazed mountain trail on Dragonspine's lower slopes. Ancient Rime, Scarlet Quartz and Warming Seelie are found along it, and the Frostbearing Tree, the frozen remnant of a tree tied to the lost kingdom of Sal Vindagnyr, stands here.
### LARGE +
Higher up lie Starglow Cavern and, at the summit, the Skyfrost Nail. The path is the lived-in part of the mountain, while the deeper caverns and the peak belong to the ruins and the dragon's remains.

## people
### STANDARD
The Dragonspine Adventurer Camp sits on the path. Its residents include the researcher Stevens, the chef Harris, the weaponsmith Orban, the adventurer Iris, and Joel and Joserf. A Treasure Hoarder named Knotty is also found on the path.
### LARGE +
The camp serves explorers who come to study the mountain and its ruins. The Knights' alchemist Albedo keeps his own, separate campsite elsewhere on Dragonspine.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Killing cold above all: without a heat source, warmth or Scarlet Quartz, travelers lose strength. Cryo hilichurls (shooters, grenadiers, shield guards, a Cryo Samachurl), Cryo Slimes and Treasure Hoarders work the path, and Fatui Geochanter, Pyroslinger and Electrohammer agents and a Fatui Cryo Cicin Mage have been seen.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
White slopes, ice underfoot, breath fogging in the air, and a constant search for the next small fire or warming Seelie.

## status
### STANDARD
As of 7.1 nothing in canon has changed here: it remains a frozen but travelable trail with a small adventurers' camp. It was introduced with Dragonspine in version 1.2.
### ULTRA +
No later Archon Quest chapter through 7.1, including the Nod-Krai and Snezhnaya chapters, returns to this particular stretch of Dragonspine, so there is no newer canon to apply.
