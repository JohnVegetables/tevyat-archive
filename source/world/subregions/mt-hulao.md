## summary
### LITE
Mt. Hulao is a mountain in Minlin, Liyue, near the Dragon-Queller and Nantianmen, and the abode of the adeptus Mountain Shaper, a red-and-orange crane. Its slopes are guarded by karst crawlers, flowers he planted whose hidden roots wrap anything that steps on them in amber; breaking the amber frees whatever was trapped, whether treasure or an enemy. A large sealed cave near the pond is his dwelling, and when the Traveler first comes the adeptus is away.
### STANDARD +
It is where the Traveler meets Mountain Shaper in the first Archon Quest chapter. The name Hulao is rendered "Amber-Prison Mountain" by the wiki.

## geography
### STANDARD
A subarea of Minlin. The path up the mountain winds past ambers; at the top stands a large talisman-sealed barrier in front of a pond and a sealed cave. The Huaguang Stone Forest lies to its northeast. Local specialties: Cor Lapis and Qingxin.
### LARGE +
An in-world stone tablet on the mountain describes the Mountain Shaper's power as coming from inner enlightenment and outer knowledge: as the dragon spits what becomes amber and the swallow spits what becomes its nest, so the adeptus used nature's power to build an amber prison. Enemies include Hilichurl fighters, Dendro samachurls, Geo and Dendro slimes, and Cryo and Pyro Abyss mages.

## places
### STANDARD
The sealed cave and pond at the top (the Mountain Shaper's abode); the mountain road lined with Amber Rock; a pagoda northeast of the waypoint.
### LARGE +
Interacting with the barrier reads at first as a mystery: Jueyun Karst is where the adepti dwell, so perhaps this is an adeptus's home, but there appears to be no way in. After the first Archon Quest act it reads instead as the abode of Mountain Shaper, who is presently absent.

## people
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Mountain Shaper, an adeptus who first appears as a crane and has a human form of a bespectacled young man with a red streak in his dark hair (a human alias, Jiehu, appears in later event content). Two brothers, Li Ding and Li Dang, appear in the Archon Quest: poor men who came to hunt for treasure in the amber and were caught.
### LARGE +
In the quest, Li Ding, praying at the top, mistakes the Traveler and Paimon for "adepti messengers" because they pass safely through Jueyun Karst. They go along with it. Li Dang is rescued from an amber and the two flee down the mountain promising to earn an honest living.

## history
@version: 1.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Mountain Shaper was one of the adepti summoned by Morax to fight in the Archon War. He explains that he planted the karst crawlers after settling here so that the mountain's tranquillity would not be disturbed; over the years they have prevented many disturbances but also trapped many curious things. He says that as the people of Liyue prospered, the adepti withdrew gradually into the mountains as a gesture of good will. According to the wiki's summary of Qiqi's character story, he once imprisoned the girl Qiqi in amber when she went on a rampage after the adepti revived her.
### LARGE +
With Moon Carver and a third adeptus, he later helped Rex Lapis subdue the Geo dragon Azhdaha, as shown by the Nameless Treasure collection. After the Traveler tells him what happened at the Rite of Descension, he says he will convene with the other adepti once he has dealt with matters that need his attention on the mountain, since he fears the amber would trap innocents in his absence.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
An austere, defended mountain: ambers like golden boulders along the path, a talisman-sealed cave and few visitors. Local folk believe an adeptus lives here, but ordinary people never meet them.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Amber Rock that traps travelers and may release enemies or treasure; Abyss mages; ordinary hilichurl camps.

## quests
### STANDARD
Moulder of Mountains (Archon Quest, Chapter I, Act I: Of the Land Amidst Monoliths). Moon Carver sends the Traveler here to Mountain Shaper with a Sigil of Permission.
### ULTRA +
In the quest, Li Ding or the freed Li Dang can be met in either order; once Li Dang is freed, Mountain Shaper appears as a crane and demands to know who trespasses and frees a thief. He softens when shown the Sigil of Permission, which he says Rex Lapis cast by hand for mortals in small numbers, so few remain. He is startled that Moon Carver trusted a mortal as messenger, and his reaction to the news of Rex Lapis is anger, not grief: he asks what power in the world could aspire to such treason.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 Mt. Hulao is unchanged. Mountain Shaper left to convene with the other adepti in Chapter I, and he reappears in later seasonal event content.
