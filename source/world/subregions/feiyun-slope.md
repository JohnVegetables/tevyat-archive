## summary
### LITE
Feiyun Slope is Liyue Harbor's prosperous merchant district and the way into the city for anyone arriving by the main road from the north. It sits between Chihu Rock to the southeast and Yujing Terrace to the west, and climbs in tiers of boulevard, bookshops, teahouses and bank offices. Wealthy merchants, guild servants, clerks of the Ministry of Civil Affairs and foreign traders all do business here.
### STANDARD +
The in-game viewpoint text describes it as where merchants who have "made it" live in comfort and carefree prosperity, with lights that never go out. The Ministry of Civil Affairs, Wangsheng Funeral Parlor and the Snezhnayan Northland Bank all have premises on the slope.

## geography
### STANDARD
Feiyun Slope is the centre of Liyue Harbor, connecting the other two districts. Visitors from the north road arrive through its entrance; from there a boulevard rises in levels. The northern wharf, with a Maritime Authority office, is at the lower edge, and the Northland Bank and Heyu Tea House are on the top level.
### LARGE +
Wanwen Bookhouse is on a middle level. A distinct Snezhnayan merchant area lies near the entrance. A bridge links the slope to Chihu Rock, and a crossroads at its western end links to Yujing Terrace, the island with Bubu Pharmacy and the road toward the Guizhong Ballista.

## places
### STANDARD
- Entrance: Baiju Guesthouse, Xigu Antiques, peddlers and the ship owner Bibo.
- Boulevard: Liuli Pavilion and Xinyue Kiosk (two eateries), Mingxing Jewelry, shops and the Wangsheng Funeral Parlor.
- Ministry of Civil Affairs: the city's civil administration.
- Wanwen Bookhouse (middle level), Northland Bank and Heyu Tea House (top level).
### LARGE +
- Wangsheng Funeral Parlor buries Liyue Harbor's dead and also performs funeral rites for adepti. Its current director is Hu Tao, with Zhongli as consultant and Ferrylady as undertaker.
- The Ministry of Civil Affairs answers to the Liyue Qixing, directing departments that run public safety, festival budgets and records, and calling on the Millelith as needed. Its staff here include the clerk Zhao and the soldier Jiayi.
- Northland Bank is the Snezhnayan bank's local branch, managed by Andrei with Ekaterina at the reception and guarded by Nadia and Vlad.
- Heyu Tea House is run by Fan Er'ye and features the storyteller Tea Master Liu Su.
- Wanwen Bookhouse is run by Jifang; Bolai owns Wanyou Boutique on the northern wharf.
### ULTRA +
A servant of the Feiyun Commerce Guild, Xu, stands near the Ministry. The Guild itself is the trading house of Xingqiu's family, which traces its roots to Yilong Wharf in Chenyu Vale; its silk, books and tea trade reaches as far as Sumeru. A merchant named Ixkotii appears on the boulevard after the quest Incandescent Ode of Resurrection.

## people
### STANDARD
Merchants such as Changshun and Bolai; shopkeepers Linlang and Xingxi; Jifang; Fan Er'ye; the fortune teller Qiming (at night); the undertaker Ferrylady; the Northland Bank staff; clerks and Millelith soldiers such as Cai Le, Cai Xun and Yicheng; and the foreign merchant Ivanovich.
### LARGE +
Hu Tao is the 77th director of Wangsheng, and Zhongli works there as a consultant. Tea Master Liu Su tells stories at Heyu Tea House, and the Adventurers' Guild has a recruiter, Tiantian, on the boulevard.

## governance
### STANDARD
The Ministry of Civil Affairs, an organ of the Liyue Qixing, administers the city from here. It watches for dangerous people through lists compiled by Yelan, issues bounties, seals dangerous areas such as the Chasm mines after an accident and organises the Lantern Rite budget and safety.
### LARGE +
Millelith soldiers stand at the entrance, by the Ministry and along the boulevard. Foreign firms such as the Northland Bank operate here, and Snezhnaya's Fatui use the bank's branches abroad as bases.

## culture
### STANDARD
Feiyun Slope shows Liyue's trading culture at its most prosperous: guild servants, shop owners, clerks, and foreign merchants, with opera and storytelling at Heyu Tea House and fine meals at Liuli Pavilion and Xinyue Kiosk.
### LARGE +
The Ministry of Civil Affairs, based on the slope, plans the Lantern Rite's budget and fireworks.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Lights that never go out, a steady hum of bargaining and a sense of ease. It feels richer and more orderly than Chihu Rock, with foreign merchants mingling among guild clerks and Millelith.

## dangers
### LARGE
Almost none physically. Social and political risks dominate: Fatui business at the Northland Bank, sharp contract terms, and the Millelith's suspicion of outsiders when security is tight (as during the Rite of Descension crisis).

## travel
### STANDARD
The main road from the north ends at the district's entrance. From here a bridge leads to Chihu Rock and a crossroads to Yujing Terrace. A northern wharf with a Maritime Authority office lies along its edge.

## quests
### LARGE
- Of the Land Amidst Monoliths (Archon Quest, 1.0): the Traveler speaks to Linlang, Bolai and other locals before the Rite of Descension, and after fleeing the Millelith is told by Childe to go to the Northland Bank.
- A New Star Approaches (Archon Quest, 1.1): the Northland Bank is the scene of Zhongli's contract with Signora, and Wangsheng Funeral Parlor is where the Traveler asks after him.
### ULTRA +
The Ministry of Civil Affairs is also where the Qixing's civil staff, including the clerk Zhao, work.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 Feiyun Slope remains Liyue Harbor's busiest commercial district. No content from 6.x or 7.0-7.1 changes it.
### STANDARD +
The Northland Bank still operates here, and the Qixing still govern through the Ministry of Civil Affairs. Hu Tao runs Wangsheng with Zhongli as consultant.

## entry: Ministry of Civil Affairs
@keys: Ministry of Civil Affairs
### STANDARD
A government body under the Liyue Qixing, headquartered in Liyue Harbor and made up of various departments. It handles intelligence and records, public safety on roads, villages and festivals, the Lantern Rite's budget and fireworks, and keeps watch lists that include dangerous individuals, among them the Traveler and some reclusive adepti. Yelan, officially in its employ, is in practice an intelligence agent who works closely with Ningguang. It sealed the Chasm mines after an accident.

## entry: Wangsheng Funeral Parlor
@keys: Wangsheng Funeral Parlor, Ferrylady
### STANDARD
Liyue Harbor's funeral parlor, which also conducts rites for adepti. Hu Tao is its 77th director and Zhongli its consultant; Ferrylady is the undertaker seen on the boulevard at night. The wiki notes that Wangsheng has ties to the Fatui; Childe says it ostensibly does not take on shady work such as assassination. During the Rite of Parting arrangements the Traveler asks Ferrylady where Zhongli has gone.

## entry: Northland Bank and the Gnosis
@keys: Northland Bank
@keys2: Liyue, Feiyun Slope, Zhongli, Signora
@version: 1.1
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
The Northland Bank is Snezhnaya's bank, with branches around the world that the Fatui sometimes use as bases. In the Liyue branch, on Feiyun Slope's top level, Childe sends the Traveler there to clear their name after the Rite of Descension, and after Osial's defeat the Traveler sees Zhongli hand his Gnosis to the Fatui Harbinger Signora under their "contract". Zhongli there reveals he is Rex Lapis, who has dwelt in the world for over six thousand years, and that he feigned death to test whether Liyue could manage without him. He calls the pact his "contract to end all contracts" and declines to say what the Cryo Archon gave in exchange.
