## summary
### LITE
Tecoloapan Bay is an island west of Tezcatepetonco Range in Natlan. It starts covered in thick fog, hidden from the map and labelled "???" until the world quest The Mystery of Tecoloapan Beach is completed.
### STANDARD +
The name is Nahuatl-derived (owl and water, with a place suffix). In the quest, a weakened Wayob manifestation, a spirit tied to the Lord of the Night, asks the Traveler to break a series of seals while a hostile Abyss-corrupted voice tries to stop them.

## geography
### STANDARD
Floating stone isles, caves and a central sealed area make up the island. Its viewpoint calls them the "Lost Ceremonial Site": isles that once stood as high sacrificial sites over the land. Its music, "When the Breeze Ceases" and "Rain of an Ancient Dream", belongs to the Eternal Sun, Eternal Want album.
### LARGE +
Finding the four seals involves a cave with an illusory floor, a room lit by three torches, a floating isle reached by wind currents from Iktomisaurs and a shattered stone restored with Spiritspeaking. The central seal opens onto the Night Kingdom.

## history
### STANDARD
According to the viewpoint text, the isles were eventually sunk into the water to seal away an evil that had seduced the masses. Carved Admonisher's Records on the island are the scrawl of someone unwell, warning visitors to leave, not to look, and that a seal was needed.
### LARGE +
In the quest, the weakened Wayob manifestation explains that the tribespeople here were long ago ensorcelled and many Nightsouls entangled by the Abyss. The seal also cut the area off from the source of the tribe's faith, wearing down even the manifestation's power. An Abyss-corrupted mimic of the Wayob tried to take over the spirit; once it was defeated, the Nightsouls could return to the Night's rest. The Nightsoul Totem on the island, an old Obsidian Totem Pole, no longer responds.

## quests
### STANDARD
The Mystery of Tecoloapan Beach (5.2) begins automatically on arrival, with the optional NPC Sakaw at the Teleport Waypoint on the west end of Tezcatepetonco Range. Completing it removes the fog and reveals the proper name on the map.

## dangers
### LARGE
Forged Sand and Tepetlisaurus enemies in Abyss-corrupted Nightsoul variants, Thundercraven Rifthound Whelps and the quest's Wayob mimic.
### ULTRA +
During the final fight the weakened Wayob fights beside the player as a Biting-Cold Wayob Manifestation. The quest also grants the achievement Truth Cannot Be Fabricated.

## status
### STANDARD
As of 7.1 the fog is gone for anyone who has completed the quest and the island remains an ordinary explorable subarea.
