## summary
### LITE
Falcon Coast is a long sandy coast in Galesong Hill, east of Mondstadt, stretching from Windrise to the sea. With Cape Oath, it shelters a bay on three sides. Citizens stroll its white sand and shallow water, and falcons can be seen above the waves.
### STANDARD +
The Temple of the Lion ruin sits on this coast. The Hexenzirkel's floating tea-party island is entered from the cliff overlooking the beach.

## geography
### STANDARD
Official text describes the long coastline and shallow water as ideal for walking. An Elemental Monument (Pyro) stands here; dandelion seeds and small lamp grass grow.
### LARGE +
Hilichurl Cryo Shooters, Wooden Shield Guards, Hydro Samachurls and slimes inhabit the beach.

## places
### STANDARD
The Temple of the Lion is an abandoned temple to the Lion of the South, taken over by monsters. The Hexenzirkel's Island in the Sky is a floating meeting place for the coven's tea parties, with a long table for seven.
### LARGE +
To enter the island, one stands at the cliff and speaks a rhyme about a squirrel, a pointy-eared cat and a pious puppy. Its access history is covered in the entry below.
## people
### STANDARD
Two children, Will and Jill, are here by day, hoping to reach Dr. Edith at Windrise; they are hiding from Mack, a Knight of Favonius who is watching over them wherever they go. The wiki notes Mack says the Knights are holding a training exercise nearby.
### LARGE +
After Alice's failed launch experiment near Springvale, her Travel Guide says Jean arranged for her to be watched; she wandered Falcon Coast bored, while Amber, who was sent to monitor her, enjoyed playing with the local children.

## history
### STANDARD
By the Wolf-Fang story, in the early days of the Knights of Favonius the nameless Knight of Boreas hunted demons from the Dark Sea on this coast, and the carrion drew thousands of falcons that circled for days. That, not the legend of Vennessa the Falcon of the West, is said to be the true source of the name.
### LARGE +
A one-time time tunnel from the Minacious Isle also led here during a limited event (see the entry below).
## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Soft surf, bright sand and long, open sightlines; calm and unhurried.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Light: hilichurls and slimes. A Knights' exercise may restrict access.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 unchanged.

## entry: Hexenzirkel's Island in the Sky
@keys: Hexenzirkel's Island in the Sky, Hexenzirkel island, Lantern of Utmost Joy
@keys2: Falcon Coast, Hexenzirkel, Windblume's Breath
@version: 3.5
### STANDARD
A floating tea-party island above Falcon Coast, kept under Barbatos' care and entered by an incantation, with seven chairs around a long table and a lantern at its centre. It was accessible during Windblume's Breath (3.5) and permanently since Paralogism (5.6).
