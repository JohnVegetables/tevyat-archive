## summary
### LITE
Mont Esus East is the eastern foothills of Mont Esus in the Liffey Region of Fontaine. Mont Esus itself is a huge mountain north-west of the Court of Fontaine that the player cannot reach, so these foothills are the part that can be explored. The area came with Version 4.1.
### STANDARD +
Two stories touch it: a world quest about Fatui towers and camps, and a scene in Arlecchino's story quest set among forgotten ruins.

## geography
### STANDARD
The foothills sit on the eastern side of the mountain. A tall observation tower and several Fatui camps stand in the area, with a broken bridge and a cliff noted in the Fatui's own deployment plan. The wiki records that the area's appearance changed after Version 4.6.
### LARGE +
Wiki trivia claims Mont Esus is the highest mountain in Teyvat and offers a view across most of the continent. That comes from the wiki's reading of the map, not an in-game text, so do not treat it as established.

## places
### STANDARD
The Fatui observation tower, climbed at the start of "In Search of Lost Time: West"; the camps around it; and the nameless ruins that Arlecchino shows the Traveler.

## people
### STANDARD
Fatui soldiers of the 34th Independent Investigative Squad hold the camps. A squad directive found in the tower assigns soldiers to a camp south of the broken bridge and to a highland camp near the cliff. Shumilov is among the characters listed for the world quest, which also mentions an absent Captain Malinovskyi.

## history
@spoiler: major
### ULTRA
The ruins were once a grand building. In "Into the Sunlight" (4.1), Arlecchino, the Knave, brings the Traveler here because this is where she killed Clervie and then Clervie's mother, Crucabena, before she became a Harbinger and before Lyney, Lynette and Freminet joined the House of the Hearth. She explains that the House takes in war orphans from all over Teyvat, and that how they are raised depends on whoever is in charge. The Traveler then faces her in a quest domain, and the quest ends with a visit to friends at the Hotel Bouffes d'ete.

## quests
### STANDARD
"In Search of Lost Time: West" (a world quest) begins by ascending the tower, where Fatui soldiers are stationed. The Traveler finds clues in nearby camps and examines a terminal. "Into the Sunlight" is the last part of "When the Hearth-Flame Goes Out" in the Ignis Purgatorius Chapter, and the wiki lists this subarea as its start location.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the foothills are accessible, while Mont Esus itself stays out of reach.
