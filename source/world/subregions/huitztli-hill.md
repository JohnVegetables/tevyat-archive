## summary
### LITE
Huitztli Hill is a mountain north of the Stadium of the Sacred Flame in the Basin of Unnumbered Flames. Local tribespeople call it Smoke Ridge because magma streams from the Great Volcano of Tollan run beneath it and send smoke up through its valleys. It is too hot even for people used to Phlogiston, which may be why a reclusive Qucusaur lives there.
### STANDARD +
Huitztli is a Nahuatl word meaning 'thorn' or 'spine'.

## geography
### STANDARD
A mountainside with valleys that smoke. The base of the southern cliff face is strewn with the bones of a huge, unknown creature.
### LARGE +
It lies north of the Stadium, within the basin.

## places
### STANDARD
Exploration features include a Coilgrass Sigil, an Everflame Seed, Flamegranate, Monetoo, Phlogiston Piles and Saurian Indwelling spots. A local specialty is brilliant chrysanthemum.

## people
### STANDARD
No settlement. The Goldflame Qucusaur Tyrant is its notable resident.
### LARGE +
The bone field at the cliff's base has no known origin.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Extreme heat. Enemies are hilichurls, tepetlisauri, yumkasaurs and their whelps, Forged Sand fighters and Swiftstep couriers, plus the Goldflame Qucusaur Tyrant as the boss.
### ULTRA +
The full list of common enemies is Hilichurls (plain, shooter, fighter, wooden-shield guard, berserker, Pyro shooter), tepetlisauri, yumkasaurs and whelps, Forged Sand interrogators, javelineers and shieldbreakers, and Swiftstep armed couriers.

## travel
### STANDARD
Found north of the Stadium of the Sacred Flame.

## status
