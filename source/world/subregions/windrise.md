## summary
### LITE
Windrise is a plain of everlasting breeze in Galesong Hill, in Mondstadt's countryside near the sea. A giant oak, the Symbol of Mondstadt's Hero, dominates it. According to legend the tree grew where the hero Vennessa, first Grand Master of the Knights of Favonius, ascended to Celestia about a thousand years ago. A Statue of the Seven stands beneath its branches, and locals treat the place with quiet reverence.
### STANDARD +
Official text calls it a valley of winds in which the oak's shade shelters the statue from rain and wind, as Vennessa once sheltered Mondstadt. The spot is also where Venti, the bard who is the Anemo Archon Barbatos, goes to recover when he is hurt.

## geography
### STANDARD
Open grassland on the Galesong Hill side of Mondstadt, facing the sea, with the oak at its centre. Falcon Coast runs east from Windrise to the water, and the underground ruin of the Temple of the Falcon lies within the area. Windwheel asters are the local specialty; there is a fishing point and a ruin torch, and an Elemental Monument stands among the grass.
### LARGE +
Windrise and Falcon Coast, together with Cape Oath, enclose the bay east of Mondstadt City; from the drop of Cape Oath's headland the plain and its tree are easy to see.

## places
### STANDARD
The Symbol of Mondstadt's Hero, the oak itself, is the heart of the area; the Statue of the Seven beneath it stands in its shade. The Temple of the Falcon is an old ruin said to have been devoted to the Falcon of the West; its depths are windy.
### LARGE +
The Temple of the Falcon is entered as a domain and holds a one-time exploration challenge and story-quest areas. Its own description claims it has watched over Windrise for thousands of years, which does not obviously match a hero from about 1,000 years ago, so treat the temple's age as unsettled.

## people
### STANDARD
Dr. Edith, a historian and folklore researcher, is usually found by the statue by day collecting material for a new book; she loves telling Vennessa's story and is the source of the Windrise oak legend. Chloris runs a small stall selling flowers and berries that Floral Whisper does not stock; she is the elder sister of Flora, who owns that shop. Godwin, a Knight of Favonius, patrols the path near the Temple of the Falcon and, per the wiki, belongs to the elite group under the Grand Master.
### LARGE +
Children from Falcon Coast want to come here to play with Dr. Edith (see Falcon Coast). Dr. Edith says she has collected far more stories and ballads about the tree than she could recount quickly.

## history
### STANDARD
Vennessa, a Muratan gladiator who led the rebellion that ended the Mondstadt aristocracy about 1,000 years ago and founded the Knights of Favonius, is said to have ascended to Celestia at this spot, taking the form of a falcon and becoming the Falcon of the West of the Four Winds. The oak is believed to have grown after her ascension; Dr. Edith says it appeared the same year she left.
### LARGE +
In the Prologue, after the Traveler purifies Dvalin's tear, Venti hurries to the tree and says the Abyssal poison he absorbed is easing. Later, when Signora has torn out his Gnosis and Barbara's healing does nothing, he returns to the tree to recover, since a body made with an Archon's power cannot be mended by ordinary means. In a world quest, Dr. Edith asks for oak leaves to study the wind; they turn out to carry Anemo energy.
### ULTRA +
The witch Alice, in her Teyvat Travel Guide, searched the oak for a launching device that might explain how Vennessa ascended; finding none, she tested her theory by hurling hilichurls, and the farthest flew only as far as the hunters' huts near Springvale.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Constant gentle wind, long grass and the sound of leaves, with the sea nearby. It feels like a memorial more than a settlement: people come to rest, to read, or to be alone.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Mostly mild: hilichurl fighters, shooters and shield guards, and Cryo, Dendro, Hydro and Pyro slimes. A Knight of Favonius patrols the path.

## travel
### STANDARD
Reached overland from Mondstadt City; the oak is visible from far off and the Statue of the Seven is a fixed landmark.

## quests
### LARGE
Relevant: the Prologue (Venti recovering at the tree), the Leaves on the Wind world quest, and Lupus Majoris Chapter Act I (6.4).

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 Windrise is unchanged: a peaceful landmark and memorial.
### STANDARD +
Nothing newer is recorded here; the Prologue-era scenes at the oak are its main canon, plus Varka's return in 6.4 (see the entry below).

## entry: Vennessa's Ascension and the Oak
@keys: Vennessa's ascension, ascended to Celestia, Symbol of Mondstadt's Hero, giant oak, great oak
@keys2: Windrise, Mondstadt
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Legend holds that Vennessa, the first Grand Master, was recognised by the gods for her deeds and ascended to Celestia at Windrise, taking a falcon's form. The oak is believed to have sprung up there afterwards. It is not proven, and surviving accounts of her later life disagree. The tree also has some link to the Anemo Archon's power: Venti rests beneath it to heal.
