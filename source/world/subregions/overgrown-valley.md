## summary
@availability: limited
### LITE
The Overgrown Valley is a green, canyon-like subarea of the Veluriyam Mirage, the bottle-shaped domain from the limited-time Version 3.8 event Secret Summer Paradise. It is the home of the Choo-Choo Cart rail line and its three keepers, the brothers Ferdinand, Jeroney and Haddo. It existed only as an event area and is not part of the permanent map.
### STANDARD +
The valley is reached in Act I of the event, when Klee mentions that a giant canyon has appeared where the group was investigating. Its attractions are built around rails: carts powered by Hydro Eidolons run through it, and a thrill ride called the Torrential Twister is its centrepiece.

## geography
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
A valley of vines, hills and cave mouths inside the Mirage, crossed by rail tracks and junctions for the Choo-Choo Cart. A cave in the southeast has two chambers shown in the wiki gallery. Local specialty: the Kalpalata Lotus. Like all of the Mirage, it sits inside a bottle; the inside is much larger than the outside.
### LARGE +
The tracks have to be switched at junctions to reach different parts of the valley, and the carts are powered by Hydro Eidolons. The Mirage as a whole is described in canon as one gigantic Preprint, a flat blueprint made solid by a projector, and structures here appear to follow the same principle.

## people
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
Three brothers in Adventurers' Guild uniforms keep the line running: Jeroney, the careful one with black glasses; Haddo, the designer, called "the sickly brother"; and Ferdinand, the sturdier brother. Like every resident of the Mirage except Idyia, they are Hydro Eidolon replicas of real people who once visited the bottle.
### LARGE +
Idyia told the group that the three had been rescued from a sandstorm while on an adventure. Their jobs, in Ferdinand's words, are "brain" (Jeroney), "designer" (Haddo) and "brawn" (himself); the Hydro Eidolons do the actual heavy lifting. Because the brothers are replicas, they carry the dreams and trades of their human originals but not their memories or full personalities, which have to be taught.

## quests
@availability: limited
@version: 3.8
### STANDARD
In Zip Along (According to Safe Cart Operating Procedures), the Traveler's group (with Klee, Kaeya and Sangonomiya Kokomi) rides the Choo-Choo Cart along the rails, frees a Hydro Eidolon from a Preprint, finds Jeroney at a junction, helps Haddo power the tracks and tests the Torrential Twister. Kaeya meanwhile collects one of the Mirage's missing components. This is event-only content that ordinary Teyvat characters do not know about.
### LARGE +
The quest comes in the first act, An Invitation From Afar!, after Glaze Domain, Multum in Parvo and before Fairground Gathering, Summer Lights Illuminated. The components gathered across the Mirage are gifts earlier visitors left Idyia, which she uses to keep the central wheel of the Mirage turning.
### ULTRA +
The Mirage's rules also shape play here: Hydro Eidolons, nicknamed Water Droplets, often disguise themselves as scenery and cannot speak, so they invite strangers to help by gesture; some freeze in fear near enemies or high places. None of the valley's residents can be read by Nahida's ability, which the wiki notes as an oddity of the Mirage.

## atmosphere
@availability: limited
### LARGE
Clattering carts, bright rails curving between hills, and cheerful, slightly overworked guild-uniformed caretakers. The mood is a theme-park ride with real mechanical stakes: playful and sunny.

## dangers
@availability: limited
### LARGE
Light combat: hilichurls, Large Anemo and Large Pyro Slimes, Winged Dendroshrooms, Stretchy Anemo Fungi, and Hydro Mimic Cranes disguised as ordinary scenery. The greater hazards in play are missed rail switches.

## travel
@availability: limited
### LARGE
Reached by walking or riding the Choo-Choo Cart from the Silver Bottle Courtyard. Outsiders enter the Mirage itself through a Magic Invite or a summoned bottle, as Klee and the group did.

## status
@availability: limited
### LITE
As of 7.1 canon, the Overgrown Valley is part of a time-limited area from Version 3.8; no later story returns to it.
### STANDARD +
It features in no content after the Secret Summer Paradise event. Treat it as a place visited by the Traveler and a few companions in the summer of that event.

## entry: The Brothers of the Choo-Choo Cart
@keys: Ferdinand, Jeroney, Haddo, Torrential Twister
@keys2: Veluriyam Mirage, Overgrown Valley, Choo-Choo Cart
@availability: limited
@version: 3.8
### STANDARD
Ferdinand (strong, cheerful), Jeroney (the brains, black glasses) and Haddo (the designer, frail) run the cart line in the Overgrown Valley as Hydro Eidolon replicas of three real adventurers. Ferdinand jokes that the carts operate around the clock and that serious repairs are needed only when Haddo designs new track. Jeroney is found high up and reminds visitors to use safety ropes.
