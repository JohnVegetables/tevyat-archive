## summary
### LITE
The Forest of Blessings is the first area of Simulanka, the storybook world of the limited-time Summertide Scales and Tales event (4.8). Its wildlife is made of folded paper: frogs, hamsters, alpacas and flying squirrels. It holds the Kingdom of Breezes and Bells, a Hut of Blessings and a railway platform from which the Maritime Express runs to Constellation Metropole.
### STANDARD +
The Traveler and Paimon wake here after opening a strange book. Nilou helps the forest dwellers, and Visions do not work anywhere in Simulanka.

## geography
### STANDARD
A green forest where paper creatures talk and sing, with ponds and waterfalls (Rainsong Pond), a large tree, the Hut of Blessings and a Calligraphy Tavern. The Kingdom of Breezes and Bells lies on a path from the hut and is reached through a big tree where flying squirrels offer gifts and talk of trials.
### LARGE +
The area's gimmicks are paper frogs that leap between lily pads and sing in chorus, paper flying squirrels the player can steer through flower garlands, and a paper alpaca whose neck the player can move. The forest has a railway station platform: carriages can be linked there for the Maritime Express.

## people
### STANDARD
The paper residents are hamsters, frogs, alpacas and flying squirrels. They are friendly and quirky: a hamster guard with two alpaca attendants who speak for "the Boss", a hamster singer, hamsters hiding in barrels. Flying squirrels at the Kingdom of Breezes and Bells say the Princess is looking for a Guardian and ask whether visitors are there for the trials. Grandpa Almond, who needs a Magic Tonic, is one of the forest dwellers named in Page I.
### LARGE +
Nilou helps the forest dwellers in Page I and, in Page III, calls herself the Forest Fairy when she gives her blessing. The wiki's cast list for Page I also names Chestnut, Citrus, Ginkgo, Firecracker, Padisarah and Stream; their roles are not described there.
### ULTRA +
Small scenes reward kindness to the forest: a hamster performs on a little stage and, after applause, a Fontaine Mora chest appears; a hamster trapped in a barrel is freed; a hamster afraid of heights declines a flight but gives a gift. The area's paper alpacas tell visitors that rewards await those kind to the forest.

## history
@spoiler: major
@version: 4.8
### STANDARD
Simulanka was made by Alice, Barbeloth and Andersdotter of the Hexenzirkel, whom its people know as the Goddesses of Creation, Prophecy and Fate. In Page III, M's journal in the Goddess of Fate's study says Alice's magic made one of M's origami frogs talk, which led M to imagine a story of paper animals in a magical forest.
### LARGE +
Squirrels near the Kingdom of Breezes and Bells say that a huge figure fell and the houses collapsed, and that everyone has moved away; Page III later shows giant footprints left by Simulanka's evil dragon, which the party learns is a lonely creature that took the stars because it missed its mother-figure M (see the uncertainties).

## dangers
### STANDARD
Large Hydro Slimes and a Tainted Hydro Phantasm frighten an alpaca near the path; Winged Dendroshrooms and floating Dendro and Hydro Fungi appear in the quest to collect materials for the Magic Tonic.

## quests
@version: 4.8
### STANDARD
Page I, The Story Begins in a Forest Without Rain, opens the event: the Traveler wakes in the book, meets the forest dwellers, helps Nilou, collects tonic ingredients and gives the tonic to Grandpa Almond, then goes to the Calligraphy Tavern. Page II (They Once Walked the Starry Sky) starts at the forest's rail platform.

## status
### LITE
Not accessible in the game as of 7.1; the event ended on 2024-08-28.
