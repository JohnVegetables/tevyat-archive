## summary
### LITE
Mt. Tianheng is the mountain range that shelters Liyue Harbor from the west and north, a natural shield for the city. Old tablets say Rex Lapis raised it when he first came down to Liyue, and that the first people there mined jade from its depths. Today it holds the Golden House, where Mora is minted, Blackcliff Forge, the Guizhong Ballista and the cliff-side district of Yujing Terrace.
### STANDARD +
In-game description calls it the pride of the range that shelters the harbor, a natural shield against anyone who comes from the west. Records speak of many wars where enemies were stopped by it, and the old walls, forts and defences are still visible.

## geography
### STANDARD
The map labels the peaks west of Liyue Harbor as Mt. Tianheng. The wiki points out that the range north of the harbor, which the road to Guili Plains crosses, is also part of it. Yujing Terrace is built along its cliffside, and Chihu Rock connects to it on the west by bridge.
### LARGE +
Tianheng Pass is the passage the Guizhong Ballista was built to defend. Ruined walls, forts and defensive structures survive in the hills.
### ULTRA +
Development note: a level artist says the entrance to Liyue Harbor through Mt. Tianheng was inspired by Tianmen Mountain in China, to give arrival a sense of ceremony and a strong contrast between the pass and the trade city that opens up beyond it.

## places
### STANDARD
- The Golden House: the mint where all of Teyvat's Mora is made.
- Blackcliff Forge: the workshops that gave their name to the Blackcliff and Prototype weapon series.
- The Guizhong Ballista: an ancient automatic crossbow.
- The Jade Chamber: the Qixing's floating palace, here classed as part of Mt. Tianheng.
- Yujing Terrace, built into the cliffs.
### LARGE +
Blackcliff Forge was built by excavating the mountains and joining workshops and furnaces with tunnels. White Tassels are also produced there. Manager Kuan is its shift supervisor; Jingda and Iron Shoulder are workers. The Golden House has a steward, Gongming, and Millelith guards. The Geo and Anemo monument puzzles and a ruin near the teleport waypoint south of Guili Plains also lie in the range.

## history
### STANDARD
According to Stone Tablet Compilations, Rex Lapis lowered the tides, raised Mt. Tianheng and calmed the waves when he first descended. People cut into the mountain, found jade and built a settlement they named Shanhui Fort ("Mountain Splendor Fort"); everyone prospered from the mines. The goddess Guizhong set up the Guizhong Ballista to guard it. Later Guizhong led her people north of the range to the Guili Plains. After she fell in the Archon War, Rex Lapis led his people south of Mt. Tianheng, where Liyue Harbor was founded.
### LARGE +
The name Shanhui Fort was later shortened to "Shanhui Rock" and then "Mountain Rock". The mountain tribes are said to have sheltered in the tunnels of the forge in times of war, and long-forgotten passages are said to lead to an ancient underground realm. Liyue's earliest mining is believed to have started here. The Yun family's founder is credited with first tunnelling through the range.
### ULTRA +
The Guizhong Ballista was designed by Guizhong, under the name Obscuro Vulpes Mechanism, with help from adepti, among them Cloud Retainer. Rex Lapis judged it better than a mechanism Cloud Retainer had built. After Guizhong died, Cloud Retainer put finishing touches on it in her memory.

## people
### STANDARD
Blackcliff Forge workers and miners; Gongming and Millelith guards at the Golden House; Treasure Hoarders, Fatui skirmishers and other enemies in the ruins.
### LARGE +
Millelith soldiers are posted around the Guizhong Ballista after its repair. Ningguang's Jade Chamber floats above Liyue Harbor.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Fatui Skirmishers (Geochanter Bracers and Electrohammer Vanguards), hilichurl shooters, samachurls, slimes, Rock Shieldwall Mitachurls and Ruin Hunters wander the range. The Guizhong Ballista fires on perceived threats, including Treasure Hoarders approaching the ruins.
### LARGE +
A Geo Monument puzzle awakens an Eye of the Storm, an elite enemy.

## travel
### STANDARD
Liyue Harbor lies at the foot of the range. The main road from the north runs through Mt. Tianheng and arrives at Feiyun Slope. Another road leaves the city from Yujing Terrace past the Guizhong Ballista.

## quests
### LARGE
- Farewell, Archaic Lord (Archon Quest, 1.0): Zhongli and the Traveler repair the Guizhong Ballista with spare parts to hunt a "cocogoat" for Qiqi, and fight off Treasure Hoarders who had broken it. The Ballista is later mysteriously repaired overnight and the Millelith guard it.
- A New Star Approaches (1.1): the Traveler fights Childe at the Golden House, then Osial; the Jade Chamber is sacrificed.
### ULTRA +
In the Interlude Chapter (2.4), "Where the Heart Finds Rest" starts in Mt. Tianheng, where the rebuilt Jade Chamber is used to lure the sea monster Beisht, while Ballistae fire from Guyun Stone Forest.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 Mt. Tianheng still shelters Liyue Harbor, with the Golden House no longer minting. No content from 6.x or 7.0-7.1 changes it.
### STANDARD +
The Golden House no longer mints Mora since the Geo Gnosis was handed to the Tsaritsa. The Jade Chamber has been rebuilt (2.4).

## entry: The Golden House
@keys: Golden House
@keys2: Mt. Tianheng, Mora, Liyue
@version: 1.1
### STANDARD
The Golden House is where all of Teyvat's Mora is minted. After the Rite of Descension the Qixing moved the dead god's body, the Exuvia, here. The Traveler faced Childe there before Osial's attack. After Rex Lapis handed his Gnosis to the Tsaritsa, it stopped minting new Mora.

## entry: Blackcliff Forge
@keys: Blackcliff Forge, Manager Kuan
### STANDARD
Blackcliff Forge is known as the birthplace of the Blackcliff and Prototype series weapons and also makes White Tassels. Its workshops and furnaces were carved into the mountains and joined by tunnels. Mountain tribes are said to have sheltered in the tunnels in wartime and unexplored passages lead to an ancient underground realm. The Yun family's founder is credited with first tunnelling through these mountains.

## entry: The Guizhong Ballista
@keys: Guizhong Ballista, Obscuro Vulpes Mechanism
### STANDARD
A giant automatic crossbow built by Guizhong, the late Goddess of Dust, and the adepti to protect Liyue. It fires at any perceived threat nearby, even Treasure Hoarders. After Guizhong's death Cloud Retainer finished it in her memory. A notice at the supply post says its spare parts should only be collected by a worthy craftsman if "that ancient evil" returns. Replica ballistae were used against Osial at the Jade Chamber, and enhanced ones against Beisht at Guyun Stone Forest.

## entry: Skybracer and the Propped-Up Mountain
@keys: Skybracer
@keys2: Mt. Tianheng, antlers
@version: 1.3
@availability: limited
### LARGE
In a tale told by Madame Ping at Lantern Rite, an enemy god's blow during the Archon War nearly toppled Mt. Tianheng onto a village at its foot. The adeptus Skybracer had a friend cut off his antlers, a gift made of Morax's divine power and the hardest substance in Liyue, and wedged them under the mountain, which rose higher than before. He kept fighting until he died of his wounds, and his blood is said to have become the Bishui River. The legend is told during the Lantern Rite event and is not confirmed elsewhere.

## entry: The Archon War and the Move South
@keys: Guili Assembly, Archon War, Guizhong
@keys2: Mt. Tianheng, Liyue Harbor, Guili Plains
### LARGE
The goddess Guizhong lived with her people on the Guili Plains north of Mt. Tianheng, tending the soil and allied with Rex Lapis. Warfare among the gods later devastated the plains and killed her. Rex Lapis led his people south of Mt. Tianheng, and they never returned. The plains became wilderness. Liyue Harbor grew from that settlement.
