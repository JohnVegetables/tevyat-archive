## summary
### LITE
Byakko Plain is a low, sparsely wooded field in the southwest of Narukami Island, Inazuma. The main road from Konda Village to Inazuma City runs through its centre, and wild kitsune can be found near the city's outskirts. Its name means "White Fox Plain".
### STANDARD +
It is the countryside just outside Inazuma City: roadside notices, a few vendors, farming folk and foxes. The small festival island of Amakane Island lies off its western coast.

## geography
### STANDARD
Low, open and thinly wooded, the plain occupies the southwest of Narukami Island. Its road links Konda Village with Inazuma City, and a shoreline path runs from it to Chinju Forest. Sakura Bloom grows here as a local specialty.
### LARGE +
Exploration features include an Earth Kitsune Statue, Electro Cubes, an Electro Seelie, Thunder Barriers and Thunder Sakura Boughs.

## people
### STANDARD
The wiki lists the NPCs Kid Kujirai and Yasuda, and the vendors Kujirai Momiji and Wakamurasaki.
### LARGE +
Missing person bulletin boards stand along the road, one at the crossroads north of the plain's waypoint. They carry a notice from Saimon Katsumi, a farmer from Konda Village, seeking news of his son Saimon Jirou, who joined the army over a year earlier and stopped writing. The world quest The Farmer's Treasure resolves this; afterwards the boards can no longer be used.

## culture
### STANDARD
The in-game expedition description says wild foxes will not appear before a traveller whose fate is not entwined with theirs twice, and that a bushy tail vanishing over the horizon is a sign of a final farewell. It is a folk saying about kitsune rather than a rule of canon.
### ULTRA +
The wiki's trivia also notes that byakko is a homophone of the White Tiger of the Four Symbols (Chinese constellations); this is an out-of-game observation about the name only and does not affect the plain's depiction.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Wind in thin trees, a dirt road, drifting sakura petals, thunder barriers in the distance, and the occasional fox disappearing into the grass.

## status

As of 7.1, Byakko Plain is unchanged.
