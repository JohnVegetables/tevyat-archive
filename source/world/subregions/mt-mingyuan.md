## summary
### LITE
Mt. Mingyuan is a mountain in Chenyu Vale's Upper Vale in Liyue. It stands west of Yilong Wharf and east of Fontaine, directly under Celestia, whose floating island is visible straight overhead. The world quest The Dealing Sands begins here.
### STANDARD +
A Millelith camp lies on its southern slope, and a beach to its west was the setting for a sting against smugglers.

## geography
### STANDARD
The wiki places it west of Yilong Wharf, east of Fontaine and under Celestia; a Treasure Hoarder camp sits west of the southern waypoint. The page lists Iron Dou'er as its NPC.
### LARGE +
Standing on the mountain, the floating island of Celestia is visible directly above. A beach west of the mountain and a cliff above it were used in the quest, with the cliff's foot the meeting place for a deal.

## people
### STANDARD
Iron Dou'er, Iron Chuan'er and other "Iron" Treasure Hoarders loiter about the beach west of the mountain; Dou'er and Chuan'er turn out to be Millelith in disguise. At a Millelith camp on the southern slope the quest page lists Hongyu, Qingye (both in hoarder outfits) and Yijun.
### LARGE +
Iron Dou'er has relatives in Liyue Harbor and recognised the Traveler at once but kept quiet so as not to spook the smugglers. The boss of the Iron and Salt Gang is Iron Ming; the buyer is Johnnie, called the Pinky Club's Pinky and from Fontaine, with a companion called Walker.

## history
### STANDARD
In The Dealing Sands the Millelith spent about a month infiltrating the Iron and Salt Gang, which was smuggling goods to buyers from Fontaine. When the Traveler stumbled onto a deal at the beach, the Millelith revealed themselves, fought Johnnie and Iron Ming, and arrested the smugglers. They paid the Traveler in Mora and said such helpers should be rewarded so that others would help too.
### LARGE +
Iron Ming had planned to silence the Traveler under what he called an unspoken contract of outlaws, and boasted of the prices that rival buyers' families paid for his cargo, citing Liyue's love of contracts. Iron Dou'er answered that they would stop villains like him from befouling Liyue.
### ULTRA +
A ragged notebook at a hoarder camp west of the southern waypoint is thought by the wiki to belong to the same ring. It mentions Pinky Club buyers waiting on a beach, wariness of Fontainian underhandedness, and goods that must be covered by tea leaves and porcelain.

## quests
### STANDARD
The Dealing Sands starts at Mt. Mingyuan with Iron Dou'er. The player goes to the beach beneath the cliff, joins talks between the two parties, defeats Johnnie and then Iron Ming, and speaks to the Millelith. It rewards the achievement Breaking Iron and Stone.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Treasure Hoarders are present, though the "hoarders" at the camp and beach are mostly Millelith in costume; real hoarders and Fontainian smugglers appear in the quest.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
A windy mountain under the vast shadow of Celestia, with a beach, a camp and tension of a secret operation. This is a suggested scene mood drawn from the trivia and the quest.

## status
### STANDARD
The smuggling ring is broken and its leaders arrested; nothing later changes it as of 7.1.
