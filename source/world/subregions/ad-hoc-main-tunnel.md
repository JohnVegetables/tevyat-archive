## summary
### LITE
The Ad-Hoc Main Tunnel is the main way into the Chasm's Underground Mines in Liyue: two huge caves joined by a smaller one. Its name suggests a tunnel dug quickly for one purpose, reaching the deeper mine. Lamps, black mud, Treasure Hoarders and slimes fill it.
### STANDARD +
The Chasm Exploration Team's noticeboard stands here, and a burnt miner's log hints at what drove the miners out. The deeper tunnels beyond were sealed when the miners dispersed.

## geography
### STANDARD
The area is built from two huge caves with a smaller cave between them, and it is described as the mine's main entrance. Lumenlamps and glowing Luminous Seelie give light; Dark Mud, Oozing Concretions and Spoutrock occur here; Cor Lapis and Noctilucous Jade can be gathered.
### LARGE +
A viewpoint here, "The Blocked Path", looks over the sealed passages. Its caption says the miners shut the tunnels leading further in when they scattered but left one "Mountainator" blasting cannon behind, and guesses that the higher-ups at the Qixing ordered it, since Liyue's trade could not long be kept apart from Glazed Sand Crystal.

## history
### STANDARD
This was a working mine before the Chasm was closed. A damaged log records that an excavation team was to be disbanded, with its collected data handed to a messenger from Huishan Hall, and that Treasure Hoarders might be to blame for something. A second page says the detailed data went to the secretary at Yujing Terrace (the wiki identifies her as Ganyu) so that the long-term effects on the Chasm could be judged, and that a strange black mud had been found.
### LARGE +
The last page of the log is fragmentary: purple-black mud below, people, and something coming through a crack in a door. The pages carry strange purple-black burn marks. Canon here does not say what happened to the author.
### ULTRA +
The English name "Ad-Hoc" matches the Chinese name, which reads as Temporary Main Mining Tunnel. The wiki reads this as a tunnel dug in haste purely to reach the deeper workings; that reading is the wiki's, not stated in game.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Elite foes are Shadowy Husk Defenders and Mirror Maidens. Common ones are Geo and Hydro Slimes (including large ones) and several kinds of Treasure Hoarders: Crushers, Pugilists, Scouts and Electro, Hydro and Pyro Potioneers.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
A mine emptied in a hurry: lamp glow on rock, abandoned notices and equipment, black mud on the floor, and hoarders' voices carrying through the caves. This is a suggested scene mood drawn from the features above, not a quoted description.

## travel
### STANDARD
Reached through the Chasm after the Chasm Delvers questline opens the Underground Mines (2.6). The Exploration Team Campsite Noticeboard stands here.

## status
### STANDARD
As of 7.1 it remains an abandoned mine passage used by the Exploration Team; later story versions add nothing to it.

## entry: Chasm Exploration Team

### STANDARD
The Chasm Exploration Team is a group hired by the Ministry of Civil Affairs to investigate anomalies in the Chasm; its base is the Chasm Exploration Team Camp in the Underground Mines. A noticeboard for the camp stands in the Ad-Hoc Main Tunnel. The team is the focus of the Chasm Delvers world quests, after which Muning makes it an official part of Huishan Hall.
