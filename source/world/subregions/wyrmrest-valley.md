## summary
### LITE
Wyrmrest Valley is a steep valley on Dragonspine, Mondstadt, where the black dragon Durin fell about 500 years ago. His skull, ribcage and heart lie there, and the life force seeping from his core has dyed the silver snow scarlet. It is cold, dangerous and eerie, and the Fatui have been interested in it.
### STANDARD +
Durin was an Abyssal dragon created by Rhinedottir. Dvalin brought him down in the cataclysm after he ravaged Mondstadt. His poisoned blood later helped drive Dvalin to become Stormterror.

## geography
### STANDARD
A snowy ravine on Dragonspine whose slopes are stained red in places. Features: Ancient Rime, Ruin Braziers, Scarlet Quartz and Warming Seelie; a Teleport Waypoint lies at its northern edge, near where patrols station. The wiki notes the skull faces west-northwest, towards where Celestia floats.
### LARGE +
Official text names the dragon's heart, still beating beneath the valley, as a source of warmth: it energises Strange Teeth into Empowered Dragonteeth. Dragon blood has reached Dragonspine's ley lines, and the Frostbearing Tree revived. Scarlet Quartz seems to be a gemstone formed from Durin's remains and carries his blood toxins.

## people
### STANDARD
Esther, an environmentalist, studies Dragonspine's terrain and gives out Frostshield Potions. Tommy, an adventurer, and Riese, a Knight of Favonius, are met at the border of the valley on patrol. The Dragonspine Adventurer Camp, where Orban and Iris are based, lies elsewhere on the mountain.
### LARGE +
In The Festering Fang, Orban at the camp has the Traveler gather Strange Teeth, one of which lies beside the heart.

## history
### STANDARD
Durin was summoned by Rhinedottir as one of her miraculous creations. He is said to have had a gentle heart and to have seen his rampage as a long dream. Dvalin pierced his neck above Vindagnyr; he fell into this valley, giving the mountain the name Dragonspine.
### LARGE +
The Fatui sought to use Durin's heart in the world quest The Festering Fang; the Traveler blocked them. Dvalin, worn and poisoned by Durin's blood, slept in Decarabian's tower until the Abyss Order deceived him.
### ULTRA +
Durin's heart was drained in Paralogism (5.6); see the entry below.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Freezing and silent, with red stains on pale snow and the sense of a vast thing lying under the earth.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Extreme cold needs warmth or cold-resistance. Fatui Skirmishers of several elements and Cryo Cicin Mages patrol.

## travel
### STANDARD
Reached by climbing Dragonspine from the camp.

## quests
### STANDARD
The Festering Fang (world quest), The Gardener (Paralogism, Interlude Chapter Act IV).

## status
### LITE
Durin's core is drained and inert as of 7.1; the dragon is replaced by the human Durin.

## entry: Draining Durin's Core
@keys: Paralogism Durin, Mini Durin, drained Durin's core
@keys2: Wyrmrest Valley, Albedo, Dragonspine
@version: 5.6
@spoiler: major
### ULTRA
In Paralogism (5.6), Albedo brought the Traveler to the heart, drained its core with the alchemical array and used it to transmute Mini Durin, a kind dragon from Simulanka, into a synthetic human body. Afterwards Albedo said the rest of the heart would gradually lose all vitality. The core is now dormant and no longer makes Empowered Dragonteeth, although it still emits warmth; the tooth conversion is available instead at the Alchemist's Crafting Bench next to Orban.
