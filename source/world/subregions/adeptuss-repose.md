## summary
### LITE
Adeptus's Repose is the former home of the Herblord, an adeptus of Chenyu Vale who healed mortals with medicines. It lies in the Southern Mountain of Chenyu Vale, behind an entrance choked with miasma, and holds her medicine jar, a withered adeptal tree and notes she left behind. The Herblord is long gone from here; her later life is as Changsheng, the snake companion of Baizhu.
### STANDARD +
Visitors can enter either by clearing the miasma around the entrance or through the world quest Silently the Butterfly Crosses the Valley (4.4), in which the Traveler restores the adeptal tree by gathering the scattered butterflies the Herblord once used to adorn her door.

## geography
### STANDARD
Adeptus's Repose is a sheltered cave-dwelling in the Southern Mountain of Chenyu Vale, linked to Lingshu Courtyard and Yaodie Valley. Inside are a bookshelf-lined vault, a medicine jar with a mechanism linked to the door, and a tree branch planted in it; to the west lies Lingshu Courtyard.
### LARGE +
The cave's soil and water are said to be nutritious; the branch the Herblord planted kept growing. The entrance is blocked by miasma, which can be cleared. A hint from the Carefree Simulacrum says that a branch from the revived adeptal tree, put in the broken jar outside the abode, opens a stone gate.

## places
### STANDARD
- The medicine jar and the withered tree: the jar nourishes the tree.
- The stone gate and door mechanism, linked to the jar with adeptal energy.
- A hidden vault with bookshelves holding the Ancient Texts.
- Lingshu Courtyard to the west, the neighbouring courtyard with its Carefree Simulacrum.
### LARGE +
Notes inside speak of how the door mechanism is connected to the jar in which the branch is planted, and how it breathes life-giving adeptal energy into the mechanism; the jar holding the mechanism was damaged long ago.
### ULTRA +
A wiki gallery note says a stone slate in the Repose came from a shelter's mural on Chizhang Wall and shows the Herblord with Fujin.

## people
### STANDARD
No one lives here. The Herblord, who once did, left when she lost the power to keep a human form. In the neighbouring Lingshu Courtyard, talking stone statues called Simulacra still scare off intruders in her name.
### LARGE +
The Herblord served the Mistress of Chenyu Vale with her friends Fujin and Lingyuan, until the three turned against their god in the Archon War.

## history
### STANDARD
The Herblord was an adeptus with the original form of a giant white snake, who took human form to make medicines and heal the sick. The unsigned notes in the Repose read as private jottings, kept locked away, and the wiki links them to the Herblord: a chatty fish friend (taken to be Fujin), a beast friend who asked for slimming tea (taken to be Lingyuan), and a master about whom the writer had a bad feeling. Fearing something might happen, the writer split off some adeptal energy and put it into butterflies, so that mortals of Chenyu Vale might later follow them to the formulae left behind.
### LARGE +
In the Archon War the Mistress of Chenyu Vale flooded the Bishui River in a last attempt to defeat Rex Lapis. The Herblord and Fujin defected to save the people; the Herblord fought her master and Fujin threw the Votive Rainjade into the water. The Herblord was almost destroyed, reduced to a tiny form, lost her human shape and her memories, and eventually left her abode. Her legend faded to a name. Much later, a doctor seeking a cure found a weak white snake at her home and made a contract with her; she became known as Changsheng, and the contract passed through his successors down to Baizhu.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Still, hidden and a little forlorn: empty medicine containers, a tree that can be revived and butterflies scattered by an absent mistress.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Miasma blocks the entrance. Once cleared, the cave is peaceful.

## travel
### STANDARD
Enter by clearing the miasma around the entrance, or through the butterflies quest: after the tree is revived, an investigation spot in the water near the ancient tree takes the player to the Repose.

## quests
### LARGE
- Silently the Butterfly Crosses the Valley (world quest, 4.4): the Traveler is asked by a talking statue in Lingshu Courtyard to return butterflies to a medicine jar, revives an ancient tree, finds the Herblord's vault and reads her notes.
### ULTRA +
The Archon-era story of the Herblord is told further in the Chenyu Vale quests, including An Ancient Sacrifice of Sacred Brocade, where Fujin recognises Changsheng.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 Adeptus's Repose is a quiet, hidden abode with no occupants. No content from 6.x or 7.0-7.1 changes it.
### STANDARD +
The Herblord lives on as Changsheng, with Baizhu. The quest leaves the adeptal tree revived and the vault open.

## entry: The Herblord and Changsheng
@keys: Herblord, Changsheng
@keys2: Chenyu Vale, Adeptus's Repose, Baizhu
@version: 4.4
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The Herblord was a white-snake adeptus of Chenyu Vale who healed mortals. After her fight with the Mistress of Chenyu Vale she was reduced to a small snake and forgot her past. A doctor who had lost his family to a plague found her in her abode and agreed to the "Way of the Dragon-Dragging Jade Snake", which gives powerful healing arts at the cost of the contractor's own health; she became Changsheng. The contract passed down generations until it came to Baizhu, who promised it would end with him.

## entry: Adeptus's Notes and Ancient Texts
@keys: Ancient Text, Adeptus's Notes, Ancient Notes
@keys2: Adeptus's Repose, Lingshu Courtyard
@version: 4.4
### LARGE
The notes in the Repose record a mix of practical and personal matters: a branch she planted keeps growing; a door mechanism connected to a jar breathes in adeptal energy; a talkative fish friend who always brings up "Motive Mainjade"; a request for slimming tea, which she grudgingly makes because it may help mortals one day; and a talk with her master that leaves her uneasy. She decides to inject part of her adeptal energy into butterflies, so that Chenyu Vale's mortals might find her formulae later.
