## summary
### LITE
Mt. Aocang is the mountain of the adeptus Cloud Retainer, one of the great peaks of Jueyun Karst in Minlin. After the Archon War she lived here in seclusion behind a sealed abode, and pilgrims who sought her blessing mostly went away empty-handed. Today she lives in Liyue Harbor as the mechanic Xianyun, but the mountain is still hers.
### STANDARD +
Its heart is a lake with a stone table laid for three, where offerings are left for the adepti. The Clear Pool and Mountain Cavern domain lies on its north side. A tablet describes Cloud Retainer as the Free Adeptus, who performs no action and sets no rule.

## geography
### STANDARD
A tall mountain in Minlin, in the adeptal highlands of Jueyun Karst. A stairway climbs to a lake at the top, in the middle of which stands a stone table with three chairs. The Clear Pool and Mountain Cavern domain lies to the north.
### LARGE +
Cloud Retainer's abode is sealed by a large talisman-barrier at the top. A tablet on the slope above the stairs speaks of the Free Adeptus. There is a fishing point on the mountain, and one of the three crane statues of the Three Cranes puzzle stands on the summit.
### ULTRA +
Cloud Retainer also created the floating island called the Dwelling in the Clouds above Qingyun Peak, which she built as a private sanctuary for reflection.

## places
### STANDARD
- The lake and stone table: three chairs marked "Retainer", "Guizhong" and "Rex". Retainer's has a wine jar, Guizhong's a bowl and chopsticks, and Rex's nothing.
- Cloud Retainer's abode: a dwelling of mechanisms, entered after an offering is made at the table.
- The Clear Pool and Mountain Cavern domain.
- Ruin-treasure monuments of a disciple's story.
### LARGE +
The tablet reads: "Tranquility of mind and purity of heart... the Free Adeptus, The Unfettered... performs no action and sets no rule." Cooking pots near the table come with recipes for dishes offered to the adepti. The monuments around the mountain and the lake record the doubts of a disciple, who says he buried what treasures he owned by the pool.
### ULTRA +
On the mountain is a Suspensus Somnium Mechanism, a device of Cloud Retainer's that releases a soft breeze and adeptal tune to relax a listener into a half-dreaming state that can help restore lost memories.

## people
### STANDARD
Cloud Retainer, an adeptus in the form of a crane, who now lives in Liyue Harbor as Xianyun. Her disciples Ganyu and Shenhe grew up under her care; Shenhe was taken in as a child. A wild crane called Yuandai once followed her faithfully here.
### LARGE +
Moon Carver of Qingyun Peak and Mountain Shaper of Mt. Hulao are friends and neighbours. Madame Ping and Xiao know her too; Xiao fought alongside her in the war and remembers her as solemn and taciturn then.

## culture
### STANDARD
Pilgrims have come to Mt. Aocang since ancient times in search of adeptal blessings, and most returned empty-handed. Offerings of cooked dishes are left at the stone table.
### LARGE +
Cloud Retainer noticed the Sigil of Permission the Traveler carried and the sincerity of their offering. A saying quoted about adepti runs that adepti come and go without a trace and green moss grows on stones in the spring breeze.

## history
### STANDARD
After the Archon War, Cloud Retainer settled on Mt. Aocang and lived in isolation. The table's three chairs suggest she, Guizhong and Rex Lapis once dined here as friends (an inference from the engraved names). She and Guizhong quarrelled over ideals, yet after Guizhong's death she finished Guizhong's Ballista in her memory. In the Archon Quest of 1.0 the Traveler offered her dishes at the stone table and crossed her abode to speak to her.
### LARGE +
Ganyu's story quest (1.2) has Cloud Retainer set up a trial near Mt. Aocang at Ganyu's own request, after Ganyu says she feels a separation from humans. In 4.4, curious what draws her disciples to Liyue Harbor, she took human form and moved into the city.
### ULTRA +
The "Jadevoid" name for an adeptus' dwelling comes from her Dwelling in the Clouds, which she lent to the adepti for their heavenly trial with Rex Lapis' consent; it has returned to her care since few can follow the adeptal path now.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Quiet and remote, high above the sea of clouds, with a lake, a laid table and stairs that feel like a place meant for visits that rarely happen.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Geovishaps, their hatchlings and Eyes of the Storm. Mortals regard the adepti's peaks as forbidden, and uninvited visitors may not be welcome.

## travel
### STANDARD
Reached by the mountain path and stairs from Jueyun Karst; the abode itself opens only after an offering is made at the stone table.

## quests
### LARGE
- Custodian of Clouds (Archon Quest, 1.0): the Traveler cooks offerings at the stone table and crosses Cloud Retainer's abode to meet her.
- Sea of Clouds, Sea of People (Ganyu story quest, 1.2): Cloud Retainer sets Ganyu a trial near the mountain.
- A Thousand Moonlit Miles (Xianyun story quest, 4.4): Cloud Retainer takes human form, meets the Traveler at the table, and uses her device here to restore the memories of the crane Yuandai.
### ULTRA +
Lantern Rite events that return to the mountain are limited-time: the 4.4 event (a lost kite), 5.3 (Zhongli, Xianyun, Madame Ping, Mountain Shaper and Moon Carver gather here) and 6.3 (the adepti seal it off during Zizhi's possession of Mountain Shaper).

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 Mt. Aocang is still Cloud Retainer's mountain. She keeps her abode but lives in Liyue Harbor as Xianyun.
### STANDARD +
No content from 6.x or 7.0-7.1 changes the mountain. Xianyun is a playable character.

## entry: Cloud Retainer's Abode and the Stone Table
@keys: Cloud Retainer, Xianyun, Retainer
@keys2: Mt. Aocang
### STANDARD
Cloud Retainer, the Free Adeptus, keeps a mechanical abode on Mt. Aocang, sealed by a talisman barrier. A stone table by the lake is set with three chairs for herself, Guizhong and Rex Lapis. When the Traveler arrived with a Sigil of Permission she sensed it and, moved by their sincere dishes, let them find their way through her abode to an audience. She is an expert in mechanical contraptions. Old records describe her human form as elegant in purple robes and her crane form as flying gracefully among the clouds; as Xianyun she has black updone hair and wears red-rimmed spectacles.

## entry: The Disciple's Unanswered Questions
@keys: Ruins Treasure, monsters evil, unanswered questions
@keys2: Mt. Aocang
### LARGE
A series of monuments around Mt. Aocang and by its lake are written by a disciple who trained from childhood with a fellow disciple to fight evil. He eventually received a Vision, and with it came doubts: whether monsters are evil by nature, whether mortals and adepti are good by nature, and whether people defend their way of life on blind judgements of good and evil. He studied the monsters' language and ancient texts, hid in meditation, and left his questions for a reader who might one day answer. His fellow disciple chastised him and left. Monuments at Guyun Stone Forest record the other disciple's view.

## entry: Yuandai and the Suspensus Somnium Mechanism
@keys: Yuandai, Shuyu, Suspensus Somnium Mechanism
@keys2: Mt. Aocang, Xianyun
@version: 4.4
### LARGE
In A Thousand Moonlit Miles, Yuandai, an elderly woman suffering dementia, proves to have been a wild crane that gained sentience and was taken in by Cloud Retainer. Cloud Retainer prepared a ten-year potion of human mimicry for her. She fell in love with an exorcist from Mt. Tianheng, stayed with him after it lapsed and later lost her memory. Seeing Mt. Aocang frightens her; at the abode the Suspensus Somnium Mechanism helps her relive her memories, and she recognises Cloud Retainer. She reverts to a crane, without sentience. Her granddaughter Shuyu becomes Xianyun's first mortal disciple.

## entry: Lantern Rite Visits to Mt. Aocang
@keys: Houzhang, Jiehu, Zizhi
@keys2: Mt. Aocang
@availability: limited
### LARGE
During 4.4's Lantern Rite event, Moon Carver and Mountain Shaper, who had taken human forms (Houzhang and Jiehu), flew a Fontainian motor kite near Mt. Aocang and lost it; Xianyun, who was running a kite workshop, caught it. In 5.3's event, Zhongli, Xianyun, Madame Ping, Mountain Shaper and Moon Carver talked here about a resolved fetor problem and Hu Tao, the director of Wangsheng Funeral Parlor. In 6.3's event, Zizhi possessed Mountain Shaper and sent him to plant mint on Mt. Aocang, so the adepti sealed it off for the Traveler to speak to Zizhi.
