## summary
### LITE
Wavey Bay is the seaside subarea of Easybreeze Holiday Resort in Natlan, known for sandy beaches, pristine waters and many aquatic attractions.
### STANDARD +
The main facilities are announced on info boards: The Twilight Cruise, Rainbow Beams, the Razzle-Dazzle Mega Slide and Asha Speedrun.

## places
### STANDARD
The Twilight Cruise is a cooperative ship-guiding game requiring timing and teamwork. Rainbow Beams is a paint-ball matching shooter. The Razzle-Dazzle Mega Slide is a seaside glide along the Spiritway. Asha Speedrun is a challenging race across a chosen route, suited to sporty tourists.
### LARGE +
Before the matching story quests (Time to Test the Twilight Cruise!, To Wavey Bay!, Time to Test the Asha Speedrun!) the info boards mark the facility as awaiting testing and ask visitors to find the staff for closed testing; afterwards they read "Operating". Five boards stand around the bay, one beside "Muchih" and one near each major attraction.

## people
### LARGE
Listed residents are "Muchih", Aqo, Chatubaya, Hayaq, Hemaki, Jayubal, Kalane, Poteh, Quemitl, Tuksina, Tzapan and Wachana.

## quests
### STANDARD
Story quests: Sunspray Summer Resort Part II (The World Is Your Canvas!, To Wavey Bay!, Time to Test the Twilight Cruise!, Time to Test the Asha Speedrun!). World quests: Shine On, Pipilpan Idol! and The Last Survivor of Tenochtzitoc.

## history
### ULTRA
A sung note found here, addressed to Tecuhtli, warns of three things: Black Boar Rekis probably sending men to grab Asha, Icahua possibly lurking nearby as a sharp, sneaky shot, and that the second warning is the most important. It ties into the pirate plot also seen on Tete Isle.

## status
### STANDARD
As of 7.1 it is a permanent explorable subarea added in 5.8.
