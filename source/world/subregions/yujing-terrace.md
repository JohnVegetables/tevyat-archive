## summary
### LITE
Yujing Terrace is the westernmost and wealthiest district of Liyue Harbor, built into the cliffside of Mt. Tianheng above the bustling port. It is where the Liyue Qixing and their staff work at Yuehai Pavilion, where Bubu Pharmacy stands on a small island, and where the great public rites of Liyue, the Rite of Descension and later the Rite of Parting, have been held. It is quiet, orderly and wealthy compared with the lower districts.
### STANDARD +
The viewpoint text describes it as far from the harbor's din, rising with Mt. Tianheng, where the Qixing and the leaders of many enterprises work tirelessly for Liyue's prosperity and stability. The Chinese name is rendered literally as "Jade-Capital Tower".

## geography
### STANDARD
Yujing Terrace is the westernmost part of the city, along the cliffs of Mt. Tianheng and overlooking the harbor. Its entrance is at the north, where a crossroads links it with Feiyun Slope, the small island that Bubu Pharmacy stands on and the road leaving the city toward the Guizhong Ballista.
### LARGE +
Inside the district are Yuehai Pavilion, the Yiyan Temple with its altar and censers where public rites are held, and Bubu Pharmacy's island; the district's viewpoint is on the pharmacy's roof. A fish pond lies nearby. The Jade Chamber floats in the air above Liyue and the Golden House is in the Mt. Tianheng area.

## places
### STANDARD
- Yuehai Pavilion: the seat of the Liyue Qixing and the place they meet to discuss official matters. Their secretaries and the Millelith work from here.
- Yiyan Temple: a temple with an altar in front of it where large public ceremonies take place; a guide named Bu'yun stands here for visitors to the Jade Chamber.
- Bubu Pharmacy: renowned as the finest pharmacy in Liyue, run by Baizhu, with Qiqi as an employee and Herbalist Gui at the counter. It is busiest during and after Lantern Rite.
### LARGE +
- Yuehai Pavilion also houses Ganyu, the Qixing's general secretary, and the secretary Huixin. Madame Ping spends her days there, and the Millelith sergeant Fengyan and soldiers such as Jinzhong and Tieshan guard it.
- A fish pond near the terrace is where Madame Ping and Xianyun are seen talking.
- The district's approach road leads past the Guizhong Ballista, an ancient crossbow on Mt. Tianheng.
### ULTRA +
Other named people listed for the district include Sturdy Wu and Musheng at the pavilion and Hongdou, Chenxiang and Ren'yu near the pharmacy.

## people
### STANDARD
The Qixing: Ningguang (Tianquan), Keqing (Yuheng) and Uncle Tian (Tianshu), who can be met at the harbor in daytime, along with their general secretary Ganyu. Madame Ping, an adeptus who lives among mortals, keeps company there by day. Baizhu and Qiqi run the pharmacy.
### LARGE +
Ganyu supports the whole of the Qixing as general secretary and has done so since the organisation began. Ningguang employs her own secretaries, Baiwen, Baishi and Baixiao, and keeps a private palace on the Jade Chamber.

## governance
### STANDARD
The Qixing are seven merchants who govern Liyue; the Tianquan keeps the book of laws and handles foreign relations, and the Yuheng manages land and construction. After Rex Lapis' apparent death they announced that he had died in a divine trial and took full governance. The adepti accepted mortal rule.
### LARGE +
Since then the Qixing have begun re-examining the old laws, and have had to deal with the fallout of the crisis, including relations with the Fatui.

## culture
### STANDARD
This is where Liyue's official ceremonies and prayers are held. During the Rite of Descension people gathered here to make wishes at censers before an altar; the belief was that a wish made at this time received the blessing of the adepti and Rex Lapis.
### LARGE +
Under custom, one Qixing member presides over the Rite each year, and Rex Lapis' dragon-qilin form appears to deliver business predictions. The Rite of Parting, a funeral rite for adepti or gods that uses Glaze Lily, Cleansing Bell and Everlasting Incense as offerings, was held before Yiyan Temple for Rex Lapis.

## history
### STANDARD
In the Archon Quest (1.0) Ningguang opened the Rite of Descension on the terrace and Rex Lapis' lifeless body dropped onto the offering table. She called for the exits to be sealed and the Millelith swept the crowd, arresting those near the altar. The Traveler and Paimon escaped with Childe's help. The body, the Exuvia, was later moved to the Golden House.
### LARGE +
After the Osial crisis the Rite of Parting was held at the altar before Yiyan Temple (1.1). Ningguang spoke of the god's passing as the end of the contract and of an era, asked whether the people would stand with the Qixing as they built a new age of prosperity, and the crowd signalled agreement. Ganyu acted as announcer. The Qixing's public statement was that Rex Lapis had passed in a trial.
### ULTRA +
The Jade Chamber, which Madame Ping says Ningguang began as a small room built from savings in her early business days, was dropped on Osial in the crisis and rebuilt in 2.4.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Calm, orderly and well kept, with the harbor spread out below. Officials, secretaries and soldiers move between pavilions, and wish-makers gather around the censers before ceremonies.

## dangers
### LARGE
Few. The danger is mostly political and security-related: when Rex Lapis' body fell, the Millelith sealed the terrace and treated anyone near the altar as a suspect.

## travel
### STANDARD
From Feiyun Slope a crossroads leads to the terrace. The same crossroads also gives access to Bubu Pharmacy's island and the road out of the city toward the Guizhong Ballista.

## quests
### LARGE
- Of the Land Amidst Monoliths (Archon Quest, 1.0): the Rite of Descension and the discovery of Rex Lapis' body.
- A New Star Approaches (Archon Quest, 1.1): the Rite of Parting.
- A Thousand Moonlit Miles (Xianyun's story quest, 4.4): Ganyu and Huixin meet Xianyun at Yuehai Pavilion.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 Yujing Terrace is still the seat of the Qixing, who govern Liyue without Rex Lapis. No content from 6.x or 7.0-7.1 changes it.
### STANDARD +
Ningguang, Keqing and Uncle Tian remain the Qixing; the Jade Chamber, destroyed in the Osial crisis, was rebuilt in 2.4.

## entry: Yuehai Pavilion
@keys: Yuehai Pavilion, Huixin
### STANDARD
Yuehai Pavilion is the Qixing's working seat on Yujing Terrace. They meet here to settle official matters, though there are days when all seven are elsewhere. Ganyu works as its general secretary, supported by the secretary Huixin. Madame Ping, Millelith soldiers and clerks come and go. Keqing often works away from it at the wharves and the Chasm, and Ningguang retreats to the Jade Chamber to decide hard matters.

## entry: The Rite of Descension
@keys: Rite of Descension
@keys2: Yujing Terrace, Liyue Harbor
@version: 1.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The Rite of Descension is the yearly event at which, in the public story, Rex Lapis descends to give business predictions for Liyue, presided over by one Qixing member. In the year the Traveler attended, Ningguang called the god down, dark clouds gathered, and the Archon's lifeless body fell onto the offering table. She ordered the exits sealed. The Traveler fled with Childe's help. In truth the death was staged by Rex Lapis, who lives on as Zhongli.

## entry: The Rite of Parting
@keys: Rite of Parting, Yiyan Temple
@keys2: Yujing Terrace, Liyue Harbor
@version: 1.1
### STANDARD
After the Osial crisis the Qixing and the Ministry of Civil Affairs organised the funeral of Rex Lapis. The Millelith announced that he had passed in a divine trial and asked people not to spread rumours. At the altar before Yiyan Temple Ningguang and Keqing spoke to the crowd, with Ganyu announcing them. The rite was held without any adepti attending, and Zhongli, who had helped organise it, was elsewhere, at the Northland Bank.

## entry: Xianyun and Ganyu at Yuehai Pavilion
@keys: Xianyun, Cloud Retainer, Huixin
@keys2: Yuehai Pavilion, Yujing Terrace
@version: 4.4
### LARGE
In A Thousand Moonlit Miles, Xianyun comes to Yuehai Pavilion in her human form and wants to speak to Ganyu alone, worried that an announced visit would make Ganyu insist she has no work for her and quietly make up for it later. Ganyu recognises her anyway and sends Huixin away, surprised to see her in human form. Xianyun later chats with Madame Ping by the pond near the terrace.
