## summary
### LITE
Tatarasuna is an island in Kannazuka, Inazuma, ringed by mountainous crags with a huge blast furnace, the Mikage Furnace, at its centre. It was where Inazuma smelted its famed Jade Steel, powered by Tatarigami energy. Twice it has gone wrong: about 400 years ago in the incident remembered as the Tatarasuna Mystery, and again in the war years when the furnace's runaway power and Fatui interest in it brought the Traveler to help. Today the furnace is purified and the area is quiet.
### STANDARD +
Its Japanese name is Tatara-suna, echoing the tatara, the traditional Japanese smelting furnace. In the Traveler's day the furnace sits inside a containment dome and its surroundings are tainted by Balethunder, until the Tatara Tales quest series settles it.

## geography
### STANDARD
A rough island of jagged, mountainous crags enclosing a central depression where the Mikage Furnace stands. It belongs to Kannazuka, near Yashiori Island, whose Tatarigami energy fed the furnace. The ground is stony and wind-scoured, dotted with thunder sakura, electric currents, lightning-strike probes and old Kamuijima cannons.
### LARGE +
The Mikage Furnace is surrounded by floating rock formations and bridges; a house on the lowest rock holds worker records. Shakkei Pavilion, on the island, is where a young man was found unconscious during the Tatarasuna Mystery.

## places
### STANDARD
Mikage Furnace: the plant itself, the largest smelting facility in Inazuma's islands, built to produce Jade Steel in bulk. Shakkei Pavilion: a pavilion tied to the Mystery. Kamuijima cannons: three unused cannons on the eastern side, later fired to breach the containment dome.
### LARGE +
The Arsenal, an Inazuman weapons store whose key was meant to be divided in three: one piece for the Inspector, one for the Armory Officer and one left in Tatarasuna. A worker's record says the staff withdrew in such haste, with neither official to be found, that the three pieces were hidden in treasure chests around the island instead.

## people
### STANDARD
Present day: Xavier, a Fontaine engineer who stayed behind to maintain the furnace; Toranosuke, a Shogunate soldier, and Miyuki, a shrine maiden, who quarrel over what to do about it; and Fatui agents who breached the dome. Four centuries ago: the Armory Officer Niwa Hisahide, Vice Armory Officer Miyazaki Kaneo, Inspector Mikoshi Nagamasa and his yoriki Katsuragi, plus Nozomu and Kinjirou of Nagamasa's entourage.
### LARGE +
Before the war the workers were led by Kagami Gozen, who accompanied Alice on a visit. Individual biographies belong to their own entries.

## history
### STANDARD
Tatarasuna and its furnace were built to smelt Jade Steel, and Dottore later claimed the site had first been established to dispose of Crystal Marrow safely. Around 400 years ago the Armory Officer Niwa Hisahide, Inspector Mikoshi Nagamasa and Nagamasa's yoriki Katsuragi befriended a young man they called the kabukimono, who carried a golden feather. He later became the Wanderer, and before that the Balladeer. A black gas, Tatarigami power from Crystal Marrow used to speed up forging, began sickening workers around the furnace, and Niwa imposed an information blackout.
### LARGE +
Truth revealed in the Sumeru Archon Quest: a mechanic calling himself Escher, from Fontaine, introduced a Crystal Marrow forging method. Niwa learnt the name was an alias and confronted him, but Escher was the Fatui Harbinger Dottore and killed Niwa; the whole affair was an experiment with the kabukimono as the main subject. The kabukimono sailed to Inazuma City to ask the Shogun for help and was refused, because Ei had already withdrawn to the Plane of Euthymia; he gave his golden feather to Yae Miko, who could only meet him briefly. He returned in despair, took a device from Escher, entered the furnace and absorbed the Tatarigami. Escher falsely said the device held the heart of a subordinate Niwa had killed, when it was Niwa's own heart. The kabukimono left Tatarasuna and was later found by the Jester and made a Harbinger.
### ULTRA +
Fallout: with Niwa gone, blame fell on Nagamasa, who refused to die while his family name was stained. Katsuragi took the blame on himself and Nagamasa killed him with the sword Daitatara Nagamasa, then threw the blade into the furnace. Nozomu drew the melted blade out and died of his burns that night, and Kinjirou hid its remains and Nozomu's drawing in the Arsenal. Nagamasa and the sword became known as Katsuragikiri Nagamasa. Notes about all this, taken by the Fatui when they seized the furnace, were seen by a Mr. Rumi and inspired the Sumeru writers Aqaba and Sawada. Later, with Nahida's help, the Balladeer saw the truth in Irminsul; his attempt to erase himself from the event left it intact but changed how it is remembered (Niwa dying while purifying the furnace with another swordsmith).

## quests
### STANDARD
Tatara Tales: an eight-quest World Quest series in which the Traveler and Xavier monitor the Mikage Furnace, break the containment dome with the old cannons, fend off Fatui, and finally purify the Tatarigami core. Unlocking it requires finishing Ritou Escape Plan.
### LARGE +
After Tatara Tales the Balethunder at the furnace disappears and Hanayama Kaoru appears with a Flowery Plan exchange. Also relevant are the Archon Quest recollection of the Tatarasuna Mystery (3.3) and the achievement Mystery of Tatarasuna.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Electro-aligned wildlife and cicins, Fatui skirmishers, hilichurls, Samachurl, Nobushi, Kairagi and Treasure Hoarders. Before Tatara Tales the area also inflicts Balethunder, a lingering Tatarigami charge that harms anyone who stays.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Grey stone, orange furnace-glow, humming electric currents and the stillness left by an abandoned industry. Everything hints at departed workers: documents torn in a raid, hurriedly hidden keys, a vast furnace no one dares approach.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the furnace is purified and stable since Tatara Tales, and the island is a quiet ruin of industry.
### STANDARD +
The Tatarasuna Mystery is no longer a mystery to those who followed the Sumeru storyline: Nahida showed the Balladeer the truth. Nothing in 6.x or 7.0-7.1 is recorded as changing the island.

## entry: Tatarasuna Mystery
@keys: Tatarasuna Mystery, Mystery of Tatarasuna, Katsuragi-Slashing Nagamasa, Daitatara Nagamasa
@version: 3.3
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
About 400 years ago workers at the Mikage Furnace fell ill from black gas of Tatarigami origin while a young kabukimono lived among the Inspector's circle. Public memory has Niwa Hisahide fleeing a crime and the kabukimono purifying the furnace; the truth is that Dottore, posing as a Fontaine mechanic, killed Niwa and used the kabukimono as a test subject. Blame fell on Mikoshi Nagamasa and cost Katsuragi and Nozomu their lives.
### LARGE +
Niwa's information blackout kept the truth hidden for four centuries. The account given at the time, by the mechanic, was that Niwa had fled and the kabukimono had cleansed the furnace in his place; help from the Shogunate arrived but found no disaster. When Yae Miko heard this, she merely had the golden feather returned.

## entry: Mikage Furnace and Tatara Tales
@keys: Mikage Furnace, Tatara Tales, Jade Steel, Balethunder, Xavier, Toranosuke
@version: 2.0
### STANDARD
The Mikage Furnace was the engine of Inazuma's Jade Steel, run on Tatarigami energy from Yashiori Island. During the Shogunate's war with the Watatsumi Army, saturation of that energy shut it down and even its auto-restart failed. Xavier raised a containment dome around it and kept the Balethunder in. Shogunate officials worried about halted production; shrine maidens feared the Tatarigami's spill-over, felt even at the Sacred Sakura.
### LARGE +
The Fatui, interested in Tatarigami power, breached the dome. With three Kamuijima cannons the Traveler broke it open for Xavier, then helped him gather data, repair paths and purify the core. Earlier, before the war, Alice visited with Kagami Gozen and modified the furnace, improving the dome and limiting output for safety.
