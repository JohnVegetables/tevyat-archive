## summary
### LITE
The Huaguang Stone Forest is a landscape of tall karst stone spires rising out of the clouds in Minlin, Liyue, hemmed in by three mountains: Mt. Aocang to the north, Qingyun Peak to the east and Mt. Hulao to the southwest. The in-game viewpoint says the place is forbidden to mortal steps and the kind of sight that draws those who yearn for the sky and who dwell in adepti realms.
### STANDARD +
It has no teleport waypoints of its own; travelers use those of the surrounding mountains. Two disciples of the declining Guhua Clan are found here searching for the adepti.

## geography
### STANDARD
A subarea of Minlin whose pillars of rock stand like great pavilions above the clouds. Mt. Aocang lies north, Qingyun Peak east and Mt. Hulao southwest; the nearby waypoints on those mountains are the easiest way in. Local specialties include Cor Lapis, Jueyun Chili, Qingxin and Violetgrass.
### LARGE +
The Chinese name Huaguang-lin is rendered by the wiki as "Brilliant Light Forest". The developers' travel-themed documentary credited by the wiki says the landscape took inspiration from Zhangjiajie National Forest Park in China.

## people
### STANDARD
Sun Yu and Little Que'er, disciples of the Guhua Clan, are found here for a time. Sun Yu has come to practise and, more importantly, to find the adepti and seek their counsel on how to revive the clan; Que'er accompanies him. Que'er is fiercely suspicious of strangers who approach, at first taking the Traveler for a mountain monster in disguise.
### LARGE +
Per the wiki, Sun Yu later decides that staying in Liyue will not revive the Guhua Clan and leaves for Fontaine with a Fontainian merchant, after a later quest in which the Traveler trains with him. Que'er is distressed that he will not stay. The wiki flags both NPC pages as missing official profile text, so treat these details as quest-derived.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Slimes of several elements, a Cryo Whopperflower and Geovishap Hatchlings.

## quests
### STANDARD
No major story content is set here. The wiki lists the commission The Sparrow Studies the Blade for the area, and Sun Yu's presence here ends after a later quest named The Day the Sword Departs.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Mist and cloud around bare stone towers, with the sense of being close to places where adepti dwell and mortals are not welcome. The few people who come here come to ask something of the adepti.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the forest is unchanged. The disciples' departure is the only story development tied to it, and no later content was found that alters it.

## entry: The Guhua Disciples of Huaguang
@keys: Sun Yu, Little Que'er
@keys2: Huaguang Stone Forest, Guhua Clan
### STANDARD
Sun Yu and Little Que'er grew up together studying the Guhua Clan's martial arts. Sun Yu, devoted to the clan and eager to revive it, searches for an adeptus to advise him; Que'er follows him everywhere. Both are minor open-world NPCs. The Guhua Clan's base is Wangshan Hall on Mt. Lingmeng.

## places
### STANDARD
The three surrounding mountains hold the waypoints: Mt. Aocang, Qingyun Peak and Mt. Hulao.
### ULTRA +
The official viewpoint for the area, "Mist-Veiled Stone Forest", is the source of the line about the spires poking out of the clouds like pavilions. The forest sits beside Mt. Hulao, whose adeptus Mountain Shaper keeps amber-trap flowers on his own mountain, but the stone forest itself has no recorded owner.
