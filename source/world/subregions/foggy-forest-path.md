## summary
### LITE
The Foggy Forest Path is a subarea in the southern part of Erinnyes Forest, by the water, where mist hangs among the trees and a glowing blue tree stands out. It is the setting of the world quest "Foggy Forest Path", part of "The Wild Fairy of Erinnyes", added in Version 4.2.
### STANDARD +
The path is hinted at by inscribed stone tablets at Marcotte Station. At its end an altar, three reed-pipe puzzles and a tree lead to a meeting with a Melusine called Pahsiv.

## geography
### STANDARD
Located at the southern edge of Erinnyes Forest beside the water, the path is shrouded in fog. A large tree with a blue glow dominates the area, and an altar stands near it. The surrounding forest belongs to the wider Erinnyes Forest, which has its own entry.

## places
### STANDARD
Carved stone tablets at a fountain in Marcotte Station, scratched out except for a few words mentioning "clear dew" and the Foggy Forest Path. The Mysterious Altar deeper in, whose inscription is likewise defaced and mentions "clear dew" and "three". The glowing tree, on which three orbs appear once the altar's puzzles are done.

## quests
### STANDARD
In "Foggy Forest Path" (4.2), Paimon notices the damaged tablets at Marcotte Station and hopes for hidden treasure. The Traveler completes three Harmonious Reed Pipe puzzles in the area, which makes three orbs appear on the tree. The item on the altar proves to be a drop of water, and a Melusine speaks to them, using only the words "Pahsiv" and "Melusine". Paimon works out that "Pahsiv" means yes and "Melusine" means no, and since the Melusine likes the name, Paimon calls her Pahsiv. She also says "Erinnyes". The quest leads on to "Within the Depths of Erinnyes".
### LARGE +
The quest has three versions depending on how many Teardrop Keys the Traveler holds. The wiki marks the first step (searching for the path after reading the tablet) as needing verification. The rest of the story belongs to the Wild Fairy of Erinnyes quest chain.
### ULTRA +
The quest begins either by reading the tablet at Marcotte Station or by completing the concurrent quests "Wilting Weeping Willow" or "Furious Mouth of the Spring". Its rewards include Primogems, Mora and Hero's Wit. Whether the drop of water is a key, a person or both is left ambiguous: Pahsiv answers "Pahsiv" when asked if it is a key and when asked if she is not a person, which Paimon can only interpret as meaning yes.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Quiet, cool and damp: drifting fog, the sound of water, a blue light among dark trunks, and half-erased carvings that suggest someone wanted the place forgotten.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the area is unchanged and accessible.
