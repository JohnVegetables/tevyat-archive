## summary
### LITE
Tiangong Gorge is a gorge on the surface of the Chasm in Liyue. It is one of the entrances to the Underground Mines, and during the Chasm's closure it became a hideout of smuggling Treasure Hoarders, which the Millelith quietly infiltrated.
### STANDARD +
Ruin Guards and Ruin Graders stand among the cliffs alongside hilichurls and a Mitachurl. Qingxin and Violetgrass grow here.

## geography
### STANDARD
A narrow stone ravine in the Chasm's overground area, part of the same cliff country as The Surface. Cliffs, Spoutrock vents, Pressure Plates, Unique Rock Pillars and Geograna give puzzles; Seelie live here. Local specialties are Cor Lapis, Qingxin and Violetgrass.
### LARGE +
A hidden two-way passage, the second of the Chasm's mine passageways, joins the gorge to a small pond southwest of the Chasm Withered Tree in the Main Mining Area, where a small group of Treasure Hoarders sits. It is blocked at the gorge end by a gate that opens only from the inside, and a Luxurious Chest is at the gorge entrance. Finding it gives an achievement.

## people
### STANDARD
Treasure Hoarders had a smuggling camp here. In the world quest "Undetected Infiltration", Millelith Squad Leader Yanbo of the Chasm District and his men turn out to have been disguised as hoarders.
### LARGE +
Yanbo waits next to the waypoint of Lumberpick Valley, then splits the clearing of the camps (cooking quarters, alchemy camp, weapons stash) between himself and the Traveler. A larger camp holds a smuggling crew, whose last bosses are named in the quest as Brass Bull Zhao and Chen the Flower.

## history
### STANDARD
After the miners were dismissed, Treasure Hoarders occupied the closed Chasm. In "Undetected Infiltration" Yanbo confirms the area was indeed one of the hoarders' lairs, and says his Millelith squad, in disguise, first took the Traveler for saboteurs.
### LARGE +
The first wave at the main camp includes a Fatui Pyro Agent and a Fatui Cryo Cicin Mage alongside the hoarders; the quest text summarised here does not explain their role.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
A cramped, echoing gorge of pale stone with the rattle of camp life, hidden in the cliffs.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Ruin Guards and Ruin Graders, a Rock Shieldwall Mitachurl, Cryo and Pyro Hilichurl Shooters, Geo Samachurls, Large Anemo Slimes, and the Treasure Hoarders.

## travel
### STANDARD
Entry from the overground Chasm; it is also an official entrance to the Underground Mines map, alongside the Chasm's Maw and others.

## quests
### STANDARD
"Undetected Infiltration" (world quest, 2.6): defeat the "Treasure Hoarders" at their lair, learn they are Millelith, clear two camps and then the main smuggling camp, and report to Yanbo. The quest grants an achievement.
### ULTRA +
The quest becomes available once the Traveler has finished first talking to Zhiqiong in "Surreptitious Seven-Star Seal Sundering". A Treasure Map Fragment (The Chasm) lies in a tent at the large camp, but the wiki calls it unrelated to the quest.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1, no change; no 6.x or 7.0-7.1 content revisits the gorge.
