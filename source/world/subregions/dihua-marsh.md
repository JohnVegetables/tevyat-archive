## summary
### LITE
Dihua Marsh is a wetland of rivers and silvergrass shaped by the Bishui River in Bishui Plain, Liyue, between the Stone Gate to the north and Wangshu Inn to the south. It lies on the main road between Mondstadt and Liyue, so merchant caravans stop and rest here. A Statue of the Seven stands at its northern end.
### STANDARD +
The name comes from the silvergrass (dihua) that grows in the shallows. The marsh is also remembered as the final resting place of many wandering fighters.

## geography
### STANDARD
An alluvial plain made by the Bishui River, with a web of rivers supporting a wide ecosystem of plants and animals. Stone Gate lies to the north, Wangshu Inn to the south and Sal Terrae to the east. A waterfall in the northwest is well known to travelers, and the marsh sits near Mt. Qingce. The area's local specialty is Cor Lapis.
### LARGE +
The official map description calls the area fertile, rich in aquatic plants and natural resources. The area's design drew on Guilin's scenery and architecture, according to the developers' travel-themed documentary cited by the wiki.
### ULTRA +
The wiki's trivia notes that dihua means Amur silvergrass, a cool-temperate grass of northeast Asia, and should not be confused with the similar Chinese silver grass. Besides Overstretched, the marsh hosts random events (Blocked Road, Hilichurl Nest, Key Supplies) and a fishing commission, Fishing Jiangxue.

## places
### STANDARD
The Statue of the Seven on the northern end, along the main road, and Wangshu Inn, a tall inn that the official description calls a symbolic structure visible from all directions.

## people
### STANDARD
Open-world residents include the Millelith soldier Qiaoxi and several travelers and merchants, among them Hui, Hulu, Mengmeng, Strong and Yu.
### LARGE +
Wangshu Inn's keepers are Verr Goldet and her husband Huai'an, agents of the Liyue Qixing who help Xiao in his work (see Wangshu Inn). Many caravan merchants pass through.

## travel
### STANDARD
The marsh is the road from Mondstadt into Liyue, so caravans rest in its shallows and at the inn rather than push on into the hills. Official text describes the setting as awe-inspiring to newcomers.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Hilichurl camps (fighters, shooters, grenadiers, Anemo samachurls), a Cryo Whopperflower and a Cryo Abyss Mage.
### LARGE +
In the world quest Overstretched, Qiaoxi near the bridge in the middle of the marsh asks the Traveler to clear a nearby hilichurl camp that includes a Rock Shieldwall Mitachurl.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
The viewpoint text calls it a place where the sifting of the swaying silvergrass joins the croaking of frogs, and where many a wandering fighter has come to rest.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the marsh is unchanged and remains a rest stop on the road between Mondstadt and Liyue. No later story content was found that alters it.

## entry: Wangshu Inn at Dihua Marsh
@keys: Wangshu Inn
@keys2: Dihua Marsh, Bishui Plain
### STANDARD
Wangshu Inn stands within Dihua Marsh, built tall for visibility. Zhongli calls it a strategic location that must be defended, and the inn has existed since at least the years after the Cataclysm, nearly 500 years ago. Seen from the top, it reaches all the way to Mt. Qingce and Jueyun Karst in clear weather. Its fuller history belongs to its own entry.
