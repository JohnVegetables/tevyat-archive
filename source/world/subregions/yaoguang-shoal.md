## summary
### LITE
Yaoguang Shoal is a shallow stretch of coast in Liyue's Qiongji Estuary, south of Mingyun Village and east of Guili Plains. In the local language it was named for the glowing sea creatures that used to wash ashore after dusk like fallen stars.
### STANDARD +
Those creatures have all been taken by visiting lovers, so the namesake sight can no longer be seen, though the beach still gives up small finds to a patient searcher.

## geography
### STANDARD
Low beach and shallow water with Hydro and Pyro elemental monuments, Ruins Torches and a windmill mechanism. Cor Lapis, Qingxin and Starconch are the local specialties.
### LARGE +
At the far eastern edge lies an unnamed hidden spot players call Heart Island, for a heart-shaped puzzle that requires two players in co-op to open its chest; the name is a community one and not used in-game.

### ULTRA +
The name Yaoguang is the Chinese name of Alkaid, a star of the Big Dipper, which is also why it matches a Qixing seat name. The creatures that once glowed in the shallows were taken by lovers who visited, according to the expedition description.


## dangers
### STANDARD
Hilichurls, samachurls and Geo slimes roam the sand; tougher foes are Hydro Abyss Mages, Fatui Pyro Agents and Electro Cicin Mages, Blazing Axe Mitachurls, Ruin Guards and Ruin Hunters.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Calm and romantic by day; the lantern-like glow of the old legend lives on only in the story.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the shoal is a peaceful beach with no later canon changes.
