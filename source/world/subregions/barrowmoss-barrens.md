## summary
### LITE
Barrowmoss Barrens is a subarea of Lempo Isle in Nod-Krai: two plateaus lying north and south of Blue Amber Lake. It is a wild, open stretch where researchers and treasure hunters carry out odd projects, and where a Radiant Moonfly roams as a boss.
### STANDARD +
The Barrens are better known for the people working in them than for any history of their own. Canon gives the place no founding story or ruler; what it offers is a handful of quests about kuuvahki research and an engineer's hidden laboratory.

## geography
### STANDARD
The name covers both the northern and southern plateau, with raised Blue Amber Lake between them. The map shows three levels: the Hidden Laboratory, the Windworn Path and the Pellucid Cavern. Thunderstorms occur in the northern part.
### LARGE +
Blue Amber Lake itself has its own entry; it is described as a highland lake with a glow at every hour, and the only place where Stellafruit grows. A Radiant Moonfly, one of the Radiant Beasts (a Mystical Beast family), is the area's boss.
### ULTRA +
The Chinese name (Taigu Huangyuan) reads roughly as a mossy, bone-like wilderness; the English name is a localisation. Lempo Isle, the larger area it belongs to, also includes the Clink-Clank Krumkake Craftshop run by Aino, and is part of the Southern Isles.

## people
### STANDARD
No settled population is recorded. Visitors seen here are the Fontaine Research Institute researcher Fanngel, the treasure hunters Anezka and Macola, and, in a Travelers' Tale, the Ratnik Flins and the automaton Ineffa.
### LARGE +
Anezka and Macola look after a laboratory belonging to their friend Drzislav, who is absent. Aino and Ineffa are linked to Lempo Isle as a whole rather than to the Barrens specifically.

## quests
### STANDARD
Three quests start here: The Power of Research (Fanngel needs his Airspace Data Collector lifted in a storm to gather kuuvahki data), Team Rigor, or Team Intuition? (Anezka and Macola recover Drzislav's all-terrain machine Bobik from Fatui troopers) and the Travelers' Tale Flins: Out of One's Depth.
### LARGE +
In The Power of Research, Fanngel hopes kuuvahki studies will free people from reliance on Arkhium, and gives the Traveler a lightning-proof Collector 2.0 that works only in kuuvahki storms. In Team Rigor, or Team Intuition?, Bobik is hijacked, the group enters the lab, and the machine is restored from a backup module; Anezka and Macola stay to guard the lab afterwards. In Flins' anecdote, Flins and Ineffa talk about the Ratniki, who once lit street lights by oil and now struggle with kuuvahki-powered ones.

## dangers
### LARGE
The Radiant Moonfly alternates between Dendro and Pyro states and can envelop players in a Radiant Cocoon. Fatui Oprichniki troopers appeared around the laboratory during Team Rigor, or Team Intuition?. Lightning storms are dangerous without protection.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the Barrens remain an open, explorable area of Nod-Krai with nothing new revealed after the 6.x quests.

## entry: Drzislav's Laboratory and Bobik
@keys: Drzislav, Bobik, Bobik-131, Anezka, Macola
@keys2: Barrowmoss Barrens, Nod-Krai
### STANDARD
Drzislav is an absent inventor whose hidden laboratory sits in the Barrens, guarded by his friends Anezka and Macola. His creation Bobik-131 is a civilian all-terrain transportation device; after Fatui interference the Traveler helps restore it from a backup program module, and it afterwards follows the Traveler's orders. Drzislav appears only through recorded voice messages.

## entry: Fanngel's Kuuvahki Experiments
@keys: Fanngel, Airspace Data Collector
@keys2: Barrowmoss Barrens, Fontaine Research Institute, kuuvahki
### STANDARD
Fanngel is a Fontaine Research Institute researcher doing field work on kuuvahki storms in the northern Barrens. He expects his thesis in a year or two and says the real impact of the work may take decades. His reward, the Nod-Krai Airspace Data Collector 2.0, protects only against kuuvahki thunderstorms.
