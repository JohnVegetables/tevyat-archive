## summary
### LITE
Starsnatch Cliff is a scenic cliff in Starfell Valley, Mondstadt, with a wide view of the night sky. At night the secret couple Marla and Marvin meet here. The Midsummer Courtyard domain, an ancient ruin, lies beside it. In the Prologue it is where Venti, Jean, Diluc and the Traveler gathered before purifying the dragon Stormterror.
### STANDARD +
The name speaks of plucking stars from the sky. The top of the cliff is a recognised spot for viewing the night sky.

## geography
### STANDARD
A cliff in the Starfell Valley subarea. Cecilia flowers are the local specialty, and a Time Trial Challenge is run in the area.
### LARGE +
The Midsummer Courtyard domain lies next to the cliff, in the same subarea. Its buried garden is the remnant of an ancient lost civilisation that the wiki links to Arcadia.

## places
### STANDARD
The summit, where the lovers sit at night, and the Midsummer Courtyard, an ancient blessed altar filled with Pyro-fearing monsters whose trial grants artifacts. Its description says the once magnificent Sommernachtgarten ("midsummer night garden") was buried when the ancient civilisation fell, leaving only ancient trees and stones.

## people
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
From 19:00 to 06:00 three Mondstadt residents can be found here. Marla (a young woman who rests on a bench in Springvale by day) and Marvin Goth (son of Goth, general manager of the Goth Grand Hotel) are secretly in love because Goth is strict about whom his son may marry. Sharp, a mercenary called "Black Hound", was hired by Goth to guard Marvin; Marvin pays him to keep quiet, so he earns from both, and he picks flowers while he waits.
### LARGE +
By day Marvin sleeps with his eyes open in the city, a ruse so he stays awake for the night. At the cliff the couple trade florid vows that keep tripping into awkward comparisons (ice at a spring's source, an eagle's talons) and correct themselves as they go. After the Stormterror crisis Marvin says a rock narrowly missing him showed him that Marla means the world to him. Canon shows only Goth's objection and never says what he decides.
### ULTRA +
During the limited-time event "Invitation of Windblume" their lines changed: Marvin wondered whether to tell his father during the festival, and Marla said dandelions, which wind scatters but cannot kill, may be why they became symbols of love.

## quests
### STANDARD
Prologue Act II, "For a Tomorrow Without Tears", part 13, "Reunion With the Dragon": once Dvalin's tears had restored the Holy Lyre der Himmel, Diluc, Jean, Venti and the Traveler met here, ready to purify Stormterror. Venti called it a lovely place for a fated reunion and warned that a fight was coming.
### LARGE +
In the hangout event "Gift and Intent" (Noelle, version 1.4) the Traveler must photograph the top of the cliff at night (19:00 to 24:00) to help Beatrice choose a gift; the resulting quest item, "Picture of Starsnatch Cliff", shows the night sky above it. Beatrice complains if people are already at the cliff, even when Marla and Marvin are out of frame.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
A hushed, windy ledge under a wide night sky: quiet by day, gently occupied at night, with the couple's whispered declarations and the bodyguard pacing nearby.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the cliff is unchanged: the lovers still meet here at night and the domain remains. No later story has altered it.

## entry: Marla, Marvin and Sharp
@keys: Marla, Marvin Goth, Sharp the Black Hound
@keys2: Starsnatch Cliff, Goth Grand Hotel
### STANDARD
Marla and Marvin Goth are secret lovers who meet at Starsnatch Cliff every night; Marla naps on a bench in Springvale by day, and Marvin feigns sleep in Mondstadt City. His father Goth, manager of the Goth Grand Hotel, would not approve. The mercenary Sharp guards Marvin and keeps the secret for double pay. Their dialogue is comic: the couple speak in grand poetic metaphors and then correct them.

## entry: Midsummer Courtyard
@keys: Midsummer Courtyard, Sommernachtgarten
### STANDARD
A domain beside Starsnatch Cliff holding an ancient blessed altar full of Pyro-fearing monsters; clearing it yields artifacts. In-world text says the Sommernachtgarten, once prosperous and magnificent, was buried when an ancient lost civilisation fell, leaving only ancient trees and stones.
