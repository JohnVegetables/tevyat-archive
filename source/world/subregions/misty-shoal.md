## summary
### LITE
Misty Shoal is a small island subarea of Dunanna Pit in Nod-Krai, east of Piramida, introduced in version 6.7. The sky above it is perfectly clear: there are no clouds at all. Beneath it lie ruins linked to a moon-landing project.
### STANDARD +
The island is reached by Moonlane. Its only quest in canon so far is the World Quest Pioneers, part of the Moon Gazing chapter.

## geography
### STANDARD
A deserted island with a lake-filled pit, drifting kuuhenki and a Statue of the New Moon. An entrance in the ground leads down to underground ruins. Floating rocks, broken machinery and fallen rings fill the lower halls.
### LARGE +
A terminal inside the ruins labels the area as a celestial orbit computation chamber. A separate sign names Dunanna Forward Station, so the base is associated with the wider Dunanna Pit installations. A lunar-gravity simulation room and a main control hall lie deeper.
### ULTRA +
Dunanna Pit has no Meeting Point of its own; it shares the Ancient Moon's Remnant at Nuur Stone Circle with the Lunar Highlands to unlock the Luna Treasure Compass. The pit lies close to Fontaine and east of Pohjola. A trivia note on the wiki observes that the sky over Misty Shoal has no clouds at all.

## quests
@version: 6.7
### STANDARD
In Pioneers, Paimon and the Traveler spot Columbina heading onto the island and follow her underground. Columbina passes on a new power, Archaeolune Ripples, which restarts a backup generator and clears the energy pathways. A terminal finds the Frost Moon coordinates and offers a route to the main control hall for moon landing preparations.
### LARGE +
At the terminal the Traveler offers Columbina a hand and she turns into a black kuuhenki: an impostor. It flees through guarded halls; after the Traveler defeats a defence array in the control hall, the base begins to self-destruct and asks for orbital coordinates. If the right three are given, the kuuhenki, speaking in fragments, asks for rescue on the moon and points to the lunar spaceship. The quest then ends at the shuttle; its aftermath belongs to later Moon Gazing quests.

## dangers
### LARGE
The ruins' automated defences treat visitors as intruders: transmission modules fire on the Traveler and the control hall's guardian attacks. The self-destruct countdown is the quest's main hazard.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Bright, windless calm above and silent, half-powered machine halls below: floating stones, humming rings, terminal voices repeating alerts.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the island is explorable and the base's story continues in Moon Gazing content; nothing here is described as resolved.

### STANDARD
Beneath Misty Shoal is a ruined installation concerned with reaching the Frost Moon. Its terminals compute Trilune coordinates and prepare moon landings, and its power is restored using Columbina's gift of Archaeolune Ripples. Whoever built it is not stated in the quest text; treat that as unknown.
