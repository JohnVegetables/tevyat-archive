## summary
### LITE
The Skyfire Circlet is a ring of islands floating over the mouth of the Great Volcano of Tollan in Natlan, suspended by the power of Phlogiston. It contains the only entrance to the Ancient Sacred Mountain, which is why it is also called Nahuatzin's Springboard.
### STANDARD +
It is a subarea of Atocpan, the area that holds the Great Volcano and the home of the Collective of Plenty.

## geography
### STANDARD
Floating rock islands in a ring above the crater. Features include ancient-variant Everflame Seeds, Gaseous Phlogiston (with Phlogiston currents, wind tunnels and Scorchwind Conflux), geothermal vents, Saurian Indwelling and Secret Source Sentinels. The local specialty is the Quenepa Berry.
### LARGE +
Iridescent Inscriptions are scattered around the islands; collecting four unlocks the first Iridescent Legacy note.

## history
### STANDARD
Iridescent Legacy Part I records the last words of the sovereign ruler Aj Nimalaj Chuq'ab Hun, one of the Thirteen Sovereign Lords. He says Xbalanque's forces have broken through the Skyfire Circlet, calls the human leader a worthy foe, notes that humanity's grasp of Phlogiston is no more refined than the vishaps' yet enough to counter dragon power, and regrets that the traitor handed fire knowledge to another race. He also admits the dragons' civilisation had stagnated and that humans climbed the Sacred Mountain unnoticed.
### LARGE +
Three further Iridescent Legacy notes, from other sovereigns, lie deeper in the Ancient Sacred Mountain rather than on the Circlet.

## dangers
### LARGE
Qucusaurus and Qucusaurus chicks, and the Secret Source Automaton: Hunter-Seeker.

## quests
### STANDARD
The world quest Nahuatzin's Leap (Path to the Flaming Peaks, part 1; version 5.5) sends the Traveler to the top of the Circlet to leap into the crater.

## connections
### ULTRA
The Iridescent Legacy notes collect the final words of four of the Thirteen Sovereign Lords, spoken to their king Xiuhcoatl. Part I is unlocked here; the others are tied to the Heart of Force Inversion, the Flame-Melding Ritual Grounds and the Sea of Shifting Sentience inside the Ancient Sacred Mountain.
