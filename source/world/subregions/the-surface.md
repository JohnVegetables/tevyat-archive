## summary
### LITE
The Surface is the overground mining district of the Chasm in Liyue: terraces, winch platforms and cliff paths atop the great pit, with the entrance to the Underground Mines in the Chasm's Maw. The Liyue Qixing closed the whole mine after unexplained accidents; it is now being reopened bit by bit, and the surface is where the guards, an exploration team and trespassing Treasure Hoarders overlap.
### STANDARD +
Hilichurl camps and Geovishap share the cliffs with Treasure Hoarders, and Cor Lapis is the local specialty. Millelith soldiers hold the access road.

## geography
### STANDARD
Broken, layered rock country of cliffs and platforms around the pit's rim, with Spoutrock vents, Unique Rock Pillars, Pressure Plates, Bloatty Floatty and Geograna. Seelie live here too.
### LARGE +
The Chasm's surface and its Underground Mines are separate map layers. The main way down is the Chasm's Maw; a crane platform hangs over the hole and the Mine Passageway lets travellers fall or ride into the mines. The Qixing's Seven-Star seal blocked it until broken during "Surreptitious Seven-Star Seal Sundering", by destroying five large Bedrock Keys on the surface.

## people
### STANDARD
Millelith guard the road, among them a soldier named Tie Hong who greets the Traveler warmly, remembering the fight at the Jade Chamber. Muning, the Ministry of Civil Affairs representative who is worried about the sealed mine, waits at the campsite by the mine entrance. Hilichurls and Treasure Hoarders squat in the ruins and workings.
### LARGE +
Official notices on the surface show the Qixing's position. One records that Yuehai Pavilion voted to halt mining after an unnatural red glow left the gems; another says the old storehouse renovation was stopped for anomalies and that it will be kept as it is for Millelith and Ministry staff. A third marks a relic of old battles bequeathed by Rex Lapis, to be left alone.

## history
### STANDARD
Before the War of Funerary Flame the Chasm was the land of the ancient human civilisation of Lang-Gan, which worshipped the angel Zibai. The wiki records that when the war's Abyssal contamination reached it, the Heavenly Principles had a Celestial Nail cast down and the people fled to neighbouring Chenyu Vale. Later legends place the fall of the Solar Chariot, and the clash of Morax and Azhdaha, here.
### LARGE +
During the cataclysm 500 years ago the Chasm was the epicentre of the Abyssal invasion of Liyue, and the Millelith and the yaksha Bosacius fought at the front. In recent years accidents led the Qixing to close the mine. Before the great expulsion of the Fatui, they discovered black ooze and offered to deal with it, but their device exploded; the Qixing then reopened the surface first, which let Treasure Hoarders slip in.
### ULTRA +
The Chasm Delvers series resolves the underlying crisis: the Traveler purifies a corrupted nail, and the earthquakes and ooze subside. This allowed the Qixing to reopen the mines while keeping miners away from the more dangerous parts.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Dusty, wind-scoured stone under a wide sky, with the deep dark of the pit beside you. Guards are cautious; the tone of the notices is one of reluctant, formal warning.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Hilichurls of several kinds (Rock Shield guards, Grenadiers, Electro and Pyro shooters, Geo Samachurls), Treasure Hoarders of all types including Potioneers, and Geovishap.
### LARGE +
Treasure Hoarders keep sneaking into the closed areas for wealth, and the Millelith ask visitors not to break the law.

## travel
### STANDARD
The Underground Mines below require Adventure Rank 28, Archon Quest Chapter I Act III and the world quest "Surreptitious Seven-Star Seal Sundering"; the surface itself can be visited earlier, past the Millelith guard.

## quests
### STANDARD
"The Chasm Delvers" (world quest series, 2.6) begins on the surface with the Chasm Charters and the breaking of the Seven-Star seal; the five Bedrock Keys are on this level.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the surface is open, the seal is gone and the Underground Mines are accessible. No later content changes it.

## entry: Chasm Notices
@keys: Chasm notice, Chasm notice poster, Yuehai Pavilion vote
@version: 2.6
### STANDARD
Notice posters on the Chasm surface record Liyue's official response. One declares the gems' crimson glow hidden by sinister forces and records the Yuehai Pavilion's verdict that mining should stop at once, telling readers to put away greed and look to a better tomorrow. Another halts renovation of an old storehouse near the Lumenstone, to be kept for the Millelith and the Ministry.
