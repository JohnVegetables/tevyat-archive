## summary
@availability: limited
### LITE
The Thinkers' Theater is the theatre area of the Veluriyam Mirage, the bottle-domain from the limited-time Version 3.8 event Secret Summer Paradise. Zosimos, a stage-director replica, puts on plays at its central theatre. A troupe of seven Hydro Eidolons that speaks as one voice, Daiya, stages three shows around the grounds. It was event-only.
### STANDARD +
The area unlocks with the Act III quest Treehouse Theater: Candlelit Shadows Dancing, and the world quest series Daiya's Three-Day Reverie starts here. A hidden Broadcasting Station above the central theatre opens after all three plays are completed.

## geography
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
Points of interest: the Central Theater (Zosimos' stage), the Director's Treehouse (where he builds props and writes scripts), an Ancient Castle in the north (always night once Petrifying Gaze is started), a Skycastle in the south, and the Broadcasting Station over the theatre. The area includes Waverider Waypoints, Dendrograna and Choo-Choo Cart track.
### LARGE +
Zosimos' sets are built entirely from Preprints, flat blueprints made solid, so props can be moved around between scenes. The wiki lists no local specialties; enemies are Large Anemo Slimes, Winged Cryoshrooms, fungi and Hydro Mimic Mallards and Squirrels.

## people
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
Zosimos is a moustached, Fontaine-dressed stage director, a Hydro Eidolon replica of a real prop designer who dreamed of directing. Daiya is the shared voice of seven Hydro Eidolons (Director-in-Chief, Engineer, Fake Playwright, Food Manager, Harum-Scarum, Hustler and Loafer), who talk through a device with a prerecorded vocabulary made by Zosimos and Idyia.
### LARGE +
The seven members run the plays behind the scenes from the Broadcasting Station. Hydro Eidolons cannot speak; Zosimos taught them to direct and design shows, and Daiya is the result. The real Zosimos, according to the wiki, had aspired to direct since he was very young.

## quests
@availability: limited
@version: 3.8
### STANDARD
In Treehouse Theater: Candlelit Shadows Dancing, the Traveler, Klee, Kaeya and Kokomi find Zosimos in his treehouse. He holds the last component of the central hub and will give it up only after they perform his script about a thief and a mage. Klee, Kaeya and Kokomi act while the Traveler works backstage. Afterwards he gives them a bottle-lamp component and lets Klee and Kaeya keep their costumes.
### LARGE +
Daiya's Three-Day Reverie asks the Traveler to attend three plays. The Black Nacre and the All-Devouring Kraken is a sea adventure about sailors and a hidden treasure, with cannons against a Kraken's minions. Petrifying Gaze follows a lone Hydro Eidolon breaking into a haunted fortress to lift a curse that has turned its friends to stone. Skycastle Saviors is about a floating city in crisis and its miraculous architect. Completing all three reveals the truth behind the curtain in the Broadcasting Station.
### ULTRA +
Furnishing blueprints and promotional posters are the quest rewards for the three plays, such as the Wind-Up Great Owl Spirit Mechanism blueprint for completing the series. The wiki notes that Kaveh, whom Idyia credits with redesigning many Mirage buildings, may have inspired the Miraculous Architect of Skycastle Saviors; this is inference, not stated canon.

## atmosphere
@availability: limited
### LARGE
Painted flats, a speaker announcement system and an air of cheerful amateurism: a pageant staged earnestly by Hydro Eidolons.

## status
@availability: limited
### LITE
As of 7.1 canon, the Thinkers' Theater is part of a time-limited area from Version 3.8; no later story returns to it.
### STANDARD +
After the event Zosimos says he has finally seen his own show, got useful feedback, and means to become a true director.

## entry: Zosimos, Stage Director
@keys: Zosimos, Director's Treehouse
@keys2: Veluriyam Mirage, Thinkers' Theater, Daiya
@availability: limited
@version: 3.8
### STANDARD
Zosimos is a Hydro Eidolon replica of a Fontaine-dressed prop designer who dreamed of directing. He is warm, grateful and open to criticism from Paimon and Idyia. His own story is a play about a thief and a mage. He calls the Hydro Eidolons dependable partners and says the outdoor stages cannot run without them.
