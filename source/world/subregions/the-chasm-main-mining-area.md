## summary
### LITE
The Main Mining Area is the central zone of the Chasm's Underground Mines in Liyue, a dark network of shafts lit by lumenstone lamps. After a series of mysterious accidents the whole mine was closed and its miners put out of work; the Liyue Qixing are gradually reopening it.
### STANDARD +
It is entered by way of Glaze Peak and is the core ground of the exploration the Traveler joins in the Chasm Delvers series, where black "dark mud" spreading Abyssal corruption has to be cleared with lumenstone light.

## geography
### STANDARD
A subarea of the Underground Mines, which have their own map tab separate from the main continent. Features include dark mud, oozing concretions, lumenlamps, luminous Seelie and elemental monuments. Noctilucous Jade is the local specialty.
### LARGE +
The mines can be entered from the Chasm's Maw, Tiangong Gorge, Glaze Peak or Avidya Forest. In play they unlock at Adventure Rank 28 after Chapter I Act III and the quest Surreptitious Seven-Star Seal Sundering.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Treasure Hoarders, Fatui skirmishers, slimes and Whopperflowers roam the shafts; elites include Pyro Abyss Mages, Geovishaps, Mirror Maidens and Rifthound Whelps.
### LARGE +
The ground itself is hazardous: dark mud spreads corruption, and dangerous gas and cave-ins hamper delvers.

## history
### STANDARD
The Chasm began when a celestial nail fell on the ancient civilisation of Lang-Gan and buried it under the hills. In the recent past accidents closed the mine and the Qixing sealed its entrance with a Seven-Star Seal. In the Chasm Delvers series the Traveler and the Ministry of Civil Affairs' representative Muning's team break the seal's keys, open the mines, set up a camp, and finally purify a corrupted Skyfrost Nail that was the source of the dark mud.
### LARGE +
A Millelith-and-Yaksha defence of the ore veins five hundred years ago, told by a miner at the Chasm's Maw, is the mining folk's own memory of the cataclysm.

### ULTRA +
Lang-Gan, per the wiki's reading of weapon and artifact texts, was part of Teyvat's unified civilisation, governed by the angel Zibai, whose people lived around the mines and worshipped the Three Moons. When the moons shattered in the War of Funerary Flame the land was tainted by the Abyss, and a celestial nail sent to heal it fell and turned the region into the Chasm; the nail is known as the Spiritstone. Zibai's people evacuated eastward first. These details are tied to the ancient history and are not spoken by the Chasm miners.


## atmosphere
### LARGE
Dim passages, glowing lumenstone and an eerie quiet, with the weight of rock overhead.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the main mining area is open to supervised exploration.
