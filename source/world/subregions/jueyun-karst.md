## summary
### LITE
Jueyun Karst is a realm of towering, cloud-ringed sandstone peaks in Minlin, northwestern Liyue, where the adepti, the immortal illuminated beasts who fought for Rex Lapis, have lived in seclusion since the Archon War. Mortals consider it sacred and seldom enter, though the brave or desperate still climb in search of adeptal blessings. Its great peaks each belong to an adeptus: Qingyun Peak to Moon Carver, Mt. Hulao to Mountain Shaper and Mt. Aocang to Cloud Retainer.
### STANDARD +
The Chinese name means roughly "Cloud-Terminating Place", for peaks that rise above the clouds. On maps "Jueyun Karst" also names one particular stretch of Minlin around the sunken Taishan Mansion, but people in Liyue use it for the whole adeptal highland.

## geography
### STANDARD
Steep karst pillars and mountains wrapped in mist, with waterfalls and lakes. The map subarea lies between Taishan Mansion and Guili Plains. Around it rise Qingyun Peak, Mt. Aocang and Mt. Hulao, which ring the Huaguang Stone Forest. Wangshu Inn offers the classic distant view.
### LARGE +
Nantianmen, where the Dragon-Queller tree holds the sealed dragon Azhdaha, lies further northwest in Minlin. Local products include Qingxin flowers, Cor Lapis and Jueyun Chilis, with Violetgrass in the stone forest. A plaustrite island, the Dwelling in the Clouds, floats high above Qingyun Peak.
### ULTRA +
The landscape was modelled on China's Zhangjiajie; the developers added trestle and suspension bridges between the mountains.

## places
### STANDARD
Qingyun Peak (Moon Carver), Mt. Hulao (Mountain Shaper, guarded by amber-making "karst crawler" plants) and Mt. Aocang (Cloud Retainer, whose abode is reached from a stone table in a lake) each hide a sealed abode. Taishan Mansion is an ancient trial site, now flooded and sealed.
### LARGE +
Mt. Aocang's table has three stone chairs engraved for Retainer, Guizhong and Rex. Moon Carver's abode lies past a censer at the foot of Qingyun Peak's steps. The Dwelling in the Clouds is Cloud Retainer's floating retreat. Huaguang Stone Forest is the valley of stone spires between the three peaks.
### ULTRA +
Inscriptions dot the area: a poem on the bridge over the Jueyun Karst lake and a tablet on the road to Qingyun Peak praise climbing above the clouds. A Qingyun Peak tablet calls Moon Carver Rex Lapis' disciple and the kindest adeptus, appearing as a stag. One on Mt. Aocang calls Cloud Retainer free and unfettered, acting without setting rules. Mt. Hulao's says Mountain Shaper builds his amber prison by harnessing nature.

## people
### LARGE
The resident adepti are Moon Carver (a stag), Mountain Shaper (a red-and-orange crane) and Cloud Retainer (a crane, also called Xianyun). Mortals also come: herb gatherers who earn a living from plants found only here, chili pickers, pilgrims seeking blessings, Guhua Clan disciples in the stone forest, and Treasure Hoarders. Cloud Retainer has raised disciples in these mountains, including Ganyu in her youth and Shenhe, who arrived as a silent girl and became her student.
### ULTRA +
Named mortals include Qilang, an herb gatherer at Qingyun Peak who sells to Bubu Pharmacy; Manie, who picks Jueyun Chilis to cook for "the kids"; and Xin Cheng, a pilgrim who once demanded a blessing from Ganyu. Yuandai, a wild crane from Mt. Aocang that gained sentience, was taught by Cloud Retainer, though not formally as her disciple.

## culture
### STANDARD
Liyue folk regard Jueyun Karst as sacred land that mortals should not enter. Entering without offending the adepti is said to be possible only for someone holding a Sigil of Permission, a talisman Rex Lapis once cast. Visitors leave food offerings, especially at Mt. Aocang's stone table.
### LARGE +
An old warning in the Void-Pacing Stone Catalog says the wise never climb a mountain that has a master or quarry its rocks. Legend says seekers of the adeptal path had to pass trials of "earth" at Taishan Mansion and of "heaven" at the Dwelling in the Clouds. The heavenly trial gave rise to the word "Jadevoid", now used for any adeptus' dwelling.
### ULTRA +
When the Traveler met him, Mountain Shaper dismissed their guess about the amber as nonsense and explained that Mt. Hulao's amber comes from karst crawlers, flowers he planted to keep intruders out.

## history
### STANDARD
After the Archon War some two thousand years ago, most adepti withdrew to Jueyun Karst and watched over Liyue from afar. Cloud Retainer remembers the peaks ringing with music when the adepti gathered; now they are quiet, since most of her old friends died in the wars.
### LARGE +
Some illuminated beasts are said to have asked Rex Lapis to turn them into stone statues instead of waiting for death. A tablet above Taishan Mansion says it was founded by royal decree. Rex Lapis chose to live among his people instead, so the way there was hidden and its treasure drowned. The Dwelling in the Clouds served the trial of heaven with Rex Lapis' approval and, with few seekers left, is back in Cloud Retainer's care.
### ULTRA +
Several hundred years ago the young herb gatherer Qiqi was caught in a battle between adepti and demons. The adepti revived her, and Mountain Shaper sealed her in amber when the power drove her wild.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Silence, wind and birdsong; mist sliding between stone pillars; stone stairs, bridges, censers and offering tables high above the clouds. It feels watched and slightly forbidding, and a mortal can never be sure whether a nearby crane or stag is an ordinary animal.

## dangers
### LARGE
Sheer cliffs; hilichurl camps, slimes, whopperflowers and Treasure Hoarders; Geovishaps around Mt. Aocang; and Mountain Shaper's amber traps on Mt. Hulao, which seal anyone who steps on the hidden plants. The adepti dislike intruders and may treat them as thieves.

## travel
### LARGE
No road leads to the abodes. Each one is sealed by a barrier and opens only to those its adeptus admits, such as visitors whose offering is accepted. A Liyue portable cooking stove is advertised as ideal for scaling the heights of Jueyun Karst.

## quests
### LARGE
- Of the Land Amidst Monoliths (Archon Quest Chapter I Act I, 1.0): after Rex Lapis' apparent death, the Traveler seeks the adepti here to clear their name.
- Sea of Clouds, Sea of People (Ganyu's story quest, 1.2): Ganyu retreats to Mt. Aocang and trains to reclaim her adeptal side.
### ULTRA +
Lantern Rite events (4.4, 5.3, 6.3) move the adepti between the peaks and Liyue Harbor. The Taishan Mansion puzzle drains the water to unlock the domain.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 Jueyun Karst remains the secluded home of the adepti and is still treated by mortals as sacred ground. Moon Carver and Mountain Shaper still keep their peaks.
### STANDARD +
Cloud Retainer moved to Liyue Harbor in human form as Xianyun before the 4.4 Lantern Rite but still keeps her abode on Mt. Aocang. The 7.0-7.1 Archon Quest takes place in Snezhnaya and shows no new events here.

## entry: The Traveler Seeks the Adepti
@keys: Of the Land Amidst Monoliths, Custodian of Clouds, Moulder of Mountains, An Impromptu Change of Plan
@version: 1.0
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
After Rex Lapis was apparently killed at the Rite of Descension, the Traveler was accused. Childe of the Fatui gave them a Sigil of Permission and sent them to Jueyun Karst to win the adepti's word. At Qingyun Peak, Moon Carver met them and asked them to drive off the pursuing Millelith. Learning of Rex Lapis' death enraged him, and he sent them to the other adepti. On Mt. Hulao, the Traveler freed a man's brother from amber, and Mountain Shaper scolded them for helping a "thief". On Mt. Aocang, they offered dishes at the stone table and made their way through Cloud Retainer's abode. She spoke of quashing Liyue, but because they came bearing a Sigil of Permission and appealing to contracts, she agreed to investigate first. Xiao was found at Wangshu Inn.
### LARGE +
The sigil Childe gave was a Fatui copy, not one of Rex Lapis' originals; such copies were later used to free Osial. The adepti then came down to confront the Qixing, fought Osial alongside the Millelith, and agreed to let mortals govern Liyue.

## entry: Rex Lapis' Survival and the Adepti
@keys: Rex Lapis, Morax, Zhongli
@keys2: Jueyun Karst, Jueyun, adepti
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
Rex Lapis was not dead: he had faked his death to step down, and lives in Liyue Harbor as Zhongli. The adepti accepted this, and at later Lantern Rites Moon Carver, Mountain Shaper, Cloud Retainer and Madame Ping have met him socially, at times on Mt. Aocang.

## entry: Ganyu's Retreat to Jueyun
@keys: Ganyu, Sinae Unicornis, Xin Cheng, Illuminated Beast of the Sea of Clouds
@keys2: Jueyun Karst, Jueyun, Mt. Aocang, Cloud Retainer, Nantianmen
@version: 1.2
### STANDARD
Ganyu, sent to deliver a letter from the Qixing to the adepti, lingered in Jueyun Karst. Seeing Ningguang's secretaries doing her work, she wrongly believed she had been replaced, and she doubted whether a half-qilin belonged among humans. At her request Cloud Retainer set her a trial near Mt. Aocang, and Xiao trained her at Nantianmen. A pilgrim, Xin Cheng, sought a materialistic blessing, which she refused. Back in the harbor, helping the official Huixin, whose family had looked up to her for generations, made her realise people valued her, and she resolved to keep working with the Qixing.

## entry: Jadevoid and the Trials of Heaven and Earth
@keys: Jadevoid, Taishan Mansion, Dwelling in the Clouds, trial of heaven, trial of earth
### STANDARD
Those seeking the adeptal path once faced two trials: "earth" at Taishan Mansion and "heaven" at the Dwelling in the Clouds, a plaustrite island above Qingyun Peak. Cloud Retainer built the Dwelling for meditation and lent it for the trial at Moon Carver and Mountain Shaper's urging, with the blessing of Rex Lapis, Guizhong and Streetward Rambler. The trial of crossing the void gave the name "Jadevoid", later applied to every adeptus' abode. Taishan Mansion is now sealed under water and has become a common treasure trove, its old altar now coursing with scorching Pyro energy.

## entry: The Mint on Mt. Aocang
@keys: Mt. Mint-cang, Zizhi
@keys2: Mt. Aocang, Mountain Shaper, Cloud Retainer, Xianyun
@version: 6.3
@availability: limited
### LARGE
During the 6.3 Lantern Rite event, a spirit called Zizhi possessed Mountain Shaper and sent him to plant masses of mint around Cloud Retainer's abode. He declared that once he had planted ten thousand, the peak would become "Mt. Mint-cang". The adepti blocked Zizhi's escape routes while the Traveler confronted him and dealt with her. Freed, Mountain Shaper admired the seedlings, assumed the Traveler had planted them and invited them to his garden on Mt. Hulao, until told the truth; Cloud Retainer then gave him a stern scolding.
