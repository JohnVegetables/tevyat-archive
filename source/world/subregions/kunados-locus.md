## summary
### LITE
Kunado's Locus is one of the three hidden corner towers of Byakuyakoku, built so that the Human realm could sit stably between the Vishap Realm and the Abyssal Void. It is concealed by secret arts, and only shrine maidens such as Eboshi and the Vassals of Watatsumi can summon it. Its trial is Kunado's Trial, guarded by a Jibashiri named Daimon.
### STANDARD +
It is one of three such places, with Yachimatahiko's Locus and Yachimatahime's Locus, whose current names replaced the lost ancient ones after Orobashi's arrival. In Kunado's Trial the Traveler faces illusions born of Sunfire Phantasms to release four torch flames.

## geography
### STANDARD
Initially hidden and unmarked, the Locus is raised by Eboshi when the Traveler agrees to the trials. After that, a Time Tunnel at the Emanant Skylight in the Serpent's Heart carries the Traveler here to a Teleport Waypoint. It can also be reached by gliding from rocks above the Dainichi Mikoshi.
### LARGE +
Most of the Locus is walled in by a barrier until the trial has been done sufficiently; only a few patches of cliff and the waypoint area lie outside it. The tower's location is one of three corners of Byakuyakoku, marking the limits of the see-saw clash between humans and vishaps.

## places
### STANDARD
A tower arena with four torches (the Flames of the High Gate) before a seal, each linked to a climbable statue; a pool and Time Tunnel at the Emanant Skylight; the Teleport Waypoint at the Locus.
### LARGE +
Daimon waits at the Tower of the Three Realms above, as he tells the Traveler; the afterimage in front of the seal speaks to the Traveler before the fight.

## people
### STANDARD
Daimon of the Jibashiri, who tests worthiness. Eboshi, the Sinshade and Sangonomiya auxiliary who offers the three trials and afterwards explains the Bathysmal Vishaps, the Watatsumi Omikami, the time before sun and moon, and the Dainichi Mikoshi.
### LARGE +
Daimon addresses the Traveler as the Hero of Sangonomiya and says he acknowledges them as worthy of the challenge.

## history
### STANDARD
The people of Byakuyakoku built the Towers of the Three Realms at the borders. Byakuyakoku lay where the Human, Elemental (Vishap) and Abyssal (Void) realms met, and the towers were meant to stabilise its tendencies. Hydrological Studies says they stand in places aligned with neither wind nor water, because their job is to control both, and if they fell the whole nation would be imperilled, hence their concealment.
### LARGE +
Their names were forgotten and changed once Orobashi (Watatsumi) arrived. The Dainichi Mikoshi, by contrast, was once Helios, built by the sage Aberaku, standing for earth between wind and water.
### ULTRA +
The Byakuyakoku Collection's folktale verse speaks of 'three corners in darkness shrouded', and a child of Enkanomiya in the same tale dives beyond the three corners, evading the Dragonheirs, to look for a Dragonbone Flower; the text ties the corners to the borders of the realm rather than to any specific tower. Completing Kunado's Trial grants the achievement 'Unmatched Throughout Tokoyo'. Before Eboshi raises the area, arriving by glider shows only floating textures and the area's name.

## quests
### STANDARD
Erebos' Secret, whose requirement is From Dusk to Dawn in Byakuyakoku, contains The Three Great Martial Trials, with Kunado's, Yachimatahiko's and Yachimatahime's Trials taken in any order.
### LARGE +
Kunado's Trial has four waves: Thundercraven Rifthound Whelps; Hydro, Cryo and Electro Abyss Mages; Primordial and Rimebiter Bathysmal Vishap Hatchlings; and an Abyss Herald, Wicked Torrents. Daimon says the foes are illusions made from Sunfire Phantasms and the great enemies the Traveler knows in their heart, and he describes valour as the power to defeat all foes, whatever the method.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Illusory enemies in the trial; the barrier that blocks entry before the trial is done.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Floating ruins, mist below, and a heavy sense of an ancient safeguard still watching. Little moves except the flames.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the Locus is unchanged, an ancient tower that the Traveler once passed through.
### STANDARD +
Nothing from 6.x or 7.0-7.1 touches Enkanomiya. After the trials the Locuses remain hidden unless summoned.

## entry: Towers of the Three Realms
@keys: Towers of the Three Realms, Tower of the Three Realms, Three Great Martial Trials, Erebos' Secret
### STANDARD
The towers stabilise Byakuyakoku and can only be summoned by shrine maidens and the Vassals of Watatsumi. They are renamed Yachimatahiko's, Yachimatahime's and Kunado's Locus. The names are borrowed from Japanese folk deities; Kunado in particular is a Japanese local god believed to guard against evil spirits, which is a real-world etymology, not an in-world one.
