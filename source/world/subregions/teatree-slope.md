## summary
### LITE
Teatree Slope lies in the southern mountain of Chenyu Vale, south of Qiaoying Village and north of Yaodie Valley. It was once part of the Herblord's land and is where the adeptus Fujin planted the vale's first tea tree, which still grows there.
### STANDARD +
The tree is the ancestor of the tea that made Chenyu Vale known in Liyue Harbor and elsewhere.

## history
### STANDARD
Long ago the vale belonged to its unnamed mistress, who was served by the snake-spirit adeptus the Herblord and the golden-carp adeptus Fujin. Fujin, who was fond of humans, planted the first tea tree in the Herblord's territory. Later its branches were grafted across the river by mountain dwellers and its tea grew famous. The tea is said to thrive only in Chenyu Vale because the tree remembers a promise between two friends.
### LARGE +
During the Archon War the mistress sent the Bishui River in flood against Morax. Fujin and the Herblord turned against her: the Herblord fought their former master and went missing, while Fujin threw the Votive Rainjade into the water and was later trapped at Carp's Rest. In the present Fujin asked the Traveler to restore the vale's spirit lines when its tea was tainted.

## places
### STANDARD
The slope's viewpoint is titled "Ancient Pact". The old tea tree is the focal point.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
A quiet mountain slope above tea country; humid, green and calm.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the tree still stands; nothing after 4.4 changes it.

### ULTRA
Accounts of the first tea tree come from item texts and Baizhu's Character Story: it was planted by Fujin on the Herblord's land, someone later tied a jade ornament to the sapling, and the pact between two friends is said to be why its tea thrives only here. The tea banquet promised for all the adepti was never held, and the Herblord's own fate is unknown.
