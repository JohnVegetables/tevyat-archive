## summary
### LITE
Vissudha Field is a green, open area of Sumeru's Dharma Forest, just outside Sumeru City. Its notable feature is the Fane of Ashvattha, which holds the Ashvattha Tree, the oldest Vasara Tree and the progenitor of the Aranara, and which seals the entrance to the Aranara's old home, Old Vanarana. Mushrooms grow here, a Jadeplume Terrorshroom lurks in it, and the Aranara tie it to the Aranyaka quests.
### STANDARD +
It was released in Version 3.0 and extended west in 3.4 so that it joins the Desert of Hadramaveth. Its name draws on Vissudha, the fifth chakra in Hindu tantra, a symbol of purity.

## geography
### STANDARD
The field lies in the Dharma Forest subregion of Sumeru, and Travelers' Tales anecdotes place it outside Sumeru City. It holds a Statue of The Seven, a fishing point, and one subarea, the Fane of Ashvattha. The wiki lists Rukkhashava Mushrooms and Nilotpala Lotus as local specialties.
### LARGE +
In Version 3.4 the area was expanded west to connect with the Desert of Hadramaveth, so it serves as a green threshold between the rainforest and the desert. The Fane's name is not shown on the map until the quest For Fruits, Seeds, and Trees has been completed.

## places
### STANDARD
The Fane of Ashvattha: a sacred hollow containing the Ashvattha Tree, the first Vasara Tree and progenitor of the Aranara. It also seals the way into Old Vanarana, the Aranara's former home.
### LARGE +
Old Vanarana is a separate subarea within the Lost Nursery area, reached from the Fane through the Door of Vana, the first of four seals the Aranara laid after the Cataclysm to imprison Marana's Avatar. The wiki's page for this field also records a notice in front of the Steeple of Ignorance warning visitors about large Rishboland Tigers; that domain itself is listed near Chatrakam Cave in the Lokapala Jungle.

## people
### STANDARD
Aranara, the small forest spirits who regard the Ashvattha Tree as their origin, are tied to the place, though most adults cannot see them. Eremites and treasure hoarders also appear in the area, and the Aranara cook Arachatora features in a quest here.
### LARGE +
Cyno and Tighnari appear in a Travelers' Tales anecdote set here: Tighnari is shopping for mushroom soup ingredients for Collei while Cyno ponders a mushroom-related question, and in a follow-up Cyno is collecting bark and soil samples.

## history
### STANDARD
Arama tells that Queen Aranyani sang here long ago, when the land still had sand, and the Ashvattha Tree responded, sprouting from Sarva. It produced a pomegranate whose first seed to sprout was Aramuhukunda, the first Aranara, and the others followed. This is the Aranara's own account.
### LARGE +
In the quest For Fruits, Seeds, and Trees (Aranyaka Part III, 3.0) the Traveler and Arama bring three Vasoma Fruits to the Ashvattha Tree, find its dream corroded by the Withering (Marana), and cleanse it. The tree cannot bear a Bija, so Arama prepares to grow a new Ashvattha Tree and leads the Traveler into Old Vanarana in For the Children of the Past. Arama did become the new Ashvattha Tree inside Old Vanarana, after which Old Vanarana began to recover.

### ULTRA +
According to the Laurel Coronet text, Viaghara, ruler of the jungle, blessed the Aranara under the first Vasara Tree, and his pact with the divine kept birds and beasts from harming them. After the Cataclysm the Aranara sealed Marana's Avatar in Old Vanarana with four layers: the Door of Vana from the Fane, three Inscriptions of Remembrance made by Aramaha, Arayama and Araji (who later became Vasara Trees in Ashvattha's Concourses), the Ararakalari seals of many Aranara, and a final seal at the Sand-Embraced Home guarded by Aramuhukunda. Despite this, Marana's corrosion slowly leaked out.

## dangers
### STANDARD
The Jadeplume Terrorshroom, a Dendro fungus, is the field's normal boss. Eremites, treasure hoarders and Withering Zones can be met, and the wiki mentions a notice about large Rishboland Tigers active at night.
### LARGE +
Withering Zones appear in quests near the Fane. In the hidden exploration Even Beasts Get Homesick, a Sumpter Beast is followed to its destination in the Ashavan Realm, where its owner, an Eremite Crossbow, must be defeated.

## quests
### STANDARD
Aranyaka Part III (Nursery of Lost Dreams): For Fruits, Seeds, and Trees and For the Children of the Past start or pass through here. The world quest Cooking, the Flavor of Nature (An Unwavering Culinary Dream) begins here. Anecdotes: Cyno: Myco-management and Cyno: Food. Hidden exploration: Even Beasts Get Homesick.
### LARGE +
Search Chronicle: For Childhood Dreams! is also in the wiki category for the field, but only its title was reviewed.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
A mild, green, mushroom-scented rural field close to the capital, with a quiet sacred place at its centre where an ancient tree holds the dreams and memories of the forest's small spirits.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the field is open to explore; nothing in 6.x or 7.0-7.1 changes it.
### STANDARD +
The Ashvattha Tree in the Fane is withered, and a new Ashvattha Tree grows in Old Vanarana from Arama.

## entry: Fane of Ashvattha

@keys2: Aranara, Vissudha Field, Arama
@version: 3.0
### STANDARD
The Fane of Ashvattha holds the Ashvattha Tree, the first and oldest Vasara Tree and the origin of the Aranara. Arama says it sprouted from Sarva when Queen Aranyani sang, and its fruit produced Aramuhukunda, the first Aranara. It also seals the entrance to Old Vanarana. By Aranyaka Part III the tree is corroded by Marana and cannot bear a Bija, so Arama becomes the new Ashvattha Tree inside Old Vanarana.

## entry: Vasara Tree
@keys: Vasara Tree, Vasara Trees, Vasoma Fruit
@keys2: Aranara, Ashvattha
@version: 3.0
### STANDARD
Vasara Trees are usually created by Aranara and hold dreams and memories. An Aranara can choose to become one and remains partly conscious. Aranara are sometimes described as seeds that may one day grow into Vasara Trees. Each tree bears a Vasoma Fruit once cleansed.
