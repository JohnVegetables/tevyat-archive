## summary
### LITE
Guiztli Path is a winding cavern linking Villa Guiztli to Castle Joquiratto on the Easybreeze resort island. It is a stretch of the resort's immersive adventure in which visitors play heroes defending a town from the villain El Capoctus Joquiratto.
### STANDARD +
Nochtli lackeys, the play-acting 'bandits', mill about the path in staged skirmishes.

## geography
### STANDARD
A cavern under Guiztli Ridge. Villa Guiztli is the underground town at one end and Castle Joquiratto, the villain's stronghold, at the other.
### LARGE +
The map names the zone as the northern part of the ridge's inner cavern.

## people
@version: 5.8
### STANDARD
Nochtli lackeys and the cardboard cutouts and Nochtlins of the villa.
### LARGE +
One lackey shoots red paint at a chest and shouts 'Enemies ahead!', then announces himself as the enemy and surrenders when the Traveler does not fall for it.
### ULTRA +
Villa Guiztli at one end is an underground town inhabited by cardboard cutouts and Nochtlins, and it is fully unlocked only by progressing the quest To Guiztli Ridge!. Before the quest ends its capybaras and alpacas grumble that the lackeys keep robbing the place.

## culture
### STANDARD
The path belongs to a staged role-play adventure for tourists, not a real conflict.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
A theatrical cave: painted stage props, cheerful 'villains' and a sense of play.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Mock ambushes; the chest in the exchange summons an Eroding Avatar of Lava, a gameplay challenge.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the path remains open as part of the attraction.
