## summary
### LITE
Guyun Stone Forest is an archipelago off Liyue's coast: seven large islands and smaller ones, crowded with jagged stone pillars that legend says are the remains of the lances Rex Lapis hurled to pin the sea god Osial to the ocean floor. People in Liyue say it is more a vista to behold than a place to visit. It holds the Domain of Guyun, a Geo Hypostasis on its northernmost island and, anchored off its shore, the Crux fleet's flagship Alcor.
### STANDARD +
The in-game text calls the pillars "proud, lonely stone mountains" and says that sensitive listeners sometimes hear a strange, stirring rhythm coming from the deep below. The expedition notes add that the forest is said to be a graveyard of the old gods. The Chinese name means "Lone Cloud Pavilion"; some English voice-over lines call it Guyun Tower.

## geography
### STANDARD
Seven large islands and small offshoots in the sea off Liyue Harbor. The northernmost island houses the Geo Hypostasis and an island in the southwest the Domain of Guyun. Tall stone pillars rise from the islands. The shallow seas around them yield sea salt.
### LARGE +
Hilichurls live on some of the small islands. Local specialties include Cor Lapis, Noctilucous Jade, Qingxin and Starconch. Roald, the adventurer, counted six giant stone pillars when he came ashore and found the shade cool and the beach crabs large. The Alcor is moored just off the islands.

## places
### STANDARD
- The Geo Hypostasis' island in the north.
- The Domain of Guyun in the southwest, an altar of ley lines.
- The Alcor, flagship of the Crux fleet, anchored offshore.
- Ruin-treasure monuments on the southern islands.
### LARGE +
A sequence of monuments on the southern islands tells the story of a disciple who trained from childhood to fight monsters and sought a Vision; the monuments at Guyun and Mt. Aocang belong together. At the smallest southern islands, Guizhong Ballistae were once installed to fight a sea monster.

## people
### STANDARD
The crew of the Alcor, including the chief mate Juza, the navigator Huixing, the ironworker Suling, the bookkeeper Mora-Grubber, the surgeon Yinxing, and sailors such as Furong, Xu Liushi and Sea Drake. Captain Beidou normally commands the ship but has often returned to Liyue Harbor, leaving it to her crew.
### LARGE +
Hilichurls hold some of the small islands, and the adventurer Roald has visited. Keqing commanded Millelith here during the Beisht crisis.

## culture
### STANDARD
Liyue folk treat Guyun as a place to look at rather than visit. The tale of Rex Lapis' victory over Osial and other sea monsters is a much-loved legend, and Liyue folk tell it in different versions.
### LARGE +
Zhongli is said to recommend sea salt from the shallow seas of Guyun for cooking, according to Xiangling. Beidou recalls that one Lantern Rite eve, as her ship limped back to port in a storm, she looked toward Guyun Stone Forest and saw an adeptus floating in the air in the form of a young boy, and took it as proof that there are those who watch over Liyue.

## history
### STANDARD
Guyun is the battlefield where, about two thousand years ago in the Archon War, Rex Lapis defeated the god Osial, the Overlord of the Vortex, a sea monster who could raise waves as tall as Liyue's mountains. Rex Lapis pinned him to the sea floor with giant lances of stone, and the pillars are said to be what remains.
### LARGE +
Liyue's sources differ on the detail: that he used the sacred lance Vortex Vanquisher to pierce the rainbow of Osial and nail him to the deep sea's centre; or that he carved a polearm out of the rocky land and drove it through the monster, and wind has since worn it into the pillars. Zhongli calls his conflict with Osial an old grudge, long gone.
### ULTRA +
Osial remained sealed for roughly two thousand years until Childe used Fatui-made Sigils of Permission in 1.1 to free him and lure out the Geo Archon. The Jade Chamber was dropped on him and he was resealed.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Cool shade under towering pillars, sea wind and the sound of waves, with beaches below. Those who sleep here are said to dream of being the sea monster, pinned and furious.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Hilichurls and shooters, Abyss mages, Fatui agents, Geovishap hatchlings, Ruin Guards, and the Geo Hypostasis. Osial's strength did not fade in two thousand years of imprisonment, so the seal over him matters.
### LARGE +
Spending the night is considered a bad idea because of the nightmares of Osial's impaling.

## travel
### STANDARD
Roald borrowed a boat to reach the islands; in practice they are reached by boat and by gliding between them. The Alcor lies just off the islands, and the Domain of Guyun is in the southwest.

## quests
### LARGE
- A New Star Approaches (Archon Quest, 1.1): Osial, freed by Childe, is fought at the Jade Chamber and resealed beneath the sea off Guyun.
- Where the Heart Finds Rest (Interlude Chapter, 2.4): Beisht, Osial's "wife", attacks, and Keqing commands ballistae here.
### ULTRA +
A world quest, A Lone Ship in Guyun, is triggered by boarding the Alcor.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 Guyun Stone Forest is a silent archipelago with Osial resealed and the Alcor anchored offshore. No content from 6.x or 7.0-7.1 changes it.
### STANDARD +
Osial was resealed at the end of the 1.1 Archon Quest, and Beisht was driven off in the 2.4 Interlude.

## entry: Osial and Rex Lapis' Stone Spears
@keys: Osial, Overlord of the Vortex, Vortex Vanquisher
@keys2: Guyun, Guyun Stone Forest, Rex Lapis
### STANDARD
Osial, the Overlord of the Vortex, was a god and sea monster of the Archon War, strong enough to raise tsunamis tall as mountains. Rex Lapis defeated him at Guyun and pinned him with stone spears, which became the pillars of Guyun Stone Forest. In 1.1 Childe freed him with Fatui Sigils of Permission to lure out the Geo Archon; the adepti and Millelith fought him and Ningguang dropped the Jade Chamber on him. A fragment of the spear, a Monolith Fragment, was recovered afterwards. Osial's "wife" Beisht later sought revenge.

## entry: The Alcor
@keys: Alcor, The Crux, Crux Fleet
@keys2: Guyun
### STANDARD
The Alcor is the flagship of the Crux fleet, anchored off Guyun Stone Forest. Beidou usually captains it but lately lets her crew run it. Boarding it begins a world quest, A Lone Ship in Guyun.

## entry: The Disciple's Monuments
@keys: Ruins Treasure, disciple, monsters evil
@keys2: Guyun Stone Forest, Mt. Aocang
### LARGE
At Guyun, monuments left by a disciple say he trained from childhood with a fellow disciple to fight evil, and that the two sought refuge here to train and to seek Visions. When the fellow disciple received a Vision, he asked whether monsters are truly evil by nature; the author answered that monsters wreak havoc and joked that too much meditation had addled him. He resolved to keep hunting monsters alone, even without a Vision. A matching series at Mt. Aocang is written by the other disciple, who received the Vision and began to doubt.

## entry: Beisht at Guyun
@keys: Beisht
@keys2: Guyun, Osial, Jade Chamber
@version: 2.4
### LARGE
In 2.4, Beisht, who hated the Jade Chamber for defeating her husband Osial, was lured out to the waters off Guyun. Ningguang rebuilt the Jade Chamber, Beidou warned her of Beisht's presence, and Keqing led the Millelith at Guyun Stone Forest, where Guizhong Ballistae there and on the Alcor attacked Beisht. Shenhe leapt into the vortex after Beisht, and afterwards the fleet reported that the sea monster had left Guyun and the waters had returned to normal. Keqing said the Millelith's casualties were within the expected range.
