## summary
### LITE
Yaodie Valley (the "Valley of Vibrant Butterflies") is a mountain valley in Chenyu Vale, in the southern mountains of Liyue. It holds the abode of the adeptus called the Herblord, hidden behind butterflies and a cave, and locals say it is not welcoming to outsiders.
### STANDARD +
It lies south of Teatree Slope and north of Mt. Xuanlian. Lingshu Courtyard, where the Herblord lived, is reached from here, and the valley's colourful butterflies are tied to her adeptal energy. Village elders speak of it only by hearsay.

## geography
### STANDARD
A butterfly-filled valley in Chenyu Vale: Southern Mountain, between Teatree Slope to the north and Mt. Xuanlian to the south. A cave in the valley leads to Lingshu Courtyard, a cavern-garden of Seelie, stalagmites, hanging jars and stone stands. Its viewpoint is titled "Valley of Vibrant Butterflies".
### LARGE +
Lingshu Courtyard holds Clearwater Jade and Glaze Lily as local specialties, with Grounded Geoshrooms and Whirling Electro Fungus as common enemies. Adeptus's Repose, the Herblord's former abode, can be entered through the courtyard's quest or by clearing the Miasma at its entrance. Teatree Slope next door was once part of the Herblord's lands and has the first tea tree of Chenyu Vale.

## people
### STANDARD
No living resident is named. The only occupants are stone simulacra: a talkative Carefree Simulacrum that scares off intruders, and a Sacred Simulacrum that has to be set on its stand. Xuanfu, a herb gatherer in the vale, tells the Traveler the valley is full of butterflies and not welcoming to outsiders, and that he knows it only from village elders.
### LARGE +
The Herblord, an adeptus, once lived here. She is the one who later became Changsheng, Baizhu's snake companion, after the Archon War, according to the Herblord's wiki page; the quest text only calls her the Herblord. Her friends in legend were Fujin, a golden carp adeptus who loves humans, and Lingyuan, a suanni.
### ULTRA +
The Carefree Simulacrum still guards the abode, saying the Herblord did not like her peace disturbed. It admits it said the butterflies would revive the withered tree on a whim and is surprised when it works.

## history
### STANDARD
In the distant past, according to the viewpoint text, ancestral peoples who had lost their homes to war were taken in by the adeptus Fujin, who sheltered them here from the disaster raging outside, so that the flame of civilisation survived the great floods. In the Chenyu Vale lore, early settlements in the vale were founded by refugees from the Chasm's cataclysm.
### LARGE +
During the Archon War the Herblord and Fujin served the unnamed Mistress of Chenyu Vale. When that goddess tried a flood against Morax that would have destroyed the vale, the Herblord fought her, Lingyuan evacuated the people and Fujin cast the Votive Rainjade into the water. The Herblord was left greatly diminished, and the abode has been empty since.
### ULTRA +
Notes left in Adeptus's Repose show the Herblord's private side: she grumbles about Fujin's chatter about the "Motive Mainjade", and made slimming tea for Lingyuan while pointing out that an adeptal human form can never gain weight. A third note says she feared something from talking to the Mistress and put some of her adeptal energy into the butterflies, so that the mortals of Chenyu Vale might follow them to the formulae she left behind.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
A hushed, hazy valley full of coloured butterflies, far from the noise of mortal affairs. The quest dialogue is comic with the Carefree Simulacrum's pretended menace; the notes are warm and a little melancholy.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Hilichurls and a Rock Shieldwall Mitachurl guard parts of the valley's cave, and Miasma can block the entrance of Adeptus's Repose. In Lingshu Courtyard Grounded Geoshrooms and Electro Fungus are the common foes.

## travel
### STANDARD
Yaodie Valley lies in Chenyu Vale's southern mountain, reached past Teatree Slope (added in 4.4). The Carefree Simulacrum says a branch from the revived tree placed in the broken jar outside the abode opens the stone gate.

## quests
### STANDARD
"Silently the Butterfly Crosses the Valley" (world quest, 4.4) starts by entering a cave in the valley. The Traveler chases five coloured butterflies back into a medicine jar, which revives the withered adeptal tree, then uses a branch to open the stone gate and reads three ancient texts left by the Herblord.
### LARGE +
Completion grants a Luxurious Chest and an achievement, and unlocks the Herb-Housing Hall viewpoint.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the valley is open and unchanged; the Herblord's abode remains empty, and her current life as Changsheng continues with Baizhu elsewhere.

## entry: The Herblord's Abode
@keys: Herblord, Lingshu Courtyard, Adeptus's Repose, Carefree Simulacrum
@version: 4.4
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
The Herblord's abode is a cave garden entered from Yaodie Valley, containing a medicine jar that holds a young tree and drives a door mechanism with adeptal energy. Her notes record how the branch she planted was growing, how the door-opening jar was damaged long ago, and her private worry about the Mistress of Chenyu Vale. The Carefree Simulacrum guards it, claims it can stay asleep, and cheerfully admits when it has invented something.
