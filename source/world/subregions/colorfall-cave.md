## summary
### LITE
Colorfall Cave lies just south of Colorfall Cliffs on the Easybreeze resort island. A crooked sideline used it as a hidden workshop for making fake Koholasaur dolls, and a young painter's apprentice named Zhaka was held there against his will until the Traveler freed him.
### STANDARD +
The shady arrangement belongs to Rekis, an underworld figure from Nod-Krai who is mentioned but never seen.

## geography
### STANDARD
A cave south of Colorfall Cliffs, in the Easybreeze Holiday Resort. Its map level is labelled as the eastern cavern of Tenochtzitoc, the island's old name.
### LARGE +
It is a minor dead end of the resort that tourists have no reason to visit.

## people
@version: 5.8
### STANDARD
Rekis's associates ran the workshop. Zhaka, Kanei's apprentice at the paint workshop, was taken there by mistake; his Asha companion is Azhka. After his rescue he appears at Easybreeze Market.
### LARGE +
Per the Rekis page, Rekis ordered the kidnapping of the painter Kanei. A henchman named Manou seized Zhaka instead. When Zhaka's skills proved poor, Rekis scolded Manou and ordered the weaker fakes to be foisted on unsuspecting tourists. Zhaka's own words to the Traveler later are that without the rescue his master might have thrown him out.
### ULTRA +
The wiki lists Zhaka as an open-world NPC of Natlan affiliated with the People of the Springs, and says he is Kanei's apprentice at the paint workshop.

## history
@version: 5.8
### STANDARD
The cave was used for a counterfeit scheme during the resort's opening season. The scheme made fake Koholasaur dolls for tourists to buy, using a hostage painter.
### LARGE +
A note called Rekis's Orders found here says word came from Nasha Town that the Curatorium of Secrets had placed an informant on the island, watching Rekis's people and the crew of Radius Bone. The informant's registered name at reception is Rantiy and the note advises avoiding conflict with him.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
A damp, hidden hideaway behind the cheerful colours of the cliffs above: crates, half-finished dolls and the nervous air of people who expect to be found out.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Rekis's henchmen were the threat while Zhaka was held. Nothing suggests that the cave is dangerous now.

## quests
@version: 5.8
### STANDARD
Zhaka's rescue takes place here as part of the 5.8 Easybreeze content.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the workshop is abandoned and the cave is open.
### STANDARD +
Zhaka lives and works at the Easybreeze Market. Rekis's operations against Asha and tourists are not shown to continue.
