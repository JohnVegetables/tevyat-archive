## summary
### LITE
Wolvendom is a deep, dark forest in Windwail Highland, Mondstadt, so silent that it chills the spine. It is the domain of Boreas, also called Andrius, the Wolf of the North of the Four Winds, and his wolf pack. Locals avoid it, and not even fearless hunters enter lightly. The boy Razor, raised by the wolves, lives here.
### STANDARD +
Boreas' physical body died long ago and now only his spirit holds the forest. In his arena there he can manifest a temporary form of ice and wind to test challengers, and he takes in wolf packs and orphaned infants as the pack's leader.

## geography
### STANDARD
A forested part of Windwail Highland. A Teleport Waypoint and a beach facing Cider Lake lie at its edge, and Boreas' arena sits deeper in. An Elemental Monument and a time-trial challenge are found here. Local plants include calla lilies, dandelion seeds, small lamp grass and wolfhook, a plant used to treat wounded wolves.
### LARGE +
Official text describes the howling of the wolves at night as carrying a long way. Springvale villagers readily blame the pack when wolves raid, and attacks were reported at Drunkard Gorge as well as Springvale in Razor's story quest.

## people
### STANDARD
Boreas leads the pack, which includes Pale-Furred Wolves. Razor, a boy raised by the pack, lives among them; Boreas has told him plainly that he is human, not a wolf. Ross the Quick, a traveller, can be found on the beach by the waypoint facing Cider Lake.
### LARGE +
Varka, the Grand Master of the Knights, earned Boreas' favour by repeatedly challenging him and, as Knight of Boreas, later took Razor under his wing, teaching him human speech and swordplay. The Knights' tradition of a Knight of Boreas dates back to the wolf's early days as one of the Four Winds.

## history
### STANDARD
Boreas began as an ordinary wolf from Snezhnaya whose pack met Koitar at Amsvartnir in the far north; contact with her unknowingly blessed him with speech and divine power, and he called her his mother. He moved south, warred with Decarabian, decided he was unfit to be Archon and went back to Nod-Krai to sleep. About a thousand years later he split his soul so a part could accompany the Wandering Knight to Mondstadt; seeing it flourish, he let his body die and poured his power into the ley lines. The spirit that remains watches the land from Wolvendom.
### LARGE +
In Chapter I Act IV (Dishonorable Trial) the Traveler found Boreas restrained by an Abyss Herald, which was trying to corrupt him as it had Dvalin; once the herald fell, Boreas explained that a Field Tiller had once blundered in and that he had taken it for a challenger, wounding it. Later, in Razor's story quest, outside riftwolves led by a black wolf were attacking villages, Boreas tested Razor and the Traveler, and told Razor not to join his pack's war since he is human; the follow-up world quest shows the pack joining the fight alongside Razor against the dark wolves.
### ULTRA +
The 6.4 expedition story adds a further chapter (see the entry below).

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
A forest of heavy shade and unnatural quiet, broken only by distant howls. It feels watched; visitors are on the wolves' terms.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Wolves hold the area, and outsiders face Boreas' challenge. Hilichurls, Abyss Mages, Mitachurls, Anemo and Hydro Samachurls, slimes and Cryo Whopperflowers are common. Rockfond Rifthound Whelps have been seen here since version 2.4, first hinted at in Razor's quest.
### LARGE +
In his arena Boreas takes the form of a large bluish wolf, Lupus Boreas, Dominator of Wolves. As a gameplay note, after Razor's story quest this fight is a recurring weekly challenge. The form cannot use his full strength.

## travel
### STANDARD
Reached overland from Mondstadt City or Springvale; the waypoint on the shore is the usual way in.

## quests
### STANDARD
Related: Chapter I Act IV (Dishonorable Trial), Lupus Minor Chapter Act I (The Meaning of Lupical), With Lupical... (Witch's Homework), and the Lupus Majoris Chapter (6.4).

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the pack and Boreas' spirit still hold Wolvendom; Razor lives there.
### STANDARD +
The Wild Hunt attack seen in 6.4 was fended off and no newer event is recorded here.

### ULTRA
During the Nod-Krai expedition story (6.4) the Wild Hunt appeared in Wolvendom and attacked Boreas, who held it off with Razor's help. When Varka needed the wolf's other half, Boreas asked Razor for his claymore and imbued it with power for the Traveler to carry to Varka. Varka then met the Nod-Krai half of Andrius, corrupted by an abyssal copy of Roland, inside the wolf; that half chose to let its power flow into the ley lines, and Varka found his way back.
