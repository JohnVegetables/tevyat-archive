## summary
### LITE
Gavireh Lajavard is the mountainous western stretch of the Girdle of the Sands, a forbidden-feeling region of Sumeru added in version 3.6. Its subareas include the Gate of Zulqarnain, the Temir Mountains, Tunigi Hollow and the buried Khaenri'ahn factory Hangeh Afrasiyab. Beneath it lie ruins of the lost nation of Khaenri'ah, and above it the Order of Skeptics keep watch.
### STANDARD +
The Girdle of the Sands is described officially as a forbidden realm beneath the desert where few have set foot, holding the ruins of an ancient civilisation, the legacy of the divine and a hidden lost Darshan. Gavireh Lajavard and the Realm of Farakhkert are the Girdle's two areas.

## geography
### STANDARD
Rugged high ground. The Temir Mountains are the main passage between the Desert of Hadramaveth and the rest of the Girdle of the Sands, and the Gate of Zulqarnain stands among them. Tunigi Hollow is a deep hollow that leads down to the Harvisptokhm, and a locked Khaenri'ahn gate lies far below at Hangeh Afrasiyab.
### LARGE +
Notable features include Fravashi Trees, Soul Bells, Kory drums, Bright Flame Altars, Sunyata Flowers, Nirodha Fruit and Khvarena Inscription Fragments. Local specialties are Mourning Flower and Trishiraite. The boss enemy is the Iniquitous Baptist, who is also met at the Gate of Zulqarnain.

## places
### STANDARD
The Gate of Zulqarnain, whose name the wiki traces to Dhu al-Qarnayn, the figure who built a barrier against Gog and Magog. The Temir Mountains. Tunigi Hollow, the epicentre of the Abyssal contamination during the cataclysm. Hangeh Afrasiyab, an abandoned underground Khaenri'ahn factory for processing Azosite. Pyrrho's campsite, southwest of the waypoint into the Temir Mountains, and the Molten Iron Fortress domain.
### LARGE +
Hangeh Afrasiyab's viewpoint is called the Forge of the Abandoned Foundry, and the sealed gate beyond is titled the Gate of Everlasting Mourning. The loading-screen text calls it a ruined Khaenri'ahn workshop that "might have other titles in their language".
### ULTRA +
In the underground ruins, the wiki records a proving-grounds chamber, a pipe room, a smelting furnace room and the room where Klingsor and Nasejuna are fought, each with documents lying around. Workshop notes tie the factory's Azosite alloys to the large Mechanical Wardens, the Ruin Golem class of machine.

## people
### STANDARD
Few live here. The Order of Skeptics hold an outpost behind the Statue of the Seven, where a note orders strict control of mountain passes to ensure a ceremony and plan can be completed. The Pari Sorush joins the Traveler, and the Pari live in the nearby Vourukasha Oasis.
### LARGE +
The NPC Pyrrho camps near the waypoint into the Temir Mountains. The Fatui have camps in the region, the subject of the world quest Investigate the Fatui Camps Marked by Sosi.

## history
### STANDARD
Tunigi Hollow was the epicentre of the Abyssal and forbidden-knowledge pollution that spread from Khaenri'ah during the cataclysm. Several gods, including Greater Lord Rukkhadevata and Egeria, came to suppress the defilement seeping from the godless nation below. Hangeh Afrasiyab, a Khaenri'ahn factory for refining Azosite, remains sealed above the lost kingdom.
### LARGE +
Documents in the ruins describe Khaenri'ah's last days: a workshop notes an Azosite alloy intended to power the large Mechanical Wardens and expresses a hope of a "perpetual machine" built through human ingenuity rather than dogma from beyond the heavens; a bulletin notes that Knight Marshal Anfortas would act as Regent of the Regnum Concilium Ultimum while King Irmin was indisposed; a Schwanenritter commander, Hadura, orders the Wardens activated and survivors evacuated; and a journal says most people fled to the surface with the Schwanenritter, leaving a few hiding below, with rumours that those who fled above became monsters.
### ULTRA +
A monument inscription revealed in Monumental Study says that with Amrita and Khvarena the ancients extinguished phantoms mirroring the bedeviled sky, thanked the protection of the Dahri, and warned later generations not to fall to decadence. Another note attributed to an unknown hand argues that Khvarena and the Abyss belong to the same order of rules, both able to rewrite them, and that their users are all stuck in an unconscious stage of being overwritten.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Abyssal and Fatui forces, the Iniquitous Baptist, hilichurls (including Hydro rogues), Thundercraven and Rockfond Rifthound whelps, fungi, and the remnants of Khaenri'ahn defences. Tunigi Hollow's defilement is a danger until it is cleansed.

## travel
### STANDARD
The Temir Mountains connect the area to the Desert of Hadramaveth. The Order of Skeptics control the mountain passes. Tunigi Hollow provides the way down to the Harvisptokhm, and after the Rite of Chinvat jumping into it sends the player there.

## quests
### STANDARD
The Splendorous Sky That Day, Asipattravana Itihasa, The Hymn of Tir Yazad (Parts 1 and 2), As the Khvarena's Light Shows and its Dukkha chapter, Monumental Study, Pale Fire, Awakening's Real Sound, Lightcall Resonance, The Recollector's Path and Investigate the Fatui Camps Marked by Sosi. These form the Khvarena of Good and Evil storyline, treated in the Vourukasha Oasis entity.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Austere, mountainous and heavy with history, with Skeptic watchposts above and silent factories below.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the region is calmer and its worst defilement cleansed, with the Khaenri'ahn ruins still sealed or silent.
### STANDARD +
The Sign of Apaosha was extinguished in The Hymn of Tir Yazad (Part 2); nothing from 6.x or 7.0-7.1 changes the area.
