## summary
### LITE
Sulfurous Veins is a subarea of Tequemecan Valley in Natlan. It is a rugged, sulfur-bright stretch of rock known for enormous nails of solid Phlogiston, called "Shadow Pins".
### STANDARD +
Its in-game viewpoint is titled "Pins of Rock Entwined With Golden Runes". The area has one listed point of interest, the Land of Myriad Megaliths, which is the entrance to a Night's Trial domain.

## geography
### STANDARD
A craggy ravine landscape of veined rock inside Tequemecan Valley, with golden runic patterns winding over the stone and giant pin-like pillars set high above. The area was added in Version 5.0 with the rest of Natlan.
### LARGE +
The Land of Myriad Megaliths lies here and is fought through as a combat domain. The area's own music tracks are "Foundry of the Golden Land" and "Ravine's Calcination".

## history
### LARGE
Local legend, quoted in the viewpoint text, says the Shadow Pins are giant solid Phlogiston nails forged by the Wayob of the Children of Echoes from the flesh and blood of their tribal warriors, then hung high up to intimidate the ancient shadows of the depths below. It is presented as legend, not as a confirmed account.

## atmosphere
### ULTRA
Scenes here suit a harsh, mineral place: sulphurous glow, hot rock, the sense of something watched from below. Keep the tone to what the viewpoint implies; do not invent named residents or ceremonies.
