## summary
### LITE
Tepeacac Rise is a stretch of slopes in the northwest of Coatepec Mountain in Natlan. The lower ground is unusually good for plant growth, so Saurians often rest there; Natlan also calls the slopes the "Genesis of the Mountains".
### STANDARD +
Its Nahuatl-style name is thought to relate to words for a settlement on a ridge or hilltop.

## geography
### STANDARD
Green, sloping ground on the edge of the mountain, richer in vegetation than most of the surrounding rock. The local specialty is the Saurian Claw Succulent.
### LARGE +
Next to the Local Legend "Sappho Amidst the Waves" lies an interactable notebook holding an unfinished musical score.

## people
### LARGE
No permanent settlement is listed. Saurians are the usual visitors, resting on the gentler ground.

## culture
### LARGE
The score's note thanks someone for suggestions, says the music became livelier after adjustments, remarks that the writer and friend have not met since the last Pilgrimage of the Return of the Sacred Flame, and proposes fruit wine and new songs next time. The writer is unnamed.
