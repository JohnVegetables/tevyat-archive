## summary
### LITE
The Chasm's Maw is the surface entrance to the Chasm's underground mines in Liyue. A miners' hut stands there, empty since the mine was shut, and a large memorial of rough stones honours the Millelith and Yaksha who died protecting the Chasm five hundred years ago.
### STANDARD +
The viewpoint is named "Mouth of the Great Mine". A miner called Wang prays at the memorial.

## places
### STANDARD
The Miner's Quarters, an abandoned wooden hut, holds an emergency notice: because of sudden changes in the Chasm, all miners were to be dispersed in three days and told to send letters and parcels through the Ministry of Civil Affairs. A battered miner's helmet, the Helm of Warding, lies inside. Nearby is the Millelith memorial.
### LARGE +
A Statue of the Seven and climbable rocks lie behind the memorial. Local specialties are Qingxin and Violetgrass; Geovishaps and Mirror Maidens are the elite threats.

## people
### STANDARD
Wang, a miner who used to work the Chasm, prays at the memorial before anyone descends. He tells the Traveler of the dark calamity five hundred years ago, when the Millelith under a Yaksha held the ore veins and some led the foe into the depths.
### LARGE +
Wang also relays a legend: a Millelith soldier crippled in the fight became a self-taught doctor and set up the memorial after gaining a Vision, and his story became tangled with tales of a Millelith treasure. Wang does not credit the tales but says they keep history alive. In the quest The Millennial Mountains the Traveler gathers six lost offerings (Helm of Warding, Cup of Commons, Skyfeather, Warrior's Spear, Flower of Farsight and Sundial of Ages) to open the treasure.

## history
### STANDARD
Five hundred years ago a dark calamity swallowed the mines and Rex Lapis ordered the Millelith to guard the ore veins; those who died are remembered here. Much older, the Chasm itself was born when a celestial nail ended the civilisation of Lang-Gan.

### ULTRA +
The Chasm's Maw keeps the practical memory of the disaster: Wang says the fallen Millelith turned into elements and became one with the mine, so praying to them is also reverence to the Chasm itself. The Ministry's notice about dispersal and parcels shows the miners were sent off in an orderly way before the closure.


## atmosphere
### LARGE
Rocky, windy, with wooden huts and old stone cairns; reverent and a little bleak.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the area is open and quiet.
