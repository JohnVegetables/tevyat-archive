## summary
### LITE
Jinren Island is a cluster of broken isles at the northernmost tip of Inazuma, lying in the shadow of Mt. Yougou. The expedition description says that in a less peaceful time it crawled with deserters who fell into banditry. Kairagi and Nobushi ronin hold it in its main story.
### STANDARD +
Its name is rendered "Blade Gang Island" in translation. It is best known for the world quest The Farmer's Treasure, in which the Traveler helps a caged young man, Saimon Jirou, and goes hunting for a treasure that turns out to be anything but.

## geography
### STANDARD
The isles are separated by water and sand, with a main island where the ronin camp and a small hill with a cage on top. A Time Tunnel here leads to the arena of the Perpetual Mechanical Array, reached by drawing an Electrogranum from a Thunder Sakura Bough and flying up on the Thunder Spheres.
### LARGE +
Several cages scattered across the island hold chests, a Seelie, a torn page of the Toki Alley Tales series, a dog named Toratarou, and Saimon Jirou. Metal Keys and a Cage Key are hidden around the island.

## people
### STANDARD
Two NPCs are tied to the island: Saimon Jirou, the son of a farming family in Konda Village, and Toratarou, a Shiba owned by the Kairagi and Nobushi band whom Jirou calls friendly and not a biter.
### LARGE +
The ronin are Kairagi and Nobushi (Kikouban, Jintouban and Hitsukeban types). A Nobushi named Saiya Tadayoshi guards Toratarou's cage and respawns even after the dog is freed.

## history
### STANDARD
Jirou had belonged to a group of young troublemakers in Konda Village and was pushed by his parents into the army. He eventually deserted, heard of a treasure and went to Jinren Island, where the ronin caged him.
### LARGE +
In The Farmer's Treasure the Traveler finds Old Stone Slates for him and defeats the ronin, then a Treasure Hoarders group and some Fatui who also turn up. The "treasury" proves to hold only wheat and vegetables, and Jirou goes home to his parents in Konda Village, who had posted missing-person notices. His father obtains a forged document certifying him unfit for service.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Ronin of the Kairagi and Nobushi orders are the main enemies, plus a miniboss guarding Toratarou's cage.
### ULTRA +
The wiki notes that Saiya Tadayoshi is unaffected by commissions and world quests set on the island. Opening Toratarou's cage grants an achievement, and the dog leads the player to a buried chest. In The Farmer's Treasure the final enemy waves are named: a Kairagi leader who 'forgot to name himself', the Treasure Hoarders Koichirou and Kojirou, and the Fatui Vitaly.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Salt wind, sand, ruined lean-tos and cages, with Mt. Yougou looming in the north. Mostly bleak, with a streak of slapstick in the farmer's tale.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1, Jinren Island is unchanged in lore; Jirou has returned home.
