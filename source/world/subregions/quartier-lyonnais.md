## summary
### LITE
Quartier Lyonnais is a street of shops along the northeastern side of the Court of Fontaine. The Fontaine branch of the Adventurers' Guild is here, alongside clockmakers, a boutique and a shop of curiosities.
### STANDARD +
It is a commercial district rather than a civic one, with shopkeepers, adventurers and a member of the Gardes among its listed residents. It was part of Fontaine from its first release in 4.0.

## places
### STANDARD
Along the street: the Adventurers' Guild branch (receptionist Katheryne); Lepaute Horlogerie, a clock shop managed by Caron; Leschots Clockwork Workshop, whose boss is Livre; the Chioriya Boutique, with Eloffe on staff; and Bertin's House of Curiosities, managed by Mequignon. A rotunda west of the Guild branch has a Fontaine Script sign that appears to say d'avocats.
### LARGE +
Behind the rotunda stands a Luxurious Chest. Paimon reads a note in it, from someone who planned to flee to another nation with the money so long as their "Champion Duelist" won, adding that the signature's date shows the schemer did not get away.

## people
### STANDARD
Residents and workers include Caron (Lepaute Horlogerie), Eloffe (Chioriya Boutique), Livre (Leschots), Mequignon (Bertin's), Katheryne (Adventurers' Guild), Ophelia (an adventurer), Santallier (a member of the Gardes), Savinien (Champion Duelist) and several shopkeepers and passers-by such as Graciette, Ligniere, Trudaine, Veta and Xugney.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
A bright, well-kept shopping street: shop signs, clock and curiosity shops, and adventurers visiting the Guild branch. Treat finer details as scene dressing, not canon.

## travel
### LARGE
The district is on the northeastern side of the Court of Fontaine. The Guild branch is the easiest landmark to find; the rotunda lies to its west.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 no change to the district is recorded; it remains an ordinary shopping street.
