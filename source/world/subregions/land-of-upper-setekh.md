## summary
### LITE
The Land of Upper Setekh is a desert region in Sumeru's Great Red Sand, named because it lies in the direction of the sun at noon. Its landmark is the Valley of Dahri, where a giant Khaenri'ahn Ruin Golem sits among Akademiya research notes, and it also holds Lamb-Devourer Rock, the secret base of the Eremite tribe Thutmose.
### STANDARD +
"Setekh" is the Egyptian name of the god Set, associated with deserts, storms, disorder and foreigners. The region's world-quest series is thin: the only quest under its category is Afratu's Dilemma.

## geography
### STANDARD
A rocky desert area with its own Statue of the Seven. A cavern north of the statue and a spot northeast of it hold parts of the Ruin Golem, with Akademiya reports left on them. The Valley of Dahri is a wind-eroded valley, and its viewpoint is titled The Giant of the Wind-Eroded Valley.
### LARGE +
Common enemies in the valley include floating Anemo, stretchy Geo and whirling Pyro fungi, Dendro samachurls and hilichurl fighters. Elite enemies are Ruin Guards and Wooden Shieldwall Mitachurls, and the local specialty is the Scarab.

## places
### STANDARD
The Valley of Dahri, with its Ruin Golem. Lamb-Devourer Rock, a Thutmose hideout reached in Hidden Mercenaries. The area's Statue of the Seven stands beside the cavern with the Ruin Golem's parts.
### LARGE +
The Ruin Golem holds cockpit and arm passages. The wiki's Afratu's Dilemma steps have the Traveler enter the golem by hiding from its shots, pass through its arm, reach the cockpit and break three instances of Suda's Flow.
### ULTRA +
Burned medical records found in the valley describe sufferers of Eleazar with peeling skin, excess hair and severe facial ulceration, some with language impairment, and say the first cases received centralised treatment on orders from above.

## people
### STANDARD
Eremites of the Thutmose tribe hide at Lamb-Devourer Rock; a character named Afratu appears in the world quest, and its first step is to talk to a researcher. Hilichurls (called Majuj by desert people) roam the valley.
### LARGE +
Akademiya investigators left notes here, and the wiki lists them as an earlier team of researchers; one hidden page is attributed to Zandik.

## history
### STANDARD
Akademiya reports found at the Ruin Golem record the team studying the machine, medical reports on the Majuj and on Eleazar sufferers, and an order from the Temple of Silence that all machines from Khaenri'ah be sealed in the format of the Revelatory Monument. Suda's Flow is the sages' name for a perturbation of elemental veins used as a seal.
### LARGE +
One torn page, attributed to Zandik, says the machine's weapons could be repaired from salvaged parts and calls Suda's Flow nothing but sediment from elemental disturbance, which he finds laughable as a seal. The researcher hid the page and kept his measurements quiet from other team members.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Ruin Guards, Mitachurls, fungi, the golem itself in the quest, and Eremite hideouts. Desert exposure is the general risk.

## travel
### STANDARD
The region is part of the Great Red Sand and is reached overland from its other lands; the Statue of the Seven is the local waypoint.

## quests
### STANDARD
Afratu's Dilemma (3.1) starts at the Valley of Dahri: the Traveler helps a researcher enter the Ruin Golem and breaks the Suda's Flow seals. Hidden Mercenaries (part of Old Notes and New Friends) leads to Lamb-Devourer Rock, where the Traveler enters Thutmose's base, finds valuables and hands slates to Soheil.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Wind-carved rock and the silent bulk of a machine from a vanished kingdom, with notes and burned records scattered in the sand.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the region is an open desert area with its Ruin Golem still immobile.
### STANDARD +
Nothing from 6.x or 7.0-7.1 changes it.
