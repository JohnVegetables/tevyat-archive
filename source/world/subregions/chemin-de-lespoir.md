## summary
### LITE
Chemin de L'Espoir (French for "Path of Hope") is a subarea in the Court of Fontaine Region of Fontaine, within the map level of the Source of All Waters, the Great Fontaine Lake. It is known in canon mainly as a place on the map and as the starting point of a timed Daily Commission. No story or lore is tied to it.
### STANDARD +
Its only recorded content is the Daily Commission "Aim Upstream", in which the Traveler must reach a marked destination within 120 seconds. The subarea appeared with Version 4.0, together with the rest of the Court of Fontaine Region.

## geography
### STANDARD
The subarea lies within the Court of Fontaine Region, the area around the city of the Court of Fontaine. The wiki lists no features, enemies, domains or landmarks for it, and there is no official description text. Describe scenes there only in general Fontaine terms and do not name specific landmarks.
### LARGE +
The name is French (chemin de l'espoir, "path of hope"), and the English localisation renders it that way. Which route, bank or street it refers to is not stated.

## quests
### STANDARD
"Aim Upstream" is a repeatable Daily Commission starting here: a short timed run to a target location, with rewards scaled to Adventure Rank. Only the Traveler is involved and it has no story significance.
### ULTRA +
The wiki's commission page shows two images of the start location but lists no dialogue, NPCs or lore. The time limit is the only mechanical detail: 120 seconds to reach the target. Because nothing else is known, any depiction of the subarea in a scene should stay vague and avoid inventing a destination or a hope-related legend.

## status

As of 7.1 it is an ordinary, accessible part of Fontaine's capital region with no recorded events.
