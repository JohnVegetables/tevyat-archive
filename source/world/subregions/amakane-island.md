## summary
### LITE
Amakane Island is a small festival island just off the western coast of Byakko Plain on Narukami Island, Inazuma. Its name means roughly "Sweet Gold Island". In the in-game description, people sit among its blooming sakura in summer to watch fireworks in the distance.
### STANDARD +
It is a gentle, recreational spot rather than a settlement: a few vendors and Yashiro Commission representatives are stationed there. Canon says very little else about it, so treat it as a scenic viewing place.

## geography
### STANDARD
The island lies a short way off the west coast of Byakko Plain. From its shore one can look across the water at Inazuma City, whose fireworks are visible from here.
### LARGE +
Its exploration features are Electro in character: a Thunder Barrier shields the Waverider Waypoint, which is opened by drawing an Electrogranum out of a nearby Thunder Sakura Bough. An Electro Seelie also lives there. These are gameplay mechanics, not lore.

## people
### STANDARD
Four open-world NPCs are placed on the island: Hirohiko and Takami, both Yashiro Commission representatives; Murakami, a street food vendor; and Sanden, a vendor. The wiki gives no further background for them.

## culture
### STANDARD
The in-game description, credited to Cissie in the Version 2.0 Special Program, pictures a summer scene: people seated beneath blossoming sakura, watching fireworks far off. The island is called a festival island, which fits the food vendors and the Yashiro Commission presence.
### ULTRA +
The description's phrasing, a place where people sit and watch fireworks far away, implies the show happens elsewhere (the wiki's screenshot shows Inazuma City's fireworks from here) rather than on the island itself. Treat any named festival or organiser as unestablished.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
A scene here is warm and lightly festive: sakura, stalls, the smell of street food, and fireworks blooming over the water towards the city. Nothing threatening is established for the island.

## travel
### LARGE
The island is reached across the water from Byakko Plain. A Waverider Waypoint is on the island, accessible once the Thunder Barrier has been passed.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1, Amakane Island is unchanged: a quiet festival spot in Inazuma.
