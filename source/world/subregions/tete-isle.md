## summary
### LITE
Tete Isle is a subarea of Easybreeze Holiday Resort, the summer resort island off Natlan's far west. It is known for a lively atmosphere and its specialty drink, Aphimead, and becomes accessible at step 5 of the story quest The World Is Your Canvas! (Sunspray Summer Resort, Part II).
### STANDARD +
Three points of interest are listed: the Dance-Off Stage, and two competing Aphimead bars, Me & Dew and The Dewdrop.

## places
### STANDARD
The Dance-Off Stage is an elaborate performance stage with dynamic music mixing, lighting and stage effects; it resembles a giant eye with the DJ booth as its pupil. Me & Dew is owned by Tlapalli (from step 22 of The World Is Your Canvas!); The Dewdrop is its rival. Both specialise in Aphimead.
### LARGE +
A lone islet to the north holds a Precious Chest guarded by Flowing Blade pirates and a Koholasaurus; defeating them brings in reinforcements led by Radius Bone Jack and her crew, who sing as they arrive.

## people
### LARGE
The wiki lists many residents and visitors, including Ca'asho, Chira, Elmi, Feiming, Ho'oliu, Icahua, Kapak, Malinche, Oquichi, Papar, Pumayna, Q'ellu, Q'utuj, Taye, Tecuhtli, Tlapalli, Uhane, Vara and Wayner. Detail about each belongs in their own entries.

## history
### LARGE
Two letters found on the isle show a pirate plot. One, signed Black Reef Rekis and addressed to Captain Radius, says powerful figures want a Monetoo captured from Tenochtzitoc and offers relief on a spine-ownership dispute and personal debts, adding that he had sent Icahua ahead to the resort as insurance. The other, from the crewman Tecuhtli, reports that Icahua took one of his eyes, that he poisoned Icahua, that he went to the Asha's home and removed Rekis's men who were caging Asha, and that the two bars sell good Aphimead and juice. Both letters carry sung, scribbled lines from the captain's side.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Music, drink and crowds: a holiday island's nightlife hub rather than a place of danger, with a pirate subplot at its edges.

## quests
### ULTRA
World quests listed here are Dreamy Paititi and Strange Encounter of the Tete Isle Kind. They are part of the permanent resort content.

## status

As of 7.1 it is a permanent, explorable part of Easybreeze Holiday Resort, added in 5.8.
