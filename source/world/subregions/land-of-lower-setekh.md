## summary
### LITE
The Land of Lower Setekh is the part of Sumeru's Great Red Sand where most people of the desert live. Its centre is Aaru Village, the largest human settlement in the desert, home to the followers and descendants of King Deshret and to scholars exiled from the Akademiya. It also holds Khaj-Nisut, King Deshret's throne-room temple, Abdju Pit, the abandoned hospital Dar al-Shifa and the hidden base of the real Temple of Silence.
### STANDARD +
Setekh echoes the Egyptian god Set, associated with deserts and storms. The area's official viewpoints are titled The Barren Land Where Sand Dances and Verdant Fields' Vantage Point.

## geography
### STANDARD
Dunes and oases south of the Wall of Samiel and Caravan Ribat, with Aaru Village at its heart. Abdju Pit is a deep sinkhole at the sands' edge, Khaj-Nisut lies in ruins hidden under the sand, and Thamud Oasis is where the city of Saleh once stood. The Temple of Silence's real base is an underground temple near Thamud Oasis.
### LARGE +
Aaru Village's water comes from an ancient stepwell below the village; the White Lift, an elevator built by a former Kshahrewar Village Keeper, lowers to the surface of the water; before it, one could reasonably draw about a bucketful at a time.

## places
### STANDARD
Aaru Village, around an old fortress. Khaj-Nisut, once called the Eye of the Sands, the King's throne-room temple. Abdju Pit. Dar al-Shifa, a ruined hospital for Eleazar patients. The Temple of Silence, an underground temple. Thamud Oasis.
### LARGE +
Official text for Khaj-Nisut says this is no place for ordinary mortals: only those who have been acknowledged may set foot there and gaze on the temple of King Deshret. It was unnamed on the map as the Eye of the Sands until The Secret of Al-Ahmar, and its throne-room ruins appear only after the three trials of Dreams Beneath the Searing Sand.
### ULTRA +
The fortress inside Aaru Village dates to the time of Gurabad and was built under a "paranoid prince" to guard the border between the desert and rainforest civilisations; a wiki item note identifies it as the Border Fort that appears as a chess piece in Safhe Shatranj, and it is the Village Chief's House today.

## people
### STANDARD
Aaru Village's people are followers and descendants of King Deshret together with exiled Akademiya scholars. Candace, whose companion story supplies one account of the village's origins, is associated with it; the Eremites, the Temple of Silence's members (including Cyno and, as its appointed leader, Sethos) and Kshahrewar Village Keepers all appear here.
### LARGE +
Layla's blue hair and pointed ears mark a lineage descended from Tulaytullah's refugees. The Eremite tribes include Thutmose, whose secret base is in the neighbouring Land of Upper Setekh.

## governance
### STANDARD
Aaru's founders swore off the rule of kings, and the old fortress now serves as the Village Chief's House. The Temple of Silence, which still exists as an independent body here, has begun re-establishing relations with the Akademiya after the events of Oathkeeper.

## culture
### STANDARD
The founders named the village Aaru after the "ancient dream of the gods" and preserve the embers of Deshret's civilisation while awaiting the return of Al-Ahmar.

## history
### STANDARD
After King Deshret's death his civilisation broke into warring city-states, and refugees eventually left the desert to build Aaru. The founders were led by a blind poet of Tulaytullah. Candace's account is that the earliest inhabitants were survivors of the city of Akhtamun.
### LARGE +
The Temple of Silence was founded by Hermanubis after the king's death and settled at Tulaytullah. Its members were betrayed by the city's elite and shunned by rainforest people, and about 400 years ago they set up the large underground temple near Thamud Oasis. In the Akademiya, the Temple later became a vestigial office responsible for custody and disposal of sensitive information.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Sandstorms, ancient guardian constructs in the pit and temple, Eremite groups, the Fatui, and monsters such as spinocrocodiles and red vultures in the oases.

## travel
### STANDARD
The route from the rainforest passes Caravan Ribat and the Wall of Samiel, then crosses the dunes to Aaru Village. Aaru Village is the base for expeditions elsewhere in the Great Red Sand.

## quests
### STANDARD
Golden Slumber (3.1): Lost in the Sands, An Introduction to Indoor Archaeology, The Secret of Al-Ahmar and Dreams Beneath the Searing Sand. Also in this area: Cry From the Eleazar Hospital, Desert Bloom, Dual Evidence, Hidden Mercenaries, Sage of Sages, The Exile (Sprouting and Blooming), A Gifted Rose series, Warehousing Business and others (titles only).

## atmosphere
### LARGE
A refugee settlement of cautious, close-knit people around a fortress and a stepwell, ringed by red dunes.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 Aaru Village and the surrounding ruins are open, and the Temple of Silence is again cooperating with the Akademiya.
### STANDARD +
Nothing from 6.x or 7.0-7.1 changes the area.
