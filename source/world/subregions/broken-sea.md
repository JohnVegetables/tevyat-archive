## summary
### LITE
The Broken Sea is an area of Simulanka, the storybook world of the limited-time Summertide Scales and Tales event (4.8). It is unlocked during the quest Until the End of the World (Page III). People are stranded around it, and a boat there is one way to leave the book's world.
### STANDARD +
Visions do not work in Simulanka. Toy blocks and toy bombs shape its puzzles.

## geography
### STANDARD
An area of sea and shore made of toy fixtures: Oracle Pillars that link in walkable lines, large Soaring Toys that launch the player, small Toy Blocks that can be assembled, Toy Bombs that destroy toytanium blocks, Toy Flowers that make wind currents when struck, and Toy Matches that burn a red toy block. During Page III the way to it on foot is blocked by an area boundary.
### LARGE +
It is a separate subarea from the Forest of Blessings and Constellation Metropole; in Page III the party is steered to board the Maritime Express instead of crossing to it on foot.

## people
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
People are stranded around the Broken Sea. Kirara notes they are clearly stranded and that the stories never said the dragon had attacked their village; Nilou suggests they may be toytanium miners or guards who came for the dragon's head and could not leave.
### LARGE +
After the dragon becomes Mini Durin, the Wanderer tells him to atone by filling the footprints he left and by helping the people around the Broken Sea get home. Alice tells the group they can leave Simulanka by taking the boat at the Broken Sea or touching the giant bookmark at the Cliff of Prophecy and focusing on where they want to go.
### ULTRA +
Alice leaves it open why the Goddess of Fate never told her summoned visitors about the boat and the bookmark; she guesses it was so they would stay engrossed in the story. Paimon, Kirara and the others choose to stay and explore for a while.

## status
### LITE
Not accessible in the game as of 7.1; the event ended on 2024-08-28.
