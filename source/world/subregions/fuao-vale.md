## summary
### LITE
Fuao Vale is a surface subarea of the Chasm in Liyue. It contains the Lost Valley, a domain on ancient ritual grounds that Ruin Machines have taken over, and a viewpoint whose caption says the site was the battlefield where Rex Lapis fought Azhdaha.
### STANDARD +
A sign here warns visitors to treat a relic of those old battles, left by Rex Lapis, with care. The valley's Chinese name is usually glossed as Valley of the Prostrate Ao, the Ao being a legendary turtle.

## geography
### STANDARD
Features: the Lost Valley domain entrance, Unique Rocks and Unique Rock Pillars, a Pressure Plate and a Mountainator blasting cannon. Cor Lapis, Qingxin and Violetgrass grow here.
### LARGE +
The domain is revealed by placing Geo constructs between the golden Unique Rock and a rock pillar and striking the pillar so a resonant quake runs through the minerals. In the game this is a puzzle, not story.

## history
### STANDARD
The Lost Valley's description says the ritual grounds were buried beneath the earth during a cataclysm that tore heaven and earth apart, raised again when an alien object fell, and finally uncovered by a destructive battle that came of a betrayal. Those who once used the place are gone.
### LARGE +
The linked wiki lore connects these events to the War of Funerary Flame, the Celestial Nail that struck the Chasm and Azhdaha's later clash with Morax. A world-quest viewpoint calls the vale the site of the deadly battle between Azhdaha and Rex Lapis, written by a young adventurer who says they are awed by it. Treat that as an in-world account, not a map of the whole story.
### ULTRA +
Chasm lore also holds that Azhdaha suffered when miners disturbed the ley lines, and that Morax, the adepti Mountain Shaper and Moon Carver and a third adeptus fought down to the Chasm's depths, creating the Dragonfall. Whether that fight began in Fuao Vale is not stated.

## quests
### STANDARD
The viewpoint "The Ancient Battlefield of Fuao Vale" appears once the world quest Surreptitious Seven-Star Seal Sundering reaches about step 8.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Enemies near the valley include Geo Samachurls, Anemo Slimes and Treasure Hoarder Scouts. Inside the Lost Valley domain, Ruin Machines have overrun the ritual site.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
A sunken battlefield valley of golden rock, silent ritual ground and warning notices, with a sense of awe at old divine violence. This is a suggested scene mood drawn from the viewpoint's wording.

## status
### STANDARD
Unchanged as of 7.1.

### STANDARD
The Lost Valley is a Domain of Blessing in Fuao Vale that gives artifacts when cleared. In-world it is described as ancient ritual grounds buried in a cataclysm, lifted by the descent of an alien object, uncovered by the battle after a betrayal and now held by Ruin Machines from outside.
