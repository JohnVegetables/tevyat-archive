## summary
### LITE
Wing of Keres is the name for the barrier islands on the western side of Wavechaser Plain in Nod-Krai. They are decorated with Verdant Crests and other artifacts of the Frostmoon Scions. A buried time capsule written by Hyperborean engineers is the main thing to find there.
### STANDARD +
The islands are a place for exploration: free kuuhenki, ride Moonlanes and solve Abyss and kuuvahki puzzles. The wiki suggests the name alludes to the keres of Greek myth.
### LARGE +
Their one piece of story is the Hyperborean time capsule letter, a first-hand glimpse of Hyperborea's outlook during the Elysium Project.

## geography
### STANDARD
A string of barrier islands off the western shore of Wavechaser Plain, within Pohjola. Winter Icelea grows here, and the islands are connected by Moonlanes, with kuuhenki in the north, west and south.
### LARGE +
Puzzles include the Evil Eye of Blightseep (west) and The Solaris Core and Nycalyx (south). Hilichurls and a Blazing Axe Mitachurl hold the northern kuuhenki.

## history
### STANDARD
No local history of the islands themselves is given in the pages read. The Verdant Crests and Frostmoon Scion artifacts imply the Scions' presence, and a letter buried in a time capsule shows that Hyperborean people also left something here.
### LARGE +
In the letter, written to a distant successor, the author says it is the tenth year since Hyperborea began the 'Elysium Project'; that all the clans have united to shape the world into an Elysium for humankind alone; that they follow Dawn's Navigator (Koitar) and the Exalted Saint (Anacharsis); and that they have mastered the Tekhneikos of tailoring Seeds of Life, so adjusted new humans wield elemental forces at will. The author hopes the successors command the elements freely, no longer need the Firmament's gifts, and have broken through the limits of the firmament.
### ULTRA +
The letter also mentions a tower raised toward the heavens with Mekhaneikos, great arrows that guard the civilisation, and the writers' sense that they are about to knock on the gates of the Lord of the Heavenly City. It disparages the earlier Unified Civilization as superstitious and ends with envy of the reader's era.

## quests
### STANDARD
A Distant Dream of Elysium (unofficial name, hidden exploration objective, 6.3): free three kuuhenki in any order, then ride the Moonlane, which completes the objective (chests and the achievement The Mound in the Moonlight). Afterwards the mound of dirt near the chests can be examined to read the Letter Buried in the Time Capsule.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Evil Eyes of Blightseep and a small group of hilichurls, including a Blazing Axe Mitachurl, in the north.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Offshore islets by the sea, dotted with Frostmoon Scion crests and white kuuhenki gliding along the Moonlanes.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the islands are an unpopulated exploration site; the time capsule has been unearthed.
### STANDARD +
Nothing in the pages read changes their state through 7.1.

## entry: Hyperborean Time Capsule
@keys: Hyperborean time capsule, Letter Buried in the Time Capsule, Elysium Project
@keys2: Hyperborea, Wing of Keres
@version: 6.3
### STANDARD
A letter buried at Wing of Keres by a Hyperborean author, addressed to a successor in a far brighter era. Written in the tenth year of the Elysium Project, it describes a united civilisation led by Dawn's Navigator and the Exalted Saint, advanced Tekhneikos and Mekhaneikos, and hopes of reaching the true cosmos.
