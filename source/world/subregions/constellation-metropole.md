## summary
### LITE
Constellation Metropole is a royal city of toy figures in Simulanka, the storybook world of the limited-time Summertide Scales and Tales event (4.8). In the story Navia is its king, and the stars are missing from its sky. It has a Maritime Express station, a gear-driven lift and a statue of the Goddess of Prophecy.
### STANDARD +
Visions do not work in Simulanka. The Metropole is unlocked after Step 7 of the quest They Once Walked the Starry Sky (Page II).

## geography
### STANDARD
A toy capital with a city highest point, a rail station for the Maritime Express, a Gear Sky Ladder (a lift raised by attacking a key mechanism), Oracle Pillars that connect in lines the player can walk, and "Superglue" that stops toy people and slows the player. A device called "Gift of the Goddess of Prophecy" lets the player rewind and replay time for certain puzzles and quests.
### LARGE +
The city can be crossed by Oracle Pillar lines and lifts; Page II also sends the player up to the city's highest point to turn a gear and catch a suspicious person.

## people
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Navia reigns in the story; Bizerte and Montagne address her as Her Majesty and stay behind to look after the city when she sets off to slay the dragon with the Traveler, Nilou and the Wanderer. Wheel drives the Maritime Express. Kirara, who was in the Metropole, hears they have gone and follows.
### LARGE +
Alice, Barbeloth and Andersdotter, the three Hexenzirkel members who made Simulanka, are the Goddesses of Creation, Prophecy and Fate to its people. M's journal says Barbeloth wanted a statue of herself in the capital; the Goddess of Fate's statue is a different one.

## history
@version: 4.8
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Page II (They Once Walked the Starry Sky) has the party take the Maritime Express to the Metropole, save a toy man, follow Navia, capture a suspicious person from the city's highest point, shape Oracle Pillars and seek answers from the Goddess of Prophecy about the scattered stars (three to find).
### LARGE +
Page III (Until the End of the World) starts from the Metropole station: the party rides the express to the end of the world to face Simulanka's evil dragon. They learn from its memories that the dragon, named Durin by its creator "M", took the stars because it missed its mother. After the party blesses it, it takes the small form Alice calls Mini Durin; stars then shine, which Navia guesses is a gift from the three Goddesses.
### ULTRA +
Alice explains why everyone was in Simulanka: when its Durin made his wish to the Goddess of Fate, the Traveler's party, Navia (who had been reading a fairy tale to children in the Fleuve Cendre), Nilou, Kirara and the Wanderer were each holding a copy of M's fairy tale, and the Goddess of Fate chose them to save the world. Alice says the Goddess of Fate probably did not tell the others how to leave because she wanted them absorbed in the story.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Hilichurls, including a Blazing Axe Mitachurl, seize a transfer platform on the way to the end of the world in Page III; in the city the hazards are mechanisms such as superglue.

## quests
@version: 4.8
### STANDARD
The Metropole is the main setting of Page II and the starting point of Page III. Event minigames played here include the Metropole Trials, in which defeating opponents fills a Valor bar.

## status
### LITE
Not accessible in the game as of 7.1; the event ended on 2024-08-28.
