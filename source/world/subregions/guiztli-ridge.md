## summary
### LITE
Guiztli Ridge is a small island of the Easybreeze Holiday Resort, topped by a single rocky ridge. Beneath it lies Villa Guiztli, an underground town that offers a role-play adventure against a make-believe villain.
### STANDARD +
Its residents include a fruit farmer, a guard and a character named Achu.

## geography
### STANDARD
A rock ridge on its own island. Visitors can see it from the Huha Hill lodgings. The caverns beneath hold Villa Guiztli, and Guiztli Path connects the villa to Castle Joquiratto.
### LARGE +
In the story quest the Traveler rides a cruise ship through the cavern.

## people
### STANDARD
"Achupalla", the Fruit Farmer, "Willapi", the Guard, and Achu.
### LARGE +
In To Guiztli Ridge! the Traveler works with Bennett, Chasca, Mualani and the Asha form of Macani, and meets characters named "El Capoctus Joquiratto" and "Nanasian".

## culture
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
Part of the 5.8 Sunspray Summer Resort story, in which a robbery in Villa Guiztli leads to a chase and a hunt for the culprits through the cavern, ending with the Traveler confronting the Nochtli ruffians.

## history
@version: 5.8
### STANDARD
Opened with the resort in 5.8.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
A rocky islet with a play-world below: quirky alpacas, capybaras and costumed villains.

## travel
### STANDARD
The cavern below is entered from the ridge, and the quest has the Traveler ride a cruise ship through it.

## quests
@version: 5.8
### STANDARD
To Guiztli Ridge! (part 2.2 of The World Is Your Canvas!).
### ULTRA +
Steps in the quest include following the sheriff to the bar, defeating the bandits, helping the sheriff activate a mechanism, then riding the cruise ship in pursuit of the Nochtli ruffians, finding a way to lift a barrier and investigating the cavern. The enemy labels are jokes, naming the culprits as Butch Churldy and Slushdance Kid.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the ridge remains open as part of the resort.
