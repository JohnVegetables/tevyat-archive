## summary
### LITE
Autake Plains is a subarea of Tsurumi Island in Inazuma, introduced in Version 2.2. It was blanketed in a fog that could not be removed, and is scattered with ruins and Electro hazards.
### STANDARD +
It features in the Through the Mists world quest series: it is one of three places where a perch must be given an offering during A Particularly Particular Author, and the plains are visited again in the final quest, The Sun-Wheel and Mt. Kanna.

## geography
### STANDARD
Autake Plains lies on Tsurumi Island, a stormy, mist-bound island off Inazuma. Its terrain is open plains broken by ruins, with Lightning Strike Probes, Electro Cubes, Ruins Torches, Phase Gates, Mysterious Carvings and Stormstones, plus Thunder Barriers and Thunder Sakura Boughs. Fluorescent Fungus is its local specialty.
### LARGE +
Fog covered most of the area even after A Particularly Particular Author; staying inside without a Stormstone nearby sent the player back to the nearest waypoint outside. This is a gameplay mechanic as well as an atmospheric detail.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Hilichurls (including Electro and Hydro samachurls and shooters), slimes and Whopperflowers roam the plains. Elite threats include Electro and Pyro Abyss Mages, Crackling Axe Mitachurls, Thundercraven and Rockfond Rifthounds and ruin machines (Ruin Cruisers and Destroyers).

## quests
### STANDARD
In A Particularly Particular Author, the Traveler follows a trail of mist-bound offerings and must make an offering at three perches: Chirai Shrine, Shirikoro Peak and Autake Plains. In The Sun-Wheel and Mt. Kanna, the fourth and last Through the Mists quest, the Traveler fights Thundercraven Rifthounds here before moving on to Mt. Kanna.
### LARGE +
Completing The Sun-Wheel and Mt. Kanna removes the fog from the area for good. Both quests date from Version 2.2.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Before the fog lifted: red-tinted storm air, rumbling thunder, fog swallowing sightlines, ruined stones and sparking machines. Afterward the plains are open and quiet, though still ruin-haunted.
### ULTRA +
The perch there is one of three in A Particularly Particular Author; the Traveler guides feathers through the plains using Elemental Sight to find the way, and monsters spawn as they approach. The Thunder Manifestation of the series is fought at Mt. Kanna, not here. The wiki's Through the Mists quest list does not describe ruin lore for the plains.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1, Autake Plains is clear of fog and unchanged since 2.2.
