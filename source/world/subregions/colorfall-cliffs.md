## summary
### LITE
Colorfall Cliffs are the sunny, colour-streaked fields of the Easybreeze Holiday Resort. Above them hangs the resort's star attraction, the Airborne Asha, a floating structure shaped like an Asha holding a torch, which visitors ride a hot air balloon up to and paint.
### STANDARD +
The area has an Alpaca Park run by a manager named Aloha, and an attendant named Tikraq through whom the Airborne Asha can be revisited.

## geography
### STANDARD
Wide green fields marked with vivid swirls of paint. Colorfall Cave lies just south, and Huha Hill's lodgings overlook the cliffs, along with Guiztli Ridge and Wavey Bay.
### LARGE +
The Airborne Asha floats in the sky above and is reached by balloon.

## places
### STANDARD
The Airborne Asha, a giant floating structure resembling an Asha holding a torch, and the Alpaca Park.
### LARGE +
The Airborne Asha is first reached during the story quest To Colorfall Cliffs! and can then be revisited by talking to Tikraq.

## people
### STANDARD
Aloha, the Alpaca Park manager, and Tikraq.
### LARGE +
In To Colorfall Cliffs!, the Traveler is joined by Paimon, Mualani, Chasca and Bennett, and holds a spray-painting contest with Bennett at the top of the Airborne Asha.

## culture
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
The quest To Colorfall Cliffs! belongs to the 5.8 Sunspray Summer Resort event story, which asks the Traveler to colour the fields with Asha-powered paint drums.
### LARGE +
Starblooms on Huha Hill's terraces are reminders of the cliffs; the in-game text suggests the flowers are a way of saying thank you.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Bright colour, open sky and holiday cheer, with paint everywhere and tourists floating up to the sky attraction.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Not hazardous; some Whopperflowers turn up during the paint-drum task in the quest.

## travel
### STANDARD
Reached on foot from Easybreeze Market and Huha Hill. The Airborne Asha is reached by hot air balloon.

## quests
@version: 5.8
### STANDARD
To Colorfall Cliffs! (part 2.1 of The World Is Your Canvas!) takes place here and at the Airborne Asha.
### ULTRA +
In the quest the Traveler first colours the Foamflower Fields while indwelling an Asha, then takes the balloon to the Airborne Asha, has a spray-painting contest with Bennett that ends in a final showdown at the top, and finishes at a scenic spot the staff recommend.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 Colorfall Cliffs are open as a resort attraction.
