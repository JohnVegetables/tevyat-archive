## summary
### LITE
The Teeming Mire is the sunken, tree-choked heart of Okurov, the hidden town in Snezhnaya's Flamefeather Valley. Below the assembly hall a giant tree grows among blue algae: the home of the so-called Perfect Tree, an experiment by the bio-alchemist Koshchei, who posed as Doctor Moreau. It is guarded by the firebird Zhara, and it is where the child Nastya chose to be transformed.
### STANDARD +
The quest chain that ends here, In the Dwelling of Life, is a world-quest series. It is a place of false comfort: inhabitants and algae murmur about happiness and eternity, while the experiment strips away meaning, speech and feeling.

## geography
### STANDARD
A swampy, overgrown area reached through the assembly hall's lift and the secret passage behind it. A huge tree stands at the centre with a giant egg-like object that proved to be a part of the Eye of Graeae's body. Blue algae lines the walls and ground and gives off a warm glow. Compared with the frozen tundra outside, the air around the town is unnaturally warm, which Nastya explains is for the 'pretty leaves' to grow.
### LARGE +
Flamefeather Valley is named for tall trees whose leaves look like flaming feathers and for bird-shaped monsters; stories hold it was once a human dwelling and is now a place from which many never return.

## places
### STANDARD
The assembly hall's Hall of Prayers leads on to the lift and the Perfect Tree's home. Nearby stands Nastya's house, now occupied by a young tree segment called Shar. Blue algae terminals along the walls speak, once Chorus of Solace is complete, in six distinct voices.
### LARGE +
Each algae voice repeats a single wish or fear: someone who wanted to catch frogs and finds none in eternity; an old person who says youth has come back as an inability to die; an infant weeping; a person asking where to go in a courtyard of happiness; one who feels growing up means doing all sorts of things; and a mother who thinks her daughter has come back into her arms.

## people
### STANDARD
Nastya, a young girl and the quest's guide, is transformed here. Koshchei speaks through her. Zhara guards the place. Furiosa, who opposed the plan, was beaten at the hall's door by the Traveler. Aksinya, the self-styled Royal Jester of the late Belyi Tsar, appears outside the tree afterward.
### LARGE +
Okurov's other people are Leshy such as Aunt LePuy and No-Nose Ove. Nastya's mother is something she remembers lying ill in bed and whom she hopes to meet again within the Perfect Tree.

## history
### STANDARD
Koshchei, who posed as Doctor Moreau and seemed to cure the villagers' ailments, built the grand ritual that turns lives into the Perfect Tree, a rooted form that cannot move or speak, in pursuit of eternity. In the final quests the Traveler and Eye of Graeae fight through Zhara to reach the tree, but Nastya refuses to turn back. The Eye of Graeae recovers the ritual's power into the egg. The thing that was Nastya says it had once expected Aksinya to be the one to appear here instead.
### LARGE +
Koshchei's notes in the sealed room argue that sorrow and individual love must be cast aside for eternal life, while acknowledging love may be the only thing that does not fade. The thing speaking through Nastya wonders what it wanted, finds that wanting has lost its meaning, and says it will return the Eye of Graeae's power, though everything will still end in a way no one wishes to see.
### ULTRA +
Nastya's route into the Mire follows a three-step offering at the final door: casting off soul (sulfur), will (silver) and body, for which she breaks off one of her own twigs as the salt standing in for the body of the past. The notes also say the Perfect Tree gains great strength at the cost of language, and that the final ceremony aims to turn everyone into embryos of an emotionless new existence.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Warm, humid, softly lit by glowing blue algae; calm, almost lullaby-like voices that are quietly horrifying. A scene should lean on contrast between cosy warmth and loss of self.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Zhara, a huge firebird made by bio-alchemy, guards the area and attacks all outsiders as 'Badbugs'. Mindless Leshy wander the town.

## quests
### STANDARD
In the Dwelling of Life: A Sprout Without a Gardener; Transplanted From 'Yesterday' to 'Tomorrow'; Down in the Dumps; Back on One's Feet; Forbidding Doors of Melancholy; To Rest in a Forgotten Field; Chorus of Solace (the last, set in the Mire).
### LARGE +
Completing Chorus of Solace unlocks the achievement Gather for Everlasting Happiness. It also runs concurrently with On One Side a Palace, On the Other a Tomb and Hesperides of Love and Hate, which changes how Aksinya is introduced.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the Mire holds the Perfect Tree with Nastya now blue algae, and the ritual's power has been returned to the Eye of Graeae.
### STANDARD +
Zhara is defeated and Aksinya says its body will break down. The experiment's wider consequences are not shown in 7.1.

## entry: Nastya and the Perfect Tree
@keys: Nastya, Perfect Tree, Doctor Moreau, Doctor Birdie
@keys2: Teeming Mire, Okurov, Koshchei
@version: 7.0
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
Nastya is a young quest NPC from Okurov who believes Doctor Moreau cured her mother and that becoming a Perfect Tree will let her be with her mother again. In Chorus of Solace she is taken over by Koshchei who confirms 'Koshchei or Doctor Moreau' are one and the same, then gives her time to say farewell. Despite Paimon's pleas she chooses to complete the ritual, saying she wants to see her mother's loving eyes once more, and she disintegrates into blue algae. Eye of Graeae says that the Perfect Tree is a form that cannot move, speak or know joy or love, and that already-changed life cannot be reversed even though the ritual can be stopped.

## entry: Zhara and Aksinya at the Tree
@keys: Zhara, Aksinya, Royal Jester
@keys2: Teeming Mire, Eye of Graeae, Perfect Tree
@version: 7.0
### LARGE
Zhara is the firebird that guards Okurov's heart and the Perfect Tree. After it is slain, Aksinya, who calls herself the late Belyi Tsar's Royal Jester and the builder of the Eye of Graeae's mechanism, appears and says she has adjusted Zhara's body so it will break down. She says she was meant to claim the Eye of Graeae, and that she and the Traveler share the goal of helping the will in the Eye find its body and reclaim its power.
