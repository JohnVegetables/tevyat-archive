## summary
### LITE
The Glowing Narrows is a subarea of the Chasm's Underground Mines in Liyue, a lightless cavern passage lit by fluorescent growths and dominated by a giant talking mushroom, Xamaran. It lies deep in the mines, beyond the main tunnel, in country where black "foul energy" still seeps from cracks and the Traveler's Lumenstone Adjuvant is the main tool for clearing it.
### STANDARD +
The viewpoint text, written as if by the miner-engineer Zhiqiong, calls it the Great Mushroom's Place and records the strange calling voice she heard there. It is a quiet, eerie stretch of the mines where the only resident with anything to say is a mushroom.

## geography
### STANDARD
A cavern passage in the Underground Mines, a separate map layer beneath the Chasm's surface. The giant mushroom sits in the southern part of the narrows, and a stream runs through the area; the source of the black foul energy lies upstream along the water, by a fissure. Luminous Seelie and fluorescent growths light the dark.
### LARGE +
The Underground Mines are entered from the Chasm's Maw on the surface and are unlocked at Adventure Rank 28 after Chapter I, Act III of the Archon Quest and the world quest "Surreptitious Seven-Star Seal Sundering". The Glowing Narrows becomes reachable only after "Perils in the Dark", the fifth part of "The Chasm Delvers". The area's listed features are Luminous Seelie, Cryo Whopperflowers, Floating Hydro Fungus and Large Hydro Slimes, with Geovishap and Shadowy Husk (Defender and Line Breaker) as elite enemies.

## people
### STANDARD
Xamaran is the only named inhabitant: a giant glowing mushroom that speaks by releasing spores into the air, so that Paimon and the Traveler hear a voice with no mouth. It addresses people as "Two-Legs" and calls itself one of the "legless", ranking creatures by their number of legs, and it has small mushroom kin around the cave that it can no longer shelter. Paimon nicknamed it "Sheeshroom".
### LARGE +
Xamaran says it has dwelt here many years and found the cavern a cosy home, but that foul energy has been rising lately and has drained its strength. It smells the heat of golden sand and the damp of a rainforest in the Traveler and calls that its hometown, and Paimon links this to Sumeru; the Sumeru scholar Khedive is at the exploration camp. The wiki records that Tighnari remarked it took Xamaran a long time to reach its present form.
### ULTRA +
Xamaran is more guarded about its ancestry. In the Natlan quest series Mushroom Realm Mystery (Tribal Chronicles, Act III "Field Fungal Traces", 5.5) the Traveler learns that Xamaran is an offshoot of Bifrons, the Sage-Lord of Fungi. Xamaran calls Bifrons "my foolish father", says they have had no business with each other for many years, and that it has nearly forgotten the name.

## history
### STANDARD
The cavern belongs to the Chasm's mines, which Liyue worked until a run of accidents and then a black ooze led the Qixing to seal them. Nothing in canon dates Xamaran's arrival or says who first named the narrows.
### LARGE +
The foul energy Xamaran fights is the same Abyss-tainted dark mud (Oozing Concretions) found throughout the mines. The Chasm Delvers summary traces its source deeper still to a corrupted Skyfrost Nail-type meteorite that the Traveler purifies, after which the ooze recedes; the narrows are part of that wider story but Xamaran's quest happens alongside it rather than as part of the Archon-level plot.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Dark and humid, lit by drifting spores and glowing caps. Sound carries oddly: the mushroom's voice seems to arrive from the air. Mood is more strange than threatening, until the black mud appears.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Geovishap, Shadowy Husks, Cryo Whopperflowers, Hydro slimes and floating Hydro fungi. The dark mud and Oozing Concretions are Abyssal corruption and are cleared with the Lumenstone Adjuvant. The viewpoint text itself jokes that the strange voice could be a hallucination from poisonous gas.

## quests
### STANDARD
"Dimming Mushroom's Call for Help" (world quest, 2.6), begun by approaching the giant mushroom after "Perils in the Dark". The Traveler lends the Adjuvant's power to Xamaran, clears three Oozing Concretions, revives five of its kin, and uses Xamaran's gift, the Blessings of Wisdom, on a fissure upstream; a mushroom grows from the fissure and blocks it. Xamaran then falls asleep. The quest also counts toward a hidden exploration objective.
### LARGE +
Xamaran's closing words wish that no regret lingers in the night bygone and all shadows fade before dawn; afterwards it only murmurs a loop of three sayings about ignorance, knowledge and wandering.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the narrows are open and quiet; Xamaran sleeps and answers only with fragments of its aphorisms.
### STANDARD +
Nothing in 6.x or 7.0-7.1 content changes the area. The only later canon tied to it is Xamaran's lineage, revealed in 5.5.

## entry: Xamaran
@keys: Xamaran, Sheeshroom
@version: 2.6
### STANDARD
Xamaran is the giant glowing mushroom of the Glowing Narrows. It speaks in an archaic, formal, third-person style ("Xamaran calls", "Xamaran begs of you") and divides living things by how many legs they use. It thanks the Traveler and Paimon as the wisest of the two-legged, lectures about hard work making up for lack of nature, and tells a joke about Paimon being eaten by mushrooms that it admits nobody found funny. Only the Traveler's help with the Lumenstone Adjuvant restores its light.
### LARGE +
Xamaran refuses to deal with the source of the foul energy itself, calling itself legless and unwilling to meddle in the matters of the Two-Legs, and says it has a greater duty to shelter its kin. After the quest it sleeps and repeats three sayings: ignorance may be a blessing and knowledge a calamity; only the graced with knowledge flourish; only the tested by wandering brave the wilderness.
### ULTRA +
In the 5.5 Natlan content, once the Traveler has done the Natlan quest series and asks about the Sage-Lord of Fungi, Xamaran calls it its progenitor, "abounding in might, yet more bereft of wisdom" than the four-legged, and "my foolish father". It adds that it can tell no more stories about it.
