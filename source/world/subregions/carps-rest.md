## summary
### LITE
Carp's Rest is a sealed, cave-like refuge on Mt. Laixin in Chenyu Vale, Liyue, where the adeptus Fujin, a golden carp who once took human form, has rested since the Archon War. It holds the Votive Rainjade, a ritual jade that the Traveler can feed with recovered adeptal energy (Spirit Carp) to strengthen the blessings of the vale's soil and water. Its entrance is closed by Fujin's own power.
### STANDARD +
Fujin insists it is not a true adeptal abode: she cannot create new adeptal realms the way the southern adepti can, and calls it at best a cave. It appeared in Version 4.4 with the Chenyu Vale region.

## geography
### STANDARD
A subarea of Mt. Laixin, the "sacred mountain" where the spirit veins of Chenyu Vale converge, so that whoever controls the mountain can control all the land of the vale. Carp's Rest is reached from the mountain's summit area near Chiwang Terrace; the gate has to be opened with adeptal energy before anyone can go down. Its one point of interest is the Votive Rainjade.
### LARGE +
The Votive Rainjade is a jade tied to an ancient rite in which the ancestors of Chenyu Vale threw jade into the waters, passing adeptal energy into the spirit veins to protect the homeland and secure good weather and harvests. The game's description says that rite has long been forgotten. Murals in caves elsewhere in the vale (one under the northwest waterfall of Mt. Lingmeng, another near Chizhang Wall) show the people of Chenyu Vale performing it.

## people
### STANDARD
Fujin lives here, in her carp form. Her voice answers visitors from below the entrance; she apologises for forgetting to unlock it and admits she is embarrassed to be seen only as a carp. She says the Traveler, Paimon and Lingyuan are the first visitors since the adeptus Herblord left. Lingyuan, her old friend, cannot enter on his own because he lacks her adeptal energy.
### LARGE +
Fujin treats the visit with warmth and a little shyness: she tries out the formal phrasing she imagines other adepti use, then laughs it off, and teases Lingyuan about embarrassing episodes from their shared past. She says she will stay and pray for the Traveler's journey rather than accept further effort on her behalf.

## history
@version: 4.4
### STANDARD
According to the story told in An Ancient Sacrifice of Sacred Brocade, Fujin hurled the Votive Rainjade into the waters from Mt. Lingmeng to save Chenyu Vale when its former mistress flooded the Bishui River during the Archon War. Afterwards she "sank into the sea," lost most of her strength, and was confined to Carp's Rest for thousands of years, able to recall the world only through the scent of tea offerings. In the present the Traveler helped her perform the Rainjade Rite; that left her reverted to a carp, which is why she is found here recovering.
### LARGE +
After the world quest The Cloud-Padded Path to the Chiwang Repose, in which Lingyuan asks the Traveler to open the gate, the Traveler can offer Spirit Carp (scattered fragments of adeptal energy) to the Votive Rainjade. Raising Rainjade's Benediction to its maximum makes two yellow spirit carps circle the jade and the core give off spirit smoke, and according to the wiki's Fujin page lets her regain her human form and be found around Qiaoying Village.
### ULTRA +
Background from the Chenyu Vale lore: Fujin, a golden carp worshipped as an adeptus, once served the unnamed mistress of Chenyu Vale alongside Herblord, a snake spirit, and was close to humans; she took human form, taught early settlers the Rainjade Rite and planted the vale's first tea tree in Herblord's territory. When the mistress sent a flood down the Bishui River in the Archon War, Fujin and Herblord turned against her; Herblord fought her and later went missing and was believed dead, while Lingyuan led the people to shelter. In the present, Lingyuan, longing for the old days, had tampered with the vale's spirit lines, and Fujin was shocked to learn her friend had harmed the land.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Quiet and closed off. A visitor is greeted by a voice rather than a figure, and the place is associated with tea scent, jade and long solitude. The area's viewpoint text is a fragment of an older saying about jade rising from a sunken mouth and fortune ebbing and flowing like the river.

## travel
### STANDARD
Unlocked by completing The Cloud-Padded Path to the Chiwang Repose, which becomes available one in-game day after An Ancient Sacrifice of Sacred Brocade (part of the Chenyu's Blessings of Sunken Jade series). Teleportation is to the Mt. Laixin area, then on foot to the gate.

## quests
### STANDARD
Chenyu's Blessings of Sunken Jade (Qiaoying of the Sacred Mountain, in which Fujin first draws the Traveler and Paimon into an adepti space; An Ancient Sacrifice of Sacred Brocade; The Cloud-Padded Path to the Chiwang Repose).

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 Carp's Rest is unchanged: Fujin recovers here, and the Votive Rainjade can be fed with Spirit Carp. No later story content was found that alters it.

## entry: The Votive Rainjade and Rainjade's Benediction
@keys: Votive Rainjade, Rainjade's Benediction, Spirit Carp, Rainjade Rite
@keys2: Carp's Rest, Fujin, Chenyu Vale
@version: 4.4
### STANDARD
The Votive Rainjade is the jade in Carp's Rest into which the Traveler offers Spirit Carp, fragments of adeptal energy, to raise the "Rainjade's Benediction" and earn rewards. In story terms it stands for the old rite of Chenyu Vale: pour adeptal energy into the spirit veins and the soil and water recover and the weather stays favourable. Fujin says balance has already returned to the water and soil, but more power in the jade would secure good weather and prosperous years ahead.
