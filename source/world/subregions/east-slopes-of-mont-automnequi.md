## summary
### LITE
The East Slopes of Mont Automnequi are the eastern side of Mont Automnequi, a mountain in Fontaine that spans two regions. This half belongs to the Morte Region; the western half is in the Belleau Region. It was added in Version 4.2.
### STANDARD +
The area is where the world quest "Cupid's Lover" begins, part of the Narcissus chain, "In the Wake of Narcissus".

## geography
### STANDARD
Mont Automnequi is described as a mountain spanning the Belleau and Morte Regions. The East Slopes are the Morte-side subarea, with the West Slopes as the Belleau-side counterpart. The name probably comes from the French words for autumn and equinox (the wiki's inference on the West Slopes page). The wiki gives no official description of the east side's terrain.

## places
### STANDARD
The Orthant of Souls is a set of mysterious ruins reached in "Cupid's Lover". A stone riddle slate marks it, and a seal must be found first. Inside, the Traveler descends to a lower floor by lift and works through Energy Flow devices, an underwater chamber and Xenochromatic Jellyfish that trigger mechanisms. Recon Log Meks and Geological Survey Meks guard it. Solving the puzzle yields a "Soul" piece.
### LARGE +
The slate's text calls the place the Orthant of Souls, the Shrine of Souls and the Room of Souls, with lines about accompanying someone in eternal rest. A 4-star weapon, Nymph's Constancy, can be picked up beside the slate. Part of the ruin floods or drains when Energy Flow is released, turning the surrounding area into dry land.
### ULTRA +
The quest has two versions depending on whether the Traveler began "Search in the Algae Sea" first, and it runs concurrently with the quests "Meteoric Lance" and "Muse's Mother" before leading to "Thalia and Melpomene". The wider Narcissus chain's plot is not resolved in this entry.

## quests
### STANDARD
"Cupid's Lover" (4.2) belongs to "Search in the Algae Sea" within "In the Wake of Narcissus". Paimon and the Traveler find signs that someone else got there first, such as a pocket watch on the ground, and the slate names the place the Orthant of Souls. Ann, Seymour and a caterpillar are listed among the quest's characters. The wider plot belongs to the quest's own entry.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 it is an accessible slope with its ruin and quest content unchanged.
