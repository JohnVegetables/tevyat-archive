## summary
### LITE
Chinju Forest is a sacred grove in a small river valley just south of Mt. Yougou on Narukami Island, and the path to the Kamisato Estate runs through it. For some unknown reason it is shrouded in perpetual night, with the glow of an unseen moon. Tanuki statues, torii gates and abandoned shrines are scattered among the trees, and legend says mischievous beings play pranks on walkers.
### STANDARD +
It is the home of the bake-danuki Ioroi, once its protector, and the setting of the "tanuki-bayashi" legend: strange drumming and music in the woods said to be made by tanuki. It was once said to be the home of many demons.

## geography
### STANDARD
A wooded valley at the foot of Mt. Yougou, with a river running through it and paths climbing toward a big tanuki statue. Stone torii and lanterns mark routes and barriers; the forest itself sits in fixed moonlight.
### LARGE +
Sakura Bloom grows here as a local specialty. The forest connects the Kamisato Estate to the mountain country; a ward-protected barrier, with lantern-and-gate puzzles, guards the spot where a cleansing ritual is carried out.

## places
### STANDARD
The tanuki statues: stone figures all over the forest, including a large one in which Ioroi was sealed. The torii gates and lanterns that mark the ritual site. Abandoned shrines hidden deep in the trees.
### LARGE +
A Sky Kitsune Statue that once held a ward is found empty. The big statue is where the Traveler receives Ioroi's Ward after playing with his fellow tanuki.

## people
### STANDARD
Ioroi, a bake-danuki titled the Hidden Protector of the Grove, speaks through the big statue; three small tanuki, Shousei, Kichiboushi and Gyoubu Koban, hide in the forest and are found by the Traveler. The shrine maiden Hanachirusato sends the Traveler here.
### LARGE +
Hilichurls and other creatures also stray in. Ioroi's past is tied to the Kitsune Saiguu, his old rival, and to the onmyouji Kamuna Harunosuke, who sealed him.

## history
### STANDARD
Legend says the forest was once home to many demons. Ioroi used to compete with the Kitsune Saiguu; after Ei nearly killed him for trying to pluck a Naku Weed from the Tenshukaku garden on Saiguu's challenge, Saiguu pleaded for him and the Shogun put him under her command as Protector of the Grove.
### LARGE +
Five hundred years ago, during the cataclysm, Saiguu tricked Ioroi into hiding in a game of hide-and-seek to protect him, then went to battle and died. Believing she was still playing, Ioroi smashed kitsune statues across Narukami Island to lure her out and kept a comb he found in one, a tool of the Sacred Sakura Cleansing Ritual. The Shogunate sent Kamuna Harunosuke, who sealed Ioroi and many bake-danuki into stone statues as a lesson.

### ULTRA +
Limited-time event material: during the Enchanted Tales of the Mikawa Festival event, Yae Miko told Ioroi he had served his time. He blustered that he could free himself whenever he liked and that the seal was nearly spent, and cut her off when she began to speak of Saiguu's fate, which suggests he knows the truth and cannot accept it. He sent tanuki to help the festival out of pride; a doppelganger incident there turned out to involve Tetsunosuke, Ginkaku and Kinkaku, whom Ioroi had thought sealed. In the follow-up story As Though Beholding Illusions he again refused release, saying he preferred being stone, and dismissed the news of Saiguu's death as a trick.

## quests
### STANDARD
Tanuki-Bayashi in the Forest, part of Cleansing Defilement within the Sacred Sakura Cleansing Ritual series: the Traveler finds the tanuki, receives Ioroi's Ward and breaks the barrier, defeating an Ochimusha.
### LARGE +
The ritual at Chinju Forest is one of three, alongside Araumi and the Kamisato Estate. Once all five barriers in Inazuma are cleansed, the Traveler and Hanachirusato go to the roots of the Sacred Sakura, where she is revealed to be filth condensed from the memory of the Kitsune Saiguu.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Hilichurls, an Electro Cicin Mage and an Ochimusha spirit tied to the barrier. Otherwise a gentle forest; its main hazard is getting lost to pranks.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Perpetual blue-black night, moonlight with no moon in sight, the creak of old torii, stone tanuki with watching eyes, and a faint sense that something is about to laugh behind you.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the forest is unchanged: moonlit, quiet and full of tanuki statues, with Ioroi still in his stone.
### STANDARD +
No 6.x or 7.0-7.1 content has altered it. The Cleansing Defilement quest's rituals are complete, so the barriers are down.

## entry: Ioroi and the Tanuki-Bayashi
@keys: Ioroi, Tanuki-Bayashi, Shousei, Kichiboushi, Gyoubu Koban
@version: 2.0
### STANDARD
Ioroi is a bake-danuki who lives in Chinju Forest and calls Saiguu 'that sneaky kitsune'. He first mistook the Traveler for a fellow tanuki and, on realising they were human, panicked and sent hilichurls to drive them off. He agreed to give up the comb and ward only if the Traveler played with three young tanuki. His seal leaves him unable to move; he relies on reports from tanuki who were not petrified.
### LARGE +
Ioroi believed Saiguu was still hiding; the Hakushin Ring's text has her hoping the 'mischievous yet innocent tanuki' will not hate her for her final deception. In Eight Locales Over Mountains and Seas he asks the Traveler to take Kichiboushi around Teyvat for training, wanting him as a future successor.
