## summary
@availability: limited
### LITE
The Pavilion of Hermits is the fairground subarea of the Veluriyam Mirage, the bottle-domain from the limited-time Version 3.8 event Secret Summer Paradise. It is always night there, and a vast tent of fairground attractions stands at its centre. Maymunah, a scholar-replica who dreams of running her own festival, manages it. It was event-only and is not on the permanent map.
### STANDARD +
Its main story is the Act II quest Fairground Gathering, Summer Lights Illuminated, in which the Traveler's group, Eula, Collei, Idyia and Maymunah repair the tent's fallen lights, find a trapped Lawrence cousin of Eula's, and free the Hydro Eidolons trapped in the tent.

## geography
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
A lantern-lit fairground under a permanent night sky, with a big central tent, Gleaming Lamps, and a light board whose three lamps turn from white to colour as they are repaired. The area is unlocked by starting the Act II quest.
### LARGE +
Preprints are scattered around the grounds: solving them removes the bubble from a Luxurious Chest and repairs the three lamps of the light board. The tent holds domain-style challenge content in game terms, though the story calls the whole Mirage a Domain.

## people
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
Maymunah, an Akademiya-robed Spantamad scholar, manages the grounds. Lafeinte the "Blade Dancer" and Basima, a scholar with a game of her own, are other residents. All are Hydro Eidolon replicas of real visitors who once passed through the bottle.
### LARGE +
The real Lafeinte, per his wiki profile, was a Fontaine swordsman of several centuries ago who went into exile after the Maison Gardiennage sought him. The real Basima was an Amurta scholar who vanished, returned after ten years with the Bing-Bang Finchball, and later used it to turn rivals into friends. The replicas hold none of these memories. Lessig, a real man from the Lawrence Clan, is not a replica: he came to the Mirage seeking to restore his clan's honour.

## quests
@availability: limited
@version: 3.8
### STANDARD
In Fairground Gathering, Summer Lights Illuminated, Maymunah leads the group into the abandoned tent, where something fell from the central hub and knocked down the Crystal Light, trapping Hydro Eidolons and Lessig. The group fixes the lights floor by floor. Lessig, helped by Idyia's candy, admits he was wrong; Maymunah gives the group the central hub component, a candy box.
### LARGE +
Maymunah tells the group the collapsed stage reminds her of helping her father with festival work in the Sumeru Grand Bazaar, and that she came to the Mirage to run her own festival and become her true self, though managing it is harder than she expected. Lessig apologises to Eula and says he had come to Sumeru to restore the glory of the Lawrence Clan, but learned that nobody cares. The next morning he confesses that he had touched the water orb at the heart of the domain, which caused the chaos, and offers to help repair it.
### ULTRA +
In game terms the light board is a puzzle: three white lamps must be turned to colour, and one is repaired by a Hydro Eidolon the group saved earlier in the event. Paimon remarks that the multicoloured light is an ingenious design. The Mirage's unusual status is noted in the wiki: story characters call it a Domain, but the game does not treat it as one in the usual sense.

## atmosphere
@availability: limited
### LARGE
Carnival lights against a permanent night: an amusement park run by replicas of people who left.

## dangers
@availability: limited
### LARGE
Mostly light combat with hilichurls, hilichurl shooters, Dendro and Hydro Samachurls and a Stonehide Lawachurl. The tent itself was dangerous during the collapse of its lights.

## status
@availability: limited
### LITE
As of 7.1 canon, the Pavilion of Hermits is a time-limited area from Version 3.8; no later story returns to it.
### STANDARD +
After the event, Maymunah says stall requests are piling up faster than she can arrange them, so the fairgrounds are busy again.

## entry: Maymunah the Fairground Manager
@keys: Maymunah, Fairground Manager
@keys2: Veluriyam Mirage, Pavilion of Hermits, fairgrounds
@availability: limited
@version: 3.8
### STANDARD
Maymunah is a Hydro Eidolon replica of a Spantamad-trained scholar, dressed in Akademiya robes with a hat bearing the Spantamad darshan symbol. She is overworked: stall demand exceeds space, and she jokes that her predecessor fled back to Sumeru. She hopes to run her own festival.
