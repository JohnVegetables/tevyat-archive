## summary
### LITE
Dadaupa Gorge is a rugged area of Galesong Hill in Mondstadt, home to three hilichurl tribes: the Meaty, the Sleeper and the Eclipse. At its centre lies the Forgotten Sword Cemetery, which witnessed the ancient battle against the monsters of Khaenri'ah.
### STANDARD +
The name Dadaupa means 'large mountain' in Hilichurlian, according to the Handy Handbook of Hilichurlian. Official text calls the place dangerous for the number of hilichurls and its Mitachurls and Samachurls.

## geography
### STANDARD
A gorge with many hilichurl camps and three Elemental Monuments (Cryo, Electro and Pyro). The quest page describes the Sword Cemetery as standing in the middle of a large lake in the gorge. Calla lilies and dandelion seeds grow. Ruin Torches and Seelie can be found.
### LARGE +
The three tribes are placed around the cemetery: the Meaty to the north, the Sleeper to the south and the Eclipse in the east valley.

## places
### STANDARD
The Forgotten Sword Cemetery is protected by three layers of seal that can be broken by activating the monuments at the camps. The Temple of the Lion, an old temple to the Lion of the South, is also listed here by the wiki.
### LARGE +
The cemetery's graves are marked with swords; Dr. Livingstone says the seals were set by survivors using elemental powers so their fallen comrades could rest. The Temple of the Lion is abandoned; folklore holds the dandelions on Mondstadt's winds symbolise the Lion of the South's blessing.

## people
### STANDARD
Dr. Livingstone, an adventurer who looks like a scholar and works with Dr. Edith, researches the cemetery. She gives the world quest Break the Sword Cemetery Seal.
### LARGE +
After the Traveler breaks the seals, she stays to keep studying the many mysteries that remain at the cemetery.
### ULTRA +
She signs off with the adventurers' toast 'Ad astra abyssosque' and speaks of unearthing ancient secrets as an adventurer's duty; Dr. Edith is her colleague.

## history
### STANDARD
The cemetery marks an ancient battlefield against Khaenri'ah's monsters; canon gives no further detail.
### LARGE +
The account of survivors raising the triple seal comes from Dr. Livingstone's explanation in the world quest.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Hilichurl fires and drum noise among steep rock, with a hush around the swords.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Heavy hilichurl presence, including Mitachurls and Samachurls, Fatui skirmishers, Cryo Abyss Mages and slimes. Treat hilichurl camps as hostile.

## quests
### STANDARD
Break the Sword Cemetery Seal (world quest), which unlocks a random Dr. Livingstone's Transport Request.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 hilichurls still hold the gorge and the cemetery remains studied.

## entry: Forgotten Sword Cemetery
@keys: Forgotten Sword Cemetery, Sword Cemetery, tri-seal
@keys2: Dadaupa Gorge, Khaenri'ah
### STANDARD
A battlefield in Dadaupa Gorge guarded by three seals, said to mark the battle against Khaenri'ah's monsters. Breaking the seals means defeating the three hilichurl camps around it, after which a treasure is found at its centre.
