## summary
@availability: limited
### LITE
The Silver Bottle Courtyard is the main town of the Veluriyam Mirage, the bottle-domain from the limited-time Version 3.8 event Secret Summer Paradise. The Traveler's group arrives here after entering the bottle, meets the caretaker Idyia here, and returns to it between stages of the repair. Its resident replicas run game stalls and a lakeside restaurant. It was event-only.
### STANDARD +
It is unlocked by the first event quest, A Mysterious Missive on Paper Wings, in which Klee, Kaeya, the Traveler and Paimon follow a Magic Invite sent by Klee's mother, Alice, on behalf of a friend in Sumeru. The great wheel at the heart of the town is the Mirage's central hub and the object the whole event is about repairing.

## geography
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
A town with a west and east side, an entrance facing west, a lakeside restaurant, and the main wheel. It contains a Waverider Waypoint, a Hydro Elemental Monument, Four-Leaf Sigils, Dendrogranum and Rocklifting Branches, and Nilotpala Lotus as its local specialty.
### LARGE +
The Spino Blaster station stands in the town but was unusable before the quest Fairground Gathering, Summer Lights Illuminated. The central wheel, ridden by Choo-Choo Cart, has a top platform where Idyia goes to be alone each time a visitor leaves. In canon terms, everything in the Mirage begins as a Preprint, a flat blueprint turned solid by light from the core Streaming Projector.

## people
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
Idyia, the caretaker, is an Oceanid in human form; she is the only resident who is not a Hydro Eidolon replica. Resident replicas here include Chatillon (who runs Spino Blaster), Madiha (Barking Fox tests), Gretchen (restaurant manager), Mandora (a former adventurer) and others, each modelled on a real visitor. Sangonomiya Kokomi came to the Mirage seeking a lost relic from Watatsumi Island.
### LARGE +
Idyia's account, per the event: the waters of Fontaine turned painful for Oceanids, she fled and was lost in the desert, and Alice granted her wish to be away from everyone by giving her the Mirage. Visitors left gifts, which she uses to power the domain, and some asked for a Hydro Eidolon copy of themselves to carry on their dreams. Chatillon, per his wiki profile, was a real engineer who has since died. Idyia says Kaveh redesigned many of the Mirage's buildings.

## history
@availability: limited
@version: 3.8
### LARGE
The Mirage began when Idyia, hiding in the desert after fleeing Fontaine, received the domain from Alice. Over time, travellers who found the bottle had their wishes granted inside it, and left behind gifts that powered the domain. Not all were pleased to learn they could not take their creations out; some destroyed them. In the event, a Lawrence cousin of Eula's, Lessig, touched the domain's core, a water orb, and the Mirage went into disarray.

## quests
@availability: limited
@version: 3.8
### STANDARD
The Traveler, Paimon, Klee and Kaeya enter the bottle in A Mysterious Missive on Paper Wings; Glaze Domain, Multum in Parvo takes them to the courtyard, where they meet Idyia and Kokomi and set out to recover missing components of the central hub. In Back to Normal: The Story Hidden in the Bottle, Idyia places the recovered components in the core. When it still will not turn, Kokomi lends the Shinrou Casket; Idyia wishes that everyone who came to the Mirage will achieve what they truly wish for in reality, and the wheel begins to turn.
### LARGE +
Returning Curios, a later quest, has Idyia ask the Traveler to find hidden treasures and bury them at a chosen spot. The Traveler and Klee also ride to the top of the wheel with Idyia.
### ULTRA +
Details of the residents: Mandora tells visitors she stopped adventuring in the stead of the real Mandora, who left to continue adventuring; Gretchen runs her lakeside restaurant with an unusual dining etiquette in which guests first fetch ingredients; Madiha speaks in test-engineer jargon. Idyia notes that residents' copies cannot hold their originals' memories, so she teaches them using her own memories of those visitors. The name of the Mirage is likely derived from a Pali word for lapis lazuli, per the wiki.

## atmosphere
@availability: limited
### LARGE
A pleasant, quiet resort town in a glass bottle, with the odd sense that everyone was modelled on someone who left.

## status
@availability: limited
### LITE
As of 7.1 canon, the Silver Bottle Courtyard is part of a time-limited area from Version 3.8; no later story returns to it.
### STANDARD +
At the end of the event the wheel was turning again and the Mirage was running normally, with Idyia welcoming visitors.

## entry: Idyia, Caretaker of the Mirage
@keys: Idyia, Veluriyam Mirage caretaker
@keys2: Veluriyam Mirage, Silver Bottle Courtyard, Klee
@availability: limited
@version: 3.8
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Idyia is an Oceanid in human shape who keeps the Mirage. She is gentle and wants guests to relax in a quiet place. She is a friend of Alice, Klee's mother. Klee calls her "Aunty Desert" and Paimon "Mud Fairy". She cannot explain Preprints fully, comparing them to the blueprint a seed or egg carries.
