## summary
### LITE
Cape Oath is a headland at the south-eastern corner of Mondstadt's surroundings, bordering Galesong Hill. A local legend says two lovers swore an oath to each other here, and young couples still come for the sunrise and sunset. A time tunnel on the cape leads to Musk Reef, the island that holds the entrance to the Spiral Abyss.
### STANDARD +
The drop in the terrain makes it easy to see Windrise, which sits facing the sea. Lovers treat Cape Oath as a favoured Windblume date spot.

## geography
### STANDARD
A cape of grass and cliff edge looking over the sea, forming one side of the bay with Falcon Coast. Dandelion seeds grow in large numbers. The time tunnel does not appear on the world map but appears on the minimap close to it.
### LARGE +
Reaching the tunnel means gathering the seelie around the Electro Slimes to raise a wind current that carries a glider up to it. Electro Hypostasis is found in the area as a boss; a Wooden Shieldwall Mitachurl, Fatui Cryogunners and Anemoboxers are among the foes.

## places
### STANDARD
The Time Tunnel leads one way to Musk Reef, which holds the Spiral Abyss portal. Stepping through it earns the 'Initiating Warp Drive!' achievement.
### LARGE +
Windrise lies within sight and Falcon Coast along the shore.

## history
### STANDARD
No narrative history is given for the cape beyond the lovers' legend. Musk Reef, its destination, was once the summit of a great mountain called Pilos Peak.
### LARGE +
Before Barbatos reshaped Mondstadt about 2,600 years ago, Pilos Peak stood as a mountain on the continent. The adventurer Leonard failed to climb it, as his journal Of Mountains and Seas records. When Barbatos blew the snow away, the peak was swept from its place and the melted ice made the sea, leaving the island of Musk Reef.
### ULTRA +
The limited event Unreconciled Stars (1.1) later revisited Musk Reef; see the entry below.
## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Open sky, sea air and wide views over the water; a romantic spot, especially at sunrise and sunset.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Few: slimes and hilichurls, with Fatui scouts and the Electro Hypostasis nearby.

## travel
### STANDARD
In the south-east corner of Mondstadt's surroundings; the time tunnel shows on the minimap on approach.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 unchanged.

## entry: Leonard's Meteorite on Musk Reef

### ULTRA
During the limited event Unreconciled Stars (1.1), about 2,000 years after Leonard's attempt, his constellation fell as meteorites that sent those who touched them into a sleep in which they felt compelled to climb. The core, holding the greatest elemental energy and Leonard's will, fell on Musk Reef. The Traveler and Mona fought off the Fatui who came to investigate and destroyed the core, ending the dreams.

## entry: Time Tunnel to Musk Reef

### STANDARD
A one-way time tunnel at Cape Oath carries travellers to Musk Reef, where the Spiral Abyss portal stands. The island was once the summit Pilos Peak.
