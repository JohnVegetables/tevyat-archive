## summary
### LITE
The Pilgrimage to the Sacred Gorge is a snowy mountain gorge in the White Birch Snowgrave of Snezhnaya, on the way to the Pale Crown Palace, the Belyi Tsar's mausoleum. The route is blocked by a collapse that looks like an artificial avalanche, and official signs say the Royal Energy Commission and the Railway Transport Bureau are investigating.
### STANDARD +
The notes found there reveal that the sabotage was itself ordered by someone the writer calls the Old Sire and carried out by Energy Commission staff in disguise, so the investigation notices are partly a cover.

## geography
### STANDARD
A cold, narrow mountain approach in the northern snowfields, part of the White Birch Snowgrave, the former itinerant court of the Belyi Tsar that became his burial ground. A railway line runs along it toward the Pale Crown Palace and stretches of it have collapsed. Prime Ice, Seelies and Tsar's White Birch are found here, with Frostfairy Flower as a local specialty.
### LARGE +
The gorge lies in the same area as the Palace, whose in-game description speaks of a stop where no one arrives and a snow-covered grave.

## places
### STANDARD
The notable sites are the blocked rail line with its notice board and a control console left near the staged collapse. The point of the gorge is its role as the road to the Palace.

## people
### STANDARD
No residents are shown. The notes mention three groups: the Royal Energy Commission of Snezhnaya, the Snezhnaya Royal Railway Transport Bureau, and brigands who want to plunder the Palace.
### LARGE +
A Transport Bureau investigator concludes the avalanche was triggered by hand and suspects someone in the Commission is hiding something or tampering with the Palace. The Commission-side writer treats the closure as an order from the Old Sire and remarks that earlier custom would have used the High Table, suggesting he is unwell and that his adopted children cannot hold out much longer.
### ULTRA +
The Orderbearer writer calls the disguise perfect and says he re-tested the control console and found no abnormalities before leaving; the investigator, meanwhile, thinks only a rather intricate operation could have caused the collapse and plans to report to Transport Bureau headquarters.

## governance
### STANDARD
Two bodies have a hand here: the Energy Commission, which put up the warning sign, and the Transport Bureau, which polices the railway and expects reports. The Orderbearer note shows the Commission acting on an order from above without telling the Bureau.

## history
### STANDARD
The collapse is recent as of the Traveler's visit. The Pale Crown Palace itself is the Belyi Tsar's mausoleum, once one of his palaces, left behind after he died in the cataclysm five hundred years ago.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Freezing wind, deep snow, damaged track and officious warning signs; a sense that the official story hides something.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Chimeric Armored Beasts, Chimeric Horned Bears, Winged Halberd-Knights and Winged Soarer Knights roam the area, with the Frostarm Lawachurl as an elite enemy. Further collapses from structural damage are warned of on the notice board.

## travel
### STANDARD
The gorge is on the route to the Pale Crown Palace, and the sign asks visitors to take care until the railway is fully repaired.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the route to the Palace is still blocked and the investigation notice stands.
### STANDARD +
The notes do not say whether the sabotage will be reversed.

## entry: The Sabotaged Route and the Old Sire
@keys: Old Sire, Orderbearer, sabotaged railway, artificial avalanche
@keys2: Pale Crown Palace, Energy Commission, Transport Bureau
@version: 7.0
@spoiler: minor
### LARGE
The notice board blames deliberate sabotage of the route to the Pale Crown Palace and says the Energy Commission and the Transport Bureau are investigating. An investigator's note judges the avalanche was triggered by hand and, finding the Commission's sign odd, suspects a cover-up or tampering with the Palace, while hoping brigands who try to plunder it regret it. A second note, the Records of the Orderbearer, says that on the Old Sire's orders a small blockage was introduced, that this would normally have been given to the High Table, and that using Commission staff for field work may be meant to keep the matter beyond the reach of gangsters. Treat the Old Sire's identity as unknown.
