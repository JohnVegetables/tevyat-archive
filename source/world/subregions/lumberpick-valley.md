## summary
### LITE
Lumberpick Valley is a surface subarea of the Chasm in Liyue. Geograna, Seelie and spoutrocks lie among its rocks, Noctilucous Jade can be gathered, and it is crowded with hilichurls, Treasure Hoarders and a Ruin Hunter.
### STANDARD +
The Chinese name means Lumber-Picking Valley, which hints at woodcutting, though no text confirms it.

## geography
### STANDARD
Features: Geograna, Seelie, Spoutrocks, Unique Rocks and Unique Rock Pillars. The local specialty is Noctilucous Jade.
### LARGE +
It lies within the Chasm's surface area, above the Underground Mines that Liyue closed after mysterious accidents and has gradually been reopening. The wiki records no description or point of interest for the valley.
### ULTRA +
Noctilucous Jade is the glowing mineral the Chasm is known for. A notice posted at the Chasm warns that its crimson glow has gone and urges people not to be drawn by noctilucous light, a reminder of why the mines were shut.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Elite enemies are Blazing Axe Mitachurls and Ruin Hunters. Common ones are ordinary and Electro Hilichurl Grenadiers and fighters, Anemo and Dendro Slimes, and many kinds of Treasure Hoarders (Marksmen, Pugilists, Scouts, Seamen and the three Potioneers).
### LARGE +
Because the area mixes camps of hilichurls with Treasure Hoarders and an old Ruin Machine, a scene here should expect more than one hostile party.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
A rocky valley with luminous stone underfoot and several hostile camps sharing it. This is a suggested scene mood drawn from the feature list, not a quoted description.

## status
### STANDARD
Unchanged as of 7.1.
