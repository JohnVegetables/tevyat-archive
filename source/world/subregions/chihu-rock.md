## summary
### LITE
Chihu Rock is the busy, ordinary side of Liyue Harbor: a district on an island at the city's southeast, joined by bridges to Feiyun Slope on the north and to Mt. Tianheng on the west. Its main plaza, main street and docks are where street food sellers, miners, sailors, dockworkers and storytellers go about their day, and where an adventurer finds the Adventurers' Guild and Wanmin Restaurant.
### STANDARD +
The wiki's location text calls it the harbor's lower-class district, while the in-game viewpoint text treats it as the pulsing heart of the city, the lively scene from which Liyue Harbor draws its energy. The name is Chihu in pinyin, which literally reads as "Eating-Tiger Rock"; no source explains why.

## geography
### STANDARD
Chihu Rock sits on an island to the southeast of the rest of the harbor. A bridge runs north to Feiyun Slope (the merchant district) and another west toward Mt. Tianheng. Inside the district the layout is a main plaza, a main street climbing from it, a side alley, a dockyard and the harbor wharves where ships tie up.
### LARGE +
Yanshang Teahouse is on the main street, and Third-Round Knockout has its own small cluster of customers and staff. The dockyard and the harbor wharves are where cargo and passengers are handled.

## places
### STANDARD
- Main plaza: street food stalls, a toy seller, Dongsheng's general goods shop Second Life and Shitou's Jade Mystery; miners and a Blackcliff Forge shift supervisor are seen here in the day.
- Main street: the Adventurers' Guild branch, Wanmin Restaurant, Hanfeng's Ironmongers and Yanshang Teahouse nearby.
- Third-Round Knockout: a storytelling tavern.
- Dockyard and harbor: laborers, a warehouse manager, boatmen, sailors and fishmongers.
### LARGE +
- Wanmin Restaurant is a famous, simple restaurant run by Chef Mao; his daughter Xiangling is its head chef. Its fare is everyday Liyue food.
- Second Life sells general goods and restocks every day.
- Hanfeng's Ironmongers is a smithy run by Master Zhang.
- A side alley holds a shop called Scent of Spring and a handyman for the Adventurers' Guild.
- The Chihu Rock Bulletin Board carries public notices for the district.
### ULTRA +
Yanshang Teahouse is, behind the name, a casino. It once admitted only rich patrons who paid a deposit. Shortly before the Traveler arrived, Childe and an associate named Javert confronted its original owner over a debt owed to the Northland Bank; the owner was jailed, and after Osial's defeat the intelligence agent Yelan took the teahouse over as a secret base. It stays a casino, with relaxed rules, and Yelan is notorious there for her gambling skill. Third-Round Knockout's name, according to the sailor Xu Liushi, comes from the night he drank three bowls of the house's wine-fermented sweet rice balls and had to be carried out; the tavern had no name before.

## people
### STANDARD
Chef Mao and Xiangling at Wanmin Restaurant; Katheryne and the branch master Lan at the Adventurers' Guild; Dongsheng at Second Life; Shitou of Jade Mystery; Master Zhang the blacksmith; Chuyi, hostess of Yanshang Teahouse; Degui, who owns Third-Round Knockout, and its storyteller Iron Tongue Tian. Millelith soldiers patrol the streets and docks.
### LARGE +
Down at the harbor are Gou San'er (a warehouse manager), the boatman Pinghai, the angler Jiawei, the dockyard laborers Hammer Li and Wrench Wang, the fishmongers Uncle Gao and Uncle Sun at night, the fortune teller Qiming and Ms. Yu, a secretary of the Ministry of Civil Affairs. The Tianshu of the Qixing, Uncle Tian, can be met at the harbor during the day. The sailor Guanhai works the wharves by day and plays cards at Yanshang Teahouse by night.
### ULTRA +
Gourmets such as Freki and Geri, the street food vendors Chen the Sharp and Su Er'niang, the toy seller Granny Shan and the miners Qian and Captain Wu give the plaza and main street their regular faces.

## culture
### STANDARD
Chihu Rock runs on food, gossip and trade. Locals eat from stalls, drink at Third-Round Knockout, trade stories in the plaza and bargain at the shops. The storyteller at Third-Round Knockout is a draw; Zhongli is found listening there in several quests.
### LARGE +
Sailors and dockworkers mix with miners (the Mt. Tianheng mines are close) and craftsmen. Everyday Liyue life is on show here more than in the wealthy districts.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Noise, cooking smells, sea air and bargaining, with a mix of locals and travellers. It feels crowded and cheerful rather than refined, a sharp contrast with the high terraces of Yujing Terrace.

## dangers
### LARGE
Little: this is a policed city district patrolled by the Millelith. The risks are social, such as sharp gamblers at Yanshang Teahouse. In the Osial crisis (1.1) the ancient sea god Osial threatened Liyue Harbor itself.

## travel
### STANDARD
Reached from Feiyun Slope by bridge, or from Mt. Tianheng on the west side. Ships dock along the wharves.

## quests
### LARGE
- Of the Land Amidst Monoliths (Archon Quest, 1.0): at the start of the Rite of Descension quest, the Traveler talks with locals including the sailor Guanhai, who tells them to go to Yujing Terrace.
- No Mere Stone (Zhongli's story quest, 1.5): the Traveler finds Zhongli listening to the storyteller at Third-Round Knockout and takes a guild commission from Katheryne.
### ULTRA +
A Thousand Moonlit Miles (Xianyun's story quest, 4.4): a late scene is set in Chihu Rock, where Xianyun, newly settled in the city, tries to sell off some of her possessions.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 Chihu Rock is a bustling everyday district of Liyue Harbor with no recorded change from 6.x or 7.0-7.1 content.
### STANDARD +
Shenhe now works as a server at Wanmin Restaurant, referred by Ganyu, and Xianyun, who is the adeptus Cloud Retainer, lives in the city in human form. Yelan runs Yanshang Teahouse from behind the scenes.

## entry: Wanmin Restaurant
@keys: Wanmin Restaurant, Chef Mao
### STANDARD
Wanmin Restaurant is a famous restaurant in Chihu Rock, owned by Chef Mao and worked by his daughter Xiangling as head chef. It sells simple, popular Liyue food, ingredients and recipes. Ningguang has financed it, and Shenhe waits tables there after Ganyu referred her. Xianyun is a regular, sitting with Guoba while she waits for her noodles.

## entry: Yanshang Teahouse
@keys: Yanshang Teahouse, Chuyi
### STANDARD
A casino disguised as a teahouse selling only high-grade tea and other goods. Under its original owner, who admitted only wealthy patrons, the hostess Chuyi was curt and unwelcoming and two security guards stood by the door. Childe exposed the owner's fraud over a debt to the Northland Bank and took his Sigil of Permission; after Osial's defeat Yelan took over. She is notorious among its patrons for her gambling skill, and Chuyi is less abrupt under her management.

## entry: Third-Round Knockout
@keys: Third-Round Knockout, Iron Tongue Tian
### STANDARD
A tavern owned by Degui, with the waitress Zhiruo and the storyteller Iron Tongue Tian. Fishmongers Uncle Gao and Uncle Sun drink here at night. Zhongli seems to favour it. The wiki notes the Chinese name is likely an allusion to a classic novel's tavern with very strong wine.

## entry: Xianyun in Chihu Rock
@keys: Xianyun, Cloud Retainer, Shaozu
@keys2: Chihu Rock
@version: 4.4
### LARGE
Near the end of A Thousand Moonlit Miles, Xianyun is found in Chihu Rock quarrelling with a buyer named Shaozu over a collection of her trinkets that she wants to sell. Shaozu, wary of spending Mora, has hired an expert, who turns out to be Zhongli; he vouches for the goods. Shaozu pays a deposit from the Northland Bank because the sum is too large to pay at once. Afterwards Xianyun and Zhongli share a brief talk about the past and future and she plans a feast to introduce her new disciple Shuyu to Ganyu and Shenhe.
