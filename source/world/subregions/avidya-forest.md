## summary
### LITE
Avidya Forest is the lush rainforest at the heart of Sumeru and the first area the Traveler reaches in the nation. It holds Sumeru City, the "City of Wisdom" built into the Divine Tree and seat of the Sumeru Akademiya, and Gandharva Ville, the base of the Forest Rangers. Rain, mushrooms and glowing lotus fill the woods, and scholars, merchants and rangers share the paths. Nahida, Sumeru's Dendro Archon, lives in the palace at the top of the tree.
### STANDARD +
The Forest Rangers guard the woods from dangerous wildlife, careless visitors and the creeping Withering. Beyond the forest lie the Lokapala Jungle, the river valley of Ardravi and, beyond the Wall of Samiel, the desert.

## geography
### STANDARD
Avidya Forest lies in the Dharma Forest subregion, Sumeru's rainforest. The wiki lists five subareas: Sumeru City, Gandharva Ville, Chinvat Ravine (a ravine below the city, at the base of the Divine Tree), Gandha Hill and Yazadaha Pool. Local specialties include Padisarah, Nilotpala Lotus, Kalpalata Lotus and Rukkhashava Mushrooms; shroomboars and many kinds of mushroom-creatures (Fungi) roam the woods.
### LARGE +
Neighbouring areas in Dharma Forest include Ardravi Valley (a great river valley and Sumeru's main waterborne trade route, whose natural life the Forest Watchers also look after), the Lokapala Jungle along the north coast and Mawtiyima Forest, the northern forest of giant glowing mushrooms.

## places
### STANDARD
Sumeru City (on the Divine Tree), Gandharva Ville (the Forest Rangers' outpost, "The City Above the Forest"), Chinvat Ravine (under the tree), and the quieter Gandha Hill and Yazadaha Pool.
### LARGE +
The official exploration blurb for Gandharva Ville reads "The city of wisdom is ours, as is the forest of ignorance. Atop the tree lies the remnants of the kingdom of lost dreams." The blurb for Sumeru City calls it the root of all wisdom, where the sages have gathered all the knowledge there is to be gathered.

## people
### STANDARD
Akademiya scholars and students, merchants, farmers and dockworkers, Eremite mercenaries, and the Forest Rangers, whose chief officer Tighnari mentors the ranger Collei. Nahida lives at the top of the Divine Tree; Naphis is Grand Sage.
### LARGE +
Other named figures tied to the city include the Scribe Alhaitham and the architect Kaveh (who share a home), General Mahamatra Cyno, the dancer Nilou of the Zubayr Theater, and the Wanderer. At Gandharva Ville the chef Ashpazi is a Watcher in charge of logistics, Shirin is the rangers' doctor, Amir trains the search-and-rescue dogs (whose captain is the dog Sag) and Izad is the liaison to the research station Pardis Dhyai.

## governance
### STANDARD
Sumeru City is under the jurisdiction of the Sumeru Akademiya, which is both Teyvat's great academy and Sumeru's government. The Corps of Thirty, an Eremite mercenary brigade under contract with the sages, keeps the peace in the city; the Forest Rangers, civil servants, protect the forest.
### LARGE +
The Corps of Thirty is headquartered at the Citadel of Regzar. The Forest Rangers send regular reports on the forest to the researchers of Pardis Dhyai, the botany centre run by the Amurta Darshan, who in return provide seeds and nutrients. Tighnari keeps close ties with those researchers but avoids Sumeru City and the Akademiya itself. He remarks in a voice-over that his ranger training courses could count as unauthorised "dissemination of information" by the Akademiya, and dares them to lock him up.

## culture
### LARGE
In Sumeru City height means status: the lower levels are for "common folk", and promotion or higher education lets someone "move up". "Down below" is understood to mean the bottom of the tree. The Grand Bazaar is known as a lively, artistic place of handmade goods, home mostly to ordinary people seeking a steady life, and Treasures Street is the liveliest street in the city. The Akademiya runs the Bimarstan, a hospital giving free healthcare to all Sumeru residents. Thesis pressure is a part of academic life: a chest in the Akademiya holds a note from a scholar promising to deposit 5,000 Mora each day the thesis proposal goes unwritten.
### ULTRA +
Razan Garden, reached through doors inside the Akademiya on the path up the tree, is a quiet garden of gazebos and decorative Padisarah where researchers and students rest; its name is a Persian word for vines. The House of Daena, the Akademiya's library, lies at the heart of the Akademiya building. The Zubayr Theater and its performers carry the arts that the old sages once scorned.

## history
### STANDARD
Legend says Greater Lord Rukkhadevata, the original Dendro Archon, built the Varuna Contraption to nurture the rainforest, and the first lord of the forest, the tiger Viaghara, was born from the moon's reflection there. About 500 years ago, in the cataclysm, Rukkhadevata died and the sages of the Akademiya kept her successor, the child-like Lesser Lord Kusanali (Nahida), confined in the Sanctuary of Surasthana. The Traveler's arrival in Avidya Forest opens Chapter III of the Archon Quest (3.0), which ends with the old sages' plot defeated and Nahida free.
### LARGE +
The Withering, a corruption that kills plants and is tied to forbidden knowledge, appears as Withering Zones in the forest; Tighnari tells the Traveler that its emergence is related to a disease of Irminsul, the world tree of memory, and that as Withering Zones have increased, Collei's Eleazar (an endemic disease) has grown worse, with similar reports from other patients. After the sages' god-making plot failed, the old sages were replaced and Naphis became Grand Sage. For the later 6.6 crisis see the sub-entry "The Irminsul Crisis in Avidya".
### ULTRA +
The ranger Rana says the earliest forest rangers were Viaghara's royal guards; this is the only account of the rangers' origin. On the map, Avidya's recorded changes are small: the Joururi Workshop domain was added in Sumeru City in 3.2.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Damp green light and rainfall, the smell of wet bark and spice from the Grand Bazaar, glowing lotuses and mushrooms on the forest floor. Gandharva Ville feels like a busy trailhead of ranger gear, travelers and chatter; the city above is hushed and academic, a tangle of terraces and stairways wound around a gigantic tree.

## dangers
### LARGE
Withering Zones, Fungi and shroomboars, Ruin Machines, hostile wildlife such as the Avidya Leopard (Tighnari complains of adventurers who try to befriend one), and in places Eremite and hilichurl groups. Pollution and reckless adventurers are among Tighnari's own listed worries. The effects are extremely harmful to humans, who need a Vision to resist them. In gameplay terms a zone builds a Decay gauge that can kill a party, and is cleared by using Dendrograna on Withering Branches and defeating the enemies the zone summons.

## travel
### LARGE
Visitors often begin at Gandharva Ville, where the rangers help travelers in need. Tighnari recommends newcomers read the "Avidya Forest Survival Guide", paying particular attention to the chapter on telling edible from poisonous mushrooms. Caravans travel between Sumeru City and Port Ormos by way of Vimara Village on the river. The Sumeru City docks have boat captains, porters and a tax officer, and the Adventurers' Guild branch is on Treasures Street.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 Avidya Forest is peaceful, rebuilding after the 6.6 Irminsul crisis.
### STANDARD +
After Irminsul was burned the people of Sumeru went back to rebuilding and normal life, and Collei was promoted to full Forest Ranger. No 7.0 or 7.1 content touches the forest; the Archon Quest has moved on to Snezhnaya.
### LARGE +
According to Nahida in the aftermath, Aaru has taken over Irminsul's role of collecting and storing the world's information; the situation was contained in time, so Teyvat's most vital information was largely preserved.

## entry: Sumeru City and the Divine Tree
@keys: Sumeru City, Divine Tree, Sanctuary of Surasthana, Zubayr Theater, Treasures Street, Razan Garden, Citadel of Regzar, Bimarstan
@order: 110
### STANDARD
Sumeru City is known across Teyvat as the "City of Wisdom" and is built on top of the colossal Divine Tree, which holds up the Sumeru Akademiya. It is organised by height: the Grand Bazaar and other everyday buildings at the bottom, the Akademiya (with the House of Daena library) in the middle, and the Sanctuary of Surasthana, Nahida's palace, at the very top. Other places include the Bimarstan hospital, the docks, the Zubayr Theater, Lambad's Tavern, Puspa Cafe and the Corps of Thirty's headquarters, the Citadel of Regzar. Nahida was kept in the sanctuary for 500 years before Chapter III.
### LARGE +
Students of the Amurta Darshan such as Yasmin monitor and record the condition of the Divine Tree's roots as a precaution. The city has farmers and traders at its southern entrance, herb farms and the Bimarstan in the north, and a lively Treasures Street with the Adventurers' Guild branch and Puspa Cafe.

## entry: Gandharva Ville and the Forest Rangers
@keys: Gandharva Ville, Forest Rangers, Forest Watchers, Forest Ranger, Vanara
@order: 110
### STANDARD
Gandharva Ville, "The City Above the Forest", is the main outpost of the Forest Rangers (also called Vanara), civil servants who protect Sumeru's forests. Higher-ranking members, the Forest Watchers, wear lighter green. They help travelers in need, suppress Withering Zones, monitor beast populations and report to Pardis Dhyai. Tighnari is the Forest Watcher and chief officer; Collei is a ranger he trained. Merchants, adventurers, Eremite mercenaries and researchers come and go.
### LARGE +
Named rangers include Amir, Ashpazi, Rana, Shirin, Iraj, Izad, Kamran and Nasrin; the wiki marks Farbod, Keon and Umm as Vanara. The Chinese name of the outpost is derived from the Lotus Sutra's "Parable of the Phantom City", and the English name combines the Sanskrit gandharva with the French word for city.

## entry: Chinvat Ravine, Gandha Hill and Yazadaha Pool
@keys: Chinvat Ravine, Gandha Hill, Yazadaha Pool
### STANDARD
Chinvat Ravine lies below Sumeru City at the base of the Divine Tree. In the ancient literary canon of Sumeru, "Chinvat" is the bitter sea of ignorance that a seeker must cross to see the light of truth on the far shore; those who navigate the forests now use the word for the ravine under the tree, perhaps in honour of the elation of finally reaching the city of wisdom. Gandha Hill and Yazadaha Pool are outlying spots in the forest; the pool has ruin torches to light and Eremite, hilichurl and Fungi enemies. The wiki gives little lore for either beyond their names.

## entry: The Irminsul Crisis in Avidya
@keys: Irminsul crisis, burning of Irminsul, Dottore's Irminsul tree, rainforest evacuation
@keys2: Sumeru City, Avidya Forest
@version: 6.6
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
In Song of the Welkin Moon Act IX (6.6), Collei dreamed that everyone's identities in Sumeru City were switched, and a voice, Nicole's, told her it was a premonition. Irminsul had stopped processing information; Nahida took over its load and collapsed, and Nahida and the new sages evacuated the entire rainforest to Caravan Ribat. Civilians who tried to return to Sumeru City were affected by the anomaly near the Yasna Monument. In Act X a huge Irminsul tree, tied to Dottore, rose in Sumeru City. Cyno, Dehya, Candace and others fought their way into the city as a distraction while Tighnari, the Wanderer, Paimon and the Traveler climbed the Divine Tree. Nahida burned Irminsul with Mavuika's Pyro Gnosis, which took seven days and nights, and Dottore died in the fire. Afterward Alhaitham and Kaveh repaired their home, Collei was promoted to full ranger, and the Traveler ate at Lambad's Tavern.
