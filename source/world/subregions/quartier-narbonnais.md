## summary
### LITE
Quartier Narbonnais lies on the southwestern side of the Court of Fontaine. It is home to The Steambird, the city's main newspaper, and to Hotel Debord, a famous restaurant, and it is considered a place to find the newest fashion and perfume.
### STANDARD +
The newspaper's offices, a salon of civic debaters and a theatrical dining house sit side by side, so the district is where Fontaine's press, public talk and entertainment meet.

## places
### STANDARD
The Steambird: Fontaine's main newspaper, read across Teyvat; Euphrasie is its Chief Editor and Sir Arthur belongs to the paper; Ethan is a reporter there. Hotel Debord: a famous restaurant whose owner Vaneigem holds that art is the best seasoning for good food and often invites artists to perform; Sanguinetti hosts the food shop. Salon Caritat: the headquarters of an advocacy group that, by its notices, studies law and the administration of justice.
### LARGE +
The Steambird columns include All Things Astrological by Mona, who took over from the previous columnist, and it employs reporters, a photographer and a newsy (see its own entry). Hotel Debord has downstairs and upstairs areas: Eloise (a critic), Leone and Argalia (hostesses) and Rodamonte and Renaud are listed among its people. A notice board advertises a show promising that "the most dazzling twin stars" of the Court will reveal a secret. Salon Caritat's notice announces its 36th symposium, on budget overruns in the Maison Gardiennage and their link to public safety.

## people
### STANDARD
Besides the above: Justeau and Riqueti of Salon Caritat, Hubel (newsstand owner), Angelique, Chesil and Guillem. A discount applies at Hotel Debord for players with Reputation Rank 4 in Fontaine (a game feature).

## culture
### LARGE
The district is where Fontaine goes to read, dine and be seen: news, fashion and perfume are its reputation, and civic debate has its own club. An NPC (Sylvie) mentions its fashion and perfume.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Newsstands, printing-house bustle, polished restaurant frontage and the chatter of salon members.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 no change to the district is recorded.
### ULTRA +
The wiki ties the district's name only speculatively to a sword called Narbonnais in a Fontaine book; treat the name as unexplained in-world.

## entry: The Steambird
@keys: The Steambird, Steambird newspaper, Euphrasie, Sir Arthur
@keys2: Fontaine, Narbonnais
### LARGE
The main newspaper of the Court of Fontaine, read across Teyvat. Its offices are in Quartier Narbonnais. Staff include Euphrasie (Chief Editor), Charlotte, Chronie, Ethan, Paban and others; Nellie Cochran is listed as a historical Chief Editor who interviewed Alain Guillotin centuries ago. Mona writes the astrology column All Things Astrological. A separate entity covers the paper.

## entry: Hotel Debord
@keys: Hotel Debord, Vaneigem, Rodamonte
@keys2: Fontaine, Narbonnais
### LARGE
A famous restaurant in Quartier Narbonnais. Its owner, Vaneigem, believes "art is the best seasoning for good food", and the hotel regularly hosts artists. The interior is designed so guests can watch the performances. Staff listed include Sanguinetti (host), Leone, Argalia, Eloise (a critic), Renaud and Rodamonte.
