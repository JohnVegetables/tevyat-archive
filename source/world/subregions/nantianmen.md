## summary
### LITE
Nantianmen is the northwestern corner of Minlin, where an enormous tree with glowing blue veins, the Dragon-Queller, rises from the rocks. Beneath it lies the Geo dragon Azhdaha, an ancient friend of Rex Lapis that the adepti sealed after erosion drove him mad. A stone tablet by the tree asks passers-by not to disturb the evil dragon beneath.
### STANDARD +
The tablet reads that life endures in Heaven and Earth thanks to the merciful Adeptus, and that an evil dragon lies here. The Dragon-Queller is described as a tree that the dragon is slowly merging with. The name means "Southern Heavenly Gate", a term in Chinese myth for the gate between mortal and heavenly realms.

## geography
### STANDARD
Nantianmen lies in the northwest of Minlin, near Mt. Hulao. The Dragon-Queller is a massive tree whose branches flow with cold moonlight; at its tip a large blue branch points straight toward Liyue Harbor and cannot be climbed. Bright yellow flowers grow around its base.
### LARGE +
Its roots reach deep into the earth. Hilichurls, Treasure Hoarders and Eremites are found around it.
### ULTRA +
The wiki says the clash of elemental energies in the battle created Dragonfall, a rare ore; Zhongli explains in No Mere Stone that it forms through elemental reactions rather than heat and was mined to obscurity a few centuries ago.

## places
### STANDARD
- The Dragon-Queller, the tree that seals Azhdaha, with the Moonlit Tree viewpoint.
- The stone tablet at the tree's foot, erected by Mountain Shaper as a warning.
- Beneath the Dragon-Queller: the domain in the earth under the tree where Azhdaha is fought.
### LARGE +
Jiu, a girl who worshipped Azhdaha, once stood by the tree in the open world before 1.5. The roots draw on ley lines.

## people
### STANDARD
Jiu, a young-looking girl who was Azhdaha's vessel, was seen here before the events of Zhongli's story quest. The miners of the Chasm were lured here. Mountain Shaper and Moon Carver sealed the dragon long ago.
### LARGE +
Kun Jun, a man possessed by the benevolent remnant of Azhdaha's mind, searched for Dragonfall in Liyue. The Millelith of Liyue Harbor, such as Jiayi, send people to check on the region.

## history
### STANDARD
Azhdaha, a Geo dragon ruling much of what is now Liyue, was woken from a long sleep and gained sight from Morax, whom he befriended and swore never to harm Liyue. Erosion made him forget, and mining in the Chasm hurt the ley lines and drove him into a rage. Morax, Mountain Shaper, Moon Carver and another adeptus fought him from the Chasm north to Nantianmen. With a shred of sanity he accepted his fate, and was sealed in the Dragon-Queller. Mountain Shaper set up the tablet.
### LARGE +
Over centuries the seal weakened. Azhdaha manifested a vessel, Jiu, to announce his return, found that nobody remembered him, and had Jiu lead missing miners from the Chasm to unearth the seal. In Zhongli's story quest (1.5) the Traveler and Zhongli fought him below the tree, assisted by his benevolent remnant in Kun Jun's body, and resealed him.
### ULTRA +
He is said to believe that Morax betrayed him, because erosion erased his memory of consenting to the seal. A flashback at the Moonlight Merriment event (2.1) shows him gazing at the night sky with the goddess Marchosius riding on his shoulder.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
A strange, moonlit stillness around a vast living tree whose branches glow cold blue, with a feeling that something huge is waiting beneath. The viewpoint text says its roots reach into the heart of the earth, perhaps joined to something that awaits an opportunity to emerge and bask in moonlight.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Hilichurls, Cryo and Geo slimes, Treasure Hoarders, Eremites, Stonehide Lawachurls, Rock Shieldwall Mitachurls and Ruin Hunters wander the area. The deeper danger is the sealed dragon, whose seal weakened over time.
### LARGE +
After the 1.5 events Azhdaha was resealed, but the Dragon-Queller stays dangerous to disturb.

## travel
### STANDARD
The tree stands between Mt. Hulao and Nantianmen proper. The Trounce Domain beneath it is entered through the tree.

## quests
### LARGE
- The Secret of Nantianmen (world quest, 1.1): Jiayi asks the Traveler to check on reports of earthquakes; they find nothing unusual.
- No Mere Stone (Zhongli's story quest, 1.5): the missing miners, Jiu, Kun Jun and the fight with Azhdaha.
### ULTRA +
Moonlight Merriment (2.1): Azhdaha appears in a cinematic with Marchosius.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 Azhdaha remains sealed under the Dragon-Queller after being resealed in 1.5. No content from 6.x or 7.0-7.1 changes the site.
### STANDARD +
Jiu faded away, and the benevolent remnant said goodbye to Morax.

## entry: Azhdaha and the Seal
@keys: Azhdaha, Dragon-Queller, Geo Dragon
@keys2: Nantianmen
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Azhdaha is an ancient earth dragon, once Morax's friend and ally in the Archon War. Erosion made him forget Morax and mortals; when miners damaged the ley lines in the Chasm he attacked humans, and Morax and the adepti sealed him in the Dragon-Queller. Centuries later his vessel Jiu tried to free him. He was beaten by the Traveler, Zhongli and his own benevolent remnant, and resealed. The tablet by the tree was set up by Mountain Shaper to warn the world.
### LARGE +
Azhdaha was born of elemental crystallisation and slept underground for thousands of years until his stirring caused earthquakes. Morax gave him eyes, they made a contract that he would never harm Liyue, and Morax warned that he would be sealed if he did.

## entry: Jiu, Kun Jun and the Missing Miners
@keys: Jiu, Kun Jun, Missing Miners
@keys2: Nantianmen, Azhdaha
@version: 1.5
### LARGE
Jiu, a small girl in a green dress, worshipped Azhdaha and despised Morax. In the story quest No Mere Stone, she led a group of miners from the Chasm to dig below the Dragon-Queller to free him. Kun Jun, a man possessed by Azhdaha's benevolent remnant and looking for Dragonfall, tracked her with the Traveler and Zhongli. Jiu was a vessel of Azhdaha's wrath; as her energy ran out she faded with Morax's name on her lips.
