## summary
### LITE
Lingshu Courtyard is a cave garden in Chenyu Vale's Southern Mountain, the abode of a long-departed adeptus known as the Herblord. A talkative stone Carefree Simulacrum guards it and asks the Traveler to chase scattered butterflies back into a medicine jar so a withered adeptal tree can bloom again. Entering for the first time starts the world quest Silently the Butterfly Crosses the Valley.
### STANDARD +
Only empty medicine containers remain of the Herblord, and a hidden vault holds her notes. The wiki thinks the name echoes the Lingshu Jing, an ancient Chinese medical text, which suits the setting.

## geography
### STANDARD
The courtyard lies in a cave reached from Yaodie Valley. Features: Seelie, a Golden Carp's Leap, a Windmill Mechanism and Rock Piles; specialties are Clearwater Jade and Glaze Lily; common enemies are Grounded Geoshrooms and Whirling Electro Fungi. Large hanging pots, stalagmites and jars fill the courtyard, and a withered adeptal tree stands there.
### LARGE +
After the quest's tree-reviving step, an Investigate spot in the water by the tree teleports the Traveler to Adeptus's Repose, bypassing miasma. A viewpoint, Herb-Housing Hall, opens.

## people
### STANDARD
The Carefree Simulacrum speaks in an old, formal and sleepy manner, boasts that it frightens off intruders, and wants the butterflies returned. Smaller Sacred Simulacra give riddling hints. It misses the Herblord, who left when she lost the power to keep her human form.
### LARGE +
The Herblord is a female adeptus who made medicine for mortals. She planted the adeptal tree herself and designed the butterfly-and-jar mechanism that nourishes it.

## history
### STANDARD
Long ago the Herblord used the butterflies to adorn the door of her abode; that mechanism was later broken. The tree withered, and the butterflies scattered once she left. The viewpoint text says her wish to help those in need never wavered, even at the very end of the Archon War.
### LARGE +
The Ancient Texts in the vault are the writer's private notes. In one, the writer grumbles that Fujin never stops talking about the Votive Rainjade. In another, someone (annotated by the wiki as Lingyuan) begs for a slimming tea, and the writer relents. They give a gentle, humorous picture of the abode's circle.
### ULTRA +
A third note says the writer spoke to a "master" about certain matters and has a bad feeling, the sort of thing Fujin would judge better. As a precaution the writer splits off some adeptal energy into the butterflies, so that if something happens the mortals of Chenyu Vale can follow them to the formulae left behind. The wiki annotates the master as the Mistress of Chenyu Vale.

## quests
### STANDARD
Silently the Butterfly Crosses the Valley: catch five butterflies (orange, golden, blue, green and purple) in the courtyard and put them in the medicine jar, then revive the tree, enter the vault and read the Ancient Text.
### LARGE +
The tree revives when the simulacrum half-recites a formula the Herblord left; a branch from it placed in the broken jar outside opens the vault. Rewards include a Luxurious Chest and the achievement When Comes Spring or Autumn?

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Quiet, overgrown and medicinal: cave light, butterflies, hanging jars and dripping water, and a chatty statue breaking the silence. This is a suggested scene mood drawn from the quest and viewpoint.

## status
### STANDARD
The Herblord remains absent as of 7.1. The tree and vault are open to visitors after the quest.
