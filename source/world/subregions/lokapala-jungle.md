## summary
### LITE
The Lokapala Jungle is the northern, coastal stretch of Sumeru's Dharma Forest. It begins at the enormous glowing mushrooms of Mawtiyima Forest, runs past Chatrakam Cave and the extravagant Palace of Alcazarzaray, and ends near Bayda Harbor, the port that looks across the water to Fontaine. Its name is Sanskrit for "guardian of the world".
### STANDARD +
Mawtiyima Forest is the jungle's best-known place: an area of fluorescent mushrooms whose official description calls it the forest defending northern Sumeru, once tainted by filth that corroded the whole world and later purified.

## geography
### STANDARD
The area spans the north coast of the Dharma Forest, starting at Mawtiyima Forest, near Minlin and Chenyu Vale, and ending on the western slope of a nameless mountain west of Bayda Harbor, where it meets the Desert of Hadramaveth and the Realm of Farakhkert. Local specialties are Rukkhashava Mushrooms, Kalpalata Lotus, Nilotpala Lotus and Padisarah.
### LARGE +
Mawtiyima Forest's monumental fluorescent mushrooms form a canopy of light that hides the sun and moon; the Electro Regisvine is its normal boss. Chatrakam Cave, a fungus-filled cave system, lies deeper in, with the Realm of Khab entered from it.

## places
### STANDARD
Mawtiyima Forest, the glowing mushroom forest. Chatrakam Cave, the damp cavern. The Palace of Alcazarzaray, a lavish palace in the woods owned by Dori and designed by Kaveh. Bayda Harbor, the coastal port facing Fontaine.
### LARGE +
The Palace of Alcazarzaray is shown with a purple garden before it, where flowers dance and warblers sing; a viewpoint (Memory of Alcazarzaray) frames it as a setting for a lovely encounter. Bayda Harbor is directly across from Fontaine, according to the quest Recollections of a Fontainian.

## people
### STANDARD
Aranara live in the forest, hard for most adults to see. At Bayda Harbor the NPCs include Shajar, the Port Master; Adel, a Mahamata; and Honorine, a Fontaine engineer. Eremites, hilichurls, Fatui and treasure hoarders also turn up.
### LARGE +
The merchant Dori owns the palace, and the architect Kaveh designed it, which ties both to the jungle. The Vanara, who consider mushroom picking a duty, are the main people who enter Chatrakam Cave.

## history
### STANDARD
The Palace of Alcazarzaray was Kaveh's commission from Dori, who asked only that it be large and extravagant. Late in construction the Withering spread overnight, wrecked the worksite and made it unsalvageable. Dori did not want to continue, but Kaveh had invested too much and took a loan from Dori to rebuild it, which left him heavily in debt. It is regarded as his masterpiece.
### LARGE +
Mawtiyima Forest, according to official text, was tainted by the filth that corroded the whole world and then revivified and purified. The Mawtiyima energy form is said to fight the Withering to keep the forest healthy and to communicate with the Aranara.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Mushrooms of many kinds, including poisonous ones, floating and other fungi, Mirror Maidens, Fatui Cicin Mages and Fatui Pyro Agents, treasure hoarders, Eremites, hilichurls, and the Electro Regisvine.
### LARGE +
Mawtiyima Forest is where the Withering shows up in its exploration features (the wiki lists The Withering among the forest's exploration objects).
### ULTRA +
Mawtiyima's other exploration features include Valaya floating-ring puzzles, Tri-Yana Seeds, Stone Pillar Seals, a Seed Mirror, a Phantasmal Gate, Auspicious Branches and bouncy mushrooms; poisonous mushrooms grow in the forest, and a viewpoint named Aranaga's Little Garden carries the line "Here, the pomegranate sings and the apples clap."

## travel
### STANDARD
Bayda Harbor connects Sumeru's coast to Fontaine by sea. Overland, Mawtiyima Forest is the way toward the northern border near Minlin and Chenyu Vale.

## quests
### STANDARD
The Risen Moon Chapter (Archon Quest) enters the Realm of Khab via Chatrakam Cave. World quests listed in the wiki category include Aranakin's Old Friend, Aranaga's Memory, Aragaru's Drawing, Garcia's Paean, Wealthy Merchant, For a Dream I Tarry and Memory's Final Chapter; only their titles were reviewed.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Dense and luminous, with mushroom canopies glowing overhead and a humid forest floor. Mawtiyima's grass seems to glow as if the place were aware of visitors.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the jungle is open to explore and nothing in later story content has altered it.
### STANDARD +
Nothing from 6.x or 7.0-7.1 changes the area.
