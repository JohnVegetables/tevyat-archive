## summary
### LITE
Qingyun Peak is the home of the adeptus Moon Carver, one of the great peaks of Jueyun Karst in Minlin. He appears as a stag, a disciple of Rex Lapis and, according to a tablet at the foot of the steps, the kindest of all the adepti. His abode is sealed from the world, and high above the peak a small island of Plaustrite called the Dwelling in the Clouds floats, reached only by solving a puzzle.
### STANDARD +
Moon Carver is the first adeptus the Traveler meets in Jueyun Karst in the Archon Quest.

## geography
### STANDARD
Qingyun Peak is a tall, cloud-ringed mountain in the adeptal highlands of Jueyun Karst. A stone staircase leads from the teleport waypoint up to a monument at the top. At the foot of the steps are tablets and a censer; the winding path east of the peak leads to another tablet.
### LARGE +
Above the peak, floating in the sky, is the Dwelling in the Clouds. The peak is one of the three summits of the three-cranes puzzle; the other crane statues are at the top of Mt. Aocang and on the sharp peak of Mt. Hulao. The adventurer Roald, climbing the precipice, found a sea of clouds, a few oddly shaped trees and Lapis Gledes crying overhead, and a bitter wind at night.
### ULTRA +
A tablet on the main road to Qingyun Peak, in Jueyun Karst, speaks of walking to the changing tide and the misty shroud before ascending to sit and gaze at the unfurling clouds.

## places
### STANDARD
- The foot of the steps: tablets, one reading "Black clouds too shall pass, just as silver linings end", and a censer where the Traveler meets Moon Carver.
- Moon Carver's abode: a sealed cave past the censer behind a barrier.
- The stairs and the Three Cranes puzzle, ending at the Dwelling in the Clouds.
### LARGE +
A second tablet near the Plaustrite steps reads: "Moon Carver, disciple of Rex Lapis. The kindest adeptus amongst all, in the manifestation of a stag." A third on the winding road east speaks of a southern shortcut and urges those seeking mysteries to watch the tides of the seas above and below.
### ULTRA +
The Dwelling in the Clouds carries a message from Cloud Retainer saying she built it as a private sanctuary, that the adepti used it to seek ultimate truth in seclusion, and that now Taishan Mansion is sealed they have returned it to her. She intends to travel again, starting with Guili Plains.

## people
### STANDARD
Moon Carver, a stag adeptus; in human form he appears as Houzhang, a middle-aged man with a streak of olive green in brown hair. Qilang, an herb gatherer, can be found on the peak. The Millelith pursued the Traveler here in the Archon Quest.
### LARGE +
Mountain Shaper of Mt. Hulao is a frequent companion; the two travel together in several Lantern Rite events. Cloud Retainer, whose creation floats above the peak, lent Moon Carver a Multi-Purpose Abode Cleaning Talisman that he has still not returned.

## culture
### STANDARD
Jueyun Karst is considered sacred and mortals rarely climb here. The Qingyun tablets praise Moon Carver and carry short verses about the view above the changing weather.
### LARGE +
A tablet reads that "Come sun or rain, all seems the same from the heights of Jueyun Karst." The Three Cranes tablet speaks of "the one who may ascend" as the one who kowtows atop three mountains, with the sun, moon and stars each shining and three divine birds sending auspicious signs.

## history
### STANDARD
Moon Carver was among the adepti Morax gathered to fight in the Archon War. He and Mountain Shaper are two of the three adepti who helped seal the dragon Azhdaha.
### LARGE +
When Rex Lapis was apparently killed, Moon Carver was the first adeptus to hear the Traveler's account, and was furious that the Qixing had accused outsiders without proof. He later helped the Millelith fight Osial and agreed to let mortals govern Liyue.
### ULTRA +
The Dwelling in the Clouds was created by Cloud Retainer for reflection, and Moon Carver and Mountain Shaper later persuaded her to lend it to the adepti for the trial of "heaven", with Rex Lapis' approval.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Remote and windy, above a sea of clouds, with stone steps and tablets leading toward a sealed cave. At night the wind above the clouds is cold and relentless, in Roald's account.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Slimes, Treasure Hoarders and whopperflowers roam the peak's paths. The adepti dislike intruders; the Millelith that followed the Traveler here were driven off.

## travel
### STANDARD
From Qingyun Peak's waypoint, a staircase climbs to the monument. The puzzle that opens the way to the Dwelling in the Clouds is solved by finding three shining crane statues and turning them to face the monument.

## quests
### LARGE
- An Impromptu Change of Plan (Archon Quest, 1.0): the Traveler shows a Sigil of Permission to Moon Carver, who drives off the pursuing Millelith and sends them on to Mountain Shaper, Cloud Retainer and Xiao.
- The Three Cranes puzzle and "No Honor Among Thieves" (Qingyun Peak version) are world content here.
### ULTRA +
Lantern Rite events (2.4, 3.4, 4.4, 5.3, 6.3) feature Moon Carver with Mountain Shaper; in 4.4 and 6.3 they appear in the city, using the aliases Houzhang and Jiehu in the first of these.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 Qingyun Peak is still Moon Carver's mountain. No content from 6.x or 7.0-7.1 changes it.
### STANDARD +
Moon Carver and Mountain Shaper still keep their peaks. Xianyun, the Cloud Retainer, lives in Liyue Harbor but retains care of the Dwelling in the Clouds.

## entry: Moon Carver
@keys: Moon Carver, Houzhang
@keys2: Qingyun Peak, Jueyun Karst
### STANDARD
Moon Carver is an adeptus with a stag form; his tablets call him Rex Lapis' disciple and the kindest of the adepti. The Traveler met him when fleeing the Millelith with a Sigil of Permission; he was enraged by Rex Lapis' apparent death and the Qixing's treatment of foreigners. In later Lantern Rites he is often seen with Mountain Shaper and wears a human form as Houzhang, a man with olive-green hair streak.

## entry: The Dwelling in the Clouds
@keys: Dwelling in the Clouds, Jadevoid, Three Cranes
@keys2: Qingyun Peak
### LARGE
A small floating island of Plaustrite above Qingyun Peak. Cloud Retainer made it as a place for meditation and later agreed, at Moon Carver and Mountain Shaper's urging and with Rex Lapis' approval, to let it serve as the heavenly trial for those who sought the adeptal path, building a small pavilion on it. The word "Jadevoid" for an adeptus' dwelling comes from it. As few can follow the path now, it is back in her care. It is reached by turning three crane statues to face the monument at the top of the stairs, which opens wind currents and platforms.

## entry: Moon Carver at Lantern Rite
@keys: Houzhang, Jiehu, Moon Carver
@keys2: Lantern Rite, Liyue Harbor, Xianyun
@availability: limited

### LARGE
At the 4.4 Lantern Rite, Moon Carver and Mountain Shaper took human forms as Houzhang and Jiehu and came to Liyue Harbor, hoping to find Cloud Retainer who had moved there as Xianyun. They overheard her and the Traveler discuss kite-making and were recognised by Paimon after they recounted shared memories. Later they flew a Fontainian-motor kite near Mt. Aocang and lost it, then played Treasure Hoarders at Xianyun's request in a staged attack meant to help Gaming and his father Yip Tak resolve their differences; Gaming saw through it, and the plan still worked.
