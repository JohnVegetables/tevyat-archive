## summary
### LITE
The Narrows is a ruined subarea of Enkanomiya. In the earliest times it was squeezed between the mountain face and an area called the Roundlands, which gave it its name. Strange tectonic shifts in Byakuyakoku later dropped the Roundlands into the depths, so the Narrows widened considerably. Its ruins hide a chamber that only appears when the land shifts between Whitenight and Evernight.
### STANDARD +
That chamber, sealed by a Sunfire Phantasm, held one of the Reins of Revival fragments the Traveler sought in The Subterranean Trials of Drake and Serpent. A hiding place used by Byakuyakoku's resistance also lies here.

## geography
### STANDARD
A broad ruin-filled stretch of the Enkanomiya underground, with cliffs on one side and, where the Roundlands once stood, open depths on the other. The land shifts between Whitenight and Evernight, revealing or hiding structures.
### LARGE +
The walkthrough for Lotus Eater notes a chest behind the afterimage holding a Secret Room Key, which belongs to a separate Hidden Exploration Objective, Adonis' Abandoned Safehouse.

## places
### STANDARD
The Narrows ruins, and the hidden chamber for the fragment; a hiding place opened by a lantern puzzle during Whitenight, where an afterimage waits.
### LARGE +
A Sunfire Phantasm conceals the chamber entrance, and Aru says its exact position must be found by the Traveler alone by switching between Whitenight and Evernight.

## people
### STANDARD
No living inhabitants. The guide Aru speaks of the Narrows, and an afterimage in the hiding place is met during Lotus Eater.

## history
### STANDARD
From the Byakuyakoku texts: in the earliest times the Narrows lay between the mountain face and the Roundlands. Tectonic upheaval brought the Roundlands down into the depths and widened the place.
### LARGE +
Aru says the people of Byakuyakoku later learnt to use Sunfire Phantasms to conceal secrets; the chamber's entrance is one example. Supada no Hiko's quest sends the Traveler to find where his 'children' are hidden; the hiding place is in the Narrows.
### ULTRA +
The Narrows is one of the three places holding Reins of Revival fragments, with the Evernight Temple and the Serpent's Heart. It appears again in Lotus Eater when the Traveler reaches the hiding place, collects the key to the safehouse, and then goes on to Tokoyo Reisen for the Waters of Lethe.

## quests
### STANDARD
The Subterranean Trials of Drake and Serpent (the fragment) and Lotus Eater (the hiding place).

## dangers
### STANDARD
The wiki lists no special hazards for this subarea beyond Enkanomiya's usual enemies.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Dark, broken stonework above an abyssal drop, with the quiet of a place where something collapsed long ago.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the Narrows are unchanged and the chamber is empty.
### STANDARD +
No 6.x or 7.0-7.1 content touches Enkanomiya.
