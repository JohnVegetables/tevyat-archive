## summary
### LITE
The Ashavan Realm is the southern part of Sumeru's Dharma Forest, a materially rich land of hills, woods and wetlands. Its western edge is defended by mighty ramparts, the Wall of Samiel, which keep out the corroding sandstorms of the desert. Its main places are Apam Woods, the frontier village Caravan Ribat and the Amurta research garden Pardis Dhyai.
### STANDARD +
Official expedition text says legend holds that many mythical creatures live in Sumeru's woods and that only a lucky few ever see them. The name comes from the Zoroastrian term ashavan, "possessing truth".

## geography
### STANDARD
A damp region of hills, woods and wetland. The Wall of Samiel runs along its western edge, separating the rainforest from the Great Red Sand. The Apam Woods rainforest, where it never stops raining unless the Varuna Contraption is told otherwise, takes up much of the interior, and the Ruins of Dahri lie to its west, linked by an underground cavern.
### LARGE +
The Ruins of Dahri are patrolled by Ruin Hunters and Ruin Cruisers, fungi float over them, and the Yasna Monument is mapped nearby. Pardis Dhyai's garden of countless flowers is a research site, and its local specialty is Padisarah.

## places
### STANDARD
Apam Woods, the rain-soaked forest with the Varuna Contraption. Caravan Ribat, a fortified village at the foot of the Wall of Samiel. Pardis Dhyai, the Garden of Deep Thought, a research site for Amurta scholars. The Ruins of Dahri and the Yasna Monument, two sparsely documented subareas.
### LARGE +
Caravan Ribat is the thoroughfare for anyone going between the forest and Aaru Village or the desert. Pardis Dhyai's official map blurb calls the garden "the paradise of people of goodwill".
### ULTRA +
The Yasna Monument's name borrows the Avestan name of a Zoroastrian ceremony and of the main body of its liturgical texts. In the Chinese name of the realm the word rendered "Ashavan" is a translation of Sudarshana, a Sanskrit term meaning roughly "good vision".

## people
### STANDARD
Caravan Ribat is full of Eremite mercenaries, researchers and merchants; the Amurta scholars work at Pardis Dhyai; Eremites and Forest Rangers also appear around the region. The Aranara, small forest spirits, tend the Varuna Contraption in Apam Woods.
### LARGE +
Caravan Ribat's residents include Maryam, who owns the Gilded Journey, and an employee, Maziar; several researchers (Behnam, Dunya, Juzjani, Ma'asudi, Railah); and Eremite mercenaries such as Nasir, Safie, Thiyya and Aybak. Pardis Dhyai's staff and students include Hazm, Isami and Kemia as researchers, Izad a Forest Ranger, and a mechanical crab named Karkata.

## governance
### STANDARD
The Akademiya actively maintains the Wall of Samiel and forbids its destruction. Caravan Ribat functions as the checkpoint between forest and desert.

## history
### STANDARD
The wall is supposedly the work of Greater Lord Rukkhadevata, built to keep sandstorms from ruining the rainforests. The Varuna Contraption, which gives Apam Woods its rain, is also her design; it fell into disrepair until the Aranara and the Traveler repaired it in Varuna Gatha.
### LARGE +
Official map text for Caravan Ribat observes that the great wall that defends against searing sandstorms has also blocked the spread of wisdom's blessing, asking whether divine wisdom was not meant to shine on all people.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Fungi that grow when the rain goes unmanaged, Ruin Hunters and Ruin Cruisers in the ruins, Eremites, wild animals such as tigers, and sandstorms at the wall.

## travel
### STANDARD
Travel to the desert runs through Caravan Ribat and over or past the Wall of Samiel toward Aaru Village. Travel in the rain-fed woods depends on the contraption's rain cycle.

## quests
### STANDARD
Varuna Gatha (3.0) is the region's main storyline: it repairs the Varuna Contraption in Apam Woods. Other world quests listed in the wiki category include Legends of the Stone Lock, Memory of Stone, Pride and Prejudice, Where the Future Stars Fall and Hilichurl Hullaballoo; only their titles were reviewed.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Wet, green and humid under heavy canopy, with a frontier town huddled at the foot of a long wall against the glare of the desert beyond.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the Ashavan Realm is intact, with rain regulated and the wall standing.
### STANDARD +
Nothing from 6.x or 7.0-7.1 changes the region.

A fortified village at the foot of the Wall of Samiel, full of Eremites, and the checkpoint through which people travelling between the forest and the desert, including Aaru Village, must pass.
