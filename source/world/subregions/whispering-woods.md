## summary
### LITE
The Whispering Woods are dense, deep woods in Starfell Valley, near Starfell Lake and Mondstadt City, cut by a small road. Wind in the leaves sounds like whispers, hence the name. In the Prologue the Traveler first sees Venti, then just a boy, calming the dragon Dvalin here.
### STANDARD +
Hilichurls and slimes roam the area, and dandelion seeds and small lamp grass grow here.

## geography
### STANDARD
A forest at the far end of Starfell Valley, close to Starfell Lake and the city, with a small road running through the trees. The wiki catalogues its own set of points of interest within it.
### LARGE +
Starfell Valley is described as stretching from Stormbearer Point to the ends of the Whispering Woods, which makes the woods the valley's wooded edge.

## history
### STANDARD
The in-game expedition text explains the name: travellers kept mistaking the fluttering sound of wind-blown leaves for whispers, so they began calling the place the Whispering Woods.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Hilichurls (ordinary Hilichurls, Fighters and Berserkers) and Hydro and Pyro Slimes.
### LARGE +
In the Prologue the area is an early combat test. After the Traveler and Paimon leave the forest, the Knights' Outrider Amber stops them and the three clear a nearby hilichurl camp.
### ULTRA +
The wiki lists no domain, boss or resident NPC for the woods; its feature list is limited to the five enemy types above and two gatherables (dandelion seed and small lamp grass).

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Dim green shade, leaves rustling in constant gusts and a narrow road, with the sense that the wind itself is murmuring.

## quests
### STANDARD
Prologue Act I, "Forest Rendezvous": the Traveler and Paimon saw a dragon fly toward the heart of the forest and followed it. There they came across a boy talking gently to the dragon, telling it not to be afraid and that he was back. When he noticed them, the dragon roared and the boy vanished in a burst of Anemo; the dragon flew away. A crimson crystal was left on a rock, which the Traveler took.
### LARGE +
This is the first meeting with Venti and Dvalin, though neither is named yet. The dragon soon becomes "Stormterror" to Mondstadters when it begins to rampage. The soundtrack piece "Reunion in the Whispering Woods" accompanies this cutscene and the later Prologue meeting before Dvalin is purified.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the woods are unchanged; no later story has used them.
