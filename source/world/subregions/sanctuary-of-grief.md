## summary
### LITE
The Sanctuary of Grief is the tomb of Signora, the Eighth Fatui Harbinger, in the Everfrozen Earth of Snezhnaya. It is an ice-sealed palace built after she died in Inazuma, where she lost to the Traveler and was executed. The remaining Harbingers arranged her burial here, and Fatui work was halted for half a day to mourn her. The mood is cold, quiet mourning.
### STANDARD +
During the Archon Quest Mistilteinn (7.0) Odette goes there to say a private farewell. In-game text frames the place as perhaps the gentlest end for someone who suffered the agony of loss and faded amid hatred and madness.

## geography
### STANDARD
Set in the Everfrozen Earth, the northern tundra of Snezhnaya. The sanctuary is a palace-like building entombed in ice. Frostfairy Flowers grow nearby. The wiki notes its architecture resembles Neo-Byzantine church design, a real-world comparison rather than a canon statement.
### LARGE +
The Eye of Graeae's database holds no description of the building, only a record that it was frozen on the day work across Snezhnaya was suspended for half a day to commemorate a Harbinger's passing. Do not invent where the ice came from.

## places
### STANDARD
The mausoleum is the centre of the area and the destination of Odette's scene. A viewpoint here is titled Where Frost Entombs All Sorrows. Near the mausoleum Odette passes a flame moth that triggers visions of past conversations between her and Signora.
### LARGE +
The area has its own mourning music: with Columbina in the party and conscious, her vocal line from the earlier Elogia Cinerosa piece joins it; otherwise it is a piano arrangement. This is a presentation detail, not lore.

## people
### STANDARD
Signora lies entombed here. Odette, who trained under Signora, comes to say goodbye. Arlecchino, who gives Odette Signora's final message, is the other person tied to the scene.
### LARGE +
In the cinematic's flashbacks Signora says she understands Odette's hatred of the Fatui, that she never opposed Odette's dancing though she became an enemy of her ballet, and that if she died Odette should be free to dance. Treat this as the quest's characterisation of Signora in this one relationship.
### ULTRA +
When the Eye of Graeae approaches the building it wonders whether the Harbinger being mourned was a colleague of the Director met at the abandoned station (Pierro), and after the Traveler guesses it is a Harbinger's tomb it records it as Signora's.

## history
### STANDARD
Signora lost the duel before the Raiden Shogun's throne to the Traveler and was executed in Inazuma. The Harbingers in Snezhnaya arranged her burial in the sanctuary; Pulcinella decreed that Fatui work halt for half a day, as the interlude teaser A Winter Night's Lazzo shows.
### LARGE +
Five years after her death her Harbinger seat was filled by another. In Mistilteinn, after the Snezhnograd events, Odette lays a letter and her Cor Ignis before the mausoleum, dances, then shatters the Cor Ignis with an ice dagger and leaves.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Silence, pale light, ice that does not melt and a faint warmth of flame remembered. Scenes here should be solemn and restrained; grief shows as stillness.

## dangers
### LARGE
The wiki lists no enemies for the sanctuary itself; only a local specialty, Frostfairy Flower.

## quests
### STANDARD
Mistilteinn (Archon Quest, Chapter VII, 7.0) has Odette visit the tomb: a flame-moth sequence of visions, then a cinematic ending with the Cor Ignis broken.
### LARGE +
The scene ties the place to the Fatui's grief for Signora and to Odette's choice to accept her past.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the sanctuary stands intact as Signora's tomb in the Everfrozen Earth.
### STANDARD +
Odette has said her farewell and left her Cor Ignis shattered. No later event at the sanctuary is recorded through 7.1.

## entry: Odette's Farewell at the Tomb
@keys: Odette's farewell, Spirit Seeker, Two Worlds' Flames
@keys2: Sanctuary of Grief, Odette, Signora
@version: 7.0
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
In the Mistilteinn cutscene Odette places a letter and her Cor Ignis before the mausoleum and dances Spirit Seeker. Flashbacks show Signora telling Arlecchino she hopes Odette can live to see the Tsaritsa succeed and, if Signora dies, be free to dance, and Arlecchino explaining that her own Two Worlds' Flames can burn away memories of the Fatui. Odette finishes, summons an ice dagger and breaks her Cor Ignis; the flames inside the ice burn the letter and become crimson moths. Odette's choice is to accept everything and move on.
