## summary
### LITE
Cider Lake is the large freshwater lake in Starfell Valley that holds the island on which Mondstadt City stands. Its water is crystal clear, cool and never freezes, and its headwaters are credited with Mondstadt's famous ciders and wines. It has become a symbol of the nation.
### STANDARD +
Official text says the lake looks unchanged for eons, so clear you can see the bottom, and is pleasant to drink.

## geography
### STANDARD
Mondstadt City stands on a large island in the lake behind a stone wall. Wolvendom's shore, where Ross the Quick stands by a waypoint, faces the lake, and the bard Davy overlooks it from the ruins at Brightcrown Canyon. An Anemo Elemental Monument and fishing spots are found along the lake.
### LARGE +
The Traveler says the lake's never-freezing waters are used to make drinks such as apple cider.
### ULTRA +
Two drinks bear the lake's name: a sweet, lightly alcoholic cider drink (mentioned in the quest Taste of Home) and Sweet Cider Lake, a juice and milk drink from the limited event Of Drink A-Dreaming (2.5).

## history
### STANDARD
No origin is given; the lake is described as ancient and unchanged.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Cool, clear water, windmills on the island and breezes across the surface.
