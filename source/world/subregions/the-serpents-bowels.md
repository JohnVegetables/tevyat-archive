## summary
### LITE
The Serpent's Bowels is a quiet subarea of Enkanomiya, the underground realm of Byakuyakoku. The wiki lists the Sangonomiya Shrine as its entrance. It is a damp network of caverns with silently flowing underground rivers, and no battle music plays here. It holds the giant Dragonbone Flower.
### STANDARD +
Its waters are home to fish, and the area includes a waterfall near which a rusted blade of the Iwakura Clan can be found, a link to Narukami Island's Byakko Plain. A note found here recalls how Watatsumi's people unwittingly fed the Bathysmal Vishaps.

## geography
### STANDARD
Caverns and tunnels of wet stone, a great gate and rivers that run in silence. The fishing point's in-game description suggests, as speculation, that these waters might link the place to the outside world.
### LARGE +
A waterfall lies near where the Iwakura blade is found. The Pathway to the Hidden Isles music accompanies the area.

## places
### STANDARD
The Dragonbone Flower: a giant flower atop which, in Lotus Eater, the Traveler pours the Waters of Lethe. A great gate, a fishing point and a spot with damp Scribbled Notes.
### LARGE +
The Hakuen Michimitsu Amenoma lies near the waterfall once the Hidden Exploration Objective Iwakura Art's Downfall has been done. Sango Pearls are the local specialty.

## people
### STANDARD
No residents. Only scribbled notes remain, left by an unnamed traveller who journeyed with Watatsumi's chosen hero.

## history
### STANDARD
The Scribbled Notes say the Bathysmal Vishaps suddenly became active, but a clerk of the Sangonomiya Shrine convinced the writer and the hero to spare them. The people of Watatsumi, throwing food offerings into the Moon-Bathed Deep, had been feeding the creatures, and the clerk said they could no longer threaten humanity, so Enkanomiya was best returned to them.
### LARGE +
Hydrological Studies in Byakuyakoku notes the sealing of the depths and the emigration of Watatsumi's people. The Dragonbone Flower shares its name with the flower a child sought in the folktale of Vol. 1, though no text identifies the two.
### ULTRA +
In Lotus Eater the flower, watered with the Waters of Lethe from near the Evernight Temple, condenses overnight, producing the Dragonbone Orb, which causes forgetfulness. The task is given by an afterimage at the Serpent's Heart. During the limited Three Realms Gateway Offering event in 2022 (2.5), the event map used Enkanomiya's layout without the Bowels; the area was reachable only by an unintended workaround, and was empty.

## quests
### STANDARD
Lotus Eater, in which the flower is watered; the Hidden Exploration Objectives Iwakura Art's Downfall and Adonis' Abandoned Safehouse touch the region.

## dangers
### STANDARD
No battle music; the wiki does not list any enemies here.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Damp stone, silent rivers and the sense of a place almost forgotten.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the area is unchanged: empty, quiet, with the flower still standing.
### STANDARD +
No 6.x or 7.0-7.1 content touches Enkanomiya.

## entry: Dragonbone Flower
@keys: Dragonbone Flower, Dragonbone Orb, Waters of Lethe, Lotus Eater
@version: 2.4
### STANDARD
A giant flower in the Serpent's Bowels. In Lotus Eater, the Traveler pours the Waters of Lethe on it, waits until the next daily reset and takes the Dragonbone Orb; the orb is said to make one forget. The Waters of Lethe flow near the Evernight Temple and do not themselves cause forgetfulness when drunk.
