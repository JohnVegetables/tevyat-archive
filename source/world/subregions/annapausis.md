## summary
### LITE
Annapausis is a flooded, hushed area in the Court of Fontaine Region, unlocked during the world quest The Narzissenkreuz Adventure. It lies where the Narzissenkreuz Institute, a Fontaine orphanage, once stood, and it is the setting in which the small Oceanid Ann leads the "Narzissenkreuz Adventure Team". The whole place has the feel of a story played out in a dream.
### STANDARD +
A statue of the Oceanid Lyris, who directed the orphanage about five centuries ago, stands near a small fountain southwest of the castle-like structure. The area is not labelled on the map until the player reaches it in the quest.

## geography
### STANDARD
Annapausis is split into two layers. The upper part is filled with water and can be crossed only by diving; floating above it, that water sits over a lower part that can be walked, climbed and glided through. Everything there feels damp even away from the water, as if drip-fed from above. Its landmarks include a castle-like structure and the Little Fountain beside a water statue of Lyris, southwest of the castle.
### LARGE +
Annapausis belongs to the Court of Fontaine Region and is entered from the nearby underwater waypoint northwest of the Court of Fontaine. Ann stays at the player's side while they remain in Annapausis and Chemin de L'Espoir.

## people
### STANDARD
Ann, a small Oceanid, and the other members of her self-styled Narzissenkreuz Adventure Team (Al, Jak, Petit Chou, Mori and "Colonel Kate") live out a play-story here in which they rescue "Princess Lyris" from the dragon Narcissus. The statue of Lyris recalls the real Oceanid who directed the orphanage.
### LARGE +
Ann was born from the dreaming of the Oceanid Mary-Ann, who is a merging of the real Mary-Ann Guillotin and Lyris. Ann carries the memories of both and does not fully understand them. Seymour, Mary-Ann Guillotin's companion machine, has said the orphanage ruins lie at Annapausis' coordinates.

## history
### STANDARD
The Narzissenkreuz Institute raised orphans and the children of criminals. It fell apart in the cataclysm of 500 years ago, when its director Lyris and vice director Basil Elton left and did not return as expected, and its location was lost beneath the water; later accounts treat the sunken site as Annapausis.
### LARGE +
Lyris survived, returned to Fontaine and circled the drowned ruins, saddened by what had been lost. Rene and Jakob, two former orphans, found her there and brought her into the secret Narzissenkreuz Ordo. A visit by Mary-Ann Guillotin and Seymour to the ruins is where the story told at the Little Fountain comes from (see the entry below).
### ULTRA +
After a battle with the Marechaussee Hunters, Lyris absorbed Mary-Ann Guillotin's personality, and the merged Oceanid Mary-Ann built a dream world called "The Real Annapausis" to hide from reality and wait for the others to return. Ann was created accidentally by that dream. These steps come from the Ann questline's wiki summaries; the order of events inside the dream is told through the quests The Narzissenkreuz Adventure, Search in the Algae Sea and Rowboat's Wake.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Still, dim and waterlogged; a hush broken by bubbles, fountain trickles and the cheerful play-acting of children's heroes. Names and ranks sound like a nursery game (a "Colonel", a "fell dragon"), and the sadness underneath is only partly visible.

## dangers
### LARGE
The tainted Hydro phantasms that attack Petit Chou and Al in the opening quest are "Evil Fangs of Narcissus" in the game's make-believe; they are Tainted Water-Spouting Phantasms. Mimic enemies such as a Hydro Mimic Boar guard key spots. Water immersion is constant.

## quests
### STANDARD
The Narzissenkreuz Adventure (Ann of the Narzissenkreuz, Act I, 4.0) introduces Ann, Petit Chou, Al, Jak and the setting. The questline continues in In the Wake of Narcissus.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 Annapausis remains a submerged, dreamlike place in the Court of Fontaine Region; no later change to it is recorded in the sources checked.

## entry: The Little Fountain and the Story

@spoiler: major
@version: 4.0
### LARGE
Southwest of the castle, near the Lyris statue, an interactable called "A Story" lets the player hear a tale narrated by Mary-Ann Guillotin to Lyris after the Institute's destruction. In it the kind Princess Lyris is beloved by friends (Al, Ney, and "Colonel Kate") and her realm withstands the dragon Narcissus because she shares her "time" with her friends. In the end she gives up her past and present, entrusts her "future" as a drop of water to her friends, and sleeps in her tower. The teller says it is only a story and leaves the rest for another time. The same fountain accepts the Sword of Narzissenkreuz, whose two forms (Pneuma and Ousia) answer with short, poetic lines; offering a Drained Conch Cup there yields a Surging Sacred Chalice.
### ULTRA +
Mary-Ann ends by proposing a new beginning set far in the future, where a tiny life is born from the princess' pearl of water and needs a name; this is a hint about Ann. Lyris, listening, is described as more interested in the story than in the lesson, so the teller invites her to write the ending herself.
