## summary
### LITE
Huha Hill is the resort's lodging district: a cluster of villas and other places to stay, with rooftop pools, outdoor dining and a fine view of Colorfall Cliffs, Guiztli Ridge and Wavey Bay. A loading screen calls it a great area for tourists and says its name comes from the 'hooh... hah...' sounds often heard nearby.
### STANDARD +
Souvenirs from other resort areas decorate it once certain quests are done.

## geography
### STANDARD
A hill above the resort looking over the cliffs, the ridge and the bay. Tete Isle is another part of the resort.
### LARGE +
The viewpoint is part of why the lodgings are popular.

## places
### STANDARD
Villas and lodging, rooftop pools and outdoor dining areas.
### LARGE +
Display items appear after quests: Starblooms from Colorfall Cliffs after To Colorfall Cliffs!, guests from Villa Guiztli with a Soothing Salve after To Guiztli Ridge!, a baby-koholasaurus wave machine from Wavey Bay after To Wavey Bay!, and a Joypop Bottle of Aphimead from Tete Isle after The Surprise That Awaits Us All!.

## people
### STANDARD
Tourists and resort staff.
### LARGE +
The wave machine's text says Mualani in particular wants one for her own house.

## culture
@version: 5.8
### STANDARD
Accommodation is expensive. According to Pumayna, some rooms cost 20,000 Mora per person per night, with fees for attractions elsewhere in the resort included.
### LARGE +
Aphimead, the festive drink, can be brewed in many flavours, and the in-game text says the glass shared with friends is the one that you never forget.
### ULTRA +
The wiki notes that huha means thigh or flank in Maori, which it lists as the name's etymology, but the loading-screen explanation is the sounds nearby. The two accounts are not reconciled.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Comfortable and relaxed: an upmarket stay with views of the cliffs, the ridge and the bay.

## quests
@version: 5.8
### STANDARD
Hosts reward decorations unlocked by the Sunspray Summer Resort story quests.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 Huha Hill is open as the resort's lodging area.
