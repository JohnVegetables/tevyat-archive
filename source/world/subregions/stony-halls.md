## summary
### LITE
The Stony Halls are rugged rock chambers in the Chasm's Underground Mines in Liyue. Dark mud and oozing concretions coat the stone, lumenlamps give light, and Abyss Mages hold the halls.
### STANDARD +
Its Chinese name reads as "Rugged Stony Hall", which suits the uneven chambers.

## geography
### STANDARD
Features here are Dark Mud, Lumenlamps, Oozing Concretions and Ruin Braziers. It is a subarea of the Underground Mines beneath the Chasm.
### LARGE +
Neighbouring subareas of the mines include the Ad-Hoc Main Tunnel and the Main Mining Area. The wiki page gives no description or history for the halls themselves.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Common foes are Geo Slimes; the elite foes are an Electro Abyss Mage and a Pyro Abyss Mage, both Abyss Order casters.
### LARGE +
The mage encounters make the halls a place where the Abyss presence in the Chasm is most obvious; see the Chasm and Chasm Delvers entries for the wider context.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Uneven halls of stone lit by lamp glow, with dark mud on the walls and the flicker of Abyss magic. This is a suggested scene mood drawn from the feature list, not a quoted description.

## status
### STANDARD
Unchanged as of 7.1.
