## summary
### LITE
Stormbearer Point is a headland in Starfell Valley. The coast just south of it is where the Traveler begins their journey across Teyvat with Paimon. A Church of Favonius nun named Vind keeps a storm watch here, and a Knight named Harry patrols near the waypoint.
### STANDARD +
Ruin Guards, hilichurl camps and old ruins share the area with a calm shoreline, and Storm Barriers wall off some of its treasures.

## geography
### STANDARD
A rocky point on the coast of Starfell Valley, with a lake, ruins and a hilichurl tower. It has a Time Trial Challenge and Storm Barriers, columns of wind that surround an object and vanish once it is reached, usually by gliding from height or with Anemograna. Valberries are a local specialty. The Stormbearer Mountains lie to the north.

## people
### STANDARD
Vind, a nun of the Church of Favonius known as the Storm Watcher, stands at the southeastern tip. Her family has kept watch for at least a thousand years at Vennessa's behest, ready to spread word of a storm across Mondstadt, but with Barbatos' gentle winds no true storm has come in generations. Harry, a Knight of Favonius, patrols near the waypoint, helps victims of monster attacks reach the city or Springvale, and gathers intelligence on monster camps along the merchants' road.
### LARGE +
Vind is devoted to the point and takes her duty to extremes, once preparing to charge a watchtower overrun by hilichurls before the Traveler cleared it. Harry offers capable adventurers basic self-defence training and a sword. Neither is a major canon figure.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Hilichurl shooters of several elements, Samachurls, slimes, a Wooden Shieldwall Mitachurl, Ruin Guards, a Cryo Abyss Mage and a Fatui Electro Cicin Mage.

## status
### STANDARD
As of 7.1 nothing new is told about the point; the storm watch continues and the Traveler's early journey is the only story the area carries.
### ULTRA +
The wiki notes that "storm barrier" is also the phrase used for the wind wall protecting Stormterror's Lair in the Prologue quest Song of the Dragon and Freedom; the point's barriers are an exploration feature, not that wall.
