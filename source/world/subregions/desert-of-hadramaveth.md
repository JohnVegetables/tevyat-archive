## summary
### LITE
The Desert of Hadramaveth is the southwestern part of Sumeru's Great Red Sand, opened up in version 3.4. Its dunes and scimitar-shaped ravines hide the ruins of the ancient city of Gurabad, and the nomadic Tanit tribe once lived in its camps. Official text says a lost city lies hidden there, with a wandering tribe seeking refuge in its ruins, and that incessant sandstorms erode all things.
### STANDARD +
The name blends Hadramaut, a South Arabian region, with the biblical Hazarmaveth ("court of death"). It has eleven subareas, more than any other area in the game. Its main story is the Dirge of Bilqis world-quest series, in which the Traveler accompanies Jeht to the Eternal Oasis.

## geography
### STANDARD
A broad desert plain crossed by canyons and wadis, with long sandstorms that the wiki lists as a regular feature. Ruins, obelisks, sandglasses, Jinn shrines and atmospheric vortices dot it, and wenut roam its dunes. The boss enemy is the Setekh Wenut, and the local specialties are Henna Berry and Sand Grease Pupa.
### LARGE +
Subareas include Gurabad (southwest), Mt. Damavand with its permanent tornado, Wadi Al-Majuj (a large canyon south of the mountain, whose name roughly means canyon of the Majuj, the hilichurls), the Sands of Three Canals (an elevated area north of the mountain), the Sands of Al-Azif (one of the flatter areas, where wenut roam and the Wenut Tunnels lie below), Safhe Shatranj, Deshret's Glass Goblet and the Tanit Camps on the southern border.

## places
### STANDARD
Gurabad, the ruined City of Brass and City of Kings. The Tanit Camps, the tribe's last settlement. Mt. Damavand, the smoking mountain with a tornado at its heart. Safhe Shatranj, the former Temples Forsaken, with a giant chessboard. Deshret's Glass Goblet, the sunken heart where the way to the Eternal Oasis lies.
### LARGE +
Safhe Shatranj is the canyon the Tanit call the Great Rift; a chessboard puzzle with five chess pieces is found here. Before the quest The Temple Where Sand Flows Like Tears it is called The Temples Forsaken, and the sand is cleared during that quest. Its pieces are said to correspond to real structures of Deshret's civilisation.
### ULTRA +
Damavand's name in the lost desert tongue means "the mountain from which smoke and ash arise". Deshret's Glass Goblet is first shown in Dune-Entombed Fecundity: Part I and opened in Part III. The Eternal Oasis (the Orchard of Pairidaeza) lies beyond it.

## people
### STANDARD
The Tanit are a matrilineal Eremite tribe who revere the Goddess of Flowers and are led by a Matriarch and nine Elders. They value words and knowledge but distrust the Akasha, trade crocodile products, spices and essential oils, and hunt wenut for spice. After the Dirge of Bilqis the Tanit are removed and the Vahumana researcher Tanjiyy takes their camps.
### LARGE +
Some desert tribes adopt or plunder young children and raise them as Falcons devoted to the elders; the Tanit's Falcon tradition is described as a perversion of King Deshret's priests' archery ceremonies. They have no written law, and their worst enemies are traitors from within. Jeht, the young Eremite the Traveler befriended in Lost in the Sands, belongs to the tribe in these quests; her father Jebrael is listed by the wiki as a former member.

## history
### STANDARD
Gurabad rose after Nabu Malikata's death and fell before the forbidden-knowledge incident that ended King Deshret's civilisation. According to the wiki, Ormazd ruled until Liloupar engineered his murder; his adopted son Kisra, who took the name Parvezravan and was in fact Liloupar's son, built Jinn-powered machines and was killed by his own son Shiruyeh. Liloupar's use of Abyssal power then sent much of the city into a chasm and a plague killed most of its people, after which Gurabad was abandoned and attempts to revive it failed.
### LARGE +
Later accounts, drawn from the same wiki page, record Shah Ramshahr, the Cities of the Seven Towers, and a succession of kings and Khagans who fought over the desert; they involve Ay-Khanoum, Tulaytullah and Saleh. The Akademiya once sent an expedition to Gurabad, noted in Zandik's papers, and a massive Ruin Golem still sits in the ruins, like the ones at Devantaka Mountain and the Valley of Dahri.
### ULTRA +
In Memories of Gurabad, Liloupar's story continues after the Traveler places chess pieces on the Safhe Shatranj board and clears constructs; the gate to Gurabad opens, previously illegible stone tablets become readable, and Liloupar leaves her bottle to reunite with her children there while her gadget still works for the player.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Sandstorms; wenut and the Setekh Wenut; Primal Constructs (Repulsors, Reshapers, Prospectors) and Eremites; hilichurls; Fatui camps (reports by a Fatui captain, Zoya Snezhevna, are among the interactables); quicksand; and the desert itself.
### LARGE +
In The Dirge of Bilqis, a Fatui conspiracy and Tanit politics turn a pilgrimage into a series of ambushes; see the Tanit entry.

## travel
### STANDARD
The Temir Mountains link the desert to the rest of the Girdle of the Sands; from the Tanit Camps the Dirge quests use Deshret's old canal tunnels to reach Mt. Damavand and the Glass Goblet. Before taking the quest the game recommends finishing Golden Slumber and accepting The Dirge of Bilqis.

## quests
### STANDARD
The Dirge of Bilqis (3.4): Wisdom Has Built Her House, She Has Hewn Out Her Seven Pillars; The Temple Where Sand Flows Like Tears; Dune-Entombed Fecundity Parts I to III; and The Eternal Dream, Ever Lush. Her Foes Rage Like Great Waters... follows it (Behold, the Sign Comes Like A Thief...; Make Bright the Arrows, Gather the Shields...; For Her Judgment Reaches to the Skies...). Memories of Gurabad, The Fallen Falcon, Tadhla the Falcon and the Culling of the Worms series also take place here.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Wind-scoured, haunted and desolate: ravines like scimitars, a tornado at the mountain's heart, and the dry hush of a city that once held kings. The official blurb hints at a story lost in time waiting to be uncovered.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the desert is an open, ruined wilderness without a Tanit tribe.
### STANDARD +
The Tanit's camps hold only the Vahumana researcher Tanjiyy; nothing from 6.x or 7.0-7.1 changes the area.

## entry: Tanit Tribe

@keys2: Desert of Hadramaveth, Jeht
@version: 3.4
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
A nomadic matrilineal Eremite tribe of the Desert of Hadramaveth, led by the Matriarch Babel. In The Dirge of Bilqis the Traveler and Jeht seek the Eternal Oasis; afterwards Babel, who had conspired with the Fatui against the other elders, tries to take the oasis for herself, and the Traveler and Jeht destroy the tribe.
### LARGE +
The Falcons are children taken in and raised to be devoted to the elders. Aderfi, Azariq and Yuften are among the named Tanit.

## entry: Gurabad
@keys: Gurabad, City of Brass, City of Kings
@keys2: Liloupar, Shiruyeh, Parvezravan
@version: 3.4
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
An ancient desert city in the southwest of the Desert of Hadramaveth that fell before King Deshret's forbidden-knowledge incident. Liloupar, a Jinni, plotted its fall over three generations because Ormazd had not freed its slaves; a revolt, a chasm and a plague ended it.
