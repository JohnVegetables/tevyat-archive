## summary
### LITE
Starsand Shoal is the western beach of Lempo Isle in Nod-Krai. The wiki records it as the place where the spaceship of the Traveler and their sibling landed. Its expedition description says that when a dark disaster engulfed Nod-Krai many heroes made their last stand on these sands, and that the whispering waves still sing of their valor.
### STANDARD +
It lies in the Southern Isles on the island's west coast. Stones placed by the Lightkeepers, the Tideseal Stones, stand to its north and south to dispel the permeation of the Wild Hunt, and Wild Hunt pockets lie close by.
### LARGE +
A small cluster of world content touches the shoal: a young reporter's notes recovered from the Fatui, a Remarkable Chest holding a boat-shaped workshop blueprint, and the first act of Durin's story quest A Mother's Last Wish.

## geography
### STANDARD
Open sand and shallows on Lempo Isle's western edge, with a Teleport Waypoint near the middle. Tideseal Stones stand north and south of it, each with a Wild Hunt area.
### LARGE +
A Remarkable Chest on the shoal holds the blueprint for Workshop Cabin: Ship Ashore, a boat-shaped sheet-metal building common around the Clink-Clank Krumkake Craftshop, said to be the result of a would-be iron ship that leaked before it left the shallows.

## history
### STANDARD
The in-game description ties the shoal to an unspecified past disaster: heroes made their final stand here when darkness overran Nod-Krai. The wiki records a later arrival: the Traveler's and their sibling's spaceship came down on this shore.
### LARGE +
The Lightkeepers' Tideseal Stones north and south of the beach appear in the world quest The Bell of Mourning Echoes, in which the Traveler gathers relics from three stones to honour a local legend, Sigurd, and later in the hidden objective Secrets of the Tideseal Stones.

## people
### STANDARD
Semyon, a child near the waypoint, hopes to publish a report on the Fatui. Alice, as seen in A Mother's Last Wish, arranges a meeting with Durin on the shoal.
### LARGE +
In the hidden objective Crimes Against Jelly-kind, Semyon asks the Traveler to retrieve notes the Fatui took, evidence of the Fatui's facilities and machines on Paha Isle that have driven out animals; he has just come back from Maroon Basin, where the jellyfish are barely hanging on. If the Traveler has finished Drifting Toward a Promised Sky and tells him what happened at Maroon Basin, Semyon concludes it is already too late but that the matter must still be reported. He asks the Traveler to credit him by name if they repeat the story.

## quests
### STANDARD
Crimes Against Jelly-kind (hidden objective, unofficial name; rewards Semyon's Notes and the Ice Cream Cutie blueprint), The Bell of Mourning Echoes and Secrets of the Tideseal Stones (Tideseal Stones nearby), and the start of A Mother's Last Wish (6.2).
### LARGE +
In A Mother's Last Wish the Traveler meets Durin amid fallen Radiant Beasts on the shoal, after Alice's invitation; Durin says the Abyssal power in him is stable since Albedo gave him his body but that his strength has been harder to control recently. Alice draws them into a story world made from the book of Durin's late mother, the author Anya "Marie" Andersdotter.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Wild Hunt creatures patrol the areas by the Tideseal Stones; Radiant Beasts appear in A Mother's Last Wish.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Sea wind and pale sand; waves quietly breaking where old battles were fought.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 it remains an open beach on Lempo Isle's west coast, with Lightkeeper stones holding the Wild Hunt at bay.
### STANDARD +
Nothing in the pages read marks any change to the shoal after 6.2.
### ULTRA +
Semyon intends to turn his notes on the Fatui's Paha Isle facilities and the struggling jellyfish of Maroon Basin into a newspaper article.
