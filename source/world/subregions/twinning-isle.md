## summary
### LITE
Twinning Isle is a limited-time island group in the northeast of the Golden Apple Archipelago, the summer-event map east of Mondstadt. In the 1.6 Midsummer Island Adventure it was a cluster of fog-wrapped islets and a central stage with a samurai puppet; in 2.8 Summertime Odyssey it held a castle of Night Ravens connected with Fischl's imagination.
### STANDARD +
The Immernachtreich of the 2.8 event is Fischl's imagined realm, and the Night Raven castle on Twinning Isle is where the event placed it on the map.

## geography
@version: 1.6
### STANDARD
In 1.6 the main island held a teleport waypoint, a cave beneath it with notes for the quest A Trip Through Fog and Wind, wind currents to its heights and Hymnal Rings; a south island, southeast islets, a northeast rock with a cave, and a Nameless Islet reachable only through sea fog completed the group.
### LARGE +
Between 10:00 and 14:00 the sea fog could be pierced by following wind lines, which led to the Nameless Islet with a mural and five chests. In event Stage 2 a Central Platform appeared southwest of the main island: a large stage with a Maguu Kenki at its centre, which could be fought after the Dodo-King storyline.
### ULTRA +
The 2.8 map built a castle on the isle in three stages. Before Immernachtreich Apokalypse was cleared, notices said the Twilight Theater's latest work was still in development and a bridge to a tower was closed for construction. Nachtraben Airlines and the Raven Forum are among the fixtures.

## people
@version: 2.8
### STANDARD
In 2.8 the isle's residents are Night Ravens: talkative and theatrical workers, guards, storytellers and a squire who hopes to become a knight like Sir Oz. They speak of the Traveler as having the Prinzessin's eyes, meaning Fischl's.

## history
@version: 2.8
### STANDARD
The Maguu Kenki on the Central Platform was, per the archipelago timeline, stolen by an Inazuma pirate crew about 500 years ago and later protected the islanders until it was damaged and went inactive. That crew's boat had split in two, and one group drifted to the southeast of Twinning Isle.
### LARGE +
Immernachtreich Apokalypse (the fourth act of the 2.8 event) takes the party into a mirage of Fischl's imagination, in which a narrator recounts her life and raven statues stand for her kingdom's citizens. Back on the isle, Fischl is glad to have faced her insecurities. At the castle's top the Traveler and Paimon try to contact Venti, and an unknown voice intercepts the call.

## dangers
### STANDARD
In 2.8, hilichurls, slimes, a Pyro Abyss Mage and Eye of the Storm and Stonehide Lawachurl elites. In 1.6, Pyro Whopperflowers, Mitachurls, a Cryo Abyss Mage, Dendro slimes and the Maguu Kenki on the platform.

## status
### LITE
Not accessible in the game as of 7.1; limited to the 1.6 and 2.8 event runs.
