## summary
### LITE
Ameyalco Waters is a coastal stretch of Natlan's Toyac Springs, in the land of the People of the Springs. It is a body of water formed where springs meet the coast, slightly warmer than the surrounding coastal waters, a warmth the official description attributes to magma chambers beneath. It is a place of surfing, Koholasaur riding and the Spiritway.
### STANDARD +
The name is Nahuatl, meaning "natural springs" or "at the spring". Toyac Springs, its parent area, is the home of the tribe that lives alongside the Koholasaurs and is known for hot springs and relaxing music.

## geography
### STANDARD
Warm sea-and-spring water on the Natlan coast, with islands (one in the southeast starts a world quest) and an Underground Lava River that the story quests reach through a whirlpool. Its listed points include the Upper Sanctum and the Underground Lava River.
### LARGE +
The local specialty is the Sprayfeather Gill. Exploration features are Everflame Seeds, Flaming Seeds of Phlogiston, Jet-Set Eddies, Saurian Indwelling and the Spiritway, a course of flowing Phlogiston for Koholasaur riders, used here for sprint challenges.

## people
### STANDARD
Residents named by the wiki are Anela, Aphila, Iwai, Pania and Rongo, plus the Master Trainers Avuia and Tepache, who are listed as "People of the Springs". A Koholasaurus also appears as an NPC.
### LARGE +
Mualani, the tribe's guide, and her teacher Nuu appear in the area's story quests. Rongo gives the world quest in which the Traveler passes a surfing test and a Spiritway test.

## dangers
### LARGE
Common enemies are wild Koholasaurs and Yumkasaurs with their whelps, Flowing Blade fighters (Grease-Saw, Harpoon-Thrower, Skirmisher), Swiftstep couriers, seekers and scouts, and Koholasaurus Warriors. Elites are the Anemo Hilichurl Rogue and the Flow-Inverted Wayob Manifestation.

## quests
### STANDARD
The Tribal Chronicle "Journey to the Mysterious Island" (Where the Springs Return, Act III, version 5.0, Mualani's story) runs through the area: Someone Trustworthy, Where the Koholasaurs Gather, The Riddle of Sea and Sky and The Mysterious Island's Truth, the last entering a whirlpool into an underground cave. World quest "Stride on Rainbows, Split the Waves" (Rongo) and the daily commission "Swelling Springs" (a Spiritway sprint) are also here.
### LARGE +
In Where the Koholasaurs Gather the party sneaks deeper into a valley and examines graffiti on a stone wall; in The Riddle of Sea and Sky, a trial Mualani activates Phlogiston Nodes to switch Spiritway paths and the party then rescues the client Viramdra and investigates the whirlpool.

## culture
### ULTRA
The Toyac Springs tribe is called the most popular with visitors from afar, and its guides, such as the top guide Koolau, take clients to hard-to-reach places; in Someone Trustworthy Koolau passes a client, Viramdra, to Mualani, and Mualani notes that even her teacher Nuu could not reach the Mysterious Island.

## status
### LITE
Open and unchanged as of 7.1; no appearance in 6.x or 7.0-7.1 story content was found.
