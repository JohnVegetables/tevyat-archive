## summary
### LITE
The Thalatta Submarine Canyon is an underwater subarea of the Court of Fontaine Region, within the map level of the Source of All Waters, the Great Fontaine Lake. Its name uses "Thalatta", the Attic Greek form of the word for sea. It debuted in Version 4.0.
### STANDARD +
Canon records only exploration content here: a group of Hunter's Rays with an impostor among them, and one of the seven Mysterious Cores.

## geography
### STANDARD
The canyon lies below the surface of the lake, so a scene there is a submerged one, with Hunter's Rays swimming between Teleport Waypoints. The wiki gives no description of its walls, depth, size or named landmarks.
### LARGE +
The English name comes from an ancient Greek word, and localisations render it as a submarine canyon or sea valley. No in-world explanation of the name is given.

## dangers
### STANDARD
An Underwater Survey Mek hides among a group of Hunter's Rays. Defeating it unlocks the hidden achievement "Like Tears in the Rain", whose name references the film Blade Runner.

## quests
### STANDARD
No quest is recorded in the canyon. One Mysterious Core, a quest item from chests after completing seven Pneumousia puzzles, is placed here as the seventh. The cores are handed to Henri to begin "Still Mouthwatering!", a quest set elsewhere.
### ULTRA +
The Mysterious Core quest item is described in game as a gear-shaped core taken from a mysterious machine. The other six cores lie in the Beryl, Belleau and Salacia areas, so this canyon is the seventh stop of a trail across the Court of Fontaine Region and surroundings, not a place with its own story. The Hunter's Ray achievement was added in 4.0 and is hidden until completed.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 it is accessible and has no known story events.
