## summary
### LITE
Mt. Lingmeng is a mountain in the Upper Vale of Chenyu Vale, Liyue, west of Qiaoying Village and southeast of Yilong Wharf. Its main landmark is Wangshan Hall, the weathered stronghold of the Guhua Clan, a martial school whose glory has faded. Local folklore also places an ancient cave and old spirits of the vale beneath its waterfalls.
### STANDARD +
The mountain, introduced in Version 4.4, is also the place from which, in the vale's history, the adeptus Fujin threw the Votive Rainjade into the water to save the people.

## geography
### STANDARD
A subarea of Chenyu Vale's Upper Vale. Wangshan Hall (a main hall and a secret chamber) is the area's only listed point of interest. An Ancient Cave beneath the northwest waterfall holds a mural, once cleaned, showing the people of Chenyu Vale performing the Votive Rainjade ritual.
### LARGE +
Chenyu Vale's climate is described as relatively humid. Wangshan Hall's main hall is unlocked during the quest A Wangshan Walk to Remember, and its secret chamber opens after Scrolls and Sword Manuals of Guhua.

## places
### STANDARD
Wangshan Hall: the stronghold of the Guhua Clan, in disrepair because of the clan's decline. Huang the Third, a clan member, tries to raise money for its restoration by luring people at Yilong Wharf to tour it with exaggerated claims of its majesty.
### LARGE +
The viewpoint "Past Glories of Wangshan" says that when Guhua was at its height, heroes from all walks of life gathered there to drink and sing, and the hall is still inscribed with the "chivalry" of years past. The clan teaches the three Guhua Greats (Light Piercer for polearms, Rain Cutter for swords, Life Ender for both), and its founder Guhua himself wielded a claymore, Rainslasher. According to the wiki the hall is where Guhua is said to have attained enlightenment.

## people
### STANDARD
Guhua Clan members live at and around the hall: Huang the Third, Wen the Fourth and others, a handful of practitioners trying to restore the clan's reputation.
### LARGE +
The clan was founded not by Guhua himself, who never took a disciple, but after his death by Boxuan (Master Baixuan), who claimed to be his disciple to carry on his legacy. At its height the brotherhood was famed across Liyue for chivalry; over a few centuries it renamed itself several times and shrank to a few people.
### ULTRA +
The wiki's member list also names Wakamatsu among current members; Chen the Sharp and Zhiruo as former members; and, historically, Yingmin (Piercing Blade, the 11th master) and Zhangsun Jijing (Rainbearer). The area's music track is titled Guhua's Legacy.

## history
@version: 4.4
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
At the end of the Archon War, in the story of An Ancient Sacrifice of Sacred Brocade, the mistress of Chenyu Vale unleashed a flood down the Bishui River. Herblord fought her while Lingyuan led the people to shelter, and Fujin climbed Mt. Lingmeng and hurled the Votive Rainjade into the waters; the flood was stopped, though Fujin lost much of her strength. The cave murals on the mountain depict the rite.
### LARGE +
The three-volume book Mt. Lingmeng Gossip, sold in Liyue Harbor, collects what its description calls far-fetched local folk legends. It says people of the mountain once worshipped many supernatural beings, that the old gods and spirits vanished in the Archon War, and that the leylines kept echoes of them, which sometimes appear as shadows in fog and rain and are taken as an ill omen. A second tale tells of a lost mariner who found a cavern of figures in ancient robes throwing jade into a dark pool, and of the physician Lan Jing, who entered the mountain in his youth and never spoke of it. A third speaks of a demon lord of the mountain beside a long-destroyed lunar chariot, ruler of the birds, beasts and adepti of wild nature, who is said to have been sealed beneath the vale.

## quests
### STANDARD
World quests Scrolls and Sword Manuals of Guhua (Arcane Archive puzzle) and Shrouded Vale, Hidden Hero (a trial and an ancient sword), plus A Wangshan Walk to Remember. A hidden exploration objective, Wangshan Swordsman's Inscriptions, is also tied to the hall.
### LARGE +
Shrouded Vale, Hidden Hero ends with a trial in four waves of enemies and an investigation of an ancient sword. After it, a Stonehide Lawachurl appears at the training place; its titles call it an uninitiated Guhua disciple.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Fog, waterfalls and old hall timbers, with a sense that the mountain remembers more than its people do. The village stories make visitors cautious of mist and rain.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 Mt. Lingmeng is unchanged, and the Guhua Clan still maintains Wangshan Hall as a modest school. No later story content was found that alters it.

## entry: Wangshan Hall and the Guhua Clan
@keys: Wangshan Hall, Guhua Clan, Guhua Brotherhood
@keys2: Mt. Lingmeng, Chenyu Vale
@version: 4.4
### STANDARD
Wangshan Hall is the base and main training ground of the Guhua Clan. It has a main hall and a secret chamber, and is kept up by the few members who remain. The clan's motto, as quoted by its former member Chen the Sharp, is that the noblest martial virtue is to pursue what benefits other people. Xingqiu counts as a member with the title Guhua Guru of Feiyun Fame.
