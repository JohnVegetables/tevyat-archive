## summary
### LITE
Villa Guiztli is an underground town beneath Guiztli Ridge, a small rocky island in Easybreeze Holiday Resort. It is inhabited by cardboard cutouts and Nochtlins, and offers an immersive experience in which visitors play heroes defending the town from the villain "El Capoctus Joquiratto".
### STANDARD +
It is unlocked fully by progressing through To Guiztli Ridge!, part of Sunspray Summer Resort Part II. Its viewpoint is titled "The Town in the Rock".

## people
### STANDARD
Residents seen in idle dialogue are capybaras and alpacas, plus Nochtli lackeys in the villain's service. Before the quest is completed the streets are empty because the lackeys keep robbing the place; the villagers advise lying low.
### LARGE +
After completion the capybaras greet guests, remark how lively and beautiful the town is, and an alpaca says peace has been restored. A capybara offers to change the colour of the town, turning buildings into Graffiti Zones or clearing them again.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
A staged, playful theatre-town: wanted signs, bandit lackeys and shouting about an invented outlaw, all set in a cavern. Treat it as an attraction, not a real settlement under threat.

## dangers
### LARGE
Joquiratto Lackeys (Nochtlins) guard chests and attack. A sealed chest challenge, Master Musketeer's Accuracy, asks the player to defeat 14 lackeys across five waves without injuring more than four civilians such as hostage capybaras and alpacas.

## history
### ULTRA
Guiztli Ridge itself has local figures including a fruit farmer, a guard and Achu. How the town came to exist is not stated; the Joquiratto story is presented as the resort's immersive show.

## status
### STANDARD
As of 7.1 it is a permanent explorable subarea of the resort, added in 5.8.
