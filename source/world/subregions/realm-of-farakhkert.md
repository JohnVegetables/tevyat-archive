## summary
### LITE
The Realm of Farakhkert is the northwestern area of the Girdle of the Sands in Sumeru, added in version 3.6. It holds Vourukasha Oasis, the sealed homeland of the Pari, as well as the thorny Asipattravana Swamp, the pine-covered Hills of Barsom and the sandy Samudra Coast at the far northwest border of the Sumeru desert.
### STANDARD +
The name probably derives from the Middle Persian name of the Vourukasha, the mythic sea. The Chemin Oublie tunnel, which connects this area to Fontaine's Romaritime Harbor, begins here.

## geography
### STANDARD
Samudra Coast has sandy beaches and clear blue waters at the northwest edge of the desert. Inland, the Hills of Barsom lie just north of Asipattravana Swamp, and the swamp itself lies just north of Vourukasha Oasis. The area's local specialties are Mourning Flower and Trishiraite.
### LARGE +
Wiki-listed exploration items include Amrita Mayflies, Bright Flame Altars, Fravashi Trees, Gray Crystals, Kory drums, Nirodha Fruit, Ruebright Bloom, Soul Bells, Sunyata Flowers and the Sorush gadget. Notable collectables include Korybantes, Khvarena Inscription Fragments, Plumes of Purifying Light and the Udumbara Pistil.
### ULTRA +
The wiki files the quest Awakening's Real Sound, which concerns Rashnu at the Hills of Barsom, under Gavireh Lajavard's quest category, a reminder that the Girdle's two areas share one storyline and that quest lists cross the boundary.

## places
### STANDARD
Vourukasha Oasis, where the Pari live around the Harvisptokhm tree. Asipattravana Swamp, a thorn-filled canyon swamp guarded by the Pari Mihir. The Hills of Barsom, home to a huge pink-leaved pine, the Barsom, and the Pari Rashnu. Samudra Coast, the beach on the far northwest. Chemin Oublie, the tunnel to Fontaine.
### LARGE +
The Order of Skeptics historically performed a musical ritual with Korybantes around the Barsom, believing it helped form a bond ("Laupa") with the Pari. In practice, the quest Awakening's Real Sound reveals that Rashnu found the noise bothersome.

## people
### STANDARD
The Pari are the main inhabitants: Zurvan the elder, Mihir, Rashnu and the young Sorush who joins the Traveler. The Order of Skeptics' researchers and their Yasnapati bonds are historically tied to the Pari.
### LARGE +
Pari are the sentient form of Khvarena and each has a responsibility to fulfil alone; they are the subject of the Vourukasha Oasis entity.

## history
### STANDARD
The area is the setting of the Khvarena of Good and Evil quests of 3.6. The Harvisptokhm, grown by Greater Lord Rukkhadevata from the remains of the former Hydro Archon Egeria, keeps the defilement of the Abyssal rift at Tunigi Hollow in check; the Pari guard it.
### LARGE +
The wiki lists a note in the underground Khaenri'ah ruins, signed by the Schwanenritter garrison commander Hadura, ordering the Mechanical Wardens' defences activated and survivors evacuated; the wiki's Farakhkert page places this note in the proving-grounds chamber of those ruins.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Abyssal purple mist, Pari twisted by defilement, and Abyss monsters tied to the Tunigi Hollow rift; Chemin Oublie holds Hydro slimes and Hydro hilichurl rogues.

## travel
### STANDARD
Chemin Oublie is the walking route to Fontaine's Romaritime Harbor. Samudra Coast is at the far northwest border of the desert. Vourukasha Oasis is sealed and admits only bonded heroes and pilgrims worshipping Egeria, except as the story opens it for the Traveler.

## quests
### STANDARD
Heart of Amrita, An Artist Adrift (Parts 1 to 3), As the Khvarena's Light Shows: Nirodha and Samudaya, and The Recollector's Path (Parts 2 to 6). The Khvarena of Good and Evil quests that run through here also touch Gavireh Lajavard; they are summarised in the Vourukasha Oasis entity.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Hushed and sacred around the oasis, harsh in the thorn-choked swamp, and bright on the open beach where the sea shows through the desert's edge.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the realm is calm, with the Sign of Apaosha gone and the Pari resting.
### STANDARD +
Nothing from 6.x or 7.0-7.1 changes the area.

Hills just north of Asipattravana Swamp where the Barsom, a huge pine with pink foliage, grows, with its familiar the Pari Rashnu. The Order of Skeptics once held a musical ritual here to bond with Pari, a ritual Rashnu found too noisy.
