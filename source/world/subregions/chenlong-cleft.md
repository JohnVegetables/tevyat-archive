## summary
### LITE
Chenlong Cleft is a hidden cavern in Chenyu Vale's Upper Vale, beneath the stretch of river that flows under Jademouth. The Traveler enters it by jumping into a whirlpool during the world quest Floating Jade, Treasure of Chenyu. Its Chinese name means Sunken Jade Gorge.
### STANDARD +
After that quest the cleft can be entered or left only by Teleport Waypoint. It holds a Golden Carp's Leap, Seelie and a Pyro Elemental Monument.

## geography
### STANDARD
A cavern below the river bed near Jademouth, part of the Upper Vale of Chenyu Vale. Features listed are a Golden Carp's Leap, Seelie and a Pyro Elemental Monument.
### LARGE +
Jademouth is the huge jade ring in the river that locals credit to an adeptus or to a giant carp, and the cleft lies below it. Folk songs in the quest describe two shrines at the north-east and south-west banks that hold down an eternal whirlpool, beneath which beautiful jade is hidden.

## history
### STANDARD
Chenyu Vale's ancestors, after great changes to the land cut them off from the heavens, took to casting jade into the river and praying for good weather, firm banks and fertile soil. The Votive Rainjade in the depths is the very last such offering ever thrown, according to the adeptus Fujin; the custom was abandoned once the long war to become gods began, though its name survived.
### LARGE +
Fujin says the Votive Rainjade grew far larger than she intended, swollen by uncontrolled adeptal energy that leaked through the spirit veins, and that this attracted or twisted nearby creatures. The quest shows such aberrations, including a boar-shaped "Beast Lord of Fuyu" made of adeptal power, and has the Traveler gather scattered adeptal energy to restore the jade, which then shrinks to a size that can be carried.
### ULTRA +
Treasure Hoarders at the north-east shrine, who tried to scare the party off in the name of an angry adeptus, left notes showing they did not understand the folk song. While the whirlpool is up, the river ferry services from Yilong Wharf are suspended; Little Mao says it will vanish after the work below is done.

## people
### STANDARD
Fujin, an adeptus in golden carp form, speaks with the Traveler here and says the jade should be restored. Little Mao, a local child, guides the Traveler to the site, and says a friend in the mountains taught him the stories.
### LARGE +
Little Mao is delighted to meet Fujin. She tells him that "adeptus" is only a title, that no one is born one, and that no one stays one forever.

## dangers
### STANDARD
In the quest's cavern scenes the Traveler meets Hydro Mimic Squirrels, a Large Hydro Slime, hilichurls and the boar-shaped "Beast Lord of Fuyu".

## travel
### STANDARD
Entry: jump into the whirlpool at Jademouth when the quest asks. After the quest, only the Teleport Waypoint takes you in or out.

## quests
### STANDARD
The cleft belongs to the second part of the world-quest series "Chenyu's Blessings of Sunken Jade", Floating Jade, Treasure of Chenyu. The next part is An Ancient Sacrifice of Sacred Brocade.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Cool, wet stone, bright water and a vast quiet beneath the river, with the faint glow of adeptal energy on the jade. This is a suggested scene mood drawn from the quest's events, not a quoted description.

## status
### STANDARD
Accessible only by waypoint after the quest; unchanged as of 7.1.

## entry: Votive Rainjade and the Rainjade Rite
@keys: Votive Rainjade, Rainjade Rite
@keys2: Chenlong Cleft, Chenyu Vale, Fujin
@version: 4.4
### STANDARD
The Votive Rainjade is the last jade thrown into the river by Chenyu Vale's ancient people. Fujin explains that it grew huge from uncontrolled adeptal energy; the Traveler restores it by gathering scattered energy. The Rainjade Rite is the ritual meant to repel the miasma on the vale and nurse the soil and water back to health.
