## summary
### LITE
The Hypostyle Desert is the central part of Sumeru's Great Red Sand, the land of King Deshret's ruined civilisation. It contains the giant Mausoleum of King Deshret, the underground Khemenu Temple and the resting place of Sobek Oasis. The ancient city-state of Tulaytullah once stood here.
### STANDARD +
The name is an architectural term for a hall whose roof rests on rows of pillars, fitting the area's pillared temple ruins. The Golden Slumber quest series (3.1) runs through it.

## geography
### STANDARD
A vast field of dunes and oases with a few plateaus. On a plateau north-east of the Sobek Oasis waypoint stands a stone tablet whose carved words promise that the golden sandstorm and burning sun will exorcise the darkness on behalf of the reader, guiding a spirit that perished here back to the Orchard of Pairidaeza. The Garden of Endless Pillars, a one-time domain, lies west of the Dune of Carouses.
### LARGE +
The Mausoleum is a giant pyramid; the Scarlet Sand Slate says it is only one corner of the palace, with many more temples beneath it. A great cyclone sat on top of it for a long time until the quest Dual Evidence switched it off for good.

## places
### STANDARD
The Mausoleum of King Deshret, a golden husk left by the king, where his intelligence is not held. Khemenu Temple, an underground temple east of the Mausoleum. Sobek Oasis, a rest stop. The site of Tulaytullah, now at the Garden of Endless Pillars. The Altar of Mirages, which may be where Ay-Khanoum's remains lie.
### LARGE +
In the Scarlet Sand Slate's description, Khemenu Temple is not the largest of the desert's countless temples but is intimately linked to the people, who enter, lower their heads and praise King Deshret's boundless wisdom. Its wall murals depict the king's reign.
### ULTRA +
The Garden of Endless Pillars has one level, Desert Vivarium, and the wiki notes that five Pyro monuments must be lit to open it and that a Dendro character is needed to finish it.

## people
### STANDARD
The Eremites roam the area, and the Tighnarians were once associated with Tulaytullah. The followers of King Deshret live mostly at Aaru Village in the Land of Lower Setekh, not here. Travelling adventurers sometimes rest at Sobek Oasis, as one of Cyno's character stories notes.
### LARGE +
In the Golden Slumber quests the archaeologist Tirzad, the Eremite Jebrael, his daughter Jeht and the merchant Nachtigal travel in the desert with the Traveler. Fatui soldiers and the Eremite Samail appear in The Secret of Al-Ahmar.

## history
### STANDARD
Tulaytullah, the City of Sapphire, rose to prominence in the desert after King Deshret's civilisation fell. The priest Hermanubis founded the original Temple of Silence there and made it a city of wisdom, and the Temple kept the knowledge that survived the king's fall. After wars between city-states and Eremite tribes, the Temple's members left, and the city declined like the other desert kingdoms.
### LARGE +
Legend says the city arose from the tears of Nabu Malikata. Ay-Khanoum, the Amphitheater City, was built for the Jinn by Nabu Malikata and King Deshret, and its name means Moon Maiden in the Jinn's old tongue; it was likely in this desert. A few generations after Gurabad's fall, Ay-Khanoum was ruled by Garshasp, who went on to unite several desert cities.
### ULTRA +
Tulaytullah's elite later paid tribute to Gurabad's kings; a power struggle followed the death of the king Badanah, and refugees from Tulaytullah and other declining desert kingdoms eventually founded Aaru Village. Layla's blue hair and pointed ears are said to mark her descent from Tulaytullah. The Eremite Shimti tribe traced its ancestry to the city; the Iab tribe massacred them about twenty years before the present day.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Primal Constructs (Repulsors, Prospectors) in the ruins, Fatui and Eremite groups, scorpions and desert beasts, sunstroke, thirst, and the Mausoleum's old defences. The Garden of Endless Pillars domain has fungi.

## travel
### STANDARD
Teleport waypoints are unlocked by stepping through Khemenu Temple and the Mausoleum. Travellers rest at Sobek Oasis. The Scarlet Sand Slate opens many of the temples and chambers.

## quests
### STANDARD
Golden Slumber (3.1): An Introduction to Indoor Archaeology, The Secret of Al-Ahmar and Dreams Beneath the Searing Sand lead through Khemenu Temple and the Mausoleum to Khaj-Nisut. Others tied here include Truth Amongst the Pages of Purana, A Tower Unto Wisdom and Folly, Headlong Into the Sands, Darkness Looming and Invisible Barrier.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
A hush of pillars, gold light and wind over dunes, with Khemenu's murals and the giant cyclone's old roar giving way to quiet oases. The Mausoleum feels like a monument to a dream that outlasted its dreamer.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the desert is open and the Mausoleum's cyclone is gone.
### STANDARD +
Nothing from 6.x or 7.0-7.1 changes the area.
