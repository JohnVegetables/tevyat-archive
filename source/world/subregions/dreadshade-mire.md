## summary
### LITE
Dreadshade Mire is a fog-shrouded swamp in the Voidsea Outlook area of Nod-Krai. Solovei, the Torchforger, and Aila, the first Moonchanter of the Frostmoon Scions, used three ancient stone seals there to imprison the Serpent of All Evils. Wild Hunt creatures roam the fog, and the Lightkeepers' signboards at the mire's edge warn that entry is not advised.
### STANDARD +
The Serpent was once a man named Raimo, a rare human high official under the Belyi Tsar and a disciple of Rotwang. The seal held for 500 years until Fatui activity in the mire weakened it, which is the setting of the 6.3 world quest To Turn Each Sin Against the Sinner.
### LARGE +
The three sealing pillars each carry an inscription naming one of Raimo's three great sins; the seal had held for 500 years, but in 6.3 the Lightkeepers found it failing.

## geography
### STANDARD
A swamp thick with fog, with low visibility. Three sealing pillars stand at the northeast, northwest and south; the Serpent's lair lies deeper in. Local flora and fauna include Cottonspring Flowers, Glimmerfruit, Mandragora, Spirebloom, Slothsheep and Winter Icelea.
### LARGE +
The edge of the mire is posted with Lightkeeper signboards warning of low visibility and Wild Hunt creatures. A Fatui camp stands near the second pillar and was abandoned in a hurry. Puzzles include Abyssal Fog Barriers, Lunar Conflux Barriers and Overflowing Abyssal Power.

## history
### STANDARD
Over 500 years ago Raimo used coercion, entrapment and murder to remove Rotwang's political opponents under the Belyi Tsar. After Rotwang was defeated by the Torchforger, Aila and Alia, Raimo transformed into the Serpent of All Evils and went to the mire, where Solovei and Aila sealed him with three stone pillars. A pillar spell is engraved on each, naming one of his sins: using blood ties to coerce, spreading slander to wreck trust, and pushing others to their deaths.
### LARGE +
In the quest the Lightkeepers Janusz and his apprentice Oskari are on patrol checking the seal when it fails. Janusz is badly hurt; the Traveler restores the pillars by purifying them with kuuvahki, leading the prism to the third, with Spirebloom flowers later weakening the Serpent during the fight. Oskari says the seal had stayed stable for five hundred years.
### ULTRA +
The Serpent's ranting during the fight accuses Rotwang of using him: it laments that it trusted his teachings, that the sins were never really its own and that its enemies were set before it. When defeated, a final whisper asks 'why, Rotwang'. At the end of The Last Night, the First Light, the Serpent of All Evils joins the Ultimate Calamity in the last fight against the Traveler and Illuga.

## people
### STANDARD
Lightkeepers Janusz, a joker who misses a cat named Jelly that left him over ten years ago, and his earnest apprentice Oskari, who writes the report. The Lightkeepers suspect Fatui involvement; they plan to investigate and file a protest.
### LARGE +
Fatui survivors flee from a chasing monster near the first and second pillars; the camp held a Prospector's Command ordering the discovery of new pillars and observation of local kuuvahki without alerting the Lightkeepers.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Wild Hunt creatures (Wilderness Exiles, Wilderness Hunters), Fishers of Hidden Depths, Radiant Glacial Wolves, Treasure Hoarders, Evil Eyes of Blightseep and Abyssal Riftlines. The thick fog makes it easy to wander into trouble.
### LARGE +
Before the 6.3 victory the Serpent of All Evils itself, an enlarged Evil Eye of Blightseep with a special core, haunted the swamp's depths and could revive itself once.

## quests
### STANDARD
To Turn Each Sin Against the Sinner (Janusz and Oskari; purify the three sealing pillars, then fight the Serpent) is the mire's story quest.
### LARGE +
It can start at the first or second pillar or by finding Janusz, 'initially ???' near the third.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Thick fog, wet ground and glimmering plants such as Spirebloom, with the stone pillars standing as the mire's only landmarks.

## travel
### LARGE
Not advised by the Lightkeepers. When Janusz is wounded he asks the Traveler to fetch reinforcements from Piramida.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the three pillars are restored and the Serpent of All Evils is defeated, but the mire is still fog-bound and dangerous.
### STANDARD +
The Lightkeepers still plan to investigate the Fatui camp that may have weakened the seal.
### ULTRA +
The pages read show no later quest through 7.1 that uses the mire as a setting.

## entry: The Serpent of All Evils
@keys: Serpent of All Evils, Raimo
@version: 6.3
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Raimo was a human official of the Belyi Tsar's era and Rotwang's disciple who used coercion, entrapment and murder for his master. After Rotwang fell he became the Serpent of All Evils, a monster resembling a giant Evil Eye, and was sealed in Dreadshade Mire by Solovei and Aila. The Traveler defeated it in To Turn Each Sin Against the Sinner.

## entry: Sealing Pillars of Dreadshade Mire
@keys: Sealing Pillar, Ancient Incantations, Dreadshade Mire pillars
@keys2: Dreadshade Mire, Serpent of All Evils
@version: 6.3
### STANDARD
Three stone pillars hold the Frostmoon Scions' seal on the Serpent of All Evils. Each is inscribed with a verse naming one of Raimo's three great sins and the words that the evidence of his crimes will be engraved upon the reefs, inscribed within the lantern's shade, and preserved beneath the moonlight. Janusz says the seal itself needs no special power: Solovei and Aila left their strength in the pillars, so channeling kuuvahki into each one restores it.
