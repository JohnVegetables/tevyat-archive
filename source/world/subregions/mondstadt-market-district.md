## summary
### LITE
The Mondstadt Market District is the shopping quarter at the lower end of Mondstadt City. Its two-storey buildings have tan walls and red-tiled roofs and house shops, a tavern and the stalls of merchants and craftsmen.
### STANDARD +
It occupies the lower end of the city, where locals buy everyday goods and the city's craftspeople work.

## places
### STANDARD
Named businesses include Mondstadt General Goods (Blanche), Floral Whisper (owner Flora), Cat's Tail tavern (boss Margaret), With Wind Comes Glory (owner Marjorie), the Good Hunter (waitress Sara), a blacksmith (Wagner, with apprentice Schulz) and a jeweller (Shiliu). The city also has notice boards, among them the Cat's Tail Message Board, Good Hunter's Daily Recommendation and the Mondstadt General Goods Order Board.
### LARGE +
Timaeus is a scholar of alchemy; Helen is a bard; Stanley, an adventurer, sits outside the bar by day. Knights Bruce, Huffman and Miles are seen here, with Hertha, coordinator of the Knights of Favonius, and Katheryne, receptionist of the Adventurers' Guild.

## people
### STANDARD
Shopkeepers, craftsmen and shoppers fill the streets by day, with extras such as Anna, Anthony, Beatrice and Donna in the crowd.
### LARGE +
Flora's elder sister Chloris sells flowers and berries at Windrise instead. Xi'er is listed as a gourmet in the district.
### ULTRA +
The wiki's full list of daytime market figures also names Eury, Nimrod, Norma, Patton and Quinn; none of them is given a distinct background in the official listing.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Busy daytime shopfronts, shoppers and craftsmen at work, with bards and knights passing through.
