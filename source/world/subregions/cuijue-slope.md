## summary
### LITE
Cuijue Slope is a stark hillside in Minlin, Liyue, ringed by nine tall stone pillars around a central temple. Walking near them turns the sky dim and grey. Local lore offers almost nothing about them beyond a tablet saying they were raised to hold nine human desires at bay, so that war would never return, and that whoever breaks them should take the treasure inside and protect Liyue.
### STANDARD +
Opening the pillars needs Stones of Remembrance, which the player gets by making offerings at Liyue's Statues of the Seven. The treasure chamber's chief prize is a very old ring.

## geography
### STANDARD
A subarea of Minlin. The Nine Pillars of Peace stand in a valley with a temple at the centre. Near the pillars the sky turns dark and grey and the land outside the slope takes on an unnaturally dim look, an effect that fades with distance. From the slope, Dragonspine and Wangshu Inn can be seen in the distance.
### LARGE +
In an early test build of the game the pillars stood closer together and the valley was shallower and dry, with no special sky effect; the current layout is the released one. The wiki records Pyro Regisvine as the area's boss and lists Cor Lapis, Qingxin and Violetgrass as local specialties.

## places
### STANDARD
The Nine Pillars of Peace (a point of interest in their own right) and the ruined temple at their centre.
### LARGE +
The temple's entrance is built in a style the wiki says is otherwise seen only in Chenyu Vale (for example at Chizhang Wall), and its door bears an insignia found nowhere else. Inside, greenish-blue lighting, unlike any other ruin, leads to a chamber with a large Pan Chang knot ringed by four more, and two yaksha statues guarding the doorway to the treasure room.

## history
### STANDARD
The tablet at the centre states the purpose in the builders' own voice. All turmoil comes from human vice; nine pillars are raised to suppress conflict so that armies are never again raised against each other. Inside the temple a second inscription names the nine desires: greed, nostalgia, vision, jealousy, anger, lust, self-aggrandizement, competition and turmoil. They can "heal the world, or do it great hurt," and the pillars are laid down to end the conflicts that plague it. A warning follows: if a later visitor breaks the pillars and enters, war will consume the world again, so any reader who does so should take the treasure and bring peace to Liyue.
### LARGE +
Who built them and when is not stated. A note on the Chinese text says the last line reads more literally as "defend Liyue in the eight directions" than "bring peace." The wiki notes that the mask on each pillar, where a Stone of Remembrance is set, resembles a squashed yaksha mask like Xiao's, and that Stones of Remembrance come from offerings made to Liyue's Statues of the Seven, suggesting a link to Morax; this is the wiki's inference, not an in-game statement.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Oppressive and ceremonial: grey sky, silent stone, a sealed temple, and an inscription that frames treasure-hunting as a duty and a risk at once.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Ordinary hilichurls, Fatui skirmishers, Treasure Hoarders and Abyss mages roam the slope. Inside the temple the Traveler faces an inactive Ruin Guard that wakes when approached, plus Geovishap Hatchlings and a Ruin Hunter in waves.

## quests
### STANDARD
Nine Pillars of Peace (World Quest, available once the Prologue's first act is done). The player sets nine Stones of Remembrance, wins the chamber's prize and then chooses whom to sell the Dull Ring to, Bolai or Linlang, both in Liyue Harbor.
### LARGE +
The ring is described as ancient yet well preserved, set with a fine gemstone, with an inscription inside the band: "Disaster runs rampant throughout the world, and all who live do so in agony." Linlang recognises the place as the ruin with nine pillars and a central tablet and says few dare to hunt treasure there.
### ULTRA +
The quest asks the player to pick a buyer: Linlang pays 200,000 Mora, while Bolai pays 180,000 Mora and five Adeptus' Temptation dishes. The choice is purely a gameplay reward decision and has no story consequence.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the slope is unchanged and still open to exploration. No later story content was found that alters it.

## entry: Nine Pillars of Peace
@keys: Nine Pillars of Peace, Stone of Remembrance, Dull Ring
@keys2: Cuijue Slope, Liyue, pillar
### STANDARD
The pillars are a sealed monument whose builders meant them as a lock on human conflict. Breaking one by inlaying a Stone of Remembrance cracks the mask on its face and breaks away a section to reveal glowing geometry beneath. The temple's chamber holds the treasure and the Dull Ring. The inscriptions treat the pillars as a deliberate gamble: a peace maintained by sealing away what could end it.
