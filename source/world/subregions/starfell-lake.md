## summary
### LITE
Starfell Lake is a lake and marsh in Starfell Valley, Mondstadt, near Mondstadt City and the Whispering Woods. A Statue of The Seven stands here. In the Prologue it is where the Traveler first felt Anemo power answer and glimpsed a green dragon flying toward a distant forest.
### STANDARD +
Its viewpoint is titled "The Marsh of Celestial Guardians", and local verse links the water to falling stars. Calla Lilies grow here.

## geography
### STANDARD
A lake with marshy surroundings in the Starfell Valley subarea, near the capital and the Whispering Woods. The Statue of The Seven is the main feature, and Calla Lily is the local plant specialty.
### LARGE +
The viewpoint is catalogued as "The Marsh of Celestial Guardians"; the Chinese name literally reads "Starfall Lake". The wiki treats the lake as its own subarea, separate from Cider Lake, where the city stands.
### ULTRA +
The wiki's catalogue for the lake lists only the Statue of The Seven and Calla Lily; no domain, named enemy group or resident NPC is attached to it.

## history
### LARGE
Folklore only: a poem says a star once fell to the earth and formed the lake, while other tales say even stars as bright as the Anemo Archon's eyes will fall into it. These are in-world stories, not established history, and canon records no real origin for the lake.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Open water and marsh under wind, with a lone statue by the shore. A quiet, scenic spot close to the city.

## quests
### STANDARD
In the Prologue (Act I, "The Outlander Who Caught the Wind"), Paimon and the Traveler went to Starfell Lake hoping to contact one of the Seven. When the Traveler touched the Anemo statue, its power resonated with them, and they saw a green dragon (Dvalin) heading toward a distant forest. They followed it into the Whispering Woods.
### LARGE +
Shortly before, the Traveler had woken in Teyvat with their powers sealed and fished Paimon from the sea near Cape Oath. The statue's resonance pointed them to Mondstadt and the Anemo Archon as the first lead in the search for the lost sibling. The dragon here is the first sign of "Stormterror", as Mondstadters later call him.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 it is an ordinary, calm area of Mondstadt; no later story has changed it.
