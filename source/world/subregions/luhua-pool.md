## summary
### LITE
Luhua Pool is a scenic landscape of brightly coloured pools watched over by two giant statues, in Qiongji Estuary, Liyue. A hidden ruin beneath it guards the entrance to the one-time domain called the Hidden Palace of Guizang Formula. The game describes it as the product of a deep, pure affection that once filled an ancient garden.
### STANDARD +
A travelling painter, Vermeer, is found here looking for inspiration. The two statues' eyes are the key to opening the ruin.

## geography
### STANDARD
A subarea of Qiongji Estuary with terraced ponds, two giant statues, and Geo and Pyro elemental monuments. Local specialty: Cor Lapis. Roaming foes are hilichurl grenadiers, a Cryo hilichurl shooter and a large Dendro slime, plus a Fatui Pyro Agent as the elite.
### LARGE +
The Chinese name Luhua-chi is rendered by the wiki as "Clear Brilliance Pond". The area appears in the soundtrack listings of the Jade Moon Upon a Sea of Clouds album (tracks such as "Hazy Light" and "Another Hopeful Tomorrow").

## places
### STANDARD
The terraced ponds; the two statues overlooking them; the ruin entrance with a central monument; the Hidden Palace of Guizang Formula domain, which holds two domains, Voyage to the Sanguine Sky and Travels of the Moonlit Venturer.
### LARGE +
To open the palace entrance, the player interacts with a circular mechanism and activates only the Pyro monuments that stand beneath tall, unbroken pillars; the ruin writing hints, in the English text, "Let anyone who follows in the footsteps of the immortals kowtow before the treasure's gate." The wiki reads the original Chinese as roughly "follow in the footsteps of those who live forever, knock on the door to the hidden chamber." The domain's name plays on guicang, one of the Three Changes, ancient Chinese divination systems that also lend names to two other hidden palaces (Lianshan and Zhou).

## people
### STANDARD
Vermeer, a travelling painter, is found near the pool. He has been looking for a good angle for a picture of the giant statues with blazing eyes that he has heard about.
### LARGE +
Vermeer is scatterbrained about his belongings: he leaves his brushes and paints where he sets them down while painting and asks the Traveler to fetch them. After the quest he can be found by the north-eastern waypoint.

## quests
### STANDARD
Luhua Landscape (world quest). After recovering Vermeer's tools from the places in his two sketches, the Traveler collects the "strange stones" (eye-like stones, one found by Vermeer in a pond), inserts one into each statue's head, activates the central monument with Geo and fights Cryo, Pyro and Hydro Abyss mages that appear, then opens the ruin's chests.
### ULTRA +
The ruin holds one Luxurious Chest and two Exquisite Chests, the latter at either end behind spiders; the quest counts as complete once the Luxurious Chest is opened. The quest becomes available after the Prologue's first act, and Vermeer remains near the pool afterwards. Vermeer's own reasoning: if the stone looks like an eye, there should be a second, since people have two eyes.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Colourful water, still air and two stern statues. The viewpoint is titled Weeping Garden, and its text speaks of an ancient garden filled with deep, pure affection that created the pool as it is now known.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Mostly mild: hilichurls, a large Dendro slime and a Fatui Pyro Agent. The ruin puzzle can summon Abyss mages briefly.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the pool is unchanged. No later story content was found that alters it.

## entry: Hidden Palace of Guizang Formula
@keys: Hidden Palace of Guizang Formula, Voyage to the Sanguine Sky, Travels of the Moonlit Venturer
@keys2: Luhua Pool, Liyue
### STANDARD
A one-time domain entrance in Luhua Pool whose description promises treasure enough to drive a person mad, though few remain who can decipher its secrets. It contains two domains, Voyage to the Sanguine Sky and Travels of the Moonlit Venturer. It is one of three "Hidden Palace" domains named for ancient Chinese divination arts.
### LARGE +
Because the palace is a gameplay challenge rather than a story location, canon says nothing about who built it. The ruin writing links it to "immortals" in the English text.
