## summary
### LITE
Teticpac Peak is a plateau with a small lake at the highest point of Coatepec Mountain in Natlan. It contains the Manse of Monetoo Murals.
### STANDARD +
The name comes from Nahuatl roots meaning roughly "large rock" and "the building above".

## places
### STANDARD
The one listed point of interest is the Manse of Monetoo Murals, whose viewpoint text, "Where the Painted Realm Unfolds", wonders whether a world was born from wall graffiti or merely hidden by expressive writing.
### LARGE +
The manse has its own entry. Its music track is "Whistles of Monetoo", shared with Spiritwall Cave.

## quests
### LARGE
Linked quests: the world quests In the Footsteps of the Chosen of Dragons, Lies and Promises and Weighty Wings, and the Hidden Exploration Objective The Secret Gate of Graffiti.

## atmosphere
### ULTRA
A high, open summit above the mountain, with a lake and a sense of looking down on Natlan. Do not invent inhabitants.

## status
### STANDARD
As of 7.1 it is an ordinary explorable subarea.
