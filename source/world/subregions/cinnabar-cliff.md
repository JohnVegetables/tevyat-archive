## summary
### LITE
Cinnabar Cliff is a surface subarea of the Chasm in Liyue. It is called a tourist spot, though locals reportedly never go there, and it holds a Statue of The Seven, a Geo Elemental Monument and hostile Fatui Skirmishers.
### STANDARD +
A quest domain from the Archon Quests, the Unknown Sanctuary, is placed here on the map even though it belongs to Sumeru's story. A self-styled hero called Tang Wuchou, who is really a Treasure Hoarder, stands here.

## geography
### STANDARD
Features are a Statue of The Seven, a Geo Elemental Monument, Geograna, Spoutrocks, Unique Rocks and Unique Rock Pillars. Cor Lapis, Qingxin and Violetgrass grow here.
### LARGE +
It sits within the Chasm's surface area above the Underground Mines. The Chinese name is Dansha Cliff, the word dansha meaning cinnabar.

## people
### STANDARD
Tang Wuchou, a Treasure Hoarder, is found here in the open world; the wiki gives him the self-proclaimed title Guhua Hero, with a question mark, and an idle line imagines a garden of Glaze Lilies. After the world quest The Missing Miner, the miner Uncle He can be found here too.
### LARGE +
Per the wiki's trivia, Cinnabar Cliff is considered a tourist spot but locals never go there, citing the quest Says He Who Seeks Stone.

## quests
### STANDARD
The Unknown Sanctuary is a quest domain used in the Archon Quests "A Lamenter at Fate's End" and "Portended Fate". Its description says hilichurls were seen heading there, faithful and unwavering, and a companion tells the Traveler he has never seen anything like it. The domain's enemies are hilichurls and slimes of several elements.
### LARGE +
The wiki flags the domain as a Sumeru quest domain placed on the Chasm's map at Cinnabar Cliff. It is in the Liyue map but belongs to Sumeru content; do not move the setting of those quests to Liyue.
### ULTRA +
As gameplay, the domain is a short run of teleports between platforms with waves of Hydro, Electro, Pyro and Cryo Slimes, including large and mutant ones, plus optional hilichurls that vanish when they or the player step into the portal. It holds four chests.

## dangers
### STANDARD
A Blazing Axe Mitachurl is the elite enemy. Common foes are Fatui Skirmishers (Geochanter Bracers, Anemoboxer Vanguards and Electrohammer Vanguards), Rock Shield Hilichurl Guards and Geo Slimes.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
An exposed cliff by the Chasm with a lone Statue of The Seven, Fatui patrols and the occasional hoarder. This is a suggested scene mood drawn from the feature list, not a quoted description.

## status
### STANDARD
Unchanged as of 7.1.
