## summary
### LITE
Qingxu Pool is a ruined site in Lisha, Liyue: stone towers and pavilions standing around a rocky outcrop in a shallow pool. Legend says an evil god once ruled a city here, that the city was drowned under water until the Archon War ended, and that the ruins were exposed as the tides withdrew. Five hidden Geo seals guard a Nameless Treasure, and an underground ruin below plays a part in a Yelan story quest.
### STANDARD +
A nearby settlement is the home of Tieshan. The ruins have attracted treasure hunters, the Liyue Qixing and the Fatui.

## geography
### STANDARD
A subarea of Lisha whose ruin entrance stands in the middle of a giant rocky outcrop rising from a shallow pool, surrounded by steep cliffs and several stone towers set among the natural scenery. Local specialties: Cor Lapis, Noctilucous Jade and Qingxin. The viewpoint, Qingxu's Forsaken Tower, describes a ruined tower amid a verdant field that still guards a bygone age.
### LARGE +
Roald's diary (volume 8), written on the westward road from Mt. Tianheng, calls it a ruin known locally as Qingxu Pool and says legends hold it predates the Geo Archon's rule over Liyue. The area is home to hilichurls, Treasure Hoarders and Abyss mages.

## places
### STANDARD
The ruin entrance on the outcrop; five pavilions with Geo seals; a stone tablet; the underground ruins reached only during a story quest.
### LARGE +
The tablet reads that Rex Lapis walks among us and Liyue is secure, and that to mark the work of the forebears he left five seals within pavilions; later visitors must retrace the ancients' path and acknowledge it to win their treasure. Using Geo on all five seals opens a Luxurious Chest at the tablet with a Nameless Treasure inside.

## history
@version: 1.0
### STANDARD
Legend, as Roald records it, says the ruins were left by an evil god whose name has been wiped from the record and by that god's people. The self-serving god was defeated and the city submerged until the end of the Archon War, then exposed as the tides withdrew. Roald heard this from Soraya, a scholar from Sumeru he met at Wangshu Inn.
### LARGE +
Whether the evil god is the Geo dragon Azhdaha, who rampaged across Lisha before Rex Lapis and three adepti sealed him in The Chasm, is unclear. The Nameless Treasure here is one of three whose carvings tell of that sealing, along with those at Lingju Pass and Dunyu Ruins.
### ULTRA +
The Liyue Qixing once approved an excavation of the ruins for rumoured buried treasure, but the ruins were completely sealed and the technology to blast through solid rock did not yet exist, so the project was dropped. Rumours persisted, and the Fatui agents in Liyue chased the treasure, to the diplomat Yusupov's annoyance.

## people
### STANDARD
No one lives in the ruins, but Tieshan comes from a settlement around the area; he says his home is Qingxu Pool, not the city.

## quests
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Hidden in Nameless Treasures (the five-seal puzzle) and, in Yelan's story quest Calculated Gambit, the Qingxu Pool Underground Ruins quest domain.
### LARGE +
Calculated Gambit (Umbrabilis Orchis Chapter, Act I, Version 2.7): Uncle Tian, the Tianshu, is choosing a successor, and Yelan assesses the candidates. The candidate Zhiyi's manifesto includes a Qingxu Pool Redevelopment Plan. Yelan realises it is a front for Fatui infiltrators in the Blackcliff Forge, who want to use the Forge's secret explosives to break into the sealed ruins and take whatever treasure is there. In Foregone Conclusion, the final part, she and the Traveler follow the Fatui diplomat Yusupov and Zhiyi into the underground ruins and capture them, though Zhiyi betrays Yusupov first.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Hilichurl berserkers and shooters, Treasure Hoarders, large Dendro slimes, Abyss mages and Blazing Axe Mitachurls.
### ULTRA +
The underground ruins in Foregone Conclusion are a story-only instance with hilichurls, Pyro Abyss mages, Blazing Axe Mitachurls, Geovishap Hatchlings, sliding walls, fire orbs, lamps and elevators; Yelan joins as a trial character.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Mist clears from cliffs in the morning; stone towers blend into the scenery. Roald found it calm and unperturbed by the development of Liyue Harbor, and thought the ruins perhaps left as a reminder of an earlier age for the adepti and other gods.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the pool is unchanged. The Fatui's excavation plot ended with Yusupov and Zhiyi's capture in Version 2.7, and no later story content was found that alters the ruins.
