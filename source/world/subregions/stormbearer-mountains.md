## summary
### LITE
The Stormbearer Mountains are the high country in the north of Starfell Valley, once a place to observe approaching storms. Now only an abandoned watchtower stands there, and monsters roam the slopes.
### STANDARD +
They lie next to Stormbearer Point on the coast, where the Traveler's journey begins.

## geography
### STANDARD
High mountains in northern Starfell Valley with an old lookout tower, fishing spots, and Dandelion Seeds and valberries as local specialties. Anemograna, a Time Trial Challenge and Shrines of Depths are found here.
### LARGE +
The merchants' road runs near enough that monsters here concern the Knights of Favonius.

## people
### STANDARD
No settlement stands here. The Knight Harry, posted at nearby Stormbearer Point, tracks monster camps along the merchants' road and hopes the evidence will persuade Acting Grand Master Jean to order a cleanup of the Stormbearer Mountains area, like the one Grand Master Varka ran in earlier days.

## history
### STANDARD
The mountains were once an observation post for incoming storms. Barbatos' winds have kept Mondstadt calm for generations, and the post fell out of use, leaving the abandoned watchtower.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Many hilichurl camps of different elements, Samachurls, slimes, a Cryo Whopperflower, Blazing Axe Mitachurls, Ruin Guards, Cryo Abyss Mages and a Fatui Electro Cicin Mage; the Anemo Hypostasis is the area's boss enemy.

## travel
### LARGE
Gliding from high ground is the natural way to cross the highlands, and Knights warn travelers about monster camps along the merchants' route.

## status
### STANDARD
Through 7.1 canon gives no change: still a monster-ridden highland with a derelict tower.
### ULTRA +
The storm-watching tradition behind the name survives at the coast: Vind's family at Stormbearer Point was ordered by Vennessa to watch for storms and relay warnings across Mondstadt, a duty that dates back at least a thousand years.
