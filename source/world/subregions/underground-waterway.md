## summary
### LITE
The Underground Waterway is a stretch of clear, cold water running through the Chasm's Underground Mines in Liyue. Zhiqiong found it while exploring alone and thought it the perfect place to rest and wash off the ill fortune of the mines.
### STANDARD +
It is one of the calmer corners of the mines, though Abyss Mages, Shadowy Husks and a Black Serpent Knight patrol it. It has a Geo and Hydro Elemental Monument and the gentle Hydro Mimic Boar.

## geography
### STANDARD
Water-filled caverns on the Underground Mines map, between the Ad-Hoc Main Tunnel, the Serpent's Cave and the Main Mining Area. Features: an Elemental Monument (Geo and Hydro), Ruin Braziers, Rock Piles, Spoutrock and Luminous Seelie. Local specialties are Cor Lapis and Noctilucous Jade.
### LARGE +
It becomes accessible after the three Uncle He clues are found in "Chasm Spelunkers", like the Serpent's Cave. The wiki also lists a fishing point here.

## people
### STANDARD
No named residents. Zhiqiong is the area's discoverer in-world: her viewpoint note says she found it alone, drew it on the map, and hopes the Traveler will follow. The "gentle elemental creatures" she mentions move very slowly and have no quarrel with the world; the wiki identifies them as Hydro Mimic creatures.

## history
### STANDARD
No history is recorded. The mines were worked by Liyue and abandoned when the Qixing sealed the Chasm.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Cool, damp, quiet; Luminous Seelie drift above still water. After the black mud and machinery of the other caverns it reads as a refuge, which is how Zhiqiong describes it.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Cryo Abyss Mages, a Black Serpent Knight: Windcutter, Shadowy Husks (Standard Bearer and Line Breaker), Hilichurls with Rock Shields or Cryo bows, and Geo and Hydro Slimes. Floating Hydro Fungus drifts through the caves. The Hydro Mimic Boar is listed as a special enemy.
### LARGE +
The Abyss Mages show that the mines' wider Abyssal corruption reaches even this cleaner cavern; the wiki does not explain the Black Serpent Knight's presence.
### ULTRA +
The viewpoint note, in Zhiqiong's voice, says the waters are clean and cool and good for washing off the ill fortune gathered on the way, and that she drew the waterway on the map.

## naming
### ULTRA
The Chinese name means roughly 'underground water swamp' and the Japanese 'underground wetlands'; both suggest a marshy cavern rather than a canal, though the English name and viewpoint speak of a waterway.

## quests
### STANDARD
No quest of its own. The area is reached during "Chasm Spelunkers" in "The Chasm Delvers" (2.6).

## status
### LITE
Unchanged as of 7.1.
