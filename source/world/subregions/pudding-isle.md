## summary
### LITE
Pudding Isle is a limited-time island in the east of the Golden Apple Archipelago, the summer-event map east of Mondstadt. It was the island where the player started in Midsummer Island Adventure (1.6) and, in Summertime Odyssey (2.8), an island whose halves a bonsai could switch between Mondstadt, Liyue and Inazuma styles.
### STANDARD +
Canon records no residents or history of its own for the island; it is known only through the events.

## geography
@version: 2.8
### STANDARD
In 1.6 the first island players landed on held a teleport waypoint, a Door of Resurrection, a waverider waypoint and two Echoing Conches. A main island lay beyond, with a Stonehide Lawachurl guarding a chest, plus a southeast islet of Hymnal Rings and a peaked islet to the northeast.
### LARGE +
In 2.8, after the quest As the Courtyard in Spring Once Appeared (Part 3), a bonsai let each half of the island be switched between Mondstadt, Liyue and Inazuma versions. Moving the mountain stones left and right of the bonsai reshaped the island's mountains, and each style came with its own monument (Anemo, Geo or Electro) and Seelie puzzles. A time trial in the north depended on the chosen setup, since part of its bridge broke in some arrangements.
### ULTRA +
The 1.6 island's Hymnal Ring challenge on the southeast islet had the player step on a pressure plate and throw the Wind-Blessed Harpastum through an Electro hoop to break a target and open a chest.

## history
@version: 1.6
### STANDARD
The island belongs to the Haar Islands, the archipelago's older name. Its Echoing Conches in 1.6 carried recorded voices of earlier visitors (titles include "The General" and "The Price of Experimentation"); more conches appeared after Act III.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Hilichurls, slimes and elite guardians such as a Stonehide Lawachurl (1.6) and a Bloatty Floatty drifting near the main island.

## status
### LITE
Not accessible in the game as of 7.1; limited to the 1.6 and 2.8 event runs.
