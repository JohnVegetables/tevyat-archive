## summary
### LITE
Wuwang Hill is a hill in Bishui Plain, Liyue, north of Mt. Qingce. A permanent grey fog and drifting blue ghost lights make it eerie in any weather. The Hidden Palace of Zhou Formula domain stands atop it, and the Wuwang Hill Disaster happened here.
### STANDARD +
The name means "prudence": locals say it is the hill where even the prudent are punished.

## geography
### STANDARD
A wooded hill with a ruined village at its foot and a pond among statues on the peak. A Geo monument, Ruins Torches and Seelie are found about. Cor Lapis, Noctilucous Jade, Qingxin and Violetgrass are local specialties.
### LARGE +
The statues on the peak face towards Stormterror's Lair, Dragonspine, Mt. Aocang and Liyue Harbor.

## places
### STANDARD
The Hidden Palace of Zhou Formula is a ritual labyrinth where, according to its description, an evil chi was sealed by the Seven-and-Eight-Gates Method; rumor says it is still sealed.
### LARGE +
Opening the palace means lighting four Ruins Torches and activating the Geo monument.

## history
### STANDARD
Wuwang Hill was an ordinary place until the Wuwang Hill Disaster over a decade ago. Fetor was leaking from the boundary between life and death, so Old Hu and Hu Tao's father performed the Art of Homa there to burn it away. The rite went wrong: Hu Tao's father died at once from the fetor, and a flood ravaged a nearby village. Old Hu contained the fallout at the cost of much of his lifespan and died before sixty.
### LARGE +
Hu Tao later attributed her father's failure to lost focus caused by his worldly attachments. The folk tale in Records of Jueyun says young villagers followed a whale-like song into the Bishui River and vanished, leaving only the elderly, and that the ley lines now recreate the dead villagers' dreams as spirits that lure trespassers.

### ULTRA +
The Hidden Palace's Chinese name is translated by the wiki as roughly the palace that takes responsibility for Wuwang, which it links to the seal on the evil there. In Chongyun's hangout quest Test of Courage (a hangout event), the hill is used as the venue for a courage test while Chongyun looks for signs of evil spirits.


## dangers
### STANDARD
Ghost lights and spirit voices lure travellers off the path towards rivers and ambushes. Hilichurls, Cicin mages, Mitachurls and Tainted Water-Splitting Phantasms also roam, and Rhodeia of Loch lives in the nearby water.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Cold grey light, thick mist, rotting trees and whispers. In Chongyun's hangout event the hill hosts a test of courage.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the hill remains eerie and abandoned.
