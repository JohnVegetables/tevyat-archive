## summary
### LITE
Ardravi Valley is a river valley in the Dharma Forest of Sumeru, named for the great Ardravi river that carved it. Official text calls the river Sumeru's great waterborne mercantile route, and the valley holds the busy harbour of Port Ormos, the quiet fishing hamlet of Vimara Village and the mountain where a huge Khaenri'ahn Ruin Golem lies dormant.
### STANDARD +
The Forest Watchers (the Forest Rangers) look after the valley's natural life, and the official expedition text says many lovely creatures live there. The name echoes Ardravi in the title of the Iranian water goddess Anahita.

## geography
### STANDARD
The valley was formed by erosion from the Ardravi, which cuts through the whole of Sumeru; following the river downstream leads to the southern shore. The river runs from Sumeru City to Port Ormos between two giant trees, and the valley around it is forest and riverbank. Its wild specialties are Nilotpala Lotus and Rukkhashava Mushrooms, and the wiki lists an Aeonblight Drake as the area's boss enemy.
### LARGE +
At the valley's edge, Devantaka Mountain rises with its Ruin Golem, and a hidden cavern, Dev's Cavern, lies beneath it. Port Ormos spreads across several levels around the harbour, from the northern, eastern and western docks up to an upper tier of hotels and shops.

## places
### STANDARD
Port Ormos, the largest port in Sumeru, a hub for trade and travel that sits beneath two giant trees with a river running to it from Sumeru City. Vimara Village, a small, tranquil fishing town on the river between Sumeru City and the port, where caravans pass between the two. Devantaka Mountain, home to the Ruin Golem.
### LARGE +
The port has a lighthouse and a tavern named Djafar on its west side, an arch bridge designed by the architect Kaveh (told in the quest A Refuge in the Sands), and docks divided into northern, eastern and western quarters. The viewpoint text for Vimara Village says there is nothing there but a tranquil life, and invites an exhausted traveller to rest.

## people
### STANDARD
Port Ormos residents are mostly traders and labourers who grew up there, together with Eremite mercenaries from various factions, merchants of many nationalities, dockworkers and ship crews. Students sometimes come to the port when academic life becomes too stressful, and some consider that lucky. Vimara Village is a village of fishers, hunters, merchants and a few researchers.
### LARGE +
The Aranara, the small forest spirits most children can see, are linked to the valley through quests; Ararycan, who tends the wood near Devantaka Mountain, appears in Vimana Agama. The Forest Rangers are the guardians of the area's wildlife.
### ULTRA +
Named port figures from the wiki's NPC lists include Reza (Port Master), Masrur (Deputy Port Master), Araby (lighthouse guardian), Feroz (an Eremite captain), Mahshid (a Mahamata by day who is also at the tavern at night), and merchants such as Berbere (spice), Dilawar and Gauhar (trade supervisors). Jazari appears at the upper tier after Vimana Agama. Vimara Village's residents include the merchant Bahram (who describes the village as a stop for caravans), the hunter Aurelius and the researcher Nabati.

## governance
### STANDARD
Parts of Port Ormos fall under Akademiya supervision, but smuggling and other illegal activity are more common there than in Sumeru City, and prices are lower. A port master and deputy port master run the harbour day to day.

## culture
### STANDARD
The harbour's official blurb has a speaker scoff that the lectures of the sages are boring and propose setting off across the seven seas with wine and floral honey: the port is Sumeru's free-and-easy counterpart to the scholarly capital.

## history
### STANDARD
Canon does not date the valley's formation. Devantaka Mountain's Ruin Golem is a leftover of Khaenri'ahn machinery; the scholar Pir Kavikavus of Kshahrewar studied it before an accident ended his work, and the Akademiya's later research there is carried on by the Kshahrewar researcher Jazari (see Devantaka Mountain).

## dangers
### STANDARD
The Aeonblight Drake, a mechanical beast, and other ruin machines around Devantaka Mountain; wild animals in the forest; Abyss Order mages seeking the Golem's core before it was sealed; smugglers and Eremite mercenaries in the port.

## travel
### STANDARD
The Ardravi is the main route between Sumeru City and the sea: caravans stop at Vimara Village, and ships dock at Port Ormos. Vimara Village has a Waverider waypoint for travel on the river.

## quests
### STANDARD
Many world quests are tied to the valley, among them Vimana Agama (Ararycan, Jazari and the Ruin Golem), Until Vana is Healed, The World of Aranara, A Refuge in the Sands, Lost in Prosperity and the series about the Port Ormos jeweller Royinjan. These are listed by title in the wiki category; only Vimana Agama and Until Vana is Healed are summarised in the Devantaka Mountain entity.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Green banks, a wide slow river and the commerce of a free port. The port feels noisy, lightly lawless and cheerful under its giant trees; the village is deliberately quiet; and the mountain looms behind, a rusted watcher.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the valley is open and peaceful, with its trade route functioning and the Ruin Golem idle.
### STANDARD +
No content from 6.x or 7.0-7.1 changes the area.

### STANDARD
Sumeru's largest port, under two giant trees, with a river running to it from Sumeru City. The Akademiya supervises parts of it, but smuggling is more common than in the capital and prices are lower. Most long-term residents are traders and labourers; Eremite mercenaries are also present, and stressed students come to escape.

Kaveh designed and built its arch bridge. The docks are split into northern, eastern and westside quarters, with a lighthouse and a tavern named Djafar at the west end.
