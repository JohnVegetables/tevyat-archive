## summary
### LITE
Fallingstar Fields is a subarea of Atocpan in Natlan. It holds the Tephric Ruins, a section of old ruins that the volcano threw out about five hundred years ago, around the time of the cataclysm. Inside are broken pieces of Secret Source machinery arranged as if for worship, and a hidden room with a sealed report about the dragonborn.
### STANDARD +
The ruins are the setting of the world quest Investigator of Ancient Ruins and are connected to the old Secret Source Servant Ehecatl.

## geography
### STANDARD
Open ground below the Great Volcano of Tollan with the Tephric Ruins at its centre. A local specialty is dracolite.
### LARGE +
The ruins are said to be a piece of a larger structure, ejected from the volcano. A floating platform in the middle of the ruins is empty at first.

## places
### STANDARD
The Tephric Ruins. Inside are a deactivated source core, three strange Secret Source structures and a floating ruins platform.
### LARGE +
The source core resembles the core of a Secret Source Automaton. The three structures resemble a hunter-seeker's sheath, outer shell and limbs. In-game text calls these broken parts an object of worship, perhaps placed by tribespeople. The platform once held something; a relic called That Which Was Once Ehecatl, the dormant form of a Secret Source Servant, fits there.
### ULTRA +
Returning the relic opens a hidden room with a Secret Source Sentinel and an Iridescent Inscription that holds the sealed records.

## people
### STANDARD
Nzaya and Kimosi are the tribespeople met during Investigator of Ancient Ruins, and the Traveler is joined by Little One.
### LARGE +
The ruins' original makers are not shown. The sealed report is written by people concerned for the Dragonborn; its authors are not named.

## history
@version: 5.5
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The wiki says the ruins were thrown from the volcano roughly five hundred years ago, linking the date to the cataclysm.
### LARGE +
The hidden records are titled by the in-game text as 'Items That Should Perhaps Be Deleted'. The report on reversing the regressive evolution of the Dragonborn says previous regressive events damaged the physical malleability of the Dragonborn. Those who have regressed can adapt to a stable changed environment, but the power such bodies can bear cannot be restored by the methods tried. Only selection through bloodlines and generations of modifications, using the mark known as the Flamelord's Blessing, might do it. The authors judge that the cost is too great: there is not enough time, the bodies of many regressed kin cannot take the power needed, and the experiments are too cruel, so the report should remain sealed.
### ULTRA +
A later comment of unknown date notes this might be an opportunity, if the experiment could be used to get past a barrier of destiny. The speaker, whom the wiki links to Kukulkan, mentions a brother, whom the wiki links to Xiuhcoatl, and wonders whether the promise seen in humanity was justified or a mistake. They hope any kin might reach a final choice that redeems the desolate years.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Ash-grey, silent and a little eerie: old stonework dotted with dead machine parts that look as if they could twitch to life the moment the viewer looks away.

## dangers
### STANDARD
During Investigator of Ancient Ruins, Abyssal monsters and Hilichurls are in the ruins, and a mimic chest triggers an earthquake with falling stalactites while the Traveler fights.
### LARGE +
The enemies appear in waves: Hilichurls, a Tenebrous Mimesis, rifthounds and tepetlisauri.

## travel
### STANDARD
Reached within Atocpan. The ruins can also be entered by dropping through a hole at the top.

## quests
@version: 5.5
### STANDARD
Investigator of Ancient Ruins, which can stand in for A Brief History of Rocks to complete The Way Into the Mountain. The hidden room is tied to the story quest The Frozen, Rekindling Land, which yields the relic.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the ruins stand quiet and open; the sealed report can be read.
### STANDARD +
No later change to the ruins is recorded through 7.1.

## entry: Tephric Ruins
@keys: Tephric Ruins, Items That Should Perhaps Be Deleted, Flamelord's Blessing
@version: 5.5
### STANDARD
The Tephric Ruins in Fallingstar Fields are a section of ancient ruins that the volcano ejected around five hundred years ago. They hold broken Secret Source Mechanism parts, an empty platform for the relic of the Secret Source Servant Ehecatl, and a hidden room whose sealed report discusses the regressed Dragonborn and the Flamelord's Blessing.
### LARGE +
The report concludes the proposed cure is too cruel, and a later undated comment treats it as a possible chance to break a barrier of destiny.
