## summary
### LITE
Nothing Passage is a narrow mountain pass cutting through the northern end of Lempo Isle in Nod-Krai. A whole Fatui company, the 6th, was wiped out here after dubious experiments by two rival researchers of the Armory Palace; only one private, Smirnova, escaped. After the world quest series Colors of Emptiness the entire passage becomes a strong kuuvahki environment.
### STANDARD +
The Fatui sealed the area off as a Military Administrative Area under an Imperial Edict, and a weathered notice at its entrance still warns off intruders as hostile agents. Inside lie abandoned camps, dormant Landcruiser defence machines and a barrier controlled by authorisation terminals.
### LARGE +
The pass is bound up with two things: the Fatui's competing kuuvahki research under the Tsaritsa's wishes, and the eerie coloured lights the Traveler follows in Colors of Emptiness (6.0).

## geography
### STANDARD
A narrow pass through the mountains of northern Lempo Isle, with a Teleport Waypoint at its northern end. Abandoned Fatui camps lie along it, among them one southeast of the northern waypoint.
### LARGE +
Inside the pass are patches of mist and a Fatui kuuvahki barrier whose authorisation terminals lock sections by ID card, a source of the researchers' feud. Moonlanes, red and blue kuuhenki and Unipolar Crystonite appear within it during the quest series, and the soldiers hunted goats in the surrounding hills.

## history
### STANDARD
The Fatui's 6th Company was posted here to guard two professors of the Armory Palace, Anton Zarkovich Karnatsky and V. A. Preobrazhensky, working on moon fragments reported to hold kuuvahki energy. Both wanted to be first to a breakthrough that would win praise from the Tsaritsa, and their rivalry became open hostility. The experiments ended in catastrophe: the whole company died except Private Elena Smirnova, who escaped to send the Kuuvahki Experimental Design Bureau a distress call.
### LARGE +
Private Pyotr Dmitriyevich Orlov's notes record the slide into disaster: the Poruchik posted him to the pass; Karnatsky despised Preobrazhensky, whom he called a lunatic and who is the fiance of his daughter; strange marks, a bitter cold and the smell of decay appeared; a goat was found living but transfixed, its eyes clouded; Karnatsky boasted of drawing out the purest primal light from a moon shard and tearing open the veil of the world. Orlov's last note, written near the end, blames the two men for waking the fallen moon rocks, says a 'color' devoured everything, and asks that Smirnova be cleared as no deserter. Karnatsky's own research notes, found in a camp, treat the energy of the 'Ancient Moon's Remnants' as a primordial life form that can be taught to feed on living things, and he plans to use it to plug the gaps left in Nod-Krai by Abyssal corruption.
### ULTRA +
An off-hand reference in the Shoemaker's Children Go Barefoot shows the Design Bureau's archive keeps a volume on staff casualty, reward and punishment records with an intriguing section on Nothing Passage. Orlov's final note says Volkov (named there as fourrier A. Volkov) died so that Smirnova could escape.

## people
### STANDARD
No living residents. The dead are the 6th Company's soldiers, among them Private Orlov, Captain Volkov and a soldier named Petrenko in Orlov's diary, and the two researchers. Smirnova, the sole survivor, is covered in her own entry.
### LARGE +
Orlov records Karnatsky calling the soldiers 'dim-witted lackeys', and Preobrazhensky answering a tirade by reeling off a diatribe in Fae, which Orlov could not understand. Karnatsky's own notes call Preobrazhensky a poor whelp of a fae.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Defensive Landcruisers, recon bots, Fatui patrols and a kuuvahki barrier. After the disaster the pass is permeated by strange coloured presences: the Traveler is chased through it by glowing red and blue orbs that behave like some kind of life.
### LARGE +
In Colors Out of Space the two orbs collide, pull the Traveler into a purple space of floating ruins, and leave the passage a strong kuuvahki environment. Afterwards Paimon, thrown elsewhere by the blast, does not know what the lights were.

## quests
### STANDARD
Colors of Emptiness is a world quest series with Crimson Cleansing and Blues of the Old World (either order), then Colors Out of Space. The series is ambiguous: no one explains what the orbs are. Nothing Passage is also the meeting point where Lauma waits before the Traveler's infiltration of the Fatui's Experimental Design Bureau in In the Shadows of Night (Song of the Welkin Moon, Act I, 6.0).
### LARGE +
Quest props include the Private's Notes (I-III), Preobrazhensky's messy note threatening Karnatsky over the authorisation terminal, and Karnatsky's research notes.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Silent, cold and unnaturally still. Camps hold untouched bedrolls and the dust of a place that was left in a hurry; colour seems to drain and return in blue and red.

## travel
### LARGE
Reached from the north of Lempo Isle by the waypoint at the passage's mouth. The Fatui notice forbids entry, but the camps are empty.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the Fatui presence is gone from the passage, the dead company remains a mystery to the Fatui archive, and the pass sits as an eerie kuuvahki-saturated place.
### STANDARD +
No later quest through 7.1 resolves what the orbs were.

## entry: The 6th Company disaster
@keys: Karnatsky, Preobrazhensky, Private Orlov, Pyotr Dmitriyevich Orlov, 6th Company, Nothing Passage disaster
@version: 6.0
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Two senior Armory Palace researchers, Karnatsky and Preobrazhensky, feuded over moon-fragment research in Nothing Passage while a Fatui company guarded them. Their experiments with moon fragments destroyed the company; Private Elena Smirnova alone escaped, at the cost of Volkov's life, to send a distress call. Private Orlov's notes say she was no deserter.
