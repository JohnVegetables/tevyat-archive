## summary
### LITE
The Serpent's Cave is a cavern in the Chasm's Underground Mines in Liyue and the arena of the Ruin Serpent, a hulking excavator-style Ruin Machine. Like the rest of the mines it is lit by Lumenstone glow and streaked with black Abyssal "dark mud".
### STANDARD +
It has a teleport waypoint, Cor Lapis deposits and wall-grown Lumenlamps, and the Chasm's mix of ancient automatons and Shadowy Husks. Nothing in canon explains its name beyond the serpentine machine that haunts it.

## geography
### STANDARD
An underground chamber complex on the Underground Mines map, a separate layer under the Chasm's surface, reached beyond the Ad-Hoc Main Tunnel and next to the Underground Waterway. Spoutrock vents, Dark Mud, Lumenlamps and Oozing Concretions fill it, with Luminous Seelie as the only gentle presence. Cor Lapis is the local specialty.
### LARGE +
The cave opens up after the Traveler has found all three clues to the missing miner Uncle He in the world quest "Chasm Spelunkers". The serpent's own arena is reached through part of that quest, and its tunnelling attacks, vacuum fields and teleport suit a machine built to dig.

## people
### STANDARD
No named residents. The exploration team's miners and Zhiqiong work out of the camp elsewhere in the mines. Treasure Hoarders also sneak through the Underground Mines, but the wiki does not list them in this cave.

## history
### STANDARD
Nothing is recorded about the cave's past in itself. The mines were worked by Liyue after the Archon War and shut during the Qixing's closure, after a mysterious run of accidents and the ooze. Canon places the 500-year-old cataclysm fighting of the Millelith and the yaksha Bosacius in the Chasm generally, not in this cave specifically.
### LARGE +
The Ruin Serpent's wiki title is "Excavator: SI/Glp" (a mechanical designation), in the family Automatons, group Ruin Machines. Its drop, the Runic Fang, is a boss material. Which era built it is not stated.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Echoing, wet-stone caves, a soft glow from Lumenstone growths and the grind of old machinery. The ground can shudder when the serpent burrows, and the dark mud gives the place a sullen, wrong feeling.

## dangers
### STANDARD
The Ruin Serpent, a normal boss, is the main threat. It burrows and charges, makes a Sand Tomb vacuum field, raises Oozing Concretions at the corners of the arena and draws power from them for barrages and shockwaves; at the start of the fight it also drains a carried Lumenstone Adjuvant to a low charge. Elite enemies include Ruin Cruisers, Defenders, Destroyers and Scouts, and Shadowy Husk Standard Bearers.
### LARGE +
The Adjuvant's blooming light can destroy the Concretions, and a yellow rock created alongside them recharges it.
### ULTRA +
Gameplay detail: the Ruin Serpent gains very high resistance to damage while it charges its Bomb Barrage or Shockwave, which is why those are the moments to hit its exposed weak points; its Oozing Concretion attack is disabled in the Spiral Abyss version of the fight. A boss drop is the Runic Fang.

## naming
### ULTRA
The Chinese name means roughly 'huge snake rock cave' and the Japanese 'huge snake rock hole'; the wiki gives no in-world explanation, and the Serpent's Cave should not be linked to the Black Serpents or the Serpent of All Evils without canon support.

## travel
### STANDARD
Entered on foot through the mines after the exploration team's camp is reached; a teleport waypoint in the cave is unlocked by exploring it.

## quests
### STANDARD
The cave is open as soon as the three clues about Uncle He are found in "Chasm Spelunkers", part of "The Chasm Delvers" series (2.6). The Ruin Serpent fight requires progressing further in that same quest.

## status
### LITE
Unchanged as of 7.1; no 6.x or 7.0-7.1 content revisits the Serpent's Cave.

## entry: Ruin Serpent
@keys: Ruin Serpent, Excavator SI/Glp
@version: 2.6
### STANDARD
The Ruin Serpent is the Chasm's mechanical boss, a drilling automaton shaped like a giant snake. It buries itself, rolls and drills, and powers its heavier attacks by absorbing the dark-mud Concretions it makes. Its weak points are exposed only while it charges its Bomb Barrage or Shockwave, and a hit disables them until it repairs itself.
