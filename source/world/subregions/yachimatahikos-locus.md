## summary
### LITE
Yachimatahiko's Locus is a hidden subarea of Enkanomiya in Inazuma, introduced in Version 2.4. It was the site of one of the three Towers of the Three Realms, built to keep harmony between the human, elemental (vishap) and abyssal realms. It appears on the map only after the world quest Erebos' Secret begins.
### STANDARD +
It is one of three trial grounds opened by the shade Eboshi, an auxiliary of Sangonomiya and a shrine maiden of Enkanomiya. Here the Jibashiri Uda, in charge of the trial of "wisdom", sets the Traveler a puzzle of four Flames of the High Gate.

## geography
### STANDARD
The Locus is reached by a Time Tunnel at The Emanant Skylight in The Narrows once Eboshi has raised it, or can be glided to from above the Dainichi Mikoshi. Most of it is shrouded by a barrier until the trial progresses. It holds a Teleport Waypoint, a Day-Night Mechanism and Triangular Mechanisms.
### LARGE +
Before Eboshi is spoken to, the area is only floating textures and its name; her first conversation raises it into the world.

## quests
### STANDARD
Yachimatahiko's Trial is part of The Three Great Martial Trials and Erebos' Secret. The Traveler passes through the Time Tunnel, speaks to an afterimage before the seal, then releases four Flames of the High Gate using the Day-Night Mechanism (Whitenight or Evernight) and the Triangular Mechanisms. Uda then acknowledges the Traveler's valour, calling it "extensive and meticulous". Uda says that was a praise the Divine Priestess once gave skilled youths, later written on pennants at Jibashiri headquarters.
### LARGE +
After all three trials are done, Eboshi tells the Traveler more about the bathysmal vishaps, Watatsumi Omikami and the time before the sun and moon. Those revelations belong to Erebos' Secret, not to this site itself.

## history
### STANDARD
Per The Byakuyakoku Collection (via the wiki), the people of Enkanomiya built the Towers of the Three Realms at the borders of Byakuyakoku to stabilise it, as it sat on the edge of the human, elemental and abyssal realms. The towers are hidden through secret arts and can be summoned only by shrine maidens like Eboshi and the Vassals of Watatsumi. Their ancient names are forgotten.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Anemo, Hydro and Geo Specters appear in the area.
### ULTRA +
The wiki observes that the trial grounds are reachable by gliding from the rocks above the Dainichi Mikoshi at any time, but the waypoint is far enough that it normally takes a mid-air talent or the Time Tunnel to reach it. Uda's own line says the trial of wisdom is for those whose bravery is joined to wisdom; foolhardiness is not enough.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
A floating, ghostly platform of ruins and flame-bearing gates, eerily quiet, with the sense of a rite few have been allowed to see.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1, unchanged: the trial is complete and the tower stays hidden unless raised by Eboshi.
