## summary
### LITE
Mt. Xuanlian is a peak in Chenyu Vale's Southern Mountain in Liyue, south of Chizhang Wall and Yaodie Valley. It is admired for strange rock and sunset views, and a nearby cave holds the blessed water sought in a world quest.
### STANDARD +
A viewpoint here is called Clear Skies Over Xuanlian. The Hezhe Post photography contest names the mountain as one of its four subjects.

## geography
### STANDARD
South of Chizhang Wall and south of Yaodie Valley; reached by a shortcut from Yaodie Valley through a cave. Pressure plates in a cave near the mountain hide a Sacred Simulacrum.
### LARGE +
A small path leads from Yaodie Valley to the mountain, and a cave and the "blessed waters" of Mt. Xuanlian lie at its end.

## people
### STANDARD
Akai, a contest competitor in Our Chenyu Vale Trek, is menaced by monsters on the mountain and shares his best photo spot with the Traveler.

## history
### STANDARD
The viewpoint text quotes a short line of verse: the weighty winds blow, the day is dark, but the cascading falls run clear. A competitor's scenery guide in Our Chenyu Vale Trek says you rarely see such strange mountains elsewhere and that the sunset there is "truly moving".
### LARGE +
In The Roaming Abode, a Qiaoying herb gatherer left to draw blessed water near the mountain for a divine plant at his house. He was injured by monsters on the way; Uncle Luo says villagers found him near Mt. Xuanlian clutching a bottle, buried him and poured the water in his yard. The Traveler fetches the Xuanlian Sacred Water too.
### ULTRA +
Luo scoffs that the water is only river water. A text in the herb gatherer's yard, Pharmacy's Amelioration, describes a strange grass named Xiangjun on an earthen raft that floats without water, a few leagues east of the mountain.

## quests
### STANDARD
Our Chenyu Vale Trek (photograph the mountain between 17:00 and 18:00 for Picture: Mt. Xuanlian) and The Roaming Abode (draw blessed water at the cave). The viewpoint is Clear Skies Over Xuanlian.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Hilichurls near the quest photo spot; monsters in the shortcut cave that wounded a villager in The Roaming Abode.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Weathered rock, falls running clear and sunset light on a lonely mountain. This is a suggested scene mood drawn from the viewpoint and the scenery guide.

## status
### STANDARD
Unchanged as of 7.1.
