## summary
### LITE
Starglow Cavern is a great, icy cave in the depths of Dragonspine. Strange blue plants called Starglow light even its darkest passages, and before the quest In the Mountains it held one of the three shards of the Skyfrost Nail.
### STANDARD +
The 1.2 preview text calls it a cavern spanning the depths of the mountain, where peculiar glowing vegetation lights every passage with a cold glow.

## geography
### STANDARD
A wide cave system in the heart of Dragonspine. Starglow plants blanket the interior; Ancient Rime, Scarlet Quartz, Warming Seelie, rock piles and an Infusion Stone are found inside.
### LARGE +
Its place in the mountain is tied to the story of Sal Vindagnyr: the Skyfrost Nail that fell on the summit later split into three pieces, and one of them lay here. See the Dragonspine and Skyfrost Nail entries for the rest.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Cryo hilichurls (shooters, grenadiers, shield guards, a Cryo Samachurl), an Ice Shieldwall Mitachurl, and a Ruin Grader, a large ancient construct, guard the cave. The cold is as severe as elsewhere on Dragonspine.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Darkness softened by blue light from the plants, with ice all around and warmth scarce.

## quests
### STANDARD
Linked to the world quest In the Mountains (version 1.2); before it, the cavern contained one Skyfrost Nail shard.

## status
### STANDARD
As of 7.1 canon adds nothing new about the cavern; it remains an explorable cave on Dragonspine.
### ULTRA +
It is a separate place from Wyrmrest Valley and Durin's Core, the other deep parts of Dragonspine; the Starglow plants are not the dragon's lingering life force.
