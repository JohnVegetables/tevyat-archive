## summary
### LITE
Easybreeze Market is the commercial heart of the Easybreeze Holiday Resort and often the first place tourists see. It has restaurants, specialty shops and playful decor made by the resort's Asha.
### STANDARD +
Vendors here include the owners of Asha's Treasure Trove, Floral Whispers and Shoal's Haven, a dessert seller and a seller of 'suns'.

## geography
### STANDARD
The market is the resort's retail district and the entrance to the island's other areas, with Colorfall Cliffs, Huha Hill and Tete Isle nearby.
### LARGE +
Its viewpoint description is titled 'Pleasant Street'.

## places
### STANDARD
Shops and restaurants: Asha's Treasure Trove (owner Macuahuitl), Floral Whispers (Motolinia), Shoal's Haven (owner Teuciztli, with Lawaia as an employee), Puff-Up Pauldrons (Mazatl), Street Fighting Fontainian (Tzompantli) and a dessert stall run by Fournier.
### LARGE +
The resort's Seven-Colored Statue, a giant octopus-like figure that visitors can paint, also stands in the market area according to the resort entry.

## people
### STANDARD
A large cast of vendors, tourists and Asha. Other named residents include Challwa (Gift Egg Ambassador), Kanei, Zhaka and Zhaka's Asha Azhka, and the Ceremonial Greeter 'Teteltetl'.
### LARGE +
Some NPCs only appear after specific quests: Tochtli, Zolin and Zolton after Traces of Chroma, and Ukucha and Nataba ('Sun' seller) after step 9 of To a Carefree Vacation!. Zhaka only appears after he has been rescued in Colorfall Cave, and is Kanei's apprentice.
### ULTRA +
Also listed are Apuaq, Ifelayo, Irqichaykuna Nui, Kapaia, Micha, Navaratra, Philpot and Tecizma. The wiki gives no more profile information for most of them.

## culture
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
Part of the market's story was part of the 5.8 Sunspray Summer Resort event; several quests start with the Traveler arriving here.
### LARGE +
Local Asha design the decor, and the merchants sell food, drink and holiday souvenirs to tourists.

## history
@version: 5.8
### STANDARD
Opened with the resort during its trial season. Quest Traces of Chroma is set here and concerns the Asha Zolton, which the Masters of the Night-Wind priest Alu made.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Busy, bright and relaxed: stalls and chatter, Asha weaving through the crowd and a feel of a holiday town.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Safe. The only trouble linked to the market is the counterfeit Koholasaur doll scam, whose hostage worked in Colorfall Cave.

## quests
@version: 5.8
### STANDARD
Traces of Chroma (world quest). To a Carefree Vacation! (resort story) begins with arrival and introductions.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the market is open.
