## summary
### LITE
Broken Isle is a limited-time island cluster in the west of the Golden Apple Archipelago, the summer-event map east of Mondstadt. Its main island has five peaks with differing water levels, linked to a group of rocks that ring a melody at high tide. It appeared in the Midsummer Island Adventure event (1.6) and again, redesigned, in Summertime Odyssey (2.8).
### STANDARD +
The island is event content, not part of the permanent world map. In both appearances it sat among hilichurl-held islands in sea fog.

## geography
@version: 1.6
### STANDARD
The main island cluster has five peaks with varying water levels. The peaks are connected to a group of rocks, and when high tide presses the rocks down, the cluster rings with a melody. The people who once lived on the archipelago used this sound as a high-tide alarm.
### LARGE +
In the 1.6 version the main island held lakes at the peaks, a mural in a cave behind a painted wall, and Hymnal Rings for the thrown harpastum. Part 2 of the event raised a new island cluster to the north. Northeast, towards Twinning Isle, lies an unnamed island with a wooden ship; a smaller stray island sits far to the northwest.
### ULTRA +
In the 2.8 version the island was rebuilt around musical fixtures (melodic harps, blooms and pedals) and ruins torches, with storm barriers, floating Anemo slimes and a Pyro elemental monument.

## history
@version: 1.6
### STANDARD
Before the event the archipelago was known as the Haar Islands. The wooden ship on the unnamed island northeast of Broken Isle belongs to the Inazuma pirates of Ako Domeki's fleet, about 500 years ago: their boat split in two, and one group was shipwrecked on that Nameless Island between Broken Isle and Twinning Isle.
### LARGE +
The event told this history partly through Echoing Conches, recordings placed around the islands. After the Dodo-King storyline, event NPCs were scattered across the map: Diluc could be found beside the teleport waypoint here after Act 3 until the event ended.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Hilichurls and Hilichurl Berserkers and Fighters, elemental Samachurls, Hydro and Pyro slimes, a Hydro Abyss Mage, and elite Blazing Axe and Wooden Shieldwall Mitachurls (and a Stonehide Lawachurl in 1.6). Sea fog and shifting tides made the area hard to leave.

## status
### LITE
Not accessible in the game as of 7.1. Both event runs ended (1.6 in June 2021, 2.8 in August 2022).
### STANDARD +
Treat it as a past summer-event location: characters may remember it, but it is no longer a place the Traveler can visit by ordinary exploration.
