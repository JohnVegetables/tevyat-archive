## summary
### LITE
Glaze Peak is a rocky subarea on the surface of the Chasm in Liyue. Spoutrocks, loose rock piles and tall unique rock pillars mark it, and its slopes hold Cor Lapis, Qingxin and Violetgrass.
### STANDARD +
The Chinese name is Liuli Peak.

## geography
### STANDARD
Features are Rock Piles, Seelie, Spoutrocks, Unique Rocks and Unique Rock Pillars. Gatherable specialties are Cor Lapis, Qingxin and Violetgrass.
### LARGE +
It belongs to the Chasm's surface, the wide whorl of rock above the mines that were closed after unexplained accidents and are being reopened by the Liyue Qixing. The wiki records no description or point of interest for the peak itself.
### ULTRA +
Glazed Sand Crystal, used to make porcelain, is named in the Chasm's lore as a Chasm specialty, but it is not listed among this peak's gatherables, so do not tie it to Glaze Peak.

## history
### LARGE
Canon gives this peak no history of its own. Its setting is the Chasm, where before the War of Funerary Flame the ancient human civilisation of Lang-Gan lived, and where legends say a falling fragment and an Abyssal invasion later struck. See the Chasm entry for those accounts.

## dangers
### STANDARD
A Stonehide Lawachurl is the elite enemy. Dendro Slimes and Treasure Hoarders (Hydro and Pyro Potioneers) roam as common foes.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Open rock under a wide sky, with Seelie flickering among stone pillars and hoarders camped in the crevices. This is a suggested scene mood drawn from the feature list, not a quoted description.

## status
### STANDARD
Unchanged as of 7.1.
