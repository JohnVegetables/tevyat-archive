## summary
### LITE
Beneath the Dragon-Queller is the Trounce Domain under the great blue-veined Dragon-Queller tree at Nantianmen in Liyue. It is the bed of rock deep in the earth where the Geo dragon Azhdaha is sealed. Within the game it is a "memory": after the Traveler and Zhongli re-imprisoned Azhdaha in Zhongli's story quest "No Mere Stone", the fight can be relived here.
### STANDARD +
The domain's own description calls the rock bed a coffin carved into the heart of the world, and says Azhdaha was in the end unable to unseal itself, so the quaking earth was put to rest. The in-game name of the replay is "Memories: Earthshaking Dragon". It is a weekly challenge rather than an ordinary Artifact domain: one dragon, five elements, and an entrance that shows which elements Azhdaha will draw on that week.
### LARGE +
It was added in Version 1.5 together with Azhdaha as a boss. Treat it as a remembered ordeal, not a place travellers wander into: what players re-enter is a recollection of the fight that took place when an attack on the seal dragged Zhongli, the Traveler, Kun Jun and the entranced miners into the dragon's prison.

## geography
### STANDARD
The entrance is reached through the Dragon-Queller, a huge tree with blue veins growing near Mt. Hulao at Nantianmen, in the Minlin area of Liyue. Its tip points straight at Liyue Harbor. The domain itself is a vast underground chamber of rock "carved into the very heart of the world", with the dragon's tail rising above as the tree.
### LARGE +
The tree is described in canon as Azhdaha's tail, which has begun merging with the tree and reaching its roots into the ley lines to draw power. Bright yellow flowers grow around its base; the same flowers are found in the nest of the Primo Geovishap. A stone tablet before the tree, set up by the adeptus Mountain Shaper, warns passers-by that an evil dragon lies here and asks them not to disturb it.

## history
### STANDARD
Azhdaha was an ancient Geo dragon who once ruled much of what became Liyue and befriended Morax, the Geo Archon (now Zhongli). As erosion ate at his memory and humans damaged the ley lines of the Chasm by mining, he turned on the people he had sworn to protect. Morax and the adepti fought him from the Chasm north to Nantianmen and sealed his eroded self under the tree, with the help of Azhdaha's own benevolent consciousness.
### LARGE +
Later the sealed dragon made a vessel in the form of a small girl, Jiu, who led a party of Chasm miners to the tree so that their pickaxes and its own attacks could break the seal. The benevolent half escaped meanwhile, took shelter in a man named Kun Jun and led the Traveler and Zhongli to the seal. Inside, the corrupted Azhdaha recognised Zhongli as Morax's vessel and called his sealing a betrayal. It was subdued and resealed; the benevolent half said its farewell and left Kun Jun unconscious but safe.
### ULTRA +
In the quest Azhdaha's benevolent side worried that no one could cope with the corrupted self if it woke again; Zhongli answered that Liyue would be prepared.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Azhdaha is a colossal Geo dragon who absorbs Cryo, Pyro, Electro and Hydro from the ley lines in a weekly rotation shown at the entrance. In the domain, characters struck by those elemental attacks while unshielded are marked and take continuing damage (the domain's ley line disorder). Players can clear it any number of times but claim the rewards once a week.
### LARGE +
Rewards are weekly-boss materials exclusive to this fight (Dragon Lord's Crown, Bloodjade Branch, Gilded Scale) plus Ascension gems, with Prithiva Topaz always possible and the others depending on the element Azhdaha has taken in. In-fiction this is mechanics, not lore; in a scene use the sense of crushing weight, shaking stone and ley line energy surging into one body.

## quests
### STANDARD
Unlocked by completing "No Mere Stone", the second act of Zhongli's Historia Antiqua Chapter story quest (the quest requires Adventure Rank 40). Its last quest, "Amidst Chaos, the Rock Is Unmoved", is the story of the fight.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Cold blue moonlit light on the tree above; below, heavy dark stone, tremors, and the dragon's slow voice. The place feels like an old grave that still breathes.

## status
### STANDARD
As of Version 7.1 Azhdaha remains sealed and the domain is a permanent replay. Nothing later in canon changes this.

## entry: Azhdaha's seal and Jiu

@version: 1.5
@availability: permanent
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The Dragon-Queller tree at Nantianmen is part of the seal on Azhdaha. The sealed dragon sent Jiu, a child-looking vessel made of a portion of its power, to guide Chasm miners to the tree and break the seal. A tablet by Mountain Shaper warns travellers off. Jiu is described factually as a small girl whose hatred of Morax was brushed off by those she met.
### LARGE +
Kun Jun, who appears as a stone-memory reader who has lost much of his own memory and is looking for the stone Dragonfall, is in fact a fragment of Azhdaha's benevolent consciousness. Zhongli suspected this when Kun Jun mentioned Dragonfall.

## entry: Erosion and the Chasm
@keys: Azhdaha's erosion
@keys2: Chasm, Morax, Zhongli
@version: 1.5
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
Azhdaha's decline is the standard example of erosion in Liyue's story: forgotten faces, forgotten contracts, then violence. The Chasm mining that disturbed the ley lines, and Morax's failed attempt to halt it by sharing his power, are both parts of the account the benevolent half gives in No Mere Stone.
