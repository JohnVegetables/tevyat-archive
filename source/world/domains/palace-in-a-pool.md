## summary
### LITE
Palace in a Pool is a one-time domain beneath Suigetsu Pool on Watatsumi Island, Inazuma. Its description says that seawater once did not fill the pool and that moonlight, brighter then, flowed through it like quicksilver. It holds the ancient mansion of Umigozen, famed throughout Inazuma for a window where one could drink wine and watch the flowers; now it is empty and only bandits and thieves dwell there.
### STANDARD +
It has a single level, Tutelage: Umigozen's Mansion, and was added in Version 2.1.

## geography
### STANDARD
Reached by draining part of Suigetsu Pool: Electro monuments are unlocked by using an Electrogranum on four probes, then the player dives through an opening toward a root, climbs it, opens a door by switches and drains the rest of the water.
### LARGE +
Inside, sliding doors must be moved to obtain two amulets that open the final gate.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Nobushi: Hitsukeban, a Mirror Maiden, a Treasure Hoarder Pugilist and Cryo and Pyro Potioneers.
### ULTRA +
Recommended elements per the wiki: Pyro, Electro, Cryo and Anemo. The level scales with World Level.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
A flooded grandeur gone silent: rooms with sliding screens and the lingering sense of a once-celebrated house now squatted by thieves.
