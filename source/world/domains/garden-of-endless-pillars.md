## summary
### LITE
The Garden of Endless Pillars is a one-time domain in the Hypostyle Desert of Sumeru's Great Red Sand, west of the Dune of Carouses. It is the last surviving garden of Tulaytullah, the ancient "city of sapphire", once a palace-like place under thousands of giant pillars. The city is long gone; the garden endures and is now a paradise for fungi.
### STANDARD +
The domain's in-game text says that priests and the powerful once walked freely beneath the pillars, and that all that remains of the proud city is ruin. The white tree, Irminsul, and the ley lines are credited with keeping the garden alive through the centuries of desert wars.

## geography
### STANDARD
A garden of broken pillars in the Hypostyle Desert, west of the Dune of Carouses. Within, ruined terraces are covered with oversized mushrooms and flowering plants. The domain has a single level, called Desert Vivarium; five Pyro monuments around its entrance must be lit in order before it opens. Dendro is recommended, and a Dendro character is needed to complete it.
### LARGE +
Inside, bouncy mushrooms and platforms of living plant growth carry visitors up through the ruin toward the top, where the last fights take place. The challenge is mechanical rather than narrative: no story is told in the domain itself.

## history
### STANDARD
The garden is what is left of Tulaytullah, a city-state said to have risen from the tears of the goddess Nabu Malikata after the fall of her fellow Angels. After King Deshret's civilisation fell, the priest Hermanubis founded the original Temple of Silence there and made it a city of wisdom, with shimmering canals, sapphire-domed buildings and gardens of padisarah and pomegranate. The Temple's departure, wars with Gurabad's kings and later conquest by foreign Khagans ended its prominence; its decline was like that of the other desert kingdoms.
### LARGE +
Refugees from Tulaytullah and other failing desert kingdoms later founded Aaru Village. See the Tulaytullah entry for the city's full history; this domain is only its surviving garden.
### ULTRA +
In the city's later history, Humayun of Tulaytullah briefly became king of kings, and after his death foreign Khagans conquered it; a young noble, Goudarz, later rose against them. These details come from desert inscriptions and weapon and artifact texts, not from the domain.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Fungi of several kinds: floating and grounded Dendro fungi, whirling Cryo and Electro fungi, and Hydroshrooms. They drift between the pillars and ambush anyone crossing the ruin.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Pale stone columns standing in a hot, quiet ruin; spore-light and soft, strange growth where a city once stood. The sense is of a place the desert forgot, preserved rather than destroyed.

## status
### STANDARD
As of 7.1 the garden is unchanged: an accessible one-time domain. No later content has altered it.

### STANDARD
Tulaytullah, the City of Sapphire, was a city-state of the Hypostyle Desert whose renown began when Hermanubis founded the original Temple of Silence there. When the Temple's members left after wars, the city lost its defenders and fell, like the other desert kingdoms. Its surviving garden is the Garden of Endless Pillars.
