## summary
### LITE
Frostladen Machinery is a Domain of Blessing on Paha Isle in Nod-Krai, in the Maroon Basin. Its in-game description calls it a once-rumbling experimental outpost of colossal machines, built, used and then abandoned for the sake of ever grander visions. Now the goal feels close, the outpost's service is over and its devices sleep in cold silence.
### STANDARD +
It was added in Version 6.0 with the first Nod-Krai content. Challengers who defeat the dregs that dwell there can win artifacts.

## geography
### STANDARD
Paha Isle is part of the Southern Isles. Before the Fatui came it was an unremarkable island known for sunset-coloured salt marshes and colonies of Tassel Jellies; everything changed when the heaviest fragment of a fallen moonstone crashed on its shores. The domain sits in the Maroon Basin on the island.
### LARGE +
The giant devices are frost-covered and idle. The text says they were built to test certain principles or assumptions, and that once all conclusions were drawn the structures lost their reason to exist.

## history
### STANDARD
Many sacrifices and obstacles were met in building the machines, according to the domain text. Colossal devices were made and then abandoned, each serving a grander vision. The text gives no names or dates.
### LARGE +
Paha Isle is the staging ground of the Fatui in Nod-Krai, operating through the high-tech Kuuvahki Experimental Design Bureau built over the fallen moonstone. This makes a Fatui experimental outpost plausible, but treat that as inference: the domain itself names no owner.

## dangers
### STANDARD
The domain is guarded by Oprichniki infantry (Bombardiers, Medics, Grenadiers), a Cutting Edge All-Purpose Scout Landcruiser, and then the Wilderness Exile and Wilderness Hunter elites.
### ULTRA +
The domain's enemy roster is titled "Derivations from the Deep". The first two stages repeat Oprichniki waves, the third pairs two Wilderness Exiles, and the last is a lone Wilderness Hunter.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Game mechanics, not in-world events. It is a four-level Domain of Blessing recommended for Dendro, Electro and Hydro teams. Its Ley Line Disorder gives all party members 200 Elemental Mastery while the party's Moonsign Level is at least "Nascent Gleam". Artifact sets it drops: Night of the Sky's Unveiling, Silken Moon's Serenade, Resolution of Sojourner and Gambler.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Frost on steel, stilled gears and the silence of something that once roared. Cold and abandoned, with no living caretakers.

## status
### STANDARD
As of 7.1 the outpost is described as at the end of its service. No later content in the pages read says anyone has reclaimed or dismantled it.

## entry: Paha Isle
@keys: Paha Isle, Maroon Basin

### STANDARD
Paha Isle is an island in Nod-Krai's Southern Isles and the staging ground for Fatui personnel there, working through the Kuuvahki Experimental Design Bureau, a high-tech complex. Its expedition description says the heaviest fragment of the fallen moonstone crashed on its shores and changed it from an unremarkable island of salt marshes and Tassel Jellies. Its boss is the Super-Heavy Landrover: Mechanized Fortress.
