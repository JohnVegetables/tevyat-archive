## summary
### LITE
Denouement of Sin is a Domain of Blessing in the Belleau Region of Fontaine, near Romaritime Harbor. In the domain text it is a remote, deserted valley where legend says the first Lochknight hid among the Lochfolk, training harshly in an attempt to atone for a sin beyond redemption. Now only cold wind blows and clockwork meka left without orders still patrol.
### STANDARD +
Clearing it yields artifacts: Marechaussee Hunter, Golden Troupe, Resolution of Sojourner and Defender's Will.

## geography
### STANDARD
A hollow among caverns on the shore of a lake, set in a deserted valley with long chill winds. It lies in the Belleau Region, with Romaritime Harbor as its sub-area.
### LARGE +
Its description says the caverns around the lakeshore became home to the escaped constructs after the accident at the Fontaine Research Institute of Kinetic Energy Engineering.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Ousia-aligned clockwork meka, runaway constructs of the types called Recon Log, Arithmetic Enhancer, Suppression Specialist, Construction Specialist, Geological Survey and Annihilation Specialist meks.
### LARGE +
In-game, the domain's Ley Line Disorder boosts the party's CRIT DMG whenever a character gains or loses HP, so healers help; this is a game mechanic and not part of the story.
### ULTRA +
Clearing level IV is recommended at around level 90 characters per the wiki; four tiers of difficulty exist (recommended levels 59, 69, 80 and 90). Rewards include artifacts from the sets above only; none are unique to this domain's story.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Still and lonely: a land that has "returned to deathly silence", with cold wind blowing through an empty valley and constructs waiting for orders that will never come.

## history
### STANDARD
Fontaine lore as hinted by the domain text: the first Lochknight chose exile among the Lochfolk, seeking penance through training. Nothing more is given about when or why.
### LARGE +
The meka were abandoned after the Research Institute accident. Canon identifies this domain only through that text.

## status
### LITE
Accessible as a permanent artifact domain as of 7.1; no story events are tied to it.
