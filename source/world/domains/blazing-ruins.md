## summary
### LITE
The Blazing Ruins are the remnants of a ring-shaped ceremonial site on Coatepec Mountain in Natlan, from an era before people truly mastered flame. Sages from all corners of the world came to ponder fire and tried to perfect the rituals needed to transform phlogiston. Today it is a domain overrun by scoundrels.
### STANDARD +
Several of the heroes who later followed the "mighty one" (Xbalanque, the Pyro Archon) into the belly of the volcano (the Ancient Sacred Mountain) are said to have trained here. The domain's description invites the player to cleanse the site and perhaps gain the revelations the ancients left. In gameplay terms it is a Mastery domain that yields talent level-up materials.

## history
### LARGE
Canon gives only the broad picture: an age when people had not yet mastered flame; sages studying fire's mysteries; a ceremonial circle used to refine phlogiston rituals; and later trainees among the heroes of Xbalanque's following. No dates, names or events are given.

## dangers
### LARGE
Gameplay layer: the opposition is Tepetlisaurs (whelps, Tepetlisaurus and Warrior variants), Forged Sand mercenaries (Interrogators, Shieldbreakers, Javelineers) and, at the end, two Fluid Avatars of Lava. The Ley Line Disorder gives CRIT DMG +60% for 6 seconds whenever a character's Nightsoul points rise or fall, so Natlan characters in Nightsoul's Blessing benefit most.
### ULTRA +
Rewards rotate by weekday through the talent book series Contention (Sublime Turning), Kindling (Myriad Illusions) and Conflict (Return).

## status
### LITE
Permanently available in Natlan as of 7.1; no plot events are tied to it.
