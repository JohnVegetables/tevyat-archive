## summary
### LITE
The Hidden Palace of Zhou Formula is a Domain of Blessing on Wuwang Hill, in the Bishui Plain of Liyue. Its entrance text says a secret ritual that hides one gate out of eight, cutting off the route by which evil could escape, was performed here long ago and sealed demons inside this labyrinth. Rumor says the method of fitting pillars with Zhou Formula still seals an evil chi. Today it is a Hydro-themed trial where Artifacts are earned by defending a ley line monolith from Pyro-empowered monsters.
### STANDARD +
Its name is one of three "Hidden Palace of [Name] Formula" domains, drawn from the old Chinese divination arts Guicang, Lianshan and Zhou Yi (the Three Changes). It is the one whose English name does not match its Chinese name, which means roughly "Hidden Palace that Assumes Responsibility for Wuwang", a reference to the formulas used to seal the serpent at Wuwang Hill.
### LARGE +
Wuwang Hill's own reputation supplies the mood. The sky above it stays dull grey day and night, blue ghost lights drift through the forest, and Liyue folk treat it as a place for tests of courage. The Records of Jueyun explain "Wuwang" as meaning prudence, and the hill as the place where even the prudent are punished.
### ULTRA +
The Hydro bonus here is a gameplay echo of the story: the monsters fight with Pyro, which Hydro quenches, and the domain's own text tells the player to use Hydro against their "raging blaze". Recommended element Hydro; Adventure Rank 30 to 45; recommended levels 59, 69, 80 and 90 across four levels. Issue 2 of the in-game Teyvat Times named the first-tier Frost II trial (the old name of Roaring Fire) as the one travellers most often failed.

## geography
### STANDARD
The domain stands at the top of Wuwang Hill, a subarea of the Bishui Plain in Liyue, on the hill that rises near Qingce Village. A large rock initially blocks the entrance, as at Cecilia Garden. To open it a traveller lights four ruins torches around the hill (one beside the Geo Monument, one inside a wind barrier, one at a hilichurl camp up the slope, and one north-east of that camp, where a Pyro Abyss Mage appears) and then activates the Geo Monument. Once it is open, the Electro cicins that wandered the area no longer appear.
### LARGE +
Near the hill's top peak a pool is ringed with statues and an incomplete inscription about a being whose bones, spirit, form and soul were wrecked while its flesh survived, with a statue left "to watch in each direction": the wind-swept ruin, the snow-capped peak, the adepti's abode and the city of Liyue. Four statues, facing Stormterror's Lair, Dragonspine, Mt. Aocang and Liyue Harbor respectively, solve it. The text echoes how the Chi was divided and pinned in Qingce, but canon does not state that it concerns this domain's seal.

## history
### STANDARD
The domain's description is the only direct account: a secret ritual that conceals one gate of eight was carried out here long ago and sealed demons in the labyrinth, and rumor says an evil chi is held by the method of fitting pillars with Zhou Formula. Nobody is named as the performer.
### LARGE +
The ritual closely resembles the Seven-and-Eight-Gates Method of Liyue lore, a secret art for suppressing evil spirits and even gods by using eight key nodes of the ley lines, closing one gate so evil cannot escape. In this lore the Gate of Death currently lies at Wuwang Hill. That identification comes from the Springtime Charms event material, not from the domain text.
### ULTRA +
Mortal history here is recent and bleak. In the Springtime Charms story a few months before the event, fetor was found leaking across the boundary between life and death near Wuwang Hill, and the Ministry of Civil Affairs, with Hu Tao of the Wangsheng Funeral Parlor, set out to redo that boundary with the Art of Homa carried by the Seven-and-Eight-Gates Method. Hu Tao herself held the Gate of Death. Separately, the Wuwang Hill Disaster over a decade earlier, in which Hu Tao's father failed to complete a similar rite and died of fetor while a flood devastated a nearby village, is why the hill has been fog-bound since. These are limited-event revelations.

## people
### STANDARD
No one lives in the domain. The Wuwang Hill area has been avoided since the disaster, and local folk tales (told by children of Qingce Village) say a sea monster once lured the young people of Wuwang away with a whale-like song and drowned them, until only elders were left.
### LARGE +
Records of Jueyun adds that the ley lines recorded the hopes and dreams of those who died and recreated them from elemental energy, which can trap travellers much as it trapped the old villagers. Treat this as an in-world legend.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Gameplay rules, labelled as such: the ley line disorder gives every party member a 75% Hydro DMG bonus at all four levels, and levels III and IV also grant a Hydro DMG bonus. The enemies are Pyro slimes, hilichurl fighters and berserkers, Blazing Axe Mitachurls, Dendro Samachurls and a Pyro Abyss Mage. At levels II to IV an elemental node empowers enemies with Pyro or Electro until the buff is removed by reactions such as Vaporize.
### LARGE +
Rewards are Artifacts from Crimson Witch of Flames, Lavawalker, Defender's Will and Martial Artist. The first stage is a defend-the-monolith fight against 20 Pyro slimes; the later stages are timed, with limits from 60 to 360 seconds.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Low grey sky, drifting blue ghost lights, thin fog on the hillside, and the heat of Pyro monsters against the damp of Hydro. It feels like a place where something is both held down and watched.

## entry: Seven-and-Eight-Gates Method
@keys: Seven-and-Eight-Gates, Seven-and-Eight-Gates Array
@keys2: Wuwang Hill, Zhou Formula, Hu Tao, eight gates
@version: 5.3
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
A secret Liyue art that suppresses evil spirits and even gods by activating an array across eight key nodes of the ley lines, each overseen by one person. The array master conceals one gate, erasing themselves from the ley lines, so that the remaining seven stand and evil cannot flee. Its best-known use is the Eight Adepts' sanctification of the god Tao Dou. The domain's own text describes a similar ritual at Wuwang Hill, but names no performer.
### LARGE +
In Springtime Charms the gates were assigned: Life (Keqing), Death (Hu Tao, concealed), Repose (Qiqi and Baizhu), Concealment (Ganyu), Initiation (Ningguang and Lan Yan), Terrain (Xiao), Fright (Beidou and the Crux) and Injury (Yelan). The Gate of Death lies at Wuwang Hill.

## entry: Chi of Qingce
@keys: Chi, Qingce Chi, Chi of Yore
@keys2: Wuwang Hill, Zhou Formula, Morax, Qingce Village
@version: 1.0
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
A serpent god (also called Qingce in the ancient tongue) who nested where Qingce Village stands and was defeated by Morax; its spirit, bones, flesh, soul and form were each bound in a different direction to keep its power down. The domain text speaks only of "an evil chi" sealed by Zhou Formula, so do not assume it is this same being; the two stories rhyme but canon does not link them.
