## summary
### LITE
The Ancient Watchtower is a domain in Natlan's Coatepec Mountain area, a ruin of enormous structures built long ago by the dragons of the land. Legend says those colossal beasts, having explored all the land and sea, raised these towers to look out toward the wider world beyond the sky. The dragons are long gone, and nobody today knows the true purpose of the place.
### STANDARD +
In-game it is a Forgery domain: clearing it yields weapon ascension materials. Its flavour text says the great dragon built many peculiar structures across the land and that old legends describe its outstretched wings blotting out the sky; the text wonders whether that was literal or a metaphor for the longing to see past the sky (the Sea of Stars). Only shattered remnants of the massive structures, and the patterns on them, let present-day people glimpse the forging techniques of that vanished civilisation.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Vast broken masonry and pattern-marked fragments of giant structures under the Natlan sky; a silent monument to a people who looked upward and then disappeared.

## dangers
### LARGE
Gameplay layer (not in-world fact): the challenge pits the party against Yumkasaurs and their whelps, Swiftstep opponents (Seekers, Armed Couriers, Storm Scouts) and, as the final target, a Secret Source Automaton: Hunter-Seeker. Its Ley Line Disorder rewards Nightsoul Burst: once triggered, all party members deal 35% more DMG for 10 seconds, and Natlan characters make this easiest.
### ULTRA +
Rewards are weapon ascension materials rotating by weekday, in the series Scrying Shadows, Attentive Observation and Estimation.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the domain is permanently accessible in Natlan. It carries no named plot; the dragons that built it are described as long vanished.
