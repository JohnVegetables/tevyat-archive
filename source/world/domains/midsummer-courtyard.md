## summary
### LITE
Midsummer Courtyard is a Domain of Blessing near Starsnatch Cliff in Starfell Valley, Mondstadt. Per its entrance text, when the ancient lost civilisation fell, the once magnificent Sommernachtgarten ("Summer Night Garden") was buried underground, leaving only ancient trees and stones to recall its glory. It is an ancient blessed altar full of monsters that fear Pyro, and clearing it rewards artifacts.
### STANDARD +
The name Sommernachtgarten is German for Summer Night Garden.

## geography
### STANDARD
The entrance is by Starsnatch Cliff, a quiet spot in Starfell Valley. Inside lie sunken garden remains with old trees and stones.
### LARGE +
Higher trial levels add an elemental node at the centre that empowers nearby foes with Cryo or Electro until reactions strip the buff.

## history
### STANDARD
Canon states only that the garden belonged to the lost ancient civilisation and was buried when it fell. No builder, date or event is named beyond that.
### ULTRA +
The wiki notes that the trial levels are all titled "Fires of Purification", apparently a reference to Pyro being the recommended element. The garden's name also appears in the title of an in-game music track.

## dangers
### LARGE
Cryo and Hydro slimes, shielded Hilichurls, Abyss Mages, Electro slimes and Fatui Electro Cicin Mages. The ley line disorder punishes certain elemental reactions with a blast, so reaction choice matters.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Overgrown stone beneath ancient trees, a once-lavish garden now silent underground.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the domain is open and unchanged since 1.0.
