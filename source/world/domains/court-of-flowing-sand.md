## summary
### LITE
Court of Flowing Sand is a Domain of Forgery on the north side of Mt. Yougou, Narukami Island, Inazuma. Its description says that in antediluvian times, now lost to history, a foolish ruler dreamed of building a high garden in honour of the white sacred tree over the primordial sands; now a dead silence fills the kingdom built on sand, but the obsessions of the past remain.
### STANDARD +
Clearing it yields Weapon Ascension Materials (the Branches of a Distant Sea, Narukami's Magatamas and Oni Masks series, which rotate by weekday). It was added in Version 2.0 with the Inazuma region.

## geography
### STANDARD
A golden-sand altar where dust 'flows into eternity', on the north side of Mt. Yougou on Narukami Island. The mountain is also the site of the Sacred Sakura.
### LARGE +
The domain text says the lurking enemies are vulnerable to Geo attacks.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Slimes of several elements (Hydro, Pyro, Electro), Abyss Mages, Electro Hilichurl Grenadiers and Crackling Axe Mitachurls, depending on the challenge level.
### LARGE +
In-game, the Ley Line Disorder raises Shield Strength by 40% and makes Geo-reaction Elemental Shards release a damaging shockwave; recommended elements are Geo and Cryo. This is a game mechanic, not story.
### ULTRA +
Four difficulty levels exist (Adventure Rank requirement 15/21/30/40, recommended levels 15/36/59/80 per the wiki). Weekday rotation: Sunken Sands (Branches of a Distant Sea), Altar of Sands (Narukami's Magatamas), Sand Burial (Oni Masks).

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Still and mournful: a ruined kingdom of sand with fine golden dust drifting past a ceremonial altar, a monument to a ruler's vanished ambition.

## history
### STANDARD
Canon says nothing beyond the rumour in the description: an unnamed ruler in an age before the great flood wished to raise a high garden for the white tree over the primordial sands. Treat it as legend.
