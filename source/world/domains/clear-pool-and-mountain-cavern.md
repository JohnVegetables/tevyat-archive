## summary
### LITE
Clear Pool and Mountain Cavern is a Domain of Blessing north of Mt. Aocang in Liyue: an ancient altar in a hidden stone cave where monsters that fear Geo guard Artifacts. Its in-game text says Mt. Aocang looks like pretty scenery but hides a cave "connecting to the heavens" that breathes out clouds.
### STANDARD +
Inside, the ley lines are tuned to Geo. Every party member deals extra Geo damage, and the three trials are all called "Stone Chamber". The enemies are Treasure Hoarders, rock-shield hilichurls and mitachurls, and finally a Ruin Guard.
### LARGE +
The domain's description adds that the saying "where the clouds emerge" was not derived from this cave. The Chinese name carries the sense of the mountain's deeply hidden wonders.

## geography
### STANDARD
The entrance is on the northern side of Mt. Aocang, in the Minlin area of Liyue. Mt. Aocang is the mountain where the adeptus Cloud Retainer (Xianyun) lived in seclusion after the Archon War. Outside the cave, nearby, a ruins treasure with a pillar of light tells the story of a disciple who received a Vision and is linked to a similar one at Guyun Stone Forest.
### LARGE +
Beneath the pretty pools and cliffs lies the stone cave. It is a classic domain: a closed, walled arena reached through an altar gate rather than an open wilderness.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Gameplay rules, labelled as such: the ley line disorder gives all party members +75% Geo damage across the three trials; levels II and III also grant a Geo DMG bonus. Recommended element is Geo. Stage one has Treasure Hoarder Crushers and Pugilists, stage two has Rock Shield Hilichurl Guards and Rock Shieldwall Mitachurls, and stage three a single Ruin Guard.
### LARGE +
Rewards are Artifacts from Bloodstained Chivalry, Noblesse Oblige, Gambler and Scholar. In a scene, treat it as a tight, echoing stone arena with pillars of earthy light.
### ULTRA +
Trial structure, as gameplay detail: stage one asks for 12 opponents (six Crushers, six Pugilists) in 300 seconds, stage two for 10 opponents (eight guards, two Shieldwall Mitachurls) in 360 seconds, and stage three for the single Ruin Guard in 360 seconds. Recommended levels run 69, 80 and 90 for Adventure Rank 35, 40 and 45 respectively. The level names are all "Stone Chamber", which probably reflects the Geo bonus rather than any place in the story.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Cool, damp stone, still pools, thin clouds drifting through cave openings, and the hum of Geo energy in the walls.

## status
### STANDARD
Permanent and unchanged as of Version 7.1.

### STANDARD
Mt. Aocang is a mountain in Liyue's Minlin region, remembered as the retreat of the adeptus Cloud Retainer after the Archon War and until the events of "A Thousand Moonlit Miles". The domain lies to its north and is not described as belonging to her.
