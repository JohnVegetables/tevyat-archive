## summary
### LITE
The False Moon Institute is a Trounce Domain in Nod-Krai, set at the Special Territory Research Institute in the far north of Ashveil Peak. It is a Fatui research facility, and the domain lets the Traveler relive the battle against the Heretic of the False Moon, the Fatui Harbinger the Doctor. The domain is presented as a memory of a fight over the fate of Nod-Krai, which was won by people standing together to defend their homeland.
### STANDARD +
It appeared in Version 6.3 and unlocks after the Archon Quest "Welkin Moon's Homecoming" (Song of the Welkin Moon, act True Moon). Its in-game name is "Memories: Twilight of the Heretic", and the final battle is remembered as one that tore the sky apart.

## geography
### STANDARD
The Institute stands in the northern reaches of Nod-Krai, in Ashveil Peak, the northernmost part of the Pohjola region. The domain text hints at structures buried deep beneath its foundations, and at dusk the place is said to hold the memory of the great battle.
### LARGE +
The arena is an open fighting ground that a rift can open into a second, separate space during the encounter (see the gameplay section).

## history
### STANDARD
The domain text calls the Institute a Fatui facility that hides secrets as deep as the structures under it. The Doctor, Second of the Eleven Fatui Harbingers, is the Heretic of the False Moon: he seized the authority of the three moons and sought what the Archive calls the divine throne of a "new world". He is described as having sought godhood through the suffering of others. He met his twilight, brought down by those who stood together in defence of their home.
### LARGE +
The Archive entry credits him with a creed that Teyvat's order was imposed, not natural: reason cast out what exceeded its limits; life and death bound people into mortal shells; time and space fenced the mind into obedience; and the gods who governed afterwards were false. Stripping every "shackle" away, he held, would reveal the essence of all things, and he called that the first light of a new world. These are the Archive's words for his philosophy, not a neutral statement of fact.
### ULTRA +
The account of how he took the marrow of the three moons belongs to the Song of the Welkin Moon arc (see the quest True Moon and the Doctor's own entry) and is not told inside the domain. In that arc he besieged Nasha Town, and the domain restages his defeat; the closing line of the domain text is that the moon has returned to its rightful home and those beneath it have found tranquility again.

## gameplay
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
Game mechanics, not in-world events. This is a weekly-boss Trounce Domain whose rewards are the Mask of the Virtuous Doctor, Madman's Restraint and the Elixir of the Heretic. The Heretic uses Physical, Cryo, Electro and Hydro attacks. The Ley Line Disorder says he turns a rift from beyond the world against challengers, and that the authority from the marrow of the three moons lets him set prohibitions that must be obeyed briefly or punished.
### LARGE +
Notable abilities: after surviving a single huge hit he raises all RES and recovers the damage ("Return of the False Moon"); Moonwither Realms are Cryo zones that grow and slow characters, with a rift to an alternate dimension where Moon Replica Shades can be defeated to remove them; his "Authority of the Three Moons" bans facing him (Iridescent Moon), moving far (Frost Moon) or reverses movement (Eternal Moon), with energy drain and a trap as punishment for breaking the first two; "Moonfall Impact" has Ancient Moon's Remnants fall and the party must shelter behind them. His voice lines include "With the marrow of the three moons in my hand, behold the false moon's fall!"

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Cold, clinical and quiet: a laboratory complex at the edge of the world under a night sky. The remembered fight leaves an afterimage of cracked moonlight, but the domain closes on calm, with the real moon back in its sky.

## status
### STANDARD
As of 7.1 the domain remains available as a replay of a conflict that ended in 6.3. The Doctor's role as the Heretic is over; the domain does not suggest any ongoing Fatui activity at the Institute. What happened to the facility itself is not stated.

## entry: Heretic of the False Moon
@keys: Heretic of the False Moon, Return of the False Moon, Moonwither Realm, Moonfall Impact, Authority of the Three Moons
@version: 6.3
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The Heretic of the False Moon is the form in which the Doctor, Second of the Eleven Fatui Harbingers, appears as the weekly boss of the False Moon Institute; the Archive files him as "The Doctor". He holds the authority of the three moons and speaks coldly and efficiently, treating weak attacks as dull and stalling as inefficient. In the Song of the Welkin Moon arc he is the antagonist whose defeat ends the crisis at Nasha Town.
