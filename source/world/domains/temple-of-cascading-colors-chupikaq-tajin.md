## summary
### LITE
The Temple of Cascading Colors: Ch'upikaq Tajin is a ruined site at Quahuacan Cliff in Natlan, entered in the Archon Quest "Searing" while following Ineffa. The domain text says Source Servants once offered flowers and candles here to the Eighth (Ix Ajkotz'i'j Iq'ismal Waxak), but the flowers have withered and the candlelight has died; only winds whisper old stories.
### STANDARD +
During the quest the party catches up to Ineffa, goes deeper into the ruins, speaks to a service bot, rides an access module and passes through a secret passageway to the deepest area, where Ineffa embarks on "The Journey Home". The Traveller then defeats the "Sovereign Ruler", the enemy Ix Raq'lapuj Chue'q Buluk in the form Mirror of the Dawning Morn. Ineffa is the trial character (Electro and Hydro recommended).

## history
### LARGE
Three readable "Priestess's Records" (several words are redacted in-game) preserve a dragon ruler's reflections. Record I: Aj Awaj K'umilal Kan storms in, angry that humans have learned to use phlogiston; a human colony near Q'umqaraq'aj, led by a self-styled "Successor of Chaac", razed villages and sacrificed people before Ix Roq'ratik'obala' Ka'alahun ended the uprising in two days. The writer suspects Kukulkan of handing phlogiston to humans but he coldly denies it, and the writer pities him. Record II: the other rulers mostly let humans use phlogiston to kill each other. Kemonel plans a dream-realm in the Tezcatepetonco Range for dragons reduced to beasts, using the souls of peace-seeking humans. The writer, in response, proposes sealing their own domain as an impervious refuge called "Xibalba"; both plans are approved by the sleeping sovereign. Record III: the writer asks Ix Je'lal K'ua'l Ka' to forge a bow (the Chain Breaker) as a farewell gift for their daughter Lianca; Je'lal refuses loudly but secretly sends it, insisting it was made by a common craftsman.
### ULTRA +
The records show dragon rulers disagreeing about humanity: one believes humans lack hierarchy and must be elevated and taught obedience; another wants to hide them in a dream; a third seemingly encouraged conflict. They also treat the Sovereign (Xiuhcoatl) as indulgent and rarely awake. These are in-world accounts by the dragons themselves and reflect their biases, not neutral history.

## dangers
### LARGE
Gameplay layer: Secret Source Automaton Hunter-Seekers in two encounters, an Avatar of Lava deeper in, and the boss fight with the Sovereign Ruler. Two Exquisite Chests lie in the temple.

## quests
### STANDARD
Archon Quest "Searing" (5.8), the third part of the prelude "The Journey Home" in Song of the Welkin Moon, between "Smoldering" and "Where the Moon Rises"; completing it grants the achievement The Journey Home.

## status
### LITE
A quest-only domain as of 7.1. The temple stays a ruin of withered offerings.
