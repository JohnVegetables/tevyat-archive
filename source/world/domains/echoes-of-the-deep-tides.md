## summary
### LITE
Echoes of the Deep Tides is a weapon-material domain of underwater caves in the Court of Fontaine Region. Bandits and smugglers use the caves, beyond the Maison Gardiennage patrol routes, as hideouts, and have stolen clockwork meka. Its flavor text recalls a God King who dreamed of an empire of harmonious music above the waves, whose pride sank with him.
### STANDARD +
Clearing it gives weapon ascension materials (the Robotic Ruse, Artisanship and Curious Contraptions sets) at the Court of Fontaine Region.

## geography
### STANDARD
Cave systems beneath the waves of the Court of Fontaine Region, remote from regular guard patrols.
### LARGE +
The flavor text hints at a ruin from Remuria: an old hall whose golden melodies are silent and echo only with the cold music of the tides.

## history
### STANDARD
The domain's legend speaks of a time before the Sovereign of the Waters had returned from confinement, when another God King dreamed of building a prosperous musical empire on the waves, which failed through human arrogance.
### LARGE +
The text does not name the places or people involved; the Remuria and Egeria links are readers' connections to the God King and Sovereign mentioned.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Treasure Hoarders (Scouts and Hydro Potioneers), Fatui agents (Pyro Agent, Electro Cicin Mage) and Pneuma-aligned clockwork meka.
### ULTRA +
The domain has four difficulty tiers (recommended levels 15, 36, 59 and 80). Its Ley Line Disorder gives a Hydro DMG bonus after Hydro-related reactions, a game mechanic. Weekday rewards rotate between the three material series named above.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Damp, echoing caves with the ring of lost music and the clutter of an underworld hideout.

## status
### LITE
A permanent weapon domain as of 7.1; no story events are tied to it.
