## summary
### LITE
Taishan Mansion is a Domain of Mastery near Jueyun Karst in Liyue that grants Character Talent materials. Its description says people who sought the way of the adepti once had to pass the trials of heaven and earth, and Taishan Mansion was the trial of earth; now the adepti have left this realm, it is a common treasure trove.
### STANDARD +
A tablet above the mansion says it was established by royal decree, but the Geo Lord wanted his home to remain among the people of Liyue, so every trace of the path to that divine place was lost in the mortal world. Its hidden treasure is now engulfed in water, and the tablet adds that people should leave at the sight of the adeptus.
### LARGE +
The tablet is canon text on the environment, not a full history. It does not say who the decree came from, when the mansion was built, or whether any adeptus still visits.
### ULTRA +
Gameplay detail: the domain uses Adventure Rank 25 to 45 and recommended levels 38, 54, 71 and 88 across four levels. Level IV pairs two Blazing Axe Mitachurls with Fatui Pyro Agents, with a limit of 480 seconds. Smoldering Flames at level IV is far stronger than at I to III.

## geography
### STANDARD
The domain sits in Jueyun Karst, the cloud-ringed peaks in northwest Liyue that are said to be where the adepti live. Waypoints stand to the southwest of the mansion and west toward Guili Plains. The mansion's entrance is under water until a puzzle is solved.
### LARGE +
Solving the puzzle: defeat the three waves guarding a glowing monument by the steps, start it to partly drain the water, then guide Geograna to light three stone lanterns around a second monument; this drains more water and exposes the entrance.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Gameplay rules, labelled as such: the ley line disorder periodically inflicts Smoldering Flames on your character, causing Pyro damage until the Pyro is removed. Enemies are Pyro slimes, Blazing Axe Mitachurls and Fatui Pyro Agents. Recommended elements are Hydro, Cryo and Electro.
### LARGE +
Rewards depend on the weekday: Prosperity, Diligence and Gold teaching series, from the Altar of Flames, Heart of the Flames and Circle of Embers levels.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Scorching heat, mist burning off the walls, and the weight of a place built for people who came looking for immortals.

## status
### STANDARD
Permanent and unchanged as of Version 7.1.
