## summary
### LITE
The Valley of Remembrance is a Domain of Blessing between Dawn Winery and Dragonspine in Mondstadt. Its entrance text says that after a time the continent's people lost track of the past and dug downward to find what they had forgotten; beyond an ancient tree's hollow, yesterday's secrets became today's treasures. It is an altar full of monsters that fear brute force, and clearing it rewards artifacts.
### STANDARD +
Its trial levels are all titled "Dance of Steel", apparently a nod to its Physical damage bonus.

## geography
### STANDARD
Located in Windwail Highland in the Dawn Winery area, toward the slopes of Dragonspine; the entrance blurb mentions the hollow of an ancient tree.
### LARGE +
The ley line disorder raises the party's Physical damage, and higher levels weaken enemies' Physical resistance.

## history
### STANDARD
Canon says nothing specific beyond the entrance blurb: forgotten history, dug up and turned into treasure.
### ULTRA +
The domain's artifact rewards are Viridescent Venerer, Maiden Beloved, Tiny Miracle and Traveling Doctor; the Treasure Hoarders and Fatui inside are ordinary hostile groups with no named leaders.

## dangers
### LARGE
Treasure Hoarders of many kinds (Potioneers, Handymen, Pugilists, Gravediggers, Seamen), Fatui Pyro Agents and Electro Cicin Mages.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the domain is open and unchanged since 1.0.
