## summary
### LITE
The Lightless Capital is a Domain of Mastery on Lempo Isle in Nod-Krai: the corpse of a once-prosperous city that later fell into ruin. Hundreds of years ago Nod-Krai had its own golden age, and this city carried humanity's arrogant dreams at the edge of the world, with steel sinews and iron bones reflecting industrial and technological glory. Then calamity descended and the city was dragged into darkness and buried by time.
### STANDARD +
It was added in Version 6.0 and is cut off from remembrance. Its enigmatic structure is said to hint at its future, and those who destroy the foes within may gain insight to improve their Talents.

## geography
### STANDARD
The ruin lies on Lempo Isle, part of the Southern Isles, the same island as Nasha Town. The domain is built of rusted-steel and iron architecture.
### LARGE +
The text describes a city bearing "the weight of humanity's arrogant dreams", with prosperity and opulence later buried by the ruthless march of time. The Chinese name means roughly "lightless deep capital".

## history
### STANDARD
Hundreds of years ago adventurers and treasure hunters gathered in droves at this prosperous city during a Nod-Krai golden age. A calamity then dragged living and non-living things into darkness, and the city decayed into ruin.
### LARGE +
The wiki links the calamity to the Wild Hunt, whose ghouls have plagued Nod-Krai since the Cataclysm; the domain text does not say so. Treat the identification as inference.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Oprichniki Line Troopers, Vanguards and Fireblade Shock Troopers, then a Cutting Edge Fire Support Ground Effect Landcruiser with Hailstorm Cannoneers at the highest level.
### ULTRA +
The first three stages repeat the same Oprichniki line-up. Lempo Isle also holds Nasha Town and the Clink-Clank Krumkake Craftshop run by Aino, and its name comes from Finnish folklore (Lempo, a demon linked with love and fire), as several Nod-Krai place names do.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Game mechanics, not in-world events. A Domain of Mastery that gives Character Talent Materials, recommended for Hydro and Electro teams. Its Ley Line Disorder releases an AoE shockwave (once every 5 seconds) when a character triggers Electro-Charged or Lunar-Charged. The three weekday challenges are named Fallen City (Teachings, Guide and Philosophies of Moonlight), Shadowed Legacy (Elysium) and Overturned Roost (Vagrancy).

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Dark, metallic and desolate: cold iron corridors, dead industry, and a faint sense of a vanished crowd.

## status
### STANDARD
As of 7.1 it is a ruin only; the pages read add no later information about the city.
