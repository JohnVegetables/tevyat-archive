## summary
### LITE
The Derelict Masonry Dock is an abandoned dragon-era facility hidden deep in the heart of the mountain at Atocpan, in the Remnants of Tetenanco area of Natlan. In its day, ash-gray mechanisms (Secret Source Automatons) driven by golden runes did the labour of flesh there, bringing grand designs to life above pools of flame. It is now a desolate shell.
### STANDARD +
The flavour text says the era ended when tyrannical outsiders (linked on the wiki to the Heavenly Principles) arrived, after which the once-powerful dragon race stagnated in submission. The only visitors left are mourners of that vanished age. The text also promises treasures for those who long to reach beyond the heavens (Celestia). In gameplay terms it is a Domain of Blessing that drops artifacts.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
A cavernous, half-ruined shipyard-like space of dead machinery, flickering firelight and the shadow of a once-grand design.

## dangers
### LARGE
Gameplay layer: Tatankasaur whelps and warriors, Ferocious Warriors (Human Ram, Wrestler, Grappler) and a Furnace Shell Mountain Weasel as the boss. Its Ley Line Disorder boosts plunging attackers: a Plunging Attack hit gives +30% CRIT DMG and +20% ATK for 15 seconds.
### ULTRA +
Artifact sets offered: Long Night's Oath, Finale of the Deep Galleries, Tiny Miracle and Scholar. It was added in version 5.5.

## status
### LITE
Permanently accessible in Natlan as of 7.1; no plot is tied to it.
