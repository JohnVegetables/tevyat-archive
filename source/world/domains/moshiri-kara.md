## summary
### LITE
Moshiri Kara is a one-time domain on Tsurumi Island, Inazuma. Its description calls it a mansion set up somewhere outside the Sea of Fog by an unknown builder, now a den of ravenous rifthounds (monsters from the rift).
### STANDARD +
It has a single level, Tutelage: Nest-Bed of Ambition, and gives one-time first-clear rewards. It was added in Version 2.2 with Tsurumi Island.

## geography
### STANDARD
A mansion on the main island of a small group near Tsurumi Island. Three timed challenges on the surrounding islets (exploding barrels, Lawachurls, Electro particles) must be done before the monuments on the main island can be lit with Electro to open it.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Thundercraven and Rockfond Rifthounds with their whelps, Blazing Axe Mitachurls and Rock Shieldwall Mitachurls (22 opponents in all).
### ULTRA +
The wiki suggests Electro and Geo characters; unlocking it grants the 'Moshiri Kara' achievement. A wiki trivia note compares the name to the Ainu 'Mosir' ('land of humans'); that is a fan observation, not stated canon.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Isolated and derelict: a mansion at the edge of the known sea, empty of masters and full of hungry beasts.
