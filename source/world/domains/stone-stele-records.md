## summary
### LITE
Stone Stele Records is a Trounce (weekly boss) domain in Natlan's Tezcatepetonco Range. It represents a huge stone tablet raised in Natlan, carved with the image of a new hero, and lets players relive the battle against the Lord of Eroded Primal Fire. It is unlocked during the Archon Quest "When All Becomes a Monument".
### STANDARD +
Its flavour text says the monument's real foundation was collective will gathered in the crucible of brutal battle, and that its silent carvings remind everyone not to forget the suffering and resolve of the fallen nor the sacrifices of the brave. The description adds that the shadow looming over Natlan finally yielded to the brilliance of the "fire of all fires", and that a plan passed down for 500 years like kindling came to fruition, so that all sacrifices bore their best reward. The memory is etched into obsidian. The domain's alternate name is "Reflection: The Deep, Transient Void".

## dangers
### LARGE
Gameplay layer: the sole opponent is the Lord of Eroded Primal Fire. Some attacks inflict stackable Attrition that eventually drains the active character's HP, and it grants itself a Void Ward that must be broken with a number of elemental attacks; Nightsoul-aligned attacks break it more effectively, leaving it confused.
### ULTRA +
Weekly boss rewards include the Eroded Horn, Eroded Sunfire and Eroded Scale-Feather materials, Borderland billets and Agnidus Agate.

## quests
### STANDARD
Unlocked by the Archon Quest "When All Becomes a Monument" (version 5.3), the conclusion of the Natlan story. Spoiler: the domain's text alludes to the outcome of that battle.

## status
### LITE
Permanently available as of 7.1 as a replay of the final battle of the Natlan Archon Quest.
