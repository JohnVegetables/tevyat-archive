## summary
### LITE
The "Realm of the Night Kingdom" is a quest domain in Natlan's Basin of Unnumbered Flames, entered during the Archon Quest "Into Eternal Night". The domain's own description calls it the realm of the dead, where the "River of Concepts" flows, and says Kachina is trapped here, struggling desperately and needing rescue before it is too late.
### STANDARD +
Trial characters for the domain are Kachina and Mualani. The route is a series of areas: Tepetlisaur and Yumkasaur beasts in abyss-corrupted form, an optional hilichurl camp, more beasts, a group of Thundercraven Rifthound whelps, and finally an Abyss Lector: Fathomless Flames. The way into the Night Kingdom runs through the Remnants of the Secret Source.

## geography
### LARGE
Set in the Basin of Unnumbered Flames region of Natlan, it is reached through the ruins of the Secret Source (see Remnants of the Secret Source). The wiki shows its name with quotation marks, and its Chinese title translates as "Realm of the Lord of the Night" according to a Kinich voice-over cited there.

## quests
### STANDARD
Archon Quest: Into Eternal Night (version 5.0). Rescuing Kachina is the premise given in the domain text; the quest's details are covered by the quest entry, not here.
### ULTRA +
The wiki's list of translated names renders the domain's Korean title as 'Space of the God of Night' and its Chinese title as 'Realm of the Lord of the Night'; the domain's recommended elements are Hydro and Pyro and its level scales with World Level.

## status
### LITE
A quest-only domain; no standing gameplay use after the quest per the wiki's "temporary" navbox. The Night Kingdom itself persists in Natlan lore as of 7.1.
