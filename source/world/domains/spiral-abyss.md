## summary
### LITE
The Spiral Abyss is a special domain on Musk Reef, an island off Cape Oath in Galesong Hill. In-game text calls it a massive underground city built by an ancient civilization long gone, whose purpose remains unknown, with forgotten secrets and treasures buried within. Its first-entry message calls it an inverse tower, and gives it the Latin name Abyssus Obscura, "Dark Abyss". Monsters and humans alike flock to it.
### STANDARD +
Adventurers reach it from Cape Oath's sky wormhole once they are strong enough (Adventure Rank 20) and challenge floor after floor for Abyssal Stars.

## geography
### STANDARD
Musk Reef is a small island reached through a time tunnel (a bluish-purple portal) at Cape Oath, or by gliding there. The Spiral Abyss portal stands on it. The structure has two parts: the Abyss Corridor, which "connects worlds", and the Abyssal Moon Spire, which "leads to the unknown".
### LARGE +
Twelve floors of three chambers each: floors 1-8 form the Corridor, floors 9-12 the Spire. A menu description says the cycles of the moon mark the turning points of fate, and that the treasures at the Spire's end show themselves as the gears turn. Menu text hints that people who ascended to Celestia saw the same helix-shaped divine ladder shown in Visions.

## history
### STANDARD
Its history is not told. The first-entry message says only that a great empire, long gone, built it. Musk Reef itself was once Pilos Peak, a mountain summit reshaped when Barbatos blew away the snow 2,600 years ago, and Leonard failed to climb it.
### LARGE +
The wiki ties "a great empire" to the unified civilization. It also notes the monthly Spire reset on the 16th, which before 16 June 2024 occurred twice monthly; a gameplay detail, not lore.

## places
### STANDARD
Within the domain: the Abyss Corridor (floors 1-8), unlocked from the start; and the Abyssal Moon Spire (floors 9-12), unlocked by clearing floor 8. Each floor has three chambers, each with a Ley Line Disorder.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Gameplay labelled: enemies vary every cycle and often include bosses or Local Legends on the last floors; each floor restricts food, party changes and levelling. Enemy HP is raised on later floors (150 percent on floors 3-7, 200 percent on 8-11, 250 percent on 12 versus the open world). From floor 5 two teams of up to four characters are required, with no character repeated.
### LARGE +
Challengers choose one Benediction of the Abyss at each chamber (buffs that refresh daily), and each month brings a Blessing of the Abyssal Moon to the Spire. Entering the next floor needs six Abyssal Stars from the last.
### ULTRA +
Gameplay detail: chambers must be cleared within 300 seconds on floors 1-4 and 600 seconds from floor 5; each chamber offers up to three Abyssal Stars through timed or Ley Line Monolith goals. Since 5.1, players who fully cleared floors 11 and 12 can quick-claim the star rewards of floors 9 and 10 at each reset. Completed chambers are retroactive for stars.

## travel
### STANDARD
Reached from Cape Oath's edge by the wormhole, or by gliding from Cape Oath to Musk Reef.

## quests
### STANDARD
The Abyss Corridor holds permanent rewards. Clearing floor 3 chamber 3 earns Xiangling through "People's Choice", and floor 4 chamber 3 earns Collei through "Curtain of Leaves". Later floors give Primogems, Mora and artifact reliquary materials; the Spire's rewards reset monthly.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
An underground structure of corridors and a spire under a moon that the wiki says looks unlike Teyvat's, monsters waiting in each chamber, and an everlasting whisper (the Benediction text) that encourages challengers to go deeper.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the Spiral Abyss remains open as a permanent special domain.
### STANDARD +
Its enemy lineup changes with the version (floors 11 and 12 each version, floors 9 and 10 yearly), but no new lore about it has been published through 7.1.

### STANDARD
The Abyssal Moon Spire resets on the 16th of every month, an event called the Moment of Syzygy, and begins a new Blessing of the Abyssal Moon. The wiki infers the moon seen in the Spire differs from Teyvat's and that the Spire follows another realm's lunar cycle; this is inferred, not stated.
