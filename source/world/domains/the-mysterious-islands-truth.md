## summary
### LITE
The Mysterious Island's Truth is a quest domain in Ameyalco Waters, Toyac Springs, Natlan: an underground lava cave the party enters through a giant whirlpool during Mualani's story quest of the same name. The name is the wiki's unofficial label.
### STANDARD +
It is the fourth part of Mualani's "Journey to the Mysterious Island" (chapter "Where the Springs Return", Act III), also linked to the Underground Lava River. Characters involved include Mualani, Karya, Koolau, Nuu and Viramdra. Mualani is the trial character. The party explores the cave, discusses what to do, fights, rescues Viramdra, then escapes and accompanies Mualani to return a talisman.

## dangers
### LARGE
Gameplay layer: Pyro Slimes and Large Pyro Slimes, then a Fluid Avatar of Lava together with Large Pyro Slimes in two waves. Viramdra must be caught within a 54-second time limit. The cave holds two Common Chests and one Exquisite Chest.

## quests
### STANDARD
Story quest "The Mysterious Island's Truth" (version 5.0), part 4 of "Journey to the Mysterious Island"; it ends with a commemorative photo with Mualani.
### ULTRA +
Completing it grants 200 reputation with the 'People of the Springs' (the reputation entry is titled 'Journey to the Mysterious Island'), among other rewards.
