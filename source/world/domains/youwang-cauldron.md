## summary
### LITE
The Youwang Cauldron is a large tea cauldron at Qiaoying Village, in Chenyu Vale's Upper Vale, said to be a treasure granted by an adeptus. Its sediment-choked interior becomes a battleground in the world quest "Threefold Expectations".
### STANDARD +
Tea-making in the village has changed, so the cauldron no longer serves its old key function, but it remains a symbol for the villagers.

## places
### STANDARD
The cauldron stands near Qiaoying Village. Three connected mechanisms in the surrounding mountains, which locals call monoliths, are tied to it; a technician explains they were designed to stabilise elemental flow.
### LARGE +
The technician, Hautemontagne, calls them "Three-Phase Asynchronous Elemental Tuners" and relates their principle to dampeners credited to Alain Guillotin of Fontaine. Uncle Luo holds that mountain Ley Lines wind through here and must be tamed by sacred stones; Hautemontagne politely says the mechanisms are not directly connected to the Ley Lines.

## people
### STANDARD
Uncle Luo of Qiaoying Village worries over the cauldron and hopes it may draw tourists. Hautemontagne, a Fontaine technician temporarily away from the aquabus project at Lumidouce Harbor, examines and repairs it.

## history
### STANDARD
Villagers say it came from the Cloud Retainer of Jueyun Karst. They no longer rely on it for tea. In the quest the technician finds that Elemental Sedimentation built up over many years and spawned elemental creatures, so that the cauldron could not run normally.

## quests
@version: 4.4
### STANDARD
In "Threefold Expectations", after fixing the three tuners, the Traveler and Paimon are drawn into the cauldron's inner space, fight Pyro, Hydro and Anemo slimes and a boss called the Eye of the Storm, and clear the sediment. Afterwards plain water flows from it.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Homely and comic rather than mysterious: village pride mixed with a cheerful technician's jargon.

## status
### STANDARD
Repaired at the end of the quest; unchanged as of 7.1.

### ULTRA +
The quest also contains a bit of dry wit: Hautemontagne quotes the saying that there is always someone better and teapots you have not been to, from Little Mao, and the quest grants an achievement for the tea-in-a-cauldron episode. Hautemontagne was sent to Liyue by the person in charge at Lumidouce Harbor while the aquabus project there was paused.
