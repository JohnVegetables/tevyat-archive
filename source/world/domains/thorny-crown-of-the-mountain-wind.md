## summary
### LITE
Thorny Crown of the Mountain Wind is a Domain of Blessing in Windrest Peak, northern Mondstadt, added in version 6.6. Its description calls it a floral realm hidden amid the mountains, seldom visited because it is remote. Its lore blurb recalls Elysium, an ancient city-state whose people believed it, sealed in its coffin, would outlast corruption and flourish like a sacred forest.
### STANDARD +
Only poetry and blooming flowers remain in the wind; the dream of Elysium, the text says, will not return.

## geography
### STANDARD
Set among the mountains of Windrest Peak, a hidden garden-like realm. The blurb places it in the era when the thousand winds could still brush the Temple of Space.
### LARGE +
The wiki lists the area only generally; no exact landmark is given.

## history
### STANDARD
The entrance text ties the place to Elysium, an Arcadian city-state in these mountains. Its people trusted that the sealed city would resist destruction from outside, but, the text says, all things wax and wane and that dream is gone.
### LARGE +
Elysium and the Temple of Space are covered in the Mondstadt nation entry and their own entries.
### ULTRA +
The domain's Artifact rewards include Celestial Gift, Disenchantment in Deep Shadow, Berserker and The Exile. In 6.6 the Spiral Abyss ley line rewards also switched to the first two of these sets. This is gameplay detail, not lore.

## dangers
### LARGE
Defenders include Mitachurls, a Hydro Hilichurl Rogue, a Stonehide Lawachurl, a Ruin Drake: Earthguard and Domain Keepers. Its ley line disorder rewards Normal and Charged Attacks with a stacking damage bonus.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the domain is open; it was added in 6.6 and no later content changes it.
