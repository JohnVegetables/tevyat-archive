## summary
### LITE
Momiji-Dyed Court is a Domain of Blessing on Yashiori Island, Inazuma. It is described as a silent scene of ever-falling red leaves, where lost causes and unforgettable loves may follow the veins of the earth and coalesce into fruits of the court's white tree. Within, summer and autumn are split into two spheres, one that strengthens Cryo and one that strengthens Pyro.
### STANDARD +
Clearing it yields artifacts: Shimenawa's Reminiscence, Emblem of Severed Fate, Resolution of Sojourner and Tiny Miracle. It was added in Version 2.0.

## geography
### STANDARD
An altar court of red maple (momiji) leaves on Yashiori Island, divided into a warm 'summer' half and a frosty 'autumn' half.
### LARGE +
The challenge platforms intermittently switch between Flame Boon and Frost Boon states; standing on one grants a 60% Pyro or Cryo DMG Bonus. This is a game mechanic, not story.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Electro slimes, Electro Hilichurl Grenadiers and Shooters, Hilichurl Berserkers, Crackling Axe Mitachurls, Electro Abyss Mages and, at level IV, a Thunderhelm Lawachurl.
### ULTRA +
Four levels (Adventure Rank 30/35/40/45, recommended levels 59/69/80/90, suggested elements Pyro and Cryo per the wiki).

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Hushed and melancholy: a drift of crimson leaves and a sense of unfinished longing gathering around the white tree.
