## summary
### LITE
The Peak of Vindagnyr is a Domain of Blessing on the summit of Dragonspine, at the Skyfrost Nail. It was once a ritual site close to the sky where Sal Vindagnyr's priests ascended to face the heavens, until a frigid nail from the firmament buried it in the mountainside. The cold inside is a trial in itself: visitors must conquer it to pass.
### STANDARD +
Clearing it yields artifacts. It opens after the world quest "In the Mountains".

## geography
### STANDARD
The entrance is on Dragonspine's peak at the Skyfrost Nail subarea, a tilted doorway unlike any other domain's. Inside, the unique frigid environment is made of platforms that alternate between a Warm and a Subzero state.
### LARGE +
The surrounding peak was once the lush mountain of Vindagnyr. Its ruins include the white Frostbearing Tree and the Entombed City below, all frozen after the nail came down.

## history
### STANDARD
Sal Vindagnyr was an ancient kingdom founded by refugees on a green mountain in the era of the unified civilization. The domain's description calls the peak a festive site where priests ascended to face the heavens. After the Skyfrost Nail fell, the site was buried and that voice fell silent; the priests did not return.
### LARGE +
In Sal Vindagnyr lore the nail fell from Celestia for unknown reasons, shrouding the city in cloud and snowstorm; the kingdom perished, and the hero Imunlaukr returned too late to find survivors. The domain is named "Elegiac Rime" at every level, an elegy being a song of mourning and rime a quick-formed ice.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Sheer Cold accumulates on characters and drains HP once full; Warm areas diminish it while Subzero areas speed it up. At higher levels Warm areas also let Pyro remove Cryo from opponents.
### LARGE +
Opponents are cryo slimes, ice-shield hilichurls, ice shieldwall mitachurls and, at the final stage, a Frostarm Lawachurl. Gameplay notes: it requires Adventure Rank 30 to 45 across four levels and rewards the Blizzard Strayer, Heart of Depth, Defender's Will and Gambler artifact sets, as of the wiki's current listing. The 4.8 update changed its recommended element to Pyro and revised the enemies.

## quests
@version: 1.2
### STANDARD
The domain is unlocked by completing the world quest "In the Mountains", in which the Traveler helps restore the Skyfrost Nail subarea. Chests lie near the entrance.
### ULTRA +
The same quest grants the Frostbearing Tree offering system and the Cryo Hypostasis boss, and finishing it restores the Skyfrost Nail so that it can be climbed to the top. On discovery the domain gives a small reward of Adventure EXP and Primogems.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 it remains a permanent Domain of Blessing, unchanged since 4.8.

## entry: Sheer Cold
@keys: Sheer Cold, Elegiac Rime, Peak of Vindagnyr
@keys2: Dragonspine, domain
### STANDARD
Sheer Cold is Dragonspine's lethal chill. It builds up on those who linger and costs HP once full. It is eased by sources of warmth and, in this domain, by Warm platforms.
