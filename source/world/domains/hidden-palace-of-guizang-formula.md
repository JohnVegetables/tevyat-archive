## summary
### LITE
The Hidden Palace of Guizang Formula is a hidden treasure chamber under Luhua Pool, in the Qiongji Estuary of Liyue. From the surface it looks like an ordinary clear pond. Entry is sealed behind an old mechanism and a riddle-like inscription, and the palace holds two separate trials: "Voyage to the Sanguine Sky" and "Travels of the Moonlit Venturer".
### STANDARD +
It belongs to a trio of similarly named hidden palaces. The sister sites are the Hidden Palace of Lianshan Formula and the Hidden Palace of Zhou Formula. All three borrow their names from ancient divination systems, which is the only link between them that the game spells out. It is a one-time domain, so a traveller can clear each of its challenges for first-time rewards only once.

## geography
### STANDARD
The entrance lies at Luhua Pool, a calm body of water in the Qiongji Estuary. A platform near the pool carries a circular mechanism. The game's description of the site stresses the contrast between the still surface and the hoard said to lie below, enough to drive a person mad, and adds that few who can decipher its secrets remain.
### LARGE +
Tall unbroken stone pillars and broken ones stand around the platform, with Pyro monuments beneath them. This layout is the heart of the entry puzzle (see travel).

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Use it as a quiet, mildly eerie ruin: still water, weathered stone and an air of old secrets. The inscription at the mechanism says, in paraphrase, that anyone who follows in the footsteps of the immortals must kowtow before the treasure's gate. A wrong guess makes the puzzle reset, so a scene here rewards patience and observation more than force.
### LARGE +
The original Chinese hint reads closer to "follow the traces of those who live forever, and knock on the door of the hidden chamber", which stresses retracing a path rather than simply bowing.

## travel
### STANDARD
Activating the Pyro monuments that stand beneath the tall unbroken pillars, and only those, undoes the seal. Lighting a wrong monument resets the puzzle. Once the seal is broken the two domain entrances open.
### LARGE +
The first challenge is recommended from adventurer rank 30 and the second from rank 35 (game progression requirements, not world lore).

## history
### STANDARD
Nothing in game text says who built the palace or why. The name echoes "Guicang", one of the ancient "Three Changes" divination texts of Chinese tradition, used here as a naming theme for three hidden palaces; this is an etymological note, not stated in-world history.
### LARGE +
No character in the story discusses it, and it has no connection to a specific Archon Quest or named historical figure.

## quests
### STANDARD
No story quest takes place here. Unlocking it grants an achievement named after the palace. The two inner domains are optional gameplay challenges.

### ULTRA +
The sister palaces (Lianshan Formula and Zhou Formula) use the same naming theme. The Guizang palace's two trials are linked to adventurer ranks 30 and 35, so it sits among the early-to-mid game exploration rewards. The Traveler and Paimon have no recorded in-world reaction beyond puzzling out the mechanism.

## status
### STANDARD
As of 7.1, unchanged since its addition in 1.0. It does not figure in the Nod-Krai (6.x) or Snezhnaya (7.0-7.1) content.

### STANDARD
The two trials inside the Hidden Palace of Guizang Formula are "Voyage to the Sanguine Sky" and "Travels of the Moonlit Venturer". They are combat challenges that pay out treasure on first clear; treat them as self-contained trials with no recorded in-world story.
