## summary
### LITE
The Sanctum of Rainbow Spirits is an empty trial ground in Ameyalco Waters, Toyac Springs, Natlan. Tradition says the first sage to learn phlogiston created a place of great purity here, where warriors were taught to wield prismatic flames and flowing light. The sanctum is now abandoned and occupied by scoundrels.
### STANDARD +
Later the powers taught here were turned into weapons in conflicts between the tribes, and the disheartened Sage of the Stolen Flame withdrew from the world and faded from many tales. The domain description says the sage, in the form of a rainbow spirit, taught the tribes' warriors the language of phlogiston, but once the Pyro Archon (Xbalanque) seized the authority of flame the trials became unnecessary. Drive out the occupiers and the player may take the artifacts stored here.

## history
### LARGE
Order of events per the flavour text: the first sage masters phlogiston and founds the sanctum; warriors train there; tribes begin using the powers against one another; the sage withdraws; the Pyro Archon takes the authority of flame; the trial grounds lose their purpose and are forgotten. Dates are not given.

## dangers
### LARGE
Gameplay layer: Koholasaurs (whelps, Koholasaurus and Warriors such as Waveshuttler and Reefsplitter), Flowing Blade soldiers (Grease-Saws, Skirmishers, Harpoon-Throwers) and three Eroding Avatars of Lava; the trial is titled "The Burning Gauntlet". The Ley Line Disorder releases a True DMG shockwave when Pyro-related reactions occur (once every 5 seconds).
### ULTRA +
Artifact sets offered: Scroll of the Hero of Cinder City, Obsidian Codex, Resolution of Sojourner and Gambler.

## status
### LITE
Permanently accessible in Natlan as of 7.1; no plot events are tied to it.
