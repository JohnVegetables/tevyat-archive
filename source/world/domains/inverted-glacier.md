## summary
### LITE
The Inverted Glacier is a Domain of Blessing in the Everfrozen Earth, the northern tundra of Snezhnaya. The domain text describes a barren mountain scourged by endless winter storms, where neither monsters nor outlaws can survive long in the ancient, desolate cold. Scoundrels who dwell there guard precious artifacts for anyone able to beat them.
### STANDARD +
The domain's longer description draws on fae legend: in the oldest tales the dead tundra was once covered in lush greenery and the frozen sea ran with sweet honey. Not even the eldest fae ever saw that paradise. Everywhere the eye turns there are only blizzards that grind down the land and the fragile but stubborn souls clinging to it. It was added in version 7.0 with the Snezhnaya region.
### LARGE +
In game the domain pays out artifacts (Scarlet Proof, Heart of the Furnace, The Exile and Brave Heart) and is staffed with Volkodlak Bashers, Glaciomancers, Glacial Wardens and several Chimeric beasts (a winged elephant, burrowbeast, armoured beast and horned bear). Its Ley Line Disorder rewards Cryo-related reactions with a team-wide Elemental Mastery boost. These are labelled game mechanics, not lore.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Howling storm and bare, ice-scoured rock; a place that feels lifeless and old. A scene here should stress cold, wind and the sense of a promised green land that no one alive ever saw.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Eternal winter storms; Volkodlak outlaws; Glaciomancers and Glacial Wardens; aberrant Chimeric beasts. The domain text says neither monster nor outlaw can long survive the cold, and that scoundrels lurk there.

## status
### STANDARD
As of 7.1 it is a permanent domain in Snezhnaya. Canon gives no named occupant, owner or history beyond the flavour text.

### ULTRA +
The domain is one of the Snezhnayan domains added in 7.0. Treat its paradise tale as folklore: the text only says it is the most ancient legend of the fae.
