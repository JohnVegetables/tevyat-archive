## summary
### LITE
Lost Mooncourt is a Domain of Forgery on Hiisi Island in Nod-Krai. By its in-game text, the Frostmoon Scions once used the place as a sacrificial site and raised a lunar court here, hoping to summon lost moonlight back, but now only desolation and frozen silence remain. Weapon-enhancement materials are earned here.
### STANDARD +
It was added in Version 6.0. Hiisi Island is the Frostmoon Scions' present home, a religious sect devoted to the Moon Goddess, and the ruin is an older altar on it.

## geography
### STANDARD
A forgotten altar of stone carvings with a "labyrinth of time" feel, on a forested island that is largely untouched wilderness under the Scions' care.
### LARGE +
Moonlight once flowed through the stone carvings and has now stopped. The text calls the setting the "border realms" of Nod-Krai.

## history
### STANDARD
The domain text describes an age when three moons rose together. The lunar people read the phases and tides, and what they touched surged with moonlit might. In the waning days the light dimmed and the leaderless people scattered. Legend says the Frostmoon Scions built a lunar court in Nod-Krai to summon the lost moonlight back.
### LARGE +
This echoes the wider Nod-Krai lore: the Three Moons, the Hyperborean golden realm and its survivors, who were guided to Hiisi Island. The court is the remains of oaths from that era ("echoes of ancient oaths"), and the text says secrets unveiled in the quiet still swirl in time.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Oprichniki Line Troopers, Medics and Thunderblitz Gvardiya, then Cutting Edge All-Purpose Storm Landcruisers at the top level.
### ULTRA +
Hiisi Island's boss is Frostnight Herra. Its name comes from Finnic mythology, where a hiisi is a sacred grove or place and, in later folklore, a trickster-like spirit; Hiisi and Paha are the two demon companions of Lempo in the Kalevala.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Game mechanics, not in-world events. A Domain of Forgery giving Weapon Ascension Materials, recommended for Dendro, Hydro, Electro and Pyro teams. Its Ley Line Disorder raises Bloom, Hyperbloom and Burgeon damage by 150% and Lunar-Bloom damage by 50%. Weekday challenges are Radiant Splendor (Artful Device), Prayers Sung (Long Night Flint) and Lunar Oblation (Far-North Scions).

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Silver-grey and hushed: frost on carved stone, a ceremonial space that has long lost the moon it was built to call.

## status
### STANDARD
As of 7.1 it remains a dormant altar. The real Frost Moon has returned to the sky (Song of the Welkin Moon), but no source read says the court has been restored.
