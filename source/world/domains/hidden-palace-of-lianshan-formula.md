## summary
### LITE
The Hidden Palace of Lianshan Formula is a Domain of Forgery near Mingyun Village in Liyue, a thundering ancient sacrificial altar full of Electro slimes that rewards weapon ascension materials. Its description says the Lianshan Formula once raised endless storms inside the mountain, and that the rumbling outside the gate calls curious souls.
### STANDARD +
Its name is one of three "Hidden Palace of [Name] Formula" places, taken from the old Chinese divination arts Guicang, Lianshan and Zhou Yi (the Three Changes). Tradition, relayed by Lan Yan in the limited event Springtime Charms, says the Two Sages of Lianshan, two of the legendary Eight Adepts, used a hidden palace in the mountains south of Stone Gate as a place of retreat. The wiki ties that to this domain; the quest itself says only that it is the traditional belief.
### LARGE +
The Two Sages practised the secret art of Lianshan and preferred to live in seclusion; very little else survives about them. The mainstream account holds that they were human, not adepti, and so likely died long ago.
### ULTRA +
The domain's rules, as gameplay detail: Adventure Rank 15 to 40 and recommended levels 15, 36, 59 and 80 for its four levels. Enemies are Electro Slimes (including Large and Mutant), then a Fatui Electro Cicin Mage at level III and two at level IV, with time limits from 360 to 480 seconds.

## geography
### STANDARD
The entrance sits near Mingyun Village in Qiongji Estuary, within Liyue. Thunder can be heard outside the gate. Inside, huge Electro energy courses through the altar, and dispersed Electro constantly drains Energy from anyone there.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Gameplay rules, labelled as such: every party member gains 75% Cryo DMG (levels I to IV), with Cryo the recommended element; the enemies are Electro-based. Level names (Thundercloud, Thundering Ruins, Trial Grounds of Thunder) fit the storm theme.
### LARGE +
Rewards depend on the weekday: Monday gives the Guyun Pillar series (Luminous Sands, Lustrous Stone, Relic and Divine Body from Guyun), Tuesday the Mist Veiled Elixir series, and Wednesday the Aerosiderite series.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Heavy, dry air, storm light flashing in ancient stone halls, and thunder like a pulse in the walls.

## status
### STANDARD
Permanent and unchanged as of Version 7.1.

### STANDARD
In the limited event Springtime Charms (Version 5.3), the Two Sages of Lianshan are named as two of the Eight Adepts (with the Three Hermits of Guicang and the Three Beasts of Tao Dou). The mainstream story says the Guicang Hermits and Lianshan Sages were human. A hidden palace in the mountains south of Stone Gate is traditionally believed to have been their cultivation retreat.
### LARGE +
Keqing notes that little survives about them besides the secret art they practised. Do not give them personalities, names or events beyond this.
