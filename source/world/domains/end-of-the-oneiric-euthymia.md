## summary
### LITE
End of the Oneiric Euthymia is a Trounce Domain on Mt. Yougou, Narukami Island, Inazuma. It is unlocked after Transient Dreams, the second act of the Raiden Shogun's Story Quest (Imperatrix Umbrosa Chapter), and lets players relive the battle against Magatsu Mitake Narukami no Mikoto, called the Shogun, as a memory. Its description says dreams once blew about like petals, never fulfilled; this is the origin of Eternity, and also 'her' end.
### STANDARD +
Version 2.5 added both the quest and the domain. Rewards are weekly-boss-style materials (Mudra of the Malefic General, Tears of the Calamitous God, The Meaning of Aeons) and Vajrada Amethyst gems.

## geography
### STANDARD
The domain sits on Mt. Yougou, where the Sacred Sakura grows. Its quest version is fought during the quest steps Farewell to the Past and Radiant Sakura.

## dangers
### STANDARD
A single boss, Magatsu Mitake Narukami no Mikoto. She absorbs 'Ominous Destiny'; when it fills she enters the Baleful Shadowlord form, which drains active characters' Energy and resists damage.
### LARGE +
In-game, the Shogun continuously absorbs Ominous Destiny, gaining more when she hits characters; using the right elements shortens the Baleful Shadowlord form and yields Energy.
### ULTRA +
Four tiers (Adventure Rank 35/40/45/50; recommended levels 60/70/80/90). The quest version differs: she fully heals twice, Ei's burst is permanently active for the player, and she gains no shield. These are game mechanics.
