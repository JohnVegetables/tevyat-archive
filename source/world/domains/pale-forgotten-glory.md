## summary
### LITE
Pale Forgotten Glory is a talent-material domain: the submerged wreckage of a once-proud ship on the seabed north-east of Poisson, in Salacia Plain. What should have been a quiet final resting place is full of mysterious creatures.
### STANDARD +
Clearing it yields materials for talent advancement, in the Equity, Justice and Order series.

## geography
### STANDARD
Underwater wreckage beneath Fontaine's waters, north-east of the town of Poisson in the Salacia Plain.
### LARGE +
Hull and decks lie ruined and flooded; what the ship carried or where it was bound is not told.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Breacher Primus creatures in Shatterstone and Overgrown varieties, with larger versions in the later stages.
### LARGE +
Gameplay note: the domain's Ley Line Disorder increases the active character's Normal, Charged and Plunging Attack damage when any character gains or loses HP.
### ULTRA +
It has four difficulty tiers (recommended levels 38, 54, 71 and 88). Weekday talent books rotate through Equity, Justice and Order, each series with Teachings, Guide and Philosophies grades.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Dim, pale and silent, with the faded grandeur of a wreck in deep water.

## status
### LITE
A permanent talent domain as of 7.1; no story events are tied to it.
