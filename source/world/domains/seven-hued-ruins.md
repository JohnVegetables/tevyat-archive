## summary
### LITE
The Seven-Hued Ruins are the underground remains of the Battle of Seven Colors in Natlan's Tezcatepetonco Range. Citlali, following directions from Etchemin, finds the entrance to the ruined battlefield, and the Traveller goes in with her. The domain's text notes that the secrets of the battle turned out to be buried deep beneath the ground.
### STANDARD +
The quest is the third part of "The Truth of the Battle of Seven Colors" in Citlali's Tribal Chronicles chapter "Memories Flowing With Color" (version 5.3). After analysing the mechanisms and leaving a labyrinth, the party must stop Montluson and his group, a Fatui party that includes the Pyro Agent Grudoc, and then capture Montluson.

## dangers
### LARGE
Gameplay layer: two colour-coded routes in the first area (Yellow: abyss-version Tepetlisaurs; Pink: abyss-version Koholasaurs), a mixed second area (abyss-version Koholasaurus, Tepetlisaurus and Yumkasaurus), and a Fatui encounter in the third in which defeating Grudoc ends the fight. A trial Citlali is provided (Cryo and Anemo recommended).
### ULTRA +
The domain holds one Common Chest (behind a purple wall) and one Exquisite Chest (after the purple platforms); both are missable once the quest moves on.

## quests
### STANDARD
Story quest "Seven-Hued Ruins" (5.3), part 3 of "The Truth of the Battle of Seven Colors", between "Please, great shaman!" and "The Color of Memory".
