## summary
### LITE
Ridge Watch is an ancient ruined site at Stone Gate, in the mountains where Mondstadt and Liyue meet. The domain description likens it to a boundary marker looking north and south, over mountain and river. Those who endure its trial can win artifacts.
### STANDARD +
It is a domain of blessing, so its place in the world is as a ritual and proving ground, not a settlement.

## geography
### STANDARD
Ridge Watch stands at the start of the ridge in the Stone Gate area of Bishui Plain, on the mountainous route linking Mondstadt and Liyue. The in-game blurb describes ancient city ruins that overlook both north and south.
### LARGE +
Legend, according to the domain text, holds that its gates and blessings open only to those whose hearts are lit with an eternal blaze, a theme echoed by its Pyro-heavy defenders.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
A stern, wind-swept stronghold of old stone. The text speaks of toughness of spirit and body and of not fearing pain: whoever conquers the trial gains the protection of the ritual site and the power to pursue far-off dreams.

## dangers
### STANDARD
The domain is guarded by Pyro slimes, hilichurl berserkers, fire-wielding Mitachurls and Geovishap. Its Ley Line Disorder (a gameplay effect) sends backlash shockwaves at attackers who strike a shielded character, which is why the levels are all titled "Unyielding".
### LARGE +
The artifact sets offered are Tenacity of the Millelith, Pale Flame, Brave Heart and Martial Artist (gameplay detail). The game gives no formal tie between the Millelith and these ruins.

## history
### STANDARD
No named historical event is attached to the ruins. Their apparent role as a boundary watchpost is an impression from the domain text, not a stated fact.

## status
### STANDARD
Added in 1.5 and unchanged as of 7.1.

### ULTRA +
All four levels carry the same Ley Line Disorder, and the later levels advise shield or Crystallize support; this is gameplay guidance, not story. The domain's recommended levels scale from 59 to 90.
