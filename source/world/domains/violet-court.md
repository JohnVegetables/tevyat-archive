## summary
### LITE
Violet Court is a Domain of Mastery in Kannazuka, Inazuma, an altar where violet sakura bloom eternal. Its description recounts that in the ancient past the mountains rose higher than the sky and the earth was larger than the dome of heaven, until the mirror shattered and the oceans arose; in these legends the court of the primeval sakura was severed from the other islands. Monsters now storm the altar.
### STANDARD +
Clearing it yields Talent level-up materials (the Transience, Elegance and Light series by weekday). It was added in Version 2.0. The enemies are Electro-themed: Hilichurls, Nobushi, Treasure Hoarders and, at the top level, the Kairagi pair.

## geography
### STANDARD
An island of Kannazuka with a sakura-ringed altar. The domain entrance island appears on the map once the Statue of The Seven on Narukami Island is unlocked, even though the domain belongs to Kannazuka.
### LARGE +
Two Electro Crystalfruits stand at the arena's sides in the challenge; during the fight they occasionally produce Nascent Electro Pearls.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Hilichurls and Electro Hilichurl Grenadiers and Shooters, Crackling Axe Mitachurls, Nobushi (Jintouban, Hitsukeban, Kikouban), Treasure Hoarders, and at level IV the Kairagi: Fiery Might and Kairagi: Dancing Thunder.
### LARGE +
In-game, collecting Nascent Electro Pearls makes attacks release Falling Thunder, a true Electro damage effect, and all characters gain a 25% Electro DMG Bonus. At level IV the two Kairagi heal and strengthen each other if one falls first, so they must be defeated together. This is game mechanics, not story.
### ULTRA +
Weekday rotation: Reign of Violet (Transience), Thundering Valley (Elegance), Vine-Infested Ruins (Light). Four levels (Adventure Rank 25/28/36/45, recommended levels 38/54/71/88). Destroying all clay pots on the entrance island summons a named Nobushi: Kikouban, whose defeat yields a Luxurious Chest.

## history
### STANDARD
Only legend: the sakura court was cut off when the mirror shattered (the wiki links the wording to the war of the funerary flame, but the domain itself gives no names). The Sacred Sakura on Mt. Yougou is the better-known primeval sakura in Inazuma.
