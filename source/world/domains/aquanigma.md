## summary
### LITE
Aquanigma is the hidden headquarters of Vacher's Sinthe operation, reached by diving beneath Salacia Plain in Fontaine. In it the Traveler and Paimon found the factory where Sinthe was mixed and the laboratory where Vacher dissolved people in Primordial Seawater. It exists in game only as a quest domain during the Archon Quest "When All Return to the Waters".
### STANDARD +
The Traveler went there while Navia was stalling in the Opera Epiclese trial; the evidence found inside let her prove that Marcel, head of the Confrerie of Cabriere, was the hidden figure Vacher.

## geography
### STANDARD
An underwater base with an unremarkable entrance that was found with the help of intelligence from Melus. Inside are an elevator, mechanisms that raise the water level to open the path upward, flooded passages needing a dive, and a final inner chamber that served as the innermost sanctum. A cracked wall can only be broken by a Xenochromatic Armored Crab's ability.
### LARGE +
The route runs from the guarded entrance through a lift, flooded sections and several mechanism rooms to the innermost laboratory; all enemies met on the way are Ousia-aligned meks.

## places
### LARGE
The innermost chamber holds the evidence the Traveler examined: an experiment table with records of the experiments, shelves of bottles labelled as ingredients, mixtures in progress and ready-to-drink Sinthe (including a fruit-flavoured kind), boxes of personal belongings each labelled with a woman's name, a basin of pure Primordial Seawater, and a bookshelf with an investigation report on Callas.
### ULTRA +
Gameplay-wise the interactable items are logged in the Case Record as "Vacher's Experimental Records", "Sinthe", "The Missing Person's Possessions" and "Primordial Seawater Pool". The experiment records mention a researcher trying to repeat Jakob Ingold's findings on the Primordial Sea so he could save his lover Vigneire, who had been dissolved; the records call many specimens dissolved without any returning.

## dangers
### STANDARD
The base is defended by Ousia-aligned clockwork meka (recon, survey and assault types) and by its mechanisms. During the quest the active party is limited to the Traveler.
### LARGE +
Combat comes in waves of meks as the Traveler works through the mechanisms (gameplay detail: an Exquisite Chest rewards the second wave).

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Cold, quiet and clinical: a business and a laboratory in one, with neat labelling, rows of bottles and mementos of the missing. The writing in the experiment records is flat and matter-of-fact about horrifying results, which unsettles Paimon.

## quests
### STANDARD
Visited in the Archon Quest "When All Return to the Waters" (Chapter IV, Act II "As Light Rain Falls Without Reason", 4.0). Finds here support Navia's charge in court and show that Sinthe, the serial disappearances and Vacher were tied together.
### LARGE +
The discoveries show that Sinthe was diluted Primordial Seawater, that the missing young women's belongings were kept here, and that the researcher behind the experiments was named Vacher.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 canon gives no further use of the base. After Vacher's arrest the Sinthe production line was stopped and authorities moved to seize remaining stock.
### STANDARD +
Later events dealt with imitation Sinthe made from ordinary ingredients; Aquanigma is not shown again.
