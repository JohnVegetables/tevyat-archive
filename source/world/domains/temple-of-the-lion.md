## summary
### LITE
The Temple of the Lion is a ruined temple on Falcon Coast in Galesong Hill, devoted to the Lion of the South, one of the Four Winds. It has long been abandoned, and its ruins show signs of monster activity. Folklore holds that the dandelions seen riding the winds across Mondstadt symbolise the Lion's blessing.
### STANDARD +
In the Prologue the Traveler clears it with Lisa, the Librarian, while Knights of Favonius work to contain the dragon Stormterror.

## geography
### STANDARD
It stands on Falcon Coast, the shoreline of Galesong Hill. Inside, wind currents carry visitors between rooms, with Doors of Resurrection, consoles and Electro monuments guarding the way and chests hidden behind crates.
### LARGE +
Gameplay notes: the one-time domain "Investigate: Secret Hideout" is recommended at Adventure Rank 18 with Electro, and Elemental Trials become available here at Adventure Rank 16.

## history
### STANDARD
Like the other Four Winds temples, it dates from the centuries after Vennessa's revolution when Mondstadters worshipped the Four Winds. After the cataclysm the worship faded and the temple was abandoned. Canon gives no precise founding date.

## quests
@version: 1.0
### STANDARD
In "Sparks Amongst the Pages" (Prologue Act I), Lisa meets the Traveler at the temple and joins as a trial character in the story domain "Deep Within the Temple of the Lion". She senses strong elemental energy deeper within; the pair destroy Stormterror's crystal, a source of the dragon's power.
### LARGE +
After the crystal is destroyed Lisa names the Four Winds (Dragon of the East, Lion of the South, Wolf of the North, Falcon of the West) as guardians tied to Barbatos, and says the Dragon, whose true name is Dvalin, can channel only three of the four winds' power. She attributes this to hatred of Mondstadt and hands over a very old book, Breeze Amidst the Forest Supplement: The Dragon Tome, for the full story. Paimon notes that clearing the third temple clears the sky.
### ULTRA +
The Prologue story domains for the three temples run concurrently: Lisa with Electro here, Amber with Pyro at the Temple of the Falcon and Kaeya with Cryo at the Temple of the Wolf. The Traveler also picks up an Anemoculus in one of the temples, which Paimon explains can be offered to the Statues of the Seven.

## dangers
### LARGE
Slimes, hilichurl fighters and shooters, and in the repeatable domain Abyss Mages (Hydro and Cryo) and slimes. The wiki describes the one-time domain as requiring the player to kill slimes in quick succession.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 an abandoned ruin on Falcon Coast; its story domain is complete and its one-time domain and Elemental Trials remain available.

## entry: Lion of the South
@keys: Lion of the South, Temple of the Lion, Lionfang Knight
@keys2: Mondstadt, Falcon Coast, Four Winds
### STANDARD
The Lion of the South is the Four Winds guardian whose title is, by Knights tradition, passed to Grand Masters and Acting Grand Masters as the Dandelion or Lionfang Knight; Jean holds it now. The temple's own text associates the Lion with dandelions carried on the wind. The title's exact scope is not settled in canon.
