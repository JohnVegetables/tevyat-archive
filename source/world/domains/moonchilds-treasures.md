## summary
### LITE
Moonchild's Treasures is a Domain of Blessing on Ashveil Peak in Nod-Krai, the sacred vault of a ruined tower. By the domain's text, descendants of the far north raised the tower for followers of the morning star, as a sign of resolve to resist fate and the will of the gods. Priests of the Moonchildren hid the last sparks of an ancient civilization's splendor here. Villains and owls now infest the ruin.
### STANDARD +
It was added in Version 6.3. The priests hoped that when the light of the Welkin Moon returned to the earth, it would guide the wandering and displaced back to their former paradise.

## geography
### STANDARD
A ruined tower and its vault in the mountains of Ashveil Peak, the northernmost part of Pohjola.
### LARGE +
The text says the tower bore witness to a dream that shone radiantly before sky-fire reduced it to ash. The top level pits challengers against Fishers of Hidden Depths, listed among Ashveil Peak's elite enemies.

## history
### STANDARD
The tower was raised by the far-north descendants of Hyperborea, the golden realm of the three moons, as an act of defiance for the followers of Koitar, the morning star. When the tower fell, its sacred Mekhaneikos were carried off by those with ill intent. The last generations of that era let even their hope fade, and drifted into ignorance and confusion.
### LARGE +
"Mekhaneikos" is the Hyperboreans' own term for their advanced apparatuses, developed under Koitar and Anacharsis; wider lore says Hyperborea was destroyed by a Celestial Nail launched by the Heavenly Principles, which the domain text's "sky-fire" probably refers to, though it does not say so. Hyperborean records also mention a tower meant to reach Celestia, but the wiki does not connect it to this vault. The owls are the only ones left to voice what was forgotten.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Oprichniki Vanguards and Fireblade Shock Troopers with Force Recon Storm and Ground Effect Landcruisers, and Fishers of Hidden Depths on the final level. The in-game description calls the invaders villains seeking sacred relics.
### ULTRA +
The enemy roster is titled "Sacred Vault". Ashveil Peak's open-world boss is the Lord of the Hidden Depths: Whisperer of Nightmares. The Chinese name of the domain is "Yuetong de Kucang" (roughly "the Moonchild's storehouse"). It arrived in the same patch as the False Moon Institute, also in Ashveil Peak.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Game mechanics, not in-world events. A four-level Domain of Blessing recommended for Hydro, Geo, Electro and Dendro teams. Its Ley Line Disorder gives all party members +35% Lunar-Charged, Lunar-Crystallize and Lunar-Bloom DMG and +30% DEF for 10 seconds after a character triggers one of those reactions. Artifact sets: Aubade of Morningstar and Moon, A Day Carved From Rising Winds, Tiny Miracle and Instructor.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Elegiac and moonlit: broken stone, hooting owls and an old hope still faintly kept.

## status
### STANDARD
As of 7.1 the vault is held by invaders seeking relics, and its Mekhaneikos were carried off long ago. The real moon has returned to Nod-Krai's sky, but no source read says the priests' hope has been carried out here.
