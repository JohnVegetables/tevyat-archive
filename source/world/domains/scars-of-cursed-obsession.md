## summary
### LITE
Scars of Cursed Obsession is a Forgery domain in Snezhnaya's Everfrozen Earth. Its description calls it a secret haven where rebels chased the dream of a return to the old order, a dream that died in crimson fire and left only ash. Clearing it grants weapon ascension materials.
### STANDARD +
The longer text sets the story in a peaceful era when the Belyi Tsar's conquests had become legend and the succession turmoil was mere history. Fae who never lived through those days still yearned to revive that ancient glory. A leaden dream, stained by countless souls' blood, left its dreamer unshaken, convinced it was the way to honour. At the road's end there was no glory and no old world, only blood that had grown cold and foul and drowned her. It appeared in 7.0.
### LARGE +
Gameplay data (labelled as such): the Monday, Tuesday and Wednesday rewards are the Cast Iron, Chain Track and Severed Steel series (with Pale Star Army, Cellared Spiritual Nectar and Frost Emperor themed names); enemies include Volkodlak raiders, sharpshooters, lobbers and bashers, a Leomastiff Guard, a Voywolf Hunter and a Fanged Zealot. Its Ley Line Disorder boosts Swirl damage.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Ash-grey ruin of a failed uprising; zeal curdled into grief. Scenes here suit themes of nostalgia, obsession and futile restoration.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Armed Volkodlak bands and wolf-like fighters, plus a fanatical zealot at the end of the trial; the winter cold of the Everfrozen Earth.

## status
### STANDARD
Permanent domain as of 7.1. The rebels are gone; canon gives no further detail.

### ULTRA +
The text mentions a succession turmoil after the Belyi Tsar's conquests; do not assume the rebels were tied to any named historical faction beyond that hint.
