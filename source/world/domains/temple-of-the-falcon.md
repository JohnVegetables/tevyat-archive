## summary
### LITE
The Temple of the Falcon is a ruined temple at Windrise in Galesong Hill, between Mondstadt City and the great oak where Vennessa is said to have ascended. It was devoted to the Falcon of the West, one of the Four Winds, and has been abandoned for centuries. Its in-game description says it has watched over Windrise for thousands of years, and that perhaps the winds of the past still linger in its halls.
### STANDARD +
It is one of three Four Winds temples the Traveler clears in the Prologue, with Amber as companion. Strong winds fill its underground corridors. Much later the Abyss Order is found to have re-infiltrated it.

## geography
### STANDARD
The temple stands at Windrise in Galesong Hill. Though the structure lies deep underground, wind currents run through every corridor, and glider updrafts carry visitors between rooms. Inside are sealed doors, pressure mechanisms and Doors of Resurrection, with chests tucked away in side chambers.
### LARGE +
The entrance sits between Mondstadt City and the Windrise oak. Hilichurl camps and monsters have taken over the ruined halls; gameplay describes the interior as a one-time domain.

## history
### STANDARD
After Vennessa's revolution a thousand years ago the Four Winds were formally established, and for roughly five hundred years Mondstadters built temples to them. After the cataclysm that worship faded; the temples were left to ruin and nearly forgotten. By the Prologue, Amber says the Mondstadters almost never visit them.
### LARGE +
Because the Falcon's identity is disputed (see uncertainties), the temple does not settle whom it honoured: it names the Falcon of the West and little more. The domain text calls it a temple of the "benevolent" Falcon.

## quests
@version: 1.0
### STANDARD
In "Winds of the Past" (Prologue Act I), Stormterror's attack on Mondstadt has left elemental flows in the old Four Winds temples. Amber waits at the Temple of the Falcon, joins as a trial character in the story domain "Deep Within the Temple of the Falcon", and helps destroy a crystal Stormterror is using to draw power. Amber remarks that even Stormterror has abandoned "its own temple". Clearing the third such temple clears the skies; the Traveler also finds an Anemoculus in one of them.
### LARGE +
During the world quest "Bough Keeper: Dainsleif", Dainsleif says that the Abyss Order has re-infiltrated the temple and leads the Traveler back to it. The story domain "Abyssal Traces" there has the Traveler fight through monster-filled rooms looking for traces of the Abyss Herald; once the monsters are beaten, the search moves on to Wolvendom. Standing at the temple, Dainsleif muses on the Falcon of the West in a cryptic aside about something that can soar on the wind but merely hovers under the gods' light; he names nobody.
### ULTRA +
The Prologue story domains for the Falcon, Lion and Wolf temples run concurrently: Amber with Pyro, Lisa with Electro, Kaeya with Cryo. Each ends with the crystal's destruction.

## dangers
### LARGE
Hilichurls, slimes and Abyss Mages (including a Pyro Abyss Mage in the Abyssal Traces domain) occupy the rooms. As a gameplay note, the one-time domain "Explore: Windy Cliff" is recommended at Adventure Rank 12.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 it is an abandoned, little-visited ruin at Windrise, its story domains complete and its one-time domain enterable.
### STANDARD +
Nothing in 6.x or 7.0-7.1 content changes it.

## entry: Falcon of the West
@keys: Falcon of the West, Temple of the Falcon, Windy Cliff
@keys2: Mondstadt, Windrise, Four Winds
### STANDARD
The Falcon of the West is the fourth of Mondstadt's Four Winds. Common belief holds it is Vennessa after her ascension, and the Knights of Favonius are sometimes called the Falcon collectively; canon never settles it. The Temple of the Falcon at Windrise is the only Falcon shrine shown in-game.
