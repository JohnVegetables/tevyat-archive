## summary
### LITE
Remnants of the Secret Source is the ruined former home of ancient dragonkind in Natlan's Basin of Unnumbered Flames, and the only path to the Night Kingdom. Influenced by the Abyss, it hides many unforeseen dangers. It is a quest domain entered during the Archon Quest "Echoes of Life".
### STANDARD +
The route winds through the ruins. The traveller uses the Spiritsconces of Natlan's saurian creatures in turn: a Yumkasaurus to cross Coilgrass Sigils, a Koholasaurus to travel along a Spiritway, and a Tepetlisaurus to cross a Molten Fissure and smash a rock wall, then climbs a solid phlogiston wall to enter the Night Kingdom.

## dangers
### LARGE
Gameplay layer: abyss-corrupted Koholasaurs, Yumkasaurs and Tepetlisaurs; hilichurls, a Pyro Abyss Mage, Crackling Axe Mitachurls, an Electro Samachurl, Hilichurl Grenadiers and a Stonehide Lawachurl. The domain holds two Common Chests and one Exquisite Chest. The recommended elements listed are Hydro and Pyro and the level scales with World Level.
### ULTRA +
Mechanisms must be activated to open doors between combat arenas. The final climb relies on the Tepetlisaurus's ability to scale solid phlogiston.

## quests
### STANDARD
Archon Quest: Echoes of Life (version 5.0), in which the Traveller is led toward the Night Kingdom; its plot is covered in the quest entry.

## status
### LITE
A quest domain; the Night Kingdom beyond it is the destination, and as of 7.1 nothing further is added to the domain.
