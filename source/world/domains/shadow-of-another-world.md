## summary
### LITE
Shadow of Another World is a Trounce Domain in Salacia Plain, Fontaine, unlocked after completing the Archon Quest "Finale". It lets the Traveler relive the battle against the All-Devouring Narwhal, the beast from another world. Its flavor text says that battle was the intersection of this world's fate, that Fontaine now treads a brighter path, and that the giant's whale-song will not fade from memory.
### STANDARD +
The fight is titled "Memories: The Titan Amidst the Stars". It rewards weekly-boss materials: Lightless Silk String, Lightless Eye of the Maelstrom and Lightless Mass.

## places
### STANDARD
The domain is a recreated arena rather than a place with its own geography. The recollection is reached from Salacia Plain.
### LARGE +
Players may enter the narwhal's belly mid-fight, where a dark shadow must be defeated.

## dangers
### STANDARD
The All-Devouring Narwhal and the dark shadow within its belly. Hostility builds as the player strikes the beast or destroys the Eye of the Maelstrom, until the narwhal devours a character; defeating the shadow inside weakens it.
### LARGE +
Pneuma or Ousia attacks can quickly destroy the whirlpool and interrupt the shadow. When the narwhal's health is low it stops devouring characters. These are game mechanics.
### ULTRA +
The domain requires Adventure Rank 35 or higher at its first tier (35, 40, 45, 50 across tiers) with recommended levels 60 to 90. Besides weekly-boss drops the reward table gives a Midlander billet and Varunada Lazurite.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Remembered rather than real: echoing whale-song, a looming starlit giant, and the relief that Fontaine's fate turned out brighter.

## quests
### STANDARD
Unlocks after the Archon Quest "Finale" in Chapter IV, whose final battle it recalls.

## status
### LITE
A permanent boss domain as of 7.1, added in 4.2; no new story events occur in it.
