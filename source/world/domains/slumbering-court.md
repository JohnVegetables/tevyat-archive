## summary
### LITE
Slumbering Court is a Domain of Blessing at Fort Hiraumi on Seirai Island, Inazuma. Its description says this great hall stood when Seirai Island had neither its present name nor the colour of thunder, and that the scenery within will probably never change. The masterless mansion has become a haunt of rifthounds.
### STANDARD +
Clearing it yields artifacts: Husk of Opulent Dreams, Ocean-Hued Clam, Defender's Will and Brave Heart. It was added in Version 2.3.

## geography
### STANDARD
A great hall in the Fort Hiraumi area of Seirai Island, the island of the perpetual thunderstorm.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Hydro slimes and a Crackling Axe Mitachurl at the lower levels, then Thundercraven Rifthound whelps and Rifthounds at the higher ones, with Electro Hilichurl Shooters.
### LARGE +
In-game, party members gain a 40% Electro DMG Bonus and Electro-Charged reactions trigger a shockwave of true damage; recommended elements are Electro and Hydro. This is a mechanic, not story.
### ULTRA +
Four levels (Adventure Rank 30/35/40/45; recommended levels 59/69/80/90).

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Still and suspended, a hall that time forgot, with the island's lightning flickering outside.
