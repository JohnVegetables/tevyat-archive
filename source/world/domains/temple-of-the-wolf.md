## summary
### LITE
The Temple of the Wolf is a ruined temple of the Four Winds near the Thousand Winds Temple in Starfell Valley, devoted to the Wolf of the North. The priests are gone and monsters have seized it, though the spirit of the Wolf is said still to watch over Mondstadt's fields from elsewhere. Its depths are described as a destroyed ancient training ground.
### STANDARD +
In the Prologue the Traveler clears it with Kaeya, Captain of the Cavalry, as part of the Knights' effort to stop the dragon Stormterror.

## geography
### STANDARD
It lies in Starfell Valley beside the Thousand Winds Temple. Hilichurls and Pyro slimes hold the approach. Inside are Doors of Resurrection, flamethrower and water-and-spike traps, wind currents and elemental trials leading to the training-ground chambers.
### LARGE +
Gameplay notes: the one-time domain "Trial: Scorching Fire and Raging Lightning" is recommended at Adventure Rank 15 with Pyro and Electro, and the player chooses between Hydro, Electro and Pyro trials.

## history
### STANDARD
The Four Winds were formally established after Vennessa's revolution a thousand years ago, and for roughly five hundred years Mondstadters built temples to them. The Wolf's temple was originally a training ground. After the cataclysm the worship faded and the temples were abandoned. At some point Boreas and his wolves moved west and settled in Wolvendom.
### LARGE +
The temple's text says only that the Wolf's spirit still watches over Mondstadt; the Wolf's later home in Wolvendom is described in the Mondstadt entry.

## quests
@version: 1.0
### STANDARD
In "Crash Course" (Prologue Act I), Kaeya meets the Traveler at the Thousand Winds Temple and joins as a trial character in the story domain "Deep Within the Temple of the Wolf". He says the temples must be cleared for the Four Winds, and that Stormterror was once one of them, referring the Traveler to the Acting Grand Master for the story. After Stormterror's crystal is destroyed he praises the Traveler's fighting.
### LARGE +
Afterwards Kaeya stays behind alone. A cutscene shows him reasoning that hilichurls could not have organised the ambush themselves; a Hydro Abyss Mage reveals itself and is killed by an unidentified red-haired man who calls the Knights inefficient. Kaeya replies that his involvement makes things more interesting.
### ULTRA +
The Prologue story domains for the three temples run concurrently: Kaeya with Cryo here, Amber with Pyro at the Temple of the Falcon and Lisa with Electro at the Temple of the Lion. Once the third temple is cleared the skies over Mondstadt clear. The Wolf's temple is the only one of the three whose domain text calls the interior an ancient civilization's training ground.

## dangers
### LARGE
Hilichurls, Pyro slimes, Hydro and Electro foes, and the temple's flame and water traps.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 it is an abandoned ruin; its story domain is complete and its one-time domain remains enterable.

## entry: Wolf of the North

### STANDARD
Canon gives two possible bearers of the Wolf of the North: Boreas (Andrius), the Great Wolf King now a spirit in Wolvendom, and the current Knight of Boreas in the Knights' tradition (Varka). The temple does not decide between them.
