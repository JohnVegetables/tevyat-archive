## summary
### LITE
Tlazolli's Forge is the workshop of the Ancient Name forger Tlazolli in Tequemecan Valley, Natlan. It was announced as abandoned and sealed after she stepped down from her tribal position, but in the quest Wailing Cacophony (5.1) it proves to be where she tries to turn an orphaned girl into her dead daughter Nechca.
### STANDARD
Tlazolli's Forge is a workshop belonging to Tlazolli, a forger of Ancient Names among the Children of Echoes in Tequemecan Valley, Natlan. She had given it out as closed down after leaving her position in the tribe. In the Tribal Chronicles quest Wailing Cacophony it turns out to be a ritual site, guarded by hired Forged Sand fighters, where she tries to imprint her dead daughter Nechca's memories onto the adopted girl Nepecha.
### LARGE +
It exists in the game as a quest domain, entered during Wailing Cacophony, the third part of the act Melodious Chant (5.1). After the ritual failed, the tribe closed the workshop officially.

## geography
@version: 5.1
### STANDARD
The entrance is blocked by mud and rocks and carries a sign saying the workshop is closed and no entry is allowed. Inside, the workshop is a series of industrial chambers joined by working transport carriages, with controller levers that open gates and move carts to deeper areas. Solid Phlogiston walls and Molten Fissures are part of the layout; Children of Echoes can use their abilities to climb on solid Phlogiston. At the centre is the ritual chamber where the Central Inscription is set up.
### LARGE +
Doors are shut along the way, so Xilonen and the Traveler have to find the controls to restart the transport system and ride it further in. The carts run on a timer, so the party has to reach the next gate before it closes.

## places
@version: 5.1
### STANDARD
Entrance: the sealed front, with the closure sign. Outer halls: where the hired fighters wait. Transport depots: carriages and levers that connect the chambers. Central chamber: the ritual space where Nepecha lay while the engraving diagram ran and the Blazegem Inscriptions were linked to the Central Inscription.

## people
@version: 5.1
### STANDARD
Tlazolli, a forger who stepped down from her tribal position and was also Xilonen's mentor, is the owner. Nepecha, an orphan she raised, is brought here against her will. Xilonen and the Traveler follow them in. The workshop is guarded by Forged Sand fighters and, in the last stretch, Tepetlisaurus warriors.
### LARGE +
Xilonen calls Tlazolli "Auntie". Nechca, Tlazolli's real daughter, died in an Abyss attack years earlier but is present in the story through a cinematic in which she speaks, her spirit having remained within Tlazolli's own Ancient Name.

## history
@version: 5.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Several years before the Traveler arrived, an Abyss attack killed Tlazolli's daughter Nechca and orphaned another child, Nepecha, who shared Nechca's birthday and was about the same age. Tlazolli took Nepecha in, then gradually came to treat her as the lost Nechca. She forged and sold Blazegem Inscriptions, decorative pieces made by methods similar to Ancient Name forging, that quietly drew memories of Nechca from their owners. These were meant to be gathered through a Central Inscription and pressed onto Nepecha. When Xilonen discovered the memory theft, Tlazolli fled with Nepecha to her workshop.
### LARGE +
In the forge, Tlazolli said she meant the Central Inscription to become Nechca's Ancient Name and that she had set up all the inscriptions. Xilonen found a flaw in the engraving diagram and, on her countdown, the ritual was turned against itself. What poured out of the inscriptions was mostly the grief and pain of everyone who had seen Nechca dying, not clean memories. Newly kindled flames purified those thoughts and awakened the real Nechca, who had remained in Tlazolli's Ancient Name, Undugu (Kinship). Nechca spoke with her mother and blessed Nepecha before fading. Tlazolli, broken, was tied up by Xilonen and taken into tribal custody. Per the wiki profile, the workshop was then officially closed, Xilonen took Nepecha to her own workshop, and the Blazegem Inscriptions were deactivated and destroyed. Later, in Journey of Stars and Flames, Tlazolli is said to have met Nechca in a dream and lent her power against the Abyss.
### ULTRA +
Tlazolli denied lying, claiming she only wanted to save her daughter, and described the dead Nechca as having stayed too long in a "Night Kingdom". Per the wiki profile, she had told the tribe that Nechca was only injured and that Abyssal energy had caused the memory loss, and had kept Nepecha isolated and given her "medicine" that made her sleepy. Nepecha's attempts to get word out are how Xilonen and the Traveler came to be involved.

## dangers
@version: 5.1
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
As a quest domain it is a combat challenge with a Geo trial Xilonen: Forged Sand Interrogators, Shieldbreakers and Javelineers in the outer areas, then a final wave including Tepetlisaurus Warriors before the confrontation with Tlazolli. Solid Phlogiston and Molten Fissures are used to cross the layout. The domain is part of the quest and not open-world content.

## quests
@version: 5.1
### STANDARD
Wailing Cacophony, part three of the Melodious Chant act in the Tribal Chronicles "A Prayer for Blessings, Told to Crested Peaks", centred on Xilonen. It follows A Faint Signal Lingers and leads to Serenity's Reprise.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
A cold, shut-up place that should be empty: stale furnace heat, the creak of carts on their rails, rows of glowing inscriptions that hum with something unwholesome. Beneath the machinery sits the sound of a mother whispering reassurance to a child who is not hers.

## status
@version: 5.1
### STANDARD
As of 7.1 the forge is closed by the Children of Echoes, its inscriptions neutralised, and Tlazolli is in tribal custody pending a judgement the sources do not give.

## entry: Wailing Cacophony
@keys: Wailing Cacophony, Tlazolli's Forge, Tlazolli's workshop
@version: 5.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The Tribal Chronicles quest in which Xilonen and the Traveler confront Tlazolli in her workshop, stop the ritual and rescue Nepecha. It ends with Nechca's blessing and with Tlazolli taken into custody.

## entry: Blazegem Inscriptions
@keys: Blazegem Inscription, Blazegem Inscriptions, Central Inscription
@version: 5.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Decorative pieces forged by Tlazolli using methods like Ancient Name forging. Sold in the tribe, they secretly absorbed their owners' memories of Nechca, along with the emotions attached to them, and were linked to a Central Inscription in the forge. After the ritual failed they were deactivated, owners regained their memories, and the remaining ones were destroyed.
