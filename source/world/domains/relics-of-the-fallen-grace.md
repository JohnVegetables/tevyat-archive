## summary
### LITE
Relics of the Fallen Grace is a Domain of Mastery on Fellfrost Peak, the great snow-sloped mountain south of Snezhnograd. According to its text it is a sanctuary once built by the ancient fae and now despoiled by petty brigands. Clearing it grants talent materials, described in game as insight and enlightenment.
### STANDARD +
The longer description tells of fae who had lost their homeland and raised a secluded sanctuary here, hoping to ease the pain of extinction through cold, solitary asceticism. Over hundreds of epochs, those who mourned the golden realm and the followers who never knew it died one after another like scattering snow. The last hermit left, and only a freezing silence remained. It appeared in 7.0.
### LARGE +
Gameplay data (labelled as such): the Monday, Tuesday and Wednesday rewards are the Charity, Fortitude and Glory teaching series; enemies include Volkodlak raiders, sharpshooters and lobbers, Winged knights, Juvenile Tree Segments and a Rugged Elder Leshy. Its Ley Line Disorder triggers an Electro shockwave on Superconduct or Stellar-Conduct reactions.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Cold, abandoned stillness: a once-sacred place gone silent under snow, now trodden by armed squatters. Suited to scenes of loss and fading faith.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Volkodlak brigands, winged armoured foes and leshy-type tree creatures; the surrounding Fellfrost Peak has heavy snowstorms, and an in-game notice there warns that hiking is prohibited.

## status
### STANDARD
Permanent domain as of 7.1. The sanctuary is empty of its original inhabitants; canon names no founder.

### ULTRA +
Its Mastery tier gives it a religious flavour (asceticism, followers, a last hermit), but canon never says which fae faith it served.
