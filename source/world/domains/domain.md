## summary
### LITE
Domains are self-contained pocket areas that adventurers enter through temple-like gateways, caves or broken structures scattered across Teyvat. Inside are combat trials, puzzles or powerful guardians, and at the end a reward. They are found in every region. Canon never explains in-world what they are, so treat them as mysterious, bounded places rather than a known institution.
### STANDARD +
Most are reached through a clearly marked entrance, which once discovered also serves travellers as a waypoint. The Adventurer Handbook presents them as trials that reward the brave with artifacts and materials for strengthening oneself.

### ULTRA +
The wiki's one in-lore oddity is that the Veluriyam Mirage of the Secret Summer Paradise event (limited) is repeatedly called a domain in event text, although it lacks any domain mechanics. Whether domains share a common origin remains unanswered by canon.

## types
### STANDARD
Game terms, not in-world classes. Challenge domains come in three kinds: Domains of Blessing (artifacts), Forgery (weapon ascension materials) and Mastery (talent materials). Trounce Domains hold exceptionally strong weekly bosses and open after the matching Archon or Story Quest. One-Time Domains give a set reward on first completion, and are often puzzles or small combat trials. Quest Domains appear only during a quest and cannot be revisited afterwards. Event Domains exist during limited-time events. The Spiral Abyss and the Imaginarium Theater are special rotating domains with their own entries.
### LARGE +
Quest Domains typically hide behind smoke-covered caves or broken structures; once finished the smoke usually clears and the entrance becomes plain stones, though some reuse an ordinary domain entrance. Event Domains may sit in the overworld behind a distinctive doorway or be reached through the event menu. Trounce Domains hold the rewards behind a Ley Line Blossom that symbolises the mighty being defeated.

## places
### STANDARD
Domains sit in every region, from Mondstadt's ruined temples to ruins in Liyue, Inazuma, Sumeru, Fontaine and Natlan. The Spiral Abyss opens through a wormhole in the sky at the edge of Cape Oath, and the Imaginarium Theater is reached from the Mondstadt Library at the Knights of Favonius Headquarters.
### LARGE +
Individual domains, such as the Domain of Guyun in Liyue, have their own entries and often reuse older ruins or sacred sites as their setting.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Crossing the threshold feels like leaving the open world: the sky, terrain and sound change, and the trial is self-contained. The end of a challenge domain is marked by a petrified tree, a quiet, almost reverent reward point. Use domains in a scene as short, enclosed ordeals with a single objective, not as wide landscapes.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 domains remain a standard part of adventuring in every region.
### STANDARD +
Permanent domains, Trounce Domains and the rotating Spiral Abyss and Imaginarium Theater remain available. The wiki notes that no one-time domains have been added to any region since Version 3.6.

## gameplay
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
Gameplay description, not lore. Rewards from challenge and Trounce Domains are claimed with Original Resin, a resource that replenishes over time. Challenge domain stages rotate by weekday, and Sunday is open to all of them. Co-Op Mode allows others to join some domains, but not Quest Domains. Adventure Rank or particular quests unlock access: Blessing needs rank 22 or Song of the Dragon and Freedom, Forgery rank 16 or For a Tomorrow Without Tears, and Mastery rank 27 or Of the Land Amidst Monoliths.

## entry: Petrified Trees and Irminsul
@keys: petrified tree, petrified trees, Domain reward tree
@keys2: domain, Albedo
@spoiler: none
### STANDARD
At the end of a challenge domain, rewards are claimed at a petrified tree. Albedo remarks that these are considered young trees, while ancient ones would stand a mile or two high with an unknown kind of spider living in their roots. Canon has not shown such a tree, and does not say how they relate to Irminsul. Present this as a curiosity, not an established link.

## entry: Quest and Event Domains
@keys: Quest Domain, Event Domain, Veluriyam Mirage, One-Time Domain
@keys2: domain
@spoiler: none
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
Quest Domains exist only inside a specific quest and are sealed afterwards; One-Time Domains pay out once. Event Domains appear during limited-time events. During the Secret Summer Paradise event (limited), the lore repeatedly calls the Veluriyam Mirage a domain even though it behaves like a normal area.
