## summary
### LITE
Shakkei Pavilion is a one-time domain in Tatarasuna, Kannazuka, Inazuma. Its description says the magnificent mansion was built by an ancient ascetic warrior in the depths of the earth, using scenery borrowed from the outside world (shakkei, a garden-design principle), and was sealed at some point for reasons unknown.
### STANDARD +
It has a single level, Tutelage: Castle of Scrolls, and was added in Version 2.0. The entrance is hidden behind rocks that must be broken by firing a Kamuijima Cannon.

## geography
### STANDARD
Deep within Tatarasuna's rocky mountains, a mansion of brittle floors (broken by plunging attacks), amulet shrines and a main door that opens when three amulets are placed.
### LARGE +
It holds common and exquisite chests, and a precious chest appears after the final fight.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Nobushi: Jintouban and Hitsukeban guard the shrines; the final room holds a Fatui Pyro Agent and a Mirror Maiden.

### ULTRA +
Recommended elements per the wiki: Pyro, Electro, Cryo and Anemo; Adventure Rank 32 required, with a level scaling by World Level. Firing the cannon at the entrance grants the Jackpot achievement.

## history
@version: 3.3
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Before Version 3.3 the domain's description ended: 'In latter days, a deeply disheartened eccentric would be discovered within.' The wiki reads this as a veiled reference to the Wanderer (Scaramouche), called a kabukimono (eccentric) in Tatarasuna.
### LARGE +
Per the wiki's summary of Inversion of Genesis, Nahida says the pavilion was like a birthplace for Scaramouche, that Raiden Ei left him there, and that Katsuragi of Tatarasuna found him after he had spent a long time in it. Treat as secondhand until re-read in the quest.
