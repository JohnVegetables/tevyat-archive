## summary
### LITE
The Domain of Guyun is an Electro-themed Domain of Blessing on the largest island in Guyun Stone Forest, in Liyue's Sea of Clouds. Ancient ley line altars here guard Artifacts behind rounds of slime and Abyss enemies. Its description speaks of delusions and disappointments of an ancient ambition to lead humanity, gathered in the caverns under Guyun.
### STANDARD +
It has six levels, with ley line disorder built around Electro-Charged, Overloaded and Superconduct reactions. The level names are all "Spring", the translation of Jingzhe, the season when thunder wakes the sleeping insects.
### LARGE +
Guyun Stone Forest itself is an archipelago of seven large islands. Legend says Rex Lapis beat a god and sea monster named Osial here and that the jagged cliffs are the stone lances he used. Those who sleep at Guyun may dream Osial's memories, though whether that comes from Osial or something else is unknown.

## geography
### STANDARD
The domain lies on the largest of the seven islands in Guyun Stone Forest. The northernmost island houses the Geo Hypostasis.
### LARGE +
A ruins treasure near Mt. Aocang is said to be related to one at Guyun and tells of a disciple who received a Vision.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Gameplay rules, labelled as such: ley line disorder boosts Electro-Charged damage by 150% with 100% larger area at levels I and IV, and makes Overloaded (II, V) or Superconduct (III, VI) trigger an Energy Blast that damages nearby opponents. Recommended element is Electro; Cryo helps at level VI.
### LARGE +
Enemies progress from Hydro and Pyro slimes to Cryo slimes with a Mitachurl, then hilichurls with Hydro Samachurls and an Abyss Mage, a Ruin Guard (with a central Pyro Elemental Node that periodically empowers enemies), and finally Fatui Skirmishers beside a Ruin Guard. Rewards are Artifacts from Archaic Petra, Retracing Bolide, Brave Heart and Lucky Dog.
### ULTRA +
Mechanics detail (gameplay, not lore): levels I to IV have you defend a Domain Monolith while clearing 28, 22, 20 and 20 enemies; level V is a 120-second fight against one Ruin Guard; level VI gives 420 seconds against three opponents. Energy Blasts deal Physical damage scaled to the level (about 1,126 at II up to about 4,811 at VI). The recommended level range is 34 to 90, with Adventure Rank 20 as the entry floor.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Thin air, low clouds, crackling static, and sheer stone pillars; the altar's walls feel old and a little bitter.

## status
### STANDARD
Permanent and unchanged as of Version 7.1.
