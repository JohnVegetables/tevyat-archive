## summary
### LITE
The Lost Valley is a hidden domain in Fuao Vale, within the Chasm of Liyue. It was an ancient ritual ground, and ruin machines from outside have overrun it. Defeating them yields artifacts.
### STANDARD +
Its past is told in layers: buried by cataclysm, lifted up by an alien object, then exposed by a battle born of betrayal.

## geography
### STANDARD
It lies in Fuao Vale, part of the Chasm. A huge golden raw-mineral rock and nearby rock pillars guard the way; the domain is revealed when a strong blow on a nearby pillar sets off a resonance quake between Geo constructs.
### LARGE +
The entry is a Geo-resonance puzzle: Geo constructs are placed at equal distances between the mineral and the nearest pillar. Paimon marvels at the minerals' size.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Desolate and hushed, a former sanctuary of grey stone. The pillars around the battlefield are blank, unlike those of other blessing domains.

## history
### STANDARD
The domain description gives the site's past: buried beneath the earth during a cataclysm that tore heaven and earth asunder (linked to the War of Funerary Flame), raised by the descent of an alien object (the Spiritstone), and unveiled by a destructive battle that followed a betrayal (linked to Azhdaha). It closes by noting that those who once frequented the place are no more.
### LARGE +
The links to the War of Funerary Flame, the Spiritstone and Azhdaha come from the domain description's own wording; it does not retell those events in full.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Ruin machines such as Ruin Guards, Scouts, Cruisers, Destroyers and Graders hold the ground. The in-game explanation is that these mechanical invaders came from outside the ancient site. Its Ley Line Disorder (game mechanic) deals true damage when characters use Elemental Skills.

## status
### STANDARD
Added in 2.6 and unchanged as of 7.1; it plays no part in later regional stories.

### ULTRA +
The domain's four levels range from two heavy ruin machines up to a Ruin Grader with escorts, and all share the Machine Nest challenge theme. Unlike other blessing domains, its pillars are blank rather than inscribed, so the site carries no readable ruin writing.
