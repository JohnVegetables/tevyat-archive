## summary
### LITE
The Red Desert Threshold is a one-time domain in Abdju Pit, in the Land of Lower Setekh of Sumeru's Great Red Sand. It is a corner of the ancient city of Gurabad where the wise once debated, later turned into a secret prison. Today monsters nest there and relics of the past remain. It opens only after starting the Golden Slumber quest.
### STANDARD +
The in-game text says legend holds that a prince exiled by his father was held in this jail, and that the "bronze revelation" first emerged in this lonely place where there is neither day nor night. This is a legend, not a confirmed account.

## geography
### STANDARD
An underground holding complex reached through Abdju Pit, which is described as the desert's edge, with a palace buried under the sand and King Deshret's realm beyond. Abdju Pit did not exist before the quest Lost in the Sands; the area was previously called Abdju Road. The domain has one level, Edge of the End. Dendro is recommended.
### LARGE +
The domain's special rule rewards triggering Dendro-related reactions (Burning, Quicken, Aggravate, Spread, Bloom, Hyperbloom, Burgeon) with a shockwave of True damage, at most once every three seconds. This is a gameplay mechanic.

## history
### STANDARD
In its first age the place belonged to Gurabad. The domain description says scholars once debated wisdom here and outlined the dream of the desert king (King Deshret). After that dream's bubble burst, it became a secret holding cell. The legend of an exiled prince (compare Shiruyeh, whom Parvezravan banished) attaches to the cells.
### LARGE +
Gurabad itself fell in a plague, sinking into the earth in the story of Shiruyeh and Parvezravan; the domain text does not say whether this occurred here.
### ULTRA +
Shiruyeh's supporters, according to the Gurabad accounts, were commoners and former slaves displaced by Jinn-powered machines, and they portrayed him as the emissary of Nabu Malikata. Whether the cells below Abdju Pit held anyone from that movement is not stated.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Mitachurls of several kinds (Blazing Axe, Wooden Shieldwall), Geovishaps, and Lawachurls (Thunderhelm, Stonehide).

## quests
### STANDARD
Unlocked through Golden Slumber, a Sumeru world quest series that starts with Bonifaz in Aaru Village. In that series the Traveler helps a Mondstadt merchant's missing archaeology team, meets the Eremite mercenaries Jebrael and Jeht, and follows King Deshret's trail to Khaj-Nisut.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Closed stone cells, no sun and no night, and the sense of a forgotten place under the sand where the old city's secrets were kept.

## status
### STANDARD
As of 7.1 the domain is unchanged and accessible. No later content has altered it.
