## summary
### LITE
Faded Theater is an artifact domain in Petrichor, in the Nostoi Region of Fontaine. Its flavor text says that the prophet and the ruler, heroes and fell dragons, the grand opera of the golden age once played out here; now the only players on the stage are monsters of unknown provenance.
### STANDARD +
Clearing it grants artifacts: Fragment of Harmonic Whimsy, Unfinished Reverie, Brave Heart and Scholar.

## geography
### STANDARD
A ruined theater-stage in Petrichor within the Nostoi Region.
### LARGE +
The text ties the place to Fontaine's golden age (the Remuria period), with the prophet (Sybilla), the ruler (Remus) and fell dragons (Scylla) as the subjects of its opera. It is the domain text that links these; no further description of the building exists.

## history
### STANDARD
Fontaine's golden-age opera, as the domain frames it, was staged here with a prophet, a ruler, heroes and dragons as characters.
### LARGE +
Nothing explains when the stage was abandoned or how the golems came to guard it.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Rock Shield hilichurls and Mitachurls, slimes and Pyro Shooters, with Praetorian Golems as the strongest foes.
### LARGE +
Gameplay note: the domain's Ley Line Disorder rewards a character with an all-element and Physical DMG bonus whenever their Bond of Life rises or falls; this is a game mechanic.
### ULTRA +
There are four difficulty tiers (recommended levels 59, 69, 80 and 90); later tiers field up to three Praetorian Golems at once, and the final waves include large slimes.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
An empty, once-splendid stage: faded grandeur, silence, and stone guardians in place of actors.

## status
### LITE
A permanent artifact domain as of 7.1, added in 4.6; no story events are tied to it.
