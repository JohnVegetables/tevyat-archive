## summary
### LITE
The Karma-Heavy Cavern is a cave in Guili Plains, Liyue, that draws creatures twisted by sinister energy. It is the setting of "The Purging of Evil", a Xiao story quest, in which the adeptus Xiao and the Traveler clear it of tainted hilichurls.
### STANDARD +
It is a quest domain, not a standing location: it is entered through the quest and used for one fight, not for exploration or trade.

## geography
### STANDARD
The cavern sits in the Guili Plains region of the Qiongji Estuary. The quest begins with the Traveler tracking tainted hilichurls across the plains with Elemental Sight, and the trail leads to the cave. Platforms inside are cleared one after another.
### LARGE +
"Karma-heavy" is how the quest and Xiao describe the place, not a name used by locals.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Oppressive and unnatural: Paimon says it gives her goosebumps. Xiao tells his companions to stay calm and not to be affected by the lingering wrath of bygone gods.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Hilichurls and creatures corrupted by an evil aura fill the cave. Xiao uses a rite called the Bane of All Evil to relieve the place of its karma. In gameplay terms (a game mechanic, not lore), the party is Xiao alone, and his Elemental Burst stays active, constantly draining his health while hits restore it.
### LARGE +
In the story the creatures' mutation comes from Xiao himself, or more precisely from the karma he has accumulated through long service. This is why he first tries to go alone.

## quests
@version: 1.3
### STANDARD
In "The Purging of Evil" (part 3 of Butterfly's Dream) Xiao is found having already slain tainted hilichurls. He admits his karma caused the changes and declines company, then lets the Traveler and Paimon come along. After the fight he learns about "Starsnatcher", a masked man posing as a wish-granting adeptus who exorcises demons with a Sigil of Permission without destroying them. Xiao concludes that this is why demons gather here and resolves to confiscate the sigil.
### LARGE +
He explains that his art Dream Trawler can call forth the spirits of others, and asks the Traveler to gather a censer, seven lamps and something cold such as Mist Flower, suggesting the old shrines by the yaksha statues on the southern face of Mt. Tianheng. The story continues in later parts of the chapter.

## status
### STANDARD
As of 7.1 nothing has changed. The cavern does not appear in Nod-Krai (6.x) or Snezhnaya (7.0-7.1) content.

### ULTRA +
What Xiao says here is also a rare moment of openness: he dismisses a book about yakshas as literati fan fiction from a few hundred years ago and rejects sympathy, saying fallen comrades would see tears as a stain on their legacy. He distinguishes demons, whom he slays, from mortals, whom he does not kill.

## entry: Karma-Heavy Cavern and Xiao
@keys: Karma-Heavy Cavern, Cavern of Karma, The Purging of Evil
@version: 1.3
### STANDARD
In the Karma-Heavy Cavern Xiao purifies living things that carry demonic taint sourced from his own karma. He accepts suffering as the price of ages of slaughter, but says others should not suffer for a burden that ought to be his alone; he resolves to reflect on this.
