## summary
### LITE
Cecilia Garden is a Forgery domain near Wolvendom in Windwail Highland, Mondstadt. The entrance text says Cecilia flowers, which grow only on high cliffs, once bloomed here in greenhouses, and that the ancient civilisation that enjoyed their scent was lost along with the sea of white flowers. It is an ancient sacrificial altar flooded with Hydro energy, and clearing it yields weapon ascension materials.
### STANDARD +
A locked Anemo monument and four Seelie Courts stand outside the entrance and must be restored before the trial opens. The ruins' ornament resembles that of Stormterror's Lair.

## geography
### STANDARD
The entrance lies in Windwail Highland by the forest of Wolvendom. Inside is a damp ancient altar chamber charged with Hydro energy.
### LARGE +
Unlocking it means freeing the Seelie around the entrance monument. Unlike most domain doors it carries no Solomon's knot above its doorway.

## history
### STANDARD
Canon gives only the entrance blurb: a lost ancient civilisation grew Cecilia in greenhouses here, and both the people and the sea of white flowers vanished. The text does not name the civilisation. Venti calls Cecilia his favourite flower, and the Nameless Bard of Decarabian's age wore a stylised Cecilia, which hints at, but does not prove, a link to Old Mondstadt.
### ULTRA +
The wiki notes the surrounding ornament matches Stormterror's Lair, supporting a Decarabian-era origin; this is inference, not stated canon.

## dangers
### LARGE
The trial pits the Traveler against Hydro slimes, Hilichurls, Hydro Samachurls and, at the top level, Fatui Pyro Agents. The ley line disorder boosts Electro damage, so Electro wielders are favoured.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Damp stone, ancient carved ornament, and the faint melancholy of a flower garden whose gardeners are gone.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the domain is open and unchanged since 1.0; nothing in 6.x or 7.0-7.1 content alters it.
