## summary
### LITE
Liyue is the nation of contracts in eastern Teyvat, the land of the Geo Archon Morax, whom locals call Rex Lapis. He founded Liyue Harbor about 3,700 years ago and ruled it as the God of Contracts and Commerce, but he has since faked his own death and lives quietly among mortals as Zhongli, a consultant at Wangsheng Funeral Parlor. The nation is now run by the Liyue Qixing, seven merchant leaders. The immortal adepti still guard it from the mountains. Liyue Harbor is the wealthiest trading port in Teyvat, and Mora, the coin used across the continent, is named after Morax.
### STANDARD +
Liyue folk take contracts seriously, haggle without shame and revere the adepti. Few people know that Zhongli is their god. The Qixing told the public that Rex Lapis died in a trial and took over every one of his duties.

## geography
### STANDARD
Liyue is mountainous country of stone forests, karst peaks, terraced fields, marshes and rivers. Its main areas are:
- Bishui Plain: Stone Gate, Wangshu Inn and Qingce Village.
- Qiongji Estuary: the Guili Plains.
- Minlin: Jueyun Karst, home of the adepti.
- Lisha: ancient ruins.
- The Sea of Clouds: Liyue Harbor, Mt. Tianheng and the Guyun Stone Forest.

Further west lie two more subregions: The Chasm, a vast mine (opened in 2.6), and Chenyu Vale, tea country on the Fontaine border (opened in 4.4).
### LARGE +
Liyue Harbor has mountains on three sides and the sea on the fourth. Inland lie foothills, fords and rich ore mines. The usual land route from Mondstadt runs southwest from Dawn Winery and through a plank-paved pass between two cliffs to Stone Gate. Chenyu Vale has three parts: the Upper Vale (Qiaoying Village and Yilong Wharf), the Southern Mountain, and Mt. Laixin, the sacred mountain where the vale's spirit veins converge.

## government
### STANDARD
The Liyue Qixing ("Seven Stars") are seven leading businesspeople who have governed since about 2,000 years ago. They meet at Yuehai Pavilion on Yujing Terrace. Each takes a title from a star of the Big Dipper, and all are formally equal. Three members are publicly known:
- Tianquan Ningguang: law and foreign relations.
- Yuheng Keqing: land, construction and real estate.
- Tianshu Uncle Tian: policymaking.

The half-qilin adepta Ganyu has been General Secretary from the start and does the calculations behind the council's policies. The Millelith, the Ministry of Civil Affairs and the Eight Trades all answer to the Qixing.
### LARGE +
The Tianquan keeps the book of Morax's ancient laws and every amendment to it, but the whole council interprets the law, closes loopholes and punishes offenders. The holders of the other four posts keep a low profile. Uncle Tian has held his seat for years by setting his enemies against one another. Ningguang lives in the Jade Chamber, a floating palace hung with her wall of business intelligence. The Qixing secretly run Wangshu Inn to support Xiao and gather intelligence. Yelan, who claims to work for the Ministry of Civil Affairs, is really an intelligence agent for Ningguang. Beidou's Crux Fleet is independent but works closely with the Tianquan.
### ULTRA +
Ningguang has three trusted secretaries: Baiwen, Baishi and Baixiao. Keqing's assistants rarely last more than three months. Madame Ping says the Qixing stopped letting her and her adeptal friends into their counsel long ago. Yun Hui, a blacksmith of the post-cataclysm era, was once a Qixing member; his title is unknown. In Yelan's story quest Uncle Tian looked for a successor, but his chosen candidate, Zhiyi, turned out to be slowly poisoning him; Tian recovered, remains Tianshu, and plans to hand over his duties gradually while mentoring new candidates.

## religion
### STANDARD
Rex Lapis is revered as the God of Contracts and Commerce and the Warrior God. The adepti are revered almost as much. They are illuminated beasts and spirits who fought for Morax under contract. Some still live in Jueyun Karst, and others asked to be turned to stone when the war ended. Wangsheng Funeral Parlor performs funerals for mortals and the rare rites that send off adepti.
### LARGE +
Before his retirement, Rex Lapis appeared each year at the Rite of Descension in his Exuvia form, part dragon and part qilin, to give predictions for the year's business. Statues of the Seven show him hooded and holding a cube, and Yiyan Temple at Yuehai Pavilion is dedicated to him. People also call him God of the Stove, but that title belonged to Marchosius, a god of the Guili Assembly. Marchosius spent all his power protecting Liyue Harbor from disaster and plague, and his legend gradually merged into Morax's. He survives today as Guoba, the chef Xiangling's small companion. The Rex Incognito books claim Rex Lapis took many shapes but always kept his amber eyes. Zhongli says the books exaggerate the shapeshifting.

## history
### STANDARD
Morax was born more than 6,000 years ago. With Guizhong, the God of Dust, he founded the Guili Assembly. About 3,700 years ago, during the Archon War, a battle destroyed it and killed Guizhong. Morax led the survivors south of Mt. Tianheng and founded Liyue Harbor. There he raised the Millelith, minted Mora and bound the adepti by contract. He then conquered the rival gods, pinning Osial beneath the Guyun Stone Forest, and became the Geo Archon about 2,000 years ago. During the cataclysm 500 years ago, monsters burst out of the Chasm and were sealed away at great cost. In Chapter I, Rex Lapis staged his own death and gave the Geo Gnosis to the Fatui.
### LARGE +
The bodies of the fallen gods became part of the earth, but their hatred lived on as miasma and monsters. The Yakshas fought these for centuries until karmic debt destroyed most of them. Doctors who burned the dead to end the resulting plague founded Wangsheng Funeral Parlor. About a thousand years ago, Morax's old friend Azhdaha, an earth dragon, was maddened by mining that shook the ley lines and by his failing memory. He destroyed Tianqiu Valley, and Morax and the adepti sealed him beneath Nantianmen. After the cataclysm, the smiths Yun Hui and Han Wu revived the martial arts with the Prototype weapons, and their sons made the Blackcliff series. In recent years the Qixing sealed the Chasm after a run of accidents. Signora won the Fatui a role in investigating it, but the Qixing cut them off after Childe's betrayal.

## customs
### STANDARD
Contracts are sacred, and breaking one's word is shameful. Tea, opera, calligraphy, fine cooking and antique-collecting flourish. The great festivals are the Lantern Rite, which begins on the first full moon of the year, and the autumn Moonchase Festival. Glaze Lilies are Liyue's traditional flower.

## dangers
### LARGE
Liyue's dangers include:
- miasma and the lingering resentment of fallen gods;
- Treasure Hoarders, who are especially active in the Chasm;
- hilichurls, Ruin machines and Geovishaps;
- the Abyss Order beneath the Chasm;
- Fatui agents and sea monsters;
- the Dark Mud of the Underground Mines.

Some places carry their own warnings. On Mt. Hulao, Mountain Shaper's plants trap intruders in amber. Wuwang Hill lies near the border between life and death. Visitors to the Guyun Stone Forest may dream Osial's memories of his defeat.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
In the city: eaved, red-pillared buildings, lanterns and harbour bustle, haggling on Feiyun Slope, street food in Chihu Rock and opera at Heyu Tea House. Beyond it: misty karst peaks where adepti may be watching, quiet rice terraces at Qingce, and tea mountains in Chenyu Vale.

## entry: Lantern Rite and Moonchase Festival
@keys: Lantern Rite, Xiao Lantern, Mingxiao Lantern, Moonchase Festival, Moonchase, Masterful Chefs
### STANDARD
The Lantern Rite is Liyue's biggest festival. It begins on the first full moon of the year and lasts five days. People release Xiao Lanterns, which began as lights to guide soldiers home in wartime and later became a way to remember the war dead. Each year a giant Mingxiao Lantern honours a hero of Liyue's past, usually an adeptus who died defending the land. The autumn Moonchase Festival is a time for family reunions and feasts. The Qixing choose its theme and host the Masterful Chefs cooking contest.
### LARGE +
The Lantern Rite coincides with one of the strongest yearly outbursts of the gods who fell in the Archon War, and Xiao fights them while the city celebrates. The Mingxiao Lantern's size depends on the largest piece of Plaustrite found the year before. After the festival, Millelith collect the spent lanterns. Moonchase grew from two roots: the Stove God festival honouring Marchosius, and the adepti's ancient "Moonchase Rite", which was said to commune with the moon. Rex Lapis later unified these festivals under the Moonchase name to honour Marchosius.
### ULTRA +
The 6.3 Lantern Rite story revealed what the Moonchase Rite really was: Rex Lapis' repeated attempts to reach Ziju, a fragment of his old friend Zibai trapped in the false Frost Moon. Ziju never answered, and the adepti eventually stopped performing the rite.

## entry: Liyue Customs and Daily Life
@keys: Liyue customs, Liyue culture, yum cha, Li cuisine, Yue cuisine, Glaze Lily
### LARGE
The Rite of Descension was held each year in Liyue Harbor. It was open to everyone and drew tourists and Liyue people living abroad. A Rite of Parting is different: it commemorates a dead adeptus. Zhongli directed the Rite of Parting for Rex Lapis himself. Liyue's cooking has two long-feuding styles: the bold mountain flavours of Li cuisine (Liuli Pavilion) and the lighter seafood of Yue cuisine (Xinyue Kiosk). Dim sum with tea (yum cha) is a popular breakfast, and every establishment serves tea its own way. Villages are home mainly to the young and the old, because working-age people leave for city jobs, and struggling villages such as Mingyun are sometimes abandoned. Education depends largely on wealth. Some people join martial clans such as the Guhua Clan. Liyue has its own script, which resembles seal script. It is impolite for outsiders to call the Archon "Morax" rather than "Rex Lapis".
### ULTRA +
Liyue's flower-ball wedding custom may come from Havria's habit of handing out flowers to her followers. In the past, ships carried sea-monster bones as figureheads to scare off other beasts. Wangsheng Funeral Parlor holds that departed souls rise as ashen butterflies.
