## summary
### LITE
Teyvat is the world of the story, a planet and the continent on it, where the seven elements (Anemo, Geo, Electro, Dendro, Hydro, Pyro, Cryo) flow. It has seven nations, each tied to one element, one ideal and one god of The Seven (the Archons): Mondstadt, Liyue, Inazuma, Sumeru, Fontaine, Natlan and Snezhnaya. Above it floats Celestia, seat of the Heavenly Principles, who have ruled the world since conquering it in the distant past. Ordinary people may receive a Vision that lets them wield an element. 500 years ago the fall of the godless underground kingdom Khaenri'ah, the cataclysm, unleashed the Abyss on every nation. That calamity has ended, but peace has not fully returned.
### STANDARD +
Most people know Teyvat simply as the world of the Archons and Celestia. Few know the truth: the visible sky is a false sky, a shell around the planet, and the true cosmos beyond it is called the sea of stars.

## geography
### STANDARD
In official descriptions, Mondstadt lies in the northeast, Liyue is a harbor nation in the east, Inazuma is an isolated archipelago far to the east bordering the Dark Sea, Sumeru is in the west-central part of the continent, Fontaine is a "terrestrial sea" in the center, Natlan lies in the western regions and Snezhnaya is the land of eternal winter in the north. Nod-Krai, an autonomous archipelago under Snezhnaya, sits on the very edge of the continent. Khaenri'ah lay deep underground beneath Sumeru.
### LARGE +
Celestia floats over Liyue near Mt. Mingyuan. Teyvat is a planet on the edge of a spiral arm of an unnamed galaxy. The voyager in the artifact text "Deep Gallery's Distant Pact" calls it an unremarkable little world there. Beyond the seven nations lies the "Dark Sea", an umbrella term for unknown lands and waters outside The Seven's rule. Climate follows the nations' elements and gods: green, windy Mondstadt, Liyue's stone forests and mountains, Inazuma's thunderstorms, Sumeru's rainforest and desert, Fontaine's waters, volcanic Natlan, and Snezhnaya's blizzards that can last a month.

Natlan's Archons are mortal humans chosen through the Pilgrimage, so it has had more than two.

## people
### STANDARD
Humans make up most of Teyvat's population, alongside adepti, spirits, Seelies (the cursed remnants of Angels), vishaps and other elemental creatures, hilichurls and Abyssal monsters. A Vision-holder is called an "allogene", someone said to have the potential to ascend to Celestia and become a god.
### LARGE +
Pure-blooded Khaenri'ahns who survived the cataclysm are cursed with immortality; Khaenri'ahns of mixed or foreign blood were turned into monsters. Some people come from beyond the false sky and are called Descenders. The Traveler is one: they and their twin arrived during the cataclysm, when Asmoday stopped them from leaving. Irminsul held no record of the Traveler.

## governance
### STANDARD
The world order works top-down. The Heavenly Principles rule from Celestia through the Four Shades (Rulers of Life, Death, Time and Space). The Seven govern the nations. Each Archon holds a divine throne in Celestia, made from an elemental Authority taken from a dragon Sovereign, and each received a Gnosis linking them to Celestia.
### LARGE +
Celestia also runs a "monitoring cycle" that watches the world and the Shades and sends directives when it decides to intervene. Archons do not choose who gets a Vision; they are only obliged to hand over part of their elemental Authority when one is granted. In punishing whole civilizations that broke its rules, Celestia has driven Celestial Nails into the land (Sal Vindagnyr, Lang-Gan and Hyperborea among them). Since the Hydro Archon's throne was destroyed in Fontaine (4.2), its Authority has returned to the Hydro Sovereign Neuvillette, and only six divine thrones remain. The Heavenly Principles themselves have slept since the cataclysm, because harming Khaenri'ah triggered the Third Descender Saarelainen's curse. Celestia's automatic oversight still runs.

## economy
### STANDARD
Mora is the currency accepted everywhere in Teyvat; its item text calls it the one language everybody speaks. It is named after Morax and could only be minted with the Geo Archon's power at Liyue's Golden House, which has stopped minting new Mora since Zhongli gave up the Geo Gnosis.
### LARGE +
The Adventurers' Guild, which posts work in every nation, originates from Snezhnaya, and its Katheryne receptionists are confirmed Snezhnayan puppets. Trade runs between the great ports (Liyue Harbor, Mondstadt's Dornman Port, Sumeru's Port Ormos, among others). Snezhnaya is the most technologically advanced nation, with large factories and the Fatui's advanced weaponry. Nod-Krai has a reputation as a place to get rich, but it is lawless apart from the Voynich Guild.

## history
### STANDARD
Teyvat's history in broad strokes:
- **The Old World:** dragons ruled under the Seven Sovereigns and their king, Nibelung, who raised Three Moons from the planet's crust.
- **The conquest:** the Heavenly Principles came from the stars, defeated the dragons, took their Authorities and built a human "unified civilization" guided by Angels.
- **The war of funerary flame, about 6,000 years ago:** Nibelung returned with the Abyss. The war shattered that civilization and two of the moons, and all history from before it was banned.
- **The Archon War, about 6,000 to 2,000 years ago:** gods fought for seven thrones, and The Seven and their nations emerged.
- **The cataclysm, 500 years ago:** Khaenri'ah fell and the Abyss spread across all seven nations.
- **The present:** the Traveler's journey through the seven nations in search of their twin.
### LARGE +
In the cataclysm Makoto, Greater Lord Rukkhadevata, Egeria and the Belyi Tsar died and were succeeded. Mavuika sealed her life in the Sacred Flame and returned 500 years later. The Abyss Order and the Fatui both arose in answer to Celestia's actions. Liyue Harbor was founded about 3,700 years ago and Barbatos rose about 2,600 years ago; the Archon War ended about 2,000 years ago when the last throne was claimed. See the master timeline entity for detail.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Most people live ordinary lives under their nation's god, treat Celestia as distant and holy, and never question the sky. Statues of the Seven stand across the land. Ruins of older civilizations are everywhere, often unexplained. Since the moon changed and the Nails began to glow, people have noticed more and more signs in the sky.

## dangers
### LARGE
Common hazards include hilichurl camps, slimes, Treasure Hoarders, rogue Ruin machines, the Fatui's schemes and the Abyss Order. Corrupted ley lines and Abyssal corruption remain, along with hostile climates: Dragonspine's cold, Sumeru's desert and Snezhnaya's blizzards. Cosmic threats: the Abyss, a destructive force from the sea of stars that has annihilated other worlds, including the Traveler's own, strains constantly to enter Teyvat. Alice and Nicole both warned that Teyvat's borders had grown fragile and that the world became easier to detect from outside after the Nod-Krai crisis.

## travel
### LARGE
People travel overland by road and caravan, and by ship between ports. Inazuma was closed for years by the Sakoku Decree. Snezhnaya can only be crossed by those who can survive the cold. Celestia is unreachable except by those who ascend, and Khaenri'ah is no longer reachable. Gameplay note: teleport waypoints and Statues of the Seven are a game convenience. In-world, walking, horses, boats and wind gliders are what people actually use.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the Archon Quest is in Snezhnaya (Chapter VII). The Heavenly Principles still sleep. The Tsaritsa's Fatui hold six of the seven Gnoses and are openly preparing to challenge Celestia. The Frost Moon has returned to Teyvat's sky.
### STANDARD +
In 7.1 the Tsaritsa outmaneuvered the Shade Rhinedottir, and Celestia stripped Ronova of the Authority of Death for abusing it. Pierro's group then defeated Ronova. In her last act, the mortally wounded Ronova made the Celestial Nail over the Cryo Dragon's corpse crumble, threatening to spread Holy Soil across Teyvat. Every other Nail began to shine a strange light into the sky, visible to people everywhere.
### LARGE +
Earlier changes still stand. Irminsul was burned by Nahida in Luna VII (6.6) after Dottore corrupted it, and the Aaru system has taken over its role. The Pyro Gnosis vanished in that fire. Six divine thrones remain. With the Curse of Death lifted, the Fatui's anti-Celestia weapon project Mistilteinn is no longer held back. Nicole has warned that Teyvat is now more detectable from outside.

## entry: The Seven Nations
@keys: seven nations, Seven Nations, nations of Teyvat
@order: 105
### STANDARD
Each of Teyvat's seven nations worships the Archon of one element, and that Archon's ideal shapes its government: Mondstadt (Anemo, freedom), Liyue (Geo, contracts), Inazuma (Electro, eternity), Sumeru (Dendro, wisdom), Fontaine (Hydro, justice), Natlan (Pyro, war) and Snezhnaya (Cryo, love). The nations were founded after the Archon War about 2,000 years ago, when the winning gods shaped their lands with their elemental power. Snezhnaya counts as independent, like Mondstadt, and it administers the autonomous borderland of Nod-Krai.
### LARGE +
Five of the original Seven have died over the centuries. The original Seven were friendly and often gathered in Liyue for drinks. Inazuma's, Sumeru's, Fontaine's and Snezhnaya's first Archons all died around the cataclysm. Fontaine's Hydro throne no longer exists, and Natlan's Archons are mortals chosen through the Pilgrimage.

## entry: Celestia
@keys: Celestia, island in the sky
@keys2: Teyvat, Heavenly Principles, ascend, ascension, gods
### STANDARD
Celestia is a floating cluster of landmasses in the sky over Liyue, near Mt. Mingyuan. It has a great central spire, outer rocks linked by a ring, and rivers that pour off its edges and fade into nothing as they fall. People believe heroes ascend there to become gods. It is home to the Heavenly Principles and the meeting place of the Four Shades (Asmoday has been absent since the cataclysm). The Angels lived there until they were cast out. Venti claims its water tastes foul and its fruit is bland.

## entry: The Dark Sea
@keys: Dark Sea, Dark Outer Sea
### STANDARD
The "Dark Sea" is the umbrella name for everything beyond the continent and outside The Seven's rule, landmasses included. Gods defeated in the Archon War who refused the new order fled there. Enkanomiya, though it lies directly beneath Watatsumi Island, counts as Dark Sea because The Seven have no jurisdiction over it. Inazuma borders the Dark Sea.

## entry: The Moons of Teyvat
@keys: Three Moons, Frost Moon, Eternal Moon, Iridescent Moon, shadowmoon, Dilimbabbar
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
Teyvat once had three moons, raised by the dragon king Nibelung from the planet's crust: the Eternal, Frost and Iridescent Moons. Long ago the spirits of the frozen north called these three silver lights "Dilimbabbar", and by them the seasons and years were first counted. In the war of funerary flame the Eternal and Iridescent Moons were shattered and the Frost Moon was flung outside the false sky. For about 6,000 years people saw only a projected false moon, the shadowmoon. In Song of the Welkin Moon Act VIII (6.3) the Frost Moon was pulled back through the false sky and now orbits inside it, with Columbina as its goddess, and people noticed the moon looked different. The shadowmoon later fell apart during a Lantern Rite.
### ULTRA +
Fragments of the Eternal Moon became faintly visible from Natlan (5.3) after Mavuika's strike against the Abyss broke the sky. Mavuika described the event to the public as an "astronomical anomaly". The far side of the Frost Moon was rebuilt as a huge cannon in Nibelung's war. Dragon research stations from Project Moongazer still stand on it, and the twins' spaceship was hidden there.

## entry: The False Sky
@keys: false sky, firmament, split-sky
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
The sky seen from Teyvat is a false sky: a physical shell the Heavenly Principles wrapped around the planet to hide it from the cosmos, built from the dragon Lusalim's "lunar veil" technology. From inside it projects an ordinary-looking sky. The book "Before Sun and Moon" calls it the eggshell of Phanes; some see it as a cage. It has split open only a few times: when Surtalogi sent Skirk through, during Mavuika's strike in Natlan, and when Dottore tore it open as the Heretic of the False Moon (which let the Frost Moon return).

## entry: Before the Seven
@keys: Old World, unified civilization, Natlantea, Nephilheim, Delphi Pytho, Meropis, Patraipolis
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
Before the Archons, Teyvat belonged to the dragons. The Seven Sovereigns divided it under Nibelung; Natlantea, for example, was the Pyro Dragon Xiuhcoatl's land. After the Heavenly Principles conquered the dragons, Angels guided a unified human civilization with several realms: Arcadia (Windrest Peak), Sal Vindagnyr (Dragonspine), Lang-Gan (now the Chasm), Delphi Pytho (present-day Inazuma), the harbor of Ys (now under Sumeru's sands), Patraipolis, Meropis (Fontaine) and golden Hyperborea (Snezhnaya). Nod-Krai, then called Nephilheim, was made from material left over from the moons' creation. The war of funerary flame destroyed this order. Afterward new god-kingdoms arose, such as Decarabian's Mondstadt, the Guili Assembly, Remuria, Byakuyakoku/Enkanomiya and King Deshret's desert realm.
### ULTRA +
The voyager of "Deep Gallery's Distant Pact" describes the change as she saw it. When she returned, the bones of the earth were bound beneath fourfold shackles. The sky's white radiance had been split into seven fixed hues. The "Winged One" now sat on the throne that ruled the three moons.

## entry: Khaenri'ah and the Cataclysm
@keys: cataclysm, Khaenri'ah, Khaenriah, fall of Khaenri'ah, Dark Calamity
@keys2: Teyvat, world, five hundred years, 500 years
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Khaenri'ah was a nation built by humans with no god at all, deep underground beneath Sumeru. People from the seven nations joined it over the millennia. 500 years ago, monsters bearing the Abyss's power, created by the alchemist Rhinedottir, poured out of it across Teyvat. The Heavenly Principles and The Seven destroyed Khaenri'ah for its use of the Art of Khemia and Abyssal power. Its surviving pure-blooded people were cursed with immortality, and those of mixed or foreign blood were turned into monsters. The bitterness this left gave rise to the Abyss Order and the Fatui.

## entry: Mora
@keys: Mora
@keys2: Teyvat, currency, Golden House, coin
### STANDARD
Mora is Teyvat's universal currency. A loading screen says none dispute its value. Mora is also a catalyst for physical transformation, which is why crafters use it. It is named after Morax, and only the Geo Archon's power could mint it at the Golden House in Liyue, which has minted no new Mora since Zhongli surrendered his Gnosis.

## entry: The 7.1 Crisis of the Nails
@keys: Celestial Nail, Nail of Retribution, pillar of light, Eitr
@keys2: Teyvat, Ronova, Holy Soil, Cryo Dragon
@version: 7.1
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
At the end of Chapter VII Act IV (7.1) Ronova had lost her Authority and was mortally wounded. She teleported to the Celestial Nail above the Cryo Dragon's corpse, once Hyperborea's Nail, and fused the life-altering substance Eitr with it. The Nail crumbled, threatening to unleash Holy Soil across all of Teyvat, land unfit for human life. Every other Nail across the world then began casting a strange light into the sky, and ordinary people saw it at once. Zhongli and Mavuika went on high alert. The outcome is unresolved as of 7.1.
