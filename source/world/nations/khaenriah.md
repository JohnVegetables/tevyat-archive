## summary
### LITE
Khaenri'ah was a kingdom of humans deep underground beneath the Sumeru desert. It was the one nation in Teyvat that never had a god. Its people built machines, worked alchemy that could create life, and dreamed of standing above the gods' order; Dainsleif calls it "the pride of humankind." Five hundred years ago its rulers' use of Abyssal power set off the cataclysm, and the Heavenly Principles and the gods destroyed it. Its survivors were cursed: pure-blooded Khaenri'ahns cannot die, and those of mixed blood became monsters. In Sumeru it is also called Dahri.
### STANDARD +
Two royal lines ruled it: first the Crimson Moon Dynasty, then the Eclipse Dynasty, whose last Vinster King was Irmin. Pure-blooded Khaenri'ahns are marked by four-pointed star pupils. Ruin Machines, the Abyss Order and the hilichurls all come from Khaenri'ah, and so do Dainsleif, Kaeya's family and the Fatui Director Pierro. In 7.1 it was revealed that all Khaenri'ahns descend from the Tsaritsa Anastasya and the Third Descender Saarelainen, which is why they honour her as the "Mother of Khaenri'ah".

## geography
### STANDARD
A subterranean realm beneath the Sumeru desert, dark and almost barren of natural life. Kaeya says only that it was "deep underground somewhere near Sumeru". A great sealed gate in Gavireh Lajavard, in the Girdle of the Sands, is said to lead down to the ruined kingdom.
### LARGE +
Its founders once lived on the surface, and in old legend few later Khaenri'ahns had ever seen the sun or the sea. The mage Barbeloth says Khaenri'ah had several large underground lakes. Water there could reflect the sea of stars beyond the false sky, and space in that region was unusually unstable. The legend Perinheri adds that the kingdom's position let things from outside the world leak in, and its army wiped out the dangerous ones. The legend also mentions a "Sea of Wells". When the gods struck during the cataclysm, the skies turned red and filled with crimson cubes, and the land the kingdom stood on was torn apart and flooded with lava.

## places
### LARGE
Named institutions and places: the royal palace with its dungeons, where the Court of Khaenri'ah sat; the Universitas Magistrorum, the royal research academy; the Royal Library, whose banned texts were closed even to nobles; and the Royal Machine Workshop "Gnade", whose smelting furnace was abandoned before the fall. On the surface, Khaenri'ahn ruins reached through Gavireh Lajavard include an Azosite-powered machine workshop and proving ground, the last in the kingdom to use that power system. The Ruins of Dahri in Sumeru and the Schwanenritter's giant Ruin Golems date from the exodus. Dainsleif says the Inverted City in the Chasm's underground mines looks somewhat like Khaenri'ahn ruins and had ties to its civilisation.
### ULTRA +
The Gate of Zulqarnain in the desert carries the eight-pointed star motif tied to Khaenri'ah. The Schwanenritter's golems are scattered along their old route: one broken down in the Valley of Dahri, one lost in the Desert of Hadramaveth, and the last at Devantaka Mountain.

## people
### STANDARD
Khaenri'ahns descend from Anastasya and Saarelainen. Pure-bloods count as a people distinct from the surface nations: their pupils are four-pointed stars, while descendants of the Crimson Moon Dynasty have red X-shaped pupils. A heavily diluted bloodline may show no mark at all. Outsiders from the surface also came to live in Khaenri'ah; in the legend Perinheri, a princess who would not submit to Morax drifts in through its gates. Khaenri'ahns whose ancestry lay in the gods' lands were later cursed differently from pure-bloods.
### LARGE +
Society was a stratified monarchy with a proud, "obstinately pure-blooded" aristocracy. Known Khaenri'ahns: Dainsleif, captain of the royal guard; his brother Vedrfolnir "The Visionary", the court's seer; Hroptatyr "The Wise", president of the Universitas, with Eftirvit (now Pierro) as vice-president; the alchemist Rhinedottir "Gold"; Surtalogi "The Foul"; Rerir, an agent of the intelligence service; the knight-commander Thrain (now Il Capitano); the knights Halfdan and Haden; the Alberichs Anfortas and Chlothar; the Moon Marrow researcher Tholindis; the exiled monk Rauthvangi (Rotwang); and Direidyth, a village girl. Albedo was made by Rhinedottir long after the fall and says he has no attachment to Khaenri'ah. Arlecchino is of Crimson Moon blood. Kaeya is of the Alberich Clan.

## government
### STANDARD
Khaenri'ah was a hereditary monarchy. In the Eclipse era the "Vinster King" ruled with the Court of Khaenri'ah. Under him were royal mages (Hroptatyr was Chief Royal Mage), the Universitas Magistrorum, several knightly orders, and an intelligence service. Irmin was the last king, and his closest advisers were Hroptatyr and Vedrfolnir.
### LARGE +
In the final crisis a bulletin announced that, with Irmin "indisposed" and unknown threats facing the kingdom, Knight Marshal Anfortas would serve as temporary Regent and lead the Regnum Concilium Ultimum until order returned. The Alberich Clan, which had no royal blood, later taught its children that it stepped in as regent when the one-eyed king's strength failed. In the earlier Crimson Moon era, the legend Perinheri shows a ruler who let a sage found an orphanage for children who had come from beyond the world.
### ULTRA +
Justice was harsh. Surtalogi was sentenced (Rerir handled his case file), and Rauthvangi was tried, expelled from the Universitas and exiled for delving into forbidden knowledge. Irmin blinded Vedrfolnir simply for warning him. Irmin ordered everyone of Crimson Moon descent hunted down, even distant branch families, and pressed them to give up their secrets.

## religion
### STANDARD
No god ever ruled Khaenri'ah. It was not abandoned by one; it never had one. Its people suspected that the Heavenly Principles were outsiders who had usurped the world, knew the rumour that Teyvat's sky is false, and planned to rise above "the fundamental principles" of the world. The motto behind the war machines was that land is not tilled with farm tools but won "with steel and blood."
### LARGE +
Early in the Crimson Moon Dynasty, priests convinced a "muddle-minded king" that the Crimson Moon was all-powerful and spread its worship. The Crimson Moon was the Iridescent Moon's remains fused with the Abyss. The priests likely wanted it as a link to the Abyss. Khaenri'ahn astrologers later glimpsed the origin of all fate in the false sky's inverted image, which helped bring that dynasty down. Hilichurls of the Eclipse Tribe still draw eclipse symbols whose meaning only their shaman knows.

## economy
### LARGE
Little grew underground. People ate mostly meat and mushrooms, including alchemically engineered strains, and imported grain. In Irmin's last years grain imports were falling. Buildings were mostly of stone. Energy came from Azosite, refined from pure elemental energy of the ley lines and pressed into energy blocks in large smelting works. Industry ran on machine workshops and the mass production of Ruin Machines.
### ULTRA +
Khaenri'ahn engineering notes mention a newly discovered, so-called "perpetual" energy source that outperformed their alloys. They also complain that a complex Azosite system was inefficient next to power cores, even for small mass-produced Field Tillers.

## technology
### STANDARD
Khaenri'ah built the war automatons now called Ruin Machines. The Ruin Guard's original name was "Field Tiller". The machines can repair themselves and keep working for centuries. After the fall they wandered Teyvat without a purpose and gathered at ruins. Its alchemy, the Art of Khemia, could create life, which mattered in a land with little natural life.
### LARGE +
Khaenri'ahns were "obsessed with mimesis": copying and improving on natural forms, even dragons and Hypostases. Under Irmin the kingdom spied on "secrets from beyond the skies" so that its machines could "throw off the shackles" of the world's laws. One engineer begged the Knight Marshal to save machines built to the old standard, trusting human will over gods and demons alike. The Universitas Magistrorum studied astrology, alchemy, Moon Marrow and the Abyss. It predicted for the king that two stars, apparently the twins, would be caught by Teyvat's gravity. Eftirvit and Hroptatyr theorised about one day summoning "the essence of Celestia itself".
### ULTRA +
The Universitas also devised "Chaos Permeation", a minor spell that used Abyssal energy to imitate the elements and mock surface Vision holders. Street performers used it in parades and needed months to learn to shift a single element. Surtalogi mastered all seven at once and renamed it the Seven-Shifting Serpent. Alchemy and the alchemically bred black wolf warbeasts of the Beastmaster Knights flourished under the Crimson Moon Dynasty, but under the Eclipse Dynasty interest moved to mechanical engineering.

## military
### ULTRA
Khaenri'ah fielded machine armies and several knightly orders. Under the Eclipse Dynasty these included the royal guard, the Black Serpent Knights, led by Dainsleif, the "Twilight Sword"; the Schwanenritter, under Knight Marshal Anfortas; and commanders such as Thrain, honoured as a Sentinel Knight. Under the Crimson Moon Dynasty there were the Beastmaster Knights. In legend, the strongest figures of one era were Njord of the Beastmaster Knights, Alf of the Universitas, an Alberich who commanded half the knights, and "Perinheri the undefeated".

## foreign_relations
### LARGE
Khaenri'ah's godlessness was known all over Teyvat, and people from the Seven Nations went there to live. More than 2,000 years ago its diplomats, among them Oogura and Ooshima, came to Enkanomiya looking for proof that the gods were usurpers. They tried to steal the forbidden book Before Sun and Moon, and Antei destroyed their Ruin Guard at the cost of his life. Ruin Machines were already on the surface during the Archon War, when Guizhong collected some. After the fall, survivors were widely distrusted. In Sumeru, however, the sage Nagarjuna fought beside the Schwanenritter and called them "heroes of Dahri", and in Natlan the chief Ayizu befriended the refugee Thrain.
### ULTRA +
The exiled Rauthvangi became the Belyi Tsar's court alchemist Rotwang in Snezhnaya, and he unleashed the Abyss on Nod-Krai around the time of the cataclysm. The Fatui were founded under the Khaenri'ahn Eftirvit (Pierro) after the fall. Anastasya says she visited the royal palace of Khaenri'ah only once, after the cataclysm.

## history
### STANDARD
Khaenri'ah was founded as a godless nation around the time Sal Vindagnyr fell, thousands of years ago. The Crimson Moon Dynasty ruled first and was overthrown by the Eclipse Dynasty. Five hundred years ago King Irmin named the newly arrived Traveler's sibling his heir and "Savior", then used them as a vessel to absorb unlimited Abyssal power. When he blinded Vedrfolnir for warning him, Dainsleif and four others stormed the palace. All five turned on Dainsleif and took the Abyssal power, becoming the Five Sinners. Abyssal monsters poured across Teyvat, Celestia and the gods destroyed the kingdom, and Ronova cursed its people.
### LARGE +
Under Irmin the Universitas was turned over to Abyssal research. Rerir served as a secret executioner of Crimson Moon descendants, and Irmin grew more obsessed and erratic. During the palace coup the king set off "secret mechanisms" that seemingly drew Abyssal monsters to the palace. Each Sinner took one sixth of the power the sibling had absorbed, and none of them defended Khaenri'ah. Rhinedottir's creatures, among them the dragon Durin and the Riftwolves, ravaged the nations. During the cataclysm the Electro Archon Makoto died in Khaenri'ah, and the Hydro Archon Egeria fell holding back the corruption at Tunigi Hollow. The sibling, told by Direidyth that the Traveler was looking for them, left the dying kingdom to wake their twin. Asmoday caught and separated the twins as they tried to leave Teyvat.
### ULTRA +
Anfortas took power as Regent, and the Schwanenritter led survivors to the surface into Sumeru, fighting the "dark beasts" with Ruin Golems. On the surface, refugees began turning into monsters. Some went back underground, and a journal left in the Gavireh Lajavard ruins ends in despair. Thrain led his platoon to Natlan. Some time after the fall, Chlothar Alberich founded the Abyss Order, and the Traveler's sibling later took it over. Pierro joined Anastasya to found the Fatui. Halfdan and his eroded Black Serpents went on guarding hilichurls in the Chasm for centuries.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Picture a kingdom of stone halls under a rock sky, lit by machine light and alchemy instead of the sun. Its people were proud, learned and armed, and lived among brass-lined machines, laboratories and academies. They prized human will and resented the gods, and the nobility was strictly conscious of rank and blood. In its last years it was a frightened, suspicious court where speaking against the king could cost you your eyes. The surviving ruins are vast in scale and feel industrial.

## dangers
### LARGE
Today: Ruin Machines that still guard old sites; cursed remnants such as Shadowy Husks; Abyss Order mages, heralds and lectors; Riftwolves; and lingering Abyssal corruption. Forbidden knowledge and Abyssal energy corrupt land, ley lines and minds.
