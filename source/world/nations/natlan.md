## summary
### LITE
Natlan is the nation of Pyro and of war in western Teyvat, beyond Sumeru's desert, a hot land of volcanoes, canyons, mesas and hot springs. Its people are the Natlanese, organised into six tribes, each bonded with one kind of Saurian, the evolved descendants of Natlan's ancient dragons. The Pyro Archon and God of War, currently Mavuika, is unlike other Archons: every Pyro Archon is a mortal human who won the throne through battle. For 500 years Natlan fought an unending war against the Abyss, until it was won in Archon Quest Chapter V.
### STANDARD +
Its heart is the Stadium of the Sacred Flame, where the ever-burning Sacred Flame stands; under Natlan lies the Night Kingdom, a realm of ancestral spirits (the Wayob) and the dead that serves as the nation's ley lines. Prized titles called Ancient Names, inherited through the generations, carry the deeds of past heroes.

## geography
### STANDARD
Natlan lies west of the Great Red Sand, past the Desert of Hadramaveth. It is hot and volcanic, with canyons, mesas and hot springs. Each of the six tribes holds its own territory (Tequemecan Valley, Coatepec Mountain, Toyac Springs, Quahuacan Cliff, the Tezcatepetonco Range and Atocpan under the Great Volcano of Tollan) around the Stadium of the Sacred Flame in the Basin of Unnumbered Flames.
### LARGE +
Further areas: the ruined Eternal Empire of Ochkanatlan, the Ancient Sacred Mountain (whose interior holds Abyss-like dark mud), and Easybreeze Holiday Resort, an island off the westernmost edge of Natlan once called Tenochtzitoc, "where the war had begun and met its end". The Great Volcano of Tollan erupts periodically, raining fireballs over Atocpan. Saurian species hold their own territories, whose borders largely define the tribal lands. Phlogiston occurs naturally as crystals, rivers of liquid and hot gas, and Pale Mist drains the colour from some areas.

## government
### LITE
The Pyro Archon leads Natlan from the Speaker's Chamber at the Stadium of the Sacred Flame; each tribe has its own chief.
### STANDARD +
By ancient tradition the six chiefs gather by the flame in the Speaker's Chamber at regular intervals and negotiate openly under the Archon's eye; the Chamber coordinates the Stadium's and the tribes' affairs and also appears to act as Natlan's highest court.
### LARGE +
Pyro Archons bear the divine name Haborym. Xbalanque, the first, borrowed the power of Ronova, the Ruler of Death, to set the rules by which ordinary people can rise to the throne; traditionally the Pilgrimage was also how the Natlanese chose their Archon, judging battle the truest test. A new Archon's "inner flame" awakens and the Sacred Flame grants them knowledge and memories of the land. Pyro Archons age and die like anyone else, so Natlan has had many. In formal settings Mavuika is a stern ruler; in private she is a friendly, big-sister figure who parties at the Weary Inn on the eve of a Pilgrimage.
### ULTRA +
Known Pyro Archons: Xbalanque (about 2,000 years ago; an outlander with no tribe); the unnamed scarlet-eyed youth of the Flower-Feather Clan who overthrew Och-Kan and later asked that his name be erased from history; an unnamed female Archon of the Muratans, acquainted with Venti and reigning more than 1,600 years ago, who held martial tournaments between the tribes; Ndenge and Xumucul (known only as Archons Gosoythoth imitated); Cochanina of the People of the Springs, who drove the "Volcano Lord" from their land, and Gloharry, Shulevitz and Isaja, who worked to contain the Bakunawa in the Mare Jivari; and Mavuika of the Scions of the Canopy, who reigned during the cataclysm and reigns again today. According to a note by the first Cryo Archon, the Heavenly Principles had fated the half-dragon Och-Kan to rule Natlan, and Xbalanque's seizure of that destiny was the culmination of millennia of rebellion against Celestia.

## religion
### STANDARD
The tribes revere their ancestors and the Wayob: formless spirits, "will without form or thought", that dwell in the Night Kingdom, see past and future, protect their tribe and choose who receives Ancient Names. The dead go to the Night Kingdom, slowly merge into its sea of souls and are eventually reborn in Natlan in another form. Warriors draw power from these souls, the Nightsouls (Nightsoul's Blessing). The Sacred Flame, also called K'ahk', is the conduit to the Night Kingdom and the foundation of Natlan's defence.
### LARGE +
A tribe's Wayob is believed to speak through its chief's dreams, and the Wayob can manifest in Natlan through obsidian totems and Nightsouls. The Night Kingdom was built about 6,000 years ago by Yohualtecuhtin, the Lord of the Night, from fragments of the ley lines shattered in the war of funerary flame, under Ronova's guidance; Xbalanque later strengthened it. Because it is not like ley lines elsewhere, it records only Natlanese deeds and long refused foreign souls; since the war ended and Capitano merged with the Lord of the Night, it accepts people from other nations. The Masters of the Night-Wind are the tribe closest to it: their shamans commune with the Wayob, watch the ley lines and Phlogiston, and weave scrolls recording major events.

## culture
### STANDARD
The official description calls Natlan "the nation of oaths"; it prizes strength and competition, and "war" is part of everyday life. Visitors describe the Natlanese as energetic people whose "souls are forged for battle from birth", fond of graffiti, music, dancing and showing off. Warriors dress in imitation of their Saurian partners, whom people treat as family. Until the war ended, Natlanese could not safely leave the nation: outside its borders the Wayob's protection weakened and leavers suffered memory loss and emotional problems, so a chief had to seek the Wayob's approval before anyone departed.

## economy
### LARGE
Because its people could not leave, Natlan's economy was long closed, and tourism was effectively its only export. Each tribe has a niche: the Children of Echoes mine, smith and appraise gems, are Natlan's only source of ritual obsidian, and train the Name Engravers who forge Ancient Names; the People of the Springs live from hot-spring tourism; the Scions of the Canopy take delivery and hunting commissions and host extreme sports; the Masters of the Night-Wind offer fortune-telling, blessings of tools and weapons, and artisan goods believed to carry special effects; the Collective of Plenty grows Natlan's best produce on volcanic soil and shares the surplus with other tribes; the Flower-Feather Clan is known for elite warriors and the saurian vet Ifa. The Pyro Archon pays for free food and lodging at the Weary Inn for Pilgrimage contestants and their families.
### ULTRA +
Natlanese ores are all associated with Phlogiston; Vennessa already praised their quality over Mondstadt's. Varesa's family orchard has sold produce abroad to Escoffier in Fontaine and Dori in Sumeru. Inazuma's fondness for Natlan's hot springs led it to commission Snezhnayan boilers to build artificial ones at home.

## history
### STANDARD
Before humans ruled, Natlan was Natlantea, a dragon civilisation under Xiuhcoatl, the Pyro Dragon Sovereign, and the artificial Thirteen Sovereign Lords. About 2,000 years ago Xbalanque slew Xiuhcoatl and became the first Pyro Archon; the tribes then founded the Eternal Empire of Ochkanatlan under Kukulkan's half-dragon son Och-Kan, who became a tyrant until a scarlet-eyed youth, the second Pyro Archon, overthrew him. Five hundred years ago the Abyss struck Natlan harder than any other nation. The exiled warrior Tenoch and six heroes, one per tribe, fought beside the newly ascended Mavuika and drove it back, but it rooted itself in the Night Kingdom.
### LARGE +
Natlan took no part in the Archon War, as the dragons were fighting each other, and the dragon Kukulkan gave humans Phlogiston to revive their civilisation. Mavuika's plan: the tribes would rebuild for 500 years, the Wayob would then each choose a hero bearing one of the six heroic Ancient Names, and with them the Archon would drive the Abyss out for good. To lead it in person she placed her life in the Sacred Flame and woke again in the present; she refused every suggestion to use the Pyro Gnosis, which would have rewritten the ley lines and erased Natlan's history and culture. Her sister Hine helped rebuild the Scions of the Canopy. Soon after the cataclysm the Khaenri'ahn knight Thrain, later the Fatui Harbinger Capitano, fled to Natlan with his platoon and fought the Abyss alongside the tribes. Over the centuries the Abyss corrupted the Wayob, so when the Traveler arrived only three heroes (Kinich, Iansan, Xilonen) had been acknowledged.
### ULTRA +
Earlier, a civil war split a seventh tribe: one branch, the Muratans, wandered abroad and settled in Mondstadt (Vennessa was one of them); the rest were confined to Tenochtzitoc. In the cataclysm Rhinedottir's Bakunawa killed all of Tenochtzitoc but the exiled Tenoch, and several Archons spent themselves containing it in what became the Mare Jivari. Among the heroes, Burkina was slain by his Abyss-tainted Yumkasaur Kongamato, Sanhaj and Tenoch died detonating a miniature Night Kingdom at the Mare Jivari, Menilek and Sundjatta fell in the final battle, and Tupac carried the unconscious Mavuika to the Stadium for her sacrifice and died on his feet. Beneath the Children of Echoes, Traore and two Khaenri'ahn warriors pinned an Abyssal invasion underground with the Shadow Pins. After the cataclysm an adventure team led by Taika, and later his daughter Bona, sought the Legendary Tonatiuh at Ochkanatlan and were lured to their deaths by Kukulkan.

## foreign_relations
### LARGE
Mondstadt: Vennessa, founder of the Knights of Favonius, descended from Natlan's exiled Muratans, and Bennett, born in Natlan and carried to Mondstadt as a baby, owes his chronic bad luck to being cut off from Natlan's ley lines. Snezhnaya: Capitano came to take the Pyro Gnosis around 3.1, and his subordinates captured Saurians for Phlogiston experiments; he ended as one of Natlan's honoured heroes. Inazuma: Natlan's hot springs inspired artificial ones there. Sumeru: in 6.6 Mavuika gave the Pyro Gnosis to Nahida through the Traveler, and Nahida later visited Natlan to thank her. Khaenri'ah: its refugees fought for Natlan after the cataclysm, and the Traveler's sibling wove New Khaenri'ah's ley lines over Natlan. Nod-Krai: visitors from Nod-Krai reached Natlan in 5.8, and in the 6.0 prelude the automaton Ineffa washed ashore there.
### ULTRA +
Natlan was the last nation to have any of its people appear in-game: before 5.0 it was known only through foreigners such as Inazuman bathhouse keepers, a Fontainian sailor who hoped to settle there, and a Sumeru merchant who called it "colorful, contradictory and charming". The sailor feared its many tribes and rules would make it hard to fit in.

## festivals
### LARGE
The Pilgrimage of the Return of the Sacred Flame is Natlan's great national contest; since the war it has been an athletic competition, whose first winner was Kinich. The Scions of the Canopy hold an annual Turnfire Night using small flames split from the Sacred Flame.

## dangers
### LARGE
Liquid and gaseous Phlogiston scald and burn, unstable Phlogiston crystals explode, and the Great Volcano of Tollan hurls fireballs. Secret Source automatons and dragon cannons still guard ancient ruins and went haywire in 6.0. Abyss Order cells struck every tribe in 5.7. Ochkanatlan was long a cursed, Abyss-infested ruin under Flower-Feather Clan blockade, and those who slipped past the patrols died there. Wild Saurians, tribal warrior bosses and survivors of Abyssal Corrosion Syndrome are reminders of the war.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Blazing sun, red rock and volcanic haze; huge graffiti murals and hip, colourful street style; Saurians everywhere, from small whelps riding on shoulders to towering Tatankasaurs; hot springs and surf on the coast, dance-offs and drills in the mining gorge, balloon platforms on the cliffs, and a roaring crowd at the Stadium whose flame can be seen from far off. The Weary Inn is the place to celebrate.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 Natlan is at peace. The Abyss was purged from the Night Kingdom in 5.3 and the Night Warden Wars are over. Mavuika lives and still rules, because Capitano took her fated death upon himself. Natlanese may now leave the nation without harm, though they still need their chief's permission. Natlan no longer has its Pyro Gnosis.
### STANDARD +
Capitano merged with the Lord of the Night, making her undying so she can keep repairing the ley lines; his body sits dormant on the Throne of the Primal Fire atop Ochkanatlan. The Pilgrimage is now a sports competition. In 6.6 Mavuika gave the Pyro Gnosis away for good to help burn the corrupted Irminsul, and it vanished. The Archon Quest has moved to Snezhnaya (Chapter VII, 7.0-7.1). At its close, after Ronova destroyed a Celestial Nail in Snezhnaya with Eitr, the other Nails began emitting pillars of light, including the one near the Stadium of the Sacred Flame, right beside where people live. Mavuika, who blames the Heavenly Principles for most of Natlan's past disasters, is warning the population to watch for danger; what the phenomenon means is not yet known.
### ULTRA +
Capitano is commemorated on a monument to the fallen near the Masters of the Night-Wind. In 7.1 the Traveler tells Anastasya that, according to Mavuika, the Gnosis has returned to the ley lines; Nahida earlier said no trace of its destruction was found. In 7.0 Pierro revealed Capitano's will, which gave most of his subordinates and all his key personnel to Tartaglia, along with his seat as a councillor of the Snezhnograd Veche.

## entry: The Pilgrimage and the Night Warden Wars
@keys: Pilgrimage of the Return of the Sacred Flame, Night Warden Wars, Night Warden War, Contending Fire, Ode of Resurrection
### STANDARD
Every tribe fields warriors who fight alongside their Saurians, and the Flower-Feather Clan's Qucusaur Riders once patrolled all of Natlan. Until the war ended, the Pilgrimage of the Return of the Sacred Flame chose the strongest Ancient Name bearers to fight the Abyss in the Night Kingdom in the Night Warden Wars; fallen bearers could be revived by the Ode of Resurrection if at least one teammate came back victorious.
### LARGE +
The Pilgrimage was a two-phase tournament at the Stadium: teams, usually led by a bearer, were dropped by balloon around the country to fight for Contending Sparks, then winners fought one-on-one. Battles between Natlanese produce Contending Fire, the only fuel for the Sacred Flame, so outlanders could not compete. If a whole team fell, its members stayed lost and their Ancient Names were forgotten: "the victors shall burn bright, while the losers must turn to ash". Since the war, the Pilgrimage is a purely athletic contest open to outsiders.

## entry: Natlanese Daily Life and Technology
@keys: Grainfruit, cacahuatl, shadow boxing, Phlogiston Wings, Flamestrider, Phlogiston engraving
@keys2: Natlan, Natlanese
### LARGE
Grainfruit is the staple food (in tatacos and soups); cacahuatl, bitter and complex, flavours sweets and drinks, and Embercore Flower nectar, sweet with a burning aftertaste, is popular. Food is hearty, heavy on starch and meat. In the Collective of Plenty, masked wrestlers keep a tradition that the loser of a duel between two masked fighters must unmask. Natlanese boxers train by "shadow boxing", which an Inazuman visitor carried home. Since the Abyss was purged, Natlanese can travel abroad without harm, but they still need their chief's permission.
### ULTRA +
Technology builds on the dragons' "Secret Source": Phlogiston engravings imitate the dragons' iridescent inscriptions to store information and create Spiritways. Phlogiston-powered vehicles are rare and almost all built by Xilonen (Kachina's drill, Chasca's flying cannon, Mavuika's motorcycle, the Flamestrider); the Phlogiston Wings jetpack was designed by Tepal, who died testing it, and finished by his mother Allpa. The Flower-Feather Clan keeps platforms aloft on hot-air balloons, and the People of the Springs press music records that also play on Fontainian players.

## entry: The Six Tribes

Six major tribes have existed since Och-Kan's era. Children of Echoes (Nanatzcayan, Tequemecan Valley): miners, excavators and gem appraisers, Geo, bonded with Tepetlisaurs. People of the Springs (Meztli, Toyac Springs): guides and hot-spring keepers, Hydro, Koholasaurs. Flower-Feather Clan (Tlalocan, Quahuacan Cliff): rangers and riders, Pyro, Qucusaurs. Masters of the Night-Wind (Mictlan, Tezcatepetonco Range): shamans who monitor ley lines and Phlogiston, Cryo, Iktomisaurs. Collective of Plenty (Teteocan, Atocpan): farmers, bodybuilders and wrestlers, Electro, Tatankasaurs. Current chiefs: Pacal (Children of Echoes), Wayna (Scions), Amina (People of the Springs), Mutota (Flower-Feather), Biram (Night-Wind) and Acatl (Collective of Plenty).
### LARGE +
Each tribe's Wayob can manifest as a guardian spirit. The Children of Echoes and Collective of Plenty were founded by the twins Ixquieh and Ahpub; the Flower-Feather Clan by Lianca; the Masters of the Night-Wind by the Priest-King Maghan. A defunct seventh tribe of Anemo warriors split after a civil war into the wandering Muratans and the Tenochtzitoc, who all but perished in the cataclysm.

## entry: Saurians and Phlogiston
@keys: Saurian, Saurians, Phlogiston, Tepetlisaurus, Yumkasaurus, Koholasaurus, Qucusaurus, Iktomisaurus, Tatankasaurus, Secret Source
### STANDARD
Saurians are Natlan's dragons: Pyro vishaps that, after the Heavenly Principles cast down nails against Natlan's Phlogiston-rich soil, adapted by losing their intelligence (a "devolution" in the eyes of old dragons such as Kukulkan and Och-Kan). They now live closely with humans, choose their own partners and cannot speak human language, though tribe members read their moods. Phlogiston is an advanced form of Pyro, a programmable matter once used by the dragons, that powers Nightsoul's Blessing, Saurian abilities and Natlanese devices, and symbolises the people's indomitable spirit. Ancient dragon "Secret Source" machines still dot the land.
### LARGE +
Yumkasaurs normally live under a century, and Kongamato's far longer life is blamed on Abyssal contamination; the Iktomisaur Elder Tlapo has lived for several centuries. Qucusaurs refuse riders who show the slightest hesitation. Cacucu, Ifa's Qucusaur, is the only Saurian able to say a few human phrases.

## entry: Ancient Names and the Heroes of Natlan
@keys: Ancient Name, Ancient Names, tocaitl, Heroes of Natlan, Name Engraver, Malipo, Uwezo, Baraka, Umoja, Bidii, Vuka, Tumaini, Kiongozi
### STANDARD
An Ancient Name (tocaitl) is a title embodying a virtue and an obsidian stone engraved with it, carrying the deeds of all its past bearers. The Wayob grant them to tribe members whose lives exemplify the Name; the Pyro Archon can also commission a new one, forged by a Name Engraver at the cost of part of their lifespan. Six Names belong to the Heroes of Natlan, first borne by Xbalanque's companions and then by Tenoch's six during the cataclysm. The modern heroes: Xilonen (Baraka), Kinich (Malipo), Mualani (Umoja), Chasca (Vuka), Ororon (Bidii) and Iansan (Uwezo). The Traveler is the first outlander to hold one, Tumaini, chosen by the Lord of the Night and forged by Xilonen.
### LARGE +
Past bearers: Baraka – Ixquieh, Sundjatta; Malipo – Yupanqui, Burkina; Umoja – Uenuku, Tupac; Vuka – Lianca, Menilek; Bidii – Maghan, Sanhaj; Uwezo – Ahpub, Wanjiru. A newly acknowledged hero receives the memories of the cataclysm-era bearer. Mavuika, Tenoch, Capitano and the Traveler with Paimon are also counted among the Heroes of Natlan. Kachina bears Uthabiti and Citlali Ukumbuko. Mavuika's Ancient Name is Kiongozi ("leader"); the wiki infers that all Pyro Archons share it. Tenoch lost his Ancient Name while still alive, probably because he was an exile.

## entry: Natlantea, Xbalanque and Ochkanatlan
@keys: Natlantea, Xiuhcoatl, Kukulkan, Xbalanque, Och-Kan, Ochkanatlan, Eternal Empire, Grand Alliance, Thirteen Sovereign Lords, scarlet-eyed youth
@spoiler: minor
### LARGE
Xiuhcoatl, born from the primal flame, split off part of it to create his brother Kukulkan, carved the Sacred City of Tollan inside the Great Volcano, and made the Thirteen Sovereign Lords to govern Natlantea. The Heavenly Principles stole his Pyro authority and left him in a false death. As Natlantea declined, Kukulkan secretly gave humans Phlogiston and fathered the half-dragon Och-Kan to unite both peoples. About 2,000 years ago Xbalanque, an outsider who had forced the warring tribes into peace and was crowned by the Lord of the Night, stormed Tollan and slew Xiuhcoatl; the Three Princes and ten of the Lords also died. Xbalanque and Kukulkan each took one of Xiuhcoatl's eyes as an oath of peace. Xbalanque's followers founded Ochkanatlan on the ruins of the Ninth Lord's realm, and when Xbalanque "entered the Sacred Flame" the seven tribes put their authority in Och-Kan's hands as the Grand Alliance.
### ULTRA +
Och-Kan betrayed Xbalanque's commandments: he tried to exterminate dragons and Saurians, mistreated his people, banned worship of the ancestors and the Night Kingdom, and imprisoned the Ninth Lord Ixlel to power his city. The scarlet-eyed youth, banished for protecting Saurians, became the second Pyro Archon and overthrew him. Och-Kan survived split into a mind sealed in the Core of Chu'ulel and a body that became the Dragon of the City of Flowing Ash; sovereignty returned to the tribes, who reconnected with the Night Kingdom and mended ties with the Saurians. Ochkanatlan became a cursed ruin. In 5.2 the Traveler met Och-Kan's mind ("Cocouik"), defeated his reunited self and freed Ixlel's spirit.

## entry: The Night Kingdom and the Lord of the Night
@keys: Night Kingdom, realm of the Wayob, Yohualtecuhtin, Lord of the Night, Nightsouls
@keys2: Natlan, Wayob, Mavuika, Capitano, Sacred Flame
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
The Night Kingdom is a realm deep underground, "a liminal space between body and mind, life and death", that functions as Natlan's ley lines. The Lord of the Night, Yohualtecuhtin, built it about 6,000 years ago under Ronova's guidance and slumbered until Capitano and Ororon's attempt to rewrite the ley lines with a Source Mechanism woke her in 5.1. When the Abyss struck in the cataclysm it rooted itself in the Night Kingdom; over five centuries the Wayob wore themselves down fighting it, and by the Traveler's arrival Mavuika estimated the realm would fall within a year.
### ULTRA +
After the war the Lord of the Night returned to the Night Kingdom to maintain the ley lines, made undying by Capitano's sacrifice. The Weaver of Secrets, one of the Sovereign Lords, merged her consciousness into the realm to create a "dream labyrinth" where dead dragons and humans find peace.

## entry: The Natlan-Abyss War
@keys: Night Warden Wars, Gosoythoth, Abyssal invasion of Natlan, Incandescent Ode of Resurrection
@keys2: Natlan, Mavuika, Abyss, Kachina, Chasca
@version: 5.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
In 5.0 Kachina fell in a victorious Night Warden War but the Ode of Resurrection failed to bring her back, because the Abyss had corrupted the Wayob. The Traveler, Mualani, Chasca and Iansan pulled her out of the Night Kingdom, and Mavuika burned the keepsakes her people had given her over the centuries, each holding Contending Fire, to rescue them. Mualani, then Ororon, then Chasca (after her adoptive sister Chuychu died of Abyssal contamination during a nationwide invasion) were acknowledged as heroes. With all six, Mavuika channelled Ronova's borrowed power to break the invasion. In 5.3 the Traveler, granted Pyro by the Lord of the Night, descended with Mavuika into the Night Kingdom, helped by the souls of the fallen, and destroyed Gosoythoth, the heart of the Abyss there, ending the war.
### LARGE +
The invasion cost thousands of lives, overran the Scions of the Canopy and hit the Flower-Feather Clan hardest, as the last tribe without a hero; Gosoythoth even extinguished the Sacred Flame for a time. In the final battle it took the form of the Lord of Eroded Primal Fire, built from Xiuhcoatl's memories, and imitated past Archons.

@keys2: Natlan, Mavuika, Capitano, Ronova, Lord of the Night
@version: 5.3
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
Using Ronova's power once was Mavuika's unique right, at the cost of her fated death. After Gosoythoth fell, Ronova demanded payment, and Mavuika prepared to give her life to repair the Night Kingdom. Capitano stepped in instead: a Khaenri'ahn cursed with undying life by Ronova herself, he offered his own "death", which would create a paradox unless she lifted the curse on his people. Ronova upheld the curse; Capitano merged with the Lord of the Night, making her immortal, and his body remains seated on the Throne of the Primal Fire atop Ochkanatlan. Mavuika lived, and one of his soldiers, Rotchev, attended the memorial.

## entry: The Pyro Gnosis
@keys: Pyro Gnosis
@keys2: Natlan, Mavuika, Nahida, Irminsul, Capitano
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Mavuika kept the Pyro Gnosis only as a last resort, refusing to use it because rewriting the ley lines would erase Natlan's history; Capitano came for it but, after losing his duel with her, declined to take it while she was weakened and later abandoned his mission. During the 5.x invasion its power kept the Sacred Flame burning.
