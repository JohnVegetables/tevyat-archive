## summary
### LITE
Sumeru is the nation of wisdom in west-central Teyvat, ruled by the Dendro Archon Lesser Lord Kusanali, known personally as Nahida and by her divine name Buer. The Sumeru Akademiya, the most prestigious seat of learning in Teyvat, also governs the country from its campus in Sumeru City, which is built into a giant tree. The north is a dense rainforest of scholars, rangers and forest spirits. The south and west are the Great Red Sand, the desert of the long-dead King Deshret and home of the Eremite mercenary clans.
### STANDARD +
Visitors find a scholarly, exam-obsessed and practical society. For centuries it lived under sages who confined their own god, scorned the arts, discouraged dreaming and fed the people's minds into the Akasha network. Nahida was freed in Chapter III. She shut the Akasha down and has been reforming the Akademiya since, while the old suspicion between forest folk and desert folk slowly eases.

## geography
### STANDARD
Sumeru borders Liyue to the east (the Dharma Forest is reached directly from The Chasm), Natlan lies beyond the western edge of its desert, and the far desert lies on the road toward Fontaine. The ruins of Khaenri'ah lie directly beneath it. It has three subregions: the Dharma Forest, the rainforest of the north and east where Sumeru City stands; the Great Red Sand (also called the Deshret Desert), King Deshret's old realm; and the remote Girdle of the Sands beyond it.
### LARGE +
The Wall of Samiel stands where forest meets sand, raised to keep the rainforest from turning into desert; the Akademiya maintains it and forbids damaging it. The rainforest grew from desert that the Dendro Archon terraformed with the Varuna Contraption. The Great Red Sand itself is said to have formed about 6,000 years ago, when the first Celestial Nail buried an older forest and destroyed the ancient city of Ys. Ruin devices, sandstorms, oases and colossal pyramids fill the desert. The Girdle of the Sands is a cataclysm-era battlefield whose great rift, Tunigi Hollow, leads down to Khaenri'ahn ruins.

## government
### STANDARD
The Dendro Archon is head of state, but the Sumeru Akademiya runs daily affairs. It has six Darshans (schools), each led by a Sage; one of them is also Grand Sage. The current Grand Sage is Naphis (Amurta), Tighnari's old teacher, who is famous for his temper and integrity. The other sages are Baladhuri (Vahumana), Alkindi (Spantamad), Firnas (Kshahrewar), Shakir (Rtawahist) and Rushd (Haravatat). The Matra, led by General Mahamatra Cyno, punish academic crimes.
### LARGE +
Most of the civil service comes from the Akademiya; Alhaitham is its Scribe. Mahamata are alumni sent around the country to oversee information, decrees, trade and healthcare. The Navbed handles trade and taxes, with port masters at Port Ormos and Bayda Harbor, and the Vizier administers the Grand Bazaar and Treasures Street. The Forest Rangers protect the rainforest. The Corps of Thirty, an Eremite brigade on permanent contract, keeps the peace in Sumeru City. Under Grand Sage Azar the old sages became authoritarian: they controlled information, banned unlicensed books and finally all public performances, and conspired with the Fatui. After Chapter III, Azar and his three collaborating sages were removed and exiled. Alhaitham turned down the Grand Sage's seat and only briefly served as Acting Grand Sage. Four new sages were later chosen under Nahida's oversight, and Naphis became Grand Sage.
### ULTRA +
The Akademiya's rules rest on three virtues (Admonition, Ingenuity, Praxis) and six forbidden "sins": interfering with human evolution, tampering with life and death, delving beyond the universe, investigating the origin of words, revering gods without acts of devotion, and attempting the forbidden without fear. Breaking them can mean expulsion or prison. The sages took credit for these rules, but the Temple of Silence originally proposed them. Research on mechanical lifeforms is banned after cruel experiments in Spantamad.

## religion
### STANDARD
Sumeru reveres the Dendro Archon as the God of Wisdom. Under the old sages she was a distant, little-respected figure locked away in the Sanctuary of Surasthana; only a few devotees such as Dunyarzad and Nilou honoured her. Many desert people instead revered the ancient King Deshret, sometimes called Al-Ahmar, and some Eremite groups still await his return. Since Chapter III the Akademiya and the people openly honour Nahida, and the Sabzeruz Festival marks her birthday.
### LARGE +
Her Statue of the Seven shows a childlike figure seated on a huge leaf. Since Chapter III, the world has forgotten that Sumeru once had an earlier Dendro Archon. The Traveler alone remembers Greater Lord Rukkhadevata, and everyone else believes Nahida has always been the Dendro Archon and simply lost her memories in the cataclysm. Desert faith runs deep. The Temple of Silence keeps the knowledge and memory of Hermanubis, Deshret's jackal-headed minister. Desert folk who settled Aaru Village long ago did so to await Al-Ahmar's return. Before Chapter III some Eremites, misled by an Akademiya disinformation campaign, believed he would soon be revived.

## history
### STANDARD
Sumeru was shaped by three god-kings, the Dendro Archon of the forest, King Deshret of the desert and Nabu Malikata, the Goddess of Flowers. After Nabu Malikata died, Deshret turned to forbidden knowledge and brought madness, scales and the Withering onto his land. He died ending the catastrophe, and his civilisation broke apart into rival city-states. In the cataclysm 500 years ago, forbidden knowledge contaminated Irminsul, and Lesser Lord Kusanali was born as the new Dendro Archon. The sages confined her for five centuries. In Chapter III they also tried to build an artificial god with the Fatui Harbinger Dottore, but the Traveler and Nahida's allies defeated the plot.
### LARGE +
Earlier still, before humans, the Dendro dragon Apep ruled this land, and Irminsul first grew in her court until the Heavenly Principles took both her authority and the tree. In 6.6, Irminsul seized up and Nahida collapsed, and Sumeru had to evacuate the whole rainforest. A Dottore working from inside Irminsul was behind it, and Pantalone backed him. Nahida had the Traveler bring her Mavuika's Pyro Gnosis and burned Irminsul down, and Dottore died with it. The reprogrammed Aaru now holds the world's records in Irminsul's place.

## customs
### STANDARD
Learning shapes daily life: seniority depends on academic progress rather than age, and thesis defences are notoriously gruelling. Before Chapter III, Sumeru adults famously did not dream, which the sages praised as rational, and art was seen as useless. People dream again now and the arts are recovering, though talking about art still kills a conversation. The great festivals are the Sabzeruz Festival and the Akademiya Extravaganza.
### LARGE +
The Sabzeruz Festival began as a birthday banquet for the old Dendro Archon, at which the Goddess of Flowers danced and purple Padisarah flowers bloomed under her feet. Today it celebrates Kusanali's birthday. Its customs are the Haft-Mewa table of seven foods, an actor playing Farris the Knight of Flowers for the children, and the Dance of Sabzeruz, which Nilou performs at the Grand Bazaar. The old sages starved the festival of funds; Nahida's restoration brought back official celebrations. The Akademiya Extravaganza combines a yearly Wisdom Gala, where the Darshans compete for students, with an Interdarshan Championship held every four years. Before the Akasha shut down, users woke their terminals with the words "May the Mighty God bless us with their voice of wisdom."
### ULTRA +
Each year the Akademiya gives the Pir Kavikavus Prize to a groundbreaking scholar under forty. The Grand Bazaar's Zubayr Theater is home to Nilou's troupe. Eremite sword-dances and music preserve their ancestors' worship rituals and fighting styles. Sumeru's local wine is frigid snake wine. Desert lore was written in a script credited to Thoth.

## economy
### LARGE
Sumeru's main trade runs through Port Ormos, its largest port. Port Ormos is cheaper and looser than the capital, and smuggling is more common there. Bayda Harbor in Lokapala Jungle is another entry point with its own port master, and Caravan Ribat is the trade post at the forest-desert border. Knowledge itself is a commodity. The Akasha used to give out information by clearance level, and illegal Canned Knowledge capsules were sold on a black market. Eremites hire out as guards and soldiers all over Teyvat and have an open partnership with the Adventurers' Guild. The Bimarstan gives free Akademiya medical care, and Pardis Dhyai supplies the Forest Rangers with seeds and research. Merchants like Dori sell almost anything to anyone.
### ULTRA +
Sumeru has many state-backed public services but also scams: training centres have sprung up for people who cannot get into the Akademiya, and some of them are frauds. A route from Mondstadt's Dornman Port to Port Ormos has opened. In 6.6 the evacuation and the Fatui's fake "merchant caravans" entering through Bayda Harbor disrupted trade. The Fatui later sent relief supplies from Snezhnaya.

## military
### LARGE
Sumeru has no national army. The Corps of Thirty is the oldest Eremite brigade, founded by thirty mercenaries the sages hired, and it guards Sumeru City from the Citadel of Regzar under its commander, the Parchamdar Rukh Shah. Other Eremite bands guard posts like Caravan Ribat. The Matra investigate and arrest offenders, and the Forest Rangers police the rainforest. In crises Sumeru depends on Vision-bearers and allies. In 6.6 Cyno, Dehya, Candace, Jeht and Eremite fighters stormed Sumeru City while others infiltrated Irminsul.

## foreign_relations
### LARGE
Snezhnaya: the old sages worked with the Fatui. Dottore, once the Akademiya student Zandik, used Sumeru for experiments and walked away with both the Electro and Dendro Gnoses. In 6.6 Pantalone financed and covered the attack on Irminsul. Afterwards Pulcinella sent a carefully worded apology and relief aid, and Nahida and Naphis agreed to keep watch on Snezhnaya. Natlan: Mavuika handed over the Pyro Gnosis to resolve the Irminsul crisis and accepted its loss calmly when Nahida came to tell her. Inazuma: the Electro Gnosis and the puppet Scaramouche (now the Wanderer, who serves Nahida) came from Ei's realm. Mondstadt: Lisa and Signora (Rosalyne) studied at the Akademiya, and Collei once lived in Mondstadt. Nod-Krai: Nefer comes from Sumeru, and the angel Nicole came from Nod-Krai with her to help in 6.6. Liyue: The Chasm links the two.
### ULTRA +
The Akademiya admits foreign students and sends Driyosh across Teyvat, so Sumeru scholars turn up in every nation. Kaeya once stowed away to Sumeru as a boy, hoping to learn about Khaenri'ah beneath it. In 7.1 the Tsaritsa questioned the Traveler about the vanished Pyro Gnosis. The Traveler also told Pierro that the forbidden knowledge had been purged from Irminsul in Sumeru.

## dangers
### LARGE
Withering Zones, whose Decay can kill people who lack a Vision. Fungi, Rishboland Tigers and Ruin Machines in the forest. Sandstorms, quicksand, heat and Primal Constructs in the desert, along with the remains of forbidden knowledge. Hostile Eremite bands and kidnappers. Abyss forces around Tunigi Hollow. Divine Knowledge Capsules and attempts to touch Irminsul's knowledge have driven scholars mad.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
In the forest: humid green shade, giant mushrooms and glowing plants, rain on broad leaves, scholars arguing over theses in taverns and the bazaar's spices and music. In the desert: red dunes, colossal ruins half-buried in sand, oasis camps and wary mercenaries. Sumeru City rises in tiers up the Divine Tree from the docks to the Akademiya, with the Sanctuary of Surasthana at the top.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1, Sumeru is at peace and rebuilding after the Irminsul crisis of 6.6. Nahida has recovered and governs openly with Grand Sage Naphis and the Akademiya. Irminsul has been burned away, and Aaru now stores the world's records in its place. The Archon Quest has moved on to Snezhnaya (Chapter VII). At the end of 7.1 Sumeru's Celestial Nail began, like every other Nail, to cast a strange light into the sky.
### STANDARD +
Dottore is dead. The Fatui still hold the Electro and Dendro Gnoses. The Pyro Gnosis disappeared in the fire. Thoth and Heryshaf of the Seven Pillars are staying at the Temple of Silence. Collei is now a full Forest Ranger and has been freed of her god remains. Alhaitham and Kaveh have repaired their damaged home. After the Nails began to glow in 7.1, Candace, Dehya and Dehya's mercenaries camped near Mt. Damavand in the Desert of Hadramaveth to keep watch.
### ULTRA +
The Akasha remains out of daily use. Nicole has moved her dependence from Irminsul to Aaru. Before collapsing, Nahida gave the Traveler a flower-shaped key whose secret about an "inverted statue" is still unexplained.

## entry: Sumeru City and the Divine Tree

### STANDARD
Sumeru City, the "City of Wisdom", is built up the trunk and branches of the Divine Tree in Avidya Forest. The docks are at the bottom. The lower and middle tiers hold the Grand Bazaar with the Zubayr Theater, Treasures Street, Lambad's Tavern and the Bimarstan. Higher up is the Akademiya campus with its library, the House of Daena. The Sanctuary of Surasthana, Nahida's treetop palace, sits at the very top. The Corps of Thirty is based at the Citadel of Regzar.
### LARGE +
The Grand Sage's office is above the House of Daena, which fell out of use while the Akasha was running. In 6.6 the city was evacuated, the Fatui occupied it and Dottore's Irminsul tree burst up nearby. It has since been rebuilt.

## entry: The Rainforest and the Aranara
@keys: Dharma Forest, Avidya Forest, Gandharva Ville, Vimara Village, Vanarana, Aranara, Pardis Dhyai, Apam Woods
### STANDARD
The Dharma Forest is a damp, layered rainforest of giant trees, mushrooms and rivers. Its areas are Avidya Forest (Sumeru City, Gandharva Ville), Lokapala Jungle (Bayda Harbor, Mawtiyima Forest, Chatrakam Cave), Ardravi Valley (Port Ormos, Vimara Village, Devantaka Mountain), Ashavan Realm (Caravan Ribat, Pardis Dhyai, Apam Woods), Vissudha Field, Vanarana and the Lost Nursery. The Forest Rangers (Vanara) work out of Gandharva Ville, where Tighnari is Forest Watcher. Pardis Dhyai is Amurta's botanical research station. The Aranara are shy, plantlike forest spirits who love memories and songs and can shape dreams. They live in hidden Vanarana and are usually seen only by children. They feared adult humans ("Nara") after the cataclysm took away people's dreams.
### LARGE +
The forest's ancient king, the tiger Viaghara, died defending it in the cataclysm; Rishboland Tigers are his successors. In 6.6 Nahida asked the Aranara to show themselves openly to humans, and the Pari Sorush flew a group of them to the desert.

## entry: The Desert and King Deshret

### STANDARD
The Great Red Sand was King Deshret's realm. Its areas are the Hypostyle Desert, the Lands of Upper and Lower Setekh (Valley of Dahri, Aaru Village, Khaj-Nisut) and the Desert of Hadramaveth. His giant pyramid, the Mausoleum of King Deshret, rises over the Hypostyle Desert, and the ruins of Khaj-Nisut lie in the Land of Lower Setekh. He ruled through his Seven Pillars and the Jinn, defeated many gods, and with Nabu Malikata's illicit celestial help began building Aaru, an artificial afterlife. After her death he succumbed to forbidden knowledge. The Dendro Archon helped contain the disaster and was shrunk to a child's size by the effort, and Deshret gave his life to end it. Ay-Khanoum, the City of Amphitheaters the three god-kings built together, lies in ruins, and later ruined cities like Gurabad and Tulaytullah record centuries of vassal kings, betrayals and wars.
### LARGE +
Aaru Village, guarded by Candace, began as the last bastion of Deshret's people. Desert dwellers long resented the rainforest and the Akademiya. Some believed the Dendro Archon had betrayed Deshret, until in Chapter III a transcript by Deshret's last priest Kasala, which Alhaitham deciphered, showed her as the desert's saviour. Gurabad fell after the Jinni Liloupar, wife of its founder Ormazd, spent three generations orchestrating its destruction in revenge for his tyranny.

## entry: The Sumeru Akademiya and the Six Darshans
@keys: Sumeru Akademiya, Akademiya, Darshan, Six Darshans, Grand Sage, Amurta, Haravatat, Kshahrewar, Rtawahist, Spantamad, Vahumana
### STANDARD
The Akademiya is thousands of years old and takes students from all over Teyvat. Its Six Darshans each have a specialty and a uniform colour: Amurta studies biology (green), Rtawahist illuminationism (blue), Spantamad elementalism (red), Haravatat semiotics (black), Vahumana aetiology (yellow) and Kshahrewar technology (white). Their Sages form the governing council, and one of them serves as Grand Sage. Former members include Lisa, Signora and Dottore. Students rise from Driyosh (travelling scholars) to Dastur (two accepted theses) and Herbad, and graduating after thirty is common.
### LARGE +
The Six Darshans began as the Six Great Schools, founded together with the Akademiya. Other Darshans came and went over the centuries. One, the Nagarjunites, broke away and survives in the Girdle of the Sands as the Order of Skeptics. The old Grand Sage Azar's console linked the Akademiya's knowledge capsules to the Akasha on Jnagarbha Days.

## entry: The Akasha
@keys: Akasha, Akasha System, Akasha Terminal, Canned Knowledge, Knowledge Capsule, Jnagarbha Day
### STANDARD
The Akasha was a mind-linking network, worn as a leaf-shaped Akasha Terminal over the left ear, that gave users knowledge according to their clearance. It ran on the Dendro Gnosis and was secretly powered by its users' harvested dreams. The sages used it to monitor people, predict their movements and spread propaganda. Nahida, the "First Akasha Terminal", shut it down after Chapter III and gave her Dendro Gnosis to Dottore.
### LARGE +
In 6.6 the scholars searched the Temple of Silence's records for a substitute for the Dendro Gnosis, built an energy amplifier and restarted the Akasha. Every connected mind together then overloaded the corrupted Irminsul. It is not part of daily life today. Long use of the Akasha dulled independent thinking.

### STANDARD
In the cataclysm the first Dendro Archon, Greater Lord Rukkhadevata, stayed behind to guard Irminsul and was contaminated with it by forbidden knowledge. Because she could not erase herself, she made Lesser Lord Kusanali from the purest branch of Irminsul to be her reincarnation. In Chapter III Act V her lingering consciousness asked to be removed, and Nahida purged her from Irminsul. The contamination cleared, the people of Sumeru could dream again and Eleazar vanished, but every memory of Rukkhadevata disappeared. The Traveler, who is not recorded in Irminsul, is the only one who remembers her.

After the erasure, history quietly rearranged itself around the gap. Her deeds remain, such as the rainforest and the Aranara, who once called her Queen Aranyani, but they are now credited to Nahida or explained in other ways. Nahida still feels a lingering "warmth" she cannot place. The Traveler keeps the secret, as Rukkhadevata wished.

## entry: The Temple of Silence and Hermanubis
@keys: Temple of Silence, Hermanubis, Ba Fragment, Thamud Oasis, Bamoun
### LARGE
The Temple of Silence keeps the desert knowledge of King Deshret's era. Hermanubis founded it at Tulaytullah. When a king of Gurabad besieged it, Hermanubis broke himself into Ba Fragments so his followers could fight back. The Temple later went to the rainforest, but about 400 years ago it left the corrupt sages and settled near Thamud Oasis, while the Akademiya kept a hollow office of the same name as a cover. Its leader Bamoun put the last two fragments into two children, his grandson Sethos and Cyno, before Cyrus took Cyno away. After the quest Oathkeeper, Cyno held both fragments. He made Sethos the Temple's leader, and the Temple has been working with the Akademiya again.

## entry: The Eremites
@keys: Eremites, Eremite, Corps of Thirty, Parchamdar, Rukh Shah, Ayn Al-Ahmar
### STANDARD
"Eremites" is a blanket name for the desert's many clans and mercenary bands, which are proud, quarrelsome and often hired out across Teyvat. They are Sumeru's only authorised mercenaries. Some draw elemental power from ancient desert spirits bound in their weapons. The Corps of Thirty, with green scarves instead of the usual red, polices Sumeru City. Other bands range from Dehya's Blazing Beasts to Deshret zealots such as Ayn Al-Ahmar.
### LARGE +
Desert people were long treated by the old sages as tools and kept from the Akasha's wisdom. Since Chapter III, Nahida has sent supplies to the desert, and Rahman's band works with Setaria to bring education to desert communities. In 6.6 a Corps of Thirty member, Qanafiya, spied for the Fatui until Nefer and Cyno caught him.
