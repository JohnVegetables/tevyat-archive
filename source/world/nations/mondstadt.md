## summary
### LITE
Mondstadt is the nation of freedom in northeastern Teyvat, the land of the Anemo Archon Barbatos, who walks among his people unrecognised as the bard Venti and deliberately does not rule. The Knights of Favonius govern in practice; the Church of Favonius tends worship. The capital, Mondstadt City, sits on an island in Cider Lake amid windmills and rolling green country. Mondstadters are easygoing lovers of taverns, song and festivals, proud of the gifts they credit to their god: dandelions, flight (gliding) and wine.
### STANDARD +
Barbatos stays out of government so that he never becomes a tyrant like the god before him. Neighbours in Liyue often mock Mondstadt as a city "without rule" whose god abandoned it.

## geography
### STANDARD
Green, windy country of lakes, oak forests and plains. Areas: Starfell Valley (the capital, Cider Lake), Galesong Hill (Windrise's great oak), Windwail Highland (Springvale, Dawn Winery, Wolvendom), Brightcrown Mountains (ruined Old Mondstadt, Stormterror's Lair), snowbound Dragonspine, and northern Windrest Peak (Dornman Port).
### LARGE +
Starfell Valley also holds Whispering Woods, Starfell Lake, the Thousand Winds Temple and Stormbearer Point; Galesong Hill holds Falcon Coast and Cape Oath. The weather in Windrest Peak is clearer and more varied than in the south. Dragonspine is the great exception to the mild climate: snow gathers there and never melts, and its cold can kill.

## government
### STANDARD
No king rules. The Knights of Favonius, founded by Vennessa about 1,000 years ago so aristocrats would never rule again, are police, garrison, lawmakers and civil service, led by Grand Master Varka (Knight of Boreas) with Jean Gunnhildr (Dandelion Knight) as Acting Grand Master and ten company Captains. The Church of Favonius is separate but managed by the Knights; its Seneschal is Seamus Pegg, Cardinal of Daybreak.
### LARGE +
Most trials are held by either the Knights or the Church alone; grave cases may require both, as when Albedo was tried in closed session with a deacon as assessor. Knighthood is earned by passing written and practical examinations, not conferred by royalty. Companies: 1st General Affairs, 2nd Garrison (Note), 3rd Vanguard (Skeiron), 4th Reconnaissance (Eula), 5th Ranged (Vice Captain Lohen; bows and muskets), 6th Logistics (Hertha), 7th Combined Arms (Frederica Gunnhildr), 8th Support (inventions), 9th Cavalry (Kaeya) and 10th Investigation (Albedo, Chief Alchemist). Also: Amber, the last Outrider; Lisa, Librarian; Klee, Spark Knight; Noelle, maid and aspiring knight; the Traveler, Honorary Knight.
### ULTRA +
Leadership titles follow old traditions: "Lion" (from Vennessa's title Dandelion or Lionfang Knight, now Jean's) and "Wolf" (from the first Knight of Boreas, now Varka's) mark knights expected to become Grand Master. Lohen had been designated the 5th Company's next captain, but Jean rescinded the promotion after he collaborated in dangerous experiments with Ursa's flesh. During Seamus Pegg's absence on the expedition, Cardinal Calvin carried his Church duties as acting Seneschal.

## religion
### STANDARD
Worship of Barbatos is light and festive rather than doctrinal, centred on the Favonius Cathedral behind the giant Anemo Archon statue. Hardly anyone knows the tipsy bard Venti is their god; his irreverent songs about Barbatos get called blasphemy. The deacon Dahlia serves as the Herald of Barbatos and is one of the few who know Venti's true identity. Deaconess Barbara Pegg is the city's singing "idol".
### LARGE +
Statues of the Seven dot the countryside, one beneath the great oak at Windrise. Older faiths left traces. The ancient Arcadians worshipped the Three Moons, above all the Frost Moon under the name Prusmene, and built the Thousand Winds Temple for Istaroth; after Barbatos' rise it was rebuilt to honour both gods until Istaroth was forgotten. For about five hundred years after Vennessa, Mondstadt also venerated the Four Winds and built temples to them, but that worship faded after the cataclysm.

## economy
### LARGE
Wine is Mondstadt's signature trade. The Dawn Winery, owned by the Ragnvindr family for about a thousand years and now by Diluc, is the chief winery and specialises in dandelion wine; part of each year's output is sold at home and the rest goes out along the trade route south of the city. Elzer has handled the winery's affairs since Crepus' death and chairs the Mondstadt Wine Guild; Adelinde is its head housemaid. Farming, orchards and hunting (Springvale) support the countryside, and the Adventurers' Guild posts work for freelance adventurers. In the north, Dornman Port is one of the continent's major trade hubs, known for cargo shipping and flower cultivation, while Millhaven's windrest-flower fields supply a fragrance trade.
### ULTRA +
Legend says the first wines were brewed in Old Mondstadt when a mischievous wind spirit in fox form fermented apples left with a careless guard. Dornman Port ships to Liyue Harbor, a route to Port Ormos in Sumeru has opened, and docking legally requires an entry permit. Taverns such as Angel's Share (Diluc's) and the Cat's Tail anchor city life; festival taverns sell limited wines such as Applebloom Cider.

## history
### STANDARD
About 2,600 years ago the people of Decarabian, God of Storms, rose against his wind-walled city (Old Mondstadt); a wind spirit among the rebels became Barbatos, cleared the snow, founded the new city and withdrew. The noble clans after him turned tyrannical until about 1,000 years ago the gladiator Vennessa, aided by Barbatos, overthrew them and founded the Knights. Five hundred years ago the dragon Durin ravaged Mondstadt until Barbatos and Dvalin slew it. In the Prologue the Traveler and Venti freed Dvalin ("Stormterror") from the Abyss Order, and the Fatui Harbinger Signora took Venti's Anemo Gnosis.
### LARGE +
Decarabian walled his city against the blizzards of Boreas, the northern wolf-god; the rebels' code word "Windblume" became a festival. The Lawrence Clan led the aristocracy's decline. Four years ago Crepus Ragnvindr died fending off an attack by "Ursa the Drake" on his caravan, engineered by the traitorous Inspector Eroch and the Fatui, and the cover-up led Diluc to quit the Knights. Half a year before the Traveler arrived, Varka led the Knights' elite north on an expedition that ended in Nod-Krai. At a recent Windblume Festival (5.6) Albedo's staged trial exposed creatures born of Durin's lingering life force, and Durin was reborn as a human; Varka came home after the Nod-Krai crisis (6.4).

## customs
### STANDARD
Festivals: spring's Windblume Festival (flowers and gifts for Barbatos and loved ones; a Windblume Star makes the formal offering), Ludi Harpastum (fifteen days of games, song and wine) and autumn's Weinlesefest (new wine uncasked for the Archon). Gliding is a national pastime.
### LARGE +
In Mondstadt a rose means a kept secret ("as wine bottles are corked, so too are my lips sealed"), not love. Nobody agrees what flower the windblume really is: Dandelionites say the dandelion (and a lovers' festival), Asterists the windwheel aster (and a remembrance of liberation from Decarabian); Barbatos lets everyone choose their own. During Ludi Harpastum the champion chooses a maiden who throws the harpastum, a ball wrapped in red cloth, into the crowd, and whoever catches it is blessed with a year of fortune. At Weinlesefest new wine stays sealed until Windcoming Day; if the Archon is pleased, a gentle breeze blesses the people. Springvale's young hunters looking for love catch foxes in Wolvendom during Windblume and release them crowned with flower wreaths. The Gunnhildr motto is "For Mondstadt, as always."
### ULTRA +
Many customs date from the early aristocracy, which once ruled for the people's benefit: nobles hunted alongside commoners and shared the spoils, and Ludi Harpastum, Windblume and Weinlesefest all took shape then. The Lawrence Clan later perverted Ludi Harpastum into an elite spectacle, which Vennessa's revolution restored. The wind glider was invented about 2,600 years ago by Leonard, a retired adventurer who had failed to climb Pilos Peak. Bards are everywhere, and Cape Oath is a favourite Windblume date spot.

## foreign_relations
### LARGE
Liyue: close trade partner; Liyue folk tease Mondstadt as godless and lawless, not knowing why its Archon refuses to rule. Snezhnaya: a Fatui diplomatic mission operates in the city and has repeatedly abused its position; the Fatui's role in Ursa the Drake's attack and Dottore's slaying of the beast left Mondstadt indebted to them, Signora seized the Anemo Gnosis, and a Lawrence, Schubert, once plotted with the Fatui to restore aristocratic rule. Nod-Krai: the Knights keep an outpost there, Favonius Keep, from which they fought the Wild Hunt. Sumeru: Mondstadters such as Lisa study at the Akademiya. Natlan: Vennessa was a Muratan from the southern plains. Inazuma's closed borders once stranded Mondstadt sailors.
### ULTRA +
Seamus Pegg is said to regret the diplomatic disadvantage Mondstadt suffered after Ursa's death. The Hexenzirkel, a coven of immensely powerful witches, has been allied with Barbatos since he answered their challenge with song instead of battle; through a pact Varka secured, the Knights and Church, the Anemo Archon and the Hexenzirkel can convene a Tripartite Conference in Mondstadt's hour of need.

## dangers
### LARGE
Hilichurl camps, slimes and wandering Ruin Machines; Treasure Hoarders; the Abyss Order, which manipulated Dvalin and has smuggled cargo through Dornman Port; Fatui schemes; the killing cold of Dragonspine; the wolf pack of Wolvendom, which even fearless hunters avoid; and old ruins such as Stormterror's Lair. Recent threats came from Durin's remains in Dragonspine and from Wild Hunt corruption that followed the ley lines into Wolvendom.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Constant breezes, dandelion seeds on the air, half-timbered houses and windmills, bards in the squares, knights on patrol, and wine-scented evenings at the tavern. Mondstadt City has a famously relaxed pace; Windrest Peak's port is busier, more cosmopolitan and full of sailors and merchants.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 Mondstadt is at peace. Grand Master Varka has returned from the long expedition to Nod-Krai but still travels to the Knights' outpost there, so Jean remains Acting Grand Master. Venti no longer holds his Gnosis.
### STANDARD +
Varka came home in 6.4; the outpost is Favonius Keep. Seamus Pegg is back too. Signora took the Gnosis for the Tsaritsa; as of 6.6 it is counted among the Gnoses in the Fatui's possession. Durin, a storybook dragon reborn in a human body, lives in Mondstadt. Windrest Peak in the north opened in 6.5. The Archon Quest has moved to Snezhnaya (Chapter VII, 7.0-7.1), and no new crisis in Mondstadt is shown there.
### ULTRA +
Frederica Gunnhildr, back from the expedition to defend Dornman Port, is on a secret mission known only to Varka and Jean. Diluc received a letter from Varka apologising for Inspector Eroch's cover-up and inviting him back, and declined.

## entry: Starfell Valley and Cider Lake

### STANDARD
Starfell Valley is Mondstadt's most varied area, from Stormbearer Point to the Whispering Woods, and the heart of the Knights' patrols. Mondstadt City (the "Crown of the North") stands on a large island in Cider Lake behind a stone wall, reached by a long bridge, with cobbled streets, windmills, the Knights of Favonius Headquarters and its library, and the Favonius Cathedral behind the Anemo Archon statue. Starsnatch Cliff is a quiet spot where Venti has met the Traveler. The ruined Thousand Winds Temple, built by ancient Arcadians for Istaroth, was later rebuilt for both Istaroth and Barbatos; a Ruin Guard still lurks there.
### LARGE +
The Mondstadt Library, which Ragnvindr converted from an aristocrats' bathhouse after Vennessa's rebellion, grew into the largest library in northern Teyvat; the Great Fire of Fall Equinox reduced it to one-sixth of its former size, yet it is still the largest in the north. The Temple of the Wolf, once a training ground, lies near the Thousand Winds Temple.

## entry: Galesong Hill and Windrise
@keys: Galesong Hill, Windrise, Falcon Coast, Cape Oath, Temple of the Falcon, Temple of the Lion
### STANDARD
In Galesong Hill, Windrise is a breezy plain where a giant oak is said to have grown on the spot where Vennessa ascended to Celestia; a Statue of the Seven stands beneath it, and travelers resting in its shade have heard a young bard singing tales of Barbatos. The Temple of the Falcon stands between the city and the oak, the Temple of the Lion on Falcon Coast, and Cape Oath is a favourite spot for Windblume couples.

## entry: Windwail Highland, Springvale and Wolvendom
@keys: Windwail Highland, Springvale, Wolvendom, Dawn Winery
### STANDARD
Windwail Highland is Mondstadt's rustic heart of clear springs, orchards and forests. Springvale is a hunting village built over a spring that, by legend, a "Spring Fairy" (an Oceanid from Fontaine) filled with life-saving water. The Dawn Winery, the Ragnvindr family estate and Mondstadt's chief winery, stands nearby. Wolvendom, a dark, silent forest, is home to Boreas (Andrius), the Wolf of the North, and his pack; locals avoid it, though the wolf-raised boy Razor lives there.

## entry: Brightcrown Mountains and Stormterror's Lair
@keys: Brightcrown Mountains, Stormterror's Lair, Old Mondstadt, Decarabian's Tower, Brightcrown Canyon
### STANDARD
The Brightcrown Mountains in the northwest hold the ruins of Old Mondstadt, Decarabian's walled city, and his broken tower. Dvalin slept there for centuries after the cataclysm, and during the Prologue it was "Stormterror's Lair", sealed by a barrier until Venti dispelled it. Ruins and wind currents make it hazardous.
### ULTRA +
Legend says the tower stood where a "wicked dragon" had once been cast down before Decarabian built his city. For about a year in the aristocratic era a damaged Field Tiller machine fired on anyone approaching the abandoned tower, an episode remembered as "The Tyrant's Final Fury".

## entry: Dragonspine
@keys: Dragonspine, Vindagnyr, Sal Vindagnyr, Frostbearing Tree, Wyrmrest Valley, Durin's Core
@version: 1.2
### STANDARD
Dragonspine is a snow-locked mountain whose snow never melts; its cold is deadly without warmth or the dragon-blood crystals called Scarlet Quartz. Long ago, as Vindagnyr, it was a green mountain where refugees founded Sal Vindagnyr; the Skyfrost Nail fell from the sky, burying the kingdom in snow, and a fragment turned its great tree into the Frostbearing Tree. Five hundred years ago the dragon Durin fell dead here; his blood seeped into the mountain and his heart still beat in Wyrmrest Valley (Durin's Core). Albedo keeps an alchemy camp on the slopes.
### LARGE +
Imunlaukr, an outlander living in Sal Vindagnyr, was away seeking a branch of Irminsul when its people perished; he is the namesake of the Imunlaukr Clan, one of Mondstadt's founding clans. About 1,000 years ago an aristocratic expedition organised by Landrich came to grief on the mountain amid a murderous plot by Eberhart. Since Albedo drained Durin's Core during Paralogism, the core has gone inert.

## entry: Windrest Peak and Dornman Port
@keys: Windrest Peak, Dornman Port, Millhaven, Old Sanatorium Site, Elysium, Thorn King, Knights of Favonius Ruins
@version: 6.5
### STANDARD
Windrest Peak, Mondstadt's northernmost area (opened in 6.5), centres on Dornman Port, a bustling port north of Cider Lake that is one of the continent's major trade hubs. In the aristocratic era it was the Gunnhildr Clan's fief and held Mondstadt's very first sanctuary to Barbatos, but the port has had no church since the late aristocratic era, when the Gunnhildrs, breaking with the tyrannical Imunlaukr Clan, threw a bishop sent from the capital out of the sanctuary's high tower and tore it down. Millhaven is an idyllic windmill town of windrest-flower fields and fragrance makers. The Knights keep a base at the port, with members of the Ranged, Logistics, Support and Combined Arms companies; Vice Captain Lohen has been stationed there.
### LARGE +
Five hundred years ago Dornman Port was a front line against the Abyss and nearly fell; the knights who died defending it lie in catacombs beneath the city (the Knights of Favonius Ruins under the old lighthouse). Long before, the Arcadian city-state of Elysium stood in these mountains under Gunnhildr control. The Old Sanatorium Site, a sanatorium demolished a few years ago, was built atop ruins left when the Temple of Space was lifted into the sky; Asmoday transformed the floating temple into "Segment-Domain Tau" (explored in the world quest series "A Long Day in the Mountains").
### ULTRA +
Mona was born in Dornman Port before settling in the capital. Eula's Reconnaissance Company patrols the long trade road between the port and the capital, especially during Weinlesefest, and once broke up an Abyss Order smuggling ring there.

## entry: Decarabian and the First Liberation
@keys: Decarabian, God of Storms, Lord of the Tower, Nameless Bard, Age of Kings, Kings of Ice and Frost
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Decarabian, God of Storms, ruled Old Mondstadt from his tower and walled it in gales against the blizzards of Boreas. He believed his rule loving and orderly, assigning every home and banning tunes that might signal dissent, but his people felt imprisoned. Around 2,600 years ago they rose under the code word Windblume, led by the Nameless Bard, a wind spirit, Amos, Gunnhildr and a red-haired warrior. Decarabian was slain; Amos and the Nameless Bard died too. The wind spirit ascended as the Anemo Archon, took the dead bard's form and name (Venti), blew away the snow, flattened Pilos Peak into what is now Musk Reef, and led the people to found the new city on Cider Lake before departing.
### LARGE +
Boreas judged himself unfit to be the Anemo Archon and withdrew north to Nod-Krai to sleep; when he later returned to Mondstadt, realising his blizzards could only take life, he let his body die and his power flow into the ley lines of the land; his spirit lingers. The red-haired warrior turned away from Barbatos and was forgotten, though his line became the Ragnvindrs. Before the rebellion the Gunnhildr Clan had left Decarabian's city; a weak wind spirit answered their prayers, and their faith strengthened him. Gunnhildr crowned the new Archon with laurels. The Nameless Bard's last song drew on a melody belonging to Istaroth, which is why he could not survive it.

## entry: The Aristocracy and Vennessa's Rebellion
@keys: Mondstadt Aristocracy, Vennessa, Lawrence Clan, Imunlaukr Clan, Barca Lawrence, Lord Lawrence
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
After Barbatos left, noble clans descended from the founders' heroes (chiefly Lawrence, Gunnhildr and Imunlaukr) led Mondstadt, at first for the people's good. From about 1,600 years ago the Lawrence Clan dominated and degenerated into tyranny: slavery, gladiatorial bloodsport, suppressed history, a knight order serving the nobles and a Church split in their favour. About 1,000 years ago Vennessa, a Muratan exiled from Natlan and enslaved as a gladiator, fought for her people's freedom; Barbatos, awakened by the people's cries, revealed himself at her final battle against Ursa the Drake. The nobles were exiled (the Gunnhildrs, who kept faith, stayed), Vennessa founded the Knights of Favonius and restored the Church, and later ascended to Celestia at Windrise.
### LARGE +
Official histories stop there, but Barbatos also forged a treaty "proving" the aristocrats had sold Mondstadt, troops included, to Morax; their soldiers turned on them, and scholars only discovered the forgery long afterwards. Ragnvindr, the disowned Lawrence Kreuzlied and Church faithful aided the revolt. Afterwards the Gunnhildrs pushed to abolish hereditary fiefs. Today Lawrence descendants still face hostility, which Eula, a captain of the Knights, endures; the Imunlaukr bloodline is lost.

## entry: The Cataclysm in Mondstadt
@keys: cataclysm, Durin, Arundolyn, Rostam, Crimson Witch of Flames, Lion of Light, Wolf Pup
@keys2: Mondstadt, Knights of Favonius, Dragonspine, Dvalin
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
Five hundred years ago Grand Master Arundolyn, the "Lion of Light", and his friend Rostam, the "Wolf Pup", led the Knights against the monsters pouring from Khaenri'ah; Rostam died and Arundolyn laid down his sword. Rhinedottir's dragon Durin then ravaged Mondstadt with no Lionfang Knight to stop it, until Barbatos called on Dvalin and they killed it together. Dvalin swallowed Durin's Abyss-tainted blood and retreated into a centuries-long sleep in Decarabian's tower. Rostam's lover Rosalyne-Kruzchka Lohefalter, returning from the Akademiya, turned herself into living flame for vengeance as the Crimson Witch of Flames; Pierro later recruited her into the Fatui as the Harbinger Signora. Afterwards worship of the Four Winds faded.

## entry: The Four Winds
@keys: Four Winds, Wolf of the North, Lion of the South, Dragon of the East, Falcon of the West, Knight of Boreas
### LARGE
The Four Winds are the guardians Barbatos entrusted with Mondstadt, formally established after Vennessa's revolution. Dragon of the East: Dvalin. Wolf of the North: Boreas (Andrius), and in Knights' tradition the Knight of Boreas, currently Varka (earlier the Wandering Knight, Ravenwood and Rostam). Lion of the South: passed to Grand Masters or Acting Grand Masters as the Dandelion (Lionfang) Knight, now Jean. Falcon of the West: Vennessa after her ascension, though the Knights are sometimes called the Falcon collectively. Venti's ballad credits the four winds, not himself, with Mondstadt's seasons. Their symbols mark the city's four sides.
### ULTRA +
Boreas came to Mondstadt from Nod-Krai, persuaded south by the first Knight of Boreas and splitting his soul so that half remained in Nod-Krai; few, even among the Knights, know this. Dvalin, once rejecting his role as "Stormterror", is honoured again since the Prologue.

## entry: The Stormterror Crisis
@keys: Stormterror, Teardrop Crystal, Holy Lyre der Himmel
@keys2: Mondstadt, Dvalin, Venti, Barbatos
@version: 1.0
### STANDARD
When the Traveler arrived, the dragon "Stormterror" was raging over Mondstadt. He was Dvalin, the Dragon of the East, poisoned by Durin's blood and manipulated by the Abyss Order into believing Mondstadt had abandoned him. Venti and the Traveler spirited the Holy Lyre der Himmel out of the Cathedral, purified Dvalin's tears, and confronted him in Stormterror's Lair with Jean and Diluc, freeing him from the Abyss's hold. Immediately afterwards, the Fatui Harbinger Signora ambushed Venti outside the Cathedral and ripped out his Anemo Gnosis.

## entry: Crepus Ragnvindr's Death and Fatui Leverage
@keys: Crepus, Eroch, Ursa the Drake
@keys2: Mondstadt, Knights of Favonius, Diluc, Fatui, Dottore
@spoiler: major
### ULTRA
Four years before the Traveler's arrival, Inspector Eroch of the Knights, jealous of Varka and colluding with Dottore, broke the seal that kept Ursa the Drake asleep. On Diluc's eighteenth birthday "Ursa" attacked the caravan of Crepus Ragnvindr, owner of the Dawn Winery, who drove it off with a Delusion and died from its backlash; later accounts identify the attacker as an imitation of Ursa bred from Eroch and Dottore's experiments. Eroch ordered a cover-up to protect the order's image, and Diluc resigned in fury. Dottore then slew the creature and took the credit, leaving Mondstadt in the Fatui's debt. Jean uncovered the truth and, with Varka's authority, had Eroch discharged; she rose to Master and rooted out his allies, and Eroch later took his own life when Varka cornered him.

## entry: The Varka Expedition and Homecoming
@keys: Varka's expedition, expeditionary force, Favonius Keep, Lupus Majoris
@keys2: Mondstadt, Knights of Favonius, Varka, Nod-Krai
@version: 6.4
### LARGE
Half a year before the Traveler arrived, Grand Master Varka took about eighty percent of the Knights' strength, including the cavalry, Seamus Pegg, Frederica Gunnhildr and Mika, on a long expedition against an Abyssal calamity he foresaw in the north. It ended in Nod-Krai, where the Knights fought the Wild Hunt from Favonius Keep until its master Rerir was defeated. Varka then sent Vice Captain Lohen home with ten letters. Wild Hunt corruption reached Wolvendom through the ley lines, while in Nod-Krai an abyssal copy of Roland, Rostam's long-lost disciple the "White Knight", corrupted the remains of the half of Andrius' soul that had stayed there. With Boreas and Razor guarding Wolvendom and Dvalin ferrying the Traveler north, Varka gathered the scattered wolf-spirit fragments, let Andrius return to the ley lines, survived being swallowed by the abyssal wolf, and woke at Windrise among his friends. Before he left, he had told Jean she would become Grand Master if he did not return.

## entry: The Windblume Trial and Durin's Rebirth

@keys2: Mondstadt, Albedo, Durin, Windblume
@version: 5.6
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
At a recent Windblume Festival, Albedo was tried behind closed doors for murder and was found guilty: a trap planned for half a year to expose beings spawned from the dead dragon Durin's life force, which devoured and impersonated Mondstadters (including a false Hertha). The retrial unmasked them and a full assault on the city followed, repelled by the Knights, Diluc, the Church and the Traveler while Venti linked defenders with wind currents. Albedo convened the Tripartite Conference (Knights and Church, the Anemo Archon, the Hexenzirkel), won reagents from Alice and Barbeloth, drew the vital energy from Durin's Core and, with Venti as Windblume Star singing, transmuted Mini Durin, a kindly dragon from the storybook world Simulanka, into a synthetic human body that took the fallen dragon's place.

## entry: Arcadia and Elysium
@keys: Arcadia, Great Arcadia, Prusmene, Knight of the Golden Bough
@spoiler: minor
### ULTRA
Before Mondstadt, the oak forests belonged to Arcadia, part of Teyvat's ancient unified civilisation. Its city-state of Elysium stood in Windrest Peak under the Gunnhildr Clan, it built the Temple of Space (for Asmoday) and the Thousand Winds Temple (for Istaroth), and it worshipped the Three Moons, especially the Frost Moon as Prusmene. With the war of funerary flame an unknown calamity buried it in endless winter. Gunnhildr, the Knight of the Golden Bough, searched the northern wastes for a branch of Irminsul to restore it, found the bough withered, and instead led the survivors to shelter under Decarabian.
