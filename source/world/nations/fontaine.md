## summary
### LITE
Fontaine is the nation of Hydro and justice, a "terrestrial sea" on a raised plateau at the centre of Teyvat, said to be the source of all the continent's waters. Its capital, the Court of Fontaine, is a Belle Epoque-style city of fountains, aquabuses, clockwork meka and theatres, and Fontainians pride themselves on art, elegance and technology. Trials at the Opera Epiclese are public spectacle. Since 4.2 Fontaine has had no Archon: the Iudex Neuvillette, secretly the Hydro Dragon, runs its affairs.
### STANDARD +
The nation once worshipped the Hydro Archon (Egeria, then Focalors, whom the public knew as Furina). For five hundred years it lived under a prophecy that its people would dissolve into the sea; that crisis was resolved in the Archon Quest "Masquerade of the Guilty". Other peoples live here too, notably the Melusines, and the Oceanids once did.

## geography
### STANDARD
The whole continental plate is elevated well above neighbouring nations and edged by a waterfall thousands of metres high. Areas: the Court of Fontaine Region (the capital, the island of Erinnyes with the Opera Epiclese), the Belleau and Beryl Regions (the mountain-sized remains of Elynas, with Merusea Village beneath), the Liffey Region (Fortress of Meropide offshore), the Fontaine Research Institute Region, the Morte Region and Erinnyes Forest, and in the south the Nostoi Region (Petrichor) and the Sea of Bygone Eras.
### LARGE +
Most of Fontaine's waters can be dived and explored underwater; the Nostoi Region is the exception because it lies off the raised plate. The Sea of Bygone Eras, south of the "Great Terrestrial Lake", hides sunken Remurian ruins. Beneath everything lies the Primordial Sea, whose largest opening is sealed under the Fortress of Meropide; smaller pools exist beneath the Tower of Ipsissimus and elsewhere. Release order: Court, Belleau and Beryl (4.0); Liffey, the Research Institute area and Meropide (4.1); Morte and Erinnyes Forest (4.2); Nostoi and the Sea of Bygone Eras (4.6).

## government
### STANDARD
The Palais Mermonia, at the highest point of the capital, is the seat of all administration and effectively the highest authority. It houses the Maisons: Ordalie (the judiciary, headed by the Chief Justice or Iudex, Neuvillette), Gestion (documents and administration), Cardinalice (financial approvals) and Gardiennage (law enforcement, with the Gardes and Gardemeks). Even when Furina reigned, the real work was done by humans and Melusines in the Palais. Since her abdication Neuvillette handles all affairs that once belonged to the Archon's title.
### LARGE +
The Maison Gardiennage has two special branches: the Special Security and Surveillance Patrol (captain Chevreuse), which apprehends violent criminals with muskets and few rules on uniform, and the Marechaussee Phantom, an investigative force led by Neuvillette and staffed mostly by Melusines for their sharp senses. The Gardes are captained by Grosrochard. Bureaucracy is famously slow: replacing one typewriter keycap can need several rounds of approvals. Fontaine's law code holds many odd statutes; Neuvillette lists bans on releasing flying objects in the first three days of a month and on naming pets after Furina, and adds that the rule requiring Melusines to be called "she" rather than "it" was his own.
### ULTRA +
Other judges from the Palais can be assigned to preside over trials; Neuvillette does not hear every case. Champion Duelists, chosen from Fontaine's best fighters, let an accused person defend their honour by duel instead of trial; winning means acquittal, losing means standing trial, and a duel is fatal only if the accused will not yield. Clorinde is the strongest current Champion Duelist. The Fortress of Meropide is legally outside the Court's jurisdiction.

## religion
### STANDARD
Fontaine worshipped the Hydro Archon, God of Justice: first Egeria, the "Mistress of Many Waters", and then Focalors. For five centuries the public face of the god was Furina, flamboyant and theatrical, who sat in a box at trials. There is a Statue of the Seven inside the Court of Fontaine itself; Snezhnograd is the only other main city with one. Since 4.2 there is no Hydro Archon and the throne cannot be refilled.
### LARGE +
Faith and justice were bound together: the Oratrice turned the people's belief in justice into energy (Indemnitium). The Fontinalia Festival commemorates the legendary Lochknights' search for the Oceanids and the return of Egeria. Melusines regard Neuvillette as their ideal father, and the public has long noticed his fondness for them, but he is not worshipped.

## economy
### LARGE
Fontaine runs on unusual energy. For centuries the Court and Erinnyes were powered by Indemnitium from the Oratrice, while machines run on the Pneuma-Ousia Arkhe system devised by Alain Guillotin, founder of the Fontaine Research Institute. The Research Institute is a pure research body (unlike Sumeru's Akademiya it neither governs nor teaches) and produces most mechanical inventions, including the soda Fonta. The Fortress of Meropide is the largest clockwork meka factory. The aquabus network (Clementine, Navia and the disused Callas lines) was funded by Callas of the Spina di Rosula. The Steambird is read across Teyvat.
### ULTRA +
After 4.2 the Opera stopped producing Indemnitium; Neuvillette, now in full command of Pneumousia, substitutes for it, which is one reason he cannot easily leave the nation. The Research Institute is notorious at home for long-winded technicians and money trouble, and its old campus was levelled by Edwin Eastinghouse's Arkhium experiment. Fontaine also exports culture: the Kamera comes from here, Xinyan says rock 'n' roll originated here, and Xingqiu's novel "A Legend of Sword" is very popular. Fontaine engineers including Xavier helped design Inazuma's Mikage Furnace.

## military
### LARGE
Fontaine has no standing army in the present story; order is kept by the Gardes, Gardemeks, the Special Patrol and the Marechaussee Phantom. Historically it had a navy: about five hundred years ago the Fontaine Armada under former admiral Basil Elton chased the great beast Elynas for 35 days and rammed it, dying to the last crew member while mortally wounding it. The old Marechaussee Hunters, monster hunters founded by Cassiodor after Remuria's fall, survive only as family traditions; Clorinde is a successor.

## history
### STANDARD
After an ancient flood drowned the first civilisation, the god Remus founded Remuria, a musical empire ruling the waters, which sank about 2,000 years ago. Egeria then became Hydro Archon, united the surviving tribes and gave Fontaine its laws. By turning Oceanids into humans with Primordial Seawater she committed a "sin" in Celestia's eyes, and a prophecy foretold that Fontaine's people would dissolve back into the sea. Egeria died in the cataclysm 500 years ago; her successor Focalors spent five centuries on a secret plan, fulfilled in 4.2.
### LARGE +
About 400 years ago Neuvillette became Iudex, invited by an anonymous letter from Focalors, and the Melusines, born from the remains of Elynas, entered Fontainian society. In the Archon Quest (4.0-4.2), serial disappearances, Childe's wrongful conviction, a breach at Meropide's sluice gate and a Primordial Sea surge at Poisson that killed 12 people led to Furina's trial, where the truth emerged (see the prophecy entry).

## customs
### STANDARD
Trials are theatre: citizens buy tickets to watch prosecution, defence and twists at the Opera Epiclese, which also hosts magic shows and plays. Fontainians love newspapers, gossip, fashion, film and fine food. The Fontinalia Festival, honouring the Lochknights, once had people in costume raise golden cups and ask door to door for pure water; Furina turned it into a carnival where children shout "Trial or treat!" for candy.
### LARGE +
Since 4.3 Fontinalia also hosts the Fontinalia Film Festival, whose top prize is still the "Furina Award" even after her abdication. Lakelight Lilies are traditionally given to people setting out on long journeys, from a legend that Egeria gave one to the last Lochknight. The Lochknights' tales are retold in plays, poems and films. The Steambird awards the La Verite Prize, Fontaine's most prestigious media honour, at the Opera Epiclese. The demonym is Fontainian.
### ULTRA +
The Steambird's "Tales of Humanity" column is where locals post commissions. The Court of Fontaine is nicknamed the "City of the Arts". Melusines value scrap mechanical parts as currency in Merusea Village. Fontaine's legal system is notoriously complicated, as even Yanfei remarks.

## foreign_relations
### LARGE
Snezhnaya: Arlecchino's Fatui stayed in Fontaine during the crisis, nominally to help, and joined the Poisson rescue; Neuvillette later gave Arlecchino the Hydro Gnosis as a "diplomatic gift", and she negotiated a gradual withdrawal of her operatives. Sumeru: Marcotte Station is a main entry point for travellers from Sumeru; Remus came from there, and Egeria's body became the Amrita in Sumeru's desert. Natlan: the Traveler left for Natlan from Romaritime Harbor. Inazuma: designer Chiori runs a boutique in the Court.
### ULTRA +
During limited-time Lantern Rite events, Neuvillette has spent his time off in Liyue. In 7.1 Fontainians are seen abroad in Snezhnaya: Charlotte reports from Snezhnograd and Lyney and Lynette visit the Korolevskiy Theater. Mona writes an astrology column for The Steambird.

## dangers
### LARGE
Clockwork meka gone wild, Fatui squads, Treasure Hoarders, hilichurls, Breacher Primuses and other monsters around Elynas, Tainted Hydro Phantasms, and the Fontemer wildlife of the deep. The Fortress of Meropide's searchlights pull swimmers who wander too close into the prison. Diluted Primordial Seawater has been sold as the drug Sinthe. The Research Institute ruins still float on unstable Arkhium. Before 4.2, contact with concentrated Primordial Seawater dissolved Fontainians outright.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Cobbled avenues, fountains everywhere, aquabuses gliding on elevated water rails, newsboys and Kamera flashes, fashionable crowds outside boutiques and cafes, and a theatre audience's gasp at a courtroom twist. The countryside offers misty forests, mint-scented highlands of Beryl, Melusines bustling between village and city, and always the sound of water. Poisson and Fleuve Cendre show the poorer side; Meropide is industrial, metal-walled and self-contained.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 Fontaine is at peace and has no Archon. In 4.2 the real Hydro Archon, Focalors, executed herself through the Oratrice, destroying the Hydro Archon's throne; Neuvillette regained the Hydro Dragon's authority and absolved Fontainians, who no longer dissolve in Primordial Seawater. Furina abdicated and now lives as an ordinary actress and director. Neuvillette runs the nation and judges trials himself.
### STANDARD +
The Oratrice no longer works. The Hydro Gnosis went to Arlecchino, and the Fatui hold it. Under her later deal with Neuvillette, the Fatui scale back operations while former House of the Hearth members are left alone. In 6.7 the Harbinger Sandrone, revived at the Research Institute, briefly ran the Wingalet before returning to Snezhnaya; the Archon Quest is now in Snezhnaya (Chapter VII, 7.0-7.1).
### ULTRA +
Navia has decided to rebuild the Callas aquabus line. The latest Furina Award went to "Great Detective Hurlock" (starring Charlotte), beating Musketeer Pictures' "A Homecoming", directed by Furina. People dissolved before 4.2, such as the Poisson victims, were not restored.

The Court of Fontaine was the court of the God of Justice and is now under Chief Justice Neuvillette. ## entry: The Opera Epiclese and the Oratrice
@keys: Opera Epiclese, Oratrice, Oratrice Mecanique d'Analyse Cardinale, Indemnitium, Champion Duelist
### STANDARD
Fontaine's main courthouse is an opera house. Trials there are public performances with tickets, and the Oratrice Mecanique d'Analyse Cardinale, a giant mechanical scale behind the judge's seat, used to deliver the final verdict, which the Iudex had to honour. It also turned the audience's belief in justice into Indemnitium, powering lamps and transport in the Court and Erinnyes.
### LARGE +
The Oratrice was built by Focalors, who hid her divinity inside it; only a fraction of the Indemnitium ever reached the city, the rest being stored to execute a god. Since 4.2 it is inert and Neuvillette passes verdicts himself. Famous trials include Lyney's (4.0), Childe's wrongful conviction for the disappearances, and Furina's own.

## entry: Fortress of Meropide
@keys: Fortress of Meropide, Meropide, Credit Coupons, forbidden zone
@version: 4.1
### STANDARD
An autonomous undersea stronghold off the Liffey Region that serves as Fontaine's de facto prison and largest meka factory. Convicts are technically exiles; the Court only lends some Gardes. Inmates work for Credit Coupons, which replace Mora inside. Its Administrator, "the Duke", is Wriothesley, a former inmate who took the post after the previous administrator fled the duel Wriothesley had challenged him to; Sigewinne runs the infirmary.
### LARGE +
It was built by repentant exiles whom Egeria forgave on condition they guard her secret: an opening to the Primordial Sea, sealed behind a sluice gate in the "forbidden zone". The Coupon system began as an economic experiment by the Harbinger Pantalone with a past administrator. About 400 years ago the convicted officer Vautrin founded the Mutual Aid Society here. During the 4.1 crisis Wriothesley secretly had a great ship, the Wingalet, built as a failsafe against the prophecy; it later helped rescue people during the flood.

Beryl, across the water from the Court, is an ancient ruin-studded range where Egeria's followers once held picnics. ## entry: Elynas and the Melusines
@keys: Melusine, Melusines, Merusea Village, Elynas
### STANDARD
Elynas was a colossal beast that brought pollution to Fontaine during the cataclysm and was mortally wounded by the Fontaine Armada; its skeleton is now a mountain ridge in the Beryl Region, with Merusea Village hidden underwater inside it. The Melusines, a small amphibious all-female species, were born from its wounds about 400 years ago. Neuvillette brought them into society, where many work in the Marechaussee Phantom; they see him as their ideal father.
### LARGE +
Melusines perceive colours and people differently from humans, which makes their art and cooking baffling to humans, and they are long-lived; Sigewinne and Kiara are among the first born. Early on, many humans feared them: about 400 years ago the Melusine Carole, framed by hardliners, took her own life to calm a riot, and her partner Vautrin avenged her and was sent to Meropide. The Maison Gardiennage's hat emblem was later redesigned to include Melusines in her memory.

## entry: Remuria and the God King
@keys: Remuria, Remus, God King, Capitolium, Harmost, Golden Troupe, Meropis
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Before Egeria, the god Remus, who came from Sumeru after Gurabad's fall, founded Remuria above Fontaine's waters, teaching farming, building and above all music. His capital was Capitolium and his emblem the golden bee. Prophesied to perish utterly and return to the Primordial Sea, Remus purged Lochfolk, vishaps and "barbarian" tribes, shared power with four Harmosts and poured his people's souls into golem bodies of stone and golden Ichor. Remuria sank anyway; its ruins lie in the Sea of Bygone Eras.
### LARGE +
Remus' Grand Symphony, Phobos, was made from the life of Sybilla, the guardian of the Golden Bees; it fed the people's desire for bloodshed. Remus secretly conspired with the dragon Scylla to destroy it, Harmost Boethius saw this as betrayal and sealed Scylla, and the empire drowned in the resulting tsunami. Resistance came from the northern tribes and the Lochknights, led by the singer Erinnyes. Cassiodor later founded the Marechaussee Hunters to hunt Remuria's leftover monsters.

## entry: Egeria, the Oceanids and the Original Sin
@keys: Egeria, Mistress of Many Waters, Oceanid, Oceanids, original sin
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Egeria was created as the new heart of the Primordial Sea after the Heavenly Principles took the Hydro Dragon's authority. Oceanids, said to spring from her tears, spread across Teyvat; when some longed to live on land, she poured Primordial Seawater into their veins, making them human. Celestia judged this unauthorised creation of life a sin, imprisoned her for a time and condemned her people. After Remuria's fall she ruled as Hydro Archon, then died at Tunigi Hollow in the cataclysm, her body becoming the Amrita.
### LARGE +
Because their blood came from the Primordial Sea, Fontainians would dissolve on touching concentrated Primordial Seawater. After Egeria's death the Oceanids largely refused to accept Focalors and left Fontaine. Egeria chose Focalors, herself a humanised Oceanid, as successor, hoping she would find a way to deceive Celestia.

### STANDARD
The prophecy said every Fontainian is born guilty, the waters will rise, the people will dissolve, and only the Hydro Archon will remain, weeping on her throne. Focalors split herself: her divinity hid in the Oratrice while her humanity, Furina, played the Archon publicly for about 500 years without being told the full plan. He and the Traveler then subdued the All-Devouring Narwhal in the Primordial Sea, which Skirk later took away; its rampage flooded Fontaine as foretold, but no one dissolved and the waters receded. Skirk warned Neuvillette that the Gnosis was dangerous, and he gave it to Arlecchino.
