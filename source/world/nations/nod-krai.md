## summary
### LITE
Nod-Krai is an autonomous region on the southernmost edge of Snezhnaya, in Teyvat's northwest: cold islands, desolate tundra, snowy groves and long, dark nights. It has no Archon and no true government. People from all over Teyvat gather in its scrap-metal port, Nasha Town, which gives the land a reputation as a lawless "Elysium". The Frostmoon Scions, its indigenous people, worship the Moon Goddess Kuutar, the former Fatui Harbinger Columbina. The land is soaked in kuuvahki, the power of the moons, and since the Cataclysm it has been plagued by the Abyssal Wild Hunt.
### STANDARD +
The main powers are the Voynich Guild merchants, the Lightkeepers militia, the Frostmoon Scions and Nefer's Curatorium of Secrets. Nod-Krai is the setting of the Archon Quest Song of the Welkin Moon (6.0-6.3), and its great festival is Moon-Prayer Night.

## geography
### STANDARD
Nod-Krai is a chain of islands surrounded by sea. Most people live in the Southern Isles: Lempo Isle (Nasha Town), Hiisi Island (the Scions' forest home) and Paha Isle (the Fatui's Design Bureau and the Final Night Cemetery). To the north lies the large island of Pohjola: Voidsea Outlook with the Lightkeepers' city Piramida, Wavechaser Plain, Ashveil Peak in the far north, and Dunanna Pit near Fontaine.
### LARGE +
Kuuvahki shapes the ecology. Plants store it and glow or turn toward people who come near. Animals grow stronger or weaker as it rises and falls. Sprite-like kuuhenki, the Moon Goddess's familiars, drift everywhere; a white one is said to bring luck and lead travellers to treasure. Since the end of Song of the Welkin Moon, the Frost Moon itself has been reachable as part of Nod-Krai.
### ULTRA +
In the north, around Kipumaki Cliff, unpolarised kuuvahki is locked in a struggle with Abyssal blight. Ancient Moon remnants, fragments of the fallen moons, dot the land. The heaviest fragment crashed onto Paha Isle, which had once been known only for its sunset-coloured salt marshes and Tassel Jellies.

## government
### STANDARD
Nobody rules Nod-Krai. Shortly after the Cataclysm, about 500 years ago, the Tsaritsa (Anastasya) approved Solovei's request to make it an autonomous region, with the Lightkeepers given jurisdiction to defend it. In practice the Voynich Guild, a merchants' guild in Nasha Town, is the closest thing to a regulator.
### LARGE +
The Guild handles trade deals, imports and exports, business certificates and disputes, and funds some technology. Nasha Town has no official governing body. It is pulled between the Voynich Guild, the Curatorium of Secrets, the Speranza restaurant and the Fatui, which makes it a paradise of freedom and a lawless jungle at the same time. Nefer, who heads the Curatorium, is herself a Guild member. After Dottore's defeat (6.3), the region's factions jointly founded a new League; the Guild employee Tarno addressed the crowd in Nasha Town's plaza, and Flins urged Illuga to represent the Lightkeepers.

## religion
### STANDARD
The Frostmoon Scions worship the Frost Moon. They honour the late goddess Canon as Huurrekuutar and her successor Kuutar (Columbina), who was born about 500 years ago, and they carved Statues of the New Moon in her image all over Nod-Krai. Most townsfolk care little for the religion.
### LARGE +
The Scions call kuuvahki "the blessing of the Moon Goddess" and think it blasphemous to use it for personal gain. The Statues of the New Moon serve travellers the way Statues of the Seven do elsewhere, but no one resonates with an element at them. In Song of the Welkin Moon, Moonchanter Lauma told her people that the sky and the moon they had revered were false. Some left the faith, and a few of them joined the Lightkeepers. After Dottore's defeat, Columbina came to live among the Scions at Lauma's invitation and asks to be treated as herself rather than as a goddess.

## economy
### STANDARD
Nasha Town is the main port and trading centre. Nod-Krai has few resources, so much of the town is built from scrap metal, with cargo rails overhead and kuuvahki-powered machines on every corner. Adventuring, salvage and trade drive the economy.
### LARGE +
The Adventurers' Guild has a branch here (Katheryne, with branch master Sarin), and so does the Northland Bank. Visitors are warned that anyone suspected of carrying a lot of Mora becomes a target. Kuuvahki technology is the region's speciality: Aino builds devices from scavenged scrap and ancient Secret Source parts, and her Boom and Blast Kuuvahki Cannon guards Nasha Town.
### ULTRA +
The Fatui also exploit kuuvahki with landcruisers and other machines. Researchers from Snezhnograd coined the word "kuuvahki", and the Fatui's push to put it to practical use sharply raised tensions with the Scions.

## military
### STANDARD
The Lightkeepers, a volunteer militia founded about 500 years ago by Solovei, the first Torchforger, hold back the Wild Hunt. They are based in Piramida and led by a Starshyna, currently Nikita, and they are badly understrength.
### LARGE +
Below the Starshyna are Sergeant Majors, Squad Leaders and rank-and-file fighters called Ratniki. Every Ratnik carries an Oath Lantern, and the order has set Tideseal Stones across the land against the Hunt. Field members complain that headquarters ignores their reports. Notable Lightkeepers include Flins, the lone Ratnik who guards the Final Night Cemetery, and Illuga, who leads the "Nightmare Orioles" squad watching Kipumaki Cliff.
### ULTRA +
The Fatui keep their own troops in the region, including Oprichniki and Ground Effect Landcruisers. The Knights of Favonius expedition under Varka camps at Favonius Keep on Wavechaser Plain.

## culture
### ULTRA
Nod-Kraians are a mix of Frostmoon Scions, settlers, merchants, exiles, wanted criminals and adventurers; some wear mechanical prosthetics, as the Lightkeeper Sousi did. Nefer calls Nasha Town an anarchist's paradise. Townsfolk often take Paimon for a fae. Few Snowland Fae live this far south; Flins, a Lantern Fae, and Linnea are two of the few who tolerate the warmer climate. The Scions appear to avoid meat, living on fruit, vegetables and spring water, and they hand out food in town. The Lightkeepers hold banquets in Piramida's great mead hall after hard victories, and the first pour always goes to the dead.

## festivals
### LARGE
Moon-Prayer Night (Kuunrukous) falls on the night the moon comes closest to Teyvat and kuuvahki peaks. Its date is never known in advance: people make a rough estimate, then watch for changes in the Verdant Crests and Moon-Prayer Blossoms, and the waiting is part of the fun. The Scions began it by wearing Moon-Prayer Blossoms, singing and dancing for the Moon Goddess's birth and giving food and handicrafts. Today it is mostly a commercial street festival, and the Scions hang Moonveils on Nasha Town's buildings so the night sky looks misty through them.

## history
### STANDARD
In the Natlantean era the land was called Nephilheim. Nibelung raised the Three Moons here, and Nod-Krai is said to be made from material left over from their making. After a Celestial Nail destroyed the northern realm of Hyperborea, the Frost Moon goddess Canon led its survivors to Hiisi Island before she died; their descendants are the Frostmoon Scions. Nod-Krai was the first land struck by the Cataclysm after Khaenri'ah fell. The Wild Hunt arose from Rerir, one of the Five Sinners, and the Khaenri'ahn exile Rotwang tried to open the false sky to the Abyss until the Scion leader Aila and the Snezhnayan noble Solovei stopped him.
### LARGE +
In recent years the Fatui arrived under Dottore and Sandrone. They pulled down two Ancient Moon remnants, built their Design Bureau over a fallen piece of the Eternal Moon, caged or defaced Statues of the New Moon, and hunted Columbina under the "Palestar Edict" after she left the Harbingers. Varka's Knights of Favonius expedition also reached Nod-Krai. In Song of the Welkin Moon (6.0-6.3), the Traveler and allies defeated Rerir, gave Columbina a true name and destroyed Dottore after he seized the moons' power, and Arlecchino, Varka and Nicole pulled the real Frost Moon back into Teyvat.

## dangers
### STANDARD
The Wild Hunt is a host of ghouls made from the dead, tainted by Abyssal energy. It is far stronger here than anywhere else. A beaten-down ghoul becomes "grief-stricken" and must be worn down again before it truly falls. The Hunt rarely goes to Hiisi Island because the moon's power deters it. Kipumaki Cliff in the far north is the worst hotspot.
### LARGE +
Elsewhere there are Fatui patrols, wild beasts, treasure-seekers and a harsh climate. About ten years ago a great battle against the Wild Hunt took Starshyna Sigurd (his corrupted remains still roam as a ghoul) and destroyed Illuga's family and village. Three years ago another surge at Kipumaki Cliff wiped out the Nightmare Orioles squad; only Illuga and the badly injured Ivar survived.
### ULTRA +
People who are too sensitive to kuuvahki suffer nosebleeds, dizziness, headaches, nausea or itching, and a chronic congenital form worsens without treatment. Snezhnayan doctors developed a medicine for it, though some Scions refuse their help, and the silver blood of Scions with strong Hyperborean lineage, such as Lauma, can counteract the symptoms. Nicole warns that the moon crisis has made Teyvat easier for the Abyss to detect.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Nasha Town is clanking, crowded and cheerful: overhead rails and scrap, a busy harbour full of sailors and Fatui soldiers, the Flagship tavern (run by Demyan) with its secret "Banquets", and machines running on moonlight. Hiisi Island is the opposite: quiet ancient forest, silver-blooded priestesses who talk with animals, and kuuhenki drifting among blue flowers. In the north, Lightkeeper lanterns burn against fog and Abyssal blight.

## travel
### ULTRA
Ships carry travellers between Nasha Town and other nations; the Traveler arrived by boat from Natlan, and after the crisis Arlecchino and the Wanderer sailed home to Fontaine and Sumeru. Moonlanes, kuuvahki paths much like Natlan's Spiritways, let a traveller briefly become a kuuhenki and race across the land. During Dottore's crisis the islands were evacuated in two directions: Lauma led people from the Southern Isles toward Natlan, and the Fatui sent those north of the Design Bureau to Snezhnaya.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 Nod-Krai is at peace again and rebuilding. Dottore was destroyed in the Song of the Welkin Moon crisis. The real Frost Moon has been pulled back inside Teyvat's sky, and Columbina lives openly among the Frostmoon Scions on Hiisi Island. The Wild Hunt has lost its leaders but still roams. The Traveler has moved on to Snezhnaya.
### STANDARD +
The Tsaritsa revoked the Palestar Edict against Columbina, Sandrone apologised to the Scions on the Fatui's behalf, and the factions founded a joint League. Rerir is outside Teyvat in the moon's reflection, and Rotwang is gone for good. At the start of Chapter VII (7.0), Columbina threw the Traveler a farewell party at the Flagship before they left for Snezhnaya.
### ULTRA +
Columbina gave the Eternal Moon Marrow to Lauma and the Iridescent Moon Marrow to Arlecchino, and returned part of the Frost Moon's power to the land as kuuvahki. Dottore's last backup Segment died when Nahida burned the corrupted Irminsul in Sumeru (6.6). The Knights' expedition went home to Mondstadt in waves, though Varka still travels back to Favonius Keep. Sandrone's core was taken to Fontaine, where she was later revived. Columbina hopes to attend the next Moon-Prayer Night openly as herself.

## entry: The Southern Isles
@keys: Southern Isles, Lempo Isles, Lempo Isle, Hiisi Island, Paha Isle, Frostmoon Enclave, Silvermoon Hall
@version: 6.0
### STANDARD
The Southern Isles are the most populated part of Nod-Krai apart from Piramida. Lempo Isle holds Nasha Town, the Clink-Clank Krumkake Craftshop (Aino's home and workshop) and Starsand Shoal. Hiisi Island is the Frostmoon Scions' home: largely untouched forest with the Frostmoon Enclave, the Light-Bathed Platform with a giant statue of Kuutar, and the only Statue of the New Moon the Fatui never defaced. Its hidden grotto, Silvermoon Hall, is where Columbina hid under Lauma's protection. Paha Isle is the Fatui staging ground, home to the Kuuvahki Experimental Design Bureau, and also holds the Final Night Cemetery, a graveyard isle of fallen Lightkeepers.
### LARGE +
The Hyperborean refugees first settled at a pillar on Hiisi Island raised long ago by Xiuhcoatl, now called the Sanctum of the Oathbearer. A trial-guarded sanctum there kept the Eternal Moon Marrow until it was stolen early in Song of the Welkin Moon.

## entry: Pohjola and the Far North
@keys: Pohjola, Voidsea Outlook, Wavechaser Plain, Ashveil Peak, Dunanna Pit, Piramida, Kipumaki Cliff, Pillar of Embla, Amsvartnir
@version: 6.3
### STANDARD
Pohjola is the great island north of the Southern Isles. It opened in 6.3, except for Dunanna Pit, which opened in 6.7. Voidsea Outlook at its southern end holds Piramida, the Lightkeepers' city, built on a mountain around a giant Hyperborean lighthouse over the ruins of Prokhor Trubetskoy's castle, as well as Dreadshade Mire. Wavechaser Plain holds the Pillar of Embla, Lake Amsvartnir and the Knights' Favonius Keep. Ashveil Peak, in the far north, holds Kipumaki Cliff and the Lightkeepers' Cliffwatch Camp. Dunanna Pit lies near Fontaine, east of Pohjola.
### LARGE +
According to Hymns of the Far North, the Tsaritsa (Anastasya) and Saarelainen opened a passage to the Shadow Realm at Pohjola. A towering statue of Solovei stands in Piramida, and the Arrow of Terpikeraunas is built into its great mead hall.

## entry: The Frost Moon as Part of Nod-Krai
@keys: Lunar Highlands, Moontide Sea, Dark Side of the Moon, Moon Gazing
@keys2: Frost Moon, Nod-Krai
@version: 6.7
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
Since 6.7 (Luna VIII) the Frost Moon can be explored as a subregion of Nod-Krai, reached through the Dunanna Forward Station via the Moon Gazing quests. Its areas are the Lunar Highlands, the Moontide Sea and the Dark Side of the Moon. Gravity is low there. Ancient dragon researchers led by Ibni-Belum built facilities on it long ago (Project Moongazer). Nibelung turned its far side into a huge cannon. Despite more than 6,000 years beyond the false sky, it holds no trace of the Abyss. The Heavenly Principles had moved the Traveler's spaceship there from Nod-Krai.

## entry: Nephilheim, Hyperborea and the Three Moons
@keys: Nephilheim, Huurrekuutar, Sanctum of the Oathbearer
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Nod-Krai was called Nephilheim in Natlantean times. Nibelung created the Three Moons here: the Eternal, Frost and Iridescent Moons, each with a goddess (Aria, Canon and Sonnet). In the war against the Heavenly Principles, the Eternal and Iridescent Moons were shattered and the Frost Moon was flung beyond the false sky. When Hyperborea in the far north was destroyed by a Celestial Nail, Canon, out of pity, led its survivors to Nod-Krai before she died. They raised a tower and preserved Hyperborean devices in the hope that a reborn Frost Moon would let them return home.
### LARGE +
The Scions treat their Hyperborean bloodline as sacred: silver blood, sensitivity to kuuvahki, antler crowns and the ability to speak with living things. Their long "Sacred Heir Project" to keep that blood pure ended in bloodshed about 500 years ago.

## entry: Founding of the Autonomous Region
@keys: Solovei, Torchforger, Aila, Lovia, Rotwang, Rauthvangi, Arrow of Terpikeraunas, Reed Miller
@keys2: Nod-Krai, Lightkeepers, Frostmoon Scions, Cataclysm, Piramida
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
About 500 years ago the Khaenri'ahn exile Rotwang (Rauthvangi), favourite of the Belyi Tsar, came to Nod-Krai seeking a way to open the false sky to the Abyss. He manipulated the outlaw Avrelian ("Reed Miller") and corresponded with the Scions' last Archpriestess, Lovia, about the Hyperborean relic called the Sampo Mill. Avrelian's friend Solovei, a Snezhnayan noble, came to defend the land. Aila, a Scion twin raised by Lovia, sided with him and killed Lovia. Her sister Alia gave her life to foil Rotwang's ascent at the Pillar of Embla, and Aila and Solovei then sealed his returning menace at Kipumaki Cliff. Aila abolished the Archpriestess role, became the first Moonchanter and gave Solovei the Arrow of Terpikeraunas. With the Tsaritsa's edict, Solovei founded the Lightkeepers and Nod-Krai became autonomous.
### ULTRA +
Aila left records for the Traveler, whom she foresaw would one day finish Rotwang. She also foresaw Kuutar's birth and offered the newborn goddess the first drop of silver blood. Only Scions of Priestess rank or higher are allowed to know Lovia's name.

## entry: The Fatui in Nod-Krai
@keys: Palestar Edict, Kuuvahki Experimental Design Bureau, Oprichniki
@keys2: Nod-Krai, Fatui, Dottore, Sandrone, Columbina
@version: 6.0
### STANDARD
A Fatui stronghold in Nod-Krai belonged to Dottore. Its public face, the Kuuvahki Experimental Design Bureau on Paha Isle, was run by Sandrone ("Marionette"), Seventh of the Harbingers. The Fatui pulled down two Ancient Moon remnants, caged or defaced Statues of the New Moon, pressed the Scions to give up Columbina, and pursued her under the Palestar Edict. Locals resented them deeply. Some rank-and-file Fatui, such as Jotautas and Gediminas, later helped the heroes against Dottore.
### LARGE +
Dottore revived Rerir, built an artificial Moon Marrow and a false moon, ambushed Columbina on Moon-Prayer Night and finally defied the Tsaritsa, who authorised Arlecchino and Sandrone to kill him. Sandrone moved from adversary to ally and died shielding the Traveler. Afterwards the Palestar Edict was revoked and Sandrone apologised to the Frostmoon Scions.

## entry: The Song of the Welkin Moon Crisis
@keys: Song of the Welkin Moon, Heretic of the False Moon, Trilune Goddess, Columbina Hyposelenia
@keys2: Nod-Krai, Nasha Town, Columbina, Dottore, Rerir
@version: 6.3
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The Traveler came to Nasha Town with Ineffa at Varka's invitation, sensing a power like the one they felt when their spaceship landed. The Eternal Moon Marrow was stolen, and Rerir, master of the Wild Hunt, murdered the Lightkeeper Sousi and framed Flins. Rerir then tricked the town into firing Aino's cannon so that it rebuilt his body. Allies including Lauma, Flins, Nefer, Jahoda, Aino, Ineffa, Varka, Columbina, Arlecchino, Albedo, Durin and Dainsleif cast him out of Teyvat through a moon gate. As Moon-Prayer Night neared, Columbina began fading because she had no true name. The Traveler named her, and she became Columbina Hyposelenia. At her farewell, Dottore seized the three moons' power, and she vanished into the moon's reflection.
### LARGE +
Dottore captured the Traveler, cursed the heroes so they could not leave Nod-Krai and swallowed the islands in time-warped fields, so the region was evacuated. Travelling backwards in time, Columbina received the Moon Sisters' authorities, the Trilune. In True Moon, Sandrone was killed but had hidden the real world-formula inside her automaton Pulonia; the Wanderer's Irminsul core computed it. Arlecchino, Varka and Nicole pulled the Frost Moon down, and Columbina returned as the Trilune Goddess with her eyes open. With the Traveler she destroyed Dottore. The Traveler's ship was then found on the Frost Moon.

## entry: Rotwang's Last Rising
@keys: Nightingale's Song, Calamity Egg, Scourge of Endless Decay, Nightmare Orioles
@keys2: Nod-Krai, Illuga, Lightkeepers, Rotwang, Piramida
@version: 6.3
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
In the Nightingale's Song world quests, the Traveler joined Illuga and the Nightmare Orioles in the north. They defeated the Scourge of Endless Decay, an Abyssal entity sealed beneath Kipumaki Cliff, and Starshyna Nikita sent Illuga to recover the Silver Lampwick that powers the Arrow of Terpikeraunas. Rotwang corrupted the Pillar of Embla and sent Abyssal forces against Piramida. Illuga and the Traveler fired the Arrow and destroyed the Calamity Egg on the pillar, ending Rotwang's threat to Nod-Krai for good, as Aila had foretold.

## entry: The Wild Hunt After Rerir
@keys: White Knight, Bloodstained Knight
@keys2: Nod-Krai, Wild Hunt, Varka, Roland
@version: 6.4
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
After Rerir was banished, the Abyss made an imitation of Roland, a Knight of Favonius from 500 years ago who had pledged himself to the Abyss, to lead the Wild Hunt. It corrupted the half of Andrius' soul that had stayed in Nod-Krai, and the corruption spread through the ley lines to Mondstadt. In Varka's story quest (6.4) the imitation expired, and the Wild Hunt has been leaderless since, driven only by an urge to destroy. The Knights now think the Lightkeepers can handle what remains, though Varka warns that a bigger threat lies ahead.
