## summary
### LITE
Snezhnaya is the Cryo nation of perpetual winter in the far north of Teyvat. It worships the Tsaritsa (Anastasya Feodorovna Snezhnaya), Cryo Archon and God of Love, who unlike most Archons rules directly and absolutely from Zapolyarny Palace in the capital, Snezhnograd. Her Fatui act as government, diplomats and army, and her goal is to overthrow the Heavenly Principles. Humans share the land with the Snowland Fae, the old aristocracy of her predecessor the Belyi Tsar, and survive the cold by burning Kresnik Kristall in great furnaces called Kresnik's Torches.
### STANDARD +
It is Teyvat's most militarily and technologically advanced nation (railways, factories, firearms, Delusions) and reputedly the second-wealthiest after Liyue. The autonomous region of Nod-Krai is its southernmost part. Snezhnaya became explorable in Version 7.0, the setting of Archon Quest Chapter VII.

## geography
### STANDARD
Frozen earth and ice fields with month-long blizzards and night auroras. Explorable areas: Volkodlak Tundra (southwest, the entry from Nod-Krai), Everfrozen Earth (north, with Snezhnograd), Fellfrost Peak (south of the capital), Flamefeather Valley (west) and White Birch Snowgrave, the Belyi Tsar's old court and tomb. Novokitezhgrad and Port Belovodye cannot yet be visited.
### ULTRA +
Some zones are so cold that travelers must retreat at once, and purple-misted rifts into the Shadow Realm, a space without wind, light or usable elemental energy, float over the land like bubbles. Railways link the towns, though some remote lines have fallen into disrepair.

## government
### STANDARD
The Tsaritsa holds absolute power; her decrees, called Edicts, are the highest law, and she reviews ministers' reports at court herself. The Fatui, headquartered in Glupov and led by Director Pierro and the Eleven Harbingers (all appointed by her), handle most governance. The Snezhnograd Veche, a council she created to replace the fae aristocracy, legislates by majority vote; its Speaker is the Harbinger Pulcinella, also Mayor of Snezhnograd and Glupov, and the Fatui usually get their way there. Nobles hold titles at her pleasure and mostly lack political power. The Druzhna, mostly Vila fae under Commander Vesna, is her personal guard. Nod-Krai is fully autonomous by Edict.
### LARGE +
Other state bodies: the Royal Energy Commission (torches and Kristall), the Royal Railway Transport Bureau, Sandrone's Armory Palace, Pantalone's state-owned Northland Bank, and Fatui Internal Affairs (the "Okhranka"), which investigates Fatui misconduct and reports to the Tsaritsa. Veche debate is real but limited: Harbingers argue openly in session, and reform councillors such as Euphrosiniya Trubetskaya complain that the Speaker dismisses their proposals to heat the poor Zolnyy district.
### ULTRA +
At court the Tsaritsa dislikes excessive etiquette and asks only for good manners. Distrusting her own memory, she records each day in a memory prism that changes to Irminsul cannot alter. Officials and nobles carry formal ranks such as Privy Councillor. In 7.1 nobles and officials were obliged to donate Seals of Life, while commoners could volunteer for compensation at the Northland Bank.

## religion
### STANDARD
Devotion centres on the Tsaritsa's throne ("In Snezhnaya, all shall be offered unto the throne of Her Majesty"); no organised church has been shown. Her divine name is Vassaga and her regnal titles include Holy One of All Winters; ordinary people call her only by her title.
### LARGE +
She refuses to use her authority of love, seeing the love the Heavenly Principles force on gods as a false curse. Dainsleif says she has no love left for her people, nor they for her, and that her followers hope only to stand with her when she rebels against the heavens; yet in 7.1 people eagerly volunteered to help her fight Death, and intimates such as Vesna and Danica think her kinder than she believes. A Statue of the Seven stands in Snezhnograd.

## economy
### STANDARD
Pantalone sets economic policy; factories mass-produce household goods for export, and the Northland Bank and Adventurers' Guild, both founded here, operate across Teyvat. Energy is the binding constraint: Kresnik Kristall is mined chiefly at Novokitezhgrad and shipped by rail.
### LARGE +
Novokitezhgrad is also known for liquor, and Port Belovodye earns vast revenue. Snezhnayan merchants once offered Fire-Water to Mondstadt, but Diluc declined after one drink knocked him out for three days. Factories live on energy quotas, so Project Stuzha set industrialists against households.

## history
### LITE
Long ago the land was green and held golden Hyperborea, destroyed about 6,000 years ago. The Belyi Tsar united the fae and became the first Cryo Archon 2,000 years ago; he died fighting the Abyss in the Cataclysm 500 years ago, and Anastasya succeeded him.
### STANDARD +
Hyperborean experiments on the Cryo Dragon's corpse froze the land and crystallised its ley lines into Kresnik Kristall. The Belyi Tsar founded Kitezhgrad and the Pale Star Court, admitted humans to his realm, built the first Kresnik's Torches and moved his seat to Snezhnograd. On her accession the Tsaritsa crushed fae princes who refused her, founded the Fatui with Pierro, created the Veche and cut ties with the other Archons. Shortly before the Traveler's journey she began gathering the other Archons' Gnoses.
### LARGE +
Her Harbingers took the Anemo and Geo Gnoses (via Signora), Electro and Dendro (via Dottore) and Hydro (via Arlecchino); Capitano was excused from taking the Pyro Gnosis, which burned with Irminsul. Signora was entombed in ice at the Sanctuary of Grief.

## customs
### STANDARD
Life is spent indoors, being alive is itself a blessing, and families are close. Loyalty is prized; betrayal and unpaid debts are deeply personal affronts, as a nursery rhyme about breaking a pinkie promise and being thrown on the ice shows. Fire-Water, the strongest liquor known, is downed a glass at a time out of respect. People use name and patronymic and say "comrade".
### LARGE +
Rye replaces wheat, milk becomes smetana, coastal towns eat mostly seafood, and borscht is called an authentic dish. Alchemy is banned on the mainland, imposed by the Harbingers after Belyi Tsar-era alchemists bred chimeric monsters. The Korolevskiy Theater is a pilgrimage site for performers, and the Snezhnaya Vestnik newspaper sells at stations.

## festivals
@availability: limited
### LARGE
Krsnik Noc is a Snezhnayan festival; it is known only from a Snezhnayan merchant likening Lantern Rite's mood to it during that limited-time event.

## military
### LARGE
The Fatui are Teyvat's strongest army; all known fighters use elemental attacks through technology or Delusions. Skirmishers wear mechanised suits, Oprichniki carry elemental guns, Landcruisers patrol. Snezhnayans are conscripted into military training, though not all trainees join the Fatui; each Harbinger commands personal troops, and the House of the Hearth raises orphans as agents.

## foreign_relations
### LARGE
The Fatui conduct nearly all diplomacy, and their aggressive encroachment makes them distrusted abroad, though ordinary Snezhnayan merchants meet little prejudice. Jean counts Snezhnaya, like Mondstadt, an independent nation. The Khaenri'ahns descend from the Tsaritsa and honour her as the "Mother of Khaenri'ah".

## dangers
### LARGE
Lethal cold away from heat, month-long blizzards, Shadow Realm rifts that leave the lost as soulless husks, chimeric monsters from banned alchemy, hostile rogue fae, and banditry born of poverty in remote towns.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Birch forests, steel railways, towns huddled around a torch's glow; Snezhnograd is beautiful under auroras and endless blizzards but increasingly industrial, and fae of many kinds live openly among humans.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 Snezhnaya has defeated the Shade of Death, Ronova, whose curse killed anyone developing anti-Celestia technology. The Tsaritsa holds six Gnoses, Project Mistilteinn has resumed, and the Traveler serves her as a consultant.
### STANDARD +
Ronova lost her authority after the Tsaritsa slowed time around Rhinedottir with the six Gnoses, and Pierro's new Delusion mortally wounded her. Dying, she fused the "Eitr" with the Nail of Retribution above the Cryo Dragon's corpse as a "parting gift"; the Nail began to crumble, threatening to spread Holy Soil across Teyvat.
### LARGE +
The energy rationing of Project Stuzha was suspended during the crisis; whether it resumed is unclear. Corruption reviews are under way. Rhinedottir spared the Tsaritsa and told her she had earned her respect; Dainsleif agrees that Ronova's defeat begins a new era, though "not a peaceful era."

## entry: Snezhnograd and Zapolyarny Palace
@keys: Snezhnograd, Zapolyarny Palace, Zapolyarny, Kresnik's Burning Heart, Snow City
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
Snezhnograd ("Snow City") is the capital, in Everfrozen Earth. It holds most of the state's headquarters: Zapolyarny Palace, the fortress-city Glupov, the Snezhnograd Veche State Hall, Druzhna HQ, the Adventurers' Guild and Northland Bank headquarters, and the nation's most important Kresnik's Torch in Kresnik's Burning Heart. It also has the Korolevskiy Theater, Central Station and the popular Podvorye Restaurant, and Zolnyy, the poor industrial "Ash District". Zapolyarny Palace ("Palace Beyond the Arctic Circle") was the Belyi Tsar's seat and is now the Tsaritsa's; she rules from a throne of sculpted ice and sometimes stands alone on a high balcony overlooking the plaza.
### LARGE +
Fae live in the city in fair numbers, though fewer than in Novokitezhgrad. Pulcinella is the city's second mayor, having succeeded the human I. Ivanovna N. A hidden chamber beneath the palace holds a huge crystalline seal left by the Third Descender, which reacts only to Gnoses; the Tsaritsa believes all seven are needed to open it. The palace treasury keeps the chariot-sized skull of King Miscislav, still marked by the Belyi Tsar's spear.

## entry: Glupov and the Armory Palace
@keys: Glupov, Armory Palace, Royal Armory Palace, Special Assignments Battalion
### STANDARD
Glupov ("city of fools") is a satellite fortress-city inside Snezhnograd and the headquarters of the Fatui, built on the Tsaritsa's order in the fourth year of her reign. Entry requires a permit or invitation. Deep within lies the Royal Armory Palace, managed by Sandrone, where weapons such as Landcruisers are developed and where Project Mistilteinn's underground research site is located.

## entry: The Snezhnograd Veche and Rokot
@keys: Snezhnograd Veche, Veche, Rokot, Lelek
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
The Veche is described as a large council where interest groups meet to discuss the nation, issue decrees and assess policy. In 7.0 it renewed Project Stuzha with sixty-one percent support, narrower than before. Outside it works the Rokot ("Rumble"), a civilian organisation serving "the common people": members take codenames so their political voice is not tied to their status, rarely meet, and avoid violence, instead gathering evidence of abuses so that official investigations follow. Because it opposed Stuzha (and sometimes the Tsaritsa herself), the Fatui monitor it as an enemy, though some Fatui consider it peaceful. Alyosha, Vodyanitsa and the Traveler are members; Odette was the infiltrator "Lelek" and is suspended.

## entry: Kresnik Kristall and Kresnik's Torch
@keys: Kresnik Kristall, Kresnik's Torch, Kristall, Royal Energy Commission
### STANDARD
Kresnik Kristall is a crystalline ore that burns with intense heat. It formed when the Cryo Dragon's released authority turned Snezhnaya's ley lines into crystal veins; early settlers found the veins by following Kristall Bugs. Without it humans could not live here: the land was once so cold that only fae survived. Kresnik's Torches, furnaces burning Kristall, were built by the Belyi Tsar once he accepted humans; every city is built around at least one, with smaller civilian models scattered across the wilds. The Royal Energy Commission maintains them and manages the ore's transport and use.
### LARGE +
The prototype torches remain in the Pale Crown Palace, where the Belyi Tsar experimented before building Snezhnograd's great torch. Under Project Stuzha, Kristall became a ration distributed by Fatui detachments, and local officials such as Sretomorozsk's liaison officer Shishkin and its mayor were caught stashing deliveries.

## entry: Snowland Fae
@keys: Snowland Fae, Filii Adamici Evoluti
@keys2: Snezhnaya, Belyi Tsar, Tsaritsa, Snezhnograd
### STANDARD
The Snowland Fae were created by Hyperborean artisans using forbidden methods taught by the first Seelie, Koitar; the "Evolved Children of Humanity" were said to be made of the Frost Moon's light upon the bones of giant beasts. Common kinds are Vila (winged), Domovoy (household), Leshy, Volkodlak (beast), Snegovik (snow; females are Snegurochka) and the mute, prankish Jack Frosts; rarer ones include Augury Birds, Lantern Fae and Water Imps. All can wield elemental power without Visions, many can change shape, and they have their own language and script. Under the Belyi Tsar they were the ruling aristocracy; after his death they lost their monopoly on power. Most now accept the Tsaritsa and live ordinary lives, while small pockets still long for the old order.

## entry: Hyperborea and the Cryo Dragon
@keys: Hyperborea, Golden Hyperborea, Cryo Dragon, Age of Polar Night
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
Over 6,000 years ago the golden city of Hyperborea flourished in a warm, green Snezhnaya. Its people tried to make a Primordial Human from the corpse of the Cryo Dragon, one of the Seven Sovereigns. The Dragon's unchecked authority covered the land in primordial ice and crystallised its ley lines, and a Celestial Nail destroyed Hyperborea, whose ruins now lie in the Shadow Realm; survivors fled to Nod-Krai and became the Frostmoon Scions. A Nail of Retribution still hangs over the Dragon's corpse; in 7.1 the dying Ronova fused the "Eitr" into it and it began to crumble. Fae legend, recorded in Saint Strivozha's Songbook, tells a different myth of a King of Light destroying Hyperborea; Snezhnayan historians treat it as legend.

## entry: The Belyi Tsar and the Pale Star Court
@keys: Belyi Tsar, Monomakh, Monomakh Snezhnyi, Pale Star Court, White Tsar, Kitezhgrad, Kitezh, Miscislav
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
Monomakh Snezhnyi hatched from an icy egg in the Cryo Dragon's remains 5,000 years ago, the incomplete Primordial Human of the Hyperborean experiment, though he and everyone else believed him a fae. Taught the spear by Yalyane Morevna of the House of Hesperides, he founded Kitezh, Snezhnaya's first city and the Pale Star Court's capital. When King Miscislav of the Volkodlak attacked Kitezh, Monomakh killed him, and Grand Dukes Skoldyryt and Vodyanoy, with hurled spears; the fae clans submitted and he took the title Belyi Tsar ("White Tsar"). He became Cryo Archon and God of Love 2,000 years ago, opened his realm to humans, and secretly sought to complete his ascension and overthrow the Heavenly Principles. In the Cataclysm he led an expedition to Khaenri'ah against the Abyss and was killed. His body, still biologically alive, was entombed in the Pale Crown Palace in White Birch Snowgrave and grew into the giant Tsar's White Birch. In the world quest An Ark That Used to Be a Soul, Aksinya transferred the Primordial Human's power into the remains; the Traveler defeated the resulting being and the body crumbled to dust.

## entry: Anastasya and the Third Descender
@keys: Saarelainen, Third Descender, Mother of Khaenri'ah, Vassaga
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
More than 6,000 years ago Anastasya loved Saarelainen, the Third Descender, who came from the sea of stars; their son's four-pointed star pupils passed to their descendants, the Khaenri'ahns. Much later, as a herald of the Pale Star Court, she and Saarelainen were incited by the Belyi Tsar to open a gate to the Abyss, creating a passage into the Shadow Realm. The Heavenly Principles erased her. Saarelainen traded his own life for her resurrection and a promise that their descendants would be spared; his remains became the seven Gnoses, and all memory of him was erased, hers included. The Belyi Tsar, hidden in the Shadow Realm, kept the memory and later told her. Unable to trust her own mind, she records every day in a memory prism that Irminsul changes cannot alter.

## entry: Project Stuzha and Project Mistilteinn
@keys: Project Stuzha, Stuzha, Project Mistilteinn, Mistilteinn
@version: 7.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Project Stuzha ("severe cold") is a set of energy-austerity policies passed by the Veche, diverting energy from the public to the Armory Palace. It cost far more and lasted far longer than planned: stoves went cold, small-town stations closed, and Kristall was rationed. Its real purpose was Project Mistilteinn, overseen by Pierro: a weapon capable of fighting the Heavenly Principles, powered by energy drawn from Stellar Linchpins. Ronova's curse halted Mistilteinn (and with it Stuzha) in 7.0; it resumed in 7.1 once her authority was revoked.

## entry: The Tsaritsa's Accession
@keys: 799th Cycle, Imperial Order Reformation Edict, Drevogorye, Tsaritsa of All Snezhnaya
@spoiler: minor
### LARGE
Snezhnayan chronicles date events by numbered Cycles. In the first year of the 799th Cycle, during the Cataclysm, Anastasya was crowned Tsaritsa of All Snezhnaya, taking the surname Snezhnaya, the feminine form of her predecessor's Snezhnyi. Many fae princes refused to recognise a non-fae ruler, and the Dalegrad clan of Water Imps broke with Snezhnograd. In the second year the humans of Port Belovodye declared self-government; in the third she founded the Fatui (Pierro its first member), which seized the lands of the Drevogorye, Volkhovan and Cirnareka clans, killed Grand Duke Drevogorye, and ended the revolt by offering amnesty for the instigators' surrender. Glupov was built in the fourth year, the Veche founded in the sixth with the human Ioanna Ivanovna as its first chairperson, and in the seventh the Imperial Order Reformation Edict led many princely fae to renounce their titles and leave. Shortly after the Cataclysm she also granted Nod-Krai autonomy at the request of Solovei.
