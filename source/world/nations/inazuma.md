## summary
### LITE
Inazuma is the island nation of Eternity off the far east of Teyvat, near the Dark Sea. Its god is the Electro Archon Raiden Ei, the Raiden Shogun. For centuries a deathless puppet (the Shogun) ruled in her place while her mind meditated in the Plane of Euthymia. The Shogunate governs from Inazuma City and the castle Tenshukaku on Narukami Island, with daily affairs run by the Tri-Commission. Inazuma is a land of cherry blossoms, red maples, thunderstorms, shrines, swordsmanship, and youkai living alongside humans. It was recently closed off by the Sakoku Decree, while the Vision Hunt Decree seized its people's Visions. Both decrees are now repealed.
### STANDARD +
Inazumans revere the Shogun as an unchanging god and have only recently begun to know Ei as a person. Watatsumi Island still worships its own slain god, Orobashi.

## geography
### STANDARD
Inazuma has six main islands. Narukami holds the capital, the foreigners' port of Ritou and Mt. Yougou. Kannazuka has the Mikage Furnace. Yashiori is split by Musoujin Gorge. Watatsumi, with Sangonomiya Shrine, lies farthest from the capital. Seirai is storm-wracked and Tsurumi fog-bound. Enkanomiya lies under the sea beneath Watatsumi.
### LARGE +
The islands are what survived a great flood after the war of funerary flame. Orobashi raised most of Watatsumi from his own coral near the end of the Archon War. Kannazuka is "the hill where no gods reside," claimed by neither faith. Thunder Sakura trees, grafted from the Sacred Sakura, draw filth from the land, and Electro barriers and storms mark old seals and wounds.

## government
### STANDARD
The Raiden Shogun rules from Tenshukaku through the Tri-Commission. The Tenryou Commission (Kujou Clan) handles the military and security. The Kanjou Commission (Hiiragi Clan) handles finance and the borders. The Yashiro Commission (Kamisato Clan, under Kamisato Ayato) oversees shrines and festivals. The Grand Narukami Shrine under Guuji Yae Miko answers to the Archon, not to the Shogunate. Watatsumi is largely governed by its Divine Priestess, Sangonomiya Kokomi.
### LARGE +
The Tenryou and Kanjou commissioners, Kujou Takayuki and Hiiragi Shinsuke, collaborated with the Fatui. Both were removed and arrested, and neither has an official successor. Kujou Kamaji and Hiiragi Chisato serve as acting clan heads, and the tengu Kujou Sara commands the Shogunate's forces.

## religion
### LARGE
Devotion to the Electro Archon is far more visible in daily life than in Mondstadt or Liyue. The Grand Narukami Shrine guards the Sacred Sakura and performs its Cleansing Ritual. Watatsumi reveres Orobashi as the Watatsumi Omikami, which the Shogun permitted after his defeat. Few people know that "Baal" was originally two gods: Makoto, the first Archon, and Ei, her kagemusha (body double), who took her titles.

## history
### STANDARD
The twin gods Makoto and Ei won the archipelago in the Archon War. Their conquered youkai helped humans found a city by the sea, and Ei slew rival gods such as Orobashi more than 2,000 years ago. In the cataclysm 500 years ago Makoto died in Khaenri'ah. Fearing "erosion," Ei pursued Eternity: about 400 years ago she left the Shogun puppet to rule, sealed her mind in the Plane of Euthymia, and gave the Electro Gnosis to Yae Miko. A year before the Traveler came, the Fatui manipulated two commissions into the Sakoku and Vision Hunt decrees, which provoked a civil war with Watatsumi.
### LARGE +
The Traveler, Yae Miko and the Watatsumi resistance confronted Ei and ended the Vision Hunt. Ei then redefined Eternity and lifted Sakoku (2.5). Older layers of history include sunken Byakuyakoku (Enkanomiya), Tsurumi's Thunderbird tribe, and Ei's killing of the Thunderbird about 1,500 years ago.

## culture
### LARGE
Youkai such as kitsune, tengu, oni and bake-danuki live among humans, though few remain and most keep to themselves. Swordsmanship and honour are central, and a Duel Before the Throne before the Shogun is fought to the death. Light novels flourish, and the main festivals are the Summer Festival, the Irodori Festival and the Mikawa Flower Festival.

## economy
### LARGE
Sakoku crippled trade, and the economy has been recovering since the reopening. Exports include printed books (Yae Publishing House), kimonos, toys and Naganohara fireworks. Swords forged from Jade Steel made at the Mikage Furnace were long famous.

## foreign_relations
### LARGE
The Fatui of Snezhnaya engineered the decrees to obtain the Electro Gnosis; Signora was executed after losing a duel to the Traveler, yet diplomatic ties continue. Liyue's Crux Fleet ran the blockade. Inazuma lies close to the Dark Sea and has fought many wars against what comes from it.

## dangers
### LARGE
Ronin (Nobushi, Kairagi), Treasure Hoarders, the Tatarigami on Yashiori, lingering Electro storms, Fatui agents, Abyss Order activity around Enkanomiya, and Abyssal filth that gathers under the Thunder Sakura.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 Inazuma is at peace and open. The Vision Hunt is over and the Visions have been returned, Sakoku is lifted, a ceasefire with Watatsumi holds, and Ei takes a more direct part in governing.
### STANDARD +
The Electro Gnosis is now in Fatui hands. The Archon Quest has since moved on to other nations, up to Snezhnaya (Chapter VII, 7.0-7.1). At the close of 7.1, after Ronova destroyed a Celestial Nail in Snezhnaya with Eitr, the other Nails across Teyvat began emitting pillars of light, and unusual activity was reported on Tsurumi Island, where a Nail is believed to have fallen. Yae Miko went to see for herself after drawing a "Great Misfortune" fortune slip; she senses a faint power there and means to bring Kokomi to look at it. What the phenomenon means is not yet known.
### LARGE +
Watatsumi keeps its army as peacekeepers while talks on its status are paused.

## entry: The Tri-Commission and the Shogun's Army
@keys: Tri-Commission, San-bugyou, Tenryou Commission, Kanjou Commission, Yashiro Commission, Shogun's Army, Shuumatsuban
### STANDARD
The three commissions meet at Tenshukaku, and any one of them can block the others' proposals: during the Vision Hunt the Tenryou and Kanjou repeatedly vetoed the Yashiro's motions to repeal it. The Tenryou Commission is the Shogun's executive arm for security, war and policing, and enforced both decrees. The Kanjou Commission, based at Ritou, manages finance, customs and borders, and was known for harsh treatment of foreigners. The Yashiro Commission, headquartered at the Kamisato Estate, runs shrines, festivals and cultural affairs. It is considered closest to the Shogun and was the only commission that refused the Fatui. The Shogun's Army is the national army, with units serving each commission. The Watatsumi Army, led by Kokomi with Gorou as general, is a separate force. The Shuumatsuban are a covert ninja corps answering to the Yashiro Commissioner.
### LARGE +
After the decrees fell, Takayuki was placed under house arrest and Shinsuke imprisoned for their part in the Fatui plot. Takayuki had fed the Shogun false reports, and Shinsuke had supplied Crystal Marrow for Delusions. When a commissioner falls, lesser clans such as the Takatsukasa may move against the leading clan. In Reflections of Mortality (2.1), Ei offered the Tenryou post through a duel. Takatsukasa Susumu refused out of fear, but Kujou Kamaji accepted although he knew he was outmatched, to let his clan's prestige end with honour. Ei judged him honourable and gave the Kujou more time. The Yashiro's clean record has earned it more sympathy and influence.
### ULTRA +
The army's ranks follow the Tokugawa model: ashigaru foot soldiers, doushin patrol officers, yoriki samurai, Hatamoto guarding Tenshukaku, and the Okuzumeshuu bodyguards who take orders only from the Shogun. Families that receive a draft order must send at least one adult. Common soldiers carry cross-bladed spears, while samurai fight with swords and bows. A navy is said to exist but has not been shown. The Tenryou suffers high desertion: some recruits expected to fight monsters, not fellow Inazumans. Ayato also uses the Shuumatsuban against corrupt officials.

## entry: Inazuman Society and Customs
@keys: Duel Before the Throne, shugenja, Inazuman customs, Musou no Hitotachi, Summer Festival, Goshintou Ceremony, Warrior's Spirit Swordfighting Exhibition
### STANDARD
Humans are the majority, but youkai also live in Inazuma, many descended from races Ei conquered and then took into her service. Yae Miko is a kitsune, Kujou Sara a tengu and Arataki Itto an oni. Samurai families are proud and honour-bound: they send children into the Shogun's service and count defeat, or help from an outsider, as shameful. Anyone with a grievance may call a Duel Before the Throne at Tenshukaku. It always ends in death, and a loser who survives is executed with the Musou no Hitotachi, the peak of Ei's swordsmanship. Inazumans who leave the country to train call themselves shugenja. The national cry is "Glory to the Shogun and her everlasting reign!"
### LARGE +
The Summer Festival on Narukami is lit by Naganohara Fireworks, and its owner Yoimiya is called the festival's "Queen." The Irodori Festival honours the ancient poets known as the Five Kasen; Yae Miko revived it as an international light-novel convention after Sakoku. The Mikawa Flower Festival is a youkai revel more than five centuries old that Ei, Makoto, the Kitsune Saiguu and Yae Miko once attended. At the Goshintou Ceremony, master swordsmiths offer their finest blades to the Grand Narukami Shrine. The Warrior's Spirit Swordfighting Exhibition once pitted champions against the Shogun herself and now uses an emissary, most recently Kamisato Ayaka.
### ULTRA +
A rite honouring both the Raiden Shogun and Orobashi, devised after the cataclysm, must precede every other ritual. Kamuna Harunosuke developed onmyoudou. It is now mostly forgotten, though Kairagi ronin still use shikifuda talismans to give their blades elemental power without a Vision. The roaming Maguu Kenki are failed puppets Ei built while searching for a perfect vessel, modelled on the swordsman Iwakura Michihiro. Rice is the staple food, and the cuisine favours light seafood dishes and sakura flavouring. Shrine maidens wear red hakama on Narukami and blue on Watatsumi. Clans bear mon crests, such as the Kamisato camellia.

## entry: Inazuman Trade and Industry
@keys: Yae Publishing House, This Novel Is Amazing, Naganohara Fireworks, Crystal Marrow, Aisa Bathhouse
### LARGE
Before the decrees Inazuma's economy was healthy. Under Sakoku few ships braved the storms, imports and exports collapsed, and fishing shrank because the Kanjou Commission treated every boat as a possible escape. Snezhnayan merchants and the Fatui were at first exempt. Publishing is a major trade: the Yae Publishing House is an industry giant and runs the "This Novel Is Amazing!" contest. Kimonos, toys and fireworks, mostly Naganohara's, are exported. Crystal Marrow from Orobashi's remains fed the Jakotsu Mine and the Mikage Furnace's Jade Steel. Inazuman blades sold steadily to Fontaine, but the mine closed after a "sickness," and Jade Steel production stopped after the peace with Watatsumi.
### ULTRA +
Konda Village and Watatsumi both struggle with poor harvests. Recent ventures include the Aisa Bathhouse in Inazuma City, whose founding investor and major shareholder is the yumekui-baku psychologist Yumemizuki Mizuki. The Kamisato Clan's Komore Teahouse shows how commission families mix business with government.

## entry: Narukami Island and Inazuma City
@keys: Narukami Island, Inazuma City, Tenshukaku, Hanamizaka, Mt. Yougou, Grand Narukami Shrine, Chinju Forest, Konda Village
### STANDARD
Narukami is the political heart of Inazuma and where public festivals are held. Inazuma City was built during the Archon War on cliffs at the island's southern tip. It is the nation's most populous and prosperous city, known for family businesses and low crime, and it climbs from the harbour and Hanamizaka's shops past the Tenryou Commission to Tenshukaku. Mt. Yougou above holds the Grand Narukami Shrine and the Sacred Sakura. The shrine maidens sell omamori and fortune slips, and armed force is forbidden on the grounds. The Kamisato Estate and Chinju Forest, home to bake-danuki pranksters, are also on the island.
### LARGE +
The Kitsune Saiguu, the great kitsune who once led the shrine, persuaded Baal to spare the tanuki Ioroi and make him Hidden Protector of Chinju Forest. Centuries of care by kitsune have given the Sacred Sakura its foxlike shape. Abyssal filth absorbed by the Thunder Sakura collects in its roots and must be purged in the Sacred Sakura Cleansing Ritual.

## entry: Ritou and Entry to Inazuma
@keys: Ritou, Outlander Affairs Agency, Travel Permit, Nation Closed by Thunder
### STANDARD
Ritou, the "outlying island" attached to Narukami, has long been the only gateway for outsiders. Hiiragi Hiroshi of the Kanjou Commission built it from a deserted island after the cataclysm, and it is still the Hiiragi Clan's base. Under Sakoku, thunderstorms ringed the archipelago. Outlanders stuck at Ritou needed a hard-won travel permit to go further inland, and Kanjou ashigaru blocked the road. Since the decree was lifted the storms no longer bar the way, and international shops and the International Trade Association fill its maple-shaded plaza.

## entry: Kannazuka and Tatarasuna
@keys: Kannazuka, Tatarasuna, Mikage Furnace, Kujou Encampment, Jade Steel
### STANDARD
Kannazuka was the civil war's disputed front line. The Kujou Encampment, a fortress Kujou Shigeyori built on the cataclysm-era battlefield, faced the Watatsumi forces. Tatarasuna's Mikage Furnace, the nation's largest smelter, made Jade Steel from Crystal Marrow until it malfunctioned during the war. Xavier contained it under a dome, and the Traveler later helped restore it.
### ULTRA +
About four centuries ago a Fontaine mechanic called "Escher", really Il Dottore, tampered with the furnace, poisoned Tatarasuna and murdered the Armory Officer Niwa. In the disgrace that followed, Inspector Mikoshi Nagamasa cut down his retainer Katsuragi with his prized blade, which was renamed Katsuragikiri Nagamasa. The story originally also involved Ei's first puppet, the Kabukimono (see the Raiden Gokaden entry for how history was rewritten).

## entry: Yashiori Island and the Tatarigami
@keys: Yashiori Island, Musoujin Gorge, Serpent's Head, Higi Village, Fort Fujitou, Jakotsu Mine, Tatarigami
### STANDARD
Yashiori Island holds the skeleton of Orobashi, the serpent god of Watatsumi. More than two thousand years ago he invaded the island. His vassal Akuou killed Ei's friend the great tengu Sasayuri, and in her fury Ei's Musou no Hitotachi killed Orobashi and Akuou together and split the island, forming Musoujin Gorge. Orobashi's resentment became the Tatarigami. Ei sealed it with wards (Narukami Pillars), and the Kitain Clan served as Yashiori Guardians until their line ended in the cataclysm. During the civil war, Orobashi fanatics aided by a Fatui agent destroyed the wards without Kokomi's knowledge. Storms, sickness and madness emptied Higi Village until the Traveler restored the wards.
### LARGE +
Kujou Masahito, the general on Yashiori, reported the Tatarigami honestly. His father Takayuki thought he was exaggerating and sent investigators, who confirmed the reports and then went missing themselves. The ancient city of Mumei, which Watatsumi seized in the invasion, was destroyed in the battle.

## entry: Watatsumi Island and Enkanomiya
@keys: Watatsumi Island, Sangonomiya Shrine, Watatsumi Omikami, Enkanomiya, Byakuyakoku, Holy Soil, Bloodbranch Coral, Dainichi Mikoshi
@version: 2.1
### STANDARD
Watatsumi Island lies opposite Narukami, lush with brightly coloured coral and plants. It is home to the Sangonomiya Clan and its Divine Priestess, currently Sangonomiya Kokomi, who rules from Sangonomiya Shrine. Its people descend from Enkanomiya and still worship Orobashi. Relations with the Shogunate stayed cool for centuries and became open war under the Vision Hunt. After the ceasefire, talks on returning the island to Shogunate rule were paused, and the Watatsumi Army now keeps the peace, helps rebuild and is clearing out the Fatui. The island's poor soil is threatened by bleaching "Holy Soil," held back by rituals that need Bloodbranch Coral from Enkanomiya.
### LARGE +
Enkanomiya (opened in 2.4), reached through a pool near Sangonomiya Shrine, was once Byakuyakoku and sank into the depths after the war of funerary flame. Istaroth's wind still reached it, and the sage Abrax (Aberaku) built the artificial sun Dainichi Mikoshi to drive off the vishaps. Corrupt nobles then ruled through puppet "Sunchildren" until the fugitive god Orobashi overthrew them, and his coral became his people's ladder to the surface. The Heavenly Principles later sentenced him for reading a forbidden text, and his invasion of Yashiori was partly a way to meet that sentence. When the Traveler recently retrieved coral for Watatsumi, the Abyss Order and Bathysmal Vishaps interfered, and future harvests may fail. Kokomi is now looking for another remedy. The shrine maiden Tsuyuko, trained at the Akademiya, blames seawater salinity for the Holy Soil.

## entry: Seirai and Tsurumi Islands
@keys: Seirai Island, Asase Shrine, Koseki Village, Amakumo Peak, Thunder Manifestation, Tsurumi Island, Kanna Kapatcir, Thunderbird, Ruu
@version: 2.2
### STANDARD
About 1,500 years ago the Thunderbird, Kanna Kapatcir, ravaged Seirai Island and ruined the city of Hiraumi. Ei killed her atop Amakumo Peak, and Asase Shrine was built to contain the hatred that lingered as the Thunder Manifestation. Later the shrine maiden Asase Hibiki broke the seals to aid the pirate Ako Domeki's rebellion. The storm that followed shattered the island and forced the Shogunate to evacuate it. Tsurumi Island to the south was the Thunderbird's earlier home. Its tribe revered her; after the boy Ruu was sacrificed to her, she destroyed them and trapped the island in a looping, fog-shrouded day. The Traveler resealed Seirai's storm and helped Ruu's spirit keep his promise to sing for her, which lifted the curse.
### LARGE +
Long before the tribe, an older civilisation left vast ruins under Tsurumi, with murals of Celestia on their walls; the researcher Sumida theorises that something, implied to be a Celestial Nail, fell there from Celestia. Ruins and Ruin Sentinels lie under both islands. Seirai has no permanent residents today, and Tsurumi holds only hilichurls, riftwolves and automatons.

## entry: The Twin Shoguns

@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Inazuma's Archon was originally two people. Makoto, the gentler twin, became the first Electro Archon. Ei, the stronger warrior, thought her sister the better ruler and gave up her own body so Makoto could ascend. Makoto remade Ei's body, and Ei served as her kagemusha, so the world knew only one god, Baal. In peaceful times Ei, Makoto, the Kitsune Saiguu, the oni Mikoshi Chiyo and the great tengu Sasayuri gathered for picnics and celebrations before the throne.

## entry: The Cataclysm and Ei's Pursuit of Eternity

@keys2: Inazuma, Ei, Raiden Shogun, Makoto, Shogun
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
In the cataclysm Makoto went alone to Khaenri'ah and died there; Ei saved a fragment of her consciousness. At home the swordsman Takamine the Mistsplitter vanished into the darkness. Mikoshi Chiyo, tainted by a "beast of sin" that had swallowed her, turned her blade on Ei and was driven off. The Kitsune Saiguu was consumed, and only fragments of her memory survived as Hanachirusato. On her return Ei found the Sacred Sakura standing on Mt. Yougou, a tree everyone else remembered as ancient, and its power helped her end the disaster. Grief and disillusion with Celestia drove her to seek Eternity, and she founded the Tri-Commission. Her first puppet wept on waking, so she sealed his powers and let him go. After further failures she created the Shogun about 400 years ago, set it to rule by fixed directives, and withdrew into the Plane of Euthymia, a technique Yae Miko had taught her. She largely ignored daily affairs, the Vision Hunt included, which she tacitly approved.

## entry: The Sakoku and Vision Hunt Decrees
@keys: Sakoku Decree, Sakoku, Vision Hunt Decree, Vision Hunt, Statue of the Omnipresent God, Vision Hunt Ceremony
@version: 2.0
### STANDARD
One year before the Traveler's arrival, the Shogun sealed Inazuma's borders and ordered every Vision seized and set into the Statue of the Omnipresent God. The plan was the Fatui's. Ei knew they were involved but judged them irrelevant to Eternity. Ninety-nine Visions were taken, and their owners lost their drive and ambition; Arataki Itto and Kuki Shinobu seemed unaffected. Kazuha's friend challenged the decree in a Duel Before the Throne, lost to Kujou Sara and was executed, and Kazuha fled with the friend's Vision. The Watatsumi Army rose in revolt. The Traveler interrupted a Vision Hunt Ceremony and was defeated by Ei in the Plane of Euthymia. With Yae Miko's help they later showed Ei the wishes held in the seized Visions and defeated her. The Vision Hunt ended, the Visions were returned, and Kujou Sara and Kokomi signed a ceasefire with the Traveler as witness.
### LARGE +
The Sakoku Decree outlasted the Vision Hunt for a while, with its restrictions on foreigners eased, until Ei lifted it after Transient Dreams. Since the Vision Hunt began, no new Electro Visions are known to have appeared.
