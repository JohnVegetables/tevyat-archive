## summary
### LITE
The Upper Vale is the northern half of Chenyu Vale, the misty tea-growing valley in northwestern Liyue that borders Fontaine. Its river, the Bishui, runs from the busy border port of Yilong Wharf past Qiaoying Village, famous for its tea, and under the giant jade rings of Jademouth. It was added in version 4.4. Its peaks Mt. Lingmeng and Mt. Mingyuan frame the valley, and a hidden cavern, Chenlong Cleft, lies beneath the river.
### STANDARD +
The land is blessed by adepti and shaped by an old goddess's war; the tea, the jade ornaments and the river rites all trace back to that history.

## geography
### STANDARD
Mountains, rivers and fog. Yilong Wharf is southeast of Lumidouce Harbor across the Fontaine border, and the Bishui runs from it through the vale past Qiaoying Village. Mt. Lingmeng lies west of Qiaoying Village and southeast of Yilong Wharf; Mt. Mingyuan lies west of Yilong Wharf and east of Fontaine.
### LARGE +
The climate is relatively humid compared to the Hills of Barsom. Chenlong Cleft is a cavern under the river section beneath Jademouth, entered by the whirlpool during Floating Jade, Treasure of Chenyu; after the quest it can only be reached by waypoint.

## places
### STANDARD
Yilong Wharf is a prosperous commercial port with upper and lower levels. Qiaoying Village is the tea village with the Chunming Teaworks and a special roasting cauldron. Jademouth is a set of massive jade pieces flanking the Bishui. Mt. Lingmeng and Mt. Mingyuan are the surrounding peaks.
### LARGE +
Yilong Wharf's artisans still make simple jade ornaments such as the Symbol of Felicitation. Qiaoying's cauldron is said to be an adeptal mechanism and gives the tea its aroma.

## people
### LARGE
Tea growers, roasters and dock workers; Fontainian traders and technicians; Grandpa Lu and Uncle Luo of the tea village; Little Mao, whom Lingyuan once saved from beasts and befriended; and the adepti Fujin and Lingyuan.

## governance
### LARGE
No separate government is described. In the 4.4 quests Qiaoying's tea trade deals with the Feiyun Commerce Guild of Liyue Harbor.

## culture
### STANDARD
Tea is the vale's identity. People believe the adepti blessed the land and water; the village holds an annual tea-serving ceremony using the wilted petals of Spiritscent Flowers. Yilong Wharf residents hold ceremonies to worship ancestors and the dead and once threw jade into the water.
### LARGE +
The tradition of tossing jade into the water continued even after the old goddess perished and Morax arrived. Yilong jade is known for its blue-green color and shine and can still be found in ruins, mountains and valleys.
### ULTRA +
Another tale says the Herblord split the jade into ornaments and hid them underwater, in mountains and in shrines; they carry the blessing of a pact with a deity, and a priest inherited a jade loop from ancestors who made that pact.

## history
### STANDARD
In ancient times the vale was ruled by an unnamed goddess, the mistress of Chenyu Vale, served by three adepti: the snake Herblord, the carp Fujin and the suanni Lingyuan. Fujin planted the first tea tree and taught early settlers rituals. In the Archon War the goddess tried to drown Morax with a flood down the Bishui; her three adepti rebelled, Fujin hurled the Votive Rainjade into the river, and Jademouth formed.
### LARGE +
Settlers had come from the Chasm after Lang-Gan's destruction, and Fujin modified the environment for them. In 4.4 Lingyuan, longing for the old days, tampered with the spirit veins and soured Qiaoying's tea, and miasma broke out; the Traveler helped Fujin perform the Rainjade Rite, which reverted her to carp form.
### ULTRA +
The goddess was not evil by Fujin's account; she made many dreams come true until the Archon War drove her cruel. After the three adepti rebelled, she is said to have been sealed deep under the vale, slumbering by the ruins of the lunar chariot. The Herblord was left diminished, Fujin lost many powers and "sank into the sea," and the Votive Rainjade made the Jademouth. The vale's name, Chenyu, "sunken jade," likely comes from the legend of a slab of jade that could summon sweet rains, coveted by demons and thrown into the river.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Mist on the peaks, the scent of roasting tea, boats on the river, jade glinting in water and, near Yilong, the bustle of a border port that Fontainians visit to taste real Liyue life.

## dangers
### LARGE
Miasma that makes nearby monsters more violent, and Treasure Hoarder camps. A ragged notebook at a camp west of southern Mt. Mingyuan, most likely tied to the world quest The Dealing Sands, speaks of shipping goods hidden under tea leaves and porcelain.

## travel
### STANDARD
The Bishui River links Qiaoying Village and Yilong Wharf and has well-developed waterborne transport; Yilong Wharf is the port for Fontaine. Adeptal energy from Fujin lets travelers use special mechanics such as Golden Carp's Leap.

## quests
### LARGE
The Chenyu's Blessings of Sunken Jade series begins with Qiaoying of the Sacred Mountain and Floating Jade, Treasure of Chenyu. Other quests include Hustle and Bustle, Covert Investigation, Dirty Money Misdeeds, The Dealing Sands and Our Chenyu Vale Trek. Zibai and Qiqi meet in a Travelers' Tales anecdote near Qiaoying.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the Upper Vale is peaceful and prosperous, with the tea restored.
### STANDARD +
Fujin spent her strength on the rite and reverted to her carp form to recover; Lingyuan agreed to it. Some miasma remains. No 6.x-7.1 upheaval here is recorded.

## entry: Qiaoying Village and its tea
@keys: Qiaoying Village, Chunming Teaworks, Spiritscent Flower, Grandpa Lu, Uncle Luo
@keys2: tea, Chenyu Vale, Liyue
@version: 4.4
### STANDARD
Qiaoying Village in northwestern Liyue is famous across Teyvat for its tea, sold in Liyue Harbor and exported; Fontaine imports it through Lumidouce Harbor. A special cauldron, said to be an adeptal mechanism, roasts the leaves. Much tea is planted around the village. People believe the adepti blessed the land and water. A yearly tea-serving ceremony uses Spiritscent Flower petals. In the 4.4 quests, tea quality drops because Lingyuan has tainted the spirit veins; Grandpa Lu blames soil and water, Uncle Luo blames the machine, and the Traveler restores the tea with Fujin.

## entry: Yilong Wharf
@keys: Yilong Wharf, Yilong Port
@keys2: Fontaine, Chenyu Vale, Liyue
### STANDARD
Yilong Wharf, also Yilong Port, is a busy trade port across the sea from Fontaine, southeast of Lumidouce Harbor, where Fontainians can sample Liyue culture without going to Liyue Harbor. The wharf has a watchtower and boats up the Bishui. Locals worship ancestors and the dead there and once tossed jade into the water; scholars dispute whether the name means 'jade left behind' or a miscopied 'dragon remnants.'

## entry: Jademouth and the Votive Rainjade
@keys: Jademouth, Votive Rainjade, Rainjade Rite
@keys2: Fujin, Mt. Lingmeng, Bishui River, flood
@version: 4.4
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Jademouth is a set of massive jade pieces flanking the Bishui; Little Mao calls it a great jade ring. Legend says an adeptus cast it down to protect people from floods. In the quest account, the goddess of the vale tried to flood the river against Morax; her adepti Fujin climbed Mt. Lingmeng and threw the Votive Rainjade into the water, pinning the river down, and the Herblord fought the goddess while Lingyuan sheltered the people. Chenlong Cleft lies in the cavern under the whirlpool.

## entry: Chasm refugees and the vale's people
@keys: Chasm refugees, Lang-Gan survivors, Qimen Arts, Rite of Homa
@keys2: Lan Yan, Yelan, Chenyu Vale
@availability: limited
### LARGE
According to a Lantern Rite event quest, many of the vale's people are descendants of refugees who fled the Chasm after Lang-Gan was destroyed; Yelan's and Lan Yan's ancestors among them. This detail is limited-time and not widely known.
