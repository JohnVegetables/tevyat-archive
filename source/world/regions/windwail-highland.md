## summary
### LITE
Windwail Highland is the rustic southwestern part of Mondstadt, a country of clear springs, orchards and lush forest. It holds Springvale, a sleepy hunting village built over a spring; the Dawn Winery, the chief winery of Mondstadt, owned by the Ragnvindr family and now by Diluc; and Wolvendom, a dark forest ruled by the wolf-god Boreas and his pack, which the locals avoid.
### STANDARD +
Enemy activity is low in the orchards and high in Wolvendom, where even fearless hunters seldom venture.

## geography
### STANDARD
Gentle hills, clear springs, orchards and forest in the southwest of Mondstadt; deeper into the highland the trees thicken into Wolvendom. The Dawn Winery lies between Springvale and the Stone Gate to Liyue, beside a lake that supplies its water. Local plants include calla lily, dandelion seed, philanemo mushroom, small lamp grass, windwheel aster and wolfhook.
### LARGE +
Springvale faces the lake that surrounds the city. The winery is a two-storey manor on a small hill, with the vineyard circling it in rows and a few storage sheds and single-storey buildings around it. Springvale's spring is in fact melted snow and ice from Dragonspine.

## places
### STANDARD
Springvale (dirt streets, one-storey tan houses with blue-tiled roofs, a windmill above the spring); the Dawn Winery and its manor; Wolvendom with the Wolf of the North challenge arena.
### LARGE +
Wolvendom has an Elemental Monument and a Time Trial Challenge. The Dawn Winery has a Statue of the Seven; its sign carries the words "Whence the flow of daylight comes" above the name and "Forget not whence thou came" below (the wiki transcribes the first as "dalight").

## people
### STANDARD
Springvale: hunters such as Draff, head of the hunters, Allan and Jotun; the cook Brook, the bard Myweiss, and others. Dawn Winery: Diluc, the owner; Adelinde, head housemaid; the winemaker Connor, the warehouse manager Ernest, the farmer Tunner and maids Hillie and Moco. Wolvendom: Boreas and his pack; an NPC named Ross the Quick; and the wolf-raised boy Razor lives in the forest.
### LARGE +
The Executive Chairman of the Mondstadt Wine Guild, Elzer, says the winery is so successful it effectively represents the whole of Mondstadt's wine trade. Several delivery staff (Fritz, Ben, Magar) and the worker Hartman are also named, along with the staff of Angel's Share, Diluc's tavern in the city.

## governance
### STANDARD
No formal authority is shown: Springvale's hunters are led by their head; the Dawn Winery is private property of its owner, the Ragnvindr family; Wolvendom is the territory of Boreas and the pack.

## culture
### STANDARD
Springvale has a long history of hunting, sweet spring water and a legend of a kind spirit in the waters; most residents are hunters who supply fresh meat to Mondstadt's large restaurants, and its rustic charm attracts tourists. The Dawn Winery is almost a place of pilgrimage for lovers of wine. A board in the village praises mushrooms.
### LARGE +
The winery sells part of each year's output in Mondstadt and the rest along the trade route south of the city, and its wines are popular across Teyvat. It is rumoured to use Cryo slimes to keep its wine cellar cool. Wolves' howling can be heard from far away at night.
### ULTRA +
According to Mondstadt's file, young hunters from Springvale hoping to find love catch foxes in Wolvendom during the Windblume Festival and release them wearing flower wreaths.

## history
### STANDARD
The legend of Springvale: a mother brought her gravely ill son to an empty well, where a fairy filled the well with life-saving water. The miracle drew people until a village grew over the spring. The Ragnvindr Clan has owned the Dawn Winery since Ragnvindr, about a thousand years ago, and Diluc inherited it four years before the story began, after his father Crepus died.
### LARGE +
Wolvendom's name is archaic; in the original Chinese it means "fiefdom of running wolves", suggesting that Boreas is a lord rather than a pack leader. Boreas once mistook the first Field Tiller, wandering through the forest, for a challenger and fought it until it fled to Old Mondstadt. Rifthounds began to appear around Wolvendom as of Version 2.4, hinted by Razor's quest The Meaning of Lupical.
### ULTRA +
Per Mondstadt's file, Wild Hunt corruption later followed the ley lines into Wolvendom (Version 6.4 events), and Boreas and Razor guarded the forest while Varka gathered scattered fragments of Andrius' soul in the north.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
In the day, sweet orchards, vineyards, tipsy taverns and the smell of grapes. The forest of Wolvendom is silent and dark, and at night wolves can be heard howling from far away.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Wolvendom's wolves, hilichurls and, later, rifthounds; the unfriendly forest after dark. The Wolf of the North challenge pits fighters against Lupus Boreas, Dominator of Wolves.
### LARGE +
The orchards and winery are peaceful by comparison.

## travel
### LARGE
Springvale lies south of Mondstadt City. The Dawn Winery lies between Springvale and the Stone Gate to Liyue; the winery's goods are sold along the trade route south of the city.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 Windwail Highland is quiet. Diluc runs the winery and Boreas still holds Wolvendom; none of the pages read records a change to the area in 7.0-7.1 content.
### STANDARD +
Diluc, who left the Knights after his father's death, has declined an invitation from Varka to return (see Mondstadt).

## entry: Springvale
@keys: Springvale, Spring Fairy
@keys2: Mondstadt, hunters
### STANDARD
A peaceful hunting village south of Mondstadt City, built over a natural spring beneath its single windmill. Most residents are hunters who supply fresh meat to Mondstadt's restaurants. Legend says a fairy filled an empty well with life-saving water to cure a dying boy; in fact the spring is melted snow from Dragonspine, though an Oceanid from Fontaine really does live in it.

## entry: Dawn Winery
@keys: Dawn Winery, Ragnvindr Clan, Dandelion Wine
@keys2: Diluc, winery, Crepus
### STANDARD
Mondstadt's chief winery and the Ragnvindr family estate, specialising in dandelion wine. It is a two-storey manor on a hill with vineyards around it, southwest of the city, owned by the Ragnvindrs for about a thousand years. Diluc inherited it four years before the story began, after the untimely death of his father Crepus. It is also the base of the taverns, such as Angel's Share, that the family runs.

## entry: Wolvendom
@keys: Wolvendom, Wolf of the North
@keys2: Boreas, Razor, wolf pack
### STANDARD
A deep, dark and silent forest ruled by Boreas, the Wolf of the North of the Four Winds, who also appears as Andrius, with his wolf pack. Locals avoid it; the wolf-raised boy Razor lives there. Even bold hunters do not enter lightly. A challenge arena in the forest lets the Traveler fight Lupus Boreas, Dominator of Wolves.
