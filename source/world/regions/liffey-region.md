## summary
### LITE
The Liffey Region is the part of Fontaine's map that holds the Fortress of Meropide, an underwater stronghold that serves as Fontaine's de facto prison and largest maker of Clockwork Meka. Its Administrator is Wriothesley, and the infirmary is run by Sigewinne. Foothills on the east side of Mont Esus also belong to the region.
### STANDARD +
Its boss is the Millennial Pearl Seahorse. The area is the setting for much of Archon Quest Chapter IV, Acts III to V, and a long run of world quests called Scenes from Life in Meropide.

## geography
### STANDARD
The Fortress sits beneath the waters behind the Opera Epiclese and is reached by a lift down River Talionis. Around and inside it lie an Administrative Area, a Production Zone and an Abandoned Production Zone, a Forbidden Zone, a Geode Mine Shaft and a Dormitory Block. Mont Esus East is the eastern foothills of Mont Esus, which cannot currently be entered.
### LARGE +
The Rift of Erosion lies west of the Dormitory Block and is described by an NPC as a potential danger. The Qualifications Review Representative Office and Bravais' Press Works are further marked buildings.

## places
### STANDARD
- **Fortress of Meropide**: autonomous underwater stronghold; its own building entry gives full detail.
- **Administrative Area / The Duke's Office**: the offices of Administrator Wriothesley.
- **Production Zone and Abandoned Production Zone**: the factory parts of the Fortress; Clockwork Meka are made here.
- **Forbidden Zone**: a restricted area sealed since ancient times, closed to everyone except the warden and trusted people.
- **Pankration Ring**, **Coupon Cafeteria** and **Rag and Bone Shop** are other marked sites inside the Fortress.
### LARGE +
- **Bravais' Press Works**: a marked workplace whose posted texts include Forging Press Device Operation Standards, a Maintenance Log and an Emergency Handbook (third edition).
- **Qualifications Review Representative Office**: a marked office whose notices include an External Reconstruction Support FAQ and a Project Proposal.
- **Mont Esus East**: foothills only.

## people
### STANDARD
Wriothesley is the Fortress's Administrator and Sigewinne, a Melusine nurse, works in its infirmary. Inmates, guards and staff appear throughout the Scenes from Life in Meropide quests. Outside the Fortress, Gardes provide some security support.
### LARGE +
In the Archon Quest the Traveler enters as an 'inmate' on trumped-up charges; Act V recalls a 45-day sentence.

## governance
### STANDARD
Technically an autonomous entity governing itself and outside Fontaine's legal system: criminals sent there are in effect exiles, and the Court of Fontaine only sends some Gardes to help with security. Wriothesley is its Administrator.

## history
### STANDARD
According to the wiki's compilation of the Fortress's lore, a group of exiled criminals first built it after Egeria began her rule in Fontaine. Shunned by society, some of them begged for mercy; Egeria heard them and told them to help protect her secret beneath the waves. The 'secret' was an entrance to the Primordial Sea, which they sealed and labelled the Forbidden Zone.
### LARGE +
The wiki says later groups of exiles continued the work even after the first group had died.
### ULTRA +
In Archon Quest Chapter IV, Neuvillette sends the Traveler to the Fortress on trumped-up charges to investigate (Act III, 4.1). At the start of Act V (4.2), when the Traveler's 45-day stay ends and the prison shakes, Wriothesley says the seal Neuvillette placed earlier would not fail, so the tremor must come from the surface. In Act IV, Calamitous Tread, the Traveler passes through the Infirmary, cafeteria, dormitories, an office and the Forbidden Zone.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
The official map blurb calls the Fortress a metal-walled fastness that has kept a great many secrets over long years.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Fatui Skirmishers, Treasure Hoarders and Clockwork Meka; the Frost and Wind Operatives and Fatui Pyro Agents are elite foes. The Millennial Pearl Seahorse is the region's boss.

## travel
### STANDARD
The lift behind the Opera Epiclese down to River Talionis is unlocked in A Tea Party Most Thorny (4.1). The wiki lists several ways to enter the Fortress for the first time; the Archon Quest sends the Traveler in as an 'inmate'.

## quests
### STANDARD
Archon Quest quests filed under the region include Fortress of Meropide (Act III), Secret Keepers and Forbidden Zones, Calamitous Tread (Act IV), and Deluge of Wrathful Waters (Act V's first quest). The wiki also lists the Scenes from Life in Meropide series, Both Brains and Brawn, Underground Crime, Into the Sunlight, A Moment's Respite, The Proud 'Beret Society' and Wriothesley: Quite the Win.
### LARGE +
Part of the Fontaine Research Institute Chronicles, such as Fontaine Research Institute, Stagnating in the Rubble, also touches this region.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the Fortress still operates under Wriothesley and Sigewinne.
### STANDARD +
Nothing newer than 4.2 was found for this area.
