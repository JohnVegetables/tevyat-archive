## summary
### LITE
The Court of Fontaine Region is the central map area of Fontaine. It holds the capital, the Court of Fontaine, where justice is a public spectacle; the open waters of Salacia Plain; the sunken Institute of Natural Philosophy; the hidden Annapausis; and the Opera Epiclese courthouse on the island of Erinnyes.
### STANDARD +
Much of Fontaine's Archon Quest is set around the capital and the Opera, and a lift behind the Opera leads down to River Talionis and the Fortress of Meropide.

## geography
### STANDARD
The region is both a city and an expanse of sea. The capital stands above the water; Salacia Plain is a calm open sea with clear water; the sunken Institute of Natural Philosophy lies beneath it. Erinnyes is an island carrying the Opera Epiclese, the Fountain of Lucine and Marcotte Station.
### LARGE +
Salacia Plain is described officially as a calm and serene stretch of open sea whose scintillating surface hides memories and secrets. A mysterious power from a statue there lets the Traveler move freely in the water, swimming with Angelic Sea Hares and hearing Blubberbeasts.

## places
### STANDARD
- **Court of Fontaine**: the capital (its own entry). Districts and sites tagged to it include Quartier Lyonnais, Quartier Narbonnais, Vasari Passage, Fleuve Cendre, the Palais Mermonia, Café Lutece, Hotel Debord, Hotel Bouffes d'ete, the Steambird newspaper, Lepaute Horlogerie and Leschots Clockwork Workshop.
- **Opera Epiclese**: a vast opera house used as Fontaine's main courthouse, on the island of Erinnyes. Besides trials it hosts performances such as magic shows.
- **Institute of Natural Philosophy**: a dilapidated underwater research facility, once the base of a group led by Director Dwight Lasker.
- **Annapausis**: a location unlocked during the quest The Narzissenkreuz Adventure; its viewpoint is titled 'Narzissenkreuz Kingdom'.
- **Salacia Plain**: a calm stretch of open, crystal-clear sea.
### LARGE +
- **Fountain of Lucine**: on Erinnyes; newlyweds still toss coins into it to wish for peaceful, happy lives.
- **River Talionis**: the waterway reached by the lift behind the Opera, leading to the Fortress of Meropide.
- **Thalatta Submarine Canyon**, **Alain Guillotin's Resting Place**, **Marcotte Station** and the **Court Region Waterway Hub** (an aquabus station) are other marked sites.

## people
### STANDARD
The capital's people include Neuvillette, the Iudex who presides over trials; other judges from the Palais Mermonia; members of the Gardes; Opera staff; and ordinary Fontainians and Melusines. The Narzissenkreuz Ordo, met at the location of the same name, features in the world quest series In the Wake of Narcissus.
### LARGE +
The Opera has a receptionist and a few named staff. Performers such as Lyney give magic shows there. In the quest An Overnight Fantasyland, Charlotte explains that cases can be assigned to judges other than Neuvillette.

## governance
### STANDARD
The Opera Epiclese is the symbol of Fontaine's trials and judgment. The Oratrice Mecanique d'Analyse Cardinale sits behind the stage below the judge's seat. The Fortress of Meropide, reached from behind the Opera, is technically autonomous from Fontaine's legal system but serves as its prison.

## history
### STANDARD
Official text says the Opera has stood on Erinnyes since the first diluvian period, as a symbol of the new era's commitment to the rule of law. The Institute of Natural Philosophy holds precursors of modern Fontainian technology and the earliest experiments of the Narzissenkreuz Ordo; its ancient logs and notes include natural-history excerpts about Oceanids and Egeria.
### LARGE +
An investigative report in the Institute records suspicion of group activity among its leaders around a so-called 'Master', with no evidence yet of aggression. Natural-history excerpts there retell the legend of Egeria's first tear and the first Oceanid.
### ULTRA +
The excerpts' author concedes the text may be thought unscientific, and notes that sceptics place the Oceanids' origin before Egeria and the realm of Remuria. The text says Oceanids were charged with understanding every form of life; those too unsuited to the continent's water systems stay in Fontaine to soothe human emotions. It adds that Oceanids multiply without pairing: after gaining enough understanding and emotion, one asks the Hydro Archon or an elder for permission to claim an abstract 'thing' and divides into little Oceanids.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Theatrical: the Opera's official blurb says the true and the fantastical, the comedic and the tragic, find a stage there equally. Salacia Plain is calm and clear, and the sunken Institute is a dilapidated underwater ruin.

## dangers
### STANDARD
The sunken Institute's enemies include Hydro Slimes, Area Alert Mek - Ousia, Armored and Sternshield Crabs, Hunter's Rays, a Ruin Guard at the entrance to its last room and a Thundercraven Rifthound Whelp.

## travel
### STANDARD
The Court Region Waterway Hub is a marked aquabus site. Underwater exploration features here include Pneumousia Blocks and Relays, Accelerated Aquaring, Currents and High-Pressure Water Vents. Annapausis is unmarked on the map until The Narzissenkreuz Adventure; the Institute is opened by the quest Aqueous Tidemarks.

## quests
### STANDARD
A Tea Party Most Thorny (Archon Quest Chapter IV, Act III, 4.1) is set in this region and ends with the Opera's rear lift opening to the Fortress of Meropide. World-quest series anchored here include The Narzissenkreuz Adventure, Ann's Story, Aqueous Tidemarks, the Toy War quests, Their Childhood and In the Wake of Narcissus, whose first part, Search in the Algae Sea, starts at the Ordo.
### LARGE +
The region also hosts many commissions, anecdotes and the Tea Party Re-Invitation quests.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the Court of Fontaine Region is intact and in everyday use.
### STANDARD +
Annapausis and the Institute of Natural Philosophy remain explorable, and the Opera remains the nation's courthouse.

## entry: Opera Epiclese
@keys: Opera Epiclese, Oratrice Mecanique d'Analyse Cardinale, Oratrice
@version: 4.0
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
A large opera house on Erinnyes that doubles as Fontaine's main courthouse. Trials are staged there; the Oratrice Mecanique d'Analyse Cardinale sits below the judge's seat. A lift behind the building goes down to River Talionis and the Fortress of Meropide.
### LARGE +
Neuvillette presides at the judge's seat, but other judges from the Palais Mermonia can be assigned. The building also hosts shows by performers such as Lyney.

## entry: Institute of Natural Philosophy
@keys: Institute of Natural Philosophy, Dwight Lasker
@version: 4.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
A sunken research facility that once belonged to an organisation of the same name led by Director Dwight Lasker. Its ruined halls keep early forms of Fontainian technology and the first experiments of the Narzissenkreuz Ordo. The Traveler first explores it in Aqueous Tidemarks while chasing a treasure promised by an overenthusiastic Fontainian.
### LARGE +
Its logs include a natural-history text with a legend of Egeria's tears and the first Oceanid, and an investigative report on a suspected 'Master'.

## entry: Annapausis
@keys: Annapausis, Narzissenkreuz Kingdom
