## summary
### LITE
The Sea of Clouds ("Cloud-Coming Sea") is the southern coastal area of Liyue and the nation's gateway to the world. It holds Liyue Harbor, the great trading city where "a thousand ships" drop anchor and goods from all seven nations change hands under contract; the mountain barrier of Mt. Tianheng that shelters the city from the west; and the island spires of Guyun Stone Forest, where Rex Lapis is said to have pinned a sea god. Its Geo boss is the Geo Hypostasis.
### STANDARD +
The area is Liyue at its most public: crowded wharves, banks and guilds, and a cliff-backed city under the watch of the Qixing and Millelith. Beyond the quay, the sea is old and quiet around the stone islands.

## geography
### STANDARD
Coastline, harbor and mountains in one view. Liyue Harbor sits on the sea with Mt. Tianheng rising to its west and north, making a natural shield; the road north leads out to Guili Plains and the Bishui River's mouth. Out on the water lies Guyun Stone Forest, an archipelago of seven large islands.
### LARGE +
The Bishui River flows through Qiongji Estuary into the sea nearby. The northernmost island at Guyun houses the Geo Hypostasis, and the southwestern island holds the Domain of Guyun. Mt. Tianheng reaches north of the city too: the range along the road to Guili Plains belongs to it.

## places
### STANDARD
Liyue Harbor has three districts: Feiyun Slope, the prosperous merchant district at the northern entrance; Chihu Rock, the lower-class district on an island to the southeast; and Yujing Terrace, the wealthy cliffside residential quarter in the west. Above the city floated the Qixing's Jade Chamber. Guyun Stone Forest lies offshore.
### LARGE +
The Jade Chamber was lost in the battle with Osial (Archon Quest Chapter I) and later rebuilt; it is the airborne seat of the Liyue Qixing and the home of Ningguang. Feiyun Slope includes a Snezhnayan merchant area with Northland Bank.

## people
### LARGE
Merchants, ship owners, sailors, miners, guild clerks, Millelith soldiers, scholars and shopkeepers fill the harbor. The ruling Liyue Qixing and Ningguang oversee trade. Foreign merchants, including Snezhnayans and Fontainians, are regular sights.

## governance
### LARGE
The Liyue Qixing, a council of seven, run the harbor with the Millelith as their army, under what the nation calls a rule of contracts. Rex Lapis, the Geo Archon, founded it but handed rule to the mortals; Zhongli, who is Rex Lapis in a mortal life, now lives among its people.

## culture
### LARGE
Liyue Harbor is a city of contracts, ceremony and festivals; the best known is Lantern Rite, when thousands of lanterns rise into the night. Guyun is considered more a vista than a destination, and its reputation as a "forest of tombstones for the old gods" keeps most locals away.
### ULTRA +
Liyue folk tell the battle differently, but all agree Rex Lapis used the sacred lance Vortex Vanquisher to pierce Osial's rainbow and pin him in the deep sea; Beidou's character story calls the tale of the sea-monster victory a beloved one.

## history
### STANDARD
Before the Archon War, Rex Lapis led his people south of Mt. Tianheng and founded what is now Liyue Harbor. They tried to extend the city to Guili Plains, but after Guizhong died in the war they came back to the harbor. In the same war Rex Lapis defeated the sea god Osial at Guyun and pinned him to the sea floor with stone spears, whose broken remnants are the jagged cliffs there.
### LARGE +
Mt. Tianheng's lore says a clash between Morax and another god nearly toppled it, until the adeptus Skybracer had his antlers, a gift of Morax's power, cut off and wedged under the peak, then fought on until he died. Liyue's earliest mines are believed to have begun in these hills. In the Archon Quest Chapter I the Fatui Harbinger Childe unleashed Osial, who shattered the Guizhong Ballistae and was defeated only when Ningguang dropped the Jade Chamber on him.
### ULTRA +
Zhongli later threw Havria's relics into the waters at Guyun in the story quest A Record of All Things, calling it a homecoming, and told Osial that their ancient grudge was a bygone memory. The Mt. Tianheng legend is also tied to the Bishui River's origin.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Quays, ships and crowded markets in the city, with the road in through Mt. Tianheng arriving suddenly in a trade capital; offshore, the lonely silence of the stone islands, where the sensitive are said to hear a strange stirring rhythm deep in the ocean.

## dangers
### LARGE
The Geo Hypostasis holds the northernmost Guyun island. Fatui agents have operated in the city. Anyone who sleeps at Guyun may relive Osial's defeat.

## travel
### STANDARD
Roads lead north to Guili Plains and the Bishui country and east toward Qiongji Estuary. Ships dock at the harbor from every nation.

## quests
### LARGE
The area hosts a large share of Liyue's world and story quests, notably the Archon Quest Chapter I Act III (Heart of Glaze), Zhongli's story quest, and Lantern Rite events.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 Liyue Harbor is thriving and the Qixing still govern; the Jade Chamber has been rebuilt.
### STANDARD +
Rex Lapis lives on as Zhongli. The 7.1 anomaly of the Celestial Nails is centered on the Chasm, not the harbor.

## entry: Osial and Guyun Stone Forest
@keys: Osial, Guyun Stone Forest, Overlord of the Vortex
@keys2: Rex Lapis, Morax, Liyue, Childe
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Osial, the Overlord of the Vortex, was an ancient sea god who clashed with Morax in the Archon War. At Guyun Stone Forest Rex Lapis pinned him to the sea floor with stone spears, and the jagged cliffs there are the remnants of those lances. Osial could raise tsunamis as tall as Liyue's mountains. In Archon Quest Chapter I the Fatui unleashed him to lure out Rex Lapis and steal his Gnosis; Ningguang crushed him with the Jade Chamber. Locals call Guyun a forest of tombstones for the old gods.

## entry: Skybracer and Mt. Tianheng
@keys: Skybracer, Mt. Tianheng
@keys2: Morax, Liyue Harbor, Bishui River, antlers
### STANDARD
Mt. Tianheng shields Liyue Harbor from the west. In a Lantern Rite telling, during the Archon War an enemy god's blow nearly toppled the peak onto a village at its foot, so the adeptus Skybracer had his antlers chopped off and wedged under the mountain, raising it higher. He fought on until his lifeblood ran out, and that blood became the Bishui River.

## entry: The founding of Liyue Harbor
@keys: Liyue Harbor founding, Guizhong, Guili Assembly
@keys2: Morax, Rex Lapis, Archon War, Liyue
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Before the Archon War, Rex Lapis brought his people south of Mt. Tianheng to found Liyue Harbor. They later tried to move the city out to the Guili Plains, the central area of the Guili Assembly ruled by Guizhong, God of Dust, and Morax, but after Guizhong died in the war they returned to the harbor. The harbor launched Liyue's seafaring trade and is called Teyvat's largest market harbor.
