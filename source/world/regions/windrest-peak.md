## summary
### LITE
Windrest Peak is the northernmost part of Mondstadt, opened in Version 6.5, and revolves around the port town of Dornman Port, one of the continent's major trade hubs. It also holds Millhaven, an idyllic windmill village of windrest-flower fields and fragrance makers, and the Old Sanatorium Site, where a hilltop sanatorium once stood. High above the peak floats the Temple of Space. The weather is clearer and more varied than in the rest of Mondstadt.
### STANDARD +
Long ago the Arcadian city-state of Elysium stood in these mountains under the Gunnhildr Clan, and the area later became that clan's fief. The Knights of Favonius keep a base at the port.

## geography
### STANDARD
Mountain country north of Cider Lake, running down to a harbour. Dornman Port sits on the coast, Millhaven in its fields inland, and the Old Sanatorium Site on a hill above. Local plants include calla lily, dandelion seed, etherwing moth, valberry and windwheel aster. Unlike the south, it has varied clouds and clearer skies.
### LARGE +
The Temple of Space is a separate area in the sky above, with its own map tab; the wiki says the mountain breezes here are fierce because, by decree of the temple's owner, no wind may enter it. A Time Trial Challenge lies at ruins just north of Dornman Port. The Knights of Favonius Ruins lie underground beneath the port.

## places
### STANDARD
Dornman Port (the harbour, bazaar, taverns and a Knights base); Millhaven; the Old Sanatorium Site; the Knights of Favonius Ruins beneath the port; the Temple of Space overhead; the Thorny Crown of the Mountain Wind domain.
### LARGE +
Dornman Port has a bulletin board, a ducks-feeding spot and Millhaven has its own bulletin board and a set of Dodoco doodles. The Thorny Crown of the Mountain Wind (added in Version 6.6) is a hidden floral domain in the mountains, whose text mourns the "dream of Elysium", the city-state sealed in its coffin.

## people
### STANDARD
Dornman Port: sailors, merchants and Knights of Favonius, among them Vice Captain Lohen, stationed there. Millhaven: fragrance-makers; a young self-styled Witch Hunter, Prune, has posted a notice on a board. Old Sanatorium Site: the doorman Mann, who oversees demolition and heritage preservation. Henry Morton and Liben are Dornman Port NPCs.
### LARGE +
Local legends of the area include the monster Churldric, and a normal boss, The Open-Eyed, roams it.
### ULTRA +
The Dornman Port pages also record Hannelore, Insigne, Struwell, Moricztill, Max, Marthe and Jones, minor named NPCs; Jones was a Dornman Port labourer who ended up trapped in Ritou by Inazuma's closed borders.

## governance
### STANDARD
Dornman Port belonged to the Gunnhildr Clan as a hereditary fief in the aristocratic era. Today the Knights of Favonius keep order from a base at the port; their Reconnaissance Company patrols the trade road to the capital. Docking legally requires an entry permit.
### LARGE +
After Vennessa's rebellion the Gunnhildr Clan, which sided with the people, led the push to abolish hereditary fiefs and likely set an example by handing its own over to Mondstadt.

## culture
### STANDARD
A cosmopolitan port that welcomes steady merchant shipping from many nations; known for cargo shipping and flower cultivation, with taverns where sailors drink. Millhaven is peaceful and sweet-scented, its economy tied to windrest flowers and fragrances. Dornman Port has had no church since the late aristocratic era.
### LARGE +
Nobody is quite sure how the port got its name. A trade route to Port Ormos in Sumeru has opened, and a company in the port plans to export fruit that others doubt will survive the voyage. A saying about the sanatorium holds that anyone who visits it stays among the enchanted hills for ever.

## history
### STANDARD
In the age of Great Arcadia, the city-state of Elysium stood in these mountains under the Gunnhildr Clan. After Arcadia fell the Gunnhildrs took refuge in Decarabian's city; once Barbatos scattered the snow they returned north, and the land became their fief and, in time, Dornman Port, with Mondstadt's very first sacred sanctuary to Barbatos. Later, in the dark age of the aristocracy, they threw a bishop sent from the capital out of the sanctuary's tower and tore it down to signal a break with the tyrannical Imunlaukr Clan; the port has had no church since.
### LARGE +
During the Cataclysm 500 years ago the port stood on the brink of destruction, and many knights died defending it; their graves lie beneath the city in what is now the Knights of Favonius Ruins, which the wiki calls an ancient battlefield where the Knights fought the Abyss. More recently the Knights' Reconnaissance Company traced a hidden Abyss Order cargo to the port, where it was about to be shipped to Liyue Harbor; the scholar Yanfei, unwitting consultant to the ship, was caught up in it and Eula helped her capture the Abyss operatives nearby.
### ULTRA +
Recently Grand Master Varka ordered several knights from his expedition, among them Frederica Gunnhildr, back to help defend the port. Mona's parents moved to Dornman Port, she was born there and found Barbeloth, and she later settled in the capital.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Ships and cargo at the port, tavern noise, clouds driven across the hills, and the sweet smell of windrest flowers around Millhaven. Its viewpoint is titled The Harbor Where a Thousand Winds Converge, and the area's own, The Northerly Wind's Soft Hymn.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Smuggling and Abyss Order activity at the port, and the wind-swept heights themselves.
### LARGE +
The Temple of Space is dangerous in its own right: the Watchers Nihilita and Melanta and various Segment-Domain Keepers enforce the owner's rules inside.

## travel
### LARGE
Reached overland north of Cider Lake or by sea. The port is far from the capital; the Reconnaissance Company patrols the trade road in between, especially during Weinlesefest. The Old Sanatorium Site has a map marker through which the Temple of Space can be entered once the world quest series A Long Day in the Mountains has begun.

## quests
### LARGE
World quest series: A Long Day in the Mountains (6.5), set in Mondstadt and the Temple of Space, which draws on Thomas Mann's novel The Magic Mountain; and the Knights of Favonius Ruins quest Winds Beneath the Tower of Silence.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 Windrest Peak is open, busy and calm; no 7.0-7.1 event is recorded in the pages read.
### STANDARD +
The Temple of Space remains hanging above the peak, and the old sanatorium on the hill has been demolished. The Knights keep a base at the port, where Lohen has been stationed.

## entry: Dornman Port
@keys: Dornman Port, Dornman, Thorn King
@version: 6.5
### STANDARD
A bustling harbour town north of Cider Lake and one of the continent's major trade hubs, welcoming merchant ships from many nations. Once the Gunnhildr Clan's fief, with Mondstadt's first sanctuary of Barbatos (since torn down, so no church remains). Famous for cargo shipping and flower cultivation. Mona was born here; the Reconnaissance Company patrols the road between port and capital. Some say the port is named for the Thorn King, a ruler lost to history, others for an ancestor of the Gunnhildrs, others for a madman in a crown of thorns.

## entry: Millhaven
@keys: Millhaven, windrest flower
@version: 6.5
### STANDARD
A peaceful windmill village known for its fields of windrest flowers and its fragrance products. A notice on the village board by the self-styled Witch Hunter Prune accuses a witch codenamed "A" of bullying; replies tell the writer to go to Jean about a lost pet, and one thinks the target sounds like Alice. Doodles of Dodoco bombs around the village involve Klee.

## entry: Old Sanatorium Site
@keys: Old Sanatorium Site, sanatorium
@keys2: Windrest Peak, Mann, Koche, Bauer
@version: 6.5
### STANDARD
A hilltop site where a sanatorium was torn down a few years ago. It had been built on the ruins left when the Temple of Space was lifted into the sky. Its doorman Mann oversees demolition and heritage preservation. Notes left behind show patients swapping a burial plot and offering free tombstone-carving; a gravestone reads "One lifetime doesn't count." The place is the map marker entrance to the Temple of Space, after A Long Day in the Mountains begins.

## entry: Temple of Space
@keys: Temple of Space, Segment-Domain Tau, Hall of Emptiness, Nihilita, Melanta
@keys2: Asmoday, Windrest Peak
@version: 6.5
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Created by Asmoday, Ruler of Space, as Segment-Domain Tau, an exhibition hall preserving records of fallen civilisations (Hyperborea, Lang-Gan, Delphi Pytho, Ochkanatlan, Remuria and Arcadia) in books, paintings, landscapes and selected individuals called the Uplifted. Long ago it adjoined the temple where Arcadian survivors worshipped Istaroth, "mother of the thousand winds"; after an unknown disaster it was lifted into the sky and hidden, leaving Istaroth's temple behind to decay. Its Watchers Nihilita and Melanta enforce the decree that the temple exist only to preserve, with innovation forbidden. Time stands still inside.
### LARGE +
The temple has three exhibition areas: Luyang Academy (painting, inspired by ancient Liyue), the Mahavaipulya Chamber (books) and the Desert Pavilion (modelled on Sumeru's desert temples). The Uplifted go through the Apathic Interval, giving up desire to leave it, and may leave the temple only by building the Path to the Heavens across the Corridor of Nothingness, which costs all their memories and wisdom. Wind is forbidden within, so it lingers in the mountains outside.

## entry: Elysium and the Gunnhildr Fief
@keys: Elysium, Thorny Crown of the Mountain Wind, Great Arcadia
@keys2: Windrest Peak, Gunnhildr
@spoiler: minor
### ULTRA
Elysium was a city-state of Great Arcadia built in what is now Windrest Peak and controlled by the Gunnhildr Clan. After Arcadia's fall the clan fled to Decarabian's city; after Barbatos cleared the snow they returned north and the region became their fief. The hidden floral domain Thorny Crown of the Mountain Wind (Version 6.6) is described in-game as a place where the ancient people of Elysium believed their sealed city would flourish for ever, and where now only poetry and flowers remain in the wind.

## entry: Alchemy Showcase Anecdote
@keys: Alchemical Ascension, Beidou
@keys2: Dornman Port, Eula, The Crux
@availability: limited
### ULTRA
During a limited-time event (the story the wiki cites as Alchemical Ascension), the Knights' Reconnaissance Company stepped up patrols after attempts to smuggle goods through the port. After the ship The Crux docked she met Captain Beidou and her crew in a Dornman Port tavern; they cleared up a misunderstanding about a bar fight Beidou had quelled, and Beidou treated her to a drink and became her friend. They travelled together to the capital to see the showcase, and Eula later accompanied Beidou back to the port. This was an event story only.
