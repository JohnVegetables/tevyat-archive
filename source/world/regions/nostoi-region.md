## summary
### LITE
The Nostoi Region is a southern island area of Fontaine that sits outside the raised continental plate the rest of the nation stands on, so the waters around it cannot be dived into. It contains Petrichor, a beautiful town known for very pure water and a view of the great waterfall that rings Fontaine, and the sunken Faded Castle, an old Remurian ruin that leads down to the Sea of Bygone Eras.
### STANDARD +
The name Nostoi echoes the ancient Greek 'Returns', an epic of kings going home after Troy.

## geography
### STANDARD
An island in the south of Fontaine. Petrichor lies on it, in the vicinity of ruins and stone statues of the Golden Troupe of Remuria. Underneath, the Faded Castle is accessible from Petrichor's deep spring. Because the island lies off the continental plate, the surrounding water is not diveable like the rest of Fontaine's.
### LARGE +
The region's exploration objects include a Mysterious Bookshelf Full of Grimoires, Mysterious Grimoires and the Symphony puzzle device. A bell tower in Petrichor carries an ancient inscription (Juliano's Legacy), and a tattered navigation log lies south of the town by a crashed boat after Canticles of Harmony.

## places
### STANDARD
- **Petrichor**: a town on an island in southern Fontaine, popular with tourists for the encircling waterfall.
- **Faded Castle**: a sunken ruin that was part of the kingdom of Remuria. Harmosts and scholars lived there, maintaining archives and researching music theory and fate. It holds the entrance to the Sea of Bygone Eras.
### LARGE +
Faded Castle has lower, middle and higher levels and a portal that links it to the Sea of Bygone Eras, which has its own map and exploration progress.
### ULTRA +
Books found in the castle include Harmost's Notes and Investigation of the Northern Barbarians; the latter mentions a sword used to choose a Holy King and a 'Mother of Many Springs'. The levels also hold a mural that can be played as a symphony, paintings of Domus Aurea and Praetorian Golems.

## people
### STANDARD
Petrichor's residents include Tiepolo, a 'Doge'; Foscari, the Doge's assistant; Bossuet, a former Senior Researcher of the Fontaine Research Institute; Babisse and Mécantre, chiefs for the Teyvat Grand Cannon construction and design; and Grundeland, a 'Good Merchant'.
### LARGE +
A local cat named Osse leads the Traveler to the castle; the former Harmost Cassiodor and the shadowy Boethius appear in the quests.

## history
### STANDARD
The Faded Castle was once known as the 'golden castellum' in Remuria's golden age. Former Harmost Cassiodor says he and companions recorded ancient scripts, scores and stories there to harness the power of fate. He opposed Boethius's wish to revive Remuria at the cost of Fontaine and sealed the passage between the castle (and the Sea of Bygone Eras) and the surface.
### LARGE +
In The Shadow Over Petrichor (4.6), strange events in Petrichor lead the Traveler and Paimon through ruins in a mountain to the deep spring and the castle, where Cassiodor lifts the seal and they meet Boethius.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
The 4.6 display page says there are 'many beautiful and moving legends' about Petrichor, and its viewpoint is titled 'The Lovely Legends of Petrichor'. The Faded Castle's viewpoints are titled 'Stage of Faded Gold' and 'Secret Locale of the Ancient Order'.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Praetorian Golems revive from statues and guard the ruins; treasure hoarders and Fatui agents appear in quests.

## travel
### STANDARD
Petrichor has a western Waverider Waypoint. The Faded Castle is entered through the deep spring after the quest chain begins, and the Sea of Bygone Eras is opened after Underwater Nocturne.

## quests
### STANDARD
Canticles of Harmony is the main series: Prelude 'Petrichorror Dream' (The Shadow Over Petrichor), First Movement 'In the Hall of the Sea King' (Underwater Nocturne) and Second Movement 'Prisoner in Shackles' (The Last Day of Remuria). Follow-ups include For Yesterday and Tomorrow and Latecoming Homecoming.
### LARGE +
The first quest requires the Prologue's Song of the Dragon and Freedom.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 Petrichor is peaceful and the castle's secrets have been opened up.
### STANDARD +
No newer content than 4.6 was found for this region.

## entry: Petrichor
@keys: Petrichor
@version: 4.6
### STANDARD
A town on an island of southern Fontaine. Famed for its beauty and unusually pure water, it is a tourist spot for seeing the huge waterfall encircling Fontaine. The name is said to mean 'golem' in an ancient language. Ruins and statues linked to the Golden Troupe of Remuria stand nearby.

## entry: Faded Castle
@keys: Faded Castle, golden castellum
@version: 4.6
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
A sunken Remurian castle where Harmosts and scholars once kept archives and studied music theory and fate. Cassiodor sealed the passage to the surface; in 4.6 he lifts the seal and the Traveler meets Boethius. Beyond it lies the Sea of Bygone Eras.
