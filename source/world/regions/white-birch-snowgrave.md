## summary
### LITE
White Birch Snowgrave was the itinerant court of the Belyi Tsar and became his mausoleum after he died in the Cataclysm five hundred years ago. Its palace, the Pale Crown Palace, is both palace and ruin: once the seat from which the ancient Tsars ruled, now their final resting place. From it sprang the giant Tsar's White Birch, a sign that the Tsar still watches over Snezhnaya.
### STANDARD +
The palace stands hidden beneath the great birch. Within lies the Hvergelmir research facility where the Eye of Graeae was created, and a beast called Zver still guards the Tsar's body.

## geography
### STANDARD
A snow-buried crown land of drifts, ruins and a giant white tree. The Pale Crown Palace's gates are choked with snow, so visitors must find another way in; inside are the Frozen Royal Hall, hanging corridors and a spiral tower. A hidden railway leads to it, and a train stop there is described as one at which no one arrives, a snow-covered grave.
### LARGE +
The Tsar's White Birch and its monuments dominate the area; the Tsar's White Birch drains energy from the Eye of Graeae if someone stands close. Local specialties: Frostfairy Flower. The area also holds Kresnik's Torch, Kresnik Kristall and Shadow Realm content.

## places
### STANDARD
Pale Crown Palace: the Tsar's mausoleum and former palace, with the Frozen Royal Hall and the first Kresnik's Torch. The hidden Hvergelmir research facility lies within it. The palace also holds six readable "Notes by a Certain Noble Individual".
### LARGE +
Pavlina kept a laboratory at the palace, and the facility holds a sealed egg containing a being called the Proto-Man, plus strange alchemical apparatus.

## people
### STANDARD
The Belyi Tsar lies buried beneath the birch. Zver, a two-headed beast, guards his remains. The researchers Izmaylov and Azazel appear in a vision of the past.
### LARGE +
Aksinya, the Tsar's loyal servant and a Hvergelmir alchemist, and the bio-alchemist Koshchei, who created the chimeric monsters, are tied to the palace. Zver began as a failed prototype Koshchei abandoned until the Tsar took it in and gave it purpose.

## history
@version: 7.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The palace was a place where the Tsar welcomed humans alongside the fae, and where the flame of Kresnik was first kindled before spreading across the land. After the Tsar died fighting the Abyss and waves of beasts, five hundred years ago, his remains were returned here and part of his body became the Tsar's White Birch.
### LARGE +
Hvergelmir, the institute housed in the palace, studied bio-alchemy for the Tsar's aim of reaching the vacant seat in the court of the stars. Researchers feared the new Tsaritsa would ban alchemy and halt their work, and an earlier experiment had gone disastrously.
### ULTRA +
Aksinya, Koshchei, Pavlina and Alvis were tasked with the work; the Eye of Graeae persuaded the other three to stop Aksinya, who was cast into the Shadow Realm, and the Eye was split into three fragments. In the quests, the Traveler and the Eye recover the fragment inside the palace, learn that the Eye is itself a prototype built to house a "Key", and are told to bring all fragments to the "Court of Bliss". A wiki summary says that later the Eye's restored consciousness was placed in the Tsar's body, but this is unconfirmed in this entry.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
A cold hush of snow-muffled halls, stone beasts and cracked royal chambers, with the pale branches of an enormous tree glimmering above.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Rugged Elder Leshy elites; winged halberd-knights and soarer knights; glacial wardens and glaciomancers; the guardian Zver; puzzle-locked doors and statues.

## travel
### STANDARD
A hidden railway is summoned by a mechanism; an old prototype train operated by the Eye of Graeae takes the Traveler there.

## quests
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
The world quest series "On One Side a Palace, On the Other a Tomb": "To Gaze Upon the Dreaming Pale Crown" and "The Stone Beasts, Like Ghouls Seated". It forms part of the Eye of Graeae's search for its scattered power.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the grave is quiet, with the Tsar's White Birch still standing.
### STANDARD +
Zver was defeated in the quest, but the palace itself remains a mausoleum.

## entry: Pale Crown Palace
@keys: Pale Crown Palace, Pale Crown, Frozen Royal Hall
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
The Pale Crown Palace is the Belyi Tsar's mausoleum, once one of his palaces, where he is buried beneath the soil. Inside the Frozen Royal Hall stands the very first Kresnik's Torch, which must be struck to restart. The palace contains the old Hvergelmir laboratories.

## entry: Tsar's White Birch
@keys: Tsar's White Birch, White Birch
@keys2: Snezhnaya, Belyi Tsar, Pale Crown Palace
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
The Tsar's White Birch is a giant tree that rose where the Belyi Tsar was buried. Official text calls it his body transformed, protecting everyone in Snezhnaya; the wiki describes it as an overgrowth of nerves from his corpse. It is an exploration mechanic that drains energy from the Eye of Graeae if the Traveler stands too close.

## entry: Zver
@keys: Zver
@keys2: Pale Crown Palace, Belyi Tsar, Koshchei
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
Zver is a two-headed beast, once a failed chimeric prototype abandoned by Koshchei, which the Belyi Tsar treated as a loyal companion. It has guarded his corpse for centuries and hates those who wronged its master. After being defeated, it warns the Traveler that the Eye of Graeae may not be wholly trustworthy.
