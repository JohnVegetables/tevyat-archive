## summary
### LITE
The Volkodlak Tundra is called the "Tundra of Ancient Beasts". It is said to have been the site of the capital of the Miscislavli Alliance, a confederation ruled by the great Volkodlak lord Miscislav. When the Belyi Tsar's spear pierced Miscislav's head and his horsemen scattered the alliance, everything here was demolished and rebuilt, with no trace left of the old city. The Archon Quest of Snezhnaya begins in the tundra, at the village of Rankova Zorya.
### STANDARD +
The in-game description says the ordinarily merciful Tsar seemed almost afraid that something might arise from this place again.

## geography
### STANDARD
Open snow-covered tundra with scattered settlements and rail lines. Rankova Zorya, a village built around an abandoned train station, is its best known place. The only boss recorded for the area is a Chimeric Winged Lion.
### LARGE +
Rankova Zorya has been revived by Alyosha, Odette and Vodyanitsa with the Traveler's help, with a restaurant, a bulletin board and snowmen that can be decorated with hats and scarves. A Jack Frost sits on a cliff north of the village, restrained by a grappling gun, in a puzzle listed on the neighbouring area's page.

## places
### STANDARD
Rankova Zorya: a small village around a former train station. The old Volkodlak capital: a vanished site, rebuilt beyond recognition.

## history
### STANDARD
About 3,000 years ago the Volkodlak were organised in the Miscislavli Alliance under King Miscislav, the "Blood-Devourer", to whom the Vila and others paid tribute. The Belyi Tsar (Monomakh Snezhnyi) of the Pale Star Court persuaded the Vila to stop paying; Miscislav demanded vast reparations and went to war.
### LARGE +
Miscislav gathered clans of the alliance and allies and marched on the Tsar's city Kitezh, but poor tactics and bad weather doomed three campaigns. In the third, the Tsar hurled his spear through Miscislav's skull and killed him, then took the title Belyi Tsar and became the undisputed leader of Snezhnaya. Miscislav's skull was kept in the Zapolyarny Palace treasury. Some say he tried to invoke the Shadow Realm in his last battle.
### ULTRA +
Miscislav's family survived and held some power in the Tsar's reign. Volkodlak remain a hostile fae in modern Snezhnaya, appearing as raiders, bandits and elites in many areas.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Wide, windy snowfields, old battlefields beneath the snow and trains stopping at half-forgotten stations.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Volkodlak war bands and bandit gangs, winged chimeric beasts such as the Chimeric Winged Lion, blizzards and cold.

## entry: Rankova Zorya
@keys: Rankova Zorya
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
Rankova Zorya is a small village built around an abandoned train station. With the Traveler's help, Alyosha, Odette and Vodyanitsa restore it and life returns; it gains a restaurant, a news stand (Vestnik) and a bulletin board. Alyosha's dog Lyumina lives there. The Archon Quest "Great Deeds on the Tundra" starts with the Traveler boarding a train here to reach Snezhnograd.

## entry: Miscislav and the Miscislavli Alliance
@keys: Miscislav, Miscislavli Alliance, Blood-Devourer, Gorynishche
@spoiler: minor
### LARGE
Miscislav was King of the Volkodlak and ruler of the Miscislavli Alliance, a rival of Monomakh Snezhnyi. His head was said to be the size of a chariot, earning him the fae name "Gorynishche", meaning Son of the Mountain. His death in battle at Monomakh's hand let Monomakh consolidate rule over Snezhnaya.
