## summary
### LITE
The Tezcatepetonco Range is the highland home of the Masters of the Night-Wind (Mictlan), Natlan's shamans and mystics, who speak with the Wayob, watch over the ley lines and Phlogiston, read futures and heal. The tribe is reclusive and eccentric and has the deepest bond with the Night Kingdom of any Natlan tribe. A fog-covered island, Tecoloapan Bay, lies off the range. In Nahuatl, Tezcatepetonco means 'mirror on the hill'.
### STANDARD +
Visitors from other tribes come here for fortune-telling, blessings on tools and weapons, and the old arts of Phlogiston engraving. The best-known shaman, Citlali, lives in the range.

## geography
### STANDARD
Rocky highlands in Natlan where the tribe's settlement sits around the Sacrificial Altar of Enigmatic Land. Tecoloapan Bay is an island lying to the west of the range. Xalac Vale, a river valley, runs from the Stadium of the Sacred Flame to the Flower-Feather Clan, passing north of the shamans' settlement. Local specialties: glowing hornshroom and sprayfeather gill.
### LARGE +
Parts of the range are muted into grey by Pale Mist, which strips colour from the land until nearby stone monuments are painted correctly and activated. A cave southwest holds the Trial of Disembodiment.

## places
### STANDARD
The settlement of the Masters of the Night-Wind and its Chamber of Weaving; the Sacrificial Altar of Enigmatic Land; Citlali's Home; Idukan's Trove, a bookstore run by Amazolli; Scorchwind Trial Grounds; Xalac Vale; and Tecoloapan Bay.
### LARGE +
The Scorchwind Trial Grounds south of the settlement host the Pakpaka Flying School, which teaches flying with Qucusaurs and seems to be run by the Flower-Feather Clan. A notice in Xalac Vale advertises the school. Tecoloapan Bay's island is initially hidden in dense fog and listed on the map only as '???'.
### ULTRA +
Three damaged stone tablets, 'Stone Tablet Amidst Gray Ash' I to III, lie in the range: one northeast of the Statue of the Seven, one west of the settlement and one in a cave above the Trial of Disembodiment. The first two can only be read once the Pale Mist is lifted.

## people
### STANDARD
Chief Biram presides at the Sacrificial Altar of Enigmatic Land. Caholom is the High Priest, Tlapo the Iktomisaurus elder and Cip a guard; Toltec and Yolotli also work at the altar and the Chamber of Weaving. Citlali is the tribe's most experienced shaman and has created many spells of her own.
### LARGE +
Their saurian companions are Iktomisaurs. Most Mictlan members seen outside are travelling doctors. Some tribe members have no spiritual sensitivity at all and are called 'spirit-duffers'. The playable Citlali has her home here.

## culture
### STANDARD
Their main role is as shamans who conduct rituals to talk with the Wayob for knowledge and guidance, and they are also storytellers, record-keepers, doctors and Phlogiston engravers. The Mictlan can perceive Phlogiston and souls, and they cast spells for healing, telekinesis, concealment and extracting memories.
### LARGE +
Their spells depend on skill and training. Citlali explains that all of the tribe's spells and problem-solving methods are based on dealing with spirits, so some spiritual sensitivity is required. Unlike other Natlanese, shamans can sense their connection to the Wayob and the Night Kingdom, and whether they are far enough from home to sever it.
### ULTRA +
The Mictlan tribe was founded shortly after the Archon War through the joint efforts of the Priest-King Maghan and his master, a young mystic.

## history
@version: 5.2
### STANDARD
Five hundred years ago the Border Master Sanhaj of this tribe joined Tenoch's coalition and died with him at the Mare Jivari, when the two used the engraved body of Tenoch to raise a miniature Night Kingdom inside the Bakunawa. A former chief, Ayizu, fell in the cataclysm; with his last words he told the ally he had fought beside, 'the Captain', the secret of the Gnosis.
### LARGE +
Damaged stone tablets in the range tell of an older disaster: the Sacred Flame burned away the 'pitch-black foe', but its embers floated like clouds, and a canyon in the sea, Ochkanatlan, was covered by flowing ashes that destroyed colour. Not even the High Priests of the tribe could fix this 'purging of color'. In a later tablet the sages in white engraved golden inscriptions like rings upon a stone dragon, and priests who venerated it each day kept Cinder City from being covered again, though the ash was never fully removed.
### ULTRA +
Version 5.3 added a new waypoint in the range along with a new boss, the Wayward Hermetic Spiritspeaker.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Hushed and uncanny: rocky heights, grey stone where the Pale Mist lies, glowing hornshrooms, and shamans who work with spirits. Tecoloapan's fog adds to the mood.

## dangers
### STANDARD
The boss is the Wayward Hermetic Spiritspeaker. The Pale Mist hides inscriptions and drains colour. Tecoloapan Bay holds Abyss-corrupted Nightsoul enemies.
### LARGE +
In The Mystery of Tecoloapan Beach two voices speak: a weak one asks the Traveler to break four seals on the island and a menacing whisper warns against it. The weak voice is the true, weakened Wayob manifestation; the whisper belongs to an Abyss-corrupted mimic trying to take over the spirit within this 'puppet'. The central seal had cut the Nightsouls off from the Lord of the Night's realm, and breaking it lets them return to rest.

## travel
### LARGE
The NPC Sakaw is posted near the waypoint on the range's western end. Tecoloapan Bay is reached by crossing the water.

## quests
@version: 5.2
### LARGE
World quest: The Mystery of Tecoloapan Beach (clearing the fog and the seals), started by approaching the fogbound island. See also the entry on the limited-time event Iktomi Spiritseeking Scrolls.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the tribe is at peace, and Tecoloapan Bay is free of fog and seals.
### STANDARD +
Natlan's war with the Abyss ended in 5.3, and the tribe can leave Natlan without loss of memory. Four Masters of the Night-Wind were potential casualties of the earlier invasion depending on player choice: Iyoba, Mayahuel, Osawe and Waqar.
### ULTRA +
The wiki lists no changes to the range in 6.x or 7.x.

## entry: Masters of the Night-Wind
@keys: Masters of the Night-Wind, Mictlan, Night-Wind, Iktomisaurus, spirit-duffers
@keys2: Tezcatepetonco, Natlan
@version: 5.2
### STANDARD
The Masters of the Night-Wind (Mictlan) are one of Natlan's six tribes, shamans who talk with the Wayob and tend Natlan's ley lines and Phlogiston. Their chief is Biram, and their saurian companions are Iktomisaurs.

## entry: Tecoloapan Bay
@keys: Tecoloapan Bay, Tecoloapan Beach, Admonisher's Records, Obsidian Totem Pole
@version: 5.2
### LARGE
Initially covered in fog and marked '???' on the map, Tecoloapan Bay is a forbidden ceremonial site. A damaged record tells visitors to leave quickly, to beware deceptive bewitchment and never to look, and shows the half-sane carvings of the writer, who wanted to seal something. A Nightsoul totem there says the long-lost Obsidian Totem Pole cannot respond. Completing the World Quest The Mystery of Tecoloapan Beach disperses the fog, and the true name of the island is shown.

## entry: Iktomi Spiritseeking Scrolls
@keys: Iktomi Spiritseeking Scrolls, Night-Wind Letter, Iktomi Scroll
@keys2: Citlali, Masters of the Night-Wind
@version: 5.2
@availability: limited
### LARGE
The limited-time event Iktomi Spiritseeking Scrolls begins with the story quest Night-Wind Letter at the Masters of the Night-Wind settlement. It features Citlali, Ororon and Leyla, and it is the source of the account that Sanhaj died in the Mare Jivari explosion that destroyed the Bakunawa. Because it was an event, not every Natlanese or visitor will know its contents.
