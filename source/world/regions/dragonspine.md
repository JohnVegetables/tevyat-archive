## summary
### LITE
Dragonspine is a huge snow-covered mountain in Mondstadt, one of the most dangerous places in the nation: its snow never melts and its cold kills the unprepared. It was once called Vindagnyr, a green mountain where the ancient kingdom of Sal Vindagnyr flourished until a Celestial Nail, the Skyfrost Nail, fell on it and buried it in endless winter. About five hundred years ago the Abyssal dragon Durin fell dead here after his battle with Dvalin, giving the mountain its present name. The alchemist Albedo keeps a research camp on its slopes.
### STANDARD +
Durin's poisonous blood seeped into the mountain's ley lines, producing the warm, toxic red crystals called Scarlet Quartz and reviving the shattered sacred tree of Sal Vindagnyr as the Frostbearing Tree. Few adventurers attempt the mountain; those who do rest at a camp at its foot.

## geography
### STANDARD
A single vast massif beyond Windwail Highland, near the border with Liyue, whose sharp summit (its design modelled on the Matterhorn) can be seen from much of northern Liyue. Its subareas are the Snow-Covered Path (the lower approach and the Frostbearing Tree), Entombed City - Outskirts and Entombed City - Ancient Palace (the buried ruins of Sal Vindagnyr), Starglow Cavern (a great cave at the mountain's heart), Wyrmrest Valley (Durin's bones and heart) and the Skyfrost Nail at the summit. A river and small islands lie on its northern side.
### LARGE +
The whole mountain lies under a "Subzero Climate": cold builds up steadily, faster in snowstorms, in certain parts of the mountain and in icy water, and only fires, torches and other heat sources, or Scarlet Quartz, hold it off. Blocks of patterned "Ancient Rime" that ordinary fire cannot melt choke many paths; only Scarlet Quartz breaks them. Blue plants that glow in the dark (called Starglow) grow around the Rime and through Starglow Cavern, a peculiarity the Celestial Nail's power is believed to cause. In Wyrmrest Valley Durin's life force has dyed the snow red.

## places
### LARGE
- Dragonspine Adventurer Camp: a rest stop on the outskirts, on the path from Dadaupa Gorge, built by the cook Harris; the weaponsmith Orban, the researcher Stevens and the adventurer Iris work there.
- Albedo's Campsite: a small cave laboratory between the Ancient Palace and the Outskirts, with torches, a cooking pot, worktables, shelves of research material and an alchemy bench.
- Frostbearing Tree: the revived sacred tree of Sal Vindagnyr, crimson-marked instead of blue.
- Wyrmrest Valley: Durin's skull, ribcage and still-warm heart.
- Entombed City: the buried palace and outskirts of Sal Vindagnyr, including the Mural Room where Imunlaukr left his greatsword.
- Skyfrost Nail and the Peak of Vindagnyr: the summit, where the restored Nail hovers above the old ritual site.
### ULTRA +
A Statue of the Seven stands near the Ancient Palace. A stone monument west of the Outskirts appears to honour someone (the scribe's box is recovered there), and a grave near Starglow Cavern's entrance is likely the princess'; on the little island east of the Frostbearing Tree, where she died, a sword stands in the ground beneath a tree.

## people
### LARGE
No one lives on Dragonspine permanently. Albedo, the Knights of Favonius' Chief Alchemist, works there for long stretches, often joined by his assistant Sucrose or the alchemist Timaeus. The adventurer camp hosts adventurers and researchers, and the Knights post a guard who keeps inexperienced climbers from heading for the summit. Researchers such as the environmentalist Esther survey the slopes, and Fatui squads lurk in Wyrmrest Valley.
### ULTRA +
Albedo told the court during Paralogism that he had been stationed on Dragonspine to keep watch over Durin's remains. Joel, a boy whose father Joserf vanished on the mountain, waited at the camp until Joserf, found alive by the adventurer Reckless Pallad, returned with most of his memories lost.

## history
### STANDARD
Several thousand years ago refugees fleeing "a land of snow and strife" found a lush green mountain, raised a monument on it and founded Sal Vindagnyr, which prospered under heavenly blessing around a great white tree. Then the Skyfrost Nail fell from the heavens onto the summit's ritual site; snowstorms buried the kingdom, the Nail split into three, a fragment shattered the sacred tree, and the people died. Five hundred years ago, during the cataclysm, Barbatos and Dvalin lured the rampaging dragon Durin over the mountain and Dvalin killed him; Durin's body fell into what is now Wyrmrest Valley, and the mountain became Dragonspine.
### LARGE +
About a thousand years ago, at the end of the aristocratic era, Mondstadt's nobles sent an expedition to uncover the mountain's secrets; it was destroyed from within by Eberhart and none of its members came home. In the Traveler's time no one had reached the summit until the Traveler freed the three Nail shards from their ice (In the Mountains, 1.2) and the restored Nail rose above the peak. Albedo's dealings with Durin's legacy run through the mountain's later story: the cursed sword Festering Desire (1.2 event), his failed predecessor Subject Two (2.3 event) and the draining of Durin's heart in Paralogism (5.6).

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Silence, wind and deep snow; breath fogging, ice cracking underfoot and the constant worry of the cold. Broken ancient stonework juts out of drifts, giant bones curve above a scarlet valley, and blue Starglow light fills the caves. A crackling campfire is the most welcome sight on the mountain.

## dangers
### LARGE
The cold itself is the chief killer; snowstorms, falling icicles, rockfalls and icy water make it worse. Cryo slimes, Cryo hilichurl tribes and their Lawachurl chieftains, Abyss Mages, dormant Ruin Guards and Ruin Graders, a Cryo Hypostasis and the boar known as The Great Snowboar King roam the slopes, and Fatui agents come for Durin's relics. Durin's blood is highly toxic to ordinary people.
### ULTRA +
Scarlet Quartz warms and adds explosive force but is poisonous to normal humans, like the flesh of the dragon Elynas and the Dark Mud of the Chasm. Many climbers have been lost: the aristocratic expedition, Joserf, and others whose belongings lie in the snow.

## travel
### LARGE
The usual approach is from Windwail Highland through Dadaupa Gorge to the Adventurer Camp at the foot of the mountain. Climbers should bring hot food and the means to light fires; Pyro users, campfires, torches and Scarlet Quartz are what make the ascent survivable. The summit path was sealed by Ancient Rime until the Skyfrost Nail's shards were freed.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 Dragonspine is still frozen and hazardous. Durin's heart has been inert since Albedo drained it during Paralogism, and Durin's name now belongs to the gentle young man created in his place. After the 7.1 Archon Quest the Skyfrost Nail began showing a strange change, and Albedo is watching it from the summit.
### STANDARD +
The drained heart can no longer empower dragon teeth, though it still gives off warmth; the Adventurer Camp has an alchemy bench that does the job instead. The change in the Nail began when Ronova destroyed the Celestial Nail at Hyperborea with Eitr, after which every other Nail showed a strange sign in the sky. Albedo, who thought the Nail's purpose had ended with Sal Vindagnyr's destruction, now doubts that and is studying how the change affects the mountain's ecosystem.

## entry: Sal Vindagnyr
@keys: Sal Vindagnyr, Entombed City, Princess of Sal Vindagnyr, Varuch, Ukko, Imunlaukr
@spoiler: minor
@version: 1.2
### STANDARD
Sal Vindagnyr was the kingdom on Vindagnyr in the era of the unified civilization. Its chief priest Varuch's daughter, the princess, was born beneath the sacred white tree and could foresee the future, painting what she saw as frescoes; she once dreamed of a black dragon filling the land with scarlet poison. After the Skyfrost Nail fell, she gave the Starsilver greatsword to Imunlaukr, an outlander hero, who left to seek a way to break the ice. Varuch climbed the summit to seek guidance and did not return; the princess tried to graft a branch of the shattered tree on a small island and died in the snow; the scribe Ukko buried her, cursed the heavens and the absent Imunlaukr, and wrote the last carvings.
### LARGE +
Ukko's last words wished that a "new nation without gods" (Khaenri'ah, then in its infancy) might have the strength to stand against the world. Imunlaukr returned to find everyone dead, left the greatsword among the frescoes and went to seek war; his descendants became the Imunlaukr Clan of Mondstadt.

## entry: The Skyfrost Nail
@keys: Skyfrost Nail, Peak of Vindagnyr
@version: 1.2
### STANDARD
The Skyfrost Nail is the Celestial Nail cast down by the heavens onto Vindagnyr's summit ritual site, turning a green mountain into an eternal winter; the damage to the ley lines is implied to be the cause of the abnormal cold. Carvings record that the Nail split in three; its shards froze a spot deep in Starglow Cavern, an underground chamber near the Outskirts and the sacred tree. During In the Mountains the Traveler thawed the shards with Scarlet Quartz, they rejoined the main body, and the restored Nail rose to hover above the Peak of Vindagnyr.

## entry: The Frostbearing Tree
@keys: Frostbearing Tree, Crimson Agate, Frostbearer
@version: 1.2
### STANDARD
The Frostbearing Tree is the sacred white tree of Sal Vindagnyr, a withered Irminsul-type tree shattered and frozen when the Skyfrost Nail fell. Durin's blood seeping into the ley lines brought it back to a hungry half-life, marking it red rather than blue. Once freed from its Ancient Rime, it absorbs Crimson Agate (crystals of Durin's life force fused with the mountain's energy) and grows, finally bearing a single crimson, icy "fruit", the catalyst Frostbearer, in memory of the kingdom's dead, meant for one who will bring justice to a poisoned world.

## entry: Wyrmrest Valley and Durin's Heart
@keys: Wyrmrest Valley, Durin's Core, Durin's heart, Scarlet Quartz
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Wyrmrest Valley holds the black dragon Durin's skull, ribcage and heart. Created by the alchemist Rhinedottir in Khaenri'ah, Durin rampaged through Mondstadt during the cataclysm until Dvalin pierced his neck over the mountain. His heart went on beating, dyeing the snow red, and its life force could empower dragon teeth; Fatui squads camped nearby to exploit it, and the smith Orban used it to forge the Dragonspine Spear. Scarlet Quartz is a gemstone formed from his decaying remains.
### LARGE +
In Paralogism (5.6) Albedo, fearing that the slowly reviving heart would bring disaster, drew its vital energy with reagents won from the Hexenzirkel and used it to turn Mini Durin, the kindly dragon from the storybook world Simulanka, into a human who took the old Durin's place. The heart is now a silent stone that still radiates warmth.

## entry: The Lost Aristocratic Expedition
@keys: Eberhart, Ancient Investigation Journal, Ragged Records, Ingbert, Luther
@keys2: Dragonspine, expedition, Stevens, aristocrats
@spoiler: minor
### LARGE
About a thousand years ago, late in the aristocratic era, Mondstadt's nobles sent a team of young aristocrats to investigate Dragonspine. Eberhart, an illegitimate son of Landrich who secretly plotted against the aristocracy, laid traps that whittled the team down, then attacked his half-brother Ingbert and Luther himself; Ingbert died, Luther was mortally wounded, and Eberhart escaped by boat, gravely hurt, expecting his ally Priscilla to light a warning beacon at Stormbearer Point if the plot were discovered. The aristocracy did uncover it. Stevens at the Adventurer Camp pieces this together from the team's journals; Eberhart's ultimate fate is unrecorded.

## entry: The Chalk Prince and the Dragon
@keys: Chalk Prince and the Dragon, Festering Desire
@keys2: Dragonspine, Albedo, Durin, Sucrose
@availability: limited
@version: 1.2
### LARGE
During this 1.2 limited-time event, Sucrose asked the Traveler to uncover Albedo's "secret": Festering Desire, a sword recovered from Treasure Hoarders whose vanished smith had coated its blade with powder made from Durin's remains, cursing it. Albedo had the Traveler, who can purify Abyssal corruption, test it in battle on Dragonspine. Fatui agents came after the sword, and some of its power accidentally revived a dead Cryo Regisvine, which had to be defeated. The sword's own text is a fond memory of Durin thanking his mother for his wings and wishing to tell a land of lovely songs about her.

## entry: Shadows Amidst Snowstorms and Subject Two
@keys: Shadows Amidst Snowstorms, Subject Two, Fellflower
@keys2: Dragonspine, Albedo
@availability: limited
@version: 2.3
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
During this 2.3 limited-time event, while Albedo, Eula, Amber, Bennett and the Traveler were on Dragonspine, the story revealed Subject Two, Rhinedottir's failed first attempt at a synthetic human, abandoned inside Durin. Escaping the corpse, he haunted the mountain, envied Albedo's life among people and planned to replace him, using Durin's blood to create a Fellflower that mimicked Albedo as a decoy. Albedo went to confront him and afterwards said only that the impostor's plan had failed; the others never learned the full truth at the time. Paralogism (5.6) revealed that Albedo had killed and buried him, that creatures of Durin's blood carried his remains back into the dragon, and that he had returned as the core of the network of impostor creatures.

## entry: Ruin Guards of Dragonspine
@keys: Cryptic Message in Dragonspine, Record of Serial No.
@keys2: Dragonspine, Ruin Guard
### ULTRA
Broken Ruin Guards around the mountain each carry a numbered record. Decoded together they read roughly "for the nation, we can't forgo this skyborne power, but we failed". Ruin Guards are Khaenri'ahn machines, so the message may concern Khaenri'ah's war with the heavens rather than Sal Vindagnyr; canon does not explain it.
