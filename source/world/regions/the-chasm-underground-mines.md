## summary
### LITE
The Chasm: Underground Mines is the dark labyrinth of mine tunnels and ruins beneath Liyue's great pit, the Chasm. Sealed by the Qixing after a string of accidents and strange tremors, it was reopened in 2.6 to an exploration team led by the Ministry of Civil Affairs. Below the working mines lie older things: Dark Mud and Abyssal corruption, a sky-blue Celestial Nail called the Spiritstone, the buried ruins of the ancient city of Lang-Gan, and hilichurls and cursed Khaenri'ahn survivors who came to the depths to shelter.
### STANDARD +
Light is the main resource down there. Explorers rely on the glowing Lumenstone and a tool called the Lumenstone Adjuvant to see and to hold back the mud.

## geography
### STANDARD
A network of tunnels, caverns and ruins stretching down beneath the Chasm's Seven-Star Array. It has its own map tab and exploration progress, separate from the main map. Entrances include the Chasm's Maw (the main mine entrance), Tiangong Gorge, Glaze Peak and Avidya Forest.
### LARGE +
The sub-areas include the Main Mining Area with the exploration camp, the Stony Halls, the Glowing Narrows, the Nameless Ruins (with the Inverted City hanging from the cavern roof), the Court of Pillars around the Spiritstone, and the Chasm's Bed deep below. Marine fossils preserved in the depths suggest the whole area was once under the sea and that whatever made the Chasm happened in an instant.

## places
### STANDARD
Main Mining Area: where the exploration team set camp. Stony Halls: where a fragment of the Nail, the Wishstone, was found. Nameless Ruins and Inverted City: ruins of Lang-Gan, with an upside-down city and pool. Spiritstone: the Celestial Nail. The Chasm's Bed: a sealed, older space below.
### LARGE +
The Chasm's Maw has an elevator down from the surface. The map text for the mines calls the place "Yu Valley" in ancient texts and warns that one should always expect the unexpected in the shafts.

## people
### LARGE
The Chasm Exploration Team, made official as part of Huishan Hall after the incident, includes the cartographer Zhiqiong, Jinwu and Khedive, with the Ministry of Civil Affairs representative Muning on the surface. Treasure Hoarders sneak in; in the deeper ruins are hilichurls living out their last years and the Black Serpent Knights guarding them.

## governance
### LARGE
The Liyue Qixing seal and reopen the mine through the Ministry of Civil Affairs. Access depended on destroying the Seven-Star Seal's Bedrock Keys on the surface.

## culture
### LARGE
Miners' lives once centered here; the Qixing's closure scattered a workforce. A notice in Lisha says the old surface mines had run low and miners moved to the Chasm. 

## history
### STANDARD
The area was the home of Lang-Gan, a unified-era civilisation guided by the angel Zibai. Abyssal taint after the War of Funerary Flame led the Heavenly Principles to drop a Celestial Nail on it; Zibai delayed the strike so many could flee east, and the Nail's impact made the Chasm. Sometime during or after the Archon War, miners disturbed the ley lines and the dragon Azhdaha, who was fought by Morax and the adepti. During the Cataclysm Abyssal monsters poured out and the Millelith and the Yaksha Bosacius fought them here.
### LARGE +
Before the Traveler, the Qixing closed the mine two years earlier after an unknown incident, and the Fatui had tried and failed to deal with the black ooze. In 2.6 the Traveler helped the exploration team break the Seven-Star Seal, light the tunnels, blast open a rockfall and purify the Abyssal-infected Nail, calming the tremors. In the Requiem of the Echoing Depths the Traveler and Dainsleif found Halfdan and the Black Serpent Knights guarding hilichurls in the Inverted City. In 2.7 the Traveler, Yelan, Yanfei, Itto and Shinobu fell into the Chasm's Bed, where Xiao was searching for Bosacius, and escaped together.
### ULTRA +
The Perilous Trail party were saved when Xiao poured out his strength to lift them out and Zhongli caught him as he fell; Xiao confirmed the "Nameless Yaksha" at the Chasm was Bosacius, and afterwards invited the Traveler to Pervases' temple.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Total darkness pierced by pale blue glow; dripping caves, hanging ruins, pools of black mud that sap strength. Deep in the Chasm's Bed the space feels outside the usual rules.

## dangers
### LARGE
Dark Mud, which drains energy and strengthens enemies; Dangerous Gas that explodes with Electro or Pyro; Abyss Mages and Lectors; Treasure Hoarders; the Ruin Serpent Haftvad; shadowy Black Serpent Knights lost to the Khaenri'ahn curse; and the Chasm's Bed, a looping space where hunger, thirst and fatigue vanish and then strike back, and where domains turn fears into illusions.

## travel
### STANDARD
Enter through the Chasm's Maw from the surface, or by the other entrances after unlocking. The Qixing's seal must be broken first, and the Lumenstone Adjuvant is needed to light and navigate.

## quests
### LARGE
The Chasm Delvers series (2.6), including Chasm Spelunkers and Wherefore Did the Spiritstone Descend?; Archon Quest Requiem of the Echoing Depths (Chapter II Act IV, 2.6); Interlude Chapter Perilous Trail (2.7); Valor's Afterglow (Zhiqiong); Paleontological Investigation in The Chasm and other investigations.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the mines are partly reopened and watched: Millelith patrol, Xiao keeps watch for the Nail anomaly, and Zhiqiong's whereabouts remain unknown.
### STANDARD +
In 7.1 the Nail of Retribution at the Chasm, like others across Teyvat, began to glow; additional Millelith patrol and Cloud Retainer and other adepti are investigating, and Rex Lapis (Zhongli) had Xiao monitor the site.

## entry: The Chasm Delvers expedition
@keys: Chasm Exploration Team, Chasm Delvers, Muning, Jinwu, Khedive
@keys2: Chasm, Underground Mines, Traveler
@version: 2.6
### STANDARD
The Traveler took a commission from the Adventurers' Guild and met Muning, the Ministry of Civil Affairs representative, who wanted to find out why the Chasm had been sealed. With the cartographer Zhiqiong and the team, they destroyed five Bedrock Keys to open the Qixing's Seven-Star Seal, set up camp, found missing miners, fended off Treasure Hoarders and used the Lumenstone Adjuvant to clear Dark Mud. After blasting a rockfall with a cannon, they found the Abyssal-infected Nail, purified it and ended the tremors. Muning made the team part of Huishan Hall.

## entry: Dark Mud, Lumenstone and the Adjuvant
@keys: Dark Mud, Lumenstone Adjuvant, Lumenspar, Oozing Concretion
@keys2: Chasm, Underground Mines
@version: 2.6
### STANDARD
The Lumenstone Adjuvant is a tool the Traveler receives in the early mine quests; it lights the surroundings and lets the wearer open blue-sealed chests, lamps and braziers, and it recharges at waypoints, Luminous Seelie and certain lamps. Dark Mud is black ooze that drains the Adjuvant, hampers movement and makes enemies stronger; it gathers around Oozing Concretions and spreads Abyssal corruption.
### LARGE +
Chunks of ore that seem related to the Nail were found in the mines. A sufficiently upgraded Adjuvant can destroy an Oozing Concretion, dispersing the mud around it.

## entry: The Inverted City and the Black Serpent Knights
@keys: Inverted City, Black Serpent Knights, Halfdan, Inteyvat
@keys2: Dainsleif, hilichurl, Chasm, curse
@version: 2.6
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
In Requiem of the Echoing Depths the Traveler and Dainsleif entered the Inverted City, a ruin of an older civilisation (Lang-Gan). An upside-down pool there weakens the Khaenri'ahn curse of immortality, so hilichurls had come to live out their final years in it, protected by Black Serpent Knights, Dainsleif's former subordinates, now mindless but still obeying their last order to protect Khaenri'ah's people. One Knight, whom Dainsleif takes to be Halfdan, led them to a hilichurl camp where a Khaenri'ahn flower, the Inteyvat, showed the Traveler a vision of their sibling and an Abyss Herald planning to amplify the pool to lift the curse and restore "the homeland". Dainsleif thought it futile and the Traveler agreed to stop it. An Abyss Lector activated the device; Halfdan sacrificed himself to stall it while the Traveler destroyed the machinery, and his soul spoke briefly with Dainsleif before passing on.

## entry: The Chasm's Bed and Perilous Trail
@keys: Perilous Trail, Fantastic Compass
@keys2: Chasm, Xiao, Yelan, Yanfei, Bosacius
@version: 2.7
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
In Interlude Chapter Act II, Yelan, Yanfei, Arataki Itto, Kuki Shinobu and the Traveler fell into a space under the Spiritstone where hunger and time warp, and domains turn fear into illusions. Xiao was inside searching for his missing brother-in-arms Bosacius. An illusory Fantastic Compass, a charm of mortal and adeptal power, lifted them to the surface; Xiao spent his last energy to hurl them out and Zhongli rescued him. Xiao learned the Nameless Yaksha at the Chasm was Bosacius.
