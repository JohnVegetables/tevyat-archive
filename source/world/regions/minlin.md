## summary
### LITE
Minlin ("Forest of Jade-Like Stone") is the cloud-wrapped mountain country of western Liyue, and the part of the nation that belongs to the adepti. Its peaks, karst spires and ruins hold Jueyun Karst, where the adepti are said to dwell; Mt. Aocang, Qingyun Peak and Mt. Hulao, abodes of individual adepti; Nantianmen, where a great tree seals an ancient dragon; and the old ruins of Tianqiu Valley and Cuijue Slope. Mortals rarely go up; those who do are usually herb gatherers or people seeking the adepti's blessing.
### STANDARD +
Legend, not law, keeps people out. Several slopes have barriers or amber traps, and the adepti's presence is more felt than seen.

## geography
### STANDARD
Layered stone peaks ringed by cloud in the west and northwest of Liyue, best viewed from far away at Wangshu Inn. Huaguang Stone Forest lies in the middle, bounded by Mt. Aocang to the north, Qingyun Peak to the east and Mt. Hulao to the southwest. Nantianmen and its sealing tree stand near Mt. Hulao; Tianqiu Valley and Cuijue Slope lie among the ruins and valleys beyond.
### LARGE +
Stone spires push through the clouds like pavilions and the Huaguang Stone Forest is described as forbidden to mortal steps, though its beauty draws anyone who yearns for the sky. Waypoints on the surrounding peaks make the forest easy to reach by the few who come.

## places
### STANDARD
Jueyun Karst is the traditional realm of the adepti; Taishan Mansion, a residence 'established by royal decree' whose path was lost to mortals, lies there, now flooded. Mt. Aocang was the domain of Cloud Retainer. Qingyun Peak is the home of Moon Carver. Mt. Hulao is the abode of Mountain Shaper, studded with amber rock. Nantianmen holds the Dragon-Queller. Tianqiu Valley is a ruined ancient settlement. Cuijue Slope guards the Nine Pillars of Peace.
### LARGE +
Above Qingyun Peak floats the Dwelling in the Clouds, a small island made by Cloud Retainer at an unknown time and reachable by a puzzle. Mt. Aocang's slopes hold the domain Clear Pool and Mountain Cavern.

## people
### LARGE
Chiefly the adepti themselves (Moon Carver and Mountain Shaper, with Cloud Retainer before she moved to the city; the yaksha Xiao is also an adeptus of Liyue) plus "herb gatherers" who make a living collecting rare plants that grow only in Jueyun Karst. Treasure Hoarders and ruin-delvers also trespass.

## governance
### LARGE
No human authority. The adepti are bound by old loyalty to Rex Lapis, the Geo Archon who now lives as Zhongli.

## culture
### LARGE
Folk belief holds that mortals who see an adeptus should leave quickly. Stone tablets throughout the mountains carry poetry and warnings, such as the verses at Jueyun Karst and the tablet at Nantianmen urging passersby not to disturb the evil dragon below.
### ULTRA +
Mt. Aocang has a stone table with three chairs engraved "Here sits Retainer" (a wine jar before it), "Here sits Guizhong" (a bowl and chopsticks) and "Here sits Rex" (nothing on the table). The adepti are remembered as the keepers of life in Heaven and Earth.

## history
### STANDARD
The adepti gathered here after serving Morax in the Archon War. In the distant past the dragon Azhdaha raged north from the Chasm to Nantianmen before being sealed in the tree by Morax and three adepti, and Tianqiu Valley's settlement was lost around the same ancient rampage. Cloud Retainer lived alone on Mt. Aocang after the war until the events of A Thousand Moonlit Miles (4.4), when she took up residence in Liyue Harbor.
### LARGE +
Azhdaha later tried to break free through Jiu, a vessel for his rage, and was defeated by the Traveler, Zhongli and his own kindly fragment in Zhongli's story quest (Version 1.5); he was resealed and the fragment bid Morax farewell. Per Qiqi's character story, Mountain Shaper imprisoned her in amber to quell her rampage after the adepti had revived her with some of their power.
### ULTRA +
Moon Carver's tablet calls him Rex Lapis's disciple and the kindest adeptus, in the form of a stag. Cuijue Slope's shrine door bears an insignia seen nowhere else, and its inlay resembles a squashed yaksha mask; Stones of Remembrance, obtained by offering Geoculi to Statues of the Seven, open the pillars.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Hushed, vertiginous and solemn: cloud seas under the peaks, the pale hum of mountain wind, tablets half-covered in moss. Cuijue Slope is stranger still; the sky darkens to grey near the Nine Pillars and the land beyond looks unnaturally dim.

## dangers
### LARGE
Amber rocks on Mt. Hulao trap whatever steps on them and release treasure or enemies. Treasure Hoarders trespass; Pyro Regisvine and Primo Geovishap are the area's bosses, and a Ruin Guard sleeps in Cuijue Slope's shrine.

## travel
### STANDARD
Teleport waypoints on the peaks let you reach each abode and Nantianmen. Climbing up is routine for Herb Gatherers; mysterious barriers seal the abodes on Qingyun Peak and Mt. Hulao.

## quests
### LARGE
World quests include An Impromptu Change of Plan (where the Traveler meets Moon Carver at Qingyun Peak), Moulder of Mountains (Mountain Shaper), Trails in Tianqiu, Nine Pillars of Peace, and Lantern Rite scenes of the 6.3 story quest A Lanternlit Ode to the Silver Moon, whose Act III begins at Mt. Aocang.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the adepti still keep their mountain abodes, but Cloud Retainer now lives in Liyue Harbor and Xiao watches the Chasm at Rex Lapis's order.
### STANDARD +
After the 7.1 anomaly in which the Celestial Nails across Teyvat began shining, Xiao says additional Millelith patrol the Chasm and Cloud Retainer and the other adepti have begun investigating.

## entry: Azhdaha and the Dragon-Queller
@keys: Dragon-Queller, Azhdaha, Nantianmen
@keys2: Liyue, Morax, Jiu, Mt. Hulao
@version: 1.5
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
The Dragon-Queller is a vast tree with blue-veined bark near Mt. Hulao and Nantianmen. It seals Azhdaha, an ancient Geo dragon who once befriended Morax before erosion ate away his memory and he began harming humans; Morax and the adepti fought him from the Chasm north to Nantianmen. A stone tablet erected by Mountain Shaper begs passersby not to disturb it. Before Zhongli's story quest, an NPC named Jiu worshipped him and prophesied his return; in the quest she is revealed as a vessel of Azhdaha's rage, and he is defeated and resealed.
### LARGE +
The tree's blue branch points toward Liyue Harbor, and the dragon has begun to merge with the tree, using its roots to tap the ley lines. A fragment of Azhdaha's kindly self escaped and possessed a man named Kun Jun to warn Morax; it remembered everything in the final battle.

## entry: Mt. Aocang and Cloud Retainer
@keys: Mt. Aocang, Cloud Retainer, Dwelling in the Clouds
@keys2: Xianyun, adeptus, Minlin, Liyue
@version: 4.4
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Mt. Aocang was the domain of the adeptus Cloud Retainer (Xianyun) during the long isolation that followed the Archon War, until A Thousand Moonlit Miles. Its stone table is engraved for three seats: Retainer, Guizhong and Rex. Ruins-treasure monuments near the Clear Pool and Mountain Cavern domain record a former disciple of the mountain's doubts about whether monsters are truly evil and whether mortals and adepti are truly good; the disciple's identity is not given. Cloud Retainer also made the Dwelling in the Clouds above Qingyun Peak.

## entry: Cuijue Slope and the Nine Pillars of Peace
@keys: Cuijue Slope, Nine Pillars of Peace, Stone of Remembrance
@keys2: Liyue, Minlin
### STANDARD
The Nine Pillars of Peace stand around a temple on Cuijue Slope. Legend says they were raised to hold nine human desires at bay (greed, nostalgia, vision, jealousy, anger, lust, self-aggrandizement, competition and turmoil) and that breaking them would bring war again; the inscription nonetheless tells any who do open it to take the treasures within and defend Liyue. Nine Stones of Remembrance, earned by offering Geoculi to the Statues of the Seven, break the seals. The shrine's door insignia is unique and little is known of the pillars even in Liyue folklore.

## entry: Tianqiu Valley
@keys: Tianqiu Valley, Tianqiu
@keys2: Azhdaha, ancient dragon king, Liyue
### STANDARD
Tianqiu Valley was once a prosperous settlement in the ancient past; three ruined towers stand on its west side. It seems to have fallen during the rampage of an ancient dragon king, whom Rex Lapis and his adepti imprisoned in an adeptal realm, and the dragon's servants fled into the earth to sleep until his return. How the valley came to ruin is not told in detail.
