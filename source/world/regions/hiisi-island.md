## summary
### LITE
Hiisi Island is a forested island in the Southern Isles of Nod-Krai (accessible from 6.0). It is the home of the Frostmoon Scions, a religious sect devoted to the Moon Goddess, and under their care much of it is untouched wilderness. Its Frostmoon Enclave and the sealed Sanctum of the Oathbearer are the heart of their faith.
### STANDARD +
Its expedition description calls it a mysterious island of ancient forest where Moon Goddess worship endures, and says legends tell of descendants of the golden realm, guided by moonlight, who travelled countless miles to find refuge here.

## geography
### STANDARD
A wooded island south of Pohjola, one of three that make up the Southern Isles with Lempo Isle and Paha Isle. Its subareas are the Frostmoon Enclave, the Scions' dwelling place, and the Light-Bathed Platform, a high plateau where the Sanctum of the Oathbearer lies. Local specialties: Frostlamp Flower and Moonfall Silver.
### LARGE +
The island's boss is the Frostnight Herra.

## places
### STANDARD
Frostmoon Enclave: home of the Scions, with a Grand Library that holds its Meeting Point. Light-Bathed Platform: a plateau with the Sanctum of the Oathbearer (also called the Court of the First Edict), sealed five hundred years ago and unsealed in the world quest Echoes of an Unfinished Past.
### LARGE +
The Enclave is thought to occupy the remains of an older Scion complex of towers, fortresses and temples that Aila razed five hundred years ago to reform the faith. The Sanctum is reached from the base of the Sanctum of the Oathkeeper.

## people
### STANDARD
The Frostmoon Scions: priestesses such as Ehrnrooth, Rahetar, Sarakka and Verna; adherents such as Krodo, Ossian, Reetta and Kukkia; the acolyte Zirnytsia; and others including Ina, Kari, Mirsky, Wecksell and Eveliina. Rebane is a traveling merchant. Their leader is Lauma, the current Moonchanter.
### LARGE +
Verna guides the Traveler in Echoes of an Unfinished Past. In the centuries after the fall of Hyperborea a prophecy arose among them that the Moon Goddess would one day return.

## governance
### STANDARD
The Scions are led by the Moonchanter, with priestesses guiding rites. After the fall of Hyperborea a line of Archpriestesses issued strict decrees; one set found in the Sanctum was signed by Saredne, the thirteenth Archpriestess, and another by Lovia.

## culture
### STANDARD
Worship of the Moon Goddess: the Scions revere the Frost Moon, the current goddess Kuutar and her predecessor Canon (Huurrekuutar). Their ancient rites used kuuvahki and a body of hymns called Runo, preserved in part in the book Hymns of the Far North.
### LARGE +
Hyperborean teachings emphasised reason over sentiment; decrees in the Sanctum forbid marrying outlanders or those outside the faith, and speak of a prophesied sovereign who would one day awaken the sacred Sampo Mill. Hymns and prayer songs honour many figures, including the Heavenly Principles, the Four Shades, Anacharsis and Saarelainen. Statues of the New Moon dedicated to Columbina were set up across Nod-Krai by the Scions. People elsewhere in Nod-Krai find the Scions strange and do not approach them (per Helka).

## history
### STANDARD
After the Celestial Nail destroyed Hyperborea, Canon guided the survivors to Hiisi Island and died. They found refuge at a pillar made by Xiuhcoatl, which became the Sanctum of the Oathbearer, and kept their rites although their goddess did not answer. Five hundred years ago Aila, the first Moonchanter, reformed the faith, tearing down the old insular towers and temples. The Sanctum itself was sealed five hundred years ago.
### LARGE +
Aila's notes, found in the Sanctum, record that she lied when Solovei asked if she felt any hate, as Lovia and her own sister Alia had lied before her, and that she saw no reason to burden him with her suffering. The Traveler opens the Sanctum with Verna's help in the quest Echoes of an Unfinished Past.
### ULTRA +
Solovei, founder of the Lightkeepers, and Aila acted together five hundred years ago. The wiki lists the Lightkeepers and the Knights of Favonius as the Scions' allies, and the Wild Hunt and the Fatui as enemies, with the exception of Arlecchino and Sandrone's group of Fatui, listed as allies.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Silver light through old trees, quiet paths, ruined stone from older temples, the drum set in the Enclave and a feeling of watching and waiting. Peaceful, a little secretive.

## dangers
### STANDARD
The Frostnight Herra is the island's boss; it can enter a state in which it absorbs an element. The wiki lists no other special hazards.

## travel
### LARGE
The island is one of the Southern Isles. The Enclave has a Meeting Point at the Grand Library; the Light-Bathed Platform is up in the hills.

## quests
### STANDARD
Echoes of an Unfinished Past (world quest, Polkka Beneath the Moon's Oracle chapter): starting at the Light-Bathed Platform with Verna, the Traveler opens the sealed Sanctum and follows the Moonlane to the Court of the First Edict, where they find Aila's sword.
### LARGE +
The chapter requires earlier quests: For a Green Island..., Gift of the Mirage, The Mirrors, the Maze, and the Tsar, and The Tale of the Gate Stone.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the island remains the Scions' home, open to explore, and the Sanctum has been unsealed.
### STANDARD +
Nothing in 7.0-7.1 is recorded as changing the island.

## entry: Frostmoon Enclave
@keys: Frostmoon Enclave, Grand Library
@keys2: Hiisi Island, Frostmoon Scions
@version: 6.0
### STANDARD
The home of the Frostmoon Scions, where they gather to listen to the teachings of moonlight. A drum set in the Enclave summons animal companions when Lauma or Columbina is in the party.
### LARGE +
The ruins of towers, fortresses and temples here were torn down by Aila, five hundred years ago, so that the Scions would no longer be cut off from the world.

## entry: Sanctum of the Oathbearer
@keys: Sanctum of the Oathbearer, Court of the First Edict, Light-Bathed Platform
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The Scions' place of refuge after Hyperborea, a pillar made by Xiuhcoatl, sealed five hundred years ago and opened in Echoes of an Unfinished Past. Inside are Aila's private notes and decrees of the Hyperborean Archpriestesses that govern marriage, rites and the Sampo Mill.
### LARGE +
The decrees show a rigid, secretive order that guarded its bloodline and forbade emotion in recorded rites.

## entry: Frostmoon Scions
@keys: Frostmoon Scions, Moonchildren, Moonchanter
@version: 6.0
### STANDARD
The tribe of Moon Goddess followers who are descendants of Hyperborea and live on Hiisi Island. They revere Kuutar, the Moon Maiden, who succeeded the late Canon. Lauma is the current Moonchanter.
