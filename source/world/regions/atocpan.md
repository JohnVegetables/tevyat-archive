## summary
### LITE
Atocpan is the volcanic southwest of Natlan. The Great Volcano of Tollan fills the region, an active volcano that erupts from time to time, and beside it lies the home of the Collective of Plenty (Teteocan), the tribe known for bodybuilding, wrestling and the best fruit and vegetables in Natlan. Ruins of the ancient dragons lie scattered around the slopes. The name Atocpan means "thick and fertile soil" in Nahuatl, which suits the rich volcanic farmland.
### STANDARD +
Atocpan is where Natlan's farming meets its oldest history: tribespeople grow crops beside a volcano that ancient legends call the Sacred Mountain, and the only way into the mountain's interior, the Ancient Sacred Mountain, is through the ring of floating islands above its mouth.

## geography
### STANDARD
A region of ash-rich, fertile soil, orchards and rocky slopes around the Great Volcano of Tollan, in the southwest of Natlan. Its sub-areas are the Collective of Plenty settlement, Fallingstar Fields, the Remnants of Tetenanco, the Skyfire Circlet and the Ancient Mountain Path. The volcano encloses the whole region and periodically sends up fireballs.
### LARGE +
Natural hot springs, a cold onsen in the west, farmland, tribal training grounds and the dig sites of ancient ruins all share the same slopes. Local specialties are Dracolite, quenepa berries, skysplit gembloom and sprayfeather gill.

## places
### STANDARD
The Collective of Plenty settlement, with its Grand Wrestling Grounds (an elevated arena decorated with giant bull horns, also called the Resonating Arena) and training ground; Varesa's Orchard just outside the settlement; Fallingstar Fields with the Tephric Ruins; the Remnants of Tetenanco, ruins left by the ancient dragons; and the Skyfire Circlet, a ring of islands held above the volcano's mouth by Phlogiston.
### LARGE +
The Skyfire Circlet holds the only entrance to the Ancient Sacred Mountain, which is why a quest calls it Nahuatzin's Springboard. A Fatui camp and boats sit near the Derelict Masonry Dock, and Fatui also camp west of the Tetenanco ruins. A small camp of one wandering stranger, Ixtelo, is the subject of two damaged notices: a bounty notice and a letter from Hawka, both warning that this wanderer is dangerous.

## people
### STANDARD
The Collective of Plenty live here under their chief, Acatl. Their members include the playable Iansan, a champion wrestler, and the orchard-keeper Varesa. Their saurian companions are the charging Tatankasaurs. Mposo watches over the Tetenanco dig site.
### LARGE +
Wrestling referees, logistics managers and a flying squad are posted at the Grand Wrestling Grounds. Quest dialogue (Despair Engulfs the Heavens) notes that this tribe lives the farthest from the Stadium of the Sacred Flame.

## culture
### LARGE
Physical fitness is highly prized: showing off one's muscles is a friendly gesture, adults tend to be large and heavily muscled, and fitness coaches must be very strong to be taken seriously. Warriors seek out wild Halberd-Crest Birds to see whose head is harder, hoping to temper their bodies to steel, and wrestling a Tatankasaurus is a long-standing tribal tradition; winning is considered a great honour. Their fruit and vegetables are said to be the best in Natlan because the soil beside the volcano is so rich.
### ULTRA +
A note by the cold onsen advertises 'The Book of Five Springs', a serialised bathhouse-fighting story from Yae Publishing House, a small sign that Inazuman publishing reaches even this far.

## history
### STANDARD
Legend holds that ancient dragons once dwelt in the volcano and that their palace lay somewhere beneath the magma. According to the same legends, the dragon prince Kukulkan stole the primal fire (Phlogiston) from there. The first Pyro Archon, Xbalanque, is said to have fought and defeated the dragon sovereign Xiuhcoatl on the volcano, after the Abyss corrupted him. After the dragon civilisation crumbled, the Abyss is said to have taken over the volcano.
### LARGE +
The Collective of Plenty was founded thousands of years ago by Ahpub, around the time of the Grand Alliance. The Remnants of Tetenanco are thought to be a satellite city of the dragons destroyed during the war between the dragons and Xbalanque, and the Tephric Ruins in Fallingstar Fields are a section of ruins ejected from the volcano about five hundred years ago, during the cataclysm. A report found in a hidden chamber of the Tephric Ruins describes experiments to reverse the regression of the 'Dragonborn' (saurians), and it concludes that the price would be too great. The same file carries a later comment, unsigned, wondering whether the experiment could break a 'barrier of destiny'. The commenter speaks of 'my brother' and of 'the promise I saw in humanity'; the wiki reads these lines as Kukulkan addressing Xiuhcoatl, but the text itself names neither.
### ULTRA +
Power of the two Golden Entreaties is tied to the volcano: according to the NPC Manqu, bringing both to the Great Volcano of Tollan can lift the Flamelord's Blessing from a saurian. Natlan/History also records that the dragon sovereign Xiuhcoatl carved out the Sacred City of Tollan inside the volcano, that the fungal god Bifrons was buried by an eruption here in the ancient dragon era, and that the city fell about two thousand years ago when Xbalanque invaded.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Warm air, ash and orange light from the volcano, farm fields set against steaming rock and a lively, noisy tribe that wrestles, shouts and eats well. Sudden rumbling signals an eruption: fireballs rain on the ground and boulders roll down the slopes, then everything goes quiet again.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Volcanic eruptions (fireballs and rolling boulders, which are gameplay hazards in the game), saurians, Abyssal monsters and, near the ruins, Secret Source automata. Fatui camps are scattered through the area.
### LARGE +
Gameplay note, not lore: eruptions recur roughly every twenty minutes of play in the region, last under a minute, and are used as a puzzle element. Bosses here include the Lava Dragon Statue and the Secret Source Automaton: Overseer Device.

## travel
### LARGE
The Skyfire Circlet and Ancient Mountain Path lead into the Ancient Sacred Mountain. A notice at the Tetenanco tunnel says the dig site is closed to the public because of recent eruptions.

## quests
@version: 5.5
### LARGE
The Mushroom Realm Mystery story chapter begins here with Ardent Land of Plenty and Orchard Repose, in which Varesa and Iansan are involved. World quests include A Brief History of Rocks (at Tetenanco, with Mposo and the Avatar of Lava) and Investigator of Ancient Ruins (Fallingstar Fields), either of which counts toward The Way Into the Mountain.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 Atocpan is quiet, with its tribe prospering and the volcano still erupting now and then.
### STANDARD +
The Night Warden Wars ended in 5.3. The people of Natlan may now leave their homeland, and the Pilgrimage of the Return of the Sacred Flame has become a sports competition. Nothing in the later versions up to 7.1 changes the region's story.
### ULTRA +
The wiki lists no Atocpan changes in 6.x or 7.x beyond bug fixes. The Tetenanco dig site remains closed to the public because of eruptions.

## entry: Collective of Plenty Settlement and Grand Wrestling Grounds
@keys: Grand Wrestling Grounds, Resonating Arena, Teteocan, Collective of Plenty settlement
@version: 5.5
### STANDARD
The Collective of Plenty settlement sits beside the Great Volcano of Tollan. Its centre is the Grand Wrestling Grounds, an elevated arena adorned with massive bull horns where the tribe's athletic competitions are held; a quest calls it the Resonating Arena. A bulletin board, referee, logistics manager and a flying squad member work here, and a training ground lies nearby.
### LARGE +
The tribe is led by Acatl. Their saurian companions are Tatankasaurs, and the tribe's warriors prove themselves by wrestling them.

## entry: Varesa's Orchard
@keys: Varesa's Orchard, Vanana, Fruits Aplenty
@version: 5.5
### LARGE
Just outside the settlement, Varesa's Orchard belongs to the tribeswoman Varesa and is guarded by her Tatankasaur companion Vanana. It grows apples, cacahuatl, grainfruit and quenepa berries, and it is the only place red berryshrooms are found. A fruit-pressing machine on its southern side can be set off by hitting it with a Tatankasaur or a Collective of Plenty character. The orchard is the setting of the story quest Orchard Repose.

## entry: Remnants of Tetenanco and Fallingstar Fields
@keys: Remnants of Tetenanco, Tetenanco, Fallingstar Fields, Tephric Ruins, Mposo
@version: 5.5
### STANDARD
Two sets of ancient ruins. The Remnants of Tetenanco are the remains of what is believed to have been a dragon satellite city, destroyed in the war between the dragons and Xbalanque; today they are also called the Remnants of the Secret Source excavation site and are guarded by Mposo. Fallingstar Fields contains the Tephric Ruins, a section of ruins ejected from the volcano around five hundred years ago.
### LARGE +
Both sites hold fragments of Secret Source machinery. At the Tephric Ruins, tribespeople placed broken pieces of Source Mechanism as if for worship, and a floating platform holds a relic, That Which Was Once Ehecatl, that opens a hidden chamber of records. The Tetenanco dig has been closed to the public because of frequent eruptions.

## entry: Skyfire Circlet and Ancient Mountain Path
@keys: Skyfire Circlet, Nahuatzin's Springboard, Ancient Mountain Path
@version: 5.5
### STANDARD
The Skyfire Circlet is a ring of islands suspended above the mouth of the Great Volcano of Tollan by the power of Phlogiston, and it contains the only entrance to the Ancient Sacred Mountain, hence its other name, Nahuatzin's Springboard. Secret Source Hunter-Seeker automata and sentinels are found there, and the Ancient Mountain Path leads up toward it.

## entry: Fatui Research in Atocpan
@keys: Praporshchik Tatiana, Kovalevskaya, Letter for Anna
@keys2: Atocpan, Fatui
@version: 5.5
### LARGE
Two interactable letters at Fatui camps hint at a quiet dispute. 'Letter for Anna' is from Madam Kovalevskaya, a researcher who studies ancient ruins in Natlan and also helps with kuuvahki research at the request of an unnamed 'Lord Harbinger'. 'Unsigned Letter' orders the Fatui Praporshchik Tatiana to guard Kovalevskaya on a perilous voyage in an old ship and heavily implies that nobody would blame Tatiana if something happened. Tatiana also appears as a one-time enemy near the west Tetenanco camp. The wiki infers that the Harbinger is Dottore, but the letters do not say.
