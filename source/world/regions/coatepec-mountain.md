## summary
### LITE
Coatepec Mountain is a towering peak in Natlan and the home of the Scions of the Canopy (Huitztlan), the tribe of explorers, couriers and hunters who live alongside the leaf-tongued Yumkasaurs. Their village hangs from the cliff-face, and the mountain is also the seat of the Turnfire rites in memory of the ancestor Yupanqui. The Pyro Archon Mavuika comes from this tribe. The name means "at the snake hill" in Nahuatl.
### STANDARD +
The terrain is harsh, but young people still come to test themselves. The mountain also holds the old Ancestral Temple, the lake at Teticpac Peak and, south of it, Firethief's Secret Isle.

## geography
### STANDARD
A high, rocky mountain with the Scions of the Canopy's village suspended off the cliff-face. Official text says their ancestors hung giant trees over the cliffs with ropes and grappling hooks, which gave the tribe its name. Teticpac Peak is a plateau with a small lake at the very top; Firethief's Secret Isle lies in the sea to the south. Local specialty: quenepa berries.
### LARGE +
Tepeacac Rise slopes are said by the Expedition text to lie in the mountain's northwest, although the wiki files that subarea under Tequemecan Valley.

## places
### STANDARD
The tribal village; the Couriers' Longhouse; the Ancestral Temple; Teticpac Peak and the Manse of Monetoo Murals; Firethief's Secret Isle; and the Phlogiston Extraction Research Center.
### LARGE +
The Ancestral Temple is a sacred site where the people of Natlan once spoke with the Night Kingdom; the tribes have since built a more stable link, so it is out of use, but it remains holy ground guarded by the Scions of the Canopy. According to the NPC Legba, only experienced Couriers who have passed three trials may enter. Firethief's Secret Isle is hidden from the map under perpetual cloud until four time-trial challenges on it are completed, after which the weather clears. The Phlogiston Extraction Research Center is a secret underground Fatui base.
### ULTRA +
Legend has it that the tribe's warriors and one hundred Yumkasaurs spent a hundred days and a hundred ropes moving the Obsidian Totem Pole, the symbol of their faith, to its present place on the mountain.

## people
### STANDARD
The Scions of the Canopy, led by chief Wayna. They are skilled explorers, messengers and hunters, fond of extreme sports. The playable Kinich is one of their hunters, and the Pyro Archon Mavuika is of this tribe.
### LARGE +
Natlan's Adventurers' Guild is mostly made up of Huitztlan. A tribal figure called the Mountain King (Kongamato) is a Yumkasaurus as old as the cataclysm, loved by the tribe and now ceremonially tended.

## culture
### STANDARD
The tribe works as couriers and guides and honours its ancestors. Its great observance is Turnfire Night, held to commemorate Yupanqui, who is called the Firethief.
### LARGE +
The tribe tends the Mountain King Flamegranates, which grow from the Mother Flamegranate Tree, and offers them to Kongamato, though rumour says Yumkasaurs sneak him some as well. Flamegranate skins are dried and made into the bags that couriers carry. To bear the Ancient Name Malipo is to carry the Turnfire.
### ULTRA +
During Turnfire Night a flame bearer, traditionally the current bearer of the Malipo name, leads a delegation that lights braziers with kindling from the Sacred Flame and takes it into the Mountain King's cave in order to cleanse the abyssal corruption within him.

## history
@version: 5.0
### STANDARD
Tradition says that in the days of the tyrant Och-Kan, the tribe's ancestor Yupanqui stole the Turnfire, a fire that burned anyone who looked back at it, and turned it on Och-Kan's army. He glanced back and was consumed. He burned for a hundred days, and the fire pierced a hole through the Ley Lines so that he fell into the Night Kingdom, where the Turnfire is said to burn still. 'Malipo' became his Ancient Name.
### LARGE +
In the cataclysm five hundred years ago the hero Burkina, a later bearer of the Malipo name, and his Yumkasaur companion Kongamato fought the Abyss beside Tenoch and the Pyro Archon Mavuika. Abyssal energy infested Kongamato, and in a fit of rage he killed Burkina, who passed some of his power and blood to him. Kongamato lived centuries longer, a living symbol of the tribe's glory. After the cataclysm, Mavuika's sister Hine helped rebuild the Scions of the Canopy, though none of her works survive.
### ULTRA +
In the story quest series Yupanqui's Turnfire (5.0), the Turnfire ritual that drains Kongamato's energy was due again because the energy had been building faster than usual. Kinich proposed placing the Mountain King in a Beastly Rift provided by Enjou; the plan was resisted and the ritual went wrong. In the fight that followed, Kongamato begged to be put out of his misery, but Kinich told him he had to live with his shame. As of The Last Turnfire Night his corruption is permanently stabilised, a shell has grown around him, and he trains tribe members. Kongamato appeared to Kinich and the Traveler before their descent into the Night Kingdom.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Vertical and exposed: a village hung from the cliffs, couriers on their rounds, Yumkasaurs on the rocks and a tribe that loves a challenge.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Steep terrain, wild saurians and the Fatui, who run a hidden facility on the mountain.
### LARGE +
The research center was extracting Phlogiston from captured saurians. Notes found there show the soldiers puzzling over why they must go to such lengths, with a technician, Nomokonov, understanding the science better than the rest. The Fontaine Research Institute supplied equipment, and it is unclear how much it knew about the use.

## travel
### LARGE
Firethief's Secret Isle appears on the map only after its four time-trial challenges are done. Only experienced Couriers who passed the three trials may enter the Ancestral Temple.

## quests
@version: 5.0
### LARGE
Main stories linked to the mountain are Yupanqui's Turnfire (I, Flame Bearer; The Last Turnfire Night) in the Kinich's Deal act. World quests include Ripe For Trouble (Flamegranates). Several Scions of the Canopy died in the Abyssal invasion of Chapter V, Act IV; this file leaves those individuals to their own entries.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the tribe is at peace and Kongamato's corruption is stabilised.
### STANDARD +
Natlan's war with the Abyss ended in 5.3, and the people of Natlan may now leave their homeland.
### ULTRA +
The wiki lists no story changes at Coatepec Mountain after 5.0; version 5.4 only fixed a rock near the mountain and the Ancestral Temple.

## entry: Turnfire and Yupanqui
@keys: Turnfire, Malipo, Turnfire Night, Firethief, Yupanqui
@version: 5.0
### STANDARD
The Turnfire is a fire from the age of the Grand Alliance that tyrant Och-Kan used as a weapon; anyone it marked felt a terrible burning behind them and would be burned to a crisp if they turned around. Yupanqui stole it and used it against Och-Kan's army, but he looked back and burned. 'Malipo' is now an Ancient Name, borne by Burkina and by Kinich. The Scions of the Canopy hold Turnfire Night to honour their ancestors.
### LARGE +
Yupanqui was a companion of the first Pyro Archon Xbalanque in his youth and lost an eye on that journey; later he worked for the Grand Alliance seeking peace between humans and saurians, joined the Scarlet-Eyed Youth's rebellion against Och-Kan and died with the Turnfire.

## entry: Kongamato, the Mountain King
@keys: Kongamato, Mountain King, Mountain King Flamegranate
@version: 5.0
### STANDARD
Kongamato is an enormous Yumkasaurus who has lived since the cataclysm and holds a special place in the Scions of the Canopy's heart. Abyssal corruption keeps him alive but also threatens to overwhelm him, so the tribe performs a ritual to drain it. After The Last Turnfire Night the corruption is stabilised, and he offers to train the tribe's young. The Archive text jokes that he is so large because he has lived so long and eaten too much.

## entry: Phlogiston Extraction Research Center
@keys: Phlogiston Extraction Research Center, Polychrome Tri-Stars, Vasily, Sidorenko, Nomokonov
@keys2: Fatui, Natlan, saurian
### LARGE
A secret underground base operated by the Fatui, where they extracted Phlogiston from captured saurians. Fontaine Research Institute equipment was used. The notes of its soldiers mention the Polychrome Tri-Stars, a group who captured saurians on behalf of Capitano's forces; the Traveler eventually stopped this effort.
