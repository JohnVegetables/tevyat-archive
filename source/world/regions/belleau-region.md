## summary
### LITE
The Belleau Region is the western part of Fontaine's map. It holds Poisson, a fishing town built in an old ship and a cavern below sea level, which is the main base of the Spina di Rosula; Romaritime Harbor, a waterfall elevator and lookout where Fontaine's aquabus line meets the way to Sumeru; and the western slopes of Mont Automnequi. In the Archon Quest it is where the Primordial Seawater disaster strikes Poisson.
### STANDARD +
The name looks like French for "beautiful water". The area's boss is the Emperor of Fire and Iron, and its local specialties are Beryl Conch, Lumidouce Bell, Rainbow Rose and Romaritime Flower.

## geography
### STANDARD
Belleau covers Poisson, Romaritime Harbor and the West Slopes of Mont Automnequi. Mont Automnequi is one of Fontaine's best-known peaks and spans both the Belleau and Morte regions; this region has its western side. A tunnel, the Poisson Tunnel, cuts through those slopes so that the Clementine Line aquabus can pass. Fontaine is mostly water, but Belleau is one of the places where mountains, forest and cliffs show.
### LARGE +
Romaritime Harbor stands beside a large waterfall, with a lower and an upper level joined by an elevator that takes a long time to reach the top. The harbour also provides passage to the Girdle of the Sands in Sumeru.

## places
### STANDARD
- **Poisson**: fishing town at the foot of Mont Automnequi, built from a great ship and a large cavern below sea level.
- **Romaritime Harbor**: coastal elevator and observation deck on a waterfall, on the aquabus rail towards the Court of Fontaine.
- **West Slopes of Mont Automnequi**: the mountainside west of the peak, containing the Poisson Tunnel.
### LARGE +
- **Poisson Tunnel**: the tunnel for the Clementine Line. The Fontaine Research Institute brought in huge engineering devices, including a boring machine nicknamed the Golden Vessel, to dig it. Its success led to a new generation of clockwork technology such as the Construction Specialist Mek.
- Romaritime Harbor has a Statue of The Seven and a Waverider Waypoint. Harbour staff include a harbour guard, sailors, a boat captain and Marechaussee Phantom members.

## people
### STANDARD
Poisson's residents mostly work in fishing or underwater salvage. The Spina di Rosula, a volunteer organisation that helps Fontaine's people with matters the Gardes cannot or will not handle, is based here; its president is Navia, who took over from her father Callas. Romaritime Harbor is staffed by sailors, harbour security, a member of the Gardes and a few travellers.
### LARGE +
Poisson also has porters, a fisher, an engineer and Spina members such as the diver Archenoul. Several Poisson residents disappear or leave during the Archon Quest disaster and some return after it.

## history
### STANDARD
Legends say that before the first diluvian period Poisson was a central hub of Fontaine's maritime traffic. When the water level changed it lost its role as a major port but kept its cosy reputation. Roughly 500 years before the game's present, before the Cataclysm, the Siege of Poisson took place (see the Poisson entry): a violent episode in which the town was razed and many residents burned.
### LARGE +
According to the wiki's compilation of quest texts, the siege began after Eduardo Baker, a vigilante peacekeeper arrested during the purge of Fleuve Cendre, was freed by his followers, who then seized Poisson and took Maison Gardiennage staff hostage. Marechaussee Hunter Emanuel Guillotin led the response. Renault de Petrichor and his wife were probably killed; his son Rene and Jakob survived and were sent to the Narzissenkreuz Institute.
### ULTRA +
The Fontaine Research Institute dug the Poisson Tunnel as one of the answers to the problem of linking the aquabus lines. A different aquabus route, the Callas Line, was shut down after the Institute's explosion (see the Fontaine Research Institute of Kinetic Energy Engineering Region).

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Romaritime Harbor is noisy: the waterfall drowns out the comings and goings of the harbour, and the trip up is long, but travellers who board the Clementine Line feel the wait is worth it. Poisson is a quiet, cosy town of fishing boats and salvage divers; after the disaster it is subdued and busy with relief work.

## dangers
### STANDARD
The region's boss is the Emperor of Fire and Iron. The great danger in the story is Primordial Seawater, which dissolves living people who touch it and which drowns the lower levels of Poisson during the disaster.

## travel
### STANDARD
The aquabus runs from Romaritime Harbor to the Court of Fontaine. The harbour also gives onward passage to the Girdle of the Sands in Sumeru. Poisson sits on the Clementine Line at the foot of Mont Automnequi.

## quests
### STANDARD
Archon Quest Chapter IV, Act V (Masquerade of the Guilty, 4.2) is set largely here: the quests Meeting Is Also Parting, Hunters, Prophets and Apocalypse are filed under the region. The rest are world quests, commissions and the like.
### LARGE +
Other quests the wiki files under the region include Days Beneath the Waves, Birds in a Cage, Yesterday's Tears, Tomorrow's Light, The Child Who Doesn't Exist, A Lady's Invitation, Sea Breeze Rendezvous and Operation Downpour Simulation.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 Poisson is recovering from the Primordial Seawater disaster and no later content has changed the region.
### STANDARD +
The Spina di Rosula still runs from Poisson under Navia. The Fontaine Research Institute's tunnel and the aquabus remain in use.

## entry: Disaster at Poisson
@keys: Poisson disaster, Deluge of Wrathful Waters
@version: 4.2
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
At the start of Archon Quest Chapter IV, Act V, an earthquake near Poisson is followed by water that rises quickly and then recedes. It turns out to be Primordial Seawater, which dissolves living beings. The lower levels of the town are hit hardest. Navia coordinates relief work, accepts Fatui help arranged by Arlecchino, and loses her friends Melus and Silver, who were swept away while carrying out the rescue plan she had started.
### LARGE +
Neuvillette arrives separately and finds the Fatui helping. Navia later falls into the water and dreams of a trial at the Opera Epiclese in which the disaster's victims urge her to join them; Neuvillette rescues her and believes two Oceanids shielded her. The ruins near Poisson where the House of the Hearth heard that a child had found something are where stone slates about the Prophecy of Fontaine are found.

## entry: Romaritime Harbor and the Clementine Line
@keys: Romaritime, Clementine Line, Waverider
@version: 4.0
### STANDARD
Romaritime Harbor is a coastal elevator and observation deck built on the side of a waterfall, at the end of the aquabus rail running to the Court of Fontaine. The Clementine Line goes through the Poisson Tunnel; the harbour also provides passage to the Girdle of the Sands, Sumeru.
### LARGE +
The lower level has dock workers and a harbour security officer; the upper level has a boat captain, Marechaussee Phantom members and a self-proclaimed adventurer.
