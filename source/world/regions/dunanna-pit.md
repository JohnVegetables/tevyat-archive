## summary
### LITE
Dunanna Pit is a mist-covered, island-like area of Nod-Krai added in 6.7 (Luna VIII), lying close to Fontaine and east of Pohjola. Its centre is Dunanna Forward Station, a half-buried ruin of a vanished civilisation with a huge mechanism and a chamber holding a spaceship. It is where the Moon Gazing quests start and where the road to the Frost Moon opens.
### STANDARD +
The area has no Meeting Point of its own. It shares the Ancient Moon's Remnant at the Nuur Stone Circle (on the Frost Moon's Lunar Highlands) for Luna Treasure Compass progress. Few people are met here; it is a place of ruins, mist and machinery rather than settlements.

## geography
### STANDARD
Dunanna Pit is a separate area of Nod-Krai, reached by sea from the main isles. The wiki places it near Fontaine and east of Pohjola, though the Pohjola page also counts it as part of that island (see uncertainties). Its named subareas are Misty Shoal, a shore under a strangely cloudless bright-blue sky, and Dunanna Forward Station, the ruin around the pit itself. A viewpoint text calls the place a deserted, mist-shrouded island with traces of a bygone civilisation, and the 'pit' of the name is a huge hole at the heart of the island.
### LARGE +
The station has an upper level on the surface and a lower level underground. The Main Control Hall is on the lower level and, per its map description, is the central room for receiving and sending every command in the station. The area introduces the Gravimeter, an exploration mechanic that exists only here, and a Statue of the Seven. Unlocking that statue completes the achievement 'A Lamentation of Catastrophe'. The achievements for this area and the Frost Moon sit in a category called Unfettered Crescent.

## places
### STANDARD
Misty Shoal: the landing shore, where the Moon Gazing quest begins. Dunanna Forward Station: the buried ruin and its mechanism; the Main Control Hall is its core.
### LARGE +
The station is dotted with viewpoints. One, 'Mystery Pit of the Misty Isle', appears after the third step of the opening quest, Pioneers. Another, 'Moonbound Ship', appears after step ten and describes the Tonatiuh trembling as a heavenly ship sets sail for the moon. The area is linked to the Frost Moon through the mechanism described in the quests section.

## people
### LARGE
No residents are listed for the area. The figure the Traveler meets in the opening quest is not a person: it is a black kuuhenki (a kuuvahki-formed being) wearing Columbina's likeness. The real Columbina does not appear in the station during Pioneers.

## history
### LARGE
Canon gives no date or name for the civilisation that built the station. The only statements are the viewpoint's reference to a bygone civilisation and the Main Control Hall's description of a working command centre. The area was released in 6.7 with the Frost Moon map, which also introduced the Precursor's Secret Source Matrix of Overseer Network enemy and the Ancient Moon's Remnant system.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Fog, a clear but strangely cloudless sky over the shoal, silence, cold metal underground, and the feeling that a machine is waiting to be woken. Mood is eerie rather than hostile until the safety systems wake.
### LARGE +
Because the shoal is cloud-free while the station is wrapped in mist, scenes can contrast the bright empty sky above with dim machinery below.

## dangers
### LARGE
In Pioneers, once the Traveler tries to board the spaceship, the safety system flags them as intruders and attacks. Aside from that, canon lists no special hazards for the area.

## travel
### LARGE
Reached by travelling to the area on the Nod-Krai map. There is no town or Meeting Point; progress is tracked with the Frost Moon's Ancient Moon's Remnant. A Teleport Waypoint network and the Luna Treasure Compass are available as in other Nod-Krai areas.

## quests
### STANDARD
Moon Gazing (6.7) is the world quest series that unlocks the Frost Moon. Its first act, Pioneers, begins at Misty Shoal.
### LARGE +
In Pioneers, the Traveler and Paimon see Columbina walking ahead and follow her to Dunanna Forward Station, but she does not answer them. Underground they find a disassembled giant mechanism and a dead energy device. The 'Columbina' figure grants the Traveler Archaeolune Energy, which powers the device. The mechanism, restored, shows a large blue sphere circled by three smaller spheres standing for the Three Moons, and a console lets the user pick Trilune coordinates; only the Frost Moon will connect. When the Traveler asks her to take their hand, as in Silvermoon Hall, she turns into a black kuuhenki and flees. The chase ends in a vast chamber with a spaceship, whose defences attack. Later acts are Visitors From the Stars and Project Moongazer; the wiki has no summary of them.
### ULTRA +
The series is notable for being the first exploration world quest series with voice-overs and fully animated cutscenes, in the manner of Archon, Story and Event quests. Its achievements fall under Unfettered Crescent.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the area is simply open to explore. No later change to it is recorded.
### STANDARD +
Moon Gazing continues past the opening act, with the Frost Moon open for exploration. The Project Moongazer thread is covered by its own entry.

## entry: Dunanna Forward Station
@keys: Dunanna Forward Station, Main Control Hall, Misty Shoal
@version: 6.7
### STANDARD
The Dunanna Forward Station is the buried ruin at the centre of Dunanna Pit. Its lower level holds the Main Control Hall, the station's central command room, and a large mechanism that is disassembled until the Traveler restores it with Archaeolune Energy. The mechanism shows the Three Moons as spheres and can reach only the Frost Moon. The builders are not named. A viewpoint calls the island deserted and shrouded in mist, with traces of a lost civilisation.
### LARGE +
Beneath the control area is a chamber containing a spaceship whose safety system attacks anyone who tries to board without clearance. The viewpoint 'Moonbound Ship' later mentions the Tonatiuh and a heavenly ship setting sail for the moon.

## entry: Moon Gazing and the Pioneers Quest
@keys: Moon Gazing, Pioneers
@keys2: Dunanna, Frost Moon, Columbina
@version: 6.7
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Moon Gazing is the world quest series (6.7) that unlocks the Frost Moon. Act I, Pioneers, sends the Traveler and Paimon to Dunanna Forward Station after a figure that looks like Columbina but never answers. It turns out to be a black kuuhenki. The restored mechanism can link only to the Frost Moon, and the chase ends at a spaceship that tries to repel them.
### LARGE +
The series continues in Act II (Visitors From the Stars) and Act III (Project Moongazer). Detailed plot of those acts is not recorded in the sources used here.
