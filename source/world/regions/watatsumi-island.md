## summary
### LITE
Watatsumi Island is the Inazuman island farthest from the capital, a lush land of giant coral and brightly coloured plants unlike anything elsewhere. The serpent god Orobashi raised it from his own coral some two thousand years ago so that his people could leave the undersea realm of Enkanomiya. Ei later killed him, but the islanders still worship him as the Watatsumi Omikami. Their Divine Priestess, Sangonomiya Kokomi, governs from Sangonomiya Shrine. Watatsumi led the armed resistance against the Vision Hunt Decree, and since the decree's repeal it has kept an uneasy ceasefire with the Shogunate.
### STANDARD +
Its distance from the capital let the islanders build their own culture and customs, and its strained relationship with the Shogun is common knowledge across Inazuma.

## geography
### STANDARD
Mountains, waterfalls and towering coral surround Sangonomiya Shrine at the island's centre. Bourou Village stands nearby. In the south lies Suigetsu Pool, with ancient ruins above and below the water, and Mouun Shrine overlooks the sea from a high cliff. A pool beneath Sangonomiya Shrine, the Moon-Bathed Deep, is the way down to Enkanomiya.
### LARGE +
The island's giant corals are branches broken from Orobashi's body. The soil is poor in resources and threatened by "Holy Soil", in which the ground bleaches and erodes and would eventually turn to desert if left alone. Five "Goshou Rocks", called the eye, fang, fin, tail and heart, anchor the barrier between Watatsumi and Enkanomiya.

## places
### STANDARD
- **Sangonomiya Shrine**: seat of the Sangonomiya Clan and headquarters of the resistance; the present shrine was built after Orobashi's death.
- **Bourou Village**: an ancient village whose people trace their ancestry to the deep; the Watatsumi Army's training camp and rallying point.
- **Suigetsu Pool**: a pool hiding the sunken mansion of Umigozen, a once-famous Inazuman figure (the domain Palace in a Pool).
- **Mouun Shrine**: a cliff-top shrine overlooking the ocean.

## people
### STANDARD
The islanders descend from the people of Enkanomiya. Kokomi is Divine Priestess and the resistance's strategist; Gorou is her general. Shrine maidens trained at Sangonomiya Shrine serve Orobashi rather than the Sacred Sakura; they include Izumi Naki, Mochizuki, Tsuyuko, Shizuru and Miwa.
### LARGE +
Bourou Village has a village elder, Komaki, and a pharmacist, Nishikino Reirei. Watatsumi soldiers, the "Sangonomiya Samurai", are stationed around the island. A researcher, Kaushik, can be found at Mouun Shrine.

## governance
### LARGE
Watatsumi is formally part of the Shogunate's realm but has largely governed itself under the Sangonomiya Clan since Orobashi's defeat. During the war Kokomi ran the island's affairs and army; afterwards she issued temporary decrees, such as a ban on price gouging, to steady its damaged economy.

## religion
### LARGE
The islanders worship Orobashi as the Watatsumi Omikami, not the Raiden Shogun; the island's name comes from the phrase "the god of the oceans" in Watatsumi's ancient language. After Ei killed him she allowed this worship to continue. According to Kokomi, an earlier shrine near the island's Statue of The Seven had also honoured the Raiden Shogun, but relations soured after Orobashi's death. She hopes to raise this grievance in the peace talks.

## history
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Orobashi, a god who fled the Archon War, became the god of the people of Enkanomiya and replaced their ancient language with Narukami's. About two thousand years ago he broke off coral from his body to create Watatsumi Island, his people climbed up to it, and he taught them to farm and smelt. For years they respected their border with the Shogunate. Driven by famine, they then urged him to invade fertile Yashiori Island, and Ei killed him there. Watatsumi accepted her rule, but tension lasted. Under the Vision Hunt Decree it broke into open war, with the Watatsumi Army fighting the Tenryou Commission's forces at Yashiori and Kannazuka.
### LARGE +
An anonymous sponsor supplied the resistance so that it would keep fighting. The sponsor turned out to be the Fatui, and the "secret weapons" they provided were Delusions that drained their users' lives. One of the victims was the Traveler's friend Teppei. The Fatui mole Nathan had infiltrated the ranks. Orobashi had in fact been sentenced to death by the Heavenly Principles for learning forbidden knowledge, and he saw the invasion as a way to meet that sentence. The true reason was kept secret; most people accept that he attacked Yashiori for its resources, though rumours that Celestia ordered his death persist. After the decree was repealed, the island held a Victory Feast and peace talks began with Kujou Sara at a site arranged by the Kamisato Clan. Rumours of Tenryou-Fatui collusion and a Fatui plot unsettled soldiers who had expected to die for the cause.
### ULTRA +
The resistance's units were named after fish (Herring, Stingray, Swordfish, Gorgasia), and the Traveler briefly captained the special-operations unit Swordfish II. Kaedehara Kazuha was sheltered by the resistance before he left Inazuma with the Crux.

## culture
### STANDARD
The island's customs grew apart from Narukami's, rooted in descent from the deep and in reverence for the sea serpent. The Watatsumi Goryou Matsuri ritual protects the soil and needs a Bloodbranch (Jade) Coral Branch from Enkanomiya.
### LARGE +
In The Moon-Bathed Deep the shrine maiden Tsuyuko, who is in charge of the Goryou Matsuri, asked the Traveler to open the barrier at the Goshou Rocks so that coral could be recovered from Enkanomiya.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Pastel coral arches, violet and teal foliage, waterfalls and the sound of the sea. Soldiers drill at Bourou Village, and shrine maidens move about the pearl-and-coral shrine. The people are proud and close-knit, slow to trust the capital, and since the war they have been weary but hopeful.

## dangers
### LARGE
Fatui remnants hid on the island after the war. Ruin Sentinels and a dormant Ruin Guard lie around Suigetsu Pool. Ronin bands have troubled the south-east. The way down into Enkanomiya leads to vishaps and Abyss Order activity.

## quests
### LARGE
In the Inazuma Archon Quest the Traveler joins the resistance at the end of Chapter II Act II (2.0), and Act III (2.1) opens on Watatsumi; as is Kokomi's story quest about the post-war Victory Feast and peace talks. World quests include The Moon-Bathed Deep, which opens Enkanomiya.

## status
### STANDARD
As of 7.1 a ceasefire holds and the island keeps its own army as a peacekeeping force, rebuilding its economy and clearing out the Fatui. Talks on Watatsumi's return to Shogunate rule have made some progress but are paused. No Watatsumi-specific story content appears in 6.x-7.1.

## entry: Orobashi and the Watatsumi Omikami
@keys: Orobashi, Watatsumi Omikami, Orobashi no Mikoto
@keys2: Watatsumi, Sangonomiya, coral, Enkanomiya, Yashiori
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Orobashi no Mikoto, the giant serpent god worshipped on Watatsumi as the Watatsumi Omikami, led his people up from Enkanomiya and raised Watatsumi Island from his coral. When famine struck he invaded Yashiori Island and was slain there by Ei's Musou no Hitotachi. His lingering power became the Tatarigami, and his remains still lie on Yashiori. He had been condemned by the Heavenly Principles for reading forbidden knowledge, and he chose the invasion partly to meet that fate. His people still revere him.

## entry: Holy Soil and the Watatsumi Coral Ritual
@keys: Holy Soil, Watatsumi Goryou Matsuri, Watatsumi Mitama Omatsuri, Jade Coral Branch, Bloodbranch Coral, Goshou Rocks
### LARGE
Legend says the depths of Enkanomiya try to draw away Watatsumi's spirit, bleaching and eroding the soil, a blight called "Holy Soil". To hold it back the shrine performs a ritual that needs a coral branch from Enkanomiya. Reaching the coral means opening the barrier anchored by the five Goshou Rocks and descending through the Moon-Bathed Deep beneath Sangonomiya Shrine. The shrine maiden Tsuyuko, who studied at the Sumeru Akademiya, thinks the salinity and alkalinity of seawater are the real cause, and that the coral works because Orobashi's power gives it nutrients for the soil.

## entry: Three Realms Gateway Offering
@keys: Three Realms Gateway Offering, Bokuso Box
@version: 2.5

### LARGE
During the limited-time event Three Realms Gateway Offering (2.5), darkness of unknown origin covered Enkanomiya and Kokomi commissioned the Traveler to go down again for the sake of Watatsumi's future. Afterwards Kokomi resolved to find a new way to counter the Holy Soil that would no longer disturb the vishaps of the depths.

## entry: The Dragon of Water Prophecy
@keys: Dragon of Water
@keys2: Watatsumi, Enkanomiya, prophecy
@spoiler: major
### ULTRA
An old Enkanomiya prophecy, recorded in The Byakuyakoku Collection, foretells the rebirth of the Dragon of Water in human form. Fontaine's later story revealed Neuvillette to be the Hydro Dragon in human form, and the prophecy is taken to refer to him.
