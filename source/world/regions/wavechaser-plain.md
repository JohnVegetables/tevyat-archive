## summary
### LITE
Wavechaser Plain is the central, coastal plain of Pohjola, the large island of northern Nod-Krai (added in 6.3). It holds the Pillar of Embla, a tower that the scholar Rotwang built to break the sky, the Knights of Favonius outpost Favonius Keep, the lake Amsvartnir and the barrier islands of the Wing of Keres.
### STANDARD +
Past and present sit side by side here: an Abyssal tower from the Cataclysm, Frostmoon Scion relics, and the tents of Mondstadt's knights, whose Grand Master's expedition made camp in Nod-Krai.

## geography
### STANDARD
A wide plain beside the sea in the middle of Pohjola, bordered by Voidsea Outlook to the south and Ashveil Peak to the north. The Pillar of Embla rises from it, Lake Amsvartnir lies north of Piramida, and the Wing of Keres is a chain of barrier islands off the western side.
### LARGE +
Local specialties: Winter Icelea and Pine Amber. The plain has a Radiant Moongecko as a boss. Exploration features include Moonlane paths, Lunar Conflux Barriers and Abyssal riftline puzzles, and the Pillar of Embla has Abyssal Fog Barriers, Abyssal Riftlines, Evil Eyes of Blightseep and Solaris Core puzzles.

## places
### STANDARD
Pillar of Embla: a divine pillar rooted in the earth and reaching skyward, built by Rauthvangi. Favonius Keep: the Knights' camp. Lake Amsvartnir: a lake with a sacred forest and a legend of a hidden court. Wing of Keres: barrier islands with Verdant Crests and other Frostmoon Scion artifacts.
### LARGE +
The pillar's loading-screen text says its presence has caused irreversible changes to the surroundings over the last five hundred years. Amsvartnir's name echoes the Old Norse lake where a wolf was bound. A viewpoint at the lake is called Palaces of Times Bygone. Ancient Handwriting appears at the lake during the quest Echoes of a Forsaken Song. The Wing of Keres hides a letter in a time capsule tied to the quest A Distant Dream of Elysium.

## people
### STANDARD
Favonius Keep is staffed by the Knights of Favonius Expedition Team: Garrison Commander Anselm and Knights including Ekbert, Erminie, Laurent, Leifhelm, Nannina and Winfried; Godwin joins after Varka's story quest.
### LARGE +
In Tower of Inversion the Traveler works with Illuga, the Lightkeeper Vlaicu; at the top of the tower the quest steps have the Traveler talk to the historical Alia.

## governance
### STANDARD
No local government. The Knights of Favonius run the Keep as an expeditionary garrison, and Lightkeepers watch the surroundings.

## history
### STANDARD
Five hundred years ago, during the Cataclysm, Rauthvangi (known at the Belyi Tsar's court as Rotwang) came to Nod-Krai and raised the Pillar of Embla to reach the false sky and open the way to the Abyss, which he called his 'Elysium'. The Scourge of Endless Decay aided him. According to Frostmoon Scion records, Aila went to the pillar at Solovei's request and ended the Abyssal catastrophe.
### LARGE +
The Lost Notes give a later layer: a group from Mondstadt following Master Roland, a knight devoted to chivalry, marched west expecting to end their campaign in Natlan but did not. In Nod-Krai they met monster hunters calling themselves the Lightkeepers, who invited them to join; Roland refused, saying the land must be cleansed with blood, and that people who seize chances to rob, and desperate outlaws, were targets of the purge. The Lightkeepers lent the group a lighthouse as shelter. Abyssal monsters pressed in and locals avoided them. The author, the last of the group, held the lighthouse alone after refusing local help, while Roland went to track monsters.
### ULTRA +
Another Lightkeeper's diary, found at the pillar, describes a Ratnik newly assigned to a support unit at Kipumaki Cliff with Captain Asebov, Chernov, Shonokovych and Patolichev, and ends with an urgent call for reinforcement against an outbreak of Wild Hunt.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Salt wind off the sea, a tall pillar dominating the skyline, ruined stone, the smell of dandelion wine at the Keep and the sense of old failures. The Lost Notes' tone is exhausted and conflicted: the writer doubts whether the campaign saves people or merely kills.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Wild Hunt and Abyssal enemies, Abyssal Riftlines and Fog Barriers around the pillar, and, among the Wing of Keres islands, Evil Eye of Blightseep puzzles. Pale-furred wolves roam Amsvartnir.

## travel
### LARGE
Favonius Keep has a Meeting Point at the large tent. The road runs from Voidsea Outlook north through the plain toward Ashveil Peak.

## quests
### STANDARD
Tower of Inversion (world quest, Nightingale's Song Act I, part 2; 6.3): the Traveler and Illuga enter the Pillar of Embla, purify its core and restore its barrier cores. It leads into When War Songs Rise in Ashveil Peak. Echoes of a Forsaken Song, the quest that unlocks the Ancient Handwriting, also touches the lake.
### LARGE +
Inside the tower, the Traveler cleanses corruption from three cores (south, east, north) and then touches the top core, after which they talk to Alia, a historical figure. Completing the quest earns the Silver Lampwick.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the plain is open to explore; the Pillar of Embla has been cleansed and the Knights' camp remains.
### STANDARD +
Varka returned to Mondstadt in 6.4 but still travels to the outpost. Nothing in 7.0-7.1 is recorded as changing the plain.

## entry: Pillar of Embla
@keys: Pillar of Embla, Rauthvangi, Rotwang
@keys2: Nod-Krai, Illuga, false sky, Elysium
@version: 6.3
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
A tower built by Rauthvangi (Rotwang) to reach the false sky and ascend to his 'Pitch-black Elysium'. Aila ended the catastrophe at Solovei's request, 500 years ago, and the pillar still shapes its surroundings. It appears in a corrupted and a cleansed form.
### LARGE +
Rauthvangi was a Khaenri'ahn scholar exiled for his obsession with the Abyss who later entered the Belyi Tsar's court; he was killed by the Traveler and Illuga in the later quest The Last Night, the First Light.

## entry: Favonius Keep
@keys: Favonius Keep, Anselm
@keys2: Nod-Krai, Knights of Favonius
@version: 6.3
### STANDARD
The Knights of Favonius' outpost and expedition camp in Nod-Krai, with a large tent as its Meeting Point. Its map text describes warm dandelion-wine-scented breezes and a quote about stories gathering wherever people live.
### LARGE +
See the Mondstadt entry for the Varka expedition. Garrison Commander Anselm leads the Expedition Team stationed there.

## entry: Lake Amsvartnir
@keys: Amsvartnir, Lake Amsvartnir, Royal Court of the Seelie
@version: 6.3
### STANDARD
A lake north of Piramida, above which the Royal Court of the Seelie is said to lie. Legend says whoever breaks the golden branch in the lake's sacred forest finds the court, which is hidden from mortal eyes by day.

## entry: Wing of Keres
@keys: Wing of Keres
@version: 6.3
### STANDARD
The barrier islands on the western side of Wavechaser Plain, decorated with Verdant Crests and other Frostmoon Scion artifacts. The name probably alludes to the winged death spirits of Greek myth (an editor's note, not stated in the game).
