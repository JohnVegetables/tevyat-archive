## summary
### LITE
The Girdle of the Sands is a remote desert subregion in the northwest of Sumeru, beyond the Temir Mountains. Here, during the cataclysm 500 years ago, Abyssal monsters poured up through a rift from the ruined kingdom of Khaenri'ah below, and the former Hydro Archon Egeria was slain there fighting them. Her remains became the Amrita, a sea of pure dew in the Vourukasha Oasis, where the Pari, flower-like bird spirits born of the divine bird Simurgh's Khvarena, still stand guard. Its two areas are Gavireh Lajavard and the Realm of Farakhkert.
### STANDARD +
It is an old war zone that never fully healed, a place of rock, swamps and sealed ruins. Travellers find the Order of Skeptics, a small band of scholars descended from rainforest Akademiya researchers, camped near the Temir Mountains.

## geography
### STANDARD
Gavireh Lajavard ("azure desert") holds the Gate of Zulqarnain, the Temir Mountains (the main passage from the Desert of Hadramaveth) and Tunigi Hollow. The Realm of Farakhkert holds the Asipattravana Swamp, the Hills of Barsom, the Samudra Coast (the far northwest border of the Sumeru desert, with sandy beaches and clear blue water) and the Vourukasha Oasis at its heart. After the Mondstadt Prologue's final act, a Teleport Waypoint here, meant to give quick access to Fontaine, can also serve as quick access to the Sumeru desert.
### LARGE +
The name Gavireh Lajavard comes from Persian words for "desert" and "lapis lazuli"; Farakhkert is the Middle Persian name of the cosmic sea Vourukasha. Below Tunigi Hollow lie Khaenri'ahn ruins, including the abandoned factory Hangeh Afrasiyab, which contains a sealed gate to the lost nation itself.

## places
### LARGE
Tunigi Hollow is the Abyssal rift opened by the cataclysm and sealed by the Harvisptokhm. Vourukasha Oasis is the Pari's homeland, with a sea of Amrita and, at its centre, the great tree Harvisptokhm. The Asipattravana Swamp lies in a thorn-filled canyon just north of the Oasis and is guarded by the Pari Mihir. The Hills of Barsom are home to a huge pine with pink foliage, the Barsom, and its familiar, the Pari Rashnu.
### ULTRA +
Hangeh Afrasiyab was the last factory in Khaenri'ah to use an entirely Azosite-powered system. Notes in the ruins mention Knight Marshal Anfortas taking the regency over the Regnum Concilium Ultimum, and a Schwanenritter garrison commander named Hadura ordering survivors evacuated. Hastily scrawled notes at a camp in Gavireh Lajavard guess that Khvarena and the Abyss are of the same order, both able to "re-write the rules".

## people
### STANDARD
The Pari are a sentient form of Khvarena in avian, flower-like bodies, each with a fated duty they carry out alone. Zurvan is the First Pari and elder; Sorush, Mihir and Rashnu are others. The Order of Skeptics, also called Shamaniyya or the Nagarjunites, is a small group of scholars (Pyrrho and Sosi among them) descended from rainforest researchers.
### LARGE +
Humans who bond with a Pari are called Yasnapati and the bond is Laupa. Pari rejected by the Oasis after Abyssal taint turn into Fravashi Trees, and their Yasnapati are meant to bring them home. At life's end a Pari becomes a Plume of Purifying Light to be returned to the Amrita Pool.

## governance
### LARGE
No settled government. Zurvan, the eldest Pari, decides who enters the Oasis. She sealed it with the Great Songs of Khvarena after the Abyss attacked, and says only heroes who have formed bonds with Pari and pilgrims who wish to worship Egeria may enter. The Order of Skeptics keeps its own leader, a Nasuraia or Vijnanapati, but its last holder defected.

## culture
### LARGE
The Order of Skeptics holds that knowledge must be transmitted at all costs and that conflict is the natural order of Teyvat. Its ritual music, the Korybantes and Kory Drums, was once believed to be needed to bond with the Pari. The Pari use flower-themed endearments such as "good petal".
### ULTRA +
In practice Rashnu found the Skeptics' drum ceremony too noisy and tossed the drums away herself, so the bond may never have depended on them.

## history
### STANDARD
Long ago the goddess Nabu Malikata foresaw the cataclysm, made a shard of Khvarena and entrusted it to Greater Lord Rukkhadevata, who shaped it into the divine bird Simurgh. When the cataclysm came, the monsters' horde and the pollution of forbidden knowledge flowed from Khaenri'ah through Tunigi Hollow; Egeria and other gods went to suppress it, and she died there. Her body became the Amrita; Rukkhadevata grew the Harvisptokhm from it, and Simurgh sacrificed herself in the Amrita, shattering into countless Khvarena that became the Pari.
### LARGE +
Zurvan, the first Pari, found a wounded Dainsleif and brought him to the Oasis; with scholars from the rainforest, led by a one-armed sage, they found a way into the Harvisptokhm and performed a rite that purified the land and made new Pari. Zurvan fused the strongest Khvarena into five Great Songs, scattered four and hid the fifth in the Oasis. Dainsleif left with the Traveler's sibling; some scholars stayed and became the Order of Skeptics. The Schwanenritter evacuated Khaenri'ahn refugees and used Ruin Golems against the Abyss, winning the title "heroes of Dahri".
### ULTRA +
Centuries later an Akademiya team came to explore the Dahrian ruins despite the Skeptics' warning. Klingsor, a Skeptic descended from the Schwanenritter Hadura, accompanied them, embraced the Abyss and became an Abyss Herald; Abyssal attacks then forced Zurvan to seal the Oasis and the ruins with the Great Songs. The Skeptics lost their archives to fire and let their duty to the Harvisptokhm lapse. The Oceanids, created by Egeria, came to Tunigi Hollow looking for their master, found only the tree and scattered.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Bleak rock under a hard sky, canyons and thorn thickets, purple Abyssal mist where the defilement lingers, and then the contrast of the Oasis, where the Harvisptokhm now carries a glowing blue canopy. Underground, silent Khaenri'ahn machine halls and factories.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Abyssal defilement (purple mist, gray crystals and Defiled Beacons), Abyss Heralds, the Iniquitous Baptist, Consecrated Fanged Beasts and Horned Crocodiles, rifthounds, Fatui skirmishers and hilichurls (including those corrupted by Abyssal energy).
### LARGE +
The ruins beneath Tunigi Hollow hold monsters and Ruin Golems; Klingsor and Nasejuna were fought in a Khaenri'ahn factory. Pari that fall to Abyssal taint turn into Fravashi Trees.

## travel
### LARGE
Enter by the Temir Mountains from the Desert of Hadramaveth, or by the Teleport Waypoint described above. The Oasis admits only those the Pari accept. Flying as Sorush and channelling her Khvarena lets the Traveler suppress Gray Crystal barriers and deal with Fravashi Trees and Defiled Beacons, and the Yasnapati bond grants access.

## quests
### STANDARD
The Khvarena of Good and Evil world quest series (3.6) unfolds here: The Splendorous Sky That Day, Awakening's Real Sound, Asipattravana Itihasa, As the Khvarena's Light Shows, and The Hymn of Tir Yazad (Parts 1 and 2). The Traveler becomes Sorush's Yasnapati, helps her gather the Great Songs of Khvarena, learns that Nasejuna and Klingsor sought to break the Harvisptokhm's seal and cleanse the Sign of Apaosha, and completes the Rite of Chinvat and Rite of Ab-Zohr.
### LARGE +
Each stage: finding Pari Mihir (Asipattravana Swamp) and Rashnu (Hills of Barsom, the Kory Drum ritual), collecting five Great Songs with Nasejuna, defeating him and an Abyss Herald, placing the songs in Tunigi Hollow, then cleansing the Harvisptokhm. Zurvan tells of the cataclysm and says she hopes the Traveler will greet the masked swordsman, Dainsleif, on her behalf.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the Sign of Apaosha has vanished and the Harvisptokhm has regrown a glowing blue canopy. The Pari still guard the Oasis, which stays closed to strangers. Nasejuna has fled and the Order of Skeptics has no leader.
### STANDARD +
No version up to 7.1 adds new areas here; Sorush does appear in the 6.6 Archon Quest, bringing Aranara to the Traveler during Sumeru's Irminsul crisis. Zurvan asked the Traveler to give her greetings to Dainsleif. The Harvisptokhm is purified, but the Abyss's interest in the Great Songs and the open question of the Skeptics' future remain.

## entry: Pari
@keys: Pari, Plume of Purifying Light, Fravashi Tree, Yasnapati
@version: 3.6
### STANDARD
The Pari are avian, flower-like beings descended from Simurgh, created when her Khvarena combined with the Amrita. They are the only sentient form of Khvarena. Each has a fated task to undertake alone; many live only to serve it. They believe their god is Egeria, whose consciousness lies in the Harvisptokhm. They fought in the cataclysm with Khaenri'ahn warriors, a masked sword-wielder and a one-armed sage, and Akademiya scholars. Their human partners are called Yasnapati.
### LARGE +
Pari tainted by Abyssal corruption are rejected by the Oasis and become Fravashi Trees. Sunyata flowers and Farrwicks are also descendants of Simurgh's Khvarena.

## entry: Vourukasha Oasis and the Harvisptokhm
@keys: Vourukasha Oasis, Harvisptokhm, Amrita, Amrita Pool, Egeria, Gaokerena
@version: 3.6
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Vourukasha Oasis holds the Amrita, a sea of pure dew made from the remains of Egeria, the Hydro Archon who ruled Fontaine for about 1,500 years and died at Tunigi Hollow in the cataclysm. Rukkhadevata grew the Harvisptokhm, a giant tree, from the Amrita to anchor Egeria's consciousness and let her absorb the defilement from the rift. Inside lies the lotus Gaokerena, where the Pari god's consciousness slumbers. Before the 3.6 quest the tree was a withered stump under the Sign of Apaosha; afterwards it has a glowing blue canopy.
### LARGE +
Zurvan sealed the Oasis with the Great Songs of Khvarena. The Oceanids, Egeria's creations, came searching for their master, did not accept her successor Focalors, and scattered; pilgrims following their trail later established the Purification Spring.

## entry: Order of Skeptics
@keys: Order of Skeptics, Skeptics, Nagarjunites, Shamaniyya, Nagarjuna, Nasejuna, Vijnanapati
@version: 3.6
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
The Order of Skeptics, once a branch of the Akademiya, descends from scholars led by the one-armed sage Nagarjuna who came to help the Pari defeat the Abyss. They settled in the Girdle and forged bonds with the Pari. The Akademiya calls them Nagarjunites. After a disastrous Akademiya expedition into Khaenri'ahn ruins and the loss of their archives, they neglected their duty. Members included Pyrrho, Sosi, Aryadeva (who moved to Sumeru City) and Nagahvaya (now in the Temple of Space).
### LARGE +
Nasejuna, the last Vijnanapati, was manipulated by Klingsor into believing the Skeptics' history was myth; he pretended to serve Sorush, aiming to break the seal so the Abyss could cover Teyvat, and fled when defeated. Kaveh's father once asked to join an investigation to find the Order.

## entry: Tunigi Hollow and the Khaenri'ahn Ruins
@keys: Tunigi Hollow, Hangeh Afrasiyab, Gate of Everlasting Mourning, Dahri
@version: 3.6
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
Tunigi Hollow was the epicentre of Abyssal monsters and forbidden-knowledge pollution during the cataclysm; Rukkhadevata and Egeria went to suppress it. The monsters tore a great rift, which the Harvisptokhm later sealed; the Sign of Apaosha appeared above it while defilement remained. Below are Khaenri'ahn workshops, with notes on Azosite smelting, the Regent, and civilians who fled. A sealed gate to Khaenri'ah lies in the depths of Hangeh Afrasiyab, whose viewpoint is named "Gate of Everlasting Mourning". "Dahri" is the name desert texts and the Skeptics use for Khaenri'ah.

## entry: Simurgh and the Great Songs of Khvarena
@keys: Simurgh, Great Song of Khvarena, Great Songs of Khvarena, Khvarena, Sign of Apaosha
@version: 3.6
### STANDARD
Khvarena is an energy created by Nabu Malikata to oppose the cataclysm; it can purify Abyssal energy and forbidden knowledge. Simurgh was a shard of it; when Egeria's Amrita started to be corrupted she flew into the pool and shattered into countless motes. Zurvan fused the strongest Khvarena into five Great Songs. The Sign of Apaosha is a phenomenon in the sky above Tunigi Hollow, a reflection of the Abyss that corrupts the land beneath it.
### LARGE +
Nasejuna argued that the Sign was a reflection of the "reality" beyond Teyvat's skies, and that the Great Songs could unleash it on the world; the Great Songs can only be retrieved by a Pari.
