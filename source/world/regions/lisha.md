## summary
### LITE
Lisha ("Glaze Sand Outskirts") is the dry ruin-country of southwestern Liyue. Its crumbling stone sites date from the Archon War and earlier: the sunken-city ruins of Qingxu Pool, the fortress of Lingju Pass, the Dunyu Ruins hollowed by the cataclysm, and a small restored temple to a long-dead yaksha. The entrance to the Chasm and its mines lies on its edge, and its old surface mines have largely moved there.
### STANDARD +
Most of its history is told in tablets, notices and legends; few people live here now.

## geography
### STANDARD
Open, sandy-hued uplands of rock and glazed stone broken by ruins and pools. The river that flows through much of Liyue does not reach Lisha. A notice northwest of Qingxu Pool, by a group of ore investigation points, announces that the miners have moved to the Chasm.
### LARGE +
Cor Lapis and Noctilucous Jade are mined here, and Qingxin and violetgrass grow in its stony ground. One of its viewpoints is titled "Beyond the Chasm."

## places
### STANDARD
Qingxu Pool: ruins of a city said to have been submerged until the Archon War's end, with a tower rising from a green field. Lingju Pass: a ruined stronghold of puzzles and Geo monuments. Dunyu Ruins: ancient vestiges around a dead lake. Pervases' Temple: a small shrine to the yaksha Pervases, restored in the Hereafter quests.
### LARGE +
A settlement from which the miner Tieshan hails still exists around Qingxu Pool. Dunyu Ruins' name means "Fleeing Jade Mausoleum." Each of the three sites holds one of the Nameless Treasures that tell the story of Azhdaha's sealing.

## people
### LARGE
Few: miners, Treasure Hoarders, ruin investigators, and the people who live around Qingxu Pool. Wang Ping'an, a reformed fraud, took on the temple's restoration.

## governance
### LARGE
No local government is named; the Liyue Qixing hold authority, and once approved an excavation of Qingxu Pool that was abandoned when blasting through the rock proved impossible.

## culture
### LARGE
Poems and notices mark the ruins. A poem at Lingju Pass says there is a divine order to the world, that death comes to every mortal and that the creator shows favor to none. The expedition text for Dunyu Ruins says a large amount of treasure of unknown origin is still to be found there.
### ULTRA +
Pervases' temple is tended by incense offerings; after restoration the Traveler can burn Fine Incense before his statue, and Xiao has reflected there on the fate of the yaksha.

## history
### STANDARD
Lingju Pass is said to have been a stronghold of Morax where mortals and adepti fought side by side. Qingxu Pool's ruin predates the Archon War and sat submerged until the war ended. The dragon Azhdaha was said to have rampaged across Lisha before Rex Lapis and three adepti sealed him in the Chasm. In the cataclysm, the Dunyu Ruins and the Chasm were two Liyue places hit hardest; Dunyu's people abandoned the city for Liyue Harbor and have never explained why.
### LARGE +
According to a legend recorded in Records of Jueyun, the Dunyu Ruins were created by a fragment of the same meteorite, the "Solar Chariot," that fell at the Chasm. In Yelan's story quest, Fatui agents in Liyue sought the buried treasure of Qingxu Pool and used a Tianshu succession plot to reach it; the Traveler and Yelan caught the conspirators in the underground ruins.
### ULTRA +
Pervases, a junior yaksha who died over a thousand years ago, lingered in his statue and was the friend of the Prime Yaksha Bosacius. After the fraud Wang Ping'an repented, he restored the temple.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Glazed stone and old ruins, with a lone tower in a green field; Dunyu's dead lake holds its vestiges suspended in time.

## dangers
### LARGE
Treasure Hoarders, hilichurls and Ruin Guards among the ruins, and the Fatui who once hunted Qingxu's treasure.

## travel
### STANDARD
Travelers mostly pass through on the way to the Chasm, whose entrance is marked by the miners' notice near Qingxu Pool.

## quests
### LARGE
The Hereafter series (Pervases' temple restoration), Equilibrium, the Nameless Treasures hunt, ruin puzzles at Lingju Pass, Dunyu and Qingxu, and Yelan's Calculated Gambit, which touches the Qingxu Pool Underground Ruins.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 Lisha is quiet ruin country beside the Chasm; no Lisha-specific change is recorded in 6.x-7.1 content.
### STANDARD +
Pervases' Temple stands restored. The Chasm nearby is the focus of the 7.1 Celestial Nail anomaly, with extra Millelith patrols.

## entry: Qingxu Pool
@keys: Qingxu Pool, Qingxu's Forsaken Tower
@keys2: Liyue, Yelan, Fatui, Tieshan
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Qingxu Pool is a ruined tower amid a green field and, beneath it, sealed underground ruins that legend says belonged to an evil god's city, submerged until the Archon War ended. The Qixing once approved an excavation for the rumored buried treasure, but the ruins were sealed and the tech to blast through solid rock did not yet exist. Many groups kept hoping, including the Fatui. A Qingxu Pool Redevelopment Plan in Yelan's story quest covered a plot to use the Blackcliff Forge's explosives to open them. The local Tieshan comes from a settlement in the area.

## entry: Dunyu Ruins
@keys: Dunyu Ruins, Dunyu
@keys2: Chasm, cataclysm, Liyue
### STANDARD
Dunyu Ruins ("Fleeing Jade Mausoleum") is a set of ancient vestiges around a dead lake, suspended in time. During the cataclysm an iron meteorite hit the Chasm and monsters poured out; Dunyu was one of two Liyue sites most affected, and its people decided to abandon the city and move to Liyue Harbor. Neither the refugees nor the adepti have talked about what happened. One of the three Nameless Treasures is hidden here.

## entry: Lingju Pass
@keys: Lingju Pass, lingju
@keys2: Morax, Liyue, adepti
### STANDARD
Lingju Pass is a ruined stronghold that legend says Morax founded, where mortals and adepti fought together. Its Geo monument puzzles lead to a floating platform and chests, and a poem on a sign about the divine order of the world is thought to relate to Morax's lingju, his divine law.

## entry: Pervases' Temple
@keys: Pervases' Temple, Pervases, Wang Ping'an
@keys2: Xiao, yaksha, Liyue
@version: 1.0
### STANDARD
Pervases' Temple honors the junior yaksha Pervases, killed in the Archon War and said to linger in a crane statue. It began as a run-down house until the repentant fraud Wang Ping'an rebuilt it in the world quest series Hereafter, sinking his earnings into the project. After the Chasm, Xiao came back to the temple to think about the fate of the yakshas, and it was shown that Pervases was close to the Prime Yaksha Bosacius.
