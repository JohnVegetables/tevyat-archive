## summary
### LITE
The Chasm is a vast mining region on Liyue's western coast. A huge pit of layered, whorled cliffs sits above a maze of underground mines rich in ore and jade. In the cataclysm 500 years ago monsters poured out of it, and the Millelith and the Yaksha Bosacius died sealing them in. A few years ago the Liyue Qixing closed the mine after strange accidents. Since the Traveler's expedition (2.6) it has been reopening. A blue-glowing Celestial Nail, the Spiritstone, hangs in its depths.
### STANDARD +
Revealed in 6.3: the Chasm was once Lang-Gan, an ancient civilisation governed by the angel Zibai. A Celestial Nail sent to purge Abyssal taint buried it, and survivors fled to Chenyu Vale. In 7.1 the Spiritstone began emitting a strange light along with every other Nail in Teyvat, and Liyue now watches the Chasm closely.

## geography
### STANDARD
Two levels. On the surface is a giant bowl of stratified rock with spiral terraces, odd-coloured soil and plants, scaffolding and old mine works. Its areas include The Chasm's Maw (the mine entrance, with abandoned miners' quarters), Glaze Peak, Tiangong Gorge and Cinnabar Cliff. Below lie the Underground Mines, an unlit labyrinth of tunnels and caverns reached through the Maw and other shafts, under the Qixing's "Seven-Star Array" seal. Ancient texts sometimes call this underground the "Yu Valley".
### LARGE +
Underground subareas include Stony Halls, The Glowing Narrows, the Nameless Ruins (inverted ancient buildings around an upside-down pool) and the Court of Pillars, where the Spiritstone hangs. The ecologist Khedive believes perfectly preserved marine fossils deep in the mines show that the whole area was once seabed and was transformed in an instant. Fluorescent plants thrive below, and a withered tree like those at other Nail sites grows near Stony Halls.

## places
### LARGE
- **The Chasm's Maw**: the main entrance to the mines, with abandoned miners' quarters and an emergency notice ordering the miners to disperse.
- **Cinnabar Cliff**: the surface area with the Statue of the Seven. In 7.1 Xiao keeps watch here.
- **The Spiritstone** (the "Chasm Nail"): a huge sky-blue crystal pillar floating in the Court of Pillars, a Celestial Nail.
- **The Chasm's Bed**: a predatory sealed space beneath the Nail.
### ULTRA +
- **Stony Halls**: where miners found the "Wishstone", a fragment of the Spiritstone.
- **Nameless Ruins**: an underground "palace" exposed when the surface was damaged in the cataclysm, probably a remnant of the civilisation the Nail destroyed.
- **Exploration camp**: the Chasm Exploration Team's base underground.
- A relic of Rex Lapis's ancient battles stands on the surface, with a notice warning visitors not to disturb it.

## people
### LARGE
Muning, a Ministry of Civil Affairs specialist, runs the site from the camp at the mine entrance. He handles miners' return to work, Millelith rotations, contracts and endless weekly reports. Millelith guards such as Tie Hong hold the approach. The Chasm Exploration Team includes Zhiqiong (adventurer and cartographer), Khedive (ecologist), Jinwu (safety technician), Taliesin (bard) and Clitopho (a former Treasure Hoarder turned demolitions expert). After the expedition it became an official part of Huishan Hall.
### ULTRA +
Muning's family is prominent in Shenglu Hall and the porcelain trade. Zhiqiong grew up in Mingyun Village, whose mines ran dry. Driven to be remembered, she vanished into the depths after leaving a farewell letter. Yelan descends from the cataclysm-era thaumaturges Boyang and Rongzhao. In 7.1 Xiao watches the Chasm on Rex Lapis's orders.

## governance
### STANDARD
The mine belongs to Liyue and is administered by the Liyue Qixing through the Ministry of Civil Affairs, with the Millelith on guard. After a notice put the question to the Yuehai Pavilion, the Qixing halted all mining. They sealed the depths with the Seven-Star Array and let in only those with permission from Yujing Terrace or a special commission. The Qixing first agreed to let the Fatui investigate, but cancelled the deal after the events of Chapter I and ran their own investigation.

## economy
### LARGE
The Chasm is Liyue's most famous mine. Its specialities are Cor Lapis, Noctilucous Jade, Qingxin and Violetgrass, and its glazed sand crystals go into porcelain. The closure put many miners out of work. Reopening is slow, with work resumed in stages, recruitment notices posted, and the Feiyun Commerce Guild involved.
### ULTRA +
Lumenstone, a catalytic stone that blazes with light in the mines but loses its power once taken out, appeared along with the Nail fragment. Refined into the Lumenstone Adjuvant, it drives back the Abyssal Dark Mud. Dragonfall, a rare ore said to have formed when Morax and Azhdaha fought here, was "mined into obscurity" centuries ago.

## history
### STANDARD
In the age of the unified civilisation the land was Lang-Gan, a civilisation of mountain settlements built around mines. Its people worshipped Zibai as the God of Soil and Grain and offered to the Three Moons at a jade altar. After the war of funerary flame tainted Lang-Gan with the Abyss, the Heavenly Principles sent a Celestial Nail to purify it. Against Istaroth's advice, Zibai held the Nail back long enough for her people to escape, and it killed her. The impact buried Lang-Gan and carved out the Chasm. Survivors went east to Chenyu Vale and are suggested to have reached the Guili Assembly too. Liyue's people later mined the Chasm. During the cataclysm 500 years ago an "iron meteorite" struck, and Khaenri'ah's monsters poured out. Morax sent the Millelith to defend the region and evacuate the miners. They, the maddened Yaksha Bosacius and the thaumaturge Boyang drove the monsters into a space beneath the Nail and sealed it with the Fantastic Compass, sealing themselves in as well.
### LARGE +
About two years before the Traveler's expedition (according to the Millelith guard Tie Hong), an incident deep in the caverns led to the closure. Miners at Stony Halls had found the Wishstone, a Spiritstone fragment that showed people visions of their desires. Lumenstone appeared, the mines shifted as if alive, earthquakes struck and black ooze spread, so the Qixing closed the Chasm. A Fatui device sent in to deal with the ooze exploded. In 2.6 the Traveler broke the Bedrock Keys of the Seven-Star seal with the exploration team, cleared the Dark Mud and purified the Spiritstone, which had been bound by Abyssal power. This roused Haftvad the Worm, a Khaenri'ahn Ruin Serpent. Yelan shot the Nail down onto it, and the Nail rose again, though lower than before. The earthquakes stopped and the ooze began to recede. In 2.7 (*Perilous Trail*) the Traveler, Yelan, Yanfei, Itto and Shinobu fell into the Chasm's Bed, met Xiao there searching for Bosacius, learned the fate of the sealed vanguard, and escaped with Zhongli's help.
### ULTRA +
Legend also claims a "Solar Chariot" fell here in the war of funerary flame. A broken-off piece formed the Dunyu Ruins, and the main body leapt back into the sky in the Archon War. Some say Morax and Azhdaha fought here. In the wiki's account, mining that disturbed the ley lines tormented Azhdaha, and Morax, Mountain Shaper, Moon Carver and another adeptus fought him down into the depths. Shortly before the 6.3 Lantern Rite, a civilian dig funded by Huishan Hall turned up Lang-Gan relics. Zhongli later explained that strange phenomena in Lang-Gan's former domain had brought back Zibai's "Earthly Soul", which led to her restoration at the 6.3 Lantern Rite.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
On the surface: ochre cliffs, wind, abandoned scaffolding, cranes and minecarts, Millelith patrols and Treasure Hoarders keeping out of sight. Below: total darkness broken only by Lumenstone, glowing plants and Abyssal ooze, groaning rock, old Khaenri'ahn machines, and the sense that the place is watching you. Viewpoint notes left by Zhiqiong capture the awe of first seeing the Nail.

## dangers
### LARGE
Cave-ins and sheer drops, poisonous gas, Dark Mud that spreads Abyssal corruption, Abyss Mages and Heralds, rifthounds and riftwolves, Ruin Machines and the Ruin Serpent, Geovishaps, Fatui skirmishers, and Treasure Hoarders who sneak in for loot. The Chasm's Bed is the worst of all. It distorts space and time, reads minds and turns fears into illusions that can attack, and it slowly "devours" whoever stays too long.

## travel
### LARGE
On foot from central Liyue, past the Millelith checkpoint where outsiders are turned away. Access to the depths was via the Maw once the Seven-Star seal was broken. Miners are now kept away from the most dangerous sections.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the Chasm is a partly reopened mine guarded by the Millelith. After Ronova destroyed a Celestial Nail, every other Nail across Teyvat began emitting a strange light, the Spiritstone among them.
### STANDARD +
Rex Lapis has ordered Xiao to watch the Chasm so he can report any change at once. More Millelith have been sent to patrol, and Cloud Retainer and other adepti are investigating. Zhiqiong is still missing in the depths.

## entry: The Spiritstone
@keys: Spiritstone, Chasm Nail, Wishstone, Court of Pillars
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The Spiritstone is the Celestial Nail that buried Lang-Gan: a huge sky-blue crystal pillar floating in the Court of Pillars at the bottom of the Chasm. Celestial Nails purify powers that should not exist in Teyvat, and Lumenstone and Lumenspar near it repel the Abyss's Dark Mud. A fragment, the Wishstone, was found in Stony Halls, gave miners visions of their desires and led to the mine's closure. In 2.6 the Traveler freed the Nail from Abyssal bindings. Yelan then shot it down onto the Ruin Serpent Haftvad, and it rose again, but lower.
### LARGE +
Yelan says the Nail works well against remnants of Khaenri'ah's civilisation. The Traveler and Yelan suspect that the force of its fall weakened the old seal over the Chasm's Bed. In 7.1, after Ronova crumbled Hyperborea's Nail with Eitr, the Spiritstone, like the other Nails, began emitting a mark into the sky.

## entry: The Chasm's Bed and the sealed vanguard
@keys: Chasm's Bed, Fantastic Compass, Bosacius, Boyang, Nameless Yaksha
@keys2: Chasm, Spiritstone, Millelith, Yaksha
@version: 2.7
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Beneath the Spiritstone lies a space older than the cataclysm. It traps whatever enters, wears away the Abyss's power, and in time devours humans as well. In the cataclysm the Millelith vanguard, the thaumaturge Boyang and the Yaksha Bosacius lured the monsters inside and sealed it with the Fantastic Compass, a catalyst powered by a mortal and an adeptus together. The monsters were destroyed, and the defenders were lost with them. Xiao later confirmed there that the "Nameless Yaksha" who had returned to fight in the Chasm was Bosacius.
### LARGE +
Inside, the space twists paths into loops, freezes hunger and fatigue and then lets them hit all at once, reads minds and builds illusions from fears and memories. The Traveler saw the Abyss and their sibling there, and an echo of Bosacius still fought on. The Traveler's group escaped in 2.7 using an illusory Fantastic Compass that the space itself produced, which responded to the combined energy of Yelan (a mortal) and Xiao (an adeptus). Xiao poured out his strength to lift them to the surface, and Zhongli caught him as he fell back. The entrance has since closed.

## entry: Lang-Gan beneath the Chasm
@keys: Lang-Gan, God of Soil and Grain
@keys2: Chasm, Zibai, Spiritstone, Chenyu Vale
@version: 6.3
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
Lang-Gan was a civilisation of the unified era on Liyue's western coast, guided by Zibai, a heavenly envoy and later Moon Envoy. She gave its first emperor-priest the longbow Yayu and taught the people silk painting, farming, spinning and weaving. Lang-Gan had no books and kept its history in ballads. Its settlements ran from peak to peak, and life centred on the mines. When the Nail fell, Zibai's sacrifice bought the people time to flee east. Lang-Gan lies buried beneath the Chasm's hills, and the vale's Chiwang Terrace copies its jade altar. In 6.3 Lang-Gan relics dug up in the Chasm went on show in Liyue Harbor, and moon poems from Lang-Gan were recited at Lantern Rite.

## entry: The Qimen gate at the Chasm (5.3)
@keys: Gate of Injury, Qimen Arts
@keys2: Chasm, Yelan, Lan Yan
@version: 5.3
@availability: limited
### ULTRA
During a Lantern Rite event (5.3), Ningguang assigned Yelan to the Gate of Injury at the Chasm. Lan Yan said her grandfather remembers the Chasm's gate as the Gate of Life, back when their ancestors lived near the Chasm before moving to Chenyu Vale. Yelan told the Traveler that the Chasm was once home to the people of present-day Chenyu Vale, and that her own ancestors died fighting beside the Yakshas there.
