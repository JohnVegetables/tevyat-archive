## summary
### LITE
The Beryl Region is a mountainous area in southern Fontaine, across the water from the Court of Fontaine. Its ridges and ruins are said to be what is left of a colossal beast, Elynas. Beneath its lakes lies Merusea Village, the home of the Melusines, and in the waters between the ridge and Mont Automnequi lies the Elton Trench, where Fontaine's old Navy sank.
### STANDARD +
In-game blurbs describe the area as beautiful but rarely visited by locals or tourists. The local boss is the Hydro Tulpa, and many Fatui, hilichurls, ruin machines and tainted Hydro phantasms roam it.

## geography
### STANDARD
The region is built around a main island with two named subareas: Elynas, the northern half of the island, with the surface remains of the beast it is named for, and Merusea Village hidden inside the beast's body under mountains and lakes. West of it lies the underwater Elton Trench, between Elynas and Mont Automnequi. Mint-dotted highlands and ruins lie along the ridge.
### LARGE +
The tall mountains are said by legend to be Elynas' skeleton, worn by wind and dust into a home for 'countless souls'. The official map blurb describes it as 'an ancient ruin-studded mountain range across the water from the Court of Fontaine'.

## places
### STANDARD
- **Elynas**: the lush northern half of the main island, with the beast's surface remains.
- **Merusea Village**: the Melusine village, entered only from underwater, by diving near Elynas' skull or in the pool above the Beryl Region Statue of The Seven. Official text calls it an adorable and mysterious village where even the chimneys bubble.
- **Elton Trench**: underwater trench with shipwrecks of the former Navy.
### LARGE +
The region also has an unmarked ruin north of Romaritime Harbor with a collapsed entrance, found in a treasure hunter's journal, and a Uninhabited Hypostasis Platform and Shrine of Depths near Elynas.

## people
### STANDARD
Merusea Village is home to the Melusines, who call Elynas 'Father'. On land there are named Melusines such as Carabosse, Cosanzeana, Flo, Iara, Laume, Lutine, Rufina, Sedile, Serene, Sluasi, Verenata and Virda; underwater there are Glaisti, Granna, Puca and Thirona. Xana is the village's vendor. The only named human NPC in the region page is Depierris.
### LARGE +
The wiki notes that Elynas' spirit appears in Melusine form and that Melusines were a race created unintentionally by him.

## culture
### STANDARD
Official text says that many centuries ago the followers of Egeria treated this ridge as a favourite excursion and picnic spot, singing about love and dreams as pure as polished gems. Those songs have faded; now only cool breezes cross the highlands.

## history
### STANDARD
Elynas was a giant being whose death and remains shaped the ridge. According to the wiki's account of the Ancient Colors quests, Fontaine's Navy fought him and its ships sank in what is now the Elton Trench; the ship Sponsian was split in half in the final attack. Elynas bled out and died, then looked after the Melusines in the afterlife.
### LARGE +
Quests in this region show Jakob collecting Elynas' blood to revive him, and the Traveler stopping this because the revived body would crush the Melusine homes. Elynas thanked the Traveler in a brief 'time of life' before the attempt failed.
### ULTRA +
Jakob hired Eremites to buy paintings made with Elynas' blood from the painter Mamere, to gather blood without violence; the Traveler's chance meeting with Mamere spoiled this. Wildlife that fed on the remains may have produced at least two Consecrated Beasts, and Elynas' flesh is said to harden into a protective membrane rather than decay.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Quiet and lonely: cool breezes over ruins and huge bones on the ridge, lakes below. Underwater, the Elton Trench is dim, strewn with wrecks and haunted by ghostly legends.

## dangers
### STANDARD
The Hydro Tulpa is the region's boss. Elite enemies include Breacher Primuses, tainted Hydro phantasms, Fatui Pyro Agents and Electro Cicin Mages, Mirror Maidens, Lawachurls and ruin machines.

## travel
### STANDARD
The ridge lies across the water from the Court of Fontaine. Merusea Village can only be entered by diving. The region has its own Statue of The Seven.

## quests
### STANDARD
Region-tagged quests include the Ancient Colors storyline (including Limner, Dreamer, and Robotic Dog), the Good Stuff, but Terrible Taste series, the Strange Stone Chronicle series, Narcissus's Waltz, The Lone Phantom Sail, Echoes of the Ancient World and several commissions and aesthetic critiques.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 Elynas' remains still shape the area and the Melusines of Merusea live there.
### STANDARD +
No later version added Beryl-specific story in the sources checked.

## entry: Elynas
@keys: Elynas, Father
@keys2: Beryl, Melusine, Merusea
@version: 4.0
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Elynas is a giant beast whose remains encompass much of the Beryl Region, according to the wiki. Melusines call him Father. He realised his joy was causing suffering for others and let himself die; in death he wished to protect the Melusines. He is mainly known through the Ancient Colors quests.
### LARGE +
The wiki ties his blood to Abyssal power and to creatures such as the riftwolves and Breacher Primuses. Jakob's attempt to revive him is stopped by the Traveler.

## entry: Elton Trench
@keys: Elton Trench, former Navy, sunken fleet
@version: 4.0
### STANDARD
An underwater trench between Elynas and Mont Automnequi where Fontaine's former Navy sank in battle against Elynas. Divers can find shipwrecks, Pneumousia blocks and relays. The name probably honours Admiral Basil Elton.
