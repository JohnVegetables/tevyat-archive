## summary
### LITE
The Brightcrown Mountains are the rugged northwestern part of Mondstadt. They hold Brightcrown Canyon and, at the heart of the range, the ruins of Old Mondstadt, the walled city of Decarabian, God of Storms. During the Prologue the ruins were Stormterror's Lair, the sleeping place of the dragon Dvalin.
### STANDARD +
Mondstadters do not live here. Hilichurls, slimes and Fatui skirmishers are the usual company in the canyon and ruins, and the bard Davy is met in the canyon, and the wind currents that help gliders also make the old city dangerous.

## geography
### STANDARD
Cliffs, canyons and wind-scoured ruins. Brightcrown Canyon runs through the range and its northeastern end leads to the eastern entrance of Stormterror's Lair. The lair itself is the former capital, with Decarabian's Tower rising in the middle. A Boar Habitat in the mountains is notable for its many forest boars.
### LARGE +
The canyon's northeastern end holds the eastern entrance to the lair. Broken arcades, stone stairs and wind currents link the levels of the old city up to the summit of the tower. No battle music plays in Stormterror's Lair, a gameplay detail rather than lore.

## places
### STANDARD
Brightcrown Canyon; Stormterror's Lair (Old Mondstadt) with its entrance, and Decarabian's Tower at its centre; the Boar Habitat.
### LARGE +
The tower's second-highest floor holds the "first Field Tiller", an inert Khaenri'ahn war machine; its top floor is where the God of Storms is implied to have personally lived, and the Confront Stormterror domain is entered there. The lowest floor of the tower is treated by the game as out of bounds, and Paimon warns the player away.

## history
### STANDARD
Old Mondstadt was Decarabian's capital until the rebellion about 2,600 years ago, when he was slain and Barbatos took his place; the people then moved to the island in Cider Lake. Five hundred years ago the dragon Dvalin, victorious but badly hurt by the corrupted Durin, fell asleep in the ruined capital. Mondstadt forgot him, feared him on waking and called him Stormterror, which is where the lair's name comes from.
### LARGE +
The Abyss Order exploited the pain from the poisonous blood Durin left in Dvalin, which is the situation the Traveler found in the Prologue. A damaged Field Tiller, the first Ruin Guard prototype, once arrived here after a fight with Boreas in Wolvendom; for about a year it fired on anyone approaching the city, which Mondstadters took as a last fury of the dead Decarabian.
### ULTRA +
Brightcrown Canyon's wear suggests a path between the city and the boar habitat that Old Mondstadt's people used for food, and Decarabian's former lover Amos is said to have been a huntress, which hints that hunting was practised; this is deduction by the wiki, not stated canon. More recently the witch Alice blew up some arcades in the tower while exploring for her Teyvat Travel Guide, to make it easier to climb, and also wrecked parts of the canyon ruins in a failed experiment to control Ruin Guards with hilichurls.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Hilichurls and their samachurls, slimes and Fatui skirmishers; Eye of the Storm, mitachurl and Ruin Guard elite enemies in the lair; steep drops and strong air currents.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Desolate and windy. Official viewpoint text describes murmuring winds shrouding a desolate city and telling a story no mortal now remembers, that of the nameless wind sprite who was the tower's master's downfall.

## travel
### LARGE
Local specialties include calla lily, windwheel aster and small lamp grass. Atop a rock formation in the southwest of the lair stands an unexplained camp with a sword stuck in the ground in front of a tent; its origin and purpose are not explained.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the ruins are quiet. The storm barrier from the Prologue is gone.
### STANDARD +
Dvalin was freed from the Abyss Order's hold long ago and is honoured again; the lair is no longer a site of crisis.

## entry: Stormterror's Lair and Decarabian's Tower
@keys: Stormterror's Lair, Decarabian's Tower, Eye of the Storm, Abandoned Capital of Howling Winds
@version: 1.0
### STANDARD
Stormterror's Lair is what the Prologue called the ruins of Old Mondstadt. A storm barrier sealed it until the Archon Quest dispelled it. Decarabian's Tower stands in the middle of the ruined city and is climbed by wind current; its upper floors contain the first Field Tiller and the domain where Stormterror is confronted.
### LARGE +
Official viewpoint text calls it the "Abandoned Capital of Howling Winds" and speaks of a nameless wind sprite and a chorus that made the tower quail, an allusion to the rebellion that ended Decarabian's rule.

## entry: The First Field Tiller
@keys: Field Tiller, Tyrant's Final Fury, Weird Statue
@keys2: Mondstadt, Brightcrown, Stormterror, Boreas
### ULTRA
"Field Tiller" was a Khaenri'ahn codename for war machines now called Ruin Guards, and the one in Decarabian's Tower is the prototype that later Ruin Guards copy; as the prototype, its combat power was unrestricted. Wandering Teyvat after the cataclysm, it reached Wolvendom, where Boreas took it for a challenger and damaged it until it fled to the ruined city. Badly damaged, it became immobile but kept firing missiles at anyone entering the old capital for about a year, and Mondstadters called that the "Tyrant's Final Fury". The game labels it a "Weird Statue" until the quest We Will Be Reunited is complete. The Abyss Order had plans for its eye.
