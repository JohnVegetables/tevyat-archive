## summary
### LITE
Ashveil Peak is the northernmost area of Pohjola, the large island of northern Nod-Krai, added in 6.3. It is a harsh, cold highland of cliffs under dark clouds. Its two subareas are Kipumaki Cliff, a hotspot of Wild Hunt activity where an undying Abyssal fiend was sealed long ago, and Cliffwatch Camp, the Lightkeepers' outpost that stands guard over it.
### STANDARD +
It is a place of vigilance and grief rather than settlement: the Lightkeepers keep a small camp with a graveyard of their own dead, and the Traveler's world quests here centre on repairing the ancient seal and fighting the Abyssal threat beneath it.

## geography
### STANDARD
Ashveil Peak is part of Pohjola and lies at its far north, beyond Wavechaser Plain and Voidsea Outlook. Kipumaki Cliff is a ring of jagged cliffs permanently under dark cloud; Cliffwatch Camp sits beside a lighthouse and a vehicle that marks its Meeting Point. The chasm of Endless Decay, the lair of the sealed fiend, lies within the area.
### LARGE +
The whole of Kipumaki Cliff carries a special climate: Abyssal Decay builds up on travelers, and once the gauge is full the active character loses HP over time. Oath Lanterns scattered through the area let a visitor reduce or avoid it. Abyssal Riftlines and Abyssal Fog Barriers are exploration puzzles here. Local specialty: Winter Icelea.

## places
### STANDARD
Kipumaki Cliff: a cliff perpetually shrouded in dark clouds, said by its loading screen to contain a sealed and undying fiend. Cliffwatch Camp: the Lightkeepers' investigation squad's long-standing sentinel post against threats from the north.
### LARGE +
Cliffwatch Camp has a cemetery south of the camp with graves of fallen Lightkeepers, among them Veyde, Olsson, Chernov, Filaev, Pieris Asebov and Shonokovych; Bjorn's grave appears after the quest The Last Night, the First Light. Each grave carries only a short inscription. A diary found elsewhere says the cliff is often called the Sanctum of Departed Heroes, the resting ground of soldiers who fell in battle (see uncertainties). Chasm of Endless Decay is the lair where the fiend is finally confronted.

## people
### STANDARD
Lightkeepers (Ratniki) man the camp. After the world quests The Moonlit Watcher and Nightingale's Song, several Lightkeepers from those quests stand as open-world NPCs at Cliffwatch Camp: Anleifr, Egle, Rollon, Valdis and Vlaicu.
### LARGE +
Illuga, a Lightkeeper and later a playable character, leads the Nightmare Orioles squad in When War Songs Rise. The Lightkeepers' leader, the Starshyna Nikita, directs the effort from Piramida, and the Traveler is invited to join their strategy meeting at the camp.

## governance
### LARGE
No civil government. The area falls under the Lightkeepers, the autonomous order that Solovei founded 500 years ago by royal edict to defend Nod-Krai against the Wild Hunt. Orders reach the camp from their headquarters in Piramida; the order is described elsewhere as thinly stretched, so small squads like the one at Cliffwatch often carry heavy duties.

## history
### STANDARD
Five hundred years ago, at the time of the Cataclysm, the first Moonchanter of the Frostmoon Scions, Aila, and the Torchforger Solovei, with Alia's help, subdued the Scourge of Endless Decay and sealed it with a sealing array under Kipumaki Cliff. The Scourge had been helping the mad scholar Rotwang (Rauthvangi) try to open the false sky to the Abyss through the Pillar of Embla. Since the sealing the area has been a hotspot of Wild Hunt activity, so the Lightkeepers built Cliffwatch Camp to watch it.
### LARGE +
Three years before the present, a surge of Wild Hunt activity here caused a great catastrophe with many deaths among the Lightkeepers and civilians; the camp was afterwards rebuilt and the fight continued. Written accounts left by Lightkeeper squads describe being sent from Piramida to clear Wild Hunt nests around the cliff in support roles.

### ULTRA +
Two records from the area show the cost of the watch. The Pillar of Embla's diaries, kept by Lightkeeper support-unit members, speak of long months of boredom at Kipumaki Cliff under Captain Asebov, then a sudden order from Piramida to clear Wild Hunt nests; a squad called the Frostgale Steeds wrote of a three-day outbreak spilling from the cliff to the ramparts. The camp graves are the visible result.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Thin cold air, low dark cloud that never quite clears, jagged stone, the steady light of the camp lighthouse and rows of simple grave markers. Mood: wary, solemn, with dry gallows humour among tired soldiers.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Wild Hunt and Abyssal enemies: Wilderness Exiles, Wilderness Hunters, Fishers of Hidden Depths and, as a boss, the Lord of the Hidden Depths: Whisperer of Nightmares (the Scourge in its true form). The Abyssal Decay climate drains health in the open.
### LARGE +
The Scourge first appears in the form of a Wilderness Hunter, then in boss form. It does not simply attack: it draws on the sorrow of those it faces. Allied Lightkeepers fight alongside the Traveler during the quest.

## travel
### LARGE
Reached by moving north through Wavechaser Plain and Pohjola. Cliffwatch Camp has its own Meeting Point; lanterns and Teleport Waypoints in the cliffs let travelers rest from Abyssal Decay.

## quests
### STANDARD
When War Songs Rise (world quest, Nightingale's Song chapter, Act II; 6.3): starting at Cliffwatch Camp, the Traveler joins Illuga and the Lightkeepers' Nightmare Orioles squad to reestablish the ancient seal on the cliff.
### LARGE +
The sequence runs Tower of Inversion (Pillar of Embla), When War Songs Rise, then The Last Night, the First Light. In the middle quest the Traveler and the squad rebuild the barrier by activating Verdant Crests, destroy the Evil Eye hanging above the cliff, and Bjorn is fatally wounded by the Scourge. Illuga pursues the fiend into its lair; it conjures illusions from his past losses to break his will, but with his squad's help he and the Traveler defeat it with the Spiralblade and cleanse the area.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the cliff is open to explore; the sealed fiend has been defeated and its influence cleansed, but the Wild Hunt remains a danger in the north.
### STANDARD +
Cliffwatch Camp continues as a Lightkeeper outpost. Nothing in 7.0-7.1 is recorded as changing the area.

## entry: Kipumaki Cliff
@keys: Kipumaki Cliff, Chasm of Endless Decay
@version: 6.3
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Kipumaki Cliff is a hotspot of Wild Hunt activity in northern Nod-Krai, perpetually shrouded in dark clouds. The Scourge of Endless Decay was sealed here 500 years ago after Aila, Alia and Solovei first banished it. The name echoes a Finnish myth place, the hill of pain.
### LARGE +
All the cliff is under the Abyssal Decay climate. A puzzle near a waterfall in the south ends in a carriage opened with a forgotten key.

## entry: Cliffwatch Camp
@keys: Cliffwatch Camp
@version: 6.3
### STANDARD
Cliffwatch Camp is an outpost of the Lightkeepers that keeps watch on Abyssal activity at Kipumaki Cliff. It was built by their investigation squad and serves as a long-standing sentinel against threats from the north; it was rebuilt after the surge three years before the present.
### LARGE +
Its graves record the dead of earlier squads. Some of the Lightkeepers who appear in The Moonlit Watcher and Nightingale's Song stay on as open-world NPCs.

## entry: The Scourge of Endless Decay
@keys: Scourge of Endless Decay, Whisperer of Nightmares, Nightmare Orioles
@keys2: Kipumaki Cliff, Illuga, Lightkeepers
@version: 6.3
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
An Abyssal entity that helped Rotwang's plan to open the false sky to the Abyss through the Pillar of Embla. After Rotwang's defeat it was sealed beneath Kipumaki Cliff by Aila and Solovei. In When War Songs Rise it is the antagonist, first as a Wilderness Hunter, later as the Lord of the Hidden Depths. It exploits Illuga's experience of loss, and is defeated by the Traveler and Illuga. The wiki notes it may once have been human; canon does not say.
