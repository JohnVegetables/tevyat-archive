## summary
### LITE
Paha Isle is an island in the Southern Isles of Nod-Krai, accessible from 6.0. Once an unremarkable island known for sunset-hued salt marshes and Tassel Jellies, it changed when the heaviest fragment of the fallen moonstone crashed on its shores. It is now a staging ground for Fatui personnel in Nod-Krai, who work from the huge Kuuvahki Experimental Design Bureau, and it also holds the Lightkeepers' abandoned Final Night Cemetery.
### STANDARD +
The two halves of the island contrast: a cold steel Fatui installation that dominates the landscape as a statement of power, and a lonely lighthouse outpost lit by a single Lightkeeper who looks after the dead.

## geography
### STANDARD
Paha Isle is the island south of Voidsea Outlook across the water, part of the Southern Isles with Lempo Isle and Hiisi Island. The Design Bureau is a colossal structure of iron and stone built by the Fatui; the Final Night Cemetery lies around a lighthouse on the island. Local specialty: Frostlamp Flower.
### LARGE +
The Bureau has several levels (top, middle, lower, bottom, a disposal workshop and a southern zone). The island's boss is the Super-Heavy Landrover: Mechanized Fortress.

## places
### STANDARD
Kuuvahki Experimental Design Bureau: the Fatui's high-tech complex. Final Night Cemetery: the former Lightkeeper outpost, lighthouse and burial ground, always dark.
### LARGE +
In the Bureau, lower levels hold ordinary enemies, and the upper levels open during The Shoemaker's Children Go Barefoot; most combat areas trigger a 20-second alarm countdown that calls in reinforcements. The wiki suggests the Bureau's name echoes the Soviet-era 'OKB' design bureaus (an editor's note, not stated in the game). The cemetery has a lighthouse with a lamp signal handbook (distress, guidance, summoning and all-safe signals), a cooking stove, a monument, an engraved inscription and a tavern bulletin board. A tavern once stood at the western part of the island.

## people
### STANDARD
The Fatui soldiers of the Bureau, including the Poruchik Raskolnikov, who oversees it. Flins, a Ratnik, lives alone at the lighthouse. Ghosts of dead Lightkeepers remain, among them the Loud Phantom (Dietrich), the Ritual-Conducting Phantom and, after The Chef's Tale, the Food-Obsessed Phantom (Jaakob).
### LARGE +
Raskolnikov grew up in Novokitezhgrad and was trained by Police Chief Stalanov, whose daughter Netochka he knows; he is unpopular with lower-ranked Oprichniki for strict rules such as a ban on drinking parties. Graves in the cemetery include those of Aimo, Dietrich, Eetu, Haapa, Jaakob, Sumrak and the Khorin family.

## governance
### STANDARD
The Bureau is run by Fatui personnel under Raskolnikov for maintaining order and efficiency. The cemetery has no authority: the Lightkeepers abandoned the island and Flins remains stationed there on his own.

## history
### STANDARD
Several decades ago the light of the historic Lightkeeper outpost was snuffed out in a conflict against the Wild Hunt, and ninety percent of the troops stationed there died. In despair the Ratniki turned to the legendary 'blue lantern', and by coincidence awakened Flins, who helped them fight despite his weakness at that moment. The survivors and Flins buried the fallen and founded the cemetery; the Lightkeepers then left, and only Flins remained.
### LARGE +
Before that, the outpost's lighthouse guided merchants and Lightkeeper fleets and reported on Wild Hunt movements; its light had never gone out and symbolised hope. The Starshyna of that time even ordered offerings to the blue lantern. The Fatui's Bureau now dominates the island as their staging ground for personnel in Nod-Krai. It is always dark at the Final Night Cemetery.

### ULTRA +
The cemetery's graves carry short inscriptions, and the wiki records Sumrak's grave as visible only when Flins is the active character after The Special Support Squad's Tale. The Lightkeepers' signal code includes a warning signal whose instruction in the handbook is blurred and unreadable.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Two moods: the Bureau, cold metal, alarms and the sense of being watched; the cemetery, a dark island with one signalling light, lonely graves and the ghosts of dead Lightkeepers.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Fatui patrols in the Bureau (Oprichniki Line Troopers, Support Troopers, Medics, Vanguards and Grenadiers, among others), alarm systems that call in reinforcements, and the boss Super-Heavy Landrover: Mechanized Fortress. After the quest Raskolnikov can be fought again as a Local Legend.
### LARGE +
In the quest the Traveler must also pass the Bureau's disposal workshop with its Landcruiser machines.

## travel
### LARGE
The cemetery has its Meeting Point at the lighthouse and requires the quest To the Lighthouse, which in turn requires Where the Moon Rises.

## quests
### STANDARD
The Shoemaker's Children Go Barefoot (world quest, chapter East of the Moon, West of the Sun): the Traveler enters the Bureau, meets the girl Netochka, and fights through Fatui to Raskolnikov; completing it unlocks him as a Local Legend. To the Lighthouse (world quest): the Traveler fuels and lights the lighthouse in the Final Night Cemetery with Flins; it leads to The Special Support Squad's Tale.
### LARGE +
In the first quest Raskolnikov, aware that the Traveler has dealings with the Fatui Harbingers, is defeated and willingly 'loses' the Proof of the Cognoscenti he was ordered to collect, Weavenexa-Cognoscenti's Intoxication, to end the disorder it caused at the Bureau. Other cemetery quests: The Chef's Tale and The Special Support Squad's Tale. One There Was, Who Hunted the Moon, a longer quest, also touches the island's history.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the island remains open to explore, with the Fatui complex still standing and Flins posted at the cemetery.
### STANDARD +
Nothing in 7.0-7.1 is recorded as changing the island.

## entry: Kuuvahki Experimental Design Bureau
@keys: Kuuvahki Experimental Design Bureau, Design Bureau
@keys2: Fatui, Paha Isle, Nod-Krai
@version: 6.0
### STANDARD
A colossal steel structure erected by the Fatui, its bulk dominating the ancient landscape as a declaration of power and control. It serves as the Fatui staging ground in Nod-Krai and is run by the Poruchik Raskolnikov.
### LARGE +
Many levels, an archive, dormitories and a disposal workshop. A note about the Proof of the Cognoscenti shows the Fatui collecting such objects.

## entry: Final Night Cemetery
@keys: Final Night Cemetery
@version: 6.0
### STANDARD
Home of Flins and of the ghosts of dead Lightkeepers, built around an old outpost lighthouse whose light was never extinguished until a Wild Hunt battle killed most of its garrison. It is always dark. The Meeting Point is at the lighthouse.
### LARGE +
Ratniki lamp signals (distress, guidance, summoning and all-safe) are explained by a handbook at the lighthouse. Flins lives alone and faithfully fulfills his duties.

## entry: Raskolnikov
