## summary
### LITE
Tsurumi Island is a remote island of Inazuma that was long wrapped in a dense fog and avoided for years. It holds the remains of two vanished peoples. The older one was linked to Celestia and lies buried underground. The later one was a tribe that worshipped the Thunderbird, Kanna Kapatcir. When the boy Ruu was sacrificed to her, the Thunderbird destroyed the tribe in grief and rage and cursed the island to relive its final days. The Traveler helped Ruu's spirit keep his promise to sing for her again. That lifted the curse and the fog, and the island's spirits are now passing on.
### STANDARD +
No one lives there now apart from hilichurls, riftwolves and old automatons. Kama, who ferried the Traveler over from Ritou and has since been seen travelling with Sumida, is the tribe's only known living descendant.

## geography
### STANDARD
A hilly island of grassland, beaches and ruins. Mt. Kanna, the tribe's sacred ground where the Thunderbird dwelt, rises in the centre. The Moshiri Ceremonial Site was where the rites were held. Shirikoro Peak and Chirai Shrine stand over a network of underground ruins. The coasts include Oina Beach and Wakukau Shoal, and the open Autake Plains lie inland. Wooden "Perches" for offerings once stood around the island.
### LARGE +
The Tsurumi Underground Ruins link Shirikoro Peak and Chirai Shrine, and both civilisations left remnants in them, including murals of Celestia. Offshore islets surround Moshiri Kara, a masterless mansion whose builder nobody knows. Riftwolves now prowl it. Even after the curse ended, thin haze drifts over the ground, and fog still sits on the sea a little way out.

## people
### STANDARD
The island has no living inhabitants. Until the curse was lifted, illusions of the tribe re-enacted the final ceremony. Ruu alone among them became self-aware: he noticed changes on the island and the passing of time without understanding why. After the curse ended, spirits of the dead appeared, and a mysterious "Boatman" began ferrying them to a "golden land" once their last wishes were settled.
### LARGE +
Known members of the tribe include Ruu, Abe, Ipe, Kina, Kito, Makiri, Mata, Onkami and Una, along with an illusory figure called "Kama" whom the Traveler saw burying the Maushiro. The living Kama in Ritou descends from tribespeople who left the island. In Inazuma City, the novelist Sumida commissioned adventurers to find the tribe's "Maushiro" for her book.
### ULTRA +
After the curse lifted, the Traveler helped several of the tribe's spirits with small last requests before they boarded the Boatman's boat. These are the follow-up exploration objectives named for Abe, Ipe, Chise, Kito and Kina, Nonno, Rero, Una and Shitoki, plus the Boatman's own task. A follow-up quest, The Saga of Mr. Forgetful, also takes place on the island.

## culture
### STANDARD
The Thunderbird tribe worshipped Kapatcir with fear and gratitude, read her unpredictable actions as divine revelations, and chose shamans to offer blood sacrifices. They called to each other through the fog with the Maushiro, at first an instrument made from her feathers and later a wooden imitation. They were taught never to leave the island, since anyone who did was believed lost in the "sea of darkness". The fog, they thought, was the Thunderbird's protection.
### LARGE +
The tribe had a strict hierarchy with priests at the top, and they wrote in the Ishine Script. They used an amethyst hourglass to predict the Thunderbird's arrival and held a festival when she came. Ruu repeats the adults' "Rule of Three": a thing must be done three times to get a result.

## history
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
In the age of the ancient unified civilisation, a people connected to Celestia lived on Tsurumi and worshipped the Three Moons. "Strange objects" later fell from the heavens, destroying that civilisation and producing the fog. Kanna Kapatcir, the Thunderbird who made the island her home, saw this and left the fog in place although she could have dispersed it. Later a new tribe came to worship her. She paid them no heed until, more than 1,500 years ago, the boy Ruu's song pierced the clouds. She landed beside him, he gave her the name "Kanna Kapatcir" (Great Eagle of the Storm), and he promised to sing for her again. When she returned she found him dead, sacrificed by his own people, his blood in a ritual cup. To her this was the murder of an innocent and a broken promise. She razed the tribe with lightning and cursed the island to repeat its last days until she heard his song again. She then flew to Seirai Island, where Ei later killed her.
### LARGE +
In Through the Mists (2.2) the Traveler took Sumida's commission and crossed to Tsurumi in Kama's boat. They met Ruu and learned the truth from Kama. They destroyed the Perches to stop the ceremony and defeated a Thunder Manifestation on Mt. Kanna. Through the Peculiar Pinion found on Seirai, they saw that only Ruu's song would end the curse. The Traveler and Paimon told Ruu that the Thunderbird had "moved" to Seirai rather than reveal she was dead, though on seeing the ruins of Amakumo Peak he understood what had happened and did not blame them. With the Pinion's power they brought him there, he sang, and the curse lifted. Ruu chose to rest on the island for a while before passing on.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Before the curse lifted: silence, grey fog that turned travellers around, and illusory villagers who acted strangely and vanished between visits. During the final confrontation the fog turned red and crimson lightning struck. Afterwards: open grass, waves, wind over ruined platforms, and the occasional spirit asking a stranger for one last favour.

## dangers
### STANDARD
The fog once teleported intruders back to the island's edge. Riftwolves, Thundercraven rifthounds, hilichurls, Abyss Mages and Ruin automatons roam today. A Thunder Manifestation formed from the Thunderbird's rage appeared in the hollow of Mt. Kanna.
### LARGE +
Gameplay note: Tsurumi runs on a three-day cycle that changes enemy and chest placement, and Remarkable Chests were introduced here.

## travel
### STANDARD
Tsurumi lies apart from the main islands and was reached by boat with Kama's help. Before the questline, only the southern peninsula and the eastern isles near the Statue of The Seven could be reached.

## status
### STANDARD
As of 7.1 the curse and the fog are gone, and the island's spirits are moving on with the Boatman. Tsurumi remains uninhabited. No Tsurumi-specific content appears in 6.x-7.1.

## entry: Ruu and the Thunderbird
@keys: Ruu, Kanna Kapatcir, Kapatcir, Great Eagle of the Storm
@keys2: Tsurumi, Thunderbird, Maushiro, Mt. Kanna
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Ruu was a boy of Tsurumi's Thunderbird tribe whose song drew down the Thunderbird. He named her Kanna Kapatcir and promised to sing for her again. Believing his sacrifice would set things right for his people, he let himself be sacrificed. She saw it as murder and a broken promise, destroyed the tribe, and bound the island in a looping curse until she could hear his song again. Through the loop Ruu lived on as an aware illusion. In 2.2 the Traveler, with Kama, helped him understand what she had wanted and brought him to Seirai to sing, ending the curse.

## entry: The First Civilisation of Tsurumi
@keys: Pre-Thunderbird Civilization, Tsurumi Underground Ruins
@spoiler: minor
### LARGE
Beneath Tsurumi lie ruins older than the Thunderbird tribe. They belong to the ancient unified civilisation, which was connected to Celestia and worshipped the Three Moons. Its murals depict Celestia, and Ruin Sentinels guard its halls. Kapatcir remembered strange objects falling from the heavens and creating the fog. The novelist Sumida guessed that something fell from Celestia, and Kama suspects it disturbed the island's ley lines. Nothing found on the island confirms what fell.
