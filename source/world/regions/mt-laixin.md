## summary
### LITE
Mt. Laixin ("the sacred mountain") is the peak where Chenyu Vale's spirit veins converge, so whoever controls it can control every acre of the vale. It was sealed until the 4.4 quest An Ancient Sacrifice of Sacred Brocade opened it. On it stand Chiwang Terrace, an altar copied from the ancient city of Lang-Gan, and Carp's Rest, the abode of the carp adeptus Fujin, with the Votive Rainjade where offerings are made.
### STANDARD +
It is the only area on the world map without a Statue of the Seven.

## geography
### STANDARD
A mountain in the vale's heart. Before it is unlocked, entering the area turns the view red and triggers an out-of-bounds reaction; the map shows it as colorless rather than red. Chiwang Terrace is on the summit; Carp's Rest is an adeptal abode on the mountain.
### LARGE +
Its peak is called Chiwang Peak in the viewpoint text, and the viewpoint of Carp's Rest is called Clarion Echo.

## places
### STANDARD
Chiwang Terrace: a jade-styled altar, unlocked by An Ancient Sacrifice of Sacred Brocade. Carp's Rest: Fujin's abode, unlocked in The Cloud-Padded Path to the Chiwang Repose. Votive Rainjade: the offering site in Carp's Rest.
### LARGE +
In the offering system, offering Spirit Carp raises Rainjade's Benediction and, at maximum, two yellow carps circle the jade and spirit smoke rises.

## people
### LARGE
Fujin, the carp adeptus, rests at Carp's Rest. Lingyuan, the suanni who presides over the vale, can be spoken to at the summit in The Cloud-Padded Path. Little Mao, who was befriended by Lingyuan, appears in the quests.

## governance
### LARGE
None. A quest states that controlling Mt. Laixin means controlling all of Chenyu Vale; by disturbing the spirit veins, Lingyuan soured the soil and water and so the tea.

## culture
### LARGE
Chenyu Vale's ancestors offered jade by throwing it into the water, passing adeptal energy to the spirit veins, protecting their homeland and securing good weather and harvests. The rite is long forgotten; the game restores a version of it through offerings of Spirit Carp.
### ULTRA +
Chiwang Terrace is a copy of the jade altar once used in Lang-Gan to honor the White Horse Adeptus, Zibai. After she sacrificed herself to delay a Celestial Nail, the refugees rebuilt their society in Chenyu Vale, altar included (per the Chiwang Terrace wiki, citing the 6.3 story quest The Realm of Rocks).

## history
### STANDARD
Fujin hurled the Votive Rainjade from Mt. Lingmeng to quell the mistress's flood in the Archon War and then sank, losing power, into Carp's Rest, where for thousands of years she could only reminisce about the outside world through the scent of tea offerings. In 4.4 the Traveler found that Lingyuan, who longed for her old friend, was disturbing the spirit veins to restore the vale to nature; she yielded after defeat and Fujin performed the Rainjade Rite.
### LARGE +
The rite restored soil and water but drained Fujin so she returned to carp form and to Carp's Rest. Some miasma still remained afterward. Lingyuan told the Traveler she would answer at their next meeting whether humans acted against their purpose.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Spirit smoke curling from the jade, golden carp circling and the hush of an old rite.

## dangers
### LARGE
Miasma and the monsters it drives wild. Before the quest, the area is sealed and walking into it triggers an out-of-bounds reaction.

## travel
### STANDARD
Locked until An Ancient Sacrifice of Sacred Brocade; afterwards open. Carp's Rest requires finishing The Cloud-Padded Path to the Chiwang Repose, a quest available one in-game day after the earlier one.

## quests
### LARGE
Qiaoying of the Sacred Mountain begins at Carp's Rest, where the Traveler is first drawn into an adepti realm. The Cloud-Padded Path to the Chiwang Repose has them ascend the summit, speak to Lingyuan and meet Fujin at Carp's Rest.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 Mt. Laixin is open to travelers; Fujin rests at Carp's Rest and Lingyuan presides over the vale.
### STANDARD +
Fujin can also be found outside Qiaoying with Lingyuan or Little Mao after completing Rainjade's Benediction.

## entry: Carp's Rest and Fujin
@keys: Carp's Rest, Fujin, Rainjade's Benediction
@keys2: Chenyu Vale, Mt. Laixin, Lingyuan
@version: 4.4
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Carp's Rest is Fujin's abode on Mt. Laixin, the hidden refuge she retreated to after the war, "sinking into the sea" and losing much of her strength. Fujin, a giant golden carp who took human form, was worshiped as an adeptus and was close to humans. The Traveler first met her there in Qiaoying of the Sacred Mountain, when she lent them adeptal energy. After performing the Rainjade Rite she is back at Carp's Rest, in her carp form, to recover.

## entry: Chiwang Terrace and Lang-Gan's altar
@keys: Chiwang Terrace, Chiwang Peak, Chiwang Repose
@keys2: Zibai, Lang-Gan, Chenyu Vale
@version: 6.3
@spoiler: minor
### LARGE
Chiwang Terrace is a copy of an altar from the ancient civilisation of Lang-Gan, built to honor the White Horse Adeptus Zibai. After her sacrifice delayed a Celestial Nail, Lang-Gan's survivors fled to Chenyu Vale and rebuilt their society, including the altar. The terrace is unlocked by An Ancient Sacrifice of Sacred Brocade.

## entry: Votive Rainjade
@keys: Votive Rainjade, Rainjade Rite, Spirit Carp
@keys2: Mt. Laixin, Fujin, Chenyu Vale
@version: 4.4
### STANDARD
The Votive Rainjade is a jade where Chenyu Vale's ancestors once offered jade by casting it into water, transferring adeptal energy to the spirit veins to guard their homeland and bring favorable weather and harvests. The old rite has long been forgotten, but if the lost adeptal energy is gathered into the jade again, the soil and water might be blessed anew. Offering Spirit Carp to the jade raises Rainjade's Benediction and unlocks rewards.
