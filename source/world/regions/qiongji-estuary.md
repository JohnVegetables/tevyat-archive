## summary
### LITE
Qiongji Estuary is the quiet eastern coastal area of Liyue where the Bishui River reaches the sea. Its heart is the Guili Plains, once the center of the Guili Assembly, the ancient civilisation that the gods Guizhong and Morax ruled together before the Archon War destroyed it. Around them lie Mingyun Village, an emptied mining hamlet, Luhua Pool with its bright terraced ponds and two watching statues, and the Yaoguang Shoal's gentle shallows.
### STANDARD +
The land is open and still. Most of what happened here is now only ruins, tablets and legend.

## geography
### STANDARD
Flat plains, ponds and shoreline in eastern Liyue. The Bishui River's second arm (the one that does not end near Dawn Winery) flows through Qiongji Estuary into the Sea of Clouds. Yaoguang Shoal lies south of Mingyun Village and east of Guili Plains. Guili Plains lies near Liyue Harbor, reached by the road that runs through the Mt. Tianheng range north of the city.
### LARGE +
At its height the Guili Assembly was said to spread a thousand miles with Stone Gate as its northern boundary; by in-game measurement the Plains lay at its heart, with the edge of Jueyun Karst to the west and the end of Mingyun Village to the east.

## places
### STANDARD
Guili Plains: ruins of the old Assembly, with Jade Plates and stone tablets. Mingyun Village: a ghost of a mining village with three small mines. Luhua Pool: a "unique landscape" of brightly colored pools watched by two giant statues. Yaoguang Shoal: a shallow, sandy shoal.
### LARGE +
Three mining tunnels near Mingyun Village yield Iron Chunk, Noctilucous Jade and Cor Lapis. Yaoguang Shoal was named, in the local tongue, after the bioluminescent creatures that once swam ashore after dusk like fallen stars; lovers took them all and the sight is gone.

## people
### LARGE
Almost no residents. Mingyun Village's inhabitants moved west toward Liyue Harbor and the Chasm when the ore ran out. The adventurer Zhiqiong was born in Mingyun Village and recalls its mines fondly.

## governance
### LARGE
None specific. Under the Guili Assembly the gods Guizhong and Morax, supported by other gods and adepti, ruled the region.

## culture
### LARGE
The Assembly's people took up agriculture; farming towns appeared throughout the area. Guizhong and the adepti built the Guizhong Ballista on Mt. Tianheng, and her four commandments were to teach with wisdom, be bound by virtue, fortify the bones and unite in ambition.
### ULTRA +
The name Guili comes from the first character of Guizhong's name and the "li" in Morax's name at the time, and the plain is also called the Plains of Returning and Departing. Luhua Pool's viewpoint text calls its site the Weeping Garden, a place where, legend has it, a deep, pure affection filled an ancient garden and made the pool.

## history
### STANDARD
When Morax first came down, much of western Liyue was under the sea; he lowered the tides and raised Mt. Tianheng. Guizhong later joined his settlement north of the mountain with her people, and together they built the Guili Assembly. About 3,700 years ago, in the Archon War, Guizhong was slain in a battle that laid waste to Guili Plains and Dihua Marsh; a great flood followed, and Morax moved his people south of Mt. Tianheng to found Liyue Harbor.
### LARGE +
The Assembly was a prosperous civilisation with highly advanced engineering, supported by gods such as Marchosius and by the adepti. Its capital area is described as a once-thriving city where a certain flower bloomed abundantly, an earthly paradise consumed by war. The Assembly is gone, but the Liyue Harbor that replaced it endures.
### ULTRA +
Mingyun Village's mines dried up several years ago, and its people left. Hilichurls moved into the abandoned facilities.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Wide plains with silent ruin tablets, the bright colors of Luhua's ponds, a quiet shoal where the glow once was, and, at Mingyun, the empty houses of a village whose ore ran out.

## dangers
### LARGE
Hilichurls have moved into Mingyun Village. In the quest Luhua Landscape, Abyss Mages guard the ruin entrance at Luhua Pool and spiders lurk inside.

## travel
### STANDARD
The Plains lie near Liyue Harbor; Mingyun Village sits at the Assembly's old eastern edge, with Yaoguang Shoal south of it. The Bishui River's mouth is here.

## quests
### LARGE
World quests include Luhua Landscape (an artist's paintbrushes and a pair of statues), The Tree who Stands Alone (Mingyun Village), Guili Plains Treasure Hunt and Troubles Traveling Through Tributaries, among others.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 Qiongji Estuary is a quiet, nearly empty coastal region, with the Assembly's ruins untouched.
### STANDARD +
No Qiongji-specific events appear in 6.x-7.1 content.

## entry: The Guili Assembly and Guili Plains
@keys: Guili Assembly, Guili Plains, Plains of Returning and Departing
@keys2: Guizhong, Morax, Archon War, Liyue
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
The Guili Assembly was an ancient civilisation in Liyue founded and ruled by Guizhong, God of Dust, and Morax, God of Geo, with Marchosius the God of the Stove and the adepti in support. Its center was the Guili Plains, which Liyue Harbor's founders tried to expand into before Guizhong died. The Plains' viewpoint text, "Ruins of Guili," calls it a thriving city where a flower bloomed in abundance, consumed by war so that its people scattered again.
### LARGE +
Guizhong was kind-hearted and wise though not especially powerful, and she loved mechanisms: she and the adepti, Cloud Retainer among them, built the Guizhong Ballista on Mt. Tianheng. Texts say she died in a destructive battle that also wrecked Dihua Marsh, and a flood followed.

## entry: Mingyun Village
@keys: Mingyun Village
@keys2: Zhiqiong, mining, Liyue
### STANDARD
Mingyun Village is home to several scattered mining deposits, but most have dried up and the few remaining ores cannot sustain the village, so its people have left for Liyue Harbor and the Chasm. Hilichurls now use the leftover facilities. The adventurer Zhiqiong was born here and remembers its mines with fondness.

## entry: Luhua Pool and Yaoguang Shoal
@keys: Luhua Pool, Yaoguang Shoal, Weeping Garden
@keys2: Qiongji Estuary, Liyue
### STANDARD
Luhua Pool is a unique landscape of brightly colored pools overlooked by two statues, with ruins hidden beneath. A legend says a deep, pure affection filled the ancient garden here and made the pool. Yaoguang Shoal, south of Mingyun Village and east of Guili Plains, is named for the bioluminescent sea creatures that once appeared after dusk like fallen stars swimming to shore; they are now gone, taken by lovers, though the shore may still have things to give.
