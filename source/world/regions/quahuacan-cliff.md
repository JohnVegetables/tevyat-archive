## summary
### LITE
Quahuacan Cliff is the cliff-top home of the Flower-Feather Clan (Tlalocan), the Natlan tribe that rides Qucusaurs, the flying saurians. The clan's riders once patrolled all of Natlan and still watch the ruins of Ochkanatlan. Since 5.8 the cliff also serves as the landing for people leaving Nod-Krai. The name may echo the Nahuatl Cuauhhuacan, a place name built from the words for tree and eagle.
### STANDARD +
It is a place of flight, pride and second chances: riders train here, the 'Wingless' who fail the rider exam find other work, and the clan's territory holds an old, long-sealed Secret Source facility.

## geography
### STANDARD
High coastal cliffs and perches in Natlan, with the Flower-Feather Clan's settlement on top. Malinalco Grotto, an underground cavern, lies beneath, reached by the right-hand tunnel at the entrance of the Tenebrous Papilla boss cave. The river valley called Xalac Vale runs from the Stadium of the Sacred Flame to the clan's settlement.
### LARGE +
Gaseous Phlogiston currents rise here, and only Qucusaurs, Mavuika and Flower-Feather characters can ride them unharmed (a gameplay mechanic that mirrors the clan's skill).

## places
### STANDARD
The clan's settlement, with its flight trial grounds and bulletin boards; Malinalco Grotto; and the new harbour Mavuika arranged for arrivals from Nod-Krai.
### LARGE +
Malinalco Grotto holds a mural depicting the legend of Kuntur and Rumi, in which three tribal warriors are defeated by the wicked sorcerer Rumi; scattered notes from restorers ask where the mural with Kuntur himself might be. The Temple of Cascading Colors: Ch'upikaq Tajin, a sealed Secret Source facility, was entered from the clan's territory in the Archon Quest Searing.

## people
### STANDARD
Chief Mutota leads the clan. Its best-known member is Chasca, a Qucusaur rider who is one of the six heroes of Natlan. Ifa, a renowned saurian veterinarian, is also of the clan, as is the elder Allpa, who appears in Chasca's story.
### LARGE +
Refugees and travellers from Nod-Krai pass through the harbour, among them the Liyue merchant Zhuoxin, who advises on the arrivals, his wife Aamu and daughter Adieta. The android Ineffa chose to stay in Natlan for the time being.

## culture
### STANDARD
Members bond with Qucusaurs, and the tribe's Qucusaur Riders are chosen by a hard entrance exam: the applicant must be a proficient warrior and must win a qucusaur's trust. Without full control, rider and saurian could be killed. Those who fail are called Wingless and must give up their saurian partners for safety; life is hard for them. Most other tribes' members rarely talk to a lone member of the clan, and if one begins whistling, something is wrong nearby.
### LARGE +
The clan was founded thousands of years ago by Lianca, an abandoned orphan raised by the Eighth Dragonlord of Natlantea. She took in other orphans, then warriors who would not follow their tribes' rules, then banished dragons seeking protection, whether friend or foe. At some point in the past the clan patrolled all of Natlan, a role still remembered with pride by many riders.
### ULTRA +
Riders now watch their own territory first but also patrol the ruins of Ochkanatlan; after that city fell the clan set a blockade to keep travellers out. The Pakpaka Flying School at Scorchwind Trial Grounds in the Tezcatepetonco Range appears to be run by the clan.

## history
@version: 5.2
### STANDARD
During the Abyss's nation-wide assault of Chapter V, the Flower-Feather Clan took the largest amount of damage of any tribe (the wiki's history page ties this to the clan's hero being the only one not yet revealed, without citing a source). Chuychu, who had adopted Chasca into human society, died of Abyssal contamination, and Chasca was then acknowledged by her Ancient Name as the last of the six heroes.
### LARGE +
Soon afterwards, in the story Time for the Showdown, the elder Allpa tried to overthrow Chief Mutota. After her son Tepal died experimenting with Phlogiston jet packs, she came to blame qucusaurs; she had wild qucusaurs captured for the stable Phlogiston their glands produce, and she built Phlogiston Wings for the Wingless. Her subordinates failed to capture Mutota, the expected support never came, and she was stopped by Chasca, the Traveler and the qucusaur Coya, then imprisoned.
### ULTRA +
In the Archon Quest prelude to Nod-Krai (The Journey Home), Chasca offers the clan's lodging to Ineffa, an android found on Natlan's shore, and to the Nod-Krai merchants who came with her, and the Fatui later use Secret Source automata to threaten Zhuoxin's family. The ruins of the Temple of Cascading Colors turn out to hold the long-waiting Eighth Lord, a dragonlord who once raised Lianca. After the events there Mavuika arranged with the clan elders to build a simple harbour for the people fleeing the Fatui's destabilisation of Nod-Krai, with Zhuoxin's advice.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Wind and wingbeats: qucusaurs wheeling over the ledges, riders training and the sea below. Pride in flying runs through everything, tempered by the effort and hardship of the Wingless.

## dangers
### STANDARD
The boss Tenebrous Papilla is an Abyssal mimic creature in a cave beneath the cliff. Secret Source automata were reported attacking people near the coast in the lead-up to the Nod-Krai Archon Quest prelude.
### LARGE +
In the Searing storyline the Fatui used a replica control device to command Secret Source automata and tried to revive a malevolent dragonlord core. That crisis was ended by Ineffa and the Traveler.

## travel
### LARGE
A harbour on the cliff receives ships from Nod-Krai; the clan registers arrivals. The way from the Stadium of the Sacred Flame runs along Xalac Vale.

## quests
@version: 5.2
### LARGE
Of Fading Flowers and Falling Feathers (Chasca's Tribal Chronicles, Time for the Showdown being Act III), and the Song of the Welkin Moon prelude The Journey Home, whose last part, Searing, begins at the clan's village (5.8).

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the clan is at peace, and the cliff doubles as a haven for Nod-Krai refugees and merchants.
### STANDARD +
The Abyss no longer threatens Natlan, and Chasca is a hero of the six. The harbour's later effect on the tribe is not described beyond its arrival.
### ULTRA +
The wiki lists no changes to Quahuacan Cliff in 6.x or 7.x except bug fixes.

## entry: Flower-Feather Clan
@keys: Flower-Feather Clan, Tlalocan, Qucusaur Riders, Wingless
@keys2: Quahuacan, Natlan
@version: 5.2
### STANDARD
The Flower-Feather Clan (Tlalocan) is one of Natlan's six tribes. Its chief is Mutota. Its warriors form the Qucusaur Riders and its saurian companions are Qucusaurs. It was founded thousands of years ago by Lianca and has taken in orphans, exiles and banished dragons.

## entry: Malinalco Grotto
@keys: Malinalco Grotto, Tenebrous Papilla, Kuntur, Rumi
@version: 5.2
### LARGE
Malinalco Grotto holds a mural of the legend of Kuntur and Rumi, reached from the Tenebrous Papilla boss cave. The name echoes a Mexican town whose name means 'where they worship the goddess Malinalxochitl'. Restorers' notes say that mural-making in a paint-producing place is easy because pigments can be gathered on the spot.
