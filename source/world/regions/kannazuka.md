## summary
### LITE
Kannazuka is an area of Inazuma made of rocky, cliff-ringed islands lying between Narukami Island and Yashiori Island. Its name is said to mean "the hill where no gods reside": neither the Shogun's faith nor Watatsumi's has a hold there. It holds Tatarasuna, where the Mikage Furnace (Inazuma's largest smelter) makes Jade Steel for swords. It also holds the Kujou Encampment, the Shogun's Army headquarters that faced the Watatsumi resistance during the recent civil war.
### STANDARD +
The war halted Jade Steel production and damaged the furnace, but the war is now over and the furnace has been made safe again.

## geography
### STANDARD
Mountainous ground with high, steep cliffs. Tatarasuna is a ring of islands enclosed by jagged crags, with the Mikage Furnace's blast furnace at its centre; the crags shelter the Shogunate's smelting works. The Kujou Encampment, the Shogunate's battlefront fortress, stands elsewhere in the area. Nazuchi Beach, a war-scarred shoal, joins Kannazuka to Yashiori Island. Thunder Sakura trees, which share their origin with the Sacred Sakura on Mt. Yougou, grow across the area.
### LARGE +
Local plants and materials include Dendrobium, Crystal Marrow, Naku Weed, Onikabuto and Sea Ganoderma. Old Kamuijima Cannons stand along Tatarasuna's eastern side.

## places
### STANDARD
- **Tatarasuna**: the island ring around the Mikage Furnace, centre of the Shogunate's weapon production.
- **Mikage Furnace**: Inazuma's largest smelting plant, producing Jade Steel from Crystal Marrow.
- **Kujou Encampment**: the Shogun's Army camp under the Tenryou Commission, built to guard against rebellion from Watatsumi.
### LARGE +
- **The Arsenal**: an old storehouse in Tatarasuna.
- **Shakkei Pavilion**: a hidden site in Tatarasuna where, about 400 years ago, a nameless young man (the future Wanderer) was found unconscious.
- **Violet Court**: a domain in Kannazuka.

## people
### STANDARD
Shogunate soldiers and officers garrison the Kujou Encampment. They include Kujou Masahito, a general of the Shogun's Army and son of Tenryou Commissioner Kujou Takayuki; the yoriki Iwakura Mitsunari is also posted there. Tatarasuna has workers and the Fontaine engineer Xavier, who maintains the furnace.
### LARGE +
During the war the Grand Narukami Shrine sent the shrine maiden Miyuki to Kannazuka to look into damaged Thunder Sakura. Treasure Hoarders, ronin (Nobushi and Kairagi) and Fatui operatives also prowl the area.

## governance
### STANDARD
The area is officially Shogunate territory and is held militarily through the Tenryou Commission and the Shogun's Army. Official descriptions call it disputed territory between the Shogunate and Sangonomiya, belonging fully to neither army. Tatarasuna's works answer to Shogunate officials. Four centuries ago an Armory Officer held the highest authority there.

## history
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Legend says that hundreds of years ago, during a disaster, Kujou Shigeyori built a front-line fortress in a single night and fought the forces of darkness under the Electro Mitsudomoe banner. His descendants inherited his skill at military engineering, and the Kujou Encampment carries his name. About 400 years ago Tatarasuna suffered a disaster: the furnace spewed Tatarigami that sickened and killed its workers. The Fatui Harbinger Dottore, disguised as the Fontaine mechanic "Escher", had secretly caused it. During the recent civil war the Tenryou Commission based its army here against the Watatsumi resistance, and the furnace was disabled by Tatarigami saturation.
### LARGE +
In the original course of events, the Armory Officer Niwa Hisahide was murdered by Dottore, who put Niwa's heart into a purification device. He gave it to the kabukimono, a puppet the Shogunate's people had befriended, who used it to absorb the furnace's Tatarigami and believed Niwa had betrayed him. Blame fell on Inspector Mikoshi Nagamasa. His yoriki Katsuragi took the punishment in his place, and Nagamasa killed him with the blade later called Katsuragikiri Nagamasa. The Wanderer (that same puppet) learned the truth through Irminsul in Sumeru and then erased himself from history. The world now remembers that Niwa and a brave swordsmith of the Hyakume Clan died purifying the furnace.
### ULTRA +
Before the war, the alchemist Alice visited Tatarasuna and modified the furnace's containment for safety at the cost of output. When Tatarigami saturation shut the furnace down during the war, Xavier raised a containment dome. The Fatui, interested in the Tatarigami's power, breached it. The surviving "Rather Aged Notes" about the Daitatara Nagamasa blade were plundered by the Fatui when they took over the furnace.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Grey crags, smoke-stained works and the hum of Balethunder around a cooling furnace. The Encampment has palisades, banners and drilling soldiers. Visitors once came to admire the Thunder Sakura, but as Miyuki put it, nobody came during the fighting.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Before Tatara Tales was completed, Balethunder made the area around the furnace harmful. Ronin, Treasure Hoarders, Abyss Mages and Fatui units roam the islands. In Sakura Arborism, Treasure Hoarders equipped by an unnamed "big-shot buyer" damaged the Thunder Sakura while siphoning their power into a crystal, and their leader Hideaki awoke a dormant Electro Hypostasis.
### LARGE +
Gameplay note: the Kujou Encampment area hosts a Pyro Hypostasis boss.

## quests
@version: 2.0
### LARGE
Tatara Tales (helping Xavier drive off the Fatui and restore the Mikage Furnace) and Sakura Arborism (healing the Thunder Sakura with Miyuki) take place here. The civil-war Archon Quests of Chapter II pass along the Kannazuka front.

## status
### STANDARD
As of 7.1: the war is over and the Mikage Furnace's Balethunder has been cleared. The Encampment remains a Shogunate post. No later events specific to Kannazuka are recorded in 6.x-7.1 content.

## entry: Tatarasuna and the Mikage Furnace
@keys: Tatarasuna, Mikage Furnace, Jade Steel, Tatara Tales
@version: 2.0
### STANDARD
Tatarasuna is the Shogunate's sheltered smelting ground in Kannazuka, a ring of crag-bound islands around the Mikage Furnace, Inazuma's largest blast furnace. It turns Crystal Marrow (mined from Orobashi's remains on Yashiori) into Jade Steel for Inazuman swords, and the process carries Tatarigami with it. Experts from Fontaine helped build the furnace; the engineer Xavier stayed on to maintain it. During the civil war Tatarigami saturation shut it down, Xavier sealed it under a dome, and the Fatui broke in. The Traveler fired old Kamuijima Cannons at the dome, drove off the Fatui and helped Xavier stabilise the furnace.

## entry: The Tatarasuna Incident
@keys: Tatarasuna incident, Tatarasuna Mystery, Escher, Niwa Hisahide, kabukimono
@keys2: Tatarasuna, Mikage Furnace, Dottore, Wanderer, Niwa
@spoiler: major
@version: 3.3
### LARGE
About 400 years ago, "Escher", supposedly a mechanic from Fontaine, helped speed up the furnace with Crystal Marrow. Black Tatarigami fumes spread and workers died, and Armory Officer Niwa Hisahide imposed an information blackout. Messengers to Tenshukaku never returned. The kabukimono, a puppet befriended by Tatarasuna's officials, was refused an audience with the Shogun, because Ei had just withdrawn into the Plane of Euthymia. Yae Miko promised help that came too late. Escher was really Dottore: he killed Niwa and put his heart into a purification device. The kabukimono used it to absorb the furnace's Tatarigami, was told Niwa had fled, and left feeling betrayed. Inspector Mikoshi Nagamasa took the blame; his yoriki Katsuragi died in his place. The kabukimono later became the Harbinger Scaramouche and then the Wanderer, who learned the truth in Irminsul.

## entry: Kujou Encampment
@keys: Kujou Encampment, Kujou Jin'ya
### STANDARD
The Kujou Encampment is the Shogun's Army camp in Kannazuka, run by the Tenryou Commission to guard against Watatsumi rebellion. In the civil war it was the Shogunate's front-line headquarters; the general Kujou Masahito is posted there. Official text judged the Shogun's Army larger, better trained and better equipped than the Sangonomiya forces. Legend credits its origins to Kujou Shigeyori, who raised a fortress in one night during an ancient disaster.
