## summary
### LITE
Easybreeze Holiday Resort is a bright, sparsely inhabited island off the westernmost edge of Natlan. It is also called Tenochtzitoc, the old settlement where the war with the Abyss began and finally ended. The resort opened in 5.8 for a trial summer season: sandy beaches, a market, dance stages and playful Asha, colour-loving mascots that are secretly the souls of people lost in the Mare Jivari. Behind the fun lies the grave of the hero Tenoch and a temple built to hold back the Bakunawa.
### STANDARD +
Tenochtzitoc means 'cavern of stone and prickly pears' in the local tongue, though prickly pears no longer grow here. The resort is the first summer-themed map added permanently to the game.

## geography
### STANDARD
An island west of Natlan. Its main areas are Colorfall Cliffs (green fields streaked with swirling colour and the 'Airborne Asha' attraction in the sky), Guiztli Ridge, Wavey Bay (sandy beaches, pristine water, water attractions) and Tete Isle, along with the Easybreeze Market and the Concealed Land of Enigmas.
### LARGE +
The Concealed Land of Enigmas is a small cavern behind a waterfall west of Wavey Bay. A mystic portal there leads to the Pyro Archon Temple and, through it, to a small corner of the Mare Jivari, the westernmost part of the island that was blown off the timeline.

## places
### STANDARD
Easybreeze Market, the resort's commercial hub and many visitors' first stop, with restaurants, specialty shops and decor by the local Asha. Tete Isle, a lively island known for the specialty drink Aphimead, with a Dance-Off Stage, Brewblossom Stores, Me & Dew and The Dewdrop. Colorfall Cliffs, Wavey Bay and Guiztli Ridge, each with attractions. The Concealed Land of Enigmas and the Pyro Archon Temple.
### LARGE +
Easybreeze Market includes Asha's Treasure Trove, Shoal's Haven (a seafood restaurant) and the Seven-Colored Statue, a giant octopus-like statue spouting seven bubbles that visitors can paint with colour. Tete Isle is only reachable after the story quest The World Is Your Canvas! reaches its fifth step. The Pyro Archon Temple holds inscriptions for Tupac, Menilek, Sundjatta, Tenoch (whose remains are buried there), Wanjiru, Sanhaj Kompore (also buried there) and Burkina, as well as tablets signed by the Pyro Archons Shulevitz, Gloharry and Cochanina.
### ULTRA +
At check-in the quest gives the player an Easybreeze Album and a resort map. Resort staff member Ukucha describes four main areas: Colorfall Cliffs, Guiztli Ridge, Wavey Bay and Tete Isle.

## people
### STANDARD
Resort staff, vendors and tourists, together with Asha. The resort staff, many Natlanese, run lodging, food and attractions. Mualani, Chasca, Bennett, and other visitors tested the new resort in 5.8.
### LARGE +
Notable figures include Ukucha at check-in, Teuciztli of Shoal's Haven, Macuahuitl of Asha's Treasure Trove and Aloha, manager of the Alpaca Park. Enjou, an Abyss Lector, appears in The Last Survivor of Tenochtzitoc searching for the Mare Jivari. Citlali investigates there. Venti and Mavuika quietly work to help souls trapped in the Mare Jivari go home.

## culture
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
During the Sunspray Summer Resort event of 5.8 the resort's opening was marked with attractions and games, and the Natlan Adventurers' Guild sent invitations to adventurers across Teyvat to try the resort out.
### LARGE +
Asha are the resort's mascots: special Monetoo that are more sociable and intelligent than ordinary ones because they come from human souls. The word 'Asha' in Tenochtzitoc culture has two meanings in the game, 'benevolent soul' and 'ashes', the second referring to ashes blown from the Mare Jivari. The resort's guests can wear Asha headbands.

## history
@version: 5.8
### STANDARD
Before the cataclysm the Muratan tribe settled at Tenochtzitoc. About five hundred years ago the Abyss invaded and the Bakunawa, a mountain-sized beast made by Rhinedottir, devoured villages on the west of Natlan. The hero Tenoch, a Muratan who had been exiled for his battle-mania, was the last survivor of the settlement. He gathered six allies from the tribes and led them into a battle against the Abyss at the place that became the Mare Jivari. Natlan/History marks the Abyss's invasion in that year as the start of the Night Warden Wars.
### LARGE +
Sanhaj of the Masters of the Night-Wind engraved Tenoch's body with Phlogiston Engraving, Tenoch let the Bakunawa swallow him, and Sanhaj raised a miniature Night Kingdom inside it. The clash shattered the beast and killed both men. The Bakunawa could regrow, so Pyro Archons built the temple to contain it. Natlan/History says Cochanina, Shulevitz and Gloharry died doing so and that Isaja, the last of them, managed to contain it. To keep the Abyss from spreading across Teyvat, Barbatos blew the whole Mare Jivari off the timeline, trapping the souls of those who died there. About twenty years ago some of those souls merged with Monetoo and became the Asha. Around the same time a mountain-sized beast was reported roaming the Mare Jivari and the region vanished.
### ULTRA +
Bennett's parents, Aroni and Macani, and the rest of the Tecpatl's Flintblades adventure team were attacked by Abyssal monsters in the Mare Jivari and gave up their forms and memories to protect the infant Bennett; they lingered as Asha. In 5.8 Venti, with Mavuika's help, collected the souls who wished to return; Aroni and Macani regained their memories, were briefly reunited with their son and passed into the Night Kingdom. After the end of the Night Warden Wars, more people began settling in the area and the Easybreeze Holiday Resort was founded and began trial operations. Enjou, who sought a forgotten homeland, finished his search in the restricted Mare Jivari and decided to enter the true one.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Salt air, sunlight and music, brightly coloured paint splashed on rocks and walls, Asha bobbing through the crowd, with a gentle undertone: the island's ashy western end and its monuments remind visitors that this was a battlefield not long ago.

## dangers
### STANDARD
The resort is mostly safe, though a few hilichurls turn up in quest areas and Abyssal monsters lurk in the caves tied to the Mare Jivari. Pirates from Nasha Town, led by Radius Bone Jack, are mentioned in letters on Tete Isle.
### LARGE +
Letters found there show a plan by Black Reef Rekis to have Radius Bone Jack's crew capture Monetoo for wealthy clients, with a man named Icahua sent to watch them. A defiant line scrawled at the end of one letter says there is to be no catching Asha. The Bakunawa still exists as a restrained fragment in the Mare Jivari, and an Abyssal simulation of the Mare Jivari exists as well.

## travel
### LARGE
Teleport waypoints are spread around the island. Visitors move between the market, bay and cliffs on foot, and the player can indwell an Asha to cross walls and water. Leaving the resort area ejects the player from the Asha.

## quests
@version: 5.8
### LARGE
The Sunspray Summer Resort story quests run: To a Carefree Vacation!, The World Is Your Canvas!, The Surprise That Awaits Us All! and A Curtain Call for a Colorful Summer Night!. Their event was limited, but version 6.3 added an Event Quests page that lets players accept such permanent event storylines later. World quests: The Last Survivor of Tenochtzitoc and Traces of Chroma.
### ULTRA +
Traces of Chroma introduces the Asha Zolton, created by the Masters of the Night-Wind priest Alu, who passed his memories to the Asha through the tribe's mnemonic arts; Alu lost access to those memories himself.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the resort is open. Venti and Mavuika have guided the souls who wished to return home out of the Mare Jivari.
### STANDARD +
Only a small corner of the Mare Jivari is explorable. The Bakunawa is restrained but could return.
### ULTRA +
The wiki reports no later story changes to the island except bug fixes in 6.0 to 6.7.

## entry: Asha
@keys: Asha
@keys2: Easybreeze, Tenochtzitoc, Monetoo
@version: 5.8
### STANDARD
Asha are exploration-mechanic Monetoo exclusive to the resort and its mascots. They roam freely, and some are created from the souls of those who perished in the Mare Jivari. They are more sociable and intelligent than ordinary Monetoo. Asha can also be created by human hands: the Masters of the Night-Wind priest Alu made the Asha Zolton while researching a link between Asha and Saurian Inscriptions.
### LARGE +
Bennett's parents Aroni and Macani lived as Asha for years, and when freed from the Mare Jivari they regained their memories and could switch between Asha and human form before passing on.

## entry: Pyro Archon Temple and the Bakunawa
@keys: Pyro Archon Temple, Bakunawa
@version: 5.8
### STANDARD
The Pyro Archon Temple is reached from the Concealed Land of Enigmas. Citlali believes the Pyro Archons built it about five hundred years ago to contain the Bakunawa. It has stone tablets from several Pyro Archons and monuments for Tenoch and the heroes who fought with him.
### LARGE +
The Bakunawa is a mountain-sized beast created by Rhinedottir whose immortality is not from Life but from a different, transcendental form of Abyssal power. It can be defeated for a time and then returns after 72 hours (a gameplay detail). A fragment of it was restrained by the Traveler and Enjou in The Last Survivor of Tenochtzitoc.

## entry: Mare Jivari
@keys: Mare Jivari, Ash Plain
@version: 5.8
### LARGE
The Mare Jivari was the westernmost part of Tenochtzitoc. It was named after a great battle there during the cataclysm. Present-day descriptions call it an empty 'endless plain of ash where the wind does not blow', but it once held a sea of fire. It was blown off the timeline by Barbatos to stop the Abyss spreading.

## entry: Tenoch
@keys: Tenoch, Tenoyollotzin, Talking Stick
@version: 5.8
### STANDARD
Tenoch, also known by the title Tenoyollotzin, was a Muratan warrior exiled for his battle-mania yet respected by the other tribes. His giant obsidian club, the Talking Stick, got its name from his habit of using it to 'mediate' disputes. He gathered six heroes to resist the Abyss and died at the Mare Jivari with Sanhaj. He could not be revived by the Ode of Resurrection because he had already lost his Ancient Name.
