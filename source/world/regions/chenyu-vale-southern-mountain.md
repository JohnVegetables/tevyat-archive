## summary
### LITE
The Southern Mountain is the quieter, older half of Chenyu Vale, added in 4.4. It is a land of tea slopes, butterfly valleys and ruins, where the first tea tree of the vale still grows, where the snake adeptus called the Herblord once kept a courtyard, and where the ancient city of Chizhang once stood under the vale's goddess. A solitary suanni, Lingyuan, ranges over its mountains.
### STANDARD +
Most of what survives here is memory: empty medicine jars, stone statues that talk in their sleep, shrine foundations and a few withered trees waiting for adeptal power.

## geography
### STANDARD
Rugged tea country south of Qiaoying Village. Teatree Slope lies south of the village and north of Yaodie Valley; Mt. Xuanlian rises south of both Yaodie Valley and Chizhang Wall, which lies to its north. Lingshu Courtyard hides in a cave reached from Yaodie Valley.
### LARGE +
Ancient notes in elegant handwriting, mostly blurred, can be read at the Adeptus' Repose. The Solitary Suanni, the area's boss, is met at Chizhang Wall.

## places
### STANDARD
Chizhang Wall: the ruin of the city governed by the mistress of Chenyu Vale; a desolate shrine foothill. Adeptus' Repose and Lingshu Courtyard: the old home of the Herblord. Teatree Slope: the first tea tree, planted by Fujin. Yaodie Valley: the valley of vibrant butterflies. Mt. Xuanlian: a lone mountain south of the valley.
### LARGE +
A cave shelter at the southeast of Chizhang Wall holds a secret mural behind a jade mechanism. The slope's viewpoint is titled "Ancient Pact," and the Lingshu Courtyard's is "Herb-Housing Hall."

## people
### LARGE
No settlements. Mostly stone simulacra and the occasional traveler. The adepti linked to the area (Fujin, Lingyuan and the Herblord) are covered in the mountain's history and the quests.

## governance
### LARGE
None now. Chizhang was once governed by the mistress of Chenyu Vale, who also ruled its animals and spirits; the Herblord held the southern lands around Lingshu.

## culture
### LARGE
The Herblord used her knowledge to make medicines and save the sick, and her courtyard kept colorful butterflies and a medicine jar meant to nourish an adeptal tree. The first tea tree on Teatree Slope is the mother of the vale's tea culture, said to remember a promise between two friends.

## history
### STANDARD
Chizhang was the mistress's city. Her three servants, Herblord, Fujin and Lingyuan, were adepti of wild mountain nature. Fujin planted the first tea tree on land that was the Herblord's. In the Archon War the mistress turned cruel and tried to flood the vale; the three rebelled, and the Herblord, her body torn apart in the fight, was reduced to a small form that could barely survive.
### LARGE +
After the Herblord left, her abode fell silent. The surviving butterflies scattered, and an adeptal tree there withered. The wiki states that the Herblord is now known as Changsheng, Baizhu's white snake companion.
### ULTRA +
The mistress was sealed deep under the vale by the ruins of a lunar chariot. A book of the vale's gossip says she presided over the birds, beasts and adepti of wild nature and controlled the Bishui's ebb and flow.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Hushed tea terraces under fog; a cave filled with drifting butterflies; the silence of ruined shrine steps. Faded notes in an abandoned courtyard and a stone statue that boasts of scaring visitors.

## dangers
### LARGE
Miasma, which makes monsters more violent; hilichurls near Lingshu; and the Solitary Suanni near Chizhang Wall. The mountain's spirit veins were disturbed in 4.4.

## travel
### STANDARD
Teleport waypoints connect the slope, valley and Chizhang Wall. Adeptal energy from Fujin is required for the puzzle mechanisms, such as Carefree Simulacra and Golden Carp's Leap.

## quests
### LARGE
Silently the Butterfly Crosses the Valley (entered at Lingshu Courtyard) and An Ancient Sacrifice of Sacred Brocade in the Chenyu's Blessings of Sunken Jade series.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the Southern Mountain is a quiet, ruined wilderness, its tea tree alive and its butterflies back in their jar.
### STANDARD +
Fujin returned to her abode at Carp's Rest to recover after restoring the spirit veins; Lingyuan, who presides over the vale, accepted the rite.

## entry: Lingshu Courtyard and the Herblord
@keys: Lingshu Courtyard, Herblord, Adeptus' Repose, Yaodie Valley
@keys2: Chenyu Vale, Changsheng, Baizhu
@version: 4.4
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Lingshu Courtyard is the abode of the Herblord, a white snake adeptus who took human shape, made medicines and healed the sick. It lies in a cave off Yaodie Valley, the valley of vibrant butterflies. A talking Carefree Simulacrum there guards the place from intruders and, in the quest, asks the Traveler to return the scattered butterflies to the medicine jar so the withered adeptal tree might revive. It says the Herblord left when she lost the power to keep her human form. The wiki identifies her now as Changsheng, the snake who accompanies Baizhu.

## entry: Chizhang and the mistress of Chenyu Vale
@keys: Chizhang, Chizhang Wall, Mistress of Chenyu Vale
@keys2: Chenyu Vale, Morax, Archon War
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Chizhang was the ancient city governed by the mistress of Chenyu Vale, an unnamed goddess of the vale's animals and spirits who was not evil according to Fujin and made many dreams come true. When the Archon War began she grew obsessed with becoming an Archon and with survival, and she ceased granting dreams. In a last attempt to defeat Morax she planned to raise the Bishui and flood out the Guili Assembly, which would have destroyed her own vale; her adepti rebelled and she was sealed. Chizhang Wall now holds a shrine style also seen at Cuijue Slope.

## entry: Teatree Slope and the first tea tree
@keys: Teatree Slope, first tea tree
@keys2: Fujin, Chenyu Vale, tea
### STANDARD
Teatree Slope is south of Qiaoying Village and was part of the Herblord's lands. Fujin was the first to plant a tea tree in Chenyu Vale, in the Herblord's territory, and the slope is named for it; when it was a sapling someone tied a jade ornament to it. The Jade Leaf tea set text says this variety only grows well here because it remembers a promise between two friends.
