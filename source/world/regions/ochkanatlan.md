## summary
### LITE
Ochkanatlan, the Cinder City, is a ruined island city in Natlan, north of Quahuacan Cliff. It was built thousands of years ago by followers of Xbalanque on the remains of an older dragon city, and was ruled by the tyrant Och-Kan, the Sacred Lord, in the era of the Grand Alliance. Today it is thick with Abyssal corruption, shrouded in mist, and forbidden to the tribes. Any human found inside is either a memory of the past or a trespasser.
### STANDARD +
It is the setting of the world quest series Lost Traveler in the Ashen Realm (Version 5.2), in which the Traveler follows a legend of a "Jade of Return" and learns that the legend was a lure laid by the dragon Kukulkan. At the end of that series Och-Kan's tainted body is put to rest and his mind, which the adventurer Bona had freed from the Core of Chu'ulel and named Cocouik, passes on.
### LARGE +
The wiki distinguishes the area (this entry) from the ancient civilisation of the same name. Use this entry for the place: how it looks, what is in it, and what a visitor would meet.

## geography
@version: 5.2
### LITE
A mist-covered island north of Quahuacan Cliff, with a castle-like structure, the Cradle of Fleeting Dreams, at its centre. Waterfalls and underground ruins lie below it. Above it, drifting over a sea of clouds, hangs the Tonatiuh.
### LARGE +
Local plants include Withering Purpurbloom, Brilliant Chrysanthemum, Saurian Claw Succulent, Glowing Hornshroom and Quenepa Berry, and different wildlife and Saurian species occupy different parts of the island. The base of the city is the old dragon city Chichen Uctokah (the "Land of Seven Flames"), which was built as the foot of a "stairway" or "cable" leading beyond Teyvat, working with the Tonatiuh as a satellite.
### ULTRA +
Beneath the streets are layered underground ruins, from the Buried Underground Ruins and a Winding Ancient Road down to the old dragon chambers. An Altar of Primal Flame is used in the story to reach the Tonatiuh. Khaenri'ahn Ruin Machines, including several disabled Aeonblight Drakes, are scattered across the island, a fact canon notes without explaining.

## places
@version: 5.2
### STANDARD
- **Cradle of Fleeting Dreams:** the castle-like structure at the island's centre, with a staircase up to a throne.
- **Vision Serpent's Sanctum:** the area above the Core of Chu'ulel, which lies in a cavern beneath it.
- **Core of Chu'ulel:** a cavern holding a glowing, heart-like light core that once powered all of Ochkanatlan. Abyssal corruption wore it down, and it loses its last vitality when cleansed.
- **Old Core of Chu'ulel:** underground chambers containing a huge dragon statue in which Och-Kan imprisoned the Dragonlord for millennia.
### LARGE +
Och-Kan took the city's authority from the Dragonlord Ixlel and imprisoned her in a statue. After the Scarlet-Eyed Youth's party overthrew him, his body and mind, which he had already separated, went different ways: Kukulkan later made the body an Abyssal conduit, the Dragon of the City of Flowing Ash, and spread the legend of treasure aboard the Tonatiuh to lure adventurers to it. Ten years after the Cataclysm the Ochkanatlan Adventure Team came and failed; some decades later Taika's daughter Bona went and likewise fell.
### ULTRA +
In Version 5.2 the Traveler frees Ixlel in Open Your Heart to Me, follows Bona's search for the Jade of Return, discovers it is a Golden Entreaty, and in Moment of Awakening defeats the corrupted dragon, which then speaks as Och-Kan. He admits he hid the Golden Entreaty because it was as important as it was dangerous, and entrusts it to the Little One, who also bears the Flamelord's Blessing. His spirit later returns to the Night Kingdom and helps the Traveler there.

## atmosphere
@version: 5.2
### STANDARD
Mist, ash and a constant sense of being watched. Waterfalls drown out the whispers that haunt former members of the adventure team. A scene here should feel like Natlan stripped of its festivals: quiet, ominous, and full of voices that should not be there.

## dangers
@version: 5.2
### STANDARD
Abyssal corruption saturates the island. Expect Tenebrous Mimiflora, which imitate people (Quenin's double is one) to lure the unwary, hilichurls, Secret Source automata, Ruin Machines and the tainted dragon. Quenin claims people who stay near the ruins hear whimpering and see pale faces like their own.

## travel
@version: 5.2
### STANDARD
Entry is forbidden by the tribes and the city is regarded as cursed. The Traveler goes in because of the Golden Entreaty seen in a vision at the Sage of the Stolen Flame's ruins, which points to Ochkanatlan.

## quests
@version: 5.2
### LARGE
- **Lost Traveler in the Ashen Realm** (5.2): Act I City Buried By Ash (Vaulting the Wall of Morning Mist); Act II A Dream of Gazing Upon the Distant Sky (Adventure in the Land of Mists, Palace of the Vision Serpent, To the Sky-Road); Act III The Tonatiuh Quivers (The Other Side of the Sky, Moment of Awakening); Epilogue The Chosen One's Promise.
- **Open Your Heart to Me:** frees Ixlel in the Old Core of Chu'ulel.
- **Twisted Extension:** Quenin's story at the waterfall camp.
### ULTRA +
The Road Ahead explains the tribes' wariness about the city, and its dialogue changes depending on whether the series has already been finished.

## status
@version: 5.2
### LITE
As of 7.1 Ochkanatlan is still a ruin and still officially forbidden, but the tainted dragon is gone.
### STANDARD +
The Core of Chu'ulel is spent, Ixlel is freed and the Tonatiuh's lure is exposed. The Golden Entreaty found there is now with the Traveler's party. No Nod-Krai (6.x) or Snezhnaya (7.0-7.1) content that was found while researching this entry revisits the island.

## entry: Och-Kan
@keys: Och-Kan, Sacred Lord, Python King, Cocouik
@version: 5.2
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Och-Kan, the Sacred Lord and Python King, was Kukulkan's son, a dragon with a human soul who revered Xbalanque; after Xbalanque entered the Sacred Flame he took power and ruled the Cinder City as a tyrant. He was overthrown and split into a corrupted body (the Dragon of the City of Flowing Ash) and a mind that lived on as the small spirit Cocouik. His full entry is historical.och-kan.
### LARGE +
Dead as of 5.2, his spirit returns to the Night Kingdom and later helps the Traveler there.

## entry: Tonatiuh Treasure Legend
@keys: Tonatiuh, Jade of Return, Skyserpent Ship
@version: 5.2
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Legend held that the Tonatiuh, a ship drifting above the clouds, was full of treasure and of a weapon able to defeat the Abyss. Kukulkan invented the tale to lure brave adventurers for an experiment of his own. The Jade of Return is in fact a Golden Entreaty.

## entry: Ochkanatlan Adventure Team
@keys: Ochkanatlan Adventure Team, Taika, Liriwu, Quenin
@version: 5.2
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
An unofficial name for the tribal adventurers who went to Ochkanatlan around the time of the Cataclysm in search of the Abyss-repelling treasure. Taika of the Flower-Feather Clan led them; only the young mystic Liriwu of the Masters of the Night-Wind came back alive.

## entry: Ixlel and the Old Core
@keys: Ixlel, Ix Uxul Tz'ib Bolon, Old Core of Chu'ulel
@version: 5.2
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The Dragonlord Ix Uxul Tz'ib Bolon Ch'ule-L, whom Paimon shortens to Ixlel, once ruled Chichen Uctokah. Och-Kan took her authority and sealed her in a statue in the Old Core of Chu'ulel, and she refused to power his city. In Open Your Heart to Me the Traveler frees her after millennia of torment.

## entry: Cradle of Fleeting Dreams
@keys: Cradle of Fleeting Dreams, Vision Serpent's Sanctum
@version: 5.2
### STANDARD
The castle-like structure at the centre of Ochkanatlan, with a staircase up to a throne. A note left there by a Spiritcaller of the Masters of the Night-Wind records the horror of the creatures found nearby and a doubt about why the Python King died in the Abyss if he held a treasure that could destroy it.
