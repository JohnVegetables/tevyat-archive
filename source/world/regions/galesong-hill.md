## summary
### LITE
Galesong Hill is the windswept eastern part of Mondstadt, a region of open hills running down to the sea. Its best-known spot is Windrise, a plain under a giant oak where a Statue of the Seven stands, said to mark the place where Vennessa ascended to Celestia. East of it lie the Falcon Coast and Cape Oath, favourite places for walks and for young couples, while the rugged Dadaupa Gorge belongs to hilichurl tribes.
### STANDARD +
There is no town here; the area is travelled rather than lived in. Offshore, the island of Musk Reef holds the portal to the Spiral Abyss and is reached through a time tunnel at Cape Oath.

## geography
### STANDARD
Rolling grassland and breezy plains that fall to a shallow, sandy coast. Windrise sits inland; Falcon Coast stretches east from it to the sea, and with Cape Oath it shelters a bay east of Mondstadt. Dadaupa Gorge is a mountain stretch of cliffs and ledges nearby. Local plants include calla lily, dandelion seed, small lamp grass and windwheel aster.
### LARGE +
Official text says the long coastline and shallow water make the shore pleasant to walk, and that falcons can often be seen above the blue waves. Cape Oath is the south-eastern corner of Mondstadt's surrounds and borders Galesong Hill; from its drop in terrain Windrise, which faces the sea, is easy to see. Dadaupa Gorge is described as a mountain near the city crowded with hilichurls. In Hilichurlian, "dadaupa" means "large mountain" according to the Handy Handbook of Hilichurlian.

## places
### STANDARD
Windrise (the oak and the Statue of the Seven; the Temple of the Falcon ruin lies beneath it as a one-time domain); Falcon Coast; Cape Oath; Dadaupa Gorge with the Forgotten Sword Cemetery; Musk Reef.
### LARGE +
Windrise has a fishing point, a Ruins Torch and an Elemental Monument. Dadaupa Gorge also holds the Temple of the Lion domain, a Ruins Torch and an Elemental Monument. The Temple of the Falcon is a ruin deep underground through which strong winds still pour.

## people
### STANDARD
The hilichurl tribes of Dadaupa Gorge: the Meaty tribe, the Sleeper tribe and the Eclipse tribe. Otherwise only passers-by: the botanist Chloris, the historian Dr. Edith and the Knight of Favonius Godwin are met at Windrise, the researcher Dr. Livingstone in the gorge, and on Falcon Coast the knight Mack and the locals Jill and Will.
### ULTRA +
These are minor open-world NPCs listed by role only; none is a resident authority on the region.

## culture
### STANDARD
Windrise is where Mondstadters remember Vennessa, the gladiator who founded the Knights of Favonius and is counted as the Falcon of the West among the Four Winds. Cape Oath is known as a romantic spot, a favourite place for couples.
### LARGE +
Official viewpoint text says the plain "bathes in an everlasting breeze" and that an ancient tree left by a heroine speaks softly in the wind. The expedition description says the oak's shade shelters the Statue of the Seven at its foot from rain and wind, as Vennessa once protected Mondstadt.

## history
### STANDARD
Windrise is the site where Vennessa is said to have ascended to Celestia about 1,000 years ago, becoming a god who now watches over Mondstadt; the huge oak is believed to have grown afterwards. Falcon Coast takes its name from the first Knight of Boreas, who fought demons from the Dark Sea on the shore: thousands of falcons circled the remains for several days.
### LARGE +
Musk Reef was once the summit of a great mountain, Pilos Peak, which the adventurer Leonard tried and failed to climb. When Barbatos blew snow and ice off the land about 2,600 years ago, the peak was swept from its place and the melting ice formed the sea, leaving the island. Dadaupa Gorge's Forgotten Sword Cemetery is described as a witness to the battle against the monsters of Khaenri'ah.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Constant sea-breeze, long grass, falcons overhead, a lone oak throwing deep shade. Sunrises and sunsets are especially fine from Cape Oath. The gorge is the opposite: rough, echoing, with hilichurl camps and the occasional mitachurl.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Hilichurls of every kind, slimes, and in the gorge Fatui skirmishers, mitachurls and Abyss mages. Dadaupa Gorge is the dangerous part; Falcon Coast and Windrise are mild.
### LARGE +
Cape Oath is listed as the area of an Electro Hypostasis boss, and Electro slimes there guard the seelie that raise the wind current to the time tunnel.

## travel
### LARGE
Reached from Mondstadt City by road east across the plain. The time tunnel at Cape Oath carries travellers to Musk Reef. Windrise is a convenient rest stop with a Statue of the Seven.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 Galesong Hill is peaceful countryside; none of the pages read for this entry records a change to it after Version 1.0.
### STANDARD +
Its Archive neighbours Windrest Peak (opened in 6.5) and Starfell Valley are where recent Mondstadt events have been centred.

## entry: Windrise and Vennessa
@keys: Windrise, Falcon of the West
@keys2: Vennessa, Statue of the Seven, oak
### STANDARD
Windrise is a plain where the giant oak grows, believed to have sprung up after Vennessa ascended to Celestia on this spot. A Statue of the Seven stands in the tree's shade. Mondstadters say she now watches over them as the Falcon of the West. See the Mondstadt entry for the full account of her rebellion.
### LARGE +
The Windrise viewpoint is titled Windswept Wilderness.

## entry: Falcon Coast and Cape Oath
@keys: Falcon Coast, Cape Oath
### STANDARD
Falcon Coast runs from Windrise to the sea and, with Cape Oath, shelters a bay east of Mondstadt. Its name comes from the first Knight of Boreas, whose fight against demons from the Dark Sea drew thousands of falcons. Cape Oath is where two unnamed lovers once swore an oath, and young couples still come, especially for sunrise and sunset. A time tunnel here leads to Musk Reef.

## entry: Dadaupa Gorge
@keys: Dadaupa Gorge, Forgotten Sword Cemetery, Sword Cemetery
### STANDARD
A rugged mountain gorge that is home to three hilichurl tribes, the Meaty, Sleeper and Eclipse tribes. The Forgotten Sword Cemetery in it is remembered as a witness to the fight against the monsters of Khaenri'ah. The place is known for its danger, with mitachurls and samachurls more common than in the surrounding country.

## entry: Musk Reef and Pilos Peak
@keys: Musk Reef, Pilos Peak
@keys2: Leonard, Spiral Abyss
### STANDARD
Musk Reef is a small island east of Cape Oath whose main feature is the portal to the Spiral Abyss. Before Barbatos reshaped the land about 2,600 years ago it was the summit of Pilos Peak, which the adventurer Leonard tried and failed to climb; his journal is the book Of Mountains and Seas.

## entry: Unreconciled Stars on Musk Reef
@keys: Unreconciled Stars
@keys2: Musk Reef, Leonard, Mona
@availability: limited
### ULTRA
During the limited-time event Unreconciled Stars, Leonard's constellation fell as meteorites about 2,000 years after the Pilos Peak era; whoever touched one slept and dreamed of climbing to a summit. The core, holding the most elemental energy and Leonard's will, landed on Musk Reef. After facing Fatui who came to investigate, the Traveler and Mona destroyed it and ended the dreams. This is event content and not common knowledge.
