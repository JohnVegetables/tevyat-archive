## summary
### LITE
Everfrozen Earth is the frozen northern region of Snezhnaya (first seen in 7.0). It holds the capital Snezhnograd, the Fatui headquarters city Glupov inside it, the fishing port of Morepesok and the Prime Ice mining station Svetloledovka. The ground is permanently frozen soil; the region is known for its birch forestry, a trade humanity carved out during Snezhnaya's pioneering era that still runs today.
### STANDARD +
The in-game description calls the north's frozen soil the home of Morepesok, the only port city on the northern coast. Snezhnograd and its Fatui satellite city Glupov are the political centre of the nation, which makes the region the backdrop for the opening of the Snezhnaya Archon Quest.

## geography
### STANDARD
Frozen ground and snow country on the northern coast. Morepesok is a seaside port whose people live by fishing; Svetloledovka sits by a Prime Ice mine; Snezhnograd lies inland as the capital. Birch groves are the region's famous feature. Local specialties include Flockingweed, Frostfairy Flower and Golden Fern, and an Immortal Construct is the area's notable boss enemy.
### LARGE +
Snezhnograd has its own district, Zolnyy, and landmarks such as the Zapolyarny Palace, the Podvorye Restaurant and the Northland Bank. A Snezhnaya train line links the capital with Svetloledovka, where Prime Ice miners who live in the city commute to work, and with Morepesok and Rankova Zorya.

## places
### STANDARD
Snezhnograd: capital of Snezhnaya. Glupov: a satellite city inside Snezhnograd and the Fatui's headquarters. Morepesok: northern fishing port and a Fatui Harbinger's hometown. Svetloledovka: Prime Ice mining station beneath the frozen mountains. 
### LARGE +
Glupov was founded when the Tsaritsa ordered the Fatui to build a fortress near the capital (an account in a history book places this in the 799th cycle) and its name means "city of fools". Morepesok's people compare catches after each outing, and an in-game remark suggests becoming a Fatui Harbinger might impress them. Svetloledovka's name can be read as "Light-Ice Village".
### ULTRA +
Fishing boats leave Morepesok to hunt in the dark northern sea; the official map text praises its people as warriors who master the raging sea and never flinch before mountain-sized sea creatures. Notice boards in Svetloledovka warn that the mine is dangerous and, later, that active monsters (Bandersnatch) forced mining to be suspended.

## dangers
### LARGE
Deep cold, blizzards and Fatui presence in the capital. Wildlife and chimeric constructs roam outside settlements. Svetloledovka's mine has been closed by monsters (Bandersnatch) after the events of the Archon Quest.

## quests
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
Archon Quest Chapter VII, Act I "Everwinter Without Mercy" passes through the region: in "Great Deeds on the Tundra" the Traveler reaches Snezhnograd by train, meets the Fatui in Glupov and Zapolyarny and then rushes to Svetloledovka to help people trapped at the Prime Ice mine.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the region is intact and open; nothing after 7.0 is recorded as changing it.

## entry: Svetloledovka and the Prime Ice Mine
@keys: Svetloledovka, Light-Ice Village, Cavern of Prime Ice Mountain
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
Svetloledovka is a mining town and train station built in the shadow of the frozen peaks, where workers dig Prime Ice. Many commute by train from Snezhnograd. During the Archon Quest the mine was cut off and people trapped inside had to be rescued; afterward a notice records that mining is suspended because of monsters inside. Its tavern and workers (engineers, a safety officer) appear in side content.
### LARGE +
An in-game puzzle has a miner nearly frozen to death who thanks the Traveler with Glaciafruit sent by his family. The area description of Fellfrost Peak says the mountain's foot hosts a mining town, so some sources treat Svetloledovka as belonging to that mountain's economy.

## entry: Morepesok
@keys: Morepesok, Sea-Sand
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
Morepesok is a small seaside port and station on the northern coast, the only port city there. It is the hometown of the Harbinger Tartaglia. Fishing is the livelihood and its harbour hosts the vessel "The Sea Tsar" (Captain Vyazemskaya, First Mate Yazykov). Local folklore and rumour about the nearby Flamefeather Valley make it the staging point for the valley quests.
