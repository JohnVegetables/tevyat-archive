## summary
### LITE
The Morte Region is the eastern waters of Fontaine's map. Its centrepiece is the Tower of Ipsissimus, also called the Sealed Ruined Tower or the Tower of Gestalt, built by the Narzissenkreuz Ordo as its base. The area also has Fort Charybdis Ruins on an island, the east slopes of Mont Automnequi, four Orthants and, reached through the story, a place called the Genesis of a Certain Micro-Universe in the Primordial Sea.
### STANDARD +
It was added in 4.2 with the world-quest chapter In the Wake of Narcissus. The name is French for 'dead'.

## geography
### STANDARD
Open water and small islands surround the tower. Mont Automnequi runs through the Belleau and Morte regions, and the east slopes belong here. Fort Charybdis Ruins stand on an island in the east. Four Orthants, linked to the Seals of Chymical Marriage, are scattered around.
### LARGE +
Local specialties are Condessence Crystal, Lumidouce Bell, Rainbow Rose, Romaritime Flower and Spring of the First Dewdrop.

## places
### STANDARD
- **Tower of Ipsissimus**: the Ordo's tower. It is not labelled on the map until In the Wake of Narcissus begins. After the quest Savior's Wake, the tower sinks and stays submerged. Its map blurb reads: 'We must reach the lowest level before the arrival of the apocalypse.'
- **Fort Charybdis Ruins**: the ruins of a fort on an island in the eastern part of the region.
- **East Slopes of Mont Automnequi**: the eastern side of the mountain.
- **Orthants of Memory, Persona, Souls and Wishes**: four marked points. The Orthant of Memory holds one of the four Seals of Chymical Marriage.
### LARGE +
- **Genesis of a Certain Micro-Universe**: a location within the Primordial Sea where the Traveler fights Narzissenkreuz in Waking from the Great Dream; the viewpoint is titled 'The World's Amniotic Fluid'.
- A floating raft east of the tower is where Urville starts Wish-Fulfilling Treasure Hunt; he later returns to a place called The Rusty Rudder.

## people
### STANDARD
The Narzissenkreuz Ordo's members (Ann, Caterpillar, Seymour, Al, Jak and others) are met in the Court region and in the tower; Jakob is the final opponent of the tower's descent. Urville and Nadia appear near the tower in Wish-Fulfilling Treasure Hunt. A boat with a Fatui banner, found after the quest Questions and Answers, holds scribbled orders to retrieve the remains of the 'Curve' lie detector before anyone in Fontaine does.

## history
### STANDARD
The tower was built by the Narzissenkreuz Ordo as its base. In the quest chain In the Wake of Narcissus (4.2), the Traveler joins the Ordo's investigation (Search in the Algae Sea), breaks four seals and enters the tower; descends it in Savior's Wake until reaching and breaking the seal over the Primordial Sea; and finally faces the being Narzissenkreuz in Waking from the Great Dream.
### LARGE +
Search in the Algae Sea ends with a fight against Jakob in the tower's lower level. In Savior's Wake the Traveler fights waves of clockwork meka after breaking the seal over the Primordial Sea. In Waking from the Great Dream the opponent is labelled 'Narzissenkreuz - Incomplete Order of the New Cosmos' and the Traveler is given a holy blade (the Sword of Narzissenkreuz) to fight it.
### ULTRA +
After Waking from the Great Dream the chain continues with Rowboat's Wake, and Wish-Fulfilling Treasure Hunt is available after it. The tower's earlier pre-quest dialogue with Nadia and Urville can be heard on its north side before the chain is finished.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
The tower's levels are built around puzzles with optical couplers, and Ann accompanies the Traveler through the upper levels. The Primordial Sea site is titled 'The World's Amniotic Fluid' in its viewpoint.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Clockwork meka in the tower and Narzissenkreuz in the Primordial Sea (both quest enemies). Contaminated Bacterial Mats and autocannons are hazards in the area.

## travel
### STANDARD
A Teleport Waypoint labelled 'Exploring the Tower of Gestalt' follows the Traveler down the tower and to the arena. The tower is not marked on the map until the quest chain starts.

## quests
### STANDARD
In the Wake of Narcissus: Search in the Algae Sea (Act I, starts at the Ordo in the Court region), Savior's Wake (Act II) and Waking from the Great Dream (Act III), then Rowboat's Wake. Others filed here: Wish-Fulfilling Treasure Hunt, An Expected Lie, A Very Fishy Encounter, An Invasion on Hold, Cupid's Lover, Muse's Mother, Meteoric Lance and Thalia and Melpomene.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the tower is permanently submerged and the Ordo story is complete.
### STANDARD +
The region remains explorable for salvage and fishing; no newer content was found.

## entry: Tower of Ipsissimus
@keys: Tower of Ipsissimus, Tower of Gestalt, Sealed Ruined Tower
@version: 4.2
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Built by the Narzissenkreuz Ordo and used as their base. In Savior's Wake the Traveler decodes a puzzle on one level, goes down, decodes another and then reaches the bottom level, beyond which lies a seal over the Primordial Sea. After the quest Savior's Wake it sinks permanently.
### LARGE +
The map shows 'Exploring the Tower of Gestalt' as a waypoint. Ann accompanies the Traveler through the upper and middle levels.

## entry: Orthants and Seals of Chymical Marriage
@keys: Orthant, Orthants, Seals of Chymical Marriage
@version: 4.2
### STANDARD
Four named points in Morte (Memory, Persona, Souls, Wishes). The wiki says the Orthant of Memory holds one of the four Seals of Chymical Marriage broken during Search in the Algae Sea; the quests Cupid's Lover, Muse's Mother, Meteoric Lance and Thalia and Melpomene go with the seals.

## entry: Wish-Fulfilling Treasure Hunt
@keys: Wish-Fulfilling Treasure Hunt, The Rusty Rudder, Urville
@version: 4.2
### STANDARD
A world quest started by talking to Urville on a floating raft east of the tower. Urville and Nadia gather lost items; the Traveler salvages items, drives off ball octopuses and finds clues to the treasure.
