## summary
### LITE
The Basin of Unnumbered Flames is the great bowl of land at the centre of Natlan. It holds the Stadium of the Sacred Flame, the nation's core landmark where the eternal Sacred Flame burns and the six tribes gather for the Pilgrimage, along with the fairgrounds and inns that serve it and the smoking Huitztli Hill to the north. Official text says the basin is not natural: something huge once crashed here, and no one knows what.
### STANDARD +
The Stadium is Natlan's main city and the seat of the Speaker's Chamber, so the basin is where tribal politics, tournaments and travellers meet. The surrounding land is dry and hot, with magma streams close beneath the surface.

## geography
### STANDARD
A broad basin whose shape the Saurian Relics Association judged to be the scar of a colossal impact rather than a natural formation. The Stadium of the Sacred Flame sits within it; Huitztli Hill, a mountain the locals also call Smoke Ridge, rises to the north. Local specialty: brilliant chrysanthemum.
### LARGE +
Magma flows from the Great Volcano of Tollan run under Huitztli Hill and send smoke between its valleys. The Stele of the Sacred Flame in the basin still burns, though no one has had an answer from its flames about the impact.

## places
### STANDARD
The Stadium of the Sacred Flame, including three districts: Chuwen Fair (shops, food stalls and the Saurian Relics Association counter), the Temple of Pax, and the Warrior's Rest Area. Also the Weary Inn, the Speaker's Chamber, the Tablet of Tona and Huitztli Hill.
### LARGE +
Chuwen Fair has Bounama's Grocery, Zakan's Street Bites and Xocolatl Beverages. The Salutary Furnace at the Temple of Pax is where the most skilled Children of Echoes craftspeople forge personal weapons for warriors using Liquid Phlogiston purified by the Sacred Flame; its owner is Alom. The Weary Inn, on the outskirts of the fair, is a restaurant and hotel that gives free food and lodging to warriors in the Pilgrimage. Its name was chosen in the hope that warriors would leave their weariness at the inn and enter the Stadium refreshed, and every manager of the inn is chosen and appointed by the Speaker's Chamber.
### ULTRA +
The Saurian Relics Association branch in Chuwen Fair is run by Siyah and sits in a corner of the fair. Its goods are not available when the Traveler first arrives because they have not yet been sorted. The Association's map text notes that its interests extend beyond saurians. Gameplay features: the Tablet of Tona takes Pyro Sigils as offerings, and a hidden area in the Night Kingdom, the Chamber of Night's Trial, copies the Stadium's layout and offers a battle against eight Nightsoul warriors once a Night Jade is obtained.

## people
### STANDARD
The Speaker's Chamber, the governing body of Natlan, sits in the Stadium. Tournament organisers, referees, engineers and warriors from all six tribes pass through the basin.
### LARGE +
The Pyro Archon Mavuika is the host behind the Pilgrimage: the Weary Inn's free food and lodging for contestants is provided courtesy of the Pyro Archon. The tribes' playable warriors, such as Kinich, Mualani and Kachina, appear here in the story of Chapter V. The Weary Inn's staff member Vitusov is its listed resident NPC.

## culture
### STANDARD
The Basin's identity is the Pilgrimage of the Return of the Sacred Flame, a national combat tournament held at the Stadium. Official site text sums up the spirit of the place: in the name of victory and honour the fires of conflict are lit, and the fervour of all souls becomes fuel for the everlasting Sacred Flame.
### LARGE +
Before the end of the Night Warden Wars, the Pilgrimage produced Contending Fire from fights between Natlanese, which fuelled the Sacred Flame, and chose the strongest warriors for the wars and, in time, the next Pyro Archon. Outlanders were barred because only Natlanese combat makes Contending Fire. The tournament has two phases: team contests across the countryside after a balloon drop, then one-on-one matches in the Stadium. Registration is done at the Stadium, and the Weary Inn feeds contestants the night before.

## history
@version: 5.0
### STANDARD
Chapter V of the Archon Quest begins with a Pilgrimage held at the Stadium. After it, Kachina does not come back from the Night Warden War as the Ode of Resurrection should have restored her, and Mavuika suspends the Pilgrimage and the Wars while she investigates. The Archon Quest Echoes of Life, in the Black Stone Under a White Stone act, begins at the Speaker's Chamber.
### LARGE +
Natlan/History records that in 5.1 a nation-wide Abyssal invasion cost thousands of lives and temporarily extinguished the Sacred Flame; the Night Warden Wars ended for good in 5.3. Afterwards, Mavuika repurposed the Pilgrimage as a purely athletic tournament and asked the Traveler to serve as a special supervisor, which meant the Traveler could not compete. The first winner of the new Pilgrimage was Kinich.
### ULTRA +
With the Abyss gone and the Pilgrimage retooled as a sports contest, outsiders are able to take part. The Stadium region is the start point of the 5.3 story quests A New Pilgrimage and After the Peace (Sol Invictus chapter, As the Blazing Sun).

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Heat, smoke from Huitztli Hill and the steady glow of the Sacred Flame. Warriors practise, fairgoers eat and trade at Chuwen Fair, and the air is competitive but friendly.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Wild saurians (yumkasaurs and tepetlisaurs), hilichurls, Forged Sand fighters and swiftstep couriers are the enemies listed for the hills, and Huitztli Hill is hot enough to trouble even tribespeople who handle Phlogiston. A boss, the Goldflame Qucusaur Tyrant, lives there.
### LARGE +
Official text says the reclusive and unusual qucusaur has chosen the hill to live on perhaps precisely because the heat keeps people away.

## travel
### LARGE
The Stadium is the hub for travel between the tribes. The Pilgrimage's balloons carry teams out across the region; the Weary Inn is a place to rest.

## quests
@version: 5.0
### LARGE
Story and Archon quests tied to the basin include the Archon Quest Pilgrimage of the Return of the Sacred Flame (5.0), Natlan! A New Adventure, Echoes of Life (Speaker's Chamber), and the Sol Invictus chapter's A New Pilgrimage and After the Peace (5.3).

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the basin is at peace. The Stadium hosts the Pilgrimage as a sports competition.
### STANDARD +
The Sacred Flame was temporarily extinguished during the 5.1 invasion; the Abyss has since been purged from the Night Kingdom and the Stadium no longer sends warriors into war.
### ULTRA +
The wiki lists no changes to the basin in 6.x or 7.x.

## entry: Stadium of the Sacred Flame
@keys: Stadium of the Sacred Flame, Chuwen Fair, Temple of Pax, Warrior's Rest Area, Stele of the Sacred Flame
@version: 5.0
### STANDARD
The Stadium of the Sacred Flame is the core landmark of Natlan and the site of the Pilgrimage of the Return of the Sacred Flame, in which members of the six tribes compete. A continually burning fire, the Sacred Flame, stands in it. The Stadium has three connected districts: Chuwen Fair, the Temple of Pax, and the Warrior's Rest Area.

## entry: Huitztli Hill
@keys: Huitztli Hill, Smoke Ridge, Goldflame Qucusaur Tyrant
@version: 5.0
### STANDARD
A mountain north of the Stadium. Locals call it Smoke Ridge because magma streams from the Great Volcano of Tollan flow through it and send smoke up between its valleys. The heat is too much even for tribespeople used to Phlogiston, which may be why a reclusive Qucusaur lives there.
