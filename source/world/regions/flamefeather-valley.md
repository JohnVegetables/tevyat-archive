## summary
### LITE
Flamefeather Valley is a strange valley west of Snezhnograd that was once home to humans. Now almost nothing remains but a small town that turns up in ghost stories. It is named for the tall trees with leaves like flaming feathers, and for the bird-shaped monsters with crimson pinions that survivors report. Many curious explorers have gone in and never come back, though no official order forbids it.
### STANDARD +
The town is Okurov, a warm, abandoned village whose past is tied to the experiments of the bio-alchemist Koshchei. In the 7.0 world quests the Traveler learns what happened there.

## geography
### STANDARD
A valley with an unnaturally warm microclimate compared with the rest of Snezhnaya, filled with tall trees whose crimson leaves look like flaming feathers. A train tunnel, caved in at the entrance, leads to Okurov, and a flooded drainage sewer lies beneath the village.
### LARGE +
Okurov has an assembly hall (with a "Hall of Prayers"), a tall building with an elevator and Nastya's home. Local specialty: Golden Fern. Enemies include chimeric armored beasts and winged elephants, Rugged Elder Leshy and Juvenile Tree Segment elites.

## places
### STANDARD
Okurov: abandoned village in a warm climate, with a dark history. Teeming Mire: the area where the guardian Zhara is finally fought. Nastya's home and the assembly hall: key buildings in Okurov.

## people
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
By the time the Traveler arrives, only a few remain: the girl Nastya, the woman Furiosa, and a handful of Leshy (tree-like beings who were once townsfolk), among them Aunt LePuy and "No-Nose" Ove. Zhara watches over the valley, and a Volkodlak gang led by "The Anvil" has been looting and kidnapping.
### LARGE +
The villagers had been persuaded that becoming trees and joining "one family" would end pain. The experiment's mastermind posed as a healer called Doctor Moreau or "Doctor Birdie", but was in fact Koshchei the Deathless.

## history
@version: 7.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Okurov was once a village of ordinary people. Koshchei, a bio-alchemist who had worked in the Belyi Tsar's Hvergelmir institute, used it to try to create a "Perfect Tree" through a final ceremony that would turn everyone into embryos of a new, emotionless existence. Those who took part dissolved into blue algae, including Koshchei himself. After the Cataclysm the town stayed sparsely populated, with a few Leshy, Furiosa and Nastya remaining.
### LARGE +
Furiosa and Ove came to see Koshchei's real intent and tried to keep other villagers from entering the assembly hall; Zhara, a winged Immortal-Construct-like guardian, kept out any "Badbugs", her word for outsiders, for several centuries. The Eye of Graeae said one fragment of its power lay in the valley.
### ULTRA +
In the final quest the Traveler defeats Zhara in the Teeming Mire; Nastya joins the Perfect Tree; and Aksinya adjusts Zhara's inert body so she cannot revive and dissolves.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Warm, humid and eerie: neat crimson foliage over silent houses, lullaby-like talk of "family", and a child's cheerful games that turn out to be about burning outsiders.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Zhara, mindless Leshy, Volkodlak bandits, chimeric beasts and traps inside the assembly hall. In Okurov the game switches to a third-person shooter mode. Locals and rumour say nobody who enters Okurov returns.

## travel
### STANDARD
The quest chain starts at Morepesok's train station and passes through a train tunnel. Okurov itself unlocks by progressing the world quest "Transplanted From Yesterday to Tomorrow".

## quests
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
The world quest series "In the Dwelling of Life": A Sprout Without a Gardener, Transplanted From "Yesterday" to "Tomorrow", Down in the Dumps, Back on One's Feet, Forbidding Doors of Melancholy, To Rest in a Forgotten Field and Chorus of Solace. It begins in Morepesok and follows Nastya's escort home.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the valley is quiet: Zhara and the Perfect Tree experiment are finished, and the village is empty.

## entry: Okurov
@keys: Okurov, Hall of Prayers
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
Okurov is an abandoned village in a warm climate with a dark history. A newspaper in the train tunnel praises its weather, its excellent doctor and its welcome to travellers. In the story its residents were persuaded to grow branches and leaves and call themselves one family, believing it would heal their sorrows.
### LARGE +
The village's name echoes a 1908 Russian novel. Its assembly hall holds a relief of Zhara, an elevator and a final "home" chamber with records revealing the experiment.

## entry: Zhara
@keys: Zhara
@keys2: Okurov, Flamefeather Valley
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
Zhara is the guardian of Okurov, a huge winged creature with leaf-feathers who attacks the Traveler and Paimon as supposed Badbugs. Her name is the Russian word for "heat" and her look nods to the Firebird of Russian fairy tales. She is a variant of the Immortal Construct, with a humanoid upper body and long blonde hair.

## entry: Nastya
@keys: Nastya
@keys2: Okurov, Zhara, Koshchei
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
Nastya is a young girl found unconscious with tree branches growing from her head, escorted home by the Traveler. She admires Furiosa and Doctor Moreau, believes herself the cleverest child in town and cheerfully talks about catching Badbugs. The truth about her home slowly emerges during the quests.
