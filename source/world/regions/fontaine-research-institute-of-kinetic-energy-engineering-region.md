## summary
### LITE
This is the area where the Fontaine Research Institute of Kinetic Energy Engineering stands. Years ago the Institute's central laboratory was largely destroyed in an explosion linked to Edwin Eastinghouse's research into Arkhium, leaving huge Allogravity-Condensed Water Bodies floating in the air. The organisation now works from a nearby New Fontaine Research Institute.
### STANDARD +
The region's boss is the Experimental Field Generator. The map blurb 'Variation of Debris and Metal' describes a pursuit of salvation that suddenly stopped, and an adventurer who never rests whether the destination is a castle in the sky or a deep-sea canyon.

## geography
### STANDARD
The region lies on the northern side of the island of Erinnyes, next to Erinnyes Forest. It has the old Central Laboratory ruins, the New Institute and a scatter of former institute buildings: dormitories, an Institute of Clockwork Applications, an Executive Office, a Materials Office, an Academic Assembly Hall and a Game Venue. Dryas Wood is a small hollow between the Institute and Erinnyes Forest.
### LARGE +
Floating Allogravity-Condensed Water Bodies hang above the ruins. Bossuet's Abandoned Laboratory, Bravais' Hidden Study, a Secret Vault and Kuisel's Clockwork Workshop are marked, and a Mary-Ann Memorial Park lies in the area.
### ULTRA +
Texts found around the area include notices from the Executive Office (a personnel-change announcement and a finance investigation notice), Materials Office memos, dormitory research notes, a paper-usage initiative and book-donation statistics at the New Institute, and the Research Notes of Master Ruggiero in Bravais' Hidden Study, which is one of the texts cited for the claim that 'Petrichor' means 'golem' in an ancient language.

## places
### STANDARD
- **New Fontaine Research Institute**: the Institute's current seat since the explosion. It was formerly known as the Alain Guillotin Research Institute and hosted weekly lectures on critical projects.
- **Central Laboratory Ruins**: the wrecked heart of the old Institute.
- **Dryas Wood**: the Institute's old 'backyard' of plant nurseries; the potioneer who made 'Pure Water' is said to have lived in seclusion here.
### LARGE +
- A stone tablet names the ancient Marechaussee Hunters and their deeds, which are unknown to the world.
- Notable item collectibles are the Cipher Letters (collecting all grants an achievement), the New Tide Anthology and the Initial Skill Sample No. 2 Development Plan, the last found in a hilichurl camp on the east edge.

## people
### STANDARD
Researchers, technicians and administrators of the Institute. According to the faction page, the co-administrators Raimondo and Choiseul now run it and Augustus Lovelace says it was a paradise for researchers before they took over. Edwin Eastinghouse, a Senior Technician, made the Prototype Cal. Breguet series of Clockwork Meka.

## culture
### STANDARD
The Institute studies machines and energy; many inventions of Fontaine, including clockwork mechanisms and kinetic cores by founder Alain Guillotin, come from it. Unlike the Sumeru Akademiya it does not educate or administer the public. It is controversial: Elphane finds its technicians' lectures ghastly, and the Mekafish it built to clean the water ended up killing fish, which Linorea resents.

## history
### STANDARD
One of the Institute's main aims was to find answers to the prophesied flood, and researchers believed harnessing Arkhium to defy gravity was the key. Eastinghouse's field generator overloaded, destroying most of the Institute and hurling blocks of water and ruins into the sky. After this the Institute rebuilt nearby and the Callas Line aquabus route was shut down.
### LARGE +
The Poisson Tunnel was dug earlier by Institute machines. After the explosion the new Institute suffers money troubles; Salsa mentions plans for a 'Ruins Tour'. The quest Fontaine Research Institute, Stagnating in the Rubble sees another explosion in the Central Laboratory ruins that scatters components of the Arkhium inhibitor.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Wrecked laboratories with huge blocks of water hanging overhead. The quest describes dealing with the new Institute's administration as a long, mind-numbing procedure.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Fatui Skirmishers, Clockwork Meka, Fontemer Aberrants, hilichurl camps, Frost and Wind Operatives and the Experimental Field Generator. In the quest, a second explosion scatters components of the large Arkhium inhibitor into the waters around the ruins.

## travel
### STANDARD
The region adjoins Erinnyes Forest and Dryas Wood. The Callas Line aquabus route was shut after the explosion.

## quests
### STANDARD
World quests include Fontaine Research Institute, Stagnating in the Rubble (the start of the Fontaine Research Institute Chronicles), In Search of Lost Time, Villains, Ardent Calibration, Buried Honor, Road to the Singularity, Science of Data Management and Treacherous Light of the Depths.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the old laboratories remain in ruins and the Institute operates from the New location.
### STANDARD +
No newer content than 4.1 was found for this area.
