## summary
### LITE
Bishui Plain is the broad, river-watered plain of northern Liyue, named for the Bishui River that runs through it ("Blue Water Plain"). It is the first stretch of Liyue most travelers from Mondstadt cross: the road runs through Dihua Marsh, past Wangshu Inn and the Stone Gate crossroads, and on south to Liyue Harbor. In its hills sit the terraced village of Qingce, the haunted Wuwang Hill and the sealed salt-god ruin of Sal Terrae.
### STANDARD +
The plain mixes the everyday and the uncanny: farmers, merchants and Millelith patrols use its roads, while the same ground holds a serpent god's old seal at Qingce, a hill under permanent gloom and a vanished civilisation's city.

## geography
### STANDARD
The Bishui River flows down from Yilong Wharf through Chenyu Vale, drops over a waterfall into central Liyue and widens in Dihua Marsh, where it splits: one arm ends in the lake near Dawn Winery, the other runs through Qiongji Estuary into the Sea of Clouds. Qingce Village sits at the foot of Mt. Qingce in the northernmost hills.
### LARGE +
Dihua Marsh is an alluvial plain south of the Stone Gate and north of Wangshu Inn, with Sal Terrae to its east; its name comes from dihua, the Amur silvergrass in its shallows. From the top of Wangshu Inn, weather permitting, Mt. Qingce and the cloud-ringed peaks of Jueyun Karst can be seen.

## places
### STANDARD
Stone Gate is the crossroads where roads from Dawn Winery (northeast), Wuwang Hill and Qingce (northwest), and Dihua Marsh and Liyue Harbor (south) meet. Dihua Marsh holds a Statue of the Seven on the main road. Wangshu Inn is a tall inn on a stone pillar. Qingce Village is a quiet wooden village with blue roofs. Wuwang Hill lies under grey skies. Sal Terrae is a sealed ruin.
### LARGE +
Qingce's community center is the seat of its elder Granny Ruoxin; the mill is Ms. Bai's, the tea shop Pops Kai's and the Fanmu Carpenter shop Master Lu's. The Vault of Qingce lies behind a waterfall north of the village. Beneath Wuwang Hill is the Hidden Palace of Zhou Formula. Stone Gate has a small cluster of vendors, including Pops Zhou.

## people
### LARGE
Mostly ordinary Liyue folk: farmers, innkeepers, traveling merchants, sailors heading for the harbor, Millelith guards and the elderly of Qingce, whose younger residents have often left for livelier places. Wangshu Inn is run by Verr Goldet and her husband Huai'an, secretly Liyue Qixing agents under Ningguang; the cook Smiley Yanxiao works there too.

## governance
### LARGE
No separate government: the plain falls under the Liyue Qixing and the Millelith who keep the roads. Qingce has a community leader, Granny Ruoxin.

## culture
### LARGE
Travelers use the Bishui River as a landmark and guide, and goods have been carried along it for generations. Wangshu Inn is the usual place to trade and hear stories; its name echoes the legendary coachwoman who drives the moon's chariot. Qingce keeps the memory of the Chi in its Geo statues and amber.
### ULTRA +
Folk stories say even the adepti sometimes bask in the moonlight at Wangshu Inn, and that it is a haven for lovers' moonlit meetings.

## history
### STANDARD
Long ago the serpent god Chi nested at what is now Mt. Qingce until Morax slew it and bound its power. Havria, God of Salt, held out at Sal Terrae through the Archon War until her own people killed her. Wangshu Inn already stood in the years after the Cataclysm. Over a decade ago a failed rite at Wuwang Hill killed Hu Tao's father and cost Old Hu much of his life.
### LARGE +
The Bishui River features in two legends: that it formed from the blood of the adeptus Skybracer, who propped up Mt. Tianheng with his antlers and fought on until he died; and that an adeptus once brewed tea with half the river and poured it back.
### ULTRA +
Qingce's inscription says the Chi's spirit was bound in the north, its bones pinned south-east, its flesh imprisoned north-west, its soul fettered north-east and its form crushed south-west.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Open skies, reed beds, croaking frogs and the creak of caravan wheels; moonlit evenings at Wangshu Inn; terraced paddies at Qingce whose ripples shimmer in several colors. Wuwang Hill is the exception: grey, fogged and quiet, with blue lights among the trees.

## dangers
### LARGE
Hilichurls, slimes and Treasure Hoarders roam the plain; Fatui skirmishers and Abyss mages have been met around Stone Gate; the Rhodeia of Loch haunts the wetlands. Wuwang Hill's ley lines are said to replay the hopes of its dead in elemental form, which can lure travelers to harm. Sal Terrae shelters Stonehide Lawachurl, and Ruin Hunters guard the ruin near Qingce.

## travel
### STANDARD
The plain is the main northern entrance to Liyue from Mondstadt: from Dawn Winery and Stone Gate down through Dihua Marsh to Liyue Harbor, with Wangshu Inn as the last comfortable stop. The river is also a working waterway.

## quests
### LARGE
The plain is thick with world quests, including The Chi of Yore at Qingce, Test of Courage on Wuwang Hill, and Zhongli's story quest Sal Flore at Sal Terrae. The wiki cites the quest Equilibrium for the Wangshu Inn keepers' secret role.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the plain is quiet working country along Liyue's northern road.
### STANDARD +
No change specific to Bishui Plain is recorded in 6.x or 7.0-7.1 content. The river still carries trade, Qingce is still a retirement-leaning village, and Wuwang Hill remains fog-bound.

## entry: The Chi of Qingce
@keys: Chi, Qingce
@keys2: Morax, Rex Lapis, Mt. Qingce, Vault of Qingce, Bishui Plain
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
The Chi was a serpent god that nested at Mt. Qingce and took the region. Morax fought and killed it at a great height, but its power kept seeping into the land, so he conquered each of its aspects in turn and taught Qingce's people to carve Geo statues to crush what remained. Adeptus amber around the village does the same. Legend says the Chi's body became Mt. Qingce and its blood fed the Blood-Fed Trees, but Zhongli points out the mountain predates the beast. The Vault of Qingce, behind a waterfall, is rumored to hold the Chi's remains; its large amber crystals are said to be congealed Chi blood.

## entry: Wuwang Hill Disaster
@keys: Wuwang Hill Disaster, Wuwang Hill
@keys2: Hu Tao, Old Hu, fetor, Art of Homa, Wangsheng Funeral Parlor
@version: 1.0
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Wuwang Hill was an ordinary hill until a calamity over a decade ago. Fetor leaking from the boundary between life and death threatened the area, so Old Hu and Hu Tao's father performed the Art of Homa to burn it away. The younger man did not perform the rite correctly (Hu Tao later attributes it to attachments that pulled his focus away) and died from the fetor; a flood devastated a nearby village. Old Hu managed the fallout at great cost to his lifespan and died before sixty. Today an eerie fog and drifting blue lights remain and the hill is a dare-spot for tests of courage. A Hidden Palace beneath it holds an evil chi sealed long ago with the Seven-and-Eight-Gates Method.
### LARGE +
Qingce's children tell a more fanciful version, in which a whale-song-singing sea monster lured Wuwang's young people into the Bishui River year after year until only the elderly remained. The ley lines are said to have absorbed the dead villagers' hopes and recreated them in elemental form.

## entry: Wangshu Inn
@keys: Wangshu Inn, Verr Goldet, Huai'an
@keys2: Liyue, Ningguang, Xiao, Qixing
@version: 1.0
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Wangshu Inn is a tall, many-storied inn on a stone pillar at the south end of Dihua Marsh, where traveling merchants trade and stay. Zhongli says it was built tall for visibility and is a strategic point that must be defended. It already stood in the years after the Cataclysm. The keepers, Verr Goldet and Huai'an, are in fact Liyue Qixing agents who report directly to Ningguang, and the commercial inn is a cover for quietly supporting Xiao as he excises plagues. Stall-keepers whisper that the tower hides a secret, but none has proved it.

## entry: Sal Terrae and the God of Salt
@keys: Sal Terrae, Havria, God of Salt
@keys2: Zhongli, Liyue, Archon War, Yinyuan Hall
@version: 1.0
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Sal Terrae ("salt of the earth") was founded by Havria, a gentle and relatively weak god who refused to join the Archon War and made concession after concession until only a tiny plot was left. According to the quest-confirmed account, her people's first and last king killed her to show that kindness would not save them, and the blast of her death turned everyone nearby to salt. Some who lived outside the blast fled to Morax's harbor. Havria herself owned a salt chalice that refilled itself and a ruler that could turn the ground to salt. The civilisation's legacy is Yinyuan Hall. A seal still guards the ruin, and in Zhongli's story quest the Traveler helps open it.
