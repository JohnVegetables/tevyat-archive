## summary
### LITE
Voidsea Outlook is the area at the southern end of Pohjola, the large northern island of Nod-Krai, north of Paha Isle (added in 6.3). It holds Piramida, the mountain city that is the headquarters of the Lightkeepers, and Dreadshade Mire, a fog-choked swamp where an ancient Abyssal evil is sealed behind three stone pillars.
### STANDARD +
Together with the Southern Isles, Piramida is one of the most populated places in Nod-Krai. The area mixes a living, watchful military city with a hazardous swamp the Lightkeepers have posted warnings around.

## geography
### STANDARD
Voidsea Outlook lies at Pohjola's southern edge, facing the Southern Isles across the water. Piramida stands on a mountain around a huge lighthouse; Dreadshade Mire spreads as a swamp whose thick fog limits visibility and is edged with warning signboards.
### LARGE +
Local specialty: Winter Icelea. The swamp has wild plants (Cottonspring Flowers, Glimmerfruit, Mandragora, Spirebloom) and wildlife (Slothsheep). Puzzles here include Abyssal Fog Barriers, Lunar Conflux Barriers and Overflowing Abyssal Power.

## places
### STANDARD
Piramida: a city built atop an ancient castle; Lightkeeper headquarters. Dreadshade Mire: a fog-shrouded swamp, the prison of the Serpent of All Evils.
### LARGE +
In Piramida: the Torchforger Statue, honouring Solovei the Torchforger, the founding Starshyna; a grand mead hall at the top, with a long table for feasts after victories; and a bulletin board for Ratniki notices. A note headed 'Prospector's Command' found in the swamp tells its reader to find new sealing pillars, observe whether the kuuvahki there behave unusually, gather data and avoid alerting the Lightkeepers; its author is not named in the sources read.

## people
### STANDARD
The Lightkeepers: volunteer soldiers called Ratniki under squad leaders, sergeant majors and the Starshyna (currently Nikita), who is based in Piramida. In the swamp, Lightkeeper observers such as Janusz and Oskari appear in the world quest.
### LARGE +
Beryna, an open-world NPC, stands beside an oven in the area. After the hidden exploration objective A Memory of Warmth, she grants the recipe for Mushroom Phantasm (a dish that needs mushrooms, rye flour, onions and Midsommar Berries).

## governance
### STANDARD
Piramida is the seat of the Lightkeepers, an order founded 500 years ago by royal edict of Anastasya as an autonomous military body within Nod-Krai. Command runs from the Starshyna through Sergeant Majors to Squad Leaders and Ratniki. Because the order is stretched thin, outsiders sometimes view its headquarters as aloof, and relaying information to it is said to be hard.
### LARGE +
Members swear an oath when they join, promising to use their bones and blood as fuel so that life may be made greater than death. Every Ratnik carries an Oath Lantern that lets other Lightkeepers and the Starshyna detect them.

## culture
### STANDARD
Lightkeeper culture centres on the vow to keep a light burning in the long night: a nightingale holding a burning wick is their traditional insignia. Banquets in the mead hall celebrate victories and honour the fallen; by long custom the first pour of the feast is offered to the dead.
### LARGE +
The banquet form comes from the old Snezhnayan court but has changed beyond recognition. In-game character interactions (Flins, Illuga) show how generations of members regard the statue and the feasts; Illuga recalls helping in the kitchens as a youth, before he joined, and not understanding why older members wept after a few drinks.

## history
### STANDARD
Piramida was built atop the ancient ruins of Prokhor Trubetskoy's castle, with a giant golden-age lighthouse at its centre. The Lightkeepers have made it their headquarters since their founding by Solovei. Dreadshade Mire is where Solovei and Aila, the first Moonchanter of the Frostmoon Scions, imprisoned the Serpent of All Evils with three ancient stone seals.
### LARGE +
The Serpent was once Raimo, a rare human high official in the days of the Belyi Tsar and a friend and disciple of Rotwang. On Rotwang's behalf he used coercion, entrapment and murder against his master's rivals. After Rotwang's defeat he became the Serpent and was sealed in the mire. The three pillars each carry an inscription naming one of his sins: using relatives as hostages in political schemes, spreading slander to exploit the nobility's arrogance, and pushing others to a point of no return.
### ULTRA +
In the present, Fatui activity weakened the seals; the world quest To Turn Each Sin Against the Sinner has the Traveler repurify the pillars before facing the Serpent. The Fatui once marched to Piramida's gates as reinforcements when the Lightkeepers were about to face the Wild Hunt.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Piramida: a tiered mountain city under a vast sky, the beacon of the lighthouse, long tables and the statue overhead; a feeling of old duty and fellowship. Dreadshade Mire: grey fog, dead water, glowing sealing pillars and the sense of being watched.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Dreadshade Mire is marked by Lightkeeper notice signs as extremely dangerous; entry is discouraged because of low visibility and roaming Wild Hunt creatures. Elite enemies include Fishers of Hidden Depths, Wilderness Exiles and Radiant Glacial Wolves; pale-furred wolves and Treasure Hoarders are the ordinary threats.
### LARGE +
The Serpent of All Evils summons Abyssal enemies, including Evil Eyes of Blightseep, Wasteland Wild Hunt enemies and Fishers of Hidden Depths, during its boss fight.

## travel
### LARGE
South: Paha Isle across the water. North: Wavechaser Plain and then Ashveil Peak. Piramida has a Meeting Point and bulletin boards; teleport waypoints connect the area to the rest of Nod-Krai.

## quests
### STANDARD
To Turn Each Sin Against the Sinner (world quest, 6.3): in Dreadshade Mire the Traveler investigates three sealing pillars and defeats the Serpent of All Evils. Nightingale's Song, the Nod-Krai quest series, also uses Piramida.
### LARGE +
Before the quest the pillars are cleared of Wild Hunt enemies and Abyssal riftlines; absorbing spirebloom in the final fight grants a light-blade charge that can strike Abyssal enemies hard. At the end of the later quest The Last Night, the First Light, the Serpent of All Evils joins the Ultimate Calamity in the final fight against the Traveler and Illuga.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the area is open; the Serpent of All Evils has been defeated in the world quest, and Piramida remains the Lightkeepers' stronghold.
### STANDARD +
After Nightingale's Song, Piramida appears in a different state from before. Nothing in 7.0-7.1 is recorded as changing the area.

## entry: Piramida
@keys: Piramida, Torchforger Statue
@version: 6.3
### STANDARD
Piramida is the city of the Lightkeepers, built on a mountain around a giant golden-age lighthouse. Its namecard line reads that in the blackest of nights, a lamp shall rise. A statue of Solovei the Torchforger stands in it; a mead hall at its top holds the Lightkeepers' feasts.
### LARGE +
By the testimony of Nikita it stands on the ruins of Prokhor Trubetskoy's castle. Its appearance differs before and after the quest Nightingale's Song.

## entry: Dreadshade Mire
@keys: Dreadshade Mire, Sealing Pillar
@keys2: Serpent of All Evils, Solovei, Aila, Voidsea Outlook
@version: 6.3
### STANDARD
Dreadshade Mire is a fog-shrouded swamp where Solovei, the Torchforger, and Aila, first Moonchanter of the Frostmoon Scions, sealed the Serpent of All Evils with three ancient stone seals. Lightkeeper signboards warn that entry is not advised.
### LARGE +
Each pillar carries ancient incantations that list one of the Serpent's three great sins and bind it to stone and spell. Wild Hunt creatures patrol the mire.
