## summary
### LITE
Fellfrost Peak is a giant snow-sloped mountain south of Snezhnograd. On its summit stands the House of Hesperides, the mountain manor of the Snegovik and their Grand Duchess, said to own the whole mountain. A Prime Ice mining town lies at its foot and the fae Jack Frosts keep a hidden village on its slopes. Blizzards and a ban on unauthorised hiking keep visitors away.
### STANDARD +
The Grand Duchess Pavlina held the mountain but seldom appeared in public. In the 7.0 world quests the Traveler climbs to the manor and becomes entangled in a doomed series of wedding rituals there.

## geography
### STANDARD
A giant, snow-sloped mountain with fierce storms and extreme cold. A notice board on the path warns that the House of Hesperides is closed to unauthorised entry and that hiking is strictly prohibited because of the snowstorm. Caves along the route offer shelter from the weather. Local specialties are Flockingweed and Frostfairy Flower.
### LARGE +
Notable sites: the House of Hesperides at the summit with its chapel, Jack Frost Village (a hidden fae settlement on the mountain) and Tidesong Cavern, a secluded cave used by the fae Vodyanitsa as her private practice space. A boss fight with a Guardian Blade of Drifting Snow takes place on the slopes, and elite enemies include Volkodlak Lobbers, Raiders and Sharpshooters.

## places
### STANDARD
House of Hesperides: mansion on the summit, home of the Snegovik and their Grand Duchess. Jack Frost Village: hidden fae homeland, besieged by Volkodlak and relieved by the Traveler. Tidesong Cavern: Vodyanitsa's hidden practice cave. Svetloledovka: the mining town at the mountain's foot (placement disputed, see uncertainties).
### LARGE +
The manor has an upper floor of documents, a rooftop and a chapel where the wedding rituals take place. Prime Ice and Kresnik Kristall can be gathered around the House of Hesperides. Jack Frost Village is the former home of the Geo Jack Frost Lumi, where Linnea first met her.

## people
### STANDARD
The Snegovik, whose leaders are called Snegurochka, live in the House of Hesperides; Pavlina was their Grand Duchess. Jack Frosts are small fae. The Volkodlak are hostile raiders on the slopes.
### LARGE +
A powerful Snegurochka knight once served Pavlina and parted ways with her after she became Grand Duchess because their paths no longer matched. Pavlina earlier kept a cat named Lara at her laboratory in the Pale Crown Palace, from her time as a researcher of the Hvergelmir institute.

## history
@version: 7.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The mountain is the Snegurochka's domain. A fragment of the Eye of Graeae's lost power lay in the House of Hesperides, which is why the Traveler and the Eye came here. Pavlina had been Grand Duchess since before the Cataclysm, five hundred years ago.
### LARGE +
Pavlina was an alchemist and, under the Belyi Tsar, a researcher at the Hvergelmir institute together with Koshchei, Alvis and Aksinya. After Aksinya's plan was thwarted and she was cast into the Shadow Realm, the Eye of Graeae was split into three. Pavlina sought to use its power to give the Snegovik complete souls and began performing wedding rituals with Ikhor, a being formed from one of the fragments.
### ULTRA +
Pavlina's own papers recall her rival Larisa, a candidate for Grand Duchess whose human fiance fled in the middle of the wedding ritual, causing her death; Pavlina later cited Larisa's fate to justify sacrificing Ikhor's power. Because the Snegovik hold that a leader must have a complete soul, gained only through true love, the rituals repeated until Pavlina lost sight of the fact that Ikhor had helped her willingly, and shackled him. A traveller's notes on the rooftop describe the same wedding day looping over and over.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Blizzards that plaster snow over a climber's face, stinging cold, and a lone lamp blessed by a fae that makes the storm ease around it. The summit manor is formal, brittle and haunted by ritual.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Whiteouts and extreme cold, starvation for the lost, Volkodlak war bands, winged knight constructs, glacial wardens and glaciomancers, chimeric beasts and the Guardian Blade of Drifting Snow boss.

## quests
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
The world quest series "Hesperides of Love and Hate" (Through the Gates of Ivory; Procrustes' Iron Bed with its parts The Sun's Gilded Apple and House of the Soulless; Bell Ringing at Dusk) is set here. A separate quest, "Small Jack Frost, Big Problems", defends Jack Frost Village from Volkodlak. Tidesong Cavern's dialogue opens after "Like a Land Where the Tide's Song Whispers".

## status
@spoiler: major
### LITE
As of 7.1 the mountain is open to explore, but its Grand Duchess Pavlina has died.
### STANDARD +
Pavlina was defeated at the Cloudborne site by Aksinya and the Traveler after taking Ikhor's power, and disintegrated as the last ritual finished, leaving a fragment of the Eye of Graeae's power. Who leads the Snegovik now is not stated.

## entry: House of Hesperides
@keys: House of Hesperides, Hesperides, Snegurochka, Grand Duchess
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
The House of Hesperides is the mountain manor at the top of Fellfrost Peak where the Snegovik and their Grand Duchess live. It contains a chapel, a mansion with an upper floor of documents and a rooftop, and Prime Ice artisanry. In the quest "Through the Gates of Ivory" a lamp blessed long ago by a fae eases the cold on the climb.
### LARGE +
Pavlina's journal says the Snegovik follow the ancestor Yalyane Morevna's decree that their leader must have a complete soul, found through true love, and that an ancient Snegurochka told Pavlina that the throne sometimes passes without it.

## entry: Jack Frost Village
@keys: Jack Frost Village, Fae's Hidden Settlement, Jack Frost Village Head
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
Jack Frost Village is the hidden homeland of the Jack Frosts on Fellfrost Peak, recently besieged by Volkodlak until the Traveler fended them off in "Small Jack Frost, Big Problems". It is also the former home of the Geo Jack Frost Lumi, where Linnea first met her. The quest unlocks the Jack Frosts' Goodies Shop, and a discarded cardboard Jack Frost face in a broken igloo seems to have been used as a disguise by some creature.

## entry: Tidesong Cavern
@keys: Tidesong Cavern, Cavern of Melodic Tides
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
Tidesong Cavern is a secluded cave on Fellfrost Peak that the fae Vodyanitsa uses as her personal practice space, and the site of a Meeting Point of the same name. Dialogue there unlocks after the quest "Like a Land Where the Tide's Song Whispers" and involves Vodyanitsa with Odette, Alyosha and the Traveler.
