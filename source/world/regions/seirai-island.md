## summary
### LITE
Seirai Island is a broken, storm-wracked island of Inazuma. About 1,500 years ago the Thunderbird, Kanna Kapatcir, ravaged it until the Electro Archon killed her on Amakumo Peak, and wards held back the storm her death left. About 500 years ago a shrine maiden broke those wards to help the pirate Ako Domeki rebel against the Shogunate. A huge Electro storm then engulfed the island's centre, shattered the land and drove its people away for good. Today only Asase Shrine stands intact, kept by a talking cat called Neko who still waits for her mistress to return.
### STANDARD +
The Traveler recently resealed the storm with the help of two Adventurers' Guild members.

## geography
### STANDARD
Seirai is a cluster of fragmented landmasses around a central basin. Until recently a perpetual thunderstorm filled that basin, with Balethunder and lightning striking anyone who entered. Amakumo Peak rises inland above a lake, with flooded underground ruins below it. Koseki Village, the abandoned fishing village, lies on the coast, as do the wreck of the flagship Seiraimaru and the ruins of Fort Hiraumi. Asase Shrine sits apart with fair weather of its own.
### LARGE +
Even after the storm ended, Electrified Water remained at the island's centre until a nearby puzzle drained it, revealing a large hole. Floating Warding Stones once hung in the sky above the island.

## places
### STANDARD
- **Asase Shrine**: built to manage the wards over the Thunderbird's lingering hatred; the only place the storm left untouched.
- **Koseki Village**: the island's former fishing village, now silent; its people helped keep the shrine and made offerings there.
- **Amakumo Peak**: where Ei slew Kanna Kapatcir and where the Thunder Manifestation later appeared.
- **"Seiraimaru"**: the stranded wreck of Ako Domeki's flagship.
- **Fort Hiraumi**: ruins of the city of Hiraumi, destroyed by the Thunderbird.
### LARGE +
- **Slumbering Court**: a domain at Fort Hiraumi.
- Amakumo Peak's drained lake opens into a maze of submerged ruins guarded by Ruin Sentinels.

## people
### STANDARD
Since the evacuation Seirai has no settled human population. Neko, a cat who can speak and no longer ages, calls herself the shrine's "Provisional Head Priestess" and watches over generations of shrine cats. The cats are named after "good things", and the names pass down the generations. The Adventurers' Guild members Etsuko and Taisuke stay at the shrine after the storm ends.
### LARGE +
After the shrine's repair, Ooshima Junpei, a craftsman left jobless when Tatarasuna was evacuated, carved a giant statue of Neko there. Konbumaru, one of the cats, is named after Takamine the Mistsplitter's nickname.

## history
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Seirai is thought to be one of the few islands that survived the great flood after the war of funerary flame. About 1,500 years ago, after destroying the tribe on Tsurumi Island, Kanna Kapatcir came to Seirai, wrecked much of it and left the city of Hiraumi in ruins. To protect her people, Ei hunted and killed the Thunderbird on Amakumo Peak. Warding Stones held back the storm left by her death, and the Asase Clan's shrine maintained the wards. About 500 years ago, a few years after the cataclysm, the pirate captain Ako Domeki led Seirai in rebellion against the Shogunate. Asase Hibiki, the shrine's head priestess, unsealed the wards with magic she had learned from the Yougou Tengu to destroy the Shogunate's ships. The storm that followed tore the island apart. The Shogunate evacuated the islanders to Narukami Island, and Hibiki never came back from the storm.
### LARGE +
Hibiki had trained at the Grand Narukami Shrine as one of the Yougou Three, and the cataclysm cost her Takamine the Mistsplitter. She agreed to help Domeki partly out of friendship and partly because he reminded her of Takamine. The storm swept Domeki's fleet far out to sea. His chief mate Janome and the ship carrying a Maguu Kenki that Domeki had captured (a detail told during the limited-time Echoing Tales event) were stranded on the Golden Apple Archipelago while protecting him. Domeki himself returned to find Seirai in ruins and sailed off to live freely and see the world as Hibiki had wished.
### ULTRA +
Neko remembers Domeki as a very tall, loud human and an undignified glutton who helped clean the shrine; he called Hibiki his "naggy, fretful aunt". It is unclear whether they were really related. The adventurers told Neko that Domeki had been a pirate with a bounty on his head, which she refused to believe.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Rain-black skies, fractured cliffs and abandoned houses, with the thunder only recently stilled. The Koseki Village viewpoint says the old laughter and cicada song have been lost to the thunder, and people now only remember that the place was abandoned to the storms. Asase Shrine, by contrast, is bright, tidy and full of cats.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Before the Traveler resealed the Warding Stones, Balethunder and targeted lightning made the centre deadly. The Thunder Manifestation, a coagulation of the Thunderbird's regret, still appears there (gameplay: a repeatable boss on a platform formed above the island). Ruin Sentinels and slimes haunt the flooded ruins under Amakumo Peak.

## culture
### LARGE
Seirai's islanders kept Asase Shrine and made offerings there, and the shrine and villagers blessed Domeki's flagship before it sailed. According to Neko, Hibiki ran the shrine, received guests and cooked for the cats, and she also gave Neko her name.

## quests
@version: 2.1
### STANDARD
In Seirai Stormchasers (2.1), sent by Katheryne, the Traveler joins the adventurers Etsuko and Taisuke. With Neko's guidance they reseal the four Warding Stones and defeat the Thunder Manifestation, which ends the storm. The Traveler also finds a Peculiar Pinion, a Thunderbird feather that matters later on Tsurumi Island. In Neko Is a Cat, the Traveler restores Asase Shrine for Neko.
### LARGE +
At the end of Through the Mists (2.2), the Traveler uses the Pinion's power to bring the spirit of Ruu from Tsurumi to Seirai. There he sings for Kapatcir once more, and the curse on Tsurumi is lifted.

## status
### STANDARD
As of 7.1 the storm is sealed but the island stays depopulated, with only adventurers passing through and Neko tending Asase Shrine. She still believes Hibiki, Domeki and Janome will come back. No Seirai-specific story content appears in 6.x-7.1.

## entry: Asase Shrine and Neko
@keys: Asase Shrine, Neko, Provisional Head Priestess
### STANDARD
Asase Shrine on Seirai Island was founded after Kanna Kapatcir's death to manage the wards over her lingering hatred, and the Asase Clan ran it. Five centuries ago its head priestess Asase Hibiki broke the wards, but the shrine itself was spared by the storm. Neko, the cat Hibiki named, somehow stopped ageing, learned to speak, and has kept the shrine ever since, calling herself its "Provisional Head Priestess". She does not know that the islanders were evacuated, or that Hibiki must have died long ago, and she hopes a restored shrine will bring visitors and Hibiki back.

## entry: Ako Domeki's Rebellion
@keys: Ako Domeki, Domeki, Seiraimaru, Seirai Rebellion
@spoiler: minor
### LARGE
About five hundred years ago, the famed bandit and pirate Ako Domeki Zaemon of Seirai rose against the Shogunate with a fleet of over ten ships led by the flagship Seiraimaru. According to the limited-time Echoing Tales event, he even captured a Maguu Kenki puppet in battle. His friend Asase Hibiki unsealed the Thunderbird's wards to sink the Shogunate's ships. The storm destroyed them, but it also tore Seirai apart and scattered Domeki's own fleet. The Seiraimaru's wreck still lies on the island. Domeki was the original wielder of the polearm "The Catch" and owned the item known as Ako's Sake Vessel.
