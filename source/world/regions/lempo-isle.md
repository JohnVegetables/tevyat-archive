## summary
### LITE
Lempo Isle is one of the three Southern Isles of Nod-Krai, accessible from 6.0. It is home to Nasha Town, the region's main port and commercial centre, and to the Clink-Clank Krumkake Craftshop where the young inventor Aino works. Its settlements are crowded, industrial and run by no single authority.
### STANDARD +
The island also has the glowing Blue Amber Lake, the beaches of Starsand Shoal where the Traveler and their sibling's ship landed, and the Eye of Kratti, a crystal-tree island over an old whirlpool.

## geography
### STANDARD
Lempo Isle is one of the Southern Isles (a group also called the Lempo Isles), south of Pohjola, together with Hiisi Island and Paha Isle. Nasha Town sits on the coast; the Craftshop lies inland; the Blue Amber Lake sits on a raised plateau between the north and south parts of Barrowmoss Barrens; Starsand Shoal is the western beach, and the Eye of Kratti is a small island off the coast.
### LARGE +
Local specialties: Frostlamp Flower and Portable Bearing. Bosses include the Knuckle Duckle and the Radiant Moonfly. The Southern Isles with Piramida are the most populated parts of Nod-Krai.

## places
### STANDARD
Nasha Town: seaside settlement, Nod-Krai's main port and commercial centre, built mostly of scrap metal with cargo rails across the sky and kuuvahki-powered machines on every corner. Clink-Clank Krumkake Craftshop: workshop begun by Aino. Blue Amber Lake: highland lake of crystal blue with bonfires on the shore. Starsand Shoal: western beaches. Eye of Kratti: island with a crystalline tree.
### LARGE +
Nasha Town hosts an Adventurers' Guild branch (Katheryne, receptionist; Sarin, branch master), the Curatorium of Secrets, Mimisbrunnr Books, a branch of Northland Bank, Nuts 'N' Nuts, the Rossum Workshop, the restaurant Speranza, the Flagship and the Vesnici Proleca Greenhouse. The Craftshop's Meeting Point stands near its centre. Blue Amber Lake's unique climate makes it glow at all hours and is the only place Stellafruit grows; Papa Wrench's scribbled notes lie there. The Eye of Kratti holds the wreck of the ship Atlas and others.

## people
### STANDARD
Nasha Town's locals include merchants, sailors, engineers, harbor officers, Fatui soldiers, Ratniki, and adherents of the Frostmoon Scions (for example Kantor and Lyylikki). Aino and her creation Ineffa are associated with the Craftshop; the shop's NPCs include Bozena, Lalli, Zaitseva and, after Problem-Sorting Robot, Chard Lombro.
### LARGE +
Others in the town include Iratxa (a Marechaussee Phantom agent), Liouba (a researcher from the Fontaine Research Institute), Zompaynco (an investigator for the Saurian Relics Association), Kaunea (a fashion consultant), Golitsyna (who owns a place called Mead-Boy), Hjerson (a detective) and Urwa, representing Lord Sangemah Bay. At the Eye of Kratti the Traveler meets Ilmari.

## governance
### STANDARD
Nasha Town has no official governing body. Several factions hold influence: the Voynich Guild, the Curatorium of Secrets, Speranza and the Fatui. The wiki says this makes it both a paradise of freedom and a lawless jungle.

## culture
### STANDARD
Nod-Krai's limited resources shape the town: it is mostly built of scrap, industrialised and noisy. People from all over Teyvat gather in Nod-Krai, and it has a reputation as an 'Elysium'. The Craftshop began as Aino's small workspace to research the 'World's Best Krumkake Machine' and grew into a renowned machine workshop after her many inventions and Ineffa's birth.
### LARGE +
Aino is a child prodigy and an orphan who regards her creations and screws as her family. A krumkake is a Norwegian cookie, per the wiki's etymology note.

## history
### STANDARD
Starsand Shoal's description says that when the dark disaster engulfed Nod-Krai many heroes made their last stand on its sands. The Eye of Kratti was once a whirlpool, known as a forbidden zone for turbulent water, monster rumours and lost souls. A few years before the present the ship Atlas sank there with the Proof of the Cognoscenti aboard.
### LARGE +
The Frostmoon Scions told Libuse the Atlas was cursed by something at sea. Soon after, a crystalline tree sprouted from the whirlpool and made a small island; rocks fell from the sky, shiny things appeared, and a friend of the NPC Ilmari heard strange singing. In the quest Whisper Beneath the Waves the Traveler and Paimon follow the ghosts of the Atlas's crew to the tree's roots, revive it with kuuvahki, and recover the lost Proof of the Cognoscenti.
### ULTRA +
Starsand Shoal is also where the spaceship of the Traveler and their sibling landed in Nod-Krai, per the wiki's location intro.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Nasha Town: scrap-metal walls, cargo rails overhead and kuuvahki machines on every corner. The Craftshop: a busy machine workshop. Blue Amber Lake: quiet, starlit bonfires on the shore. Starsand Shoal: whispering waves over a battlefield of heroes.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Nod-Krai as a whole is harsh: people suspected of holding a lot of Mora are painted as targets, and the Fatui have a presence in the town. The wiki lists no further hazards for the island beyond its bosses. Bosses include the Knuckle Duckle and the Radiant Moonfly. The Eye of Kratti is a forbidden zone with a haunted history.

## travel
### LARGE
Nasha Town is Nod-Krai's main port and has a Bulletin Board. Hiisi Island and Paha Isle are the neighbouring Southern Isles.

## quests
### STANDARD
Quests and series set here include Whisper Beneath the Waves (Eye of Kratti), The Tale-Telling Heart (Blue Amber Lake), Problem-Sorting Robot (Craftshop). Details of those quests were not read for this entry.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 Lempo Isle is open to explore; Nasha Town remains Nod-Krai's trading hub.
### STANDARD +
Nothing in 7.0-7.1 is recorded as changing the island.

## entry: Nasha Town
@keys: Nasha Town
@version: 6.0
### STANDARD
Nod-Krai's main port and commercial centre, built from scrap metal with rails across the sky. It has no official government; the Voynich Guild, Curatorium of Secrets, Speranza and the Fatui all hold influence, making it both free and lawless. It has an Adventurers' Guild branch.

## entry: Clink-Clank Krumkake Craftshop
@keys: Clink-Clank Krumkake Craftshop, Krumkake Craftshop
@version: 6.0
### STANDARD
A workshop begun by Aino to build the 'World's Best Krumkake Machine'. Following her many inventions and the birth of Ineffa, it became a renowned machine workshop.

## entry: Eye of Kratti
@keys: Eye of Kratti, Atlas, Proof of the Cognoscenti
@keys2: Nod-Krai, Kratti, Ilmari
@version: 6.0
### STANDARD
A small island with a crystalline tree at its centre, over a former whirlpool. The ship Atlas sank here a few years before the present with the Proof of the Cognoscenti. The quest Whisper Beneath the Waves revives the tree with kuuvahki and recovers the lost object.

## entry: Blue Amber Lake
@keys: Blue Amber Lake, Stellafruit, Barrowmoss Barrens
@version: 6.0
### STANDARD
A highland lake of crystalline blue on a plateau between the north and south of Barrowmoss Barrens. Its climate gives it a constant glow, and Stellafruit grows nowhere else. Papa Wrench's notes about his life after his supposed death aboard the Fortunate One lie near it.
