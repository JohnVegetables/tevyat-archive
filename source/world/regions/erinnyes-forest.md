## summary
### LITE
Erinnyes Forest is a green, lake-filled area on the island of Erinnyes in Fontaine. Its landmarks are the Weeping Willow of the Lake, the Roots of Erinnyes (the water veins whose centre is called Hauteclaire), the lake Loch Urania and Lumidouce Harbor, a port built on a waterfall. The world-quest series The Wild Fairy of Erinnyes deals with cleansing its polluted waters.
### STANDARD +
It is the same island as the Opera Epiclese, the Fountain of Lucine and Marcotte Station. Official text says the Fontaine Research Institute's ruins lie on the north side of the island, and the depths of Erinnyes are considered ill-advised to explore by Neuvillette.

## geography
### STANDARD
Forest and lakes make up the region. A large willow tree grows at the centre of a lake, its roots reaching down into the water. Beneath the forest lie caverns and water veins, many connecting to the willow, collectively the Roots of Erinnyes. Lumidouce Harbor clings to the side of a waterfall, in the same fashion as Romaritime Harbor.
### LARGE +
Dryas Wood, a small hollow between the Institute region and this forest, once served as the Institute's 'backyard' with plant nurseries.
### ULTRA +
The forest's notable items are the Foggy Forest Branch and the Drained Conch Cup; its local specialties are Lakelight Lily, Lumidouce Bell, Rainbow Rose and Romaritime Flower. Urville appears in Lumidouce Harbor by day after the Morte quest Wish-Fulfilling Treasure Hunt.

## places
### STANDARD
- **Weeping Willow of the Lake**: the big willow at the lake's centre; its health is tied to the water veins. Before the veins are cleansed the lake looks sickly yellow; afterwards it turns bright blue.
- **Roots of Erinnyes**: subterranean caverns and water veins, serving as an information system whose central part is called Hauteclaire.
- **Loch Urania**: a lake with a large water vortex that forms a bubble at the top; tainted by Contaminated Bacterial Mats until cleansed.
- **Lumidouce Harbor**: a coastal elevator and lookout on a waterfall; a vital trading port.
- **Foggy Forest Path**, **Freminet's Temporary Camp** and a place called The Rusty Rudder are also marked.
### LARGE +
Lumidouce Harbor handles a large volume of international trade, including tea from Qiaoying Village and spices from Sumeru. Named NPCs include Cassigneul, Kimpel, Chourei and Xing Cheng; a few, such as Chaussivert, Fusilier and Lemarcq, appear only after the quest An Expected Lie.

## people
### STANDARD
Lumidouce Harbor has a dozen or so named NPCs. Pahsiv, a Melusine, appears in the Wild Fairy of Erinnyes quests. Freminet has a temporary camp marked here.

## culture
### STANDARD
The harbour is a trading port, linked through tea from Qiaoying Village and spices from Sumeru. The forest's own folklore concerns the water veins and a Local Legend, Mageblade Corrouge, who can be found in the Roots after the Wild Fairy of Erinnyes quests.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Foggy paths, lakes and a great willow. Before cleansing, the lake and willow look sick and Loch Urania produces high winds and a dark sky even by day; afterwards the winds die and the sky clears.
### LARGE +
Reed pipes sound when dew bubbles pop, and jellyfish-shaped devices carry visitors upward.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Common enemies are hilichurls, slimes, Clockwork Meka, a Pyro Whopperflower and treasure hoarders; elites include Lawachurls, Hydro Hilichurl Rogues, a Blazing Axe Mitachurl and a Hydro Abyss Mage. Contaminated Bacterial Mats and autocannons are environmental hazards.

## travel
### STANDARD
Lumidouce Harbor is the island's trading port, and Marcotte Station is also on the island. Exploration puzzles use the Xenochromatic Ball Octopus to clear mats, disable cannons, push Dew Bubbles and open floating crystal flowers.

## quests
### STANDARD
The Wild Fairy of Erinnyes is a world-quest series: Foggy Forest Path, Wilting Weeping Willow, Furious Mouth of the Spring and Within the Depths of Erinnyes. Furious Mouth of the Spring starts at Loch Urania and includes a fight with an enraged vishap. Other quests listed here include Flower, Feathers, and Bullets, Free Verse, Impromptu Poem of the Crimson Dawn, Miraculous Crown, Prince's Country, Sightlines and Camera Tricks and Neuvillette: Standing in the Rain.
### LARGE +
Foggy Forest Path, Wilting Weeping Willow and Furious Mouth of the Spring run concurrently; the latter two each have three versions depending on how many Teardrop Keys the player holds.
