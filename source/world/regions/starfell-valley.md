## summary
### LITE
Starfell Valley is the central and most varied region of Mondstadt, running from Stormbearer Point on the south coast to the Whispering Woods. Its centre is Cider Lake, on whose island stands Mondstadt City, and it is where the Knights of Favonius mainly carry out their duty of protecting locals and travelers. Lakes, woods, ruins and cliffs make it the area most visitors, including the Traveler at the start of the story, first see.
### STANDARD +
The valley also holds Starfell Lake, Starsnatch Cliff, the Whispering Woods, where the Traveler first meets Venti and Dvalin, and the ruined Thousand Winds Temple in the northeast.

## geography
### STANDARD
Abundant lakes and a wide variety of trees. Stormbearer Point lies at the southern tip, with the Stormbearer Coast just south of it; Cider Lake lies at the centre, with Mondstadt City on its island; Starfell Lake and the Whispering Woods are nearby. Local plants include calla lily, cecilia, dandelion seed, philanemo mushroom, small lamp grass and valberry.
### LARGE +
Cider Lake's clear, never-freezing waters are the headwaters that made Mondstadt's ciders and wines famous. The Whispering Woods are dense and deep, cut by a small road; travelers took the sound of wind in the leaves for whispers, hence the name. Starfell Lake has a Statue of the Seven, and its viewpoint is titled The Marsh of Celestial Guardians.

## places
### STANDARD
Mondstadt City and Cider Lake; Starfell Lake; the Whispering Woods; Starsnatch Cliff; Stormbearer Point (with the Storm Barrier ruins); the Thousand Winds Temple, with the Temple of the Wolf domain beside it.
### LARGE +
Stormbearer Point and Starsnatch Cliff each hold a Time Trial Challenge. The Thousand Winds Temple carries an inscription: "Seeds of stories, brought by the wind and cultivated by time."

## people
### STANDARD
Mondstadt City's people are covered in the Mondstadt entry. Out in the valley the Knight of Favonius Harry and the Storm Watcher Vind are met at Stormbearer Point; the researcher Patchi the Curious, Doolan and Henry Morton at the Thousand Winds Temple; and Marla, Marvin and Sharp appear at Starsnatch Cliff at night.
### LARGE +
The wiki describes the valley as where the Knights' duties primarily take place, with guards patrolling the area.

## governance
### STANDARD
No lord holds the valley. The Knights of Favonius patrol it from their headquarters in Mondstadt City and protect locals and travelers alike.

## culture
### STANDARD
Cider Lake is regarded as a symbol of Mondstadt; two drinks are named for it, a sweet milk-and-juice "Sweet Cider Lake" and an only lightly alcoholic "Cider Lake". Starsnatch Cliff is a quiet nighttime spot.
### LARGE +
The Thousand Winds Temple's carvings and its maxim on wind carrying the seeds of tales are the nearest thing to a monument to Mondstadt's old gods. A giant sundial stands east of the Temple of the Wolf.
### ULTRA +
In the city, the Mondstadt Library was converted by Ragnvindr from an aristocrats' bathhouse after Vennessa's rebellion and grew into the largest library in northern Teyvat; the Great Fire of Fall Equinox reduced it to a sixth of its size, yet it remains the largest in the north (per the Mondstadt entry).

## history
### STANDARD
The Thousand Winds Temple was built by the people of Arcadia to worship Istaroth, Ruler of Time, and left in ruins when Arcadia fell. During the Archon War the Gunnhildr Clan settled among the ruins, seeking help against the relentless blizzards of the war between Decarabian and Boreas, built a small shrine to Istaroth and prayed. A small wind spirit, Barbatos, answered instead.
### LARGE +
Still only a wind spirit, Barbatos met Istaroth at the temple, and after hearing his wish she entrusted him with fragments of her power and authority. After he became the Anemo Archon the temple was rebuilt to honour both him and Istaroth, and the weapons of the Sacrificial Series were once ritual implements there. Istaroth was gradually forgotten for unknown reasons. In the aristocratic era the temple became an arena in which slaves were forced to fight for the nobles, and it was later abandoned again.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Open skies, windmills and lakeshore on the south side; hushed, whispering woodland near the lake; cold stone and weeds in the temple ruins. The city's walls and windmills rise over it all.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Hilichurls and slimes, with a hilichurl tower near Stormbearer Point. Area bosses are an Anemo Hypostasis and a Cryo Regisvine.
### LARGE +
The Temple of the Wolf is a one-time trial domain in which the player chooses Hydro, Electro or Pyro trials.

## travel
### LARGE
The city is reached by a long bridge to its island. Springvale lies south of the city and the Dawn Winery southwest; Windrise and the coast are east, and the Brightcrown Mountains northwest (see the Mondstadt entry). The Stormbearer Coast to the south is where the Traveler's journey begins.

## quests
### LARGE
The Traveler's story begins on the coast south of Stormbearer Point, and the Whispering Woods are where they first meet Venti and Dvalin.

## status

As of 7.1 Starfell Valley is the calm, patrolled centre of Mondstadt; the pages read for this entry record no new threat here after 1.0.
### STANDARD +
Mondstadt City, its Knights and the Cathedral are covered in the Mondstadt entry and their own files.

## entry: Stormbearer Point
@keys: Stormbearer Point, Stormbearer Coast, Storm Barrier
@keys2: Vind, Traveler
### STANDARD
The southern tip of Starfell Valley and the coast just south of it are where the Traveler begins their journey across Teyvat with Paimon. Ruins and a Storm Barrier stand here, a hilichurl tower and a seelie court nearby; the Knight Harry and the Storm Watcher Vind can be met.

## entry: Whispering Woods
@keys: Whispering Woods, Starfell Lake, Marsh of Celestial Guardians
### STANDARD
Dense woods near Starfell Lake and the city, where wind in the leaves sounds like whispering. The wiki records it as where the Traveler first meets Venti and Dvalin. A Statue of the Seven stands by Starfell Lake.

## entry: Thousand Winds Temple and Temple of the Wolf
@keys: Thousand Winds Temple, Temple of the Wolf
@keys2: Istaroth, Gunnhildr, Barbatos
### STANDARD
Ruins northeast of the city of an ancient temple. The Arcadians built it for Istaroth; the Gunnhildr Clan prayed to her here during the Archon War and were answered by the wind spirit who became Barbatos, who also met Istaroth here and received fragments of her power. Rebuilt for both gods, later a slaves' arena, now abandoned. The nearby Temple of the Wolf was an ancient civilisation's training ground, a trial domain today.

## entry: Starsnatch Cliff
@keys: Starsnatch Cliff
### STANDARD
A cliff in Starfell Valley with a Time Trial Challenge and, at night, the NPCs Marla, Marvin and Sharp. The Mondstadt entry describes it as a quiet place where Venti has met the Traveler.

## entry: Cider Lake
@keys: Cider Lake
@keys2: Mondstadt City, wine, apples
### STANDARD
A natural freshwater lake so clear its bottom shows, with waters that never freeze. Official text credits its headwaters with making Mondstadt's ciders and wines famous and the lake a symbol of the nation. Mondstadt City stands on a large island in it.
