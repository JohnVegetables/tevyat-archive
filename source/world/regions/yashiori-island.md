## summary
### LITE
Yashiori Island is a rain-lashed island in Inazuma, near Watatsumi Island. Here the serpent god Orobashi, worshipped by the people of Watatsumi, was slain by the Electro Archon's legendary Musou no Hitotachi, which split the island in two and left a canyon, Musoujin Gorge. The god's bones lie scattered across the island, and its lingering will, the Tatarigami curse, brings storms, sickness and madness. The Shogunate and the Watatsumi Army fought over it during the Vision Hunt Decree war.
### STANDARD +
The island yields Crystal Marrow, a mineral drawn from Orobashi's remains, which has long made it worth contesting. Its ancient wards, the Narukami Pillars, kept the curse in check until they were destroyed shortly before the Traveler's arrival.

## geography
### STANDARD
A rugged, wet island whose east is cut by the narrow, deep Musoujin Gorge. Orobashi's colossal skull and bones form landmarks (the Serpent's Head). Subareas include Fort Fujitou, a Watatsumi Army camp; Fort Mumei, the ruins of an ancient city; Higi Village; Jakotsu Mine; Nazuchi Beach; the Serpent's Head; and the gorge. Local specialties include Crystal Marrow, Naku Weed, Onikabuto, Dendrobium and Sea Ganoderma.
### LARGE +
While the Tatarigami are strong, the open island suffers constant rain and frequent thunderstorms (in gameplay, rain keeps characters Wet and lightning during rain can cause Electro-Charged). Musoujin Gorge has its own hazard, Balethunder, and a river of electrified water; Thunder Sakura Boughs shelter travellers. In the quest series Orobashi's Legacy, Fort Mumei rises out of the sea after the first three wards are repaired, and the perpetual storm ends once its final ward is restored.

## places
### LARGE
Fort Fujitou: a Watatsumi Army camp with a smith, soldiers and workers. Fort Mumei: ruins of an ancient city, whose final ward is guarded by a Maguu Kenki. Higi Village: a nearly abandoned village. Jakotsu Mine ("Snake Bone Mine"): a mine whose refugees and workers once depended on Crystal Marrow. Serpent's Head: Orobashi's skull, with a tomb beneath it. Musoujin Gorge: the canyon cut by the killing blow. The Mikage Furnace on Tatarasuna, a separate island in Kannazuka, draws on the same Tatarigami energy.

## people
### LARGE
Kaji investigates the weather and the wards. Fort Fujitou holds Watatsumi soldiers and workers, among them the officer Kaida Ryouma, the samurai Nathan and the blacksmith Miyazaki Saburou. In Higi Village the pharmacist Yasumoto remains while the chief Washizu has fallen into madness; the boy Chouji lives near Jakotsu Mine. Pirates under "Uncle" Onitaka operate from the coast.
### ULTRA +
The Kitain Clan, descendants of Kitain Bunsou, were the Yashiori Guardians who helped suppress the Tatarigami; the bloodline ended after Kitain Hideie fled during the cataclysm, according to item text and the last part of Orobashi's Legacy. After the quest series Kaji moves on to the Kujou Encampment.

## governance
### LARGE
Part of Inazuma under the Shogunate. After Orobashi's conquest its vassal ruler was Touzannou (later called Akuou), who died in the war. In the modern era the Tenryou Commission represents the Shogunate while the Watatsumi Army holds Fort Fujitou. General Kujou Masahito reported the Tatarigami crisis to his father, Commissioner Kujou Takayuki, who refused to believe him.

## culture
### LARGE
Watatsumi folk keep faith with Orobashi, the Watatsumi Omikami, who taught them to farm and smelt; to them Yashiori is a holy graveyard. People from Narukami Island mined Crystal Marrow from the god's remains, which angered Watatsumi. According to the Shogun, the Watatsumi were allowed to keep worshipping Orobashi so that it could keep receiving their prayers.

## history
### STANDARD
Towards the end of the Archon War, about 2,000 years ago, Orobashi, the Watatsumi Omikami, invaded Yashiori Island, perhaps because Watatsumi was starving and pressed by Celestia's decree. Ei, then acting for her sister Makoto, waged a brutal war and killed Orobashi with the Musou no Hitotachi, splitting the island and leaving its remains. Its lingering will became the Tatarigami, which the Shogunate suppressed with Narukami Pillars. Recently, radicals in the Watatsumi Army, urged on by a Fatui mole, destroyed the wards and unleashed the curse.
### LARGE +
Orobashi first built a navy under the shrine maiden Mouun and her twin sister Ayame; Mouun's disciple Touzannou (Akuou) became the island's vassal ruler. Ei's lieutenant Sasayuri died in the war and Mouun was ambushed and slain while retreating. After the Sakoku Decree and the Vision Hunt Decree, the Watatsumi Army and the Shogun's Army fought to a stalemate around Yashiori, which the Tatarigami's return deepened with storms, illness and desertion. Takayuki considered replacing Masahito with Kujou Sara, but the investigation team he sent was lost to the curse.
### ULTRA +
Four centuries ago the Mikage Furnace at Tatarasuna was sabotaged; the modern furnace uses Tatarigami energy with Fontaine technology, and the Grand Narukami Shrine's priestesses distrust it. Kokomi was away negotiating with mercenaries when the wards fell. The Traveler's work with Kaji and the Tatara Tales quests belong to this period.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Tatarigami-tainted enemies (shown with a black aura), Kairagi, Nobushi, hilichurls, Treasure Hoarders and Ruin Machines; a Maguu Kenki guards the final ward. Constant thunder, Balethunder in the gorge, and illness for anyone without a Vision or similar sensitivity.
### LARGE +
Exposure to the Tatarigami causes untreatable fevers, bleeding, migraines and rashes, then hallucinations and insanity, often centred on a monstrous god called "Him". Vision-bearers resist best. Plants in tainted soil wither.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Grey skies, endless rain and thunder over huge bleached bones; abandoned huts, wet stone, the smell of storm and sea. Wind whistles through the serpent's skull. During the civil war two armies sat here in uneasy stalemate; since the war ended, the Watatsumi camp at Fort Fujitou has thinned out.

## travel
### LARGE
Reached by sea from Narukami Island or Watatsumi Island; the nearby islets have typically served as transit points for supplies to Watatsumi. Fort Mumei emerges only after the first three wards are repaired. Expect storms until the Narukami Pillars are restored.

## quests
### LARGE
Orobashi's Legacy (Kaji and the wards), Tatara Tales (the furnace), the Higi Village stories (Yasumoto, Chouji, Washizu) and Sangonomiya Kokomi's story quest around Fort Fujitou.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the island is quiet but scarred. The civil war fought over it ended when the Vision Hunt Decree was revoked (2.1). The wards were repaired in Orobashi's Legacy, the storms have eased, and the Tatarigami linger.
### STANDARD +
No Yashiori event is shown in 6.x or 7.0-7.1; the island is described as of the last released content.

## entry: Orobashi's Death and the Musou no Hitotachi
@keys: Orobashi, Watatsumi Omikami, Musou no Hitotachi, Musoujin Gorge
@version: 2.0
### STANDARD
Orobashi was a coral-bodied serpent god who ruled Enkanomiya and then Watatsumi Island, having learned forbidden history that Celestia wished hidden. It invaded Yashiori Island and was slain by Ei's Musou no Hitotachi, which tore Musoujin Gorge across the island and left the serpent's remains. Watatsumi's people still worship it.

## entry: The Tatarigami
@keys: Tatarigami, Narukami Pillar, Narukami Pillars, Balethunder
@version: 2.0
### STANDARD
The Tatarigami is the curse formed of Orobashi's will and power. Wards called Narukami Pillars suppress it; when they fall it brings storms, sickness and madness. Vision-bearers resist it best, and Crystal Marrow from the remains is mined.

## entry: Higi Village
@keys: Higi Village, Washizu, Yasumoto, Chouji
@version: 2.0
### STANDARD
Higi Village was devastated by the war and then by the Tatarigami. It is nearly empty: Washizu, the chief, has gone mad; Yasumoto is a pharmacist trying to cure the illness; Chouji, a boy, gathers Crystal Marrow near Jakotsu Mine.
### LARGE +
Chouji's mother left Inazuma for Sumeru seeking a cure, since the Tatarigami flowed freely inside her without corrupting her; his father fled earlier. Washizu began sacrificing the remaining villagers while hearing a god's voice, and Yasumoto's final fate is unknown.

## entry: Fort Fujitou and Fort Mumei
@keys: Fort Fujitou, Fort Mumei, Kaji, Kaida Ryouma
@version: 2.0
### STANDARD
Fort Fujitou is a Watatsumi Army camp where Kaji studies the weather. Fort Mumei is an ancient city's ruins that rose from the sea after the first three wards were repaired; a Maguu Kenki guards its final ward.

## entry: The Watatsumi War over Yashiori
@keys: Sangonomiya Resistance, Watatsumi Army, Nathan, Kujou Masahito
@version: 2.0
@spoiler: minor
### LARGE
The Watatsumi Army and the Shogun's Army fought to a stalemate here. A radical group in the Resistance, acting without Kokomi's knowledge or permission, destroyed the Narukami Pillars, urged on by Nathan, a Fatui agent working to prolong the war. Nathan vanishes after the Archon Quest exposes the Fatui's role in the war. Even so, in the world quest Clean House (2.2) Watatsumi's Shizuru reports that the remaining Fatui have not given up their surveys of Yashiori, working through infiltrated Shogunate soldiers, and the Traveler helps expose one of their recruiting operations.
