## summary
### LITE
Toyac Springs is the hot-spring coast of Natlan and the home of the People of the Springs (Meztli), a tribe of guides, cartographers, musicians and spa-keepers who live with their water-loving Koholasaurs. Official text calls it the most popular tribe region with outside visitors. Its name resembles the Nahuatl atoyac, 'on the river'.
### STANDARD +
An underground river of lava here is the real site behind the tribe's legend of a mysterious island. The playable hero Mualani is one of its people.

## geography
### STANDARD
Springs, warm pools and beaches along Natlan's shore, with sub-areas Ameyalco Waters (Spiritways and eddies for Koholasaurs), the floating islands of the Upper Sanctum, Spiritwall Cave and the tribe's settlement. The only local specialty is sprayfeather gill.
### LARGE +
The Underground Lava River is a cavern filled with Liquid Phlogiston and shows periodic volcanic activity. The Upper Sanctum is a ring of floating islands held up by Phlogiston and only reachable via a Spiritway.

## places
### STANDARD
The People of the Springs' settlement, with Colomche Plaza (an open space where musicians and street artists perform), Golden Dusk (a restaurant and lodge by the beach), Leisurely Puffer (a watersports shop owned by Mualani's family, known for custom surfboards), Djembe Studios (an instrument shop run by Anchieta), Xochicuicatl Records (a record store owned by Pakaa Nui that also composes, records and markets music), Atlahua's Net (a fishing-gear store owned by Yelafath) and Yumemizuki's Cozy Corner, a small psychotherapy shop staffed by a dream-eating baku called Mint Daifuku and presumably owned by Yumemizuki Mizuki; Ameyalco Waters; the Upper Sanctum; the Underground Lava River; and Spiritwall Cave.
### LARGE +
Golden Dusk is an old home of the Nui family, turned into a restaurant at Chief Amina's suggestion; its matriarch Kupo Nui owns it and Tirda sings there. It has held 242 rounds of the Inferno Stomach Supremo eating contest, whose current champion is Varesa of the Collective of Plenty. The tribe uses natural springs for locals and vetted guests and artificial springs for foreigners who cannot tolerate the heat of natural ones.
### ULTRA +
Spiritwall Cave contains the notes of a graffiti maker who was trapped by a rockfall and kept drawing. He wrote that he had devised an art of pictures from Phlogiston Engraving, hoped it would one day be appreciated, and left without giving his name.

## people
### STANDARD
Amina is chief. Mualani, the 'swellrider' of the hot springs, is a hero from the tribe, and her family owns the Leisurely Puffer. The Nui family run Golden Dusk.
### LARGE +
Past members include Atawallpa, the giant Tupac, the former chief Paio and, by the wiki's inference, the ancient Pyro Archon Cochanina. Cochanina drove the Volcano Lord out of the People of the Springs over a thousand years ago, according to Natlan/History.

## culture
### STANDARD
The tribe has long served as guides and cartographers. Its people like to bathe in hot springs with their Koholasaurs, fish together, listen to music and relax; the region is regarded as a vacation spot.
### LARGE +
The first chief, in legend, set out for a mysterious island, gave up the 'outcome' to save his companions, came home empty-handed and realised he had already found the treasure; with his companions he founded Meztli. Surfing runs through the tribe's image: the ancient Pyro Archon Cochanina is remembered as a surfing champion (the weapon text Surf's Up), which is why the wiki assumes she was one of the springs' people.

## history
@version: 5.0
### STANDARD
The People of the Springs are one of Natlan's six tribes. Natlan/History says Cochanina drove the Volcano Lord out of their land over a thousand years ago. Tupac, a giant of the tribe, fought beside Tenoch against the Abyss five hundred years ago and later died of its taint or his wounds.
### LARGE +
The quest The Mysterious Island's Truth, part of Mualani's story, takes the Traveler into the Underground Lava River, the true location of the legendary island; there Vulcanite, a rare golden crystal formed by rapidly cooled Liquid Phlogiston, appears only when volcanic activity begins and is swallowed by the lava almost immediately.
### ULTRA +
According to legend the Upper Sanctum (Hanan Pacha) was raised by Waxaklahun Ubah Kan and his disciple Chaac so that those who passed their trials could be taught Phlogiston, and those people became the first human tribe. In the world quest Seeker No Finding the Traveler completes the trials and finds a hidden island with a Phlogiston hot spring; the NPC Chikya says a hero accepted by the trials can gain the power to wield Phlogiston by bathing in it.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Steam over warm pools, music in the plaza, holidaymakers and the glow of lava below the cave floors. Official text sums up the tribe as renowned for hot springs and relaxing music.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Hostile Koholasaurs and Yumkasaurs, wild fighters of the Flowing Blade and Swiftstep bands, and lava or Liquid Phlogiston which rapidly drains HP. The boss is the Secret Source Automaton: Configuration Device.
### LARGE +
The Underground Lava River periodically fills with lava, and it is unclear how the Koholasaurs that live there cope. Flow-Inverted Wayob Manifestations are elite enemies in Ameyalco Waters.

## travel
### LARGE
Koholasaurs swim in water and liquid Phlogiston, leap from eddies and ride Spiritways; Mualani can do the same (a gameplay mechanic). The Upper Sanctum is reached by activating a Spiritway.

## quests
@version: 5.0
### LARGE
Tribal Chronicles: Where the Springs Return (Mualani's story), including The Mysterious Island's Truth. World quest series: Tale of Dreams Plucked From Fire (Tracer No Tracing and Seeker No Finding), which opens the Upper Sanctum.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the tribe is at peace and Toyac Springs is the most popular holiday stop in Natlan.
### STANDARD +
The Abyss assault on Natlan ended in 5.3. The tribe lost Atea, who died of Abyssal poisoning in Chapter V, as well as Inna and Kwame in the invasion; Awhina, Haronga and Uturunku survive or die depending on a player choice.
### ULTRA +
Atea had been stabbed by an Abyssal monster; doctors saved her life but could no longer let her bathe in hot water because Abyssal power remained in her body. The wiki lists no changes at Toyac Springs in 6.x or 7.x.

## entry: People of the Springs
@keys: People of the Springs, Meztli, Koholasaurus, Koholasaur
@keys2: Toyac, Natlan
@version: 5.0
### STANDARD
The People of the Springs (Meztli) are one of Natlan's six tribes. Their chief is Amina. They live along Toyac Springs with Koholasaurs and are known as guides and cartographers.

## entry: The Upper Sanctum (Hanan Pacha)
@keys: Upper Sanctum, Hanan Pacha, Chikya
@version: 5.0
### LARGE
Hanan Pacha, the Upper Sanctum, is a group of floating islands in the sky above Ameyalco Waters, held together by Phlogiston. Legend says Waxaklahun Ubah Kan created it as a site to share Phlogiston with humanity. Today it is a ring of smaller islands, accessible only by activating a Spiritway; its ancient trials are made of Spiritways and Iridescent Inscriptions. The name is also an Inca term for the upper world.

## entry: Underground Lava River
@keys: Underground Lava River, Vulcanite, Mysterious Island
@version: 5.0
### LARGE
An underground cavern full of Liquid Phlogiston that shows periodic volcanic activity. It is the real location of the 'Mysterious Island' from the People of the Springs' legends, as revealed in The Mysterious Island's Truth. Koholasaurs live in the cave.
