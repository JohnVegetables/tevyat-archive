## summary
### LITE
The Great Red Sand, also called the Deshret Desert, is the vast desert of western Sumeru. Once the realm of King Deshret, Lord of Deserts, it is now a sea of dunes dotted with buried temples, ruined cities, oases and hidden machine halls. The Eremite tribes, descendants of his people, wander here, and Aaru Village keeps his memory. Beyond the Wall of Samiel on the east lies the rainforest of the Dharma Forest. Sandstorms, Ruin Golems and the old Jinn legends make it dangerous and rich in secrets.
### STANDARD +
Its four main areas are the Hypostyle Desert, the Land of Lower Setekh, the Land of Upper Setekh and the Desert of Hadramaveth. The nation's wisdom-seeking scholars have long coveted its ruins, while the desert folk remember it as a lost kingdom.

## geography
### STANDARD
Four areas make up the subregion. The Hypostyle Desert holds Khemenu Temple, Sobek Oasis, three named dunes and the Mausoleum of King Deshret. The Land of Lower Setekh holds Aaru Village and Khaj-Nisut; the Land of Upper Setekh holds the Valley of Dahri. The Desert of Hadramaveth, the largest area, stretches west of them and holds the old city of Gurabad, the Tanit Camps and Mt. Damavand. The east is bounded by the Wall of Samiel, with Caravan Ribat at its foot.
### LARGE +
Hypostyle Desert subareas include Khemenu Temple, Sobek Oasis, the Dune of Carouses, the Dune of Elusion, the Dune of Magma and the Mausoleum of King Deshret; Lower Setekh includes Aaru Village, Abdju Pit, Dar al-Shifa and Khaj-Nisut; Upper Setekh includes the Valley of Dahri. The Desert of Hadramaveth has eleven subareas, the most of any area: Debris of Panjvahe, Dunes of Steel (which links it to Old Vanarana), Mt. Damavand, the Passage of Ghouls, Qusayr Al-Inkhida', Safhe Shatranj, the Tanit Camps, the Sands of Al-Azif, the Sands of Three Canals, Wadi Al-Majuj and the Wounded Shin Valley. Upper Setekh is named for lying toward the sun at noon.

## places
### LARGE
Aaru Village is the largest human settlement in the desert and houses followers and descendants of King Deshret as well as exiled Akademiya scholars. The Mausoleum of King Deshret is a giant pyramid that is only one corner of a palace with many more temples beneath; a great cyclone long wheeled above it, shut off after the quest "Dual Evidence". Khemenu Temple is an underground temple "intimately linked to the people" where all bowed to Deshret's wisdom. Khaj-Nisut, once called the Eye of the Sands, holds Deshret's throne room. The Valley of Dahri holds a giant Ruin Golem and Akademiya investigation notes; Dar al-Shifa, now abandoned, was a hospital for Eleazar patients.
### ULTRA +
The Tanit Camps stood in the depths of Deshret Desert amid the ruins of Gurabad. Mt. Damavand, where a massive tornado whirls round the centre, is where Apep resides; its name is said to mean "the mountain from which smoke and ash arise" in the lost desert tongue. Thamud Oasis is where the electrum city of Saleh once stood. The Sands of Al-Azif are a flat region of wenut and the Wenut Tunnels. Safhe Shatranj is called the Great Rift by the Tanit, has a viewpoint named "The Palace Abandoned by the Gods", and was named The Temples Forsaken before its quest.

## people
### STANDARD
The Eremites are the desert's mercenaries, descended from the vanished civilisations. They fall into many tribes and factions, each with its own code, bound by stories passed down orally and by dance and sword traditions inherited from Deshret's age. Aaru Village's people and its Guardian, Candace, are spoken of as his descendants; Dehya and Cyno also have ties here.
### LARGE +
The Tanit, a matrilineal tribe led by a Matriarch and Nine Elders who believed in the Goddess of Flowers, lived at the Tanit Camps and valued words and knowledge, trading crocodile goods, spices and oils. After the Hadramaveth events in 3.4 the Tanit were destroyed (see Tanit sub-entry). Vahumana researcher Tanjiyy later arrived to offer education aid and found the camps empty. Rukh Shah appoints consultants to ease disputes between Eremite groups.

## governance
### LARGE
Nobody governs the desert as a whole. Each Eremite tribe has its own leaders and law; Aaru Village is led by its chief, Uncle Anpu, and protected by its Guardian, Candace; its "Village Keepers", such as Khalil, are exiled Akademiya scholars whom the village shelters. The Akademiya in the rainforest maintains the Wall of Samiel. Dehya charges that the sages used the wall to keep the Eremites out of Sumeru and treated the desert folk as tools, undeserving of the Akasha's wisdom.
### ULTRA +
After the Chapter III Archon Quest events the Akademiya's grip loosened. A Tanit merchant's letter, found after Act V, hopes for easier trade and says banned books are being released; the General Mahamatra Cyno is to oversee education aid.

## culture
### LARGE
The Eremites honour Deshret's art of archery and sword-dancing, the latter now an art as well as a combat style; Eremite weapons can host fragments of ancient desert spirits that awaken in peril. Desert people value oral history; the Tanit in particular prized books smuggled from the Akademiya library. A saying traced to the Eremites describes someone who "isn't afraid of dying so that they might truly live".
### ULTRA +
The sun-and-eye emblem of Deshret appears throughout desert architecture. The eye symbol is a light-blue eye in the middle of a dark ten-pointed sun. Legends of the thousand and one Jinn, Deshret's servants, and of the magic bottle form a folk mythology.

## history
### STANDARD
Long ago the desert was the domain of the gods Nabu Malikata, Greater Lord Rukkhadevata and King Deshret, the Three Eternal Companion-Gods; Deshret and Nabu Malikata had earlier built the city of Ay-Khanoum together. Nabu Malikata died, and Rukkhadevata left to build a rainforest civilisation while Deshret kept the desert. He pursued a dream paradise through forbidden knowledge until the knowledge ruined his people, brought the Withering and drove him to sacrifice his life. His vassal city Gurabad had already fallen, destroyed by the Jinni Liloupar's revenge.
### LARGE +
Deshret made a deal with the Dendro Dragon Apep, who would consume his body after his death. With Nabu Malikata gone he built the Eternal Oasis as her cemetery, and later created Aaru, a culmination meant to protect his people from age and decay. When the disaster came, Rukkhadevata returned to help and had priests build temples with the power of life to mitigate it, but only Deshret's sacrifice ended it. His civilisation splintered into city-states ruled by humans.
### ULTRA +
Gurabad was founded by the shepherd Ormazd with the help of Liloupar; her plan against him unfolded across three generations: Ormazd and his children were poisoned, Parvezravan (Kisra) took the throne, and her grandson Shiruyeh's Brass Mask revolt let her sink most of the city into an abyss by Abyssal power. Deshret punished Liloupar by splitting her soul into seven parts given to his seven sages. Later kingdoms (Saleb Dowleh, Muzaffar, Tulaytullah and others) built new Gurabads and sank into the sand; the Old Blind Poet and others finally retreated to Aaru to await Deshret's return. About 400 years ago the Temple of Silence left the rainforest for a large underground temple near Thamud Oasis. Around 20 years ago Kaveh's father, an Akademiya scholar, died in the desert after running into quicksand.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
A scorching, sand-scoured expanse where the wind builds and erases dunes, gold-red light, silent stone gates and vast temples half buried in sand. Aaru Village's refugee shelters are crowded and neighbourly; the temples feel solemn and watchful.

## dangers
### LARGE
Sandstorms in the Desert of Hadramaveth, atmospheric vortices, tumbleweed, loose sand piles that hide enemies, and wenut such as the Setekh Wenut. Ancient devices protect the ruins: Primal Constructs, Ruin Golems, Primal Beams and barriers that need clearance from the Scarlet Sand Slate. Eremite bands, Treasure Hoarders, hilichurls (called Majuj in the desert), Fatui and Abyss remnants also roam. Eleazar and forbidden knowledge are quiet dangers near old Deshret sites.
### ULTRA +
Algorithm of Semi-Intransient Matrix of Overseer Network guards the Dune of Elusion. Jinn shrines allow the Jinni in the Magic Bottle to dispel vortices or call and calm sandstorms once upgraded.

## travel
### LARGE
Most travellers enter from Caravan Ribat through the Wall of Samiel, or from Aaru Village; waypoints and Sobek Oasis serve as rest stops. Wadi Al-Majuj and the Temir Mountains lead on to the Girdle of the Sands. Sumeru City is reached through Caravan Ribat and the rainforest.
### ULTRA +
Four-Leaf Sigils work in the desert too, and Anemo can disperse loose sand. The Magic Bottle jinni predicts approaching sandstorms.

## quests
### LARGE
Chapter III Act IV, "King Deshret and the Three Magi", takes the Traveler, Cyno and Dehya to Aaru Village and Caravan Ribat investigating missing Village Keepers and a false claim that King Deshret is resurrected; Act V gathers the desert allies. Desert world quests such as "Secret of the Scorching Desert" and the Hadramaveth series (3.4: "Wisdom Has Built Her House, She Has Hewn Out Her Seven Pillars", "The Dirge of Bilqis") expand the story of Deshret's kingdom, Gurabad and the Tanit, and "Homecoming" (3.6) brings the Traveler to Apep at Mt. Damavand. In 6.6 the Archon Quest returns: "As All Falls to Emptiness" (Song of the Welkin Moon Act IX) and "Truth Amongst the Pages of Purana" (Act X) move Sumeru's people into the desert, reawaken Liloupar at Safhe Shatranj, and open Aaru from the Mausoleum to take over from Irminsul.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the Great Red Sand has come through the Irminsul crisis of the 6.6 Archon Quest. During it the rainforest's people were evacuated to Caravan Ribat and on to Aaru Village, Jinn sandstorms besieged the desert until the reawakened Jinni Liloupar calmed them, and the Seven Pillars opened Aaru, whose reconfigured system now collects and stores the information Irminsul once held. The Tanit tribe remains destroyed.
### STANDARD +
Opening Aaru freed Heryshaf, Sobek and Thoth from their imprisonment (Nefer answered Thoth's three questions) and released the countless souls of the Golden Slumber to the ley lines, ending King Deshret's afterlife. Afterwards Thoth and Heryshaf chose to stay at the Temple of Silence for the time being, and Liloupar later faded away at Windrise in Mondstadt after absorbing Collei's god remains. Separately, a 5.0 patch adjusted terrain in the Desert of Hadramaveth, and the 6.5 Temple of Space includes a Desert Pavilion built in the style of the desert temples; neither changes the desert's story.
### ULTRA +
Aaru Village still shelters its Village Keepers and refugees. In 6.6 Tighnari, Collei and Dori defended it against Jinn attacks until Collei, guided by Nicole's voice, recited a prayer written by Nabu Malikata that made the Jinn submit. During the same crisis a Fatui detachment led by Pantalone, disguised as a subordinate of Dottore, crossed the desert and trapped the Traveler inside desert ruins.

## entry: King Deshret and the Three Eternal Companion-Gods
@keys: King Deshret, Al-Ahmar, Lord of Deserts, Three Eternal Companion-Gods, Amun
@version: 3.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
King Deshret, the Lord of Deserts, also called Al-Ahmar and Amun, ruled the desert alongside Greater Lord Rukkhadevata and Nabu Malikata, the Goddess of Flowers, as the Three Eternal Companion-Gods. Each held a different realm: banquet, authority and life. He loved Nabu Malikata. When he pursued forbidden knowledge to defy the Heavenly Principles, Rukkhadevata left; Nabu Malikata ultimately gave her life for the ritual. Mourning, Deshret became the Mourning King, secluded himself, and built the Primal Constructs and the mechanism at Khaj-Nisut in pursuit of an eternal "golden slumber". He is said to have separated his mind from his body, leaving a golden husk in the mausoleum.
### LARGE +
Deshret refused a "gift" from Celestia (implied to be a Gnosis). He divided his power among retainers, the Seven Pillars: Heryshaf (his deputy and chief of the Pillars), Sobek and Thoth (with Heryshaf the "Three Retainers"), the sage Hermanubis, the constructs Bennu and Shesepankh, and Apep, who never accepted the title. He later banished Heryshaf, Sobek and Thoth to Aaru. Remus, a god whose homeland Deshret conquered, refused to serve him and left Sumeru. He ended his own life to stop the disaster, and Apep consumed his body, which corrupted her. Apep believes he planned it as a fail-safe.

## entry: Gurabad and Liloupar
@keys: Gurabad, City of Brass, City of Kings, Liloupar, Shiruyeh, Ormazd, Parvezravan
@version: 3.4
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Gurabad, the "city of brass" or "City of Kings", was a vassal city in the southwest of the Desert of Hadramaveth. A shepherd, Ormazd, united the tribes with the help of Deshret and the Jinni Liloupar, but he grew tyrannical and kept slavery. Liloupar plotted his city's fall across generations. Her daughter Shirin, tricked, served poisoned honey that killed Ormazd and Shirin's siblings; her son Kisra became King Parvezravan, forced Shirin to make mindless Jinn to power machines that replaced slave labour, and faced the Brass Mask rebellion led by his own son, Shiruyeh. Liloupar used Abyssal power and most of the city sank into a chasm; a plague then killed half the survivors.
### LARGE +
The Council of Nobles refused to fund infrastructure while desertification, shrinking oases and poverty (in Panjvahe, for example) fed unrest, and the clergy blamed lack of faith in Deshret. The revolt lasted thirteen days. Later rulers rebuilt Gurabad to claim the title "king of kings". The plague that followed was called "Shiruyeh's Plague", and the name Shiruyeh came to be associated with cowards and exiles.

## entry: Aaru Village
@keys: Aaru Village, Village Keepers, Uncle Anpu, Abdju Pit
@version: 3.1
### STANDARD
Aaru Village is the largest human settlement in the desert, holding Deshret's followers and descendants and Akademiya exiles. It stands as the last embers of his civilisation, guarded by Candace and led by its chief, Uncle Anpu; its Village Keepers are exiled Akademiya scholars. The Old Blind Poet and others settled here to await his return. Caravan Ribat is the checkpoint on the road to the forest.
### LARGE +
It is built around an old border fortress from the time of Gurabad. Refugees tired of the warring desert kingdoms, led by a blind heir of Tulaytullah (the Old Blind Poet), renounced kings and founded it, naming it after Aaru, the "ancient dream of the gods"; Candace says its earliest people came from Deshret's city of Akhtamun. In Chapter III, radicals exploit rumours that King Deshret is returning to abduct the Village Keepers. In 6.6 it housed evacuees from the rainforest during the Irminsul crisis.

## entry: The Tanit and the Tanit Camps
@keys: Tanit, Tanit Camps, Babel Tanit, Matriarch Babel
@version: 3.4
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
The Tanit were a nomadic Eremite tribe, led by a matriarch and Nine Elders, who dwelled in Tanit Camps in the south of the Desert of Hadramaveth. They wandered the desert, mistrusted the Akasha and relied on smuggled Akademiya books. Their enemies were traitors, not outsiders. They also raised captured children as Falcons. Late in their story Babel allied with the Fatui to remove rival elders and, once Jeht and the Traveler found the Orchard of Pairidaeza, tried to kill the Traveler to claim the Eternal Oasis. Informed of the betrayal, Jeht and the Traveler destroyed the tribe, leaving only a few survivors. The Vahumana researcher Tanjiyy later found the camp empty.

## entry: Apep and Mt. Damavand
@keys: Apep, Mt. Damavand, Dendro Dragon, Heart of Oasis
@version: 3.4
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
Apep, the Dendro Dragon Sovereign, once ruled Sumeru and nurtured the first elemental lifeforms in her inner world, the Heart of Oasis. Her pursuit of forbidden knowledge to fight the Heavenly Principles contaminated her and her creatures; Tumors of the Withering are those lifeforms at the end of their lives. She holds court beneath the Desert of Hadramaveth and, in the quest "Homecoming", appears at Mt. Damavand. After Kusanali purged forbidden knowledge, a void remained; the Traveler and Nahida helped restore the Heart of Oasis, and Apep chose to rest and watch. In 6.6 she sheltered the unconscious Nahida in the Heart of Oasis, sharing her Dendro energy, and later returned her to the Mausoleum of King Deshret.

## entry: Eremite Spirits and Legends
@keys: Eremites, Spirit of Omen, Rukh Shah, Corps of Thirty
@version: 3.0
### STANDARD
The Eremites are Sumeru's only authorised mercenary organisation. They hail from the fallen civilisations of the Great Red Sand and are very loosely organised: anyone can join, though traditionally only desert folk could. Rukh Shah is one exception. Some borrow power from fragments of ancient desert spirits that live in their weapons; the spirits wake in danger and, it is said, demand blood. They work all over Teyvat and partner with the Adventurers' Guild.

## entry: Khemenu Temple and the Ruin Devices
@keys: Khemenu Temple, Scarlet Sand Slate, Primal Beam, Everlight Cell, Plinth of the Secret Rites
@version: 3.1
### LARGE
Deshret's temples are guarded by ruin devices: Everlight and Neverlight Cells, Primal Beams, fan blades, sandglasses, plinths and barriers, each requiring a clearance level from the Scarlet Sand Slate, a tablet that unlocks Deshret's technology.
