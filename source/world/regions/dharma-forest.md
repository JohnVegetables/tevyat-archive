## summary
### LITE
Dharma Forest is the rainforest half of Sumeru: a green, humid, many-layered country of giant trees, mushrooms, rivers and rain, in the east and centre of the nation. Sumeru City, the "City of Wisdom", is built on a vast tree here and houses the Sumeru Akademiya. Forest Rangers patrol the woods, merchants and students crowd Port Ormos, and the small plant-like Aranara live out of sight in the dreams of children. Its great danger is the Withering, a rot that spreads through the land. The desert lies beyond the Wall of Samiel to the west.
### STANDARD +
Travellers from Liyue can reach it directly from the Chasm, and Avidya Forest is the first area the Traveler enters in Sumeru. The forest is Dendro country: the nation's Archon, Lesser Lord Kusanali (Nahida), is the God of Wisdom, and the plants, fungi and "dream" magic here all reflect that.

## geography
### STANDARD
Seven areas make up the subregion: Avidya Forest (the heart, with Sumeru City and Gandharva Ville), Ardravi Valley (the great river and its trade route), Ashavan Realm (the southern wetlands and hills), Lokapala Jungle (the north coast), Vissudha Field, Vanarana and the small Lost Nursery. The Wall of Samiel separates the forest from the Great Red Sand on the west. The river Ardravi cut the valley that carries Sumeru's main waterborne trade down to the southern shore.
### LARGE +
Ashavan Realm is described as a materially rich southern land of hills, woods and wetland, with mighty ramparts guarding its western edge from the sandstorms. Lokapala Jungle runs along the north coast from Mawtiyima Forest, near Chenyu Vale in Liyue, to the slope of a nameless mountain west of Bayda Harbor, where it meets the Desert of Hadramaveth and the Realm of Farakhkert. The forest's flora includes Padisarah, Nilotpala lotus, Kalpalata lotus and Rukkhashava mushrooms, and fungi of every kind roam the undergrowth.

## places
### STANDARD
Sumeru City sits on the Divine Tree, with the Akademiya in the middle and upper part of the tree and the Sanctuary of Surasthana, where Nahida lives, on top. Gandharva Ville, "the City Above the Forest", is the Forest Rangers' main outpost. Port Ormos is the largest port in Sumeru, a trade hub under two giant trees; Vimara Village is a quiet fishing town on the river between the two. Caravan Ribat is a fortified village at the foot of the Wall of Samiel, the checkpoint for everyone going to or from the desert and Aaru Village. Apam Woods is a rainforest where the rain never fully ceases; Pardis Dhyai is a garden research site of Amurta scholars.
### LARGE +
Devantaka Mountain holds a huge Ruin Golem that the legendary scholar Pir Kavikavus once studied, with a terrible accident as the result; the Akasha has no signal on the mountain. Mawtiyima Forest is a forest of enormous glowing mushrooms; Mawtiyima is described as an energy form born of the forest's ancient dreams that fights the Withering, and in an ancient tongue the name once meant "human death". The Palace of Alcazarzaray in the Lokapala Jungle belongs to Dori and was designed by Kaveh. The Fane of Ashvattha in Vissudha Field shelters the first Vasara Tree and seals the way down to Old Vanarana. Ruins of Dahri are Khaenri'ahn remnants in Ashavan Realm.
### ULTRA +
Other named subareas are Chinvat Ravine, Gandha Hill, Yazadaha Pool, Yasna Monument, Bayda Harbor, Chatrakam Cave, Old Vanarana and Vanarana (the real Vanarana and its dream form Mahavanaranapna). The Lost Nursery is unlocked only by finishing the Aranyaka quest series' third part; before that, Paimon turns the Traveler back.

## people
### STANDARD
Forest Rangers (the civil-service guardians of the forest; the senior ones are Forest Watchers, among them Tighnari) patrol the woods and report to Pardis Dhyai's researchers. Students and scholars of the Akademiya fill Sumeru City and visit Port Ormos when academic life gets too stressful. Eremite mercenaries from the desert work in Port Ormos and Caravan Ribat. Traders, dockworkers and fishers fill the river towns. Children are the ones who see the Aranara.
### LARGE +
Notable residents: Tighnari (Forest Watcher, Chief Officer) and Collei (Forest Ranger) at Gandharva Ville; the architect Kaveh, who designed Port Ormos's arch bridge and the Palace of Alcazarzaray; Dori, the merchant who owns the palace. The Rishboland Tigers, the forest's great cats, have inherited the "tiger" name of the lords of the forest, a line that began with Viaghara and whose last lord died defending the forest in the cataclysm.

## governance
### LARGE
Authority rests with the Sumeru Akademiya, whose darshans (colleges) run the city and manage the forest's affairs, with the Forest Rangers as the field service and the Corps of Thirty and General Mahamatra supplying order. Nahida is the Archon, kept for a long time in the Sanctuary of Surasthana and treated as a distant figurehead by the sages.
### ULTRA +
The Akademiya also maintains the Wall of Samiel, and destroying it is prohibited. In the Archon Quest the sages used the Akasha, a network that connects minds, to control the populace and Nahida; by the end of the chapter Nahida had taken the Akasha back for herself and cut off the sages' access. By the 6.6 crisis a new council of sages served alongside Naphis: Baladhuri (Vahumana), Alkindi (Spantamad), Firnas (Kshahrewar), Shakir (Rtawahist) and Rushd (Haravatat).

## culture
### LARGE
Sumeru's ideal is wisdom, and the forest shows it in its schooling, theatres and rapt debates. The Sabzeruz Festival, Lesser Lord Kusanali's birthday celebration, was revived in the Archon Quest; the Akademiya Extravaganza is the Akademiya's festival of an annual Wisdom Gala and a four-yearly Interdarshan Championship (seen in the 3.6 limited-time event A Parade of Providence). Folk belief holds the Aranara are the imaginary friends of children; adults mostly dismiss them. Student life and bazaar bargaining colour the towns.
### ULTRA +
Desert mercenary culture, the Eremites, is visible at the edges, at Caravan Ribat and in Port Ormos. Place names draw on Sanskrit and Persian vocabulary (dharma, avidya, ardravi, vanarana and so on).

## history
### STANDARD
By the wiki's reconstruction, the forest was once the domain of the Dendro Dragon Apep's court and the cradle of the first fern that grew into the Irminsul. After the Archon War era, Greater Lord Rukkhadevata left her friend King Deshret's desert kingdom, came to the east of the Great Red Sand and used the Varuna Contraption to turn desert into rainforest. She raised the Wall of Samiel against sandstorms, and the forest king Viaghara was born. Her followers became the founders of the Amurta darshan of the Akademiya; Sumeru City was built on the Divine Tree. She also created the Aranara.
### LARGE +
Thousands of years ago she returned to the desert to help when King Deshret's forbidden knowledge poisoned his people, created the Akasha to unite human wisdom against it, and spent so much power that she shrank to a child's stature. Five hundred years ago, in the cataclysm, forbidden knowledge contaminated the Irminsul and Rukkhadevata herself. Lesser Lord Kusanali succeeded her, the last lord of the forest in Viaghara's line died protecting the forest, and the Aranara withdrew to dreams as the people of Sumeru stopped dreaming. Old Vanarana had been the Aranara's home for hundreds of years before then.
### ULTRA +
More than 400 years ago the scholar Zandik studied at the Akademiya before the Fatui took him as the Harbinger Dottore. Roughly the same time, the Temple of Silence left the rainforest for the desert in protest at the sages' corruption. Faruzan, trapped for a century in a ruin of Deshret's civilisation, was found in the forest. Kaveh's first palace at Lokapala was destroyed overnight by the Withering at 70 per cent completion; the Forest Rangers quickly dispersed the Withering, but the buildings could not be saved.

In 6.6 (Song of the Welkin Moon, Acts IX-X) Irminsul stopped recording properly because of Dottore's meddling. Nahida and the sages evacuated the whole rainforest (Sumeru City, Lokapala Jungle, Avidya Forest, Ashavan Realm and Ardravi Valley) to Caravan Ribat and then on to Aaru Village; civilians who tried to return were driven mad by the anomaly near Yasna Monument and killed one another. Silver branches burst from the rainforest floor into a colossal Irminsul tree tied to Dottore. The people of Sumeru overloaded Irminsul through the Akasha, the Traveler and Wanderer infiltrated it via the Divine Tree, and Nahida burned the corrupted Irminsul with the Pyro Gnosis obtained from Mavuika, ending Dottore; the reconfigured Aaru took over Irminsul's role. Afterwards the rainforest's people returned and began rebuilding.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Humid green light through giant leaves, rain on broad canopies, glowing mushrooms and petals at night, the murmur of rivers, bazaar bustle in the city. It feels alive and a little dreamlike; in places there is a quiet wrongness where the Withering has rotted the plants.
### ULTRA +
Mawtiyima's fluorescent fungi and the flower gardens of Pardis Dhyai give night scenes a glow, and the dream version of Vanarana changes the light entirely.

## dangers
### LARGE
The Withering: zones of decaying land with Fetid Boughs, Withering Branches and enemies that hit with Decay, which saps the party's strength and can kill. Fungi and Ruin Machines roam the woods, and hilichurls, Eremite bands, Treasure Hoarders and Fatui agents are found in the forest. The Rishboland Tiger, Spinocrocodiles and Shaggy Sumpter Beasts are real wildlife dangers.
### ULTRA +
The worst Withering zones are Old Vanarana and the cave in Mawtiyima Forest, where decay accumulates by itself. Eleazar, the Withering's manifestation in humans and once endemic to Sumeru, vanished after Nahida removed Rukkhadevata from Irminsul in Chapter III, but Withering Zones that already existed remain. Aeonblight Drakes, powerful boss-class monsters, are found in Ardravi Valley and around Devantaka Mountain.

## travel
### LARGE
The forest is reached from Liyue by a tunnel from the Chasm's Cinnabar Cliff, or by river and sea at Port Ormos. The river carries boats between Sumeru City and Port Ormos, and plant-based Four-Leaf Sigils let travellers cross difficult ground quickly. The Wall of Samiel and Caravan Ribat gate the road to the desert.
### ULTRA +
In the Apam Woods the Varuna Contraption can be operated, once its quest is done, to start or stop the rain and to lower the water for access. The Vintage Lyre plays Aranara songs that activate Silapna (including the dream/real switch for Vanarana) and open Gloomy Paths.

## entry: Avidya Forest and Sumeru City
@keys: Avidya Forest, Sumeru City, City of Wisdom, Divine Tree
@version: 3.0
### STANDARD
Avidya Forest is the most populous part of Sumeru apart from Ardravi Valley and the first area the Traveler enters. Sumeru City, the "City of Wisdom", is built on a gigantic tree, the Divine Tree, with its Grand Bazaar, Zubayr Theater and Treasures Street below the Akademiya, which occupies the middle and upper part of the tree. On top is the Sanctuary of Surasthana, a treetop palace and the Archon's home. Gandharva Ville, the Rangers' outpost, lies in the forest nearby.
### LARGE +
The name Avidya is Sanskrit for "unwisdom", the opposite of vidya (wisdom), a pointed name for the home of the Akademiya. Local flora include Padisarah and Rukkhashava mushrooms, and the forest is home to shroomboars and countless fungi.

## entry: The Withering
@keys: Withering, Withering Zone, Fetid Bough, Tumor of the Withering
@version: 3.0
### STANDARD
The Withering is a blight that causes Withering Zones where land and life decay. Within one the party gains Decay, reducing resistances and Max HP, and can fall if it reaches the limit. Dendrograna (Dendro-charged seeds) destroy its Withering Branches, and defeating the enemies from the Tumor of the Withering clears the zone. After clearing one in Chapter III, Tighnari voices concern at how quickly new zones are appearing and how they worsen Eleazar.
### LARGE +
According to the lore recounted on Apep's page, Tumors of the Withering are the form Apep's elemental lifeforms take at the end of their lives because of the torment of forbidden knowledge. King Deshret's forbidden-knowledge disaster brought the Withering into being, and Eleazar is its manifestation in humans. Kusanali purged the forbidden knowledge in Chapter III, which left a void in Apep that the Traveler and Nahida later helped to heal.

## entry: Aranara and Vanarana
@keys: Aranara, Vanarana, Mahavanaranapna, Ashvattha, Vasara Tree, Queen Aranyani
@version: 3.0
### STANDARD
The Aranara are small, plant-like forest spirits created by Greater Lord Rukkhadevata (whom they called Queen Aranyani) to guard the forest. They hide from adult eyes but befriend children; they love memories, fear fire and salt, and when in grave danger turn to seed. Their homeland, Vanarana, exists both in reality and as a dream place, Mahavanaranapna. At life's end an Aranara grows into a Vasara Tree.
### LARGE +
After the cataclysm the Aranara withdrew; they had lived beside humans before the people of Sumeru stopped dreaming. They hold that adults are those who have "grown up". The Fane of Ashvattha holds the first Vasara Tree, the progenitor of the Aranara. After Kusanali erased Rukkhadevata's memory from the Irminsul, the Aranara forgot Queen Aranyani and now regard the current Dendro Archon as the Lord of Verdure.
### ULTRA +
In the last war (the cataclysm) the Aranara crushed Marana's Avatar and imprisoned it deep in Old Vanarana with four seals, and Aramuhukunda became a Vasara Tree there to hold it down; in Nursery of Lost Dreams the Traveler and Arama destroy it. Three other Aranara of that era, Aramaha, Arayama and Araji, sealed the three Ashvattha's Concourses with their own rhythms and became Vasara Trees.

## entry: Forest Rangers and Gandharva Ville
@keys: Forest Rangers, Forest Watchers, Vanara, Gandharva Ville
@version: 3.0
### STANDARD
The Forest Rangers (also called Vanara) are civil servants who protect the forests of Sumeru: they help travellers, suppress Withering Zones and monitor beast populations. Higher-ranking members are Forest Watchers and wear lighter green. Their main outpost, Gandharva Ville, is "the City Above the Forest". They send regular reports to the researchers of Pardis Dhyai, who supply seeds and nutrients in return.
### LARGE +
Tighnari is Forest Watcher and Chief Officer, Amir trains search-and-rescue dogs, Ashpazi runs logistics and cooks, Shirin is the doctor and Collei is a Ranger. According to Rana, the earliest Rangers were royal guards of the forest king Viaghara.

## entry: Port Ormos and the Ardravi
@keys: Port Ormos, Ardravi, Vimara Village, Djafar Tavern
@version: 3.0
### STANDARD
Port Ormos is Sumeru's largest port and a hub for trade and travel, beneath two giant trees with a river running to Sumeru City. Parts of it are under Akademiya supervision, yet smuggling and illegal trade are more common here than in the city and prices are lower. Most long-term residents are traders and labourers who grew up there; Eremite mercenaries from several groups work here too, and students come to relax. Kaveh designed its arch bridge.
### LARGE +
The Ardravi valley was formed by the erosion of the great river and is Sumeru's great waterborne mercantile route, kept pure by the Forest Watchers. Vimara Village, a quiet fishing town, lies on the river between the port and the city.

## entry: Wall of Samiel and Caravan Ribat
@keys: Wall of Samiel, Caravan Ribat
@version: 3.0
### STANDARD
The Wall of Samiel separates the rainforest from the Great Red Sand, supposedly built by Greater Lord Rukkhadevata to keep sandstorms from ruining the forest. The Akademiya maintains it and forbids damaging it. Caravan Ribat, full of Eremites, is a fortified village at its foot and the checkpoint for travel to the desert and Aaru Village.
### LARGE +
Official map text says the wall that stops the sandstorms has also blocked the spread of wisdom's blessing to the desert. After the Chapter III events Dehya notes the Akademiya has taken a kinder view of desert folk and hopes the Wall of Samiel will from now on mark only where the sandstorms end, not where her people's hopes and dreams end.

## entry: Rukkhadevata's Rainforest
@keys: Varuna Contraption, Viaghara, Rishboland Tiger, Amurta
@version: 3.0
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
Per the wiki's tentative chronology, Greater Lord Rukkhadevata parted ways with King Deshret and Nabu Malikata and came to the east of the Great Red Sand. She created the Varuna Contraption to terraform part of the desert into rainforest, raised the Wall of Samiel and, after the forest labyrinth was made, Viaghara was born from the moon's reflection on the water in the shape of a tiger. Her followers founded the Amurta darshan, and the sages of the Akademiya helped her manage the nation. Viaghara's successors kept the title of lord of the forest; the last of them died defending the forest when the cataclysm struck, and the Rishboland Tigers have inherited the "tiger" name since.
