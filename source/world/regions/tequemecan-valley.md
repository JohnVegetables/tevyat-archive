## summary
### LITE
Tequemecan Valley is the mining country of eastern Natlan and the home of the Children of Echoes (Nanatzcayan), the tribe of miners, gem appraisers and craftspeople who live in a gorge with their burrowing Tepetlisaur companions. Clanging hammers and tapping gem-hunters echo through the canyon, and dance battles are held daily. Beneath the valley the Shadow Pins keep the Abyss sealed, and the ruins of an ancient oath lie below its hills.
### STANDARD +
The name is Nahuatl: tetl is stone, quemitl a ritual chest garment, and -can marks a place. The valley's rock walls are canvases for graffiti, a tradition said to start with a sage who engraved Phlogiston into flowing lines.

## geography
### STANDARD
A canyon and gorge region in eastern Natlan with a settlement inside the gorge, mining tunnels, rocky slopes and underground ruins. Subareas include Sulfurous Veins (with the Land of Myriad Megaliths) and Tepeacac Rise. Local specialties are quenepa berries and saurian claw succulent.
### LARGE +
The Children of Echoes have dug so deep into the mountains that sunlight is scarce in places. The Sulfurous Veins are described in-game as pins of rock entwined with golden runes, and the Land of Myriad Megaliths as a stone chamber of shifting crystal pillars.

## places
### STANDARD
The Children of Echoes' settlement; Stage Tepetl, the grand dance-battle stage; Muhuru's Gemporium; the Mother Flamegranate Tree; Sulfurous Veins and the Land of Myriad Megaliths; the Hall of Parting Vows; Catastrophe's Edge; and the Ruins of the Sage of the Stolen Flame.
### LARGE +
Stage Tepetl has a DJ booth, fire-spewing props and a very sturdy frame. The Hall of Parting Vows is an underground ruin where Xbalanque and the Sage of the Stolen Flame swore a sacred oath. Glowing stairs below the hall lead to Catastrophe's Edge, and touching the inscription there takes the player to the Ruins of the Sage of the Stolen Flame. Tlazolli's Home on the settlement's outskirts can be entered only during the quest Melodious Chant.
### ULTRA +
Tepeacac Rise is described as lower slopes surprisingly good for plants and a frequent resting place for saurians, called the 'Genesis of the Mountains'. Its Local Legend (Sappho Amidst the Waves) leaves an unfinished musical score with a note about meeting after the last Pilgrimage.

## people
### STANDARD
Chief Pacal, whose Tepetlisaur companion is Uchu, leads the Children of Echoes. The playable Xilonen has her workshop at the tribe's settlement.
### LARGE +
Titu is the tribe's old storyteller. Traore, an elder and storyteller, is a former acting chief. The tribe fields 19 mining teams. The Scions of the Canopy post a guard at the Mother Flamegranate Tree, and Muhuru's Gemporium sells gems.

## culture
### STANDARD
Stone is one of the tribe's symbols. The Children of Echoes value gemstones highly, some calling them a second layer of skin, and decorate tools, weapons and accessories with them as a sign of skill and status. The tribe is the sole producer of obsidian, an important ceremonial material. They love rhythmic music and dance, especially breakdance.
### LARGE +
Dance competitions are constant: the Drillbit Dance Dance-Off has run daily for over 350 years, its tally standing at 127,751 rounds. The Salutary Furnace in the Stadium of the Sacred Flame is staffed by the tribe's best craftspeople.
### ULTRA +
The tribe was founded before the Grand Alliance by Ixquieh, the first bearer of the Ancient Name Baraka. Its chiefs are counted by number; Sundjatta was the 52nd and fell in the last battle against the Abyss five hundred years ago.

## history
@version: 5.0
### STANDARD
Natlan/History says that during the cataclysm, five hundred years ago, the Children of Echoes' acting chief Traore led the tribe against an Abyssal invasion in their territory. With help from two Khaenri'ahn warriors, Zephaniah and Irizar, they trapped the Abyss deep underground using the Shadow Pins. The Land of Myriad Megaliths is given as that underground place.
### LARGE +
The Shadow Pins are large stone pillars with smaller Lesser Shadow Pins nearby, and the old storyteller Titu says the giant pin once plunged beneath the earth in an earthquake. In the world quest Beneath the Crystal Rock, two of three Lesser Shadow Pins had recently floated up, a sign the seal was weakening. The Traveler and Titu repaired the pins and reinforced the seal on the Abyss.
### ULTRA +
In Revelations from the Past the Traveler, Paimon and the Little One enter the Hall of Parting Vows; a mural shows Xiuhcoatl's defeat, and visions show the annihilation of the Sacred City of Tollan and a giant dragon with pierced pupils falling with a tiny human. The trial leads down to Catastrophe's Edge and to a Golden Entreaty token left by the Sage of the Stolen Flame. The NPC Manqu says the Sage believed humans could guide and lead saurians toward a future brighter than the age of the giant dragons.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Hammers ringing off the canyon walls, dust in the tunnels, glittering crystals, thumping beats from Stage Tepetl and bright graffiti on the cliffs. The mood is rowdy and warm.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Rockfond Rifthounds and Abyss-corrupted Nightsoul variants of Forged Sand fighters and Tepetlisaurus warriors turn up in the underground ruins, and swiftstep couriers roam above. The boss is the Gluttonous Yumkasaur Mountain King, Kongamato.
### LARGE +
A boundary-eroding Abyss-corroded Golden Wolflord is the final trial at Catastrophe's Edge. Unstable Phlogiston crystals can be sensed by Tepetlisaurs and the tribe's playable characters, which makes tunnelling safer.

## travel
### LARGE
Tepetlisaurs can burrow through certain surfaces and enter Molten Fissures. A tribe member or Tepetlisaur indwelling lets one ascend Phlogiston crystals.

## quests
@version: 5.0
### LARGE
World quests include Beneath the Crystal Rock (the Shadows of the Mountains chapter, begun with Titu), Peace to the Slumbering, Ripe For Trouble (the Mother Flamegranate Tree and its Mountain King Flamegranates) and Revelations from the Past (Between Pledge and Forgettance chapter).

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the valley is quiet; the Shadow Pins hold, and the tribe's music and mining carry on.
### STANDARD +
The Abyss's assault on Natlan ended in 5.3. The Children of Echoes lost members in the earlier invasion, including Batz and Nechca.
### ULTRA +
The wiki lists no story changes at Tequemecan Valley in 6.x or 7.x.

## entry: Shadow Pins and the Land of Myriad Megaliths
@keys: Shadow Pin, Shadow Pins, Land of Myriad Megaliths, Lesser Shadow Pin
@version: 5.0
### STANDARD
Huge stone pillars in the Land of Myriad Megaliths, below Tequemecan Valley, hold the Abyss beneath the earth. Tribal tales say the Wayob hung them to pin the shadow of the 'Volcano Lord'; the storyteller Titu says what lies beneath is truly the Abyss. The Children of Echoes, with Khaenri'ahn help, set the seal five hundred years ago.
### LARGE +
The Lesser Shadow Pins have floated before, and the old way to deal with it is to repair the engraved inscriptions with their power.

## entry: Mother Flamegranate Tree
@keys: Mother Flamegranate Tree, Mountain King Flamegranate, Tisoc
@version: 5.0
### STANDARD
The source of the Scions of the Canopy's Flamegranates. A guard, Tisoc, stands at the entrance to the cave holding its roots and admits only Scions with special named passes. Its special roots thrive in lava rather than burning, and the fruit must grow near liquid Phlogiston to mature. Larger Flamegranates grow in the open on the cliffs; Mountain King Flamegranates, grown in the cave, are offered to Kongamato.
### LARGE +
Initially the tree is blue and its fruit unripe; after the quest Ripe For Trouble it turns orange and the fruit ripens.

## entry: Hall of Parting Vows
@keys: Hall of Parting Vows, Catastrophe's Edge, Sage of the Stolen Flame
@version: 5.0
### STANDARD
An underground ruin unlocked in Revelations from the Past. It is where Xbalanque and the Sage of the Stolen Flame swore their oath. Murals show Xiuhcoatl's defeat. Below the hall, Catastrophe's Edge holds a trial and the token the Sage left.
