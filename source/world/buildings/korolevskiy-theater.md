## summary
### LITE
The Korolevskiy Theater is the main theater of Snezhnograd, where the Korolevskiy Troupe stages ballet, opera and musical performances. It is favoured by the Fatui Harbingers and by Mitya, and it is the daily base of the prima ballerina Odette and the prima donna Vodyanitsa. Odette's Meeting Point is in the dressing room behind the stage.
### STANDARD +
In the Version 7.0 Archon Quest Wraith's Nocturne a snowstorm traps the Traveler and the audience in the theater, where people begin dying one by one.

## geography
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
The theater sits in Snezhnograd. The interior has a lobby with four purple sofas, an auditorium with 44 blue three-seat sofas for a capacity of 312, a stage, a dressing room behind it, an administrative room and a cellar that opens during Winter's Mysterious Silence.
### LARGE +
Security is tight: staff such as Kulish inspect every visitor at the entrance, since Snezhnaya's business and political leaders pass through.

## people
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
Odette (prima ballerina) and Vodyanitsa (prima donna) are the Troupe's stars. Staff in the Archon Quests include Kulish (entrance inspection) and Dymov; the theater's NPCs inside include Arkady, Istomina, Ksenia, Leanid, Lev, Marat, Olenina and Taisia, and the wider area has Balkonov, Osipova, Pisemsky and others. Chugunov, a factory owner, attends performances.
### LARGE +
Valeriy comes looking for Mitya, who hides in the theater when he should be at the Armory Palace. After the incident, dialogues may include Tartaglia, Vesna, Alyosha, Mitya and Arlecchino and Sandrone in the open world.

## culture
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
The official description says the relentless toil backstage is secondary to giving the audience a momentary escape into a beautiful illusion. The Troupe's repertoire includes the classic Snezhnayan ballet 'Spirit Seeker' and the new 'Black Snow Swan', a test of dancers' technique and emotional understanding.
### LARGE +
Odette's schedule collides with the Fatui missions she insists on taking; Vodyanitsa nags her to rest.

## atmosphere
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
A well-guarded public theater: visitors queue for ticket and security checks, wealthy patrons like Chugunov grumble at latecomers, and a performance leaves the audience entranced. During the afterlife incident the same hall turns into shelter against a relentless storm, where people sleep wherever they find space, eat the food Vodyanitsa hands out and gather on the stage each night.

## history
### STANDARD
The wiki speculates the name comes from Nepla Korolevitsna, the late wife of the fae Filipov, but this is not confirmed.
### LARGE +
Opening night of 'Spirit Seeker' in Wraith's Nocturne brings a rehearsal and group photo, after which a blizzard prevents anyone leaving. Over several days the staff and audience die in pairs, then singly, Mitya's reasoning leading to the conclusion that everyone is dead and living in an afterlife theater, an echo of Ronova's Curse of Death. When Ronova's disguise is destroyed, everyone wakes in the real world, with the theater's Ley Lines letting people keep memories.
### ULTRA +
Night by night: Soboleva and Torgov die first, then Kulish and Rampana, then Valeriy on night duty; each time the Traveler dreams of a spiral staircase and a pursuing black mist beforehand. Chugunov accuses Lelek, and Odette answers by revealing on stage that she is Lelek. Shram turns out to be a ghost who died in the theater long ago protecting his girlfriend, whose name he withholds; he leaves after Vodyanitsa tells him about her later life. The girl who called herself Tonia was Ptashka, who recalled being ill with Frostblight and taken to the theater by her brother; Chugunov had realised and projected his own dead daughter Iskra onto her. Finally Vodyanitsa dies, and Mitya returns through her Requiem with the conclusion that everyone is dead.

## quests
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
Archon Quest: Silence Alone is Disfavored (Alyosha brings the Traveler to a performance and then to the greenroom), and Wraith's Nocturne (The White Curtain Falls, Lelek's Confession, Winter's Mysterious Silence, Wraith's Nocturne, Mistilteinn). ### LARGE +
Variations on Belyi and Chernyi has the Traveler help schedule Odette's days before the 'Black Snow Swan' premiere. In 7.1 One Move Left to Make brings a captured suspect, dragged back from Central Station, to the theater.

## status
@version: 7.1
### STANDARD
Open and running performances. Odette's Meeting Point is in the dressing room, and open-world dialogue with the Troupe and visitors unlocks after Mistilteinn.

## entry: The Afterlife Theater Incident
@keys: afterlife theater, Wraith's Nocturne, Spirit Seeker, theater murders
@version: 7.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
During a rehearsal of 'Spirit Seeker', Ronova's Curse of Death kills everyone in the theater and gathers them in an afterlife theater, where they believe a snowstorm keeps them inside. The dead appear to be murdered in pairs during the night. The Traveler learns that Mitya, Shram (a ghost from the past), and a girl calling herself Tonia, really named Ptashka, are among the dead, and that the extra person in the staff logbook was Shram. Odette admits she is Lelek, the Rokot's infamous member.
### LARGE +
The incident ends when Mitya and the Traveler unmask Ronova, who had taken on the form of Alyosha. Everyone awakens, though Mitya's carved symbol on his soul lets him remember. Chugunov says that he will shut down his factory.

## entry: Variations on Belyi and Chernyi
@keys: Black Snow Swan, Variations on Belyi and Chernyi
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
A world quest in the theater in which the Traveler helps plan Odette's schedule for two weeks before the Black Snow Swan premiere. Vodyanitsa complains that Odette keeps taking Fatui monster-slaying missions during rehearsal time.
### LARGE +
Odette picks the Traveler because they proved trustworthy while trapped together in the theater, and because they know how the Fatui operate; Vodyanitsa objects that she has known Odette far longer.
