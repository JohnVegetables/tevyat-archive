## summary
### LITE
A subarea of the Ancient Sacred Mountain in Natlan with large pools of Liquid Phlogiston. The ancient dragons once submerged themselves in them to take in the knowledge and wisdom stored in the primal flame.
### STANDARD +
The Chinese name means roughly Sea of Divergent Sentience, and the area has a viewpoint description titled Fiery Flowing Sea of Sentience.

## places
### STANDARD
The pools are the area's main feature. One of the Iridescent Legacy collectibles (Part IV) is found here.

## culture
### LARGE
The pools tie the dragons' wisdom to the Sacred Flame and its phlogiston, according to the wiki description.

## atmosphere
### ULTRA
Large pools of Liquid Phlogiston fill the chamber; this is a scene suggestion, not extra canon.

## status
### LITE
Accessible as of 7.1.
