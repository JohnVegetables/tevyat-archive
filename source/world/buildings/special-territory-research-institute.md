## summary
### LITE
The Special Territory Research Institute is a Fatui research facility on Ashveil Peak, in the far north of Nod-Krai. It is Dottore's base during Song of the Welkin Moon, and secrets lie deep beneath its foundations. After the story it becomes the False Moon Institute, a domain where players can replay the fight with the Heretic of the False Moon.
### STANDARD +
Ashveil Peak is part of Pohjola, the northernmost part of that island. In the domain's text, the facility "stands upon the northern reaches of Nod-Krai" and conceals secrets "as deep and uncharted as the structures buried far beneath its foundations." The same text says that at dusk memories of a battle that tore the sky apart linger, and that the moon has at last returned home.

## geography
### STANDARD
It lies on Ashveil Peak, the northernmost part of Pohjola, in the far north of Nod-Krai, north of Voidsea Outlook and Paha Isle where the Fatui's Kuuvahki Experimental Design Bureau stands. The wiki lists it as a subarea of Ashveil Peak, and the only listed feature is the False Moon Institute domain.
### LARGE +
On the approach, the party finds wrecked Landcruisers scorched by Arlecchino's flames and the bodies of slain Fatui soldiers, with monsters in the corridors beyond.

## people
### STANDARD
Dottore, the Fatui Harbinger known as the Doctor, treats it as his lair. A Fatui soldier named Jotautas is found inside; he had served in the unit assigned to Columbina in Nod-Krai, then came under Dottore's command when she left the Fatui. Sandrone and Arlecchino, also Harbingers, go to the facility before the Traveler's party.
### LARGE +
Jotautas tells the party that Dottore had been preparing something ambitious for a long time, that he had come back with two gemstones that Lauma recognizes as the Moon Marrows taken from Columbina, and that her former unit learned he was trying to make an artificial Moon Marrow. He declines to rejoin Lauma, saying his loyalty is to the Tsaritsa, and the Traveler sends him to the Design Bureau where Sandrone's unit is.

## history
### STANDARD
After Columbina banished herself into the moon's reflection and Dottore lost control of the powers of the Three Moons that he had gained on Moon-Prayer Night, the wiki's account says he retreated to this facility. He later made an artificial Moon Marrow to replace Columbina's, and the Traveler's group returns to confront him there in Act VII of Song of the Welkin Moon.
### LARGE +
Dottore meets them at the heart of the facility, having expected them. He says Arlecchino "disappeared" when she entered and that Sandrone confronted him; he describes his ascension to godhood as fact, not claim, and says he is sending his people to Sandrone's Design Bureau. The group fights him, and Arlecchino joins mid-battle, but he restores his injuries with the artificial Moon Marrow. Arlecchino tells the others to leave while she faces him; instead Dottore captures the Traveler and teleports the rest away.
### ULTRA +
Afterward Dottore moves against Nasha Town as the Heretic of the False Moon, the fight the domain replays. The domain text says the heretic who sought godhood through others' suffering met his twilight at the hands of those who stood together to protect their home. The Ley Line Disorder describes the heretic using the authority of the Three Moons' marrow to bend the world's order, set prohibitions and use a rift from beyond the world, so that players must follow them for a short time or be punished.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Fatui soldiers, Landcruisers and monsters in the approach; Dottore's own traps and spaces. In domain form, the Heretic of the False Moon is a weekly boss whose rewards include the Mask of the Virtuous Doctor, Madman's Restraint and Elixir of the Heretic, with recommended levels of 60 to 90.

## quests
### STANDARD
Act VII, A Traveler on a Winter's Night (Version 6.3), has the party fighting Dottore here. The domain False Moon Institute unlocks after completing Welkin Moon's Homecoming.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the Institute is a replayable domain, and the story treats the moon as returned to its place in the sky.

### STANDARD
Dottore made an artificial Moon Marrow to substitute for Columbina after she banished herself from his control, and with it he could simply restore his injuries during the fight at the facility.
