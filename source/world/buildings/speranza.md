## summary
### LITE
Speranza is the reasonably priced restaurant that retired bounty hunter Katya runs in Nasha Town, Nod-Krai. It is the main eatery of the region, popular with ordinary residents and treasure hunters, and the Conch Gang, the young staff and strays Katya looks after, treat it as their home base.
### STANDARD +
The in-game map text calls it Katya's reasonably priced eatery and the Conch Gang's unofficial headquarters when they are not helping out. Speranza is also one of the powers with influence over Nasha Town, which has no official governing body: the others the wiki names are the Voynich Guild, the Curatorium of Secrets and the Fatui. The name means "hope" in Italian.

## people
### LITE
Katya, the Boss of Speranza, and the Conch Gang children work here.
### STANDARD
Katya is a former adventurer of the Treasure Hoarders who retired after injuries. She takes in children who end up on the street, gives them jobs, food and housing, and lets them live as freely as they like. Staff include Jurva, called the super chef, and the hosts Maisie and Mikko. Other regulars are Bedriskaya and Borzyslaw, and the dog Pavlov. Three Knights of Favonius, Arved, Helmut and Silja, are listed among its NPCs.
### LARGE +
Aino was once one of Katya's wards. Katya's own account, in her shop dialogue, is that she could not bear to see the children left to fend for themselves, so they work for their share of the food. The children treat her with affection: Maisie and the others look forward to Helka's return from her travels, and Maite once told the Traveler that mentioning her name might get a free drink if Katya is in a good mood.
### ULTRA +
Katya was also an old friend of Frith, and she says she has not been in the treasure-hunting business for a long time; she tells visitors who want news to ask at the Flagship or the children, but warns them not to involve the children in anything dangerous.

## places
### STANDARD
A restaurant in Nasha Town, marked on the map with a restaurant icon. Katya sells food, recipes and ingredients: Nod-Krai Hot Dog, Lakkaberry Madame, Midsommar Torte and Crispy Roast daily, plus weekly basics such as flour, cream and sausage. After the Archon Quest Rest Beyond Reach, the shop gives a permanent 10 percent discount.
### LARGE +
Outside is the "Lucky Spin!" wheel, with a childishly written notice saying that after the wheel stops, "Hot Dog" will tell the visitor's fortune. The wheel yields one of eight proverb-like fortunes, from lucky windfalls to warnings of obstacles. Pavlov, a dog, uses the wheel to ask for food and can be understood only by those who hear animals; Columbina and Lauma, when active, talk to him and offer him snacks.

## history
### STANDARD
Per one story told by Jurva, a group of children once "conquered" Nasha Town by blowing a conch to call gatherings, which is how the gang got its name; the town later grew into a bustling settlement and the gang no longer dominated it. In Aino's character story, the Snezhnayan mechanic Papa Wrench ate at Speranza, saw the children, and with Katya's approval taught a small class on machines in the restaurant's downtime, which is how Aino began her training.
### LARGE +
In Aino's story, the smoke from Speranza drifted away and the Conch Gang's laughter faded when she left for the craftshop. Katya later saw her come in with Ineffa to buy sweets, including two Midsommar Tortes.

## quests
### STANDARD
After the world quest Priorities First (Version 6.0), a white Weasel Thief that stole Paimon's Mora is found outside Speranza, surrounded by Maisie, Mikko and Jurva, who pin it down; the Mora is returned, and the children discuss what to do with it later with Katya.
### LARGE +
In the Archon Quest Welkin Moon's Homecoming (Version 6.3), the Traveler and Paimon find a seat at Speranza after saying goodbye to friends, and Varka is seen at a table drafting a letter. In Act VII's Where Our Space and Time Intersect, the Traveler finds themselves at Speranza with residents behaving strangely, before Dottore speaks and changes the setting.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Warm and busy, with hot food, young staff and a fortune wheel and a food-hunting dog outside. Katya keeps an eye on the children and is firm about not letting them be endangered.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 Speranza is open, still run by Katya, and still the Conch Gang's base.

## entry: Conch Gang

### STANDARD
The Conch Gang is the group of stray children under Katya's care. Members include Auni, Semyon, Helka, Jurva, Maisie, Maite and Mikko; former members include Aino, Linnea and Tapani. A new member needs the agreement of all existing members, and the gang traditionally accepts children and asks members to leave at a certain age, though there is precedent for admitting an older person. Maite calls the Traveler an official friend of the gang during The Mirrors, the Maze, and the Tsar.

## entry: Katya
@keys: Katya
@keys2: Speranza, Nasha Town
### STANDARD
Katya is the owner of Speranza, a former Treasure Hoarders adventurer who retired after injuries. She welcomes visitors, sells food, jokes that the kids are happy to work for their share, and tells people to look elsewhere for treasure rumors. After The Mirrors, the Maze, and the Tsar, once only, she gives the Traveler Nod-Krai Hot Dogs and Fonta because of Maite's message.

## entry: Pavlov and the Lucky Spin
@keys: Pavlov, Lucky Spin
@keys2: Speranza, Katya
### STANDARD
Pavlov is a dog at Speranza who asks guests to spin a wheel because, once it has been spun and stopped, good food falls. He is understood by people who can hear animals, such as Lauma and Columbina. Passers-by are allowed to spin the wheel too, but a notice reminds visitors that everything belongs to Speranza and Pavlov is watching.
