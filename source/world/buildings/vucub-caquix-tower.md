## summary
### LITE
The Vucub Caquix Tower stands in the middle of Ashflow Street in Ochkanatlan. Its lower level, reached by elevator, holds notes from different eras about an expedition into the city and the calamity that ended the old alliance.
### STANDARD +
The name comes from the Popol Vuh bird demon Vucub Caquix; the Chinese name means Fire Parrot Tower.

## places
### STANDARD
There is a wall mural, a door beside which scrolls are kept, and a lower level with an elevator and desks. A hole at the east side of the lower level has a note on a desk near its edge.

## people
### STANDARD
Four notes can be read. A mountain ranger's journal tells of an expedition guided by Copan's claim that a mechanism in the city leads to the sky-vessel, and laments that a very young Spiritcaller of the Masters of the Night-Wind was allowed to come. A Spiritcaller's note (by the young prodigy Liriwu) says she expected to catch up with Uncle Taika and go on to Cinder City, and that even the Wayob's voices cannot be heard here. A priest's note describes the old alliance's last days. A scholar's note concerns an ancient scroll.
### LARGE +
Liriwu names Grand Master Sanhaj as her teacher's teacher and finds no mystic art, healing included, works in the tower, so she doubts even Sanhaj could dispel the curse.

## history
### STANDARD
The Nameless Priest's note records the Recording Officer Tlamachtihquetl, the craftsman Chupicua avoiding waterway labour with three bags of obsidian, and Manqu taking his place. It then describes repairs halting after a dark calamity submerged the deep well and transformed nearby residents; the Cuauhtli Warriors sealing the entrance and burning the infected with flame borrowed from the Holy Sovereign; an old woman raving about the sun and the alliance's collapse; and commoners blaming the ban on sacrifices to the Lord of the Night. Chichiri tried to persuade them. The writer ends barricaded with sheltering children, holding a gate.
### LARGE +
The Traveling Scholar's note reasons that an ancient scroll from the era of the One Entombed With the Primal Fire shows dragon artisans made a weapon able to annihilate the Abyss, and mentions a treasure ship moored above Cinder City. The scholar later writes that they were all deceived about the treasure's essence; the rest is torn away.

## dangers
### LARGE
The ranger's journal warns that Abyssal erosion has ruined roads, that earlier missing adventurers were found beyond help, and that the city must be entered to reach the sky-vessel. Liriwu considers the place cursed.

## status
### LITE
Ruined and silent as of 7.1; open for exploration.
### ULTRA +
The tower's notes span at least three periods: the alliance's final days, an ancient scroll's era, and the recent expedition. They share one theme, that hopes of salvation (a weapon, a treasure, a rescue) failed in Ochkanatlan.
