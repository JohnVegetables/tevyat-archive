## summary
### LITE
The Komore Teahouse is a teahouse in Inazuma City owned by the Yashiro Commission and run on a strict members-only basis. The Kamisato Clan uses it as a local base and private meeting place; only guests it approves may enter. Its boss is Taroumaru, a Shiba Inu who once served as a ninja dog of the Shuumatsuban.
### STANDARD +
After a customer drinks tea, they leave payment in a special box rather than at a counter. The name Komore means sunlight leaking through leaves; the shop's advertisement board reads "Sunlight leaks through wood, mottles in the courtyard."

## geography
### STANDARD
In Tenryou, a prime street location in Inazuma City, with an exterior and an interior. Outside are the shop assistant Kozue, Heihachi and the Snezhnayan merchant Ramsay; inside are Taroumaru, Nobumori and the retired ninja Nakanishi, beside a copy of Sangonomiya Chronicles. The wiki notes a chest behind the kitchen holding Raw Meat, Onikabuto and a large sum of Mora, implied to be Taroumaru's own stash.
### LARGE +
Several Teahouse furnishing items (candlestand, drums, cushions, folding screen, lamp and stool) cite the shop in their descriptions.

## people
### STANDARD
Taroumaru is the owner. Kozue is the shop assistant who jokes about the staff's eternal contract; Nakanishi is a retired Shuumatsuban ninja and old friend of Taroumaru; Nobumori and Heihachi also appear. Ramsay, a Snezhnayan merchant, found the tea and atmosphere agreeable.
### LARGE +
Taroumaru can't speak, but Gorou (a general of the Watatsumi Army) can translate his barks with fair accuracy. The dog sometimes asks Kozue for Iron Chunks, whose purpose she does not know.

## governance
### STANDARD
A Yashiro Commission property used by the Kamisato Clan; membership is by the Clan's approval. Before the Sakoku Decree, people reportedly travelled from overseas to admire Taroumaru.

## history
### STANDARD
Taroumaru was a ninken of the Shuumatsuban, serving with Nakanishi, until both retired. He took the position of owner and the teahouse became well regarded. During Gorou's hangout Ordinary Memorial, Treasure Hoarders break in; the dog stays out of the fight because he is outnumbered, and the thieves speak in front of him thinking he is no threat, but he cannot report what he heard until he meets Gorou.
### LARGE +
During the Irodori Festival event (Hues of the Violet Garden, limited-time), Taroumaru competes in the Clash of Lone Blades, whose title for him, "Deposit Stolen, Teahouse Boss Enraged", references the stolen chest, and is called the mightiest participant.
### ULTRA +
Thoma mentions Taroumaru in his hangout when comforting a stray Shiba Inu. The legend that all employees signed a contract with Taroumaru appears in the Version 2.0 Special Program.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Dappled light on wood, steam from teapots, soft talk and the careful discretion of a place where clan business is quietly done.

## quests
### STANDARD
Ordinary Memorial (Gorou's hangout, Inazuma City), Kozue's Ironwork, Three Wishes, and Thoma's hangout all pass through. The Irodori Festival event "Clash of Lone Blades" is limited.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the teahouse remains a members-only Kamisato Clan haunt in Inazuma City, with Taroumaru as boss.
