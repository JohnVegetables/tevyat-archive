## summary
### LITE
The Royal Hot Springs is a hot-spring bathhouse at the headquarters of the Tenryou Commission in Inazuma City, near Tenshukaku. It is reserved for the lords of the Shogunate, who are served and housed by its manager Mikawa Akane, and outsiders are not allowed in.
### STANDARD +
Access is based on a strict hierarchy. With private rooms and very few customers, the springs are often used for the lords' private discussions. Inazuma has no natural volcanic hot springs, so the water is heated artificially and mixed with minerals and plants from many nations.

## places
### STANDARD
The baths lie in the Tenshukaku part of Inazuma City, within the Tenryou Commission's headquarters. The Court Lantern "Thundering Heart" furnishing describes lanterns found in Tenshukaku and at the Royal Hot Springs, coated in a solution made from trace amounts of Amethyst Lumps and Electro Crystals, which makes them brighter and lightning-resistant.
### LARGE +
A courtyard by a cliff lies on the grounds, where the Fatui spy Tae slacks off in the 2.4 quest "Shuumatsuban Operations".

## people
### LITE
Mikawa Akane manages the springs.
### STANDARD
Mikawa Akane greets visitors by naming the Royal Hot Springs and its location at the Tenryou Commission headquarters, and asks whether an unfamiliar visitor has got lost. She arranges service and rooms for Shogunate lords who come for the hot springs. She is happy to tell new arrivals about Inazuma's hot-spring culture, but refuses to let an outsider bathe.
### LARGE +
Akane explains that the Kanjou Commission's Hiiragi Hiroshi brought a high-efficiency boiler from Snezhnaya to reach hot-spring temperatures, and that the water is mixed with precious minerals and plants from various nations. She says ordinary commoners' hot springs cannot afford those minerals and so do not offer the same benefits, though one funded by the Kanjou Commission could be different.
### ULTRA +
In version 2.4 a Fatui spy named Tae works at the baths under a pseudonym in the quest "Shuumatsuban Operations". Akane complains that she skips shifts, and that one guest suffered frostbite while Tae was on duty; the Tenryou Commission has suspected her since. Tae says the hot water made her dizzy and she let her Cryo Delusion slip.

## governance
### STANDARD
The springs are for the Shogunate's lords, and the baths sit at the Tenryou Commission headquarters. Akane says access priority follows a strict hierarchy. The Kanjou Commission figure Hiiragi Hiroshi is credited with the first Inazuma hot spring, and Akane muses that a spring funded by that Commission could offer commoners the same benefits.
### LARGE +
Akane says outlanders are not allowed in, which fits the Sakoku-era restrictions on outsiders but is stated in terms of hierarchy rather than the decree.

## history
### STANDARD
Hot-spring culture came to Inazuma from elsewhere, and the locals were enchanted by it. Having no volcanic springs, they tried many ways to make one. According to Akane, Hiiragi Hiroshi of the Kanjou Commission solved it with a Snezhnayan boiler, creating the first Inazuma hot spring.
### LARGE +
Akane contrasts Inazuma with Natlan, where natural hot springs form from volcanic activity. The springs here are therefore a product of engineering and trade rather than geology.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Quiet, formal and secluded: private rooms, very few customers and staff who know who may enter. The temperature of the water is, as Akane says, just right.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the springs are still reserved for Shogunate lords with Akane as manager; the wiki records no change since 2.0.

### STANDARD
Mikawa Akane is the manager of the Royal Hot Springs. She is polite but firm, offers hot-spring history to newcomers, and refuses outsiders because the springs are for the Shogunate's lords by strict rank. She is irritated by Tae, a new employee who skips work.

## entry: Tae, the Fatui Spy at the Baths

@keys2: Royal Hot Springs, Shuumatsuban, Mikawa Akane
@version: 2.4
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
In the world quest "Shuumatsuban Operations" the Traveler is told one of three Fatui spies has recently started working at the Royal Hot Springs. Tae is a Fatui agent, disguised as staff, who dislikes Lyudochka Snezhevna, whom she accuses of getting the Grand Narukami Shrine post through luck. She is touchy, uses Fatui code phrases, and is suspected by the Tenryou Commission after the frostbite incident.
