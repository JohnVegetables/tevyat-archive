## summary
### LITE
Kvasnik is a well-known beverage shop in Zolnyy, the Ash District of Snezhnograd, run by Zyryanov, a volkodlak. Its map description says the owner always claims that only his shop serves the most authentic kvass.
### STANDARD +
The same text adds a wry note: long-lived locals all know the drink came first, and only then did the shop named after it appear. The word kvasnik is also Russian for a traditional ceramic vessel used to store and serve kvass.
## people
### STANDARD
Zyryanov, a volkodlak, owns the shop. He calls out his "tasty and affordable" drinks, offers free ice-cream samples, and says he came up with a new ice-cream recipe himself.
### LARGE +
Zyryanov reacts differently to famous Snezhnayan visitors: he stammers with nervous respect when Odette appears, and he greets Alyosha as someone he knows, asking how his last hunt went.
## culture
### STANDARD
Kvass is an everyday Snezhnayan drink, and shops like this are neighbourhood fixtures where affordability matters. The shop also sells food and recipes.
## atmosphere
### ULTRA
A lively counter in a working district, with cheap warming drinks, a boastful proprietor and regulars who have heard his claim many times.
## gameplay
### STANDARD
Game mechanics: the stock of food refreshes daily; a daily free ice cream is offered, and Snezhnayan party members trigger unique themed ice creams.
