## summary
### LITE
The Jiulue Teahouse is a teahouse in Yilong Wharf, owned by Baishu. It has a stage for performances and an outdoor dining area, and the indoor dining area, private seats and rooms are temporarily under renovation.
### STANDARD +
Its Chinese name is Jiulue Chaguan. It is decorated for the Lantern Rite festival in the Springtime Charms event.

## people
### STANDARD
Baishu is the boss, an expansive businessman who trades on good wishes and flattery. He is also behind many of the nearby inns. Quezhi is the waitress who offers a choice of tea with a performance or a quick work meal; Jiewu is the storyteller. Regular customers include the tea merchants Jiuguan and Fryslan and, at night, Lixing of the Communications Office.
### LARGE +
Quezhi proudly lists the Yauwan and Sunglo teas Chenyu Vale is famous for, in every grade, plus export-quality Tianheng Bohea, Yunlai White Tea and Bishui Green Tea, which are popular in Liyue Harbor. Jiewu tells short tales about visitors, such as a Fontaine merchant who misjudged rinse water for fine tea and a Mondstadt poet in a wine contest, and promises to discuss storytelling craft in the evening.
### ULTRA +
Baishu says he started as an ordinary tea farmer who grew mediocre tea; Master Jiuguan's inspiration and the upgrade from tea cauldrons to machinery in Chenyu Vale let him build funds early, start a teahouse, and expand into inns and restaurants. He wants to support his former colleagues with money and publicity and to help more business people.
