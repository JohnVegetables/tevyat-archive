## summary
### LITE
The Kujou Encampment is the headquarters of the Shogun's Army in Kannazuka, a fortified military camp run for the Tenryou Commission. Its purpose, as the official map text puts it, is to guard against rebellion from the people of Watatsumi Island. It was the Shogunate's front line in the civil war between the Raiden Shogun's followers and the Sangonomiya resistance, and the Kujou Clan, who head the Tenryou Commission, take their name onto its banners.
### STANDARD +
General Kujou Masahito commands the troops here. The camp is the setting for Tatara Tales, the world quest series about the Mikage Furnace, and for a handful of ordinary soldiers whose private doubts about the war show how the Vision Hunt Decree wore down morale.

## geography
### STANDARD
The camp sits in Kannazuka, the disputed ground north of the Shogunate and Watatsumi lands whose name means roughly "the hill where no gods reside". Rocky outcrops and steep cliffs surround it, and the Mikage Furnace, Inazuma's largest smelting site, lies close by behind the rocks. The camp has a gate and watchtowers, tents, a blacksmith and a Teleport Waypoint, and an island west of it is where a faded Family Letter can be found.
### LARGE +
The area around it carries Thunder Sakura, Thunderwood and the Thunder Barrier puzzles typical of Inazuma, plus the Pyro Hypostasis boss that the wiki lists under the camp's features. Hilichurls, Treasure Hoarders, Abyss mages and Shogunate-aligned Nobushi and Kairagi all roam nearby. A daily "Voltage Charge" commission starts at the camp's north-western gate, with a 90 second time limit.

## people
### STANDARD
Kujou Masahito, a Shogunate General and the eldest son of Kujou Takayuki, commands the camp. Around him are rank-and-file samurai such as Ono (at the gate), Ishikawa, Ogura Yuu and Matsudaira, the officer Toranosuke, the yoriki Iwakura Mitsunari, the blacksmith Ikuhara Gai, and later the former furnace worker Kaji, Yousuke (who oversees repairs at the Mikage Furnace) and the Fontaine inventor Xavier.
### LARGE +
Ikuhara Gai invented the Ikuhara Art forging method; he left Tatarasuna after a furnace accident and took a smith's post in the army so he could watch Tatarasuna from a distance. Iwakura Mitsunari is the former head of the Iwakura Art school, who lost his Vision to the Vision Hunt Decree, lost some of his memory and ambition with it, and chose to disband his school and serve the Shogunate. The shrine maiden Kuki Miyuki is met here at the start of Tatara Tales.
### ULTRA +
Kaji was a miner from Yashiori Island. When a radical faction of the Watatsumi Army broke the ancient Narukami wards in a bid to revive the slain god Orobashi, the lingering hatred of that god (the Tatarigami) forced his family to leave. He left a note for Shogunate soldiers, kept watching his home from Fort Fujitou, and after Orobashi's Legacy is found at the camp. Kuki Miyuki is the elder sister of Kuki Shinobu of the Arataki Gang; after Tatara Tales she moves south to a Thunder Sakura and later to the Grand Narukami Shrine stairs. Xavier, from the town of Petrichor in Fontaine, stayed behind when the other Fontaine experts left, and calls himself a great inventor.

## governance
### STANDARD
The camp answers to the Tenryou Commission, the Shogunate's executive arm for security and the military, and so to the Kujou Clan. Officially the Shogun's Army here outnumbers and out-trains the resistance and is better equipped; the map text calls victory seemingly assured.
### LARGE +
Masahito is a loyal officer who is also privately uneasy: he doubted the Vision Hunt Decree and thought it harmed the people, yet he fights on as a soldier. He believes either side could still win if the war drags on. After his father's deception came to light he was entitled to become clan head as eldest son, but he let his brother Kujou Kamaji take the post, promising to support him as a warrior of the clan.

## history
### STANDARD
Tradition ties the camp's style to the ancestor Kujou Shigeyori, a mortal general whom the Raiden Shogun valued greatly. The viewpoint text says that in a disaster hundreds of years ago he built a battlefront fortress in a single night and fought the forces of darkness under the Electro Mitsudomoe banner, and that his descendants inherited his military engineering skill. In the present war, Narukami's believers in the Raiden Shogun and Sangonomiya's believers in Watatsumi Omikami were already at odds, and the Vision Hunt Decree made it worse; the Tenryou Commission stationed the army here to put down the Sangonomiya rebellion.
### LARGE +
Masahito noticed that the Tatarigami suppressed on Yashiori Island had grown stronger and wrote to warn his father, who scolded him. A force Takayuki sent to look into it disappeared, overcome by that energy. When Kokomi's side won the peace talks and a ceasefire was signed, Masahito stayed at the camp, started rebuilding at Yashiori with Sangonomiya, and accepted that Jade Steel imports would suffer.

## customs
### LARGE
Discipline here is Shogunate drill rather than ceremony: gate guards challenge outlanders and civilians and tell them to leave a battlefield, and the officers think in terms of strategy and supply. The soldiers' private feelings are another matter. Deserters, grief for dead comrades and unease over the Vision Hunt Decree are all voiced in idle talk and small side stories.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
A busy military camp on a bleak, thunder-struck headland: banners with the Shogunate crest, spears racked outside tents, smoke from nearby smelting, officers in lavender and eggplant-coloured armour, and a mood of tired vigilance. After the ceasefire the same soldiers say they hope the fragile peace will last.

## dangers
### STANDARD
The camp is guarded, and officers may warn off or confront outsiders who wander in during the war. The surroundings hold Treasure Hoarders, hilichurls, Abyss mages and, during the war, active Nobushi and Kairagi, and the Pyro Hypostasis boss near the camp.

## quests
### STANDARD
Tatara Tales (2.0) begins here: Miyuki and Toranosuke argue over the Mikage Furnace, and the Traveler works with Xavier over seven days until the Tatarigami core is purified. Afterward Xavier, Toranosuke and Yousuke are found at the camp. Kaji moves here after Orobashi's Legacy. A hidden exploration objective, unofficially called "Searching for Saimon Jirou", sends the Traveler to ask Ishikawa, Ono and Ogura Yuu about a deserter, and leads on to the quest The Farmer's Treasure.
### LARGE +
Matsudaira, a friend of the nameless samurai Kenji Kazumichi, helps the Traveler restore the samurai's memory by sharing a wine jug. The Mikage Furnace repair storyline continues through the camp's NPCs after the Traveler purifies the core.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the camp has not appeared in 5.x-7.1 content. Its last known state is the post-war period of Inazuma 2.x: a ceasefire between the Shogun's Army and the Watatsumi Army, with soldiers who expect the peace to be brittle. Masahito remains a Shogunate officer, and Kujou Kamaji heads the clan in an acting capacity.
### STANDARD +
Do not assume the camp was disbanded or that fighting resumed; canon does not say.

## entry: Kujou Masahito's doubts
@keys: Kujou Masahito, General Masahito
@keys2: Kujou Encampment, Shogun's Army, Kannazuka
@version: 2.0
### STANDARD
Kujou Masahito is the eldest son of Kujou Takayuki, the elder brother of Kamaji and the adoptive elder brother of Sara. As the camp's commander he told the Traveler civilians should leave a battlefield, said the war was a stalemate, and admitted that talks with Kamaji about the correctness of Inazuma's policies had been on his mind. After the ceasefire he praised Sangonomiya Kokomi's handling of the crisis, said he did not regret negotiating, and warned that a short ceasefire would not close the gap between those who serve the Shogun and those who worship the dead god Orobashi.
### LARGE +
He tells the Traveler that if Takayuki had led the troops he suspects the methods would have been crueler. He also remembers that the Traveler accompanied the Shogun to rescue his brother from the Takatsukasa Clan, and says he will not forget the favour. If the two ever face each other in renewed war, he warns, he will have to fight the Traveler. His idle remark that any peace will be short-lived is spoken after the ceasefire.

## entry: The Tatara Tales at the camp
@keys: Tatara Tales, Mikage Furnace dispute, Toranosuke, Miyuki, Xavier
@keys2: Kujou Encampment, Tatarigami
@version: 2.0
### STANDARD
Years before, the Tenryou Commission hired Fontaine experts to build the Mikage Furnace and run it on Tatarigami power to make Jade Steel. Once the war began the power surged. At the camp the officer Toranosuke and the Grand Narukami Shrine's Miyuki disagree about the evacuation order, and Toranosuke resents the shrine's interference. The Traveler enlists Xavier, learns that Fatui are behind the trouble, and spends seven days preparing before purifying the Tatarigami core. Afterward Xavier and Toranosuke settle in the camp, and Xavier turns the events into a film.

## entry: Deserters and the dead of the camp
@keys: Saimon Jirou, Ogura Yuu, Kenji Kazumichi, Matsudaira
@keys2: Kujou Encampment, deserter, desertion
@version: 2.0
### LARGE
Ishikawa, who faints at the sight of blood, was talked into deserting by fellow soldier Saimon Jirou. Ogura Yuu raised the alarm, Ishikawa tripped and was punished with barracks rations, and only Jirou got away. Matsudaira was once close to Kenji Kazumichi and to a man named Masanori; Masanori's desertion and death, and Kenji's madness afterward, still weigh on him. A faded letter found on an island west of the camp, a soldier's note to his father that mentions his friend Kenji, shows the same ordinary lives behind the army's uniform.
