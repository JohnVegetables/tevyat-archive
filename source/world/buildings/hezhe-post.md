## summary
### LITE
Hezhe Post is where the Communications Office conducts its business at Yilong Wharf in Chenyu Vale. It handles registration, inspection and shipping applications for people and goods entering, leaving or moving within Liyue.
### STANDARD +
The Communications Office is one of the Eight Trades under the Liyue Qixing, and specialises in transportation.

## practice
### STANDARD
Fangju lists four main tasks of the post: registering people and goods entering and leaving; inspecting and, if necessary, quarantining them; taking applications for delivery inside Liyue's borders; and providing ad-hoc storage. Outlanders who want to do business in Liyue and arrive at the wharf register here, and staff inspect and disinfect their goods.
### LARGE +
The post evaluates the quantity of goods and the distance to the destination, then sends manifests to transport units or to partners at the Secure Transport Agency, which do the actual hauling. It can store goods in a warehouse if no final destination is planned, and can act as proxy to apply for a business permit, although that is less efficient than applying in Liyue Harbor. Fangju says the wider Office also handles road, bridge, waterway and dock construction, and encourages visitors to consider working there; staff face monthly performance evaluations.
### ULTRA +
Lixing, who works for the Communications Office, evaluates and optimises transport plans for set routes. The Office cannot cover all of Liyue, so some work is outsourced to local Secure Transport Agencies, whose varied equipment makes timing hard to control; standardising it would need investment these small agencies cannot afford. Lixing is often found muttering about transfer points along the route from Lumidouce Harbor to Liyue Harbor.

## people
### STANDARD
Fangju and Lixing are Communications Office staff; Fangju is a steady, cautious clerk who describes himself as a lowly staff member and recommends the Ministry of Civil Affairs for more detail. Sai Jinzhuan also stands near the post and has idle lines about disobedient underlings.
