## summary
### LITE
Nastya's Home is a house in the abandoned village of Okurov where the young girl Nastya lived with her mother. "Nastya's Home" is an unofficial name for the place.
### STANDARD +
In Back on One's Feet, Nastya points to it and says she and her mother used to live there. The Traveler can enter it after a hidden exploration objective about a being called "Shar".
## places
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
Inside the house is a worn old photograph of Nastya with her mother. After the quest Chorus of Solace, a blue algae in the house also plays Nastya's thoughts.
## people
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The only NPC is "Shar", whose name is the Russian word for "ball". It resembles a Juvenile Tree Segment, with clothing and a red ribbon attached to its branch. In its dialogue it repeatedly says new friends can be family too; if the Traveler declines it says it will play with the doll alone.
### LARGE +
Through the blue algae Nastya says the little bed, blanket, pillow and toys are now used by "another life", and that although her mother's hug feels different she can feel her mother's hand stroking her hair.
## history
@version: 7.0
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
Nastya and her mother lived in Okurov, a village closed off from the rest of Snezhnaya. Other residents included Furiosa and Zhara. Nastya remembers a doctor called Moreau, who is actually Koshchei. By the time of the quest line she says she has slept for an unknown time, and most of the villagers have gone missing.
## quests
@version: 7.0
### ULTRA
The house belongs to the quest line In the Dwelling of Life (Back on One's Feet, Chorus of Solace) and to the hidden exploration objective about Shar's matryoshka dolls. It is a small personal space within a large and unsettling story, so keep scenes here gentle.
## atmosphere
### STANDARD
A small, quiet house with a child's bed and toys, a family photograph, and an unsettling companion who treats the Traveler as a new member of the family.
