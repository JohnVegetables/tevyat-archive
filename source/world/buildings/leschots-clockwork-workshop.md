## summary
### LITE
Leschots Clockwork Workshop is a toy shop in Quartier Lyonnais run by Livre, who makes mechanical clockwork toys.
### STANDARD +
The workshop sells toy birds, puppets, clockwork horses, trumpet-blowing blubberbeasts and penguins. The penguin designs are consignments by Freminet, whom Livre admires.

## places
### STANDARD
In Quartier Lyonnais, next to the Fontaine Northland Bank and Lepaute Horlogerie, run by his younger brother Caron.

## people
### STANDARD
Livre is the boss; Alberre and Arbon are staff. Freminet is commissioned for toy designs.
### LARGE +
Livre once considered inviting Freminet to develop toys but did not press when the boy stayed silent.

## history
@availability: limited
@version: 4.0
### LARGE
In the Mega Meka Melee event (4.0), staff asked the Traveler to collect data for a Champion Duelist plan for a new toy line. The brothers' rivalry with Caron comes to a head in later acts and they talk openly in the final one.

### ULTRA +
The name Leschots may allude to Swiss watchmakers; the wiki mentions one who worked with the Jaquet-Droz automata makers. This is a guess, not canon.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
A toy shop full of clockwork creatures and a friendly sibling rivalry.
