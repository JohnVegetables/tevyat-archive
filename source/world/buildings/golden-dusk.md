## summary
### LITE
Golden Dusk is a restaurant and lodge in the People of the Springs' settlement at Toyac Springs in Natlan, owned by Kupo Nui, the matriarch of the Nui family. It serves chilled drinks, has beautiful scenery and a convenient beach location, and has an in-house singer, Tirda.
### STANDARD +
It is an old Nui family home that was turned into a restaurant at Chief Amina's suggestion. Business is steady, but the family is frequently short-staffed. Guests may use the kitchen and sleep in tents near the water.

## places
### STANDARD
A beachside house turned restaurant, with lodging and a bulletin board. The Hot Spring Retreat: Secret Menu furnishing is designed to resemble it.
### LARGE +
Services listed on the bulletin board include use of the kitchen facilities and tents for guests who want to sleep near the water. Kupo Nui lets people help themselves or bring their own ingredients and cook, at no extra charge.

## people
### STANDARD
Kupo Nui owns the place and greets visitors warmly; Mahina Nui staffs reception; Tirda sings; Wakea Nui appears at night.
### LARGE +
At night Wakea Nui asks Kupo Nui whether the family might unite to expand the business instead of each working separately, and she replies that everyone is gainfully employed. Wakea admits to being a little sad that no relative will give them the time of day; at this rate they will never get a second employee for their international travel agency.

## history
### STANDARD
Originally an old Nui family home, it was converted after Chief Amina noted the wonderful scenery and suggested a restaurant. It has been steadily busy ever since, with both locals and tourists enjoying drinks by the beach.
### LARGE +
Kupo Nui says her biggest problem has always been securing staff: she has a big family, but few of them are interested in helping.
### ULTRA +
The Golden Dusk has held 242 iterations of the regional eating contest Inferno Stomach Supremo. The current champion is Varesa of the Collective of Plenty.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 Golden Dusk remains open and steady, with no recorded story role after 5.0.
