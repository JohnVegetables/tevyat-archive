## summary
### LITE
Mona's Laboratory is a house at the southern end of Mondstadt City, a little past Schulz's Blacksmith. Goth owns it and Mona, the astrologist, rents it. For most of the game its door is shut.
### STANDARD +
Before the quest Beyond This World's Stars is completed, a Hydro seal blocks the door. The prompt reads that the door seems locked by a curious seal and wonders whose place it is.

## places
### STANDARD
One house with a door the player can only examine or speak through.
### LARGE +
The house becomes interactable again during the quest Owe Mora, Pay Mora.
### ULTRA +
The interior is shown in the hangout quest The Ancient Azure Stars.

## people
### STANDARD
Goth, a landlord who also runs the Goth Grand Hotel, owns the property. Mona rents it. During the quest the Traveler can talk to Mona through the door.
### LARGE +
Goth says someone had put a Hydro seal over the house and he could not lease it. Mona broke the seal and moved in, and Goth let her rent it at the lowest possible price.

## history
### STANDARD
The seal predates the quest. Canon does not say who placed it.
### LARGE +
After the quest ends and the player leaves the area, the house is no longer interactable.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
A shut door in a quiet southern lane, with a faint magical seal and the sense of someone busy inside.

## travel
### STANDARD
Go to the southern end of Mondstadt City, beyond Schulz's Blacksmith.

## status
### LITE
Nothing after the quests is recorded; the door stays closed to the player outside them.
