## summary
### LITE
Fanmu Carpenter is a carpentry workshop in Qingce Village, owned by Master Lu. In the game he sells blueprints for furnishings.
### STANDARD +
He calls it one of the oldest carpentry workshops in the village. Its Chinese name is read Fanmu-tang.

## history
### STANDARD
By Master Lu's account the workshop began as the property of his grandfather's grandfather and has stayed in the family ever since. He says it built the residence of Chang the Ninth and the village mill, and that even the Qixing come to it when they need furniture made to order.
### LARGE +
Master Lu says he is getting old and his joints suffer after a lifetime in the workshop, so he wants an apprentice to pass on the craft and secure the shop's future. If the player says they would like the job, he is doubtful because the player seems restless and travel-bound, and tells them to do what they must first and return later.
### ULTRA +
Dayu, a young but capable carpenter, is the official apprentice per the wiki: he had worked for the carpenter who built the stage for Yun Jin's premiere at the rebuilt Jade Chamber, which prepared him for Master Lu's first official job for him, rebuilding the stage of the Heyu Tea House. Master Lu also offers a Genius Invokation TCG match, a gameplay feature, and says playing cards is relaxing.

## people
### STANDARD
Master Lu is genial, proud of the family trade, and boasts that he can build anything from a yo-yo to a house. Dayu is the younger carpenter in his orbit.
