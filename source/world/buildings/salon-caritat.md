## summary
### LITE
Salon Caritat is a society in the Quartier Narbonnais of the Court of Fontaine devoted to the study of law and the improvement of justice. In practice its members act like a civic advocacy group, currently pressing on government spending and water pollution.
### STANDARD +
Its headquarters notice states its purpose as furthering the study of law in Fontaine and improving the administration of justice. It organises numbered symposia to which all members are invited. The name is Occitan for "charity", per the wiki's etymology note.

## people
### STANDARD
Two open-world members are known. Riqueti worries about the Palais Mermonia's sense of crisis; he once served in the Special Security and Surveillance Patrol of the Gardes before transferring to the Maison Gestion. Justeau is preoccupied with ocean pollution and complains that hardly anyone else cares.
### LARGE +
Riqueti describes the Patrol as the least mechanised branch of the Gardes, recruiting on ability regardless of family background, and says its captain Chevreuse would have no use for gardemeks. He holds that Fontaine should not assume life will be easy after a crisis, and that it must keep attending to its other problems.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Expect earnest, slightly pompous talk: members using formal legal and administrative language about budgets and the environment, convinced that the public is ignoring an obvious danger. It is a place of speeches and petitions rather than action.

## history
### STANDARD
The notice announces the salon's 36th symposium, on the Maison Gardiennage's budget overruns and their link to public safety in the Court of Fontaine. That implies regular meetings over some time, but canon gives no dates.
### ULTRA +
The notice also frames the salon's aim as improving justice generally, so its interest in pollution and spending sits alongside, not in place of, its legal-study purpose. Riqueti's dialogue changes after the Archon Quest events around the prophecy, when he treats the prophecy as the largest crisis Fontaine has faced and urges attention to other problems (revelation tied to Version 4.2 content). Note that the wiki gives no building description beyond the headquarters marker in the Quartier Narbonnais.

## status
### LITE
Active as of Version 7.1, with members still voicing concerns around the Court of Fontaine.

Riqueti and Justeau are the Salon Caritat members met in the open world. Riqueti is a former Patrol member turned administrator who doubts how Fontaine's institutions handle risk; Justeau is fixated on the water pollution crisis and says the ocean cannot be relied on to clean itself.
