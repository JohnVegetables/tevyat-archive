## summary
### LITE
Wanyou Boutique is a shop in Liyue Harbor run by Bolai, who sells cooking ingredients. Its stock is mostly fruit and fish, restocked every few days. Bolai speaks in whispers as if his goods were contraband, though they are not.
### STANDARD +
The wiki says the shop once saw the patronage of Morax. After the Rite of Parting, Bolai speaks of Rex Lapis' death with sorrow.

## places
### STANDARD
A store near the northern wharf of Liyue Harbor.
### LARGE +
Items on sale include apples, sunsettias, fish and starconches, according to the wiki's list of stock.

## people
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Bolai is the owner. He acts secretive in manner. Before the quest Of the Land Amidst Monoliths, a second, non-interactive Bolai also stands near the Main Plaza, according to the wiki.
### LARGE +
After Rex Lapis' death, Bolai says he is honored to have supplied the silk flowers for the Rite of Parting. He is no Qixing and has no Vision, so that was the only help he could give.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Furtive and a little comic: a man whispering about perfectly ordinary produce.

## travel
### STANDARD
On Feiyun Slope according to the wiki, with Bolai himself found beneath the northern wharf.

## history
@version: 1.0
### STANDARD
Bolai is a bit player in Liyue's story: he names the Rite of Parting as his one contribution, and a Character Tale about Zhongli on the wiki is linked from his page.
### ULTRA +
Bolai is among those listed as present in the Archon Quest opening in Liyue Harbor, where the player is prompted to talk to locals including him, Linlang and Guanhai.

## status
### LITE
Open as of 7.1, as far as the sources show.
