## summary
### LITE
The Weary Inn is a restaurant and hotel in one, on the outskirts of the Chuwen Fair in the Stadium of the Sacred Flame (Warrior's Rest Area), Natlan. It offers free lodging and food to warriors taking part in the Pilgrimage of the Return of the Sacred Flame.
### STANDARD +
Its owner is Chanca, a former contestant who once stayed here for free herself. She also runs the food stall Zakan's Street Bites near the entrance.

## places
### STANDARD
Located in the Warrior's Rest Area of the Stadium, beside the Chuwen Fair. Per the official map text, the name was chosen in the hope that warriors would leave their weariness at the inn and walk into the Stadium full of energy.
### LARGE +
The stall at the entrance sells the inn's cooking to anyone, not only contestants, and teaches recipes such as Tatacos.

## people
### STANDARD
Chanca, owner. A guest, Vitusov, a visitor whose origin the wiki lists as Snezhnaya, is seen here musing about a half-hour clean-plate challenge on a triple portion of Surf, Turf and Perch.
### LARGE +
Every manager of the inn is said to be selected and designated by the Speaker's Chamber, which ties it to the Pilgrimage's organisers. Chanca says that anyone who knows a contestant can stay with them and eat and sleep at the inn on the house.

## governance
### LARGE
The Speaker's Chamber appoints the inn's managers, per the in-game description. Contestants' costs are covered; others pay for food at the stall.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Hearty, noisy and unpretentious: large high-calorie meals, tired fighters and old comrades trading stories.

## status
### LITE
Open as of 7.1 under Chanca.
### STANDARD +
After the Pilgrimage's battles ended, Chanca says many old comrades come by for meals; because of her old injuries she cannot help them in other ways, so she feeds them. The remark becomes available after the quest No One Fights Alone.
### ULTRA +
Canon gives the inn no named founder or history beyond the map description. Chanca recalls staying here free of charge as a contestant, so the arrangement predates her ownership.
