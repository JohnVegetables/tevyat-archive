## summary
### LITE
The Jade Mystery is a jade shop in Liyue Harbor run by the cheerful boss Shitou. Its Chinese name is read Jiecui-hang, roughly "the store that reveals jadeite".
### STANDARD +
Shitou sells ores, cut stones and uncut rocks that customers can gamble on, in the hope of finding valuable jade inside.

## practice
### STANDARD
Shitou says the shop takes pride in generations of experience crafting jade finery and has quite a reputation in Liyue. The shop sells pieces cut from raw stone, though not exactly rare jade, and its stock refreshes every few days. He greets customers by telling them they look radiant and must be lucky today.
### LARGE +
Shitou says business has slowed because the Ministry of Civil Affairs shut down the Chasm, cutting off supplies, so raw materials are now worth more than the finished goods. So he decided to sell the uncut rocks and let customers bet on them. In the daily commission "Diamond in the Rough...", the Traveler picks stones for him in a gambling game.
### ULTRA +
After Rex Lapis' death, Shitou muses on renaming the type of Noctilucous Jade used in the Rite of Parting in his memory, and cannot decide between "Parting Jade" and "Ascending Jade".

## people
### STANDARD
Shitou is the boss, bright and persuasive, and enjoys a card game with customers.
