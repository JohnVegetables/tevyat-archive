## summary
### LITE
Sangonomiya Shrine, also called the Pearlescent Palace, stands at the centre of Watatsumi Island, the outermost island of Inazuma. A palace flanked by shells and sea rocks, it is the island's centre of government and religion alike: here the Divine Priestess Sangonomiya Kokomi rules, and here rest relics of Orobashi, the dead serpent god whom Watatsumi's people still worship instead of the Raiden Shogun. During the war against the Vision Hunt Decree it was the headquarters of the Watatsumi Army.
### STANDARD +
Its name means "Coral Palace". The island's shrine maidens are trained here. The present shrine is said not to be the island's original one, but a newer building raised after Orobashi was slain by the Electro Archon about 2,000 years ago.

## geography
### STANDARD
The shrine lies in the middle of Watatsumi Island, ringed by mountains and waterfalls and surrounded by the island's strangely coloured, densely growing vegetation, with its violet-leaved forests. Bourou Village, the island's main settlement and the resistance's training camp, lies nearby, and Mouun Shrine stands elsewhere on the island. Sango Pearls and Sea Ganoderma are gathered in the surrounding area.
### LARGE +
The whole island is built on coral that Orobashi broke from its own body to raise a home for its people. Watatsumi has little arable land; its soil is also prone to "Holy Soil", a bleaching and erosion that legend blames on Enkanomiya drawing on the island's spirit.

## places
### STANDARD
The main palace-shrine, where Kokomi receives officers and guests; the shrine grounds, where Watatsumi soldiers keep watch; and nearby woods with a tree with purple foliage that Kokomi's written directives name as a landmark for finding her when she has slipped away.
### LARGE +
Kokomi keeps a private retreat away from the shrine where she reads in peace; her standing orders tell guards to send important visitors southward to that purple-leaved tree if she has vanished without word. Victory feasts after the war were held on the island under open sky at night.

## people
### STANDARD
Sangonomiya Kokomi, Divine Priestess and supreme leader of Watatsumi Island, lives and works here. General Gorou commands the island's army. Shrine maidens trained at the shrine include Izumi Naki, Mochizuki, Tsuyuko, Shizuru and Miwa; Sangonomiya samurai such as Shibata and Sugiyama stand guard, and officers such as Ookubo Daisuke report here on supplies.
### LARGE +
Tsuyuko, a shrine maiden who studied at the Sumeru Akademiya, comes from a family charged with the Watatsumi Goryou Matsuri and keeps an ancestral secret about a second set of trials. Kokomi's mother taught her that "as the ruler is, so shall the people be".

## governance
### STANDARD
The shrine is the seat of the Sangonomiya Clan, the only great Inazuman clan with no post in the Shogunate. The heir of their bloodline holds the title Divine Priestess, which means inheriting the will of a god in a mortal body and defending the land and people in that god's place. Kokomi handles war, trade, diplomacy and domestic affairs herself and governs through detailed written directives.
### LARGE +
When Kokomi became Divine Priestess, crowds came to the shrine to look at her and were openly doubtful because she looked so young; ambitious people schemed, yet the island soon returned to normal under her rule. Her directives were at times very thick, but they kept the island developing steadily; as more capable officials have been appointed, she has had to issue fewer of them. Her fairness in rewards and punishments won most people over, and the island's most common saying became that things will be alright as long as Her Excellency is here.
### ULTRA +
Officially, the people of Sangonomiya have recognised the Shogunate as rightful ruler since the Great Serpent's death, according to a Shogunate chronicle, while keeping their own faith. The Raiden Shogun herself says Watatsumi was allowed to go on worshipping Orobashi so that it could still receive its people's prayers.

## culture
### STANDARD
Watatsumi's people descend from Enkanomiya, a realm deep beneath the sea; Orobashi brought them to the surface, and they revere it as Watatsumi Omikami, their guardian god. Faith in the slain god, pride, and a long memory of defeat bind the community. The island keeps customs distinct from the capital's.
### LARGE +
A recurring rite, the Watatsumi Goryou Matsuri, holds back the Holy Soil using a Jade Coral Branch (Bloodbranch Coral) from Enkanomiya; the shrine maiden Tsuyuko enlisted the Traveler to prepare it in the world quest series "The Moon-Bathed Deep". After the Three Realms Gateway Offering events, Kokomi resolved to find a new way to counter the Holy Soil so as not to disturb the vishaps of the depths.

## history
@version: 2.4
### STANDARD
About 2,000 years ago, near the end of the Archon War, Orobashi raised Watatsumi Island from coral and led the people of Enkanomiya there. Hunger drove them to invade Yashiori Island; the Electro Archon struck back, and Orobashi was slain with the Musou no Hitotachi, which split Yashiori and left the Tatarigami behind. The old main shrine was abandoned and the present one built, with the Divine Priestess leading the island. When the Vision Hunt Decree came, the shrine became the resistance's headquarters.
### LARGE +
Orobashi's navy was led by the shrine maiden Mouun and her twin sister Ayame; Mouun fell to the troops of the Shogun's lieutenant Sasayuri and Ayame vanished. A Sumeru scholar's study, found in Bourou Village, records the people's grievance at seeing their god's body mined for its ore by their overlord, and warns that such old resentments can be used by people with their own agendas. In the 2.1 Archon Quest, Kokomi accepted an anonymous sponsor's supplies at the shrine to grow the army. The sponsor turned out to be the Fatui, whose Delusions killed resistance soldiers, Teppei among them.
### ULTRA +
The Enkanomiya world quests (2.4) add that Orobashi had learned forbidden history in the depths and was condemned by the heavens, taking the guilt upon itself so its people would be spared. The Sumeru study had already floated a similar theory, that the war was a sacrifice ordered from above. Most islanders still give the simpler account of a war fought for food.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
A luminous, sea-washed palace of pearl and coral tones, with the sound of waves, soft violet woods and shells on the shore. During the war it was quietly busy: officers reporting on food shortages, soldiers on guard, a young leader calm in public whose hands shook before speeches. Since the peace it has felt calmer and more welcoming: Kokomi invites friends to stay, eat and read books with her.

## quests
### STANDARD
Archon Quest Chapter II, Act III "Omnipresence Over Mortals" (2.1): in "Sword, Fish, Resistance" Teppei brings the Traveler to the shrine, where Kokomi appoints them captain of the special operations unit Swordfish II; later the Traveler returns here and identifies the sponsor's weapons as Delusions. Kokomi's Story Quest (Dracaena Somnolenta Chapter) starts near the shrine. The world quest series "The Moon-Bathed Deep" begins when Tsuyuko, a shrine maiden of Sangonomiya Shrine, approaches the Traveler at Mouun Shrine; the entrance to Enkanomiya she describes is the deep pool directly across from Sangonomiya Shrine.
### LARGE +
The anecdote "Sangonomiya Kokomi: Reunion" (Travelers' Tales) begins with Kokomi standing outside the shrine.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the shrine is peaceful and still Kokomi's seat. The Vision Hunt Decree was abolished, peace talks with the Shogunate followed, and the Watatsumi Army now serves mainly as peacekeepers and helps rebuild.
### STANDARD +
Talks on bringing the island back under direct Shogunate rule made progress but were paused after the Fatui's meddling in the war came to light, so the island kept its army. Some residents still find peace with the Shogunate hard to accept. No 6.x (Nod-Krai) or 7.0-7.1 (Snezhnaya) story shown involves the shrine.

## entry: Divine Priestess of Watatsumi
@keys: Divine Priestess, Her Excellency Sangonomiya
@keys2: Watatsumi, Sangonomiya, Kokomi
### STANDARD
The Divine Priestess is the hereditary head of the Sangonomiya Clan and supreme leader of Watatsumi Island, ruling from Sangonomiya Shrine. The title means carrying a god's will in a mortal body and protecting the island and people in Orobashi's place. The current holder, Sangonomiya Kokomi, would rather have been a strategist working behind the scenes, but took up the role and is deeply loved by her people. Soldiers and islanders call her "Her Excellency".

## entry: Orobashi's relics at the shrine
@keys: Watatsumi Omikami, Orobashi, Orobaxi, Great Serpent
@keys2: Sangonomiya Shrine, Pearlescent Palace, Sangonomiya
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
The viewpoint text calls the shrine the place where the relics of the serpent god lie. Orobashi, called Watatsumi Omikami, once ruled in Enkanomiya, taught its people to farm and smelt, and raised Watatsumi Island from its own coral before dying at the Electro Archon's hand. Its bones lie on Yashiori Island and its resentment became the Tatarigami. The shrine remains the heart of its worship.

## entry: Three Realms Gateway Offering at Sangonomiya
@keys: Three Realms Gateway Offering, That Which Our Ancestors Entrusted
@version: 2.5
@availability: limited
### LARGE
During the limited-time event Three Realms Gateway Offering (2.5), Tsuyuko at Sangonomiya Shrine, now that the island was no longer under threat, revealed a family secret: besides the Goryou Matsuri, her ancestors had set a second set of trials concerned with the truth. This led the Traveler to Eboshi (Erebos), who introduced herself as an auxiliary of Sangonomiya and one of Enkanomiya's shrine maidens, and into the "Erebos' Secret" quests. Kokomi also commissioned the Traveler to go back down to Enkanomiya, then shrouded in darkness, for the sake of Watatsumi's future.
