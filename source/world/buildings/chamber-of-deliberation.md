## summary
### LITE
The Chamber of Deliberation is the assembly hall in the Sacred City of Tollan, inside Natlan's Ancient Sacred Mountain, where the Thirteen Sovereign Lords of the ancient dragon civilization once gathered to settle administrative and judicial matters. It is now a gold-trimmed, self-repairing ruin, and its preserved council records show a ruling class arguing over how to deal with humanity.
### STANDARD +
A "Speaker" presided and a numbered record was kept for each session. The surviving records show votes to impeach the Regent Kukulkan, to burn large human settlements, and to execute one of the lords for supposed betrayal. The hall also holds the permit needed to reach the Heart of the Sacred Mountain.

## geography
### STANDARD
Within the Ancient Sacred Mountain, the Chamber is a subarea on the dragons' level of the city. It has a second floor with a left passageway, a pool of lava, a dragon statue whose head hides one of the record boards, and a chamber of repair and purification facilities. The viewpoint here is named "Court of the Dragons".
### LARGE +
The hall was built "to outlast the very eons", in the words of the Secret Source Servant who guides the Traveler: bristling with defences and able to repair itself. An underground room north of the Chamber holds a separate inscription, the "Record Left by the 'Flamestealer'".

## governance
### STANDARD
The Thirteen Sovereign Lords were each styled by a title and numbered CL-01 to CL-13. CL-01, "Valiant One" Aj Nimalaj Chuq'ab Hun, acted as Speaker. Motions began with an agenda and were decided by vote; when consensus was reached, even those who abstained were bound by the decision. Any lord could bring a motion, and the Speaker invited the proposer to present the case before the vote.
### LARGE +
The lords named in the records, with their titles, are: CL-01 "Valiant One" Aj Nimalaj Chuq'ab Hun; CL-02 "Jade Maiden" Ix Je'lal K'ua'l Ka'; CL-03 "Weaver of Secrets" Ix Kemonel Jasjatem Oox; CL-04 "Hermit of Myriad Fates" Aj Awaj K'umilal Kan; CL-05 "Bestower of Bitter Springs" Aj K'astajibal Cha'ama' Ho'o; CL-06 "He Who Shakes the Earth Like Thunder in His Wrath" Aj Tininik Oyowal Waak; CL-07 "Toxic Rain Over Boundless Forests" Aj Chojojik Kamikabal U'uk; CL-08 "Priestess of Floral Candles and Skyborne Feathers" Ix Ajkotz'i'j Iq'ismal Waxak; CL-09 "Chronicler of the End Times" Ix Uxul Tz'ib Bolon; CL-10 "Undying Wings of Lofty Isolation" Aj Imuch' Paxlaq Lahun; CL-11 "Mirror of the Dawning Morn" Ix Raq'lapuj Chue'q Buluk; CL-12 "Murmuring Spring" Ix Roq'ratik'obala' Ka'alahun; CL-13 "Bonebreaker" Aj Q'uplajik Ooxlahu.
### ULTRA +
By the time of the latter records the Sixth and Tenth Lords had fallen, the Fourth Lord was absent for long periods, working on the "Phlogistonization of Life" in a citadel on the high cliffs, and the sovereign Xiuhcoatl is invoked as the lords' king whose vision they claim to serve.

## history
### STANDARD
The Chamber belonged to the era of the dragon civilization of Natlantea, whose capital was the Sacred City. The three readable records come from a time when the lords felt threatened by humanity's spread and its theft of the secrets of phlogiston.
### LARGE +
Record B-CL-11053 is an impeachment inquiry into Regent Kukulkan, proposed by the Second Lord. Its argument was deliberately erased, but the tally is 9 in favour, none against and 4 abstentions (CL-01, CL-03, CL-04 and CL-08): consensus. Phlogiston inscriptions over the erased section later emerge: a furious tirade against cowering, change-fearing creatures that ends with the line "The Sacred City has fallen."
### ULTRA +
Record B-CL-13097, on "Neutralization of Human Settlements", was proposed by the Fifth Lord Aj K'astajibal Cha'ama' Ho'o, overseer of Ek' Pulinik, to keep human numbers down by periodically incinerating settlements above a certain size. The Second Lord voted for it but called its reasoning weak; the Eighth and Twelfth Lords opposed it, the Twelfth urging instead that humans be manipulated into policing their own numbers by backing selected tribal leaders; the Ninth Lord, associated with Chichen Uctokah, abstained with scorn for the proposal's fear of humans and was taunted in turn over that domain. The vote was 5 for (CL-02, 05, 07, 11, 13), 2 against (CL-08, 12) and 3 abstentions (CL-01, 03, 09), so consensus was not reached. Record B-CL-16130 is a motion by the Twelfth Lord, custodian of Q'umqaraq'aj, to execute the Eighth Lord, Ix Ajkotz'i'j Iq'ismal Waxak, whom the Twelfth accuses of betraying their kind by tolerating a human who follows Xbalanque. The Eighth refused to defend herself; the Speaker defended respect for Xbalanque; the vote was 4 for (CL-02, 05, 07, 12), 2 against (CL-01, 08) and 1 abstention (CL-03): consensus, so the motion carried.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Gold-trimmed stone beside a pool of lava, with phlogiston inscriptions and a dragon statue. The preserved voices are contemptuous and ritualised, though some lords speak with respect of the human leader Xbalanque.

## quests
@version: 5.5
### STANDARD
Part of the World Quest Once, the Sacred Seat of Judgment (inside In Search of a Hidden Heart, in the Chronicler of the Crumbling City series). The Traveler, with Paimon and the Little One, follows a Secret Source Servant that takes the name Awanyu, which once belonged to a scribe of the Chamber. A permit needed for the Heart of the Sacred Mountain was left above the Chamber, but Rifthounds have broken in and the permit receptacle is found empty. The party activates a purification device and the Chamber's self-repair, raises a phlogiston barrier, receives the permit receptacle and finally activates the Permit to the Sacred Heart.
### LARGE +
Awanyu dislikes the place: it calls the hall a congress of arrogant fools and a den of pretenders who claimed the right to judge their own kin, and says no one regarded there as a criminal would like it either. Without the permit, Astral Assemblages inside the Heart attack unauthorised visitors.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the Chamber is unchanged since 5.5: a ruined hall with its records open to the Traveler. No later story use is recorded.

## entry: Sovereign Rulers' Council Records
@keys: Sovereign Rulers' Council Records, B-CL-11053, B-CL-13097, B-CL-16130
@keys2: Chamber of Deliberation, Tollan, dragon, Natlantea
@version: 5.5
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
Three numbered records survive: B-CL-11053 (impeachment inquiry into Regent Kukulkan, carried 9-0-4 with the argument erased), B-CL-13097 (a motion to incinerate large human settlements, failed 5-2-3) and B-CL-16130 (a motion to execute the Eighth Lord, carried 4-2-1). Each lists attendees by number and title and a vote count. They show a council divided between those who wished to cull humanity, those who preferred to manipulate it, and those who admired Xbalanque.
### ULTRA +
Quotes worth keeping in mind: the Fifth Lord claims humans have "stolen the secrets of wielding phlogiston", the Twelfth argues that humans can be "shepherded into betraying their own kin", and the Speaker declares the hero Xbalanque worthy of facing him in combat. Treat the lords' contempt for humans as characterisation, not neutral narration.

## entry: Awanyu the Scribe's Servant
@keys: Awanyu
@keys2: Secret Source Servant, Chamber of Deliberation, Little One
@version: 5.5
### STANDARD
Awanyu is the name a Secret Source Servant takes in the quest, saying it once belonged to a scribe of the Chamber of Deliberation who seldom touched quill or ink himself once Secret Source Servants were invented. It guides the party to the Permit to the Sacred Heart while voicing its contempt for the hall.
