## summary
### LITE
The Jade Chamber is a palace that floats in the sky above Liyue Harbor. It is the home and office of Ningguang, the Tianquan of the Liyue Qixing. She paid an astronomical sum for Plaustrite, a stone that can levitate, to build it, and from it she keeps watch over Liyue's commerce and affairs. Being invited up to the Jade Chamber is the greatest honour among Liyue's merchants. In the Archon Quest Ningguang sacrificed it to crush the ancient god Osial. She later rebuilt it, and the new Chamber floats over the harbor again.
### STANDARD +
Its map description calls it a symbol of Ningguang's might and of Liyue Harbor's unyielding will. The Chinese name means "Chamber of Many Jades".

## places
### STANDARD
Decks outside the palace gates look out over almost all of Liyue Harbor on a clear day. The main hall holds Ningguang's desk and her famous intelligence wall. This wall is covered in shipping schedules, profit-and-loss accounts, warehouse inventories, road-safety reports and diplomatic intelligence, all kept up to date. Visitors are carried up on a specially carved Plaustrite platform run by the guide Bu'yun.
### LARGE +
Items on her desk include a scale engraved with words that call contract the unchanging bedrock on which Mt. Tianheng stands. The shelves hold an aquamarine porcelain vase inscribed "From The Crux", and her scrolls are written on paper as thin as insect wings. In the rebuilt Chamber a pot holds a very old piece of Plaustrite and a scarred green plant, which seems to have come from the first Chamber.

## people
### STANDARD
Ningguang does business here and allows only her three trusted confidants into the working rooms. These are the secretaries Baiwen, Baixiao and Baishi, who sort intelligence and pin summaries to the wall. Zhenhai serves as a guard. Guests come by invitation, and in the Archon Quest Ganyu delivered the Traveler's invitation for Ningguang.
### LARGE +
In her character story, Ningguang leans on the railing looking over Liyue while her confidants fill the wall. She reaches her decision before they finish, shreds the papers and throws them out of the window. Merchants below read the falling scraps, her "snow", as a sign that big changes are coming to the business world. Once, a Sumeru alchemist who stumbled on records of an old noctilucous jade deal was invited up that same night for a talk.

## governance
### STANDARD
The Chamber is Ningguang's personal property, not a Qixing office, but she runs much of her Tianquan work from it. The Qixing are said to monitor all of Liyue's goings-on from it. Liyue folk believe "Ningguang knows everything". Her real network is the harbor's children, whom she rewards with treats, and she checks their reports against other sources.
### LARGE +
Rumour claims she has enchanted the Chamber to eavesdrop on private conversations across the city. Her character story presents this as rumour; the children are the truth.

## history
@spoiler: major
### LITE
In the Archon Quest the Fatui released Osial, Overlord of the Vortex, on Liyue Harbor. When the adepti's ballista attack failed, Ningguang brought the Jade Chamber down on him and sank him back beneath the sea. Months later she rebuilt it and used it to fight off Osial's wife Beisht, and this time the Chamber survived.
### STANDARD
The Traveler first came to the Jade Chamber at Ningguang's invitation in A New Star Approaches. Keqing told them the way up and the password: "Excuse me, do you sell the moon here?" Ningguang received their gift, talked about gods, archons and the Fatui, and a clue on her wall led to evidence that the Fatui were copying Sigils of Permission. When Childe released Osial, the Qixing and the adepti gathered on the Chamber. They fought from Guizhong Ballistae that Ningguang had replicated, manned by Cloud Retainer, Mountain Shaper and Moon Carver. Osial destroyed the ballistae. Ningguang then sacrificed the Chamber, which the Traveler helped send down onto the god, crushing him into the sea. She bid it farewell as an old friend and promised to meet again.
### LARGE +
In The Crane Returns on the Wind (Interlude Chapter Act I, 2.4), Ningguang held a public contest for the rebuilding materials. She promised to answer honestly any one question from the first three people to supply them. The Traveler, Shenhe, Yun Jin and Beidou won. Shenhe hauled a huge piece of Sunset Vermillionite out of the adeptus Sea Gazer's abode. Master Zhang forged the Wonder Cores from Starsplinter Iron and Subrosium, and Shenhe made the Adepti Sigils that Cloud Retainer had sent her to provide. Ningguang had sped up the rebuilding to lure out Beisht, about whom Beidou had warned her, and was ready to sacrifice the Chamber again. This time Liyue's people fought instead of the adepti. The Chamber fired on Beisht, supported by Keqing, Ganyu, the Millelith and Beidou's fleet. Shenhe froze Beisht's tidal wave, the Chamber fired on her again, and the Traveler and Shenhe followed her into the vortex and defeated her. Ningguang then held a victory banquet on the Chamber, where Yun Jin performed "The Divine Damsel of Devastation".
### ULTRA +
Before Turning Point, a coded notebook in the first Chamber spelled out "The Fatui will strike the Golden House". Its numbers formed a book cipher keyed to Customs of Liyue: Receiving God, which lay on Ningguang's desk. Between the two Chambers, the Ministry of Civil Affairs cleared the larger wreckage from the harbor, and the Millelith soldier Kun enlisted the Traveler to fish out the smaller flotsam (Fishing For Jade). Baiwen later began preparing the reconstruction. She was looking for a rare book, "Meadow of Gems", and bamboo ornaments for the interior (Return of the Jade Chamber?).

## functionality
### STANDARD
The Chamber floats on a great core of Sunset Vermillionite, the name for especially large pieces of Vermillionite, a variety of Plaustrite. Plaustrite has to be activated before it floats; until then it weighs about as much as ordinary stone. The Chamber is steered from a control compartment, where Wonder Cores combined with Adepti Sigils act as its control centre. In the battle with Beisht, Ningguang fired projectiles at her from the Chamber.
### LARGE +
Ningguang explained that the Chamber beat Osial through both its tremendous weight and the energy stored inside it. The weight struck the god with enormous force, and the energy set off the explosion. Every stone, beam and ornament added to that weight.

## culture
### LARGE
To the merchants of Liyue, being received in the Jade Chamber is the highest honour, and visitors bring rich gifts to win Ningguang's favour. Her schedule is booked far ahead. Ningguang's ambition reaches well beyond Liyue. She has said the Chamber began as a single room and now clouds Liyue, and she hopes one day it will hang above all seven nations and cast its shadow across Teyvat.
### ULTRA +
Ningguang has told the Traveler that a carefully made home reminds her of the effort she put into the Chamber, and called it the Traveler's own "Jade Chamber". She invites them back for the finest tea.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
High above the harbor, the Chamber is lavish and orderly. Its intelligence wall is crowded with pages and its open decks look down on the whole of Liyue Harbor. Ningguang's paper "snow" drifts down from it onto the city.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the rebuilt Jade Chamber floats above Liyue Harbor again as Ningguang's residence and office, and visitors can be ferried up to it.
### STANDARD +
It is the second Jade Chamber. Some items from the first one's collection were never recovered.

## entry: The Fall of the First Jade Chamber
@keys: Turning Point, Overlord of the Vortex
@keys2: Jade Chamber, Osial, Ningguang
@version: 1.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
In Turning Point (Chapter I, Act III) the Qixing and the adepti set aside their quarrel on the Jade Chamber to fight Osial, who had been released by Childe's Sigils of Permission. The Fatui sent soldiers through portals to wreck the ballistae; Keqing led the Millelith, and Xiao, Ganyu and Madame Ping blessed the Traveler. The adepti's combined shot failed and Osial smashed the ballistae and their platform. Ganyu insisted that the Chamber was their last line of defence and they could not give another inch; Ningguang answered that she would sacrifice it, and the Traveler drove a sword into it to send it crashing onto Osial.

## entry: The Lost Jade Chamber Collection
@keys: Jade Chamber collection, lost collection, Auction Games, Jade Chamber's Returning Guest
@keys2: Ningguang, Jade Chamber
@version: 2.4
### LARGE
When the first Chamber fell, much of Ningguang's collection sank into the sea near Guyun Stone Forest. She organised salvage, but many pieces passed into merchants' hands. In her hangout, The Jade Chamber's Returning Guest, she staged an auction so that she could identify and reclaim them. She admitted that she simply wanted her things back.

## entry: Beisht and the Second Jade Chamber
@keys: Jade Chamber Rising, Where the Heart Finds Rest, Sunset Vermillionite, Wonder Core
@keys2: Jade Chamber, Ningguang, Beisht, Shenhe
@version: 2.4
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
In The Crane Returns on the Wind, Ningguang rebuilt the Chamber with materials gathered through a public contest, lured Beisht, Osial's wife, and fought her with the people of Liyue rather than the adepti. Cloud Retainer praised the Qixing's preparation as considerable progress since the Osial crisis, while noting Shenhe's important part in the victory.
