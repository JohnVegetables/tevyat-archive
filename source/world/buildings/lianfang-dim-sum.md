## summary
### LITE
Lianfang Dim Sum is a shop in Yilong Wharf run by Lianfang. It sells local food, cooking ingredients and recipes, and is open at all times of the day.
### STANDARD +
Its stock includes tea eggs, a tea brew, smoked squab and, per the wiki, recipes for them; Lianfang pitches them as simple, convenient foods you can even eat on board a ship.

## philosophy
### STANDARD
Lianfang explains that Chenyu Vale's traditional dining is leisurely: sitting by the water with a pot of tea and a few dishes, drinking, enjoying the view and composing poems. But Yilong Wharf is a commercial port whose visitors want working meals they can finish quickly, so she adapts traditional dishes to suit them.
### LARGE +
Her plan is to win visitors over with convenient local food and then guide them toward the unhurried, proper way of eating, promoting traditional cuisine as commerce grows. She says a chef named Baatjan improved the dishes with supplemental ingredients that ease seasickness while keeping their original taste, so some regulars buy snacks before boarding.
### ULTRA +
The wiki notes that the shop lacks the map marker of the General Goods shops.
