## summary
### LITE
The North Harbor is one of two harbors serving the Kuuvahki Experimental Design Bureau on Paha Isle in Nod-Krai. A posted notice says its operation is suspended and cargo is rerouted to the South Harbor.
### STANDARD +
The notice tells anyone sending goods into the Design Bureau to use the South Harbor instead and promises a separate announcement of reopening.

## places
### STANDARD
A dock for the Bureau's freight, now shut. Two readable items stand here: the official suspension notice and a notice board with scrawled complaints from workers.
### LARGE +
One scrawl curses a Poruchik, Raskolnikov (name partly blacked out), over rules that forbid drinking; another orders machines to clean up before the Poruchik catches the staff holding a drinking party again. They suggest a strict officer and a crew that bends the rules.

## people
### LARGE
No resident NPCs are listed. Raskolnikov, the Bureau officer named on the board, has his own entry.

## status
### LITE
Closed to operations by notice as of the version read; goods go through the South Harbor. The sources read record no reopening by 7.1.
### ULTRA +
The North and South Harbors lie in the same subarea, so the closure is a redirect inside one facility rather than the end of all sea traffic to the Bureau. The Chinese name is simply 'North Harbor'.
