## summary
### LITE
Blackcliff Forge is Liyue's most important mining and smelting facility, carved into Mt. Tianheng. It was made by digging out the mountain and linking workshops and furnaces with tunnels blackened by dust. It is known as the birthplace of the Blackcliff and Prototype series of weapons, and white tassels are made there too.
### STANDARD +
A shift supervisor, workers and a Millelith guard work at the site. The forge's motto is "Let our actions be recognized by hard work. Let our achievements be what speaks first."

## places
### STANDARD
The forge is a network of workshops and furnaces joined by tunnels in the rock of Mt. Tianheng, in the Sea of Clouds area above Liyue Harbor. The tunnels are blackened by soot and dust, hence the name.
### LARGE +
Weapon text says it is said that long-forgotten passages inside lead to an ancient underground realm deep beneath the earth, and that mountain tribes sheltered in the forge during the tumultuous years when war dyed the land red. Neither claim is confirmed beyond the text.

## people
### STANDARD
Manager Kuan is the shift supervisor; Jingda and Iron Shoulder are workers; Gummer is a Millelith soldier posted there. The forge motto is repeated by Kuan himself.
### LARGE +
The Yun family, a house of smiths, claims the forge's founding tradition: the Stone Tablet Compilations credit the founder of the Yun family with carving the first dark paths through the mountains, and artisans honoured the family as progenitors thereafter.

## history
@version: 1.0
### LITE
The forge's best-known tradition is that the founder of the Yun family was the first to tunnel through the mountain. Five centuries ago the Yun family and the Han family smith Han Wu produced the Prototype designs, and the Blackcliff Series is also from here.
### STANDARD
During the cataclysm period the Yun family, who ran a family forge for hundreds of years, were led by Yun Huang and Yun Hui (seventh and eighth family heads per weapon text). After the monster threat ended, a martial arts renaissance created more demand for weapons than makers could meet, and Yun Hui turned to the master craftsman Han Wu, who drew up the Prototype designs. Over the following centuries the Yun family moved from smithing to Liyue opera, and by Yun Jin's generation few of them still forge.
### LARGE +
Weapon text also records that the Yun family's forging legacy had lasted hundreds of years when Yun Huang, tossing and turning at night, feared it would end with her.
### ULTRA +
The Yun family's later shift to opera is the origin of the Yun-Han Opera Troupe, founded by the time of Yun Jin's grandfather; its present director is Yun Jin.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Workshops and furnaces linked by dust-blackened tunnels inside the mountain; a working site with a supervisor, workers and a guard.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the forge remains in use, still producing weapons and white tassels.
### STANDARD +
No major change is recorded. The Yun family's founding link is told as history; canon does not say who runs the forge today.

## entry: Blackcliff and Prototype Series
@keys: Blackcliff Series, Prototype Series, Blackcliff weapons
@keys2: Forge, Liyue, Tianheng
@version: 1.0
### STANDARD
The forge's weapons are the Blackcliff Series and the Prototype Series. The Prototype designs were drawn up by Han Wu after research with the Yun family head Yun Hui, about five hundred years ago. White tassels are also produced here.

## entry: Cloudforged and the Yuheng
@keys: Cloudforged
@keys2: Blackcliff Forge, Keqing, Yuheng
@version: 4.7
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
Cloudforged, a weapon released as a reward of the limited-time event Mutual Security Enhancing Simulation (2024), is described in its text as a prototype of a standard-issue weapon that the Yuheng, Keqing, commissioned from Blackcliff Forge after analysing the ancient mechanisms that guard the adepti's abode on Mt. Tianheng. The same text gives the forge's lore (tunnels blasted black by dust, the Yun family's founder as first digger, mountain tribes sheltering there in war).
