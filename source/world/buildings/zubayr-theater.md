## summary
### LITE
The Zubayr Theater is a small theater troupe's home in the Grand Bazaar of Sumeru City, set in a tree hollow. Its manager, playwright and director is Sheikh Zubayr, and Nilou, the dancer styled the Star of Zubayr Theater, is associated with the troupe.
### STANDARD +
The troupe is small enough that nearly everyone except the actor Kourosh wears several hats; Zubayr himself also composes, choreographs and directs action. His name is a pun on Shakespeare, according to the wiki.

## people
### STANDARD
Sheikh Zubayr is exacting and dry, and says one must act with gumption. Kourosh is the juggler and aspiring actor, whom Zubayr describes as mediocre but persistent and still worth teaching. Najia is the props engineer, and Nilou is the troupe's gifted dancer.
### LARGE +
After Nilou's story quest Zubayr says new members have joined, and that Nilou is teaching the young Inayah to dance and judges her diligent. He considers talent the deciding factor in the arts.

## quests
### STANDARD
Nilou's Story Quest "To the Wise" (Lotos Somno Chapter Act I, 3.0) is built around the troupe's evening play and banquet. The Sumeru Archon Quest also involves Sheikh Zubayr among its cast.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
A cramped, busy backstage world of scripts, props and rehearsals in the middle of the market.

## status
### STANDARD
As of 7.1 the troupe still performs; the pages consulted record no later change.

### ULTRA
A playwright and manager who refuses interviews and asks that staff not be bothered over small matters. He tells the Traveler that Kourosh is the weakest performer yet worth teaching, and that he cannot expect everyone to be as gifted as Nilou. The name "Zubayr" is an Arabic masculine name meaning strong or brave.
