## summary
### LITE
Yumemizuki's Cozy Corner is a small store in the People of the Springs' area of Toyac Springs, Natlan, that offers psychotherapy through dream-eating yumekui-baku. It is presumably owned by Yumemizuki Mizuki, the Inazuman clinical psychologist and Aisa Bathhouse shareholder, and is currently staffed by a baku named Mint Daifuku.
### STANDARD +
A message board outside advertises that negative emotions can seep into dreams and that visitors on holiday can soothe lingering worries here. It also announces that the Natlan branch of Aisa Bathhouse was already under construction.

## people
### STANDARD
Mint Daifuku, a small floating mint-green baku with a white stripe on its belly, keeps the shop while its owner is away. It communicates only in squeaks, which the narration interprets: it greets the Traveler warmly, seems to treat them as a close partner of the owner, and is shy when patted.
### LARGE +
Yumemizuki Mizuki is not on site; she has her own entry.

## culture
### LARGE
The shop brings Mizuki's Inazuman therapeutic practice to Natlan, as part of the same wellness venture as Aisa Bathhouse. Its services depend on baku devouring nightmares.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Quiet, soft and slightly whimsical, with a restful, spa-like mood.

## status
### LITE
Open as of 7.1, minded by Mint Daifuku. The owner is not present.
### STANDARD +
The Natlan Aisa Bathhouse branch was only "under construction" in the board text; its later status is not covered here.
### ULTRA +
The message board texts cycle through several short notices when read; only the introduction was read here. Mint Daifuku shares its model with the Dough King, a wiki observation rather than lore, and the name is a pun on the Japanese sweet daifuku.
