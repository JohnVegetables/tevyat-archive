## summary
### LITE
Torchlight Books is a bookshop in Snezhnograd owned by Moes. It stocks books on Snezhnayan history and ballet.
### STANDARD +
Sandrone, one of the Fatui Harbingers, says the shop has a very nice selection of teas and cakes and takes the Traveler there to talk, remarking that Snezhnograd has some class compared with Nasha Town.
## people
### STANDARD
Moes is the owner. Other named people connected to the shop are Brovinov and Shchukina, who is listed as a fortune teller.
## quests
@version: 7.0
### LARGE
In the Archon Quest Great Deeds on the Tundra, Sandrone, speaking as the Harbinger Marionette, leads the Traveler to Torchlight Books to catch up over tea and insists the Traveler pay, since she says she already wasted a good cup of tea that day. What is discussed belongs to that quest, not to the shop.
## culture
### STANDARD
The sign reads "Enlightened Torch" in Snezhnayan script. The shop's books tie into the city's culture of ballet and historical scholarship.
## gameplay
### ULTRA
Game mechanics: the stock does not refresh and each book can be bought only once. It includes volumes of General History of Snezhnaya and Polestar Ballet Anthology.
