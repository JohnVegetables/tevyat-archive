## summary
### LITE
Zapolyarny Palace stands in Snezhnograd, Snezhnaya's capital, and is the residence from which the Cryo Archon rules. The Belyi Tsar held court here until his death in the cataclysm 500 years ago; Tsaritsa Anastasya has reigned from it since. It looks wrought from ice and snow, everything inside revolves around the Tsaritsa, and it holds her throne room (where she holds court with Harbingers and ministers), her private office and, in a hidden basement, a seal left by the Third Descender.
### STANDARD +
The name means "beyond the Arctic Circle". Venti named it as the Tsaritsa's seat as early as the Prologue, but the Traveler first entered it in the 7.1 Archon Quest, when Anastasya granted them unrestricted access.

## geography
### STANDARD
The palace stands in Snezhnograd in the Everfrozen Earth area, above a plaza, with steps where crowds gather. The Tsaritsa is sometimes seen standing alone on a high balcony overlooking that plaza. Its highest point can be reached by gliding along a chain of Stellar Vortices nearby (the hidden achievement "Because the Palace Is There", 7.0).

## places
### STANDARD
Rooms shown inside: a main hall; the throne room holding the Cryo Archon's divine throne of sculpted ice; the Tsaritsa's office beside the throne room; a hidden basement chamber holding the Third Descender's seal; the Tsaritsa's memory prism, in a corner of the office; and a visitors' lounge with a fire and hot tea for those waiting to be received. A Treasury is mentioned in lore.
### LARGE +
The lounge sits next to rooms where the Tsaritsa rests, so guards ask visitors to keep quiet. According to "A Brief History of the Pale Starborn", the chariot-sized, bear-shaped skull of King Miscislav of the Volkodlak, still marked by the Emperor's spear that killed him, is kept in the palace Treasury. In the Belyi Tsar's day the palace had rotating galleries where banquets and dances seemed never to stop (Vessel of Plenty).

## people
### STANDARD
Tsaritsa Anastasya lives and works here. Danica, Head Maid of the royal household, and Vesna, Commander of the Druzhna and considered the Tsaritsa's right hand, are often at her side. Chayka, a Domovoy, is her Confidential Secretary and head of Her Imperial Majesty's Own Chancellery. Druzhna soldiers guard the palace; Harbingers such as Pierro and Pulcinella attend court.
### LARGE +
Named staff: reception officer Lozkaya, who checks guests' identity, dress, audience times and knowledge of protocol and takes charge of any object unfit for the Tsaritsa's presence; Druzhna guards Mezhnaya, Myatezhnaya, Nevedomskaya and Gorkin (and Yermolay, placed here to uphold the old rites of courtesy); messengers Chistyakov and Shmeleva, who go out to summon Harbingers and other Fatui when the Tsaritsa wants them; the civil servant Innokenty, who handles Novokitezhgrad's affairs and struggles with letters full of archaic fae words; the maid Tropina; and the Druzhna officer Avreliya, on patrol nearby.
### ULTRA +
Palace officials are posted outward when needed: Zarechnaya was sent from the palace as Acting Chair of the Royal Energy Commission after the previous chair died suddenly. The conspiracy-minded NPC Arsenyev claims the Pyro Archon plans to melt the palace with volcanic heat (an obviously unreliable rumour).

## governance
@version: 7.1
### STANDARD
Snezhnaya is governed from here. Anastasya rules directly and with absolute power; her Edicts are the nation's highest authority. At morning court assemblies she sits before her ministers and Harbingers ("My Lords, Ladies, and Gentlemen"), hears reports, gives rulings and dismisses court. Tartaglia says she dislikes excessive court etiquette: basic good manners and respect are enough.
### LARGE +
At the assembly in 7.1 Pulcinella reported on civil affairs and Kristall rationing during her absence and submitted an Audit Office list of corrupt officials; Anastasya produced a further list from the House of the Hearth and assigned him the Druzhna's 3rd Company to review it. She also endorsed Pierro and Mitya's report halting their research until Ronova could be dealt with. At a later assembly she refused extra rail-maintenance funds to a Transportation Minister whose audit had missed a section, and instead honoured a child who had been maintaining it with the title "Track Captain". Her stated principle: let the wicked tremble and the righteous be rewarded.

## history
### STANDARD
The Belyi Tsar ruled Snezhnaya from Zapolyarny Palace until he died in the cataclysm 500 years ago, and Anastasya succeeded him. He was also the original guardian of the Third Descender's seal beneath it. At the end of 7.0 the Tsaritsa returned in triumph from slaying a blizzard-bringing monster and took her throne again, with Pierro bowing and Fatui ranks at attention. In 7.1 she received the Traveler at court, and later used the palace for her meeting with Rhinedottir that brought about Ronova's fall.
### LARGE +
Under the Tsar, fae nobles fond of luxury treated banqueting at the palace as their chief duty, and affairs of state were often settled over wine. The foreign alchemist and astrologer Professor Rotwang, nicknamed the "mad monk", became the Tsar's favourite and a frequent courtier; rumours told that Grand Duke Photius failed to poison him and that he summoned the late wife of the fae shapeshifter Filipov, though he never confirmed either tale (Vessel of Plenty).

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Grand, solemn and cold, standing "majestic" amid wind and snow. Courtiers and guards are formal and anxious to be correct; the Tsaritsa herself is calm, brief and hard to read.
### LARGE +
The maids are held to a standard of perfection: not a speck of dust on the tablecloth, every utensil in place, every light polished. Danica insists that every visitor, "an envoy of the heavens or a beggar on the street", is received with the same courtesy.

## status
@version: 7.1
### LITE
As of 7.1 the palace remains Anastasya's seat of rule. The Traveler may come and go freely and acts as her informal consultant. The Third Descender's seal beneath it is still closed; Anastasya holds six Gnoses, and the Pyro Gnosis is missing.
### STANDARD +
After Ronova's fall the research restrictions were lifted, and the Tsaritsa personally commended everyone involved. Each week Chayka hands the Traveler new Secret Tsarist Assignments at the palace.

## entry: The Third Descender's Seal Chamber
@keys: Third Descender's seal, seal of the Third Descender, Seal Chamber
@keys2: Zapolyarny, Tsaritsa, Anastasya, Gnosis, Gnoses
@version: 7.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
In a hidden basement of the palace stands a massive crystalline seal. The Belyi Tsar, its first custodian, said it was the last thing the Third Descender left before the Heavenly Principles killed him, and it seems to have escaped their notice. Every attempt to open it over the centuries has failed. Only Gnoses (the Third Descender's remains) draw any response, so Anastasya believes all seven must be gathered to reveal what is inside. Opening it is her second reason for collecting the Gnoses, besides amplifying her power to face the Heavenly Principles. She showed it to the Traveler in 7.1 as compensation for their cooperation.
### LARGE +
Its contents are unknown to everyone, the Tsaritsa included. In the same conversation she told the Traveler that her authority is "love", which she refuses to use or identify with, because the gods' love for humanity was imposed by the Heavenly Principles as a curse.

## entry: The Tsaritsa's Transcription Prism
@keys: transcription prism, memory prism, memory recording
@keys2: Tsaritsa, Anastasya, Zapolyarny, Pierro
@version: 7.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
A large prism resembling a Starlight Prism stands in a corner of the Tsaritsa's office. Believing her own memory cannot be fully trusted, Anastasya has for a long time recorded each day's memories in it. Pierro explains that, like a Stellar Linchpin, it carries the Third Descender's power and so is immune to certain laws of Teyvat, including alterations made through Irminsul.
### LARGE +
The wiki ties this to her past: when she was resurrected, the Heavenly Principles erased her memories of Saarelainen, the Third Descender, and the Belyi Tsar later told her about him. Pierro calls such objects variables from beyond this world and indispensable in the fight against the Heavenly Principles.

## entry: Court Assembly of 7.1
@keys: court assembly, morning assembly, Consultant to the Tsaritsa, unrestricted access
@keys2: Zapolyarny, Tsaritsa, Anastasya, Traveler
@version: 7.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Tartaglia arranged the Traveler's first audience by having their application approved for a court session. Anastasya opened by announcing the death of the blizzard-summoning beast, dealt with corruption and the Ronova problem, then turned to "the Traveler from afar": she called them a formidable adversary worthy of respect, proposed cooperation, ordered all investigations into them stopped and granted them unrestricted access to the palace. Afterwards she took them to her office, asked where the Pyro Gnosis was, explained why she wants the Gnoses and why Snezhnaya, having long pushed its technology to the limits the Heavenly Principles permit, lives under threat of a Nail of Retribution.
### LARGE +
In the office she introduced Danica, who had already sounded out the Traveler at Podvorye Restaurant; Anastasya told her that such methods risked the trust of a potential friend, and Danica promised to change her approach. The Traveler said the Pyro Gnosis had burned with Irminsul and gone into the Ley Lines; Anastasya answered that, as part of the Third Descender, it cannot coexist with the Ley Lines for long and will be cast out eventually. Pierro then took her to the Armory Palace for the Seals of Life experiment.

## entry: Rhinedottir's Audience and the Fall of Ronova
@keys: Rhinedottir's audience, Rhinedottir's visit
@keys2: Zapolyarny, Tsaritsa, Anastasya, palace
@version: 7.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
In Act IV Anastasya invited Rhinedottir, the Ruler of Life, to the palace, offering her information about Asmoday, whom she had glimpsed with a hooded figure beside the storm-beast. While Rhinedottir and Anastasya talked over tea, the Traveler, Pierro and Dainsleif held Ronova at Rankova Zorya. As she rose to leave, Rhinedottir sensed that the flow of time was wrong: Anastasya had used her six Gnoses as the nodes of a Gnosis-amplified Cryo field that slowed time in the hall. The window for resurrection that Ronova and Rhinedottir had agreed on passed unnoticed, Celestia reclaimed Ronova's authority, and Anastasya reminded Rhinedottir of her duty to resurrect those Ronova had killed, which she agreed to do.
### LARGE +
Naberius urged punishment for overstepping her authority, but Rhinedottir declined, praised the Tsaritsa as a worthy rival and left her unharmed. Anastasya credited both the Gnoses and her own insight over the past thousand years, and admitted the trick would not work twice. Earlier, Danica had escorted the guest in without fear, and Rhinedottir remarked that the Tsaritsa's taste differs greatly from the Vinster King's; Anastasya said she had visited Khaenri'ah's royal palace only once, after the cataclysm.

## entry: Secret Tsarist Assignment
@keys: Secret Tsarist Assignment, Envoy's Medal, Chayka
@keys2: Zapolyarny, Tsaritsa, Anastasya, Traveler, Chancellery
@version: 7.1
### STANDARD
Vesna lured the Traveler to the palace with a "prize" letter. Anastasya wants an adviser of real skill who answers to no faction, so she can understand the nation she is supposed to "love" without leaving the palace or relying on biased ministers. Danica doubted a free traveler would obey. The Traveler refused to become a servant of the court but agreed to help as a friend. Anastasya made them a Consultant to the Tsaritsa, with inquiries going through Chayka, and gave them the Envoy's Medal, proof of their status and unrestricted access.
### LARGE +
First tasks: judging three portraits of the Tsaritsa (the Korolevskiy Troupe's "The Tsaritsa in Spring", a stern portrait submitted by the Harbingers Pulcinella and Pantalone, and Chayka's own picture); she accepted all three, sending the Harbingers' portrait to the hall of Glupov, the spring portrait to the Korolevskiy Troupe's theater and Chayka's to the streets of Zapolyarny, where fae go to relax. At a later assembly the Traveler's railway report exposed a minister's faulty audit. Vesna notes that officials fearing a secret envoy is useful to the Tsaritsa; Danica says she does not mean to use the Traveler to spy on them. In gameplay terms this is the Tsaritsa's reputation system.

## entry: The Belyi Tsar's Court
@keys: Belyi Tsar's court, Pale Star court, Tsar's court, rotating galleries
@keys2: Zapolyarny, palace, banquet

### LARGE
Before the cataclysm the palace was the Belyi Tsar's seat. Artifact text from the Night of the Sky's Unveiling set describes endless banquets and dances in its rotating galleries, fae nobles who considered banqueting their most important duty, and state business decided while slightly tipsy. Professor Rotwang, an outlander favoured for his alchemy and astrology, became a fixture there, and some nobles may have trusted him more because he had no local ties. The text hints at a tragic ending that the courtiers did not foresee. After the Tsar's death, some fae nobles, furious that a non-fae had taken the throne, refused to seek an audience with Anastasya.
