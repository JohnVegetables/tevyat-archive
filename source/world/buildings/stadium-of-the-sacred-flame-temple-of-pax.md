## summary
### LITE
A section of the Stadium of the Sacred Flame along its southern side, in the Basin of Unnumbered Flames. It has an outer concourse and an inner fair-and-temple area, and seems to handle scholarship on ancient dragon relics.
### STANDARD +
The name Pax matches the sixteenth month of the Maya Haab' calendar, as the wiki notes.

## places
### STANDARD
Its map level is called the Fair and Temple, and a separate Outer Concourse holds many of its residents. A stall called The Salutary Furnace, owned by Alom, trades there.

## people
### STANDARD
On the outer concourse are the researcher Battuta and the coach Nzinga, among others (Coa, Coyopa, Hampeq, Ossie, Sumac, Vilca, Yantasar) whose roles the sources read do not give. Inside the fair and temple are Arcantel, a self-proclaimed emerging artist, and Oia, described as a hunter without a target; Keita and Tlilhua are also listed.
### LARGE +
Do not assign extra roles to the listed NPCs beyond what their own entries state.

## culture
### LARGE
Academic work on ancient dragon relics is linked to the Temple. A Saurian Relics Association message-board entry about an ancient Iktomisaurus dragon object is the evidence the wiki cites.

## status
### LITE
Open and populated as of 7.1.
### ULTRA +
The Temple of Pax sits on the same Stadium as the Speaker's Chamber, where the six tribes' chiefs meet; its fair and temple area is a separate map level from the open concourse.
