## summary
### LITE
Devil's Cut is the tavern of Dornman Port, in Windrest Peak (opened in 6.5). Its owner is Groovy. The name pairs with Mondstadt City's Angel's Share, and the two taverns are counterparts. The port tavern serves sailors, merchants and locals.
### STANDARD +
Angel's Share takes its name from brewers' slang for liquor lost to evaporation; by the same real-world slang, the devil's cut is the portion a barrel absorbs. Angel's Share belongs to the Dawn Winery; no source ties Devil's Cut to the winery.

## people
### LITE
Groovy is the tavern owner. Odolf, a Dornman Port businessman, drinks here.
### STANDARD
Groovy has blond hair in a ponytail, a mustache and beard, blue eyes and a brown and purple blazer. He greets visitors cheerfully, offers fruit juice to those who do not drink, and notes that the merchant Matthaus has just unloaded a batch of Snezhnayan Fire-Water, which tempts even him. He says "Hey, feeling down? Try a cup of this".
### LARGE +
Odolf is a businessman who recently travelled to Snezhnaya and returned shortly before the Traveler meets him. He sits at the tavern after the world quest "Winds Beneath the Tower of Silence" and says Mondstadt's beer still tastes best. He found Snezhnaya freezing cold and envied the fluffy fae guides there. He once dreamed of joining the Knights but took over his father's stall at the Guild instead.

### ULTRA +
Signs outside the pub, written in Teyvat script, read "Pub" and "Devil's Cut". The wiki records no shop or menu here; Groovy only chats, and the tavern is mainly a place of conversation and a quest start point.

## quests
@version: 6.5
### STANDARD
The world quest "Winds Beneath the Tower of Silence" begins when the Traveler approaches a group of locals gathered in front of the tavern, and leads to the old lighthouse. Its story: Odolf, disliking the grave-keeper Winslow, had planted a lure to attract elemental creatures to the lighthouse mechanism; while he was in Snezhnaya it drew an Eye of the Storm, which frightened Winslow into paranoia. Afterwards Odolf admits what he did, though not proudly: he thought it would blow over.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the tavern is open and unchanged; nothing in 6.x-7.1 content after its debut alters it.
