## summary
### LITE
Third-Round Knockout is a tavern on Chihu Rock in Liyue Harbor, owned by Degui. Zhiruo waits tables there and Iron Tongue Tian tells stories. The wiki notes it seems a favourite of Zhongli's.
### STANDARD +
Its name is said to come from a sailor who got drunk on three bowls of the house's wine-fermented sweet rice balls; before that the tavern had no name.

## places
### STANDARD
One tavern in the harbor, staffed by its owner, a waitress and a storyteller.

## people
### STANDARD
Degui is the boss and owner and is found outside in the daytime. Zhiruo is the waitress. Iron Tongue Tian is the storyteller.
### LARGE +
After dark the regulars include Jiangzhou, Uncle Gao and Uncle Sun.
### ULTRA +
Overheard in dialogue, Degui scolds Zhiruo for serving full portions and putting real wine into the boozy desserts, preferring to halve portions and use wine sparingly to make more money. Zhiruo says customers complain, but Degui tells her to listen to him and not to them.

## culture
### STANDARD
Xu Liushi, a sailor of the Alcor, names wine-fermented sweet rice balls as the tavern's specialty and says he got drunk after three bowls, which gave the tavern its name.
### LARGE +
The Chinese name echoes the Water Margin story of Wu Song, who drank many bowls of strong wine at a tavern named 'After Three Bowls, Do Not Cross the Ridge'. The wiki offers this as a likely reference.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
A harbor tavern of wine, stories and sweet rice balls, run by an owner who watches the takings closely.

## travel
### STANDARD
On Chihu Rock, the plaza area of Liyue Harbor.

## status
### LITE
Open as of 7.1, as far as the sources show.
