## summary
### LITE
The Brewblossom Stores are an underground facility on Tete Isle, in the Easybreeze Holiday Resort off Natlan's western edge, where Aphimead is refined and stored. Visitors can take part in a hands-on brewing experience called Honeydew Craftbrew.
### STANDARD +
Aphimead is Tete Isle's specialty drink. It is made with the help of aphids that tend Brewblossoms, and the Stores are the place where that process is opened up to visitors as part of the resort's offering.

## places
### STANDARD
The facility lies underground in the Northern Cavern of Tenochtzitoc, the older name of the resort island. It holds brewing barrels, a collecting device and refining equipment for Aphimead, plus storage for the finished mead.
### LARGE +
In the quest demonstration, brewing equipment has a suction port that takes delivered honeydew, and finished Aphimead flows from aphid-shaped and star-shaped hinges into cup-shaped structures. The area's music, "When the Mead First Ferments", is shared with the Easybreeze Market.

## culture
### STANDARD
Brewblossoms are plants known to possess spirits, and they close their petals on anyone who strays too close. Their pollen is a key ingredient of Aphimead. Brewblossom Aphids live with them in symbiosis, tending the flowers and feeding on nectar.
### LARGE +
Staff describe the method: aphids are guided to gather ripened honeydew from the brewing barrels; the honeydew is collected in a special device and refined using the strength of the staff's tatankasaur companions; a splash of added flavour or alcohol then turns the base into different kinds of Aphimead. Brewblossoms wither without nutrients and are revived by sending aphids to feed them.

## quests
@version: 5.8
### STANDARD
In the Story Quest The World Is Your Canvas! (the second part of the Sunspray Summer Resort), the Traveler's party is invited to try Honeydew Craftbrew. The staff member Takhar explains the process, and the bard Venti turns it into a friendly brewing competition with the Traveler. Venti jokes that the resort's meagre mead stock means visitors are meant to help replenish it.
### LARGE +
Later in the same quest a Whopperflower is found hiding among the Brewblossoms; Tlapalli reports that more may be lurking in a store room, and the party clears them from the lower level of the Me & Dew bar. Mualani quotes the brochure's boast about "honey-sweet Aphimead".
### ULTRA +
Elsewhere on Tete Isle, a letter from a crew member (Tecuhtli) to their captain recommends the bars Me & Dew and The Dewdrop for their good Aphimead and suggests stealing some to bring back to Nasha Town.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Honey-sweet stonework, humming aphids, clinking cups and cheerful holiday patter from staff: a working brewery presented as an attraction.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the facility is open as part of the resort. No role in 6.x (Nod-Krai) or 7.0-7.1 (Snezhnaya) story content is recorded.

## entry: Honeydew Craftbrew
@keys: Honeydew Craftbrew, Aphimead
@version: 5.8
### STANDARD
Honeydew Craftbrew is the visitor experience in which guests steer Brewblossom Aphids to carry collected honeydew to the brewing equipment, raising brewing progress until a lever completes the batch. The wiki describes it as hosted at the Brewblossom Stores; in the quest Takhar frames it as a way to help produce the island's Aphimead.
