## summary
### LITE
The Goth Grand Hotel is Mondstadt City's hotel, owned by Ludwig Goth. The Fatui delegation has reserved the entire building, and the guard Luke stands at the door to keep unauthorised people out. Ordinary guests can no longer stay.
### STANDARD +
The Goth family has long been in the hotel and catering trade and prides itself on serving Mondstadt. The Fatui lease runs against that tradition, which bothers Goth.

## people
### LITE
Owner: Ludwig Goth. Door guard: the Fatui member Luke.
### STANDARD
Goth also owns the Dawn Breeze Inn and rents out other housing in the city. Luke is dedicated to his post, repeatedly saying that the hotel is reserved for the Fatui delegation alone. He sometimes lets secrets about the Fatui slip and then hastily denies it. Early on, he predicts the Mondstadters will come begging the Fatui for help and expects Signora to arrive and handle the Stormterror crisis.
### LARGE +
Goth is upset about the lease but accepted it: Harbinger Pantalone paid the entire period upfront with a sum Goth could not refuse. Goth says the Fatui's behaviour concerns him more than Stormterror's: they seem always to be in meetings, and they even chased away the cleaning staff. He worries about what they are planning and fears for the hotel's name. He also admits he had planned to renovate the hotel, which the lease made moot.

## history
### STANDARD
Before the Traveler arrived, Pantalone booked the whole hotel for the Fatui's exclusive and indefinite use. Goth objected in principle, since his family motto is serving the people, but took the payment.
### LARGE +
Trivia: in the Chinese, Japanese and Russian versions of the game the hotel's name uses "Goethe" rather than "Goth".

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
A closed, tense building at the heart of the city: a doorway watched by a surly Fatui guard, and a hotel booked solid by a single party. Goth's cleaning staff have been chased away from the Fatui's rooms, and no ordinary traveller is admitted.
### ULTRA +
The sign hanging beside the door says "Inn" in the Mondstadt script rather than the usual Teyvat script, and the signboard text is simply mirrored when seen from behind.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the wiki records no end to the Fatui reservation; treat the hotel as still closed to the public unless a scene says otherwise.

### STANDARD
Goth is the owner and manager of the Goth Grand Hotel. He has a son, Marvin, whose betrothal to Marla he opposes. Goth also owned the house with a magic laboratory that Mona rents in her story quest, after she dispelled a Hydro seal on it. He wonders aloud whether he might take a trip to Sumeru.
