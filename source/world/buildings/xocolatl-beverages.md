## summary
### LITE
Xocolatl Beverages is a juice bar in the Stadium of the Sacred Flame in Natlan, run by Yantasar of the Masters of the Night-Wind. It specialises in unconventional drinks made from fresh fruit and spices, which locals enjoy.
### STANDARD +
Yantasar sells the idea as much as the drink: she advertises blessings of the Wayob and a taste as light as a Qucusaurus feather floating into the clouds.

## people
### STANDARD
Yantasar is the owner. Asked about it, she admits there is nothing special in the drinks beyond good, healthy ingredients; the draw is that they sound strange and people leave feeling elated. Natlan natives, she says, like that kind of thing.
### LARGE +
When the Traveler asks for something ordinary she falls silent for a long time, unable to picture an ordinary drink, and offers Grainfruit Meat Soup as a compromise.

## culture
### LARGE
The shop is an example of Natlan's playful food culture, where the novelty of a dish is part of its charm. The name comes from xocolatl, a Nahuatl word for a cacao drink, per the wiki's trivia note.

## status
### LITE
Open as of 7.1; nothing about it has changed in canon.
### STANDARD +
The stall sits within the Stadium near the Temple of Pax. Scenes should keep it light and visitor-facing.
### ULTRA +
Yantasar is affiliated with the Masters of the Night-Wind. Her idle remark that the drinks "can't taste that bad" and her admission that nothing special is in them make her salesmanship self-aware; she is sincere about ingredient quality. Whether alcohol is served is only implied by another NPC's remark and stays unconfirmed.
