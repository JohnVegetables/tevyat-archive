## summary
### LITE
The Heart of Force Inversion is a subarea of the Ancient Sacred Mountain, in Natlan's Great Volcano of Tollan, filled with pools of liquid phlogiston that flow upwards into the sky. Abyssal corrosion clogs its waterfalls until the Traveler clears it.
### STANDARD +
The World Quest Where Once Force Was Reversed sends the Traveler and the Little One here to destroy Oozing Cores and solidify the waterfalls with a Coagulation Pearl, opening the way toward the Heart of the Sacred Mountain.

## geography
### STANDARD
Liquid phlogiston cascades through channels and pools; one waterfall flows upwards towards the sky. Waterfalls of liquid phlogiston can be solidified into platforms and pillars, and corrosion has blocked some of the channels.
### LARGE +
The final solidified pillar connects to the lava pool just north of the Sea of Shifting Sentience, one map layer higher, with a Pyroculus part-way up. In places a see-saw of stone slabs lowers one while raising another, and a Secret Source Servant is stranded until a Tepetlisaurus is indwelled to add weight.

## people
### STANDARD
Secret Source Servants appear, and the Little One digs one out. Pyro Abyss Mages first accost the Servants, and Oozing Cores pelt the party with mud from above.
### LARGE +
The Little One and Paimon are the only named companions. Paimon is dismayed that the Abyss's influence has reached the Sacred Mountain.

## quests
@version: 5.5
### STANDARD
Where Once Force Was Reversed (World Quest, part of In Search of a Hidden Heart) can start by progressing In Search of a Hidden Heart or by defeating the Pyro Abyss Mages. The Traveler destroys the Oozing Cores and follows the Little One as the Servants turn waterfalls solid. At the top a new waterfall flows up from the pool's centre and is solidified into a tall pillar with the Coagulation Pearl, revealing a Phlogiston Unit.
### LARGE +
The quest awards a Luxurious, an Exquisite and a Common Chest, and a Phlogiston Unit. It is one of three companion quests with Where Once There Was a Calculation Array and Where Once There Were Arms Aplenty.
### ULTRA +
The quest's stated aim is to uncover a path to the Heart of the Sacred Mountain by cleansing the Abyss that is corrupting the ancient ruins. Completing it together with its two companions awards the achievement "Questioning the Heart of Flames".

## entry: Iridescent Legacy Part II
@keys: Iridescent Legacy, Kamikabal U'uk
@keys2: Heart of Force Inversion, Seventh Lord
@version: 5.5
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
Found by destroying the Oozing Cores beneath a rock arch, this inscription holds the last words of the Seventh Lord, Aj Chojojik Kamikabal U'uk. He marvels at humans who keep coming though weak enough to fall to a single blow, even as their skin sloughs off corroding corpses and more of their kin rise to take their place. Mortally wounded, he asks whether the bright light in their eyes once shone in dragon eyes too, and tells Xiuhcoatl that was a time before they abandoned their glory.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the area has been cleansed and is unchanged; no later story use is recorded.
