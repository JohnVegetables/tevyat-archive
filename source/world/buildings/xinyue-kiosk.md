## summary
### LITE
Xinyue Kiosk is a famous restaurant in Liyue Harbor, across from Mingxing Jewelry. It is considered the most authentic place for Yue cuisine and is correspondingly expensive: the wiki says it has a waitlist of about three months. Its dishes are mostly seafood.
### STANDARD +
Yueshu, the waitress at the door, offers dishes to take away. Notable dishes include Crystal Shrimp, Golden Crab and Fullmoon Egg, according to the character Francis.

## places
### STANDARD
Entered through the door next to Yueshu on Feiyun Slope. The upstairs section faces Northland Bank and stands above Heyu Tea House, though there is no way between them from inside.

## people
### STANDARD
Yueshu is the waitress outside, Heli is inside, and Francis is inside at night. Clerk Zhao is upstairs at night.
### LARGE +
Yueshu tells customers without a reservation that the kiosk has a limited number of tables at specific hours of the day and so tends to be fully booked.

## culture
### STANDARD
The kiosk is to Yue cuisine what Liuli Pavilion is to Li cuisine. Its specialty is seafood.

## quests
@spoiler: minor
### LARGE
In the quest Know Thyself, Know Thy Enemy, Gentry Maocai meets a Fatui Pyro Agent at the kiosk.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Elegant, busy and exclusive: a restaurant people book months ahead.

## travel
### STANDARD
Across from Mingxing Jewelry on Feiyun Slope.
### ULTRA +
The Chinese name, Xinyue-xuan, means 'New Moon Pavilion'. Yueshu's food stock includes Stir-Fried Fish Noodles, Golden Shrimp Balls, Fullmoon Egg and Golden Crab, and she also sells recipes for some of them.

## status
### LITE
Open and still booked up as of 7.1, as far as the sources show.
