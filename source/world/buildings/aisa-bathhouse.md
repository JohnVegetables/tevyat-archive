## summary
### LITE
The Aisa Bathhouse is an indoor bathhouse in Inazuma City's Tenryou district, inspired by the volcanic hot springs of Natlan. It offers a spa, fine dining and, since a renovation led by Yumemizuki Mizuki, psychotherapy from yumekui-baku (dream-eating) psychologists. Samurai of the Tenryou Commission are among its regulars.
### STANDARD +
Aikawa Susumu opened it after visiting Natlan and manages it day to day; Mizuki, the major shareholder, directs its important decisions. Many Inazumans treat it as a go-to place to relax. The bathhouse's map text mentions rumours of a special investor behind the scenes.

## geography
### STANDARD
In Tenryou, within Inazuma City. It has a hot-spring hall and a therapy room; the signboard shows a sea-duck and reads "ST" in the Inazuman script, probably an abbreviation of the Japanese sentou (public bath). The Japanese name 秋沙銭湯 uses "aisa", a kind of sea duck.

## people
### STANDARD
Aikawa Susumu is the owner, called "Boss" by neighbours but says Mizuki is the real boss. Two baku staff, Hyakujitsuhaku Shion and Shiba Murasaki, appear after the quest Dream Eater's Melancholia. Yumemizuki Mizuki is the clinical psychologist and major shareholder, and a yumekui-baku who devours nightmares to ease patients.
### LARGE +
Shion uses food in her therapeutic approach; Murasaki once blacklisted a difficult former patient. Aikawa says Mizuki usually vanishes in the evenings.

## culture
### STANDARD
The bathhouse sells "warmth for body and soul" in a Natlan-inspired setting. Aikawa says the spring water has restorative power, and reports that guests, including Tenryou samurai, speak candidly while bathing.

## history
### STANDARD
Aikawa went to Natlan, fell in love with the hot springs there and, once home, wanted friends in Inazuma to feel the same, but had no funds. Mizuki, then living among the People of the Springs, heard of the plan and invested a large sum, becoming the core founding investor. After the events of Incandescent Ode of Resurrection (Natlan), she returned to Inazuma and renovated the bathhouse, adding therapy sessions to wide acclaim.
### LARGE +
In Dream Eater's Melancholia (5.4) the bathhouse is struggling financially. Yae Miko urges Mizuki to sell her shares and invest in Yae Publishing House instead, and warns that Emura Ichigensai has begun a rival psychotherapy service; one of the bathhouse's baku suspects he is a former patient, Emura Chuuya. Mizuki is now working on expanding the brand to Watatsumi Island and to the People of the Springs in Natlan.
### ULTRA +
Aikawa notes that Tenryou samurai are so common that he gives regulars a discount, and half-regrets setting prices so low. During the rival-clinic episode, two patrons of Emura's clinic, Susukida and Umihiko, first call the bathhouse a scam but later arrive to book therapy there. Mizuki herself, when the Traveler asks, says digesting nightmares costs her emotionally; she asks them to return early the next day to book a follow-up.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Steam, warm light, quiet laughter and the clink of dishes, with dreamlike therapy rooms behind the baths. It is a place where powerful people drop their guard.

## quests
### STANDARD
Dream Eater's Melancholia (Mizuki's story quest, Tapirus Somniator Chapter, 5.4): the Traveler is invited to the bathhouse with a VIP pass after meeting Mizuki, receives therapy, and helps look into the rival clinic.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the Aisa Bathhouse is open and popular. It has operated since Inazuma opened (2.0), was renovated after Natlan, and Mizuki is working on expanding the brand.

## entry: Yumemizuki Mizuki and the Therapy Service
@keys: Yumemizuki Mizuki, Mizuki, yumekui-baku, Little Baku
@keys2: Aisa Bathhouse, therapy
@version: 5.4
### STANDARD
Yumemizuki Mizuki is a renowned clinical psychologist and a yumekui-baku, a dream-eating yokai. She eats a patient's nightmares and works through the emotions that come with them, which is a heavy burden for her. She runs one-on-one sessions at the bathhouse and sits behind its important decisions. The Traveler once finds her crying at the shore, where she explains this is the final stage of digesting the nightmares, when she feels all of their negative emotions.
