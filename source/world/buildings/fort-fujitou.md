## summary
### LITE
Fort Fujitou is a Watatsumi Army camp on Yashiori Island, where soldiers of the Sangonomiya resistance, led by Sangonomiya Kokomi, held ground against the Shogunate's forces. It sits among thunderstorms and the Tatarigami-stricken land of the island. The Traveler meets the officer Kaida Ryouma and the blacksmith Miyazaki Saburou there, and a man named Kaji who starts the Orobashi's Legacy quests.
### STANDARD +
The Japanese name means "Wisteria Helmet Fort". After Kokomi's story quest the background soldiers leave the camp, and by then the resistance has become a peace-keeping and rebuilding body.

## geography
### STANDARD
On Yashiori Island, beside the electric weather of the island, with an Electro Cube, Electro Rock Pile, Electro Seelie, Narukami Pillar, Thunder Barrier and Thunder Sakura Bough around it. Local specialty: Crystal Marrow.
### LARGE +
The island's storms come from the Tatarigami released when the wards of the Electro Archon were destroyed; fixing the wards in Orobashi's Legacy calms them.

## people
### STANDARD
Kaida Ryouma is a Sangonomiya officer who used to work for the Kujou Clan and fought against them. Miyazaki Saburou is a swordsmith from Tatarasuna, a successor of the Miyazaki Art, who joined the Army for protection and forges its arms. Others include the samurai Moriguchi, Nathan and Takahashi, and the workers Sumi and Yuuzou. Kaji moves to the Kujou Encampment after Orobashi's Legacy.
### LARGE +
Ryouma says the Army trails the Shogunate's elite troops but must believe it can win; after the war he is grateful for Kokomi and Gorou and wonders what became of his old Tenryou Commission. Saburou says Kannazuka is disputed ground, "the hill where no gods reside", and tells the Traveler about Orobashi.

## governance
### STANDARD
Military camp run under Kokomi and General Gorou. Kokomi is the leader; Gorou is the general; Ryouma is an officer. The Army is called "rebels" by the Shogunate.

## history
### STANDARD
During the Vision Hunt Decree war, the camp was a staging post for the resistance. Kaji arrives there to look into the Yashiori storms, which he ties to the destroyed wards and the remains of Orobashi. After the Traveler persuades the Raiden Shogun to change course, the Decree ends, and peace talks begin; Ryouma says soldiers there are glad, and the Army keeps order and helps rebuild.
### LARGE +
Miyazaki Saburou's account of Orobashi and the Electro Archon's battle explains why Yashiori Island lies in ruin and how Fort Mumei came to be. See Orobashi's Legacy and the entry on Fort Mumei.
### ULTRA +
The wiki lists "Nathan" among the Army samurai; elsewhere he is flagged as a Fatui infiltrator, so treat him as a hidden agent rather than a loyal soldier.

## dangers
### LARGE
Hilichurls, Nobushi and Kairagi, Treasure Hoarders, slimes, Mirror Maidens and Crackling Axe Mitachurls; the Tatarigami weather of the island.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Rain, thunder, tents and forge-smoke; soldiers worn down by war, with a note of relief once it is over.

## quests
### STANDARD
Orobashi's Legacy (Kaji's quests, Parts I to V) begins here; Kokomi's story quest Warriors' Dreams Like Spring Grass Renewing ends the war's aftermath.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the camp is still a Watatsumi Army site, though quieter, since the Vision Hunt Decree ended and soldiers moved on to peace work.

### STANDARD
Kaji, a man at Fort Fujitou, investigates the island's endless rain and lightning, which he believes comes from destroyed ancient wards linked to the slain Orobashi. The Traveler repairs wards at Musoujin Gorge, Serpent's Head and Jakotsu Mine; Fort Mumei then rises from the sea and the final ward is restored.
