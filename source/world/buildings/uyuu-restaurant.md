## summary
### LITE
Uyuu Restaurant is a tavern-style restaurant on the commercial street of Tenryou in Inazuma City, owned by Okazaki Rikuto, who calls it an izakaya, with Okazaki Erika cooking and serving. A bulletin board beside the entrance carries messages from regular customers, including General Gorou. Its Japanese name Uyuu-tei is written with characters that echo a Chinese word for "nothing" or "nonexistence".
### STANDARD +
The owner is a retired figure of the Tenryou Commission who still gets visits from yoriki and doushin. Patrons use the bulletin board to thank him, tease him, ask him for favours and leave letters and gifts in his keeping, and he answers them there.

## places
### STANDARD
Uyuu Restaurant has a ground floor with tables and an upstairs with a mural. A bulletin board stands by the entrance, and an aged temari, a children's ball with scars and burrs, sits by a table; the name Okazaki Seishimaru is written on it. Outside, the food critic Matsumoto can usually be found.
### LARGE +
The temari raises two questions in the Traveler's mind: whether it has been there all along or was found and placed there by someone. Erika takes orders at the restaurant, while Rikuto sits and drinks sake, saying that after a busy life he is owed a drink or two.

## people
### LITE
Okazaki Rikuto owns it; Okazaki Erika cooks and serves.
### STANDARD
Rikuto is the owner, an older man who was once with the Tenryou Commission and says he had a stand-up reputation there; yoriki and doushin still ask his advice. He praises Erika's cooking as among the best in Inazuma City, drinks sake at the restaurant and tells a young Traveler there is no hurry about drinking. Erika is the cook. When asked how they met, she says it is a long story: when she was young she was ordered to watch an important figure at the Tenryou Commission, and she thanks the archons he eventually retired so they could marry.
### LARGE +
Matsumoto, a travelling food critic found outside, rates Uyuu and Kiminami Restaurants both mediocre; he prefers plain food and says he can only give fair reviews if food is given to him rather than paid for. Rikuto notes that major figures from headquarters have visited recently and that something seems afoot in Inazuma; asked about politics, he says the Tenryou Commission believes in the power of the Almighty Shogun, who must have her reasons, and that a traveller from afar need not worry.
### ULTRA +
Rikuto tells the Traveler, once they have done the quest series "Film Notes", that Seishimaru was once a relative of his and that the tale was the talk of the town in his younger days; he adds that real life is often more exciting than fiction. During the limited-time event Hues of the Violet Garden (2.6) he remarks that there have been many lively young customers.

## governance
### STANDARD
It is a private business owned by a retired Tenryou Commission man. Customers include officials, soldiers and travellers, judging by the messages left there.
### LARGE +
The Tenryou Commission and the Sakoku Decree touch the restaurant through its patrons. A message from someone close to the Tenryou Commissioner thanks Rikuto for his guidance, while a customer asks him whether his status at the Commission could help him find a way round the decree to get Dandelion Wine; Rikuto answers that there is no such way.

## culture
### STANDARD
The bulletin board is the restaurant's best-known institution, a place where people leave messages and the owner answers them. The messages are sometimes comic and sometimes moving.
### LARGE +
Examples from the board: Arataki Itto threatens Kujou Sara, the "tengu of the Kujou Clan", over taking his Vision and says he will await their duel; Rikuto replies that whatever Sara did, Itto is welcome to relax there. Beidou praises Rikuto's friendliness and the wine, and Rikuto asks for stories of Haishan. Madarame Hyakubei apologises to the Kamisato Clan for furniture damaged by his drunk son. Yae Miko thanks him for the sakura wine and mochi and notes that the wine tasted different; he says he prepared it specially. Ogura Yuu leaves a message that he is being stationed at Tatarasuna, and worries for his sister Ogura Mio's business. General Gorou and his people accidentally reveal in a message that they were conducting secret surveillance, and Rikuto notes nothing was actually removed.
### ULTRA +
Other messages: a patron tells Hiromi to go back to being a good public servant, and Rikuto replies that his own career in public service ended long ago. A group says it will take big risks and may not return; Rikuto reminds them there is still much to live for and that they still have two years before they can drink legally. Several anonymous guests mention the Sakoku Decree: one asks when it will be lifted, and Rikuto answers that he believes better days will come and says Hiromi left a letter and a gift that he is keeping for them. After the story quest Radiant Sakura, new messages appear saying that the decree is history and the wine will be shipped once another party's shipment arrives, and a patron hesitates over whether to give away items that a person who left might one day return for; Rikuto offers to keep them.

## history
### STANDARD
The restaurant is first mentioned in Someone's Drifting Bottle, a message found in the world from version 1.2. The bottle's note, written by Hiromi to Yan'er, asks whether she remembers Uyuu Restaurant, says they will meet there and encloses money for wine. In the 2.0 world quest "Hiromi's Watch" the Traveler delivers the bottle's contents to Yan'er, who says the restaurant seems a fine place and that she will go there for drinks, and the Traveler later reminds Hiromi of the name.
### LARGE +
Shun, one of Hiromi's old friends, says the three had a drink at Uyuu Restaurant the day before the Sakoku Decree took effect; Yan'er left Inazuma, he says, because of the decree. This ties the restaurant to the era of the decree.
### ULTRA +
Rikuto, Erika and Matsumoto also appear in the cast of a version 7.1 event, described in its own entry below.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
A drinking house with sake on the table, a bulletin board crowded with notes and a landlord who greets customers as youngsters. The board's messages show soldiers, adventurers and officials passing through under the shadow of the Sakoku Decree.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the restaurant is open under Rikuto and Erika; the board has Sakoku-era messages plus ones added after Radiant Sakura.

## entry: Okazaki Rikuto
@keys: Okazaki Rikuto, Rikuto, Mr. Okazaki
@keys2: Uyuu Restaurant, Tenryou
### STANDARD
Rikuto is the owner of Uyuu Restaurant, a retired Tenryou Commission man who drinks sake at his own table. He answers the board's messages in person-to-person replies, calls young customers youngsters, offers barbecued skewers, and says Seishimaru was once a relative. His exact former rank is not stated.

## entry: Okazaki Erika
@keys: Okazaki Erika, Erika
@keys2: Uyuu Restaurant, Tenryou
### STANDARD
Erika is the cook and waitress at Uyuu Restaurant, who welcomes customers and takes orders. She was once assigned to keep watch on an important Tenryou Commission figure, who later retired, and is implied to be Rikuto's wife.

## entry: Someone's Drifting Bottle and Hiromi
@keys: Someone's Drifting Bottle, Hiromi's Watch, Hiromi, Yan'er
@keys2: Uyuu Restaurant
@version: 2.0
### STANDARD
A drifting bottle found in waters southeast of Dragonspine holds a letter to a lost friend suggesting a reunion at Uyuu Restaurant with money for wine. The Traveler carries it to Hiromi, then to Yan'er, who says she does not know any officer who catches thieves but may go for drinks. Shun, the third friend, says the three drank at Uyuu the day before the Sakoku Decree took effect.

In the limited-time 7.1 event "Silverwing in Pursuit of the Moon" (24 September to 12 October 2026), Rikuto, Erika and Matsumoto appear in the event's cast. Rikuto says it feels like being young again, and Erika wonders how long it has been since they took a trip together, suggesting they cook another hundred dishes together. Matsumoto notes that the food is seasoned boldly and says he will talk to Anna when he gets back. The event is canon but time-limited.
