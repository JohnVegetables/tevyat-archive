## summary
### LITE
The Snezhnograd Veche State Hall is where the Snezhnograd Veche, Snezhnaya's legislative assembly, holds its regular meetings. Citizen representatives from different groups discuss newly issued laws there, a tradition kept since the early days of the nation. It is presided over by 'The Rooster', the Fatui Harbinger Pulcinella.
### STANDARD +
Because members of the Eleven Fatui Harbingers are entitled to take part, the debates blend popular representatives with Fatui officials, and often turn on Kristall and Project Stuzha.

## people
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
Pulcinella, as The Rooster, chairs the sessions and is Speaker of the Veche (the previous Speaker was I. Ivanovna N.). Named at the hall: the Veche Councillors Eteri and Euphrosiniya, the Privy Advisor Golitsyn, Slavina, Zotov and a guard named Stepan. In the 7.0 session delegates and speakers include Tartaglia as 'Childe', Pantalone as 'Regrator', Sandrone as 'Marionette', the scholar Arapov, the Druzhna officer Akvilina and Sharova, who speaks of the Energy Commission's reports.
### LARGE +
Spectators include the troupe singer Vodyanitsa, who brings the Traveler, and the reporter Mironova of 'Bring Out the Kvass', a street-stall newspaper.

## governance
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
Decisions are made by majority vote in sessions chaired by a Speaker. The Veche was founded by Anastasya to replace the aristocracy of the Snowland Fae from the Belyi Tsar's reign. Motions are drafted by the usual procedure and raised later for evaluation, as when the Rooster supports drafting a proposal on collecting regional Kristall data.
### LARGE +
Although nominally deliberative, the Fatui are usually able to carry the policies they favour with little resistance. Its chair, Pulcinella, gives statistics on energy commitments and the stabilising production and consumption of Kresnik Kristall, and presents disaster-relief figures supplied by Pantalone.

## culture
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
Debate is formal: the chair calls 'Order', opens the floor to representatives, and moves to a vote when no new substantive issue remains. In the audience seats, a reporter supplies a running commentary.
### LARGE +
Vodyanitsa says the Veche covers the same ground every time and that people are not seeing tangible benefits; in the aftermath of Project Stuzha's suspension the atmosphere is said to have lightened.

## atmosphere
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
A hall of long speeches: rows of delegates, a dull and lifeless room that livens up when statistics are challenged, and the slam of a hand on the table when tempers rise.

## quests
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
Archon Quest Silence Alone is Disfavored (Chapter VII, Act I, 7.0): the Traveler listens from the audience seats to a session about extending Project Stuzha. Arguments include whether Fatui families in remote areas receive 15 percent less Kristall than the average civilian family in Snezhnograd, according to Tartaglia, and how Pantalone justifies the current framework.
### LARGE +
The scene introduces the Rokot and their most infamous member, Lelek, who in Vodyanitsa's words is obsessed with ending Project Stuzha and uses any means to change votes. Tartaglia argues for a clear and consistent rationale for allocation; Pantalone answers that fairness is relative and that census data are outdated.
### ULTRA +
The session ends with a final vote: sixty-one percent favour continuing Project Stuzha, so it stays in effect until the next Veche period, though the margin has shrunk again after the factory owner Chugunov votes against it. Vodyanitsa suspects Lelek forced his vote. The Rooster closes the session with a short motto about debating for the nation's glory and solidarity and the aurora shining on. The Harbingers Tartaglia and Sandrone then approach the Traveler outside.

## status
@version: 7.1
### STANDARD
In use. After Project Stuzha was put on hold, the mood at the Veche eased, as Tartaglia says in 7.1.

## entry: Snezhnograd Veche
@keys: Snezhnograd Veche, the Veche
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
The Veche is Snezhnaya's legislative body for administration and governance, based in this hall. Anastasya established it to replace the Snowland Fae aristocracy. Pulcinella is Speaker, and Harbingers can take part in sessions.
### LARGE +
The name echoes the veche, an assembly in medieval Russia where citizens debated key matters.

## entry: The Veche Session of Silence Alone is Disfavored
@keys: Veche session, Project Stuzha debate
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
In the Archon Quest the Traveler sits through a Veche session in which Childe presses for fairer Kristall allocation for Fatui families, and Regrator answers that consistent allocation fails when equipment breaks down and census data are old. Arapov defends Project Stuzha as a framework rather than the cause of hardship.
### LARGE +
Akvilina proposes taking Childe's suggestion to Commander Vesna, warning of the Druzhna's workload; the Rooster supports drafting a proposal, and Sharova points out the Energy Commission already collects statistics for each city.
