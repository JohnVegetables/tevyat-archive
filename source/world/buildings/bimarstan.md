## summary
### LITE
Bimarstan is the hospital of Sumeru City, run by the Akademiya, which gives free healthcare to every resident of Sumeru. It stands just southeast of the city's northwestern Teleport Waypoint. Interns and a doctor staff it, and its name is a variant of the old word for hospitals in the historic Islamic world.
### STANDARD +
When Eleazar cases rose in Sumeru City, a notice from the mahamatas urged residents with symptoms to go to Bimarstan for treatment. In the Traveler's voice-over, Paimon is amazed that medicine is free.

## people
### STANDARD
The doctor Zakariya and the intern doctors Golshan and Tamara work at Bimarstan. Patients and their families seen here include Javad (by day), Lila and Tahmina.

## places
### STANDARD
A message board nearby, Bimarstan's Proverb Column, displays the sayings of past Akademiya researchers at random, with the stated aim of following the pioneers' trail to the light of wisdom. Behind the building is a medical file that disappears once read.
### LARGE +
That file records the case of Giyath Khayyam, a 65-year-old male patient with a brain disease of unknown cause: his memory worsened over time and he was emotionally unstable, talking about childhood memories. He had applied to retire from Amurta, hoping a stable life would slow the decline, and Amurta announced that the sage Siman Farrokhzad would ensure his treatment.

## governance
### STANDARD
Operated by the Akademiya as a public service for all Sumeru residents.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
A calm, clean clinic in the city, with the quiet of waiting families and the cautious optimism of a free-of-charge ward.

## status
### STANDARD
As of 7.1 it remains the city's free hospital; the pages consulted record no later change.

## trivia
### ULTRA
Of the Proverb Column's quoted researchers, the wiki lists Albiruni, Alva Nikola, Ayesha, Cyrus, Fadhlan, Hipakretisa, Imhotep, Iskandar and Mahitab, with lines such as Cyrus' "Change is the only constant." The Chinese name of the hospital translates roughly as House of Health.
