## summary
### LITE
Central Station is the largest railway station in Snezhnaya, in the capital Snezhnograd. The official map text calls the railways the nation's veins and the Central Station the heart of a 'titan of cold iron and steel'. Arriving passengers pass inspections by Snezhnaya Royal Railway Transport Bureau staff, then ride an elevator up to the heart of Snezhnograd.
### STANDARD +
The West, East and North public train routes all start here.

## geography
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
The station is split into an Exit Level, where passengers leave the station and meet inspectors and news sellers, and a Platform Level with the tracks. An elevator lifts people from the station to the core of Snezhnograd.
### LARGE +
The viewpoint named 'Central Railway Hub' is located here.

## people
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
Transport Bureau personnel include Safety Officers Gurevikov, Iliana and Sochikov; Conductors Kholodnaya, Aryna, Bazhenov, Kira and Rudov; and the Ticket Agent Voskresensky. Newsies Laktionov and Katia work the concourse. Mamontov is a freelance writer. Other NPCs include Andrevich, Chagina, Emma, Kholmogorov, Leonie, Tsvetkov, Uliana, Urusov and Vedomek on the Exit Level, and Arseny, Daniil, Dobronravov, Fyodor, Gerard, Ilya, Nadyushenka, Pecherskaya, Shemyakin, Svetlana, Veronika and Vikhrov on the Platform Level.
### LARGE +
The Bureau also has train announcers such as Agapov and Kasova on the public trains; they announce stops such as Morepesok to passengers.

## governance
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
The Snezhnaya Royal Railway Transport Bureau, also called the Transport Bureau, runs Snezhnaya's railway system, and its staff inspect arrivals here. The Bureau answers to Zapolyarny Palace according to the wiki's infobox.
### LARGE +
The wiki also notes the Bureau is sometimes called Chicota, based on a weapon description.

## culture
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
Newsies sell papers on the concourse, and passengers pass inspection before they enter the city.

## travel
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
Public trains are managed by the Transportation Bureau and make all stops on a route. The West route runs Central Station, Morepesok, Rankova Zorya; the East route runs Central Station, Svetloledovka, Sretomorozsk, Rankova Zorya; the North route's other stations are not yet known.
### LARGE +
To call a public train, players select 'Wait for the train to arrive at the station' at a station sign. This is a gameplay option; it is not part of the world setting.

### ULTRA +
Train announcers call Rankova Zorya the 'Temporary Station' (one has to remember its old name) and remark that someone must be keeping the abandoned stations running. At Morepesok they note that passengers interested in sea fishing may wish to seek out the captain of the 'Sea Tsar'.
## atmosphere
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
Official map text compares the place to the ever-pounding heart of a nation of cold iron and steel, with the railways as its veins.

## quests
@version: 7.1
### STANDARD
In the Archon Quest One Move Left to Make (7.1), the group learns that someone has possibly stolen Seals of Life from the frail and elderly, and they come to Central Station, where Alyosha, made up to look old, approaches a suspicious man named Avaz.
### LARGE +
Alyosha pretends he wants his life extended. If Avaz is convinced, he leads Alyosha outside the station and asks for payment in a few days; if not, he tries to flee and the Traveler catches him.

## status
@version: 7.1
### STANDARD
Running. The station remains the Snezhnograd entry point for rail travel, with inspections still in place.

## entry: Snezhnaya Royal Railway Transport Bureau
@keys: Transport Bureau, Railway Transport Bureau, Transportation Bureau
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
The Snezhnaya Royal Railway Transport Bureau, often shortened to the Transport Bureau, is the organisation in charge of Snezhnaya's railway system. At Central Station its safety officers, conductors and ticket agent are posted, and its staff inspect arrivals before they enter Snezhnograd.
### LARGE +
The wiki's faction page is a stub; it lists the Bureau as affiliated with Zapolyarny Palace and based in Novokitezhgrad.
