## summary
### LITE
Bounama's Grocery is a small general-goods shop in the Chuwen Fair of the Stadium of the Sacred Flame, in Natlan's Basin of Unnumbered Flames. It is run by Bounama, who is affiliated with the Masters of the Night-Wind, and it sells ordinary cooking ingredients. The shop is open at all hours and restocks every day.
### STANDARD +
Bounama is protective of quiet: he asks customers to keep their voices down because noise can disturb the mixing process he carries out there. Payment is on trust. Customers take what they need and leave the money to one side, and a crafting bench in the shop may be used by anyone when it is free, without asking him first.

## places
### STANDARD
The shop is a point of interest inside the Chuwen Fair, marked on the map as a general-goods vendor. It is not a separate district: it belongs to the fairground at the Stadium of the Sacred Flame.
### LARGE +
A crafting bench stands in the shop. Bounama treats the surroundings as part of his craft, which is why he insists on quiet.

## people
### STANDARD
Bounama is the only person associated with the shop. He is curt but willing to explain himself when someone asks a thoughtful question, and he is pleased by customers who think about environmental factors in crafting.
### LARGE +
He says that most makers know a potion changes with the amount of spinel fruit powder, or that one extra line can change a talisman, yet forget that sound can affect crafting too. When it is pointed out that the surroundings are not quiet, he replies that he has already accounted for that and bases everything on his existing environment, adding that the comings and goings of people and their stories are part of production. He quotes a saying of the Masters of the Night-Wind: "All has been understood, and the future becomes the past."

## economy
### STANDARD
The stock is simple cooking ingredients, including mushrooms, salt, pepper, onions, milk, tomatoes, cabbage, potatoes, wheat and glabrous beans. Stock refreshes daily.
### LARGE +
Asked whether he fears theft, Bounama says the idea of profiting from another's labour can plant a bad seed even in an honest person, but he shrugs it off: the goods are only odds and ends that the Mol Stone House sells on commission, and if something goes missing he can draw a couple of prayer talismans to make the money back.
### ULTRA +
Game note (gameplay, not lore): the shop restocks daily, and reaching Tribe Reputation Rank 4 with two of Natlan's tribes gives a permanent 10 percent discount.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Hushed and slightly conspiratorial for a market stall: customers are shushed, coins are left to one side, and the owner talks about mixing. Around it the fair carries on as usual.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the shop is unchanged since its introduction in 5.0, and no later story role is recorded.
