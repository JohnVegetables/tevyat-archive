## summary
### LITE
Hanfeng's Ironmongers is a blacksmith in Liyue Harbor, marked on the map with a forging icon. It was founded by Hanfeng, nicknamed "Cold Blade", and is now run by his son, Master Zhang.
### STANDARD +
Its map description says Liyue has known the smithy for hundreds of years, that it is a favourite with the people of Liyue, and that with the right materials it never disappoints a customer. It quotes the saying "May the flames spread to all, and never be extinguished".

## people
### STANDARD
Master Zhang is the blacksmith; he sells basic weapons and will forge to order if the customer brings the right materials. He says repairs are free, and asks to be told whenever a customer finds good ore. Through repairing people's gear he has built up a network that feeds him tips about ore, including odd crystal ore veins he cannot yet work.
### LARGE +
Hanfeng, the founder, has retired to Qingce Village, where he works the fields below his house. He misses his son and worries that he is devoted only to work; he even tries to matchmake the Traveler with him. He is also uncle to Suling. He says he built the business from nothing, that his body has grown too weak to lift a hammer, and that he still absentmindedly gathers iron chunks out of habit.
### ULTRA +
Hanfeng says his apprentices in the Harbor would have been the Traveler's age, and that he dislikes the Harbor's bustle. He asks the Traveler to tell his son that his father is safe and well.
