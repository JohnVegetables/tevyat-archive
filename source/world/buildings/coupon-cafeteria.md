## summary
### LITE
The Coupon Cafeteria is the canteen of the Fortress of Meropide, in its Administrative Area, where convicts gather for daily meals. Each prisoner may claim one free Welfare Meal a day from the clockwork meka Bran.
### STANDARD +
Sigewinne and the chef Cuistot help prepare the meals. The supervisor, Wolsey, sells dishes and recipes for Credit Coupons, the Fortress's currency. Its description stresses that the Welfare Meal is the one thing in the Fortress where equality need not be bought with coupons.

## places
### STANDARD
Located in the Administrative Area of the Fortress of Meropide, under the Court of Fontaine's justice system. Bran stands at the Welfare Meal point and Wolsey keeps the counter.
### LARGE +
Wolsey's menu is small and priced in coupons, covering dishes such as Trout Amandine, Barbeque Ribs, Coffee Bavarois and Haggis, plus their recipes.

## people
### STANDARD
Wolsey is the supervisor and shopkeeper. Bran is a robotic meka employee, built on a harvester meka body with its Arkhe alignment disabled; it was repaired by Wolsey after being left in the abandoned Production Zone and then given a job here.
### LARGE +
Wolsey says Welfare Meals are meant to bring surprise to monotonous lives and admits convicts have asked for better odds of a good meal; he defends giving weaker cooks a chance.

## culture
### STANDARD
A Welfare Meal comes with a fortune slip and a random dish of variable quality, from delicious to suspicious. Bran enforces the rule strictly: only one per person per day under the cafeteria regulations.

### ULTRA +
Possible Welfare Meal dishes include Fish and Chips, Fontainian Onion Soup, Lasagna, Haggis, Steak and Trout Amandine; the dish may be delicious, normal or suspicious in quality.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Institutional but oddly cheerful: a canteen queue, a deadpan robot and a gamble of a lunch.

## status
### LITE
Operating at the cutoff (7.1).
