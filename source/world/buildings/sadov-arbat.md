## summary
### LITE
Sadov-Arbat is a long-established handicrafts and souvenir shop in Snezhnograd. According to its map description, in the Belyi Tsar's time the site was a market where fae bartered with one another before it became a proper shop.
### STANDARD +
Its owner is Blyushko, and the shop also employs staff such as the clerk Yevlena. The wiki records that its goods cannot be bought because newly arrived stock is still being inventoried and organised.
## people
### STANDARD
Named NPCs here are Blyushko (owner), Yevlena (salesclerk), Lopushina and Tsoslina. Blyushko's only recorded idle line is an invitation to step inside and discuss the details.
## history
### LARGE
It is said that during the rule of the Belyi Tsar the original site was a market where fae traded with each other, and that it later developed into an ordinary shop. This is stated as hearsay in the map description, so treat it as local tradition rather than established history.
## culture
### ULTRA
The name abbreviates the Russian Sadovyy Arbat, "Garden Arbat". The real-world Arbat is a historic pedestrian street in Moscow known for souvenir shops; this is an etymological note from the wiki, not in-world lore.
## atmosphere
### STANDARD
A craft shop full of boxed, unsorted new stock, with staff busy counting and sorting it.
