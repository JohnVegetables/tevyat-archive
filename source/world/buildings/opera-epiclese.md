## summary
### LITE
The Opera Epiclese is a grand opera house on the island of Erinnyes in the Court of Fontaine Region, and also Fontaine's main courthouse. Trials are held on its stage as public performances before a ticketed audience, with the Iudex Neuvillette presiding from a raised seat. On other nights the same stage hosts plays, musicals, concerts and magic shows. Behind the opera house a lift descends to River Talionis, the wharf from which convicts are shipped to the Fortress of Meropide.
### STANDARD +
For about five centuries a giant mechanical scale, the Oratrice Mecanique d'Analyse Cardinale, stood on the stage beneath the judge's seat and gave the final verdicts. It has been inert since 4.2, and Neuvillette now passes judgment himself.

## geography
### STANDARD
The building stands on Erinnyes, an island the official description calls consecrated, overlooking the city. The island also holds the Fountain of Lucine, Marcotte Station (the Navia aquabus line stop) and Erinnyes Forest. The courtroom is a multi-level auditorium with raised seating sections, an upper floor of sofas, a lobby and backrooms.
### LARGE +
Under the building runs an underground passage, used in the Archon Quest when the stage tunnel of Lyney's magic box was investigated. The Fontaine Research Institute's ruins lie on the north side of the island, and an entrance to the Fortress of Meropide is also reached from Erinnyes.

## places
### STANDARD
Inside, the judge's seat rises at the back of the stage with the (now inert) Oratrice below it. Until 4.2 Furina attended trials as the opera house's divine icon. A receptionist, Luzerne, greets visitors at the entrance beside a notice board of rules and programmes.
### LARGE +
Posted rules require reserved tickets, assigned seating and proper dress, and ban outside food on pain of being expelled and punished by the Maison Gardiennage. Programmes have included the Steambird's La Verite Prize ceremony, which Furina insisted on presenting herself, Lyney's magic shows (a new "water tank escape"), a Melusine Philharmonic Orchestra concert, and the stage play "The Death of Aurelius".

## people
### STANDARD
Neuvillette presides over trials, and other judges of the Palais Mermonia can be assigned cases. Until 4.2 Furina was the opera house's star and "icon". She had the right to speak at any stage of a trial and starred in revived musical classics, and her performance dates became festivals in their own right. The Maison Gardiennage and the Marechaussee Phantom keep order. Champion Duelists fight accused people who choose a duel before trial.
### LARGE +
Staff named in game include the receptionist Luzerne. Neuvillette's connection to the place began with an anonymous letter from Focalors promising him a seat with an excellent view. He did receive a seat in the middle of the front row and found the view as promised, and later understood that the letter meant more than a chair.

## governance
### STANDARD
Trials follow Fontaine's theatrical procedure: prosecution and defence argue before the audience, witnesses are called, and the judge rules. Until 4.2 the Oratrice delivered the final verdict, which the Iudex had to honour even when he disagreed. The Oratrice also turned people's belief in justice into Indemnitium, a power source used in the Court of Fontaine and on Erinnyes.
### LARGE +
Only a small share of that Indemnitium ever reached the city. Most was stored over 500 years so that the Oratrice could execute a god. Since the Oratrice fell silent, Neuvillette's restored authority over Pneumousia energy has stood in as a temporary substitute, and he has taken over the Oratrice's role in court.
### ULTRA +
Even after Alain Guillotin's Arkhe system came to power Fontaine's clockwork meka, the Court and Erinnyes still ran on the Oratrice's Indemnitium, and replacing it fully had proved difficult. Before 4.2 Lyney theorised that the Oratrice had something to do with the prophesied crisis.

## culture
### STANDARD
Fontainians treat a trial as entertainment as much as justice. Some come for the emotion of real cases, others for the drama of staged productions, and the receptionist says the Hydro Archon recognised both as fit for this stage.
### LARGE +
Furina recalled that every reform of the opera house drew noisy opposition from crowds inside it. On one occasion she silenced them with a decree just as Neuvillette tapped his cane. She held productions to exacting standards. When one troupe pretended to accept her demands and ignored them, she simply rehearsed with a different troupe.

## history
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
When the Traveler arrived (4.0), Lyney and Lynette's first show here ended with a stagehand dead in a magic box. Furina prosecuted Lyney, the Traveler defended him, and with Navia's help the real story emerged. In 4.0 the Oratrice then shocked everyone by finding Childe guilty in the disappearances case although the true culprit had been exposed. In 4.2 Furina herself was trapped onto this stage and tried as a fraud. The Oratrice condemned "the Hydro Archon" to death, and Focalors used that sentence to execute herself and destroy the Hydro Archon's throne, which ended the prophecy's threat.
### LARGE +
Before Furina's trial, a show at the opera turned into a small riot when spectators accused her of ignoring the prophecy, and she fled while the Marechaussee Phantom restored order. During the trial a rift opened in the opera house and the All-Devouring Narwhal emerged. Neuvillette and Childe, in his Foul Legacy form, forced it back. Some time afterwards Furina abdicated, packed up her belongings and left the opera house.
### ULTRA +
When Navia fell into Primordial Seawater in the ruins near Poisson (4.2), she found herself in a dream-trial at the Opera Epiclese. The prosecutor Joinville and an audience of the flood's dissolved victims demanded that she "join" them, until the spirits of Melus and Silver defended her and Neuvillette pulled her back to reality.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Rows of red seats, a hushed full house waiting for a twist, the judge's cane on the floor, gasps and applause at a verdict. Lyney noted that the weather tends to change when trials take place. Outside, newlyweds toss coins into the Fountain of Lucine.

## quests
### LARGE
Chapter IV Act I (Lyney's trial, 4.0), Act II (Childe's verdict, 4.0) and Act V (Furina's trial, 4.2) are set here. Furina's story quest "The Little Oceanid" (4.2) ends with a performance here.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the Opera Epiclese still hosts both trials and performances, with Neuvillette as presiding judge.
### STANDARD +
The Oratrice stands inert on the stage. Furina no longer presides over it as the Hydro Archon: she abdicated after 4.2 but has since performed on this stage again as an actress.

## entry: Lyney's Trial at the Opera Epiclese
@keys: Lyney's trial, Soloist's Prologue, Cowell, Liliane, magic box trick
@keys2: Opera Epiclese, Lyney, Furina, Neuvillette
@version: 4.0
### STANDARD
During Lyney and Lynette's debut, a box-swap trick went wrong: the stagehand Cowell was found dead in a crushed box and the chosen audience member had vanished. Neuvillette locked down the house, and Furina, to please the crowd, made herself prosecutor and the Traveler defence attorney. Furina exposed the twins as members of the Fatui's House of the Hearth. Evidence of Primordial Seawater in Cowell's bag turned the case, and Navia brought the "vanished" woman back on stage. She was Liliane, a pickpocket who had panicked in the box, knocked Cowell out and put him in her place. The Oratrice found the twins not guilty.
### LARGE +
Lyney later tried to investigate the Oratrice for "Father". He retreated after hearing a voice from it that seemed to know him and was contaminated with Indemnitium. The stage tunnel used for the trick became evidence, investigated by the Traveler and Navia.

## entry: Childe's Verdict
@keys: Childe's trial, Childe's verdict
@keys2: Opera Epiclese, Oratrice, Tartaglia, Childe
@version: 4.0
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
In the serial disappearances case, the Oratrice declared Childe guilty although the actual culprit had already been exposed and convicted. It was the first time it had disagreed with the Chief Justice. Furina covered the error with bravado, Neuvillette began investigating the machine, and Childe was sent to the Fortress of Meropide. Neuvillette suspected Focalors was using Childe to hold back the All-Devouring Narwhal.

## entry: The Trial of Furina
@keys: Furina's trial, Apocalypse, The Opera of Noirceur and Blancheur, Giant Magic Box
@keys2: Opera Epiclese, Furina, Focalors, Oratrice, Neuvillette
@version: 4.2
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
To force Furina to reveal what she knew of the prophecy, Neuvillette, the Traveler, Navia, the House of the Hearth siblings and Clorinde set a trap. Furina, hiding in a house in Poisson, saw its walls fall away to reveal the opera stage. The Fatui had built a "Giant Magic Box" and tunnelled to the opera house. Offered a duel with Clorinde or a trial, she chose trial. The Traveler charged her with being a fraud. Diluted Primordial Seawater and Sigewinne's examination proved she was human, and Neuvillette ruled her guilty. The Oratrice then sentenced "the Hydro Archon" to death. Freminet arrived with the missing first prophecy slate, the Narwhal burst through a rift, and as the blade fell, Focalors, hidden in the Oratrice, executed herself.
### LARGE +
Focalors had split herself, leaving Furina to play the god on this stage for 500 years while the Oratrice stored Indemnitium. Her death destroyed the Hydro Archon's throne and returned the power to Neuvillette, who absolved Fontainians of their "original sin". As the Oratrice no longer worked, Neuvillette took the Traveler with him as his executor into the Primordial Sea to subdue the Narwhal.

## entry: Erinnyes Island and the Fountain of Lucine
@keys: Island of Erinnyes, Erinnyes island, Fountain of Lucine
### LARGE
Erinnyes is the island in the Court of Fontaine Region that holds the Opera Epiclese, the Fountain of Lucine, Marcotte Station and Erinnyes Forest. The official description calls it consecrated and says the opera house there symbolises the new era's commitment to law and justice. An old custom survives: newlyweds toss coins into the Fountain of Lucine for a happy life. Lynette believes tears that flow into the fountain carry intense emotion, and the Traveler first heard an Oceanid's voice there. Wines called Sassica and Ornella are said to be produced on Erinnyes, and Neuvillette considers exploring its depths ill-advised.

## entry: Later Performances at the Opera Epiclese
@keys: The Little Oceanid, Animula Choragi
@keys2: Opera Epiclese, Furina
@version: 4.2
### LARGE
After her abdication, Furina helped a struggling amateur troupe, whose director Aurelie had died, stage Aurelie's musical "The Little Oceanid" at the Opera Epiclese. She directed the rehearsals, and when the ailing actress Dulphy was incapacitated before the finale, Furina played the heroine Clio in the final scene. The performance was a success.
### ULTRA +
In the 4.2 world quest series "Questioning Melusine and Answering Machine", the villain Jenck was later found maimed and kneeling on the opera stage with a note of apology to the Traveler, Paimon and Talochard; the Traveler suspected a Fatui Harbinger was behind it.
