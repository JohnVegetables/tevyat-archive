## summary
### LITE
Zakan's Street Bites is a food stall near the Weary Inn's entrance in the Stadium of the Sacred Flame, Natlan. Run by Chanca, it is described as Natlan's main restaurant and sells hearty meals, ingredients and recipes.
### STANDARD +
Chanca's personal specialty is Tatacos, a dish she says almost everyone in Natlan knows how to make.

## people
### STANDARD
Chanca, a former Pilgrimage contestant affiliated with the Scions of the Canopy, runs the stall and the Weary Inn. She is friendly and generous, and would rather teach a recipe than hand over a finished dish.
### LARGE +
She says injuries from her fighting days limit what she can do now, so feeding old comrades is how she helps.

## economy
### LARGE
Gameplay detail: the stall is open at all hours and sells daily-stocked dishes such as Cup O' Grainfruit, Grainfruit Meat Soup, Tatacos and Blazed Meat Stew, weekly cooking ingredients, and recipes including Cup O' Grainfruit, Grainfruit Meat Soup, Xocoatl and Magmic Ode. Reaching top reputation with two of the six Natlan tribes earns a permanent 10% discount.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Busy and cheerful, serving the Stadium's crowd of contestants, spectators and visitors.

## status
### LITE
Open as of 7.1 under Chanca.
### STANDARD +
Zakan's identity is unexplained in the canon read.
### ULTRA +
Chanca also says she left fighting after old injuries, and that Tatacos is easy enough that she teaches it rather than serving it. The stall's Natlan-wide status as the main restaurant comes from the wiki's description, not from in-game text read here.
