## summary
### LITE
Lepaute Horlogerie is a clock shop in Quartier Lyonnais, owned by Caron. Horlogerie is French for clockmaking, and the shop stands near his older brother Livre's toy workshop.
### STANDARD +
Caron designs clocks and makes clockwork toys as a side business. Armand also appears at the shop.

## places
### STANDARD
In Quartier Lyonnais, between Livre's workshop and the Fontaine branch of the Northland Bank on the same street.

## people
### STANDARD
Caron owns it. Armand is also found at the shop and stammers when he greets visitors; his role is not stated.
### LARGE +
Caron's elder brother Livre owns Leschots Clockwork Workshop nearby.

## history
@availability: limited
@version: 4.0
### LARGE
During the Mega Meka Melee event in 4.0, Caron's new toy sparked a rivalry with Livre's workshop. In the final quest, The Toy War: An Armistice, the brothers speak openly; Caron says that although Bennett gave him key inspiration, Livre was the real reason he insisted on the adventurer toy.

### ULTRA +
The name Lepaute may allude to historical French clockmaking brothers, echoing Caron and his brother Livre; this is a wiki etymology guess.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
A clockmaker's shop with a rival workshop a few steps away.
