## summary
### LITE
Xigu Antiques is an antiques shop on Feiyun Slope in Liyue Harbor. It is run by Linlang and opens only at night. It is also the setting of the Rex Incognito storybooks, whose first owner, Min'gui, tries to sell a forgery to the Geo Archon in disguise.
### STANDARD +
During the day the shop stands untended. The shop sells 2-star versions of the five Traveling Doctor artifacts and its stock is refreshed every seven days.

## places
### STANDARD
A single antique shop on Feiyun Slope. Its stock is replenished weekly, and the same sources list another NPC, Qiming, in connection with it.

## people
### STANDARD
Linlang owns it at present. By day she browses the stalls at the northern end of the harbor and returns to tend the shop at night. The Rex Incognito books name its first owner as Min'gui.
### LARGE +
Linlang will buy certain quest items from players for Mora, and each sale can only be done once. These are the three Nameless Treasures, the Dull Ring from the Nine Pillars of Peace quest and the Dragonbone Orb from a Hidden Exploration quest.

## history

### STANDARD
The Rex Incognito series is a fantasy novel, but it sets its scene here: the shop's first owner Min'gui passes off a fake as genuine to a disguised Rex Lapis. The wiki doubts that these events are real.
### ULTRA +
The shop is also named in the description of a furnishing item, Fine Brushwork: Bowl of Blossoms.
