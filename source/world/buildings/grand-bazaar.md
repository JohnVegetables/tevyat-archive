## summary
### LITE
The Grand Bazaar is the market quarter of Sumeru City, a lively, artistic place crowded with merchants selling handmade goods. Its people are mostly ordinary folk who want a steady life rather than scholars or sages. The Zubayr Theater, Nilou's troupe, sits inside it.
### STANDARD +
It has three entrances from the rest of Sumeru City. It is where everyday Sumeru life, craft, music and dance meet, away from the scholarly world of the Akademiya.

## places
### STANDARD
The Zubayr Theater is the Bazaar's best-known landmark, a small theater run by the playwright Sheikh Zubayr. Around it are stalls for woven goods, vases and jars, spices, toys and blankets. Treasures Street, Lambad's Tavern and the Citadel of Regzar belong to other parts of Sumeru City rather than the Bazaar itself.

## people
### STANDARD
Stallholders and passers-by fill the market: Shevirme (stall vendor), Sulaykha (blanket shop owner), Hushang (toy seller), Jut (spice merchant), Afshin (traveling merchant), Farhad (porter) and the wealthy businessman Bhabha by day. Mercenaries of the Corps of Thirty (Azra, Sahar, Taher) keep order.
### LARGE +
The Zubayr Theater's own people also work here: the juggler Kourosh, the props engineer Najia and the manager Sheikh Zubayr, alongside Nilou. A researcher, Zohreh, is found here by day, and the NPCs Kumari, Swoof and Yusuf round out the crowd.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Bustling, colourful and noisy: the Traveler's voice-over line has Paimon marvelling that the residents all seem so talented, selling woven goods, vases and fragrant spices and singing and dancing very well. It feels like a place where anyone might burst into a song or a dance.

## quests
### LARGE
Nilou's Story Quest "To the Wise" (Lotos Somno Chapter Act I) opens at dawn in the Bazaar. The Archon Quest "As by a God's Side" ends with the Traveler waiting for the agreed evening time and then going to the Bazaar, and later Nilou, back at the Bazaar, tells the Traveler she has sent invitations to several friends (Akasha Pulses, the Kalpa Flame Rises). These are Sumeru Archon Quest (3.0) events; avoid them for characters who have not witnessed them.
### ULTRA +
Nilou's story quest and the Archon Quest both revolve around an evening play and banquet at the Bazaar, so a scene set around them should treat the Bazaar as busy with preparations rather than as a plain market.

## status
### STANDARD
As of 7.1 the Bazaar is an ordinary working market, with no later event recorded in the pages consulted that changes it.
