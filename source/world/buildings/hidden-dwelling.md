## summary
### LITE
The Hidden Dwelling is the former home of Alain Guillotin and of his creations Sandrone and Pulonia. Its map description calls it a place where flowers and grasses flourish and time passes gently. It is a quiet house that bears witness to everything that unfolded in the 'home'. It becomes accessible in the 6.7 event story Those We Have Lost and stays open afterwards.
### STANDARD +
Sandrone, known to the Fatui as the Marionette, was brought to life here and later left. Alain Guillotin is the founder of the Fontaine Research Institute of Kinetic Energy Engineering.

## geography
### STANDARD
A house with a garden, a workshop area with a workbench, a fireplace with an old easy chair, a desk and Alain's bedroom, kept almost as it was when Sandrone left. In the event quest the party goes beneath Fontaine's waters, with the Wingalet as their base, and activates a water-insulating mechanism to reach it.
### LARGE +
Flowers and grasses grow around it. The wiki's infobox places it in the Beryl Region of Fontaine, and it appears as a point of interest with its own interior map.

## places
### STANDARD
The workbench is where both Sandrone and Pulonia were brought to life; later Sandrone worked on Pulonia there. A desk holds old maths toys and puzzles, among them the Pagoda Stack, a gear-moving puzzle. Alain's bedroom has a box with a hidden gear-locked compartment. By the fireplace sits an easy chair. The mechanical piano, which Alain once built, stood outside the house. A picture of Alain with Rene hangs in the house.
### LARGE +
The Pagoda Stack puzzle (a variant of the Tower of Hanoi) can be replayed after the quest in easy (3 layers), normal (5 layers) and hard (7 layers) modes. After the event ended the piano that the Traveler had recovered was returned to its old place outside. With Sandrone as the active character, interacting with the piano plays a cutscene of her performing her theme.
### ULTRA +
Items kept here include Alain's drafts from his later years, letters Sandrone says she never bothered to sort, a Multidimensional Algorithmic Core Alain used to store information (only Sandrone and Pulonia can access it), and two pictures. One shows Alain and Rene at the Institute of Natural Philosophy before their falling-out. The other shows Sandrone as a newborn among the plants, with a message wishing her to grow and flourish like them. After Those We Have Kept both pictures return from the Wingalet to the dwelling.

## people
### STANDARD
Alain Guillotin lived here, growing bedridden in his last years. He taught Sandrone mechanics and let her take over his workbench. Sandrone and Pulonia are described as his prime creations. The three are said to have kept a quiet, repetitive but peaceful life here, which Sandrone later called a world where time did not exist.
### LARGE +
Alain looked at the picture of himself and Rene by the fireplace with a mix of guilt and longing, and told Sandrone he sometimes regretted not stopping his friend. At the end of Those We Have Kept Sandrone says the home is as quiet and warm as ever and that she will tend its flowers more often.

## culture
### LARGE
The household turned on puzzles and music. Sandrone beat Alain at the games, so played with Pulonia instead, taught herself the piano because music is, to her, mathematics, composed her own pieces, and eventually got rid of the piano when Alain's illness made music hard to bear.

## history
@spoiler: major
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
During Sunny Summer Fontinalia (6.7), Sandrone brings the Traveler and Paimon to the dwelling in the event quest Those We Have Lost to look for a gift Alain left behind. They find it in a hidden compartment in his bedroom. In the next quest, Those We Have Kept, the home is restored to a quiet state and Pulonia's body is returned to the workstation.
### LARGE +
The quest also has Sandrone and the party face 'anomalies' in the house and sit down to tea together, and speak with Columbina. Pulonia's body stands inert after the second quest.

## dangers
### STANDARD
No hazards are described. Sandrone only warns the Traveler to be careful with the old belongings and the dust.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Still and gentle: flowers, fireplace and a dusty workshop. Sandrone's idle remarks as she wanders the house are brief, such as 'Old notes...', 'Pulonia...' and 'A warm hearth...'.

## travel
### STANDARD
Freely accessible after Those We Have Lost. Teleport to the point of interest named Hidden Dwelling.

## quests
### STANDARD
Those We Have Lost and Those We Have Kept, the second and third parts of Sunny Summer Fontinalia (6.7, a limited-time event).

## status
### LITE
Quiet and open to visit as of 7.1.
### STANDARD +
Alain's piano stands outside again, Pulonia's inactive body is in the workstation, and the two pictures are back in the house.
