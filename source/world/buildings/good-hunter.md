## summary
### LITE
Good Hunter is the most popular restaurant in Mondstadt City. The waitress Sara serves simple food and cooking ingredients and sells recipes. It stands in the market district, across from Mondstadt General Goods.
### STANDARD +
Its map description says the only venue busier is the tavern, and that it is always full of appetising smells. When tables are taken, take-out is the fallback.

## places
### STANDARD
A restaurant with a take-out counter and an outdoor billboard, Good Hunter's Daily Recommendation, beside it. The sign shows Teyvat script reading OPEN and Fine Food.
### LARGE +
The billboard advertises a Cold Cut Platter of Good Hunter sausages, Wolvendom boar bacon and ham dried in the winds of the Archon, served with Mist Flowers to keep the meat cold.

## people
### STANDARD
Sara is the waitress. Before the Stormterror affair was resolved she complained that business had collapsed because no one felt like eating.
### LARGE +
A quest called Food Delivery shows that the take-out service is badly understaffed because demand is so high.

## culture
### STANDARD
Good Hunter prides itself on fresh local ingredients: meat comes from hunters in Springvale, and it uses home-grown Mondstadt apples. Sara says it is known for its venison steak and Satisfying Salad.
### LARGE +
The tavern Angel's Share is the only other venue the map description rates above it for popularity.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
A loud, welcoming eatery full of cooking smells, always short of staff.

## travel
### STANDARD
In the market district of Mondstadt City, across from Mondstadt General Goods. Open all day.

## status
### LITE
Open and busy as of 7.1, as far as sources show.
