## summary
### LITE
The Vision Serpent's Sanctum is a site in Ochkanatlan listed beneath the Cradle of Fleeting Dreams. It has a mural and a note written by the Holy Sovereign.
### STANDARD +
The mural is shown in a gallery before and after being revealed during the world quest Adventure in the Land of Mists.

## history
### STANDARD
Holy Sovereign's Notes (VII) lies to the right of the mural. The ruler writes that a hostile entity (named on the wiki as Ix Uxul Tz'ib Bolon Ch'ule-L) has been imprisoned in a statue but will not obey, and that only draconic blood can operate the ancient machines. He resolves to build a Core of Chu'ulel loyal to humanity alone, by enslaving the draconic part of himself so that it stays imprisoned after his human self dies. The rest is smeared and unreadable.
### LARGE +
He writes that no power greater than humanity can be trusted, save one gentle person the wiki links to Xbalanque. This is the ruler's own view, not an objective account. Notes I to VI are in the Old Core of Chu'ulel.

## status
### LITE
Ruined and open for exploration as of 7.1.
### ULTRA +
The sanctum belongs to the same set of Holy Sovereign's Notes as the earlier ones in the Old Core of Chu'ulel, which together show the ruler's shift from reasoning about waterworks to fearing and confining draconic power; he is the sole named author.
