## summary
### LITE
Mingxing Jewelry is a jewelry shop on Feiyun Slope in Liyue Harbor. The map calls it renowned, and says that in its prime its shelves were filled with gems shining like stars in the night sky. For reasons the game does not give, it now runs a side business swapping rare stones for the Geo Sigils that adventurers carry. The owner is Xingxi.
### STANDARD +
Its Chinese name, Mingxing-zhai, can be read as 'Bright Star Studio'; the wiki notes that 'mingxing' also means 'celebrity'.

## places
### STANDARD
A single shopfront, set across the street from Xinyue Kiosk. Its stock is sold only for Geo Sigils, not Mora: gem slivers, a few ascension materials and weapon billets, and occasionally Mora itself, in exchange for sigils.
### LARGE +
A poster beside the shop advertises seasonal goods in a salesman's tone: a notice that a rare item is in stock and only eight remain, which later reads sold out.

## people
### STANDARD
Xingxi is the owner and the only named person. The wiki describes her as a girl with long straight black hair and brown eyes, in a blue and white dress. No history, family or personality is given.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
A shop that remembers being grander than it is now: a polished storefront on a busy street, doing a quiet trade with adventurers instead of the high-end customers its name suggests.

## travel
### STANDARD
Feiyun Slope, in the upper part of Liyue Harbor. Xinyue Kiosk is the nearest well-known neighbour.
### ULTRA +
Game mechanics, not lore: the sigil shop's stock is the six gem slivers, a few Liyue ascension materials, the four Northlander billets, and Mora, each in limited supply.

## status
### LITE
Open and trading as of 7.1, as far as the sources show.
