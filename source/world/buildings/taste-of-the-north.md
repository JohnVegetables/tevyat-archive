## summary
### LITE
Taste of the North is a restaurant in Dornman Port, owned by Frith, who is said to come from Nod-Krai. It sells simple Mondstadt food and cooking ingredients, and first appeared in Version 6.5. The name is the wiki's unofficial label.
### STANDARD +
Frith says she is just an ordinary restaurant owner. She does not cook the food herself, since the restaurant has its own cook.

## places
### STANDARD
A single restaurant in the port. In gameplay terms the food restocks daily and the ingredients weekly; the menu includes a Harbor Fish Burger.

## people
### STANDARD
Frith is the owner. Her idle line is the invitation 'A taste of the north - who wants to try?'. The wiki also lists Yangzhen among the people there.
### LARGE +
Lohen, a diner there, says the dishes are common Mondstadt staples, that word is she is from Nod-Krai and no ordinary civilian, and that he and others kept an eye on her for a while; it seems she wants to run an honest restaurant in Mondstadt.

## history
@version: 6.5
### STANDARD
Frith asks the Traveler to cook Lohikeitto, a Nod-Krai soup, because a guest wanted to taste the past and she has started to forget what it tasted like. When the Traveler hands one over she delivers it and thanks them.
### LARGE +
Afterwards Lohen says the Lohikeitto he was served was flawless, though later visits never match it. He wonders aloud whether the restaurant hired a new chef.
### ULTRA +
The Traveler's reward includes Mora, Hero's Wit and the book The Song of Ay-Khanoum.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
A harbour-side dining room with a quiet owner whose past seems wider than her menu.

## travel
### STANDARD
In Dornman Port, Windrest Peak. Reaching it requires the northern area of Mondstadt added in Version 6.5.

## status
### LITE
Open as of 7.1, as far as sources show.
