## summary
### LITE
Treasures Street is the liveliest street in Sumeru City, one of the city's named districts alongside the Grand Bazaar and the Akademiya. It is a busy commercial strip with vendors, an Adventurers' Guild branch, Puspa Café and the Citadel of Regzar among its listed places. Its local specialty is padisarah.
### STANDARD +
Mercenaries of the Corps of Thirty, students, researchers and shopkeepers all pass through it.

## places
### STANDARD
Places tied to the street include Puspa Café and the Citadel of Regzar, where Rukh Shah and other Corps of Thirty members are listed. Barmak, Mahamata at the Exchange, is on the roster, as are the Adventurers' Guild branch staff Kamal (branch master) and Katheryne (receptionist).
### LARGE +
Vendors include Ahangar (blacksmith), Hamawi (general goods) and Khalid (jeweler), and Enteka runs the shop at Puspa Café. Also listed are Hassan (hairdresser, daytime), Feresht (billed as a treasure appraiser extraordinaire) and Halva (a tax officer, at night).

## people
### STANDARD
Listed street figures include Barmak, Hatim (a self-proclaimed millionaire), Mawyo (a self-described freelancer), Kimiya (a student), Mansur (a researcher), and the mercenaries Farid and Hakan of the Corps of Thirty.
### LARGE +
At the Citadel of Regzar: Rukh Shah, Parchamdar of the Corps of Thirty; the members Sekkura ('Swallow'), Udad ('Goat') and Yunus ('Bigfish'); Asfand (a consultant) and Effendi (a researcher). At Puspa Café: Gata, Iris, Izem, Maddah, Nayab, Sanad and Zohreh, with Hatim and Mawyo at night.
### ULTRA +
Viktor appears on the street only after the quest Return to Days of Winter has been completed; nothing more is stated.

## governance
### STANDARD
No governing body is named for the street itself. The Exchange's Mahamata, a tax officer and Corps of Thirty mercenaries work in or near it, within the Akademiya's jurisdiction over the city.

## culture
### STANDARD
Trade and talk: sweets and padisarah, jewelry and smithing, haggling, card games and storytelling in the café, and colourful characters boasting of wealth or freelance work.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Lively and crowded, with a different mix of people by day and night: a hairdresser and a freelancer by day, a tax officer and café regulars after dark.

## dangers
### STANDARD
No dangers are described in canon.

## travel
### STANDARD
Treasures Street lies within Sumeru City, listed with the Grand Bazaar and the Akademiya as one of its districts. The wiki lists padisarah as its only local specialty.

## status

Still the city's busiest street as of 7.1, as far as the sources show.
