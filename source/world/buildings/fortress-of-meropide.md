## summary
### LITE
The Fortress of Meropide is a metal-walled stronghold under the sea in Fontaine's Liffey Region. It serves as Fontaine's de facto prison: people convicted at trial are sent there to serve their sentences. It is also the nation's largest maker of clockwork meka. Legally it governs itself. Convicts there count as exiles, and the Court of Fontaine has no jurisdiction beyond lending some Gardes for security. Inside, Credit Coupons replace Mora. The Administrator is Wriothesley, a former inmate who holds the honorary title of "Duke". The Melusine Sigewinne runs the infirmary.
### STANDARD +
To ordinary Fontainians the name is a byword for punishment ("I'll punch you straight into the Fortress of Meropide"). Its real founding purpose was forgotten for centuries: it was built to guard a sealed opening to the Primordial Sea.

## geography
### STANDARD
The Fortress lies on the seabed off the Liffey Region, ringed by searchlights. Convicts reach it from River Talionis, a reception wharf beneath the Opera Epiclese. A lift behind the opera house descends to the wharf, and a boat carries them through an underground tunnel to the Fortress. A second entrance is reached from Erinnyes. Inside, its levels run from the Reception Area and the Dormitory Block down to the Administrative Area, two levels of the Production Zone and the Abandoned Production Zone.
### LARGE +
The Fortress's viewpoint is titled "The Copper Keep". Below the Duke's office, a stair leads to the "forbidden zone", where a great sluice gate holds back Primordial water. In the Abandoned Production Zone a notice says that work in the Geode Mine Shaft has been suspended and its connecting paths sealed.

## places
### STANDARD
The Administrative Area is the hub, built around a central office. The Duke's Office stands at its centre. It has three floors: Wriothesley's office with a desk, sofa and tea table on top, and a basement with a lift to the forbidden zone. The Coupon Cafeteria serves meals. The Pankration Ring, reached by its own lift, is the Fortress's favourite entertainment. The infirmary, run by Sigewinne, is open to everyone at all hours. The Production Zone, two levels below the Administrative Area, is where inmates work.
### LARGE +
Other fixtures are the Rag and Bone Shop (run by Alvard), the registration desk at reception and the dormitory cells. At the cafeteria the clockwork meka Bran hands out one free Welfare Meal per person per day. The meal is a surprise dish with a fortune slip, and the only thing in the Fortress that costs no Coupons. A room off the Duke's basement overlooks the dock where the Wingalet was built in secret.

## people
### STANDARD
Wriothesley, the Duke, rarely leaves the Fortress. He gets the information and resources he needs through his own network and treats inmates, guards and staff alike. Sigewinne, the Head Nurse, is a Melusine who came in as a convict and stayed after her sentence to care for everyone. The population is mostly inmates working off sentences, plus guards (some of them Gardes lent by the Court) and staff. Gardemeks also patrol.
### LARGE +
Named residents include the receptionist Monglane, the guard Deakin (who escorted the Traveler in), the cafeteria supervisor Wolsey, the cook Cuistot, and Edwin Eastinghouse, a former senior researcher of the Fontaine Research Institute. Some inmates choose to stay after their sentences end, partly because his reforms made life bearable and partly for fear of being shunned outside.
### ULTRA +
Inmates sometimes form their own clubs. Some are benign, such as Vautrin's Mutual Aid Network. Others are predatory, such as the Beret Society. The researcher Jurieu, once Edwin Eastinghouse's assistant and now Wriothesley's technical consultant, works here with his assistant Lourvine on the Wingalet. The chef Escoffier once served a short sentence here: she insisted on taking responsibility for a food-safety incident with lumitide, which turned out to have been planted by Monica.

## governance
### STANDARD
The Fortress is formally autonomous and self-governing. Criminals sent there are technically exiles from Fontaine, and the Court cannot even investigate inside without the administrator's consent. In 4.1 Neuvillette had to send the Traveler in undercover. The Administrator holds all authority. Wriothesley runs the place through Mora, manpower and the right people, and comes down hard on anyone who exploits others. The Fortress sells meka to the surface, and the Palais Mermonia is one of its best customers, so he gladly satisfies the Maison Gestion's strict paperwork.
### LARGE +
The Fortress is said to have changed hands chaotically several times over the centuries. Through all of it, residents have kept an unspoken agreement never to harm the medical staff. Wriothesley came to power by challenging the previous, corrupt administrator. The Palais later granted him the title of Duke. The Fortress has a logistics agreement with the Spina di Rosula, which Wriothesley keeps to the terms he agreed with the late Callas.

## culture
### LARGE
Credit Coupons are the only currency inside, earned by work and spent on food, goods and comforts. Pankration bouts in the Ring are the main entertainment. Inmates get a monthly "free day" off, and pipe-cleaning days come round every month.
The Fortress keeps "hidden rules" that work as lessons rather than threats. An inmate who works three days in a row is served "mystery meat" at the next Welfare Meal. It is really a restorative dish Sigewinne cooks, but because Melusines perceive colour differently, it looks inedible to humans. An inmate who bets on both fighters in a Pankration match receives a bottle of blood-red liquid the next day. It is an experimental Fonta recipe Wriothesley judged of low value, sent to teach the worth of Coupons. A third rule, never explained in the Archon Quest, concerns Sigewinne and the people in the infirmary. Anyone rude to Sigewinne is said to be punished extra harshly.
### ULTRA +
In Wriothesley's youth Coupons bought far more: drugs, the winners of fixed fights, people's loyalty, even a killing. The Pankration Ring then had no fixed venue or rules. His reforms ended that. Today pankration is billed as good exercise, and anything from a Sternshield Crab to a clockwork meka may enter the Ring if special conditions are met.

## history
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
In the age of the first Hydro Archon, Egeria, Fontaine exiled its criminals and refused them help. Some repented and begged for mercy. Egeria forgave them and set them to guard her secret beneath the waves, and they built the first fortress. Later generations of exiles kept working on it long after the first had died. The secret was an opening to the Primordial Sea, sealed behind a sluice gate in a "forbidden zone" that only the warden and trusted people may enter. Over the centuries almost everyone forgot why the Fortress existed.
### LARGE +
About 400 years ago Neuvillette sentenced Vautrin, former head of the Special Security and Surveillance Patrol, to the Fortress for killings he committed to avenge the Melusine Carole. There Vautrin founded the Mutual Aid Network. Later the Melusine Sigewinne was sent here for breaking an ancient law against changing a creature into human form. She had altered her own body to look human so she could treat a sick human friend. She served her sentence and stayed as nurse. At some point the Fatui Harbinger Pantalone introduced the Credit Coupons, together with an administrator of the time, as an economic experiment. Wriothesley arrived as a teenage convict and later took over as administrator.
### ULTRA +
Wriothesley built his wealth in the old, rule-less Pankration Ring and by investing, until he held more Coupons than every other inmate combined. The administrator emptied his account in one night. Wriothesley turned that injustice into a rallying cause and publicly challenged the administrator to a duel, and no inmate or guard objected. The administrator fled before the duel, on the very day Wriothesley's sentence ended, so no one was left to sign his release. He walked into the central office and took over the duties.

## dangers
### LARGE
Before the Duke's reforms the Fortress was violent and corrupt, and canon still describes it as a place of conflicting interests that can corrupt people. Searchlights around the exterior catch swimmers who come too close and bring them in. The real danger lies below: Primordial water behind the sluice gate, which dissolved Fontainians on contact until 4.2. In 4.1 its concentration around the Fortress rose sharply, and Freminet collapsed after swimming through it.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Industrial and enclosed: riveted metal walls, hissing pipes, the clang of the production lines, guards and Gardemeks on the walkways, and no sunlight. The infirmary is the warmest place in the Fortress. Wriothesley jokes that a real problem would be feeling water drip on your head.

## quests
### LARGE
Archon Quest Chapter IV Acts III-IV (4.1) take place mostly here: the Traveler serves a 45-day "sentence" under false charges to look for Childe. Wriothesley's story quest, Cerberus Chapter Act I "Reborn in the Land of Grievances" (4.1), and the world quest series "Scenes from Life in Meropide" also take place here.
The Traveler first enters in "A Tea Party Most Thorny" and "Fortress of Meropide" (Act III). "Secret Keepers and Forbidden Zones" and "Calamitous Tread" (Act IV) reveal and breach the sluice gate. Act V opens with the Traveler's release.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the Fortress still operates as prison and factory under Wriothesley, with Sigewinne as Head Nurse.
### STANDARD +
Neuvillette resealed the breached sluice gate in 4.1. Since 4.2 Fontainians no longer dissolve in Primordial water. After the prophecy's flood the Wingalet went back into storage at the Fortress.

## entry: Credit Coupons and the Welfare Meal
@keys: Credit Coupons, Credit Coupon, Welfare Meal, Coupon Cafeteria
@version: 4.1
### STANDARD
Credit Coupons are the Fortress of Meropide's internal currency and replace Mora for every transaction inside. Inmates earn them through work and spend them at the Coupon Cafeteria, the Rag and Bone Shop and elsewhere. With enough Coupons one can order meals to taste. Everyone gets one free Welfare Meal a day from the meka Bran: a surprise dish of unpredictable quality plus a fortune slip. It is the one equality in the Fortress that need not be bought.
### LARGE +
The text of the weapon Cashflow Supervision says the Fatui Harbinger Pantalone invented the Coupons and set them up in the Fortress as an experiment in his project of replacing Mora, a god-controlled currency, with a man-made one. A past administrator was his partner. Before Wriothesley's reforms, Coupons could buy drugs, fixed-fight results, loyalty and even murder.

## entry: The Forbidden Zone and the Sluice Gate
@keys: forbidden zone, sluice gate, Secret Keepers and Forbidden Zones, Calamitous Tread
@keys2: Meropide, Fortress, Wriothesley, Primordial Sea, Egeria
@version: 4.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Beneath the Duke's Office, behind three large isolation doors, is a chamber with a giant sluice gate and gauges. Only the Administrator may enter. Wriothesley had long wondered what lay behind it. Over the year before the Traveler's arrival its gauge crept into the red. After Freminet collapsed from Primordial Seawater in the waters near the Fortress, Wriothesley concluded the gate held back the Primordial Sea. Inmates with historians' backgrounds had also argued to him that the sea which once swallowed Remuria might someday return. Soon afterwards the gate burst. Wriothesley held the water with Cryo ice, Clorinde shot a control panel to drop the security gates, and Neuvillette arrived, pushed the water back and resealed the gate.
### LARGE +
Wriothesley first noticed the gauge rising about a year before 4.1. After dealing with Fatui spies, he swam briefly in the surrounding sea, found his skin tinged red for a while, and told no one. Inmates were evacuated upward during the breach. The eruption beneath the Fortress was later cited at Furina's trial as evidence that the prophecy was coming true.

## entry: The Traveler's Imprisonment
@keys: A Tea Party Most Thorny, Fruity Trio, Lost in Deep Seas, Into the Deepwater Murk
@keys2: Meropide, Fortress, Wriothesley, Childe, Tartaglia
@version: 4.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Childe, wrongly convicted by the Oratrice in the disappearances case, was sent to the Fortress and then vanished. The Knave asked for access, and Neuvillette, who has no jurisdiction inside, proposed a third party instead. He charged the Traveler and Paimon with eating a cake meant for Furina, and they were given 45 days. Wriothesley guided part of their tour himself. They found that a group of inmates who had come to follow Childe had invented a "hidden rule" about cannibals in the pipes to cover his escape: he had left through the pipes after hearing a "voice" again. Lyney, Lynette and Freminet were inside too, having got themselves imprisoned by staging a theft on the Knave's orders.
### LARGE +
Wriothesley had already spotted and removed the Fatui's planted informants, and he watched the House of the Hearth siblings. When Lynette vanished from the infirmary, Lyney stormed his office and charged him, and Sigewinne felled Lyney with a tranquilliser dart. Clorinde, hired by Wriothesley for "side work", then brought in Freminet, who had collapsed from Primordial Seawater while tracing Childe's route. After the sluice gate was resealed, the Traveler served out the sentence, signed release papers with Monglane and felt a tremor from the surface: the Poisson disaster.

## entry: The Mutual Aid Network
@keys: Mutual Aid Network, Mutual Aid Society, Vautrin
@keys2: Meropide, Fortress, inmates
### LARGE
The Mutual Aid Network is an inmates' society founded about 400 years ago by Vautrin, the exiled former captain of the Special Security and Surveillance Patrol. He never resorted to violence or abuse and urged the weaker convicts, mostly people jailed for minor crimes, to band together. Its badge is modelled on the Medal of Peace that Neuvillette gave him. Its three principles are helping others, promoting justice and accepting proper judgment. It encourages cleaning, exercise, tutoring and study, and asks members to face up to their crimes together. It still exists.

## entry: The Beret Society Incident
@keys: Beret Society, Dougier, Reborn in the Land of Grievances, Cerberus Chapter
@keys2: Meropide, Fortress, Wriothesley
@version: 4.1
@spoiler: minor
### LARGE
In Wriothesley's story quest, the Traveler returned to the Fortress and was shown the Beret Society, a club of apparently model prisoners whose berets marked them as reformed. Its founder Dougier secretly controlled the members through "censure": thorn-like clips hidden under the berets and a fear-inducing solution called "Aqua Doloris". He bribed an overseer, hid a real headquarters in the Production Zone, and gathered decommissioned Gardemeks for an all-out move against Wriothesley. With the inmate couple Avice and Faissolle's help, Wriothesley and the Traveler freed the members, destroyed the Gardemeks and subdued Dougier. Wriothesley then personally apologised to each member. The quest also shows contraband arriving in supply shipments, here a gem that stirred up bad memories.

## entry: The Wingalet at the Fortress
@keys: Wingalet, Jurieu, Lourvine
@keys2: Meropide, Fortress, Wriothesley
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
Expecting the gate to fail, Wriothesley secretly had a huge vessel built, inspired by Remurian legend (the golden Fortuna), with guidance from the researchers Jurieu and Lourvine and a factory's worth of labour, materials and technology. He kept it from the other inmates for fear of riots. When the prophecy's flood came, the Wingalet rose to the surface and rescued people. It was then returned to the Fortress, where Charlotte later interviewed Jurieu and Lourvine about it.

## entry: The Wingalet at the Fontinalia Film Festival
@keys: Wingalet
@keys2: Fontinalia, Film Festival, Sandrone, Marionette, aquarium
@version: 6.7
@availability: limited
@spoiler: major
### ULTRA
During the limited-time event Sunny Summer Fontinalia (6.7), the idle Wingalet, expensive to maintain, was brought to the surface and rented out during the Fontinalia Film Festival to earn Mora and lift the morale of the inmates who maintain it. The renter was Sandrone, who paid a high fee without haggling and had a fishing permit and business license on her desk; she set up an aquarium aboard. At the end of the event Wriothesley asked her, as the ship's current captain, to leave some of the aquarium installations in place so the inmates who clean and maintain the Wingalet could enjoy them back at the Fortress.
