## summary
### LITE
Schulz's Blacksmith is the main forge in Mondstadt City, near the front gate. Wagner the blacksmith and his apprentice Schulz run it, and it forges and sells weapons.
### STANDARD +
Its map description calls it the most trusted blacksmith in Mondstadt, always hot from the fires and from the owner's passion. It is named after the apprentice Schulz, because Wagner lost a drinking game to him.

## places
### STANDARD
A forge with a shopfront. Wagner forges weapons and sells self-defence weapons; Schulz sells the cheapest ones and wanders about the forge all day. The sign over the door reads Schulz Smithshop in Teyvat script.
### LARGE +
Mona's Laboratory lies a little further south from here.

## people
### STANDARD
Wagner is a burly man with dark hair, dark eyes and a white shirt who is proud of his forging. Schulz is his apprentice. Their idle lines are a master's boast that nothing at his shop is second-rate, and an apprentice's awe at the master.
### LARGE +
According to the character stories, Wagner nearly sank into despair trying to forge a sword that would hold up for Noelle: over ten blends of alloy dulled quickly because her strength outstripped them. He finally made her a sturdy, heavy claymore reinforced to suit her Geo affinity.
### ULTRA +
Lawrence says that his father, a merchant from Fontaine, later had a drinking contest with Wagner and that Wagner won. Had Wagner lost, he would have had to name his prized weapon Pinky the Rabbit. This too is only Lawrence's account.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Heat, hammer noise and warm pride: a working forge in a market district, with a master and a modest apprentice.

## travel
### STANDARD
Near the front gate of Mondstadt City, in the market district.
