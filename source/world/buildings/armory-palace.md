## summary
### LITE
The Armory Palace, formally the Royal Armory Palace, is the Snezhnayan institution that develops weapons. Sandrone manages it, and it lies deep underground within Glupov, the Fatui's seat. It is closed to outsiders, guarded by Fatui troops and the Special Assignments Battalion, and is where the Landcruisers were engineered and where Project Mistilteinn's experiments take place.
### STANDARD +
Its secrecy is practical as well as political: the research there feeds the Tsaritsa's plan to be able to resist the Heavenly Principles. In the Version 7.0 Archon Quest Mistilteinn the Traveler and Odette break in to rescue Paimon, and the facility turns out to be the epicentre of the Ruler of Death's Curse of Death.
### LARGE +
It is also where Mitya works when he is not slipping away to the theater, and where, in 7.1, the Armory Palace's newest invention, the Mirror of Remembrance, is used to examine the Curse of Death.

## geography
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
The Armory Palace is hidden deep inside Glupov, reached by taking an elevator down from the surface. The complex is large and spreads over many floors and chambers, including a restricted outer area with guard posts, corridors lined with automatic turret mechanisms, laboratories and an enormous deep chamber with heavy machinery. Odette remarks that the style does not look like Fatui construction and guesses that structures from an earlier civilisation were requisitioned and also serve as a disguise.
### LARGE +
In the deepest parts a large circular elevator takes visitors down to Mitya's laboratory, and a cell-like room off it was where Paimon was kept during the incident. A broad gap on the route is crossed by activating a Starlight Prism-like object that replays images of the past.

## places
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
Known areas: the guarded gates near Glupov; the research halls; a turret chamber; a prism room; the heavy machinery hall where the large-scale experiment runs; and Mitya's laboratory below it.
### LARGE +
In 7.1 a room houses the large Mirror of Remembrance prism. The 7.1 prism is presented by Pierro as a recent Armory Palace innovation made by enhancing and solidifying an ordinary prism.

## people
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
Sandrone manages the institution. Mitya is the researcher most closely tied to it, and Valeriy, a member of the Special Assignments Battalion, is tasked with guarding and keeping tabs on him. Pierro, the Jester, oversees the experiments of Project Mistilteinn and visits in person. Odette, who knows the Fatui's inner workings, guides the Traveler through it.
### LARGE +
Other named staff: Zinenko, another Special Assignments Battalion guard. Historically the wiki also lists the deceased researchers Preobrazhensky and Anton Zarkovich Karnatsky, whose rivalry led to a disaster in the Nothing Passage in Nod-Krai.
### ULTRA +
Odette says everyone in the Fatui has heard that Pierro has been overseeing the experiments here lately, and the guard captain inside invokes his name as an order ("No one interferes with The Jester's work!").

## governance
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
Sandrone manages the Armory Palace, though Pierro, the Fatui's Director, takes personal oversight of Project Mistilteinn there and Mitya reports to him. The wiki says its administrative system is allegedly rather complex (item text).
### LARGE +
The Special Assignments Battalion belongs to the Armory Palace Royal Grenadier Guards and protects important figures such as Mitya while also keeping watch on them.

## culture
### LARGE
Staff are researchers and soldiers under strict secrecy. Outsiders are forbidden, so the effects of the Curse of Death went unnoticed outside for a long time. The name echoes the Kremlin Armoury in Moscow, which began as a royal arsenal. Armory Palace staff speak of 'breakthroughs' and 'verified hypotheses' in a scientific register; Mitya answers to evidence rather than rank, as when he recommends suspending the project 'purely as a scientist, guided by reason alone'.

## history
### LITE
The Armory Palace first appears in Version 6.0 (Luna I) content and is best known for developing the Landcruisers, automated war machines used by the Fatui across Snezhnaya and Nod-Krai.
### STANDARD +
The Adventurer Handbook says Landcruisers were first engineered by its finest engineers as armed ships that cruise on land. Cyclic Military Kuuvahki Core item text ties the Landcruisers to the Kuuvahki Experimental Design Bureau, a joint effort with the Fatui.
### LARGE +
According to item text cited by the wiki, two Armory Palace researchers, Preobrazhensky and Anton Zarkovich Karnatsky, were sent to research kuuvahki in the Nothing Passage of Nod-Krai; their rivalry ended in a disaster that killed them and the entire Fatui Sixth Company except Smirnova. In Version 7.0 the Armory Palace became the setting of Project Mistilteinn's experiments, which required enormous energy that Project Stuzha was gathering. When Pierro took Paimon here, Mitya used the same energy to cancel the experimental effects on her. The Ruler of Death's Curse of Death had been killing researchers whenever a technological breakthrough threatened the Heavenly Principles, which explained earlier reports of memory loss. In 7.1 the Tsaritsa endorsed halting the project until the Ruler of Death was dealt with; after Ronova's fall the restrictions were lifted.

## atmosphere
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
A vast, cold, humming facility of spotless corridors, guard calls and turret mechanisms that track movement, with the sense that something large is being built just out of sight. Researchers are polite and curious even towards intruders.
### LARGE +
Guards shout warnings, but at least one researcher recognises Odette as a star of the theater and does not realise the visitors are intruders. When the large-scale experiment starts the whole complex shakes, and moments later the Curse of Death strikes.

## dangers
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
Fatui guards, Oprichniki troops, turret mechanisms and Landcruisers defend it. The greatest hazard is the Curse of Death, which kills everyone in the vicinity whenever Snezhnaya's technology approaches a breakthrough; its victims wake in an afterlife and later return to life with their memories of death gone.
### LARGE +
Even a stray experiment can kill and revive the whole staff, as Mitya found when a final-phase energy experiment brought the curse down on everyone present. In 7.0 he judged that no breakthrough was possible without eliminating the Ruler of Death.

## travel
### STANDARD
There is no public access. Entry is through Glupov's guarded gates and a long elevator ride down. The Traveler first goes there as an intruder and later as a guest of Pierro and the Tsaritsa.
### LARGE +
Mitya says outsiders are forbidden, which is why the Curse of Death's effects stayed within the Armory Palace for so long.

## quests
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
Archon Quests: Mistilteinn (Chapter VII Act II, 7.0), in which the Traveler and Odette storm the Armory Palace; Light and Shadow, Intertwined (Act III, 7.1), in which Pierro presents the Mirror of Remembrance. It also appears in the background of Wraith's Nocturne, where Odette finds Mitya dead there before he returns to life.
### LARGE +
Mistilteinn is also the quest where Project Mistilteinn is first named.

## status
@version: 7.1
### LITE
Active. As of 7.1 Project Mistilteinn is free to move forward after Ronova's fall, and the Armory Palace is investigating the application of life force.
### STANDARD +
In 7.1 the Tsaritsa tasked Mitya with researching life-force applications, which are important to restoring Project Mistilteinn, and Vesna extracted 'Seals of Life' at the Armory Palace using her small authority.
### LARGE +
Canon still does not state what Project Mistilteinn finally builds.

## entry: Special Assignments Battalion
@keys: Special Assignments Battalion, Royal Grenadier Guards
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
The Special Assignments Battalion is a division of the Armory Palace Royal Grenadier Guards. It protects important figures such as Mitya and keeps tabs on them. Valeriy and Zinenko are among its members.
### LARGE +
Alyosha says Valeriy visits the theater only to look for Mitya, who sneaks off there when he should be at work. Valeriy later serves as the Traveler's assist character in Wraith's Nocturne.

## entry: Landcruisers
@keys: Landcruiser, Landcruisers
@version: 6.0
### STANDARD
Landcruisers are automated war machines developed by the Armory Palace, described as armed ships that cruise on land. They appear in Nod-Krai and Snezhnaya as common enemies, from scouts and storm models to the Super-Heavy Landrover: Mechanized Fortress, and are tied to the Kuuvahki Experimental Design Bureau.
### LARGE +
Landcruisers guard the Armory Palace itself in the Mistilteinn quest.

## entry: The Mistilteinn Break-In
@keys: Mistilteinn quest, Armory Palace break-in, Curse of Death
@version: 7.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
After Pierro takes the sleeping Paimon, the Traveler and Odette force their way into the Armory Palace through its guards and turret chambers. Deep inside the heavy machinery starts a large-scale experiment and the Curse of Death kills the Traveler, Odette and the staff. The Traveler wakes first, finds Mitya, then Ronova arrives and tries to claim Paimon until Pierro blocks her. Mitya concludes that Ronova's curse triggers on technological progress and recommends suspending Project Mistilteinn; Pierro agrees.
### LARGE +
Pierro and Mitya also used their energy to cancel what made Paimon unwell. The Traveler suspects Odette's Cor Ignis helped guide their soul back from the afterlife.

## entry: Mirror of Remembrance
@keys: Mirror of Remembrance, Svetovid Stone
@version: 7.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Pierro calls the Mirror of Remembrance a recent Armory Palace innovation made by enhancing and solidifying an ordinary prism so that it can store memories and, to a limited degree, collect memories from its surroundings. It is used in the Armory Palace to review snippets that show Ronova collaborating with Rhinedottir.
### LARGE +
With Vesna's small authority of life, the Armory Palace also extracts Seals of Life as a possible way to counter death.
