## summary
### LITE
The Tenryou Commission Headquarters is the seat in Inazuma City of the Tenryou Commission, the executive arm of the Shogunate. The Commission is led by the Kujou Clan and handles security and military affairs; in the Archon Quests it is the organisation that carries out the Vision Hunt Decree and the Sakoku Decree. The building has a courtyard exterior and an interior that the story opens in Blind Loyalty, Reckless Courage.
### STANDARD +
The Commission is also the one the story exposes: its head, Kujou Takayuki, concealed the cost of the war from the Shogun and dealt with the Fatui. It remains in disarray, with Kujou Kamaji standing in as acting head.

## geography
### STANDARD
The headquarters is a point of interest in Inazuma City on Narukami Island, near Tenshukaku. It has an outdoor courtyard, where Shogunate samurai, yoriki and doushin keep watch, and an interior instance, entered as a quest domain during the Archon Quest. The courtyard NPCs listed on the wiki are Ikeda Soushirou, the yoriki Mikoshi Genjirou and Yata Kouki, and the doushin Watanabe; Okawara Goemon, also a yoriki, is in the interior.

## people
### STANDARD
The Commission's personnel include Kujou Kamaji (acting clan head), Kujou Sara (General), Kujou Masahito (General), Okawara Goemon (Chief Samurai and Kujou Clan security head), and rank-and-file Shogunate Samurai, yoriki and doushin. Shikanoin Heizou is a Tenryou "Detective". Kujou Takayuki is a past commissioner who is under house arrest.
### LARGE +
The Commission suffers a higher desertion rate than the other two, since the Vision Hunt Decree put its soldiers in fights against people rather than ancient monsters and Abyss denizens.

## governance
### STANDARD
Of the three Commissions (Tenryou, Kanjou, Yashiro) in the Tri-Commission, the Tenryou Commission is the Raiden Shogun's executive arm, responsible for security and military matters including enforcement of the Vision Hunt Decree and the Sakoku Decree. The three Commissions share equal authority and can check each other.
### LARGE +
After the events of Omnipresence Over Mortals the Commission is in disarray over Takayuki's collusion with the Fatui, and the Shogun planned to punish him in due course. In the Imperatrix Umbrosa Chapter (Act I), Ei offers the post of Tenryou Commissioner as the prize of a duel; Takatsukasa Susumu refuses, Kujou Kamaji accepts knowing he will lose, and Ei afterwards says she will give the Kujou Clan more time.

## history
### STANDARD
The Traveler is placed on a wanted list by the Commission after escaping the Vision Hunt Ceremony with Thoma. Later, in Omnipresence Over Mortals, the Traveler and Yae Miko show Kujou Sara a Commission document that omits the cost of the war and a letter to the Fatui. Sara goes to the headquarters to demand an explanation.
### LARGE +
In Blind Loyalty, Reckless Courage Sara leads the Traveler into the headquarters as an instance: they defeat Commission guards, then reinforcements, then the Kujou Clan Internal Affairs Head, Takatsukasa Tokibeitetsu, who summons bodyguards, before the Traveler speaks with Takayuki.

### ULTRA +
Other named officers on the wiki's Commission roster include the Shogunate officer Toranosuke, the quartermaster Yasutake, the yoriki Takasaka Izumi and Owada, and doushin such as Andou, Amano and Sanada. An item text by the samurai Inaba Kyuuzou, found in the world, records the disillusion of soldiers who expected to fight monsters and ended up fighting people.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
A formal military-bureaucratic compound: guards at the gate, officers moving papers, the strain of an institution that has turned on its own conscience. It is quieter in the courtyard than inside.

## quests
### STANDARD
Blind Loyalty, Reckless Courage (Chapter II, Act III: Omnipresence Over Mortals). The courtyard is also an ordinary map location with Tenryou NPCs.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the headquarters is still the Tenryou Commission's seat. The commissioner's post stays unsettled: Kujou Kamaji acts as head and the Kujou Clan was given more time. Many Tenryou samurai relax at the Aisa Bathhouse.

### LARGE
Kujou Takayuki, the commissioner and Kujou clan head, was shown by Yae Miko and the Traveler to have hidden the war's cost from the Raiden Shogun and to have exchanged a secret letter with the Fatui. Sara, who had served the Kujou Clan loyally, was shaken and went to confront him in the headquarters. Later accounts place him under house arrest.
