## summary
### LITE
The Vishap Research Lab is a hidden laboratory of ancient Byakuyakoku in Enkanomiya, where researchers studied and bred Bathysmal Vishaps (the "Dragonheirs of the Depths") under the personal direction of Watatsumi Omikami. It does not appear on the map. The Traveler can reach it through a phase gate on the small island north of the Dainichi Mikoshi and finds there the afterimage of a researcher called Michiya, who guards the experiment records.
### STANDARD +
The lab is the starting point of the world quest "Tricolor File", which yields the Bathysmal Vishap Experimental Records, one of the five lost books in "Collection of Dragons and Snakes". Its text shows a programme of controlled breeding, intelligence tests and blood-grafting that was meant to protect Enkanomiya from a prophecy, and whose staff disagreed about whether to keep or burn the results.

## geography
### STANDARD
The lab lies outside the ordinary Enkanomiya map. To reach it, go to the small island north of the Dainichi Mikoshi, either by gliding down from the northmost waypoint of the Dainichi Mikoshi or by gliding from the ghostfish cluster at the far east of The Narrows, and then use the Phase Gate at the island's northmost point.
### LARGE +
Inside are a restricted area, an experimental site and a locked cell. An elemental monument must be activated (it uses Cryo energy) to open the cell that holds the experiment records and a captive Rimebiter Bathysmal Vishap Hatchling. The wiki lists the hatchling as the area's elite enemy.

## people
### STANDARD
The only resident is Michiya, the afterimage of a researcher of the lab, one of the Shades of Tokoyo. The quest that begins with him can only be started during Evernight. Like most shades he is a translucent figure without distinguishing features and does not seem to realise he is only an afterimage; Paimon notes it in an aside.
### LARGE +
Michiya speaks as if it were still the eve of the exodus: he says this is the third group the others have sent to him on Enkanomiya's last day, assumes visitors are either Jibashiri or retainers sent by Sangonomiya, and says he will stay in the restricted zone until it is time to leave to keep anyone from approaching. He says he will probably have to change his name once he reaches the surface and begins a new life, because "our wrongdoings are not so easily wiped away".

## governance
### STANDARD
The programme was directed by Watatsumi Omikami in person. The records say the research team was split between those who wanted to burn the records and those who wanted to save them for a later generation that might forgive them. Michiya favoured burning but accepted the hidden-records compromise.
### LARGE +
The records set strict house rules: from a certain day, experiments were to be directed by Omikami; files from before the founding year of the Watatsumi calendar were destroyed; serial numbers were to use Earth, Water, Fire, Wind, Ether and Void instead of the ancient Alpha-to-Epsilon categories; authors could be left unnamed or given by internal lab title, with ancient Byakuyakoku names and modern Watatsumi, Narukami or Inazuman names forbidden; and the records were not to be used for diaries, love letters or fantasy novels.

## history
### STANDARD
The records describe a programme on the Dragonheirs of the Depths. Researchers raised vishaps in altered environments and found they could awaken or generate traits for their descendants: a heat-resistant vishap raised in cold gave offspring with more body fat, more sleep and the Cryo element, while descendants returned to heat chose heat resistance again without gaining Pyro. After four generations the vishaps' language ability approached that of a twelve-year-old student, and the writer argued the experiments should stop, citing the prophecy that the Dragon Sovereign of Water would be born in human form, which they did not want to happen in Enkanomiya.
### LARGE +
Under Omikami's special orders, researchers tried grafting its blood onto vishaps. Early attempts failed because the vishaps could not accept the blood. A later round counted as a success, with the rejection explained by the vishaps being creatures of the Light Realm (elemental beings), at odds with the Human Realm of Omikami and its coral vassals. The log closes with the claim that no vishap died in the experiments and that all lived out their days.
### ULTRA +
In Michiya's plan, once everyone had left Byakuyakoku, the enhanced Bathysmal Vishaps would be released. They were bred to react to a special kind of elemental energy and to pass their traits on strongly, so that in a few years the place would swarm with powerful vishaps, and in the meantime the records would be safe. Michiya says he sides with Sangonomiya and that it is better for the records to stay buried in the dark.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
A cold, enclosed research site of cells, an experiment area and an elemental monument, visited in the blue dark of Evernight, when the quest can be started. The sense is of scientists who ended their work with guilt and left a time capsule they could neither burn nor publish.

## dangers
### STANDARD
The locked cell holds a Rimebiter Bathysmal Vishap Hatchling that is released when the elemental monument is lit. Bathysmal Vishaps hate man-made light and can drain a fighter's energy or, if energy runs out, health. Paimon warns that lighting the monument will summon dangerous vishaps, so prepare first.

## quests
### STANDARD
"Tricolor File" (World Quest, 2.4; requires "The Subterranean Trials of Drake and Serpent"): speak to Michiya, then light the Cryo monument to open the cell and obtain the Bathysmal Vishap Experimental Records. The records can be obtained without the quest. "Collection of Dragons and Snakes" requires them: Ema, an afterimage in The Narrows library, wants five lost books returned, and handing in the records contributes the fourth volume of The Byakuyakoku Collection.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the lab is unchanged: a hidden, abandoned Enkanomiya site whose records are in the Traveler's archive. No content from 5.x to 7.1 mentions it. Enkanomiya itself is little known outside Inazuma.

## entry: The Bathysmal Vishap Experimental Records
@keys: Bathysmal Vishap Experimental Records, Dragonheir Evolution, Vishap Intelligence Research
@keys2: Enkanomiya, Vishap Research Lab
@version: 2.4
### STANDARD
Volume 4 of The Byakuyakoku Collection. It is signed with no individual name and kept in the file numbering that mixes Earth, Water, Fire, Wind, Ether and Void categories. It contains the evolution experiments, the intelligence study, and the grafting trials directed by Watatsumi Omikami. The final entry praises Omikami as "gracious and beneficent" and notes that none of the Dragonheirs died in the experiments. Volume 5 in the same collection, a puzzle book for children, echoes the same prophecy and warns against making vishaps sound adorable lest children lower their guard.

## entry: Michiya
@keys: Michiya
@keys2: Vishap Research Lab, Tricolor File, Enkanomiya
@version: 2.4
### STANDARD
Michiya is a Shade of Tokoyo, the afterimage of a researcher of the Vishap Research Lab. He speaks as one who feels guilt and duty: he will keep watch until departure, believes the research team did wrong, and will not give the records away easily. After "Tricolor File" he refuses to say more. He expects that he and his colleagues are about to start a new life above the surface.
