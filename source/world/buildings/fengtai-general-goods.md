## summary
### LITE
Fengtai General Goods is the general store of Yilong Wharf in Chenyu Vale, run by Fengtai. It stocks everyday items and local specialties, and sells commemorative jadeware.
### STANDARD +
It is open at all times of the day and its stock restocks at different intervals.

## people
### STANDARD
Fengtai welcomes customers with a cheerful pitch about everyday items and commemorative jade jewelry. He says there is a build-to-order jadeware service, but it requires a reservation and the time taken depends on the design's complexity. He believes that "you don't choose a piece of jade, it chooses you".
### LARGE +
Fengtai trained under Deyin, a jade artisan of Qiaoying Village. Deyin told him that a carver must suffer no distraction, so in Chenyu Vale the selling and the making of jade are strictly separate. Deyin gives some of his jade works to Fengtai to sell on his behalf, which is why the shop stocks them.
### ULTRA +
Fengtai adds that there might be a hidden artisan in any house you pass, quietly shaping jade. He is the disciple who sells, not the carver.
