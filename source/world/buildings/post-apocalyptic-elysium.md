## summary
### LITE
"Post-Apocalyptic Elysium" is a bunker in Wavechaser Plain, Nod-Krai, built in the reign of the Belyi Tsar to survive the supposed destruction of the world. Its researchers lived there believing the Abyss had devastated everything outside; their supervisor, Morozov, secretly served the plotter Rotwang.
### STANDARD +
The Traveler finds it in the world quest Return to Sender (6.3), restores its kuuvahki purification system and defeats the Abyssal monster that Morozov became. The bunker has a maintenance zone, a second-floor research zone and a third-floor supervisor's office.

## history
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The bunker held a team of researchers and their families under Supervisor R. M. Morozov. Senior Researcher Grazina Myklukho bred plants with an affinity for kuuvahki that absorbed it and purified Abyssal corruption, and Morozov approved pipes to circulate it through the ventilation as the "Apocalypse Purification System". But Morozov used the experiments to let Abyssal monsters loose in the experiment zones. Repeated breaches killed people, including a child, Payva, and Grazina's written protests were ignored. Maintenance Worker Kotelnikov learned that the outside world was intact and was silenced.
### LARGE +
The master thief Brahe, the third Reed Miller, entered hunting Rotwang's followers, and with Grazina secretly led the surviving children out to protectors outside. Morozov died, but the bunker's corruption meant he would rise again as a mindless monster. Grazina declined Brahe's offer to leave and stayed behind to prevent that and to keep outsiders away; she could not switch off the bunker's broadcast signal or close its gates. Brahe took the purified plants and named them Mandragoras. A diary by a member of the Seahook Gang, lured in by the signal, tells of finding no way out.

## places
### STANDARD
A maintenance zone at the entrance; a second-floor research zone with two experiment sites, valves and a control terminal; a third-floor supervisory office. Readable logs, recordings and a private journal tell the story.
### LARGE +
The research zone holds Grazina's reports, communications logs addressed to Morozov, a child's note about watering flowers for a friend, and recordings of Grazina and Brahe. The supervisor's office holds her private journal.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Rifthounds and, on the third floor, the Ultimate Calamity that Morozov became; Abyssal corruption.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the bunker is open and its monster destroyed. Grazina's fate is recorded as sealing herself inside about 500 years ago.
### ULTRA +
Completing Return to Sender unlocks the Bunker No. 0 viewpoint and the achievement Dead Man's Hand; the next quest in the series is The Raven's Legacy. Grazina's journal says Brahe took the name Mandragora from a legend of the Far North told to him by a woman named Alia, and that in the language of flowers the plant stands for happiness and hope. She also writes that destroying the bunker would spread its Abyssal corruption.
