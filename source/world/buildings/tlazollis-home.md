## summary
### LITE
Tlazolli's Home is the dwelling and workshop of Tlazolli, an Ancient Name forger of the Children of Echoes, on the outskirts of the tribe's settlement in Tequemecan Valley, Natlan. Its interior can be entered only during the quest Melodious Chant. It is where Nechca's birthday party is held and where a mother's grief turned into a harmful deception.
### STANDARD +
In canon the house is tied to one story: Tlazolli, Xilonen's former mentor, hid a plan here to overwrite the memories of an orphaned girl, Nepecha, so that she would become her dead daughter Nechca. After the plan failed the workshop was officially closed off by the tribe.

## places
### STANDARD
The house is a private dwelling at the edge of the Children of Echoes' settlement. The wiki lists an interior used only in the Melodious Chant quest and a separate workshop where Tlazolli worked on her Ancient Name forging; the nearby domain Tlazolli's Forge is a related site.
### LARGE +
The quest-only interior is where Xilonen, the Traveler and Paimon bring a gift to Nechca's birthday, as set up in the act's first quest, Threadlike Whispers. The workshop later becomes the scene of the confrontation in which Tlazolli tries to finish a "Central Inscription" meant to imprint synthetic memories onto Nepecha.

## people
### STANDARD
Tlazolli, the owner, is an adult Ancient Name forger whose Ancient Name is Undugu. Nepecha lives with her as an adopted daughter; the tribe believes the girl is Nechca, Tlazolli's real daughter who died in an abyssal attack some years earlier. Xilonen, her former pupil, is a frequent visitor.
### LARGE +
Tribe members who owned Blazegem Inscriptions unknowingly lost memories of Nechca to the scheme; the effect was reversed once the inscriptions were deactivated.

## history
@version: 5.1
### LARGE
Several years before the Traveler's arrival Tlazolli's daughter Nechca died in an abyssal attack. Nepecha, a girl of about the same age with the same birthday, was orphaned in the same attack and was taken in. Tlazolli told the tribe that Nechca had merely been wounded and that abyssal energy had erased her memories, and she kept Nepecha isolated and sedated with a "medicine" so the truth would not surface. She also made and sold Blazegem Inscriptions that quietly gathered memories of Nechca from their wearers.
### ULTRA +
In Wailing Cacophony Xilonen noticed the missing memories and the knowledge "Nechca" should not have had. Tlazolli fled with Nepecha to her workshop; Xilonen and the Traveler burned away the stolen memories before the transfer could finish, which also let Nechca's soul, held within Tlazolli's Ancient Name, speak to her mother a last time. Tlazolli was taken into tribal custody and the workshop was closed. In Serenity's Reprise the inscriptions were gathered and destroyed. Later, in a dream, Tlazolli agreed to lend her power to the Traveler's fight against the Abyss.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
A modest craftsperson's home that looks warm and ordinary from outside, which is what makes the hidden strain inside it feel unsettling. Before the truth is exposed, the household is polite, watchful and a little too attentive to what Nepecha says.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the home is closed off by the tribe after Tlazolli's crimes; she is in custody and Nepecha stays with Xilonen.
### STANDARD +
Nobody lives here now. Do not stage new scenes inside as an open, welcoming house unless setting them before the events of Melodious Chant.
