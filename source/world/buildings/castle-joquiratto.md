## summary
### LITE
Castle Joquiratto is the stronghold of the villain "El Capoctus Joquiratto", the main antagonist of the immersive adventure experience at Villa Guiztli. It sits in the western zone of the Guiztli Ridge caverns in the Easybreeze Holiday Resort. It is a comic, theatrical place full of scheming Nochtli lackeys, capybara and alpaca townsfolk, and booby-trapped chests.
### STANDARD +
Visitors play heroes defending the underground town from Joquiratto. The tone is pantomime: villains boast, barrels explode in the wrong direction, and treasure chests are rarely what they seem.

## geography
### STANDARD
The castle lies in the Guiztli Ridge Inner Cavern, whose viewpoint is titled "Cavern of Stone and Prickly Pears". The resort island's older name, Tenochtzitoc, translates to "cavern of stone and prickly pears", though prickly pears no longer grow there in the modern day.
### LARGE +
A teleport waypoint stands in the castle area. In the northeast a lackey guards a chest and insists there is no treasure inside; to the south of the waypoint, lackeys guard a sealed chest, threaten to send intruders to "the Night Kingdom", and detonate nearby explosive barrels, which in turn releases a lava avatar.

## people
### STANDARD
The area is occupied by Nochtli lackeys (the villain's henchmen) and by capybara and alpaca townsfolk, including an Alpaca Sheriff who speaks with a "meh" and leads the hunt for "the evil capybara". The lackeys shout "For El Capoctus Joquiratto!".
### LARGE +
Examples of staged mischief: a lackey scowls at a capybara that has been carried to the top of a mountain on a Chroma Kite and now declares that it holds the high ground; two lackeys unearth a treasure chest, cannot split it evenly, and settle it by duel (the "Three Musketeers" challenge).

## quests
@version: 5.8
### STANDARD
The Story Quest To Guiztli Ridge! (the Sunspray Summer Resort, part 2.2) has the party ride a cruise ship into the cavern, defeat bandits with the Sheriff, help activate a mechanism, and chase Joquiratto until his hot-air balloon is brought down and crashes on a mountaintop. The castle area is accessed partway through that quest.
### LARGE +
A Hidden Exploration Objective that the wiki names unofficially "Joquiratto's Not-So-Great Plan" spans Guiztli Ridge and the castle: Nochtli bandits propose a fifty-fifty plan to a passer-by, then trick them with explosive barrels, and defeating them awards the achievement "A Grand Plan".
### ULTRA +
A puzzle near the castle is a liar-and-truth-teller riddle about Red-Top and Blue-Top capybaras. Picking the Blue-Top who accuses Red-Top reveals a Nochtli lackey, who flees by boat and must be shot down using a cannon.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Bright, noisy and cartoonish: flat-cut villains, shouting capybaras and cheerfully sabotaged heists. Even the explosions are staged comedy.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the castle remains part of the resort and has no recorded role in later story content.

## entry: El Capoctus Joquiratto

### STANDARD
"El Capoctus Joquiratto" is the villain of Villa Guiztli's adventure show. His lackeys rob the town, the Sheriff asks the heroes for help, and in the Guiztli Ridge quest the party pursues him until his hot-air balloon is brought down. Nothing is recorded of any real person behind the title.
