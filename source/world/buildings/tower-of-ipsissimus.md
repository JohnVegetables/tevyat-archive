## summary
### LITE
The Tower of Ipsissimus, also called the Tower of Gestalt or the Sealed Ruined Tower, stands in Fontaine's Morte Region. The secret society known as the Narzissenkreuz Ordo built it as its base. The Court later declared the Ordo an illegal extremist group and sealed the site. The tower is the way down to a seal over the Primordial Sea, and it sinks beneath the water for good after the 4.2 world quest Savior's Wake.
### STANDARD +
Its name comes from an old Remurian idea of a will powerful enough to rule, sustain and destroy the world. Its other names, such as Gestalt, point to the Ordo's goal of merging many wills into one.

## geography
### STANDARD
A tall tower with a clock face high on it, rising from the Morte Region. Inside it has a top, a middle and a bottom level, each with its own puzzle, and below the bottom level lies the sealed way to the Primordial Sea. A hidden meeting chamber, also called the Narzissenkreuz Ordo, sits at the top and has a Doomsday Clock set into its ceiling.
### LARGE +
The chamber can also be entered through a book at the end of the Central Quartier in the Institute of Natural Philosophy. A vent on the tower roof, a crack in the floor and a book on a music stand serve as other ways in and out. Teleport waypoints called 'Exploring the Tower of Gestalt' follow the Traveler down through the levels during the quests.

## places
### STANDARD
Each level carries exhibit-style panels and certificates for Ordo members, plus optical couplers (an Ordo design) used for puzzles. Notes describe the tower as a model of the evolution of the human soul. The bottom level holds a storybook with comments in purple ink, and a long investigation log about studying ruined temples and ancient Remurian script.
### LARGE +
Certificates honour members by level. Madam M. Laveaux, Level 4, is said to have returned from Natlan. Ms. E. Lenormand, a book merchant, was promoted to Level 3. Erich, a historian formerly of the Institute of Natural Philosophy, is at Level 2. Griffin L. Randolph and Susannah hold honorary Level 1 rank. Ms. Giuffria, a dancer, reached Level 3. The panels credit a sponsor, Mr. Eliphas, for the optical-coupling design.
### ULTRA +
The investigation log says the Ordo's scholars had trouble reading antediluvian bronze slates and stone tablets in Remurian script until they were reminded by Erich about a copy at the Institute of Natural Philosophy. They sorted a spirit into memory, wishes, soul and persona, with 'True Will' as the state nearest to divine freedom.

## people
### STANDARD
The Ordo was founded over 400 years ago by Rene de Petrichor and Jakob Ingold, former members of the Narzissenkreuz Institute, and led by Narzissenkreuz. Jakob personally confronts the Traveler at the top of the tower. Ann, Caterpillar and Seymour come to the tower with the Traveler.
### LARGE +
Alain and Mary-Ann Guillotin, also from the Institute, refused Rene's methods and never joined the Ordo.

## governance
### STANDARD
Members were grouped in clearance levels from one to four, with honorary levels for supporters who could not pass the tests. A notice on a wall panel high in the tower, carrying the seal of the courts and the signatures of Alain Guillotin and Neuvillette, declares the Ordo an illegal extremist group and criminal organisation under Fontainian law and the Mermonia Act, and orders the site sealed permanently. Destroying the notice board would count as a challenge to national authority.

## culture
### LARGE
The Ordo believed people refine themselves through cycles of samsara: Hyperborea, Natlantea, Remuria and the first half of the fourth cycle, Khraun-Arya. The tower's introduction text insists this is a spiritual idea and no challenge to the Akademiya's research. The Ordo's goal was the loss of paradise, the defeat of evil dragons, original sin and baptism, and finally freedom from the gods.

## history
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The Ordo, formed after the All-Devouring Narwhal devoured the Primordial Sea, held that the world's destruction was coming and that the only escape was to merge people's wills into one transcendent being. It used the Tower of Ipsissimus as its base while it studied the Primordial Sea beneath. In Search in the Algae Sea (4.2) the Traveler is taken to the top of the tower, where Jakob demands a component of the Holy Blade of Narzissenkreuz. After a fight the blade is forged, and the Doomsday Clock strikes twelve and crushes Jakob.
### LARGE +
Rene de Petrichor later removed his own personality and was 'reborn' as the Master, merged with the Director of the old Narzissenkreuz Institute, as part of a plan for an Oceanid that would gather the minds of all Fontainians. In Savior's Wake the Traveler goes down the three levels, breaks the seal above the Primordial Sea and fights reactivated clockwork meka. In Waking from the Great Dream the Traveler defeats Narzissenkreuz.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Clockwork meka of 'Special Order 172' wake at the lowest level when the seal is broken. The Primordial Sea beneath is dangerous.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Eerie, ceremonial and unfinished. Panels read like a trade-fair exhibit, with polite thanks to sponsors whose names have been struck out, and a countdown clock hangs over everything.

## travel
### STANDARD
The tower is on the ground close to a teleport waypoint. After Savior's Wake it is permanently underwater, so the structure cannot be walked through again.

## quests
### STANDARD
The world quest series In the Wake of Narcissus (4.2) covers Search in the Algae Sea, Savior's Wake, Waking from the Great Dream and Rowboat's Wake. Savior's Wake is the quest that takes place in the tower itself.

## status
### LITE
Sealed by the courts and, since 4.2, permanently submerged.

## entry: The Narzissenkreuz Ordo Chamber
@keys: Narzissenkreuz Ordo chamber, Doomsday Clock, Tower of Gestalt
@keys2: Ordo, Narzissenkreuz, Ipsissimus
@version: 4.2
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
A hidden meeting chamber at the top of the tower. A Doomsday Clock made by the mage Narzissenkreuz is built into its ceiling to count down to the apocalypse. Its map description reads that great dreams were once born here and countless souls melted. Notes left inside speak of forsaking the self and being reborn as a holy infant, and warn that receiving a Vision means selling oneself to the fate of the world.
