## summary
### LITE
Shattuck is a restaurant in the village of Millhaven in northern Mondstadt. Benthem owns it. It sells simple food and cooking ingredients and first appeared in Version 6.5.
### ULTRA +
The wiki says the name is an archaic word for grapefruit.

## places
### STANDARD
A single restaurant. In gameplay terms the food restocks daily and the ingredients weekly, and the stock includes a local dish, Millhaven's Morning Plate.

## people
### STANDARD
Benthem is the owner. His idle call is to gather round and see the fresh venison.
### LARGE +
The wiki lists the Knight of Favonius Genna and a man named Uchida Kotaro among the people there; their roles are not given.

## travel
### STANDARD
In Millhaven, in the Windrest Peak region, the northern part of Mondstadt added in Version 6.5.

## status
### LITE
Open as of 7.1, as far as sources show.
