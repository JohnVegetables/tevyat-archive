## summary
### LITE
Shapur Hotel is the inn of Port Ormos, opened by Shapur and now managed by his daughter Sareh. A lift in front of the building saves guests the long climb up the incline, and it is free for anyone to use. Guests are asked to register their name, party size, reason for the stay and length of stay, though they may decline. Many guests play Genius Invokation TCG.
### STANDARD +
The hotel's interior can only be entered during Emilie's Story Quest. A cat called Sisi belongs to Shapur and often wanders off.

## people
### STANDARD
Shapur, the owner, is an older man who is constantly calling for his cat Sisi and, asked who he is, simply says he owns the inn. Sareh, the hostess, is polite and organised and is worried about Amandeep, a messy-looking researcher who lived there for a long time, mentoring young researchers on their papers, and who vanished on the day sea monsters were about without paying his bill. She has asked all of Port Ormos to help look. Also listed around the hotel: Alexandra and Przheva (merchants), Ruying (night merchant), Arayesh (hairdresser), Farahnaz (researcher) and the cats Nargis, Lale and Rozan.
### LARGE +
During the Sabzeruz Festival event Chromatic Ode of Candies and Roses (5.1, limited), Shapur calls after Sisi for interfering with the Parade, then insists he saw a Flower Carriage and a cat even when told there is none. Separately, Sareh's idle thought is that, with no news lately, she will not contact the Akademiya for a while.

## quests
@version: 4.8
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Floral Debt, Blood Due is Act I of Emilie's Story Quest, the Pomum de Ambra Chapter. Emilie, a Fontaine perfumer, arrives in Port Ormos for a flower exhibition in the hope of tracing a rumour that the extinct flower Auguste has reappeared. Tighnari helps with the exhibition. Emilie and the Traveler go to the hotel to meet her old teacher Edgar, who is in charge of the exhibition, and the stay at the hotel turns into a murder mystery.
### LARGE +
Per the wiki summary, guests die in their rooms over the following days. Emilie and the Traveler work out that a sample of Auguste was hidden in a Glaze Lily with Slime Condensate so that its scent would release only at night, that the whole hotel was a trap set by a culprit called Kirya, and that Kirya had been disguised as Edgar. The chase ends in a domain in the final part, Base Note: Auguste. Motive and aftermath are left to Emilie's own entry.
