## summary
### LITE
Dunanna Forward Station is a buried installation under Dunanna Pit, an area of Nod-Krai near Fontaine and east of Pohjola. It is an old moon-landing base that includes a chamber for computing the orbits of the Three Moons, a generator room and a Main Control Hall, and it launches a lunar shuttle. It appears in the world quest Pioneers (Version 6.7).
### STANDARD +
The Traveler and Paimon find it while following someone they believe is Columbina to a small island. The facility is dormant until the Traveler uses Archaeolune power to restart it.

## geography
### STANDARD
The station lies beneath a small island reached by Moonlane from Misty Shoal, on the east side of Piramida. A huge pit filled with water lies on the island, and a hole in the ground leads down to the ruins. Floating Archaeolune rocks, broken machines and sealed doors fill the ruins. Dunanna Pit has no Meeting Point of its own; it shares the Ancient Moon's Remnant at Nuur Stone Circle with the Lunar Highlands.
### LARGE +
The map shows the station as having a lower level, where the Main Control Hall sits. Inside the ruins are rings that fall to the floor until restored, and signs in Edict Module form label rooms (for example the Celestial Orbit Computation Chamber).

## places
### STANDARD
The Celestial Orbit Computation Chamber holds a terminal that calculates the positions of the Three Moons (Trilune coordinates). Only the Frost Moon can be reached from it; the Eternal and Iridescent Moons return "connection lost". The generator room has a Backup Power Generator that does not respond until it is struck with Archaeolune Ripples. Another room simulates lunar gravity, and the Main Control Hall is the central hub for command reception and transmission for the whole station. A lunar shuttle waits beyond the hall.
### LARGE +
Moving parts include Archaeolune energy pathways, blocked by Ancient Moon Pearls that must be lifted before power can flow. Intrusion defences include Transmission Modules that target visitors and a guardian called Anzu, a Precursor's Secret Source Matrix of Overseer Network, in the Main Control Hall; destroying it sets off a self-destruct countdown that asks for orbital coordinates.
### ULTRA +
After the correct three-symbol coordinates are entered, the station announces engine ignition and tells everyone to board the lunar spacecraft, while the alarm keeps sounding. The spacecraft has escape pods and an archaeolune spectrometer, which the black kuuhenki uses to speak. Before launch, the spacecraft offers landing sites such as Moonheart Gap, the Archaeolune Storm Research Center, Ungien's Circle and the Eye of Ibni-Belum; in the quest only Ungien's Circle connects.

## quests
### STANDARD
In Pioneers (Moon Gazing, Act I), the Traveler and Paimon follow what looks like Columbina but is a black kuuhenki wearing her likeness. Before the reveal, the figure silently grants the Traveler the power of Archaeolune Energy so that together they can restore the station. The kuuhenki says Columbina had rescued it on the moon and taken it to Silvermoon Hall, and that it wants to return to the moon; Paimon names it Eggie. The Traveler decides to ride the shuttle with it.
### LARGE +
Moon Gazing is a world quest series on the Frost Moon map whose later acts are Visitors From the Stars and Project Moongazer; this entry covers only the station.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
An underground complex of broken machines and drifting, weightless-looking rocks, empty of people. Automated voice announcements and alarms speak for it; once the Traveler restores power, the machinery comes back to life and treats visitors as intruders.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Automated defences: Transmission Modules that attack intruders, Secret Source Automatons, and the Main Control Hall's guardian Anzu. The self-destruct system treats any visitor as an intruder.

## status
### LITE
After Pioneers the station is working and its shuttle has left with the Traveler's party. Nothing after 7.1 is recorded.

### STANDARD
The black kuuhenki that leads the Traveler through the station first appears as Columbina and changes into its real form when the Traveler asks for the gesture they share in Silvermoon Hall. It can only speak in short, breathless phrases, and speaks more clearly through the shuttle's archaeolune spectrometer. It says it belongs on the moon, has a mission it has forgotten, and was rescued by Columbina. Paimon nicknames it Eggie after being tricked into boarding the shuttle.

### STANDARD
The terminal prompts for Trilune coordinates of the Three Moons. In the quest only the Frost Moon coordinates are found; it then sends base engineering blueprints and a route map to the Main Control Hall for moon landing preparations.
