## summary
### LITE
Tenshukaku is the castle keep at the top of Inazuma City on Narukami Island, the residence of the Raiden Shogun and the place from which she rules Inazuma. Built during the Archon War, it is one of the three parts of the city alongside Hanamizaka and Tenryou, and the Tenryou Commission Headquarters stands within its grounds. The Statue of the Omnipresent God, built to hold Visions confiscated under the Vision Hunt Decree, stands before it.
### STANDARD +
Its name means a castle's tower keep, literally "heavenly protector". Official material describes it as the residence of the most exalted one in Inazuma, standing on the city's highest point and watching over all under the Electro Archon's rule. Entry into the Shogun's presence is tightly controlled.

## geography
### STANDARD
Inazuma City rises on cliffs at the southern tip of Narukami Island. Tenshukaku crowns it, above the city's other two parts, Hanamizaka and Tenryou. The Statue of the Omnipresent God stands in front of the keep.

## places
### STANDARD
Entrance area, guarded by Shogunate officers; a central courtyard where officials go about their business; a southern tower; the Tenryou Commission Headquarters with its courtyard and offices; and the Shogun's throne chamber, where she receives visitors and where the duel before the throne was fought.
### LARGE +
The building is decorated with antler-like ornaments of the kind worn as crests on samurai helmets. The chamber where the Shogun was found pacing alone during her seclusion is entered through the same doors that lead to the scene of the duel with Signora.

## people
### STANDARD
The Raiden Shogun resides here: the puppet Shogun who handles Inazuma's affairs, with Ei meditating within her Plane of Euthymia. The Tenryou Commission, led by the Kujou Clan, works out of its headquarters here. Okuzumeshuu guards such as Takeshi control access to the Shogun.
### LARGE +
After the war, Kujou Kamaji, acting head of the Kujou Clan, is often found near the entrance. Other officials seen around the keep include yoriki and doushin of the Tenryou Commission, hatamoto retainers, the tax secretary Takatsukasa Tomohide, Mikawa Akane of the Royal Bath Commission, and the reserve official Nagaoka Hidemitsu.

## governance
### STANDARD
Tenshukaku is the seat of the Raiden Shogun's rule over the Inazuma Shogunate. The Tenryou Commission, the Shogun's executive arm in charge of security and the military, enforced the Vision Hunt Decree and the Sakoku Decree from here. Inazuman law recognises the duel before the throne, fought in the Shogun's presence with her approval; the loser dies.
### LARGE +
The Shogun holds that the right to punish Inazumans for their crimes is hers alone. During the 2.1 Archon Quest the Fatui Harbinger Signora, styling herself a Snezhnayan diplomat, was before the throne when Kujou Sara stormed in after learning that the Tenryou Commissioner Kujou Takayuki had been colluding with the Fatui. After the war Kujou Kamaji took on his father Takayuki's position as acting head of the Kujou Clan.

## history
@version: 2.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Inazuma City, Tenshukaku included, was built during the Archon War. A year before the Traveler's arrival the Vision Hunt Decree began, and the statue in front of the keep was built to receive confiscated Visions. In 2.0 the Shogun personally presided over a ceremony there marking the hundredth Vision. In 2.1 the Traveler defeated Signora in a duel before the throne inside Tenshukaku, and the Shogun executed her.
### LARGE +
At the Vision Hunt Ceremony, the Traveler snatched back Thoma's Vision; the Shogun named them an "exception" and an enemy of eternity, overpowered them, and let them escape only when Thoma created a distraction. The Traveler became a wanted criminal and joined the resistance.
### ULTRA +
Kujou Sara stormed into Tenshukaku and was found knocked unconscious before Signora; the Shogun told Signora that the right to punish Inazumans for their crimes was hers alone. Signora reminded the Traveler that, as the nation's most wanted criminal, they had no such protection, so they invoked the right of a duel before the throne, and the Shogun allowed it. Signora lost, threatened the Shogun with the Fatui's revenge, and was struck down with the Musou no Hitotachi; the Shogun let the Traveler leave alive as the victor. Just outside the keep the Shogun attacked again, Kazuha blocked her stroke with the power of his late friend's Vision, and the resistance led by Gorou charged. The Traveler fought Ei in the Plane of Euthymia, and Ei agreed to abolish the Vision Hunt Decree. The Visions on the statue were later returned to their owners.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Formal, hushed and guarded. Officials pass through the courtyard, and the guards at the entrance remind visitors to behave impeccably in the Shogun's presence. Before the throne the Shogun's word is law, and her judgment can be final.

## dangers
### LARGE
Entering without leave is dangerous: during her seclusion the Shogun's first words to the Traveler were that trespassers are to be eliminated at once. A duel before the throne ends in the death of the loser.

## quests
### STANDARD
Archon Quest Chapter II: "Amidst Stormy Judgment" (Act II, 2.0) at the statue before the keep; "Blind Loyalty, Reckless Courage", "Duel Before the Throne" and "The Omnipresent God" (Act III, 2.1). Raiden Shogun Story Quest: "The Lonesome Euthymia" (Imperatrix Umbrosa Chapter, Act I).
### LARGE +
The Trounce Domain "Narukami Island: Tenshukaku" re-creates the battle with Signora as a gameplay memory; this replay is not a new event in the story.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 Tenshukaku remains the Raiden Shogun's residence and seat of rule. The Vision Hunt Decree was abolished in 2.1, Signora died here, and Ei has since reconsidered how she rules.
### STANDARD +
After the war Ei briefly withdrew into seclusion inside Tenshukaku, temporarily disabling many of the puppet's functions rather than modifying its rules while she worked out the form Eternity should take; she then went out among her people in person. No later story found is set at Tenshukaku in 6.x (Nod-Krai) or 7.0-7.1 (Snezhnaya).

## entry: Duel before the throne
@keys: duel before the throne, Gozenjiai
@version: 2.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
A duel before the throne is a contest under Inazuman law, fought in the Raiden Shogun's presence at Tenshukaku with her consent; the loser forfeits their life. Signora called it making the law of the land work for you. The Traveler invoked it against Signora in 2.1; after her defeat the Shogun herself carried out the sentence with the Musou no Hitotachi and, acknowledging the victor's honour, let the Traveler leave Tenshukaku alive.

## entry: The Shogun's seclusion and the Pass Permit
@keys: Pass Permit, Takeshi
@keys2: Tenshukaku, Shogun, Yae Miko, Ei
@version: 2.1
### LARGE
For a time after the war the Shogun saw no one, and rumours spread in the city that she was unwell. Only Yae Miko held a Pass Permit allowing entry to Tenshukaku and an audience with the Shogun, kept for emergencies and apparently never used; she lent it to the Traveler, and the guard Takeshi, astonished that it was genuine, let them in. Inside, the half-disabled puppet threatened them as trespassers before Ei drew them into the Plane of Euthymia and agreed to walk among her people.
