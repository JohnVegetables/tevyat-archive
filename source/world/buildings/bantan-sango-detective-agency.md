## summary
### LITE
The Bantan Sango Detective Agency is a private detective office in Hanamizaka, the suburb of Inazuma City north of the river. It is run by the detective Sango, who is its manager and president, with Ryuuji as her assistant. Its advertisement board promises to investigate anything on a client's behalf, from missing persons and pets to marital investigations, commercial commissions, procurement and delivery.
### STANDARD +
The name in Japanese reads roughly "Everything Sango Detective Agency", which fits its pitch. Sango says it handles murder, banditry, affair investigations and pet searches alike, warns that the fees are not cheap and should be ready before anyone walks in, and adds that she does not make empty promises. Sango does the thinking and Ryuuji does the legwork.

## places
### STANDARD
The agency stands in the eastern part of Hanamizaka, east of the Amenoma Smithy. An advertisement board outside lists its services, and Sango receives clients inside.
### LARGE +
The board's text is short and brash: the agency will investigate anything for you, and the list of services ends with "we do it all". Ryuuji is usually out on a lead rather than at the office; in the first Case File commission the Traveler meets him on the beach south of the northern Inazuma City teleporter.

## people
### LITE
Sango is the detective and manager; Ryuuji is her assistant who gathers evidence.
### STANDARD
Sango handles analysis. She says gathering intelligence is Ryuuji's job and that she waits for the clues and works through them, and she objects hotly if anyone calls that slacking. Her advice to clients is blunt: emotion is the enemy of truth, and anyone not ready to bear the truth should not cross the threshold of wisdom lightly. Ryuuji handles the physical side, collecting evidence and clues and scouting places to confirm her deductions; he admits his strength is running errands rather than stealth.
### LARGE +
Ryuuji used to be a doushin at the Police Station. Someone framed him and he was suspended; Sango cleared his name without charging him once she saw he was broke, and then took him on as her assistant. His old training lets him look after himself in risky investigations. He says Sango has a short fuse but is upright, and that her intelligence is what saved him. Owada, the Police Station's yoriki, is an old acquaintance who wanted to help during the frame-up but had little power. Uesugi, a young doushin, sometimes works cases alongside Ryuuji and enjoys a sense of accomplishment that desk duty does not give him.
### ULTRA +
Ryuuji's real name is Takeo, but Sango thinks it does not sound tough enough and insists on Ryuuji; he finds it a little bothersome but goes along with it. He credits the Electro Archon for the fact that his name was cleared. He also remarks that, in Inazuma's present state, even the police are not fully reliable. According to the wiki profile, Shikanoin Heizou is the one person who can follow Sango's train of thought, though she dislikes him.

## history
### STANDARD
No founding date or earlier history is given for the agency. Ryuuji's frame-up and Sango's rescue of him happened earlier still, with no date stated. When the Traveler says they are looking for a family member lost to an unknown deity, Sango calls it a worthwhile case for the agency.
### LARGE +
In the repeatable daily commission series "Bantan Sango Case File", the Traveler is sent first to Sango, then to meet Ryuuji, and fends off two waves of Treasure Hoarders before reporting back; completing it opens the next case-file commission.
### ULTRA +
In version 2.3 the agency also took part in a limited-time event, described in its own entry below.

## quests
### STANDARD
The agency appears in the repeatable "Bantan Sango Case File" commissions and, in version 2.3, in the Warrior Dog event.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
A small, businesslike detective office in a working suburb. Sango's manner is brisk and tart, Ryuuji comes and goes on errands, and clients are told to lean in close rather than let anyone eavesdrop on critical information.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the agency is open and unchanged: Sango still runs it and Ryuuji still works for her.

## entry: Sango, the Detective
@keys: Sango, Detective Sango, President Sango
@keys2: Bantan Sango, detective agency, Hanamizaka
### STANDARD
Sango is the competent, short-tempered president of the Bantan Sango Detective Agency, a woman in ordinary Inazuman dress who solves cases by reasoning from the clues Ryuuji brings her. She charges high fees and says she does not make empty promises. This entry records only her role at the agency; her own page carries her personality in more detail.

## entry: Ryuuji, the Assistant
@keys: Ryuuji, Takeo
@keys2: Bantan Sango, detective agency, Sango
### STANDARD
Ryuuji is Sango's assistant, a former doushin framed and suspended from the Police Station, whose name Sango cleared. He is capable in a fight and good at errands, less so at stealth. His real name is Takeo, which Sango thinks too soft; he lets her call him Ryuuji. He sees his work as supporting Sango's reasoning with his own physical effort.

## entry: Bantan Sango Case Files - The Warrior Dog
@keys: Warrior Dog, Bantan Sango Case Files, Ubiquity Net, Canine Bunshin
@version: 2.3
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
During a limited-time event in version 2.3 (7 to 20 December 2021), the Bantan Sango Detective Agency posted a commission at the Adventurers' Guild for help with a mysterious case: the mass disappearance of small animals. Players rescued the animals with a gadget called the Ubiquity Net while sneaking past patrolling Canine Bunshin. The event unlocked in four segments and required Adventure Rank 30 and the Archon Quest "The Immovable God and the Eternal Euthymia". The event is canon but was limited, so most people in Inazuma would not know its details.
