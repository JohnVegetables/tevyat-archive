## summary
### LITE
The Speaker's Chamber is a watchtower-like building in a corner of the Stadium of the Sacred Flame. The Pyro Archon presides over it, and it coordinates the Stadium's affairs and the six major tribes.
### STANDARD +
By ancient tradition the tribal chiefs gather by the flame there at regular intervals and negotiate openly under the Pyro Archon's watch.

## governance
### STANDARD
The Chamber is the meeting place of the chiefs of Natlan's six major tribes. The Pyro Archon, now Mavuika, presides. Business is to be settled without reservation beside the flame.
### LARGE +
Wiki trivia suggests it also serves as Natlan's main court, based on a world quest called The Case of the Crafting Bench. Treat this as likely but not stated in the building's own description.

## places
### LARGE
A small display room at the back can be entered only during certain quests (Echoes of Life and Past and Future) and is closed during Into Eternal Night. Its contents are quest-specific and not described here.

## status
### LITE
Active as the Stadium's council hall as of 7.1.
### ULTRA +
The Chamber is part of the Stadium complex, which also contains the Temple of Pax to the south; the Stadium is the shared gathering ground of the six major tribes under the Pyro Archon.
