## summary
### LITE
Puspa Café is a coffee shop with a long history on Treasures Street in Sumeru City. Its map text calls it a place serving authentic Sumeru-style coffee and a rare social space where people from all walks of life like to gather. Enteka is its acting manager, and she reads customers' fortunes in the coffee grounds.
### STANDARD +
It is a spot for conversation, storytelling and Genius Invokation TCG games, and its shop is open at any hour.

## places
### STANDARD
A single café with a counter and a shop. The stall is open at all times and restocks daily with desserts such as baklava, candied ajilenakh nut and padisarah pudding, plus coffee beans, and it also sells recipes for several sweets. A message board stands outside, to the left just before the entrance.
### LARGE +
Regulars mention a card table where the card game is played.

## people
### STANDARD
Staff are Enteka (acting manager), Maddah (the resident storyteller) and Gata, a young cat. Customers vary with the time of day: Iris (a researcher) by day; Nayab (a relaxed Matra), Izem (a veteran of the Corps of Thirty), Zohreh (a researcher), Sanad (a student), Mawyo (a self-described freelancer) and the self-styled millionaire Hatim at night.
### LARGE +
Enteka says she can make a coffee to any taste, however offbeat, and asks first-time customers whether they like it sweet or bitter, strong or mild. She says Gata is not yet a year old, is curious, and jumps up to watch whenever she reads the grounds; patting her is welcome.
### ULTRA +
Hatim and Mawyo also appear on Treasures Street by day or night, at different times. The board's posts name Cyno, Nilou, Kaveh, Alhaitham and Dori in passing.

## governance
### STANDARD
Enteka is acting manager 'for the time being', as the board puts it. The original owner went missing on a desert expedition three years before a Kalimi Exchange notice on the café, which the wiki cites. The reason Enteka holds the post is not explained.

## culture
### STANDARD
Coffee, cards and talk. Regulars include students preparing for the Akademiya entrance exam, who value the friendly atmosphere and the seniors they can ask for guidance. Enteka's fortune-telling from coffee grounds is a house speciality.
### LARGE +
The message board mixes humour and gossip: a repentant player who invented a non-existent card to cheat others and was set straight by Cyno; the 'Puspa Card Club' recruiting Genius Invokation players, with Enteka asking its founder to at least buy a coffee; a storyteller advertising the next instalment of the legend of Antara, answered by Enteka offering free coffee if he stops posting ads; and a veteran of the Corps of Thirty noting that he gets the same coffee and hears the same story every day.
### ULTRA +
Two posts show how scholars treat the place. An unsigned debate between voices identified with Kaveh and Alhaitham, about art and commerce, ends with Enteka reminding them of a monthly tab; a message seemingly from a noble asks to put it on Dori's tab. A Matra notice warns that forbidden knowledge obtained illegally can cost a student the right to study at the Akademiya, and that Canned Knowledge smuggling has been reported. These are board posts, not quest events. The name Puspa is Sanskrit for 'flower' or 'blossom' (wiki note).

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Warm, talkative and slightly old-fashioned: the smell of coffee, a storyteller repeating familiar tales, card players arguing over decks, and a cat underfoot.

## dangers
### STANDARD
None are described. The only caution is a joke that Enteka accepts no liability for losses from tips posted on the board.

## travel
### STANDARD
Puspa Café is on Treasures Street in Sumeru City. Its message board is just outside the door.

## status
### LITE
Open and operating as of 7.1, as far as the sources show. Enteka is still acting manager.

### STANDARD
Enteka is the acting manager of Puspa Café and runs its shop. She introduces herself as Acting Café Manager at the customer's service. She reads coffee grounds, and the map text says her readings are rumoured to be more accurate than the professional diviners at the bazaar. No fuller profile exists.
### LARGE +
In her thoughts she wonders whether Gata is shedding a lot. On the message board she writes the Acting Manager's replies: polite but dry, teasing customers and reminding patrons to pay.

Gata is the café's young cat, under a year old by Enteka's account, curious and affectionate. Nilou's board message shows she is fond of Gata, and Enteka says Gata seems fond of her too.
