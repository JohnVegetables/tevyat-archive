## summary
### LITE
A rest area within the Stadium of the Sacred Flame, in the Basin of Unnumbered Flames, Natlan, serving the stadium's fighters and visitors.
### STANDARD +
It holds the Weary Inn, a Contestant's Lounge and a street-food stall, with a receptionist and several named residents.

## places
### STANDARD
The Weary Inn has a Weary Inn Bulletin Board, and the Contestant's Lounge has a Contestant Lounge Noticeboard.

## people
### STANDARD
Hiia is the receptionist. Vitusov is inside the Weary Inn. In the Contestant's Lounge, Erinle is a doctor and Karpa the manager. Chanca owns Zakan's Street Bites. Also listed, with no roles stated: Izze, Kumayo, Kwame, Machinna, Purum and Tizoc.
### LARGE +
Inna is listed here only until the quest A Fuel Named "Fate", after which she is no longer there.

## status
### LITE
Open and populated as of 7.1.
### ULTRA +
The area is inside the Stadium itself, unlike the Chuwen Fair along its northern side; its map level has its own name, Warrior's Rest Area.
