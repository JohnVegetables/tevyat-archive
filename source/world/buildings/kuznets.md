## summary
### LITE
"Kuznets" ("Blacksmith") is the smithy in Snezhnograd run by Kuzmichev, a domovoy blacksmith. It sits near the city's front gate.
### STANDARD +
Kuzmichev forges and sells weapons and takes pride in quality: he keeps checking his furnace temperature because, he says, small differences decide whether a weapon succeeds or fails.
## people
### STANDARD
Kuzmichev is a domovoy who works as the town's smith. He is proud and jovial and addresses customers as "dear customer". He tells of once tapping a boastful man's cheek with a shovel until the man could barely summon any Prime Ice, as his argument that steel in hand beats relying on Prime Ice alone.
### LARGE +
He has dealings with Alyosha the hunter, who asked him for hunting traps. The wiki also notes a bulletin-board message hinting that Kuzmichev is in his fifties.
## culture
### STANDARD
Smithing here is a craft of temperature and detail. The shop is a place where weapons are bought, commissioned and made, in a city where many people also rely on Prime Ice for protection.
## atmosphere
### ULTRA
Heat and hammering inside a cold city: the glow of the furnace against frost at the door, a smith who talks while he checks his gauges.
## gameplay
### STANDARD
Game mechanics: Kuzmichev forges certain four-star weapons and weapon-enhancement materials, and sells low-rarity weapons and forging diagrams.
