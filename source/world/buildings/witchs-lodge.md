## summary
### LITE
The Witch's Lodge is a secret home of Nicole, an angel and member of the Hexenzirkel, tucked away in The Witch's Garden in Nod-Krai's Voidsea Outlook. Legend says it holds vast riches that only a mind as sharp as the witch's can find. The Traveler enters it room by room through Nicole's world quests.
### STANDARD +
Nicole says she builds several homes so that someone will eventually wander in by mistake. The first quest, Witch's Lodge (Version 6.3), has her tell a story in which she poses as "Nicola Coco, the Great Mage Everlasting", who keeps her secret home and needs a helper to build her territory. She then asks the Traveler for photos of finches, a squirrel and a mandragora as models for statues.

## geography
### STANDARD
The lodge stands in The Witch's Garden, a point of interest in the Voidsea Outlook area at the southern end of Pohjola, north of Paha Isle. The exterior unlocks with the quest Witch's Lodge, and the interior after the quest The Joyous Floating Island. The garden's Meeting Point stands at the statue before the bridge.
### LARGE +
The grounds are dotted with exquisite statues. In Nicole's story they are opponents turned to stone, but she then admits she was joking and says they are her way of capturing a moment in time.

## places
### STANDARD
Rooms unlock one at a time: a tea room with a mandragora statue standing in for the Dodo-King, a study with a painting, a studio with an angel statue, a tall study with bookshelves and a typewriter, an upstairs room on the left of the entrance, and a hidden room on the right.
### LARGE +
The tea room's statue is Nicole's stand-in for the Dodo-King in her tea-party game. The painting in the study shows a gathering Nicole misses, with candlesticks and a pot of tea left for the Dodocos. The studio angel statue prompts Nicole to say that the humans of today live comfortably, addressed to "sisters". In the tall study, Nicole misplaces a favorite book and a wind tunnel appears to help the player reach it; a chest of 200,000 Mora appears when it is found.
### ULTRA +
The hidden room, unlocked by A Window Into the World, has a platform at its end; interacting with it as Nicole produces "Observation... commencing" as the camera pans out over the view. Nicole describes herself as one of the fixed observation points for changes in the world, which is why the room needs excellent visibility; the wiki notes the hidden room resembles structures in the Temple of Space. The scenery seen from it, she says, exists only in her memories.

## people
### STANDARD
Nicole is its owner and appears in person during the quests, usually at the top of the stairs. She tells stories, jokes about being a "great mage" and takes the Traveler's help in building and carving. Paimon accompanies the Traveler throughout.
### LARGE +
Alice of the Hexenzirkel borrowed the lodge key to set up a tea room, which Nicole mentions when the Traveler finds a large Seelie Court chair in the tea room during Witch's Homework: The Role of a Guide...?, a quest in which Barbeloth also appears.

## quests
### STANDARD
The Witch's Lodge quest opens the series: after the photo task and a fight with an enraged mandragora, the Traveler carves a statue by following number clues that show how many blocks must stay in each row. The series Beyond Silver and Fine Gold then unlocks one room each week, after the previous one is fully explored, over about four weeks: The Joyous Floating Island, The Silent Theater, The Lost Palace and The Forgotten Lighthouse. A fifth quest, A Window Into the World, opens the hidden room.
### LARGE +
Once a quest in the series is done and before the next unlocks, Nicole says she has no more chests to give and offers to scrape some together if the Traveler gives her a few hundred years. After A Window Into the World, she says the scenery in the hidden room exists only in her memories.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Cozy, theatrical and full of storybook touches: statues, tea sets, cluttered shelves and Nicole's habit of narrating her life as a tale.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the lodge is a furnished retreat that Nicole shares with the Traveler; she may be away at other homes.

## entry: Nicola Coco
@keys: Nicola Coco, Great Mage Everlasting
@keys2: Nicole, Witch's Lodge
@version: 6.3
### STANDARD
Nicola Coco, the Great Mage Everlasting, is the character in Nicole's story-in-progress who lives in a secret home, turns people into statues as a "joke" and needs a hero to build her territory. Nicole later reveals that the voice and the story were hers. She says a figure called Mr. Nine can refine the text later.

## entry: Statue Carving
@keys: statue carving, carving puzzle
@keys2: Witch's Lodge, Nicole
@version: 6.3
### STANDARD
In the lodge, the Traveler and Paimon help Nicole carve statues from blocks. Numbers on the blocks show how many blocks in that row or column must stay; players use Trim Excess and Preserve Shape skills, and a squirrel statue rotates the model. This is a gameplay puzzle presented in-story as Nicole's craft lesson.
