## summary
### LITE
The Knights of Favonius Headquarters is the order's main building in Mondstadt City and the centre of the nation's government. Inside are the Grand Master's office, where Acting Grand Master Jean works almost without pause, Chief Alchemist Albedo's office on the second floor, a locked solitary confinement room that Klee has more or less made her own, and the Mondstadt Library, kept by the librarian Lisa. The library is the largest in northern Teyvat and is open to everyone.
### STANDARD +
This is where newcomers are brought to meet the Knights: Kaeya escorted the Traveler here after Stormterror's attack, and Jean welcomed them and laid out the plan against Dvalin.

## places
### STANDARD
Main hall on the ground floor, with the library and the Grand Master's office leading off it. The Grand Master's office holds a large map of Mondstadt on the wall, a desk and bookshelves, and a grandfather clock. Albedo's office is upstairs and usually kept shut. Three rooms stay locked; the ground-floor one is solitary confinement. The library has two levels: an upper floor with the librarian's desk and a lower floor.
### LARGE +
In the library basement stands a heavy poplar-wood door that is older than the Knights and survived the great fire. The Knights officially say the restricted section lies behind it. A door on the library's lower floor leads to the Theater Lobby of the Imaginarium Theater (4.7). Kaeya takes the Traveler to a courtyard just outside the building for a private talk. The stairwell continues to upper floors that are never shown.
### ULTRA +
After Noelle's story quest, a sword can be seen plunged deep into the office ceiling. A clutch of books sits in the office, among them The Legend of Vennessa, History of Kings and Clans and The Mondstadt Tower; the library shelves hold series such as Hex & Hound, The Boar Princess (evidently Lily's family's copy, going by the notes in its margins and a card suggesting it be donated to the library), The Fox in the Dandelion Sea and Vera's Melancholy.

## people
### LITE
Jean (Acting Grand Master), Lisa (librarian), Albedo (Chief Alchemist), Klee (when confined), and the knights and captains who report here, including Kaeya.
### STANDARD +
Knights Athos and Porthos guard the front door. Inside, Wood watches the confinement room and Wyratt guards a library door, asking visitors to keep quiet and wipe their shoes before going in. The hilichurl-language scholar Ella Musk studies in the library; the travelling scholar Sayid reads there at night.
### LARGE +
Grand Master Varka's desk is here, and he has been back since 6.4. When Hertha, Eula and the captains of the 2nd and 3rd Companies, Note and Skeiron, were called in for Albedo's case in 5.6, headquarters was crowded with knights.

## governance
### STANDARD
Mondstadt's government works out of this building: the Grand Master's office sets the Knights' orders, receives captains' reports and the Grand Master's letters, and handles the paperwork the order is famous for. Discipline is enforced here too, from Klee's confinement to the trial Jean convened for Albedo, for which every captain was summoned.

## culture
### STANDARD
The library is free to use and lending is not limited to Mondstadters: apart from the restricted section, every book is open to the public. The posted "Library Rules - Seventh Edition" cut the list down to three rules: be quiet, do not damage books, return books on time. It adds, darkly, that everyone already knows how violations are dealt with.
### LARGE +
With the right paperwork even a whole set of volumes can be borrowed for a year or more. Works from Yae Publishing House are shelved under "Children and Teens". The Favonius Codex was the Knights' scholars' handmade tome, and the library's founding ideal is that songs fade and wine is fleeting, but what is written lasts.

## history
### STANDARD
When the Knights were founded after Vennessa's rebellion, the Dawn Knight Ragnvindr turned the aristocrats' indoor baths into a library. Gifts from bards, scholars and travellers made it the largest collection in the northern half of the continent. The "Great Fire of Fall Equinox" later burned it, leaving only a fraction of the collection (one-sixth, per the Chinese text), yet it remains the largest library in the north.
### LARGE +
The building was the base for the Knights' response to Stormterror in the Prologue (1.0). In 3.1 Varka's letter from the expedition was read in the main hall and the office. In 5.6 Albedo was tried and later carried out the ritual for Durin from his office. In 6.2 Alice smuggled a mages' book in among a large donation of books, and in 6.4 Varka returned.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Hushed in the library (the guard will remind you), businesslike in the hall, and buried in paperwork in the Grand Master's office, where Jean works late and a grandfather clock ticks. Faint crackling from behind the confinement door means Klee is in again.

## quests
### STANDARD
Prologue Act I "Knights of Favonius" (meeting Jean and Lisa), Prologue Act II "Shadow Over Mondstadt", Kaeya's story quest (1.0), Interlude Chapter Act IV "Paralogism" (5.6), the world quest "Unexpected Commission" (6.2), and the Lupus Majoris act "To Those Who Embark on the Expedition" (6.4).
### LARGE +
Kaeya's Hangout (3.8) involves reporting in the Acting Grand Master's office, and the Traveler can talk to Klee through the confinement-room door. The Of Ballads and Brews event quest "Sounds From Afar" (3.1) and the 6.4 event "Homeward, He Who Caught the Wind" also use the building.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the headquarters is fully active. Varka has been home since 6.4, Jean still holds the title Acting Grand Master and works long hours here, and Lisa still runs the library.
### STANDARD +
Varka's secret drinking money, tucked in the office desk, was found and handed to the Traveler as a reward after the 6.4 rescue. Seamus Pegg has mentioned Jean working overtime at headquarters after his return (6.5).

## entry: The Mondstadt Library
@keys: Mondstadt Library, Knights of Favonius Library, Favonius Library
### STANDARD
The library of the Knights of Favonius, run by Lisa, is the largest in northern Teyvat even after the Great Fire of Fall Equinox. Ragnvindr founded it in the old aristocrats' indoor baths. Anyone may read or borrow books, except from the restricted section, which officially lies behind an ancient poplar door in the basement, and which Lisa consults for dangerous research.
### LARGE +
Lisa offered the Traveler books on monsters and a part-time job on first meeting. Xilonen of Natlan once napped in the library before moving to trees so as not to disturb readers. The Traveler is given the Teyvat Travel Guide - Mondstadt here in a world quest.

## entry: The Grand Master's Office
@keys: Grand Master's Office, Grand Master's office, Acting Grand Master's office, Jean's office
### STANDARD
The office of the Grand Master, used day to day by Acting Grand Master Jean. Here Jean briefed the Traveler on Stormterror, consulted Lisa there as the Stormterror crisis deepened, and later assigned the Traveler their own investigation into Albedo's case. A large wall map of Mondstadt hangs inside.
### LARGE +
In 3.1 Varka's letter told Lisa that a wooden box of things Razor's parents left him was in a cabinet above the third bookshelf to the right of the office's grandfather clock, and that it was time to give it to Razor.

## entry: Albedo's Office and the Paralogism Trial
@keys: Albedo's office, Paralogism, Crimes One and Two, The Gardener
@keys2: Albedo, Knights of Favonius, headquarters
@version: 5.6
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
During Windblume in 5.6, Jean called the captains to headquarters because Albedo stood accused of murder and of disposing of remains. The Knights and the Church held a closed trial together, with Hertha prosecuting, Kaeya defending and Deacon Dahlia as assessor. Albedo said he had been in his second-floor office, and Sucrose confirmed he had been with her in the library, though she admitted she had fallen asleep. He was found guilty and taken away. Jean privately doubted the verdict and sent the Traveler to investigate, and a search of Albedo's office with Kaeya turned up a secret compartment in his desk, holding notes on an experiment. Later Mini Durin was found hiding in that office. During the festival fireworks Albedo began his long-prepared ritual there, lighting arrays across the city with Mini Durin at the centre. This led to Durin's rebirth. The trial itself turned out to have been planned by Albedo and the Knights over half a year to lure out a Durin-spawned impostor, the prosecuting "Hertha".

## entry: Klee's Solitary Confinement Room
@keys: solitary confinement, confinement room
@keys2: Klee, Knights of Favonius, headquarters
### STANDARD
A locked ground-floor room that is formally the Knights' solitary confinement cell but is used almost only for Klee, who is shut in after her explosive mischief. The guard Wood jokes that it is more or less her private property and that crackling noises inside suggest she is working on new bombs.

## entry: The Poplar Door and the Imaginarium Theater
@keys: Imaginarium Theater, Theater Lobby, poplar door, restricted section
@keys2: library, Mondstadt, Knights of Favonius, Lisa
@version: 4.7
### LARGE
The Favonius Codex says a sturdy poplar door in the library basement predates both the library and the Knights, and survived the fire. Officially the restricted section is behind it, but tales hint at a deeper secret. Since 4.7 a door on the library's lower floor opens onto the Theater Lobby of the Imaginarium Theater, whose concierge Wolfy is said to have been made by a mage.

## entry: The Hexenzirkel's Hidden Dictionary
@keys: Unexpected Commission, Witch's Homework, Little Witch's Dictionary
@keys2: library, Alice, Lisa, Hexenzirkel
@version: 6.2
### ULTRA
An anonymous benefactor donated a pile of entirely new books, some of them banned, and Lisa had the job posted as a premium commission. While the Traveler sorted the books, one glowing volume opened into a mage's domain and Alice appeared. She had donated the rest only to hide this book in plain sight. Written by the Hexenzirkel as a supposed companion dictionary to a storybook, it holds tasks for Mondstadters to grow stronger together, in keeping with the mages' promise to Varka at the Tripartite Conference.

## entry: Varka's Secret Stash
@keys: Varka's Secret Stash of Cash, secret stash
@keys2: Varka, Grand Master, desk
@version: 6.4
### ULTRA
Varka kept drinking money in a drawer of the Grand Master's desk, saying the most dangerous hiding place is the safest. More likely he trusts Jean neither to spend it nor to give it away. It had been meant for Dandelion Wine as an offering to the Archon. After the 6.4 rescue it was handed to the Honorary Knight as a reward instead.

## entry: Homeward Event at Headquarters
@keys: Homeward, He Who Caught the Wind
@keys2: headquarters, Varka, Jean, office
@version: 6.4
@availability: limited
### ULTRA
In the limited-time 6.4 event "Homeward, He Who Caught the Wind", after Alice's bubble-magic re-enactment of their first arrival the Traveler and Paimon find themselves at the headquarters with Alice, who jokes that tampering with the real Statue of The Seven would have landed her in "the teensy room next door". Later, when Varka turns up early at the welcome-event venue and Jean asks him to go back to headquarters to look over a few things, he groans that he will be trapped in the office until tomorrow morning. The event's Echoes of Memory replays are reached from the Grand Master's office.
