## summary
### LITE
The Flame-Melding Ritual Grounds are a subarea of the Ancient Sacred Mountain, in Natlan's Great Volcano of Tollan. According to the World Quest Sealed Site of Sacrifice it is where Xiuhcoatl created the first Astral Assemblage, a feat comparable to forging a star. The altar was sealed, and the Traveler unseals it.
### STANDARD +
Activating the Astral Assemblage replays a vision of the ritual: the dragons' success, a quarrel between its creator, identified as Xiuhcoatl, and Kukulkan, who addresses him as his king and elder brother, and a warning of a looming catastrophe. The place also holds the third "Iridescent Legacy" inscription, the last words of one of the Thirteen Sovereign Lords.

## geography
### STANDARD
The site has an upper altar where the sealed Astral Assemblage stands, high above, and lower levels with pulverite and a furnace-shell mountain weasel guarding one inscription. The viewpoint here is called "Condensed Altar" and opens only after the quest.
### LARGE +
Reaching the sealed device involves pushing stone steps into place to form a path and, optionally, pulling levers. Breaking the seal restores the Assemblage to normal operation and lets it fire a beam of light at the Celestial Axis at the centre of the Sacred City, activating one of the Axis's pillars.

## history
### STANDARD
The Ritual Grounds are where Xiuhcoatl created the first Astral Assemblage. Phlogiston was compressed under great pressure into a floating, controllable ball of heat, a miniature celestial body.
### LARGE +
The creator's brethren cheer "We've done it!" while he stands apart, as if unrelated to the deed. In the vision, Kukulkan congratulates him coldly, saying the achievement is comparable to lunar reconstruction, and says that "that one" (whom the wiki identifies as Nibelung) would have nodded in approval if still alive.
### ULTRA +
The creator says that if Kukulkan had helped, it might have been finished long ago. Kukulkan answers that he has no interest. The creator urges Kukulkan to help: the All-Plunderer's herald has been driven into the deepest of mountain valleys, but its master's awakening would bring another catastrophe. Kukulkan sneers at the idea of protecting their "pitiable brethren"; the creator says his days are numbered and he must forge a new path for the Dragonborn before the pitch-black shadow smothers them. Kukulkan suggests the Dragonborn find their own way, warning the path may be too narrow, but the creator insists "the narrow way must be walked". Kukulkan finishes by saying that on this alone he cannot concede, or in another version, that the creator once said he would understand the desire in his final moments.

## quests
@version: 5.5
### STANDARD
Sealed Site of Sacrifice is part of The Frozen, Rekindling Land (Chronicler of the Crumbling City). The Traveler, Paimon and the Little One find the Astral Assemblage locked high up, push stone platforms to form a path, break the seal and activate the device, triggering the vision.
### LARGE +
Paimon realises that the figures surrounding the platform were the dragons who lived here. The quest awards a Luxurious Chest and two Exquisite Chests, and unlocks the Condensed Altar viewpoint.

## entry: Iridescent Legacy Part III
@keys: Iridescent Legacy, Cha'ama' Ho'o
@keys2: Flame-Melding Ritual Grounds, Fifth Lord
@version: 5.5
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
Found in the lower levels after destroying pulverite and defeating a furnace-shell mountain weasel, this inscription holds the last words of the Fifth Lord, Aj K'astajibal Cha'ama' Ho'o. He rages at humanity's crude misuse of phlogiston, laments that his proposal was rejected by "traitors", taunts humans to sing while they can before the Lord of Light and Heat burns them all, and breaks off addressing Xiuhcoatl: "you alone must not...".

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the grounds are unsealed and unchanged; no later story use is recorded.
