## summary
### LITE
Aino's House is the home of the child inventor Aino and her robot Ineffa, set inside the Clink-Clank Krumkake Craftshop on Lempo Isle in Nod-Krai. It is an eclectic, colorful building made of scrap metal and creativity.
### STANDARD +
The in-game map describes it as the place where parts, gears and half-finished contraptions fill the space and the "Whiz-Kid Mechanic's wild ideas take root". Each odd invention sparkles with inspiration, so that an ordinary dwelling becomes a private gallery of marvels and mess.

## places
### STANDARD
The house is an instance entered from the craftshop. The wiki shows Aino's bedroom as one interior location; her bedroom holds a cabinet, and her character story says she keeps the first robot she ever built there.
### LARGE +
That robot has no intelligence module, only jingles when shaken, and was meant purely as company for a girl who had said too many goodbyes. It is now rusted and falling apart, but Aino keeps it as family, and Ineffa learned its history while helping to tidy the workshop.
### ULTRA +
Ineffa's own analysis found the corrosion had spread across its joints and that even careful polishing could no longer restore it. Aino asked Ineffa to help her move it to the cabinet, and the story says it is still family and still part of her home, presenting this as the machine's remaining purpose: to be there for her.

## people
### LITE
Aino and Ineffa live here.
### STANDARD
Aino, a young orphan prodigy, treats her creations and screws as family, and Ineffa is her robot helper. Visitors are not guaranteed a welcome: the craftshop's door is said to slam in the face of people who do not appreciate Aino's inventions.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Messy, warm and busy, with the scent of machine oil, metal and krumkakes drifting through the craftshop. Half-built gadgets sit on every surface and nothing is quite finished, which suits the place.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 it is simply Aino and Ineffa's home; the wiki records no change.
