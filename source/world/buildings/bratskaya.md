## summary
### LITE
"Bratskaya" is a small general-goods shop in Zolnyy, the Ash District of Snezhnograd, in Snezhnaya. Its owner, Grigory, sells cooking ingredients and treats his regulars as neighbours rather than just customers.
### STANDARD +
The shop is open at all hours and its stock is restocked daily. Its customers are mostly nearby residents of Zolnyy. Next door in the same district stands the beverage shop Kvasnik.
## people
### STANDARD
Grigory is the owner. In his shop dialogue he says a purchase is final once the customer leaves the counter and that in principle he does not sell on credit, "in principle" being the operative words. He frames credit as a favour for struggling villagers rather than a service for travellers who can afford to pay.
### LARGE +
Grigory is on friendly, familiar terms with Alyosha, the Snezhnayan hunter. He packs jerky, flatbread and fruit for Alyosha's trips and says he often relies on Alyosha's help while out making deliveries. Alyosha answers formally; Grigory teases him for it and asks him to turn back if a hunt turns dangerous. Alyosha's companion Tugarin is mentioned as always knowing the quickest way out.
## culture
### STANDARD
Grigory says credit has become more common lately and that "around these parts" misfortune is always waiting round the corner, so the shop acts partly as a safety net for the people who live nearby. The sign over the door is written in Snezhnayan script and, per the wiki, reads "Berezka".
## atmosphere
### STANDARD
A cramped neighbourhood shop in an industrial district: shelves of staples, a cheerful but practical owner who jokes with customers, and the cold outside never far from the conversation.
## scene_use
### ULTRA
Suits a low-stakes scene: restocking before a trek, hearing neighbourhood talk about Zolnyy, or seeing how ordinary Snezhnograd residents get by through informal credit. Keep Grigory warm and practical; do not give him an invented backstory.
## gameplay
### STANDARD
Game mechanics: Grigory's stock of cooking ingredients refreshes daily. With Alyosha in the party, a first conversation gives a small gift of Golden Ferns and Rimecurrants.
