## summary
### LITE
The Golden House is Liyue's mint, and the only mint in Teyvat. Every Mora coin in circulation was struck here. It stands on Mt. Tianheng above Liyue Harbor. The Liyue Qixing run it, a steward manages it, and Millelith soldiers guard it. Minting needed the Geo Archon's power, so the mint has stood idle since Rex Lapis "died" and gave up his Gnosis. The Mora that already exists stays in circulation.
### STANDARD +
During the Liyue Archon Quest the Qixing hid Rex Lapis' body, the Exuvia, inside the Golden House. The Fatui Harbinger Childe broke in to take the Gnosis from it, fought the Traveler there and found the body empty.

## places
### STANDARD
Inside, the building is even more lavish than its exterior and holds great piles of Mora. It has at least two levels: during the duel in Heart of Glaze, Childe smashed through the floor and the fight continued on the level below. The Exuvia was laid out in the hall where the duel began.
### LARGE +
Guards stand at the entrance and turn away anyone without business there. To the steward, the vaults are "the source of all wealth" and the lifeblood of the economy. Locals call it simply the Liyue Mint, or even the Teyvat Mint.

## people
### STANDARD
Gongming is the steward. She is proud and wary, insists visitors state their business, and calls the place one of paramount importance to Liyue. The guards are Millelith soldiers, among them Youxu, Zexuan, Zhe'yuan and Zheng.
### LARGE +
Youxu, younger brother of the Millelith instructor Fengyan, is eager for a chance to prove himself. After the break-in he is bitter that his unit had been redeployed into the city when the adepti came, so he missed the one real incident on his watch. Zexuan transferred to the post because he loved the clatter of coins being struck. He now misses that sound, although mountains of Mora still sit inside.

## governance
### STANDARD
The mint belongs to the Liyue Qixing's administration. Before the Archon Quest, Gongming noticed that some of her recent orders from the Qixing went far beyond her usual authority. They were part of the measures the Qixing took after Rex Lapis' apparent death: the Exuvia was moved here, and Childe later said the Qixing had stopped the mints.

## history
@spoiler: major
### LITE
Mora is named after its creator, Morax (Rex Lapis), and was minted here with his power. In the Archon Quest, after the Exuvia was moved here, Childe broke in for the Geo Gnosis and lost his duel with the Traveler. Zhongli, really Rex Lapis, later handed the Gnosis to the Fatui. As he explained, the Golden House must now stop working for a long time.
### STANDARD
Mora is named after its creator, Morax, known in Liyue as Rex Lapis, and the Golden House struck it with his power. After his body fell at the Rite of Descension, the Qixing secretly moved the Exuvia here. Using what he learned from the preparations for the Rite of Parting, Childe found the hiding place. He knocked out the Millelith guards and was waiting by the body when the Traveler and Paimon arrived. Childe fought the Traveler, reached into the Exuvia and found no Gnosis in it. He assumed the Traveler had taken it, used his Foul Legacy transformation and smashed the fight down to the lower floor. He lost, realised the Exuvia had been a decoy, and left to carry out his backup plan: releasing Osial. Afterwards Zhongli revealed himself as the Geo Archon and gave the Gnosis to Signora for the Tsaritsa. At the Rite of Parting he told Paimon that existing Mora would not vanish, but the Golden House would have to cease operations for a long time.
### LARGE +
Earlier in the same act, a coded notebook in the Jade Chamber, written as a book cipher keyed to Customs of Liyue, spelled out that the Fatui would strike the Golden House. After the break-in Gongming took inventory and found not a single Mora missing, which baffled her. Childe also used his Electro Delusion during the duel before the Foul Legacy transformation.
### ULTRA +
Childe accused the Traveler of either reaching the Gnosis first or deliberately leaking the Golden House's location to him. He later admitted they could have had no connection to the Gnosis and that Signora's report on them had underrated them.

## economy
### STANDARD
In-world, Mora is the currency accepted throughout Teyvat. It is also a catalyst for physical transformation, which is why crafting and forging consume it. Since the Golden House was Teyvat's sole mint, its closure means no new coin enters circulation. Zhongli called this a serious financial problem and, characteristically, left it for the Qixing to debate.
### ULTRA +
Neuvillette once called Mora the "flesh and blood" of Morax. In Snezhnaya (7.1), Paimon asks whether Northland Bank or the Golden House holds more Mora. Vesna answers that she has never been to the Golden House, and adds that Northland Bank safeguards many other valuable assets besides Mora.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
The building is hushed and gleaming, full of gold and heaps of coin. Its guards are visibly anxious and admit they are on edge. Since the shutdown, the clatter of minting has stopped and only the stockpiles and sentries remain. One guard calls it, without exaggeration, the most important place in Liyue.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the Golden House still stands under guard but does not mint Mora.
### STANDARD +
The Geo Gnosis went to the Tsaritsa through Signora, and no canon source up to 7.1 shows minting resuming or another way of minting being found.

## entry: The Duel in the Golden House
@keys: Heart of Glaze, Enter the Golden House, Memories: The Golden Shadow
@keys2: Childe, Tartaglia, Exuvia, Golden House
@version: 1.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Heart of Glaze is part 4 of the Archon Quest act A New Star Approaches (Chapter I, Act III). With Liyue Harbor in turmoil between the Qixing and the adepti, the Traveler realised that Childe had gone to the Golden House after the Exuvia. Inside, the Millelith guards lay unconscious and Childe was waiting by the body. He fought the Traveler for the pleasure of the fight. After reaching into the Exuvia and finding no Gnosis, he unleashed his Foul Legacy transformation, then lost and released the sealed god Osial on Liyue Harbor with mass-produced Sigils of Permission.
### LARGE +
The Trounce Domain "Enter the Golden House" frames a rematch with Childe as the Traveler's memory of this duel ("Memories: The Golden Shadow"). It is a combat replay, not a new canon event.
