## summary
### LITE
Citlali's Home is the residence of Citlali, the great shaman of the Masters of the Night-Wind, in the Tezcatepetonco Range. It stands far from the tribal settlement and is hard to miss: its outer walls are covered in graffiti. Inside it is a mess of wine bottles, snacks and shelves crammed with light novels.
### STANDARD +
The Traveler first enters in The Truth of the Battle of Seven Colors and gains permanent access after receiving Citlali's Handkerchief and Key in The Color of Memory. Citlali gave the key so that the Traveler wouldn't wake her by banging on the door while she was drunk.

## places
### STANDARD
The home has an exterior, an interior instance, a bedroom, a table and a bookshelf. After The Color of Memory the bedroom door carries the faint sound of Citlali snoring, and the table and shelves hold books and the Traveler's note.
### LARGE +
The shelves are packed with light novels, and the whole home is described as a hermit's nest rather than a witch's lair. A concealed compartment in the bookshelf, protected by several layers of mystical seals, holds an obscure Inazuman light novel, New Chronicles of the Six Kitsune.
### ULTRA +
The light novel Citlali discusses most is "Mirage Warriors", perhaps the first she ever read; it is said to have changed authors three times without ever being finished, and most of its original readers are dead, with Citlali the last. Nobody can say which book is her true favourite.

## people
### STANDARD
Only Citlali lives here. She has lived here as a recluse, reluctant to go out; Character Stories note that after she reached seventy she grew unwilling to leave her home and attended only to matters that demanded her involvement. For her birthday celebration, the tribe lured her out of her home with a knock at the door.
### LARGE +
The graffiti was a deliberate tradition: another great shaman, Huitzilin, had her students plaster Citlali's walls with taunts so that Citlali would be the stern opponent that made them improve. After Huitzilin died, her disciples and their disciples kept it up, claiming it was her dying wish. A thief once broke in claiming to have found the concealed compartment; Citlali thrashed him, handed him to the tribal guards and added another layer of defences, without denying that the compartment existed.
### ULTRA +
Mavuika remarks in the Character Stories that after two hundred years Citlali is as unenthused about going outside as ever, and that it might take a figure more fascinating than light novels to draw the genius shaman toward the real world.

## history
### STANDARD
The home is the target of a Masters of the Night-Wind tradition: shamans have their students plaster its walls with taunts, as Huitzilin once did, so that Citlali becomes the formidable opponent who pushes them to improve.
### LARGE +
The tribe held a birthday celebration for Citlali by luring her out with a knock at the door and unveiling a cake and gifts when she stepped outside.

## quests
@version: 5.3
### STANDARD
In the Tribal Chronicles quest series Memories Flowing With Color, the interior is first accessed during The Truth of the Battle of Seven Colors. In The Color of Memory the Traveler receives the handkerchief and key. If the Traveler has the limited-deluxe "Pretty Please, Kitsune Guuji?" and the first-edition "Shogun Almighty: Reborn as Raiden With Unlimited Power", they can be left on the table with a note from the Traveler and Paimon saying they were found while she slept.
### LARGE +
After one daily reset the light novel sits on the table in pristine condition, the area around it wiped clean, as if its new owner cannot bring herself to read it and prefers to admire it. After a second reset two light novels are on the bookshelf, which has been tidied especially for them.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Cluttered with wine bottles and snacks, with snoring behind the bedroom door and obvious pride in a few treasured books.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 Citlali's Home is unchanged: a permanent instanced interior, with no later story role recorded.

## entry: Graffiti Tradition
@keys: graffiti, Huitzilin
@keys2: Citlali, Masters of the Night-Wind
### STANDARD
Among the Masters of the Night-Wind, a great shaman sometimes chooses a strong peer as an opponent for her students. Huitzilin chose Citlali and had students cover her home with taunting graffiti as a direct challenge. Citlali answered by humbling each arrogant novice. After Huitzilin's death, disciples continued the practice, claiming it was her dying wish. Most of the tribe accepted this, though it departed somewhat from tradition.
