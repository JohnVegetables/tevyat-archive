## summary
### LITE
The Clink-Clank Krumkake Craftshop is a huge, rusty, scrap-metal workshop on the outskirts of Nasha Town, on Lempo Isle in Nod-Krai. Aino, a child prodigy, started it to build the world's best krumkake machine, and it became famous as a machine workshop through her many inventions. She lives there with her robot Ineffa, and the air smells of machine oil, metal and krumkakes.
### STANDARD +
The name is a pun on the sound of the place (clink, clank) and on krumkake, a Norwegian rolled cookie of flour, butter, eggs, sugar and cream. Locals know the building as a place where odd contraptions are born, from flower-shooting guns to floor sweepers that behave like puppies, according to Aino's character story.

## geography
### STANDARD
The craftshop stands outside Nasha Town on Lempo Isle, a short walk from the port town. It is a single enormous weather-beaten structure that Aino's story compares to a mechanical beast sprawled along quiet ridges, with rusty mechanical arms that sway in the wind. A Meeting Point of the same name sits near its centre, and Aino's own home is inside it (see Aino's House).
### LARGE +
A local specialty, the Portable Bearing, can be found on the isle. Only a few residents are named at the shop: Bozena, Lalli and Zaitseva, plus Chard Lombro who appears once the Traveler has helped with the world quest Problem-Sorting Robot.

## people
### LITE
Aino, the founder, and Ineffa, the all-purpose household robot she built, live and work here.
### STANDARD
Aino is an orphan who treats her machines and screws as her family; she is a young child, and the craftshop is both her laboratory and her home. Ineffa, a tall robot built here, helps with housework and makes the krumkakes. Members of the Conch Gang, the children raised at Speranza, helped Aino find the building and move in. NPCs at the shop include Bozena, Lalli, Zaitseva and Chard Lombro.
### LARGE +
Aino's official description warns that anyone who fails to appreciate her creations will find the craftshop's door slammed in their face, so a visitor should praise her designs before making requests. Ineffa's character story says word spread through Nasha Town that the craftshop had made a robot, and rumors of impossible powers followed.
### ULTRA +
Before the craftshop, Aino's closest friend was Lyulka, the daughter of the owner of the Nuts and Bolts shop, whom she met through the clinking sounds of the Rossum Workshop in Nasha Town. Aino once spotted a fix that raised a machine's kuuvahki efficiency by exactly 2.8 percent, which convinced Lyulka that she should study. Ineffa's story calls the finished workshop a patchwork paradise cobbled together by a lonely child.

## history
### STANDARD
Aino was taken in as one of the children under Katya at Speranza and learned mechanics from a Snezhnayan mechanic called Papa Wrench before he left with his merchant convoy. At dawn one day, carrying a small robot and followed by a wheeled toolbox, she was led by Conch Gang members to the abandoned structure and named it the Clink-Clank Krumkake Craftshop, vowing to invent a machine that makes the most delicious krumkakes. The shop's own loading-screen text says it began as her little workspace and became a renowned machine workshop after her many intricate inventions and Ineffa's birth.
### LARGE +
Katya later saw Aino come to Speranza holding hands with Ineffa to buy sweets, and noticed the once-gaunt girl had grown healthy and bright. Ineffa's story adds that Aino often worked late, falling asleep beside her unfinished creation while building Ineffa. The craftshop also keeps an old rusted robot, the first one Aino ever assembled, which is kept in a cabinet in her bedroom.

## quests
### STANDARD
In the Archon Quest chapter Song of the Welkin Moon, Aino and Ineffa are part of the Traveler's circle in Nasha Town. Aino's self-built cannon in town is mentioned in Rest Beyond Reach, where the pair are also optional conversation partners. In Welkin Moon's Homecoming, Aino mentions the shop's habit of using eggs and heating tubes, and Flins describes the gathering as including "the adorable Krumkake Craftshop". When the group goes to confront Dottore in the far north, Aino and Ineffa stay behind with Varka and Jahoda to guard Nasha Town.
## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Clanking, hissing and rattling day and night, with the warm smell of baking krumkakes mixed into machine oil. It is cluttered and cheerful: parts, gears and half-finished contraptions everywhere, run by a cheerful child inventor and a precise, literal-minded robot.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the craftshop is open and in use as Aino's workshop and home. The wiki records no change to it after its introduction.

### STANDARD
The Flagship Event Clink Clank Clash (Version 6.0, September 2025) begins with the Traveler and Paimon finding that Ineffa and Aino have been dragged into a curious crisis. Its games involve chasing rogue recon bots along the Moonlanes, testing a self-defense device, finding malfunctioning robots and sneaking a stealth robot past guards. It was a time-limited event; it is not a standing feature of the shop.

## entry: Aino's Krumkake Creed
@keys: Krumkake Craftshop, Clink-Clank Krumkake
@keys2: Aino, Ineffa
### STANDARD
Aino's stated goal for the shop is to invent the best krumkake machine in the world, and her creed is to build interesting machines for fun and enjoy krumkakes in between. Ineffa makes the actual krumkakes. Asked how to befriend Aino, Ineffa answers: say no to savory krumkakes, admire Aino's inventions and play with Aino often.

## entry: Papa Wrench
@keys: Papa Wrench

### STANDARD
Papa Wrench is the Snezhnayan mechanic who taught Aino at Speranza. When he left with his merchant convoy, he put a screw in her hand and told her not to underestimate her talent or put down her tools. His full background is not given.
