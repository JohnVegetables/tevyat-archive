## summary
### LITE
Razan Garden is a serene garden inside the Akademiya in Sumeru City, along the path up the Divine Tree. It is reached by the right or left doors inside the Akademiya and is dotted with gazebos and decorative padisarah plants. Researchers and students come here to read, talk or think, and an Akademiya public message board stands in the garden.
### STANDARD +
The people here are mostly scholars: Darya, Farghani, Homayun, Makhfi, Muganna and Qushji are listed as researchers, and Arina and Armin as students.

## people
### STANDARD
Muganna is a gloomy researcher who calls the garden the place where silence will ultimately reign and thinks that everyone dies, gods included. Homayun, a researcher, worries about pollution in the rainforest and mentions requesting earlier research from the House of Daena; he hopes to think uninterrupted here. The others are ambient residents without individual stories.
### LARGE +
Homayun's lines change after the Archon Quest chapter that reveals the fate of the Dendro Archon: before it he speaks of honouring Greater Lord Rukkhadevata by caring for the rainforest, afterwards of the Dendro Archon in the same way. During the Akademiya Extravaganza event he grumbles about the commotion.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Still and green, a pocket of calm within the Akademiya's grand architecture, where the occasional passer-by is a scholar lost in thought rather than a tourist.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 Razan Garden is unchanged.
### ULTRA +
No quest in canon through 7.1 is set here; it is an ambient location.
