## summary
### LITE
Pervases' Temple is a small temple in Lisha dedicated to Pervases, a Yaksha who died in the Archon War, and holds a statue of him. It began as a run-down house, then was restored through the Hereafter quests. Wang Ping'an, once the false adeptus Starsnatcher, now keeps it and gives out incense for visitors to offer to the statue.
### STANDARD +
Pervases was a junior Yaksha who served Rex Lapis and perished during the Archon War. The temple is where his spirit lingered in a crane statue, and where Xiao is moved by the loss of his fellow Yakshas.

## places
### STANDARD
Before restoration the temple looks like a dilapidated house in poor repair, with Treasure Hoarders nearby. After the quests it is repaired and tidy, with a censer and a statue of Pervases. The statue shows an illuminated beast form, a crane wearing a large-bead necklace like the one Xiao wears.
### LARGE +
After the restoration Wang Ping'an stands by the temple and gives out Fine Incense to anyone who asks. Offering it to the statue is an interaction in the game. During the limited-time event Waterborne Poetry (4.1), Xiao composed a poem about the Yaksha and left it on the table inside.
### ULTRA +
The Ministry of Civil Affairs posted Millelith to guard the temple while it was being rebuilt, since Wang Ping'an feared that Treasure Hoarders might raid the material deliveries.

## people
### STANDARD
Wang Ping'an looks after the temple, hands out incense and says few people come, that the temple is off the beaten track and he wonders how to let people know it is there. He is writing a book about Xiao, the Vigilant Yaksha, and collects evidence for the stories.
### LARGE +
Xiao is connected to the temple through his fellow Yaksha. Pervases is described as close to Bosacius, the Prime Yaksha, and Xiao regrets that Pervases was one of many Yakshas who paid the ultimate price.

## history
@version: 1.0
### LITE
A thousand years after Pervases died, on the anniversary of his death, the Traveler and Paimon meet his spirit at the run-down temple while searching for a censer and seven-star lamps so that Xiao can teach them the Dream Trawler ritual to punish the false adeptus Starsnatcher. Pervases gives the items up, ending his own ritual. Starsnatcher is later redeemed as Wang Ping'an and restores the temple.
### STANDARD
In Xiao's story quest Butterfly's Dream, Pervases performs the Mortal Lingering ritual with a censer and seven-star lamps to see modern Liyue, then permits the Traveler to take them, knowing that dispels his ritual. He senses the karmic burden Xiao bears, tells them the truth about himself and leaves a message for Xiao before going dormant. Starsnatcher, now Wang Ping'an, repents, repays his followers and later sets out to restore the temple. In Hereafter the Traveler buys grilled tiger fish near Wanmin Restaurant and brings a serving for Pervases. They find Wang Ping'an attacked by hilichurls and Treasure Hoarders and help him; across the three quests of the series the temple is rebuilt, and he stays on as its keeper. After Rex Lapis saves Xiao in The Chasm, Xiao returns to the temple to think about the fate of the Yakshas.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
A small, quiet shrine off the beaten track with incense burning before a crane statue; a place for remembering those who died in the war without recognition.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the temple stands restored in Lisha under Wang Ping'an's care.
### STANDARD +
No later canon changes it.

## entry: Pervases
@keys: Pervases
@keys2: Yaksha, temple, Xiao
@version: 1.0
### STANDARD
Pervases was a junior Yaksha under Rex Lapis, who gave him the name. He died during the Archon War and a thousand years later, on the anniversary, performed the Mortal Lingering ritual to see modern Liyue. His favourite food was grilled fish, and he is shown as a man with short slicked-back black hair or, in statue form, a crane. His spirit lies dormant in the statue at the temple.

## entry: Wang Ping'an and Starsnatcher
@keys: Wang Ping'an, Starsnatcher
@keys2: Pervases' Temple, temple, Xiao
@version: 1.0
### STANDARD
Wang Ping'an posed as the adeptus Starsnatcher, using a Sigil of Permission and an ornate mask, until Xiao's story quest exposed him as a fake. He repented, repaid his followers and, in Hereafter, rebuilt Pervases' temple. He remains there as its keeper and is writing a book on Xiao.
