## summary
### LITE
The Citadel of Regzar is the headquarters of the Corps of Thirty, the mercenary brigade that keeps the peace in Sumeru City for the Akademiya. It stands in the middle of the city, near Treasures Street. The name is a Persian word for desert.
### STANDARD +
The Corps began as thirty Eremite mercenaries hired by the sages; the barracks here fly the Eremite crest and colours on their banners, while members wear teal-green scarves with a petal-like emblem.

## people
### STANDARD
Rukh Shah, the Parchamdar, commands the Corps and is the youngest ever to hold the post. Asfand is the Consultant (the administrative leader). Others here are the researcher Effendi and the members Sekkura (called "Swallow"), Udad ("Goat") and Yunus ("Bigfish").
### LARGE +
The Corps is the oldest of the Eremite brigades and coordinates the others, though its relations with other Eremites are tenuous, and Rukh Shah warns the Traveler about Eremites elsewhere. In the quest "After Repose" Collei and Asfand can be found at the Citadel.

## governance
### STANDARD
Not a formal army but a permanently hired mercenary brigade working for the Akademiya; the Parchamdar is military commander and the Consultant handles administration.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Disciplined but not grim, a barracks of hired soldiers in the middle of a scholarly city.

## status
### STANDARD
As of 7.1 it remains the Corps' base.

## trivia
### ULTRA
The Corps takes its name from its first thirty members and kept it as it grew. Alhaitham notes its poor relations with the Ayn Al-Ahmar, devout followers of King Deshret. The Chinese name of the building can be glossed as Sand-Gathering Hall.
