## summary
### LITE
Mondstadt General Goods is a small shop in the market district of Mondstadt City, run by Blanche. It sells cooking ingredients and is open at all hours. An order board stands beside it.
### STANDARD +
Blanche says that despite its size the shop has the largest catalogue and the fastest shipping in Mondstadt, because it keeps a warehouse in Liyue Harbor.

## places
### STANDARD
The shop itself, and the Mondstadt General Goods Order Board next to it, where customers pin slips with the wares they want and wait for a clerk's reply.
### LARGE +
Stock is basic: salt, pepper, onion, milk, tomato, cabbage, potato and wheat, refreshed daily.

## people
### STANDARD
Blanche is the shopkeeper. She greets customers with quality goods at honest prices and has her own Genius Invokation TCG challenge for players.
### LARGE +
When asked about Mondstadt she says it is known for wine and that most people head to a tavern after work.
### ULTRA +
She is described as having short brown hair and brown eyes.

## culture
### STANDARD
The order board replies politely in a formal shop voice: requests that cannot be met get a standard apology. It refuses an order for a deliberately revolting dish, since the shop will not demean Mondstadt's hospitality, and cannot supply hair-loss medicine or books not yet published.
### LARGE +
Other replies include sending someone who wants Chicken-Mushroom Skewers to Good Hunter across the street, a note that a rare history book is hard to find, and a stern reply to a customer who wondered whether the shop inspired the magical shop in a storybook.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Brisk and businesslike, with an eager shopkeeper and a board full of slightly absurd customer requests.

## travel
### STANDARD
Market district of Mondstadt City, across from Good Hunter.
