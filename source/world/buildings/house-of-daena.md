## summary
### LITE
The House of Daena is the great library at the centre of the Sumeru Akademiya in Sumeru City, the largest library in all of Teyvat. It was once reckoned the repository of all knowledge in the world because of its unmatched collection, but the Akasha System has since taken that role. It still stands as a symbol of Sumeru, the Nation of Wisdom.
### STANDARD +
Officially only students and teachers of the Akademiya may borrow its books. It is run by a Grand Conservator, who is responsible for acquiring new material and maintaining the existing collection.

## places
### STANDARD
An elevated platform in the middle of the library carries a lift. After the events of the Archon Quest "Jnagarbha Day" it can rise to the Grand Sage's office or descend to the confinement room.
### LARGE +
Books on its shelves include the Scroll of Streaming Song, The Folio of Foliage and The Tale of Shiruyeh and Shirin, plus quest volumes such as An Ode to Khvarena, The Lay of Al-Ahmar and The Shepherd and the Magic Bottle.

## people
### STANDARD
The librarian Katayoun and the matra Shohre work here, with students and researchers such as Hunayn, Sanad, Gregor, Jafar and Nargess. After the Archon Quest the mahamata Panah is found here.
### LARGE +
The next Grand Conservator is expected to be Vikram or Rifaet. The character Shatir calls Lisa "Mondstadt's Grand Conservator" and says she would have been his first pick for the Sumeru post had she stayed.

## governance
### STANDARD
Run as part of the Akademiya under a Grand Conservator; only its students and teachers may borrow books.

## history
### STANDARD
Once the world's bank of knowledge, it was superseded by the Akasha System, but the old library remains in use.
### LARGE +
In the Archon Quest the Traveler and Alhaitham enter it during the Akademiya's Jnagarbha Day, and the Grand Sage Azar intercepts them there and has the Traveler detained. Later Alhaitham is found in the library arguing with Kaveh, who has returned from the desert to discover that the sages have been overthrown.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Vast, hushed and scholarly, an old stack of shelves and reading rooms where researchers work by day and night; a symbol of Sumeru's reverence for learning.

## quests
### LARGE
Appears in the Archon Quest chapter "Akasha Pulses, the Kalpa Flame Rises" (Jnagarbha Day and its aftermath). These events are a major spoiler for characters who have not witnessed them.

## status
### STANDARD
As of 7.1 the library stands as it did at the close of the 3.0 Archon Quest; no later change is recorded in the pages consulted.
