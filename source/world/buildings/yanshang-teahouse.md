## summary
### LITE
Yanshang Teahouse looks like a teahouse in Liyue Harbor but is actually a casino. It sells high-quality tea and 'other goods'. It first served only wealthy patrons and required a deposit to enter. Tartaglia exposed the original owner, and Yelan now runs it as a secret base.
### STANDARD +
Under Yelan its rules are relaxed. Chuyi, the hostess, was unfriendly to ordinary visitors and drops that manner afterward.

## people
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Chuyi is the hostess. Two guards, called the Halberd and the Fang, stand at the door. Guanhai plays cards inside at night. Yelan manages the house and is said to be notorious among its patrons for her gambling skill, with people warned not to play against her.
### LARGE +
In the Chinese version the two guards say only a single syllable each, which the wiki reads as a nod to the gate-god generals Heng and Ha.

## history
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Shortly before the Traveler arrived in Liyue, Tartaglia and Javert confronted the owner over a debt to Northland Bank. After incapacitating those inside, Tartaglia took the owner's Sigil of Permission for himself and exposed his fraud, and the owner was imprisoned.
### LARGE +
After Osial's defeat, Yelan took over the teahouse as a secret base.
### ULTRA +
Chuyi appears as a silhouette in the Character Tale about the confrontation, discussing the teahouse's finances with its owner.

## culture
### STANDARD
The teahouse trades on an air of exclusivity. Its original policy kept ordinary people out, and Chuyi's rudeness helped.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
A teahouse on the surface, with cards played behind it.

## travel
### STANDARD
On a street above Chihu Rock in Liyue Harbor, with a cliff behind the building.
### ULTRA +
The name may mean 'on a cliff' (there is a cliff behind the shop) or 'above the Rock', per the wiki.

## status

A casino under Yelan's management, as of 7.1.
