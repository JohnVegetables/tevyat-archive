## summary
### LITE
Pardis Dhyai is a research site for Amurta scholars, set in a flowering garden in the Ashavan Realm of Sumeru. Its official description calls it the garden of deep thought, a paradise for people of goodwill. Tighnari is met here, and it is a gathering point for the Traveler's allies.
### STANDARD +
Its name roughly means garden of meditation: pardis from the Persian word for a garden (related to the word paradise) and dhyai probably from a Sanskrit root meaning to contemplate.

## places
### STANDARD
A garden of countless flowers with a greenhouse; the plant Padisarah grows here. Karkata, a mechanical being, can be found in the greenhouse by day as a small cube, and wandering outside at night once Tighnari's story quest "The Unanswerable Problems" is complete.

## people
### STANDARD
Researchers and students include Hazm, Isami, Kemia, Ashshab, Dana and Jaleh, with the forest ranger Izad, the merchant Furat and the Eremite mercenaries Safiyya and Shahin. Tighnari is met here during the Archon Quest.
### LARGE +
Karkata was built by the researcher Abattouy, who left the Akademiya after it banned the study of mechanical beings. After he died, Karkata kept trying to repair him with parts taken from passing convoys, which is part of what the Traveler and Tighnari untangle in "The Unanswerable Problems".

## quests
### STANDARD
In the Archon Quest "As by a God's Side" the Traveler finds Tighnari here, a Fatui attack on the site is repelled, and the allies settle the plan for the evening. "Garden Memories", the last part of Tighnari's first story act, ends with a meeting here.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Quiet and green, a place of scholarly calm in contrast to the market bustle of Sumeru City.

## status
### STANDARD
As of 7.1 it remains a research garden; no later events at it are recorded in the pages consulted.

### LARGE
During the limited-time flagship event "Chromatic Ode of Candies and Roses" (5.1), friends gather at Pardis Dhyai to prepare Nilou's birthday celebration, and in the part "Give Her Memories" the group meets there again before Nilou is found. The event is no longer available, so most characters will not know of it.

## trivia
### ULTRA
The map entry for Pardis Dhyai doubles as a viewpoint called The Garden of Deep Thought, and the wiki notes that Karkata's name comes from the Sanskrit word for crab. In the Archon Quest, after tracking the Doctor with Dehya and following Fatui soldiers to Port Ormos, the Traveler hurries back and defeats attacking Fatui here.
