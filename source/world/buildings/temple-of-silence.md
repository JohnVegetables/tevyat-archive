## summary
### LITE
The Temple of Silence is the underground base of the organisation of the same name, in the Land of Lower Setekh near Thamud Oasis. Its map description says it keeps silent vigil over all that is ancient. It guards knowledge that survives from the age of King Deshret, and it is now led by Sethos, with Cyno as its de jure leader.
### STANDARD +
Players unlock it in Cyno's story quest Oathkeeper, in the part "Footprints Sunken Beneath the Sands", after which its entrance reveals itself when approached. It has a square and an inner temple; the Akademiya's office of the same name is only a front.

## places
### STANDARD
An entrance leads into a square and then, through an inner door, the temple itself. Inside are a great hall, a ceremonial hall, an east hall, the room of its former head Bamoun, a statue of Hermanubis and a mural of the Bennu.

## people
### STANDARD
Members shown here include Asenath, Betresa, Charmian, Djer, Intef and Khensa. Sethos leads it; Bamoun, its former head, has died.
### LARGE +
Duaenre also appears among its members in the quest "Sage of Sages". Cyrus, a former member, defected from it.

## governance
### STANDARD
Tradition says the Temple follows the person who holds the most Ba Fragments (pieces of Hermanubis' spirit). After the duel in Oathkeeper Cyno held both, so he is formally leader, but as General Mahamatra he handed the running of the Temple to Sethos, who pledged to open its knowledge to those he and Lesser Lord Kusanali judge worthy.

## history
### STANDARD
Hermanubis and his followers founded the Temple after King Deshret's death and settled in Tulaytullah as keepers of Deshret's surviving knowledge. When the king of Gurabad besieged the city and Tulaytullah's rulers betrayed the Temple, Hermanubis shattered his spirit into Ba Fragments and gave them to his followers, who drove the army off but were then shunned. They left for the rainforest, and about 400 years ago they raised a large underground temple near Thamud Oasis.
### LARGE +
Distrust of the Akademiya's sages led the Temple back to the desert, and the Akademiya hid the break by founding an empty office with the same name. As faith faded without Hermanubis, Bamoun tried to restore the Temple by implanting Ba Fragments in children: his adoptive grandson Sethos and Cyno. In Oathkeeper Bamoun used Cyrus as a bargaining chip; Cyno beat Sethos in a duel, took both fragments, and Bamoun then let himself pass away.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Silent, ancient and solemn, a hushed underground sanctuary of statues, murals and guarded halls.

## dangers
### STANDARD
The Temple's trials are fought against guardians (Grounded Geoshrooms and Hydroshrooms, and Primal Construct Reshapers) during the duel with Sethos.

## quests
### STANDARD
Oathkeeper (Lupus Aureus Chapter Act II): The Mysterious Letter, After Repose, Footprints Sunken Beneath the Sands, Sage of Sages, The Dead Past is the Living Present. These are a major spoiler for Cyno and Sethos.

## status
### STANDARD
As of 7.1 the Temple is active under Sethos and is rebuilding ties with the Akademiya.

### LARGE
The organisation proper is covered by its own faction notes. Key points: it is led by Sethos with Cyno as de jure leader; it was founded by Hermanubis; the Akademiya's office of the same name is a hollow front; and the Akademiya's six cardinal sins were originally proposed by the Temple, though the sages took credit for them.

## trivia
### ULTRA
The wiki notes that a Temple of Silence member once commented on Alhaitham's default outfit, but that member likely belonged to the Akademiya's front office. In the book A Thousand Nights, envoys of the Temple of Silence tell a Vahumana Dastur of his mentor's death, and a rumour about Faruzan's disappearance claims she was held within the Temple. A report in the Valley of Dahri also cites an order from the Temple that Khaenri'ah machines be sealed.
