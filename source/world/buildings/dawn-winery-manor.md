## summary
### LITE
The Dawn Winery Manor is the central house of the Dawn Winery in Mondstadt's Windwail Highland, the Ragnvindr family estate that Diluc now owns. Its housemaids and butler run the winery's day-to-day affairs. Its name is unofficial: the manga calls it Diluc Manor, and the game's own text calls it the Manor of Daybreak.
### STANDARD +
The winery around it is Mondstadt's chief winery and specialises in dandelion wine, and has belonged to the Ragnvindrs for roughly a thousand years.

## people
### LITE
Staff here: the head housemaid Adelinde, the butler Elzer, and the maids Hillie and Moco.
### STANDARD
Adelinde is a polite young housemaid who spends most of her time cleaning rooms; she knows the history between Diluc and Kaeya. Elzer served the winery under Crepus, the man he says was like a father to him, and took on most of the winery's affairs after Crepus died and Diluc left Mondstadt; he is also Executive Chairman of the Mondstadt Wine Guild. Hillie and Moco are maids who trade gossip: in one exchange they share a rumour of a hidden room at the far end of the second-floor corridor, which one of them says she noticed while waxing floors.
### LARGE +
Elzer is among the few who know that Diluc is the rumoured "Darknight Hero".

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
A grand, quiet house surrounded by vineyards, with an owl statue at its door. The inscription on the statue is a prayer to the "watcher of the night" not to forget the splendour of the dawn and not to sway from the path of righteousness. Inside is a strange vase that looks completely out of place.
### LARGE +
Books kept in and around the manor include volumes of A Drunkard's Tale, Ballads of the Squire, Biography of Gunnhildr, Heart of Clear Springs, The Fox in the Dandelion Sea and Vera's Melancholy.

## history
### STANDARD
In the manga, the vase was a gift from Kaeya, which he used to discreetly return Diluc's Vision after Diluc came back to Mondstadt. In the quest "A Child's Secret", Kaeya asks Diluc what happened to the vase and Diluc claims not to remember; Reckless Pallad says he saw one in Diluc's home.

### ULTRA +
The English manga calls the building "Diluc Manor", and the TCG support card for the Dawn Winery calls it the "Manor of Daybreak". A signboard behind the house marks an employee resting area with refreshments, and the surrounding estate is described in its viewpoint text as a place of pilgrimage for all who love the bottle.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the manor remains the winery's home and offices; no change in 6.x or 7.0-7.1 content is recorded on the wiki.
