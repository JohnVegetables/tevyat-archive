## summary
### LITE
A secret underground Fatui base inside Coatepec Mountain in Natlan. The Fatui extract Phlogiston there from captured Saurians, with machinery supplied by the Fontaine Research Institute.
### STANDARD +
It has two map levels, a passage and an experimental site, and a control room guarded by Fatui Skirmishers. Its documents show a bored, grumbling garrison rather than a fanatical one.

## geography
### STANDARD
The base lies underground beneath Coatepec Mountain. Heavy extraction machinery runs on a core that can be shut down by a manual emergency brake, operated from two control consoles on either side, to prevent damage from malfunctions.
### LARGE +
One handwritten note wonders whether the shipped equipment is truly useful and whether it could be modified to run entirely on Natlan's lava.

## people
### STANDARD
The garrison is Fatui. Each day two Fatui Skirmishers (Anemoboxer, Pyroslinger, Electrohammer, Geochanter or Cryogunner types) spawn in the control room. Notes name three soldiers: Vasily, Sidorenko and Nomokonov. The same three names make up the Polychrome Tri-Stars Local Legend, who are met far to the west at Huitztli Hill; the wiki links the names but does not explain the connection. Sidorenko appears to be the diarist who got locked in a cage and loses his Fire-Water to Vasily. Nomokonov is said to have had a technical background before joining the Fatui.
### LARGE +
Vasily is by-the-book and keeps order; Sidorenko is easygoing and fond of Fire-Water, a drink sent from Snezhnaya. A mock written vow in the manual promises never again to lock himself in the cage or smash the lock and force the brake. The handbook also mentions catching small creatures and returning them to a cage; what they are is not stated.

## history
### STANDARD
When the base was built is not stated. A diary says the soldier does not understand why such lengths are needed to capture Saurians from Natlan. Maintenance records show the Fontaine equipment in excellent condition, to the writer's surprise, who had expected cast-off gear.
### ULTRA +
The diary also mentions that "that person from Natlan" brought by their Lord on recent visits seemed suspicious to the writer, though the soldiers trust the Lord's judgement. No name is given in the sources read, so do not attach this to any specific character.

## dangers
### LARGE
Fatui Skirmishers hold the control room. The extraction machinery needs a manual emergency brake because Phlogiston equipment malfunctions can damage the core.

## status
### LITE
Active Fatui site as of 7.1; open to the player as a Point of Interest in Coatepec Mountain.
