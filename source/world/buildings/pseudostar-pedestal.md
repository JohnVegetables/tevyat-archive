## summary
### LITE
A subarea in the Dragons' Residence of the Ancient Sacred Mountain (the Sacred City of Tollan) in Natlan, next to a teleport waypoint.
### STANDARD +
Its notable feature is a Secret Source Dragon Idol Workshop Engraving, a first-person declaration that the wiki attributes to Xiuhcoatl.

## places
### STANDARD
Part of the Dragons' Residence map level. The engraving sits beside the Teleport Waypoint.

## history
@version: 5.5
### STANDARD
The engraving's speaker calls himself the Creator, Shaper and Forger of all names, and promises ruin on the enemies of Tollan, wishing disease and a collapse by the wayside on them. He says he will not be trampled by the usurper of the sky nor consumed by the black tides of the earth, and ties Tollan's glory and the radiance of the primal flame to the survival of his craft.
### LARGE +
The wiki reads the usurper of the sky as the Heavenly Principles and the black tides as the Abyss. Since the engraving gives no names, treat these links as interpretation.

## atmosphere
### ULTRA
An inscription of the dragons' age stands in a ruined corner of the city; this is a scene suggestion, not extra canon.

## status
### LITE
Accessible as of 7.1, the same as the rest of the Ancient Sacred Mountain.
