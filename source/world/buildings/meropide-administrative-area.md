## summary
### LITE
The Administrative Area is the central hub of the Fortress of Meropide, the undersea prison-factory in Fontaine. The Duke's Office, belonging to the Administrator Wriothesley, stands at its centre. The Fortress's teleport waypoint is here, across from the office. Guards, inmates, shopkeepers and the doctor all pass through it.
### STANDARD +
Credit Coupons are the only currency, and the area contains the places where they are spent.

## geography
### STANDARD
It lies below the Dormitory Block and above the Production Zone. The Duke's Office stands in its middle, and a waypoint opens across from it once the Fortress's first quest is under way. A lift from the area leads to the Pankration Ring.
### LARGE +
The Fortress's map levels are the Reception Area, Dormitory Block, Administrative Area, Production Zone (upper and lower) and Abandoned Production Zone. The wiki lists both the Dormitory Block and the Administrative Area as map levels of this page.

## places
### STANDARD
The Duke's Office has three floors: the Duke's own office on top with a desk, sofa and tea table, and a basement with a lift to the Forbidden Zone and a door onto a glass-walled room overlooking the secret ship. The Coupon Cafeteria serves meals and the free daily Welfare Meal. The Rag and Bone Shop is owned by Alvard. The Pankration Ring is the Fortress's entertainment venue.
### LARGE +
Near the waypoint three notices hang on the wall: one offers 10 Coupons for Welfare Meals and fortune slips (apply at Supply Warehouse 2), one calls for people to join in destroying the loudspeaker, and one grumbles about a day wasted and a bowl of Minty Bean Soup. Maupoli's job is tearing down notices like these.
### ULTRA +
Inside the area a blackboard, table and bookshelf change after the quest An Eye for an Eye: before, the blackboard shows basic knowledge that children could understand and the table holds story books, detective novels and papers from the Fontaine Research Institute; afterwards the blackboard shows economic principles and some sociology and the table holds books on economics. A pressed purple flower in the storybook is replaced by a faint scent.

## people
### STANDARD
Named NPCs include Alvard (Rag and Bone Shop owner), Arderne (doctor), Bran (Coupon Cafeteria staff, a clockwork meka), Wolsey (cafeteria supervisor), Monglane (receptionist), guards Camden, Estienne, Galvana, Galvaryet and Goncourt, Millereau (Mutual Aid Network advertiser), and Edwin Eastinghouse, a former senior researcher at the Fontaine Research Institute.
### LARGE +
Others in the area are Nakul (researcher), Nizami (bodyguard), Nacker (former Fontaine Research Institute researcher), Darcy (called 'Dirty Face'), Carranza, Fourmon, Lutz, Meilhat, Ottnit and Rosolino, as well as unnamed convicts. 'Tricky Bulle Fruit', a former Fruity Order operative, and 'Intrepid Valberry' are also listed.

## governance
### STANDARD
Wriothesley governs the Fortress from the central office. The Fortress is formally autonomous and runs on Coupons rather than Mora. The area is where the Coupon economy is felt: the cafeteria and the Rag and Bone Shop are here.

## culture
### LARGE
Notices by the waypoint show daily inmate life: bargains for Welfare Meals, grumbling about food and a call to wreck the loudspeaker. Pankration bouts are held in the Ring, and Millereau advertises the Mutual Aid Network in the area.

## history
### STANDARD
The area is the setting for the Traveler's 45-day sentence in Chapter IV Acts III-IV and the Fortress is also the setting of Wriothesley's story quest (4.1). In Secret Keepers and Forbidden Zones, Wriothesley opens the hidden stair in his office to the Forbidden Zone.

## dangers
### STANDARD
No specific hazards are described here. Guards are posted in the area, and Pankration bouts take place in the Ring.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Industrial and busy, with notices on the walls and a loudspeaker. The Duke's door is often shut tight, yet its map description says he knows everything that happens in the Fortress.

## travel
### STANDARD
Reached through the Fortress's reception, via a waypoint that appears in the first Fortress quest, or by being caught by searchlights outside. It connects downward by lift to the Production Zone.

## status
### LITE
Active as of 7.1, with Wriothesley as Administrator.
