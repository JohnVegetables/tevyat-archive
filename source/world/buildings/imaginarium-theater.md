## summary
### LITE
The Imaginarium Theater is a hidden room entered from Mondstadt's Knights of Favonius library, a stage and lounge where guests perform fantasy plays. It is looked after by Wolfy, a small talking stuffed wolf. It appeared in Version 4.7.
### STANDARD +
Guests must be invited, and can invite friends. The room is described as the place before the story begins and after it ends, so guests have all the time they need.

## places
### STANDARD
The Theater Lobby, formerly called the Mysterious Room, holds a sofa where Wolfy dozes, a painting whose figure moves, a table of fresh desserts, a locked treasure chest, a wardrobe, a curtain and an old desk with a stopped clock behind it. Furnishings are self-serve: guests can change the music or have their fortune told.
### LARGE +
The wiki notes that the Traveler's remarks at the wardrobe and desk echo The Lion, the Witch and the Wardrobe and Goethe's Faust, and that the moving painting features the Boar Princess of the storybook of that name.

## people
### STANDARD
Wolfy is the concierge. He is a small stuffed wolf wearing a golden purple crown and a purple bow tie with two stars. Madame Mage put him in charge, and he waited a long time for guests, falling asleep while he did.
### LARGE +
Madame Mage is an unnamed mage whom Wolfy says can see the path of fate in starry reflections. He says her friends are mages too, each a master of one kind of magic, and one of them writes with a quill and a bottle of ink holding 'fantasy truth'. He says the others turn that into 'true fantasy'.

## culture
### STANDARD
The room runs on performance: Wolfy says the stage becomes a place of magic only when guests believe from the heart that it is real. Toy Medals, which the audience is said to give for a good show, can be exchanged with Wolfy for Thespian Tricks, which are poses for photographs.
### LARGE +
Wolfy likes storybooks and imagines himself as Wolfy the Brave, Wolfy the Wise or the Big Bad Wolfy. He remembers a curse of unmelting ice, playing with White Fang, and a victory parade in which a queen honoured him, and cannot tell these dreams from real memory.
### ULTRA +
He also retells a tale in which the boar tribe of Angels were once wild boars; a master offered them a rusted scale, and those who placed their heads or muscles on it became wolves, lizards, snakes and rabbits. One boar balanced both sides until the scale broke, and spent her voice on it, so she became mute and speaks only in people's heads. Wolfy says she is also a friend of Madame Mage.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay, not story. Unlocked at Adventure Rank 35 after the world quest Unbegun, Unending Story. The player assembles a cast of characters, plays through numbered acts that include boss stages, and earns Fantasia Flowers, Stella and Toy Medals. Each season features three elements, and there are five difficulties up to Lunar Mode.
### LARGE +
Each character has two units of Vigor, spent as stages are cleared. Supporting Cast characters borrowed from friends can be added. Thespian Tricks from past seasons stay on sale after a season ends.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Warm, strange and theatrical: soft light, curtains, a toy host who cheerfully forgets his own name, and the sense that the room sits outside ordinary time.

## travel
### STANDARD
Enter by a door on the lower level of the Knights of Favonius library in Mondstadt City, which does not open until the player has completed the unlocking quest.

## quests
### STANDARD
In the unlocking quest, Unbegun, Unending Story, the Traveler and Paimon enter the room, find the toy and, once an act is performed, meet Wolfy properly. The wiki also notes the Theater Lobby is visited during the quest The Gardener.

## status
### LITE
Open as of 7.1, with new seasons rotating in.

### STANDARD
Wolfy is the concierge of the Imaginarium Theater: a small stuffed wolf with a golden purple crown, purple bow tie and beady black eyes. He woke from a long sleep when the Traveler arrived, at first could not recall his own name, and says Madame Mage made him concierge in a dream of a garden of forking paths. He does not know where the theater is.
