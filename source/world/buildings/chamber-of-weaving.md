## summary
### LITE
The Chamber of Weaving is a minor point of interest within the Masters of the Night-Wind's Sacrificial Altar of Enigmatic Land, in Natlan's Tezcatepetonco Range. Its only listed occupant is the NPC Yolotli, who is affiliated with the Masters of the Night-Wind. Canon says very little about the place itself.
### STANDARD +
Yolotli explains that messages from Mr. Toltec often mean a new commission to investigate the Ley Lines, and that they are not ready to go out again yet.

## places
### STANDARD
It is listed as a named point of interest inside the larger Sacrificial Altar of Enigmatic Land, itself a ceremonial site of the Masters of the Night-Wind. The altar's other listed NPCs include the tribe's chief Biram, the High Priest Caholom, a guard named Cip, the Iktomisaurus elder Tlapo and Toltec.
### LARGE +
The altar's viewpoint is titled "Ceremonial Site Lost in Night's Dim Light".

## people
### STANDARD
Yolotli is the sole occupant. They sound anxious and apologetic, and consider many mystical arts and prepared materials necessary before travelling again. Their own Ley Line perception is mediocre, so they depend on Saurians for that task.
### LARGE +
Yolotli explains that Iktomisaurs will not simply go out "for walkies" with humans. They despise selfish use of their abilities, cannot be bribed, see through deceit, and expect visitors to follow their etiquette and state the purpose of the request first. A gift given with nothing asked in return can also make an Iktomisaur suspicious, depending on how much it trusts the giver. Yolotli says the Iktomisaurs love a certain mushroom and gives the Traveler three Glowing Hornshrooms to try.

## culture
### STANDARD
Yolotli is affiliated with the Masters of the Night-Wind and says there are many mystical arts to learn and materials to prepare first. In Yolotli's words, you cannot just choose any old mystical ability: it has to be the right one for you.
### LARGE +
According to Yolotli, Ley Line investigations are commissioned by message from Toltec, and for Yolotli the work depends on Saurian help because of their mediocre Ley Line perception.

## trivia
### ULTRA
The wiki's etymology note says Yolotli's name derives from the Nahuatl word yollohtli, which means "heart" and "to live". This is a real-world naming note, not an in-world statement.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 no change to the Chamber of Weaving is recorded, and it has no known role in later story content.
