## summary
### LITE
Wikala Funduq lies on the southeastern side of Port Ormos. It is the port's trade hub and, according to the wiki's citation of an NPC, the centre of Sumeru's commerce. The Sumeru Akademiya controls it. Traders, supervisors and Eremite mercenaries pass through.
### STANDARD +
Trade supervisors Dilawar and Gauhar oversee transactions; merchants Afanasi and Tavernier and the Eremite mercenaries Desai and Vanita are also stationed here.

## people
### STANDARD
Dilawar, a trade supervisor, is cynical: he says traders must be ready to end up penniless, as total wealth is fixed and anyone who profits has tricks up their sleeve; he suggests a casual trader try the Kalimi Exchange. Gauhar, also a trade supervisor, is a stickler for rules: only transactions within regulations are allowed, with registration, inspection, approval, contracts and a second approval, and anyone skipping a step takes the consequences. Afanasi and Tavernier are merchants; Desai and Vanita are Eremite mercenaries.
### LARGE +
Dilawar's idle thought is that simple-minded people should not do business; Gauhar wishes everyone followed the rules as he does.

## quests
@version: 3.0
### STANDARD
In Ever So Close (Chapter III Act I), after dealing with Dori, the Traveler and Paimon go to Wikala Funduq to meet Alhaitham. Alhaitham says he chose it because the Akademiya controls the Wikala and has people working there, and he came to Port Ormos under the pretence of official business; he says no one here is interested in what they say and that the matra will not come. He tests the Traveler with Canned Knowledge in the wilds and returns here to wait for their answer about Dori's information.
### LARGE +
Alhaitham's reasons for the choice are his own: the Akademiya presence gives him cover for official business in Port Ormos.
