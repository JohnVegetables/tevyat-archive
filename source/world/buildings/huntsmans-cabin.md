## summary
### LITE
The Huntsman's Cabin is a cabin in the Volkodlak Tundra, Snezhnaya, where the hunter Alyosha's Meeting Point sits in a workshop beneath it. A fae gang once held it while guarding a prototype Kresnik's Torch and Shadow Realm antiques; after Alyosha and the Traveler clear it out, the torch warms a shelter for villagers who risk freezing.
### STANDARD +
In public Chaplina is credited with founding the shelter, while Alyosha keeps a low profile as a member of the Rokot.

## geography
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
The cabin stands on the snowy tundra. Behind a locked door and a freshly formed ice barrier lies a cavern leading to a hidden chamber with a Shadow Realm barrier device and a large machine, which proves to be a prototype Kresnik's Torch. The workshop beneath the cabin holds the Meeting Point. A pedestal found with the barrier opens the Firearm Accessory Archive.
### LARGE +
Part of the ice must be melted with a basic heat source or Pyro to proceed, and a second set of pipes must be freed to get the furnace working.

## places
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
The cabin proper, the hidden cavern with its barrier, the furnace chamber warmed by the prototype Kresnik's Torch, and the workshop beneath the cabin where Alyosha's Meeting Point is. A Shadow Realm pedestal nearby opens the Firearm Accessory Archive once the Eye of Graeae establishes user permissions.

## people
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
Alyosha, the young hunter, with his dog Tugarin. Chaplina, publicly the shelter's founder. NPCs in the workshop include Aksakov, Belsky, Bystrov, Plate, Serebrov, Whistle and Zhemchuzhina, and Trapeznikov appears after Remnants of the Shadow Realm. Bystrov is Belsky's father and is busy looking for work; Whistle is a strong dog who trained with Tugarin.
### LARGE +
Trapeznikov, a friend of Alyosha in the Rokot, is an expert on Shadow Realm technology. Fae gang members in the story speak of an 'Old Sire' who is dead, of Zmei and Lady Voronina, and of the Izmaylov family and a High Table.

## governance
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
Fae gangs claim loot by their own rules, and the Rokot keep their involvement anonymous. Alyosha asks Chaplina to keep to the story that she established the shelter.

## culture
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
A hard-pressed rural community: villagers freeze, Kristall is scarce and smugglers trade in it. Alyosha, a hunter, goes after smugglers rumoured to have connections at the Royal Energy Commission, hoping to give their Kristall to freezing villagers, and calls himself 'just one crook robbing another'.
### LARGE +
Alyosha tells the Traveler a hunter turns back once the prey is caught, while an adventurer goes the extra step further.

## history
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
In Hunter on the Snowfields, Alyosha stalks a fae gang rumoured to hoard Kristall, and the Traveler joins him. They defeat the gang at the cabin, find a Shadow Realm Vault Access Card, melt a barrier, and discover Belyi Tsar era devices. A gang member returns with a modified detonator and is subdued by Tugarin.
### LARGE +
Alyosha decides to use the prototype Kristall furnace as a shelter, rather than chase the gangs' schemes. The Traveler keeps the access card and the weapon parts found there.
### ULTRA +
The gang members argue among themselves: one says that finds in the wild are shared by the rule of their dead leader, 'Old Sire', while another complains that since the old man died the High Table has treated them as expendable, so they risk digging in the Shadow Realm only for the big shots to profit. Alyosha also remembers that a fae gang had been buying blueprints from the Belyi Tsar's time, which is how he recognises the device as a furnace.

## atmosphere
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
A snowbound hideout turned warm refuge: Alyosha says the furnace can warm the whole area, and villagers at risk of freezing are brought there.

## dangers
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
Volkodlak gang members (bashers, lobbers, sharpshooters and raiders), explosives rigged in the cavern and ice barriers.

## quests
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
World quests: Hunter on the Snowfields (unlocks the Meeting Point); Many Hands Make Light Work (requires Meeting Point level 5); Remnants of the Shadow Realm Parts 1 to 3 (unlocks the Firearm Accessory Archive); Childhood Happiness.
### LARGE +
In Childhood Happiness, Belsky dog-sleds with Whistle while Alyosha fills in for Bystrov. In Many Hands Make Light Work, Chaplina reports the furnace burns Kristall more efficiently and that no gang members have come lately.

## status
@version: 7.1
### STANDARD
The shelter is stable: output is steady, Kristall shortages persist, but the stock is enough pooled together, and no gang has returned.

## entry: The Prototype Kresnik's Torch
@keys: prototype Kresnik's Torch, Kresnik's Torch furnace
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
The prototype is a furnace of the Belyi Tsar's era, heat-giving and stronger than any Kristall-powered furnace Alyosha has seen. The fae gang sought it for 'the plan' of their boss. Alyosha repurposes it to warm a shelter.
### LARGE +
Chaplina says Kristall burns much more efficiently with this design, and the output stabilised after maintenance.

## entry: Alyosha and the Rokot
@keys: Alyosha, Rokot hunter, Tugarin
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
Alyosha is a hunter and a member of the Rokot; he says keeping a low profile is a matter of principle. His dog Tugarin goes with him on hunts.
### LARGE +
Trapeznikov, from the Rokot, helps decode the Shadow Realm pedestal and its archive.
