## summary
### LITE
The Production Zone is the work area of the Fortress of Meropide. It has two levels below the Administrative Area, joined by a lift in the east of the Fortress. Inmates work here, and further below, through a lift in the lower level, lies the Abandoned Production Zone.
### STANDARD +
The Fortress is described as Fontaine's largest maker of clockwork meka.

## geography
### STANDARD
An Upper Level and a Lower Level, connected by a lift. A pool of water with wooden crates sits in the centre of the Production Zone. The Lower Level has a teleport waypoint and a lift down to the Abandoned Production Zone.
### LARGE +
The Abandoned Production Zone has four paths whose doors are controlled by a central drive valve. They lead to Supply Warehouse 1, the Geode Mine Shaft, the Energy Storage Warehouse and Supply Warehouse 2. A notice says all work related to the Geode Mine Shaft has been suspended and the connecting paths sealed. Opening one for official business requires a technical expert to operate the drive valve.

## places
### STANDARD
Upper Level residents include Clynes, Grainville, Irus and Nelly. The Lower Level has Lechlade and Maupoil. A pool at the centre has three empty wooden crates; Grainville thanks anyone who clears them, because keeping the place clean counts as maintaining order, and gives Coupons.
### LARGE +
During evacuation in Calamitous Tread, guards at the Lower Level lift tell people to leave immediately and go up and out.
### ULTRA +
In Wriothesley's story quest he deduces that Dougier bribed an overseer and heads to the Production Zone with the Traveler. They find an unconscious guard and some society members here, and one of them points them to the Beret Society's real headquarters, where Gardemeks and signs of missing clockwork meka confirm Wriothesley's suspicions.

## people
### STANDARD
The wiki's NPC list, without titles, is Clynes, Grainville, Irus, Nelly (Upper Level), Lechlade and Maupoil (Lower Level).

## governance
### STANDARD
Under the Administrator's authority. Inmates earn Credit Coupons by working.

## culture
### LARGE
Work is how inmates earn their Coupons, and Grainville's thanks show the Fortress's habit of rewarding small tasks with Coupons.

## history
### STANDARD
The zone appears in the world only from 4.1. The Abandoned Production Zone is closed off, with the Geode Mine Shaft suspended.

## dangers
### STANDARD
No hazards are listed on the zone's page. During the Cerberus Chapter, Beret Society members were found here, and during the breach in Calamitous Tread guards at the Lower Level lift ordered everyone to evacuate upward and outward.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Industrial and enclosed, a factory floor with water standing in the middle of it.

## travel
### STANDARD
Take the lift in the east of the Fortress down from the Administrative Area.

## quests
### STANDARD
Appears in Reborn in the Land of Grievances (Wriothesley's Cerberus Chapter Act I) and in the Archon Quest evacuation in Calamitous Tread (4.1).

## status
### LITE
Operating as the Fortress's factory floor as of 7.1.
