## summary
### LITE
The Cat's Tail is a tavern in Mondstadt City, owned by Margaret and known for its cats and for Diona, its young bartender whose cocktails taste wonderful whatever goes into them. It sits between the Adventurers' Guild post and the Good Hunter restaurant, and is where Mondstadt's Genius Invokation TCG players meet.
### STANDARD +
It is the rival of Angel's Share, which belongs to the Dawn Winery. Since Diona was hired it has done a roaring trade, though Angel's Share is still said to sell more.

## people
### LITE
Margaret owns the tavern. Diona is its star bartender. Prince, a black cat, is the tavern's TCG helper.
### STANDARD
Margaret hired Diona for a simple reason: she is cute, despite being too young to drink alcohol herself. Margaret owns several cats, including Prince and Butterfly, and enjoys writing witty replies to messages on the Cat's Tail Message Board, which has become a sort of advice board. After the TCG took off at the tavern, Prince, assisted by a woman named Shuyun, helps patrons who come to play.
### LARGE +
Diona, a cat-eared young woman from Springvale, openly hates alcohol and sees herself as the "Wine Industry Slayer", set on inventing the worst drink in the world. Every attempt turns out delicious, to her fury. In a message-board exchange Margaret answered Diluc's wistful note kindly and welcomed him despite their rivalry. A set of regular guests are drawn to the tavern's tables each day, plus a rotating weekly guest who may be a familiar character.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
A relaxed place to put your feet up for an afternoon or evening, with cats on the counter and drinks that are far better than their bartender wants. The official description teases that guests should be careful not to praise the bartender's skills. It also serves pizza, which is Jean's favourite when she has time to order one.
### LARGE +
The building has a main room, two smaller rooms and a second floor. After the world quest "A Guest From Liyue" the tavern's invitation board is upgraded, by Tubby using Adeptus arts, into an Invitation Duel Room where invited characters come to duel the player.

### ULTRA +
Game note: in the TCG tavern challenges, five cats (Baobab, Finnick, Little Rose, Night Pilot and Sneku) appear on the counter beside Prince, and four of five regular guests rotate in daily (Atra, Belen, Gary, Makri, Nanaya Yuumi). A poster outside advertises the new world of drinking to be had with Diona behind the bar. The tavern is also mentioned in the description of the Pine Dining Chair furnishing.

## history
### STANDARD
Before Diona the Dawn Winery's Angel's Share dominated Mondstadt's wine trade. Her arrival made the Cat's Tail a challenger overnight. Elzer, the winery's butler, tried to headhunt her for Angel's Share and was bitten.
### LARGE +
The tavern became the TCG hub in 3.3. In the world quest "Come Try Genius Invokation TCG!" the scholar Timaeus tells the Traveler that the community goes there to play, and Margaret, Diona and Shuyun teach the rules and arrange the first duels, including one against Fischl.

## quests
@version: 3.3
### STANDARD
"Come Try Genius Invokation TCG!" (3.3) opens the tavern's interior to the player. "Margaret's Longing" is a quest in which Prince steals Venti's lyre strings. "Master's Day Off" is a quest after which Margaret thanks the Traveler for help with her cat.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the tavern is open and the TCG hub of Mondstadt; no later content changes that.

## entry: Margaret

### STANDARD
Margaret is the proud owner of the Cat's Tail, shoulder-length blonde hair, a red and white dress and a brown hat. By day she wanders the fountain plaza. She is a little simplistic at times, quick with jokes, calls Mondstadt "a city of romance", and is fond of her cats.

### STANDARD
Prince is a black cat belonging to Margaret, who calls him a mischievous little rascal while spoiling him. He loves fish and playing with string and has keen senses. He serves as the tavern's shopkeeper and TCG helper.
