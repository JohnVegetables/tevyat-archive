## summary
### LITE
Mimisbrunnr Books is a bookshop in Nasha Town, on Lempo Isle in Nod-Krai. It is owned by Dhabit and sells books.
### STANDARD +
Its stock does not refresh, and each book can be bought only once. The shop has been available since Nod-Krai opened in version 6.0.

## people
### STANDARD
Dhabit owns the shop and is its vendor. The wiki lists Hannu (described as a newsy), Pechna and Viliana among the NPCs found at the location.

## culture
### LARGE
The name echoes Mimisbrunnr, the well beneath the world tree in Norse myth, a real-world reference noted by the wiki as trivia. The game does not explain it.

## status
### LITE
Operating in Nasha Town as of 7.1; no change recorded.
### ULTRA +
Because every book is single-purchase and the stock never refreshes, the shop's range can only shrink as a player buys.
