## summary
### LITE
Fragrant Whispers is a flower shop in the middle of Dornman Port, the port town in the northern Windrest Peak area of Mondstadt. Alodia owns it. It first appeared in Version 6.5.
### STANDARD +
Besides the flowers common in Mondstadt, Alodia says she sells new varieties cultivated by Lilian of Millhaven.

## people
### STANDARD
Alodia runs the shop and works with Lilian from Millhaven, relying on her skill in growing flowers. Her idle greeting offers customers a flower.
### LARGE +
If the player has the Special Perfume: Wind's Breath effect, Alodia remarks on the unusual scent.
### ULTRA +
The wiki also lists the sailor Hafner and a man named Mann among the people by the shop; nothing more is said about them.

## culture
### STANDARD
Alodia says a few flowers around a home lift the mood. The Millhaven varieties are unique to that village, so even Flora of Mondstadt City sometimes buys them. Alodia mentions she should send Flora more for the next Windblume Festival.
### LARGE +
The stock mixes flowers commonly found in Mondstadt with the newer varieties Lilian has carefully cultivated.

## travel
### STANDARD
In the centre of Dornman Port.

## status

Operating as of 7.1, as far as sources show.
