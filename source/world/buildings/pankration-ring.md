## summary
### LITE
The Pankration Ring is the arena of the Fortress of Meropide, the underwater prison of Fontaine. Pankration, a bare-fisted combat sport, is the Fortress's most popular entertainment. The host, Roussimoff, announces bouts from the platform above the stage.
### STANDARD +
The ring sits in the south-western part of the Administrative Area and has its own lift. Official text says that as long as special conditions are met, anyone or anything, from a Sternshield Crab to a clockwork meka, can stand on its stage. Inmates, staff and visitors treat it as both spectacle and exercise.
### LARGE +
The arena is the most visible part of the Fortress's social life under Administrator Wriothesley: a place where inmates earn attention and Credit Coupons rather than only serving time. Roussimoff says it is larger than any arena in the Court of Fontaine's Fleuve Cendre, which is why he prefers it.
### ULTRA +
Its name comes from the ancient Greek combat sport pankration ("all powers"), which allowed almost every technique except weapons. The drink sponsorship by Fonta that Roussimoff mentions ties the ring to the Fortress's wider economy.

## geography
### STANDARD
The Pankration Ring lies in the south-western part of the Administrative Area of the Fortress of Meropide, reached by its own separate lift. It has a central stage, with the host's announcer platform overlooking it, and an entrance area outside where fighters wait and talk.
### LARGE +
The Administrative Area is the Fortress's central hub, with the Duke's Office in its middle; the ring is entered separately from it.

## people
### STANDARD
Roussimoff is the host, an open-world NPC titled Pankration Ring Host. He is jovial and energetic, says he would rather fight than announce, and wants to face the best opponents. Goncourt, an inmate with a theatrical bent, uses the ring as a stage and spars there to loosen his joints.
### LARGE +
Poiret and Genevieve train for bouts in the Both Brains and Brawn world quest; Champvallon appears at its end. Before the Traveler completes that quest, Roussimoff turns them away as a fighter; afterwards he greets them as "the coach".
### ULTRA +
Once Both Brains and Brawn is complete, a one-on-one challenge becomes available: Goncourt fights first, then Roussimoff, who calls himself the champ. Each win gives a chest with Credit Coupons. This is a gameplay feature; keep it light in narrative scenes.

## culture
### STANDARD
Pankration is described as the Fortress's most popular form of entertainment. Roussimoff calls it a very healthy sport, and a notice board in the arena says it is a great form of exercise. Matches are partly theatre: performers talk about acting a scene on the stage, and Roussimoff worries that plain hand-to-hand fighting lacks theatricality.
### LARGE +
Fonta (a sweet drink) sponsors recreational matches; Roussimoff himself prefers Pure Water. He notes that most inmates have hit rock bottom, and that a little sweetness is something they remember after release.

## quests
@version: 4.1
### STANDARD
The world quest Both Brains and Brawn (Administrative Area) is built around the ring: Genevieve and Poiret want to take part, the Traveler helps them train for a series of bouts against Roussimoff, and the quest ends with a clockwork meka fight and Champvallon.
### LARGE +
Its steps run across several in-game days, with bouts held in the evening (18:00 to 22:00).
