## summary
### LITE
Druzhna HQ is the headquarters of the Druzhna in Snezhnograd, the capital of Snezhnaya. The Druzhna is the Tsaritsa's personal guard, led by Commander Vesna and made up mostly of Vila. The building is where the Tsaritsa's special recruitment drive to clear Shadow Realms is run by the officer Yarovaya.
### STANDARD +
The Druzhna also contains Shadow Realm breaches as part of its duties, which is why a dedicated operation to recruit gunners is based here.

## people
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
Yarovaya, a "Druzhna" Podporuchik, runs the Shadow Realm operations from here. The wiki lists Malinina, Musina, Rozhdestvensky and Shakhovskaya as other NPCs stationed at the headquarters. Yarovaya is a Vila with blonde hair, green eyes and small round glasses, in the Druzhna uniform.
### LARGE +
Yarovaya's duties span verifying public reports of anomalies, processing intelligence from the Royal Energy Commission of Snezhnaya, setting containment perimeters, and finally entering the Shadow Realm to carry out clearing operations.

## governance
### STANDARD
The Druzhna answers only to the Tsaritsa. Vesna commands it, and the Druzhna works alongside the Fatui. The Belyi Tsar founded it, and the Tsaritsa kept it as her personal guard rather than using it to suppress revolts against her authority; she raised the Fatui for that instead, according to "A Brief History of the Pale Starborn".
### LARGE +
Akvilina, a Druzhna officer, tells the Veche in 7.0 that the Druzhna's workload is heavy and that supervising Tartaglia's proposed regional data collection might prove difficult.

## history
### STANDARD
Historically the Druzhna was subordinate to the Belyi Tsar and its commander was Filipov. It remains active as the Tsaritsa's guard.
### LARGE +
The special recruitment project run from the headquarters was arranged by the Tsaritsa herself, who believes talent is hidden among the populace. Yarovaya admits to a superior that the Druzhna is "severely short-handed" and normally would handle such missions itself.

## quests
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
A Special Selection (World Quest, 7.0): the Traveler passes a recruitment test and Yarovaya offers a Shadow Realm clearing job, with Kristall for villagers as a stipend. It unlocks Special Recruitment Project: Cycle Training, in which Yarovaya gives weekly Shadow Realm missions. The follow-up world quest series is Looming Shadows, Parts I to VI.
### LARGE +
Applicants are tested on their shooting, and the Druzhna wants only people strong enough for what Yarovaya calls "a matter of life and death". Many applicants join for the pay, which rumour says includes Kristall. A senior woman later says the Tsaritsa has other plans for the new recruits beyond filling gaps in the official forces.
### ULTRA +
A Special Selection only becomes available after the Archon Quest Wraith's Nocturne. In the first training quest, Looming Shadows: Part I, Yarovaya says she suspects the Shadow Realm may be the most unstable of all known disasters. The Traveler activates a Shadow Realm Anchor and fights through waves of Oprichniki troopers, a medic and a Hydrogunner skirmisher, the last of whom is described as a soldier corroded by the shadows.

## atmosphere
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
A uniformed, disciplined place with queues of nervous applicants, short curt verdicts ("Next!") and officers who treat Shadow Realm work as life-or-death business.

## status
@version: 7.1
### STANDARD
Open and recruiting, with weekly Shadow Realm training missions available through Yarovaya.

## entry: Special Recruitment Project
@keys: Special Recruitment Project, Cycle Training, Looming Shadows
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
The Special Recruitment Project is the Tsaritsa's initiative to recruit capable shooters to enter Shadow Realms and clear them. Yarovaya assigns each week's training missions. The game text describes Shadow Realms as strange zones that appear suddenly in Snezhnaya's tundra like bubbles in water; people swallowed by one vanish and are warped into monsters.
### LARGE +
The map text suggests that helping Yarovaya might even earn the favour of the figure on the throne of Zapolyarny Palace.
