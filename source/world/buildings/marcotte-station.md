## summary
### LITE
Marcotte Station is the terminus of the aquabus Navia Line, serving the Erinnyes area.
### STANDARD +
Riders from the Court Region Waterway Hub take the Navia Line, run by Elphane, to reach it. The Opera Epiclese is the other stop on the same line.

## travel
### STANDARD
It is reached by the Navia Line from the Court of Fontaine main station; the line was named for Navia and its planning followed her mother's death.
### LARGE +
The Aquabus page notes that two further planned lines were never built.

## status
### LITE
Active at the cutoff (7.1).
### ULTRA +
Quests associated with the area are listed under Marcotte Station on the wiki; canon gives few other details.
