## summary
### LITE
Shamshir's Smithy is Sumeru City's blacksmith, on Treasures Street. Its seasoned owner Ahangar has been critically acclaimed among Eremite mercenaries. He forges and sells weapons and enhancement materials. The map describes it as proof that even forging can be learned by study in Sumeru, though long experience still counts for a craftsman.
### STANDARD +
Mansur, an Akademiya researcher from the Amurta school who came to study air pollution at the smithy, has become Ahangar's apprentice.

## people
### STANDARD
Ahangar says he is a blacksmith who crafts weapons for the Eremites, plus tools, accessories and furniture for others. He can also make wooden weapons, but most of his orders are pattern-welded steel weapons for mercenaries, since many Sumeru researchers are not in the habit of arming themselves and find metal weapons off-putting. He is brief and practical, advises regular maintenance of one's weapons, and has adopted several children (Iradj and Dwarkanath are named); his thoughts turn to hoping the children are well. Mansur is an Amurta researcher who arrived to study air pollution for his thesis, became Ahangar's apprentice by accident and now finds ironworking much more fun than writing papers.
### LARGE +
Mansur says the children Ahangar adopted all became researchers or joined the Eremites, so Mansur may end up the sole heir to the craft. He has kept the smithy's serious air-pollution problem from the Akademiya so that it is not shut down, which would leave him nothing to write about; he refuses to discuss the thesis. Ahangar is glad to finally have an apprentice. Ahangar will also mark ore deposits on the Traveler's map and challenges visitors to the card game, Genius Invokation TCG.

## customs
### LARGE
The shop's notices record Akademiya rules: large orders of pattern-welded steel weapons must first be reported to the Citadel of Regzar and approved; private orders outside the Teyvat Catalogue of Common Weapons, especially for automatic devices, are suspended; special requests need supporting documents from the relevant school. This shows how closely the Akademiya watches weapons in Sumeru City.
