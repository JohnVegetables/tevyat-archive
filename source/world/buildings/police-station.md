## summary
### LITE
The Police Station is the Inazuma City office of the police, in the Tenryou district. Its in-game description says it is responsible for maintaining law and order in Inazuma, and the Japanese name means Commissioner's Office. It is staffed by the yoriki Owada, who just wants a quiet career, and a new doushin, Uesugi, who wants bigger cases.
### STANDARD +
Day-to-day the station takes reports from neighbours, which are mostly minor troubles and clumsy mistakes. It is a place for citizens to report anything out of order or any suspicious outsider, and a notice outside urges everyone to stay alert after hostile people were seen in Inazuma City.

## places
### STANDARD
The office sits in Tenryou, the commercial district below Tenshukaku. Both officers wear the standard ashigaru-style uniform of the Inazuma Shogunate: purple-grey underclothes, eggplant-coloured armour with the Shogunate crest, and a cross-shaped spear. The notice board outside carries a public safety notice about hostile people in the city.
### LARGE +
The notice asks citizens to report any suspicious outsiders entering the city immediately. The board's heading calls it a 'Hanamizaka Public Safety Notice', which is why the station's precise district is recorded as disputed.

## people
### LITE
Owada is the yoriki; Uesugi is a doushin.
### STANDARD
Owada, the yoriki, is superior to every doushin there. He describes himself as getting too old, hoping only to clock out on time and retire in peace, and insists he is not slacking: he has seen everything in his long career and simply wants a peaceful world. He prays each day that nothing eventful happens, and refers pet-loss cases to Ryuuji. Uesugi, the doushin, is a recent recruit who greets visitors with a cheer for the Shogun and her everlasting reign and wishes the job gave him a big case worth his skill.
### LARGE +
Owada and Ryuuji are old acquaintances. Ryuuji, now a detective at the Bantan Sango Detective Agency, used to be a doushin here until someone framed him and he was suspended; Owada wanted to help but, as he admits, his power was limited, and he is glad Sango cleared Ryuuji's name. Uesugi sometimes works alongside Ryuuji and prizes the sense of accomplishment that desk work lacks. He fears turning into Owada, insignificant until retirement, and is unsettled by how casual things at the station can be.
### ULTRA +
Uesugi tells visitors about one clumsy episode: during the Vision Hunt Decree, Arataki Itto was defeated by Kujou Sara and plotted revenge; Sara, impressed by his earnestness, agreed to a rematch, and Itto insisted on a sumo showdown at the crossroads under oni rules. Uesugi thought this was basically harassment of Sara. The duel never took place, as a masked lady (identified in the wiki as Kuki Shinobu) dragged Itto away. He mentions Kamisato clan retainers going out on missions as a sign of how busy others are while the station is quiet.

## governance
### STANDARD
The station is part of Inazuma's law-and-order machinery under the Shogunate; Owada and Uesugi swear by the Shogun and the Electro Archon, and doushin answer to the yoriki. The link to the Tenryou Commission, which the Kujou clan leads, is implied by Rikuto's remarks below rather than stated by the officers.
### LARGE +
Rikuto, the retired Tenryou Commission man who owns Uyuu Restaurant, says that yoriki and doushin still come to him for advice even though he is retired. Ryuuji says that in the present state of Inazuma even the police are not fully reliable, a remark about the Sakoku era rather than about this station in particular.

## history
### STANDARD
No founding history is given. Within the game's story the station appears during the period of the Sakoku Decree and the Vision Hunt Decree, when the city is on guard against outsiders.
### LARGE +
The station figures in the background of Ryuuji's frame-up, which Owada recalls and which happened before the Traveler's arrival. Uesugi's Itto-and-Sara anecdote belongs to the time of the Vision Hunt Decree.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Quiet, stiff and a little sleepy. Owada startles easily and hopes nobody has come to report anything; Uesugi is eager and slightly bored. Behind the calm there is a sense that officials are anxious about outsiders and that the world outside is getting complicated.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the wiki records no change to the station: Owada and Uesugi are still listed as its staff and the notice text is the same.

## entry: Owada, the Yoriki

### STANDARD
Owada is the yoriki at the Police Station, an older officer who wants peace and a calm retirement rather than excitement. He prays that the Electro Archon will bless everyone, treats frequent reports with alarm, and recommends the Bantan Sango Detective Agency for lost pets. He is an old acquaintance of Ryuuji and regrets he could not help him when Ryuuji was framed.

## entry: Uesugi, the Doushin

### STANDARD
Uesugi is a new doushin, eager for important cases and mildly horrified at the thought of ending up like Owada. He sometimes helps Ryuuji with cases, takes missing-family reports (including the Traveler's), and tells the story of Arataki Itto's planned sumo rematch with Kujou Sara.
