## summary
### LITE
Heyu Tea House is a teahouse in the upper part of Liyue Harbor, on Feiyun Slope, just below the Northland Bank and the upstairs part of Xinyue Kiosk. Yun Jin and the Yun-Han Opera Troupe perform there. Its owner is Fan Er'ye, a retired opera star who bought the place with the wealth from his performing days.
### STANDARD +
Besides opera, the house has a storyteller, Tea Master Liu Su. Fan Er'ye is there at all hours and is passionate about the stories and shows on his stage.

## places
### STANDARD
Fan Er'ye describes the house to the Traveler: the stage, the tables and chairs, private rooms upstairs, performers and kitchen staff. He says he made it what it is. The stage is the focus; the Yun-Han Opera Troupe performs there, and a storyteller tells tales for customers.
### LARGE +
It stands in the upstairs region of Liyue Harbor, below the Northland Bank and beside the Xinyue Kiosk.
### ULTRA +
During the limited-time Lantern Rite event Fleeting Colors in Flight, Fan Er'ye says that every time he sees Miss Yun perform it reminds him of Ruoxin (event dialogue, not part of the permanent game).

## people
### STANDARD
Fan Er'ye is the boss. Tea Master Liu Su is the storyteller. Yun Jin directs the Yun-Han Opera Troupe, which performs here, and Changhu and Gentry Maocai are also seen there by day. Granny Ruoxin, now of Qingce Village, once performed at the house with Fan Er'ye.
### LARGE +
By day Fan Er'ye is so absorbed in the storyteller that he shushes the Traveler until it ends; even news of Rex Lapis' death after the Rite of Descension is, at first, something he will worry about "after the story ends". He insists the Traveler call him simply Er'ye.  At night he welcomes visitors and talks about the house.

## history
@version: 1.0
### LITE
In his youth Fan Er'ye and Ruoxin sang together on this stage. Forty years ago, he says, she was as celebrated an actress as Yun Jin is now. Fan Er'ye later made his name, saved enough to buy the teahouse and runs it today.
### STANDARD
Ruoxin fell in love with an adventurer and planned to leave her luxurious life for Mt. Qingce. He left on an expedition and never came back, and she now lives in Qingce Village. Fan Er'ye wishes he had had the courage to tell her how he felt.
### LARGE +
The Yun-Han Opera Troupe was established by the time of Yun Jin's grandfather. The troupe is associated with the Yun family, who once forged weapons and later turned to Liyue opera as a family tradition. Several of its veterans joined while her grandfather led it.

## culture
### STANDARD
The teahouse is part of Liyue Harbor's opera and storytelling life: a place to watch Liyue opera, listen to a storyteller and drink tea in company.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Audiences who stop talking to follow the performance, and a boss who shushes anyone who interrupts the story.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the Heyu Tea House is open, with Fan Er'ye as owner and Yun Jin's troupe performing there.
### STANDARD +
Fan Er'ye can be found there at all times of the day, and his dialogue changes with the time. No change of ownership is recorded.
