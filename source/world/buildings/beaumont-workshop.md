## summary
### LITE
Beaumont Workshop is a blacksmith's shop in the Vasari Passage of the Court of Fontaine, owned by Estelle. Its map description calls it the most renowned forging workshop in the Court.
### STANDARD +
Forging here is done by improved, fully automated machines that make works to an engineer's specifications, provided enough materials are supplied. It is unusual among Teyvat smithies in that its worker uses no hammer or anvil.

## places
### STANDARD
The shop sits in the Vasari Passage district of the Court of Fontaine. Estelle serves customers at the counter, where the machines do the work behind her.
### LARGE +
Its notice board advertises a one-stop custom service: clients are asked to bring a deposit, documents, their requirements and any relevant proof to a consultation, and the small print adds a surcharge for non-professional blueprints.

### ULTRA +
Per the wiki, this is the first blacksmith in the game whose worker has no hammer or anvil, because the forging is done by machine.

## people
### STANDARD
Estelle is the owner and the only named person associated with the workshop. Nothing about her history is stated in canon.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
A tidy, mechanised smithy: the sense is of precise engineering and paperwork, closer to a bespoke manufactory than a clanging forge.

## status
### LITE
Open as a shop at the cutoff (7.1); nothing in later content changes it.
