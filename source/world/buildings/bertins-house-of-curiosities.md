## summary
### LITE
Bertin's House of Curiosities is a shop in Quartier Lyonnais of the Court of Fontaine, run by Mequignon. It sells curios such as jewels, instruments, perfumes and souvenirs, yet is technically a fashion workshop.
### STANDARD +
Mequignon collects curios as inspiration for her designs. Because of her artistic ideals she exchanges collection pieces that have stopped inspiring her for Hydro Sigils brought by adventurers.

## places
### STANDARD
The shop lies in Quartier Lyonnais. Behind the counter stands a cabinet of violins that resembles a real-world orchestrion, a purely decorative detail.

### ULTRA +
The shop's name may allude to a historical French fashion designer; this is a wiki etymology guess, not canon.

## people
### STANDARD
Mequignon is the owner. Her goods are described as out of stock at times because nothing has caught her fancy lately, so her wares reflect her taste rather than steady supply.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
A cluttered, artful boutique: a designer's private cabinet of oddities that doubles as a shop.
