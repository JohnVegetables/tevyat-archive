## summary
### LITE
Lambad's Tavern is the main tavern and restaurant of Sumeru City, close to the lively Treasures Street. Its owner Lambad says he was a legendary sailor who sailed far and wide; the map description notes it is hard to tell how exaggerated that is, but the food makes the cuisine part of the claim believable. It sells food, ingredients and recipes.
### STANDARD +
Its signature dish is the Lambad Fish Roll, which Lambad says he devised in his youth by combining grilled fish he tasted in Mondstadt, Liyue and Inazuma with Sumeru's pitas.

## places
### STANDARD
A ground-floor bar and dining room with an upstairs section. A noticeboard outside advertises a daily special and the line that a wonderful day starts with a drink.
### LARGE +
The upstairs booth seating area was redesigned and refitted by Kaveh. After an accident in the tavern a few days earlier, the tableware was replaced: Kshahrewar researchers commissioned craftspeople to make it, and Haravatat researchers designed the patterns.

## people
### STANDARD
Lambad runs it. By day the researcher Jafar sits here; by night come the Corps of Thirty mercenary Hakan, the Eremite mercenary Lemta and, upstairs, the researchers Khaldun and Yavanani, the international student Gregor and the boat captain Shams.
### LARGE +
After going bankrupt, Kaveh spent his time drinking here, where he met Alhaitham again and was invited to live with him.

## quests
### STANDARD
No quest is set mainly here; it appears in the background of Kaveh's history with Alhaitham.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Warm and talkative, smelling of grilled fish and fresh bread, with Lambad ready to tell a sailing tale.

## status
### STANDARD
As of 7.1 the tavern is open as before; the pages consulted record no later change. Players who reach Reputation Rank 4 in Sumeru get a 10% discount there.

## trivia
### ULTRA
Stock refreshes daily or weekly, depending on the item. The tavern's name in Chinese is Lambad Tavern. In his idle line Lambad says he went on his fair share of crazy adventures back in the day, and in dialogue he claims to have passed storms more wrathful than the Raiden Shogun and glaciers steelier than the Tsaritsa's heart.
