## summary
### LITE
The Northland Bank in the Court of Fontaine is a branch of the Snezhnayan Northland Bank, in Quartier Lyonnais. Its sign is written in Fontaine Script as BANQUE DU NORD.
### STANDARD +
It stands between Caron's Lepaute Horlogerie and Livre's Leschots Clockwork Workshop. Its interior cannot be entered by the player.

## places
### STANDARD
A narrow bank front between the two clockwork shops, seen only from the street.
### LARGE +
Quests in the Toy War event series place the bank between the two brothers' shops, so it sits at the boundary of their rivalry.

## status
### LITE
Present at the cutoff (7.1); its interior remains inaccessible.
### ULTRA +
The bank is part of the Fatui-linked Northland Bank network that has branches in other nations; canon does not detail this branch.
