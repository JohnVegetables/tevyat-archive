## summary
### LITE
The Northland Bank (Liyue) is the Liyue Harbor branch of the Northland Bank, the Snezhnayan bank with branches around Teyvat. It stands on Feiyun Slope and is the only non-local bank in Liyue. The Fatui use Northland Bank funds abroad and sometimes its branches as bases, and in the Liyue Archon Quest this branch is where the Traveler meets Childe's contact Ekaterina and where Childe arranges to meet them.
### STANDARD +
The branch is staffed by a manager, a receptionist, guards and the occasional customer. It is also the stop from which Childe sends the Traveler on to Liuli Pavilion, where he has arranged a dinner with Zhongli.

## places
### STANDARD
The bank has a public hall inside, where the receptionist works at her desk and the manager is found by day, and a back room behind it. Guards stand outside. It sits on Feiyun Slope in upper Liyue Harbor, just above the Heyu Tea House.
### LARGE +
The reception desk handles Mora exchanges: a player who still holds a Mora pouch left by Childe (from Downtown or Teucer's Terrific Tour) can swap it for ordinary Mora there. That is a gameplay convenience. A Precious Chest in the back room is also a gameplay detail.

## people
### STANDARD
Andrei is the manager and is inside only by day. Ekaterina is the receptionist and is present day and night; she greets the Traveler as a friend of Childe and passes on his messages. Vlad guards the door by day and Nadia (the Liyue guard) by night. A customer, Mister Qi, is inside by day.
### LARGE +
Ekaterina speaks with the polished confidence of a Fatui functionary. In the Wangsheng quest she jokes that the Traveler's first day of an illustrious career with the Fatui has begun, says ideas are fluid like water and only the Tsaritsa's will is as solid as permafrost, and stresses that Harbingers do not lightly break promises. Vlad, asked, says it is the only non-local bank in Liyue.

## governance
### STANDARD
The Northland Bank belongs to Snezhnaya. Later canon (7.0) says Pantalone is in charge of its operations as a whole, though other Harbingers may handle the opening of new branches. The Liyue branch operates openly in Liyue Harbor as a bank, even though Fatui business passes through it.

## history
@version: 1.0
### LITE
The branch is a central location of the early Liyue Archon Quest. During the Rite of Descension Childe has the Traveler meet him there after he escapes the Millelith. Later the Traveler returns to it, and in The Fond Farewell goes there once more in search of Zhongli.
### STANDARD
After the Rite of Descension Childe uses the bank as the place where the Traveler can find him once he is free of the Millelith. Back from visiting the adepti, the Traveler and Paimon find that the Qixing have hidden the Geo Archon's vessel. Childe has promised to find someone to break the stalemate, so they visit the bank again. Ekaterina says Childe has kept his side of the agreement and that he is at Liuli Pavilion; there he introduces Zhongli. The final visit is in The Fond Farewell, in search of Zhongli.
### ULTRA +
The leftover funds Childe provides in Downtown and Teucer's Terrific Tour (a story quest) are exchanged for Mora at this branch. This is a gameplay mechanic, and canon does not explain how his funds reach the branch.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Quiet and businesslike compared with the loud restaurants and teahouses on Feiyun Slope. The staff speak politely and give nothing away; a promise made to a Harbinger is treated as a debt.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the branch remains open in Liyue Harbor as one of the Northland Bank's overseas branches.
### STANDARD +
No later canon changes it. In 7.0 the Snezhnograd headquarters and the bank's founding by Pantalone were shown; that history belongs to the main bank, not this branch.

## entry: Northland Bank as the Fatui's Foreign Base
@keys: Northland Bank
@keys2: Fatui, Pantalone, Harbinger
@version: 7.0
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Loading-screen text says the Northland Bank has branches in many places; when the Fatui operate outside Snezhnaya they use funds provided by the bank and even use it as a base. Pantalone founded the bank shortly after becoming a Harbinger and is in charge of its operations. Loans are generous but must be repaid in full and on time, and the Fatui have a debt-collection division for those who default.
