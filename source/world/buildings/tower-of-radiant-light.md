## summary
### LITE
The Tower of Radiant Light is a large tower in Luyang Academy, the painting gallery of the Temple of Space. The Traveler climbs it during the quest Long Live Life, in the effort to restore the Academy.
### STANDARD +
Its name is rendered as 明光楼 in Chinese, which translates literally as Tower of Brilliant Light.

## geography
### STANDARD
The tower stands within the Liyue-styled compound of Luyang Academy, which is built on a small island. The painter Daozi says the direct path to it is barred, so the party must approach through the Abode of Resplendent Talent.
### LARGE +
In the quest the final approach is made in a painted world, at a moment when ink is chasing the party. A lift carries the Traveler to the top, where the other side of an important painting can be reached from inside the tower.

## history
### STANDARD
Daozi, freed from a painting early in Long Live Life, explains that the Academy cannot be restored until a particular painting is completed. Its title, as he gives it, is "Once the Heavenly Heart Emerges, the Four Horizons Fade to White", and in his words it should be possible to complete the missing part of it in the Tower of Radiant Light.
### LARGE +
The party reaches the tower after being led into an ink trap by a figure who falsely claimed to be the painter Escher. They flee through re-projected paintings with Dex's help until a bridge painting is destroyed and they drop to the ground near the tower. At the top they come to the painting from its other side, and Daozi is waiting there. He then adds the finishing stroke that connects the set of paintings and opens the way to the Pavilion of Paintings at the centre of the Academy. Plucking the star there unrolls a great scroll, reveals the whole Academy and frees its inhabitants from the ink.
### ULTRA +
During the climb the game limits the party to the Traveler, restricts movement to a fixed side-on or top-down view and disables combat talents. Daozi attributes the ink and the false Escher to Melanta, the Watcher who punishes people by sealing them in paintings.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Ink, mist and Liyue-style halls; the sense of climbing out of a painted world while a dark flood presses behind.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Ink blots and flowing ink are harmful to touch, and the Domain Keepers of the Temple of Space guard the Academy. Neither is tied specifically to the tower beyond the chase that leads to it.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the Academy is restored and its painters are free; the tower has no further role in the story.
