## summary
### LITE
The Rossum Workshop is a smithy in Nasha Town, Nod-Krai. The blacksmith Arkhyp owns it, and his daughter Lyulka currently runs it.
### STANDARD +
Lyulka is the vendor, listed as a researcher; Arkhyp is named as the workshop's blacksmith.

## people
### STANDARD
Arkhyp, the blacksmith and owner. Lyulka, his daughter, who operates the workshop at present and has her own entry.

## culture
### LARGE
The wiki notes the name may allude to the 1920 play R.U.R., about a factory making artificial workers. This is an editor observation, not stated canon.

## status
### LITE
Operating as of 7.1; no change recorded.
### ULTRA +
The sources read do not say where Arkhyp is now, only that Lyulka runs the forge.
