## summary
### LITE
A grand stage in the Children of Echoes' settlement in Tequemecan Valley, Natlan, where the tribe hosts dance battles every day and night.
### STANDARD +
It has a DJ booth, fire-spewing stage props and a very sturdy frame. The Children of Echoes Bulletin Board stands there.

## people
### STANDARD
The NPCs Kaan, Pochtli and Tano are listed at the stage; the sources read give no roles for them.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
A scene here is loud and showy: music from the DJ booth, flames from the stage props and a crowd of the tribe watching rival dancers. This reflects the facts above and is a scene suggestion, not extra canon.

## culture
### ULTRA
The name Tepetl means mountain in Nahuatl, per the wiki. The stage's setup is mirrored by the furnishing Tequemecan Dance-Off.

## status
### LITE
In use for dance battles as of 7.1.
