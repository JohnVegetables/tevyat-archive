## summary
### LITE
The Favonius Cathedral is the great Gothic-looking church at the top of Mondstadt City, standing behind the plaza with the giant statue of the Anemo Archon Barbatos. The Church of Favonius runs it as the centre of Barbatos worship: nuns pray and hear confessions there, Deaconess Barbara works there, and its guarded basement held the Holy Lyre der Himmel, which the Traveler tried to steal for Venti in the Prologue.
### STANDARD +
The Church is overseen by the Knights of Favonius, so knights guard the doors and stairs. The building's grandeur is a point of debate: the map description wonders whether a city taught freedom by its god needs to spend so much on so lavish a cathedral, and Dainsleif remarked on the same thing.

## geography
### STANDARD
The Cathedral crowns the upper end of Mondstadt City. Long stairs climb from the Anemo Archon statue plaza to its doors; inside are rows of pews leading to the apse, and a staircase at the far end leads down to a guarded basement.
### LARGE +
The statue in front shows Barbatos hooded and winged, hands cupped together, with the words "The Gateway of Celestia" carved on its pedestal in Teyvat script. The basement is a separate, stone-walled level with bookshelves, ladders and a back room where the Holy Lyre was kept.

## places
### STANDARD
Nave and pews, where townsfolk and nuns pray; the confessional, where Sister Victoria listens to confessions all day; the front lawn and steps; the basement vault, normally closed to the public and watched by knights.

## people
### LITE
Clergy of the Church of Favonius: Deaconess Barbara (healer, singer and "idol" of Mondstadt), the nun Rosaria (who skips nearly every Church duty), Deacon Dahlia, and, since his return from Nod-Krai, Seneschal Seamus Pegg.
### STANDARD +
Regular faces include Sister Victoria (sharp-tongued, keen on "sinners"), Sister Grace (who leads prayers at the statue by day and is inside by night), and the nun Jilliana, a former Treasure Hoarder who prays while her husband Rudolf and young daughter Lily wait nearby. Knights of Favonius Otto and Aramis guard the entrance and the stairs. Albert, head of the Barbara fan club, loiters outside and is regularly shooed off by the sisters.
### LARGE +
Cardinal Calvin ran the Church's affairs while Seamus was away and was so buried in work that the sisters barely saw him; Barbara last saw him when the Lyre was stolen. Other sisters on record include Gotelinde, who handles the Cathedral's paperwork. For a long time the Fatui diplomat's assistant Viktor idled inside the Cathedral, sneering at Barbatos while befriending little Lily; he left Mondstadt after Signora's death.

## governance
### STANDARD
The Cathedral belongs to the Church of Favonius, which the Knights of Favonius manage. Its leader is the Seneschal (Seamus Pegg, the Cardinal of Daybreak); Cardinal Calvin served as acting head during the long expedition. Use of the Holy Lyre der Himmel is regulated: it is normally used only at Ludi Harpastum, and any other loan needs documents signed by the Grand Master, the Seneschal and the Community Representative.

## culture
### STANDARD
Worship here is gentle and informal. The faithful say a prayer spoken on the breeze will reach Barbatos; the usual blessing is "may Barbatos' gentle wind be with you". Nuns hear confessions, offer guidance, run hymn and choir practice, care for the sick and tend children without parents; Barbara's healing and singing make her the Cathedral's most beloved figure. Almost nobody inside knows that the bard Venti is Barbatos himself; when Venti declared himself the god, a nun simply went back to her paperwork.
### LARGE +
Rosaria's records show the Church's formal side: compulsory classes, theological essays and hymn practice supervised by sisters such as Mother Maria and Sister Ophila. Barbara's Vision came while she was nursing a feverish boy for the Church and sang for him for the first time.
### ULTRA +
Small scenes show everyday faith: Rudolf tells his daughter Lily that her mother helps and guides other believers and that their happy life is Barbatos' kindness, and a card depicting the Cathedral (titled "Gathering of the Devout") says that sincere words spoken into the wind will reach the Archon some day. Nuns chase loiterers from the doors, and Albert's excuse is that the steps are as close as he can get to Barbara without disturbing her work. Viktor's open contempt for Barbatos earned him hostile looks from the worshippers. Lily, who called him "Mr. Fatooey", missed him after he was recalled, and after being reassigned to Sumeru he prepared a letter and a gift for her.

## history
### STANDARD
The Viewpoint text says the site was once a palatial estate from which the aristocracy displayed its power, before it became a place of worship tended by Barbatos' devotees. During the Stormterror crisis the Lyre was stolen from its basement, and outside its doors Signora took Venti's Gnosis.
### LARGE +
Later the Cathedral kept records of strange incidents such as "The Lost Statue of The Seven" and "The Tyrant's Final Fury", which Barbara consulted for the Traveler while investigating the Abyss Order. In 6.5, after the Nod-Krai expedition, Seamus Pegg was back at work there, hearing confessions and receiving the expedition's personnel report.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Quiet and high-ceilinged, with many windows (the sisters clean them all by hand), with murmured prayers, the odd scolding from Sister Victoria and singing practice. Outside, wind sweeps the high plaza, Sister Grace leads open-air prayers at the statue by day, Albert hovers near the doors, and a knight on the steps grumbles about his knees.

## quests
### STANDARD
Prologue Act II (Venti's Plan, Venti's New Plan): the attempt to borrow and then steal the Holy Lyre. Prologue Act III (Song of the Dragon and Freedom): the Lyre is returned and Signora attacks Venti outside. Chapter I Act IV (Dishonorable Trial): the Traveler consults the clergy about the Abyss Order.
### LARGE +
Barbara's Hangout (1.4) starts with Sister Victoria worried about her, and asks the Traveler to clean the Cathedral. The daily commission "Stop, Albert. Stop!" has Victoria sending the Traveler to chase off Albert. Dahlia's anecdote "Day of Confession" (6.5) is set at the doors and inside. Lohen's hidden objective "Reporting Back" (6.5) ends there with Seamus.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the Cathedral is in normal use. Seamus Pegg has returned from Varka's expedition and works there again, taking on work the clergy carried alone for years. The Church has the Holy Lyre back, and Barbara has refused to lend it out again.
### STANDARD +
Before his return, Cardinal Calvin carried his duties. Dahlia expects Seamus to be working at the Cathedral for some time; Barbara, still awkward around her father, calls him by his title there and has avoided confessing to him.

## entry: The Theft of the Holy Lyre der Himmel
@keys: Holy Lyre der Himmel, Holy Lyre, Venti's New Plan, Venti's Plan
@keys2: Cathedral, Favonius Cathedral, Church of Favonius, basement
@version: 1.0
### STANDARD
In Prologue Act II, Venti needed the Holy Lyre der Himmel, the rosewood lyre Barbatos once played, to cleanse Dvalin. Sister Gotelinde turned him away when he asked to borrow it and ignored his claim to be Barbatos, going back to the Cathedral's paperwork. When the Traveler asked on the Knights' behalf, she explained the three signatures required and noted the Seneschal was away. So the Traveler sneaked into the basement at night, past patrolling guards, only for a Fatui Cicin Mage to snatch the Lyre first. Knights caught the Traveler at the scene and they fled with Venti.
### LARGE +
Knight Otto guards the stairs to the basement by day during this period. The Lyre came back into the Traveler's hands in the quest Chasing Shadows, its Anemo power exhausted, and was partly repaired before the confrontation with Dvalin.

## entry: Signora's Ambush at the Cathedral
@keys: Signora, Anemo Gnosis, Gnosis
@keys2: Cathedral, Favonius Cathedral, Venti, Barbatos, Mondstadt
@version: 1.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
At the end of the Prologue, Jean asked the Traveler to return the Holy Lyre at the Cathedral. Barbara received it badly damaged; Venti gave it a quick illusion so it only looked repaired, then slipped out. Outside, Fatui agents froze Paimon and Venti, held the Traveler, and the Harbinger Signora tore Venti's Gnosis from his chest before fleeing ahead of the Knights. Barbara found them afterwards, but her healing did nothing for Venti, who left for Windrise.

## entry: Clergy of the Cathedral
@keys: Sister Victoria, Sister Grace, Jilliana, Gotelinde, Cardinal Calvin, Mother Maria, Sister Ophila
@keys2: Cathedral, Church of Favonius, Mondstadt
### STANDARD
Victoria hears confessions, gives guidance not everyone wants, and obsesses over sinners who will not change; she keeps an eye on the heavy drinker Nimrod and on Albert. Grace is a motherly nun who cares for children without parents in Mondstadt (Glory and Timmie call her mother) and leads open-air prayers. Jilliana, Lily's mother, left the Treasure Hoarders for her family and became a nun. Gotelinde manages Cathedral paperwork. Cardinal Calvin was acting Seneschal during Seamus' absence.
### LARGE +
Mother Maria and Sister Ophila appear in Rosaria's stories, despairing of her truancy and of her theological essays.

## entry: Dahlia's Day of Confession
@keys: Day of Confession
@keys2: Dahlia, Seamus, Cathedral
@version: 6.5
### LARGE
After Seamus Pegg's return, Deacon Dahlia was one of the first to make confession to him at the Cathedral. Light-heartedly, he confessed to hiding from Barbara that Venti is Barbatos, to drinking, and to slipping off for a drink with Rosaria. He asked Seamus about Frederica's whereabouts on Barbara's behalf, then heard Seamus' own confession in turn. Seamus mentioned that Jean was working overtime at headquarters when he got home.

## entry: Dainsleif at the Cathedral
@keys: Dishonorable Trial, Lost Statue of The Seven, Tyrant's Final Fury
@keys2: Cathedral, Church of Favonius, Dainsleif, Barbara
@spoiler: minor
### LARGE
While tracking the Abyss Order in Chapter I Act IV, the Traveler and Dainsleif came to the Cathedral. Dainsleif questioned how much the Anemo Archon gave back for so grand a statue and cathedral, then waited outside rather than risk insulting the Church. Barbara refused to lend the Holy Lyre again, and told from Church records of the vanished "Lost Statue of The Seven" and "The Tyrant's Final Fury". Rosaria passed on Jean's order that Barbara keep to her duties with the clergy.
