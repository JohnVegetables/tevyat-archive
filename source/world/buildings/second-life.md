## summary
### LITE
Second Life is a small general goods shop in the main plaza of Chihu Rock, Liyue Harbor. Its owner, Dongsheng, is a former adventurer who left the road after a serious accident in Dihua Marsh, where a masked adeptus rescued him. He named the shop to remember that day.
### STANDARD +
It sells ordinary cooking ingredients such as salt, pepper, onion, milk, rice and tofu, and its stock is refreshed daily. The wiki says that high Liyue reputation earns a discount.

## places
### STANDARD
One shop on the plaza of Chihu Rock, open day and night.

## people
### STANDARD
Dongsheng runs the shop. He used to be an adventurer who travelled everywhere, and has stayed put since being rescued. He calls his current life a simple one, and says the life he has was given to him by the adeptus.
### LARGE +
He is also seen in a Yun Jin hangout memory image set at the shop, according to the wiki.

## culture
### STANDARD
The shop's name plays on a Liyue adage, 'a withered tree lives again', which Dongsheng uses for the idea of a life given back. The Chinese name takes the last part of the saying, meaning something like '(re-)flourishment'.
### LARGE +
Asked about the shop, Dongsheng is happy to tell the story, but does not press it on anyone and says it is best not to raise old matters when a visitor declines.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
A modest corner shop where the owner is polite and unhurried and the slogan is plain: satisfaction guaranteed.

## travel
### STANDARD
In the central plaza of Chihu Rock in Liyue Harbor.
### ULTRA +
The shop's Chinese name, Rongfa Shangpu, is explained on the wiki as commemorating the rescue, drawing on the idiom 'kumu farong'; Japanese and Korean names use the same idiom.

## status
### LITE
Open as of 7.1, as far as the sources show.
