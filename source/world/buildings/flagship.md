## summary
### LITE
The Flagship is the rowdiest tavern in Nasha Town, Nod-Krai. Drifters, treasure hunters and bounty hunters gather there to swap intel and pick up commissions, and the bartender Demyan runs it with a cheerful, lively air. It also rents rooms: the Traveler stays here in the Song of the Welkin Moon story.
### STANDARD +
The in-game map text calls it once a haven for drifters and treasure hunters, brimming with secrets, lies and raucous energy, and says recent management changes have made it safer. It still warns that a visitor who lets his guard down for a second might regret more than the choice of drink.

## people
### LITE
Demyan is the bartender; the staff and regulars include recruiters, hosts and wandering travelers.
### STANDARD
Demyan, a young man in a cap and cardigan, sells food and drinks and offers rooms. He says business is good, that a few small incidents beat a stale evening, and that his boss sent him here as a good fit. Others listed at the tavern are Kruchek, a Fatui recruiter, Leszek, an employee, Paavo, a host, Pentti, a nostalgic traveler, plus Suronja and Tapani.
### LARGE +
Demyan keeps the stock honest: he tells Flins that he waters nothing and sells no poor quality drinks, even if it costs him some Mora. Flins, a regular, recommends wines that Demyan says sold out quickly; when Flins collects a bottle Demyan has kept in the cellar, Flins asks for two glasses so that they can share it. Katya, who runs Speranza, tells visitors who want gossip to go to the Flagship rather than to her.

## places
### STANDARD
A bar with a jukebox and a bounty board, plus a hallway and guest rooms. The jukebox has four records: "A Carefree Cup", "Homecoming to Port", "A Borrowed Hour" and "Desire's Intent". The default is the leftmost record.
### LARGE +
Demyan sells Berries and Fried Meat and the Roulette Special daily, with the drinks Piramida Nightingale, Frontier Land and Wildberry Trail, and the recipes for the two dishes. The Traveler's room has a desk where the Marionette's Notebook can be read after Welkin Moon's Homecoming.

## quests
### STANDARD
The Intel quest The Dust Settles ends the tavern's bounty line about Hark the Crackjaw: Jahoda and Nefer of the Curatorium of Secrets meet the Traveler at the Flagship and conclude that the man living in the hermit's hut is the wanted Hark, who took over from the real hermit Derwent, probably dead. After Hark is defeated, the notice changes to say the bounty is fulfilled.
### LARGE +
In Welkin Moon's Homecoming, the Traveler and Paimon wake in the Flagship after a restful night and later treat themselves to a feast there before heading to Speranza.

## bounty_board
### STANDARD
The board lists four notices. Hidimbi, a fortune teller from Sumeru, is wanted for suspected curses and large-scale fraud. Gonpachi, an Inazuman in Kairagi garb, is wanted by the Voynich Guild for attacks on traveling merchants. The New Iron and Salt Gang is wanted by Spina di Rosula for suspected smuggling of restricted hazardous goods. Hark the Crackjaw, former helmsman of the ship Atlas, is wanted for murder.
### LARGE +
The notices change when the matching quests are done: Gonpachi's is marked fulfilled after Peco's testimony; the smuggling bounty is marked complete with Spina di Rosula's regards; Hark's refers visitors to the Curatorium of Secrets after The Dust Settles; and Hidimbi's becomes a search request because the buyer now suspects he was not sincere enough in courtship.
### ULTRA +
The Hidimbi notice, written by an angry client, says she swindled him out of 18,000,000 Mora with a necklace she said came from Al-Ahmar, the God of Love, and that the necklace is real but cursed him never to find love. The notice itself points out that Al-Ahmar died thousands of years ago without winning the girl he loved. This is the poster's version of events.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Loud, crowded and full of rumors, with a jukebox playing and a bounty board on the wall. It is described as safer than it once was, but gossip about the Fatui and the Frostmoon Scions still travels fast.

## history
### STANDARD
Under its earlier management it was a place for drifters and treasure hunters to hide out. Demyan says that since the Fatui set up their Kuuvahki Experimental Design Bureau on Paha Isle the atmosphere has changed a lot, that some resent how the Fatui throw their weight around while others want their new technology, and that the Frostmoon Scions' view of them has stayed constant. After the Bureau suffered a blow, he adds that many people were pleased but they must still do business with the Fatui.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the tavern is open and doing good business. Nothing in 7.0 or 7.1 content changes it.

## entry: Demyan

### STANDARD
Demyan is the bartender of the Flagship, a young man with light blond hair, light blue eyes and a dark brown cap decorated with a small flower. He greets visitors with a friendly invitation to sit anywhere, gives the Traveler a Guide to Elysium on first chat, and has a good-humored view of trouble: handling it is fun on its own. His full background is not given.
