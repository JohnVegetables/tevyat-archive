## summary
### LITE
The Main Control Hall is the central control room of Dunanna Forward Station, an underground installation in the Dunanna Pit area of Nod-Krai. The in-game map marker describes it as the core processing room for every command the station receives and transmits.
### STANDARD +
It is a point of interest on the lower level of the station's map, reached in the world quest Pioneers (added in 6.7).

## places
@version: 6.7
### STANDARD
In Pioneers the Traveler and Paimon follow Columbina into ruins beneath Misty Shoal, restore power at a generator room, and later enter the hall while chasing a black kuuhenki. A message at the Celestial Orbit says the Frost Moon coordinates, the station's base engineering blueprints and a route map to the Main Control Hall have been transmitted. The hall is guarded by an Anzu array, and a lunar shuttle is reached afterwards.
### LARGE +
The hall is one stop in a sequence of station spaces in the quest: the generator room, the Celestial Orbit, the Main Control Hall, and the lunar shuttle where the black kuuhenki is confronted.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the hall remains part of the explorable Dunanna Forward Station; the sources read record no later change.
### ULTRA +
The wiki lists the hall, like the station around it, among the additions of version 6.7. The page is a stub whose only descriptive text is the map marker line, so nothing about the hall's inner layout or staff is established.
