## summary
### LITE
Damoville Purveyor is a general goods shop in the Vasari Passage of the Court of Fontaine, run by Boucicaut.
### STANDARD +
Its supplies restock daily. Players who reach Reputation Rank 4 in Fontaine gain a permanent 10 percent discount here (a gameplay benefit).

## places
### STANDARD
The shop is in the Vasari Passage, the same district of the Court of Fontaine as Beaumont Workshop.
### LARGE +
It is classed as a general goods shop rather than a specialist one.

## people
### STANDARD
Boucicaut is the only named person. Canon gives no personal history for him.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
A plain general store for picking up supplies.

## status
### LITE
Open at the cutoff (7.1).
### ULTRA +
The wiki suggests the shop's name may allude to a historical dressmaker to French royalty; treat this as an etymology guess only.
