## summary
### LITE
The Asase Shrine is a subarea of Seirai Island in Inazuma. It was founded to manage the wards that hold down the lingering resentment of the Thunderbird, Kanna Kapatcir, felled over the island. The shrine is now looked after by Neko, a big black cat who calls herself its Provisional Head Priestess, waiting for the shrine maiden Asase Hibiki, who never came back from a great thunderstorm.
### STANDARD +
Around five hundred years ago, Hibiki unsealed the wards to help the pirate captain Ako Domeki against the Shogunate, which unleashed the Electro storm that still marked the island in the Traveler's time. The shrine itself was little damaged, only emptied of visitors.

## geography
### STANDARD
A shrine on Seirai Island, in noticeably better condition and with fairer weather than the rest of the island. Features: a Rock Pile, a Waverider Waypoint, an offering box, and statues of Neko added after quests. Villagers of Koseki Village once helped upkeep it and left offerings.

## places
### STANDARD
The offering box, which Neko has the Traveler repair so that Mora can be left in it. A statue of Neko, which Neko has Ooshima Junpei make; its finishing touch is followed by a cloud forming the shape of a cat. Renovation work and cat care also take place at the shrine. that formed the shape of a cat.
### LARGE +
The island's four Warding Stones, which the Traveler and the adventurers Etsuko and Taisuke re-seal in Seirai Stormchasers, are not at the shrine but are the wards it exists to manage.

## people
### STANDARD
Neko, a black cat larger than ordinary cats, speaks to the Traveler and is the self-styled head. After the quests, the adventurers Etsuko and Taisuke (Adventurers' Guild) and Ooshima Junpei can also be found there. The shrine cats named Konbumaru (after Takamine the Mistsplitter), Hachihou and Meshi have names of "good things" passed down through generations.
### LARGE +
Asase Hibiki, the head priestess five centuries ago, is not present; she and Domeki are known to the island only by memory.

## history
### STANDARD
After the Thunderbird was killed by the Electro Archon at Amakumo Peak, the shrine, managed by the Asase Clan, was established to suppress what was left of her. Hibiki trained at the Grand Narukami Shrine, mentored by the Kitsune Saiguu, and returned to Asase Shrine after the cataclysm. When Domeki's crew fought the Shogunate she broke the wards using "true magic" from the Yougou Tengu, loosing the Thunder Manifestation on the Shogun's ships. The island was devastated and Koseki Village was evacuated to Inazuma City; Hibiki disappeared into the storm.
### LARGE +
Neko was named by Hibiki for her habit of sleeping, and has lived for over 500 years. She waited at the shrine through the storm, kept it up as best she could, and was unaware Domeki's fleet had returned. The Traveler found her still waiting, unaware the villagers were gone for good. Hibiki told Neko and Domeki stories of her past and of her wish to see the world, but never mentioned the sad endings of Takamine and her other friends.
### ULTRA +
The wiki's account of Hibiki states she once loved Takamine the Mistsplitter, a high-ranking samurai who vanished in the Abyss; much later he returned and she killed him with an arrow, believing his corruption irreversible. This is told in artifact and weapon text (Shimenawa's Reminiscence, Thundering Pulse) and she never told Neko or Domeki.

## culture
### LARGE
The shrine cats are named after "good things" and the names are passed down. Neko says the storm made her fur stand on end, and is glad once the Warding Stones are sealed.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Weathered wood, a calm patch in a stormy island, and the slow time-sense of a cat who has waited centuries; mild, dignified, and a little forlorn.

## quests
### STANDARD
Seirai Stormchasers (Parts I-IV, a world quest series after Ritou Escape Plan): the Traveler meets Neko, repairs the offering box, seals four Warding Stones with Etsuko and Taisuke, and faces the Thunder Manifestation. The separate Neko Is a Cat series has Neko has the Traveler renovate the shrine and tend the cats, and takes a trip to the Grand Narukami Shrine.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the shrine stands renovated, with Neko still waiting for Hibiki's return. Sealing the Warding Stones quieted the island's storm.

### STANDARD
Neko is a large black cat who can speak to people and says human ears cannot usually understand cats. She addresses the Traveler grandly, refers to herself as Her Ladyship, and sets them tasks. She expects Hibiki to return and is ready to introduce the Traveler and friends to her. She remains unaware that the village has been empty for hundreds of years. When she visits the Grand Narukami Shrine she is amazed by its offering box but insists her own shrine is better.

### LARGE
The Thunderbird, Kanna Kapatcir, was a monster once wrongly thought a guardian of Tsurumi Island; it destroyed the tribe there and Seirai Island, and was felled by the Electro Archon. A fragment of her power remained as the Thunder Manifestation. The four Warding Stones (two on the ground and two in the sky) keep the lingering regret in check; sealing them ends the storm. Hibiki's breaking of the wards released the manifestation.
