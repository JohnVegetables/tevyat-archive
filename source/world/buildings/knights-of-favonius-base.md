## summary
### LITE
The Knights of Favonius Base is the Knights' forward base at Dornman Port in Windrest Peak, built around the 5th (Ranged) Company. Detachments from the 6th (Logistics), 7th (Combined Arms), 8th (Support) and 10th (Investigation) Companies are stationed there too. "Knights of Favonius Base" is an unofficial name.
### STANDARD +
It opened to players with Windrest Peak in 6.5. Lohen, the Ranged Company's Vice Captain, is posted here.

## people
### LITE
Vice Captain Lohen commands the Ranged Company's people here, among them the snipers Gunther and Ursula.
### STANDARD
Residents named on the wiki: Ematol, the Investigative Team's weapons officer; Glenfidia of Logistics; Gunther and Ursula, Ranged Company snipers; Herstal and Koch, Support Company gunsmiths; and Phonia, a Combined Arms intelligence officer.
### LARGE +
Lohen loves fighting up close but admits that ranged firepower is the company's bread and butter. Observed scenes: he uses himself as the target, apple in hand, during shooting practice, with terrified Gunther and Ursula under threat of "special training" if they miss. He asks the Traveler to help choose between roasting Electro Slimes and freezing Hydro Slimes, saying he likes the plunge of a blade into solid ice. Herstal, the gunsmith, offers tea and runs marksmanship challenges, telling the Traveler that aim slips if practice is skipped for a day.

## places
@version: 6.5
### STANDARD
A compound with a shooting range, training dummies, and a bulletin board for Dornman Port requests. A knight watching the dummies praises the Honorary Knight's strength as the Traveler smashes them.
## history
### STANDARD
Dornman Port is a major trade hub that was an Abyss front line five hundred years ago. The Combined Arms Company's Captain Frederica Gunnhildr was ordered back to Mondstadt to help defend it. The base reflects the Knights' modern emphasis on firearms: the Support Company provides muskets, mostly for the Ranged Company's snipers.
### LARGE +
After the events of "A Knight's Legacy" (6.6), the Ranged Company's Captain seat is vacant for the time being, so the chain of command falls to the second-highest officer, Vice Captain Lohen.

### ULTRA +
Herstal offers the Traveler two timed marksmanship rounds, rewarding skill, and says the best marksmen grow up on lead and gunpowder. The Reconnaissance Company's Captain Eula separately patrols the long trade road between the port and the capital, so the base is part of a wider Knights presence in Windrest Peak.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the base is active and the Ranged Company remains stationed there.
