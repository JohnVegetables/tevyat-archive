## summary
### LITE
With Wind Comes Glory is a popular souvenir shop in Mondstadt City, run by Marjorie. Adventurers can pawn precious items there for Anemo Sigils and spend the Sigils on rare goods.
### STANDARD +
Its map description calls it a popular souvenir shop and a place for able adventurers to pawn treasures, with Marjorie at their service.

## places
### STANDARD
A shopfront with Marjorie standing just outside it. By day a music box tune plays from inside, louder the closer you stand.
### LARGE +
Beyond souvenirs she runs a curiosities section selling gadgets for Mora, among them a Windblume Festival balloon, a Windsong Lyre and a Wind-Blessed Harpastum. In the souvenir section Anemo Sigils buy ascension materials, Mora and rarer items.

## people
### STANDARD
Marjorie is Cyrus's daughter and Iris's niece. She opened the shop to share the stories behind the treasures her father found on his adventures. She now buys treasures from adventurers and resells them as souvenirs, and orders specialised gear for them on request.
### LARGE +
She has orange hair and eyes, a gray shirt and a dark blue skirt. Her idle lines invite customers to look around and pass on gossip about the strange wanderer near Wolvendom, who is Razor; during the Of Ballads and Brews event she says he has been seen in Springvale.
### ULTRA +
During the Windblume's Breath event, a limited-time event, she reacts to talk of a flower that is not of this world.

## culture
### STANDARD
The shop treats keepsakes as stories: Marjorie's aim is to share the tales behind her father's finds. Her counter trades in Anemo Sigils rather than Mora for its rarer souvenirs.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
A cheerful, curio-filled shop with a music box tinkling in the daylight.

## travel
### STANDARD
In the market district of Mondstadt City.
