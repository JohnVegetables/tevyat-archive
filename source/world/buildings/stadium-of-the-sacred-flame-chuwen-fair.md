## summary
### LITE
The fair along the northern side of the Stadium of the Sacred Flame in the Basin of Unnumbered Flames, Natlan, on the Fair and Temple map level.
### STANDARD +
It is a market area with several vendors and an Adventurers' Guild receptionist. A Saurian Relics Association message board stands here.

## places
### STANDARD
Part of the same Fair and Temple level as the Temple of Pax section. Interactable: the Saurian Relics Association Message Board.

## people
### STANDARD
Acat owns Frida's Palette; Agemo is a delivery courier for tournaments only; Katheryne is the Adventurers' Guild receptionist; Mansa is an adventurer; Siyah manages the Saurian Relics Association store. Other listed NPCs without stated roles: Bagayogo, Ichca, Kanaloa, Kane, Sibu.
### LARGE +
Vendors listed are Bounama, Cintli, Cipac, Echuah and Siyah.

## culture
### ULTRA
Chuwen is the eleventh day of the Maya Tzolk'in calendar, represented by the howler monkey, as the wiki notes.
