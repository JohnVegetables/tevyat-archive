## summary
### LITE
The VIP Lounge is a room on the second floor of the House of Hesperides, the mountain manor of the Snegovik in Fellfrost Peak, Snezhnaya, set aside for honoured guests. A guard at the door admits only holders of a VIP Pass, a gilded letter said to be issued by the manor's mistress. Inside, a main hall and a side room hold tables for guests who come to drink and rest.
### STANDARD +
Ordinary visitors are turned away politely. The Traveler can get in by finding a lost pass, after which the lounge becomes the setting of a small seating puzzle.

## places
### STANDARD
The lounge has a main hall, nearer the bar, and a side room with a gap in one wall. Only two tables are free in the whole lounge. A hat-wearing Jack Frost by the entrance offers a catching game (a time trial).
### LARGE +
The Hidden Exploration Objective "Please, Be Seated" (the name is unofficial) is played here. Prime Ice Cubes can be used to block the gap in the side room. Completing the objective, and the Jack Frost game, rewards chests.

## people
### STANDARD
A guard checks passes, and waiting staff look after guests. The guard is startled that a human holds a pass. Named guests: Zlatov, who wants a quiet table away from the gap in the wall; Iskelina, a hard drinker who wants the main hall; the sisters Koshakova and Khmelyova from Novokitezhgrad; and Lazoreva. Abuzova, a harried staff member, appears only during the objective.
### LARGE +
Zlatov is anxious that someone might peek at him through the gap. Iskelina rarely visits the manor and wants to drink more than usual. Koshakova is happy to sit with anyone, but her younger sister Khmelyova will not let her sit alone with a man and refuses to share a small table with two other people. Abuzova fears offending important guests and her superior Aglaya, and is thankful for a guest who solves problems instead of causing them.

## governance
### LARGE
The manor belongs to the Snegovik, and the lounge is under the authority of its mistress, Pavlina, whose pass admits guests; the guests refer to it as her VIP Room. Staff report to Aglaya.
### ULTRA +
The House of Hesperides itself was built thousands of years ago by the first Grand Duchess of the Snegovik, after the Belyi Tsar unified the north and set up the Pale Star Court, and Pavlina is its Grand Duchess. Abuzova's remark that many Snegovik who seldom visit are flocking to the manor for the wedding is the stated reason the lounge is short of seats.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Quiet and slightly tipsy, with polite staff, murmured complaints about tables, and stray drinkers challenging each other. It is crowded with unusual guests because the manor is busy with preparations for a wedding.

## quests
### LARGE
"Please, Be Seated" starts at the lounge door. Steps: talk to a Snegovik waiter and a Snegovik who has had too much to drink, find the VIP Pass, show it to the guard, talk to Abuzova, and settle the four guests' seating so each is content. The reward is a chest.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the lounge is accessible to anyone who finds a pass.
### STANDARD +
Nothing about the room has changed since its release in 7.0.

## entry: VIP Pass
@keys: VIP Pass, VIP Lounge Guard
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
The VIP Pass is a quest item described as a gilded letter said to be issued by the master of the mountain manor, Pavlina. Those bearing it were once frequently invited to the House of Hesperides. It allows a brief respite in the VIP Lounge. In "Please, Be Seated" it is hidden near a drunken guest and her waiter, and appears only once they have been spoken to.
### LARGE +
When the Traveler shows the pass, the guard remarks that he wonders whether such a pass has ever been granted to a human, but says his job is only to verify and stand guard, and lets them in.
