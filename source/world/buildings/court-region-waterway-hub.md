## summary
### LITE
The Court Region Waterway Hub is the aquabus main station in the centre of the Court of Fontaine. It serves the Clementine Line to Romaritime Harbor, the Navia Line to the Opera Epiclese and Marcotte Station, and the Callas Line to the Fontaine Research Institute.
### STANDARD +
The Callas Line is suspended indefinitely according to the station notice, so only the other two lines run for travellers.

## places
### STANDARD
The hub has several floors: the main hall on the ground floor, the Clementine Line platform on 2F, the Navia Line on 3F and Palais Mermonia on 4F. An information clerk and a maintenance worker staff the main hall.
### LARGE +
A notice board near the 2F elevator lists the lines. The Navia Line terminus is Marcotte Station, in the Erinnyes area.

## people
### STANDARD
Named staff include the information clerk Sylvie, maintenance worker Pruniere and guide Grosley; Esteban, a stenographer, is on the Palais Mermonia floor. Melusine guides narrate the ride.

## history
@version: 4.0
@spoiler: minor
### LARGE
The system was built under Callas, father of Navia, with the Court and the Research Institute, to improve relations between the Court and Poisson; the lines carry the names Clementine (his late wife), Navia and Callas. The Callas Line was lost when an explosion struck the Research Institute.
### ULTRA +
After the Prophecy of Fontaine, a plot by Romeuf's faction against the Clementine Line was foiled by the Traveler and Navia, who then resolved to rebuild the Callas Line and add stops.

## travel
### STANDARD
Riders call an aquabus from the bench if none waits. The Clementine Line goes to Romaritime Harbor and the Navia Line to Marcotte Station.

## status
### LITE
Active at the cutoff (7.1) with two lines running.
