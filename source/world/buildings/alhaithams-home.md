## summary
### LITE
Alhaitham's Home is the house where Alhaitham lives with Kaveh, whom he calls only his roommate. It sits near Treasures Street in Sumeru City. Its interior is closed to players except in a few quests. It was first an Akademiya research center and became a home after Kaveh gave up his claim to it.
### STANDARD +
Kaveh pays rent to Alhaitham and handles the housework; the two bicker over the furniture, which Kaveh finds ugly. The arrangement is a secret Kaveh tries to keep from visitors.

## places
### STANDARD
Inside are a central living room, a study to the right, and hallways with a painting, a broom, a red floor cushion and a shelf of books. Two further doors cannot be opened.

## people
### STANDARD
Alhaitham, who bought the furnishings, and Kaveh, who has his own room. Kaveh retreats to his room when Alhaitham has guests and avoids answering the door when Alhaitham is out, to avoid being discovered living there.

## history
### STANDARD
The Akademiya first provided the building as a research center for the joint research of Alhaitham and Kaveh. It fell into disuse for lack of a shared vision and manpower, was allotted to the two, and became a home after Kaveh forfeited his rights. Kaveh later sold his own home and possessions to fund the second construction of the Palace of Alcazarzaray and went bankrupt, drinking at Lambad's Tavern until Alhaitham met him there and offered a room on condition that he pay rent.
### LARGE +
Kaveh then offered to do all the domestic duties, such as tidying. Despite being in effect Kaveh's landlord, Alhaitham calls him only his roommate. In the quest "A Toast to Victory", Alhaitham mentions accidentally taking both sets of house keys. The two keys reflect their owners: Alhaitham's is a plain silver key with a green and silver bow; Kaveh's is gold with a lion-shaped keychain, echoing his darshan, the Kshahrewar.

## quests
### STANDARD
Enterable in Alhaitham's Story Quest "Pride and Prejudice" (Vultur Volans Chapter, 3.4) and in Kaveh's hangout quest "Past Mementos". It also appears in the limited-time event quest "Suspicions Aroused", part of the 3.6 event A Parade of Providence.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
A lived-in, slightly cluttered scholar's house, with books piled up waiting to be sorted and a running quarrel over decor between two men who would never call themselves friends.

## status
### STANDARD
As of 7.1 both men still live here as far as the pages consulted show.

## trivia
### ULTRA
Kaveh's room is used only when Alhaitham has guests. The decor dispute is a running gag: Kaveh wants to be consulted before more furniture is bought and in Pride and Prejudice complains about a boring wood carving. In Competition on the Sands, Kaveh also avoids answering the door when Alhaitham is out.
