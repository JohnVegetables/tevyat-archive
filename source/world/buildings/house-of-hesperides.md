## summary
### LITE
The House of Hesperides is a remote mountain manor on Fellfrost Peak in Snezhnaya, the home of the Snegovik (snow fae) and their Grand Duchess. It was built thousands of years ago, and until Bell Ringing at Dusk it belonged to Pavlina, the Grand Duchess of the Snegurochka, who ruled its other residents.
### STANDARD +
In the quest series Hesperides of Love and Hate the manor hosts a 'wedding' that is really a ritual to give the Snegovik souls, with a human sacrifice as its price.

## geography
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
The manor stands alone on a snowy summit above the cloud line, reached by climbing the peak. It has a front hall, a main hall, a VIP lounge, an upper floor of private rooms and a rooftop, plus a chapel used for ceremonial 'rituals'. A garden and its courtyard surround the building, guarded by Jack Frosts.
### LARGE +
Higher up the mountain the Cloudborne Altar and the Cloudborne Palace lie beyond the manor, where the final ritual takes place.

## places
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
Front Hall (reception), Main Hall (the wedding and entertainment), VIP Lounge, Mountain Mansion 2F (private rooms with the brittle document written by Pavlina), a rooftop with a hidden attic, the Chapel and the Garden Bathed in Radiant Dew. Kresnik Kristall, Kresnik's Torches, Prime Ice and a Rumblebranch are all found here.
### LARGE +
The garden is out of bounds, 'under maintenance' except for fae help, and the Eye of Graeae's fragment is hidden there in the quest The Sun's Gilded Apple.

## people
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
Residents and staff are Snegovik, with a few visitors. Aglaya is a Snegovik receptionist who appears after Bell Ringing at Dusk; Ladon bars the way into the garden; other named residents include Bogdana, Liubov, Pletnev, Raduzhnaya, Safonov, Saltykov and Taisiia in the Front Hall. Pavlina was the Grand Duchess of the Snegurochka.
### LARGE +
Ikhor, a quest-exclusive young man whose appearance is that of the young Belyi Tsar and who is the 'groom' of the ritual, is one of the three fragments of the Eye of Graeae.

## governance
### STANDARD
The Grand Duchess of the Snegurochka rules from here. According to the document written by Pavlina, the Snegovik ancestor Morevna decreed that the one leading the Snegovik must have a complete soul, which can only be gained through true love. When Pavlina was installed as princess no such love was required, which angered her.
### LARGE +
Another Snegurochka, held in high esteem, told Pavlina at her ceremony that 'these things also happen'. Pavlina read this as meaning that Larisa's sacrifice had been pointless.

## culture
### STANDARD
A Snegovik's love is described in Pavlina's document as a sacrifice, a voluntary exchange in which one gives all and expects the beloved to give all in return. Prime Ice artisanry, ceremonial skill and etiquette are valued, as are flower-tending and elegance.
### LARGE +
Larisa, Pavlina's rival, said that the greatest misfortune in the world is to love no one at all.

## history
### LITE
According to the receptionist Raduzhnaya, the House of Hesperides was built thousands of years ago by the first Grand Duchess of the Snegovik, after the Belyi Tsar unified the north and set up the Pale Star Court.
### STANDARD +
Pavlina, who the wiki says had lived for centuries before the Cataclysm 500 years ago and was a researcher of Hvergelmir, later became Grand Duchess. She contended with Larisa for the title. Larisa brought a human to the manor to marry and complete her soul; he fled the ceremony and she died. Pavlina swore on Larisa's sword that her own wedding to Ikhor would succeed.
### LARGE +
The ritual repeated for an unknown number of times, trapping the manor in a loop of Pavlina's wedding day, until she forgot Ikhor had willingly helped and shackled him instead. Aksinya appeared and objected; Pavlina warned that her tricks would only gain time before another ritual.
### ULTRA +
In the last cycle Pavlina took Ikhor's power and ascended to the Cloudborne Palace, where Aksinya and the Traveler defeated her. As she disintegrated she asked Aksinya whether she had thought her own plan through and whether she had given 'him' a choice, leaving behind a fragment of the Eye of Graeae's power.

## atmosphere
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
The in-game viewpoint calls it 'a lonely mansion against the whiteout'. Guests are welcomed with entertainment and told the house's dos and don'ts; behind the festive surface is a ritual, and the mood tilts to uneasy once the ceremony begins.
### LARGE +
Rooftop notes by a human named Myshkin describe days that never end.

## dangers
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
Chimeric armoured beasts on the approach, Glacial Wardens and Glaciomancers (Snegovik warriors and mages) inside, and Jack Frosts in the garden. When the ritual runs out of control, the manor's Snegovik turn on visitors.
### LARGE +
Pavlina herself, the 'Guardian of the Hesperides', fights as a boss at the Cloudborne Altar.

## travel
### STANDARD
Visitors need an invitation, which an Invitation to the 'Ritual' provides; it can be obtained in several ways in the open world. After the quests the receptionist and other Snegovik welcome guests as normal. A Teleport Waypoint at the Frozen Fulgurbranch and the Cloudborne Altar are unlocked in the series.

## quests
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
The World Quest series Hesperides of Love and Hate: Through the Gates of Ivory, Procrustes' Iron Bed (with sub-quests House of the Soulless and The Sun's Gilded Apple) and Bell Ringing at Dusk. It runs alongside On One Side a Palace, On the Other a Tomb and In the Dwelling of Life, and leads into An Ark That Used to Be a Soul.
### LARGE +
In Through the Gates of Ivory, the Traveler follows Ikhor to the manor, shows the invitation at the statue, meets Aglaya, enjoys the entertainment and takes a seat in the chapel. In Procrustes' Iron Bed, Pavlina tries to sacrifice Ikhor but is thwarted by Aksinya, and out-of-control Snegovik must be defeated. In Bell Ringing at Dusk, the Traveler climbs through the clouds to the Cloudborne Altar and stops Pavlina.

## status
@version: 7.1
### STANDARD
Open as of 7.1. Pavlina is gone, the loop is broken and Aglaya works at the reception. Patch notes for 7.1 mention a Jack Frost catching challenge and a time trial offered by an NPC named Vershina in the manor.

## entry: Pavlina's Wedding Ritual
@keys: Pavlina, Grand Duchess Pavlina, Hesperides ritual
@version: 7.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Pavlina, Grand Duchess of the Snegurochka, performs a 'wedding' with Ikhor to gather the Eye of Graeae's scattered power and so give her people souls. The Snegovik lack souls, so she treats the marriage as a ritual of sacrifice. Aksinya and the Traveler stop it in Bell Ringing at Dusk.
### LARGE +
In the document written by Pavlina, her bitterness over the doomed ceremony of Larisa's husband drives her. She swears that hers will not fail.

## entry: Aglaya and Myshkin
@keys: Aglaya, Myshkin
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
Aglaya is a Snegovik receptionist at the manor. She fell in love with Myshkin, a human who stayed in the manor, and during Pavlina's looping ritual they promised to flee together once the wedding was over. Myshkin grew old and died in a hidden attic.
### LARGE +
Once the loop is broken Aglaya, who had forgotten his death, learns of it again. Notes found on the rooftop in his hand ask her to forget his name.

## entry: Document with Yellowed Pages
@keys: Document with Yellowed Pages, Larisa
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
A stiff, brittle document in the second-floor rooms, written in Pavlina's cold hand. She says she has no love for Larisa, who equalled her in Prime Ice artisanry and surpassed her in ceremony, and whom every Snegovik loved. She watched Larisa choose a human lover, resented him, and then watched him flee the ritual, after which Larisa's body dissolved into snow in the storm.
### LARGE +
After becoming princess, Pavlina resolves to find a way out of the rules that bind her kin, 'for Larisa, for whom I held no love'.
