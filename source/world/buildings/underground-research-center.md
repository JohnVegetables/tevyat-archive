## summary
### LITE
The Underground Research Center is a classified Fatui research site below the Kuuvahki Experimental Design Bureau on Paha Isle in Nod-Krai. It holds experiments on kuuvahki, the lunar energy, and collects data from three surface hubs to study how to generate high-density energy barriers.
### STANDARD +
The Traveler finds it through the world quest Priorities First (Version 6.0). It is a Strong Kuuvahki Environment. A notice there says Command raised the alert level because of recent suspicious activity around the Design Bureau, to protect military secrets and personnel.

## geography
### STANDARD
The center is reached from the Paha Isle surface by an entrance and a long elevator shaft that descends well underground. The first area has a sealed barrier and moving barrier traps, and a further elevator leads deeper to the laboratory.
### LARGE +
The quest begins when the Traveler defeats the Cutting Edge All-Purpose Storm Landcruiser in the center. One door then stays shut, so the party takes another elevator deeper. Three data collection hubs on the surface of Paha Isle feed the center, each yielding a Data Storage Cassette.

## places
### STANDARD
The laboratory holds many jars of what looks like kuuvahki, a large jar in the center, piles of papers and experiment machines. Notices and notes here include an alert from Command raising the security level, a research log and a set of research records, all describing barrier tests.
### LARGE +
The research log mentions a tenth stability experiment on the barrier generator, a prototype now a third of its original size, and an energy bottleneck, and its writer's transfer request being refused because the project is highly classified. The research records state that harnessing kuuvahki to produce high-density energy barriers of various shapes, including spheres, has been proven feasible, but long, intense use causes overheating, material fatigue and slight energy leakage whose long-term effects are uncertain.

## people
### STANDARD
Only unseen researchers are present; one log writer complains about sharing a space with those who tinker with plants and animals. The Fatui officer Kholodov, a Podporuchik, arrives with soldiers when the party submits the data, and accuses them of being the criminals who ruined an experiment site.

## quests
### STANDARD
In Priorities First, the party gathers three data cassettes from the surface hubs, enters the lab and submits them. A large barrier traps them inside and Kholodov's Fatui attack; the Traveler breaks the barrier and defeats them. Afterwards the machine shows no result, and a white Weasel Thief steals Paimon's Mora.
### LARGE +
The quest ends with the thief running off. Later, at Speranza, the Conch Gang children catch it and return the Mora. The quest also awards the achievement Tempus Fugit, Pecunia Fugit.
### ULTRA +
Kholodov's group in the lab consists of two Oprichniki Vanguards, two Oprichniki Line Troopers and an Oprichniki Hailstorm Cannoneer, with Kholodov listed as the Podporuchik among them.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Sealed and moving barriers, the Cutting Edge All-Purpose Storm Landcruiser at the entrance, Force Recon Scout Landcruisers around the data hubs, and the Fatui who arrive when the data is submitted.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
A sunless place of glass jars, machines and dry bureaucratic notes. Paimon jokes that the lack of sunlight is meant to stop workers from knowing when to clock off.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 it remains a Fatui research facility; the quest's results were never revealed.

The three data collection hubs on Paha Isle store experiment data on cassettes. The Traveler collects them and inputs them into the lab's computer. The data tracks high-density energy barrier generation; submitting it triggers a barrier that traps the party inside.
