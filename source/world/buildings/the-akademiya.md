## summary
### LITE
The Akademiya is the main building of the Sumeru Akademiya, the prestigious institute of learning and governing body of Sumeru. It is built into the middle of the Divine Tree that carries Sumeru City, above the Grand Bazaar and below the Sanctuary of Surasthana, and holds the House of Daena library at its heart. Scholars, students and officials of the Six Darshans work here. For centuries it was ruled by six sages whose control of knowledge, through the Akasha System, went far beyond teaching.
### STANDARD +
Under the sages the Akademiya regulated information and secretly collaborated with the Fatui to create a false god. After the plan was stopped in Chapter III Act V (3.2), four of the six sages were removed.

## geography
### STANDARD
The Akademiya sits in the middle of the Divine Tree, between the Grand Bazaar and ordinary buildings at the bottom and the Sanctuary of Surasthana at the very top. Its doors open on their own as visitors approach. Inside, paths lead up the tree through places such as Razan Garden, and the House of Daena stands at the centre.
### LARGE +
The Grand Sage's office is above the House of Daena, reached by a lift on a raised platform in the library; after the quest Jnagarbha Day the lift is shown going up to the office or down to the confinement room. The confinement room is a completely enclosed space inside the Akademiya, close to the Sanctuary. The Divine Tree is old enough that, in an NPC's joke, it has seen more years than there are rice grains in all the pilaf in Port Ormos, and Amurta students record the state of its roots.
### ULTRA +
Height in Sumeru City is also a social ladder: the bottom is for common folk and moving up means promotion or higher education. A speaker who says "down below" means the foot of the tree. Many areas have their own message boards, including the Akademiya Public Message Board in Razan Garden.

## places
### STANDARD
House of Daena: the largest library in Teyvat, described on the map as once the bank of all the world's knowledge, now overshadowed by the Akasha System but still a symbol of Sumeru. Razan Garden: a serene garden up the tree with gazebos and Padisarah, entered from the Akademiya's doors; its name is Persian for "vines" and the Chinese name means a quiet garden.
### LARGE +
Beyond the building: the Bimarstan, a free public hospital staffed by Amurta; Pardis Dhyai, Amurta's botany research centre; the Grand Bazaar and Treasures Street; and the Corps of Thirty, an Eremite mercenary brigade under permanent contract with the sages to keep the peace in Sumeru City. In the House of Daena, borrowing rules say only students and teachers may take books, and some volumes are restricted to higher-level researchers.
### ULTRA +
Books in the library include the Scroll of Streaming Song, The Folio of Foliage and The Tale of Shiruyeh and Shirin; quest-item books kept there include An Ode to Khvarena, The Lay of Al-Ahmar and The Shepherd and the Magic Bottle. A researcher's note found in one chest jokes about donating 5,000 Mora each time a thesis is put off.

## people
### STANDARD
The Six Great Sages head the Akademiya, one for each Darshan, and one of them is the Grand Sage. The Mahamata are civil administrators and the Matra enforce discipline.
### LARGE +
Researchers hold the ranks of Driyosh (travelling scholars), Dastur (those who passed two theses) and Herbad (prestigious senior researchers). Seen near the entrance and inside: the mercenary Hana of the Corps of Thirty, researchers and students such as Khaldun, Sitt, Yasmin, Yavanani, Ziryab, Hawa, Iris, Shatir and Zinat, and in the House of Daena the librarian Katayoun and the international student Gregor. Panah the Mahamata appears in the library after Chapter III Act V.
### ULTRA +
Notable alumni and associates include Tighnari (a graduate of Amurta, student of Naphis), Alhaitham (enrolled at seven, now Scribe), Kaveh, Cyno (the General Mahamatra, of Spantamad), Lisa (who graduated in two years and was called their best student in two hundred years, then left for Mondstadt), and the expelled scholar Il Dottore, who as a Harbinger returned and offered the sages a new god. Tsuyuko, Henry Morton and Signora are mentioned as having studied there.

## governance
### STANDARD
Sumeru's day-to-day affairs are run from the Akademiya. It is divided into six Darshans, each led by a sage, and the Grand Sage is the central leader among them. Its rules rest on three virtues and six sins.
### LARGE +
The Darshans, each with a specialty and uniform colour, are Amurta (biology, green), Rtawahist (illuminationism, blue), Spantamad (elementalism, red), Haravatat (semiotics, black), Vahumana (aetiology, yellow) and Kshahrewar (technology, white). The three virtues, from Sumeru's talent books, are Admonition, Ingenuity and Praxis, likened to branches, leaf-veins and roots of the nation of wisdom. The six sins, banned to protect scholars from careless pursuit of knowledge, are interfering with human evolution, tampering with life and death, delving beyond the universe, investigating the origin of words, revering gods without acts of devotion, and attempting the forbidden and fearing none. Breaking these invites expulsion or imprisonment.
### ULTRA +
Other offices: the Mahamata (Akademiya alumni sent across Sumeru to oversee information, decrees, trade and healthcare; the Scribe belongs here), the Matra (disciplinary officials, including inspection for illegal Canned Knowledge), the Navbed (port and tax affairs), the Vizier (administration in the Grand Bazaar and Treasures Street) and the Temple of Silence, which the wiki says was a front, the true order having left for the desert four hundred years ago (revealed in the quest Oathkeeper). Mechanical-life research is banned after past cruelty in animal vivisection.

## culture
### STANDARD
The Akademiya admits students who pass its entrance exams, children included at schooling age. Degrees are famously long and stressful, with the thesis defence the hardest hurdle.
### LARGE +
Students call each other seniors and juniors by academic progress rather than age, and those who cannot enrol rely on the Akasha, independent study or training centres. The Akademiya's views shaped Sumeru: it disparaged art as impractical, which spread to the public, marginalising performers, dancers and designers; even after Chapter III, talk of the arts is called a quick way to kill a conversation. Each year the Pir Kavikavus Prize goes to a promising scholar under forty.
### ULTRA +
Students may take elective courses outside their own Darshan, and there is a swimming elective. The habitual liar Kasim claims some sages are officially affiliated with the Fatui, and the Amurta sage Siman Farrokhzad has ties to an Eremite tribe that once worked for the Fatui; the wiki cautions that little evidence supports a wider link.

## history
### STANDARD
The Akademiya was founded by Greater Lord Rukkhadevata with its first Darshans, and was already prestigious by the cataclysm five hundred years ago. After her death, the sages, disappointed with the new god Kusanali's lack of obvious wisdom, locked her in the Sanctuary of Surasthana, took control of the Akasha System, and ran the nation themselves.
### LARGE +
In Chapter III, Il Dottore returned and offered to make a new god. Azar and three other sages accepted; Naphis of Amurta and Baladhuri of Vahumana refused and were imprisoned. The plan involved harvesting divine knowledge from crazed scholars, a dream samsara run through the Akasha, and misinformation about King Deshret's revival, with Scaramouche modified as the vessel. The sages banned unapproved information, then public performances, which led to the Sabzeruz Festival crisis. In Dreams, Emptiness, Deception, Dottore also used the Akasha to stage a mob scene at the Akademiya's entrance and had the sage's secretary Setaria imprisoned.
### ULTRA +
Jnagarbha Day, when new information is entered into the Akasha, was chosen for the rescue. Alhaitham led the Traveler in through the front door and into the House of Daena, where Azar caught them, exiled Alhaitham to Aaru Village and jailed the Traveler in the confinement room; this was a staged ploy. Nilou's dance outside drew attention, the forged order drew away the guards, and Cyno forced Azar to answer to Nahida. Afterwards the four sages involved were removed and exiled to the Avidya Forest to cultivate their wisdom, Naphis and Baladhuri were freed, and Nahida personally oversaw the choice of new sages. Nahida then halted the Akasha System.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
A scholarly place where wisdom is also pressure: students in the colours of different Darshans pass by, researchers worry about theses, and mercenaries of the Corps of Thirty keep order. Under the sages it was watchful and controlling over information; after Chapter III it is a looser, less certain institution.
### LARGE +
The House of Daena at the centre is vast and, per the wiki, used less than before because people relied on the Akasha. Razan Garden is serene, with gazebos and the public message board.

## dangers
### STANDARD
The risks are social and informational: the Matra punish forbidden research and Canned Knowledge, the Akasha once shaped what people were allowed to know, and entry to certain places, such as the Sanctuary, was restricted.

## travel
### STANDARD
The Akademiya is reached by climbing the Divine Tree from the Grand Bazaar. Doors lead up to Razan Garden and on to the Sanctuary of Surasthana.

## quests
### STANDARD
Chapter III of the Archon Quests: Dreams, Emptiness, Deception (3.1) and Akasha Pulses, the Kalpa Flame Rises (3.2), especially Jnagarbha Day. 

## status
### STANDARD
As of 7.1 the Akademiya continues as Sumeru's centre of learning and administration under reformed leadership. The sages who planned the false god were removed after 3.2; Naphis is Grand Sage, with Baladhuri, Alkindi, Firnas, Shakir and Rushd as the other sages (as shown in 6.6). Nahida halted the Akasha after Chapter III; during the 6.6 Irminsul crisis it was briefly restarted so the allies could overload Irminsul, and it is not shown back in everyday use. The sages' history of controlling information still shapes habits.
### LARGE +
The old assumption that knowledge could be obtained from the Akasha has been replaced by the slower route of study; training centres and the Mahamata remain.

## entry: House of Daena
@keys: House of Daena, Daena
@version: 3.0
### STANDARD
The largest library in Teyvat, at the heart of the Akademiya. Run by a Grand Conservator who procures and maintains the collection; the previous holder retired, and Rifaet and Vikram were the candidates. Shatir calls Lisa "Mondstadt's Grand Conservator" and says she would have been his first choice had she stayed. Daena is a Zoroastrian term for insight and revelation.
### LARGE +
Only students and teachers may borrow books. A lift on the central platform goes to the Grand Sage's office and the confinement room after Jnagarbha Day. In Chapter III the Traveler and Alhaitham go into the library to lie low before Azar finds them.

## entry: Razan Garden
@keys: Razan Garden
@version: 3.0
### STANDARD
A calm garden on the path up the Divine Tree, reached by the Akademiya's left or right doors, with gazebos and Padisarah. Researchers and students such as Darya, Farghani, Homayun, Makhfi, Muganna, Qushji, Arina and Armin are found here. The Akademiya Public Message Board stands in the garden.

## entry: Grand Sage's office and the confinement room
@keys: Grand Sage's office, confinement room
@keys2: Akademiya, Azar, Alhaitham, Jnagarbha Day
@version: 3.2
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Above the House of Daena sits the Grand Sage's office, with the Grand Sage's console, through which restricted commands are executed; on Jnagarbha Day the sages load research and decrees into Knowledge Capsules for entry into the Akasha. Below it is the confinement room, a sealed chamber close to the Sanctuary where Azar jails the Traveler. From there the Traveler wakes Nahida's consciousness with a modified Akasha Terminal.
### LARGE +
Alhaitham's plan treats the arrest as the point: the sages would think the Traveler contained, lowering their guard. Dehya frees the Traveler later.

## entry: The Six Darshans
@keys: Six Darshans, Darshan, Six Great Schools
@keys2: Akademiya, sage
@version: 3.0
### STANDARD
Amurta (biology, green) is the Darshan of Tighnari and Naphis and runs the Bimarstan and Pardis Dhyai. Rtawahist (illuminationism, blue) was Azar's. Spantamad (elementalism, red) was Cyno's and Cyrus's. Haravatat (semiotics, black) was Khajeh's. Vahumana (aetiology, yellow) is Baladhuri's. Kshahrewar (technology, white) is Firnas's per the wiki. The Chinese names give each a descriptive name and a phonetic name; the English uses the phonetic ones.
### LARGE +
The first Darshans were founded with the Akademiya as Six Great Schools by Rukkhadevata, each representing a type of wisdom. Others arose later but only the original six lasted. The Order of Skeptics, called Nagarjunites by the Akademiya, was once a Darshan and long ago cut its ties.

## entry: Virtues and sins of the Akademiya
@keys: six sins, three virtues, Admonition, Ingenuity, Praxis
@keys2: Akademiya, sages
@version: 3.0
### STANDARD
The three virtues are Admonition, Ingenuity and Praxis; the six sins are listed in the governance section. They form the basis of the Akademiya's rules, to which the sages have added new rules and amendments ever since. Researching sinful topics can result in expulsion or imprisonment.

## entry: The sages' false god scheme
@keys: false god, new god, sages' plan, Divine Knowledge Capsule
@keys2: Akademiya, Dottore, Scaramouche, Azar
@version: 3.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Four of the six sages, led by Grand Sage Azar, agreed with Il Dottore to build a replacement god. They harvested divine knowledge from crazed scholars at a secret facility, used the Akasha to run a dream samsara, and spread false claims of King Deshret's return. Naphis and Baladhuri opposed and were locked up. Scaramouche, delivered by Dottore, was modified as the vessel.
### LARGE +
The scheme collapsed when Nahida was freed and the Traveler defeated the made god, Shouki no Kami. The sages were removed afterwards.

## entry: The Akademiya after Chapter III
@keys: new sages, Acting Grand Sage, Grand Sage Naphis
@keys2: Akademiya, Alhaitham, Nahida, Naphis
@version: 6.6
### STANDARD
After the victory Azar and the three sages who joined him were removed, Naphis and Baladhuri were released and four new sages were to be chosen under Nahida's supervision. Alhaitham was offered Grand Sage, refused and served as Acting Grand Sage before resigning to return as Scribe, leaving the Akademiya briefly without a clear leader. By the 6.6 Archon Quest (A Divergence Leading to Oneself) Naphis is Grand Sage and introduces the new sages Alkindi (Spantamad), Firnas (Kshahrewar), Shakir (Rtawahist) and Rushd (Haravatat), alongside Baladhuri (Vahumana); when he was appointed is not shown.

## entry: Akademiya Extravaganza
@keys: Akademiya Extravaganza, Interdarshan Championship, Wisdom Gala
@version: 3.6
@availability: limited
### LARGE
A festival run by the Akademiya: Darshans set up booths around Sumeru City for the annual Wisdom Gala, and every four years each Darshan sends one representative to the Interdarshan Championship (sages may not take part). It is shown in the limited-time 3.6 event A Parade of Providence, where Kaveh (Kshahrewar) became champion over Tighnari, Faruzan, Layla, Cyno and the Wanderer.
