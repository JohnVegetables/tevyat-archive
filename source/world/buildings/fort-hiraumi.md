## summary
### LITE
Fort Hiraumi is a subarea at the eastern tip of Seirai Island, the Inazuman island scarred by a long-ago calamity and a perpetual thunderstorm. It is a fort-like site on the island, roamed by Nobushi, Kairagi and Treasure Hoarders and home to the Slumbering Court, a domain. The in-game map calls it Hiraumi-toride.
### STANDARD +
Nothing in the game text says who built the fort or what it once guarded. What canon shows is its present state: a stormy, lightning-charged place with Electro puzzles, Thunder Sakura Boughs and Phase Gates, with an abandoned mansion on its grounds that the domain description calls the haunt of monsters from the rift.

## geography
### STANDARD
The fort lies on Seirai Island, the island whose centre was once wrapped in constant storm and Balethunder until the Seirai Stormchasers quests were completed. Electro runs through the whole area. Features listed for the subarea include Electro Cubes, Electro Seelie, Lightning Strike Probes, light-up tile puzzles, Thunder Barriers, Thunder Sakura Boughs, Phase Gates, a Waverider Waypoint and bake-danuki. Local specialties include Amakumo Fruit, Naku Weed and Sea Ganoderma.
### LARGE +
One of the Seirai Stormchasers' Warding Stones is reached from Fort Hiraumi: the Traveler enters a Phase Gate south of the fort's southern Teleport Waypoint and climbs floating stones to a waypoint where the stone waits. This is a quest-time route: the floating rocks that carry the player up are despawned during Part IV, which finishes the series.

## places
### STANDARD
The main landmark is the Slumbering Court, the domain on the fort's grounds. Its official description calls it a masterless mansion that has become the haunting ground of rift monsters, and it rewards those who master its Electro trial with artifacts. A cat-shaped hidden objective, "The Cat's Affection" (an unofficial name), is located to the right of the domain and needs the recipe from the quest Neko Is a Cat: Shrine Recipe.
### LARGE +
In "The Cat's Affection" the player feeds the Delicious Invigorating Kitty Meal to a cat on a paw-shaped rock once a day. After each daily reset the kitten shifts to the next toe, and on the last day it produces a chest. The recipe for the meal comes from Kiminami Anna through Neko's tasks to revive the Asase Shrine.

## people
### STANDARD
There are no named residents. The area is held by Nobushi (Hitsukeban, Jintouban and Kikouban), Kairagi, Treasure Hoarders, Hilichurl Shooters and Mirror Maidens among the elite enemies.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Electro Slimes and Whopperflowers, Nobushi and Kairagi patrols, Treasure Hoarder gangs, specters, Crackling and Blazing Axe Mitachurls, Mirror Maidens and Ruin Machines (Ruin Cruisers and Scouts). Lightning-charged terrain and Thunder Barriers shape how safely anyone can move.
### LARGE +
The Daily Commission "A Gentleman Strikes in Broad Daylight" has a Fort Hiraumi version in which the Traveler must filch supplies from a Treasure Hoarder camp west of the waypoint; the commission "Operation Thunderous Ignition" starts at the island's eastmost tip, where Kairagi appear and the Thunder Dwellings can be used to charge Electro attacks.

## history
### STANDARD
No history of the fort itself is given. The island around it is said to have suffered a calamity that left its centre in a perpetual thunderstorm.
### LARGE +
Before the Seirai Stormchasers quests, the centre of the island was constantly afflicted with Balethunder and targeted lightning, and exploration at the edge was hampered by storms.
### ULTRA +
In the Seirai Stormchasers series the Adventurers' Guild scouts Etsuko and Taisuke, with the Traveler's help and the guidance of the talking cat Neko of the Asase Shrine, seal four Warding Stones, one reached via Fort Hiraumi. The storm then passes, though Electrified Water remains in the middle of the island and can be cleared by a puzzle. The series also unlocks the Thunder Manifestation boss.

## travel
### STANDARD
Fort Hiraumi has a Teleport Waypoint and a Waverider Waypoint. Fort Hiraumi and Seirai Island were added in version 2.1.

## quests
### STANDARD
The fort appears in the Seirai Stormchasers world quest series (Part III's Warding Stone route), the Neko Is a Cat shrine recipe chain, the commissions mentioned above, and a Torn Page, "Toki Alley Tales (III)", whose location is listed as east of the fort.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Electro everywhere: lightning probes, Thunder Barriers and Thunder Sakura Boughs, with hostile patrols and the shadow of the haunted mansion nearby. It is an island that bears the marks of a calamity and a storm that raged for a long time.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 Fort Hiraumi is an open, hostile exploration area on Seirai Island; the wiki records no change since 2.1.

## entry: Slumbering Court Domain
@keys: Slumbering Court

### STANDARD
The Slumbering Court is an Electro-themed domain on Seirai Island. Its in-game description calls it a masterless mansion that has become the haunting ground of monsters from the rift, and says those who can wield the eternal lightning to conquer its trial will win artifacts. Thundercraven Rifthounds are among its enemies, and Electro-Charged is the effective way to fight them. The domain's own page is the place for its gameplay detail; the lore tie to the fort is not explained.
