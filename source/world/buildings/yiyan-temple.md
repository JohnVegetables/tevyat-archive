## summary
### LITE
Yiyan Temple is a temple in Liyue Harbor dedicated to the Geo Archon, Morax. His worshippers go there to offer incense and prayers. Before the events of Of the Land Amidst Monoliths, the yearly Rite of Descension took place here.
### STANDARD +
The Chinese name, Yiyan-dian, translates as the 'Rock-Reliant Temple'.

## places
### STANDARD
A temple on the higher parts of Liyue Harbor, near Yujing Terrace.

## people
### STANDARD
Fengyan, a Millelith sergeant, is posted near it, as is Bu'yun, a guide at the Jade Chamber, and Musheng during the day.
### LARGE +
Fengyan is known for unarmed combat and teaches martial arts to Millelith soldiers. He patrols the area around the temple to see that the Rite of Descension is safe and properly done.

## religion
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Prayer and incense offerings to Morax. The yearly Rite of Descension was the occasion on which Rex Lapis gave guidance on the economic direction Liyue should follow in the coming year, per the quest description.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
A place of worship with a Millelith presence.

## travel
### STANDARD
In the upper part of Liyue Harbor, around Yujing Terrace.
### ULTRA +
In the Archon Quest, the player is sent to pray and wish at Yujing Terrace and to wait for the Rite to begin with the crowd; the wiki's Yiyan Temple page places the Rite at the temple itself.

## status
### LITE
Still a temple to Morax as of 7.1, though the Rite no longer takes place there as before.
