## summary
### LITE
The Kalimi Exchange is Sumeru City's stock exchange and marketplace, on Treasures Street. One can buy futures contracts here, and a bulletin board beside the building posts goods for sale. The Mahamata Barmak, a researcher with an economics degree, monitors it for illegal or fraudulent practice. A man who calls himself a millionaire, Hatim, stands outside during the day.
### STANDARD +
In Port Ormos, the Wikala Funduq trade supervisor Dilawar tells casual traders to "try your luck" at the Exchange, while warning that profit does not come easily.

## people
### STANDARD
Barmak is a Mahamata, an Akademiya official, posted at the Exchange. He welcomes "all law-abiding businessmen", says illegal trade is strictly prohibited, and says that as an economics researcher no speculation gets past him. In his private thoughts he is irritated by cocky youngsters who think they can get away with anything on his watch. Hatim is a self-proclaimed millionaire seen outside by day and inside Puspa Cafe at night. He calls the Exchange a place where people's strength, courage and insight are tested, and complains that its forecasters are amateurs.
### LARGE +
Hatim claims to be the descendant of the oldest aristocratic family of the Great Red Sand, whose treasury holds the Dendro Archon's legacy and King Deshret's relics; he says the Homayani family have nothing on his. Others do not believe he is rich. He says his aim is to increase the family fortune so that his children never have to become merchants.

## quests
### LARGE
Hatim is the client of the repeatable Daily Commission Gold Devouring and Mora Gathering, in which he chases a "Golden Dish", a meal he believes raises diners' affinity for Mora. Follow-up versions have him lose everything, make a small profit, go bargain-hunting for Apple Cider, or strike a fortune that takes him to Lambad's Tavern. The commission is a gameplay feature rather than major lore.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
A bustling, orderly market: notice boards, people comparing forecasts and Barmak's watchful eye. Confidence and bluster are common; reliable fortunes are rare.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the Exchange is open and unchanged.
### ULTRA +
No quest or event up to 7.1 changes the Exchange, Barmak or Hatim. Per the wiki's etymology note, Kalimi is a Telugu word meaning riches or wealth.
