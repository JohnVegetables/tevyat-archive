## summary
### LITE
The Narzissenkreuz Ordo, as a place, is the hidden meeting chamber of the secret society of the same name. It sits at the top of the Tower of Ipsissimus in Fontaine. In its ceiling is a Doomsday Clock made by the mage Narzissenkreuz to count down to the apocalypse of the Prophecy of Fontaine.
### STANDARD +
The chamber is now empty of ceremonial items and has not seen a Dispelling Ritual for a long time, but Caterpillar recognises it as the place where the sinners of Fontaine once gathered. Ann, Caterpillar and Seymour use it as a base while looking into the Ordo's past. The map text calls it a place where great dreams were once born and countless souls melted.
### LARGE +
The society behind the room was founded over 400 years ago by Rene de Petrichor and Jakob Ingold, former members of the Narzissenkreuz Institute, who sought to merge the wills of many people into a Descender to survive the prophesied destruction. It was later declared an illegal extremist group and the tower sealed.
### ULTRA +
The wiki notes that the clock and the large round window resemble the Nod-Krai emblem, which also represents the Three Moons; this is an observation, not a stated link.

## geography
### STANDARD
The chamber is an instance entered by choosing "Read" on a book at the end of the Institute of Natural Philosophy's Central Quartier; the first entry unlocks it as a waypoint on the map. A second book on a music stand inside lets the player leave. Its ceiling holds the Doomsday Clock, and a large circular window lights the room.
### LARGE +
During the world quest Search in the Algae Sea, a vent on the south-west of the Tower of Ipsissimus roof opens as another route in, and a large crack in the floor becomes a second exit.

## people
### STANDARD
The Ordo's former members and leaders are described in their own entries. In the room today are Ann, Caterpillar and Seymour, who investigate the Ordo here. Caterpillar says he belonged to the Ordo but not to its inner circle, and that he was personally mentored by Narzissenkreuz.
### LARGE +
Members had clearance levels from one to four; honorary levels were given for significant contributions. Rene founded the Ordo with Jakob, who performed tricks to attract recruits from the Institute.

## culture
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Two notes found in the chamber after Search in the Algae Sea show the Ordo's outlook: one explains a ritual line about sinking into the great sea to be reborn, tied to a play about Ajax and an ancient Sumeru text phrase read as "primordial human"; the other argues that to excise the self is to die before death, so one can avoid receiving a Vision, which it treats as selling oneself to the fate of the world.
### LARGE +
The notes reflect a goal of becoming a will able to protect, sustain, destroy and create a world, distinct from a Descender.

## history
@version: 4.0
### STANDARD
The society formed after its founders learned of the coming destruction of the world, and held that merging many wills into one being was the way to survive it. Their later work, including research on the Primordial Sea, leads on to the events investigated in Search in the Algae Sea. See the faction entry for details.

## quests
@version: 4.0
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Riddles Awaiting Answers begins automatically when the Traveler first enters the chamber after Unfinished Comedy. Search in the Algae Sea, the only world quest of In the Wake of Narcissus Act I, is started here by talking to Ann, Caterpillar and Seymour two in-game days after Initial Facts.

## status
### STANDARD
As of 7.1 the chamber is unchanged in the sources read, serving as the investigators' base.
