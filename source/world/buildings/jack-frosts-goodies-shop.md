## summary
### LITE
The Jack Frosts' Goodies Shop is a trading post in Jack Frost Village where Jack Frosts swap goods they treat as "goodies" for uniquely crafted precision buttons. It is run by the Jack Frost Village Head.
### STANDARD +
Mora means nothing to the Jack Frosts: the village head says it is heavy and bad, while buttons are light and pretty to wear. The shop becomes available after the Traveler helps the village in Small Jack Frost, Big Problems.
## people
### STANDARD
The Jack Frost Village Head is a somewhat large Jack Frost who communicates by writing short messages on a wooden slate, calling itself simply the village chief. It uses its own words, calling buttons "bite-ons".
## history
@version: 7.0
### LARGE
In the quest, Volkodlak had invaded the village and bullied the Jack Frosts. Helping them earns the Traveler a first handful of buttons and unlocks the shop. The Eye of Graeae then records a message about the buttons that Jack Frosts love.
## culture
### ULTRA
The map description says it was once believed the fascination comes from a curious urge to imitate humans. It notes that for creatures with no use for clothes, buttons are delicate ornaments humans pin directly to their skin. The shop treats them as the village's currency.
## gameplay
### STANDARD
Game mechanics: the shop sells various materials and other items, including a Construct appearance, in exchange for Intricately Patterned Buttons; the quest awards ten buttons.
