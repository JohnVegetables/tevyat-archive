## summary
### LITE
Tartaglia's Home is the home of Tartaglia's family in Morepesok, a small seaside fishing village in Snezhnaya. Tartaglia, called Ajax at home, grew up here, and his younger sister Tonia and little brother Teucer live there. He seldom visits, which is the main thing the people at the house talk about.
### STANDARD +
Morepesok is a station on the West train line from Snezhnograd. The house is where Tartaglia's Anecdote quests about his family take place.

## geography
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
The home stands in Morepesok, by the coast, in the Everfrozen Earth area. Morepesok is described as a station by the coast where fishing is the livelihood; locals compare their catches, and the most impressive catch earns respect.
### LARGE +
Morepesok has a train station and a harbor. The wiki gives no further description of the house itself.

## people
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
Tonia, Tartaglia's younger sister, and Teucer, a young boy and younger brother of Tartaglia, Tonia and Anthon, are the NPCs at the home. Tartaglia's dialogue also speaks of his father and mother. Tonia is extremely close to Tartaglia and holds him in high regard, while he writes to her as 'Dearest Sister' and signs as her 'loyal knight'.
### LARGE +
Tartaglia is the third son of the family; two older brothers are mentioned in his character stories, and Anthon is also a younger brother. His father taught him ice fishing in childhood and told him adventure stories.

## culture
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
An ordinary family home: his father's headaches are eased by medicine that Ajax brings back from his travels, and gifts of scarves, boots, toys and snacks are the way Tartaglia makes up for missed family gatherings.
### LARGE +
He sends letters addressed to Tonia, with medicine from Liyue and kites, dolls and snacks for the family.

## history
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
Ajax grew up in Morepesok. At fourteen he ran away with a shortsword and a bag of bread, fell into a chasm and met a swordswoman in the Abyss. When he came back, his father found him changed and brawling, and eventually handed him over to the Fatui.
### LARGE +
His worried mother and sisters found him in the forest after only three days, though he felt three months had passed. Tonia says that because he was always picking fights, their father would not let him stay in the village.

## atmosphere
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
A family home that revolves around waiting for Tartaglia, shadowed by Tonia's worry that if they are not holding his hand he might 'slip away to a place where nobody can ever reach him'.

## quests
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
Anecdote 'Tartaglia: A Promise' (part four of the Anecdote Chronicles, started by approaching the home): Tartaglia gives Tonia their father's medicine and snacks for Teucer and says he will come home when his work is done and stay a little longer. Related is 'Tartaglia: Back Home' at the Morepesok train station, where he packs gifts as apologies for a missed family dinner.
### LARGE +
Tonia answers that he must not let them down again. In the open world, Tonia appears in Morepesok after Wraith's Nocturne, asking whether Ajax was trouble and, if he turns up, asking him to visit more often.
### ULTRA +
When Tartaglia himself is the one she is speaking to in the open world, Tonia complains that he promised to take her to watch a show and the younger brothers out to sea and did neither. She says she knows he has important things to do and asks only that he come back more often because Teucer misses him. Do not confuse her with the girl who called herself Tonia in the Korolevskiy Theater in Wraith's Nocturne, who was really Ptashka.

## status
@version: 7.1
### STANDARD
Occupied. Tonia and Teucer remain at home, and Tartaglia still promises to come home more often.

## entry: Tartaglia's Family
@keys: Tonia, Teucer, Anthon, Ajax's family
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
Tartaglia has siblings: Tonia, who wants him home, and Teucer and Anthon, younger brothers. In Morepesok he is called Ajax, a name given by his father after a hero's tale.
### LARGE +
Teucer 'has really missed' him, according to Tonia. Tartaglia's letters send regards to his parents and siblings and ask Tonia not to make trouble for anyone at home.

## entry: Tartaglia: A Promise

@version: 7.0

A hidden Anecdote. Tonia asks if Tartaglia is coming too; he says he has work to tie up and promises to stay longer this time. 'I won't, Tonia. I promise.'
