## summary
### LITE
The Vesnici Proleca Greenhouse is a flower shop in Nasha Town, Nod-Krai, run by Richeza.
### STANDARD +
It sells local specialties and cooking ingredients, and Richeza's stock restocks every three days.

## people
### STANDARD
Richeza is the employee who runs the shop and its vendor; Viljami is also listed at the location.

## culture
### LARGE
The name is Serbo-Croatian in style; the wiki glosses it as "Heralds of Spring".

## status
### LITE
Operating as of 7.1; no change recorded.
### ULTRA +
The Chinese name translates as 'Spring Scenery Greenhouse' and the Japanese name as 'Spring Village'; the wiki traces the Japanese rendering to reading 'vesnici' as the Czech word for village.
