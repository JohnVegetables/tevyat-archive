## summary
### LITE
Floral Whisper is a small flower shop in the market district of Mondstadt City. Its owner is Flora, who sells flowers for Mora. The shop is open from 06:00 to 19:00.
### STANDARD +
Flora is the younger sister of the botanist Chloris, who wanders around Windrise. Unlike her sister, Flora cares only about the flowers themselves.

## places
### STANDARD
A flower stall-shop with five kinds of flower on offer: Sweet Flower, Windwheel Aster, Cecilia, Small Lamp Grass and Calla Lily. In gameplay terms the stock refreshes every three days but the range never changes.
### LARGE +
The flowers on display are strictly for sale. If a customer tries to handle them by day, Flora scolds them that they are goods; at night Donna takes over the scolding in her place.

## people
### STANDARD
Flora is the owner. She is described as having short blonde hair, light blue-green eyes and a pink and white dress. She keeps an imaginary friend, a Bloatty Floatty she calls Mr. Floatty.
### LARGE +
Flora is Chloris's younger sister and shares none of her interest in botany. Alodia of Fragrant Whispers in Dornman Port says Flora sometimes buys flowers from Millhaven.

## culture
@availability: limited
@version: 1.0
### LARGE
Flora's festival-time dialogue appears only during the Invitation of Windblume event. Outside it she talks plainly about flowers, for example insisting that dandelions count as flowers.
### ULTRA +
A dialogue branch with Flora grants the first volume of the storybook The Fox in the Dandelion Sea.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
A daytime shop of cut flowers in a busy market, with an owner who treats the display as stock and not as something to touch.

## travel
### STANDARD
In the market district of Mondstadt City, near the Donna and Flora spot on the map. It closes in the evening.
