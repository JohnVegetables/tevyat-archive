## summary
### LITE
Restaurant Zorya is a re-opened restaurant in Rankova Zorya, in the Volkodlak Tundra. Tolstaya runs it and sells food and recipes there.
### STANDARD +
It is rebuilt during the world quest Daybreak After the Snow and cannot be used before then. The sign reads "Rankova Kafe" in Snezhnayan script.
## people
### STANDARD
Named NPCs are Tolstaya (the proprietor), Ignatov, Nezhdanov and Vakhrushina. Tolstaya keeps the shop open at all hours.
## history
@version: 7.0
### LARGE
In Daybreak After the Snow, Alyosha, Odette and Vodyanitsa have made the station town warmer and busier: a furnace now supplies heat, an extra trade route is agreed, and Odette has driven off bandits so trains can pass safely. Vodyanitsa insists a station needs a restaurant and, with a book of train-station dishes in hand, holds a cleaning "ceremony" to open the building. A hand-drawn signboard shows her, Odette and Alyosha as little chefs.
### ULTRA +
Alyosha's dogs, including one named Lyumina, greet the Traveler on arrival. Rimecurrant ice cream, Glupov rye bread and Snezhnaya shashliks are among the dishes sold.
## culture
### STANDARD
The restaurant is part of a station community. Vodyanitsa calls train-station food a major culinary institution.
## atmosphere
### STANDARD
A warm, crowded station restaurant with a freshly hung sign, mismatched cheerful decor and the smell of hot food against the frost outside.
## gameplay
### ULTRA
Game mechanics: food restocks daily, and recipes do not restock.
