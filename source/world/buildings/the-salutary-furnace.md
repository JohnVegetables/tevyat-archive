## summary
### LITE
A blacksmith's stall in the Stadium of the Sacred Flame, owned by Alom, built around a giant furnace at one side of the Temple of Pax.
### STANDARD +
Per its map description, the Children of Echoes' most skilled craftspeople use Liquid Phlogiston purified by the Sacred Flame to forge unique personal weapons for warriors from across the land. Alom is its vendor.

## places
### STANDARD
It stands on one side of the Temple of Pax section, in the Basin of Unnumbered Flames.

## culture
### LARGE
The map description presents it as a place where fighters get weapons made specifically for them rather than standard issue, using phlogiston refined by the Sacred Flame, which fits the Stadium's role as a venue for tournaments.
### ULTRA +
Do not assume Alom is the only smith there; the description speaks of the most skilled craftspeople of the Children of Echoes in general.
