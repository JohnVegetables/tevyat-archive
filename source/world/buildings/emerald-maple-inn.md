## summary
### LITE
The Emerald Maple Inn stands west of the Statue of The Seven and overlooks Luhua Pool, in the Sea of Clouds area of Liyue. Its owner, Mr. Zhu, struggles to attract customers because of its remote location.
### STANDARD +
Mona used to be a regular before moving to Mondstadt, because the prices were low.

## people
### STANDARD
Mr. Zhu is the innkeeper and sells simple dishes and tea. He greets visitors warmly but is quickly drawn into complaining that he picked a bad spot: it lies between the Wangshu Inn and Liyue Harbor, so travellers from the north stop at the Wangshu Inn, while those coming from the south typically arrive only after drinking heavily in the Harbor. He says he wishes he had listened to his father and stayed home.
### LARGE +
He says he will keep the inn going as long as he can, and will otherwise sell it for whatever it fetches. He also says he is not a top-tier talent and, if it comes to it, will return home and take over the three jade mining facilities his father left him. His signboard invites guests cordially to the Emerald Maple Inn.
### ULTRA +
In Mona's Astrolabos story quest, "Astrology and the 50-Year Pact", the Traveler and Paimon accompany Mona to say farewell to Mr. Zhu before she leaves Liyue, then help recover his lost nephrite ring from slimes. The detail that Mona frequented the inn comes from that quest context.

## status
### LITE
Still open as of 7.1, as quiet as ever.
