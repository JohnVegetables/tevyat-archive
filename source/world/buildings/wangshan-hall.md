## summary
### LITE
Wangshan Hall is the stronghold and training ground of the Guhua Clan on Mt. Lingmeng, in the Upper Vale of Chenyu Vale. The clan has declined, so the hall has fallen into disrepair. To raise Mora for its repair, the Guhua disciple Huang the Third entices visitors in Yilong Wharf to tour it with exaggerated claims about how grand it is.
### STANDARD +
The tour is in the quest A Wangshan Walk to Remember. The Traveler finds a modest hall, then, using a backup key, discovers a flooded secret chamber holding the real treasure of the clan: books and trials left by the hero Guhua.

## places
### STANDARD
Wangshan Hall has a Main Hall and, below, a Secret Chamber that opens after the Scrolls and Sword Manuals of Guhua quest. The Main Hall holds the clan's manuscripts of martial ways, with annotations in a free-spirited hand and a crooked one: the Way of the Rain Cutter (swordwork) is in the Main Hall, and the Way of the Light Piercer (polearms) in the Secret Chamber, waterlogged and mostly illegible.
### LARGE +
A key and a series of trials open the gates; the Hall includes an archive in which books must be matched to coloured frames by elemental sight, and mechanisms that drain the water level when a trial is completed. Guhua's own handwritten notes on a failed training day and his dug fish pond are kept there. Later, a Stonehide Lawachurl appears near the waypoint outside, for players to fight (a gameplay detail).
### ULTRA +
In Shrouded Vale, Hidden Hero an engraved stone and a sword carry an ancient swordsman's inscriptions of slaying tigers beneath Mt. Tianheng and skewering a scaled beast in the Sea of Clouds, with a later hand adding a different message.

## people
### STANDARD
Huang the Third, called the Cloudpiercing Arrow, gives the tours at Yilong Wharf. Wen the Fourth, the Snowstriding Sword, is gloomy that, after a thousand years of heritage, the clan's head doorkeeper (Huang) must cheat visitors to get by. Other members include Little Que'er, Sun Yu and Wakamatsu; Xingqiu holds the title of Guhua Guru of Feiyun Fame.
### LARGE +
Huang the Third and Wen the Fourth are the clan's last elders. Huang asks Wen what will happen to the clan's books once the last elders are gone and nobody is left to test those who might join; Wen has no answer. After the quests he says earnest guests have contributed to the repair fund, though they left little Mora.

## governance
### STANDARD
The Guhua Clan's base. The clan has shrunk to a handful of practitioners, most of whom maintain the hall or try to restore the clan's former reputation.

## history
@version: 4.4
### LITE
The clan recognises Guhua, a wandering hero, as its founder, but he never took disciples; Boxuan (Master Baixuan) set up the clan after his death. Wangshan Hall is its base and main training ground, where Guhua is said to have attained enlightenment.
### STANDARD
In its heyday the Guhua Brotherhood had hundreds of followers and was famed for chivalrous deeds across Liyue. Over time it declined, changed names several times and shrank to a few practitioners. In the Traveler's visit, Huang the Third lures them in with a story of treasure, then is exposed by Paimon (who serves him a burnt, over-salted steak) and, begging not to be reported to the Millelith, hands over the Wangshan Hall Backup Key. He says they were honest compared with others and that the hall's books are better off with them. The key opens the real hidden area, which had been submerged in water, and Guhua's notes and trials beyond.
### LARGE +
The Guhua teaching centres on the Three Guhua Greats: Light Piercer for polearms, Rain Cutter for swords and Life Ender for those who master both. Guhua himself used a claymore, Rainslasher, according to weapon text.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
A run-down hall kept going by a few elders; Huang's tours are a bit of theatre, and the depths hold a waterlogged archive.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the hall is still in disrepair, with a few Guhua disciples keeping it going.
### STANDARD +
Huang the Third is again found at Yilong Wharf by day, and says the earnest guests he brings have added to the repair fund.

## entry: Guhua Clan Techniques
@keys: Light Piercer, Rain Cutter, Life Ender, Three Guhua Greats
@keys2: Guhua, Wangshan Hall
@version: 4.4
### STANDARD
The Guhua Clan teaches three techniques, the Three Guhua Greats: Light Piercer (polearms), Rain Cutter (swords) and Life Ender (for those who master both). Guhua's notes describe devising Rain Cutter after sitting hungry in a courtyard and falling while practising it, which led him to dig a pond and raise fish.

## entry: Huang the Third
@keys: Huang the Third, Cloudpiercing Arrow
@keys2: Wangshan Hall, Guhua Clan
@version: 4.4
### STANDARD
Huang the Third is a Guhua disciple who tours Yilong Wharf visitors through Wangshan Hall to raise repair funds. He is theatrical and polite, begs Paimon and the Traveler not to report him and later admits they were more honest than his usual guests. He gave them the backup key to the hall.
