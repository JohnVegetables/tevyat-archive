## summary
### LITE
Haeresys is an underground arena operated by the Fatui. It appears only in the official Genshin Impact manga, where captured test subjects are brought to fight unknown creatures as part of experiments.
### STANDARD +
Il Dottore and his menial Krupp carried out these experiments there. The manga's one named subject is Anthony, a boy from Mondstadt who was captured and entered as Contender 139. The place is not reachable in the game.
## history
### STANDARD
In the manga, Krupp first appears at Haeresys. Dottore calls his work pathetic and threatens to make Krupp a test subject too; Krupp saves himself by pointing out an invitation to Mondstadt's Ludi Harpastum festival, suggesting more subjects could be recruited from there. The pair then visit Dawn Winery, where Diluc is holding a party.
### LARGE +
Later in the manga, Diluc interrogates Krupp in the winery basement and mentions Anthony, who had worked at a bakery in Mondstadt before being taken. Anthony was sent into the arena against an unidentified tentacled creature and died.
## secrets
@spoiler: minor
### ULTRA
The experiments were carried out by Dottore, one of the Eleven Fatui Harbingers, and the manga ties his recruitment effort to Mondstadt. Nothing in the game itself mentions Haeresys, so a character who has only played the game would not know of it.
## atmosphere
### STANDARD
A sealed underground arena with fights set up as experiments, overseen by a cold scientist and a frightened subordinate.
## etymology
### ULTRA
Haeresis is a Latin word meaning "sect" or "heresy", derived from the Ancient Greek haíresis.
