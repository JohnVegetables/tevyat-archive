## summary
### LITE
Wanmin Restaurant is a well-known restaurant on Chihu Rock in Liyue Harbor, run by Chef Mao, whose daughter Xiangling is its head chef. It sells simple dishes and cooking ingredients and teaches recipes, and its menu is known for unusual dishes. Its map text says some believe it aims to keep everyone fed, others that it wants to unite the world's regional cuisines.
### STANDARD +
It is the sort of place where Liyue Harbor residents and travellers eat and where a hungry visitor can pick up takeout. Chef Mao, a renowned chef, runs it, and Xiangling is its head chef. Its poster declares a new twist on an old classic and welcomes everyone.

## places
### STANDARD
The restaurant stands on Chihu Rock, in the harbour, marked on the map with a restaurant icon. A poster outside advertises "a new twist on an old classic" and says Wanmin Restaurant welcomes you. The shop is open at all hours of the day.
### LARGE +
Its map description calls it a famous restaurant in the Chihu Rock area and suggests that anyone short of time can grab takeout. In play terms Chef Mao sells food, ingredients and cooking recipes; stock refreshes on different timers, recipes are sold once each, and a permanent 10% discount applies from Liyue Reputation Rank 4. These are gameplay details.

## people
### STANDARD
Chef Mao runs the restaurant and serves customers at the counter. His daughter Xiangling is the head chef according to her official page, and the restaurant sells recipes such as Grilled Tiger Fish. Shenhe works there as a server after Ganyu recommended her, and Ningguang has financed the restaurant in the past, as she mentions in her voice-overs about Xiangling.
### LARGE +
Xiangling's character entry carries her personality and cooking passions; this entry only notes that she is the restaurant's head chef and that the family's name is attached to its reputation.

## culture
### STANDARD
Wanmin Restaurant sells simple food. Among the recipes Chef Mao teaches are Grilled Tiger Fish, Honey Char Siu and Rice Buns.
### ULTRA +
In the Hereafter quest series the Traveler walks past Wanmin Restaurant, smells Grilled Tiger Fish and buys three servings, one of them to take to the temple of the late Yaksha Pervases. The restaurant is thus where the Traveler gets the food for that visit.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
A busy harbour-side eatery; in the Hereafter quests the smell of Grilled Tiger Fish draws the Traveler in from the street. A scene here is most naturally one of ordering food, trying a new dish and chatting with Xiangling or Chef Mao.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 Wanmin Restaurant is open, run by Chef Mao with Xiangling as head chef.
### STANDARD +
Shenhe works there as a server. No closure or change of ownership is recorded.
