## summary
### LITE
Angel's Share is a tavern in Mondstadt City, established and funded by the long-standing Dawn Winery. Charles is its bartender, and the winery's owner Diluc sometimes takes a shift himself. It is the traditional home of Mondstadt's wine trade and has a friendly rival in the Cat's Tail.
### STANDARD +
The name is brewers' slang. Charles explains that some liquor is absorbed by barrels and some evaporates during distilling, and the makers call that lost share "the angels' share." The tavern sells drinks, not meals: visitors buy drink-based items from Charles, and its house special is the Dawn Winery's apple cider.

## people
### LITE
Charles, the bartender, has worked here more than a decade as a Dawn Winery employee.
### STANDARD
Charles works most nights and says he cares little for alcohol itself, it is simply his job. Occasionally Diluc, the winery's owner, tends the bar and gives him the night off, which Charles is grateful for. Regulars include the Knight of Favonius Bruce, the Adventurers' Guild branch master Cyrus, the adventurer Jack, Quinn, and by day Nelson and Payne. The bard Six-Fingered Jose performs here at night.
### LARGE +
Patton is listed among the staff. In one official social-media post, a song Jose sings here mocks Diluc as "like a fine wine with too much ice", after which Charles throws the bard out. Charles is dry and measured: during the Stormterror crisis he said he was not worried, that people who numbed themselves with drink were frightened, and that the "Knight Who Emerges At Night" would not stand idle.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
A warm, busy, adult tavern in the city's heart: a counter and two floors, regulars who return night after night, a bard in the corner, and a bartender who keeps order quietly. The tavern does not accept credit or tabs. Mondstadt's famous love of wine is on display here; the menu also lists juices and cider.
### LARGE +
Dawn Winery recruitment notices hang here, appealing to skilled fighters to defend the vineyard from boars. A job board carries posts almost exclusively from the Adventurers' Guild's Mondstadt branch. Charles voices an old-fashioned view: life is not all sweet fruit juice, it needs some bitter alcohol too.

## history
### STANDARD
The Dawn Winery opened the tavern and for a time held a near-monopoly on Mondstadt's wine trade. That ended when Diona became bartender at the Cat's Tail, which overnight became a serious challenger, though Angel's Share is still said to sell more. Elzer, the winery's long-serving butler, tried to hire Diona away to Angel's Share and was bitten for his trouble.
### LARGE +
Margaret, owner of the Cat's Tail, noted that the Stormterror attacks cut the supplies of Angel's Share's popular drinks, but also that the tavern is supplied directly by the Dawn Winery, which gives it a decisive advantage. Charles said the tavern's variety of wines dropped during that period. Diluc stopped importing the strong Snezhnayan spirit Fire-Water because it did not suit local tastes, leaving one bottle from the original batch on display.
### ULTRA +
The recruitment notice claims the winery's tax revenue last year equalled about 11.4% of the Knights of Favonius's annual spending, but its own footnote says that figure is an estimate and not an official financial statement. The notice also blames the thinning of wild monsters under the Knights for a boom in wildlife: boars uproot ten or more vines a week.

## quests
### STANDARD
Scenes in Diluc's Noctua Chapter story quest "Darknight Hero's Alibi" take place at the tavern, where Diluc and the Knight Huffman wait on events. In the world quest "Equivalent Exchange" the Traveler fetches an unusual cocktail ingredient for Charles, who makes a special beverage from it.
### LARGE +
Charles's favourite recommendations: Death After Noon, which he says is Captain Kaeya's favourite, and the Dawn Winery's apple cider. He offers a first cocktail free to new visitors.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the tavern is open and operating; nothing in later content changes it.

## entry: Charles the Bartender
@keys: Charles, bartender Charles
@keys2: Angel's Share, tavern, Mondstadt
### STANDARD
Charles is the bartender employed by the Dawn Winery for more than a decade. He wears an amulet on a necklace, has a mustache and beard, and speaks with a calm, practical wit. He describes himself as uninterested in alcohol, comparing himself to Diona, who hates it. He answers to "Master Diluc" when talking about the owner.

## entry: The Origin of the Name
@keys: angels' share, angel's share meaning, share of angels
### STANDARD
In the wine trade, the "angels' share" is the portion of liquor that soaks into the barrel wood and evaporates during distillation. Charles says brewers use the phrase for this loss, which is where the tavern's name comes from. The in-game map description confirms the tavern was established and funded by the Dawn Winery.

## entry: Bartender Academy Week
@keys: Bartender Academy Week, Of Drink A-Dreaming, Luka
@keys2: Angel's Share
@version: 2.5
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
During the limited-time event Of Drink A-Dreaming (2.5), Luka, a worker at Angel's Share whom Charles credits with a talent for recruiting, ran "Bartender Academy Week", inviting the Traveler to try the job. The academy was originally meant to teach every kind of drink, but after an attendee mixed a drink from three hard liquors the sessions were restricted to non-alcoholic drinks. This was an event only; it is not a standing feature of the tavern.
