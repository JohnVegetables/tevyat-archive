## summary
### LITE
Café Lutece is a well-known coffee house in the Vasari Passage of the Court of Fontaine. The wiki calls it the main restaurant for Fontaine. Arouet manages it. Its map description calls it the most popular café in the passage and says that the owner encourages customers from every background to discuss philosophy and other subjects as they drink their coffee. Any topic is allowed except death.
### STANDARD +
It sells coffee, snacks and sweets, and it has a message board where patrons trade gossip, dilemmas and opinions.

## places
### STANDARD
A single café with a counter run by Arouet, whose stall is open at all times and restocks daily. Fonta, Île flottante, Conch Madeleine and coffee beans are on sale, and the café also sells recipes for Île flottante, Conch Madeleine, Crêpes Suzette, La Lettre à Focalors, Blubber Profiterole and Bulle Soufflé. Its message board stands beside Arouet.
### LARGE +
Staff the wiki names are Arouet (manager), Chatelet (labelled 'Gestionnaire'), Gontharet and Menthe.

## people
### STANDARD
Arouet is the manager and sole named proprietor. His idle line is 'Coffee is life!'. The wiki lists him as formerly affiliated with Maison Gestion.
### LARGE +
Clorinde is a regular customer, who tracks the days on which her favourite blend is on offer (mentioned in the quest Charlotte: Patience).

## culture
### STANDARD
The café keeps one rule for conversation: all topics are welcome, philosophy above all, except death. Its message board invites gossip, deep thoughts, and suggestions for the café. Posts cycle at random.
### LARGE +
The board's posts show a cross-section of Fontaine: a father asking whether to tell his son that the Oceanids left Fontaine long ago (with a child's message saying the real reason for the trip was to spend time with his father, and the owner suggesting that being together matters more than the truth); and a rumour thread about a missing-persons case, answered by a notice from Maison Gardiennage telling people not to spread unfounded rumours.
### ULTRA +
Coffee and conversation are the point of the place. The café offers a featured blend on particular days. The wiki notes that 'Lutèce' is the French name of the Gallo-Roman town that preceded Paris, and 'lucerne' is an Old French word for a lamp or lantern.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
A relaxed café where customers of all backgrounds sit over coffee and talk ideas, as its map description invites them to.

## dangers
### LARGE
No dangers are described in canon. The one house rule is to keep death out of the conversation.

## travel
### STANDARD
The café lies in the Vasari Passage of the Court of Fontaine, marked on the map as a restaurant. It is open at any hour.

## status
### LITE
Open and operating as of 7.1, as far as the sources show.
