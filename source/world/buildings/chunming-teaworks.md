## summary
### LITE
The Chunming Teaworks is the tea workshop of Qiaoying Village in Chenyu Vale. Most of the village's picked tea leaves are sent here to be processed, and its map description says its reputation reaches far beyond the village.
### STANDARD +
It is run by the veteran Ding Sr. with the younger Ding Jr., and it blends age-old methods with newer machines for drying leaves and roasting tea.

## practice
### STANDARD
Fresh leaves need several careful steps before they can become tea. According to Ding Sr., the workshop handles sunning, baking and stirring the leaves in a pan to drive off excess moisture, which deepens the fragrance and makes the tea easier to store. Some varieties are then taken back by the farmers for further work such as piling and fermenting.
### LARGE +
Stirring used to be done in a huge tea cauldron that Ding Sr. says was some kind of adeptal mechanism. The workshop now uses faster machines to meet demand, and he stresses that more heat and speed mean quality control matters even more, which is why people with thirty-odd years of experience, like him, supervise. He advises visitors to stay in the village about a month to see the process from picking to the table.
### ULTRA +
Ding Sr. says Ding Jr. still has about a decade of learning ahead of him. After the quest "An Ancient Sacrifice of Sacred Brocade", Ding Jr. says he always believed the legends of the Carp Adeptus were true. If a visitor tramples the leaves drying in the middle of the room, Ding Sr. scolds them, reminding them the leaves are for everyone's teacups and that Uncle Luo may be angry enough to have them evicted from the village.

## people
### STANDARD
Ding Sr. is the experienced overseer; Ding Jr. is his junior in the craft. Both are ordinary villagers with no established family or story beyond the workshop.

## status
### LITE
A working workshop as of 7.1.
### STANDARD +
Its map description credits it as perhaps the reason Qiaoying Village's tea is so famous.
