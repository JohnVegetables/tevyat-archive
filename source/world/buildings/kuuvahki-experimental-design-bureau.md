## summary
### LITE
The Kuuvahki Experimental Design Bureau is the Fatui's enormous steel-and-stone research and arms complex on Paha Isle, in the Southern Isles of Nod-Krai. The Expedition text calls it a colossal structure whose bulk is an unsubtle declaration of power and control. It studies kuuvahki, the moon-derived energy found only in Nod-Krai, and co-developed war machines, notably the Landcruisers, with Snezhnaya's Armory Palace. Sandrone (Marionette) oversaw its construction and ran it during the Nod-Krai crisis; Poruchik Raskolnikov keeps order there. Locals, especially the Frostmoon Scions, resent it.
### STANDARD +
It stands as a deliberate challenge to nature itself, in the words of the in-game description. It is guarded by Oprichniki troops and automated Landcruisers, and its upper levels are a maze of dormitories, archives, a disposal workshop and a command platform.

## geography
### STANDARD
The Bureau fills much of Paha Isle, once an unremarkable island known for salt marshes and Tassel Jellies until the heaviest fragment of the fallen moonstone crashed on its shores. Its levels (the map lists Top, Middle, Lower and Bottom levels, a Southern Zone and a Disposal Workshop) climb up from the water. South Harbor, with a console that starts one of its quests, is one way in.
### LARGE +
Beneath it lies the classified Underground Research Center. Certain parts of the Bureau are Strong Kuuvahki Environments: while inside, the player is in a kuuvahki-empowered state whose polarity (red or blue) follows the environment.

## places
### STANDARD
The Bureau is a layered compound rather than one room. Known areas include the South Harbor intake, containers moved inside by lift, a dormitory for the garrison, archives where the Cognoscenti chest was taken, the Disposal Workshop where defective products are destroyed, a command platform above it, and the highest point of the complex where Raskolnikov waits. Inside, the Disposal Workshop has moving "destruction modules" that can crush even a Landcruiser.
### LARGE +
The Underground Research Center holds jars of kuuvahki, research records and barrier generators. Its Secret Research viewpoint is described as a classified underground Fatui facility with experiments on kuuvahki. It gathers data from three collection hubs on Paha Isle to research high-density energy barrier generation. A barrier there can be dropped only if all its generators verify an ID card within a time limit.

## people
### STANDARD
Sandrone (the Seventh of the Fatui Harbingers, also called Marionette) ran the Bureau during the Nod-Krai crisis, with her automaton Pulonia as her companion until Dottore destroyed him. Poruchik Raskolnikov, a lieutenant from Novokitezhgrad trained by Police Chief Stalanov, maintains order and efficiency; the lower-ranked Oprichniki dislike him for strict rules such as a ban on drinking parties. Rank-and-file Fatui (Oprichniki troopers, medics, grenadiers and cannoneers) staff it, among them Podporuchik Kholodov, who leads the guard against intruders at the Underground Research Center.
### LARGE +
Dottore, Second of the Harbingers, was said to be present at the Bureau's opening and stayed in Nod-Krai alongside Sandrone, but see the uncertainties on his actual role. The Armory Palace's engineers contributed to the Landcruiser designs. In the Song of the Welkin Moon quests, Fevroniya and Ozerov guard its entrance.
### ULTRA +
Netochka, a thief-minded newcomer from Novokitezhgrad and the daughter of Stalanov, infiltrated the Bureau in the World Quest The Shoemaker's Children Go Barefoot; Raskolnikov knew her from home. Other lesser figures include a Fatuus lamenting that Raskolnikov uses his troops to dig for the Proof of the Cognoscenti, and Fatui soldiers hiding savings in the ventilation ducts, both overheard in that quest.

## governance
### LARGE
Fatui chain of command applies: a Harbinger (Sandrone) at the top, officers such as Raskolnikov below, Oprichniki troops beneath them. Raskolnikov's orders come from superiors; he describes his own role as guarding the Bureau and ensuring its efficient operation, and he admits that handing over the Cognoscenti was insubordination. Staff grumble that the higher-ups do not like him either.
### ULTRA +
A security alert posted in the Underground Research Center raises the alarm level around the Bureau after suspicious activity, treating any unauthorised approach as hostile. Inside the complex, a raised alarm triggers a short countdown, after which reinforcements arrive (a gameplay detail).

## history
### STANDARD
The Fatui came to Nod-Krai under the Tsaritsa's Palestar Edict, the highest of commands, to retrieve the defector Columbina. The Harbingers were dispatched there; Sandrone oversaw construction of the Bureau over ancient moon ruins, which brought the Fatui into conflict with the Frostmoon Scions, who revere the moons. Helka remarks that the Fatui brought many building materials without explaining their plans. Most Harbingers then returned to Snezhnaya; Sandrone later says Arlecchino went back once work on the facility was completed, and Dottore and Sandrone stayed on.
### LARGE +
Kuuvahki research gave the Fatui barrier generators and war machines. The Landcruiser, an autonomous "armed ship that cruises on land", was first engineered by the Armory Palace's finest engineers and is now deployed across Nod-Krai and Snezhnaya. The Super-Heavy Landrover "Type Drzislav" was jointly developed by the Armory Palace and the Bureau; the in-game description says it was originally built for civilian heavy transport (drills and cutters) before later models were militarised. The Cyclic Military Kuuvahki Core, an ascension material, comes from these machines.
### ULTRA +
During Song of the Welkin Moon the Traveler, Lauma and Jahoda infiltrated the Bureau to recover the stolen Moon Marrow; Sandrone, bored and irritated after the Wild Hunt attacked her troops, returned it. Rerir's Wild Hunt later burst into the Bureau hunting Columbina, and Pulonia was easily disabled. Arlecchino sent Lyney, Lynette and Freminet to help repair the damaged Landcruisers. After Rerir's defeat, Arlecchino and Sandrone met at the Bureau to discuss Dottore, whose later experiments in Nod-Krai turned some Fatui into monsters, to Sandrone's disgust. Sandrone died in the final confrontation with Dottore and was later revived in Fontaine; she afterwards invited Lauma, apologised to the Scions for the Fatui's actions, and the two agreed to work toward reconciliation.

## culture
### LARGE
Fatui life here is regimented and a little absurd. The garrison grumbles about reports, pay, hidden savings and an unpopular officer. Locals see the complex as an invader's fortress. The wider Nod-Krai attitude is hostile to Fatui rule, though some rank-and-file Fatui, like those who helped in the Dottore crisis, prove decent.
### ULTRA +
The Proof of the Cognoscenti, the goblet Weavenexa-Cognoscenti's Intoxication tied to the legendary thief Reed Miller, attracted would-be thieves who stirred disorder. Raskolnikov gave it up on purpose, in his words, to stop "an endless swarm of rats" coveting it. Fatui staff overheard in the quest also mention the Reed Miller stories.

## dangers
### LARGE
Oprichniki patrols, Landcruisers and, at the upper levels, an alarm system that calls reinforcements. The Disposal Workshop's destruction modules and Strong Kuuvahki Environment zones damage intruders; the Super-Heavy Landrover: Mechanized Fortress is a normal boss on Paha Isle.
### ULTRA +
Raskolnikov fights as a Battle-Hardened Fireblade Shock Trooper with extra gear, and after the quest becomes a Local Legend. The Underground Research Center is defended by barriers and a guard led by Kholodov.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Cold iron and stone, steady machine noise, alarms and ventilation. Corridors lined with crates, chests, dormitories and archives. A bureaucratic military feel: stamped forms, strict schedules and officers who value precision. Outside, the sunset salt marshes of Paha Isle make a strange contrast with the fortress.

## travel
### LARGE
Paha Isle belongs to the Southern Isles; South Harbor is one way in. Intruders can hide in cargo containers; upper levels open as the World Quest The Shoemaker's Children Go Barefoot progresses. Using the Moonstriders and the Special Lightning Storm lets one jump to the top level.

## quests
### LARGE
World Quests set here: The Shoemaker's Children Go Barefoot and Priorities First (with its follow-up Priorities First: Afterword). Special Operation, part of the Song of the Welkin Moon Archon Quest, begins here; Just Like Old Times, a Tea Party quest, uses the top level.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the Bureau remains a Fatui facility in Nod-Krai, now with a tense peace with the Frostmoon Scions.
### STANDARD +
Sandrone died in the crisis, was revived in Fontaine and has since returned to her duties in Snezhnaya, so who now oversees the Bureau at Harbinger level is not stated in the sources read. Raskolnikov can still be fought there as a Local Legend, and Sandrone holds a tea party with Arlecchino, Tartaglia and Columbina on its top level in Just Like Old Times.

## entry: The Shoemaker's Children Go Barefoot
@keys: Shoemaker's Children Go Barefoot, Netochka, Proof of the Cognoscenti, Weavenexa-Cognoscenti's Intoxication
@keys2: Design Bureau, Raskolnikov, Reed Miller
### STANDARD
In this World Quest the Traveler and Paimon hide in a supply container and are carried into the Bureau. They are mistaken for accomplices of Netochka, a thief hunting the Proof of the Cognoscenti, a treasure tied to the legendary thief Reed Miller. Together they slip through the dormitory and archives to the Disposal Workshop. Raskolnikov, who knows Netochka from Novokitezhgrad, springs a trap and fights with Landcruisers. After defeating him on the top level, the Traveler receives the goblet Weavenexa-Cognoscenti's Intoxication. Raskolnikov loses willingly and warns that a repeat intrusion will meet his full strength. Netochka leaves, calling herself the Traveler's rival.
### LARGE +
Raskolnikov says the treasure belongs to the Tsaritsa and that bringing it to Snezhnograd could earn promotion, then reveals that keeping it at the Bureau only invites thieves. Netochka's feelings about him and her father, Police Chief Stalanov, are tangled: she says Stalanov cares for his cases and promising juniors, and she once admired Raskolnikov. Hidimbi in Nasha Town had given her clues, which Netochka later suspects were not meant only for her.

## entry: Raskolnikov
@keys: Raskolnikov, Poruchik Raskolnikov
@keys2: Design Bureau, Fatui, Oprichniki
### STANDARD
The Poruchik (lieutenant) in charge of the Bureau's order. He wears the inverted-colour uniform of an Oprichniki Fireblade Shock Trooper and holds to rules and efficiency. He respects strength (Stalanov and the Traveler) and has contempt for people he regards as lucky fools. His character entry holds the full detail.

## entry: Underground Research Center and Priorities First
@keys: Underground Research Center, Priorities First, Kholodov, Data Storage Cassette
@keys2: Design Bureau, Paha Isle, kuuvahki
### STANDARD
Entered by defeating a Cutting Edge All-Purpose Storm Landcruiser, the Center is a classified Fatui lab filled with jars of kuuvahki and records on barrier generation. In Priorities First, the Traveler retrieves data cassettes, submits them and escapes Podporuchik Kholodov's guards. The sounds that follow lead to a white Weasel Thief that had been stealing from Fatui and from Paimon, tracked down by Jurva, Maisie and Mikko.

## entry: Landcruisers and the Armory Palace
@keys: Landcruiser, Landcruisers, Type Drzislav, Super-Heavy Landrover
@keys2: Armory Palace, Fatui, Design Bureau
### LARGE
Landcruisers are automated war machines first engineered by the Armory Palace, which is run by Sandrone and based in Glupov in Snezhnaya. The Bureau co-developed the newer generations, including the Super-Heavy Landrover. The wiki lists close-combat, force-recon and cutting-edge variants. Landcruisers are common in Nod-Krai and Snezhnaya, and they were repaired inside the Bureau by Freminet's help in the Song of the Welkin Moon quests.

## entry: Dottore in Nod-Krai
@keys: Dottore in Nod-Krai, Palestar Edict, Special Territory Research Institute
@keys2: Design Bureau, Nod-Krai
@spoiler: major
### ULTRA
Dottore pursued the Three Moons' power under cover of the Palestar Edict, trapped Columbina with a false moon and took the Eternal and Iridescent Moon Marrows. When it failed he retreated to his own facility, the Special Territory Research Institute on Ashveil Peak, and made parts of Nod-Krai into testing zones. He is not stated, in the pages read, to have taken over the Bureau. Sandrone discovered he was turning her subordinates into monsters and sided with the Traveler, dying in the final confrontation near Nasha Town. Columbina killed Dottore, and the Tsaritsa revoked the Palestar Edict. (His soul later seized Irminsul in Sumeru until Nahida ended him for good; that belongs to his own entry.)
