## summary
### LITE
The entrance hall of the Sacred City of Tollan in the Ancient Sacred Mountain, Natlan, connected to the volcano's mouth at the Skyfire Circlet.
### STANDARD +
It is where the Traveler first reawakens the ancient city and enters its depths. The wiki shows an entrance and a main room.

## places
### STANDARD
The hall links the volcano's mouth at the Skyfire Circlet with the interior of Tollan. Its Chinese name means roughly Hall of Summoning.

## history
@version: 5.5
### LARGE
It marks the point where the Traveler's arrival wakes the dormant city, making it the doorway between the surface volcano and the dragons' old home. The wiki does not describe the hall's original purpose beyond calling it an entrance hall.
