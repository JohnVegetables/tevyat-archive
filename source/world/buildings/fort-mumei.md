## summary
### LITE
Fort Mumei is a group of ruins on Yashiori Island, remains of an ancient, majestic city that fell in the Archon War when the serpent god Orobashi invaded the island and clashed with the Raiden Shogun's forces. It lay under the sea until the Traveler finished the Orobashi's Legacy quests, when the fort rose out of the water near Watatsumi Island.
### STANDARD +
Its name means "ignorance" (the Buddhist avidya) in Japanese and sounds like "nameless". The small islets nearby were often used as transit points for supplies going to Watatsumi Island.

## geography
### STANDARD
A cluster of ruined structures on Yashiori Island near Watatsumi Island, ringed by islets. Features: Electro Seelie, a Light-Up Tile Puzzle, a Narukami Pillar, a Thunder Sakura Bough, a Waverider Waypoint, a sword hilt (from the quest Put Them To Rest), a Handy Notepad, and Sea Ganoderma. Enemies are Hilichurl Fighters, Pyro Hilichurl Shooters, Blazing Axe Mitachurls, Ruin Destroyers and Ruin Scouts.
### LARGE +
After Orobashi's Legacy is completed, the fort stands raised out of the sea. A Maguu Kenki guards the last, largest ward there.

## history
### STANDARD
Late in the Archon War, Orobashi invaded Yashiori Island and came into conflict with the Electro Archon (Beelzebul, the Raiden Shogun), which destroyed the ancient city. The Archon slew Orobashi, splitting the island and leaving the Musoujin Gorge, and set wards to hold down the Tatarigami, the slain god's lingering hatred.
### LARGE +
When the Watatsumi Army rebelled against the Shogunate, rebel soldiers attacked Fort Mumei and destroyed the Electro Archon's wards on Yashiori Island, unleashing the Tatarigami. Another account says a radical faction acting without Kokomi's knowledge did this in disguise, hoping to restore Orobashi's dignity.
### ULTRA +
Orobashi is described elsewhere as a guardian god of Watatsumi who had learnt a forbidden truth Celestia wished concealed. A great serpent's skeleton, Orobashi's remains, still lies on Yashiori Island.

## dangers
### LARGE
Tatarigami-driven storms; Ruin Machines; Mitachurls; and, in the quest, a Maguu Kenki guarding the final ward.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Wet grey stone and drowned halls, heavy with the sense of an ended age; thunder over a battle site.

## quests
### STANDARD
Orobashi's Legacy, Parts IV and V (with Kaji): the fort rises from the sea, and the last ward is restored. The Kokomi quest Sword, Fish, Resistance mentions the ruins as a supply route near Watatsumi.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the ruin stands above water and the island's Tatarigami weather has calmed with its wards repaired.

## entry: Orobashi and the Battle of Yashiori
@keys: Orobashi, Orobashi no Mikoto, Watatsumi Omikami, Musoujin Gorge
@keys2: Yashiori Island, Archon War, Fort Mumei
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Orobashi no Mikoto, the Watatsumi Omikami, led its forces to Yashiori Island near the end of the Archon War and fought the Electro Archon. The Archon killed it with the Musou no Hitotachi, splitting the island and creating the Musoujin Gorge. Its lingering hatred became the Tatarigami, held back by the Archon's wards. Watatsumi's people kept worshipping Orobashi.
