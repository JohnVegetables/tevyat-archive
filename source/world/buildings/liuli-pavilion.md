## summary
### LITE
Liuli Pavilion is the leading restaurant for Li-style cuisine in Liyue, a style centred on mountain delicacies. It stands on Feiyun Slope in Liyue Harbor, deliberately across from Xinyue Kiosk, the flagship of the rival Yue style. In the Archon Quest Childe arranges a business dinner here to introduce the Traveler to Zhongli.
### STANDARD +
The Traveler is directed there by Ekaterina at the Northland Bank. The staff includes the waiter Hongru and Licai, a waitress who stands outside; a visitor from Fontaine called Francis also dines there.

## places
### STANDARD
The pavilion has an exterior, an interior that looks different by day and by night, and private rooms; Hongru tells Childe that Zhongli awaits in the room Childe booked. Licai stands outside and sells the pavilion's specialties. The pavilion is the Li style's counterpart of the Yue-style Xinyue Kiosk, close by on Feiyun Slope.
### LARGE +
Francis, a trader from Fontaine, has booked the place to himself by day while praising Li cuisine, saying he travelled a long way to do business in Liyue Harbor and has fallen in love with the food. His idle lines praise Li cuisine by day and Yue cuisine at night.

## people
### STANDARD
Hongru is the waiter who greets Childe, who is a returning guest. Licai is a waitress outside who sells the house specialties. Francis is a Fontaine visitor inside by day.
### LARGE +
Zhongli quietly drinks tea in the private room when the Traveler is brought to dinner. Childe made the booking in line with Liyue custom of doing business over a meal.
### ULTRA +
Childe introduces him as a consultant to Wangsheng, an organization he calls a trusted associate of the Fatui, and Zhongli says Wangsheng organizes burials so that the dead pass on in peace. He adds that he can bring the Traveler to see Rex Lapis' vessel.

## culture
### STANDARD
Liyue has two main styles of cooking, Li style and Yue style, which have competed for centuries without a clear winner. Li cuisine uses mountain delicacies; Liuli Pavilion is its flagship, while Xinyue Kiosk is the flagship of Yue style. Paimon explains this to the Traveler on the way.

## history
@version: 1.0
### LITE
In the Archon Quest An Organization Known as Wangsheng, Childe sets up a business dinner at Liuli Pavilion to introduce the Traveler to Zhongli, whom he found to help solve the mystery of why the Qixing hid the Geo Archon's vessel.
### STANDARD
Ekaterina at the bank says Childe is at Liuli Pavilion. Paimon explains the Li and Yue rivalry. At the pavilion Hongru welcomes Childe, saying Zhongli awaits them in the booked room. Childe says he followed Liyue custom in arranging a business dinner. In the private room he introduces Zhongli as a consultant to Wangsheng, and Zhongli says he can bring the Traveler to see Rex Lapis' vessel.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
A prestigious restaurant where business is conducted over food, close enough to its rival to compete face to face.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 Liuli Pavilion remains open on Feiyun Slope.
