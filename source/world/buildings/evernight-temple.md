## summary
### LITE
The Evernight Temple is a subarea of Enkanomiya, the sunken ancient land reached from beneath Watatsumi Island. It is a ruined sacred site where a faintly glowing stream, the Tokoyo Reisen (once called the River Lethe), runs close by. Offerings, hidden rooms and spectral figures from old Byakuyakoku are scattered through it, among them a fragment of the dead inventor Aberaku.
### STANDARD +
Enkanomiya shifts between "Whitenight" and "Evernight" with the Dainichi Mikoshi, and some barriers, rooms and spirits at the temple exist only in one phase. Its Japanese name is written 常夜霊廟; the wiki glosses it as "Evernight Mausoleum".

## geography
### STANDARD
Enkanomiya lies under Watatsumi Island and is entered through a deep pool east of Sangonomiya Shrine once the seals on the five Goshou Rocks are broken. The Evernight Temple is one of its subareas, a glide down from the eastern teleport waypoint of the Dainichi Mikoshi. It holds an Elemental Monument, Triangular Mechanisms, Places of Essence Worship and Seelie, and its local specialty is Sea Ganoderma. A quiet coral-ringed open space near the temple is one of the sitting spots in a hidden objective.
### LARGE +
Doors, lanterns and barriers are tied to Enkanomiya's day-night mechanisms: the Daises of Day and Night, Triangular Mechanisms that are struck to move things, and essence-worship sites that carry one phase's power into the other. Secret rooms are opened by switching between Whitenight and Evernight and working these in order.

## places
### STANDARD
The Tokoyo Reisen, the stream beside the temple, marked as its own point of interest. A secret room, reached by following the traces of Bathysmal Vishaps, which holds one fragment of the Reins of Revival. The precinct where part of Aberaku's remains (his "sacred crown") was enshrined.

## people
### STANDARD
There are no living residents. Spectral figures appear in Evernight: the spirit of Aberaku, who calls himself Helios-Forger, and seven Sunchildren (Ion, Isumenasu, Orupeusu, Piramumon, Rikoru, Risutaiosu and Surepio), who can be spoken to only in Evernight and vanish afterwards. The Sunchildren were child figureheads of old Byakuyakoku, ruled through by a cabal of nobles.
### LARGE +
The Sunchildren talk about ordinary things. Ion reads fortunes and admits that he softens bad readings; Isumenasu dismisses the Traveler's map as a fraud because Byakuyakoku could not be that small; Orupeusu has sworn not to sing until he hears Clymene's voice; and Piramumon, delighted that other lands exist, mentions the Rite of Solar Return he must attend. Treat them as wistful afterimages, not as living children.

## history
### STANDARD
Enkanomiya (earlier Byakuyakoku, Tokoyokoku, and originally Delphi Pytho) sank into the sea in the war of funerary flame and was cut off from the surface. Its people held off the Dragonheirs of the Depths, now called Bathysmal Vishaps, with the Dainichi Mikoshi, the artificial sun Aberaku built. Corrupt nobles later set up child Sunchildren as puppet rulers; Aberaku, who opposed them, was imprisoned on the first Sunchild's accusation and died in captivity, and his clothes and body were enshrined in several places. The crown was kept at the Evernight Temple. Orobashi later overthrew the Sunchildren and led the survivors up to the surface.
### LARGE +
Aberaku's spirit, scattered by these separate enshrinements, cannot rest. At the temple he recalls being held somewhere very high, dark and cold, and cannot say why a high place should feel wrong to him. The offerings left here carry a trace of his thoughts.
### ULTRA +
Whether the temple's builders meant it as a shrine, a tomb or both is not stated in the sources read; treat it as a place of enshrinement and memory rather than a documented institution.

## dangers
### LARGE
Hilichurls, samachurls, slimes and Anemo Specters; Electro, Hydro and Pyro Abyss Mages; Primordial Bathysmal Vishaps and hatchlings; Thundercraven and Rockfond Rifthound whelps; Ruin Guards, Ruin Graders and Ruin Destroyers. Damaged mechanisms can be repaired by switching phase.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Dark stone beneath a lightless sky, lantern and sigil glow, a stream that shines faintly in the dim, and ghostly figures who speak briefly and are gone. It is a place of memory more than of life.

## quests
### STANDARD
Temple Inquiry (Hyperion's Dirge): Aberaku's spirit-trial is passed here and the Offerings of the Temple are collected. The Trail of Drake and Serpent (From Dusk to Dawn in Byakuyakoku): Vishap traces lead to a secret room and a fragment of the Reins of Revival. Sunchildren Hide and Seek: a hidden objective unlocked after The Phaethons' Syrtos. Lotus Eater: the Waters of Lethe are drawn from the Tokoyo Reisen.
### LARGE +
Another hidden objective, Reading Kabayama's Lanterns, uses a sitting rock in the coral-ringed space near the temple as one of its three viewpoints.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the temple stands unchanged in Enkanomiya, explorable and empty of living people. Nothing newer than its 2.4 content was found.
### STANDARD +
Enkanomiya itself is little known outside Inazuma; when the Traveler mentioned it to Wriothesley in Fontaine, he had to ask what it was.

## entry: Tokoyo Reisen and the Waters of Lethe
@keys: Tokoyo Reisen, Waters of Lethe, River Lethe, Dragonbone Orb, Dragonbone Flower
@version: 2.4
### STANDARD
The Tokoyo Reisen is a stream at the temple, once called the River Lethe. Its water shines faintly and does not by itself make a drinker forget, but the Dragonbone Orb, a rare fruit that a Dragonbone Flower bears when watered with it, calms the mind and sweeps worries away. In the Lotus Eater quest Adonis, a student of the resistance leader Supada no Hiko (called Spartacus), says remnants of the resistance sold the orbs to fund their cause; many fighters then lost their will to resist, and he wonders if the formula was planted by the nobles who ruled through the Sunchildren.
### LARGE +
The orbs are made by watering the flower with the stream's water and waiting for it to condense. Supada no Hiko is himself only an afterimage held in the altar at the Serpent's Heart; Adonis learns from the Traveler that his master is trapped there.

## entry: Aberaku at the Evernight Temple
@keys: Aberaku, Helios-Forger, Offerings of the Temple, sacred crown
@keys2: Evernight Temple, Enkanomiya
@version: 2.4
### STANDARD
In the Temple Inquiry, Aberaku's spirit calls himself "Helios-Forger" and does not remember how he died, only that a child ordered his imprisonment and that he feels no hatred for that child, who was a puppet. He asks the Traveler to defeat the monsters that act as warders of his spirit, and hands over the Offerings of the Temple, which hold part of his thoughts. His speech keeps breaking off mid-thought.

## entry: The Sunchildren of the Temple
@keys: Sunchildren, Sunchild, Phaethons, Rite of Solar Return, Rikoru
@keys2: Enkanomiya, Byakuyakoku
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
The Sunchildren (Phaethons) were child figureheads of Byakuyakoku after the Dainichi Mikoshi was built; a cabal of corrupt nobles used them as puppets and let them take the blame for harm done in their name. When a Sunchild reached a certain age the Rite of Solar Return ended that Sunchild's life, so they could not realise they were being manipulated. Rikoru was the first Sunchild. In the Sunchildren Hide and Seek objective seven of them are found as afterimages at the temple. Describe them factually and gently: lonely children of an unjust system.

## entry: Reins of Revival at the Temple
@keys: Reins of Revival, Golden Bridle
@keys2: Enkanomiya, Evernight Temple
@version: 2.4
### LARGE
In The Trail of Drake and Serpent the Traveler follows Bathysmal Vishap traces at the temple, defeats whelps guarding a mechanism, opens the secret room by switching phases, and collects one of three fragments of the Reins of Revival. The other two come from Dreams in the Gaps and The Heart of Ouroboros; together they continue The Subterranean Trials of Drake and Serpent, which leads to the Golden Bridle used elsewhere in Enkanomiya.
