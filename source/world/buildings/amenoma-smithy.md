## summary
### LITE
The Amenoma Smithy sits at the eastern end of Inazuma City, in Hanamizaka. Its master, Amenoma Tougo, and apprentice Hajime follow the Amenoma Art, one of the five bladesmithing schools of the Raiden Gokaden. The school is known for avoiding special tricks and relying on endless practice and willpower.
### STANDARD +
Tougo accepts only commissions for fine Jade Steel swords and knives because of his age, and sends everyday forging to Hajime. Kaedehara Kazuha says that of the old Gokaden schools only the Amenoma Clan's art survives.

## places
### STANDARD
An ordinary craft shop with a forge and a board for orders, in Hanamizaka. The signboard reads "KJ" in Inazuman script, probably short for kaji (smithing). Hajime forges certain items and sells low-grade weapons (gameplay shop; stock matches blacksmiths elsewhere); Tougo stands in front of the building.
### LARGE +
The name Amenoma (天目) likely nods to Ame no Mahitotsu no Kami, a Japanese smithing deity; this is an out-of-world etymology note.

## people
### STANDARD
Amenoma Tougo is the master and owner; his nephew is named Amenoma Yuuya (mentioned in Kazuha's story quest). Hajime is his senior disciple, practises forging daily, earns a modest salary, and is unsure he will be the heir. Both keep repeating the phrase about patience and willpower that moves mountains.
### LARGE +
Tougo once asked a member of the Crux Fleet to bring him a Liyue-made weapon, studied it and mastered the technique, then gave the blade away to a traveller with ties to Liyue. He believes Inazuman weapons are among the world's best because of Jade Steel from Tatarasuna.

## culture
### STANDARD
The Amenoma Art tries to copy water, which turns stone to sand by patience. Tougo says there is nothing mystical in it, only daily practice until every motion becomes part of the smith's life. In the Raiden Gokaden tradition, a ronin is said to have taught forging to five disciples, each of whom developed a different school.
### LARGE +
Kazuha explains that the techniques came from the Almighty Shogun herself and were refined over time, and that the Kaedehara and Amenoma clans have long been linked. A series of events led most of the other schools to decline.

## history
### STANDARD
Around five hundred years ago, after Iwakura Michihiro beat Teruyo in a duel and founded the Iwakura Clan, he had the Amenoma master of the time forge a blade to pass to each holder of the "-in" title: the Hakuen Michimitsu Amenoma. It disappeared after Iwakura Mitsunari's death in the quest Iwakura Art's Downfall, and was later found rusted in Enkanomiya. The Amenoma Kageuchi is a second copy, given to the Saimon Clan and later to the Traveler.
### LARGE +
In the artifact lore of Calabash of Awakening, the Amenoma Art is the only school that escaped Scaramouche's revenge plot against "the bladesmith", probably Mikoshi Nagamasa, with whom he clashed in Tatarasuna about five hundred years ago.

### ULTRA +
Accounts differ on how the Amenoma Kageuchi, the second copy of the blade, reached the Saimon Clan: one page says Doukei gave it, another that Iwakura Michihiro gave it in thanks for a place to live. Keep this open.

## quests
### STANDARD
Through the Mist, a Name Unveiled (Kazuha's story quest, A Strange and Friendless Road, Acer Palmatum Chapter), in which Kazuha and the Traveler ask Tougo for information. During the permanent Tatara Tales world quests, the board announces that Jade Steel supplies have temporarily stopped, so exquisite orders are paused.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the smithy continues to operate in Hanamizaka, with Tougo as owner and Hajime as apprentice. Nothing newer has been found.

## entry: Amenoma Art and the Raiden Gokaden
@keys: Amenoma Art, Raiden Gokaden, Amenoma Clan
@keys2: Inazuma, bladesmith, swordsmith
### STANDARD
The Raiden Gokaden are Inazuma's five bladesmithing schools; the Amenoma Art is one. It is said to have no secret tricks, only the patience and willpower to give everything. Tougo says only a few visitors have the perseverance to carry the spirit of the art.

## entry: Hakuen Michimitsu Amenoma
@keys: Hakuen Michimitsu Amenoma, Amenoma Kageuchi, Iwakura Clan heirloom
@version: 2.0
### LARGE
The Hakuen Michimitsu Amenoma was forged by the Amenoma master for Iwakura Michihiro. Its item text says it has no special powers; the Iwakura Succession never held a Vision and its skill came only from honed mind, body and technique. After Mitsunari surrendered his Vision under the Vision Hunt Decree, the clan fell apart. The blade is rusted from long immersion in water.
