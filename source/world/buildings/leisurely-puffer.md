## summary
### LITE
A watersports shop in Toyac Springs, in the People of the Springs' area of Natlan, owned by the family of the surfer Mualani.
### STANDARD +
It sells a wide range of gear, from swimsuits to waterskis, and its custom surfboards are said to be especially popular. A message board stands at the shop.

## places
### STANDARD
The shop sits in the Toyac Springs area under the People of the Springs' name on the map. Its Leisurely Puffer Message Board is the shop's interactable.

## people
### LARGE
Ownership by Mualani's family comes from the quest Home of the Hot Springs. For Mualani herself see her own entry; the shop's other staff are not named in the sources read.

## culture
### LARGE
The prize for the planned First All-Tribe Surfing Championship is currently kept at the shop, which hints that it may host or sponsor the event. This is the wiki's inference, not a stated fact.

## status
### LITE
Open as of 7.1.
### ULTRA +
Nothing in the sources read changes the shop's status after its introduction in 5.0.
