## summary
### LITE
Kiminami Restaurant is a well-known family restaurant in Hanamizaka, the suburb of Inazuma City north of the river, owned and cooked for by Kiminami Anna. Its Japanese name marks it as a ryoutei, and its family says its cooking descends from the Shogunate's royal head chef. Locals know it for classic Inazuman dishes, unlimited lemon water and, since the Traveler's visit, a Mondstadt-style mushroom pizza on the menu.
### STANDARD +
Anna's greeting calls it "the first and last stop for gourmet culture in Inazuma", and she runs it with her mother because it has been short-staffed since her father vanished. She plans one day to take over fully. The restaurant is a regular of the Kamisato clan, whose staff often order food there to carry back.

## places
### STANDARD
The restaurant stands in Hanamizaka, with Anna at the counter doubling as shopkeeper. She sells prepared dishes such as rice cake soup and, once the pizza has been added to the menu, Mushroom Pizza or Special Mushroom Pizza, and she also sells recipes for Inazuman dishes including soba noodles, tuna sushi, radish and fish stew, Wakatakeni, berry mizu manjuu, tricolor dango, tri-flavored skewers, taiyaki and More-and-More. An outside notice board announces new dishes.
### LARGE +
The notice announces the all-new Mushroom Pizza as an exotic delicacy bringing another country's flavours to the doorstep, and asks diners who like it to thank "our good friend" who helped bring it to the restaurant. Seating is limited by staffing: Anna warns that dine-in customers must wait and offers unlimited lemon water in the meantime.

## people
### LITE
Kiminami Anna owns and cooks; her mother helps her.
### STANDARD
Anna learned to cook from childhood and is slowly earning her place as heir. She says she is proud of every dish she serves, but also knows she is still short of her grandfather's level and means to match it. Her father came to Inazuma as a foreign gourmet, volunteered as a kitchen assistant at the restaurant, and married into the Kiminami clan; after her grandfather was called to serve the Shogunate as head chef, her parents ran the place together. Her father disappeared without a trace the year before her dialogue, and her mother and she have worked on since.
### LARGE +
Regulars and acquaintances: Kamisato Ayaka and Kamisato Ayato are both fond of the food and send staff to order take-out; Anna has known the clan for years of business and admires Ayaka for taking on so much so young. Matsumoto, a travelling food critic who regards the district's restaurants as mediocre, still calls Kiminami's unlimited lemon drink the tastiest thing in the world and cites its egg roll as the standard a dish must meet to deserve his review.
### ULTRA +
Anna knows Ayaka's reputation as a model young lady and has heard that few ordinary children dare approach her, which she says explains why Ayaka has so few friends. When Ayaka books a table for friends, Anna is surprised, never having heard of such a group reservation before. In the quest "Chisato's Letter", Kujou Kamaji orders a box of tricolor dango from Anna for the Traveler to carry back to Hiiragi Chisato, telling the curious Anna it is a return gift for Chisato's father.

## history
### STANDARD
The restaurant's roots are the Kiminami clan, whose cooking skill reached the Shogunate: Anna's grandfather became the royal head chef. Her foreign father joined as a kitchen assistant before marrying into the family. Her father vanished about a year before her first dialogue, which is when she started cooking in earnest to help her mother.
### LARGE +
Anna also mentions that Treasure Hoarders have been targeting her staff when they go out to collect ingredients in the wild (told to the Traveler during Sayu's quest "Bottleneck Breaking"). Her kitchen later helped the Traveler and Paimon with a cat meal for the Asase Shrine on Seirai Island: after hearing that the last shrine maiden, Asase Hibiki, once made a dish of fish, raw meat, seagrass and rice for the island's cats, Anna developed her own version, Kiminami Anna's Invigorating Kitty Meal, and gave the recipe to the Traveler.

## quests
### STANDARD
The restaurant features in Kamisato Ayaka's story quest "Food From Afar" (Grus Nivis Chapter), where the Traveler writes the Mushroom Pizza recipe for Anna; in "Chisato's Letter"; in "Neko Is a Cat: Shrine Recipe"; in the follow-up "Pizza From Another Land"; and in Sayu's "Bottleneck Breaking".

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
A busy neighbourhood eatery rather than a grand banquet hall: Anna's brisk, friendly welcome, lemon water on the table, and a short wait because the kitchen is short-handed. It is proud of ordinary dishes done well and is somewhere Kamisato staff and travellers come to eat.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the restaurant is open and Anna runs it. Her father has still not come back, as far as canon says.

## entry: Kiminami Anna
@keys: Kiminami Anna, Anna of Kiminami
@keys2: Kiminami Restaurant, Hanamizaka, Inazuma
### STANDARD
Kiminami Anna is the owner and cook of Kiminami Restaurant, a young woman with brown hair and eyes in a red and gray dress who learned cooking from childhood. She hopes to match her grandfather's skill as the Shogunate's royal head chef and sells recipes and prepared dishes. She worries about her missing father, whom she expects back. She also appears in Ayaka's story, in Sayu's quest "Bottleneck Breaking" and in the cat-meal quest.

## entry: Food From Afar and the Mushroom Pizza
@keys: Food From Afar, Mushroom Pizza, Special Mushroom Pizza
@keys2: Anna, Ayaka, Kiminami Restaurant
@version: 2.0
### STANDARD
In Ayaka's story quest "Food From Afar", the Traveler and Paimon dine at Kiminami Restaurant with Ayaka, who rushes off to deal with something and later fails at making an overseas dish to give as a gift to Tsubaki. Paimon proposes pizza as an easily transportable choice, and the Traveler writes down a recipe that is either the standard Mondstadt Mushroom Pizza or a Special Mushroom Pizza using Inazuman ingredients. Anna adds it to the menu. In the follow-up quest "Pizza From Another Land" the other version is developed after Anna receives feedback from Rie or Matsumoto.

### STANDARD
In the limited-time event "Silverwing in Pursuit of the Moon" (7.1), Matsumoto the food critic appears among the cast and remarks that the food where he is seasoned far more boldly than at home. He says he will have a good talk with Anna when he returns, because there is always more to learn. This means Anna is mentioned in a 7.1 line but does not herself appear in the event. The event is canon but time-limited.
