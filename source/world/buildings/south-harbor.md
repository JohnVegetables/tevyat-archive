## summary
### LITE
The South Harbor is a harbor of the Kuuvahki Experimental Design Bureau on Paha Isle, Nod-Krai. A notice at the North Harbor routes all goods entering the Bureau through it.
### STANDARD +
Seafarers are found here, and a board carries stern notices about the Bureau's equipment.

## people
### STANDARD
Listed NPCs: Azalea, Przybor (crew member of the Ocean Dawn), Risto, Tuokko (captain of the Never Fade Away) and Veroslava.

## places
### LARGE
The notice board carries three warnings: operating the Kuuvahki Barrier Activation Terminal now needs at least two certified safety officers; Landcruisers and soldiers alike are valuable assets of Her Majesty, and non-standard operations have already cost some of both; and a test operator is on indefinite leave awaiting a disciplinary hearing for gross negligence. Names and figures are blacked out.

## status
### LITE
In use as of 7.1; it carries the Bureau's freight while the North Harbor stays closed.
### ULTRA +
The Chinese name is simply 'South Harbor'. Its notices mention Landcruisers and soldiers as assets of the Tsaritsa, tying the harbor to the Bureau's official work.
