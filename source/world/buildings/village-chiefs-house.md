## summary
### LITE
The Village Chief's House is the home of Uncle Anpu, the Aaru Village chief, in the Great Red Sand. He has led Aaru Village for a long time and offers travellers food and lodging to the best of his ability. At night the village guard Belkacem is also found here.
### STANDARD +
Anpu warns desert travellers that loose sand or unusually strong wind is a sign to shelter in the village at once, although it does not guarantee a sandstorm.

## people
### STANDARD
Uncle Anpu is kind, a little long-winded and proud of looking after his village; he says he has seen many reckless people get into trouble in the desert and urges the Traveler to respect danger. After the Traveler completes King Deshret and the Three Magi, he mentions that the village boy Isak wants to thank the Traveler and says he is a good example to children. His idle thoughts wonder whether he is fulfilling his duties as village chief; at night he is surprised by some events he heard of. Belkacem is a guard on duty by night.
### LARGE +
In Dreams, Emptiness, Deception (Chapter III Act III) the Traveler and Dehya are staying in the village when Anpu arrives and explains that the Village Keepers have gone missing without a trace. Cyno tells the Traveler that the Keepers are exiled Akademiya scholars whose ramblings the Akademiya wanted away from other people, and explains how the village first shunned them.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 Anpu is still village chief.
### ULTRA +
No quest through 7.1 changes the house. The Village Keepers' fate is told in Archon Quest content rather than here.
