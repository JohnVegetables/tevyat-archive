## summary
### LITE
The Esoteric Arrays are a subarea of the Ancient Sacred Mountain, within the Great Volcano of Tollan in Natlan, where the ancient dragons once ran arrays that performed arithmetic calculations. Today the ruins are overgrown with Abyssal corrosion.
### STANDARD +
They lie on the way to the Heart of the Sacred Mountain. To uncover that path, the Traveler and the Little One must cleanse the corrupted ruins here, with the help of a Secret Source Servant and a Secret Source Beam Cannon.

## geography
### STANDARD
The Arrays sit in the Dragons' Residence level of the Ancient Sacred Mountain. The ruins are ancient stonework, now fouled by Oozing Cores that carry the Abyss's corrosion.
### LARGE +
A Secret Source Dragon Idol stands in the area; interacting with it unlocks the Secret Source Beam Cannon, a cannon that destroys Oozing Cores.

## people
### STANDARD
No named residents. Secret Source Servants move through the ruins, and one is harassed by Rockfond Rifthound Whelps, and the Traveler defends it.
### LARGE +
The Little One accompanies the Traveler throughout, and Paimon comments on a glowing object after the cores are destroyed.

## history
### STANDARD
The arrays were built by the dragon civilization, which used them for arithmetic calculation. The wiki's source for this is the quest Where Once There Was a Calculation Array.
### LARGE +
Beyond that, only the ruin's condition is known: the Abyss's corrosion has crept into the ancient works, and it blocks the road to the Heart of the Sacred Mountain.

## quests
@version: 5.5
### STANDARD
Where Once There Was a Calculation Array is one of three sub-quests in In Search of a Hidden Heart (part of the World Quest series Chronicler of the Crumbling City). The Traveler must clear Abyssal corrosion: defeat the Rockfond Rifthound Whelps accosting a Secret Source Servant, follow the Servant, defeat more whelps near the Dragon Idol, then use the Beam Cannon to destroy the Oozing Cores. The reward is a Phlogiston Unit.
### LARGE +
As the Traveler nears the exit a message reads "O Chosen of Dragons, the power of blazing fire now rests within you", and the cannon gains a Lava Buff. Afterwards Paimon notes that the Abyss around the place should be cleaned up and spots a glowing object after the Little One roars; it is the Phlogiston Unit.
### ULTRA +
Gameplay note: the cannon destroys Oozing Cores in an area of effect and never runs out of ammunition. The quest awards one Precious, one Exquisite and one Common Chest. Completing this quest and its two companions, Where Once Force Was Reversed and Where Once There Were Arms Aplenty, awards the achievement "Questioning the Heart of Flames".

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the Arrays are cleared of corrosion and idle; no later story role is recorded.
