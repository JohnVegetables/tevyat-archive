## summary
### LITE
The Cradle of Fleeting Dreams is the castle-like structure at the centre of Ochkanatlan, the ruined ancient city of Natlan. It stands over Ashflow Street, the settlement at its base, and has a throne at the top of a staircase.
### STANDARD +
It is a Point of Interest in the Ochkanatlan area, added with Version 5.2. The meaning of the in-game name is not explained in the sources read.

## places
### STANDARD
Ashflow Street lies at the foot of the building. A staircase leads up to the throne, and at its foot stands a pot of scrolls holding a Spiritcaller's note. The Vision Serpent's Sanctum is listed as a Point of Interest under the Cradle.
### LARGE +
The building can be viewed both near the top and from lower down; no official floor plan or room names are given in the sources read.

## history
### LARGE
Nothing in the sources read states who built the Cradle or who ruled from its throne. The surrounding city, Ochkanatlan, belonged to the ancient alliance led by the Holy Sovereign (Och-Kan), but the Cradle itself is not tied to him by name.
### ULTRA +
A note at the stair foot was written by Liriwu, a young Spiritcaller of the Masters of the Night-Wind. She records being sickened by what she saw, being told by her uncle Copan that the souls of such creatures could be freed if the Python King's treasure were found, and wondering why that king died in the Abyss if the treasure could destroy it. She resolves not to waver for everyone's sake. This is her own account and fear, not an authoritative explanation.

## status
### LITE
Ruined and abandoned; open for exploration in Ochkanatlan as of 7.1.
