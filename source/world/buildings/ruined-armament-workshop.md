## summary
### LITE
A ruined facility in the Dragons' Residence of the Ancient Sacred Mountain, Natlan. It once made and stored weapons, and the ancient dragons may also have used it for human experimentation.
### STANDARD +
A Nest Experiment Log beside a huge dragon statue records one such experiment: a human child cast among the vishaps.

## places
### STANDARD
The Nest Experiment Log is on the right side of the huge dragon statue.

## history
@version: 5.5
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The log's researcher describes the Regent's so-called test subject, a seemingly ordinary human child, being thrown in with the vishaps and expected to be treated as food. The subject showed strength and recovery far beyond an adult human's, took food from the vishaps and then shared the rest with them, a kindness the researcher notes exceeds anything seen in similar human experiments.
### LARGE +
Following protocol, a neutralization injection was given to a surplus entity judged unfit for dragonhood. The subject showed fear that vishaps lack and empathy for a fallen enemy that humans lack, and was briefly incapacitated without needing treatment. Reports were sent to the Regent. The subject then escaped the nest and a pursuer was dispatched, but the hunt protocol was rescinded by order of the Regent on the grounds that the subject had been personally neutralized. The wiki links the Regent to Kukulkan and the subject to Och-Kan; the log uses titles only.

## atmosphere
### ULTRA
The log reads as a detached lab report. The log is a record by a dragon-side researcher, so treat its framing of the child as a specimen as that researcher's view.

## status
### LITE
Ruined and accessible as of 7.1.
