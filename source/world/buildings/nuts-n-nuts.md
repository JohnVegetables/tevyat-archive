## summary
### LITE
Nuts 'N' Nuts is a combined grocery and repair shop in Nasha Town, Nod-Krai, run by Cairuo and Lestocq.
### STANDARD +
Cairuo sells the goods and her stock restocks daily; Lestocq is the shop's engineer. An alchemy bench stands in the shop. Completing the Archon Quest Rest Beyond Reach gives a 10% discount here, a gameplay reward.

## people
### STANDARD
Cairuo is the food merchant and vendor; Lestocq is the engineer who does the repair work.
### LARGE +
The Chinese name translates as 'Nuts and Bolts', a pun that fits a shop selling food (nuts) and doing mechanical repairs (bolts).

## status
### LITE
Operating as of 7.1; no change recorded.
### ULTRA +
The Archon Quest Rest Beyond Reach, which grants the discount, itself begins in Nasha Town, in Act II of the Song of the Welkin Moon chapter.
