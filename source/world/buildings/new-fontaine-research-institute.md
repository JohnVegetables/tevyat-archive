## summary
### LITE
The New Fontaine Research Institute is where the Fontaine Research Institute of Kinetic Energy Engineering works now. It was built near the ruins of the Central Laboratory after the Arkhium explosion there. Before the disaster the Institute was known as the Alain Guillotin Research Institute and hosted weekly lectures about critical projects. Its staff here include a Material Collation team, a fish-type clockwork meka research team and a Fonta vending machine.
### STANDARD +
The Institute studies machines and energy, unlike the Akademiya, and has no authority over the people of Fontaine nor does it educate them. According to the NPC Salsa it has financial problems.

## geography
### STANDARD
The Institute sits in the Fontaine Research Institute of Kinetic Energy Engineering Region, close to the Central Laboratory Ruins that float in the sky above. The quest names the Institute's Qualifications Review Representative Office, Academic Assembly Hall, Executive Office and Materials Office.
### LARGE +
A Fonta vending machine stands beside Heinry. After the quest Both Brains and Brawn, players can fill the Pankration Fonta Cup there.

## places
### STANDARD
The Qualifications Review Representative Office is on the ground floor, where the quest begins. The Institute is rebuilding the Bossuet Library, formerly the Alain Guillotin Memorial Library, which was destroyed in the accident, and has asked the public to donate books. Donation records lie in a pile of books under a shelf with a ladder.
### LARGE +
Research teams the NPCs mention include Material Collation, Fish-Type Clockwork Meka Research, a Finances Office (No. 5) and a Department of Statistics and Mathematics. A notice on a desk complains that paper use has been unusually high for three months, with a warning that paper is for research, not for wrapping snacks, propping up desks or horseplay, and that gambling with it is banned.
### ULTRA +
The donation paperwork is a running joke. The Statistics and Mathematics department is mistakenly asked to log book donations, and its staff, Juglar, Dupre and Bottin, file them in dry exchanges. The donations include a historical-criticism book, a boxed set of sword novels bought by mistake, a Steambird annual set, a negotiation manual from the Fortress of Meropide, and ten volumes on kinetic theory from a donor thanking the Institute. After the quest Villains, an anonymous donor returns a formula for 'Pure Water'.

## people
### STANDARD
Named staff are Chisseray (Material Collation Team Director), Damestier (Fish-Type Clockwork Meka Research Team Director), Lanty (Finances Office No. 5 Director), Vuillermoz (researcher), Denichere and Heinry (whose titles advertise Fonta), and, after the quest, Choiseul (Administrative Officer) and Raimondo (Senior Technician). Broglie (hydrology researcher) and Sevigne appear after later quests.
### LARGE +
Augustus Lovelace says the Institute was once a paradise for researchers until Raimondo and Choiseul took over. Nacker, a researcher who worked with Edwin Eastinghouse, caused a smaller explosion at the ruins. The two men, Edwin and Nacker, were fired and sent to the Fortress of Meropide.

## governance
### STANDARD
Unlike the Sumeru Akademiya, the Institute does not govern or educate; it researches machines and energy. It is run by administrators such as Choiseul and Raimondo, whose management has been criticised. Its inventions, many from Alain Guillotin, include clockwork mechanisms and kinetic cores.
### LARGE +
Salsa says the Institute has financial problems and that the building is not as pretty as the old ruins. She also says the ruins have been rebuilt and the Institute is considering a 'Ruins Tour' for outsiders. The Institute is controversial in Fontaine, since it is said to avoid responsibility.

## culture
### STANDARD
Researchers are earnest but bureaucratic: memos, departmental turf wars and dry humour fill the interactable texts. The Institute is a place for research and a 'temple of science', as the paper memo puts it.

## history
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
The Institute's old campus at the Central Laboratory was levelled when the Arkhium core in a field generator led by Edwin Eastinghouse overloaded. The blast sent blocks of condensed water and ruins into the sky. The Institute set up a new site near the ruins, and the Callas Line was shut down. In Fontaine Research Institute Chronicles (4.1) the Traveler helps survey and stabilise the ruins and earns the title Honorary Senior Researcher.
### LARGE +
A second, accidental explosion followed Nacker's tampering. Nacker, once in Edwin's Arkhium research group, was believed by Raimondo to be selling an Arkhium core. The Traveler shut down the prototype generator and installed inhibitors to stabilise the Arkhium traces. They later traced Nacker, who had a new benefactor named Concini, and learned that Edwin was also alive and continuing Arkhium research.

## dangers
### STANDARD
No danger is listed for the Institute itself. The Central Laboratory Ruins nearby held out-of-control meka and unstable Arkhium, until the Traveler installed inhibitors to stabilise the Arkhium traces.

## atmosphere

## entry: Chronie's Special Report

@spoiler: minor
### LARGE
An annotated copy of Chronie's 'Special Report' lies on a table near Raimondo. The report claims the Institute is hiding that the old Central Laboratory ruins remain unsafe and that a researcher, named as Nacker, died in a second explosion. Two memos attached disagree: one finder asks if it is true, while the Institute's reply calls it categorically false and says it has handed the matter to Maison Gardiennage.
