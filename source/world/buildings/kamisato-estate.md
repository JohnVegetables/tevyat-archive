## summary
### LITE
The Kamisato Estate is the large residence of the Kamisato Clan on Narukami Island in Inazuma, and a point of interest in the Inazuman Archon Quests. The clan head Kamisato Ayato and his younger sister Kamisato Ayaka live and work here. The Yashiro Commission, one of Inazuma's three Commissions, has its headquarters inside the main building.
### STANDARD +
The map text describes the Yashiro Commission as ostensibly handling the ceremonial and cultural life of the people, though its duties are so wide that even Shogunate officials cannot make sense of them. It also notes that Ayaka, the Shirasagi Himegimi, is better loved by the people than the head of the household.

## places
### STANDARD
The main building houses the Yashiro Commission Headquarters, which has its own map icon and interior; the Traveler compares its grand feel to Dawn Winery and the Jade Chamber. Bookshelves in the left-hand room hold volumes of A Legend of Sword, New Chronicles of the Six Kitsune and Princess Mina of the Fallen Nation. Furnishing items sold in game refer to rooms here, such as a kimono rack that holds the robe Ayaka wears for calligraphy.
### LARGE +
Outside the estate, the clan also runs the Komore Teahouse in Inazuma City, a members-only teahouse used as a base and private meeting place.

## people
### STANDARD
The household and staff named on the estate's page are the housekeeper Furuta, the house servant Koharu, the shrine maiden Toda, and the Yashiro Commission representatives Hirano, Hirotatsu and Madarame Hyakubei, with Hiratsuka also present. Thoma is the clan's chief retainer, and the ninja Sayu belongs to the Shuumatsuban, the clandestine group that the clan, and Ayato in particular, controls.
### LARGE +
Ayato handles governmental affairs of the Yashiro Commission; Ayaka looks after the clan's internal and external affairs, and her kindness and public work make her widely beloved.

## governance
### STANDARD
The Yashiro Commission, led by the Kamisato Clan, is part of the Tri-Commission under the Inazuma Shogunate and is in charge of shrines, festivals and cultural events. It has less influence than the Kanjou and Tenryou Commissions, yet each of the three can keep the others in check. The clan also controls the Shuumatsuban, a clandestine organisation of ninja that gathers information.
### LARGE +
Soldiers of the Shogun's Army assigned to the Yashiro Commission are described as relaxed, and the Commission is regarded as the least controversial of the three: it did not get involved with the Fatui during the Vision Hunt Decree or the Sakoku Decree, and Ayato declined a Fatui offer of cooperation. The Commission's reputation and influence rose a little afterwards.

## history
### STANDARD
When a generation of the clan caused great errors that shamed the family, Ayato and Ayaka's father worked to undo the damage and died, and their mother soon followed. Following their dying wishes, Ayato took the post of Yashiro Commissioner and protected Ayaka from those who saw them as expendable. Several groups tried to profit from the clan's weakness; Ayato outmanoeuvred them and restored its prestige.
### LARGE +
The clan's early history is unknown. Its martial and elemental skills (Ayaka's "Senho" sprint is a Kamisato Art) imply that the bloodline is trained in swordsmanship and elemental magic. A past head, great-grandfather of the siblings by wiki inference, is mentioned in the Archon Quest The Palace, Dyed in Black.

### ULTRA +
The wiki's staff lists name further people attached to the Yashiro Commission: Furusawa, Hirojiko, Kazumasa, Ogawa and Takami besides those above. At the Komore Teahouse, the owner is the dog Taroumaru, with Kozue, the retired ninja Nakanishi and Nobumori; Sayu and the Shiba Inu Inu Shoushou are listed with the Shuumatsuban.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
An orderly, formal noble house with quiet servants, calligraphy, shrine business and ledgers; official work of the Commission mixes with family life. Gentle, dutiful and politically careful rather than martial.

## quests
### STANDARD
A point of interest in the Inazuma Archon Quests; the headquarters can be entered as an interior. The clan's Komore Teahouse and Thoma's and Ayaka's stories connect to it.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the estate is the working seat of the Kamisato Clan and the Yashiro Commission, with Ayato as head and Ayaka as the public face of the clan. No change to its position has been recorded.
