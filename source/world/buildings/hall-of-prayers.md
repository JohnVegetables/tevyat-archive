## summary
### LITE
The Hall of Prayers, also called the assembly hall, is a ceremonial hall in the abandoned village of Okurov in Flamefeather Valley. The townspeople used to gather here and recite prayers that Doctor Moreau wrote for Zhara.
### STANDARD +
In the quest In the Dwelling of Life, the Traveler and Nastya reach it after defeating Furiosa near its bridge. Notes inside show that the doctor behind the prayers is actually Koshchei the Deathless.
## places
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
The hall is reached by lift and a bridge. Inside, the way forward is blocked by mechanisms the party must work: a chandelier on a rope, curtain switches and a locked door that Nastya opens. Beyond it lies the Teeming Mire.
## history
@version: 7.0
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
Two sets of papers lie in the hall. The Yellowed Records are a researcher's notes about the fae, about bodies formed from trees that regrow and grow stronger but lose language, and about experiments carried out in the sewers; they say the next step is to get townspeople to accept the "treatment", with Furiosa as an example. Less-Than-Legible Notes describe a "final ceremony" in which everyone will be reborn from an embryo like the writer, argue that an eternal view makes pain and joy alike meaningless, and mention Aksinya. The Eye of Graeae compares the handwriting and identifies the author as Koshchei.
### ULTRA +
The writer speaks of the "love of sameness" Aksinya pursued and of a love of the individual that must not be reduced to sameness. These notes are one character's in-world justification and should not be treated as neutral fact. Paimon reacts with alarm and recognises Furiosa's name from the bridge.
## people
### STANDARD
Furiosa guards the approach and had refused the doctor's "gift". Zhara, for whom the prayers were written, attacks the Traveler as a "Badbug". Nastya, a young child of the village, guides the Traveler and treats Furiosa as her idol.
## atmosphere
### STANDARD
A grand but melancholy hall with heavy curtains and a chandelier, abandoned, its quiet broken only by echoing footsteps and the weight of what happened here.
## quests
@version: 7.0
### LARGE
The hall is the setting of Forbidding Doors of Melancholy, the fifth quest of In the Dwelling of Life. The next quest is To Rest in a Forgotten Field.
