## summary
### LITE
The Palais Mermonia stands at the highest point of the Court of Fontaine and is where all of Fontaine's administration is handled. To Fontainians it is effectively the highest authority in the land. It houses Iudex Neuvillette's office and the government bodies called Maisons: Ordalie (the judiciary), Gestion (administration and documents), Cardinalice (financial approvals) and Gardiennage (law enforcement), along with the Marechaussee Phantom. It was also Furina's residence while she reigned as Hydro Archon. She moved out after 4.2.
### STANDARD +
Humans and Melusines staff it side by side. Even in Furina's day they did the real work of government, while she handled social and ceremonial appearances.

## geography
### STANDARD
The Palais crowns the northwestern part of the Court of Fontaine, overlooking the city. The area around the Palais has its own Statue of The Seven. An official description pictures citizens below leading orderly lives, as regular as the aquabus, under its gaze.

## places
### STANDARD
A front hall with a reception desk, staffed by the Melusine officer Sedene, controls access to the offices beyond. Neuvillette's office holds the court's case-file archives. The square outside is busy with Gardes, Gestionnaires and Palais staff by day, and Steambird reporters loiter behind the building.

## people
### STANDARD
Neuvillette, Chief Justice (Iudex) and head of the Maison Ordalie and the Marechaussee Phantom, works here. Sedene, a Melusine of the Marechaussee Phantom, keeps the front desk and decides who may enter. Gestionnaires of the Maison Gestion (Cornelia, Eunomia, Imena and others) process the paperwork, and Isadora works as one of the Palais's auditors.
### LARGE +
Other judges besides Neuvillette work out of the Palais and can be assigned trials. Gardes such as Bruneau stand near the entrance, the Cardinal Assistant Plessia works there, and Steambird staff (the reporter Houallet and the photographer Antila) watch from behind the building. Isadora says her work is confidential, so she cannot complain about it to anyone.

## governance
### STANDARD
The Maison Ordalie runs the judicial system under the Iudex. The Maison Gestion manages documents and administrative tasks, the Maison Cardinalice oversees financial approvals, and the Maison Gardiennage provides law enforcement through the Gardes and Gardemeks. The Marechaussee Phantom, an elite investigative force made up mostly of Melusines, answers directly to the Chief Justice. The Palais also confers Fontaine's honorary titles.
### LARGE +
The Palais is famous for red tape. Replacing a single typewriter keycap takes several rounds of forms, and the clockwork-toy maker Livre needed a police officer's help to get paid for meka the Palais had ordered, because the Gestionnaire handling his file kept him waiting on forms. The Maison Gestion has an official seal, a new key-operated stamping machine, and rules requiring a Kamera record of everyone who uses it. The Fortress of Meropide counts the Palais among its best customers. The Court's jurisdiction does not extend inside the Fortress.

## history
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
For about five centuries Furina, playing the Hydro Archon, lived at the Palais, while Neuvillette and the staff governed. Furina once tried to join its daily work out of boredom, but humans and Melusines alike politely insisted there were no duties that needed their god. During the 4.x crisis the Palais was the Traveler's point of contact with Neuvillette. After Furina's trial and Focalors' sacrifice in 4.2, Furina abdicated, left the office's affairs (though not the title) to Neuvillette, packed her bags and left the Palais Mermonia for a rented apartment.
### LARGE +
In Act II (4.0), Navia talked her way past Sedene into Neuvillette's office to search the case files for the witness Vacher, found nothing, and told Neuvillette his apology for her father Callas sounded empty. In Act III (4.1), Neuvillette summoned the Traveler here and proposed sending them undercover into the Fortress of Meropide on a false charge. After the Poisson disaster (4.2) the Palais was buzzing with activity, and in Neuvillette's office he and Furina argued over the list of victims and the missing prophecy slate. Furina fled, and Neuvillette concluded that only a trial would make her talk.
### ULTRA +
Furina says it has been a long time since she visited the Palais, and that no longer working together is good for both her and Neuvillette.

## culture
### LARGE
The Palais grants honorary titles. Recipients normally attend an investiture ceremony, and the ceremony for a title as high as Duke is more elaborate. Callas of the Spina di Rosula once turned down the title of Baron. Wriothesley, offered the title of Duke, asked simply to sign the certificates. After many workdays of correspondence the Palais agreed, and Neuvillette bestowed the title in person.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Formal, busy and bureaucratic: Melusine and human clerks at their desks, forms waiting for the right stamp, Gardes and reporters outside, and the Iudex in his office reading case files. Wriothesley once found Neuvillette standing drenched in the rain by the road near the Palais and held an umbrella over him.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the Palais Mermonia remains the seat of Fontaine's government, with Neuvillette handling the nation's affairs from his office there.
### STANDARD +
Furina no longer lives or works there. Sedene now tells visitors that Furina has left her official post and seemed much more relaxed the last time she saw her.

## entry: Neuvillette's Office
@keys: Neuvillette's office, Iudex's office, case file archives
### STANDARD
The Iudex's office in the Palais Mermonia holds the court's case-file archives, a place outsiders normally never reach. Neuvillette keeps working there through crises. Sigewinne writes to him from the Fortress of Meropide and adds anecdotes in case he is bored at the Palais. In 4.0 Sedene let Navia and the Traveler through after Navia said they had come to archive material for a follow-up case. In 4.1 Neuvillette gave the Traveler a Fruity Trio cake from his desk before sending them into the Fortress of Meropide on a false charge, and in 4.2 Sedene let them in as welcome guests.

## entry: The Maisons of the Palais
@keys: Gestionnaire, Gestionnaires, A Certain Stamp, Trial Court Gazette
### LARGE
The Palais houses Fontaine's four Maisons. Ordalie runs the courts under the Iudex. Gestion handles documents and administration, with offices for confidential items and staff matters, and its employees are called Gestionnaires. Cardinalice oversees financial approvals. Gardiennage enforces the law with Gardes, Gardemeks, the Special Security and Surveillance Patrol and the Marechaussee Phantom. In the Fontaine world quest "A Certain Stamp", the Gestionnaire Iaune, after a night of drafting with Roialte and Semaine, must still have a proposal stamped by Thales's new key-operated machine and then by Difor before it can be printed.

## entry: Furina at the Palais
@keys: Furina's residence, Furina's former residence
@keys2: Palais Mermonia, Furina
@version: 4.2
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
While she reigned, Furina lived at the Palais and attended social and diplomatic functions, while others did the real government work and sometimes even gave her speeches. After the prophecy was resolved she felt she had no place in the new order, packed her bags and left the Palais and the Opera Epiclese. In her new rented apartment she left her luggage unpacked and lay staring at the ceiling, until a struggling troupe drew her back to the stage.

## entry: Palais Mermonia Bulletin Board
@keys: Palais Mermonia Bulletin Board, Whirling Waltz
@availability: limited
### ULTRA
During the limited-time event Whirling Waltz, a bulletin board stood by the Palais entrance. It carried event listings, a note that the Trial Court's annual judicial statistics report could be requested from Officer Sedene of the Marechaussee Phantom, an offer to subscribe to the monthly Palais Mermonia Trial Court Gazette (legal documents and case files compiled by the Maison Gardiennage), and a lost-and-found list. The board was removed after the event.
