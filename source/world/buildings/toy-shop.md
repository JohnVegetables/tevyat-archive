## summary
### LITE
The Toy Shop is a small unnamed stall in Liyue Harbor kept by Granny Shan. Its map description says people always want to enjoy themselves whatever the time or situation, and that sentiment seems to be why she has kept running it year after year. It sells spinning tops, wooden swords, kites and other knick-knacks, with rarer toys sometimes on offer.
### STANDARD +
Only Mora is accepted. The stock includes a zither, lanterns, firework-style toys and a launching tube.

## places
### STANDARD
A single stall on Chihu Rock, Liyue Harbor, marked as a special souvenir shop on the map.

## people
### STANDARD
Granny Shan is the owner. The wiki says she has been a toymaker for over forty years and especially likes making kites. She is the aunt of Zhenyuan, according to her wiki infobox.

## culture
### STANDARD
Her toys are simple, traditional playthings: tops, wooden swords and kites.
### LARGE +
The shop's list also includes a floral zither, Xiao Lanterns and a Launch Tube, along with toys named for fireworks and festival scenes. The wiki gives no further description of them.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
A cheerful corner stand run by a patient old maker, with a sense of long continuity.

## travel
### STANDARD
In the Chihu Rock plaza of Liyue Harbor.
### ULTRA +
The wiki dates the shop's map entry to Version 2.7. Its stock consists of Mora-priced items only; the Chinese name literally means 'toy stall'.
