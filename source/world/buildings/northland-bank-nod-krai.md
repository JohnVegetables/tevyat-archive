## summary
### LITE
This is the Nasha Town branch of the Northland Bank, the Snezhnayan bank with branches around Teyvat. It is managed by Jarosznski. From the outside it looks unremarkable, but its inside is a fortress of defenses and alarms.
### STANDARD +
Northland Bank hails from Snezhnaya and, according to a loading-screen text, the Fatui use its funds and sometimes its branches as bases when they operate abroad. The Nod-Krai branch is a regional office, separate from the bank's headquarters in Snezhnograd.

## people
### LITE
Jarosznski is the manager. Drahomira, Kamenka and Kudratov, an agent, also work here.
### STANDARD
Jarosznski is the smooth-talking manager who sells loans. He greets visitors by offering a business loan or the "Happy Home Loan", which has zero interest for the first three installments. Asked about the catch, he says there are packages for different needs and that the bank needs a name, address, guarantor and a detailed three-month travel log, all to offer a more professional payment plan. Drahomira and Kamenka are listed as NPCs of the branch; Kudratov is an agent.
### LARGE +
Jarosznski admits he doubted locals would pay but says the branch's reimbursement rate is higher than Fontaine's branches. He credits this to telling at-risk clients that Northland Bank will find "suitable" jobs for people who cannot pay their debts, after which, he jokes, they settle within days. Whether that means anything darker is not stated.

## places
### STANDARD
The branch is a common-looking shopfront in Nasha Town that blends in with the town's other shops; the furnishing text describes a two-story building. The interior, per that text, is an intricately built fortress rigged with defenses and alarms that will teach trespassers a painful lesson.
### LARGE +
The vault-like layout is a security measure, as the furnishing text says that security is paramount to a bank.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
A businesslike shopfront in a town otherwise built from scrap metal. Outwardly ordinary, it hides tight security behind polite offers of credit.

## governance
### STANDARD
It belongs to the Northland Bank, a Snezhnayan institution, and acts as a branch office. The bank's map text names Regrator, Pantalone, the Ninth Fatui Harbinger, as the master of its web, but the Nasha Town branch is not shown doing Fatui work.
### LARGE +
Wiki lore on the bank says it was founded by Pantalone and that new branches may be set up by other Harbingers; nothing specific is stated about who opened the Nasha Town branch.
### ULTRA +
Nasha Town has no official governing body; it is shaped by the influence of the Voynich Guild, the Curatorium of Secrets, Speranza and the Fatui. The branch operates in that mix of rival influences.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the branch remains open in Nasha Town, with Jarosznski as manager.

### STANDARD
Jarosznski is the manager of the Nasha Town Northland Bank. His idle line asks whether a customer needs a loan for their business. He is polite and persuasive, pushes the "Happy Home Loan", and deflects questions about the catch by pointing to flexible packages and a thorough personal history requirement.
