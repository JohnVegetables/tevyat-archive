## summary
### LITE
River Talionis is a small wharf beneath the Opera Epiclese in the Court of Fontaine, the external reception area of the Fortress of Meropide. A single boat carries prisoners through an underground tunnel straight to the Fortress.
### STANDARD +
New inmates are registered here before the crossing. The receptionist, Marette, handles the paperwork, including recording that a sentence has been served.
### LARGE +
The place sits where Fontaine's court system meets the autonomous prison: sentencing happens above, and the sentence begins at this wharf.
### ULTRA +
The name is Latin-styled; the wiki reads it as "River of Retribution" after the phrase lex talionis, a translation note rather than in-game text.

## geography
### STANDARD
The wharf lies under the Opera Epiclese, where Fontaine's trials are held, and is reached by an elevator behind the opera house. It has a small dock with one boat, which runs through an underground tunnel to the Fortress of Meropide.
### LARGE +
This is the Fortress's formal front door; other ways in (the spotlights around the prison, a broken pipe leading to the dormitories) are unofficial.

## people
### STANDARD
Marette is the Fortress of Meropide Receptionist. She is a little sleepy and procedural (her idle line asks you to register), remembers the Traveler and Paimon as the pair the Iudex had personally escorted over, and tells them not to make waves until their sentence is complete.
### LARGE +
After Step 4 of Deluge of Wrathful Waters she updates the Traveler's file, declares the sentence officially served and says they are free to go.

## governance
### STANDARD
The Fortress of Meropide is Fontaine's de facto prison but is technically self-governing and outside the jurisdiction of Fontaine's legal system; those sent there are in effect exiles. The Court of Fontaine only sends some Garde to help with security (per A Tea Party Most Thorny). The reception wharf is the formal point where a convicted person passes from one authority to the other.
