## summary
### LITE
Hotel Debord is a famous restaurant in Quartier Narbonnais of the Court of Fontaine. Its owner, Vaneigem, holds that art is the best seasoning for good food and often invites performers to entertain diners.
### STANDARD +
The interior is designed so guests can see performances from any seat. It was founded long ago by the chefs Modeste and Savelli, and is tied to the Gateau Debord and the head chef Escoffier.

## places
### STANDARD
Downstairs are the dining hall, with the host Sanguinetti and the hostess Leone; upstairs hold more staff including Argalia, Rodamonte, Renaud and the boss Vaneigem. Sanguinetti sells food, ingredients and recipes, with daily restocks; Reputation Rank 4 in Fontaine gives a permanent 10 percent discount (gameplay).
### LARGE +
A notice board advertises a dazzling night of "twin stars" revealing a secret, tying the restaurant to its stage shows.

## people
### STANDARD
Vaneigem owns it, Sanguinetti hosts and the critic Eloise dines in the hall. Savanna is the current head chef. Escoffier, a former head chef, was also Furina's chef, and Monica is a former chef.
### LARGE +
The Codex Debord holds Modeste's original recipes and is limited to current and former head chefs, with copies made as needed.

## history
@spoiler: minor
@version: 5.6
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
Modeste and Savelli, two chefs from Fleuve Cendre, made Fontainian cuisine popular through ingredients found on their expeditions and founded the restaurant, which was soon a hit with wealthy diners. About ten years later they found a container in sunken Remurian ruins whose contents sharpened taste, and Modeste created the original Gateau Debord, limited to sixteen orders a day.
### LARGE +
The ingredient eventually cost Modeste his sense of taste, and he took his own life in despair. Savelli, who learned the cause from a letter, took the blame for his death to protect both reputations. The restaurant's name fell for a time, but careful management restored it over decades.
### ULTRA +
Before the Traveler arrived, Escoffier led the kitchen after the hotel offered her its kitchen on learning she was Furina's chef; a cook-off left both the former head chef and the chosen successor judging her the better fit. A lumiline food-safety incident, actually caused by Monica, led Escoffier to take responsibility; she served a sentence in the Fortress of Meropide and Savanna took over. During the Treasured Above All story quest, Navia persuaded Escoffier to return, and afterwards she declined the post but agreed to visit periodically to check standards.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Opulent and theatrical: dining as spectacle, with performers and a polished kitchen behind it.

### LARGE
The legendary cake created by Modeste and later recreated by Escoffier. Originally limited to sixteen a day and queued for; it is regarded as the pinnacle of Fontaine desserts of its time.
