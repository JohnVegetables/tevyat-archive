## summary
### LITE
The Pale Crown Palace is the Belyi Tsar's mausoleum in White Birch Snowgrave, Snezhnaya. Once one of his palaces, it became his tomb after he died in the Cataclysm 500 years ago, and his body grew into the Tsar's White Birch, a pale mass of 'nerves' over the ruin. A hidden train line leads to it.
### STANDARD +
The Eye of Graeae says it was created here in the research facility Hvergelmir, and in the 7.0 quests the Traveler recovers a fragment of its power and fights the palace's guardian, Zver.

## geography
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
The palace lies beneath the Tsar's White Birch. Its map layers are the Hall of the Tsar's Palace, the Hanging Corridor, the Spiraling Tower, 'Where the Hidden Door Was Knocked' and 'Once, the Court of Bliss'. Inside are a Frozen Royal Hall with a throne, a Suspended Tower, sealed gates and a lift to the upper levels, and the Ruined Hvergelmir.
### LARGE +
The gates and doors are blocked by Prime Ice and by the White Birch's tendrils, and are opened with statues, heat pipes and prototype Kresnik's Torches.

## places
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
The Frozen Royal Hall, where two stone statues belong in front of the throne; the Suspended Tower, which must be rotated to connect heat pipes; the Ruined Hvergelmir below; and the Court of Eternal Bliss, entered through a door that the White Birch keeps regrowing over until all three Eye fragments are gathered.
### LARGE +
A prototype Kresnik's Torch in the palace recalls the Tsar's experiments, and the palace is where Kresnik's flame was first kindled before it spread across Snezhnaya.

## people
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
No living residents. The Belyi Tsar lies in the White Birch. Zver, his two-headed winged lion companion created by Koshchei, guarded the remains for 500 years. Pavlina and Koshchei once worked in the Hvergelmir laboratories here, and Aksinya, the Tsar's adoptive child, appears in the palace's flashbacks.
### LARGE +
In the To Gaze quest the Traveler glimpses two past researchers, Izmaylov and Azazel, and Pavlina's cat Lara, which she left in the researchers' care.

## governance
### STANDARD
The wiki calls it the Belyi Tsar's private residence and, in the quest text, his itinerant court, where he welcomed humans alongside the fae. His research institute Hvergelmir worked beneath him here.

## history
### LITE
The Belyi Tsar welcomed humans and fae in this palace, and Kresnik's flame was first lit here. After he fell fighting the Abyss and its beasts during the Cataclysm, his remains were returned to the palace.
### STANDARD +
Zver carried the Tsar's body home and guarded it for five centuries. The research facility Hvergelmir, where the Eye of Graeae was made, was hidden inside the palace.
### LARGE +
The researchers' notes found inside describe experiments on prisoners and Snegovik concerning 'love', and show Izmaylov worrying that the new Tsaritsa's rumored ban on alchemy would end Hvergelmir's bio-alchemy research and the Tsar's last aspiration. By its own account, the Eye of Graeae realised the danger of Aksinya's experiment, asked to be divided into three segments, and persuaded Koshchei, Pavlina and Alvis to stop her.
### ULTRA +
Aksinya later takes the restored Eye in the Court of Eternal Bliss, and the Traveler fights her and the awakened Primordial Human; both Aksinya and the Eye then fall into the Shadow Realm.

## atmosphere
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
A silent snow-buried grave: frozen halls, ice-locked doors and a pale tree-like growth creeping through the rooms. The Train's navigation text calls it 'a stop at which no one arrives, a snow-covered grave'.

## dangers
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
Chimeric monsters such as armoured beasts, winged elephants and horned bears; Winged Soarer Knights, Winged Halberd-Knights, Glaciomancers, Glacial Wardens and a Rugged Elder Leshy; the White Birch, which drains the Eye of Graeae's energy if the Traveler stands too close; and the boss Zver.

## travel
### STANDARD
Reached by a hidden railway summoned by a mechanism and an old prototype train operated by the Eye of Graeae. The Traveler's own Train has a navigation stop there.

## quests
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
Quest series On One Side a Palace, On the Other a Tomb: To Gaze Upon the Dreaming Pale Crown, and The Stone Beasts, Like Ghouls Seated. The finale, An Ark That Used to Be a Soul, forms the series Her Palace Collapses Into the Blizzard.
### LARGE +
In The Stone Beasts, Like Ghouls Seated, the Traveler restores statues, shoots a prototype Kresnik's Torch, passes the Suspended Tower and defeats Zver before reaching the Ruined Hvergelmir to recover the fragment.

## status
@version: 7.1
### STANDARD
Abandoned and accessible. Its guardian is dead and the Court of Eternal Bliss has been entered.

## entry: Zver
@keys: Zver
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
Zver is a Chimeric Winged Lion made by Koshchei: a lion and a bird embryo fused so that it has two heads. Because it had some sentience, the Belyi Tsar spared it and brought it into his court; it became the commander of the Aberrant Chimeric Monsters in the war against the Abyss. After the Tsar's death it guarded the palace.
### LARGE +
When the Traveler defeated Zver in 7.0, it mocked the Eye of Graeae and asked the Tsar if it could finally follow him into the afterlife.

## entry: Tsar's White Birch
@keys: Tsar's White Birch, White Birch
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
An overgrowth of nerves from the Belyi Tsar's corpse that grows over the ruin and saps energy from the Eye of Graeae if someone stands too close. Part of his body became the White Birch after he was buried five hundred years ago.
### LARGE +
The in-game train description says the Tsar's body 'continues to protect all who dwell in Snezhnaya'.
