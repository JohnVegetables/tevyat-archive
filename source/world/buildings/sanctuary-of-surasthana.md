## summary
### LITE
The Sanctuary of Surasthana is the palace at the very top of Sumeru City, above the Akademiya, in the crown of the Divine Tree. It is the residence of the Dendro Archon, Lesser Lord Kusanali (Nahida). For centuries no one outside the Akademiya's sages had seen the god living there, and the sages used the place as her prison. In the Chapter III Archon Quest she was freed and the Traveler met her face to face for the first time.
### STANDARD +
The name is Sanskrit-derived and means roughly "abode of a god". Under the sages' rule the Sanctuary was off limits even to ordinary Akademiya staff; since Nahida's rescue the Traveler and Paimon can visit, and she receives them there.

## geography
### STANDARD
Sumeru City is built on the Divine Tree. The Grand Bazaar and ordinary buildings sit at the bottom, the Akademiya in the middle, and the Sanctuary at the very top. The official map text calls it a palace at the highest point of the city. Reaching it means going up through the Akademiya.
### LARGE +
The map description before the rescue says the Dendro Archon is believed to dwell here and that no one apart from the sages has seen her for the last few hundred years. After Chapter III Act V the text changes to say that the light of wisdom has shone out from here over all of Sumeru under the sages' guidance. The interior opens only after the quest Jnagarbha Day; before that the Traveler sees only the outside, where the guard Ashk stands.
### ULTRA +
Branches are seen growing from the top of the Sanctuary in the closing scenes of Chapter III Act V, and a seed-like object with branches is glimpsed inside afterwards. The wiki records these as images only; canon gives no explanation, so treat them as atmosphere rather than a plot point.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Quiet, enclosed and still. Nahida jokes in a voice-over that the wind never blows in the Sanctuary. A scene here feels like a high, sealed room above a busy city, with the noise of Sumeru City far below.
### LARGE +
After the rescue the mood is lighter. Nahida talks with visitors here and, after the Archon Quest victory, answers the Traveler's questions in the Sanctuary. In a voice-over about Dori she recalls that Dori's first words on seeing the palace were "Nice palace. How much?" and that she has been unable to work out how much Mora it would be worth.

## history
### STANDARD
After Greater Lord Rukkhadevata died in the cataclysm five hundred years ago, the sages of the Akademiya found her successor, Lesser Lord Kusanali, and brought her to Sumeru City. Disappointed that the child god showed neither great wisdom nor great power, they shut her in the Sanctuary and turned the people's devotion to Rukkhadevata's memory. A device there, made by Rukkhadevata so she could meditate in peace, was altered by the Grand Sage of the day so that it could no longer be controlled from the inside; Alhaitham describes it as one god trapped by the power of another.
### LARGE +
Under the Akademiya's rule the people of Sumeru almost never saw their Archon and most knew little about her. In Chapter III the sages' collaboration with the Fatui Harbinger Dottore aimed to replace her with a made god and to trap her consciousness in the Akasha. In Dreams, Emptiness, Deception the Traveler briefly trades places with Nahida's puppet-self and sees, from inside her real body in the Sanctuary, Scaramouche becoming a god. In Jnagarbha Day the Traveler, jailed on purpose in the confinement room near the Sanctuary, wakes her sealed consciousness. Alhaitham's plan then fills the Akasha with a forged order saying she has escaped the Sanctuary, drawing the guards away.
### ULTRA +
Grand Sage Azar went to the Sanctuary alone to check the order; because the Akasha had made him expect her absence, he at first failed to perceive her. Cyno met him there and demanded he plead guilty before the archon herself. Dehya frees the Traveler, who then meets Nahida in the Sanctuary. She reverts the Akasha to her own use, revokes the sages' access and prepares to stop the false god, giving the Traveler an upgraded Akasha Terminal. Later Act V quests start from the Sanctuary, including the preparation for the fight in the Joururi Workshop and the conversation after the victory.

## people
### STANDARD
Nahida lives here, and the guard Ashk stands outside. Before Chapter III only the sages were allowed in; after the rescue Viraf, stationed at the Akademiya, remarks to the Traveler that entering the Sanctuary is normally forbidden.
### LARGE +
Cyno, then still General Mahamatra, says at the confrontation with Azar that under the Akademiya's own regulations even its staff were forbidden to come here. Visitors since the rescue include the Traveler and Paimon.

## governance
### STANDARD
The Sanctuary has no government of its own. In practice the sages held it as a restricted site, and Nahida had little power over the nation while confined. After the rescue she can overrule the sages; four of the six sages were removed from their posts and sent to retrain in the Avidya Forest.

## status
### STANDARD
As of 7.1 the Sanctuary of Surasthana is the Dendro Archon's palace at the top of Sumeru City, no longer a prison. Nahida is free and the false-god scheme is over. Whether she still lives there permanently is not stated in the sources read.

## entry: Rukkhadevata's meditation device
@keys: meditation device, Surasthana device, confinement device
@keys2: Nahida, Kusanali, Rukkhadevata, sages
@version: 3.2
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Inside the Sanctuary is a device made by Greater Lord Rukkhadevata so she could isolate herself and meditate undisturbed. According to Alhaitham in Jnagarbha Day, the Grand Sage of five hundred years ago modified it so that it can no longer be operated from the inside. The Traveler reaches Nahida's sealed consciousness not through the device but through a modified Akasha Terminal used from the confinement room.
### LARGE +
A barrier surrounds Nahida in the Sanctuary until Cyno forces Azar to release her; the barrier then disappears when the Traveler enters. The wiki adds that, because the device was made by divine power, Nahida could not escape it on her own.

## entry: The rescue of Nahida (Jnagarbha Day)
@keys: Jnagarbha Day, rescue of Nahida, Nahida's rescue
@keys2: Sanctuary of Surasthana, Alhaitham, Cyno, Azar, Akademiya
@version: 3.2
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
On Jnagarbha Day, when the Akademiya enters new information into the Akasha, Alhaitham's plan has the Traveler jailed on purpose in the confinement room, where a modified Akasha Terminal wakes Nahida's consciousness. Nilou dances outside the Akademiya while the guards, thanks to Alhaitham's forged order, run into the city to hunt for the supposedly escaped Archon; Dehya and the Eremites deal with them in the Bazaar. Cyno confronts Azar at the Sanctuary, after which Dehya frees the Traveler and the Traveler meets Nahida in person.
### LARGE +
The forged order spreads through the Akasha, so guards receive it as if it came from the sages. Azar checks the Akasha himself, discovers an order he did not issue and goes to the Sanctuary to verify it. Nahida wakes only after working through her own self-doubt about being a worthy archon.

## entry: Nahida's isolation
@keys: Nahida's isolation, imprisoned in the Sanctuary
@keys2: Nahida, Kusanali, sages
@version: 3.2
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Azar's justification in Jnagarbha Day is that it is better to keep her isolated in the Sanctuary so the Akademiya does not become embroiled in turmoil; Paimon calls this a pathetic excuse. Nahida was nevertheless able to learn through the Akasha and to act in a limited way in the outside world, for example through the bionic puppet Katheryne and through the mad scholars exiled to Aaru Village, without leaving the Sanctuary.
### LARGE +
The original text of her "Toy Box" character story says the Akademiya researchers never supported her novel ideas and would not satisfy her wish for more toys, so she asked for only wood and some tools and made chessboards, cards and puzzles herself, following the instructions offered by the Akasha. After Where the Boat of Consciousness Lies the story is altered and that line about the researchers no longer appears.
