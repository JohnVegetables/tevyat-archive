## summary
### LITE
Naganohara Fireworks is the fireworks shop of the Naganohara Family in Hanamizaka, on the north bank of Inazuma City. NPC Takami of the Yashiro Commission calls it the oldest and one of the best fireworks shops in all of Inazuma. It is owned by the pyrotechnician Yoimiya, who learned the craft from her father Naganohara Ryuunosuke, and it organises the Naganohara Fireworks Show that lights up festivals across Inazuma.
### STANDARD +
Besides fireworks the shop sells small toys and curios that children and adults both love, some imported by the Crux fleet and some made by Yoimiya. It is a neighbourhood gathering place as much as a business: Yoimiya treats chatting with customers as part of the work.

## geography
### STANDARD
The shop stands in the eastern suburb of Hanamizaka, one of the three parts of Inazuma City, north of the river through the city. Outside it, the aspiring comedians Yamaue and Tokunaga rehearse their routine, with a shiba called Pika for an audience, and Ryuunosuke can be found nearby.
### LARGE +
A notice board by the shop carries a joking warning from the fire brigade: it asks Yoimiya to stop risky behaviour and says that otherwise the brigade will have to report her to the Yashiro Commissioner again.

## people
### STANDARD
Yoimiya runs the shop and is renowned in Hanamizaka as the most skilled pyrotechnician in Inazuma, the "Queen of the Summer Festival". Her father Naganohara Ryuunosuke, the previous owner, handed it over because his hearing had faded after years of work with explosions, but still insists he makes the best fireworks in Inazuma and is proud of his ability to tune colours. The Naganohara Family's other present member named in canon is Yoimiya; her mother is mentioned but unnamed.
### LARGE +
Yamaue and Tokunaga, two young men who want to be professional comedians, practise outside the shop. Takami of the Yashiro Commission, who manages the fireworks show on Amakane Island, saw the show as a child with her parents and decided to work in the field; she credits Yoimiya for helping her dream of presenting Inazuma's fireworks to all Teyvat come true.

## culture
### STANDARD
The shop's tradition is the paper slip. When a family orders fireworks for a special occasion, the order comes with a slip in an envelope on which a Naganohara code records the exact recipe. If a customer shows the slip years later, Yoimiya says, the shop can recreate that very firework, even ten or a hundred years on. The shop also sells knickknacks: Scatterbloom Marbles that bloom when knocked together, Leaping Tops that chirp as they spin, the Neon Dragonfly that changes colour in flight, the "somewhat-failed" Onikabuto Chariot, and the Katydid Coil, a ring of flammables and spices hung up to repel summer mosquitoes.
### LARGE +
Hanamizaka's children believe an old folk song's "rainbows" can be traded to Yoimiya for fireworks of their own, so they bring shiny trinkets to the shop. She went along with the idea and expanded what counts as a rainbow, accepting coloured stones or shells, but refuses to take valuables and instead helps find the owner. Arataki Itto is a regular buyer of her specially made knickknacks. By-products of firework making can be mixed with plant extract to produce cheap scentless candles, per a furnishing description.
### ULTRA +
Ryuunosuke explains that colours, shapes and firing intervals of fireworks once carried meanings that islanders used to signal one another over distance, and that different ore powders give different colours. Yoimiya's own view, voiced in her story quest, is that fireworks vanish in a flash but the feelings people attach to them do not.

## history
### STANDARD
The Naganohara Family is said to have been in Inazuma for around 500 years, and Yoimiya's first character story traces the show to a commemorative display organised by the Yashiro Commission over a century ago, made by the best pyrotechnicians of the time. It then became more frequent and eventually a show in which families join by setting off their own fireworks, with the shop choosing the finale. In Yoimiya's first year as owner, Kamisato Ayaka asked her to include the Raiden Shogun's emblem, the Electro Mitsudomoe, because the Shogun would watch from Tenshukaku. Yoimiya laid thousands of launch tubes across the city so that, from the Tenshukaku angle, the fireworks formed the crest.
### LARGE +
Ryuunosuke recalls that Yoimiya was once invited by the Yashiro Commission to host a ceremony and accidentally started a very big fire, and the angry fire brigade wanted fireworks banned from the occasion. Imatani Saburou proposed to his wife Imatani Kaori at the show fifty years before the Traveler's visit. Yoimiya has had the Crux fleet carry Naganohara fireworks overseas to spread the shop's name.

## quests
### STANDARD
The shop is a hub of Yoimiya's story quest, the Carassius Auratus Chapter Act I, "Dreamlike Timelessness". In "The Firework Girl's Strange Stories" the Traveler meets children chasing the invented Great Mujina Youkai; Yoimiya comforts them and invites the Traveler to the show. In "A Sliver of Hope" and "Together Under the Fireworks" the shop hides Sakujirou, a man who slipped back into Inazuma after the Sakoku Decree and wants to leave again. Yoimiya works with the Traveler and with the Imatani family until Sakujirou can make peace with his childhood friend Imatani Keisuke of the Tenryou Commission, and the Traveler receives the Naganohara Fireworks Note, the recipe of a special gold firework.
### LARGE +
The Tenryou Commission closes in on the shop during the final part, which is why Yoimiya fears for Sakujirou's last chance to carry out his wish.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the shop is still run by Yoimiya, with her father living in Hanamizaka. The Sakoku Decree, which was in force while Takami spoke of shipping lanes, has since been lifted. No 5.x-7.1 content changes the shop.

## entry: The paper slip system
@keys: Naganohara paper slip, firework recipe slip, Naganohara Fireworks Note
@keys2: Naganohara Fireworks, Yoimiya
@version: 2.0
### STANDARD
The Naganohara shop writes each custom firework recipe in a family code on a slip of paper and gives it to the buyer. Returning the slip lets the shop reproduce the same firework later; the slip is handed back instead of copied, so a customer's copy is the only record. At the end of her quest Yoimiya gives the Traveler a Naganohara Fireworks Note for the gold firework her father made for them, so the Traveler can request it again later, however much changes over time.

## entry: Sakujirou's shelter
@keys: Sakujirou, Imatani Keisuke, Imatani Saburou, Imatani Kaori
@keys2: Naganohara Fireworks, Tenryou Commission, Yoimiya
@version: 2.0
### LARGE
Sakujirou left Inazuma because it seemed too peaceful and wanted to see the world. He sneaked back after the Sakoku Decree because he regretted it and wanted to make up for past mistakes, and the Tenryou Commission chased him, so he took refuge at Naganohara Fireworks until it was safe for him to leave. In "A Sliver of Hope" Yoimiya remembers an elderly couple among the shop's clients, the Imatanis of Konda Village, who might help; their son Keisuke turns out to be Sakujirou's former childhood friend and an officer of the Tenryou Commission, who regrets confiscating Visions.
