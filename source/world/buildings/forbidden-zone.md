## summary
### LITE
The Forbidden Zone is the hidden lowest part of the Fortress of Meropide, Fontaine's undersea prison. It is built over Primordial Water, and a giant sluice gate there was made to stop that water from flooding Fontaine. Only the Fortress's Administrator, Wriothesley, may enter, and he reaches it by a secret stair from his office.
### STANDARD +
Prisoners tend to go quiet when asked about it, though the guards confirm that it exists. For years almost nobody remembered why the Fortress had been built, and the zone holds the answer.

## geography
### STANDARD
A hidden staircase opens beneath the Duke's Office in the Administrative Area, and the office's basement has a lift down to the zone. Three large isolation doors lead into a chamber with the sluice gate and a bank of dashboards. A glass-walled room off the Duke's basement overlooks a huge ship under construction.
### LARGE +
After Masquerade of the Guilty (the end of Chapter IV) the door to that glass room stays open permanently, but the ship is no longer there.

## places
### STANDARD
The main chamber holds the sluice gate and the dashboards. Wriothesley watched the readings for years without knowing what they measured. A secret works nearby holds the Wingalet, a huge vessel he had built as a lifeboat in case the Primordial Water broke through.
### LARGE +
Wriothesley modelled the ship on the legendary golden ship Fortuna of ancient Remuria, using a factory's worth of labour, materials and technology. He showed the Traveler and Paimon the ship before telling them its purpose.

## governance
### STANDARD
Entry belongs to the Administrator alone. Wriothesley became Administrator and kept the zone from the inmates; even many of the people who worked on the ship were not told what it was for.

## history
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
When exiles were first sent to the seaside in the age of the Hydro Archon Egeria, they began building the Fortress under her guidance to guard a secret deep beneath the waves. Their community grew into a home for sinners, and over centuries the reason for its founding faded from memory. Wriothesley learned it from an elderly research historian. As the prophecy says, if the gate fails, Fontaine will drown and all will dissolve into the sea.
### LARGE +
About a year before the Traveler arrived, Wriothesley noticed the dashboards creeping upward without knowing why. Freminet later traced Childe's route to a sewer opening and found the seawater there diluted with Primordial Water, which let Wriothesley work out what the gauges measured. Soon after, the water behind the gate burst through. Neuvillette pushed it back and resealed it in the vault, for the time being.

### ULTRA +
Wriothesley says the Fortress can do more than visitors expect, and treats the ship as a long-shot hedge: he told the Traveler that hope is often found at the end of a voyage, and that trying could not hurt. Lyney, sent to look into Childe's disappearance, had already established from the guards that a forbidden zone existed, even though prisoners refused to discuss it.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Primordial Water dissolved Fontainians who touched it, until the change of 4.2. In 4.1 it was seeping into the sea around the Fortress, and the Fortress was evacuated 'upwards and outwards' during the breach.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Heavy isolation doors, the hum of gauges and a sense of pressure from the water above and beyond. The Duke's quiet confidence makes it feel more like a closely kept secret than a prison cell.

## quests
### STANDARD
Revealed in the Archon Quest Chapter IV, Act IV, in 'Secret Keepers and Forbidden Zones', after the Traveler answers Wriothesley's riddles about the Fortress's hidden rules. The breach is in 'Calamitous Tread' (4.1).

## status
### LITE
Its sluice gate was resealed by Neuvillette in 4.1.
### STANDARD +
What became of the chamber after the flood of the prophecy is not described in the sources read; treat it as unknown.

## entry: The Dashboards and the Sluice Gate
@keys: sluice gate, forbidden zone dashboards, Secret Keepers and Forbidden Zones
@keys2: Meropide, Fortress, Wriothesley, Primordial Water
@version: 4.1
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
The gauges in the Forbidden Zone measure Primordial Water levels, though Wriothesley did not know this at first. He only knew they were rising. The Fortress was built with Egeria's help to hold back the Primordial Sea, so the gate stands between Fontaine and the flood that the prophecy foretells.
