## summary
### LITE
The Duke's Office is Wriothesley's office, in the centre of the Administrative Area of the Fortress of Meropide. Its door is often shut tight, yet its owner knows everything that happens in the Fortress. It has three floors.
### STANDARD +
The top floor holds Wriothesley's desk and a sofa with a tea table; in Chapter IV of the Archon Quest it is where the Traveler comes to ask him for answers about the Fortress. A basement opens later and holds a lift to the Forbidden Zone.
### LARGE +
It stands directly across from the Administrative Area's teleport waypoint, so it is the first landmark most visitors see. In the Archon Quest Sigewinne stops by there to check on Clorinde.
### ULTRA +
The Fortress was originally a self-governing community of exiles; Wriothesley took over as Administrator after challenging his predecessor, and this office is the seat of that authority.

## geography
### STANDARD
The office has three floors. The top floor is the working office, with a desk and a sofa with a tea table. The basement floor becomes available during Secret Keepers and Forbidden Zones and contains a lift down to the Forbidden Zone and a door to a room overlooking a ship, the Wingalet. After Masquerade of the Guilty the door stays open but the ship is gone.
### LARGE +
The Fortress's map description calls it central: from there the Duke can know what goes on in the prison even when the door is closed.

## people
### STANDARD
Wriothesley, the Duke, works here. Sigewinne, the Fortress's medical officer, comes by in the quest to check on Clorinde, and Freminet's recovery is mentioned. The quest also features Jurieu and Lourvine.
### LARGE +
Wriothesley meets the Traveler's questions with a playful correction of the word "explain" to "enlighten", then leads them down into the Forbidden Zone.

## governance
### STANDARD
The Administrator of the Fortress of Meropide is known as the Duke. The Fortress is autonomous, and Wriothesley's reforms since taking over its administration are described on his own entry; this office is where he receives visitors and makes decisions.

In Secret Keepers and Forbidden Zones (Chapter IV, Act IV, Part 3 of the Archon Quest) the Traveler comes to the office to question Wriothesley directly, then follows him through the newly opened basement lift into the Forbidden Zone. Masquerade of the Guilty (4.2) later changes the basement room.
