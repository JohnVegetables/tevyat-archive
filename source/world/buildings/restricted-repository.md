## summary
### LITE
The Restricted Repository is the Akademiya's vault of forbidden documents, accessible only to the Mahamatra and their trusted associates. When the matra stop a dangerous project, most of its files are destroyed, but some are kept here as a warning to later researchers. In the game it is entered only during the quest All Returns to Silence.
### STANDARD +
It is a small room of bookshelves behind a secret door, run by a keeper named Utayba.

## geography
### STANDARD
The entrance is hidden in a wall, near the entrance of the House of Daena. Behind it is a small room full of bookshelves holding research archives of former students and researchers who studied banned subjects.
### LARGE +
The bookshelves carry alarms, and a device operated by the keeper opens the hidden door. The wiki notes a similar room appears in Ilyas's thoughts in the quest Pride and Prejudice.

## places
### STANDARD
Three files are readable in the Traveler's visit: a manual on interpreting secret texts, recording a Haravatat researcher sentenced to prison for deciphering forbidden documents; research data on mechanical life forms, recording a Spantamad researcher expelled from the Akademiya; and the sealed materials on the Court of Desolation.
### LARGE +
The Court of Desolation file describes three stages of research: Discovery, Investigation and Fanaticism. Early work hinted that treasures bearing on life and death might lie in the Court and spawned a rumour of bringing the dead back to life. The Court itself was never found. Surviving stone tablets read that entering it costs one's life, and the view that the price makes the Court appear overtook the view that it is the key to entry. Researchers then attempted to sacrifice themselves, and the research was banned.
### ULTRA +
The file says Akademiya scholars kept researching in secret despite the ban, until seven scholars, including Murtada Radkani, were arrested; research then stagnated again.

## people
### STANDARD
Utayba is the administrator. Very few are allowed in even among the matra, and entry is granted only by him; he guides and supervises visitors. Nabil, a matra who does not normally have authorisation, brings the Traveler in using a letter from Cyno.
### LARGE +
Utayba trusts Cyno, who has left the Akademiya, enough to open the room to Cyno's friends, and says very few people can be trusted with a real secret. He advises visitors to read only the Court of Desolation files, because sometimes the less one knows, the better, and warns them not to bump the alarm behind the bookshelf.
### ULTRA +
From Utayba's account in the quest: Murtada Radkani was the only son of Taj Radkani, Cyno's former partner and a long-serving, respected matra. Murtada was arrested in secret over the Court of Desolation research, so it was never public, and he took his own life not long after being jailed. Nabil wonders whether this is why Taj moved from active duty to an administrative post; the scene does not say.

## governance
### STANDARD
The matra decide when research is too dangerous and must be halted. The Repository belongs to that system of control and secrecy: kept for warning, protected by a hidden door and alarms, and open to very few.

## culture
### STANDARD
The Repository reflects the Akademiya's two faces. Nabil notes the Akademiya supports research and encourages all kinds of study, but when work proves dangerous the matra step in and suppress it, and the knowledge goes behind locked doors.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Quiet, cramped and watchful: shelves of files, a keeper who urges haste, and the sense that every record is a warning.

## dangers
### STANDARD
The alarms behind the shelves. Reading the wrong material is itself treated as a hazard, and the Court of Desolation file records researchers who tried to sacrifice themselves and others who were imprisoned.

## travel
### STANDARD
Inside the Akademiya, hidden in a wall near the House of Daena. Nobody can enter without the keeper's consent. In the game the interior cannot be re-entered after the quest.

## quests
### STANDARD
All Returns to Silence, the third part of Sands of Solitude in the Lupus Aureus Chapter, Act I. Cyno sends the Traveler with a letter to Nabil to look up information in the repository, in the Canned Knowledge smuggling case that leads to the Court of Desolation. The wiki records the quest as released in 3.1.

## status
### LITE
Still the Akademiya's secret archive in the story as of 7.1, as far as the sources show.
