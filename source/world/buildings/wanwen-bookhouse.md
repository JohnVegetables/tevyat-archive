## summary
### LITE
Wanwen Bookhouse is a bookshop on Feiyun Slope in Liyue Harbor. Jifang is the owner. Its books are sold one copy of each, and a notice by her table lays down firm rules for readers.
### STANDARD +
Books on the tables and shelves can be read freely, among them Verses of Equilibrium, a Customs of Liyue volume, a Records of the Gallant volume and a Tales From the Waves volume. Others are for sale at Jifang's counter, including Rex Incognito, Moonlit Bamboo Forest, Records of Jueyun and Mt. Lingmeng Gossip volumes.

## places
### STANDARD
A single shop with tables and shelves where certain books can be picked up and read, plus Jifang's counter for the books on sale.

## people
### STANDARD
Jifang is the boss. Gao Fei and Mu Chen are also present in the daytime. Jifang's spouse is Langqing, according to the wiki's infobox.

## culture
### STANDARD
A notice beside Jifang's table sets the house rules: no running, shouting, folly, noise-making, dirty hands or dog-earing books. A second line reads 'Pay first, read later'.
### LARGE +
The shop's stock does not refresh, so a title bought is gone. Titles on offer include volumes of Rex Incognito, Moonlit Bamboo Forest, String of Pearls, Legend of the Lone Sword, Tales From the Waves, Customs of Liyue and Records of Jueyun.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Quiet and orderly, with a proprietress who expects readers to be careful.

## travel
### STANDARD
On Feiyun Slope in Liyue Harbor.
### ULTRA +
Talking to Jifang once may earn a free copy of Records of Jueyun, Vol. I.
