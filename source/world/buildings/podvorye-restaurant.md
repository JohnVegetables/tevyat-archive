## summary
### LITE
Podvorye Restaurant is a long-established eatery in front of Snezhnograd Square in Snezhnaya. It serves refined versions of traditional local dishes and Fire-Water, and its owner and entire staff are Volkodlak and other fae, who are long-lived. The owner, Nefedov, keeps it a dine-in house where food is served piping hot.
### STANDARD +
Odette and Vodyanitsa, performers of the Korolevskiy Troupe, are regulars whom the staff greet by name. The name is Russian for "courtyard" or "homestead".

## places
### STANDARD
Guests come in from the square to a dining room with numbered tables, a bar and a busy kitchen. Servers (Buturlina and Eliseev) work the floor, and Smirnov tends the bar. Nefedov, the owner, oversees orders, and Gromov cooks. Surikov and Menshikov, two regulars, drink inside, while Hussaina and Samir stand outside.
### LARGE +
Buturlina runs the restaurant's shop: cooked food and ingredients, with the dishes refreshed daily and ingredients weekly. It sells the Podvorye Smoked Sausage recipe, and its stock includes borscht and medovik.

## people
### STANDARD
Every member of staff is a Volkodlak (beast fae of Snezhnaya). Nefedov, the owner, is a brisk, practical boss who says his Volkodlak-founded house serves everyone in Snezhnaya. Buturlina, a server, is courteous and proud of the menu. Eliseev, also a server, gets sleepier when the weather changes, which he says varies among Volkodlak. Smirnov is a polite bartender who recommends Fire-Water from a Novokitezh distillery and says his clawed paws handle bottles steadily. Gromov is a nervous young cook who was an apprentice until his mentor took leave for back pain.
### LARGE +
Surikov and Menshikov drink together inside; Surikov boasts of his hunting past when drunk and Menshikov tells him to drink water. A drunk named Vanya sleeps in the alley next to the restaurant (the Hidden Exploration Objective "Fire-Water, Oh Fire-Water..." has the Traveler wake him). Hussaina and Samir have no stories told.
### ULTRA +
Surikov's tale, slurred, is that an old man named Lugovskoy from Morepesok raised a bear, saw talent in him and took him into a birch forest to hunt; his famous shot hit a rabbit, and Menshikov points out it was only Flockingweed. Once the Hidden Exploration Objective is complete, Vanya can be found in the open world at the first (alley) location. Smirnov's recommended pairings for Fire-Water are milk, Sunsettia and rimecurrant jam.

## culture
### STANDARD
The house rule is that guests eat at the restaurant so the food arrives hot: according to Gromov, the boss refused requests for take-out, as is done in Mondstadt. Reservations are not taken, though Buturlina hints that an advance word can get a table assigned. Staff keep fur out of the cooking, and a dish with a stray hair is replaced.
### LARGE +
The signature dish is the Podvorye Smoked Sausage, sliced and eaten as a shared starter. Odette is a regular who gets Snowball Blooms Atop Snowfields and a small ice cream. Vodyanitsa orders double meat and extra ice cream. The restaurant also serves Rimecurrant Ice Cream, medovik and stroganina.

## history
### STANDARD
No founding date is given. Staff note that the restaurant has long existed with Volkodlak founders, and that the present boss has been head chef for a very long time.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
A lively working restaurant: orders called out, a bar with bottles of Fire-Water, and flustered or sleepy staff with tails and paws. It feels homely rather than formal, but serious about food.

## status
### LITE
Open and busy as of 7.1.
### STANDARD +
A 7.1 Travelers' Tales anecdote, "Odette: Food Is No Small Matter", starts when the player enters the restaurant. Odette eats her special diet and Vodyanitsa tries to tempt her with sweets on her cheat day.

### STANDARD
Podvorye Restaurant staff are all Volkodlak. Nefedov: owner, whose house keeps food dine-in so it stays hot. Buturlina: server and shopkeeper. Eliseev: server who tires in weather changes. Gromov: apprentice cook, likely to take over the main entree. Smirnov: bartender who recommends Fire-Water from Novokitezh. Their wiki profiles are otherwise empty.

## entry: Odette at Podvorye Restaurant
@keys: Podvorye Restaurant, Food Is No Small Matter
@keys2: Odette, Vodyanitsa
@version: 7.1
### LARGE
In the open world Odette is found at the restaurant considering her order; once she asks for Snowball Blooms Atop Snowfields and is told they are out, and elsewhere she says "same as yesterday". In the 7.1 anecdote "Odette: Food Is No Small Matter" she is eating snowball flowers, rimecurrants, beets, cheese, egg, chicken, fish and rice. This is a special diet from the Fontaine nurse Sigewinne, with cheat days. She rejects Vodyanitsa's ice cream, medovik and stroganina, but Vodyanitsa has the kitchen make a dessert, checked by letter with Sigewinne, and congratulates her on a rehearsal.
