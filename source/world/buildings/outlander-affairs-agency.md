## summary
### LITE
The Outlander Affairs Agency is the Kanjou Commission office in Ritou that handles documents and requests from visitors who are not from Inazuma. While the Sakoku Decree was in force, any outsider who wanted to stay in Ritou had to apply here for a Residence Permit and pay a "handling fee", and the agency used its position to fleece stranded foreigners. Inspector Yurika runs the counter and Chiba guards the entrance.
### STANDARD +
The agency sits on the Kanjou Commission's watch over Inazuma's finances, customs and borders. Its reputation changed when the commission was purged of corruption: it grew more lenient and later quoted a modest official fee.

## geography
### STANDARD
The agency is a point of interest in Ritou, the port island that handles all foreign arrivals and departures. Yurika wanders its courtyard, Chiba stands by the entrance, and the merchant Robert paces on the stairs outside.
### LARGE +
Ritou also holds the border checkpoint where the inspector Kageyama checks entry papers and the International Trade Association, whose members alone could import and export goods under the Decree. The agency is the next stop after the checkpoint for anyone who wants to remain on Ritou.

## people
### STANDARD
Yurika is the Inspector at the counter; Chiba, an officer of the Kanjou Commission, keeps the door; the merchant Robert is a recurring applicant. The wiki lists no other staff. The commission's head, Hiiragi Shinsuke, stood above them until his arrest, after which his daughter Hiiragi Chisato became acting head of the clan.
### LARGE +
Yurika's patter barely changes across the story: forms to fill in, a handling fee, and a reminder to bring the necessary "materials". Chiba tells arrivals to have documents and identification ready and warns them to keep a wallet to hand.

## governance
### STANDARD
The agency answers to the Kanjou Commission, the branch of the Tri-Commission responsible for Inazuma's wealth and borders. Rules for foreigners in Ritou were set by the Kanjou Commission's administration. Thoma tells the Traveler that to everyday Inazumans anyone from elsewhere is an "outsider" and that outlanders are unwelcome while the Sakoku Decree stands, even in the Outsider Settlement on Ritou.
### LARGE +
Thoma explains that some agency officials treat the fees as easy money and charge as much as they think a stranded merchant can pay, so a local friend helps. Outlanders have little recourse because of their social and economic position.
### ULTRA +
The Kanjou Commission is led by the Hiiragi Clan, based in Ritou, and manages Inazuma's finances, customs and borders. The wiki notes it was authoritarian at the border and abusive towards foreigners in Ritou under the Decree, and that it lightened up, like the Tenryou Commission, after the events of Omnipresence Over Mortals.

## history
### STANDARD
When the Sakoku Decree left outsiders stranded on Ritou, the agency took advantage. In Confessions of an Outlander (2.0), Yurika asked the Traveler and Paimon for a processing fee of one million Mora each, two million in all. Out of courtesy to Thoma she cut it to 400,000 Mora, and Thoma then offered 600 Mora and dinner, which she accepted and recorded. The quest sets this in the Archon Quest scene where the Traveler registers at the border and the agency.
### LARGE +
After the events of Omnipresence Over Mortals (2.1) the Kanjou Commission was purged of corruption, Shinsuke was arrested over the Fatui plot, and Yurika said the official fee was 500 Mora per visitor, remarking that it was the first time she had heard anyone complain about prices being too low. Once the Sakoku Decree ended in Transient Dreams, she described a procedure for foreign merchants wanting to do business in Ritou long term: an Entry Permit, identification papers and the same 500 Mora fee.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
A bureaucratic building where money changes hands openly. Counter clerks speak in official phrases while the applicant counts coins. Merchants outside grumble about missing "materials". After the reforms the same routines continue with less menace.

## quests
### STANDARD
Confessions of an Outlander (Archon Quest, Chapter II Act I: The Immovable God and the Eternal Euthymia, 2.0) sends the Traveler through the agency with Thoma. The agency is otherwise an ordinary map location with NPCs.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the agency still exists in Ritou. Yurika and Chiba were last seen at their posts after the Sakoku Decree ended, and processing has become routine. No content from 5.x to 7.1 mentions the agency.

### STANDARD
Yurika quotes fees according to urgency: the more important an applicant's business, the higher the "handling fee" should be. During the Decree she demanded a Residence Permit application, an Entry Permit and unnamed "materials". After Omnipresence Over Mortals she says the fee is the official 500 Mora per visitor and invites doubters to check the Kanjou Commission's latest regulations. After the Decree ends she asks merchants for an Entry Permit and identification papers.
