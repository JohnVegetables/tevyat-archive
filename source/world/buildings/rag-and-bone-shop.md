## summary
### LITE
The Rag and Bone Shop is a small shop in the Administrative Area of the Fortress of Meropide, Fontaine's undersea prison. Its owner, Alvard, sells all sorts of goods to inmates, who pay in Credit Coupons, a currency used only inside the fortress.
### STANDARD +
The in-game map description calls it the best-stocked non-official merchandise store in the fortress: anything from Hydro Phantasm taint cleaners to support items for the Pankration Ring can be had there, provided the buyer has coupons. It sits in the southeast part of the Administrative Area and was added in Version 4.1 with the Meropide content.

## places
### STANDARD
The shop is a counter run by Alvard in the southeast of the Administrative Area. Alvard is friends with Chamboduc and with Wriothesley, the Duke who runs the fortress; Chamboduc minds the shop when Alvard is out restocking.
### LARGE +
The Welfare Meal handed out free by Wolsey is the only thing in the area that costs nothing: Alvard jokes that the only free thing in his shop is the air.

## culture
### STANDARD
The shop is part of the fortress's coupon economy. Credit Coupons are earned from small jobs and favours, so a purchase here is either a minor luxury or a practical necessity. Alvard's patter is "you need it, we got it", but he values discretion: he will not say where his goods come from, and whether he takes orders depends on who is asking and for what.
### LARGE +
During the world quest Game of the Rich the girl Lanoire is taken to the shop; for that scene the shelves show a thematic stock of sold-out toys, food and curios, which is flavour only and cannot be bought. The real stock is a handful of one-off items such as bottled "Pure Water", blueprints, and Genius Invokation TCG goods (a card back and an event card), some of which stay reserved until the quest The Art of Negotiation is done. Buying everything grants the Consumer Society achievement.
### ULTRA +
The regular stock is paid for in Credit Coupons and includes a vending machine item, two blueprints (a Linden Wood bunk and one called Majesty of the Forbidden Fortress), the Fortress of Meropide card back and an item titled The Art of Negotiation. The wiki counts 4,560 coupons available in the fortress outside the Archon Quest, enough to buy out both this shop and the Coupon Cafeteria with nothing left over.

## status
### LITE
Open and operating as of Version 7.1.
### STANDARD +
Once every item has been bought the shop icon disappears from the map, but Alvard can still be found at his counter and has extra dialogue.

### STANDARD
Alvard owns the Rag and Bone Shop in the Fortress of Meropide. He is a salesman first: breezy, a little sly and unwilling to name his suppliers, though he insists his goods are safe and fairly priced. He is friends with Chamboduc, who covers for him when he is away, and with the Duke Wriothesley.
