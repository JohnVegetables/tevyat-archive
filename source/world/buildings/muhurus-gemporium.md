## summary
### LITE
A gem shop in the Children of Echoes' settlement in Tequemecan Valley, Natlan, run by Cheven.
### STANDARD +
Cheven's apprentice is Imix, and the NPC Iknal is also listed there. The shop sells gems and similar goods; the in-game vendor is Imix.

## people
### STANDARD
Cheven is the owner and Imix the apprentice. Iknal is listed at the shop without a stated role.

## culture
### LARGE
Muhuru was also the name of a stegosaur Saurian, the companion of the Nanatzcayan hero Sundjatta. Weapon text says it fought alongside Sundjatta and the other heroes led by Tenoch against the Abyss. Stegosaurs are no longer found in Natlan today. The wiki connects the shop's name to this figure, but no text explains the choice of name.
### ULTRA +
The furnishing Tequemecan Treasures is designed to resemble the shop.
