## summary
### LITE
Hotel Bouffes d'ete is a luxurious hotel in the Vasari Passage of the Court of Fontaine. It is legally owned by the Fatui Harbinger Arlecchino, called 'Father' by the orphans there, and is the base of her House of the Hearth in Fontaine. A children's home runs behind the hotel front. Lyney, Lynette and Freminet live there.
### STANDARD +
Its residents think of it as a family home. Its name means roughly 'Summer Bouffe Singers Hotel' in French.

## geography
### STANDARD
In a corner of the Court of Fontaine in the Vasari Passage. Its story text mentions a grand hall with a phonograph, a small maintenance workroom and water-pumping equipment.
### LARGE +
The wiki's map point is in the Vasari Passage. Sandrone's tea table is set up near the entrance for the 'Whispers by the Hearth' tea party.

## people
### STANDARD
Current residents the wiki names are Lyney, Lynette, Freminet, Chapleau, Foltz and Heloir. Former residents include Filliol, Horrer, Jeambel, Landois, Lapouyade, Nanteuil, Sotto and Tadie. Arlecchino visits infrequently and is addressed as Father; the House's rule is that betrayal is not tolerated.
### LARGE +
The house is full of children who share similar sad backgrounds and help each other as family would. Lyney was a newcomer and became the person around whom the Hotel's life turns. Freminet worked in maintenance there. The twins were recommended by the hotel's manager and still found the other children slow to accept them.
### ULTRA +
Lyney spends time with residents without prying into their pasts. He joined Freminet in the workroom after a water pump broke while Lynette used it. A Maison Gardiennage official, funded by Lyney, once fed information to the Hotel and covered up traces of some of its clandestine actions, until he was caught. Cats of the Hotel each have their own territory, and one called Rosseland likes Lyney's hat.

## governance
### STANDARD
Arlecchino holds legal ownership and rules by her word. Orphans of the House carry the surnames Snezhevich and Snezhevna. The House's operations in Fontaine pursue what it calls a just cause but with radical methods, with Arlecchino's influence and her control over the inner workings of the Court of Fontaine preventing serious incidents, according to Lyney's profile.
### LARGE +
After her duel with Lyney, Lynette and Freminet in Into the Sunlight (Ignis Purgatorius Chapter Act I), Arlecchino let former members Filliol and others take a memory-erasing potion instead of being killed. They left the House and, as Lynette overheard, appear to be living the lives they wanted.

## culture
### STANDARD
Residents see the Hotel's festivals as the only warm events in their lives, since outside festivals feel cold and foreign to them. On its Founding Day the children decorate the hall with streamers, eat desserts and watch a magic show by Lyney, Lynette and Freminet.
### LARGE +
In Lyney's Founding Day show, 'Father' herself appears from the magic box. She praised Lyney for keeping the secret as a good magic show should, cut cake for every child and joined them.

## history
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The Hotel is the House of the Hearth's known Fontaine base. The House traces back to an orphanage in Khaenri'ah and was later refounded by an earlier Knave. Arlecchino took over after Crucabena, and wanted the children to call her 'Father'. In Lyney's Character Stories the twins arrive at the Hotel after tragic years and settle in.
### LARGE +
In Ignis Purgatorius Act I, two days after the duel, the Traveler returns to check on Lynette and Freminet, who are recovering at the Hotel. Lyney had earlier told the Traveler that Lynette and Freminet were recuperating at the Hotel.

## dangers
### STANDARD
It is a Fatui front. Anyone who betrays the House faces death, though Arlecchino can spare people with a potion of her flame that erases their memories of the House.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Luxurious yet lived-in. Children bustle about the halls, music plays from the phonograph, and the family atmosphere is warm until the Father arrives, when everyone straightens up.

## travel
### STANDARD
A point of interest in the Vasari Passage. Sandrone's Portable Tea Table can be placed near the entrance to start the tea party quests Whispers by the Hearth and Tea Party Re-Invitation: House of the Hearth.

## quests
### STANDARD
Appears in the Ignis Purgatorius Chapter (Arlecchino's story quest, requires the Archon Quest through Masquerade of the Guilty), in Lyney's profile, and in the tea party quests Whispers by the Hearth and its re-invitation.

## status
### LITE
The House of the Hearth's Fontaine base, with Arlecchino as owner, as far as the sources read.
