## summary
### LITE
Hanamizaka is the suburb of Inazuma City on the north side of the river that cuts through the city's northern part, one of the city's three parts alongside Tenryou and Tenshukaku. It is a district of family workshops and shops: the Naganohara fireworks shop, Amenoma Smithy, Kiminami Restaurant, Yae Publishing House and the Bantan Sango detective agency. Its name means "Flower-Viewing Slope".
### STANDARD +
Compared with the commercial street of Tenryou it feels more residential and artisan. It is where a firework-maker, a master bladesmith, a restaurant owner, a pair of detectives and a famous publisher are neighbours, and where the Shogunate's bulletin board carried everything from official notices to graffiti and complaints.

## geography
### STANDARD
Hanamizaka lies north of the river through the north of Inazuma City, on Narukami Island. Amenoma Smithy stands at the city's eastern end, in the eastern suburb; the western outskirts hold quieter homes and a few city watchmen. The Hanamizaka bulletin board stands north-east of the city's northern Teleport Waypoint.
### LARGE +
Canon describes the three parts of the city only briefly. Hanamizaka is the suburb, Tenryou the commercial heart below Tenshukaku, and Tenshukaku the Raiden Shogun's castle at the top. The Naganohara Fireworks Show is watched from Amakane Island, from which the whole of Inazuma City can be seen.

## places
### STANDARD
Naganohara Fireworks: the Naganohara family's shop, run by Yoimiya's family, called by an Inazuman named Takami the oldest and one of the best in Inazuma. It also sells small toys and trinkets, some imported by the Crux fleet and some made by Yoimiya.
Amenoma Smithy: the workshop of the Amenoma Art, one of the five bladesmithing schools of the Raiden Gokaden. Amenoma Tougo owns it; his disciple Hajime handles ordinary ironware and weapons.
Kiminami Restaurant: owned by Kiminami Anna, with a menu notice advertising a new dish from abroad.
Yae Publishing House: the great publisher founded by Yae Miko, with editors in the suburb.
Bantan Sango Detective Agency: Ryuuji and Sango's agency, taking missing persons, lost pets, deliveries and procurement.
### LARGE +
The Bantan Sango Agency advertises its readiness to catch even bandit leaders if the price is right. Yae Publishing House runs the "This Novel Is Amazing!" competition and lists Yae Miko herself as editor-in-chief. The fireworks shop's own board carries a fire-brigade notice scolding Yoimiya's risky behaviour and threatening to report her to the Yashiro Commissioner. Its by-products can be mixed with plant extract to make scentless candles.
### ULTRA +
The Amenoma Art, in its own words, has no secret tricks, only patience and willpower, likened to water slowly turning stone to sand. Yae Publishing House was founded no more than 500 years ago, since the Shogun says it did not exist when she retreated to the Plane of Euthymia. Its writers include a Liyue author known as Zhenyu and the illustrator "Calx".

## people
### STANDARD
Notable residents of the eastern suburb: the firework-maker Naganohara Ryuunosuke, the bladesmith Amenoma Tougo, the detectives Ryuuji and Sango, and the editors Shigeru and Kuroda of Yae Publishing House. City watchmen (doushin) such as Andou and Amano and the yoriki Kurosawa Kyounosuke patrol the suburb on behalf of the Tenryou Commission.
### LARGE +
Yoimiya, who runs the shop's festival work, is the best-known figure here, and the Naganohara Fireworks Show is her family's craft. The vendor Hajime and the restaurateur Kiminami Anna also trade here.

## governance
### STANDARD
Hanamizaka falls under the Shogunate's city administration. Order is kept by the Tenryou Commission's watchmen; festivals and ceremony fall under the Yashiro Commission, which created the first fireworks show.
### LARGE +
During the war of the Vision Hunt Decree, the Shogunate's bulletin board in Hanamizaka carried agent notices praising the Decree, Tenryou Commission notices enforcing the Sakoku Decree, and rental or sale notices for the property of fugitives. Vandals and critics posted replies that the Commission answered in turn.

## culture
### STANDARD
Hanamizaka is where Inazuma's festival craft lives: fireworks launched for the Naganohara show, forged blades and the novels on sale. People associate fireworks with what matters to them (friendship, marriage, belief), and families pick their own fireworks through paper slips supplied by the Naganohara family.
### LARGE +
The fireworks show began over a century ago as a commemorative display by the Yashiro Commission with expert pyrotechnicians; as fireworks spread, families took part and it became a tradition. One year Kamisato Ayaka asked Yoimiya to shape the display so that, from Tenshukaku, it formed the Electro Mitsudomoe, the Shogun's emblem. Yoimiya has said that fireworks, which vanish in a flash, are the opposite of the Shogun's ideal of eternity, but that the feelings people attach to them last.

## history
### STANDARD
Canon gives Hanamizaka no founding story. Its notable history comes from the Archon Quest era: during the Vision Hunt and Sakoku Decrees the suburb's bulletin board and smithy notices show the war's effects, such as the stalled supply of Jade Steel, and Arataki Itto's public quarrel with the Kujou tengu.
### LARGE +
The notices from that time included a complaint that crime, smuggling and forged papers were rising, which officials answered as lies. After the war ends, those first sets of messages are replaced, and the Decrees are gone.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Narrow, busy streets of wooden shopfronts and noren curtains, the clang of hammers from the smithy, the smell of the restaurant, children buying marbles and tops, and paper notices on boards. Evenings are alive with festival preparations.

## quests
### LARGE
Yoimiya's story quest "Together Under the Fireworks" and the Naganohara Fireworks Show; the Tatara Tales world quest, which interrupts the Jade Steel supply at Amenoma Smithy; "This Novel Is Amazing!" at Yae Publishing House.
## travel
### STANDARD
The Hanamizaka bulletin board stands near the city's northern Teleport Waypoint. Amakane Island is the place to watch the fireworks.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 Hanamizaka is an ordinary peaceful suburb; the Vision Hunt and Sakoku Decrees are over and the shops are open.
### STANDARD +
Yoimiya's family runs the fireworks shop and show as before. No 6.x or 7.0-7.1 plot touches the suburb.

## entry: Naganohara Fireworks and the Fireworks Show
@keys: Naganohara Fireworks, Naganohara Fireworks Show, Naganohara family, Naganohara Ryuunosuke
@version: 2.0
### STANDARD
Naganohara Fireworks is the shop of the Naganohara family in Hanamizaka, said to be the oldest and one of the best in Inazuma. Yoimiya, the family's firework-maker, even had the Crux fleet fire fireworks overseas to spread its name. The shop also sells knickknacks beloved by children and adults: Scatterbloom Marbles that bloom when they strike each other, Leaping Tops that chirp like birds, Neon Dragonflies that change colour in flight, and Katydid Coils that repel mosquitos.
### LARGE +
The Naganohara Fireworks Show is held at festivals through the year. The Naganohara family prepares fireworks for each family that takes part, with paper slips containing the recipes. Over a century ago the Yashiro Commission began the show as a commemoration. Imatani Saburou proposed to his wife at the show fifty years ago, and Takami of the Yashiro Commission helps run it from Amakane Island.

## entry: Amenoma Smithy
@keys: Amenoma Smithy, Amenoma Art, Amenoma Tougo, Raiden Gokaden
@version: 2.0
### STANDARD
Amenoma Smithy is a smithy on the eastern end of Inazuma City, in Hanamizaka. Its master, Amenoma Tougo, practises the Amenoma Art, one of the five bladesmithing schools of the Raiden Gokaden, and in his old age accepts only orders for exquisite Jade Steel swords and knives. Everyday ironware and ordinary weapons are handled by his disciple Hajime.
### LARGE +
Tougo says there is nothing mystical in the craft, only practice day after day until body and mind absorb every motion. The Art tries to imitate the patience of water turning stone to sand. During the war, a notice at the smithy apologised that the Jade Steel supply had stalled and asked customers for their understanding.

## entry: Yae Publishing House
@keys: Yae Publishing House, This Novel Is Amazing, Lady Yae
@version: 2.0
### STANDARD
Yae Publishing House is a giant of Inazuma's publishing industry, founded by Yae Miko, chief priestess of the Grand Narukami Shrine, who is also its editor-in-chief. It runs a contest named "This Novel Is Amazing!" and is famous across Teyvat. It publishes light literature, and the best-selling Liyue novel "A Legend of Sword" is serialised there.
### LARGE +
The publisher has tried to hire Albedo for his art. Its staff includes editors Kuroda and Shigeru, event manager Aratani, and columnist "Ms. Hina". During the war, the Grand Narukami Shrine announced that its reader forum would welcome readers from both Shogunate and rebel territory, because Yae Miko treated a common hobby as a reason for equal treatment.

## entry: Bantan Sango Detective Agency
@keys: Bantan Sango, Bantan Sango Detective Agency
@version: 2.0
### STANDARD
A small detective agency in Hanamizaka, staffed by the detectives Ryuuji and Sango. Its board advertises investigation of missing persons, missing pets, marital questions, commercial commissions, procurement and delivery.

## entry: Hanamizaka Bulletin Board
@keys: Hanamizaka Bulletin Board
@version: 2.0
### LARGE
The Shogunate's Hanamizaka bulletin board carried a first set of notices that is replaced once Sangonomiya Kokomi's first story quest is completed. Those notices include a praise of Kujou Takayuki for implementing the Vision Hunt Decree quickly, sales of fugitives' property, arrest warrants, and Arataki Itto's challenges to the Kujou tengu, which he blames for taking his Vision. The messages were put up before the war's end and describe a city under strain.
