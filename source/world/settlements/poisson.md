## summary
### LITE
Poisson ("fish" in French) is a fishing village in the Belleau Region, at the foot of Mont Automnequi and built from a great ship and a large cavern below sea level. Its people mostly fish or salvage from underwater. It is the headquarters of the Spina di Rosula and Navia's home ground. In 4.2 an earthquake near Poisson sent Primordial Seawater flooding through the lower town, killing twelve people; it has since been rebuilt with Fatui help.
### STANDARD +
Legend says that before the first diluvian period Poisson was a central hub of Fontaine's maritime traffic, but changing water levels cost it its status as a major port. Visitors still come for its cosy, warm feel.

## geography
### STANDARD
The village sits against Mont Automnequi, half in a cavern below sea level, with a huge ship making up part of the town. Lower streets run close to the water, and higher ground is where people fled in the flood. The nearby ruins to the south of town were investigated after the 4.2 flood.
### LARGE +
There is no aquabus station near Poisson; Navia says most of the Spina's businesses do not use it as a hub, so she and the Traveler travel by one of the Spina's own boats.

## places
### STANDARD
The Spina di Rosula's headquarters is in the village. The large ship is part of the town and appears in quests. The lower streets near the water were where people were dissolved in the 4.2 flood.
### LARGE +
After the flood the survivors were housed in shelters while the town was rebuilt. A statue honouring Navia's parents, funded by donations from the residents, is planned.

## people
### STANDARD
Open-world residents include Alcina, Archenoul (the Spina's diver), Cava, Kosel (a porter), Melissa (a fisher), Munaciello, Rege, Sandino, Yvette (the engineer) and, after the flood begins, Vincenzo. Several are absent after the flood: some are in shelters, others are unlisted.
### LARGE +
The Spina's Florent works closely with Navia on rebuilding. Colter, a friend of the late Melus, returned to Poisson after prison. Melus and Silver, Navia's bodyguards (listed on the Spina page as a butler and an attendant), died helping others in the flood.

## governance
### STANDARD
Poisson has a mayor in its historical record, Renault de Petrichor, but today the Spina di Rosula administers the town in practice, as Navia herself says. After the flood the Court of Fontaine paid relief to victims, and Navia holds that the Spina should take its own share of responsibility.

## culture
### STANDARD
A close-knit, working community of fishers, porters and salvagers, tied to the Spina. People call Navia "Boss". The town's people have donated for a statue to honour Callas and Clementine.
### LARGE +
The Spina's founding story is tied here: Poisson is the Spina's place of origin and Navia says its headquarters are here. A long-standing dispute within the Spina, between Callas (who backed the aquabus lines) and Romeuf (who wanted the money spent on improving Poisson), runs through the village's recent history.

## history
### STANDARD
More than 500 years ago, shortly after the purge of Fleuve Cendre, the outlaw Eduardo Baker was being taken into exile when his followers freed him. With allies from near Mont Automnequi they seized Poisson and took Maison Gardiennage officers hostage, making unreasonable demands. The reporter Karl Ingold photographed Eduardo, his son Jakob, Mayor Renault de Petrichor and his son Rene, the magician "Parsifal", Rosa Reed with her husband and baby, and Tom Alter before the situation worsened. This became the Siege of Poisson.
### LARGE +
The Marechaussee Hunter Emanuel Guillotin led the siege. The town was burned and many inhabitants died; Renault and his wife were likely killed. Rene and Jakob survived and were sent to the Narzissenkreuz Institute. "Parsifal" saved Karl Ingold, but was later arrested and died in a duel with Marfisa.
### ULTRA +
Three years before the game, Callas's partner Marcel secretly sold Sinthe (diluted Primordial Seawater) to the people of Poisson, and its abuse ruined families. Callas was later accused of a murder and chose to duel Clorinde. In 4.2 a quake near Poisson raised the water suddenly, and Primordial Seawater dissolved people near the water on the lower street. The Spina led rescue and evacuation, and Arlecchino's Fatui helped; Melus and Silver died in the rescue. The Fatui built a "Giant Magic Box" for the later staged trial of Furina, and Poisson was part of the lure.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
A warm, cosy fishing village before the flood; afterwards a half-empty one, where Navia says the living are too exhausted to find words for the dead. In the 4.3 story the town is busy with construction, with Navia making many trips between the Court and the village.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Before 4.2 there was little danger. After the flood, the lower levels were hazardous because of Primordial Seawater, and the Spina asked residents to leave. In 4.3 Romeuf and his followers plotted to blow up the Clementine Line's supports and bury Poisson in rubble, but the Traveler and Navia stopped the plan and the Maison Gardiennage arrested them.

## travel
### STANDARD
Poisson has no aquabus station; in Navia's story quest the Spina's own boat carries visitors from the Court of Fontaine.

## quests
### LARGE
Poisson features in the Archon Quest (Chapter IV, Act II As Light Rain Falls Without Reason, and Act V Masquerade of the Guilty, starting with Deluge of Wrathful Waters) and in Navia's story quest chain Braving the Tides Together (Rosa Multiflora Chapter, 4.3).

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 Poisson has been rebuilt after the 4.2 flood and remains the Spina di Rosula's headquarters. No later canon changes its state.
### STANDARD +
Rebuilding was still under way in 4.3, with Fatui help and with Navia and Florent overseeing it. Romeuf's faction was arrested. Clorinde laid low here for a time afterwards.

## entry: The Siege of Poisson
@keys: Siege of Poisson, Eduardo Baker, Renault de Petrichor
@keys2: Poisson, Fleuve Cendre, Fontaine
@version: 4.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
A catastrophe before the cataclysm. After Eduardo Baker of Fleuve Cendre was seized in the purge and freed by his men, he and allies occupied Poisson and took Maison Gardiennage staff hostage. Marechaussee Hunters under Emanuel Guillotin ended the incident with the town burned and many killed. Rene de Petrichor and Jakob survived to join the Narzissenkreuz Institute.
### LARGE +
Item texts disagree on who bears blame; Rene's notes blame the Gardiennage's lackeys and Guillotin's role. The Doge of Petrichor recalls that Renault, the last patrono of Petrichor, lost his life as Poisson's mayor.

## entry: The Poisson Flood
@keys: Poisson flood, Deluge, Primordial Seawater
@keys2: Poisson, Navia, Spina di Rosula
@version: 4.2
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
In Deluge of Wrathful Waters the ground shakes near Poisson and the water level rises quickly. The water turns out to contain Primordial Seawater, which dissolves Fontainians, and those on the lower street disappear. A stampede follows on higher ground. The Spina organises rescue; Arlecchino's Fatui help. Twelve people die, including Navia's bodyguards Melus and Silver.
### LARGE +
Neuvillette connects the flood to the Primordial Sea, and after it the Traveler, Neuvillette and the Spina lure Furina into a staged trial. Neuvillette later restores power that prevents Fontainians from dissolving in the Primordial Sea.

## entry: Romeuf's Plot
@keys: Romeuf, Colter
@keys2: Poisson, Spina di Rosula, Clementine Line
@version: 4.3
### STANDARD
Romeuf was a Spina member who thought the aquabus money should improve Poisson. He left the Spina when Callas and Clementine chose to go ahead with the aquabus lines. In Navia's story quest Braving the Tides Together, he and his followers plan to bomb the Clementine Line's supports to bury Poisson in rubble and Colter attempts to assassinate Navia; the plan fails and they are arrested.

## entry: Sinthe
@keys: Sinthe
@keys2: Poisson, Callas, Marcel
@version: 4.0
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Sinthe was a popular drink in Poisson that was actually diluted Primordial Seawater. Its long-term effects were severe, and it wrecked many families. Callas banned it and began investigating its sellers.
