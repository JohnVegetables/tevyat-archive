## summary
### LITE
Koseki Village is a silent, abandoned fishing village on Seirai Island in Inazuma. A calamity that created a perpetual thunderstorm at the island's heart drove its people out, and only the memory remains that it was abandoned because of furious, unchecked storms. Today it is a ruin of empty houses where Treasure Hoarders and monsters loiter.
### STANDARD +
The village belonged to the Asase Shrine's circle: its people kept the shrine's offerings and helped maintain it before the storm. The cat Neko, who calls herself provisional head priestess, still looks after the shrine nearby.

## geography
### STANDARD
Koseki Village lies on Seirai Island, whose centre was swallowed by an eternal storm. The village has a Statue of the Seven and a Phase Gate, and two fishing points are located here.
### LARGE +
Other features include Electro Seelie, Electro cubes, a lightning strike probe and a light-up tile puzzle. Local specialties are Naku Weed and Sea Ganoderma. Seirai Island's centre has Balethunder and lightning until the Seirai Stormchasers quests are completed.

## places
### STANDARD
Empty houses and fishing spots. During Seirai Stormchasers Part I, Treasure Hoarders are found in the village.
### LARGE +
The village is where Neko says the human who repaired the shrine used to live. In Part I the Traveler fights those Treasure Hoarders and gathers Box of Nails, Planks and Rope from their camp to repair the Asase Shrine's Offering Box.

## people
### STANDARD
No residents remain. The adventurers Etsuko and Taisuke of the Inazuma Adventurers' Guild work on the island, and the cat Neko lives at the shrine.
### LARGE +
Former inhabitants were evacuated to Inazuma City after the storm. Treasure Hoarders were seen in the village during the quest.

## governance
### STANDARD
Canon does not describe how the village was governed. The nearby Asase Shrine was managed by the Asase Clan, and no administration remains now.
### LARGE +
The Shogunate's conflict with Ako Domeki, the pirate and bandit from Seirai, is the background to the evacuation.

## culture
### STANDARD
A fishing community: its villagers helped maintain the Asase Shrine, which the Asase Clan managed, and made offerings there.
### LARGE +
Cats at the Asase Shrine are named after "good things" and the names pass down; their lore is linked to the shrine rather than the village.

## history
### STANDARD
About five hundred years ago, several years after the cataclysm, Ako Domeki and his crew rebelled against the Shogunate. Asase Hibiki, head priestess of the Asase Shrine, tried to help him by unsealing the wards around Seirai Island using the "true magic" she had learned from the Yougou Tengu, freeing the Thunderbird's power. A great Electro storm formed at the island's centre; most of Seirai was devastated and Koseki's inhabitants were evacuated to Inazuma City.
### LARGE +
The shrine was originally set up to suppress the lingering hatred of the Thunderbird Kanna Kapatcir after it was felled over the island. The Thunder Manifestation was drawn to Amakumo Peak. Hibiki walked into the storm and never returned, leaving Neko to guard the shrine.
### ULTRA +
Domeki tried to dissuade Hibiki from joining his rebellion. His flagship was the Seiraimaru, and he once seized a Maguu Kenki from the Shogunate. Both he and Hibiki headed into the storm.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Silence and empty houses, with the sea nearby and thunder rolling over the island. The viewpoint text says the laughter and cicada cries of the old days were lost to the thunder.

## dangers
### LARGE
Fatui skirmishers, hilichurls and Specters; Mirror Maidens; Mitachurl and Ruin machines; Treasure Hoarders in the village; and the island's lightning storms before they are calmed.

## travel
### STANDARD
Seirai Island is a separate island; the Asase Shrine on it has a Waverider waypoint. The evacuees went to Inazuma City.

## quests
### LARGE
Seirai Stormchasers (Parts I-IV), Neko Is a Cat, and "Octave of the Maushiro".
### ULTRA +
In Part I, Neko says the human who repaired the shrine lived in the village but has not come in a long time. The Traveler goes there and meets Treasure Hoarders who remark that the village looks untended for ages.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 Koseki Village remains abandoned, though the island's storm has been calmed.
### STANDARD +
Completing Seirai Stormchasers sealed the four Warding Stones and the storm passed, but no text shows anyone returning to live in the village. No 6.x or 7.0-7.1 content revisits it.

## entry: Seirai Island and the Thunderbird
@keys: Seirai Island, Kanna Kapatcir, Thunderbird, Thunder Manifestation, Warding Stones
@keys2: Koseki, Asase Shrine, Neko
@version: 2.1
### STANDARD
Seirai Island suffers a perpetual thunderstorm at its centre, with Balethunder and lightning that hunts the player, resolved by the quest series Seirai Stormchasers. The Asase Shrine was founded to suppress the lingering hatred of the Thunderbird Kanna Kapatcir, and Warding Stones across the island keep the bird's power sealed. When Hibiki broke the wards, the Thunder Manifestation appeared.
### LARGE +
In Seirai Stormchasers, the Traveler, Etsuko and Taisuke seal four Warding Stones, two of them up in the sky; afterwards a platform forms, the Thunder Manifestation is fought, and the Peculiar Pinion is found. The storm ends. Electrified Water persists in the island's centre until a puzzle is solved.

## entry: Kosekimaru
@keys: Kosekimaru, Miitoboru
@availability: limited
### LARGE
In the Summertime Odyssey event, the Waverider the Traveler pilots in the Golden Apple Archipelago is Kosekimaru, a ship of Ako Domeki's fleet from Koseki Village. Once named Miitoboru by Paimon, it recovers its true name, given by Asase Hibiki. This is event-only material: the ship drifted to the archipelago during a battle with a Shogunate vessel at Seirai Island and was revived as a sapient Waverider by a Fatui machine.
