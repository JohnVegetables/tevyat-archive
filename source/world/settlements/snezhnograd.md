## summary
### LITE
Snezhnograd, "Snow City", is the capital of Snezhnaya, a blizzard-country city kept habitable by Kresnik's Torch. It holds the Tsaritsa's Zapolyarny Palace, the Snezhnograd Veche, the Druzhna headquarters and the Adventurers' Guild's headquarters. Pulcinella, "The Rooster", is its Mayor.
### STANDARD +
The Fatui's headquarters at Glupov lies beside it. Alchemy is outlawed in Snezhnaya, and Snezhnograd is the only main city in the game without a Crafting Bench. Flins calls it beautiful, with a brilliant night sky and endless blizzards, though recently much more industrial.

## geography
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
Snezhnograd lies in the Everfrozen Earth of Snezhnaya. Snowstorms rage outside, but Kresnik's Torch holds a temperate zone over the city; a resident, Gorchakova, says she still prefers it in a snowstorm, since that is when the Torch's warmth is truly felt. The great torch core, Kresnik's Burning Heart, lies in a chamber beneath the central plaza. Zapolyarny Palace overlooks the plaza. A Statue of The Seven stands in the city; only Snezhnograd and the Court of Fontaine among the main cities have one.
### LARGE +
The industrial district of Zolnyy, the "Ash District", sits within the city. A local specialty is the Frostfairy Flower, and the city has a bulletin board, a bookshop (Torchlight Books), a blacksmith, a Northland Bank branch and the Podvorye Restaurant.

## places
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
Principal places: Zapolyarny Palace, home of the Cryo Archon; the Snezhnograd Veche State Hall; the Druzhna headquarters; the Adventurers' Guild headquarters; Kresnik's Burning Heart; Snezhnograd Square, with the Podvorye Restaurant in front of it; and Zolnyy, the Ash District.
### LARGE +
Glupov, the Fatui's satellite fortress-city, lies near Snezhnograd and is called a satellite city of it. A research organisation of some kind is also said to have a base in the city (a Nod-Krai loading screen mentions it, without detail). Details of each major site are in the sub-entries.

## people
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
Citizens are mostly human, with a fair number of Snowland fae living among them, though fewer than in Novokitezhgrad. Volkodlak and fae staff run the Podvorye Restaurant. Notable officials are the Mayor, Pulcinella; Veche councillors such as Eteri and Euphrosiniya; and a Veche advisor, Golitsyn. The Adventurers' Guild has adventurers posted here.
### LARGE +
The Savage Bears, a local group with named members Miroslava, Krylov and Anfissa, and a pair of "Mystery-Hunters", Pasha and Vadim, are among the colourful locals the wiki lists. These are minor residents; do not turn them into plot-relevant figures.

## governance
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
Anastasya, the Tsaritsa and Cryo Archon, rules Snezhnaya from Zapolyarny Palace. Administration is carried out by the Snezhnograd Veche, the council she founded, presided over by Pulcinella. The Fatui act as government, diplomatic corps and military; the Druzhna are the older soldiery. Pulcinella is the second and current Mayor of Snezhnograd, the first being his mentor.
### LARGE +
The Veche meets at the State Hall, where citizen representatives deliberate on new laws, a custom kept since the early days. In 7.0 a Veche vote on Project Stuzha was held; a minority opposed it and the project continued until the next Veche period.

## culture
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
Daily life revolves around heat and work: the Torch is the city's centre, a resident (Gorchakova) says snowstorm days are when its warmth is truly felt, and the Podvorye Restaurant is known for refined takes on traditional food and fire-water. Alchemy is forbidden, which shapes what shops can sell. Fae and humans mix, with a longstanding awareness of the old fae aristocracy that the Veche replaced.

## history
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
Kitezh was Snezhnaya's first city and imperial capital from the 417th Cycle. After the Belyi Tsar pardoned humanity and admitted it to his realm, he moved his seat to Snezhnograd. He ruled from Zapolyarny Palace until his death, after which Anastasya took his place. She was crowned Tsaritsa in the first year of the 799th Cycle; many fae princes refused to come to Snezhnograd to seek an audience, and the Dalegrad clan broke ties with the city. The Fatui built Glupov nearby in year four, and the Veche was established in year six with the human Ioanna Ivanovna as its first chairperson.
### LARGE +
Kresnik's Torch was built almost as soon as the Belyi Tsar finished his experiments in the Pale Crown Palace, and the city's most important Torch burns in Kresnik's Burning Heart. The Pale Crown Palace text places the Belyi Tsar's death in the Cataclysm five hundred years ago. In the seventh year of the 799th Cycle the Imperial Order Reformation Edict was published; many fae princes gave up their titles and left Snezhnaya. The Royal Energy Commission oversees the Torches and Kresnik Kristall.
### ULTRA +
The cycle dating comes from the in-game history book; canon sources read here give no exact conversion to modern years. During the Version 7.0 to 7.1 story the Traveler is brought to Snezhnograd, meets Sandrone and Tartaglia there and attends a Veche meeting (see the Project Stuzha entry).

## atmosphere
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
A glittering, orderly northern capital: deep snow, the glow of Torch heat, steam and iron in industrial streets, a night sky Flins admires. Officials bustle, and the memory of the old fae nobility and the Tsaritsa's distant presence hang in the air.
### LARGE +
Outdoor scenes in a blizzard still feel warm within the Torch's range; scenes in the Veche hall feel formal and slightly tense.

## dangers
@version: 7.0
### LARGE
Inside the city, ordinary danger is low and mostly political. Beyond its limits the blizzards, the Shadow Realm and Fatui patrols matter. Outsiders may be investigated by the authorities, though in Light and Shadow, Intertwined (7.1) the Tsaritsa ordered all investigation of the Traveler to cease.

## travel
@version: 7.0
### LARGE
Snezhnograd is the hub of Snezhnaya, near the Fatui fortress of Glupov. Zapolyarny Palace is not freely open to visitors; the Traveler was given unrestricted access by the Tsaritsa's decree after meeting her.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 Snezhnograd is the working capital of Snezhnaya under the Tsaritsa and Mayor Pulcinella, with its Torch burning and alchemy still outlawed.
### STANDARD +
The Traveler has visited and been granted free entry to Zapolyarny Palace.

## entry: Zapolyarny Palace
@keys: Zapolyarny Palace, Glacial Palace of the Polestar
@version: 7.0
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
The residence of the Cryo Archon, whom the official map text describes as ruling from a throne of sculpted ice and standing alone on her high balcony as if holding the plaza and the world in her sight. The Belyi Tsar ruled from it until his death, after which Anastasya took his place. Named from a Russian word for "beyond the Arctic Circle". Two NPCs are listed: Avreliya and Yermolay.
### LARGE +
The Tsaritsa's decree after meeting the Traveler gave them unrestricted access. Pulcinella and Pantalone attended an audience with her there when Dottore presented the Gnoses he had taken in Sumeru.

## entry: Snezhnograd Veche State Hall
@keys: Veche State Hall, Snezhnograd Veche, Snezhnograd Veche State Hall
@version: 7.0
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
The hall where the Snezhnograd Veche holds its regular meetings, where citizen representatives from various groups deliberate on new laws, a tradition kept since Snezhnaya's early days. Established by Anastasya to replace the fae aristocracy and first chaired by Ioanna Ivanovna, it is now presided over by Pulcinella. Listed NPCs: Councillors Eteri and Euphrosiniya, Privy Advisor Golitsyn, Slavina and Zotov.
### LARGE +
The Veche voted on Project Stuzha during the Traveler's visit; Chugunov voted against it, but sixty-one percent were in favour and the project survived. Pulcinella, as Speaker, announced the result.

## entry: Kresnik's Burning Heart
@keys: Kresnik's Burning Heart, Ever-Burning Hearth Core
@version: 7.0
@spoiler: none
### STANDARD
A large chamber beneath the central plaza containing Snezhnaya's most important Kresnik's Torch, a furnace fuelled by Kresnik Kristall. The Belyi Tsar made the Torches so humans could survive the cold; every Snezhnayan city is built around one. Figures listed there include Yelagina, Zarechnaya (acting chair of the Royal Energy Commission), Zaretsky (vice president), Zavyalov and Zybina.
### LARGE +
Smaller civilian-model Torches are scattered across the country. The prototype Torches remain in the Pale Crown Palace.

## entry: Zolnyy, the Ash District
@keys: Zolnyy, Ash District
@version: 7.0
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Snezhnograd's industrial district. Official text says that even among the ashes one can hold fast to a dream with a heart as steadfast as tin, perhaps how it got its name. Local figures include Agrafena, Artyem, Iryna, Raissa, Scheffield and two shopkeepers, Grigory ("Bratskaya") and Zyryanov ("Kvasnik").
### LARGE +
The World Quest "Disputes Without Honor and Humanity" starts here and involves the Luchkin family, Zmei and a rival Izmaylov family base. Afterwards a children's drawing at the Luchkins' base becomes interactable.

## entry: Podvorye Restaurant
@keys: Podvorye Restaurant, Podvorye
@version: 7.0
@spoiler: none
### STANDARD
A long-established Snezhnayan restaurant in front of Snezhnograd Square, serving refined takes on traditional local cuisine and fine fire-water. Its owner and whole staff are volkodlak and fae. Staff include Nefedov (owner), Gromov (cook), Smirnov (bartender), Eliseev and Buturlina (servers).

## entry: Druzhna HQ and the Adventurers' Guild Headquarters
@keys: Druzhna HQ, Druzhna headquarters, Adventurers' Guild Headquarters
@version: 7.0
@spoiler: none
### STANDARD
Druzhna HQ is the base of the Snezhnayan soldiery, with listed NPCs Malinina, Musina, Rozhdestvensky, Shakhovskaya and Yarovaya (a podporuchik). The Adventurers' Guild's headquarters stands in the city because the government founded the Guild; adventurers such as Khitrov and Lesnova are posted there.

## entry: Project Stuzha in Snezhnograd
@keys: Project Stuzha, Stuzha
@version: 7.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Project Stuzha is a Fatui-run set of energy reforms passed by the Veche: a framework for rationing and allocating Kresnik Kristall that makes everyone cut back, redirecting the saved energy to the Armory Palace. Pulcinella and Pantalone are among its organisers, and the Fatui handle Kristall shipments. In Silence Alone is Disfavored (7.0) the Veche voted on extending it: sixty-one percent were in favour, a shrinking margin after the Veche member Chugunov voted against it, and Pulcinella declared it would stay in effect until the next Veche period. Later events presented it as a lead-up to Project Mistilteinn.
### LARGE +
In the quest Mistilteinn (7.0) Pierro revealed that Project Stuzha consolidates the nation's resources to serve Project Mistilteinn, a weapon meant to let the Tsaritsa contend with the Heavenly Principles using energy reverse-engineered from the Stellar Linchpins. Ronova's curse of death blocked the Fatui's technological breakthroughs and the project was briefly suspended; per the wiki it resumed after Celestia revoked Ronova's authority for misusing it. The wiki's Pulcinella page also records Stuzha's suspension. This is major spoiler material; use only if the scene calls for it.
