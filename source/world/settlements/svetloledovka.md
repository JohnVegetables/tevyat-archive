## summary
### LITE
Svetloledovka ('Light-Ice Village') is a Prime Ice mining town and railway station in Snezhnaya, built in the shadow of Fellfrost Peak. Many of its workers live in Snezhnograd and commute by train. In Chapter VII a malfunction and a monster trapped miners here, and the Traveler first met Odette during the rescue.
### STANDARD +
It is also where the Traveler finds the mysterious Exaiphanes Blade deep in the cavern. A Local Legend, the Bandersnatch, becomes available afterwards.

## geography
### STANDARD
The town stands below Fellfrost Peak, the great snow-sloped mountain south of Snezhnograd. The Cavern of Prime Ice Mountain is entered through a mining area at the settlement, with a notice at the entrance warning people away. A tavern stands in the middle of the town and a lower level holds work areas and machines. The giant excavators are heavy enough that the town can be endangered by snow load.
### LARGE +
The site's heat sources matter: one worker, Zhuravlev, is found freezing on a lower level and thanks the Traveler for placing a heat source next to him.

## people
### STANDARD
Named residents and workers include Demidov, Ignat, Klim, Lyubava, Nekrasov, Pashkov (the Safety Officer), Sobolev (Engineering Supervisor), Veselov, Vorontsov, Vvedensky, Zaleskaya, Zhuravlev and Zvezdina. The trapped miners in the 7.0 incident include Pevtsova, Ryabushinsky and Gornova.
### LARGE +
Zhuravlev, after the Traveler warms him, gives them Glaciafruit sent by his family and a modest reward; he says a rough fellow like him has nothing valuable to offer. A Fatui staff member, Zverev, reports the failure to Sandrone.

## economy
### STANDARD
Prime Ice is mined here and the settlement is run around it; the train Navigation text says many workers who mine Prime Ice live in Snezhnograd and commute. Sandrone says relief takes weeks to months, because Pantalone must approve the budget and Pulcinella sign off the plan before work can proceed.

## history
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
During unusually heavy snow, a support on one of the giant excavators broke and fell, which woke a dormant burrowing beast in a cave and trapped miners. Odette's Fatui team, which was nearby, started the rescue; Sandrone, who was in the middle of a tea break with the Traveler in Snezhnograd, came with the Traveler to help. The Traveler and Odette led out the five trapped people, found the Exaiphanes Blade deep in the cave and escaped as the cave collapsed behind them, trapping the Chimeric Burrowbeasts inside.
### LARGE +
Sandrone reinforced all the equipment so that it could stand months of heavy snowfall. A notice at the cave entrance, placed by Odette's team, was later edited to warn that monsters are active inside and that mining there is suspended. The incident report still had to go through Snezhnograd's slow processes, and the Traveler left with the feeling that there ought to be a better way.

## dangers
### STANDARD
The Cavern of Prime Ice Mountain holds chimeric burrowbeasts, electro slimes and the Bandersnatch (Ice Marauder, Lurking Burrowbeast), with a Local Legend, Bandersnatch Grounded Snowhawk, available later. Mining has been suspended. Snow load and cold are constant dangers.

## quests
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
The town is the setting for the second half of 'Great Deeds on the Tundra' (Archon Quest, Chapter VII, Act I, part 3). A notice and the 'Zhuravlev's Troubles' puzzle are found here.

## interactables
### ULTRA
A notice at the cave entrance changes after the rescue: before it warns that the mining area is dangerous, after it says that active monsters are inside and mining is suspended. Until the quest reaches the site, the cavern is closed with a message that the area is restricted to authorised personnel. A 7.1 patch note mentions a bug in a challenge run by an NPC named Zharkaya at Svetloledovka; the challenge itself was not read.

## status
### LITE
Inhabited and functioning as of 7.1, with the monster's cave closed and the machines reinforced. Sandrone expected relief to take weeks to months.

## entry: The Svetloledovka Rescue and the Exaiphanes Blade
@keys: Exaiphanes Blade, Pevtsova, Zverev, Burrowbeast
@keys2: Svetloledovka, Odette
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
When Zverev reports the failure of the Svetloledovka equipment, Sandrone drops her tea party, and the Traveler goes along. Odette tells them five people are trapped and a hostile monster of unknown type is nearby. In the cave, a spotlight mechanism casts a huge shadow of the creature on the ice; Pevtsova shouts at the creature to stay back. After the fight the Traveler touches a strange star floating in the cave and receives the Exaiphanes Blade. Sandrone later introduces Odette to the Traveler as the prima ballerina of Snezhnograd and a rising Fatui figure, and says the two crowning jewels of the Korolevskiy Troupe are Odette and Vodyanitsa.
