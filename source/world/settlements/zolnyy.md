## summary
### LITE
Zolnyy is the industrial "Ash District" of Snezhnograd, the capital of Snezhnaya. Its official map description says that even amid ashes a person may hold fast to a dream, perhaps how the district got its name. It holds ordinary residents, a kvass shop, a general store and the stronghold of the Luchkin, one of the fae gangs of the High Table.
### STANDARD +
Zolnyy is where the Traveler is drawn into the gang quarrel of the Mean Streets of the Despicable quests (7.0). The Luchkin, led by Zmei, treat it as home ground; the rival Izmaylov gang, led until its collapse by Vasiliy, operates elsewhere around the city.

## geography
### STANDARD
A district inside Snezhnograd, in the Everfrozen Earth region of Snezhnaya, described in canon as industrial. The world quest that opens the gang storyline begins on a path that crosses the rail tracks north of Central Station. Its borders are not described.

## places
### STANDARD
"Kvasnik" is a beverage shop run by the Volkodlak Zyryanov; its owner claims to serve the most authentic kvass, though long-lived locals know the drink came first and the shop after. "Bratskaya" is a general-goods shop run by Grigory whose stock restocks daily. The Luchkin stronghold is where the gang's leader Zmei receives allies.
### LARGE +
After the Izmaylov conflict, a table at the stronghold holds a children's drawing showing Vasiliy, Iryna, Zmei and apparently Lyubov, the four adoptive children of Izmaylov; interacting with it unlocks the achievement "A Promise of Old". Kvasnik also sells ice cream, and a playable Snezhnayan character in the party gets unique dialogue and a themed variety.

## people
### STANDARD
The Luchkin gang: Zmei (leader, and Speaker of the High Table), Iryna (the High Table's enforcer, feared among the fae gangs) and members such as Sofya, the Volkodlak who brings the Traveler to the stronghold, and the undercover operative Gureva. Shopkeepers Zyryanov and Grigory, and residents including Agrafena, Raissa and Scheffield, also live here.
### LARGE +
Residents seen in passing are ordinary and a little comic: Raissa blames a "terrifying cat-spirit" for stealing her cured meat while Agrafena warns neighbours to look out for themselves, and Scheffield nurses a headache from Fire-Water. Odette of the Snezhnaya Theatre can be met at Kvasnik, remarking that the vegetable juice there is good but she cannot drink much. These are idle open-world lines, not plot.

## governance
### STANDARD
No municipal government is attributed to Zolnyy itself; Snezhnograd has its Veche and a Mayor. In practice the gang quests treat the district as fae-gang ground governed by the High Table's Laws: no harm to families, debts must be repaid, and do not commit acts devoid of mercy or justice.
### LARGE +
In Burn the Storehouses and the Stables a girl named Marfa is pressed over a debt by Izmaylov collectors; Luchkin members step in because her situation falls under their jurisdiction, though Sofya admits that justice rarely earns much Mora.

## culture
### LARGE
Gang culture here stresses keeping to the Law, repaying debts and sheltering the poor. Vasiliy resented that fae gangs get the jobs the Druzhna and the Energy Commission do not want, calling them dogs summoned and dismissed at will. Zmei's counter-view is that the small measure of compassion, being unable to bear hurting the innocent, is what separates people from beasts.

## history
### STANDARD
Canon gives Zolnyy no founding story. The High Table that its gangs belong to was founded some 400-500 years ago by Izmaylov's forerunners after the Burner of Cities' short-lived rebellion was put down. In more recent years Nikolay "Pakhan" Izmaylov, the "Old Sire", adopted four children and gave them gang surnames: Vasiliy Izmaylov, Zmei Luchkin, Iryna Voronina and Lyubov Remusov.
### LARGE +
Several months before the Traveler's quests the Old Sire fell ill and died suddenly; Vasiliy later admitted he killed him. Smaller gangs fell into disorder, and the Izmaylovs began spending heavily and collecting debts by force to build a device to open the Shadow Realm. The Luchkin, with the Traveler, took a sample from an Izmaylov shipment, rescued the undercover Gureva from an Izmaylov stronghold (a former Fatui base northwest of Snezhnograd), and stopped Vasiliy in the Train Boneyard, where he died.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
A working-class neighbourhood in Snezhnograd's snow, with shop signs in Snezhnayan script and neighbours trading gossip. The Luchkin stronghold feels like a warm family firm that is quietly worried about the gangs' future.

## dangers
### LARGE
Debt collectors and gang disputes. In the quests, the Izmaylovs' former Fatui outpost and the Train Boneyard hold guards and Shadow Realm distortion; the district itself is not shown as hostile, and friends of the Luchkin are welcome.

## travel
### LARGE
Snezhnograd has a Central Station and rail links. The Luchkin rented a train for the Traveler's party to reach the Train Boneyard, which lies past a freezing zone.

## quests
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
The Mean Streets of the Despicable world quests: Burn the Storehouses and the Stables (the Traveler helps Sofya and the Luchkin, then steals a sample from an Izmaylov shipment) and Disputes Without Honor and Humanity (rescue Gureva, then stop Vasiliy).

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 Zolnyy is peaceful. The Luchkin remain under Zmei, and the Izmaylov gang lacks a leader.
### STANDARD +
After Vasiliy's death the High Table held a funeral for him and is seeking a new Izmaylov leader. Zmei is drafting his first proposal as Speaker to ease tensions, wanting to reassure the remaining Izmaylovs without turning against the people they harmed. Nothing in 7.0-7.1 changes the district further.
### ULTRA +
Zmei's stance: Izmaylov as a name bears no blame for Vasiliy's mistakes and deserves a future, yet he cannot drive either side further apart, since too many people have already bled. The rewards he gave the Traveler were a block of refined Kristall (the spent key, now little more than raw energy) and a map found in the Butcher's box, leading to the "legendary thief's treasure" of an old rumour about thieves who robbed gangs and hid the loot. Zmei chose to give the map away rather than chase it, saying justice stays clean when it is not about gain.

## entry: The Luchkin Stronghold
@keys: Luchkin, Luchkins, Luchkin stronghold, Zmei Luchkin
@keys2: Zolnyy, Snezhnograd, High Table
@version: 7.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The Luchkin are a fae gang of Snezhnograd led by Zmei, one of Izmaylov's adopted children, who also serves as Speaker of the High Table. Their stronghold is in Zolnyy. They hold to the High Table's Laws, and treat the Traveler as a friend. Members include Sofya and the undercover operative Gureva; Iryna, the High Table's enforcer, works alongside them.
### LARGE +
Vasiliy claimed the Old Sire meant to leave his High Table seat to Zmei because Zmei wants nothing and would change nothing. Zmei says he entered the Shadow Realm once as a child and wanted nothing more to do with it.

## entry: Vasiliy, the Young Sire
@keys: Young Sire, Vasiliy Izmaylov, the Butcher
@keys2: Zolnyy, Izmaylov, Luchkin
@version: 7.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Vasiliy "Oedipus" Izmaylov, the Young Sire, led the Izmaylov gang after the Old Sire's death. Resenting how the powerful use fae gangs, he worked to open the Shadow Realm with a mechanism and a key of refined Kresnik Kristall, hoping to win a place at Snezhnaya's real council, and admitted killing the Old Sire. His second-in-command is called the Butcher. The Luchkin, Iryna and the Traveler stopped him in the Train Boneyard, where he died recalling a childhood game of hide-and-seek in which Zmei and Iryna found him.
