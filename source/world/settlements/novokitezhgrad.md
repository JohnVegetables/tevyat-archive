## summary
### LITE
Novokitezhgrad ('New Kitezh') is a city in Snezhnaya southeast of Snezhnograd. It is famed for its liquor and its mining, above all of Kresnik Kristall, and many Snowland fae live there alongside humans. It was founded to replace Kitezhgrad, the old imperial capital destroyed by the Abyss. Players cannot visit it as of 7.1.
### STANDARD +
Its Kristall travels from here by rail to the rest of the country. A character named Netochka says she even saw Novokitezhgrad liquor being drunk in Nod-Krai and that 'we' are known for liquor as much as mining, which suggests she speaks as someone from the city or its region.

## geography
### STANDARD
Southeast of Snezhnograd. The trail between Nasha Town and Novokitezhgrad is called a tough one by Tolfin, a character in a Nod-Krai quest.

## people
### STANDARD
Humans live alongside plenty of Snowland fae. Netochka remarks that she assumed there would be plenty of fae in other places too, 'like back home'.
### LARGE +
The Domovoy Grand Duke Alvis oversaw the construction of the city.

## economy
### STANDARD
Two industries stand out: liquor and mining. Most Kresnik Kristall, which heats Snezhnaya, is mined here and sent out by rail, under oversight of the Royal Energy Commission.
### LARGE +
Alvis had monopolised mining in old Kitezhgrad since its founding, and Novokitezhgrad shares the trade. Foundryman's Calculus says he had wanted the new city to serve as the cornerstone of his standing in the Tsaritsa's new regime.

## history
### STANDARD
The old Kitezhgrad was founded by Monomakh Snezhnyi before he became the Belyi Tsar and was the imperial capital of the Pale Star court and the first city of Snezhnaya. After the king of the Volkodlak, Miscislav, was killed, the Emperor took the name Belyi Tsar and eventually moved the seat of government to Snezhnograd. The Abyss later destroyed Kitezhgrad, and Novokitezhgrad was built to replace it.
### LARGE +
Foundryman's Calculus presents Alvis as wanting the construction of Novokitezhgrad to serve as the cornerstone of his standing in the Tsaritsa's new regime, and says that when he returned from Novokitezhgrad he found the Royal Energy Commission already had a new master.
### ULTRA +
The wiki's Alvis page dates the loss of Kitezhgrad to the Cataclysm about 500 years ago, says Alvis was Lord of Kitezhgrad for the city's whole existence and says he founded Novokitezhgrad afterwards. The same page places the Belyi Tsar's unification of Snezhnaya about 3,000 years ago.

## travel
### STANDARD
Not accessible; known only from dialogue, loading-screen text and item descriptions. The city's direction from Snezhnograd and the difficulty of its trail are mentioned only in dialogue.

## status
### LITE
Exists and works as a mining and liquor centre as of 7.1; never shown on screen.

## entry: Kitezhgrad
@keys: Kitezhgrad, Kitezh, old capital
@keys2: Snezhnaya
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Kitezhgrad (Kitezh) was the first city of Snezhnaya, an imperial capital built in the fae manner and the seat of the Pale Star court under Monomakh. It stayed the centre of power over the frozen lands until the Emperor, having pardoned humanity and taken them into his realm, moved his seat to Snezhnograd. Its destruction by the Abyss is the reason for Novokitezhgrad.
