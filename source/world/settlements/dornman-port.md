## summary
### LITE
Dornman Port is the busy port town of northern Mondstadt, on Windrest Peak north of Cider Lake. Its loading screen calls it a bustling harbor and one of the continent's major trade hubs, welcoming merchant ships from many nations. In the aristocratic era it was the fief of the Gunnhildr Clan. Mondstadt knights are stationed here, and the port has no church.
### STANDARD +
It is known for cargo shipping and flower cultivation. Legally docking requires a valid entry permit. The area opened in game with Windrest Peak in version 6.5.

## geography
### STANDARD
The port sits on the water in the far north of Mondstadt, where the weather is clearer and more varied than in the south. It has a western bridge, a bazaar, a central square, the Knights of Favonius base, and outskirts. The Old Sanatorium Site lies on a hill above, and the Knights of Favonius Ruins lie beneath the old lighthouse. Nearby is Millhaven.
### LARGE +
Henry Morton says it is too far to charter a boat to the Nameless Island from here. Ruins just north of the port hold a time-trial challenge, and the Open-Eyed boss roams Windrest Peak.

## places
### STANDARD
The Knights of Favonius base, an unofficial name, is the Ranged Company's centre in Windrest Peak, with members of the Logistics, Combined Arms, Support and Investigation companies. The Devil's Cut tavern, owned by Groovy, mirrors the capital's Angel's Share in name. Fragrant Whispers is a flower shop run by Alodia. The bazaar, a "Taste of the North" venue and a windmill are also noted.
### LARGE +
The Knights of Favonius Ruins are catacombs beneath the city where the knights who defended the port during the Cataclysm lie. The Old Sanatorium Site is a demolished sanatorium built on the ruins left when the Temple of Space was lifted into the sky.

## people
### STANDARD
Lohen, Vice Captain of the Ranged Company, has been stationed here and teases locals and troublemakers alike. Knights at the base include the gunsmiths Herstal and Koch and snipers Gunther and Ursula. Hannelore and Philomena (a nun) are also local. Groovy runs the Devil's Cut, Alodia the flower shop, and Insigne, Lietzberg and Patricia work in the bazaar.
### LARGE +
Beidou's crew (The Crux) has docked here. Frederica Gunnhildr was sent back by Grand Master Varka to help defend it. Mona was born here, to parents who had moved in from elsewhere, and found Barbeloth before settling in the capital.

## governance
### STANDARD
Local order is kept by the Knights of Favonius from the base. In aristocratic times the Gunnhildr Clan held the fief; after Vennessa's rebellion they led the push to abolish hereditary fiefs and likely handed theirs to the people.
### LARGE +
The Reconnaissance Company under Eula patrols the long trade road between the port and Mondstadt City, especially during Weinlesefest.

## history
### STANDARD
In the age of Great Arcadia the area belonged to the city-state Elysium under the Gunnhildr Clan, who after its fall took refuge in Decarabian's city. After Barbatos scattered the snow they returned north. The port is said to be named after the Thorn King, a possible clan ancestor. They founded Mondstadt's first sanctuary to Barbatos here.
### LARGE +
In the tyrannical age the Gunnhildrs broke with the Imunlaukr Clan by seizing the bishop sent from the capital, throwing him from the sanctuary's high tower and tearing it down, so the port has had no church since. The Gunnhildrs sided with the people in Vennessa's rebellion and were spared exile. In the Cataclysm 500 years ago the port nearly fell and many knights died defending it.

## quests
### LARGE
The Windrest Peak story quests, including Winds Beneath the Tower of Silence, take place around the port and the Knights' ruins. In Alchemical Ascension (limited-time event) Eula and Beidou traveled from the port to the alchemy showcase after a smuggling crackdown.

## dangers
### LARGE
Smuggling is a recurring problem. Eula's company once tracked a dangerous Abyss Order cargo here that was bound for Liyue Harbor; Yanfei, a consultant on the merchant vessel, was imperilled until Eula rescued her.

## travel
### STANDARD
The port is a hub for sea travel. A route to Port Ormos in Sumeru has opened and a local trading company plans to export fruit, though one reply doubts the fruit would survive the trip.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Salt air and flower scent, sailors, merchants and bards in the square, knights keeping a watchful eye, and a lively, cosmopolitan mood quite unlike the capital's slow pace. Lohen jokes that even the Treasure Hoarders avoid the place since he arrived.

## status
### LITE
A thriving trade port with a Knights of Favonius base as of 7.1.
### STANDARD +
Varka ordered several returning knights to the port to help defend it (in-game mail, 2025). No Dornman Port content from 7.0-7.1 was found.

## trivia
### ULTRA
Its viewpoint is titled "The Harbor Where a Thousand Winds Converge". A boy by the water beneath the western bridge feeds ducks, and a Shooting Practice challenge is run by Herstal at the base. In one open-world scene Lohen and Hannelore discuss three children's pranks on Granny Marthe, thwarted by gusts of wind carrying notes that the Anemo Archon is always watching; Lohen's teasing earns Hannelore's rebuke. Lohen also hunts a disguised thief in the bazaar with Insigne hiding up high.

Three loading-screen accounts compete: a madman in a crown of thorns from Windrest Peak preaching apocalypse; a Thorn King god whose people called themselves Dornman before Barbatos defeated him; or a Gunnhildr ancestor named the Thorn King. Researchers reject the first. Keep it unresolved.
