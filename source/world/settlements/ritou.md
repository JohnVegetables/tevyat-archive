## summary
### LITE
Ritou is the port on Narukami Island where every non-Inazuman entering or leaving Inazuma is processed. It is under the jurisdiction of the Kanjou Commission, whose ruling Hiiragi Clan has its estate here, and it holds the Outlander Affairs Agency, the foreign merchants' International Trade Association, and a plaza shaded by a great red maple. Under the Sakoku Decree, foreigners who arrived could go no further without a hard-won Travel Permit.
### STANDARD +
Legend says Lord Hiiragi Hiroshi of the Kanjou Commission built it centuries ago from a deserted island into a thriving trade centre, greatly impressing the Shogun. The Traveler arrived here at the start of the Inazuma Archon Quest (2.0).

## geography
### STANDARD
Ritou sits on a peninsula of Narukami Island, its name meaning "outlying island", with the docks at its entrance, a central area around the plaza, the Hiiragi Estate, a southern coast, and a guarded exit on the road toward the rest of Narukami. The official map describes a fishing village of red maples and green roof tiles. A Statue of The Seven stands near the shore.

## places
### STANDARD
The docks, with Kanjou inspectors and the harbour bulletin board; the Outlander Affairs Agency, which handles foreigners' documents; the International Trade Association; the Hiiragi Estate, which serves as the Kanjou Commissioner's office; and the central plaza with its large maple, known since the Irodori Festival as the Five Kasen Plaza.
### LARGE +
Shops include Eipekkusu (Kaede's souvenir shop: Inazuman novels, porcelain tanuki and lucky fox figures, and once statues of the Shogun), Sailing Breeze (Karpillia's old Mondstadt liquor brand, which moved here after failing to compete with the Dawn Winery), the Syavushi Bakshesh Shoppe (Vahid's shop selling Sumeru's Anahitian Blessing fertiliser), a branch of Liyue's Chuyu Trading Company, and street-food stalls.

## people
### STANDARD
Kanjou Commission officers, inspectors and ashigaru; Hiiragi Chisato, acting head of the Hiiragi Clan; and a community of foreign merchants and sailors from Mondstadt, Liyue, Snezhnaya, Sumeru and elsewhere. Kurisu, born Christopher in Fontaine, founded and leads the International Trade Association and has helped many newcomers.
### ULTRA +
Other named locals include the inspectors Yurika and Kageyama, squad leaders Ebina Gonshirou and Sasaki, the officer Shinnojou (who admires Chisato), the guard Master Qin "Thunder Hand", Qiuyue of the Chuyu Trading Company and the merchant Harrison. After the 5.3 Archon Quest a Natlan visitor, Ruta of the People of the Springs, can be found here hoping to climb Mt. Yougou for a fortune slip.

## governance
### STANDARD
The Kanjou Commission, one of the Tri-Commission, manages Inazuma's finances, customs and borders from Ritou. Under the Sakoku Decree it ran a strict and corrupt regime: complicated assessments for entry or exit, heavy fees, and harsh treatment of foreigners. Commissioner Hiiragi Shinsuke conspired with the Fatui and was arrested after the 2.1 Archon Quest; his daughter Chisato now acts as clan head, and the commission has treated foreigners far better since.
### LARGE +
Kanjou notices under Sakoku required valid documents to land, registration and taxation of weapons, camping gear and foreign goods, and a year's surveillance in Ritou for anyone who had dealt with the Sangonomiya. Snezhnayan merchants and the Fatui were initially exempt from the restrictions thanks to diplomatic ties; after the Fatui plot came to light, Snezhnayan merchants' special trade privileges were revoked and their arrears called in.

## history
### STANDARD
Lord Hiiragi Hiroshi wanted foreign trade centralised under the government and built Ritou to do it. To prove its worth he imported foreign inventions, among them a Snezhnayan blast furnace used to create the hot springs at Tenshukaku as a gift for the Shogun. When the Sakoku Decree sealed Inazuma about a year before the Traveler arrived, storms ringed the islands and foreign merchants were stranded in Ritou; the official viewpoint says the streets lost the bustle of a century before while the Kanjou Commission itself still flourished.
### LARGE +
The Traveler landed here with Beidou's Crux fleet, which once docked at Ritou, and was met by Thoma (2.0). They exposed a Kanjou officer's Crystal Marrow racket against the International Trade Association, then escaped Ritou with Chisato's help after learning that Signora had conspired with her father to keep them there. After the Vision Hunt Decree ended, Shinsuke was detained, Ei abolished the Sakoku Decree (2.5), and visitors and trade returned. Yae Miko then revived the Irodori Festival in Ritou (2.6).

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Red maples, green tiles, the creak of the docks and a mix of accents. Under Sakoku it felt like a waiting room: outlanders idling in the plaza, staring out at the empty sea, short of money and patience. Since the decree ended it has been busier, though still the place where foreigners first deal with Inazuman paperwork.

## dangers
### LARGE
The worst danger in Ritou was bureaucratic: extortionate fees, arbitrary taxes and threats of arrest from corrupt officers. The harbour board once warned sailors about piracy and told them to stock up before leaving. Outside the town, nobushi and kairagi ronin and Treasure Hoarders work the roads, as the Traveler found when escorting Chisato's supply convoy.

## quests
### STANDARD
Chapter II Act I (2.0): Setting Sail, Confessions of an Outlander and Ritou Escape Plan, followed by the world quest Chisato's Letter, which earns the Travel Permit. Kamisato Ayato's story quest (2.6) involves a plot inside the Hiiragi Clan. The 2.6 event Hues of the Violet Garden takes place here.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 Ritou is open: the Sakoku Decree was abolished in 2.5, foreigners come and go freely after processing, and the Kanjou Commission is far less corrupt under Hiiragi Chisato.
### STANDARD +
Hiiragi Shinsuke remains imprisoned. No new events in Ritou appear in the Nod-Krai (6.x) or Snezhnaya (7.0-7.1) storylines.

## entry: Outlander Affairs Agency
@keys: Outlander Affairs Agency, Yurika
### STANDARD
The Kanjou Commission's office for foreign visitors' documents and requests. Under the Sakoku Decree it fleeced stranded outlanders: the inspector Yurika first asked the Traveler for two million Mora, and Thoma talked her down to 600, saying such practices were common. After the commission was purged of corruption, the agency became more lenient and charges a processing fee of 500 Mora.

## entry: International Trade Association
@keys: International Trade Association, Kurisu, Five Kasen Plaza
### STANDARD
Founded by foreigners to trade in Inazuma (importing goods such as Silk Flowers) and to help newcomers survive the Kanjou Commission's harsh treatment. Its president Kurisu, a Fontainian who took an Inazuman name, welcomes outlanders in trouble. During the Irodori Festival it housed the foreign guests of honour, and the plaza in front of it was named the Five Kasen Plaza.

## entry: The Crystal Marrow Racket
@keys: Crystal Marrow, Keijirou, Werner, Confessions of an Outlander
@keys2: Ritou, International Trade Association, Kanjou Commission
@version: 2.0
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
The Kanjou Commission suddenly demanded the association's taxes in Crystal Marrow rather than Mora. The officer Keijirou and his men bought up the supply and resold it through Werner, a former member from Mondstadt, at ever-higher prices. The Traveler found Keijirou's hidden ledger; when an officer, Katsuie, moved to arrest the merchants, Thoma produced a Yashiro Commission card and an ornate fan, and the matter was dropped. The Crystal Marrow the commission collected later turned out to feed the Fatui's Delusion factory.

## entry: Ritou Escape Plan and the Travel Permit
@keys: Ritou Escape Plan, Travel Permit, Chisato's Letter, Hiiragi Estate
@keys2: Ritou, Hiiragi, Kanjou
@version: 2.0
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Barred from leaving Ritou, the Traveler visited Commissioner Hiiragi Shinsuke, who set absurd tasks (300 freshly picked Qingxin, or delivering 709 letters). His daughter Chisato secretly summoned them at night, revealed that an arrogant woman (Signora) had conspired with her father to keep them on Ritou, and slipped them out as the "qualified escort" for a Kanjou shipment she accompanied personally. Her letter to Kujou Kamaji of the Tenryou Commission in Inazuma City then earned them a Travel Permit to come and go freely.

## entry: Irodori Festival in Ritou
@keys: Irodori Festival, Hues of the Violet Garden
@keys2: Ritou, Inazuma, Yae Miko, Ayato
@version: 2.6
@availability: limited
### LARGE
After the Sakoku Decree ended, Yae Miko revived the long-forgotten Irodori Festival, which once honoured the poets known as the Five Kasen, as an international cultural convention centred on light novels. Kamisato Ayato invited celebrities from several nations to Ritou. The plaza before the International Trade Association became the Five Kasen Plaza, shops sold light-novel goods and fan-fiction stalls appeared outside the Outlander Affairs Agency. This took place during the limited-time event Hues of the Violet Garden (2.6).

## entry: The Hiiragi Wedding Plot
@keys: Hiiragi wedding, Matsuura, The Firmiana Leaf Falls
@keys2: Hiiragi Chisato, Kujou Kamaji, Ayato
@version: 2.6
@spoiler: minor
### LARGE
In Kamisato Ayato's story quest, rumours spread that Chisato would marry Kujou Kamaji. Chisato had sent no proposal letter: the match was pushed by plotters among the Hiiragi Clan's supporters, including the Kanjou official Matsuura, while the commission was unsettled by Shinsuke's imprisonment, and Shinnojou was seized after he objected. Ayato, with Kamaji's help, turned public opinion so that Chisato became an asset the plotters could not discard, and she asserted her authority over them for the first time.
