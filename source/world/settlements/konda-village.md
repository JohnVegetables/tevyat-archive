## summary
### LITE
Konda Village is a small, quiet village on Narukami Island, long the Shogunate's chief dye-producing village and famous for lavender melons and its dish Konda Cuisine. A few old families live there, led by the village chief Konda Densuke. With the Sakoku Decree and the war, trade dried up. Beneath its dry well lies a seal tied to the Sacred Sakura.
### STANDARD +
The village's secret is a mix of the small and the large. On the surface, young villagers stole the Shogunate's Mora from a cargo cart and the chief covered it up to protect the village's name. Beneath, the well hides an old barrier that the Sacred Sakura Cleansing Ritual requires the Traveler to lift.

## geography
### STANDARD
A farming village on Narukami Island, with fields of lavender melons, a stream and gully, a village well behind the chief's house, and a deserted shrine at the foot of the mountain. An abandoned shrine northeast of the village holds another barrier of the cleansing ritual.
### LARGE +
Local specialties include Naku Weed, Onikabuto and Sakura Bloom. Earth Kitsune and Sky Kitsune statues stand nearby, as do Electro cubes, Thunder Sakura Boughs and a Thunder Barrier. Hilichurls, Nobushi and Treasure Hoarders are found in the area.

## places
### STANDARD
The village chief's house, with the dry well behind it; the cart at the northeast entrance by the river; the gully; and a fox statue where the masked shrine maiden Hanachirusato waits.
### LARGE +
Under the well is a hidden underground space with a sealed shrine and rooms holding old objects such as an Old Stone Slate and a Rust-Worn Key. A Missing Person Bulletin Board stands in the village.

## people
### STANDARD
Konda Densuke is the chief; his adopted daughter Futaba and his grandson Takeru, whom Futaba calls her nephew, are also villagers. Others include Saimon Eri, whose son Jirou has been missing, Imatani Saburou, and villagers Tejima, Yasuda and Shinozuka.
### LARGE +
Saimon Jirou appears after the quest The Farmer's Treasure. The village has only a few families, all with long histories who "know each other's roots". Takeru is a boy who loves high places and says he saw a ghostly woman in thunderstorms.

## governance
### STANDARD
The Konda family has managed the village since, in the chief's words, an Almighty Shogun granted his ancestors the land. Taxes are paid regularly to the police station despite poor economic conditions.
### LARGE +
Densuke says Konda is older than Ritou Harbor. Traders and tourists once passed through on the way from Ritou to Inazuma City, but the Sakoku Decree stopped that.

## culture
### STANDARD
Konda is known for lavender melons, whose flowers make a durable natural dye, and for Konda Cuisine, a simmered dish everyone in the village knows how to make. It had no name until outsiders spread it across Inazuma and called it after the village.
### LARGE +
In game, it is cooked from fowl, snapdragon, lavender melon and mushroom. The name echoes the Japanese dish Chikuzen-ni, according to the wiki's trivia. Merchants and dyehouses like the melons, and they also serve as a pest repellent.

## history
### STANDARD
The village was once lively with outlanders and merchants; the Sakoku Decree and war aged it prematurely. In the Ritual quest, young villagers, drunk and bold, robbed a Shogunate cargo cart; the chief damaged the cart and covered up the theft, afraid the whole village would be shamed, and recorded it in a diary.
### LARGE +
Long ago, the villagers' ancestors were charged with guarding a barrier beneath the village, a duty now lost to time. The Sacred Sakura and the Thunder Sakura roots are connected, and filth gathers in them over time, so barriers are placed around the roots and must be cleaned in a ritual normally done by the Grand Narukami Shrine.
### ULTRA +
The Traveler traces a coin pouch, a bag from the gully and a cargo list to Densuke, then obtains the Old Key and unlocks the well. At the bottom a ward from Hanachirusato opens the barrier, and an Ochimusha appears and is defeated. Hanachirusato says she is loyal to Narukami Island, not the police station, and that she has long had dealings with the Konda families.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
A calm, ageing village of farms and old houses, smelling of simmered stew at night, with its empty roads and its shuttered shrine a reminder of a livelier past.

## dangers
### LARGE
Small groups of hilichurls and Treasure Hoarders on the roads; Nobushi; and the ghost stories of thunderstorms.

## quests
### LARGE
A Strange Story in Konda, the first quest of the Sacred Sakura Cleansing Ritual, followed by Sacrificial Offering, Cleansing Defilement (including Tanuki-Bayashi in the Forest) and Yougou Cleansing. Also The Farmer's Treasure and the random event Warden of Konda, in which the Traveler scares pigeons off the crops.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 Konda is a quiet village; the well's barrier is open and the ritual is complete.
### STANDARD +
The Sacred Sakura Cleansing Ritual ended with Hanachirusato dissipating after the Depths of Mt. Yougou. The Sakoku Decree has since been repealed. No 6.x or 7.0-7.1 plot touches it.

## entry: A Strange Story in Konda
@keys: A Strange Story in Konda, Konda Densuke, Hanachirusato
@keys2: Konda Village, well, Mora
@version: 2.0
### STANDARD
Hanachirusato, a masked shrine maiden, asks the Traveler to lift a barrier under Konda Village after they free a ward from the Sky Kitsune Statue with Electro. The villagers hint at oddities: floating bags, a broken cart, missing young men, a ghost in thunderstorms and a locked well full of Mora. Densuke at first denies any secret, then admits to a cover-up of young villagers' robbery of Shogunate goods.
### LARGE +
He first tried to pass the Mora pouch off as his own greed, a decoy to end the inquiry, until the Traveler used the cargo bag and the ragged list of goods to show the real story. He asked that the Shogunate not be told, to spare the village. He then pointed the Traveler to a diary he had thrown onto his roof, which led to a designated area where, after defeating the enemies there, the Traveler obtained the Old Key that opens the well.

## entry: Hanachirusato and the Cleansing Ritual
@keys: Hanachirusato, Sacred Sakura Cleansing Ritual, Memento Lens
@keys2: Konda, Sacred Sakura, Kitsune Saiguu
@version: 2.0
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
Hanachirusato is a masked shrine maiden who guides the Traveler through the five barriers of the Sacred Sakura Cleansing Ritual. At the end she reveals that she is filth condensed from the memory of the Kitsune Saiguu, so she must be cleansed too. The Traveler says goodbye and she dissipates. In the Konda quest she says her physical state prevents her from performing the ritual herself.
