## summary
### LITE
Mondstadt City, the "City of Wind" and capital of Mondstadt, stands on a large island in Cider Lake in western Starfell Valley, ringed by a stone wall and reached by a long bridge. Cobbled streets climb past windmills and half-timbered houses to a plaza where a giant statue of the Anemo Archon Barbatos stands before the Favonius Cathedral. The Knights of Favonius govern from their headquarters here; the city is known for its relaxed pace, bards, festivals and taverns.
### STANDARD +
Also called the City of Mondstadt and the "Crown of the North"; Signora sneered at it as the "City of Bards" and Albedo has called it the "City of Pastorals". The god it worships, Venti, wanders its streets unrecognised as a tipsy bard.

## geography
### STANDARD
The city fills a large island in western Cider Lake. A stone wall encloses its lower, eastern end, where the main gate opens onto the bridge; the ground rises through market streets and a fountain plaza to the high ground of the statue, Cathedral and Knights' headquarters.
### LARGE +
Cider Lake is clear, never freezes, and is credited with the fame of Mondstadt's ciders and wines. Besides the main gate there are side and southern gates. Windmills stand within the walls (a windmill keeper, Michelle, tends the northern and western mills). A small park lies south of the Knights' headquarters. Starsnatch Cliff, a quiet lookout where Venti likes to meet the Traveler, is a short walk outside the city in Starfell Valley.

## places
### STANDARD
Anemo Archon Statue and Favonius Cathedral (the Church's seat) on the upper plaza; Knights of Favonius Headquarters with the Mondstadt Library; the taverns Angel's Share (Dawn Winery, owned by Diluc) and The Cat's Tail (Margaret's, with bartender Diona); the Market District with Good Hunter and the Adventurers' Guild post; and the Goth Grand Hotel, taken over by the Fatui delegation.
### LARGE +
Further sites: Floral Whisper (Flora's flower stall), Mondstadt General Goods (Blanche), With Wind Comes Glory (Marjorie), Wagner's smithy (Schulz's Blacksmith) at the south end, and Mona's Laboratory, a sealed house just beyond the smithy that Mona now rents. A door in the lower library of the headquarters leads to the lobby of the Imaginarium Theater.
### ULTRA +
The statue's pedestal reads "The Gateway of Celestia" in Teyvat script. One map description wonders whether a city taught freedom by its own Archon really needed to spend so much on so grand a cathedral. In the Prologue the Traveler slipped into the Cathedral basement at night to take the lyre.

## people
### LARGE
Knights, clergy, merchants and bards fill the streets. Notable residents and regulars include Acting Grand Master Jean, Grand Master Varka (home from Nod-Krai), Captains Kaeya and Albedo, Librarian Lisa, Outrider Amber, the deaconess and singer Barbara, the deacon Dahlia, Klee, Diluc of the Dawn Winery, the astrologist Mona, and Durin, who lives in the city since his rebirth as a human. Everyday faces: Katheryne, receptionist at the Adventurers' Guild, and Cyrus, its branch master; Charles, bartender at Angel's Share; Margaret, owner of The Cat's Tail; Sara, waitress at Good Hunter; the blacksmith Wagner and his apprentice Schulz; Flora the florist; Timaeus, an alchemy scholar; Ella Musk, who studies the hilichurl language in the library; Hertha, a Knights coordinator; Ludwig Goth, the hotelier and landlord; nuns such as Victoria, Jilliana and Grace; and street bards including Six-Fingered José, Helen and Sansa. A Fatui diplomatic staff (Lyudmila, Mikhail and Viktor among them) works openly in the city, with the guard Luke posted outside the Goth Grand Hotel.

## governance
### LARGE
There is no monarch or city council separate from the nation: the Knights of Favonius police, garrison and administer the city from their headquarters, and the Church of Favonius, managed by the Knights, runs the Cathedral. Trials are normally held by either the Knights or the Church; grave cases can require both. The Grand Master's Office in the headquarters is Jean's workplace as Acting Grand Master. The building also holds a ground-floor solitary confinement room, used almost exclusively for Klee. Visitors, not only Mondstadters, may borrow from the library.

## culture
### STANDARD
City life centres on taverns, song and festivals (Windblume, Ludi Harpastum, Weinlesefest), which decorate the streets and statue plaza.
### LARGE +
Amber calls Sticky Honey Roast a traditional Mondstadt delicacy. Gliding is a point of local pride; Amber gives the Traveler a wind glider on their first visit. Wine culture is everywhere. Angel's Share takes its name from brewers' slang for the portion of liquor lost to evaporation and the barrels. It long held a near monopoly on the city's drinking, until Diona's cocktails, which taste good whatever goes into them, made The Cat's Tail a real rival; Angel's Share still sells more. The Cat's Tail is also where Mondstadt plays the card game Genius Invokation TCG. Good Hunter, the most popular restaurant in town, buys meat from Springvale hunters and prides itself on local apples.
### ULTRA +
The library has had several editions of house rules; the seventh keeps only three (be quiet, do not damage books, return them on time) and leaves the penalty for violations unstated because everyone already knows it. Angel's Share does not run tabs, and a job board there is filled almost entirely with Adventurers' Guild recruitment notices.

## history
### STANDARD
About 2,600 years ago, after Decarabian's fall, Barbatos led the people from Old Mondstadt to Cider Lake, where they built the present city. In the early aristocratic era Venerare of the Lawrence Clan raised the Barbatos statue before the Cathedral, inscribed by the clan leaders with a pledge to defend Mondstadt; the late, tyrannical aristocracy pulled it down. After Vennessa's rebellion (about 1,000 years ago) the statue was restored without its original inscription.
### LARGE +
Ragnvindr then turned the aristocrats' indoor bathhouse into the Mondstadt Library. The library grew into the largest in northern Teyvat until the Great Fire of Fall Equinox cut it to one-sixth of its size; it remains the largest in the north, and its restricted section behind the poplar doors in the basement survived untouched. In the Prologue (1.0) the dragon Stormterror attacked the city on the Traveler's first day there; after Dvalin was freed, Signora took Venti's Gnosis outside the Cathedral. In the Windblume Festival of the Paralogism crisis (5.6) monsters born of Durin's remains assaulted the city.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Constant breezes turning windmills, dandelion seeds on the air, bards at the plaza, knights on patrol, and wine-scented evenings in the taverns. The pace is famously unhurried; one shop door even carries a note saying it is closed because friends came for a drink.
### LARGE +
During Stormterror's attacks the streets emptied, though Amber noted that the tavern by the wall did better business than ever.

## dangers
### LARGE
Inside the walls the city is normally safe and well policed. Real threats have come from outside or in disguise: Stormterror's attacks in the Prologue, Fatui intrigue around the diplomatic mission, and, during Paralogism, Durin-born impostors who took the identities of residents before monsters stormed the gates.

## travel
### LARGE
The long bridge from the main gate links the city to the roads of Starfell Valley: toward Windrise and the Dawn Winery to the south and west, Springvale and Windwail Highland beyond, and the northern trade road that the Knights patrol to Dornman Port. Wind currents and gliding from the high ground are a quick way down; the Traveler's first glider lesson was on the city's heights.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the city is at peace. Jean still works as Acting Grand Master from the headquarters, Grand Master Varka is home from the Nod-Krai expedition, and Durin now lives in Mondstadt. The Archon Quest has moved to Snezhnaya, and no new crisis in the city is shown.
### STANDARD +
The Fatui diplomatic mission still occupies the Goth Grand Hotel. Venti no longer holds his Anemo Gnosis.

## entry: Favonius Cathedral and the Anemo Archon Statue
@keys: Favonius Cathedral, Mondstadt Cathedral, Anemo Archon Statue, Barbatos Statue, Gateway of Celestia
### STANDARD
The Gothic Favonius Cathedral, seat of the Church of Favonius, stands at the top of Mondstadt City behind the giant statue of Barbatos, whose hooded, winged figure cups its hands over the plaza. The deaconess Barbara serves here and nuns such as Victoria tend daily worship. The Holy Lyre der Himmel was kept under guard in the Cathedral basement until Venti and the Traveler took it during the Stormterror crisis.
### LARGE +
The statue was first raised by Venerare of the Lawrence Clan to commemorate Barbatos' descent, with a pledge by the clan leaders carved beneath it; the late aristocracy tore it down and it was restored after Vennessa's rebellion without the lost inscription. Its pedestal now reads "The Gateway of Celestia". Signora seized Venti's Gnosis outside the Cathedral after Dvalin was freed, and in Chapter I Act IV the Traveler came here to ask the Church about an Abyss Order plan.

## entry: Knights of Favonius Headquarters and the Mondstadt Library
@keys: Knights of Favonius Headquarters, Mondstadt Library, Knights of Favonius Library, Grand Master's Office, Great Fire of Fall Equinox
### STANDARD
The headquarters on the city's high ground holds the Grand Master's Office (Jean's workplace), Albedo's office as Chief Alchemist, a solitary confinement room used almost only for Klee, and the Mondstadt Library, kept by Lisa. Ragnvindr converted the library from an aristocrats' bathhouse after Vennessa's rebellion; it was the largest library in northern Teyvat until the Great Fire of Fall Equinox reduced it to a sixth of its size, and it is still the largest in the north.
### LARGE +
Its restricted section lies behind poplar doors in the basement and survived the fire. Anyone, Mondstadter or not, may borrow books, and with the proper paperwork a whole set can be checked out for a year or more. Lisa offered to teach Durin to read, and in Durin's story quest unlisted restricted books that seemed to move between shelves led him and the Traveler into Andersdotter's unfinished storybook.

## entry: Angel's Share and The Cat's Tail
@keys: Angel's Share, Cat's Tail, The Cat's Tail
### STANDARD
Mondstadt City's two great taverns. Angel's Share, established and funded by the Dawn Winery and owned by Diluc, stands near the city gate with Charles behind the bar. The Cat's Tail, owned by Margaret, is famed for its cats and for its bartender Diona, whose drinks taste delicious whatever she puts in them, though praising her skill is unwise. Diona's arrival broke Angel's Share's monopoly, and Elzer of the Dawn Winery was bitten when he tried to poach her.
### LARGE +
Angel's Share is named for the brewers' term for liquor lost to evaporation and does not give credit. The Cat's Tail, between the Adventurers' Guild post and Good Hunter, is the city's gathering place for Genius Invokation TCG players.

## entry: Mondstadt Market District
@keys: Mondstadt Market District, Good Hunter, Floral Whisper, Mondstadt General Goods, With Wind Comes Glory, Schulz's Blacksmith
### STANDARD
The lower end of Mondstadt City, inside the gate, is a market of two-storey tan-walled, red-roofed shops: Good Hunter (Sara's restaurant, the most popular eatery in town, with take-out always in demand), Katheryne's Adventurers' Guild post, Flora's Floral Whisper, Blanche's Mondstadt General Goods, Marjorie's With Wind Comes Glory, and Wagner's forge with his apprentice Schulz.
### LARGE +
Good Hunter sources meat from Springvale's hunters and boasts of Mondstadt apples. Mona's Laboratory, a house owned by the hotelier Goth that had been locked behind a mysterious Hydro seal, sits just beyond the smithy; Mona broke the seal and Goth rented it to her at his lowest rate.

## entry: Goth Grand Hotel and the Fatui Delegation
@keys: Goth Grand Hotel, Ludwig Goth, Fatui delegation, Fatui diplomats
@keys2: Mondstadt, Goth, Fatui, Pantalone, Luke
### STANDARD
The Goth Grand Hotel belongs to Ludwig Goth, whose family has long served Mondstadt in hospitality. Before the Traveler arrived, Goth accepted a large sum from the Harbinger Pantalone to rent the whole hotel to the Fatui indefinitely, so a guard, Luke, now turns away everyone else. In the Prologue the Traveler found Jean outside the hotel rebuffing the Fatui diplomat Anastasia, who offered to have the Fatui "put an end" to Stormterror; Jean refused to hear talk of killing one of the Four Winds.
### ULTRA +
Goth regrets being unable to take other guests. He also lets other properties in the city and opposes his son Marvin's betrothal.

## entry: Stormterror over Mondstadt City
@keys: Stormterror attack, Dragon Storm
@keys2: Mondstadt, Dvalin, Stormterror
@version: 1.0
### STANDARD
On the Traveler's first day in the city, Stormterror (Dvalin) descended on Mondstadt City, darkening the sky and whipping up tornadoes around the walls. The Traveler was caught up and drove the dragon off in an aerial fight, kept aloft by a mysterious voice (Venti) commanding the winds, and then met Kaeya on landing. The attacks had already emptied the city's usual crowds. The crisis ended with Dvalin freed from the Abyss Order's hold, after which Signora took Venti's Gnosis outside the Cathedral.

### LARGE
During a Windblume Festival (Interlude Chapter Act IV, "Paralogism") several residents behaved oddly, and Albedo was tried behind closed doors at the headquarters and found guilty of murder. The trial was a trap planned for half a year: creatures born of Durin's lingering life force had devoured and impersonated Mondstadters, including a false Hertha. Exposed, they launched a full assault, storming the main and side gates and fighting through the city centre, the residential streets, Angel's Share, the plaza, the Goth Grand Hotel and the windmill, with Ruin Machines striking at the side gate. Knights, Diluc, Kaeya, the Church, adventurers and residents defended their homes while Venti, feigning helplessness, linked them with long-distance wind currents. When Venti sensed a "child's anger" in the winds nearby, Phonia, missing for over half a year, came forward and Jean had her taken into custody; Albedo assured Jean he could save her. Two days later Venti, as Windblume Star, sang while Albedo transmuted Mini Durin into a human body to take the fallen dragon's place.
