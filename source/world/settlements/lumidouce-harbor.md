## summary
### LITE
Lumidouce Harbor is a coastal elevator and observation deck built into the side of a waterfall in Fontaine's Erinnyes Forest, much like Romaritime Harbor. It is a vital trading port for Fontaine, handling a large volume of international cargo, including tea from Qiaoying Village in Liyue and spices from Sumeru. For some years it has been underfunded and worn down, with its planned expansion and aquabus line stalled.
### STANDARD +
It also serves as a transfer point for illegal goods, and a few underground groups hold influence among the docks.

## geography
### STANDARD
The harbor is a coastal elevator and observation deck built on the side of a waterfall. A run-down tavern, "The Rusty Rudder", stands a short distance northwest of it, and the lower levels and the ships there are where underground trading takes place.
### LARGE +
Because the harbor has neglected its routine hydrological surveys, its maps of the complex underwater terrain are out of date, which makes it harder for navigators to guide ships safely.

## places
### STANDARD
The main deck and elevator are where most harbor staff work. "The Rusty Rudder" tavern is where employees gather after work and where shady dealings are arranged. A noticeboard next to Cassigneul carries staff complaints, trade adverts and notices.
### LARGE +
Below the harbor, around Chaussivert's ship, is where the underground trade is arranged. The follow-up world quest sends the Traveler to Nacker's hideout in the Fort Charybdis ruins.

## people
### STANDARD
Cassigneul is the Lumidouce Harbor General Manager and handles the staff's complaints; she has promised to pay salaries from her own pocket if she has to. Others include the traders and staff Afsoon, Chourei, Crapet, Hilmer, Jalade, Kimpel, Mermet, Xing Cheng and, after An Expected Lie, Chaussivert, Fusilier and Lemarcq.
### LARGE +
Chaussivert is a self-proclaimed "Harbor-Watcher" with a strict personal code; he refuses dirty money and is said to run the largest underground trading operation. His position has weakened since he lost his entire crew except Lemarcq. Mr. Doutey, the former overseer, wanted to expand the port but left, apparently for a posting elsewhere.

## governance
### STANDARD
Day-to-day administration falls to Cassigneul, who took over from Doutey; the Maison Cardinalice is the body whose promised works (expansion, the aquabus line) have not started. The Spina di Rosula watches the balance of power among the underground groups and grew worried when senior members of the Club di Pantaloni Novita crime organisation were all arrested.
### LARGE +
Don Pesci and De Niro families, as well as the former Club di Pantaloni Novita, are named as underground factions that hold or held sway at the harbor.

## history
### STANDARD
Until a few years before 4.2, Mr. Doutey oversaw the harbor and planned to expand it for ever-increasing trade. A fourth aquabus line connecting it to the Court of Fontaine was also designed, but was suspended after the death of Callas, its chief financial backer. Around then an unnamed event (probably related) led many employees, most of the navigators and Doutey himself to leave, and Cassigneul was assigned to replace him.
### LARGE +
In the roughly three years since, the harbor has been in crisis, mostly for lack of investment. The share of foreign ships has risen, hinting at a trade imbalance, and cargo is increasing while infrastructure is not. Neither the expansion nor the revival of the line has progressed, despite the Maison Cardinalice's assurances of an imminent start to work.
### ULTRA +
Engineers are idle and discontented, and some seek jobs elsewhere, for example with Hautemontagne at Yilong Wharf. Navigators are overworked and underpaid, and a posting here is called "practically a black hole for career progression." Staff also complain of a lack of nearby recreation and shops.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Working at the harbor is currently very safe and uneventful, perhaps because there are so few jobs, and the busy trade of a major port sits uneasily beside idle engineers and unfinished plans. The in-game viewpoint is titled "A Distant Harbor"; the sound of the waterfall surrounds the elevators and decks.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Chiefly smuggling and crooked deals rather than open fighting. The harbor has been a key transfer point for illicit goods, such as imitation Sinthe. According to Chaussivert, bribery, fraud and betrayal have declined recently.

## travel
### STANDARD
The harbor lies in Erinnyes Forest. No aquabus runs there yet: the Lumidouce Harbor line is planned but unbuilt. Tea from Liyue's Qiaoying Village and spices from Sumeru pass through it.

## quests
### LARGE
The world quests An Expected Plan and An Expected Lie (both 4.2) begin here and follow the Traveler and Chaussivert's crew in tracing a suspicious shipment and the missing researcher Nacker; they continue the Fontaine Research Institute story. Several quests and noticeboard threads touch on the harbor's trade and smuggling.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 Lumidouce Harbor is still a stagnant but working port. No later canon changes its state.
### STANDARD +
Its expansion and aquabus line remain stalled, and staff are still short. Cassigneul keeps the harbor running on promises and her own pocket.

## entry: Chaussivert and the Harbor-Watcher's Crew
@keys: Chaussivert, Harbor-Watcher, Lemarcq, Fusilier
@keys2: Lumidouce Harbor, Fontaine
@version: 4.2
### STANDARD
Chaussivert is the self-styled "Harbor-Watcher" of Lumidouce Harbor, a man of strict honesty who refuses dirty money. In An Expected Plan the Traveler meets him while chasing Nacker, who nearly blew up the Fontaine Research Institute; Chaussivert had been asked to smuggle someone out of Fontaine and refused. After the Traveler beats him in a scuffle, he helps track the shipment with his crew. He later loses his crew, apart from Lemarcq.
### LARGE +
The quest chain ends with the capture of Nacker and the discovery that the researcher Edwin Eastinghouse, believed dead, was still alive. Chaussivert and Lemarcq are compensated by the Institute and return to the harbor.
