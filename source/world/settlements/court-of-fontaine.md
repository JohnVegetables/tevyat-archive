## summary
### LITE
The Court of Fontaine is the capital of Fontaine, a Belle Epoque-style city of fountains, cobbled avenues, boutiques, cafes and theatres standing high above the surrounding waters. It was once the court of the God of Justice; since the events of the Archon Quest "Masquerade of the Guilty" (4.2) it has been under Chief Justice Neuvillette, who runs the nation from the Palais Mermonia at the city's summit. The Steambird, a newspaper read across Teyvat, is based here, and the city is nicknamed the "City of the Arts".
### STANDARD +
Official text describes it as a city that "alone stands above all waters", whose people live lives as orderly and predictable as the aquabus timetable under the Palais' gaze. Beneath the elegant streets lies Fleuve Cendre, an undercity in the old sewers that the Court long left to its own devices.

## geography
### STANDARD
The city sits in the Court of Fontaine Region on raised ground above the lake, ringed by water. Its quarters: the Palais Mermonia at the highest point; the Court Region Waterway Hub in the centre, holding the aquabus Main Station; Quartier Lyonnais, a street of shops on the northeastern side; Quartier Narbonnais on the southwestern side; Vasari Passage, shop-lined streets at the bottom of the city leading to a large fountain; and Fleuve Cendre in the sewers below. The Opera Epiclese stands offshore on the island of Erinnyes.
### LARGE +
Mont Automnequi lies between the Court and Romaritime Harbor. Tour guide Elphane says a submerged old city of the Court lies in the nearby waters, its convoluted underwater ruins open to well-equipped explorers.

## places
### STANDARD
- **Palais Mermonia**: the administrative heart, seat of the four Maisons and the Marechaussee Phantom, holding Neuvillette's office and Furina's former residence.
- **Court Region Waterway Hub**: the aquabus Main Station (Clementine, Navia and suspended Callas Lines); its top floor connects to the Palais.
- **Vasari Passage**: the main shopping streets and Statue Plaza, with Cafe Lutece, Damoville Purveyor (general goods), Beaumont Workshop (the blacksmith) and the Hotel Bouffes d'ete.
- **Quartier Lyonnais**: the Adventurers' Guild branch (Katheryne), Chioriya Boutique, clockmakers and Bertin's House of Curiosities.
- **Quartier Narbonnais**: The Steambird's offices, the restaurant Hotel Debord, the Salon Caritat, and the latest fashion and perfume.
- **Fleuve Cendre**: the undercity of outcasts, under the Spina di Rosula's de facto rule.
### LARGE +
The city contains a Statue of the Seven; it and Snezhnograd are the only main cities that do. Cafe Lutece, the most popular cafe in Vasari Passage, invites patrons to discuss any topic except death. Beaumont Workshop, the most renowned forge in the city, uses fully automated forging machines instead of hammer and anvil. Leschots Clockwork Workshop (Livre, maker of clockwork toys, with Freminet commissioned for designs) and Lepaute Horlogerie (his brother Caron, a clockmaker) are rival shops. Bertin's House of Curiosities is technically a fashion workshop whose owner collects curios for inspiration. Chiori, a designer from Inazuma, runs Chioriya Boutique.
### ULTRA +
The Salon Caritat is an advocacy society that holds symposia on law, with recent topics such as the Maison Gardiennage's budget overruns and environmental pollution. A small rented rehearsal space in Vasari Passage was used by an amateur troupe staging the musical "The Little Oceanid". The Hotel Bouffes d'ete, a luxury hotel legally owned by the Harbinger Arlecchino, is the House of the Hearth's base in Fontaine, where Lyney, Lynette and Freminet live.

## governance
### STANDARD
All of Fontaine's administration is handled at the Palais Mermonia, which Fontainians regard as essentially the highest authority in the land. It houses the Maison Ordalie (judiciary), Maison Gestion (documents and administration), Maison Cardinalice (finance) and Maison Gardiennage (law enforcement, with the Gardes and Gardemeks), plus the Melusine-staffed Marechaussee Phantom. Trials are held at the Opera Epiclese. Since Furina abdicated, Neuvillette handles the affairs that used to belong to the Archon's title, though not the title itself.
### LARGE +
Security is tight: a person with a criminal record still flagged "Fiche S" by the Maison Gardiennage is placed under surveillance from the moment they arrive in the city, according to the aquabus guide. The Court has always known about Fleuve Cendre but historically treated it as a grey area outside its jurisdiction; today the Spina di Rosula mediates between the undercity and the Court. The Fortress of Meropide is likewise legally outside the Court's jurisdiction.
### ULTRA +
Sedene, a Melusine officer of the Marechaussee Phantom, keeps the front desk at the Palais. About four hundred years ago, after Neuvillette brought the Melusines into the city, distrust of them led to the framing and death of the Melusine Carole and to Vautrin's vengeance; four centuries later, when a Melusine named Kiara was threatened, she was kept inside the Palais Mermonia for her safety while Neuvillette investigated personally. The Court once banned unauthorised possession of certain heavy construction tools, apparently because Fleuve Cendre's residents used them as weapons.

## people
### LARGE
Fashionable citizens, newsies and Kamera-carrying reporters, Gardes on patrol, Gestion clerks, and Melusines working at the Palais and on the aquabuses. Notable figures who live or work here: Neuvillette; Furina, now an ordinary actress; Charlotte of The Steambird; Chevreuse, captain of the Special Security and Surveillance Patrol; Clorinde, the Champion Duelist; Navia and the Spina di Rosula; Chiori; and the Hearth's Lyney, Lynette and Freminet.
### ULTRA +
Fleuve Cendre houses ex-convicts from Meropide seeking a fresh start, the destitute and outlaws; Chevreuse and Freminet grew up there, as did the historical Champion Duelist Marfisa. Mona writes an astrology column for The Steambird. Escoffier was head chef of the Hotel Debord before her conviction. The Court's women are reputedly beautiful, and legend says the ladies of the Court wept when the harpist Sojourner left without a farewell.

## culture
### LARGE
The Court is Fontaine's centre of art, fashion, cuisine and spectacle: theatre and trials at the Opera Epiclese, magic shows, cafes where people argue philosophy, boutiques and perfume, and an insatiable appetite for The Steambird's news and gossip. Hotel Debord's owner believes "art is the best seasoning for good food" and invites artists to perform while guests dine.
### ULTRA +
The Steambird runs a "Tales of Humanity" column for readers' submissions and commissions. City life also has a bureaucratic, litigious streak: one dishonest merchant once tried to trademark Neuvillette's name with two prominent lawyers, and the whole city was in uproar (as the aquabus guide tells it).

## history
### STANDARD
The Court served for centuries as the court of the Hydro Archon, the God of Justice: Egeria, then for five hundred years Focalors, whose public face was Furina. About four hundred years ago Neuvillette became Iudex and brought the Melusines into city life. In 4.2 Furina was put on trial at the Opera Epiclese; the truth of the prophecy came out, Focalors was executed by the Oratrice, the prophesied flood swept Fontaine without dissolving anyone, and the Court passed to Neuvillette's administration.
### LARGE +
Hundreds of years ago the Court turned the city's sewers into an undercity for society's "shadows" and for labourers displaced as technology advanced, then left it largely unmanaged. Before the cataclysm, the outlaw Eduardo Baker ruled Fleuve Cendre with a protection racket until the Maison Gardiennage raided it in the "purge of Fleuve Cendre" and sentenced him to exile in the Great Red Sand; his henchmen freed him on the way, and he and his allies occupied Poisson. Centuries ago, by a tour guide's account, the Palais hired half the workers in the Court to build the Opera Epiclese, whose surplus materials piled into a small hill on the shore. The aquabus network was later built with Callas of the Spina di Rosula's funding.
### ULTRA +
Hotel Debord was founded by two chefs from Fleuve Cendre, Modeste and Savelli; its legendary Gateau Debord relied on a secret Remurian ingredient that destroyed Modeste's sense of taste and drove him to suicide, and Savelli took the blame to protect his name. The restaurant fell into disrepute and took decades to recover. In the recent past, under head chef Escoffier, a lumiline incident (secretly caused by another chef) ended with her taking responsibility and serving time in the Fortress of Meropide.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Fountains everywhere, aquabuses gliding along raised water rails, the smell of fresh coffee at the station, newsies calling the latest headline, Kamera flashes, and shop windows full of clockwork and couture. Elegance above, damp and danger below: Fleuve Cendre is dirty, poorly ventilated and short on sunlight, and most residents there carry a weapon openly.

## dangers
### LARGE
The surface city is orderly and heavily policed; its dangers are social (scandal, lawsuits, swindlers, being dragged into a trial) rather than monstrous. Fleuve Cendre is rougher: theft is routine and violence common, although under the Spina di Rosula armed conflict is punished severely and illegal funding is harder to obtain.

## travel
### LARGE
Most visitors arrive by aquabus: the Clementine Line from Romaritime Harbor, or the Navia Line from Marcotte Station on Erinnyes. Melusine guides narrate the trips (Aeval on the Clementine Line, Elphane on the Navia Line). Fleuve Cendre is reached by two entrances in the city, one beside Cafe Lutece and one across the street from Damoville Purveyor. The Waterway Hub's lift also stops at a floor connected to the Palais Mermonia.
### ULTRA +
The Callas Line, once linking the Court to the Fontaine Research Institute, has been suspended since the explosion that levelled the old Institute; after foiling Romeuf's plot against the Clementine Line, Navia decided to rebuild it and add stops to the other lines.

## quests
### LARGE
The city is the hub of Archon Quest Chapter IV (4.0-4.2), from the Traveler's arrival and Lyney's trial through Furina's trial in "Masquerade of the Guilty". Many story, world and event quests are set here, including Navia's story quest ("Where the Roses Bloom"), Chiori's ("Stained Spots", "The Woven and the Cut") and the Fontinalia film festival events ("Roses and Muskets", 4.3; "Sunny Summer Fontinalia", 6.7).

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the Court of Fontaine is at peace. Fontaine has no Hydro Archon; Chief Justice Neuvillette administers the nation from the Palais Mermonia and judges trials himself now that the Oratrice no longer works. Furina has left the Palais and lives in the city as an ordinary human and actress.
### STANDARD +
Fontainians, made "true humans" by Neuvillette in 4.2, no longer dissolve in Primordial Seawater. The House of the Hearth still keeps its hotel here. The Archon Quest has since moved on to Snezhnaya (Chapter VII, 7.0-7.1).

## entry: Palais Mermonia
@keys: Palais Mermonia
@keys2: Court of Fontaine, Neuvillette, Maison, Iudex, Sedene
@version: 4.0
### STANDARD
The Palais Mermonia stands at the highest point of the Court of Fontaine and is where all of Fontaine's administrative affairs are handled; to Fontainians it is effectively the highest authority in the land. The Maison Gardiennage, Maison Gestion, Maison Cardinalice, Maison Ordalie and Marechaussee Phantom all work from here, and it holds Iudex Neuvillette's office and Furina's former residence. Even in the Archon's day, the real work was done by human and Melusine officials here.
### LARGE +
In "Masquerade of the Guilty" the Traveler visited the Palais repeatedly, overhearing Neuvillette and Furina argue over the Poisson victims and the prophecy before Neuvillette concluded that only a trial would make her talk.

## entry: Fleuve Cendre
@keys: Fleuve Cendre, Ashen River
@version: 4.0
### STANDARD
Fleuve Cendre ("Ashen River") is a settlement in the sewers beneath the Court of Fontaine, home to ex-convicts, outlaws, orphans and the poor. The Spina di Rosula is its de facto government: it mediates disputes, handles relations with the Court, gives aid and jobs, and punishes armed conflict harshly. Life there is damp, cramped and dangerous, and most residents openly carry weapons. Many Spina members live here, and it serves as the Spina's secondary base (its main base is Poisson).
### LARGE +
The Court created the undercity centuries ago for society's "shadows" and long treated it as a lawless grey area. Before the cataclysm the outlaw Eduardo Baker ran a protection racket there with a bronze pipe, until the Maison Gardiennage's "purge of Fleuve Cendre" sent him toward exile. The Spina funds residents' futures on its own terms; it sponsored Areta's medical studies at the Akademiya on condition she return to work as the undercity's doctor. Chevreuse and Freminet grew up here.

## entry: Vasari Passage
@keys: Vasari Passage, Cafe Lutece, Café Lutece, Statue Plaza
@version: 4.0
### STANDARD
Vasari Passage, at the bottom of the Court of Fontaine, is a set of shop-lined streets leading to a large fountain and the Statue Plaza. It holds Cafe Lutece (coffee, snacks and open philosophical debate on any subject except death), Damoville Purveyor, the automated forge Beaumont Workshop and the Hotel Bouffes d'ete. Gardes, Gestion clerks, newsies and photographers frequent it.

## entry: Quartier Lyonnais and Quartier Narbonnais
@keys: Quartier Lyonnais, Quartier Narbonnais
@version: 4.0
### STANDARD
Quartier Lyonnais, on the city's northeastern side, is a street of shops with the Fontaine branch of the Adventurers' Guild, Chioriya Boutique, Leschots Clockwork Workshop, Lepaute Horlogerie and Bertin's House of Curiosities. Quartier Narbonnais, on the southwestern side, is the place for the newest fashion and perfume and is home to The Steambird's offices (chief editor Euphrasie), the Hotel Debord and the Salon Caritat.

## entry: Aquabus and the Court Region Waterway Hub
@keys: aquabus, Waterway Hub, Clementine Line, Navia Line, Callas Line
### STANDARD
Aquabuses run on raised aquarails from the Main Station in the Court Region Waterway Hub. The Clementine Line goes to Romaritime Harbor, the Navia Line to Marcotte Station on Erinnyes (for the Opera Epiclese), and the Callas Line to the Fontaine Research Institute, suspended "due to force majeure" since the Institute's explosion. Callas of the Spina di Rosula funded the system to improve relations between Poisson and the Court, splitting the Spina when Romeuf's faction objected; the lines bear the names of Callas, his late wife Clementine and their daughter Navia.
### LARGE +
Before 4.2, the guides credited Indemnitium, energy that a Palais report linked to trials, with powering the aquabuses and the city. After the prophecy was resolved, Romeuf's faction plotted to collapse the Clementine Line's supports onto Poisson; Navia and the Traveler foiled it, and Navia then resolved to rebuild the Callas Line and add stops to the other lines.

## entry: Hotel Debord
@keys: Hotel Debord, Gateau Debord, Codex Debord
@version: 4.0
### STANDARD
Hotel Debord is a famous restaurant in Quartier Narbonnais whose owner, Vaneigem, holds that art is the best seasoning and invites performers to entertain diners; its interior is designed so every seat can see the stage. It was founded by Modeste and Savelli, two chefs from Fleuve Cendre, and its recipes are kept in the Codex Debord, reserved for its head chefs. Escoffier was head chef until a food-safety case sent her to the Fortress of Meropide; Savanna kept the kitchen together afterwards.

## entry: Furina's Trial and the Flood
@keys: Masquerade of the Guilty, Furina's trial, trial of the Hydro Archon
@keys2: Court of Fontaine, Opera Epiclese, Palais Mermonia
@version: 4.2
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
In 4.2, after Primordial Seawater erupted at Poisson, Neuvillette, the Traveler and allies lured Furina into a staged trial at the Opera Epiclese. The trial exposed that she was not a god, the Oratrice condemned "the Hydro Archon" to death, the All-Devouring Narwhal burst into the opera house, and Focalors, the real Archon hidden in the Oratrice, accepted the execution so that Neuvillette regained the Hydro Dragon's authority and absolved Fontaine's people. When the prophesied flood came, no one dissolved, and Wriothesley's ship the Wingalet rescued people caught in the water. Afterwards The Steambird printed free copies to calm the city, and Furina left the Palais and the Opera.
### ULTRA +
In the days before the trial, Paimon asked the Court's people what they would do if the flood came: Sedene, at Hotel Debord, said she would keep her routine, and Charlotte said she would stay and keep reporting. A small riot broke out at the Opera Epiclese when audience members accused Furina of ignoring the "prophecy crisis".

## entry: Fontinalia Film Festival in the Court
@keys: Fontinalia Film Festival, Furina Award
@keys2: Court of Fontaine, Fontinalia, Furina, Charlotte
@availability: limited
@version: 4.3
### LARGE
The Fontinalia Festival honours the Lochknights' search for the Oceanids; Furina turned it into a carnival where children shout "Trial or treat!" for candy. During the 4.3 event "Roses and Muskets" the Fontaine Film Association added the Fontinalia Film Festival, whose top prize is the "Furina Award", and The Two Musketeers (produced by Xavier, directed by Furina) won the first one. The name was never changed after her abdication. At a later festival (Sunny Summer Fontinalia, 6.7) the award went to "Great Detective Hurlock", directed by Lepine-Pauline and starring Charlotte, over Furina's "A Homecoming", starring Columbina; new awards for directing, costume design and Kamera work were added.
