## summary
### LITE
Cliffwatch Camp is a Lightkeepers' outpost on Ashveil Peak in northern Nod-Krai, built by the Nightmare Orioles investigation squad to keep watch on the Abyssal activity at nearby Kipumaki Cliff. It has a lighthouse, a fortified perimeter, Moonwagons, a Statue of the New Moon at its entrance and a small cemetery to the south. It is a working post, busy and short of hands.
### STANDARD +
Illuga's squad runs it. His men are overworked, forever short of kuuvahki-refined fuel and supplies, and the camp feels like a place where people are trying very hard not to let the dark in.

## geography
### STANDARD
The camp stands on Ashveil Peak in the cold north of Nod-Krai, near Kipumaki Cliff, a cliff perpetually shrouded in dark cloud. Nearby plants are rich in kuuvahki, which helps the camp's machines for a while but cannot replace the special refined oil needed for a lighthouse.
### LARGE +
The Pillar of Embla lies in the same northern country. Supplies come in from Piramida on Moonwagons.

## places
### STANDARD
The entrance with its Statue of the New Moon; a command post where reports and base data are kept; a Moonwagon parked near the lighthouse, which doubles as the meeting point; fortifications; and a cemetery just south of the camp.
### LARGE +
The graves read simply 'Here lies Lightkeeper' followed by a name: Veyde, Olsson, Chernov, Filaev, Pieris Asebov, Shonokovych and, after the 6.3 victory, Bjorn. A final tomb, Patolichev's, is empty for now; its inscription is a mock epitaph, written as if a century from now, for the living Ratnik Patolichev, praising his service with the Frostgale Steeds.
### ULTRA +
Olsson and Asebov were squad leaders; Olsson led the Nightmare Orioles before Illuga.

## people
### STANDARD
Squad Leader Illuga is in charge. The camp's known members are Anleifr, Egle, Rollon, Valdis and Vlaicu, all of the Nightmare Orioles; after the world quests several of them become open-world NPCs there.
### LARGE +
Rollon compiles data on the bases the Lightkeepers still hold. Vlaicu relays messages about clashes with the Fatui in the east. Valdis handles transport and likes to study insects and plants; he was trapped by mandragoras protecting him when he followed them off the road. Anleifr and Egle press Illuga about fuel.

## governance
### STANDARD
Illuga reports to Starshyna Nikita in Piramida but shoulders the groundwork himself, so that Nikita is not worried by every detail. He is strict about orders but fair, and kept Valdis in a barrier until he had explained himself.
### LARGE +
Illuga lets the Traveler and Paimon join the investigation squad as honorary members during the quest, and no longer treats them as outsiders.

## culture
### STANDARD
Food and rest matter as much as battle. At Paimon's suggestion a food truck was set up at the camp; Illuga says filling stomachs is part of a Ratnik's work. The cemetery reflects the Lightkeepers' custom of honouring the dead; Illuga talks to Bjorn's grave and reports what has happened in the city.

## history
### STANDARD
Kipumaki Cliff became a hotspot of Wild Hunt activity after the Torchforger, Aila and Alia sealed the Scourge of Endless Decay beneath it about 500 years ago, and the Lightkeepers built the camp to watch it. Three years ago a surge of Wild Hunt activity there overran the Nightmare Orioles; only Illuga and a badly injured Ivar survived, and the camp was rebuilt afterwards.
### LARGE +
In The Moonlit Watcher the camp is made into a permanent base, with fortifications and a lighthouse stocked with half a month of fuel, so that it can hold out in a major assault until reinforcements come. Later, in the 6.3 finale, the Lightkeepers launched the Arrow of Terpikeraunas from Piramida and destroyed the Scourge and its Cataclysm Egg.

## dangers
### STANDARD
The Wild Hunt, which grows stronger around Kipumaki Cliff, and Abyssal Decay seeping from the cliff. Mandragoras in the area react to danger before humans do. In the east the Lightkeepers have clashed with Fatui.
### LARGE +
Supply lines are the camp's weak point: a supply transport went missing in the quest, and a lighthouse needs a special oil refined with kuuvahki.

## travel
### STANDARD
Moonwagons run between Piramida and the camp. It is the base for watching Kipumaki Cliff.

## quests
### STANDARD
The Moonlit Watcher and The Moonlit Watcher (II), world quests in the Nightingale's Song series, centre on the camp. The first finds missing supplies and rescues Valdis, which unlocks the meeting point; the second has the Traveler visit Illuga's home in Piramida. Bjorn's grave becomes available after The Last Night, the First Light.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Cold wind, the glow of the lighthouse on dark cloud, Ratniki at their posts and a smell of stew. Quiet moments are rare and treasured.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 Cliffwatch Camp is a fortified, functioning Lightkeepers outpost; its original threat has been defeated.
### STANDARD +
The Scourge of Endless Decay was destroyed in 6.3, the Piramida bulletin board celebrates the Nightmare Orioles' victory, and Bjorn's grave has been added to the cemetery. Wild Hunt dangers continue in the north. No 7.0-7.1 content changes the camp.

## entry: The Moonlit Watcher
@keys: Moonwagon, Moonwagons, Valdis
@keys2: Cliffwatch Camp, Illuga, Lightkeepers
@version: 6.3
### STANDARD
Moonwagons are Lightkeeper vehicles modified from Snezhnayan mechanical vehicles and powered by kuuvahki, described by Illuga as mobile lighthouses. Valdis, in charge of transport, was trapped by a ring of mandragoras after following them off the road to study them, delaying supplies for the camp's lighthouse. The Traveler helps rescue him, and the Wild Hunt monsters that the mandragoras had sensed attack.
### LARGE +
Once the supplies arrive, Illuga finishes the camp: fortifications are complete, the lighthouse fuelled, and Illuga takes the Traveler to his home in Piramida.
