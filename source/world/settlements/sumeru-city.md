## summary
### LITE
Sumeru City is the capital of Sumeru and is known across Teyvat as the "City of Wisdom". It is built on the Divine Tree, a colossal tree in Avidya Forest, and falls under the Sumeru Akademiya, the nation's great school and government. Ordinary life, markets and taverns sit near the foot of the tree, the Akademiya occupies its middle, and at the very top rests the Sanctuary of Surasthana, the palace of the Dendro Archon Lesser Lord Kusanali (Nahida).
### STANDARD +
Height in the city doubles as social rank: the lower levels are for the common folk, and promotion or higher study is spoken of as "moving up". It is where the central events of Sumeru's Archon Quests unfolded: the Akademiya's god-making plot and, much later, the Irminsul crisis.

## geography
### STANDARD
The city is built on the Divine Tree, which carries the Akademiya and everything below it. The Grand Bazaar and everyday buildings stand at the base, the Akademiya in the middle, the Sanctuary of Surasthana at the top. The city lies in Avidya Forest, within Dharma Forest, and a river runs from it to Port Ormos.
### LARGE +
Saying "down below" in the city is understood to mean the bottom of the tree. Besides the tree itself, the city has outlying districts: southern outskirts with farms and a docks area, and a northern area with farmland and the Bimarstan (the city's medical institution). The Grand Bazaar has three entrances. Students of the Amurta Darshan such as Yasmin monitor and record the condition of the Divine Tree's roots as a precaution.

## places
### STANDARD
Grand Bazaar: a lively, artistic market, home to the Zubayr Theater (Nilou's troupe). Treasures Street: the liveliest street, with the Puspa Cafe, the Adventurers' Guild branch and the Citadel of Regzar of the Corps of Thirty. The Akademiya: the main building of the Sumeru Akademiya, with the House of Daena library at its heart. Sanctuary of Surasthana: the palace at the top. Lambad's Tavern: Sumeru's main restaurant, run by Lambad. Bimarstan: the city's medical institution.
### LARGE +
Lambad claims to be a legendary sailor; the Adventurers' Guild branch is run by Kamal with Katheryne at reception. The Akademiya's doors open automatically on approach, and it also holds the Razan Garden. In the story the Sanctuary's interior only opens up after the quest Jnagarbha Day. Kaveh redesigned the booth seating on the second floor of Lambad's Tavern. Notices are carried on public boards such as the Akademiya public message board, the Diwan, Kalimi Exchange and Mahamata boards, the Puspa Cafe board and the Zubayr Theater billboard.

## people
### STANDARD
Residents include Akademiya students and researchers, the Matra under General Mahamatra Cyno, Corps of Thirty mercenaries, merchants, farmers, dockworkers and performers. Nahida lives at the Sanctuary, and Naphis leads the Akademiya as Grand Sage as of 7.1. Kaveh (an architect), Alhaitham (a scribe) and Nilou (a dancer at the Zubayr Theater) live and work in the city; the Forest Rangers Tighnari and Collei visit often.
### LARGE +
The Grand Bazaar's inhabitants are described as mostly ordinary people seeking a steady life. Local mercenary groups include the Corps of Thirty (whose Parchamdar, Rukh Shah, is stationed at the Citadel of Regzar) and Eremite mercenaries who take jobs in the city.

## governance
### STANDARD
The Akademiya runs the city and nation day to day. Six Darshans (schools: Haravatat, Vahumana, Kshahrewar, Amurta, Rtawahist and Spantamad) are each led by a sage, and the Grand Sage leads them all. The Matra enforce order. Its rules rest on three virtues (Admonition, Ingenuity, Praxis) and six banned fields of research; breaking them can bring expulsion or imprisonment.
### LARGE +
The Akasha System, a knowledge network used through terminals, ran through the city, though it is not part of daily life now. The old sages, led by the Grand Sage Azar, scorned the arts as useless, restricted public performance and kept Nahida confined in the Sanctuary for five hundred years. After the Archon Quest's third chapter, Azar and the other sages who sided with the plot were removed and Naphis and Baladhuri were released from imprisonment; Alhaitham served briefly as Acting Grand Sage. As of 7.1 the sages are Naphis (Amurta, Grand Sage), Baladhuri (Vahumana), Alkindi (Spantamad), Firnas (Kshahrewar), Shakir (Rtawahist) and Rushd (Haravatat). Naphis and Nahida work together.

## culture
### STANDARD
Learning is the city's pride and its pressure: a degree is notoriously long and stressful to earn, and theses are the hardest hurdle. Under the old sages the arts were marginalised; once Nahida took her place, performance, books and music returned.
### LARGE +
Stressed students often escape to Port Ormos. The Sabzeruz Festival honours the Dendro Archon, with the Zubayr Theater as a stage, though talking about art can still be a conversation-killer. Padisarah flowers are a local specialty of Sumeru City. The tree's physical hierarchy is mirrored in a social one: the lower levels belong to the common folk, and advancement means climbing.

## history
### STANDARD
Greater Lord Rukkhadevata's rainforest civilisation built the city on the Divine Tree. Five hundred years ago forbidden knowledge corrupted Irminsul and Rukkhadevata, and Lesser Lord Kusanali, her successor, was kept by the sages in the Sanctuary. In Chapter III the sages and the Fatui Harbinger Dottore built an artificial god; Nahida was freed and Rukkhadevata was erased from Irminsul at her own request.
### LARGE +
By the wiki's reading, Rukkhadevata made the rainforest after parting from King Deshret, her followers (who would found the Amurta Darshan) helped, and the sages assisted her in running the nation. The sages' plot also trapped the people in a dream loop. Recent history (6.6): after the Nod-Krai crisis, Collei dreamed, through Nicole, that the identities of everyone in Sumeru City had been switched. Nahida and the new sages evacuated the whole rainforest's population to Caravan Ribat. Civilians who tried to return were afflicted by an anomaly near the Yasna Monument, and some killed one another. A surviving Dottore, backed by Pantalone, attacked Irminsul from inside, and a massive Irminsul tree emerged in Sumeru City. The Traveler, the Wanderer, Paimon and Tighnari climbed the Divine Tree towards the Sanctuary while Cyno, Dehya, Candace and others fought into the city as a distraction. The allies overloaded Irminsul with the Akasha, and Nahida then burned it with the Pyro Gnosis over seven days and nights.
### ULTRA +
The wiki's Sumeru history adds other threads touching the Akademiya: Zandik (later the Fatui Harbinger Il Dottore) studied there more than 400 years ago before Pierro recruited him; Lisa, counted its best student in 200 years, graduated in two years and returned to Mondstadt; the Temple of Silence left the rainforest more than 400 years ago, after which the Akademiya created a front organisation to handle information unfit for public release.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
A city of terraces and walkways climbing a great tree: dappled green light, spiced air from Treasures Street, hawkers and students arguing over theses, and the quiet gravity of the Akademiya above. Down below, the Grand Bazaar is warm and noisy; in the Akademiya's halls people speak softly about citations and funding; at the very top the Sanctuary stands in near-silence. After the Irminsul crisis there is a sense of recovery and relief.

## dangers
### LARGE
Inside the city proper, danger is mostly social and institutional: academic misconduct, banned fields of research, and the Akademiya's old habit of imprisoning dissenters. The Matra and the Corps of Thirty watch for crime. In the story the Fatui have operated in the city, through the Akademiya's conspiracy and again during the Irminsul crisis. Outside, the rainforest has the Withering and wild fungi.

## travel
### LARGE
A river runs from the city to Port Ormos, the nation's largest port and a trade and travel hub. Gandharva Ville, the Forest Rangers' base, lies in Avidya Forest. During the Irminsul crisis the population was evacuated to Caravan Ribat.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 Sumeru City is inhabited again after the evacuation of the Irminsul crisis (6.6). Nahida lives in the Sanctuary of Surasthana, free; Naphis leads the Akademiya as Grand Sage.
### STANDARD +
Irminsul no longer exists in its old form: it was burned and its storage function was taken over by the reconfigured Aaru, with no lasting damage reported. Dottore died in the burning. The Pyro Gnosis that Nahida used then disappeared without trace. A letter from Pulcinella of the Fatui apologised for the trouble Dottore caused and said relief supplies had been sent; Nahida and Naphis agreed to keep watch on what Snezhnaya does. No later crisis in the city is shown through 7.1.

## entry: Divine Tree
@keys: Divine Tree
@keys2: Sumeru City, Sumeru
@version: 3.0
### STANDARD
The Divine Tree is the tree on which Sumeru City is built; it holds up the Akademiya. A student named Yasmin jokes that its years exceed all the rice grains in all the pilaf of Port Ormos. Amurta students record the condition of its roots as a preventative measure. The bottom is the common area with the Grand Bazaar, the middle the Akademiya, and the top the Sanctuary of Surasthana.

## entry: Sanctuary of Surasthana
@keys: Sanctuary of Surasthana, Surasthana
@version: 3.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The treetop palace above the Akademiya is the home of the Dendro Archon, Lesser Lord Kusanali. For about five hundred years it was also her prison, as the sages kept her confined within; no one but the sages saw her for centuries. A map description updated after the Archon Quest says that for centuries the light of wisdom shone out from here to illuminate Sumeru. Surasthana is Sanskrit-derived and can mean a temple or residence of a god.
### LARGE +
During the Irminsul crisis Nahida invited the Traveler to visit her in the Sanctuary, where a guard, Mu'tasim, was high-strung because of her very vulnerable state. In the final infiltration the Traveler's party climbed the Divine Tree's branches up to it. After the crisis the Traveler met Nahida and the Wanderer there to talk it over.

## entry: The Akademiya and House of Daena
@keys: Akademiya building, House of Daena, Razan Garden
@version: 3.0
### STANDARD
The Akademiya is the main building of the Sumeru Akademiya, built into the Divine Tree; its library, the House of Daena, lies at its heart, and the Grand Sage's office is above it. Students and researchers of every Darshan study here, and the Matra keep order.

## entry: Grand Bazaar and Zubayr Theater
@keys: Grand Bazaar, Zubayr Theater, Sheikh Zubayr
@version: 3.0
### STANDARD
The Grand Bazaar is the lively, artistic heart of everyday Sumeru City, with three entrances and many stalls of handmade goods. The Zubayr Theater, within it, is managed by Sheikh Zubayr and associated with Nilou's troupe.

## entry: Treasures Street and Puspa Cafe
@keys: Treasures Street, Puspa Cafe, Citadel of Regzar
@version: 3.0
### STANDARD
Treasures Street is the liveliest street in Sumeru City. It holds the Puspa Cafe, shops, the local Adventurers' Guild branch (Branch Master Kamal, receptionist Katheryne) and the Citadel of Regzar, where the Corps of Thirty are based under their Parchamdar, Rukh Shah. Padisarah can be gathered there.

## entry: Lambad's Tavern
@keys: Lambad's Tavern, Lambad
@version: 3.0
### STANDARD
Sumeru's main restaurant, near Treasures Street. Its owner, Lambad, claims to be a legendary sailor who sailed far and wide; it is hard to tell how much he exaggerates, but the food at least supports the claim to know many cuisines. Kaveh redesigned the booth seating on the second floor as thanks to Lambad, who gave him a seat and free drinks when he was nearly penniless; he spent over a fortnight drinking there. The Traveler and Paimon ate there after the Irminsul crisis.

## entry: The Irminsul Crisis in Sumeru City
@keys: Irminsul crisis, Irminsul tree, burning of Irminsul, As All Falls to Emptiness
@keys2: Sumeru City, Nahida, Dottore
@version: 6.6
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
In Song of the Welkin Moon Acts IX and X the city was evacuated, Dottore's Irminsul tree emerged there, and Pantalone, the Ninth Harbinger, operated from the city with his own agents while a Corps of Thirty spy at Caravan Ribat was exposed. The Traveler fetched Mavuika's Pyro Gnosis through the Natlan border, and the allies overloaded Irminsul with the Akasha. Inside it Nahida got the three Shades' permission and burned it; Dottore was dragged into the flames by his own other segments. Afterwards, Pantalone watched the burning and Dottore bade him farewell using his real name, Feofan.

## entry: Chapter III in Sumeru City
@keys: Chapter III, Shouki no Kami, god-making plot
@keys2: Sumeru City, Akademiya, Nahida, Scaramouche
@version: 3.0
@spoiler: major
### ULTRA
The Akademiya's old sages colluded with Dottore and the Fatui to create an artificial God of Wisdom, crowning Scaramouche as Shouki no Kami with the Electro Gnosis and feeding him the dreams of the populace through the Akasha. The Sabzeruz Festival's dream loop trapped the people of the city. The Traveler, Alhaitham, Cyno, Nilou, Dehya and others freed Nahida, defeated the false god, and Nahida took the Electro Gnosis. She then asked the Traveler to help purge forbidden knowledge by removing Rukkhadevata from Irminsul, after which everyone except the Traveler forgot her and history recorded Nahida as the only Dendro Archon.

## entry: Akademiya Extravaganza
@keys: Akademiya Extravaganza, Interdarshan Championship, Diadem of Knowledge, Wisdom Gala
@version: 3.6
@availability: limited
### ULTRA
The Akademiya Extravaganza is a festival run by the Akademiya in Sumeru City, made up of the annual Wisdom Gala (Darshan booths across the city) and the Interdarshan Championship, held every four years. It is shown during the limited-time 3.6 event A Parade of Providence, in which Kaveh won the championship and the right to wear the Diadem of Knowledge, then destroyed the diadem after placing it on its stand.
