## summary
### LITE
Bayda Harbor is a Sumeru port on the coast of the Lokapala Jungle in the Dharma Forest, facing Fontaine across the sea. The Sumeru Akademiya runs it, with inspectors who watch the traffic and keep smugglers in check. Its quays are crowded with merchants and stranded foreigners from Liyue, Fontaine and the desert.
### STANDARD +
Many influential parties cross paths here, and the port's officers say the peace has held because everyone is kept in their own corner. It also serves as the Sumeru end of sea trips from Petrichor.

## geography
### STANDARD
The harbor lies on the north coast of Dharma Forest, in the Lokapala Jungle. The Lokapala Jungle runs west of the harbor up a nameless mountain's slope, where it meets the Desert of Hadramaveth. The sea in front of it runs straight to Fontaine.
### LARGE +
Its position puts it very close to Fontaine, so much so that merchant Zayah complains that Fontainian goods are no longer fashionable there. The wiki's note on the name: "Bayda" is a word for a desert without water or grass, an odd label for a seaside port.

## places
### LARGE
The docks hold ships and are watched by the Akademiya's officers. A playground area for Fungi, named the Lil' Fungi Playground, is the base of the limited-time event Sightseeing With Friends. Open-world NPCs are scattered along the quay: officers, merchants and a few travellers.

## people
### STANDARD
Shajar, the Port Master and an officer of general affairs, is a former Eremite mercenary who took the Akademiya's job offer; she is blunt, wants to be called Shajar or Boss, and disliked being doubted. Adel, a Mahamata of the Akademiya, supervises the Navbed who inspect arrivals and departures; he is an old sailor who once patrolled by boat as far as the Natlan border and likes watching the sea. Boyan, a Liyue merchant, carries goods between Liyue, Fontaine and Sumeru. Zayah is a trader belonging to the Sangemah Bay business group, looking for opportunities in Liyue food. Honorine is a Fontainian engineer stranded when her aquabus project was halted. Anaka is a child whose scholar father has gone to Petrichor.
### LARGE +
Adel is proud of his years on the sea; Shajar says she has contacts at Lumidouce Harbor and Yilong Wharf with whom she shares information about outlander smugglers. Honorine would like to hire Eremite guards to take her into the desert, but fears the cost and the risk, and mentions an engineer, Hautemontagne, whom she knows. Boyan once worried about the tea trade; after later events his supplies of Chenyu Adeptea recovered. Anaka loves music, wants to see Petrichor and says her uncle Giustino also made the crossing.
### ULTRA +
Haniyyah, a professional beast tamer and former Eremite, and the novelist Souka, a writer from Yae Publishing House who stays with her, are named as residents of the harbor, where they care for intelligent Fungi; this belongs to the Sightseeing With Friends event and may not be permanent.

## governance
### STANDARD
The Sumeru Akademiya controls the harbor through its officers: Navbed inspect traffic, a Mahamata (Adel) oversees them, and an officer of general affairs (Shajar) keeps day-to-day order. Entry has strict anti-smuggling rules.
### LARGE +
Shajar says ordinary smugglers know better than to try anything there, that she would hear of any large-scale Liyuean or Fontainian smugglers well in advance, and that she exchanges information with the heads of Lumidouce Harbor and Yilong Wharf. She also says the post is as busy as Port Ormos, which she had hoped to leave behind.

## culture
### LARGE
A cosmopolitan port. Liyue tea and food, Fontaine's engineering and a Sumeru scholarly and mercantile mix all trade here. Merchants talk freely about trade, and some hint at schemes they should not mention.

## history
### LARGE
Introduced in Version 3.8 as a Lokapala Jungle subarea and expanded in 4.6 with residents and a sea link to Fontaine; the wiki records no earlier history for the harbor. In the Fontaine world quest Latecoming Homecoming (4.6), the instrument seller Giulietta needs the Traveler's boat to reach Bayda Harbor to settle a contract payment, and the party crosses from Petrichor.
### ULTRA +
The harbor's Fungi playground is the start and end point of the Sightseeing With Friends event in Luna V (6.4), where the Traveler and Paimon meet familiar faces and go on a photo tour with Fungi companions.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Salt air, gulls and rigging at the edge of a rainforest; officers pacing with their inspection lists; traders bargaining in half-whispers; the occasional Fungi trotting along the quay. The mood is brisk and watchful rather than idle.

## dangers
### LARGE
Not a dangerous place in itself, as its officers take pride in order. The risk is smuggling and trade scams, not violence; the desert beyond the mountain is the real hazard.

## travel
### STANDARD
Ships connect Bayda Harbor with Fontaine, and in the story the Traveler's Waverider and a boat from Petrichor cross to it. By land it lies on the Lokapala Jungle coast of Dharma Forest.
### LARGE +
Fontainian goods are less valued here because of the proximity; Liyuean goods, especially tea and food, are in demand.

## quests
### LARGE
Latecoming Homecoming (Fontaine, 4.6) ends with a boat trip to the harbor. Reunite, My Shroom Buddies! and A New Day at the Lil' Fungi Playground, both part of the limited-time event Sightseeing With Friends (Luna V, 6.4), start at the harbor. The Fabulous Fungus Frenzy event is where Haniyyah and Souka first appeared.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 Bayda Harbor is an ordinary working port with no major story tied to it. The Archon Quest and later events have not changed it.
### STANDARD +
Its officers still inspect arrivals and its traders still complain about trade in Fontainian goods.

### LARGE
Shajar is an officer of general affairs and former Eremite mercenary. She says the work is much the same as before: paid to do a job, with a warning not to cross her. Adel, a Mahamata, calls himself an officer of the law who supervises the Navbed; he is a retired sailor who insists he is not idle.

## entry: Zayah, Boyan and Harbor Trade
@keys: Zayah, Boyan, Sangemah Bay, Chenyu Adeptea
@keys2: Bayda Harbor, merchant
### LARGE
Zayah represents the Sangemah Bay business group under Lord Sangemah Bay and wants to profit from Liyue food without breaking harbor rules. Boyan is a Liyue merchant who moves goods between Liyue, Fontaine and Sumeru; he worries about the supply of Chenyu Adeptea tea, whose quality fell after trouble in Chenyu Vale.

## entry: Honorine and Anaka

Honorine is a stranded Fontainian engineer who wants a guided trip into the desert to see Khaenri'ahn machines. Anaka is a child at the harbor who longs to join her father in Petrichor and loves music.
