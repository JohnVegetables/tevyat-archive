## summary
### LITE
Vimara Village is a small, tranquil fishing village on the river between Sumeru City and Port Ormos, in Ardravi Valley. Many caravans pass through it travelling between the two, though few merchants stay. Its viewpoint text says there is nothing to the village apart from a tranquil life and invites exhausted travelers to rest a while. It is also where the Aranyaka quest series, about the shy plantlike Aranara and the village's missing children, takes place.
### STANDARD +
The village chief is the elderly Amadhiah. Forest Rangers (called Forest Watchers in the quests), such as Rana, patrol the surrounding woods, which contain Withering zones.

## geography
### STANDARD
Vimara Village stands beside the great river Ardravi, in Ardravi Valley within the Dharma Forest region. Ardravi Valley's own description calls the river Sumeru's great waterborne mercantile route, kept pure by the Forest Watchers. The river path leads one way to Sumeru City and the other to Port Ormos.
### LARGE +
Nearby are Gandharva Ville (the Forest Rangers' base), Devantaka Mountain and, further into the forest, Vanarana, the Aranara's dream-nursery. The valley's local specialties are Nilotpala Lotus and Rukkhashava Mushrooms. In the open world the village has a fishing spot, a Waverider waypoint and a Four-Leaf Sigil, plants used for quick travel.

## people
### STANDARD
Vimara has a small cast of villagers: the chief Amadhiah, the merchants Bahram and Yuewen, the hunter Aurelius, a researcher named Nabati, and several children. Bahram says business just covers his costs, but fewer merchants means less competition and it is peaceful there.
### LARGE +
Amadhiah's family includes Iosaphat, his child, and the grandchildren Kavus and Sudabeh. Iotham is another of the village children. Alphonso, a young man who says he is an apprentice from Fontaine, has lived in the village for years and is close to Rana and the children. Other villagers listed in the area include Arezo, Banka, Granny Jehiet (who comes from the desert), Kumar, Kurash and Zadith (a student). Rana, a Forest Watcher, grew up in the village and still keeps a house there, though her patrols bring her home less often.

## culture
### LARGE
Village life is slow and modest, with fishing, herb gathering (Sudabeh went out for herbs for Amadhiah) and passing trade. Amadhiah keeps a Vintage Lyre that he received from a bard who once came from Mondstadt, and lends it to the Traveler. Iotham calls the desert "Valuka" and says Granny Jehiet comes from there; he claims he has heard of the Aranara only through her stories. Amadhiah worries over the children and says Rana pushes herself too hard, hoping she finds something worthwhile on her journey.

## history
### LARGE
Nothing in the sources read gives a founding date or older history for Vimara Village. It was introduced in version 3.0 with Sumeru, and the village's story in canon is that of the Aranyaka series. Its name's etymology is a variant of Sanskrit vimala, meaning "purity".

## quests
@version: 3.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The Aranyaka world quests begin around the village. In Woodland Encounter, the Traveler and Paimon meet Rana, who guides them here. The village's children have gone missing in turn and come back days later with no memory of it. After the Traveler's party drives off Eremites chasing Iotham, defeats a Fatui ambush in a cave and frees him from a Withering zone, Rana falls ill and is taken by the Aranara Arana, who explains she needs a Bija to recover.
### LARGE +
Amadhiah lends the Traveler a Vintage Lyre so they can play the Rhythm of the Great Dream and enter the dream version of Vanarana. In the later parts the Aranara host the Festival Utsava, the corrupted Marana is fought, and Arana gives up her memories to make the Bija. Back in Vimara Village, the Bija wakes Rana, who receives a Dendro Vision and leaves with Arana to make new memories.
### ULTRA +
Arana confessed that she had put the missing children to sleep and removed part of their memories before returning them, which is why they remembered nothing; she says that children forget the Aranara when they grow up. Sudabeh was the first to go missing, while gathering herbs for Amadhiah, and Kavus followed. In the hidden exploration objective The Bad Guy in Vimara Village, the Traveler can unmask Alphonso as Trofin Snezhevich, a Fatui agent raised by the House of the Hearth and sent to capture an Aranara: he had tricked the children into going into the forest alone so the Fatui could seize them, and the Aranara rescued them and wiped those memories. Confronted, he either leaves for good or dies fighting; Amadhiah, unaware of the truth, later wonders why he left without a word.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Quiet water, fishing lines, boats passing and caravans stopping to rest. Under the trees there are patches of Withering, and Forest Watchers walk the paths. The air is warm and slow, and the villagers expect little excitement.

## travel
### LARGE
Caravans and boats pass along the river between Sumeru City and Port Ormos. A Waverider waypoint helps river travel. Few merchants stay overnight because most go on to Port Ormos.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 Vimara Village is a quiet riverside stop. Rana left on a journey with Arana at the end of the Aranyaka quests, and Amadhiah hopes she finds something worthwhile.
### STANDARD +
No story event specific to the village is recorded after the Aranyaka quests. The rainforest was evacuated in the 6.6 Irminsul crisis; the sources do not say anything about the village in particular.

## entry: Amadhiah
@keys: Amadhiah, Grandpa Amadhiah
@keys2: Vimara Village
@version: 3.0
### STANDARD
The elderly chief of Vimara Village, with weak eyesight he apologises for. He lends the Traveler a Vintage Lyre that he received from a Mondstadt bard. He is a grandfather to Kavus and Sudabeh, and fond of Rana. He vaguely remembers the Aranara as plantlike fairies and knows only stories.

## entry: Children of Vimara and the Aranara
@keys: missing children, Iotham, Alphonso, Sudabeh, Kavus, Arana
@keys2: Vimara Village, Aranara
@version: 3.0
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Several children of Vimara Village vanished into the woods and came back days later with no memory, because the Aranara Arana had put them to sleep and wiped part of their memories after befriending them. The adults could not find them and the Traveler's early attempts to coax the truth out of the children by telling stories failed. Iotham was later rescued from a Withering zone in a cave.

## entry: Rana
@keys: Rana
@keys2: Vimara Village, Forest Watcher, Aranara
@version: 3.0
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
A Forest Watcher who grew up in Vimara Village and guides the Traveler there. Despite lacking a Vision, she charges into a Withering zone to save Iotham and falls sick, then recovers when the Bija is brought. At the end she receives a Dendro Vision and leaves the village with Arana, after telling Tighnari of her plans.

## entry: Port Ormos and the River Route
@keys: river route, caravans, Port Ormos
@keys2: Vimara Village
@version: 3.0
### LARGE
Port Ormos is Sumeru's largest port, a trading hub beneath two giant trees where smuggling is more common than in Sumeru City and prices are lower. A river runs from Sumeru City to the port, and Vimara Village sits on it, so caravans between the two often pass through.
