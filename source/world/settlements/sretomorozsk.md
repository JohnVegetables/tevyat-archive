## summary
### LITE
Sretomorozsk is a quiet border town in the southwest of Snezhnaya, a railway station and port that connects the country with Nod-Krai. In Chapter VII it is where the Traveler first sees the effects of Project Stuzha: homes are cold, inns are closed and the station is shut. Alyosha, who keeps a small place there, uncovers a Kristall-hoarding scheme.
### STANDARD +
Most people who board a train here are going to or from Nod-Krai. The name combines the Russian words for Candlemas and frost, and may echo the Candlemas frosts.

## geography
### STANDARD
The town lies in the Volkodlak Tundra, in the far southwest, with a station and an eastern port. The wiki's Navigation text calls it a little deserted. Shishkin offered to open the lower combustion chamber of the Kresnik's Torch to residents so they could at least boil water and cook.
### LARGE +
Alyosha's house, the station, the Kristall distribution booth, residents' houses and a tunnel from the station to the mayor's house are all in the town. The Snezhnaya Vestnik newspaper is sold here.

## people
### STANDARD
Eastern port: Buranova, Gryaznov (the mayor after the quest), Obolensky, Ryabov and Vetrova. Station: Burtsev, plus the Transport Bureau conductors Shagova and Vyorskaya. Other residents include Cherkasskaya, Ermolov, Guseva, Samirka, Yarugin and Zolotova. The Fatui representative who handles Kristall deliveries is Polonsky, and the liaison officer who mediates between the Fatui and the townspeople was Shishkin.
### LARGE +
Repnina, an elderly woman with failing memory who also comes from Alatyr, lives here; Alyosha visits her, cleans her stove and tidies her room. Alyosha himself spends time here but, in his own words, does not stay at his place often. His dog Tugarin joins him.

## governance
### STANDARD
A mayor leads the town, helped by a Fatui liaison officer and a Fatui representative who handles deliveries. The Veche's border-support resolution lets residents buy Kristall from the Fatui. The old mayor and Shishkin were arrested and sent to Snezhnograd for judgment, and the Fatui took over energy distribution until new officials were named.
### LARGE +
Alyosha's evidence was arithmetic: he counted train carriages during the snowstorm and compared the train's energy use with the quota figures, finding one or two missing carriages every time and three on the last delivery.

## economy
### STANDARD
Under Project Stuzha businesses have closed, the price of Kristall rose by about a third while the town's quota fell, and the Fatui deliver only twice a month. Burtsev complains that a shipment does not last a week. Residents cannot afford to heat their homes, let alone run inns.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Cold and shortages are the main dangers. The Traveler's train ride out of town passes through a Shadow Realm distortion full of twisted Oprichniki troopers. The Fatui keep an eye on the Rokot, a covert group Polonsky mentions (Alyosha is later shown aligned with it).

## quests
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
'Gunfire in the Silent Lands' (Archon Quest, Chapter VII, Act I, part 2) begins here. Alyosha shelters the Traveler and Paimon, exposes Shishkin and the mayor, and the Traveler then drives the train using the Eye of Graeae; the ride runs into Shadow Realm trouble and the next quest begins at Rankova Zorya.
### ULTRA +
Shishkin claimed he took the Kristall for his parents in the abandoned village of Alatyr. Alyosha, who is from Alatyr, knows that nobody lives there. Polonsky, impressed by Alyosha's work, asks whether he is really a hunter and mentions that the Rokot has been recruiting people with his talents, which Alyosha says he had not heard of. Paimon, hit by a snow pile when she arrived in Snezhnaya, is cared for at Alyosha's house while he and the Traveler investigate.

## status
### LITE
By 7.1 the corruption has been exposed, the Fatui supervise distribution and have promised that the station will resume service, but the shortages are not over: Alyosha notes Project Stuzha is not going away soon. Sretomorozsk continues to serve as the transit town for Nod-Krai traffic.

## entry: Shishkin and the Kristall Scheme
@keys: Shishkin, Gryaznov, Polonsky, Sretomorozsk mayor
@keys2: Kristall, Sretomorozsk
@version: 7.0
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
The liaison officer Shishkin smoothly calmed angry residents while secretly stockpiling Kristall. With the old mayor, who picked the workers who unloaded cargo, he moved crates through a tunnel to the mayor's house and sold them out of town, so the town stayed cold. Alyosha and Tugarin tracked the scent to a tunnel behind Shishkin's home; the Fatui recovered stolen crates and arrested both men, while Gryaznov became mayor.
