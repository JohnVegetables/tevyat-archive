## summary
### LITE
Glupov is a fortress-city in Snezhnaya, described as a satellite city within Snezhnograd, and the headquarters of the Fatui. Its name means "the city of fools". Its present Mayor is Pulcinella, "The Rooster", the Fifth of the Fatui Harbingers.
### STANDARD +
In the 7.0 Archon Quest Alyosha describes it as the block adjacent to Zapolyarny Palace, "kind of a city in its own right", and says it contains the Armory Palace, a munitions factory. The Fatui raised it on the Tsaritsa Anastasya's command in the fourth year of the 799th Cycle, a year after she created the Fatui to take the lands of the rebellious fae princes.

## history
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
In the first year of the 799th Cycle Anastasya was crowned Tsaritsa after the death of Emperor Monomakh, the Belyi Tsar. In the third year she used a new force, the Fatui, rather than the old Druzhna to subdue the princes who had defied her. In the fourth year she had the Fatui build a fortress near Snezhnograd and named it Glupov. The next significant act the same book records is the founding of the Snezhnograd Veche two years later.
### LARGE +
The book that records this, A Brief History of the Pale Starborn, gives no further account of Glupov's construction. The Fatui have been described since as the organisation that acts as Snezhnaya's government, diplomatic corps and military, with the city as their base. Pulcinella's own profile ties him to both cities: he is Mayor of Snezhnograd and, per the Guiding Notes, of Glupov, and he took the Snezhnograd mayoralty after his mentor.

## people
@version: 7.0
### LARGE
Mayor: Pulcinella. NPCs the wiki places in Glupov are ordinary Fatui personnel: Anton (former acting captain of the Ninth Company), Oktyabrskaya (field medic), Sereda (mess chef), Stepanyan, Tikhon (sapper), Trofimova (maintenance technician), Uspensky and Uvarov (trainees), Zinenko (quartermaster) and Zinoviy (drillmaster). They give the place a barracks feel: drills, canteens, repairs and recruits.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Think of a fortress that is also a workplace: drill grounds and mess halls, orderly Fatui in uniform, field medics, sappers, trainees and a quartermaster going about their work. Pulcinella had a cold, stoic portrait of the Tsaritsa commissioned and, with her approval, hung through Glupov's halls, so her image presides over the place.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 Glupov remains the Fatui's headquarters under Mayor Pulcinella. No later change to its role is established.
### ULTRA +
For scene use: Glupov is a closed site. In the 7.0 Archon Quest "Great Deeds on the Tundra" a Fatui guard at the gate, Yuzhanin, turns the Traveler away because Glupov does not accept meeting requests without a prior appointment and an issued invitation, and adds that Harbingers rarely stay there for long; the Harbinger appearing as Marionette comes out to meet the Traveler but says Glupov's rules forbid inviting them inside. Even the Tsaritsa's portrait that now hangs in its halls was commissioned in a world quest (The Tsaritsa's New Portrait) that starts at Zapolyarny Palace. Treat Glupov as the Fatui's working base; do not invent secret chambers, Harbinger council halls or specific officers beyond those listed.
