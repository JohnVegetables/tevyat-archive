## summary
### LITE
Yilong Wharf, also called Yilong Port, is a prosperous commercial port in Chenyu Vale, Liyue, across the sea from Fontaine and southeast of Lumidouce Harbor. People and goods come and go. Its closeness to Fontaine makes it a convenient place for busy Fontainians to sample Liyue culture without travelling all the way to Liyue Harbor.
### STANDARD +
Chenyu Vale's people hold an ancestral ceremony here, and its artisans still carve simple jade ornaments such as the Symbol of Felicitation.

## geography
### STANDARD
The wharf sits in the Upper Vale of Chenyu Vale, on three levels: an upper level of shops and teahouses, a lower level of food stalls and fishers, and outskirts where researchers and Millelith stand watch. Its viewpoint is titled "The Clouds Cascade Around the Wharf".
### LARGE +
Violetgrass is its local specialty. After the quest Our Chenyu Vale Trek, photos from the "Our Chenyu Vale Trek" album are displayed in the central courtyard.

## places
### STANDARD
The Jiulue Teahouse, owned by Baishu, has a stage for performances and an outdoor dining area. Fengtai runs the general goods store and Lianfang a street-food stall. A communications office and a Millelith presence serve the port.
### LARGE +
Tea merchants such as Fryslan, Hermanson and Jiuguan do business here. Guards for hire, including Dip Yeuk, Hanqian, Yongzan and Yougui, wait for work. The storyteller Jiewu performs at the teahouse. Chan Baatjan is the chef; the angler is Wangjuan and the hydrologist is Woliu; the researcher Ctesiphon works in the outskirts.

## people
### STANDARD
Baishu (teahouse boss), Quezhi (waitress), Fengtai, Lianfang, Sergeant Lin and the Millelith Xuanzhu and Keshen. Hautemontagne appears here as a random visitor.
### LARGE +
Huang the Third, known as "The Cloudpiercing Arrow", is seen by day. The brand image consultant Vert Jr. and the staff of the communications office, Fangju and Lixing, also work here, as does Lixin, the Feiyun Commerce Guild messenger.

## culture
### STANDARD
The people of Chenyu Vale go to Yilong Wharf to worship ancestors and the dead. They once threw carved jade into the water in sacrifice; this continued even after the ancient god who ruled the vale perished and Morax took over Liyue's order.
### LARGE +
Yilong jade is blue-green and lustrous, and herb gatherers still occasionally find it in ruins in the mountains and valleys. The old art of carving the offering ornaments was lost, but artisans at the wharf still make simple ornaments. The tradition reflects the vale's cultural difference from Liyue Harbor.

## history
### LARGE
Sacrificial Jade's text recounts that when Rex Lapis arrived, the vale's ancestors lived as scattered hill tribes, and that over time they merged with Liyue migrants. The ancestral ceremony has survived to the present. Elsewhere, tales of the jade slab that summoned rain and sank into the river explain the vale's name.
### ULTRA +
During Lantern Rite events the wharf is festively decorated: Vibrant Harriers Aloft in Spring Breeze (4.4) and Springtime Charms (5.3), the latter involving the Jiulue Teahouse. Both are limited-time.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Bustling, with cloud cascading around the wharf, merchants haggling and travellers from Fontaine strolling the stalls.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 Yilong Wharf is a thriving port.
### STANDARD +
Nothing in 6.x or 7.0-7.1 disrupts it.

## entry: Jiulue Teahouse
@keys: Jiulue Teahouse, Baishu, Quezhi, Jiewu
### STANDARD
A teahouse owned by Baishu, with a stage and an outdoor dining area, at Yilong Wharf. Quezhi waits tables and Jiewu tells stories. The indoor dining area, private seats and rooms are described as under renovation.

## entry: Yilong Jade and the Ancestral Rite
@keys: Yilong jade, Symbol of Felicitation, Sacrificial Jade, jade offerings
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Blue-green Yilong jade was once thrown into the vale's waters in sacrifice. The custom faded, but the ceremony at the wharf survives. The Symbol of Felicitation (artifact text) ties the carved ornaments to a pact with a deity and legends of the Herblord.
