## summary
### LITE
The Stadium of the Sacred Flame is the heart of Natlan, a great arena in the Basin of Unnumbered Flames that serves as the nation's main city and the common ground of its six tribes. Above the arena burns the Sacred Flame, the ever-burning fire that holds back the Abyss and links the living world to the Night Kingdom. Here Natlan held the Pilgrimage of the Return of the Sacred Flame, its great combat tournament, and the Ode of Resurrection that brought fallen heroes back to life; here too stands the Speaker's Chamber, where the Pyro Archon Mavuika governs. Since the Abyss was defeated (5.3) the Stadium is a place of peace and sport.
### STANDARD +
Around the arena lie three busy quarters: Chuwen Fair to the north (market stalls and the Adventurers' Guild), the Temple of Pax to the south (research, shops and the Speaker's Chamber) and the Warrior's Rest Area with the Weary Inn, where Pilgrimage contestants eat and sleep for free.

## geography
### STANDARD
The Stadium is a subarea of the Basin of Unnumbered Flames in central Natlan. The Sacred Flame rises from an outlet above the arena itself and can be seen from far outside the Stadium. Chuwen Fair runs along the northern side of the stadium, the Temple of Pax along the southern side, and the Warrior's Rest Area and Weary Inn sit on the outskirts of Chuwen Fair. The Speaker's Chamber is a watchtower-like building in one corner of the complex.
### LARGE +
According to the expedition text, surveyors of the Saurian Relics Association concluded that the basin is not natural but the crater left by a colossal object that crashed there long ago; what the object was remains unanswered. The flames on the Stele of the Sacred Flame, the text adds, have not answered anyone's questions either.

## places
### STANDARD
- The arena: the tournament ground at the centre, overlooked by the Sacred Flame; ceremonies, speeches and matches take place here.
- Speaker's Chamber: the Pyro Archon's seat of government and council hall.
- Chuwen Fair (north): market and shops, the Adventurers' Guild desk (Katheryne) and the store of the Saurian Relics Association.
- Temple of Pax (south): an outer concourse, a fair inside, and the temple, which handles academic research such as the study of ancient dragon relics.
- Warrior's Rest Area: the Weary Inn, the Contestant Lounge and street food stalls.
### LARGE +
Named businesses include Frida's Palette (owner Acat) in Chuwen Fair, The Salutary Furnace (owner Alom) in the Temple of Pax and Zakan's Street Bites (owner Chanca) in the Warrior's Rest Area. The Contestant Lounge has its own doctor (Erinle) and manager (Karpa). Notice boards include the "Saurian Relics Association" Message Board, the Contestant Lounge Noticeboard, the Stadium bulletin board and the Weary Inn bulletin board.

## people
### LARGE
People from all six tribes, their Saurian companions, merchants, adventurers and foreign visitors mix here. The Pyro Archon Mavuika is the Stadium's most prominent figure, and the Ancient Name bearers and allies of the war all pass through it. The six heroes who finally empowered her were Kinich, Iansan, Xilonen, Mualani, Ororon and Chasca; Kachina and Citlali were also central to the defence.
Tournament staff include the organiser Balam, the referee Camac and the engineer Shana. During the Pilgrimage, a courier (Agemo) works the fair only on tournament days. The Weary Inn's managers are said to be chosen and appointed by the Speaker's Chamber.

## governance
### STANDARD
The Speaker's Chamber, presided over by the Pyro Archon, coordinates the affairs of the Stadium and of the six major tribes. Ancient tradition requires the tribal chiefs to gather by its fire at regular intervals and negotiate openly under the Archon's eye. It also appears to act as Natlan's main judicial body. In Natlan the Pyro Archon is a mortal chosen through the Pilgrimage, which tests candidates in battle.
### LARGE +
The Chamber pays for public needs directly: the Pyro Archon funds the free food and lodging at the Weary Inn on the eve of each Pilgrimage, and before the final battle Mavuika promised that the Chamber would buy up surplus feast supplies for redistribution. Behind the Chamber is a small display room where generations of Pyro Archons kept keepsakes entrusted to them by warriors.

## culture
### STANDARD
The Stadium exists for contest: it hosted the Pilgrimage of the Return of the Sacred Flame, whose fights produced the Contending Fire that fuels the Flame, and the Ode of Resurrection.
### LARGE +
The Pilgrimage of the Return of the Sacred Flame was held here whenever the Abyss threatened, and ever more often as the Night Warden Wars intensified. Team rounds sent contestants out by hot-air balloon across the basin to win Contending Sparks; the winners then fought one-on-one in the arena. The top Ancient Name bearers earned the right to fight in the Night Warden Wars. Only Natlanese could compete, because Contending Fire, the fuel of the Sacred Flame, is only produced when people of Natlan fight one another.
Contestants pre-register at the Stadium; team captains register their team before evening on the day before the tournament. Missing the morning opening ceremony disqualifies the whole team, and persistent harassment of rivals can earn a ban. Natlanese consider the contest a matter of honour: some feel a team with two Ancient Name bearers looks cowardly. The Ode of Resurrection was the Stadium's other great rite. An officiant recited a eulogy, the Pyro Archon stepped into the Sacred Flame to find the fallen warrior in the Night Kingdom, and the crowd bowed their heads and sang until the Archon emerged with the revived hero. It only worked for those whose dispatched unit achieved victory, and Mavuika's own Vision story recalls the Stadium receiving news of such a victory when she was a child.

## history
### STANDARD
Natlanese tradition holds that after Tenoch united the tribes during the cataclysm 500 years ago, the first Pyro Archon laid down the rules still followed at the Stadium. Mavuika placed her life into the Sacred Flame five hundred years ago as part of her plan to save Natlan, and returned to become Pyro Archon again before the Traveler arrived. In 5.0 Kachina's failed Ode exposed the Abyss's interference and Capitano challenged Mavuika in the arena; in 5.1 the Stadium became Natlan's last stronghold against the Abyssal invasion; in 5.3 Mavuika and the Traveler ended the war from the Night Kingdom while Natlan waited around the Flame.
### LARGE +
- 5.0 (Act I): in the arena Kachina beat Nkunga and Liliuo and then, in the final elimination bout, her friend Mualani, earning a place in the Night Warden Wars alongside Chasca, Iansan, Kaiulani and Kibangu.
- 5.0 (Act II): when Kachina did not return, Mavuika performed her Ode but emerged alone, saying neither Kachina nor her Ancient Name could be found. Some in the crowd questioned whether Kachina had been worthy; Mavuika declared her a hero and suspended the Pilgrimage. Contending Fire was by then nearly exhausted, and Mavuika began feeding the Flame with her own power. Capitano then walked into the Stadium with Fatui Skirmishers, accused her of breaking an oath made 500 years ago and fought her; she left a searing wound on his chest before a fog from the Masters of the Night-Wind let him escape. Mavuika also burned the Pyro Archons' collection of warriors' keepsakes, which held traces of Contending Fire, to rescue Kachina from the Night Kingdom.
- 5.1 (Act III): Capitano came to the Stadium a second time to confront Mavuika and was saved only by Ororon's intervention.
- 5.1 (Act IV): as the Abyss poured from a rift in the sky, Natlan's defenders fell back on the Stadium, the one place the Sacred Flame could still protect. Chuychu's body was brought there. Mavuika and the six heroes performed the ritual at the centre of the arena that extended the Ode to every Natlanese warrior; the Abyss then sent larger forces against the Stadium to put out the Flame, and the defenders held until Mavuika destroyed the rift. The Pyro Gnosis was used to keep the Flame alive during this attack.
- 5.2 (Interlude): the heroes reassembled at the Stadium.
- 5.3 (Act V): the Traveler's newly forged Ancient Name was announced in the arena, and Mavuika took them into the Night Kingdom. During the battle the Sacred Flame went out and the crowd despaired, until Kachina rallied them; the restored Flame helped Mavuika and the Traveler destroy Gosoythoth. Afterwards a monument to the fallen of the final battle was raised, and the Traveler left their hero's crown there.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
In peacetime the Stadium is loud and festive: crowds, food stalls, Saurians wandering among the stalls, challengers sizing one another up and nights of music and feasting at the Weary Inn. The towering flame dominates every view. The official map line sums up the mood: in the name of victory and honour, the fires of conflict are lit, and every burning spirit feeds the everlasting flame. In wartime scenes it was the last line of defence, ringed by checkpoints and filled with the wounded.

## dangers
### LARGE
Duels and brawls are part of daily life, so outsiders should expect challenges. Real danger came only from the Abyss, which twice assaulted the Stadium and the Flame in 5.1-5.3, and from Capitano's open attacks in 5.0-5.1. Since the end of the Night Warden Wars no further threat to the Stadium has been shown.

## travel
### LARGE
The Stadium is the hub most routes in Natlan lead to; tribal settlements send their people here for the Pilgrimage and for council. Kachina's guidance on the Traveler's first visit led along the road from the Children of Echoes, and she rode the Saurian Toto to just outside the city. The north entrance is mentioned as a parting place in the Archon Quest.

## quests
### STANDARD
Much of Archon Quest Chapter V takes place here, together with Mavuika's story quest (Sol Invictus Chapter) and many world quests and commissions.
### ULTRA +
Quests starting at the Stadium or its quarters include Pilgrimage of the Return of the Sacred Flame (5.0), Echoes of Life and Past and Future (Act II), The Flickering Light Splits in Twain (Act III), The Surging Darkness and A Fuel Named "Fate" (Act IV), Subterranean Ruins and As the Sun Rises and Sets (Act V), After the Peace (Mavuika's story quest), the world quest The Case of the Crafting Bench, and Where the Moon Rises (6.0). Episodes in Chapter V are told in the sub-entries below.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 Natlan is at peace. The Night Warden Wars are over, so the Ode of Resurrection is no longer held and Ancient Name bearers no longer have to compete. Mavuika has turned the Pilgrimage into a purely athletic competition, which opens the possibility of outsiders taking part.
### STANDARD +
The first Pilgrimage of the new kind was won by Kinich. Mavuika remains Pyro Archon and still governs from the Speaker's Chamber, though after the events in Nod-Krai she gave the Pyro Gnosis, through the Traveler, to Nahida, who used it to burn down the corrupted Irminsul (Song of the Welkin Moon Act X); the Gnosis has been unaccounted for since. In 6.0 the Traveler's Natlanese friends and Mavuika threw a farewell party for them before they sailed with Ineffa for Nod-Krai.

## entry: Chuwen Fair
@keys: Chuwen Fair
### STANDARD
Chuwen Fair is the market quarter along the northern side of the Stadium of the Sacred Flame. It holds food and goods vendors, the Adventurers' Guild desk run by Katheryne, the store of the Saurian Relics Association (manager Siyah) and the paint shop Frida's Palette (owner Acat). On tournament days a courier works the fair, and the Weary Inn lies on its outskirts.

## entry: Temple of Pax
@keys: Temple of Pax
### STANDARD
The Temple of Pax occupies the southern side of the Stadium of the Sacred Flame: an outer concourse, an inner fair and the temple itself, which handles academic research, including the study of ancient dragon relics. Vendors include The Salutary Furnace (owner Alom). The Speaker's Chamber belongs to this part of the complex.

## entry: Warrior's Rest Area and the Weary Inn
@keys: Warrior's Rest Area, Weary Inn, Contestant Lounge
### STANDARD
The Warrior's Rest Area of the Stadium of the Sacred Flame houses the Contestant Lounge (doctor Erinle, manager Karpa), street food at Zakan's Street Bites and the Weary Inn. The Weary Inn is a restaurant and hotel on the outskirts of Chuwen Fair that feeds and houses Pilgrimage contestants and their families for free on the eve of the tournament, at the Pyro Archon's expense. Its name expresses the wish that warriors leave their weariness there and enter the Stadium refreshed. Its managers are said to be chosen by the Speaker's Chamber, and it has become the city's favourite place to celebrate.

## entry: Speaker's Chamber
@keys: Speaker's Chamber
@version: 5.0
### STANDARD
The Speaker's Chamber is a watchtower-like building in a corner of the Stadium of the Sacred Flame. The Pyro Archon presides there over the affairs of the Stadium and the six tribes, whose chiefs traditionally meet around its fire at regular intervals to negotiate openly. It also seems to serve as Natlan's highest court. A fire pit at its centre is itself an outlet of the Sacred Flame. A small display room at the back kept the Pyro Archons' collection of warriors' keepsakes until Mavuika burned them in 5.0 to rescue Kachina.

## entry: Kachina's failed Ode at the Stadium
@keys: Kachina's Ode, failed resurrection, Ode for Kachina
@keys2: Stadium of the Sacred Flame, Ode of Resurrection, Kachina
@version: 5.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
After Kachina fell in the Night Warden Wars, an Ode of Resurrection was held for her in the Stadium (Archon Quest Chapter V Act II). Ntotila officiated and Mavuika entered the Sacred Flame, but she came back alone: neither Kachina nor her Ancient Name could be found in the Night Kingdom. Some spectators questioned whether Kachina had been worthy; Mavuika publicly named her a hero, suspended the Pilgrimage and investigated. The cause was Abyssal interference in the Night Kingdom, and Kachina was later rescued.

## entry: Capitano at the Stadium
@keys: Capitano, The Captain
@keys2: Stadium of the Sacred Flame, Sacred Flame, Mavuika
@version: 5.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
In 5.0 the Fatui Harbinger Capitano walked into the Stadium with two Fatui Skirmishers, accused Mavuika of failing an oath made 500 years ago and fought her. She gave him a searing wound on the chest; a fog created by someone from the Masters of the Night-Wind covered his escape. ## entry: Defence of the Stadium
@keys: Defence of the Stadium, Abyssal invasion of Natlan, siege of the Stadium
@keys2: Stadium of the Sacred Flame, Sacred Flame
@version: 5.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
When the Abyss invaded Natlan from a rift in the sky (Chapter V Act IV), the tribes' outposts fell one by one and the defenders retreated to the Stadium, where the Sacred Flame could still repel the Abyss. Mavuika and the six heroes performed a ritual at the centre of the arena that let her extend the Ode of Resurrection to every warrior of Natlan, Ancient Name or not. The Abyss responded by sending larger forces against the Stadium to put out the Flame; the defenders held, and Mavuika destroyed the rift.

## entry: The final battle and the monument
@keys: monument to the fallen, final battle against the Abyss
@keys2: Stadium of the Sacred Flame, Sacred Flame, Gosoythoth
@version: 5.3
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
In Chapter V Act V the Traveler's Ancient Name was revealed to the crowd in the arena, and Mavuika led them from the Stadium into the Night Kingdom to destroy Gosoythoth. At the height of the battle the Sacred Flame went out; Kachina began the Ode herself and rallied the crowd, and their support renewed the Flame. After the victory a monument to the fallen of the final battle was raised, and the Traveler left their hero's crown before it. A storyteller (Sadigua) was later seen telling children the tale at the Stadium.

## entry: The new Pilgrimage
@keys: new Pilgrimage, athletic Pilgrimage, A New Pilgrimage
@keys2: Stadium of the Sacred Flame, Pilgrimage, Mavuika
@version: 5.3
### STANDARD
With the war over, Mavuika revived the Pilgrimage as a pure sport (Mavuika's story quest, Sol Invictus Chapter Act I). Iansan designed a competition course to use the Stadium's maximum capacity, the Traveler served as a special supervisor and was therefore not allowed to compete, and only one winner was crowned. Kinich won the first new Pilgrimage. The change opens the possibility of outsiders competing.
