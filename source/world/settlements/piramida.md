## summary
### LITE
Piramida is the city of the Lightkeepers, Nod-Krai's volunteer militia against the Wild Hunt. It stands on a mountain around a giant golden-age lighthouse and is a garrison town of Ratniki: soldiers' lodgings, an infirmary, a forge, a plaza with a bulletin board, a great mead hall at the top, and a towering statue of the Torchforger, Solovei. It is proud, short of people, and run on stubborn hope.
### STANDARD +
The people who live here come from every corner of the world, so cooking and tastes are varied. The Lightkeepers' oath ends with the line about shining the eternal light of hope into the endless snow-swept plains, and the city treats it as a working motto.

## geography
### STANDARD
Piramida lies in Voidsea Outlook, at the southern end of the island of Pohjola, north of Paha Isle. It is built on a mountain around a giant lighthouse from the golden age, which in turn rests on the foundations of an older castle. Its highest level holds the great mead hall.
### LARGE +
Kipumaki Cliff and the Pillar of Embla lie in northern Nod-Krai. From the lighthouse at the Final Night Cemetery, where the Ratnik Flins lives, Piramida can be spotted far to the north. Dreadshade Mire and Afterglow Cavern fall within the patrol routes noted on the city's bulletin board.

## places
### STANDARD
The plaza with its bulletin board and officers, the Torchforger Statue, the Great Mead Hall, an infirmary with doctors, a blacksmith, and the central lighthouse. Illuga's house, where he cooks for guests, is also in the city.
### LARGE +
The statue depicts Solovei the Torchforger, the Lightkeepers' first Starshyna, and has clearly endured centuries; its plaque, in Snezhnayan script, reads 'We will tear the snowclad veils, we will give the wind to the wilderness.' The mead hall hosts victory banquets and remembrance feasts, and the first pour is always offered to the departed. Flins notes the banquet custom descends from Snezhnayan court tradition, while Illuga recalls kitchen work there as a boy and elders weeping over their drinks.
### ULTRA +
Nikita says the old coat of arms of the fae governor can still be seen in the stone of the foundations, and jokes that the governor's buried treasure might be found. The mead hall was built to house the Arrow of Terpikeraunas, with its fireplace shaping the Spiralblade into the arrowhead, and the lighthouse can transform into a huge cannon.

## people
### STANDARD
Starshyna Nikita leads the Lightkeepers from here. Illuga, squad leader of the Nightmare Orioles and Nikita's adopted son, calls the city home. Ratniki in the plaza include Lyubertsi, Malusha, Marushkin (Sergeant Major) and Zimin, with Gabieta as Squad Leader; Ivar, Radoslaw and Lauri are in the infirmary, Hertta is the blacksmith, and Patolichev and Wszebor serve elsewhere in the city.
### LARGE +
Olav, a squad leader whom Nikita once nudged into the role with a staged card game, is at the mead hall. Anleifr and Rollon of the Nightmare Orioles come here for banquets. Dragana and Paivio are found at Illuga's house.
### ULTRA +
Nikita rose to Starshyna after his predecessor Sigurd fell in battle about a decade ago and took in Illuga soon after, when the boy's village was ravaged by the Wild Hunt. Ivar, now a doctor, was badly injured with Illuga's squad three years ago and was given a new post so he could keep serving.

## governance
### STANDARD
Piramida is the Lightkeepers' headquarters. The Starshyna leads, below him are Sergeant Majors, who coordinate Squad Leaders, who command Ratniki. Every Ratnik carries an Oath Lantern. Nod-Krai's autonomy under the Tsaritsa's old edict rests on the Lightkeepers, and the city defends it.
### LARGE +
Headquarters has a reputation among field members for ignoring reports, partly because there are so few Lightkeepers. When medical stocks ran low, Sergeant Major Gabieta asked the Voynich Guild for bandages.

## culture
### STANDARD
Duty and remembrance. Banquets after victories are for both celebration and mourning, graves are visited, and the oath is sworn by every Lightkeeper on joining. The Lightkeepers' symbol, a lighthouse on their flag and a nightingale with a burning wick, stands for keeping a beacon lit in the dark night.
### LARGE +
Notices on the bulletin board show daily life: new recruits are told that Ratnik is neither glory nor fortune, only the north wind; a Ratnik was caught drunk on sentry duty and reassigned; and a recruit was once cheated by a woman posing as a Scion priestess.

## history
### STANDARD
The city sits on the foundations of the palace of Prokhor Trubetskoy, a fae Grand Duke who governed Nod-Krai before the Cataclysm. About 500 years ago the Torchforger, Solovei, won the Tsaritsa's leave to found the Lightkeepers and establish autonomy for Nod-Krai. Piramida became their base. In the 6.3 world quests the Lightkeepers launched the Arrow of Terpikeraunas from the mead hall and lighthouse to destroy the Abyssal Scourge of Endless Decay, which had lain sealed beneath Kipumaki Cliff since the Torchforger's day and which Nikita calls the undying fiend and the 'Professor'.
### LARGE +
After the Cataclysm, Solovei and the Frostmoon Scions' first Moonchanter, Aila, with Alia's help, subdued and sealed the fiend; the Arrow of Terpikeraunas has been passed down among Lightkeepers since. Nikita says the Lightkeepers have nearly faded into history several times and each time rebuilt.
### ULTRA +
An artifact legend tells how the Wild Hunt's leader rode abroad on a moonless night as the Lightkeepers prepared to march south, and a Fatui host arrived at the north gate offering reinforcement that the Starshyna saw as a threat to their autonomy. This is only a legend and not confirmed by a quest.

## dangers
### STANDARD
The Wild Hunt is the constant threat, and patrol routes toward Dreadshade Mire and Afterglow Cavern have been hit. The Lightkeepers are understrength after years of losses; the Wildnight Owls squad was suspended after a failed operation.
### LARGE +
In 6.3 the Abyss struck at Piramida itself during the operation against the Scourge, sending monsters to the plaza and a spirit called the Ultimate Calamity against the Traveler and Illuga.

## travel
### STANDARD
Moonwagons, the Lightkeepers' kuuvahki-powered mobile lighthouses made from modified Snezhnayan vehicles, carry supplies between Piramida and the camps. The city lies in Voidsea Outlook on Pohjola.
### LARGE +
Moonwagons also run between Cliffwatch Camp and the city. The city lighthouse can be seen from far away; at the Final Night Cemetery, a telescope shows a distant city of unusual design.

## quests
### STANDARD
The Nightingale's Song world quest series (6.3) runs through Piramida: Wind-Stirred Ripples, Tower of Inversion, When War Songs Rise and The Last Night, the First Light, the last of which sets the Arrow of Terpikeraunas and ends with a banquet. The Moonlit Watcher (II) has Illuga host the Traveler to dinner at his house.
### LARGE +
Illuga's mailbox and books offer small scenes: a child's letter asking to join, a fairy tale he was given as a boy, and his intelligence file on Wild Hunt outbreak patterns. The Traveler can read these with Illuga in the party.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Stone, lamp oil and woodsmoke under a huge lighthouse, with the cold wind off the plains. Ratniki move purposefully, lanterns on their belts. At feasts the mead hall roars with noise and then falls silent for the dead.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 Piramida is the Lightkeepers' working headquarters, still understrength but hopeful after destroying the Scourge in 6.3.
### STANDARD +
Nikita appears in the mead hall as Starshyna once the world quests conclude. The Abyssal crisis at Kipumaki Cliff is over, but the Wild Hunt remains and the Lightkeepers rebuild. Illuga says more people are asking to join and that even Nasha Town's merchants, normally indifferent to the public interest, are starting to see why the Lightkeepers fight. Nothing in 7.0-7.1 changes the city.
### ULTRA +
Illuga is expected to speak for the Lightkeepers in Nod-Krai's new joint League after Dottore's defeat, at Flins' urging.

## entry: The Arrow of Terpikeraunas and the Mead Hall
@keys: Arrow of Terpikeraunas, Spiralblade, Great Mead Hall
@keys2: Piramida, Lightkeepers, Nikita
@version: 6.3
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Nikita explains that the Torchforger, five centuries ago, could only seal the undying fiend beneath Kipumaki Cliff, and so he and the first Moonchanter forged a bow and arrow to finish the job later. The mead hall was built to house it, and the Spiralblade recovered in the quests is its missing arrowhead. With the Spiralblade set in place, the lighthouse becomes a huge cannon, aimed at the Cataclysm Egg atop the Pillar of Embla.
### LARGE +
Defending the arrow, the Traveler and Illuga fight through Abyssal monsters and the Ultimate Calamity in the plaza, riding Moonlanes between amplifiers. The shot destroys the egg. At the pillar's top Alia, who takes the name Reed Miller, confronts the 'Professor', and both fade away.

## entry: The Lightkeepers' Oath
@keys: Lightkeepers' oath, Oath Lantern, Starshyna
@keys2: Piramida, Ratnik, Lightkeeper
### STANDARD
Every Lightkeeper swears an oath on joining, which speaks of foreseeing a night sky devoured by silence, vowing to burn their bones and blood as fuel to make life greater than death, and ends with their promise to shine the light of hope into the snow-swept plains. Ratniki carry Oath Lanterns that let comrades and the Starshyna find them. The Starshyna is the leader, then Sergeant Majors, Squad Leaders and Ratniki.
