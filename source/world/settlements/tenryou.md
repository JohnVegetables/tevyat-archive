## summary
### LITE
Tenryou is the commercial heart of Inazuma City, the district below Tenshukaku built around a bustling commercial street. It is one of the city's three parts, with Hanamizaka and Tenshukaku. Adventurers, shoppers, watchmen and Shogunate officials mix here, among teahouses, restaurants, craftsmen, the Adventurers' Guild and a police station.
### STANDARD +
The word tenryou is not the same as the Tenryou Commission, though they share a root: the Commission is the arm of the Shogunate that keeps public order, and the district is where much of that order is visible, in its watchmen, notices and the nearby Commission headquarters.

## geography
### STANDARD
Tenryou lies just below Tenshukaku on the city's hillside, where Inazuma City rises on the southern tip of Narukami Island. Its main feature is a commercial street. Komore Teahouse, Uyuu Restaurant, the Aisa Bathhouse, the police station and a park lie off it.
### LARGE +
The Tenryou Commission Headquarters is classed under Tenshukaku rather than Tenryou, but its watchmen are seen in the district constantly. A small park area is home to a resident named Oda Tarou.

## places
### STANDARD
Commercial street: the Adventurers' Guild receptionist Katheryne, shop owners and street-food vendors.
Komore Teahouse: a members-only teahouse owned by the Yashiro Commission, used by the Kamisato clan as a local base and private meeting place. Its boss is a Shiba Inu named Taroumaru.
Aisa Bathhouse: a Natlan-inspired bathhouse owned and run by the returnee Aikawa Susumu, with rumours of a special investor behind the scenes.
Uyuu Restaurant: run by Okazaki Rikuto and Okazaki Erika.
Police Station: the yoriki Owada and the watchman Uesugi work here.
### LARGE +
Shops include Ogura Textiles & Kimonos (Ogura Mio), Yayoi Lacquerware (Yayoi Nanatsuki), Tsukumomono Groceries (Aoi), Netsuke no Gen Crafts (Mikoshi Genichirou) and Shimura's (Shimura Kanbei), plus street-food vendors such as Yukio and Tomoki. The teahouse's staff includes Kozue, a shop assistant who is the subject of a bulletin board joke and a police file.
### ULTRA +
The Komore Teahouse is said, in official materials, to have been famous among dog lovers before the Sakoku Decree, with visitors from overseas coming to see Taroumaru. The teahouse's furnishings are described in several furnishing items.

## people
### STANDARD
Adventurers such as Furuya Noboru, Satou, Takeda and Yuzu use the street; the researcher Tang Wen, the gourmet Matsumoto and the watchman Sanada are among the regulars. The Guild receptionist Katheryne is here.
### LARGE +
Komore Teahouse patrons and staff include Heihachi, Nakanishi, Nobumori and Ramsay. Taroumaru is a former ninja dog of the Shuumatsuban.

## governance
### STANDARD
Tenryou is the city district the Tenryou Commission watches most closely. The Commission, led by the Kujou clan, manages security and military affairs and enforced the Vision Hunt and Sakoku Decrees. Its yoriki and doushin are the police of the district.
### LARGE +
After the Inazuma Archon Quest, Tenryou Commissioner Kujou Takayuki is under house arrest for colluding with the Fatui and Kujou Kamaji acts in his place; Ei challenged the Takatsukasa clan head for the post, Kamaji accepted the duel instead, and Ei decided to give the Kujou clan more time. A Tenryou notice about Takayuki was posted on the commercial street's bulletin board.
### ULTRA +
During the war, the Grand Narukami Shrine posted notices offering aid loans and halved food aid to businesses and families hurt by the fighting, directing applicants to the Kamisato clan, and said that its Yae Publishing House reader forum would welcome people from both the Shogunate and the rebel side. A bulletin board message praising Kozue was answered by a police notice about her lawsuit.

## culture
### STANDARD
Tenryou is the city's shopping and dining quarter. Inazuma City is renowned for family businesses and a low crime rate, and Tenryou's street has lacquerware, textile and netsuke shops, a grocer, a restaurant, a teahouse and street-food vendors.
### LARGE +
The Aisa Bathhouse is called a go-to place to relax and is visited by Tenryou Commission samurai.

## history
### STANDARD
The district's recent history is the war period under the Decrees, then peace after the Archon Quest. Later, after the Natlan events, the Aisa Bathhouse was renovated at the direction of Yumemizuki Mizuki, who became its main investor and decision-maker, adding therapy sessions run by yumekui-baku psychologists.
### LARGE +
Yumemizuki Mizuki is working to expand the brand to Watatsumi Island and to Natlan.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
A busy street of shops below the Shogun's castle, with street-food vendors, watchmen in the crowd and adventurers coming and going. During the war the same street had a sharper edge, with notices, aid queues and rumours.

## dangers
### LARGE
Inazuma City is renowned for low crime. The district's dangers belong to the Decree era: arrests by the Tenryou Commission and its warrants and wanted notices.

## travel
### STANDARD
The Adventurers' Guild's Inazuma receptionist, Katheryne, works on the commercial street, making it the usual first stop for travellers.

## quests
### LARGE
The Traveler is wanted by the Tenryou Commission after escaping the Vision Hunt Ceremony; later, the Komore Teahouse's staff figure in the quest "Three Wishes", and the Aisa Bathhouse in the quest "Dream Eater's Melancholia".

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 Tenryou is a safe, prosperous market district; the Decrees are over.
### STANDARD +
The Aisa Bathhouse is now a therapy-and-spa venue under Yumemizuki Mizuki. No 6.x or 7.0-7.1 plot is set in the district.

## entry: Komore Teahouse
@keys: Komore Teahouse, Taroumaru
@version: 2.0
### STANDARD
Komore Teahouse is a members-only teahouse in Tenryou, owned by the Yashiro Commission and used by the Kamisato clan as a local base and private meeting place. Only people approved by the Kamisato clan may enter. Its boss is the Shiba Inu Taroumaru, a former ninja dog of the Shuumatsuban, and customers leave their payment in a box after drinking tea.
### LARGE +
Staff are said to have signed a contract with Taroumaru to work there forever, a joke repeated by the shop assistant Kozue. The teahouse is mentioned in the descriptions of several furnishings, such as candlestands that burn without scent.

## entry: Aisa Bathhouse
@keys: Aisa Bathhouse, Aikawa Susumu
@version: 2.0
### STANDARD
The Aisa Bathhouse is an indoor bathhouse in Tenryou inspired by Natlan's hot springs, offering baths, fine dining and, since its renovation, psychotherapy from yumekui-baku. Aikawa Susumu, a returnee from Natlan, is the operator.
### LARGE +
After the events of Incandescent Ode of Resurrection, the bathhouse was renovated at the direction of Yumemizuki Mizuki, its core investor and decision-maker, who is expanding the brand to Watatsumi Island and Natlan. Many Tenryou Commission samurai visit it.
