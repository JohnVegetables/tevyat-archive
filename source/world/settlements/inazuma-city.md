## summary
### LITE
Inazuma City is the capital of Inazuma, built on cliffs at the southern tip of Narukami Island. Most of the nation's people live here, and it is Inazuma's liveliest and most prosperous place, known for low crime and a great many family businesses. The city rises in three parts: the suburb of Hanamizaka, the commercial streets of Tenryou, and at the top Tenshukaku, the castle where the Raiden Shogun resides and rules.
### STANDARD +
The Tenryou Commission, which handles public order, security and the Shogunate's military affairs, has its headquarters inside the Tenshukaku complex. In front of the castle stands the Statue of the Omnipresent God, built as a symbol of Eternity during the Vision Hunt Decree.

## geography
### STANDARD
The city climbs from the harbour up the cliffs. Hanamizaka ("Flower-Viewing Slope") is a suburb north of the river that cuts through the northern part of the city. Tenryou lies just below Tenshukaku, a tangle of streets and lanes that all converge before the castle, which stands on the highest point and looks out over the whole of the Shogun's domain. Mt. Yougou and the Grand Narukami Shrine rise inland on the same island; Ritou, the foreigners' port of entry, is also on Narukami Island.
### LARGE +
The population keeps growing, so the city expands outward. Simple, easily built houses have spread through the newer districts; families are meant to enlarge them once they have saved enough, though few have managed it in recent years. The outskirts keep an older, unhurried feel along old paths that the city's prosperity has barely touched.

## places
### STANDARD
Hanamizaka: Amenoma Smithy (a Raiden Gokaden bladesmithing school), Naganohara Fireworks (Yoimiya's family shop), Yae Publishing House, Kiminami Restaurant and the Bantan Sango Detective Agency. Tenryou: the commercial street with the Adventurers' Guild desk (Katheryne), shops for kimonos, lacquerware, netsuke and groceries, street-food stalls, Uyuu Restaurant, the Police Station, Komore Teahouse and the Aisa Bathhouse. Tenshukaku: the Shogun's residence, the Tenryou Commission Headquarters, the castle's hot springs and the Statue of the Omnipresent God.
### LARGE +
Shops named on the street include Ogura Textiles & Kimonos, Yayoi Lacquerware, Netsuke no Gen Crafts, Shimura's and Tsukumomono Groceries. Tomoki's stall sells Dango Milk. The Police Station (Bugyou-sho, "Commissioner's Office") keeps law and order and held prisoners such as the Vision forger Masakatsu. A Meikyou Shisui Art dojo, Domon's, is also in the city.

## people
### STANDARD
Merchants, craftsfolk, artisans and family-run businesses make up much of the city; doushin and yoriki of the Tenryou Commission patrol the streets, and Hatamoto and Okuzumeshuu guard Tenshukaku. Notable residents or regulars include Yoimiya of Naganohara Fireworks, Yae Miko's editors at Yae Publishing House, the smith Amenoma Tougo, and Katheryne at the guild desk.
### LARGE +
The Shogun herself does not patrol: in Ei's words that is the Tenryou Commission's job, and the Shogun has no need of recreation. When Ei walked the market in person (2.1), shopkeepers were startled to see the "Almighty Shogun" in the street. Komore Teahouse's famous "boss" is Taroumaru, a Shiba Inu said to be a former Shuumatsuban ninja dog.

## governance
### STANDARD
The city is the seat of the Inazuma Shogunate. The Raiden Shogun governs from Tenshukaku, and the three commissions of the Tri-Commission meet there. The Tenryou Commission keeps order in the city, runs the Police Station and enforced both the Vision Hunt and the Sakoku decrees.
### LARGE +
A duel before the throne can be fought at Tenshukaku with the Shogun's approval; its victor is granted a second chance, which is how the Traveler survived their confrontation with Signora. In the days when Inazuman houses were mostly wooden, the Shogun charged the Tenryou Commission with setting up fire brigades; today a Fire Brigade notice outside Naganohara Fireworks warns Yoimiya against further "risky behaviour".

## culture
### LARGE
The city is the centre of Inazuman commerce and popular culture: fireworks, light novels (Yae Publishing House runs the "This Novel Is Amazing!" contest), traditional crafts and street food. Komore Teahouse is a members-only teahouse owned by the Yashiro Commission and used by the Kamisato Clan as a private meeting place; guests leave payment in a box. The old Fire-Soothing Festival Committee, once chaired by the Tenryou Commission and managed by the Grand Narukami Shrine, prayed for a year without fires; Yae Miko later turned its festival into a light novel contest and "borrowed" its budget for the Yae Publishing House.

## history
### STANDARD
During the Archon War, youkai under the banner of Narukami used their powers to help humans who had sought refuge in the mountains build a city by the sea; Yae Miko's story calls this the beginning of Inazuma. Tenshukaku dates from the same war. The Shogun closed the nation with the Sakoku Decree, and about a year before the Traveler arrived she issued the Vision Hunt Decree; the Statue of the Omnipresent God was raised before Tenshukaku to hold the confiscated Visions. In the Archon Quest (2.0-2.1) the Traveler confronted the Shogun at the Vision Hunt ceremony, Signora was executed after a duel before the throne, and the Traveler defeated Ei, after which the Visions were returned.
### LARGE +
A tale of the Kitsune Saiguu's era has her goading the bake-danuki Ioroi into plucking a Naku Weed from the Tenshukaku garden, where Ei caught him. About five hundred years ago Lord Hiiragi Hiroshi of the Kanjou Commission gave the Shogun a Snezhnayan blast furnace that heats Tenshukaku's hot springs. The Yae Publishing House is no more than about five hundred years old. After the Sakoku Decree ended (2.5) the city opened to foreign visitors again. By 5.4 the Natlan-inspired Aisa Bathhouse in Tenryou had been renovated with Yumemizuki Mizuki as its core investor.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
The bustle of family shops and street stalls below, the castle looming above. The official viewpoint text describes Tenryou's lanes all gathering beneath the "eternal and silent gaze" of the Shogun. Since the decrees ended the mood is open and busy; under them, Vision bearers were stopped in the street and the Tenryou Commission's patrols set the tone.

## dangers
### LARGE
Inside the city, danger is mostly political or criminal: the Tenryou Commission's enforcement under the old decrees, Fatui intrigue, Treasure Hoarders and petty fraud of the kind the Bantan Sango detectives take on. The countryside beyond has the usual monsters; within the city itself, crime is famously low.

## quests
### STANDARD
Chapter II Act I (2.0) brings the Traveler to the city to visit people who lost their Visions; Act II opens with the Vision Hunt ceremony; Act III (2.1) ends with the duel before the throne and the battle with Ei. Many Inazuma story quests are set here, including those of the Raiden Shogun, Yoimiya, Yae Miko, Kamisato Ayaka, Kamisato Ayato, Kaedehara Kazuha, Arataki Itto and Yumemizuki Mizuki.
### ULTRA +
In the 3.3 Interlude (A Dance of Destruction) the Traveler visited Amenoma Tougo at his smithy to learn how the Raiden Gokaden's history had changed after the Wanderer erased himself from Irminsul. World quests include Chisato's Letter (the Ritou travel permit, delivered to Kujou Kamaji, with snacks collected at Kiminami Restaurant), the Bantan Sango case files and the Yae Publishing House requests.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 Inazuma City is peaceful and open. The Vision Hunt Decree was abolished in 2.1 and the Sakoku Decree in 2.5; the Shogun still rules from Tenshukaku.
### STANDARD +
Ei now takes a more direct interest in her people. The confiscated Visions were removed from the statue and returned to their owners. No new crisis in the city appears in the Nod-Krai (6.x) or Snezhnaya (7.0-7.1) storylines.

## entry: Tenshukaku
@keys: Tenshukaku, Shogun's castle, Tenryou Commission Headquarters
### STANDARD
Tenshukaku ("heavenly protector tower", the keep of a Japanese-style castle) is the Raiden Shogun's residence and the seat of Inazuma's government, built during the Archon War on the highest point of Inazuma City. The Tenryou Commission Headquarters lie within the complex, guarded by Hatamoto, doushin and yoriki; Okuzumeshuu of the Shogun's personal guard are posted there too. Officials such as a tax secretary and a member of the Royal Bath Commission work in the central courtyard. Duels before the throne are fought here.
### LARGE +
Its hot springs are heated by a Snezhnayan blast furnace that Lord Hiiragi Hiroshi of the Kanjou Commission imported as a gift for the Shogun about five hundred years ago. The Shogun's private interior was seen in the Raiden Shogun's story quest (2.1), when the Shogun was indisposed and the Traveler drew Ei out of the Plane of Euthymia.

## entry: Hanamizaka and Tenryou
@keys: Hanamizaka, Tenryou, Kiminami Restaurant, Uyuu Restaurant, Bantan Sango, Ogura Textiles, Tomoki
@keys2: Inazuma City, Inazuma, Narukami
### STANDARD
Hanamizaka is the suburb north of the river in the city's north, home to Naganohara Fireworks, Amenoma Smithy, Yae Publishing House, Kiminami Restaurant (owner Kiminami Anna) and the Bantan Sango Detective Agency (detectives Sango and Ryuuji). Tenryou is the commercial district below Tenshukaku: the Adventurers' Guild desk, Uyuu Restaurant (owner Okazaki Rikuto), clothing, lacquer and craft shops, food stalls, the Police Station, Komore Teahouse and the Aisa Bathhouse.

## entry: Statue of the Omnipresent God
@keys: Statue of the Omnipresent God, Statue of the Thousand-Armed, Hundred-Eyed God
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
A huge statue raised in front of Tenshukaku after the Vision Hunt Decree, into which every confiscated Vision was set as the "symbol of Eternity". Thoma told the Traveler it was still under construction when they arrived, and calls it the symbol of Eternity. The public Vision Hunt ceremony for Thoma was held before it. After the Traveler defeated Ei (2.1), the Visions were removed and returned to their owners. Who the statue depicts is disputed (see uncertainties).

## entry: Komore Teahouse
@keys: Komore Teahouse, Taroumaru
### STANDARD
A members-only teahouse in a prime spot on Inazuma City's streets, owned by the Yashiro Commission and used by the Kamisato Clan as a local base and private meeting place; only those the Kamisato approve may enter. Its nominal boss is Taroumaru, a Shiba Inu much admired by dog lovers and said to be a former Shuumatsuban ninja dog. During the 2.0-2.1 Archon Quest Ayaka and Thoma planned their covert operations against the Vision Hunt Decree here with the Traveler, and Thoma took refuge here after the Vision Hunt ceremony.

## entry: Naganohara Fireworks
@keys: Naganohara Fireworks, Naganohara Fireworks Show
### STANDARD
Yoimiya's family business in Hanamizaka, described as the oldest and one of the best fireworks shops in Inazuma. It also sells toys and knickknacks, some imported through the Crux Fleet and some made by Yoimiya, and Yoimiya has had fireworks shipped overseas to spread the shop's name. The fire brigade's warning notice outside asks her to stop risky behaviour.

## entry: Yae Publishing House
@keys: Yae Publishing House, This Novel Is Amazing!
### STANDARD
A giant of Inazuma's publishing industry in Hanamizaka, founded and owned by Yae Miko, chief priestess of the Grand Narukami Shrine. It publishes light novels, holds the "This Novel Is Amazing!" competition, and serialises foreign works such as Xingqiu's A Legend of Sword (under the pen name Zhenyu). It has existed for no more than about five hundred years. Ei visited it during her walk through the city (2.1) to sample modern novels.

## entry: Amenoma Smithy
@keys: Amenoma Smithy, Amenoma Art, Amenoma Tougo
### STANDARD
The smithy of the Amenoma Art, one of the five schools of the Raiden Gokaden, on the eastern end of the city in Hanamizaka. Its owner, Amenoma Tougo, teaches that the Art has no secret tricks, only endless practice and willpower, like water wearing stone to sand. His disciple Hajime takes everyday orders.
### LARGE +
The Amenoma Art is the only Gokaden school that came through the Wanderer's old revenge against the bladesmiths unscathed. In the 3.3 Interlude, after the Wanderer erased himself from Irminsul, Tougo told the Traveler the revised history: disaster at Tatarasuna four hundred years ago, and about a hundred years ago a vengeful smith's descendant who murdered Gokaden smiths until the Kamisato and Kaedehara clans stopped him.

## entry: The Vision Hunt Ceremony and the Duel Before the Throne
@keys: Vision Hunt ceremony, Duel Before the Throne, Musou no Hitotachi
@keys2: Inazuma City, Tenshukaku, Raiden Shogun, Signora, Thoma
@version: 2.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
At a public ceremony before the Statue of the Omnipresent God, Thoma was to be the hundredth person stripped of a Vision. The Traveler intervened and used Electro without a Vision; the Shogun named them "an exception", an enemy of eternity, and defeated them, but Thoma helped them escape (2.0). Later (2.1) Sara learned that Kujou Takayuki had betrayed the Shogunate, the Traveler fought through the Tenryou Commission Headquarters with her, and at Tenshukaku the Traveler won a duel before the throne against the Harbinger Signora, whom the Shogun then executed with the Musou no Hitotachi. Outside, Kaedehara Kazuha blocked the Shogun's strike with his late friend's Vision, the Traveler was drawn into the Plane of Euthymia and defeated Ei, and the Vision Hunt Decree ended.

## entry: Ei's Walk Through the City
@keys: To Traverse the Mortal Plane, Dango Milk
@keys2: Ei, Raiden Shogun, Inazuma City
@version: 2.1
### LARGE
In her story quest, with the Shogun puppet indisposed, Ei came out of the Plane of Euthymia and toured the market with the Traveler. She found the shops and vendors much changed from her memory, startled the townsfolk, tried Dango Milk at Tomoki's stall and visited the Yae Publishing House to learn about light novels.

## entry: Aisa Bathhouse
@keys: Aisa Bathhouse, Aikawa Susumu
@version: 5.4
### LARGE
An indoor bathhouse in Tenryou inspired by Natlan's hot springs, offering baths, fine dining and therapy from yumekui-baku psychologists. It is run by Aikawa Susumu, a returnee from Natlan. After a major renovation, the yumekui-baku psychologist Yumemizuki Mizuki became its core founding investor and decision-maker. Its official description says everyone from high officials to commoners comes to unwind. Its troubles are the subject of Mizuki's story quest (5.4).
