## summary
### LITE
Liyue Harbor is the capital of Liyue: a vast, prosperous port on the coast beneath Mt. Tianheng, founded by the Geo Archon Morax (Rex Lapis) and now governed by the merchant council of the Liyue Qixing. It is Teyvat's largest market harbour, where a thousand ships drop anchor and goods from every nation change hands under a dense web of contracts. Its three districts are the clifftop Yujing Terrace (wealth and government), the merchant quarter of Feiyun Slope, and the working-class Chihu Rock by the docks. Ningguang's floating palace, the Jade Chamber, hovers above the city.
### STANDARD +
Every year the Lantern Rite fills the night sky over the harbour with thousands of lanterns. Since the Rite of Descension at which Rex Lapis was apparently killed, the city has run without a living god's guidance; in truth he lives here quietly as Zhongli.

## geography
### STANDARD
The harbour lies in the Sea of Clouds area, between the sea and the cliffs of Mt. Tianheng. Yujing Terrace climbs the western cliffside; Feiyun Slope sits in the middle and is the entrance from the northern road; Chihu Rock is an island to the southeast, bridged to Feiyun Slope and Mt. Tianheng.
### LARGE +
Bubu Pharmacy stands on its own small island at the crossroads below Yujing Terrace, where a road also leaves the city toward the Guizhong Ballista. The Guyun Stone Forest lies offshore. Glaze lilies and silk flowers grow around the city. Ships moor along the southeastern wharf and the northern wharf of Feiyun Slope. The Golden House and the Jade Chamber are officially counted as part of Mt. Tianheng rather than the city proper.

## places
### STANDARD
Yujing Terrace holds Yuehai Pavilion, seat of the Qixing. Feiyun Slope has the shops, guilds, restaurants, Heyu Tea House, the Northland Bank and Wangsheng Funeral Parlor. Chihu Rock has Wanmin Restaurant, street food, the Adventurers' Guild and the docks. Bubu Pharmacy is the city's famous pharmacy.
### LARGE +
In detail:
- Yujing Terrace: Yuehai Pavilion (the Qixing and their secretaries), Yiyan Temple, and the way up to the Jade Chamber.
- Feiyun Slope: the commercial boulevard, Feiyun Commerce Guild (Xingqiu's family), the Ministry of Civil Affairs, Liuli Pavilion and Xinyue Kiosk (fine dining), Wanwen Bookhouse, Mingxing Jewelry, Heyu Tea House (opera), a Snezhnayan merchant area and the Northland Bank on the top level, and a Maritime Authority office at the northern wharf.
- Chihu Rock: Hanfeng's Ironmongers, Jade Mystery, Third-Round Knockout (a storyteller's teahouse), Yanshang Teahouse, the dockyard, warehouses and the fish market.
- Bubu Pharmacy, called "the finest pharmacy in all of Liyue", run by Baizhu.
- Above the city: the Golden House, Teyvat's only mint, and the floating Jade Chamber.
### ULTRA +
Smaller shops include Second Life, Xigu Antiques, Wanyou Boutique and the Baiju Guesthouse. A cracked stone orb beneath a guardian lion by the docks carries a polite notice asking people not to kick it. Notice boards in Chihu Rock and Feiyun Slope carry public notices, and posters around town have included a lost-dog appeal and a missing-person notice for the Traveler's twin directing informants to the Millelith or the Ministry of Civil Affairs.

## people
### STANDARD
Residents include the Qixing (Ningguang, Keqing, Uncle Tian) and their secretary Ganyu; Zhongli and Hu Tao of Wangsheng Funeral Parlor; Xiangling of Wanmin Restaurant; Xingqiu; Baizhu and Qiqi; and Yun Jin. Beidou's Crux berths here.
### LARGE +
Ningguang is the Tianquan, Keqing the Yuheng and Uncle Tian the Tianshu, who can be met by the harbour in daytime. Xiangling is the chef at Wanmin Restaurant, which her father Chef Mao runs; Xingqiu belongs to the Feiyun Commerce Guild; Yun Jin is an opera singer. Katheryne staffs the Adventurers' Guild desk in Chihu Rock. The Millelith garrison the streets, the Terrace and the wharves. The Northland Bank branch is run by Andrei as manager with Ekaterina at reception; Tartaglia (Childe) has helped it collect debts. Yelan owns Yanshang Teahouse as a front for her intelligence work. Madame Ping, an old adeptus (Streetward Rambler), can be found on Yujing Terrace in the daytime. The adeptus Cloud Retainer lives in the city in human form as Xianyun, and her disciple Shenhe has been trying to find her place here.
### ULTRA +
Many families came from elsewhere: the survivors of Havria's people who escaped salinification became the salt trade's Yinyuan Hall; the people of Dunyu abandoned their city and moved here; and within the last few decades the last villagers of Wuwang Hill did the same. Iron Tongue Tian tells stories at Third-Round Knockout; Fan Er'ye, a retired performer, owns Heyu Tea House.

## governance
### LARGE
The Liyue Qixing govern Liyue from Yuehai Pavilion. The Ministry of Civil Affairs handles everyday administration, and the Millelith serve as army and police. Since the Rite of Descension, the Qixing rule without Rex Lapis' yearly guidance and the adepti have accepted mortal rule. Ningguang watches the city and the nation from the Jade Chamber, whose intelligence wall covers shipping schedules, accounts, warehouse inventories, road safety reports and diplomatic intelligence. The Qixing also run a quiet network of agents, including the staff of Wangshu Inn and Yelan.

## culture
### STANDARD
Commerce and contracts define the city. The Lantern Rite, Liyue's five-day new year festival from the first full moon, is the greatest celebration: people release Xiao Lanterns and raise the giant Mingxiao Lantern in remembrance of the fallen. Opera, teahouses, storytelling and food thrive.
### LARGE +
Releasing lanterns began as a wartime custom to guide soldiers home. The Moonchase Festival is held in autumn. Until his apparent death, Rex Lapis descended to the city once a year at the Rite of Descension to give his predictions for the coming year's trade; it was open to everyone and drew crowds to Yujing Terrace. Each Lantern Rite the Mingxiao Lantern is built from materials donated by citizens and visitors and usually depicts an adeptus who died protecting Liyue; its size depends on the largest piece of plaustrite found in the previous year. After the festival, Millelith gather up the released lanterns so they do not become litter.

## history
### STANDARD
About 3,700 years ago, after the Guili Assembly on the Guili Plains was destroyed in the Archon War and Guizhong died, Morax, Marchosius and the adepti led the survivors south of Mt. Tianheng and founded Liyue Harbor. Morax raised the Millelith, made a contract with the adepti to defend the city and minted Mora. When the Traveler arrived, Rex Lapis' body fell from the sky during the Rite of Descension; the "death" was staged by Morax, who then gave his Gnosis to the Fatui after Childe released the sea god Osial against the city and Ningguang dropped the Jade Chamber onto it.
### LARGE +
Legend says Morax taught the first settlers to build houses using a model cast from Mora, because he could create Mora at will. After the god-war, the first gathering of the Seven often met in Liyue Harbor. After the Osial crisis the Golden House stopped minting Mora, and the Northland Bank was made to pay heavy reparations for Childe's actions. Months later the Jade Chamber was rebuilt, and on the day it rose Osial's mate Beisht attacked; this time mortals, with Shenhe, fought her off and she fled badly wounded (2.4).

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Crowded wharves, hawkers and haggling, teahouses and opera, street food in Chihu Rock, merchants and foreigners on Feiyun Slope, and the stately calm of Yujing Terrace beneath the floating Jade Chamber.

## dangers
### LARGE
The city itself is well guarded, but its history shows its risks: sea monsters sealed in the Guyun Stone Forest offshore, Fatui intrigue around the Northland Bank, and intrigue among merchants. Petty swindlers and gamblers work its streets, and Yanshang Teahouse in Chihu Rock is a casino disguised as a teahouse.

## travel
### LARGE
The main road from the north (via Stone Gate and Wangshu Inn) enters at Feiyun Slope. Ships from every nation dock at the wharves; Beidou's Crux sails from here.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 Liyue Harbor is prosperous and at peace under the Qixing. Rex Lapis is publicly believed dead, but lives in the city as Zhongli. The Jade Chamber has been rebuilt and again floats above the harbour.
### STANDARD +
The Golden House has stopped minting Mora. Cloud Retainer lives in the city as Xianyun. The White Horse Adeptus Zibai, restored during the 6.3 Lantern Rite event, is now associated with Liyue Harbor. The Archon Quest has moved to Snezhnaya (Chapter VII); no new crisis in the harbour is shown there.
### LARGE +
After the 7.1 Celestial Nail anomaly, Zhongli (as Rex Lapis) sent Xiao to watch the Chasm, extra Millelith were deployed on patrols there, and Cloud Retainer and other adepti began investigating; nothing has happened in the harbour itself.

## entry: Yujing Terrace (Liyue Harbor)
@keys: Yujing Terrace, Yuehai Pavilion, Yiyan Temple
### STANDARD
Yujing Terrace is the westernmost and wealthiest district of Liyue Harbor, built along the cliffs of Mt. Tianheng above the harbour. It holds Yuehai Pavilion, seat of the Liyue Qixing and their secretaries, and Yiyan Temple, where a guide admits visitors to the Jade Chamber. The Rite of Descension was held here each year, and it was here that Rex Lapis' body fell before the crowd.
### LARGE +
At the crossroads by its entrance, roads lead to Feiyun Slope, to the island of Bubu Pharmacy and out of the city toward the Guizhong Ballista.

## entry: Feiyun Slope
@keys: Feiyun Slope
### STANDARD
Feiyun Slope is Liyue Harbor's prosperous merchant district and the city's entrance from the northern road. Along its tiers stand the Ministry of Civil Affairs, Liuli Pavilion and Xinyue Kiosk, Wanwen Bookhouse, Mingxing Jewelry, Heyu Tea House (where the Yun-Han Opera Troupe performs), the Northland Bank on the top level, a Snezhnayan merchant area, Wangsheng Funeral Parlor, and the northern wharf.
### ULTRA +
Its name ("scarlet clouds") is written with different characters from that of the Feiyun Commerce Guild ("flying clouds"), though they sound the same in Chinese.

## entry: Chihu Rock
@keys: Chihu Rock
### STANDARD
Chihu Rock is Liyue Harbor's lower-class district, on an island southeast of the rest of the city, bridged to Feiyun Slope and Mt. Tianheng. It is the city's working heart: Wanmin Restaurant and food stalls, the Adventurers' Guild branch, blacksmiths, a jade shop, the storytelling teahouse Third-Round Knockout, Yanshang Teahouse, the dockyard, warehouses and the fish market.

## entry: Jade Chamber
@keys: Jade Chamber
### STANDARD
The Jade Chamber is the floating palace of the Tianquan Ningguang, hovering over Liyue Harbor, from which she monitors happenings across Liyue; her famous intelligence wall is kept there. It floats on a great chunk of Sunset Vermillionite, a kind of plaustrite, and is steered by Adepti Sigils and Wonder Cores. Ningguang sacrificed it to crush Osial, and its weight and stored energy defeated him. She later rebuilt it (2.4), partly to lure out Beisht, and the second chamber survived the battle.
### LARGE +
Many people now see the rebuilt Jade Chamber as a symbol both of Ningguang's power and of the harbour's will. The Millelith had to clear wreckage of the first chamber from the sea. For the rebuilding, Ningguang offered to answer one question for each of the first three people to bring her materials; the Traveler, Shenhe, Yun Jin and Beidou won.

## entry: The Golden House
@keys: Golden House
### STANDARD
The Golden House, on Mt. Tianheng above the harbour, is where all of Teyvat's Mora was minted using the Geo Archon's power. After Rex Lapis' "death" the Qixing moved his body, the Exuvia, there, and the Fatui tried to strike it during the Archon Quest. Since he gave up his Gnosis it has stopped producing new Mora; Zhongli says existing Mora will not vanish and leaves the problem to the Qixing.

## entry: The Rite of Descension and the Osial Crisis
@keys: Rite of Descension, Osial, Overlord of the Vortex
@keys2: Liyue Harbor, Rex Lapis, Jade Chamber, Childe, Tartaglia
@version: 1.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
When the Traveler arrived, Liyue Harbor was preparing the yearly Rite of Descension at Yujing Terrace. Instead of the god appearing, Rex Lapis' body fell dead before the crowd; the Millelith sealed off the Terrace and hunted suspects, and the Traveler fled with Childe's help. Childe, believing he was flushing out the god, used stolen Sigils of Permission to free Osial from the Guyun Stone Forest. As the monster bore down on the harbour, the Qixing, Millelith and adepti fought together and Ningguang dropped the Jade Chamber onto it. Zhongli then presided over the Rite of Parting and handed the Geo Gnosis to Signora under his contract with the Tsaritsa.
### LARGE +
The whole affair was Morax's test of whether Liyue could stand without him. Afterwards the Qixing assumed full governance and the adepti accepted it.

### LARGE
Months after the Osial crisis, as the rebuilt Jade Chamber rose over Liyue Harbor, a storm brought Beisht, Osial's mate. Ningguang, warned beforehand by Beidou, was ready and chose to defend the city with mortal forces: the Chamber's weapons, Keqing, Ganyu, the Millelith and the Crux. Shenhe froze Beisht's tsunami, and the Traveler and Shenhe followed the creature into its vortex and beat it; Beisht fled badly wounded. Cloud Retainer praised Ningguang's foresight, and a victory banquet followed on the Jade Chamber, where Yun Jin performed "The Divine Damsel of Devastation".

## entry: Lantern Rite in Liyue Harbor
@keys: Lantern Rite, Mingxiao Lantern, Xiao Lantern
@keys2: Liyue Harbor
@availability: limited
### LARGE
The harbour is the centre of the Lantern Rite, and several limited-time events have shown it. In 1.3 the Mingxiao Lantern honoured the adeptus Skybracer while Xiao fought the surge of evil that erupts each festival. In 2.4 (Fleeting Colors in Flight) Treasure Hoarders stole the festival fireworks and Keqing, with the Traveler's help, recovered them. In 3.4 an international concert, the Iridescence Tour's Lantern Rite Grand Concert, was staged with Xinyan and Hu Tao opening. In 4.4 the Mingxiao Lantern depicted the Yaksha Menogias, and Xianyun had settled in the city. In 5.3 the lantern unusually took the form of the god Tao Dou. In 6.3 the Traveler and Zhongli restored the White Horse Adeptus Zibai, who then toured the city and its surroundings.
### ULTRA +
These event stories are canon, but not every character will have witnessed them; treat them as remembered festivals rather than common knowledge.

## entry: Northland Bank and Yanshang Teahouse
@keys: Northland Bank, Yanshang Teahouse
@keys2: Liyue Harbor, Liyue, Childe, Tartaglia, Yelan, Ekaterina
### LARGE
The Liyue branch of the Snezhnayan Northland Bank, at the top of Feiyun Slope, lends generously and has long served the Fatui as a base; rivals claim it used underhanded methods to establish itself. Ekaterina will not admit any Fatui ties but concedes Harbingers are among its clients. After Childe's attack, Ningguang made the bank pay heavy reparations. Shortly before the Traveler arrived, Childe collected a debt from the owner of Yanshang Teahouse in Chihu Rock (a casino posing as a teahouse), took his Sigil of Permission and exposed his fraud. With the owner jailed, Yelan took over the teahouse after Osial's defeat as a secret base, relaxing its rules.

## entry: Zibai's Visit to the Harbor

@version: 6.3
@availability: limited
@spoiler: minor
### ULTRA
During the Lantern Rite of 6.3, after her restoration, the White Horse Adeptus Zibai, an old friend of Zhongli's, toured the harbour, Yilong Wharf, Qiaoying Village and Qingce Village with the Traveler and Paimon; they then flew by her jade chariot to Wangshu Inn, ate with Xiao and stayed the night before returning to the harbour. Her three spirits had only just merged and not yet settled, so her moods shifted abruptly. She is now associated with Liyue Harbor.
