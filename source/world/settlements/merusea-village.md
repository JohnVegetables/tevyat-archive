## summary
### LITE
Merusea Village is the home of the Melusines, the small amphibious all-female people of Fontaine. It lies underwater, inside the body of the dead beast Elynas, and the game calls it "an adorable and mysterious village where even the chimneys bubble". It has no official leader. Very few humans ever visit, but those who do tend to enjoy it.
### STANDARD +
The village can be entered only from underwater, by diving near Elynas' skull or in the pool just above the Beryl Region Statue of the Seven. Most Melusines live here, though many who work in the Court of Fontaine now live in the city.

## geography
### STANDARD
Merusea Village has a northern zone (on land, where most villagers are found) and a southern zone (underwater), within the colossal remains of Elynas that fill nearly all of the Beryl Region. Local plants include the Romaritime Flower and Beryl Conch.
### LARGE +
Elynas' skeleton and body are far larger than those of the dragon Orobashi. The Melusines' homes and the village itself are inside his remains.

## places
### STANDARD
The village has human-inspired structures made long ago by the Melusines: street lamps (installed because visitors found the space darker than they were used to) and swing sets. A vendor, Xana, trades goods for mechanical "components".
### LARGE +
Melusines treat scrap mechanical parts as currency because of their abundance and prettiness; they exchange them for goods and give them to each other as tokens of thanks.

## people
### STANDARD
Residents include Serene, Cosanzeana, Flo, Iara, Laume, Lutine, Rufina, Sedile, Sluasi, Verenata and Virda on land, and Glaisti, Granna, Puca and Thirona underwater. Xana is the vendor. Mamere, the artist, is a Melusine from here who could hear Elynas.
### LARGE +
Serene, one of the older Melusines, regards herself as the village's "big sister" and takes responsibility for daily affairs. Melusines generally have strong senses and a sense of justice, and are not strong fighters; some are skilled with rifles.

## governance
### STANDARD
There is no official leader. Serene handles day-to-day matters informally.

## culture
### STANDARD
Melusines eat Tidalga but also enjoy human food, and adore the Breacher Primuses that share Elynas' body. They respect Neuvillette as their ideal "father". A Newsstand Board notice in the Court of Fontaine says bathing in the waters around the village is calming and leaves skin soft and smooth.
### LARGE +
A world quest in 6.3, A Gift From the Sea Spirits, shows the villagers secretly preparing a handmade outfit as a gift for Neuvillette, who often sends gifts to everyone in the village. Every Melusine is born with a different token, a mechanical memento.

## history
### STANDARD
About 400 years ago, in the battle between the Marechaussee Hunters and the Narzissenkreuz Ordo inside Elynas' remains, an explosion killed many and drove the mechanical parts of clockwork meka through the beast's body. The Melusines were born from those wounds, and Elynas, who was dead but still conscious, regarded them as his daughters and taught them how to make a home: Merusea Village.
### LARGE +
According to Carole, the Melusines lived in the village for almost twenty years before they met Neuvillette, who answered their wish and brought them to the Court of Fontaine. Fontainians were wary of Melusines at first, and Neuvillette's reforms and Carole's death led people to trust them.
### ULTRA +
Later the Melusines could no longer hear Elynas, except Mamere. In the Ancient Colors quests, Jakob (by then an Iniquitous Baptist) tried to revive Elynas by collecting his blood, which Elynas did not want because his revival would crush the Melusines' homes. The Traveler stopped him, and Elynas thanked them.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Bubbling chimneys, handmade street lamps and swing sets: the village feels gentle, secluded and a little strange. It was dark enough that the Melusines added lamps for human visitors, who tend to enjoy their stay.

## dangers
### STANDARD
The village is safe for Melusines, since Breacher Primuses do not attack them, but the waters around Elynas' remains hold Breacher Primuses and Consecrated Beasts. Jakob's attempts to restart Elynas' heart once threatened the village.

## travel
### STANDARD
Only from underwater: dive near Elynas' skull or into the pool above the Beryl Region Statue of the Seven. Humans seldom come.

## quests
### LARGE
Ancient Colors (4.0), also called Seymour's Story: Prologue, centres on Mamere, her friend the clockwork meka Seymour and Elynas. A Gift From the Sea Spirits (6.3) follows the villagers' present for Neuvillette. Melusines also feature in many Court of Fontaine quests.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 Merusea Village remains a hidden, peaceful Melusine home with no leader. No later canon changes its state.
### STANDARD +
Elynas remains dead and his heart has not been restarted. Many Melusines live and work in the Court, and some visit the village on holiday.

## entry: Elynas

@version: 4.0

### STANDARD
Elynas is the vast creature whose remains hold Merusea Village. The Melusines call him "Father". An Abyss-infused creation of Rhinedottir (per the wiki's reading of the quest), he loved exploring the world but realised that his happiness hurt others, and he let himself die. After death he kept his consciousness and craved company. He thinks of the Melusines as his children, though most can no longer hear his voice.
### LARGE +
During the cataclysm the Fontaine Armada pursued him for about 35 days before he bled out. The wiki records that Rene de Petrichor and Jakob Ingold later entered his body and Jakob took his blood, which made him an Iniquitous Baptist. Elynas considered them friends. The Breacher Primuses protect his body like cells.

## entry: Serene

### STANDARD
Serene is an indigo-skinned Melusine with short brown hair and red eyes, one of the older villagers, who sees herself as the others' big sister. Her open-world dialogue shows her helping with everyone's troubles.
