## summary
### LITE
The Frostmoon Enclave is the village-like home of the Frostmoon Scions, the moon-worshipping tribe of Nod-Krai, on forested Hiisi Island. It is a calm, book-filled place with a Grand Library, animals wandering freely, priestesses in quiet debate, and the Moonchanter Lauma at its centre. The Scions revere the Frost Moon and, since 6.3, the Moon Goddess herself lives among them.
### STANDARD +
The Scions avoid conflict with outsiders. Their doctrine treats kuuvahki as a sacred power, so using it for personal gain, including through kuuvahki devices, counts as blasphemy to many of them.

## geography
### STANDARD
The Enclave lies on Hiisi Island, part of the Southern Isles of Nod-Krai. Under the Scions' stewardship much of the island remains untouched wilderness. Moonfall Silver and Frostlamp Flowers grow in the area, and the Moon Mirror, which once sealed a relic, stands on the island.
### LARGE +
Silvermoon Hall, the hidden refuge where Columbina once rested, and the Sanctum of the Oathbearer, a pillar raised by Xiuhcoatl where Hyperborean survivors found refuge, are on the same island, along with the Light-Bathed Platform.

## places
### STANDARD
The Grand Library is the Enclave's best-known building, and its meeting point stands inside. Shelves are uneven in height, like a flight of stairs, and are set up for reading undisturbed by moonlight. Cushions and perches have been put out so visiting animals can rest. A drum set stands in the open: striking its five drums summons animal companions if Lauma or Columbina is in the party (a gameplay interaction).
### LARGE +
Lauma has been collecting the Scions' scattered texts, relics and records here; a huge, uninspiring running chronicle of the sayings and doings of past Moonchanters sits on a bottom shelf and doubles as a footstool. The plain carved Lunar Semblance, recovered from the Moon Mirror, is kept in the library as a relic that matters to the Scions but has little monetary value.

## people
### STANDARD
Lauma, the Moonchanter, leads the Scions. Priestesses include Ehrnrooth, Sarakka and Rahetar; Zirnytsia is an acolyte, and Krodo, Ossian and Reetta are adherents. Others in the settlement include Ina, Kari, Mirsky and Wecksell, and the traveling merchant Rebane. Verna appears on the Enclave's list as a priestess but is ranked an acolyte on the Scions page.
### LARGE +
Verna is trying to change the Scions' view of kuuvahki devices. Ehrnrooth has been shelving the library's books and, mildly puzzled, follows Lauma's unusual instructions, such as making cushions and stands for visiting animals. Men of the tribe cannot become acolytes or priests.

## governance
### STANDARD
The Moonchanter leads. Beneath her are priestesses, acolytes (who have passed an exam on hymns and blessings) and adherents. The role replaced the earlier Archpriestess, abolished about 500 years ago by Aila, the first Moonchanter.
### ULTRA +
Lauma's authority rests on faith more than command. When she told the Scions the truth about the false sky, some left the Scions for good while others drew closer to her.

## culture
### STANDARD
The Scions are quiet, benevolent and pacifist toward outsiders, appear to abstain from meat, and revere the Frost Moon. They remember the dead goddess Canon as Huurrekuutar and the present goddess as Kuutar. Their home is shaped by Aila's reform: no great towers or fortresses, reverence for nature and kindness to outsiders.
### LARGE +
An emblem of a moon and deer carved in silver fir, called the Shadow of the Frostmoon, marks the Enclave, and the prayer Aila left is said to be etched in every Scion's memory. Tea is brewed in the Scions' own way, which most outsiders dislike; the Fatui's Sandrone learned it as an act of respect.

## history
### STANDARD
Before the reforms, the Scions' home on Hiisi Island held towers, fortresses and temples. About 500 years ago Aila, the first Moonchanter, had them torn down, because she thought isolation fed delusion. The pillar of the Sanctum of the Oathbearer was the one structure she spared. The wiki presumes the Enclave stands where those ruins were.
### LARGE +
Under Archpriestess Lovia the Scions ran a eugenic Sacred Heir Project and committed murders in its name; Aila ended it, killed Lovia, and sealed the evidence in a separate court. The Scions later took the newborn Kuutar as their goddess but, as she realised, cared more for wishes granted than for her. The Fatui later pressed the island, hunting Columbina under the Palestar Edict, until Dottore was defeated.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Stillness, silver-blue light, the smell of herbal tea and old paper, and the constant low conversation of animals only Lauma fully understands. Evenings feel unhurried. Children are curious about Columbina; the older generation is shy.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Few are recorded. Before Dottore's defeat the Fatui threatened the island; since then the main annoyance is mischief, like the weasel thief who keeps digging holes looking for treasure.

## travel
### STANDARD
Hiisi Island lies off the main Nod-Krai landmass in the Southern Isles; the meeting point inside the Grand Library lets the Traveler help develop the Enclave. Quests here include Blessings of the Frost Moon, Lauma's anecdotes and the Tea Party invitation with Sandrone.

## quests
### STANDARD
Blessings of the Frost Moon (6.0 world quest) unlocks the Enclave and its meeting point: Lauma and the Traveler retrieve the Lunar Semblance from the Moon Mirror. A Small Overture (6.7) has Lauma and Sandrone share tea in the Enclave and begin a careful reconciliation between Scions and Fatui.
### LARGE +
In the Lauma anecdote The Gathering she pretends to nap, with a cat in on the joke, and offers the Traveler fresh fruit and her blessing as Moonchanter.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the Enclave is at peace. Columbina lives here among the Scions, Lauma still leads them, and the Fatui have apologised.
### STANDARD +
After Dottore's defeat, Columbina took Lauma's invitation to live with the Scions instead of hiding in Silvermoon Hall. The older Scions are timid around her, the children curious; she asks to be treated as a person, not a goddess. Sandrone apologised for the Fatui's treatment of the Scions, and the Palestar Edict is revoked.
### ULTRA +
Columbina hopes that by the next Moon-Prayer Night she can attend openly as herself. Nothing in 7.0-7.1 changes the Enclave's situation.

## entry: Grand Library of the Enclave
@keys: Moon Mirror, Lunar Semblance, Grand Library
@keys2: Frostmoon Enclave, Lauma, Frostmoon Scions
@version: 6.0
### STANDARD
The library holds Scion texts and relics. Lauma retrieved the Lunar Semblance, a plain, crudely carved statue sealed in the Moon Mirror by a previous Moonchanter, with the Traveler's help; she values it as proof that several old documents are reliable and says her job is to dig up stories, verify them and share them with people. She asked the Traveler to bring any texts about the Scions found on their travels so she can copy them. A weasel thief who helped now begs her for the next treasure hunt.

## entry: Aila's Reform of the Scions
@keys: Aila's reform, first Moonchanter, Lovia's removal
@keys2: Frostmoon Enclave, Frostmoon Scions, Hiisi Island
@spoiler: major
@version: 6.0
### LARGE
Aila, the first Moonchanter, ended the Archpriestess's rule about 500 years ago and ordered the old walls, towers, fortresses and temples torn down, arguing that long isolation nurtured fleeting ambitions and hollow delusions. She wanted reverence for nature and kindness toward outsiders to define the tribe instead, so that there would be no second Lovia. Lovia's name was removed from the record; only priestesses and above know it.

## entry: Columbina Among the Scions
@keys: Kuutar, Moon Goddess, Palestar Edict
@keys2: Frostmoon Enclave, Columbina, Lauma
@version: 6.3
@spoiler: major
### ULTRA
Born on Hiisi Island some 400 to 500 years ago and worshipped by the Scions as Kuutar, Columbina left them for the Fatui, when she saw they cared for her powers more than for her. She later returned to Hiisi Island, hidden in Silvermoon Hall by Lauma, who apologised for her ancestors. After Dottore's defeat the Palestar Edict was revoked and she moved into the Enclave to live among the Scions as herself.
