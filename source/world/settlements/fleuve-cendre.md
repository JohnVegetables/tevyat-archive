## summary
### LITE
Fleuve Cendre ("Ashen River") is the undercity of Fontaine: a damp, poorly lit settlement in the sewers beneath the Court of Fontaine. Societal outcasts live here: ex-convicts, active criminals, the destitute, orphans and manual labourers whom the surface no longer needs. The Court of Fontaine has long treated it as a grey legal area outside its jurisdiction. Today the Spina di Rosula, led by Navia, is its de facto government.
### STANDARD +
It is the home district of several well-known Fontainians: Navia, Freminet, Chevreuse and the champion duelist Marfisa are all said to have been born or raised here. Many residents served time in the Fortress of Meropide and came here for a fresh start, sometimes with their families.

## geography
### STANDARD
A sewer-level settlement with little sunlight, poor air circulation and cramped living space; the air is dirty and damp. It lies below the Court of Fontaine and is reached from two access points in the Court: one beside Cafe Lutece on the west side and one across the street from Damoville Purveyor on the east.
### LARGE +
The pipes and passages of the undercity are part of the geography of childhood for its residents; Navia's character story recalls her hiding in the pipes during games with the local children.

## places
### STANDARD
L'or noir is a bar owned by the Spina di Rosula; its bartender Tetreaux also helps residents with supplies and household matters. A message board there carries local notices. There is a general goods store (Bracari) and a travelling merchant (Crossa), and a doctor, Areta.
### LARGE +
The Spina di Rosula keeps a stronghold in Fleuve Cendre (its secondary base, with Poisson as its headquarters), and the Archon Quest scene in which the Traveler learns about Navia's father Callas takes place there.

## people
### STANDARD
Open-world residents include Areta (doctor), Bracari (store manager), Tetreaux (bartender), Lelienne, Aurele, Hugues, Lisette, Verut, Vitruvia, Faustier, Potier (seen at night) and the Spina's Consigliere. After the world quest A Fontainian Message, the Spina enforcers Luca ("The Fisherman") and Sonny ("The White Glove") appear here.
### LARGE +
Areta's medical studies at the Akademiya were funded by the Spina on condition that she return to work in Fleuve Cendre after graduating and she was offered a salaried post; this shows how the Spina helps people while expecting loyalty in return. Notable people raised here include Navia, Freminet, Chevreuse and Marfisa.

## governance
### STANDARD
Historically the Court of Fontaine had no real jurisdiction here. The Spina di Rosula now mediates conflicts, manages relations with the Court and keeps order. Residents say it watches everyone "like hawks"; illegal funding is harder to find, and anyone who starts an armed fight can be "sent away to start a new life in the ocean" by the Spina.
### LARGE +
The Spina also provides practical help (supplies, household affairs, financial aid and jobs). Navia, as its second president after her father Callas, is addressed here as "Boss".

## culture
### STANDARD
Poor but close-knit. Parents are often too busy making ends meet to watch their children, who are left to their own devices. Most people keep a weapon, often a gun, and few bother to hide it. Locals are wary of outsiders and quick to say that anything left in the open will be stolen.
### LARGE +
Navia's birthday mail (dated 2026-08-16) mentions a party held in Fleuve Cendre, with food and drink paid for by the Spina; the mail is a light, in-world glimpse of the community's festivities.

## history
### STANDARD
Hundreds of years ago, to "improve" the city, the Court of Fontaine turned its sewers into an undercity for society's "shadows". Many manual labourers ended up here as technology reduced the demand for their work. The early inhabitants believed their diligence might win them the right to live on the surface and turn the Ashen River into somewhere as beautiful as the world above.
### LARGE +
The Court always knew about the undercity but never managed it, and for a time it was lawless and overrun with gangs. Before the cataclysm (about 500 years ago) the outlaw Eduardo Baker became its protector and head of a protection racket, using a bronze pipe called the "Ferryman" to defeat crocodiles and enforce order; his words are quoted in the weapon's text: "Let all that the sun shines upon belong to them, and let the Fleuve Cendre belong to us."
### ULTRA +
During Eduardo's rule the Maison Gardiennage raided Fleuve Cendre in what became known as the purge of Fleuve Cendre. Eduardo and his entourage were arrested and sentenced to exile in the Great Red Sand. En route, his henchmen freed him and, with allies near Mont Automnequi, he occupied Poisson, which led to the Siege of Poisson. The Court later banned unauthorised possession of construction tools such as the Dockhand's Assistant and Prospector's Drill, which the weapon texts imply were used as hidden weapons.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Dripping pipes, grey light and the constant damp. The mood is wary rather than hopeless: people share what little they have, know each other's business, and laugh in the bar. Tetreaux's line, that knowing who was killed is as easy as knowing what clothes you are wearing, sums up how normal violence feels.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Weapons are common, theft is routine, and violence is a daily possibility. Armed conflict is discouraged and punished by the Spina, so most trouble is petty or hidden rather than open warfare.

## travel
### STANDARD
Enter through the two access points in the Court of Fontaine (beside Cafe Lutece, and across the street from Damoville Purveyor). Fleuve Cendre has its own teleport waypoint underground.

## quests
### LARGE
Fleuve Cendre appears in Archon Quest Chapter IV, Act II, As Light Rain Falls Without Reason (where the Traveler visits the Spina's stronghold and learns about Callas), in the world quest A Fontainian Message (Luca and Sonny), and again when plans to trap Furina are discussed in the aftermath of the Poisson flood (Masquerade of the Guilty).

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 Fleuve Cendre remains a poor, damp undercity under the Spina di Rosula's watch. No later canon changes its state.
### STANDARD +
The Court of Fontaine still does not manage it directly. Navia continues to lead the Spina from Poisson and the Court, and its members still run L'or noir and the local relief work.

## entry: Eduardo Baker and the Purge of Fleuve Cendre
@keys: Eduardo Baker, Ferryman, purge of Fleuve Cendre, Fleuve Cendre Ferryman
@version: 4.0
### STANDARD
Eduardo Baker was an outlaw leader of Fleuve Cendre before the cataclysm. He protected merchants from gangs, settled local disputes and blessed newlyweds, using a bronze pipe he called the "Ferryman". He was among those arrested in the purge of Fleuve Cendre and was being taken to exile in the desert when his henchmen freed him. The wiki adds that he ran taverns in Fleuve Cendre where the stage performers who became Parsifal the Great (and her sister Josephine) got their start. His son Jakob (later Jakob Ingold) was born there.
### LARGE +
Eduardo's group then occupied Poisson and took Maison Gardiennage guards hostage; the reporter Karl Ingold photographed the key figures before the Siege of Poisson. Eduardo presumably died in that siege, though canon does not state his fate; the Marechaussee Hunters ended the incident.

## entry: The Spina di Rosula in Fleuve Cendre
@keys: Spina di Rosula, L'or noir, Tetreaux
@keys2: Fleuve Cendre, Fontaine, Navia
@version: 4.0
### STANDARD
The Spina di Rosula is a citizens' organisation of Fontaine that helps ordinary people and investigates matters the Gardes will not or cannot handle. It was founded by Callas and is now led by his daughter Navia. In Fleuve Cendre it runs the bar L'or noir, a stronghold and relief services, and is the practical authority.
