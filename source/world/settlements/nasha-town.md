## summary
### LITE
Nasha Town is the main port and commercial centre of Nod-Krai, on Lempo Isle. It has no official government. Merchants, adventurers, exiles, treasure hunters and Fatui all rub shoulders there, and the Voynich Guild and the Curatorium of Secrets do the most to keep it working. It is built largely of scrap metal, and kuuvahki-powered machines and cargo rails fill the sky above it.
### STANDARD +
It is both a haven of freedom and something of a lawless jungle. The Traveler first reaches it in the Song of the Welkin Moon Archon Quest (6.0), guided by Ineffa. Landmarks include the Flagship tavern, Speranza restaurant, the Adventurers' Guild in the central plaza and the Curatorium of Secrets.

## geography
### STANDARD
A seaside settlement on Lempo Isle in the Southern Isles of Nod-Krai, with a harbour and docks on one side and a central plaza at its heart. Because Nod-Krai has few resources, much of the town is built from scrap metal. Cargo-transport rails criss-cross above the roofs and kuuvahki-powered machines stand on nearly every corner, giving it an industrial look unusual for the region.
### LARGE +
The Clink-Clank Krumkake Craftshop, Aino's workshop, lies on the same isle just outside town. The town has no clear plan: official furnishing text says it has no true administrator and no clear planning structure, so countless narrow passages and gaps run between buildings. To most residents they are just channels for noise; to people who gather information they are routes for secrets and shortcuts.

## places
### LITE
The Flagship tavern, Speranza restaurant, the Adventurers' Guild in the central plaza, the Voynich Guild and the Curatorium of Secrets, the port, and a handful of shops.
### STANDARD +
- The Flagship: a tavern run by the bartender Demyan, where treasure hunters and bounty hunters swap intel and pick up commissions. It has a bounty board and a jukebox.
- Speranza: Nod-Krai's main restaurant, run by Katya and home base of the Conch Gang. A prize wheel on its side, joked about as a repurposed roulette, tells daily fortunes.
- Adventurers' Guild: in the central plaza, with Katheryne as receptionist and Sarin as branch master.
- Curatorium of Secrets: an information broker's headquarters directly behind the Voynich Guild's.
### LARGE +
Mimisbrunnr Books (a bookshop owned by Dhabit), Rossum Workshop (a smithy), Nuts 'N' Nuts (groceries and repairs, with an alchemy bench), Northland Bank's local branch, the Vesnici Proleca Greenhouse, and the Voynich Guild's souvenir outlet managed by Tarno.

The port holds a harbour office, warehouses, foremen, repairmen and sailors; the central plaza has stalls, merchants and fashion and research visitors. A guide map handed to newcomers advertises a Mimisbrunnr Books investment scheme, Speranza's wheel, sugar sculptures by the confectioner Hunajatta and the experiments of Fontaine researcher Liouba.

## people
### LITE
Residents include Nefer and Jahoda of the Curatorium, Katya of Speranza, Demyan of the Flagship, and the stray children of the Conch Gang. Ineffa and Aino of the Clink-Clank Krumkake Craftshop are frequent visitors.
### STANDARD +
Tarno is a Voynich Guild employee who manages its souvenir outlet and later speaks for the Guild in the League. The newsy Alida sells the paper "Nasha Express". Fatui soldiers, Snezhnayans, exiles and travellers from Sumeru, Liyue and Fontaine pass through the port. Many locals are Ratniks, treasure hunters or dockworkers.
### LARGE +
Zinaida, now a treasure hunter employed by the Voynich Guild, was once a mid-level leader of the Seahook Gang and brought Jahoda into it. Lauma and Flins of the Frostmoon Scions visit town and have friends there. Nicole of the Hexenzirkel helped defend it during the 6.3 crisis, and Durin and Albedo were assigned to it.

## governance
### STANDARD
Nasha Town has no formal government. Wiki summaries say it is under the influence of the Voynich Guild, the Curatorium of Secrets, Speranza and the Fatui. The Voynich Guild is a commerce guild of local merchants that handles trade deals, import and export, business certificates and dispute resolution, so it is the nearest thing to a regulator. Nefer leads the Curatorium and is also a Guild member.
### LARGE +
The Curatorium's information network gives it quiet leverage. After the Dottore crisis a new League was formed as a joint effort of Nod-Krai's factions. Tarno hosted its opening ceremony as the Voynich Guild's representative, and Flins persuaded Illuga to represent the Lightkeepers; Aino and Ineffa helped hand out food on behalf of the Frostmoon Scions.

## culture
### STANDARD
A mixed, frontier-style port culture of trade, bargaining, gossip and street food, where outsiders are accepted as long as they do business. Speranza's hot dog is a local favourite, and locals take their fortunes from its wheel. The town is also a stage for Moon-Prayer Night celebrations, with stalls, games and candy.
### LARGE +
Information is a currency here: Nefer says that with the town in turmoil everyone wants intel, and the Curatorium's reception desk is staffed in shifts by different characters, Nefer included. Children are given real freedom; the Conch Gang raise themselves under Katya's wing.

## history
### STANDARD
Little of Nasha Town's founding is recorded. The Conch Gang's own story is that a gang of children first 'conquered' the empty site, calling gatherings with a conch shell, until the settlement grew too big for them. A bandit group, the Seahook Gang, is said to have once held hidden control of the town before its boss was killed and the gang scattered. The town was first mentioned in 5.3 and first visited in 6.0.
### LARGE +
Before the Song of the Welkin Moon, the Fatui built the Kuuvahki Experimental Design Bureau on nearby Paha Isle. In Fulminations and Undercurrents, repeated Wild Hunt attacks strained the few Lightkeepers stationed near town under Sergeant Major Sousi, whose cannon was waiting on repairs from Aino, and Nefer remarked that with Nasha Town in turmoil everyone wanted intel.
### ULTRA +
During the Dottore crisis (6.3, True Moon act, Descending Moon) civilians were evacuated and his forces besieged Nasha Town. Nicole's golden flame protected it while Albedo and Durin held the town and the Traveler, Flins, Lauma and Nefer fought; Dottore then confronted them there as the Heretic of the False Moon. Afterwards the newsy Alida's paper proclaimed "Nasha Town reborn beneath a new moon", and residents gathered in the central plaza for the League's opening ceremony, hosted by Tarno.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Clanking, steam, rails overhead, and smells of fried sausage, sugar and sea. The sky is full of cargo lines and the lanes between houses are narrow and loud. Everyone seems to be selling, buying or listening, and there is always a rumour moving through the crowd. At night the lamps and fishing boats glow, and during Moon-Prayer Night the whole town is hung with lanterns and games.

## dangers
### LARGE
Pickpockets, shady deals and local rivalries are the everyday risks, since nobody enforces law uniformly. Wild Hunt incursions and Fatui activity have threatened the area, and the town was besieged once. Outside town, the surrounding isle has monsters and kuuvahki hazards.

## travel
### LARGE
Nasha Town is the arrival point for sea travellers heading into Nod-Krai and a hub for the islands. The Traveler comes in by sea from Natlan in the 6.0 Archon Quest. The Adventurers' Guild and the Flagship are the usual first stops.

## quests
### LARGE
Where the Moon Rises (6.0) introduces the town; Fulminations and Undercurrents is set here and at the Flagship; The Night the Moon Returns Home hosts the Moon-Prayer Night festival; Descending Moon and Welkin Moon's Homecoming (6.3) cover the siege and its aftermath. Intel quests from Nefer and Jahoda also take place here.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 Nasha Town stands, recovered after the siege. It remains a free port with no formal ruler, and the new League of factions first met there.
### STANDARD +
The 7.0-7.1 Archon Quests take place in Snezhnaya; the sources read describe no new upheaval in Nasha Town.

## entry: The Flagship
@keys: The Flagship, Flagship tavern, Demyan
### STANDARD
A tavern run by the young bartender Demyan, where treasure hunters and bounty hunters gather. It has a jukebox and a bounty board. In Fulminations and Undercurrents Nefer brings the Traveler to the tavern's invitation-only Banquet, where they mix drinks for guests and gather intel.
### LARGE +
The wheel at Speranza is jokingly said to be a repurposed roulette from the Flagship. Demyan sells food and drinks, including the Roulette Special.

## entry: Speranza
@keys: Speranza, Katya
### STANDARD
Nod-Krai's main restaurant, run by Katya, who also looks after the Conch Gang children and employs them. It sells hot dogs and other dishes, and a prize wheel outside gives daily fortunes. Completing a certain quest gives permanent discounts.

## entry: Conch Gang
@keys: Conch Gang
### STANDARD
Stray children who live and work at Speranza with Katya's care and a great deal of freedom. Their legend is that kids once conquered Nasha Town and called meetings with a conch shell. New members need unanimous approval.

## entry: Seahook Gang
@keys: Seahook Gang, Ratfan
### STANDARD
A once-notorious bandit gang said to have secretly controlled Nasha Town, taking in exiles from across Teyvat. A merchant, Ratfan, had its boss betrayed and killed and the gang scattered. Jahoda later avenged her boss. Imposters now use the name. Details come largely from Jahoda's account.

## entry: Voynich Guild
@keys: Voynich Guild, Voynich
### STANDARD
A commerce guild of local merchants and one of Nod-Krai's most powerful factions. It manages trade, issues business certificates and resolves disputes. Nefer is a member, and its headquarters sits directly in front of the Curatorium's. Tarno runs its souvenir outlet and represents it in the League.

## entry: Curatorium of Secrets
@keys: Curatorium of Secrets, Curatorium
### STANDARD
An information-trading institution in town led by Nefer. Its second floor holds a reception and Nefer's office; the Traveler can later enter Nefer's secret room. Jahoda works for it. Loading screens call it a deep chasm of intelligence tied to a northlands-wide network.

## entry: Moon-Prayer Night in Nasha Town
@keys: Moon-Prayer Night, Kuunrukous
### STANDARD
A Nod-Krai festival held when the moon is closest to Teyvat and kuuvahki peaks. In The Night the Moon Returns Home, Nasha Town hosts it with games by the Hexenzirkel, the Frostmoon Scions and locals, including "Hundred-Flavored Moon-Prayer Candy", "Bitey the Frostfin Whale" and "Kuuhenki Concentration".

## entry: Siege of Nasha Town
@keys: siege of Nasha Town, Dottore's forces
@version: 6.3
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
In the climax of the Song of the Welkin Moon, Dottore's forces attacked Nasha Town after its civilians were evacuated. The Traveler and allies, with Nicole's help and Albedo and Durin posted to the town, repelled the attack before Dottore confronted them there as the Heretic of the False Moon. Afterward the town recovered and hosted the opening of a new League of factions.
