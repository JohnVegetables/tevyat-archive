## summary
### LITE
Gandharva Ville, nicknamed the City Above the Forest, is the main outpost of Sumeru's Forest Rangers, set in the Avidya Forest. Travellers, merchants and adventurers frequent it. It is home to the Forest Watcher Tighnari and his student Collei, and it is where the Traveler recovers after a vision in Chapter III.
### STANDARD +
Its tone is quiet and caring. The Rangers here help travellers in need, keep watch over the forest's health and treat patients, and the village is where the early threads of Sumeru's Archon Quest begin.

## geography
### STANDARD
The village stands above the forest floor in the Avidya Forest; official map text calls it "the city above the forest". Its local specialty is the Kalpalata Lotus, and Shaggy Sumpter Beasts wander near it.
### LARGE +
From the village the Rangers set out on patrol routes through the forest, and Collei guides the Traveler along one in Chapter III.

## places
### STANDARD
Ashpazi, a Forest Watcher who supervises logistics and is a master chef, serves as the village's vendor; Collei is cared for in the village when her illness flares.
### LARGE +
The same map text reads: "The city of wisdom is ours, as is the forest of ignorance. Atop the tree lies the remnants of the kingdom of lost dreams."

## people
### STANDARD
Forest Watchers (higher-ranking Rangers) and Rangers include Tighnari (chief officer), Amir (who trains the search and rescue dogs), Shirin (doctor), Iraj, Kamran and Nasrin; Collei, long a trainee, was promoted to official Forest Ranger after the 6.6 crisis. Keon, Farbod and Umm are designated Vanara; Keon is a blind elder who greets travellers by their footsteps. Visitors and workers include researchers Hayyan and Rudaba, adventurer Philby, the merchant Darina and Eremite mercenaries Futuh, Tuman and Yohanan.
### LARGE +
Sag leads the search and rescue dog team. Before the Akasha was shut down, Keon noted fewer Dusk Birds leaving Sumeru City as Akasha use grew and hoped it meant progress; afterwards he says even more birds sing in the forest.

## governance
### STANDARD
The Forest Rangers run the village. They are civil servants who protect Sumeru's forests, help travellers and preserve the ecosystem, suppress Withering Zones and monitor beast populations.
### LARGE +
They send regular reports about the Avidya Forest to researchers at Pardis Dhyai, who in turn supply seeds and nutrients for ecological support. The earliest Rangers were, according to the Ranger Rana, the forest king Viaghara's royal guards.

## culture
### STANDARD
The Rangers are devoted to protecting the forest. Locals such as Keon once blessed travellers in the name of Greater Lord Rukkhadevata; since her erasure from Irminsul in Chapter III they credit every blessing to Lesser Lord Kusanali instead. They are wary of the Akademiya's recruiters, as when Tighnari refuses a top-secret project.

## history
### STANDARD
Little is known of the village's origin. In the story Collei is brought here by Cyno after the Black Fire incident in Mondstadt and becomes Tighnari's trainee. Tighnari's ancestors learned of the Withering and of Irminsul from aranara.
### LARGE +
Collei calls Tighnari her master. She was born with Eleazar and later healed when Nahida removed the Greater Lord's presence from Irminsul, which cured the disease everywhere (see quests).
### ULTRA +
The name joins the Sanskrit gandharva with the French ville and echoes gandharvanagara, an illusory city that looks real from afar. The Chinese name draws on the Lotus Sutra's parable of the phantom city, a city conjured to let tired travellers rest before moving on to their true destination.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
A quiet, high-up outpost where Rangers in green clothes (higher ranks wear lighter green), search dogs and visiting travellers come and go; Keon counts the Dusk Birds that pass.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Withering Zones in the surrounding forest, whose effects only those with elemental power can withstand; Eremites hired by Dottore in Chapter III; hostile wildlife such as Shaggy Sumpter Beasts.

## travel
### STANDARD
Gandharva Ville is the Rangers' base in the Avidya Forest; the Traveler also reaches Pardis Dhyai from it, and the road through Dharma Forest leads on to Caravan Ribat and the desert.

## quests
@version: 3.0
### STANDARD
In Chapter III Act I the Traveler wakes here after Haypasia's incense gave them a vision; Collei shows them around on patrol, and Tighnari explains the Withering and Irminsul. Khajeh and his Akademiya retinue visit to recruit Tighnari and are refused. In Act III Collei, barred from expeditions by a worsening Eleazar, is found here, and Tighnari has left for Pardis Dhyai.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 Gandharva Ville remains the Forest Rangers' main outpost, with Tighnari and Collei (now an official Forest Ranger) based there.
### STANDARD +
Eleazar was wiped out after the Chapter III Archon Quest, curing Collei. In 6.6, during the Irminsul crisis, the whole rainforest including the village was evacuated to Caravan Ribat and then Aaru Village, with Tighnari, Collei and the Rangers helping defend the evacuees; after the crisis people returned and Collei was promoted to official Forest Ranger.

## entry: A Forest of Change and Further Observation
@keys: Gandharva Ville, City Above the Forest
@keys2: Tighnari, Collei, Traveler, Haypasia
@version: 3.0
### STANDARD
After passing through the tunnel from Cinnabar Cliff, the Traveler and Paimon follow the scholar Haypasia into a cave, where her incense gives the Traveler a vision of a white tree under a red sky and a woman's voice saying "World... Forget me...". They wake in Gandharva Ville, where Tighnari and Collei treated them. Tighnari calls it a hallucination; suspecting a lie, the Traveler asks Collei and joins her patrol the next day.
### LARGE +
On patrol Collei tells them there have been two Dendro Archons: Greater Lord Rukkhadevata, who died in the cataclysm, and Lesser Lord Kusanali, who lives in Sumeru City. She knew who the Traveler was from letters from Amber. When Paimon touches her in jest, Collei panics and flees back to the village, ill.

## entry: Collei's Eleazar
@keys: Eleazar, Collei's illness
@keys2: Gandharva Ville, Collei, Tighnari
@version: 3.0
### STANDARD
Collei was born with Eleazar, a disease endemic to Sumeru that causes slow, painful death. Dottore had once suppressed her symptoms through experiments that traumatised her and made her avoid touch. In Chronic Illness the Traveler and Tighnari gather medicine ingredients and clear a Withering Zone on a patrol route. After Nahida removes the Greater Lord's presence from Irminsul in the Archon Quest, Eleazar disappears and Collei recovers.
### LARGE +
Dottore had kept her alive by infusing her with remains of the god Nabu Malikata. In 6.6 those remains let her command hostile Jinn at Aaru Village; afterwards, at Windrise, the Jinni Liloupar absorbed them from her and died at peace.

## entry: Khajeh's Recruitment Visit
@keys: Khajeh, Naphis
@keys2: Tighnari, Gandharva Ville
@version: 3.0
### STANDARD
In Silent Seeker of Knowledge the Traveler and Paimon return to find Sage Khajeh and subordinates trying to recruit Tighnari into a top-secret Akademiya project. Even mention of disappointing his teacher, Sage Naphis, does not move him, and they leave empty-handed. Tighnari then tells the Traveler to meet Rohawi in Sumeru City.

## entry: Chapter III Act III at Gandharva Ville
@keys: The Gaze From a Certain God
@keys2: Gandharva Ville, Collei, Tighnari
@version: 3.1
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
On the way to the village the Traveler and Paimon repel an ambush by Eremites hired by Dottore. They find Collei, whose Eleazar has worsened and who is barred from expeditions. Tighnari has uncharacteristically left for Pardis Dhyai on short notice, so they follow him there.

## entry: Keon
@keys: Keon
@keys2: Gandharva Ville, Vanara
### STANDARD
Keon is a blind elder Vanara in Gandharva Ville who recognises people by their footsteps and says that travellers, merchants and adventurers are frequent. Before the Chapter III climax he worried that Akasha use was growing while fewer Dusk Birds flew from Sumeru City; afterwards he says more birds sing in the forest now the Akasha is shut down, believes things will improve under Lesser Lord Kusanali, and hopes to live to see her restore Sumeru's glory.
