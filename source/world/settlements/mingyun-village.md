## summary
### LITE
Mingyun Village is an abandoned mining village in Qiongji Estuary, Liyue. Its scattered ore deposits ran dry, and the miners moved west toward Liyue Harbor and the Chasm. Hilichurls now use the leftover facilities. A ghostly father haunts it in the quest The Tree who Stands Alone.
### STANDARD +
Three small mine tunnels around the village hold iron chunks, Noctilucous Jade and Cor Lapis. The Hidden Palace of Lianshan Formula domain lies near the village.

## geography
### STANDARD
The village sits in Qiongji Estuary with its mining tunnels in the surrounding hills. Local specialties of the estuary include Cor Lapis, Noctilucous Jade, Qingxin and Violetgrass.
### LARGE +
Hilichurl groups (including shield-bearers, shooters and Mitachurls) and Fatui agents have been met around the village, and Geovishap hatchlings and Ruin Guards roam the area.

## places
### STANDARD
Mining tunnels, a billboard, houses, including one with lost notes. Notices still pinned up announce the mine's closure.
### LARGE +
One notice orders workers to take tools and belongings and seal the mine entrance; the crew was to be re-dispatched to the Chasm. A road sign tells senders to deliver letters and parcels to the Ministry of Civil Affairs for forwarding. A safety slogan board advises never entering without a finch and staying calm if one sees a hilichurl, and ends with a scribbled-out mention of a former comrade named Erge.

## people
### STANDARD
No living residents remain. Yuan Hong is a ghost, father of three deceased sons, each of whom mistook his will for a treasure map: Yuan Cheng, Yuan Qing and Yuan Liang.
### LARGE +
Treasure Hoarders also visit, hoping to profit from the will. A notice asks miners to ignore rumours about Hong's letter and the hidden treasure.

## economy
### LARGE
The village existed for its mine. When the ore dried up, the remaining ore could not sustain it and the inhabitants left for better opportunities in Liyue Harbor and the Chasm.

## history
### STANDARD
The mine was run by a mining crew until its ores failed; notices say the crew was to be re-dispatched to the Chasm. The date is not given.
### LARGE +
The spirits of Hong and his sons appear in The Tree who Stands Alone. Hong planted a tree and left a will in the hope that his sons would understand it. Each son took it to mean real treasure was buried in the mines and left in search of it. The Traveler finds the four parts of the will and the tree, where a chest holds items Hong placed every year for his sons to find.
### ULTRA +
The will points to the tree, so the real "treasure" is the family bond. The four spirits reunite and then disappear; Hong is disappointed that his sons looked to the mines. The quest's name echoes Hong's verse about a tree that stands alone with no forest for its home.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Eerie and abandoned. Paimon calls the village spooky on arrival, with broken tunnels, unused tools and a hilichurl presence.

## dangers
### LARGE
Hilichurls and Mitachurls in the tunnels, Fatui, Geo slimes, Electro cicins and Treasure Hoarders hunting treasure.

## quests
### LARGE
The Tree who Stands Alone is the village's world quest (1.0).

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 Mingyun Village is abandoned.
### STANDARD +
The mines are closed, hilichurls roam it, and Yuan Hong's ghost has departed after the quest.

## entry: Yuan Hong's Will
@keys: Yuan Hong, Yuan Cheng, Yuan Qing, Yuan Liang, The Tree who Stands Alone
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Yuan Hong's will, left in verse, was misread by his three sons as a treasure map. They searched the mines instead of understanding it. The Traveler finds the will's four parts in the surrounding caves and follows it to a large tree. Treasure Hoarders arrive hoping for riches. The chest at the tree holds items Hong placed every year, showing that the family was the treasure.
