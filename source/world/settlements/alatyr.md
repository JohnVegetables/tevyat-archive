## summary
### LITE
Alatyr is a remote village in Snezhnaya, abandoned several years before the Traveler arrives, and the hometown of the Rokot hunter Alyosha. A brutal winter bandit raid left its people destitute; when no help came, some villagers became highwaymen. The village cannot be visited in the game.
### STANDARD +
It had no train station and lay so far out that official aid would take days to arrive. Alyosha says that, years later, nobody lives there; Alatyr's former residents scattered to other towns and cities.

## history
### STANDARD
In a particularly harsh winter, when Alyosha was still young, a gang of bandits robbed Alatyr and spared no household. Alyosha, using the hunting skills his father had taught him, hunted the bandits down and recovered the stolen goods, but the raiders had owned nothing worth taking back, so the village still could not rebuild and neighbouring villages could not help either. Some villagers then planned to become highwaymen themselves, and Alyosha took charge reluctantly, setting one rule: ordinary travellers are never to be touched.
### LARGE +
Alyosha's character stories call the target travellers 'fowl' (merchants and ordinary people) and the armed highwaymen and gangs 'beasts', and say he steered the group to prey only on 'beasts', so that they would not draw the attention of the Druzhna. The household's finances were crushed by medical bills and his parents' lost livelihood, and Alyosha's father had insisted he learn to count coins and weigh grain as well as shoot.
### ULTRA +
Later the Alatyr highwaymen grew greedy. When the cost of living soared under Project Stuzha and many travellers tried to cross the tundra toward Nod-Krai, some members dropped the rule and began attacking the defenceless. Alyosha, feeling responsible because he had taught them, went into the forest alone and hunted down his former comrades, killing several and forcing the rest into submission. By the time the Fatui answered reports of the disturbance everything was over, and they filed it as a turf war between bandits. The 'bandit-ridden village of Alatyr' reputation faded for Alyosha himself only as the people around him learned to trust him.

## people
### STANDARD
Former townspeople named in canon are Alyosha and his parents, Repnina (an elderly woman from the same village who now lives in Sretomorozsk; her memory is failing and Alyosha visits her, sharing fond memories of the place) and Taras (one of the highwaymen, described in Alyosha's stories as a man who could barely hold his rifle straight yet grew eager to join the attacks). Alyosha's hunting dog, Tugarin, is also from this period.

## travel
### STANDARD
Not accessible. Alatyr is a place people mention rather than visit.

## status
### LITE
Abandoned and inaccessible as of 7.1. It is only known through Alyosha's story and the Sretomorozsk episode of Chapter VII.

## entry: Shishkin's Alatyr claim
@keys: Shishkin, Alatyr, Alyosha, Polonsky
@keys2: Kristall, Sretomorozsk
@version: 7.0
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
In Sretomorozsk the liaison officer Shishkin was caught with stashed Kresnik Kristall and claimed he took it for his elderly parents in Alatyr, a village with no train station where deliveries had stopped. Alyosha cut in: Alatyr is empty and has been for years, and the missing Kristall was far more than Shishkin admitted. This exposed Shishkin and the Sretomorozsk mayor, who had been selling stolen Kristall. Polonsky later thanked Alyosha and remarked that the tale was probably meant to shield the mayor.
