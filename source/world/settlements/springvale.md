## summary
### LITE
Springvale is a small, peaceful hunting village in Windwail Highland, south of Mondstadt City and facing the lake around it. It is a cluster of one-storey tan houses with blue-tiled roofs, a single windmill, and a natural spring beneath it. Most residents are hunters who supply fresh meat to Mondstadt's restaurants, and the village attracts tourists with its rustic charm. Locals pass down a legend of a benevolent spirit living in the spring.
### STANDARD +
The legend says a Spring Fairy once filled an empty well with life-saving water for a dying boy, and the settlement grew around the miracle. The fairy is real: an Oceanid from Fontaine who settled here.

## geography
### STANDARD
Dirt-paved lanes, a windmill and the spring below it. Anemo Slimes and the occasional Fatui Electro Cicin Mage are the creatures noted here. Local specialties are calla lilies, dandelion seeds and Philanemo mushrooms. A seelie can be found for exploration progress.
### LARGE +
The Dawn Winery lies a short way southwest, and the dark forest of Wolvendom is nearby, avoided by locals. The village is also the game's safe return point if the open world hits an error.

## people
### STANDARD
Draff is head of the hunters and father of Diona; Allan and Jotun are hunters, Olaf also lives here. Old Finch, the village's elderly believer in the Spring Fairy, stands by the spring by day. Brook is a chef, and Hopkins the Marvelous also sells goods. The bard Myweiss, Marla and Pepe are further residents.
### LARGE +
Younger villagers call Finch "Grandpa". The hunters' reputation for fresh meat is reflected in Mondstadt's restaurants, and a billboard in the village (written by an unnamed local) extols mushroom dishes.

## culture
### STANDARD
Hunting, spring water and storytelling define Springvale. Its young hunters seeking love catch foxes in Wolvendom during Windblume and release them wearing flower wreaths. The book series "Heart of Clear Springs" retells the village's fairy tale.
### LARGE +
Most villagers regard the Spring Fairy as a fairy tale; a few, like Old Finch, still believe in her. Diona's Character Story credits the fairy's blessing for her gift of making delicious drinks.

## history
### STANDARD
By legend a mother brought her deathly ill son to an empty well; a fairy filled it with life-saving water, the miracle drew people, and a settlement was built over the spring. Olaf says the water is really melted snow from Dragonspine. The fairy is the Oceanid Callirhoe, who settled here after the Hydro Archon Egeria's death.
### LARGE +
Old Finch befriended her as a boy and waited at the spring into old age; his story inspired "Heart of Clear Springs". She befriended Diona as a lonely child and blessed her on her seventh birthday, then withdrew because of the gap between Oceanid and human lifespans.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Sleepy and sunlit; the windmill turns, hunters come and go, and the spring murmurs. The mood is tender and slightly melancholy, tied to the unrequited fairy tale.

## status
### LITE
Quiet and unchanged as of 7.1.
### STANDARD +
No Springvale story after 1.4 was found beyond Callirhoe's appearance in the Waterborne Poetry event.

## trivia
### ULTRA
A signboard on the shop door reads that the manager went drinking in town and the place is temporarily closed. Mondstadt's official description adds that visitors lucky enough may find a restaurant that truly brings out the quality of Springvale's meat. Localised names include Clear Spring Town (Chinese) and Quellingen (German). The viewpoint "Land of Clear Springs" emphasises hunting, sweet spring water and the beneficent spirit legend.

## entry: The Spring Fairy
@keys: Spring Fairy, Callirhoe, Heart of Clear Springs
@keys2: Springvale, Old Finch, Diona
### STANDARD
Callirhoe, the Spring Fairy, is an Oceanid who settled in Springvale after Egeria's death. Heart of Clear Springs is a four-volume book collection retelling a story in which a tearful boy makes a wish at the spring and the fairy listens. In the book she gives a parting kiss and he believes in her to his death; per the quest Heart of the Deep, the real events differ.
### LARGE +
During Wishful Drops (1.4, limited) Endora sensed her but got no answer at first; later she relayed a poem and a message imploring Old Finch to move on. In Waterborne Poetry she took human form briefly and took part in the Neighbouring Nations Congenial Poetry Gala as "Riddle Writer".
