## summary
### LITE
Qiaoying Village is a mountain village in Chenyu Vale, north-western Liyue, close to the Fontaine border. It is known throughout Teyvat for high-quality tea, sold in Liyue Harbor and exported to other nations, and is home to the Chunming Teaworks. Its architecture is inspired by real-world Hui-style buildings.
### STANDARD +
Locals credit the blessing of the adepti on Chenyu Vale's land and water for the ideal tea-growing conditions. A special cauldron, said to be an adeptal mechanism, roasts the leaves and gives them their aroma.

## geography
### STANDARD
Qiaoying sits in the Upper Vale of Chenyu Vale, the Liyue subregion around Mt. Yaojun, with significant numbers of tea trees planted around the village. It has docks. The vale is humid compared with the Hills of Barsom.
### LARGE +
The village borders Fontaine. Fontaine imports tea via Lumidouce Harbor, so the village feeds a trade line that crosses the sea. Yilong Wharf, the vale's trading port, lies in the same Upper Vale.

## places
### STANDARD
The Chunming Teaworks is the best-known workshop: it combines methods passed down for thousands of years with the newest tea-drying and roasting machines, and its reputation extends well beyond the village. The Yanshang Teahouse employs an evaluator and hired muscle, and the docks have a handful of workers.
### LARGE +
After the quest The Roaming Abode and a one-day wait, a blue flower appears behind a house in the north-east; interacting with it yields a Soulscent Bloom artifact.

## people
### STANDARD
Ding Sr. and Ding Jr. work inside the Chunming Teaworks and Jin stands outside it. Hanquan (hired muscle) and Qianyun (evaluator) work at the Yanshang Teahouse. Other residents include Grandpa Lu, Uncle Luo, Leong, Moyan and Muli; the researcher Djarkutan is also in the village. Fujin, one of the vale's adepti, and Lingyuan appear here at times.
### LARGE +
Zhiqiong was once commissioned to map the village's terrain for a construction project that was later shelved. She remarks that the village smells strongly of tea and mentions one particularly skilled tea maker, whom she calls "the old man".

## culture
### STANDARD
Tea is life here. Cultivars named in canon include Dipped Dew, Tianheng Bohea, Qiaoying floral tea, Sunglo Tea and Single-Origin Yauwan Tea. The village performs an annual tea-serving ceremony, using the wilted petals of Spiritscent Flowers to prepare floral tea.
### LARGE +
Chenyu Vale lore holds that Fujin planted the first tea tree in the vale. The tree is said to thrive there because it remembers a promise between two friends, which is why this type of tea grows well only in Chenyu Vale.

## history
### STANDARD
Chenyu Vale's first human settlements were founded some 6,000 years ago by tribes fleeing the Chasm, guided by Fujin. Its tea later became famous in Liyue Harbor and elsewhere. Qiaoying itself is described mainly through its tea trade.
### LARGE +
In 4.4, the adeptus Lingyuan, yearning for the vale's old state, tampered with its spirit lines. The soil and water were tainted, Qiaoying's tea fell in quality and miasma erupted in places. The Traveler calmed Lingyuan, and Fujin spent most of her remaining power restoring the tea; she was left stuck in carp form, and some miasma remained.
### ULTRA +
Qiaoying features in Lantern Rite events (limited-time). In The Exquisite Night Chimes, at Hu Tao's request, Zhongli collected tea seed oil and sesame oil here because they could not be found around Liyue Harbor. In Vibrant Harriers Aloft in Spring Breeze (4.4), the Traveler and Paimon came to Qiaoying and met Navia and Clorinde on a nearby mountain top, where hikers like to shout after the climb, and locals tolerate it. Zhongli's 2023 birthday mail has him returning to the village to relax and reflect with friends.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Tea fragrance carried on mountain breezes, terraces of tea trees, Hui-style roofs and the sound of leaf-roasting. The 4.4 showcase described spring sun, wind and the Spiritscent Flower drifting together through peaceful streets.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 Qiaoying is peaceful, with its tea quality restored.
### STANDARD +
Fujin remains in carp form after the repair, and some miasma lingers elsewhere in the vale.

## entry: Chunming Teaworks
@keys: Chunming Teaworks, Ding Sr., Ding Jr.
@version: 4.4
### STANDARD
A tea-processing workshop famous well beyond Qiaoying Village. It uses millennia-old methods alongside modern machines for drying and roasting, which may explain the village's renown. Ding Sr. and Ding Jr. work inside.

## entry: The Chenyu Vale Taint
@keys: Lingyuan, Fujin, Chenyu Vale taint, spirit lines
@spoiler: minor
@version: 4.4
### LARGE
Lingyuan, a suanni who preferred the wilds and longed for how the vale used to be, tampered with the spirit lines. The tainted soil and water degraded Qiaoying's leaves and set miasma loose. The Traveler stopped her after a fight, and Fujin spent her power to restore the tea but could not clear all the miasma.
