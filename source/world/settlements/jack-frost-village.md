## summary
### LITE
Jack Frost Village is the hidden homeland of the Jack Frosts, a small settlement on Fellfrost Peak in Snezhnaya. It was recently under siege by Volkodlaks until the Traveler helped the villagers fend them off, and it is the former home of the Geo Jack Frost Lumi, who now travels with Linnea.
### STANDARD +
The Jack Frosts here cannot speak in sentences; they cry 'Ya-ho' and gesture. The village head, a somewhat larger Jack Frost, can write brief words on a wooden slate.

## geography
### STANDARD
The village lies on Fellfrost Peak, the great snow-sloped mountain south of Snezhnograd. Volkodlaks reach it along at least two paths into the settlement, which is why the Jack Frosts wanted defences along those routes. Its waypoint stands in the village; a broken igloo to the north-east holds a discarded Jack Frost disguise.
### LARGE +
The siege defence uses Prime Ice constructs: snowball launchers and blockade spikes that the Traveler sets up as Prime Ice constructs.

## people
### STANDARD
The Jack Frost Village Head, who answers to 'Chief' and says that is both his job and his name, speaks in broken words written on a slate ('Volkodlaks. Invaded. Bullied.'). Other Jack Frosts include one in a blue three-pointed hat, one who looks confused and several that are stuck in snow when the Traveler arrives. After the defence, the Jack Frosts open a goods shop run from the village.
### LARGE +
Lumi is the best-known former resident. Unlike other Jack Frosts she looks earthy (Geo), and she was bullied for standing out: other Jack Frosts chased her from her sleeping places and took her food. Linnea found her and, after working up the courage, asked whether she would travel with her; Lumi agreed and left her disguise at her old home here.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Volkodlaks raid the village, take what they like and leave a mess. They say they will return with the whole pack, which is why the Traveler helps build a second layer of defences.

## quests
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
The World Quest 'Small Jack Frost, Big Problems' starts when the Traveler frees two trapped Jack Frosts. It leads to the Prime Ice defences, three waves of Volkodlak attackers and, at the end, the unlocking of the Jack Frosts' Goodies Shop. Linnea's Alcyon Chapter later adds the 'Strange Jack Frost's Paper Bag' near the broken igloo (the wiki flags the exact trigger as unverified).
### ULTRA +
The paper bag, a cardboard bag with an ordinary Jack Frost's face, appears after the Alcyon Chapter's first act, 'Up! Toward Homecoming Wings'. The text suggests its owner no longer needs a disguise and is free to walk toward a new future.

## status
### LITE
Inhabited by Jack Frosts as of 7.1. Its defences hold, and it has a shop open to adventurers.

## entry: Lumi and Linnea at Jack Frost Village
@keys: Lumi, Linnea, Jack Frost disguise, Paper Bag
@keys2: Jack Frost Village
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
Lumi lived at the village before Linnea. A Jack Frost with Geo colouring, she was teased by her kin and used a cardboard disguise at times. Linnea met her here, and Lumi eventually joined her travels; their story is told in Linnea's profile and the Alcyon Chapter.
