## summary
### LITE
Wangshu Inn is a tall inn built on a giant stone pillar at the southern end of Dihua Marsh, on the main road through Liyue's Bishui Plain toward Liyue Harbor. Travelling merchants rest and trade at its stalls, and its top floor has one of the best views in Liyue. Its boss is Verr Goldet, who insists she is the boss and not merely the boss's wife; her husband Huai'an is the innkeeper. Secretly, the whole staff are Qixing agents, and the inn exists to support the Vigilant Yaksha Xiao, who often stands watch on its top floor and is fond of its Almond Tofu.
### STANDARD +
Its name means the moon; originally it names the goddess who drives the moon's chariot.

## geography
### STANDARD
The inn rises from a massive rock pillar among the reeds and water of Dihua Marsh. From the top, in good weather, one can see as far as Mt. Qingce and Jueyun Karst; to the southeast lies the Guyun Stone Forest. Zhongli explains that it was built tall to survey the terrain and placed on one of the main roads into the region, making it a strategic point that must be defended.

## places
### STANDARD
- Lower level: stalls and trading space for travelling merchants, porters, guards for hire and sailors.
- Upper level: the reception and guest rooms, where Huai'an greets guests, and the kitchen of the chef Smiley Yanxiao.
- Top floor: a balcony and viewing deck where Xiao is usually found.
### LARGE +
A calligraphy painting called the Witness Sigil, said by Verr Goldet to have circulated for over a thousand years, hangs in the inn; its pattern matches a rock formation outside. A decorative rockery on the topmost floor bears a mark matching a peak near the Statue of the Seven in the Sea of Clouds.
### ULTRA +
The regular crowd includes travelling merchants such as Great Merchant Huang, a hired guard, sailors, a porter, a poet, and a waitress named Yuhua on the lower level; upstairs are more merchants, the researcher Soraya and other guests.

## people
### STANDARD
Verr Goldet runs the inn, sells its dishes (from both Mondstadt and Liyue) and insists she is "the boss", not the boss's wife. Huai'an, the friendly innkeeper, is a skilled martial artist; Verr says she and the kitchen staff also train, as a hiring requirement. Smiley Yanxiao is the gruff chef who makes Xiao's Almond Tofu. Xiao, the last Guardian Yaksha, stays at the inn between battles.
### LARGE +
Verr claims to come from Mondstadt, and she reads strangers' backgrounds from small details, though one guest notes that no one from Mondstadt seems to know her. Huai'an jokes that he reversed the old rhyme about Liyue girls marrying into "a winery town". Smiley Yanxiao admits he was once a thief who tried to steal from the boss, took a beating and was locked up, and was then taken on in the kitchen; he is terrified of ghosts, once lost narrowly to Xiangling in a Masterful Chefs contest, and Zhongli says he took second prize representing Dihua Marsh in a Moonchase cooking contest in the harbour. A stray cat, Wei, invited itself to stay, and a dog, Richie, is named for good luck. Dusky Ming, the harmless ghost of a little girl, lives at the inn: Xiao found her while hunting demons, and since she was not yet able to move on, he asked the couple to keep her there for a time.

## governance
### STANDARD
The inn needs the Qixing's approval to operate, which Verr admits while dismissing the rest as rumour. In truth it is a front: Ningguang revealed to the Traveler that everyone at Wangshu Inn is one of her people, and that she tracked the Traveler's movements through it. Its real task is to quietly assist Xiao in his fight against the lingering evil of fallen gods.

## culture
### LARGE
Official descriptions call the inn a haven for lovers' moonlit meetings, and folk stories say even the adepti sometimes bask in the moonlight here. Stall-keepers also whisper that the tower hides a mysterious secret, a rumour no one has verified. The couple play cards with their guests.

## history
### STANDARD
The inn is old: Yelan says her clan's elders taught that their ancestor's madness eased for a while only when passing Wangshu Inn, which she credits to the Yaksha's presence. The wiki dates the inn on this basis to at least the years after the cataclysm, about 500 years ago; canon gives no founding date.
### LARGE +
During the Archon Quest the Traveler came here with Moon Carver's message after Rex Lapis' "death", calmed Dusky Ming, and brought Xiao a meal with Almond Tofu; Xiao then went to gather the other adepti. Ningguang later revealed that her agents at the inn had been watching the Traveler.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
A busy roadside stop: traders' stalls at the foot of the pillar, guests coming and going, kitchen smells, and above it all a quiet, windy top floor overlooking silver reeds and water. Folk tales make the place romantic and moonlit; staff stay polite and alert, and say little about one particular "young gentleman".

## dangers
### LARGE
Monsters roam Dihua Marsh, and Huai'an often asks travellers to clear hilichurls from the roads so guests and deliveries can get through; they rarely dare approach the inn itself. Some of the night-time damage the staff blame on rowdy "guests" is really the overflow of Xiao's battles, which they discreetly cover for.

## travel
### LARGE
The inn sits on the main route between Stone Gate and Liyue Harbor. Zhongli notes that Xiao travels often between here and the Guyun Stone Forest, where sealed gods breed monster activity.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the inn operates normally under Verr Goldet and Huai'an and remains Xiao's usual base.
### LARGE +
After the 7.1 Celestial Nail anomaly, Rex Lapis tasked Xiao with monitoring the Chasm, because he travels swiftly and can report at once; how long this keeps him away from the inn is not stated.

## entry: Xiao and the Inn
@keys: Xiao, Alatus, Vigilant Yaksha, Conqueror of Demons
@keys2: Wangshu Inn, Wangshu, Verr Goldet, Almond Tofu
### STANDARD
Xiao, the Vigilant Yaksha, lodges at Wangshu Inn and is usually found on its top floor. The Qixing keep the inn as a base for him, and its staff quietly support his fight against the residue of fallen gods. He rarely eats human food, but Almond Tofu is an exception, because its texture reminds him of the dreams he was once forced to devour. Few guests know he is an adeptus; a bulletin-board message asks about the youth standing on the top floor.
### LARGE +
Each Lantern Rite he battles the year's worst surge of evil, and the couple worry about him during the festival.

## entry: Wangshu (Archon Quest)
@keys: Dusky Ming, Witness Sigil, Smiley Yanxiao
@keys2: Wangshu Inn, Wangshu, Xiao
@version: 1.0
### STANDARD
In the Archon Quest part "Wangshu", the Traveler came to the inn seeking Xiao on Moon Carver's behalf. Xiao warned them off, so Verr Goldet suggested Almond Tofu and a dish of the Traveler's own. The frightened chef refused to cook until the "ghost" in his kitchen was dealt with; using the Witness Sigil, the Traveler found Dusky Ming, who promised to stop teasing guests. Xiao, hearing of Rex Lapis' death, resolved to consult Moon Carver, Mountain Shaper and Cloud Retainer.

## entry: Events at Wangshu Inn
@keys: Unreconciled Stars, Moonlight Merriment
@keys2: Wangshu Inn
@availability: limited
### LARGE
During limited-time events the inn has played small roles. During Unreconciled Stars (1.1), when meteorites spread a sleeping sickness, it set rooms aside for victims. During Moonlight Merriment (2.1), all its rooms were booked by moon-watchers. During the Lantern Rite of 2.4, Zhongli visited the viewing deck hoping to comfort Xiao, and Xiao watched the fireworks from atop the inn.
### ULTRA +
During the Lantern Rite of 6.3, Hu Tao advised the Traveler to rest at the inn; they flew there by chariot with the White Horse Adeptus Zibai, who mused on the name (Xiao explained its meaning), shared a meal with Xiao, for which Zibai cooked the Lang-Gan dish Flavor of Spring, and stayed the night before returning to Liyue Harbor.
