## summary
### LITE
The Dawn Winery is the chief winery of Mondstadt, on a hill in Windwail Highland southwest of the capital. It specialises in dandelion wine and is the estate of the Ragnvindr family, which has owned it for about a thousand years. Its owner is Diluc Ragnvindr, who inherited it four years before the Prologue when his father Crepus died. A game viewpoint describes it as a pilgrimage site for anyone who loves the bottle.
### STANDARD +
Elzer, who handles its affairs and chairs the Mondstadt Wine Guild, says the winery is so successful that it effectively stands for Mondstadt's whole wine industry. Diluc also runs the Angel's Share tavern in the city, which belongs to the same business.

## geography
### STANDARD
The estate lies in the south-western reaches of Mondstadt, between Springvale and the Stone Gate to Liyue. A two-storey manor stands on a small hill; the vineyard circles it in several rows, with a few storage sheds and single-storey buildings nearby. A lake southwest of the manor supplies the winery's water. Local specialties around it include calla lilies, Philanemo mushrooms and windwheel asters.
### LARGE +
The official map description calls it an estate in the land southwest of the city that has handed down Mondstadt's traditional wines and ciders for generations. Each year's output is split: part is sold in Mondstadt and the rest goes out along the trade route south of the city. A Statue of the Seven stands in the area.

## places
### STANDARD
The manor is the heart of the estate, and a game viewpoint is named for it ("Manor of Daybreak"). Inside, an owl statue carries an inscription urging the watcher of the night to remember the splendour of the dawn, and those who contend with evildoers to keep to righteousness. A signboard behind the manor marks an employee resting area.
### LARGE +
Books found in and around the manor include parts of the series "A Drunkard's Tale", and volume 4 of the "Heart of Clear Springs" collection lies on a table by the stairs. The winery sign is written in Teyvat script and carries the name, a welcome and two short sayings (see uncertainties for the motto).

## people
### STANDARD
Diluc Ragnvindr is the owner. Elzer, executive chairman of the Mondstadt Wine Guild, handles the winery's affairs. Adelinde is the head housemaid, with the maids Hillie and Moco; Connor is the winemaker, Ernest the warehouse manager, Tunner a farmer. The late Crepus Ragnvindr, Diluc's father, owned the estate before him.
### LARGE +
Delivery staff named in quests include Fritz, Ben and Magar, and a worker called Hartman. Staff of the Angel's Share tavern include the bartender Charles, the caller Patton and the winemaker Georg, with Luka also listed.

## history
### STANDARD
The Ragnvindr family has held the winery at least since Ragnvindr himself, about a thousand years ago. Crepus Ragnvindr died four years before the game begins and Diluc inherited the estate (see the Mondstadt entry for the disputed details of Crepus' death).
### LARGE +
During the limited-time event Wishful Drops (1.4) the water sources near the winery turned bitter, which Katheryne warned could ruin Mondstadt's wine industry within a few years. Connor sent the Traveler to follow the water northwest; the cause was the young Oceanid Endora and the Oceanid Rhodeia's anger, not the water's composition.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Fragrant with grapes and wine, orderly and hardworking, with signboards advertising seasonal juice and staff who treat the estate as a proud household.

## status
### LITE
In operation as of 7.1, owned by Diluc.
### STANDARD +
Nothing in the pages read shows the estate changing hands or being damaged after the Prologue. Diluc has declined the Knights' invitation to return (see the Mondstadt entry).

## trivia
### ULTRA
The winery's sign, written in Teyvat script, is one of several game items that mention the estate; the furnishing items Dawn Orchard, Lush Grapevine and Newly Grown Grapevines reference it in their descriptions. A signboard near Tunner advertises summer-special grape juice, two bottles for one Mora. Another description says its wines are popular across Teyvat, not only in Mondstadt.

## entry: Dawn Winery motto and sign
@keys: Shine True, Dawn Winery sign, Forget not whence thou came
@version: 1.0
### STANDARD
Diluc's first Character Story gives the winery motto as "Shine True". His fifth story glosses it as: evil cannot go unpunished, never settle for mediocrity, never forget that the dawn will one day come. The in-game sign reads differently (a line about the flow of daylight, and "Forget not whence thou came"), so keep the two accounts separate.

## entry: Wishful Drops and the bitter water
@keys: Wishful Drops, bitter water, Rhodeia, Endora
@keys2: Dawn Winery, Connor
@version: 1.4
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
In the 1.4 event Wishful Drops, water sources on the Mondstadt-Liyue border turned bitter without warning, including the water near the winery. Connor explained that no ordinary purification removed the bitterness, so the water could not be used for wine. The Traveler traced it to the Oceanids: the young Oceanid Endora had come to Mondstadt, Rhodeia mistook her for a threat, and the clash soured the waters. Springvale's spring alone stayed sweet.
