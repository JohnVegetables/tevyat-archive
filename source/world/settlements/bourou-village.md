## summary
### LITE
Bourou Village is an ancient village on Watatsumi Island, the far island of Inazuma opposite Narukami. Its people are said to have come from Enkanomiya deep beneath the waves, and it became a rallying point for those opposed to the Vision Hunt Decree. The village served as a camp where the Watatsumi Army's soldiers trained during its fight against the Shogunate.
### STANDARD +
It is an everyday village of farmers, merchants, a pharmacist and Sangonomiya Samurai, led by the elder Komaki, a fortune-teller. Kokomi's government runs the island, and the village is part of her army's base of support.

## geography
### STANDARD
Bourou Village stands on Watatsumi Island, whose dense foliage and unusual colours set it apart from the rest of Inazuma. The Sangonomiya Shrine is the island's heart, among mountains and waterfalls. The village has an upper and lower level, and a Waverider waypoint lies south of it.
### LARGE +
Nearby is the Mouun Shrine, a separate subarea. Local specialties include Sea Ganoderma. The area holds Electro Seelie, Electro cubes, Thunder Sakura boughs and the usual hilichurls, Fatui skirmishers and Specters, with Ruin machines among the tougher foes.

## places
### STANDARD
The wiki's NPC list splits the village into a lower level, where most residents live, and a smaller upper level. A merchant, Kiyoko, and a pharmacist, Nishikino Reirei, work in the lower village.
### LARGE +
The island's shrine, Sangonomiya Shrine, is separate from the village; Bourou is the main settlement while the shrine is the political and religious centre.

## people
### STANDARD
Komaki is the village elder, an old woman who tells fortunes and tends Spiritherbs. Many villagers are Sangonomiya Samurai of the Watatsumi Army, such as Hiroyuki, Koyama, Matsuda, Ookubo Daisuke, Raku, Kagawa and Nami. The rest include Kiyoko, Mirai, Momoko and Shirai.
### LARGE +
Nishikino Reirei is the pharmacist, Hirai and Iwata live in the upper level. The army's general, Gorou, and its leader, Divine Priestess Sangonomiya Kokomi, are not listed among the village's residents.

## governance
### STANDARD
The island is led by the Sangonomiya Clan, whose Divine Priestess Kokomi commands the Watatsumi Army. Under the Decree it was in open opposition to the Shogunate; the Shogunate called its fighters "rebels".
### LARGE +
After the Vision Hunt Decree was abolished, Kujou Sara met Kokomi to negotiate returning the island to Shogunate rule. Progress was made, but talks stopped when instability appeared in the Shogun's Army because of the Fatui, and the Watatsumi Army stayed on as a peacekeeping force.

## culture
### STANDARD
The islanders worship the Watatsumi Omikami rather than the Raiden Shogun. Komaki's fortune-telling and Spiritherbs are local traditions, and Komaki says that the village has seen more history than most places, though not as much as Sangonomiya Shrine.
### LARGE +
The ancestors of the Watatsumi people came from Enkanomiya and rose to the surface on giant coral branches, which are said to be pieces of Orobashi's body. The island's soil is said to bleach when Enkanomiya's depths try to absorb its spirit, which is why the Watatsumi Mitama Omatsuri is held regularly.

### ULTRA +
The Watatsumi Mitama Omatsuri needs a Jade Coral Branch from Enkanomiya. After the Three Realms Gateway Offering, Kokomi decided to find a new way to counter the Holy Soil phenomenon so that the vishaps would no longer be disturbed. An old Enkanomiya prophecy, recorded in a book, foretells the rebirth of the Dragon of Water in human form.

## history
### STANDARD
The Watatsumi Army trained soldiers at Bourou during its fight against the Shogunate. The village was a rallying point for those who feared losing their Visions or opposed the Vision Hunt Decree.
### LARGE +
According to a quest text cited on the wiki, Orobashi created the island after the Archon War. Orobashi's war on the rest of the archipelago ended with his death, and the Sangonomiya Clan sought peace. Relations between the island and the Shogunate remained tense until the Vision Hunt Decree drove them to an all-time low.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Quiet and coastal, with the colours of Watatsumi's plants, soldiers drilling or talking in the lanes, elders quietly telling fortunes and the sea always nearby.

## quests
### LARGE
Komaki appears in a time-gated Hidden Exploration Objective, informally called Komaki's Spiritherb Fortune, which unlocks after "Divine Plant of the Depths" and sends the Traveler off to nearby sites day by day.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 Bourou Village is calm; the army patrols as peacekeepers and the village lives on.
### STANDARD +
The Decree is gone, but the island has retained its army while the negotiations over return to Shogunate rule are unfinished. No 6.x or 7.0-7.1 plot touches the village.

## entry: Watatsumi Island Background
@keys: Watatsumi Island, Watatsumi Omikami, Watatsumi people
@keys2: Bourou, Komaki, village
@version: 2.1
### STANDARD
Watatsumi Island lies farthest from Narukami Island and is known for plants with unusual colours. Its name comes from a phrase in the ancient Watatsumi language for the god of the oceans. The Sangonomiya Clan leads the people, who worship the Watatsumi Omikami.
### LARGE +
Orobashi is said to have created the island after the Archon War. Its giant corals are broken-off branches of his body. According to the wiki, Orobashi invaded the other islands because Watatsumi lacked resources, though this is also said to have been due to a death sentence from the Heavenly Principles for learning forbidden knowledge. The war ended with Orobashi's death and the Sangonomiya Clan seeking peace.

## entry: Watatsumi Army
@keys: Watatsumi Army, Sangonomiya Rebels, the resistance
@keys2: Bourou, Watatsumi Island, Kokomi
@version: 2.1
### STANDARD
The Watatsumi Army, also called the resistance, is led by Sangonomiya Kokomi and opposes the Vision Hunt Decree. It includes people who feared losing or had lost their Visions, and others who simply opposed the Decree. After the Decree ended, it remains as peacekeepers, helps rebuild the island's economy and works to clear the Fatui from Watatsumi Island.
### LARGE +
Gorou is its general, with Kaida Ryouma among officers. The wiki lists the Traveler as captain of Swordfish II. The Shogunate calls its fighters "Sangonomiya Rebels".
