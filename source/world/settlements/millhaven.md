## summary
### LITE
Millhaven is a peaceful, tranquil village in Windrest Peak, in northern Mondstadt, known for its sweet-smelling flowers and fragrance products. It is an idyllic place of windmills and fields of the Windrest Flower. Its loading screen calls it a peaceful and tranquil town.
### STANDARD +
Windrest Flowers grow only in Millhaven's fields and are a key ingredient in perfume making. The village appeared with Windrest Peak in version 6.5.

## geography
### STANDARD
Millhaven lies in Windrest Peak, near Dornman Port. It has windmills (one in the northeast), flower fields, a bulletin board and shops. Local specialties include Windrest Flower, calla lilies, dandelion seeds, Etherwing moths, Philanemo mushrooms and windwheel asters.
### LARGE +
Windrest Flower is found only in the village's flower fields. A harpastum can be found here as an exploration object.

## places
### STANDARD
Scents and Senses is the fragrance shop of Annick, who trades a Special Perfume: Wind's Breath for Windrest Flowers. Shattuck is run by Benthem. Estragon is a merchant.
### LARGE +
A small signboard near Shattuck displays a notice by Prune, who signs as a Witch Hunter and accuses a witch codenamed "A". Replies from anonymous locals mock or redirect the notice, and the Traveler can tear it down (an open-world interactable).

## people
### STANDARD
Annick (perfumer), Estragon (merchant), Lilian (florist), Benthem (shop owner), Genna (Knight of Favonius), Leonore (veteran of the Combined Arms Company), Leopoldine, Tyutcheva and Uchida Kotaro live or work here.
### LARGE +
Prune and Klee show up in open-world scenes: doodles of Dodoco on walls draw a remark from Prune about crayon that will not wash off, and Klee offers to blow the wall up.

## culture
### STANDARD
Fragrance making is the village's trade. A folk tale from Dornman Port says the first Windrest Flower grew from the lyre of the Lord of the Wind, who healed snow-covered wounds in the age of white earth; the Church does not recognise it as a miracle. The Archive text adds that the real credit belongs to countless humble people whose names are forgotten.
### LARGE +
A dish called Millhaven's Morning Plate was added to cooking in 6.5.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Scented, gentle and slow; flower fields rolling under turning windmills, with children's mischief (doodles, notices) and squirrels to add humour.

## status
### LITE
Peaceful and unchanged as of 7.1; no Millhaven content from 7.0-7.1 was found.

## trivia
### ULTRA
Five doodles of Dodoco are scattered around the village; investigating three reveals a note saying the drawn bomb has been defused. A Weasel Thief steals squirrels' pinecones near the northeastern windmill, and the Traveler can recover them. The village's viewpoint is titled "Blossoms in Fragrant Bloom", and Windrest Peak's own viewpoint is "The Northerly Wind's Soft Hymn".

## entry: Windrest Flower
@keys: Windrest Flower, Scents and Senses, Special Perfume: Wind's Breath
### STANDARD
A fragrant, delicate flower found only in Millhaven's fields (24 can be found every two days in game). Five can be traded to Annick for Special Perfume: Wind's Breath. Dornman Port folklore credits its origin to the lyre of Venti.
