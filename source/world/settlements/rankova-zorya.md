## summary
### LITE
Rankova Zorya is a railway halt in the Volkodlak Tundra of Snezhnaya that was nearly abandoned until Alyosha, Odette and Vodyanitsa, with the Traveler's help, renovated it. It now has a furnace, a stage, a Bing-Bang Finchball stall and a restaurant, and new residents have arrived. In 7.1 the battle against Ronova, the Ruler of Death, took place here.
### STANDARD +
The in-game description calls it a temporary station on the verge of abandonment. At a time when Snezhnayans' desire to develop the tundra has weakened, someone wanting to maintain it is described as quite romantic. The name means 'dawn'.

## geography
### STANDARD
A small station village on the open tundra with a train track, a platform, a stage for dancers, a furnace that provides stable heat, a restaurant (Restaurant Zorya) and a carriage that serves as a base. Snowmen near the stall can be decorated with hats and scarves after 'Newcomers in the Snow'. A Teleport Waypoint, Prime Ice and a Stellar Vortex are nearby.
### LARGE +
A cliff north of the village holds a Jack Frost puzzle. Vodyanitsa says the rumbling of arriving trains hides her singing practice, and jokes that her voice might delay a train's departure.

## places
### STANDARD
Restaurant Zorya, with the restaurant vendor Tolstaya, opens after 'Daybreak After the Snow'. Bing-Bang Finchball stall: a game booth run by Mayskaya on behalf of Basima, decorated by Vodyanitsa. The stage, where Odette coaches Rytova. The furnace, which was repaired using a Smelted Kristall Ore obtained from a fae gang. A Bulletin Board and a Vestnik stand that sells newspapers.
### LARGE +
Vestnik articles available here include 'The Headless Train Attendant in the Snow', a Transport Bureau statement about unusual disturbances along the railway line, and 'The Myth of Al-Ahmar, the False King'.

## people
### STANDARD
Alyosha, Odette and Vodyanitsa are the founders and recurring presences: Alyosha picks guard dogs (including Lyumina) and makes Rimecurrant ice cream; Odette keeps the place safe and coaches the dancer Rytova; Vodyanitsa manages the station, builds the Finchball stall and sings on the restaurant roof. Domovoy friends of Rytova, whom she is teaching to dance, live nearby; Zarubina and Tomilina join her practice.
### LARGE +
Other named residents: Mayskaya, Shatskaya, Knyazev (a general goods vendor), Shishkina, Cherkassky, Dorokhov, Golovin, Ignatov, Khrustalev, Nezhdanov, Vakhrushina, Yarkova, Zheleznov, Zimnyakov and Krivda (tied to a hidden exploration objective). Vesna may appear as a confused traveller who got off at the wrong stop, and Tartaglia and Vodyanitsa are seen talking at the restaurant, where he says he and his siblings are fans and that he waits in line for show tickets for his sister Tonia.
### ULTRA +
Rytova's dance practice is Odette's side project: Odette noticed her struggling in the snow, told her talent without opportunity can be a curse, and said that seeds that survive winter sprout with deep roots. Odette is blunt but invested, and she asks Rytova to teach the Domovoy the basic steps so she consolidates her own learning.

## history
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
The renovation began with a chain of World Quests after the Mistilteinn Archon Quest. 'Atop the Floating Snow': Odette coaches Rytova. 'Awaiting the Dawn': Vodyanitsa and Mayskaya set up the Finchball stall. 'A Thread of Dawn-Light': Alyosha wants to repair the heat source and, with the Traveler, obtains a power core. 'Daybreak After the Snow' completes the restaurant and the heat. 'Newcomers in the Snow' adds Vodyanitsa's snowmen, which stand in for her, Odette and Alyosha.
### LARGE +
Alyosha says the idea began as something Vodyanitsa randomly raised at a party. He explained that the government is too busy with a grander plan to notice a small station, so citizens must step up, which he can do without taking up his gun for the Rokot. Odette drove bandits out of the area to keep the trains safe; Vodyanitsa arranged an extra trade route with merchants she knows.
### ULTRA +
In Chapter VII, Act IV the Jester's plan brings the Traveler, Dainsleif and the Snezhnayan allies here, where Mitya delivers equipment for Project Mistilteinn. When Ronova arrives to punish the researchers, she turns Rankova Zorya into a battlefield; the Traveler is killed and returns through memories of other nations. After the battle, Celestia has taken back her authority and her Curse of Death on Snezhnayan technology lapses, so Mistilteinn can proceed, and within days the buildings are restored.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Cosy and slightly chaotic: bright restaurant windows against the dark tundra, a snowman 'supervising' practice, dogs trotting among stalls, Vodyanitsa singing from the roof and the roar of arriving trains. Money is tight but optimism is high.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Bandits and dangerous elements have been removed from the area by Odette; ambient dangers elsewhere on the tundra remain.

## status
### LITE
A living village as of 7.1. Its founders are busy with other work, so they visit instead of living there permanently.
### STANDARD +
The 7.1 battle damaged the site, but the structures were restored within a few days.

## entry: Restaurant Zorya
@keys: Restaurant Zorya, Tolstaya, Rimecurrant ice cream
@keys2: Rankova Zorya
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
The restaurant opens after 'Daybreak After the Snow' with a signboard that Vodyanitsa drew of herself, Odette and Alyosha as ingredients in a pot, and a free meal for the first customer, who turns out to be Mitya. Vodyanitsa has been studying a book called '100 Train Station Delicacies' and teases that nobody eats her cooking. She admits to a three-bowls-a-week limit on Rimecurrant ice cream, which she is tempted to stretch.

## entry: Bing-Bang Finchball
@keys: Bing-Bang Finchball, Finchball, Mayskaya, Basima
@keys2: Rankova Zorya
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
A game booth where players launch Finchballs into scoring rings. Mayskaya runs it because Basima is busy with a new Finchball mode. Vodyanitsa, who manages the station, wants the pieces dressed fancy, beautiful and cute (in that order) and offers clothes from her own closet; she later toys with dressing them as Odette and Alyosha.

## entry: The Battle of Rankova Zorya
@keys: Ronova, Curse of Death, Project Mistilteinn, Rankova Zorya battle
@keys2: Rankova Zorya, Mitya, Dainsleif
@version: 7.1
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
Ronova, the Ruler of Death, attacked the researchers delivering Mistilteinn equipment while the Tsaritsa met Rhinedottir elsewhere. At the Jester's request the Traveler stalled her, with Dainsleif, Odette and Vesna. Her Curse of Death sent the Traveler to the afterlife temporarily, and Vesna's Authority of Life created Seals of Life from the Traveler's memories to escape it. Once Celestia reclaimed her authority she was stripped of power and defeated, and the curse was undone.
