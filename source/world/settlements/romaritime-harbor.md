## summary
### LITE
Romaritime Harbor is a large coastal elevator and observation deck built into a waterfall on Fontaine's edge. A lift carries people between the harbor below and the top of the falls, where the Clementine Line aquabus runs on rails to the Court of Fontaine. It is also the way to the Girdle of the Sands in Sumeru, and Aeval calls Romaritime Station "one of the great gateways of Fontaine".
### STANDARD +
Its name joins the French words for rosemary (romarin) and maritime.

## geography
### STANDARD
The harbor has a lower level at the waterline and an upper level reached by the great lift, which takes a long time even by elevator. The thundering waterfall drowns out the noise of arrivals below. A Statue of the Seven and a Waverider waypoint are here, and the aquabus line begins at the top.
### LARGE +
The harbor lies in the Belleau Region. The Maison Gardiennage's notice warns visitors to follow the prescribed route and use the lift, and disclaims responsibility for injuries from gliders or other dangerous shortcuts.

## places
### STANDARD
The lower level has the docks and a security post; the upper level has the aquabus station and the harbor's Navbed, Rena, who handles queries about the aquabus. A bulletin board outside the lift carries the Harbormaster's notices.
### LARGE +
According to the Clementine Line tour, the great lift was designed to work on several energy sources.
### ULTRA +
An engineer riding the aquabus, Edwin Eastinghouse, spoke of a mysterious Arkhium that could lift even heavier things, and wanted to use it to raise cargo ships from below the station; Aeval says this was probably not a joke.

## people
### STANDARD
Lower level: Augereau (a member of the Gardes), Baguette (the harborworker hound), Etienne (harbor security), Everallin (Marechaussee Phantom) and Lectoure (a sailor). Upper level: Baiji, Cheriez, Galathee (boat captain), Hermosa (a self-proclaimed adventurer), Rena, Siora (Marechaussee Phantom) and Zadra. Melusine guides such as Aeval run the aquabus tours.
### LARGE +
Melusines work as aquabus guides in uniform; the Marechaussee Phantom is largely made up of Melusines.

## governance
### STANDARD
A Harbormaster issues notices; the Maison Gardiennage polices the harbor, with Gardes and Marechaussee Phantom on site. All duelling in Fontaine requires registration, and unapproved duels are banned.

## culture
### STANDARD
The bulletin board tells visitors to check local rules, avoid homemade potions, use licensed divers and beware of scams. One ad offers dive services at 30,000 Mora per minute, and a reply points out that even Freminet, the best diver around, does not charge that much.
### LARGE +
Aeval, the Melusine guide of the Clementine Line, cheerfully narrates the journey; one of her tour talks covers the Steambird's serialised novels and Merusea Village.

## history
### STANDARD
The harbor provides passage to the Girdle of the Sands in Sumeru. The Callas Line, a sister aquabus route, was suspended after the Fontaine Research Institute's explosion; the Clementine Line is the line that ends here.
### LARGE +
In the finale of Masquerade of the Guilty (4.2), the Traveler and Charlotte meet Lyney, Lynette and Freminet at the harbor; Lyney says their "Father" has been given the Hydro Gnosis, and Arlecchino appears, saying that as a Harbinger it is her duty to hand it to the Tsaritsa. In Chapter V (5.0), the Traveler says goodbye to Fontaine friends, including Furina, Navia, Clorinde and Charlotte, at the docks before leaving for Natlan.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
The official description stresses the thunder of the waterfall drowning out the harbor's comings and goings, and a wait for the lift that is forgotten once the Clementine Line carries you toward the heart of the nation of Hydro.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Mostly everyday hazards: the lift's long ascent, unsafe gliding, and Eremites active in the nearby desert, which the Maison Gardiennage warns merchants about. The notice board also warns that Fontaine's currents can be treacherous for unlicensed divers.

## travel
### STANDARD
Clementine Line aquabus: Court of Fontaine main station to Romaritime Station (the terminus). Passage onward to the Girdle of the Sands in Sumeru. Visitors can call an aquabus from the bench if none is at the station.
### LARGE +
The planned Lumidouce Harbor line, serving the other major harbor in Erinnyes Forest, has not been built.

## quests
### LARGE
Features in the Archon Quest finale of Chapter IV (4.2), the start of Chapter V Act I, Natlan! A New Adventure (5.0), and many Fontaine journeys. The Clementine Line is named for Navia's late mother.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 Romaritime Harbor is a working port and aquabus terminus. No later canon changes its state.
### STANDARD +
The bulletin board still lists the Callas Line as suspended indefinitely; according to the Aquabus entry Navia decided afterwards to rebuild it and add stops. The Traveler left for Natlan from here in 5.0.

## entry: Clementine Line
@keys: Clementine Line, Aeval
@keys2: Romaritime, Fontaine, aquabus
@version: 4.0
### STANDARD
The Clementine Line is the aquabus line between the Court of Fontaine and Romaritime Station, operated by the Melusine guide Aeval. It was named for Clementine, Navia's late mother, and the system was designed with Callas's cooperation. The engineer considered a second waterway for cargo but dropped it because there was no demand.
### LARGE +
Callas's insistence on building the aquabus lines led Romeuf to break away from the Spina; after the Prophecy of Fontaine was dealt with, Romeuf's faction tried to topple the line's supports to bury Poisson.
