## summary
### LITE
Favonius Keep is the Knights of Favonius' camp in Nod-Krai, on Wavechaser Plain. It is the base of Grand Master Varka's long expedition from Mondstadt: tents, a command tent with a meeting point, and a small garrison that gathers intelligence on the Wild Hunt and other Abyssal threats. It is deliberately modest and a little scruffy, and it smells of Mondstadt dandelion wine whenever a shipment has come in.
### STANDARD +
Varka describes it as a station for sharing what the knights have learned about the Abyss with the Northern Intelligence Network, so the camp is half military post, half listening post. Any Knights of Favonius camp is open to the Honorary Knight (the Traveler), by Varka's own word.

## geography
### STANDARD
The camp sits on Wavechaser Plain in northern Nod-Krai, a cold, windswept open country that the wiki links to the Pohjola island group; the Pillar of Embla and the Amsvartnir lake area lie in the same plain. Pine Amber can be gathered nearby.
### LARGE +
Varka's justification for an outpost in Nod-Krai is that countless winds cross it every day.

## places
### STANDARD
A large command tent (where Varka is usually found and where the meeting point stands), smaller tents for the knights and a quartermaster, a training patch used for drills, and a store of casks. A tent near the entrance houses Godwin once he has joined the force.
### LARGE +
The camp is the knights' mail room too. Varka hands three letters to the knight Mika for the Lightkeepers' headquarters at Piramida, for Nefer of the Curatorium of Secrets, and for Mondstadt, with the instruction to keep up the same communication routine as before.

## people
### STANDARD
Commander Anselm ("Expedition Team" Garrison Commander, formerly Captain of the 6th Company) runs the garrison, with the knights Ekbert, Erminie, Laurent, Leifhelm, Nannina and Winfried. Grand Master Varka is the camp's leader and central figure. Anselm, Nannina and Laurent are described as handling administrative work left behind by Cardinal Seamus Pegg, who has since returned to Mondstadt.
### LARGE +
Ekbert is from Springvale; he, Leifhelm and Winfried are the three who get sobered up with parade drills after the wine arrives. Erminie keeps the liquor stock and also helps devise safer explosives so the camp is not blown up after a night out. Godwin joined the force late: he missed the departure from Mondstadt, was later sent north by Varka to make his own way, and now serves in a tent at the entrance. Mika, a knight in Varka's force, carries the Grand Master's letters out of the camp.

## history
### STANDARD
Varka led the expedition out of Mondstadt on the strength of a warning of an Abyssal storm in the north, with no clear idea where it would strike. The Keep is where the force settled in Nod-Krai to gather intelligence and fight the Wild Hunt. After the Wild Hunt's leader Rerir was sealed, Varka told the Traveler that things had not entirely quietened and that the camp would become a long-term intelligence station.
### LARGE +
Varka's own account, told on a night of feasting, is that the force marched knowing only that an Abyssal storm was gathering and that every hour of hesitation cost lives. He says different Abyssal disasters have been occurring all over Teyvat, always shifting and never alike, and wants the Northern Intelligence Network to share findings with the knights.

## culture
### STANDARD
Drinking is treated as serious business and also strictly regulated. The expeditionary force may drink, but there are rules about when and how much, and one sober guard is always kept on watch over the rest. Varka has kept that duty for himself on the nights that matter, and jokes that the cup in his hand is the most special brew, only to reveal apple cider.
### LARGE +
Barrel-rolling is a Knights of Favonius training drill, teaching force control and precision. Varka tells anecdotes of running it with Noelle, Razor, and the Spark Knight Klee, and of Lohen, who skipped it to poke a monster nest instead. The knights are homesick: the first taste of Dawn Winery dandelion wine in years makes Ekbert, Leifhelm and Winfried wistful for Mondstadt, and Ekbert asks the Traveler about his home village of Springvale.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Wild Hunt stragglers wander the plain without a leader and with a primal urge to destroy; the Lightkeepers and Varka's knights hold them in check. Pale-Furred Wolves hunt on the slopes around the camp and cornered the wine courier Donatas at the foot of a mountain.
### LARGE +
Varka expects a larger threat behind the stragglers. At the time of the Lupus Majoris story the knights faced a red wolf calling itself Roland, a Wild Hunt leader raised by the Abyss after Rerir fell, and the Keep served as the base where they brought samples and held council.

## travel
### STANDARD
The Keep has a meeting point (unlocked by On This Intoxicating Night) that can be built up with the Traveler's help. Reaching it means travelling to Nod-Krai and crossing Wavechaser Plain; the Traveler's own trip north in Varka's story quest was made on Dvalin's back.
### LARGE +
Supplies from Mondstadt arrive slowly: the Dawn Winery dandelion wine in On This Intoxicating Night is carried most of the way across the continent and nearly lost at the foot of a mountain near the camp.

## quests
### STANDARD
On This Intoxicating Night (world quest, 6.3) starts here and unlocks the meeting point; Like Seeds on the Wind follows at construction level 5. Varka's story quest, To Those Who Embark on the Expedition (Lupus Majoris Chapter Act I, 6.4), ends with a council at the Keep. The anecdotes Varka: Communications and Varka: A Suitable Weapon are set here.
### LARGE +
Anecdote text in A Suitable Weapon has Varka training with no suitable weapon or ore to hand, for himself or for Noelle; he concludes the matter will wait until everyone is back in Mondstadt.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Canvas and woodsmoke against pale northern light, with the smell of Mondstadt wine and a few knights drilling or arguing over rations. The camp is understaffed, a bit of a ghost town by Varka's own joke, and cheerful about it. Evenings are the loudest.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the Keep is a small Knights of Favonius garrison in Nod-Krai that Varka still uses; most of the expedition has returned to Mondstadt.
### STANDARD +
The garrison is led by Commander Anselm, with Cardinal Seamus Pegg gone home and Varka present on his visits. The camp is a quiet intelligence post focused on the Wild Hunt and Abyssal activity. Nothing in 7.0-7.1 changes this.
### ULTRA +
Varka has said the force will form a lasting partnership with the Northern Intelligence Network. Anecdote text hints the knights will return to Mondstadt in time, so the camp's long-term role is open.

## entry: Varka's Command and the Drinking Rules

@version: 6.3
### ULTRA
In the Keep, Varka plays the jovial Grand Master who hides seriousness behind jokes. He says every Knights of Favonius camp belongs to the Honorary Knight, will not let a barrel of wine be opened until the wine is rested properly and the whole camp is back, and runs sobering drills the morning after. Underneath is deliberate leadership: a sober watch every night, intelligence before action, and the conviction that the force should march as soon as lives are at stake. This conversation takes place in the quest On This Intoxicating Night.

### ULTRA
In Varka's story quest the Traveler, ferried by Dvalin, reaches Nod-Krai as Varka's knights finish off a wave of Wild Hunt remnants. Back at the Keep, Varka meets the Traveler, Jahoda, Ineffa, Flins, Albedo and Durin. The group learns the attacks are led not by Rerir but by a Roland-shaped creature the Abyss raised to replace him, which has corrupted the remains of Andrius' soul. The Traveler hands over the item Boreas prepared, and Varka draws the wolf spirit into his claymore, glimpsing Boreas' last thoughts.
