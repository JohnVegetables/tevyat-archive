## summary
### LITE
Port Belovodye is a port in Snezhnaya that earns enormous sums. It is known chiefly from one historical episode: in the Tsaritsa's second year, its human inhabitants declared self-governance and rebelled against the fae princes, and in her third year the newly created Fatui brought them back, ending the revolt by negotiation.
### STANDARD +
Canon does not show the port in game as of 7.1. Its name echoes the folklore realm Belovodye, 'land of white waters'.

## history
### STANDARD
In the winter of the second year after Anastasya became Tsaritsa of All Snezhnaya, the humans of Port Belovodye used the death of the Belyi Tsar as their chance to declare self-governance. The fae Grand Duke Drevogorye marched on them and failed. The following year the Tsaritsa sent troops of the new Fatui, occupying the lands of several fae clans on the way, to attack the independent government. On the tenth day of the third month she sent envoys, who calmed the separatists and asked them to hand over the instigators, promising amnesty to everyone else. They accepted, and the port was spared from being levelled.
### LARGE +
The same chronicle (A Brief History of the Pale Starborn) records that the Tsaritsa did not muster the older Druzhna but founded the Fatui for the campaign, that the Drevogorye, Volkhovan and Cirnareka clans lost their princely titles for negligence, and that Grand Duke Drevogorye accused the Tsaritsa herself of treason and died fighting the Fatui. Thirteen days later, on the twenty-third day of the same month, she demanded that the absent fae princes surrender their authority for honorary titles.

## economy
### STANDARD
The port is very profitable. Someone's Research Notes, written by an unnamed boastful researcher, claim that even 5 per cent of the port's income would be an acceptable reward for his work.

## sources_note
### ULTRA
The chronicle is a book on fae history that is believed from internal details to have been written by fae elders, so the revolt is told from the fae side. The human rebels' own reasons and leaders are not recorded in what was read, and the identities of the instigators who were handed over are not given.

## status
### LITE
No events beyond the old revolt are shown; the port is not accessible in the game as of 7.1.
