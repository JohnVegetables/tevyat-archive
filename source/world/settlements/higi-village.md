## summary
### LITE
Higi Village is a small village on Yashiori Island in Inazuma, whose main export was Crystal Marrow. The war between the Watatsumi Army and the Shogun's Army, and then the Tatarigami curse of the serpent god Orobashi, emptied it. Most residents fled, died or were sacrificed; the chief Washizu, once a kind man, went mad, while a boy named Chouji tries to sell Crystal Marrow to leave.
### STANDARD +
The village's secret is grim: Washizu drew passing travellers into praying at his shrine, then tried to kill them as sacrifices to the voice in his head. The Traveler kills him in self-defence after several days of prayer. The pharmacist Yasumoto and the samurai Inaba Kyuuzou disappear along the way.

## geography
### STANDARD
Higi lies on Yashiori Island in Inazuma. A Statue of the Seven stands on the northeastern side of the village, and the viewpoint for Serpent's Head, which lies southeast of the village, can be found inside it. The shrine at which Washizu keeps watch stands in the village, and Jakotsu Mine lies nearby.
### LARGE +
Local plants include the Naku Weed, which is steeped in Tatarigami energy. Thunder Sakura Boughs and Electro Seelie are found here, as are Kairagi and Nobushi (rogue samurai) who prowl the village. A Bake-Danuki can be found too.

## places
### STANDARD
The village shrine, the chief's house, the pharmacist's house, Jakotsu Mine (where Chouji lives) and the central tree.
### LARGE +
Items found around the village include the Incomplete Register, Washizu's list of the dead and sacrificed; the Incomplete Notes; Yasumoto's Pharmacist's Notebook; and the Sword Hilt tied to the quest Put Them To Rest.
### ULTRA +
Chouji is counted as an inhabitant of Higi Village although he lives at Jakotsu Mine.

## people
### STANDARD
Washizu: village chief, now deranged. Yasumoto: pharmacist, student of the late Naoko. Chouji: a boy selling Crystal Marrow near Jakotsu Mine. Chouji's mother and father: both gone from the village.
### LARGE +
Before the Tatarigami, Washizu is described as a kind man who set up shrines to pacify Orobashi's soul and welcomed outsiders; he is mentioned in the Teyvat Travel Guide as having greeted the traveller Alice with a feast. Naoko, the village pharmacist, went mad and died after being locked up. "Uncle" Onitaka's pirates have a symbiotic relationship with the village.

## governance
### STANDARD
Washizu governed as village chief with help from the village's pharmacists. Under the Shogunate, officers regularly visited to prevent the village from siding with the resistance and to take its Crystal Marrow, the main export, for the war.
### LARGE +
Early in the war Washizu pooled the villagers' wealth and shared it equally, setting a part aside for the local pirates, and decided to guard the shrine as his duty. His notes complain that neither the Shogunate nor the Watatsumi Army cared about the village beyond propaganda.

## culture
### STANDARD
Higi's way of life revolved around Crystal Marrow, a crystal containing a sliver of Tatarigami power that makes smelted metal stronger and tougher, and around shrines to quiet Orobashi's soul.
### LARGE +
Some of the sick fled to the local pirates for help. The "medicine" of lavender melon and sugar soup that Yasumoto made was a comfort rather than a cure.

## history
### STANDARD
The village was one of the places worst hit by the Watatsumi Army-Shogun's Army war: constant losses, officers commandeering Crystal Marrow, refugees from Jakotsu Mine and a shortage of food. According to the wiki, when Orobashi fanatics in the Watatsumi Army broke the wards over Yashiori, the Tatarigami returned and the villagers fell sick or went mad. Many fled; some joined the pirates.
### LARGE +
Washizu began recording the dead in a register, then became unstable and began sacrificing villagers and outsiders to the voice he called "Him". Chouji's mother, whom he named as special to this god, escaped with the pirates to Sumeru, hoping that the Akademiya could cure her fever and bleeding. The villagers told Chouji his father had died; the pirates' diaries imply he left after recovering and chose to vanish.
### ULTRA +
Yasumoto, working on a vaccine with Naku Weed, left his scattered notes and went to the southern shoals to test it on himself. Inaba Kyuuzou, a Tenryou Commission samurai, was besieged by pirates, was helped by the Traveler, and vanished leaving a letter of apology; the Commission has a high desertion rate because of the horrors of the war.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
A hush hangs over empty houses under a persistent storm. Crystal Marrow is found near the mine, pages of notebooks are scattered about, and the chief sits muttering at the shrine.

## dangers
### LARGE
The Tatarigami: illness, hallucinations and madness; the enemies who roam the village; the chief Washizu, who attacks those who pray too long; and pirates around Nazuchi Beach. Anyone without a Vision is at greater risk of the curse.

## quests
### LARGE
Washizu's Prayers, Sinister Instruction, Gazing Three Thousand Miles Away (Chouji), Put Them To Rest, Pay Your Respects, and the Hidden Exploration Objective Tatarigami Investigation Expedition.
### ULTRA +
Naku Weed gathering for Yasumoto and the search for the village's records link Higi with Jakotsu Mine, Nazuchi Beach and Fort Mumei.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 Higi Village remains largely abandoned; Washizu is dead and the curse has not been lifted.
### STANDARD +
Chouji has set out to find his parents, Yasumoto is missing and the chief is dead. No 6.x or 7.0-7.1 content revisits it.

## entry: Washizu and the Outsider Shrine
@keys: Washizu, Outsider Shrine, Incomplete Register
@version: 2.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Washizu was Higi Village's chief and a kind, welcoming man until the Tatarigami drove him mad. He took to praying at a shrine and urged strangers to join him, leaving rewards at their doorsteps. Over several days the Traveler prays with him, and on the last he tries to stab them, defeated in combat. His notes show he had been sacrificing villagers and passersby to a voice he calls "Him", using their belongings to lure others.
### LARGE +
He accused Yasumoto of being a fraud, locked up the pharmacist Naoko and another man when they went mad, and listed the sacrificed in the Incomplete Register. After failing to recapture Chouji's mother, he turned to the Traveler, whom he recorded as a "Nameless Outlander".

## entry: Chouji
@keys: Chouji, Zushi Chouji
@keys2: Higi Village, Jakotsu Mine, Crystal Marrow
@version: 2.0
### STANDARD
Chouji is a boy who lives near Jakotsu Mine. After the village emptied and his mother left, he began selling Crystal Marrow to travellers at inflated prices to earn the Mora to leave Yashiori and search for his parents. He is serious beyond his years, bargains sharply, and is wary of both Washizu and Yasumoto. The Traveler helps him mine; in return he teaches how to cook Imported Poultry and asks them to learn about his mother at the local shrines.
### LARGE +
He believes his mother left without him and thinks his father is dead; neither is true: the Tatarigami flowed freely in his mother, who left for Sumeru with the pirate Onitaka in search of a cure, and the pirates' diaries imply his father left under the curse's influence.

## entry: The Tatarigami on Yashiori
@keys: Tatarigami, Orobashi's curse, Yashiori Island
@keys2: Higi Village, Crystal Marrow, village
@version: 2.0
### STANDARD
The Tatarigami is the curse formed from the will and power of the serpent god Orobashi after Ei slew him on Yashiori Island, kept in check by wards. When the wards fail it causes thunderstorms, withering plants and untreatable illness. Victims suffer bleeding, fever, rashes, migraines, dizziness, hallucinated voices and madness, and Vision-holders are less affected.
### LARGE +
Some people resist it: Chouji and Yasumoto show no symptoms, and Chouji's mother stayed clear-headed while ill. Crystal Marrow, found on Orobashi's bones, carries a sliver of the power.
