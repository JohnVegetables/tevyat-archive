## summary
### LITE
Caravan Ribat is a fortified village at the foot of the Wall of Samiel, the great wall that keeps desert sandstorms from the rainforests of Sumeru. It is the checkpoint between the forest and the desert, where travellers going to or from Aaru Village and the Great Red Sand must pass, and it is full of Eremites. Its official tagline calls it the frontier beneath the high wall.
### STANDARD +
Its name uses the Arabic ribat, a small frontier fortification that later served as a caravanserai defending trade routes. The Ribat sits in the Ashavan Realm of the Dharma Forest, so it feels like a border town that belongs to both worlds.

## geography
### STANDARD
Caravan Ribat sits directly under the Wall of Samiel, the barrier between the Dharma Forest and the Great Red Sand. The road through it links the forest side (towards Gandharva Ville and Sumeru City) with the desert side (Aaru Village and the Great Red Sand).
### LARGE +
The wall is described as an enormous barrier against searing sandstorms. Official text notes it also blocks the spread of "wisdom's blessing", asking whether divine wisdom is not meant to shine on all people.

## places
### STANDARD
The Caravan Ribat Bulletin Board carries notices such as a recruitment drive for Wall of Samiel maintenance and a reminder that destroying the wall is prohibited. Gilded Journey is a business run by Maryam, with Maziar as an employee.

## people
### STANDARD
Eremite mercenaries (Aybak, Nasir, Safie, Thiyya) work here alongside merchants (Hawad, Yinjie), an adventurer (Baharak) and researchers (Behnam, Dunya, Juzjani, Ma'asudi, Railah) who monitor the wall. Arshia is also listed.
### LARGE +
Behnam is one researcher who states that the Akademiya actively maintains the wall to stop the desert eroding the rainforest.

## governance
### STANDARD
No local authority is named. The Akademiya looks after the wall itself; the village is described as the checkpoint between forest and desert.

## culture
### STANDARD
Many people here are Eremites, and the village is a place where the desert and the Akademiya's world meet. Dehya says that as she grew up she realised the wall is not only there to keep out sandstorms but also to keep out desert people like her.

## history
### STANDARD
Collei tells the Traveler that Greater Lord Rukkhadevata created the rainforests and the Wall of Samiel around the desert, to protect the rainforests from sandstorms. Its upkeep has since passed to the Akademiya.
### LARGE +
Samiel is another name for the simoom, a strong dry dust-laden wind of the Sahara and Arabian deserts.
### ULTRA +
In the Chinese name the place is a "post station" for caravans; other regional names stress the caravan or the ribat. The ribat was originally a frontier fortress for volunteer defenders, later used as a caravanserai.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
A frontier stop beneath a towering wall: Eremite mercenaries, merchants, researchers and adventurers gather around a bulletin board, and the village is a way-station between forest and desert.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Sandstorms beyond the wall; in Chapter III the Traveler is trailed by Eremites here and needs Alhaitham's help to get away.

## travel
### STANDARD
Caravan Ribat is the usual stop for anyone crossing between Gandharva Ville and the forest and Aaru Village and the desert.

## quests
@version: 3.1
### STANDARD
In the Archon Quest the Traveler and Paimon narrowly escape Eremites trailing them at Caravan Ribat, thanks to Alhaitham; he then offers to go with them to Aaru Village. While searching for the missing Village Keepers, Dehya takes the Traveler back to the Ribat to meet her drinking friends.
### LARGE +
The 4.6 Cyno story quest "Footprints Sunken Beneath the Sands" begins here and continues to Aaru Village and the Temple of Silence. In the 6.6 Welkin Moon story "Your Past Is Like a Garden" Dehya and Candace, having escorted people home, talk here with Sethos before heading back to Aaru Village.
### ULTRA +
During the Village Keepers search, Dehya meets her drinking buddy Zaki at the local tavern and says she has a spice-trading deal for Delavar. Zaki leads her and the Traveler to the spot, which proves to be an ambush by Delavar and Engur, Eremite radicals; Dehya and the Traveler defeat them, and the captives are questioned back in Aaru Village.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 Caravan Ribat is an operating checkpoint under the Wall of Samiel. Nothing in canon shows it destroyed or abandoned.
### STANDARD +
It was last shown as a gathering place in the 6.6 Welkin Moon story, with Candace and Dehya saying they have finished escorting people home and are heading back to Aaru Village.

## entry: The Four at Loggerheads at Caravan Ribat
@keys: Caravan Ribat, Wall of Samiel
@keys2: Alhaitham, Dehya, Eremite, Traveler
@version: 3.1
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
In Chapter III Act III the Traveler and Paimon escape Eremites trailing them at Caravan Ribat, thanks to Alhaitham, who says he is still studying the capsule and suspects the Traveler is hiding something. He offers to accompany them to Aaru Village, and they accept. Later, in Act IV, Dehya explains why she resents the wall: she says it serves to keep out desert-dwellers as well as sandstorms, that the sages treat desert people as tools to be used and discarded, and that she would tear the wall down with her own hands if she could.

## entry: The Wall of Samiel
@keys: Wall of Samiel
### STANDARD
The Wall of Samiel separates the Dharma Forest from the Great Red Sand and shields the rainforest from sandstorms. It is said to have been created by Greater Lord Rukkhadevata. The Akademiya maintains it, researchers such as Behnam work on it, and destroying it is forbidden. Caravan Ribat is at its foot.
