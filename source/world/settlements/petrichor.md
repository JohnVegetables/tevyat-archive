## summary
### LITE
Petrichor is a picturesque town on an island in the south of Fontaine, off the continental plate that carries the rest of the nation. It is known for its beauty and its exceedingly pure waters, and is a popular spot for viewing the great waterfall that encircles Fontaine. A council of elders, the Consilium Sapientium, governs it by tradition, with a "Doge" as its head. Its mountains hold ruins and stone golems from the ancient kingdom of Remuria.
### STANDARD +
The town is polite to outsiders but wary of change. It is home to the Daydream Club of inventors and to the hometown of the researcher and filmmaker Xavier.

## geography
### STANDARD
An island town in the Nostoi Region, off the main Fontaine landmass. Ruins and stone statues from the Golden Troupe of Remuria dot the mountains, and a deep spring leads underwater to the Faded Castle. A bell tower rises over the town, and the southern shore has a crashed boat and a distant island.
### LARGE +
The Faded Castle is a sunken Remurian ruin where Harmosts and scholars once kept archives and researched music theory and fate; it contains the entrance to the Sea of Bygone Eras.

## places
### STANDARD
The town centre has a Statue of the Seven and a bell tower. Mécantre and Babisse, brothers of the Daydream Club, work on a giant cannon here (the Teyvat Grand Cannon). Inscriptions, ruins in the mountains, and the southern beach are the main sights for adventurers.
### LARGE +
The Ancient Inscription on the bell tower records Juliano's story and the riddle to his four hidden caches ("heart of the mountains", "eye of the springs", "corner's apex", "the distant island of pouring pearls").

## people
### STANDARD
Tiepolo is the current Doge. Others include Foscari (his assistant), Goldoni, Grundeland (a "Good Merchant"), Contarini, Este, Giovanni, Giustino, Jules, Loredan, Martha, Moce, Paula, Venier, Chandraputra, the former Fontaine Research Institute researcher Bossuet, and the Daydream Club's Mécantre and Babisse.
### LARGE +
The Daydream Club, a group of inventors from Fontaine, gathers here; its members include Xavier, Garcia, Felix Yogue, Mécantre, Babisse, Este and Grispan under their president Grobel. Xavier, who made films in Inazuma, comes from Petrichor.

## governance
### STANDARD
The Consilium Sapientium is a committee of elders that, by tradition, assesses all affairs of the town; the most trustworthy elder is made "Doge". Tiepolo holds that title now. It was founded by Ruggiero, the first Doge.
### LARGE +
Foscari, the Doge's assistant, has been arguing that the town should change. Tiepolo describes the town as romantic and idyllic, with people who are polite to guests but suspicious because they seldom leave the island.

## culture
### STANDARD
Tradition guides daily life and customs are simple. Residents recall that the town believed its past decline came from getting too close to the outside world, though the young now think otherwise. Realm furnishing sets refer to it as the "Musicmaking Town".
### LARGE +
The Doge says Petrichor's history is longer than the Court of Fontaine's. Tiepolo says the town was once governed by the de Petrichor family, whose first duke was appointed by Egeria.

## history
### STANDARD
The de Petrichor family ruled the town for centuries, from the first duke appointed by Egeria until at least 500 years ago, and were just and benevolent leaders. Their last patrono, Renault, was transferred to be mayor of Poisson and died there in the Siege of Poisson, which ended the line in Petrichor. The town then nearly became an abandoned fishing village until Ruggiero founded the Consilium Sapientium.
### LARGE +
Ruggiero came seeking proof of the Grand Symphonies, collecting special conches, and sensed a mysterious energy lingering in what was then a run-down fishing village. He met the Golden Hunter, helped him prevent Boethius from awakening the seals to Remuria, and eventually decided that humanity should not control knowledge beyond its abilities. He stayed on to rebuild Petrichor, attracting refugees and spreading other versions of the legend so that the town's secrets were protected. The adventurer Juliano gave him money.
### ULTRA +
The wiki notes that the Institute of Natural Philosophy's Carter Scherbius, Mary-Ann Guillotin, Rene, Alain Guillotin and Jakob once went on a picnic here, according to an Ancient Log. Rene de Petrichor, son of the last patrono, is the same Rene who later founded the Narzissenkreuz Ordo with Jakob Ingold.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
Calm and old-fashioned: a bell tower, tradition-minded locals who are polite and friendly to guests, and an undercurrent of wariness toward outsiders, with the roar of Fontaine's encircling waterfall nearby.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Praetorian Golems and Tainted Hydro Phantasms guard or haunt the ruins, and Fontemer Aberrants roam the shore. Treasure Hoarders arrived at the time of The Shadow Over Petrichor.

## travel
### STANDARD
Petrichor is an island off the continental plate, so travel there is over water (the treasure hunters of the opening quest arrive by ship). A deep spring leads to the Faded Castle. The town gets tourists who come to see the waterfall.

## quests
### LARGE
The world quest chain Canticles of Harmony (4.6) begins here with The Shadow Over Petrichor. Other activities include the hidden exploration objectives Juliano's Legacy and Foscari's Dilemma, and the quest Daydreams Beyond Space and Time.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 Petrichor is a calm island town ruled by the Consilium Sapientium, with its Remurian ruins quiet. No later canon changes its state.
### STANDARD +
Tiepolo is still Doge, Foscari still urges reform, and the Daydream Club keeps working on its inventions.

## entry: Consilium Sapientium and the Doge

@keys2: Petrichor, Fontaine
@version: 4.6
### STANDARD
The Consilium Sapientium is Petrichor's council of elders, set up by Ruggiero, the town's first Doge, after the de Petrichor line died out. Tiepolo, the current Doge, says there is little to oversee because the townspeople live simple lives by tradition. He welcomes strangers kindly and asks them not to blame wary locals.

## entry: Ruggiero and the Faded Castle
@keys: Ruggiero, Faded Castle
@keys2: Petrichor, Remuria
@version: 4.6
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Ruggiero was a scholar who came to Petrichor in search of the Grand Symphonies, and became the town's first Doge. He helped seal the Faded Castle after failing to find the relics he sought there, and later gave up his quest, feeling humans should not control knowledge beyond them. His notes survive in the Faded Castle.
### LARGE +
In his notes, Ruggiero came to suspect that the Golden Hunter (Cassiodor) was a surviving Remurian. Bravais, a scholar, believed Ruggiero's accomplishments never got a fair assessment because of Fontaine's weak academic environment.

## entry: The Shadow Over Petrichor
@keys: Shadow Over Petrichor, Canticles of Harmony, Osse
@keys2: Petrichor, Faded Castle
@version: 4.6
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
The Traveler arrives to find the town strangely quiet and a gang of Treasure Hoarders hauling statues from the mountain ruins. After a revived golem attacks, a talking cat named Osse leads the Traveler through a deep spring to the Faded Castle, where they find a music box.
### LARGE +
During the Canticles of Harmony quests, townsfolk such as Foscari, Goldoni and Grundeland are temporarily possessed by Remurian spirits (according to the wiki's NPC pages), which explains why their dialogue changes afterwards.
