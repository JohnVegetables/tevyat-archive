## summary
### LITE
Okurov is an abandoned little town in Flamefeather Valley, Snezhnaya, with a warm climate and a dark history. Old stories say that nobody who goes in comes out. The whole area is played in third-person shooter mode.
### STANDARD +
Locals in Morepesok remember Okurov as a place with warm weather and a famous doctor, where moving in once seemed the natural thing to do, until people noticed that no one who went there ever came back. A folk tale of a beautiful firebird that lures people deep into the valley grew up around it.

## geography
### STANDARD
Okurov lies in Flamefeather Valley, a warm pocket among the snow, and is reached through the old railway tunnel and broken carriages by the valley's train line. Unnatural warmth is noticeable at the end of the tunnel. The town has a station, an assembly hall (the 'home' of the town's religion-like community) and thick trees and blue algae; a huge winged creature guards it.
### LARGE +
Nearby are tracks, stashes left by Volkodlak gangs, and pieces of Shadow Realm machinery. The assembly hall (Hall of Prayers) is entered by a small gap in its iron door and holds a mechanism that can lift visitors to the 'home' beneath it.

## people
### STANDARD
The resident the Traveler escorts home is a child, Nastya, who has tree branches growing from her head. The location page also lists an NPC named "Shar". The rest of her 'family' are tall, mostly mindless Leshy-like beings who respond to her, including one she calls Aunt LePuy. A mysterious guardian called Zhara is a giant winged creature, the 'firebird' of the stories. The town's doctor, 'Doctor Moreau', is the alchemist Koshchei.
### LARGE +
Others met include Furiosa (Nastya's idol, who warns visitors away) and Ove, a hostile neighbour who hides the elevator control wheel. A newspaper article in the Snezhnaya Vestnik, found in a train carriage, once praised Okurov's warm weather, its exceptional doctor and its open-door policy for all travellers.

## history
### STANDARD
Okurov was once an inviting town. A doctor there attracted residents with the promise of cures; the town's people adopted a belief that becoming trees, all one family, was the cure for pain and the path to an eternal courtyard. Over time no one who went in came out.
### LARGE +
The documents in the assembly hall reveal that the experiments were conducted by Koshchei the Deathless posing as Doctor Moreau, who sought a form of eternity by changing people into a 'Perfect Tree' that gives strength but wears away speech and emotion. The final ceremony aimed to turn everyone into embryos of a new, emotionless existence.
### ULTRA +
Nastya, taken from the town and stowed in a crate by Volkodlak thieves, wakes in the tunnel and asks the Traveler to take her home. As summarised on the wiki, she chooses at the end to complete the ritual and merge into the Perfect Tree to be with her mother, and the Traveler and Paimon respect that choice. Afterwards the Eye of Graeae recovers a crystal fragment of the ritual's power, and Aksinya, who calls herself the 'Royal Jester', appears and says she has neutralised the threat of Zhara reviving.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Rugged Elder Leshy and Juvenile Tree Segments, Chimeric Armored Beasts and Chimeric Winged Elephants; Shadow Realm rifts; and Zhara, the winged guardian, who attacks intruders. Visitors must also pass old railway infrastructure and drainage tunnels.

## travel
### STANDARD
Reached only by following the 'In the Dwelling of Life' series from Flamefeather Valley after unlocking the town in 'Transplanted From Yesterday to Tomorrow'. Local specialty: Golden Fern. Features: Prime Ice, Shadow Wingdrop, Shadow Realm.

## atmosphere
### STANDARD
A warm, damp, too-quiet place: smoke on the horizon, blue algae glowing in the dark, trees that are 'strong and healthy' because of Zhara, and children's games ('Catch the Bug', with a 'Badbug' to be caught) that hint at something wrong. The name references the 1908 Gorky novel 'The City of Okurov'.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 Okurov stands abandoned. By the wiki's summary of the series, the experiments are exposed, the Eye of Graeae holds a crystal fragment of the ritual's power and Nastya has gone into the Perfect Tree.
