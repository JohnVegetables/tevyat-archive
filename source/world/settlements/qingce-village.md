## summary
### LITE
Qingce Village is a tranquil village at the foot of Mt. Qingce in Bishui Plain, at Liyue's northern edge, with clear water and terraced fields of flowers. Many younger residents have left for livelier places such as Liyue Harbor, so it has become something of a retirement spot. It is led, in all but title, by Granny Ruoxin.
### STANDARD +
According to legend, the serpent god Chi once nested here before Morax slew it and sealed its power. "Qingce" is said to be an ancient name of the Chi itself.

## geography
### STANDARD
Houses are simply built, mostly of wood with blue roofs, on terraces cut into the mountain. The mountain is described as cradling the village and its croplands. Crescent-shaped water paddies sit between hills and bamboo forests, with cloud banks in the valleys, and the wind makes the paddy water ripple in many colours.
### LARGE +
Local specialties include Cor Lapis, Glaze Lily, Jueyun Chili, Qingxin and Violetgrass. The area is home to slimes, Treasure Hoarders and, near the ruins, Ruin Machines.

## places
### STANDARD
The community centre is where Granny Ruoxin is found. Ms. Bai owns the mill, Pops Kai a tea shop and Master Lu the Fanmu Carpenter shop, and there is a market. The Qingce Village Bulletin Board carries local posts. The Vault of Qingce lies hidden to the north-west.
### LARGE +
Geo statues stand scattered around the village, especially to the west, which Granny Ruoxin names as the spot where the Geo Lord crushed the Chi. Some glow. They are said to bear Morax's likeness and, together with orange adeptus amber, help suppress the Chi's power.

## people
### STANDARD
Granny Ruoxin is the community leader and not an official village chief; the name stuck. Others include Little Liu, Little Xing, Little You, Little Zuo and Little Yi, Pops Kai, Master Lu, Ms. Bai, the herb gatherer Zhi, Hanfeng, Jiangcheng, Dongdong, Chang the Ninth and Uncle Ghast.
### LARGE +
Granny Ruoxin grew up in Liyue Harbor and acted at the Heyu Tea House. She moved toward a life at Mt. Qingce with an adventurer who left on an expedition and never returned. She no longer hopes so much for his return as for his happiness. She asked the villagers, on the bulletin board, to help the ailing Jiangcheng with his fieldwork.

## governance
### LARGE
There is no formal village head. Ruoxin says she has done a village chief's work for so long that people call her that, and the old tend the fields and look after grandchildren so younger family members can work elsewhere.

## culture
### STANDARD
Village life is gentle and slow, with food and water in plenty. The elderly raise their grandchildren while the young work in Liyue Harbor and beyond.
### LARGE +
Ruoxin plays Genius Invokation for relaxation, not to win. At the Lantern Rite event Fleeting Colors in Flight (limited-time), she invited Yun Jin to perform opera in the village.

## history
### STANDARD
Thousands of years ago the Chi made its nest on Mt. Qingce and seized the region. Morax fought it at a great height and killed it, but its power seeped into the land, so he subdued each aspect of it in turn: spirit, bones, flesh, soul and form, bound in different directions. He taught the villagers to erect Geo statues to crush what remained. The magic formula of the Hidden Palace of Zhou Formula is said to have bound it.
### LARGE +
An inscription in the village says the Chi did not entirely perish and that three statues were used against it. In the quest The Chi of Yore (1.0), a self-styled researcher named Yan'er pursues the Chi's rumoured corpse; the Traveler gathers three fragments (pictographic carvings from before writing), enters the Vault of Qingce and finds treasure but no trace of the Chi.
### ULTRA +
Local belief says the Blood-Fed Trees grew from the Chi's blood and mark its eyes, while the terraces are its scales. Zhongli denies the mountain's origin in the Chi's body: Mt. Qingce stood long before the Chi ravaged it. During the Unreconciled Stars event (limited), Ruoxin says some villagers had fallen asleep and could not wake after the meteorites came down.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Gentle, sleepy and nostalgic: cloud-filled valleys, terraced paddies, elders chatting, and young people absent. Inside the village life is slow but warm.

## dangers
### LARGE
Slimes, Treasure Hoarders and the Ruin Machines of the ruins near the village; the Ruin Hunter guarding them blocked even Yan'er.

## quests
### LARGE
The Chi of Yore (World Quest, Bishui Plain) is the main story; Granny Ruoxin also features in the Lantern Rite events Fleeting Colors in Flight and Unreconciled Stars (both limited).

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 Qingce Village is quiet, mostly elderly, and unchanged by recent main-story events.
### STANDARD +
Granny Ruoxin still lives in the village. The Chi remains a legend that few still remember.

## entry: Granny Ruoxin
@keys: Granny Ruoxin, Ruoxin
### STANDARD
Ruoxin leads Qingce Village's community centre. She grew up in Liyue Harbor, was an actress at the Heyu Tea House alongside Fan Er'ye, and came to the countryside seeking a simpler life. She wonders whether her lost lover would like the village and has stopped waiting, although she says she does not regret their love. She is kind, wry, and enjoys gossip and Genius Invokation. She is a gentle storyteller who remembers things most people have forgotten, like the old tale of the Geo Lord suppressing the Chi.

## entry: The Chi of Qingce
@keys: Chi, Qingce legend, Blood-Fed Trees, Vault of Qingce
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
The Chi was a serpent god or dragon-like monster of Mt. Qingce, defeated by Morax. Its power seeped into the land afterward. Morax subdued it facet by facet, spirit in the north, bones south-east, flesh north-west, soul north-east, form south-west, and taught villagers to raise Geo statues. The Vault of Qingce, with a glowing orb and treasure rooms, contains no sign of the beast.
