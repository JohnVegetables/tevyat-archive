## summary
### LITE
Morepesok ("Sea-Sand") is a fishing settlement on the northern coast of Snezhnaya, in the Everfrozen Earth, and the hometown of Tartaglia (Childe), the Eleventh Fatui Harbinger. Boats go out from its harbor and a railway station links it to the rest of the country. Tartaglia's family, including his sister Tonia and youngest brother Teucer, lives here.
### STANDARD +
Fishing is the way of life, and the fisherfolk are proud of it: they compare catches after each outing, and the biggest catch earns the most respect. The official description jokes that the surest shortcut to impressing them is to be a Fatui Harbinger, which Tartaglia is.

## geography
### STANDARD
Morepesok sits on the frozen northern shore of the Everfrozen Earth, the area that holds Snezhnograd. Its two public hubs are a harbor, where fishing boats and a larger ship, the "Sea Tsar", dock, and a train station that connects it to inland Snezhnaya. The Expedition text singles out the birch trees that grow in this part of the north.
### LARGE +
Ice fishing is practised here as well as sea fishing (Tartaglia plans an ice-fishing trip with Teucer in one of his anecdotes). A tunnel and old railway line leading from Morepesok's station toward the abandoned town of Okurov in Flamefeather Valley are the subject of a local rumour (see the Okurov entry below).

## places
### STANDARD
Harbor: fishing boats and the ship "The Sea Tsar", whose captain is Vyazemskaya and first mate Yazykov. Train station: a platform area with a waypoint, where the engineer Jary and his fiancee Vinezka can be met. Tartaglia's Home: the family house, where Tonia and Teucer are found. A fishing-supplies vendor, Zherebtsov, works as the local angler.

## people
### STANDARD
Named residents include Dozorov, Gorchakov, Gorshkov, Lugovskoy, Rakitina, Razumovsky and Shatalov, plus Tartaglia's siblings Tonia and Teucer. At the harbor: Korsakov, Pweza, Smirnova, Vyazemskaya and Yazykov. At the station: Jary, Vinezka and Znamensky. The sources read give little more than names for most of them.
### LARGE +
The Tartaglia family is the best-known household. In Tartaglia's character stories his father worried about the third son, who as a boy kept stirring up fights around the village. Tartaglia now brings home gifts and medicine for his family and has said that missing a family dinner for a mission weighs on him.

## economy
### LARGE
Fishing supplies the village; the ship and station also move goods and people. Burtsev, a resident of Sretomorozsk, says he has family in Morepesok who can buy about twice his town's allotment of Kresnik Kristall, hinting that the coast is better supplied than the inland borderlands. Tartaglia, however, says life in Morepesok is not going well either under the energy policy of Project Stuzha.

## culture
### LARGE
Seafaring pride is central: a good catch is respected, and official text calls the local fisherfolk warriors who face the raging sea in simple fishing craft and do not flinch before sea monsters the size of mountains. Treat this as local self-image and tall-tale tone, not a documented bestiary.

## atmosphere
### LARGE
Cold wind off the grey northern sea, moored boats creaking, birches on the shore and an unhurried railway halt; people who talk about catches the way others talk about weather. This is an atmospheric suggestion, not a canon description.

## history
### STANDARD
The sources read give no founding history for Morepesok. What canon records is Tartaglia's youth here: as a boy he ran away at fourteen into the snowy forest, fell into an Abyss-touched chasm and was trained there by a mysterious swordswoman before being found by his mother and sisters. When he came home he brought fights and squabbles to the village.
### ULTRA +
After a large brawl, Tartaglia's father felt he had no choice but to hand his third son over for conscription into the Fatui, hoping the discipline would settle his temper. Instead the boy beat fully armed troops, which drew the attention of Pulcinella, who inducted him into the Fatui; he later became the Eleventh Harbinger. His letters home, quoted in his character stories, show a standing habit of sending parents and siblings gifts and medicine for his father, and promising to return to Morepesok by the first ship. A note of disputed detail: the accounts that put these events in his 'youth' are character stories, so they reflect the family's and the Fatui's view, not an objective record.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 Morepesok is a living fishing town. Tartaglia visits when his duties allow and, in the Travelers' Tales anecdotes added in 7.1, is shown buying gifts, planning to take Teucer ice fishing and promising Tonia to stay home longer next time.
### STANDARD +
He told the Traveler he is from Morepesok while discussing Project Stuzha in Snezhnograd (Archon Quest Chapter VII, Act I). No attack on, or disaster at, Morepesok is shown up to 7.1.

### STANDARD
Tartaglia's Home is the Tartaglia family house in Morepesok. Tonia and Teucer, his sister and youngest brother, are found here. Their personal details belong to their own entries.
### LARGE +
In the 7.1 anecdote "Tartaglia: A Promise", he hands Tonia a bag with their father's medicine and snacks for Teucer and says he will follow once his work is done and stay longer this time; Tonia answers that their mother has been asking after him and that he must not let the family down again.

## entry: Tartaglia's Morepesok anecdotes
@keys: Tartaglia: Back Home, Tartaglia: Ice Fishing, Tartaglia: A Promise, Snowstride Chapter
@version: 7.1
### LARGE
Three hidden Travelers' Tales anecdotes (Snowstride Chapter, 7.1) are set in Morepesok. In "Back Home" Tartaglia, at the station, sorts out tickets for Tonia, toys for Teucer, a scarf for his mother and boots for his father as apology gifts for missing the last family dinner because of a sudden mission. In "Ice Fishing" he is at the harbor, free for once, gathering gear for an ice-fishing trip and deciding to take Teucer along, which means he needs extra stove fuel. "A Promise" takes place at the family house (see Tartaglia's Home).

## entry: The Okurov rumour at Morepesok Station
@keys: Vinezka, Jary, Okurov rumour, Okurov tunnel
@keys2: Morepesok
@version: 7.0
@spoiler: minor
### LARGE
At the station the engineer Jary has been hired by outsiders to blast open a tunnel toward the abandoned town of Okurov. His fiancee Vinezka begs him not to go, citing the local belief that nobody who enters Okurov ever comes back and a tale of a beautiful firebird that lures people deep into the valley. Jary says he is bound by a contract with a heavy penalty. The Traveler can take over the job, which starts the World Quest series "In the Dwelling of Life".
