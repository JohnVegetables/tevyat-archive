## summary
### LITE
Fatui Internal Affairs, also known as the Okhranka (Russian for "security"), is a special group within the Fatui that investigates misconduct among Fatui ranks. It answers to the Tsaritsa. Little else about it is known: canon names no members, leader or base.
### STANDARD +
It is not a combat division or a public face of the Fatui. It appears in canon only as the body that gets handed a case when the Tsaritsa wants a Fatui member's loyalty or conduct judged.

## structure
### STANDARD
Canon gives no structure for the division: no ranks, no named staff, no headquarters. All that is established is that it is a special group of Fatui and that it reports to the Tsaritsa, not to a Harbinger. It should be treated as an unseen office. Scenes may refer to its existence, but should not show it with invented officers or an invented building.
### LARGE +
Because it reports to the Tsaritsa, its findings carry her authority, as the Dottore case shows: the Harbingers acted on the outcome. How it gathers evidence, who sits in judgement and whether it has its own detention is never described.

## leadership
### STANDARD
The only authority above the division that canon names is the Tsaritsa (Anastasya). The Harbinger Tartaglia, who describes the division to Arlecchino, is a messenger of the Tsaritsa's orders and is not shown to belong to it. No head of Internal Affairs is named through 7.1.

## goals
### STANDARD
Its job, as canon states it, is to look into misconduct inside the Fatui and to deliver a formal verdict when the Tsaritsa asks, for example on whether a member has turned traitor and whether he is a threat to Snezhnaya.

## methods
### LARGE
Nothing is shown of its investigative methods. What canon does show is its role in the process: the Tsaritsa authorises it to review a case, it reaches a formal verdict, and the Tsaritsa then acts on that verdict. In the Seals of Life matter it is the destination for a suspect, who is to be handed over to it, which suggests it also takes custody of those it examines. That last point is an inference from the wording, not something shown on screen.

## history
### STANDARD
Its earliest appearance in canon is in Version 6.3 (Song of the Welkin Moon, Act VII), during the crisis in Nod-Krai. Dottore, the Harbinger called the Doctor, was recalled to Snezhnaya on the grounds that he was redundant, but did not leave. He stayed to attack Columbina and seize the power of the Three Moons. When the Tsaritsa learned of this, she authorised Fatui Internal Affairs to review his case and reach a formal verdict on whether he had turned traitor and whether he threatened Snezhnaya. Tartaglia relayed this to Arlecchino while he was sent to receive the refugees from Nod-Krai.
### LARGE +
The wiki's account of the Tsaritsa summarises the result: the division found that Dottore had acted against her will. On that basis the Tsaritsa issued a decree, which Tartaglia read to Arlecchino, giving the Harbingers unilateral executive powers while the exceptional circumstances lasted and permitting Arlecchino and Sandrone to eliminate Dottore should he pose a threat. She also decided not to attack the evacuated Nod-Krai because unretrieved research material remained there. The Palestar Edict was lifted afterwards.

Version 7.1 (Chapter VII Act III) gives its second mention. After the Traveler learned from Avaz, a Northland Bank employee, that Pantalone might be behind the missing Seals of Life, the Traveler reported it to the Tsaritsa. She said the culprit should be handed to Fatui Internal Affairs, and that Pantalone himself was too proud to be involved.
### ULTRA +
Both known cases involve a high-stakes matter that the Tsaritsa wanted settled on her own terms: a Harbinger's possible betrayal and the theft of the Seals of Life. In the second, the Traveler's own suspicion of Pantalone was set aside by the Tsaritsa without the division being mentioned. Canon does not show whether the division investigated Pantalone or only the man handed to it.

## relations
### STANDARD
Its relationship to the Fatui is that of an internal watchdog. It answers to the Tsaritsa, and its verdict on Dottore bound the Harbingers Arlecchino and Sandrone. Its dealings with Pantalone, the Northland Bank and the other Harbingers are not described.
### LARGE +
The Harbingers lead their own divisions and are not shown to command Internal Affairs. In the Dottore case they waited for its verdict before acting. Characters who are shown to know of it include Tartaglia and Arlecchino, who discuss it in Nod-Krai, and the Tsaritsa herself.

## reputation
### STANDARD
Canon gives no public reputation. It is spoken of matter-of-factly by senior Fatui, as a known part of the organisation.

## secrets
### LARGE
Nothing about its personnel or operations is established. The roleplay-safe approach is to leave its members unnamed and its methods vague, and to let it appear through the effects of its verdicts.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 it is still the Tsaritsa's tool for judging Fatui misconduct. It has not appeared directly in any scene.
### STANDARD +
Its most recent mention is in Version 7.1, when the Tsaritsa said the suspect in the Seals of Life theft should be handed to it. Its future is not shown.
