## summary
### LITE
The Eleven Fatui Harbingers are the executive heads of Snezhnaya's Fatui. The Tsaritsa gives them god-like authority and Delusions, and each leads a division. The Director, Pierro ("The Jester"), leads them. In the early Archon Quest chapters they were sent abroad to collect the Archons' Gnoses.
### STANDARD +
Seat numbers reflect combat power, and empty seats are not filled by moving everyone up.

## structure
### STANDARD
- **The Tsaritsa** appoints every Harbinger. She does it in person at a ceremony in Zapolyarny Palace, or through the Director.
- **The Director** (Pierro) assigns missions and calls meetings. He also chairs votes on decisions that carry national-level risk. Such a decision needs the in-person, unanimous approval of every active Harbinger. Incapacitated members are not counted.
- **Divisions**: each Harbinger commands at least one company or division, and abroad also takes charge of the ordinary Fatui in that nation.
### LARGE +
Harbingers are said to be chosen from the eleven most powerful members of the Fatui, and the most recent holders of the top three seats had power that could rival gods. A codename can pass from one holder to the next; there have been two Knaves. Tartaglia once had to give new recruits in Liyue their welcome speech because he was the only Harbinger there.

Harbingers have wide autonomy and may act on their own judgement even against central policy. Pierro said that during the Gnosis hunt the Harbingers did not fully represent Snezhnaya's official stance. That freedom has limits. The Tsaritsa forbids Harbingers from fighting one another, though she can lift the ban, and Fatui Internal Affairs (the Okhranka) can review a Harbinger's conduct. The wiki says that, on Capitano's orders, a Harbinger whose soldier dies must look after that soldier's family. Wanderer's character stories mention a "wild, frenzied banquet" of the Harbingers, which seems to play a part in deciding rank. Nobody has explained what it actually is.

## leadership
### LITE
Pierro (Eftirvit), a former Khaenri'ahn royal mage, is the Director and was the first Harbinger.
### LARGE +
The Tsaritsa founded the Fatui under Pierro's leadership, and he fights with an Electro Delusion.

Pierro personally recruited Signora, Dottore and Scaramouche. When Tartaglia was made a Harbinger, Pierro pinned the Delusion on him. More than a decade ago he announced the Tsaritsa's pardon of Peruere, who had killed the previous Knave, and her appointment as the new Knave, Arlecchino. Shortly before the Traveler's journey began, he gathered the Harbingers to explain the Tsaritsa's plan to collect all the Gnoses. He gave Pantalone the financial arrangements and Sandrone the technical needs.
### ULTRA +
Pierro's authority over the others is real but not absolute. Columbina warned the Traveler about the Jester, the Regrator and the Rooster together, and she said she was not sure how power is shared among those three. At Signora's funeral, Pierro put a stop to the others' "foolish theatrics" and called the dead Harbinger by her real name, Rosalyne. Many years ago he had a notebook from his old rival Hroptatyr sealed. He decreed that it could only be opened if every active Harbinger agreed, and in 7.1 he called them together to vote it open.

## members
### LITE
Roster as of 7.1: 1 Capitano (incapacitated, seat kept), 2 Dottore (dead), 3 Columbina (resigned), 4 Arlecchino, 5 Pulcinella, 6 Scaramouche (resigned, erased), 7 Sandrone, 8 Signora (dead), 9 Pantalone, 10 unknown, 11 Tartaglia (Childe). Seats without a note are active, as is the Director, Pierro.
### STANDARD +
Titles: Capitano is "The Captain", Dottore "The Doctor", Columbina "The Damselette", Arlecchino "The Knave", Pulcinella "The Rooster", Scaramouche "The Balladeer", Sandrone "Marionette", Signora "The Fair Lady", Pantalone "Regrator" and Tartaglia "Childe".

- Arlecchino: Peruere

Their portfolios differ widely:

- **Pulcinella** is Mayor of Snezhnograd and Glupov, Speaker of the Veche and its "linchpin". Sandrone says he represents politics itself.
- **Pantalone** runs the Northland Bank, speaks for finance in the Veche and is the richest Harbinger.
- **Sandrone** handles the Harbingers' technical needs and leads the Adventurers' Guild. She speaks in the Veche for her engineers and researchers.
- **Arlecchino** is "Father" of the House of the Hearth.
- **Tartaglia** inherited most of Capitano's troops and his Veche seat as the voice of the Fatui soldiers.

## goals
### STANDARD
The Harbingers serve the Tsaritsa's plan, and Pierro explains that plan's purpose as confronting the Heavenly Principles. In practice this meant gathering the Archons' Gnoses, and later, in Snezhnaya, Project Mistilteinn, along with the energy-rationing Project Stuzha that feeds it. Individual Harbingers also pursue goals of their own. Some align with the Tsaritsa's will and some do not, as Dottore's fate shows.

## methods
### LARGE
Each Harbinger works in their own way. Signora used coercion and diplomacy. Tartaglia started a crisis in Liyue as a decoy. Dottore ran human experiments. Pantalone works through loans, and Pulcinella through politics and carefully worded letters. Arlecchino uses intelligence work and negotiation. Their status gives them weight: Tartaglia could not simply be handed over to the Liyue Qixing after Osial, so the Northland Bank paid heavy reparations instead. Many rank-and-file Fatui respect their Harbingers, and some act without orders to advance them or avenge them. After Signora's death, some of her subordinates stayed behind in Inazuma, apparently to settle the score.
### ULTRA +
Harbinger power also has a social side. Many are wealthy and politically powerful. Ekaterina says Tartaglia's Northland Bank savings "boggle the mind", and Pantalone paid in advance to rent Mondstadt's whole Goth Grand Hotel indefinitely. Tartaglia's interest in a fishing gadget was enough to make it a sought-after item in Snezhnaya. Inside Snezhnaya, Harbingers compete through the Snezhnograd Veche, where Sandrone says "all the factional strife" plays out in "one little room".

## history
### LARGE
**Origins.** After the cataclysm 500 years ago, the Khaenri'ahn Eftirvit joined the Tsaritsa's cause. In the third year of her reign she founded the Fatui under his leadership, and he became Pierro, the first Harbinger. Over the following centuries he brought in Signora (the Crimson Witch of Flames, whose fire he suppressed with a Cryo Delusion), Dottore (an expelled Sumeru scholar he found 400 to 450 years ago) and Scaramouche (the Raiden Shogun's discarded puppet, after the Tatarasuna affair). Arlecchino rose to the Fourth seat over a decade ago by killing Crucabena, and Tartaglia was conscripted as a youth and became the Eleventh.

**The Gnosis hunt (1.0-4.x).**
- Signora took the Anemo Gnosis from Venti.
- Signora also obtained the Geo Gnosis through Zhongli's contract with the Tsaritsa, while Tartaglia's summoning of Osial served as a decoy.
- Scaramouche secured the Electro Gnosis in Inazuma. Signora lost a duel before the throne to the Traveler, and the Raiden Shogun executed her (2.1).
- Dottore made Scaramouche into an artificial god in Sumeru and took the Electro and Dendro Gnoses back to Snezhnaya (3.x).
- Scaramouche erased himself from Irminsul, which left the Sixth seat remembered as empty for centuries (3.3).
- Neuvillette gave Arlecchino the Hydro Gnosis as a diplomatic gift (4.2).
- In Natlan (5.x), Capitano fought Mavuika and refused to take the Pyro Gnosis from her while she was weakened. He later took Mavuika's place as the death Ronova demanded and merged with the Lord of the Night (5.3). The Tsaritsa kept his rank as it was.
### ULTRA +
**Nod-Krai (6.0-6.6).** After Columbina abandoned the Fatui, the Tsaritsa issued the Palestar Edict, which ordered her brought back at any cost. Pierro, Dottore, Sandrone and Arlecchino were all involved in Nod-Krai. Arlecchino argued that three Harbingers there were too many and had Dottore recalled, but he only pretended to obey. He seized the Three Moons' authority and killed Sandrone. After his apparent betrayal, Tartaglia relayed the Tsaritsa's orders: Fatui Internal Affairs would review Dottore's case, the Harbingers could use unilateral powers, and Arlecchino and Sandrone were allowed to eliminate him if he posed a threat. Columbina destroyed him (6.3), and the Edict was revoked. Pantalone and Pulcinella were summoned to answer to the Tsaritsa over their ties to Dottore. Pantalone then secretly led Fatui into Sumeru, disguised as one of Dottore's subordinates, so Dottore could take over Irminsul. Nahida burned the tree with the Pyro Gnosis, and Dottore was ended for good (6.6). Pulcinella wrote to Nahida to disown the attack. Sandrone's salvaged core was revived in Fontaine (6.7, the Sunny Summer Fontinalia story), and she has returned to her duties.

**Snezhnaya (7.0-7.1).** The Traveler found the remaining Harbingers running the state. Tartaglia now commands Capitano's former troops and sits in the Veche. Pulcinella chaired the Veche votes that kept Project Stuzha going, though by 7.1 the project is on hold. Pantalone manages the Seals of Life against Ronova's Curse of Death. Arlecchino agreed to serve as the base for Pierro's improved Delusion, on the condition that the House of the Hearth stop working for the Fatui and stop training children as spies. In Etched in Time (7.1) the active Harbingers voted unanimously to unseal Hroptatyr's notebook. Pierro then used the resulting Delusion against Ronova, which left him badly depleted.

## relations
### LARGE
- **The Tsaritsa**: the Harbingers' source of authority and their only superior. She appoints them, forbids them to fight one another and decides their fate. She kept Capitano's rank, and she later revoked the order against Columbina.
- **Among themselves**: they trade barbs openly. Arlecchino and Sandrone despised Dottore. Columbina says Pantalone and Pulcinella despise each other even though they get along in public. Sandrone says Capitano left his troops to Tartaglia because he expected Pulcinella and Pantalone to "swoop in on his army like vultures" and divide it between them. Capitano privately respected Tartaglia and sometimes brought him to Sandrone's tea parties. Sandrone, Arlecchino, Columbina and Signora were tea-party companions.
- **The Archons**: mostly adversarial during the Gnosis hunt. The exception was contracts and gifts, such as Zhongli's and Neuvillette's.
### ULTRA +
- **Fatui Internal Affairs**: reports to the Tsaritsa and can investigate even a Harbinger.
- **The Snezhnograd Veche**: Tartaglia, Sandrone, Pantalone and Pulcinella all sit in it as representatives of different interest groups. Tartaglia and Sandrone raise concerns about Project Stuzha but do not vote against it.
- **The Traveler**: has fought several Harbingers, but by 7.1 works alongside Pierro, Arlecchino, Sandrone and Tartaglia against Ronova. Tartaglia jokingly offered them the title "Twelfth of the Fatui Harbingers". The Traveler declined, since it would rank them below him.

## reputation
### ULTRA
Abroad, the Harbingers are feared and distrusted, and most of the nations they visited suffered from their schemes. At home they are powerful public figures. Many rank-and-file Fatui admire them; when Dottore returned with two Gnoses, one soldier said that with someone that strong in the ranks it hardly mattered that other seats sat empty. When Signora died, Pulcinella ordered work halted for half a day of mourning, and Pantalone and Arlecchino criticised him for how short it was. In 7.1 Tartaglia remarked how few of them were left, thinking back to Signora's funeral.

## secrets
### ULTRA
Some things are known to few. Pierro and Capitano were pure-blooded Khaenri'ahns whom Ronova cursed with immortality. Hroptatyr's notebook, which held forbidden knowledge, was sealed for centuries in the innermost Armory Palace archives by the Tsaritsa's leave and the Harbingers' agreement. Scaramouche's existence was erased, so most Fatui know only that the Sixth seat stands empty. Pantalone's part in Dottore's Sumeru gambit was hidden behind a disguise.

## entry: Harbinger seats and codenames
@keys: Harbinger seat, Harbinger rank, Fourth Harbinger, Fifth Harbinger, Seventh Harbinger, Ninth Harbinger, Eleventh Harbinger, First Harbinger, Second Harbinger, Third Harbinger, Sixth Harbinger, Eighth Harbinger
### STANDARD
Each Harbinger is appointed by the Tsaritsa and given a new codename and title, plus a seat number that reflects combat power. Wanderer's stories say the young Scaramouche was boosted until he matched the lower-ranked Harbingers, and that he later earned the sixth seat. Codenames can be inherited: Peruere took "The Knave" from Crucabena. Seats do not move up when a higher one empties, and they can stay empty indefinitely. As of 7.1 the 2nd and 8th belong to the dead, the 3rd and 6th to resigned members, and the 10th has never been identified. Capitano's 1st seat has been kept for him.

## entry: Unanimous vote of the Harbingers
@keys: Hroptatyr's notebook, unanimous vote, Harbingers' vote, national-level risk
@version: 7.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
In the Fatui, any decision carrying a national-level risk must be voted on in person by every active Harbinger and must pass unanimously. The Director chairs the vote. Pierro had Hroptatyr's notebook sealed by this rule centuries ago. In Etched in Time (7.1) he called the Harbingers to the Armory Palace archives. The votes went like this:
- Pulcinella said the old fear of making Snezhnaya a "second Khaenri'ah" no longer applied, since they were already at odds with the Ruler of Death.
- Tartaglia was in favour because the Traveler could deal with Abyssal corruption.
- Arlecchino would not leave "a valuable card" unplayed.
- Pantalone called the risk acceptable.
- Sandrone, repelled by the comparison with Dottore, agreed out of curiosity.

Pierro, who had raised the motion, did not need to vote, and it passed. Capitano was not required because he is incapacitated.

## entry: Prohibition on Harbinger infighting
@keys: drawing steel against one another, Palestar Edict
@keys2: Harbinger, Harbingers, Arlecchino, Dottore, Columbina, Tsaritsa
@version: 6.3
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The Tsaritsa forbids Harbingers from fighting each other. They may insult one another, but they may not draw steel. During the Nod-Krai crisis Arlecchino said that if the ban was not lifted, she would fight Dottore as Peruere rather than as the Knave. The Tsaritsa then had Fatui Internal Affairs review Dottore's case, granted the Harbingers unilateral powers and allowed Arlecchino and Sandrone to eliminate him if needed. Columbina destroyed him. Afterwards the Tsaritsa revoked the Palestar Edict, the order to bring Columbina back.

Before leaving for Natlan, Capitano gave Pierro a detailed letter arranging the future of his subordinates. ## entry: Signora's funeral

The 2022 teaser A Winter Night's Lazzo shows the surviving Harbingers, without Scaramouche, gathered around Signora's coffin after her death in Inazuma. ## entry: The Tenth seat and the previous Knave
@keys: Tenth Harbinger, 10th Harbinger, previous Knave, Crucabena
### LARGE
No Tenth Harbinger has been named in canon through 7.1, and nothing about the holder is known. The wiki notes that every active Harbinger attended the 7.1 vote and no Tenth appeared, so the seat is either empty or held by Pierro. A previous Knave, Crucabena, ran the House of the Hearth. Peruere killed her more than a decade ago, and the Tsaritsa pardoned Peruere and made her the new Knave, Arlecchino. Capitano, Scaramouche and Signora were present when Pierro announced the decision.
