## summary
### LITE
The House of the Hearth is an orphanage run by Arlecchino, "The Knave" and Fourth of the Eleven Fatui Harbingers. The children it raises call her "Father" and think of themselves as children of the Tsaritsa. Those judged to have potential are enlisted into the Fatui, often as spies or sleeper agents. Its best-known base is the Hotel Bouffes d'ete in the Court of Fontaine. Lyney, Lynette and Freminet are its best-known members. As of 7.1, Arlecchino has agreed with Pierro that the House will become independent of the Fatui and stop training children as spies.
### STANDARD +
Members treat each other as family, "tighter than blood". The House is both a refuge and a tool: Arlecchino rebuilt it after killing her predecessor Crucabena, ended the old regime's cruelty, and kept one absolute rule, loyalty.

## structure
### STANDARD
There is no published rank structure. The head is the Knave, who is addressed as "Father". Below her are the resident children and young adults. Orphans receive the surnames Snezhevich (male) and Snezhevna (female), meaning roughly "son of snow" and "daughter of snow"; some also get a new given name. The Knave's heir is called the "King".
### LARGE +
Members raised in Fontaine go by Fontainian names and present themselves as Fontainians. Orphans are gathered from across the continent, which makes it easier for people of a given heritage to blend into their homeland. Clervie's shadow speaks of two rosters under Crucabena, one for the ideal candidate and another for those to be executed. Under Arlecchino, members spar to resolve disputes and the loser backs down; under Crucabena the loser lost their life. In 4.6 Arlecchino accepted such a duel from Lyney, Lynette and Freminet. The House is not of one mind: Filliol and Nanteuil broke its rules under the phantom child's influence.

## leadership
### LITE
Arlecchino leads the House. Her birth name is Peruere; she was raised in it and took over by killing the previous director, Crucabena, whom the children called "Mother".
### STANDARD +
Arlecchino chose the name "Father" as a deliberate break from Crucabena's false maternal act. She has decided that Lyney will one day succeed her as "King"; in his voice-over he admits the prospect terrifies him and asks that his sister and Freminet not be told. Her own account is that "the difference between Crucabena and myself lies in our formulation of the rules, not our policy for enforcing them."
### ULTRA +
Crucabena taught the children to fight and made them duel each other to choose the "King" who would inherit her title. When Clervie was sixteen she was paired against Peruere and chose to let Peruere end her life. Arlecchino abolished the death duels but kept the idea of a successor. She chose Lyney after seeing his resolve to protect his family (his Pyro Vision awakened while saving Lynette), and in 4.6 told him that despite his lack of strength his conviction was strong, the same conviction that let her defeat the stronger Crucabena.

## members
### LITE
Lyney (the Great Magician), his twin sister Lynette and Freminet, the diver, are the trio the Traveler knows best. All three are playable.
### LARGE +
Other Fontaine members seen in quests include Chapleau, Foltz and Heloir, and former members such as Filliol and Nanteuil. Alumni serve elsewhere in the Fatui: the soldier Katarina and her younger brother, Corporal Nikolay, were raised in the House, and Trofin Snezhevich served in Vimara Village. Efim Snezhevich, an old instructor, led a House-raised spy cell in Inazuma that included Lyudochka Snezhevna.
Members are given work suited to their talent; Arlecchino assigns missions by name, for example sending Chapleau with Lyney to retrieve intelligence while Foltz and Filliol guard the Hearth. Individual profiles are covered by the characters' own entries.

## goals
### STANDARD
Officially, the House supplies the Fatui with intelligence and trained operatives. Arlecchino's own aims are the children's safety and their future. In Fontaine the House acted to protect the homeland where the children were born. In 7.1 she said she should first free the children from the "least familial elements" of their home environment before discussing what a real family looks like.
### LARGE +
Pierro described the House's "fundamental contradiction" as "the inseparable mix of love and exploitation", adding that many are aware of it but she is the only one willing to make this deal with him. Her stated price for joining his Delusion project is that the House becomes independent of the Fatui; Pierro summarises the terms as the children following her leadership "in the name of the Crimson Moon".

## methods
### STANDARD
Orphans who show potential are enlisted in the Fatui when grown and sent abroad as sleeper agents to await orders. They can also work for Arlecchino directly or for other Harbingers on tasks other than espionage. Children of a given ethnicity may study their homeland's affairs from an early age. Training is harsh, covering combat, stealth, surveillance and cover identities. Under Arlecchino the one vital rule is loyalty, and betrayal is punishable by death. She may spare someone with a potion containing her flame, which erases all of their memories of the House and so "kills" them in her eyes. These potions go only to those with the strength or will to fight.
### LARGE +
Day-to-day life at the Hotel is domestic, with a fireplace where the family gathers. Operatives use many codewords. Under Crucabena the hopscotch-like "Marelle" was played over boxes lined with upturned blades; Arlecchino chose the word as the codeword for "At Ease".

## history
### STANDARD
**Origins.** The name goes back to an orphanage in Khaenri'ah's Crimson Moon Dynasty that sheltered outlanders who fell from the sea of stars. The Fatui-era House was started by an earlier Knave.

**Crucabena's House.** Under Crucabena the children saw a fairytale family. She made them fight each other, sent them on missions not meant to be survived, and passed some to Dottore for experiments. Only her daughter Clervie and Peruere saw through the act.

**Arlecchino's House.** Peruere killed Clervie in a duel and, a year later, killed Crucabena on the same spot. The Tsaritsa pardoned her and made her the Knave. She reworked the House and asked to be called "Father".
### LARGE +
**Fontaine (4.0 to 4.6).** The House served Arlecchino's mission to obtain the Hydro Gnosis. In 4.6, in her Ignis Purgatorius chapter, a "phantom child" urging others to flee the House appeared in Fontaine. Arlecchino meant to execute the members who had broken the rules, but after Lyney's group dueled her she let them leave by drinking a potion of her flame that erased their memories of the House, and she laid Clervie's shadow to rest. She also agreed with Neuvillette to slowly withdraw her subordinates from Fontaine and refrain from special operations there unless necessary, in return for former operatives not being harassed by the authorities.
### ULTRA +
**Funding and Project Stuzha.** Pantalone and Pulcinella gave the House a great deal of funding and, after the Fontaine Archon Quests, made it "key members" of Project Stuzha. Expecting the heaviest casualties, the House suspects this is a ploy to weaken it and keep its growing influence in check; Arlecchino said that with the weaker members removed, they would turn the plan against its authors when the time came. **Nod-Krai and Snezhnaya (6.x to 7.1).** By Chapter VII the House works with Arlecchino in Snezhnaya. In 7.1 the Traveler meets Lyney, Lynette and Freminet in Snezhnograd, after a mission with Father and others from the House, gathering intelligence and preparing for officials who might defect. Arlecchino then agreed to Pierro's Delusion project in exchange for the House's independence (quest "Delusion", Act IV). The trio do not appear in that quest.

## relations
### LARGE
The House answers to the Tsaritsa through Arlecchino, but Pantalone and Pulcinella's funding of Project Stuzha is read by its members as an attempt to control an intelligence service outside their reach. Pierro accepted the independence deal, saying that provided Arlecchino remains loyal to the Tsaritsa and acts in Snezhnaya's interests, it makes little difference whether her subordinates are independent. In Fontaine its relationship with the authorities rests on Arlecchino's 4.6 agreement with Neuvillette.

## reputation
### LARGE
In Fontaine the House presents as an ordinary hotel and family. Orphans of the House consider each other family, "tighter than blood". Rumours spread about it too: Pulcinella told Tartaglia that Arlecchino had killed her House family, leaving out that it happened under Crucabena. In Snezhnograd in 7.1, a man explains he dresses plainly so as not to become a target for the House, and the con artist Avaz says they have been "acting like wild dogs lately".

## secrets
### LARGE
Under Crucabena, orphans were sometimes created by Fatui schemes to feed the House, and Efim Snezhevich still endorsed the practice. Arlecchino herself descends from the Crimson Moon Dynasty, which Pierro told her; he remarked that the House was "once again" led by an orphan of that bloodline. The 7.1 independence terms speak of the children following her "in the name of the Crimson Moon".

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the House is active and operating in Snezhnaya with Arlecchino. She has agreed with Pierro that it will become independent of the Fatui and stop training children as spies.
### STANDARD +
How and when this takes effect is not shown.

## entry: Hotel Bouffes d'ete
@keys: Hotel Bouffes d'ete, Bouffes d'ete
### STANDARD
A hotel in the Court of Fontaine and the only known location where the House of the Hearth operates. The wiki notes that other orphanages outside Fontaine are implied. Lyney said everyone there, Arlecchino included, comes from Fontaine; Arlecchino was raised there but is not Fontainian by blood.

## entry: Crucabena
@keys: Crucabena, previous Knave
### STANDARD
Crucabena was the previous Knave and head of the House, addressed as "Mother". She kept a warm face while forcing children to fight, sending them on deadly missions and giving some to Dottore. She blackmailed others, such as Freminet, into compliance. Peruere killed her a year after Clervie's death, on the same spot, and the Tsaritsa then made Peruere the Knave.

## entry: Clervie
@keys: Clervie, Perrie, phantom child
@version: 4.6
### STANDARD
Crucabena's daughter and Peruere's friend, who called her "Perrie". She died in a duel against Peruere, and her shadow was absorbed into Peruere. It later surfaced in the Fontaine House as a "phantom child".

## entry: Bottled flames
@keys: bottled flames, bottled flame, memory-burning potion
@version: 4.6
### STANDARD
Arlecchino's way of "executing" House members who betray her without killing them. The potion contains her flame and erases every memory of the House.

## entry: Independence deal with Pierro
@keys: House independence, independent organization, Pierro's Delusion
@keys2: House of the Hearth, Arlecchino
@version: 7.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
In the 7.1 Archon Quest (quest "Delusion"), Arlecchino agreed to take part in Pierro's project to create a new Delusion drawing on her Crimson Moon heritage, the Crimson Moon having once held the responsibilities of death. As her terms, the House of the Hearth is to become an independent organisation, no longer serving as a Fatui intelligence agency, and no more children are to be trained as spies; instead they will follow her leadership "in the name of the Crimson Moon". A test at maximum output pushed her to the point where the Traveler had to rescue her from abyssal corrosion.

## entry: Efim Snezhevich and the Inazuma cell
@keys: Efim Snezhevich, Shuumatsuban Operations
@keys2: Inazuma, House of the Hearth
### LARGE
Efim was an instructor under Crucabena who broke away and pursued her old directives while pretending to serve Arlecchino. After Signora's death he led House-raised spies in Inazuma. Lyudochka Snezhevna, of Inazuman descent, stalled the plot; with the Traveler and the Shuumatsuban's Momoyo the plan was dismantled, Lyudochka's death was faked so Efim would not pursue her, and she stayed in Inazuma. Efim was recalled to Snezhnaya before the operation.
