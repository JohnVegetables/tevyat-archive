## summary
### LITE
The Northland Bank is a Snezhnayan bank with branches across Teyvat. Its president is Pantalone, "Regrator", the Ninth Fatui Harbinger. The Fatui use funds from the Bank when operating outside Snezhnaya and sometimes use its branches as bases. Its main branch and the underground vaults beneath it are in Snezhnograd. Visible branches stand in Liyue Harbor, Nasha Town in Nod-Krai and the Court of Fontaine, and a branch was being opened in Sumeru City.
### STANDARD +
The Bank describes itself as no ordinary bank. It embodies what Snezhnaya values, loyalty and promises, and it is generous with loans but expects them repaid in full and on time.

## structure
### STANDARD
Pantalone is president and runs the Bank's operations as a whole. Each branch has its own manager and staff; Andrei manages the Liyue branch, with Ekaterina as receptionist and Javert as debt collector. The Fatui have a debt-collection division staffed mostly by Fatui Pyro Agents. New branches may be set up by other Harbingers: Pulcinella handled the Sumeru opening, assisted by Viktor and Vsevolod.
### LARGE +
The Snezhnograd branch has named staff including the account manager Strinov, receptionist Kamenov, banker Avaz, several other employees, and guards. The Nod-Krai branch in Nasha Town is run by Jarosznski, with Kudratov as a debt-collection agent. Fontaine's branch building stands in the Court of Fontaine between two clockwork workshops, with its interior inaccessible.

## leadership
### LITE
Pantalone founded the Bank shortly after becoming a Harbinger and has been president since. He also oversees Snezhnaya's finances.
### STANDARD +
He is said to have merged human rules with fae customs when designing its system, and given his great age the Bank has grown exponentially and survived numerous crises. A map description credits him as the master of a "web" of exchanges across Teyvat.

## goals
### STANDARD
Pantalone saw that Snezhnaya's private banks served only themselves and that no money reached the leadership, so he created the nation's first state-run bank. The Bank's purpose is to channel the flow of Mora and wealth, and Pantalone aims to take the continent's economy out of the gods' hands. According to Vesna, he once told the Tsaritsa that the Bank would become the beating heart of finance for the whole world.

## methods
### STANDARD
The Bank lends generously. If a borrower defaults, an agent from the Fatui's debt-collection division is usually sent, using as much force as needed, though other options are tried too. In Nod-Krai the branch gave a loan to Zhuoxin; instead of collecting, it made him a spy for ten years, which he accepted to protect his family. Branches also exchange funds for the Fatui. In Liyue the Bank marks its bags with elemental energy, to identify what belongs to it.
### LARGE +
In Liyue Tartaglia occasionally helps the Bank collect debts. After he and Javert confronted the former owner of the Yanshang Teahouse, he uncovered the owner's fraud and reported it to the Millelith, which let Yelan take the teahouse over. The episode contributed to the Bank's notorious reputation among borrowers.

## history
### STANDARD
**Origins.** Pantalone founded the Bank soon after he became a Harbinger.

**Liyue.** The Liyue branch was set up before the Traveler arrived. In the first Archon Quest chapter it served as a rendezvous for the Traveler and Tartaglia. After Tartaglia's attack on the authorities during the Osial incident, Ningguang made the Bank pay a large sum in reparations, which left its staff resentful. In Endless Smoke she confronted them over spying on her.
### LARGE +
**Sumeru and Fontaine.** A Sumeru City branch was being opened when the Traveler arrived, and a branch building also stands in the Court of Fontaine.

**Natlan and Nod-Krai.** Zhuoxin, a Nod-Krai borrower working as the branch's spy, was offered a waiver of his debt in connection with the elite Fatui squad that Sandrone sent to Natlan under Velimiru after Ineffa. In the Archon Quest The Journey Home the squad was killed, and Zhuoxin, fearing Fatui reprisal, stayed with his family under the protection of the Flower-Feather Clan.
### ULTRA +
**Snezhnograd (7.1).** In the Chapter VII Act III quest One Move Left to Make, the Seals of Life donated by the public were being kept in the deepest vaults of the Bank. When some went missing, the Traveler and Vesna entered the vaults. Pantalone met them there, saying he too was looking for the culprit. A creature made with the Ruler of Life's power was draining the seals, and when it shielded itself Pantalone activated the vault's turrets to give them an opening. After it was defeated, Rhinedottir appeared, reclaimed the Ruler of Life's power from Vesna and returned the taken seals; that night the seals floated back to their donors, and the Veche announced they had served their purpose, with the Bank to negotiate compensation for any damaged or missing ones. Pantalone then returned to Vesna a beacon made from the small sample of that power she had provided, which the Bank had held in trust for the seal collection. He later approached Sandrone about improving the Bank's security; she found the request excessive and his offer too low.

## relations
### STANDARD
The Bank is tied to the Fatui but is not identical to them, and the exact tie differs by account. Harbingers use its funds, and Liyue's Qixing are wary of it. Tartaglia helps in Liyue. Pulcinella oversees some branch openings. Dottore's research was funded by Pantalone, who in turn received an immortality elixir.
### LARGE +
In Snezhnaya the Bank is a state institution; Pantalone sits on the Snezhnograd Veche and voted to continue Project Stuzha. Together with Pulcinella he gave the House of the Hearth money and a key role in Project Stuzha.

## reputation
### STANDARD
Liyue customers know the Bank for generous loans but also for hard methods; rivals such as Gentry De'an claim it used underhanded means to establish itself. Staff stress client service and security. In Snezhnaya it is spoken of as extremely secure.

## secrets
### LARGE
The Seals of Life were donated by the public and kept in its deepest vaults. When seals went missing, the banker Avaz, who had noticed the discrepancy and was scouting buyers to sell seals himself, pointed the blame at Pantalone as the only person with unrestricted access; the Tsaritsa dismissed this, saying Pantalone was too prideful to involve himself. The losses turned out to be the work of a creature tied to Rhinedottir, and Avaz was to be handed to Fatui Internal Affairs.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the Bank operates normally with Pantalone as president. The Seals of Life it stored in Snezhnograd were returned to their donors at the end of the 7.1 Archon Quest, and the Bank is negotiating compensation for damaged or missing seals.
### STANDARD +
Branches continue in Liyue, Sumeru City, Fontaine and Nod-Krai; nothing in 7.1 shows any branch closing.

### STANDARD
The main branch in Snezhnograd has a huge underground complex reached by a short train ride. The most valuable items are stored there.
