## summary
### LITE
The Marechaussee Phantom is Fontaine's elite detective force, led by Chief Justice Neuvillette and made up largely of Melusines, whose sharp eyesight lets them notice more than most humans. It is a special branch of the Maison Gardiennage, based at the Palais Mermonia, and answers directly to Neuvillette. Its name comes from the Marechaussee Hunters, who once hunted monsters across Fontaine with special sword techniques.
### STANDARD +
Today the Phantom spends its time on criminal investigation, not battle. The Hunters survive only as a tradition passed down through families and chosen successors, and none of them are known to serve in the Phantom now.

## structure
### STANDARD
Canon gives little formal structure. The Phantom is one of two special branches of the Maison Gardiennage, alongside the Special Security and Surveillance Patrol, and it is chiefly responsible for investigation. It is led by Neuvillette, and its uniform is typically blue. Aquabus operators belong to the same service with slightly different, custom-coloured outfits: Aeval of the Clementine Line wears light blue and Elphane of the Navia Line wears red.
### LARGE +
No ranks, headcount or captains are given for the Phantom itself. The Special Patrol, by contrast, has a captain (Chevreuse), and about 400 years ago Vautrin, its captain, worked beside the Melusine Carole when the first Melusines joined the Phantom.

## leadership
### STANDARD
Neuvillette leads the Phantom, and it answers directly to the Chief Justice. The Iudex of Fontaine has the historical right to prosecute and enforce the law in person, a power now exercised mostly in symbol, and the Phantom serves as his investigative arm. The Hunters, the Phantom's predecessors, were founded by Cassiodor, called the Golden Hunter.
### LARGE +
The wiki lists no other current leaders. Melusines revere Neuvillette as a father figure, and he calls them his dependents and successors, which colours how the Phantom's Melusine staff relate to him.

## members
### STANDARD
Most of the Phantom's staff are Melusines. Confirmed members include Sedene, who works at the Palais Mermonia, and Kiara, who helped solve a smuggling case. Historical members include Carole. Emanuel Guillotin, a Hunter, and the Narzissenkreuz Institute survivors Alain and Mary-Ann Guillotin are linked to the force in artifact text. The Marechaussee Hunters are Cassiodor, Este, Walter, Petronilla, Emanuel Guillotin, Florian and his father, and Clorinde.
### LARGE +
The wiki's count of current members is a category list rather than a roster. Clorinde is the best-known heir of the Hunters. She was trained by Petronilla in their marksmanship, but she serves the Trial Court as Champion Duelist, not the Phantom. Florian, a descendant of Hunters, appears in Clorinde's story quest.

## goals
### STANDARD
The Phantom investigates crime in the Court of Fontaine and brings its findings to the Chief Justice. The older Hunters sought to hunt down monsters, and the founding motto credited to Cassiodor was to pursue the phantoms in the dark and drive them out. Neuvillette also hoped Melusines in the force would help them win the trust of humans.

## methods
### STANDARD
Melusines' powerful eyesight and ability to perceive more than ordinary people make them well suited to detective work, which is why Neuvillette recruited them. The Hunters' techniques were different. A Hunter's weapons were traditionally a musket or pistolet to drive prey and a sword for the kill, with training in close combat, tracking, camouflage, footwork and marksmanship. The Hunters enchanted weapons to glow near monsters.
### LARGE +
Hunters carried keys passed down generation to generation, and quickly revealed their identity to fellow Hunters to avoid accidental injury. On a new member's first birthday as a Hunter, their mentor makes them a short-sword necklace engraved with their name, symbolising interrogating one's own heart at swordpoint. These customs are known mainly through Clorinde, whose voice-overs and profile describe them.

## history
### LARGE
**The Hunters.** After Remuria fell, Cassiodor, a former Remurian warrior later known as the Golden Hunter, founded the Marechaussee Hunters to hunt down the monsters the dynasty left behind. Remurian survivors such as Este and Walter followed him, and the group is said to have included heirs of the Lochknights. The Hunters thinned the monster population and returned to ordinary lives; their special methods faded and their names were forgotten. Cassiodor himself disliked the name "Marechaussee Hunter", seeing it as a humiliation, according to artifact text.

**The Siege of Poisson.** More than 500 years ago, outlaws under Eduardo Baker occupied Poisson and held Maison Gardiennage escorts hostage. The Hunter Emanuel Guillotin directed the operation, and the Hunters entered through secret paths to end it by force. The town was razed and many of its inhabitants burned to death. Disillusioned, Emanuel left the Hunters.

**The cataclysm and after.** After the Narzissenkreuz Institute's Director and Vice Director vanished in the cataclysm, some members, including Alain and Mary-Ann Guillotin, were taken in by the Marechaussee Phantom or the Special Patrol to protect Fontaine. Years later, the Court of Fontaine noticed the Narzissenkreuz Ordo, and the Phantom was told to investigate. Knowing this, the Ordo invited the Marechaussee Hunters to guard construction of the Tower of Ipsissimus and so allay suspicion. The conflict ended in a fierce battle at Elynas with many casualties on both sides.
### ULTRA +
**The Melusines.** About 400 years ago, Neuvillette, newly Iudex, brought Melusines into Fontaine's society. In the 4.1 story Ever-Surging Memories, Melusines such as Carole joined the Phantom; he gave Carole and Vautrin, the Special Patrol's captain, Medals of Peace after five years. Opponents from the old regime disliked the reforms and the Melusines. They framed Carole, and she died to prove her innocence. Vautrin killed those responsible and was sent to the Fortress of Meropide after Neuvillette's trial. Later, Kiara, another early Melusine, faced a threat after solving a smuggling case, and Neuvillette personally investigated to prevent a repeat.

**The Hunters in the present.** In Clorinde's story quest Silent Night (4.7), Florian, a Hunter descendant, lured Clorinde, Navia, Lyney, Furina and the Traveler into opening the Hunters' hidden vault. It held a tablet carved with the Hunters' names and descriptions of their lives, plus their old broken weapons. Clorinde decided to leave the vault undisturbed, so that their deeds stay forgotten as they would have wanted.

## relations
### STANDARD
- **Maison Gardiennage**: the Phantom is a special branch and shares the Palais Mermonia with it.
- **Special Security and Surveillance Patrol**: sister branch, led by Chevreuse. The Patrol makes arrests; the Phantom investigates.
- **Champion Duelists**: Clorinde is both the Trial Court's strongest duelist and the Hunters' heir, which gives the old tradition a living link to the courts.
- **Melusines**: the Phantom's main workforce. Neuvillette treats them as dependents and successors.
### LARGE +
- **Narzissenkreuz Ordo**: investigated by the Phantom, which led to the battle at Elynas.
- **Clorinde and Navia**: Petronilla introduced the young Clorinde to Navia, and in Silent Night's finale Navia calls Petronilla "the Hunter I respect the most".

## reputation
### STANDARD
Most Fontainians know the Hunters only as a legend, usually through the "Hunter's Oath" they have met in plays and novels. The Phantom itself is less known, but hardliners once complained that Neuvillette had given Melusines official posts and investigation powers. Neuvillette, for his part, praises Clorinde as a successor to the Hunters who ensures the courtroom's fairness.

## secrets
### ULTRA
The Hunters' names and deeds were deliberately left forgotten, according to the choice of Hunters who knew what the path entailed. The vault in Silent Night preserves them, and it is known to Hunters' descendants such as Florian and Clorinde, and to the friends who opened it with them.

## entry: Marechaussee Hunters
@keys: Marechaussee Hunters, Marechaussee Hunter, Hunter's Oath, Golden Hunter
### STANDARD
The Marechaussee Hunters were founded by Cassiodor, the Golden Hunter, to hunt the monsters and phantoms that threatened Fontaine. They used special sword techniques, musket marksmanship, tracking and camouflage, and enchanted weapons that glow near monsters. Over time the monsters were thinned out, the Hunters returned to ordinary work, and their names were forgotten. Today the Hunters are a family tradition: Clorinde inherited the training from Petronilla, and Florian descends from Hunters. The best-known surviving custom is the Hunter's Oath, which most Fontainians know only from stories.
### LARGE +
Cassiodor's motto was to pursue the phantoms in the dark, expel them all and hunt them down. The Hunter's Brooch was an emblem awarded for feats of arms in Fontaine's defence, though some recipients hid or drowned theirs. Hunters whose names are called in Silent Night's finale include Este, whom the tablet records as having inherited the Golden Hunter's responsibilities ("inheritor of Cassiodor's will"), Walter and Petronilla.

## entry: Cassiodor, the Golden Hunter
@keys: Cassiodor, Golden Hunter
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Cassiodor founded the Marechaussee Hunters. He was a warrior of Remuria's age, and the Hunters gathered Remurian survivors and heirs of the Lochknights around him. His epithet "Golden Hunter" is credited to him as a hero, though the artifact text says he saw the Marechaussee Hunter name as a humiliation. Accounts disagree on when he founded the Hunters, and the wiki says he guided later generations over a very long time.

## entry: Siege of Poisson (Phantom view)
@keys: Siege of Poisson, Emanuel Guillotin
@keys2: Marechaussee Hunters, Poisson
### LARGE
More than 500 years ago the Marechaussee Hunter Emanuel Guillotin led the operation that ended the siege of Poisson, entering the occupied town through secret paths. The town was burned and many residents died. He left the Hunters disillusioned and later became cold and detached, until an old friend asked him to live among people again, and he raised Alain and Mary-Ann Guillotin. He is recorded as a member of the Narzissenkreuz Institute and the Phantom.

## entry: Melusines in the Phantom
@keys: Melusine detectives, Melusines, Carole
@keys2: Marechaussee Phantom, Neuvillette, detective force
@version: 4.1
### LARGE
Neuvillette brought Melusines from Merusea Village into the Phantom because their special sight suits detective work. Carole, one of the first, believed Melusines would find meaning in work and live in peace with humans. Distrust remained, and she died after being framed. The tragedy led to Vautrin's imprisonment and to the Garde hat emblem being redesigned to include Melusines. Kiara, who came to the Court with Carole, later joined the Phantom and was threatened after a smuggling case, which Neuvillette personally investigated.

## entry: Silent Night and the Hunters' vault
@keys: Silent Night, Hunters' vault, Florian, Buried Honor, Vanquishing Phantoms
@keys2: Clorinde, Marechaussee Hunter, Hunter
@version: 4.7
### LARGE
In Clorinde's story quest Silent Night, an apparent Tabletop Troupe script cast Navia, Lyney, Furina and the Traveler as Marechaussee Hunters, with Clorinde as game master. It was actually Florian's script, written under the influence of a monster called the Devorator. He led the group to the Hunters' vault, which held a tablet of names and the broken weapons of past Hunters. When the Devorator took over Florian's body, the group recited the names and the weapons glowed. Clorinde struck the final blow, the group accepted Florian's help in making amends, and Clorinde left the vault untouched.

## entry: Narzissenkreuz Ordo investigation and Elynas
@keys: Narzissenkreuz Ordo, Elynas, Tower of Ipsissimus
@keys2: Marechaussee Phantom, Marechaussee Hunters
### ULTRA
The Court of Fontaine ordered the Phantom to investigate the Ordo. In response, the Ordo asked the Marechaussee Hunters to take charge of security while the Tower of Ipsissimus was built, to disarm suspicion. A fierce battle followed at Elynas, with many casualties. According to the Melusine page, the explosion and the meka parts that pierced the beast are tied to the origin of the Melusines about 400 years ago. The Phantom page itself leaves the date open.
