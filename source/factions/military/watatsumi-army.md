## summary
### LITE
The Watatsumi Army, also called the Resistance, is the armed force of Watatsumi Island in Inazuma. It was raised against the Vision Hunt Decree and the Shogunate's Sakoku order and is led by Sangonomiya Kokomi, the island's Divine Priestess, with General Gorou commanding in the field. Its soldiers are called Sangonomiya samurai or troops, and the Shogunate calls them rebels. The decree is now abolished, so the army works as peacekeepers and rebuilders.
### STANDARD +
Its ranks include people who had lost their Visions, people who feared losing them, and people who simply opposed the decree, Visions or not. The Resistance also hid those unwilling to fight the Shogun's forces. Its bases are Watatsumi Island, where the Sangonomiya Shrine stands, and Fort Fujitou on Yashiori Island.

## structure
### STANDARD
The army is organised into small numbered units named after fish: Herring, Stingray, Swordfish and Gorgasia. Each is divided into numbered squads (Herring I, Swordfish II, Gorgasia II and so on). Kokomi sits at the top, Gorou is general, and officers such as Kaida Ryouma hold camps like Fort Fujitou. Rank-and-file soldiers are Sangonomiya samurai in a standard uniform based on the ashigaru foot-soldier. It is red and white, with the Sangonomiya crest on the back and on the right shoulder guard, and soldiers such as Nathan carry a cross-bladed spear (juumonji yari). The Traveler was made captain of Swordfish II.
### LARGE +
Named squads and officers: Teppei, the first captain of Herring I (until his death in Chapter II); Yabe of Stingray III; Ueno, a provisional squad leader of Swordfish I who appears during a limited-time event; Koutoku, Kaneda, Shikishima and Yoshihisa of Swordfish II; and Gorou, who rose from Gorgasia II member to its captain. Kokomi governs through detailed written directives, and soldiers are expected to follow them. She also issues temporary decrees for island affairs, such as one against price gouging. Support staff, workers and smiths are attached to the camps. One example is the blacksmith Miyazaki Saburou at Fort Fujitou.

## leadership
### LITE
Sangonomiya Kokomi, the Divine Priestess, is the supreme leader. She is a brilliant strategist who plans for every scenario in advance. General Gorou, who is open-hearted and trusted by his troops, leads the soldiers on the battlefield.
### STANDARD +
Kokomi inherited the Priestess role through the Sangonomiya bloodline. It passes on the will of the dead god Orobashi in a mortal body, to defend Watatsumi Island. She has read military treatises since childhood, favours winning wars at the lowest cost by shaping the larger strategic situation, and in her story quest admits privately that she never wanted to be a leader, though her bloodline made it inevitable. The islanders say "Things will be alright as long as Her Excellency is here." Gorou came up through the ranks. In one crisis his unit's captain fell, and he led a feint that held the line for days until Kokomi's own relief force arrived. That earned him command of Gorgasia II and later the generalship. He is humble and straightforward. His troops call him the "ever-victorious pointy-eared general," and he has a short temper in the heat of battle.
### LARGE +
Kokomi's directives are often long because she predicts so many scenarios. Gorou's task is to choose the right one in the moment, and he believes he will internalise her ideas in time. She once told him that materiel, supplies and wages are what an army depends on, so he began explaining weapon upkeep personally and watching the granaries to prevent illegal deductions. Gorou leads from the front and values his own life less than his soldiers', as his story puts it. Kokomi, by contrast, tires from social duties and uses a private "energy" measure to decide when to retreat into a book. She even leaves a directive for her own absences, so a guard at the shrine knows where to send visitors.

## members
### STANDARD
Notable members: Kaida Ryouma, an officer at Fort Fujitou who once worked for the Kujou Clan on Narukami Island and who remembers the Kujou brothers as sympathetic to commoners; Teppei, a logistician turned front-line captain; and many named samurai, including Moriguchi, Takahashi, Denjirou, Arisawa and Koutoku. Kaedehara Kazuha was sheltered by the Resistance before leaving Inazuma to join the Crux. Beidou and Kazuha appeared at the battle of Nazuchi Beach as mercenary reinforcements that Kokomi brought.
### LARGE +
Others include the blacksmith Miyazaki Saburou and the workers Yuuzou and Sumi at Fort Fujitou. Yoshihisa is a veteran of Swordfish II. Kouzen, Tsuneo and Uchimura appear in Kokomi's story quest. Nathan appeared to be a foreign volunteer from Mondstadt but was a Fatui infiltrator; he vanishes after the Delusion quest, and in the world quest Clean House the shrine maiden Shizuru says he tried to flee but was caught and thrown into a cell.

## goals
### STANDARD
During the war the army's aim was to resist the Vision Hunt Decree and to protect Watatsumi Island and its people. It also opposed the Sakoku Decree, which was imposed at the same time. Kokomi had her own aim as well, which was to win in a way that kept costs low. After the war the army's aims are to keep the peace, rebuild the island's economy, and root out Fatui remnants.

## methods
### LARGE
The army was outnumbered and short of materiel, so it relied on Kokomi's planning, defensive lines, ambushes and morale. A "fight of honour," a ceremonial contest between each side's best fighter that Kujou Sara proposed, was accepted at Nazuchi Beach. Gorou explains that a win in such a contest can steady a weaker side's nerve. Kokomi also brought mercenary reinforcements to turn that battle. Kokomi's tactics, in the army's own account, rely on forcing the opponent to give up rather than on bloody victories.
### ULTRA +
After the war the soldiers also worked as labour and security for civilians. Kokomi handled disputes by facts rather than rumour, such as a merchant fined for breaking her anti-price-gouging decree. She also arranged for a new corps to guard the island because Tenryou leaders had proved unable to do so.

## history
@version: 2.0
### LARGE
**Before the war.** Watatsumi Island's people once lived in Enkanomiya under the sea, until their god Orobashi lifted them to the surface. The Electro Archon slew Orobashi in the Archon War, leaving a skeleton and resentment that gave rise to the Tatarigami. Relations between Watatsumi Island and Narukami Island have been poor since. The island's military tradition is carried by the Sangonomiya bloodline, whose heir is the Divine Priestess.

**The war.** The Vision Hunt Decree was issued about a year before the Traveler reaches Inazuma, with the Tenryou Commission enforcing it. The Watatsumi Army declared war on the Shogunate, which fought back with the Shogun's Army under Kujou Sara. The Fatui secretly fed the conflict in order to build a market for Delusions.

**Joining the Traveler.** In Stillness, the Sublimation of Shadow, Thoma sends the Traveler to the Resistance after the Vision Hunt Ceremony. They rescue Teppei, meet Gorou at Fort Fujitou, join the defence against Sara, and are invited by Kokomi to Watatsumi Island. She makes them captain of Swordfish II.
### ULTRA +
**The Delusion crisis.** Kokomi had accepted an anonymous patron's supplies on condition that they kept fighting the Shogunate, despite doubting the motive. The supplies included secret weapons. They were Fatui Delusions that drained the vitality of anyone who used them. Teppei aged rapidly and died. Kokomi ordered all use to stop and the users to be treated. The Traveler destroyed the Delusion factory, which turned out to use Crystal Marrow gathered by the Kanjou Commission, and found that Nathan was a Fatui mole.

**The turn.** With Yae Miko's help the Traveler and Sara exposed Kujou Takayuki's treason. After Signora fell at Tenshukaku, the Resistance led by Gorou arrived outside as the Raiden Shogun struck at the Traveler. Kazuha used his friend's Electro Vision to block the blow, and the Resistance rallied. The Traveler then defeated Ei in the Plane of Euthymia, and she agreed to end the Vision Hunt Decree. The Visions were returned.

**Peace.** In Warriors' Dreams Like Spring Grass Renewing, Kokomi held a Victory Feast on Watatsumi Island, defused a plot by embittered soldiers to restart the war, and held peace talks with Kujou Sara off Kannazuka. The Fatui remnants were hunted down, and the talks moved relations toward a thaw. At Fort Fujitou, after the story quest, the background soldiers leave.

**Later.** In the Three Realms Gateway Offering event (2.5), Kokomi commissioned the Traveler to explore Enkanomiya for Watatsumi Island's future. Army members such as Ueno appear there, and the event is limited-time. In the 2025 Travelers' Tales anecdote Reunion, Swordfish II members still treat the Traveler as their captain, and Kokomi says the island's security has improved.

## relations
### LARGE
- **Inazuma Shogunate and the Shogun's Army**: former enemies. The Shogunate called the army "Sangonomiya Rebels." Peace talks followed the decree's end, and relations are warming. Gorou now visits Narukami Island often.
- **Tenryou Commission**: enforced the decree and led the war. Kujou Sara represented it in the peace talks.
- **Kanjou Commission**: Crystal Marrow it collected was used to make the Delusions.
- **Fatui**: secretly armed the Resistance with Delusions and infiltrated it with Nathan. Fatui camps and ships remained on Watatsumi Island afterwards.
- **Yae Miko and the Grand Narukami Shrine**: Miko trained the Traveler and helped bring down the Shogun's puppet rule. She later enlisted Gorou to write an advice column for her publishing house.
- **Kamisato Clan and Yashiro Commission**: neutral, but Thoma pointed the Traveler to the Resistance and the clan arranged the venue for the peace talks.
### ULTRA +
- **The Crux**: Kazuha, who had been sheltered by the Resistance, left Inazuma for it.
- **Kokomi and the Raiden Shogun**: a shrine near the island's Statue of the Seven once honoured both; relations soured after Orobashi's death. Kokomi hoped to address those grievances in talks.

## reputation
### STANDARD
Among the islanders the army is deeply trusted, and Kokomi's name is a byword for reassurance. Gorou is beloved by his men, and even Shogunate warriors repeat his "ever-victorious" nickname. The Shogunate treated it as a rebel force during the war.

## secrets
@spoiler: major
### ULTRA
The Resistance did not know at first that its mysterious patron was the Fatui, though Kokomi had doubts. Nathan was a Fatui agent sent to prolong the war. The Delusions killed at least Teppei before Kokomi stopped their use.

## status
@version: 2.1
### LITE
The Vision Hunt Decree has been repealed and the Watatsumi Army no longer fights the Shogunate. It serves as peacekeepers and helps the island recover, while still clearing out Fatui remnants.
### STANDARD +
Peace talks with the Tenryou Commission followed, and Kokomi created a new corps for island defence. No new Watatsumi Army story was found in 6.0 to 7.1.

## entry: Swordfish II
@keys: Swordfish II, Swordfish squad
### STANDARD
Swordfish II is a special operations unit that Kokomi gives the Traveler to captain in Sword, Fish, Resistance. Members named in canon are Koutoku (a former provisional squad leader), Kaneda, Shikishima and Yoshihisa. The unit investigates the disappearance of supplies near Fort Mumei while staying at Bourou Village. In the Reunion anecdote, the members still call the Traveler their captain and invite them to a bonfire party.

## entry: Fort Fujitou
@keys: Fort Fujitou
### STANDARD
Fort Fujitou is the Resistance camp on Yashiori Island. Kaida Ryouma is its officer, and workers and a blacksmith live there. Teppei brings the Traveler there to meet Gorou, and the camp is the base from which the fight against Sara's troops is organised. After Kokomi's story quest the camp's background soldiers withdraw.

## entry: Teppei
@keys: Teppei
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Teppei was a logistics officer who wanted to make a name for himself. He guides the Traveler to the Resistance and becomes captain of Herring I. Fatui supporters gave him a "secret weapon" that was really a Delusion. It drained his life, and he died rapidly aged. Kokomi ordered all use stopped afterwards.

## entry: Nathan the Fatui mole
@keys: Nathan
@keys2: Watatsumi Army, Fort Fujitou, Fatui
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
Nathan claimed to be a Mondstadt volunteer sympathetic to the cause. A notice in the Delusion factory shows he was a Fatui infiltrator. His job was to sabotage the Resistance and prolong the war. He disappears from Fort Fujitou after the Delusion quest. In the world quest Clean House (2.2), Shizuru of Sangonomiya Shrine says he tried to escape but was punished and imprisoned, and the Traveler recovers a report he had written for the Fatui.

## entry: Battle of Nazuchi Beach
@keys: Nazuchi Beach, fight of honour
@keys2: Kujou Sara, Gorou, Traveler
@spoiler: minor
### LARGE
Kujou Sara arrives at Nazuchi Beach to take the Traveler, offering a fight of honour. The Traveler wins the contests and the two sides clash. The Shogun's Army starts to prevail until Kokomi arrives with mercenaries including Beidou and Kazuha, and Sara retreats.

## entry: Victory Feast and peace talks
@keys: Victory Feast, Kannazuka peace talks, peace talks
@keys2: Kokomi, Sara, Resistance
@version: 2.1
@spoiler: major
### ULTRA
After the war, Kokomi plans a Victory Feast and peace talks with the Tenryou Commission. A group of embittered soldiers, among them Kouzen, feel their comrades died for nothing and want war again. Kokomi finds out and handles it quietly. At the talks, Sara learns that Fatui remnants had approached some Tenryou guards. She apologises to Kokomi and vows to hunt them down. Kokomi refuses to return her troops to Tenryou command for now, and she announces a new corps to defend the island, which spares the soldiers a punishment.
