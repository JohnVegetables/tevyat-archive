## summary
### LITE
The Corps of Thirty is the mercenary brigade that guards Sumeru City. It is not an official army. It is a permanently hired brigade of Eremites, kept on retainer by the Sumeru Akademiya, and its members wear teal-green scarves instead of the crimson ones of other Eremites. Its base is the Citadel of Regzar, and its commander is the Parchamdar, Rukh Shah.
### STANDARD +
Its name remembers its origin: thirty mercenaries hired by the Akademiya's sages. It has since grown far larger, but the name stayed. The Corps defends the city, polices it, and handles the everyday problems of residents.

## structure
### LITE
A Parchamdar commands the Corps (the word means roughly "standard-bearer"). A Consultant serves beside the Parchamdar as a second, administrative leader.
### STANDARD +
The wiki calls the Corps the oldest of the Eremite brigades (Rukh Shah himself says only "among the oldest"). The wiki, citing the quest "The Trail of the God of Wisdom", says it manages and coordinates the affairs of the other mercenary brigades, though what is visible in play is mostly its work in Sumeru City. The Akademiya employs a liaison, Effendi, to coordinate between the scholars and the Corps. Staff at the Citadel of Regzar include a logistics officer (Yunus, nicknamed "Bigfish"), a messenger called the "Swallow" (Sekkura) and a member nicknamed "Goat" (Udad). Rank-and-file mercenaries are posted around Treasures Street and the Puspa Cafe.
### LARGE +
Management of manpower, training and supplies is relaxed. Sido, who spoke for the Corps at the 4.7 wargame event, said that the Corps relies on individual blacksmiths rather than a standard supply system, and that skill levels vary greatly among its members. A Su Ding briefing in the same event relays the Corps representative's claim that it is lightly armed on purpose, so that it can fight in environments from desert to rainforest on small supplies, which leaves it at a disadvantage against heavily armoured foes.

## leadership
### LITE
Rukh Shah is the Parchamdar. He is the youngest person ever to hold the post. The Consultant, Asfand, is a retired veteran who still advises him.
### STANDARD +
Rukh Shah greets visitors by explaining his role and offering the Corps' help in town, then warns them to be careful of other Eremites outside the city, even in Port Ormos. According to the Swallow Sekkura, he is supposedly from the rainforest, not the desert, which is very rare among Eremites, and he gets along both with desert people and with Akademiya researchers. She also repeats a rumor that he was once a researcher himself; Effendi, the Akademiya's liaison, says the Parchamdar used to be a researcher. Asfand says he retired but was asked by Rukh Shah to advise because he has served in both the Corps and other groups, and he offers to settle unexpected conflicts.
### LARGE +
Asfand's idle thoughts say he wishes Dehya would join the Corps, because it needs more people like her. Rukh Shah's idle thoughts say only that the workload is increasing again.

## members
### LITE
Dehya, the "Flame-Mane", was once a member and is respected by the Corps. Other named members include Sido, the Swallow Sekkura, the logistics officer Yunus, Udad, the mercenaries Hakan and Farid, and Izem, a veteran now retired to the Puspa Cafe.
### STANDARD +
Asfand calls Dehya a reliable partner and one of the best in the Corps, adds that she speaks frankly, and says she handled her recent commission well (she met the young lady of the Homayani family, Dunyarzad, and made friends). Sido says that everyone in the Corps greatly respects her. She looked out for many of its members, and many followed her into battle. The wiki lists her Corps affiliation as "formerly". Sekkura longs to visit Aaru Village again and says she has not seen Candace for ages.
### LARGE +
Sido appears only in limited-time events. In Fabulous Fungus Frenzy (3.2), he led a Corps operation after the Beast Tamers Tournament: Cyno's letter says Sido confirmed all the locations where the remaining criminals were likely to appear and that the Corps could capture them with a limited number of people. In the 4.7 wargame event Mutual Security Enhancing Simulation he represented the Corps in a delegation of regional armed services.
### ULTRA +
Qanafiya is a Corps member who appears in the 6.6 Archon Quest Darkness Looming. While helping to clear the last people out of Caravan Ribat, he offers Nefer (who is posing as a stranded merchant) a lift and asks if she can sell on an item taken from King Deshret's mausoleum. Cyno arrests him at the southern gate. See the history section.

## goals
### STANDARD
The Corps' job is to keep Sumeru City safe: defend it, guard it against criminals, and deal with disorders that the Akademiya or ordinary residents report. Most tasks are assigned by the Akademiya, plus requests from civilians. The Corps does not make policy.

## methods
### STANDARD
Corps members patrol the city, take on residents' commissions and keep watch over crowds during festivals. During the Akademiya's Parade of Providence event, Rukh Shah said he had increased patrols because the influx of tourists gave schemers cover, and during the Chromatic Ode of Candies and Roses event he asked people to report anything unusual to the nearest Corps member. Sekkura says that dusk birds now carry many messages, which lightened her errands but added the care of the birds.
### LARGE +
In the world quest Corps of Thirty Recruitment, Effendi explains that the Corps is overburdened by residents' small requests on top of defending the whole city. He recruits the Traveler as an assistant for those commissions, with fair pay for each job, and says that doing them well will raise the Traveler's standing with residents. That quest unlocks the Sumeru reputation menu.
### ULTRA +
Dress and equipment are close to those of other Eremites, but the scarf is teal-green with a petal-like emblem similar to the Akademiya's, instead of the crimson scarf with a crossed-sword triangle. The Eremite crest and colours still appear on banners near the barracks.

## relations
### LITE
The Corps works for the Akademiya, has an uneasy relationship with other Eremites, and shares Sumeru City's security with the matra.
### STANDARD +
Other Eremites are sometimes hostile toward it. In the 3.1 Archon Quest The Gaze From a Certain God, an Eremite leader named Tashfin says his brigade is nothing like "those government lapdogs who don't even get scraps", meaning the Corps. The Eremites page mentions a swindler, Kasim, who uses the Eremite name for personal gain and is known as a fraud by the Corps. Alhaitham notes the Corps' poor relationship with the Ayn Al-Ahmar, devout followers of King Deshret who loudly denounce Greater Lord Rukkhadevata. In the repeatable Reputation Request Eremite Mayhem, residents ask the Corps to deal with Eremites dumping refuse on Devantaka Mountain, troubling the road to Caravan Ribat and skulking on the outskirts of Sumeru City.
### LARGE +
Besides the matra, who deal with scholars and Akademiya affairs, the Corps is the city's armed force. It is not a branch of the Akademiya or of the matra, and its pay depends on the Akademiya. Sido told the 4.7 wargame audience that the Akademiya pays straightforwardly but is unlikely to set up a dedicated department to supply the Corps.

## reputation
### STANDARD
Residents treat the Corps as the people to turn to in town. Rukh Shah tells the Traveler to feel free to seek the Corps' help there. Outside the city, some independent Eremites regard its members as the Akademiya's servants.

## history
### LARGE
The Corps began as a band of thirty hired by the sages of the Akademiya and is the oldest of the Eremite brigades. It grew well beyond thirty, but the name remained in memory of the founders (Paimon notes during Gourmet Supremos: Within Our Duties that the Corps has more than thirty people, and the staff there agree). The 3.1 Archon Quest depicts attackers wearing Corps of Thirty uniforms in a cinematic at Pardis Dhyai; canon does not explain this.
### ULTRA +
In the 6.6 Archon Quest Darkness Looming, a Fatui-linked crisis centred on a new Irminsul tree threatens Sumeru City, and the Caravan Ribat staging post is being evacuated toward the desert. The Corps is under orders to be especially cautious with unknown people. The Aranara's intuition about people leads Cyno to conclude there is a traitor among those at Caravan Ribat. He and Nefer, an intelligence broker whom nobody there knows, set a trap. Nefer, posing as a stranded merchant, gets close to the Corps member Qanafiya, who shows her an unmarked gold coin, and Cyno arrests him at the southern gate. Cyno says the spy was planted long ago and gets the location of his contact out of him. The Wanderer then trails the contact, but finds no one at the meeting spot. Afterwards the Wanderer suggests the Fatui never intended to follow through with the mole and had written him off from the start.

## secrets
### ULTRA
The 6.6 Archon Quest shows at least one Corps member apparently being paid (with an unmarked gold coin) as a Fatui-side informant. The quest does not say whether any other members were bribed, and the Corps' leaders are not accused of anything.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the Corps of Thirty is still the working force of Sumeru City, based at the Citadel of Regzar under Rukh Shah.
### STANDARD +
Dehya is still regarded as a friend of the Corps, and Asfand still hopes that she will join it.

@keys: Citadel of Regzar
@version: 3.0

### STANDARD
The Citadel of Regzar is the Corps of Thirty's headquarters and barracks, in the middle of Sumeru City near Treasures Street. It is where Corps members coordinate and meet the Akademiya's liaison Effendi. "Regzar" is a Persian word for "desert". NPCs posted there include Rukh Shah, Asfand, Effendi, the Swallow Sekkura, Udad and the logistics officer Yunus.
