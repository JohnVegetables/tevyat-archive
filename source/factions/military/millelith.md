## summary
### LITE
The Millelith are Liyue's army and guard. They patrol the land, defend settlements against monsters and bandits, and answer to the Liyue Qixing. Their soldiers are famous for their skill with polearms, and their motto promises that evil will not prevail while they stand guard.
### STANDARD +
Their name combines Latin and Greek words for "thousand stone"; in Chinese the force is the Qianyan-jun, the Thousand Rock Army. The modern force is the successor of the Millelith Brigade that served the Geo Archon, Morax, in the Archon War.

## structure
### STANDARD
According to Phonia of the Knights of Favonius, the Millelith concentrate on formations and the disposition of their troops, a style that demands a very high degree of awareness and discipline from every soldier. Squads are led by sergeants and unit leaders, and each unit has both offensive and defensive abilities and can split into smaller formations to suit difficult terrain. After the battle against Beisht, the Qixing reorganised the chain of command and created the General Staff, a department that gathers military advisers so they can share intelligence and divide work, rather than each strategist working alone.
### LARGE +
The Yuheng (Keqing) introduced "scenario-based training", a way of testing a soldier's ability to raise, deploy and command forces from many angles. The assessment is stricter than the tabletop wargames made from it. All of the force's equipment comes from Blackcliff Forge.

## leadership
### STANDARD
The Millelith report to the Liyue Qixing. Keqing, as Yuheng, drove the post-Beisht reform of the chain of command. The sources checked name no single commander-in-chief. Named personnel are mostly low-ranking: Fengyan and Lin (sergeants), Yijian (a unit leader) and Yanbo (a squad leader).

## members
### STANDARD
Ordinary soldiers carry the White Tassel, the standard-issue polearm. Some front-line officers use the heavier halberd, and soldiers have also been seen with other weapons such as the Dark Iron Sword. Fengyan, for example, earned his sergeant's rank through skill at unarmed combat and now teaches martial arts to recruits.
### LARGE +
Su Ding spoke for the General Staff during the 4.7 wargame event, and Turenna and Phonia, two characters in that event, comment on the force's armour and tactics. The Millelith of the distant past favoured the Lithic Spear and Lithic Blade, which modern people find too heavy to lift.

## goals
### STANDARD
Their job is to protect Liyue's people and roads, keep order, and be ready for the day a true threat appears. Fengyan says the present Millelith rarely face the evil forces they are sworn against, but serve with the same rock-solid commitment as their predecessors. He admits that discipline in daily life can look lax.

## methods
### STANDARD
In battle the Millelith lean on the size of their formations to overwhelm an enemy, with offence and defence built into each unit. According to Phonia, studying their tactics and formations still leaves little weakness to exploit. They also work undercover, infiltrating criminal groups to weaken or arrest them.
### LARGE +
The force is developing a new armour of ceramic blocks wedged between layers of hardened fabric, meant to spread the force of projectiles and reduce penetration. Turenna describes this. During the Rite of Descension the Millelith also keep Liyue Harbor safe, with sergeants such as Fengyan patrolling around Yiyan Temple.

## history
### STANDARD
The Millelith began as the Millelith Brigade, raised during the Archon War to protect the people and fight monsters. Item lore says local elders swore oaths to Morax and chose soldiers. Once the war ended the force reported to the Qixing. Many Millelith soldiers died in the Chasm, fighting beside the yaksha Bosacius against the Abyss and the monsters of Khaenri'ah during the cataclysm 500 years ago.
### LARGE +
**The Chasm.** Monsters poured out of the Chasm after an iron meteorite struck it. Morax sent the Millelith to defend the miners and help them flee. The Millelith vanguard, the thaumaturge Boyang and the maddened Bosacius used a spatial catalyst to seal the monsters in with themselves, and few survived. Boyang's brother Rongzhao, an exorcist who helped seal the space from outside, returned home but went insane, which Xiao and Yelan attribute to the yaksha's karma. In the world quest The Millennial Mountains, a stone tablet left by a Millelith soldier who lost the use of his four limbs at the Chasm leaves his armour and equipment for someone fated to claim them, and the miner Wang relates the tale that he became a famed self-taught doctor and set up the memorial to his comrades after obtaining a Vision.

**Modern reforms.** After Beisht was driven off, the Qixing moved to strengthen Liyue's military and created the General Staff.
### ULTRA +
**The 4.7 wargame.** During the limited-time event Mutual Security Enhancing Simulation (2024-06-07 to 2024-06-17) the General Staff hosted a cooperative wargame with the armed forces of neighbouring nations, with Knights of Favonius present. Its aim was to test and share tactics and to build scenario-based training manuals. Su Ding thanked the Traveler for acting as a talented commander.

## relations
### STANDARD
- **Liyue Qixing**: the Millelith's masters, who set policy and reorganised the command.
- **Yaksha**: the Millelith fought beside Bosacius at the Chasm, and the yakshas were once Morax's guardians.
- **Criminal groups**: the force infiltrates and arrests them.
### LARGE +
- **Knights of Favonius**: present at the wargame event, which also drew forces from other nations.
- **Xiao and Wangshu Inn**: the Qixing's own agents, not the Millelith, run Wangshu Inn to support the last yaksha.

## reputation
### LARGE
Sergeant Fengyan recalls how childhood visits to the Rite of Descension shaped his life, and the force's ancient predecessors are remembered for the sacrifice at the Chasm. The achievement for completing the world quest The Millennial Mountains is titled "The Millelith Shall Never Be Moved".

### STANDARD
The motto, as Fengyan gives it, runs: "While rock stays hard, the mountain shall never fail; while the Millelith stands guard, evil shall never prevail." He recites it when asked about the force's history.

## entry: Battle of the Chasm

@keys2: Millelith, Bosacius, Chasm
@version: 2.7
@spoiler: minor
### LARGE
Five hundred years ago the Abyss pushed monsters through the Chasm. The Millelith vanguard fought beside the thaumaturge Boyang and the yaksha Bosacius, who no longer remembered his own name and was later called the Nameless Yaksha. They used the Fantastic Compass to trap the monsters in a sealed space. It needed an adeptus and a mortal to power it, so Bosacius, Boyang and the vanguard stayed inside and died. Survivors outside, such as Boyang's brother Rongzhao, carried the memory.

### LARGE
The General Staff is a department set up after the battle against Beisht. It brings advisers together to pool intelligence and streamline planning. Su Ding explained this during the limited-time event Mutual Security Enhancing Simulation. Scenario-based training tests soldiers on organising, deploying and commanding forces. In 4.7 Su Ding told the Traveler it was a concept Yuheng promoted.
