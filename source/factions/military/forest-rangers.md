## summary
### LITE
The Forest Rangers are Sumeru's civil servants of the rainforest. They help travelers who are lost or in trouble, patrol the woods, watch the animals, and fight the spread of the Withering. Their main outpost is Gandharva Ville, "The City Above the Forest", in Avidya Forest. Higher-ranking members are called Forest Watchers and wear lighter green clothes. Tighnari is the Chief Officer, and Collei is an official ranger.
### STANDARD +
The Rangers are a public service, not a military unit. Shirin, their doctor, calls bows a common choice for hunting and for defence against monsters.

## structure
### LITE
Two grades are seen: ordinary Forest Rangers and the higher-ranking Forest Watchers. Specialist roles include a doctor, a logistics chef and a search-and-rescue dog trainer.
### STANDARD +
The wiki lists Tighnari (Chief Officer), Amir, Ashpazi, Rana and Shirin as Forest Watchers, and Iraj, Izad, Kamran, Nasrin and Collei as ordinary rangers. Gandharva Ville's NPC list calls Shirin a ranger, so her rank is not fully consistent.
### LARGE +
Newcomers train as Trainee Forest Rangers. Collei held that rank when she first appeared, and Amir mentions "a Trainee Forest Ranger" stationed with them. Tighnari occasionally gives training courses to the Rangers, and he jokes that the Akademiya could regard this as illegal "dissemination of information" while daring them to act on it.

## leadership
### LITE
Tighnari is the Chief Officer of the Forest Rangers. He is an Avidya Forest Watcher and a botanist who graduated from Amurta.
### STANDARD +
His character stories say that he first arrived at Gandharva Ville as an ordinary member, fresh from Amurta and parted from the Akademiya. After he arrived the Rangers began to rely on him as a knowledgeable and active researcher, and he eventually got everyone to go back to calling him "Forest Watcher", "Master" or "Mr. Tighnari". He is stern and often lectures travelers on survival and the forest, which is why his Wilderness Survival Skills Lecture is well known.
### LARGE +
Tighnari raised funds for the Rangers by arranging with city flower sellers to collect their unsold flowers for free, in return for a receipt that buyers could exchange for a dried-flower decoration made by the Rangers. Part of the Mora went to the shop owner as a handling fee and the rest to forest upkeep. His character stories describe this as practical, and Collei in particular quickly agreed with it.

## members
### STANDARD
**Tighnari**: Forest Watcher and Chief Officer; mentor of Collei. **Collei**: a trainee when first seen, an official ranger since 6.6. **Rana**: Forest Watcher from Vimara Village. **Amir**: trains the search and rescue dogs. **Ashpazi**: logistics supervisor and "Master Chef". **Shirin**: the Rangers' doctor. **Izad**: liaison to Pardis Dhyai. **Sag**: forest-patrol hound, captain of the dog team.
### LARGE +
Iraj, Kamran and Nasrin are rangers posted at Gandharva Ville. Kamran is blunt about Collei's illness. He says that without the forest's alleviating effect on Eleazar, "someone with Eleazar" would never have been allowed to join, and he is surprised to see her cured. Amir hopes that more kind people will join the Rangers. Izad says the Rangers pick suitable planting areas and distribute seeds to villagers nearby so the rainforest can be a little greener.

## goals
### LITE
Protect the rainforest and the people who pass through it.
### STANDARD +
Concretely the Rangers help travelers in need, suppress Withering Zones, monitor beast populations and preserve the ecosystem of Sumeru's rainforests. They also send regular reports on the state of Avidya Forest to the researchers at Pardis Dhyai, who supply ecological help such as seeds and nutrients in return. Tighnari lists his troubles as pollution in the forest, adventurers who cause problems and rescue dogs that play with his tail, and says he has no larger worries.

## methods
### STANDARD
Rangers patrol on foot, record anomalies in patrol logs, give travelers practical advice, and use trained dogs to search for missing people. Rana tells the Traveler during Into the Woods that when a missing person is found they should be given something easy to digest. They keep the outpost supplied: Ashpazi hunts and cooks as part of his logistics duties, and the Rangers' food at the outpost is a subject Tighnari takes an interest in (he asked for a recipe to put on the Rangers' menu).
### LARGE +
When the Withering struck, rangers faced it directly. In the Aranyaka World Quest series (3.0), Rana protects a trapped child, Iotham, in a Withering Zone and falls severely ill from the exposure. The Aranara guard her until the Traveler obtains Bija to purge the corruption. When she recovers, a Dendro Vision appears at her waist, and she leaves to travel with the Aranara Arana. Furnishing descriptions add that a ranger's provisions backpack holds tools, rations, specimen containers and a tent, but is so heavy that rangers seldom carry it, and that their break rooms are designed for quick departures and sample analysis.

## history
### LARGE
Little is known about the Rangers' origins. According to Rana in Into the Woods, the earliest rangers were the royal guards of Viaghara, the forest king, and walked forests where there were real tigers. Separate lore about Viaghara describes his squire Cinnamon, who taught other forest children to follow the rules of nature and began a line of forest guardians, but the wiki does not say that this is the same organisation.
### ULTRA +
In the 3.1 Archon Quest The Gaze From a Certain God, the Traveler and Tighnari run from the Akademiya's pursuers. Eremite mercenaries led by Tashfin, hired by a client calling himself "The Outcast", ambush the Traveler at the entrance to Gandharva Ville. The 2.8 web event Go Go, Forest Rangers! (limited-time) used the Rangers to introduce Tighnari and Collei before release: players followed forest patrol logs and spotted anomalies in three forest zones. Tighnari states in that event that pollution in the rainforest had become rampant.

## relations
### STANDARD
The Rangers work with Pardis Dhyai's Amurta researchers on conservation. Tighnari's Amurta training links the Rangers to the Akademiya's scholars. They also treat the Aranara kindly: Rana befriended Arana as a child, and the quest series reveals their friendship. The Eremites around Gandharva Ville include a few mercenaries, and a group of Eremites under Tashfin attacked the Traveler there in 3.1, but they were mercenaries hired by an anonymous client, whom Paimon connects with the Doctor, and not part of the Rangers. Kamran says the Bimarstan advises Collei to rest despite her work.
### LARGE +
Gandharva Ville also hosts traveling merchants, researchers and adventurers who stop at the Rangers' outpost, along with a few Eremite mercenaries.

## reputation
### STANDARD
Travelers are told to flag down a Forest Ranger if anything happens. The Rangers are known for patient, practical help, and for Tighnari's lectures. Merchants bow to the diminutive Forest Watcher Tighnari, which amuses the rangers, according to his character stories.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the Forest Rangers still patrol Avidya Forest from Gandharva Ville. Collei is an official ranger, and Tighnari remains their Chief Officer.
### STANDARD +
In the 6.6 Archon Quest Your Past Is Like a Garden, Naphis tells Collei she has finally become a full-fledged ranger, and Cyrus congratulates her on no longer being a trainee. Collei wrote to Sucrose that she is now an official Forest Ranger. Cyno introduced her to the party as the "official" ranger.

Rangers, a few Vanara, researchers, Eremite mercenaries and traveling merchants share the village. Official map text says the remnants of the "kingdom of lost dreams" lie atop the tree. ### LARGE
Rana is a Forest Watcher from Vimara Village. In the Aranyaka World Quest series she guides the Traveler and Paimon through the forest, nearly dies of exposure while saving a child from a Withering Zone, and is healed with Bija. As a child she was befriended by the Aranara Arana, whom she had forgotten. At the end a Dendro Vision appears at her waist and she leaves with Arana. She is the first NPC to display a Vision on her character model.

### STANDARD
Suppressing Withering Zones is one of the Rangers' core duties. They log pollution in the forest, and Rana mentions them in connection with monsters and unusually aggressive wildlife. Exposure to the Withering can make a person severely ill, as happened to Rana. Cure and cause belong to the lore of the Withering, not to this entry.

### STANDARD
Pardis Dhyai is the Amurta research station near Avidya Forest. The Rangers send it regular reports on the forest, and its researchers send seeds, nutrients and other ecological help. Izad is the Ranger who handles this liaison, and he says the Rangers choose planting areas and distribute seeds to nearby villagers.
