## summary
### LITE
The yakshas were a fierce kind of illuminated beast-adepti. After the Archon War, Morax summoned them to wage an unending war on the monsters, plagues and curses born from the hatred of defeated gods. Five were the strongest and became known as the Five Yakshas. The work burdened them with karmic debt, and nearly all died, went mad or vanished. Only Alatus, now called Xiao, remains.
### STANDARD +
In Liyue the yakshas are now mostly forgotten, though statues and a few old books still keep their memory. The wiki spells "Yaksha" with a capital only for the five foremost, but the word is also used for the whole kind.

## structure
### STANDARD
The yakshas were adepti, not a mortal organisation. Many served, but five stood above the rest: Bosacius, Indarias, Bonanus, Menogias and Alatus. Bosacius, the eldest, led them. The wiki lists Pervases as a lesser yaksha who was slain in the Archon War, and the book notes many other yakshas of unknown name who also died or fled.
### LARGE +
Each of the Five Yakshas has a title: Alatus the Golden-Winged King (also the Conqueror of Demons and the Vigilant Yaksha), Bosacius Marshal Vritras, Indarias General Musatas, Bonanus General Chizapus and Menogias General Kapisas. Each also has an element: Alatus Anemo, Bosacius Electro, Indarias Pyro, Bonanus Hydro and Menogias Geo. In battle they wore masks based on Nuo opera masks, whose symbols resemble the Seven's elemental symbols but are cruder.

## leadership
### STANDARD
The yakshas answered to Morax, the Geo Archon, who summoned them. The book says they followed him wherever battle took him. Bosacius, the eldest, was their leader.

## members
### STANDARD
- **Alatus (Xiao)**: the last yaksha. Morax freed him from a cruel god who had enslaved him and gave him the name Xiao for his protection.
- **Bosacius**: leader of the Five, an Electro yaksha with four arms. He left his treasures at Minlin, later went mad and forgot his own name, and died in the Chasm as the "Nameless Yaksha".
- **Indarias, Bonanus, Menogias**: the other three of the Five, all dead.
- **Pervases**: a junior yaksha who died in the Archon War and whose spirit lingers in a crane statue at his run-down temple near Mt. Tianheng.
### LARGE +
Sources checked do not say how Indarias, Bonanus and Menogias died. The book says some yakshas fell to rage, others to fear-driven madness, and many turned on each other.

## goals
### STANDARD
Their duty was to defend Morax's rule and Liyue by purging the monsters and miasmas that grew from defeated gods' lingering hatred. The cinematic gives their oath as restoring order through slaughter and purging evil through battle, to which they gave their lives.

## methods
### STANDARD
They fought without mercy, as the book puts it. They are adeptal arts users: Xiao performs Dream Trawler to cultivate himself and Mortal Lingering was used by Pervases. Xiao's Nuo Dance of Evil Conquering, a rite for purging demons and divine karma, is thought to have been practised by several yakshas, each in their own version.
### LARGE +
Bosacius sealed his treasures with adeptal arts in the region of Minlin, and the puzzles of the quest The Yaksha's Wish reflect this.

## history
### STANDARD
Morax summoned the yakshas to purge the demons born from the bodies and hatred of the gods he had defeated. For about a thousand years they fought. The accumulating karmic debt wore at their souls: some raged, some went mad with fear, some turned on their own, and some were lost to illusion. Three of the five died, a fourth vanished and the rest of the many yakshas died or fled. Alatus alone survived.
### LARGE +
**Bosacius.** Tired of an endless duty, and believing Liyue at peace with only himself and Alatus left, Bosacius sealed his treasures in Minlin and departed. His karmic debt took his sanity and his name. Five hundred years ago, drawn by the bloodshed of the cataclysm, he joined the Millelith and the thaumaturge Boyang at the Chasm and gave his life to seal the Abyssal monsters in. Before he died he became lucid enough to remember his comrades, his duty and his name.

**Xiao.** Xiao nearly succumbed himself, but according to the story teaser video he was saved by the music Venti played on a visit to Liyue. He later wondered whether the Nameless Yaksha was Bosacius, asked Zhongli's leave to investigate, and in the Chasm's depths confirmed it (End of the Line, Interlude Chapter Act II, 2.7).
### ULTRA +
**Pervases.** On the thousandth anniversary of his death, Pervases used Mortal Lingering to see modern Liyue. He sensed Xiao's karmic burden on the Traveler and Paimon, and gave them the censer and lamps they needed for the Dream Trawler ritual in Xiao's Alatus Chapter. Xiao later ate his favourite food, grilled tiger fish, in his memory.

**The Chasm's survivors.** Boyang's younger brother Rongzhao, an ancestor of Yelan, survived the Chasm but went insane from Bosacius' karma and found relief only when passing Wangshu Inn, where Xiao lives.

## relations
### STANDARD
- **Morax and Zhongli**: the yakshas shared a special bond with Morax, who named Xiao and Pervases himself. As Zhongli he prepares medicine for Xiao to ease his karmic pain.
- **Other adepti**: respect the yakshas' strength. Xiao holds high seniority and is called Conqueror of Demons; Ganyu has asked him for training, though Xiao thinks himself unfit to teach her.
- **Liyue Qixing**: its secret agents built Wangshu Inn at Dihua Marsh to support Xiao's duty.
### LARGE +
- **Millelith**: fought beside Bosacius at the Chasm.
- **Hu Tao and Chongyun**: through their lineages they know more of the yakshas than most, and have met Xiao.
- **Starsnatcher (Wang Ping'an)**: his grandfather, a folklorist, researched the yakshas and shared the tales with him.

## reputation
### STANDARD
Long ago mortals revered the yakshas as Liyue's protectors, raising large statues (for example at Luhua Pool) and offering chants and food to ease their pain. Today the statues are battered and the features worn away. The surviving books, such as Yakshas: The Guardian Adepti and the "Ode to Windborne Wraith" chapter of a travel guide to Liyue, are dull and little read, and the Liyue edition omitted the latter. Hu Tao notes that talk of curses and miasmas would cause panic if people knew they were real, so the yakshas remain legend.

## secrets
### ULTRA
Xiao's karmic debt makes monsters near him stronger, and unleashing his full strength can be lethal to bystanders. Few people know he is a yaksha. Most do not know the Qixing run Wangshu Inn as cover for helping him.

## status

## entry: The Five Yakshas

### STANDARD
Bosacius (Electro, Marshal Vritras, eldest and leader), Indarias (Pyro, General Musatas), Bonanus (Hydro, General Chizapus), Menogias (Geo, General Kapisas) and Alatus (Anemo, the Golden-Winged King and Conqueror of Demons) were the strongest yakshas. Only Alatus, now Xiao, lives. The wiki gives the element for Indarias and Menogias from their Japanese voice actors; Bonanus is Hydro by elimination.

## entry: Yakshas: The Guardian Adepti
@keys: Yakshas: The Guardian Adepti, Guardian Adepti, Ode to Windborne Wraith
@keys2: yaksha, Liyue, book
@version: 1.3
### STANDARD
A volume from the "Hidden Wonders and Folklore of Liyue" series, written in archaic, wordy prose by a Sumeru scholar. It says Rex Lapis summoned the yakshas to purge demons, that the five strongest were Bosacius, Indarias, Bonanus, Menogias and Alatus, and that after a thousand years only Alatus survived. It adds that at the Lantern Rite people see a glow at Guyun Stone Forest and hear a flute among the marsh reeds of Dihua Marsh, which they take as the Vigilant Yaksha at work. Starsnatcher buys it during the Alatus Chapter and Paimon and the Traveler read it.

## entry: Karmic debt
@keys: karmic debt, karma, karmic disease
@keys2: yaksha, Xiao, Liyue
### STANDARD
In this setting karma builds up in those who slay the lingering remnants of fallen gods. It wears away at the soul, causing rage, madness and pain, and it taints the surroundings: Xiao's karma can strengthen nearby monsters and stain mortals who stay near him. Xiao and Yelan attribute the madness of Rongzhao, who fought beside Bosacius without a Vision, to that karma, reasoning that without a Vision he could not resist it for long. Hu Tao says part of the yakshas' karma once became a plague in Liyue, which the founders of Wangsheng Funeral Parlor stopped by purifying the air and burning the dead.

## entry: Nuo Dance of Evil Conquering
@keys: Nuo Dance, Nuo Dance of Evil Conquering, Bane of All Evil
@keys2: Xiao, yaksha
### STANDARD
A rite for purging demons and divine karma. The wiki notes the English name was rewritten to match its use as a title. Liyue's rulers, who know Xiao's truth, call his millennia of work the "Bane of All Evil". It is not known how many other yakshas used the same technique.

## entry: Wangshu Inn and the yakshas
@keys: Wangshu Inn
@keys2: Xiao, yaksha, Qixing
### STANDARD
The Liyue Qixing's secret agents built Wangshu Inn at Dihua Marsh to give Xiao a place to rest and to help him purge plagues. Verr Goldet and Huai'an run it and cover for the damage his night work can cause. The wiki says it has stood at least since the years after the cataclysm, about 500 years ago.

## entry: Xiao and the other yakshas
@keys: Xiao, Alatus
@keys2: yaksha, Yakshas
### STANDARD
Xiao's character story and voice-overs describe a reserved warrior who avoids mortals for their sake and does not want sympathy, believing his fallen comrades would find tears a stain on their memory. He is fond of his fellow yakshas. When the Traveler met Pervases, he quickly grew nostalgic. Xiao's own entry carries his full history.
