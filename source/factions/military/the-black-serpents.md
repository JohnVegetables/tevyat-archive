## summary
### LITE
The Black Serpents are what is left of Khaenri'ah's Royal Guard, the knights who served under Captain Dainsleif before the nation fell. The curse on Khaenri'ah wore away their minds and memories. The Adventurer Handbook says only a will to "fight for something, someone, and some matter" remains. Depending on how worn they are, they are called Shadowy Husks or Black Serpent Knights. Outside quests they appear as elite enemies in the Chasm's underground mines and in the Spiral Abyss.
### STANDARD +
Canon files them under the Abyss, but they are not the Abyss Order, and the two meet in the Chasm. Many of them still guard what they once swore to protect.

## structure
### STANDARD
No ranks or chain of command survive among the cursed. In life they were the Royal Guard under the Eclipse Dynasty, with Dainsleif as captain and bearer of the title "Twilight Sword". The wiki dates his promotion to about the time Irmin made the Traveler's sibling a vessel for Abyssal power. Today the Handbook groups them into two kinds. Shadowy Husks come as archers (Defender), lancers (Line Breaker) and banner bearers (Standard Bearer). Black Serpent Knights come as dual-axe fighters (Rockbreaker Ax) and greatsword fighters (Windcutter). Individual enemies in the Chasm carry names and epithets, such as Skeld, Augur of the Mirrors, and Serkir, Scribe of Swords.
### LARGE +
The wiki notes that most of these Chasm names borrow from the 1999 film The 13th Warrior, so they are labels, not recorded Khaenri'ahn identities.

## members
### STANDARD
Few members have histories. Halfdan, an elite Royal Guard, led the Chasm band. Irizar and Zephaniah fought beside the Children of Echoes in Natlan. Ynghildr was a Schwanenritter lost in Sumeru, not a Royal Guard, and Irizar and Zephaniah are called only Khaenri'ahn soldiers. Their sub-entries carry the detail.
### LARGE +
In the Chasm, knights wear the same silent armour yet speak in broken lines when they spot the Traveler. They call the Traveler an outlander, an envoy of the heavens or a companion of "fate", echoing Khaenri'ah's old grievance against the sky.

## goals
### STANDARD
Their purpose survives only as fragments. Dainsleif's order to protect the people of Khaenri'ah at all costs still guides Halfdan's band, and those people are now hilichurls, whom the knights shelter. Elsewhere the cursed seem to defend a place or duty, such as the control module of the Ruin Golem in Devantaka Mountain.

## methods
### STANDARD
In battle they are durable and use slow elemental attacks. When a player's shield fully absorbs one of their hits, they buff themselves or nearby allies at the cost of some of their own health. This is a gameplay note, not lore.

## history
### STANDARD
**Before the fall.** The Royal Guard served the Eclipse Dynasty. Dainsleif led them as the Abyssal power that destroyed Khaenri'ah rose.

**The cataclysm.** When Khaenri'ah fell about 500 years ago, its people were cursed, and the Guard's members faded into armoured shades that outlived their memories.
### LARGE +
**The Chasm.** In the Archon Quest Requiem of the Echoing Depths (2.6), Dainsleif and the Traveler enter the Chasm's mines. Dainsleif is pursuing an Abyss Herald and the Traveler is investigating hilichurl movements. A band of knights attacks them without recognising Dainsleif, but Halfdan does and orders the others to withdraw. The Abyss Order's amplification device then torments the knights and hilichurls; Halfdan shields others from its light at the cost of his life while the Traveler destroys its charging devices. The wiki says Dainsleif comforts his spirit with the untrue claim that Khaenri'ah still stands.

**Natlan.** During the cataclysm, Irizar and Zephaniah helped the Children of Echoes funnel Abyssal forces into a cavern in the Sulfurous Veins. They held the line while Elder Traore sealed it with the Shadow Pins, and their souls were trapped in the fight. In the Natlan world quest Peace to the Slumbering the Traveler frees them.

**Sumeru.** Ynghildr of the Schwanenritter went missing fighting the "Onslaught of Dark Beasts" after the order's last Ruin Golem reached Devantaka Mountain. In Vimana Agama: Jazari's Chapter the Traveler finds her turned into a cursed knight guarding the Ruin Golem.
### ULTRA +
In the Chasm, hilichurls had been drifting into the underground depths, seemingly aware of a fountain that could lift part of the curse. The Abyss Order knew of this and tried to use the fountain to restore the cursed to consciousness, though it judged the chances low. The result was only pain. Dainsleif is himself a cursed Khaenri'ahn who has not eroded in the same way, and the sources checked do not explain the difference.

## relations
### STANDARD
Dainsleif is their former captain and the one person who can still reach them. The Abyss Order's amplification device, meant to lift the curse, only caused them agony. Hilichurls, former Khaenri'ahn civilians, are the people they protect. The Children of Echoes remember Irizar and Zephaniah first as villains and then as heroes.
### LARGE +
The Traveler fights them as hostile enemies, but the quests show them as victims. When Jazari hears the Traveler met Ynghildr, he dismisses the idea that a Khaenri'ahn could have lived so long, which shows how little ordinary people know of the curse.

## reputation
### STANDARD
Most of Teyvat knows them only as monstrous armoured enemies. The Children of Echoes first told a tale of two evil Khaenri'ahn soldiers, which Irizar and Zephaniah had themselves requested so the tribe would stay wary of the Abyss. Titu reversed that after learning the truth.

## secrets
### LARGE
That these monsters were once loyal knights is known to Dainsleif, the Traveler and those who took part in the quests. Dainsleif's white lie to Halfdan is one of the few kindnesses the knights receive.

## status
### STANDARD
As of 7.1 the Black Serpents survive only as cursed enemies. The one organised band seen, in the Chasm, lost its leader Halfdan in the Archon Quest. Nothing new was found for 6.x or 7.0-7.1.

## entry: Halfdan and the Chasm Knights
@keys: Halfdan, Black Serpent Knights' Glory, Requiem of the Echoing Depths
@version: 2.6
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Halfdan was an elite of the Royal Guard who kept Dainsleif's final order to protect Khaenri'ah's people. Cursed and eroded, he still guarded dying hilichurls entering the Chasm, recognised Dainsleif and shielded others from the Abyss Order's device at the cost of his life. His spirit passed on after Dainsleif told him Khaenri'ah still stood.

## entry: Irizar and Zephaniah
@keys: Irizar, Zephaniah, Peace to the Slumbering
@version: 5.0
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Two Khaenri'ahn soldiers who held the line for the Children of Echoes in the Sulfurous Veins during the cataclysm. Their souls were bound to an endless fight against the Abyss when the Shadow Pins sealed the cavern. They asked the tribe to remember them as villains, and the Traveler later set them free.

## entry: Ynghildr
@keys: Ynghildr
@version: 3.0
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
A Schwanenritter who went missing fighting the "Onslaught of Dark Beasts" in Sumeru. Only her signet ring and one other item were recovered, and Anfortas believed the order had fought to the last. In truth she became a cursed knight and guarded the Ruin Golem's control module until the Traveler defeated her.
