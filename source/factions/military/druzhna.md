## summary
### LITE
The Druzhna is Snezhnaya's personal guard of the Tsaritsa. It answers only to her, and most of its members are Vila, winged fae of the Snowland Fae. Besides protecting her, it is responsible for containing Shadow Realm breaches. Its headquarters is Druzhna HQ in the Everfrozen Earth area, and its Commander-in-chief is Vesna Strivozha.
### STANDARD +
The word is Russian: a druzhina was a warrior band that served a medieval prince. In Snezhnaya the name belongs to a force older than the Tsaritsa's own reign, founded by the Belyi Tsar. It is separate from the Fatui, which is the army and government apparatus she built, and the two are described as allies.

## leadership
### LITE
Vesna Strivozha, a Vila of House Strivozha, is Commander-in-chief. Her profile calls her the Tsaritsa's "keen blade", and she and the head housemaid Danica are both called the Tsaritsa's right hands and are often at her side.
### STANDARD +
Vesna joined as a young recruit, known at first as "that little lass from House Strivozha". Her profile says that by the time she took command, "Vesna" shone so brightly that people forgot the surname. She was trained in her youth by Capitano. In her character story "Druzhna Recruit's Badge", an audience with the Tsaritsa ended with Vesna receiving a Druzhna recruit's badge and Capitano, not Pulcinella, as her mentor. Capitano told her to become a disciple he could be proud of and said he would wait for the day she stood at the pinnacle of the Druzhna. Following a story of her mother's, she left that badge high on the roof of the family estate, and dug it out again after the banquet celebrating her appointment as commander-in-chief.

The first Commander was Grand Duke Filipov Vseslavyevich, the shapeshifter and the Belyi Tsar's closest comrade, who vanished during the Cataclysm.
### LARGE +
Other named members are Yarovaya, a Podporuchik who runs the Shadow Realm work at Druzhna HQ, and Akvilina, who introduces herself as a Privy Councillor (3rd Class) and Officer of the Druzhna Guards. Both are Vila.

## structure
### STANDARD
The Druzhna is a single armed force with a Commander-in-chief at its head and, in canon, no other formal structure shown. Ranks glimpsed include Commander-in-chief, officer of the guards and Podporuchik, a junior officer rank. Its base is Druzhna HQ, a subarea of Everfrozen Earth in Snezhnaya. It sits under Zapolyarny Palace, the Tsaritsa's seat, and takes its orders from her alone.
### LARGE +
Recruits are drawn mainly from the Vila. During the recruitment drive that began in 7.0 the Druzhna also took on volunteers from the public for the dangerous Shadow Realm work. Yarovaya admits that ordinarily the Druzhna itself should be completing such missions, and an applicant says the entry standards this time are much stricter than usual.

## members
### STANDARD
Members are overwhelmingly Vila: winged fae with pointed, green-tipped ears and insect-like wings. Their wings let them fly briefly, which suits scouting and long-range raids. Vesna is the only playable member. Named non-playable members are Yarovaya and Akvilina at Snezhnograd. Soldiers of the Druzhna also appear in Vesna's stories.
### LARGE +
Akvilina speaks of herself as a blade "ever prepared to strike and fall" in the Tsaritsa's service, and her lines on the Veche and on rogue Vila show the force's fierce loyalty.

## goals
### STANDARD
Protect the Tsaritsa and keep peace across the lands she surveys. Yarovaya states the duty plainly: the Druzhna exists so that "all lands the Tsaritsa surveys are at peace". Its other standing task is dealing with Shadow Realms, which it treats as the opposite of that peace.

## methods
### STANDARD
For Shadow Realms, the Royal Energy Commission detects breaches and reports them, and the Druzhna acts on each report. According to Yarovaya, it organises search groups; if the group finds the Realm's energy core, it neutralises it, and if not, it seals the Realm off. Most Shadow Realms vanish by themselves over time, so the main danger is people entering one by mistake.
### LARGE +
From 7.0 the Druzhna has run the Special Recruitment Project, which Vesna says the Tsaritsa herself arranged, with weekly training missions in which volunteers helped clear Shadow Realms for rewards. The map text describes Yarovaya as a high-ranking officer who verifies sightings, processes intelligence from the Commission, sets up containment and then goes in to clear the Realm.

## history
### STANDARD
**Origins.** The Belyi Tsar founded the Druzhna and led it as his personal guard. Its ranks were drawn from the Vila, who were among the first fae to follow him when he united the Snowland Fae. The Vila had earlier been vassals of King Miscislav of the Volkodlak, paying him tribute. After their first Grand Duchess, Vaselisa Strivozha, was beaten by the Tsar, they switched sides. Grand Duke Filipov Vseslavyevich was its first Commander.

**Under the Tsaritsa.** After the Belyi Tsar died in the Cataclysm and Anastasya became Cryo Archon, some Vila rebelled over her policy toward the fae nobility. These revolts were put down, which split the Vila. A history book says that when she moved against the fae princes early in her reign, she did not call up the Druzhna but built the Fatui instead. She later took the Druzhna as her own personal guard, as the loading-screen text says it is today.
### LARGE +
**Shadow Realms and recent years.** The Druzhna handles the Shadow Realm breaches reported by the Energy Commission. In Chapter VII (7.0 to 7.1), the Commander Vesna was beside the Tsaritsa when she dealt with a storm-causing monster in the east, and was shown greeting the crowd at the Armory Palace after Ronova had left it. Shortly afterwards the Druzhna's Special Recruitment Project signed up the Traveler, Vesna noticed the name on the roster, and she later met the Traveler in person. Her own part in that story, including her power of Life, belongs to her character entry.
### ULTRA +
**Filipov.** General History of Snezhnaya presents Filipov as the Belyi Tsar's closest friend, a cunning strategist who could take many forms, and first Commander of the Druzhna. A character note on him says his one defeat by the Abyss came when he vanished in the Cataclysm. His human wife, Nepla Korolevitsna, is tied to the legend of how he lost faith in truth, since a later fraud used her phantom against him.

**Dispute over uniforms.** Akvilina says the Vila bandits who fight the Traveler on the tundra are not Druzhna, yet the winged "Winged Soarer Knight" enemies wear what looks like the Druzhna uniform. Canon does not explain this.

## relations
### STANDARD
- **The Tsaritsa**: sole master. Loading-screen text says the Druzhna answers only to her.
- **Fatui**: described as allies. The Tsaritsa created the Fatui rather than using the Druzhna to subdue the fae princes, so the two forces have different roots.
- **Vila**: the people from whom the Druzhna is mostly drawn. Vila Druzhna such as Akvilina treat rogue Vila bandits as traitors who shame their people.
### LARGE +
- **Royal Energy Commission**: reports Shadow Realms to the Druzhna and is described as more mobile, since its staff travel to maintain Kresnik's Torches. Yarovaya finds it odd that the Commission sometimes seems to know more about the Realms than it says.
- **House Strivozha**: the Vila house that Vesna comes from. According to the wiki, the house submitted to the Belyi Tsar in the era of the unification.
- **Snezhnograd Veche**: Akvilina says the Veche is corrupt and that she would uncover whether its members are truly loyal to the Tsaritsa. This is her opinion, not official Druzhna policy.
- **Rokot**: in the world quest A Special Selection, the Traveler can treat the recruitment drive as a job for the Rokot, the civil group; the Druzhna side never mentions the Rokot.

## reputation
### ULTRA
Loyalists call the force a blade that will strike and fall for the Tsaritsa. Its Vila members say they owe the Belyi Tsar thanks for the amnesty that let them live alongside humans, and credit it for their present wealth. The rogue Vila of the tundra, loyal to old noble privileges, are in turn hated and called traitors. Applicants see the special recruitment as well paid but hard to get into: one mentions Kristall as a reward and says the standards are much stricter than usual.

## status
@version: 7.1
### LITE
As of 7.1 the Druzhna is active under Commander Vesna. It is short on hands for Shadow Realm work and is running the Special Recruitment Project to bring in volunteers.
### STANDARD +
Vesna and Danica returned to Snezhnograd with the Tsaritsa after dealing with the eastern monster. No change to the Druzhna's structure or command has been shown in 7.0 to 7.1. Nothing after 7.1 is covered.

## entry: Vesna Strivozha as Commander
@keys: Vesna, Vesna Strivozha, Commander of the Druzhna
@version: 7.1
### STANDARD
Vesna is the Druzhna's Commander-in-chief and its only playable face. She is a Vila of House Strivozha, trained by Capitano, who holds a fragment of the Ruler of Life's authority. Her profile calls the Druzhna the blade the Tsaritsa wields and Vesna its keenest edge. For her personality, voice and personal history, see her own entry; this entry covers only her role in the force.

## entry: Druzhna HQ
@keys: Druzhna HQ, Druzhna headquarters
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
Druzhna HQ is the force's base in the Everfrozen Earth area of Snezhnograd. Named NPCs on the wiki map include Malinina, Musina, Rozhdestvensky, Shakhovskaya and Yarovaya, the last the "Druzhna" Podporuchik who runs the Shadow Realm cleanup. In the world quest A Special Selection (7.0) the Traveler finds applicants lining up to be tested there.

## entry: Special Recruitment Project
@keys: Special Recruitment Project, A Special Selection, Looming Shadows
@keys2: Druzhna, Yarovaya, Shadow Realm
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
After the world quest A Special Selection, Yarovaya offers weekly training missions in which the Traveler enters set Shadow Realms and clears them. The project is a manpower measure: the force is stretched, so it takes volunteers who pass strict shooting tests. This is a gameplay system as well as a story beat, and in the story the Traveler joins as a recruit. The map description says the work might even earn the favour of the one on Zapolyarny Palace's throne.

## entry: Filipov Vseslavyevich
@keys: Filipov, Filipov Vseslavyevich
@version: 6.3
### LARGE
Filipov was a Snowland Fae Grand Duke, called the progenitor of the shapeshifters, and the Belyi Tsar's Synaimos, a fae word for a comrade-in-arms. General History of Snezhnaya tells how he challenged the young Monomakh and lost, changing shape again and again, then swore to him. He became the first Commander of the Druzhna. According to the book he lost three times in his long life: to Monomakh, to his wife Nepla Korolevitsna, and to the Abyss during the Cataclysm.

## entry: Vila and the Belyi Tsar
@keys: Vila, Vaselisa Strivozha, Burner of Cities
@keys2: Druzhna, Belyi Tsar, Snezhnaya
@version: 7.0
### LARGE
The Vila were vassals of Miscislav, king of the Volkodlak, until the Belyi Tsar's campaign. Their first Grand Duchess, Vaselisa Strivozha, was defeated by the Tsar in single combat, and the Vila submitted willingly to the Pale Star Court. He granted them amnesty and let them live with humans, which Druzhna Vila such as Akvilina remember with gratitude. After he died, some Vila nobles' descendants staged small rebellions against the Tsaritsa. A raider called the Burner of Cities rode the railway in one of them. The revolts were crushed but split the Vila people. The Druzhna's Vila see the rebel remnants as a lasting disgrace.
