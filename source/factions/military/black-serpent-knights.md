## summary
### LITE
Black Serpent Knights are one of the two forms of the cursed Royal Guard of Khaenri'ah, alongside the Shadowy Husks. They fight with greatswords (Windcutter) or dual axes (Rockbreaker Ax). Canon describes them as once-honoured knights of the realm whose reason and memory were stripped away by the curse. For the wider group, see the Black Serpents.
### STANDARD +
Halfdan, Irizar, Zephaniah and Ynghildr are the named Knights with stories.

## structure
### STANDARD
The Adventurer Handbook says members are called Husks or Knights depending on how much wear they have undergone, so the Knights are not a separate organisation. The two Knight types are Windcutter (greatsword) and Rockbreaker Ax (dual axes). In the Chasm, Halfdan led a band of them. Individuals carry epithets, such as Buliwyf, Guardian of the Desolation, a Windcutter in the Stony Halls.

## members
### STANDARD
Halfdan, an elite Royal Guard, appears as a Windcutter. Irizar appears as a Rockbreaker Ax and Zephaniah as a Windcutter. Ynghildr, a Schwanenritter titled "Sword of a Lost Homeland", is fought as a Windcutter inside the Ruin Golem in Vimana Agama: Jazari's Chapter.
### LARGE +
The named Windcutters in the Chasm speak in broken phrases: Buliwyf calls intruders craven trespassers and speaks of "the majestic one's dark curse", Edgetho calls the Traveler a companion of the tyrant "fate", and Rethel speaks of death, injustice and the void.

## goals
### STANDARD
Halfdan's band kept Dainsleif's order to protect Khaenri'ah's people, now hilichurls. Irizar and Zephaniah guarded the Children of Echoes by holding back the Abyss. Others guard places, like the Ruin Golem's control module.

## history
### STANDARD
In the Chasm (2.6, Requiem of the Echoing Depths), Halfdan's band (fought in the quest as Shadowy Husks, though the quest steps call them Black Serpent Knights) first attacks Dainsleif and the Traveler, then stands down at his order. He later gives his life shielding others from the light of an Abyss Order amplification device while the Traveler shuts it down. In Natlan (5.0), Irizar and Zephaniah's long battle ends when the Traveler breaks the hold of the Shadow Pins.
### LARGE +
Irizar and Zephaniah asked the tribe to remember them as villains so the Abyss would stay feared. After the Traveler cleansed the corruption under the Shadow Pins, the storyteller Titu chose to tell of their courage instead.
### ULTRA +
Halfdan's band is the only sizeable group shown acting together. After he ordered them to stand down, the wiki says he sent them away to various areas of the Chasm, which may explain why named Knights and Husks guard separate areas such as the Glowing Narrows and the Underground Waterway. The quest does not confirm that each named enemy belonged to Halfdan's band.

## relations
### STANDARD
Dainsleif is their former captain. The Children of Echoes, the hilichurls and the Schwanenritter all cross paths with them. The Abyss Order's amplification device, meant to lift the curse, only tormented them.

## status
### STANDARD
As of 7.1 the Knights survive only as enemies. No new story involvement was found in 6.x or 7.0-7.1.

## entry: Rockbreaker Ax and Windcutter
@keys: Rockbreaker Ax, Windcutter

@version: 2.6
### STANDARD
The Windcutter (added 2.6) wields a greatsword and, when a shield fully absorbs its hit, gains attack and can blink away from the next blow. The Rockbreaker Ax (added 3.5) uses dual axes, enters a Geo-infused rampage and can shield nearby Husks; outside quests it appears only in the Spiral Abyss, Imaginarium Theater and some events. These are gameplay behaviours of the enemy types. Their Archive entries call both "a guard of some standing in the court" using the sword art "Truthseeker", and the Windcutter's adds that the Twilight Sword was once one of their number.
