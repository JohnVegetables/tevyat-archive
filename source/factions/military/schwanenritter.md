## summary
### LITE
The Schwanenritter ("Swan Knights" in German) were an order of Khaenri'ahn knights who fought the Abyssal monsters when the cataclysm struck Khaenri'ah about 500 years ago. Led by Knight Marshal Anfortas, they escorted able-bodied survivors up into Sumeru and held the line with machines called Ruin Golems. Their known members all fell, one by one, in a foreign land. Nagarjuna's scholars, the future Order of Skeptics, honoured them as the "heroes of Dahri".
### STANDARD +
Almost everything known about them comes from texts left in Sumeru's ruins, not from living witnesses. Their story is told through logs, letters and epitaphs.

## structure
### STANDARD
Canon gives only fragments of the order's organisation. Its head was the Knight Marshal, and below him were ranks such as Knight Commander and Garrison Commander, whose relative order is unknown. Members carried epithets, such as "Sentinel of the Golden Hall", "Beard-Father" and "Damsel of the Dale". The order's machines were called Mechanical Wardens. Its size, founding and place in the Khaenri'ahn state are not stated.

## leadership
### LITE
Knight Marshal Anfortas led the order and, when King Irmin was left "indisposed" by the crisis, proclaimed himself Marshal Regent and led the Regnum Concilium Ultimum, the Final Kingdom Council.
### STANDARD +
Under Anfortas, Hadura served as a Garrison Commander and later as a Knight Commander, and received the epithet "Sentinel of the Golden Hall" for a successful operation that stalled the beasts. Anfortas lost his left eye in the fight that ended with Hadura's execution. A coded message left on Ruin Guards in Dharma Forest, attributed to him, says the Schwanenritters "fought to the last one".

## members
### STANDARD
Four named members are known:
- **Anfortas**: Knight Marshal and Marshal Regent, the last of them standing.
- **Hadura**: "Sentinel of the Golden Hall", a Knight Commander. Later called a traitor, executed and given a knight's funeral anyway. Klingsor of the Order of Skeptics later called Hadura his ancestor.
- **Hildric**: "Beard-Father". Died of multiple-organ failure in a foreign land.
- **Ynghildr**: "Damsel of the Dale". Went missing in battle against the dark beasts, leaving only her signet ring and a second personal item.
### LARGE +
Ynghildr is also titled "Sword of a Lost Homeland" in the quest text of Vimana Agama: Jazari's Chapter, where she is the Shadowy Husk guarding the control module of the fallen Valley of Dahri golem. This later reading of her fate is not something the order's records know: they list her only as missing.

## goals
### STANDARD
First, to hold back the dark beasts that overran Khaenri'ah, and second, to get the survivors who could still fight or work out of the dying kingdom and onto the surface. In Sumeru the aim narrowed to fighting the cataclysm to the end. The Order of Skeptics later took up that purpose.

## methods
### STANDARD
The knights fought with Mechanical Wardens, huge Ruin Golems of an earlier design than the common Ruin Guards. Hadura also ordered a proving-ground workshop to activate all its self-defence systems before the evacuation went on, because the workshop was unsuitable for the Wardens' large-scale attacks. In Sumeru the order salvaged parts from broken golems to keep weapons working, for want of spares.
### LARGE +
One golem was lost in the Desert of Hadramaveth. A second, at the Valley of Dahri, suffered a design defect that made it unstable, and its smart-boot system finally failed. The knights stripped it for parts and left it to await retrieval. Those parts kept the last golem running at Devantaka Mountain. The work log that records these decisions reads like an engineer's logbook, matter-of-fact about shortages and trade-offs.

## history
### STANDARD
**Cataclysm.** Khaenri'ah's use of knowledge from beyond the skies, understood to be forbidden knowledge, ended in an onslaught of dark beasts. While Irmin was unfit to rule, Anfortas took the regency, and the order chose to leave the kingdom.

**Evacuation.** The knights led able-bodied survivors through the Girdle of the Sands and east to the Dharma Forest, using the Ruin Golems against the monsters. Once on the surface, some refugees were afflicted by a strange disease that turned them into monsters, and some of those not yet affected chose to go back underground.

**Last stand.** In Dharma Forest the knights fought beside Nagarjuna's Akademiya darshan, who acknowledged them as the "heroes of Dahri" despite the bad name Khaenri'ahns had earned for the cataclysm.
### LARGE +
**Order of events at the golems.** The Desert of Hadramaveth golem was lost first, where an unnamed Schwanenritter died of what was probably disease. The Valley of Dahri golem was abandoned next, and the Devantaka Mountain golem was the last. Hildric died of organ failure. Ynghildr vanished in battle. That left Hadura and Anfortas.

**The betrayal.** Hadura then betrayed the order and wrecked a machine beyond repair. Anfortas killed them in the ensuing fight and lost an eye, but still granted a knight's funeral because they were the last of his comrades. He carried on alone until his presumed death.
### ULTRA +
**Aftermath.** The Order of Skeptics followed the knights' path back to the Girdle of the Sands to see the fight against the cataclysm through, and with Dainsleif and the Pari they helped quell the calamity by purifying the Harvisptokhm. No Schwanenritter went with them. Hundreds of years later, Klingsor, a Skeptic, resented how Khaenri'ahns were treated, pointed to Hadura's efforts in subduing the monsters and came to believe Nagarjuna had hidden the truth. He eventually embraced the Abyss and became an Abyss Herald, which led to the events of the Khvarena of Good and Evil quest.

## relations
### STANDARD
- **Khaenri'ah's crown**: the order served a kingdom whose king, Irmin, was incapacitated at the crisis. Anfortas replaced him at the council.
- **Order of Skeptics**: Nagarjuna's darshan fought beside the knights and later continued their cause. Hadura's relatives presumably followed Nagarjuna and joined them.
- **Abyss**: the enemy throughout. The later Abyss Order is a separate story.
### LARGE +
- **Alberich Clan**: Anfortas is presumed to belong to the clan of non-royal regents.
- **Sumeru Akademiya**: an Akademiya investigation team's interim report, left at the Zandik's Legacy site, records the golems' salvaged parts. Zandik's own note says the golem parts have a unique, ingenious style unlike anything made under the Archons, and that he meant to keep the secret from his team.

## reputation
### LARGE
Among Nagarjuna's followers the knights were remembered as heroes. Khaenri'ahns in general were blamed for the cataclysm, and Klingsor's grievance shows how that stigma could harden into doubt about the Order of Skeptics' own history.

## secrets
### ULTRA
Hadura's motive for betrayal and the machine they destroyed are unrecorded. Anfortas' fate is likewise unrecorded.

## status
@version: 7.1
### LITE
The order no longer exists. Its last known member, Anfortas, is presumed dead.
### STANDARD +
Nothing about the Schwanenritter was found in 6.x or 7.0-7.1 content. Their ruins and logs in Sumeru remain the only places their story is told.

## entry: Anfortas, Knight Marshal
@keys: Anfortas, Knight Marshal Anfortas, Marshal Regent
@keys2: Schwanenritter, Khaenri'ah, Khaenriah, cataclysm
### STANDARD
Anfortas was the Knight Marshal of the Schwanenritter. When King Irmin became "indisposed" in the cataclysm, he declared himself Marshal Regent and led the Final Kingdom Council. He led the survivors into Sumeru and fought to the end, losing his left eye to Hadura. Like Irmin, he is one-eyed. See his own entry for his biography.

## entry: Hadura, Sentinel of the Golden Hall
@keys: Hadura, Sentinel of the Golden Hall
@keys2: Schwanenritter, Anfortas, Khaenri'ah, Khaenriah
### STANDARD
Hadura began as a Garrison Commander who ordered defensive systems armed so the evacuation could go on. As a Knight Commander they were given the epithet "Sentinel of the Golden Hall" after leading a successful operation against the beasts. Later they betrayed the order and destroyed a machine, and Anfortas executed them, losing an eye. Hadura was the last of Anfortas' comrades, so a proper knight's funeral was granted. Klingsor, much later, called Hadura his ancestor. Canon does not say why they betrayed the order. Their gender is not stated here; the wiki uses "they".

## entry: Hildric and Ynghildr
@keys: Hildric, Ynghildr, Beard-Father, Damsel of the Dale, Sword of a Lost Homeland
@version: 3.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Hildric, "Beard-Father", died of multiple-organ failure in a foreign land, and the Schwanenritter chronicle asks that he rest in peace there. Ynghildr, "Damsel of the Dale", went missing in a battle with the dark beasts. Only her signet ring and one other item were recovered, and a knight's funeral was arranged. Later quest text reveals she was not simply lost: she became a Shadowy Husk and kept guard over the control module of the Valley of Dahri golem until the Traveler defeated her in Vimana Agama: Jazari's Chapter.

## entry: Ruin Golems and Mechanical Wardens
@keys: Mechanical Wardens, Ruin Golem, Ruin Golems
@keys2: Schwanenritter, Khaenri'ah, Sumeru, Khaenriah
### STANDARD
Three huge Ruin Golems stand in Sumeru: in the Desert of Hadramaveth, the Valley of Dahri and on Devantaka Mountain. The wiki identifies them as the Schwanenritter's Mechanical Wardens. In Vimana Agama, Jazari notes that the golem's Amiti device carries Khaenri'ahn patterns of an earlier type than those on common Ruin Guards. The golems' work logs are the main source for the order's last days.

## entry: Final days of the Schwanenritter
@keys: We Schwanenritters have fought to the last one, Cryptic Message in Sumeru, In the Name of Anfortas
### LARGE
Fragments of a coded message can be found on Ruin Guards around Dharma Forest. When all five parts are deciphered, they read: "We Schwanenritters have fought to the last one." It is the clearest statement of the order's end. Reading all five parts earns the achievement In the Name of Anfortas, whose description sends the player to the place where the heroes met their end.
