## summary
### LITE
The Shogun's Army is the military of the Raiden Shogun, Ei, and of the Inazuma Shogunate. It keeps peace and order across Inazuma and is divided among the three bodies of the Tri-Commission. The Tenryou Commission, led by the Kujou Clan, provides the main fighting force. The Kanjou Commission handles border security, and the Yashiro Commission handles protection of shrines, festivals and public events. Some members serve the Shogun directly. Its soldiers are also called Shogunate Samurai, and their ranks are modelled on those of the historical Tokugawa shogunate.
### STANDARD +
Players first meet the army enforcing the Vision Hunt Decree and the Sakoku Decree in Chapter II, and fighting the Watatsumi Army around the Kujou Encampment in Kannazuka. After the Traveler and their allies brought the Vision Hunt Decree to an end, the army stopped seizing Visions, and a ceasefire now stands between it and the Watatsumi Army.

## structure
### STANDARD
Soldiers are assigned to a commission, and each commission gives them a different job.
- **Tenryou Commission**: the executive arm of the Shogun and the army's main fighting force. It handles security and military matters and is led by the Kujou Clan.
- **Kanjou Commission**: guards Inazuma's borders, finances and customs. Its soldiers, under the Hiiragi Clan based in Ritou, are the ones who stop travellers at the port.
- **Yashiro Commission**: protects shrines, festivals and public events. It is led by the Kamisato Clan.

A few serve the Shogun directly, outside any commission: Hatamoto and the Okuzumeshuu.
### LARGE +
The wiki lists these ranks, which it says mirror the Tokugawa shogunate's military:
- **Ashigaru** are foot soldiers. Kanjou Ashigaru block the way out of Ritou.
- **Doushin** are patrol officers who do police and general-affairs work. Many patrol Inazuma City.
- **Yoriki** are middle-ranking officers who command them. They patrol the city and appear on the battlefield at the Kujou Encampment.
- **Hatamoto** are high-ranking samurai in direct service to the Shogun. The wiki names two, Naoe Hisamasa and Shima Masaoki, both stationed at Inazuma City to guard Tenshukaku.
- **Okuzumeshuu** are the bodyguards of the Almighty Shogun and take orders only from her. The wiki lists Takeshi and Saizou.
- **General** is the highest rank shown. Only Kujou Sara and Kujou Masahito hold it.

Generic terms also occur: "Shogunate Officer" for an officer of any rank (Masahito is one, as is Toranosuke) and "Shogunate Samurai" for a soldier.
### ULTRA +
Each commission has named personnel the wiki tracks. In the Tenryou Commission, Okawara Goemon is Chief Samurai and Kujou Clan Security Head, Yasutake is the Quartermaster, and Yoriki such as Takasaka Izumi, Mikoshi Genjirou, Owada and Yata Kouki serve with doushin like Asakawa and Sanada. Shikanoin Heizou is the Tenryou Commission's "detective". The Kanjou Commission has a Chief Samurai, Katsuie, squad leaders Ebina Gonshirou and Sasaki, and a roster of officers and ashigaru. The Yashiro Commission's soldiers appear as representatives such as Hirano, Hirohiko and Hirotatsu. Former members include the Hatamoto Masanori (deceased), a Hatamoto named Kenji Kazumichi, and Iwakura Mitsunari (deceased). Historically, Kujou Shigeyori served as General and Takamine the Mistsplitter as Hatamoto.

## leadership
### LITE
The Raiden Shogun, Ei, is the army's head, and the Kujou Clan commands it in her name through the Tenryou Commission. Kujou Sara, an adopted tengu, and Kujou Masahito are its two Generals.
### STANDARD +
Sara is described as the pride of the army. She is the Vision Hunt Decree's main enforcer, she surveys battle lines and plans training herself rather than commanding from the rear, and she is said to hold herself to a regimen over ten times as tough as ordinary troops. Her profile says she gave clear orders forbidding harm or exploitation of civilians while carrying out the decree. Masahito, the eldest son of Kujou Takayuki, commands at the Kujou Encampment.
### LARGE +
The Kujou Clan's own leadership shifted after Omnipresence Over Mortals. Kujou Takayuki, the Tenryou Commissioner, was found to have colluded with the Fatui and is under house arrest. Masahito, as eldest son, had the right to take over as clan head, but he stood aside for his younger brother Kujou Kamaji, whom he judged better suited, and promised to support him as a warrior of the clan. Kamaji, who manages the Tenryou Commission's government business in Inazuma City, is Proxy Clan Head. After the Imperatrix Umbrosa Chapter, Ei challenged Takatsukasa Susumu, head of the Kujou branch Takatsukasa Clan, to a duel for the Tenryou Commissioner post. Susumu refused. Kamaji accepted, knowing he was outmatched. Afterward Ei said he was not a great descendant of the clan but that his honour was admirable, and she gave the Kujou Clan more time.
### ULTRA +
Kujou Shigeyori is the clan's namesake. Viewpoint text at the Kujou Encampment says he was a mortal general the Almighty Shogun valued highly. During a disaster hundreds of years ago he built a battlefront fortress there in one night and fought the forces of darkness, flying the Electro Mitsudomoe banner. The text adds that his descendants have inherited his skill in military engineering. Of the other commissions' leaders, Hiiragi Shinsuke of the Kanjou Commission was arrested for his part in the Fatui plot, and his daughter Hiiragi Chisato is acting head of that clan. Kamisato Ayato, the Yashiro commissioner, refused a Fatui offer of cooperation, which is why the Yashiro Commission is the one commission not caught up in the Fatui's scheme.

## goals
### STANDARD
The army exists to keep order and defend the Shogun's realm. Under the Vision Hunt Decree, the Tenryou Commission's job was to seize Visions across Inazuma, which were to be set into the Statue of the Omnipresent God in service of Ei's pursuit of Eternity. The Tenryou Commission also had the stated aim of putting down the Sangonomiya rebellion, and the Kujou Encampment was set up to guard against revolt from Watatsumi Island. Sara in particular believed that Visions in the hands of people with "incorrectly aligned" minds would unsettle the foundations of Inazuma.
### LARGE +
Since the repeal, the army's role has moved toward security and keeping the truce. Ei has relaxed the Shogun's functions and let the country open to foreigners, and the army no longer seizes Visions. A Tenryou notice had ordered families with a draft order to send at least one adult. It is not stated whether that draft was ended.

## methods
### LARGE
The army fights as disciplined formations of infantry led by yoriki and officers. Most Shogunate Samurai behave much like Millelith, according to the wiki. Its leaders favour ordered procedure: Vision Hunt Ceremonies, formal duels, and orders against harming civilians. At the Kujou Encampment, the Shogunate has stationed a larger, better-trained and better-equipped force than the Sangonomiya rebels. The Traveler fights Shogunate infantry, infantry captains and yoriki in quests such as In the Name of the Resistance, but these are not enemies that can be met freely in the open world.
### ULTRA +
The army also uses formal combat to settle political disputes. In the Vision Hunt era, an Inazuman who challenged the Shogun's decree to a duel before the throne lost to Kujou Sara and was executed. Kaedehara Kazuha took his friend's Vision and fled. Duels remain part of Inazuma's customs, and Ei used one to decide the Tenryou Commission's leadership after the Imperatrix Umbrosa Chapter.

## history
### STANDARD
Most of the army's visible history is recent. About a year before the Traveler's arrival, the Shogun instituted the Sakoku Decree and the Vision Hunt Decree, with the Fatui manipulating the Tenryou and Kanjou commissions behind the scenes. The Tenryou Commission carried out the Vision Hunt. In response the Sangonomiya Resistance (the Watatsumi Army) formed, and the two armies fought a war that had stalled by the time the Traveler arrived. The Traveler and their allies helped end the Vision Hunt Decree, and the Shogun's Army agreed a ceasefire with the Watatsumi Army, signed by Kujou Sara and Sangonomiya Kokomi with the Traveler as witness.
### LARGE +
After the ceasefire, Ei descended from the Plane of Euthymia, agreed a new idea of Eternity with the Shogun puppet and lifted the Sakoku Decree. The Tenryou Commission's leaders were then reorganised as described above. The wiki also notes that Masahito had warned his father that the Tatarigami suppressed on Yashiori Island had intensified, and was scolded; the team Takayuki sent to look was lost to its energy. The Kanjou and Tenryou commissions lightened their treatment of foreigners after Omnipresence Over Mortals.
### ULTRA +
The war cost the army both lives and morale. An item left behind by Inaba Kyuuzou, a soldier who had joined the military roughly ten years earlier hoping to fight legendary evils for "Eternity", records his despair: the enemies turned out to be ordinary people, some perhaps former drinking companions, and he called the so-called just war the greatest sin. The wiki notes that the Tenryou Commission suffers a higher desertion rate than the other two commissions because soldiers expected to fight monsters and Abyss denizens, not Inazumans. The Iwakura Art sword school's head, Iwakura Mitsunari, lost his Vision to the hunt, disbanded the school and served the army as a Kujou Clan sword instructor, to the confusion and sadness of his deserting disciples. He is listed as deceased.

## relations
### LARGE
- **Watatsumi Army**: its opponent in the war, led by Sangonomiya Kokomi with Gorou as General. The Shogunate calls them "Sangonomiya rebels". The two sides now keep a ceasefire.
- **Fatui**: manipulated the Tenryou and Kanjou commissions into pushing the decrees and fed the war.
- **Kamisato Clan**: leads the Yashiro Commission and also controls the Shuumatsuban. Kamisato Ayato refused Fatui help.
- **Hiiragi Clan**: leads the Kanjou Commission and the border. Chisato is acting head.
### ULTRA +
- **The Crux**: Kaedehara Kazuha, branded a criminal for seizing his friend's Vision, fled Inazuma and later sailed with the Crux.
- **The Traveler**: after the Traveler escaped the Vision Hunt Ceremony with Thoma and Paimon, the Tenryou Commission placed them on a wanted list.
- **Yae Miko and the Grand Narukami Shrine**: helped the Traveler show the Shogun the people's wishes inside the confiscated Visions.

## reputation
### LARGE
Inazumans' view of the army is mixed. The Kujou Clan is widely regarded as the most loyal and powerful of the families supporting the Shogun, and many ordinary people see Sara as an inaccessible, stone-cold soldier. During the Vision Hunt, the Tenryou Commission was resented. Yashiro soldiers have the best reputation: they are relaxed, care for the people, and are the least controversial of the three groups. Kanjou border guards were known to be abusive toward the foreigners living in Ritou.

## status
@version: 7.1
### LITE
As of 7.1 the army remains Inazuma's military, but under a more open, post-decree Shogunate. The Vision Hunt and Sakoku decrees are gone and the ceasefire with the Watatsumi Army holds.
### STANDARD +
The Tenryou Commission has no confirmed new commissioner: Kamaji is acting clan head, Sara and Masahito remain Generals, and Takayuki is under house arrest. No Shogun's Army events were found in the 6.x (Nod-Krai) or 7.0-7.1 (Snezhnaya) content.

## entry: Tenryou Commission
@keys: Tenryou Commission, Tenryou Commissioner, Tenryou Commission Headquarters
### STANDARD
The Tenryou Commission is the Shogun's executive arm and the part of the Tri-Commission responsible for security and military matters. The Kujou Clan leads it, and its troops are the army's main fighting force. It enforced both the Vision Hunt Decree and the Sakoku Decree, which gave it the worst desertion problems of the three commissions. Its leadership is unsettled after Takayuki's collusion with the Fatui was revealed.

## entry: Kujou Encampment
@keys: Kujou Encampment, Kannazuka front, Front Line Kannazuka
### STANDARD
The Kujou Encampment in Kannazuka is the headquarters of the Shogun's Army under the Tenryou Commission and was built to guard against rebellion from Watatsumi Island. Its fortress is attributed to Kujou Shigeyori. Kujou Masahito, the Shogunate Officer Toranosuke and the yoriki Iwakura Mitsunari are among the NPCs there.

## entry: Okuzumeshuu and Hatamoto
@keys: Okuzumeshuu, Hatamoto
### LARGE
The Okuzumeshuu are the Almighty Shogun's guard and take orders only from Ei. Hatamoto serve the Shogun directly and are not part of any commission. Two Hatamoto, Naoe Hisamasa and Shima Masaoki, guard Tenshukaku in Inazuma City.

## entry: Kujou family officers
@keys: Kujou Masahito, Kujou Kamaji, Kujou Takayuki
### LARGE
Masahito, the army's second named General, doubted the Vision Hunt Decree and thought it harmed rather than helped the people. He still fought the Watatsumi Army, and believed either side could win if the war dragged on. Kamaji, his younger brother and Proxy Clan Head, privately held doubts about the Shogun's new policy but kept them to himself. Takayuki, their father, was the Tenryou Commissioner and is now under house arrest. For Sara, see her own character entry.
