## summary
### LITE
The Blazing Beasts are a small mercenary brigade of Eremites from Sumeru's desert. Their members are tightly bonded and loyal, and their best-known member is Dehya, the Flame-Mane. They take paid work as bodyguards and fighters. Dehya says outright that they are not a large group.
### STANDARD +
Most Eremites work in loose bands rather than one organisation, and the Blazing Beasts are one such band. Dehya counts them as her chosen family.

## structure
### STANDARD
There is no formal hierarchy in canon. Like most Eremite brigades, the Blazing Beasts take commissions on their own account and do not answer to a larger body. Dehya is the most renowned member. The wider Eremite tradition is that anyone can join, and Dehya says "Eremites" is only a title that any Sumeru mercenary can use.
### LARGE +
The brigade has grown over time. Dehya says that since she received her Vision she has been able to bring more mercenaries into it. No numbers are given.

## leadership
### LITE
Dehya is the face of the brigade. Her profile calls her a legend among the Eremites for her strength, passion and conscientiousness.
### STANDARD +
The sources never give her a formal rank. Later event dialogue treats her as the one who manages the brigade's affairs (see the sub-entries on the events).

## members
### STANDARD
Dehya is the only named member in the game's main content. Raised among mercenaries, she joined the brigade at a young age, after her last argument with her adoptive father Kusayla. She regards its members as the family she chose for herself, trusted comrades who can say a great deal with a glance.
### ULTRA +
Jeht, a mercenary the Traveler meets in the 6.6 Archon Quest, recognises Dehya as one of the Blazing Beasts when they meet at the Temple of Silence. He is not himself described as belonging to the brigade.

## goals
### STANDARD
Dehya describes the brigade as small, but loyal and brave. Her profile says she works for good pay on reasonable commissions. She wants to bring hope back to the desert, which she considers her home. Members place mutual support above everything.

## methods
### STANDARD
Mercenary work such as bodyguard commissions, escorts and combat for hire. Dehya's profile says she is a conscientious professional who charges a high premium and has a long line of clients. Her character story says the bonds between companions grow through fighting side by side. The brigade sings a simple campfire song praising Dehya, ending with a warning that she is to be feared when angry.

## history
@version: 3.5
### STANDARD
Dehya was found as an infant in the desert, according to the wiki, and raised by Kusayla, leader of Dakan Al-Ahmar, an Eremite group under Deshret's Relics. The Relics bound their members by recording their shameful deeds. Kusayla did not want Dehya recorded, and he referred her to the Blazing Beasts, who took her in. After an argument between the two, he burned the records room with himself inside, an event told in Dehya's story quest Lionsblood (3.5). Kusayla had once saved the family of Shahzaman from bandits, and in the quest Shahzaman and Dunyarzad recall that it was the Blazing Beasts who sent Dehya to work as Dunyarzad's bodyguard.
### LARGE +
The wiki says Kusayla secretly arranged the brigade's help so that she could leave Dakan Al-Ahmar without harassment from Deshret's Relics. In the quest itself, Shahzaman only says that Kusayla insisted on sending Dehya, hoping to get her out of the mercenary life. Dehya's later telling in Shade Upon Red Silk presents her brigade as honourable and sets it against the Relics.
### ULTRA +
Versions of its appearance: 3.5 introduces the brigade through Dehya's profile and story quest. Limited-time events add it in 5.6 and 6.1 (see the sub-entries). In the 6.6 Archon Quest, Dehya is in the Temple of Silence. Nothing further was found through 7.1.

## relations
### STANDARD
Within the Eremites the Blazing Beasts keep clear of Deshret's Relics, a faction that crossed all kinds of lines for Mora, as Dehya says herself. Dehya is also tied to the Corps of Thirty through her past, and to Candace, the guardian of Aaru Village, who is her close friend.
### LARGE +
Dehya sees the Eremites' reputation as falling because some fakers use the name to cheat outsiders, and she distinguishes her brigade from them.

## reputation
### STANDARD
Among Eremites, Dehya is celebrated and her brigade shares in her good name. Outsiders often judge all Eremites as unscrupulous, but this brigade is portrayed as honest.

## status
### LITE
Active as of 7.1. No base is stated for the brigade, and it was last shown staying in Aaru Village (6.1 event).
### STANDARD +
Dehya remains in Sumeru during the 6.6 events as a mercenary. No Blazing Beasts developments were found in 6.7 to 7.1 content.

## entry: Whirling Waltz partnership

### STANDARD
During the Whirling Waltz flagship event (5.6, May 2025) in the Court of Fontaine, Dehya agrees to take part and Paimon jokes that she and the Traveler are now official partners of the Blazing Beasts. Uncle Scimitar, a mercenary actor, also appears and calls the Traveler his boss. He admits he once fought the Traveler before he knew they were Dehya's friend. This is event material, not main story.

## entry: Uncle Scimitar
@keys: Uncle Scimitar
@version: 5.6
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
An event-only Eremite NPC listed in the wiki as a "mercenary actor" of the Blazing Beasts. He appears in Whirling Waltz (5.6) and Dance Dance Dynamic Disco (6.1). He calls himself one of Dehya's lads and treats the Traveler as an honorary sibling because they are Dehya's friend.
### LARGE +
In Fontaine he says he helped a director with an event and applied for a job at the Traveler's cinema. In Natlan he says Dehya manages the Blazing Beasts and coordinates with Candace between the desert and the rainforest, and that members are picking up arithmetic to lighten her load.

## entry: Fieldwise Center security work
@keys: Fieldwise Center, Security Division
@keys2: Blazing Beasts, Candace, Dehya
@version: 6.1
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
In the Ruins Exploration: Fieldwise Center event (6.1), the Akademiya's new Fieldwise Center has a Security Division that ensures field teams include trained fighters. Candace says some of this work has been given to the Blazing Beasts and other mercenaries, though team members can also qualify by passing the combat test. She says the brigade will be staying in Aaru Village for the foreseeable future.
### LARGE +
Dehya had picked out fine clothes as a gift for Candace, who suggested they go to the brigade's accountant instead, so that the Blazing Beasts have someone suitably dressed to represent them in business settings. Candace offered to sew sand-resistant cloaks for the members if Dehya brought cloth.
