## summary
### LITE
The Lightkeepers are Nod-Krai's militia against the Wild Hunt, the Abyssal host that has haunted the borderland since the Cataclysm. Solovei the Torchforger founded them about 500 years ago, and a royal edict from Snezhnaya made Nod-Krai an autonomous region under their protection. They are led by a Starshyna, currently Nikita, from the fortress-city of Piramida. Their fighters are called Ratniki, and every one of them carries an Oath Lantern. Their emblem is a nightingale holding a burning wick in its beak.
### STANDARD +
They are volunteers, chronically short of people, and proud of it. In their own words they "shine the eternal light of hope into the endless snow-swept plains". Playable members are Flins and Illuga.

## structure
### STANDARD
- **Starshyna**: the single leader, based in Piramida.
- **Sergeant Majors** (sometimes addressed as Captains): each is responsible for an area of Nod-Krai and coordinates its squad leaders.
- **Squad Leaders**: command a squad, marked by a special brooch on the left lapel of the uniform.
- **Ratniki** (singular Ratnik): the ordinary fighting members.
Every Ratnik carries an Oath Lantern, which lets other Lightkeepers and the Starshyna detect where they are. Members disabled by injury may be moved to other posts if they want to keep serving. Disobeying direct orders brings real trouble. There are also non-combat staff such as doctors and a blacksmith.
### LARGE +
Known squads include the investigation squads "Nightmare Orioles" (now led by Illuga and posted at Kipumaki Cliff), "Wildnight Owls" (suspended after a reckless operation) and "Snowfield Falcons" (patrolling around Piramida). There was also a Special Support Squad, "Sumrak", which waited on the island that is now the Final Night Cemetery for a lighthouse signal to reinforce Piramida. All its members died there at some unknown time.

## leadership
### LITE
Nikita, a middle-aged veteran the Ratniki fondly call "old man", is the current Starshyna. He took over about a decade ago when his predecessor Sigurd fell fighting the Wild Hunt.
### LARGE +
Nikita is meticulous, perceptive and even-handed. He never asks of his people what he would not do himself, and he quietly places each member where they fit. He adopted Illuga after the Wild Hunt destroyed Illuga's village. He knows Flins' true nature and treats him as a trusted confidant. Nikita himself says he lacks Sigurd's strength and that the Lightkeepers "are no longer what they once were", so they must act with extra caution.

The known Starshynas are Solovei the Torchforger (the first), Sergei Semyonovich Mayakov (named in a book), Sigurd and Nikita. Named Sergeant Majors include Marushkin and the late Sousi. Lyubertsi is a former Sergeant Major.

## members
### LARGE
- **Flins**: a courtly Ratnik who lives alone in the lighthouse on the Final Night Cemetery, tending the graves of fallen Lightkeepers. He is secretly one of the Snowland Fae.
- **Illuga**: Nikita's adopted son, the young leader of the Nightmare Orioles, and the Lightkeepers' representative in the new League.
- **Olav**: a calm Ratnik recently promoted to squad leader.

Other named members include the squad leaders Gabieta, Vytautas and the late Asebov and Olsson; the doctors Ivar and Radoslaw; the blacksmith Hertta; the quartermaster Patolichev; Inga, a former squad leader; and Ratniki such as Anleifr, Egle, Rollon, Valdis, Vlaicu, Lushne, Sukhovykh and Malusha. Chicherin is a former member who took in Malusha after he and Sigurd rescued her as a child. The Final Night Cemetery holds many Lightkeeper graves.

## goals
### STANDARD
Their stated purpose, in Nikita's words, is to safeguard the freedom and peace of Nod-Krai. In practice this means holding back the Wild Hunt, protecting the merchants and travellers who cross their lands, and keeping alive the "paradise" (Elysium) that Solovei defended before the Tsaritsa's court. Illuga describes Nod-Krai as an Elysium built by human hands and kept alive by generations of Lightkeeper sacrifice. He prefers self-reliance to waiting for any god, even the new Moon Goddess.
### ULTRA +
Illuga hopes that once the Wild Hunt is driven from Nod-Krai, the Lightkeepers might someday help people in hardship beyond their borders, as he understands the Knights of Favonius were founded to defend their homeland too.

## methods
### STANDARD
Lightkeepers patrol the land, answer Wild Hunt incursions wherever they appear, and keep watch from lighthouses and camps. They have planted Tideseal Stones across Nod-Krai to stop the Wild Hunt from seeping in. Their oath, sworn by every new member, ends: "We are the Lightkeepers, who shine the eternal light of hope into the endless snow-swept plains." Flins names prudence, modesty and loyalty as their three cardinal virtues.
### LARGE +
Item lore stresses how little they have: no ancient techniques or grand titles, only "weak flesh clad in silver armor", swords, lamps and an inherited oath. Their one great relic is the Arrow of Terpikeraunas, a giant Hyperborean-era cannon at Piramida that turns light into a projectile to purge Abyssal contamination. Aila of the Frostmoon Scions gave it to Solovei. For machinery, they rely on the engineer Aino, who keeps trying to make them learn repairs themselves.
### ULTRA +
After hard-won victories, they hold banquets in the great mead hall at the top of Piramida. The form of these banquets goes back to the old Snezhnayan court, and the first pour always goes to the dead. The uniform is a black long coat with silver ornaments and dark purple accents, often worn with an ushanka. Some descendants of the Torchforger's first companions still wore archaic tasseled helmets as a mark of their bloodline.

## history
### LITE
When the Cataclysm struck about 500 years ago, Abyssal beasts overran Nod-Krai and the northern court could no longer protect its borderlands. Solovei, a Snezhnayan noble, came south, and with power lent by Aila, priestess of the Frostmoon Scions, drove the darkness back. The survivors raised a lighthouse in the mountains, and Solovei won a royal edict for Nod-Krai's autonomy in return for an oath of vigilance and sacrifice.
### STANDARD +
Since then the Lightkeepers have been nearly wiped out several times and rebuilt each time. About a decade ago, a great battle against the Wild Hunt killed Starshyna Sigurd and most of the order, and destroyed Illuga's village. Nikita had to rebuild almost from scratch. The disaster convinced many young people of the Lempo Isles that the old tales were true, and they volunteered. Three years before the Traveler came, a Wild Hunt surge at Kipumaki Cliff overwhelmed the Nightmare Orioles. Captain Olsson died covering the retreat of Illuga and the badly wounded Ivar.
### LARGE +
**Song of the Welkin Moon (6.0).** Sergeant Major Sousi helped the Traveler recalibrate the Boom and Blast Kuuvahki Cannon at Starsand Shoal against a Wild Hunt incursion. Half a day later he was found dead in the Lightkeepers' Nasha Town office, and a witness had seen Flins enter. The Traveler cleared Flins: Rerir had already killed Sousi, worn his face during the repairs and then taken Flins' form. During Dottore's later attack on Nod-Krai, Illuga led the Lightkeepers in evacuating civilians, emptying Piramida itself.

**Nightingale's Song and the League (6.3).** Nikita sent Illuga to deal with the Abyssal blight at Kipumaki Cliff, which was caused by Rotwang's disciples rather than Rerir. With the Traveler, the Nightmare Orioles recovered Solovei's lost Spiralblade, the Arrow's ammunition. The Arrow of Terpikeraunas then destroyed the Calamity Egg on the Pillar of Embla. The Ratnik Bjorn died in the operation. After Dottore's defeat, Nod-Krai's factions formed a joint League, and Flins persuaded a reluctant Illuga to represent the Lightkeepers.
### ULTRA +
**Solovei.** Solovei came from a Snezhnayan noble house whose emblem was the nightingale, a family of former spies raised to the gentry by the Winter Tsar (the Belyi Tsar). As a boy he and his friend Avrelian (later the bandit Reed Miller) shared a dream of a "paradise". Aila had foreseen his coming and became his benefactor. He was an enemy of the Archpriestess Lovia, and he hunted the "mad monk" whose schemes had cost Aila's sister Alia her life. Legend says Solovei also founded Piramida on the old palace of Nod-Krai's fae governor.

**Item-lore episodes.** One tale tells of the Fatui arriving at Piramida's gates during a Wild Hunt crisis, offering "reinforcements" that the Starshyna saw as a threat to centuries of autonomy. Another tells of a Fatui Warrant Officer who defied orders to save children from the Wild Hunt and, branded a deserter, was absolved by a new oath. A third tells of a young Ratnik praying among his fallen comrades whose plea woke the "ghostly blue wildfire" of legend, which Flins' own story identifies as his awakening.

## relations
### STANDARD
- **Frostmoon Scions**: allies since Aila aided Solovei. Flins mentions that some former Scions recently joined the Lightkeepers, apparently after Lauma revealed the truth about the false sky.
- **Knights of Favonius**: allies. Varka's expedition fought the Wild Hunt alongside them, and Illuga has fought beside Varka.
- **Fatui and the Tsaritsa**: Nod-Krai is formally Snezhnayan, but its autonomy rests on the old edict. Flins notes that Lightkeepers do not habitually say "Her Majesty the Tsaritsa".
- **The Wild Hunt**: their enemy since the Cataclysm.
### LARGE +
- **Nasha Town factions**: they work with the Voynich Guild and Nefer's Curatorium of Secrets, which smoothed their operations in town. All three, with the Frostmoon Scions, sit in the new League.
- **Field and headquarters**: some field members grumble that headquarters is haughty and slow to act on reports, while expecting tiny squads to handle incursions anywhere.

## reputation
### LARGE
Townsfolk respect the Lightkeepers' sacrifices. Flins was once given a civilian commendation medal that the people organised themselves. The work is thankless, though. Many recruits retire early or quit, and recruitment drops after every brutal battle. Lately, Illuga says, more and more people want to join, including children too young to be accepted, and even Nasha Town's merchants have begun to see why the Lightkeepers fight.

## secrets
### ULTRA
Very few know that Flins is a fae noble centuries old. Nikita does, and Illuga suspects it but has not been told outright. Few in Nod-Krai know the Wild Hunt's true origin in Rerir. Folk tales call it a demonic host from another world or the phantoms of an ancient nightmare. The fallen Starshyna Sigurd was not at rest: the Abyss twisted him into a ghoul that kept fighting the Wild Hunt until the Traveler (or Flins or Illuga) laid his relics at his empty grave.

## status
@version: 6.3
### LITE
As of 7.1 the Lightkeepers are active under Starshyna Nikita, still understrength but recruiting again, and part of Nod-Krai's new League.
### STANDARD +
Illuga is their League representative, and his squad is rebuilding a Lightkeeper outpost at Cliffwatch Camp. The Wild Hunt has been leaderless since Rerir was cast out and the Abyss's imitation of Roland expired in 6.4, but its incursions continue. No new Lightkeepers story was found for 7.0-7.1, whose Archon Quests take place in Snezhnaya.

## entry: The Lightkeepers' Oath
@keys: Lightkeepers' Oath, Lightkeeper oath, Oath Lantern, Oath Lanterns
@version: 6.0
### STANDARD
Every new Lightkeeper swears an oath in seven lines, which is also carved on Oath Lanterns and on a monument at the Final Night Cemetery. It moves from a vision of the sky swallowed by silence, through a promise that whoever keeps the flame burning need not descend into darkness, to a vow to burn "bones and blood" as fuel and "make life greater than death". It ends: "We are the Lightkeepers, who shine the eternal light of hope into the endless snow-swept plains." Oath Lanterns are standard equipment. Through them other Lightkeepers and the Starshyna can find a Ratnik, and lanterns of the fallen are still found across the southern isles.

## entry: Starshyna
@keys: Starshyna, Starshynas
@keys2: Lightkeepers, Lightkeeper, Nikita, Sigurd, Solovei, Torchforger, Piramida
### STANDARD
Starshyna is the title of the Lightkeepers' leader. Solovei the Torchforger was the first. Later holders include Sergei Semyonovich Mayakov and Sigurd, a famously brave longsword fighter whose warning bell was said to make beasts flinch. Sigurd vanished in the great battle about a decade ago. The present Starshyna is Nikita, who fought beside him and rebuilt the order afterward.

## entry: Piramida
@keys: Piramida, Torchforger Statue, great mead hall
### STANDARD
Piramida is the Lightkeepers' headquarters, a city on top of an ancient castle that was once the palace of Nod-Krai's fae governor, built around a giant golden-age lighthouse. A statue of Solovei the Torchforger watches over it, and the great mead hall at its summit hosts victory banquets where the first pour goes to the dead. The Arrow of Terpikeraunas is housed there.

## entry: Arrow of Terpikeraunas
@keys: Arrow of Terpikeraunas, Spiralblade
@version: 6.3
### LARGE
The Arrow of Terpikeraunas is a giant Hyperborean-era cannon at Piramida that turns light into a solid projectile to purge Abyssal contamination. The Frostmoon Scions rebuilt it after Hyperborea fell, and Aila gave it to Solovei 500 years ago. His sword, the Spiralblade, was its ammunition but was lost for centuries. In Nightingale's Song (6.3), Illuga and the Traveler recovered it, and the Arrow destroyed the Calamity Egg atop the Pillar of Embla.

## entry: Nightmare Orioles
@keys: Nightmare Orioles, Wildnight Owls, Snowfield Falcons, Sumrak
### LARGE
The Nightmare Orioles are an investigation squad. Under Captain Olsson they were the first to meet a Wild Hunt surge at Kipumaki Cliff three years ago, and only Illuga and Ivar survived. Illuga took command and now holds Cliffwatch Camp with Anleifr, Egle, Rollon, Valdis and Vlaicu. Bjorn died in 6.3. The Wildnight Owls were suspended after their leader Inga's recklessness got Lauri hurt, and the Snowfield Falcons replaced them on patrol around Piramida. The Special Support Squad "Sumrak" all died on the island now called the Final Night Cemetery.

## entry: Tideseal Stones
@keys: Tideseal Stones, Tideseal Stone
### LARGE
The Lightkeepers placed Tideseal Stones across Nod-Krai to dispel the seeping of the Wild Hunt. Three of them bear a blue Lightkeeper symbol and are linked to the fallen Starshyna Sigurd, whose corrupted spirit was seen fighting the Wild Hunt near them.

## entry: Ratnik
@keys: Ratnik, Ratniki
### STANDARD
A Ratnik (plural Ratniki) is an ordinary fighting member of the Lightkeepers. The word comes from an old Slavic term for a warrior of a militia or host. Flins calls himself "a humble Ratnik" and says the role, keeping humanity's light burning on nights when monsters roam, suits a nocturnal creature like him perfectly.
