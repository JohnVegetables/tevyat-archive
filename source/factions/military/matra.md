## summary
### LITE
The Matra are the disciplinary officers of the Sumeru Akademiya, its executive arm for academic integrity. They hunt down and sentence researchers guilty of serious offences such as plagiarism, fraud, bribery, misappropriation of funds and forbidden research. Their leader is the General Mahamatra, Cyno. Researchers fear them: a visit from the Matra almost never goes well.
### STANDARD +
The Matra also police the trade in Knowledge Capsules and smuggled books, and apply a special seal to goods that have passed their inspection.

## structure
### STANDARD
Matra answer to the General Mahamatra, currently Cyno, who is formally under the sages of the Akademiya. They are separate from the Mahamata, the Akademiya's civil administrative officials. The Matra have an office in the Akademiya, a canteen and a reputation for being stretched thin. A message-board post calls them short-staffed.
### LARGE +
A Matra's usual path: the offence reaches them, a Matra visits the offender in person, and the case ends in a disciplinary action or trial. A personal visit from the General Mahamatra signals a serious offence; Tighnari remarks that most research is nowhere near significant enough to warrant a talk with a Matra. Matra are often posted away from the Akademiya: Nayab is in Sumeru City, Shohre in the House of Daena library, and Nabil at the Akademiya.

## leadership
### LITE
General Mahamatra Cyno commands the Matra. He is a young man of the desert who is stern, tireless and fond of jokes that nobody finds funny.
### STANDARD +
Cyno's profile says he will judge every lawbreaker by the sages' six cardinal sins, and that even sages shall not be exempt. He once says that, as a Matra, one of his duties is to help people "remember" their mistakes. He pursues escapees across the Great Red Sand but will rescue them from danger first so that only the law may judge them. He sees the Matra as guardians of those who seek knowledge, and he accepts a researcher's nickname for the Matra, "the Akademiya's Sumpter Beasts", as an apt description of carrying the weight of the rules.

## members
### STANDARD
Known Matra: Cyno (General Mahamatra), Nabil (a Matra who first meets the Traveler in the Akademiya in Cyno's story quest), Taj Radkani (Cyno's former senior partner, died in 3.1), Usem, Shohre, Nayab ("Relaxed Matra") and Aarav. The Matra office has a custom: after Cyno tells a joke, everyone in the room forces a laugh so that he won't start explaining it. A Matra who tries to imitate his style is punished by having their canteen meal tickets confiscated.
### LARGE +
Shohre, in the House of Daena, dreads Cyno's jokes and, during the limited-time event A Parade of Providence, wondered whether to use his vacation as a chance to visit the office. Nayab is found at Puspa Café, fretting over a card-game deck. Taj taught Cyno polearm training and is the Matra's most important recent loss.

## goals
### STANDARD
Keep the Akademiya's research within its laws. Cyno's profile says the sages laid down six cardinal sins, among them interfering with human evolution, tampering with life and death, and delving beyond the universe. The Matra judge breaches of these laws and the laws that evolved from them. Their purpose, as Cyno explains it, is to stop uncontrolled "wisdom" from becoming calamity.
### LARGE +
Cyno cites Cyrus, a scholar who tutored him, who told him that without the Akademiya's laws, wisdom would turn to calamity. In Character Story 5, he is resolved to judge all lawbreakers by the six sins, "even sages".

## methods
### STANDARD
Investigation, arrest, trial and seizure. Matra inspect and seal goods, check Knowledge Capsule transactions, and halt banned research. Files from halted projects are mostly destroyed, but some are kept as a warning in the Restricted Repository, a heavily guarded vault of the Akademiya accessible only to the General Mahamatra and trusted associates. Bribes and force are treated as aggravating, so offenders who try them are punished more severely. Cyno is described as patient with excuses. Researchers have claimed reasons including cut funding, favouritism and stolen results.
### LARGE +
In the Sands of Solitude quest, Cyno and the Traveler investigate a Canned Knowledge smuggling ring. The Matra also use bulletin boards, posting public notices through the Akademiya message boards. Typical notices remind readers to observe protocol, warn that spreading rumours will be referred to the Matra, and announce investigations of Canned Knowledge smuggling.

## history
### LARGE
**Before 3.0.** The Matra are the Akademiya's law enforcement. They are not described as founded at a particular time. They enforce the Akademiya's modern regulations, which Cyno says grew from six cardinal sins laid down by the sages after early researchers' experiments went too far.

**Sands of Solitude (3.1).** Nabil, a Matra, asked the Traveler for help with a Canned Knowledge smuggling case while Cyno was absent. Cyno was already investigating. The trail led to a ring seeking information on the Court of Desolation, a ruin whose study the Akademiya had banned. In the last part, All Returns to Silence, Cyno's former senior partner Taj Radkani was found to have joined the ring on purpose, leaving a trail for Cyno. Taj had lost his son Murtada, whom he had himself arrested for entering the Court of Desolation. He died in the quicksand he caused to bury the Court. Over the next four days Cyno cleared his name, the Matra arrested the traitors in the Akademiya from Taj's list of some 300 names, and some who escaped were caught by Cyno.

**Archon Quest (Chapter III).** The Matra's exact role is not covered in the pages checked. In Ever So Close, they arrived to arrest Mizri after Alhaitham defeated him.
### ULTRA +
Cyno's Vision story is told as the day before his inauguration. His appointment appears to predate the Archon Quest, since the board entry credits him with a better tenure than a previous General Mahamatra.

## relations
### STANDARD
**Researchers and the Darshans.** The Matra are feared by researchers across the Darshans, who call them demolishers or paper shredders. A Haravatat researcher coined the "Sumpter Beasts" nickname. Cyno's character stories mention cases from Amurta, Spantamad and Vahumana.
**Sages.** Cyno treats even the sages as subject to law. The sages create the rules the Matra enforce.
**The Eremites.** Cyno hails from the desert, and the proudest mercenaries are said to dare not defy him after a group was destroyed for sheltering wanted researchers. The Matra arrived to arrest Mizri of Ayn Al-Ahmar in Port Ormos.
**Cyno's friends.** Tighnari, a friend of Cyno's, warns that if Cyno chases you for work you should prepare for a nightmare. Cyno visits Collei in Gandharva Ville when he can.
### LARGE +
Tighnari says he told the Matra that Karkata is his research assistant so that nobody touches it.

## reputation
### STANDARD
Feared in the Akademiya, resented by researchers who say they obstruct knowledge, and quietly respected elsewhere. Researchers say the Matra "cut off the path to knowledge". Cyno does not mind: Tighnari remarks that most research is nowhere near significant enough to need a Matra.

## status
### LITE
Active under General Mahamatra Cyno as of 7.1.
### STANDARD +
After the Sumeru Archon Quest the Akademiya was reformed under Lesser Lord Kusanali. No source found shows the Matra changing in structure or leadership.

### STANDARD
Cyno leads the Matra. His Electro Vision, and his profile titles "Judicator of Secrets" and "General Mahamatra", are covered in his own entry. As leader he is feared by researchers, and his profile says even sages shall not be exempt from his judgement. Colleagues find his jokes dry and long to explain, and the Matra office has agreed to force a laugh after each one so he will not explain it.

@keys2: Matra, Cyno, Court of Desolation

### STANDARD
Taj was a Matra and Cyno's senior partner, who taught him polearm combat and retired from field work a year before the Sands of Solitude quest, taking an administrative post. He arrested his own son Murtada for entering the banned Court of Desolation, and Murtada took his own life in prison. Taj later pretended to join the ring that had lured Murtada, left a trail for Cyno, and collapsed the Court under quicksand at the cost of his life.
