## summary
### LITE
The Beret Society was a prisoners' club in the Fortress of Meropide in Fontaine. Its founder, Dougier, presented it as a way to help inmates turn over a new leaf, and its members wore berets as a badge of pride. In truth it was a scheme that ruled members through fear. Wriothesley and the Traveler exposed it during the Cerberus Chapter, and it was disbanded.
### STANDARD +
Publicly it was a model-prisoner organisation, and Wriothesley, the Duke of Meropide, helped it get started by approving Dougier's application. Privately Dougier meant to use it, and a stolen force of Gardemeks, to seize control of the prison from Wriothesley.

## structure
@version: 4.1
### STANDARD
Dougier was the sole leader. Members were organised into groups of five, and a whole group was punished for one member's offence. The society met near the Coupon Cafeteria in the Fortress, where it offered drinks, snacks, books, seminars and relaxation. It ran on donated goods and Credit Coupons from its members. Behind this front was a hidden headquarters beneath the Production Zone, which the quest calls Dougier's "Kingdom".
### LARGE +
Dougier watched the public meeting place through a surveillance port when he was absent, and put his most presentable members forward as the face of the society. The members named on the wiki are Aucelet, Avice, Boulmier, Deparis, Eclancher, Faissolle, Fidelin, Gouzien, Rigas and Wyler.

## goals
@version: 4.1
### STANDARD
The stated goal was to help prisoners look out for one another, improve their state of mind, make friends and start a new life. The real goal, revealed late in the Cerberus Chapter, was to topple Wriothesley. Dougier believed Wriothesley lived in comfort on the prisoners' labour and wanted that wealth and workforce for himself.

## methods
@version: 4.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The berets were an identity badge. Dougier said he wanted a visible mark of belonging and pride, and the hats also helped recruit members. Hidden in them were thorns, which could inject a dark liquid called Aqua Doloris, a concentrated form of fear, into a member's head. The society's real rules forbade speaking to one another without permission, leaving without permission, and breaking Dougier's orders. They also required groups of five to share punishment and rewarded informers with food and water. A breach brought "censure": the liquid was injected while the member was conscious.
### LARGE +
Dougier also built a force by taking Gardemeks, the prison's labour machines, through "disappearances" or false decommissioning orders. He abused his authority over members in other ways: the wiki cites Avice being forced to punish her partner Faissolle, and Dougier silencing Faissolle when he was about to be punished.

## history
@version: 4.1
### STANDARD
In the opening of the Cerberus Chapter, Reborn in the Land of Grievances, the Traveler and Paimon meet Faissolle, who has lost his hat and is hiding in a box. Wriothesley then introduces the Beret Society as model prisoners and takes the Traveler to see its daily life. Wriothesley had investigated the society, which Dougier knew about and judged he could hide from. In the third part, "It's What He Deserves", members escape into the Production Zone, and Wriothesley and the Traveler find the hidden headquarters, rescue victims and destroy the Gardemek force. Dougier drew a revolver on Wriothesley and then on the Traveler and Paimon before Wriothesley disarmed him. Wriothesley sent him back to confinement, and the society ended with his arrest.
### ULTRA +
Dougier appealed to the rule that prisoners may not be punished or tortured without authority, although he had done exactly that to his own members. Wriothesley answered that he made the rules there. He later told the Traveler that he left Dougier alive deliberately: he wants Dougier to fear what punishment is coming and when, and to let the former members have a part in it.

## relations
### STANDARD
- **Wriothesley**: approved the society and later destroyed it. He ran the prison as the Duke of Meropide.
- **Traveler and Paimon**: found Faissolle, visited the society with Wriothesley and helped uncover the scheme.
- **Gardemeks**: machines that served the Fortress and became Dougier's muscle.

## reputation
@version: 4.1
### STANDARD
Within Meropide it looked like a harmless, popular social club with well-attended events. Its true nature was known only to Dougier's inner circle and its victims.

## secrets
@version: 4.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The berets concealed the thorns of Aqua Doloris, and the society's real rules and the hidden headquarters were never meant to reach Wriothesley. Dougier believed his cover would hold.

## status
### LITE
Disbanded. Dougier was arrested and held in confinement after the Cerberus Chapter (4.1).
### STANDARD +
No later canon, up to 7.1, was found that revives the society. The Fortress of Meropide itself carries on under Wriothesley.

## entry: Dougier
@keys: Dougier
@keys2: Beret Society, Meropide, Wriothesley
@version: 4.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Dougier is the quest-only prisoner who founded the Beret Society. He seems a friendly do-gooder, but is manipulative and abusive toward his own members. He turned the society against Wriothesley and fell short when confronted. Wriothesley calls him a coward without the hat, the members and the Gardemeks. He was imprisoned again after the Cerberus Chapter.

## entry: Aqua Doloris
@keys: Aqua Doloris
@version: 4.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Aqua Doloris, Latin for "Water of Pain", is a dark liquid that is a highly concentrated form of fear. In the Beret Society, thorns hidden under the berets injected it into the head, and the fear made members compliant. It was kept in a black gem that Dougier obtained from unnamed contacts.
