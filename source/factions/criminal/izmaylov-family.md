## summary
### LITE
The Izmaylov family, or the Izmaylovs, are a Snezhnayan fae gang, the biggest and richest in the High Table. The Domovoy Nikolay Izmaylov, the "Old Sire", founded the gang, and his adopted son Vasiliy, the "Young Sire", inherited it. Vasiliy killed the Old Sire, pushed the gang into brutal debt collection to fund a plan to open the Shadow Realm, and died when the Traveler and the Luchkins stopped him. The gang is now leaderless.
### STANDARD +
The Izmaylovs are the main villains of the Mean Streets of the Despicable world quests in Snezhnograd. Other gangs see them as having broken the Law that the High Table was founded on.

## structure
### STANDARD
The Izmaylovs have a top leader, the Old Sire and later the Young Sire, and a second-in-command known as the Butcher who runs a major stronghold. Below them are guards and heavies, shipping crews and debt collectors. Members called by name include Lindenovich, a debt collector, Lorie, a small Volkodlak who handled cargo shipments, and the shippers Kirov, Meselson and Ishogu. Most members are fae, including Volkodlaks.
### LARGE +
Members use "Old Sire" and "Young Sire" as titles. Guards at the strongholds demand a password, and the Train Boneyard password was "Oedipus", Vasiliy's own nickname.

## leadership
### STANDARD
Nikolay "Pakhan" Izmaylov, the Old Sire, founded the gang and led it until his death. His adopted son Vasiliy Nikolayevich Izmaylov, nicknamed "Oedipus" and called the Young Sire, inherited the gang. He was the most favoured of the Old Sire's four adopted children. Zmei still calls him "Vasya". After Vasiliy died the gang was left leaderless.
### LARGE +
Vasiliy resented that the gangs were treated like "dogs" by the lords above and that his father spent his life serving others. He rejected the surname "Nikolayevich" and the title "Young Sire" and wanted to be called "Lord Vasiliy". The Butcher is his second-in-command.

## members
### STANDARD
Known members are Lindenovich (debt collector), Lorie (shipping chief), Kirov, Meselson and Ishogu (shippers) and the Butcher (second-in-command). Guards include a tall guard and a short guard at the Gang-Occupied Cavern near Glupov, a muscular guard (who sends a guard called Kubiyev to check a noise), an Izmaylov guard leader and an Izmaylov deputy. The gang also uses Volkodlak workers; one said that when the Old Sire was alive, they never worked so hard.

## goals
### STANDARD
Under the Old Sire, the Izmaylovs followed the ideals of the High Table. Under Vasiliy, the gang's goal changed to opening the Shadow Realm in the Train Boneyard and using the technology and power it offered to win the Izmaylovs a seat in the real council, the Snezhnograd Veche, and respect from the lords above. Vasiliy told the Luchkins that "there will always be a place for Izmaylovs at Snezhnaya's table".

## methods
### STANDARD
The Izmaylovs make their money from debt collection, and under Vasiliy they pursued it ruthlessly. They collected from people who could not pay, took a father to a lumberyard for his debts, and threatened the daughter and grandmother left at home, despite the Law's ban on harming families, women and children. They rented train carriages to ship raw materials in secret, moved supplies by rail, guarded abandoned bases with armed patrols, and used force against the Luchkins. Disputes can still be settled by a formal duel, as Lindenovich offered to Vseslav.
### LARGE +
Lindenovich quotes the Old Sire's own rule, "debts must be repaid", to defend his debt collection, while Sofya says the Izmaylovs now ignore the founding rules. The gang also digs for ancient machinery and prototypes in the Shadow Realm, such as a prototype Kresnik's Torch in the Kristall cavern of Hunter on the Snowfields, where Izmaylov members argued over treasure shares.

## history
### STANDARD
The Domovoy Nikolay Izmaylov helped found the High Table after the Burner of Cities' rebellion and later gave each of his four adopted children a gang name. The Izmaylovs went to Vasiliy. For years the Old Sire let the Energy Commission take on Shadow Realm commissions so he could search for his teacher. When he found her, he stopped, and he tried to steer the gang and the Commission away from Shadow Realm matters.
### LARGE +
Several months before the Traveler arrived in Snezhnograd, the Old Sire fell ill and died overnight. Gang members suspected Vasiliy and the Butcher, who were the last to see him. After that, discipline slipped across the gangs, punishments for breaking the Law became more frequent, and the Izmaylovs began to collect debts by force.
### ULTRA +
**Mean Streets of the Despicable.** In Burn the Storehouses and the Stables, the Traveler helped Vseslav of the Luchkins when Lindenovich tried to take a girl called Marfa's family's debts by force. The Traveler beat Lindenovich in a formal duel, and the Luchkins then asked the Traveler to find out what the Izmaylovs were shipping. The Traveler followed Lorie to the Snezhnograd Central Station, took a sample from a freight car, beat Lorie and escaped on Sofya's sled. The Luchkins suspected the shipment was raw material for a device that could open the Shadow Realm.

In Disputes Without Honor and Humanity, the Luchkin informant Gureva, who had slipped into the Butcher's stronghold in an abandoned Fatui base northwest of Snezhnograd, went missing. The Traveler and Paimon sneaked in while Sofya and Iryna waited outside, freed her and took a box from the Butcher's desk. Gureva told the Luchkins that the device was at the Train Boneyard. The Luchkins rented a train, got in with the password "Oedipus", and followed Vasiliy through guards who were driven mad by the Shadow Realm. Vasiliy confessed that he had killed his father in a fit of rage after the Old Sire refused to let him take over Shadow Realm work, and attacked them. As the Traveler defeated him, Vasiliy lost his sight, asked Zmei to take him away, and died as the device shut down and the tunnel began to collapse.

**Aftermath.** The Luchkins gave the Traveler the refined Kristall key as a gift. Zmei says that the High Table will decide what to do with the Izmaylovs and that the Luchkins are busy with it.

## relations
### STANDARD
- **High Table**: the Izmaylovs are the largest of its gangs. Vasiliy held the Law and the High Table in contempt, which could have brought down the whole alliance if the Druzhna had found out.
- **Luchkins**: rivals turned enemies. The Luchkins oppose the Izmaylovs for breaking the Law, but Zmei, Iryna and Vasiliy grew up as siblings.
- **Energy Commission and Druzhna**: Vasiliy complained that the Izmaylovs got the dirty work these bodies did not want. Zmei says the Old Sire had strings to pull at the Commission, which is how the gangs came to know Shadow Realm secrets.
- **Fatui**: the abandoned Fatui base northwest of Snezhnograd later passed through several hands before the Izmaylovs got it.
### LARGE +
- **Remusov**: Lyubov, the head of House Remusov, is another of the Old Sire's adopted children and one of Vasiliy's siblings.

## reputation
### STANDARD
Among Snezhnayans, especially in Zolnyy, the Izmaylovs are feared as the biggest gang and hated for harassing debtors' families. Sofya calls them "blood-sucking insects" on the people, and the guards at the strongholds know to expect trouble from the Luchkins. Some members respected the Old Sire, and a short fae in Hunter on the Snowfields says the Izmaylovs get "their fair share, for Old Sire's sake".

## secrets
### ULTRA
Vasiliy killed the Old Sire, which the gang's guards will not discuss. The Izmaylovs hid a Shadow Realm machine and a nearly finished Kristall key in the Train Boneyard. The Butcher kept a box that held a map, which the Luchkins gave to the Traveler.

## entry: Vasiliy, the Young Sire

@keys2: Izmaylov, Luchkin, Snezhnaya, High Table
@version: 7.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Vasiliy Nikolayevich Izmaylov, the Young Sire, was the Old Sire's adopted son and the favourite of his four adopted children. The wiki gives his appearance in the fight as that of a Fanged Zealot. He believed the fae gangs were treated as dogs, resented his father's service to the Energy Commission, and planned to use the Shadow Realm to raise the Izmaylovs' standing. He killed his father when the Old Sire refused. In the Train Boneyard he fought Zmei, Iryna and the Traveler and died when his device shut down. His last words recalled being lost in the dark as a child and being found by Zmei and another sibling.

## entry: The Butcher
@keys: the Butcher, Butcher of Izmaylov
@keys2: Izmaylov, Gureva, Young Sire
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
The Butcher is Vasiliy's second-in-command, who ran a major Izmaylov stronghold in an old Fatui base northwest of Snezhnograd. Gureva infiltrated it as a spy and was held there for days, but she says the Butcher could not make her talk. He visited her himself, and she smiled at him, according to gossip among the guards. The Butcher is not shown in person in the quests found, and his fate is not stated.

## entry: Debt collection in Snezhnograd
@keys: Izmaylov debt collectors, Lindenovich, Marfa, Izmaylov debt
@keys2: Izmaylov, Snezhnograd, Luchkin
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
Under Vasiliy the Izmaylovs collected debts early and from people who could not pay. Lindenovich took Marfa's father away to work in a lumberyard for his gambling debts, then demanded payment from the girl and her grandmother, who sell dolls to make ends meet. The Traveler beat him in a duel to give them two days. Sofya says people like Marfa seek out the Luchkins, who can protect them but have little money to pay off their debts. The Luchkins claim that the gang does this to fund the Shadow Realm plan.

## entry: Train Boneyard
@keys: Train Boneyard
@keys2: Izmaylov, Vasiliy, Shadow Realm, Luchkin
@version: 7.0
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
The Train Boneyard is a local nickname for a half-excavated tunnel whose construction was halted after a Shadow Realm disaster collapsed it. Abandoned trains were later dumped there. Zmei, Iryna, Vasiliy and Lyubov played in it as children. Vasiliy chose it as the place to open the Shadow Realm, and used the nearby Izmaylov guards, a password and activation devices to guard it. In the finale, the Shadow Realm opened around Vasiliy's mechanism and the tunnel began to collapse. The Luchkins left by mine cart, and Zmei recalled how Vasiliy once hid in the deepest part of a collapsed tunnel during hide-and-seek.
