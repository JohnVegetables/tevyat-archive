## summary
### LITE
Ayn Al-Ahmar is a small Eremite mercenary brigade based in Port Ormos, whose members are fervent believers in King Deshret and hate Greater Lord Rukkhadevata. It is a subgroup of Deshret's Relics. Its leader Mizri bought a Divine Knowledge Capsule in a secret auction, convinced it held Deshret's power. Using it drove him mad, and the Matra took him away.
### STANDARD +
The name borrows "Al-Ahmar", which the Eremites treat as King Deshret's alleged true name. In the Archon Quest the Traveler meets two of its members, Tariq and Tumart. The group is poor, zealous and willing to scheme to raise the Mora for the capsule.

## structure
### STANDARD
Ayn Al-Ahmar is one brigade among the many under the banner of Deshret's Relics, whose headquarters issues orders to its subgroups. In practice the Port Ormos group acts on its own, with a boss, two named lieutenants who talk and negotiate for him, and a handful of rank-and-file mercenaries. Its fighters, such as crossbow users, sword-dancers and halberdiers, use ordinary Eremite combat styles.
### LARGE +
Rumour among Port Ormos locals says the group likes to set up at Djafar Tavern and will sell information on anything for half a million Mora, which is how the Traveler's party finds Tariq. No ranks beyond "boss" and "member" are named in canon.

## leadership
### LITE
Mizri leads the brigade. His followers call him "boss" and credit him with fervent devotion to King Deshret.
### STANDARD +
The Port Ormos business is run through Tariq, a talker who sells information, and Tumart, who speaks for the group on rivals. Both defer to Mizri, who appears in person only at the end of the Archon Quest. By then he is already deranged.

## members
### LITE
Named members are Mizri (leader), Tariq and Tumart.
### STANDARD +
**Tariq** is a smooth, hostile spokesman who takes 500,000 Mora from the Traveler and Paimon in exchange for a merchant's address. He is the brigade's loudest preacher and spews contempt for the Akademiya. **Tumart** appears alongside him, assesses rival brigades and shares Tariq's views. The wiki has no further profile for him.
### LARGE +
Both men fight in the confrontation that follows Tariq's challenge to Alhaitham. Tumart's group, with crossbow users, makes up the first wave, and Tariq's group, with a halberdier and a sword-dancer, the second. Neither is shown after being beaten.

## goals
### LITE
To bring King Deshret back. The brigade believes his power can be recovered, and that he will then punish Rukkhadevata's followers and the desert dwellers who forgot him.
### STANDARD +
Tariq tells the Traveler that Deshret founded the great desert nation of his people's ancestors, that he was the rightful God of Wisdom, and that Rukkhadevata betrayed him, took the title and destroyed his civilisation. He claims the voice of the Oracle has been heard in the desert, prophesying Deshret's resurrection. Members treat the Divine Knowledge Capsule as the vehicle for that return. They also refuse to let the Akademiya study it, because, in Alhaitham's words, that would tarnish their deity's soul.

## methods
### STANDARD
The brigade is short of funds but determined to get the capsule "by any means necessary". Alhaitham says they have used methods "more foul than fair" to raise the Mora. They bid in a secret auction held among the Port Ormos brigades. When challenged, Tariq settles matters with a duel at an agreed time and place rather than negotiating.
### LARGE +
Tariq answers Alhaitham's pressure with a challenge: a week's notice, the pier in front of the Pharos Lighthouse, at a fixed hour. Alhaitham says he had been deliberately sabotaging the brigade's business to force it into negotiating over the right to study the capsule.

## history
### STANDARD
In the Archon Quest chapter "Through Mists of Smoke and Forests Dark" (3.0), the Traveler and Paimon go to Port Ormos looking for an item the Akademiya lost, which is said to be linked to the gods. Several Eremite brigades were competing for it. Alhaitham explains that it is a Divine Knowledge Capsule that the brigades were bidding for in a secret auction. Dori's information shows that Mizri bought it. In the arranged duel, Tumart's and Tariq's men are beaten. Mizri arrives after using the capsule, mad, and attacks his own people. Alhaitham breaks his Akasha Terminal and the Matra arrest him. Alhaitham quietly keeps the capsule, and neither the Traveler nor the Matra notice.
### LARGE +
Before the duel, Tariq, Tumart and Alhaitham clash at Djafar Tavern. Tariq calls Alhaitham a lunatic and refuses to deal with him. Mizri's last words as he is taken away, "World... forget me", are the same ones the Traveler heard from Irminsul. Later, in Dehya's story quest "Shade Upon Red Silk" (3.5), Dehya names Ayn Al-Ahmar as one of the brigades under the Relics' banner.

## relations
### STANDARD
- **Deshret's Relics**: parent organisation. Dehya describes a group whose brigades are willing to cross all kinds of lines for Mora.
- **The Caracal Battalion**: rival Port Ormos brigade after the same capsule. Tariq and Tumart dismiss it as "money-grubbing opportunists".
- **Alhaitham**: hostile adversary who undermines their business and who ends up with the capsule.
- **The Akademiya**: hated by the group. Tariq believes it helped Rukkhadevata cover up her crime.
- **The Matra**: arrest Mizri. The capsule's buyers were to keep their win quiet to evade the Matra.
- **Corps of Thirty**: a Port Ormos local says the Citadel of Regzar is getting fed up with the group's shouting about Deshret.
### LARGE +
Dakan Al-Ahmar, led by Dehya's father Kusayla, is a different brigade that shares the "Al-Ahmar" name. Dehya says she has crossed paths with Ayn Al-Ahmar, but the two groups have no other tie in the quests checked.

## reputation
### STANDARD
Port Ormos locals hear them as noisy fanatics who keep shouting about Deshret's "resurrection". Alhaitham calls them poor but dogged. Dehya places them among the Relics, whose brigades she distrusts.

## secrets
### ULTRA
The one established secret is the auction itself: the capsule was bought in a covert deal, and the winning brigade was not to announce it. Dori photographed the transaction with a Kamera and sold the picture to the Traveler's party.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 nothing is known about the brigade's present state. Its leader was arrested in 3.0, and its parent group is described as dismantled.

## entry: Mizri
@keys: Mizri
@version: 3.0
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Mizri is the leader of Ayn Al-Ahmar and a devout believer in King Deshret. He bought the Divine Knowledge Capsule in Port Ormos. After using it, he went mad, attacked his own men and was subdued by Alhaitham, who destroyed his Akasha Terminal. The Matra took him away, and he muttered "World... forget me." Beyond a flashback in a later quest, he has no other canon appearance.
### LARGE +
The Akasha Terminal in his ear turned red and then dark during the breakdown. The wiki gives him no backstory.

## entry: Tariq and Tumart
@keys: Tariq, Tumart
@keys2: Ayn Al-Ahmar, Port Ormos, Eremite, Eremites
@version: 3.0
### STANDARD
Tariq and Tumart are the brigade's talkers at Djafar Tavern. Tariq takes 500,000 Mora from the Traveler for an address, adds a sermon about King Deshret and challenges Alhaitham to a duel. Tumart fills in the brigade's view of the Caracal Battalion and the rival bidders. Both are beaten in the duel.

## entry: The Divine Knowledge Capsule affair
@keys: Divine Knowledge Capsule, glowing red capsule
@keys2: Ayn Al-Ahmar, Mizri, Port Ormos
@version: 3.0
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
A glowing red Divine Knowledge Capsule that the Akademiya lost in the desert was contested by several Eremite brigades in Port Ormos. Alhaitham negotiated for the right to study it. Ayn Al-Ahmar refused, bid and won. After Mizri used it he went mad, which Alhaitham likened to the researchers who lose their minds after taking knowledge from Irminsul. The Matra took Mizri, but Alhaitham took the capsule. The quest does not say what it contained.

## entry: Al-Ahmar
@keys: Al-Ahmar
@keys2: King Deshret, Deshret, Eremite, Eremites, Dakan Al-Ahmar
@version: 3.0
### STANDARD
"Al-Ahmar" is treated by the Eremites as King Deshret's alleged true name. Both Ayn Al-Ahmar in Port Ormos and Dakan Al-Ahmar, which was led by Dehya's father Kusayla, took it for their names. They are separate brigades with separate aims.
