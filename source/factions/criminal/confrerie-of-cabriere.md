## summary
### LITE
The Confrerie of Cabriere was a merchants' guild in Fontaine, founded and led by Marcel. Publicly it was one of the richest and most powerful trading guilds in the nation and a friendly sister body to the Spina di Rosula. In truth it was a front for Marcel's underground trade in Sinthe, which paid for his experiments. It was dissolved after Marcel was exposed in the 4.0 Archon Quest.
### STANDARD +
Callas and the Spina di Rosula helped it grow when it was only reselling everyday goods. Its Sinthe business was wiped out with the guild, though a successor "Imitation Sinthe" trade later appeared.

## structure
### STANDARD
Marcel led and founded the guild. The wiki names two other people tied to it: Calcagni, a staff member (found as an open-world NPC in the Court of Fontaine, listed as formerly of the Confrerie), and Truand, an enforcer who collected debts. Its shops were built to a single design with a plain, approachable look, so that customers of every budget would feel welcome. The same buildings carried anti-theft features: walls that would warp if hammered repeatedly and a claimed 100-year rustproof finish.
### LARGE +
The guild owned a countryside estate that once hosted a large banquet. Marcel also kept a large number of privately owned Gardemeks, which the wiki says his wealth made possible.

## goals
@version: 4.0
### STANDARD
In public, trade and commerce. In reality, to bankroll Marcel's experiments with Primordial Seawater. Marcel's deeper aim was personal, not commercial, and belongs to his own story.

## methods
@version: 4.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Marcel sold Sinthe, a drug made from diluted Primordial Seawater that causes euphoria and pleasant hallucinations. It is highly addictive, can cause lasting problems with focus and emotional control, and brings withdrawal symptoms of fatigue, anxiety and paranoia. Many families were ruined by it. By the time of his arrest he had even made a fruit-flavoured version. Marcel protected the operation by removing threats. He framed Callas for murder after she banned Sinthe in Poisson and found its production base.
### LARGE +
The Confrerie's front business gave the trade a legitimate face and a reason to move goods and money. Truand's debt collecting was part of how it applied pressure.

## history
@version: 4.0
### STANDARD
At first the Confrerie only resold daily goods, with support from Callas and the Spina di Rosula, and grew into one of the richest guilds in Fontaine. Marcel later began the Sinthe trade. When Callas, the head of the Spina di Rosula, banned Sinthe in Poisson and tracked down the production base, Marcel framed her for murder, which led to her death in a duel against Champion Duelist Clorinde. In the Chapter IV act As Light Rain Falls Without Reason, the Traveler and Navia exposed Marcel as the culprit behind the serial disappearances, and he died in the final quest of that act. Authorities then stopped Sinthe production and began confiscating what remained on the market.
### ULTRA +
In Navia's story quest Braving the Tides Together (4.3), a former Spina member named Florent tells her that, since Marcel was exposed, the Confrerie of Cabriere is no more, along with its Sinthe production. The final batch of Sinthe was seized around the time of the first Fontinalia Film Festival. A successor "Imitation Sinthe" trade, linked to ingredients with many ordinary uses, was dealt with in the later Checks and Cats event (4.5).

## relations
### STANDARD
- **Spina di Rosula**: the Confrerie was regarded as its sister organisation. Callas led the Spina and supported the Confrerie early on, and her daughter is Navia.
- **Marcel**: founder and leader, whose double life the guild hid.
- **Navia and the Traveler**: exposed the scheme.
- **Clorinde**: the Champion Duelist who fought Callas in the duel Marcel arranged.

## reputation
@version: 4.0
### STANDARD
Before the exposure the Confrerie was known as one of the richest and most powerful merchant guilds in Fontaine, and as an honest sister to the Spina di Rosula. Afterwards its name is linked with the Sinthe scandal.

## secrets
@version: 4.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Its Sinthe business and Marcel's identity were hidden until the Chapter IV investigation. Marcel was really Vacher, an adventurer from Snezhnaya who took on a new identity.

## status
### LITE
Disbanded. The Confrerie ended when Marcel was exposed in 4.0.
### STANDARD +
As of 7.1 no revival of the Confrerie was found. Canon only mentions it in the past tense, although Imitation Sinthe smuggling (4.5) shows the drug's market outlasted it.

## entry: Sinthe
@keys: Sinthe, Imitation Sinthe
@keys2: Fontaine, Primordial Sea, Marcel, Confrerie
@version: 4.0
### STANDARD
Sinthe is a drink made from Primordial Seawater diluted with ordinary water. It gives short-term euphoria and pleasant hallucinations, then erodes concentration and emotional control, and stopping brings fatigue, anxiety and paranoia. It is highly addictive and wrecked many families. Marcel made and sold it to fund his experiments, and the Confrerie of Cabriere was its commercial front. Production ended after his arrest. Later, "Imitation Sinthe" appeared to mimic its effects, made from ingredients that have many ordinary uses, and the Traveler helped the Special Security and Surveillance Patrol expose a smuggling ring in the 4.5 event Checks and Cats.

## entry: Truand and Calcagni
@keys: Truand, Calcagni
@keys2: Confrerie of Cabriere, Fontaine, Marcel
@version: 4.0
### LARGE
Truand is a quest-only Fontainian enforcer for the Confrerie who is shown collecting debts, including from a woman named Estelle. The wiki says these debts were later tied to the Gardemek force used against Navia. Calcagni is an open-world NPC in the Court of Fontaine's Vasari Passage, labelled as a former member of the Confrerie's staff; canon says nothing more about the role.
