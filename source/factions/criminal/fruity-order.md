## summary
### LITE
The Fruity Order is a Treasure Hoarder group that operates in Fontaine and Nod-Krai. It is run by an unnamed boss, with Five Great Operatives who carry fruit and plant codenames, and several dozen other members. Betrayal is punishable by death. Its members claim the order exists to keep order and balance in the world. They are more eccentric than most Treasure Hoarders.
### STANDARD +
Only three of the Five Great Operatives remain: "Guileless Wolfhook", "Ruthless Calla Lily" and "Trendy Zaytun Peach". "Tricky Bulle Fruit" and "Intrepid Valberry" have left.

## structure
### STANDARD
At the top is the boss, unnamed. Beneath the boss are the Five Great Operatives: Zaytun Peach, Bulle Fruit, Wolfhook, Valberry and Calla Lily, each with a title (Trendy, Tricky, Guileless, Intrepid, Ruthless). Below them are several dozen ordinary members. The order belongs to the wider Treasure Hoarders.
### LARGE +
The same five codenames appear in the Godfather's original order, in the Nod-Krai lore, but canon does not say if the modern order is that group's descendant.

## members
@version: 4.1
### STANDARD
- **"Guileless Wolfhook"**, **"Ruthless Calla Lily"** and **"Trendy Zaytun Peach"**: the remaining operatives. Peach loves trendy slang, mocks outdated ideas ("Ley Lines are outdated") and hands out new names to people, such as calling Paimon the "Trendy Flyer". Wolfhook is polite and friendly while accepting majority votes against his own side, saying "This is how the world works". Calla Lily fights the Traveler in Villains and is the one who speaks for the order in the 6.5 event.
- **"Tricky Bulle Fruit"**: left the order after the Villains quest. He keeps a younger sister, Blanche, and is later found in the Fortress of Meropide.
- **"Intrepid Valberry"**: expelled or left earlier, then joined a different group.
- **Lakkaberry**: a fan who is not a member (see below).
### LARGE +
Lakkaberry applied for a booth at the Nod-Krai Trade Expo under the Fruity Order's name, learned of it through the "New Tide Anthology" and tales of Valberry heard in the Fortress of Meropide, and has no real standing in the order.

## goals
@version: 4.1
### STANDARD
The order says it exists to keep order and balance. In practice the quests show the members pursuing valuable potion formulas and pursuing those they see as traitors.

## methods
### STANDARD
Theft, threats and intimidation. Members use slang and rank titles, settled one dispute in Villains by a vote, and treat betrayal as a capital offence. They hunted Bulle Fruit as a traitor over a potion formula, as seen in Villains. They have won the Treasure Hoarder booth at the Nod-Krai Trade Expo several years running, and in the latest expo they removed rival groups before they could cause trouble for a lookalike.
### LARGE +
A weapon-lore story claims the original order's creed is that theft alone can balance an unjust world, and that its leader recruited three rival thieves into it by having them swallow fruits.

## history
@version: 4.1
### STANDARD
In Villains (4.1) the Traveler and Paimon meet Blanche, who is being threatened by Zaytun Peach and Wolfhook over her brother, Bulle Fruit, whom they call a traitor for keeping the "Pure Water" potion formula to himself. The Traveler helps Blanche and Bulle Fruit, and repels Calla Lily, who has come for him. By the end Bulle Fruit has left the order and later appears in the Fortress of Meropide. The Red and the Black (4.2) then shows Valberry's diary, which says he left the order and joined another group, the Elegant Tableware Confrerie, whose boss later fired him. In the follow-up Questions and Answers, Valberry is an NPC. In the Nod-Krai content, the Problem-Sorting Robot quest has the order steal "Curve"'s core from the Fatui and hide it in an Aino prospector robot, leading to Curve's rebuilding as Chard Lombro. The wiki infers that Wolfhook, Calla Lily and Zaytun Peach did this.
### ULTRA +
In the limited-time event Where Waves Meet the Reef (6.5, 2026-04-27 to 2026-05-14), the tale "The Fruity Order is Here" has a self-declared member, Lakkaberry, claim the order's Expo booth. Calla Lily arrives and says she belongs to the real order, which had already stepped back from the expo that year to avoid fights with other Treasure Hoarder groups. She tells the event organiser Jahoda that the order has kept the rival factions in check, and warns her to look after the young exhibitors Ati, Neb and Raya. She orders Lakkaberry to meet her at Nasha Town's harbour once the expo is over.

## relations
### STANDARD
- **Treasure Hoarders**: it belongs to this wider group of thieves, but behaves as a distinct organisation with its own codenames and rules.
- **Fatui**: the order took Curve's core from them, according to the wiki's reading of the Problem-Sorting Robot quest.
- **Voynich Guild and the Nod-Krai Trade Expo**: the Guild's rules forbid trouble at the expo, and Calla Lily says so in her dealings with the organisers.
- **Traveler**: met Wolfhook, Zaytun Peach, Bulle Fruit and Calla Lily in Villains.

## reputation
@version: 6.5
### STANDARD
Calla Lily says the order has proven untouchable so far, and a fan such as Lakkaberry wants to join it. The Curatorium of Secrets suspects the group may be outsiders to the area.

## secrets
### STANDARD
The identity of the boss is not known. Calla Lily's pursuit of Bulle Fruit is explained by Valberry's diary as an order from "higher-ups".

## status
@version: 6.5
### LITE
Active but diminished: three of five operatives remain, and the boss is unnamed.
### STANDARD +
As of the 6.5 event Calla Lily was still acting on the order's behalf in Nod-Krai. No Fruity Order content was found for 6.7 to 7.1.

## entry: Fruity Order operatives

@keys2: Fruity Order, Order of the Fruit, Treasure Hoarders
@version: 4.1
### STANDARD
The Five Great Operatives of the Fruity Order are known by a title and a fruit or plant name. Guileless Wolfhook is the soft-spoken one who supports majority votes. Ruthless Calla Lily fights in Villains and later speaks for the order at the expo. Trendy Zaytun Peach is the slang-loving one. Tricky Bulle Fruit held the "Pure Water" formula, left the order and is later found in Meropide. Intrepid Valberry was removed from the order and later worked for a different group.

## entry: Godfather of Nod-Krai and the original order
@keys: Soini, Godfather, Blackmarrow Lantern
@keys2: Nod-Krai, Fruity Order, black market, Treasure Hoarders
@version: 6.0
### LARGE
Weapon lore for the Blackmarrow Lantern and the Prospector's Shovel describes Soini, a poor farmer after the cataclysm who found buried treasure and became the feared Godfather of Nod-Krai's black market. Three rival thieves, a fae, a young woman and a hunter, failed to beat his vault. He had each swallow a fruit (Bulle Fruit, Wolfhook and Valberry) to join his creed that theft alone could rebalance a corrupt world. His butler then turned on him, seized the master thief's relic, left the Calla Lily fruit behind as an antidote and spared his life because he had spread the thieves' ideals. The wiki also lists Nashchokin, Schanzkowska and Dmitri Dubrovich Sukhotin as the first holders of the Bulle Fruit, Wolfhook and Valberry codenames.

## entry: Lakkaberry
@keys: Lakkaberry
@keys2: Fruity Order, Nod-Krai Trade Expo, Expo
@version: 6.5
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
Lakkaberry is an enthusiast in the 6.5 event Where Waves Meet the Reef who admires the Fruity Order and applied for an expo booth in its name. He is not a member. The order's real operative Calla Lily appears, tells him to meet her at the Nasha Town harbour after the expo. She says the order itself dealt with the rival Treasure Hoarders angered by his stunt.
