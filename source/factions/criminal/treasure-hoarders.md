## summary
### LITE
The Treasure Hoarders are a gang of bandits who hunt treasure in ruins and domains all over Teyvat and get rough with bystanders who happen to be nearby. They are poorly organised because the groups are so far apart. The gang's official description credits a founder called Reed Miller, who called it "an organization of mutual cooperation." The group is said to have originated in Nod-Krai. Members join over crime or lack of steady income, and they tend to burn through loot fast and stay poor.
### STANDARD +
Members use a raven (Ravenwing) motif, and most wear a mask to hide their faces from the law. The few unmasked ones are either important or bait to draw victims into traps. Not all members get along, and some are looked down on more than others. The Nod-Krai content shows that the gang's roots were more idealistic than its present: a thief who wanted a world without borders.
### LARGE +
The name Reed Miller turns out to have belonged to several different people, so the "founder" is both a legend and a succession of real, dead individuals. The modern gangs live off that name.

## structure
### STANDARD
There is no known central command or fixed headquarters. Each region has its own groups, some led by a named boss such as Freckle Huang in Liyue. The wiki describes them as disorganised, mainly because of the distances between groups. Some members belong to sub-groups of the wider organisation, such as the Fruity Order in Fontaine. In Nod-Krai several groups follow in the footsteps of their founder.
### LARGE +
Rank shows in who is unmasked and who holds an elite post. In the Traveler's fights the common types are scouts, marksmen, seamen, handymen, crushers, gravediggers, pugilists and four kinds of Potioneer (alchemists who throw Cryo, Electro, Hydro or Pyro concoctions). Named elites include Raptor, Carmen, Liuliu, Wallenstein and "Boss". Under Alia, Tolfin says in the 6.3 quest, the gang became "a highly disciplined, quasi-military force that almost resembled a religious order," whose mantra was that people should not put their faith in gods.

## leadership
### STANDARD
Today no overall leader is named. Regional bosses include Freckle Huang (Liyue, defeated in a Guizhong quest) and "Boss" or "Big Sis" in Liyue, who turns out to be Yan'er. The legendary leaders are the three holders of the Reed Miller name: Avrelian, Alia and Brahe, all long dead. See the entries on each. Hiljetta, the "Black-Barreled Hag," is a retired but renowned Hoarder in Nod-Krai who wants to restore the gang's old glory.
### LARGE +
Others have taken the name since, and some only borrow it: in the 6.0 quest The Shoemaker's Children Go Barefoot, a thief named Netochka fancies herself the next Reed Miller. Jahoda named her chic badger companion Reed Miller XIII.

## members
### STANDARD
The wiki lists many members by region. A few are worth knowing.
- **Mondstadt**: small-time thieves such as Tyler and Nimrod (an informant to Kaeya), plus the elite Raptor.
- **Liyue**: Freckle Huang (leader), Yan'er ("Boss"), Carmen, Liuliu and Wallenstein. Liyue is the gang's main haunt, including beneath the Chasm.
- **Inazuma**: Shun, Kojirou, Koichirou and others.
- **Sumeru**: Narzam, Pabag and others.
- **Fontaine**: the Grand Thief and several nicknamed members.
- **Natlan**: Coztic and Matthias.
- **Nod-Krai**: Hiljetta, Kaleva, Basu and Hoyka.
### LARGE +
Former members include Jilliana, who left the gang secretly to raise a family with Rudolf and became a nun in Mondstadt's Church of Favonius, and Jahoda, whom the wiki lists as a former member (she is formerly of the Seahook Gang and now works with the Curatorium of Secrets and the Northern Intelligence Network). Tolfin, Hiljetta's grandson, is a travelling storyteller and merchant and is also on the former-members list.

## goals
### STANDARD
Most members want treasure and a living. Their reasons for joining vary, but the wiki says they are usually crime or a lack of stable income. The idealistic founders had a bigger aim, an "Elysium" for the poor and hungry built on the wealth of the powerful. Hiljetta and others in Nod-Krai want to claim Reed Miller's hidden treasure and restore the gang's reputation.

## methods
### STANDARD
Treasure Hoarders sack ruins, domains and chests, ambush travelers, and get rough with anyone who stumbles on them. In Liyue the leader called "Boss" leaves false trails and notes so that curious adventurers walk into ambushes. When beaten, they throw smoke bombs and vanish. Masks hide identity. Some groups in Fontaine use rogue or stolen Clockwork Meka, and some groups travel with a Sumpter Beast.
### LARGE +
Regional partnerships are opportunistic. In Inazuma some work with the Nobushi, and one member colluded with the Fatui over the Electro Hypostasis in Kannazuka. In Sumeru they sometimes work with the Fatui and the Eremites. In Natlan they cooperate with tribal warriors in robbery and saurian trafficking. A sub-group introduced in 6.0 uses Fatui-style and Natlan-style weapons.

## history
### STANDARD
**Origin legend.** Nod-Krai folklore says the gang was founded by Reed Miller, an infamous master thief who buried a "great treasure" in the land that became Nod-Krai. The real story is told in quests of 6.3 and is covered in the entries below.

**Founding.** About 500 years ago, Avrelian, the son of a Snezhnayan officer and a Frostmoon Scion mother, graduated from the Snezhnayan military academy, "died in the line of duty" on paper shortly after becoming a non-commissioned officer, and turned to banditry. He raised the black raven emblem, gathered the forsaken and despised, and preached a world without borders. He was manipulated by Rotwang into stealing the Sampo Mill, then gave himself up in Snezhnograd hoping to speak to the Tsar. He was executed without ever meeting the Tsar.
### LARGE +
**Alia and Brahe.** Alia took the mask and reorganised the gang. In a single night her force emptied the governor's palace of its ill-gotten wealth and shared it among the poor of Nasha Town. Brahe, the governor's adopted son, saw the deed and fell in love with her at first sight. Alia died stopping Rotwang's scheme during the Cataclysm, and Brahe became the third Reed Miller.

**Legend-making.** Seeing people commit cruelty in the name of Reed Miller's ideals, Brahe spread deliberately contradictory tales of Reed Miller, and set up the Great Treasure and the Proofs of the Cognoscenti, so that anyone abusing the name would look absurd.
### ULTRA +
**Modern era.** In the Traveler's time the Hoarders are low-level criminals in most nations. Beidou's Crux terrifies Liyue's group, Kaeya has an informant in Mondstadt's, and in Nod-Krai the Reed Miller legend drives the world-quest series East of the Moon, West of the Sun (6.0-6.3), ending with The Raven's Legacy in 6.3. Earlier in that series, Netochka tried to steal a Proof of the Cognoscenti from the Fatui's Kuuvahki Experimental Design Bureau.

## relations
### LARGE
- **Fatui**: sometimes allies of convenience in Inazuma and Sumeru. In Nod-Krai, Fatui agents accosted Hiljetta and she easily dealt with them. Netochka targeted a Fatui facility.
- **Crux / Beidou**: Liyue's Hoarders fear meeting Beidou and try to placate her.
- **Abyss Order**: a team of Hoarders tried to rob it and failed, as shown through the Grand Thief.
- **Eremites, Nobushi, tribal warriors**: occasional partners in the regions where they operate.
- **Frostmoon Scions and Snezhnaya**: Avrelian had Frostmoon blood, and Alia was a Scion by birth; both tangled with Rotwang and with the Snezhnayan state.
### ULTRA +
- **Church of Favonius**: Jilliana left the gang and joined the Church. The Mondstadt group is small and causes only infrequent trouble.
- **Adventurers' Guild**: Ihanti put a bounty with the Guild against Kaleva and his two followers in They Who Abandoned the Past.
- **Seahook Gang**: a different organisation; Jahoda, a former Seahook member, is also listed by the wiki as a former Treasure Hoarder.

## reputation
### ULTRA
Public attitudes vary. In most nations the Hoarders are an annoyance or a menace. In Nod-Krai the founder is a folk hero whose legend inspires imitators. According to the wiki, Fontaine's group has a fairly high reputation: Hoarders from Mondstadt and Liyue turned to the Grand Thief, who is said to be from Fontaine, to help with a raid on the Abyss Order. Hiljetta is known among Hoarders as a renowned fighter.

## secrets
@version: 6.3
@spoiler: major
### ULTRA
The Reed Miller everyone tells stories about was three people: Avrelian, Alia and Brahe. Brahe made up much of the legend on purpose. Avrelian was used by Rotwang, who wanted the Sampo Mill and flattered him with a "chosen one" destiny; Avrelian was later executed in Snezhnograd. Alia, a Frostmoon Scion twin, gave her life at the Pillar of Embla, and local legend turned her into a fae. She helps the Traveler recover the Silver Lampwick in the Nightingale's Song quests. Hiljetta and Tolfin are Brahe's descendants.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the Treasure Hoarders remain active, scattered local gangs across every nation. No 7.0-7.1 story content about them was found. Their last major appearance is in the Nod-Krai content: the Reed Miller legend, Hiljetta and the Proofs of the Cognoscenti (6.3).
### STANDARD +
The 6.3 quest series revealed the truth about Reed Miller. Tolfin gave the Traveler a raven insignia (which he believes Avrelian left behind) as a keepsake, and Hiljetta wants the Traveler to visit for further sparring matches.

## entry: Reed Miller
@keys: Reed Miller, Reed Miller XIII
@version: 6.3
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
In Nod-Krai folklore, Reed Miller is the Treasure Hoarders' founder and an infamous master thief who buried a great treasure. In truth the name was worn by at least three people: Avrelian (first), Alia (second) and Brahe (third). Brahe wrote many conflicting legends under other names, such as "Brahe the Bard" and "Grand Duke Trubetskoy," so that nobody could use the name seriously. Popular tellings are collected in the book The Legends of Reed Miller, Thief Extraordinaire. Others have adopted the name since; Jahoda named her chic badger partner Reed Miller XIII, and Netochka dreams of being the next Reed Miller.

## entry: Avrelian
@keys: Avrelian, Relya
@version: 6.3
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Avrelian, nicknamed Relya, was the first Reed Miller and the Treasure Hoarders' founder. He was the son of a Frostmoon Scion Maiden and a Snezhnayan officer, and after his parents died he was adopted by an officer named Mikhnenko. He attended the Snezhnayan military academy and befriended Solovei, with whom he went treasure hunting; on one such hunt a young Brahe, the governor's adopted son, told them where the treasure was. Shortly after becoming a non-commissioned officer he faked his death. Avrelian dreamed of an Elysium for the poor and founded the gang under a raven emblem. Rotwang told him of his Frostmoon blood and a destiny as ruler of Elysium and got him to steal the Sampo Mill. Persuaded by Solovei to explain himself to the Belyi Tsar, he was arrested without meeting the Tsar and executed in public, in view of Solovei and Alia.

## entry: Alia
@keys: Alia
@keys2: Reed Miller, Treasure Hoarders, Nod-Krai, Aila
@version: 6.3
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Alia was the second Reed Miller and Aila's older twin sister. Their adoptive grandmother Lovia, the Frostmoon Scions' Archpriestess, raised them in seclusion on Hiisi Island and had them kill other Scions and drink their blood. Alia escaped, apparently after Avrelian stole the Sampo Mill, and joined the Treasure Hoarders. After Avrelian's execution she took his mask and reorganised the gang, raiding the governor's palace for Nasha Town's poor. She and Solovei worked to stop Rauthvangi's plan to use the Pillar of Embla to pierce the false sky, and she gave her life by casting herself into the Sampo Mill and igniting it. Her last wish was that her sister live a life of happiness and freedom. Local legend made her a fae.

## entry: Brahe
@keys: Brahe, Brahe Trubetskoy
@keys2: Reed Miller, Treasure Hoarders, Nod-Krai, Governor
@version: 6.3
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Brahe was the adoptive son of Prokhor Trubetskoy, governor of Nod-Krai, and later the last governor himself, and the third Reed Miller. He regarded Avrelian and Alia as true idealists and himself as a realist who understood human malice. After the Cataclysm he faked his death in the records and, over years, seeded contradictory legends about Reed Miller, including an invented companion named "Wagtail." He built the Great Treasure and later turned it into the Proof of the Cognoscenti trial, a test of wit rather than greed. A former academy instructor, Sukhotin, once found the vault, mistook him for a thief and stood down on recognising Avrelian's gauntlet.

## entry: Hiljetta and Tolfin
@keys: Hiljetta, Black-Barreled Hag, Black-Barrel Hag, Tolfin
@version: 6.3
### STANDARD
Hiljetta is a very old woman living in an alcove in Wavechaser Plain and a retired Treasure Hoarder known as the Black-Barreled Hag for her fighting skill. She wants to bring back the gang's glory days under Reed Miller. She holds the last Proof of the Cognoscenti and gives it only to someone who will restore the legacy. She resents Brahe, her ancestor, for cheapening Reed Miller's legacy. After the Traveler beats her in a spar she hands it over. Her grandson Tolfin is a wandering storyteller and merchant who steered the Traveler to her, has a special bond with Weasel Thieves and travels with a weasel named Huginn. Hiljetta scolded him for not telling the Traveler that they were all linked to the gang.

## entry: Liyue Treasure Hoarders
@keys: Freckle Huang, Yan'er, Big Sis of the South, Wallenstein, Carmen, Raptor
@keys2: Treasure Hoarders, Liyue
### STANDARD
Liyue is where the Treasure Hoarders are most visible. Freckle Huang leads the group in a Guizhong quest and is defeated there. Notes in several world quests refer to a female "Boss" who lays false treasure trails to lure adventurers into ambushes; she is later revealed to be Yan'er, also called "Big Sis." Yan'er is a childhood friend of Hiromi and Shun; after the Sakoku Decree she and Shun went treasure hunting in Liyue. She tried to plunder ruins south of Wuwang Hill during The Chi of Yore, and later teamed with Raptor on a raid on the Abyss Order. They hired the Grand Thief, who died. Beidou of the Crux frightens Liyue's Hoarders.

## entry: Grand Thief
@keys: Grand Thief
@keys2: Treasure Hoarders, Abyss Order, Osial
### STANDARD
The Grand Thief is a popular Hoarder, said to come from Fontaine. After Osial was defeated, a group of Hoarders banded together to distract the Abyss Order while the Grand Thief slipped in to steal valuables. The Traveler and Paimon find the defiled statue and his kneeling corpse in Involuntary Sacrifice (Archon Quest Chapter I, Act IV, We Will Be Reunited). The cause of his death is unknown.

## entry: Fontaine Treasure Hoarders
@keys: Fruity Order, Clockwork Meka thieves
@keys2: Treasure Hoarders, Fontaine
### STANDARD
Fontaine's Hoarders roam the countryside in groups. Some use rogue or stolen Clockwork Meka for mining or guarding. Others belong to sub-groups such as the Fruity Order. Some members have crossed the sea to Chenyu Vale. According to the wiki their standing seems fairly high.

## entry: Treasure Hoarders in other nations
@keys: Nobushi, Kannazuka, Sumpter Beast, Sauroform Tribal Warrior
@keys2: Treasure Hoarders, Hoarders
### LARGE
In Mondstadt the Hoarders have little presence but cause trouble occasionally. In Inazuma some cooperate with the Nobushi, and one colluded with the Fatui over the slumbering hypostasis in Kannazuka. In Sumeru they sometimes work with the Fatui and the Eremites, and a few groups in Liyue travel with a Sumpter Beast. In Natlan they have far less influence than elsewhere, cooperating occasionally with tribal warriors in robbery and saurian trafficking.

## entry: Jilliana
@keys: Jilliana
@keys2: Treasure Hoarders, Mondstadt, Lily, Rudolf
### LARGE
Jilliana was a Treasure Hoarder until she met and married Rudolf and had a daughter, Lily. Having a family made her see where she was heading, so she left the gang in secret, joined the Church of Favonius and became a nun at the Favonius Cathedral. In A Surprise Gift the Traveler helps Rudolf pick a gift for her. According to the wiki, she is also the source of the remark that Hoarders burn through money quickly, leaving a cycle of wealth and poverty.

## entry: Proofs of the Cognoscenti
@keys: Proof of the Cognoscenti, Proofs of the Cognoscenti, Reed Miller's Great Treasure
@version: 6.3
@spoiler: minor
### LARGE
The Great Treasure was Brahe's invention to keep the Reed Miller name harmless. It became the Proof of the Cognoscenti, a set of pieces and trials. The Traveler collects four pieces, and the last is held by Hiljetta, who tests the Traveler first. Netochka tried to steal one from the Fatui's Kuuvahki Experimental Design Bureau, and Tolfin gave the Traveler the Stratagema-Cognoscenti's Mask after testing the Shesternin heirs. At the site the Traveler opens several doors, defeats a Radiant Lunapod guardian and finds a mysterious ship.

### ULTRA
In They Who Abandoned the Past, Kaleva and two followers stole the bag of a terminally ill man named Ihanti, thinking he had died, though he was alive. Ihanti put a bounty with the Adventurers' Guild. Jahoda, who knew Kaleva, took the job with the Traveler. The talk turned into a fight, and the two easily won. She gave Kaleva money to see a doctor and learned about Ihanti's illness. Kaleva remarked that she is "scary smart one moment, unbelievably naive the next."
