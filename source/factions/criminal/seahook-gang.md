## summary
### LITE
The Seahook Gang was a gang of thieves and bandits that once held real sway in Nod-Krai, centred on Nasha Town. It no longer exists in its original form: its boss was killed by his own men and the rest scattered. Today the name is used by impostors who want its intimidating reputation. The best-known former member is Jahoda, now an employee of Nefer's Curatorium of Secrets.
### STANDARD +
Almost everything the world knows of the gang comes from Jahoda's memories, since she joined when it was already established. She describes it as a hard place where making friends was foolish, though some members looked after one another in their own way.

## structure
### STANDARD
The gang had a boss, an unnamed man spoken of with a mixture of respect and exasperation, and mid-level leaders such as Zinaida, who brought Jahoda in. Members included skilled earners like Ihanti, who relied on persuasion rather than brute force. Most of the rank and file were thieves who drank, feasted and fought together. The base was a house in Nasha Town where Jahoda had her own room as a child.
### LARGE +
The Heizou anecdote relays a rumour that the gang took in exiled criminals from across Teyvat, which fits a hideout where nobody asked about the past. No ranks, headcount or formal organisation are given beyond "boss" and "mid-level leader".

## leadership
### STANDARD
The original boss is never named. He is shown as shrewd but fond of his youngest member: he coached Jahoda on how to quiet a rowdy crowd, took her stray apple in the middle of a negotiation, and docked her share when she secretly returned a necklace. One of his sayings was "Grant rest to those who seek it", a code Jahoda and Ihanti both remember. Zinaida, who once protected him, later calls him a blood-soaked bandit chief.

## members
### STANDARD
- **Jahoda**: recruited as a child by Zinaida; now an employee of Nefer's Curatorium of Secrets and a playable character. Her own entry carries her story.
- **Zinaida**: a former mid-level leader who brought Jahoda into the gang. According to the intel Nefer holds, she now works for the Voynich Guild as a treasure hunter and covert bodyguard of the port supervisor Manez.
- **Ihanti**: a skilled earner and smooth talker who treated Jahoda as a sidekick. In the Intel Quest "They Who Abandoned the Past" (6.2) he is dying, and he is dead by the end of it.
- **Ratfan**: the wiki lists him as formerly of the gang, but canon presents him as a rich merchant who bribed members to kill their boss. Jahoda killed him.
### LARGE +
Other former members are unnamed. Some died, some left Nod-Krai for other nations, and some stayed in Nasha Town and quietly put the past behind them.

## goals
### STANDARD
The gang lived by theft and banditry, according to Jahoda. Ihanti stood out from "dumb scams and petty robberies" by getting victims to hand money over willingly. Beyond profit, no ideology is stated.

## methods
### STANDARD
Theft, robbery and swindling, plus rough "business deals" with rich targets. In the one job shown in detail (Jahoda's Character Story 2), the gang captured a merchant known as a black-hearted swindler, made him repay his victims and then split the rest among themselves at an all-night feast.
### LARGE +
In that story the merchant begged for a pendant, saying it held the only picture of his wife and daughter. The veteran thieves took it for the usual ploy, since prisoners often plead for an item that hides a clue to treasure. Jahoda secretly returned it anyway and lost her share as the price.

## history
### STANDARD
**Rise.** Nothing is known of the founding. By the time Jahoda arrived the gang was powerful enough to be called a hidden force that once controlled Nasha Town.

**Fall.** Jahoda's Character Story 3 says the rich merchant Ratfan bribed members to assassinate their boss, then burned the evidence with oil. The gang fell into chaos and scattered. In a looser account in the Heizou anecdote, Jahoda says the old leader ended up thrown overboard while underlings fought over the scraps.

**Aftermath.** Jahoda stalked Ratfan over many nights, was badly wounded, and eventually killed him. Nefer then took her on as an employee. By Jahoda's story, Nefer had told her where to find Ratfan and saw her as a pawn who might remove a troublesome man.
### LARGE +
**Impostors.** Some time after the breakup, others began using the Seahook name to scam and rob people. In the anecdote "The Crimes of Others", Shikanoin Heizou came to Nod-Krai tracking a fugitive from Inazuma who had joined these self-styled Seahooks. Hired through the Curatorium of Secrets, Jahoda told him they were not the original crew.
### ULTRA +
**Ihanti.** In "They Who Abandoned the Past" (6.2), Jahoda takes up an old comrade's bounty at the Adventurers' Guild and, with the Traveler, recovers his belongings from a thug. She finds Ihanti ill and nearly blind. In keeping with the old code she honours his wish for no doctor, and when she returns he has died, leaving a letter saying that people like him have no stories worth leaving behind.

## relations
### STANDARD
- **Nefer and the Curatorium of Secrets**: Nefer employs Jahoda and, in Jahoda's story, pointed her at Ratfan. Nefer keeps watch on what remains of the gang.
- **Voynich Guild**: employs Zinaida as a treasure hunter and bodyguard.
- **Treasure Hoarders**: Nefer once offered Jahoda introductions to more reliable Treasure Hoarders as a career option, and Jahoda refused.
### LARGE +
- **Marechaussee Phantom**: in a Nefer dialogue, Nefer asks Lestocq to track down former gang members said to have reached Fontaine.
- **Shikanoin Heizou**: the Inazuman detective who learned from Jahoda that the Seahook Gang now active are impostors.

## reputation
### STANDARD
The name once carried weight and still frightens people, which is why impostors borrow it. Heizou was relieved to learn his targets were only impostors, since he did not fancy facing the real gang and its international cohort. Jahoda treats the gang as a past to leave behind and says Ihanti was no friend.

## secrets
### ULTRA
Ratfan's bribe and the burning of the evidence are told only in Jahoda's own character story. Nefer's role in steering Jahoda toward Ratfan, and her cool motive in doing so, also comes from that story rather than anything said in public.

## status

## entry: Zinaida
@keys: Zinaida
@keys2: Seahook Gang, Nasha Town, Jahoda, Manez
@version: 6.0
### STANDARD
Zinaida is an open-world NPC in Nasha Town, titled Treasure Hunter. She was a mid-level leader in the Seahook Gang and recruited Jahoda. As a child Jahoda would slip out to wake her in the night to talk through bad days, and Zinaida would carry her back to bed. She now guards Manez, the port supervisor, for the Voynich Guild, and says that beats protecting a blood-soaked bandit chief. Nefer invites her to visit Jahoda at the Curatorium; Zinaida says it is best left as it is.

## entry: Ihanti
@keys: Ihanti
@keys2: Seahook Gang, Jahoda, Nasha Town
@version: 6.2
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Ihanti was a Seahook Gang member skilled at persuading people to hand over money, and he took Jahoda along as a sidekick. In 6.2 he posts a bounty at the Adventurers' Guild with a stolen pendant as collateral. When Jahoda finds him he is terminally ill, nearly blind and has been robbed. He says his wife and daughter are gone. He asks to be left to die in peace, reminds Jahoda of the boss's code, and gives her a doll he made himself. He is dead the next day and leaves a short letter.

## entry: Ratfan
@keys: Ratfan
@keys2: Seahook Gang, Jahoda, Nasha Town, Nod-Krai
@version: 6.2
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Ratfan was a wealthy Nod-Krai merchant. According to Jahoda's Character Story 3, he used money to have gang members kill their boss, burned the evidence, and bought intelligence from a mysterious businesswoman. Jahoda hunted him over many nights, then killed him in revenge. He is dead before the Traveler reaches Nod-Krai.
