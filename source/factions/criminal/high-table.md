## summary
### LITE
The High Table is an informal alliance of Snezhnaya's fae gangs. Its members live by a shared code called the Law, which limits how gangs treat ordinary people and one another. It was founded by the Domovoy Nikolay Izmaylov, the "Old Sire", and the surviving heroes who ended the Burner of Cities' rebellion. Since the Old Sire was killed by his adopted son, the alliance has been under strain.
### STANDARD +
It is not part of the Tsaritsa's government or the Fatui. The player meets it through the Mean Streets of the Despicable quests in Snezhnograd, where the Luchkins present it as the guardian of the ideals of the heroes who protected Snezhnaya hundreds of years ago.

## structure
### STANDARD
The High Table is a council of gang leaders chaired by a Speaker. Each member gang runs its own territory and affairs. The known gangs are the Izmaylov family, the Luchkins and House Remusov. The Old Sire adopted four children and gave each a surname tied to a gang he wanted them to lead one day: Vasiliy Izmaylov, Zmei Luchkin, Iryna Voronina and Lyubov Remusov. Iryna has no gang of her own and is described as the High Table's enforcer of the Law. Lyubov was sent to Novokitezhgrad and now heads House Remusov, though Zmei says his hands are not clean.
### LARGE +
Beneath the great gangs are smaller ones. A short fae in Hunter on the Snowfields says the Old Sire's rules are the only reason anyone gets by, and that anyone who breaks them will be reported to "Lady Voronina". Disputes between gangs can be settled by formal duels, in which each side fights only with the weapon in its hands, or by a proxy, as when the Traveler fought Lindenovich on Vseslav's behalf. Zmei and Iryna were children of the Old Sire together with Vasiliy and Lyubov and grew up playing in the Train Boneyard.

## leadership
### STANDARD
The founder and first Speaker was Izmaylov, known to the gangs as the Old Sire, who also presided over the Royal Energy Commission. To the gang leaders he was the chairman, and to his four adopted children he was a father. Zmei Luchkin is now Speaker according to the wiki. Vasiliy claims the Old Sire meant to give him the seat because Zmei "wants nothing and would change nothing". Zmei speaks for the High Table in the final scenes of the quest.
### LARGE +
Zmei is the human leader of the Luchkins. Sofya explains that since the Tsaritsa took the throne, humans have enjoyed more favour with those in power, so a human leader finds it easier to talk to the authorities. Iryna enforces the Law, and Zmei says her workload has grown heavier since the Old Sire died.

## members
### STANDARD
Three gangs are known: the Izmaylov family (the largest and richest), the Luchkins (who present themselves as defenders of the weak) and House Remusov (led by Lyubov). Most gang members are fae, including Volkodlaks, though Zmei is human and Gureva remarks that she does not recall humans in the Izmaylov ranks.
### LARGE +
Named Luchkins include Vseslav, Sofya and the informant Gureva. Named Izmaylovs include Lindenovich, Lorie and the second-in-command known as the Butcher. Their entries cover those gangs; this entry covers the shared code and council.

## goals
### STANDARD
The High Table exists to keep order among the gangs and uphold the ideals of the heroes who stopped the Burner of Cities: protect Snezhnaya's peace and stop gang business from becoming predation. The gangs also make a living through debt collection, odd jobs for the Druzhna and the Energy Commission, and recovering old machinery and relics.

## methods
### STANDARD
The Law is the High Table's central tool. Rules established by the sources:
- No harm to families, and no abuse of women and children.
- Do not commit acts devoid of mercy or justice. Zmei calls this the first line of the Law.
- Debts must be repaid.
- Whoever finds treasure first has the right to keep it. If several gangs find or help recover the same treasure, the reward is divided among them (the "Old Sire's rules" cited in Hunter on the Snowfields).
- Matters concerning the Shadow Realm are left to the Druzhna and the Energy Commission. The Old Sire forbade the gangs from touching them.
### LARGE +
Disputes between gangs may be settled by formal duels, with the victory decided by skill and courage rather than by tricks. The Old Sire also left behind friendships and connections, such as the contact that let the Luchkins rent a whole train.

## history
### STANDARD
**Founding.** After the Tsaritsa took control of Snezhnaya and cut the fae nobility's privileges, a Vila who called herself the Burner of Cities rallied resentful old nobles and raided the country by railway. She was stopped by the Luchkin heroes, who used weapons forged by the young Domovoy Izmaylov. According to the artifact text Foundryman's Magnanimity, the leader of the heroes and the rebel died in the same flames, and the modest fame the heroes had earned passed to Izmaylov. In the narrow alleyways he and the surviving heroes swore an oath, and the High Table was born. From then on, "amid soot and rust", the gangs would live by the Law.
### LARGE +
**Politics behind the founding.** Izmaylov, by then President of the Energy Commission, had a bitter rivalry with Grand Duke Alvis, also a Domovoy. They were at odds over who would direct the new Transport Bureau: Alvis wanted a noble fae, and Izmaylov nominated humans, knowing the Tsaritsa preferred them. Alvis suddenly dropped his claim. The artifact text says he was one of the old nobility himself, and that he held back because he expected Izmaylov could not quell the rebellion, planning to step in and cast him down. Izmaylov's heroes won instead, and Alvis' scheme failed.

**The Old Sire's last years.** Izmaylov adopted many children, human and fae, raised them, gave them surnames and meant for them to inherit the gangs. Over the years the Energy Commission took on commissions about the Shadow Realm while he searched for his teacher Aksinya. When she returned after five centuries, he lost interest in the Realm and wanted the Commission to stop pursuing it, and he intended to leave the Speaker's seat to Zmei.
### ULTRA +
**Fraying.** Several months before the Traveler's visit the Old Sire fell ill and died overnight. Gang members suspected foul play because the last people to see him were Vasiliy and the Butcher. Afterwards many smaller gangs fell into disorder, and Iryna notes that punishments for breaking the Law rose sharply. In Hunter on the Snowfields a hulking fae complains that, once the old man died, the High Table decided the gangs had "outlived their usefulness" and that finds from the Shadow Realm end up in the pockets of big shots.

**The Izmaylov incident.** Vasiliy resented that the gangs get the thankless jobs that the Druzhna and the Energy Commission do not want, and he denounced the High Table as a "make-believe council". In the Mean Streets of the Despicable quests he used heavy debt collection to fund a machine for opening the Shadow Realm in the Train Boneyard, a collapsed, half-excavated tunnel. Gureva says he wanted to prove that fae gangs could match the Energy Commission's technology and bargain for a seat at the Veche. The Luchkins asked the Traveler to help. The Traveler stole a cargo sample, rescued Gureva from an Izmaylov stronghold, and followed Vasiliy into the Realm. There Vasiliy admitted killing the Old Sire and fought them. He died as the device shut down. Zmei thanked the Traveler on behalf of the High Table and said it would decide what to do next. The Luchkins later gave the Traveler the refined Kristall key as a gift.

## relations
### STANDARD
- **Izmaylov family**: the largest gang, founded by the Old Sire and now leaderless after Vasiliy's death.
- **Luchkins**: the gang of the heroes who ended the Burner of Cities; their leader Zmei is the current Speaker.
- **Energy Commission and Druzhna**: gangs do odd jobs for the Commission's higher-ups, but the Law leaves Shadow Realm affairs to the Commission and the Druzhna. Zmei fears that if the Druzhna learn of Vasiliy's work, the entire High Table would be finished.
- **Fatui**: not a Fatui body. Alyosha says that fae gangs are not legitimate organisations like the Fatui, "but their roots run just as deep".
### LARGE +
- **Tsaritsa and the Snezhnograd Veche**: the Tsaritsa's favour toward humans shapes the gangs' dealings with power. Vasiliy wanted a seat at the Veche.
- **Alvis**: the Old Sire's rival at the Veche, whose manoeuvring in the Burner of Cities episode is part of the founding story.

## reputation
### LARGE
Ordinary Snezhnayans mostly know the gangs through debt collection. The Luchkins are seen as a source of help by people such as Marfa. A short fae in Hunter on the Snowfields credits the Old Sire's rules for his being able to survive in Snezhnaya at all. Vasiliy complains that the lords above treat fae gangs as dogs to be summoned and dismissed at will, which Zmei does not dispute when he says the gangs have always been looked down on.

## secrets
### ULTRA
The Old Sire's killing is the High Table's open suspicion: gang members suspected Vasiliy and the Butcher, and Vasiliy confirms it only in the finale. Zmei also says that as a child he once entered the Shadow Realm, an experience he never wanted to repeat.

## entry: The Law of the fae gangs
@keys: Law of the gangs, Old Sire's rules, finder's fee, fae gang Law
@keys2: High Table, Izmaylov, Luchkin, Snezhnaya
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
The Law is the code agreed by the High Table. Known articles: no harm to families and no abuse of women and children; do not commit acts devoid of mercy or justice; debts must be repaid; and a first finder of treasure may keep it, with rewards shared if several gangs help. Zmei treats the first line as the moral core and says that choosing to hurt others "just once" makes it easier every time. Gang members invoke "the Old Sire's rules" in disputes, and Iryna Voronina enforces them. Vasiliy called the Law "a leash" the old man left around their necks.

## entry: Burner of Cities' rebellion
@keys: Burner of Cities, Kresnik's Torches, Luchkin heroes
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
The Burner of Cities was a Vila of the old fae nobility who raised a short-lived revolt after the Tsaritsa curtailed the nobles' privileges. She gathered followers, used the new railway to raid the country and planned to destroy the Kresnik's Torches. The Luchkin heroes, helped by weapons that Izmaylov forged, stopped her, and she died in the fight. Izmaylov and the survivors then founded the High Table. The exact events of the final fight are said to be lost to time.

## entry: Old Sire
@keys: Old Sire, Pakhan, Nikolay Izmaylov, Nikolay Pakhan Izmaylov
@version: 7.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Nikolay "Pakhan" Izmaylov, the Old Sire, was a Domovoy from Kitezhgrad who first served as a spy at Hvergelmir and then became President of the Royal Energy Commission. He founded the High Table with the surviving heroes of the Burner of Cities episode and raised four adopted children as heirs to the gangs. He searched the Shadow Realm for his lost teacher Aksinya and, after finding what he sought, told Vasiliy that the Commission would stop pursuing the Realm. Vasiliy killed him in a rage. His own account is the dialogue in the quest; other details come from the wiki and artifact texts.

## entry: Izmaylov incident
@keys: Izmaylov incident, Train Boneyard, Shadow Realm gate, Young Sire's plot
@version: 7.0
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
Vasiliy Izmaylov, the Young Sire, pressed debtors for money, rented train cars to ship raw materials in secret, and built a machine in the Train Boneyard to open the Shadow Realm with a key of refined Kresnik Kristall. The Luchkins sent the Traveler to steal a sample and then to rescue Gureva, whom the Butcher held. In the Boneyard, Vasiliy attacked the Luchkins and the Traveler, was beaten, and died as the device shut down and the tunnel collapsed. The High Table then took charge of the Izmaylovs' fate.
