## summary
### LITE
The Abyss Order is a secretive legion of intelligent monsters of the Abyss, such as Abyss Mages, Heralds and Lectors, who hate the Seven and Celestia and work to overthrow the world the gods rule. Most of its members were once people of Khaenri'ah, the godless nation destroyed 500 years ago, who were cursed into monstrous forms. It was founded by Chlothar Alberich after the cataclysm and is now led by the Traveler's lost twin, whom the Order reveres as its Prince or Princess. Dainsleif hunts it constantly.
### STANDARD +
Ordinary people in Teyvat know almost nothing about it. Diluc, who has access to Mondstadt's underground networks, calls it an enemy of the human race about which even those networks rarely hear anything. Its members usually live in the Abyss and emerge for specific operations, often using hilichurls as manpower.

## structure
### LARGE
The Order has no publicly known hierarchy, size or headquarters. What the Traveler has seen suggests rough tiers:
- **Leader**: the Traveler's sibling, addressed as "Your Highness", the Prince or Princess.
- **An "inner circle"** close to the sibling, whose members and purpose are unknown.
- **Commanders and specialists**: Abyss Heralds (melee blade-wielders) and Abyss Lectors (ranged spellcasters) who lead operations, and Iniquitous Baptists such as Haden, who commanded the Natlan campaign.
- **Abyss Mages**: the most common members, elementally armed and able to speak with hilichurls.
- **Hilichurls**: conscripted labour and troops. They are not truly members, but are cursed Khaenri'ahn citizens whom the Order regards as its people.
### ULTRA +
Members often take or keep names. The wiki lists the Abyss Lectors Agnarr and Egill by name, and in Natlan the Traveler fought Heralds titled "Ansger the Frigid Glare" and "Haldher the Corrupt". Not every Abyssal being is in the Order: the Cryo Herald guarding Vedrfolnir's crystal in Avidya Forest was stated not to be, an Electro Lector fought by the Natlanese hero Burkina during the cataclysm predates it, and Fontaine's Abyss-adapted Jakob Ingold despises it.

## leadership
### LITE
The Traveler's twin sibling (Lumine if the player chose Aether, Aether if the player chose Lumine) leads the Order. Their goal is to restore Khaenri'ah and confront the Heavenly Principles, and they refuse to reunite with the Traveler until their war against the gods is over.
### STANDARD +
The sibling was once named heir of Khaenri'ah and hailed as its "Savior". After waking from Asmoday's seal they travelled Teyvat with Dainsleif, met Chlothar in Sumeru, and in time decided that Chlothar had been right about the world. They joined the Order and became its leader, while Dainsleif chose to fight it. The sibling still loves the Traveler and asks them to keep travelling and see the truth for themselves.
### LARGE +
The founder, Chlothar Alberich, was a Khaenri'ahn noble of the Alberich Clan who called the sibling "Prince/Princess" from the start and installed them at the head of the Order. Chlothar went mad roughly a hundred years after the founding and disappeared. Dainsleif tells Kaeya Alberich that his family founded the Abyss Order, and Kaeya, who now treats the Order as an enemy, declines to say more.

## members
### LARGE
Named members include:
- **Haden**: once the sword instructor of Khaenri'ahn knights, including Dainsleif and Surtalogi, now an Iniquitous Baptist and commander of the Natlan offensive.
- **Enjou**: a Pyro Abyss Lector and self-described "lowly clerk" who studies ancient lore. He has appeared in Enkanomiya and in Natlan under several aliases.
- **Klingsor**: an Abyss Herald, once a Sumeru Skeptic of Khaenri'ahn descent, who tried to break the seal over the Abyss beneath the desert.
- **The "Presager of the Abyss"**: an unnamed Hydro Abyss Herald who served the sibling directly. He was killed in the Chasm.
The wiki also lists the Abyss Lectors Agnarr and Egill. The Shade Asmoday is not confirmed as a member, but she seems to have abandoned her duties to Celestia and taken the sibling as her new master.
### ULTRA +
The wiki says many, if not all, members came from Khaenri'ah. Klingsor is the exception that proves the pull of the Abyss: he was a Sumeru Skeptic descended from the Khaenri'ahn Hadura, and he "embraced" the Abyss of his own will. Enjou is stranger still. He is a long-lived sage of unknown origin who has seen several ancient civilisations, does not remember his homeland, and says the Abyss Order is no more his home than any other place.

## goals
### LITE
The Order wants revenge on the gods for destroying Khaenri'ah, an end to the curse on its people, and the rebirth of Khaenri'ah.
### STANDARD +
Dainsleif says the Order seeks to destroy the nations watched over by the Seven because the gods destroyed Khaenri'ah. Under the sibling the aims are more specific. The Order wants to cleanse the hilichurls' curse, so that they either return to the cycle of life and death or become the foundation of a rebuilt Khaenri'ah. It wants to complete the **Loom of Fate**, weave the ley lines of a new Khaenri'ah, and set that world down somewhere so that the sibling, backed by an entire world, can speak to the Heavenly Principles as an equal.

## methods
### STANDARD
The Order corrupts and manipulates powerful beings: it poisoned Dvalin's mind into attacking Mondstadt as Stormterror and planned to rebuild the sea god Osial as a mechanical god. It scavenges Khaenri'ahn technology, especially the Ruin Guards that Khaenri'ah called "Field Tillers". It works through hilichurls, whom Abyss Mages can talk to in a long-forgotten language. It also tampers with ley lines, which carry memories as well as energy.
### LARGE +
Its operations are patient and often deceptive. Abyss Mages spent long stretches digging through dormant Ruin Guards for the eye of the first Field Tiller. Caribert's power planted false memories in villagers and in Dainsleif to lure him to where he had hidden that eye. The Natlan offensive was a deliberate feint, in which Haden spent his army to draw out Dainsleif's unusually clear memories of Khaenri'ah. Members communicate with talismans and letters written in Teyvat's script. In the quest "Darknight Hero's Crisis", a partly redacted letter challenged the "Darknight Hero" (Diluc) to meet the Order's march on Mondstadt's front gate, or else something of Mondstadt's (the word is redacted) would "take the fall" in his stead. Hilichurl Shooters' crossbows are suspected to come from the Order.
### ULTRA +
The Order is ruthless with its own. In the Chasm, the sibling approved a device that might lift the curse or might cause the hilichurls extreme suffering, accepting the gamble because a dignified end seemed better than their existence. In Natlan, Haden sacrificed his forces for one deception. Enjou, by contrast, prefers running away to fighting and bargains freely with outsiders, from the Bathysmal Vishaps to the Natlanese, which suggests individual members have wide latitude.

## history
### STANDARD
**Origins (about 500 years ago and after).** When Khaenri'ah fell in the cataclysm, the gods cursed its people. Pure-blooded Khaenri'ahns became immortal, and the rest became hilichurls. Chlothar Alberich's half-Mondstadter son Caribert became a hilichurl. In Avidya Forest, Chlothar and the travelling sibling found Vedrfolnir, a Sinner of Khaenri'ah, as a chained crystal worshipped by hilichurls. Vedrfolnir's Abyssal power restored Caribert's mind. Chlothar decided that the Abyss could do what the Seven would not, began to worship the Sinner, and founded the Abyss Order. He named his son the "Loom of Fate", free to weave his destiny anew.

**Present day.** The Order poisoned Dvalin against Mondstadt (Prologue, 1.0), plotted to make Osial a mechanical god from the Defiled Statue beneath Mt. Aocang (Chapter I Act IV), tried to cure the hilichurls in the Chasm (2.6), and secured the Loom of Fate (4.7). In 5.7 it attacked every Natlanese tribe at once as a feint while the sibling wove the ley lines of a new Khaenri'ah.
### LARGE +
**Mondstadt and Liyue.** The sibling oversaw the Dvalin plot from Stormterror's Lair, where an Abyss Mage reported the plan had been impeded and they watched the Traveler from a cliff. In Liyue, Dainsleif and the Traveler read an Order talisman describing the "Loom of Fate" operation. The Defiled Statue, an inverted Statue of the Seven, was to hold the eye of the first Field Tiller and be given Osial's body, creating a god able to "topple the divine thrones of Celestia". The Traveler found that eye first at Stormterror's Lair, and Dainsleif kept it. When an Abyss Herald was cornered at the Defiled Statue, the sibling came through a portal to save it. They told the Traveler that the Abyss Order's monsters were Khaenri'ahns transformed by the gods' curse, and promised a reunion at the journey's end.

**The Chasm (2.6).** The sibling found hilichurls dying beside a pool in the Chasm whose water suppressed the curse, and authorised a device to amplify it a hundredfold. Dainsleif, the Traveler and Halfdan destroyed it, and the Presager of the Abyss died.

**Sumeru (3.5-3.6).** The Order meddled with the ley lines near Chlothar's old home, which let the Traveler relive the founding through their sibling's memories. Separately, Klingsor and the Skeptics' leader Nasejuna tried to trick the Pari Sorush into breaking the seal over the Abyss at Tunigi Hollow.

**The Loom of Fate (4.7).** After the Hydro throne fell without any reaction from the Heavenly Principles, the sibling concluded that Celestia had troubles of its own. Through false memories, Caribert lured Dainsleif to the hidden eye. The sibling wounded Dainsleif, took the eye and completed the device, then met the Traveler briefly inside Caribert's fading mind.

**Natlan (5.7).** After Natlan defeated the Abyss, the Order struck all the tribes in an unusually coordinated attack. Its stronghold was at Ochkanatlan. Dainsleif tore through its lines and killed Haden "a hundred times" twice over. Meanwhile the sibling, with the Lord of the Night's permission, wove New Khaenri'ah's ley lines and stored them in an "Atlas". The remaining forces then withdrew.
### ULTRA +
**Early operations in Inazuma.** A group under Enjou went into Enkanomiya looking for the book Before Sun and Moon, to prove that Celestia came from beyond Teyvat, and lost many members when the Traveler arrived (2.4). During the Three Realms Gateway Offering event (2.5), Enjou plunged Enkanomiya into darkness in a bargain with the Bathysmal Vishaps.

**Later Natlan.** Enjou also travelled through Natlan to study the Turnfire and the Mare Jivari. He bargained with Kinich, and eventually entered the burning Mare Jivari with the Traveler's and Citlali's help (5.0-5.8).

**After Chlothar.** Chlothar went mad about a century after the founding and disappeared. The sibling inherited the Order's leadership and Chlothar's Loom of Fate.

**Nod-Krai and Snezhnaya (5.8-7.1).** Varka's letter in 5.8 warned that the Abyss Order, like the Fatui, was causing trouble in Nod-Krai. In 6.5 Asmoday's abandoned Domain Keeper Melanta lamented that its creator had chosen the sibling over it. In 7.1 the Tsaritsa told Rhinedottir that she had seen Asmoday with a hooded figure watching the monster behind Snezhnaya's storms. The figure remarked that the end of the Third Descender's story was worth witnessing, and then both vanished.

## relations
### LARGE
- **The Seven and Celestia**: the Order's declared enemies.
- **Dainsleif**: its most persistent foe. He takes commissions only if they concern the Order. He was once the sibling's travelling companion.
- **The Traveler**: the sibling's twin. The Order watches them closely. Its Heralds call them "Our Highness's kin", and they keep foiling its plans.
- **Hilichurls**: cursed kin whom the Order directs and hopes to cure. Some hilichurl groups resist it.
- **Five Sinners of Khaenri'ah**: the Sinner Vedrfolnir inspired the founding. Other Sinners, such as Rhinedottir and Rerir, act on their own agendas.
- **Alberich Clan**: Chlothar's family. Kaeya Alberich, its present-day descendant, serves the Knights of Favonius and considers the Order an enemy, though Dainsleif and Mona doubt he can keep his distance.
- **Natlan**: the tribes and Iansan fought the Order in 5.7, and Citlali found its members irritating. Mavuika did not turn against the Traveler on learning that their sibling leads the Order.
- **Lord of the Night**: Yohualtecuhtin allowed the sibling to weave the new ley lines once assured that Natlan would not be affected, on a condition she did not reveal.
- **Asmoday**: the Shade of Space, who sealed the twins 500 years ago, now apparently follows the sibling. The other Shades suspect a defection.
- **Knights of Favonius and Mondstadt**: the Knights count it among Mondstadt's threats, and Diluc tracks it through underground intelligence. The 5.7 feint was meant to distract the Fatui forces in Natlan as well as the tribes.

## reputation
### LARGE
To most of Teyvat, the Order is a faceless monster threat. Its members are rarely seen outside a fight, and almost nobody knows they were once human. The Traveler stopped asking nations to put up missing-person posters for their twin once they learned the twin leads the Order. Even Dainsleif concedes that only the Order has persistently sought out the remnants of Khaenri'ah, though it is "far from human".

## secrets
### ULTRA
Some things are known only to a few. The monsters of the Order are cursed Khaenri'ahns. Its leader is the Traveler's sibling, the former Khaenri'ahn heir and "Savior". Its founder was Kaeya's ancestor. Its inspiration was the Sinner Vedrfolnir, Dainsleif's brother. The sibling carries guilt for leaving Khaenri'ah during the cataclysm, after Direidyth told them the Traveler was looking for them, and the Order's restoration project is partly an act of atonement.

## entry: Loom of Fate
@keys: Loom of Fate, Atlas of New Khaenri'ah, Atlas of the New World
@spoiler: major
@version: 5.7
### STANDARD
"Loom of Fate" began as Chlothar's name for his Abyss-touched son Caribert, who he believed could weave his destiny anew. It then became an Abyss Order operation. Before Fontaine it meant building a mechanical god from the Defiled Statue, Osial and the eye of the first Field Tiller. After the Hydro throne fell, it meant a device that, fully activated, can weave new ley lines into the world. While unfinished it could weave memories, and Caribert used that to calm the hilichurls around Vimara Village and give himself false memories of village life. The sibling secured the eye in 4.7 and, in 5.7, used the Loom in Natlan's Night Kingdom to weave the ley lines of a new Khaenri'ah, stored in an "Atlas" until a suitable place is found.

## entry: Chlothar Alberich and the founding
@keys: Chlothar, Chlothar Alberich, Eide, Caribert
@spoiler: major
@version: 3.5
### STANDARD
Chlothar was a Khaenri'ahn noble of the Alberich Clan who resented his predestined life and had a son, Caribert, by a Mondstadt woman. At the cataclysm he was cursed with immortality, and the half-blooded Caribert became a hilichurl. Living in Avidya Forest under the name "Eide", he met the Traveler's sibling. Together they found the Sinner Vedrfolnir's crystal, and its Abyssal power, together with a forbidden medicine, briefly restored Caribert's mind. When Caribert unmasked and Abyssal power poured out, Chlothar was won over completely. He declared that only a sinner could save a sinner, hailed the sibling as Prince/Princess, and founded the Abyss Order. The Traveler relived this through ley-line disturbances in Caribert (3.5).

## entry: The Order's ranks
@keys: Abyss Mage, Abyss Mages, Abyss Herald, Abyss Heralds, Abyss Lector, Abyss Lectors, Iniquitous Baptist
@keys2: Abyss Order, Order, Khaenri'ah, hilichurl, Dainsleif
### STANDARD
Abyss Mages are the Order's common casters. They are shielded, elemental, and able to speak with hilichurls in a long-forgotten language that is said to give them power. Abyss Heralds are blade-wielding melee elites, and Abyss Lectors are their ranged spellcasting counterparts. Both can draw on intense Abyssal energy. Iniquitous Baptists are rarer and more powerful, and Haden, the Natlan commander, takes this form. Not every being of these kinds belongs to the Order.

## entry: The Defiled Statue operation
@keys: Defiled Statue, first Field Tiller, Field Tiller's eye, mechanized god
@version: 1.4
### STANDARD
In Liyue the Order hid an inverted, Abyss-holding Statue of the Seven, the Defiled Statue, in ruins beneath Mt. Aocang. A talisman carried by an Abyss Mage described the first phase of the "Loom of Fate". The plan was to attach the sea god Osial's limbs to the statue and place the eye of the very first Field Tiller (Ruin Guard) in its hands, creating a god able to challenge Celestia. Abyss Mages searched dormant Ruin Guards for the eye in vain. The Traveler and Dainsleif found it at Stormterror's Lair, and Dainsleif kept it.

## entry: The Chasm curse experiment
@keys: Presager of the Abyss, curse-cleansing device
@keys2: Chasm, hilichurl, Abyss Order, Halfdan
@version: 2.6
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Deep in the Chasm, hilichurls were drawn to a pool whose water suppressed the curse and went there to die. The sibling approved a device meant to amplify the water a hundredfold and turn the hilichurls back into Khaenri'ahns, knowing that failure would cause them terrible pain. Their Hydro Abyss Herald, the "Presager of the Abyss", defended it. Dainsleif, insisting the curse cannot be undone, fought it with the Traveler, and the Black Serpent Knight Halfdan destroyed the device. The Herald died there.

## entry: Enjou
@keys: Enjou, Lavawalker, Sanka, Kokuchuu, Hayne
### STANDARD
Enjou is a Pyro Abyss Lector and the Order's self-styled "lowly clerk", who chases ancient lore and knows of, but is not part of, the inner circle. He posed as the Traveler's helper in Enkanomiya (2.4) while seeking Before Sun and Moon. He then revealed himself, fought, and left on friendly terms, warning that Paimon might one day betray them. In Natlan he reappeared under new aliases, traded favours with Kinich, and finally walked into the Mare Jivari. He is a long-lived sage of unknown origin who has forgotten his homeland, and he avoids fights where he can.

## entry: Klingsor and Khvarena
@keys: Klingsor, Forsaker of the True Way, Priest of the Dark Hollow, Great Songs of Khvarena
@version: 3.6
@spoiler: minor
### LARGE
Centuries ago, Klingsor, a Skeptic ranger descended from the Khaenri'ahn Hadura, found records that convinced him his people's history had been hidden. He embraced the Abyss and became an Abyss Herald, and he led Order forces against Vourukasha Oasis to seize the Great Songs of Khvarena and break the seal over the Abyss at Tunigi Hollow. The Pari and the Skeptics repelled him. In the present he persuaded the Skeptics' leader Nasejuna to help him again, and they tricked the Pari Sorush into gathering the songs. ## entry: Natlan feint and Haden
@keys: Haden, Wyrdsword of Heaven's End, Natlan offensive
@version: 5.7
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
In 5.7 the Order attacked every Natlanese tribe at once, with unusual coordination. Many members simply meditated, trying to recall Khaenri'ah. The whole campaign was a feint so the sibling could reach the Night Kingdom's Lord, weave a new Khaenri'ah's ley lines with the Loom of Fate, and lure out Dainsleif, whose memories of Khaenri'ah are clearer than most. Its commander, the Iniquitous Baptist Haden, was once the cruel sword-master of Dainsleif and Surtalogi, and he has lost much of his memory and former feelings. He first claimed that Khaenri'ah was already revived, then admitted that this was a bluff. Haden says he believes in the sibling's sincerity despite their departure during the cataclysm.

## entry: Asmoday and the Order

@keys2: sibling, Abyss Order, Prince, Princess, new master
@version: 7.1
@spoiler: major
### ULTRA
Asmoday, the Shade of Space, sealed the twins as they tried to flee Teyvat during the cataclysm, and kept the sibling in a coffin in the Temple of Space. She has since disappeared from her duties. Ronova guessed she would defect only for a "new master" she loved more than herself. Her portals closed behind the sibling's 5.7 conversation.
