## summary
### LITE
The Narzissenkreuz Ordo was a Fontainian secret society founded by Rene de Petrichor and his adoptive brother Jakob Ingold. Convinced a second flood would end the world, it sought to fuse countless wills into one being able to survive it. Its base was the Tower of Ipsissimus. It is defunct as of 4.2.
### STANDARD +
The name means "Narcissus Cross" and is shared with the Narzissenkreuz Institute where the founders grew up. Its style mixed scholarship, ritual and showmanship. The Ordo appears only through the past: the Enigmatic Pages and the Fontaine world quests.

## structure
### LITE
The Master, Narzissenkreuz, led; Jakob Ingold served as his right hand. Members held clearance levels 1 to 4.
### LARGE +
Clearance came with exams and a reading list of the Ordo's ideals. Those who could not pass but gave much, such as sponsors, received honorary levels. The Ordo hid among, and recruited from, the Institute of Natural Philosophy. The tower itself expresses its creed. Its plaque calls Ipsissimus an old Remurian idea of a will able to rule, sustain and destroy the world, and presents spiritual evolution through four ages: Hyperborea, Natlantea, Remuria and the present Khraun-Arya (the Ordo's own names, not a rival to Akademiya findings).

## leadership
### LITE
Narzissenkreuz, the Master, is Rene after he dissolved himself and was reborn in a body drawn from the Oceanid Lyris, Carter Scherbius and Alain's watch. Jakob built him.
### STANDARD +
Rene was the planner and Jakob the performer who designed rituals and won people over. After the Master's creation Jakob ran the society day to day and hid that the first ritual had failed.
### LARGE +
Jakob's grip hardened after Rene's near-loss: he grew used to deception and was ready to use defecting members as test subjects. The Master believed his plan should leave a place for Alain and Mary-Ann Guillotin. He is also said to have created Caterpillar, the hilichurl with human speech.

## members
### LITE
Rene and Jakob (founders), the Master, the Oceanid Lyris ("Red Empress"), Caterpillar, and scholars and patrons of lower levels.
### STANDARD +
Certificates in the tower name Madam M. Laveaux (Level 4), Ms. E. Lenormand and Ms. Giuffria (Level 3), Erich (Level 2) and Eichi Karasawa, an Inazuman scholar and novelist (Level 1).
### LARGE +
Mr. Griffin L. Randolph and Susannah held honorary Level 1; the wiki also lists Mr. Eliphas, Mr. Forsamiro and Ms. Zenaida, with little canon detail. Lenormand was promoted by exception for spreading the teachings, Giuffria's injured leg was healed by the Master, and Erich, a historian from the Institute of Natural Philosophy, joined the Ordo at Jakob's invitation after the Institute disbanded. The Master dissolved and absorbed three agents sent against the society, among them Emanuel Guillotin and the Champion Duelist Marfisa. Alain and Mary-Ann Guillotin refused to join. Dwight Lasker, the Institute's director, received a report about strange activity and asked Emanuel to look into it.

## goals
### LITE
To outlive the end of the world. Rene's "world-formula" predicted a flood, and the Ordo meant to merge many wills into a Descender-like being.
### STANDARD +
The method was to use the Primordial Sea and an Oceanid to gather the egos of all Fontainians into one consciousness, freed from bodies and separation. According to Caterpillar, Narzissenkreuz taught that one's own will is an obstacle and that receiving a Vision is a "guaranteed path to destruction".
### LARGE +
Later, Jakob's final version was to reverse the Seal of Chymical Marriage, which he described as an ancient ritual that sealed off the source of life, reforge the Holy Blade, free the Primordial Sea and bring Rene back so all souls were preserved as one. The Ordo also drew on Golden Troupe techniques, whose symbols Rene linked to the Institute's archives.

## methods
### STANDARD
Research and ritual. The Ordo gathered knowledge from Remuria and Khaenri'ah ruins and from archives, and ran initiations, reading lists and ceremonies with the Holy Blade of Narzissenkreuz. It also experimented on people: Carter Scherbius, Alain's sick assistant, was treated with materials from Elynas, which failed and ended Alain's friendship with the founders. Rene had earlier saved Jakob with the Abyss's power, making him a "neohuman".
### LARGE +
Later experiments targeted defecting members. The founders rebuilt a Circle of Four Orthants and a Tree of Emanation from ancient records and posted members to candidate sites to find the nodes. Rene's notes call the ritual behind the sword "extremely effective witchcraft" that exploits the situation. Caterpillar says the Master led ceremonies with the Holy Blade "in accordance with ancient dictates".

## history
### LARGE
**Roots.** Rene de Petrichor and Jakob Ingold grew up in the Narzissenkreuz Institute, an orphanage run by the Oceanid Lyris and her Vice Director Basil Elton, after the Siege of Poisson. During the cataclysm the Director and Vice Director left and never came back, and the Institute disbanded. Rene and Jakob went to the Girdle of the Sands with Karl Ingold, studied Khaenri'ahn technology and derived the world-formula.

**Founding.** Back in Fontaine they joined the Institute of Natural Philosophy through Alain Guillotin. Alain would not believe their results. After the failed attempt on Carter, Rene turned the scholars who had become believers into a society. They built a headquarters in the Morte Region, the Tower of Ipsissimus.

**Rebirth.** Rene found Lyris, still haunting the sunken Institute, and brought her into the project. Rene obtained the Holy Blade at great cost and made himself the first subject. The ritual failed, but Jakob built Narzissenkreuz from Lyris's essence, Carter's remains and Alain's pocket watch and placed Rene's mind in him. The Master later calculated the Doomsday Clock from the world-formula.

**Downfall.** The Court of Fontaine declared the Ordo an illegal extremist group and sealed the tower. The Master absorbed agents and failed to win over Alain. He sent Jakob, Lyris and many members to Elynas, where they were used as bait, and the Marechaussee Hunters, led by Alain and Mary-Ann, defeated them. Lyris absorbed Mary-Ann's personality and Mary-Ann was listed as missing in action, while Narzissenkreuz fell into "the chasm that dissolves all things", the Primordial Sea beneath the tower. Caterpillar was jailed in the Fortress of Meropide about 400 years before the main story.
### ULTRA +
**The 4.x story.** Players first learn of the Ordo through the Enigmatic Pages found with Canotila's Book of Revealing (4.0), and Ann, a young Oceanid with Mary-Ann's memories, leads them to the Narzissenkreuz Ordo chamber (4.1). In Search in the Algae Sea (4.2) Jakob, now alive centuries later, confronts the Traveler, Ann, Caterpillar and Seymour in the meeting chamber, where the Holy Blade is reforged. The Doomsday Clock then strikes its final hour and he vanishes beneath its falling hand. The party descends through the Tower of Ipsissimus, which sinks into the sea, and in Waking from the Great Dream faces the Master in the Primordial Sea. Defeated, he is calmed by Ann, who forgives him. The Traveler reseals the entrance. In Rowboat's Wake, Mary-Ann forgives Narzissenkreuz as Rene and invites him along. Having spent all his power, he fades and lies down to rest in Annapausis. Caterpillar goes his own way.

## relations
### LARGE
- **Narzissenkreuz Institute**: the founders' childhood home and the source of the Ordo's name. The Ordo was built partly on the Institute's archives.
- **Institute of Natural Philosophy**: the Ordo's recruiting ground. It was hidden within the Institute, and some staff left with it.
- **Guillotin family**: Alain and Mary-Ann were old friends and refused to join. Emanuel was absorbed by the Master. They were the main force against the society.
- **Court of Fontaine and the Marechaussee Phantom**: declared the Ordo illegal. The tower's sealing notice carries Alain Guillotin's and Neuvillette's signatures.
- **Golden Troupe and Remuria**: sources of the techniques the Ordo tried to reverse.
### ULTRA +
- **Abyss Order**: not affiliated. Jakob relies on Abyssal power but dismissed the Order as primitive, and Paimon had compared his methods to theirs.
- **Marcel (Vacher)**: the wiki says Jakob's research later served as the basis of the Sinthe experiments, a drug made from diluted Primordial Seawater.
- **Fortress of Meropide**: Caterpillar was imprisoned there after a task he carried out for the Master failed.

## reputation
### LARGE
To the law the Ordo was a criminal extremist organisation. Caterpillar remembers most members as kind, yet calls the Ordo the chief culprit of a great sin.

## secrets
### ULTRA
Rene's first rebirth failed, and Jakob hid this from the members and even from the Master. The Master believed he had succeeded. Jakob erased all memories of the failed attempts, and the defecting members were used as test subjects. Mary-Ann's falling out with Jakob came from her discovering part of this truth. The Master's body contains Carter Scherbius's remains, and Caterpillar was created from the leftover material.

## status
@version: 4.2
### LITE
Defunct as of 7.1. The Master lies at rest in Annapausis, Jakob was lost in the Doomsday Clock's collapse and the tower has sunk.
### STANDARD +
The Traveler resealed the Primordial Sea. Caterpillar, Ann and Seymour live on; other survivors are unnamed. Nothing new about the Ordo appeared in 5.x to 7.1 in the sources checked.

## entry: Tower of Ipsissimus
@keys: Tower of Ipsissimus, Tower of Gestalt, Sealed Ruined Tower
### STANDARD
The Tower of Ipsissimus, also called the Tower of Gestalt, is the Ordo's headquarters in the Morte Region. Its top is a hidden meeting chamber with the Doomsday Clock, reachable through a book in the Institute of Natural Philosophy. The tower was sealed by court order and, once the 4.2 quest Savior's Wake is complete, sinks permanently to the sea floor. Mek guardians left behind when it was sealed protect the lowest level and the seal over the Primordial Sea. Inside, floor certificates list members and their clearance levels.

## entry: Doomsday Clock
@keys: Doomsday Clock
### STANDARD
The Doomsday Clock is mounted in the Ordo's meeting chamber, where it was set by Narzissenkreuz using the world-formula to count down the apocalypse. When it strikes midnight, the apocalypse is meant to come. Caterpillar notes that when he was there, the Ordo thought the end was far off and the point was to stop it for later generations, but that if the clock is accurate it is now "not far off". In Search in the Algae Sea it strikes its final hour as Jakob declares the appointed time has come, and a giant hand falls.

## entry: Clearance levels of the Ordo
@keys: Ordo clearance level, Ordo member certificate, honorary member of the Ordo
### LARGE
Members of the Ordo held clearance levels from one to four, depending on their work and conduct. Honorary levels went to people who could not complete the tests but had contributed significantly, such as patrons. Level 1 required finishing the Ordo's reading list and understanding its ideals. The wealthy sponsor Mr. Randolph failed the tests because he could not remember the keywords, and Susannah, whose scones and cookies brought Jakob and the Master "lucky" rewards, never finished the list; both were made honorary Level 1 members. Madam Laveaux, the highest listed, held Level 4.

## entry: Enigmatic Pages and the Book of Revealing
@keys: Enigmatic Page, Book of Revealing, world-formula
@version: 4.0
### LARGE
Rene's notes, called the Enigmatic Pages, were hidden in Elynas and found by the Melusine Canotila, who studied them in the Book of Revealing. They cover his reading of an ancient order's texts, his world-formula, Jakob's transformation and Carter's failed treatment, the founding of the society, visiting the Director, the sword as a "key", Lyris's persuasion and his final decision to dissolve himself. Later pages in the Master's voice describe his rebirth, the mission sent to Elynas and Alain's refusal.

## entry: Jakob Ingold in the Ordo
@keys: Jakob Ingold, Narzissenkreuz Artificial Apostle
@keys2: Ordo, Narzissenkreuz Ordo
### LARGE
Jakob was born to a Fleuve Cendre family and was raised with Rene in the Narzissenkreuz Institute. Rene saved him with the Abyss's power and made him the first neohuman, able to turn into an Iniquitous Baptist. In the Ordo he became Rene's right hand, a ritual designer and showman who cried easily in his youth. After Rene's failed rebirth he led the Ordo, deceived members and experimented on defectors in order to bring Rene back. Centuries later he reversed the Seal of Chymical Marriage in the hope of freeing the Primordial Sea, then was lost when the Doomsday Clock's hand fell. See his own entry for more.

## entry: Narzissenkreuz the Master
@keys: Narzissenkreuz, Incomplete Order of the New Cosmos, Master Narzissenkreuz
@keys2: Ordo, Rene, Jakob, Lyris
### LARGE
Narzissenkreuz is what Rene de Petrichor became when he dissolved himself and was placed in a Hydro Tulpa body that drew on Lyris, Carter and Alain's watch. His memories of the failed rebirth were erased. He absorbed enemy agents' minds, taught the Ordo that a personal Vision is a path to ruin and created Caterpillar. In the Primordial Sea in 4.2 he fought the Traveler and Ann, convinced that he could free everyone from the disaster. After his defeat, he accepted Mary-Ann's forgiveness and faded away to rest.

## entry: Lyris, the Red Empress
@keys: Red Empress
@keys2: Ordo, Lyris, Annapausis
### LARGE
Lyris, the former Director of the Narzissenkreuz Institute and an Oceanid, was persuaded by Rene and Jakob to join the Ordo although she was reluctant. In the society she held the title Red Empress, and her essence was used to extract egos for merging. Her health declined as the project went on. During the Elynas clash, her mind merged with Mary-Ann's, which led to the Oceanid Mary-Ann in Annapausis and later Ann. In the sources, Lyris herself is said to have longed to return to the days when the Institute's children played.

## entry: Caterpillar and the Ordo
@keys: Caterpillar, Chrysalis of the Phantasmic Moth
@keys2: Ordo, Narzissenkreuz, Meropide
### LARGE
Caterpillar is a hilichurl that the Master gave human speech, memory and a name in the Ordo, Chrysalis of the Phantasmic Moth, in the hope he would one day become human. After a task for his master failed, he was jailed in the Fortress of Meropide around 400 years before the main story. He regards the Ordo with mixed feelings, remembers most members as kind and does not call himself human. He helped the Traveler in Fontaine and in 4.2 helped reach the sunken tower. His own origin is still partly lost to him.

## entry: Elynas clash
@keys: Elynas clash, battle of Elynas
@keys2: Ordo, Marechaussee, Alain, Mary-Ann
@version: 4.2
### ULTRA
Under the Court of Fontaine's orders, the Marechaussee Hunters led by Alain and Mary-Ann Guillotin and Mary-Ann's mechanical steward Seymour went to destroy the Ordo. The Master sent Jakob, Lyris and other members to Elynas as bait, which gave him time. The Hunters defeated them there. Lyris absorbed Mary-Ann's personality, Mary-Ann was listed as missing in action and Seymour was badly damaged. The Master himself was defeated and fell into "the chasm that dissolves all things" (the wiki's pages differ on whether this happened at Elynas or at the tower above the Primordial Sea), but not before Rene's mind told Alain he would one day return to save all souls. The artifact Fell Dragon's Monocle (Nymph's Dream set) records this moment.

## entry: End of the Ordo (4.2 quests)
@keys: In the Wake of Narcissus, Search in the Algae Sea, Waking from the Great Dream, Rowboat's Wake
@version: 4.2
@spoiler: major
### ULTRA
In Search in the Algae Sea, the Traveler, Ann, Caterpillar and Seymour confront Jakob in the Ordo's meeting chamber atop the Tower of Ipsissimus, where the Holy Blade is reforged. Jakob announces that he has reversed the Seal of Chymical Marriage to free the Primordial Sea and bring back the Master. The Doomsday Clock then strikes its final hour, its hand crashes down, and Jakob is gone. In Savior's Wake the tower descends, and mek guardians must be defeated. In Waking from the Great Dream the Master fights the Traveler and Ann, believing he is saving them, and loses. In Rowboat's Wake the Traveler reseals the Sea and helps free Mary-Ann from the dream of Annapausis. Spent, the Master fades.
