## summary
### LITE
The Hexenzirkel is a secret society of extraordinarily powerful witches, "mages" in the game's English, founded by the adventurer Alice. Members are not chosen for virtue. Alice's main requirement was that a person be "interesting". They mostly pursue their own goals, meet at tea parties and keep a low profile. Its best-known members are Alice (Klee's mother), the seer Barbeloth (Mona's master), the alchemist Rhinedottir, and the voiceless former angel Nicole. It is allied with Mondstadt by a pact.
### STANDARD +
Its name is German for "circle of witches". Some members rival gods in power, and canon never casts the coven as simply good or evil. It is tied to world-changing events such as the cataclysm (through Rhinedottir), yet it also quietly props up Teyvat, as when Alice tends the world's borders.

## structure
### STANDARD
The Hexenzirkel has no stated chain of command or headquarters. Each member has a single-letter codename and an epithet. They meet at "formal tea parties", one regular spot being a floating island above Falcon Coast. Not every member is a sorceress: the novelist Anya Andersdotter and Ioanna Ivanovna N. never had magical or combat training.
### LARGE +
Each mage is represented by a personal teacup in The Mages' Tea Party story teaser. Being a member's heir does not guarantee membership.

Seven founding members are named, and the table on the island has seven chairs. Their codenames and epithets are:
- A, Alice, the Merry-Witch.
- B, Astromancer Barbeloth Trismegistus, the Seer-Witch.
- J, Ioanna Ivanovna N., the Tricksy-Witch.
- M, Anya "Marie" Andersdotter, the Story-Witch.
- N, Nicole Reeyn, the Memory-Witch.
- O, Octavia, the Wander-Witch.
- R, Rhinedottir ("Gold"), the Sage-Witch.

Alice came up with the system of letters and gave each member a bespoke "Self Introduction Magic" that announces them. According to the wiki, Andersdotter's codename is M, not A, because Alice had already claimed A. When J was about to marry and leave, the circle sent her a riddle in the form of a prophecy, inviting her to one last gathering.

## members
### LITE
- **Alice** (A): founder, a polymath adventurer and mother of Klee. She maintains the stability of Teyvat's borders.
- **Barbeloth** (B): a master astrologer and hydromancer, teacher of Mona.
- **Rhinedottir** (R): the Khaenri'ahn alchemist "Gold", one of the Five Sinners, who now holds the Ruler of Life's role.
- **Nicole** (N): a former angel, deprived of her voice, who guides others toward the truth.
### STANDARD +
- **Octavia** (O): a wanderer of the Sea of Stars who is often away.
- **Anya Andersdotter** (M): a Mondstadt writer, author of The Boar Princess. Dead.
- **Ioanna Ivanovna N.** (J): an ordinary human who became Snezhnograd's first Mayor. She left to marry and is dead, but her successors remain.
### LARGE +
Octavia has visited at least 1,896 planets, created Miliastra Wonderland and appears in the book series The Little Witch and the Undying Fire as a self-insert. Andersdotter taught Alice the meaning of grief and helped create Simulanka. Ivanovna rose to power in Snezhnaya under the Belyi Tsar.

Alice is described by Albedo as a near-omnipotent sorceress able to lift or shape whole island chains. She is at least 500 years old. Barbeloth can read the fortunes of nations, of Teyvat and of the world. She mapped the fates of all living things in Simulanka. Rhinedottir created Albedo, Durin and many Abyssal beasts, and she consumed Naberius' heart. Nicole is one of a very few angels who kept both her form and some power, at the cost of her voice. Each member's own entry carries their full story.
### ULTRA +
Wiki trivia treats Barbeloth as Mona's teacher but not her heir, and treats Klee as Alice's daughter, not her magical successor, since Klee has not learned Alice's arts. Alice plans, it is hinted, to teach her once she is ready. Scarlett, a successor of J, says it has been centuries since the first successor took on J's mantle and that Alice had never acknowledged any of them. Alice tells her she is "much more fascinating than any of your predecessors".

## goals
### STANDARD
Canon gives no unified goal. The tea parties exist for members to trade stories and secrets, settle differences and keep a pledge never to fight each other. Individual aims vary: Alice tends Teyvat's borders, Barbeloth studies fate, Nicole guides people toward truth, and Rhinedottir pursues her own research.
### LARGE +
Alice drew a line between watching and interfering. Skirk says Alice made her promise not to interfere too much in Teyvat's affairs, and that when asked why, Alice compared it to ecologists who find it hard to watch predators and prey yet choose not to intervene: "some call this being a callous observer, but I think it's merciful at the same time." The coven's intervention in Mondstadt, Nod-Krai and Durin's case happens when friends ask or when the world's stability is threatened.

## methods
### LARGE
The witches work through scrying, hydromancy, alchemy, narrative magic and long-range speech. Nicole speaks directly into people's minds, sometimes without warning. Alice has spoken through a phonograph, a teacup set and the Lantern of Utmost Joy, and members appear in Dodoco form in their own colours. The circle also builds secret worlds and stages: Simulanka, the Imaginarium Theater and Alice's puzzle-islands in the Golden Apple Archipelago.

The coven prefers trials, games and riddles to direct argument, as when Alice and Barbeloth set Albedo a chess-themed trial before handing over reagents. Prophecy is a form of correspondence: Barbeloth's predictions are written in the Little Witch's Dictionary, and homework tasks derived from them unlock the abilities of Hexerei characters.

## history
### STANDARD
According to Nicole's profile, Alice, then a tiny mage in an enormous hat, listened to Nicole's stories and invited her to join a club whose name she had only just come up with, promising it would be "the funnest, awesomest, best club in all of Teyvat". Later members included the writer Andersdotter, the politician Ivanovna, the wanderer Octavia and the Khaenri'ahn alchemist Rhinedottir. In Alice's own telling (Joy Above the Clouds), the coven once challenged the Anemo Archon, who replied, "Let us make music, not war, and resolve our conflicts through song." From then on the mages convened in the woods, in the skies or on cliff edges.
### LARGE +
**Mondstadt ties.** The island above Falcon Coast was left in Barbatos' care, and reaching it requires an incantation at the cliff. During the Windblume's Breath event (3.5, limited), a hidden prophecy turned out to be a letter the circle had sent to J before her wedding, asking her to come share her happiness one last time. Scarlett, a successor of J, spoke to Alice through the lantern, and Alice told the story of the Hexenzirkel and its members.

**The cataclysm and its creations.** Rhinedottir's Abyssal beasts, including the dragon Durin, took part in the cataclysm 500 years ago. After it, she created Albedo and then consumed the heart of Naberius, becoming Ruler of Life. Her place among the witches is complicated by this history, and Alice has spoken of "cleaning up after" her.
### ULTRA +
**Simulanka (4.8, limited).** During Summertide Scales and Tales, Alice brought the Traveler and others into Simulanka, a mirror-world built by Alice, Barbeloth and Andersdotter. Its people call them the Goddesses of Creation, Prophecy and Fate. Visions do not work there.

**Mondstadt in crisis (5.6).** In Paralogism (part "The Gardener"), Albedo invoked the Tripartite Conference and met Alice and Barbeloth for the trial that gave him the reagents he needed for his plan to make Durin human. The floating island is permanently open after that quest.

**Durin and Andersdotter's wish (6.2).** Before she died, Andersdotter asked Alice to help her character Durin live among the people of Teyvat. In the Draco Rubedo Chapter, Alice gave the Traveler a magical book and ink, and Durin rewrote his own fate. Barbeloth examined him afterwards and found that his fate no longer existed among the stars.

**Nod-Krai (6.2).** Alice came in person to Nod-Krai because the three moons and the Trilunar Syzygy disturbed Teyvat's borders. She explained the moon goddesses' state and told the Traveler to find the true name of Columbina. She had to leave when a second disturbance hit the border, which Dottore had caused as a distraction. Nicole then met the party in person and used an ancient Hyperborean portal, choosing the Traveler, Lauma and Arlecchino as the three who could resonate with kuuvahki. Later, with Varka and Arlecchino, she brought the Frost Moon down from the false sky.

**Sumeru (6.6).** Nicole warned Collei of coming events in Sumeru, persuaded Nefer to go home, and gave Collei an old song of Nabu Malikata. When Nahida burned the corrupted Irminsul tree, Nicole transferred a compatibility with Irminsul into Aaru (the wiki's wording does not make clear whose).

**Snezhnaya (7.1).** Rhinedottir visited the Tsaritsa's palace, met her over tea and, speaking as a representative of the Hexenzirkel, said she was pleased with Snezhnaya's growth.

## relations
### STANDARD
- **Mondstadt**: the circle has long ties to the city. Barbatos keeps the access to their island in his care and set up the Tripartite Conference with them at Varka's request, and Varka's agreement gives Mondstadt a claim on their help.
- **Knights of Favonius and Church of Favonius**: partners in the Tripartite Conference, with Barbatos as the third party.
- **Albedo and Klee**: Albedo is Rhinedottir's creation and calls Alice "Aunt". Alice entrusted Klee to Albedo and the Knights.
- **Mona**: Barbeloth's student, who calls her "the old hag".
### LARGE +
- **Snezhnaya**: Ivanovna was a first Mayor appointed by the Tsaritsa (the wiki infers of Snezhnograd), and Pulcinella was her pupil. Rhinedottir met the Tsaritsa in 7.1.
- **Non-members**: Lisa, called the Witch of Purple Rose, says she has no interest in the Hexenzirkel. La Signora, once the Crimson Witch of Flame, has no known tie to it and resents being called a witch.
- **Skirk**: Skirk, a disciple of the Sinner Surtalogi, calls Alice the "Sustainer of the World's Borders" and says Alice tolerates her presence but made her promise not to interfere too much with Teyvat's affairs.
- **Dottore**: he distracted the circle by deliberately disturbing the border in Nod-Krai. Alice and Nicole were angry with him.
### ULTRA +
- **Heavenly Principles**: Alice says that talking openly about their rules should make her a target, but they cannot tell her what to do.
- **Irminsul**: the coven runs Irminsul explorations, and Nicole has intimate knowledge of its workings and of fate.
- **Little Hexenzirkel**: children in Mondstadt and beyond have a play-version of the coven (see the entry below).

## reputation
### LARGE
Most people have never heard of the Hexenzirkel. Alice jokes that the coven keeps such a low profile that the usual name-dropping does not work for them. Mondstadters know individual members: Andersdotter's fairy tales, Mona's master, Klee's mother. Varka treated the coven as worth securing as an ally.

## secrets
### ULTRA
The terms of Varka's pact are undisclosed. Barbeloth keeps her readings to herself: when Paimon asked for her fortune, Barbeloth hesitated, asked in a serious tone whether Paimon truly wished to know her fate, and then fell silent without answering.

## status
@version: 7.1
### LITE
As of 7.1 the Hexenzirkel is scattered. Alice is on duty at Teyvat's borders, Barbeloth is away in meditation, Nicole acts as guide and homework supervisor, Octavia is travelling, and Rhinedottir has been seen in Snezhnaya.
### STANDARD +
Nicole became playable in 6.6. J and M are dead, but J's line of successors lives on. The circle's links with Mondstadt still stand: Albedo invoked the Tripartite Conference in 5.6, and Alice cites the promise made to Varka there when launching the Witch's Homework (6.2). No other Hexenzirkel developments were found for 6.7-7.0 in the sources checked.

The Hexenzirkel's tea parties are the heart of its culture. A long table with seven chairs and a lantern at its centre stands on a floating island above Falcon Coast. During the limited-time event Windblume's Breath (3.5), a prophecy in a storybook asked for four people to match four descriptions: a flower that is not of this world, a guide who will never get lost, one who would never lie and a legend that never ends. ## entry: Tripartite Conference
@keys: Tripartite Conference, Barbatos' pact, Hexenzirkel pact
@keys2: Hexenzirkel, Varka, Albedo, Venti
@version: 5.6
### LARGE
The Tripartite Conference is a standing arrangement among three parties: the joint forces of the Knights and Church of Favonius, the Anemo Archon Barbatos and the Hexenzirkel. Barbatos set it up at Grand Master Varka's request, and through an agreement with Varka whose terms are undisclosed, the mages promised to return and help Mondstadt in its greatest time of need. Albedo invoked it in 5.6 (Paralogism, "The Gardener") to obtain reagents for his plan to deal with the dead dragon Durin's lingering will. Alice and Barbeloth set the Traveler and Albedo a chess-like trial, then handed over what he asked for. Alice told Albedo to bring a partner to the trial. See the Knights of Favonius entry for later uses.

### LARGE
Simulanka, a mirror-world modelled on Teyvat, was built by Alice, Barbeloth and Andersdotter. Its people revere them as the goddesses of Creation, Prophecy and Fate. Barbeloth single-handedly mapped the fates of everything alive there. Andersdotter wrote Durin, a gentle dragon, into the world despite Barbeloth's warning that he could share his real-world counterpart's fate. Players saw Simulanka in a limited-time event, Summertide Scales and Tales (4.8). The Hexenzirkel is also credited with the Imaginarium Theater, a permanent feature from 4.7.

## entry: Witch's Homework, Hexerei and the Little Witch's Dictionary
@keys: Witch's Homework, Little Witch's Dictionary, Hexerei, Witch's Eve Rite, Hexerei: Secret Rite
@keys2: Nicole, Barbeloth, Hexenzirkel
@version: 6.6
### LARGE
Witch's Homework is a quest series, opened by Alice in "Unexpected Commission" (6.2), that uses The Little Witch's Dictionary, which holds Barbeloth's prophecies, to hand out tasks to certain characters. Completing the homework strengthens those characters when two or more of the group (called Hexerei in game) are in the same party. This is a gameplay system built around the coven's lore. In the 6.6 quest "The Role of a Guide...?", Barbeloth is leaving to meditate because the Frost Moon's shift has thrown her hydromancy off, so Nicole takes over supervising everyone's homework. Nicole notes that Alice is busy maintaining Teyvat's borders, that Octavia "didn't bother to come back", and that "you can forget about Rhinedottir". She also says that Barbeloth wrote her own homework by telling her the ending of her story in advance.

## entry: Ioanna Ivanovna N. and her successors
@keys: Ioanna Ivanovna, I. Ivanovna N., successor of J, Scarlett
@keys2: Hexenzirkel, Snezhnograd, Mayor
@version: 3.5
### LARGE
Ivanovna (codename J, the Tricksy-Witch) was an ordinary human with no magical training who gained great political power in Snezhnaya under the Belyi Tsar and was later appointed the first Mayor of Snezhnograd by the Tsaritsa. She left the Hexenzirkel when she married and left successors who have carried on her title for centuries. Scarlett, who appeared in Windblume's Breath (3.5), is one of them. Alice had never accepted any of the successors until Scarlett caught her interest. Pulcinella was Ivanovna's pupil; the wiki infers from this that he later held the same offices she had.
