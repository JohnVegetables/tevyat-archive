## summary
### LITE
The Northern Intelligence Network is an underground organisation in Nod-Krai that works as a forum, or in Varka's words a black market, for intelligence. Well-connected people trade what they know for payment, with no real rules or regulations. It is run by Nefer, its current representative, and sells to clients of any faction across the world, though it has no foothold in Liyue.
### STANDARD +
Its main point of sale is the Curatorium of Secrets, Nefer's ordinary-looking agency in Nasha Town, which serves as its front. Anyone may enter the Curatorium, but only people with a "key" are admitted to the inner sanctum that is the Network. The Network is not a conventional spy service or a state body: members are volunteers, and its customers include a Liyue intelligence agent and the Knights of Favonius.

## structure
### LITE
Loosely structured, with no rules or regulations to speak of. It is made of volunteers, informants and paying clients who deal with one another through Nefer's Curatorium of Secrets.
### LARGE +
- **Representative**: Nefer, who also owns the Curatorium. She is the person clients deal with.
- **Employees**: Jahoda, the Curatorium's only official employee, and the black cat Ashru. Jahoda acts as a public face that covers for Nefer's informants and for the Network's real activity.
- **Contacts and members**: Diluc is a contact under the codename "Owl". The wiki also lists Nefer herself as the member holding the representative's seat.
- **Informants**: Cairuo and Lestocq, who run the Nasha Town shop Nuts 'N' Nuts, plus Iratxa, Sarin, Sieciech and Tarno, are listed on the wiki as informants. Their ties vary. Sieciech, for instance, is one of Nefer's contacts inside the Lightkeepers, while Lestocq is secretly with the Marechaussee Phantom.
- **Clients**: Yelan and Varka.
Members join voluntarily. Diluc's character story says many have willingly given up their former positions and reputations, and a few have even abandoned their names, which fits the culture of codenames. Nefer explicitly enforces this: she will not use a contact's real name at work.

## leadership
### LITE
Nefer, the owner of the Curatorium of Secrets, is the Network's current head and representative.
### LARGE +
Nefer's character story describes her as the unseen hand that keeps Nod-Krai's waters "just the right degree of murky". The same story shows an emissary of the Network bringing her a request, and an aging, influential representative who had long been looking for a successor and had approved the deal. It hints that the talks might change who leads the Network. In the main story she is introduced by Varka as the current head of the Network. When Varka first brings the heroes to the Secret Base, he says that if they want intel, she is the one to see.
Nefer's own account of the Network is businesslike: the Curatorium's doors are open to all, but only those with a "key" reach the inner sanctum, and whether a visitor leaves with fool's gold or the real thing depends on their own abilities. The Curatorium abides by a policy of strict neutrality towards Nod-Krai's other factions. In 6.1 she at first declined to join the alliance against Rerir, saying she does not work for free, and agreed to help "just this once" only after Jahoda pleaded with her.

## members
### LITE
Nefer is the head. Jahoda is the only official employee. Diluc is a contact with the codename "Owl". Yelan and Varka are customers.
### LARGE +
- **Nefer**: representative and owner of the Curatorium.
- **Jahoda**: a former Seahook Gang member whom Nefer took in. She serves as the Curatorium's sole front-line employee, covering for the informants. According to the wiki's account of her story, Nefer and she agreed that Jahoda would take over the Curatorium if Nefer could no longer run it.
- **Diluc Ragnvindr**: a key Mondstadt informant. After resigning from the Knights, he travelled across Teyvat hunting the source of the Delusion that killed his father. A third-party observer from the north saved him from the Harbingers and said that he approved of his methods. Later he joined the Network, and rose to the upper echelons.
- **Yelan**: a valued customer but not a member of the Network in the usual sense. She buys information for her own purposes, not for the Ministry of Civil Affairs.
- **Varka**: a customer on behalf of the Knights of Favonius' expedition.
- **Ashru**: Nefer's black cat, the Curatorium's receptionist.
- **Cairuo and Lestocq**: run Nuts 'N' Nuts in Nasha Town. Cairuo is a Liyue-born merchant who works with Yelan; she appears in Yelan: Motives and Means, where she packs up samples to send back to the Ministry of Civil Affairs.
- **Sieciech**: an informant inside the Lightkeepers, whom Nefer calls "my old friend". In One There Was, Who Hunted the Moon (6.0) he briefs her at the scene of a Lightkeeper Sergeant Major's murder, reporting that Flins was seen entering and leaving around the time of the crime.
- **Iratxa**: a Marechaussee Phantom agent hunting someone called Thianges in Nasha Town; Nefer's intel notes read her as having a fruitless day.
- **Sarin and Tarno**: Sarin is the Adventurers' Guild branch master in Nasha Town; Tarno manages the Voynich Guild's souvenir outlet and has since represented the Guild in the League. The wiki counts both as informants.

## goals
### STANDARD
The Network's aim is trade: to move information from those who have it to those who will pay, and to protect its own members and interests in the process. Nefer does not do favours for free. Beyond commerce, the Network's other purpose is shaped by the people it serves, as in Varka's use of it to gather intelligence on the Abyss and the Wild Hunt.

## methods
### STANDARD
Information is bought and sold at the Curatorium of Secrets. Nefer reads clients carefully and prices by risk ("the riskier the job, the more it'll cost"), and she prizes clients who pay up front. Members use codenames or other aliases. The Network also takes on discreet jobs: the wiki's Lohen profile shows it asking the Knights' expedition to deal with an underground auction house in Nod-Krai that traded in smuggled and dangerous goods, a confidential request from its current head.
### LARGE +
Nefer's playable kit reflects the same style: the Curatorium lets her and Jahoda gather intel on certain Nod-Krai NPCs by approaching them, as an in-game mechanic, and Nefer's secret room opens after A Nation That Doesn't Exist. In the story, intelligence is often exchanged in person or through letters sent to the Curatorium. Nefer treats favours warily, telling Jahoda never to accept them because "they're the hardest debts to repay".

## history
### LITE
The Network was first hinted at in Diluc's fourth Character Story as a vast underground intelligence network in the north. It was named in 6.1 and became central to the Song of the Welkin Moon story, in which Varka brought the heroes to a Network contact hub.
### LARGE +
**Before Nefer.** The Network had an aging representative who wanted a successor. Nefer came to Nod-Krai and set up the Curatorium; by 6.1 she is the Network's head, though the handover is never shown. Her character story describes how Nod-Krai's so-called "leaders" once carved the land into private spheres of influence, and implies that she has personally destroyed a gang, led a certain boss to ruin and sparked conflict between rival factions.

**Diluc.** After his encounter with the Harbingers, Diluc joined, and he rose through the ranks. Nefer refers to him by the codename "Owl". She declines to say how they met, saying only that he pays promptly.

**Liyue.** Efforts to put the Network down in Liyue failed. Yelan and her associates turned back every approach, whether it came from official representatives or smuggled spies, with a polite but firm rejection. She says Liyue is one of the very few unknown spots on the Network's map.
**Song of the Welkin Moon (6.1).** When Rerir returned and Varka rescued the Traveler's group from the Wild Hunt, he took them to the Secret Base, the Knights' meeting place in Nod-Krai and a contact hub for the Network. He described it as the black market of intel, with headquarters in Nod-Krai and contacts in every nation, and said that working with it was the best way for a foreign expeditionary force to get reliable local information. He then introduced Nefer as its current head. She first refused to help, saying she did not work for free; Lauma offered a formal commission, which Nefer said she could not afford, and Nefer finally agreed "just this once" after Jahoda offered to cover the difference from her own pay and begged her. During the final planning in Echoes of Fate, Nefer refused to let Varka name Diluc and referred to him only as "Owl".

**Favonius Keep (6.3).** In the world quest On This Intoxicating Night, Varka told the Honorary Knight that the expeditionary force intended to form a long-term partnership with the Network and turn the temporary camp into an intelligence station, to share findings on the Abyss and keep collecting information about it.
### ULTRA +
**Yelan's dealings.** In Yelan: Motives and Means, Yelan is in Nasha Town to buy intel from Nefer while sending kuuvahki samples and Fatui photographs home through Cairuo. She says the Network has plenty of information that only she is willing to buy. She also warns the Traveler to stay vigilant with Nefer and says it is better to spend a little Mora than to end up owing her a favour. Nefer's voice-over confirms that Yelan is among the Network's most valued clients.

**After the Frost Moon.** The Network's role continues beyond 6.1. Lohen's story, set while the expedition was still in Nod-Krai, shows the Knights earning the Network's trust by closing the underground auction. No later story in 6.4-7.1 turned out to add a new development for the Network.

## relations
### LARGE
- **Knights of Favonius**: customers and partners. Varka's expeditionary force used the Network for local intelligence, and its camp, the Favonius Keep, was planned as an intelligence station.
- **Yelan**: a major client, and also an opponent in the sense that she and her colleagues have kept the Network out of Liyue.
- **Diluc**: a contact and informant in Mondstadt.
- **Curatorium of Secrets**: the Network's front and primary point of sale.
- **Lightkeepers and Voynich Guild**: some of their members double as informants; the Curatorium itself stays neutral between Nod-Krai's factions.
- **Dori**: Lord Sangemah Bay, a regular business partner of Nefer's who brings her news from Sumeru, and who is in Nod-Krai during Echoes of Fate. Nefer drained the kuuvahki charge from a pendant Alice had made for Dori, and promised to make it up.
- **Marechaussee Phantom and Spina di Rosula**: listed as allies on the wiki, but based only on NPC listings; see the uncertainty notes.
- **Ministry of Civil Affairs and the Qixing**: Yelan's side. She says her dealings with the Network are personal and not the Ministry's business.

## reputation
### LARGE
To outsiders it looks like a mysterious secret society. In-game dialogue disagrees: when Paimon calls it a secret society pulling the strings, the Traveler remarks that "secret society" seems like a stretch, since they know a whole bunch of the people involved. Within Nod-Krai, Nefer's skill with information is widely respected; Aino calls her "the intel queen of Nod-Krai", and the shut-doored Curatorium is a wellspring of urban legends in Nasha Town. The Network is regarded as the place to buy and sell intelligence whatever faction a person belongs to.

## secrets
### ULTRA
The Network's real work is hidden behind the Curatorium's ordinary commissions, such as lost cats, debts and missing persons. Jahoda is the only employee shown publicly, which hides the informants behind her. The identities behind its codenames are protected, and Nefer refuses to confirm even a well-known name. Diluc's membership is hidden from most people, although the Traveler knows he is involved. Nefer's predecessor and the details of her rise are not revealed.

## status
@version: 7.1
### LITE
As of 7.1 the Network is active under Nefer, based in Nasha Town, Nod-Krai.
### STANDARD +
The Knights of Favonius remain its clients; in 6.3 Varka said the expeditionary force planned a long-term partnership with it and would turn the Favonius Keep into an intelligence station. Nothing in 7.0 or 7.1, whose Archon Quests take place in Snezhnaya, was found that changes the Network's position.

## entry: Curatorium of Secrets
@keys: Curatorium of Secrets, the Curatorium
@version: 6.1
### STANDARD
The Curatorium of Secrets is the intelligence agency that fronts the Network in Nasha Town, Nod-Krai. Nefer owns and runs it, and Jahoda is its only official employee. Ashru the cat works as receptionist. In public it takes small commissions from anyone. Its second floor has a reception area and Nefer's office. Her secret room opens after A Nation That Doesn't Exist, and letters sent to the Curatorium appear there after Echoes of Fate. It abides by a policy of strict neutrality in relation to Nod-Krai's other factions.

## entry: Secret Base
@keys: Secret Base
@keys2: Nod-Krai, Varka, Nefer, Northern Intelligence Network, Knights of Favonius
@version: 6.1
### STANDARD
The Secret Base is a well-hidden underground meeting point that Varka calls the Knights of Favonius's meeting place in Nod-Krai and a contact hub for the Network. Nefer says she helped the Knights settle in Nod-Krai and, as the one running the Network, knows where it is. Varka brought the Traveler's group here in 6.1 after rescuing them from the Wild Hunt, and it was where Nefer met them at his request.

## entry: Yelan and the Network

@keys2: Northern Intelligence Network, Nefer, Curatorium of Secrets
@version: 6.1
### LARGE
Yelan and her colleagues have kept the Network out of Liyue, turning away every approach, both official representatives and smuggled spies. Even so, she buys intel from Nefer for large sums, and Nefer in turn says she is one of the Network's most valued clients, one who pays up front, in cash and in full, often at two or three times the asking price. Yelan says her purchases are personal, not on behalf of the Ministry of Civil Affairs.

## entry: Diluc's path to the Network
@keys: Diluc's resignation, Diluc's journey
@keys2: Northern Intelligence Network, Harbingers, Delusion
@version: 1.0
@spoiler: minor
### LARGE
After resigning from the Knights, Diluc left Mondstadt and travelled all seven nations tracing the source of the Delusion that had killed his father. He destroyed many Fatui strongholds, which drew the Harbingers' attention. A third-party observer from the north, part of a vast underground intelligence network, saved him from them, said that they had been watching him for some time, and approved of his methods. He later joined the network and rose to the upper levels. The character story does not name the Network, but the wiki connects it to the Northern Intelligence Network and the observer to the Curatorium of Secrets.

## entry: The expedition's underground auction operation
@keys: underground auction, auction house
@keys2: Lohen, Northern Intelligence Network, Nod-Krai
@version: 6.4
### ULTRA
In Lohen's fifth Character Story, the head of the Network asks the expeditionary force to deal with an underground auction house that profits from the chaos in Nod-Krai by trading in smuggled goods and dangerous items. The Knights plan carefully, and Lohen's task is to quietly capture isolated individuals and learn when and where the auction will happen. He instead strikes the heart of the operation on his own, secures all attendees and keeps the fighting away from Nasha Town. Jean writes to Varka that, judged by outcome, it was a true act of protection, but asks that he make clear to the Network's contacts that such conduct is not normal for the Knights.

## entry: Favonius Keep partnership
@keys: Favonius Keep, intelligence station
@keys2: Northern Intelligence Network, Varka, Nod-Krai
@version: 6.3
### ULTRA
At the Favonius Keep, Varka told the Honorary Knight that the expeditionary force would partner with the Network over the long term and turn its temporary camp into an intelligence station. The force had seen Abyssal disasters of many kinds across Teyvat, all unusually cunning and varied, and he hoped the Network could help share and gather information about them. He called Nod-Krai a good place for an outpost, as winds from everywhere pass through it.
