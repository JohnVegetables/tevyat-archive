## summary
@version: 7.0
### LITE
The Rokot is a covert popular movement in Snezhnaya that serves "the common people" and works against Project Stuzha, the Tsaritsa's grand scheme that has strained the country's supply of Kresnik Kristall. Members use codenames and mostly do not know who one another are. The movement avoids violence and tries to help the public by quieter means. The Fatui keep watch on it and count it as an opponent. Alyosha, Vodyanitsa and Odette are members, and the Traveler joins in the Archon Quest.
### STANDARD +
The name is the Russian word for a rumble. The Traveler and Paimon joke about whether it means a howl or a gust of wind. The movement acts as an outlet for people who disagree with how the country is run but have no easy way to be heard. Alyosha describes it as giving the disaffected false identities and an outlet for their frustrations.

## structure
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
The Rokot is deliberately loose. Members get a codename on joining, usually do not meet in person and often do not know each other's real identities, which keeps each one independent and means nobody else is exposed if something goes wrong. Members choose their own methods, so long as the result does not break the group's principles, and they may also choose not to act at all, since many members have never done anything. A president approves new recruits by letter and sends notices to all members.
### LARGE +
A written code of conduct exists, though Vodyanitsa admits she has never finished reading it. Technicians check equipment for members' projects, and some members act as sources of intelligence. New members fill in an application form with a name, race, codename and a stated reason for joining. In the Archon Quest the choices are mostly jokes (a Primogem as a "race", for example), and Vodyanitsa accepts the Traveler's form regardless. Recruitment goes by recommendation: Alyosha wrote to the president about the Traveler after working beside them, and the reply came back almost at once. Because members hide even from one another, a member can hold a second life, as a Fatuus for example, or work beside another member without either knowing the other's affiliation.

## leadership
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
No leader is named. The only authority shown is a "president" who answers recruitment requests, and the leadership's notice to members ends with a motto: "We act only for the greater glory and solidarity of our nation. May the aurora shine ever on and the tundra always stand firm." Nothing identifies the president or where he works.

## members
@version: 7.0
### LITE
Alyosha (a hunter from Sretomorozsk), Vodyanitsa (Prima Donna of the Korolevskiy Troupe) and Odette (Prima Ballerina of the troupe and a Fatuus), plus the Traveler. Trapeznikov, a research-minded NPC, also belongs.
### LARGE +
- **Alyosha** (Electro Vision): a young hunter with a strong sense of justice. He recommends the Traveler. He sets money aside partly for the Rokot's medicine and plays Genius Invokation TCG against fellow members, who often beat him. When Polonsky hints that the Rokot recruits people with his talents, Alyosha claims never to have heard of it.
- **Vodyanitsa** (Hydro Vision): the Prima Donna of the Korolevskiy Troupe. Alyosha calls her a vital source of intelligence for the Rokot.
- **Odette** (Cryo Vision): Prima Ballerina and a Fatuus. Under the codename Lelek she was the movement's best-known and most controversial member. She says Project Stuzha has emptied the theatre while corrupt people prey on a struggling public, and she refuses to turn a blind eye. Her codename is now withdrawn and she is suspended.
- **Trapeznikov**: a bookish man obsessed with Shadow Realm technology. He says the Rokot helped him after he spent his Kristall funds on research data, and that he might otherwise have frozen.
- **The Traveler**: invited by Alyosha and Vodyanitsa in Silence Alone is Disfavored. Players choose a codename from a list of jokes such as Wayfarer, Chest Mimic or King Jabberfries!.
### ULTRA +
Mitya, the energy expert who attends every performance, is not shown to be a member. In Silence Alone is Disfavored Vodyanitsa and Alyosha plan to pass him information so that an official investigation looks like his idea. Alyosha's dog Tugarin is not a member, but his nose let Alyosha recognise Vodyanitsa during an operation despite disguises. They chose not to report it upward.

## goals
@version: 7.0
### LITE
The Rokot wants to improve ordinary people's lives and to see Project Stuzha ended, which is why it opposes the plan's backers and, on occasion, the Tsaritsa herself.
### LARGE +
Members focus on practical help: medicine, repairs to freezing stations, exposing waste, and getting scarce Kristall to where it is most needed. Alyosha says ordinary people have no easy way of being heard in Snezhnograd, and the Rokot gives them one. He also hopes that Snezhnaya will one day change and that "the Rokot won't need to exist forever". Odette says Project Stuzha has driven audiences away from her theatre while corrupt people prey on a struggling public, and that she refuses to turn a blind eye.

## methods
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
The Rokot works by gathering information, steering evidence toward people with authority and doing small acts of service, such as recon, repairs and distributing Kristall.
### LARGE +
In practice the work means recon at factories, steering information toward people the authorities take seriously, repairing shelters and stations, and distributing Kristall. Alyosha says quiet work fits his creed: he can stand up for what the Rokot believes in without reaching for his gun. He also practises low-profile discretion as a matter of principle, for example keeping secret that he built a shelter for refugees. The movement is non-violent by principle, but each member decides how to act, and one went well beyond the others. As Lelek, Odette became notorious. Vodyanitsa says the more decent members disapprove: Lelek believed votes in the Veche were all that mattered, and used threats, coercion or bribery to change them. Lelek worked alone, and Valeriy has read the case files at the Armory Palace.
### ULTRA +
The operation in Silence Alone is Disfavored shows the typical shape of a Rokot job. The target is Chugunov, owner of the Iskra Manufacturing Plant. Reconnaissance suggests its energy use is beyond the legal limit, but the Rokot lacks direct evidence; it wants proof to present to the Veche to demand a shutdown, so that the Kristall can be allocated where it is most needed.

## history
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
Nothing in the sources checked says how the Rokot began. It first appears in the 7.0 Archon Quest after the Traveler reaches Snezhnaya. In Gunfire in the Silent Lands the Fatui officer Polonsky mentions the group to Alyosha, who then explains it to the Traveler. In Silence Alone is Disfavored the Traveler joins. The Chapter VII story then turns on the exposure of Lelek.
### LARGE +
**Svetloledovka to recruitment.** After the incident at Svetloledovka, Alyosha arranges tickets to the Korolevskiy Theater and a Rokot contact. Odette has spoken highly of the Traveler, and in the greenroom, once Odette has left, Alyosha shows Vodyanitsa the president's approval and both reveal their membership. They recruit the Traveler.

**Lelek.** In the Veche's Project Stuzha debate, the finance scholar Arapov alludes to "extremist activists", and the commentator Mironova raises the Rokot and the notorious Lelek by name. Childe and Sandrone come to suspect Odette. In Lelek's Confession, while the theatre's audience is trapped by Ronova's Curse of Death, two merchants are found dead and Chugunov, who had been threatened by Lelek, blames Lelek. The Traveler passes Childe's intel to Vodyanitsa and "Alyosha", and Odette publicly admits to being Lelek while denying the murders. The killer was Ronova, who had taken Alyosha's form after he died; once the disguise was destroyed everyone in the theatre woke up alive.

**Aftermath.** In Mistilteinn, after Project Stuzha is suspended, the Rokot's leadership writes to Alyosha and Vodyanitsa. It permanently withdraws the codename over the effect of Lelek's radical actions on the group's reputation and social stability, says Lelek acted for the people's benefit, and notes that the Rokot negotiated with the authorities to waive all penalties. Lelek is suspended and might return under a new codename depending on future conduct. The letter reminds members that motives and methods matter equally, and mentions Project Stuzha's suspension as an "interim operational victory" that must not breed complacency.
### ULTRA +
**After the Archon Quest.** In 7.0 and 7.1 side stories Alyosha and Vodyanitsa still act as members. In A Thread of Dawn-Light, at Rankova Zorya, Alyosha has Rokot technicians inspect a furnace and hears of a local gang from a Rokot friend. In The Tsaritsa's New Portrait (7.1) Alyosha, Vodyanitsa and Odette commission a "warm" portrait of the Tsaritsa, and Alyosha plans to give the leftover funds to the Rokot. Vodyanitsa is surprised that a Rokot member, the Traveler, is now the Tsaritsa's special envoy; Alyosha replies that it is not that surprising, since they already have a Fatuus working with them.

## relations
@version: 7.0
### LARGE
- **Fatui**: officially an opponent. Polonsky says the Rokot is non-violent but opposes Project Stuzha, so the Fatui keep tabs on it. Yet one of its stars, Odette, is a Fatuus, and Alyosha admits he never expected a Fatui member to share the group's principles.
- **Snezhnograd Veche**: the Rokot is a topic of debate there. Vodyanitsa explains that the commentator Mironova is rephrasing Pantalone's remark that critics take no responsibility for their actions, and the critics he means are the Rokot.
- **Tsaritsa**: the Rokot opposes Project Stuzha and, occasionally, the Tsaritsa herself. Alyosha stresses that members still believe in her, and that they differ only over how best to tackle the country's problems.
- **Korolevskiy Troupe**: two of its stars are members, and Vodyanitsa's greenroom doubles as a meeting place.
### ULTRA +
- **Armory Palace**: Valeriy knows the codename Lelek from case files and describes it as an anti-Stuzha loner.
- **Authorities**: Alyosha says a group like this exists in a grey area and that a crackdown would follow if the Veche thought a line was crossed. In Mistilteinn the Rokot nevertheless negotiated a waiver of penalties for Lelek.

## reputation
@version: 7.0
### LARGE
Polonsky calls it non-violent with the goal of improving the lives of common folk, Pantalone's side treats its critics as irresponsible, and Alyosha says it is in a grey area. Lelek's methods damaged the group's reputation, as Odette's story shows and the organisation's own letter admits.

## secrets
@version: 7.0
@spoiler: major
### ULTRA
Odette is Lelek, the Rokot's most infamous member, and she is also a Fatuus. Vodyanitsa learns this only when the Traveler passes on Childe's intel (the "Alyosha" present at that moment was Ronova in disguise); Alyosha later says he was very surprised to learn that Odette was a member. Members' identities are guarded the same way: Vodyanitsa and Alyosha discovered each other's membership by chance, through Tugarin's nose, and did not report it.

## status
@version: 7.1
### LITE
As of 7.1 the Rokot is active. Its president remains unnamed, the codename Lelek is retired, and its members continue everyday good works in Snezhnaya.
### STANDARD +
Odette is suspended and may rejoin later under a new codename. The Traveler remains a member with a codename of their own choosing. Nothing in 7.0 or 7.1 resolves the long-term fate of the movement.

## entry: Lelek (codename)
@keys: Lelek
@keys2: Rokot, Project Stuzha, Snezhnograd Veche, Odette
@version: 7.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Lelek was the Rokot's most famous and most infamous member, an anonymous figure who made ending Project Stuzha an obsession. Vodyanitsa says Lelek used threats, coercion or bribery to change votes in the Veche, unlike the more decent members. Valeriy knows the codename from Armory Palace files as a lone operator. In Lelek's Confession Chugunov blames Lelek for the deaths of two merchants, but Vodyanitsa objects that such a roundabout method is not Lelek's style; the killer was in fact Ronova, disguised as Alyosha. The holder is Odette, who joined while serving the Fatui. Citing the effect of Lelek's radical actions on its reputation and on social stability, the Rokot withdrew the codename for good and suspended her.

## entry: Rokot application form
@keys: Rokot application form, Rokot recruitment
@keys2: Vodyanitsa, Alyosha, Traveler
@version: 7.0
### LARGE
The form asks for a name, a race, a codename and a reason for joining. The options are playful: names such as Monpai, races such as Primogem or Dodoco, codenames such as Wayfarer, Chest Mimic, Seamount Abyss, The 12th, King Jabberfries! or Lord of the Seven Calamities, and reasons that range from "I oppose Project Stuzha" to "I need a part-time job". Vodyanitsa tweaks any answer she considers unacceptable and says the form will be kept. Filling it in earns an achievement called The Howling Gale.

## entry: Rokot operation against Chugunov
@keys: Iskra Manufacturing Plant, Iskra plant
@keys2: Rokot, Chugunov, Vodyanitsa
@version: 7.0
### LARGE
When the Traveler joins, the Rokot's current target is Chugunov, owner of the Iskra Manufacturing Plant. Recon suggests its energy consumption is far above the legal limit. The Rokot wants proof for the Veche so that the plant is shut down and the Kristall goes where it is needed most. Vodyanitsa had hoped Chugunov would let something slip in conversation, but he stays tight-lipped, so she plans to let the issue slip to Mitya, a renowned energy expert whom the authorities would take seriously. Later, in Mistilteinn, Chugunov tells his staff he is closing the plant.
