## summary
### LITE
Hvergelmir was a secret research institute of the Pale Star Court in Snezhnaya, active before the Cataclysm 500 years ago. It was devoted to bio-alchemy and the study of the Shadow Realm and its Hyperborean ruins. The Belyi Tsar founded it and entrusted it to his adopted daughter Aksinya, whose public post was Court Jester. Its aim was to find "the stairway to the vacant seat in the court of the stars" and, concretely, to build a Proto-Man who would let the Tsar become the Primordial Human. It is long defunct, and its ruined halls lie beneath the Pale Crown Palace in the White Birch Snowgrave.
### STANDARD +
Its products outlived it. The Eye of Graeae, the Aberrant Chimeric Monsters and Koshchei's later experiments all trace back to Hvergelmir. The institute is revealed in the 7.0 World Quests of Snezhnaya. Nothing in 6.x (Nod-Krai) or 7.1 was found that involves it directly.

## structure
### STANDARD
Hvergelmir was not a public body. The Belyi Tsar founded it, but Aksinya ran it quietly, and her title of Court Jester served as cover for her real influence in the Pale Star Court. Its leading researchers each had their own laboratory in the premises beneath the Pale Crown Palace: Aksinya, Alvis (Grand Duke of the Domovoy), Koshchei the Deathless, and Pavlina (Grand Duchess of the Snegurochka). Other staff included Izmaylov, Azazel and Vsevolod. The ruins today show four named labs, shelves of notes and a hidden chamber where the Proto-Man's egg was kept.
### LARGE +
The leaders came from different peoples (Domovoy, Snegurochka, a Snowland Fae alchemist) plus Aksinya, a human survivor of Hyperborea. Their notes show a mix of rivalry and ordinary office life, such as a bet over drinks at Podvorye Restaurant, a watch given to a colleague to keep to his schedule, and a note asking a colleague to feed a pet cat. Alvis had also placed Izmaylov at the institute as a spy on Aksinya.

## leadership
### STANDARD
The Belyi Tsar set the goal and gave his adopted daughter the day-to-day running of the institute. Aksinya was the real leader, and even Alvis, who looked down on humans, came to respect her after the research reached what he regarded as success. Alvis, Pavlina and Koshchei were the senior researchers beside her. Alvis taught Aksinya what he knew, and he is the one who declared her dead after the Cataclysm.
### LARGE +
Aksinya found a place for people others might have discarded. The Tsar spared Koshchei from execution after he experimented on Snowland Fae and recruited him to Hvergelmir, where Aksinya met him. She also took Izmaylov on as a student and gave him a pen when she anchored him on a Shadow Realm expedition. Izmaylov had been sent to spy on her but became a true believer.

## members
### STANDARD
- **Aksinya**: Court Jester and leader. A Hyperborean survivor found as an infant in a life-preserving vessel.
- **Alvis**: long-lived Grand Duke of the Domovoy, an ally of the Tsar from before the Tsar ruled all Snezhnaya.
- **Koshchei**: bio-alchemist, the creator of the Aberrant Chimeric Monsters.
- **Pavlina**: Snegurochka Grand Duchess and alchemist, with a laboratory in the Pale Crown Palace.
- **Izmaylov**: Domovoy researcher and Aksinya's disciple. He later became president of the Royal Energy Commission.
- **Azazel** and **Vsevolod**: researchers seen only in a flashback in 7.0. Vsevolod works for Alvis.
### LARGE +
Zver, an intelligent Chimeric Winged Lion, began as one of Koshchei's creations and was taken in by the Tsar. It is linked to Hvergelmir by origin but is not listed as a member of the institute. The Eye of Graeae was both the institute's masterwork and, in effect, its conscience (see the entry on the Eye of Graeae project).

## goals
### STANDARD
The Belyi Tsar's charge was to find a passage to a place where "worry and sorrow would no longer trouble them", described as a stairway to the vacant seat in the court of the stars. This meant studying the world's limits and taboos. Quest text states the concrete aim: engineer a Proto-Man who would be the key for the Tsar to become the Primordial Human and change the world. The Tsar's own origin lies in a failed Hyperborean attempt at a Primordial Human, so the project took up what the Hyperboreans had started.
### LARGE +
Not everyone agreed. In a flashback Azazel hints that the research might be best abandoned after the failure of an earlier experiment. Alvis' private notes in the ruins show a darker ambition: a longing to obtain the Proto-Man's power for himself.

## methods
### STANDARD
The researchers made expeditions into Hyperborean ruins in the Shadow Realm. They recovered technology, including devices that stored elemental energy, and modified the designs to produce firearms. Bio-alchemy let them combine animals and later other beings: Prime Chimeric Nexuses allowed embryos of several species to share one body, and the Eitr was later found to be a bio-furnace that fully absorbed embryos. They also kept a Proto-Man in an egg and monitored him constantly.
### LARGE +
Koshchei treated life as a puzzle that could be taken apart and put back together, and he noted that the Hyperboreans may have turned from the Eitr toward more stable forms of life, such as the Leshy. Aksinya treated his work with dry practicality. When he grew an ear on her hand to drive her away, she was unmoved, and she told him that any repeat would need an official request. The culmination of their research was the Eye of Graeae.

## history
### LARGE
**Origins.** The Belyi Tsar found a functioning life-preserving vessel holding an infant girl while exploring Hyperborean ruins in the Shadow Realm, and raised her as Aksinya. Inspired by the technology, he later founded Hvergelmir and put her in charge, drawing in Alvis, Pavlina and Koshchei.

**The work.** Over time the researchers explored Hyperborean ruins, built firearms from recovered designs and developed chimeric creatures. Their greatest product was the Eye of Graeae, built to be the key for the Tsar to become the Primordial Human. It was divided into three segments before the Cataclysm to create the first "prototype human".

**The Cataclysm.** The Belyi Tsar died fighting the Abyss 500 years ago and Anastasya became Tsaritsa. A flashback shows Hvergelmir staff, around the time of the Tsar's death, fearing that the new ruler would ban alchemy and end their work.

**Betrayal and end.** Realising that a fully restored Eye would bring disaster to humans and fae, the Eye of Graeae persuaded Koshchei, Pavlina and Alvis to stop Aksinya. They pushed her into the Shadow Realm and split its power in three, and she was said to have died. Koshchei settled in Flamefeather Valley as "Doctor Moreau", Pavlina returned to the House of Hesperides, and the Eye of Graeae was left in an abandoned prototype train.
### ULTRA +
**After 500 years.** Aksinya, kept young by the Shadow Realm, returned and questioned the aged Izmaylov about what had become of the world. She planned to restore the Eye of Graeae's power over about twenty years, but the Traveler, helping the Eye recover its three fragments (Flamefeather Valley, the House of Hesperides and the Pale Crown Palace), forced the matter. At the Pale Crown Palace Aksinya took the Eye and moved its restored power into the Belyi Tsar's inert body in the Court of Bliss. The Traveler defeated her and the resulting Primordial Human, and both fell into the depths of the Shadow Realm.

**The ruins.** Hvergelmir's premises survive beneath the Pale Crown Palace. In "To Gaze Upon the Dreaming Pale Crown" the Traveler is carried by means unknown, during a train journey, into the intact institute as it was, among researchers who cannot see them.

## relations
### LARGE
- **Belyi Tsar / Pale Star Court**: Hvergelmir was his project, and he gave it his tacit protection. Without it, the staff feared that the new Tsaritsa would end their work.
- **Tsaritsa (Anastasya)**: she succeeded the Tsar. The flashback suggests Hvergelmir expected hostility from her towards alchemy. No direct meeting with the Hvergelmir leadership is described.
- **Royal Energy Commission**: Izmaylov first hoped to work there, and later took its presidency from Alvis after Aksinya's disappearance.
- **Domovoy, Snegurochka and Snowland Fae**: the senior researchers came from these peoples.
- **Eye of Graeae**: its creation; it later turned the senior researchers against Aksinya. The Eye survives as the Traveler's companion in 7.0.
### ULTRA +
- **Okurov and the Leshy**: Koshchei's later work at Okurov grew from his Hvergelmir research, ending with villagers transformed into the Perfect Tree or into Leshy.

## reputation
### ULTRA
The institute was meant to be secret and is not public knowledge in modern Snezhnaya. Its leading figures are known mostly from legend or lost records: Aksinya was said to have died in the Shadow Realm. The Eye of Graeae remembers the institute, and the flashback shows how its staff saw themselves, as people who crossed into forbidden territory under the Tsar's protection.

## secrets
### ULTRA
The Court Jester title was a cover for Aksinya's real standing. Her colleagues' betrayal, her true fate, and the Proto-Man project were not public. Alvis' private notes reveal that he wanted the Proto-Man's power for himself.

## status
@version: 7.0
### LITE
Hvergelmir no longer exists. After the Cataclysm its leading members dispersed and its leader was trapped in the Shadow Realm. Its premises survive as the Ruined Hvergelmir.
### STANDARD +
As of 7.1 nothing further has been revealed about the institute. Aksinya and the Primordial Human fell into the depths of the Shadow Realm after the 7.0 confrontation. The Traveler still has the Eye of Graeae device, which kept its functions but not its personality.

## entry: Eye of Graeae project
@keys: Eye of Graeae, Proto-Man, Proto Man, prototype human
@keys2: Hvergelmir, Aksinya, Belyi Tsar
@version: 7.0
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
The Eye of Graeae was the culmination of Hvergelmir's research. It was meant as the key for the Belyi Tsar to become the Primordial Human, and it was divided into three segments to create the first "prototype human". Its stated aim was to bring humanity to "Tomorrow". Understanding what its full authority would mean, it asked to be split and persuaded Koshchei, Pavlina and Alvis to keep it from Aksinya. After the Cataclysm it was left in an abandoned train and lost many of its memories. In 7.0 it recognised the Traveler as a Descender and asked for their help restoring its power. In the end it asked the Traveler to stop both itself and Aksinya.

## entry: Koshchei's chimeric research
@keys: Prime Chimeric Nexus, Chimeric Beast, Doctor Moreau
@keys2: Koshchei, Hvergelmir
@version: 7.0
### LARGE
Koshchei's notes in the Ruined Hvergelmir describe how a Prime Chimeric Nexus lets embryos of different species share one circulatory system. Three types was the accepted limit, but Aksinya's version briefly held five, as part of a bet. The Eitr (Embryonic-Ichoric-Telluric-Rudiment), a Hyperborean creation, could absorb any embryo, though it could not preserve the special traits of each. Koshchei used this line of work to create the Aberrant Chimeric Monsters. After the Cataclysm, working under the name Doctor Moreau in Okurov, he pursued eternal life.

## entry: Alvis and Pavlina at Hvergelmir
@keys: Grand Duke Alvis, Grand Duchess Pavlina
@keys2: Hvergelmir, Aksinya
@version: 7.0
### LARGE
Alvis, a long-lived Domovoy, feared and resented humanity but set aside that feeling for the Tsar's sake and taught Aksinya. He also had Izmaylov spy on the institute. His private notes in the ruins show an obsessive wish to get hold of the Proto-Man's power. Pavlina, a Snegurochka alchemist, met Aksinya as a child at a banquet in her manor and became her friend. She had a laboratory in the Pale Crown Palace and a cat named Lara. After the betrayal she returned to the House of Hesperides, where she later pursued her plan involving Ikhor.

## entry: Izmaylov and Aksinya's pen
@keys: Izmaylov, Foundryman
@keys2: Hvergelmir, Aksinya, Alvis
@version: 7.0
### LARGE
Alvis placed Izmaylov at the institute to spy, but Aksinya took him as a student, and the kindness she showed him made him a believer. She anchored him during an expedition into Hyperborean ruins and gave him a pen. When she vanished, he suspected foul play, turned against Alvis and took the presidency of the Royal Energy Commission. When Aksinya returned after 500 years, she found him elderly. He confessed his joy at seeing her and returned her pen, and they parted owing each other nothing.

## entry: The Ruined Hvergelmir
@keys: Ruined Hvergelmir, Pale Crown Palace laboratories
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
The Ruined Hvergelmir is a hidden space in the White Birch Snowgrave, beneath the Pale Crown Palace, which the Traveler reaches on an old prototype train. It contains four laboratories belonging to Aksinya, Alvis, Koshchei and Pavlina, notes on alchemy, potion formulae and a locked chamber. In the 7.0 world quest "To Gaze Upon the Dreaming Pale Crown" the Traveler sees the institute as it was, with Izmaylov, Azazel and Vsevolod unaware of their presence, and finds Pavlina's note about her cat.
