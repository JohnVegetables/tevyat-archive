## summary
### LITE
Spantamad is one of the six Darshans of the Sumeru Akademiya. Its field is elementalism, the study of the elements and the elemental reactions and changes that follow from them, with related work in Ley Line studies and alchemy. Its school colour is red. Because Ley Line research needs fieldwork, it has the most Driyosh, the Akademiya's traveling scholars. Lisa of the Knights of Favonius and Cyno both studied here.
### STANDARD +
Its crest shows a peacock with seven feathers above a tree, which the wiki reads as the seven elements and the Ley Lines of the Irminsul. The Darshan's name comes from the Zoroastrian goddess Spenta Armaiti, who is associated with the earth. Spantamad has also had the highest percentage of Interdarshan Championship winners.

## structure
### STANDARD
Spantamad is led by a sage, one of the Six Great Sages, and staffed by professors, researchers and students. A large share of its scholars are Driyosh: they travel in the field to study Ley Lines. Its disciplines include alchemy, elemental science and Ley Line studies.
### LARGE +
Elective courses in other Darshans are open to students in general. Spantamad had a stream of mechanical-life research until it was banned.

## leadership
### LITE
Alkindi is the current Spantamad sage. Cyrus, a retired professor, is a former sage.
### STANDARD +
Alkindi was once Cyrus's classmate and rival. He threw himself into many research fields for years before becoming sage, and his receding hairline is blamed on academic stress. He is tall, with grey hair and round blue goggles, and wears a crimson sherwani. In As All Falls to Emptiness (Chapter III) he helped the Traveler by investigating Ley Line anomalies.
### LARGE +
Cyrus was an eminent sage, and a line of his, "Change is the only constant", appears on Bimarstan's Proverb Column. His students included Lisa, Cyno and Gafoor. When Nefer arrived at the Akademiya as a young girl, Azar's policy barred her from the entrance exam as one of the desert-folk, and Cyrus complained that such students were not treated fairly. Later, as a sage, he wrote a formal letter of protest. That letter ended up among unclaimed mail in the House of Daena, where an older Nefer found it.

## members
### STANDARD
- **Alkindi**: the current sage.
- **Cyrus**: a retired professor and former sage who raised Cyno as a foster son.
- **Lisa**: a former student who graduated in two years, got disillusioned with the Akademiya and joined the Knights of Favonius.
- **Cyno**: a former student of Cyrus who became General Mahamatra.
- **Hosseini**: a Driyosh from Vimara Village, a Spantamad researcher on Ley Lines who is under 30 but looks middle-aged from thesis stress. He appears in Version 2.2's event quests.
- **Abattouy**: a researcher who studied mechanical lifeforms and was expelled for continuing; he later built Karkata.
### LARGE +
Gafoor is a professor who ran a Ley Line research project in the Second Blooming event (limited-time) and was Lisa's classmate under Cyrus. Hajanad is a student alchemist who appears in event content. Kimiya, Zinat and others are minor Spantamad scholars who appear in events and open-world commissions.

## goals
### STANDARD
Spantamad researchers treat the Seven Great Elements as the essence of all matter and life. They aim to understand elemental reactions, how Ley Lines behave and what alchemy can produce, and to apply that knowledge. Hosseini's own goals show the school's practical side: he wants to use ancient devices such as the Energy Amplifier and Pursina's Spikes, and regrets that the Spikes lie unused in the Akademiya's warehouses.

## methods
### LARGE
Sitt sums up the school as conducting reaction experiments when not travelling in the field. Driyosh scholars go out to the Ley Lines, collect data and run commissions. Alchemists brew elixirs and test reactions in labs. In the Glittering Elixirs web event (limited-time), the potions and elixirs shown could be taught at Spantamad. Hosseini's thesis work is an example: he went to collect a perfectly preserved Irminsul Fruit, which burst into fragments in a monster ambush, and the Traveler helped recover them and take them to a Domain with an Energy Amplifier to gather data.

## history
### LARGE
Spantamad is one of the Six Great Schools set up with the Akademiya under Greater Lord Rukkhadevata. It was prominent in mechanical-life research until overzealous researchers began to perform unregulated vivisections and other cruel experiments on animals, after which the Akademiya banned the field; students caught researching it risk expulsion. Abattouy is a case in point: he kept working on machines after his expulsion.
### ULTRA +
**Under Azar.** Azar's policy blocked desert-born students from the entrance exam, which Cyrus protested. When Azar fell in Chapter III, the four Darshans that had supported him had their sages replaced; the wiki does not say whether Alkindi was one of the replacements.

**Interdarshan Championship.** The championship has long been a stage for the Darshans' talent, and Spantamad has produced more champions by percentage than any other Darshan. Huvishka names Cyrus, Ruzicka, Lisa and Cyno as examples of the school's talent. The championship later removed the events in which people fought to the death.

## relations
### STANDARD
- **Other Darshans**: Spantamad is one of six, alongside Amurta, Rtawahist, Haravatat, Vahumana and Kshahrewar. Rtawahist, its sibling in astronomy, also had its sage replaced after Azar's fall.
- **The Knights of Favonius**: Lisa carries her Spantamad training to Mondstadt and works as the order's librarian.
- **The Fatui and Sumeru politics**: nothing in the sources read ties the Darshan as a whole to the Fatui plot, though Cyno, once its student, played a key role in Chapter III.
### LARGE +
- **Cyrus and Cyno**: Cyno was raised as Cyrus's foster son and followed him into the Akademiya.
- **Nefer**: she was assigned to Vahumana after being barred from the entrance exam, and Cyrus protested the policy on behalf of such students.

## reputation
### ULTRA
Spantamad is known as the Darshan of talented achievers. Its Driyosh are a common sight in the field, and its overworked researchers, such as Hosseini, are used for jokes about thesis stress. Banned mechanical-life research remains a mark against the school's earlier freedom.

## secrets
### ULTRA
No hidden activity of the Darshan as a whole is established. Individual stories include Abattouy's secret laboratory and Cyrus's unread protest letter.

## entry: Alkindi
@keys: Alkindi

### STANDARD
Alkindi is the current sage of Spantamad and a former classmate and rival of Cyrus. He is tall, with medium-length grey hair that flares at the sides, round blue goggles and a crimson sherwani. His receding hairline is blamed on academic stress. In Chapter III's As All Falls to Emptiness he helped the Traveler by studying Ley Line anomalies.

## entry: Cyrus
@keys: Cyrus
@keys2: Spantamad, Akademiya, Cyno, Lisa, professor
### STANDARD
Cyrus is a retired Akademiya professor and former Spantamad sage. Lisa and Cyno were his students, and he raised Cyno as his foster son. His line "Change is the only constant" is on Bimarstan's Proverb Column. He once nearly died exploring the Great Red Sand and was saved by the Temple of Silence. He also protested the rule that kept desert-born students out of the entrance exam, and his formal protest letter ended up unclaimed in the House of Daena.

## entry: Hosseini
@keys: Hosseini
@keys2: Spantamad, Driyosh, Irminsul Fruit
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
Hosseini is a Driyosh in Spantamad from Vimara Village who appears in event quests such as Energy Amplifier. His hair has greyed from the stress of getting papers approved. For his thesis he went to collect a preserved Irminsul Fruit found by Liyue's Adventurers' Guild. Treasure Hoarders ambushed him and the fruit burst into fragments, so the Traveler helped recover them. He admires inventor-researchers such as Pursina, who made Pursina's Spikes, and Ayesha, who made a Chaos Prospector.

## entry: Abattouy
@keys: Abattouy
@keys2: Spantamad, Karkata, Tighnari
@version: 3.0
### LARGE
Abattouy is a former Spantamad researcher who enrolled to study mechanical lifeforms, which the Akademiya banned. After his expulsion he carried on in secret, building a machine called Karkata that resembled a Ruin Defender and had the mindset of a child. Roughly two years before the start of the game he built a laboratory in a secluded area. He was Tighnari's junior at the Akademiya and appears in flashbacks in The Unanswerable Problems. He is also credited with the eight Energy Extraction Devices of The Foolish Fatuus.

## entry: Driyosh
@keys: Driyosh, traveling scholar, traveling scholars
@keys2: Spantamad, Akademiya
### STANDARD
Driyosh are the Akademiya's traveling scholars. Spantamad has the most of them because Ley Line research needs fieldwork. Their commissions often reach the Adventurers' Guild, which is how the Traveler meets scholars such as Hosseini.

## entry: Interdarshan Championship
@keys: Interdarshan Championship
@keys2: Spantamad, Akademiya, Cyno
### LARGE
The Interdarshan Championship is a competition among the Darshans in which Spantamad has had the highest percentage of champions. Huvishka lists Cyrus, Ruzicka, Lisa and Cyno as examples of its talent. The championship later cut the events in which people fought to the death. Twenty years ago it was the setting for Kaveh's father's entry for the Diadem of Knowledge.
