## summary
### LITE
Vahumana is one of the six Darshans (schools) of the Sumeru Akademiya. Its field is aetiology, the study of the causes and origins of things, and it covers history, sociology and other social sciences. Its colour is yellow. It is led by Sage Baladhuri.
### STANDARD +
The name comes from the Avestan Vohu Manah, "Good Mind", as the other Darshan names are drawn from the Amesha Spenta. Its emblem's mascot resembles a horse.
### LARGE +
The receptionist Sitt sums up the Darshan with a quip: if it is not memorising facts, it is refuting rumours. She says it began with the rejection and reflection of the theories King Deshret left behind, and then became the basis of the social sciences.

## structure
### STANDARD
Vahumana is one of six Darshans, alongside Amurta, Haravatat, Kshahrewar, Rtawahist and Spantamad. Each has a Sage at its head, and the six Sages sit together in the Akademiya's leadership. Scholars carry ranks such as Herbad (Yarshater is a Vahumana Herbad) and Professor (Manetho is a Vahumana professor in the quest Dance of Death: Serpent and Scorpion). Students normally take an entrance exam that decides which Darshans they are eligible for.
### LARGE +
The Akademiya's school colours mark each Darshan, and Vahumana's is yellow. A scholar's Darshan shapes their approach even in a casual argument. In an open-world exchange outside the House of Daena, the Vahumana researcher Khaldun and a Haravatat scholar, Yavanani, trade jibes about each other's methods, and Yavanani calls the sociological approach to history "putting the cart before the horse".

## leadership
### LITE
Baladhuri is the current Vahumana Sage.
### STANDARD +
He is a kind, gentle scholar, tall, with an elaborate stone-grey beard he is proud of. He took office before Azar fell from power. He and Naphis of Amurta opposed the Akademiya's secret collaboration with the Fatui and were locked up by the other four Sages until the conspiracy collapsed. Tighnari calls him "Uncle Baladhuri", which shows a personal bond. Baladhuri has a wife and a young daughter and is devoted to them. He teaches students such as Khaldun and, in Where Flowers Awaken (6.6), stays with them through the city's crisis.
### LARGE +
In A Divergence Leading to Oneself (6.6), Naphis introduces the six sitting Sages: Baladhuri and herself as the old guard, and four who took office after Azar's clique fell, among them Alkindi of Spantamad, Firnas of Kshahrewar and Shakir of Rtawahist. Baladhuri and Tighnari greet each other warmly there. In the As All Falls to Emptiness story, Baladhuri helps during the crisis with his historical knowledge.
### ULTRA +
Iskandar is a historical Vahumana Sage, counted among the Akademiya's greatest. Jazari quotes him in Vimana Agama: Jazari's Chapter when he looks at an ancient ruin golem and invokes thousands of years of Khaenri'ah history. Nothing else is known of his life.

## members
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
Known Vahumana scholars include the following.
- Baladhuri: the Sage.
- Khaldun: a researcher and Baladhuri's student.
- Yarshater: a Herbad who helped translate the Scroll of Streaming Song.
- Manetho: a professor named in a Sumeru quest.
- Nefer: assigned here as a student (see history), now listed as a former member.
- Vikram: mentioned by NPCs as one of the candidates for Grand Conservator.
### LARGE +
Other named members are Ebert, a researcher who reviewed and rated the films in a book about Fontaine's cinema, and Siraj, a scholar with a thesis on a unified "collective consciousness" who was jailed under Azar's regime for activities the Akademiya deemed illegal. Raed was a Vahumana researcher who led an expedition into the Great Red Sand and was secretly selling relics on the black market.
### ULTRA +
Sachin, a brilliant student of the past, appears in A Parade of Providence, a limited-time event (3.6). He enrolled at nine and graduated as valedictorian. He later went to the desert to research its people's suffering, found no answer in Vahumana knowledge, history, anthropology or his meetings with the Order of Skeptics, and ended his life there. His legacy funds the Interdarshan Championship and the Diadem of Knowledge.

## methods
### STANDARD
Vahumana scholars study history and society. They mount expeditions to ruins and bring artefacts back for study. They also translate old documents, often with the Haravatat, who handle language and runes. Cooperation has its edges: in the Scroll of Streaming Song, a Haravatat Herbad (Tafazzoli) added footnotes correcting the Vahumana translators, and the Vahumana Herbad Yarshater replied in the margin that he had never been told. The wiki infers these remarks are annotations, since the House of Daena lets borrowers annotate books.
### LARGE +
Applied work reaches the city. Furnishing descriptions say that Vahumana students favour the topic of time-sensitive commodities, and their advice has helped fruit and vegetable sellers at the Grand Bazaar waste less and save Mora. They also design products with Amurta scholars to address food preservation and overfishing.
### ULTRA +
In their own writing, Vahumana scholars use the archaic name "Dahri" for Khaenri'ah, as seen in the quest Afratu's Dilemma.

## history
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
Sitt places the roots of Vahumana in the reaction to King Deshret's remaining theories, and it later grew into the basis for the social sciences. Nothing more precise about its founding is stated.
### LARGE +
Under Grand Sage Azar, desertfolk accepted into the Akademiya were assigned to Vahumana automatically and did not get the entrance exam that other students took. Nefer's first character story shows a scholar telling her this, with Professor Cyrus objecting and being warned that Azar's circle would make trouble for him. After Azar was exiled, the Darshan, which had been declining, became more popular, as Nefer says in a voice-over.
### ULTRA +
In the Akademiya event A Parade of Providence (3.6, a limited-time event), a Vahumana Flag blueprint was among the rewards.

## relations
### STANDARD
- **Haravatat**: the nearest neighbour in translation work. They collaborate and also criticise each other's methods.
- **Amurta**: Naphis and Baladhuri were allies against the Akademiya's secret plans, and the two Darshans co-design food-preservation products.
- **Sumeru Akademiya**: Vahumana is one of its six schools, and its Sage sits among the Sages.

## entry: Baladhuri
@keys: Baladhuri, Sage Baladhuri, Vahumana Sage
@version: 6.6
### STANDARD
Baladhuri is the Sage of Vahumana, a kind man who opposed the Akademiya's deal with the Fatui and was imprisoned for it. He is a devoted husband and father with a prized beard, and Tighnari calls him Uncle. He is a teacher first, and stays with his students through the 6.6 crisis.

## entry: Scroll of Streaming Song
@keys: Scroll of Streaming Song
### STANDARD
A three-volume book collection in the House of Daena. Vahumana researchers recovered torn pages from desert ruins and translated them. The Haravatat Herbad Tafazzoli and the Vahumana Herbad Yarshater trade footnotes about mistranslations and about what each knew while they worked together. It is a small example of scholarly rivalry between the two Darshans.

## entry: Nefer's assignment to Vahumana
@keys: Nefer
@keys2: Vahumana, Akademiya, Darshan
### STANDARD
As a student from the desert, Nefer was assigned to Vahumana without sitting the entrance exam, a rule that held during Azar's time. Cyrus objected on principle that anyone admitted had a right to take the test. She says in a voice-over that Vahumana grew popular again after Azar's exile. Nefer is listed as a former member.
