## summary
### LITE
The Daydream Club is a small group of inventors and researchers from Fontaine, centred on the town of Petrichor. Its members are united by their 'daydreams', ambitious ideas that other people call unrealistic. Known members include Garcia (Numbering Machine), Xavier (Film Kamera), Felix Yogue (a perpetual-energy machine), the brothers Mecantre and Babisse (a giant cannon), Grispan (phlogiston and thermal hypnosis) and Este. Their 'President' is a cat, Lady Grobel.
### STANDARD +
The club is informal and good-humoured: a group that values curiosity and, by the account of a recording in Daydreams Beyond Space and Time, began with the idea of providing research for free. Felix Yogue says the name was chosen on purpose: since outsiders call the members' dreams unrealistic, they decided to call the club 'daydream'.

## structure
### STANDARD
There is no formal hierarchy in the sources. The club's 'President', or 'Director', is Lady Grobel, a cat whom Grispan treats as his leader and whose meows he translates for others. Members each pursue a separate project and trade ideas and equipment; Mecantre plans to borrow Garcia's Numbering Machine to calculate a bridge, for instance. The club has a base in Petrichor stocked with unusual equipment, including divesuits that Grispan bought to study underwater hypnosis.
### LARGE +
Some members travel widely while others stay in Petrichor. Xavier went to Inazuma, Garcia and his assistant Lutong to Liyue and Sumeru, and Grispan to Natlan; Grispan's letters to Petrichor show they keep in touch by post. Wiki infoboxes call Mecantre 'Chief Designer' and Babisse 'Chief of Construction' of the Teyvat Grand Cannon, titles for that one project.

## members
### STANDARD
- **Garcia**: builds the Numbering Machine, a calculating device. Absent-minded and fond of formal language, he travels with his assistant Lutong.
- **Xavier**: inventor of the Film Kamera and a filmmaker. He helped maintain the Mikage Furnace in Inazuma and later founded Musketeer Pictures in Fontaine.
- **Felix Yogue**: wants to build an energy source that runs forever using only mechanical parts. He appears in the Evermotion Mechanical Painting event (limited).
- **Mecantre and Babisse**: brothers designing a super-sized cannon meant to carry people up to touch the stars.
- **Grispan**: researches phlogiston and 'thermal hypnosis'.
- **Este**: a Petrichor resident, a descendant of the Remurian golem Este, who takes up Grispan's hypnosis notes.
- **Lady Grobel**: the cat President.
### LARGE +
People close to the club but not members include Lutong (Garcia's assistant), Kaia (who accompanies Garcia), and Grundeland, a self-styled 'Good Merchant' who tried to get hold of the club's research for profit.

## goals
### STANDARD
Members chase ideas for their own sake. According to a recording heard in Daydreams Beyond Space and Time, the club's original purpose is to provide research for free so that it belongs to everyone, and Garcia insists its research will not be used to make Mora. Mecantre explains why daydreaming matters: it is the reef on which a researcher's torrent of ideas breaks into inspiration, and without it research becomes a stagnant pool.

## methods
### STANDARD
Members work by imaginative leaps and practical engineering: calculating machines, cameras, a cannon, phlogiston-powered hypnosis. When Grundeland pressured them, Mecantre and Babisse planted phonographs around Petrichor to record what he said.
### LARGE +
Grispan's thermal hypnosis is a steam-heated box big enough to seat a person with only the head outside, meant to make hypnosis work faster and better. The Traveler points out that it would knock the person out, and Grispan answers that they define 'knocking out' differently. Garcia's method for the Numbering Machine is to turn cooking into a formula, which he tested on Radish Veggie Soup in Garcia's Paean (2.7).

## history
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
**Garcia's Paean (2.7).** At Wangshu Inn in Liyue, Garcia and Lutong ask the Traveler to taste-test soup made from a formula, and say they are heading for Sumeru to improve the Numbering Machine.

**Evermotion Mechanical Painting (2.8).** In this event (12 to 22 August 2022), Felix Yogue introduced the club to the Traveler in Mondstadt and invited them to visit Petrichor.
### LARGE +
**Petrichor mass dream (4.6).** In Canticles of Harmony, the Prelude 'Petrichorror Dream', Petrichor's residents, including Mecantre, Babisse and Grundeland, were caught up in the Symphony of Remuria under Phobos. They spoke as Remurian scientists and praised the God King. Este, descended from a Remurian, was unaffected.

**Daydreams Beyond Space and Time (4.6).** Garcia, with Kaia and Lutong, hurries home to Petrichor out of fear for his friends and finds them dazed and Grundeland remorseful. The Traveler explains Remuria and Phobos, and the group dives to the ruins using the club's divesuits. Mecantre and Babisse find the statues whose 'shadows' they were in the dream: a researcher of high-altitude aqueducts and one of aquabuses. They conclude the statues lacked the ability to daydream. Grundeland, in tears, finds the statue of the director of the Remurian Academy of Sciences. Back in Petrichor, Mecantre and Babisse vow to carry the statues' ideas forward as their own work, and Lutong plans to hand Grundeland to Officer Menthe.
### ULTRA +
**Natlan (5.0).** Far from Petrichor, Grispan and Grobel are at the Stadium of the Sacred Flame, Chuwen Fair, where Grispan gathers phlogiston from Candlecap Mushrooms and Embercore Flowers; Saurians make him wary of fieldwork. He has lost contact with Petrichor. If the Traveler has finished Daydreams Beyond Space and Time, they can tell him what happened, and he regrets missing a mass hypnosis of that scale and the data it would have yielded. The quest is a repeatable Daily Commission.

**Xavier's later years.** Xavier first appears in Tatara Tales (2.0) in Inazuma. In Roses and Muskets (4.3) he returned to Fontaine for the Fontinalia Film Festival, and after winning the Furina Award he founded Musketeer Pictures. In Sunny Summer Fontinalia he entered a second film with Columbina. These are limited-time events.

## relations
### STANDARD
The club is on good terms with Petrichor's people. When Garcia fears for his friends, Lutong suggests trusting Mr. Bossuet and hoping the Fontaine Research Institute will see them through; Officer Menthe is the authority who is to receive Grundeland. Grundeland is an antagonist who turns penitent. Outside Fontaine, members have been helped by the Traveler and Paimon in Inazuma, Liyue and Natlan.
### LARGE +
Mecantre and Babisse argue for giving Grundeland a chance to reform, while Garcia stays wary.

## reputation
### STANDARD
Felix Yogue admits outsiders call the members' plans unrealistic, and the members embrace the label. Lutong calls them brilliant people. The Traveler is warmly welcomed by them.

## secrets
### ULTRA
Nothing hidden is established about the club. The Petrichor mass dream is the one notable hidden matter: the Traveler is the one who explains Remuria and Phobos to the members. The sources read do not say whether the club knew of any link to Remuria beforehand.

## status
### LITE
Active as of 7.1 though scattered. The club's Petrichor members survived the mass dream and returned to their projects, and Grispan remains in Natlan.
### STANDARD +
The Teyvat Grand Cannon, the Numbering Machine and thermal hypnosis are described as ongoing, not finished. No source after 5.0 adds a new club appearance.

## entry: Garcia

@version: 2.7
### STANDARD
Garcia is a quest NPC from Fontaine who is building the Numbering Machine, a device for calculation. He is formal, distracted and fond of long phrases, and he is short of Mora. In Garcia's Paean (2.7) he and Lutong cooked Radish Veggie Soup at Wangshu Inn to find a recipe formula and left for Sumeru's Akademiya. In Daydreams Beyond Space and Time (4.6) he rushes home to Petrichor with Kaia and Lutong, fears his friends were exploited and leads the dive to Remuria with the Traveler. He asks the Traveler whether the machine possesses 'thought'; the question is left open.

## entry: Grispan and Grobel
@keys: Grispan, Grobel, Lady Grobel, thermal hypnosis
@keys2: Daydream Club, Natlan, phlogiston
@version: 5.0
### STANDARD
Grispan is a florid, anxious Fontainian researcher who is working on thermal hypnosis in Natlan. Lady Grobel is the club's cat President and speaks only in meows, which he translates; she dislikes unexpected trouble. If the Traveler has finished Daydreams Beyond Space and Time, they can fill him in on events in Petrichor. He is uncomfortable around Natlan's exuberant residents and wants to return home once his research is done.

## entry: Mecantre and Babisse
@keys: Mecantre, Babisse, Teyvat Grand Cannon
@keys2: Daydream Club, Petrichor, cannon
@version: 4.6
### STANDARD
Brothers and club members in Petrichor. Felix Yogue says they are building a cannon big enough to take people to the stars and are still looking for the right fuel. During the mass dream they were under the Remurian Symphony and spoke of an aqueduct and a fast aquabus. Afterwards they use the dreaming researchers' ideas as seeds for a large bridge and a speedy boat. They hold that research needs a 'daydream' as its spark, and after the quest each says the cannonball must be 'super-sized'.

## entry: Daydreams Beyond Space and Time
@keys: Daydreams Beyond Space and Time, Petrichorror Dream
@keys2: Daydream Club, Petrichor, Remuria
@version: 4.6
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
A Fontaine World Quest that follows the Petrichor mass dream. Garcia and company return to Petrichor, learn of Phobos and Remuria from the Traveler, and dive to the ruins. Mecantre and Babisse find the statues of two researchers they dreamed as, and Grundeland finds the director of the Remurian Academy of Sciences. The club members conclude those researchers lacked the ability to daydream. The achievement 'In Those Ruins of Dreams...' is granted at the end.
