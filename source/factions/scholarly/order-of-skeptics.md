## summary
### LITE
The Order of Skeptics, which the Akademiya calls the Nagarjunites, is a small group of scholars living in the Girdle of the Sands in southern Sumeru. They descend from an Akademiya Darshan that came to the desert 500 years ago, during the cataclysm, and helped the Pari purify the Harvisptokhm. They have long since cut ties with the Akademiya and are rarely acknowledged. Their creed is the "transmission of knowledge at all costs" and that "conflict is the natural order of Teyvat". In 3.6 their leader Nasejuna turned on the Pari and tried to take the Great Songs of Khvarena for the Abyss Order.
### STANDARD +
Outsiders also know them by the name Shamaniyya. Their leaders hold the title Nasuraia, also read as Vijnanapati, which the game glosses as "one who has epiphanies". Many people in the rainforest treat them as a myth.

## structure
### STANDARD
The order is led by a single Vijnanapati, the equivalent of a sage. Ranks below that are not described, and canon names only a handful of members. The order keeps a camp near the Temir Mountains in the desert. Its old archives burned, so much of its structure and ritual knowledge has been lost.
### LARGE +
Five ritual Kory Drums were made to mark the bond between the Skeptics and the Pari, and loading-screen text says they were meant to symbolise that union. The order has since lost the tradition. The Skeptics also used to perform the Rite of Chinvat at the Harvisptokhm, but they neglected the duty after their records were destroyed.

## leadership
### LITE
Leaders are called Nasuraia or Vijnanapati. Nagarjuna, a one-armed sage, is counted as the first. The last, Nasejuna, fled in 3.6, and the order is currently leaderless.
### STANDARD +
Before his betrayal Nasejuna was stationed in the Girdle of the Sands to study the Abyssal phenomenon there. Pyrrho, who calls himself one of the order's oldest qualified members and a desert native, praised him as more knowledgeable than anyone he had met and said he was helping the order recover its traditions.
### LARGE +
Nasejuna told the Traveler that the order's old traditions had been lost with the Kory Drums, and that he depended on Aryadeva, an elder who had gone to Sumeru City. When the Traveler told Pyrrho and Sosi of his betrayal, both were shocked. Afterwards Sosi calls him by his name alone, "Nasejuna", rather than by his title.

## members
### STANDARD
- **Nagarjuna**: the first Nasuraia, a one-armed sage who led the founding scholars.
- **Nasejuna**: the last known Vijnanapati, now a defector.
- **Pyrrho**: a desert-born member who is suspicious of outsiders.
- **Sosi**: a member who studies the order's documents and keeps looking for the truth despite finding discrepancies between records and reality.
- **Aryadeva**: a former elder who left to write light novels in Sumeru City but is still best versed in the order's traditions.
- **Nagahvaya**: a Herbad who calls himself a Skeptic and a follower of Nagarjuna; he is in the Desert Pavilion.
- **Klingsor**: a former ranger who became an Abyss Herald.
### LARGE +
Klingsor is described as a descendant of Hadura, a Khaenri'ahn of the Schwanenritter. Hundreds of years ago the then-Vijnanapati sent him to accompany an Akademiya investigation team into Khaenri'ahn ruins, and he came back as an Abyss Herald.

## goals
### STANDARD
Under its founders the order's duty was to keep the Harvisptokhm purified and remember the heroes who fought the Abyss. Its stated ideals are the transmission of knowledge at all costs and the belief that conflict is the natural order of Teyvat. Pyrrho says the surviving members want to recover the order's lost traditions and glory. Nasejuna's real aim was different: to use the Great Songs of Khvarena to destroy the Harvisptokhm so that the Abyss would flood Teyvat, which he called bringing truth without mercy to an unjust world.

## methods
### LARGE
The order works through the study of old documents and ruins and its bond with the Pari. It takes no part in Akademiya politics. In the 3.6 events Nasejuna used deception, sending the Pari Sorush to collect the Great Songs under the belief that she must sacrifice herself, and he misled the Traveler until the last song was found.

## history
### LARGE
**Founding.** About 500 years ago, as the cataclysm struck, scholars from an Akademiya Darshan led by the one-armed sage Nagarjuna met the Schwanenritter fighting the Abyss in the Dharma Forest. They took the knights for the "heroes of Dahri" and followed their trail to the Girdle of the Sands, where the Pari Zurvan and Dainsleif were defending the Vourukasha Oasis. With documents and machines left by Khaenri'ah's survivors, Nagarjuna found the way into the Harvisptokhm. Together they performed the first Rite of Ab-Zohr, which purified the sky and the land and gave rise to more Pari. Dainsleif left with a golden-haired traveller, Nagarjuna disappeared into the ruins, and the other scholars stayed, bonded with the Pari and became the Order of Skeptics.

**The fall of the archives.** Centuries ago an Akademiya investigation team came to study Khaenri'ahn ruins. The Skeptics warned against it and then sent Klingsor along. He returned as an Abyss Herald, and the team was never found. Abyssal monsters came to the Vourukasha Oasis hunting for the Great Songs, and Zurvan sealed the Oasis and many ruins. The Skeptics lost their archives to fire in the conflict, and over time they neglected their duty to maintain the seal.
### ULTRA +
**Nasejuna's plot (3.6).** Klingsor had shown Nasejuna documents that convinced him the order's origins were myth and the Khaenri'ahn account more factual. In Khvarena of Good and Evil, a Sign of Apaosha appeared above the Harvisptokhm. Nasejuna sent the Pari Sorush, who had made the Traveler her Yasnapati, to gather the five Great Songs. When the last was found in a Khaenri'ahn factory, Nasejuna demanded to examine them, snapped at Sorush and revealed he had only pretended to revere her. He and a summoned Abyss Herald tried to take the songs by force. The Traveler beat them and Nasejuna fled. The Traveler, Paimon and Sorush went on to finish the Rite of Ab-Zohr at the Harvisptokhm.

**Aftermath.** Sosi says the Fatui officer Markozov has withdrawn his troops. Aryadeva says he hid the fact that he held a Kory Drum, entrusting it to an acquaintance in the Akademiya, because he knew Nasejuna was up to no good.

## relations
### STANDARD
- **Pari**: the order's oldest bond, symbolised by the Kory Drums. Zurvan sealed the Oasis after Klingsor's attack, and the Pari still guard the Harvisptokhm.
- **Sumeru Akademiya**: the order was once one of its Darshans, but the Akademiya calls it the Nagarjunites and the two have long been separate. The Akademiya's investigation team led to Klingsor's fall.
- **Abyss Order**: Klingsor, a former member, is now one of its Heralds, and he and Nasejuna worked together.
### LARGE +
- **The Traveler**: Pyrrho and Sosi were wary of outsiders until Sorush spoke for the Traveler. After the betrayal the Traveler told them the truth about Nasejuna.
- **Nagahvaya**: he apologises for the modern Skeptics' deeds, saying his Darshan's methods have become their purpose and its philosophy has been simplified and distorted over time.

## reputation
### ULTRA
In the rainforest the order is nearly a myth. Aryadeva says its glory days are bygone legends and prefers to see its desires turned into light novels. Pyrrho speaks of reclaiming its lost glory. After 3.6 its name is also tied to Nasejuna's betrayal.

## secrets
### ULTRA
The order's founding story was largely lost with its archives. Zurvan holds the account, and the Traveler saw part of it through Sorush's vision at the Harvisptokhm. Whether Nagarjuna hid something, as Klingsor suspected, is not answered.

## status

## entry: Nasejuna
@keys: Nasejuna
@version: 3.6
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Nasejuna was the last Vijnanapati of the Order of Skeptics, stationed in the Girdle of the Sands to study the Abyssal phenomenon there. He learned that his ancestors, who resisted the land's impurities, were heroes in Khaenri'ah's eyes but were smeared and forgotten by the Akademiya, and he plotted with the Abyss Order. In Khvarena of Good and Evil he guided Sorush and the Traveler to the Great Songs of Khvarena while secretly planning to use them to destroy the Harvisptokhm. When the plot was revealed he showed contempt for the Pari, attacked and fled. He is currently at large.

## entry: Klingsor
@keys: Klingsor
@version: 3.6
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Klingsor was once a ranger of the Order of Skeptics and a descendant of Hadura of the Schwanenritter. Resentful of how Khaenri'ahns were remembered, he doubted the order's origin story. Hundreds of years ago he was sent to accompany an Akademiya investigation team into Khaenri'ahn ruins. He found material that confirmed his doubts, heard a "gospel" and became an Abyss Herald, and later led an attack on the Vourukasha Oasis. In 3.6 he gave Nasejuna the documents that won him over and fought beside him. His fate after that battle is unknown.

## entry: Kory Drums
@keys: Kory Drum, Kory Drums, Barsom
@keys2: Skeptics, Pari, Sorush
@version: 3.6
### LARGE
The Kory Drums are five ritual drums made in honour of Barsom's familiar, which liked music. According to Nasejuna, on the day the Skeptics and Pari made their bond formal, the drums were played together to a score. With the drums lost, the Skeptics lost the ceremony, and Barsom's familiar stopped answering them. In the 3.6 quest chain a map in a cave shows the drums' locations. Loading-screen text says the drums were meant to symbolise the union, and also hints that the order "might have gotten something wrong".

## entry: Aryadeva
@keys: Aryadeva
@keys2: Skeptics, Light Novel Society, Kory Drum
### STANDARD
Aryadeva is a former elder of the Order of Skeptics living in Sumeru City near the Kalimi Exchange. He left the desert to write light novels and is planning a Light Novel Society, though Yae Publishing House keeps rejecting his manuscripts. Nasejuna calls him a ridiculous old geezer but relies on him, since Aryadeva is the best versed in the order's traditions. After the plot failed, Aryadeva said he had kept one Kory Drum away from Nasejuna on purpose.

## entry: Rite of Ab-Zohr and the Sign of Apaosha
@keys: Rite of Ab-Zohr, Rite of Chinvat, Sign of Apaosha
@version: 3.6
### LARGE
The Rite of Ab-Zohr is the ceremony that purifies the Harvisptokhm of Abyssal defilement. The Order of Skeptics used to perform the Rite of Chinvat for the same purpose, and the first Ab-Zohr was performed 500 years ago by Zurvan, Dainsleif and Nagarjuna. The Sign of Apaosha, a rift-like mark in the sky above the Harvisptokhm, appears as defilement builds up. Zurvan says that if defilement erodes the tree, the Tunigi Hollow's rift might reopen. In 3.6 the Traveler, Paimon and Sorush performed the rite and the sign disappeared.
