## summary
### LITE
Kshahrewar is the Sumeru Akademiya's Darshan of technology. It covers mechanisms, architecture and puzzle-solving, and its colour is white with a lion on its crest. Its sage is Firnas. It built much of Sumeru City, but it has been underfunded since the loss of its greatest scholar, Pir Kavikavus. Its best-known graduate is the architect Kaveh, the Light of Kshahrewar.
### STANDARD +
Kshahrewar's designs shape ordinary life: a cheap single-storey house plan that serves as a template across the city, and loom designs behind Sumeru's textile industry. Most people never notice the theory behind these. The Darshan also studies King Deshret's ancient technology, which it has not yet explained.

## structure
### STANDARD
Kshahrewar is one of the Six Darshans, with one Great Sage. According to the receptionist Sitt, it began as a school for studying and building propulsion mechanisms, and its scope grew because making machines draws on many kinds of knowledge. Its disciplines now include architecture, mechanical skills and puzzle-solving. Members are students, researchers and teachers. Many scholars are serious about sculpting as a hobby, Firnas says.
### LARGE +
Funding comes from the Akademiya's allocation and from private contracts. Dori, the merchant known as Lord Sangemah Bay, supplies Mora in return for the Darshan's technology and craftsmanship. A Kshahrewar researcher helped Aaru Village build the White Lift into its stepwell. The name "Kshahrewar Lift" was proposed, but because relations between the desert peoples and the Akademiya were prickly, the villagers settled on "White Lift", after the Darshan's colour.

## leadership
### LITE
Firnas is the current Kshahrewar Sage. He reasons like an engineer, comparing crises to faulty machines. His predecessor was removed after the Akademiya-Fatui plot.
### STANDARD +
Hosseini, in the 2.7 event A Muddy Bizarre Adventure, mentions "our great tutor Firnas". In 6.6 Firnas is one of the six sages gathered at Caravan Ribat. He judged that the Irminsul anomaly resembled a malfunctioning machine and spoke of stress fractures. At Aaru Village he reported on the Jinn breaching the defences and helped move the villagers north. He later judged the Temple of Silence plan sound with manageable risk.
### ULTRA +
Firnas said that even with Kaveh, Faruzan and every other mechanics scholar working full time, the proposed modifications for the Akasha Terminal plan would take time. Before him, Kshahrewar's seat belonged to a sage who backed Azar and was removed.

## members
### STANDARD
Kaveh is the Darshan's most celebrated alumnus: a Kshahrewar graduate with honours, the architect of the Palace of Alcazarzaray, and known as the Light of Kshahrewar. His mother Faranak graduated from Kshahrewar and was a famous architect too. Faruzan is a Haravatat professor, but her theories underpin the Darshan's mechanics. She also studied there for a time. Alhaitham's grandmother was a Kshahrewar scholar who raised him. Jazari is a researcher who wants to follow Kavikavus and win the Pir Kavikavus Prize.
### LARGE +
Alkami, a Kshahrewar senior of Kaveh's, runs a training centre. Zaha Hadi is a veteran teacher of many students. Others the wiki lists include Anis, Ashvapati, Atashye, Daneshvar, Daya, Dzenana, Fawara, Goli, Judar, Kashif, Marwa, Moseis, Narendra, Naysaburi, Pharez, Piyush, Sasani and Vinayak, but little is known about most of them.

## goals
### STANDARD
Kshahrewar wants to design and improve working things: buildings, looms, machinery and ancient mechanisms. Its scholars also want to revive their school's standing. Jazari hopes the rediscovery of Kavikavus' work will do that. The wiki says that, likely because of the accident, the Darshan came to advocate strict control over machines and technology, and a forum poster says those above wanted a stable, peaceful society.

## methods
### LARGE
Kshahrewar proceeds by building and testing. Its people design from low-cost, adaptable templates and tune looms to make cloth faster while preserving its beauty. Over the last century they have used Faruzan's manuscripts to establish the fundamentals of mechanics and have more recently developed their own mechanism theories. Cyno's profile jokes that the General Mahamatra works harder than the "Great Vayuvyastra", a machine made by Kshahrewar. Jazari says his school is concerned only with genuine techniques.

## history
### STANDARD
**Pir Kavikavus.** A prodigy who joined some time after the cataclysm 500 years ago, Kavikavus became a Herbad before thirty. He was friends with the Aranara Aralohita. He was the last person recorded to enter the Ruin Golem on Devantaka Mountain for systematic research. In an accident his research was lost and he likely died. Since then Kshahrewar has been regarded as in decline, with no raise in funding and no major breakthroughs. The Akademiya's prize for outstanding young scholars bears his name.
### LARGE +
**The 3.0 revival.** In Vimana Agama and Until Vana is Healed, the Traveler and Paimon helped Jazari recover one of Kavikavus' papers, hidden behind Aralohita's puzzles. Jazari used it to seal the Golem's Chaos Core, which weakened the Abyss Mages and reduced the Abyss Order's threat to the forest. He called it the first breakthrough of its kind in many years.
**The sages' plot (3.0-3.2).** Kshahrewar's sage backed Azar and Dottore and was removed. After Scaramouche fell, Alhaitham told Kaveh and the Traveler that the Kshahrewar and Haravatat seats needed new sages, and said the first thing he would do as Acting Grand Sage was reject Kshahrewar's funding application. Firnas later took the seat.
### ULTRA +
**6.6.** Firnas, Kaveh, Faruzan and Kshahrewar's mechanics scholars worked on the plan to restart the Akasha Terminal at the Temple of Silence. Kaveh and Alhaitham then worked with the Aaru mainframe, as described in Truth Amongst the Pages of Purana.

## relations
### STANDARD
- **Haravatat**: Kshahrewar built its mechanics on Haravatat's old work, notably Faruzan's. Faruzan says Kshahrewar tried to recruit her, with more students and a bigger budget.
- **Dori**: the merchant lends Mora to Kshahrewar and to Kaveh. Dori says they signed a private contract: she has the Mora, they have the technology.
- **Tighnari's view**: he called Kshahrewar and Spantamad researchers quite prejudiced, so he would not trust them with a delicate matter.
### LARGE +
- **The Akademiya**: it never raised Kshahrewar's funding after Kavikavus, and it favours strict control over machines. Alhaitham jokes that he would deny its funding request.
- **Alhaitham and Kaveh**: Kaveh is the Darshan's representative in Alhaitham's eyes, and the two former students have a complicated relationship.
- **Aaru Village**: a former Kshahrewar researcher helped build the White Lift.

## reputation
### LARGE
Kshahrewar is respected for its builders but short of money. Students treat Kaveh as a role model and speculate about him at the Palace of Alcazarzaray. Others note that few genius architects of Kshahrewar find happiness, as Zaha Hadi says. Faruzan calls the school's scholars "freeloaders" for using her manuscripts. Alkami's training centre used Kaveh's fame as the Light of Kshahrewar to attract students, a scheme that turned out to be a ruse.

## entry: Firnas
@keys: Firnas, Sage Firnas
@keys2: Kshahrewar, Akademiya, sage
@version: 6.6
### STANDARD
Firnas is the Kshahrewar Sage, appointed after the previous sage fell with Azar. Earlier, Hosseini called him a "great tutor" in an event. In 6.6, at Caravan Ribat and the Temple of Silence, he approached the crisis like an engineer: the anomaly looked like a malfunctioning machine, not an outside attack, and he warned of supply problems on a move to the desert. He also remarked on sculpting among Kshahrewar scholars and asked Faruzan whether she had once studied in his Darshan.

## entry: Pir Kavikavus
@keys: Pir Kavikavus, Kavikavus, Pir Kavikavus Prize
@keys2: Kshahrewar, Jazari, Akademiya
@version: 3.0
### STANDARD
Pir Kavikavus was a Kshahrewar prodigy who joined some time after the cataclysm, reached the rank of Herbad before thirty, and was friends with the Aranara Aralohita. While investigating the Ruin Golem on Devantaka Mountain, an accident destroyed his research and he likely died. Aralohita saved some of his work behind puzzles. In 3.0, Jazari used a recovered paper to seal the Golem's Chaos Core. The prize that honours him goes to a scholar under forty with groundbreaking research.

## entry: Jazari
@keys: Jazari
@keys2: Kshahrewar, Kavikavus, Akademiya
@version: 3.0
### STANDARD
Jazari is a Kshahrewar researcher who worries about money and hopes to follow Kavikavus in studying the Ruin Golem. In Vimana Agama he tells the Traveler about Kavikavus and says Kshahrewar is concerned only with genuine techniques. In Until Vana is Healed he uses Kavikavus' rediscovered paper to seal the Chaos Core and hopes this revives Kshahrewar.

## entry: Alkami's training centre
@keys: Alkami, training center
@keys2: Kaveh, Kshahrewar, architect
@version: 3.7
### LARGE
Alkami, a Kshahrewar senior, runs a training centre for those who could not enrol in the Akademiya. In Kaveh's Hangout Event, he offers Kaveh a generous per-session fee to teach there. It turns out that he wants Kaveh's fame as the Light of Kshahrewar to boost enrolment, since he is not a talented architect himself. Kaveh suspects that the Akademiya does not know of such centres, and that, with all the necessary permits, they would not act even if it did.

## entry: Competition on the Sands

### LARGE
During the limited-time event A Parade of Providence (3.6), Kaveh represented Kshahrewar in the Akademiya Extravaganza's Interdarshan Championship. The wiki adds that Kaveh bought a machine core excavated from King Deshret's ruins and used it, modified, in an automated case, Mehrak.
