## summary
### LITE
The Saurian Relics Association is a Natlan organisation that gathers, appraises and studies relics left by the ancient saurians. It was founded by Copan, a scholar of the Collective of Plenty. It is poorly funded and of uneven reputation. Its branch at the Chuwen Fair in the Stadium of the Sacred Flame has become a souvenir counter, a notice board and, in the manager's words, a giant flea market.
### STANDARD +
Its name promises dragon scholarship, but the Chuwen Fair counter's own map description notes that its interests also include many topics unrelated to saurians.

## structure
### LITE
Researchers, excavators, investigators and shop staff, with no named president today.
### STANDARD
The Association employs researchers and excavators, accepts donations from the public, and manages excavation sites across Natlan that visitors enter for a fee. At one long-running site, the guard Quipia charges 300 Mora to dig. The Chuwen Fair branch is managed by Siyah, a Child of Echoes. Relics are appraised at an unnamed "headquarters". No present leader is named.
### LARGE +
Known present-day staff are the researchers Akeor (also a part-time archaeologist), Chuno, Hoa (also a part-time courier), Manqu and Mtwa; the investigator Zompaynco; the member Peyotli; the guard Quipia; and the manager Siyah. Members come from several tribes: Chuno, Siyah and Manqu are Children of Echoes, and Zompaynco is from the Collective of Plenty.

## goals
### LITE
To collect and catalogue saurian-era relics and keep them as part of Natlan's heritage.
### STANDARD
The Association's stated aim is to study the treasures of the age of the ancient dragons and keep them in Natlan. Its notices say relics must not be sold to foreign merchants or research institutions. Surveying and analysis inside Natlan are allowed once the relevant access fees are paid.
### LARGE +
It also wants income and reputation. Its notice board advertises "unlimited potential" in place of a base salary, promises diggers fame in both adventuring and archaeology, and presents its finds as contributions to Natlan's technology.

## methods
### STANDARD
It appraises and exhibits finds, charges entry fees at dig sites and access fees to foreigners who wish to study relics, and posts "research bounties" with money rewards. Chuno, for example, took a bounty to decipher Iridescent Inscriptions to make up an overspent monthly budget. Its announcements give each find a use, such as a mount saddle or a back-muscle trainer, dated with instruments.
### LARGE +
Announcements and replies appear on the Chuwen Fair message board, which also serves for commissions. Appraisals are sometimes contested in public. When Hoa suggested her find might simply be a saddle lost by a recent Qucusaur Rider, the Association asked how badly the Rider would have had to fall to bury it that deep, and Hoa answered by asking about compensation for work injuries.

### ULTRA +
Example finds from the board: Hoa's Qucusaurus saddle, said to be tied to the Flower-Feather Clan's territory; a back-muscle training device from the Collective of Plenty's territory, found by Akeor and Tahmures, which the Association suggested could help improve that tribe's fitness equipment; and a find by Tizoc near the Masters of the Night-Wind's territory, linked to early Phlogiston Engraving.

## reputation
### STANDARD
Mixed. Siyah admits the collection has "essentially turned into a giant flea market". Researcher Hoa has doubted one of its conclusions. On the other hand, the Speaker's Chamber has publicly thanked the Association for its excavations and said an object related to early Phlogiston Engraving will be used for research at the Temple of Pax.
### LARGE +
The Association has also been dragged into local rumour. A board thread claimed that Citlali's divination tools had been dug up and that her curse might spread. The Association replied that the objects were just liquor bottles and that someone had been sent to return them. This is a joking exchange on a message board, not a verified event.

## history
### STANDARD
Copan is the earliest known figure. Artifact text describes a scholar of the Collective of Plenty who called himself President of the Association, carried woven scrolls and tinkered with mysterious mechanisms. When the Cataclysm destroyed his scrolls, he put his research aside and helped adapt machines of the secret source from ancient ruins to aid warriors. He later joined the Ochkanatlan Adventure Team and died on the way to the Tonatiuh. At some later point a group of Association members was commissioned to investigate Ochkanatlan and disappeared, so the Association has avoided anything to do with the place.
### LARGE +
In the present the Association is a modest outfit whose dig sites have been open for years, and Quipia says her site is the cheapest because it has been open the longest. In Version Luna I (6.0), Zompaynco appears in Nod-Krai's Nasha Town, supposedly there to look into saurian ruins but mostly warming himself by a furnace.

## relations
### STANDARD
It has close ties to the Collective of Plenty (its founder and an investigator) and the Children of Echoes (its shopkeeper and several researchers). The Speaker's Chamber has expressed appreciation for its work. It serves Natlan's public and visitors but refuses relic exports. During Nefer's Research Spillover quest, her private thought is a guess that Zompaynco's arrival might worry the Guild about rivals.
### LARGE +
Its notices attribute finds to the territories of the Flower-Feather Clan, the Collective of Plenty and the Masters of the Night-Wind.

## status
### LITE
Active as of 7.1 and still short of money.
### STANDARD
As of 7.1 the Association is an ordinary part of Natlan life. Its Chuwen Fair counter becomes available to the Traveler once Tona's Flame's Restitution reaches level 50, and it sells goods for Pyro Sigils. Before then Siyah explains that stock has arrived in a random state and is not yet sorted.

## entry: Siyah and the Chuwen Fair counter
@keys: Siyah
@version: 5.0
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
Siyah manages the Association's branch at the Chuwen Fair. He is a Child of Echoes, who he says are reputed to have a knack for business, which is why he was made shopkeeper, though he says the shop really needs a storage specialist. He says the Association's official purpose is to collect and sort relics, but in truth it has become a giant flea market. For Pyro Sigils the counter sells weapon ascension materials, Mora and furnishing blueprints. It sits beside the Association's message board.
### LARGE +
Siyah's idle line is a complaint that sorting is hard. When the Traveler jokes that their backpack is just as cluttered, he briefly believes them, then laughs and asks for organising tricks.

### STANDARD
Copan was the Association's founder and self-styled president, a scholar of the Collective of Plenty. He died in the Cataclysm era as a member of the Ochkanatlan Adventure Team. Liriwu refers to him as "Uncle Copan" in a note. Little more is stated.

### STANDARD
Members of the Association once took a commission to investigate Ochkanatlan, the ruined city north of the Flower-Feather Clan's territory, and vanished. Manqu, a Child of Echoes who also works with the Association, tells the Traveler that both his tribe and the Association forbid him from going there.

### STANDARD
Chuno, a researcher, takes Association research bounties to cover his bills. His current project is deciphering Iridescent Inscriptions, a task he says has never been completed. He is angry at a seller who once sold him fake "Remnants of the Secret Source" and wants only genuine fragments.

## entry: Zompaynco in Nod-Krai
@keys: Zompaynco
@keys2: Nasha Town, Nod-Krai, Saurian Relics Association
@version: 6.0
### STANDARD
Zompaynco, an investigator from the Collective of Plenty, came to Nasha Town because he heard Nod-Krai had ruins related to saurian relics. He hates the cold, warms himself by a furnace and is more taken with Nod-Krai's machines than with his research. He wants to meet the Frostmoon Scions, and Nefer's quest has him asking for an introduction through Lyylikki. He says the funds he requested are for research, and admits he cannot keep buying "funny little machines".
