## summary
### LITE
Amurta is the Sumeru Akademiya's school of biology, which also covers ecology, botany and medicine. It is recognised as the oldest and largest of the Six Darshans, and its colour is green. Its sage is Naphis, a famously short-tempered man of integrity who is now also the Akademiya's Grand Sage. Tighnari, the Forest Watcher, graduated from Amurta under Naphis.
### STANDARD +
The Darshan grew from the researchers who helped Greater Lord Rukkhadevata cultivate the rainforest, and its symbol carries a bird that resembles a Huma. Because the rainforest, its creatures and the public hospital all fall under its subject, Amurta is the Darshan most visibly tied to Sumeru's living land.

## structure
### STANDARD
Amurta is one of the Six Darshans, each led by a Great Sage who sits on the Akademiya's advisory councils. Its members run from students and Driyosh (travelling scholars) up through formal researchers. Two institutions belong to it in practice. The Bimarstan, Sumeru's free public hospital, is staffed by Amurta researchers and students. Pardis Dhyai, a botany research site in the Dharma Forest, is run by Amurta, and Tighnari keeps close ties with its researchers. Disciplines taught include biology, ecology, botany and medicine.
### LARGE +
Students may take elective courses from other Darshans, so Amurta scholars sometimes work alongside Spantamad or Kshahrewar people. Amurta researchers are also found abroad. Khedive, an ecologist of the House of Pursina, funds and directs a field-study team at the Chasm in Liyue.

## leadership
### LITE
Naphis is Amurta's Sage. Tighnari calls him a man of integrity who sticks to his beliefs. Researchers fear his legendary temper, and even the former Grand Sage Azar gave him leeway.
### STANDARD +
Naphis was Tighnari's master and academic adviser. He wanted Tighnari to join the Amurta faculty after graduation but did not push when Tighnari chose a freer life as a Forest Watcher. During the Akademiya-Fatui plot, Naphis and Baladhuri of Vahumana opposed the plan and were locked up by the other four sages. Someone else posed as Naphis during that period. The other sages used his name on letters to recruit Tighnari. After the plot fell apart he was freed, weakened but otherwise well. By 6.6 he had been appointed Grand Sage, as stated in the 6.6 Archon Quest, while remaining head of Amurta.
### ULTRA +
Naphis is also called the "old guard" with Baladhuri: when he introduces the six sages in 6.6, he says the other four took office after Azar's faction fell. A past Amurta sage, Siman Farrokhzad, appears only in a hospital record about Giyath Khayyam's retirement from Amurta.

## members
### STANDARD
Tighnari is Amurta's best-known alumnus: he was admitted early, studied botany under Naphis and graduated, then left the Akademiya's centre to serve in the Avidya Forest. Collei, his trainee ranger, is expected to enrol in the Akademiya once her studies finish. Kaveh once suggested Amurta for her, since it is Tighnari's Darshan. Khedive Pursina is an Amurta ecologist who studies the Chasm's changing life forms. Khayyam retired from Amurta because of a patient's condition, according to a Bimarstan record. Zakariya, a Bimarstan doctor, is probably Amurta, but this is not stated.
### LARGE +
Other named Amurta people on the wiki include Araby, Darwish, Farabi, Jami, Juzjani, Kautilya, Makhfi, Mansur, Rehan, Rudaba and Yasmin, though the sources checked for this entry give little more than the affiliation. Karkata is Tighnari's research assistant, and Tighnari told the Matra that anyone wanting to do anything to it must go through him.

## goals
### STANDARD
Amurta studies life: plants, animals, ecosystems, and the health of people. Khedive says Amurta takes a dim view of those who rely purely on theory. The Darshan was founded by people who helped cultivate the rainforest.

## methods
### LARGE
Amurta works through fieldwork and clinical practice. Tighnari's seminars at Pardis Dhyai are so popular that the Akademiya limited attendance to Amurta students and ranger personnel. The Bimarstan treats the public for free. Khedive hires miners and survey crews out of his own pocket and says he will share findings with the Liyue authorities, even though doing so bends Akademiya rules. Tighnari prefers a freer research environment than the Akademiya allows.

## history
### STANDARD
Amurta is described as the first Darshan, created by the researchers who helped the old Dendro Archon cultivate the rainforest. It has been the largest Darshan ever since. The Akademiya has changed around it, but Amurta's mission is still natural science and medicine.
### LARGE +
**The sages' plot (3.0-3.2).** When Dottore returned to Sumeru and the sages planned to make an artificial god, Naphis opposed the plan and was imprisoned. A stand-in took his seat. The conspirators sent Tighnari letters in Naphis' name, offering prestige. Tighnari noticed that the letters lacked the weather-dots Naphis normally used and that they did not sound angry enough to be his. He asked Cyno to look into it quietly. At the start of the Sumeru Archon Quest, Haravatat's sage Khajeh went to Gandharva Ville to invite Tighnari personally, hinting that his refusal had hurt Naphis' reputation. After the plot was exposed, Naphis was freed. Tighnari wrote to thank him, and Naphis replied that someone had already passed on his regards in person.
### ULTRA +
**After the plot.** Naphis helped investigate the missing Cyrus with Alhaitham and Kaveh in the sage's old-friend capacity, then scolded Cyrus and set him to work. Later Naphis was made Grand Sage. In 6.6 (Song of the Welkin Moon, Act IX), with Irminsul in disorder and Nahida in a coma, Naphis led the sages' discussions at Caravan Ribat and said he had only just been appointed. He described feeling responsible for the lives lost at Yasna Monument.

## relations
### STANDARD
- **Sumeru Akademiya**: Amurta is one of its Six Darshans. The Akademiya's other schools are Rtawahist, Spantamad, Haravatat, Vahumana and Kshahrewar.
- **Forest Rangers**: Tighnari, an Amurta graduate, serves as a Forest Watcher, which ties the Darshan's botanical work to the ranger corps.
- **Vahumana**: its sage Baladhuri was Naphis' fellow prisoner and remains a sage with him.
- **Matra**: they enforce Akademiya rules on researchers, including Amurta, as Tighnari's remark about Karkata shows.
### LARGE +
- **Spantamad**: Khedive says Amurta often has to work with Spantamad "blockheads" despite disliking pure theory.
- **Kshahrewar and Spantamad**: when Tighnari needed discretion in a delicate matter, he said he could not trust researchers from Kshahrewar or Spantamad, calling them often prejudiced.
- **Sages removed after the plot**: Azar of Rtawahist and the sages of Spantamad, Haravatat and Kshahrewar were removed. Naphis and Baladhuri were not.

## reputation
### LARGE
Amurta is the biggest Darshan, and its work reaches the public through medicine at the Bimarstan and botany seminars. A message-board poster in the Akademiya wishes they had joined Amurta so they could sightsee and write stories rather than sit in Haravatat. Inside the school, Naphis' temper is a source of fear: a researcher says they are terrified of him.

## secrets
### ULTRA
Established only: the sages' plot kept Naphis locked away while his name was used in letters, and Tighnari learned the full circumstances only after the plot was exposed. Whether Siman Farrokhzad worked with the Fatui is inferred by the wiki, not stated in canon.

## entry: Naphis and the sages' plot
@keys: Naphis, Sage Naphis, Amurta Sage
@version: 3.2
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Naphis, a short-tempered, principled scholar, is the Amurta Sage and Tighnari's former master. When Dottore and four sages plotted to build a new god out of Scaramouche, Naphis and Baladhuri refused to go along and were locked up. The conspirators kept up the pretence that Naphis was working on the project, writing to Tighnari in his name. Tighnari's suspicion about the missing details in those letters led him to ask Cyno to investigate. The Traveler learned the details when the plot was exposed. Naphis was freed and later became Grand Sage.

## entry: Bimarstan and Pardis Dhyai
@keys: Bimarstan, Pardis Dhyai
@keys2: Amurta, Akademiya, Sumeru
### STANDARD
Two Amurta institutions are worth naming. The Bimarstan is a hospital where Amurta researchers and students treat residents for free. Pardis Dhyai is a botanical research site in the Dharma Forest where Tighnari holds seminars. A Bimarstan record about the retired Amurta scholar Giyath Khayyam mentions the Amurta sage Siman Farrokhzad overseeing his treatment.

## entry: Khedive Pursina
@keys: Khedive, Khedive Pursina
@keys2: Amurta, Chasm, Akademiya
### LARGE
Khedive Pursina is an Amurta ecologist from the House of Pursina, based in Liyue's Chasm. Under an agreement with the Liyue Qixing, he pays the Chasm Exploration Team and studies how the area's life forms and geology are changing. He admits that sharing his results with Liyue's historical society breaks Akademiya rules in principle, but says the Liyue authorities were firm. He prefers to fund the digging rather than go down himself.
