## summary
### LITE
The Universitas Magistrorum was a Khaenri'ahn institute of learning whose members worked in astrology, alchemy and other arts. It was directly subordinate to the Vinster King Irmin and held real influence in the kingdom. Before the cataclysm it was led by President Hroptatyr, "The Wise", with Eftirvit, later called Pierro, as Vice-President. After Irmin became obsessed with the Abyss, it turned into a major centre of Abyssal research. What became of it after the kingdom fell 500 years ago is not stated.
### STANDARD +
The name is Latin, roughly "University of the Magisters". The institute is known only through memories, book text and story quests set in the last years of Khaenri'ah, never as a place the Traveler visits in the present day.

## structure
### STANDARD
At the top were a President, who was also the Chief Royal Mage with the royal title "The Wise", and a Vice-President. Beneath them worked professors and researchers, among them Professor Mimameith (the teacher of both leaders) and the Moon Marrow researcher Tholindis. The Universitas also held a collection of literary works, and in at least one legend it had a champion fighter. It answered to the king directly and had no other known superior.
### LARGE +
The only unit named is the Moon Marrow lab, which studied the Crimson Moon's remains as a possible energy source. President Hroptatyr's own field of research was the Crimson Moon. No source lists other departments, and the institute's size is not given.

## leadership
### STANDARD
Hroptatyr was the President, a rising prodigy who had studied under Mimameith and was favoured by the king. Eftirvit, his friend and rival, was passed over for the presidency and served as Vice-President. Both were counted among the most promising scholars of their generation. Rhinedottir remarked to Hroptatyr that the Vice-President did not hide his envy, and Hroptatyr answered that missing the presidency spared Eftirvit a great deal of stress.
### LARGE +
The two shared a respectful rivalry until their teacher fell ill. Hroptatyr's discovery about the Crimson Moon's authority over death, and his sudden refusal to use it to save Mimameith, left Eftirvit embittered. By the time the Traveler's sibling and Paimon each arrived in Khaenri'ah, the pair were at odds, and each tried to win the king's favour in his own way. See the entry on the two leaders below.

## members
### STANDARD
Named members are Hroptatyr, Eftirvit (Pierro), their teacher Professor Mimameith, and Tholindis, a young Moon Marrow researcher. Alf is described in the legend Perinheri as the greatest warrior in the Universitas Magistrorum.
### LARGE +
Tholindis was a distant descendant of the Crimson Moon Dynasty who hid her ancestry, and she was engaged to Rerir. Her story ends at the Universitas on the night of the raid (see below). Hroptatyr later became one of the Five Sinners of Khaenri'ah, and Eftirvit later became Pierro, the Director of the Fatui.

## goals
### STANDARD
The Universitas served the crown by forecasting from the stars, working with alchemy and seeking sources of power for the kingdom. Under Irmin's growing fixation, its purpose narrowed to understanding and using the Abyss. Individual researchers had their own aims, such as a cure for Mimameith or an alternative energy source to the Abyss.

## methods
### LARGE
The Universitas offered the king predictions drawn from the stars. In the text of the Orbits achievement namecard, it tells Irmin that two stars captured by the world's gravity will, after a long orbit, cross paths again. The namecard does not name the stars; the wiki connects it to the twin Travelers. Its alchemy worked on principles Jakob, as the wiki reports, thought neglected the foundations of the art. It also produced devices and spells, from a party trick (see Chaos Permeation below) to the instruments Tholindis used to try to hold open a gate through the Moon Marrow.
### ULTRA +
When Irmin turned to the Abyss, the Universitas moved most of its people from Moon Marrow and Crimson Moon projects to Abyssal Energy research. Tholindis, left alone in the lab, said she thought it unwise to rely on one poorly understood power source while ignoring other promising technology. Hroptatyr said he appeased the king because it served the Universitas' best interests.

## history
### STANDARD
**Before the cataclysm.** The institute was an established, esteemed body. In their youth Hroptatyr and Eftirvit studied under Professor Mimameith there and became its leading minds. Hroptatyr rose to the presidency.
### LARGE +
**The Abyss turn.** Irmin's research into the Abyss drew in Mimameith, whose health declined. Hroptatyr found a way to take the Authority of Death from the Crimson Moon, which could make a person immune from death, but he realised that this "blessing" was a curse of endless suffering and did not use it on his teacher. She died, and the rift with Eftirvit began. The Universitas was by then redirected to Abyssal research.

**The night of the raid.** Dainsleif gathered Hroptatyr, Rhinedottir, Surtalogi and Rerir to rescue his imprisoned brother Vedrfolnir from the palace. As the raid released Abyssal energy, monsters appeared in the Universitas, which was a nest of Abyss research. Vedrfolnir told Rerir to go there. Barricaded in the lab and without weapons to defend herself, Tholindis opened a Moon Gate and leapt into the "moon's reflection". Rerir arrived in time to see it but could not stop her.
### ULTRA +
**After the fall.** The kingdom's destruction ended the institution's known history. Eftirvit lived on under the immortality curse and eventually became Pierro. Hroptatyr, after taking a sixth of the power the Traveler's sibling had absorbed, gave Eftirvit a notebook and departed for the sea of stars. The Fatui Harbingers sealed that notebook in the Armory Palace in Glupov. In Etched in Time (7.1) it was opened. Its forbidden knowledge had been purged from Teyvat, so most pages were blank, but it held fragments of Hroptatyr's personal thoughts and findings from the Universitas.

## relations
### STANDARD
- **King Irmin**: the direct master of the institute, whose Abyss obsession redirected its work.
- **Eclipse Dynasty**: the royal house it served. Pierro is described as having served it as a royal mage.
- **Crimson Moon Dynasty**: the Universitas studied the Crimson Moon's remains, and Tholindis was a hidden descendant of the dynasty.
- **Fatui**: Pierro, its former Vice-President, later headed the Fatui. In 7.1 Arlecchino said that whatever methods the Universitas used on the Crimson Moon were centuries old and "history", and that she cared more about the future.
### LARGE +
- **Five Sinners of Khaenri'ah**: Hroptatyr, its President, became one of them, and so did Rerir, the fiancé of its researcher Tholindis.
- **Skirk and Surtalogi**: Surtalogi mastered the Universitas' Chaos Permeation spell (see below) and taught it to his disciple Skirk.

## reputation
### LARGE
In the Khaenri'ah of the story, the Universitas is spoken of as influential and as flattering its leaders. Rhinedottir said it "won't stop fawning" over Hroptatyr. Skofnungr, recalling Surtalogi's childhood in Unbounded (5.7), said he faced more criticism than the literary works of the Universitas, which only shows that the institute produced written works. Rerir, hearing of monsters in the city, feared for anyone at the Universitas because of its Abyss research.

## secrets
### ULTRA
Hroptatyr's research on the Authority of Death and the true nature of "immortality" was hidden from Eftirvit, who was never told why he stopped. Tholindis' ancestry and her work to help fellow Crimson Moon descendants were also secret. The notebook that preserves these findings was sealed away for centuries and opened only in 7.1.

## status
@version: 7.1
### LITE
The Universitas Magistrorum no longer exists as a known body. Its two leaders live on as the Fatui Director Pierro and as Hroptatyr, a Sinner of Khaenri'ah whose memory was glimpsed in 7.1. Canon does not say whether the institute itself survived the cataclysm.

## entry: Hroptatyr and Eftirvit
@keys: Hroptatyr, The Wise, Chief Royal Mage, Eftirvit
@keys2: Universitas, Mimameith, Khaenri'ah, Pierro
@version: 7.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Hroptatyr and Eftirvit studied side by side under Professor Mimameith at the Universitas and were its brightest scholars. Hroptatyr became President and the royal mage titled "The Wise"; Eftirvit became Vice-President. When Mimameith fell ill from her work on the Abyss, Hroptatyr found a way to extract the Authority of Death from the Crimson Moon, but realised it would curse her to endless suffering and refused to use it, without telling Eftirvit why. She died, and Eftirvit grew bitter. Around the time Eftirvit brought Paimon down from Celestia, Hroptatyr summoned the Traveler's sibling. Hroptatyr later took a share of the sibling's power, became a Sinner, and gave Eftirvit a notebook of his research and a prism holding his memories before leaving. Pierro told Paimon in 7.1 that, five hundred years on, he still felt unable to step out of Hroptatyr's shadow.

## entry: Moon Marrow lab and Tholindis
@keys: Moon Marrow lab, Tholindis
@keys2: Universitas, Rerir, Khaenri'ah
@version: 6.2
### STANDARD
The Moon Marrow lab at the Universitas studied the remains of the Crimson Moon as a possible new power source for Khaenri'ah. After Irmin shifted resources to the Abyss, Tholindis, a young researcher from a hidden branch of the Crimson Moon Dynasty, was its only remaining staff member. She found that the Moon Marrow could open a spatial gateway to a place where time runs backwards, the "moon's reflection", but could not explore it for lack of funding and technology to keep the gate open. She was engaged to Rerir. On the night of the raid, trapped by monsters, she jumped through a Moon Gate into the reflection, and her loss pushed Rerir toward joining the Five Sinners. Her fiancé's earlier role as an executioner of the Crimson Moon's descendants is told in the quest A Nation That Doesn't Exist.

## entry: Chaos Permeation
@keys: Chaos Permeation, Seven-Shifting Serpent
@version: 5.7
### STANDARD
Chaos Permeation was a minor spell developed by the Universitas to mock Vision wielders. A user drew on Abyssal energy to create negative pressure in reality that called up the flow of an element, producing effects like an elemental power. In Khaenri'ah it was a party trick for performances and parades, and ordinary street performers needed months of practice to shift just one element. Surtalogi, who understood Abyssal power, mastered it almost at once, cycling through all seven elements on his first attempt. He renamed it the Seven-Shifting Serpent and taught it to his disciple Skirk, who uses it to hide her Abyssal powers on Teyvat.
