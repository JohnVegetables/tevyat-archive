## summary
### LITE
The Fontaine Research Institute of Kinetic Energy Engineering, usually just "the Institute", is Fontaine's centre for the study of machines and energy. It was founded by the genius inventor Alain Guillotin, whose clockwork mechanisms and kinetic cores are still built in Fontaine's factories. Unlike Sumeru's Akademiya, it has no governing or teaching role. Its central laboratory was wrecked by an explosion during Edwin Eastinghouse's Arkhium experiment, and the rebuilt Institute is better known for muddle and money trouble than for brilliance.
### STANDARD +
Many of Fontaine's mechanical inventions come from the Institute, though some projects have embarrassed it, such as the "Mekafish" water cleaners that began killing fish.

## structure
### STANDARD
The Institute is a working organisation of researchers, engineers, technicians, assistants and clerks. Known departments include Advanced Artificed Technology Research and Development and the Department of Statistics and Mathematics; a Natural Ecological Environment Monitoring Department was dissolved after the accident. Notices mention executive, materials, logistics, finance investigation and disciplinary offices. Titles seen include Senior Technician, Senior Researcher, Researcher, Engineer, Research Assistant and Department Director.
### LARGE +
Internal notices show a bureaucracy that argues about paper use and bans gambling with paper chips. Researchers compete for backing, and Augustus Lovelace, an Institute researcher met in Sumeru, expects Raimondo to "reorganise", meaning end, his project.

## leadership
### LITE
Raimondo is the Senior Technician appointed to lead the Institute, and Choiseul is its administrative officer. Both took over the rebuilding after the explosion. Founding Director Alain Guillotin is long dead.
### STANDARD +
Edwin Eastinghouse, the Senior Technician who led the Arkhium project, was first presumed dead, then dismissed for long unauthorised absence, and finally fired after being found alive. Choiseul says the Institute is better ordered without Edwin's influence. Augustus Lovelace claims that research spirit has been in freefall since Raimondo and Choiseul took over, and says the Institute used to be a paradise for researchers. Rogier Cotes is named as a deceased Third Director.

## members
### STANDARD
Named staff include the engineer Anatole, who talks about the Institute to the Traveler in Liyue; Desnos, who handles recruitment at the temporary office; the researchers Xavier and Refait; and Maurice Juglar of Statistics and Mathematics. Delaroche, a former clerk now partly in charge of the Fishing Association, appears to remain formally a member. Former members include Edwin Eastinghouse and Nacker (both fired and sent to the Fortress of Meropide), Jurieu (a former assistant of Edwin who helped design the airship The Wingalet), and deceased members such as Bravais, Teilhard, Mosso Lombroso and Pierre Lefevere.
### LARGE +
The Traveler is an Honorary Senior Researcher and Paimon is Assistant to the Honorary Senior Researcher, titles given after the Fontaine Research Institute Chronicles (4.1). Salsa, a young Fontainian whose parents are on the staff list, says the Institute has financial problems and is considering "Ruins Tours" for outsiders.

## goals
### STANDARD
The Institute researches machines and energy for Fontaine's benefit. Its most ambitious project sought a way to survive the prophesied flood: researchers believed that harnessing Arkhium to defy gravity was the key, and planned to lift Fontaine into a "city in the sky". Since the disaster, leadership aims to stay solvent, restore order and keep the Arkhium ruins from exploding again.
### LARGE +
Edwin's own aim remained Arkhium research whatever the cost, and he treats lost researchers as replaceable. Raimondo told him to stay away from research and Arkhium.

## methods
### STANDARD
Researchers prototype machines, kinetic cores and automatons and track hazards with Survey Anchors. The Arkhium project used an Experimental Field Generator, a huge device that creates a gravity-reduction field, powered by an Arkhium Kinetic Core with a suppression device. Edwin's Prototype Cal. Breguet clockwork meka belonged to this work. Unlike the Akademiya, the Institute does not administer or educate the people of Fontaine.
### LARGE +
Alain's technology underlies the Clockwork Meka. He developed controlled Pneumousia Annihilation reactions, artificial Pneumousia Blocks and the kinetic core, and the Arkhe system that makes meka pneuma- or ousia-aligned. An attack of the opposite alignment disrupts a mek.

## history
### STANDARD
Alain Guillotin, who had been a member of the Narzissenkreuz Institute, a researcher at the Institute of Natural Philosophy and a Marechaussee Phantom hunter, founded the Institute. His inventions made it famous, and late in life he retired to his workshop, where he created Sandrone and Pulonia.

The Institute's crisis came when Edwin Eastinghouse's Arkhium experiment in the Central Laboratory overloaded. The explosion levelled most of the old Institute and sent blocks of anti-gravity water and remnants of the old ruins into the sky. The Callas Line aquabus route was shut down, and the Institute rebuilt at a new site near the ruins. Edwin was presumed dead.
### LARGE +
**Chronicles (4.1).** Desnos recruits the Traveler at a temporary office to investigate the ruins, which hold out-of-control meka and residual Arkhium. The Traveler learns the details of the first explosion and of the Institute's efforts to downplay the danger. A second accidental explosion follows when Nacker, a researcher who worked with Edwin, tampers with an Arkhium chunk and damages the cooling mechanism, which also reactivates the old field generator. The Traveler and Raimondo shut it down and place inhibitors to stabilise the ruins for the time being. Raimondo and Choiseul reward the Traveler with the honorary title.

Nacker then disappears, and the Traveler helps track him down. He has taken up with Concini, one of the financial backers (with Loillier) who had first funded Edwin, and Edwin turns out to be alive too: he had been inspecting other departments during the explosion and had set up a new laboratory in the Morte Region. Both are brought back to the Institute, dismissed, and handed to the Maison Gardiennage, who take them to the Fortress of Meropide.
### ULTRA +
According to Salsa, the Central Laboratory Ruins in the sky have been rebuilt, though she thinks the building the Institute now uses is less pretty than the ruins. Edwin, still keen on Arkhium, shows interest in Jurieu's airship The Wingalet and insists that only Arkhium could power it.

## relations
### STANDARD
Fontaine regards the Institute with mixed feelings: useful machines, poor accountability. Elphane finds its technicians' long-winded talk "ghastly", and Linorea holds a grudge over the Mekafish. It is separate from Sumeru's Akademiya. The Maison Gardiennage removed Edwin and Nacker to the Fortress of Meropide.
### LARGE +
Alain's creation Sandrone later became a Fatui Harbinger, and the Institute's link to the larger story runs through her (see status). Raimondo and Choiseul are on good terms with the Traveler and Paimon.

## reputation
### STANDARD
Fontainians see the Institute as a source of both useful machines and disasters. Complaints include long-winded technicians, the Mekafish failure, the explosion and money worries. Engineer Anatole admits the incident "put the Institute on the map".

## secrets
### LARGE
The danger around the ruins was bigger than the public was told: activated Arkhium can resonate and set off chain explosions, and the first explosion left shattered crystals that could be re-activated. Edwin's survival was unknown to the Institute at first; officially he was still an employee until dismissed for absence.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the Institute works from the New Fontaine Research Institute, still active but troubled. After Sandrone's death in Nod-Krai, her core was delivered to the Institute, and she has since reawakened in Fontaine.
### STANDARD +
The Institute is rebuilding under Raimondo and Choiseul, with financial problems, and Edwin and Nacker are in the Fortress of Meropide.
### LARGE +
Honouring a long-standing request that she called Sandrone's will, Arlecchino delivered Sandrone's and Pulonia's cores to the Institute after Sandrone's death in Nod-Krai (True Moon, 6.3). Sandrone woke on a workbench in a Fontainian-style room with Institute books around her; the manifestation of a Stellar Linchpin let Alain's built-in "Gift", a second chance, trigger, while Pulonia was beyond repair (Should We Meet Again, 6.7). Canon does not say what Institute staff knew of this.

### STANDARD
Alain Guillotin was the Institute's Founding Director, a prodigy raised at the Narzissenkreuz Institute who later researched at the Institute of Natural Philosophy and served in the Marechaussee Phantom with his sister Mary-Ann. He invented the Arkhe system and the Clockwork Meka. Legend says he retired to his workshop and published nothing. He created Sandrone and Pulonia before his death. His own entry holds the detail.

## entry: Edwin Eastinghouse and the Explosion
@keys: Edwin Eastinghouse, Eastinghouse, Experimental Field Generator, Central Laboratory Ruins
@version: 4.1
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
Edwin Eastinghouse was a Senior Technician and acknowledged genius who led the Arkhium project, building the Prototype Cal. Breguet meka and the Experimental Field Generator to lift Fontaine above the flood. He is dismissive of colleagues, treats lost researchers as replaceable and blames others for the explosion, saying he was inspecting other departments. After being found alive he was fired and sent to the Fortress of Meropide, where he still wants to research Arkhium.

## entry: Arkhium
@keys: Arkhium, Arkhium core, Tri-Arkhium
@version: 4.1
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
Arkhium is a yellow, green and blue crystal and a potent energy source. Inert, it is stable; activated, it emits powerful pulses and can trigger chain-explosive resonance in nearby crystals. An inhibitor can make activated Arkhium inert. Exposure makes most people ill, but the Traveler and Paimon resist it. It occurs naturally in parts of Fontaine such as the Geode Mine Shaft beneath the Fortress of Meropide.

## entry: Sandrone at the Institute
@keys: Sandrone, Marionette, Pulonia
@keys2: Research Institute
@version: 6.7
@availability: permanent
### LARGE
After Sandrone was killed by Dottore in Nod-Krai (True Moon, 6.3), Arlecchino honoured a request Sandrone had made long before, which she called her will, and delivered her core and Pulonia's to the Fontaine Research Institute. Sandrone later reawakened in Fontaine: a Stellar Linchpin manifested and let Alain's built-in "Gift" trigger, after which she repaired her body and imported the numerological array and some of Pulonia's surviving algorithms into herself. Pulonia was beyond repair (Should We Meet Again, 6.7). She then rented The Wingalet during the Fontinalia Film Festival to search for Alain's old home. Earlier, Alain had asked her to take Pulonia to the Institute for maintenance, but she kept it.
