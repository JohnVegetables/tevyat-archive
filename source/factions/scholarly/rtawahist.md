## summary
### LITE
Rtawahist is one of the six Darshans, the great schools of the Sumeru Akademiya. Its field is illuminationism, the study of the stars, including astronomy and astrology. Its members believe the sky reflects truth and that the stars write people's destinies. Its school colour is blue, and its crest shows an elephant-like mascot.
### STANDARD +
The Darshan's name comes from the Zoroastrian concept of Asha Vahista, "best truth". In Sumeru only academics may study astrology, although other forms of fortune-telling are allowed. Shakir is the current sage. Azar was its sage until Chapter III, when he fell as Grand Sage of the Akademiya.

## structure
### STANDARD
Rtawahist is led by a sage, one of the Six Great Sages who sit on the Akademiya's advisory councils. Below the sage come professors, researchers and students. Students may take elective courses in Darshans other than their own, although how often that happens is not stated. Its disciplines include astronomy and astrology. Students work on star charts, dissertations and papers, and scholars keep regular long hours.
### LARGE +
A Rtawahist student can specialise further. Layla, for example, studies Theoretical Astrology. Voice-overs also mention a chief editor of Rtawahist who keeps Starlogs showing the fates of all living beings, and Mona's astrology column in The Steambird is well known among Rtawahist scholars.

## leadership
### LITE
Shakir is the current Rtawahist sage. Azar was the Darshan's sage and the Akademiya's Grand Sage before he was removed.
### STANDARD +
Shakir looks stern but is soft-spoken. He is devoted to his work and tracks his tasks on a whiteboard. He once taught Layla and, in the Chapter III quest As All Falls to Emptiness, helped the Traveler with his expertise on dreams and foresight.
### LARGE +
Azar rose to Grand Sage. After the death of Greater Lord Rukkhadevata, he and other sages wanted a proper god to replace her successor Lesser Lord Kusanali, whom they judged a disappointment. As Grand Sage he barred desert-born students from the entrance exam that decides which Darshan a new student may join. When the Archon's authority was restored in Chapter III, Azar was removed. He was a Rtawahist sage, and the sages who supported him were replaced with new ones.

## members
### STANDARD
- **Shakir**: sage and the Akademiya's expert on dreams and foresight.
- **Layla**: a student of Theoretical Astrology, chronically sleep-deprived, who sleepwalks and finds her work finished in the morning.
- **Azar**: former sage and Grand Sage, now exiled.
- **Haypasia**: a researcher the Akademiya considers a genius for linking to the Irminsul with Spirit Borneol after three years of training.
- **Ashk, Qushji and Farghani**: scholars met in the open world, who watch the sky or calculate star courses.
- **Kaveh's father**: a Rtawahist scholar who entered the Interdarshan Championship 20 years ago and later died helping a caravan stuck in quicksand.
### LARGE +
Faruzan says most Rtawahist students, like Layla, are geniuses. Layla's own profile says her first year ended with her listing good observation and estimation skills, and that she often worries about whether her papers are good enough.

## goals
### STANDARD
Rtawahist holds that the stars show the truth about the world and that studying them will lead to it. Ashk says the untouchable sky conceals the truth, and that the stars "show" a destiny rather than decide it, because a decided destiny could be changed. Ashk adds that mortal sight is limited and asks who can guarantee they see all the celestial signs clearly.

## methods
### LARGE
Rtawahist scholars watch the sky, draw and recalculate star charts and write theses on the movements of the heavens. Calculation takes as much time as stargazing, which is why Sitt jokes that if they are not watching the sky they are calculating. Haypasia's research took a different route, linking her mind to the Irminsul using Spirit Borneol.

## history
### LARGE
The Akademiya's Six Great Schools were set up with the Akademiya itself under Greater Lord Rukkhadevata, each representing a type of wisdom, and Rtawahist is one of the six that endured. In the wiki's account, the Darshan's later history is mostly recorded through its people.

**Twenty years ago.** Kaveh's father, a Rtawahist scholar working for the Akademiya, entered the Interdarshan Championship to win the Diadem of Knowledge for his son Kaveh. After meeting Sachin he told his family he was going to Aaru Village in the desert, where he supported the villagers financially and medically. He died saving a caravan stuck in quicksand.

**Azar's rule.** Under Azar the Akademiya shut desert-born students out of the entrance exam. When Dottore, a scholar expelled long ago and now a Fatui Harbinger, offered to make a new god, Azar and three other sages accepted at once. Naphis and Baladhuri, who opposed it, were locked up on his orders. Azar oversaw the preparations, which included a dream samsara that harvested the energy of the city's inhabitants. He threatened Nilou during the Sabzeruz Festival and banned public art performances. When Cyno noticed anomalies in the data he submitted to the Akasha System and asked to investigate, Azar refused and told him he had no right to judge, and Cyno resigned as General Mahamatra.
### ULTRA +
**Fall of the sages.** After Kusanali was freed from the sages' custody, Cyno arrested Azar and made him repent in front of the Archon. After hearing that Nahida had restored the Irminsul, Azar and his accomplices supposedly repented, and they were exiled to the Avidya Forest. The four Darshans that had backed Azar had their sages replaced. Naphis, who had opposed the plot, became the Akademiya's Grand Sage.

## relations
### STANDARD
- **Other Darshans**: Rtawahist is one of six schools and the others (Amurta, Spantamad, Haravatat, Vahumana and Kshahrewar) each have a different specialty and colour. Sitt's service-desk line sums up Rtawahist as watching the sky or calculating.
- **The Steambird**: a magazine whose astrology column by Mona is popular among Rtawahist scholars.
- **Cyno**: he is a former General Mahamatra rather than a member, but he arrested Azar in Chapter III.
### LARGE +
- **Spantamad**: a sister school; Lisa and Cyno both studied there. Rtawahist and Spantamad both had sages replaced after Azar's fall.
- **The Archon**: Azar's group wanted a replacement for Lesser Lord Kusanali. Her return and the restoration of the Irminsul ended that plan.

## reputation
### ULTRA
Rtawahist is seen as a studious and slightly dreamy school whose members spend their nights on the sky. The sleep-deprived student is a running joke, with Qushji complaining that staring at stars hurts his neck. Its scholars are said to be brilliant, but the school is also remembered for Azar, whose policies hurt desert-born students.

## secrets
### ULTRA
The Rtawahist sage Azar was among the sages who plotted with Dottore to make a new god. The wiki describes the dream samsara as harvesting the energy of the city's inhabitants.

## entry: Azar
@keys: Azar
@keys2: Rtawahist, Akademiya, Grand Sage, Dottore
@version: 3.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Azar was the Rtawahist sage and the Grand Sage of the Sumeru Akademiya. After Greater Lord Rukkhadevata died, he and other sages wanted a new god, and he accepted Dottore's offer to build one. He locked up Naphis and Baladhuri for opposing it, harvested the city's energy through a dream samsara, and threatened Nilou to shut down her performance. In Chapter III Cyno arrested him; after the Irminsul was restored he and his accomplices supposedly repented and were exiled to the Avidya Forest.

## entry: Shakir
@keys: Shakir
@keys2: Rtawahist, Layla, Akademiya, sage
### STANDARD
Shakir is the current sage of Rtawahist. He is tall, with long brown hair, a full beard and round golden glasses, and wears a deep blue sherwani. He is stern in manner and soft-spoken in practice. He was once Layla's master and helped the Traveler during As All Falls to Emptiness by contributing his expertise on dreams and foresight.

## entry: Layla
@keys: Layla
@keys2: Rtawahist, Akademiya, astrology
### STANDARD
Layla is a Cryo character and a student of Theoretical Astrology in the Rtawahist Darshan. She is prone to sleepwalking and loses sleep over academic stress, and believes "the Stars' Blessing" is why she wakes to find her papers completed. Her own character story says she is slower to finish papers than her peers, but has good observation and estimation skills. Her full entry is a separate character entry.

## entry: Haypasia
@keys: Haypasia
@keys2: Rtawahist, Irminsul, Tighnari, Spirit Borneol
@version: 3.0
### LARGE
Haypasia is a Rtawahist researcher and considered a genius for connecting to the Irminsul with Spirit Borneol after only three years of training. In A Forest of Change she ignored the Traveler's request for directions to focus on her work, which caused the Traveler to faint and see a vision of the Irminsul. Later she believed she had made contact with a divine being and began acting strangely, so Tighnari had her taken to Pardis Dhyai for evaluation rather than see her labelled a Village Keeper and exiled to Aaru Village.

## entry: Kaveh's father and the Interdarshan Championship
@keys: Interdarshan Championship, Diadem of Knowledge
@keys2: Rtawahist, Kaveh, Sachin
### LARGE
The Interdarshan Championship is a competition between the Darshans. Twenty years ago Kaveh's father, a Rtawahist scholar, entered to win the Diadem of Knowledge for Kaveh. Sachin of Vahumana, who sponsored the event, influenced him, and he left for Aaru Village, where he died saving a caravan. Spantamad has produced the highest percentage of champions, and the competition later dropped its fight-to-the-death events.
