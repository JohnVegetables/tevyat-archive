## summary
### LITE
The Sword and Strongbox Secure Transport Agency is a guild of guards for hire based at Yilong Wharf in Liyue. Its guards escort valuable cargo and lone travellers so that bandits and wild beasts cannot rob them. It is a sub-organization of the Communications Office, which handles shipments and transport across Liyue. Its best-known guard is Gaming.
### STANDARD +
The agency advertises delivering goods safe and sound and giving clients peace of mind, and warns that customers bear every consequence of not honestly disclosing what is in the cargo.

## structure
### STANDARD
The agency is a sub-organization of the Communications Office. Hanqian, a guard who calls himself one of the leaders and "half an owner", is a senior figure; an owner has stepped back after the leadership group thinned out. Staff are called guards for hire. The recruitment notice offers food and lodging and excellent pay, and asks for physically fit applicants who can endure hardship and heavy labour.
### LARGE +
Hanqian says there were originally three leaders. After the agency began working with the Communications Office, the best of them lost his drive, and another was ambushed by bandits and took almost a year to recover, so Hanqian is the one left, waiting for newly recruited guards to improve things.

## members
### STANDARD
Gaming is the agency's gold-standard guard, a young man praised for pleasing clients while beating off bandits, and who also leads the Mighty Mythical Beasts troupe. Dip Yeuk is a newer guard and drums for that troupe. Hanqian, an open-world NPC at Yilong Wharf, is a leading guard. Huang the Third is another guard Hanqian worries about.
### LARGE +
Hanqian says a good guard needs to be agile, patient, good with directions, observant and quick-witted, and above all chivalrous, immune to bribery. He recalls a guard who colluded with bandits and stole a shipment, was chased down by other guards, handed to the Millelith, made to repay everything and imprisoned for life.

## goals
### STANDARD
The agency's business is delivering goods and clients intact. Its guards also protect customers who are travelling alone through mountains and forests.

## methods
### STANDARD
Guards focus on stamina, endurance and weight training rather than adventurers' skills. They train in horse stance, and Gaming has asked for plum blossom poles in the yard to firm up their fundamentals. Gaming's character story shows guards lifting cargo by hand to build strength.
### LARGE +
The agency's public bulletin board carries its advert, recruitment notice and messages from the public: one client thanks Gaming for a rescue in the mountains, another asks for a sparring match (refused because the guards are busy), a lovelorn customer complains that a delivered confession got no reply, and Baizhu writes that his apprentice is delivering medicine for the guards. The agency also takes outside bookings, such as arrangements for visits to Wangshan Hall, the Guhua Clan's old stronghold.

## history
### STANDARD
Gaming joined as a temporary hand during a Lantern Rite when he led a Fontainian merchant there while a lead guard was short of staff, and was kept on as an official guard after that run. The agency is first documented in version 4.4.

## relations
### STANDARD
- **Communications Office**: parent organization; per Hanqian the partnership changed the agency's rhythm.
- **Mighty Mythical Beasts**: Gaming's Wushou troupe, whose members are the agency's guards.
- **Millelith**: guards hand over criminals to them.

## reputation
### STANDARD
Merchants favour its guards, and a thanks plaque to Gaming praises his hawk-like sight. Public notices show a mix of admiration, jokes and complaints.

## secrets
### ULTRA
Nothing hidden is established. The agency's own account of its decline is modest: Hanqian blames the loss of two of its three leaders (one to lost drive after the Communications Office partnership, one to bandit injuries), and the unnamed owner chose to stay hands-off. Gaming's story adds that the lead guard fishes for ideas to improve guards' comfort, such as soft beds or Genius Invokation TCG decks.
