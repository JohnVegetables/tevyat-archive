## summary
### LITE
The Chasm Exploration Team is a small crew hired by Liyue's Ministry of Civil Affairs to look into the strange happenings in the Chasm, the mining region that was sealed off after a mysterious crystal appeared. They are the heart of the Chasm Delvers world-quest series, in which the Traveler helps them break the seal, set up camp in the Underground Mines and trace the dark mud to its source. Afterwards the team became an official part of Huishan Hall.
### STANDARD +
It is a practical, mixed crew rather than a military unit: an adventurer-cartographer, a foreign ecologist, a safety technician, a bard and a reformed Treasure Hoarder, all supplied by a bureaucrat who would rather be somewhere else.

## structure
### STANDARD
The team has no named commander. Muning, a specialist of the Ministry of Civil Affairs sent to the Chasm, handles its supplies and paperwork. The members are Zhiqiong (Adventurers' Guild, an adventurer chosen for her mapping talent), Khedive (an ecologist from the Sumeru Akademiya's Amurta), Jinwu (the Chasm's safety technician), Taliesin (a bard from Mondstadt) and Clitopho (a former Treasure Hoarder and alchemy student from Mondstadt). The team runs two camps, one near the Chasm's eastern surface entrance and, after the seal is broken, camps inside the Underground Mines.
### LARGE +
Khedive's presence comes from a collaboration agreement with the Liyue Qixing: he funds the team and studies the Chasm's ecological changes. Jinwu runs a simple crafting bench that upgrades the Lumenstone Adjuvant, the light source used against the darkness underground. Clitopho joined as the demolitions expert after the Traveler and Zhiqiong rescued him from other Treasure Hoarders, a gap that Jinwu says was meant to be filled by Klee with Kaeya as her partner before Klee was grounded.

## goals
### STANDARD
The team's job is to learn why the Chasm turned dangerous and to map and make safe the sealed mines. Individual members want different things: Zhiqiong wants to be remembered for a discovery, and Khedive wants to understand the ecological change.

## methods
### LARGE
Expeditions advance in stages. The Traveler first shatters the Bedrock Keys of the Seven-Star Seal that the Liyue Qixing placed over the Chasm. The team then establishes a camp underground, lights the mines with Lumenstone Adjuvant, maps the tunnels and clears obstructions, including a rockfall that is blown open with a giant cannon loaded with specially prepared fuses.

## history
### STANDARD
The team first appears in version 2.6 in The Chasm Delvers. The Traveler accepts an Adventurers' Guild commission and meets Muning, who is worried about the sealed Chasm. After the Seven-Star Seal is broken the team moves into the Underground Mines, where it meets miners, deals with Treasure Hoarders and finds the "dark mud" that spreads Abyssal corruption.
### LARGE +
Deeper down, the Traveler finds an ancient ruin tied to the cataclysm 500 years ago and a corrupted Skyfrost Nail, a meteorite-like object that fell from Celestia and was tainted by the Abyss Order. Purifying it makes the black ooze recede and the earthquakes stop. Zhiqiong, still hungry for answers, chooses to continue alone into the Abyss-touched depths, and Muning makes the rest of the group an official part of Huishan Hall.

## relations
### STANDARD
- **Ministry of Civil Affairs**: hired the team; Muning is its representative.
- **Liyue Qixing**: sealed the Chasm and, via Khedive, funds the research.
- **Huishan Hall**: the group became an official part of it.
- **Treasure Hoarders**: a recurring threat in the mines; Clitopho is a former member.

## members
### ULTRA
Member notes from the NPC pages. Muning enjoys leisure, drinks and probably gambling at Yanshang Teahouse, often owes people Mora, and Lan suspects the Ministry posted him to the Chasm to straighten him out. His family is prominent in Shenglu Hall and the porcelain trade, and his father paid off his debts but then told him he now owed the father. Clitopho studied alchemy in Mondstadt, became a Treasure Hoarder seeking freedom, and sold Chasm miners chilled valberry jam as fake "Real Man's Meds" under the alias Toclipho; the miners saw through it but bought it as a cheap cold treat. His fellow Hoarders imprisoned him after he abandoned a food-stealing party and released the Weasel Thieves. Jinwu has a former spouse and an unnamed daughter, as mentioned in the Chasm Delvers quest. Camp trivia: golden finches are kept in cages at the camps, which resembles the use of canaries to detect bad air in mines.

## status
@version: 2.6
### STANDARD
After The Chasm Delvers the team remains at its camps as an official arm of Huishan Hall. Zhiqiong is no longer with the group. No later developments for the team were found for 6.x or 7.0-7.1.

## entry: Zhiqiong
@keys: Zhiqiong
@keys2: Chasm, Chasm Exploration Team, Adventurers' Guild
### STANDARD
Zhiqiong is an Adventurers' Guild member chosen for the Chasm Exploration Team for her cartography. She comes from Mingyun Village, whose mines have dried up, and she admires great adventurers such as Stanley, Alice and the Traveler. She resents authorities who do little fieldwork of their own, and her wish to leave a mark makes her reckless around dangerous Abyssal forces. At the end of The Chasm Delvers she disappears into the mines' depths. See her own entry for more.
