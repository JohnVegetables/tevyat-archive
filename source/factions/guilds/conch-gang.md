## summary
### LITE
The Conch Gang is an informal band of stray children in Nasha Town, in Nod-Krai. Katya, the retired adventurer who runs the restaurant Speranza, feeds, houses and employs them while letting them live freely. The gang makes its own decisions by vote, and Speranza is its home base.
### STANDARD +
It is not an official guild or criminal outfit. Maite describes it as a mutual aid and coordination group for like-minded people of similar age. Its name comes from a local folk tale about children who ruled Nasha Town and called each other together by blowing a conch shell.

## structure
### STANDARD
There is no named leader. A new member can join only if every existing member agrees. The Traveler remarks that the gang has many members and some pretty good benefits: food, shelter and work at Speranza. Members have great freedom to follow their own interests, from studying jellies to travelling abroad. The gang usually takes only children and sends members away at a certain age, though there is a precedent for admitting someone older.
### LARGE +
Katya is the adult guardian but not a member. Helka calls her "not one of us" and a trustworthy person to turn to. Members treat each other as a chosen family: Helka calls her friends more like family, even though they are not related by blood. Havu, a local child outside the gang, claims not to care about them and admits they once fought, yet her idle line suggests she might think about joining if they begged.

## members
### STANDARD
Current members named on the wiki:
- Jurva: the super chef at Speranza. She hopes everyone can live the lives they want.
- Maisie: a timid girl who works at Speranza as a host and does simple chores. Helka says her constitution is too frail for travel, and she keeps in touch with Helka through pictures and postcards.
- Mikko: a Speranza host who worries Katya is angry after he dropped and broke some things.
- Helka: a member who left Nod-Krai to catalogue monsters of other nations.
- Maite: a self-styled master thief, met at the Crab Tsar's Palace after The Mirrors, the Maze, and the Tsar.
- Auni: a small girl who holds funerals for animals, met in Friends of Moleyvalley.
- Semyon: a member who went to Maroon Basin to research Tassel Jellies.

Former members: Aino and Linnea (both playable characters) and Tapani.
### LARGE +
Maite dubs the Traveler an official friend of the gang, but tells them they are too old to join. Auni tells the Traveler and Paimon to call her "Big Sis", says Jurva gave her pocket money, and insists that anyone who gets help should repay it and say thanks. Tapani, met at The Flagship tavern, says Katya raised him but that he does not hide it or shout it from the rooftops, and he recommends Speranza.
### ULTRA +
Some gang members told Jahoda that Derwent, a hermit on Lempo Isle, taught them to read. Aino, now known as the founder of the Clink-Clank Krumkake Craftshop, was once one of Katya's wards. Linnea, a playable advisor to the Adventurers' Guild, is also listed as a former member, but her dates are not documented.

## goals
### STANDARD
The group has no stated mission. Their shared aim is to look after one another and live as they like. Jurva says Katya taught her that it is better to have impossible dreams than none, and that the younger kids living as they do now means her own dream has come halfway true.

## history
@version: 5.3
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
Canon gives no founding date. The story of children conquering Nasha Town is folklore. In the present, Katya, a former Treasure Hoarders adventurer who retired because of injuries, took in children left to fend for themselves on the streets. She says she could not stand seeing them without shelter and warm food. The kids work for their keep.
### LARGE +
Helka and Maisie are first mentioned in the On the Trail of Behemoths event, which is where the Conch Gang first appears.
### ULTRA +
In that event Helka, travelling in Natlan, hires the Traveler and Paimon to help record giant monsters for her illustrated handbook of 100 monster varieties. Afterwards she says her Natlan and Fontaine series are done and she will head to Sumeru. The Traveler asks to join, and she answers that the others must all vote yes, then points them to Speranza and Katya.

## relations
### STANDARD
Katya is their protector and employer, and Speranza is their base. The Traveler is a friend of the gang but not a member. Derwent taught some of them to read.
### LARGE +
Maite says Katya warned against making contact with the Frostmoon Scions, and that she herself was nearly caught by them once.

## status
### STANDARD
As of 7.1 the Conch Gang is active around Speranza in Nasha Town. Helka remains abroad on her monster-cataloguing journey, and Maisie stays in town. The sources checked show no appearance by the gang in Version 7.0 or 7.1 content.

### STANDARD
Joining takes a unanimous yes from every current member. Havu says it is somewhat difficult. The gang usually accepts only children and sends members away at a certain age, though there is a precedent for older newcomers. Maite told the Traveler they are too old, and Helka said age might complicate things but did not rule it out.

## entry: The Conch Shell Tale
@keys: conch shell, Nasha Town conquered, children conquered Nasha Town
@keys2: Conch Gang, Jurva
### STANDARD
Jurva says a story goes that children once built Nasha Town and called each other together by blowing into a conch shell, voting on every decision. She says nobody really believes it and wonders who made it up. The Traveler's voice-over line adds that the town developed into the bustling settlement it is today, and the gang no longer dominates it.

## entry: Helka and the Monster Handbook

### STANDARD
Helka appears in the On the Trail of Behemoths event. She is a Conch Gang member who knows every monster in Nod-Krai and left to learn about monsters elsewhere. Her task is an illustrated handbook of 100 monster varieties, and she posted a commission with the Adventurers' Guild for help. She says Katya told her a good enough book might get her published. She sends copies and pictures to Maisie, who loves monster stories.

## entry: Speranza
@keys: Speranza
@keys2: Conch Gang, Katya
@spoiler: none
### STANDARD
Speranza is the main restaurant in Nod-Krai, in Nasha Town on Lempo Isle, run by Katya. Staff include the chef Jurva and the hosts Maisie and Mikko. A fortune wheel stands outside, and a dog named Pavlov is nearby. The Conch Gang treats it as its home base. It sells food and ingredients, and completing the quest Rest Beyond Reach gives a permanent discount.
