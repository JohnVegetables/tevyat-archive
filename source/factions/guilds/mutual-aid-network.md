## summary
### LITE
The Mutual Aid Network is a small society of prisoners in the Fortress of Meropide, Fontaine's underwater prison. Vautrin, a former Garde captain exiled there, founded it about 400 years ago. Its members are mostly minor offenders who band together so that stronger inmates cannot push them around. Their badge is based on Vautrin's Medal of Peace.
### STANDARD +
Membership is free and has no compulsory events. The Network sees itself as a way of getting through a sentence with dignity, not as a gang, and it is not the same group as the dissolved Beret Society.

## structure
### STANDARD
The Network is informal. Vautrin was its first president, and it has kept a badge, a pamphlet and a short list of principles ever since. Canon names no current president or ranks. Joining costs nothing and no event is compulsory. Members the Traveler meets are Millereau, who hands out the pamphlet in the Dormitory Block, Gordes, and Auriol.
### LARGE +
Gordes stresses that the Network has never had many members and has little reputation, but that its members treat each other like family. Millereau mentions that a new member had joined the previous month, so recruitment is slow and steady.

## goals
### LITE
Members help one another, work for justice and accept lawful judgment. They try to do something useful with their time inside and to face up to the crimes they committed.
### STANDARD +
The pamphlet gives three principles: help each other, do all you can to further justice, and do not detest just judgment. It encourages cleaning, exercising, tutoring and studying, and hopes members will support one another as they face their crimes and welcome the future together.

## members
### STANDARD
- **Vautrin**: founder and first president, deceased. His own file carries his full story.
- **Millereau**: the Network's advertiser in the Dormitory Block. He greets passers-by with the phrase "Help each other".
- **Gordes**: a member who explains the Network's history to the Traveler and helped put down Domenico's rebellion.
- **Auriol**: a member who says the Network turned a miserable sentence into one with friends and shared purpose.
### LARGE +
Auriol describes the first president as powerful but never using that strength against anyone, and says every member respects him. Members are chiefly people with minor offences. Gordes insists none of them has committed a serious crime and that they are not especially strong, which is why they are at a natural disadvantage inside the Fortress.

## methods
### STANDARD
The Network's main weapon is solidarity: weaker prisoners stick together so that others will not pick on them. Members tutor, study, clean and exercise, and keep to the Fortress's rules. They are willing to act when order is threatened. During the failed rebellion of Domenico against Neuvillette, Network members stepped in and, in Gordes's words, taught him a lesson. Domenico complained that nobody would team up with him, the Network in particular.

## history
### STANDARD
**Founding.** Vautrin was the captain of the Special Security and Surveillance Patrol when Neuvillette brought the Melusines into Fontainian society. His friend, the Melusine Carole, died after hardliners opposed to Neuvillette framed her, and in revenge Vautrin killed those hardliners. Neuvillette sentenced him for abuse of authority. He was exiled to the Fortress of Meropide, where he never showed physical or verbal aggression, urged weaker criminals to stick together, and founded the Network about 400 years ago.
### LARGE +
**The badge.** Vautrin designed the Network's badge from the Medal of Peace that Neuvillette had made for him. Neuvillette says he crafted two such medals, for Carole and Vautrin. Carole's was lost in a fire, so the surviving one should be Vautrin's. Members today know the badge only as a symbol, and Auriol has never seen the medal itself.

**The Traveler's investigation (4.1, In a New Light).** While visiting the Fortress with Neuvillette during the Diluvies Chapter, the Traveler and Paimon notice inmates wearing the familiar badge. Questions to Auriol, Millereau and Gordes reveal the Network's history. Wriothesley, the Fortress's Duke, later says he had noticed the small group when he took over. He studied its founder and reasoned that Vautrin's courtroom anger had been staged to prove Neuvillette's fairness to the undecided. Neuvillette reflects with regret on that trial.
### ULTRA +
Wriothesley's account is an inference from the records, not a confession. Neuvillette says only that Vautrin's feelings seemed complicated and that his words still echo in his mind. Paimon at first suspects the Network may be a shady organisation like the Beret Society. Gordes replies that the Beret Society was dissolved and that the Network is completely different.

## relations
### STANDARD
Wriothesley, who runs the Fortress, regards the Network as small but principled. Neuvillette has a personal link to its founder and the badge. Among inmates, the Network opposes bullying and rebellion, and has no recorded link to any group outside the Fortress.

## reputation
### STANDARD
Gordes admits the Network has little reputation. Wriothesley sees it as a group that always stood up for what was right.

## status
### LITE
As of the 4.1 Diluvies Chapter, the Network is active in the Fortress of Meropide and canon records no later change.
### STANDARD +
It is not mentioned in the 6.x Nod-Krai or the 7.0 to 7.1 Snezhnaya content. A similarly named group, the Mutual Aid Advancement Network, appears in a Version 7.0 limited event but is a separate organisation.

## entry: Mutual Aid Network Badge and Pamphlet
@keys: Mutual Aid Network, Mutual Aid Society, Mutual Aid Network badge, Mutual Aid Network pamphlet
@version: 4.1
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
Members of the Network wear a badge on their chests, designed by Vautrin from the Medal of Peace he received from Neuvillette. The pamphlet lists three principles: help each other, do all you can to further justice, and do not detest just judgment. It states that membership is free, that there are no compulsory events, and that members should do what they are able: cleaning, exercising, tutoring, studying. Its closing wish is for members to face their crimes in the Fortress, encourage and support one another, and welcome the future together.

## entry: Vautrin's Founding and Trial
@keys: Vautrin, Vautrin's trial
@keys2: Mutual Aid Network, Neuvillette, Carole, Meropide
@version: 4.1
@spoiler: minor
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
Vautrin killed the hardliners who had framed his friend Carole, and was sentenced by Neuvillette and exiled to Meropide. In court he showed fury, but in prison he was disciplined and non-violent. Wriothesley suggests the court display was a deliberate act to prove Neuvillette's impartiality, which Vautrin trusted him to show. Treat that as an inference, not stated fact. Use Vautrin's own file for more.

## entry: Mutual Aid Advancement Network (Snezhnaya event)
@keys: Mutual Aid Advancement Network, Mutual Aid in Bloom, Chichikova
@version: 7.0
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
In the limited-time 7.0 event Mutual Aid in Bloom: Into the Frostlands, the Traveler joins a group called the Mutual Aid Advancement Network, managed by an enthusiastic young Snezhnayan girl named Chichikova. Its stated aim is to pool diverse talents to help more people with a wider range of challenges, and its camp has a storage device for sharing spare equipment. It is a separate group from the Fontainian prison society, and canon shows no connection.
