## summary
### LITE
The Korolevskiy Troupe is a performance company in Snezhnaya that specialises in opera, theatre, ballet and musical performance. It performs at the Korolevskiy Theater in Snezhnograd and is very popular, especially among the Fatui Harbingers and Mitya. Its two star performers are Odette, Prima Ballerina, and Vodyanitsa, Prima Donna. Both also have a hidden life beyond the stage.
### STANDARD +
The name is Russian for "royal". The troupe's reputation rests on its leading women, whose work critics compare with the older, more classical Snezhnayan stage tradition. A critic in the theater says their performances drop the excess of traditional grand-tragedy opera in favour of restraint and elegance. The official map description of the theatre is about the work behind the illusion: success is measured by whether the audience can be given a moment's escape into something beautiful.

## structure
### STANDARD
Canon names no director or manager. The two top roles are Prima Ballerina (Odette) and Prima Donna (Vodyanitsa). Odette's character story shows the two principals also coordinating the programme, preparing costumes and calming nervous newcomers before their first performance. Below them are the chorus and ballet performers, who appear mostly unnamed. The wider theatre has its own stage and front-of-house staff, such as the ticket-seekers' queue, critics and local fans, but individual staff are minor.
### LARGE +
Former members show that a career can lead out of the troupe. Dorofeya was a rising star who retired and married a nobleman. Pashkov left to become the safety officer in Svetloledovka. Violetta, Odette's mother, was an acclaimed ballerina at the theater until a fall ended her career.

## members
### LITE
Odette, the Prima Ballerina, is calm, exacting and nearly expressionless on and off stage. Vodyanitsa, the Prima Donna, is a Water Imp (a Snowland fae) whose singing is said to carry an otherworldly allure; she is lively and outspoken.
### STANDARD +
Odette Spessiva comes from an old ballet family and was taught by her mother Violetta. Her admirers call her dancing precise and austere, and the press describes it as "perfection, polished to an icy sheen". Vodyanitsa is the last of her kind on the stage: the Water Imps once filled Snezhnaya's streets and stages with song, but by the time of her story most had left, and she stayed on at the Korolevskiy Theater. The two are friends and the troupe's senior figures, and few colleagues can talk freely with Odette outside a performance.
### LARGE +
Former members named in the sources are Dorofeya, Pashkov and Violetta (see their entries). Fans and critics fill the theater's lobby. Examples are Taisiya, who admires Odette, and Zamorov, who prefers Vodyanitsa and argues with her about which of them best represents the theater. Pisemsky, a critic, appreciates both performers.
### ULTRA +
Odette's character story records her habit of rehearsing alone in the early morning, and how colleagues arriving at dawn sometimes found her still in the rehearsal room. After a performance she rarely returns for an encore and answers fans briefly. Vodyanitsa, by contrast, sings with open warmth and takes fans' attention in her stride. A backstage candy given by Odette to Vodyanitsa before a performance marks the start of their friendship, in Odette's second character story.

## goals
### STANDARD
The troupe's stated purpose is performance: giving audiences a beautiful illusion, with the labour kept behind the curtain. No political aim is attributed to the troupe as an organisation. Its two leading performers have additional commitments outside the theatre (see secrets).

## methods
### LARGE
Its repertoire includes opera, ballet and musical shows. The classic Snezhnayan ballet Spirit Seeker is a family inheritance of the Spessivas, and Violetta carried it before Odette. Performances often run in the evening, and seats are in demand, with the theatre not selling standing tickets, taking extra chairs or allowing a draught through the doors. A related item, the Polestar Ballet Anthology, is a book about ballet in Snezhnaya.
### ULTRA +
Backstage routines in Odette's and Vodyanitsa's stories show the troupe's working style: rehearse the rhythm, take each step one at a time, and encourage the chorus with energy before they go on. Vodyanitsa has also performed in the open, and the Archon Quest of Chapter VII (7.0) has her singing in Russian.

## history
### STANDARD
Nothing is recorded about the troupe's founding. The only dated material comes from the Chapter VII period. Before the Traveler's visit, Odette and Vodyanitsa had already built their reputations: critics use their names as shorthand for the theater, and the old-style gloom of grand tragedies has given way to their sharper, cleaner shows. Violetta's earlier career is part of the troupe's past, and her injury left Odette to carry the Spessiva name forward.
### LARGE +
Odette's father, a soldier, died suddenly, and the family lost his income. Her mother Violetta, whose career had already ended in a fall, taught her ballet, and Odette accepted Signora's offer to join the Fatui against her mother's wishes (per Violetta's page, citing her fourth character story). Vodyanitsa's story says that as the Water Imps left Snezhnograd and the city's songs faded, a thread of song was still heard from the Korolevskiy Theater, where she remained.
### ULTRA +
In the Rokot quest chain (7.0), Alyosha brings the Traveler to the theater to meet Vodyanitsa. After a later performance a snowstorm traps everyone inside; the wiki summary attributes it to Ronova's Curse of Death, triggered when Mitya thought of Project Stuzha (see the Wraith's Nocturne and Winter's Mysterious Silence quests). Vodyanitsa's Requiem saved the Traveler and Mitya. Later, Odette, Vodyanitsa and Alyosha helped renovate Rankova Zorya.

## relations
### STANDARD
- **Fatui and Harbingers**: the wiki says the troupe is especially popular among Fatui Harbingers. After the Traveler's visit, Tartaglia, Arlecchino and Sandrone can be seen as audience members at the theater. Odette works for the Fatui and, per the wiki, is the favoured candidate to inherit her mentor Signora's Harbinger seat.
- **Mitya**: a notable fan of the troupe.
- **Rokot**: Odette (as Lelek) and Vodyanitsa belong to this anti-Project-Stuzha group, though neither knew the other was a member at first.
### LARGE +
- **Critics and fans**: papers publish heated commentary at the end of each season. A critic called Tripolev wrote that Odette dances "like a dagger honed to perfection".
- **Spessiva family**: Odette's mother Violetta was a former ballerina of the theatre, and her story describes the Spessivas as a family whose stage glory had faded.

## reputation
### STANDARD
Highly regarded, with devoted fans and a queue that never shortens. The audience praises Odette's austerity and Vodyanitsa's voice. Gossip about Odette's coldness is a running joke: one tale has a nobleman reserving a whole row and waiting two hours with a bouquet only to be told she had left by the side door. Others, including Zamorov and Taisiya, fight over which of the two stars represents the theater best.

## secrets
@spoiler: major
@version: 7.0
### ULTRA
Both principals have lives outside the theater. Odette is a Fatui member, mentored by Signora, and also the Rokot agent Lelek (the codename is a Winter's Mysterious Silence revelation). Vodyanitsa is also a Rokot member. At first they did not know about each other's membership. Rokot seeks to oppose Project Stuzha. The troupe as an institution is not described as taking part in either secret.

## status
@version: 7.1
### LITE
As of 7.1 the troupe is active at the Korolevskiy Theater in Snezhnograd. Odette and Vodyanitsa are both playable characters.
### STANDARD +
The Korolevskiy Theater is an open area in Everfrozen Earth with its own Meeting Point (unlocked via the world quest One Yet to Take a Bow) and a set of NPCs. Odette and Vodyanitsa appear in the theater after the Chapter VII events. Nothing further about the troupe is canon beyond 7.1.

## entry: Korolevskiy Theater
@keys: Korolevskiy Theater, Korolevskiy Theatre
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
The Korolevskiy Theater is a subarea of Everfrozen Earth in Snezhnograd where the troupe performs. Its named staff and visitors include Balkonov, Osipova, Pisemsky, Polovikova, Roshchina, Shuvalov, Taisiya, Vzdykhalov, Yadova and Zamorov. The wiki notes that the theater and the troupe may take their name from Nepla Korolevitsna, wife of the fae Filipov, though this is not confirmed.

## entry: One Yet to Take a Bow
@keys: One Yet to Take a Bow, Odette's music box
@version: 7.0
### LARGE
In this world quest, the Traveler hears that eerie music and a dancing shadow haunt the theater at midnight. The ghost turns out to be Odette, who is repairing a swan-shaped music box that her mother gave her as a child, at night in her private lounge because her days are full. She asks for help collecting parts and keeping it secret. Her lounge is almost bare, which suggests she gives everything to dancing. Completing the quest unlocks the theater's Meeting Point.

## entry: Odette and Vodyanitsa as a pair
@keys: Odette and Vodyanitsa, prima ballerina and prima donna
@keys2: Korolevskiy, Troupe, Theater, Theatre
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
The Prima Ballerina and Prima Donna contrast in manner and stand close in friendship. Odette is quiet and dry. Vodyanitsa teases and drags her to meals. A backstage scene shows Odette giving Vodyanitsa honey candy "for your throat" and Vodyanitsa insisting that she saw Odette smile, which Odette explains away as the expression the role required. Post-quest dialogue also has Odette complain that Vodyanitsa would ambush her backstage if she skipped one of her performances.

## entry: Former members
@keys: Dorofeya, Pashkov, Violetta Spessiva
@version: 7.0
### LARGE
Dorofeya was once a rising star in the troupe, grew up begging in Zolnyy before joining, worked with Violetta, still admires Odette's skill and is married to a nobleman who loves her. Pashkov left to become the new safety officer in Svetloledovka and is known for stubbornness. Violetta Spessiva was an acclaimed ballerina who inherited the classic ballet Spirit Seeker from her family; a fall ended her career and she then focused on teaching her daughter Odette. Her grief over her husband's sudden death is said in Odette's story to have led to her injury.

## entry: Spirit Seeker
@keys: Spirit Seeker
@version: 7.0
### LARGE
Spirit Seeker is a classic Snezhnayan ballet that Violetta inherited from her family. Ballet fans like to compare how Violetta and Odette dance it. A critic in the theater notes that Violetta's style leans towards absolute classical restraint, while Odette's choreography has a sense of martial restraint.
