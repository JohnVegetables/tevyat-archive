## summary
### LITE
The Courier's Longhouse is the courier agency of the Scions of the Canopy, the Natlan tribe of Coatepec Mountain. Its couriers carry letters and parcels. It accepts ordinary deliveries only, not anything extremely valuable, fragile or dangerous. It is run from a base in the Scions' settlement.
### STANDARD +
The bulletin board calls the couriers highly skilled professionals whose deliveries almost always arrive, though the Longhouse will not guarantee against accidents along the way.

## structure
### STANDARD
The base is the Courier's Longhouse inside the Scions of the Canopy settlement, with a bulletin board beside it. Michica is its manager and is the person customers see to hire a courier. Commissions are taken per delivery, not for fixed periods. Couriers pay management fees, which the Longhouse says go toward standard-issue gear. Michica's gear assessment report goes to Chief Wayna, and the board invites couriers to send feedback to Miss Amauta. Couriers are ranked each month by completed deliveries.
### LARGE +
A courier's work is not limited to the settlement. Sorimana, a courier in Tequemecan Valley, leaves regular supply drops of food and other goods at places where couriers tend to stop off. Some are for humans and some are made for their saurian companions. The monthly top ten receive a cash prize, and a chew stick made by Aclla.

## members
### STANDARD
Michica is the manager. The bulletin board names Zeila, who topped one monthly ranking with 110 long-distance deliveries in twenty days, and Adama, a star courier who fell to fifth while preparing for the Pilgrimage of the Return of the Sacred Flame. Tundama, Sausa and Sibu are named in that order for the next places, and the top ten includes others. Sorimana is a courier who handles supply drops.
### LARGE +
Michica describes some of the younger couriers as careless, thrill-seeking youngsters. In the daily commission "Hey, Your Letter!", the Traveler is asked to recover parcels lost by couriers who ignored instructions and slid down slopes as if on holiday.

## methods
### STANDARD
Customers sometimes demand the impossible, such as falcon-speed delivery across a distance greater than the deserts of Sumeru with no damage to the packaging, and the Longhouse says plainly it cannot promise that. It warns couriers delivering to the Flower-Feather Clan to drop off their parcels before accepting any gliding race with clan members. The clan is better at gliding, and a race could damage the contents.
### LARGE +
Michica inspects parcels and has confiscated a great deal of contraband, including explosive paint. The usual hazards of the job are broken contents and misdelivered parcels, for example an address written in ink that changes with the outside temperature.

### ULTRA +
Michica says that in the course of the job, a package's address can vanish and contents can break, and that the amount of confiscated contraband is large. Michica also challenges the Traveler to a Genius Invokation TCG match in an adventure challenge, with the stakes being relief from work frustrations. Michica pretends complaints have been "burned away" by the Sacred Flame, after joking about selling confiscated goods.

## goals
### STANDARD
The Longhouse aims to deliver ordinary items reliably, to maintain the couriers' reputation, and to improve its protective gear, whose usage rate it admits is low.

## relations
### STANDARD
It is part of the Scions of the Canopy under Chief Wayna. Its couriers deal with the Flower-Feather Clan, whose gliding skill the board warns couriers about. The board says Kinich, a Scion, is the only one capable of the most extreme deliveries, but he is not a courier.
### LARGE +
The board shows couriers taking part in the preparations for the Pilgrimage of the Return of the Sacred Flame, since the Pilgrimage is the reason Adama's ranking slipped.

## reputation
### LARGE
Publicly the Longhouse presents its couriers as skilled and reliable. Michica is more candid, complaining about contraband and careless couriers, and says the carelessness damages the Scions' reputation.

## status
@version: 5.0
### LITE
As of 7.1 the Longhouse is still operating at the Scions of the Canopy under its manager Michica.
### STANDARD +
After the Natlan war (the Archon Quest No One Fights Alone), Michica says the Longhouse is short-staffed. Many couriers carrying battle reports collapsed from exhaustion and others are still running between camps. Intelligence about the Abyss and reconstruction takes priority, so other parcels may be delayed. No later developments were found for 6.x or 7.0-7.1; the 7.0-7.1 Archon Quests take place in Snezhnaya.

## entry: Michica
@keys: Michica
@keys2: Courier's Longhouse, Longhouse, courier, Scions of the Canopy
### STANDARD
Michica is the manager of the Courier's Longhouse and an open-world NPC at the Scions of the Canopy. Michica sounds worn down: after joking that the confiscated contraband could stock a shop, Michica insists that it was only an analogy and not a plan to change jobs. After the war in Natlan, Michica reports that couriers are exhausted and stretched thin. Pronouns and age are not stated in the sources checked, and Michica asks the Traveler for help in the daily commission "Hey, Your Letter!" because the Longhouse has too few hands to look for lost parcels.

## entry: Couriers' Longhouse Bulletin Board
@keys: Couriers' Longhouse Bulletin Board, Longhouse bulletin board, courier rankings
### LARGE
The board carries cycling notices from the Longhouse. They include the monthly courier rankings, a warning about racing the Flower-Feather Clan, instructions on hiring a courier, and a request for feedback on standard-issue gear. The gear notice asks couriers to report which parts of the protective gear break, where screws fall out, and which materials suffer from moisture and Gaseous Phlogiston. It admits the gear is not used much at present. The board also says the Longhouse takes commissions per delivery only and cannot guarantee against accidents.
