## summary
### LITE
The Graffiti Alliance is a group in Natlan that promotes the art and history of graffiti. Anyone can join, whatever their experience or style, and the Alliance gives new members free supplies and lessons. It is hundreds of years old and now has hundreds of members.
### STANDARD +
Members paint all over Natlan. The Alliance's headquarters is a plain-looking house at the Masters of the Night-Wind, but its members' works are not kept there. They are painted on cliffs, walls and houses across the region.

## structure
### STANDARD
The Alliance is loose. There is no test or ceremony to join and no required meetings or duties. A new member is simply noted down by "Mural Maniac", the Guru-in-Chief, who manages the headquarters. Members are mostly out painting, and visit only to pay fees or ask for technical help. Dues are one thousand Mora per person each year, and members are expected to spread the word about the Alliance.
### LARGE +
The Alliance has a gallery (Yolo's setting) with members' paintings on the walls, and it was planning a training area at its headquarters. Dopey Dazzler describes the group's "lofty ambition" of matching the Adventurers' Guild, which is why a headquarters mattered.

## leadership
### STANDARD
"Mural Maniac" is the Guru-in-Chief and runs the headquarters. "Dopey Dazzler" is a mainstay who promotes a philosophy of avant-garde art. Neither has a stated real name; Dopey Dazzler says titles matter more than real names to graffiti artists.

## members
### STANDARD
Known members are "Mural Maniac" (Guru-in-Chief), "Dopey Dazzler" and Yolo. Mural Maniac says anyone in Natlan holding a spray can and a paint bucket is probably a member. Yolo is also affiliated with the Collective of Plenty.
### LARGE +
Dopey Dazzler was once badly injured painting a cliffside when a safety line snapped, and was laid up for three months with bandages around her head; she envies the Scions of the Canopy's skill at working at height. Mural Maniac took some commissions drawing comics and is also a registered member of the Adventurers' Guild, which makes survey trips abroad easier.

## goals
### STANDARD
The Alliance teaches and spreads graffiti across Natlan. Some members, such as Dopey Dazzler, want to advance graffiti as a whole through "avant-garde" thinking, first in Natlan and then in the rest of the world.

## methods
### STANDARD
The Alliance funds supplies and free lessons from yearly dues. Dopey Dazzler's advice is to study Natlan's landscape and customs to find the shape and colour of emotions, and her notebook says bold lines and vivid, bright colours are the key to eye-catching graffiti. Members mix pigments from local minerals, petals and saurian scales, according to Yolo.
### LARGE +
Dopey Dazzler says graffiti grew out of imitations of Phlogiston Engravings, the tribal symbols cut by inscribers, which ordinary people painted as marks of courage and honour. Over time the designs became more free-spirited. Her notebook adds that Natlan is not strict about technique, unlike the formal art schools of Liyue and Fontaine: anyone can paint on any flat surface as long as they cause no trouble for others.

## history
### STANDARD
The Alliance was established a few centuries ago, possibly by someone from the Masters of the Night-Wind. Outsiders told the Alliance that art elsewhere prized heritage, and a master then introduced the dues system. The fees have not changed for hundreds of years.
### LARGE +
The Alliance's growth is recent. In Traces of Artistry (5.0) Dopey Dazzler says the group has just over ten people and is gathering funds for a headquarters. By Mural Maniac's dialogue the headquarters is built and there are a couple of hundred members, which Mural Maniac says has caused an expense problem because the dues no longer cover costs.

### ULTRA +
The Alliance's wider reach was visible during Soulblazers' Snowbound Journey, when Mural Maniac mentioned that comic-book techniques are borrowed in some graffiti styles and that Mural Maniac has drawn comics on commission. Mural Maniac hoped to see how Mondstadt's people would adapt their art style into graffiti, within areas permitted by the administrators. The "Saury-Saury Scout" was designed to survey dangerous terrain and may go into mass production for adventurers and future members if enough data is collected.

## relations
### STANDARD
The Alliance's headquarters sits at the Masters of the Night-Wind. Its members admire the Scions of the Canopy's skill at heights. Dopey Dazzler treats the Adventurers' Guild as a model.
### LARGE +
In Soulblazers' Snowbound Journey (6.2, a limited-time event), Mural Maniac says the Alliance is recruiting talented artists from across the world. In that event the Guru-in-Chief collaborated with the Knight of Favonius Mika on the "Saury-Saury Scout" after Mika rescued Mural Maniac from the cold on Dragonspine.

## reputation
### LARGE
The Alliance thinks of itself as a long-standing craft community and its members as the makers of Natlan's most visible art. The dues and free lessons are credited with spreading graffiti's popularity. Mural Maniac says people should only paint in areas the administrators permit and never damage local scenery.

## entry: "Mural Maniac"
@keys: "Mural Maniac", Mural Maniac, Guru-in-Chief
### STANDARD
"Mural Maniac" is the Graffiti Alliance's Guru-in-Chief and an open-world NPC at the Masters of the Night-Wind. Mural Maniac welcomes visitors, admits anyone interested in graffiti on the spot, and worries about money because membership grew faster than the yearly dues can support. Mural Maniac considers taking portrait commissions to raise funds. In the limited-time event Soulblazers' Snowbound Journey, Mural Maniac appears in Mondstadt to scout locations for graffiti, and credits Mika with saving them on Dragonspine. Pronouns and age are not stated in the sources checked.

## entry: "Dopey Dazzler"
@keys: "Dopey Dazzler", Dopey Dazzler
### STANDARD
"Dopey Dazzler" is a graffiti artist and Graffiti Alliance mainstay who hosts the Traces of Artistry event in Natlan (5.0). She invites the Traveler and Paimon to travel to find inspiration, and preaches avant-garde art. She says the nickname came from a friend who praised her colour combinations while others laughed that she gets dopey blending paint, and that her real name is less important than her title.
### LARGE +
Her notebook, left for a future graffiti genius, says graffiti is the simplest emotions made visible and that Natlan has no strict teachers. She gave the Traveler a Graffiti Kamera for collecting source material during the event. This material comes from a limited-time event.

## entry: Yolo
@keys: Yolo
@keys2: Graffiti Alliance, graffiti, Natlan
### STANDARD
Yolo is a member of the Graffiti Alliance and the Collective of Plenty who paints at the Alliance's gallery. Yolo says every piece of graffiti has a soul, and that long ago a group of mystics could infuse paint with the power of the Wayob to protect Natlan from calamities. Today the "soul" comes from pigments made of different minerals, petals and saurian scales.

## entry: Traces of Artistry
@keys: Traces of Artistry, Avant-Garde Graffiti Visionary
@version: 5.0
@availability: limited
### LARGE
Traces of Artistry was the 5.0 flagship event (2024-08-28 to 2024-09-16) in which "Dopey Dazzler" invited the Traveler to tour Natlan in search of inspiration, with stops at the Children of Echoes, the Scions of the Canopy and the People of the Springs. It introduced the Graffiti Alliance in game. The event is limited-time canon.
