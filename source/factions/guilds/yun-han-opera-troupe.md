## summary
### LITE
The Yun-Han Opera Troupe is the best-known Liyue Opera company in Liyue Harbor. The house of Yun has run it for generations, and its current director is the opera singer and playwright Yun Jin. It performs regularly at Heyu Tea House.
### STANDARD +
Liyue Opera is a traditional art handed down over generations, and its performers usually work in troupes; canon calls this the most acclaimed of them. Beyond its star, it is a working organisation of veteran performers, a manager and backstage staff.

## structure
### STANDARD
A director sits at the top and makes the big decisions. Managers handle daily work. Yunning is the manager seen in canon, and Yinggong looks after wardrobe and props. Many veteran performers belong to the troupe, and some joined under Yun Jin's grandfather. No other ranks or headcount are given.
### LARGE +
Putting on a Liyue Opera needs many people on and off stage, as Yinggong puts it. His own work covers cannons, flags, costumes, accessories and other props. Some costumes and props are hand-me-downs from earlier years, and others were bought or made by him.

## leadership
### LITE
Yun Jin is the director, the troupe's lead performer and the author of its new plays. Her manager Yunning handles bookings and press.
### STANDARD +
Yun Jin's grandfather directed the troupe before her, and long-time fans called him "Sir Yun". After she took over, those fans came to her shows too and began to use the same title for her. Business circles call her "Director Yun" because she makes the big decisions. Veterans follow her lead in rehearsal, but offstage they treat her like a granddaughter and nag her about diet and tastes. Yunning is protective and anxious about Yun Jin's safety and public image.
### ULTRA +
Yun Jin has said that she used to think a good performance alone would hold the troupe together, but now she must watch her conduct offstage as well. In the Hangout Event she also mentions having read through Yuehai Pavilion documents about troupe contracts, which shows that she deals with the legal side herself.

## members
### STANDARD
- **Yun Jin**: director, singer, playwright and star. See her own entry.
- **Yunning**: manager. Panics when Yun Jin disappears to seek inspiration, screens interviews and had the Millelith pursue tabloid impersonators.
- **Yinggong**: wardrobe and props manager and stagehand. He also makes paper theatre plays for children as a hobby and appeared in the 3.4 Lantern Rite event.
- **Veteran elders**: unnamed senior performers, some from her grandfather's time, who stay devoted to traditional Liyue Opera.
- **Yun Jin's grandfather**: former director, unnamed.
### LARGE +
Canon also mentions backstage helpers who have time off at festival time, but names none of them.

## goals
### STANDARD
The troupe's work is to perform Liyue Opera well and keep the art alive. Under Yun Jin it also stages new plays beyond the classic repertoire. Yun Jin has privately wished to sing the stories of ordinary people's joys and sorrows rather than the adepti legends that dominate the genre. That is her own wish, and canon does not call it troupe policy.

## methods
### STANDARD
The troupe performs for paying audiences at Heyu Tea House, where Yun Jin appears from time to time. Fans gather there hoping to catch her and ask about her schedule, which keeps the tea house busy. According to Yun Jin, troupe rules forbid accepting private gifts from patrons. Interview requests go through the manager and not directly to the star.
### LARGE +
The repertoire includes old adeptal legends such as "The Divine Damsel of Devastation", written by Yun Jin's father, alongside the newer plays that canon credits to Yun Jin.

## history
### LITE
The Yun family were famous weaponsmiths about 500 years ago, and over time the family took up Liyue Opera. By the time of Yun Jin's grandfather the Yun-Han Opera Troupe existed.
### STANDARD +
Yun Jin's mother took up the mantle from her grandfather and was a famous opera singer in Liyue Harbor, and her father is a playwright. Yun Jin grew up around the troupe, and its veterans watched her grow into a rising star. She debuted young and later became director.
### LARGE +
In 3.4 (limited-time Lantern Rite event) Yinggong said that no special troupe performance was planned that year, because the troupe had already performed at many Lantern Rites in a row and could finally rest.
### ULTRA +
During the same event Yun Jin gave some backstage helpers time off, and Yinggong used the holiday to put on a paper play for local children.

## relations
### STANDARD
- **Heyu Tea House**: the troupe's regular venue. Its owner, Fan Er'ye, earns a great deal from Yun Jin's shows.
- **Xinyan**: a close friend of Yun Jin. The elders look down on rock 'n' roll, so Yun Jin keeps her visits secret from them.
- **Adventurers' Guild**: Yunning commissioned it in 2.4 to find Yun Jin.
- **The Millelith**: Yunning had them pursue tabloid impersonators.
### LARGE +
- **The Steambird**: a reputable publication. Two tabloid reporters falsely claimed to be from it, and the troupe now handles press through its manager.
- **Shenhe**: told Yun Jin a less heroic version of the divine damsel legend, which influenced how Yun Jin approaches the role.

## reputation
### STANDARD
The troupe is regarded as the most famous opera company in Liyue Harbor. Yun Jin's character story says nine out of ten Heyu Tea House patrons are her fans. The elders are known for being devoted to traditional Liyue Opera and rather rigid about other kinds of music.

## entry: Yunning
@keys: Yunning
@keys2: Yun-Han Opera Troupe, Yun Jin, manager
@version: 2.4
### STANDARD
Yunning is the manager of the Yun-Han Opera Troupe and appears in Yun Jin's Hangout Event. When Yun Jin vanished days before a big show, she commissioned the Adventurers' Guild and begged the Traveler to find her, afraid the audience would tear the place down and that Yun Jin might get hurt. She insists on handling interviews herself and had the Millelith pursue the fake Steambird reporters. In canon she is excitable, dramatic and fiercely protective of her star. Her full name, age and background are not given.

## entry: Yinggong
@keys: Yinggong
@keys2: Yun-Han Opera Troupe, paper theater, Lantern Rite
@version: 3.4
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
During the 3.4 Lantern Rite event "The Exquisite Night Chimes", Yinggong ran a paper theatre for children in Liyue Harbor, and the Traveler helped perform "Homeward, Bravebeard". He explained that he works for the Yun-Han Opera Troupe on wardrobe and props, that he can carry a tune after years with the troupe, and that backstage helpers had the festival off. He stayed in the city himself because his parents had died two years earlier. He says paper plays are simpler than Liyue Opera because one person can do everything.

## entry: Yun Jin's Hangout Event, Act I
@keys: A Song That Knows Grace, Countryside Wandering, After the Curtain Falls, Backstage Suspicions
@keys2: Yun Jin, Yunning, Traveler
@version: 2.4
### LARGE
In Act I of Yun Jin's Hangout Event, Yunning asks the Traveler to find Yun Jin, who has gone to Lingju Pass to feel real danger for her acting. The Traveler brings her back. After a show, admirers invite Yun Jin to dinner and she uses the Traveler as an excuse. At Heyu Tea House two journalists claim to represent The Steambird but are really from a small tabloid, and one is bound by a strict contract to her editor. Yunning later reports the tabloid to the Millelith and tells the troupe to be careful. The act has several possible endings.

## entry: The Yun family and the troupe
@keys: Yun family, house of Yun
@keys2: Yun-Han Opera Troupe, Yun Jin, opera
@version: 2.4
### LARGE
The Yun family were prominent Liyue weaponsmiths about 500 years ago and worked with the Han family on the Prototype series of weapons after the cataclysm. Over the centuries a family member tired of forging became interested in the performing arts, and by Yun Jin's generation few Yuns still forge. Yun Jin carries a lock-shaped copper pendant that was forged and handed down. The wiki notes that the "Han" in the troupe's name uses a different character from the Han weaponsmith family and is unrelated to it.
