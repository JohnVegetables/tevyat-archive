## summary
### LITE
The Spina di Rosula is a Fontainian organisation that helps ordinary people and takes on the problems the Gardes cannot or will not handle. Callas founded it, and his daughter Navia is now its President and "Boss". Its members work from Fleuve Cendre, the undercity beneath the Court of Fontaine, and from Poisson. Officially it describes itself as law-abiding and citizen-focused, and it cooperates with the authorities where needed. Navia's own description is "a business partner" that helps with any problem, using the velvet glove or the iron fist as the case demands.
### STANDARD +
The Spina also runs a quieter, harder side. It has enforcers for the dirty work, and it keeps order in Fleuve Cendre better than the Court of Fontaine ever tried to. Core businesses sustain the Spina, while much of Navia's problem-solving brings little profit, and its help can come with conditions. Navia's profile notes that people who adopt her own description of the Spina will find it easier to make friends with her.

## structure
### STANDARD
The Spina has a single President. Navia lists her titles as president, leader, commander-in-chief, executive director and Boss, and members simply say "Boss". Below her are advisors and specialists rather than a rigid rank ladder. Florent is Senior Advisor and Yvette is Technical Advisor. Areta is the organisation's physician, Archenoul a diver on its payroll, and Tetreaux a bartender in Fleuve Cendre. Faustier, Rege and others do manual and construction labour, Hinterman is a fisherman, and Sonny and Luca are the enforcers.
### LARGE +
The Spina deliberately recruits from people the surface world has given up on. Faustier was invited to join after serving time in the Fortress of Meropide for petty theft. Areta, who grew up poor, had her medical studies at the Akademiya paid for by the Spina on the condition that she come back and work as a salaried doctor in Fleuve Cendre, and she says she thought it a fair deal. Yvette quit the Fontaine Research Institute because she could not work with people at her short temper, and she now says it does not matter whether a Spina member is eight or eighty, so long as they have ability. A black bowler hat is commonly worn by members. In-game text says the hat sits between a luxury good and an accessory, and that since Callas led, an unwritten rule has held that a measured display of wealth can itself be a warning to rivals. The emblem of the Spina, which Navia says is shaped like a heart, was designed by her mother Clementine.
### ULTRA +
The house name is the House of Caspar: the family of Callas, Clementine and Navia, which was trusted in Poisson for fairness and reliability and was renowned as the leader of the Spina. The Spina's mark is also tattooed on the front legs of Consigliere, the scarred Tent Tortoise that Callas counted as a confidant, who is now Navia's quiet counsellor. A former attendant of Consigliere is also a Spina member and appears as an animal caretaker at Navia's manor during the Whirling Waltz event (5.6, limited). Chiori handles the organisation's formal wear, but according to the wiki she did not take the contract for its uniforms because she found it too boring.

## leadership
### LITE
Navia is the second President. She took the post from her father Callas, who founded the Spina, after his death. She is cheerful, tireless and theatrical, and her own people treat her as the one to bring problems to.
### STANDARD +
Navia says plainly that she will not become the same sort of person as her father, though some veterans think she is becoming more like him. She says the Spina has changed a lot since Callas's day and she intends to be "the president for the Spina of today". Unlike Callas, she says she is willing to hear opinions. In the Romeuf case she handed her former members over to the law instead of settling it privately.
### LARGE +
Callas ran the Spina as a stubborn, closed-off man. He was a respected figure in Fontainian society, so well regarded that Neuvillette and Clorinde both spoke highly of him, yet he refused the honorary title the Palais Mermonia sought to grant him after the aquabus lines were completed (the Callas wiki page calls it the title of Baron). Navia's guess, which she never confirmed with him, is that he feared a title would set the Spina above the common folk it worked with. He taught her business, negotiation and weapons, took her to the negotiating table, and appeared to be grooming her as his successor, but he never spoke of his own feelings or of how her mother died.
### ULTRA +
After Callas's death Navia had two attendants who tailed her as bodyguards, Melus and Silver. After they were lost in the flood, Florent took over some of Melus's duties, and Navia had to learn to organise her own memos.

## members
### STANDARD
Current members named in canon include Florent (Senior Advisor), Yvette (Technical Advisor), Areta (physician), Archenoul (diver), Tetreaux (bartender), Faustier and Rege (manual and construction labour), Hinterman (fisherman) and Sonny and Luca (enforcers). Consigliere is the organisation's tortoise confidant, and Bizerte leads its Simulanka branch. Former members include the founder Callas and his wife Clementine, who are dead; Melus and Silver, who were dissolved in the flood; Colter, who was dismissed and detained; Romeuf, who left and is imprisoned; and Virgil, who was killed.
### LARGE +
Sonny, called "White Glove", and Luca, called "The Fisherman", are the enforcers. Navia herself says that someone who is not in the mood to sit and talk will be handled by "White Glove". They are named after characters from a well-known crime film. The two meet the Traveler after Virgil, a rogue member who led the Traveler into a trap while hunting for treasure, is dealt with. Sonny's and Luca's later fish gift to the Traveler is a pointed reference to that scene.
### ULTRA +
Virgil is a lesson in who the Spina counts as its own. He was selling Sinthe, a drug made from Primordial Seawater, which Callas had banned. Sonny and Luca were hunting for him to settle a score, and they cornered him at a hidden base beneath the Elton Trench, where Sonny called him worthless dross and said he did not need to worry about whoever had trained him. The Traveler can later find Sonny and Luca beside Consigliere in Fleuve Cendre. If the Traveler asks about Virgil, Sonny smoothly changes the subject to fishing and gives the Traveler four fish, strongly implying that Virgil is dead.

## goals
### STANDARD
The Spina exists to help the people of Fontaine, above all the poor and the forgotten, and to investigate matters that the Gardes would not or cannot. Its public statement is that it is a citizen-oriented society involving many walks of life, focused on hard problems among the common folk, and cooperative with the Fontainian authorities. Navia puts it more briefly: the work is "a little bit of everything", but the focus is always on solving other people's problems. Under Navia, the Spina also aims to rebuild Poisson, keep Fleuve Cendre safe and keep faith with the people who died for it.
### LARGE +
Navia sees two kinds of trouble, the solvable and the unsolvable, and the Spina is aimed at the first kind. She takes on small, unprofitable cases such as separated lovers or a middle-aged breadwinner left unable to work by an accident, because she believes in "a stitch in time saving nine" and that dealing with them early saves someone from being hauled up onstage at the opera house.

## methods
### STANDARD
The Spina prefers to settle matters peacefully, through mediation, negotiation and favours, but it does not rule out force. In Fleuve Cendre it mediates conflicts, manages relations with the Court of Fontaine and keeps the peace. Residents say that under the Spina it is much harder to find illegal funding and that anyone who starts an armed fight may be "sent away to start a new life in the ocean". It also supports its people with supplies, household help, jobs and sponsored study. Tetreaux says that, in theory, nothing exists that can only be solved by force, and that the Spina will resolve a problem with minimal harm.
### LARGE +
Its aid is not pure charity. Areta's sponsorship came with a return-to-work condition, and Navia's profile contrasts her unprofitable problem-solving with the core businesses that sustain the Spina. For large jobs, such as building the aquabus or running the Chevalmarin Film Fantasyland, it works with the Court of Fontaine, the Fontaine Research Institute and private partners. Navia uses a network of informants and agents, and she sometimes deploys Spina people as escorts, as she did for the courier Kirara when Kirara was harassed.
### ULTRA +
Navia's voice-over jokes that as President she has "ways of making people keep their mouths shut". She also warns that the Spina is only one of many groups in Fleuve Cendre, and that the others have their own agendas. In the Nasha Town trade expo (6.5, limited), Navia and Clorinde, with local agents Clorinde had won over, helped the Spina apprehend agents of an international smuggling syndicate, while Navia also sold Fontaine Research Institute devices at her stall.

## history
### STANDARD
Callas founded the Spina and led it as first President. A dispute over funding the aquabus split the organisation, and after Callas died in a duel three years before the Fontaine Archon Quest, his daughter Navia took over.
### LARGE +
**Founding and the aquabus dispute.** Callas founded the Spina and was its first President. His wife Clementine was its mediator in disputes between members. Callas decided to sponsor the aquabus system in partnership with the Court of Fontaine and the Fontaine Research Institute, hoping it would improve the relationship between the rest of Fontaine and Poisson and pay off in the long run. Romeuf, a senior member, thought the money should go into Poisson itself. When Clementine sided with Callas, Romeuf's faction decided that her pregnancy had made her an unfair mediator and left, with a large share of the membership, on extremely bad terms. They continued to despise the family, including Navia. After Clementine died, Callas went on with the construction to honour her wish, and the lines are named after Callas, Clementine and Navia.

**Callas's death.** Three years before the Fontaine Archon Quest, Callas banned Sinthe, the popular drink that Marcel secretly made from Primordial Seawater, and turned his informant Jacques against the producers. Jacques was killed and Callas was blamed. Rather than stand trial, Callas chose a duel against the Champion Duelist Clorinde and refused to yield. His benefactors withdrew and the public called him "Callas the Unfaithful". Navia, with Melus and Silver as bodyguards, took over as President, and the strain between her and Clorinde lasted for years.
### ULTRA +
**The Fontaine Archon Quests (4.0-4.2).** Navia met the Traveler while trying to clear Lyney and Lynette. She cleared her father's name and exposed Marcel and his Confrerie of Cabriere. The Spina's Fleuve Cendre base became the Traveler's refuge between investigations. When a tremor sent Primordial Seawater into lower Poisson, the Spina treated the wounded, and Melus and Silver died rescuing people. Navia accepted the help of Arlecchino and the Fatui while keeping her guard up.

**Romeuf's plot (4.3, Rosa Multiflora Chapter).** Colter, who had served a sentence in the Fortress of Meropide, came home to learn Melus was dead and blamed Navia. Romeuf's faction, short of Mora, planned to destroy the Clementine Line and bury Poisson under the rubble. Colter tipped them off about Florent's security checks, then tried to kill Navia. The Traveler and Navia stopped most of the damage, and the Maison Gardiennage arrested Romeuf and his followers. Navia refused to settle the matter privately with the former Spina members. She dismissed Colter from the Spina but promised him he would be welcome back when his sentence ended. A small girl named Adele, whose father's name had been cleared, was given a Spina uniform as a promise that she could join when she was old enough.

**Later appearances.** During the Whirling Waltz event (5.6, limited), Navia taught the Traveler the business side of the Chevalmarin Film Fantasyland, which she had created with Lepine-Pauline. After the Nod-Krai Archon Quests, she took part in the Nasha Town expo (6.5, limited).

## relations
### LARGE
- **Gardes and the Court of Fontaine**: the Spina handles what the Gardes cannot, and it manages relations with the Court on behalf of Fleuve Cendre. The Court has never governed the undercity and left it to the Spina. The Maison Gardiennage arrests Spina rebels when the Spina calls on them.
- **Clorinde**: Navia's childhood friend, whom she could not forgive after Callas's death. They are now close, and Clorinde often accompanies her.
- **Neuvillette**: respected Callas and later rescued Navia from the Primordial Seawater.
- **The Fatui**: Arlecchino's group helped in the 4.x flood. The Spina accepted the aid but did not lower its guard.
- **Confrerie of Cabriere**: Marcel's organisation was the source of Sinthe and later of the plot against Navia. It no longer exists.
### ULTRA +
- **Charlotte, Chiori and Escoffier**: friends and sometime helpers. Charlotte's photo of Navia hangs in the Steambird's exhibition hall, and her photo of Navia talking to Consigliere drew many visitors to the tortoise. Escoffier helped with the Chevalmarin Film Fantasyland and the Hotel Debord matter.
- **Wriothesley**: knew Callas before his death, according to the Hoyolab article cited on Callas's wiki page, but this archive has not traced that to a quest.
- **Simulanka**: Navia, as king of Constellation Metropole during the Summertide Scales and Tales event (4.8, limited), founded a Spina di Rosula di Simulanka and made the toy man Bizerte its head.

## reputation
### ULTRA
Inside Fleuve Cendre the Spina is both relied on and feared: residents say it watches everyone "like hawks", and it supplies help with living supplies and household affairs. On the surface it is seen as an organisation that does good works, helped by the family's reputation for fairness and reliability in Poisson. The fall of Callas damaged that reputation, as his benefactors turned away and the public called him "Callas the Unfaithful". Navia has worked since to be the kind of President her father was not, and the Spina's name has recovered enough that Poisson's villagers have drawn up a statue design showing her beside her parents.

## secrets
### ULTRA
Established but not widely known: Callas chose to die in the duel so that Marcel's group would not target Navia, since his associates would otherwise share crucial intelligence with the authorities. Navia learned this only during the Fontaine Archon Quests.

@keys: Spina di Rosula base, Spina base
@keys2: Fleuve Cendre, Fontaine, Spina
### STANDARD
The Spina's seat is in Fleuve Cendre, the settlement in the sewers beneath the Court of Fontaine. For centuries it was a gray legal area where the Court had no jurisdiction, once ruled by gangs and then by a protection racket under the outlaw Eduardo Baker. Today the Spina is its de facto governing body, and many members also live there. Residents include ex-convicts, people who have served time in the Fortress of Meropide, the poor and orphans. The air is damp, there is little sunlight and children are often left to themselves, but the Spina supplies food, medicine and help with household affairs. Tetreaux tends the bar. During the Fontaine Archon Quests the Traveler stays at the base between cases.

## entry: Aquabus dispute and the split
@keys: aquabus dispute, Romeuf's faction, Clementine Line, Callas Line
@keys2: Spina di Rosula, Callas, Navia, Poisson
### LARGE
The aquabus system was proposed by Callas as a joint venture with the Court of Fontaine and the Fontaine Research Institute. Romeuf, who thought the money should go into Poisson, left with a large number of Spina members when Clementine backed her husband. Clementine later died after Navia's birth, and Callas went on with the project. The three lines carry the names of Callas, Clementine and Navia. The Callas Line was later destroyed in an explosion at the Fontaine Research Institute, and after Romeuf's plot Navia decided to start rebuilding it.

## entry: Romeuf and the plot against Poisson
@keys: Romeuf, Martinat, Colter
@keys2: Spina di Rosula, Poisson, Navia
@version: 4.3
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
Romeuf was a Spina member who left over the aquabus funds and whose followers saw Navia as no different from her father. Short on Mora, he plotted to collapse the Clementine Line and let the rubble destroy Poisson. He recruited Colter, a former Spina member who had just finished a sentence in the Fortress of Meropide and who blamed Navia for the death of his old colleague Melus. Colter failed to kill her at first because the Traveler was with her, and he then warned Romeuf that Florent was going to check on the faction. The Traveler and Navia stopped most of the damage. The Maison Gardiennage arrested Romeuf and his followers, and Navia refused to treat them gently because they had endangered innocents. She dismissed Colter but promised to take him back when he is free.

## entry: Sonny and Luca
@keys: Sonny, White Glove
@keys2: Spina di Rosula, Fleuve Cendre, Navia, Luca
### STANDARD
Sonny, called "White Glove", and Luca, called "The Fisherman", are the Spina's enforcers. Sonny does the talking in a courtly, theatrical manner and Luca speaks very little. Navia says anyone who is not in the mood to talk will be dealt with by White Glove. They help the Traveler during the Aqueous Tidemarks and A Fontainian Message quests, and they hunt down Virgil, a rogue member who dealt in Sinthe. They are later found beside Consigliere in Fleuve Cendre.

## entry: Melus and Silver
@keys: Melus, Silver
@keys2: Spina di Rosula, Navia, Poisson
@version: 4.2
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Melus, the elder, and Silver, the younger, were Navia's two attendants and bodyguards after Callas's death. Melus managed her supplies, memos and daily logistics. When Primordial Seawater flooded Poisson they were dissolved while trying to save others. Their consciousnesses were forged into Oceanids, which helped shield Navia from the other victims who wanted to absorb her, long enough for Neuvillette to rescue her. They are the reason Colter blamed Navia.

## entry: Simulanka branch
@keys: Spina di Rosula di Simulanka, Bizerte
@keys2: Navia, Constellation Metropole, Simulanka
@version: 4.8
@availability: limited
### ULTRA
During the limited-time event Summertide Scales and Tales (4.8), Navia was crowned king of Constellation Metropole in the toy world of Simulanka. She proposed founding a formal organisation, "the Spina di Rosula di Simulanka", and appointed the toy man Bizerte as its head, with the job of working with the guards and looking after the Metropole's people while she was away fighting the dragon. This branch exists only inside the event.

## entry: Spina di Rosula uniform and hat
@keys: Spina di Rosula hat, Spina uniform, Spina di Rosula Uniform Gift Box
@keys2: Spina di Rosula, Navia, Fontaine
### LARGE
Members wear a black bowler hat. In-game text says it sits between a luxury and an accessory, and that since Callas's time a careful display of wealth has acted as a quiet warning to rivals (item text from the Sunny Summer Fontinalia event, 6.7). During Lost and Inherited, Navia collected a Spina uniform from Ludivine's shop as a promise for Adele, a child who admired the organisation after her father's name was cleared. It was too large for her for now.
