## summary
### LITE
The Arataki Gang is a small band of friends in Hanamizaka, a district of Inazuma City, led by the oni Arataki Itto, who calls himself its "first and greatest head". Everyone in it means well, but its flyers, events, late-night music and accidents keep landing members in front of the Tenryou Commission. The Commission refuses to treat it as an official organisation. Deputy Kuki Shinobu gets the others out of trouble and handles the paperwork.
### STANDARD +
Itto insists that the gang is for outliers and those shunned by society. Granny Oni, who raised him, thinks it is only Itto and his friends spending time together. Both views fit what canon shows. The gang's odd jobs include logistics and transportation, home repair and performance, and Shinobu has added tax, legal consultation and event management.

## structure
### LARGE
There is no fixed charter, venue or official standing. Itto is the boss, and members call him "boss" while he calls them "my boys". Kuki Shinobu is Deputy Officer, who runs the gang's legal and professional side and enforces discipline. Itto's Character Story 2 says there are fewer than ten regular members. The gang's emblem on its hats is a stylised version of Ushi's face.
### ULTRA +
The gang never had a fixed place to hold activities, which creates logistical problems whenever it plans something. When it decides things, it sometimes draws lots. Its end-of-year plan in "The Great Singalong" was chosen that way, after roasted Lavender Melons and an Onikabuto festival were rejected.

## leadership
### LITE
Arataki Itto leads. He is brash and loud but honest and fiercely loyal, and he treats members with the respect of family.
### STANDARD +
Per the wiki, apart from Shinobu the members are not very intelligent, so she is the gang's practical brain. Itto admits that nobody questions his leadership except Shinobu, who challenges everything he says, returns loot he brings back to share, and gets him out of jail. He lets it slide, and says an angry Shinobu would make his life a nightmare. Their relationship is the core of how the gang runs day to day.

## members
### LITE
Besides Itto and Shinobu, the named members are Akira, Genta, Mamoru and Ushi, a young Akaushi bull.
### STANDARD +
- **Kuki Shinobu**: Deputy Officer. A masked, ninja-trained former Grand Narukami Shrine maiden who studied law in Liyue and holds many certifications. She joined and brought the others to heel within days.
- **Akira, Genta, Mamoru**: the gang's rank and file. Itto says Akira snores loudly and Mamoru is colourblind. Akira's account of the gang's founding is in the uncertainties.
- **Ushi**: a young bull the gang counts as an auxiliary member. He cannot communicate with the others but has his own principles, refusing anything unreasonable or law-breaking, and he sometimes helps Shinobu. When she advised him to find his own path, he insisted on staying with Itto. He assists Itto in combat through Itto's Elemental Skill.
- **Grandmaster Hanakado**: a boy obsessed with Onikabuto beetle battles, who joined after losing to Paimon and the Traveler in the 3.4 beetle event.
- **Traveler**: named a member by Itto, who says the gang will always answer their call.
### LARGE +
Granny Oni is not a member. She is a human woman who took Itto in as a boy and is called "Granny Oni" because she looks after him. The wiki stresses that every member has a good heart, and that their accidents come from trying to promote the gang and help their community.

## goals
### LARGE
The gang wants to be taken seriously, to have fun and to help its community in its own loud way. It also promotes Itto's causes: Onikabuto beetle battles, respect for oni, and parties and festivals. Itto says the gang welcomes people who have experienced adversity, and it should not be called freaks or weirdos.
### ULTRA +
Its recruitment drive is part of the aim of events like the Drumalong Festival, whose first stated goal was to promote the gang and recruit members. Another is to counter anti-oni prejudice: when Itto had a photo of his softer side posted on the Inazuma City message board as an attempt to end it, it was graffitied over and the gang was blamed for disrupting the peace.

## methods
### LARGE
The gang uses showmanship, street games and simple force of personality instead of violence. Itto settles snack disputes with children in fair "ritual combat" and can lose to a child without any reservations. The gang posts flyers, hosts events, plays music, and stages performances. These often fail because of permits and licences. Shinobu's role is to find the legal route, and to clean up after the gang when it cannot.
### ULTRA +
The gang also does odd jobs. Out of respect for Itto's severe allergy to beans, none of the members orders a dish containing beans, even in private. Its members hold to a code of loyalty to each other, and the profile stresses that Itto never uses his strength to terrorise people or to steal what he has not earned.

## history
### STANDARD
**Origins.** After Itto's parents died of illness, the young Itto was driven from a village where his oni family had been suspected of theft. An elderly human woman saved him from a beating and fed him, and he eventually settled in Hanamizaka. The gang grew out of his friendships there, but members give conflicting accounts of when it began (see uncertainties). Shinobu joined after leaving the Grand Narukami Shrine, and her arrival gave the gang its first deputy, legal advice and discipline.
### LARGE +
**Taurus Iracundus Chapter (2.3).** In Itto's story quest "Rise Up, Golden Soul", the Tenryou Commission issued a warrant for him over thefts and kidnapping. Itto took the blame for the crimes alone but privately believed a coerced blue oni was responsible. That oni, Takuya, was working for ronin who held hostages in a hideout. The gang arrived to distract Commission soldiers while Itto helped. After the hideout was destroyed, both oni were arrested, along with Itto's crew for obstruction. Granny Oni then took the blue oni tribe under her care.

**Drumalong Festival (2.7).** The gang tried to hold a huge festival at Ritou but had no permit, venue booking or registration. The Tenryou Commission shut it down with only a warning, because the gang had at least prepared a safety plan, and sent it to the Yashiro Commission's festival venue on Amakane Island, where it was given a small beach. The festival's stated aims were to promote the gang and recruit, and to celebrate Shinobu's graduation. This was during a limited-time event.
### ULTRA +
**Beetle events (3.4-5.3).** In the 3.4 event the gang challenged all comers to Onikabuto battles, and Hanakado joined after being beaten. In the 4.3 event, Itto and Shinobu took Hanakado to Liyue to spread beetle battling, with help from Yanfei, and left him there to learn from Guoba and the adepti. Hanakado reappears in the 5.3 event "Shuyu's Baffling Beetle Battle Bowl". All three are limited-time events.

**Iridescent Arataki Rockin' for Life Tour de Force of Awesomeness (4.6).** Itto, enthused by a music festival in Fontaine, hosted a bigger one on Watatsumi Island with Shinobu, and invited the Traveler by letter. A limited-time flagship event.

**Latest.** Itto's 2026-06-01 birthday mail says Takuya will soon leave prison and describes the gang's plan to greet him: Genta pats him down, Akira hands him flowers, Mamoru whistles. Shinobu and Ushi refuse to join in. This is the newest canon found. No gang story content was found for 6.x-7.1.

## relations
### STANDARD
- **Tenryou Commission**: acts as the gang's opponent. It arrests members regularly and refuses to recognise the gang. Its officials once logged Itto as "unemployed", although he does odd jobs. Shinobu's friend Kujou Sara, a Tenryou commander, once took Itto's Vision under the Vision Hunt Decree, and Itto still wants a sumo rematch.
- **Yashiro and Kanjou Commissions**: the Yashiro Commission is the gang's route to festival venues, and in Itto's account it complained about the photo on the message board, while the Kanjou Commission demanded advertising tax over it.
- **Watatsumi Army**: Itto has a contact (Masato) at its Yashiori Island camp.
### LARGE +
- **Children of Hanamizaka**: Itto is liked by them and parents use his name to scare them into behaving. He plays street games, stands up against bullies and battles them with Onikabuto.
- **Oni**: Itto's pride in his oni blood shapes the gang's aims. Itto's story quest revealed the blue oni tribe, whose sacrifice for oni acceptance he honours.

## reputation
### LARGE
The gang's reputation is mixed. Officials see a nuisance that needs oversight. Hanamizaka's children like Itto. Rumours spread when Shinobu joined that the gang now had a polite, reasonable second-in-command, though some worried that giving it a "brain" might lead to worse incidents. In Itto's story the Commission's investigators marked him down as "unemployed" without hearing him out.

## entry: Kuki Shinobu in the gang

@keys2: Arataki Gang, Itto
### STANDARD
Shinobu introduces herself as the gang's deputy who handles its affairs, and tells people to contact her about Itto's inappropriate behaviour. She fled the shrine's duties and received a Vision, and later wore her mask after a child recognised her as the shrine maiden who never smiled. In the gang she mediates, litigates, cooks, repairs lacquerware and keeps the others in line. Her outfit is called Arataki's Demonic Deputy. In the hangout "The Gang's Daily Deeds", the Traveler acts as a "Chief Advisor" while helping her with bylaws and trouble the gang causes.

## entry: Ushi
@keys: Ushi, Akaushi
@keys2: Arataki Gang, Itto
### STANDARD
Ushi is a young brown Akaushi bull with golden horns, a crystal-shard-shaped tail tip and a small roof on his back decorated with oni faces. Itto found him before the Traveler arrived and he joined as an auxiliary member. He has a principled streak but cannot communicate with the gang. The wiki says his design is inspired by the akabeko, a traditional toy.

## entry: Drumalong Festival

@version: 2.7

### LARGE
During the limited-time event in 2.7 the gang tried to hold "The Almighty Arataki Great and Glorious Drumalong Festival" at Ritou. A Tenryou doushin said the gang had earlier made a scene at the Irodori Festival and now lacked any permit, venue booking or registration, and shut the event down with a warning. The gang moved to a small beach on Amakane Island. Itto invited the Traveler, who played a small drum he provided. In a later letter Itto jokes that the Tenryou Commission wrongly arrested him.

### LARGE
Itto and the gang promote Onikabuto battles: beetle fights between the horned beetles of Inazuma. Grandmaster Hanakado, a boy who is passionate about the sport, joined the gang after losing to the Traveler's team in the 3.4 event. In the 4.3 event the group travelled to Liyue and got a permit with Yanfei's help. Hanakado stayed behind to study. Shinobu left the Traveler a pack of instant ramen she devised in Liyue.
