## summary
@version: 6.2
@availability: limited
### LITE
The Legion of Soulblazers is a loose group of friends and pen pals led by Varesa of Natlan. Members come from several nations and began as a comic-book fan club. They are devoted to The Soulblazer Saga, a comic series about Natlan's fight against the Abyss, and treat its hero Fietena as a role model. Varesa says the group's real aim is to share life's joys and get rid of life's woes. It appears only in the limited-time event Soulblazers' Snowbound Journey (6.2).
### STANDARD +
Before that event the members had never met in person. They kept in touch by writing letters and trading illustrations. The Traveler and Paimon join the Legion during the event and take the group's oath. The Legion has no known base, charter or official standing in any nation; it is a club of friends.

## structure
### STANDARD
There is no formal hierarchy beyond a captain. Varesa is the captain. Xilonen is called vice captain during the event, a title she says she ended up with without quite agreeing to it. She is standing in for her pen-pal-turned-student Nepecha and says she is not a member of the fan club herself. Alrani calls Varesa "Captain" and often speaks for the group, and Tang Yigang jokes that her camp duty, logistics, is the easiest job. New members are sworn in with a short ceremony that uses a mask of Fietena as the relic and a shouted oath.
### LARGE +
The group works as a pen-pal network first and an in-person team second. Members keep one another informed by letter and swap drawings and props. The one meeting shown on screen is the Dragonspine event, which was arranged around Minnie's suggestion. In that meeting the Legion enters a four-person relay, so it also functions as a sports team with a captain, a vice captain and runners.

## leadership
### STANDARD
Varesa is the captain: a cheerful, huge-appetited competitor from the Collective of Plenty who trains with her coach Iansan. She leads by enthusiasm, sets the training plans for the race, and takes personal responsibility for her teammates, saying she feels powerless as their captain when Minnie's parents press her to quit. Xilonen, the vice captain, is the practical one. She keeps the group's training sensible, notices when a teammate is hiding something, and steps in when words are needed.
### LARGE +
Varesa's leadership style is built on her hero Fietena: persist, stay cheerful, never give up on a dream. Xilonen uses a quieter style of care. She asks direct questions and offers rest before pushing harder. The two split the work in the event. Varesa is the motivator, and Xilonen mediates with outsiders such as Minnie's parents.

## members
### STANDARD
- **Varesa**: captain; from Natlan's Collective of Plenty.
- **Alrani**: a student at the Sumeru Akademiya who is still writing her thesis. She is the group's most formal speaker and runs the oath.
- **Tang Yigang**: from Liyue. Dramatic, easily excited about the Saga, and prone to catching colds on the cold mountain.
- **Minnie**: from Mondstadt. A young artist and prop maker who suggested the Dragonspine meetup. Her parents worry about her health and her plans.
- **Nepecha**: a girl in Natlan's Children of Echoes tribe, not present at the event because she is unfit for extreme sports. Xilonen represents her.
- **Traveler and Paimon**: inducted during the event.
### LARGE +
Members are tied together by letters. Varesa, who met Minnie only in writing, hugged and lifted her in greeting when they first met in person, which Xilonen teasingly said was an odd first impression. Xilonen is Nepecha's teacher and, in her words, something like family. Minnie's mask and artwork of Fietena travelled to Natlan through Nepecha and became well known in the Collective of Plenty.
### ULTRA +
Minnie says she wants to work in comics: perhaps as an assistant artist in Inazuma, or as a prop maker building real-world versions of comic items. She is the baby of her family and was sickly as a child. Her parents, Moritz and Sherilyn, and her much older brother Patton, who works at a Mondstadt tavern, appear in the event. Alrani is an aspiring herbad (still a driyosh) who treats the Saga as a source of courage whenever her thesis is rejected. Tang Yigang is also a collector of comic merchandise and complains about how costly the figures are.

## goals
### LITE
The Legion exists to share life's joys and get rid of life's woes, in Varesa's words. The members read and discuss comics together, eat together and support one another.
### STANDARD +
The comic fandom was the original bond. The tenets they recite come straight from the Saga: peace is not won by tears and strong words alone, and fighters should be ready to give everything even when the cause may fail. Their motto asks them to meet pain and defeat without fear and to keep championing love and imagination. In practice the Legion's goals are mostly personal. It backs Minnie's wish to work in comics and gets her to the finish line of the race.

## methods
### STANDARD
Members write letters, trade illustrations and share copies of the comics, handing spare volumes to newcomers. In person they discuss the series, eat large meals, rest and train together. They recruit by lending out comics and inviting people in, and they swear in members in a ceremony that uses a Fietena mask as a relic and a shouted oath.
### LARGE +
Quoting the series is a core habit. Alrani says she knows every line in the Saga across the whole Soulblazer universe, and Varesa brings Fietena lines into everyday talk (about meals, about courage). The group is also good at taking up a cause together: in the event they plan the race, rally behind Minnie, and give the Traveler a place on the relay at the last moment.

## history
@version: 6.2
@availability: limited
### LARGE
**Origins.** The Legion of Soulblazers began when a few comic readers from different nations started writing to one another. Canon does not name a founder or give a date. Its members stayed pen pals for some time and never met face to face.

**The Dragonspine meeting.** At Minnie's suggestion the members agreed to meet in Mondstadt and enter the Dragonspine International Cross Country Challenge, a contest organised jointly by the Knights of Favonius and representatives from Springvale. Varesa, Alrani, Tang Yigang and Xilonen came from abroad, and Minnie took part from her home city.

**Joining the Traveler.** In the event's first part, the Traveler and Paimon meet the group at its Natlanese camp, where Alrani and Tang Yigang bombard them with quotes. Xilonen explains that the Legion is basically a comic-book fan club. After reading the volume of the Saga they were lent, the Traveler and Paimon accept Varesa's invitation, wear a Fietena mask and swear the oath.

**Training and the deal.** During training, Minnie reveals she has made a deal with her parents: if her team gets ranked, they must take her wishes seriously. Her parents later confront her in person, and Xilonen settles the argument with a group painting made by Nepecha and her friends back home. Minnie's parents let her race but ask her to sleep at home at night.

**The race.** Tang Yigang falls ill on race day and the Traveler takes his place in a relay. The order is Xilonen, the Traveler, Varesa and Minnie. Minnie stops on the last leg to help an injured Inazuman contestant, so the team finishes late and misses a medal. The organising committee still recognises her effort, and her father agrees to let her travel abroad as long as she is careful.
### ULTRA +
After the quests, the Legion's reach spreads further. Yae Miko, hearing about the group from the Traveler, gives them first-edition Soulblazer Saga comics to share at the camp, and Yoimiya, who reads the Saga in Inazuma, gives them a character stand of Fietena. The family's plan to visit Yae Publishing House together is mentioned at the end of the race.

## relations
### LARGE
- **Knights of Favonius**: the Knights, through Amber and Field Officer Heinz, help organise the contest. The Legion visits the Knights' headquarters and library, where Lisa is friendly toward them.
- **Yae Publishing House**: publishes The Soulblazer Saga. Yae Miko rewards the fandom with free first-edition volumes.
- **Collective of Plenty and Children of Echoes**: Varesa comes from the first and Nepecha lives in the second. Xilonen, who fought for the Children of Echoes, travels with the Legion.
- **Iansan**: Varesa's coach, whose advice on food and pacing she repeats to her teammates.
### ULTRA +
- **Springvale and other nations' teams**: The cross-country event also includes teams from Sumeru, Inazuma, Liyue and Mondstadt and an adventurer team, all in friendly competition. The announcer is Draff.
- **Inazuma**: Yoimiya meets the Legion through their shared love of the Saga, and says it is very popular in Inazuma.

## reputation
### ULTRA
The Legion is not widely known. To outsiders it looks like a group of friends with a lot of private jokes: Alrani and Tang Yigang's opening speech baffles the Traveler and Paimon, and Xilonen tells newcomers to think of it as a comic fan club. The Saga itself is popular, though. Yoimiya says it is a hit in Inazuma, and Minnie's Fietena mask and artwork are already admired in the Collective of Plenty. Varesa says that if Minnie visits Natlan she would meet crowds asking for her autograph.

## status
@version: 6.2
### LITE
The Legion is last seen at the end of the 6.2 event, with Minnie free to travel and the group still in touch. It has not been mentioned again in anything checked through 7.1.
### STANDARD +
Nothing suggests the Legion has been dissolved. Varesa remains its captain and still appears as a playable character, but no later quest or event features the group.

## entry: The Soulblazer Saga
@keys: The Soulblazer Saga, Soulblazer Saga, Soulblazers comic
@version: 6.2
### STANDARD
The Soulblazer Saga is a long-running comic series, published by Yae Publishing House, that dramatises Natlan's struggle against the Abyss during the Cataclysm. Varesa says it runs to 12 volumes, with other comic series focused on the Pyro Archon, and that its characters are based on real people although it is mostly meant as entertainment, not history. The hero is Fietena, a rookie warrior who grows into a commander and survives a period of disillusionment, with friends such as Wanjiru and Mavuika at her side. In volume 2, as shown in the event, Fietena goes into her first battle with Mavuika and the others, with the Pyro Archon confronting the monsters' leader while the rest deal with the minions. Fietena wears five different masks in the story. Varesa's own combat mask is modelled on one of them, and a different one serves as the oath relic. The wider franchise has merchandise such as badges, notepaper and figures.
### LARGE +
Quotations from the Saga are the group's shared language. Volume 6, Shadow of Tenebrium, is the source of the Legion's two tenets. Volume 3 supplies a line about proving doubters wrong. In-world, the real Fietena was a Collective of Plenty warrior and renowned wrestler who married into the Flower-Feather Clan, a point covered under Fietena's own entry.

## entry: Legion oath and tenets
@keys: Soulblazer oath, Legion creed, Legion tenets, Soulblazer tenets
@version: 6.2
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
Members swear a short oath by shouting it aloud, which Varesa says is about believing in something enough to shout it for the world to hear. The wording used in the event vows never to fear pain and defeat and to always champion love and imagination. The two founding tenets are taken from the Saga: that tears and strong words alone will not broker peace, and that the Soulblazer will give their life fighting darkness even if the fight is in vain. The oath is taken while holding a mask of Fietena as a relic. Varesa picks the mask Fietena wore when she returned to the fight after her darkest period.

## entry: Dragonspine International Cross Country Challenge
@keys: Dragonspine International Cross Country Challenge, Dragonspine cross country, cross country challenge
@version: 6.2
@availability: limited
### LARGE
The Dragonspine International Cross Country Challenge is the contest at the heart of Soulblazers' Snowbound Journey. Teams from Natlan, Sumeru, Inazuma, Liyue and Mondstadt compete in a relay over Dragonspine's marked course in cold, snowy weather. The Knights of Favonius, represented by Amber and Heinz, handle registration and security, while a Springvale group referees and supports. The Legion enters a four-person team. Its runners are Xilonen, the Traveler, Varesa and Minnie, after Tang Yigang falls ill. Draff announces the teams. The Legion helps an injured Inazuman runner, loses time and misses a medal.

## entry: Minnie
@keys: Minnie
@keys2: Soulblazers, Legion, Soulblazer Saga, Dragonspine
@version: 6.2
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
Minnie is the Mondstadt member of the Legion. She is an artist and prop maker, Nepecha's long-time pen pal, and the one who proposed the Dragonspine meeting. She is a young member of the group and a local of Mondstadt, where her older brother Patton works at the tavern and her parents want her to choose a safer career. She hopes to work in the comic industry, and she made a deal with her parents that a ranked result in the race would make them take her seriously. She is thoughtful, anxious about letting teammates down, and keen to rest properly when Xilonen insists. During the race she stops to help an injured rival, which costs her team a medal. Her parents allow her to travel afterwards, subject to caution.

## entry: Nepecha and the Legion
@keys: Nepecha
@keys2: Soulblazers, Legion, Minnie, Xilonen
@version: 6.2
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
Nepecha is a member of the Legion who stays in Natlan. She is a girl from the Children of Echoes, and Xilonen is her teacher and something like family. She and Minnie are pen pals, and Xilonen says Nepecha praises Minnie as an artist and as a source of life advice. Nepecha wanted to join the Dragonspine event but is not fit for extreme sports, so she asked Xilonen to attend in her place. She had friends from the tribe contribute to a group painting for Minnie, which Xilonen delivers early to help persuade Minnie's parents.
