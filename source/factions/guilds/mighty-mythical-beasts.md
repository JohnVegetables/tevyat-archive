## summary
### LITE
The Mighty Mythical Beasts is a small Wushou dance troupe in Liyue. Gaming founded and leads it, and his aim is to make the beast dance a regular sight in Liyue Harbor. He promotes it by busking and by recommending it to the clients of the transport agency where he works as a guard. It has not yet had much professional success.
### STANDARD +
The Chinese name uses a Cantonese word meaning "amazing", which gives the troupe a cheerful, big-talking character.

## members
### STANDARD
Gaming is the founder, head and lead dancer. Dip Yeuk, a new guard at the Sword and Strongbox Secure Transport Agency, is the troupe's drummer. Gaming has taught him the basics and Dip Yeuk practises them daily. Gaming's suanni companion, Man Chai, is closely tied to the troupe's origin, but the wiki does not list it as a member.

## goals
### STANDARD
The troupe exists to make Wushou Dance a regular fixture in Liyue Harbor. Gaming's character story says the dream still has a long road ahead.

## methods
### STANDARD
Gaming performs in the street with drums and gongs, and announces the troupe when he is thanked for escort work. He recommends it to the transport agency's clients as a booking. The troupe trains by the same discipline as the guards: horse stance, stamina and core strength. Dip Yeuk repeats Gaming's saying that one second on stage takes ten years of training off stage.

## history
### STANDARD
Gaming loved Wushou Dance from childhood and left his family's tea business in Qiaoying Village to make a career of it in Yilong Wharf. His first attempt burned through his savings, and he took a job at the transport agency during a Lantern Rite. He later founded the Mighty Mythical Beasts formally, soon after meeting Man Chai.
### LARGE +
In the Lantern Rite of version 4.4 (the event Vibrant Harriers Aloft in Spring Breeze) the troupe was featured, and its performance at Liyue's Lantern Rite was very well received. This is limited-event material: most Liyue residents saw it only as a festival act.

## relations
### STANDARD
- **Sword and Strongbox Secure Transport Agency**: the troupe's members are the agency's guards. A bulletin-board exchange has someone ask whether Liyue's hired muscle also moonlights as dancers, and the agency replies that one need not be surprised to find its people performing.
- **Liyue Harbor audiences**: crowds cheer, but the troupe more often gets applause than paid bookings.

## reputation
### ULTRA
Gaming's official introduction says the troupe is mostly met with applause rather than paid work, and that he laughs it off, saying the audience's attention is recognition enough. A merchant's thanks plaque to the agency praises Gaming for chasing off bandits with the momentum of a suanni, linking his guard work and his troupe's beast imagery. Dip Yeuk invites visitors to come and watch the troupe perform.

## status
@version: 4.4
### STANDARD
As of the pages read, the troupe remains a two-named-member amateur outfit led by Gaming, with the Lantern Rite performance its high point. No later developments were found.

## entry: Dip Yeuk

### STANDARD
Dip Yeuk is a newer guard-for-hire at the Sword and Strongbox Secure Transport Agency in Yilong Wharf, and the drummer of the Mighty Mythical Beasts. By his own account his kung fu is not yet up to standard, so he drills horse stance for hours and tries to follow Gaming's advice on training.

## entry: Wushou Dance
@keys: Wushou Dance, Wushou, beast dance
@keys2: Gaming, Liyue Harbor, Mighty Mythical Beasts, Lantern Rite
### STANDARD
Wushou Dance is a Liyue performance art in which a dancer wears a beast mask and moves like a waking beast, shaking the head and blinking. Gaming's version is suanni-themed. The art needs precise movement and core strength, and the Sword and Strongbox Secure Transport Agency's bulletin board mentions that its guards sometimes take part in it.
