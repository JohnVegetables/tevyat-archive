## summary
### LITE
The Adventurers' Guild is Teyvat's adventurer-for-hire service. It gathers word-of-mouth information and requests from ordinary people and organisations and redistributes them as commissions for registered adventurers. Its motto is "Ad astra abyssosque" (to the stars and the abyss), though in practice the work runs from finding lost kittens to exploring domains. Every region has a branch or post with a receptionist named Katheryne; most known branches are led by a Branch Master. Its headquarters is in Snezhnograd, Snezhnaya.
### STANDARD +
Each adventurer carries an Adventurer Handbook that logs completed commissions and is used to pay out rewards. The Guild is not a government and does not give orders to nations; it sells a service and keeps submitters' identities confidential where it can. Its Snezhnayan roots are tied to the Fatui through Pantalone and Sandrone, which most adventurers never see.
### LARGE +
Roleplay note (this project's interpretation): the Guild is a mundane, bureaucratic background institution. Most scenes with it involve a notice board, a fee, a rank, a rumour and a polite, terse receptionist. Its lore significance is the contrast between the cheerful public face and the Fatui ownership revealed in later content.

## structure
### LITE
One branch (or reception post) per region; most known branches are run by a Branch Master (none is named for Inazuma or Fontaine). Katheryne receives adventurers at each. Known branch masters: Cyrus (Mondstadt), Lan (Liyue), Kamal (Sumeru), Mansa (Natlan), Sarin (Nod-Krai).
### STANDARD +
Branch Masters write the entrance exams for new adventurers, advise newcomers on where to go and where not to go, and keep local information useful. The Guild has two known departments. The Intelligence Department does not take commissions: investigators collect information in the field, and organizers sort it and give it to adventurers. The Recruitment Department finds new members. Organizers rank commissions by difficulty from S downward to help newcomers adjust. Companions of a registered adventurer are recognised as members too: Paimon is listed in the Traveler's Handbook as their guide.
### LARGE +
Branches also keep practical resources. A Branch Master may keep illustrated guidebooks, because many adventurers cannot read well, or wooden models of ruin mechanisms for demonstration. In Natlan the branch is staffed largely by tribe members and, unusually, handles logistics for blacksmithing commissions and saurian bounties. The wiki suggests this may reflect Natlan's decentralised society, but canon does not state a reason.
### ULTRA +
Branches report on the adventurers they handle to headquarters at an unknown interval; this is how Lan was promoted to Branch Master. Uniforms feature an eight-pointed star on front and back. Several notable members, including the Traveler, Fischl, Bennett, Linnea, Dr. Livingstone and Katheryne, do not wear the standard uniform.

## leadership
### STANDARD
Pantalone is the Guild's president (Pulcinella's artifact text calls him its "nominal president"), and Katheryne in Snezhnograd addresses him as branch master and reports the Guild's expenses to him. Dottore taunts the Traveler that adventurers claim "minuscule rewards" from Pantalone. Sandrone, a Harbinger, built Katheryne and holds a share of the profits, which Fatui artifact text says Pantalone ceded to her sincerely after Pulcinella's failed attempt to use her to claim a share. Branch masters answer upward to headquarters in Snezhnograd.
### LARGE +
Sarin, the Nod-Krai Branch Master, is described as Pulcinella's subordinate who covertly leaks information about Fatui movements to Nefer, implicitly on his superior's behalf. This comes from Sarin's coded special dialogue with Nefer and Jahoda (talk of "weather" standing for Fatui deployments, and Nefer sending a message to Pulcinella through him), not from a quest.

## members
### STANDARD
Branch masters: Cyrus (Mondstadt), Lan (Liyue), Kamal (Sumeru), Mansa (Natlan), Sarin (Nod-Krai). Other named members include Alfry and Fischl of the Intelligence Department, Bennett, Nadia, Linnea (advisor), Talalikhin (clerk in Snezhnaya) and the retired Lohen and Valerina. The Traveler is a registered adventurer.
### LARGE +
- **Mondstadt**: Alfry (organizer), Fischl (investigator), Bennett, Lynn (survival expert), Jack and Gerald (recruits), Dr. Livingstone, Ylgr and many others.
- **Liyue**: Zhiping (investigator), Tiantian (recruiter), Brother Qian (handyman), Wenze, Dandy and others.
- **Inazuma**: only ordinary adventurers are listed, such as Etsuko, Taisuke and Yuzu.
- **Sumeru**: adventurers such as Aberdeen and Philby.
- **Fontaine**: Nadia (with her strategist Urville), Ophelia, Persone and Ubu.
- **Natlan**: Mansa (a shaman of the Masters of the Night-Wind), Ackon, Liliuo.
- **Nod-Krai**: Linnea (advisor), Jahoda.
- **Snezhnaya**: Pantalone, Sandrone, Talalikhin, and adventurers Khitrov, Lesnova and Nadyushenka.
- **Retired**: Herman, Likhovidova, Lohen, Valerina (who runs the Furball Fortress and still wears the uniform) and Vacher.
- **Others**: Aloy and Roald are listed as adventurers outside any branch.

The Guild's own review of a stalled commission notes that the Traveler has had the highest commission completion rate of any adventurer since joining. Jahoda says the Guild was asked to revoke her membership. Lohen, now a Knight of Favonius, appears on the retired list.

## goals
### STANDARD
Publicly, to match people who need work done with adventurers willing to do it, and to keep adventurers safer by sharing knowledge about ruins and dangers. It is run as a business: rewards are paid in Mora and posting a commission carries a processing fee.
### ULTRA +
Fatui artifact text states that one purpose of the Guild in Snezhnaya is to nourish talent and intelligence for the Fatui's grand cause. No branch outside Snezhnaya is shown acting on that purpose.

## methods
### STANDARD
A client files a request and pays a fee; a commission may be designated so that only named adventurers are offered it. The submitter's identity is kept confidential unless the adventurer must know it to finish the job; if an adventurer deduces it from unrelated information, that is not the Guild's concern. Stalled commissions pile up at each branch and are reviewed periodically. If no capable adventurer can complete one, Katheryne talks to the client about cancelling it. Adventure Rank is a gameplay progression system, with rewards claimed from Katheryne.
### LARGE +
Katheryne also dispatches characters on expeditions, hands out daily commission bonuses and, when the matching features are unlocked, handles Valiant Chronicles and Intel Exchange. These are game mechanics and do not describe in-world procedures beyond the commission system.

## history
@version: 1.0
### STANDARD
The Guild's origins are unknown. It is present in Mondstadt and Liyue from the start of the story, and Lan's dialogue indicates its headquarters is in Snezhnaya. In Natlan, after the Night Warden Wars ended, some Natlanese adventurers were among the first to travel beyond their borders.
### LARGE +
Sandrone's creation of Katheryne is stated in the quest "Where the Moon Rises".
### ULTRA +
Nod-Krai (6.0) has a branch in Nasha Town led by Sarin, with Linnea as advisor. Fatui artifact text from 7.0 (the Scarlet Proof set) reveals Pantalone's presidency, Sandrone's share and the Guild's Snezhnayan purpose. No further changes to the Guild are recorded for 7.0-7.1, whose Archon Quests take place in Snezhnaya.

## relations
### STANDARD
- **Fatui**: tied to Pantalone and Sandrone, yet the Handbook lists Fatui as enemies and some commissions target them. The wiki reads this as a sign of outward independence.
- **Mondstadt**: the Mondstadt branch advertises for recruits on the message board in the Angel's Share.
- **Akademiya**: Kamal hopes the Akademiya will not interfere with adventurers' activities.
### LARGE +
- **Northern Intelligence Network**: Sarin passes Fatui information to Nefer.
- **Nod-Krai locals**: Linnea advises the Guild as a naturalist and is one of the last Snowland Fae and Augury Birds.

## reputation
### STANDARD
Seen as a reliable, if unglamorous, service for odd jobs. The Mondstadt branch struggles for recruits, with recruitment ads filling the Angel's Share message board, and Cyrus uses stories of danger and bravery to entice young people. The would-be adventurer Doolan says he refuses to join the Guild because of its boring clothes.

## secrets
@version: 7.0
@spoiler: major
### ULTRA
The Guild's Fatui connection is not widely known. Pantalone is president, Sandrone built the receptionists, and one stated Snezhnayan purpose is to feed the Fatui's talent and intelligence needs. Sarin's leak of Fatui movements is a quiet arrangement tied to Pulcinella.

## status
@version: 7.1
### LITE
Active everywhere. As of 7.1 it still operates posts in all eight regions, with Katheryne at each.
### STANDARD +
No canon event up to 7.1 shows the Guild changing hands, disbanding or openly taking sides in a conflict.

## entry: Katheryne
@keys: Katheryne
@keys2: Adventurers' Guild, receptionist, Sandrone
### STANDARD
The Guild's receptionist, found in every region. She is a bionic Snezhnayan puppet created by Sandrone. Multiple identical Katherynes exist. She assigns commissions, shares local intel, and pays out daily commission and Adventure Rank rewards. Her idle lines are system-style messages ("Error", "Rebooting"), and she greets Sandrone as "My Lady".
### LARGE +
Whether the Katherynes share memories or wills is unconfirmed. Dialogue in Requiem of the Echoing Depths implies the Liyue one is connected to the one met in Inazuma.

## entry: Adventurer Handbook
@keys: Adventurer Handbook, Adventurer's Handbook
### STANDARD
Each adventurer is issued an Adventurer Handbook that records completed commissions and is used to pay out rewards. It also records commission history, income statement, evaluations, statistics and average positive ratings, since clients can rate an adventurer. When a Handbook is full a new one is issued; each has a serial number recorded in the adventurer's file. The Handbook lists Fatui as enemies.

## entry: Branch Masters
@keys: Cyrus, Branch Master, Lan, Kamal, Mansa, Sarin
@keys2: Adventurers' Guild
### STANDARD
Cyrus leads Mondstadt, which is short on members. Lan, Liyue's Branch Master, is a former mercenary who joined the Guild for its information network while searching for her missing brother's sword. Kamal, a former mariner, leads in Sumeru City. Mansa is a shaman of the Masters of the Night-Wind and leads Natlan. Sarin leads Nasha Town's Nod-Krai branch.

## entry: Great Expeditionist Challenge
@keys: Great Expeditionist Challenge
@keys2: Adventurers' Guild, Katheryne, Snezhnaya
@version: 7.0
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
During the limited-time 7.0 event Great Expeditionist Challenge, the Traveler and Paimon visit the Adventurers' Guild Headquarters in Snezhnaya and take part in a tournament among adventurers. Details revealed there: Branch Masters can issue a part-time adventurer's certificate, so the Eremite mercenary Lemta can be paid for commissions; Urville is registered as Nadia's strategist; Ophelia manages Guild supplies in Fontaine; and the Pilgrimage veteran Liliuo joined the Guild after the Night Warden Wars.
