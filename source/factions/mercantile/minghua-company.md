## summary
### LITE
The Minghua Company, with its Minghua Bank, was the business of Gentry De'an in Liyue Harbor. A decade ago it was among the proudest firms on its street. Today the Bank is sold and the Company is languishing.
### STANDARD +
Gentry De'an blames Snezhnayan bankers, backed by the Fatui, for undercutting his businesses and forcing their way into Liyue's market. In daytime he can be found near the Adventurers' Guild, looking at his old bank.

## leadership
### LITE
Gentry De'an owned and managed both the Company and the Bank. No other officer is named.

## goals
### STANDARD
The Company has no stated goals now. De'an says that were he a young man he would fight to make a comeback, but there is little an old man can do.

## history
### STANDARD
A decade ago, everyone from Feiyun Slope to the Dockyard knew De'an's name. The Fatui's growing involvement in Liyue's economy, through the Northland Bank's foothold in the Harbor, cut into the Minghua Bank's trade. After his daughter Huachu's supposed death, he sold the bank to pay for her funeral, and a notice on the Feiyun Slope bulletin board advertises the former building.
### LARGE +
The death was staged. The "corpse" found in a Feiyun Slope well, which fouled the water supply, was another person's, dressed as Huachu, while she and the arithmetic teacher Jianqiu eloped. De'an believes his refusal to accept the match drove her to suicide. The pair are met later at Stone Gate. In the Lantern Rite Tales event (1.3, limited-time), Huachu was homesick and the pair stopped at the Emerald Maple Inn, where Jianqiu again claimed to work for the Minghua Bank. Mr. Zhu knew of both the death and the bank's closure, grew suspicious, and chose not to pry.
### ULTRA +
De'an's two nightly and daily moods differ: by day he complains about the Fatui and lost wealth near the Adventurers' Guild; by night, at Bubu Pharmacy, he talks of heartache and the medicines that cannot cure it. The couple left the inn the same evening without staying the night.

## relations
### STANDARD
- **Fatui and Northland Bank**: De'an treats them as the force that ruined him.
- **Huachu**: his second daughter, whom he calls "Chu".
- **Jianqiu**: the teacher Huachu loves, whom De'an would not accept for his daughter.

## reputation
### LARGE
The firm was once known across the Harbor. De'an's bitterness shows how far it has fallen: the street remembers it, but its business has dwindled.
