## summary
### LITE
Shenglu Hall governs Liyue's glazed ceramics industry. It is one of the Eight Trades, the commerce sectors that answer to the Liyue Qixing. Its members are said to descend from tribes that lived near the Chasm long before Rex Lapis arrived, and the trade depends on materials from the Chasm.
### STANDARD +
The Hall is one of only four of the Eight Trades named in canon. In English its name is a "Hall", which follows the Chinese word for a government office.
### LARGE +
The Hall appears mostly in the background: on a bulletin board at Feiyun Slope, in the speech of Chasm workers, and through a scholar met in Zhongli's story quest.

## structure
### STANDARD
Shenglu Hall is a trade body, not a military or religious order. Canon names an industry representative, Miss Ruoyu, who speaks for the glazed ceramics trade, and a research department, Human Culture and Civilization Research, headed by Wanyu. Ordinary members are ceramics merchants and craftspeople, who are told to follow the Qixing's guidance.
### LARGE +
The Hall's notices are posted publicly, such as on the Feiyun Slope board, so merchants in the trade can follow developments.

## leadership
### LITE
Miss Ruoyu is the Hall's industry representative.
### STANDARD +
She has been discussing the reopening of the Chasm mine with the Tianquan, Ningguang. The notice says she may go to inspect the situation herself because of the continued incidents there. Nothing more is known about her.

## members
### STANDARD
- Miss Ruoyu: industry representative.
- Wanyu: Head of Human Culture and Civilization Research. A scholar who was on her way to a conference when two Geovishap Hatchlings and three Pyro Slimes attacked her near Lingju Pass. Zhongli and the Traveler saved her. She had seen four miners and a child heading toward Nantianmen and said so.
- Muning: not an official of the Hall but a specialist of the Ministry of Civil Affairs posted at the Chasm. Canon says his family plays a significant role in the Hall and the porcelain trade.
### LARGE +
Muning's father paid off his son's heavy debts and then told him that he now owed his father. Muning accepted the second chance and now works seriously at the Chasm, though he sometimes finds it pointless. He also likes to spend leisure time at the Yanshang Teahouse and is said to gamble there.

## goals
### STANDARD
Keep the glazed ceramics trade supplied and calm. The Hall's main concern in canon is getting the Chasm mine open again, because its ceramics rely on resources from that area.

## methods
### LARGE
The Hall works through the Qixing. Its representative talks to the Tianquan, merchants are asked not to panic, and the trade follows official guidance. Its research department, headed by Wanyu, studies human culture and civilization, which shows an interest in Liyue's early peoples, though canon does not detail that work.

## history
### STANDARD
According to the Chasm safety technician Jinwu, tribes already lived near the Chasm before Rex Lapis arrived. They gathered Glazed Sand Crystal and traded finished porcelain. After he came they made blood pacts with his yaksha and became his allies, fighting Azhdaha and the monsters of the cataclysm. Most later moved to Liyue Harbor and formed Shenglu Hall, whose glazed ceramics were an art handed down through the generations.
### LARGE +
Jinwu is relaying a story ("they say"), and she jokes that the nitpicky members of the Hall had ancestors who fought beside Rex Lapis himself. She adds that the claim stirred up the Hall.
### ULTRA +
The Hall's notice records that the closure of the Chasm was a serious concern for the glazed ceramics trade, which relied on resources from there.

## relations
### STANDARD
Under the Liyue Qixing, with Ningguang as the Tianquan dealing with its representative. A sister Hall in the Eight Trades. The Ministry of Civil Affairs runs the Chasm and posted Muning there. Its trade need links it to the Chasm Exploration Team's work.
### LARGE +
Yinyuan Hall (salt) is another Hall, with an entirely different founding story.

## reputation
### STANDARD
Jinwu calls its members nitpickers. Beyond that, canon does not describe how the Hall is regarded.

## status
### STANDARD
As of 7.1 the Hall remains an active part of the Eight Trades. Nothing about it was found in 6.x (Nod-Krai) or 7.0-7.1 (Snezhnaya) content. Its most recent canon appearance is its notice about the Chasm mine.

## entry: Shenglu Hall Notice
@keys: Shenglu Hall, Miss Ruoyu, glazed ceramics, Shenglu Chamber
@version: 1.0
### STANDARD
The Feiyun Slope bulletin board carries a Shenglu Hall announcement. Industry representative Miss Ruoyu and the Tianquan have discussed restarting the Chasm mine and made progress. Ningguang, speaking for the Qixing, expressed deep concern about the closure's effect on the glazed ceramics trade and promised to organise the reopening quickly. Because of continuing incidents, Miss Ruoyu says she may investigate personally. Ceramics merchants are told not to panic and to follow the Qixing's guidance.

## entry: Wanyu
@keys: Wanyu
@keys2: Shenglu Hall, Liyue, Lingju Pass, scholar
@version: 1.5
### STANDARD
Wanyu is a scholar of Shenglu Hall and head of its Human Culture and Civilization Research. In the Historia Antiqua story quest she appears in the third part of No Mere Stone, attacked by monsters near Lingju Pass on her way to a conference. She is rescued, tells Zhongli and the Traveler that she saw four miners with a child, and leaves for her conference.
