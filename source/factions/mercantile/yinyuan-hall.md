## summary
### LITE
Yinyuan Hall governs Liyue's salt industry and is one of the Eight Trades under the Liyue Qixing. Its members descend from people who survived the fall of Sal Terrae, the last haven of the God of Salt, Havria. The Hall tells a story of her death that the truth contradicts.
### STANDARD +
The Hall's version blames the Geo Archon Morax, who they say killed Havria out of envy. Zhongli, Morax's current vessel, corrects this in the Sal Flore story quest.

## structure
### STANDARD
Yinyuan Hall is a trade body for salt merchants and producers. The only named member is Wanyan. The Hall passes down its history among its own people by word of mouth.
### LARGE +
Canon does not name a representative or any other office-holder.

## members
### STANDARD
Wanyan is the only member named. In Sal Flore she first said she was an archaeologist but lacked even basic archaeological knowledge. She is in truth a devotee of Havria from the Hall, and she wanted proof that Morax was guilty. She joined Zhongli, the Fatui researcher Kliment and the Traveler in the sealed ruins of Sal Terrae.
### LARGE +
Zhongli made her and Kliment agree to alternate claims on treasures found there, one object per claim. Kliment took the self-refilling salt chalice and Wanyan the salt ruler, which covers the ground in salt. When Kliment broke the contract out of greed, Zhongli and the Traveler made an example of him. Later Zhongli told Wanyan she could claim only part of a broken sword, and she chose to break the contract rather than leave it. Zhongli then punished her by telling her the truth.

## goals
### STANDARD
Its trade is salt. Beyond that, the story shown in canon is Wanyan's personal aim to prove her god's death was Morax's crime.

## methods
### LARGE
Its heritage is oral. The Hall passes down Havria's story through generations, and the account that survives has drifted away from the facts.

## history
### STANDARD
The Hall's origins lie in the Archon War. Havria refused to fight, gave way to other gods and finally held a small plot at Sal Terrae. According to Zhongli, her people's king killed her with a sword as a mercy, because her kindness could not protect them. Her power spread out and turned most of her followers into salt. The survivors scattered across Liyue, and some reached Liyue Harbor, which Morax protected, and eventually formed Yinyuan Hall.
### LARGE +
Over the millennia the survivors' story warped into one where Morax murdered Havria out of envy. Other descendants of Sal Terrae's survivors kept a truer account, which appears in the Diary of Roald the Adventurer. Descendants fearing a curse later returned, broke the sword and placed it on a pedestal with two of her relics, then resealed the ruin.
### ULTRA +
Wanyan said it was Morax's Liyue and its history was written as he wished. Zhongli, who treated Havria's fate as a lesson of the Archon War, told her it was her punishment to learn the real facts, and she rejected them at first. Havria's remains in the domain were a single flower of salt. Zhongli believes Havria could not have cursed her people, since she loved them too much.

## relations
### STANDARD
Under the Liyue Qixing, in the same Eight Trades as Shenglu Hall. Its tradition is hostile to Morax, Liyue's old archon, though the Hall sits in his harbour city.
### LARGE +
Wanyan did not know Zhongli was the Geo Archon when she told him about her hatred of Morax.

## secrets
### STANDARD
The Hall holds a mistaken belief rather than a secret. The truth was told only to Wanyan, within a sealed ruin, so other members should not be assumed to know it.

## status
### STANDARD
Active as of 7.1. The last event concerning the Hall in canon is the Sal Flore story quest from Version 1.1. No appearances were found in 6.x (Nod-Krai) or 7.0-7.1 (Snezhnaya) content.

## entry: Havria and the Hall
@keys: Havria, God of Salt, Sal Terrae, Yinyuan Hall
@version: 1.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Havria was a gentle, weak god of salt who fled the Archon War and was killed by her own king. The Hall's descendants believe Morax assassinated her. Zhongli says the sword found in Sal Terrae was the murder weapon, not her relic, and that her people acted from mercy. She left two relics: a chalice that refills itself with salt and a ruler that covers ground in salt.
