## summary
### LITE
The Curatorium of Secrets is an intelligence and commission agency in Nasha Town, Nod-Krai, owned and run by Nefer. It takes commissions from the public, such as finding lost cats, tracking down people or things, and righting wrongs. It is also the public face of the underground Northern Intelligence Network. It follows a policy of strict neutrality toward every faction in Nod-Krai, and its only official employee is Jahoda.
### STANDARD +
To townspeople it is a busy, efficient, fairly cheap commission shop with a black cat on the counter. Beneath that is a broker of secrets with contacts in nearly every nation. Neutrality is the principle it sells.

## structure
### LITE
Nefer is the owner and decides everything. Jahoda is the only official employee. Ashru, Nefer's cat, sits at the reception counter.
### STANDARD +
The building stands at the heart of Nasha Town and has at least two floors. The upper floor holds a reception area and Nefer's office, and visitors can walk in.
### LARGE +
Nefer is also a member of the Voynich Guild. A secret room on the same floor holds her private records. In the Northern Intelligence Network, the Curatorium is the main point of sale; the Network's other people are informants and clients, not Curatorium staff. The Network is made up of volunteers. Many of them gave up their old posts, reputations or even names to join. It is loosely organised, with no formal rules, and runs as an exchange where well-connected people sell what they know. The Curatorium sits at its centre. Network members include Diluc, whom Nefer codenamed "Owl", and informants such as Sarin of the Adventurers' Guild, Iratxa, Lestocq, Sieciech, Cairuo and Tarno. (This roster is taken from the wiki's Northern Intelligence Network page.) The Curatorium's own staff are Jahoda and Ashru.

## leadership
### LITE
Nefer, who came to Nod-Krai from Sumeru, owns the Curatorium and also heads the Northern Intelligence Network. She is calm, mercenary, and several moves ahead of whoever is in front of her.
### STANDARD +
Nefer treats information and favours as currency and nothing as free. In Hidden in the Shadows she tells Jahoda never to accept favours, since they are the hardest debts to repay.
### LARGE +
In Character Story 3, Nefer deliberately removes the "leaders" who had carved Nod-Krai into private territories, and she then lets their followers fight among themselves. She takes care to keep innocent people out of it, because she expects to serve them as clients later. Character Story 4 describes her calmly brewing tea for visitors as if she had never destroyed a gang or ruined a boss, and calls her the unseen hand keeping Nod-Krai's waters "just the right degree of murky". In the same story she tells a young man who drew a blade on her that Nod-Krai's powerful figures are weighed down by their names and fortunes, and then offers him a commission.

## members
### LITE
- Nefer: owner, head of the Network.
- Jahoda: the only official employee, a former Seahook Gang member whose life Nefer saved.
- Ashru: Nefer's black cat, the receptionist.
### STANDARD +
Jahoda handles most field commissions, from searching for lost cats to chasing down clients who cheat. Ashru descends from cats once kept by Thoth, the Ibis King.
### LARGE +
According to the wiki, citing Nefer's voice-over, Nefer paid in full for Jahoda's robotic arm. While imprisoned in Aaru, the Ibis King once spoke to Nefer through Ashru. The Curatorium's lines of trust run through Nefer: the Adventurers' Guild's Nod-Krai head Sarin has an understanding with her, and the two collaborate. Dori, a merchant and a fellow Sumeru native, is shown dropping in to chat. Nefer is credited with saving her when she arrived in Nod-Krai in a dire state.
### ULTRA +
Other people who act for the Curatorium on specific jobs include the Traveler, whom Nefer asks to assist Jahoda in a trade-expo commission (6.5, see the entry below), and Paimon, who is jokingly listed as a receptionist on the reception message board. Neither is a standing member.

## goals
### STANDARD
The Curatorium sells information and handles commissions at a profit. Through Nefer it also aims to keep Nod-Krai stable and no single faction dominant.
### LARGE +
Nefer says she joined the League only for appearances, until the venture becomes worth her while. Behind the profit motive, the stories show Nefer protecting people she values: she paid for Jahoda's arm, is credited with saving Dori, and risked her sight to help seal Rerir. The Curatorium's neutrality reads as a business principle that she will bend quietly for such people.

## methods
### STANDARD
The Curatorium does public commissions at reasonable prices, lower when Jahoda is on duty, and brokers intelligence through the Network, where informants sell facts and clients pay for them.
### LARGE +
Nefer reads people closely and often arranges things in advance, so visitors find she already knows why they came. Nefer's tools include a chess-like ritual board given to her by Thoth. With it, and with objects tied to a person or place, she can view their past, a technique she uses on Rerir's memories and on Luonnotar's. The ritual carries danger, as Rerir imprisoned her inside her own memory. The Curatorium also uses ordinary trade methods: letters, tips from informants and favours called in, as when Nefer calmed Nasha Town after Rerir's attack.
### ULTRA +
Gameplay note (Intel Hunt): playable members of the Curatorium, meaning Nefer and Jahoda, can use their Elemental Skills on certain NPCs to gather intel. The first time gives Recorded Intel, which Katheryne exchanges for Intel Quests. A member can earn a combined total of six per weekly reset. Some NPCs also unlock a world quest, with a different version depending on whether Nefer or Jahoda gathered the intel.

## history
### STANDARD
Nefer came to Nod-Krai after leaving Sumeru. She eliminated the leaders who had divided the region, then opened the Curatorium and hired Jahoda. At some point she also came to head the Northern Intelligence Network. The doors were nearly always shut until the Curatorium announced that it would take commissions from anyone.
### LARGE +
The first ordinary client was a crying little girl whose parents had not come home, and Jahoda rescued them. Before that, Nasha Town told rumours about the mysterious owner.
**Frostmoon Scions' relic (6.0).** When the Frostmoon Scions' sacred relic, the Moon Marrow, was stolen, Lauma tried several times to commission Nefer. Nefer repeatedly declined, citing neutrality. After hearing the Traveler's account, Nefer argued that the Fatui may have taken the relic, rather than the Traveler's sibling. Still citing neutrality, she turned down an open request to help infiltrate the Fatui's facility. She then agreed to a final condition from Lauma: to persuade whoever walked through the door next to help. That was Jahoda, whom Nefer politely asked to go and then fired on the spot, so that Jahoda would be earning on her own account. The Curatorium would take her back once the job was done.
**Secret Base (6.1).** In Hidden in the Shadows, Varka arranged a meeting at the Knights of Favonius's covert Nod-Krai base, a contact hub for the Network, and asked Nefer to help unite the factions of Nod-Krai against Rerir. She first declined, until Jahoda offered her own wages and Lauma offered a formal commission. She then agreed to follow up leads, and borrowed the fragment that Rerir had been after from Flins.
### ULTRA +
**Rerir's memories (6.1).** In Special Operation and following quests, Nefer used Dori's protective charm and her chessbox to view Rerir's past, with Lauma and the Traveler. Dainsleif also separately commissioned her to learn why Rerir became a Sinner. Rerir noticed and trapped her in her own memories. She freed the group with the charm, sealed him in the chessbox, and later goaded him in the final fight to buy time. In Echoes of Fate, she paid for it with her sight, which Lauma's medicine restored over some days.
**After Rerir.** Nefer agreed to lead the inquiry into the phantom ship Jahoda reported in Starsand Shoal (6.2), helped search for Columbina after Dottore's ambush (6.2), and fought Dottore alongside the others in Acts VII and VIII (6.3). When the League formed after the crisis, the Curatorium joined as a member.
**The trade expo (6.5).** For the Nod-Krai Trade Expo, which the Voynich Guild hosts, she asked the Traveler to help Jahoda, then brokered the arrests of an arms syndicate outside the expo. The Guild's rule against violence meant visiting Liyue and Fontaine officials could not act inside the venue.
**Sumeru (6.6).** Nicole persuaded Nefer to help in Sumeru. Nefer left Jahoda in charge of the Curatorium, answered Thoth's riddle with "time", helped free the trapped Pillars, and helped Cyno catch a Fatui spy.

## relations
### STANDARD
**Voynich Guild:** works closely with it; Nefer is a member. **Adventurers' Guild:** cooperates through an understanding with Sarin. **Knights of Favonius:** Varka is a Network client. **Frostmoon Scions:** Nefer and Lauma know each other well and spar constantly. **Fatui:** the Curatorium has refused to help either side infiltrate the other.
### LARGE +
**Liyue:** the Network has no foothold in Liyue because Yelan and her colleagues prevented it, though Yelan buys intelligence from the Curatorium's side and Nefer bears no grudge. **Marechaussee Phantom and Spina di Rosula:** the wiki lists both as allies of the Network. In coded talk, Nefer asked Lestocq of the Marechaussee Phantom about remaining Seahook Gang members who had made their way to Fontaine. **League:** Nefer is the Curatorium's representative in the League, and has said her participation is for appearances until the venture proves worth her while.

## reputation
### STANDARD
Residents of Nasha Town know it as an efficient, comparatively cheap commission shop. Children remember Nefer as the kind lady with candy and slightly scary stories.
### LARGE +
Adults told lurid urban legends about the owner, such as an army of grotesque minions inside, and some avoided the place. People who deal with Nefer on serious business speak of her very differently. Lauma calls the Curatorium's professional standards beyond question after watching how Nefer handled Jahoda's commission.

## secrets
@version: 6.1
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
The Curatorium's polite exterior hides the Northern Intelligence Network, an underground exchange for intelligence. Nefer's earlier career in Nod-Krai included the planned killing of its criminal leaders, according to her Character Story 3. At the Curatorium's heart is a hidden back room where Nefer keeps private papers and a store of the agency's money. Letters from clients and the finance chest are only meant for Curatorium members to open.
### ULTRA +
In Reunion Beneath the Moonlight, Nefer says a source told her that morning that the Fatui had abruptly called off their search for the stolen relic. Who the source is, is not stated.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1, the Curatorium is open and operating in Nasha Town. Nefer is back at its head, her sight having returned, and Jahoda still handles most of its commissions.
### STANDARD +
The agency has joined the Nod-Krai League as a representative body. Nefer has said she will stay a member only while it remains worthwhile. Since Truth Amongst the Pages of Purana (6.6) the reception board lists Nefer herself at the counter again. Versions 7.0 and 7.1 add no new Curatorium story in the sources checked; in the 7.1 Anecdote Jahoda: A Thief's End, Jahoda is in Snezhnograd and says she has another errand to run for her boss before heading back to Nod-Krai.

## entry: Northern Intelligence Network
@keys: Northern Intelligence Network, intel black market
@version: 6.1
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
The Northern Intelligence Network is an underground exchange of information, headquartered in Nod-Krai with contacts in every nation except Liyue. Its members are volunteers, many of whom gave up their old lives to join. It is loosely organised and has no firm rules. People trade what they know for payment. Nefer is its current head, and the Curatorium of Secrets is its main point of sale. Varka calls it the black market of intel and says the Knights of Favonius rely on it for local information.
### LARGE +
The Network's roster, as listed on the wiki, includes Diluc as a contact with the codename "Owl", Jahoda and Ashru as employees, a spread of informants, and clients such as Yelan and Varka. Its Liyue operation failed because Yelan's side blocked it. Nefer's Character Story "Moon Wheel" hints at an older representative who wanted a successor, but does not say how she took over.

## entry: Neutrality policy
@keys: strict neutrality, neutrality
@keys2: Curatorium, Nefer
@version: 6.0
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
The Curatorium of Secrets says it has always followed a policy of strict neutrality and takes no side in Nod-Krai's factions. Nefer cites it to refuse commissions that would make her an enemy of the Fatui or the Frostmoon Scions. She has refused a Harbinger's request to help infiltrate the Scions' territory and refused Lauma's request to help infiltrate the Fatui facility. She still works around it. When Lauma asked her to persuade whoever walked in next to help, she was willing, as long as she was not personally involved. She treats the rule as a business principle, not a moral one.

## entry: Jahoda's firing
@keys: Jahoda fired, fired Jahoda
@keys2: Curatorium, Nefer, Nasha Town
@version: 6.0
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
In Reunion Beneath the Moonlight, Nefer politely asks a startled Jahoda to help the Traveler, Paimon and Lauma infiltrate the Fatui's Kuuvahki Experimental Design Bureau. When Jahoda agrees, Nefer fires her on the spot, effective immediately, and says the Curatorium will take her back after the job. This keeps the Curatorium neutral on paper, since Jahoda is a private contractor, and Jahoda earns more without Nefer's cut. Lauma later praises the Curatorium's professionalism.

## entry: The Secret Base
@keys: Secret Base
@keys2: Varka, Curatorium, Network
@version: 6.1
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
The Secret Base is an underground meeting place used by the Knights of Favonius and other clients of the Northern Intelligence Network. It is hidden and secure. In Hidden in the Shadows, Varka leads Lauma, Flins, Aino, Ineffa, Jahoda and the Traveler there to hide from the Wild Hunt. Nefer arrives in response to a letter from a "humble knight of Favonius". She turns out to be the Network's head, and Varka asks her to help unite Nod-Krai against Rerir. The interior is only accessible during certain quests.

### LARGE
Nefer owns a ritual chess-like board from Thoth, which she calls her chessbox. She uses it to view memories tied to objects and places. In the Rerir arc she used it, plus a protective charm from Dori, to look into Rerir's past, and was drawn into a counter-attack in which Rerir trapped her inside her own memory. She sealed him inside the box. During the final fight, she risked her sight to hold him back. In 6.3 she used the box again on the Traveler's belongings to locate where Dottore had them, and later to view the memory of Luonnotar.
### ULTRA +
Thoth also strengthened the board afterward as a reward for answering his riddle. Whether Nefer can control the board's effects exactly, or only partly, is not stated.

## entry: Ashru

@version: 6.1
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
Ashru is Nefer's black cat and the Curatorium's receptionist. He descends from the cats Thoth once kept. While imprisoned in Aaru, Thoth took over Ashru's body to speak to Nefer, then left it before she could exploit it. Nefer has kept Ashru close in case Thoth ever speaks through him again. In daily life he is a lazy cat who eats well; Nefer keeps his bowl filled and Jahoda pets him. Ashru's dialogue hints that Lauma or Columbina might be able to understand him.

@version: 6.1

### STANDARD
According to Nefer's fifth Character Story, the Curatorium used to be a place the town feared, and the news that it would take commissions from anyone alarmed parents. The first ordinary client was a tearful little girl whose parents had not come home. Jahoda rescued the couple from bandits, and Nefer asked only for a hairpin as payment. Word spread, and the Curatorium came to resemble a river winding through town: visible at the surface, deeper currents hidden. The story adds that only one person can uncover its true secrets.

### STANDARD
During the event Where Waves Meet the Reef (6.5, April to May 2026), Nefer asks the Traveler to help Jahoda manage VIPs at the Voynich Guild's annual expo. Among them are Beidou, captain of a large fleet, and Navia, who leads an underworld organisation, and Jahoda separates their booths. The expo's rule against violence stops visiting Liyue and Fontaine authorities from arresting an arms syndicate inside it, so Nefer lets Yelan and Chevreuse handle the arrests outside. A man called Ashvapati, who joined the Voynich Guild with a hidden agenda, lies about everything except his desert origins. Nefer later suggests that he return to the desert to build a peaceful life there.
