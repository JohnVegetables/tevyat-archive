## summary
### LITE
The Eight Trades are eight commerce sectors that run Liyue's major industries, under the jurisdiction of the Liyue Qixing. Their divisions are called Halls or Offices. Canon names four: the Communications Office (transport), Huishan Hall (mining), Shenglu Hall (glazed ceramics) and Yinyuan Hall (salt).
### STANDARD +
When Rex Lapis relinquished his post, his duties fell to the Qixing and the Eight Trades. The Chinese and Japanese names mean "Eight Gates". The English "Hall" translates the Chinese word for a government office.

## structure
### STANDARD
The Qixing hold authority over the sectors. Each Trade is a Hall or Office with its own representatives and members. The wiki lists only four of the eight, and no shared leader or headquarters is described. The Sword and Strongbox Secure Transport Agency is a sub-organisation of the Communications Office.
### LARGE +
Known sectors, members and tasks:
- **Communications Office** (Hezhe Post): transportation, shipments around Liyue and overseas logistics, plus related construction and road management. Master Qin "Thunder Hand" acts as a bodyguard, and Yanfei's friends are tied to it.
- **Huishan Hall**: mining and matters involving the Chasm. Uncle Dai is its foreman, and Zhiqiong was offered a place there as consultant and guide.
- **Shenglu Hall**: glazed ceramics. Miss Ruoyu is the representative, Wanyu heads human culture and civilisation research, and Muning is its specialist.
- **Yinyuan Hall**: salt. Wanyan is the only known member.

## members
### STANDARD
Members named in canon come from four Halls: Fangju, Master Qin and others at the Communications Office; Uncle Dai at Huishan Hall; Miss Ruoyu, Wanyu and Muning at Shenglu Hall; and Wanyan at Yinyuan Hall.
### LARGE +
After the events of the limited-time event The Chasm Delvers, the Chasm Exploration Team became an official part of Huishan Hall.

## goals
### STANDARD
The Eight Trades keep Liyue's industries and shipping running. Their activities include reopening Chasm mining, researching rare Archaic Stones, and handling transport routes and docks. Under the Qixing they also help carry on the administrative duties that once fell to Rex Lapis.

## methods
### STANDARD
They work through official notices (such as bulletin boards at Feiyun Slope and Mingyun Village), commissions from the Ministry of Civil Affairs and trade specialists. Fangju of the Communications Office says its large staff is needed because transport depends on good roads, bridges, waterways and docks. A staff member mentions that the office plans to build small lifts.
### LARGE +
Huishan Hall has forbidden people working in the Chasm from eating plants and mushrooms that grow in the underground mines, because they may harm the body.

## history
### LARGE
Shenglu Hall's members are mostly descendants of the indigenous tribes who lived near the Chasm. After Morax arrived, these tribes made blood-pacts with the Yaksha, became his allies and fought against Azhdaha and the monsters of the cataclysm. Most later moved to Liyue Harbor and formed Shenglu Hall, which specialises in glazed ceramics passed down through generations. The Chasm's closure worried the Hall, which relied on materials from there.
### ULTRA +
Yinyuan Hall's members revere the long-dead God of Salt, Havria. In their tradition, their ancestors were sheltered by her in Sal Terrae during the Archon War, and survivors who escaped her death's effects scattered across Liyue, some taking refuge in Liyue Harbor under Morax's protection. Over thousands of years the story changed into a form in which Morax killed her out of envy, which is the version in Farewell, Sweet Salt. Another account, kept by other descendants of Sal Terrae's survivors, says Havria's own people killed her. The Communications Office appears in canon from 1.5.

## relations
### STANDARD
- **Liyue Qixing**: they supervise all eight Trades.
- **Ministry of Civil Affairs**: it works with the Halls, for example by commissioning a Shenglu Hall specialist to oversee the Chasm.
- **Sword and Strongbox Secure Transport Agency**: a guard company that works under the Communications Office.

## status
@version: 7.1
### LITE
As of 7.1 the Eight Trades remain Liyue's commercial administration under the Qixing. No new developments were found in 6.x or 7.0-7.1.

## entry: Communications Office
@keys: Communications Office
### STANDARD
The Communications Office is the Trade that handles transport in Liyue: shipments, roads, bridges, waterways and docks, and overseas shipping logistics. Its staff is large. Fangju explains its role, and another worker mentions plans for small lifts. The Sword and Strongbox Secure Transport Agency, whose guards escort goods, is one of its sub-organisations. Gaming's profile says the Agency has many skilled guards but only Gaming can both keep clients happy and beat bandits. The Communications Office first appears in 1.5.

## entry: Huishan Hall
@keys: Huishan Hall
### STANDARD
Huishan Hall oversees mining and matters involving the Chasm. Its foreman is Uncle Dai. Its higher-ups are studying rare Archaic Stones from the Chasm, which Muning of the Ministry of Civil Affairs was sent to fetch. The Chasm's changes lowered the quality of the stones that remain.

## entry: Shenglu Hall
@keys: Shenglu Hall, Miss Ruoyu
### STANDARD
Shenglu Hall governs the glazed ceramics trade. Miss Ruoyu is the industry representative. Her notice on the Feiyun Slope bulletin board says she is investigating the possibility of restarting Chasm mining. Wanyu is head of human culture and civilisation research.

## entry: Yinyuan Hall
@keys: Yinyuan Hall, Wanyan
### STANDARD
Yinyuan Hall governs the salt industry and worships Havria, the long-dead God of Salt. Wanyan, an archaeologist, is the member seen in canon. Its account of Havria's death differs from other accounts, as the uncertainties note.
