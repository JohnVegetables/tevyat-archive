## summary
### LITE
The Crux is an armed fleet based in Liyue Harbor and led by Captain Beidou. Its sailors carry cargo across seas no ordinary merchant would risk, fight sea monsters and pirates, and are famous throughout Liyue for daring voyages and rich bounties. Its flagship is the Alcor. Everything it does is approved by the Qixing, in the words of Beidou's profile, "more or less".
### STANDARD +
Its crew is fiercely loyal to Beidou, and she in turn risks her ship for strangers. The fleet is also known for its feasts, its hired chefs and its guests, which have included Xiangling and the Inazuman wanderer Kaedehara Kazuha.

## structure
### STANDARD
The crew is split into two arms. The Spear of the Crux is trained for naval warfare and handling cargo deals. The Shield of the Crux is trained in survival and safe navigation: maritime rescue, cartography, weather reading and even counselling, and it also plans the crew's recreation. Beidou's hangout quests show both arms training an outsider, one with a boat-handling drill and one with seamanship lessons from the ship's surgeon, navigator and a sailor.
### LARGE +
Known roles aboard the Alcor include Juza (Chief Mate), Huixing (navigator), Yinxing (surgeon at sea), Mora-Grubber (bookkeeper), Suling (ironworker) and Muzhen (chief technical officer), plus sailors such as Furong, Xu Liushi and Sea Drake. A young girl called Little Yue also lives among the crew. The fleet operates at least two ships of the Alcor class. The second, called "No. 2" because she was launched without a naming ceremony, carries upgraded Guizhong Ballistas that can remotely intercept incoming projectiles. Beidou says it is an older model that the Millelith have already moved beyond, approved for the civilian market.

## leadership
### LITE
Captain Beidou, the Uncrowned Lord of the Ocean, established and leads the Crux. When she goes ashore for business, Chief Mate Juza minds the Alcor and the crew, as he tells visitors himself.
### STANDARD +
Beidou treats her crew with respect and in return they trust her to tame storms. Juza says he was a dockworker with no direction until she invited him aboard. His version of the legend is that Beidou stood at the wheel in a terrible storm and sang a fishermen's song until the waves died and dawn broke. He tells new sailors that story over drinks after their first safe return.

## members
### STANDARD
Beidou is the only playable member. Kaedehara Kazuha is a temporary crew member, taken aboard at Ritou while wanted in Inazuma, and he stayed even after the Vision Hunt Decree ended. Xiangling is a regular guest rather than crew, sailing with them in search of new seafood ingredients.
### LARGE +
Alcor crew in the open world include Juza, Yinxing, Huixing, Furong, Mora-Grubber, Suling, Sea Drake, Xu Liushi and Little Yue. Fengyun, Gongmiao, Huoxiang, Seahawk, Stone Stool and Zhiyao are further crew who appear at the expo. Muzhen appeared in a limited-time Inazuma event, where she was studying Ruin Sentinels for a client from Sumeru.
### ULTRA +
Former members show where the fleet's reach ends. Zhang Shun left the fleet after being injured in the battle with Haishan, where Beidou helped him, and now sells goods at Liyue Harbor. Farzaneh once served as Lieutenant Captain and now runs an armed commercial shipping company from Sumeru City, where she still feels dizzy on dry land.

## goals
### STANDARD
Profit and freedom, in that order, with a strong streak of justice. Beidou tells the Traveler that almost every confrontation the Crux faces is settled by negotiation, that weapons come out only when talks fail, and that the Alcor opens fire only in a fight to the death. In her words the ship is there to deter or to destroy, and the fleet is an armed crew with a sense of justice rather than war-hungry lunatics.

## methods
### LARGE
The Crux earns money in two ways. It acts as an armed carrier, delivering goods between places separated by sea, and it also deals in illegal trade. One hangout quest shows Beidou meeting trading partners and staging an after-hours handover on the beach at Guyun Stone Forest. It also trains navigators. In a Lantern Rite event quest (limited-time), the navigator Huixing explains that sailors need nine Ministry of Civil Affairs qualifications plus two years of maritime training and apprenticeship, and that the Crux and the Ministry have organised an intensive navigation training programme whose graduates receive a Deep Sea Navigation Qualification certificate. Its prices are very high, yet Beidou is known to tow a stranded boat to safety at risk to her own keel and to share the fleet's last food with the rescued.
### ULTRA +
Beidou's profile gives the fleet one more custom: after every long voyage it holds a three-day feast. According to a limited-time Mondstadt event, the Crux has a valid permit to dock at Dornman Port in Mondstadt. It has traded in Inazuma, where Yoimiya sometimes commissions it to ship fireworks to Liyue. Beidou and her crew were also hired by Sangonomiya Kokomi as mercenaries for the Watatsumi Army, according to the wiki's reading of the Inazuma Archon Quest.

## history
### LARGE
**Early reputation.** The fleet earned its name over many years. Its landmark feat is the slaying of the sea monster Haishan, which Beidou had dreamed of since she was nine. Her Vision profile says the battle lasted four days, with cannons, harpoons and ropes pinning the beast while she fought it for ten hours, then finished it with one strike at dawn. A bolt of lightning struck the sea and she received her Electro Vision. Pier gossip adds that she has not sung a fishermen's shanty since. Tea Master Liu Su tells the story as "The Wrath of Haishan" in a Liyue storytelling quest.

**Kazuha comes aboard.** After Ei enacted the Vision Hunt Decree, Beidou befriended Kazuha at Ritou and took him on the Alcor as a sailor. She was wary of staying docked there longer than necessary. In the Inazuma Archon Quest she and the Alcor carry the Traveler from Liyue to Ritou.
### ULTRA +
**The 6.5 expo.** During the limited-time flagship event Where Waves Meet the Reef (2026-04-27 to 2026-05-14) the Crux brought Alcor-class No. 2 to the Nod-Krai Trade Expo, organised by the Voynich Guild, to show off long-range Guizhong Ballista fire. Beidou met Navia and Clorinde of the Spina di Rosula and the Court of Fontaine at the expo, said she wanted to make friends, and suggested they come aboard for a drink. After the event's trouble was settled, Beidou let the Traveler choose No. 2's name between "Alkaid" and "Alioth".

## relations
### STANDARD
Beidou and Ningguang, the Tianquan of the Liyue Qixing, are an odd pair. Beidou does not fear her as most of Liyue does and insists nobody is her boss. Ningguang says she hires Beidou, never the Crux. Ningguang's advisers call Beidou unpredictable, but Ningguang calls her the most reliable person in Liyue. Ningguang also lays hefty fines on the fleet's shipments, which Beidou simply pays, and the two play chess and bet against each other.
### LARGE +
Beidou dislikes Treasure Hoarders but honours first-come, first-served in treasure hunts, and she offers hoarders a chance to leave that life. Rumour holds that Ningguang is the fleet's secret owner, a claim both women reject. The Qixing approve the expeditions "more or less". Abroad, the Crux deals with Inazuma through Yoimiya and Kokomi, and has met Fontaine's Navia and Clorinde at the Nod-Krai expo.

## reputation
### STANDARD
Every merchant in Liyue Harbor knows Beidou and her crew. The harbour tells exaggerated tales of her feats, from cutting a sea monster in two to winning money from Ningguang at chess, and merchants gossip about the fines she pays on shipments. Children look up to her. Criminals such as Treasure Hoarders fear and avoid her. Her crew consider her capable of anything and the stories grow with every telling.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the Crux is active and still led by Beidou. Juza keeps the Alcor, which is anchored off Guyun Stone Forest while Beidou is in the city.
### STANDARD +
It last featured prominently in the 6.5 expo event. No 6.x Nod-Krai Archon Quest or 7.0 and 7.1 Snezhnaya content was found that changes the fleet's status.

## entry: Alcor
@keys: Alcor, the Alcor
@version: 1.0
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
The Alcor is the Crux's flagship, anchored off Guyun Stone Forest when at rest. The map text says it crosses oceans and can penetrate tempestuous storms. Juza calls it the fastest ship on Liyue's seas, armed to the teeth, and says pirates flee at the sight of it. A world quest, A Lone Ship in Guyun, has the Traveler help the crew with small chores after Juza first mistakes them for an intruder. The name comes from a star. Beidou says in a hangout quest that the Alcor is thought an omen of death.
### LARGE +
The Alcor is the lead ship of its class. A second ship, No. 2, appeared at the 6.5 expo with upgraded Guizhong Ballistas, as the map description for that event notes.

## entry: Shield and Spear of the Crux

### STANDARD
The Shield of the Crux covers survival: rescue at sea, charts, weather, counselling and crew recreation. The Spear covers naval warfare and cargo deals. In Life on the High Seas, Yinxing teaches the Traveler about health risks (underestimating danger is the answer to the greatest danger for new sailors), Huixing about navigation and Xu Liushi about reading storms, with Beidou setting a test afterward. In Beneath the Surface, the Spear's training uses older Skirmish Boats kept as backups on the Alcor.

### STANDARD
Juza is the Chief Mate of the Alcor. He is loud, proud of the ship and devoted to Beidou, a former dockworker she invited aboard. When Beidou is ashore he treats unknown visitors suspiciously and threatens to have them fed to the fishes, until Yinxing steps in. His name is Inazuman in style, which fits the fleet's links to Inazuma.

### STANDARD
Kazuha joined the Alcor at Ritou as a temporary sailor, hiding from those hunting him after the Vision Hunt Decree. Beidou did not realise at first that he was a trained swordsman and told her crew to take care of him. They welcomed him for his skill and for predicting weather, and overlooked his reticence about his past. After the Decree ended he stayed, and Kazuha's profile calls the Crux his new home. The crow's nest gives him space to think.

### STANDARD
Ningguang is said to be the Crux's hidden owner. Neither woman accepts that. Ningguang hires Beidou, not the Crux; Beidou says they are equals. The Qixing's administrators see her as a lawbreaker who pays hefty fines without complaint, while the two women still keep each other company over chess and cooperate when Liyue's greater good is at stake.

## entry: Haishan
@keys: Haishan
@version: 1.0
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
Haishan was a leviathan-like sea monster that merchants were bound to meet sooner or later. Beidou had challenged it many times with the Crux. She struck it down after a four-day fight in which the fleet pinned it with ropes and harpoons. Her Electro Vision appeared afterwards. The crew sang a sea shanty during the battle, per her profile. Zhang Shun, a sailor injured then, retired and became a peddler.

## entry: Xiangling's feasts aboard the Crux
@keys: Xiangling
@keys2: Crux, Alcor, Beidou
@version: 1.0
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
After a long voyage, the Crux held its customary three-day banquet and hired the then little-known Xiangling to save money. She objected to being asked for Yue-style seafood, but Beidou's offer of a fifty thousand Mora retainer won her over. Beidou told the crew to call her "Madam Xiangling" and predicted she would be famous. Xiangling has since joined voyages to find new ingredients, though she does not always heed Beidou's advice on what is edible.

### STANDARD
During the limited-time event Where Waves Meet the Reef (6.5), the Crux took part in the expo in Nod-Krai with a new Alcor-class warship.
