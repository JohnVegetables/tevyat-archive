## summary
### LITE
The International Trade Association is a group of foreign merchants based in Ritou, the port island of Inazuma. It imports overseas goods such as Silk Flowers and gives practical help and shelter to outlanders who are new to Inazuma. Its president is Kurisu, a merchant from Fontaine. Under the Sakoku Decree, Ritou's Kanjou Commission treated its members harshly.
### STANDARD +
Merchants in trouble usually go to Kurisu directly, and newcomers are pointed to the Association when the Outlander Affairs Agency has taken them for all they are worth. During the lockdown, the Agency's Kageyama told the Traveler that only Association members were authorised to import and export goods.

## structure
### STANDARD
No formal ranks beyond the president are given. Kurisu leads and handles the hard cases, and other members run the day-to-day work. Xixi says she does only the simpler reception work, and Harrison runs a shop of imported goods. The Association has a building in Ritou with a small plaza in front. Its members come from several nations, so it is as much a community as a trade body.
### LARGE +
Membership has changed over time. Werner, a Mondstadter who had shipped goods between Liyue and Inazuma, was once a member. Thoma says the Association was very generous to him at first, but he left when the decrees made life hard for everyone.

## leadership
### LITE
Kurisu is the president. His real name is Christopher and he comes from Fontaine; he took the name Kurisu after arriving in Inazuma. He is courteous and patient, and he tells his members that outlanders must look after each other.
### STANDARD +
Kurisu is a friend of Thoma, the Kamisato clan's housekeeper and fixer. Xixi says he has lived in Inazuma for a long time and that every outlander in Ritou can count on him. In the 2.0 Archon Quest he openly misses Fontaine, but he deals with a corrupt tax collector through reputation and leverage rather than force. After the Sakoku Decree ended, Thoma says he was working to mediate disagreements within the Association.

## members
### STANDARD
- **Kurisu:** president and spokesman, from Fontaine.
- **Harrison:** a shopkeeper from Mondstadt who sells imported goods at International Groceries. His shop was closed for weeks by the tax squeeze.
- **Xixi:** a sleepy receptionist whose home region is Liyue.
- **Werner:** a former member from Mondstadt, central to the Crystal Marrow affair (see entries).
### LARGE +
Xixi says the Association used to be a place where foreign traders gathered to mingle and swap business news. After the decree ended she says the number of outlander merchants rose sharply, so she has had little sleep.

## goals
### STANDARD
Its aims are practical: keep foreign goods flowing into Inazuma, keep outlander merchants solvent, and give newcomers a place to turn. Mutual help among outlanders is also a stated principle, as Xixi says Kurisu always tells them.

## methods
### STANDARD
The Association imports foreign goods for sale in Ritou, advises and shelters newcomers, and negotiates quietly when pressed. In the 2.0 Archon Quest, Kurisu confronted the tax collector Keijirou by pointing out how a scandal would tarnish a samurai's reputation, then bargained for compensation. Thoma sometimes acts as an outside fixer who helps the Association with problems.
### LARGE +
Harrison's whispered talk about "stocking up" and "premium products imported from merchants all over the world" plays on the line between import trade and smuggling. The scene treats it as a joke, and he insists these are imported goods only.

## history
### STANDARD
**The decree years.** After the Sakoku Decree was enacted, Ritou's Kanjou Commission treated foreign merchants harshly. Outlanders faced high taxes and strict rules, and the Association was a place of mutual support.

**The Crystal Marrow tax (Chapter II, Act I).** A new Tax Decree required the Association to pay in Crystal Marrow instead of Mora. A single vendor controlled the supply and kept raising the price. The Traveler traced the vendor, Werner, to Keijirou, a Kanjou Commission tax collector who was embezzling the Marrow and selling it back to the merchants. See the entry for details.

**After the decree.** After the Kanjou Commissioner was arrested and replaced and the Sakoku Decree ended, the Commission began treating the merchants on better terms, which delighted Kurisu. Thoma says the Association is still going strong and is still needed in many situations.

### ULTRA +
**Thoma's later visit (A Trip for Two in Ritou).** In a hangout quest set after the decree ended, Thoma brings the Traveler to the Association's entrance and asks after Kurisu. He says Kurisu works tirelessly to keep some order in a difficult trade, and that the Association is still going strong even though the Sakoku Decree has been abolished, because merchants still often need its help. He also mentions that Kurisu is mediating disagreements within the group after the decree ended.

## relations
### STANDARD
**Kanjou Commission.** Hostile under the Sakoku Decree, better afterward. Kurisu says the Commission's tax collectors cared only about Mora.

**Thoma.** A good friend of Kurisu who sent the Traveler to help in the Crystal Marrow affair, and later talked down the Kanjou Commission's chief Katsuie.

**Outlander Affairs Agency.** A separate state office that processes foreign entry and residency. Kurisu assumes newcomers will have been fleeced there.

**Fontaine, Mondstadt, Liyue.** These are the home nations of Kurisu, Harrison and Werner, and Xixi's home region. Kurisu's longing for Fontaine is a recurring note.
### LARGE +
In Kamisato Ayaka's Story Quest, Ayaka and the Traveler asked Kurisu for silk. He said it had been stolen by vagrants, Ayaka helped him recover it, and he gave them some as thanks.

## reputation
### STANDARD
Within Ritou's outlander community the Association is well trusted, and Kurisu is regarded as reliable. To the old Kanjou Commission its members were an easy source of tax revenue. Thoma says many people in Ritou are still grateful to the Traveler for stopping Katsuie's persecution of the merchants.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1, the Association remains in business in Ritou under Kurisu. The Sakoku Decree is over, the former Kanjou Commissioner has been replaced, and the number of outlander merchants has grown.

## entry: Crystal Marrow Tax Affair
@keys: Crystal Marrow, Keijirou, Hidden Ledger
@keys2: Ritou, Kanjou Commission, merchants, tax
@version: 2.0
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
In Confessions of an Outlander (Chapter II, Act I), the Kanjou Commission issued a Tax Decree that required foreign merchants to pay in Crystal Marrow instead of Mora. Almost nobody knew where to find it. One older Liyue merchant remembered once shipping some to Snezhnaya. A single vendor, Werner, soon held the only supply and kept raising the price. Thoma sent the Traveler to help. With a Mondstadt and a Liyue local specialty, the Traveler won Werner's trust and learned he was a small cog. Keijirou, a Kanjou Commission tax collector, was feeding him the Marrow, taking nearly all the profit, and recording every payment in a ledger buried on the beach.
### LARGE +
Kurisu read the ledger and used the threat of a ruined reputation to make Keijirou hand over his remaining Marrow as compensation. Harrison, whose shop had been shut for weeks, wanted more. Katsuie, Keijirou's chief, arrived and ordered the merchants seized. Thoma showed Ayaka's fan, the Shirasagi Himegimi's, and mentioned a petition to the shogun, and Katsuie backed down. Werner left before facing Kurisu. The Kanjou Commissioner was removed later in the Archon Quest.

## entry: Werner
@keys: Werner
@keys2: Crystal Marrow, Ritou, International Trade Association
@version: 2.0
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Werner is a Mondstadt-born merchant who shipped goods between Liyue and Inazuma until the Sakoku Decree stranded him in Ritou. He joined the Association, left because he felt unable to contribute, sold shells on the beach, and then became the sole Crystal Marrow vendor. He is abrasive at first, but gifts of Mondstadt and Liyue specialties remind him of home, and he admits the truth about Keijirou.
### LARGE +
He was too ashamed to face Kurisu directly in that quest. Whether he later made peace with the Association is not stated.

## entry: Kurisu
@keys: Kurisu
@keys2: International Trade Association, Ritou, Fontaine, Christopher
@version: 2.0
### STANDARD
Kurisu is the Association's president, a Fontainian who adopted an Inazuman name. He is courteous, tired of the Kanjou Commission, and homesick for Fontaine. He is acquainted with Thoma, and a cat named Kaiser is often near him. In the open world he offers a Genius Invokation TCG duel and, after a particular dialogue branch, a Guide to Prosperity.

## entry: Irodori Festival at the Association

@keys2: International Trade Association, Ritou
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
During the Irodori Festival event Hues of the Violet Garden, the small plaza in front of the Association was called the Five Kasen Plaza. Kurisu remarked that it looked absolutely amazing. The Association also provided housing for the foreign guests of honour. This happened during a limited-time event.
