## summary
### LITE
Yae Publishing House is a light-novel publisher and bookshop in Inazuma City. Yae Miko, the Guuji of the Grand Narukami Shrine, founded it and is its editor-in-chief. She started it, by her own account, because she wanted more entertaining light novels to read. Its flagship event is the periodic "This Novel Is Amazing!" writing contest, and its books are read well beyond Inazuma.
### STANDARD +
The ground floor is a shop where the house sells its own titles. Editors, authors and illustrators work under Miko, who is feared and relied on in equal measure.

## structure
### STANDARD
Miko is boss, founder and editor-in-chief. Under her are editors who each manage particular authors, such as Kuroda (executive editor and shopkeeper, who handles Chang the Ninth), Shigeru (Junkichi's editor), Aratani (organises the writing contest and runs author correspondence) and Onodera (organises release events). Authors write under pen names more often than not. The shop on the ground floor sells the house's books directly, including serialised volumes and early printings.
### LARGE +
The wiki's staff list adds Hirayama (an event-only editor-in-chief), Hiratsuka (sales analyst, who appears in the Kamisato Estate), Murata and Fukumoto (festival stall editors), and a columnist signing as "Ms. Hina". The house is small enough that its editors personally deliver invitations and letters, and it often turns to the Traveler when it needs a courier.

## leadership
### LITE
Yae Miko decides what gets published. She is known to be warm and bookish in the house and openly mercenary about profit, and she teases her authors about their deadline excuses.
### STANDARD +
Miko says she has never written a novel herself and that being an editor is its own entertainment. She told the Traveler that if they ever finish their journey and want it recorded, she might write it, if she has time. She is said to review the house's novels personally. She has also sent manuscripts back for being "not free enough", telling the author to ignore genre fashions and write the story well. In one character story, the author's very next manuscript worried her only because the pen name seemed too ordinary for its content.
### ULTRA +
Miko would like to recruit Ayaka's capable sibling at the Yashiro Commission for the house, but cannot because she belongs to the Kamisato Clan. After Mizuki said that stress leaves people with no energy for novels, Miko joked that her staff needed to work harder, and that if Mizuki still was not satisfied she would write one herself, perhaps "The Baku Chronicles".

## goals
### STANDARD
The house exists to publish and sell light novels. Miko's own motives are entertainment and a taste for watching writers struggle with deadlines. Commercial success also matters: she is described as having an obsessive side about profit. The house wants to attract new talent, and the writing contest is its way of finding it.
### LARGE +
When a rival hit threatened to spread dangerous spirit-summoning methods, Miko's solution in Friendship in Writing was to outcompete it with a better-selling novel, not to issue a warning that would only advertise the book. That fits the house's habit of solving problems through popularity.

## members
### LARGE
Named staff and authors from canon include:
- Yae Miko: boss, founder and editor-in-chief.
- Kuroda: executive editor who sells books at the shop and handles the Liyue author Chang the Ninth.
- Aratani, Shigeru, Onodera, Murata, Fukumoto and Hirayama: editors and event staff.
- Hiratsuka: sales analyst (wiki list).
- Junkichi: author of the Onibudou series, managed by Shigeru.
- Satomi: the house's "ace in the hole", who writes under many pen names.
- Souka: up-and-coming author who joined Miko at the beast-tamers tournament in Sumeru for inspiration (3.2).
- Chang the Ninth: Liyue author of "First Apprentice of Guhua", who won a contest.
- Xingqiu, writing as Zhenyu: author of "A Legend of Sword".
- Ms. Hina: advice columnist, in truth Gorou.
### ULTRA +
Albedo is listed as the illustrator "Calx" by the wiki, and the house has openly tried to hire him for greater royalties (Albedo declined). Mr. Nine, author of "Legend of the Shattered Halberd" and "Flowers for Princess Fischl", communicates only through letters sent from many places.

## methods
### STANDARD
The house runs the "This Novel Is Amazing!" contest, invites past winners to judge it, and holds writers' submission events. It publishes light novels in serial form, then collects them into volumes. Citlali notes that a serial takes about two weeks to reach Natlan and about a year before it is collected as a single volume. It also prints comics, guidebooks and military-strategy works such as the Teyvat Art of War, which Kokomi loves. It sells collectable first and deluxe editions and gives away early prints as gifts, as Miko did for the Legion of Soulblazers. It keeps in touch with authors by mail, even for those who never show up in person.
### LARGE +
Editors give authors advice by letter, often as suggestions on future subjects. Aratani sent Chang the Ninth a pile of "creative suggestions" with his invitation. The Hina column is a running service in which readers write letters and receive replies.
### ULTRA +
Release events are carefully planned. For the festival launch of A Legend of Sword, Onodera wanted signed copies, but Zhenyu's handwriting was poor, so Onodera and the author took printed volumes from the warehouse to Onodera's house to sign by hand over a long stretch (wiki profile).

## history
### STANDARD
The house is no more than 500 years old. Ei, who withdrew into the Plane of Euthymia about that long ago, says there was no Yae Publishing House at the time. By the time of the Traveler's first visit to Inazuma the house was a giant of the industry and a lively bookshop. During the Sakoku Decree (before Transient Dreams) it could not invite outlander authors or send them royalties. Afterwards its editors eagerly planned visits.
### LARGE +
**This Novel Is Amazing! (1.0).** A Liyue commission has the Traveler carry Chang the Ninth's entry to the Feiyun Commerce Guild for shipping to Inazuma. Xu says he cannot imagine Chang winning against the Inazuman craze for "A Legend of Sword".

**Reborn as Raiden and the Ei visit (2.1).** Ei visits the shop in To Traverse the Mortal Plane and reads some of its recent winners to understand mortal fiction.

**Friendship in Writing (2.5).** Miko uses her editors, Junkichi and Satomi to write a new hit novel in a hurry, with the Traveler as a consultant.

**Hues of the Violet Garden (2.6).** The house co-runs the Irodori Festival with the Yashiro Commission and has booths, including a children's books stall.
### ULTRA +
**Sumeru (3.2).** Miko and Souka attend the Nilotpala Cup beast tamers tournament (Fabulous Fungus Frenzy), partly for inspiration.

**Nod-Krai (6.2).** A letter from Mr. Nine brings Onodera to Mondstadt to commission the Traveler and Fischl in the Witch's Homework quest. The same letters reveal royalties she had set aside for Chang the Ninth.

**Soulblazers' Snowbound Journey (6.2, limited-time).** Miko gifts first-edition prints of The Soulblazer Saga to the fan group in Mondstadt and muses about another reprint.

## relations
### LARGE
The house is closely tied to its founder's other posts. The Grand Narukami Shrine is where Miko is Guuji, and readers see no clash between her two jobs. The Yashiro Commission, headed by the Kamisato Clan, co-runs the Irodori Festival with the house. The Shogun Ei is both Miko's friend and one of the house's unlikely readers. Outside Inazuma, the Feiyun Commerce Guild helps ship manuscripts from Liyue, and readers in Natlan, Fontaine and Liyue follow its serials.
### ULTRA +
Authors from abroad matter to the house. Its editors like Chang the Ninth (Liyue) and Zhenyu/Xingqiu (Liyue), and have even asked Albedo to work with them. The house's wider audience includes Kokomi and Citlali, both fans.

## reputation
### LITE
Inazuma's publishing giant: famed for light novels, bestsellers and the "This Novel Is Amazing!" contest. Fans make pilgrimages to buy its books.
### STANDARD +
Kuroda says many light-novel readers from other islands travel to the shop, and calls it a "pilgrimage". Kokomi names the house's military-strategy publications as her favourites, and Citlali says she has read almost every novel it has published. Not every reader is impressed: Chang the Ninth's editor Aratani calls his themes slightly dated, though his book sells steadily.
### LARGE +
Citlali also treasures an old house serial, "Mirage Warriors", which was started by Nakajima Jun and later continued by other authors up to 200 years ago.

## secrets
@version: 2.3
@spoiler: minor
### LARGE
"Ms. Hina", the house's beloved advice columnist (the "That's Life" column), is Gorou. He agreed to write replies after an editor told him that only he could do it. Hina's fan club does not know. The Traveler hides the truth from him and from the fans.
### ULTRA +
Mr. Nine, one of the house's most popular authors, is in truth a female Seelie, formerly an Angel, who is a friend of Nicole. Nicole has ideas for stories but dislikes writing, so Mr. Nine possesses writers during sleep, including Chang the Ninth, to write the manuscripts. Nicole came up with the "Mr. Nine" persona, and the house deals with the author only by letter.

## status
### LITE
Active and thriving as of Version 7.1. Miko still runs it, the bookshop in Inazuma City still sells its books, and the contest is still held.
### STANDARD +
No canon shows any change in the house's standing in the 6.x or 7.0-7.1 content checked. Its latest appearances in canon are the 6.2 Witch's Homework quest with Onodera and the Soulblazers event gift.

## entry: Kuroda
@keys: Kuroda
@keys2: Yae Publishing House, publishing house, bookshop, editor
@version: 2.0
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
Kuroda is the executive editor of Yae Publishing House and runs its bookshop in his spare time. He edits Chang the Ninth, wishes he had gone to Liyue to chase him for manuscripts during the Sakoku Decree, and wanted the author to use a flashier pen name. He knows the collection value of the house's books. For the Traveler's friend Citlali he handed over a first edition of "Shogun Almighty: Reborn as Raiden With Unlimited Power" and the limited deluxe edition of "Pretty Please, Kitsune Guuji?", which are no longer sold. He is nervous around Miko and, when Ei visited, worried she was there to inspect regulations or fire safety.
### LARGE +
Kuroda is also the staff member who deals with the Hina column's business. When the Traveler asked for an endorsement, he said Ms. Hina rarely does them, but would make an exception for the Guuji. He also edits the "King of Invokations" novel, which is why he studies the card game.

## entry: This Novel Is Amazing contest
@keys: This Novel Is Amazing, Novel Is Amazing contest, novel-writing competition, writing contest
@keys2: Yae Publishing House
@version: 1.0
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
The house's recurring writing contest is famous across Teyvat. Past winners are invited to serve as judges. Chang the Ninth entered and won with his martial-arts novel, "First Apprentice of Guhua". The title is a nod to a real-world light-novel guidebook. In Yae Publishing House's Invitation (2.0), Aratani sends gifts and letters to Chang the Ninth and Zhenyu to ask them to judge.
### LARGE +
Xu notes in the first commission that Chang's entry would struggle, since another Liyue author's "A Legend of Sword" was the rage in Inazuma. That author is Xingqiu, who did not know.

## entry: A Legend of Sword
@keys: A Legend of Sword, Zhenyu, Calx
@keys2: Xingqiu, Yae Publishing House, Inazuma
@version: 2.0
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
"A Legend of Sword" is a six-volume space-opera adventure by Zhenyu, illustrated by Calx. Zhenyu is Xingqiu's pen name. Liyue publishers had rejected it, so Xingqiu printed a few copies privately and left them at Wanwen Bookhouse. A merchant carried one to Inazuma, where it was a smash hit and later reached Fontaine. It sits at the top of the bestseller list. Xingqiu is surprised to learn this. Kokomi is an enthusiastic reader.
### LARGE +
Albedo's voice-over reveals the house asked him to switch authors for more royalties. The wiki credits "Calx" to Albedo, but his voice-over does not confirm it. Volume 5 is found at the Yashiro Commission headquarters rather than in the shop.

## entry: Ms. Hina and That's Life
@keys: Ms. Hina, That's Life
@keys2: Gorou, advice column, fan club
@version: 2.3
@availability: permanent
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
"That's Life" is a magazine whose advice column is answered by "Ms. Hina". Readers love her, and a fan club of three has formed in Inazuma City. Its club rules are not to pry into her identity, not to say controversial things and not to push the column on the uninterested. Gorou secretly writes the replies. He enjoys it, he says, because letters let readers treat him as a friend, unlike soldiers who are reserved around a general.
### LARGE +
The house keeps Hina away from publicity and she declines most promotional requests. When the Traveler needs her endorsement in Friendship in Writing, Kuroda says that has to come through Miko's request.

### STANDARD
In the Witch's Homework quest "Alan Smithee, Author of Fischl", Mr. Nine, in danger in Nod-Krai, writes to the house. ### STANDARD
During the limited-time event Hues of the Violet Garden, the house co-hosted Ritou's Irodori Festival with the Yashiro Commission. Murata ran its official stall, Fukumoto a children's book booth, and Onodera planned the release party of A Legend of Sword.
