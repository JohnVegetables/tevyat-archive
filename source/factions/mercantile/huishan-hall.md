## summary
### LITE
Huishan Hall is one of the Eight Trades under the Liyue Qixing. It is in charge of the mining industry and of matters concerning the Chasm, the great mining region north of Liyue Harbor.
### STANDARD +
Mingyun Village's billboard states its remit. After the Chasm Delvers events, the Chasm Exploration Team became an official part of the Hall.

## structure
### STANDARD
Canon shows only the working level: Uncle Dai is a foreman, and unnamed higher-ups direct research. No head is named and no headquarters is given. The Hall answers to the Qixing like the other Halls.
### LARGE +
Zhiqiong, who knew the Chasm's passages, was offered a post as consultant and guide through the Ministry of Civil Affairs, but she left before the news reached her. She asked in her letter that Muning keep the offer open in case she returns. Her place in the Hall is therefore potential only.

## goals
### STANDARD
The Hall's concern is productive mining, the safety of its workers, and understanding what the Chasm holds. The rare Archaic Stones from the Chasm interest its higher-ups, who are studying them.

## methods
### STANDARD
The Hall hires miners and foremen, sets work rules and commissions specialists. One rule forbids workers from eating the plants and mushrooms growing in the underground mines, because they may harm the human body. It also had Muning, a Ministry of Civil Affairs specialist, commissioned to find Archaic Stones and bring them back.
### LARGE +
Item text says the changes in the Chasm have lowered the quality of the few Archaic Stones that remain.

## history
### LARGE
The Hall appears from version 1.0 (Mingyun Village notices). After the events of The Chasm Delvers, the Chasm Exploration Team joined it officially, as stated in the quest Wherefore Did the Spiritstone Descend?.
### ULTRA +
The Eight Trades page notes that the English "Hall" follows the Chinese word for a government office. Canon records no earlier leadership, budgets or disputes for the Hall.

## relations
### STANDARD
- **Chasm Exploration Team**: now part of the Hall.
- **Ministry of Civil Affairs**: Muning, one of its specialists, was commissioned to find Archaic Stones.
- **Eight Trades and the Liyue Qixing**: parent bodies.
### LARGE +
- **Zhiqiong**: associated with the Hall only through the offer described above.
