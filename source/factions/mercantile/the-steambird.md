## summary
### LITE
The Steambird is the main newspaper of the Court of Fontaine, read across Teyvat. Its office stands in Quartier Narbonnais, and its Chief Editor is Euphrasie. Its best-known reporter is Charlotte. Besides news it runs a reader-submission column, and it organises the La Verite Prize for journalism.
### STANDARD +
The paper covers everything from celebrity gossip and opera to politics, crime and science. Its pitch on the Newsstand Board is "your credible source of the unbelievable truth". Euphrasie says a good newspaper should be a lighthouse of truth. The paper also works as a community notice board through its "Tales of Humanity" column, which is the in-game explanation for Fontaine's Reputation system.
### LARGE +
Citizens bring it both scoops and troubles: readers write in, reporters chase leads across Fontaine, and the Maison Gardiennage and Palais Mermonia use it to publicise wanted notices. Other publications compete for readers, such as La Gouttiere (also sold at the Newsstand Board), and, per Charlotte, "The Observer" for celebrity gossip and "Leisure Weekly".

## structure
### STANDARD
The paper is led by Chief Editor Euphrasie. Beneath her is a staff of reporters who each cover an area, plus a photographer, a reviewer, a columnist and a trainee. Sir Arthur, apparently a mechanical newscaller, stands in Quartier Narbonnais shouting headlines and special issues. The office, called the main office in an item description, is in Quartier Narbonnais of the Court of Fontaine.
### LARGE +
Reporters have distinct beats. Chronie covers political affairs, Ethan covers real estate, Charlotte is a field and special correspondent, and Staelle writes a romance-novel column. Delerue is a reviewer, Antila a photographer, Gaston a trainee reporter, and Paban is listed as a "newsy". Galanopoulo, Herbier and Houallet are also listed as reporters. The wiki gives these roles from NPC profiles and map descriptions; most staff have no detailed profile.
The Serenitea Pot furnishing "Court of Justice's Authenticity" shares its model with the main office: a metal-and-glass structure with a circular centre divided into eight panels and a blue glass roof. Its description says sunlight is often likened to truth and justice, and that the paper may have chosen the design to remind people entering and leaving of their duty to be a credible source.
### ULTRA +
Copy is produced under daily deadlines. In a voice-over the Traveler describes editors correcting spelling, squeezing text into columns and begging colleagues for five more minutes, while Paimon imagines a "magic printer" doing it instead. That is the Traveler's description, not a staff account. Some articles carry no individual byline: the Dollander exposé in Charlotte's story was credited to "The Steambird Reporters".

## leadership
### LITE
Euphrasie is the Chief Editor. She runs the paper, assigns and recalls reporters such as Charlotte, and deals with the complaints and threats that follow. She speaks formally and with dry humour.
### STANDARD +
Euphrasie also posts the paper's commissions for locals' everyday troubles, helps the Palais Mermonia put up wanted posters for criminals and dangerous monsters issued by the Maison Gardiennage, and uses the paper's sources to find the right people for jobs. An earlier Chief Editor, Nellie Cochran, interviewed Alain Guillotin. In Charlotte's recruitment story, Euphrasie personally approached Charlotte to ask her to become a special correspondent.
### LARGE +
Per Charlotte's profile, Euphrasie has repeatedly sent Charlotte on faraway field assignments to keep her away from the Court of Fontaine until the Maison Gardiennage or the Special Security and Surveillance Patrol have cleared up whatever she stirred up. Captain Chevreuse is quoted there trading exasperated words with her over a trip Charlotte made to Romaritime Harbor that nearly ended with her being thrown into the sea.

## members
### STANDARD
Key people: Euphrasie (Chief Editor), Charlotte (reporter and special correspondent), Sir Arthur (newscaller), and columnists such as Mona. Nellie Cochran is a historical Chief Editor. Charlotte's father Galanopoulo, a veteran reporter and nature photographer, is listed among the staff, and the paper first published the photos he and Charlotte took.
### LARGE +
Mona writes the astrology column "All Things Astrological". Her predecessor wished to retire and recommended her to the editor; her academic style drew readers interested in astrology's details. The Traveler is credited as the columnist "Ryuji-kun" for the real-world "Teyvat Food Notes" blog posts on the official Chinese site, a marketing tie-in outside the story. Chronie, a political reporter, appears in the Fontaine Research Institute Chronicles quest and can later be found at Cafe Lutece.

## goals
### STANDARD
The paper's stated purpose is to keep information flowing so that everyone can understand what is happening around them, and then to let readers express themselves through "Tales of Humanity". Euphrasie calls it a lighthouse illuminating humanity. Practically, it also needs to be read: its pages mix serious reports with gossip because, as the Traveler puts it, trivial stories grab attention and sell copies.

## methods
### LARGE
Reporters pursue stories in person. Charlotte's creed is that nearer to the action is closer to the truth. The Traveler's account of the layout is that the front page leads with opera and actor news, early pages carry positive local news such as falling prices and aquabus progress, and bigger stories run later or as multi-day series. Sir Arthur advertises special editions in the street. When a reporter is threatened, Euphrasie says the paper has its own ways of dealing with it and would have gone bust long ago if it could not handle a tussle or two.
### ULTRA +
Examples of the paper's range from the Newsstand Board and Sir Arthur: a profile of a crowbar-wielding honored citizen detective, a report that a diver's discovery turned an old drowning into a murder case, a Knowledge Section piece on rising-water panic quoting Fontaine Research Institute researchers who say Fontaine would be submerged in about a hundred thousand years, and a Culture and Arts piece on a custom timepiece made for Furina. Sir Arthur also advertises interviews with the president of Spina di Rosula about rebuilding aquabus lines. Charlotte's own work includes, by her account, a six-month undercover investigation of a merchant selling shoddy food.

## history
### LARGE
The paper is old. A past Chief Editor, Nellie Cochran, interviewed Alain Guillotin about his early years, which the wiki dates by inference to roughly 400-500 years ago. In the present day, the wiki, citing quests, says the paper covered the Traveler's fight with Dvalin (headlines for three weeks, with expert interviews and hand-drawn illustrations) and Kazuha blocking the Raiden Shogun's attack, a topic that held Fontaine's imagination for a while. The Traveler did not know about the first of these.
Charlotte became a Steambird reporter after her father sent in her photographs. The editors praised them, and Euphrasie then asked her to become a special correspondent. Charlotte's La Verite exposé and her long absence after investigating the diving employer Dollander are told in her character stories.
### ULTRA +
During the Fontaine Archon Quest, Sir Arthur's headlines follow events. He announces the Great Magician Lyney's show at Opera Epiclese, the return of a legendary bandit, abolition of departments at the Fontaine Research Institute after its disaster, and a report that the Traveler had been arrested after an incident at the Palais Mermonia. Later he announces the floods subsiding, the Wingalet, rumours that Furina saved everyone by plan, and the arrests after an attack attempt on the Clementine Line. These lines are gated in-game by quest progress; the wiki ties them to specific quests rather than to dates.

## relations
### LARGE
The Steambird works closely with law and order: the Maison Gardiennage once posted a guard detail at its entrance while Charlotte lived in the office, and the paper helps the Palais Mermonia advertise wanted people. It competes for readers with La Gouttiere and other papers. It is connected to Yae Publishing House through the Great Detective Hurlock series, which Paimon says the publishing house will print. The Traveler explains that the paper cannot easily publish it itself, because Yae has the best illustrators on its payroll.
### ULTRA +
In Nod-Krai (6.x), Paimon asks whether the Nasha Express is as full of juicy stories as The Steambird, and later says a stack of free newspapers had more ads than The Steambird and the Nod-Krai papers combined. These are casual comparisons; no Nod-Krai or Snezhnaya storyline involves the paper itself.

## reputation
### LARGE
The Steambird is trusted enough that readers send it their problems, and Charlotte's profile records that it has also faced accusations of favouritism around its prize. Charlotte says in a voice-over that it has many outstanding journalists, an open-minded Chief Editor and, most importantly, pays its writers on time. Paimon treats it as the model of a gossip-filled paper.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 The Steambird is active in the Court of Fontaine with Euphrasie as Chief Editor. Sir Arthur, Charlotte and the columns continue as before.
### STANDARD +
Euphrasie's Reputation menu and the "Tales of Humanity" commissions remain available, and the La Verite Prize is still described in the Opera Epiclese bulletin. Nothing checked indicates that The Steambird changed in 6.x or in 7.0-7.1, and it plays no part in Snezhnaya content.

## entry: Euphrasie
@keys: Euphrasie, Chief Editor of The Steambird, Editor-in-Chief of The Steambird
### STANDARD
Euphrasie is the Chief Editor of The Steambird and can be found in Quartier Narbonnais. Her map description says she posts commissions for local residents' troubles, helps the Palais Mermonia put up wanted posters for criminals and monsters the Maison Gardiennage wants, and uses the paper's sources to find the best-suited people. In the Steambird Interview quest (after Chapter IV Act II) she recruits the Traveler as a "troubleshooter" for Tales of Humanity, which unlocks Fontaine's Reputation menu. She is formal and wry and given to sighing, and she claims to have settled the Traveler's suitability before the interview began. Her idle line says a good newspaper should be a lighthouse of truth.
### LARGE
She is the one who repeatedly pulls Charlotte back from danger. When the Traveler asks about Charlotte, she first fears someone has come to make a threat, then says the paper has its own ways of dealing with that.

## entry: Sir Arthur
@keys: Sir Arthur
@keys2: Steambird, Fontaine, Narbonnais, newspaper
### STANDARD
Sir Arthur is the Steambird's herald in Quartier Narbonnais. He cries "Extra, extra!" followed by headlines, and says "beep-boop". His content changes with story progress and ranges from opera and exhibition notices to crime news, rumours and special issues. He once announced that his regular maintenance had been completed and asked the public not to believe rumours that he had been stolen and split into three. The wiki lists his title as "The Steambird". He appears to be a machine, but canon does not say so outright.

## entry: Tales of Humanity
@keys: Tales of Humanity
### STANDARD
Tales of Humanity is the Steambird's reader-submission column, begun so that people could express themselves and not only read. Euphrasie says it is "drowning in all manner of content" and that this is acceptable, because readers also want light reading and helping them is good for the paper's reputation. Many locals use it to post commissions, which Euphrasie coordinates; small jobs feel like splinters to those who write in. It is the in-game basis for Fontaine's Reputation system: finishing commissions raises the Traveler's standing, and Euphrasie later offers harder jobs.

## entry: La Verite Prize
@keys: La Verite Prize, La Verite Award
### STANDARD
The La Verite Prize is organised by The Steambird and, per Charlotte's profile, funded by it. The Opera Epiclese bulletin calls it the most prestigious media award in all of Teyvat. It honours the best individual and collective achievements in newspapers, magazines, journals and other media, with a ceremony held at the Opera Epiclese.
### LARGE
Charlotte's profile says she won it several times in a row, which detractors blamed on collusion between the paper and the selection committee. She then published an exposé of the committee's long-running money dealings and unfair judgments, and also wrote about figures at the paper tied to the committee. The Maison Gardiennage investigated for three months and punished unjust judges, undeserving contestants and editors seeking favours. Charlotte stayed in the office meanwhile, Euphrasie turned visitors away, and a guard detail was posted at the entrance.

## entry: Nellie Cochran
@keys: Nellie Cochran, Madame Cochran
### LARGE
Nellie Cochran was a Chief Editor of The Steambird in the past. In Alain Guillotin's text she asks him about his early years, and the surrounding text describes him as a lonely, reclusive man. The wiki assumes she lived 400-500 years ago because she interviewed him; this is an inference. The unnamed Steambird editor who appears at a banquet in the same texts is not confirmed to be her.

## entry: All Things Astrological
@keys: All Things Astrological
@keys2: Mona, Steambird
### LARGE
"All Things Astrological" is the Steambird's astrology column. Its previous columnist wanted to retire and recommended Mona to the editor. Her writing was more academic and complicated, which attracted readers interested in the intricacies of astrology. In Owe Mora, Pay Mora she has written a manuscript for the paper on celestial shifts and their relation to a person's changing fates.

## entry: Newsstand Board
@keys: Newsstand Board
@keys2: Steambird, La Gouttiere, Narbonnais
### STANDARD
The Newsstand Board is a news board in Quartier Narbonnais, beside Angelique near Hubel's shop. It pitches the Steambird as "your credible source of the unbelievable truth" and notes that the stand also sells La Gouttiere and other magazines. Its messages rotate. Samples include the crowbar-wielding honored citizen detective, a reopened drowning case, the rising-water panic, and a verdict report by Charlotte on the trial after the serial disappearances case. La Gouttiere's pieces are gossip in tone, for example on Clorinde and on the Fontaine Research Institute explosion investigation.

## entry: Great Detective Hurlock series
@keys: Great Detective Hurlock, Hurlock series
### LARGE
Great Detective Hurlock is a detective series connected to The Steambird. Paimon says Yae Publishing House will publish it and asks why the paper does not print it itself. The Traveler replies that a successful light novel depends on illustrations, and that Yae has the best illustrators under contract. Detective stories such as Hurlock and The Case Files of Miss Orith are popular in Fontaine. Authorship and other in-world details are not given in the pages checked.
