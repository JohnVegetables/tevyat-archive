## summary
### LITE
The Voynich Guild is a commerce guild of local merchants based in Nasha Town, Nod-Krai, and one of the most powerful forces in the region. With no government in Nasha Town, it is the nearest thing to a regulator. It manages trade deals, imports and exports, business certificates and many disputes.
### STANDARD +
Nefer, owner of the Curatorium of Secrets, is a member, and the Curatorium's headquarters stands directly behind the Guild's. Its representative in the Nod-Krai League is Tarno.

## structure
### STANDARD
No guildmaster, charter or rank structure is given in canon. Tarno is the manager of its Nasha Town souvenir outlet. According to Zinaida's intel text, the Guild also hired Zinaida, a former Seahook Gang member, as a covert bodyguard for Manez, the port supervisor. The Guild keeps a bulletin board in Nasha Town for business notices.
### LARGE +
The bulletin board's welcome note says the Guild created the board so that public opinion could be heard in an authoritative and convenient way, with no restriction on the content or format of posts. A disclaimer says the Guild accepts no legal or financial liability for what is posted and offers no mediation of its own for disputes arising from the board.

## leadership
### STANDARD
Leadership is unnamed. After the crisis in Nod-Krai, Tarno, the outlet manager, became the Guild's representative in the League. He hosted the League's opening ceremony and gave its welcome address in that role.
### LARGE +
In his speech, Tarno spoke of the unity and cooperation learned from the crisis and of sailing toward prosperity, freedom and hope. The wiki lists Nefer as a Guild member, with no stated rank. The story never says who chose Tarno, and Illuga privately wonders whether Tarno was pushed onto the stage by bigger names.

## members
### STANDARD
Known members and staff are Nefer, who is also the owner of the Curatorium of Secrets, and Tarno, listed as a "Voynich Guild" employee. The Guild issues certifications: Sarin says a bounty hunter lost his Guild certification over his old habits (limited-time event, Clink Clank Clash).
### LARGE +
A man named Ashvapati joined the Guild during the 6.5 trade-expo event with a private agenda and later admitted that he had nearly ruined its reputation (limited-time event).

## goals
### STANDARD
The Guild's stated aim, in its bulletin-board announcement, is to promote economic activity in Nasha Town. It hopes people will find endless business opportunities there.
### LARGE +
Beyond trade, the Guild appears interested in technology. It developed a hovering daybed using artifacts from the pioneering age, though it has been unable to fully reverse-engineer the original and the daybed remains somewhat unstable.

## methods
### STANDARD
The Guild issues business certificates, manages trade deals, handles imports and exports, and investigates and resolves disputes when its name is called in. A merchant who was robbed just before reaching the city was told to go to the Guild. They brought back his stolen goods and bought them all, in a story the merchant told as an example of the Guild's reach (limited web event, 2024).
### LARGE +
The Guild also enforces order at its own events. At the annual Nod-Krai Trade Expo, which it hosts, violence is forbidden. Outside authorities cannot make arrests inside the venue without breaking the Guild's rule, so they act outside it (limited-time event, 6.5).
### ULTRA +
Its Nasha Town outlet is a souvenir shop. Gameplay note: Tarno sells weapon ascension materials, Mora and furnishing blueprints for Luna Sigils, once all Meeting Points and Ancient Moon's Remnants reach their top level.

## history
### STANDARD
Canon gives no founding history. The Guild first appears in the Marvelous Merchandise web event (2024), where a traveling seller describes Nod-Krai as a wild place that plays by its own rules and the Guild as the strongest force in the area. Its own members first appear in 6.0 (Luna I).
### LARGE +
In a mail dated 2025-08-26, sent before the Traveler's trip to Nod-Krai, Ningguang advised the Traveler that the Voynich Guild was a significant piece on Nod-Krai's chessboard and an accord with it might be worth considering. In 6.0 the Moonchanter Lauma sought a joint investigation with the Guild after the Moon Marrow was stolen. After the True Moon crisis (6.3), the Guild took a seat in the newly formed League.
### ULTRA +
In 6.5, in the Where Waves Meet the Reef event, the Guild's trade expo drew guests from across Teyvat, including an arms syndicate that attracted investigators from Liyue and Fontaine. The Guild's no-violence rule shaped how it was dismantled.

## relations
### STANDARD
**Curatorium of Secrets:** closely linked, since Nefer is a member and the two headquarters are back to back. **Frostmoon Scions:** Lauma approached the Guild for a joint investigation of the stolen Moon Marrow. **League:** Tarno represents the Guild. **Others:** the Guild is one of several factions in Nasha Town's power balance, alongside the Curatorium, Speranza and the Fatui.
### LARGE +
The sources checked say nothing definite about how the Guild deals with the Fatui or the Adventurers' Guild. Tarno speaks warmly of the League's ideals of unity, suggesting the Guild supports it.

## reputation
### STANDARD
Liben was told it is the strongest force in the area, and his experience bore that out. Ningguang considers it a significant player worth cultivating.
### LARGE +
Its bulletin board carries a blunt liability disclaimer. In Clink Clank Clash a bounty hunter is said to have lost his certification, which suggests the Guild enforces its standards.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1, the Voynich Guild remains Nasha Town's leading merchant body, with Tarno speaking for it in the League.
### STANDARD +
The sources checked show no Voynich Guild story content in 7.0 or 7.1.

## entry: Nod-Krai Trade Expo
@keys: Nod-Krai Trade Expo, trade expo
@keys2: Voynich Guild, Nasha Town
@version: 6.5
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
The Nod-Krai Trade Expo is an annual event hosted by the Voynich Guild in Nasha Town. In the Where Waves Meet the Reef event (6.5, April to May 2026), Nefer asks the Traveler to help Jahoda handle VIP guests. Guests include Beidou, captain of the Crux fleet, and Navia, who leads an underworld organisation. The Guild forbids violence at the venue, so officials from Liyue and Fontaine had to wait before arresting an arms syndicate that had taken an interest in the expo. Nefer refuses to let customers be seized at the expo, so Chevreuse notes that only deals made through the expo are protected; Nefer agrees she cannot interfere outside it, on condition that the Traveler takes part in the operation.

### STANDARD
During the Marvelous Merchandise web event (November 2024), a seller named Liben describes his trip to Nod-Krai. He was robbed by vagrants before even entering Nasha Town, where he found no courts of law or Knights of Favonius. Someone told him to visit the Voynich Guild, the strongest force in the area. The Guild recovered his goods and then bought everything from him. He was robbed again on the way out.
