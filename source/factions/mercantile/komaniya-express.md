## summary
### LITE
Komaniya Express is a well-known delivery company based in Inazuma. It is widely praised for punctual and safe delivery, and it has recently begun expanding its service overseas. Its best-known employee is Kirara, a nekomata and the company's Gold Level Courier.
### STANDARD +
Business has been good enough that the company has hired more staff. Its couriers carry a logo at the waist, which Kaedehara Kazuha recognised when he met Kirara abroad.

## structure
### STANDARD
Canon shows a simple structure: an owner or boss, couriers, a warehouse with an "Undelivered Packages" section, and a customer feedback board where clients can leave star ratings. There is a Gold Level Courier title, created for Kirara. The company has an office in Inazuma City, where customers can bring packages.
### LARGE +
The owner is unnamed. When a customer asks for the neko courier, the owner calls Kirara over as though it were an everyday occurrence. The owner once privately offered Kirara higher pay and quieter secretarial work, which she turned down.

## leadership
### STANDARD
The owner is not named. They are shown as supportive. Kirara's profile says they never considered letting her go, and they designed the Gold Level Courier title overnight when they heard she had delivered every parcel in the Undelivered Packages section.

## members
### LITE
Kirara is the one employee canon names: a youkai (a nekomata) who loves her job and human society, and whose reputation has done much for the company.
### STANDARD +
Kirara is the sole courier handling international deliveries, because she is the quickest of the staff. She sometimes changes back into her original cat form and climbs over walls to keep to a schedule. Her profile says she takes a polite, friendly attitude with clients; customers describe her as safe, punctual and vivacious.

### ULTRA +
Her Character Stories add details about how she works. Her tails sway wildly when she lies, so she is a poor liar. She suppresses cat habits such as sleeping in boxes and pouncing on small animals while in human form. She treats each parcel as precious and keeps her cat form for emergencies because it attracts attention. If anyone tries to take a parcel that is not theirs, or touches her tails, she will make them regret it.

## goals
### STANDARD
The company's evident aims are fast, careful delivery, customer satisfaction and growth into other nations. Kirara's own motive, which the company benefits from, is that she treats every delivery as an outing and wants to keep her job.

## methods
### STANDARD
Couriers deliver parcels on foot and across rooftops, and ask recipients for a signature. Kirara tracks down the right recipient for stranded packages in her spare time, so the "Undelivered Packages" section of the warehouse ends up empty. Customers can rate service on a feedback board.
### LARGE +
Bandits have tried to rob Kirara on the road, according to her Character Story. She sends them to the Tenryou Commission in packages labelled "Meanies". The company has also handled orders such as clothing between Inazuma and Fontaine.

## history
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
**Before Kirara.** The company had a long reputation in Inazuma as the most reliable delivery service; asked which firm is most dependable, people there mention Komaniya Express first.

**Kirara joins.** After Yae Miko, the Lady Kitsune of the Grand Narukami Shrine, saw how much Kirara enjoyed the streets of Inazuma City, she recommended that she seek work at Komaniya Express. Miko's reasoning was that to blend into human society one must work like an ordinary human.

**Overseas expansion (3.7).** The company recently began delivering to other nations. Kirara's first appearances abroad are in Mondstadt, where she spoke with Diona at the Cat's Tail, and in Liyue Harbor, where she delivered to Hu Tao.
### LARGE +
**Teyvat's Special Delivery (5.0).** In a fourth-anniversary web event, Kirara said Komaniya Express had set up temporary locations elsewhere, that orders had gone through the roof, and that she worried about delayed packages. She asked the Traveler for help delivering them across Mondstadt, Liyue and Inazuma.

## relations
### STANDARD
**Yae Miko.** Kirara's mentor in human society. Miko says packages addressed to Guuji Yae at the Grand Narukami Shrine must be treated as top priority.

**Tenryou Commission.** One of the addresses Kirara knows best, since she climbed the Tenshukaku roof in her first days in Inazuma City, and it receives the bandits she delivers.

**Chiori.** An old customer in Fontaine, an Inazuman designer who dressed Kirara in her current outfit. Kirara's deliveries to Fontaine are in part deliveries to her.

**Hu Tao.** A customer in Liyue who asked whether the company could deliver living people or those in between. Kirara's tails stood on end.

## reputation
### STANDARD
Komaniya Express is the best-regarded courier firm in Inazuma. Typical customer feedback is "safe and punctual", "service assured" and "vivacious and adorable". Kirara's tireless record has raised the firm's renown, and no package is undeliverable.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 Komaniya Express remains in business, now offering overseas delivery. Kirara handles all international orders.
### STANDARD +
No 6.x or 7.x story content found in this research changes the company's status.

## entry: Kirara and the Gold Level Courier title
@keys: Gold Level Courier, Undelivered Packages
@keys2: Komaniya Express, Kirara
### STANDARD
When Kirara first arrived she noticed that some packages could not be delivered because of wrong addresses, moved recipients or wrong names. She quietly recorded the details and used her routes and spare time to find each recipient. The warehouse's Undelivered Packages section became empty, the owner at first thought they had been robbed, and the owner created the title Gold Level Courier for her.
### LARGE +
Her Character Stories add that she keeps working because she likes it: at the end of a day she climbs somewhere high and listens to the market. Her fear of being fired is that she would have to return to being a wild youkai in the mountains.

## entry: Kirara in Liyue Harbor (Summoners' Summit)

@keys2: Liyue Harbor, Wangsheng Funeral Parlor, Hu Tao, Duel! The Summoners' Summit!
@version: 3.7

### STANDARD
During the limited-time event Duel! The Summoners' Summit!, in Roll! A Sea of New Acquaintances, the Traveler, Paimon and Kazuha met Kirara in Liyue Harbor. She was lost on her first delivery there. She explained that Komaniya Express had just begun overseas service and that she now effectively travels the world as part of her job. She was looking for the Wangsheng Funeral Parlor, where she asked Hu Tao for a signature and a five-star review.
