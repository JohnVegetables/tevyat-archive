## summary
### LITE
Bubu Pharmacy is the pharmacy and clinic that Liyue calls the finest in the land, set below Yujing Terrace in Liyue Harbor. Dr. Baizhu owns it and treats patients there. His apprentice Qiqi, an undead girl, gathers herbs, and the pharmacist Herbalist Gui makes up the medicines and minds the shop. It is especially busy during and after the Lantern Rite.
### STANDARD +
Its Chinese name, Bùbǔ-lú, means "Not-Divining Hut". A nursery rhyme spread through Feiyun Slope sings that Dr. Baizhu's skill cures every illness but his medicines are terribly bitter, and parents use it to scare children into staying healthy.

## structure
### LITE
Baizhu owns and leads the pharmacy. Qiqi works as an apprentice and herb gatherer, and Herbalist Gui is the pharmacist behind the counter.
### STANDARD +
Gui is still in training, so he will not hand out the stronger ingredients without a prescription. Because Baizhu is frail, Gui is also the one who puts the medicines together. When Baizhu and Qiqi are out gathering herbs, Gui keeps the shop. The roof has a viewpoint over Yujing Terrace, and the door on the eastern side leads to a back room with living quarters.
### LARGE +
The shop sells herbs and a few recipes to customers: plants such as Qingxin, Horsetail and Violetgrass, and recipes for soups like Jade Fruit Soup (a gameplay shop, restocked every few days). Gui's reason for refusing some herbs is that medicine can help or harm depending only on the dose.

## leadership
### LITE
Dr. Baizhu runs the pharmacy. He is courteous, says he treats every patient alike regardless of wealth or status, and is rarely seen without his white snake Changsheng.
### STANDARD +
Liyue's citizens trust him. Keqing considers him the right person to see when symptoms cannot be identified, and Xinyan is grateful for his voice-preserving medicine. A sickly young girl, Hongdou, dislikes his medicine but visits because his kindness makes her feel better. Gui credits Baizhu with the pharmacy's endless stream of customers.
### ULTRA +
Baizhu's own goal sits behind the clinic. He is the latest holder of a generational contract with Changsheng and seeks immortality so that he will be the last to bear it. He has remarked that a poison to one patient can be a boon to another, and that he does not explain such details to patients who might be frightened by them.

## members
### LITE
- Baizhu: owner and physician.
- Qiqi: apprentice and herb gatherer, a zombie raised by the adepti.
- Herbalist Gui: pharmacist and shopkeeper, still completing his training.
### STANDARD +
Qiqi's memory is poor and she keeps a notebook. In her profile she wandered into the hills to gather herbs out of an old habit, met Baizhu, and was taken in even though she could not sort the herbs. Baizhu would go and find her whenever she forgot to come back at night. Changsheng, Baizhu's snake, is a regular presence.

## methods
### STANDARD
The pharmacy runs as a clinic and a herb shop. Baizhu sees patients, diagnoses and prescribes. Gui prepares and sells. Qiqi collects materials. Stronger ingredients need a prescription, while ordinary herbs can be bought freely. According to a boy named Ayu, Baizhu also runs a clinic in Qingce Village during the Lantern Rite.
### LARGE +
Baizhu's skill goes beyond herbs. In The Heart of Healing he diagnosed god remains inside a patient, stabilised the man by lending him some of his own life force through Changsheng's arts, and later had Qiqi prepare an Elixir of Immortality so the patient could say goodbye to his family before it expired.

## history
### LARGE
No source checked records the pharmacy's founding. It appears in the game from 1.0, and Baizhu became playable in 3.6.
### ULTRA +
**Lagenaria Chapter (3.6).** In the story quest The Heart of Healing, a boy named Ayu comes to the pharmacy to find his father, Jialiang. Baizhu, Qiqi, Gui, Hu Tao, the Traveler and Paimon follow the trail to Qingce Village, where the man's illness is traced to the remains of the dead god Chi, which his wife Jiangli had been using to treat his heart condition. Baizhu destroys the remains. The pharmacy is then where Jialiang wakes in an undead state to say his farewells. Hu Tao, whose grandfather's younger brother was Baizhu's master, is wary of Baizhu because of his pursuit of immortality.

## relations
### STANDARD
- **Wangsheng Funeral Parlor**: its director Hu Tao distrusts Baizhu's quest to defy death.
- **The adepti**: their arts preserved a thread of Qiqi's consciousness, which Baizhu says helped him devise a way to save someone near death.
- **Millelith**: in the search for Ayu's father they could not spare many people, so the pharmacy's staff helped look.
### LARGE +
According to Hu Tao's family account, Baizhu's master met a pharmacist in Chenyu Vale, quarrelled with them and left the family business. The family's disapproval of Baizhu's school of medicine, which they say goes against natural rules, stems from there.

## reputation
### STANDARD
Liyue regards Bubu Pharmacy as the finest in the region, and Baizhu's skill is known across Teyvat. His reputation for terribly bitter medicine is a running joke.

## entry: Herbalist Gui
@keys: Herbalist Gui
### STANDARD
Gui is a pharmacist at Bubu Pharmacy and a trainee: he says he has yet to finish his training. He prepares medicine because Baizhu is frail, sells herbs, and refuses restricted ingredients without a prescription. He has known Baizhu to be ill since they first met but does not know the cause. When Shenhe visited Liyue Harbor and asked for herbs, she ate them immediately in front of him, which astonished him.

## entry: Baizhu's nursery rhyme
@keys: Baizhu nursery rhyme, bitter medicine rhyme
@keys2: Bubu, Baizhu, pharmacy
### LARGE
A rhyme widely repeated on Feiyun Slope says that a pharmacy in Liyue called Bubu has a Dr. Baizhu whose skills heal every illness but whose medicines are bitter. Parents use it to warn children not to get sick. Xiangling has also heard that his herbal medicines are extremely bitter.

## entry: The Heart of Healing
@keys: The Heart of Healing, Lagenaria Chapter
@version: 3.6
@spoiler: major
### ULTRA
In this story quest the pharmacy's team and the Traveler help Ayu find his father Jialiang, who is dying from an illness linked to the remains of Chi. Baizhu keeps Jialiang alive and then destroys the remains. Afterwards Jiangli warns Baizhu to use Changsheng's powers wisely. Changsheng explains that she and her host share a contract and that all of her previous hosts died young. Baizhu hopes to learn immortality so he can be the last host and spare both Changsheng and any later inheritor.
