## summary
### LITE
The Communications Office is one of the Eight Trades, the commercial sectors under the jurisdiction of the Liyue Qixing. It is the Hall in charge of transportation: moving goods and shipments around Liyue and arranging overseas shipping.
### STANDARD +
Its staff are officials rather than merchants. The Office also takes on related public works, so it is as much a transport authority as a trade body. Its base is Hezhe Post.

## structure
### STANDARD
The Office is a Hall of the Eight Trades and answers to the Liyue Qixing like the other Halls. It is large because its duties cover more than shipping. No head is named in the sources checked, and no ranks are described. The Sword and Strongbox Secure Transport Agency is listed on the wiki as a sub-faction.
### LARGE +
Known associates are Master Qin "Thunder Hand", a bodyguard, and friends of Yanfei, who are named as members on the Eight Trades page. Treat any further individual as unknown.

## goals
### STANDARD
By its staff's own account, the Office exists to keep goods moving. In the words of a member, good roads, bridges, waterways and docks are critical to getting goods to every part of Liyue.

## methods
### STANDARD
The Office handles shipments and transport within Liyue and overseas shipping logistics, and oversees some construction and road management. An NPC mentions that it plans to build several small lifts, which should improve local access.
### LARGE +
In a scene, expect paperwork, route planning and requests to repair or extend a road or dock, more than market haggling. Outsiders hear of it as the Liyue agency that specialises in overseas shipping.

## history
### LARGE
It first appears in version 1.5 content. Canon gives no founding date or earlier history.
### ULTRA +
The wiki's naming note is a small curiosity: the Chinese name follows the pattern of other government offices, which is why the English version says "Office" while the sister Halls (Huishan, Shenglu, Yinyuan) are called "Halls".

## relations
### STANDARD
- **Eight Trades and the Liyue Qixing**: the Office is one of four Halls named in canon, and the Qixing hold jurisdiction.
- **Sword and Strongbox Secure Transport Agency**: a listed sub-faction.
### LARGE +
- **Yanfei**: her troubles, as told in her voice-overs, involve friends who are members of the Office.
- **Overseas contacts**: foreign visitors know it as the place to arrange shipping from Liyue.
