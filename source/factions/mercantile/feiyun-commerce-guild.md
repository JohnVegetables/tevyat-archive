## summary
### LITE
The Feiyun Commerce Guild is a powerful trading guild in Liyue, run as a family business. It deals in silk, tea and printed books. Its best-known member is Xingqiu, the second son of the Guild Manager, a studious young man with a mischievous streak.
### STANDARD +
The Guild apparently originated at Yilong Wharf in Chenyu Vale and still keeps the family's old home there. Merchants in Liyue Harbor know the family well, and its reputation depends on its children behaving as befits it.

## structure
### LITE
The Guild's leadership is the family: a retired great-grandfather who founded it, a grandfather who is patriarch, a father who manages it, and an elder son who is heir. Xingqiu is the second son.
### STANDARD +
The family estate is a multigenerational household, with servants. The wiki lists Xu, Sheng and Hongxing as servants, Qiuwei as a retailer, Lixin as a messenger at Yilong Wharf, and An Su as a butler. The family members are not named in canon. Xingqiu's older brother is learning to run the business, and Xingqiu is expected to support him once he takes over, though Xingqiu is not required to shoulder the Guild's affairs.

## leadership
### STANDARD
Xingqiu's father holds the post of Guild Manager. His grandfather is the patriarch, and a great-grandfather, styled "His Lordship", founded the Guild and is now retired. Xingqiu's brother handles business tasks such as arranging transport through the Sword and Strongbox Secure Transport Agency. He and the father also host influential figures. Xingqiu once concluded from the way they treated Zhongli that Zhongli must be a high-profile person.

## goals
### STANDARD
The Guild's goals are commercial: moving goods, selling them and keeping the family's reputation. It also wants its heirs to act the part. Xingqiu's brother tells him that if he cannot tidy a room he cannot tidy the world, because both brothers are responsible for the Guild's name.

## methods
### STANDARD
The Guild trades silk, prints books and sells tea, and it has a warehouse in the Lisha region, left of Mt. Tianheng on the map. It hires the Sword and Strongbox Secure Transport Agency to move and protect goods. A shop in Sumeru's Port Ormos sells jewellery and silk, and the materials used to make them.
### LARGE +
Xingqiu sometimes uses the Guild's influence to solve problems that violence cannot, in the manner of the disguised-king heroes in his novels. In a story quest, he averted a disaster and earned the Guild a sizable sum while doing so.

## history
### LARGE
The Guild apparently originates at Yilong Wharf in Chenyu Vale. The old family home there remains part of its property. The wiki links this to the Liyue Celebrates and Eight Adepts Face a Hidden Calamity quest, which establishes the family's multigenerational estate.
### ULTRA +
Xingqiu's profile says he was a model student, praised by tutors, whose reading of commerce and philosophy volumes was a cover for hiding martial-arts novels inside hollowed-out copies. His father, who knew the Guhua Clan was a dying art, let him study with it to keep him away from Wanwen Bookhouse, and treated it as a pastime. Xingqiu's skill surprised him. The family has worried about the Guild's reputation when his antics caused controversy.

## relations
### STANDARD
- **Sword and Strongbox Secure Transport Agency**: a guard company commissioned to move and protect the Guild's goods. It belongs to the Communications Office.
- **Guhua Clan**: Xingqiu studies its sword arts with his father's approval.
- **Ningguang, Yelan and Ganyu**: the family hosts or meets them. Yelan says she often has dealings with the family and expects Xingqiu will one day be "outsmarting everyone around him".
### LARGE +
Xingqiu's older brother never discusses business with him directly and lets him pursue his interests. Xingqiu says he now sympathises with his brother, who has gone prematurely grey from stress. This is stated in a 2026 birthday mail from 7.1.

## reputation
### STANDARD
Every merchant in Liyue Harbor knows of Xingqiu and the Guild. People see Xingqiu as kind, polite and promising, and parents hold him up as a model for their own children. Xingqiu says people see him only as the Guild's second son.

## status
@version: 7.1
### LITE
As of 7.1 the Guild is active, with Xingqiu still balancing its affairs against his wandering adventures. The wiki records no changes to it in 6.x or 7.0-7.1.

## entry: Xingqiu's family
@keys: Xingqiu's brother, Xingqiu's father, Xingqiu's family
@keys2: Feiyun, Xingqiu
### STANDARD
Xingqiu's family are unnamed in canon. His father is the Guild Manager, strict about behaviour befitting the Guild's name. His elder brother is the heir and lectures Xingqiu about messy rooms, bad calligraphy, skipped banquets and skipped meals, yet is gentler than the father. He once told the servants not to disturb Xingqiu's room, having mistaken the martial-arts novels under the bed for something more adult. Xingqiu in turn has developed an elaborate method for evading both, recounted in birthday mails.

## entry: Feiyun warehouse and shops
@keys: Feiyun warehouse, Feiyun storefront
@keys2: Feiyun Commerce Guild
### LARGE
The Guild keeps a warehouse in the Lisha region, to the left of Mt. Tianheng on the map. It appears in Xingqiu's story quest Bookworm Swordsman (Fabulae Textile Chapter, Act I). There is also a Feiyun storefront in Port Ormos in Sumeru, selling jewellery and silk and their raw materials.
