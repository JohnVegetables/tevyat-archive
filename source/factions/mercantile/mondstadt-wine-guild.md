## summary
### LITE
The Mondstadt Wine Guild, also called the Mondstadt Wine Appreciation Guild, is the trade body of Mondstadt's wine industry. Its Executive Chairman is Elzer, who also manages affairs at Dawn Winery for Diluc. It is allied with the winery.
### STANDARD +
Canon says very little about the guild itself. Almost everything known comes from Elzer's open-world dialogue at Dawn Winery.

## leadership
### LITE
Elzer is the guild's Executive Chairman, called Chief Executive in his own dialogue.
### STANDARD +
Elzer served at Dawn Winery while Crepus was alive and says Crepus was like a father to him. After Crepus died and Diluc left Mondstadt in grief and anger, Elzer took on much of the winery's business and, some time later, became chairman. He says he does not hide the role or do it for show.
### LARGE +
Elzer explains that, given Dawn Winery's standing in Mondstadt, there is barely any difference between running the winery and running the whole industry. In his words, anyone who wants to talk business at the winery should talk to him directly, because Diluc does not trouble himself much with such matters.

## members
### STANDARD
Elzer is the only named member. No other members are named in canon.

## goals
### STANDARD
The guild's purpose is never stated outright. From Elzer's dialogue, it looks after the commercial side of Mondstadt's wine trade, including partnerships with merchants and festival organisers.
### LARGE +
During the Weinlesefest event "Of Ballads and Brews" (3.1, limited-time), Elzer greeted visitors as potential Weinlesefest partners and said he had Diluc's permission to help. That event material is not a permanent feature of the guild.

## relations
### STANDARD
- **Dawn Winery**: the guild's ally, and Elzer's other post. The winery belongs to Diluc, and the Ragnvindr family founded it.
- **Diluc**: Elzer's master. Elzer is one of the few people who know Diluc is the rumoured "Darknight Hero".
### LARGE +
- **Angel's Share and Cat's Tail**: Elzer once tried to headhunt Diona for Angel's Share after her drink-mixing made Cat's Tail popular. He did not know she hates alcohol, and she bit him.

## reputation
### ULTRA
Nothing about the guild's public standing is stated. Elzer himself is polite and formal, and addresses guests as "distinguished guest" or "honored guest".

## status
@version: 7.1
### STANDARD
No change to the guild was found in any version from 1.0 to 7.1. Elzer remains at Dawn Winery in the open world.

## entry: Elzer
@keys: Elzer
@keys2: Dawn Winery, Wine Guild, Diluc, Mondstadt
### STANDARD
Elzer is a formal, businesslike open-world NPC at Dawn Winery. He handles the winery's major affairs when Diluc is away and is Executive Chairman of the Mondstadt Wine Guild. He grew up in Crepus's service, took on part of the winery's burden after Crepus's death, and says he did so partly because he thought Crepus would have wanted it. He knows Diluc is the "Darknight Hero".
