## summary
### LITE
The Kujou Clan is one of Inazuma's three great families and the head of the Tenryou Commission, the Raiden Shogun's executive arm for security and the army. It is regarded as the most loyal and powerful of the families supporting the Shogun's rule. During the Vision Hunt Decree the Kujou enforced the Shogun's order to seize Visions, through the general Kujou Sara. Its head, Kujou Takayuki, was then exposed as having worked with the Fatui for his own gain. His younger son Kujou Kamaji now leads the clan as acting head. Its estate stands next to Tenshukaku.
### STANDARD +
The clan counts three generations of difficulty in this period: Takayuki's disgrace, the Shogun's postponement of its punishment, and the rival Takatsukasa waiting to replace it. Its members are not of one mind. Several of them doubted the decree while carrying it out.

## structure
### STANDARD
The clan head is also the Tenryou Commissioner. Under him the family splits duties: Kujou Masahito commands the Shogun's Army at the Kujou Encampment on the Kannazuka front, Kamaji handles the Commission's civil business in Inazuma City, and Kujou Sara leads the Commission's forces as a general. Shogunate samurai, yoriki and doushin serve beneath them, and the Takatsukasa Clan supports the Kujou in running the Commission. Kujou retainers and security staff are called the clan's own guard.
### LARGE +
Named Tenryou personnel include Okawara Goemon (chief samurai and head of Kujou Clan security), Toranosuke (a Shogunate officer), Yasutake (quartermaster), and Shikanoin Heizou, a Tenryou "detective" among the doushin. Iwakura Michihiro, founder of the Iwakura Art sword style, once became the clan's sword instructor with the title Douin, according to the wiki's history. Staff directly attached to the family include Ikeda Soushirou (retainer) and Ippei, Kamaji's servant.

## leadership
### LITE
Kujou Takayuki, the former head, is under house arrest. Kujou Kamaji is acting head and Tenryou Commissioner in his place. Masahito and Sara serve as generals.
### STANDARD +
Takayuki was proud and rigid, loyal to the family's influence above all else. Kamaji is an earnest, self-doubting official who has questioned the Shogun's policies privately but never found anyone to tell. Masahito is a soldier who thinks the war can no longer be won cleanly. Sara is the Shogun's most devoted general.
### LARGE +
Takayuki had two sons, Masahito and Kamaji, and adopted Sara after finding her unconscious with a Vision. He used her as a weapon to raise the clan's standing, as Kamisato Ayato puts it in a voice-over, and lost the ability to control her when she learned the truth. Kamaji's relationship with Sara is warm, and Sara says he is the only one who treated her as a real sister. After Takayuki's collapse Masahito, the eldest, could have taken the headship but gave it to Kamaji, whom he judged fitter, and promised to support him as a warrior. Kamaji is also in love with Hiiragi Chisato of the Hiiragi Clan.

## members
### LITE
Kujou Takayuki (former head), Kujou Masahito (general), Kujou Kamaji (acting head), and Kujou Sara (adopted daughter, general). Kujou Shigeyori is the clan's founding-era ancestor.
### LARGE +
**Kujou Takayuki** was a tall, thin elderly man who held the Tenryou Commissioner's post until his dealings with the Fatui came out in Omnipresence Over Mortals. The Shogun was angry at the extent of his deceit, and he seemed to age overnight, losing his will and his position. **Kujou Masahito** is stationed at the Kujou Encampment, from which he commands the Kannazuka troops. He sent his father a warning about the Tatarigami growing stronger on Yashiori Island, was scolded, and the investigation team Takayuki sent went missing. **Kujou Kamaji** is the younger son, in charge of the Commission's government business in Inazuma City. **Kujou Sara** is a tengu who was adopted as a child; she is Electro, uses a bow, and holds the title Devotee of the Divine. Her entry holds her story. 
### ULTRA +
**Kujou Shigeyori** lived hundreds of years ago. Loading-screen text calls him the builder of the battlefront fortress at the Kujou Encampment in a single night and a brave fighter against the forces of darkness. Inazuma's history ties this to the cataclysm, when Ei, acting as the Raiden Shogun, led the campaign against the dark forces. The wiki also lists Kujou Michizane, said to be author of the Sangonomiya Chronicles, but nothing else is known.

## goals
### STANDARD
Protect the Shogun's rule and keep Inazuma secure. Under Takayuki this meant enforcing the Sakoku and Vision Hunt Decrees and protecting the clan's influence. After his fall the surviving Kujou have a narrower goal: to keep the Tenryou Commission from passing to the Takatsukasa and to earn back the Shogun's trust. Kamaji said in the duel that he accepted the clan's decline as punishment for doubting without acting.

## methods
### LARGE
The Tenryou Commission uses armed force and police authority: patrols, the Shogun's Army, the police station, and investigations led by officers such as Heizou. Sara trains hard, leads from the front, forbade harming civilians in carrying out the decree, and personally tends the wounded after battle. The wiki records, however, that the Commission suffered a high desertion rate, because soldiers were forced to fight people instead of the monsters they expected.
### ULTRA +
Takayuki himself used the Commission's power differently: he sent deceitful reports to the Shogun, according to quest documents, and the wiki records an order to blame killings on the Watatsumi Army. The wiki also says he used his authority in a feud with Takatsukasa Isamu, the former police station head, which ended in Isamu's murder and a scapegoat, although this rests on one hangout quest.

## history
### LARGE
**Founding era.** The clan's origins are unknown. Shigeyori built the Kujou Encampment on the Kannazuka front during the cataclysm. After the cataclysm Ei founded the Tri-Commission, and the Kujou Clan came to head the Tenryou Commission.

**The Vision Hunt (Chapter II, 2.0).** Under pressure from the Fatui and its own clan head, the Tenryou Commission enforced the Vision Hunt Decree and the Sakoku Decree. A protester challenged the Shogun before the throne and lost to Sara; his friend Kaedehara Kazuha took the Vision and fled. Sara also took Arataki Itto's Geo Vision, according to her wiki profile. When the Traveler was named a wanted criminal after the ceremony at the Statue of the Omnipresent God, Sara led the Shogun's Army against the Watatsumi resistance. She proposed a ceremonial fight of honour and the Traveler won; the resistance's reinforcements then turned the battle and Sara ordered a retreat. Takayuki's falsified reports and Fatui letters were later found with help from the Kamisato clan and presented to Sara, who confronted her father. The Traveler defeated Signora, but Sara lost to her first. Ei ended the decree.
### ULTRA +
**The aftermath (2.1).** A ceasefire between the Tenryou Commission and the Watatsumi Army was signed by Sara and Sangonomiya Kokomi, with the Traveler as witness; Masahito's idle lines predict that peace will be brief. The Commission fell into disarray and the Takatsukasa moved against the Kujou. In To Hear Mortal Hearts (Imperatrix Umbrosa Chapter, 2.1) Ei appears at the Takatsukasa's hidden mountain base on Mt. Yougou as Kamaji is being pressured to sign a confession listing the Kujou's crimes while flattering the Takatsukasa. Kamaji refuses, admits that he doubted the decree and did nothing, and asks to end the clan's story with a duel. Ei accepts, defeats him, and postpones the Kujou's punishment, telling the Takatsukasa to keep supporting the Kujou.

**Later (2.6 onward).** In Ayato's story quest (Cypressus Custos Chapter, 2.6) Kamaji agrees to marry Hiiragi Chisato and later accepts Ayato's plan to turn the match into an advantage. No story through 7.1 shows the Kujou in Nod-Krai or Snezhnaya.

## relations
### LARGE
- **Raiden Shogun**: the sworn lord. Ei decided to give the clan more time because it showed honour in Kamaji.
- **Takatsukasa Clan**: a long-time supporter and rival. Its members tried to take over the Commission after Takayuki's fall, and the Kujou were nearly forced to sign a confession.
- **Kamisato Clan and the Yashiro Commission**: the other major commission family. In the Archon Quest the Yashiro Commission gathered the evidence that exposed Takayuki. Sara respects Ayaka and has always hoped to call on her.
- **Hiiragi Clan and the Kanjou Commission**: Kamaji's love Chisato leads it as acting head.
- **Fatui**: Takayuki dealt with them and then lost his post. Sara, who was devoted to the Shogun, was among those hurt most by his betrayal.
- **Watatsumi Army**: opponents in the civil war, later a ceasefire. Sara called the resistance's leader Kokomi a worthy opponent in her own words, while Kokomi says Sara's loyalty and sincerity is admirable.

## reputation
### LARGE
The Kujou were considered the most loyal and powerful of the Shogun's families, but the Vision Hunt Decree made the Tenryou Commission hated by many ordinary people. Soldiers admire Sara and Masahito. Kamaji is thought a decent but unremarkable heir; Ei said he "is not a great descendant" but his honour is admirable, according to the wiki.

## secrets
### ULTRA
Takayuki's collusion with the Fatui was hidden from the Shogun by falsified reports. A Lost Text records an order to kill villagers on Yashiori Island and blame the Watatsumi. Sara loves sweets but rarely eats them, because they put her at ease and she thinks that unfitting for duty.

## status
@version: 7.1
### LITE
As of 7.1 the Kujou Clan still heads the Tenryou Commission under acting head Kamaji. Takayuki remains under house arrest awaiting punishment, and the Shogun has postponed any ruling on the clan. Masahito and Sara continue to lead the Shogun's forces.

## entry: Tenryou Commission
@keys: Tenryou Commission, Tenryou Commissioner, Tenryou-bugyou, Tenryou Commission Headquarters
### STANDARD
The Tenryou Commission ("Bugyou of the Shogun's Domain") is the Tri-Commission's executive arm, responsible for public security and military affairs. It enforced the Vision Hunt Decree and, with the Kanjou Commission, the Sakoku Decree, and posts notices on Inazuma's bulletin boards. It is led by the Kujou Clan and uses Shogunate samurai, yoriki and doushin. Sara, Masahito and Kamaji serve as its leaders. Its rank structure includes police and detectives such as Heizou, and its headquarters stands in Inazuma City beside Tenshukaku. The Commission has a high desertion rate and was in disarray after Takayuki's removal.

## entry: Takatsukasa Clan
@keys: Takatsukasa Clan, Takatsukasa Susumu, Takatsukasa Isamu, Takatsukasa
### STANDARD
The Takatsukasa have served the Kujou for generations and support them in heading the Tenryou Commission, but the two clans have long been at odds over its leadership. The head is Takatsukasa Susumu. His younger brother Isamu was the police station head and was murdered; Isamu's daughter is Takatsukasa Chika. After Takayuki's fall, Takatsukasa members tried to oust the Kujou by coercing Kamaji into signing a confession that painted themselves favourably, using a hidden mountain base on Mt. Yougou. Ei ended the matter in To Hear Mortal Hearts, and the clan abandoned the base. In the name, takatsukasa means "master of falconry".

## entry: Kujou Encampment
@keys: Kujou Encampment, Kujou Shigeyori
### STANDARD
The Kujou Encampment is a fortress at the front line in Kannazuka, said to have been built by Kujou Shigeyori in a single night in the age of the cataclysm. During the civil war Kujou Masahito commanded the Shogun's troops there. He thought the war was a stalemate that either side could win if it dragged on. After the ceasefire he predicted that any peace would be short-lived.

## entry: Takayuki's collusion with the Fatui
@keys: Kujou Takayuki, Takayuki, Tenryou Commissioner Takayuki
@version: 2.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Takayuki agreed to advance the Vision Hunt Decree so that the Fatui could sell Delusions, in return for power, wanting to keep his clan's influence and believing the Fatui would be no match for the Shogun. He sent the Shogun optimistic, false reports of the war. Yae Miko planned to turn Sara against the Tenryou Commission by proving this. With help from Ayaka, Thoma, Yoimiya and the ninja Sayu, the Traveler obtained his falsified report and the Fatui letters. Sara read them and confronted him; he confirmed it and gave selfish reasons. After this, the Shogun was displeased; he aged overnight and lost his post. He is now under house arrest.

## entry: Kujou Kamaji and Hiiragi Chisato
@keys: Kujou Kamaji, Kamaji, Hiiragi Chisato, Chisato
### STANDARD
Kamaji is Takayuki's younger son and runs the Commission's civil business in Inazuma City. In Chapter II, Chisato asks the Traveler to take a letter to him; she calls him her love and the two begin writing. In the Cypressus Custos Chapter (2.6), a marriage between them is announced. Kamaji says Chisato proposed first, but she tells the Traveler she never sent a letter. Ayato suspects a plot by the Kanjou Commission's plotters to use the match, and resolves it with the Traveler's help. Chisato asserts her own authority at the end.

## entry: Kujou Sara and the Kujou family
@keys: Kujou Sara, Devotee of the Divine, Tengu General
### STANDARD
Sara is a tengu who was found unconscious by the roadside, a Vision in her hand, after being thrown from a cliff while fighting monsters near her mountain home. Takayuki saw her as a child sent by heaven, named her Sara and raised her as a soldier. She is grateful to him but knows he does not look at her as he does Kamaji; Kamaji is the one who treats her as family. She believes the Shogun saved her life by granting the Vision. Soldiers first thought she was a boy when she began to train young, and one punishment from Takayuki taught her to keep a distance from others.
