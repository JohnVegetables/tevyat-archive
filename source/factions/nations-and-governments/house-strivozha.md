## summary
### LITE
House Strivozha is a famous noble house of the Vila, the winged fae of Snezhnaya. It was founded after the fall of Hyperborea under Grand Duchess Vaselisa von Strivozha, whom the Vila revere as a saint. Its people are known for sincerity, honour and loyalty, and for turning plants into perfumes and elixirs. In the present day its best-known member is Vesna, Commander-in-Chief of the Druzhna.
### STANDARD +
Vesna's Character Details say the Strivozha name is one of "certain importance" in Snezhnaya, an ancient Vila bloodline that long predates the nation's early turbulent days. Behind the polished reputation lies a history of divided loyalties that the Tsaritsa has never forgotten.

## structure
### STANDARD
The house is led by a Grand Duke or Grand Duchess of the Vila. In Vesna's childhood story her grandfather Pyotr is the reigning Grand Duke, her mother Benigna is a Grand Duchess and Pyotr's daughter, and the family keeps a secluded country estate on the outskirts of Snezhnaya. Elders of the house oversee the education of its heirs. According to Pulcinella, the house holds only a few seats in the Snezhnograd Veche, fewer than the merchant class has.
### LARGE +
Earlier, the house's head also ruled the Silver Court of Veneter. Vaselisa is called the Lady of the Vila of Veneter, and the Beyond the Chrysalis text says the Silver Court fell into Strivozha hands again after Photius died. The wiki also lists Eteri as a modern member, but her place in the family is not explained in the sources read.

## leadership
### STANDARD
Known heads, in order: Grand Duchess Vaselisa von Strivozha, "The Pure One", at the founding; Grand Duke Photius, around the time of the Cataclysm; Grand Duchess Gluvasha, his counsellor and killer, who later gave up her title and surname; and, much later, Grand Duke Pyotr, with his daughter Benigna as another Grand Duchess. Who holds the title as of 7.1 is not stated.
### LARGE +
Vaselisa is regarded as a saint and the Vila's pride. Photius is said to have planned a rebellion against the new Tsaritsa together with the Druzhna. Gluvasha was the eldest daughter of the Strivozhas and was honoured in her own right for loyalty. After her renunciation, the Tsaritsa decreed that no one should remember her by her surname.

## members
### STANDARD
- **Vaselisa von Strivozha**: founder, "The Pure One", ancestor of the house. She gave the Belyi Tsar a perfume made from the flame fern.
- **Photius**: Grand Duke at the time of the Cataclysm, killed by poison disguised as a stroke.
- **Gluvasha**: his counsellor and successor, who aided the rebels and then betrayed them to the Tsaritsa.
- **Pyotr**: Grand Duke in the modern era, Vesna's grandfather, who told her of her inheritance.
- **Benigna**: Pyotr's daughter, Vesna's mother, a gardener who grew rare plants.
- **Vesna**: playable Anemo character and Commander-in-Chief of the Druzhna, born with the power of Life.
### LARGE +
The wiki also lists Eteri among modern members without further explanation. Vesna left the family's tutelage as a child when the Tsaritsa's agent arranged for her to be trained outside the house.

## goals
### STANDARD
Canon shows the house seeking to secure its standing under the Tsaritsa. Grand Duke Pyotr believes the Tsaritsa distrusts the Vila and has tried to shape Vesna into an heir worthy of her favour while keeping her loyal to the house. The house also holds on to its traditions of perfumery, herb lore and the legend of the flame fern.

## methods
### LARGE
The house's craft is botanical. Its perfumers and apothecaries create hybrids and blends of herbs, and Vila have an instinctive affinity with nature. Its political method, as the Tsaritsa's mayor frames it, is to keep contact with several camps and to speak the language of loyalty. Pulcinella says the Vila's history is "littered" with those who smiled while keeping a knife close at hand, which is his view, not a neutral fact.

## history
### STANDARD
**Origins.** After Hyperborea fell, Vila survivors assembled under Vaselisa and formed the house. Around 3,000 years ago the Miscislavli Alliance made the Vila its vassals and demanded tithes of gold, grain and slaves. When Monomakh Snezhnyi, the Belyi Tsar, set out to unite the fae, Vaselisa fought him in a sword duel to decide the house's fate. She lost, and the house swore allegiance to the Pale Star Court.
### LARGE +
**The Cataclysm.** The house remained the Court's ally until Monomakh fell. When the Tsaritsa took power, Grand Duke Photius planned to rebel, but he died from poison disguised as a sudden stroke, and his counsellor Gluvasha took his title. Rebels sought her out because of her mother's loyalty to the old Tsar. She publicly denounced the Tsaritsa, drove off the palace's observer and raised retainers without sanction, so the rebels trusted her with their plans. She then delivered everything to the Tsaritsa and the rebellion was crushed in one blow. She afterwards asked to be stripped of her title, fief and surname, and the Tsaritsa did so. Gluvasha told the rebels she had not been their vassal, because Anastasya alone was her sovereign.
### ULTRA +
**The modern era.** Five hundred years after Gluvasha, Vesna was born at a country estate a month early, just as the garden's plants began growing out of season. The Tsaritsa visited and gave her the name Vesna, which means spring. At five, Vesna learned from Pyotr that the fragment of Life power, said to have been stolen from Naberius by the Angel of Angels Koitar, had awakened in her bloodline. She was then privately tutored for five years. Pulcinella arrived on the Tsaritsa's behalf, told her the story of Gluvasha and the crimson fern, and returned three days later to ask her what love is. Her answer pleased him, and she was trained under Capitano and the Fatui instead of in the house, eventually joining the Druzhna.

## relations
### STANDARD
- **The Tsaritsa**: sovereign of the Vila. Gluvasha swore that Anastasya alone was her ruler. She is also the one who named Vesna, and her mayor says the house has never been fully trusted.
- **Pale Star Court and the Belyi Tsar**: the house changed sides to the Court after losing Vaselisa's duel.
- **Miscislavli Alliance**: lords who held the Vila as vassals until Monomakh's rise.
- **Druzhna and Fatui**: Vesna is Commander-in-Chief of the former, and Pulcinella and Capitano oversaw her training.
### LARGE +
Pulcinella, the Mayor of Snezhnograd, was the Tsaritsa's agent in revealing the family's history to Vesna. Other fae clans in the rebellion trusted Gluvasha even though they were wary of one another.

## reputation
### LARGE
Publicly, the house is a byword for honour, benevolence and sincerity, symbolised by the flame fern. Vesna's profile says the name has earned "countless honors". The Beyond the Chrysalis text says histories recorded that the Strivozhas were never the enemy of any and kept their honour to the end. Privately, the Tsaritsa's circle regards the house with suspicion because of Gluvasha's double game.

## secrets
### ULTRA
The truth about Gluvasha was buried. Pulcinella tells Vesna that she poisoned Photius with fern mead and aided the rebels, and the Beyond the Chrysalis text shows she secretly worked for the Tsaritsa throughout. The wiki reads this as the reason the Vila later received citizenship under the Tsaritsa, but the sources read here do not say so outright (see uncertainties).

## entry: Grand Duchess Gluvasha
@keys: Gluvasha, Gluvasha Strivozha, Grand Duchess Gluvasha
@version: 7.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Gluvasha was the eldest daughter of House Strivozha and the counsellor of Grand Duke Photius. After Photius died she became Grand Duchess and sat at the top of Vila power. According to Beyond the Chrysalis, rebels asked her to join them, citing her mother's devotion to the Belyi Tsar. She agreed, denounced the Tsaritsa in public and gathered her own retainers, but was secretly serving the Tsaritsa. When the rebels were caught, she told them she had never been their vassal. She then asked the Tsaritsa to strip her of her fief, title and last name, saying a noble name was a chain and freedom the gift she valued most. The Tsaritsa granted it and decreed that no one should remember her by her surname. Pulcinella's account in Vesna's story is that she poisoned Photius with fern mead.
### LARGE +
Whether she acted from loyalty, guilt or calculation is told differently by the two sources and is recorded as disputed.

## entry: Flame fern
@keys: flame fern, crimson fern, Crimson Fern mead
@version: 7.1
### STANDARD
The flame fern is a red fern grown by House Strivozha since the days of Saint Strivozha. Vaselisa blended its fronds into a perfume for Monomakh and earned lasting renown. It came to stand for the purity, benevolence, sincerity and immortality of the Vila. Gluvasha is said to have brewed its fronds into the mead that poisoned Photius. The specimen at Benigna's country estate was crushed in the accident that began Vesna's early birth, and it never grew again.

## entry: The Life power
@keys: Life power, power of Life
@keys2: Vesna, Vila, Strivozha
@version: 7.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Pyotr told Vesna that, in the age of Hyperborea, the Angel of Angels Koitar stole a fragment of the power of the one who must not be named, Naberius. It passed through many hands and finally disappeared into the Vila bloodline. Its name was Life. Vesna is described as the only member of the family in five hundred years to awaken it.

## entry: Vesna's upbringing and the Pulcinella visit
@keys: Pulcinella, Mayor Pulcinella, Miss Fern
@keys2: Vesna, Strivozha, Vila
@version: 7.0
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
After her fifth birthday, Vesna was taken from public school and tutored at the estate. Her grandfather supervised her closely and limited her time with friends and her mother. At ten, Pulcinella visited by the Tsaritsa's order. He confirmed the Tsaritsa's distrust of House Strivozha and told her the story of Gluvasha. Three days later he returned to ask on the Tsaritsa's behalf what love is, and her answer pleased him. He informed Pyotr that she would be trained by forces outside the house. Vesna later became part of the Druzhna.
