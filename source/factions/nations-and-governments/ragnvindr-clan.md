## summary
### LITE
The Ragnvindr Clan is a rich and respected Mondstadt family who own the Dawn Winery, the city's premier winery. It takes its name from Ragnvindr, the Dawn Knight, who sided with Vennessa against the aristocracy about 1,000 years ago. It is one of the two known noble houses that were not exiled when the aristocracy fell. Its present head is Diluc.
### STANDARD +
The winery dominates Mondstadt's drinks trade, and Diluc's profile calls him in effect the uncrowned king of the city because of the money and gossip network that comes with it. The family's recent history is shaped by the death of Crepus Ragnvindr and the break between the family and the Knights of Favonius that followed.

## structure
### LITE
Diluc is head of the family and owner of the winery. His adopted brother Kaeya belongs to it by adoption.
### STANDARD +
The household is small. Crepus, the previous head, is dead. Staff are close to the family: Adelinde is head housemaid and ran the winery for a time when Diluc left, and Elzer is a long-serving employee who also minds the winery when Diluc is away. Other named winery staff include the winemaker Connor and warehouse manager Ernest. The staff mourned Crepus and treat Kaeya as part of the household.

## history
### LITE
The family's namesake Ragnvindr was a young nobleman and knight's squire in the aristocratic age who joined Vennessa's rebellion. Later generations ran the winery. Four years before the main story Crepus died, Diluc left the Knights of Favonius, and he returned to take over the winery.
### STANDARD +
**Ragnvindr.** Ragnvindr was moved by the enslaved Dawnlight Swordswoman of the Wanderer's Troupe, who kept singing of freedom as a gladiator. After she died in the arena he buried her with her sword and took the title Dawn Knight in her honour. He sided with Vennessa. Afterwards, he turned the aristocrats' public baths into the Mondstadt Library. The Ballads of the Squire are said to be his songs.

**Crepus.** Crepus dreamed of becoming a knight when he was young, but he was not accepted. He became a talented winemaker and wished his son to become the most esteemed knight. He took in Kaeya, a boy who was found at the winery about ten years before the main story, and raised him as a son.
### LARGE +
**Diluc and the break with the Knights.** Diluc trained hard, passed the Knights' trials and became the youngest Cavalry Captain in the order's history. On the night of his coming-of-age celebration at Angel's Share, Ursa the Drake attacked the convoy that Crepus and Diluc were travelling with. Crepus used a Delusion to defeat the drake, but it mortally wounded him. Back at headquarters, Inspector Eroch ordered Diluc to conceal the truth to protect the Knights' reputation, and Diluc resigned that day, left his Vision behind and left Mondstadt. Kaeya's stories record his own version of that night: Diluc ended his father's suffering and Kaeya arrived too late. Their paths diverged from then on.

**Return.** Diluc handed the winery to Adelinde and spent about three years travelling Teyvat to learn where the Delusion came from. He traced it to the Fatui. After a near-fatal clash with Harbingers he was spared by a watcher from a northern intelligence network. He then returned to take over the winery, and by night became the "Darknight Hero" while publicly appearing to despise the hero.
### ULTRA +
**Since the Eroch affair.** Eroch was later exposed as a traitor and purged from the Knights. Varka has since written to Diluc to apologise, but Diluc still declines to rejoin the order. He works with the Knights when Mondstadt is threatened, as in the events of 6.4 and 6.6. See the Knights of Favonius entry for those events.

## members
### LITE
Diluc Ragnvindr is the head. Kaeya, adopted by Crepus, is Diluc's estranged brother and is Cavalry Captain of the Knights. Crepus is dead. The founder-figure is Ragnvindr, the Dawn Knight.
### STANDARD +
Diluc and Kaeya were once close and now keep their distance. Staff in Taste of Home (3.8) say Kaeya is always welcome at the winery, and Adelinde says she has never considered him an outsider. Kaeya's profile calls him an adopted son of the Ragnvindr family and says he has not called Diluc "brother" for a long time.

## goals
### STANDARD
Diluc's profile gives him a simple creed behind the winery's motto "Shine True": evil must not go unpunished, he must never settle for mediocrity, and the dawn will one day come. The family's wider role is to supply Mondstadt with wine and cider, and to support the city, as the old mutual support between winery and Knights recalls.

## methods
### STANDARD
Business is run from the manor, with Diluc as owner. At his request the winery has developed alcohol-free drinks, including apple cider, which sells as well as dandelion wine. Diluc himself does not drink alcohol, and gives work as the reason. By night he hunts monsters and criminals alone.

## relations
### STANDARD
- **Knights of Favonius**: a long history of mutual support; Crepus and Diluc both served the city this way. Since Crepus's death Diluc has distrusted the order, though he still cooperates with it in a crisis.
- **Gunnhildr Clan**: the other house known to have escaped exile.
- **Lawrence Clan**: the former rulers, exiled after the rebellion.
- **Fatui**: Diluc tracked them as the source of the Delusion that killed his father.
- **Dawn Winery**: the family's seat and business; see the Dawn Winery location entry.

## reputation
### STANDARD
The Ragnvindrs are among Mondstadt's best-known and most affluent houses. At winery festivities, fathers present their daughters to the unmarried winery master, and onlookers joke that Diluc should stay married to the winery. Diluc is known as the Dawn Winery's owner; few link him to the Darknight Hero.

## secrets
### ULTRA
Diluc's identity as the Darknight Hero is not publicly acknowledged. The full truth of Crepus's death was concealed at Eroch's order and is not public. Kaeya's secret origin as an agent of Khaenri'ah is revealed in his own character stories; no source says who in the household knows it.

## entry: Ragnvindr, the Dawn Knight
@keys: Dawn Knight, Ragnvindr, Ballads of the Squire
### STANDARD
Ragnvindr was born into Mondstadt's upper class during the aristocratic age, became a knight's squire and grew sick of the nobles' indifference to the common people. He befriended the enslaved Dawnlight Swordswoman and, after she died fighting in the arena, buried her together with her sword, The Flute. He chose "Dawn Knight" as his title in her memory and took Vennessa's side. After the rebellion he converted the aristocracy's baths into a library. The Ballads of the Squire are said to be his own songs. It is unknown whether he joined the Knights of Favonius or whether the winery began with him.

## entry: Crepus Ragnvindr
@keys: Crepus, Master Crepus
@keys2: Diluc, Kaeya, Dawn Winery, Ragnvindr
### STANDARD
Crepus was the head of the family and of the Dawn Winery until his death. He wished for his son Diluc to become the most esteemed knight, and he told him, "Sometimes, life can change in an instant." He raised Kaeya as a second son. When Ursa the Drake attacked the winery's convoy, he used a Delusion, and the power that defeated the drake also mortally wounded him. Winery staff such as Elzer and Connor remember him as a talented winemaker and a father figure. In Taste of Home (3.8), Adelinde says Crepus mixed and named a nearly alcohol-free drink to comfort the young Kaeya after a punishment.

## entry: Diluc and Kaeya
@keys: Diluc, Kaeya
@keys2: brother, Ragnvindr, Crepus, winery
### LARGE
Kaeya was taken in as a boy and grew up beside Diluc. The night Crepus died, Kaeya arrived too late and hung back behind Diluc. That night also saw him receive his Vision. Diluc became the head of the family and Kaeya joined the Knights as Cavalry Captain, and the two grew apart. In Taste of Home (3.8) Kaeya visits the winery and the staff welcome him warmly. Kaeya's secret as a Khaenri'ahn agent is told in his own stories.

## entry: Dawn Winery household staff
@keys: Adelinde, Elzer, Connor, Ernest
@keys2: Dawn Winery, Diluc, Kaeya
### STANDARD
Adelinde is the head housemaid who kept the winery running while Diluc was away and still says Kaeya is part of the winery family. Elzer is a long-time employee who regarded Crepus as a father figure and manages the winery whenever Diluc is absent. Connor is the winemaker and Ernest the warehouse manager. All are devoted to the family.
