## summary
### LITE
The Hiiragi Clan is the family that heads the Kanjou Commission, Inazuma's finance, customs and border authority. Their estate stands in Ritou on Narukami Island. They serve the Raiden Shogun and have existed for at least 500 years. Their former head, Hiiragi Shinsuke, was jailed after a Fatui plot was exposed in 2.1, and his daughter Hiiragi Chisato now acts as head.
### STANDARD +
Together with the Kujou Clan (Tenryou Commission) and the Kamisato Clan (Yashiro Commission) they make up the leading houses of the Tri-Commission. The Kujou and Hiiragi are described as political rivals who keep each other in check, which is why a rumoured marriage between the two houses matters so much.

## structure
### STANDARD
The clan is led by a head, who has also been the Kanjou Commissioner. The clan's estate and the Commission's offices are in Ritou, and the Commission's officials, officers and ashigaru work under the clan's authority. Retainers also serve the clan and are shown taking sides over who should lead it. See the Kanjou Commission entry for its civil officials, soldiers and ashigaru.
### LARGE +
Under Shinsuke the Commissioner's office stood in Ritou and was guarded at its entrance by Kanjou soldiers who turned away outlanders. The clan's head and the Commissioner have been the same person in the cases canon shows (Hiroshi, Shinsuke), and Chisato acts as head of both after Shinsuke's arrest.

## leadership
### LITE
The acting head is Hiiragi Chisato, daughter of the jailed Hiiragi Shinsuke.
### STANDARD +
Shinsuke ran the clan and the Commission until the Fatui plot caught him in 2.1. Chisato, a young woman who loves Kujou Kamaji, was left as the Commission's figurehead. She was inexperienced, and she has since taken a more serious and hands-on approach to the work.
### LARGE +
Chisato says the estate's affairs are far more complicated than she expected, that people have been willing to listen to her suggestions, and that she is starting from the simplest matters and working outwards. The wiki notes that the Cypressus Custos Chapter wedding announcement names her and Kamaji as "Proxy Clan Heads" of their houses.
### ULTRA +
The clan's earliest figure in canon is Hiiragi Hiroshi, who led it about 500 years ago and was also the Commissioner of his day. Past heads between him and Shinsuke are not named in any source found.

## members
### STANDARD
- **Hiiragi Chisato**: acting head, daughter of Shinsuke. She is in love with Kujou Kamaji.
- **Hiiragi Shinsuke**: former head and Kanjou Commissioner, imprisoned.
- **Hiiragi Hiroshi**: head and Commissioner about 500 years ago, founder of Ritou.
### LARGE +
Commission staff such as Yokoyama, Kobayashi and Kenzaburou serve the Commission rather than being family. The Hiiragi Sanjuuro achievement, unlocked in Ritou Escape Plan, uses the clan's name but no matching person is shown in the sources checked.

## goals
### STANDARD
The clan's role is to manage Inazuma's money, trade and borders for the Shogunate. In the past, Hiroshi wanted foreign trade brought under the Inazuman government by building a port. Under Shinsuke the Commission's aims turned to personal gain, and under Chisato the declared aim is to steady the Commission after the scandal.

## methods
### STANDARD
Hiroshi's way was to prove the value of foreign trade. He imported foreign inventions, including a Snezhnayan blast furnace that heats the hot springs at Tenshukaku (a gift for the Shogun) and a special Kamera that records memories and emotions, built using a demon-exorcising mirror the Kitsune Saiguu gave the clan. Under Shinsuke the clan enforced the Sakoku Decree's border controls strictly through the Commission, which treated outlanders harshly.
### LARGE +
In 2.0 the Commission used travel permits, checkpoints and the Outlander Affairs Agency to hold outlanders in Ritou, and its tax collectors gathered Crystal Marrow. In Ritou Escape Plan, Shinsuke secretly agreed with Signora to keep the Traveler stuck in Ritou, while Chisato quietly helped them leave under cover of an escort mission.

## history
### STANDARD
**Origins.** About 500 years ago Hiiragi Hiroshi, then clan head and Kanjou Commissioner, turned a deserted island into the thriving trade port of Ritou. This impressed the Shogun. The Kitsune Saiguu counted him a friend.

**2.0-2.1.** When the Fatui came to Inazuma, they worked with Kujou Takayuki of the Tenryou Commission to push the Vision Hunt Decree and Sakoku Decree and create a market for Delusions. Shinsuke was drawn in with promises of wealth and power and stockpiled Crystal Marrow for the Fatui. After the Traveler exposed the plot, Shinsuke was imprisoned to await the Shogun's judgment.
### LARGE +
**The aftermath.** Chisato became the Commission's figurehead. In the Cypressus Custos Chapter (2.6), some retainers who wanted her out forged a love letter to Kamaji proposing marriage. A match would have absorbed her into the Kujou Clan and cost her influence. The Traveler and the Yashiro Commissioner, Kamisato Ayato, stopped the plot. Its failure pushed Chisato to take her role more seriously. The wedding plans were postponed indefinitely and the announcements treated as false.
### ULTRA +
**Hiroshi and the cataclysm.** After the cataclysm, when Iwakura Michihiro became sword instructor to the Kujou Clan, Hiroshi trained under him while still young. He said he would have recruited Michihiro for the Hiiragi himself if the Kujou had not done so first. The Kitsune Saiguu's last thoughts included his work to bring prosperity to Inazuma.

## relations
### STANDARD
- **Kanjou Commission**: the clan heads it.
- **Kujou Clan**: political rivals within the Tri-Commission who must check each other's power. Chisato and Kujou Kamaji are in love and exchange letters, though their families' bad blood makes this hard.
- **Kamisato Clan**: the Yashiro Commissioner Ayato helped Chisato in the Cypressus Custos Chapter.
- **Raiden Shogun**: the clan serves her.
- **Fatui**: Signora and others used Shinsuke and the Commission for the Crystal Marrow scheme.
### LARGE +
Kamaji answered Chisato's first letter with a snack box, which he described as a simple public-relations gesture, while admitting someone might take advantage of their situation. Chisato told the Traveler a visit from him would be impossible given the bad blood between the clans.

## reputation
### STANDARD
Before 2.1 the Commission under the clan was known for its authoritarian hold on Inazuma's border, its harsh assessments of anyone wanting to leave or enter, and its mistreatment of foreigners in Ritou. After Shinsuke's arrest it eased its stance on outlanders. Chisato is portrayed as sincere and polite, although the earlier scandal and the forged-letter plot show that some in the clan distrust or undermine her.

## secrets
### ULTRA
Shinsuke's meeting with Signora was hidden from his daughter until she overheard it. The identity of the retainers behind the forged marriage letter is not given. No secret Hiiragi lineage or history beyond these is established in the sources checked.

## entry: Hiiragi Chisato
@keys: Hiiragi Chisato, Chisato
@keys2: Hiiragi, Kanjou, Ritou, Kamaji
### STANDARD
Chisato, daughter of Shinsuke, appears in Ritou. In Ritou Escape Plan (2.0) she overheard her father plotting with Signora and secretly led the Traveler out of Ritou under an escort mission, asking in return that they carry a letter to Kujou Kamaji, her sweetheart. Her father opposed the match. After her father's arrest she became the Commission's figurehead and acting clan head. She says she will wait for the Shogun's judgment on her father because wrongdoing must be answered for, while worrying for him.

## entry: Hiiragi Shinsuke
@keys: Hiiragi Shinsuke, Shinsuke
@keys2: Hiiragi, Kanjou, Ritou, Commissioner
@version: 2.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Shinsuke was head of the clan and Kanjou Commissioner, and wore a grey kimono with a black hakama and red haori. He welcomed the Traveler as an honoured guest in Ritou Escape Plan while plotting with Signora to keep them stuck on the island. He accepted a Fatui offer of wealth and power by stockpiling Crystal Marrow. When the plot was exposed in Omnipresence Over Mortals he was imprisoned. His punishment is up to the Shogun.

## entry: Hiiragi Hiroshi
@keys: Hiiragi Hiroshi, Lord Hiiragi Hiroshi
### LARGE
Hiroshi lived about 500 years ago and led the clan as Kanjou Commissioner. He wanted a port to centralise foreign trade, and created the trading hub of Ritou from a deserted island. He imported a Snezhnayan blast furnace and the Kamera, and was a friend of the Kitsune Saiguu, who gave the clan a mirror. After the cataclysm he trained with the Kujou swordsman Iwakura Michihiro. Dates here are the wiki's deduction.

## entry: Forged marriage letter
@keys: forged love letter, Delayed Matrimonial Ceremonies, marriage announcement
@keys2: Chisato, Kamaji, Hiiragi, Kujou
@version: 2.6
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
In the Cypressus Custos Chapter some of the Hiiragi retainers, seeking the commissioner's seat, wrote a false love letter to Kamaji suggesting marriage. Marriage would have absorbed Chisato into the Kujou Clan and stripped her of influence. The Traveler and Kamisato Ayato exposed it. The Matsuura Clan then handled the correction, so that people now see it as a misunderstanding. Wedding plans were postponed indefinitely.
