## summary
### LITE
The Masters of the Night-Wind, also called Mictlan, are one of Natlan's six tribes. They live in a settlement perched on a foothill of the Tezcatepetonco Range. Of all the tribes they have the deepest bond with the Night Kingdom: their shamans commune with the Wayob, read fortunes, heal, bless weapons and keep watch over Natlan's Ley Lines and Phlogiston. Their chief is Biram, and their most famous member is the great shaman Citlali ("Granny Itztli").
### STANDARD +
Outsiders find them mysterious, reclusive and fond of cryptic speech. Most Mictlan folk met outside the settlement are travelling doctors or diviners on a commission. The tribe also records Natlan's history in woven scrolls, and it is known for graffiti.

## structure
### STANDARD
The tribe is led by a chief, who keeps its relics and oversees its rites. Below and beside the chief stand its spiritual specialists: high priests and priestesses, shamans of varying skill, and, rarely, a "great shaman", a title reached only at the very peak of the mystic arts. Citlali is the only great shaman alive. Shamanic knowledge passes from master to student, and some shaman families keep whole schools of technique. The tribe also has guards, trainers, Phlogiston inscribers, artisans and a Graffiti Alliance. Its bonded Saurians are the Iktomisaurs, and an ancient one, Elder Tlapo, is treated as an elder of the tribe.
### LARGE +
Like every Natlanese tribe, it has its own Wayob, which protects it and makes its will known through the chief's dreams. The Mictlan Wayob is the soul of the tribe's last Holy Sovereign, Maghan's master. A chief's duties include granting (on the Wayob's behalf) permission to leave Natlan. Special scrolls for events that affect all of Natlan are woven by someone Tlapo appoints.

## leadership
### LITE
Biram, the current chief, is an enormous man (more than twice the Traveler's height) who wears an Iktomisaurus-like mask. He is progressive but unwilling to let change destroy tradition.
### STANDARD +
Biram speaks in omens and likes to let questioners "stew" before answering. He keeps the ceremonial sword Calamity of Eshu between ceremonies, and for certain rites he wears the Soul-Shielding Cloak, a relic handed down through generations of chiefs. He treats Citlali with open respect, calling himself a humble junior beside the tribe's eldest priestess, where other elder shamans are frankly afraid of her. Citlali, for her part, says her duties include advising the chief and speaking with the Wayob.
### LARGE +
Past leaders include Maghan, the founding priest-king; his successor, the priest Wagadu; the fourth high priest, Hanga; and Ayizu, chief 500 years ago, who died during the cataclysm-era fighting after telling the man later known as Capitano the secret of the Gnosis.

## goals
### STANDARD
The tribe's role is spiritual stewardship: keeping the bond between the living, the Wayob and the Night Kingdom, monitoring the health of Natlan's Ley Lines and Phlogiston, guiding lost souls home, and preserving memory. Its founding charge, given by the Holy Sovereign to Maghan, was to spread the stories of heroes among the tribes and weave memories into scrolls, to "anchor" humanity's home in Natlan.

## methods
### STANDARD
Mictlan shamans see Phlogiston energy and even souls. Their arts include divination, potion-making, healing, astral projection (clairvoyance), telekinesis, concealment, memory extraction, border spells that blur the line between the Night Kingdom and the waking world, and Phlogiston Engraving. A shaman's repertoire depends on training and talent. Citlali has mastered many rare spells and invented her own. Shamans can also sense their own link to the Wayob and tell when they are about to travel too far from home to keep it.
### LARGE +
Every spell and every "problem-solving method" of the tribe works through spirits, so a degree of spiritual sensitivity is required. A tribesperson born with none at all is called a "spirit-duffer". A spirit-duffer can be forced into clairvoyance, but if their spirit gets lost or harmed it is very hard to recover, and even the tribe's healing does not work on them. Shamans teach through rivalry: a master often engineers clashes between students and a formidable peer to push them to improve.

## members
### STANDARD
- **Citlali**: great shaman and high priestess, a Cryo Vision holder who has lived for more than two centuries. She lives in seclusion away from the settlement.
- **Ororon**: a young man abandoned in the mountains as an infant and raised by the whole tribe, with Citlali as his "Granny". He has a fragmented soul and lives alone in the wilds, farming.
- **Biram**: chief.
- **Tlapo**: the Iktomisaurus Elder, "He of the Sharp Mist-Shaded Claws", once Huitzilin's companion "Claw".
### LARGE +
Other named members include the high priest Caholom; the Phlogiston inscriber Ixim; the witch doctor Matla; the trainer Bitsin; the ley-line inspector Toltec; Leyla, a spirit-duffer whose spirit got lost during clairvoyance; and Huitzilin's living school of shamans (Aywastin, Besuci, Caholom and Chogan). Historical members include Maghan, the Holy Sovereign, Sanhaj Kompore, Ayizu, Huitzilin, and Aroni, Bennett's biological mother.

## history
### LARGE
**Before the tribe.** Near the end of Xiuhcoatl's reign, the tribe's forerunners lived in a valley ruled by the Dragonlord Ix Kemonel Jasjatem Oox, the "Weaver of Secrets". She guarded them with a labyrinth of mirrors and a fortress of mist, and in return they appointed a "Holy Sovereign", a child with a "star-bright soul" who was blinded and maimed so as to guide the dragons into dreams. The last Holy Sovereign was served by a silent attendant, Dinga.

**Founding.** Prophesying the future, the Holy Sovereign sent Dinga to follow Xbalanque and asked him to stab her in the heart when they next met. After Xiuhcoatl fell and Cinder City was founded, Och-Kan declared the valley's people traitors. Dinga, now the dragon slayer Maghan, led the attack and killed his master as she had asked, and she became the tribe's Wayob. Maghan then refused Och-Kan's favour, retired and built the Mictlan tribe, around 2,000 years ago. His successor Wagadu renewed the pact with the Weaver of Secrets, giving rise to the first Iktomisaurus.

**Cataclysm.** Five hundred years ago, Chief Ayizu fought the Abyss beside the Khaenri'ahn later known as Capitano. The tribe's Border Master Sanhaj Kompore, one of the six heroes, is said to have exploded a miniature Night Kingdom at the Battle of the Mare Jivari, dying with Tenoch.

**Recent past.** About two centuries ago Citlali and Huitzilin became great shamans on the same day. Later, with the Ley Lines failing, a chief tried to use the newborn Ororon as a vessel to carry lost souls into the Night Kingdom. The ceremony failed and he survived.
### ULTRA +
**The Abyss war (5.1-5.3).** When Capitano escaped after his defeat by Mavuika in 5.1, she recognised Night-Wind magic in his escape and asked the tribe to find the member responsible. Citlali discovered it was Ororon, who had helped Capitano in exchange for a promise of her safety. During the Abyss's simultaneous assaults on the tribes, the Masters' settlement was attacked; Citlali rushed home and Ororon returned to help defend it before leaving again. Citlali stayed with the tribe, which needed manpower, while others went on the mission to recover Ororon. She then visited the Lord of the Night, revealed she had divined only death for Mavuika at the end of her plan, and in 5.3 begged Mavuika at Ochkanatlan to reconsider her sacrifice, until Capitano offered his own life force in her place. In the final battle, Sanhaj's spirit appeared beside Ororon, who bears his Ancient Name, Bidii. After the war, the Wayob told Citlali that the ancient soul of Xbalanque was about to return from the Night Kingdom to observe present-day Natlan.

## relations
### LARGE
- **Pyro Archon**: Mavuika values Citlali's help and has said she thought Citlali was chief material. The tribe fought under her in the Abyss war.
- **Other tribes**: the six tribes have coexisted since Och-Kan's era. Members of other tribes travel to Mictlan for divination, healing and weapon blessings.
- **Fatui**: tense. Ororon briefly worked with Capitano. In the "Memories Flowing With Color" Tribal Chronicles, Montluson, a Fontainian printing-company owner backed by Pantalone's Fatui, tried to destroy Huitzilin's legendary dye so he could market his own; Tlapo's Iktomisaurs caught him.
- **Abyss Order**: the Abyss Lector Enjou (under the alias Kokuchuu) manipulated Leyla into "hypersensitivity training" to learn about the Mare Jivari during a limited-time event.
### ULTRA +
- **Lord of the Night**: Citlali consults Yohualtecuhtin directly, for example over the Traveler's Ancient Name.
- **Scions of the Canopy**: Citlali commissioned Kinich to deliver a Spiritspeaker Stone to them.

## reputation
### STANDARD
Other Natlanese regard the Masters as profound, eccentric and a little alarming. Shamans are trusted as both doctors and prophets, and people believe that items crafted by the tribe have special effects. Within the tribe, "Granny Itztli" is a bogeyman parents invoke to quiet crying children.

## secrets
### ULTRA
A loading-screen description says the tribe's real ceremonial grounds lie hidden somewhere deeper in the range than the visible settlement. The Holy Sovereign's story, that the tribe's Wayob is the soul of a mystic slain by its own founder at her request, survives mainly in relic lore. In 5.1 Ororon secretly aided Capitano; Citlali later asked Coyopa at the Speaker's Chamber to petition Mavuika for forgiveness on his behalf, and stood as his guarantor.

## status
@version: 5.3
### LITE
As of 7.1 the tribe is intact under Chief Biram, after the Abyss war ended in 5.3. Citlali remains its great shaman, and Ororon still lives in the wild.
### STANDARD +
No tribe story was found in the 7.0-7.1 Snezhnaya Archon Quests. A few members appear abroad in Nod-Krai: Kofitse sells Natlanese hornshrooms in Piramida, saying the Wayob sent him to guide the outlanders there.

## entry: Shamans and great shamans
@keys: great shaman, great shamans, Night-Wind shaman, Mictlan shaman
### STANDARD
"Shaman" is the respected Mictlan profession of mystic practitioners who serve as doctors and prophets for all of Natlan. A "great shaman" is a title earned only at the very peak of the art, and there have been few in Natlan's history. Citlali and her rival Huitzilin both became great shamans on the same day, and Citlali is the only one alive now. Shamans cast spells through spirits: divination, clairvoyance, healing, telekinesis, concealment, memory extraction and border spells.

## entry: Woven scrolls
@keys: woven scroll, woven scrolls, scroll weaving, spiritloom
### STANDARD
The tribe records stories, legends and histories in woven scrolls instead of writing. Thread patterns are abstract and carry the weaver's feelings. For an event that affects all of Natlan, a special scroll is woven by someone Elder Tlapo appoints, and Huitzilin's art of using "color" as a medium for memory lets these scrolls hold memories. A huge graffiti mural above the settlement has been with the tribe since its founding, which is one reason Biram refuses to abandon weaving. ## entry: Maghan and the Holy Sovereign
@keys: Maghan, Dinga, Holy Sovereign, Maghan's master
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
Maghan (born Dinga) was the silent attendant of the tribe's last Holy Sovereign, a blinded young mystic. She foresaw her own end, sent him to follow Xbalanque and spread heroes' stories, and told him to stab her in the heart when they met again. When Och-Kan branded her valley traitors, Maghan led the assault and killed her. She became the Mictlan Wayob, and he founded the tribe as its priest-king. The tribe keeps her statuette, Starcaller's Watch, as a sacred relic. Grainfruit lore says Maghan, guided by night spirits, grew the first Grainfruit from animal teeth watered with his own blood, so its stalk must never be casually thrown away.

## entry: Sanhaj Kompore, the Border Master
@keys: Sanhaj, Sanhaj Kompore, Border Master, Bidii
### LARGE
Sanhaj Kompore was a high priest, storyteller and legendary Border Master, and one of the six heroes who fought the Abyss under Tenoch 500 years ago with his Iktomisaurus Mahamba. Born silent, he travelled to the Night Kingdom in search of half his soul and returned able to see past and future. He forged the ceremonial sword Calamity of Eshu, which chiefs still keep. At the Battle of the Mare Jivari he is said to have summoned and exploded a miniature Night Kingdom, dying with Tenoch. His Ancient Name, Bidii, has passed to Ororon.

## entry: The graffiti challenge
@keys: Huitzilin, Granny Itztli graffiti, graffiti challenge
### LARGE
Huitzilin, a great shaman and scroll weaver, challenged Citlali for twenty years before their first draw and became her closest friend. In old age she had her students paint provocative graffiti on Citlali's house as a challenge, believing only those brave enough to face Citlali deserved her legacy. Her disciples kept the custom after her death, calling it her dying wish. Challengers always lose, and Citlali erases their graffiti, but many go on to high posts in the tribe. Huitzilin's school still survives, split among several disciples.

## entry: Ororon's ceremony
@keys: vessel for lost souls, Ororon's ceremony
@keys2: Ororon, Masters of the Night-Wind, Mictlan
@version: 5.1
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
The Masters view a fragmented soul as a bad omen. When the infant Ororon was found with one, the chief of that time planned to make him a vessel: lost souls would gather in him, and he would be sent into the Night Kingdom to return them and strengthen the Ley Lines, dying in the process. Citlali neither approved nor objected. The ceremony failed and he lived. The plan was never a secret, and Ororon never said he resented it, but some elders still carry an unspoken guilt. Ororon has asked whether Natlan would have been saved had it succeeded; Citlali says he knows the Ley Lines' state is not his fault but still feels guilty.
