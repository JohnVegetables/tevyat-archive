## summary
### LITE
The Children of Echoes, or Nanatzcayan, are one of Natlan's six tribes. They live in the canyons of Tequemecan Valley in eastern Natlan, where the ring of hammers and the tapping of gem hunters echo off the cliffs. They are miners, excavators, smiths and gem appraisers who work alongside drill-horned Tepetlisaurs, and they are known for their love of dance and rhythmic music. Their chief is Pacal. Kachina and Xilonen are the best-known members.
### STANDARD +
Stone is one of their symbols. They are Natlan's only source of obsidian, a material used in important rites, and their Name Engravers forge the obsidian stones that carry Natlan's Ancient Names.

## structure
### STANDARD
Like Natlan's other tribes, the Children of Echoes govern themselves. A chief, normally chosen by the previous chief, oversees the tribe's customs and may change them. Even the Pyro Archon does not overrule a chief's decision as long as the tribe agrees. The tribe works in 19 mining teams: 12 handle the tribe's own work and 7 can be hired for mining or excavation elsewhere. Large ore deals and the hire of mining teams are negotiated directly with the chief. Guards such as Avilix and Cauich watch the settlement. The tribe's Wayob, its ancestral spirits in the Night Kingdom, choose who receives the tribe's Ancient Names.
### LARGE +
The tribe keeps its business informal. Its bulletin board admits that its people dislike paperwork and the fine print of commercial terms, and trust partners not to cheat them. Disputes that cannot be settled go to Chasca's mediation table. The chief issues decrees through the tribal bulletin board next to Stage Tepetl.

## leadership
### LITE
Chief Pacal is a big, warm-hearted man who prefers looking after and supporting his people to managing the details of their lives. As a young man he hoped to be chosen by the Wayob, but he never received an Ancient Name. He took up eating to cope with stress, and once ate 71 servings of Glittering Gemstones in a contest. He made his peace with it, and he lives by the tribe's mantra: no one fights alone.
### STANDARD +
Pacal has looked out for Kachina since she received her Ancient Name. Kachina calls him "Uncle Pacal" and says he defends her whenever others in the tribe run her down. His Tepetlisaur companion is Uchu. Manqu warns that, Ancient Name or not, Pacal is not to be taken lightly.
### ULTRA +
Known earlier chiefs include Ixquieh, the founder; Sakkuk, called the "Peace-Reforger"; Hun Toh, the 13th chief, who spent his whole life failing to master true phlogiston-forging; Mbande, the 51st; and Nare Magan Sundjatta, the 52nd, who fell in the cataclysm 500 years ago. Monetech was chief during the last Bloomflower Trials, hundreds of years ago, as told during the limited-time event Tournament of Glory in Bloom. When Sundjatta left to fight beside Tenoch, the elder and storyteller Traore served as acting chief.

## members
### STANDARD
- **Kachina**: a young warrior who bears the Ancient Name Uthabiti ("toughness and tenacity"). She is a gifted dancer and a tireless digger, and her Tepetlisaur companion is Ayo. She was the Traveler's first guide in Natlan.
- **Xilonen**: a renowned smith and the tribe's Name Engraver, bearer of the Ancient Name Baraka. Her parents and most of her relatives were Name Engravers too. Most of her work is tools, weapons and machines for the tribe.
- **Pacal**: chief.
### LARGE +
Other residents include Cheven, owner of Muhuru's Gemporium, and her apprentice Imix; Apoxpalon of the Macuahuitl Workshop; Titu, an old storyteller who tends the Shadow Pins; Pochtli, a regular at Stage Tepetl who rarely hands out compliments; and Itzna, a champion of the daily Drillbit Dance. Tepal, who designed the Phlogiston Wings, was raised in the tribe and died during an experiment. Batz died in the Abyss's invasion. Siyah, of the Saurian Relics Association, says he was given its stand at the Stadium's Chuwen Fair because the Children of Echoes "have a knack for business".

## goals
### STANDARD
The tribe wants to keep its people, its Tepetlisaurs and its mountains safe, and to pass on its craft. Its members still dream of the glory of the Pilgrimage of the Return of the Sacred Flame, and they see their work as a "blessing" made by mortal hands rather than granted by gods or masters. The bulletin board's welcome promises visitors sincerity "as solid and steady as the stones and as hot as the fires of the forge".

## methods
### STANDARD
**Mining and gems.** The tribe digs deep into the mountains for ore, solid phlogiston and gems, so deep that one guard jokes it can be hard to get enough sunlight. Members decorate their tools, weapons and accessories with gems, and some people read the gems as a sign of skill or status. Most members love tunnelling, and finding a vein of ore gives them a warmth that everyone in the tribe understands.

**Tepetlisaurs.** Tepetlisaurs can drill through rock and solid phlogiston. They have lived beside the tribe since before anyone's great-great-grandfather, and their nests are never far from human homes. The tribe treats them as equals rather than livestock or pets, and most bonds form freely: a Tepetlisaur simply starts following a person home. Legend says the ancestor of all Tepetlisaurs was a drake as large as a mountain, and that the huge drill-shaped objects in the cliffs above the settlement are the remains of its horn.
### LARGE +
**Craft and machines.** The tribe's smiths use phlogiston engravings to make many of Natlan's phlogiston-powered machines. Xilonen built Kachina's bouncing drill, the Turbo Twirly, as well as Chasca's oversized gun, Mavuika's phlogiston-powered motorcycle and sunglasses, and her own phlogiston-propelled roller skates. Forging Ancient Names is the tribe's most solemn craft, and Xilonen insists it is above all a matter of technique and study.

**Dance.** Other tribes joke that dancing runs in the Children's blood. The tribe holds dance-offs at Stage Tepetl, and the daily Drillbit Dance Dance-Off has reached its 127,751st round.

## history
### LARGE
**The founding.** In the age of myth, before Och-Kan's reign some 2,000 years ago, the twins Ixquieh and Ahpub, orphaned under a Dragonlord, reunited with help from the Collective of Plenty and "the clans of the mines". They became two of Xbalanque's Six Heroes. Ixquieh, first bearer of Baraka and called the "Hero of the Forged Flame", is honoured as the tribe's founder and first chief. When Och-Kan ordered that fleeing dragons be hunted, she refused to hand over the "beasts hidden in the mountains", the Tepetlisaurs. Ahpub, by then chief of the Collective of Plenty, came for them, and the twins' quarrel ended in Ixquieh's death. Accounts of how she died differ.

**Against Och-Kan.** Sakkuk's father was executed for protecting the tribe's saurians. Sakkuk became the first hero to join the Scarlet-Eyed Youth against Och-Kan, was the last of her companions left alive, and later served as chief. She also bore the Ancient Name Baraka.

**The cataclysm.** Five hundred years ago, Chief Sundjatta and his saurian companion Muhuru joined Tenoch's heroes against the Abyss. He died in the final battle. At home, Elder Traore led the tribe against an Abyssal tide that reached the mountains, and the tribe trapped the Abyss deep underground beneath the Shadow Pins in the Sulfurous Veins.

**The present.** In 5.0 Kachina brought the Traveler to the tribe. She later failed to return from the Night Kingdom after her team's victory, until a rescue brought her back. When the Abyss invaded Natlan in 5.1, Pacal, Kachina and Xilonen oversaw the tribe's evacuation to the Stadium, with Pacal among the last to leave, covering the rear. After the war ended in victory (5.3), the tribe announced a celebration at Stage Tepetl for the victory and the fallen.
### ULTRA +
**The Shadow Pins.** For centuries, the tribe's stories blamed two Khaenri'ahn soldiers for the disaster. In Shadows of the Mountains (5.0), the Greater Shadow Pin fell, and Titu and the Traveler found the truth that Traore had recorded discreetly. The two soldiers had fought beside the tribe and asked to be remembered as villains so that people would avoid the place. Traore had leapt first into the pit, crying that if the Abyss ensnared death, they would ensnare the Abyss with death and sacrifice. The Traveler laid the soldiers' tormented spirits to rest, and Titu vowed to retell their story as one of heroes.

**Rite of the Bold.** When Manqu sought to cleanse the Flamelord's Blessing from the Traveler's Tepetlisaur companion, Little One, custom required the blessing of the Tepetlisaurs' tribal chief. Pacal found the rule on an old woven scroll, because no one had attempted the rite in almost 500 years. He tried to talk them out of it, then replaced the ancient formalities with a duel. He lost, gave them the Talisman of Waxaklahun Ubah Kan and his blessing.

**Ixquieh and Ineffa.** Flashbacks in the Song of the Welkin Moon Prelude (5.8) show Ixquieh working with Lianca to rebuild the core of the sovereign remnant now called Ineffa. Ixquieh built the "heart" that Lianca placed in it, then left with the words that things were looking very bad for the Children of Echoes. Sundjatta's forge notes in Xilonen's workshop say that most of Ixquieh's forging knowledge was lost when her workshop burned.

## relations
### LARGE
- **Pyro Archon**: Mavuika visited the tribe several times after the war to ask about its losses and support rebuilding. Xilonen has made things for her, including a pair of sunglasses.
- **Other tribes**: Pacal recommends the People of the Springs and the Flower-Feather Clan to visitors. Chasca mediates disputes over trade. Xilonen forges for the Collective of Plenty, and Ifa of the Flower-Feather Clan treats the tribe's Tepetlisaurs.
- **Outsiders**: the tribe likes making friends with outsiders and believes the two sides can learn from each other. Pacal once found the Adventurers' Guild a mixed bag, but he welcomed the Traveler warmly once he knew who they were.
### ULTRA +
- **Collective of Plenty**: the two tribes' founders were the twins Ixquieh and Ahpub, and their story inspired traditions in both tribes.
- **Khaenri'ah**: the tribe now honours two Khaenri'ahn soldiers, Zephaniah and Irizar (fought in the quest as Black Serpent Knights), as heroes rather than villains.
- **Saurian lovers**: during the limited-time event Saurian Sojourn (6.1), Kweku set up his saurian training course at the Children of Echoes.

## reputation
### ULTRA
Across Natlan, the Children are known as fine miners and gem experts, rowdy, good at business and always ready to dance. Children grow up on the elders' hero stories, and the tribe takes pride in its craft. Its people can be hard on failure, though: some of Kachina's peers mocked her and kept their distance after her repeated losses in the Pilgrimage.

## secrets
@spoiler: major
### ULTRA
Tlazolli, a respected forger who bore the Ancient Name Undugu, lost her daughter Nechca in an Abyss attack. She took in Nepecha, an orphan of the same attack, passed her off as Nechca, and made Blazegem Inscriptions that secretly drew memories of Nechca from their owners so she could overwrite Nepecha's identity. Xilonen and the Traveler stopped the ritual in the Tribal Chronicle (5.1). Tlazolli was taken into tribal custody, her workshop was closed, and Xilonen took Nepecha in.

## status
@version: 5.3
### LITE
As of 7.1 the tribe lives on in Tequemecan Valley under Chief Pacal, rebuilding after the Abyss's invasion. Pacal stresses that the victory was real but temporary, and that the tribe must stay vigilant.
### STANDARD +
Kachina and Xilonen remain its best-known members. After the Night Kingdom was purified, Natlanese people could leave their country more freely. No new Children of Echoes story was found for 6.0-7.1; the 7.0-7.1 Archon Quests take place in Snezhnaya.

## entry: "Children of Echoes" settlement
@keys: Children of Echoes settlement, Land of Many Echoes, Stage Tepetl, Muhuru's Gemporium, Macuahuitl Workshop
### STANDARD
The tribe's home lies inside a gorge in Tequemecan Valley, beneath an enormous drill embedded in the cliffs. Stage Tepetl, the dance stage, stands beside the bulletin board. Muhuru's Gemporium sells gem accessories just over the bridge, and steps lead up to the chief for large ore deals. The Macuahuitl Workshop makes heavy-duty tools and weapons, and Xilonen's workshop is nearby. Duels are held on grounds in the mountain behind the stage, because, as Pacal says, the stage is no place for tunnelling. Tepetlisaurs nest close to the houses. An official map description says that, long ago, a sage of Phlogiston Engraving painted murals across the valley's cliffs, and that this is the origin of Natlan's graffiti.

## entry: Ancient Names of the Children of Echoes
@keys: Baraka, Uthabiti, Undugu, Name Engraver
### STANDARD
**Baraka** ("blessings") was first borne by the founder Ixquieh; later bearers include Sakkuk, Sundjatta and now Xilonen. **Uthabiti** ("toughness and tenacity") belongs to Kachina, who long doubted she deserved it. Ancient Names are given by the tribe's Wayob and forged into obsidian by Name Engravers. Xilonen, the current Name Engraver, studied under the respected engraver Teyiz, who taught that "stories and equations are, in essence, one and the same". Tlazolli, another forger, bore **Undugu**.

## entry: Shadow Pins
@keys: Shadow Pin, Shadow Pins, Greater Shadow Pin, Lesser Shadow Pin
### STANDARD
The Shadow Pins are great stone pillars in the Sulfurous Veins, one Greater Pin and three Lesser Pins. Children of Echoes legend says they pin down a "shadow": the Abyss that Elder Traore and his warriors sealed underground 500 years ago at the cost of their lives. The storyteller Titu looks after them. Even miners know not to touch the phlogiston around them. In 5.0 the Pins began to float and the Greater Pin fell. With the Traveler's help, Titu discovered the true story beneath them.

## entry: Tepetlisaurs and the tribe
@keys: Tepetlisaur, Tepetlisaurs, Tepetlisaurus
@keys2: Children of Echoes, Nanatzcayan, Kachina, Pacal, Ayo, Uchu
### STANDARD
Tepetlisaurs are the tribe's saurian partners. Each tribe bonds best with one kind of saurian, and the Children's is this burrowing kind with its drill-horn, which helps them mine and find the best ore. Companions known by name include Kachina's Ayo, who shows affection by headbutting, and Pacal's Uchu, whom Pacal is always telling to eat less. In Och-Kan's age, the tribe protected its Tepetlisaurs from his dragon hunts, and the father of the hero Sakkuk was executed for it.

## entry: Drillbit Dance
@keys: Drillbit Dance, Drillbit Dance Dance-Off
### LARGE
The Drillbit Dance Dance-Off is the tribe's daily dance contest at Stage Tepetl. By the Traveler's time it had passed its 127,751st round, which the wiki works out as roughly 350 years of daily contests. Itzna has won it 200 times, and veterans Zacatula and Tlaco have taken part on 1,200 and 1,700 days. Kachina's dancing is famous in the tribe. In Searing (5.8), Ineffa amazed onlookers by performing "the robot" on the stage.

## entry: Sundjatta's forge notes
@keys: Sundjatta's Phlogiston Forge Notes, phlogiston-forging
@version: 5.8
### ULTRA
Sundjatta, the 52nd chief, left woven scrolls on smithing, which turn up outside Xilonen's workshop in Flamelets (5.8). In them he writes that the dragons could forge phlogiston directly into other materials, and that Ixquieh truly mastered this art. Later smiths, including Hun Toh and Sundjatta himself, failed to recover it, so he advises using phlogiston only to temper metal and jade. He adds that many inventions imagined in Sakkuk's time now exist, such as golden discs that store music and phlogiston-lifted platforms. He calls this the legacy of the Nanatzcayan: "a 'blessing' born of mortal hands."
