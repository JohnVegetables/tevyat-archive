## summary
### LITE
The Sumeru Akademiya is both Sumeru's government and the most prestigious academy in Teyvat. Greater Lord Rukkhadevata founded it, and its main building, the Akademiya, sits on the Divine Tree above Sumeru City. Its scholars are divided into six Darshans (schools), each led by a Sage, and one Sage serves as Grand Sage. It runs Sumeru's day-to-day affairs, from trade and ports to hospitals and law. The old sages under Grand Sage Azar confined the Dendro Archon and plotted with the Fatui to make a new god. Since their fall, the Akademiya has served Lesser Lord Kusanali (Nahida), and its current Grand Sage is Naphis.
### STANDARD +
It accepts students of any age and from any nation, provided they pass its hard entrance exams. Its scholars travel all over Teyvat to research, and in Mondstadt, Liyue or Inazuma a travelling Sumeru scholar fretting over a thesis is a familiar sight.

## structure
### STANDARD
The six Darshans began as the Six Great Schools that Rukkhadevata founded with the Akademiya, and today the two names mean almost the same thing. Each has a specialty and a uniform colour:
- **Amurta**: biology, ecology and medicine (green). The oldest and largest Darshan.
- **Rtawahist**: illuminationism, meaning the stars, astronomy and astrology (blue).
- **Spantamad**: elementalism, ley lines and alchemy (red).
- **Haravatat**: semiotics, languages and ancient scripts (black).
- **Vahumana**: aetiology, history and the social sciences (yellow).
- **Kshahrewar**: technology and architecture (white).

Each Darshan is led by one of the Six Great Sages, and the central leader among them is the Grand Sage. The Grand Sage's office is above the House of Daena. Alongside the schools are administrative bodies: the **Mahamata** (civil officials, including the Scribe), the **Matra** (disciplinary officers under the General Mahamatra), the **Navbed** (trade and taxes), the **Vizier** (the Grand Bazaar and Treasures Street) and an office called the **Temple of Silence**, which handles information unfit for the public.
### LARGE +
Academic rank depends on progress, not age. Students address one another as seniors and juniors by who graduated first. That is why Tighnari is senior to the older Abattouy. A **Driyosh** is a travelling scholar on leave to gather material, and passing one thesis is enough to graduate. A **Dastur** has passed two theses and may stay and research at the Akademiya. A **Herbad** is a high-ranking researcher in a leadership role. Thesis defences are notoriously hard. Graduating after thirty is common, there seems to be a limit on how long a student may stay without graduating, and some students never leave the Akademiya until they finish. Students may take electives outside their own Darshan; there is even a swimming elective.

## leadership
### LITE
Grand Sage Naphis, the Amurta Sage, is a man of integrity with a legendary temper. He was Tighnari's teacher. Lesser Lord Kusanali now guides the Akademiya directly.
### STANDARD +
The current sages were introduced together in 6.6: Naphis (Amurta, Grand Sage), Baladhuri (Vahumana), Shakir (Rtawahist), Alkindi (Spantamad), Rushd (Haravatat) and Firnas (Kshahrewar). Naphis and Baladhuri kept their seats because they had opposed Azar's conspiracy, and the other four replaced sages who were removed.
### LARGE +
- **Baladhuri** is a kind, gentle historian who is proud of his beard. Tighnari calls him "Uncle Baladhuri".
- **Shakir** looks stern but is soft-spoken. He tracks his tasks on a whiteboard and was once Layla's master.
- **Alkindi** was Cyrus' classmate and rival, and he tried many fields before becoming Spantamad's Sage.
- **Rushd** is the youngest of the current sages. He was a junior of Khajeh, the disgraced former Haravatat Sage, and is still targeted inside his Darshan because of an old dispute with Khajeh.
- **Firnas** is the Kshahrewar Sage. Weapon lore about an older "great master Firnas" stresses that he was not the current lecturer.
### ULTRA +
Notable past sages include Azar, the Rtawahist Sage who was Grand Sage during the conspiracy; Khajeh of Haravatat; Cyrus, a retired Spantamad Sage who taught Lisa and Cyno and fostered Cyno; Iskandar of Vahumana, one of the greatest sages in history; Biruni, who founded the House of Purbiruni five centuries ago; and the Amurta Sage Siman Farrokhzad. When Azar fell, the people choosing his successor called Alhaitham the most suitable candidate for Grand Sage. He refused and served only as Acting Grand Sage.

## goals
### STANDARD
In principle the Akademiya exists to pursue and spread wisdom and to help the God of Wisdom govern Sumeru. Its three virtues, Admonition, Ingenuity and Praxis, are the "branches", "leaf-veins" and "roots" of the nation of wisdom. Its six cardinal sins mark the knowledge it considers too dangerous to pursue. Under Azar, these ideals hardened into control: the sages wanted to monopolise knowledge, restore Irminsul and replace a god they found disappointing.

## methods
### STANDARD
The Akademiya governs mainly through the **Akasha System**. Rukkhadevata created it and the Akademiya expanded it after her death. It delivered knowledge to anyone wearing an Akasha Terminal, filtered by clearance, and it carried decrees and announcements. New material entered it on Jnagarbha Day through the Grand Sage's console. Unknown to most people, it drew its computing power from users' dreams. After the cataclysm the sages slowly stigmatised dreaming so that no one would notice. Leaked Knowledge Capsules ("Canned Knowledge") are banned, and the Matra pursue smugglers. The Akasha was shut down after Chapter III.
### LARGE +
Rules are built on the six sins, and the sages have kept adding and amending them over the centuries. Research touching those sins can mean expulsion or imprisonment. Research on mechanical life forms, once popular in Spantamad, was banned after researchers began cruel, unregulated experiments on animals. The Matra visit offenders in person and are widely feared. The Mahamata relay decrees and oversee trade, ports and public healthcare. Each year the Pir Kavikavus Prize goes to a promising scholar under forty. Peace in Sumeru City is kept not by an army but by the Corps of Thirty, an Eremite mercenary brigade on permanent contract to the sages.

## history
### LARGE
**Founding.** With her followers, who later founded Amurta, Rukkhadevata grew the rainforest. She founded the Akademiya and its Six Great Schools, and its sages helped her govern. Other Darshans have come and gone, but only the six original schools lasted. One of the lost Darshans, Nagarjuna's school, left for the desert during the cataclysm and became the Order of Skeptics, whom the Akademiya called the "Nagarjunites".

**After the cataclysm.** About 500 years ago, forbidden knowledge contaminated Irminsul and Rukkhadevata, who vanished and left a newborn successor. The sages distrusted the child-like Lesser Lord Kusanali and kept her confined in the Sanctuary of Surasthana for 500 years. About 400 years ago, the real Temple of Silence left for the desert in disgust at the sages' corruption, and the sages set up an empty office of the same name to hide its departure. In the same era the student Zandik was expelled for unauthorised and unethical research. He later became the Harbinger Dottore.

**Chapter III (3.0-3.2).** Dottore returned and offered to build the sages a new God of Wisdom from Scaramouche. Azar and three other sages agreed, and they imprisoned Naphis and Baladhuri for objecting. The conspirators harvested divine knowledge from scholars driven mad, ran a dream samsara through the Akasha during the Sabzeruz Festival, spread rumours of King Deshret's revival, and banned public art. Cyno resigned as General Mahamatra after Azar refused to let him investigate. The Traveler, Nahida, Alhaitham, Cyno, Tighnari, Nilou, Dehya and their allies freed Nahida and stopped the plot. Nahida revoked the sages' access to the Akasha. Azar and his three accomplices were removed from office and sent to the Avidya Forest, supposedly repentant, to "cultivate wisdom" for the rest of their lives.
### ULTRA +
**Interregnum (3.2-3.4).** Alhaitham turned down the post of Grand Sage. He served as Acting Grand Sage to keep the Akademiya running and handled the Siraj "Hive" affair in that role (3.4). He then resigned and went back to being Scribe, because he worried that "Acting" would become permanent. The selection of new sages, overseen by Kusanali, took a long time. Naphis eventually became Grand Sage, and four new sages took the empty seats.

**The Irminsul crisis (6.6).** Collei's prophetic dream, passed on by Nicole, warned of a disaster. Nahida and the new sages evacuated the whole rainforest to Caravan Ribat, and a barrier sealed Sumeru off. Dottore had entered Irminsul through death. The sages, scholars, Eremites and the Seven Pillars of Aaru worked together on a plan to reconfigure Aaru and restart the Akasha. A Fatui mole inside the Corps of Thirty was caught. In the end, everyone tapped into the Akasha to overload Irminsul. Nahida used the Pyro Gnosis to burn the corrupted tree, Dottore died, and the reconfigured Aaru took over storing information. Afterwards Naphis brought Nahida a letter of apology from Pulcinella. They agreed it was diplomatically airtight and that they would watch Snezhnaya closely.

## reputation
### LARGE
The Akademiya's scorn for art spread through Sumeru. Plays had to be approved, books were not openly sold, and practicality came to be valued over beauty in everything from dance to architecture. Even after the sages fell, talking about art is still the quickest way to kill a conversation. People grumble that the Mahamata are inefficient and that students may be assigned to them against their will. Abroad, people respect its learning but also know how gruelling its degrees are. Lisa will not speak well of it, having seen scholars driven mad in the forests and sages sitting idle on councils.

## secrets
### ULTRA
The six sins were reportedly proposed by the Temple of Silence, and the sages took the credit. The Akademiya's "Temple of Silence" is only a front, set up to hide that the real one had left. The Akasha ran on stolen dreams. Rukkhadevata herself has been erased from Irminsul, so nearly everyone now believes Kusanali was always the Dendro Archon and that she lost her memories in the cataclysm.

## entry: Six cardinal sins and three virtues
@keys: six cardinal sins, six deadly sins, Admonition, Ingenuity, Praxis
@keys2: Akademiya, Sumeru, sin, sins, virtue, virtues
### STANDARD
In the Akademiya's early days, researchers reshaped terrain, controlled the weather and reached for the stars and for life and death. To protect them from their own knowledge, six sins were declared to be the root of all crimes: interfering with human evolution; tampering with life and death; delving beyond the universe; investigating the origin of words; revering gods without acts of devotion; and attempting the forbidden and fearing none. Every later rule builds on them, and Cyno judges by them, "even sages". Over time most people forgot them. In 3.4, Alhaitham noted that Siraj's Hive project counted as research on human evolution. The three virtues, Admonition, Ingenuity and Praxis, are the qualities the Akademiya praises, and the Sumeru talent books are named after them.

## entry: Akademiya academic ranks
@keys: Driyosh, Dastur, Herbad, travelling scholar, traveling scholar
### STANDARD
Driyosh are students on leave to gather research material; they apply through an eligibility review with interviews. Passing one thesis graduates a student. A Dastur has passed two and may research at the Akademiya instead of travelling. A Herbad is a senior researcher in a leadership role. Above them are the Six Great Sages and the Grand Sage. Seniority depends on progress, not age. Thesis review panels are feared, and some students pile up rejected manuscripts.

## entry: Mahamata, Matra and the Scribe
@keys: Mahamata, Matra, General Mahamatra, Navbed, Vizier
### STANDARD
The Mahamata are Akademiya alumni sent across Sumeru to oversee information, enforce decrees and run public services such as trade and the Bimarstan hospital. The Scribe, Alhaitham's post, belongs to the Mahamata. The Matra are disciplinary officers who punish serious academic offences such as plagiarism and misused funds, and who inspect for Canned Knowledge and smuggled books. They are led by General Mahamatra Cyno, whom some scholars snipe at for not being a renowned researcher. The Navbed manages trade, ports and taxes, and the Vizier oversees the Grand Bazaar and Treasures Street.

Inside the Akademiya, the Temple of Silence is an office for keeping and disposing of information unfit for the public. Rumour holds that it detains people.
