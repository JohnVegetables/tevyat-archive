## summary
### LITE
The Snezhnaya Royal Railway Transport Bureau, usually called the Transport Bureau and sometimes Chicota, is the organisation in charge of Snezhnaya's railway system. Its staff run Central Station in Snezhnograd and the trains that cross the snowfields. The wiki also notes an uncommon translation as the Snezhnaya Imperial Railway Transport Bureau.
### STANDARD +
Canon treats it as both a transport service and a guardian of order. Weapon lore makes the head of the Chicota the "guardian of law and order" and gives each head an oath. The wiki page on the organisation is only a stub, so most of what is known comes from NPC dialogue and item text.

## structure
### STANDARD
Named job titles in the Bureau are Conductor, Safety Officer and Ticket Agent. Conductors work on the platforms and aboard trains, safety officers stand watch at the station concourse and platforms, and ticket agents sell passage. Staff listed at Central Station include the conductors Aryna, Bazhenov, Kholodnaya, Kira and Rudov, the safety officers Gurevikov, Iliana and Sochikov, and the ticket agent Voskresensky. Other conductors such as Lukova and Baryatinsky are found aboard trains that can be summoned at several stations.
### LARGE +
Bureau staff are also found outside the capital. Nekrasov, listed with the Bureau on the wiki, works in the ice-mining town of Svetloledovka, though no job title is given for him. Donya, a quest NPC of the Forested Snowfield quest, is also affiliated with it.

## leadership
### STANDARD
No present-day head is named. Weapon lore describes the leader of the Chicota as the keeper of law and order within Snezhnaya, who must judge by the law that binds all living beings even when he feels sorrow and compassion as a person.
### LARGE +
The text describes an oath that every head swears on taking the seat. Its lines speak of conquering humanity's weaknesses, forsaking useless emotions while keeping a frozen heart beating, using authority with humility, and keeping conscience and distance from cowardice and arrogance.

## goals
### STANDARD
The Bureau's duties, as seen in its staff, are safe and orderly travel and the security of the stations. Bazhenov describes Snezhnograd Central Station as the lifeline of the city and the city as the heart of Snezhnaya, so even a tiny error is unacceptable. The oath adds a larger aim of sanctioning acts of terror and preserving peace along the long borders.

## methods
### STANDARD
Staff manage crowds, ask passengers to board and leave in an orderly way, and guard the exit gates. Kira says that, under new regulations, staff are authorised to carry out random checks on anyone seen as a potential risk, including luggage inspection and questions about the traveller. The official map text for Central Station confirms that visitors pass inspections by Bureau staff before taking the elevator up to the centre of Snezhnograd.
### LARGE +
Baryatinsky says a conductor must keep the train comfortable and, in extreme circumstances, take immediate corrective action against anything that threatens the train's proper operation. Gurevikov notes that the platforms are always especially crowded during boarding and disembarking. Lukova warns passengers that the Bureau will not pay compensation for falls while the train is moving, and avoids saying when it does.

## history
### LARGE
Canon gives only a legend. In the Covenant of Frost and Snow text, a young human who was not yet head of the Bureau shared ideals with a pale fae. Both lamented injustice, and they were close at a time when humans and fae seldom agreed. They then split: the human chose order and new laws, the fae chose destruction as a path to rebirth. The human went on to lead the Chicota, and the fae became "the wise one", inciting those who longed for destruction in the name of rebirth. The conflict ended on a train near white birches. The fae fled with brigands' help but could not escape the Chicota's chief, who raised the bow and loosed on his friend. The bow came with the words that it was not given to Eprokin but to the guardian of order. That promise became the oath of every later head. The text does not name the two or date the events.

## relations
### LARGE
- **Snezhnaya's capital and government**: the wiki lists the Zapolyarny Palace as its affiliation, but this is not confirmed in the in-game text read.
- **Passengers and visitors**: staff are courteous but firm, and treat inspection as routine.
- **Fae**: the weapon legend frames the Chicota as having stood against a fae rebel, but gives no general policy toward fae.

## reputation
### ULTRA
Staff present themselves as dutiful and strict. In dialogue, Lukova is curt when asked about compensation, and Kira treats checks as ordinary under new regulations. Neither amounts to a stated public opinion of the Bureau, and no source read gives one.

## entry: Chicota
@keys: Chicota, Chicota oath
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
Chicota is the alternate name of the Bureau used in the Covenant of Frost and Snow text. Its head is the guardian of law and order in Snezhnaya, and each head swears the oath that descends from the legend. The weapon was said to be given to such a guardian, not to Eprokin, an unexplained name.

## entry: Central Station staff
@keys: Central Station, Snezhnograd Central Station
@keys2: Transport Bureau, conductor, Kira, Bazhenov, Gurevikov
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
Central Station is the largest station in Snezhnaya and the entry to Snezhnograd. Visitors who leave the platforms pass inspection by Bureau staff before riding the elevator into the city. Bazhenov watches the exit gates, Gurevikov and Iliana keep platforms orderly as safety officers, Kira greets arriving passengers, and Voskresensky acts as a ticket agent.

## entry: Train conductors

Lukova can be met aboard a summonable train at Central Station, Svetloledovka, Sretomorozsk and Rankova Zorya. Baryatinsky serves on a train reachable from Central Station, Morepesok and Rankova Zorya. He emphasises passenger safety and quick corrective action if the train is threatened.
