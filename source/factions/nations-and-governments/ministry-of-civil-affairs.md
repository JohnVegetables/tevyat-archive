## summary
### LITE
The Ministry of Civil Affairs is the working civil administration of Liyue, a body under the Liyue Qixing headquartered at Feiyun Slope in Liyue Harbor. It is made up of various departments and deals with the paperwork of a trading nation: permits, ports, taxes, festivals, information and public order. It can call on the Millelith when safety is at stake. Its staff are known for being overworked and curt.
### STANDARD +
The Ministry's own explanation of its trade is "resource management": goods, information and people, exchanged and combined to solve problems. In Liyue, where commerce comes first, that makes it central to daily life. It also employs outside help. After the Traveler has finished the Archon Quest chapter that ends with Rex Lapis's departure, Ms. Yu offers paid Ministry work that feeds Liyue's reputation system.

## structure
### STANDARD
The Ministry is not a single office but several departments, for example a Maritime Authority that administers the harbor and ships, and staff who handle commerce, labor and festivals. Named staff are modest in rank: Clerk Zhao and Yanbei are clerks, Ms. Yu is a resource manager, Nervous An administers the Maritime Authority, Muning is a specialist sent to the Chasm, and Woliu is a hydrologist. Its records are said to cover every employee in Liyue, from ordinary members of the Eight Trades to officials of Yujing Terrace. It answers to the Qixing, who set policy; the Ministry carries it out.
### LARGE +
Nervous An supervises Linling in the Maritime Authority, and clerks like Zhao staff the counters near Feiyun Slope. Mail forwarding is also among its tasks. A library is housed in the Ministry building, and Yanfei has used it. The Ministry also hires temporary groups: the Chasm Exploration Team was assembled to investigate anomalies in the Chasm, and Muning later made it part of Huishan Hall.

## leadership
### STANDARD
No head of the Ministry is named in the sources checked, so do not give it one. Policy comes from the Qixing, and the Ministry is the executor. Yelan claims to be a Ministry employee, but see the secrets section for why that is doubtful.

## goals
### STANDARD
The Ministry keeps Liyue running smoothly and safely, which in Liyue means protecting trade and order. Its documented concerns are the safety of the harbor, roads, villages and festivals, honest trade and tax declarations, and early warning of anything that could harm Liyue. Yelan's profile adds a deeper aim behind the watch lists: that Liyue should never again be caught unprepared by a disaster as it was 500 years ago.

## methods
### LITE
It governs by permits and paperwork: business permits, exit permits, proficiency tests, tax and customs checks, port rent, bounties and watch lists.
### STANDARD +
Typical examples. Sedan chair bearers need a permit and must pass written and road tests. Sailors need nine certifications (four major, five minor) and a two-year training and apprenticeship before sailing the sea. Business and exit permits are issued here, and transactions are checked for contraband and correct tax declarations. Outsiders register with it: Kliment cites his Ministry registration for an archaeological survey. The Ministry handles customs for imports and exports and mediates disputes; in a quest, Ganyu tells a Fatui Skirmisher to file a complaint with the Ministry. Its notice board offers temporary unemployment, medical and food stipends and reviews tax exemption and deduction applications. It also issues bounties for wanted criminals.
### LARGE +
Intelligence is part of the job. Ganyu says she hears from the Ministry about big-time Treasure Hoarders planning a joint operation across Liyue and Mondstadt. Yelan's profile says the Ministry keeps two watch lists compiled by her: a fairly short Special Attention List of people with the power or means to cause Liyue great harm (Beidou, the Traveler and some reclusive adepti are named), and a higher-level Secret Intelligence List. Beidou knows the second exists but cannot see it.

## members
### STANDARD
Clerk Zhao (clerk, near Feiyun Slope by day and the Xinyue Kiosk at night), Ms. Yu (resource manager who runs Ministry Missions), Nervous An (overworked administrator of the Maritime Authority), Muning (specialist posted to the Chasm), Yanbei (clerk), Woliu (hydrologist), and Mingbo, Qi Nan and Weijin, about whom little is recorded. Many staff and Millelith sent by them complain of too much work and too few people, and Zhao and An are blunt with visitors.
### LARGE +
Yelan is listed by the wiki as a self-proclaimed member and a "non-entity" on its rosters. Ganyu says she does not know what the Ministry does, yet in her voice line about Yaoyao says someone she knows in the Ministry asked her to keep an eye on the girl.

## history
### LARGE
The Ministry has been part of Liyue's government since the earliest content, and its founding date is not given. Documented episodes:

**The Chasm.** After an accident in the Chasm mines, the Ministry sealed the area, ordered work stopped and made its approval necessary for work to resume. In the Chasm Delvers quests, the Adventurers' Guild notes that nobody can enter without the Qixing's agreement, and the Ministry sends its specialist Muning along with a hired Chasm Exploration Team to investigate.

**Public-safety crises.** During the meteorite incident of the Unreconciled Stars event (limited-time), it sent nearly every available Millelith to guard main roads and key villages. During the Siege of Qingce it sent Millelith to protect the movement of supplies. During The Stars Inscribe the Year's Wishes (limited-time) it sent Millelith against Treasure Hoarders near Qingce Village. After the battle with Osial, the Traveler helped it clear flotsam from the harbor.
### ULTRA +
**Lantern Rite.** The Ministry manages the festival's budget and fireworks, supplies other villages with Xiao Lanterns and has to clean them up afterward so that they do not end up in odd places. For the 2026 Lantern Rite (limited-time event, January 2026) it sent the Traveler a gift and an invitation back to Liyue. Nervous An is delighted to have a five-day holiday during the festival.

**Business records.** In A Teapot to Call Home: Part I, a quest item is a set of Ministry filing documents obtained from Mingxing Jewelry, and Xingxi had to put transactions on record with the Ministry. In The Art of Hospitality, Swan asks Alois for the documents the Ministry issues.

## relations
### STANDARD
It stands under the Qixing, which makes policy, and gives orders to the Millelith when public safety needs them. It works with Huishan Hall and the Feiyun Commerce Guild on Chasm and commercial matters. The Adventurers' Guild depends on others' passes for restricted places. It is wary of outsiders with power: its Special Attention List includes Beidou and the Traveler. Fatui and foreign visitors are dealt with through ordinary regulation and complaint procedures.

## reputation
### STANDARD
Liyue's people see it as essential and overstretched: there is always a queue, a form or a clerk who wants the Traveler to be quick. Yelan's profile calls it an organisation marked by transparency, because everyone's basic information is on its lists, which is exactly why her own absence from them is notable.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the Ministry is still Liyue's active administration. No major Ministry-led story event after the 2026 Lantern Rite was found in the checked sources.

## entry: Ministry of Civil Affairs headquarters
@keys: Ministry of Civil Affairs, Feiyun Slope
@version: 1.0
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
The Ministry is based at Feiyun Slope in Liyue Harbor. Nearby are Clerk Zhao and a notice board carrying Ministry announcements and sedan-chair rules. A shishi (lion-dog) statue stands outside. The building also contains a library. The Qixing's own offices are elsewhere, at Yujing Terrace.

## entry: Ministry watch lists
@keys: Special Attention List, Secret Intelligence List, watch list
@version: 3.6
@availability: permanent
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Two lists compiled by Yelan. The Special Attention List is short and names people with the power or means to cause great harm to Liyue: Beidou, the Traveler and certain reclusive adepti. The Secret Intelligence List is more classified and is understood to cover individuals and organisations the Qixing are extremely wary of; Beidou cannot see it. Yelan's stated reason is that Liyue should not be surprised by disaster again as it was 500 years ago.

### STANDARD
