## summary
### LITE
The Tenryou Commission is the Raiden Shogun's executive arm in Inazuma: it keeps public order, commands the military and police, and enforced the Vision Hunt Decree and the Sakoku Decree. It is one of the three bodies of the Tri-Commission and is led by the Kujou Clan. After the Fatui plot in Inazuma was exposed, its Commissioner Kujou Takayuki was removed, and his son Kujou Kamaji now leads it as acting head.
### STANDARD +
Its name refers to Tenryou, the part of Inazuma City directly governed by the Shogun, below Tenshukaku. Its headquarters stand in the Tenshukaku area above Inazuma City, and the Kujou estate is directly adjacent to Tenshukaku.

## structure
### STANDARD
The Commission is led by the head of the Kujou Clan. Its military side is the Shogun's Army, whose generals include Kujou Sara and Kujou Masahito; Masahito commands from the Kujou Encampment on Kannazuka. The wiki lists a chief samurai and security head (Okawara Goemon), a quartermaster (Yasutake) and a Shogunate officer (Toranosuke). Below these are Shogunate samurai (such as Inaba Kyuuzou and Ogura Yuu), yoriki (such as Takasaka Izumi and Iwakura Mitsunari), and doushin, the foot officers who patrol the city. Shikanoin Heizou is listed as a Tenryou "Detective" among the doushin.
### LARGE +
The police station in Tenryou, the street below the castle, is staffed by a yoriki (Owada) and a doushin (Uesugi). The Kujou Clan also keeps retainers of its own, including Ikeda Soushirou and Ippei, who serves Kamaji.

## leadership
### LITE
The Commission is led by Kujou Kamaji, an official who fills in as Proxy Clan Head. Generals Kujou Sara and Kujou Masahito command its forces. Sara is a tengu adopted into the Kujou Clan.
### STANDARD +
Kamaji is the younger son of Takayuki and handles the Commission's government business in Inazuma City. Before the Fatui plot came out he was doubtful about the Vision Hunt and Sakoku Decrees but kept his doubts private. He took over after his father's fall, and Masahito, who as the elder son had the right to the headship, passed it to him because he judged Kamaji the better fit. Kamaji's sweetheart is Hiiragi Chisato, who was left as figurehead of the rival Kanjou Commission after her father's fall.
### LARGE +
Takayuki, the former head, is described as an old man who aged overnight after his exposure. He is under house arrest and was denied bail. He had taken Sara in as an adoptive daughter after she was found as a tengu child with a glowing Vision. Kamaji's relationship with his adoptive sister Sara is amicable.
### ULTRA +
Founders and forebears matter to the Commission's identity. The Kujou Clan is named after its namesake Kujou Shigeyori and is regarded as the most loyal and powerful of the families supporting the Shogun. It includes branch families such as the Takatsukasa. In Reflections of Mortality, Ei says she appoints the Tenryou Commissioner and that anyone who beats her in a duel would take the office. The first Commissioner demonstrated his will with the blade, which is why the title has passed down in the Kujou Clan.

## members
### LARGE
Named staff include Okawara Goemon (Chief Samurai and head of the Kujou Clan's security, who describes himself as the best swordsman in Inazuma), Toranosuke, Yasutake, Ishikawa, Matsudaira, Motosuke, Ono, Sue Yoshitaka, Inaba Kyuuzou, Takasaka Izumi, Iwakura Mitsunari, Mikoshi Genjirou, Owada, Yata Kouki, Kagami Gozen, and the doushin Andou, Amano, Asakawa, Asakura, Inoue, Sanada, Uesugi, Watanabe and Heizou. Not part of the Commission: Naoe Hisamasa, a hatamoto in charge of security at Tenshukaku, tells visitors that he and his men take orders only from the Shogun and directs requests to the Tri-Commission.
### ULTRA +
Okawara Goemon, together with Kinoshita, guarded Thoma during the Vision Hunt Ceremony. After Omnipresence Over Mortals, Goemon can be found in the Commission's headquarters.

## goals
### STANDARD
Officially the Commission preserves order and carries out the Shogun's will. Under Takayuki this meant keeping the Shogun's decrees running at any cost to preserve the Kujou Clan's influence. Under Kamaji it is trying to preserve the clan's honour and adjust to a country that no longer follows the decrees.

## methods
### STANDARD
The Commission runs patrols and police, mans checkpoints, keeps wanted lists and posts notices on Inazuma's bulletin boards. During the Vision Hunt Decree it carried out the confiscation of Visions from ordinary people, setting up the Vision Hunt Ceremony at the Statue of the Omnipresent God. It enforced the Sakoku Decree against illegal entry and exit, while the Kanjou Commission handled legal travel. It was also the Shogun's field force against the Watatsumi Army in the civil war.
### LARGE +
After Thoma escaped the Vision Hunt Ceremony with Paimon and the Traveler in Stillness, the Sublimation of Shadow, the Tenryou Commission put the Traveler on a wanted list. Sara, the Commission's general, kept her own Electro Vision throughout, one of the exceptions the wiki says were made for those who serve the Shogun and remain useful.
### ULTRA +
The wiki says the Commission suffers a higher desertion rate than the other two commissions, because soldiers who joined to fight ancient monsters and the Abyss instead found themselves fighting and killing people under the Vision Hunt Decree. This is supported by a former samurai's last words in an item description, cited by the wiki.

## history
### LARGE
**Before the decrees.** The Kujou Clan is described as the most loyal of the major families serving the Shogun. At some point in the past the clan recruited the swordsman Iwakura Michihiro as its sword instructor and gave him the title "Douin". The Commission had the Shogun's trust over public security in Tenryou and Inazuma.

**The decrees.** A year before the Traveler arrived, the Fatui promised Takayuki support and rewards if he enforced the Vision Hunt Decree. He believed the Fatui could never threaten Inazuma, and, to protect the clan's influence, agreed. He sent the Shogun deceitful, optimistic reports. When Masahito reported the destructive power of the Tatarigami on Yashiori Island and asked for help, Takayuki reacted with anger, suspected incompetence and sent an investigation team to the island.
### ULTRA +
**The exposure.** In Omnipresence Over Mortals (2.1) the Traveler and Sara obtain Takayuki's falsified reports and the Fatui correspondence with the help of the Shuumatsuban ninja Sayu, who infiltrates the Commission. Sara and the Traveler fight through the Tenryou guards to Takayuki, who confirms his selfish reasons. When he learns that Signora is meeting the Shogun at Tenshukaku, Sara runs to confront her. Takayuki lost his position and was placed under house arrest, and Kamaji says he seemed to age decades overnight.

**The duel (2.1).** In Reflections of Mortality, Kamaji has gone to negotiate passing the Commission to the Takatsukasa Clan, and is found held by its head Susumu at a hidden Tenryou base guarded by ronin. Susumu was pressing him for a confession of the Kujou's crimes. Ei, the Raiden Shogun, says that anyone who can beat her in a duel becomes Commissioner, as the first Commissioner once showed his will with the blade, and that the Takatsukasa would have to do the same. Susumu refuses out of fear, and Kamaji challenges her to atone for his father's misconduct. She accepts. He holds his ground until his body gives out. Ei says she admires his honour, though he is not a great heir to the clan, and decides to give the Kujou more time.

**Marriage plot (2.6).** In The Firmiana Leaf Falls (Cypressus Custos Chapter), Hiiragi retainers sent Kamaji a forged love letter in Chisato's name and put out public wedding notices, hoping the marriage would absorb her into the Kujou Clan and end her influence in the Kanjou Commission. Ayato foiled the plot with the Traveler's help, and the wiki says the marriage was postponed indefinitely and the announcements treated as false.

## relations
### LARGE
- **Kanjou Commission and the Hiiragi Clan**: rivals who keep each other in check; both were drawn into the Fatui's scheme, and both now have acting heads. Kamaji and Chisato are close, but their relationship is limited by the clans' rivalry.
- **Yashiro Commission and the Kamisato Clan**: the Yashiro is the only commission that did not take part in the Fatui plot. Ayato sided with Chisato and Kamaji in 2.6.
- **Raiden Shogun**: the Commission is her executive arm and ultimately answers to her.
- **Fatui**: Signora's faction manipulated Takayuki through promises of rewards and chaos, creating a market for Delusions.
- **Watatsumi Army**: the opposing force in the civil war the Vision Hunt Decree caused.
### ULTRA +
- **Thoma and the Kamisato household**: Thoma was the target of the Vision Hunt Ceremony, and the Commission's guards were present at it.
- **Takatsukasa Clan**: a Kujou branch family. Its head Susumu was caught pressuring Kamaji over the Commission and then refused to duel the Shogun for it.

## reputation
### ULTRA
The Commission is remembered as the enforcer of the Vision Hunt Decree. The wiki says desertion was a problem, and Kamaji himself questioned the decrees' cost to ordinary people. After the scandal the wiki describes the Commission as in disarray. The Shogun's choice to give the Kujou more time, after Kamaji's duel, left the clan's honour partly intact.

## secrets
### ULTRA
Takayuki's dealings with the Fatui were hidden from the Shogun, who was fed false reports. The wiki says Takayuki also ordered the Tatarigami investigation team to kill villagers they met and blame the Watatsumi Army. This is attested only by one team's lost text.

## entry: Kujou Takayuki and the Fatui plot
@keys: Kujou Takayuki, Tenryou Commissioner, Takayuki
@keys2: Tenryou, Fatui, Kujou, Vision Hunt
@version: 2.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Kujou Takayuki was head of the Kujou Clan and Tenryou Commissioner until the end of Chapter II. A year before the Traveler arrived the Fatui promised him support and generous rewards if he pushed the Vision Hunt Decree, and he accepted, thinking the Fatui no real threat. He lied to the Shogun in letters. He also adopted Kujou Sara. After the Traveler exposed him in Omnipresence Over Mortals, the Shogun stripped him of his post and he was placed under house arrest, denied bail and awaiting punishment. His son Kamaji replaced him as acting head.

## entry: Kujou Kamaji and the Tenryou duel
@keys: Kujou Kamaji, Kamaji, Proxy Clan Head
@keys2: Tenryou, Kujou, Hiiragi, Chisato
@version: 2.1
### STANDARD
Kamaji is the younger son of Takayuki and acts as clan head. Before the scandal he was an official handling the Commission's government business in Inazuma City and privately questioned whether the decrees were really good for people. After the scandal he says he is trying to stem the chaos and trusts that the next generation can mend its forebears' mistakes. He loves Hiiragi Chisato, the daughter of the Kanjou Commissioner, and they exchange letters. In Reflections of Mortality he challenges the Raiden Shogun to a duel for the Commission, knowing he will lose, and impresses her with his honour.
### LARGE +
Kamaji says he sometimes feels that the burden is too much for someone like him, but that because the Shogun recognised his resolve he cannot disappoint her. He regards his father's actions as wrong and says he does not fully understand them.

## entry: Kujou Sara and Kujou Masahito
@keys: Kujou Sara, Kujou Masahito
@keys2: Tenryou, Shogun's Army, general
@version: 1.6
### STANDARD
Both are generals of the Shogun's Army who serve the Kujou Clan. Masahito, the clan's eldest son, commands from the Kujou Encampment on Kannazuka. He disliked the Vision Hunt Decree and believed it harmed the people, but kept fighting the Watatsumi Army and warned his father about the Tatarigami on Yashiori Island. Sara is the Shogun's aide, an adopted tengu who keeps her Vision as an exception. In 2.1 she works with the Traveler to expose Takayuki. See their own entries for fuller detail.

## entry: Vision Hunt Ceremony enforcement
@keys: Vision Hunt Ceremony, Statue of the Omnipresent God
@keys2: Tenryou, Commission, Visions
@version: 1.6
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
The Tenryou Commission carried out the Vision Hunt Decree: seizing Visions from Inazuma's people and inlaying them in the Statue of the Omnipresent God. By the time of Amidst Stormy Judgment, 99 Visions had been seized. During the ceremony in Stillness, the Sublimation of Shadow, Thoma escaped with the Traveler, and the Commission listed the Traveler as wanted. Okawara Goemon and the samurai Kinoshita guarded Thoma. After the Traveler showed the Shogun the power of the seized Visions' wishes, the decree was repealed and the Visions were returned.

## entry: Duel for the office of Tenryou Commissioner
@keys: duel for the Commissioner, Takatsukasa Susumu, Reflections of Mortality
@keys2: Tenryou, Kamaji, Shogun, Ei
@version: 2.1
### LARGE
In To Hear Mortal Hearts, a quest of Reflections of Mortality, the Shogun and the Traveler find Kujou Kamaji held by Takatsukasa Susumu, head of a Kujou branch family, at a hidden Tenryou base guarded by ronin. Kamaji had gone to negotiate handing the Commission to the Takatsukasa, and Susumu claimed the ronin were for security while pressing him for a confession of the Kujou's crimes. Ei says the office goes to whoever can beat her in a duel, and Susumu backs down. Kamaji accepts the challenge on behalf of the Kujou, fights until he collapses and is cared for afterwards. Ei tells the Traveler that the Shogun puppet would have beheaded him without a second thought, that he is hardly a great heir but shows that the Kujou honour remains, and that she will give the clan a little more time.

## entry: Tenryou Commission Headquarters
@keys: Tenryou Commission Headquarters, Tenryou Commission HQ
@keys2: Tenshukaku, Tenryou
@version: 2.0
### STANDARD
The Commission's headquarters stand in the Tenshukaku area above Inazuma City (the wiki lists it as a point of interest of Tenshukaku). Yoriki Okawara Goemon, Mikoshi Genjirou and Yata Kouki and the doushin Watanabe work there, and the Kujou retainer Ikeda Soushirou appears there as well. The police station and day-to-day patrol offices are in Tenryou, the commercial street below the castle.
