## summary
### LITE
The Yashiro Commission (Sha-bugyou, "Commission of Shrines") is one of the three bodies of Inazuma's Tri-Commission, alongside the Tenryou and Kanjou Commissions. It handles shrines, festivals, ceremonies and the cultural life of the people, and is run by the Kamisato Clan from the Kamisato Estate on Narukami Island. The Commissioner is Kamisato Ayato. His sister Ayaka is its warm public face. Behind the festivals it also commands the secret Shuumatsuban ninja.
### STANDARD +
It has less raw influence than the Tenryou and Kanjou Commissions but is the best liked. Its soldiers are relaxed and care about ordinary people, and in the Inazuma Archon Quests it stayed clear of the Fatui's schemes. All three commissions hold the same authority and can keep one another in check.

## structure
### STANDARD
Authority sits with the Kamisato Clan. Ayato, as Clan Head, is the Commissioner and handles governance, while Ayaka handles the clan's public image and dealings with ordinary people. The Commission is headquartered inside the main building of the Kamisato Estate. Representatives such as Hirano, Hirotatsu and Madarame Hyakubei work at the estate, and other listed staff include Furusawa, Kazumasa, Ogawa and Takami. Shogun's Army soldiers assigned to it are described as more relaxed than those under the other commissions. The clan also owns the members-only Komore Teahouse in Inazuma City and controls the Shuumatsuban.
### LARGE +
The Kamisato Clan's household and the Commission overlap heavily. The clan's housekeeper and well-known fixer, Thoma, also appears on the Commission's staff list. The Traveler compares the headquarters to the Dawn Winery or the Jade Chamber, which are likewise seats of a powerful institution.

## leadership
### LITE
Kamisato Ayato is the Yashiro Commissioner and head of the Kamisato Clan. He keeps a low public profile, works on government affairs and is known as clever, polite and hard to read. His sister Kamisato Ayaka, the Shirasagi Himegimi ("White Heron Princess"), is the clan's popular public face.
### STANDARD +
Ayaka says that after their parents died she and Ayato took responsibility for clan matters together, though she still relies on Ayato for every important decision. Ayato took the post young, after his father's early death, following his parents' wishes and to protect Ayaka. A character story says that at first nobody was impressed by the inexperienced young head, and that he stabilised the clan's position. Thoma, who came to Inazuma from Mondstadt, serves as housekeeper and is a trusted fixer.
### LARGE +
Ayato and Ayaka divided the work deliberately. Ayato avoids public events and handles dealings with Shogunate officials, while Ayaka keeps up the clan's image, which her profile says matters in Inazuman noble politics. Ayato says the Tri-Commission looks calm but is turbulent beneath the surface, and claims he has never dishonoured the clan.

## goals
### STANDARD
Officially the Commission arranges ritual matters, shrines and public festivals, so that both the gods and the people are pleased. Its quieter aim, shown through Ayato, is to protect Inazuma and the Kamisato Clan, deal with corrupt officials, and use the Shuumatsuban where the Commissioner cannot act openly. The Commission also proposed repealing the Vision Hunt Decree, according to Ayaka.

## methods
### STANDARD
Public work includes festival planning, ceremonies and community outreach. Ayaka says the Commission and shrine maidens share festival affairs, and that safety concerns such as fire prevention are planned in advance rather than cancelling events like fireworks. Ayato prefers to deal with corrupt officials within legal bounds, though he uses a wide range of methods.
### LARGE +
Information and discreet fixing are major tools. Thoma builds a large network of contacts, learning first-hand news and striking deals that help the Commission without personal gain. The Shuumatsuban handle espionage, intelligence gathering and covert operations. In Sayu's hangout quests they are shown making blinding powder and faked corpses.

## relations
### STANDARD
Within the Tri-Commission, which divides the executive powers of what Ayaka's profile calls the Triarchal Council, the Yashiro are equal to the Kujou-led Tenryou Commission (security and military) and the Hiiragi-led Kanjou Commission (finance and borders). Ayaka says Yashiro proposals to repeal the Vision Hunt Decree were vetoed by both. Naoe Hisamasa calls the Yashiro the commission closest to the Raiden Shogun. The Grand Narukami Shrine's Guuji Yae Miko and Ayato work together because their duties overlap, though she dislikes his ways and calls him the "Yashiro rascal".
### LARGE +
Ayato says the Kaedehara Clan was once a subordinate branch of the Kamisato Clan. He feels a duty to support Kazuha and says he could help him avoid arrest by the Tenryou Commission. Ayaka says she has had disputes with Kujou Sara of the Tenryou Commission, but that Sara is a loyal servant of Inazuma.

## reputation
### STANDARD
Many Inazumans see it as the least controversial of the commissions, and as caring. After the Archon Quests, which left it mostly unaffected, it gained slightly more influence and goodwill. The public admires Ayaka warmly. Ayato is far less known, and rumours about him run from meticulous festivals to political tricks.

## secrets
### STANDARD
The Shuumatsuban is a secret unit that reports directly to the head of the Kamisato Clan. Ayato inherited command from his father. Most members are orphans taken in young, which Ayato says guarantees loyalty. Members' identities are highly classified, and members who die on secret missions are not publicly honoured.
### LARGE +
Ayato's profile says he risked his life to smuggle the Watatsumi Army into Tenshukaku as a small favour for Ayaka. In the Fatui plot, Ayato's character demo shows him rejecting a Fatui offer of cooperation.

## history
### STANDARD
The Commission's early history is not described. Its recent past is tied to the Kamisato Clan's decline: Ayaka says the clan once failed to protect a swordsmith of national significance. The Fatui harassed it, many clan people were lost, and the Shogunate's trust fell. This aged their father and badly damaged the clan's standing. Ayato rebuilt it, and Ayaka says the Commission is now unified again.
### LARGE +
Thoma's profile places the Kamisato Clan's loss of power ten years before the present, when Ayato was caught in a dispute over succession rights after the death of both parents (Ayato's own story says his father died first, while his mother was also unwell). Several groups tried to exploit the clan's weakness, but Ayato's cunning let him overcome them.
### ULTRA +
Of the three commissions, the Yashiro was the only one not drawn into the Fatui's plans during the Vision Hunt and Sakoku Decrees, per the wiki. After Thoma escaped the Vision Hunt Ceremony in the Archon Quest, the Commission was mostly unaffected.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the Yashiro Commission is active under Ayato and remains based at the Kamisato Estate.
### STANDARD +
The wiki shows no major role for it in the Nod-Krai or Snezhnaya storylines of 6.x or 7.0-7.1.

## entry: Shuumatsuban
@keys: Shuumatsuban, Shuumatsu-ban
@version: 2.0
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
The Shuumatsuban is a secret organisation of ninja under the Yashiro Commission. It is led by the head of the Kamisato Clan, currently Ayato, and its members report to him. Its work is espionage, intelligence gathering and covert action, and its slogan appears to be about sending the Shuumatsu to end traitors and criminals alike. Sayu is described as its resident ninja, and its headquarters is a ninja village. Members are paid monthly: Sayu's salary is 200,000 Mora. Most members are orphans raised from a young age, so loyalty runs deep. Known members include Inu Shoushou and Momoyo, plus staff such as Ogawa and Kano Nana. The retired Nakanishi and Taroumaru are former members.
### LARGE +
The Shuumatsuban can make a toxic elemental powder that can blind, and can fake a death with an artificial cadaver built from meat, white iron and sea ganoderma, a day-long task. Members killed in secret missions are not publicly commemorated.

## entry: Komore Teahouse
@keys: Komore Teahouse, Taroumaru
@version: 2.0
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
The Komore Teahouse in Inazuma City is owned by the Yashiro Commission. Entry is members-only, limited to people the Kamisato Clan approves, and the clan often uses it as a local base and private meeting place. Its boss is Taroumaru, a Shiba Inu and a former Shuumatsuban ninja dog, who was famous with dog lovers, even overseas, before the Sakoku Decree. Other staff include Kozue, Nobumori and the retired Nakanishi. Customers leave payment in a box after drinking tea.

## entry: Kamisato Estate
@keys: Kamisato Estate, Kamisato Yashiki
@version: 2.0
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
The Kamisato Estate is the clan's residence. The Yashiro Commission Headquarters is inside its main building, and staff such as Furuta, Koharu, Hirano, Hirotatsu and Madarame Hyakubei work there. Its map text says the Commission's duties are so pervasive that even Shogunate officials cannot follow them, and that the Shirasagi Himegimi is more popular than the head of the household.
