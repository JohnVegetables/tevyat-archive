## summary
### LITE
The House of Trubetskoy is a noble house of Snezhnaya. Before the Cataclysm, 500 years ago, it held hereditary rights to the title of Grand Duke and to the governorship of Nod-Krai. Its current head is Euphrosiniya Ignatyevna Trubetskaya ("Euphie"), a Privy Councillor and reformer on the Snezhnograd Veche.
### STANDARD +
The house no longer rules Nod-Krai. Shortly after the Cataclysm its last Governor, Brahe, gave up all claim to the borderlands so that they could become an autonomous region.

## leadership
### LITE
Euphrosiniya heads the house today. Its named past heads are Prokhor Trubetskoy and his adoptive son Brahe.
### STANDARD +
Prokhor Trubetskoy was a Snowland Fae Grand Duke and Governor of Nod-Krai. Brahe Prokhorovitch Trubetskoy inherited the title through him; Euphrosiniya notes that under the laws of that time a human inheriting a fae's title was nothing improper. She says Prokhor died in glorious service to the Belyi Tsar. Euphrosiniya takes her duty as head seriously. She says she would quit the Veche tomorrow if she could, but cannot abandon her post in good conscience while the people of Zolnyy lack even the ash they need to stay warm.
### ULTRA +
Euphrosiniya is also the one who speaks for the house in public. She holds that House Trubetskoy has the right to speak plainly, even before the Tsaritsa. She is loyal to Her Majesty's new world but does not think the people of the old one should be left to starve or freeze. In a second dialogue set, after the events of the quest Mistilteinn, she says that she will pay for relief for Zolnyy out of her own pocket if the Veche again refuses, and that she no longer cares what motives people assign her.

## members
### STANDARD
- **Euphrosiniya**: the living head, a Privy Councillor on the Snezhnograd Veche.
- **Brahe**: the last Governor of Nod-Krai and, in his own hidden story, the third bearer of the name Reed Miller.
- **Prokhor Trubetskoy**: a fae Grand Duke and Governor before the Cataclysm; Brahe's adoptive father.
### LARGE +
The wiki also lists Tolfin, a travelling merchant of Nod-Krai, and his grandmother Hiljetta, a veteran Treasure Hoarder, as descendants of Brahe and so as relatives of Euphrosiniya. No living member other than Euphrosiniya is named as a member of the house in her own dialogue.

## goals
### STANDARD
Canon states no collective goal for the house. Euphrosiniya's own aims are practical: she wants the Veche to pass her budget proposals, and above all to improve heating and food for the poor of Zolnyy. She has filed five proposals to heat Zolnyy, and the Speaker has dismissed them all.

## history
### LITE
Under Prokhor Trubetskoy and Brahe the house governed Nod-Krai from a palace that stood where the Lightkeepers' fortress Piramida now stands. After the Cataclysm Brahe gave up the land, and Nod-Krai became autonomous.
### STANDARD +
Prokhor was an unpopular, greedy ruler. When Brahe was five, Avrelian and Solovei tricked him into revealing where his father's treasure was hidden, and then gave the haul to the poor. Years later, Alia of the Treasure Hoarders robbed Prokhor's palace in a single night, and Brahe fell in love with her at first sight. After the Cataclysm, Solovei asked the Tsaritsa to grant the borderlands autonomy. Brahe appeared at the throne beside him and willingly renounced the family's territorial claims, then entrusted the governor's palace to the Lightkeepers, who turned it into a military fortress. That is how modern Nod-Krai began, in Euphrosiniya's telling.
### LARGE +
Brahe's private story is much stranger than the family's official one. After Alia died, he abandoned his noble identity and took the name Reed Miller. He later invented contradictory legends, including a Great Treasure and the Proofs of the Cognoscenti, so that nobody could use the name for serious evil. He spread tales of a failed "new Grand Duke Trubetskoy" looking for the treasure, which was himself. By Brahe's account he faked his own death in the records.
### ULTRA +
Euphrosiniya herself repeats the opposite rumour as a slander on her ancestor: that a bard in the borderlands made up the story of Brahe being made a fool of by Reed Miller's companion and greedily seeking a "Great Treasure". She says she would very much like to know who started it. She tells the story as a family defence and gives no sign of knowing the truth behind it.

## relations
### STANDARD
- **Snezhnograd Veche**: Euphrosiniya sits on it as a Privy Councillor and clashes with the Speaker, whom she calls a fae and not truly human.
- **The Tsaritsa**: Euphrosiniya speaks of her with respect and as the one who will forge a new world.
- **Lightkeepers**: Brahe handed over the governor's palace to them, and Piramida was built on the ruins of Prokhor's castle.
### LARGE +
- **Treasure Hoarders and Reed Miller**: the house is tied to the legend through Brahe, the third Reed Miller, who knew the first two (Avrelian and Alia).
- **Nobility of Snezhnaya**: Euphrosiniya despises the "incompetent nobles" on the Veche who pass the blame.

## reputation
### LARGE
Euphrosiniya presents the house as honourable and loyal. In Nod-Krai its past is remembered through Reed Miller folklore, in which Prokhor appears as a greedy duke and Brahe as a bard or a gullible nobleman, and the house's name is attached to a hunt for a fabricated treasure. Euphrosiniya resents these stories.

## secrets
### ULTRA
The main hidden fact is that Brahe, the last Governor, lived on as Reed Miller and invented the legends of the Great Treasure. Euphrosiniya does not mention it, so it is not shown to be known within the house.

## entry: Euphrosiniya Trubetskaya
@keys: Euphrosiniya, Euphie, Euphrosiniya Ignatyevna Trubetskaya
@keys2: Trubetskoy, Veche, Zolnyy
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
Euphrosiniya Ignatyevna Trubetskaya, known as Euphie, is a Privy Councillor and Veche Councillor of the Snezhnograd Veche and head of House Trubetskoy. She can be met at the Snezhnograd Veche State Hall muttering about a budget proposal. After six months of watching nobles pass blame to one another, she says she would resign if she could, but feels bound by duty to the people of Zolnyy. She is a reformer and an open opponent of the Speaker, and she says that if the proposal fails again she will pay for the relief herself.

## entry: Prokhor and Brahe, Governors of Nod-Krai
@keys: Prokhor Trubetskoy, Brahe, Brahe Prokhorovitch Trubetskoy, Governor of Nod-Krai
@version: 6.3
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
Prokhor Trubetskoy, a fae Grand Duke, governed Nod-Krai before the Cataclysm from a palace on the spot that is now Voidsea Outlook, and the Lightkeepers' Piramida stands on its foundations. His adoptive son Brahe was a self-described realist who believed in the "soil of malice" in human nature. After the deaths of his idealist friends Avrelian and Alia, Brahe built a mock legend of Reed Miller's Great Treasure and left it for a worthy successor. Euphrosiniya's own account stops at his renouncing the territory and does not include any of this.

## entry: The renunciation of Nod-Krai
@keys: autonomy of Nod-Krai, Brahe's renunciation
@keys2: Trubetskoy, Solovei, Tsaritsa
@version: 6.3
### LARGE
Shortly after the Cataclysm, Solovei the Torchforger asked the Tsaritsa to grant the borderlands autonomy. Brahe Trubetskoy came before the throne in the Zapolyarny Palace and willingly gave up all claim to the land and everything on it. He gave the governor's palace to the Lightkeepers, who made it a fortress. The Trubetskoy line therefore lost its title and territory by choice, rather than through conquest.
