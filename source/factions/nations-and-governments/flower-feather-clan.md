## summary
### LITE
The Flower-Feather Clan, whose older name is Tlalocan, is one of Natlan's six tribes. Its people live alongside the Qucusaurs, the proudest of the flying Saurians, and its elite flying squad patrols the skies on Qucusaur-back. The settlement sits among the cliffs of Quahuacan Cliff in northern Natlan, overlooking the ruins of Ochkanatlan. The current chief is Mutota. Its most famous member is Chasca, the Peacemaker who bears the Ancient Name Vuka.
### STANDARD +
Some clan members, like the Qucusaurs, have mastered riding Natlan's hot currents of gaseous Phlogiston without being burned. Joining the flying squad means passing a famously hard trial, and those who give up are called the "Wingless". The tribe was founded thousands of years ago by Lianca, a girl raised by dragons, as a refuge for people who had nowhere else to go.

## structure
### STANDARD
A chief leads the tribe, advised by elders. Its pride and best-known institution is the Qucusaur Riders, the flying squad, which has both active and reserve members. Any applicant must be a capable warrior and must also win a Qucusaur's trust; the flying trials are held at a trial arena in the valley below the settlement. People who repeatedly fail, or who stop trying, are called the "Wingless". Many keep retaking the trials, some leave to work for other tribes, and some find other work in the clan. Clan members summon their Saurian companions by whistling or blowing a horn.
### LARGE +
The clan also has Peacemakers, mediators who settle disputes between people, between tribes, between humans and Saurians, and even between Saurians. Chasca is the most famous, and by her own account she named the role after a lesson about communication being humanity's strongest weapon. Other roles seen in the settlement include a tribal doctor (Chuychu, who specialised in Abyssal corruption), a Saurian veterinarian (Ifa), a trial-arena administrator, hot-air-balloon operators and a reputation advisor, Abundi, who handles the clan's dealings with outsiders.

## leadership
### LITE
Chief Mutota is a scarred, grey-haired veteran who values patience and caution above bravado. He likes to remind people that scars are a badge of honour but also a record of mistakes.
### STANDARD +
Mutota led the defence during the Abyss invasion and then the rebuilding. He accepted Iansan's help and asked the Traveler and Chasca to look into trouble inside the tribe. He gets on with ordinary members as well: Pixica calls him "Boss", and he coaches Wingless applicants himself. When the elder Allpa tried to depose him, he escaped capture only because he was out coaching, and he quickly rallied the tribe against her.
### ULTRA +
Earlier leaders are known only in fragments. Lianca, the founder and "First Speaker", wanted no titles or formalities and asked no one to submit to her. In the distant past Chief Imara hosted the Bloomflower Trials for his brother Nampole and then failed him, as told in a limited-time event (5.5). Mutota himself told Kachina he knew nothing about that old quarrel.

## members
### STANDARD
- **Chasca**: Peacemaker and flying-squad warrior, bearer of the Ancient Name Vuka. She was raised by the Qucusaur Chimpu and adopted by Cusco's family.
- **Ifa**: a renowned Saurian veterinarian, known as the "Tlalocan Vet".
- **Mutota**: the chief.
- **Allpa**: a former elder and Flying Captain who found the infant Chasca. She is now imprisoned for her coup.
- **Cusco**: Chasca's adoptive father and Chuychu's father. He also checks injured Saurians.
### LARGE +
- **Chuychu**: Cusco's daughter, Chasca's younger sister and the clan's doctor for Abyssal corruption. She died in the Abyss invasion.
- **Pixica**: a Wingless warrior who chose to stay and now runs the hot-air balloons.
- **Coyllur and Zhunan**: Wingless applicants. Mutota coached them during the Phlogiston Wings crisis.
- **Coya**: a Qucusaur, Chimpu's daughter and Chasca's "sister". She is listed with the clan because of her bond with Chasca.
### ULTRA +
Historical members include the founder Lianca, the Pyro-wielding hero Menilek (an earlier bearer of Vuka, who rode a Qucusaur and died in a war against the Abyss), the girl Bona and the Ochkanatlan Adventure Team members Taika and Fietena (all killed by Waxaklahun Ubah Kan), the brothers Imara and Nampole, and the Scarlet-Eyed Youth who became Pyro Archon. Hawka is a former member exiled for an unstated crime.

## goals
### STANDARD
The clan's ideals go back to Lianca: a place where the weak can live freely and without fear, where no one has to kneel to anyone. In practice its people guard their own territory from the air, keep watch over the ruins of Ochkanatlan, and protect the bond between riders and Qucusaurs that the clan is built on. Chasca says that clan pride, and especially the riders' pride, sometimes "borders on arrogance".

## methods
### STANDARD
Riders fly Qucusaurs on patrol and in battle. Qucusaurs and some clan members can use the high-temperature currents of gaseous Phlogiston without being burned. Some members, such as the trainee Kallfu, ride the currents without any equipment. Riders are expected to train relentlessly: a Qucusaur that is not fully under control can kill both itself and its rider. To other tribes, a lone Flower-Feather member who starts whistling is a sign that something nearby is wrong.
### LARGE +
Since the fall of Ochkanatlan the clan has kept watch over the ruins, and at some point it set up a blockade to stop travellers from entering. Its weapons include firearms: Xilonen built Chasca's oversized gun, and Xilonen called it the strongest weapon she had ever made "for your tribe". The tribe's own Peacemakers prefer to settle conflicts with words first.

## history
### LARGE
**Founding.** Thousands of years ago, the abandoned girl Lianca was raised by the Eighth Sovereign Lord, a dragon who taught her to "kneel to no one". She left on her own journey, came to see how rare freedom was, and gathered other orphans. Then came warriors who would not live under other tribes' rules, and finally banished dragons, and she took in friend and foe alike. Lianca travelled with Xbalanque and later joined Och-Kan's Grand Alliance. As Och-Kan became obsessed, she refused his orders to send more labourers. Her own son, who was also her chosen successor, secretly turned Och-Kan against her, and she died fighting his forces. By legend she shot her treacherous son with her first arrow, and with her last pinned her own body to her bow rather than fall.

**Liberation from Och-Kan.** Under Och-Kan, people sympathetic to Saurians were persecuted. A tribal elder of Tlalocan banished a scarlet-eyed youth to save him from capture. The youth later gathered heroes against Och-Kan and burned the "ineffable city", freeing the Flower-Feather Clan, and he became the second Pyro Archon about 2,000 years ago. He asked that his name be erased so that others would take up his mantle. Since then the clan has lived on the cliffs, looking down on the ruins of Ochkanatlan.

**The Abyss invasion (5.1).** When the Abyss attacked all of Natlan, the Flower-Feather Clan was hit hardest. Chuychu died of Abyssal corruption, and in that moment Chasca was acknowledged by her Ancient Name and became the sixth hero.
### ULTRA +
**Aftermath (5.2).** Mutota sent teams into the wilds to clear out monsters and bring back missing warriors. Some warriors presumed dead were found alive but badly contaminated by the Abyss, and they suffered lasting terrors. Iansan and the Traveler helped treat them. Chasca, now more reckless after her sister's death, worried her father Cusco. Then the flying trials reopened, merchants arrived selling Phlogiston Wings, jetpacks that let the Wingless fly, and a Qucusaur began attacking people. The investigation showed that the attacker was Chasca's Qucusaur sister Coya, whose hatchling had been stolen. Behind it all was Elder Allpa, who was using her dead son Tepal's name. She had Qucusaurs captured and drained of Phlogiston to power the wings, and she planned to overthrow Mutota. Her plan collapsed, and part of her flying workshop, which ran on an Ochkanatlan relic, became her prison.

**After Natlan's war (5.8).** Ineffa was found in a weakened state on a beach near the clan and taken in, and the Traveler followed her into the sealed Temple of Cascading Colors, where they learned that Lianca had once rescued Ineffa's mind. Pyro Archon Mavuika then arranged with the clan's elders to set up a simple harbor at Quahuacan Cliff, where people fleeing Nod-Krai are received and registered.

## relations
### LARGE
- **Qucusaurs**: the clan's partners and the core of its identity. Riders must earn their trust. Allpa's betrayal, which treated Qucusaurs as a resource, horrified the tribe.
- **Other tribes**: the clan is one of six. Iansan of the Collective of Plenty came to help rebuild, and Xilonen of the Children of Echoes makes the clan's weapons. Allpa's son Tepal was raised among the Children of Echoes.
- **The Pyro Archon**: Mavuika visited Cusco's family to give her condolences after Chuychu's death and later worked with the clan's elders on the Nod-Krai harbor. The clan counts the second Pyro Archon among its own.
- **Ochkanatlan**: an old wound. The clan once served in Och-Kan's Grand Alliance and was later liberated from it. Mutota was disgusted to learn that the Phlogiston Wings were based on Ochkanatlan technology.
### ULTRA +
- **Fatui**: in 5.8 Fatui agents caused trouble near the clan to lure Ineffa away. The Liyue spy Zhuoxin, who had been working for them, reasoned that once he was dead, the Fatui would not risk the Flower-Feather Clan's anger by going after his family. He and his family now live with the clan, and he advises on the new harbor.
- **Fontaine and Mondstadt**: during a limited-time event (6.7), Chasca and Chevreuse agreed to plan a long-term exchange in which clan warriors would train with Fontaine's Special Patrol and Special Patrol shooters would train in Natlan, sharing the results with the Knights of Favonius.

## reputation
### LARGE
Natlan knows the clan for its flying squad and its proud riders. Furnishing text says that nearly every member dreams of joining the flying squad and setting off on a "crusade to conquer the sky". Other tribes treat the clan's warning whistles seriously. Chasca won the clan new respect when she competed for it in the Pilgrimage of the Return of the Sacred Flame, though people in the clan once called her a "little vulture" before she became a respected Peacemaker.

## status
@version: 5.8
### LITE
As of 7.1 the clan has recovered from the Abyss invasion under Chief Mutota, and its flying trials have resumed. Allpa and her accomplices are in custody.
### STANDARD +
Since 5.8 the clan has run a simple harbor at Quahuacan Cliff for people coming from Nod-Krai. No clan story developments were found in the 6.x Nod-Krai or 7.0-7.1 Snezhnaya Archon Quests. In a 6.7 limited-time event, Chasca agreed to plan a training exchange with Fontaine's Special Patrol, but whether it has started is not shown.

## entry: Qucusaur Riders and the flying trials
@keys: Qucusaur Riders, flying squad, flying trials, Rider Exams, flight trial arena
### STANDARD
The Qucusaur Riders are the Flower-Feather Clan's elite flying squad. Applicants must prove themselves as warriors and then win a Qucusaur's approval in the flying trials, held at an arena in the valley below the settlement. Losing a Qucusaur's trust is the most common reason for failure, and the clan treats it seriously because an uncontrolled flight can kill both rider and Saurian. Chasca, who was raised by Qucusaurs, says even she had to earn their approval. Applicants call Qucusaurs by whistle or horn, and a weak, unsure whistle will not bring one. After the Abyss invasion the trials were held again. The arrival of Phlogiston Wings led Chasca to argue that wing users should be judged by different standards.

## entry: The Wingless
@keys: Wingless
@keys2: Flower-Feather, Tlalocan, Qucusaur, flying trials, Allpa, Pixica, Coyllur
### STANDARD
"Wingless" is the clan's name for people who fail the flying trials repeatedly or give up on them. They must give up their Saurian companion for their own safety, and life in the tribe can be hard for them. Some keep trying. Allpa eventually passed. Others, like Pixica, who runs the hot-air balloons, make peace with it and find other ways to reach the sky. Chasca says that only the best warriors attempt the trials at all, and that the clan quietly watches over the Wingless until their fighting spirit returns, as Qucusaurs watch their unfledged young. Allpa used the Wingless' longing to fly to win support for her Phlogiston Wings.

## entry: Peacemakers of the Tlalocan
@keys: Peacemaker, Peacemakers, Peacemaker of the Tlalocan
### STANDARD
The Peacemakers are the Flower-Feather Clan's mediators. As a child, Chasca was so prone to fighting that Allpa taught her from a woven scroll titled "Peacemaker - the Art of Communication", which taught that language is humanity's greatest weapon. Chasca later adopted the name for herself and her role. Peacemakers step into disputes between people, tribes, humans and Saurians, or Saurians among themselves, and settle them with words first and force only when words fail. In one story a pair of feuding chiefs signed a very formal joint statement thanking the Peacemaker for their "reconciliation".

## entry: Lianca, founder of the Flower-Feather Clan
@keys: Lianca, First Speaker of the Flower-Feather Clan
@version: 5.8
### LARGE
Lianca, the clan's founder and first bearer of the Ancient Name Vuka, was an abandoned infant raised by the Eighth Sovereign Lord (Ix Ajkotz'i'j Iq'ismal Waxak), who gave her the bow Chain Breaker. She built the tribe from orphans, rule-breaking warriors and banished dragons, accepting anyone. She journeyed with Xbalanque and joined the Grand Alliance, then defied Och-Kan. She also saw through a scheme by the Eleventh Lord, imprisoned its vengeful half in the Temple of Cascading Colors, and sent its benevolent half, later rebuilt as Ineffa, away from Natlan. Her son, also her chosen successor, betrayed her to Och-Kan, and she died in battle refusing to kneel. Afterwards, a former clansman tried to seize the tribe's banner by marrying her second daughter, who lured him into the warehouse where it was kept and set it on fire, killing them both.

## entry: The Scarlet-Eyed Youth of Tlalocan
@keys: Scarlet-Eyed Youth, scarlet-eyed youth of Tlalocan
@keys2: Flower-Feather, Tlalocan, Pyro Archon, Och-Kan, Sakkuk
@spoiler: minor
### LARGE
The second Pyro Archon, about 2,000 years ago, was a young man of Tlalocan with red eyes. Under Och-Kan, people who sympathised with Saurians were persecuted, so the tribe's elder banished him to save him from capture. After trials in which he faced his own death, he gathered heroes such as Sakkuk against Och-Kan, burned the ineffable city and freed the Flower-Feather Clan. He then asked Sakkuk to erase his name from history, so that others would be inspired to take up his mantle. His real name is unknown.

## entry: Allpa's coup and the Phlogiston Wings
@keys: Phlogiston Wings, Allpa's coup, Tepal's workshop
@version: 5.2
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
After the Abyss invasion, merchants brought Phlogiston Wings to the clan. These Phlogiston jetpacks, steered with a handheld trackball, let the Wingless fly without a Saurian. A Qucusaur kept attacking the merchants. It was Coya, whose hatchling Chime had been stolen by hunters supplying the wings' maker, supposedly the failed applicant Tepal. Chasca, the Traveler and Coya traced the operation to a flying workshop above the trial grounds, powered by an Ochkanatlan relic, where Qucusaurs were being drained of the Phlogiston their glands produce. Tepal had long been dead, and the mastermind was his mother, Elder Allpa. She blamed Qucusaurs for his death, saw them as beasts that cared only for themselves, and believed the tribe's safety could not be entrusted to wild Saurians. She threatened the caged Chime to force the group to surrender and sent followers to seize Mutota, but Mutota was away coaching, and her coup collapsed. Coya shielded Chasca from the exploding relic, the two "sisters" made peace, and part of the dismantled workshop became the jail for Allpa and her followers.

## entry: The Flower-Feather Clan in the Abyss invasion
@keys: Chuychu, Flower-Feather Clan invasion
@version: 5.1
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
During the nationwide Abyss invasion (5.1), Abyssal "tumours" fell across Natlan, and the Flower-Feather Clan suffered the worst losses. Chuychu, Chasca's sister and the clan's doctor for Abyssal corruption, was overwhelmed on the way to the Stadium of the Sacred Flame. She died after thanking Chasca for being her sister, and at that moment Chasca was acknowledged by the Ancient Name Vuka. Chuychu's name is among the dead in the Record Penned by All. Afterwards, Mutota still caught himself calling for her help. In the aftermath, missing warriors were found in the wilds suffering lasting Abyssal terrors, and Iansan and the Traveler helped treat them.

## entry: Flower-Feather Clan settlement
@keys: Flower-Feather Clan settlement, Quahuacan Cliff, Where Feathers Are Intertwined Like Flowers
@version: 5.8
### STANDARD
The tribe's home sits on Quahuacan Cliff in northern Natlan, looking down on the ruins of Ochkanatlan. It is a settlement of floating platforms, hot-air balloons, Saurian stables and training grounds, with the flight-trial arena down in the valley. Residents include Chief Mutota, Saurian riders, guards, the trial-arena administrator Inkanak, the balloon operators Pixica and Zyanya, and the reputation advisor Abundi. A message board carries the vet Ifa's notes reminding people to bring their Saurians for regular check-ups. From 5.8 a simple harbor below the cliff receives people coming from Nod-Krai.

## entry: Bloomflower Trials of Chief Imara
@keys: Bloomflower Trials, Imara, Nampole
@version: 5.5
@availability: limited
### ULTRA
During the limited-time event Tournament of Glory in Bloom (5.5), the Traveler uncovered an old episode of clan history. Nampole, a Saurian vet, asked his brother, Chief Imara, to host the Bloomflower Trials so that Sacred Flame Crystals could be formed to cure his sick Saurian, Antu. Imara kept crystals for himself, ignored excessive violence during the trials, and spent all of them on tribal research after Antu disappeared. Nampole left the tribe with Antu for good. Ifa is a descendant of the family. Today's chief, Mutota, knew nothing of the story.
