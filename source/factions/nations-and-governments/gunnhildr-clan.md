## summary
### LITE
The Gunnhildr Clan is Mondstadt's oldest family of knights. Its motto is "For Mondstadt, as always". Its roots go back to the priestess Gunnhildr, who stood with Barbatos against Decarabian, and the clan stayed loyal to the people through the age of the aristocracy. Many Knights of Favonius and Church clergy come from it. Jean is its best-known member today.
### STANDARD +
The clan was one of the founding houses of the aristocratic age, alongside the Lawrence and Imunlaukr clans, but unlike the Lawrences it never turned tyrant. When Vennessa rebelled, the Gunnhildrs sided with the people and were not exiled.

## history
### LITE
The clan traces itself to Gunnhildr, daughter of a nomadic clan leader who left Decarabian's city. She led her people alongside the wind spirit that became Barbatos and crowned him with laurels after Decarabian fell. In the aristocratic age the clan kept its oath to protect Mondstadt, and it sided with Vennessa's rebellion about 1,000 years ago.
### STANDARD +
**Decarabian's era.** The Biography of Gunnhildr says the clan's leader had served Decarabian and led his people out of the city rather than live under a despot. In the blizzards beyond the walls, his daughter Gunnhildr prayed, and a wind spirit heard her. The faith of her people let it build a small shelter and gave her the power to protect them. After her father died she became chief and the clan's first priestess. A shrine in the Thousand Winds Temple, where the clan prayed to Istaroth, is attested in weapon lore.

**After Decarabian.** When Barbatos won, the clan settled north in an old fortress. That land later became Dornman Port, and an NPC says the clan founded Mondstadt's first sanctuary of Barbatos there. Later, as the Church became a puppet of the nobility, the Gunnhildrs of the time threw the bishop sent from Mondstadt out of the sanctuary tower and tore it down, and the port has had no church since.
### LARGE +
**Vennessa's rebellion.** The clan sided with the people and escaped the exile imposed on the other noble houses. A loading-screen text says Vennessa won the clan over before setting up the resistance's first base at Dornman Port. Afterwards, the wiki says, the clan led a push to abolish hereditary fiefs.

**The cataclysm era.** Dornman Port was a front line when the Knights fought the monsters of the cataclysm 500 years ago, as the Knights' own history records.
### ULTRA +
**Earlier roots (6.5).** Weapon lore released in 6.5 describes a knight named Gunnhildr, the Knight of the Golden Bough, from fallen Arcadia. Prophecy named him Arcadia's last hope. He went north to Snezhnaya for the Golden Bough, beat the Jack Frost King and found that the Bough had already lost its blessing. He then gave up the quest, gathered willing survivors and led them south to the tower where Decarabian ruled. The wiki treats him as a distinct, earlier bearer of the name and says the clan descends from the same hallowed Arcadian line. The relationship between his story and that of the priestess is not explained in canon.

## structure
### STANDARD
The clan is a family house of knights and clergy, not an organisation with ranks. Its identity is carried by its motto and its tradition of service. No head of the clan is named in canon. A cadet branch, the Dornmannshaven family, uses the motto "Sail Toward Freedom".
### LARGE +
Swordsmanship became required of nobles, and the Royal Longsword's text suggests the habit grew out of the Gunnhildr culture of always being ready to protect Mondstadt. The wiki also notes that the oath of the ancient Mondstadters echoes the clan's own saying.

## members
### LITE
Jean Gunnhildr, Master of the Knights of Favonius, is the clan's heir and public face. Her mother is Frederica Gunnhildr, the Alder Knight. Her younger sister Barbara Pegg was born into the clan but left with their father, Seamus Pegg.
### STANDARD +
Jean was raised by Frederica, with a strict regime, to succeed her as the clan's heir. Her profile says a Gunnhildr learns to say the motto before saying "Mommy". Frederica is a captain and long-serving front-line commander. Seamus Pegg, a former adventurer, is the Church's Cardinal of Daybreak and Seneschal. The parents' marriage ended; Barbara joined the Church and became a deaconess. Eckhard Gunnhildr is a modern historian of the clan and the author of the Biography of Gunnhildr. Historical bearers of the name are the priestess Gunnhildr and the Knight of the Golden Bough.

## goals
### STANDARD
The clan's only stated purpose is its oath to the Anemo Archon: to protect Mondstadt, its land and all its people. The motto sums this up.

## relations
### STANDARD
- **Knights of Favonius and Church of Favonius**: the clan supplies many knights and clergy, and its members hold senior posts in both. Frederica and Jean are knights; Seamus and Barbara serve the Church.
- **Barbatos**: Gunnhildr crowned the new Anemo Archon, and the clan has served him since.
- **Lawrence Clan**: the Biography contrasts the Gunnhildrs with the scheming Lawrences. Eula, of the disgraced Lawrence line, serves under Jean's command today.
- **Ragnvindr Clan**: the other house known to have escaped exile.
- **Imunlaukr Clan**: the third founding house of the aristocracy, not mentioned in the present day.

## reputation
### STANDARD
Gunnhildrs are seen as reliable and old-fashioned in the best sense. Jean is trusted across Mondstadt, and the Alder Knight is a heroine to the young. The weight of the name is also a burden: Jean's profile says the lineage can feel heavy.

## status

### LITE
As of 7.1 the clan remains in service. Jean is Acting Grand Master of the Knights of Favonius.
### STANDARD +
Frederica went north with Varka's expedition as second-in-command, was later sent back to defend Dornman Port, and is now said to be on a secret mission known in detail only to Jean. No clan developments from 6.7 to 7.1 were found.

## entry: Biography of Gunnhildr
@keys: Biography of Gunnhildr, Eckhard Gunnhildr
### STANDARD
The Biography of Gunnhildr is a history of the clan's apical ancestor, compiled by Eckhard Gunnhildr, a modern Mondstadt historian, from fragments of aristocratic-age legend. A copy can be found in Dawn Winery. It tells the priestess Gunnhildr's story and ends by predicting that the clan will keep its duty to the Anemo Archon.

## entry: Gunnhildr family motto
@keys: For Mondstadt as always, Gunnhildr motto
### STANDARD
"For Mondstadt, as always" is the clan's motto. Jean links it to the sense of duty that made her give up childhood play, and her Vision story says she heard it in her mind when she received her Vision. The cadet Dornmannshaven branch has its own motto, "Sail Toward Freedom".

## entry: Dornman Port and the clan
@keys: Dornman Port, Thorn King
@keys2: Gunnhildr, clan, fief, ancestors
### LARGE
Dornman Port lies in the lands of the clan's old hereditary fief. Loading-screen text says it was named after the Thorn King, an ancestor of the clan. Mondstadt's first sanctuary of Barbatos stood there. The port has no church because the Gunnhildrs of the late aristocratic age tore down the sanctuary after throwing the bishop out. In 6.x, Frederica was sent back to Dornman Port to help defend it.

## entry: Gunnhildr and Istaroth
@keys: Istaroth
@keys2: Gunnhildr, Thousand Winds Temple
@version: 6.5
### ULTRA
Weapon lore says the clan once revered Istaroth, the God of Time, as their lady, and the Sacrificial Fragments text says they built a shrine to her high on a cliff in the Thousand Winds Temple. Sacrificial Sword text says Mondstadt later worshipped Anemo and Time together, with the Gunnhildrs staging defence rites using prop weapons. No source records when worship of Istaroth stopped.

## entry: Knight of the Golden Bough
@keys: Knight of the Golden Bough, Golden Bough
@keys2: Gunnhildr, Arcadia
@version: 6.5
@spoiler: minor
### ULTRA
In Arcadia's last days a prophecy named a knight, Gunnhildr, as its hero: if the pure Golden Bough were found, the withered sacred wood might revive. He travelled to a sacred grove in Snezhnaya and beat the Jack Frost King, then found that the Bough had lost its blessing. Giving up on restoring his homeland, he turned south and led the willing survivors to Decarabian's tower. His fate is unknown. The wiki says his descendants continued the line of the Gunnhildr Clan.
