## summary
### LITE
The Knights of Favonius are Mondstadt's order of knights, founded about 1,000 years ago by Vennessa, with Barbatos' help, so that aristocrats could never rule again. They guard the city and its roads, keep order, make and enforce the law, and help manage the Church of Favonius. In practice they are Mondstadt's government. Their headquarters is in Mondstadt City.
### STANDARD +
Nobody in the order was knighted by royalty. In Mondstadt, "knight" is a title earned through the duties a person takes on.

## leadership
### LITE
Grand Master Varka, the Knight of Boreas, is a huge, cheerful, famously strong man who hates paperwork. Jean Gunnhildr, the Master and Dandelion Knight, has governed as Acting Grand Master since Varka left on a long expedition half a year before the Traveler arrived.
### STANDARD +
Boreas (Andrius) recognised Varka as the north wind's human champion, and Jean calls him "the legend of his generation". Jean was made Dandelion (Lionfang) Knight at fifteen and was promoted from captain to Master during the Eroch affair. Varka has told her that if he does not return, she is to succeed him and train the next Knight of Boreas. Officers the Traveler meets most often:
- Kaeya: Cavalry Captain and Quartermaster
- Albedo: Chief Alchemist and Investigation Captain
- Eula: Reconnaissance Captain
- Hertha: Logistics Captain and Coordinator
- Note and Skeiron: captains of the 2nd and 3rd Companies
- Lohen: Vice Captain of the Ranged Company
- Lisa: Librarian
- Amber: the last Outrider
### ULTRA +
Past leaders shape the order's identity. Vennessa was the first Grand Master and Dandelion Knight. Five hundred years ago, Grand Master Arundolyn, the "Lion of Light", led alongside the Master Rostam, the "Wolf Pup" and Knight of Boreas. Shortly before Varka, Valentine was Grand Master and Lionfang Knight. He was the one who sent the young Varka abroad to find his own reason for knighthood. Varka's generation of senior knights included Frederica Gunnhildr (the Alder Knight, Jean's mother and, as stated during a limited-time event, the expedition's second-in-command), Adorno (the Benevolent Knight, Captain of the Ranged Company) and Feroux (a former Cavalry Captain). Varka's friend Crepus Ragnvindr once dreamed of knighthood but ran the Dawn Winery. Crepus's son Diluc became the youngest Cavalry Captain in the order's history at fourteen and resigned four years ago.

## structure
### STANDARD
A single Grand Master leads the order. The Master is the reserve and becomes Acting Grand Master whenever the Grand Master is away. Below them are ten Captains, each heading a numbered company; the companies can be disbanded and re-formed as needs change. Their specialties include general affairs, reconnaissance, ranged fire, logistics, technical support, cavalry and alchemical investigation. There are also special units such as the Outriders. Becoming a knight takes written and practical exams. Not every member is a knight: Sucrose is an Assistant Alchemist, and Noelle serves as the Knights' maid while she keeps trying to pass.
### LARGE +
Some knights carry personal epithets: Eula is the Spindrift Knight, Amber the Crimson Knight and Klee the Spark Knight. Klee belongs to no company because she is so young. By tradition, the titles "Lion" and "Wolf" go only to those with great leadership potential, who are expected to become Grand Master one day. "Lion" comes from Vennessa's Dandelion or Lionfang title, which Jean holds; "Wolf" comes from the Knight of Boreas, which Varka holds. Before Eroch's fall there was also an Inspector, who was trusted with Mondstadt's affairs while the Grand Master was away. Most trials are held by the Knights or by the Church alone, but Albedo's trial in 5.6 required both together.

## goals
### STANDARD
The Knights exist to protect Mondstadt and the freedom Vennessa won. That means keeping monsters, bandits and foreign schemes away from the people, upholding order fairly, and making sure no tyrant ever rules again. Under Varka they also strike at distant dangers, such as the Abyss in the north, before those dangers reach home.

## members
### LARGE
Playable members include Jean, Varka, Kaeya, Albedo, Eula, Amber, Lisa, Klee, Noelle, Mika, Lohen and Sucrose (staff, not a knight). The Traveler holds the honorary title of Honorary Knight for resolving the Stormterror crisis. Diluc is a former Cavalry Captain who is now openly critical of the Knights. Razor is not a knight, but Varka named him and taught him the claymore. Mika is a surveyor of the Reconnaissance Company. Varka trained him personally and placed him on the expedition's front line.
### ULTRA +
Named rank-and-file knights include Guy, who began training at 8 and became a squire at 16; Field Officer Heinz (1st Company); Disciplinary Officer Wernher (2nd); and Field Officer Nymph (8th), who opposed the plan to make the academic Lisa her captain. Lisa first joined the Support Company. Varka wanted her as its captain, but after a short contest with Nymph, arranged on Kaeya's suggestion, she declined and chose the library. The Favonius Keep in Nod-Krai is supervised by Anselm, a former 6th Company captain. Ekbert, Erminie, Laurent, Leifhelm, Nannina and Winfried are among the knights stationed there. Godwin missed the expedition's departure because he was searching for a misplaced good-luck charm.

## methods
### LARGE
Most of the order's work is everyday service: patrols, escorts, monster bounties, citizens' troubles and record-keeping. Heavier work goes to specialist companies, which scout the wilds, garrison Dornman Port, provide ranged support, carry out alchemical investigation and make cavalry strikes. Knights learn Favonius Bladework, the sword style Rostam developed 500 years ago. Conduct is governed by the Knights of Favonius Handbook, which Jean re-edited. It rests on three virtues, modesty, honesty and self-control, because "freedom cannot exist without the support of duty".
### ULTRA +
Varka has modernised the order. The Ranged Company now has firearms, the Support Company builds modern fortifications, and he works to train a capable new generation instead of relying on a few heroes. On campaign, knights sharpen their tactics at the game Tabletop Troupe, with Varka as Game Master. When they drink, one knight chosen at random stays sober to keep watch. The order also works quietly when it must. Kaeya trades in information with the city's underside, and in 5.6 Albedo and the Knights spent half a year staging a sham murder trial to flush out a disguised enemy.

## history
### LARGE
**Founding.** About 1,000 years ago the Lawrence-led aristocracy ruled Mondstadt as tyrants. Vennessa, an enslaved Muratan gladiator, led a rebellion with Barbatos, Ragnvindr and others. Afterwards most nobles were exiled, though uncorrupted houses such as the Gunnhildrs stayed. Vennessa founded the Knights as their first Grand Master and Dandelion Knight and restored the Church. A loading-screen tip says she set up the resistance's first base at Dornman Port after winning over the Gunnhildr clan. She later ascended to Celestia at Windrise, where a great oak grew.

**The cataclysm.** Five hundred years ago, Grand Master Arundolyn and the Master Rostam led the Knights against the monsters that poured out after Khaenri'ah fell, and Dornman Port was one of the front lines. Rostam died, and Arundolyn gave up his title and his sword.

**Varka's era.** Varka's campaigns brought Mondstadt an unusual peace. Four years ago, while he was abroad, Inspector Eroch's treachery led to Crepus Ragnvindr's death, and Diluc quit the order. Half a year before the Traveler arrived, Varka took about eighty percent of the Knights' manpower north on an expedition that eventually reached Nod-Krai.
### ULTRA +
**The first Knights of Boreas.** In the era of the founding, a nameless wandering knight came to Mondstadt and became the first Knight of Boreas, though his wolf companion would not enter the city. When the wolf died, he buried it and left to wander again. Accounts differ on his ties to the order: Wolf-Fang's lore says the Knights tried many times to recruit him and he never agreed. Ravenwood, a blacksmith's son, later took up the title. Rostam and now Varka are the other known holders. Rostam's student Roland, the "White Knight", was a candidate but became the "Bloodstained Knight" during the cataclysm wars and was lost in Khaenri'ah.

**Eroch.** Eroch had been trusted with the seal on Ursa the Drake. Secretly in league with Dottore, he broke it, then ordered a cover-up of how Crepus died. Jean exposed him, beat him in a second formal duel (as a knight recalled during a limited-time event), became Master, and purged his allies while fending off the Fatui and the Abyss Order. Eroch took the credit for defeating the drake, which Varka refused to accept, and in the manga the Fatui gained influence in Mondstadt after Dottore slew Ursa. That same year the Outriders' founder (Amber's grandfather) resigned and vanished.

**The expedition.** The expedition was planned about three and a half years ago. Varka had glimpsed a calamity spreading across Nod-Krai and Snezhnaya in the Hexenzirkel's scryglass, and the Knights were also quietly tracking Andrius' scattered wolf spirits. The elite knights, the whole cavalry except Kaeya, Seamus Pegg and Frederica went with him.

**Under Jean.** Stormterror attacked Mondstadt while Varka was away. Jean, Kaeya and Lisa organised the response, and the Traveler, who helped resolve it with Venti, was made an Honorary Knight. Signora then took Venti's Gnosis. In Mika's story quest (3.5), Mika brought home Varka's letter: the expedition was safe and had met Capitano, and Lisa was to give Razor his parents' mementos. Frederica was later sent home to defend Dornman Port. In 5.6 the Knights staged Albedo's trial to expose a mimic spawned from Durin, and Albedo invoked the Hexenzirkel pact.

**Nod-Krai and homecoming (6.0-6.6).** The expedition set up the Favonius Keep in Nod-Krai. Varka invited the Traveler by a letter Ineffa carried, helped defeat Rerir (6.1), and in True Moon (6.3) called in the Hexenzirkel favour so that Nicole would help him and Arlecchino pull down the Frost Moon. The knights then came home in waves. In 6.4 Lohen brought home ten of Varka's letters, including an apology to Diluc, who still declined to rejoin. Varka then settled the Abyss-twisted remains of Andrius and the Roland-shaped wolf leading the Wild Hunt, woke at Windrise, and was welcomed home. In 6.6 Adorno died, and Jean refused to honour his will naming Lohen and Theodore his successors.

## relations
### LARGE
- **Church of Favonius**: separate from the Knights but managed alongside them. Its Seneschal is Seamus Pegg, father of Jean and Barbara. Cardinal Calvin covered his duties while he was away.
- **Barbatos**: Varka, an old friend of Venti, is among the few who know Venti is the Anemo Archon. Barbatos mediated the Knights' pact with the Hexenzirkel.
- **Boreas (Andrius)**: the Knight of Boreas acts as intermediary between the Wolf of the North and Mondstadt.
- **Diluc**: a former captain who distrusts the Knights but still fights for Mondstadt. He has refused to rejoin.

## reputation
### ULTRA
Most Mondstadters trust the Knights and admire Jean, and children grow up on stories of famous knights. The order also draws grumbles. In the long peace, some citizens said the Knights should hang up their swords and chase lost cats, and with most knights away, those left behind were stretched thin. Abroad, the order is known mainly through Varka's legend: Tartaglia calls him the titan of the Knights of Favonius, and an expedition knight said Capitano was the only opponent they met who could compare with him.

## secrets
### ULTRA
Some things are known to few. Eroch's betrayal and Dottore's part in Crepus's death were long hidden from the public. Even among the Knights, very few know that Andrius came from Nod-Krai or that the expedition was partly a search for his wolf spirits. The terms of the Hexenzirkel pact are undisclosed. Only a handful of people, among them Varka and Dahlia, are confirmed to know that Venti is Barbatos.

## entry: Grand Master, Master and the knightly titles
@keys: Acting Grand Master, Grand Master of the Knights of Favonius, Grand Master Varka, Master Jean, Lionfang Knight, Dandelion Knight, Knight of Boreas
### STANDARD
The Knights have one Grand Master. The Master is the reserve who becomes Acting Grand Master when needed, which is why people call Jean both "Master Jean" and "Acting Grand Master". The Dandelion Knight, also called the Lionfang Knight, carries Vennessa's title. Jean has held it since she was fifteen, and it is linked to the "Lion of the South" among the Four Winds. Earlier holders were Vennessa, Arundolyn (the "Lion of Light") and Valentine. The Knight of Boreas is a human knight recognised by the wolf god Boreas, who acts as intermediary between the god and the people. Known holders are the Wandering Knight, Ravenwood, Rostam and now Varka, whom Boreas judged the rightful bearer of the title in this age. As Varka put it in a letter Mika read aloud, "There are ten captains in the Knights of Favonius but only one Grand Master."

## entry: Outriders

## entry: Varka's expedition

@keys2: Varka, Knights of Favonius, Grand Master, Nod-Krai, Mondstadt

Half a year before the Traveler arrived, Grand Master Varka led about eighty percent of the Knights' manpower north, including the elite knights and the cavalry. ## entry: Eroch's betrayal
@keys: Eroch, Inspector Eroch
@version: 6.4
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Eroch was a knight of Varka's generation who often criticised Varka for falling short of the chivalric code. He rose to Inspector, and Varka trusted him with Mondstadt's affairs, including the seal on Ursa the Drake, whenever the Grand Master went abroad. Envious of Varka's fame, Eroch let Dottore's Segments draw him into treason. Four years ago he woke Ursa. Crepus Ragnvindr died stopping the drake with a Delusion, and Eroch ordered Diluc to keep the truth quiet for the Knights' reputation, so Diluc resigned. Jean reported it. Varka had his inner circle investigate and gave Jean authority to act. Jean fought him in two formal duels, losing the first and winning the second decisively (as a knight recalled during a limited-time event), and Eroch was expelled. When Varka finally cornered him, Eroch killed himself, laughing. Varka's character story calls this his most devastating defeat, and in 6.4 he wrote to Diluc to apologise.

## entry: Knights of Favonius Handbook
@keys: Knights of Favonius Handbook, three virtues of knighthood
### LARGE
The Knights of Favonius Handbook is the order's code of conduct. Jean personally edited its fifth edition. It names three virtues of knighthood, modesty, honesty and self-control, and calls them pillars of Mondstadt's freedom, since "freedom cannot exist without the support of duty". It requires knights to help citizens promptly and to greet foreign travellers courteously and without suspicion. Knights must stay ready even during breaks, must not be drunk on duty, and must never help minors drink. It even notes that Noelle has been told to report knights who try to hand their work to her.

## entry: Becoming a Knight of Favonius
@keys: knighthood exam, knightly exam, selection test, squire
@keys2: Favonius, Knights, Noelle, knight
### LARGE
Becoming a knight requires passing both written and practical examinations. Training can begin in childhood. Guy started at eight, learned the sword at ten and became a squire at sixteen before being knighted. Noelle has failed the selection test seven times and keeps serving as the Knights' maid. The young Varka knocked down every examiner and still got a rejection letter, because Grand Master Valentine wanted him to travel first and find his own reason for being a knight. Varka threw the letter away without reading to the end, where Valentine's postscript asked to meet him. That night he ambushed Valentine to prove a point, and over drinks afterwards he was sent abroad.

## entry: Knights of the cataclysm
@keys: Arundolyn, Rostam, Lion of Light, Wolf Pup, Favonius Bladework, Bloodstained Knight
### LARGE
Five hundred years ago, Grand Master Arundolyn (the Lion of Light, who had no Vision and fought with a greatsword in one hand and a longsword in the other) and his childhood friend Rostam (the Wolf Pup, Master and Knight of Boreas) led the Knights against the monsters of the cataclysm. Rostam developed Favonius Bladework, which knights still practise. He was rumoured to secretly run Kreuzlied's old anti-aristocracy society. He died after 23 years of service, and Arundolyn never fought again. Rostam's student Roland, the White Knight, became the feared Bloodstained Knight and vanished into Khaenri'ah. Varka is the only knight said to have mastered Arundolyn's dual-blade style, and even surpassed it.

## entry: Fifth Company succession
@keys: Adorno, Benevolent Knight, Theodore
@version: 6.6
@spoiler: major
### ULTRA
Adorno, the Benevolent Knight, served as Captain of the Ranged (5th) Company in a semi-retired capacity. Years earlier he had rescued Lohen and Theodore from Eroch's and Dottore's experiments. In A Knight's Legacy (6.6), it emerged that Theodore, a trainee medic of the 8th Company, had been keeping the ageing Adorno alive with a serum made from Ursa's flesh, and that Lohen had joined the experiments. Varka, Diluc and the Traveler tracked down the hidden laboratory, where Ursa's remaining flesh was destroyed, and Adorno died of his injuries and was buried at the Favonius Cathedral. Adorno had wanted to pass his title to Theodore and his post to Lohen. As punishment for Theodore and Lohen, Jean refused to honour that will, leaving the captaincy vacant. Lohen accepted that judgement.
