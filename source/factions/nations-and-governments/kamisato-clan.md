## summary
### LITE
The Kamisato Clan is one of the three great families of Inazuma under the Raiden Shogun. It leads the Yashiro Commission, the part of the Tri-Commission that oversees shrines, festivals and ceremony. The clan head is Kamisato Ayato, who handles government and politics. His younger sister Kamisato Ayaka, called the Shirasagi Himegimi, is the clan's public face, and Thoma is its chief retainer. The clan also commands the Shuumatsuban, a secret corps of ninja. It lives at the Kamisato Estate in Inazuma City.
### STANDARD +
Among the people the Kamisato name stands for grace, courtesy and good sense. Behind that image is a family that survived a near-collapse: after elders failed in a matter of national importance and the previous heads died, Ayato rebuilt the clan's standing by cunning, and Ayaka grew into the role of its dignified public figure. The clan holds that it owes its survival to the Raiden Shogun's mercy and must follow her path to eternity. Ayato nevertheless kept the Kamisato out of the Fatui's schemes.

## structure
### STANDARD
The head of the clan is also the Yashiro Commissioner. The siblings divide the work. Ayato deals with the Shogunate's courts and officials and holds "absolute control" of the Shuumatsuban. Ayaka watches over the clan's internal and external affairs and carries out most contact with commoners. Thoma, a Mondstadt-born man, serves as chief retainer and housekeeper and is a well-known fixer in the city. The household includes a housekeeper, servants and a doorman. The clan also runs the Komore Teahouse, which only people it approves may enter.
### LARGE +
The Kamisato Estate contains the Yashiro Commission's headquarters in its main building, so clan, household and commission share one address. Commission staff from the Shogun's Army are called Yashiro Commission representatives. They are relaxed compared with soldiers under the other two commissions, because their duties are mostly civilian. The Shuumatsuban answers to the clan head directly, and Ayaka and Thoma have some authority over it without a stated rank.

## leadership
### LITE
Kamisato Ayato leads the clan and the commission. He became clan head young, with the family weakened and rivals circling, and succeeded by tactics rather than force. He is courteous, shrewd and fond of tea, chess and calligraphy, and few people understand what he truly values.
### STANDARD +
Ayato believes people act from interest: find the leverage and most disputes can be settled. He lets others expect him to help and often attends meetings with a teacup and little more than a smile. He still keeps the clan's sense of duty; his character stories say he accepted that the Kamisato stand because of the Narukami's mercy and must never betray her. His private circle of trust is very small, with Thoma at the centre, according to his own voice-over.
### LARGE +
Ayaka is the more visible head. She is known to the people as the Shirasagi Himegimi (the "Princess" or "White Egret Princess"), admired for her beauty and manners. Her kindness reaches far beyond ceremony: she took Thoma in against the wishes of some, and during the Vision Hunt she and Thoma ran the clan's quiet help to the Traveler. Ayaka was once a playful child. When the clan met disaster she took up swordsmanship and poetry to ease her brother's burden. Her Cryo Vision came after long practice.

## members
### LITE
Kamisato Ayato (clan head and Yashiro Commissioner), Kamisato Ayaka (the clan's public face), and Thoma (chief retainer). Their late parents were the former clan head, who is unnamed, and Kamisato Kayo.
### LARGE +
Staff and affiliates include Furuta (housekeeper), Koharu (house servant, also a ninja in training), Nobumori (doorman), and at the Komore Teahouse its owner Taroumaru, Kozue and Nakanishi. Sayu is the best-known Shuumatsuban ninja. Other named ninja include Inu Shoushou, Momoyo and Hisashi. Hirano, Hirotatsu and Madarame Hyakubei act as Yashiro Commission representatives at the estate.
Ayato's Vision is Hydro, Ayaka's Cryo, and Thoma's Pyro. Ayato received his Vision on the night his gravely ill father spoke to him of being the clan's successor. Ayaka's came after a lone strike in the dojo. According to Ayato's voice-over, Thoma was advised by him to leave during the clan's crisis, chose to stay, and has spared Ayato much worry; Ayato counts him among the few he trusts completely.
### ULTRA +
The former Kamisato heads form a short, bitter history. A previous head, Ayaka's great-grandfather by the wiki's reading, was hurt chasing the swordsmiths who sabotaged a Goshintou forging and died soon after, leaving the clan leaderless. Ayato's father worked to repair the damage and died of the strain. Their mother Kayo, who kept an alter-ego named Tsubaki that dreamed of an ordinary life, died shortly afterwards.

## goals
### STANDARD
Protect the clan and restore its prestige, and in doing so keep Inazuma stable. Ayato is committed to the Shogun's rule in public but works to keep the Kamisato from being entangled in the schemes of others. Ayaka's aim is to carry her brother's burden gracefully while keeping time for the ordinary joys of life. As Yashiro Commission head, the clan also wants festivals, shrines and rites carried out properly, so deities and people are both satisfied.
### ULTRA +
Ayato's fifth character story says that in his youth he believed the clan belonged to its members and service to the gods was a job. He grew to accept that only peace in Inazuma could guarantee the clan's longevity, while keeping his true feelings beneath well-kept appearances.

## methods
### LARGE
Quiet pressure, information and social skill. The Shuumatsuban gather intelligence, shadow suspects, and carry out discreet missions; Ayato says members are usually orphans taken in and raised by the corps, which is how loyalty is secured. Ayaka works through etiquette, festivals, hospitality and personal charm, and Thoma through his wide network of acquaintances. The Komore Teahouse acts as a meeting place and cover for discussions the clan wants private.
In Ayato's story quest (2.6) he shows how the clan operates: he suspects that the rumoured marriage between Kujou Kamaji and Hiiragi Chisato is a plot, sends the Shuumatsuban to watch the Kanjou Commission's representatives, and then turns the public mood against the schemers by making the bride an asset rather than a burden. He explains that he has done such things "plenty of times". Ayaka's story quest (2.0) shows her approach is more direct: she intervenes personally when a trade association clerk is robbed.

## history
### LARGE
**Before the story.** The clan's founding is not given in canon. It served Ei's Shogunate alongside the Raiden Gokaden schools. A generation of Kamisato elders failed the Shogunate in the affair of the Goshintou forging, which hurt the Gokaden. According to Ayato's stories, Guuji Yae Miko spoke for the clan and kept the Shogun from punishing it, and the clan has since stayed loyal. Later the clan head and his wife died and Ayato, a young man, was left to restore its position; several groups sought to take advantage, and he defeated them.

**The Vision Hunt (Chapter II, 2.0).** When the Fatui helped drive the Sakoku and Vision Hunt Decrees, the Yashiro Commission was the only one of the three that did not collaborate. Ayato refused the Fatui's offer, and, as Ayaka says in the Archon Quest A Flower Blooms in a Prison, every Yashiro proposal to repeal the decree was vetoed by the other two commissions. The Komore Teahouse is where the Traveler learns of the next operations from Ayaka and Thoma. When the Tenryou Commission took Thoma to be the 100th victim of the Vision Hunt ceremony, he escaped with the Traveler, told them to seek the Watatsumi Army, and himself took refuge at the teahouse, safe in Yashiro Commission territory. Later Ayaka, Thoma and Sayu helped obtain Kujou Takayuki's falsified reports and the Fatui letters, which turned Kujou Sara against Takayuki.
### ULTRA +
**After the decree (2.1 and 2.6).** With the decree abolished, the Yashiro Commission gained some goodwill, since it alone had stayed out of the Fatui's dealings. In Ayato's story quest (Cypressus Custos Chapter, 2.6) he visits both Kujou and Hiiragi estates and helps Chisato assert her authority over the Kanjou Commission's plotters. Ayaka's story quest (Grus Nivis Chapter, 2.0) explores her mother's alter-ego Tsubaki; the wiki says Ayaka later attends a festival to fulfil both her wish and her mother's. As of 7.1, no Nod-Krai (6.x) or Snezhnaya (7.0-7.1) story involves the clan.

## relations
### LARGE
- **Raiden Shogun**: the clan's sworn lord. Ayato describes the clan as standing by her mercy.
- **Kujou Clan and the Tenryou Commission**: fellow commission families, but the Tenryou Commission enforced the decrees the Yashiro Commission opposed. Ayato's voice-over says Kujou Takayuki turned Sara into a formidable weapon he can no longer wield. In Ayato's story quest he visits Kujou Kamaji to give his blessings on a marriage.
- **Hiiragi Clan and the Kanjou Commission**: during the Vision Hunt the Kanjou Commission sided with the decrees. In Ayato's story quest he helps Chisato, the acting head, assert herself over the plotters in her own commission.
- **Grand Narukami Shrine**: Ayato says Guuji Yae has been kind to the clan but never quite took to him personally, and calls him "the Yashiro rascal"; he says their working relationship is nonetheless harmonious. His stories say her intervention once spared the clan from the Shogun's punishment.
- **Kaedehara Clan**: formerly a subordinate branch of the Kamisato. Ayato still feels a duty to help Kazuha.
- **Fatui**: the clan refused them, which left the Yashiro Commission mostly unaffected.
- **Traveler**: Ayaka's friend, and a guest and ally of the household. Ayato's voice-over says that Ayaka has mentioned them on many occasions.

## reputation
### LARGE
Inazumans consider the Yashiro Commission the least controversial of the three. Ayaka is loved by ordinary people, and her popularity exceeds her brother's. Ayato's stories say officials say "should we ask the Yashiro Commissioner?" when a matter proves hard. Political opponents once called him "that greenhorn", and ordinary people "the poor young master of the Kamisato Clan".

## secrets
### LARGE
The Shuumatsuban's existence is little known, and the identities of its members are classified. Ayato tells the Traveler it reports directly to the clan head, and that after his father died command passed to him.

## status
@version: 7.1
### LITE
As of 7.1 the clan still leads the Yashiro Commission and commands the Shuumatsuban from the Kamisato Estate. Ayato is clan head and Commissioner, Ayaka manages its public life, and Thoma serves as chief retainer. No later story has changed this.

## entry: Yashiro Commission
@keys: Yashiro Commission, Yashiro Commissioner, Sha-bugyou
### STANDARD
The Yashiro Commission ("Bugyou of Shrines") is the third arm of the Tri-Commission, the other two being the Tenryou (security and army) and Kanjou (finance and borders). It manages shrines, festivals and cultural events. Although its influence is smaller than the other two, each commission has equal authority to check the others. The Kamisato Clan leads it and its headquarters sits inside the Kamisato Estate. During the Vision Hunt and Sakoku Decrees it was the only commission that avoided entanglement with the Fatui, and its soldiers from the Shogun's Army are comparatively relaxed. When it proposed repealing the Vision Hunt Decree, the other two commissions blocked the proposal.

## entry: Shuumatsuban
@keys: Shuumatsuban, Shuumatsu-ban
### STANDARD
The Shuumatsuban are a secret organisation of ninja under the Yashiro Commission and reporting directly to the head of the Kamisato Clan, now Ayato. They handle intelligence, espionage, discreet work and, as the wiki puts it, assassination. Their duties keep Inazuma safe. Most are orphans raised from childhood by the corps, and their identities are secret to the point that even members killed on secret missions are not publicly honoured. Sayu is its best-known ninja. Their base is described as a ninja village.
### LARGE +
Members receive a monthly salary, according to a quest line. A Shuumatsuban slogan quoted in a quest says to send the Shuumatsu to end "traitors and criminals alike". Ayato also uses them for small private errands. Several named agents appear in quests, including Inu Shoushou, Momoyo and Hisashi.

## entry: Komore Teahouse
@keys: Komore Teahouse, Taroumaru, Kozue
### STANDARD
Komore Teahouse is a teahouse in Inazuma City run by the Kamisato Clan. Only visitors the clan approves may enter. Its owner is Taroumaru, a Shiba Inu that used to be a Shuumatsuban ninja dog, and customers pay by leaving coins in a box. The Traveler used it as a base in Chapter II, and Thoma took refuge here after the Vision Hunt ceremony. Staff include Kozue, Nakanishi and Nobumori. A story says the staff signed a contract with Taroumaru to work there forever.

## entry: Kamisato Kayo and Tsubaki
@keys: Kamisato Kayo, Tsubaki
### STANDARD
Kamisato Kayo was the mother of Ayato and Ayaka and died when Ayaka was young. As "Tsubaki", a name she used only privately, she imagined an ordinary life: pretty kimonos, foods from other places, and sights she had never seen. In Ayaka's story quest (2.0), Ayaka finds a note naming Tsubaki, learns of the alter-ego, and attends a festival in her place to fulfil both wishes. Ayato's stories recall her talk of cypress offerings and the line that the camellia in the courtyard stays fragrant in the bitterest winter.

## entry: The Goshintou affair
@keys: Goshintou, Isshin Art, Raiden Gokaden
@version: 2.6
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
An in-world account from a limited-time event (True Tales of the Violet Garden, Act IV: The Palace, Dyed in Black, 2.6). In Kazuha's great-grandfather's generation, the Kaedehara's Isshin Art was ordered to forge an important Goshintou from an ancient diagram. The forging was sabotaged, swordsmiths fled, and the Kamisato clan head was injured pursuing them and died soon afterwards. Treacherous people used the leaderless clan's weakness to threaten its standing in the Yashiro Commission, and it never fully recovered until Ayato. Ayaka tells this account to Kazuha and keeps the old diagram that the commission preserved. Ayato's own story says the failings of the Kamisato elders hurt the Raiden Gokaden, and that Yae Miko spoke up to spare them.

## entry: Kamisato Ayato and Ayaka's rise
@keys: Kamisato siblings, Yashiro rascal, Shirasagi Himegimi
### STANDARD
After their father died of illness and their mother soon followed, Ayato became clan head and Yashiro Commissioner and Ayaka matured quickly. She is called the Shirasagi Himegimi; nobody knows where the nickname began. The siblings agreed a division of labour: Ayato does politics out of sight, Ayaka maintains the clan's public image and social standing. Ayato once advised Thoma to leave rather than be dragged into the clan's feuds, and Thoma stayed.
