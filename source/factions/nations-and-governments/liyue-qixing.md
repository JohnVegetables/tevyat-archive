## summary
### LITE
The Liyue Qixing ("Seven Stars of Liyue") are a committee of seven business leaders who govern Liyue. Each is a magnate in their own field, and each holds a title named after a star of the Big Dipper. They meet at Yuehai Pavilion on Yujing Terrace in Liyue Harbor. For millennia they carried out the policies of Rex Lapis, the Geo Archon, and handled day-to-day government. Since his apparent death, which he staged, they have ruled Liyue without divine guidance. The best-known members are Ningguang, the Tianquan, and Keqing, the Yuheng.
### STANDARD +
Liyue's people call them "the hands and feet of Rex Lapis". They oversee all of Liyue's major trades, and very little business, inside or outside the nation, escapes their notice.

## structure
### LITE
The seven members are equal in rank, and each seat has its own area of responsibility. Ganyu, the General Secretary, has supported every generation of the Qixing.
### STANDARD
The seven members are equal in rank, but they divide the work clearly. Known seats:
- **Tianquan** (Ningguang): keeps Morax's book of ancient laws and its amendments, and handles foreign relations.
- **Yuheng** (Keqing): land, livelihood, construction works and real estate.
- **Tianshu** (Uncle Tian): plans policy and advises from behind the scenes, and traditionally rarely appears in public.
- **Kaiyang**: an unnamed current member.
- **Tianxuan**: mentioned in Ganyu's stories, but the current holder is unnamed.

The remaining seats have never been named in canon. Responsibilities overlap: water quality falls under the Yuheng, yet a Tianquan amended the sanitation act on sabotaging water sources, and every member helps make law. The Millelith (Liyue's security force), the Ministry of Civil Affairs and the Eight Trades (the offices running Liyue's major industries) all answer to the Qixing.
### LARGE +
Ganyu, the General Secretary of Yuehai Pavilion, is not attached to any one member. She produces the calculations and data behind their decisions and drafts every ordinance they announce. She has been with the Qixing since the first generation, through every change in its membership. Other secretaries such as Huixin work at the pavilion as her junior colleagues. Some members keep personal staff: Ningguang has three trusted secretaries, Baiwen, Baishi and Baixiao, and Keqing's assistants rarely last beyond three months.

## leadership
### LITE
Ningguang, the Tianquan, is the Qixing's most public face. Keqing, the Yuheng, is known for hard work and for doubting reliance on the gods.
### STANDARD +
Most members keep a low profile and play it safe. Ningguang is the exception: she lives in the floating Jade Chamber, appears all over Liyue Harbor and is the subject of endless gossip. Keqing works in the field, from the south wharf to the Chasm, and once made a famous speech at the Rite of Descension asking how long Liyue could depend on Rex Lapis. Uncle Tian, the Tianshu, passes as an old, forgetful fisherman at the docks, and only a handful of people outside key posts know who he is. The Kaiyang has not been shown on screen. The Kaiyang proposed a multinational wargame to the Tianquan and, together with her, approved a long-term partnership with the Traveler.
### ULTRA +
Their positions are coveted, and some members have many enemies. Ganyu says Uncle Tian kept his seat secure for years by setting his rivals against each other and watching them drag each other down. Ganyu looked after him when he first joined the Qixing, decades ago, and he still calls her "Lady Ganyu" out of old habit. A Tianshu chooses their own successor, and the other members approve the choice.

## members
### LARGE
- **Current**: Ningguang (Tianquan), Keqing (Yuheng), Uncle Tian (Tianshu), the unnamed Kaiyang, and the unnamed Tianxuan.
- **Staff**: Ganyu (General Secretary); Huixin, Uncle Tian's daughter (secretary); Baiwen, Baishi and Baixiao (the Tianquan's secretaries).
- **Agents**: Yelan presents herself as an employee of the Ministry of Civil Affairs. She is actually a special intelligence officer who reports directly to Ningguang, though she sees the arrangement as a pact rather than service. After the Liyue Archon Quest she took over Yanshang Teahouse as a front. Every employee of Wangshu Inn, including its boss Verr Goldet and innkeeper Huai'an, is a Qixing agent.
- **Past members**: the weaponsmith Yun Hui, seventh master of the Yun family, joined the Qixing after handing his forge to his daughter. A former Tianxuan ("the then-Tianxuan" in Ganyu's stories) once nearly filed a missing-person report after Ganyu fell asleep in a roadside haystack following a lunch outing and was carted off to Dihua Marsh.

## goals
### LARGE
The Qixing aim to keep Liyue prosperous and orderly. Their tools are law, contracts and commerce. They punish lawbreakers relentlessly, both to uphold Morax's rulings and to keep trade alive. Since the god's departure they have also had to rewrite old divine strictures into laws that suit a human-run Liyue.

## methods
### STANDARD
The Qixing govern through law, money and information. Morax's ancient laws, with each Tianquan's amendments, are published with a 279-page commentary, and every citizen gets a copy. Merchants treat any loophole as fair game until the Qixing patch it, which is why Ningguang is privately called the "Tailor of Liyue".
### LARGE +
Their intelligence reaches far. Ningguang learns the city's secrets from street children (in return for treats) and checks them against other sources. Her confidants pin the summaries to the intel wall of the Jade Chamber. Yelan handles foreign agents. Wangshu Inn gathers news of travellers entering Liyue. The Millelith make arrests, and Keqing once worked for two months as a labourer to refine a plan for workers' treatment, using the time to gather evidence to report a powerful man known for his abuses. The Qixing also preside over state ceremonies. Each year one member asked Rex Lapis for his prediction at the Rite of Descension, and they organise cultural events such as the Moonchase Festival's Masterful Chefs contest.
### ULTRA +
The Qixing work closely with the Crux, Beidou's armed merchant fleet, though it is independent. Ningguang fines Beidou heavily for her smuggling and has given up trying to change her. All the same, she personally chose Beidou to do her "dirty work", and Beidou is said to be the only person in Liyue Harbor who dares to bet against her, often winning Mora from her. Policy also reaches into the markets: after each Rite of Descension, the new direction from Rex Lapis reshuffled Liyue's economy every few years, and merchants invested more on faith than on analysis.

## history
### LITE
The Qixing formed after the Archon War, when Liyue's seven most successful merchants joined together. In the Liyue Archon Quest (1.0), after Rex Lapis' "death", they took full control of Liyue.
### STANDARD
**Founding.** When the Archon War ended, Liyue turned to commerce and craft, and those who prospered most formed the Qixing. Ganyu, who had fought for Morax, stayed on as their first secretary.

**End of divine rule (1.0).** Rex Lapis was apparently killed during the Rite of Descension, and the Qixing sent the Millelith after the culprit. Childe remarked that the Qixing had always been suspicious of the Fatui. The Qixing then received word in secret that the god was not really dead. They declared that he had died failing a divine trial, dropped the hunt for the killer and moved to fill the power vacuum, which brought them into open confrontation with the adepti. When the Fatui released the ancient god Osial, the Qixing, the adepti, the Millelith and the Traveler fought him together, and Ningguang dropped her Jade Chamber on him. The Qixing then held the Rite of Parting and took over all of Rex Lapis' duties together with the Eight Trades.
### LARGE +
Zhongli later said that their seizure of power was the one thing he had not anticipated, and the best parting gift he could have received. Existing Mora remains valid, but the Golden House had to stop minting for a long time, because making Mora requires the Geo Archon's power, and Ningguang used the Fatui's sabotage at the Jade Chamber to pressure Snezhnaya's diplomats. Keqing spent months stuck rewriting land-infrastructure law. Over that time she gave up her old resentment of Rex Lapis and began to see his smile at her Rite speech as a sign of approval.

**Rebuilding (2.4).** Months later, Ningguang announced a contest to gather materials for a new Jade Chamber. She had secretly sped up the rebuilding to lure out Beisht, Osial's consort. This time the chamber survived, thanks to Shenhe.
### ULTRA +
**Older history.** The Qixing built Wangshu Inn to give Xiao a place to rest; it already stood around the time of the cataclysm, when Yelan's ancestor Rongzhao found his madness eased whenever he passed it. When the cataclysm struck 500 years ago, the Qixing sent Yelan's ancestors Boyang and Rongzhao with the Fantastic Compass to help the Millelith in the battle at the Chasm.

**The Chasm.** A little over two years before the story, the Qixing closed the Chasm after mysterious accidents, quakes and a harmful find. They sealed its mines with the Seven-Star Array, anchored on Bedrock Keys. Signora negotiated trade agreements that let a Fatui team investigate inside. After the Jade Chamber incident, the Qixing cancelled the deal, took over the exploration themselves and forbade the Millelith from contact with the Fatui.

## relations
### LARGE
- **Rex Lapis**: the Qixing served as his executors for millennia. Since his "death" they have ruled alone.
- **Adepti**: the adepti seem to have worked more closely with the Qixing in the past. Madame Ping says the Qixing stopped letting her and her friends into their counsel long ago. The two sides clashed after the god's "death", then fought Osial together. Ganyu bridges the two worlds.
- **Fatui**: mutual suspicion. After the Jade Chamber incident the Qixing nullified their Chasm agreement with Snezhnaya and pressed the Fatui's diplomats hard.
- **The Crux**: an independent ally. Beidou is the only person in Liyue Harbor who dares to bet against Ningguang.
- **Eight Trades and the Ministry of Civil Affairs**: both work under the Qixing. The known Eight Trades offices include Yinyuan Hall (salt), Shenglu Hall (glazed ceramics), Huishan Hall and the Communications Office.
- **Neighbouring nations**: the Tianquan handles foreign relations. During a limited-time event in 4.7, the Yuheng invited the armed services of neighbouring nations to a joint wargame in Liyue.

## reputation
### LARGE
Most citizens regard the Qixing as the cream of Liyue and the pillars of its prosperity. Their staff consider it an honour to serve them. Others think of the Qixing mostly as mortals living in the god's shadow: before the god's "death", the merchant Linlang remarked that the Qixing were mere mortals while Rex Lapis was a god. Locals warn foreigners that "Ningguang knows everything". Rumours about her contradict each other but all agree she is extraordinary.

## secrets
### LARGE
The Tianshu's identity is kept from the public. Wangshu Inn's role as a Qixing front and support base for Xiao is not public knowledge. Yelan's true role is hidden behind her Ministry cover. The Qixing know that Rex Lapis did not really die, but publicly claim he fell in a divine trial.

## status
@version: 6.3
### LITE

## entry: Qixing titles and seats
@keys: Tianshu, Tianxuan, Tianquan, Yuheng, Kaiyang, Seven Stars
@keys2: Qixing, Liyue
### STANDARD
Each seat of the Qixing is named after a star of the Big Dipper, in Chinese reading. Five titles appear in official material. The Tianshu (Dubhe) is Uncle Tian, who plans policy behind the scenes. The Tianxuan (Merak) is mentioned in Ganyu's stories but no current holder is named. The Tianquan (Megrez) is Ningguang, who keeps the laws and runs foreign relations. The Yuheng (Alioth) is Keqing, who oversees land, livelihood and construction. The Kaiyang (Mizar) is an unnamed current member who is addressed as "Lord Kaiyang". The two remaining stars' names are fan extrapolations that have never appeared in canon. Members are addressed as "Lady Tianquan", "Lady Yuheng" and so on, and are of equal rank.

## entry: Yuehai Pavilion
@keys: Yuehai Pavilion, General Secretary
### STANDARD
Yuehai Pavilion on Yujing Terrace is where the Qixing meet, discuss official business and rule on matters affecting all of Liyue. Their day-to-day operations and staff are based there, though some days all seven members are away on private business. Ganyu works there as General Secretary, alongside junior secretaries such as Huixin. Those with civil matters are sent on to the Ministry of Civil Affairs. Bu'yun at the pavilion controls access to the Jade Chamber. In the Liyue Archon Quest the password to go up was "Excuse me, do you sell the moon here?"

## entry: The Qixing and Wangshu Inn
@keys: Wangshu Inn, Verr Goldet, Huai'an
@keys2: Qixing, agent, agents, front, Xiao
### STANDARD
Wangshu Inn in Dihua Marsh was built by the Qixing and is secretly run by them; it already stood about 500 years ago, when Yelan's ancestor Rongzhao passed by it. It gives the yaksha Xiao a place to rest and covert support in his fight against evil spirits. It also collects intelligence on notable people entering Liyue. All its staff are Qixing agents, including the boss Verr Goldet and the innkeeper Huai'an.

## entry: The Qixing take power
@keys: end of divine rule, Rex Lapis' death, death of Rex Lapis, failed a divine trial
@version: 1.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
After Rex Lapis was apparently killed at the Rite of Descension, the Qixing at first hunted the culprit. Then a secret message told them the god was not dead, and they abruptly ended the investigation. They announced that he had died failing a divine trial and moved to take sole control of Liyue. This set them against the adepti until the Fatui unleashed Osial, when Qixing, adepti, Millelith and the Traveler joined forces. Ningguang sacrificed the Jade Chamber to defeat him. At the Rite of Parting she told the people that the contract with Rex Lapis had ended and that Liyue must build a new age of prosperity. Keqing told the Traveler that the Qixing always repay their debts. Zhongli, watching in mortal guise, called their seizure of power the best parting gift he could have wished for.

## entry: Tianshu succession
@keys: Zhiyi, Qianwei, Mingbo, Seven-Star Selection, Calculated Gambit
@version: 2.7
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
In Yelan's story quest (2.7), Uncle Tian felt his energy failing and decided to name a successor as Tianshu. Because the Tianshu works behind the scenes and must be shielded from outside influence, the outgoing Tianshu nominates a candidate in private, and the other Qixing approve the choice. Tian asked Yelan to assess three of his own students. Qianwei was a wealthy entrepreneur, Mingbo worked at the Ministry of Civil Affairs, and Zhiyi studied and travelled. Whoever was appointed would be bound to carry out the manifesto they had submitted. Zhiyi seemed ideal, but his background had been suspiciously cleaned up. Yelan and the Traveler traced him to a meeting with a Fatui contact and found he had been slowly poisoning Tian with fish soup. Baizhu made an antidote in time. Shaken, Tian chose to hand over his duties gradually while mentoring new candidates. Yelan had half-jokingly offered herself for the seat, and Tian said Ningguang would not let her go easily.

## entry: The Chasm and the Seven-Star Array
@keys: Seven-Star Array, Bedrock Key, Bedrock Keys
@version: 2.6
### LARGE
A little over two years before the Traveler arrived, the Qixing closed the Chasm despite its mineral wealth, after mysterious accidents, strange quakes and a harmful "wish-granting" find by the miners. They sealed the underground mines with the Seven-Star Array, whose anchor points were Bedrock Keys around the seal, while a few professional miners stayed on. Shortly after the closure, Signora negotiated trade agreements that let a Fatui advance team investigate inside. After the Jade Chamber incident, a Qixing order cancelled the agreement with Snezhnaya, handed the exploration back to the Qixing and banned the Millelith from contact with the Fatui. The Traveler later broke the weakened seal to enter the mines in "The Chasm Delvers".

## entry: Rebuilding the Jade Chamber
@keys: Jade Chamber Rising, Sunset Vermillionite, rebuilding the Jade Chamber
@version: 2.4
### LARGE
Several months after Osial's defeat, Ningguang publicly offered to answer one question each, honestly, from the first three people to bring her materials for a new Jade Chamber: Sunset Vermillionite, Wonder Cores and Adepti Sigils. The Traveler, Shenhe, Yun Jin and Beidou took part. Ningguang revealed that she had sped up the rebuilding to lure out Beisht, Osial's consort, and was ready to sacrifice the chamber again. This time it survived the battle, thanks to Shenhe. Huixin later sighed with relief that the reconstruction was finally finished, and Uncle Tian was glad to see the chamber "back up where it belongs".

## entry: Wargame of Five Armies
@keys: Wargame of Five Armies, Mutual Security Enhancing Simulation
@version: 4.7
@availability: limited
### ULTRA
During the limited-time event "Mutual Security Enhancing Simulation" (4.7, from June 2024), Liyue hosted a joint wargame. The Kaiyang proposed it to the Tianquan as a limited trial of whether miniature simulations could produce useful intelligence. The Yuheng sent invitations to the armed services of neighbouring nations, and the Millelith, Knights of Favonius, Yashiro Commission, Corps of Thirty and Gardes took part. The Millelith staff officer Su Ding invited the Traveler to serve as senior consultant.

## entry: Ley Line disturbance at Sal Terrae
@keys: Stygian Onslaught, Tumult Subduer, A Defensive Strategy
@version: 5.7
### ULTRA
In 5.7 a Ley Line disturbance drew monsters to Sal Terrae, and the Millelith sealed the area off. Keqing, leading the garrison there, asked Ningguang for more defence funding and the Kaiyang for more manpower so the troops could be rotated. She told the Traveler that keeping the peace in Liyue is the duty of the Millelith and the Qixing. After the Traveler cleared the disturbance's domain, Yujing Terrace sent word that the Tianquan and the Kaiyang had agreed to a long-term partnership with the Traveler.

## entry: The moon at the 6.3 Lantern Rite
@keys: Moon-Devourer, Zhanzhu, lunar eclipse at Lantern Rite
@keys2: Qixing, Ningguang, Keqing, Lantern Rite
@version: 6.3
@spoiler: minor
### ULTRA
During the Lantern Rite of 6.3, in the story "A Lanternlit Ode to the Silver Moon", the Qixing investigated reports that part of the moon had vanished. There had also been an incident at Wanmin Restaurant. Keqing traced the trouble to Lu Xiangxiang, who had joined a civilian dig funded by Huishan Hall near the Chasm and came back badly shaken. The artifacts from that dig were registered with the Ministry of Civil Affairs and reviewed by Keqing herself before Linlang exhibited them. Their inscriptions referred to the ancient nation of Lang-Gan and to moon worship. Ningguang noted that the people of Chenyu Vale had migrated from the Chasm area, and Ganyu consulted the adepti. Before the festival night, the Ministry of Civil Affairs issued a notice that the moon might disappear but that the people would be in no danger.
