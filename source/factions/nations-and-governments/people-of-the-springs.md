## summary
### LITE
The People of the Springs (Meztli) are one of Natlan's six tribes. They live by the hot springs and coast of Toyac Springs, in a settlement also called "People of the Springs". Their Saurian partners are the swimming Koholasaurs. They are famous as guides, mapmakers, surfers and hosts, and their land is the tribe most popular with visitors. Their current chief is Amina. Mualani, a guide and surf-shop owner, bears the tribe's hero Ancient Name, Umoja ("unity").
### STANDARD +
The tribe is easygoing and fond of music, fishing, watersports and long soaks. Its chief jokes that seriousness is a rare virtue among her people. It has still produced some of Natlan's most respected warriors, and it suffered heavily in the Abyss's attacks on Natlan.

## structure
### STANDARD
Like the other tribes, the Meztli are led by a chief, with the tribe's elders behind them. When Amina's grandfather died, the elders voted her in because of her many new ideas. The tribe shares in Natlan's tribal institutions: its members take part in the Pilgrimage of the Return of the Sacred Flame, and its Wayob, the ancestral spirit in the Night Kingdom, grants Ancient Names to worthy members. Hero names are tied to their tribe, and the Meztli's is Umoja. In the wiki's comparison of the tribes, they are paired with the Hydro element and the Flow-Inverted Wayob Manifestation.
### LARGE +
Daily life rests on trades rather than ranks. There are guides (Nuu is a famous one and Koolau is called the top guide), hot-spring administrators, lifeguards, guards, musicians, shopkeepers and inn staff. Guide-craft is taught by apprenticeship. Nuu told the young Mualani to spend about three years learning wilderness survival, the land, the tides and the timing of eruptions before she could start, and she paid him a large tuition fee.

## leadership
### LITE
Chief Amina leads the tribe. She is young, full of ideas and determined to take her job seriously. She took over after her grandfather died, and the late warrior Atea was her mentor.
### STANDARD +
Amina's grandfather taught her that "positivity should be our guiding star", and she quotes him. As chief she has designed a new stage, strengthened patrols, run the defence of the tribe during the Abyss invasion and dealt with scammers who used the tribe's legends. She believes the Natlanese dead are reunited in the Night Kingdom. She says that idea is also how Ancient Names carry heroes' memories forward.
### ULTRA +
Other chiefs appear in the tribe's history. The unnamed "first chief" founded the tribe. During the limited-time event Tournament of Glory in Bloom (5.5), a chief named Paio is shown leading the Meztli in the last Bloomflower Trials, hundreds of years ago. Paio angrily demanded Sacred Flame Crystals from the Flower-Feather Clan after seven Meztli warriors were badly injured, and he realised too late that he had been played.

## members
### STANDARD
- **Mualani**: playable Hydro guide who runs the Leisurely Puffer watersports shop. She bears Umoja and fought in three Night Warden Wars without ever needing resurrection.
- **Amina**: chief.
- **Atea**: an elder warrior with a Pyro Vision, titled Candlekeeper. She died of long-term Abyssal corrosion during the Chapter V story.
- **Kaiulani**: an Ancient Name bearer and veteran warrior.
- **Nuu**: a famous guide who taught Mualani.
### LARGE +
In the settlement, the Traveler can meet Kupo Nui (owner of the Golden Dusk), Mahina Nui (Golden Dusk reception), Mataoho and Pele Nui (hot-spring administrators), Yelafath (owner of Atlahua's Net), Pakaa Nui (owner of Xochicuicatl Records), Anchieta (owner of Djembe Studios), Milu Nui (freelance musician), Wakea Nui (guide), Umi (lifeguard), and Ipora and Tairona (guards). Hinaeia Nui, a sailor who calls herself the "Gourmet Hunter", trades the tribe's goods in Nod-Krai. Kaulu is a tribesman Amina caught swindling tourists.
### ULTRA +
Historical members include the first chief; Uenuku, the Treader of the Rainbow and first bearer of Umoja; his descendant Tupac the Giant; the legendary angler Matavaru; and Atawallpa, a hero from artifact lore who was the elder twin of Waskar. The ancient Pyro Archon Cochanina was remembered as the tribe's surfing champion. Akamai and Kau Kau were two old adventurers in weapon lore from a limited-time event.

## goals
### STANDARD
The tribe wants to live well and share that with others. It keeps its springs clean and welcoming, guides travellers safely through Natlan, defends its home against the Abyss, and keeps its heroes' memories alive through Ancient Names. Its founding legend sets its central value: companions matter more than the prize.

## methods
### STANDARD
**Guiding.** The Meztli have a long tradition as guides and cartographers. Their ancestors "measured the world with their feet" and turned dangerous paths into maps. Guides are expected to bring clients to their destination safely. As Nuu put it, guides are not treasure hunters.

**Hot springs.** The tribe has both natural and artificial springs. The natural springs are hotter and are for locals and vetted visitors. After some travellers were scalded, an unwritten rule now says outlanders must first acclimatise in the cooler artificial springs. Atea spent years digging new artificial springs so that more visitors could enjoy them.

**Livelihood.** Tourism is the tribe's main income, and its hot springs are famous across Teyvat. The tribe's leisure is fishing, music, surfing and relaxing alongside the Koholasaurs.
### LARGE +
The Meztli make record discs for portable players, which also work on Fontainian record players, and they build string and percussion instruments. A trader in Nod-Krai said the instruments sell well there even though most of the tribe's other goods suit the cold poorly. Their rooftops often have wavy canopies meant to evoke "sailing through waves". In the hot-spring culture the Traveler sees, visitors swim, surf, dive and play water polo, and hosts recommend lucky colours and the best spring for each guest.

## history
### LARGE
**Founding.** According to the tribe's legend, the first chief was the best guide in Natlan but began as a treasure hunter who was never satisfied. He set out for a legendary mysterious island. His companions helped and trusted him, and at the end he gave up the glowing prize, an ore that also warned of an eruption, to bring them all home safely. Realising that they were the treasure, he settled down with them and founded the People of the Springs. Elders say the tribe began on a small island that later joined the coast.

**Heroes.** Uenuku, the first bearer of Umoja, fought the dragon order alongside an Oceanid. Five hundred years ago his descendant Tupac joined Tenoch's band against the Abyss, survived the final battle long enough to carry Mavuika to the Sacred Flame, and then died. Legend credits Tupac with thrusting rocks into the earth so that the hot springs of the southwest bubbled up.

**Present day (Chapter V, 5.0-5.3).** After the Pilgrimage ended Mualani's bid for the Night Warden Wars, she brought the Traveler home. An Abyss attack on the tribe was driven back without deaths. Atea destroyed an Abyssal pylon at the abandoned spring, and the Traveler briefly purified her, but she died soon after. During the invasion (5.1), the Abyss fouled the tribe's springs and weakened its defences. The tribespeople, with Mualani and Amina, worked to purify the waters. Many people died. ## entry: The first chief and the mysterious island
@keys: mysterious island, first chief of the People of the Springs, Where the Springs Return
@version: 5.0
### STANDARD
For as long as the Meztli have existed, they have told of a mysterious island in a rarely visited Domain that promises wealth and glory. Few have ever reached it, and even top guides cannot guarantee passage. In Mualani's Tribal Chronicle (5.0), she guided the Traveler and two Sumeru scholars there. Statue text left by the first chief tells his story: a restless treasure hunter whose companions trusted him to the end. He gave up the glowing prize, which was also the warning sign of an eruption, to bring everyone home alive. Then he founded the tribe. Nuu explained that the chief left the legend and map on purpose, to lure young guides into the challenge and teach them what he had learned.
### LARGE +
The legend draws tourists, and some tribesfolk exploited it. Kaulu and others charged visitors for fake trips to the island. Amina could not prosecute them because the scammers claimed the money covered travel costs, so she gave the Traveler marked coins as bait and caught Kaulu with them.

## entry: Umoja and the heroes of the Springs
@keys: Umoja, Treader of the Rainbow, Rainbow King, Uenuku, Tupac the Giant
### STANDARD
Umoja ("unity") is the Ancient Name of the People of the Springs' hero line. Its known bearers are Uenuku, Tupac and Mualani. Uenuku, the Treader of the Rainbow, travelled with Xbalanque, served in the Grand Alliance and later left. Tupac, Uenuku's descendant, was a giant of a man whom no Saurian could carry. In songs he breaks mountains, makes hot springs, fights a two-tailed whale, and spars with the young Tenoch over a drinking horn. Five hundred years ago he was one of the six warriors who joined Tenoch against the Abyss. Gravely wounded, he lived long enough to carry Mavuika to the Sacred Flame. In Into Eternal Night (5.0), Tupac's projection appeared to Mualani in the Night Kingdom. He passed his memories to her and told her she would unite the tribes.

## entry: Hineila and the rainbow legend
@keys: Hineila, Ianeira, Footprint of the Rainbow
### LARGE
Many versions exist of Uenuku's meeting with an Oceanid, Hineila (or Ianeira), who came from Fontaine. In one, she blesses his spear in exchange for "the spring where vicious beasts prowl", because she meant to purge Phlogiston from Natlan's waters. Each of them knows the other is a means to an end, yet they fall in love, and she dies shielding him from the dragonlord Xiuhcoatl's flames, leaving a rainbow behind. The Meztli's own scrolls tell a happier tale. Uenuku drove her away in a moment of suspicion and searched for her for decades, until a rainbow guided him to "the sea's other side" and they were reunited. Canon does not settle which version is true.

## entry: Atea, the Candlekeeper
@keys: Atea, Auntie Atea, Candlekeeper
@version: 5.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Atea was an Ancient Name bearer with a Pyro Vision, a famous Night Warden Wars veteran, and a neighbour and friend of Mualani. She loved the hot springs and threw parties for her "hot spring buddies", including Kaiulani, Liliuo, Vichama and Mavuika. Years earlier an Abyssal monster had impaled her. She survived, but the corruption stayed in her organs, so she could no longer fight on the front line or bathe in hot water. She turned instead to digging cooler artificial springs for visitors. In Home of the Hot Springs (5.0), she destroyed an Abyssal pylon at an abandoned spring, and the Traveler purified the fresh contamination. She told Mualani she had only days left, gave the Traveler her talisman to deliver to Mavuika, and died soon afterwards with Mavuika at her side. Amina, whom she had mentored, called her an elder she deeply respected. Her spirit later helped the Traveler in the Night Kingdom (5.3).

## entry: Hot-spring etiquette of the Meztli
@keys: artificial hot springs, natural hot springs, hot spring rule
### STANDARD
The People of the Springs keep two kinds of springs. The natural springs are hotter, and locals and vetted visitors use them. The artificial springs are deliberately cooler. After some travellers were scalded, an unwritten rule took hold that outlanders must first get used to the artificial springs, a "trial" in Atea's words, before using the natural ones. In scenes, locals greet guests by asking whether they have had a bath today, and an ailing person may be forbidden hot water altogether.

## entry: Cochanina and the Volcano Lord
@keys: Cochanina, Volcano Lord, Iridescent Chalice
### LARGE
Told as a lively children's tale in the lore of Surf's Up: the Volcano Lord, a huge black salamander-like monster that had squatted in the Great Volcano of Tollan, dumped his filth into the Meztli's springs. ## entry: Matavaru the angler
@keys: Matavaru
### ULTRA
Matavaru was a legendary fisherman of the People of the Springs. In Echoes of Life (5.0), Mavuika told the Traveler she met him in a tavern. He told her about his meticulous plan to catch a certain giant fish. The fish turned out to be corroded by the Abyss; he killed it but was so badly hurt that he could never go deep-sea fishing again, and he asked her whether a fisherman can claim victory without bringing back his catch. Mavuika answered that the experience was worth more than the prize. In the end he gave her his whole set of fishing tools so they would not go to waste.

## entry: Settlement of the People of the Springs
@keys: Golden Dusk, Atlahua's Net, Xochicuicatl Records, Djembe Studios
### LARGE
The settlement called "People of the Springs" lies in Toyac Springs, and its viewpoint calls it the "home of hot springs and flowing waters". It has the Golden Dusk inn (Kupo Nui), Atlahua's Net shop (Yelafath), Xochicuicatl Records (Pakaa Nui), Djembe Studios (Anchieta), Mualani's Leisurely Puffer, a market street, guards, lifeguards and spring administrators. Bulletin boards serve tribe notices and visitors' messages. Nearby Ameyalco Waters is the setting for part of the mysterious-island story. Out of universe, many Meztli names come from Polynesian languages, which is a helpful guide when naming new tribesfolk in roleplay.
