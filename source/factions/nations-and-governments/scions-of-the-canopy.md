## summary
### LITE
The Scions of the Canopy, also called the Huitztlan, are one of the six tribes of Natlan. They live in a village of huge trees and platforms hung by ropes off the cliffs of Coatepec Mountain, alongside their saurian partners, the Dendro Yumkasaurs. They are known as couriers, hunters and explorers, and they love extreme sports. Their chief is Wayna. The tribe's best-known member today is the Saurian Hunter Kinich, and the Pyro Archon Mavuika was born among them about 500 years ago.
### STANDARD +
The tribe's hero lineage is the Ancient Name "Malipo", tied to the legendary Turnfire: the ancestor Yupanqui, the cataclysm hero Burkina, and now Kinich have carried it.

## structure
### STANDARD
The tribe is led by a chief, currently Wayna, together with a council of elders. Its village, also named "Scions of the Canopy", is a sub-area of Coatepec Mountain. According to the official site, the ancestors hung giant trees on the cliff with ropes and grappling hooks, which gave the tribe its name. Within the tribe operates the Courier's Longhouse, a courier agency led by Michica, which delivers goods that are not too valuable, fragile or dangerous. The tribe also runs a Learning Lodge where elders teach the children for free.
### LARGE +
Like every Natlan tribe, the Scions have an ancestral Wayob spirit that selects worthy members for Ancient Names. Their bonded saurian is the Yumkasaurus, whose grappling tongue suits life on the cliffs, and their tribal warriors are known in-game as the "Swiftstep" fighters. The tribal emblem marks them as specialists in communications and in tracking saurians.

## leadership
### STANDARD
Wayna is the chief. He insists he is not as old as the nickname "Uncle Wayna" suggests: in his view, leading the tribe simply means he has the most adventuring experience of anyone in it. He is easygoing and fond of risk, but he still warns people not to be reckless. He borrowed a Spiritspeaker Stone from Citlali of the Masters of the Night-Wind, and he gave it to the Traveler's group when they set out to rescue Kachina.
### LARGE +
Elders share major decisions. Elder Trinidad cared deeply about the Turnfire Night ritual. When Kinich showed no interest in it, he sought outside help, which brought in the Traveler. Elder Leik taught at the Learning Lodge and raised the young Kinich for years after his father died.

## members
### STANDARD
- **Kinich**: Saurian Hunter, former courier, current bearer of "Malipo" and one of Natlan's six modern heroes. He is bound by contract to the self-proclaimed dragonlord Ajaw.
- **Mavuika**: the Pyro Archon, born to an ordinary Scions of the Canopy family before the cataclysm.
- **Wayna**: the chief.
- **Michica**: manager of the Courier's Longhouse.
- **Vichama**: a famed scout and Ancient Name bearer who helped the Traveler's group find Kachina's Ancient Name. He died defending the village in the Abyss invasion.
### LARGE +
Other named villagers include Aclla (the village "doctor"), Hakau ("Wayfinder"), Roca (a rock-climbing instructor), Sinchi (a hunter), Tundama (a courier), the guards Illapa and Yawar, and the children Toba and Huni, who call Kinich "Malipo" Kinich. Poca and Tisoc guard the Mother Flamegranate Tree. Historical members include Yupanqui, Burkina, Mallko (Vichama's friend, who died five years before the Archon Quest when his whole Night Warden War team fell), and Mavuika's mother Haumia and younger sister Hine.
### ULTRA +
The Record Penned by All memorial lists tribespeople killed in the 5.1 Abyss assault, including Wayta, Vichama and Hunza. The wiki also counts Ekwensu, Pumpu, Purum and Vilca among the dead. Qispi died in an exploration side objective. The tribesmen Achiyaku and Raman live or die depending on the player's choices in that Archon Quest.

## goals
### STANDARD
The Scions want to honour their ancestors, keep their bonds with the Yumkasaurs, and serve Natlan as its messengers and pathfinders. For generations they have also cared for Kongamato, the ancient Yumkasaur called the Mountain King, and kept his Abyssal corruption in check.

## methods
### STANDARD
The tribe earns its living from delivery and hunting commissions. Its people climb, rappel and grapple across terrain other Natlanese avoid, and they track and hunt dangerous saurians. Visitors come for extreme sports: rock climbing, rappelling, bungee jumping and "volcano parkour". Natlan's local Adventurers' Guild is said to be made up mostly of Huitztlan.
### LARGE +
A courier's work, as one tribal furnishing describes it, needs more than an adventurous spirit: travel over varied ground, long survival in the wild, and precise navigation. In wartime these skills become intelligence work. During the 5.1 Abyss invasion Wayna sent many tribesmen out as messengers to carry real-time news across Natlan, and Kinich's tribe delivered battle reports between fronts. Kinich's own habit of setting a price for every job, which troubled some elders, is a personal trait, not tribal law.

## history
### LARGE
**Yupanqui and the Turnfire.** About 2,000 years ago the tyrant Och-Kan used a terrible fire, the Turnfire, to oppress Natlan. Anyone it touched felt burning pain behind them and was burned to ash if they looked back. The tribe's ancestor Yupanqui, an old companion of Och-Kan who turned against him, stole the Turnfire and hurled it at the tyrant's army. Then, thinking he heard Och-Kan's voice, he looked back. He burned for a hundred days until he pierced the ley lines and fell into the Night Kingdom, where the Turnfire is said to burn still. The tribe remembers him as the "Firethief", and his Ancient Name Malipo is bound to the Turnfire.

**The cataclysm.** Five hundred years ago, Burkina and his Yumkasaur Kongamato fought the Abyss alongside the hero Tenoch and the Pyro Archon Mavuika. Kongamato was corrupted by the Abyss and killed Burkina, who passed his blood and power to the saurian as he died. Kongamato survived for centuries and became the Mountain King, a living symbol of the tribe's glory. After the war, Mavuika's younger sister Hine became an architect and helped rebuild the tribe, though none of her buildings survive.

**The present (5.0-5.1).** Wayna gave Citlali's Spiritspeaker Stone to the group rescuing Kachina, and Vichama helped them. In Kinich's story quest the tribe held what became its last Turnfire Night (see the entry below). In the 5.1 Abyss invasion the village fell and was evacuated. Many Scions died, among them Vichama. They returned after Mavuika ended the invasion.
### ULTRA +
Yupanqui was one of the first companions of the first Pyro Archon Xbalanque. He later served as an ordnance officer for the tribes' Grand Alliance and protected saurians when doing so was a crime. In the uprising against Och-Kan he broke the chains on his tribe's saurian with his sword. Legend also says the tribe's warriors, with a hundred Yumkasaurs, spent a hundred days and a hundred ropes moving the "Obsidian Totem Pole", the symbol of their faith, to where it now stands on Coatepec Mountain. Five years before the Archon Quest, Mallko's whole Night Warden War team died; Vichama had been excused from it because of an injury. After the invasion, Kinich expected deliveries across the nation to be delayed but gradually restored as volunteers came forward, and he later won the first reworked, friendly Pilgrimage.

## relations
### LARGE
- **Pyro Archon**: Mavuika is a daughter of the tribe. Her father was one of three warriors killed in a three-day battle in her youth. When she visited the site of her family's old home 500 years later, a descendant of a Huitztlan family Hine had helped gave her Hine's architecture manual.
- **Masters of the Night-Wind**: Wayna borrowed a Spiritspeaker Stone from Citlali, and the Traveler's group destroyed it during Kachina's rescue. Wayna dreaded her scolding but agreed that a life mattered more.
- **Other tribes**: the six tribes share the Pilgrimage, the Ancient Name system and the war against the Abyss. Kinich is close to Mualani of the People of the Springs and Kachina of the Children of Echoes.
- **Abyss**: the tribe's old enemy. It corrupted Kongamato and overran the village in 5.1. The Abyss Lector Enjou has been studying the Turnfire, and he gave Kinich the Beastly Rift used in the last Turnfire Night.
### ULTRA +
- **Saurian Relics Association**: tried repeatedly to buy Ajaw from Kinich, and he refused every time.
- **Couriers who break trust**: in Lies and Promises a dishonest Huitztlan courier fled the tribe for fear of punishment and abandoned his Yumkasaur at Teticpac Peak. Wayna, out of pity, arranged for people to feed it every day.

## reputation
### LARGE
Other Natlanese see the Scions as fearless climbers and reliable messengers, and the village is a destination for thrill-seekers. Wayna treats even war like an extreme sport: whichever way a jump ends, he says, every outcome has value and deserves to be remembered. Kinich's cold, price-first manner is famous, and Mavuika defends it. In her view, "Malipo" also means "cost" or "price" and teaches people to stay calm and ask the questions others do not want to hear.

## secrets
### LARGE
The tribe's stories say Burkina fell fighting the Abyss. Kinich, the current Malipo, revealed in his story quest that the Mountain King killed Burkina in a fit of Abyss-driven madness. Burkina chose not to fight back and gave his blood and power to the saurian. Kongamato carried that shame for five centuries and even asked to be put out of his misery.

## status
@version: 5.1
### LITE
As of 7.1 the tribe is back in its cliff village under Chief Wayna, rebuilding after the 5.1 Abyss invasion killed many of its people.
### STANDARD +
Kongamato's corruption is now sealed by a protective shell, so the yearly Turnfire Night is no longer needed, and the Mountain King has offered to train future heroes. No new tribe story developments were found for 6.0-7.1; the 6.x Archon Quests take place in Nod-Krai and 7.0-7.1 in Snezhnaya.

## entry: Turnfire and the Malipo name
@keys: Turnfire, Malipo, Firethief, Turnfire Hunt
### STANDARD
The Turnfire was Och-Kan's weapon of oppression. It burned anyone who looked back at it. Yupanqui stole it, used it on the tyrant's army, then looked back and burned for a hundred days before falling into the Night Kingdom. The Ancient Name "Malipo" comes from it. Its bearers are Yupanqui, Burkina and, today, Kinich, whose profile title is "Turnfire Hunt". Malipo is one of the six heroic Ancient Names, and a bearer who truly connects with it can receive memories of earlier holders. Mavuika notes that Malipo also means "cost" or "price".

## entry: Kongamato, the Mountain King
@keys: Kongamato, Mountain King, Gluttonous Yumkasaur Mountain King
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Kongamato is a gigantic Yumkasaurus, Burkina's partner from the cataclysm, who joined the great heroes against the Abyss. Abyssal corruption both stretched his lifespan and threatened his sanity. The tribe kept him asleep in a cave and fed him Mountain King Flamegranates grown from the Mother Flamegranate Tree. Some tribesfolk think he is huge because of a special build, but the Archive says he has just lived a long time and eaten far too much. After the last Turnfire Night a shell formed around him that stops him from absorbing or leaking Abyssal energy. He now trains the tribe's warriors.
### LARGE +
Before the Traveler descended into the Night Kingdom, Kongamato appeared with Kinich as a projection. He said Burkina had come to him in a dream and urged him to support the hero of the present age.

## entry: Turnfire Night
@keys: Turnfire Night, Turnfire Ritual, Turnfire Night ceremony
@version: 5.0
### STANDARD
Turnfire Night was the tribe's ceremony, usually held every year, to commemorate their ancestors and drain the Mountain King's Abyssal corruption. A flame bearer, traditionally the current Malipo, led a delegation that lit braziers with kindling from the Sacred Flame and carried the fire into Kongamato's cave. In Kinich's story quest the corruption was building up faster than usual. Kinich planned to put Kongamato into a Beastly Rift provided by Enjou, and Wayna reluctantly agreed. Elder Trinidad, believing they meant to kill the Mountain King, smashed the device. Kongamato woke and badly burned him. Kinich and the Traveler subdued the saurian, and Kinich refused his plea to die, telling him to live with his shame. The shell that formed afterwards made this the last Turnfire Night.

## entry: Courier's Longhouse
@keys: Courier's Longhouse, Couriers' Longhouse
### STANDARD
The Courier's Longhouse is the courier agency based in the Scions' village and managed by Michica. It handles ordinary deliveries, not extremely valuable, fragile or dangerous goods. Michica regularly loses patience with couriers who lose packages. Kinich and his late father once worked for it.

## entry: Mother Flamegranate Tree
@keys: Mother Flamegranate Tree, Mountain King Flamegranate
### LARGE
The tribe guards the Mother Flamegranate Tree, whose root cave is taboo ground. Its Mountain King Flamegranates are picked and offered to Kongamato, and some say Yumkasaurs secretly bring him Flamegranates too. In the world quest Ripe For Trouble the guards Poca and Tisoc look into why the fruit will not ripen.

## entry: Learning Lodge

### LARGE
The Learning Lodge is where the tribe's elders teach children, free of charge. Elder Leik let the young, impoverished Kinich attend in exchange for running errands. When Leik asked the class to name heroes, the children answered with "Malipo" Burkina and Master Yupanqui. Leik later took Kinich in and trained him for years. Kinich's methods sometimes stirred controversy among the elders, and Leik defended him.
