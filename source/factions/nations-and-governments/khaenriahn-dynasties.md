## summary
### LITE
Khaenri'ah, the subterranean kingdom that fell in the cataclysm 500 years ago, had two known ruling dynasties. The older Crimson Moon Dynasty is known almost only through a legend. The later Eclipse Dynasty was the last, and its final king, the Vinster King Irmin, pursued Abyssal power until the kingdom collapsed. The later house's Chinese name means "Black Sun Dynasty", and the legend that describes the change speaks of a "dark sun" replacing the crimson moon.
### STANDARD +
The two are linked by blood and by grudge. Descendants of the Crimson Moon line still exist; Arlecchino of the Fatui is one. In the Eclipse era the crown hunted such descendants as potential rivals.

## structure
### STANDARD
Canon gives no organisational chart for either house. The Eclipse Dynasty ruled through a king, a council of the kingdom's most respected figures and a royal court, with royal guards (the Black Serpent Knights) and a Universitas Magistrorum for research. Vedrfolnir and Hroptatyr were the most trusted members of Irmin's council. A Knight Marshal named Anfortas served under Irmin and took the regency afterwards. The Perinheri legend describes the Crimson Moon era as a realm under a "great ruler" (addressed as "high lord of the nobles"), with sages as advisers and an orphanage whose graduates later became knights; the book's footnotes call alchemy and the Beastmaster Knights the era's mainstays.
### LARGE +
The legend names figures from the later part of its story, which takes place after the dark sun had replaced the crimson moon: Njord, who walks with black wolf warbeasts ("Darksprites"); Alf, the greatest warrior of the Universitas Magistrorum; an Alberich, commander of half the knights; and Perinheri himself. The footnotes treat the Darksprites as an early alchemical warbeast, which fits the era's stress on alchemy and Beastmaster Knights. The legend also calls the kingdom's aristocracy obstinately pure-blooded.

## leadership
### STANDARD
No Crimson Moon ruler has a name in canon. The Eclipse Dynasty's last ruler was Irmin, the one-eyed Vinster King, who became the kingdom's undoing. See Irmin's own entry for his character; this entry covers the dynasty as an institution. After his fall, Knight Marshal Anfortas declared himself Regent while "unknown threats" faced the kingdom.
### LARGE +
A family-history page Kaeya kept says the Alberich Clan, who lacked royal blood, stepped in as regents when Irmin's strength failed. The wiki infers from this that both Anfortas and Chlothar Alberich (the Abyss Order's founder) belonged to the Alberich Clan; no quest states the link for Anfortas.

## members
### STANDARD
Legend: the orphanage founded in the Crimson Moon era produced the knight Perinheri, and the foreign "princess" Angelica later joined it; Perinheri's rival Hleobrant came from the descendants of those who forsook their god. The legend's main events take place after the change of dynasty. Tholindis, a Universitas researcher in the Eclipse era, came from a minor branch distantly related to the main Crimson Moon line. Arlecchino carries the blood too, though she learned of it only after Pierro told her.
### LARGE +
Eclipse side: Irmin, Anfortas, the Black Serpent Knights (the "Twilight Sword" Dainsleif and Halfdan among them), Irmin's council (Vedrfolnir and Hroptatyr were its most trusted members), and the secret agent Rerir, whose duty included hunting Crimson Moon descendants.

## goals
### STANDARD
The Eclipse Dynasty's stated aim in All Is Foretold, through Rerir's work, was to protect itself by removing any possible rival. Irmin's own aims turned toward Abyssal power and the machinery of "secrets from beyond the skies". The Crimson Moon remnants in the same quest, such as Tholindis' ally Orlog, wanted the old dynasty restored. The Perinheri legend adds that a sage of the Crimson Moon era hoped for children from beyond the world, among them beings who could "transcend the gods", and the ruler told him to do as he pleased.

## methods
### LARGE
The legend's Crimson Moon government relied on alchemy and Beastmaster Knights. Its sages founded an orphanage to raise children from outside the world and from the kingdom's own orphans, and many of its graduates became knights. The Eclipse Dynasty put its weight on mechanical engineering and on knowledge from beyond the sky, so alchemy and the beast-masters lost ground. Some Khaenri'ahns distrusted this: a letter to Marshal Anfortas argued that only human will could pry open "the corners of this hollow world", and asked that some of the old machinery be kept.
### ULTRA +
In the quest All Is Foretold, Rerir's files record the purge in clerical language: age, job, and a note that the person was a confirmed Crimson Moon descendant who posed a threat to the dynasty, followed by "Neutralized". The files include children. The campaign widened from the direct line to side branches, which Rerir protested. Meanwhile the Universitas moved its staff toward Abyssal energy research, leaving Tholindis the only researcher left in its Moon Marrow lab.

## history
### STANDARD
**Crimson Moon era.** Only the legend remains. In it a crimson moon shone over the underground realm, outside things leaked in from beyond the world, and a sage advised the ruler to prepare for a child who could surpass the gods. Later the crimson moon set and a dark sun took its place. That dark sun was the Eclipse Dynasty, and the book's notes place the change of rule probably around the Archon War.

**Eclipse era.** The Eclipse Dynasty ended with the cataclysm 500 years ago. Under Irmin the kingdom pursued Abyssal power. The Traveler's Sibling arrived and was named heir, Vedrfolnir was blinded for warning against Irmin's path, and Dainsleif's raid on the palace on his brother's behalf let the Five Sinners take the Abyssal power Irmin had drawn into the Sibling. The kingdom then fell, and the Heavenly Principles' response left the survivors cursed.
### LARGE +
The Eclipse era is told in several sources. A ballad in the Mondstadt book Breeze Amidst the Forest sings of "an atrous sun" that befell its kingdom, and its Chinese text reads as the Eclipse Dynasty falling. A bulletin in the ruins at Gavireh Lajavard says Anfortas took the regency over the "indisposed" king and the council Regnum Concilium Ultimum. A traveller's journal in the same ruins records most people fleeing to the surface with the Schwanenritter while others hid below, and rumours of those who went up turning into monsters.
### ULTRA +
The purge of the Crimson Moon descendants shaped Rerir's life and that of his fiancee Tholindis, since her family had been a minor branch of the line. She kept this hidden even from Rerir. The quest shows Rerir turning against his orders by letting at least one marked person escape. Pierro, once a Khaenri'ahn royal mage, recalls in his artifact text failing to win "the previous ruler's" favour or to stop "them" tearing away the veil of sin.

## relations
### STANDARD
- **Court of Khaenri'ah**: the Eclipse Dynasty's royal court under Irmin, covered in its own entry.
- **Arlecchino**: a Crimson Moon descendant whose Balemoon Bloodfire draws on Abyssal energy and slowly corrupts her, so she uses it with great caution.
- **House of the Hearth**: Pierro hints that it began as the old orphanage of the legend, and that its later link to the Fatui could be his doing.
### LARGE +
- **Five Sinners of Khaenri'ah**: Eclipse-era figures who split the Abyssal power taken from the Sibling.
- **Universitas Magistrorum**: the research body that Irmin ordered to study the Abyss. It had already existed by the early Eclipse Dynasty, since Perinheri mentions it.
- **Abyss Order**: later founded by Chlothar Alberich, whose clan the legend and the family-history page both tie to the dynasty's upper ranks.

## reputation
### ULTRA
Surviving sources judge the Eclipse Dynasty harshly. Dainsleif says the title "Savior" gave the heir was only an expression of the Vinster King's lust for power. Orlog calls Irmin a blind fool whose eyes were clouded by powers from beyond this world. The Perinheri anthology is more romantic. It portrays a land of knights and rivals, and says the aristocracy persists "obstinately pure-blooded" because of an old betrayal of a god.

## secrets
@spoiler: major
### ULTRA
The best-kept fact is the purge itself: the Eclipse Dynasty exterminated Crimson Moon descendants down to children. Nothing in canon suggests it is general knowledge in Teyvat today. The cause is only partly explained. All Is Foretold links it to Irmin's research into Abyssal power, but it does not say what the Crimson Moon line had that Irmin feared.

## status
@version: 6.1
### LITE
Both dynasties are gone. Their legacy survives in the curse of the Khaenri'ahns, the Abyss Order and in Crimson Moon descendants such as Arlecchino.
### STANDARD +
As of 7.1 canon has not shown a revival of either house. Khaenri'ah itself is a ruin beneath Teyvat. The 6.x Nod-Krai Archon Quests revisit the Eclipse era through the memories of Five Sinners such as Rerir and Vedrfolnir, but show no restored dynasty.

## entry: Crimson Moon Dynasty
@keys: Crimson Moon Dynasty, Crimson Moon descendants, Balemoon Bloodfire
@version: 4.5
### STANDARD
The Crimson Moon Dynasty is Khaenri'ah's older ruling house, known chiefly through the legend Perinheri (also titled Hleobranto Innamorato). Under it, alchemy and Beastmaster Knights had their peak, and its sages founded an orphanage for children from other worlds, from which came many of the kingdom's best knights. The dynasty ended when a dark sun replaced the crimson moon. Its descendants carry the Balemoon Bloodfire, as Arlecchino does, a power that draws on Abyssal energy and corrupts its user. She was warned that if the corruption reached her heart she would become a different person.

## entry: Eclipse Dynasty
@keys: Eclipse Dynasty, Dark Sun Dynasty, Black Sun Dynasty, Blacksun Dynasty
@version: 1.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The Eclipse Dynasty was Khaenri'ah's last ruling house, ending with King Irmin in the cataclysm 500 years ago. It favoured mechanical engineering and knowledge from beyond the sky over alchemy and the beast-masters, and later Abyssal power. Its royal guards, the Black Serpent Knights, included Dainsleif the Twilight Sword and Halfdan, and its research body was the Universitas Magistrorum. The Traveler's Sibling was adopted into the royal house as the heir. The dynasty's end brought the cataclysm and left the Khaenri'ahns cursed.

## entry: Perinheri legend
@keys: Perinheri, Hleobranto Innamorato, Hleobrant, Angelica
@version: 4.5
### LARGE
Perinheri is a two-volume legend sold in Fontaine, put together over generations. It tells of an orphan, Perinheri, raised in the orphanage of the Crimson Moon era, and his best friend Hleobrant, who falls for a foreign "princess" named Angelica and starts picking duels to impress her. All three leave the kingdom while Perinheri hunts her, believing that killing her will cure his friend. Hleobrant begins to howl like a beast, and Angelica says it is the price of his ancestors forsaking their god. Perinheri, a drifter, bears no such curse. At sunrise Angelica reveals herself as "freedom, that which has broken free of fate". The book's circumstances may be embellished, but Pierro told Arlecchino its key details are accurate.

## entry: Purge of the Crimson Moon descendants
@keys: Crimson Moon purge, purged targets, Orlog, Frithr
@version: 6.1
@spoiler: major
### ULTRA
In All Is Foretold (6.1), Rerir's files show that the Eclipse Dynasty ordered Crimson Moon descendants killed as threats, children included. Rerir at first insists the work is lawful and fair, then refuses to continue as the targets reach younger and more distant branches. Tholindis' family was one such branch, which she hid from him. Orlog, a hidden Crimson Moon survivor who wanted the dynasty restored, urged her to kill Rerir and was later arrested; Frithr, another survivor, is let go by Rerir on condition that nobody find him. The quest presents these events as a memory that Nefer investigates through a chess-like board.

## entry: Irmin's regency
@keys: Regent, Regnum Concilium Ultimum, Knight Marshal Anfortas
@keys2: Khaenri'ah, Irmin
@version: 3.6
### LARGE
When Irmin could no longer rule, the Knight Marshal Anfortas proclaimed himself Regent. A badly worn bulletin at Gavireh Lajavard says the post was temporary, held while the king was "indisposed" and the kingdom faced "unknown threats", and that Anfortas would lead the Regnum Concilium Ultimum until things returned to normal. The Alberich family history Kaeya kept presents the regency as the Alberich Clan's doing. Whether Anfortas and Chlothar Alberich were kin is the wiki's inference.
