## summary
### LITE
The Kanjou Commission is the part of Inazuma's government that runs finances, customs and the borders. It is one of the three bodies of the Tri-Commission, serves the Raiden Shogun's Shogunate and is led by the Hiiragi Clan from Ritou, the port where foreigners are processed. During the Sakoku Decree it was feared for its strict rules and for abusing outsiders. After the Fatui plot in Inazuma was exposed, its Commissioner was jailed and his daughter Hiiragi Chisato took over as acting head.
### STANDARD +
Its Japanese name, Kanjou-bugyou, echoes the Edo-period finance magistrates, and the English "Commission" is just a translation of the same title that the other two bugyou bodies carry.

## structure
### STANDARD
The Commission is led by the head of the Hiiragi Clan, who holds the office of Kanjou Commissioner. Below that, the wiki lists three kinds of staff. Civilian officials (Hishida, Matsuura and Yagi) handle administration. A military arm is headed by a Chief (Katsuie) with squad leaders such as Ebina Gonshirou and Sasaki, and by officers such as Chiba, Hiromi, Kenzaburou, Kobayashi, Kousaka, Kubo, Nakano, Shinnojou and Yokoyama. Ordinary guards and collectors are called ashigaru, including the tax-collection ashigaru Keijirou. The Commission's seat is the Hiiragi Estate in Ritou, and its Outlander Affairs Agency, where the officer Chiba and the inspector Yurika work, handles documents for visitors from abroad.
### LARGE +
The Commission acts as a veto in the Tri-Commission: In A Flower Blooms in a Prison, Kamisato Ayaka says that whenever the Yashiro Commission proposed repealing the Vision Hunt Decree, it was promptly vetoed by both the Tenryou and Kanjou Commissions.

## leadership
### LITE
The acting head is Hiiragi Chisato, a young woman who runs the Hiiragi Estate in Ritou. Her father, Hiiragi Shinsuke, was Kanjou Commissioner until he was jailed.
### STANDARD +
Shinsuke is described as the head of the Hiiragi Clan and the Kanjou Commissioner. He met Signora before the Traveler reached Ritou and agreed with her to keep the Traveler stuck there. Chisato overheard, and secretly offered to help the Traveler leave Ritou if they would deliver a private letter to Kujou Kamaji, her sweetheart in the rival Kujou Clan. Her father opposed the relationship. After the plot came apart, Chisato was left as figurehead. In a wedding announcement during the Cypressus Custos Chapter she is styled Proxy Clan Head, which the wiki reads as confirming her acting leadership.
### LARGE +
Some of the clan's retainers regarded her as inexperienced and tried to remove her and claim the commissioner's post. They forged a love letter from her to Kamaji so that marriage would absorb her into the Kujou Clan and strip her of influence in the Hiiragi Clan. Kamisato Ayato and the Traveler foiled the plot (see the entry below). The wiki says this made Chisato take a more serious stance in Commission affairs. In the quest she says that, for the first time, she had asserted her authority.
### ULTRA +
Earlier Commissioners include Hiiragi Hiroshi, who led the Commission during and after the cataclysm about 500 years ago. His Ornate Kabuto text, a statue message near Araumi and the Hakushin Ring tell of a man who wanted to make Inazuma's foreign trade central and under government control. Hiroshi trained under the Kujou swordmaster Iwakura Michihiro when he was young.

## members
### LARGE
Named staff on the wiki's list include the civilian officials Hishida, Matsuura and Yagi, the military chief Katsuie, squad leaders Ebina Gonshirou and Sasaki, and officers such as Nakano, Chiba, Kobayashi, Kenzaburou, Shinnojou and Yokoyama (Sasaki, Yokoyama and Shinnojou are posted at the Hiiragi Estate). Kageyama, the inspector at the border checkpoint, and Yurika, inspector at the Outlander Affairs Agency, apply the entry rules in person.
### ULTRA +
Shinnojou is a Kanjou officer who is infatuated with Chisato. In the Cypressus Custos Chapter he burst into the estate to tell her not to accept the marriage because it would harm the clan name, and was then seized and held at a cave near Araumi by his own colleagues before Ayato and the Traveler rescued him. Matsuura is the civilian official who threatens Ayato and then, when the plot collapses, apologises to him.

## goals
### STANDARD
Officially the Commission manages Inazuma's treasury, trade and the control of who enters and leaves, so that Inazuma's wealth and borders stay in order. Since the Fatui plot, it is trying to prove that it can serve the Shogun without the corruption of the previous Commissioner. Its reform under Chisato is a stated change in tone, not a documented new programme.

## methods
### STANDARD
The Commission works through paperwork and checkpoints. During the Sakoku Decree it ran approvals for anyone entering or leaving Inazuma: Atsuko says the process was long and wore people down so that she fled by raft, and Bibo called the border impenetrable. Non-Inazumans could not leave Ritou without a Travel Permit. The Outlander Affairs Agency used the stranded visitors as a source of fees. The Traveler was first quoted two million Mora for entry and Thoma negotiated it down to six hundred, and he says such charges were common. Through taxes it also shaped Ritou's merchant economy.
### LARGE +
**The Crystal Marrow tax decree.** Kurisu, president of the International Trade Association, tells the Traveler that a new decree demanded taxes be paid in Crystal Marrow instead of Mora. The scarcity pushed the price up. Werner explains that the tax-collection ashigaru, led by Keijirou, overtaxed the merchants, stockpiled the extra Marrow and had it sold back at an inflated price, with the proceeds going to the collectors. The Traveler finds Keijirou's ledger, exposes him in front of the Association and Kurisu secures the stockpile as compensation.
### ULTRA +
The Commission was not only passive. Anyone wishing to enter or leave Inazuma legally had to pass the Kanjou Commission's strict assessments, while the Tenryou Commission also enforced the Sakoku Decree. Kanjou notices on the bulletin boards of Ritou are used to publish decisions, such as the detainment of the Commissioner.

## history
### LARGE
**Origins.** The Hiiragi Clan has served the Shogun for at least 500 years. About 500 years ago Hiiragi Hiroshi turned the deserted island of Ritou into a trading hub that impressed the Shogun, and he imported foreign inventions, including a Snezhnayan blast furnace for the hot springs of Tenshukaku and a special kamera built with a mirror given to his clan by the Kitsune Saiguu. She counted him a friend.

**The decrees.** The Fatui conspired with the Tenryou Commissioner, Kujou Takayuki, to manipulate the Shogun into enacting the Vision Hunt Decree and the Sakoku Decree and so create a market for Delusions. Shinsuke was also roped in with promises of power and wealth. He accepted by stockpiling Crystal Marrow for them. During the Sakoku Decree the Kanjou Commission turned Ritou into a bottleneck for outsiders.
### ULTRA +
**The reckoning.** In Omnipresence Over Mortals (2.1) the Traveler unravels the Fatui's plot with Tenryou and Kanjou officials. Shinsuke is imprisoned awaiting the Shogun's judgment. Afterwards the Commission lightens up towards foreigners, and the Outlander Affairs Agency fell to a processing fee of 500 Mora. Chisato's post-quest dialogue shows that she accepts her father must answer for his mistake regardless of who he is.

**The forged marriage (2.6).** In Cypressus Custos Chapter Act I, retainers publish a wedding announcement without Chisato's knowledge. Ayato notices that a low-ranking officer objected while the higher-ups approved. He and the Traveler trace it to Matsuura and other plotters, and Ayato proposes to Kamaji that they turn Chisato from a burden into an asset, which Kamaji agrees to support. Chisato confronts Matsuura and the plotters, who conclude she is a valuable asset after all. Matsuura apologises to the Traveler and to Ayato. The wiki reports that the marriage was later postponed indefinitely and the announcements treated as false.

## relations
### LARGE
- **Tenryou Commission and the Kujou Clan**: political rivals who check each other's power; a Hiiragi-Kujou marriage would be politically significant. They have a personal link through Chisato and Kamaji.
- **Yashiro Commission and the Kamisato Clan**: the Yashiro Commission was the only one of the three to stay out of the Fatui's scheme. Ayato helped Chisato keep her position in 2.6.
- **International Trade Association**: its members in Ritou dealt with the Commission's taxes and customs and suffered under the Crystal Marrow decree.
- **Fatui**: Signora worked through Shinsuke, and the wiki says Snezhnayan merchants were initially exempt from the Sakoku restrictions because of diplomatic ties.
- **Raiden Shogun**: the Commission serves under her and is subject to her final judgment, which is why Shinsuke's fate is pending.
### ULTRA +
- **Thoma**: Thoma (of the Kamisato household) negotiates the entry fee with Yurika and accompanies the Traveler through registration, showing that foreigners could rely on connections rather than the rules.
- **Shuumatsuban**: Ayato sets a Shuumatsuban ninja to watch the Hiiragi retainers during the marriage plot, which shows that the Yashiro Commission keeps an eye on the Kanjou's internal affairs.

## reputation
### ULTRA
During Sakoku the Commission was disliked by people who had to deal with it. The wiki calls its grip on the border strong and authoritarian, and says it treated Ritou's foreign residents abusively. Merchants in the Trade Association considered its taxes unbearable. After the reforms its image is slowly mending, with the Agency's lower fee as one visible sign.

## secrets
### ULTRA
The role of Shinsuke in the Fatui's scheme was hidden from the public until the Traveler uncovered it. The wiki reports that the Fatui plot used Crystal Marrow that the Kanjou Commission had collected. Chisato says that she only learned the full truth after the events of Omnipresence Over Mortals.

## entry: Hiiragi Shinsuke and the Fatui plot
@keys: Hiiragi Shinsuke, Commissioner Shinsuke, Kanjou Commissioner
@keys2: Kanjou, Fatui, Ritou, Signora
@version: 2.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Hiiragi Shinsuke was head of the Hiiragi Clan and Kanjou Commissioner when the Traveler reached Ritou in 2.0. He seems friendly: he receives the Traveler as an honoured guest and invites them in. In truth the Fatui had enlisted him with promises of power and wealth, and he stockpiled Crystal Marrow for them. He met Signora and planned to keep the Traveler indefinitely stuck in Ritou so they would not interfere. His daughter Chisato overheard and helped the Traveler escape. After the plot was unravelled he was imprisoned and awaits the Shogun's punishment.

## entry: Hiiragi Chisato
@keys: Hiiragi Chisato, Miss Chisato
@keys2: Kanjou, Hiiragi, Ritou
@version: 2.0
### STANDARD
Chisato is Shinsuke's daughter and the acting head of the Hiiragi Clan. She lives at the Hiiragi Estate in Ritou. In 2.0 she asked the Traveler to deliver a private letter to Kujou Kamaji in exchange for helping them out of Ritou; the delivery earned the Traveler a Travel Permit from Kamaji. After her father's arrest she became a figurehead and, after the forged marriage plot in 2.6, took a firmer hand. She says that despite her worry, anyone who commits a wrong must answer for it.

## entry: Outlander Affairs Agency
@keys: Outlander Affairs Agency, Travel Permit
@keys2: Ritou, Kanjou, Traveler
@version: 2.0
### STANDARD
The Outlander Affairs Agency in Ritou is the Kanjou Commission's office for documents and requests from non-Inazumans. During the Sakoku Decree it charged vast and arbitrary fees to stranded visitors. Yurika, the inspector, asked two million Mora from the Traveler before Thoma negotiated six hundred, and Thoma says this was normal. After the Commission was purged of corruption, the Agency became more lenient and charged only 500 Mora. Without a Travel Permit, outsiders could not leave Ritou, and Kanjou ashigaru would turn the Traveler back at the town's edge until Kamaji granted a permit.
### LARGE +
Kageyama, the checkpoint inspector, asks visitors to state their name, purpose and goods. At that time only members of the International Trade Association were allowed to import or export goods, and purely personal reasons for visiting were not accepted.

## entry: Hiiragi Hiroshi and the founding of Ritou
@keys: Hiiragi Hiroshi, Lord Hiroshi, founding of Ritou
@keys2: Ritou, Hiiragi, Kanjou
@version: 2.0
### LARGE
Hiiragi Hiroshi was the leader of the Hiiragi Clan and Kanjou Commissioner about 500 years ago, after the cataclysm. He wanted all foreign trade to pass through one port under the Inazuman government and built Ritou on a deserted island. To show what trade could bring he brought in foreign inventions: a Snezhnayan blast furnace that heats the hot springs of Tenshukaku as a gift for the Shogun, and a kamera that records memories and emotions, built with a demon-exorcising mirror given to the clan by the Kitsune Saiguu. The Saiguu counted him a friend and remembered his work in her last moments. When Iwakura Michihiro became the Kujou Clan's sword instructor, Hiroshi trained under him and said he would have recruited him for the Hiiragi if the Kujou had not.

## entry: The Crystal Marrow tax decree
@keys: Crystal Marrow tax, Tax Decree, Keijirou
@keys2: Kanjou, Ritou, tax, merchants
@version: 2.0
### LARGE
In Confessions of an Outlander the International Trade Association's president Kurisu reports that the Kanjou Commission has changed the tax from Mora to Crystal Marrow. The Marrow's price climbed because merchants all searched for it, until taxes threatened to exceed profits. Werner, a merchant, reveals that the tax-collection ashigaru under Keijirou overtax merchants, hoard the extra Marrow and have Werner sell it back at an inflated price. The Traveler follows Keijirou and finds a hidden ledger, then confronts him in front of Kurisu and Harrison. Keijirou claims he only wanted a modest living, but he is made to hand over his stockpile as compensation. The Commission's higher authorities appear only briefly, and the quest does not say whether Shinsuke knew about it.

## entry: The forged marriage announcement
@keys: Chisato and Kamaji wedding, wedding announcement, Cypressus Custos Chapter
@keys2: Hiiragi, Kujou, Ayato
@version: 2.6
### ULTRA
In The Firmiana Leaf Falls (Cypressus Custos Chapter Act I), a rumour spreads that Kujou Kamaji and Hiiragi Chisato are to wed. Kamaji believes Chisato proposed by letter, but Chisato never sent one. Ayato suspects a trap, rescues the Kanjou officer Shinnojou, who objected, and confirms that retainers around Matsuura are behind it. They wanted to remove her and claim the commissioner's post. Ayato proposes turning Chisato from a burden into an asset, and Kamaji agrees to support the plan. The plotters retreat, and the wiki reports that the marriage plans were then postponed indefinitely.
