## summary
### LITE
The Maison Ordalie is the part of Fontaine's government that runs the judicial system. It is led by the Chief Justice of Fontaine, called the Iudex, who is Neuvillette. It works from the Palais Mermonia in the Court of Fontaine, and its most public work is the trials held in the Opera Epiclese.
### STANDARD +
The name is French and the wiki glosses it as "Ordeal House". Fontaine's other main government bodies sit in the same palace: the Maison Gardiennage (law enforcement), the Maison Gestion (document and administrative work) and the Maison Cardinalice (financial approvals and other administration). The Palais Mermonia is, as far as Fontainians are concerned, the highest authority in the land.

## structure
### STANDARD
At the top is the Chief Justice, or Iudex. Besides Neuvillette, other judges working from the Palais Mermonia can be assigned to preside over trials, so he does not hear every case himself. The Maison Ordalie judges. It does not police or investigate by itself. Arrest, transport and punishment of offenders belong to the Maison Gardiennage, which also keeps civil case records, and archiving and paperwork are handled by the Maison Gestion. The detective force known as the Marechaussee Phantom belongs to the Maison Gardiennage but answers directly to the Chief Justice.
### LARGE +
Trials are staged in the Opera Epiclese, an opera house on the island of Erinnyes that doubles as Fontaine's main courthouse. The Fortress of Meropide, reached by a lift from behind the opera house, receives people sentenced there, but it is autonomous and sits outside the Court of Fontaine's jurisdiction. Its administrator is Wriothesley.

## leadership
### LITE
The Iudex is Neuvillette, a figure of strict impartiality. He has been the only Iudex for a very long time, so long that people assume divine favour gave him an unusually long life.
### STANDARD +
The Iudex has duties beyond judging trials. Under historical and traditional legacies, the Iudex may also prosecute and enforce the law in person. This was once meant to keep order in chaotic times and is now mostly symbolic. If the Iudex investigates or prosecutes a case, he must step down as judge and have someone else take the seat, and Neuvillette is said to have followed that rule in the few cases he handled personally.
### ULTRA +
Much of the Iudex's work is private administration needed to keep the justice system running, and Neuvillette keeps a formal distance from the public so that close ties cannot cast doubt on his rulings. He is also the reborn Hydro Dragon, one of the Seven Sovereigns. That is revealed in the Archon Quests and is not publicly known in Fontaine. His own voice-over says he presides over trials from duty, not enthusiasm, and the profile text quotes him saying "Iudex" is only one of the hats he wears. His private feelings about the Heavenly Principles are not part of the Maison's ordinary work.

## members
### STANDARD
Neuvillette is the only person the wiki lists as a member. Other judges exist, but none are named in the sources checked. Charlotte, a reporter for The Steambird, relays in a quest that one trial was assigned to another judge from the start. Champion Duelists are a separate group of fighters who stand in for an accused person's honour, and the strongest of them is Clorinde.
### LARGE +
Neuvillette describes Clorinde as his nation's most powerful Champion Duelist and a successor to the old Marechaussee Hunters, playing a vital part in keeping the courtroom fair and impartial. The Special Security and Surveillance Patrol of the Maison Gardiennage is currently led by Chevreuse.

## goals
### STANDARD
The Maison exists to maintain order through law and trial. A map description of the Court of Fontaine says law serves a stable order, which is the foundation for equity and justice. Neuvillette's own voice-overs are more guarded: he says society's ideas of justice, fairness, equality and the rule of law are not equivalent and never can be.

## methods
### STANDARD
Cases begin with an accusation, usually from the Maison Gardiennage, and go to a public trial in the Opera Epiclese. Before the trial the accused may choose to face a Champion Duelist instead in order to keep their honour. If the accused wins the duel they are acquitted. If they lose, they stand trial, and a duel can end in death if the accused refuses to yield. Public trials were put on like performances, with bulletins announcing the charge and the verdict. Convicted people are sent to the Fortress of Meropide.
### LARGE +
Fontaine's legal code is famously complex and full of odd old articles. Neuvillette's own examples include a rule about pets and Furina's name, one about tomato ketchup being only a condiment, and the requirement that Melusines be addressed as "she".

Until 4.2 a verdict was formally handed down by the Iudex together with the Oratrice Mecanique d'Analyse Cardinale, a large mechanical scale in the Opera Epiclese. The Oratrice could reach a verdict that differed from the Iudex's own view, and its result had to be honoured whatever he believed. After the Oratrice stopped working at the end of Masquerade of the Guilty, the wiki records that Neuvillette decided to hear every case and pass verdicts himself.

## history
### STANDARD
The Court of Fontaine is said to have been the court of Focalors, God of Justice, for at least a thousand years. Neuvillette's Iudex-led judiciary continues that court under his charge, and the sources found do not date the Maison Ordalie more exactly.
### LARGE +
Roughly 400 years ago, shortly after Neuvillette was appointed Iudex, he brought the Melusines into Fontainian society. Many people feared them. The Melusine Carole, one of the first to join the Marechaussee Phantom, was framed by hardliners opposed to him and died proving her innocence. Her partner Vautrin, then captain of the Special Security and Surveillance Patrol, took revenge and was sentenced by Neuvillette to the Fortress of Meropide. The emblem of Fontaine's law enforcement now shows two Melusines beneath the scales of law to honour Carole.
### ULTRA +
Neuvillette also sentenced Sigewinne to the Fortress for breaking an ancient law against changing species. She had become more human so that she could treat a human friend whose parents refused a Melusine healer. He suspected the law was Egeria's, meant to stop others following her example. After her sentence she stayed as the prison's nurse.

**Early Fontaine story.** The public trial of the magician Lyney ended when the Oratrice found him not a suspect in the disappearances case, while the real culprit Marcel was convicted. In the same case the Oratrice also found Tartaglia guilty, which Neuvillette and Furina did not expect, since it was the first time it had disagreed with the Chief Justice's view. Neuvillette investigated the machine himself and Tartaglia was sent to the Fortress.

**Masquerade of the Guilty (4.2).** To avert the prophecy of Fontaine, Neuvillette and the Traveler staged a trial in which Furina was shown to be a fraud. The Oratrice agreed that she was guilty but sentenced "the Hydro Archon" to death, a penalty so rare that some thought it a myth. In the vision that followed, Focalors explained to Neuvillette that the Oratrice had been built to gather Indemnitium so that she could execute herself and destroy the Hydro Archon's divine throne. That returned the Hydro authority to Neuvillette. The Oratrice has been non-functional since.

## relations
### STANDARD
- **Maison Gardiennage**: arrests, investigates, transports and punishes, and supplies the cases that the courts hear. Its Marechaussee Phantom answers directly to the Chief Justice.
- **Maison Gestion and Maison Cardinalice**: administrative and financial sister bodies in the same palace.
- **Fortress of Meropide**: where sentences are served. It is independent of the court's jurisdiction.
- **Champion Duelists**: a separate group offering the accused a duel in place of a trial.
### LARGE +
- **Furina**: the former Hydro Archon, who treated trials as theatre in her public role. In 4.2 she abdicated the post of Hydro Archon and left its affairs to Neuvillette, who now runs the nation and helps take care of her needs.
- **The Steambird**: Fontaine's newspaper reports on trials and on the Iudex. Neuvillette has had run-ins with it, such as its story claiming he called his post a springboard.
- **Melusines**: Neuvillette secured their rights and the rule that they be called "she".
### ULTRA +
- **The Seven and the Heavenly Principles**: as the Hydro Dragon, Neuvillette says he pardoned humanity's sins but means to judge each of the Seven in turn. This is his personal vow and not a function of the Maison.

## reputation
### STANDARD
Fontainians see the Palais Mermonia as the top authority and the Iudex as unassailably impartial. To children, the person in the judge's seat just wears a wig, keeps order and acts as the Oratrice's messenger. The Fortress of Meropide has a notorious reputation among law-abiding people.

## secrets
### ULTRA
The Oratrice was never a pure enactor of justice. It stored belief in justice as Indemnitium so that Focalors could execute herself and destroy the divine throne. That was known to almost no one until 4.2. The Iudex is also the reborn Hydro Dragon, which the public does not know.

## status
@version: 4.2
### LITE
As of 7.1 the Maison Ordalie still runs Fontaine's judicial system under Chief Justice Neuvillette, who has also effectively taken over the running of Fontaine from the Palais Mermonia.
### STANDARD +
The Oratrice has not worked since 4.2, so verdicts no longer come from it. The Archon Quests of 6.x (Nod-Krai) and 7.0-7.1 did not touch this body in the sources checked.

## entry: The Iudex
@keys: Iudex, Chief Justice of Fontaine
@keys2: Fontaine, Neuvillette, Maison Ordalie, trial
### STANDARD
"Iudex" is the title of Fontaine's Chief Justice, held by Neuvillette. The Iudex presides over trials, may prosecute and enforce the law in person under an old tradition, and must step down as judge when doing so. The title heads the Maison Ordalie. The Marechaussee Phantom answers to the Iudex directly.

## entry: Trials at the Opera Epiclese
@keys: Opera Epiclese, public trial, Fontaine trial
@keys2: Fontaine, Iudex, Neuvillette
### STANDARD
Fontaine's trials are held in the Opera Epiclese, which is also a theatre. The public can watch, and under Furina they were treated as entertainment. The Iudex sits in the seat above where the Oratrice stands at the back of the stage, though other judges can preside too. Before 4.2, verdicts were issued by the Iudex together with the Oratrice. Convicts go to the Fortress of Meropide.
### LARGE +
Notice boards at the opera house showed public trials of burglars, an impostor who pretended to be the Maison Gardiennage, and the serial-disappearances case. The announcements state the charge, the investigation's findings and the verdict in a fixed style.

## entry: Duel instead of trial
@keys: Champion Duelist, Champion Duelists
@keys2: Fontaine, trial, Clorinde
### STANDARD
Anyone accused may ask an official Champion Duelist to fight instead of standing trial, as a way to defend their honour. If the accused wins, they are acquitted. If they lose, they go to trial, and death in the duel is possible if they refuse to yield. Duelists are chosen from Fontaine's best fighters, and the strongest today is Clorinde.

## entry: Oratrice Mecanique d'Analyse Cardinale
@keys: Oratrice, Oratrice Mecanique d'Analyse Cardinale, Indemnitium
@version: 4.2
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
The Oratrice is a large mechanical scale in the Opera Epiclese, made by Focalors. It turns belief in justice into Indemnitium, which supplies power to the Court of Fontaine and Erinnyes, and it delivered verdicts that the court had to accept. In 4.2 it condemned "the Hydro Archon" to death, which let Focalors destroy the divine throne. It has not functioned since. The wiki says Neuvillette's restored authority now substitutes for its power supply and he hears cases himself.

## entry: Carole and the Melusines' place in the law
@keys: Carole, Medal of Peace
@keys2: Melusine, Melusines, Neuvillette, Vautrin
@spoiler: minor
### LARGE
Carole was a Melusine who joined the Marechaussee Phantom alongside Vautrin to build goodwill with humans. Hardliners opposed to Neuvillette framed her, and she died proving her innocence. Neuvillette sentenced Vautrin for his revenge and, in the years after, defended the Melusines' standing. The rule that they be addressed as "she" is part of that legacy.

## entry: Sister Maisons of the Palais Mermonia
@keys: Maison Gestion, Maison Cardinalice, Gestionnaire, Gestionnaires
### STANDARD
Beyond the Maison Ordalie, the Palais Mermonia houses the Maison Gardiennage, the Maison Gestion and the Maison Cardinalice. Gestion handles documents and administrative work, divided into offices. Its employees are called Gestionnaires, and it is known for heavy bureaucracy and overworked staff. Cardinalice oversees financial approvals and other administration. Gardiennage polices the nation.

## entry: Trial of Furina
@keys: Furina's trial, trial of Furina
@keys2: Masquerade of the Guilty, Oratrice, Hydro Archon
@version: 4.2
@spoiler: major
### ULTRA
In the 4.2 Archon Quest, Neuvillette and the Traveler arranged a trial meant to expose Furina as a fraud posing as the Hydro Archon. The Traveler argued the case with evidence gathered during their journey and won the audience's support. The Oratrice agreed with the finding of guilt but sentenced the Hydro Archon to death, shocking everyone present. Focalors, inside the Oratrice, then executed herself with the accumulated Indemnitium. Furina lived on as an ordinary human.
