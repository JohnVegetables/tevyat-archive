## summary
### LITE
The Maison Gestion is Fontaine's governmental body for document management and administrative tasks, based in the Palais Mermonia in the Court of Fontaine. Its employees are called Gestionnaires. It is known for paperwork, official stamps, endless approvals and an overcomplicated bureaucracy.
### STANDARD +
Where the Maison Ordalie runs the courts, the Gardiennage keeps order and the Maison Cardinalice approves money, the Gestion keeps Fontaine's paper moving. Its workers are not villains. They are tired, quarrelsome, often witty clerks who care, in different ways, whether their work means anything.
### LARGE +
The Maison Gestion is also Fontaine's face abroad in small ways: two of its Gestionnaires are sent to Natlan on a preliminary mission to look for cooperation opportunities. Several former colleagues have left to do other things, such as the café owner Arouet.

## structure
### STANDARD
The Maison Gestion is divided into offices, for example one responsible for confidential items and one for staff matters. No office is directly in charge of Kameras, which shows how rigid the division is. Staff carry thick notepads to remember who handles what. It has its own official seal, and documents get a series of stamps from different people before they count.
### LARGE +
Named offices in A Certain Notice: Gattineo's handles secrecy and the security classification of documents, and Difor's handles staff. Cornelia fields enquiries and expense claims and sits nearest the entrance. Roialte acts as a senior coordinator who guides younger colleagues, and Semaine is a former courts assistant secretary who was transferred back. Some of them are mentioned in other places: Jacquetta says she chases signatures from two leaders whose desks stand side by side and who rarely agree, and Eunomia moved over from external publicity to a job about measurement standards. The Maison Gestion also has members in the Fountain of Lucine and Vasari Passage areas.

## leadership
### STANDARD
No overall head is named. Individual office heads appear, and Chief Justice Neuvillette's orders are treated as binding by Gestionnaires. Carsix, who has his own office and a backup official stamp and, according to Difor and Gattineo, relies on others to draft his speeches, is a senior figure whose exact title is not given.
### LARGE +
Difor says that if his office must take charge of something unwelcome, someone named Maneval should come and tell him in person, which suggests a higher authority but gives no title. Neuvillette appears more as a distant superior whose signature the Gestionnaires fear to need than as someone who manages the office day to day.

## members
### STANDARD
Gestionnaires seen in the Palais Mermonia include Roialte, Iaune, Semaine, Cornelia, Gattineo and Difor. Iaune is a newer, idealistic and sleep-deprived junior. Roialte is easy-going and experienced and explains the unwritten rules. Cornelia is polite and procedural. Gattineo is described as affable and as an exemplary Gestionnaire who avoids risk, while Difor is quick to anger but clever. Gravier is a shy newcomer and Chatelet is an old colleague of Arouet.
### LARGE +
Thales operates the stamping machine in A Certain Stamp. Clapiers and Monthois work in the office that stamps payments to outside parties, and Monthois is said to be waiting to retire without working. Arouet used to be a Gestionnaire and now manages Cafe Lutece in Vasari Passage. Fleicher is another Gestionnaire, met in Erinnyes, who says he cannot put work down once he has picked it up.

## goals
### STANDARD
The Gestion's stated work is to manage documents and handle administrative tasks, and in practice it tries to follow correct procedure: proper signatures, reports and classifications. Its staff also try to avoid taking sole responsibility, preferring shared accountability. Roialte teaches that in a large organisation everyone must take some responsibility.

## methods
### STANDARD
Work runs on stamps, forms, written proposals and reports. A policy needs signatures from the offices concerned, and each office tends to push the matter toward another. The Autostamper 3000 and the key that activates it are meant to preserve confidentiality, though the machine only works after a hard knock. Office notices are drafted by juniors and signed by those above them.
### LARGE +
In A Certain Notice, Gattineo and Difor both sign as 'co-organizer', which Difor defines as 'at least I'm not responsible for this', until Roialte reframes the task: Gattineo handles enforcement as before and Difor merely posts a large proposal. Gestionnaires also use a staff rule that no Kameras may be brought in except for work, to prevent leaks. Small expenses must pass through the Maison Cardinalice, so even a missing typewriter keycap generates multiple rounds of paperwork.

## history
### LARGE
No founding or earlier history is given. The Gestion first becomes visible in 4.0, when the Traveler meets Iaune and Roialte in the Palais Mermonia.
### ULTRA +
Events seen in 4.0: Iaune is told to write a notice banning Kameras after a staff member's photo of a desk exposes an internal memo about the dull menu. The memo was published in The Steambird. Later Carsix himself, who had insisted on the Kamera rule, is reported to have photographed his office, including meeting minutes, and passed the pictures to reporters, and Iaune faints from overwork and is taken to a doctor, and the colleagues show concern by planning to buy fruit and apply for sick leave. In 5.0 Iaune and Roialte appear in Natlan for an official mission, trying to find the office that handles trade there and discovering there is none of the kind they expect.

## relations
### STANDARD
The Maison Gestion shares the Palais Mermonia with the Maison Ordalie, Maison Gardiennage, Maison Cardinalice and the Marechaussee Phantom. It depends on the Maison Cardinalice for payment approval, and Gestionnaires treat Neuvillette's orders as imperatives. It is also tied to the press: The Steambird often reports the office's internal matters before its own memos spread, a point Gattineo notes with bitterness.
### LARGE +
Outside suppliers also feel its weight. In the commission Tales From the Court, Livre, a maker of Clockwork Meka, has had to return to the Palais Mermonia three times for payment on an order. Each time a different formality blocks him, such as an end-of-month audit, empty stamp pad ink or an updated form, and a police officer helps him. Caron says problems like this occur often.

## reputation
### STANDARD
Fontainians do not think much of the Maison Gestion. Gattineo says the people of the Court of Fontaine take every chance to mock and deride it. Its bureaucracy is a joke, and quests treat the office's slowness and overcomplication as typical. At the same time it is a source of employment and sometimes sympathy: its staff feel overworked, and colleagues care for one another.

## secrets
### ULTRA
Established so far: the Gestion's security classifications are criticised internally as overused. Difor says Gattineo's people stamp 'secret' on almost every document, so that nobody knows which secrets matter. This is Difor's opinion; Gattineo says only that the Gestion must act with caution because outsiders mock it. No hidden agenda is shown.

## status
### STANDARD
As of Version 7.1 the Maison Gestion is still in operation. Its latest appearance found is the 5.0 Natlan daily commission. No later change in leadership, structure or role was found.

## entry: Gestionnaire

@keys2: Maison Gestion, Palais Mermonia
@version: 4.0

### STANDARD
Gestionnaire is the title of an employee of the Maison Gestion. Staff are seen at their desks inside the Palais Mermonia and sometimes elsewhere in Fontaine, such as Vasari Passage and the Fountain of Lucine. They speak formally, quibble over procedure, and often talk about the purpose of their work.
### LARGE +
Newer Gestionnaires are expected to learn by doing. Roialte refuses to write Iaune's document for him because 'he will not remember this lesson', and says that fantasies about the job are for the young, not for Gestionnaires.

## entry: A Certain Notice, Stamp and Trifle

@keys2: Maison Gestion, Gestionnaire, Palais Mermonia
@version: 4.0

### STANDARD
These three linked Fontaine world quests follow Iaune in the Palais Mermonia. First, a ban on Kameras bounces between Gattineo and Difor until Roialte resolves it. Second, a missing typewriter keycap grows into a reimbursement problem that could reach Neuvillette. Third, the notice gets stamped by the Autostamper 3000, a colleague finds the keycap and Iaune collapses from overwork, ending with the question of what his work is for.
### LARGE +
The Traveler's role in each is mostly observation, nudging debates and even tapping the stamping machine to start it. Gattineo explains that Carsix leaked his own minutes to reporters, and that the meetings behind such minutes exist to decide what must not appear in a speech.

### STANDARD
Staff may not bring Kameras into the Maison Gestion or take photos except for work, to prevent leaks of secret information. The rule follows a case in which a staff member showed his desk to a Kamera Club and a reporter spotted an internal memo in the photo, which appeared in The Steambird.

## entry: Autostamper 3000
@keys: Autostamper 3000, autostamper
@keys2: Maison Gestion, stamp
@version: 4.0
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
A slow, temperamental stamping machine that Thales brought in for confidentiality. It needs a key and a correctly aligned document, and users must sign in and be photographed with a Kamera, per an instruction attributed to Carsix. In A Certain Stamp it only works after the Traveler hits it hard. A backup stamp is kept in a box in Carsix's office, which Thales has a key to.

## entry: Livre's unpaid order
@keys: Livre, Clockwork Meka order, Clapiers
@keys2: Palais Mermonia, Maison Gestion
@version: 4.0
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
In the Tales From the Court daily commission, Livre cannot get payment for Clockwork Meka that the Palais Mermonia ordered. Cornelia says only Clapiers, who holds the stamp key, can process the case. Livre is turned away over an audit, a lack of ink and a form update. Officer Rhemia helps him obtain the form so he can try again, and Caron says that this happens often.

## entry: Natlan delegation
@keys: An Official Mission, Roialte, Iaune
@keys2: Natlan, Maison Gestion, delegation
@version: 5.0

### STANDARD
The daily commission An Official Mission shows Roialte and Iaune in Natlan on behalf of the Maison Gestion, sent to make preliminary contact and find areas for cooperation in trade or travel, though they say trade specialists would handle the real talks. They are puzzled by the lack of an administrative structure and the Traveler suggests they speak to the tribes directly.
