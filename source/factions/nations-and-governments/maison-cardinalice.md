## summary
### LITE
The Maison Cardinalice is one of the government offices of Fontaine, based in the Palais Mermonia in the Court of Fontaine. It oversees financial approvals and related administration, which in practice means reviewing and signing off on expense reports and salary payments from other offices.
### STANDARD +
It is a separate body from the Maison Gestion, which handles documents and general administration, and from the Maison Ordalie, which runs the courts under Chief Justice Neuvillette. Ordinary Fontainians rarely deal with it directly. Most of what is known comes from office workers who must submit paperwork to it.
### LARGE +
It is seen mostly from outside. Clerks in other offices speak of it as the last gate before money is released, and its dealings stay in the background of the Palais Mermonia's everyday routine.

## structure
### STANDARD
No internal ranks, departments or staff names are given in canon. The Maison Cardinalice is one of the offices that share the Palais Mermonia with the Maison Gestion, the Maison Ordalie, the Maison Gardiennage and the Marechaussee Phantom. It receives batched summaries from other offices rather than individual claims.
### LARGE +
Cornelia of the Maison Gestion describes the chain: a Gestionnaire files a claim, the Gestion compiles all receipts and documents monthly into summary reports, and these go to the Maison Cardinalice at month's end. Whether the Cardinalice has staff divisions of its own is not stated.

## leadership
### STANDARD
No head of the Maison Cardinalice is named in game text. The wiki infobox lists Chief Justice Neuvillette as leader, but the quests show something narrower: when a sum is large enough, the Maison Cardinalice sends the matter up to Neuvillette for approval, and its requests for written explanations are said to come from him. Treat his leadership as unconfirmed.
### LARGE +
The Gestion staff are not sure those requests really come from Neuvillette, so the office has an air of using the Chief Justice's name as cover. This is the opinion of Iaune, a Gestionnaire, not a confirmed fact, and nobody dares to test it.

## goals
### STANDARD
Its role is to control spending and to make sure that salaries and expense payments are reviewed and signed before money leaves the public purse. Workers who deal with it describe a system built around audit trails, including end-of-year checks on where funds came from.

## methods
### STANDARD
Approval is slow, written and cumulative. Claims are batched monthly, reviewed, signed and only then paid. If an expense raises questions, the person who made it must submit a written report. For large totals, the Maison Cardinalice submits the matter to Neuvillette himself.
### LARGE +
In A Certain Stamp, a missing typewriter keycap leads to a demand for a separate report to the Chief Justice, because keycaps are not on the list of eligible items. Cornelia says the Maison Cardinalice relayed this as Neuvillette's own request. A cheaper route exists: the claimant buys the item and holds an acknowledgment of debt, which is paid from the public purse once the process finishes.

## relations
### STANDARD
Its closest working partner in the quests is the Maison Gestion, whose staff depend on it for payment approval. Because Neuvillette signs off on large sums, the Chief Justice sits at the top of its approval chain in practice. It shares the Palais Mermonia with the other Fontainian governing offices.

## reputation
### STANDARD
Among those who deal with it, the Maison Cardinalice is seen as slow and exacting. Palais Mermonia bureaucracy is a running joke, and this office is the last obstacle in the payment chain. The Strict Adherence to Office Codes furnishing set, which includes items named after the Maison Cardinalice, recreates the regimented, always-busy look of the Palais.
### ULTRA +
The Palais Mermonia's own map text calls it Fontaine's administrative centre and, to Fontainians, essentially the highest authority in the land. Within that building the Maison Cardinalice is the office others wait on for money. The furnishing set is a gift set that Neuvillette and Clorinde both like when placed in a Serenitea Pot, which hints at their shared taste for orderly work spaces but says nothing about the office itself.

## status
### STANDARD
As of Version 7.1 it is still an active office in Fontaine. Nothing about it was found in Nod-Krai or Snezhnaya content, and no scandal, reform or change of leadership has been shown.

## entry: Reimbursement approval process
@keys: Maison Cardinalice, Cardinal House
@keys2: reimbursement, expense report, approval
@version: 4.0
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
In A Certain Trifle, Cornelia of the Maison Gestion explains how an office expense is paid. The claimant signs a request; receipts are compiled into monthly reports; everything goes to the Maison Cardinalice at month's end; payment follows once the review and signatures are done. The same approval covers salaries across the Maison Gestion, and the Maison Cardinalice may pass a large sum to Neuvillette. A queried expense needs a written report. Iaune, the Gestionnaire in the quest, decides to buy a keycap himself rather than risk needing the Chief Justice's signature.
### LARGE +
In the next quest, A Certain Stamp, the Maison Cardinalice is blamed for the demand for a separate report on one keycap. Roialte tells Iaune that nobody tests whether such orders truly come from Neuvillette, because for a Gestionnaire an order from the Chief Justice carries the full weight of the administration.
