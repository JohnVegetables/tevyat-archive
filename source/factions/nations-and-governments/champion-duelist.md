## summary
### LITE
The Champion Duelists are Fontaine's official trial fighters. A person on trial who wants to defend their honour can request a duel against one of them. The duelists are drawn from the country's best fighters, and the strongest at present is Clorinde. If the defendant wins, they are acquitted; if they lose, they simply stand trial.
### STANDARD +
Duels are a legal right, not a spectacle, though they are staged within Fontaine's theatrical court culture. Few defendants can beat an official duelist, so most who request one end up conceding.

## structure
### STANDARD
Canon describes the Champion Duelists as a group of selected fighters, not a ranked order. No leader, rank system or parent body is named. A Gardes member says the duelists keep an office in Quartier Lyonnais of the Court of Fontaine, across from the Adventurers' Guild headquarters.
### LARGE +
Known current members are Clorinde, Savinien and Guillaume Marais. Ismenor is a retired Champion Duelist. Clorinde's own story shows a qualification trial, but the wiki does not describe a general process beyond saying duelists are chosen from among the country's best fighters.

## members
### STANDARD
Clorinde is the best known and, per the wiki, the strongest of them: undefeated in formal duels. Savinien, an open-world NPC in Quartier Lyonnais, is a duelist who jokes about an extra fee. Guillaume Marais is named as a current member in the lines of Sir Arthur, the Steambird newspaper-seller. Ismenor, now retired, spends his evenings in the Vasari Passage and talks about the profession wryly.
### LARGE +
Historical member: Marfisa, the earliest known Champion Duelist, who lived over 500 years ago. Clorinde has her own character entry for her history, personality and relationships; this entry only covers the office.

## methods
### STANDARD
A defendant who does not accept that they should be tried can request a duel to defend their reputation. Ismenor says only a concession or expiry of the duel can end it, and few people can defeat an official duelist. The worst case is the defendant being killed, which per the wiki only happens when they refuse to yield.
### LARGE +
Because the duelist fights on behalf of the court, the choice is deliberate: win and walk free, or lose and face the ordinary trial. Vitruvia, a legal adviser who once wished to be a duelist but cannot fight, builds her business around helping clients avoid both duelists and the theatre by exploiting gaps in the law. She treats the duelists as the ultimate risk of a case.

## history
### STANDARD
The origins of the office are unknown, but it existed over 500 years ago. The earliest known member, Marfisa, lived in that era.
### LARGE
**Marfisa.** She came from Fleuve Cendre, where the weak became prey, and made her name with self-taught spearmanship. Emanuel Guillotin, a Marechaussee Hunter, defeated her and pushed her to adopt justice as her rule; she became a Champion Duelist and offered justice to those who sought to evade judgment. According to weapon text, she was later said to have faced Parsifal the Great in a trial by combat during the Siege of Poisson. While trying to hunt down Emanuel for a final duel, she met the Narzissenkreuz Ordo and was dissolved by its leader, who absorbed her mastery of the spear.

**Callas.** Years before the Fontaine Archon Quest, Callas, president of the Spina di Rosula, chose to settle an accusation by duelling Clorinde. He died in the duel. Clorinde recognised that he had let it happen, though his reasons were not understood publicly until years later.
### ULTRA +
Marfisa is the second person known to have been dissolved by the leader of the Narzissenkreuz Ordo. The Parsifal duel and the Siege of Poisson episode come from weapon and artifact descriptions only; the wiki itself notes the Parsifal in question may be Josephine, so treat that as an unsettled account.

## relations
### STANDARD
The duelists work within the justice system alongside the Gardes and the court. Clorinde once served as Furina's bodyguard and stood ready to duel at the trial that exposed Furina. The Spina di Rosula's president Callas died in a duel with Clorinde, which shaped her later bond with his daughter Navia.
### LARGE +
The office has older ties to the Marechaussee Hunters: Emanuel Guillotin's defeat of Marfisa turned her toward justice, and Clorinde is the inheritor of the nearly forgotten Hunters. See her entry for details.

## reputation
### STANDARD
Citizens treat the duelists as a sobering last resort. Opponents tend to surrender rather than face Clorinde, and journalists follow her. Vitruvia says she would have liked to join them, and Savinien jokes about extra fees.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the Champion Duelists remain active in Fontaine. Clorinde is still the strongest and is no longer on a bodyguard post; she now works as a senior Game Master with the Tabletop Troupe and is travelling with Navia in Snezhnaya.
### STANDARD +
No change to the office's legal role has been shown after the events of Fontaine's Archon Quest.

### STANDARD
Marfisa was a Champion Duelist over 500 years ago, originally from Fleuve Cendre and a spear fighter. She was beaten by the Marechaussee Hunter Emanuel Guillotin, took up his idea of justice and became a duelist. She was later dissolved by the leader of the Narzissenkreuz Ordo, who took her spear technique.
### LARGE +
She has a link to Parsifal the Great through the Lumidouce Bell, which Parsifal gave her as an invitation to a surface performance. In weapon text she faces Parsifal in a trial by combat; the wiki says Parsifal may be Josephine using the name.
