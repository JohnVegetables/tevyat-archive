## summary
### LITE
The Royal Energy Commission of Snezhnaya, often just the Energy Commission, is the state organisation responsible for Snezhnaya's energy upkeep. It maintains the Kresnik's Torches, the furnaces that make Snezhnayan cities habitable, and manages the transport and use of Kresnik Kristall, the ore the torches burn. It answers to Zapolyarny Palace.
### STANDARD +
Its staff are mostly administrators, inspectors and technicians who travel between torches. A few are scientists: Mitya, an expert in energy technology, also works on Pierro's Project Mistilteinn at the Armory Palace.

## structure
### STANDARD
The Commission has a President, a Vice President and rank-and-file administrators, technicians and prospectors who work in the field. The post of President fell vacant on Izmaylov's death and has been covered by an Acting Chair assigned from Zapolyarny Palace, with Zaretsky as Vice President. Its people are constantly on the move, because the torches they tend are spread across the whole nation. Staff such as Rakitina inspect local torches, while others, such as Yelagina and Zaretsky, work in the central Kresnik's Burning Heart beneath Snezhnograd. Zavyalov and others report that outdoor teams sometimes cross paths with Shadow Realms.
### LARGE +
Field teams appear in the open world as Energy Commission Prospectors and logistics or contract staff at facilities, ice quarries, ruins and maintenance centres. Rakitina, at the Morepesok torch, describes herself as an administrator for the Commission, so the work is not only manual. Staff also work from Nasha Town, where Manez says he was working on kinetic mechanisms for the Commission before his father pulled him back to run a port. The Commission is not the same body as the Snezhnaya Royal Railway Transport Bureau, which runs the trains that carry the ore.

## leadership
### LITE
The Commission has been led by an Acting Chair, Zarechnaya, since its long-time President Izmaylov, an old Domovoy, died. She was sent from Zapolyarny Palace to keep the Commission running until a permanent chair is found.
### STANDARD +
Zarechnaya says the post could not be left empty, since the Commission is vital to people's livelihoods across Snezhnaya, and hopes to return to the Palace soon. She finds the handover paperwork in perfect order yet suspects things have been carefully hidden, and then shrugs it off as long as the Commission keeps functioning. Zaretsky, the Vice President, grumbles about the High Table and the gangs and says some matters left by the former Chairperson now fall to people lower down. Staff such as Zybina still call Izmaylov "the President" out of habit.
### LARGE +
Izmaylov was long-lived and held the presidency for a very long time. He had hoped to join the Commission as a young man, but Grand Duke Alvis placed him at Hvergelmir to spy on Aksinya. Her kindness converted him, and after she vanished in the Cataclysm he took the presidency from under Alvis' nose after making an undisclosed deal with the new Tsaritsa. Later he also became Speaker of the High Table, the council of the fae gangs. Late in life he handed that post to Zmei and moved the Commission away from Shadow Realm commissions.

## members
### STANDARD
Named staff, all met as NPCs in 7.0, include Zarechnaya (Acting Chair), Zaretsky (Vice President), Yelagina (keeps the central torch's log), Rakitina (inspects a local torch), Zavyalov, Zybina (who has to handle technical problems alone), and Mitya. Izmaylov is the deceased President. Manez is a former member who left for family reasons.
### LARGE +
Mitya is the Commission's most prominent scientist, an energy expert known for being talkative, sociable and fond of the Korolevskiy Theater. His sentient scarf sometimes chokes him to stop him leaking secrets. He is escorted by Commander Valeriy of the Special Assignments Battalion, and in Pierro's Project Mistilteinn he extracted energy from Stellar Linchpins and reverse-engineered it into Anti-Celestia Energy. Zybina says the Commission once had a "technical expert" who made troubleshooting much faster and has since been sent elsewhere; the text does not name him, so do not assume it is Mitya.

## goals
### STANDARD
The Commission exists to keep Snezhnayans warm and alive. Its work is to keep the Kresnik's Torches running, supply them with Kresnik Kristall, and ensure that ore and heat reach every settlement. Rakitina describes the network design: instead of one mega-torch for the entire permafrost, which proved a pipe dream, Snezhnaya maps habitable zones with torches as local nodes joined by railways, cutting the heat lost in long-distance transport. The Eye of Graeae says that before the torches existed the land consisted only of Frigid and Subzero zones, utterly unsuited to human habitation.

## methods
### STANDARD
Routine work is inspection and logging: staff listen to a torch, check its exhaust pipes and record its state, then clear faults. Ore is mined mainly in Novokitezhgrad and shipped by rail to every torch. A village such as Alatyr, which has no train station, can lose its deliveries and risk freezing, as a Druzhna soldier fears in the quest Gunfire in the Silent Lands. When teams find a Shadow Realm while travelling, protocol is to leave safely and report it to the Druzhna.
### LARGE +
The Druzhna in turn say they handle every Shadow Realm report the Commission sends, by forming search groups that neutralise the energy core if they can find it or else seal the Realm off. Most Shadow Realms vanish on their own, so the real danger is entering one by accident. Yarovaya, a Druzhna officer, finds the Commission oddly well informed about Shadow Realms and calls it good enough that they detect them in time.

## history
### LARGE
**Origins.** The Commission is tied to the Belyi Tsar's era. According to the loading-screen text, the Belyi Tsar created the Kresnik's Torch to help humans survive. In Yelagina's words, construction of the central torch began almost immediately after his experiments in the Pale Crown Palace were finished, and the prototype torches remain there. The wiki's history section for the Commission itself is blank, so no founding date is known.

**Izmaylov's presidency.** Izmaylov took the chair after the Cataclysm destroyed Kitezhgrad and the new Tsaritsa showed little regard for the old guard. Alvis went off to build Novokitezhgrad to win her favour, and Izmaylov instead struck his own bargain with the Tsaritsa. Their rivalry later played out over who would direct the Transport Bureau, until the heroes of Luchkin, whom Izmaylov backed, put down a rebellion and credited him with the win. He and the survivors founded the High Table.

**The Shadow Realm commissions.** For about five centuries the Commission took on Shadow Realm work, in part so that Izmaylov could search for Aksinya. It cost him his health. Aksinya was eventually rescued, visited the aged Izmaylov, and he returned the pen she had given him. Satisfied, he steered the Commission away from deep involvement in Shadow Realm matters.
### ULTRA +
**Izmaylov's death.** His son Vasiliy asked to take over his work and resented how the fae gangs got only the jobs the Druzhna and the Commission refused. In Disputes Without Honor and Humanity (7.0), Vasiliy says Izmaylov refused to continue with the Shadow Realm and that in a rage he killed his father. By the time of the open-world dialogue, the Commission's staff believe he died of a sudden illness. Gang members also say the Old Sire had strings to pull at the Commission and that some gang members had done odd jobs for its senior staff, which let Vasiliy's side find sensitive information.

**After.** The Palace appointed Zarechnaya as Acting Chair. As of 7.1 no permanent chair has been named in the content checked.

## relations
### STANDARD
- **Zapolyarny Palace and the Tsaritsa**: the Commission is affiliated with the Palace, which sent its Acting Chair.
- **Druzhna**: they take over Shadow Realm reports that Commission teams pass on.
- **High Table and the Izmaylov gangs**: Izmaylov led both the Commission and the High Table, so their histories are linked. Gang members say the Commission and Druzhna leave them the thankless jobs.
- **Snezhnaya Royal Railway Transport Bureau**: carries the ore the Commission oversees.
### LARGE +
- **Armory Palace and Pierro**: Mitya carries out energy research for Project Mistilteinn there, and Pierro treats his eccentricity as the price of results.
- **Hvergelmir and Aksinya**: Izmaylov's loyalty to Aksinya shaped the Commission's Shadow Realm work.
- **Alvis**: Izmaylov's lifelong rival, from the contest over the presidency.

## reputation
### ULTRA
The Commission is respected as vital rather than glamorous. NPCs treat its work as ordinary public service, and staff complain about workload and paperwork. The gangs resent it and the Druzhna as masters who keep the best jobs. Zavyalov remembers Izmaylov as a remarkably interesting old Domovoy, Zybina remembers him warmly as someone who solved problems himself and told stories of old Snezhnaya, while Vasiliy calls him someone else's dog.

## entry: Kresnik's Torch
@keys: Kresnik's Torch, Kresnik's Torches, Kresnik Torch, Kresnik's Burning Heart
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
A Kresnik's Torch is a furnace that burns Kresnik Kristall and keeps a temperature-controlled zone alive around a city or settlement. Every Snezhnayan city is built around one, the loading-screen text credits the Belyi Tsar with creating it, and smaller civilian models stand across the land. The most important one is the central torch in Kresnik's Burning Heart beneath Snezhnograd, built soon after the Belyi Tsar's work at the Pale Crown Palace ended, where prototype torches still stand. The Commission is the organisation responsible for overseeing and maintaining them. In gameplay, small torches dispense Kresnik Energy for the Eye of Graeae.

## entry: Kresnik Kristall
@keys: Kresnik Kristall, Kristall, Kresnik crystal ore
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
Kresnik Kristall is the crystalline ore that Snezhnaya burns for warmth. Quest text describes it as power refined from the authority released when the Cryo Dragon lost control in Snezhnaya about 6,000 years ago, and Aksinya says the outpouring turned ley lines into veins of pure crystal. Early settlers are said to have found the veins by following Kristall Bugs. It burns very efficiently, and it is mined mostly in Novokitezhgrad and sent out by rail, with the Commission overseeing transport and use.

## entry: Izmaylov

@keys2: Energy Commission, High Table, Domovoy, Snezhnaya
@version: 7.0
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
Izmaylov, the Old Sire, was a Domovoy of Kitezhgrad who served first at Hvergelmir under Aksinya and later served as President of the Royal Energy Commission and Speaker of the High Table. He was devoted to Aksinya, spent centuries seeking her, and saw her rescued. His son Vasiliy killed him. For the full account see his own file.
