## summary
### LITE
The Collective of Plenty, also called the Teteocan, is one of Natlan's six tribes. Its people farm the rich volcanic soil beside the Great Volcano of Tollan in Atocpan and live alongside Tatankasaurs, their bonded saurians. The tribe is known across Natlan for two things: the best fruit and vegetables in the nation, and a culture built around physical strength, from bodybuilding contests to wrestling. Its chief is Acatl, a cheerful masked wrestler.
### STANDARD +
Notable members include Iansan, head coach of the tribe's fitness club and bearer of the Ancient Name Uwezo ("Power"), and her star pupil Varesa, an orchard manager and Patrol Team warrior whom local children call the "Masked Hero". Of all the tribes, they live the farthest from the Stadium of the Sacred Flame.

## culture
### STANDARD
Fitness is close to sacred here: Iansan's stories say it is second in importance only to the Pilgrimage of the Return of the Sacred Flame. Most adults have huge, heavily muscled frames. Young people show off their muscles to each other as a friendly greeting, and a fitness coach is only taken seriously if they are very strong. Wrestling Tatankasaurs is a long-standing tradition, and beating one is a great honour. Some warriors even seek out wild Halberd-Crest Birds to find out "whose head is harder".

Many wrestlers wear masks. When two masked fighters duel, the loser takes off their mask and the winner keeps theirs, keeping an air of mystery that invites others to challenge them. Acatl says he has never once been unmasked by human or saurian. When young tribespeople ask how they would know he is not an imposter, he tells them to face him in the ring, because "muscles... never lie".
### LARGE +
The tribe values courage, perseverance and refusing to back down over formality, and treats guests generously. Acatl's own motto is "If it's something you believe in, just do it!" He welcomes returning friends with a hearty tribal meal. Varesa says that in the tribe, a person's birthday is celebrated with a bonfire feast among friends. Tired tribespeople drink a special fruit juice, Fruits Aplenty, to recover. Honours are physical too: Kitera, a warrior of the tribe, won an honorary belt so heavy that two people struggled to lift it, so he had a lighter copy made to wear while training.
### ULTRA +
The tribe tells legends of "hidden heroes". These are Teteocan warriors whose bodies can use every bit of energy in their food when it matters most, which gives them enormous strength and appetites to match. Most never inherit an Ancient Name or become famous. Instead they serve quietly as the tribe's cornerstones until their moment comes. Acatl used this legend to explain young Varesa's appetite. Rumours also circulate among outsiders that Iansan's strength comes from a secret tribal weapon. Members answer such questions coldly, and say only that she trains hard.

## places
### STANDARD
The tribe's settlement, also named "Collective of Plenty", lies on fertile plains below the Great Volcano of Tollan. Volcanic ash has made the land rich, and its golden harvests are the tribe's pride. At its centre is the Grand Wrestling Grounds, also called the Resonating Arena: a raised arena decorated with huge bull horns, where the tribe's athletic competitions are held. Varesa's family orchard stands just outside the settlement and is guarded by her Tatankasaur Vanana.
### LARGE +
The settlement also has a training ground with a fitness coach and trainees, machines for making Fruits Aplenty and puffed grainfruit bars, and bulletin boards. Runes are supposed to keep saurians from other places out of the tribe's grounds. The volcano sometimes throws boulders called Pulverite into the area. Locals simply smash them, with their shoulders, feet or heads, or with a charging Tatankasaurus. Tonaca, at the Grand Wrestling Grounds, handles the tribe's relations with visitors who help out.

## structure
### STANDARD
The chief leads the tribe, with the elders also taking part in major decisions such as war planning. The fitness club is one of the tribe's central institutions. Meztcala founded it, and its head coach is a figure of real authority: Acatl was both chief and head coach before Iansan took over the club. The Patrol Team defends the tribe against monsters and the Abyss; Varesa is a core member, and Iansan led its counterattacks in the three-pronged Abyss assault. Like every tribe, the Collective has a Wayob, which chooses bearers of Ancient Names. For the Collective of Plenty, the name "Power" (Uwezo) carries special weight, standing for unrivalled honour and responsibility.
### LARGE +
The tribe's Tatankasaurs have a chief of their own. In Iansan's time this was Emela, an orphaned Tatankasaurus raised by the tribe who grew larger than any other Tatankasaurus there. She was killed fighting in the war against the Abyss invasion. In Ahpub's era, when the tribe's chief vanished, Och-Kan's emissaries tried to persuade the tribe to hand authority to the Grand Alliance until it chose a new chief.

## leadership
### LITE
Chief Acatl is a tall, powerfully built man who always wears a horned wrestling mask. He is warm, relaxed about formalities and attentive to the tribe's young people, and he has long kept a concerned eye on Varesa.
### STANDARD +
Acatl coached the fitness club when the young Iansan, newly chosen by the Ancient Name "Power", asked to train. When she was badly hurt trying to lift a barbell, he was criticised for not stopping her. He still visited her and told her that fitness is "a question of choice", not of credentials. He also kept a watchful eye on the young Varesa, and agreed with the warriors to admit her to wrestling training. During the war he led the tribe's defence and repelled the Abyss.
### ULTRA +
Earlier chiefs remembered in canon include Ahpub, the founding hero of the Grand Alliance era. Meztcala led the tribe when it was still struggling to settle by the volcano during an eruption cycle. He taught all his people his training methods, telling them, "You can all choose to become like me", until together they made the volcano's foot a safe home. Tepanahui was chief during the last Bloomflower Trials, hundreds of years ago.

## members
### LARGE
- **Iansan**: head coach, nutritionist and founder of the Pilgrimage Victors Academy. She holds an all-time record for Pilgrimage victories and bears the Ancient Name Uwezo.
- **Varesa**: orchard manager, Patrol Team warrior and famous competitive eater. Her fruit is sold to every tribe.
- **Acatl**: the chief.
- **Bennett** is the son of Macani, a member of the tribe (see the entry on Macani).
- Residents of the settlement include the chef Atolli, the reputation advisor Tonaca, the fitness coach Tecoma, the wrestling referee Macacoz, the blacksmith Xona and the self-styled "strongest in the Collective of Plenty" trainer Ma'chah. The merchant Cintli sells the tribe's produce at the Stadium.
### ULTRA +
Members who died in the Abyss invasion include Nzinga, a coach seen training a scholar at the Stadium, and Tlilhua. Zompaynco, a tribe member and Saurian Relics Association investigator who hates the cold, was later found in Nasha Town in Nod-Krai (6.0). Figures from the tribe's history are covered under history and in sub-entries: Ahpub, Waskar, Wanjiru, Fietena, Copan and Macani.

## goals
### STANDARD
The tribe's aims are simple: feed Natlan, grow strong, and protect its homeland and its neighbours. Its teaching, passed down from Meztcala, is that strength is something anyone can choose to build. Iansan has pushed this outward, offering training to people from any tribe.

## methods
### LARGE
The tribe's economy rests on farming, and it grows enough to sell a good share of its produce to the other tribes. Varesa's produce even reaches buyers abroad, including Escoffier and Dori. Tonaca says that the tribe, as Natlan's most agriculturally developed, puts resource coordination and management first. In war, the tribe relies on its Patrol Team and on warriors toughened by constant training. When the Abyss struck in force, the elders spread the defence out in layers, kept the front lines far from civilians, and sent elite strike teams to take out the enemy's leaders.

## history
### LARGE
**Founding era.** The tribes date back at least to the age of Xbalanque and Och-Kan's Grand Alliance; Och-Kan's rule is placed about 2,000 years ago. The tribe's founding is linked to Ahpub, first bearer of the Ancient Name Uwezo and one of the original heroes. He and his twin sister Ixquieh followed Xbalanque and joined Och-Kan's Grand Alliance. Ahpub became chief of the Collective of Plenty, and Ixquieh chief of the Children of Echoes. Ahpub hated dragons. When Och-Kan ordered the pursuit of the fleeing dragons, Ahpub was the only hero who obeyed, and he led the tribe's warriors to his sister's mountains. Their argument ended with Ixquieh dead by his blade and Ahpub gone into the fire. Accounts of their deaths differ.

**Settling the volcano.** Under Chief Meztcala, during a dangerous eruption cycle, the tribe finally secured its home at the volcano's foot by teaching every member to fight monsters and magma. That was the origin of the fitness club.

**Five hundred years ago.** Wanjiru, a "great runner" of the tribe and bearer of Uwezo, was one of the six heroes who fought the Abyss beside Mavuika. She was Mavuika's friend and regular wrestling partner, and she won their last match before Mavuika became Archon. Wanjiru died in the final battle. Fietena, a masked wrestler born in the tribe who had grown up listening to Wanjiru's stories, helped defend Natlan through the hard decade that followed.

**Modern era.** When Varesa was five, the Abyss raided the tribe and ruined her family's orchard. Later, during an eruption cycle, the Abyss launched a three-pronged assault meant to isolate and wipe out the tribe's warriors. With only a few Scions of the Canopy couriers to help, the elders' layered defence and repeated counterattacks under Iansan won through. Varesa won her Vision in that battle.
### ULTRA +
**The war (5.0-5.3).** During Chapter V, Acatl's tribe held off the Abyss invasion on its own. Iansan told the Traveler that Varesa had things under control. The tribe lost people, including Nzinga, Tlilhua and the Tatankasaur chief Emela. Before the final battle, Acatl came to the Stadium to give the tribe's blessing, and Iansan invited the Traveler to visit once the war was over.

**Mushroom Realm Mystery (5.5).** The Traveler visited the tribe after the war. By then it had finished its repairs and was helping other tribes. Red and green berryshrooms had sprung up in Varesa's family orchard, and the tribe had happily eaten them for years, following an unexplained warning from a Sumeru scholar to eat any green ones at once. With Varesa, the Traveler found that the green mushrooms came from Bifrons, an ancient fungus god buried under the volcano who hoped to rebuild his power. When the green mushrooms suddenly multiplied, the tribe defused the danger by holding a huge feast and eating them. Tighnari later promised to send scholars to monitor the mushrooms.

**A Space and Time for You (5.7).** A bodybuilding competition was cancelled when the Abyss Order attacked the tribe. Iansan noted that this was the Order's first coordinated direct attack on them, and it was part of a simultaneous strike on every tribe. The tribe became a base for the Traveler's investigation, and Dainsleif broke the Order's front lines there.

## relations
### LARGE
- **Other tribes**: the Collective of Plenty feeds them. Varesa's cart reaches from the Children of Echoes to the Flower-Feather Clan, and Iansan's academy welcomes other tribes' trainees. Fietena's marriage into the Flower-Feather Clan strengthened ties between the two tribes.
- **Children of Echoes**: linked through the twin founders Ahpub and Ixquieh, whose adventures inspired traditions in both tribes. Varesa learned mask-making from their smiths.
- **Scions of the Canopy**: their couriers were the only reinforcements in the three-pronged Abyss assault.
- **Masters of the Night-Wind**: Citlali helped Iansan bind a young Tatankasaurus spirit, Alaye, into its skull.
- **Pyro Archon**: Mavuika is admired here; Wanjiru was her friend and wrestling rival.
- **Abyss**: the tribe's chief enemy, repelled many times.
### ULTRA +
- **People of the Springs**: Mualani has helped with Varesa's swimming drills.
- **Grand Alliance**: the tribe was part of the Alliance, but Ahpub's death left Och-Kan pressing it to give up its own authority. Waskar, a warrior of the tribe, later died in the Scarlet-Eyed Youth's rebellion against Och-Kan.
- **Saurian Relics Association**: its founder is said to have come from this tribe.

## reputation
### STANDARD
To other Natlanese, the Collective of Plenty means "universal fitness" and sweet fruit and fresh vegetables. Not everyone agrees: Suriqui, a dissenter within the tribe, calls its muscle-worshippers a "bunch of meatheads" and insists the heroes of old carried a protective layer of fat. In children's stories, the tribe's "Masked Hero" (Varesa) shows up, wreathed in lightning, wherever monsters run amok.

## entry: Iansan and the fitness club
@keys: Iansan, Pilgrimage Victors Academy, Blood of Egungun, Uwezo
@keys2: Collective of Plenty, Teteocan, fitness club, Acatl, Meztcala
### STANDARD
Iansan's family carries the "Blood of Egungun": none of them ever grow taller than a child. The tribe took the family in and always protected them, keeping them off the front lines as medics and cooks. When the Ancient Name "Power" chose Iansan, many tribespeople doubted her. She asked Acatl, then chief and head coach, to train her, injured herself badly on her first lift, and came back anyway. Her mother suggested building on what the family was good at, treating injuries and cooking. Iansan then created a training method of steady routines, controlled calories and precise nutrition, which introduced the science of nutrition to Natlan. She later beat the Tatankasaurs' chief, succeeded Acatl as head coach and set a Pilgrimage victory record. When other tribes began calling her bloodline a gift that explained her wins, she moved the club close to the Stadium as the Pilgrimage Victors Academy. Her flyers promise that toil, not birth, gives strength.

## entry: Varesa's orchard and the Masked Hero
@keys: Varesa's Orchard, Masked Hero, Vanana, Inferno Stomach Supremo
@keys2: Varesa, Collective of Plenty, Teteocan
### STANDARD
Varesa's family orchard, just outside the settlement, sends fresh produce to every tribe. Varesa took it over after years of training and runs it with her Tatankasaur Vanana. Children's stories tell of a lightning-wreathed "Masked Hero" whose rescues happen to follow the orchard's trade routes. Varesa's mask is based on one worn by the tribe's heroine Fietena, and grateful villagers draw a little mask on their thank-you letters to her. She has entered the Pilgrimage five times and never got past the team stage, because she cannot bring herself to fight her fellow Natlanese seriously. Against the Abyss, though, she has never lost while defending the tribe.

## entry: The three-pronged Abyss assault
@keys: three-way assault, three-pronged assault
@keys2: Collective of Plenty, Teteocan, Varesa, Iansan, Abyss
@spoiler: minor
### LARGE
In Varesa's second month on the Patrol Team, the Abyss attacked the Collective of Plenty from three sides during one of the volcano's eruption cycles. Only a handful of Scions of the Canopy couriers arrived to help. The elders spread the defences out in layers, kept the front lines away from civilians, and posted the couriers on high ground to track the enemy's leaders. Elite warriors then struck those leaders from behind. Each counterattack lost more fighters. In the last, after Iansan cleared the way, Varesa came back carrying a wounded older warrior and dragging a mitachurl, put on her tattered mask and destroyed the enemy leader. Iansan dug her out of the battlefield asleep, with a new Electro Vision in her hand. She slept for half a month and remembers nothing of the fight.

## entry: Ahpub and the Grand Alliance
@keys: Ahpub
@keys2: Collective of Plenty, Teteocan, Ixquieh, Och-Kan, chief
@spoiler: minor
### LARGE
Ahpub, the first bearer of Uwezo, fought with blowdarts and had a fiery temper. He and his twin Ixquieh lost their parents to a dragon lord's cruelty and were reunited with the help of the Collective of Plenty and the mine clans. They swore to end suffering, followed Xbalanque and joined Och-Kan's Grand Alliance, and Ahpub became the Collective's chief. When Och-Kan ordered the fleeing dragons pursued, Ahpub alone obeyed. He marched the tribe's warriors to his sister's mountains and demanded she hand over the saurians she sheltered. Their private argument ended when his blade, meant only to intimidate her, struck her throat. He carried her into the fire and was never seen again. Afterwards Och-Kan quietly tried to take both leaderless tribes under the Alliance's authority.

## entry: Wanjiru and Fietena
@keys: Wanjiru, Fietena, Fearless Rock, Kayeke
### LARGE
Wanjiru, the tribe's Uwezo bearer 500 years ago, was called "the great runner". With her Tatankasaurus Kayeke she joined the six heroes who fought the Abyss alongside Mavuika. She was Mavuika's close friend and wrestling rival, and she died in the heroes' final battle. Fietena, who had followed Wanjiru as a girl, was a masked wrestler of the tribe who never received an Ancient Name. She defended Natlan through the decade after Mavuika entered the Sacred Flame, married into the Flower-Feather Clan, and passed the torch to Taika once Abyssal wounds had worn down her body. Stories say she could send lightning back into the sky. She is now a comic-book favourite who wore five different masks, and Varesa's mask is modelled on one of them.

## entry: Bifrons and the berryshrooms
@keys: Bifrons, berryshroom, berryshrooms, Sage-Lord of Fungi
@keys2: Collective of Plenty, Teteocan, Varesa
@version: 5.5
@spoiler: minor
### ULTRA
After Varesa's parents planted their orchard, red and green berryshrooms began to grow there. The tribe found them fruity and versatile and shared them around. A visiting Sumeru scholar warned the tribe, without explaining why, to eat any green mushrooms right away, and they simply did. In Mushroom Realm Mystery (5.5), Varesa and the Traveler traced the green mushrooms to Bifrons, an ancient fungus god from Sumeru who had been buried under the volcano by an eruption over 2,000 years ago. He still believed the Archon War was going on. He hoped to spread green mushrooms by heat to regain his power, but the tribe answered with a giant mushroom feast. Varesa took responsibility for keeping an eye on him.
