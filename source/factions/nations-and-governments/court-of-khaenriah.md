## summary
### LITE
The Court of Khaenri'ah was the household of King Irmin, the last Vinster King, made up of his advisers, subordinates and others who served him in the underground kingdom. Its best-known member is the seer Vedrfolnir, called "The Visionary". It is chiefly remembered for the ceremony in which the Traveler's Sibling was named heir.
### STANDARD +
Canon says little about the court as an institution. It is a stub on the wiki, and nearly everything known comes from Dainsleif's memories and from item text about the last years of the Eclipse Dynasty.

## structure
### STANDARD
No ranks, offices or numbers are given. The court sat around a king who ruled with a circle of trusted figures, a Knight Marshal, royal guards and the Universitas Magistrorum, but canon does not say which of these counted as the court itself.
### LARGE +
Dainsleif was a young knight keeping order in the plaza before the palace gate on the day the heir was announced. He was promoted afterwards and given the title "Twilight Sword"; his talent text in A Space and Time Without You calls him the Khaenri'ahn court guard captain.

## leadership
### STANDARD
King Irmin was the only ruler. The glider lore calls him "the great king", and the court acted as his voice. After he was gone, Knight Marshal Anfortas declared himself Regent, according to a damaged bulletin in the ruins at Gavireh Lajavard.

## members
### STANDARD
Vedrfolnir, the seer, is the only member the wiki lists. Rerir described him as "The Visionary", renowned throughout the court for a wisdom beyond ordinary men. He was Dainsleif's elder brother, and Surtalogi said his prophecies were never wrong.
### LARGE +
Others in the king's circle are covered in their own entries. Hroptatyr and Vedrfolnir were among the king's most trusted council. Rerir served Irmin as a secret agent, Pierro tried in vain to win the king's favour, and Anfortas was Knight Marshal. Whether each should count as a member of the court is not settled in canon.

## goals
### STANDARD
Publicly, the court presented itself as a source of power that would bring Khaenri'ah a bright future. Dainsleif says it was loudly proclaimed that, with the new heir, all pain and suffering would be washed away. He now reads that rhetoric as covering the king's lust for power.

## methods
### LARGE
The court's main recorded act is public ceremony and persuasion. The announcement of the heir was a great celebration at the palace, with flowers both real and fake strewn about, and the title "Savior" was coined for the heir by those who believed the rhetoric. Behind the scenes the king used harsher means. When Vedrfolnir warned him that the kingdom would fall if he kept to his path, he had the seer's eyes gouged out and had him imprisoned.

## history
### STANDARD
Late in the Eclipse Dynasty the king named the Traveler's Sibling heir and Prince or Princess of Khaenri'ah. The king and court announced the heir as a bringer of prosperity, and many took up the idea of a "Savior". Soon after, Irmin led the heir into the kingdom's deepest depths and used them as a vessel for limitless Abyssal power. Vedrfolnir, imprisoned for his warning, was freed when Dainsleif and four allies stormed the palace by night. The court did not outlive the fall of the kingdom.
### LARGE +
The loss of his sight also cost the seer his gift of foresight. Wishing to see the future again, Vedrfolnir yielded to the Abyss and shared its power with four of the others (Surtalogi, Rhinedottir, Hroptatyr and Rerir), and they became known as the Five Sinners. Long afterwards a voice and presence of Vedrfolnir is seen again in fragments of memory, including one in which he observes the Traveler and Varka in Nod-Krai (6.4).

## relations
### STANDARD
- **Eclipse Dynasty**: the court served its last king.
- **Five Sinners of Khaenri'ah**: Vedrfolnir, Hroptatyr and Rerir were tied to the king's circle, and the group came together in a raid against the palace.
- **Dainsleif**: a young knight at the heir's announcement, later the court guard captain, who turned against the king.
### LARGE +
- **Universitas Magistrorum**: the king ordered it to research the Abyss.
- **Traveler's Sibling**: the court's chosen heir, on whom its promises rested.

## reputation
### ULTRA
In hindsight the court is remembered as a deceiver. Dainsleif says the "Savior" title stood only for the Vinster King's lust for power. Paimon observes that the people of Khaenri'ah really did believe in the heir, which is why the proclamation weighs so heavily on the Sibling.

## secrets
@spoiler: major
### ULTRA
The court's public show hid the king's turn to forbidden knowledge and the blinding of his own seer. It is not stated whether anyone at court besides the king knew the Sibling would be used as a vessel.

## status
@version: 6.1
### LITE
The court no longer exists. It ended with the cataclysm about 500 years ago.
### STANDARD +
As of 7.1 no court or royal household has been revived. Former associates persist as individuals: Dainsleif the Bough Keeper; Vedrfolnir, one of the Five Sinners, whose consciousness survives in an Abyssal crystal; Rerir, another Sinner, sealed beyond a gate into the moon's reflection in the 6.x Nod-Krai story; and Pierro as the Fatui's Jester. The wiki still marks the entry a stub.

## entry: Proclamation of the heir
@keys: Princess of Khaenri'ah, Prince of Khaenri'ah, heir proclamation, Savior of Khaenri'ah
@keys2: Court of Khaenri'ah, Khaenri'ah, Irmin
@version: 5.7
### STANDARD
When King Irmin named the Traveler's Sibling heir, he and the court proclaimed that the heir would bring a bright future. Dainsleif, then an ordinary young knight keeping order in the plaza, remembers the celebration. The glider lore sets the scene: the heir gazed out at the crowd and realised only then how many people lived underground. The word "Savior" came from those who believed the rhetoric. Dainsleif calls the whole thing a cruel irony in hindsight.

## entry: Vedrfolnir the Visionary
@keys: Vedrfolnir, The Visionary
@keys2: Court of Khaenri'ah, Khaenri'ah, Irmin
@version: 6.1
### STANDARD
Vedrfolnir was the court's seer, famed for prophecies that were never wrong, and Dainsleif's elder brother. He warned King Irmin that Khaenri'ah would fall if the king continued down his path. The king had his eyes gouged out and cast him into a cell, which also took away his sight of the future. Dainsleif's raid freed him, and he later became one of the Five Sinners. See his own entry for his later life.

## entry: Regency of Anfortas
@keys: Regnum Concilium Ultimum, Knight Marshal Anfortas, Regent of Khaenri'ah
@keys2: Khaenri'ah, Irmin
@version: 3.6
### LARGE
A notice in the ruins of Gavireh Lajavard says that, in view of the king's indisposed state and unknown threats, Knight Marshal Anfortas proclaimed he would temporarily take the post of Regent and lead the Regnum Concilium Ultimum until the kingdom returned to normal. The notice is badly worn and its last lines are lost. Kaeya's childhood lessons on Alberich Clan history said that the Alberichs, who lacked royal blood, stepped in as regents when the strength of the one-eyed king Irmin failed; the wiki infers from this that Anfortas was an Alberich, which canon does not state. Canon does not say what became of the rest of the court.
