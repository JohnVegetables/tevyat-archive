## summary
### LITE
The Lawrence Clan was the foremost noble house of Mondstadt's aristocracy. It began as hardy frontier survivors, grew into arrogant rulers and, about a thousand years ago, was deposed by Vennessa's rebellion and driven out of the city. Its descendants still live in Mondstadt, where the name draws hostility. The best-known member today is Eula, who left the clan's ways behind and serves in the Knights of Favonius.
### STANDARD +
The clan's emblem is the Glacial Seal, which stands for a cold, composed, unshaken character. Eula now carries it. The Lawrences are counted among Mondstadt's historic great clans with the Gunnhildrs, Imunlaukrs and, later, the Ragnvindrs, but only the Lawrences are remembered as tyrants.

## history
### STANDARD
**Before Mondstadt.** The clan lived outside Decarabian's city and survived the frozen wilderness as pioneers, which led them to think of themselves as conquerors. **Early aristocracy.** When Barbatos refounded Mondstadt, the clan's leader Venerare had a statue of him raised in the plaza, inscribed with an oath from the leaders of all the clans. In this period the clan's nobles hunted to show their strength and shared the spoils with the people, and royal mages kept the history and taught virtue. **Late aristocracy.** The Lawrences' view of themselves slid from conquerors to overlords to kings. They tore down the statue, the mages abandoned their duties, and the gladiatorial games became bloodsport. A thousand years ago they were effectively rulers of Mondstadt City. Vennessa, an enslaved gladiator, rose against them with Barbatos' help, and the clan was overthrown and forced to leave the city for good.
### LARGE +
**Details of the decline.** The late nobles held that only swords were fit for them and that bows and spears were for commoners and gladiators, abandoning the bow heritage of their hunting ancestors. In the manga, Lord Lawrence took Vennessa's refugee tribe in and then enslaved it, and his son Barca was offended by Vennessa at Ludi Harpastum, which led to her forced entry into the arena. Venti's Character Story adds that Barbatos forged a document selling Mondstadt to Morax, so the clan's soldiers, fearing slavery themselves, switched to Vennessa's side. **The traitor in the ranks.** One Lawrence, later called Kreuzlied, turned on his family and joined the Wanderer's Troupe in its failed attack on the city. He was spared severe punishment because of his birth and later founded an underground society that helped Vennessa. Some exiled nobles in later years came to see that the clan had betrayed the people, not the reverse.
### ULTRA +
**Source caution.** Lord Lawrence and Barca appear only in the manga and are separate from the in-game weapon and artifact lore, which speaks of the clan generally. The in-game novel Hex & Hound names its hero Dietrich as a Lawrence, but it is not clear he was a real person.

## members
### STANDARD
Known members: Venerare (early leader), Lord Lawrence and his son Barca (a thousand years ago, manga), Kreuzlied (disowned), and in the present Eula, her uncle Schubert and her cousin Lessig. Eula is the only playable one.
### LARGE +
Eula carries the Glacial Seal, yet she has chosen to be a Knight of Favonius and is called a traitor by many Lawrences. Schubert, her uncle, is haughty and certain that Mondstadt has fallen into disorder without nobles. Lessig, her cousin, is a young man who once set off on his own to restore the family's honour.

## goals
### STANDARD
The clan's later generations cling to the dream of recovering Mondstadt and its former glory, and they raise children to fit the rule they feel entitled to. Individuals who give up that dream, as Eula has, are rare.

## methods
### STANDARD
Historically the clan ruled by force of arms and by the arena. Today it has no power, so it relies on pride, etiquette and discipline and, in one case, secret alliance with outsiders. Schubert plotted with the Fatui to take Mondstadt back, regardless of the harm to ordinary people.

## culture
### STANDARD
The clan puts great stress on looking noble in the eyes of commoners. Children are taught by the best tutors available in combat, dance, cooking and etiquette, under a regimen most would call abusive. Art is called the clan's "second soul". In battle a Lawrence must appear refined and unshaken, and being knocked down or frozen is thought shameful.
### LARGE +
**Dance.** The Dance of Sacrifice is a noble ritual whose third act, Flickering Candlelight, belongs to the Lawrences. It is a solo passed to the eldest daughter, and bleeding toes were once a point of honour. Because the clan lacked dancers to teach it, it was dropped from the curriculum and is now an artistic pastime. **Food.** Since they refused to trust commoners to cook, they trained to cook for themselves with severe penalties for mistakes. This produced dishes of high quality, such as Eula's modified Stormcrest Pie, now a local favourite as Moon Pie. They also like Gebratenes Fleisch mit Sauerkraut, a very sour dish. **Speech.** Forms of address are strictly graded: "dear friend" is for acquaintances and would estrange someone close, while "good friend" is considered offensive. Commoners who offended a noble were expected to kneel, apologise swiftly and not stare. Teaching these rules to outsiders is forbidden.

## reputation
### STANDARD
Ordinary Mondstadters hold the clan in poor regard and some become aggressive at the mere mention of the name. Descendants are treated as a social threat. Before joining the Knights, Eula said, almost every shop refused her money, so she hunted and cooked for herself. When clan members clashed with locals, the Knights arrived to mediate, with little effect.
### LARGE +
Not every Lawrence fits the stereotype. The wiki notes that about once a generation someone rises above the family's narcissism and earns respect on their own merits. Eula is the example most Mondstadters know.

## relations
### STANDARD
- **Mondstadt Aristocracy**: the Lawrences were its foremost clan.
- **Gunnhildr, Imunlaukr and Ragnvindr Clans**: other great clans. Uncorrupted houses such as the Gunnhildrs stayed after the rebellion, while the Lawrences were driven out.
- **Wanderer's Troupe**: Kreuzlied, a disowned Lawrence, was its last known member. The troupe's attack on the city failed.
- **Knights of Favonius**: the order replaced noble rule. Varka and Jean allowed Eula to join despite the uproar, since her combat skill was too valuable to waste.
- **Fatui**: Schubert called them ruffians but treated them as useful business partners.
### LARGE +
When Eula applied to the Knights, Lawrences confronted her outside the headquarters and then denounced her as a traitor. Common folk also objected to her joining. The Knights accepted her anyway.

## entry: Glacial Seal
@keys: Glacial Seal
### STANDARD
The Glacial Seal is the symbol of the Lawrence Clan. It represents the clan's temper since the early days of Mondstadt: cold and unsullied, undaunted by flame, composed in every circumstance. Its mark appears on weapons and artifacts of the Royal Series and Noblesse Oblige and on Eula's namecard. Eula currently carries it.

## entry: Schubert's Fatui plot
@keys: Schubert, Schubert Lawrence
@keys2: Eula, Lawrence, Fatui
@version: 1.5
### STANDARD
Schubert Lawrence, Eula's uncle, believed present-day Mondstadt had fallen into ruin for lack of noble oversight. In The Spindrift Shall Never Return to the Sea he secretly worked with the Fatui to restore proper rule, indifferent to civilian risk. He is easily flattered, and being praised lowers his guard. The Traveler and Eula foiled the plan, and he was taken into custody. Eula found it disgusting, but also surprising, since she had known the other nobles as people who complained and did nothing.

## entry: Lessig Lawrence
@keys: Lessig
@keys2: Lawrence, Eula, Veluriyam Mirage
@version: 3.8
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
Lessig, Eula's cousin, appeared in the limited-time event Secret Summer Paradise. Having heard of a way to restore the clan's honour, he went to Sumeru through Liyue and found that nobody knew or cared about his name. He lost his wallet, lived rough and ended up at the Veluriyam Mirage, where he tried to fix things and made them worse. When the Traveler, Collei and Eula found him he thought Eula had come to drag him home. Instead he said he wanted a Mondstadt where people did not fear him and decided to return, realising Eula had seen the truth before the rest of the clan. Eula offered to help him tell his parents.

## entry: Venerare
@keys: Venerare
@keys2: Lawrence, statue, oath, Barbatos
@version: 1.0
### STANDARD
Venerare led the Lawrence Clan when new Mondstadt was founded. She guided the people to build the first statue of Barbatos in the plaza, with an oath inscribed by the leaders of all the clans. Her descendants in the late aristocracy tore the statue down, so the oath was lost and does not appear on the rebuilt statue. A book says she helped Leonard reach Pilos Peak, though how the two were related is unclear.

## entry: Kreuzlied
@keys: Kreuzlied
@keys2: Lawrence, Wanderer's Troupe, The Bell
@version: 1.0
### STANDARD
Kreuzlied was born a Lawrence and turned against the clan; he was disowned. He joined the Wanderer's Troupe as its last known member, wielding the greatsword The Bell, and after the troupe's failed uprising was spared severe punishment because of his privileged birth. He then founded an underground society against the aristocracy, which later aided Vennessa. His real name and fate are unknown. Weapon lore calls him a traitor and outcast from the ruling Lawrences.
