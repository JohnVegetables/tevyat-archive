## summary
### LITE
The Five Sinners of Khaenri'ah are five people who were once among Khaenri'ah's most admired, each the best in their field: "The Wise" Hroptatyr, "The Visionary" Vedrfolnir, "Gold" Rhinedottir, "The Foul" Surtalogi and the "Rächer of Solnari" Rerir. About 500 years ago, on the night they joined Dainsleif to storm the royal palace, they could not resist the call of the Abyss. They divided among themselves a world-shattering Abyssal power and became transcendent beings. When the cataclysm came, none of them defended their nation. Dainsleif, who fought beside them that night, has sworn he will never forgive them.
### STANDARD +
They were never a formal order. "Five Sinners" is what others call them, and the name stuck to all five. The power they took was the Abyssal power that King Irmin had the Traveler's sibling absorb. Dainsleif first named all five to the Traveler in Sumeru (4.7).

## members
### STANDARD
- **Hroptatyr, "The Wise"**: President of the Universitas Magistrorum and Chief Royal Mage. He wanted limitless knowledge.
- **Vedrfolnir, "The Visionary"**: a prophet and Dainsleif's older brother. He wanted to see the future again.
- **Rhinedottir, "Gold"**: an alchemist of the Art of Khemia who wanted to create life. She later created Albedo.
- **Surtalogi, "The Foul"**: a knight who wanted supreme strength. He later became Skirk's master.
- **Rerir, "Rächer of Solnari"**: an ordinary intelligence officer. Dainsleif could not understand his fall until Nefer read his past in 6.1.

Dainsleif summed up their motives in 6.1: each was driven by a personal desire, and the Abyss tempted each of them through that desire. Rerir's was Tholindis: after watching his fiancée flee through a moon gate, he broke down and took the power hoping to follow her.
### LARGE +
Before the fall, Hroptatyr and Vedrfolnir were the two councillors King Irmin trusted most. Rhinedottir said Hroptatyr was by far the most favoured of the "Four Pillars of Khaenri'ah". Surtalogi was born in prison to parents condemned to death. He and Dainsleif trained under the knight Haden, and he lost limbs as punishment for following the code of knighthood. Rerir had handled Surtalogi's case file during his sentencing. In secret, Irmin had Rerir hunting down the last descendants of the Crimson Moon Dynasty.

## structure
### STANDARD
The five had no hierarchy, no leader and no shared goal. They came together only once, as allies Dainsleif called on for one night. Afterwards they scattered, and none of them has been shown acting with another since.
### LARGE +
Ties between individuals did survive, though. Vedrfolnir prophesied that Surtalogi would find a disciple out in the universe and bring her to Teyvat. Surtalogi trusted his prophecies and later took Skirk as that disciple. Skirk learned the names of Rhinedottir and Vedrfolnir from Surtalogi, and in 4.2 she said her master and Rhinedottir were both "pursuing some form of perfection". Hroptatyr said Vedrfolnir once told him a parable about two blond heirs to the crown.

## goals
### STANDARD
They shared no goal. What they had in common was that each wanted something beyond human limits: foresight (Vedrfolnir), infinite knowledge (Hroptatyr), the creation of life (Rhinedottir), supreme strength (Surtalogi) and Tholindis (Rerir). Afterwards each went after that desire alone:
- Hroptatyr resolved to leave Teyvat and merge with the Abyss to gain infinite knowledge.
- Surtalogi crossed the sea of stars looking for an opponent who could beat him.
- Rhinedottir went on making life through alchemy.
- Rerir tried to follow Tholindis into the moon gate.

## history
### STANDARD
Late in the Eclipse Dynasty, Vedrfolnir warned King Irmin at court that Khaenri'ah would fall if he kept to his path. Irmin had him blinded in both eyes and thrown into the dungeons. Furious, Dainsleif secretly called together Hroptatyr, Rhinedottir, Surtalogi and Rerir, and that same night they stormed the palace. Dainsleif and Surtalogi fought the guards, Hroptatyr and Rhinedottir disabled the palace's security measures, and Rerir went to free Vedrfolnir. The rescue succeeded. Then the Abyss tempted the five, and each betrayed Dainsleif by taking a share of the Abyssal power held by the Traveler's sibling. Even Vedrfolnir, the brother Dainsleif had come to save, took his share.
### LARGE +
**The cataclysm and after.** None of the five defended Khaenri'ah when the cataclysm came. Their fates afterwards went in different directions:
- **Rhinedottir**: her creations attacked the seven nations, among them the dragon Durin, the beast Elynas, the Rifthounds and the Bakunawa. She later made Albedo and then devoured the Heart of Naberius, taking on the role of Ruler of Life (revealed in 5.6).
- **Surtalogi**: told Teyvat he had lost interest in it and left for the stars.
- **Hroptatyr**: handed his notebook to Eftirvit (Pierro) in the ruins and departed for the sea of stars.
- **Vedrfolnir**: his consciousness was later seen as a chained crystal in the Unknown Sanctuary, empowering Chlothar.
- **Rerir**: entered the moon gate and was torn into fragments that became the Wild Hunt of Nod-Krai.

**Revelation order.** Gold was mentioned in Mondstadt book lore long before the group was named. Vedrfolnir's voice was heard in 3.5. Surtalogi was named as Skirk's master in 4.2. The group was named in 4.7, and the full story of the raid was told in 6.1.
### ULTRA +
**The night in detail (6.1).** Nefer viewed Rerir's memories and saw the night replayed as a game board. At Dainsleif's secret meeting, Rhinedottir needled Hroptatyr about his favour with the king and teased Surtalogi about his missing arm and leg. Surtalogi told a royal guard that the king himself had betrayed the throne. In his cell, Vedrfolnir told Rerir that fate cannot be bargained with. He also told him to persevere if he wanted to stay with Tholindis, and to hurry to the Universitas Magistrorum, where Abyssal monsters were attacking. There Rerir watched Tholindis vanish through a moon gate. Vedrfolnir's voice told him to come to the palace and join them. When Rerir arrived, Rhinedottir greeted him, Dainsleif said "Not you, too...", and Vedrfolnir invited him to step forth and take his share. Rerir took it, and the power began to corrupt his memories of Tholindis. He then used it to reopen the gate.

**Rerir in Nod-Krai (6.0-6.3).** Dottore had gathered Rerir's fragments and revived him in time to strike at Columbina. Rerir served as the main antagonist of the early Nod-Krai Archon Quests. In 6.1 Columbina, strengthened with his own siphoned power, threw him through a gate beyond the sky, and Dainsleif held it open and closed it on his old friend. In 6.3 Columbina met him inside the moon's reflection. The voices in his head were gone, and he regretted what he had done.

**Hroptatyr's notebook (7.1).** The notebook Hroptatyr left with Pierro held forbidden knowledge, his own research and a prism holding the memories he was discarding. Pierro and the other Harbingers sealed it in the Armory Palace in Snezhnaya. When it was opened in 7.1, it showed that Hroptatyr had discovered the "curse of immortality" while trying to save his teacher Mimameith, and had refused to inflict it on her. Pierro also revealed that, when he summoned Paimon from Celestia, Hroptatyr summoned the Traveler's sibling at the same time.

## relations
### STANDARD
- **Dainsleif**: their former ally and the organiser of the raid. He calls them traitors to himself and to their nation and has vowed vengeance. He still remembers Rerir as a friend.
- **King Irmin**: their sovereign. Vedrfolnir and Hroptatyr were his most trusted servants until Vedrfolnir was blinded.
- **The Traveler's sibling**: the vessel of the Abyssal power the five divided.
### LARGE +
- **Pierro (Eftirvit)**: Hroptatyr's old colleague and rival. He blames Hroptatyr for the nation's ruin, and in 7.1 he still said Hroptatyr had betrayed Khaenri'ah and the world.
- **Capitano**: told Ronova in 5.3 that most Khaenri'ahns knew nothing of the Five Sinners' exploitation of Abyssal power, yet all were cursed.
- **Skirk**: Surtalogi's disciple, brought to Teyvat in line with Vedrfolnir's prophecy.
- **Albedo and Durin**: two of Rhinedottir's creations, now in Mondstadt.
- **Abyss Order**: Chlothar turned to worshipping Vedrfolnir after the Sinner empowered him, and Paimon concludes Vedrfolnir inspired the Order's founding.
- **Hexenzirkel**: Rhinedottir is one of its witches (codename "R").
### ULTRA +
- **Haden**: teacher of both Surtalogi and Dainsleif. Haden says Vedrfolnir left Dainsleif a ring that keeps resetting his body's condition, which is why Dainsleif remembers more than other surviving Khaenri'ahns.
- **Skofnungr**: Surtalogi once stole forbidden knowledge to treat him. Before leaving Teyvat, Surtalogi offered to lift his curse of immortality (he declined) and left him a weakened All-Devouring Narwhal.
- **Snezhnaya**: in 7.1 Rhinedottir took back the life authority Vesna held from Naberius, and she met the Tsaritsa at Zapolyarny Palace.
- **Varka**: in 6.4 he and the Traveler were observed by Vedrfolnir through a memory of Roland.

## reputation
### LARGE
Among Khaenri'ahns the five are hated for forsaking their nation in its hour of need. Dainsleif says he will never forget their names, and Pierro, speaking as Eftirvit inside Hroptatyr's memory in 7.1, told him he is "the scum of the earth". Elsewhere they are known mostly through their legacies: Gold's monsters, the Abyss Order and the Wild Hunt. Their power is spoken of with dread. Whole civilisations among the stars have failed to defeat Surtalogi, and Rhinedottir now holds a seat among the Four Shades.

## secrets
### ULTRA
Some things are known to very few. Dainsleif said in 6.1 that he had never spoken openly about the raid before. The identity of the voice that whispered to Rerir, and to Vedrfolnir, before the fall is unrevealed. Vedrfolnir appears to see across time: he spoke to the Traveler through their sibling's memory (3.5) and through Roland's (6.4). After learning who the Sinners were, the Traveler suspected Vedrfolnir also placed the stone slates outlining the Prophecy of Fontaine.

## status
@version: 7.1
### LITE
As of 7.1 they act separately. Rhinedottir is the Ruler of Life, Surtalogi roams the stars, Rerir is trapped in the moon's reflection, and Hroptatyr and Vedrfolnir are unaccounted for.
### STANDARD +
- **Rhinedottir**: appeared in person in Snezhnograd in 7.1. She reclaimed the life authority stolen from Naberius and met the Tsaritsa.
- **Surtalogi**: last seen still seeking a worthy opponent, waiting for Skirk to surpass him.
- **Rerir**: sealed beyond the moon gate since 6.1. When Columbina met him there in 6.3 he was remorseful.
- **Hroptatyr**: in 7.1 only a memory of his younger self was met, preserved in his notebook's prism.
- **Vedrfolnir**: no confirmed whereabouts.

## entry: The night of the palace raid
@keys: palace raid, rescue of Vedrfolnir, storming of the palace
@keys2: Dainsleif, Vedrfolnir, Khaenri'ah, Sinners, Irmin
@version: 6.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
After King Irmin blinded and imprisoned Vedrfolnir for warning that Khaenri'ah would fall, Dainsleif gathered Hroptatyr, Rhinedottir, Surtalogi and Rerir and stormed the palace that same night. Each had a task. Dainsleif and Surtalogi fought the guards, Hroptatyr and Rhinedottir disabled the security measures, and Rerir freed Vedrfolnir. The rescue worked, but the five then gave in to the Abyss and divided among themselves the power Irmin had placed in the Traveler's sibling. Dainsleif was left alone and betrayed. The Wings of Fate's Course Intertwined glider lore calls the raid's "righteous deed" of saving the nation "but a deed done in passing".

## entry: The Sinners' share of Abyssal power
@keys: world-shattering power, transcendent beings, one sixth of the Abyssal power
@keys2: Sinner, Sinners, Khaenri'ah, Abyss
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
Dainsleif calls what the five took "a power that could destroy the world", which turned each of them into a transcendent being. The power came from the Traveler's sibling, whom Irmin had used as a vessel to absorb the Abyss. In her Character Story 5, Skirk recalls Surtalogi mentioning that the group divided enough Abyssal power to overturn a world, "or at least a sixth of it", and asks how he alone is now many times stronger than that. Canon does not say who holds the remaining sixth.

### ULTRA
The recurring challenge event Stygian Onslaught, introduced in 5.7, offers five weapon appearances as rewards. Each one's description says that "legend has it" the weapon was once wielded by one of the Sinners in an ancient land:
- **Hallowed Fetters**, once "Gleipnir": Vedrfolnir's catalyst, power of Time.
- **Ardent Storm**, once "Starkuthr": Hroptatyr's claymore, power of Reason.
- **Serpent Devourer**, once "Logseims": Rhinedottir's sword, power of Life.
- **Shattered Moon**, once "Barnstokkr": Rerir's polearm, power of Death.
- **Starpiercer**, once "Gusisnautar": Surtalogi's bow, power of Void.

In 6.1 Vedrfolnir remarked that the polearm had always been Rerir's weapon of choice.

## entry: Dainsleif's vow against the Sinners
@keys: never have my forgiveness
@keys2: Dainsleif, Five Sinners
@version: 4.7
### STANDARD
In Sumeru (4.7), Dainsleif told the Traveler that the Sinner who had empowered Chlothar was his older brother Vedrfolnir. He then named all five. They had been the hope of their nation, he said, and the six of them together should have stopped the Vinster King. Because none of them defended Khaenri'ah, they will never have his forgiveness, and he vowed vengeance. He contrasted them with the Traveler's sibling, saying the twins could still be reconciled. By 6.1 he was willing to go further: he hired Nefer to uncover Rerir's past and held open the gate that sealed his old friend away.
