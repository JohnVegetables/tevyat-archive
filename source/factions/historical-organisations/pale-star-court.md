## summary
### LITE
The Pale Star Court was the empire and royal court of the Belyi Tsar (White Tsar), whose personal name was Monomakh Snezhnyi. He united the rival Snowland Fae kingdoms of the far north under one crown, and his realm is the direct ancestor of the nation of Snezhnaya. Later texts also use the name for his regime as the first Cryo Archon. It ended when he fell in the Cataclysm about 500 years ago, and Anastasya, the Tsaritsa, took his place.
### STANDARD +
Fae chroniclers call the Tsar "Emperor" and "lord of all fae". The court's memory survives in Nod-Krai and Snezhnaya through books, a palace-tomb, and fae nobles who remember it. It is history rather than a living institution.

## structure
### STANDARD
The court was a fae empire built on clan loyalty. The Tsar sat at the top, with fae Grand Dukes ruling territories under him. The Grand Dukes were clan rulers of peoples such as the Domovoy, and the court had ministers who handled matters like finance. A personal guard, the Druzhna, answered to the Tsar and was mustered in wartime; the Tsaritsa later kept it as her own guard.
### LARGE +
The Snegovik had their own Grand Duchess, who lived at the House of Hesperides, a mountain manor that a receptionist there says was built by the first Snegovik Grand Duchess after the Tsar unified the north. The seat of the Grand Duchy has changed hands several times since, but the house is still kept as it was. The Tsar also set up an institute, Hvergelmir, under his foster daughter Aksinya, which was separate from the fae clans.

## leadership
### LITE
The Belyi Tsar, Monomakh Snezhnyi, founded and ruled the court. He claimed a "sacred bloodline" that set him above other fae, though the fae chronicle suggests the claim was a way to appear legitimate. He later became the first Cryo Archon and God of Love.
### STANDARD +
As a youth he was taken into the House of Hesperides by Yalyane Morevna, who taught him the spear, and he fought with a polearm named Frostbreath during his campaign. In the book's account, the Emperor's reasoning was practical: giving in to Miscislav would only invite worse wars for the next generation. He is shown as merciful too, returning the bodies of fallen enemy dukes to their clans.
### LARGE +
Palace notes written in his own hand show a private man who was torn about Hyperborea, the ancient civilisation he sought on expedition. At first he resented the Hyperboreans and Anacharsis as heretical usurpers and blamed the Voyager for inviting Hyperborea's destruction, while respecting the Heavenly Principles as the world's Great Judge. Later notes show him revising these views once he learned the truth about his own origin. He raised an infant girl, Aksinya, whom he found in a time capsule in the Shadow Realm, and he favoured the Khaenri'ahn exile who called himself Professor Rotwang. These details come from palace notes and artifact texts, not from the fae chronicles.
### ULTRA +
Named officials and nobles include the finance minister Porfiry; Grand Duke Seriviiki of the Cirnareka, who slipped easily into the court's ranks after the war; the fae spy Filipov, who later commanded the Druzhna; and Alvis, Grand Duke of the Domovoy. The Tsar tasked Koshchei, Pavlina, Alvis and Aksinya with seeking the "stairway leading to the vacant seat in the court of the stars".

## members
### STANDARD
The court's people were the Snowland Fae: Domovoy, Snegovik and Snegurochka, Vila, Jack Frost and others, plus beings such as Volkodlak and water imps (the Dalegrad clan) who were once rivals. Humans were later admitted by the Tsar. His closest figures include Aksinya (his foster daughter), Zver (a Chimeric Winged Lion he named), Koshchei, Pavlina and Alvis. The Lantern Fae Flins, who claims to be six or seven hundred years old, is listed as formerly of the Pale Star Court.
### LARGE +
Anastasya was a herald of the Pale Star Court before she became the Tsaritsa. The Tsar once incited her and her lover Saarelainen, the Third Descender, to open a gate in Pohjola, which led to the Heavenly Principles erasing her. In the wiki's account he later explained what had happened to the revived Anastasya, whose memories of the incident had been removed, so the story no longer felt real to her.

## goals
### STANDARD
At first the court's aim was to unite all fae under one banner and secure the frozen north. After the Tsar learned the truth about Hyperborea and his own nature, his private aim became to complete his ascension as a Primordial Human. He established the Hvergelmir institute to build a "Proto Man" and seek the vacant seat in the court of the stars. This purpose was the Tsar's own, not the court's public programme.

## methods
### LARGE
The Tsar waged unification by war, such as the three battles at Kitezh, and tempered it with mercy. After victory he honoured defeated nobles (Seriviiki received the ceremonial tap of a sword). He later "pardoned humanity" and gave humans membership in his realm. In the "pioneering age" he had the borderland of Nod-Krai explored and industrialised. His expeditions into the Shadow Realm and Hyperborea's ruins were a major project: his notes count at least 446 forays.
### ULTRA +
Hvergelmir's researchers adapted Hyperborean energy-storing devices into firearms, and Koshchei's artificial-life experiments produced the Aberrant Chimeric Monsters. Zver, one of his discarded prototypes, was spared by the Tsar because it had some self-awareness.

## history
### STANDARD
**Unification.** The Tsar raised Kitezh, the first city of Snezhnaya, on frozen ground and made it the imperial capital. Seventy-six years later (in the fae calendar), he told the Vila to stop paying tribute to King Miscislav of the Volkodlak. Miscislav accused the court of breaking a standing peace, demanded thirty times the tribute the Vila had paid, and the Emperor refused. Miscislav then attacked Kitezh. In three battles (at Kitezh, in the foothills, at the frozen lake and on the plains) the Tsar killed Miscislav and Grand Dukes Skoldyryt and Vodyanoy with his thrown spear. All remaining fae clans then swore loyalty to him and he took the title Belyi Tsar.
### LARGE +
**The long reign.** Kitezh remained the capital for around a thousand years. Then the Tsar welcomed humans into his realm, moved the seat to Snezhnograd and renamed the nation Snezhnaya. About 2,000 years ago he accepted Celestia's offer to become the Cryo Archon. After the Archon War he attended the first gatherings of the original Seven in Liyue.

**The end.** About 500 years ago, in the Cataclysm, the Tsar led an expedition to Khaenri'ah with Zver, fae and many chimeric monsters. They fought forces serving Chernobog for a week and were routed, and the Tsar was killed. Zver dragged his body home. Anastasya was crowned Tsaritsa of all Snezhnaya.
### ULTRA +
**Transition.** In the first year of the 799th Cycle, Anastasya was crowned. Several fae princes stayed away from Snezhnograd, and the Dalegrad clan broke ties. Port Belovodye's humans declared self-government. In the third year, she suppressed the rebellion and stripped the Drevogorye, Volkhovan and Cirnareka princes of their titles. She did not muster the Druzhna as the Tsar had, but founded the Fatui. She then demanded the remaining princes surrender their authority in return for honorary titles. The fortress of Glupov was built near Snezhnograd in the fourth year, the Snezhnograd Veche was set up in the sixth, and an Imperial Order Reformation Edict followed in the seventh. Many fae then gave up their titles and left Snezhnaya.

**The Tsar's remains.** His corpse stayed biologically active after he died and was laid in the Pale Crown Palace, where it grew into the Tsar's White Birch. During the Traveler's 7.0 world quests, Aksinya moved the Primordial Human's power into the corpse. The Traveler and Paimon, aided by the Eye of Graeae, defeated it, and the body fell to dust.

## relations
### LARGE
- **Tsaritsa and Snezhnaya**: the Pale Star Court is the direct predecessor. Anastasya ended the fae princes' rule and broke up the clan system. Her state keeps the Druzhna as a guard, but the Fatui became its main arm.
- **Volkodlak and Miscislavli**: the main rivals, defeated at Kitezh.
- **Fae today**: the Snowland Fae lost most of their old glory but still influence the nation.
- **Celestia and the Heavenly Principles**: the Tsar accepted their offer to become Archon but later pursued the Hyperboreans' goal of overthrowing them.
- **Hyperborea**: the Tsar sent research expeditions to its ruins in the Shadow Realm.
### ULTRA +
- **Heavenly Principles**: by delving into the Shadow Realm he escaped their punishment, according to the wiki, while Anastasya was erased.
- **Khaenri'ah**: the Tsar's fatal expedition went there, and a Khaenri'ahn exile served at his court.

## reputation
### ULTRA
Fae chronicles present the Tsar as a merciful and brave protector of all fae. Some accounts, including a Traveler voice-over cited on the wiki, say he beat Miscislav alone, which the chronicle itself corrects. Frostmoon Scions know him as Pakkaisukko. A folk tale gives him a twin brother he killed, causing endless winter. The Tsaritsa's own history book describes his conquests as fierce as the harshest winter gale.

## secrets
@spoiler: major
### ULTRA
The Tsar was not a fae. He was an incomplete Primordial Human made from the Cryo Dragon's remains, a leftover of a Hyperborean project, and he believed himself to be a fae. His "sacred bloodline" claim was a way to look legitimate. After becoming Cryo Archon he pursued completing that transformation, the Hyperboreans' original aim, and the Hvergelmir institute supported the effort. Most of this is known only from palace notes and the 7.0 world quests.

## status
@version: 7.0
### LITE
The court no longer exists as a state. It ended with the Belyi Tsar's death roughly 500 years ago.
### STANDARD +
As of 7.1 its ruins and relics remain: the Pale Crown Palace (the Tsar's mausoleum in the White Birch Snowgrave), the House of Hesperides, and the Tsaritsa's Snezhnaya built on its foundations. Long-lived fae such as Flins, listed as formerly of the court, still carry its memory. No further story about the court itself was found for 7.1.

## entry: Battle of Kitezh and King Miscislav
@keys: Miscislav, Miscislavli, Battle of Kitezh, Skoldyryt, Vodyanoy
### STANDARD
King Miscislav, the Blood-Devourer, ruled the Volkodlak and led the Miscislavli alliance. The Vila paid tribute to him until the Tsar's envoys told them to stop. Miscislav then demanded a huge tribute from the Pale Star Court, and the Emperor refused. His host included the Nyholmgarthir (bovine Volkodlak), the Dalegrad (water imps) and the wolf-like Cirnareka. The Tsar threw his spear like an arrow and killed Miscislav, Grand Duke Skoldyryt of the Nyholmgarthir and Grand Duke Vodyanoy of the Dalegrad. Seriviiki of the Cirnareka escaped. Miscislav's skull is said to be kept in the Zapolyarny Palace treasury. A sudden icy fog that misled the Schpekin Legion is recorded by historians, but its cause is unknown.

## entry: Kitezh
@keys: Kitezhgrad, Kitezh
### STANDARD
Kitezh was the first city of Snezhnaya and the Pale Star Court's imperial capital, built in the fae style on frozen ground. It remained the seat of power until the Tsar moved to Snezhnograd. The Abyss later destroyed Kitezhgrad, and Novokitezhgrad was built to replace it under the supervision of Alvis.

## entry: House of Hesperides
@keys: House of Hesperides, Snegovik Grand Duchess
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
A mountain manor in Fellfrost Peak and home of the Snegovik and their Grand Duchess. The receptionist Raduzhnaya says it was built by the first Snegovik Grand Duchess after the Belyi Tsar unified the north and founded the Pale Star Court. It has changed leaders but still looks as it did originally. According to a book on Snezhnayan history, the young Monomakh was taken in here by Yalyane Morevna.

## entry: Hvergelmir institute
@keys: Hvergelmir, Proto Man
@version: 7.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Hvergelmir was a research institute for bio-alchemy and Shadow Realm study, founded by the Belyi Tsar and entrusted to Aksinya. Its goal was to find the stairway to the vacant seat in the court of the stars, using a "Proto Man" as the key to let the Tsar become the Primordial Human. It modified Hyperborean devices into firearms, and Koshchei's work there led to the Aberrant Chimeric Monsters.

## entry: Druzhna
@keys: Druzhna
@keys2: Pale Star, Belyi Tsar, Tsaritsa, Vesna
### STANDARD
The Druzhna were the personal guard that the Tsar mustered in wartime. They are mostly Vila. Filipov, a fae spy and infiltrator who foiled Skoldyryt's plan in the second battle, later commanded them. After her coronation the Tsaritsa chose not to call the Druzhna against rebels and founded the Fatui instead. Today they answer only to her and are also responsible for containing Shadow Realm breaches, under Vesna.

## entry: Tsaritsa's succession
@keys: Anastasya Feodorovna, crowned Tsaritsa, Imperial Order Reformation Edict
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
Anastasya Feodorovna was crowned Tsaritsa of all Snezhnaya in the fourth month of the first year of the 799th Cycle. The fae princes were not all present. In the following years her troops moved against the self-government declared by the humans of Port Belovodye, which accepted her offer of amnesty in return for its instigators, ended hereditary rule in the Drevogorye, Volkhovan and Cirnareka lands, and created the Fatui. The edict and the Veche shifted power from the princes to appointed officials and the Veche, whose first chair, Ioanna Ivanovna, was human.

## entry: Pale Crown Palace and the Tsar's remains
@keys: Pale Crown Palace, Tsar's White Birch, Primordial Human
@version: 7.0
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
The Pale Crown Palace is the Belyi Tsar's mausoleum, once one of his palaces. His preserved corpse grew into the Tsar's White Birch. Notes by a Certain Noble Individual, written in his own voice, were left there describing his visits to Hyperborea's ruins and his ascension. In the quest An Ark That Used to Be a Soul, Aksinya transferred the Primordial Human's power into the corpse, and the Traveler defeated it using the Eye of Graeae.
