## summary
### LITE
The Lochknights were the knights of Fontaine's legends: sworn, in silver armour, to protect the Oceanids (spirits born of pure water), the unpolluted springs and one another, and to resist the golden empire of Remuria. Their leader was Erinnyes. They are remembered less as a documented order than as a tale retold in plays, poems and films, and the Fontinalia Festival commemorates them.
### STANDARD +
Canon itself says their history is "heavily shrouded in legend". Remurians dismissed them as barbarians who worshipped a false god, so most of what survives comes from later romance.
### LARGE +
Read them as a layered subject: a real rebellion against Remuria in its final years, and the many-versioned legend that grew on top of it.

## structure
### STANDARD
The Lochknights were a band of sworn knights around a leader, not a state office. The books name a court of followers: Agricane, Blancandrin, Drest, Guillaume of Gellone (whose sword is Narbonnais), Marsile, Naimes, Otkerus of Rhenanie and Tulpin, with Kudenin of Dunnaer named in NPC dialogue. In the Chanson the knights come from many regions and unite behind Erinnyes. There is no recorded rank system, headquarters or founder.
### LARGE +
In the Chanson, Erinnyes is addressed as "Knight Erinnyes of the Lakelight" and is the beloved leader of the knights. Her sword Hauteclaire is described there as a gift from the maiden Daeira. The knights meet in council: Drest and his companion Adsiltia, Guillaume, Naimes and Tulpin each speak or pledge their swords before Erinnyes answers the envoy of Remus.

## members
### STANDARD
Erinnyes is the one figure tied firmly to the order: High King of Aremorica, a singer who claimed to hear the voice of Egeria, and the unifier of Fontaine's tribes against Remuria. She has her own entry. The other named knights appear only in legend-style sources.
### LARGE +
- Guillaume of Gellone: quick to draw Narbonnais when Remus' envoy threatens Erinnyes, but obeys her order to spare him.
- Drest, Prince of Lyonnais, and Adsiltia: Drest is a bowman who counsels against trusting Remus' promised harmony. Their farewell is regarded in Fontainian culture as a classic romance.
- Otkerus of Rhenanie: praised in the Chanson for his battle with a venom-breathing dragon.
- Others named: Agricane, Blancandrin, Marsile, Naimes, Tulpin and Fierabras (who prepares gifts for the envoy).
### ULTRA +
Kudenin of Dunnaer is the one name on the wiki list that comes from an NPC (Tolfin) rather than the Chanson or Goldoni. The envoy sent to Erinnyes is Cassiodor, a musician of Remus' court from the same tribe as she; his own entry covers him.

## goals
### STANDARD
By the oath, Lochknights protect the Oceanids and the pure springs until "the pure spring flows as before", protect their fellow believers in Egeria, and drive the Remurian "golden usurper" out of Fontaine. Two vows run through every version of the pledge: never draw a sword against those who pledge faith in Egeria, and never compromise in the face of evil. The legends also give the knights a quest for the Pure Grail, a vessel said to grant any wish and wash away sin, and to welcome Egeria back.
### LARGE +
The historical motive behind the quest is the prophecy that all Fontainians would be dissolved by the Primordial Sea for their original sin. Erinnyes sought the Grail to save her people and to free Egeria from her prison. According to her sword's lore, she did not find it.

## methods
### STANDARD
War and oath. Erinnyes united warring tribes by force of arms, then required them to pledge themselves to Egeria, and her army struck at Remurian holdings. Remurian sources call these acts atrocities and call her mad. The legend's version is gentler: courtly councils, tournaments and a quest.
### LARGE +
The order's imagery matters as much as its tactics: silver armour against Remuria's gold, and a feathered helm that opera composers later copied in their hats (Poetry of Days Past).

## history
@version: 4.0
### STANDARD
The Lochknights are placed in the last years of the Remurian Empire, when Remus' rule over Fontaine was being resisted from the northern border, Aremorica. Erinnyes united the tribes and made her stand. When Remuria sank, Egeria was freed and became the new Hydro Archon, and the knights laid down their armour not long afterwards. A few decades after the fall, the idea of the Lochknight died out. An old legend says Egeria gave a Lakelight Lily to the last Lochknight as a farewell present.
### LARGE +
In the Chanson, the envoy of Remus demands water and earth as tribute and threatens war, hunger and plague. Erinnyes refuses to bend the knee to any ruler but Egeria, and the knights back her. Later, Erinnyes bids Egeria farewell and throws her sword into a lake three times; the third time it sinks, and she departs with a spirit. The wiki notes this romanticises her exile. According to her weapon lore, she met Egeria after Remuria's fall, learned that Egeria herself had created humans from the Oceanids (the "original sin"), threw down her sword and was never seen again.
### ULTRA +
The wiki adds that some descendants of the Lochknights joined the Marechaussee Hunters, the heroes gathered by the Golden Hunter to wipe out the monsters threatening Fontaine (citing Clorinde's Character Story 3). The Chanson also has Erinnyes entrust the surviving knights to the noble musician, who is to lead them against "lingering shadows"; the book is a romance, so treat this as legend.

## relations
### STANDARD
- **Oceanids and Egeria**: the order's reason for being. The knights serve the "Mistress of Many Waters", and the Oceanids are the spirits born of pure water.
- **Remuria**: the enemy. Remurians, such as the musician Goldoni speaking for Boethius' regime, called Lochknight tales ridiculous ravings.
- **Marechaussee Hunters**: later monster-hunters; some of their members descend from Lochknights.
### LARGE +
- **Fontinalia Festival**: established to commemorate the Lochknights' quest (see the entry below).
- **Song of Days Past artifacts**: the set's flavour text is the main in-game portrayal of the vows and the helm.
- **Weapon materials**: the Silver Goblet of the Pristine Sea text carries the oath of silver armour and the later shedding of armour.

## reputation
### STANDARD
In modern Fontaine the Lochknights are beloved literary heroes. Their adventures are told in plays, poetry and films, such as the historical film "Lochknight of the Lance" (seen in a limited-time event) and a play about a mounted python and the Pure Grail. Remurian-era views were hostile: the Empire called them barbarians, and their tales fictions told by slaves.
### LARGE +
The ballads and the real woman are not the same thing. An artifact text insists the hero of the ballads has nothing to do with the real Erinnyes, and the modern stories are "dreams of the present".

## status
### LITE
Defunct for centuries, existing now only as legend and festival.
### STANDARD +
No Lochknight story development was found for 6.x or 7.0-7.1. The name still appears in item text, the Fontinalia Festival and Remuria-related lore.

## entry: Fontinalia Festival
@keys: Fontinalia, Fontinalia Festival, Fontinalia Film Festival
@version: 4.0
### STANDARD
The Fontinalia Festival commemorates the Lochknights' search for the Oceanids and the return of Egeria. Traditionally, people dress as the knights, raise golden cups and go from door to door asking for pure water. In recent years Furina turned it into something closer to a carnival, trading the pure-water custom for candy, and children now call "Trial or treat!" at each house. A Fontinalia Film Festival, part of the limited-time event Roses and Muskets, was added by the Fontaine Film Association.

## entry: Lakelight Lily
@keys: Lakelight Lily, Lakelight Lilies
@version: 4.2
### STANDARD
The Lakelight Lily is a pale-blue flower that grows where Water Veins meet, notably in Erinnyes Forest. Its meaning in the language of flowers is "waiting" and "everlasting promise". The legend that Egeria gave a lily of this kind to the last Lochknight as a farewell is the origin of a living custom: Fontainians pick a lily for those starting a long journey, wishing them a road as calm as pure water and a safe return.

## entry: The Lochknight oath
@keys: Lochknight oath, oath of the Lochknights, Pure Grail
@version: 4.3
### LARGE
Versions of the pledge differ across poems and plays. The artifact text gives two constants. First, never draw a sword on those who pledge faith in Egeria. Second, never compromise in the face of evil (also rendered as tolerating not even a trace of filth). One recorded wording swears by the ever-blooming silver flower to drive the golden usurper from the high sea and to protect the spirits born of pure water until the spring flows again. The Pure Grail, the object of the quest, is a vessel in the legends that can grant any wish and wash away any sin.

## entry: La Chanson d'Erinnyes
@keys: La Chanson d'Erinnyes, Chanson d'Erinnyes, Hauteclaire
@version: 4.0
@spoiler: minor
### LARGE
This three-volume in-game book is a verse romance about Erinnyes. Volume 1 shows Remus' emissary demanding the gifts of Aremori Castle's water and earth, while Volume 2 shows the knights of every region uniting behind Erinnyes. In Volume 3 she bids farewell to Egeria and casts her sword Hauteclaire into the lake three times. It is a romantic retelling, and the wiki points out that its picture of her exile differs from what her weapon lore says really happened.
