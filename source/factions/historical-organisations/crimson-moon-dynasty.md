## summary
### LITE
The Crimson Moon Dynasty is the earlier of the two known ruling dynasties of Khaenri'ah, the underground kingdom that fell in the cataclysm. It takes its name from the crimson moon that once shone over the realm. The dynasty is long gone, but some descendants survive and carry its Balemoon Bloodfire, most notably Arlecchino.
### STANDARD +
Almost everything known about its age comes from the legend of Perinheri, which cannot be verified. In Khaenri'ah's last years the later Eclipse Dynasty hunted its remaining descendants.

## structure
### STANDARD
Canon names no ruler, court or lineage list for this dynasty. The legend of Perinheri shows a single 'great ruler' who answers the counsel of sages and lets them found an orphanage for children who may have come from beyond the world. In that era alchemy and the Beastmaster Knights were at the height of their influence. The legend also mentions the 'Darksprites', the first black-wolf warbeasts, created through alchemy.
### LARGE +
Not all descendants stayed close to the main line. Tholindis belonged to a sub-branch whose family was only distantly related and had cut ties long before. Canon notes that a Crimson Moon descendant does not always show any sign of it. Tholindis had ordinary purple eyes and Frithr's eyes show no sign of the bloodline either.

## members
### LITE
No member of the dynasty's ruling house is named. Known descendants are Arlecchino and, in Khaenri'ah's last years, Tholindis, Frithr and Orlog.
### STANDARD +
- **Arlecchino**: the best-known living descendant, the Knave, a Fatui Harbinger and head of the House of the Hearth. She did not know of her heritage until she met Pierro. The Balemoon Bloodfire runs in her blood, and she uses it carefully.
- **Tholindis**: from a distant sub-branch, a Moon Marrow researcher at the Universitas Magistrorum, engaged to Rerir without knowing his secret mission.
- **Frithr** and **Orlog**: other descendants hunted in the last years of Khaenri'ah. Orlog was arrested and probably killed; Frithr was spared by Rerir.
### LARGE +
- **Rerir**: not a descendant but an agent of the Eclipse Dynasty (the Rächer of Solnari), ordered to kill the remaining Crimson Moon line.

## goals
### STANDARD
As a dynasty it has no known goals now. The Perinheri legend says the founding of its orphanage aimed to catch a child from beyond the world who could transcend the gods. Surviving descendants are mainly concerned with staying alive and, in the Khaenri'ah period, hiding their ancestry to avoid persecution.

## methods
### LARGE
The dynasty's era is remembered for alchemy and warbeasts. The Narzissenkreuz Ordo's archaeological records say these were at their peak then. The Universitas Magistrorum later ran a Moon Marrow Lab, studying the energy left by the crimson moon's remains, as a possible new energy source for Khaenri'ah.

## history
### STANDARD
**The age of the crimson moon.** In the legend, the underground kingdom was lit by a crimson moon rather than the later dark sun. Sages persuaded the ruler to establish an orphanage for children said to come from beyond the sea of stars. Perinheri, an outlander, was raised there.

**The change of dynasty.** The legend says the crimson moon set and the dark sun descended, replacing one dynasty with the other. The way this happened is not told. The Crimson Moon era ended before the events of Perinheri (II), set during the Archon War.
### LARGE +
**Purge.** Long after the dynasty fell, the last ruler of the Eclipse Dynasty, King Irmin, wanted the secrets the Crimson Moon had left and sent his agent Rerir, the Rächer of Solnari, to find and kill its few remaining followers. Irmin's purge deepened as his madness grew. Tholindis kept her own ancestry secret and quietly helped other descendants to escape. She entrusted Frithr with a piece of the Crimson Moon so the bloodline would not fade out. Rerir, losing faith in the king, spared Frithr and told her to keep hidden.
### ULTRA +
**The moon's reflection.** Tholindis discovered that Moon Marrow could open a gateway to a reflection of the moon in which time runs backwards. When the cataclysm hit the Universitas, she jumped through it and died, and her soul drifted there for 500 years until Columbina found it. These events were revealed in the Nod-Krai Archon Quest (6.1) and should be treated as spoilers.

## relations
### LARGE
- **Eclipse Dynasty**: the dynasty that followed it and later hunted its descendants. Dainsleif's official introduction alludes to both dynasties with the line that the eclipse is swallowed by the crimson moon; the original phrasing implies revenge.
- **House of the Hearth and Fatui**: Pierro implies that the original orphanage is the House of the Hearth, whose later link to the Fatui he may be responsible for.
- **Universitas Magistrorum**: housed the Moon Marrow research and employed Tholindis.
### ULTRA +
- **Three Moons**: before the Four Great Rulers, the authorities of Life, Death, Time and Space belonged to the Three Moons, per Pierro (7.1). The Crimson Moon took the authority of death. After the moon goddesses were destroyed, its broken remains merged with the Abyss.

## reputation
### ULTRA
In Khaenri'ah's last years, descendants were persecuted and feared. Today the name is nearly forgotten outside a few people such as Arlecchino and Pierro.

## secrets
@spoiler: major
### ULTRA
Arlecchino's descent from the dynasty, and the use of her Balemoon Bloodfire as the basis of an experiment with Delusions, are known only to Pierro and a few others. The Abyssal energy in her blood corrodes her, and if the corruption reached her heart she would become someone entirely different.

## status
@version: 7.1
### LITE
As of 7.1 the dynasty is long extinct as a ruling house. Its descendants include Arlecchino.
### STANDARD +
In A Rekviem for the Underworld (7.1), Pierro starts a project with Arlecchino, using her status as a Crimson Moon descendant and the Crimson Moon's authority over death, to create new Delusions to oppose Ronova. The test at the Snezhnayan facility was only mildly successful. In return for her help, Arlecchino has the House of the Hearth stop working for the Fatui and end its training of children as spies.

## entry: Perinheri
@keys: Perinheri, Hleobranto Innamorato, Hierobranto Innamorato
### STANDARD
Perinheri is a two-volume in-world book, also called Hleobranto Innamorato, sold in Fontaine. It claims to retell an old Khaenri'ahn legend that nobody can confirm and was written over generations. Its first volume sets the story in the Crimson Moon era and describes the orphanage for children from beyond the world. Its second volume has the knights Perinheri and Hleobrant, and Angelica, a woman who claims to be a noble princess from a defeated land. The volume names the four mightiest figures in the kingdom: Njord, Alf of the Universitas Magistrorum, Alberich, and Perinheri.

## entry: Balemoon Bloodfire
@keys: Balemoon Bloodfire, Balemoon
@keys2: Arlecchino, Crimson Moon
@version: 4.6
### LARGE
The Balemoon Bloodfire is the ancient flame in the blood of Crimson Moon descendants. In Arlecchino it is both a curse and a source of strength. It uses Abyssal energy and slowly corrupts the user. She learned that if the corruption reaches the heart, the user becomes a different person, and she uses it with great caution.

## entry: Tholindis and Rerir
@keys: Tholindis, Rächer of Solnari
@version: 6.1
@spoiler: major
### ULTRA
Tholindis, a young researcher in the Universitas Magistrorum's Moon Marrow Lab, was a distant Crimson Moon descendant and secretly aided others of the bloodline. Her fiancé Rerir was King Irmin's agent hunting that very bloodline; he spared Frithr and, in the end, could not bring himself to act against Tholindis. When the cataclysm began she died in the moon's reflection, and Rerir became one of the Five Sinners of Khaenri'ah.
