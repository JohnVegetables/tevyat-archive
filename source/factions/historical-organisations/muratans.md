## summary
### LITE
The Muratans were Natlanese exiles from the seventh tribe of the old Grand Alliance. After the civil war that ended Och-Kan's rule, one group of the defeated tribe accepted exile and wandered the land for generations before settling in Mondstadt. There they eventually forgot their heritage and the name Muratan. The best-known Muratan is Vennessa, the gladiator who led the rebellion against Mondstadt's aristocracy.
### STANDARD +
Their sister group, who refused to leave Natlan, became the Tenochtzitoc.

## structure
### STANDARD
No Muratan government or leadership is described. They were a wandering people, and Vennessa's story shows them as a clan led by elders and families. Their knowledge passed down through the generations was mostly combat and survival technique rather than history.

## members
### STANDARD
Historical figures tied to the Muratans:
- Vennessa: a "flame-touched Muratan", slave warrior, and later first Grand Master of the Knights of Favonius.
- Lind: Vennessa's younger sister (named in the manga; relation per the wiki).
- Tenoch: a warrior of the Muratan tribe who was exiled from it before the Cataclysm and who later gathered heroes against the Abyss.
### LARGE +
Vennessa inherited the Muratan "fiery-red" vermilion hair, according to the wiki's description of her.

## goals
### STANDARD
The Mondstadt Muratans wanted food, safety and freedom. Vennessa fought in the Colosseum to win her people's freedom. The Tenochtzitoc branch refused to abandon their homeland.

## history
### STANDARD
Before the civil war, the Muratans were the seventh Natlanese tribe in the Grand Alliance. After the Alliance's collapse, the tribe split. One group accepted exile and wandered for generations before settling in Mondstadt. The other refused to leave and was eventually allowed to settle far from the other tribes, at Tenochtzitoc, now the Easybreeze Holiday Resort.
### LARGE +
**Mondstadt.** By Vennessa's time, around a thousand years ago, the Muratans were enslaved by Mondstadt's nobles. According to the manga, Ursa the Drake hounded her clan for four days, starving them, and they fled into the city only to be enslaved. Vennessa fought in the Colosseum, and with Barbatos, Ragnvindr and others overthrew the aristocracy. The descendants of the Muratans had by then forgotten the word.

**Tenochtzitoc.** The Tenochtzitoc were Anemo warriors, who lived in neglect for millennia. When the Cataclysm came, the Bakunawa killed all of them except Tenoch, who had been exiled from his tribe earlier. Tenoch died as a sacrifice in Sanhaj's border spell against the Bakunawa in the Mare Jivari.
### ULTRA +
**What Enjou said.** In The Last Survivor of Tenochtzitoc (5.8), Enjou told the Traveler the exiles' story as a tale of a kingdom whose wise king died, whose follower succeeded him and was corrupted, and whose civil war ended with the wicked king slain and his people exiled. He named the Mondstadt group as "Muratans" and added that it was a story for another day.

## relations
### STANDARD
- **Grand Alliance**: the Muratans belonged to its seventh tribe.
- **Och-Kan**: the wiki places the tribe on his side in the civil war.
- **Mondstadt aristocracy**: enslaved them, and was overthrown by Vennessa.
- **Knights of Favonius**: founded by Vennessa, a Muratan.
- **Tenochtzitoc**: the sister group who stayed in Natlan.

## reputation
### LARGE
Natlan's Easybreeze Holiday Resort stages actors playing Tenochtzitoc tribe members as part of its themed attractions, according to Pucli in the quest. Mondstadt remembers Vennessa as a hero of freedom, not as a Muratan.

## status

### LITE
No Muratan community is known to survive under that name as of 7.1. Their legacy lives on in Mondstadt's Knights of Favonius and its ideal of freedom.

## entry: Tenochtzitoc
@keys: Tenochtzitoc, Tenoyollotzin
@keys2: Muratan, Tenoch, Bakunawa
@version: 5.8
### STANDARD
Tenochtzitoc was the settlement of the Muratans who refused to leave Natlan, set in a remote, barren part of the nation where the Easybreeze Holiday Resort now stands. Its people were warriors who wielded Anemo and were neglected for millennia. When the Cataclysm arrived, they were the first to fight and fell one by one. Only Tenoch, who had been exiled earlier, survived. Enjou calls the place where their bones were buried the Windless Land.

## entry: Vennessa as a Muratan
@keys: Vennessa, flame-touched Muratan
@keys2: Muratan, Natlan, Colosseum
### STANDARD
Vennessa is called a "flame-touched Muratan", a gladiator enslaved by Mondstadt's nobles. The wiki notes that by her time the Muratans in Mondstadt had forgotten their heritage and the term Muratan. Her own entry holds her story as the first Grand Master and Dandelion Knight.
