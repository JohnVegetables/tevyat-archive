## summary
### LITE
The Imunlaukr Clan was one of the founding clans of old Mondstadt, alongside the Gunnhildr and Lawrence clans. It descended from Imunlaukr, an outlander warrior, and became known for gifted fighters who fought hard and died young. In the dark age of the old aristocracy it turned tyrannical. Its bloodline is now gone.
### STANDARD +
The clan is known almost entirely through weapon lore and two remarks from Dornman Port townsfolk. It is not mentioned in present-day Mondstadt, unlike the Gunnhildr and Lawrence clans.

## members
### LITE
No individual clan member is named apart from the ancestor Imunlaukr, who gave the clan its name, and an unnamed "tyrant of Imunlaukr".
### STANDARD +
Imunlaukr himself was an outlander who lived in Sal Vindagnyr, the lost city on Dragonspine, and survived its destruction because he had left to seek a way to save it. His own entry covers his story. In Dornman Port, Irmgard, a Dornmannshaven descendant, boasts that her family once outwitted the "tyrant of Imunlaukr" in trade. That is the only reference to a clan leader of the aristocratic era.

## goals
### STANDARD
Weapon lore says the clan inherited its founder's outlook: combat was an entertainment for the gods watching from above, not a means of protection, glory or territory. Its warriors fought monsters and outlaws alike, with no thought of whether they would return home, because roaring in the heat of battle counted as duty fulfilled. Such a life had no end and no hope of victory. When Barbatos swept away the snow and Mondstadt took shape, the clan came to feel it had found something worth fighting for.
### LARGE +
This is the clan's outlook as the Sacrificial Greatsword's text tells it. The text does not say what happened to that new purpose once the aristocracy turned tyrannical.

## methods
### STANDARD
In the early period, when Mondstadt's people worshipped the gods of Anemo and Time together, the clan acted out war tales with prop weapons. They also staged duels between warriors stained black with blood as part of sacrificial ceremonies. The Sacrificial Greatsword was one such prop; the text says it began as a stage weapon and was hardened by the passage of time. A separate text on the Royal Greatsword says gladiatorial combat in Mondstadt began as a religious ceremony alongside prayers to the wind gods, and the wiki infers that the clan's culture inspired it. Later the practice was twisted into a sport for the pleasure of the Lawrence Clan, and the Knights of Favonius outlawed it.

## history
### LITE
The clan came from Imunlaukr, whose descendants settled in what became Mondstadt. By the time Decarabian's rule ended it was known for brave, short-lived warriors. In the aristocratic age it grew tyrannical, and the Gunnhildr Clan broke with it. Today its line has died out.
### STANDARD +
**Origin.** Imunlaukr returned empty-handed to Sal Vindagnyr and found everyone dead. He decided that Celestia only watched the people below suffer, and went in search of a war-torn land to entertain the gods. He ruined three thriving kingdoms until the survivors hunted him as a sworn enemy. At that point he gave up the life of a warrior for good. His fate after this is not stated, but his descendants formed the clan.

**Decarabian's era and after.** By the time Decarabian's Mondstadt fell, the clan was known for gifted fighters who died young. After Barbatos cleared the snow and made Mondstadt a land of breezes and green pastures, they believed they had found a cause.
### LARGE +
**The dark age.** Under the old aristocracy the clan became more and more tyrannical. The Gunnhildr Clan, then settled at Dornman Port, made a total break with it. According to Hannelore, the priest there, the Gunnhildrs of that time seized a bishop sent from Mondstadt, threw him from the high window of the port's sanctuary tower, and tore the sanctuary down. The Church had by then become a puppet of the aristocracy, and the Church of Favonius regards the act as true piety. The sanctuary was never rebuilt, and the place remains roofless. Hannelore's account names the Imunlaukr as the tyrannical clan being cut off.

**Afterwards.** After Vennessa's uprising, the Gunnhildrs pushed to abolish all hereditary noble fiefdoms. Hannelore says the once-mighty Imunlaukr line has since vanished and its accomplices have lost their former prominence.

## relations
### STANDARD
- **Gunnhildr Clan**: broke with the Imunlaukr over their tyranny, famously by the bishop incident at Dornman Port. The Gunnhildrs later stayed in Mondstadt as the oldest knightly house.
- **Lawrence Clan**: the third founding clan named alongside the Imunlaukr. The Lawrences are the aristocrats later associated with perverting gladiatorial combat into a sport.
- **Knights of Favonius**: founded after the uprising that ended the aristocracy, and outlawed the gladiatorial combat that the clan's culture seems to have inspired.
### LARGE +
- **Dornmannshaven family**: present-day merchants at Dornman Port who recall outwitting a tyrant of Imunlaukr in trade.
- **Sal Vindagnyr**: the clan's founder came from that city. His story is told separately by weapon lore and in the Dragonspine murals, and it ties the clan to the lost city only through his ancestry.

## reputation
### ULTRA
In the sources, the clan has two faces. Its own weapon lore honours its warriors as brave and gifted, while the Dornman Port accounts treat it as a tyrannical house of the old aristocracy that good people cut ties with. In-world, the clan is now remembered mostly as a cautionary example of how power fades; Hannelore ends that remark by saying time erodes all mighty powers and only the wind keeps blowing, a blessing from Barbatos.

## status
@version: 6.5
### LITE
Extinct. The bloodline has vanished, and the clan is no longer mentioned in present-day Mondstadt.
### STANDARD +
As of 7.1 nothing new has appeared about the clan. Its legacy survives only in old weapons and in remarks from Dornman Port locals.

## entry: Imunlaukr Clan weapons
@keys: Imunlaukr Clan weapons, Sacrificial Greatsword, Royal Greatsword
@keys2: Imunlaukr, Mondstadt, clan
### LARGE
Several weapons carry the clan's story. The Sacrificial Greatsword, once a ceremonial prop for war tales that hardened over time, belonged to the clan and tells of its warriors and their sacrificial duels. The Royal Greatsword belonged to nobles who once ruled Mondstadt and records how gladiatorial combat began as a religious rite. Snow-Tombed Starsilver and Disaster and Remorse belong to the story of the founder Imunlaukr rather than the later clan.

## entry: Dornman Port and the break with the Imunlaukr
@keys: Dornman Port sanctuary, roofless sanctuary, tyrannical Imunlaukr
@keys2: Gunnhildr, Hannelore, bishop
@version: 6.5
### LARGE
At Dornman Port, Hannelore tells that the port was a Gunnhildr fief long before Barbatos ended Decarabian's rule, and that Mondstadt's first sacred sanctuary stood there. When the Church became the aristocracy's puppet, the Gunnhildrs of the age ended their ties with the Imunlaukr by hurling a visiting bishop from the sanctuary tower and demolishing the building. The Church of Favonius sees this as true piety. The sanctuary was never rebuilt.
