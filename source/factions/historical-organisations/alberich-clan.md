## summary
### LITE
The Alberich Clan is an old, pure-blooded noble family of Khaenri'ah, the sunken kingdom destroyed in the cataclysm 500 years ago. It was known for producing leaders of the kingdom's knights. It is not royal, but members stepped in as regents when King Irmin failed. Its present-day line includes Kaeya, the Cavalry Captain of the Knights of Favonius, who was raised in Mondstadt as Kaeya Alberich.
### STANDARD +
The clan existed at least as far back as the Eclipse Dynasty. It also produced Chlothar, who founded the Abyss Order, so the same family name connects the kingdom's last defenders to the Abyss Order, which Kaeya counts as an enemy. The clan survived the kingdom's fall in secret and still keeps no written records.

## structure
### STANDARD
The Alberichs were nobles of Khaenri'ah's pure-blooded aristocracy, held to strict standards of conduct and given access to institutions such as the Royal Library. They had no royal blood. Canon names no head of the clan and gives no internal ranks. In the present day the clan keeps no written records of its affairs, a rule Kaeya's childhood scrap of clan history was saved in defiance of.
### LARGE +
The family was not open to outsiders. Chlothar's relationship with a commoner woman from Mondstadt was not accepted by other members of the clan, which left him unable to protect her or their son.

## members
### LITE
Anfortas, the Knight Marshal and regent; Chlothar, founder of the Abyss Order; Kaeya, the present-day Cavalry Captain of the Knights of Favonius.
### STANDARD +
- **Alberich** (name only): in the book Perinheri, commander of half of Khaenri'ah's knights, counted among its four strongest. Whether this is the clan's founder or a later member is not stated.
- **Anfortas**: Knight Marshal and leader of the Schwanenritter, who declared himself Marshal Regent when Irmin could not rule. Canon never says outright that he was an Alberich; the inference is strongly supported.
- **Chlothar**: a young noble who resented his predestined life. He fathered Caribert with a woman from Mondstadt, was cursed with immortality in the cataclysm, and later founded the Abyss Order.
- **Kaeya**: a descendant of Chlothar by Dainsleif's account, left in Mondstadt as a child by his unnamed father.
### LARGE +
- **Caribert**: Chlothar's illegitimate son, who was turned into a hilichurl by the curse and later vanished wrapped in Abyssal power. He was not pure-blooded.
- **Kaeya's father**: unnamed. He left Kaeya in Mondstadt as an agent of Khaenri'ah, calling him 'our last hope'.

## goals
### STANDARD
The present-day clan's aims are not explained. The clan history Kaeya was taught says the Alberichs could not restore Khaenri'ah to life and should live as those who blaze like fire rather than wallow in embers. Kaeya's father sent him to Mondstadt as an agent of Khaenri'ah, and Kaeya long feared he would have to choose a side if Khaenri'ah and Mondstadt fought. He now says he will not carry out that duty and treats the Abyss Order as an enemy.

## history
### STANDARD
**Before the cataclysm.** The clan stood at the heart of Khaenri'ah's military. Perinheri names an Alberich as commander of half the knights, which shows the name was established by the time of the Eclipse Dynasty. The legend itself cannot be verified.

**The cataclysm.** When King Irmin was 'indisposed' and dark beasts attacked, Anfortas took over as regent. He and the Schwanenritter chose to lead survivors out of the kingdom to the surface, using old Ruin Golems against the beasts. The clan's own history says the Alberichs, who had no royal blood, ruled as regents when Irmin's strength failed.
### LARGE +
**Chlothar and the Abyss Order.** The gods cursed Khaenri'ah's people. Chlothar, being pure-blooded, was made immortal. His son Caribert was turned into a hilichurl. Chlothar went to Sumeru, worked with the Traveler's sibling on a medicine to restore his son, and found Abyssal power from Vedrfolnir. The medicine worked for a time, then Caribert shed his mask and vanished in Abyssal energy. Chlothar came to see the Abyss, and the 'sinner' Vedrfolnir, as salvation. He founded the Abyss Order, and the sibling became its Prince or Princess. Roughly a hundred years after its founding he went mad and seemingly disappeared.
### ULTRA +
**Aftermath.** In the present day the Traveler and Dainsleif dug up a field and found two skeletons, a man and a woman. The Traveler took the man's to be Chlothar's, and it was interred much later than hers; Dainsleif mused that Chlothar must have found a way to escape his curse. The woman is suggested, not stated, to be Caribert's mother. Anfortas is presumed to have died in the Dharma Forest as the last of the Schwanenritter. The clan itself carried on through the centuries out of sight.

## relations
### LARGE
- **Abyss Order**: founded by Chlothar. Dainsleif calls Kaeya a descendant of the order's founder, which Kaeya says confirmed old suspicions about why his father left him in Mondstadt. He now treats the order as an enemy.
- **Eclipse and Crimson Moon Dynasties**: the clan existed during the Eclipse Dynasty and says it was never royal. Nothing links it to the Crimson Moon Dynasty's bloodline.
- **Ragnvindr Clan**: Crepus Ragnvindr adopted Kaeya, which is why he lives as Kaeya Alberich of the Ragnvindr household. Diluc is his adoptive brother.
### ULTRA +
- **Dainsleif**: identified Kaeya's heritage. Mona and Dainsleif both doubt Kaeya can stay distant from his past forever.

## reputation
### ULTRA
In Mondstadt the name Alberich appears openly only as Kaeya's own surname; Kaeya is trusted as Cavalry Captain by Jean. The clan history Kaeya was taught stresses Anfortas's regency, while Chlothar's line leads to the founding of the Abyss Order, a mixed legacy.

## secrets
@spoiler: major
### ULTRA
Kaeya's Khaenri'ahn ancestry is something he does not discuss; he avoids talking about his past, even with the Grand Master. His father's mission and the clan's present intent are hidden. The clan's own rule against recording its affairs means little documentation survives. Kaeya's saved sheet about the clan's regent role is kept in a hidden compartment on top of the Knights of Favonius headquarters, found during the limited-time event Hidden Strife.

## status

### LITE
As of 7.1 the Alberich Clan exists only in the shadows. Its only publicly known member is Kaeya, who lives in Mondstadt and does not serve the clan's agenda.
### STANDARD +
No new information about the clan was found in the 6.x Nod-Krai or 7.0-7.1 Snezhnaya story content that was checked. Its history is known from the Caribert quest (3.5), the Hidden Strife event (2.8), Kaeya's character stories and the in-world book Perinheri.

## entry: Anfortas and the Schwanenritter
@keys: Anfortas, Marshal Regent, Knight Marshal, Regnum Concilium Ultimum
### STANDARD
Anfortas was Khaenri'ah's Knight Marshal and leader of the Schwanenritter. When Irmin became unable to rule and dark beasts attacked, he declared himself Marshal Regent and led the Final Kingdom Council. The Schwanenritter decided to evacuate able survivors to the surface, into Sumeru, and used Ruin Golems to hold the beasts off. He is presumed to have died in the Dharma Forest as the last of his order. Whether he was an Alberich is inferred, not stated.

## entry: Chlothar and Caribert
@keys: Chlothar, Eide, Caribert
@version: 3.5
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Chlothar Alberich, who first introduces himself to the Traveler's sibling as Eide, was a pure-blooded noble who loved a woman from Mondstadt. They had a son, Caribert. When the cataclysm came, Chlothar was cursed with immortality, and Caribert, not pure-blooded, became a hilichurl. Chlothar's search for a cure led to the Abyss and eventually to founding the Abyss Order. Caribert, whom Chlothar later called the 'Loom of Fate', disappeared. Chlothar went mad about a century after founding the Abyss Order and seemingly disappeared; a later burial that the Traveler took to be his suggests he escaped his curse before dying.

## entry: Kaeya's clan history
@keys: Alberich Clan history, Alberich regents, blaze like fire
@keys2: Kaeya, Hidden Strife, letter
@version: 2.8
@availability: limited
### LARGE
During the limited-time event Hidden Strife (2.8), a hidden compartment behind a brick on top of the Knights of Favonius headquarters held a half-burned sheet in a child's and then an adult's handwriting. It reads that the Alberich Clan, who had no royal blood, stepped in as regents when one-eyed King Irmin's strength failed, and that, unable to revive Khaenri'ah, the clan should live as those who blaze like fire rather than wallow in the embers. An annotation on the back, implied to be Kaeya's, says he saved it from the fire 'Father' made while he was not looking, against the clan's principle that its affairs should never be recorded. This is the main source for the clan's own account of itself.
