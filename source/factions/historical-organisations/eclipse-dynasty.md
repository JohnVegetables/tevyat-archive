## summary
### LITE
The Eclipse Dynasty was the second and last known ruling dynasty of Khaenri'ah, the underground kingdom destroyed in the cataclysm 500 years ago. A dark sun stood as its emblem, replacing the earlier crimson moon. Its final ruler was the one-eyed King Irmin, and it fell with the kingdom.
### STANDARD +
Its era leaned on mechanical engineering and knowledge said to come from beyond the sky, and later turned to the Abyss. Khaenri'ah's survivors, such as the Alberich Clan, and figures such as Dainsleif, trace their roots to its court.

## structure
### STANDARD
The Eclipse Dynasty is known mainly from fragments. A king ruled, with a military of knights, a scholarly body (the Universitas Magistrorum), and by the end a Royal Guard called the Black Serpent Knights. The Alberich Clan produced leaders of the kingdom's knights, and the Knight Marshal could advise the king directly.
### LARGE +
The knights' roles shifted from the earlier dynasty. The Beastmaster Knights and alchemy, mainstays of the Crimson Moon era, were pushed aside for mechanical engineering. By the cataclysm the Schwanenritter used earlier-model Ruin Golems, and the Black Serpent Knights included Dainsleif, called the Twilight Sword, and Halfdan.

## members
### LITE
King Irmin, the last ruler; Dainsleif of the Royal Guard; and the Traveler's sibling, adopted into the royal house.
### STANDARD +
- **Irmin**: the one-eyed Vinster King, last of the dynasty.
- **Dainsleif**: Twilight Sword and a captain of the Black Serpent Knights, who later became the immortal wanderer of Teyvat.
- **The Traveler's sibling**: arrived 500 years ago, was adopted as royalty and was believed able to command the Abyss.
- **Rerir**: an intelligence officer and secret agent called the Rächer of Solnari, later one of the Five Sinners.
- **Anfortas and Chlothar**: Alberich nobles, with Anfortas serving as regent at the end.
### LARGE +
- **Halfdan**: another Black Serpent Knight, later a Shadowy Husk.
- **Pierro**: says he once served the final dynasty, before the Fatui.

## goals
### STANDARD
Little is said of official aims. The dynasty valued mechanical engineering and secrets from beyond the skies, which drew resistance from people who held that human will mattered more than such techniques. Late in its reign Irmin redirected the Universitas Magistrorum's resources toward researching the Abyss.

## methods
### LARGE
The dynasty's rule leaned on technical progress. Mechanical Wardens, now known as Ruin Golems, guarded the kingdom, and forbidden knowledge was used. Irmin also deployed secret agents, notably Rerir, to purge descendants of the earlier dynasty. The Traveler's sibling was set to absorb the Abyss's world-shattering power, which was later split among the Five Sinners.

## history
### STANDARD
**Rise.** The legend says the crimson moon set and the dark sun descended, replacing the Crimson Moon Dynasty, though the dates and manner are not known. The Perinheri book already shows the Universitas Magistrorum and an Alberich as established names in this time.

**Final reign.** The last king, Irmin, ruled when the cataclysm struck 500 years ago. The Traveler's sibling arrived and was adopted as royalty. Gods cursed the kingdom's people, the Abyss spread, and Khaenri'ah fell.
### LARGE +
Before the fall, Irmin grew more erratic. His resources shifted toward the Abyss, and he ordered the purge of the earlier dynasty's remaining descendants. Dainsleif led an assault on the palace to free Vedrfolnir, releasing Abyssal energy that spawned monsters across the kingdom. With Irmin indisposed, Knight Marshal Anfortas declared himself regent. The Schwanenritter led survivors to the surface, using Ruin Golems to hold off the beasts.
### ULTRA +
**After the fall.** The dynasty's survivors scattered. Dainsleif was cursed with immortality, Chlothar founded the Abyss Order, and the Five Sinners went on to shape Teyvat. Pierro's memory of the era in A Rekviem for the Underworld (7.1) adds that during the cataclysm Hroptatyr left him a notebook of forbidden knowledge before going to merge with the Abyss, and Pierro sealed it away.

## relations
### LARGE
- **Crimson Moon Dynasty**: its predecessor; the Eclipse Dynasty hunted its remaining descendants, partly for its secrets.
- **Alberich Clan**: provided the regents and knights; the clan says it had no royal blood.
- **Abyss Order**: founded by Chlothar, formerly of the dynasty's nobility.
- **Universitas Magistrorum**: the scholarly body, later redirected to Abyss research.
### ULTRA +
- **Fatui**: Pierro says he once served the final dynasty.

## reputation
### ULTRA
Among surviving Khaenri'ahns the Eclipse Dynasty's name is tied to the kingdom's destruction. Most of Teyvat remembers only 'Khaenri'ah' and its curse.

## secrets
@spoiler: major
### ULTRA
That the dynasty ordered the purge of the Crimson Moon descendants through Rerir, and that the Traveler's sibling sat in its royal house, are revealed in the Nod-Krai Archon Quest (6.1) and in the Teyvat chapter quests, in which the Traveler witnesses the kingdom's fall.

## status

The dynasty ended with Khaenri'ah's fall and no ruling house survives.
### STANDARD +

## entry: King Irmin
@keys: King Irmin, Irmin, Vinster King, one-eyed king
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Irmin was the last king of Khaenri'ah, called the Vinster King and described as one-eyed. When the cataclysm began he was 'indisposed' and unable to deal with the dark beasts, so Knight Marshal Anfortas declared himself regent. Earlier he redirected the Universitas Magistrorum toward Abyss research and ordered the purge of Crimson Moon descendants. The Traveler's sibling absorbed the Abyssal power he demanded.

## entry: Black Serpent Knights
@keys: Black Serpent Knights, Twilight Sword, Royal Guard of Khaenri'ah
@keys2: Dainsleif, Halfdan, Khaenri'ah
### LARGE
The Black Serpent Knights were Khaenri'ah's royal guard at the time of the cataclysm. Dainsleif, the Twilight Sword, was among their captains, and Halfdan another member. Apart from Dainsleif, the others have become Shadowy Husks by the time the Traveler meets them in The Chasm's Underground Mines.

## entry: Perinheri's two dynasties
@keys: crimson moon, dark sun, Hleobrant, Angelica
@keys2: Perinheri, Khaenri'ah
### LARGE
In the legend of Perinheri, the crimson moon set and the dark sun descended into a darker dusk. Alchemy and Beastmaster Knights were a mainstay under the crimson moon, but the Eclipse era's interest in mechanical engineering sidelined them. In the book's second volume, Angelica names the four mightiest figures of the kingdom: Njord, Alf of the Universitas Magistrorum, Alberich, and Perinheri.

## entry: Rächer of Solnari
@keys: Rächer of Solnari, Rerir
@version: 6.1
@spoiler: major
### ULTRA
Rerir was an ordinary intelligence officer secretly assigned by Irmin to hunt down Crimson Moon descendants. His fiancée, Tholindis, was herself a descendant. When Irmin's actions went beyond reason, Rerir spared at least Frithr and warned her to hide. After Tholindis died in the cataclysm he became one of the Five Sinners of Khaenri'ah.
