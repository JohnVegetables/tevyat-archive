## summary
### LITE
The Narzissenkreuz Institute was an orphanage and school in Fontaine that raised orphans and the children of criminals. An Oceanid, Lyris, was its Director, and the retired admiral Basil Elton was its Vice Director. It scattered during the cataclysm 500 years ago, but its alumni, among them Alain and Mary-Ann Guillotin, Rene and Jakob, went on to shape Fontaine for good and ill.
### STANDARD +
The building stood over the ruins of an older civilisation. Life there was gentle, with make-believe adventures and afternoon tea. Its legacy is bittersweet: its children founded both the Fontaine Research Institute and, separately, the secret Narzissenkreuz Ordo.
### LARGE +
Treat the Institute as a lost home: almost every later character connected to it is defined by what they did after it was gone.

## structure
### STANDARD
The Director was always an Oceanid, because the post was created in honour of the first Hydro Archon, Egeria. Lyris was a gentle Oceanid who played with the children but, per the Court of Fontaine's register of Oceanids, was not good with cause and effect or counting. The Vice Director, chosen for "exceptional moral quality and ability", was the practical manager: Basil Elton, who took the post on retiring as Admiral of the Fontaine Navy. The institute was part of a public welfare system and, being built over ruins, was described as a home that would see neither sun nor moon.
### LARGE +
Children were raised together across generations. The "second-to-last" generation included Basil herself, Emanuel Guillotin, Karl Ingold and Dwight Lasker, who stayed friends for life. Carter Scherbius also belonged. The last generation included Rene de Petrichor, Jakob, Alain Guillotin and Mary-Ann Guillotin.

## leadership
### STANDARD
Lyris (Director) and Basil Elton (Vice Director). Lyris regarded Basil as her assistant, but Basil actually ran the orphanage's affairs. Basil was an easygoing woman, beloved by the children, who baked and made refreshments that the children thought were the best part of the day.
### LARGE +
Basil's old ship, the Sponsian, had been the pride of the Fontaine Navy. When the Armada was disbanded after an incident she retired, and was picked for the Vice Director's post, which an unnamed person in the records joked about. She learned baking over a few years at the Institute, although her cooking was poor in her youth. A dark-green feather on her hat fascinated the children.

## members
### STANDARD
Known members from 500 years ago: Lyris, Basil Elton, Emanuel Guillotin, Karl Ingold, Dwight Lasker, Carter Scherbius, Alain Guillotin, Jakob, Mary-Ann Guillotin and Rene de Petrichor. The children are minors in the Institute-era accounts; they are described here only factually, by what they did and what happened to them.
### LARGE +
- Alain and Mary-Ann Guillotin: siblings. Alain played a champion in the children's games; he later enrolled in the Institute of Natural Philosophy and eventually founded the Fontaine Research Institute. Mary-Ann joined the Marechaussee Phantom.
- Rene and Jakob: Rene cast as the "fell dragon" in the games, Jakob as a knight (per the artifact text). Rene's notes show they went to the Girdle of the Sands with Karl to research energy sources, and both later founded the Narzissenkreuz Ordo.
- Dwight Lasker went on to direct the Institute of Natural Philosophy. He visits Basil's grave in the present.
### ULTRA +
Carter Scherbius later became Alain's assistant at the Institute of Natural Philosophy, and a notebook-based account has an incident with him strain Alain and Rene's relationship. Karl Ingold, who was involved at Poisson shortly before the Siege, appears to be the "Mr. Karl" in Rene's notes.

## goals
### STANDARD
To house, feed and raise orphans and the children of criminals. Beyond that the Institute had no known political aim.
### LARGE +
Play was part of the routine. Children acted out Narzissen champions, wicked mages, evil dragons and heroes, with the Vice Director's tea breaks pausing every adventure. The story of the hero Narzissenkreuz that Alain had them play became the allegory Mary-Ann later told Lyris.

## history
@version: 3.6
### STANDARD
The founding date is unknown, but the Institute kept an Oceanid Director from the start. When the cataclysm came, both Lyris and Basil were called away to deal with it, and expected not to return. Basil placed the children with trusted friends, and the Institute flooded and was lost. Basil died at sea in the last battle against the beast Elynas. The Director and the "sisters" she left with never came back to the children. The members scattered: some were taken in by the Marechaussee Phantom or the Special Security and Surveillance Patrol, others by explorers who took them abroad.
### LARGE +
Before leaving, the Vice Director gave Jakob the dark-green feather from her hat for safekeeping and entrusted Rene with another child's care, according to artifact text in which the children's names are blanked. Rene and Jakob later went to the Girdle of the Sands with Karl Ingold. Alain enrolled in the Institute of Natural Philosophy, Mary-Ann joined the Marechaussee Phantom, and Rene and Jakob joined the Institute of Natural Philosophy on Alain's recommendation.
### ULTRA +
Lyris survived the cataclysm and went back to Fontaine, weakened, living in the submerged ruins. Rene and Jakob found her first, and Mary-Ann and her robotic dog Seymour visited. Rene then founded the Narzissenkreuz Ordo, drawing in Lyris, who agreed to his plan. Mary-Ann and the Ordo later clashed at Elynas, where Mary-Ann was lost and Lyris absorbed her personality. The Oceanid called Mary-Ann made the dream world of the Real Annapausis in the ruins, and the Oceanid Ann appeared as its by-product. The Ordo and Lyris's end are covered in their own entries.

## relations
### STANDARD
- **Marechaussee Phantom**: took some of the orphans in, and later hunted the Ordo.
- **Institute of Natural Philosophy**: where Alain, Rene and Jakob studied; Dwight Lasker directed it.
- **Fontaine Research Institute**: founded later by Alain Guillotin.
- **Narzissenkreuz Ordo**: the secret society of Rene and Jakob; unrelated to the orphanage as an organisation, but made up of its former children.
### LARGE +
- **Annapausis**: the Institute's location now. A character, Seymour, confirms the orphanage ruins' coordinates match Annapausis.
- **Egeria**: the Director's post honours her as the first Hydro Archon.

## reputation
### STANDARD
Little is known of its public standing. The only official views come from records that treat it as a branch of the public welfare system, and from the Court of Fontaine's Oceanid register, which labels Lyris as affectionate and caring.

## status
### LITE
Gone for 500 years. Its ruins are submerged beneath Annapausis.
### STANDARD +
No new Institute developments were found for 6.x or 7.0-7.1.

## entry: Lyris, the Director
@keys: Lyris, Princess Lyris, Red Empress
@version: 4.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Lyris was the Oceanid Director. She was tall and gentle, played with the children, and relied on her Vice Director. She returned to the flooded Institute after the cataclysm, saddened, and swam around its ruins. Mary-Ann once told her a fairy tale in which Lyris was a princess. Rene and Jakob persuaded her to join the Narzissenkreuz Ordo, where she was called the Red Empress; her Oceanid essence was used in the Ordo's experiment, and she later absorbed Mary-Ann's personality in the clash at Elynas.

## entry: Basil Elton, the Vice Director
@keys: Basil Elton, Vice Director Basil, Sponsian
@version: 4.0
### STANDARD
Basil Elton was a former admiral of the Fontaine Navy and a childhood alumna of the Institute. She became its Vice Director after retiring and was the one who really ran it. When the cataclysm came she sent the children to trusted friends and sailed on the repaired Sponsian against Elynas. The ship broke in two and she died. People still visit her grave, including Dwight Lasker.

## entry: Annapausis and the Institute ruins
@keys: Annapausis, ruins of the orphanage
@version: 4.0
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
The Institute's coordinates are the same as those of the submerged Annapausis, according to Seymour in Search in the Algae Sea. The place reappears in the Ann of the Narzissenkreuz quests as a dreamlike castle realm built by an Oceanid out of memories of the Institute. Ann says the assembly hall is where the children studied, always lively though never sunlit.

## entry: Nymph's Dream artifacts
@keys: Nymph's Dream, Heroes' Tea Party, Odyssean Flower, Wicked Mage's Plumule, Veteran's Visage
@version: 3.6
### LARGE
The Nymph's Dream artifact set holds most of what the game says about the Institute's daily life and its end: the tea breaks, the feather on Basil's hat, the children's role-play, and the scattering after the cataclysm. Its text is poetic and sometimes blanks out names, so the wiki's identifications (for example of who took in which child) are partly inferences.
