## summary
### LITE
The Grand Alliance was the coalition of Natlan's seven tribes, created to build the new nation of Ochkanatlan under Och-Kan, the Sacred Lord. It began as an agreement between autonomous groups, but Och-Kan, growing obsessive, gathered the tribes' authority into his own hands. The Alliance ended in a civil war: by the wiki's reading, six tribes rose with the Scarlet-Eyed Youth and the seventh stood with Och-Kan. It is a historical organisation, long gone by the present day.
### STANDARD +
It is known mostly through relics: Och-Kan's notes in the ruins of Ochkanatlan, and the texts on weapons belonging to its officers and rebels.

## structure
### STANDARD
Seven tribes joined as autonomous groups. Their leaders handed over authority, and Och-Kan acted as the Alliance's single ruler. Known offices include the Ordnance Officer (held by Yupanqui) and the Recording Officer (Amoxcihuatl). The tribes also supplied labour: Och-Kan's notes describe requisitioning one laborer from every ten households for waterworks under his city.
### LARGE +
A weapon text describes the Ordnance Officer post as idle, or perhaps it always was. Yupanqui took it while dispirited, expecting to wait out his days. The Alliance's other offices are not described in the sources checked.

## leadership
### STANDARD
Och-Kan led the Alliance as the Sacred Lord, also called the Holy Sovereign. He took the reins after Xbalanque, the first Pyro Archon, entered the Sacred Flame. He held that Xbalanque alone deserved the title of god and that no other ruler should arise. Details of his rule and fall belong to his own entry.
### LARGE +
Tribal leaders mattered before the Alliance consolidated. Ixquieh and Ahpub, twin chiefs of the Nanatzcayan and Teteocan, died, and Och-Kan then persuaded their tribes to hand authority over "temporarily". Sakkuk was later appointed the Nanatzcayan's agent. Uenuku and the Oceanids who followed him left the Alliance around the time Ixquieh died. The First Speaker of the Flower-Feather Clan, Lianca, doubted Och-Kan and later rebelled.

## members
### STANDARD
Known members and figures tied to the Alliance:
- Yupanqui: Ordnance Officer, who later joined the Scarlet-Eyed Youth against Och-Kan.
- Amoxcihuatl: Recording Officer.
- Ixquieh and Ahpub: twin chiefs of the Nanatzcayan and Teteocan.
- Lianca: First Speaker of the Flower-Feather Clan.
- Uenuku: left the Alliance with his Oceanid followers.
### LARGE +
Tribes known by name in connection with it are the Nanatzcayan (Children of Echoes), the Teteocan (Collective of Plenty), the Flower-Feather Clan and the seventh tribe, whose people are later called Muratans. Canon does not name all seven in one place.

## goals
### STANDARD
The Alliance was created to build Ochkanatlan, a new nation for the human tribes. Under Och-Kan the aim hardened into making humanity safe from "all that is beyond human comprehension", dragons and the Night Kingdom alike.

## methods
### LARGE
Och-Kan used labour levies, treated objections as betrayals of the alliance, banned live sacrifice and worship of the Night Kingdom, and used the Turnfire against opponents. His notes show him reasoning that divided rulership led to endless strife and that any chance of revolt must be eliminated. He also kept the ancient dragon Ixlel imprisoned in a statue so that her power could run his city.

## history
### STANDARD
After Xbalanque entered the Sacred Flame, the seven tribes formed the Alliance and placed authority in Och-Kan's hands. The tribes' consent frayed as the Sacred Lord grew more suspicious. The Alliance ended in a civil war in which the Scarlet-Eyed Youth gathered heroes against Och-Kan and his city fell. (That the seventh tribe sided with Och-Kan is the wiki's reading, not a direct statement.)
### LARGE +
**Strains inside.** Och-Kan's notes record that only the Flower-Feather Clan's First Speaker refused to recruit laborers, and that he saw her storming out as an abuse of power and disregard for the oaths of alliance. Uenuku and the Oceanids had already left. The First Speaker later rebelled and died in battle against Och-Kan's forces.

**The split.** The wiki reads The Frozen, Rekindling Land and The Last Survivor of Tenochtzitoc as placing the seventh tribe on Och-Kan's side and the other six on the Youth's. Enjou tells it as a kingdom torn apart by civil war, with the wicked king slain and his people exiled. The wiki's Muratans page says the seventh tribe split in two: the Muratans, who became wanderers, and the Tenochtzitoc, who settled apart on islands to the north-west of Natlan.
### ULTRA +
**After the fall.** The Scarlet-Eyed Youth, remembered as the second Pyro Archon, burned the "ineffable city" in the rebellion and freed the Flower-Feather Clan. Och-Kan survived by splitting his mind and body, and the Alliance was not revived in any source checked. Ehecatl's remark in The Frozen, Rekindling Land says that the followers of a tyrant are driven out by heroes and, after many years of wandering, their descendants end a tyranny in another realm. The wiki links the remark to the Muratans and Mondstadt's aristocracy; the line itself names neither.

## relations
### STANDARD
- **Ochkanatlan**: the city the Alliance built, and its seat.
- **The Scarlet-Eyed Youth**: led the revolt of the six tribes.
- **Muratans**: by the wiki's account, the wandering branch of the seventh tribe, who later settled in Mondstadt; Vennessa is their representative in history.
- **Dragons and Saurians**: persecuted during Och-Kan's rule. The Youth was among those sympathetic to the Saurians.

## status

## entry: Holy Sovereign's Notes
@keys: Holy Sovereign's Notes, Holy Sovereign
@keys2: Och-Kan, Alliance, Ochkanatlan
@version: 5.0
### STANDARD
The Holy Sovereign's Notes are Och-Kan's fragmentary writings in the Old Core of Chu'ulel and elsewhere in Ochkanatlan. They record his requisition of laborers from the tribes, the First Speaker's refusal, his fear that surviving dragons might regain their power, and his decision that there must be one ruler, one sovereign, one god. A later note shows that he saw himself as loyal to the Alliance to the end.

## entry: Alliance officers and rebels
@keys: Ordnance Officer, Recording Officer, First Speaker
@keys2: Grand Alliance, Alliance, Och-Kan
### LARGE
Yupanqui was the Alliance's Ordnance Officer and, by a weapon text, accepted the empty post out of loyalty to his last surviving friend. The Scarlet-Eyed Youth later convinced him to fight Och-Kan. Amoxcihuatl served as Recording Officer. Lianca, First Speaker of the Flower-Feather Clan, doubted Och-Kan, refused his labor quotas and died rebelling against him.
