## summary
### LITE
The Golden Troupe was an order founded by Boethius, a Harmost of Remuria, from the survivors of the empire after its first fall. Its aim was to save Remuria and the Symphony (Phobos) so that music could unite the world in peace. It gathered in the Faded Castle, built music devices, studied spirit and fate, and used golems and sacrifice. It is long gone, but its remains lie under Petrichor in Fontaine.
### STANDARD +
Because its work is known only from ruins, items and later scholars' notes, the troupe is a historical faction: it is met through relics, not living members.

## leadership
### STANDARD
Boethius, once one of Fontaine's greatest musicians and a Harmost of the God King Remus, gathered the remnants of Remuria into the troupe. The sources name no other officers.
### LARGE +
Boethius had been devoted to Remus's vision to the point of fanaticism. His devotion continued after Remus himself changed his mind, and the wiki states that he was ultimately corrupted by the Symphony. His own entry carries his life story.

## goals
### STANDARD
The troupe wanted to save Phobos, the Symphony of Remuria, so that its power could unite the whole world and bring peace through music and the "Grand Symphony". Item text says Remuria's remnants never gave up their wish to rescue their waning civilisation and surpass fate.
### LARGE +
An artifact text in the set voices the troupe's bitterness: musicians who had lost their score mourned the empire, scorned the "savages" who inherited its lands, and promised that when the great symphony played again the loyal would be rewarded and humanity sorted into masters and the rest.

## methods
### STANDARD
Members met in the Faded Castle to study the spirit and fate, and built musical devices in imitation of Remuria's golden-age constructs, including autoharmonic music boxes that read scores. The troupe saw fate as a fixed script. By collecting and copying "cruel dramas" they produced what outsiders called magic. Its music was also used to record things, including magic, and conches carried information.
### LARGE +
To survive the Prophecy of Fontaine, the troupe studied ways to preserve lives and bodies: moving a consciousness into a stone golem filled with golden ichor and making seals such as the "Seal of the Chymical Marriage". Este, a Remurian, is said to have survived the ages as a golem. Item text states the troupe used melodies to entice children to give their souls to sleeping golems, and the wiki calls this child sacrifice.
### ULTRA +
The wiki says Ruggiero judged that the troupe's arcane arts were older than Remuria, going back to a time when pre-Remurian gods used symphonies such as the Fortuna to rule Fontaine.

## history
### LARGE
After Remuria's first fall, Boethius gathered its remnants and founded the troupe. The troupe worked in the Faded Castle and left its remnants, golems and records in the Petrichor area and the waters of the Nostoi Region and Sea of Bygone Eras.
### ULTRA +
Cassiodor, Boethius's close friend in Remuria, later founded what became the Marechaussee Hunters; according to artifact text and the wiki, their original purpose was to fight the troupe, its magic and its enslaved golems. Cassiodor also helped Ruggiero seal the Faded Castle beneath the rebuilt Petrichor, and the Consilium Sapientum spread false stories about the castle to bury its secrets. Rene later found troupe remnants and golem statues in Petrichor. The Narzissenkreuz Ordo, founded by Rene and Jakob, drew on the troupe's techniques; the wiki says Jakob's long preservation of his body was related to them and that the Ordo tried to merge people's consciousnesses into one being to survive the prophecy.

## relations
### LARGE
- **Remuria and Remus**: the troupe was the afterlife of Remuria's court, led by one of its Harmosts.
- **Marechaussee Hunters**: founded to oppose it.
- **Narzissenkreuz Ordo**: inherited and adapted its methods.
- **Petrichor's Consilium Sapientum**: concealed its secrets.

## status
@version: 4.0
### LITE
The troupe no longer exists. Its remains are in Petrichor and the Faded Castle, and nothing in 6.x or 7.0-7.1 content was found that changes this.
### STANDARD +
Players meet it through the Faded Castle exploration in the Petrichor quests, the Ancient Autoharmonic Music Box, and the Golden Troupe artifact set.

## entry: Boethius
@keys: Boethius, Bleeding Idol
@keys2: Golden Troupe, Remuria, Harmost
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
A musician from a conquered island tribe, Boethius rose to be one of Remus's four Harmosts and a close friend of Cassiodor. He enforced Remus's orders against barbarians and Lochfolk, and when Remus relented he rallied Remuria's last armies, took the golden ichor and sealed the dragon Scylla. He was swept away with Remuria by the Symphony's tsunami, and the troupe was his attempt to continue Remuria's cause afterward. His full entry holds the details.

## entry: Praetorian golems and the Seal of the Chymical Marriage
@keys: Seal of the Chymical Marriage, golden ichor, Praetorian Golem
@keys2: Golden Troupe, Remuria, Petrichor, Faded Castle
### LARGE
The troupe's preservation techniques put a consciousness inside stone golems with golden ichor. Item texts describe silent, hungry golems that wait in the theatre of the Faded Castle for innocent souls. The troupe's sealing methods included the "Seal of the Chymical Marriage".
