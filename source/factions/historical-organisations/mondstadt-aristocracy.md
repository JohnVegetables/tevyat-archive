## summary
### LITE
The Mondstadt Aristocracy was the class of noble clans who ruled Mondstadt after the fall of Decarabian's old city. They began as protectors of the people and, over centuries, became tyrants who enslaved commoners and staged gladiatorial games. The foremost clan, the Lawrences, ruled the city until Vennessa, an enslaved gladiator, led a rebellion with the help of Barbatos about a thousand years ago. Most nobles were exiled. The Knights of Favonius were founded so that nobles could never rule again.
### STANDARD +
The era still shapes Mondstadt. Its festivals partly come from noble customs, its freedom is defined against nobility, and its old families still carry reputations: the Gunnhildrs are respected as the clan that stayed honourable, while the Lawrences are widely resented.

## structure
### STANDARD
The aristocracy was made up of clans, each claiming descent from heroes of the early days of Barbatos' Mondstadt. Named clans are the Lawrence, Gunnhildr and Imunlaukr, and the Ragnvindrs are presumably of noble standing. Other nobles belonged to no named clan, such as the family of Landrich.
### LARGE +
The Gotini, Hlaewringi, Bructeri and Sitones clans are named in the Windborne Flower's Spruchdichtung. The late aristocracy was served by the order of "starlit knights", royal mages and a divided Church. Landrich's family produced the brothers Parsifal, Ingbert and Eberhart. Luther and Priscilla are further named aristocrats outside any clan. The Gunnhildrs held Dornman Port as a hereditary fief. After the rebellion the Gunnhildrs led a push to abolish hereditary fiefdoms and likely gave up their own.

## leadership
### STANDARD
No single ruler stood over the whole class. In the early period Venerare led the Lawrence Clan when the new city was founded, and the leader of all the clans inscribed the clans' oath of protection beneath Barbatos' statue. By the end the Lawrences were effectively the rulers of the city. The manga calls the ruler at the time of Vennessa's rebellion Lord Lawrence, and names his son Barca Lawrence as heir.

## goals
### STANDARD
The early aristocracy's role was to defend Mondstadt and keep Barbatos' freedom, an oath written beneath a statue of the god. In the late period the nobles instead protected their own power, with the Lawrences seeing themselves as overlords and kings.

## methods
### STANDARD
By the late period the nobles kept the people ignorant: knowledge of the past was suppressed, and anyone seen as a threat could be enslaved for labour or entertainment. The knight order of the time served them and the Church justified their rule.
### LARGE +
In the early period, nobles hunted to show strength and shared the spoils with the people, and the royal mages taught virtue and kept the history. Later the nobles tore down the statue of Barbatos to erase their oath and the mages stopped speaking of history. The gladiatorial games, once religious ceremonies, were turned into bloodsport for noble enjoyment. Nobles came to consider swords the only proper weapon, and looked down on bows and spears as tools for commoners and gladiators. The Lawrences' own sense of identity moved, in the words of weapon lore, from conquerors to overlords to kings.

## history
### STANDARD
After Barbatos freed the people from Decarabian, the Gunnhildr, Imunlaukr and Lawrence clans were prominent in founding the new city. Barbatos left so he would never become a tyrant, and the clans took over. The Lawrences rose to be the foremost clan and drifted into tyranny, while the Gunnhildrs refused to follow. Vennessa, a Muratan enslaved with her people after fleeing Ursa the Drake, became the gladiator known as the Lionfang Knight. In the manga's account, the Lawrences pitted her whole clan against Ursa for their freedom, and with Barbatos' help she drove the drake off. The rebellion that followed was broader than that one battle. Most nobles were exiled, uncorrupted houses such as the Gunnhildrs stayed, and Vennessa founded the Knights of Favonius and restored the Church.
### LARGE +
**Early period.** Nobles set up festivals such as Ludi Harpastum, Windblume and Weinlesefest for the people's benefit. The Church split in the late period between clergy backing the nobility and those opposed. In the manga's account Vennessa and Venti met on Ludi Harpastum, which angered Barca Lawrence.

**Failed earlier revolt.** Before Vennessa, the Wanderer's Troupe, a travelling band of musicians and fighters, tried and failed to overthrow the aristocracy in Mondstadt. Most of its members died, were enslaved or fled. The Dawnlight Swordswoman was turned into a gladiator but kept singing of freedom, and her example inspired Ragnvindr to rise against the nobles. Kreuzlied, a Lawrence who had abandoned his clan, survived because of his lineage and founded an underground society that later helped Vennessa.

**The wider rebellion.** According to Venti's Character Story, Barbatos forged a secret treaty in which the aristocrats sold all of Mondstadt, soldiers included, to Morax. The nobles' own soldiers, fearing enslavement, turned to Vennessa's side, leaving the clan with few loyal troops. Ragnvindr and the Gunnhildrs, who had refused to follow the other nobles, also supported the rebellion.

**Aftermath.** Ragnvindr converted a noble bathhouse into the first Mondstadt library, according to the wiki timeline. Vennessa ended her life ascending to Celestia at Windrise, where the great oak now stands.
### ULTRA +
**The Dragonspine expedition.** Landrich organised an expedition to Sal Vindagnyr around the time of the rebellion, hoping it would silence those who wished to rebel. Members included his sons Ingbert and Eberhart, plus Luther and others. Eberhart, who secretly wanted to overthrow the decaying aristocracy, attacked his companions; the party never returned, and records are sparse. Priscilla, one of Eberhart's co-conspirators, was caught after the plot was revealed. The wiki's reconstruction comes from artifact, weapon and journal texts, which gives it medium confidence.

## relations
### STANDARD
- **Knights of Favonius**: founded by Vennessa to replace the aristocracy. The order of "starlit knights" that served the nobles was a different, earlier body.
- **Church of Favonius**: the Church split during the late period, and the higher clergy backed the nobility. Vennessa restored it afterwards.
- **Lawrence Clan**: the main ruling house. See its own entry.
- **Gunnhildr Clan**: maintained its honour, broke with the Imunlaukrs, and was allowed to stay; it still supplies many of Mondstadt's knights and clergy.
- **Barbatos**: left Mondstadt so as not to become a tyrant, and returned when the land cried out for freedom.
### LARGE +
- **Ragnvindr Clan**: Ragnvindr, a squire, joined the rebellion and his family later became one of Mondstadt's most prominent.
- **Fatui**: in the present day Eula's uncle Schubert conspired with the Fatui to restore Lawrence rule, and the Traveler and Eula foiled the plot.

## reputation
### STANDARD
The aristocracy is remembered as a cautionary tale. The Lawrence Clan especially carries a poor reputation, and some citizens grow hostile at the mere mention of the name.
### LARGE +
Before she joined the Knights, Eula reports that almost every shop refused to take her money. Eula says one aristocratic tradition is to dance for loved ones on one's birthday, which grants the blessing of ancestors to both. The Lawrences kept up a strict regimen of combat, dance, cooking and etiquette, stressing appearances.

## secrets
### ULTRA
The true story of the rebellion is only partly public. The official history centres on Vennessa and Barbatos. Historians learned many years later that the Morax treaty which turned the soldiers was fake, but not that Barbatos forged it; that twist comes only from Venti's own Character Story, and it is not widely known that the bard is Barbatos.

## status
### LITE
The aristocracy no longer exists as a ruling class. Descendants of its clans, especially the Lawrences, live in Mondstadt as ordinary citizens under suspicion.
### STANDARD +
Eula, a Lawrence, serves as a captain in the Knights of Favonius. Her relatives denounced her as a traitor. Another Lawrence, Lessig, left for Sumeru hoping to restore the clan's honour, and found that nobody cared. As of 7.1 no new aristocracy-related story was found in the Nod-Krai or Snezhnaya content checked.

## entry: Lawrence Clan
@keys: Lawrence Clan, House Lawrence, Lord Lawrence, Barca Lawrence, Glacial Seal
### STANDARD
The Lawrence Clan was the foremost clan of the aristocracy and its last ruling house. In Decarabian's time they were said to have lived outside the city and survived in the frozen wilderness, which made them see themselves as conquerors. Their emblem is the Glacial Seal. They were disgraced and exiled after Vennessa's rebellion. Eula carries the seal today. Other living members include her uncle Schubert and Lessig.
### LARGE +
Many Lawrence traditions survive. The curriculum covers combat, dance, cooking and etiquette, and Eula's character stories describe the Dance of Sacrifice, whose third act, Flickering Candlelight, is passed down the family. Members cook for themselves rather than rely on commoners. The clan has strict phrasings for addressing others, which outsiders are not supposed to learn.

## entry: Vennessa's rebellion
@keys: Vennessa's rebellion, fall of the aristocracy
@keys2: aristocracy, aristocrats, nobles, Lawrence
### STANDARD
The rebellion that ended aristocratic rule began, in the manga's account, when an enslaved Muratan gladiator named Vennessa was made to fight Ursa the Drake in the arena in return for her people's freedom. Barbatos, travelling as the bard Venti, helped her win. A wider uprising followed; a forged treaty selling Mondstadt to Morax turned the nobles' soldiers against them, and most nobles were exiled. Afterwards Vennessa founded the Knights of Favonius. See the Knights of Favonius entry for the later history.

## entry: The Wanderer's Troupe's revolt

@keys2: aristocracy, Mondstadt, nobles
@version: 1.0
### LARGE
The Wanderer's Troupe was a band of travelling musicians and fighters that made its last stand in Mondstadt, where it tried and failed to overthrow the nobles. Its Conductor was killed in the fighting. The Sojourner died protecting his allies. The Dawnlight Swordswoman was enslaved as a gladiator and kept singing of freedom, influencing Ragnvindr. Kreuzlied survived and went on to build an underground society against the aristocracy.
