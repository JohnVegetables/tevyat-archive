## summary
### LITE
The Four Winds are four guardians of Mondstadt: the Wolf of the North, the Lion of the South, the Dragon of the East and the Falcon of the West. The Anemo Archon Barbatos entrusted them with the city's safety before he vanished a thousand years ago. Mondstadt once worshipped them and built temples to them. Today the worship has faded, and the titles live on mainly through the Knights of Favonius, the wolf spirit Boreas and the dragon Dvalin.
### STANDARD +
They are a tradition rather than an organisation. There is no headquarters or roster, and who bears each title is a matter of old ballads, weapon lore and a few quest lines that do not always agree.

## structure
### STANDARD
Each wind holds one compass direction and a symbol on a side of Mondstadt City. Per the wiki's trivia: the Lion above the main gate (south), the Wolf on the stairs up to the cathedral (north), the Dragon over the side entrance (east) and the Falcon on both sides of the Knights' headquarters entrance (west). The order's name, Favonius, is the Roman name for the west wind. Each wind had a temple, though only the Wolf, Lion and Falcon temples exist as ruins the Traveler can visit.
### LARGE +
Three of the four are tied to ranks or beings, not to a single bloodline. The Wolf is Boreas the wolf king and, by some accounts, the Knight of Boreas whom he recognises. The Lion is passed down through the Grand Masters of the Knights as the Dandelion (Lionfang) Knight. The Dragon is Dvalin. The Falcon is the vaguest and is most often read as Vennessa.

## members
### STANDARD
- **Wolf of the North:** Boreas (Andrius), the great wolf king whose spirit dwells in Wolvendom, and the human Knight of Boreas he recognises. Known Knights of Boreas are a nameless wandering knight, Ravenwood, Rostam and now Varka, the Grand Master. Roland was named a candidate but never granted the title.
- **Lion of the South:** the Dandelion (Lionfang) Knight. Past holders include Vennessa, Arundolyn and Valentine; Jean holds it as of 7.1.
- **Dragon of the East:** Dvalin, the Wind Dragon, called Stormterror when Mondstadt feared him.
- **Falcon of the West:** most commonly Vennessa, said to have taken the title after her ascension, or symbolically the whole Knights of Favonius. This is disputed.
### LARGE +
The lists of current and former holders come from a HoYoLAB anecdote about Varka that the wiki cites, not from in-game text. Treat names beyond the in-game weapon and book lore as sourced to that anecdote.

## goals
### STANDARD
Barbatos charged the Winds with guarding Mondstadt and its people. In the ballads, the Wind Dragon is the one shown acting on that charge, fighting the corrupted dragon Durin at the height of the cataclysm as the city's last defender.

## history
### STANDARD
The wiki dates the formal establishment of the Four Winds to after Vennessa's rebellion against the aristocracy about 1,000 years ago, when Barbatos entrusted Mondstadt's safety to them before he disappeared. The Dragon Tome adds that Dvalin predated the others: the ballads say Barbatos befriended him and taught him to speak, so he is regarded as one of the oldest guardians and the longest worshipped. For roughly five hundred years people worshipped the Winds and built temples to them.

During the cataclysm five hundred years ago, Dvalin was badly wounded fighting Durin and slept for centuries. Afterward worship declined, the temples were abandoned and most people forgot the Winds. When Dvalin woke he believed Mondstadt had abandoned him, turned on the city and was called Stormterror, until the Traveler, Venti and their allies freed him from the Abyss Order's manipulation during the Prologue.
### LARGE +
The Dragon Tome records a belief that Dvalin would wake whenever Mondstadt faced a new threat. By its account, the people's prayers woke Barbatos, who called Dvalin to defend the city. The book's one-hundred-year figure is a beta leftover; canon places the cataclysm five hundred years ago.

In the Prologue, the Traveler visits the temples of the Falcon, Wolf and Lion in the quests Winds of the Past, Crash Course and Sparks Amongst the Pages, where they appear as ruins. In Fallen Tears, Diluc says the Fatui have been hunting traces of Barbatos' power, and a vial of the Anemo God's breath was stolen from an East Temple of the Four Winds. That detail implies a Temple of the Dragon existed about six months before the game began.
### ULTRA +
In Ending Note, Venti says Dvalin never forgot his duty as one of the Four Winds and that he has no wish to strip him of it. At the end he offers the Traveler a place among the Winds once they find their sibling. Dainsleif, in the quest Bough Keeper: Dainsleif, is the only character in-game to mention the Falcon of the West by name, remarking that it can soar in the wind yet in the end only hovers beneath the light of the gods; he does not say whom he means.

## relations
### STANDARD
Barbatos is the one who set them in place, and Venti, who is Barbatos, still treats the Winds as his own. Dvalin is the Wind with the best-attested personal friendship with him. The Knights of Favonius inherited the Lion and Wolf titles through their Grand Master and the Knight of Boreas, so the order is the Winds' most active human heir. Dahlia, Barbatos' herald, serves the same god.
### LARGE +
The Winds are bound to the Knights through objects as well as titles. Favonius Sword and Favonius Greatsword lore ties the Lion and Wolf tradition to Rostam and Arundolyn, and Wolf's Gravestone and Wolf-Fang preserve the story of the first Knight of Boreas and his wolf. Aquila Favonia lore carries the belief that Vennessa became the Falcon.

## reputation
### STANDARD
Mondstadt remembers the Winds only dimly. Most people treat them as folklore. Dandelion seeds on the wind are said in the Temple of the Lion's text to be the Lion's blessing. After the Stormterror crisis the people of Mondstadt resumed their belief in Dvalin, and the Winds' symbols still mark the city. The ballad collections that tell their stories were compiled by scholars from bard songs, and the book itself warns that bards embellish history.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the Four Winds are a fading tradition. Dvalin is again regarded as a guardian of Mondstadt; Boreas's spirit is in Wolvendom; Jean is Acting Grand Master and holds the Lion title; Varka is Grand Master and the Knight of Boreas. The Falcon is disputed.
### STANDARD +
No change to the Winds as a concept was found in the 6.x Nod-Krai or 7.0 to 7.1 Snezhnaya material checked. The wiki's holder list rests on a HoYoLAB anecdote about Varka.

## entry: Wolf of the North
@keys: Wolf of the North, Knight of Boreas, Temple of the Wolf, North Wind
@version: 1.0
### STANDARD
The Wolf of the North is both Boreas, the wolf king also called Andrius who now rests as a spirit in Wolvendom, and the Knight of Boreas, a human recognised by him. The Temple of the Wolf near the Thousand Winds Temple in Starfell Valley was originally a training ground and is now a monster-infested ruin. Known Knights of Boreas: the nameless wandering knight (original wielder of Wolf's Gravestone), Ravenwood, Rostam and Varka. Roland was a candidate who never received the title.
### LARGE +
Accounts differ on how the Wolf title relates to the Knights' Wolf and Lion tradition. Favonius Sword lore says leaders get one of the two, but whether the Wolf of the Winds is always the Knight of Boreas is unclear. At some point Boreas and his wolves moved west and settled in Wolvendom.

## entry: Lion of the South
@keys: Lion of the South, Dandelion Knight, Lionfang Knight, Temple of the Lion
@version: 1.0
### STANDARD
The Lion is handed down among the Grand Masters and Acting Grand Masters of the Knights of Favonius as the Dandelion or Lionfang Knight. The Temple of the Lion lies in Falcon Coast in Galesong Hill; dandelions riding the wind are said to be the Lion's blessing. Vennessa, Arundolyn, Valentine and now Jean are listed holders. The Lion symbol is above Mondstadt City's main gate.

## entry: Dragon of the East
@keys: Dragon of the East, Wind Dragon, Temple of the Dragon, East Temple of the Four Winds
@version: 1.0
### STANDARD
Dvalin is one of Barbatos' oldest friends among the Winds and has been worshipped the longest. Ballads say Barbatos won the people's trust for him and taught him human speech. Wounded by Durin in the cataclysm, he slept for centuries, woke to a city that feared him and was called Stormterror, and in A Long Shot said he was no longer part of the Four Winds. After the Prologue Mondstadt resumed believing in him. His temple does not exist as a visitable site.

## entry: Falcon of the West
@keys: Falcon of the West, Temple of the Falcon, West Wind
@version: 1.0
### STANDARD
The most uncertain of the Winds. Common belief says Vennessa became the Falcon on ascending to Celestia, and the wiki reads Dainsleif's cryptic line the same way. Breeze Amidst the Forest suggests instead that the whole Knights of Favonius is the Falcon, which the book calls debatable. The Temple of the Falcon is at Windrise in Galesong Hill, between Mondstadt City and the oak where Vennessa is said to have ascended. The Falcon symbol flanks the entrance to the Knights' headquarters. The English name uses falcon, but the emblem looks like an eagle, which may be a translation quirk.

## entry: Breeze Amidst the Forest
@keys: Breeze Amidst the Forest, Dragon Tome, Breeze Upon the Lake
@version: 1.0
### LARGE
A two-volume ballad collection assembled by scholars from bard songs, with a supplement, The Dragon Tome, quoting a cultural survey by Jacob Musk. It is the main in-world source for the Four Winds as a tradition and for Dvalin's friendship with Barbatos. The collection itself warns that bards embellish history, so its details are of low reliability.
