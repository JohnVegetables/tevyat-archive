## summary
### LITE
The Frostmoon Scions, also called the Moonchildren, are an indigenous people of Nod-Krai descended from the fallen golden realm of Hyperborea. Their home is the Frostmoon Enclave on Hiisi Island. For centuries they have worshipped the Goddess of the Frost Moon, and in particular the Moon Maiden Kuutar, who was later revealed to be the former Fatui Harbinger Columbina. They are led by the Moonchanter Lauma. They are known for antlered crowns, silver blood, a gift for speaking with animals, vegetarian food, charity and deep suspicion of outsiders who threaten them.
### STANDARD +
To most of modern Nod-Krai they seem ancient and set in their ways. They believe the wintry woods shelter them and that the full moon's light can cure every ill, and for generations they prayed to a goddess who never answered.

## leadership
### LITE
Lauma, the Moonchanter, is the spiritual leader, healer and voice of the Scions. She was chosen for the role at birth because she was born with an antler crown, silver blood and an extraordinary sensitivity to moonlight. Her people bring her every question, and senior members salute her as she passes.
### STANDARD +
The title of Moonchanter dates from about 500 years ago, when Aila became the first to hold it. Before that the Scions were led by Archpriests or Archpriestesses who ran Hyperborea's ancient rites. Lovia was the last of them, and her name has been struck from the record.
### LARGE +
Lauma was born to an ordinary Scion couple, and the faithful hailed her as proof that the Moon Goddess had not abandoned them. She kept two truths from her people to protect them: that the moon in Teyvat's sky is false, and that their goddess Kuutar had become the Harbinger Columbina and was hiding on their island. In 6.0 she decided this had been a mistake and told them. Among the elders is the priestess Sarakka, whom Lauma calls Grandma Sarakka. It was Sarakka who said Lauma's Moon Wheel fell out of her swaddling clothes.

## structure
### STANDARD
The Moonchanter leads. Below her, in descending order, are:
- **Priestesses**, who carry more responsibilities.
- **Acolytes**, who must first pass an exam on hymns and blessings.
- **Adherents**, the ordinary followers.

Men of the tribe cannot become acolytes or priestesses. One Scion grumbled about this while hauling heavy festival decorations.
### LARGE +
Some knowledge is restricted by rank. Only those of priestess rank or higher are supposed to know Lovia's name. The Enclave holds a library, where hymns are copied and records kept; Lauma once searched its entire records for the origin of her Moon Wheel without finding an answer.

## members
### LARGE
The Scions named in the 6.x story include Lauma (Moonchanter), the priestesses Ehrnrooth, Sarakka and Rahetar, the acolytes Verna, Zirnytsia and Eveliina, adherents such as Reetta, Lyylikki and Krodo, and other members such as Kapy. Kapy's daughter Kaarina suffers from a sensitivity to kuuvahki that flares up as illness; Lauma treats it with her own silver blood and urges Kapy to accept help from Nasha Town's doctors. Former members include Vilemina, Lauma's childhood friend, who defected to the Fatui in 6.0, and Columbina herself.
### ULTRA +
Verna, whose full name is Vernadska, works in Nasha Town and wants the Scions to accept kuuvahki devices. She helped the Traveler unseal the Court of the First Edict. Lauma's punishment for that was gentle: she sent Verna to help Ehrnrooth sort documents and copy hymns in the library. Historical figures recorded in the Sanctum of the Oathbearer include Saredne, Archpriestess in the 13th generation after Hyperborea's fall, Spargapiris of Haxathrausa, Acting Archpriestess soon after the fall, and Ariapeithea of Massagetae, a senior Mekhaneikos priestess of the same period.

## goals
### STANDARD
For generations the Scions' goal was faith itself: to honour the Moon Goddess, keep the old blessings alive and wait for a prophecy that the goddess would return. Under Lauma, especially since 6.0, the aim has shifted. In her words they are learning to thrive on their own, without relying on the Moon Goddess, and to make their home a better place to live. Lauma still defends their people: when the Fatui delivered their ultimatum she answered that the Scions would never hand anything over, and that with intruders' blood they would "draw an inviolable line in the snow".

## methods
### STANDARD
Aila's reform turned the Scions toward benevolence and faith, and they avoid conflict with outsiders where they can. They hand out food in Nasha Town, even to Fatui soldiers who sneer at them. Lauma warned such soldiers that kindness is "the first lantern" left for those who stumble in ignorance, but those who still refuse the moonlit path will be shown its shadow by frosted blades. They eat no meat and live on spring water, fruit and vegetables.
### LARGE +
- **Prayer and hymns.** Prayers, blessings and Runo hymns, some of them preserved in the book "Hymns of the Far North", are the centre of daily life.
- **Kuuvahki.** Kuuvahki is the moonlight's blessing. Using it for personal gain, including in kuuvahki devices, has traditionally been considered blasphemy.
- **The Moon Marrow.** The Scions guarded a Moon Marrow in a sealed chamber below the statue of Kuutar on Hiisi Island. Moonchanters use it to see the "true" past, and normally it could only be taken out when the moon is closest to Teyvat.
- **Animals.** Through Lauma, ibises, moles and finches carry messages and act as lookouts.
- **Life and death.** Scion teaching holds that death is not the end. Life is in constant flux, the dead return to nature, and loved ones will meet again.
- **Ceremonies.** They have their own rites, such as a formal engagement ceremony with a master of ceremonies, a member of the bride's family and an ecclesiast.

## history
### LARGE
**Hyperborea.** The Scions' ancestors lived in Hyperborea. The apostle Anacharsis taught them to build Mekhaneikos constructs and to predict and use the lunar tides for magic, founded on the Runo hymns. Their old rites honoured the Frost Moon and many other powers. When the Celestial Nail destroyed Hyperborea, the Frost Moon goddess Canon, whom they call Huurrekuutar, guided the survivors to Hiisi Island and then died. The exiles kept practising their rites even though no one answered their prayers. A prophecy grew up that the Moon Goddess would return through a Sacred Heir born among them.

**Lovia and Aila (about 500 years ago).** Kuuvahki was fading, and Archpriestess Lovia grew desperate for the Sacred Heir. She conspired with Rotwang and had people killed. Aila turned against her, killed her, and became the first Moonchanter. Aila abolished the Archpriestess office and the Hyperborean rites, rewrote many hymns, and refocused the faith on the moonlight. Together with Solovei she fought an Abyssal calamity at the Pillar of Embla, and she gave him the Arrow of Terpikeraunas, which became the basis of the Lightkeepers.

**Kuutar.** Aila foresaw and witnessed the birth of a new moon goddess in the Sanctum of the Oathbearer 400-500 years ago, and offered her the first drop of silver blood with the sword Moonweaver's Dawn. The Scions worshipped her as the Moon Maiden Kuutar, successor of the Frost Moon, and carved Statues of the New Moon in her likeness across Nod-Krai. They cared more about having their prayers granted than about her as a person. About 350-400 years ago she left them and joined the Fatui as the Harbinger Columbina.
### ULTRA +
**The Fatui crisis and the truth (6.0).** Shortly before the Traveler arrived, Columbina left the Fatui and returned to Hiisi Island. Lauma apologised for her ancestors and hid her in Silvermoon Hall. The Fatui set up the Kuuvahki Experimental Design Bureau over a fallen remnant of the Eternal Moon and defaced the Statues of the New Moon; only the statue on Hiisi Island was left untouched. Then two Fatui soldiers delivered an ultimatum: hand over the relic and reveal where "Kuutar" was within three days, or the island would be "cleansed". A figure posing as the Traveler's sibling (later revealed to be Rerir in disguise) opened the chamber below the statue of Kuutar, and Vilemina stole the Moon Marrow for the Fatui. After it was recovered at Starsand Shoal, the Traveler took the Marrow into their own body for safekeeping. That same night Lauma told the gathered Scions that their moon was false and their goddess had been a person all along. Some left, because losing their faith felt like losing their home. Others thanked her and said she finally felt close to them.

**Rerir and the moons (6.0-6.3).** Lauma kept Rerir's identity from her people to avoid panic. She fought beside the Traveler, Flins and Nefer against Rerir and then Dottore. She took part in the search for Columbina's true name and, during Dottore's attack, led the evacuation of the Southern Isles and Hiisi Island toward Natlan. In 6.3 it came out that the mysterious shield that had protected the Frostmoon Enclave from the lethal kuuvahki of the cannon fired at Starsand Shoal was Columbina's work: she had travelled back through time and spent most of her strength to save the Enclave.

## relations
### LARGE
- **Columbina (Kuutar)**: the goddess they once burdened with demands. After True Moon she accepted Lauma's suggestion to live among the Scions. She gave Lauma the Eternal Moon Marrow.
- **Fatui**: bitter enemies through 6.0-6.3. They defaced the Scions' statues, threatened to cleanse Hiisi Island, recruited Vilemina and hunted Columbina. Lauma still reminds her people that not every Snezhnayan is a Fatuus.
- **Lightkeepers**: allies through history. Aila helped Solovei found them and gave them the Arrow of Terpikeraunas.
- **Curatorium of Secrets**: Lauma and Nefer trade favours, medicine and Hyperborean records.
- **Nasha Town**: the Scions hand out food there. Some, like Kapy, distrust its Snezhnayan doctors.
- **League**: in 6.3 the Scions joined Nod-Krai's new League, alongside the Voynich Guild, the Curatorium, the Lightkeepers and others.
### ULTRA +
- **Knights of Favonius**: Varka worked closely with Lauma during the Nod-Krai crisis. He oversaw Nasha Town's preparations for Moon-Prayer Night and left the decorations to the Scions, and he took part in the alliance to bring Columbina back.
- **Aino and Ineffa**: they answered Lauma's call after the disaster and handed out food in Nasha Town on the Scions' behalf.
- **Wild Hunt**: enemies. Its master Rerir hunted the Moon Marrow the Scions guarded, though the moon's power usually keeps the Wild Hunt away from Hiisi Island.

## reputation
### ULTRA
Outsiders see the Scions as gentle, pious and old-fashioned, a people from another age who "do not seem to fit" in modern Nod-Krai. Fatui soldiers mocked them, among other things for serving no meat. Nefer half-jokingly calls Lauma stubborn and enterprising. The Scions' devotion and Lauma's standing are strong enough that the truth about their moon caused less upheaval than Paimon expected.

## secrets
### ULTRA
Lovia's crimes and the Sacred Heir Project lay sealed in the Court of the First Edict for 500 years, until the Traveler and Verna opened it. Only those of priestess rank or above are supposed to know Lovia's name. Before 6.0, Lauma alone among the Scions knew that the moon in the sky was false and that Kuutar was Columbina, hidden in Silvermoon Hall.

## status
@version: 6.3
### LITE
As of 7.1 the Scions still live in the Frostmoon Enclave under Lauma. Columbina lives among them as herself, not as a goddess to be petitioned. Older Scions still bow and keep their distance from her, but the children are curious.
### STANDARD +
After the 6.0 revelation some members left. Those who stayed have joined the League and are trying to stand on their own. Lauma holds the Eternal Moon Marrow. In a 6.3 world quest the Scions refurbished a statue of Columbina, adding wings, and Lauma invited her to a feast. No new Frostmoon Scions story was found in the 7.0-7.1 Snezhnaya Archon Quests. In 7.0 Columbina appears only to see the Traveler off from Nod-Krai.

## entry: Sacred bloodline and antler crowns
@keys: silver blood, antler crown, antlered crown, Sacred Heir, Sacred Heir Project
### STANDARD
The Scions treat their Hyperborean bloodline as sacred. Its marks are silver blood, sensitivity to kuuvahki, antler crowns (said to be the Frost Moon's last gift to the Hyperboreans) and an ability to communicate with all living things. Antlers grow under moonlight. Lauma's sensitivity is said to occur only once a century, and her silver blood can be mixed into medicine. The Sacred Heir Project was an old plan to keep the bloodline pure so that a prophesied heir would revive Hyperborea. After the fall, the Hyperborean genetic methods were lost, so it relied on controlled family lines. It was meant to be wound down by the seventh generation to avoid inbreeding, but later priestesses only intensified it.

## entry: Lovia, the last Archpriestess
@keys: Lovia, Archpriestess
@version: 6.0
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
About 500 years ago, Archpriestess Lovia was desperate for the Sacred Heir as kuuvahki faded. A maiden of exceptionally pure blood, who had been chosen to bear the heir, eloped with a Snezhnayan officer and gave birth to Avrelian. Lovia asked Rotwang to bring her back. The officer was killed, and the woman took her own life. Lovia then raised the twins Alia and Aila in isolation. To raise their purity, she had them kill other Scions and drink their blood. She also sought the Sampo Mill through Rotwang, and urged Aila to kill Solovei. Instead Aila killed Lovia, reportedly discovering that Lovia herself had no silver blood. Lovia was then struck from the record.

## entry: Aila, the first Moonchanter
@keys: Aila, first Moonchanter, Lady Aila
@version: 6.0
### LARGE
Aila became the first Moonchanter after killing Lovia. She abolished the Hyperborean rites and the Archpriestess office, rewrote hymns, and turned the faith toward the moonlight and toward kindness. With Solovei she ended an Abyssal calamity at the Pillar of Embla, and the two of them later sealed away the returned Rotwang. She gave the Lightkeepers the Arrow of Terpikeraunas. She witnessed Kuutar's birth, offered her the first drop of silver blood with Moonweaver's Dawn, and carved the Idol of the New Moon. She sealed the Court of the First Edict, with evidence of Lovia's crimes inside, for the Traveler to open 500 years later. When Solovei died, she declined to see the Reed Miller Brahe, who came to tell her the news.

## entry: Kuutar, the Moon Maiden
@keys: Kuutar, Moon Maiden, Statues of the New Moon, Statue of the New Moon
### STANDARD
Kuutar is the Scions' name for the moon goddess born on Hiisi Island 400-500 years ago, whom they hailed as the successor of the Frost Moon. They carved Statues of the New Moon in her likeness across Nod-Krai. Their constant, demanding prayers drove her away. She joined the Fatui as Columbina, the Damselette, and centuries later returned to hide on Hiisi Island. She has said that while she lived among the Scions, people went solemn and stiff whenever she appeared. Since 6.3 she has lived among them by choice. On one visit to the Enclave with the Traveler, she noticed that the Scions' greeting had changed to "May the moonlight connect us" and was glad that they had changed.

## entry: Vilemina's defection
@keys: Vilemina
@version: 6.0
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
Vilemina grew up with Lauma and came to resent what she saw as empty, inherited prayers. She joined the Fatui, followed the Traveler into the sealed chamber and stole the Moon Marrow for Marionette (Sandrone). When Lauma confronted her and admitted that the sky's moon was false, Vilemina asked what happens to those who betray the Scions. Lauma recited the punishment: their antlers are cut off with silver blades and they are cast into "the long night of unknowing". Vilemina cut off her own horns as a last act of respect, then told them where the Marrow had gone.

## entry: Moonchanter
@keys: Moonchanter, Lady Moonchanter
### STANDARD
Moonchanter is the title of the Frostmoon Scions' leader, created by Aila about 500 years ago to replace the Archpriest office. It is held now by Lauma, who was chosen at birth. The Moonchanter leads prayer and the community, heals, keeps the Moon Marrow and reads the true past with it. When the bloodline was stronger, one Moonchanter even sheltered the Moon Marrow inside her own body, though she could endure it for only two days. Lauma believed she could last five at most.

## entry: Sampo Mill and the Arrow of Terpikeraunas
@keys: Sampo Mill, SAMPO, Arrow of Terpikeraunas
@version: 6.0
### ULTRA
Records in the Sanctum of the Oathbearer say the SAMPO was a Hyperborean Mekhaneikos device. Its structure survived the fall, but its controls were destroyed and could not be rebuilt, so the Scions sealed it away to protect it. Over the generations it became a religious symbol, the "Sampo Mill", said to produce endless wealth and food and to bring about Elysium. The Scions did manage to recreate the Arrow of Terpikeraunas, which Aila later gave to Solovei and his Lightkeepers.

## entry: Hymns of the Far North and the Runo
@keys: Hymns of the Far North, Runo
### ULTRA
Runo are the ancient hymns that grounded Hyperborean lunar magic. The first incantations are lost, but they are said to be capable of stirring change. The book "Hymns of the Far North" preserves hymns and prayers from the old rites. It suggests the ancestors honoured not only the Frost Moon but also the Heavenly Principles, the Four Shades, Nibelung, Koitar, Anacharsis and Saarelainen with songs and sacrifices. Aila changed many of these hymns when she reformed the faith.
