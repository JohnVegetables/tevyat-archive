## summary
### LITE
The Church of Favonius is Mondstadt's church of the Anemo Archon Barbatos, the nation's patron of freedom. Its clergy pray, run morning prayers and choir, help the sick and answer letters from believers at the Favonius Cathedral in Mondstadt City. The Knights of Favonius manage it, and the two institutions work side by side. Its Seneschal is Seamus Pegg, Cardinal of Daybreak.
### STANDARD +
The Church is a community of devoted adherents more than a ruling body. Government belongs to the Knights, and the Archon himself does not rule. The Church supplies ritual, charity and a place to bring troubles. Its best-known members are the Deaconess Barbara, the Deacon Dahlia and the unorthodox Sister Rosaria.

## structure
### STANDARD
At the top is the Seneschal, Seamus Pegg, who also carries the title Cardinal of Daybreak. Cardinal Calvin ran the Church's affairs during Seamus' absence. Below them are deacons and a deaconess (Dahlia, Barbara), a Mother (Maria), and a body of nuns and sisters. Dahlia also leads the choir as cantor and holds the unique post of Herald of Barbatos. The clergy take turns at routine tasks, such as writing replies to letters from the congregation, and attend compulsory classes, hymn practice and morning prayers.
### LARGE +
Some posts are tied to one person. The wiki lists Barbara's title as Shining Idol, Rosaria's as A Nonconforming Sister and Vind's as Storm Watcher. Named nuns and sisters include Annette, Edna, Gotelinde, Grace, Hannelore, Jilliana, Philomena, Victoria and Ophila. Maria is called Mother in Rosaria's story. The Church holds sacred objects in the Cathedral, and the Deaconess is the one who safeguards them. Borrowing the Holy Lyre der Himmel needs written permission from three people: the Grand Master, the Seneschal and a community representative.

## leadership
### STANDARD
Seamus Pegg is Seneschal and the senior figure. He is a former famous adventurer born in Nod-Krai who settled in Mondstadt and joined the Church. He is the father of Jean (the Knights' Acting Grand Master) and Barbara (the Deaconess), though he and Jean's mother Frederica Gunnhildr are no longer together. He joined Varka's expedition and handled its paperwork, then came home to Mondstadt and the Cathedral once the Nod-Krai conflict wound down. He greets visitors with a blessing from the Anemo Archon and says he means to shoulder more of the Church's work.
### LARGE +
Cardinal Calvin took over Seamus' responsibilities while he was gone. Barbara says he is so busy that he almost never leaves his room, and that she last saw him in person when the Holy Lyre was stolen and he was distraught. Calvin has no voice lines of his own and is known only through others.

## members
### LITE
Notable members are Seamus Pegg (Seneschal), Cardinal Calvin, Dahlia (Deacon and Herald of Barbatos), Barbara (Deaconess and idol), Mother Maria and Sister Rosaria (who works alone).
### STANDARD +
- **Barbara**: cheerful, hardworking and loved by the whole city. She became a healer on her father's advice and sings to lift spirits. She admires her sister Jean and is unaware, according to the wiki, that she has met Barbatos.
- **Dahlia**: a young deacon with a love of "drama" and of meddling in other people's troubles. He is not especially devout. He knows Venti is Barbatos and relays news and feedback between the Archon and the people.
- **Rosaria**: the Church's least devout sister, who skips services and dodges overtime. Her real work is investigating suspected threats to Mondstadt, and she is trusted to do it.
- **Victoria**: a nun who looks after Barbara and worries when she goes missing. She asked Dahlia to write a letter for Rosaria's birthday.
### LARGE +
Other nuns seen in or around the Cathedral include Jilliana, Grace and Victoria, and the NPCs Lily and Rudolf in daytime, whose roles the wiki does not state. Ophila is a sister who scolds Rosaria for skipping hymn practice in her character story, and the same scene, addressed partly to Mother Maria, ends with Rosaria being asked whether she intends to do any work at all at the Church ("No. I've already found other work.").

## goals
### STANDARD
The Church worships the Anemo Archon and tries to keep Mondstadt's faith in freedom alive. It offers spiritual guidance, prayer and charity. Part of its role is practical: looking after the sick, answering the congregation's letters and giving people somewhere to bring their worries. It also cares for the sacred objects that belong to Barbatos.

## methods
### STANDARD
Daily life centres on morning prayer, hymn practice and choir, led by Dahlia. Clergy take turns answering letters, and sisters like Barbara use healing and song. Dahlia's approach is unusual: he has been known to settle quarrels by taking the parties to a tavern for a drinking contest, which Jean thinks may not be fair or just. Seekers can bring problems to him because many believe Barbatos listens to him.
### LARGE +
Rosaria's methods are quieter and harsher. After sunset she follows, questions and, if she must, kills people judged to be threats, and she does so as a sister of the Church. Her character stories say she learned this skill from her former bandit family, and that she accepts her work being done in secret. The Church also lends its authority at formal events, as when Deacon Dahlia sat as assessor at Albedo's trial in 5.6.

## history
### LARGE
**Old Mondstadt and the aristocrats.** Over the centuries the Church followed Mondstadt's rulers. Under the Lawrence-led aristocracy, wiki history based on in-world books says the Church was used to justify the nobles' actions and keep commoners from the truth, and that it split, with higher-ranked clergy backing the nobility.

**Vennessa's restoration.** About 1,000 years ago, after the rebellion, Vennessa founded the Knights of Favonius and restored the Church of Favonius, in the stated aim of keeping tyrants from taking power again. Believers mobilised by a "Nameless Shepherd" are said to have joined the rebellion.
### ULTRA +
**The Prologue.** In Venti's plan to borrow the Holy Lyre, the Church refused to release it without three signatures. With the Seneschal gone, Venti and the Traveler could not get them, so they snuck into the Cathedral basement at night. A Fatui agent got to the Lyre first. When it was later returned to Deaconess Barbara in worse condition, Venti used Anemo to make it look repaired.

**The expedition years.** Seamus went north with Varka. Calvin took on the Seneschal's work and became overloaded. The Church kept operating through the diligence of its priests and sisters. Barbara became a public idol, and her popularity forced her to hide from fans while collecting herbs for medicine.

**Return.** After Rerir fell in Nod-Krai (6.1), Seamus left the expedition for private matters. Back in Mondstadt, the Cathedral clergy cleaned in preparation for him. As of the sources checked, he is Seneschal and has said he wants to take on more work to ease others' burdens.

## relations
### STANDARD
- **Knights of Favonius**: manage the Church and work with it. Jean is Seamus' daughter. Adorno was buried at the Favonius Cathedral after dying in 6.6. Albedo's 5.6 trial involved both bodies.
- **Barbatos**: the Church's god. Venti is Barbatos, and Dahlia is the Herald who knows it.
- **Hexenzirkel**: Alice, an elder of the Hexenzirkel, coached Barbara on becoming an idol, a link that has no direct connection to Church doctrine.
### LARGE +
- **Rosaria and Varka**: Varka brought Rosaria to Mondstadt after the Knights wiped out her bandit crew and told her to be cleansed by the Archon's light at the Cathedral. She stayed in the clergy but kept to her own path.
- **Fatui**: a Fatui diplomat is seen at the Cathedral, and a Fatui agent stole the Holy Lyre during the Prologue.

## reputation
### STANDARD
Mondstadters love Barbara, so the Cathedral draws visitors who hope to see her. Many believe Dahlia has the Archon's favour. Rosaria is thought of as a lazy, chain-smoking sister, while few know of her secret work. The map description of the Cathedral muses on whether a city taught freedom by its Archon should spend so much wealth on such a grand building.
### LARGE +
Barbara says that even inside the church people come hoping to greet her, and in her hangout she hides from an admirer while gathering herbs at Wolvendom. Jean and others admire Barbara's effort. The Church's reputation for gentleness fits Barbara, but Rosaria and Dahlia show that the clergy is not as uniform as its image.

## secrets
### ULTRA
Rosaria's work as an investigator and killer is known to few. Dahlia knows that Venti is Barbatos, a secret kept from most of the clergy. Barbara has handled the Holy Lyre without realising the bard she met was the Archon.

## entry: Favonius Cathedral
@keys: Favonius Cathedral, Mondstadt Cathedral, Cathedral of Favonius
### STANDARD
The Favonius Cathedral, also called the Mondstadt Cathedral, stands in Mondstadt City and is run by the Church of Favonius. Clergy work and pray here, and its grand architecture resembles real-world Gothic cathedrals. A basement at the back holds the Holy Lyre der Himmel under guard. The Cathedral is a place of prayer, choir practice and counsel. The Traveler can meet nuns such as Jilliana, Grace and Victoria there, and Seamus Pegg in the later story.

## entry: Holy Lyre der Himmel
@keys: Holy Lyre der Himmel, Holy Lyre
@version: 1.0
### STANDARD
The Holy Lyre der Himmel is the lyre the Anemo Archon Barbatos once played. Its frame is rosewood, carved with a pattern of flowing wind, according to Venti. It is used in the Ludi Harpastum festival, and any other use requires signed documents from the Grand Master, the Seneschal and a community representative. The Church keeps it in the Cathedral basement. In the Prologue the Traveler and Venti obtained it, lost it to a Fatui agent, and later returned it to Barbara in damaged condition, which Venti hid with an Anemo illusion.

## entry: Seamus Pegg, Seneschal
@keys: Seamus Pegg, Seamus, Cardinal of Daybreak, Seneschal
@keys2: Church of Favonius, Barbara, Jean, Cathedral, Seneschal
### STANDARD
Seamus is the Church's Seneschal. He was an adventurer from Nod-Krai who met the wandering Varka abroad, persuaded him to return home and then settled in Mondstadt himself. He married Frederica Gunnhildr and had two daughters, Jean and Barbara. The marriage ended, and Barbara stayed with him. He dryly scolds Varka for his antics and handles most administrative work on his own. He saw Eroch's plot unfold, and he regretted the diplomatic cost to Mondstadt when Dottore took credit for killing Ursa. Seamus went on Varka's expedition, returned after the Nod-Krai conflict and now helps shoulder the Church's work.

## entry: Dahlia, Herald of Barbatos
@keys: Dahlia, Herald of Barbatos, Deacon Dahlia
@keys2: Church of Favonius, Barbatos, Venti, Cathedral, deacon
### STANDARD
Dahlia is a deacon of the Church and choir cantor who holds the post of Herald of Barbatos. He knows Venti is the Anemo Archon, passes news to him and brings his feedback to the people. He was a nosy helper before joining the clergy, and he is not especially religious. Venti helped create the Herald role so that he could contribute without being tied to dull routine. Dahlia is a quick learner who memorised hymns overnight when challenged. He took part in the Knights' plan around Albedo's trial in 5.6.

## entry: Barbara, Deaconess
@keys: Deaconess Barbara, Shining Idol
@keys2: Church of Favonius, Cathedral, Barbara
### STANDARD
Barbara is the Deaconess and a beloved idol. She joined the Church young, and her father Seamus served in it too. She has a Hydro Vision that appeared after she sang a lullaby to a feverish boy. Her father counselled her to become a healer. She safeguards religious artifacts and worships Barbatos, but the wiki states she is unaware she has met him. She sometimes hides from crowds to recover alone, and her story with Victoria shows how the nuns look after her.

## entry: Rosaria, the Nonconforming Sister
@keys: Rosaria, Sister Rosaria
@keys2: Church of Favonius, Cathedral, sister, nun
### STANDARD
Rosaria is a sister in name, famous for skipping classes, hymn practice and morning prayers. Raised by bandits and offered a second chance by Varka, she entered the Church and secretly works as its investigator of threats. She is unpredictable and disappears for days. Barbara keeps trying to bring her back to duties, and Dahlia once covered her letter-writing shift. She refuses overtime. The Church has never imposed serious consequences for her behaviour, and Rosaria has said she has found "other work".

## entry: Vennessa and the Church
@keys: Vennessa, Nameless Shepherd, schism
@keys2: Church, aristocracy, Knights of Favonius
@version: 7.1
### LARGE
Wiki history based on in-world books says the Church was bent to support the Lawrence aristocracy, and its higher clergy took the nobles' side in a schism. Adherents, mobilised by a figure called the Nameless Shepherd, joined Vennessa's rebellion. After victory, Vennessa founded the Knights and restored the Church, so that tyrants could not seize Mondstadt again. These accounts come from medium-confidence sources and some details remain inferred.

@keys2: Church of Favonius, Dahlia, Rosaria, Barbara
@availability: limited
### LARGE
Dahlia leads morning prayers, which the congregation uses to ask Barbatos for guidance. The clergy take turns answering letters. Rosaria, whose turn came up during a long absence, left a pile that Dahlia dealt with in her place. During the Weinlesefest event, Rosaria said she hid to avoid extra work and the sung poetry event, watching for Dahlia or Victoria. The Weinlesefest remark comes from a limited-time event.
