## summary
### LITE
The Grand Narukami Shrine is Inazuma's chief religious organisation. It worships the Electro Archon and leads the faith of the Narukami people from the largest shrine in the nation, on the summit of Mt. Yougou on Narukami Island, beside the ever-blooming Sacred Sakura. Its head is the Guuji, Yae Miko, a kitsune who is also the Shogun's friend and the owner of Yae Publishing House. Its shrine maidens wear white and red.
### STANDARD +
The shrine's everyday work is receiving worshippers, handing out fortune slips and omamori, performing rituals and ceremonies, and protecting the Sacred Sakura and its offshoots, the Thunder Sakura. Use of military force is forbidden on its grounds, because it would dishonour the Shogun, so the shrine stands outside the Shogunate's armed jurisdiction while still serving the Electro Archon.

## places
### LARGE
The shrine sits at the peak of Mt. Yougou. Pilgrims climb the long stairway and pass through many red torii gates; visitors favoured by Electro can ride Thunder Spheres up instead. The huge Sacred Sakura grows in the shrine grounds and is said to symbolise the eternity of Narukami. Its five-petal blossom appears on the shrine's buildings and on Yae Miko's clothing. Fortune slips are drawn from a box at the shrine maidens' stall, and a bad slip can be tied to a hanger in the hope of changing one's luck. Wishes can also be written on prayer plaques and hung on a prayer rack.
### ULTRA +
Beneath the mountain lie the Depths of Mt. Yougou, where the Sacred Sakura's central roots are cleansed. The abandoned Hakushin lands around Byakko Plain, at the foot of the mountain, once belonged to the kitsune clan that served the shrine. Miko uses them as a training ground in the Archon Quest.

## leadership
### LITE
Guuji Yae Miko leads the shrine. Worshippers and maidens call her "Lady Guuji" or "Lady Yae". She is solemn and mysterious in her ceremonial role and far more relaxed as head of her publishing house.
### STANDARD +
Miko says she never wanted to manage the shrine. The Kitsune Saiguu, her elder in the Hakushin line, persuaded her (partly by promising all the fried tofu she could eat) and then left on a "long trip" from which she never returned. The Guuji must attend every ceremony in full dress, which Miko finds the most tiresome part of the job.
### LARGE +
Earlier leaders were kitsune and their companions. Hakushin, matriarch of the Hakushin Clan, managed the shrine with her six apprentices. About 500 years ago it was led by the Kitsune Saiguu together with Reizenbou, the Great Tengu and chieftain of the Yougou Tengu, who became the Saiguu's bodyguard. After the Saiguu was lost in the cataclysm, the human shrine maiden Asase Hibiki is said by the wiki (without a cited source) to have taken over, before she returned to Asase Shrine on Seirai Island.

## structure
### STANDARD
Below the Guuji serve the shrine maidens. The Kuki Clan has supplied them for generations, and other old families such as the Inagi serve too. Newcomers start with simple tasks such as cleaning. Specific duties include greeting worshippers and handing out fortune slips, looking after particular people (Kano Nana is responsible for finding the Shuumatsuban ninja Sayu when she slips away), and being sent out on errands such as investigations. Toda acts as liaison between the shrine and the Yashiro Commission, handling fund-raising and manpower.
### LARGE +
Miko describes a ladder of ranks. A trainee becomes a Chokkai after completing the required studies and learning to chant correctly for ceremonies, which she calls the hardest step for kitsune. A Guuji's Assistant, or Seikai, must endure strict restrictions. The honorary rank of Joukai has only ever been held by the Guuji of the Grand Narukami Shrine. According to the wiki, most shrine maidens in Inazuma (outside Watatsumi Island) train at the Grand Narukami Shrine and stay affiliated with it, and their care of smaller shrines keeps them aligned with it.

## members
### STANDARD
- **Yae Miko**: Guuji and head of the shrine.
- **Miyuki**: a Kuki Clan shrine maiden, Kuki Shinobu's older sister. She is fiercely eager for Lady Yae's approval and a little jealous of Kano Nana.
- **Kano Nana**: shrine maiden, novel enthusiast and Miko's favourite at the shrine. She is Shikanoin Heizou's older distant cousin and is forever chasing Sayu.
- **Inagi Hotomi**: shrine maiden who greets worshippers coming for fortune slips. She descends from Inagi Misato, a shrine maiden who was the Kitsune Saiguu's friend 500 years ago.
- **Maki**: shrine maiden at the fortune-slip stall who explains the Sacred Sakura to visitors.
- **Enyashi Yukari**: a nervous newcomer who keeps the prayer-plaque area tidy.
- **Toda**: the Yashiro Commission liaison, usually found at the Kamisato Estate.
- **Gendou Ringo**: the curt maiden who hands out fortune slips. See the entry below for who she really is.
### LARGE +
Kuki Shinobu is a former trainee shrine maiden. Her parents sent her to the shrine as a child after Miyuki. She came to doubt the shrine's unexplained rules, received a Vision when she left, and is determined never to be dragged back to being a shrine maiden. She wears a mask so that people she knows will not recognise her while she works with the Arataki Gang. Miyuki first meant to talk her out of leaving but, seeing the Vision, helped persuade their parents to let her go.

## goals
### STANDARD
The shrine exists to serve and honour the Electro Archon, guide the people's worship, and keep the Sacred Sakura healthy, which in turn protects Inazuma from accumulated filth. Its maidens describe everything there as being done according to the Archon's will. Under Miko, the shrine also quietly works to protect Ei's path to Eternity and Inazuma's stability, sometimes in ways the Shogunate itself does not see.

## methods
### LARGE
The shrine's tools are ceremony, purification and prestige rather than force. Maidens perform exorcisms on request; the disciples of the swordmaster Domon hired them, for example, when he seemed possessed after losing his Vision. They also run the Sacred Sakura Cleansing Ritual, sell omamori and fortune slips, and host festivals and ceremonies such as the Goshintou Ceremony, at which master smiths offer swords to the shrine. The ban on armed force at the shrine makes it a refuge: Miko sheltered the wanted Traveler there and faced down Kujou Sara by reminding her of that rule.
### ULTRA +
Miko also uses the shrine's authority for her own ends. When she needed money to found Yae Publishing House, she took over the old Fire-Soothing Festival Committee. That committee had been chaired by the Tenryou Commission and run by the shrine, with the proceeds split forty-sixty. She turned the festival into a light novel contest and diverted the committee's budget into the shrine's coffers, arguing to Tenryou Commissioner Kujou Takayuki that literature also pleases the Archon.

## history
### LARGE
**Origins.** The shrine is very old. Hakushin, the kitsune matriarch, served there with her apprentices, and the wiki reads New Chronicles of the Six Kitsune as placing it before the Archon War. Over centuries the Hakushin kitsune were its keepers. Miko says the Sacred Sakura owes its foxlike shape to centuries of their care.

**The cataclysm era.** About 500 years ago the Kitsune Saiguu, worshipped as a kitsune goddess, led the shrine with the Great Tengu Reizenbou. The young Yae Miko had arrived shortly before the cataclysm. A circle of mortals gathered around the mountain: the shrine maiden Asase Hibiki, Inagi Misato, and the "Yougou Three" (Hibiki, Kamuna Harunosuke and Mikoshi Nagamasa), who trained under the Yougou Tengu. When Makoto and Ei went to Khaenri'ah, the Saiguu stayed to defend Inazuma and was consumed by the darkness. Reizenbou went into self-imposed exile, the Yougou Three parted ways (the wiki says, without a source, that Hibiki ran the shrine for a time before returning to Asase Shrine), and the Hakushin lands at the foot of the mountain were abandoned. Echoes of the Saiguu's memory later formed Hanachirusato, who kept the Cleansing Ritual alive and once persuaded the returning Kamuna to perform it.

**The Sakoku era (2.0-2.1).** While the Vision Hunt Decree and Sakoku Decree were in force, worshippers prayed for the closure to be lifted because of their economic hardship. Miko watched the Traveler from the start of the Inazuma Archon Quest. In Omnipresence Over Mortals, she rescued them from the Balladeer, brought them back to the shrine, trained them against the Shogun, and used the shrine to meet Kujou Sara. There she gave Sara proof that Kujou Takayuki had betrayed the Shogunate, and gave the Traveler an omamori. She later revealed that she had paid the Balladeer the Electro Gnosis, which Ei had entrusted to her, for the Traveler's life. After Ei abolished the Sakoku Decree, worshippers came instead to give thanks and pray for business and safe travels.
### ULTRA +
**Restoring the Sakura.** The shrine was preparing a major cleansing of the Sacred Sakura when Miko called it off. She had Inagi Hotomi keep the Memento Lens ready for a "blond-haired outlander", and the Traveler completed the ritual with Hanachirusato. At about the same time, Miko sent Miyuki to Tatarasuna, where the shrine had ordered the evacuation of the Mikage Furnace as the Tatarigami grew out of control, and then to investigate the ailing Thunder Sakura of Kannazuka. There Miyuki uncovered Treasure Hoarders and Fatui draining their sap.

**The tree's true origin.** In Ei's later story quest (Radiant Sakura), Ei explains that the Sacred Sakura grew from a seed Makoto gave her in the realm of consciousness, which took root retroactively in Inazuma's past. Everyone, Miko included, remembers it as always having been there, and Ei could not convince Miko otherwise.

**Youkai and the shrine.** In Miko's story quest, the Divina Vulpes Chapter, the shrine was the site of a farewell rite for the lingering memories of the youkai Urakusai and his companions, with Miko guiding the spirits and the Traveler clearing the mountain path.

## relations
### LARGE
- **Electro Archon**: the shrine worships her. Ei and Miko are old friends, and Ei entrusted Miko with the Electro Gnosis before entering the Plane of Euthymia.
- **Yashiro Commission and Kamisato Clan**: the Commission manages religious and ceremonial affairs, and Kano Nana says shrine maidens must follow its orders. Its ninja Sayu is looked after by a shrine maiden. Miko once saved the Kamisato Clan from dismissal after a scandal over the Raiden Gokaden, leaving them with a debt of loyalty to the Shogun's path.
- **Tenryou Commission and Kujou Clan**: Tenryou once chaired the Fire-Soothing Festival Committee the shrine ran, until Miko took its budget. Kujou Sara visits the shrine, and Miko finds her incessant questions about Ei tiresome.
- **Watatsumi Island**: a separate faith with its own shrine maidens in blue. The Raiden Shogun says Sangonomiya destroyed the local Narukami shrine on the island.
### ULTRA +
- **Shuumatsuban and the Fatui**: the fortune-slip maiden "Gendou Ringo" was in fact a Shuumatsuban agent, and later the Fatuus Lyudochka Snezhevna took over the role. Miko allowed her to stay.
- **Yae Publishing House**: Miko's business, funded partly through shrine resources. Kano Nana reads its drafts.
- **Kuki Clan**: a generational shrine-maiden family. Kuki Shinobu broke with that tradition and ran away from home, though Miyuki helped persuade their parents to let her go.

## reputation
### LARGE
Ordinary Inazumans revere the shrine and climb the mountain for blessings and fortune slips, and visitors from abroad are told they too will be blessed if they believe. Its head inspires a different feeling: few people have ever "figured out" Miko, and some, such as Thoma and Gorou, are openly wary of her. In the shrine, Miko's solemn manner at ceremonies awes the junior maidens.

## secrets
### ULTRA
Several things about the shrine are known to very few. The Guuji held the Electro Gnosis for Ei and traded it to the Balladeer. Miko knew that the Shogun in Tenshukaku was a puppet and that Ei herself dwelt in the Plane of Euthymia. The fortune-slip maiden was an undercover agent, though Inagi Hotomi says everyone at the shrine "more or less" knew. Only Ei remembers that the Sacred Sakura did not always exist.

## entry: Sacred Sakura Cleansing Ritual
@keys: Sacred Sakura Cleansing Ritual, Cleansing Ritual, Yougou Cleansing
@keys2: Sacred Sakura, Thunder Sakura, Grand Narukami Shrine, Hanachirusato, shrine
### STANDARD
The Sacred Sakura and its grafted offshoots, the Thunder Sakura, purify Inazuma by drawing filth into their roots. The roots are all connected, so filth flows toward the central roots beneath Mt. Yougou. It cannot dissipate on its own, so the Grand Narukami Shrine normally performs a Cleansing Ritual. Minor cleansings check the roots and set barriers that protect the roots from people and people from the filth. A major cleansing is due every 60 years. The barriers are broken with wards, the filth condenses into "defilement incarnate" (harmless in peaceful times, dangerous in troubled ones) and is destroyed, and finally the tumour at the central roots is purged. Records say that when the ritual once lapsed, centuries ago, monsters ran rampant and the seas turned black. The most recent major cleansing was done by the Traveler and Hanachirusato after Miko cancelled the shrine's own preparations.

## entry: Gendou Ringo
@keys: Gendou Ringo, fortune slip maiden
### LARGE
"Gendou Ringo" is the name of the curt shrine maiden who exchanges bamboo slips for fortune slips at the Grand Narukami Shrine. The name was a cover: the first person using it was a Shuumatsuban agent (later codenamed Momoyo), and in The Very Special Fortune Slip the Fatuus Lyudochka Snezhevna took over the role and became far friendlier to visitors. The other maidens noticed she "seems like a different person", but trust her because Lady Yae let her stay.

## entry: Shrine maiden ranks
@keys: Chokkai, Seikai, Joukai
### LARGE
According to Yae Miko, a trainee shrine maiden earns the title Chokkai once she has finished her studies and can chant correctly at ceremonies. A Guuji's Assistant (Seikai) must keep many strict restrictions; Miko's advice is to snack at night and not get caught. The honorary rank Joukai has only ever been held by the Guuji of the Grand Narukami Shrine.

## entry: Fire-Soothing Festival Committee
@keys: Fire-Soothing Festival, Fire-Soothing Festival Committee
### ULTRA
The Fire-Soothing Festival Committee was set up long ago by the Grand Narukami Shrine at the Tenryou Commission's request, to calm a city of wooden houses and pray for a year without fires. Shrine maidens danced and the proceeds were split forty-sixty between the Commission and the shrine. Wanting funds for Yae Publishing House, Yae Miko turned the festival into a light novel submission contest, moved the committee's budget to her publishing house and sent the profits to the shrine. When Kujou Takayuki came to protest, she told him that writing and scholarship also please the Archon, and he left without success.

## entry: Hakushin kitsune and the shrine
@keys: Hakushin Clan, Hakushin Kitsune, Hakushin lands
@keys2: shrine, Grand Narukami Shrine, Yae Miko, Kitsune Saiguu
### LARGE
The Hakushin Clan was a prominent, influential kitsune lineage in Inazuma that managed the shrines around Mt. Yougou. Its matriarch Hakushin ran the Grand Narukami Shrine with six apprentices. The Kitsune Saiguu and Yae Miko both descend from her line. After the Saiguu's death in the cataclysm, the clan's lands at the foot of the mountain were left abandoned, and statues of Earth and Sky Kitsune still wait for her return.
