## summary
### LITE
"Fatui Harbinger" is the title of the eleven top officers of the Fatui, the Tsaritsa's organisation that serves Snezhnaya as both diplomats and military. Each Harbinger heads a division, answers to the Tsaritsa, carries a Delusion and a commedia dell'arte codename (Pierro, Capitano, Dottore and so on), and holds a numbered seat that reflects combat strength. The Fatui's Director, Pierro, leads them. As of 7.1 several seats are dead, resigned or vacant.
### STANDARD +
Harbingers are the Fatui's executive heads, given god-like authority and ability-boosting Delusions. In the first six Archon Quest chapters Harbingers were sent to other nations to obtain their Gnoses. A Harbinger is a person of real political weight in Snezhnaya, not just a field commander.

## definition
### LITE
A Harbinger is chosen from the Fatui's eleven most powerful members. On appointment they receive a new name and title, a rank number tied to combat power, and a Delusion. Appointments come from the Tsaritsa, either in person at Zapolyarny Palace or relayed by the Director. Names and titles can be inherited from a previous holder (there have been two Knaves).
### STANDARD +
The Director of the Fatui, Pierro, sits above the ranked Harbingers: he assigns missions, calls meetings and chairs votes. He is often still called a Harbinger, but canon never gives him a number. Codenames come from commedia dell'arte stock characters, and the English game uses the codename and the title almost interchangeably.
### LARGE +
Etymology of the codenames as noted by the wiki: Pierro (Pierrot, the sad fool), Capitano (the boastful captain), Dottore (the pompous doctor), Columbina (the servant, "little dove"), Arlecchino (Harlequin, a trickster servant), Pulcinella (the self-serving rooster), Scaramouche (the unscrupulous schemer), Sandrone (the cunning peasant), Signora (the proud lady), Pantalone (the greedy merchant), and Tartaglia (the stutterer). Pierro and Scaramouche use the French forms rather than the Italian Pedrolino and Scaramuccia. This is wiki commentary on real-world theater, not in-world fact.

## rank_and_seats
### STANDARD
Seat numbers reflect combat power. They are not automatically reassigned when a higher seat empties, and a seat can stay vacant indefinitely; open-world dialogue from Mikhail and Lyudmila confirms this. The most recent holders of the top three seats could rival gods. A Harbinger's first appearance in the game is marked by a dramatic title card with their name, title and rank.
### LARGE +
In Etched in Time Paimon jokes about "The Traveler, Twelfth of the Fatui Harbingers"; the Traveler declines because it would rank them below Tartaglia, and Tartaglia later teases them with the same idea. This is banter, not an offer. Wanderer's character stories say he joined the Harbingers' "wild, frenzied banquet" and won the sixth seat, and that Dottore unlocked a seal on Scaramouche's body, raising his power to match the lower-ranked Harbingers; the banquet is implied to decide placement but is not explained.

## authority_and_conduct
### STANDARD
Harbingers have wide autonomy, even to act against central policy, so each has their own methods and outlook that do not necessarily represent Snezhnaya's official stance (the Director says as much in Mistilteinn). Their limits: the Tsaritsa forbids them to fight each other, though she can lift the ban, and Dottore was censured for ignoring a recall order and for trying to seize the power of the Three Moons. Harbingers can trade insults freely.
### LARGE +
National-level risks require a unanimous vote of all active Harbingers chaired by the Director, excluding incapacitated members; in 7.1 this opened Hroptatyr's sealed notebook without Capitano. Harbingers command divisions and troops in the nations they visit: Tartaglia welcomed new recruits in Liyue as the only Harbinger there, and Signora's Delusion Factory in Inazuma passed to Scaramouche when he arrived. Per Capitano's orders, a Harbinger is responsible for the families of subordinates who die in service. Many Harbingers are rich and influential: Pantalone is described as the wealthiest and rented the Goth Grand Hotel in Mondstadt; Tartaglia's savings at the Northland Bank are said to boggle the mind, and his Harbinger status kept the Fatui diplomats from handing him over to the Liyue Qixing after the Osial incident; the Northland Bank paid heavy reparations instead. Harbinger fame can shape Snezhnayan popular taste: Tartaglia's interest in a fishing line stabilizer made it a sought-after item. Most Fatui respect Harbingers, and some act without orders for them or seek revenge when one falls.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
The title carries fear and prestige abroad and ordinary bureaucratic weight at home. Fatui soldiers address Harbingers with deference but not uniformly; devotion varies by commander. Do not assume Harbingers are a unified bloc: they often disagree, compete and loathe each other, and each has a distinct agenda.
### LARGE +
Remember the time frame. Early-game scenes treat the Harbingers as a feared set of eleven; by 7.1 the group is down to six active members plus a depleted Director. A character should not speak of Scaramouche as a living member of the group unless they are someone who remembers him.

## chronology
### ULTRA
Harbingers have been part of the story since 1.0, when Signora took the Anemo Gnosis in the Prologue and Tartaglia appeared in Liyue. Each of the first six Archon Quest chapters sent a Harbinger or more to take a nation's Gnosis. Scaramouche's erasure from Irminsul came in 3.3, Dottore's recognition of the Wanderer in 6.6, and the unanimous vote and Pierro's use of the upgraded Delusion in 7.1. The Pale Flame and Scarlet Proof artifact sets first described the backstories of several Harbingers.

## mistakes
### STANDARD
Do not call the Director the Tenth Harbinger, or invent a Tenth. Do not say seats shift up when someone leaves. Do not treat Columbina or Scaramouche as active. Harbingers are not the Tsaritsa's equals and are not all villains in the same way; they differ in motive. Do not use the Fatui as a synonym for the Harbingers, since most Fatui are ordinary soldiers and staff.
