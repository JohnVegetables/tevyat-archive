## summary
### LITE
"Iudex" is the title of Fontaine's Chief Justice, the senior judge of the Court of Fontaine. The Iudex presides at the Opera Epiclese, leads the Marechaussee Phantom and has an office in the Palais Mermonia. Neuvillette has held it for more than four hundred years and is seen as the symbol of impartiality.
### STANDARD +
Neuvillette calls himself both "Chief Justice" and "Iudex", and English uses the two interchangeably. It is a working judicial and administrative post, not a ceremonial rank.
### LARGE +
After Archon Quest Chapter IV (4.2) the office carries more weight in practice: Furina gave up the Hydro Archon's role, and Neuvillette, still Iudex, handles the nation's affairs as well as the courtroom.

## definition
### LITE
The Iudex sits in the judge's seat of the Opera Epiclese on Erinnyes during trials. To the public the job is wearing a wig, keeping order in court and delivering the Oratrice's verdict.
### STANDARD +
Neuvillette's profile says there is also much private administrative work behind the scenes to keep the justice system running. The Palais Mermonia, at the highest point of the Court of Fontaine, houses the Iudex's office and is treated as the highest authority in the land.
### LARGE +
Because Neuvillette has been the only Iudex for so long, people speculate that divine favour gave him an unnaturally long life, and offer many theories about what he is (a familiar of the Hydro Archon, a Hydro elemental creature). He has never commented.

## powers_and_limits
### STANDARD
The Iudex may prosecute and enforce the law in person, a legacy of chaotic early days that is now mostly symbolic. When the Iudex investigates or prosecutes, another judge must take the seat; Neuvillette has always followed this rule. He must also honor the Oratrice's verdict whatever he personally believes.
### LARGE +
In As Light Rain Falls Without Reason the Oratrice found Tartaglia guilty and the verdict stood, though Neuvillette and Furina were baffled. In A Tea Party Most Thorny Neuvillette tells Arlecchino the Fortress of Meropide is outside his jurisdiction.
### ULTRA +
Other judges of the Palais Mermonia exist. Charlotte says in An Overnight Fantasyland that the Palais has more than one judge and Neuvillette cannot handle every case, so some are assigned elsewhere from the start. The Iudex presided over high-profile cases such as Lyney's trial and Furina's.

## duties_and_roles
### LARGE
The Iudex leads the Marechaussee Phantom, Fontaine's detective force made up mostly of Melusines. Neuvillette has personally investigated when a vulnerable person such as a Melusine is threatened (Kiara in The Remains of the Past Day). He wrote the legal clause that a Melusine is addressed as "she", not "it". Asked why he secured human rights for Melusines, he replied that it was not up to him, as they prefer being around humans.
### ULTRA +
He keeps the public at a distance on purpose, since close personal ties could raise doubts about the justness of his judgments. His greeting asks to be called by his surname for that reason, and a later voice-over has him answer doubts about his palate with the line that he is the Iudex and does not lie. A profile story quotes him saying that Iudex is simply one of the hats he wears.

## history_of_the_office
@version: 4.1
### STANDARD
About four hundred years ago Neuvillette became Iudex, after an unsigned letter from the Hydro Archon Focalors invited him to Fontaine with the promise of a seat with the best view in the greatest theater. That seat proved to be in the front row of the Opera Epiclese.
### LARGE +
He took the post to understand humans and himself. Newly appointed, he brought the Melusines into Fontainian society, where they were mistrusted. His Melusine aide Carole was framed and died, and her friend Vautrin carried out a revenge killing; Neuvillette sentenced Vautrin to the Fortress of Meropide.
### ULTRA +
Later sentences by him include Sigewinne (for the forced change of her species), Wriothesley and Escoffier. He watched the duel between Callas and Clorinde and did not stop it, which is why Navia resents him.

## the_4_2_turning_point
@version: 4.2
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
Chapter IV's finale reveals the Iudex is also the reborn Hydro Dragon, one of the Seven Sovereigns. At Furina's trial the Oratrice sentenced the "Hydro Archon" to death; Focalors executed herself, the Hydro authority returned to him, and he pardoned Fontainians of the original sin. Furina abdicated, and Neuvillette now runs the nation while remaining Iudex.
### ULTRA +
His profile says the role of Chief Justice is as much his true self as the dragon is his essence; only the role of ordinary human is one he must play. The Traveler learned his nature in Calamitous Tread and was asked to keep it secret. The Eleven Fatui Harbingers know it, and Wriothesley strongly implies he knows too. The profile suggests ordinary Fontainians never understood why the throne shattered or why they needed forgiveness.

## status
@version: 7.1
### STANDARD
As of 7.1 Neuvillette is still Iudex. No other Iudex or successor is named in canon.
### LARGE +
In Birds in a Cage Arlecchino agrees to withdraw the House of the Hearth's forces from Fontaine, in return for the Gardes ignoring former operatives. His default outfit, Clear Adjudication, is the garb of the Iudex; its description says the complicated design hinders movement but suits his standing.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Treat the Iudex as a public office with strict propriety. Characters call him "Monsieur Neuvillette" or "Iudex" with respect and some awe, as the man who never wavers or lies.
### LARGE +
His Hydro Dragon nature is a secret until Calamitous Tread and the 4.2 finale, and the profile suggests the public never learned the full story. Treat it as hidden unless a character was told or present.

## mistakes
### STANDARD
Do not call Neuvillette the Hydro Archon: the wiki says he has taken up the affairs of the title, and no further Hydro Archons are expected. Do not invent earlier Iudices, elections or term limits. Do not say the Iudex alone rules Fontaine, overrules the Oratrice or tries every case.
