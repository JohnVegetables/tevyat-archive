## summary
### LITE
Guuji (head priest or priestess of a shrine) is the title of the person who runs the Grand Narukami Shrine, Inazuma's main place of worship of the Electro Archon. The present Guuji is the kitsune Yae Miko, so the word is often used for her directly, as in "Lady Guuji" or "Guuji Yae". She is also editor-in-chief of Yae Publishing House.
### STANDARD +
The Guuji answers for the shrine's rites and for protecting the Sacred Sakura, and must attend every shrine ceremony. Miko says the Kitsune Saiguu, the older kitsune who mentored her, persuaded her to take the post shortly before leaving and never returning. The shrine answers to the Electro Archon rather than to the Shogunate.
### LARGE +
Miko treats the title as both an honour and a nuisance, and uses its authority for her own amusement as well as for serious ends. Outsiders have seen it used at a decisive moment, as when she intervened to spare the Kamisato clan.

## holder
### LITE
Yae Miko, a kitsune of the Hakushin lineage, is the Guuji today and is described as the shrine's most experienced member.
### STANDARD +
Miko was a young kitsune about 500 years ago, living at the shrine under the Kitsune Saiguu. She recalls that the Saiguu announced a long trip and never came back, and that she herself did not want the burden of managing the shrine. The Saiguu argued that as Guuji she could eat all the fried tofu she liked. Miko is also a long-standing friend and servant of Ei, the Raiden Shogun, and speaks to her frankly. Shrine maidens such as Kano Nana serve under her.
### LARGE +
Before Miko, the Kitsune Saiguu mentored the shrine maiden Asase Hibiki and was a central figure at the shrine until she vanished around the cataclysm. Whether the Saiguu formally bore the title Guuji, and who led the shrine in the years between, is not settled in the sources read (see uncertainties). Miko's own voice-over is the only direct account of how she came to hold the office.
### ULTRA +
Miko's Character Story about the festival adds a wry detail: she says that when she was asked to become Guuji nobody mentioned she would only be able to sneak out to watch the festivities from then on. The word itself is a real-world Shinto term for a shrine's chief priest; the archive uses it only as the in-game title.

## duties_and_ranks
### STANDARD
The Guuji heads a religious body that leads Narukami faith, tends the Sacred Sakura and the Thunder Sakura, assists visitors who pray to the Electro Archon and performs ceremonies such as the Sacred Sakura Cleansing Ritual. Military force is not permitted around the shrine, so it stands outside the Shogunate's jurisdiction while remaining under the Electro Archon. In one of Miko's Character Stories the Guuji must sit solemnly through every festival ceremony, which she finds the most tiresome part of the job.
### LARGE +
In voice-overs about shrine training, Miko names ranks in ascending order: Chokkai (studies completed and able to chant correctly), Seikai (the Guuji's assistant), Meikai (understanding the workings of the world, usually with a Vision and a long life) and Joukai. She says only the Guuji of the Grand Narukami Shrine has ever held Joukai, that it is honorary, and that it has nothing to do with ability.

## scene_use
### STANDARD
Use "Guuji" as a respectful or official form of address for Miko at the shrine, often as "Lady Guuji". She uses the title with irony as well as pride. It is not a government office: her Character Stories describe her as someone who usually does not meddle in governance.

### STANDARD
Guuji of the Grand Narukami Shrine is one of Yae Miko's official titles. At the shrine she keeps a mysterious air, while with friends or at Yae Publishing House she is blunter and more mischievous.

## entry: Kitsune Saiguu and the succession
@keys: Kitsune Saiguu, Hakushin Kitsune, Lady Saiguu
@version: 2.5
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
The Kitsune Saiguu ("fox high priestess") was a Hakushin descendant who lived at the Grand Narukami Shrine, mentored the shrine maiden Hibiki and doted on the young Miko. She disappeared around the time of the cataclysm. Miko recalls that she was asked to take over managing the shrine and that the Saiguu never returned.
