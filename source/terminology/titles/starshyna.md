## summary
### LITE
Starshyna is the title of the leader of the Lightkeepers, the volunteer order that defends Nod-Krai from the Wild Hunt. The first Starshyna was the order's founder, Solovei the Torchforger. As of 7.1 the Starshyna is Nikita, who took the post after his predecessor Sigurd fell fighting the Wild Hunt about a decade earlier.
### STANDARD +
The Lightkeepers' headquarters are at Piramida, and the Starshyna commands from there. Under the Starshyna come the Sergeant Majors (sometimes called Captains), the Squad Leaders and the rank-and-file Ratniki.
### LARGE +
The word is Russian in origin (the real-world rank roughly means sergeant major) and fits Nod-Krai's Snezhnaya-influenced naming. In English the term is used as both title and form of address, as in "Starshyna Nikita" or Flins's talk of "the legendary Starshyna, Sigurd".

## definition
### LITE
The Starshyna is the single head of the Lightkeepers. The post is a leadership one: the holder directs the order's response to Wild Hunt incursions and is the person the Ratniki ultimately answer to.
### LARGE +
The Lightkeepers are described as an organization made up entirely of volunteers. The Starshyna leads volunteers who have sworn the Lightkeepers' oath, not conscripts, which explains why members can retire or leave and why shortage of manpower is a constant problem. Nikita's uniform is a modified Lightkeeper coat in purple and black with silver ornaments, a black ushanka and a special brooch on the left lapel that marks his rank as Starshyna. A similar lapel brooch marks Squad Leaders, so the brooch signals rank in general rather than the Starshyna alone.

## holders
### LITE
Known holders: Solovei the Torchforger (first), Sigurd (previous) and Nikita (current). The wiki also lists the poet Sergei Semyonovich Mayakov as a historical holder.
### STANDARD +
Solovei founded the order about five hundred years ago, after the Cataclysm, and was appointed its first Starshyna. Sigurd, remembered as a formidable and brave Starshyna who fought to the very end, was lost in a great battle with the Wild Hunt about a decade before the present day. Nikita inherited the post from him.
### LARGE +
Nikita has said he once fought at Sigurd's side, and he admits that the Starshyna before him would have lived up to a visitor's expectations of the title better than he does. The surviving Lightkeepers built an empty grave for Sigurd near Barrowmoss Barrens, and the Hidden Exploration Objective Secrets of the Tideseal Stones leads there.
### ULTRA +
Nothing is told of the holders between Solovei and Sigurd. The collection Tales Carried by the Northwind, found in Piramida, is described as a poetry collection chronicling the deeds of successive Starshyna; most of it was lost in past calamities. It carries verses credited to Silvestr Petrovich Solovei and to Sergei Semyonovich Mayakov. A surviving Mayakov passage pictures an aged man facing dark towering waves, with a voice accusing him of letting young men die. Another passage speaks of generations that inherited "this name" through the ages.

## duties_and_roles
@version: 6.3
### STANDARD
In practice the Starshyna is a commander and a figurehead. Nikita leads the Lightkeepers from their headquarters in Piramida; per the wiki he rebuilt the order after Sigurd's fall.
### LARGE +
Every Ratnik must carry an Oath Lantern, and Flins explains that the Starshyna (together with other Lightkeepers) can detect it. Flins jokes that if his own lantern could not be detected, the higher-ups would foist a standard-issue lantern on him. Sigurd's former belongings, now the artifact set Long Night's Oath, and the Arrow of Terpikeraunas given to Solovei by Aila are both bound up with the order's identity.
### ULTRA +
The order's rank structure is Starshyna, then Sergeant Majors, then Squad Leaders, then Ratniki. Some Lightkeepers view the headquarters as haughty because the few staff at Piramida can seldom relay information to the field. This is a remark about headquarters in general, and no text blames the Starshyna personally.

## history_of_the_title
@version: 5.5
### LARGE
The Lightkeepers were established by a royal edict of the Cryo Archon Anastasya shortly after the Cataclysm, approving Solovei's request for jurisdiction within Nod-Krai as an autonomous region. Solovei was appointed the order's first Starshyna. Illuga says the Snezhnayan tundra is visible from Piramida's highest point and that it was the homeland of "our first Starshyna, Torchforger Solovei", who lost everything and found a place to belong. A plaque below his statue in Piramida carries a Latin line about tearing the snowclad veils and giving the wind to the wilderness.
### ULTRA +
Solovei's own story: a Snezhnayan noble of a house that used a nightingale as its symbol, he came to Nod-Krai during the Cataclysm and fought the Abyss with Aila's help. The nightingale remains the Lightkeepers' traditional insignia, shown as a bird holding a burning wick in its beak. His biography belongs in his own entry.

## status
@version: 7.1
### STANDARD
As of 7.1 Nikita is the Starshyna. Canon names no successor, and no change of holder after his is recorded.
### LARGE +
The Lightkeepers are described as severely weakened by past battles. Nikita's adoptive son Illuga leads a squad, and Flins serves as a Ratnik, both under the Starshyna's authority.

## roleplay
### LARGE
Treat "Starshyna" as a respectful title for the order's leader. Lightkeepers address Nikita by it; Illuga also calls him "old man" in the Tideseal Stones objective. Mention Sigurd with respect: Nod-Krai remembers him as a legend, and Nikita himself says Sigurd would have met expectations of a Starshyna better than he does. Do not make the Starshyna a grand ruler. Nod-Krai was granted autonomy as a region, and the Starshyna leads an order of volunteers, not a government.

## mistakes
### LARGE
Do not call Nikita the founder or the first Starshyna: that is Solovei. Do not say Sigurd was merely a Ratnik without noting the disagreement, and do not treat the Starshyna as hereditary, elected or term-limited, since canon says nothing about how it is filled. Do not put the Starshyna in charge of Nod-Krai as a whole, only the Lightkeepers.

### STANDARD
Starshyna is the title of the Lightkeepers' leader. Solovei the Torchforger was the first; Sigurd held it before falling to the Wild Hunt about a decade ago; Nikita holds it now (as of 7.1). Command is exercised from Piramida over Sergeant Majors, Squad Leaders and Ratniki.
### LARGE +
Ratniki carry Oath Lanterns that other Lightkeepers and the Starshyna can detect. The order is entirely volunteers.

## entry: Succession from Sigurd to Nikita
@keys: Sigurd, previous Starshyna, Starshyna before Nikita
@keys2: Nikita, Starshyna, Lightkeepers
@version: 6.0
### STANDARD
Sigurd was lost in a great battle with the Wild Hunt about a decade ago, a battle in which Illuga's village was also destroyed. His empty grave lies northeast of Barrowmoss Barrens. Nikita, who had fought beside him, took the title and rebuilt the order. In the Hidden Exploration Objective Secrets of the Tideseal Stones, Flins hears of the legendary Starshyna Sigurd and his longsword.
### LARGE +
Sigurd's spirit remains as a corrupted fighter in the Abyss, bound to an unfinished oath. In the world quest The Bell of Mourning Echoes he fights beside the Traveler, and Lushne and Sukhovykh recognise him from his relics. If the Traveler chooses to fight him, he says at his defeat that it is finally over.

## entry: Solovei, the first Starshyna
@keys: Solovei, Torchforger, Silvestr Petrovich Solovei
@keys2: Starshyna, Lightkeepers, Nod-Krai
@version: 5.5
### STANDARD
Solovei, the Torchforger, founded the Lightkeepers about five hundred years ago and was appointed their first Starshyna. He was a Snezhnayan noble who asked Anastasya for autonomy for the borderlands. Aila gave him the Arrow of Terpikeraunas to fight the Abyss, and the Lightkeepers have kept it since.
### LARGE +
His statue stands in Piramida. The nightingale of his house became the Lightkeepers' traditional emblem.

## entry: Tales Carried by the Northwind
@keys: Tales Carried by the Northwind, Mayakov, Sergei Semyonovich Mayakov
@keys2: Starshyna, Piramida, poetry

### STANDARD
A one-volume poetry collection found near Kofitse in Piramida. It chronicles the deeds of successive Starshyna; most of it was lost in past calamities. Surviving verse is credited to Solovei and to Sergei Semyonovich Mayakov, whom the wiki lists as a historical Starshyna.
