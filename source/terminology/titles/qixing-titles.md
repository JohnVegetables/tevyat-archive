## summary
### LITE
The Qixing ("Seven Stars") is Liyue's seven-seat governing committee, and each seat is named for a Big Dipper star: Tianshu, Tianxuan, Tianji, Tianquan, Yuheng, Kaiyang, Yaoguang. Canon uses five. Uncle Tian is Tianshu, Ningguang Tianquan, Keqing Yuheng.
### STANDARD +
All seats are equal in rank but differ in duty. People address a holder by title: "Lady Tianquan", "Lady Yuheng". The committee itself is covered in its faction entry.
### ULTRA +
Beidou's Chinese name is the Big Dipper's and her ship Alcor is one of its stars, but neither is a Qixing seat.

## the_seven_titles
### LITE
Tianshu: policymaking (Uncle Tian). Tianxuan: holder and duties unknown. Tianji: never shown. Tianquan: laws and foreign relations (Ningguang). Yuheng: land, construction, urban management (Keqing). Kaiyang: unnamed holder. Yaoguang: never shown.
### STANDARD +
The order follows the Big Dipper from Dubhe (Tianshu) through Merak, Phecda, Megrez (Tianquan), Alioth (Yuheng) and Mizar (Kaiyang) to Alkaid (Yaoguang). Tianji and Yaoguang are only extrapolated from the real star names; give them no holder or portfolio.
### LARGE +
Yaoguang is unrelated to Yaoguang Shoal, which canon says was named for glowing sea creatures that resembled fallen stars. Duties overlap: the Tianquan keeps the law book, but other members also help make law and the whole Qixing interprets and enforces it.

## selection_and_status
@version: 2.7
### LARGE
By Uncle Tian's account the incumbent Tianshu recommends a successor and the other Qixing approve, kept discreet to shield candidates from outside influence. Whether this holds for every seat is not stated.
### ULTRA +
As of 7.1 the Qixing govern Liyue without a god's direct rule and meet at Yuehai Pavilion in Yujing Terrace. No change to the roster of titles has been shown since the 2.7 Tianshu succession. Ganyu is general secretary to the whole committee and attached to no seat.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay interpretation (this project's recommendation): use title plus name in formal speech, and treat a title as an office, not a name. Most of Liyue sees Uncle Tian as an old fisherman, so do not have strangers call him Tianshu.
### LARGE +
Treat Tianxuan and Kaiyang as real but mysterious, and Tianji and Yaoguang as unmentioned. Before Rex Lapis' staged death at the Rite of Descension, speak of the Qixing as acting under the god; afterwards they govern without him.

## entry: Tianshu
@keys: Tianshu
@keys2: Qixing, Liyue Qixing, Uncle Tian, Liyue, Yuehai Pavilion
@version: 1.2
### STANDARD
Tianshu is the Qixing seat for policymaking. Uncle Tian holds it and, as of the cutoff, still does despite seeking a successor. By his account the Tianshu rarely appears in public and works behind the scenes planning and advising. He is said to have kept his place for years by setting rivals against each other. His daughter Huixin is a secretary at Yuehai Pavilion.
### LARGE +
In the Seven-Star Selection chain (2.7), Uncle Tian, tired and wishing to step down, names three candidates and asks Yelan to assess them. A new Tianshu is expected to carry out the manifesto they submit and not change it barring a major disaster. One candidate, Zhiyi, is revealed to have poisoned Uncle Tian's food. After Baizhu supplies an antidote, Uncle Tian decides to hand over duties gradually while mentoring other candidates. No replacement is named.

## entry: Tianquan
@keys: Tianquan
@keys2: Qixing, Liyue Qixing, Ningguang, Liyue, Jade Chamber
### STANDARD
Tianquan is the seat that keeps the book of ancient laws laid down by Morax with every later amendment, and handles relations with other nations. Each Tianquan over the centuries has amended the laws to suit the times, and they are published with a 279-page commentary. Ningguang holds the seat. Unlike most Qixing she is often seen around Liyue Harbor, and when a hard problem needs solving she shuts herself in the Jade Chamber until she decides.
### LARGE +
Ningguang keeps an information network, relies on three personal secretaries (Baiwen, Baishi and Baixiao) and works closely with Beidou, whom she chose for tasks she wants kept at arm's length. Yelan, nominally of the Ministry of Civil Affairs, is her intelligence agent. An amendment by the Tianquan, such as the Urban Areas Sanitation Act, can touch matters the Yuheng manages.

## entry: Yuheng
@keys: Yuheng
@keys2: Qixing, Liyue Qixing, Keqing, Liyue, Yuehai Pavilion
### STANDARD
Yuheng is the seat over land, livelihood, construction and real estate, which Keqing's voice-over says carries strict regulation and over a hundred contractual obligations. Keqing holds it and often works away from Yuehai Pavilion: at the south wharf, in the Chasm and in the wilderness. Her assistants rarely last beyond three months because of her perfectionism.
### LARGE +
After Rex Lapis stepped down, Keqing began rewriting old rules, starting with land infrastructure. She is a sceptic of the Geo Archon's one-sided policymaking, though she gained respect for how much he had done. In the 4.7 limited-time event Commanders and Wargames and in the 5.7 quest Tumult Subduer she coordinates the Millelith and neighbouring forces.

## entry: Tianxuan
@keys: Tianxuan
@keys2: Qixing, Liyue Qixing, Ganyu, Liyue
@version: 1.0
### STANDARD
Tianxuan is a Qixing seat whose current holder and duties are unknown. Ganyu's Character Story recalls a "then-Tianxuan" who took her to lunch outside the city and nearly reported her missing when she fell asleep in a haystack. That shows the seat has existed for some time but identifies no one.

## entry: Kaiyang
@keys: Kaiyang
@keys2: Qixing, Liyue Qixing, Ningguang, Keqing, Liyue
@version: 4.7
### STANDARD
Kaiyang is a Qixing seat held by an unnamed person, called "Lord Kaiyang" in dialogue. Official promotion for Ningguang quotes the Kaiyang saying she is the biggest secret in Liyue only because she knows all its secrets. No portfolio is stated.
### LARGE +
In the 4.7 limited-time event Mutual Security Enhancing Simulation, Su Ding says the Kaiyang proposed a Wargame of Five Armies to the Tianquan, and Keqing invited neighbouring nations' forces. In the 5.7 quests Tumult Subduer and A Defensive Strategy, Keqing contacts the Kaiyang about manpower, and the Kaiyang and Tianquan agree from Yujing Terrace to a long-term partnership with the Traveler. The seat looks tied to military coordination, but canon does not say so.

## entry: Tianji and Yaoguang
@keys: Tianji, Yaoguang
@keys2: Qixing, Liyue Qixing, Big Dipper, seven stars
### STANDARD
Tianji and Yaoguang are the remaining Big Dipper star names and are assumed to be the other two Qixing seats, but neither appears in canon through 7.1. Give them no holder or backstory; if a scene needs another member, say "another of the Qixing". Yaoguang Shoal is named for sea creatures, not the seat.
