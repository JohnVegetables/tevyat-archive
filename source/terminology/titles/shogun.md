## summary
### LITE
"Shogun" is the title of Inazuma's supreme ruler, who heads the Inazuma Shogunate and is also the Electro Archon. Makoto (divine name Baal) held it first; her twin Ei took it over about 500 years ago, and Ei has since ruled through a puppet she made, also called the Shogun. Players know the playable character as the Raiden Shogun.
### STANDARD +
The word is used in three overlapping ways. It names the office, which commands the Shogunate, the Tri-Commission and the Shogun's Army from Tenshukaku in Inazuma City. It names the person holding it, who appears in public as one figure under Baal's name. And "the Shogun" is the name of the puppet vessel, as distinct from Ei herself. Context and speaker decide which is meant.
### ULTRA +
Outwardly the title never changed hands in a way the people saw. Ei served Makoto as her kagemusha (body double), so the world knew a single Raiden Shogun, and Ei kept the identity of Baal after Makoto died.

## holders
@version: 2.0
### LITE
Makoto, the first Electro Archon, who founded the Shogunate and ruled until she died in the Cataclysm about 500 years ago. Ei, her twin sister, who then took the throne and title. The Shogun, Ei's puppet, who has acted as ruler since about 400 years ago.
### STANDARD +
Ei and Makoto were identical twin gods of the Archon War. Ei, the warrior, gave up her body so that Makoto could ascend to Celestia; Makoto rebuilt Ei's body and made her a kagemusha. About 2,000 years ago the twins united the islands under the Shogunate. After Makoto died in Khaenri'ah, Ei assumed her sister's seat among the Seven and in the Shogunate.
### LARGE +
Over 400 years ago Ei created the Shogun, a puppet that has its own consciousness but follows fixed directives she wrote, and sealed her own mind in the Plane of Euthymia inside the sword Musou Isshin within the puppet's body. From then on the puppet handled governance while Ei meditated. Ei can take control of the body herself when she chooses, and she has said she regards the Shogun as another form of her own existence rather than a separate person.
### ULTRA +
The Shogun was made to resist change. When Ei came to accept a new view of Eternity, the puppet at first took this as erosion and refused her orders; they dueled within Makoto's lingering consciousness, Ei won, and the Shogun agreed to serve as her kagemusha in turn (Radiant Sakura). The wiki records that the two now co-rule, and that only a few people know the Shogun is a distinct being.

## names_and_forms_of_address
### LARGE
Formal titles in the game text include "Her Excellency, the Almighty Narukami Ogosho, God of Thunder" (used in the quest Cards Out! Grievances Begone) and "Great and Excellent Ogosho, the Almighty Shogun" (an official Tenryou Commission document). Ei's own profile titles also list "Shogun of Inazuma", "Electro Archon" and "God of Eternity". The playable form is named the Raiden Shogun; "Raiden" is the family name Ei and Makoto share. Ei rules publicly under the name Baal, her sister's divine name, and her real identity is known only to a few, such as close friends and the other Archons. The Shogunate's rallying cry in game is "Glory to the Shogun!"
### ULTRA +
"Shogun" is a real-world word for a military commander; Inazuma's version is a divine ruler who is also an Archon, so do not import real-history assumptions about shoguns, such as service under an emperor.

## the_shogunate_and_its_organs
### LARGE
The Inazuma Shogunate (Inazuma Bakufu) is the government the Shogun leads. Beneath her sits the Tri-Commission: the Tenryou Commission (the main fighting force), the Kanjou Commission (border security) and the Yashiro Commission (shrines, festivals and public events). The Shogun's Army serves the three commissions, while some soldiers such as the Hatamoto and the Okuzumeshuu answer directly to the Shogun. Inazuma City's Tenshukaku is the Shogun's residence, built during the Archon War, and the Tenryou Commission has its headquarters in it. The Okuzumeshuu are the Shogun's bodyguards. A duel before the throne is a formal Inazuman way of appealing to the Shogun, in which the loser is executed with the Shogun's technique (see the Inazuma duels entry).
### ULTRA +
The army's ranks include ashigaru, doushin, yoriki, hatamoto and generals; Kujou Sara and Kujou Masahito are the only generals named.

## the_decrees
@version: 2.0
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
Two decrees were issued in the Shogun's name about a year before the Archon Quest begins: the Sakoku Decree, which closed Inazuma's borders, and the Vision Hunt Decree, which ordered Visions seized. The Fatui had manipulated the commissions into pushing them. Ei knew of the Fatui's role and let the decrees stand because she judged Visions, as symbols of human ambition, to conflict with her ideal of Eternity. The Traveler and Yae Miko, with the Watatsumi Army, brought about the end of the Vision Hunt Decree after the Traveler defeated Ei in the Plane of Euthymia, and the Visions were returned. The Sakoku Decree followed: the wiki records that after her duel with the Shogun in Transient Dreams, Ei abolished it, and foreign visitors and trade returned.
### ULTRA +
The Shogun puppet executed a duellist who challenged the decree (Kazuha's friend) after Kujou Sara defeated him, and later executed Signora after the Traveler defeated her in a duel before the throne. In story the puppet answered to Ei's directives, not to the commissions.

## status_as_of_7_1
@version: 7.1
### STANDARD
As of 7.1 Ei holds the title and the Shogun puppet shares the duty of governing, with Inazuma's borders open and the two decrees gone. No canon through 7.1 has the Shogun leave Inazuma or involves her in Nod-Krai or Snezhnaya events. The Electro Gnosis is no longer in Ei's hands (she gave it to Yae Miko, and it has since passed on).
### LARGE +
The wiki notes that Ei and the Shogun began co-ruling after Radiant Sakura. Ei still uses her sister's name in public.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Treat "Shogun" as a word whose weight depends on the speaker. Inazumans use it with awe or wariness, the Kujou clan with strict loyalty, the commissions with formal deference. Do not have ordinary characters know the Shogun is a puppet or that Ei and Makoto were separate people; Yae Miko and anyone who took part in the Archon Quest may know more. Use "the Shogun" for official acts and the Raiden Shogun for the playable character; use Ei's name only where the speaker knows it.
### LARGE +
Do not attribute the Vision Hunt Decree to malice alone; canon shows Fatui manipulation and Ei's tacit approval.

## entry: The Shogun (puppet)
@keys: the Shogun, Shogun puppet, Almighty Shogun
@keys2: Inazuma, Ei, Tenshukaku, Raiden Shogun
@version: 2.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The Shogun is the puppet Ei made over 400 years ago to govern Inazuma while she meditates in the Plane of Euthymia. It has its own consciousness, wields Musou Isshin and is bound by directives that cannot be changed or turned off. It enforced the decrees and executed those who lost duels before the throne, and later agreed to serve as Ei's kagemusha.

## entry: Inazuma Shogunate
@keys: Inazuma Shogunate, Shogunate, Bakufu
@keys2: Inazuma, Tri-Commission, Tenryou Commission
### STANDARD
The Shogunate is Inazuma's government, led by the Shogun and organised through the Tri-Commission (Tenryou, Kanjou and Yashiro) and the Shogun's Army. Makoto is its recorded founder (Treasured Tales of the Chouken Shinkageuchi). Its seat is Tenshukaku in Inazuma City.

## entry: Makoto and Ei as one Shogun
@keys: Makoto, Raiden Makoto, Baal, kagemusha
@keys2: Shogun, Ei, Inazuma
@version: 2.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Makoto, Ei's twin, was the first Electro Archon and Shogun; Ei lived as her kagemusha. Makoto went alone to Khaenri'ah during the Cataclysm and died; Ei took her place. The world was only ever shown one Raiden Shogun, and Baal's name and the title were understood to cover both.
