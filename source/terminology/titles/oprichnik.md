## summary
### LITE
Oprichnik (plural Oprichniki) is the name for a class of Fatui soldier found across Nod-Krai and Snezhnaya. Unlike the Skirmishers seen abroad, Oprichniki are formally recruited, rigorously trained volunteers, posted to the Fatui's most critical strongholds or thrown into the deadliest frontline battles.
### STANDARD +
The Adventurer Handbook describes an Oprichnik as a soldier encased within a Fatui war machine. Their gear is powered by kuuvahki, and their officers come from the Snezhnaya Royal Military Academy.
### LARGE +
The word is Russian in origin and fits Snezhnaya's naming. It entered the game with Luna I (6.0), alongside the Nod-Krai content.

## definition
### LITE
An Oprichnik is a trained Fatui regular, closer to a professional soldier than to a Skirmisher. The term is used both for the whole group (Oprichniki) and for an individual soldier.
### STANDARD +
Low-ranking Oprichniki are the line troops: Line Troopers, Vanguards, Grenadiers, Support Troopers, Medics and Bombardiers. Higher-ranking ones are the Fireblade Shock Troopers, Hailstorm Cannoneers and Thunderblitz Gvardiya, who fight with elemental power.
### LARGE +
Other Fatui material lists poruchik (a lieutenant-grade rank) among Oprichniki, so the word can also sound like a rank. Canon through 7.1 does not define a formal chain of command for the group.

## equipment_and_role
@version: 6.0
### STANDARD
Oprichniki use kuuvahki-powered equipment. During certain attacks the gear is infused with kuuvahki; a character attuned to kuuvahki can interrupt the attack and disarm a low-ranking Oprichnik, who then falls back on punches and kicks. Officers keep their equipment intact under the same interruption.
### LARGE +
Low-ranking Oprichniki deal mainly physical damage with some elemental effect from their gear, while the higher ranks deal elemental damage only. This is gameplay presentation, not a statement of in-world rules. The Royal Military Academy page also records that the Fatui and the Academy co-wrote combat doctrine for Landcruisers working alongside human soldiers.
### ULTRA +
Unlike other Fatui enemies, which fade away when defeated, Oprichniki puff away in smoke, as Treasure Hoarders do. Some ordinary Oprichniki can be seen performing the prisiadki squat dance, a traditional men's dance, which is a small piece of everyday colour.

## origins_and_training
@version: 6.0
### LARGE
The Royal Military Academy is said to be over five hundred years old, open to those born into nobility or to commoners with talent or connections. The wiki's Academy page says the Fatui Oprichnik comprises humans who graduated from it; the enemy entries say the officer ranks come from there. Oprichniki are not Skirmisher-style rank and file but volunteers who go through formal recruitment.
### ULTRA +
The word comes from the real-world oprichnina, a policy of Ivan the Terrible between 1565 and 1584 that included Russia's first secret police. This is the wiki's etymology note; the Fatui in game are a field army and garrison, not shown as a police force.

## status
@version: 7.1
### STANDARD
As of 7.1 Oprichniki remain trained Fatui troops in Nod-Krai and Snezhnaya, found in strongholds and towns, and guarding the Northland Bank's vaults in Snezhnograd.
### LARGE +
Individual Oprichniki appear in Snezhnaya content, such as officers leading deliveries and escorts, but no source consulted names a commander of the whole group.

## roleplay
### LARGE
Treat an Oprichnik as a disciplined volunteer soldier, proud of formal training and loyal to the Fatui, a chosen professional unlike a Skirmisher. Do not invent a rank structure, secret police role or personal backstory beyond what canon gives.

## mistakes
### LARGE
Do not confuse Oprichniki with Skirmishers (the common line infantry abroad), Harbingers (the top commanders), or Cicin Mages and Agents (elite Delusion users). Do not claim they are a political police because of the real-world word's history.
