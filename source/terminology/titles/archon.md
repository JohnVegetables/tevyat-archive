## summary
### LITE
"Archon" is the title of the gods who sit on the seven divine thrones and govern Teyvat's seven nations, together called the Seven or the Seven Archons. Each is tied to an element and an ideal. Barbatos and Morax are the last of the original Seven. As of 7.1 the Hydro throne has been destroyed, so six thrones remain.
### STANDARD +
The Seven were selected from the many gods of the Archon War, which ended about 2,000 years ago. The word names an office, not a species, and is used for the god and the seat together: the Anemo Archon, the Pyro Archon and so on.
### ULTRA +
An Archon is not the same thing as a Gnosis holder, a Vision holder or a Dragon Sovereign. Gnoses are symbols and links to Celestia, Visions are lesser focuses held by mortals, and the thrones are built from Authority taken from the Seven Sovereigns.

## the_seven
### LITE
Mondstadt: Barbatos (Venti), Anemo, Freedom. Liyue: Morax (Zhongli), Rex Lapis, Geo, Contracts. Inazuma: Beelzebul, the Raiden Shogun (Ei), Electro, Eternity. Sumeru: Buer, Lesser Lord Kusanali (Nahida), Dendro, Wisdom. Fontaine: Focalors, Hydro, Justice (throne destroyed). Natlan: Haborym, Pyro, War (Mavuika). Snezhnaya: Vassaga, the Tsaritsa, Cryo, Love.
### STANDARD +
Divine names are an Archon's name as a god: Barbatos, Morax, Baal and Beelzebul, Buer, Haborym and Vassaga. Regnal titles are what the people call them: Rex Lapis, the Raiden Shogun, Lesser Lord Kusanali, Regina of All Waters, Kindreds, Peoples and Laws, and Tsaritsa of All Snezhnaya. Barbatos has no regnal title, and Natlan's Archon is addressed as Kiongozi, an Ancient Name. The Tsaritsa's personal name is Anastasya Feodorovna Snezhnaya, while Focalors' own name is unknown.
### ULTRA +
Ei is the current Electro Archon and was a veteran of the Archon War beside her elder twin Makoto (Baal), the first Electro Archon. Makoto died in the Cataclysm, about 500 years ago. In the same era Nahida replaced Greater Lord Rukkhadevata, Focalors replaced Egeria and the Tsaritsa replaced the Belyi Tsar, Monomakh Snezhnyi. Mavuika first ascended as Pyro Archon some years before the Cataclysm and placed her life in the Sacred Flame about 500 years ago so that she could return in the present; her current reign began within living memory (the wiki estimates less than 100 years ago).

## the_war_that_chose_them
@version: 1.0
### LARGE
The Heavenly Principles set up seven divine thrones in Celestia, each holding Authority taken from one of the Seven Sovereigns, along with seven matching Gnoses. They then drove the gods of Teyvat to fight for them. This Archon War was a patchwork of local conflicts, in Mondstadt, Liyue, Inazuma and Sumeru above all, and the wiki dates it to about 6,000 to 2,000 years ago, with the last seat claimed 2,000 years ago.
### ULTRA +
Barbatos became the first Anemo Archon about 2,600 years ago, after Decarabian fell. Morax was already regarded as Geo Archon by the time the war ended. After the war the Seven shaped their nations with their elements and governed by their ideals. The original Seven were reportedly close, often meeting for drinks in Liyue, and shared a duty of guiding humanity. Over time five of them died, and newer Archons did not all keep that duty.

## powers_and_thrones
@version: 4.2
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
An Archon's power is attributed to three sources: ruling their region, their people's faith, and the elemental Authority bound into the divine throne. Barbatos calls himself the weakest of the Seven because he does not govern directly. The Archons do not control who receives a Vision; they only give a portion of their Authority when notified.
### ULTRA +
The thrones are made from Authorities stolen from the Seven Sovereigns by the Heavenly Principles, a fact revealed in the Fontaine Archon Quest. Destroying a throne takes great power and the death of the Archon seated on it, after which the Authority returns to its Sovereign. When an Archon dies the throne is normally passed on, often to someone related, as with Ei and Makoto, Kusanali and Rukkhadevata, and Focalors and Egeria. Pyro Archons are mortals who win the seat through the Pilgrimage, and the Sacred Flame passes on knowledge of the land. Several Archons had elemental power before ascending: Barbatos was a wind spirit, Ei an incarnation of lightning, and Mavuika held a Pyro Vision.
Archons can reshape bodies as an act of divine power. Barbatos took the late bard's form, Makoto reforged Ei's body, and Ei made the Shogun as a vessel and withdrew into the Plane of Euthymia. Venti's body cannot be healed by ordinary means and needs the power of Mondstadt's hero symbol.

## gnosis_and_status
@version: 1.0
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
Each of the Seven was given a Gnosis, which usually sits in the chest and links the holder to Celestia. In Ending Note (1.0) Venti explained this and called Visions "primitive" by comparison. Later canon shows an Archon's seat does not depend on holding one. As of 7.1 the Fatui hold six Gnoses; the Pyro Gnosis was never taken and vanished after the burning of Irminsul.
### ULTRA +
Ei gave her Gnosis to Yae Miko about 400 years ago. Nahida used the Dendro and Electro Gnoses together to enter Rukkhadevata's realm of consciousness, and the Pyro Gnosis to burn Irminsul. Some Gnoses reached the Fatui by bargain: Dottore obtained the Electro and Dendro Gnoses from Nahida, and Arlecchino received the Hydro Gnosis as a diplomatic gift from Neuvillette. Signora took the Anemo Gnosis from Venti by force. The Traveler was later told that the Pyro Gnosis had returned to the Ley Lines.

## status_as_of_7_1
@version: 7.1
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
As of 7.1: Barbatos (Venti), Morax (Zhongli), Beelzebul (Ei), Buer (Nahida), Mavuika and Vassaga (the Tsaritsa) are alive. Makoto, Rukkhadevata, Egeria, the Belyi Tsar and earlier Pyro Archons are dead. Focalors was executed by the Oratrice Mecanique d'Analyse Cardinale at the end of the Fontaine trial, as she had planned, and her throne was destroyed and its Authority went back to the Hydro Dragon Sovereign. Furina, who played the Hydro Archon's public role, was never the real Archon.
### ULTRA +
Representatives have also stood in for Archons: Ei for Makoto, the Shogun for Ei, and Furina for Focalors. Not every Archon is present in daily life. Barbatos lives among Mondstadters as a bard, and the Raiden Shogun rules Inazuma through a puppet vessel.

## roleplay
### LARGE
Treat "Archon" as a title spoken with awe, politics or affection depending on the nation. Mondstadters are casual about their god, Liyue honours Rex Lapis, and Fontaine has recently lost its Archon. Ordinary people do not know that the Archons do not personally grant Visions. Do not have anyone casually reveal a hidden Archon's identity, and do not let characters know the origin of the thrones unless they have the matching story knowledge.
### ULTRA +
Characters set before 4.2 should still believe Fontaine has an Archon. Most people in the world do not know that the thrones were built from stolen Sovereign power.

## entry: Divine Throne
@keys: divine throne, divine thrones
@version: 4.2
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
A divine throne is the seat in Celestia that makes a god an Archon. Each is made from one stolen elemental Authority of the Seven Sovereigns, and it gives the holder authority over that element. When the holder dies the seat is normally given to a successor. The Hydro throne was destroyed in 4.2, returning its Authority to the Hydro Dragon Sovereign.

## entry: Pyro Archon
@keys: Pyro Archon, Pyro Archons
@version: 5.0
### STANDARD
Natlan's Archon is unlike the others: Pyro Archons are mortals who age and die, and the title is won through the Pilgrimage, with the Sacred Flame passing on memory of the land. Many past Pyro Archons are named, most only in passing; the best known is Xbalanque, the first to bear the Ancient Name Kiongozi, who held the seat about 2,000 years ago. Mavuika, the present Archon, is addressed as Kiongozi, the Ancient Name of the office.
