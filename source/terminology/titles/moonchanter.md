## summary
### LITE
"Moonchanter" is the title of the spiritual leader of the Frostmoon Scions, the Hyperborean-descended tribe of Nod-Krai. The first holder was Aila, about 500 years ago, and the present holder is Lauma, whom her followers call Lady Moonchanter. The Moonchanter speaks for a Moon Goddess who no longer answers prayer.
### STANDARD +
The title replaced "Archpriestess" when Aila took over the Scions and reformed their faith. Beneath the Moonchanter the Scions have Priestesses, Acolytes and Adherents; men of the tribe cannot become Acolytes or priests.
### ULTRA +
The title is a role, not a name: Aila and Lauma each hold it in their own era. Do not treat "the Moonchanter" as always meaning Lauma when speaking of the Scions' past.

## definition
### LITE
The Moonchanter is the head of the Frostmoon Scions' clergy and the person the congregation turns to when they ask when the Goddess of the Frost Moon will answer. Lauma's introduction describes her as the agent of an absent god.
### STANDARD +
Before the title existed, the leader was the Archpriestess, who led the ancient Hyperborean rites. Lovia was the last Archpriestess. Aila abolished that office and made herself the first Moonchanter, with the aim of reforming the faith.
### LARGE +
Lauma's profile describes the role in terms of trust: a Moonchanter's words are easily taken as truth by believers, which is why Lauma holds herself to never lying. The Scions also pass down a prophecy that the Moon Goddess will return, and the Moonchanter stands at the centre of that hope.

## aila_first_moonchanter
@version: 6.0
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
Aila, one of twin girls raised in isolation by Lovia, killed Lovia and took over leadership of the Scions after her sister Alia died. She dropped the Archpriestess office, became the first Moonchanter, and ended the old Hyperborean rites. Hymns of the Far North, annotated by Priestess Ehrnrooth, records several rites that Aila abolished or simplified, including sacrifice-based prayers.
Aila also worked with Solovei, using the Arrow of Terpikeraunas against the Abyssal threat at the Pillar of Embla and later giving him the Arrow, which he used to found the Lightkeepers. She could see through time and foresaw and witnessed the birth of the Moon Maiden Kuutar. Under her reforms the Scions turned toward benevolence and avoided conflict with outsiders. Lovia was struck from the records, and only Priestesses and above may learn her name. Aila had the old towers and temples on Hiisi Island torn down, believing they isolated the Scions and let their delusional beliefs fester.
### ULTRA +
The Moonchanter title therefore began as reform born of revulsion at the Sacred Heir Project. Aila sealed evidence of Lovia's misdeeds and the project inside the Court of the First Edict (the Sanctum of the Oathbearer), hoping to end the Scions' fixation on Hyperborea. Her recorded foresight there says the Traveler would one day come to Nod-Krai; this is Aila's own prophecy as left in her sanctum.

## lauma
@version: 6.0
### LARGE
Lauma is the current Moonchanter, also called the Maiden of the Grove. Her story says she was chosen at birth, a Moon Wheel appearing beside her as a baby, and that the Scions saw her as proof the Goddess had not abandoned them. An elder predecessor taught her the role.
Lauma has an unusual sensitivity to moonlight and kuuvahki, said to appear about once a century, and she can communicate with animals. She came to realise that the sky is false and that the Scions prayed to the wrong moon, and in In the Shadows of Night she told her followers; some stayed and some left the Scions. The Moonchanter's role is now to guide a congregation whose faith has been openly changed. The Scions who remained agreed with Columbina to treat her as herself and not as a goddess.
### ULTRA +
Lauma holds three personal precepts as Moonchanter: never lie, never give way to wrath, and never change. By her Character Story 3 she regards them as already unsettled. Her aliases Frostmoon's Emissary and Moon-Tide Maiden come from the quest Reunion Beneath the Moonlight. Lauma says Moonchanters use Moon Marrow to see the true past.

## status
@version: 7.1
### STANDARD
As of 7.1 Lauma holds the title and no other living Moonchanter is named. The office has not been abolished. The Scions remain under the Moonchanter's lead in Nod-Krai, with Priestesses such as Ehrnrooth, Sarakka and Rahetar below her.
### LARGE +
After Lauma told the Scions the truth about the Frost Moon, the Moonchanter leads a changed community. No change to the title itself was found in 7.0-7.1 content.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Scions address Lauma as Lady Moonchanter; outsiders may simply use her name. Treat the title as a role of trust and spiritual guidance, not of command.
### LARGE +
Treat Aila's era as one of reform and secrecy, not a golden age. When portraying Scions, remember that they take the Moonchanter's word as near to truth, and that Lauma is careful for that reason.

## mistakes
### STANDARD
Do not call the Moonchanter the Moon Goddess. Do not say the title existed in the Hyperborean era; it began about 500 years ago. Do not use "Archpriestess" for Lauma or Aila. Do not invent a succession ritual or a list of Moonchanters between Aila and Lauma.
