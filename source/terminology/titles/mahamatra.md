## summary
### LITE
The Matra are the Sumeru Akademiya's disciplinary officers. They hand out punishment to researchers who commit serious academic offences such as plagiarism or misusing funds, and are feared for it. The General Mahamatra is their leader, a post held by Cyno.
### STANDARD +
Cyno holds the title "General Mahamatra" besides his profile title "Judicator of Secrets". The Matra enforce academic integrity for the Akademiya; they are separate from the Mahamata, its civil administrators.
### LARGE +
The Akademiya's rules rest on three virtues and six ancient sins, the six banned lines of inquiry. Researchers shrink away from Cyno on sight, and even mercenaries and Treasure Hoarders in the desert avoid trouble with him.

## definition
### LITE
Once the Matra learn of an offence they visit the offender in person, which is why most meetings with them are known to go poorly. Offences named in Cyno's profile include fraud, false declarations, misappropriating funds, malicious competition and stealing someone else's dissertation.
### STANDARD +
They also control information and goods: Knowledge Capsule transactions, illegal sources such as canned knowledge and smuggled books, and a seal on goods that pass inspection.
### LARGE +
The wiki lists the Matra, the Mahamata and the Akademiya's Temple of Silence as separate bodies. It cautions that, from the Chinese text, the Mahamata and the Matra are different groups. The English pages use look-alike names, so check which one a speaker means.

## the_general_mahamatra
### LITE
The General Mahamatra is the head of all Matra, in charge of supervising the Akademiya's researchers. Cyno is the current holder. The post is high-ranking and hard to meet: Tighnari says it is not easy to find the person, who comes and goes.
### STANDARD +
Cyno's profile says he holds the post despite his youth and that even the sages are wary of him. He says it is part of a Matra's duty to help offenders remember their mistakes.
### LARGE +
His stories add that researchers fear him and he does not mind, since the reputation deters some wrongdoing. Cyno received his Vision the night before he was to be inaugurated, while reading the Akademiya's rules in the library, and went from there to the ceremony. His own stated principle is that the General Mahamatra judges lawbreakers under the six ancient sins, and sages are not exempt.
### ULTRA +
A message-board remark criticises Cyno for not being a renowned researcher, and a reply says he has done better than the previous holder, who is read as an alumnus of Rtawahist. His introduction voice-over has two versions: one calls himself the former General Mahamatra who now punishes evil as he sees fit, the other calls himself General Mahamatra of the Akademiya.

## history
@version: 3.0
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
During Chapter III, Cyno grew suspicious of the Akasha System. When he found anomalies in the data he submitted into it and asked to investigate, Azar refused and told him he had no right to judge him, so Cyno resigned in protest. Tighnari had also asked him to quietly look into whether Naphis was being forced to write letters.
### ULTRA +
After Kusanali was freed in Chapter III, Cyno apprehended Azar and made him repent before the Archon, and was reinstated as General Mahamatra; Nilou's letter in A Toast to Victory (3.2) announced a Grand Bazaar feast to celebrate it. Cyno did not know about the feast beforehand. The Matra's later appearances show Cyno working alongside the sages and the Traveler, as at Caravan Ribat in 6.6.

## status
@version: 6.6
### STANDARD
As of 7.1 Cyno remains General Mahamatra; he is confirmed in that role during the Irminsul crisis of 6.6 (A Divergence Leading to Oneself), when Rukh Shah addresses him alongside the sages. No other holder of the title appears.
### LARGE +
Alongside his Matra duties Cyno remains tied to the Temple of Silence and its history with Cyrus. This file does not track his other plotlines; those belong in his own entry.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Treat the Matra as a feared but fair body. They speak in rules and procedure; a Matra will not take bribes and treats attempts at them as aggravating. General Mahamatra Cyno uses the title when on duty and tells terrible puns off duty.
### LARGE +
Do not make the Matra secret police or a military: canon presents them as Akademiya discipline officers who go after academic crimes. Under Azar they enforced a harsher regime, but Cyno himself opposed it.

## mistakes
### STANDARD
Do not confuse the Matra with the Mahamata, the Temple of Silence or the Corps of Thirty. Do not give them powers over Sumeru's citizens in general beyond what is shown. Do not invent a rank structure below the General Mahamatra, and do not give the other named Matra personalities canon has not shown.

## entry: Mahamata versus Matra
@keys: Mahamata
@keys2: Akademiya, Matra, Sumeru, Scribe
@version: 3.0
### STANDARD
The Mahamata are the Akademiya's civil administrative officials, alumni sent out to oversee the public flow of information, relay decrees and run public services. Alhaitham, as Scribe, belongs to them. The Matra are a different group, for discipline.
