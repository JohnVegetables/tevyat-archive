## summary
### LITE
Version 3.7, Duel! The Summoners' Summit!, was released on 2023-05-24. It introduced Kirara and a large Genius Invokation TCG update with a TCG-themed flagship event. It had no Archon Quest and no new region.
### STANDARD +
The flagship event, the King of Invokations Grand Prix, centres on the card game. The version blurb compares a card game to life: fortune deals the hand, but experience can shift the odds.
### LARGE +
Genius Invokation TCG gained two new modes, Arena of Champions (PvP) and The Forge Realm's Temper (PvE), plus new opponents and more than fifty new cards.
### ULTRA +
The event is limited-time, but the TCG additions are permanent game content.

## characters_and_banners
### STANDARD
Kirara ("Cat Upon the Eaves") is a 4-star Dendro sword user. Phase I banners were Tapestry of Golden Flames (Yoimiya and Kirara) and Everbloom Violet (Yae Miko and Kirara); phase II, from 2023-06-13, was Leaves in the Wind (Kaedehara Kazuha) and Caution in Confidence (Alhaitham). The new weapon was Ibis Piercer, a 4-star bow.
### LARGE +
Story and hangout quests: Yoimiya's Carassius Auratus Chapter Act II, Star-Pickers' Passage, and Kaveh's Hangout Event Series X Act I, The Pendulum of Weal and Woe. A new World Quest, A Guest From Liyue, was added.
### ULTRA +
Other content included the recipe Energizing Bento, the namecard Kirara: Neko Box and the achievements added to Memories of the Heart.

## duel_the_summoners_summit
@availability: limited
### LARGE
The flagship event ran from 2023-05-25 to 2023-06-12 and required Adventure Rank 32 and the World Quests Ritou Escape Plan and Battlefield of Dice, Cats, and Cards. Players unlocked four gameplays in sequence: A Tour of Wonders, Zero Hour Invokation, Evermotion Mechanical Painting: Invoker and Heart of the Dice. The reward was Ibis Piercer.
### ULTRA +
The event used Genius Invokation TCG as the setting for the King of Invokations Grand Prix. It drew on characters such as Luozi, Takugen, Dunia, Amber, Xingqiu, Kamisato Ayaka and Collei, and for the best experience recommended having finished Archon Quest Chapter III Act V, Mona's Astrolabos Chapter Act I and the Raiden Shogun's Imperatrix Umbrosa Chapter Act II. Players who had not could use a Quick Start.

## tcg_additions
### LARGE
New character cards included Abyss Lector: Fathomless Flames, Amber, Electro Hypostasis, Fatui Cryo Cicin Mage, Hu Tao, Nahida, Raiden Shogun, Shenhe, Tartaglia, Venti, Xiao, Yae Miko and Zhongli, along with new equipment, event, and support cards. New invitation duels and Open World Players were added, with ten more unlocking at each of Player Levels 9 and 10.
### ULTRA +
New support cards included Tenshukaku, Sangonomiya Shrine, Sumeru City, Vanarana and Chinju Forest, among others. Cards for characters, weapons and artifacts such as Amos' Bow, Skyward Blade and Tenacity of the Millelith were also introduced. These are gameplay and collection notes, not lore.
