## summary
### LITE
Nightsoul is Natlan's word for the souls that gather in the Night Kingdom, the realm of the dead beneath the land. Living Natlanese warriors can draw on them to move and fight beyond ordinary limits; that is Nightsoul's Blessing.
### STANDARD +
The term covers a family of related labels: Nightsouls (the souls themselves), Nightsoul's Blessing (the empowered state), Nightsoul points (the resource that sustains it), Nightsoul Transmission (swift movement on swapping to a Natlanese character) and Nightsoul Burst (a periodic surge). Nightsoul is not an element.
### LARGE +
Introduced in Version 5.0 with Natlan. Per the wiki, the Night Kingdom is described in story as a liminal space between body and mind, life and death. The people of Natlan go there at death and eventually lose their sense of self in the sea of souls.

### ULTRA +
The Night Kingdom, per the wiki, does not accept souls from non-native inhabitants apart from very few exceptions, and it holds no record of deeds done outside Natlan; this is why Xilonen and Citlali expected to need the Lord of the Night's help when making an Ancient Name for the Traveler.

## usage
### STANDARD
Use "Nightsoul" for the souls or the power drawn from them, "Nightsoul's Blessing" for the state a Natlanese character enters, and "Wayob" (not Nightsoul) for the tribal guardian spirits who watch over the realm. Nightsoul-aligned damage is a game term for attacks that are more effective against Void Wards and the shields of Wayob Manifestations.
### LARGE +
Gameplay labels: a Nightsoul Burst triggers when elemental damage is dealt while the party holds a Natlanese character, at intervals of 18, 12 or 9 seconds for one, two or three-plus such characters. It does nothing alone; certain talents and artifacts respond to it. When Nightsoul points are unavailable, Phlogiston is consumed instead.

## limits
### STANDARD
Do not treat Nightsoul as an element, a Vision power, or something outsiders can freely wield. Canon through 7.1 shows it only in connection with Natlan.

## entry: Nightsoul Transmission
@keys: Nightsoul Transmission
### STANDARD
A game mechanic of Natlan (5.0): when a Natlanese character's avatar lights up, switching to them allows swift sprinting, climbing, swimming or jumping, depending on that character's Night Realm's Gift passive.
