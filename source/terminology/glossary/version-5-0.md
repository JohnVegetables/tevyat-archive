## summary
### LITE
Version 5.0, "Flowers Resplendent on the Sun-Scorched Sojourn", was released on 2024-08-28. It opened Natlan, the Pyro nation, as a new nation, and began Chapter V of the Archon Quest. New characters were Mualani, Kinich and Kachina.
### STANDARD +
It follows Version 4.8 and precedes 5.1. The version added four Natlan subregions (the Basin of Unnumbered Flames, Coatepec Mountain, Tequemecan Valley and Toyac Springs) and the Chapter V Archon Quest Acts I and II.
### LARGE +
Natlan arrived with its own set of systems: Saurian Indwelling, Phlogiston, Nightsoul's Blessing, Tribe Reputation and the Tablet of Tona. A parallel event of the same title as Act I, run during the version, is separate from the Archon Quest.
### ULTRA +
The version also introduced World Level 9 (reachable at Adventure Rank 58), the Artifact Transmuter and a revised Character Event Wish mechanic called Capturing Radiance.

## release
@version: 5.0
### LITE
Released 2024-08-28, between 4.8 and 5.1.
### STANDARD +
Phase I banners were Sharktacular Surfari (Mualani and Kachina) and Leaves in the Wind (Kaedehara Kazuha and Kachina). Phase II brought Seeker of Flame-Wrought Secrets (Kinich) and Reign of Serenity (the Raiden Shogun).
### LARGE +
New five-star weapons on the event banner were Fang of the Mountain King (Claymore) and Surf's Up (Catalyst). Forgeable four-star weapons were Flute of Ezpitzal, Earth Shaker, Footprint of the Rainbow, Ring of Yaxche and Chain Breaker, and the event weapon Ash-Graven Drinking Horn. New artifact sets were Obsidian Codex and Scroll of the Hero of Cinder City.
### ULTRA +
Imaginarium Theater's first season of the version opened on 2024-09-01 (Pyro, Hydro and Dendro required) and the second on 2024-10-01 (Pyro, Electro and Geo). New bosses included the Gluttonous Yumkasaur Mountain King and the Goldflame Qucusaur Tyrant. Players could also salvage unenhanced artifacts into Sanctifying materials, and the maximum Fate Points for the Epitomized Path weapon banner fell from 2 to 1.

## characters
@version: 5.0
### STANDARD
Mualani ("Splish-Splash Wavechaser") is a five-star Hydro Catalyst character. Kinich ("Turnfire Hunt") is a five-star Dendro Claymore character. Kachina ("Mottled Gold Yet Unsmelted") is a four-star Geo Polearm character. Their own files carry the profiles.
### LARGE +
The permanent event Turbo Twirly!, unlocked by progressing Act I of the Archon Quest, lets the player invite Kachina. The namecards were Mualani: Sharky, Kinich: Ajaw and Kachina: Twirly.

## archon_quest
@version: 5.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Chapter V Act I, also titled Flowers Resplendent on the Sun-Scorched Sojourn, has the quests Natlan! A New Adventure, Pilgrimage of the Return of the Sacred Flame and Home of the Hot Springs. Act II, Black Stone Under a White Stone, has A Decision, To Reclaim an Ancient Name, Echoes of Life, Into Eternal Night and Past and Future. Both need Adventure Rank 40, and Act I could be started at rank 28 through a quick start after A New Star Approaches.
### LARGE +
Act I shows the Traveler and Paimon reaching Natlan, meeting Kachina, Mualani and Kinich, watching the Pilgrimage and visiting Mualani's village, where Atea is mortally wounded by the Abyss. In Act II the Night Warden Wars are won, but Kachina is not found in the Sacred Flame. The Traveler and friends go into the Night Kingdom to recover her. The Fatui Harbinger Capitano appears, challenges Mavuika and is wounded.
### ULTRA +
At the act's close, Mavuika gives up much of her power to keep the Sacred Flame lit and reveals a plan to find six warriors whose Names the Wayob will acknowledge. Mavuika also tells the Traveler she will have an Ancient Name forged for them. Rezanov, a Fatui under Capitano, asks whether to take the Pyro Gnosis from her weakened state; Capitano refuses, saying he respects her win and will not use dishonourable means.

## new_region
@version: 5.0
### STANDARD
Natlan's four launch subregions were the Basin of Unnumbered Flames, Coatepec Mountain, Tequemecan Valley and Toyac Springs. Story quests in the Tribal Chronicles series introduced three of the tribes: the People of the Springs, the Scions of the Canopy and the Children of Echoes.
### LARGE +
The series' acts were Where the Springs Return, Yupanqui's Turnfire and A Prayer for Blessings, Told to Crested Peaks (Acts I and II). New enemies include Secret Source Automatons, Avatars of Lava, Wayob Manifestations and Sauroform tribal warriors, with Koholasaurs, Tepetlisaurs and Yumkasaurs as the first Saurian companions.
### ULTRA +
Wildlife added includes the capybara, alpaca, red flamingo and Pyro Crystalfly. Books added include Maawe and Monetoo, Records of Hanan Pacha, The Tale of Qoyllor and Ukuku and The Volcano Lord and the Shadow Pins.

## entry: Tribal Chronicles
@keys: Tribal Chronicles
@version: 5.0
### STANDARD
The Story Quest series that gives each Natlan tribe its own chapter. Version 5.0 opened three: People of the Springs (Where the Springs Return, Acts I to III), Scions of the Canopy (Yupanqui's Turnfire, Acts I to III) and Children of Echoes (A Prayer for Blessings, Told to Crested Peaks, Acts I and II).
### LARGE +
Later versions continued the series: Act III of the Children of Echoes chapter in 5.1, Tlalocan and Mictlan in 5.2, and Teteocan in 5.5.
