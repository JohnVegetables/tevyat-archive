## summary
### LITE
Version 3.5, "Windblume's Breath", was released on 2023-03-01, after Version 3.4. It added Dehya and Mika, the Windblume's Breath festival event in Mondstadt and Archon Quest Chapter III Act VI. It opened no new region.
### STANDARD +
The Archon Quest Act, "Caribert", has the Traveler and Dainsleif meet Kaeya in Sumeru and then investigate a hut in Avidya Forest. Dehya's first Story Quest and a Hangout Event for Faruzan also arrived.
### LARGE +
Windblume's Breath was the Windblume Festival event of 2023. It was open to players who had finished Prologue Act III, with a quick start for those who had not completed Chapter III Act V or Albedo's Story Quest Act I, and its weapon reward was the claymore Mailed Flower.
### ULTRA +
This entry is a patch-note summary, not character lore. The Caribert Act reveals major information about Khaenri'ah, the Alberich family and the Traveler's sibling and is described in a version-tagged sub-entry.

## entry: Version 3.5 Windblume's Breath event
@keys: Windblume's Breath, Floral Pursuit, Mailed Flower
@keys2: Version 3.5
@version: 3.5
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
This flagship event ran from 2023-03-03 to 2023-03-20 (shop to 2023-03-27) and needed Adventure Rank 18 and Prologue Act III, "Song of the Dragon and Freedom". Its weapon reward was Mailed Flower. The story, "Windblume Encounters", has three parts: A Gathering of Outlanders, A Riddle Amidst the Crowds and Joy Above the Clouds.
### LARGE +
The event world quests were Floral Pursuit, Ballads of Breeze and Breezy Snapshots. Players could earn Floral Coupons and Festive Tickets to exchange for rewards. This is limited event content and is not commonly known in-world. The wiki's plot summary for the three story parts was incomplete when read, so their events are not described here.

## entry: Version 3.5 Lionsblood
@keys: Lionsblood, Mantichora Chapter
@keys2: Dehya
@version: 3.5
### STANDARD
"Lionsblood" is the first Act of Dehya's Story Quest, the Mantichora Chapter. It needs Adventure Rank 40 and the Archon Quest "Akasha Pulses, the Kalpa Flame Rises". It has three quests: Shade Upon Red Silk, Annals of the Scarlet Sands and Sincerity's Facade.
### LARGE +
Dehya, a mercenary of the Blazing Beasts, becomes uneasy about the Dakan Al-Ahmar brigade of Eremites, who have been robbing and killing travellers. With the Traveler she investigates in Sumeru's desert and learns the brigade's leader is her father, who adopted her.
### ULTRA +
Dehya's father Kusayla has died, and his former men, who feel guilty for being free of Deshret's Relics, are acting against that organisation with forged letters. The Traveler and Dehya storm Deshret's Relics headquarters and the Corps of Thirty takes the remaining Eremites into custody. A letter among Kusayla's belongings reveals he found Dehya as an abandoned infant in the desert, and she resolves to find her birth parents.

## entry: Version 3.5 Caribert
@keys: Caribert, Chapter III Act VI, Eide
@keys2: Avidya Forest, Kaeya, Dainsleif, Chlothar
@version: 3.5
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
"Caribert" is the sixth Act of Chapter III and needs Adventure Rank 35. Its four quests are Destined Encounter, Fortune-Mocking Pedigree, A Lamenter at Fate's End and Portended Fate. The Traveler and Dainsleif meet Kaeya in Port Ormos and then search the forest for a place Dainsleif remembers from his time with the Traveler's sibling.
### LARGE +
Kaeya says he once stowed away on a merchant caravan to Sumeru as a child, wanting to learn about Khaenri'ah, and that his surname Alberich is the last link to his homeland. Dainsleif then says the Alberich family founded the Abyss Order. In Avidya Forest the Traveler meets a man called Eide, who is caring for a masked hilichurl named Caribert, his illegitimate son, and tries to make medicine for him.
### ULTRA +
Eide says that when the gods cursed Khaenri'ah, pure-blooded Khaenri'ahns became immortal and the rest became hilichurls. At a hidden sanctuary a voice calling itself a sinner speaks, and Caribert later appears imbued with Abyssal power. Later Eide reveals he is Chlothar Alberich and, after the Traveler sees their sibling's reflection in a mirror, calls the Traveler "Prince" or "Princess" and vows to use the Abyss's power against the gods. Digging in the field turns up the remains of a man and a woman, and Dainsleif leaves to confirm who the sinner is. These are characters' statements and visions.
