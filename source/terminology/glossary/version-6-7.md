## summary
### LITE
Version Luna VIII (numbered 6.7 on the wiki), "Sunny Summer Fontinalia", was released on 2026-07-01 with the subtitle "Song of the Welkin Moon: Scherzo". It opened two new Nod-Krai areas, Dunanna Pit and the Frost Moon, added Sandrone as a playable five-star Cryo Claymore user, and brought a summer film-festival event in Fontaine. It had no Archon Quest.
### STANDARD +
It follows Version Luna VII and precedes Version 7.0. The headline story content was the Fontaine Story Quest Sunny Summer Fontinalia, the Frost Moon world-quest series Moon Gazing, and a long series of tea-party quests tied to Sandrone.
### LARGE +
New systems were the Cryo-and-Electro reaction Stellar-Conduct, the Witch's Revelation talent system, the Repertoire of Myriad Melodies music feature and the Ancient Moon's Remnant waypoints on the Frost Moon.
### ULTRA +
The wiki notes that the Frost Moon lies geographically outside Teyvat's surface, though the game counts it as a Nod-Krai subregion for gameplay purposes.

## release
@version: 6.7
### LITE
Released 2026-07-01, between Version Luna VII and 7.0.
### STANDARD +
Phase I banners were Heavenlit Prophecy (a Witch's Revelation Lightrace Wish), To the Looking-Glass the Mademoiselle Said (Sandrone) and Starry Night's Whispers (Citlali). Phase II, from 2026-07-21, was Somnias a Luna (Columbina) and a Raiden Shogun rerun.
### LARGE +
The new weapon was A Teaspoon of Transcendence, a five-star Claymore. Outfits were Whispers of Stars and Smoke (Citlali) and Hurlock Variations (Charlotte). The Spiral Abyss refreshed on July 16 with a Cryo and Electro focus, and a new Imaginarium Theater season opened on August 1.
### ULTRA +
The version also introduced a quest-dialogue change, first on Sunny Summer Fontinalia and Moon Gazing, letting players advance lines without waiting for them to finish. A Souvenir Shop run by the Voynich Guild opened for players who upgraded all Nod-Krai Meeting Points and both Ancient Moon's Remnants.

## areas
@version: 6.7
### STANDARD
Dunanna Pit lies in Nod-Krai, close to Fontaine and east of Pohjola. The Frost Moon is reached by progressing the Moon Gazing quests and has three parts: the Lunar Highlands, the Moontide Sea and the Dark Side of the Moon. Outside Ungien's Circle its turbulent Archaeolune energy alters gravity, giving higher jumps and slower falls.
### LARGE +
Per the wiki, the Frost Moon is the second of the Three Moons made by Nibelung and the only one to survive the war of funerary flame intact, though it was banished beyond the false sky. Dragons led by Ibni-Belum later settled it for Project Moongazer. The Moontide Sea came from Lusalim's failed Lunar Veil project, and Ungien's Circle is a vivarium built by the dragon Ungien.
### ULTRA +
Secret Source technology is present on the moon, and new enemies include the Precursor's Secret Source Matrix of Overseer Network and Prism Slimes. New wildlife are the Moonglow Frostfin Whale and Moonglow Jellyfish. The wiki says the Frost Moon was revitalised and drawn back inside the false sky in Act VIII (True Moon) of Song of the Welkin Moon.

## quests
@version: 6.7
### STANDARD
The Story Quest Sunny Summer Fontinalia has three acts: Should We Meet Again, Those We Have Lost and Those We Have Kept. World quests include the Moon Gazing series (Pioneers, Visitors From the Stars, Project Moongazer), Beneath the Lunar Sea, The Unmanned Vessel, Origin of the Sequence and Within the Sequence.
### LARGE +
Seven Witch's Revelation quests unlock extra talents for Wriothesley, Yae Miko, Cyno, Yumemizuki Mizuki, Qiqi, Diona and Beidou, after Revelations by Chance grants the Witch's Revelation Case. The quests need a character at level 70 or higher. A series of Sandrone's Portable Tea Table quests includes re-invitations for a range of guests.

## events
@version: 6.7
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
The flagship event Sunny Summer Fontinalia ran from 2026-07-01 to 2026-08-11 around the Fontinalia Film Festival, with Charlotte as the character reward. Players run an aquarium through Operation Deep-Dive Treasure, Kaleido-Catch and Curio Ledger. Other events were Final Long-Range Sightlines, Dance Dance Easy-Breezy Disco and Ley Line Overflow.
### LARGE +
When We Look Up at the Moon was an exploration mission running until 2026-09-22, giving 400 Primogems for completing Moon Gazing and for exploration milestones across the four new areas. The Story Quest stays playable after the event ends.

## entry: Stellar-Conduct
@keys: Stellar-Conduct, Starlight Prism, Polestar Field
@version: 6.7
### STANDARD
Stellar-Conduct is a Stellar Glimmer reaction between Cryo and Electro. It leaves a Starlight Prism lasting 7 seconds that sustains a Polestar Field, in which Cryo and Electro hits are recorded and buffs granted. This is a gameplay mechanic, not an in-world description.
