## summary
### LITE
Version 3.2, "Akasha Pulses, the Kalpa Flame Rises", was released on 2022-11-02. It brought Sumeru's Archon Quest, Chapter III, to its conclusion with Act V, and added the playable characters Nahida and Layla. It opened no new region.
### STANDARD +
It follows Version 3.1 and precedes Version 3.3. The version tagline describes a classified Akademiya project, carried out by the collective effort of the Six Darshans, approaching its final stage with an outcome that "is not up to them to decide".
### LARGE +
The Story Quest for Nahida, Sapientia Oromasdis Chapter Act I, "Lingering Warmth", came with the version, and Act II, "Homecoming", followed in Version 3.6.
### ULTRA +
This is the version in which the Sumeru Archon Quest ends and the story points forward to Fontaine; its revelations are listed in the sub-entry marked as a major spoiler.

## release
@version: 3.2
### LITE
Released 2022-11-02, between Version 3.1 and Version 3.3.
### STANDARD +
Phase I (2022-11-02) had The Moongrass' Enlightenment (Nahida) and Tapestry of Golden Flames (Yoimiya). Phase II (2022-11-18) had Everbloom Violet (Yae Miko and Layla) and Farewell of Snezhnaya (Tartaglia and Layla).
### LARGE +
The only new weapon was the 5-star catalyst A Thousand Floating Dreams. A new item, Nature's True Fruit, and a new Serenitea Pot realm layout, Sublime Spicewood, were also added.

## characters
@version: 3.2
### LITE
Nahida ("Physic of Purity", a 5-star Dendro catalyst user) and Layla ("Fantastical Evening Star", a 4-star Cryo sword user) joined the game.
### STANDARD +
Layla's Hangout Event, Act I, "Ever Silent Stars", arrived in Version 3.6; Layla could also be invited to the party through the Version 3.8 event Secret Summer Paradise.

## quests
@version: 3.2
### STANDARD
Archon Quest Chapter III Act V is "Akasha Pulses, the Kalpa Flame Rises". World Quests were "Next Time, On King of Invokations..." and the now-accessible "The Gourmet Supremos: Cleanup". The Serenitea Pot gained its Replication system.
### LARGE +
The wiki notes that the earlier inability to access "The Gourmet Supremos: Cleanup" had been listed as a bug and was resolved in this version.

## entry: Chapter III Act V revelations
@keys: Akasha Pulses the Kalpa Flame Rises, Chapter III Act V, Rukkhadevata erased
@version: 3.2
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
In Act V the Traveler and Sumeru's allies free Nahida from the Akasha and enter it with her. She takes the Electro Gnosis from Scaramouche, who had been made into a false god, and she and the Traveler reach Rukkhadevata's last memory. There Rukkhadevata tells her that saving Irminsul requires erasing her, and the forbidden knowledge is removed.
### LARGE +
Dottore then comes to bargain with Nahida in person. She agrees to hand over the Electro Gnosis on the condition that he destroys his other segments, and he in turn offers information about the sky of Teyvat for her Dendro Gnosis. After a feast, Nahida tells the Traveler that their sibling was recorded in Irminsul as appearing in Khaenri'ah, and that the Fatui had classed them as Teyvat's fourth "Descender". These details come from the wiki's quest summary.
