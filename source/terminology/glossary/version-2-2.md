## summary
### LITE
Version 2.2, "Into the Perilous Labyrinth of Fog", was released on 2021-10-13. It added fog-bound Tsurumi Island, the playable Thoma, and Aloy for all platforms. It had no Archon Quest.
### STANDARD +
Tsurumi Island's story is told by the "Through the Mists" world quest series, which follows on from the Seirai Stormchasers of 2.1. It tells how a child's ceremony for the Thunderbird Kanna Kapatcir ended in a curse.
### ULTRA +
The patch also added the Silken Courtyard realm layout, rifthound enemies and Sacred Sakura's Favor level 40.

## contents
### STANDARD
Characters: Thoma (4-star Pyro polearm); Aloy (5-star Cryo bow), previously PlayStation-only, now for all platforms. Weapons: Polar Star (5-star bow), Wavebreaker's Fin, Akuoumaru, Mouun's Moon.
### LARGE +
Banners: "Farewell of Snezhnaya" (Tartaglia) in phase 1; "Moment of Bloom" (Hu Tao and Thoma) from 2021-11-02. Hangout events Series III: Sayu Act I "Yoohoo Art: Seichou no Jutsu" and Thoma Act I "A Housekeeper's Daily Chores". New enemies: Rockfond and Thundercraven Rifthounds and their whelps.
### ULTRA +
Events were Labyrinth Warriors, Shadow of the Ancients, Tuned to the World's Sounds, Dreams of Bloom and a Ley Line Overflow, all limited-time. Other world quests were The Saga of Mr. Forgetful and Clean House.

## story
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
No Archon Quest. The Traveler helps the novelist Sumida by going to Tsurumi Island, where the locals repeat a ceremony and the Maushiro keeps vanishing. The truth is that the child Ruu offered himself to the Thunderbird, who cursed the island to relive it. The Traveler lifts the curse by letting Ruu sing for her once more.
### LARGE +
Kama's boat reaches the fog-bound island. The Peculiar Pinion found in Seirai (2.1) shows memories of the Thunderbird. Ruu offered himself because the island's situation was worsening. The Raiden Shogun had killed the Thunderbird, so the Traveler and Paimon say she has moved to Seirai Island, and the Pinion carries Ruu there to sing. With the loop over, Ruu will pass on.
### ULTRA +
Tsurumi Island also has a three-day cycle that changes enemy and chest spawns. This is a gameplay feature on the wiki, separate from the story.

## placement
### STANDARD
Follows 2.1 and precedes 2.3. The Hu Tao banner also features Thoma. The quest series states that Kapatcir was killed by the Raiden Shogun.

## entry: Version 2.2 Tsurumi Island
@keys: Version 2.2, Update 2.2, Patch 2.2
@keys2: Tsurumi Island, Through the Mists, Kanna Kapatcir, Thunderbird, Ruu, Maushiro
@version: 2.2
### STANDARD
Tsurumi Island is a fog-shrouded island in Inazuma that once held two ancient civilisations: an underground Pre-Thunderbird one and the Thunderbird civilisation that worshipped Kanna Kapatcir. Its modern inhabitants are hilichurls, rifthounds and automatons. The fog is lifted by the quest series "Through the Mists". Its legend: a child, Ruu, offered himself to the Thunderbird, and she cursed the island to relive that ceremony until she heard his song again.
### LARGE +
The series' parts: "A Particularly Particular Author", "Octave of the Maushiro", "The Sea of Fog and the Rite of the Trees", and "The Sun-Wheel and Mt. Kanna". Kama's ancestors came from Tsurumi, and Kama tells Ruu they lived happily elsewhere and only regretted not returning for him.

### STANDARD
Version 2.2 made Thoma playable, a Kamisato Clan attendant already seen in 2.0 and 2.1, and extended Aloy to all platforms, delivered by mail at Adventure Rank 20 or above. The Hu Tao banner ran beside Thoma's. Banners were limited-time and have been rerun.
