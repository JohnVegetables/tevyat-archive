## summary
### LITE
Version 1.3, "All That Glitters", was released on 2021-02-03. It is the Lantern Rite update: it added the Liyue characters Xiao and Hu Tao and a large festival event celebrating Liyue's Lantern Rite. It introduced no new region and no Archon Quest.

### STANDARD +
Content was centred on Liyue. Two Story Quests opened (Xiao's Alatus Chapter Act I and Hu Tao's Papilio Charontis Chapter Act I), the Geovishap and Primo Geovishap were added as enemies, and the Archive gained a "Living Beings" section.

## contents
### STANDARD
New playable characters: Xiao ("Vigilant Yaksha", 5-star Anemo Polearm) and Hu Tao ("Fragrance in Thaw", 5-star Pyro Polearm). New weapons: Lithic Spear, Lithic Blade, Primordial Jade Cutter and Staff of Homa. The event wishes ran Invitation to Mundane Life (Xiao) first, then Dance of Lanterns (Keqing), then Moment of Bloom (Hu Tao).
### LARGE +
New world content included the World Quests "Bough Keeper: Dainsleif", "No Restoring This Past Land of Beauty", "Perfect Shot" and "Tianqiu Treasure Trail", plus 26 festive World Quests delivered through the "Lantern Rite Tales" part of the event. The Parametric Transformer gadget and Dust of Azoth (used at the Crafting Bench to convert the elemental type of some ascension materials) were added.
### ULTRA +
Other additions listed for the version: new recipes (Fullmoon Egg, Vegetarian Abalone, Stone Harbor Delicacies), Archive "Living Beings" tracking of defeated enemies, achievement progress and a Character Showcase on profile pages, and iOS controller support. Zhongli's revamp and a Geo resonance and shield rebalance were announced for this version. These are game-system changes, not story.

## story
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
No Archon Quest was added. Xiao's Alatus Chapter Act I, "Butterfly's Dream", has the Traveler meet a fraudulent "adeptus" called Starsnatcher near Wangshu Inn and then find Xiao, who admits that his karma is behind the agitated hilichurls. Hu Tao's Papilio Charontis Chapter Act I, "Yet the Butterfly Flutters Away", introduces her as the 77th Director of the Wangsheng Funeral Parlor, together with her intern Meng.
### LARGE +
In the Xiao quest a book titled "Yakshas: The Guardian Adepti" tells how Rex Lapis assigned adepti to become Yakshas to fight demons born of the gods' wrath; Xiao later dismisses the book as fan fiction. In the Hu Tao quest the Traveler helps her with Meng's long-standing paranormal trouble after a failed attempt to market the Parlor.

## lantern_rite
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
The Lantern Rite event ran from 2021-02-10 to 2021-02-28. It was a limited-time event celebrating Liyue's Lantern Rite Festival, involving Xiao and honouring the deceased adeptus Skybracer. Its parts were All That Glitters, Lantern Rite Tales, Theater Mechanicus, the Xiao Market and Stand by Me; in Stand by Me a player could claim a 4-star Liyue character of their choice (Beidou, Chongyun, Ningguang, Xiangling, Xingqiu or Xinyan). Because it was a limited event, its story is canon but not experienced by every Traveler.

## placement
### STANDARD
Follows 1.2 "The Chalk Prince and the Dragon" and precedes 1.4 "Invitation of Windblume". Nothing in this version depends on later events.
