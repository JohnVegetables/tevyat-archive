## summary
### LITE
Version Luna VI (numbered 6.5 on the wiki, with the Archon Quest subtitle "Song of the Welkin Moon: Rondo") was released on 2026-04-08, after Luna V. It added Linnea, the area Windrest Peak in northern Mondstadt and the sky realm Temple of Space. It had no Archon Quest.
### STANDARD +
Linnea's first Story Quest, the Alcyon Chapter Act I, is set in Nod-Krai. The world quest series "A Long Day in the Mountains" introduces Windrest Peak and the Temple of Space, which is reached from its Old Sanatorium Site.
### LARGE +
The version also ran the event "Where Waves Meet the Reef" with the character Jahoda as a reward. Windrest Peak's world quests begin at Dornman Port with "The Wind Has Ceased".
### ULTRA +
This entry is a patch-note summary, not character lore. Details of the Temple of Space and Linnea's family are major spoilers and sit in version-tagged sub-entries.

## release
@version: 6.5
### LITE
Released 2026-04-08. Previous: Luna V. Next: Luna VII.
### STANDARD +
New character: Linnea ("Augur of Wonders", 5-star Geo bow). New weapon: Golden Frostbound Oath (5-star bow). New areas: Windrest Peak and Temple of Space.
### LARGE +
Phase one wishes: "Ya-hoho! Compendium" (Linnea) and "Piercing Shot's Crimson Wake" (Chasca). Phase two, from 2026-04-28: "Moonsong of the Groves" (Lauma), "Temptation of the Crimson Sands" (Nefer) and "Dewlit Tranquility" (Fontaine Chronicled Wish).
### ULTRA +
New events: Where Waves Meet the Reef, Rapid Capture and Surveying & Mapping Studies. New monsters: The Open-Eyed, Domain Keeper, Churldric and "Prince Mahavasudeva-Yava". New materials included the Etherwing Moth and Windrest Flower, and wildlife such as the Windvigil Angler Gull and Camelhorn Deer. A new achievement was "Demon Mountain's Breath".

## entry: Version 6.5 Windrest Peak
@keys: Windrest Peak
@keys2: Dornman Port, Millhaven, Old Sanatorium Site, Version Luna VI
@version: 6.5
### STANDARD
Windrest Peak is the northernmost part of Mondstadt, centred on the port town of Dornman Port, with Millhaven and the Old Sanatorium Site nearby. It is new in this version.
### LARGE +
The wiki notes that Windrest Peak has different clouds and clearer weather than the rest of Mondstadt as it was before this version. A loading tip says wind is forbidden from entering the Temple of Space, which leaves Windrest Peak perpetually windy.
### ULTRA +
According to the wiki, the Temple of Space was originally connected to a temple at Windrest Peak where survivors of Arcadia worshipped Istaroth. After the temple above was lifted away, the earthly one fell into ruin and later became the site of the local sanatorium.

## entry: Version 6.5 Temple of Space
@keys: Temple of Space, Segment-Domain Tau, Apathic Interval, Uplifted
@keys2: Asmoday, Nihilita, Dex, Windrest Peak
@version: 6.5
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The Temple of Space is an area in the sky above Windrest Peak, with its own map and exploration progress. It is unlocked through the world quest "The Wind Has Ceased" and entered from a marker at the Old Sanatorium Site. The wiki says it was created by Asmoday, called the Ruler of Space, as an exhibition hall that preserves the records of fallen civilisations.
### LARGE +
The wiki lists Hyperborea, Lang-Gan, Delphi Pytho, Ochkanatlan, Remuria and Arcadia among those preserved. It has three exhibition areas: Luyang Academy, the Mahavaipulya Chamber and the Desert Pavilion. Inside, time stands still, and residents do not age, tire or die. A juvenile Watcher named Dex lets the player manipulate objects in the temple.
### ULTRA +
Asmoday also made the Watchers Nihilita and Melanta, who bring chosen people called the Uplifted into the temple and enforce her decree that it exist only for preservation, with all innovation forbidden. The Uplifted pass through the Apathic Interval and cannot leave unless they build a Path to the Heavens in the Corridor of Nothingness, which requires offering up all their memories. The uplifting is said to have ended. These are the wiki's summaries of quest text.

## entry: Version 6.5 Alcyon Chapter
@keys: Alcyon Chapter, Up! Toward Homecoming Wings, Spirit Envoy Championship, Celaeno
@keys2: Linnea, Lumi
@version: 6.5
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
"Up! Toward Homecoming Wings" is the first Act of Linnea's Story Quest, the Alcyon Chapter. It needs Adventure Rank 40 and "An Elegy for Faded Moonlight" from Song of the Welkin Moon, and the chapter is fully finished only after "A Nocturne of the Far North". Its quests are The Championship Begins, Twice Upon a Gaze and Tread the Right Path.
### LARGE +
In Nasha Town Katheryne tells the Traveler about the Spirit Envoy Championship, which the Frostmoon Scions hold to strengthen bonds between people and animals, and which only allows common species. Linnea, an Adventurers' Guild advisor who lectures at Speranza, enters with the Jack Frost fae Lumi, while the Traveler enters with Aguara, and Mualani and Arataki Itto also compete.
### ULTRA +
Linnea is a changeling with wings on her head. She learns that Celaeno, a rival competitor with the Radiant Bladehorn Haemus, is her sister and that they belong to the rare Augury Birds, fae who can foresee their own futures. Their parents had Linnea live among humans so her ability would develop later. In the semifinal Lumi grows in size and Linnea realises Geo is what makes her special among Jack Frosts. The Traveler wins the final, and Celaeno promises to tell their parents how Linnea is doing. Linnea decides that a foreseen future need not be accepted as it is.
