## summary
### LITE
Version 6.1, officially "Luna II" (Song of the Welkin Moon: Reprise), was released on 2025-10-22 as "An Elegy for Faded Moonlight". It added no new area. Its main content was Archon Quest Acts III and IV, the 5-star Dendro Catalyst Nefer and the open beta of Miliastra Wonderland.
### STANDARD +
It follows Version 6.0 (Luna I) and precedes Luna III (6.2). In the Archon Quest, the Traveler and the people of Nod-Krai finish the fight against the Wild Hunt's mastermind, Rerir.
### LARGE +
The wiki also lists the Wonderland Manekin, a 5-star Sword character tied to Miliastra Wonderland. Act III is "A Nation That Doesn't Exist" and Act IV, which gives the version its title, is "An Elegy for Faded Moonlight". Act IV ends with Rerir expelled from Teyvat through a moon gate.
### ULTRA +
Act IV makes Nefer's past a stage of the plot, with a ritual that opens into her memories of the Sumeru desert. The version also re-runs Furina, Arlecchino and Zhongli in its banners.

## release
@version: 6.1
### LITE
Released 2025-10-22, after Luna I (6.0) and before Luna III (6.2).
### STANDARD +
Phase I banners: Temptation of the Crimson Sands (Nefer) and Chanson of Many Waters (Furina). Phase II: The Hearth's Ashen Shadow (Arlecchino) and Gentry of Hermitage (Zhongli).
### LARGE +
New weapons were Reliquary of Truth (5-star Catalyst), Sacrificer's Staff (4-star Polearm) and Dawning Frost (4-star Catalyst). New events included Ruins Exploration: Fieldwise Center, Dance Dance Dynamic Disco, Saurian Sojourn and Overflowing Abundance. Imaginarium Theater seasons began on 2025-11-01 and 2025-12-01; the Spiral Abyss updated on 2025-11-16.
### ULTRA +
The Archon Quest was joined by a set of Nefer World Quests (A Small Venture, Research Spillover, The Disturbance Caused by Theft, The Haunted Pirate Shipwreck, The Suspicious Hermit, The Vanishing Bounty Target, The Dust Settles and The Man Who Once Lied) that need Nefer in the account, plus The Spurious Cannot Be Made Genuine. The new monster is Frostnight Herra, dropping Radiant Antler. New recipes included Lohikeitto and Lakkaberry Pie, new namecards Nefer: Secrets, Travel Notes: Bygone Ruins and Nod-Krai: Moonlight, and the Genius Invokation TCG gained Varesa, Ifa and Hydro Tulpa.

## characters
@version: 6.1
### STANDARD
Nefer ("Secret Beneath the Sands") is a 5-star Dendro Catalyst. The Wonderland Manekin is a 5-star Sword character for Miliastra Wonderland.
### LARGE +
In the story, Nefer is the boss of Nasha Town's Curatorium of Secrets and, as Act IV shows, grew up in the Sumeru desert. Her own file carries the detail.

## miliastra_wonderland
@version: 6.1
### STANDARD
Miliastra Wonderland entered open beta in this version, with creator-made stages, social features and customisation content.
### LARGE +
The related new systems are the Miliastra Pass, Ode, Explore Wonderlands, the event Invitation to Wonderland: A Curious Awakening, the Wonderland Level and the Lobby.

## archon_quest
@version: 6.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Song of the Welkin Moon Act III, "A Nation That Doesn't Exist", and Act IV, "An Elegy for Faded Moonlight". Both need Adventure Rank 40.
### LARGE +
Act III (Hidden in the Shadows, Special Operation, Everlasting As the Moon): Rerir, now with a body, attacks Columbina after the Wild Hunt drains the quarry's kuuvahki. Varka and the group make a secret base, Nefer is persuaded to help, Sandrone allows Columbina to use her bureau, and Arlecchino, who is a descendant of the Crimson Moon Dynasty, opens a way to the Iridescent Marrow. Act IV (All Is Foretold, Dance of Death: Serpent and Scorpion, Echoes of Fate): the group learns Rerir's past with Dainsleif's help, he is trapped in Nefer's chessbox and finally expelled from Teyvat.
### ULTRA +
Act III: Columbina and the Traveler are cornered by the Wild Hunt at Thunderclap Reef until Arlecchino burns it away. Arlecchino's hands are corrupted by the Abyss when she forces open the way out of the Iridescent Marrow's domain; she refuses the Traveler's offer to purify it. She tells Columbina to change her name after Rerir is sorted out. Nefer's ritual shows that Rerir was a Khaenri'ahn under King Irmin, engaged to Tholindis, and ordered to kill descendants of the Crimson Moon Dynasty. Act IV: Albedo, with Durin (Mini Durin from Simulanka), and Dainsleif join; ritual memories show Rerir ordered to eliminate his own fiancee, helping Dainsleif free Vedrfolnir, then finding Tholindis falling into a portal, accepting a voice's offer of power. Rerir intrudes on Nefer's own memory-domain, and the group fights him; Albedo and Durin siphon his power into Columbina, who opens a moon gate that Dainsleif holds from behind. Nefer is blinded temporarily. The act closes with Sandrone, Columbina and the Traveler at the bureau, and with Sandrone mentioning the Lord-Artificer, whom the Traveler recognises as Alain Guillotin.

## entry: A Nation That Doesn't Exist
@keys: A Nation That Doesn't Exist, Hidden in the Shadows, Special Operation, Everlasting As the Moon
@version: 6.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Act III of Song of the Welkin Moon. The Wild Hunt drains the kuuvahki from places such as the quarry, and Rerir attacks Columbina. Varka rescues the group and brings the Curatorium's Nefer into the plan. Columbina goes with the Traveler to the Kuuvahki Experimental Design Bureau, where Sandrone agrees to help, then to Arlecchino, who agrees to find the Iridescent Marrow.
### LARGE +
Nefer's ritual with Jahoda and Lauma shows Rerir's identity as a Khaenri'ahn under Irmin, and a memory of Tholindis that is oddly in his possession. Columbina and the Traveler recover the Iridescent Marrow from a domain guarded by riftwolves, and Columbina gains it.

## entry: An Elegy for Faded Moonlight
@keys: An Elegy for Faded Moonlight, All Is Foretold, Dance of Death: Serpent and Scorpion, Echoes of Fate
@version: 6.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Act IV of Song of the Welkin Moon and the title of Version 6.1. Dainsleif explains how the Five Sinners of Khaenri'ah came to be; Nefer's ritual shows Rerir's past and then pulls the group into her own memories. Rerir is sealed in Nefer's chessbox, breaks out, and is thrown out of Teyvat through a moon gate.
### LARGE +
Rerir's past, as shown in the ritual: he was told to kill Tholindis, helped Dainsleif free his brother Vedrfolnir, and then found Tholindis stepping into a portal so that he could live. Nefer's memory-domain shows her adopted family in the Shimti tribe, their destruction by the Iab tribe, her time at the Sumeru Akademiya and a temple of Thoth, one of the Seven Pillars of King Deshret.
