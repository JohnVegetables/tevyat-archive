## summary
### LITE
Version 3.8, "Secret Summer Paradise", was released on 2023-07-05. It is a summer update centred on the limited-time area Veluriyam Mirage, a domain inside a bottle that the Traveler visits during the flagship event of the same name. It added no Archon Quest and no new playable characters.
### STANDARD +
It sits between Version 3.7 and Version 4.0, the last update before the game moved on to Fontaine. The event reward was the already-released Cryo character Layla, who could be invited to the party by collecting event currency.
### LARGE +
The wiki notes that this is the first and only version with no new characters, weapons or food items. Its new story content was a Kaeya Hangout Event, two permanent World Quests and four limited-time World Quests, rather than main-story chapters.
### ULTRA +
Because the headline content was a limited-time event, nearly everything about the Veluriyam Mirage is canon but not universally known; only people who played during the event period would recall it in detail.

## release
@version: 3.8
### LITE
Released 2023-07-05, between Version 3.7 and Version 4.0.
### STANDARD +
Banner phases reran Eula and Klee in Phase I, and Sangonomiya Kokomi and the Wanderer in Phase II. New outfits were Klee's Blossoming Starlight and Kaeya's Sailwind Shadow.
### LARGE +
Genius Invokation TCG gained cards for Candace, Kaedehara Kazuha and Yanfei. The Spiral Abyss updated its Floor 11 disorder to a 75 percent Physical DMG bonus and refreshed the Floor 11 and 12 monster lineups.

## veluriyam_mirage
@version: 3.8
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
The limited-time area was called the Veluriyam Mirage, colloquially Bottleland. Official text describes it as a quiet, beautiful domain hidden inside a bottle, home to special Hydro life-forms, and as a rumoured desert domain that grants the heart's desire to those who enter. The same text asks whether the crowd of travellers filling the bottle is nothing but a mirage.
### LARGE +
Exploration features included Joyeux Vouchers found in voucher boxes and chests, Hydro Eidolons that start challenges, Streaming Projectors that put the player into a two-dimensional platformer called a Preprint, the Choo-Choo Cart for fast travel and challenges, and Gleaming Lamps.
### ULTRA +
A Story detail from the wiki: the Mirage's buildings were reportedly redesigned by Kaveh, according to Idyia in the quest "Glaze Domain, Multum in Parvo". The wiki says the area is supposedly within Sumeru; see the uncertainties.

## event
@version: 3.8
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
Secret Summer Paradise ran from 2023-07-05 to 2023-08-16 and was a flagship event. Its story had three acts (An Invitation From Afar!, Mirage Paradise in Crisis!, Dreams and First Encounters!) and four gameplay modes: Spino Blaster, Sojourns of the Barking Fox, Dance of Flashing Thought and Bing-Bang Finchball. It required Adventure Rank 18 and the Archon Quest Song of the Dragon and Freedom.
### LARGE +
Collecting Fascinating Phenocrysts invited Layla, and collecting Joyeux Vouchers earned Kaeya's outfit Sailwind Shadow, which was sold in the outfit shop after the version ended. Recommended earlier quests were the Archon Quest Akasha Pulses, the Kalpa Flame Rises, Eula's Aphros Delos Chapter Act I and Kokomi's Dracaena Somnolenta Chapter Act I.

## quests
@version: 3.8
### STANDARD
New quests were the Kaeya Hangout Event Act I, "Shenanigans and Sweet Wine"; the World Quests "Purbiruni's Commandment" and "Recollections of a Fontainian"; and the limited-time World Quests "A Starry Night, as Remembered", "Daiya's Three-Day Reverie", "Returning Curios" and "Capturing Light and Shadow".
### LARGE +
"A Starry Night, as Remembered" could not be accepted or completed after the end of 3.8.

## entry: Secret Summer Paradise
@keys: Secret Summer Paradise, Veluriyam Mirage, Bottleland
@version: 3.8
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
Secret Summer Paradise is the Version 3.8 flagship summer event. The Traveler and Paimon return to the Knights of Favonius headquarters and learn of a special adventure, which leads to the Veluriyam Mirage, a bottle domain. Layla was the character reward.
### LARGE +
The event ran 2023-07-05 to 2023-08-16 with its story unlocking in three acts across the first days of the version.
