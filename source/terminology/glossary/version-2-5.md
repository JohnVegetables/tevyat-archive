## summary
### LITE
Version 2.5, "When the Sakura Bloom", was released on 2022-02-16. It added Yae Miko, Kagura's Verity and two Inazuma Story Quests. It had no new region or Archon Quest.
### STANDARD +
"Transient Dreams" resolves Ei's reconsideration of eternity and ends with the abolition of the Sakoku Decree. "The Great Narukami Offering" is Yae Miko's first Story Quest and involves a rumoured wish-granting book.
### ULTRA +
The patch also introduced the Magatsu Mitake Narukami no Mikoto weekly boss and the Trounce Domain "End of the Oneiric Euthymia", where the Shogun puppet can be fought again.

## contents
### STANDARD
Character: Yae Miko (5-star Electro catalyst). Weapons: Kagura's Verity (5-star catalyst) and Oathsworn Eye (4-star catalyst). Banner: "Everbloom Violet" (Yae Miko) in phase 1.
### LARGE +
Phase 2 reran "Reign of Serenity" (Raiden Shogun) and "Drifting Luminescence" (Sangonomiya Kokomi) from 2022-03-08. New enemies: Shadowy Husks (Standard Bearer, Line Breaker, Defender). A new Coral Butterfly was added.
### ULTRA +
Events: Three Realms Gateway Offering, Divine Ingenuity, Of Drink A-Dreaming, Hyakunin Ikki (from 2022-03-18) and Overflowing Mastery (from 2022-03-21), all limited-time.

## story
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Rifthounds attack the Sacred Sakura, and Ei appears to help the Traveler defend it. The Sacred Sakura's roots manifest memories of the Khaenri'ah cataclysm 500 years ago, when monsters also struck Inazuma and Kitsune Saiguu died leading its defence. Ei duels her Shogun puppet inside Makoto's consciousness, wins, and then offers it a role as her kagemusha. She abolishes the Sakoku Decree.
### LARGE +
Makoto's fragment, kept in the sword Musou Isshin, apologises to Ei for suddenly handing her the rulership and for not persuading her gently, and gives her a seed that grows into the Sacred Sakura. Before the duel, Ei had sent the Traveler to tell Yae Miko to govern Inazuma in her stead.
### ULTRA +
Gameplay note: after the quest, the "A Nation Closed by Thunder" climate ends and travellers can leave Inazuma by sea freely. In "The Great Narukami Offering", Miko recognises the wish-granting book's summoning spirit as her friend Urakusai, who meant no harm and wanted youkai to leave life without regrets; a Hyakkiyakou is held in farewell.

## placement
### STANDARD
Follows 2.4 and precedes 2.6. Both Story Quests require Adventure Rank 40 and Chapter II Act III; Transient Dreams also requires Reflections of Mortality, and Miko's quest requires Transient Dreams. Yae Miko appears later as a guest star in 7.1's Imaginarium Theater.

## entry: Version 2.5 Transient Dreams
@keys: Version 2.5, Update 2.5, Patch 2.5, Transient Dreams
@keys2: Imperatrix Umbrosa Chapter, Raiden Shogun, Ei, Makoto, Sacred Sakura, Sakoku Decree
@version: 2.5
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Transient Dreams is the second act of the Raiden Shogun's Story Quest, the Imperatrix Umbrosa Chapter. Its quests are "Cleansing Light", "Farewell to the Past" and "Radiant Sakura". Ei joins the Traveler in tracking rifthounds around the Sacred Sakura and Araumi, meets echoes of the 500-year-old cataclysm, and shares that Makoto went to Khaenri'ah without telling her and died; she then decided to pursue eternity.
### LARGE +
Ei describes the Sacred Sakura as a tree that purifies the ley lines, whose roots can evoke memories. Furuyama's tea set, Kitamura, Fujiki and Fukami all appear as echoes. She is taken to a cave beneath the Grand Narukami Shrine for her duel. The Shogun puppet thinks she has fallen to erosion; the Traveler returns later and the duel ends. Afterwards Ei looks at Inazuma's current state and abolishes the Sakoku Decree.

## entry: Version 2.5 Yae Miko and The Great Narukami Offering
@keys: Version 2.5, Update 2.5, Patch 2.5
@keys2: Yae Miko, Divina Vulpes Chapter, Great Narukami Offering, Urakusai, Yae Publishing House
@version: 2.5
### STANDARD
Version 2.5 introduced Yae Miko, the Guuji of the Grand Narukami Shrine, who also runs Yae Publishing House. Her Story Quest "The Great Narukami Offering" has four quests: "Mysterious Urban Legend", "Friendship in Writing", "A Centuries-Long Dream" and "Banquet of Parting". A book on summoning spirits causes odd behaviour; the Traveler and Miko write a rival novel, trace the author, and learn that the spirit, Urakusai, meant no harm. The banner was limited-time and has been rerun.
