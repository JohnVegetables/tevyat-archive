## summary
### LITE
Version 5.3, "Incandescent Ode of Resurrection", was released on 2025-01-01. It added no new region. Its main content was Chapter V Act V, which ends the Natlan war, and three new characters: Mavuika (five-star Pyro Claymore), Citlali (five-star Cryo Catalyst) and Lan Yan (four-star Anemo Catalyst). It also gave the Traveler a Pyro form.
### STANDARD +
It follows Version 5.2 and precedes 5.4. In Act V the Traveler receives an Ancient Name, goes with Mavuika into the Night Kingdom to end the Abyss threat, and then helps decide what Natlan's victory will cost.
### LARGE +
Mavuika's own Story Quest, Sol Invictus Chapter, began in this version with As the Blazing Sun, and the Tribal Chronicles chapter Memories Flowing With Color received its third act, The Truth of the Battle of Seven Colors.
### ULTRA +
The Archon Quest's final scenes point the Traveler towards Snezhnaya and Nod-Krai, the next regions of the story.

## release
@version: 5.3
### LITE
Released 2025-01-01, between 5.2 and 5.4.
### STANDARD +
Phase I banners were Ancient Flame Ablaze (Mavuika) and Starry Night's Whispers (Citlali). Phase II banners were The Hearth's Ashen Shadow (Arlecchino and Lan Yan) and Illuminating Lightning (Clorinde and Lan Yan), and a Chronicled Wish named Remembrance of Jade and Stone.
### LARGE +
New five-star weapons were A Thousand Blazing Suns (Claymore) and Starcaller's Watch (Catalyst). Outfits were Cherries Snow-Laden (Hu Tao) and New Year's Cheer (Xiangling). A new Serenitea Pot realm layout, Tletl Ilhuicatl, was unlocked with the Scroll of the Fiery Firmament.
### ULTRA +
Imaginarium Theater's first season of the version opened on 2025-02-01 (Pyro, Hydro and Anemo required). The Repertoire of Myriad Melodies music system and the gadgets Ukulele and Djem Djem Drum arrived. Spiral Abyss refreshed on 2025-01-16.

## archon_quest
@version: 5.3
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Chapter V Act V has five quests: Subterranean Ruins, As the Sun Rises and Sets, Journey of Stars and Flames, Where All Hopes Lie and When All Becomes a Monument. It needs Adventure Rank 40.
### LARGE +
Xilonen finishes the Traveler's Ancient Name, Tumaini, which means hope. After a feast and a test of the Name, the Traveler and Mavuika enter the Night Kingdom to destroy the Abyssal core, fighting the dragon-like Gosoythoth with the help of spirits who guard the path. The Traveler receives Pyro power from the Lord of the Night during the fight. The Sacred Flame briefly goes out above, then the war is won.
### ULTRA +
Afterwards, Natlan celebrates the Traveler, and Chasca crowns them. Mavuika is still slated to die because she used the Ruler of Death's power. At the ruins of Ochkanatlan the Traveler and Citlali find a rift in the sky, and Ronova demands Mavuika's death as the price. Capitano arrives with Ororon and offers himself in her place, merging his life force with the Lord of the Night, using a curse-given immortality to put Ronova in an unresolvable paradox. Mavuika writes a letter to her sister Hine and burns it, and the Traveler leaves their crown at a new monument to the fallen.
Mavuika also tells the Traveler that Capitano had asked the Tsaritsa to cancel his mission to take the Pyro Gnosis and that the request was granted. She warns that entering Snezhnaya would put the Traveler in conflict with the Fatui, and says the Doctor's subordinates are investigating relics that appeared in the sky, with a stronghold in Nod-Krai.

## entry: Pilgrim's Chronicle
@keys: Pilgrim's Chronicle
@version: 5.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
A parchment made by the Lord of the Night that records the Traveler's deeds in Natlan, past and future, so that Xilonen can forge a Name for an outsider. It first recorded their help in Kachina's victory in the Pilgrimage.
