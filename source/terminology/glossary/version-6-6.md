## summary
### LITE
Version Luna VII (numbered 6.6 on the wiki), "Truth Amongst the Pages of Purana", was released on 2026-05-20 and is subtitled "Song of the Welkin Moon: Andante". It added Acts IX and X of the Song of the Welkin Moon Archon Quest, which bring an Irminsul crisis to Sumeru and end it, and the playable characters Nicole and Prune, followed by Lohen in the second half. It opened no new area.
### STANDARD +
It follows Version 6.5 and precedes Version Luna VIII (6.7). The Archon Quest begins with the Traveler in Nod-Krai and then returns to Sumeru. The tagline describes an absurd dream in which a not-yet-decided future comes briefly into view.
### LARGE +
The version announced the first season of Imaginarium Theater for 2026-06-01, and introduced the Witch's Homework World Quest series and a score album titled Hexerei: Ballad of Whimsical Polyphony.
### ULTRA +
Much of this version's story is spoiler-heavy; it is recorded in a marked sub-entry so that readers who want only release facts can skip it.

## release
@version: 6.6
### LITE
Released 2026-05-20, between Version Luna VI and Version Luna VIII.
### STANDARD +
Phase I (2026-05-20) had Angel's Reverie (Nicole and Prune) and Rubedo, of White Stone Born (Durin and Prune). Phase II (2026-06-09) had Frostedge Nocturne (Lohen) and Ancient Flame Ablaze (Mavuika).
### LARGE +
New weapons were the 5-star catalyst Angelos' Heptades and the 5-star polearm Disaster and Remorse, plus a new weapon appearance, Super Awesome Magic Key. The new artifact sets were Celestial Gift and Disenchantment in Deep Shadow.

## characters
@version: 6.6
### LITE
Nicole ("Clamor Within", a 5-star Pyro catalyst user of the Hexenzirkel), Prune ("Childlike Dreams Cherished", a 4-star Anemo catalyst user) and Lohen ("Sharpened Arrowhead", a 5-star Cryo polearm user and Knights of Favonius vice captain) were added.
### STANDARD +
Nicole and Prune debuted on 2026-05-20, and Lohen on 2026-06-09. The wiki gives all three the Hexerei team bonus. Lohen's Story Quest is the Lepus Miles Chapter, Act I, "A Knight's Legacy".

## quests
@version: 6.6
### STANDARD
Archon Quest: Song of the Welkin Moon Act IX, "As All Falls to Emptiness", and Act X, "Truth Amongst the Pages of Purana". World Quests were the Beyond Silver and Fine Gold chain (Nicole), the Witch's Homework series with "A Proposition Beyond Refutation", "Proof of Strength" and "The Role of a Guide...?", and "A Window Into the World".
### LARGE +
The version added a Guiding Notes function for active quests and a quest-saving notification, the Repertoire of Myriad Melodies album Hexerei: Ballad of Whimsical Polyphony with score themes Eclipsing Flame and Demon-Slaying Gale, and the weekly reward feature To Temper Thyself and Journey Far.

## entry: Song of the Welkin Moon Acts IX and X
@keys: As All Falls to Emptiness, Truth Amongst the Pages of Purana, Of Myriad Paths Flux and Dissolution, Irminsul anomaly
@version: 6.6
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Act IX: an anomaly stops Irminsul from recording and processing information, Nahida takes over its work and collapses, and Sumeru's people evacuate to Caravan Ribat. Dottore's Fatui, the Jinn and a barrier around Sumeru complicate matters, while Nicole and Nefer arrive from Nod-Krai and the Traveler wakes Liloupar. Act X: the group overloads Irminsul through the Akasha System and Aaru, the Traveler brings Nahida the Pyro Gnosis from Natlan, and Nahida burns the corrupted tree. Dottore dies in the process.
### LARGE +
Per the wiki summary, Nahida explains that the attacker entered Irminsul through death, and a second Irminsul tree emerges in Sumeru City. Wanderer fights Dottore, who reveals that he knows the Wanderer's erased identity as Scaramouche. Nahida asks permission of the three Shades before burning Irminsul, which took seven days and nights, and Aaru took over the tree's role of collecting information. The Pyro Gnosis disappears without a trace. A letter from Pulcinella to Nahida says he had killed the Harbinger Sandrone and apologises for Dottore's trouble. Liloupar removes Collei's god remains at her request and fades away.

## entry: Version 6.6 events and gameplay
@keys: Phantasmal Pals, Trials of the Brave Warrior, Kaleidoscopic Color Chase, Frost-Surging Moon
@version: 6.6
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
Limited events were Phantasmal Pals, Trials of the Brave Warrior and Kaleidoscopic Color Chase. Imaginarium Theater's first season from June 1 required Pyro, Cryo and Geo characters, and a new Spiral Abyss season from June 16 gave a 60 percent Cryo and Pyro damage bonus on Floor 11 and added the Blessing of the Abyssal Moon, Frost-Surging Moon. The domains Thorny Crown of the Mountain Wind and Binding Field of Universal Nirvana were added.
