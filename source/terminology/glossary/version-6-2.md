## summary
### LITE
Version 6.2, officially "Luna III" (Song of the Welkin Moon: Interlude), was released on 2025-12-03 as "A Nocturne of the Far North". It added no new area. Its main content was Archon Quest Acts V and VI, the Story Quest Draco Rubedo Chapter, and the characters Durin (5-star Pyro Sword) and Jahoda (4-star Anemo Bow).
### STANDARD +
It follows Luna II (6.1) and precedes Luna IV (6.3). The Archon Quest centres on Moon-Prayer Night, the festival on which the fading Moon Goddess Columbina planned to return to the moon.
### LARGE +
Act V is "A Nocturne of the Far North" and Act VI is "Melting Moonlight in the Morning Mist". The acts cover the search for Columbina's true name, her return as Columbina Hyposelenia, the festival, and an ambush by the Fatui Harbinger Dottore that leaves Columbina trapped beyond reach.
### ULTRA +
The acts also reveal the history of the Three Moons as told by murals in Hyperborean ruins, and let the Traveler's friends from Mondstadt, the Hexenzirkel, the Curatorium and the Frostmoon Scions commit to opposing Dottore.

## release
@version: 6.2
### LITE
Released 2025-12-03, after Luna II (6.1) and before Luna IV (6.3).
### STANDARD +
Phase I banners: Rubedo, of White Stone Born (Durin and Jahoda) and Ballad in Goblets (Venti and Jahoda). Phase II: Cornucopia of Contention (Varesa) and Forgefire's Blessing (Xilonen).
### LARGE +
New weapons were Athame Artis (5-star Sword), The Daybreak Chronicles (5-star Bow) and Rainbow Serpent's Rain Bow (4-star Bow). New events were Soulblazers' Snowbound Journey, Bing-Bang Finchball, Heroic Clash: Saurian Brawl and Marvelous Merchandise. The Spiral Abyss updated on 2025-12-16 and a new Imaginarium Theater season began on 2026-01-01.
### ULTRA +
New systems included a Component Sharing Platform for Miliastra Wonderland (with Frosty Court and the event Invitation to Wonderland: In Search of Inspiration), the Witch's Homework event with the Hexerei: Secret Rite for eight Mondstadt characters (Sucrose, Mona, Durin, Klee, Albedo, Venti, Fischl and Razor), and Repertoire of Myriad Melodies additions: Specializations and a Co-op Ensemble. New enemies and drops included the Super-Heavy Landrover: Mechanized Fortress, Cyclic Military Kuuvahki Core and the Pale-Furred Wolf, and the new material Refined Heart.

## characters
@version: 6.2
### STANDARD
Durin ("The Undying Fire") is a 5-star Pyro Sword character; Jahoda ("Windthreading Shadow") is a 4-star Anemo Bow character.
### LARGE +
Durin was already a story figure: the wiki's Act IV summary identifies him as Mini Durin from Simulanka, appearing alongside Albedo. Jahoda is the Curatorium of Secrets employee introduced in Version 6.0. Their own files carry the detail. Durin's Story Quest is Draco Rubedo Chapter Act I, "The Magic Known as Storytelling".

## archon_quest
@version: 6.2
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Song of the Welkin Moon Act V, "A Nocturne of the Far North", and Act VI, "Melting Moonlight in the Morning Mist". Both need Adventure Rank 40.
### LARGE +
Act V (In the Shifting Light of the Moon, Ripples That Will Never Reach): about two months after arriving, the Traveler prepares for Moon-Prayer Night. Columbina plans to return to the moon, phantoms of the past appear across Nod-Krai, and Columbina begins to fade because she lacks a name or a role the world accepts. Alice and Nicole of the Hexenzirkel explain what must be done. Act VI (Where Does Her Name Rest?, The Night the Moon Returns Home, Return to the Moon, The Empty Courtyard): the group finds that she needs only a name she chooses, enjoys a final Moon-Prayer Night, and loses her when Dottore attacks.
### ULTRA +
In Act V, Durin's group studies visions of Nod-Krai during the Khaenri'ah Cataclysm, which Flins calls fixed history, and Nicole selects the Traveler, Lauma and Arlecchino as the three people able to enter the Hyperborean ruins after three days of kuuvahki infusion. In Act VI murals show the Three Moons created by Nibelung, used in his war with the Heavenly Principles and then shattered or cast away; one moon survived, which allowed Columbina to be born. At the platform Hyperboreans used to contact the moons, Nicole concludes Columbina never had a true name, and the Traveler calls her "Columbina". Back in Silvermoon Hall her full name appears: Columbina Hyposelenia. The townspeople greet her, "Welcome home, Moon Goddess." On the night she hangs a wish, receives a handmade greeting card, and plans hidden moonlight messages for her friends. At the ritual Dottore appears, uses a false moon and the power of all three moons to freeze time, and Columbina leaps into a moon gate of her own, vanishing into the same realm where Rerir fled. Arlecchino says that she will act as Peruere against Dottore if the Tsaritsa will not judge in time, and Varka rallies the allies. The optional quest The Empty Courtyard shows the Luonnotar and the kuuhenki waiting at Silvermoon Hall, and a brief meeting with Sandrone.

## entry: A Nocturne of the Far North
@keys: A Nocturne of the Far North, In the Shifting Light of the Moon, Ripples That Will Never Reach
@version: 6.2
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Act V of Song of the Welkin Moon and the title of Version 6.2. About two months after the Traveler's arrival, Nod-Krai prepares for Moon-Prayer Night. Columbina tells the Traveler she will return to the moon on that night; if she stays in Teyvat she will cease to exist. Phantoms and mirages of Nod-Krai's past appear. Columbina then disappears from Silvermoon Hall and fades, and Alice appears to tell the Traveler the border of Teyvat is destabilising and to name two tasks: find Columbina's true name and send her to the real moon.
### LARGE +
Durin's side of the investigation shows Cataclysm-era Nod-Krai; Durin struggles with his abyssal nature and concludes that his darkness can also be strength. Nicole, called Mage N, explains how Hyperboreans tried to reach the moons and says that the moon goddesses were erased from the world's natural order. Durin, Albedo and the Wanderer partly change the fate of a phantom and, for the first time, one remembers them.

## entry: Melting Moonlight in the Morning Mist
@keys: Melting Moonlight in the Morning Mist, Where Does Her Name Rest, The Night the Moon Returns Home, Return to the Moon, The Empty Courtyard
@version: 6.2
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Act VI of Song of the Welkin Moon. In the Hyperborean ruins the group learns the history of the Three Moons and that Columbina has no true name from the past; she is restored by being called Columbina. After a night of festival, Dottore ambushes her at the ritual, and she vanishes into a moon gate. The story ends with the allies pledging to find her.
### LARGE +
Dottore, the wiki says, had distorted the causal structure of the Hyperborean phantoms to extract forbidden lunar knowledge and craft a false moon. Columbina's last words to the Traveler are that her name, once a codename, became real because of her friends, and that moonlight will always connect them.
