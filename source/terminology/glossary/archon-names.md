## summary
### LITE
The Archons have up to three kinds of name: a divine name (Barbatos, Morax, Beelzebul, Buer, Haborym, Vassaga), a regnal title (Rex Lapis, Raiden Shogun, Lesser Lord Kusanali, Tsaritsa) and a personal name (Venti, Zhongli, Ei, Nahida, Mavuika, Anastasya). Barbatos has no regnal title.
### STANDARD +
Which name fits depends on who is speaking; a stranger who uses a divine name knows a secret. In the Prologue Venti tells a nun he is Barbatos and she does not believe him.
### ULTRA +
The names are not a flat list of aliases. Some titles pass from one holder to the next (Raiden Shogun, Tsaritsa, Lesser Lord and Greater Lord for Dendro), and some names belong to a predecessor, not the current holder. Use the right one for the right person and period.

## roster
### STANDARD
Anemo: Barbatos, called Venti, no regnal title. Geo: Morax, Rex Lapis, called Zhongli. Electro: Beelzebul, the Raiden Shogun, personal name Ei. Dendro: Buer, Lesser Lord Kusanali, personal name Nahida. Hydro: Focalors, Regina of All Waters, Kindreds, Peoples and Laws; no personal name given, and the throne was destroyed in 4.8. Pyro: Haborym, addressed as Kiongozi, personal name Mavuika. Cryo: Vassaga, Tsaritsa of All Snezhnaya, personal name Anastasya Feodorovna Snezhnaya.
### LARGE +
Furina, who played the Hydro Archon's public role, was never the Archon and should not be listed under Focalors' name. The Hydro Authority returned to the Hydro Dragon Sovereign when the throne was destroyed in Masquerade of the Guilty (4.8). The wiki dates Barbatos' rise to about 2,600 years ago, after Decarabian's fall, and Morax's ascension between about 3,700 and 2,600 years ago.

## name_layers
### LARGE
Divine name: the god's name as a god. Regnal title: the formal title of an Archon in their nation, such as Rex Lapis, Raiden Shogun, Lesser Lord Kusanali, Regina of All Waters, Kindreds, Peoples and Laws, or Tsaritsa of All Snezhnaya. Personal name: the name used among mortals. Ancient Name: Natlan uses Kiongozi for its Archon. Barbatos is the only Archon with no regnal title; Mondstadt is not ruled through him. Divine ideals are separate from names: Freedom for Anemo, Contracts for Geo, Eternity for Electro, Wisdom for Dendro, Justice for Hydro, War for Pyro and Love for Cryo.
### ULTRA +
Not every Archon has every layer in the sources read. Focalors, Egeria, Greater Lord Rukkhadevata and the Belyi Tsar have no divine name listed, and Focalors has no personal name. Pyro Archons are mortals, so the divine name Haborym belongs to the office rather than to one person. The wiki also lists earlier Pyro Archons by name only (Isaja, Shulevitz, Gloharry, Cochanina, Ndenge, Xumucul and Xbalanque), without divine names of their own; their stories belong elsewhere. Ei and Makoto are both connected to the Raiden Shogun title, as is the puppet Shogun that Ei later created.

## predecessors_and_successors
@version: 5.0
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
Four seats changed hands around the Cataclysm, about 500 years ago, and the names change with them. Makoto (divine name Baal) gave way to Ei (Beelzebul); Greater Lord Rukkhadevata to Lesser Lord Kusanali, Nahida (Buer); Egeria, Mistress of Many Waters, to Focalors; and the Belyi Tsar, Monomakh Snezhnyi, to the Tsaritsa, Anastasya (Vassaga). Mavuika placed her life in the Sacred Flame about 500 years ago so as to return in the present; the wiki lists her separately as Pyro Archon for under 100 years.
### ULTRA +
Representatives complicate this. The wiki lists Ei as Makoto's representative from over 2,000 years ago until 500 years ago, then the puppet Shogun as Ei's representative from 400 years ago onward, and Furina as Focalors' representative until Masquerade of the Guilty. A character who says "the Shogun" is therefore not necessarily naming the same person in every period.

## divine_name_notes
@version: 4.1
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
The wiki's trivia notes that the divine names Barbatos, Morax, Baal, Buer and Haborym correspond to demon names in the Ars Goetia, that Focalors and Vassaga are derived from Focalor and Vassago, and that Beelzebul is not itself an Ars Goetia name but is associated with Baal. This is a naming observation, not an in-world claim.
### ULTRA +
Within the world, the pairing of Zhongli with Morax and Venti with Barbatos is revealed in the first Archon Quests; the other pairings come from the later chapters. Do not let a character use a pairing before they could have learned it.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Use the layer the speaker would plausibly use. Most people say "the Anemo Archon", "the Geo Archon" or "the Raiden Shogun" rather than a divine name.
### LARGE +
Venti reveals to Sister Gotelinde that he is Barbatos in For a Tomorrow Without Tears and she is unamused and thinks he is unwell, which is a good model for how most Mondstadters would react to the claim. Zhongli's mortal name and the name Morax only meet in A New Star Approaches.
### ULTRA +
A scene may use the divine name, the title and the personal name to mark awe, formality or intimacy, but the archive does not assign any particular name to any particular relationship beyond what the sources state.

## entry: Barbatos
@keys: Barbatos
@version: 1.0
### STANDARD
Barbatos is the divine name of the Anemo Archon of Mondstadt, who lives among mortals as the bard Venti. He has no regnal title. The wiki dates his rise to about 2,600 years ago, after Decarabian fell. In the Prologue he calls himself the weakest of the Seven because he is so often absent.

## entry: Morax
@keys: Morax, Rex Lapis
@version: 1.0
### STANDARD
Morax is the divine name of the Geo Archon, whose regnal title is Rex Lapis. In A New Star Approaches he is revealed to be the mortal Zhongli, a consultant of the Wangsheng Funeral Parlor, and he hands his Gnosis to Signora. He is one of the two surviving original Archons.

## entry: Baal and Beelzebul
@keys: Baal, Beelzebul
@version: 1.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Baal and Beelzebul are the two divine names of the Electro pair. The wiki gives Baal to Makoto, the first Electro Archon of the pair, who died in the Cataclysm, and Beelzebul to Ei, her younger twin and the present Archon. In A New Star Approaches Zhongli calls Inazuma's living Archon Baal; the wiki notes Ei has continued to rule under that name.

## entry: Buer
@keys: Buer, Lesser Lord Kusanali
@version: 3.0
### STANDARD
Buer is the divine name of the present Dendro Archon, Nahida, whose regnal title is Lesser Lord Kusanali. Her predecessor, Greater Lord Rukkhadevata, appears only under her title.

## entry: Haborym and Kiongozi
@keys: Haborym, Kiongozi
@version: 5.0
### STANDARD
Haborym is the divine name the wiki gives to the Pyro Archons of Natlan. Kiongozi is the Ancient Name used to address the Pyro Archon; the wiki lists it for both Mavuika and Xbalanque. The Pyro Archons are mortals, so the name belongs to the seat and not to one person.

## entry: Vassaga and Anastasya
@keys: Vassaga, Anastasya, Tsaritsa of All Snezhnaya
@version: 1.0
### STANDARD
Vassaga is the divine name of the Cryo Archon of Snezhnaya, called the Tsaritsa of All Snezhnaya. The wiki gives her personal name as Anastasya Feodorovna Snezhnaya and names her predecessor as the Belyi Tsar, Monomakh Snezhnyi.

## entry: Focalors
@keys: Focalors, Regina of All Waters
@version: 4.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Focalors is the divine name of the Hydro Archon of Fontaine, titled Regina of All Waters, Kindreds, Peoples and Laws. She replaced Egeria about 500 years ago, and her throne was destroyed in 4.8. No personal name is given; Furina, who played the Hydro Archon's public role, was a stand-in.
