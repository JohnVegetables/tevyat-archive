## summary
### LITE
Version 4.6, "Two Worlds Aflame, the Crimson Night Fades", was released on 2024-04-24. It added Arlecchino (5-star Pyro Polearm) and two new areas in Fontaine: the Sea of Bygone Eras and the Nostoi Region. It has no Archon Quest.
### STANDARD +
The exploration content is the ancient civilisation Remuria, which preceded Egeria's rule. The Faded Castle, once home to the Harmosts and scholars of Remuria, leads down to the deeper ruins. Arlecchino's own Story Quest arrives with the version.

## new_content
### LITE
New weapon: Crimson Moon's Semblance (5-star Polearm). New artifact sets: Unfinished Reverie and Fragment of Harmonic Whimsy.
### STANDARD +
Banners ran as The Hearth's Ashen Shadow (Arlecchino), Conjuring Chiaroscuro (Lyney), From Ashes Reborn (Wanderer) and Immaculate Pulse (Baizhu). Arlecchino's Story Quest is Ignis Purgatorius Chapter Act I - When the Hearth-Flame Goes Out. The Lupus Aureus Chapter Act II - Oathkeeper, a Story Quest for Cyno, also released.
### LARGE +
New domains were Faded Theater and Scattered Ruins. New exploration features included the Mysterious Grimoire, Washer Octopus, and the Wonderchime Flowers and Allochromatic Anemones. The Focused Experience Mode was added, first for Arlecchino's quest, and the Fountain of Lucine cap rose to level 50, after which Hydro Sigils can be exchanged with Mequignon.
### ULTRA +
World quests included For Yesterday and Tomorrow, Latecoming Homecoming, Where His Life Lies and Daydreams Beyond Space and Time. Limited-time events included Windtrace: Seekers and Strategy, Specially-Shaped Saurian Search, Vibro-Crystal Applications and a Rockin' tour event with Arataki Itto.

## areas
### LITE
The Nostoi Region sits off the main Fontaine plate, so its waters cannot be dived into. The Sea of Bygone Eras is a separate map reached from the Faded Castle.
### STANDARD +
The Sea of Bygone Eras has its own map tab and exploration progress and unlocks after completing the Underwater Nocturne quest. Its in-game text calls it the undersea capital and lost homeland and says its long-forgotten symphony sounds once more. Petrichor and the Faded Castle are within the Nostoi Region, and the Faded Castle is first reached during The Shadow Over Petrichor.
### LARGE +
Remuria was founded by the god Remus, called the God King, with Capitolium as its capital and a golden bee as its emblem. It fell after Remus tried to avert its prophesied destruction by sharing power with four human Harmosts; the empire sank with its people and its god. The History of the Decline and Fall of Remuria records the story.

## story_content
### LITE
Arlecchino's quest follows an "aftershadow" in the House of the Hearth.
### STANDARD +
The official quest blurb describes a shadow that should not exist wandering the House of the Hearth's halls, bringing goodwill and hastening ill deeds. It requires the Archon Quest Act V as a prerequisite. Cyno's Act II is set in Sumeru City and concerns a case tied to a mysterious organization.

## placement
### LITE
Follows 4.5 and precedes 4.7.
### STANDARD +
Characters who have not explored Nostoi or the Sea of Bygone Eras may know Remuria only from rumours and books.
