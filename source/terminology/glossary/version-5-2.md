## summary
### LITE
Version 5.2, "Tapestry of Spirit and Flame", was released on 2024-11-20. It added three Natlan areas, the Interlude Archon Quest of Chapter V, All Fires Fuel the Flame, and two characters: Chasca, a five-star Anemo Bow user, and Ororon, a four-star Electro Bow user. It was also the first version to support Xbox Series X and S and Xbox Game Pass.
### STANDARD +
It follows Version 5.1 and precedes 5.3. The new areas are Tezcatepetonco Range, Quahuacan Cliff and Ochkanatlan. The Interlude is a one-quest story set after the invasion of 5.1, in which the Traveler helps a tribe recover from the war.
### LARGE +
The Interlude sits between Act IV and Act V of Chapter V. Story quests added chapters for two more tribes, the Flower-Feather Clan (Tlalocan) and the Masters of the Night-Wind (Mictlan), and a Natlan Fishing Association.
### ULTRA +
Two more Saurian companions, the Qucusaur and the Iktomisaur, were added to the Saurian Indwelling system alongside Tribe Reputation for those two tribes.

## release
@version: 5.2
### LITE
Released 2024-11-20, between 5.1 and 5.3.
### STANDARD +
Phase I banners were Piercing Shot's Crimson Wake (Chasca and Ororon) and Conjuring Chiaroscuro (Lyney and Ororon). Phase II banners were Decree of the Deeps (Neuvillette) and Gentry of Hermitage (Zhongli).
### LARGE +
New weapons were the five-star bow Astral Vulture's Crimson Plumage and the four-star Flower-Wreathed Feathers (Bow), Waveriding Whirl (Catalyst) and Calamity of Eshu (Sword). Events included Iktomi Spiritseeking Scrolls, Adventurer's Trials: Metamorphosis, Exercise Surging Storm, Heated Battle Mode: Automatic Artistry, Claw Convoy and Of Fading Flowers and Falling Feathers.
### ULTRA +
A new boss, Tenebrous Papilla, arrived with Wayob Manifestations that burn or freeze and a set of Qucusaurus and Iktomisaurus warriors. The Natlan Fishing Association added fishing points. Spiral Abyss refreshed on 2024-12-16.

## characters
@version: 5.2
### STANDARD
Chasca ("Skyborne Arbiter") is a five-star Anemo Bow character. Ororon ("Shadow of the Night-Wind") is a four-star Electro Bow character. Their own files carry the profiles.

## archon_quest
@version: 5.2
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Chapter V: Interlude, All Fires Fuel the Flame, has one quest, For the Same Land. It needs Adventure Rank 40.
### LARGE +
After the war, Iansan and the Traveler help the Flower-Feather Clan, whose people were hit hardest. Three of the tribe's warriors are struggling with what they saw. Two recover once the Traveler and Iansan find a way to reach them, and the third, Ocotlan, who mistakes his own tribe for Abyssal monsters, has to be given the Draught of Lucidity to treat Abyssal Corrosion Syndrome.
### ULTRA +
Capitano appears to explain that syndrome and the cure, which was developed by his late second-in-command Guthred. He says Ronova is responsible for his people's suffering, then shows the Traveler his true face. The act ends with Xilonen beginning the Traveler's Name, which will need the Pilgrim's Chronicle and the Traveler's feats recounted in front of it.

## new_areas
@version: 5.2
### STANDARD
The new areas are Tezcatepetonco Range, Quahuacan Cliff and Ochkanatlan. World quest chains include Lost Traveler in the Ashen Realm, Every Aspect of a Warrior and additions to In the Footsteps of the Chosen of Dragons.
### LARGE +
The Tribal Chronicles added two chapters: Tlalocan, Of Fading Flowers and Falling Feathers (Acts I to III), and Mictlan, Memories Flowing With Color (Acts I and II). Act III of the latter came in 5.3.

## entry: Abyssal Corrosion Syndrome
@keys: Abyssal Corrosion Syndrome, Draught of Lucidity
@version: 5.2
### STANDARD
An illness that follows fighting the Abyss and can leave a warrior in hallucinations. Capitano says some cases ease through talk or contact, while severe ones need the Draught of Lucidity, which leaves the patient with lasting migraines.
