## summary
### LITE
Version 2.4, "Fleeting Colors in Flight", was released on 2022-01-05. It opened Enkanomiya beneath Watatsumi Island, began the Interlude Chapter with Act I "The Crane Returns on the Wind", and added Shenhe and Yun Jin.
### STANDARD +
The Interlude Act I is set in Liyue, where Ningguang rebuilds the Jade Chamber and the Traveler meets Shenhe, a human raised by the adeptus Cloud Retainer. Enkanomiya, a hidden underground realm of Inazuma's Watatsumi people, is opened by world quests.
### ULTRA +
The patch is mostly a Liyue-and-Inazuma release: Zhongli, Ganyu and Xiao had banners again, and six existing characters received new outfits.

## contents
### STANDARD
Characters: Shenhe (5-star Cryo polearm) and Yun Jin (4-star Geo polearm). Weapon: Calamity Queller (5-star polearm). New area: Enkanomiya.
### LARGE +
Banners: "The Transcendent One Returns" (Shenhe and Yun Jin) and "Invitation to Mundane Life" (Xiao and Yun Jin) in phase 1; "Gentry of Hermitage" (Zhongli) and "Adrift in the Harbor" (Ganyu) from 2022-01-25. Hangout events Series V: Ningguang Act I "The Jade Chamber's Returning Guest" and Yun Jin Act I "A Song That Knows Grace". Enemies: Abyss Lector: Fathomless Flames, Specters, Bathysmal Vishaps, Coral Defenders.
### ULTRA +
Events were Fleeting Colors in Flight, Prosperous Partnerships, May Fortune Find You, Flowing Lights and Colors, A Study in Potions, Eight Locales Over Mountains and Seas and Windtrace, all limited-time. Outfits: Ningguang, Keqing, Amber, Jean, Mona and Rosaria. The Sacred Sakura's Favor level cap rose to 50.

## story
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Ningguang announces a contest: the first three people to bring Sunset Vermillionite, Wonder Cores and Adepti Sigils get an honest answer to any question. The Traveler teams up with Shenhe and Yun Jin, wins, and the Jade Chamber ascends. Beisht, Osial's wife, attacks, and Ningguang's defences, the Millelith and Beidou's fleet drive it off with Shenhe's help.
### LARGE +
Cloud Retainer reveals Shenhe is human, not an adeptus, and says the adepti sense a coming calamity and would take control of Liyue if Ningguang cannot handle it. Yun Jin's opera "The Divine Damsel of Devastation" turns out to be based on Shenhe's own life. At the banquet, Yun Jin performs it on the Jade Chamber.
### ULTRA +
Ningguang cannot find a lead on the Traveler's sibling, so she answers Paimon's question instead. Shenhe asks how to fit in in Liyue Harbor, and Ningguang suggests building a sense of belonging.

## placement
### STANDARD
Follows 2.3 and precedes 2.5. The Interlude Chapter continues with Act II, "Perilous Trail", in 2.7.

## entry: Version 2.4 Interlude Chapter Act I
@keys: Version 2.4, Update 2.4, Patch 2.4, The Crane Returns on the Wind
@keys2: Interlude Chapter, Jade Chamber, Shenhe, Yun Jin, Cloud Retainer, Beisht
@version: 2.4
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The Crane Returns on the Wind has four quests: "Jade Chamber Rising", "Seagaze Sunset", "Bygones Times Like Dust Passing" and "Where the Heart Finds Rest". Shenhe, new to the city, is almost cheated by two brokers, then eats herbs like Qingxin and Violetgrass at Bubu Pharmacy. The Traveler secures Sunset Vermillionite from Sea Gazer's abode (guided by Xingqiu and the Feiyun Commerce Guild), Starsplinter Iron and Subrosium for the Wonder Cores, and Shenhe crafts the sigils.
### LARGE +
Beidou introduces Yun Jin, whose opera is to be staged on the Jade Chamber. Mingjun, a villager, is surprised that the rumoured Shenhe is alive. The Traveler reads Yun Jin's father's diary and realises the opera's heroine is Shenhe, who says she prefers the opera's version of herself. After Beisht's defeat, Zhu Tao, drunk, bothers the group and is rebuked by Shenhe.

## entry: Version 2.4 Enkanomiya
@keys: Version 2.4, Update 2.4, Patch 2.4
@keys2: Enkanomiya, Byakuyakoku, Watatsumi, Dainichi Mikoshi, Evernight, Whitenight
@version: 2.4
### STANDARD
Enkanomiya is a subterranean realm under Watatsumi Island, entered by a deep pool east of Sangonomiya Shrine. Its entrance needs the Key of the Moon-Bathed Deep, made from pearls in five shrines, plus unlocking requirements (Adventure Rank 30, "Ritou Escape Plan" and "The Still Water's Flow"). A light mechanism, the Dainichi Mikoshi, switches the realm between Whitenight and Evernight. Wiki lore says it was once called Byakuyakoku; the god Orobashi saved its people by leading them up to the surface. The Watatsumi people descend from them.
### LARGE +
The realm's quests mention the Abyss Order seeking the book "Before Sun and Moon" for a figure called Enjou. The Watatsumi Goryou Matsuri uses a branch from Enkanomiya, and after the Three Realms Gateway Offering (a limited-time event in 2.5) Kokomi looks for another approach.
