## summary
### LITE
Version 4.2, "Masquerade of the Guilty", was released on 2023-11-08. It added Furina (5-star Hydro Sword) and Charlotte (4-star Cryo Catalyst) and the Archon Quest finale of Fontaine's prophecy, Chapter IV Act V.
### STANDARD +
Act V is a trial of the Hydro Archon: Furina is put in the dock and found guilty of not being the Archon. The act then reveals what the prophecy actually meant and ends with the sacrifice of the true Archon Focalors.

## new_content
### LITE
New areas: the Erinnyes Forest and the Morte Region. New weapons: Splendor of Tranquil Waters (5-star Sword) and Sword of Narzissenkreuz (4-star Sword).
### STANDARD +
Banners ran as Chanson of Many Waters (Furina and Charlotte), Immaculate Pulse (Baizhu and Charlotte), Twilight Arbiter (Cyno) and Azure Excursion (Kamisato Ayato). Furina's Story Quest, Animula Choragi Chapter Act I - "The Little Oceanid", arrived this version.
### LARGE +
Limited-time events included Thelxie's Fantastic Adventures, Lil' Fungi's Fun-Tastic Fiesta, Graph Adversarial Technology Experiment Log and Misty Dungeon: Realm of Water. New books included La Chanson d'Erinnyes, Tales of a Snow-Winged Goose and The Fall of the Faded Castle. A new Xenochromatic Ball Octopus was added.
### ULTRA +
Quality-of-life changes included a higher custom map-marker limit (200) with batch deletion, a Trounce Domain Quick Challenge from Adventure Rank 40, a Lower Difficulty option for quest domains, Fountain of Lucine level 40, and TCG deck-sharing codes.

## story
@spoiler: major
### LITE
Archon Quest Chapter IV, Act V: Masquerade of the Guilty.
### STANDARD +
After the Poisson flood, which dissolves people on the lower levels, including Navia's companions Melus and Silver, the Traveler helps Neuvillette force Furina onto the stage of the Opera Epiclese. The Traveler prosecutes her as a fraud, and when diluted Primordial Seawater affects her like any Fontainian, she is judged not to be the Hydro Archon. Yet the Oratrice still returns a death sentence for the Hydro Archon.
### LARGE +
A recovered stone slate shows Egeria turning Oceanids into humans using the Primordial Sea, which is why the Fontainians dissolve when exposed to it and why Celestia punishes the Hydro Archon and her people. Neuvillette notes the fourth slate foretells a coming creature, which then rises from a rift: the giant narwhal seen in Childe's dream. Childe, in his Foul Legacy form, helps push it back.
### ULTRA +
In a realm opened by Furina's tear, the Traveler and Neuvillette meet Focalors, the true Archon, who explains that she was herself a transformed Oceanid and that she split her divinity from her human body, Furina, to outwit the Heavenly Principles. She cursed Furina so that she could not die while Focalors' divinity existed, and Furina played her part for 500 years, and now executes herself to return the Authority to Neuvillette. Neuvillette absolves all Fontainians so the Primordial Sea no longer dissolves them. Skirk then appears, hurls the weakened narwhal and Childe into a rift, and talks of Surtalogi and others "pursuing perfection". Afterwards the people are unharmed by the flood, Furina steps away from being Archon, and the Hydro Gnosis is handed to Arlecchino for the Tsaritsa. Skirk calls the Gnosis the remains of a third Descender.

## characters_introduced
### STANDARD
Furina is Fontaine's celebrated public Archon and, as Act V shows, a human who has played that role for five centuries. Charlotte is a reporter for the Steambird who has appeared since 4.0 and became playable here. Focalors, the true Archon, appears only briefly.
### LARGE +
Skirk, Childe's master, appears for the first time in person at the end of Act V. Mona and the mage "N" appear in short scenes.

## placement
### LITE
Follows 4.1 and precedes 4.3.
### STANDARD +
Act V closes the Fontaine Archon Quest apart from the epilogue (Act VI) released in 4.7. Anything that treats Furina as a god is set before the end of Act V.
