## summary
### LITE
Version 2.0, "The Immovable God and the Eternal Euthymia", was released on 2021-07-21. It opened Inazuma, the Electro nation of the Raiden Shogun, and began Archon Quest Chapter II. It added Kamisato Ayaka, Yoimiya, Sayu and the Electro Traveler.
### STANDARD +
New areas: Narukami Island, Kannazuka and Yashiori Island. The Chapter II prologue came earlier in 1.6, so 2.0 delivered Act I, "The Immovable God and the Eternal Euthymia", and Act II, "Stillness, the Sublimation of Shadow".
### ULTRA +
The patch also launched Inazuma reputation, Thunder Sakura Boughs, Thunder Barriers, Balethunder and the Epitomized Path weapon-banner system. These are game features, not in-world facts.

## contents
### STANDARD
Characters: Kamisato Ayaka (5-star Cryo sword), Yoimiya (5-star Pyro bow), Sayu (4-star Anemo claymore). Weapons: Thundering Pulse and Mistsplitter Reforged, plus forgeable Hamayumi, Amenoma Kageuchi, Kitain Cross Spear, Katsuragikiri Nagamasa and Hakushin Ring.
### LARGE +
Banners: "The Heron's Court" (Ayaka) in phase 1; "Tapestry of Golden Flames" (Yoimiya and Sayu) from 2021-08-10. Artifact sets: Emblem of Severed Fate and Shimenawa's Reminiscence. Story quests: Grus Nivis Chapter Act I (Ayaka) and Carassius Auratus Chapter Act I (Yoimiya), both required for Act II.
### ULTRA +
Events were Thunder Sojourn, Lost Riches, Theater Mechanicus: Stage of Wonders, Phantom Flow and Ley Line Overflow, all limited-time. New foes included Kairagi, Nobushi, Mirror Maidens, the Pyro Hypostasis, Ruin Sentinels and Electro Samachurls.

## story
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Act I: the Traveler reaches Ritou under the Sakoku Decree. Thoma helps them past the port, then reveals he serves the Kamisato Clan; Kamisato Ayaka quietly opposes the Vision Hunt Decree. Act II: Thoma is taken as the hundredth Vision Hunt victim, the Raiden Shogun declares the Traveler "an exception" and the "enemy of eternity", and the Traveler joins the Watatsumi Army's resistance.
### LARGE +
Ayaka sends the Traveler to three people who lost their Visions, and then to free the craftsman Masakatsu with Yoimiya's help; Kujou Sara catches them but lets them go. After Thoma's rescue, the Traveler fights Sara in a ceremonial contest of honour, and Sangonomiya Kokomi arrives with mercenaries, among them Beidou and Kaedehara Kazuha, so Sara retreats.
### ULTRA +
The Shogun's reasons, the Fatui's role and the Plane of Euthymia are not revealed until Act III (2.1). Do not give characters Act III knowledge in a 2.0-period scene.

## placement
### STANDARD
Follows 1.6 and precedes 2.1. The Sakoku Decree ended in the 2.5 story quest "Transient Dreams", and the Vision Hunt Decree ended after Act III in 2.1.

## entry: Version 2.0 Archon Quest Chapter II
@keys: Version 2.0, Update 2.0, Patch 2.0
@keys2: Archon Quest, Chapter II, story, Vision Hunt, Ritou
@version: 2.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Act I, "The Immovable God and the Eternal Euthymia" (Adventure Rank 30), starts at Ritou, where foreign merchants of the International Trade Association, led by Kurisu, are squeezed by the Kanjou Commission, which demanded Crystal Marrow as tax. Thoma, backed by a Yashiro Commission card, forces the officers off. Commissioner Hiiragi Shinsuke stalls the Traveler's travel permit, and his daughter Hiiragi Chisato helps in secret; she says an arrogant-sounding woman, identified on the wiki as Signora, had arrived and conspired with her father.
### LARGE +
Ayaka, speaking from behind a screen, sends the Traveler to three Vision-bearers: Tejima at Konda Village, the samurai Kurosawa Kyounosuke in Inazuma City, and the swordmaster Domon, whose case shows that losing a Vision can bring buried regrets back, not a demon. Yae Miko notes the Traveler with interest. In Act II the Traveler is a wanted criminal and Thoma retreats to Komore Teahouse, safe within Yashiro Commission territory.
### ULTRA +
Act II needs the Story Quests "The Whispers of the Crane and the White Rabbit" and "Dreamlike Timelessness", and is the shortest Archon Quest act (two quests). The Chapter II prologue "Autumn Winds, Scarlet Leaves" is a 1.6 quest.

## entry: Version 2.0 New Characters
@keys: Version 2.0, Update 2.0, Patch 2.0
@keys2: Kamisato Ayaka, Yoimiya, Sayu, banner, Electro Traveler
@version: 2.0
### STANDARD
Version 2.0 introduced Kamisato Ayaka, Yoimiya and Sayu as playable characters, and let the Traveler use Electro. In the story Ayaka is the Kamisato Clan's eldest daughter, the Shirasagi Himegimi, and Yoimiya is the Naganohara fireworks maker who, in Act I, helps a Vision-bearer hand over a fake Vision to the confiscating samurai. Thoma appears here as a Ritou contact but was not playable until 2.2; Sayu's story role comes in Act III (2.1). Banners were limited-time and have been rerun.
