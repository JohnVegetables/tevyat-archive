## summary
### LITE
Version 3.3, "All Senses Clear, All Existence Void", was released on 2022-12-07. It added Act III of the Interlude Chapter of the Archon Quests, "Inversion of Genesis", the card game Genius Invokation TCG, and the playable characters Wanderer and Faruzan. It opened no new region.
### STANDARD +
It follows Version 3.2 and precedes Version 3.4. The Interlude act is a Sumeru story, while the event content of the version was Inazuman: the tagline speaks of kaidan and legends about Inazuma and its long-lived youkai.
### LARGE +
The Wanderer's debut is tied to the Interlude act: the Wanderer is the identity taken up by the former Scaramouche, whose fate the act deals with.
### ULTRA +
The wiki lists two text errors in English voice-over lines for the version's event content: "Akitsu Yuugen" should read "Akitsu Yuugei" and "Akitsu Hazura" should read "Akitsu Haneasobi".

## release
@version: 3.3
### LITE
Released 2022-12-07, between Version 3.2 and Version 3.4.
### STANDARD +
Phase I had From Ashes Reborn (Wanderer and Faruzan) and Oni's Royale (Arataki Itto and Faruzan). Phase II had Reign of Serenity (Raiden Shogun) and Azure Excursion (Kamisato Ayato).
### LARGE +
New weapons were the 5-star catalyst Tulaytullah's Remembrance and the 4-star sword Toukabou Shigure. New artifact sets were Desert Pavilion Chronicle and Flower of Paradise Lost. Turkish and Italian were added as game languages.

## characters
@version: 3.3
### LITE
The Wanderer ("Eons Adrift", a 5-star Anemo catalyst user) and Faruzan ("Enigmatic Machinist", a 4-star Anemo bow user) joined the game.
### STANDARD +
Faruzan's Hangout Event is covered in the archive as a quest entry. Both characters use Anemo.

## quests
@version: 3.3
### STANDARD
The Archon Quest content is Interlude Chapter Act III, "Inversion of Genesis". Commissions were "The Day the Sword Departs" and "Where Is the Unseen Razor?". World Quests included "Battlefield of Dice, Cats, and Cards", which introduced the card game through "Come Try Genius Invokation TCG!", and the Ascension Challenges I to IV.
### LARGE +
The Interlude act requires earlier Archon Quest progress to start and has four quests: "The Night-Bird Falls at the Curtain's Call", "A Dance of Destruction", "The Kabukimono's Finale" and "As Though Morning Dew".

## entry: Genius Invokation TCG
@keys: Genius Invokation TCG, Invokation TCG
@version: 3.3
### STANDARD
Genius Invokation TCG, a collectible card game played with elemental dice, arrived as new gameplay in Version 3.3. It is a gameplay system rather than an in-world story, though the characters on the cards are the game's roster.
### LARGE +
Later updates added modes and cards to it; for example Version 3.4 added a casual Heated Battle Mode and a match history.

## entry: Inversion of Genesis
@keys: Inversion of Genesis, Interlude Chapter Act III
@version: 3.3
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
In this act the Traveler and Paimon, visiting Sumeru, overhear two scholars researching a legend about the Tatarasuna incident, which includes a puppet-like kabukimono. They follow Scaramouche into the Sanctuary of Surasthana and learn that Nahida made a deal with him to investigate Irminsul on her behalf. He had been discarded by Dottore and the other Harbingers after his defeat. At his request the Traveler picks a new name for him, and he asks the Traveler to tell the Raiden Gokaden's descendants that he was responsible for their downfall.
### LARGE +
Nahida promises to keep searching Irminsul for records of the Traveler's sibling, and the Traveler tells him they went to Inazuma to see what he had changed. The act closes with a strange voice saying that the fate of Teyvat cannot be changed easily, which the wiki describes as leaving the Traveler wondering what comes next. The playable character in this version appears as the Wanderer.

## entry: Version 3.3 events
@keys: Akitsu Kimodameshi, Across the Wilderness, Windtrace, Misty Dungeon Realm of Sand
@version: 3.3
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
Limited events were Akitsu Kimodameshi, Across the Wilderness, Windtrace and Misty Dungeon: Realm of Sand, plus a Ley Line Overflow. The permanent domain City of Gold was added.

## entry: Spiral Abyss changes in 3.3
@keys: Terminal Moon, Breezecall Moon, Supreme Moon
@version: 3.3
### STANDARD
Gameplay note: Floor 11 gave all characters a 75 percent Anemo damage bonus, and the lunar phases were Terminal Moon, Breezecall Moon and Supreme Moon. This is game mechanics, not in-world lore.
