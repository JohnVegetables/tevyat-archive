## summary
### LITE
Version 1.2, "The Chalk Prince and the Dragon", was released on 2020-12-23, after Version 1.1. It opened Dragonspine, the frozen mountain in Mondstadt that holds the remains of the dragon Durin, and added Albedo and Ganyu. It had no Archon Quest.
### STANDARD +
Its flagship event, also called The Chalk Prince and the Dragon, follows Albedo's research on Dragonspine and a mysterious sword. The version introduced Albedo's Princeps Cretaceus Chapter and Ganyu's Sinae Unicornis Chapter, their first Story Quests.
### LARGE +
Anything that mentions Dragonspine's snow, the Frostbearing Tree, Albedo's camp or Ganyu's Story Quest belongs to this version or later. Before 1.2 the mountain was only visible in the distance and the game counted it as a subarea of Windwail Highland.
### ULTRA +
This entry is a patch-note summary, not character lore. Sub-entries cover the characters, Dragonspine and the event; the Archon Quest sequence is not part of this version.

## release
@version: 1.2
### LITE
Released 2020-12-23. Previous: 1.1. Next: 1.3.
### STANDARD +
New characters: Albedo (5-star Geo sword) and Ganyu (5-star Cryo bow). New weapons: Summit Shaper (5-star sword), Festering Desire, Snow-Tombed Starsilver, Dragonspine Spear and Frostbearer (4-star), plus the Blizzard Strayer and Heart of Depth artifact sets.
### LARGE +
Event wishes ran in two phases: "Secretum Secretorum" (Albedo) from 2020-12-23 and "Adrift in the Harbor" (Ganyu) from 2021-01-12. Other events in the window were Lost Riches, Hypostatic Symphony and Marvelous Merchandise. Smaller additions: the Kamera gadget (World Quest "Snapshots"), a Travel Log in the Archive for re-reading completed Archon and Story Quests, private friend chats and a "Stellar Reunion" returnee event.
### ULTRA +
The patch also corrected many English names and descriptions (Kaeya's passive "Heart of the Abyss" became "Glacial Heart", for example) and adjusted exploration progress for Galesong Hill, Windwail Highland, Bishui Plains and Lisha. These are game-text changes, not lore changes.

## new_content
@version: 1.2
### LITE
Albedo, "Kreideprinz", Chief Alchemist of the Knights of Favonius; Ganyu, "Plenilune Gaze", a secretary of the Liyue Qixing. New area: Dragonspine (Frostbearing Tree, Peak of Vindagnyr domain).
### STANDARD +
Story Quests: Princeps Cretaceus Chapter Act I, "Traveler Observation Report" (Albedo), and Sinae Unicornis Chapter Act I, "Sea of Clouds, Sea of People" (Ganyu).
### LARGE +
New enemies were Cryo-themed hilichurls, Cryo Samachurl, Fatui Cryo Cicin Mage, Cryo Cicin and the Ruin Grader, along with wildlife such as snowboars and snow foxes.

## entry: Version 1.2 Albedo and Ganyu quests
@keys: Princeps Cretaceus, Sinae Unicornis, Traveler Observation Report, Sea of Clouds Sea of People
@version: 1.2
### STANDARD
Albedo's Princeps Cretaceus Chapter Act I, "Traveler Observation Report", has the Traveler help him test whether someone from another world differs from the people of Teyvat. Sucrose, his assistant, brings the Traveler to him. Ganyu's Sinae Unicornis Chapter Act I, "Sea of Clouds, Sea of People", centres on her feeling caught between her adeptus and human sides.
### LARGE +
In Albedo's quest the experiments cover elements, the world, life, and a puzzle in a ruin on Dragonspine, with Rosaria and Barbara also appearing. Albedo concludes the Traveler responds like an ordinary person of Teyvat, and the otherworldly seed he was studying blooms. In a private moment he wonders whether he will one day lose control and whether the Traveler could stop him. The quest needs only Prologue Act II, "For a Tomorrow Without Tears".
### ULTRA +
In Ganyu's quest she believes her qilin blood makes her an outsider in Liyue after Ningguang's three secretaries cover her work. The Cloud Retainer and Xiao set her trials near Mt. Aocang, a pilgrim named Xin Cheng asks the group for a blessing, and in Liyue Harbor she helps Huixin, daughter of Uncle Tian (secretly the Qixing's Tianshu), with a tax investigation. It requires the Archon Quest "A New Star Approaches".

## entry: Version 1.2 Dragonspine
@keys: Dragonspine, Vindagnyr, Frostbearing Tree, Peak of Vindagnyr
@keys2: Version 1.2
@version: 1.2
### STANDARD
Dragonspine is the snowy mountain in Mondstadt's territory where the dragon Durin fell after fighting Dvalin. Its Subzero Climate drains health, and the Knights of Favonius run an adventurer camp at its foot. Few adventurers dare the deep snow, sheer cold, poisonous blood and monsters.
### LARGE +
Ruins record a civilisation called Sal Vindagnyr, a princess with the gift of foresight through visions and painting, and its fall when the Skyfrost Nail struck. The Frostbearing Tree is the sprout of an old Irminsul tree broken by the Nail. On the wiki's reading, Durin's blood in the ley lines revived it, and it grows when travellers offer it Crimson Agates, finally bearing the catalyst Frostbearer.
### ULTRA +
Item descriptions (Snow-Tombed Starsilver, Frostbearer, the Princess' Box and others) tell of an outlander entrusted with a Starsilver weapon who returned to find the city dead. Ruin Guards on the mountain carry serial-number records that may relate to Khaenri'ah. The designers said they left Dragonspine's story open to interpretation, so unanswered questions should be answered with "the ruins do not say". The wiki dates the Nail to about 6,000 years ago and Durin's death to about 500 years ago from other pages' text.

## entry: The Chalk Prince and the Dragon event
@keys: Chalk Prince and the Dragon, Festering Desire, Raiders Amidst Snow Mist, Uninvited Guests
@version: 1.2
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
This flagship event ran from 2020-12-23 to 2021-01-05 (shop to 2021-01-12) in five acts: Festering Desire, Archon's Rival, Raiders Amidst Snow Mist, Uninvited Guests and Afterword. The Traveler helps Albedo test a mysterious sword, earning the weapon Festering Desire.
### LARGE +
Sucrose notices that Albedo is hiding something unusual. Albedo suspects the sword draws on Durin's venom. In "Uninvited Guests" Fatui try to take the sword and the Traveler fights them off. In the afterword Albedo says the sword caused no side effects, corruption or poison. This is event content and not everyone in-world knows it.
