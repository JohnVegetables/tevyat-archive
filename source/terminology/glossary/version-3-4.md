## summary
### LITE
Version 3.4, "The Exquisite Night Chimes", was released on 2023-01-18. It added the Desert of Hadramaveth, a desert region of Sumeru, and the flagship Lantern Rite event that gave the version its name. It added the playable characters Alhaitham and Yaoyao and had no Archon Quest.
### STANDARD +
It follows Version 3.3 and precedes Version 3.5. The story focus of the update is therefore split: desert World Quests and a character Story Quest in Sumeru, and a Liyue-set festival event.
### LARGE +
The tagline speaks of fireworks in full bloom, dancing where the wind sweeps the sand, and ancient melodies dissipating in "the dreamscapes of memories", pairing the Lantern Rite with the desert's ruins.
### ULTRA +
Many of the desert quests concern Gurabad and the Tanit, and are covered in their own entries; this version entry only records where and when they appeared.

## release
@version: 3.4
### LITE
Released 2023-01-18, between Version 3.3 and Version 3.5.
### STANDARD +
Phase I had Caution in Confidence (Alhaitham and Yaoyao) and Invitation to Mundane Life (Xiao and Yaoyao). Phase II had Moment of Bloom (Hu Tao) and Discerner of Enigmas (Yelan).
### LARGE +
The only new weapon was the 5-star sword Light of Foliar Incision. New outfits were Springbloom Missive for Kamisato Ayaka and A Sobriquet Under Shade for Lisa.

## characters
@version: 3.4
### LITE
Alhaitham ("Admonishing Instruction", a 5-star Dendro sword user) and Yaoyao ("Burgeoning Grace", a 4-star Dendro polearm user) joined the game.
### STANDARD +
Alhaitham's Story Quest, Vultur Volans Chapter Act I, "The Illusions of the Mob", arrived with the version.

## quests
@version: 3.4
### STANDARD
There was no Archon Quest. New World Quest chains included Apocalypse Lost, "Her Foes Rage Like Great Waters...", The Dirge of Bilqis, and the single quests "Rejoice With Me, for What Was Lost Is Now Found", "Tadhla the Falcon", "The Falcon's Hunt" and "The Fallen Falcon".
### LARGE +
New hidden exploration objectives were Chessboard of Safhe Shatranj, Memories of Pairidaeza and The Murals of Gurabad. The wiki recommends completing "Golden Slumber" and accepting "The Dirge of Bilqis" before exploring the Desert of Hadramaveth.

## desert_of_hadramaveth
@version: 3.4
### STANDARD
The new area was the Desert of Hadramaveth in Sumeru. Its listed subareas include the Tanit Camps, Debris of Panjvahe, Dunes of Steel, Mt. Damavand, Passage of Ghouls, Qusayr Al-Inkhida', The Sands of Al-Azif, The Sands of Three Canals, Safhe Shatranj, Wadi Al-Majuj and Wounded Shin Valley.
### LARGE +
The wiki's trivia notes it had the most subareas of any area at the time of reading, a total of eleven. New domains were Fane of Panjvahe and City of the Deceased.

## entry: The Exquisite Night Chimes
@keys: The Exquisite Night Chimes, Lantern Rite 2023
@version: 3.4
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
The Exquisite Night Chimes was the Version 3.4 flagship event and ran 2023-01-19 to 2023-02-06. It required Adventure Rank 28 and the Archon Quest "A New Star Approaches". Players took part in four themed challenges, Radiant Sparks, Paper Theater, Vigilance at Sea and Behind the Scenes, and could invite a chosen 4-star Liyue character to the party.
### LARGE +
The other limited events of the version were Iridescent Splendor, the Arataki beetle event, Warrior's Spirit, Second Blooming and Overflowing Mastery.

## entry: Version 3.4 system changes
@keys: Heated Battle Mode, Tree of Dreams level cap
@version: 3.4
### STANDARD
Gameplay note: Genius Invokation TCG gained a casual Heated Battle Mode and match history, new Beidou and Klee cards were added, and the Tree of Dreams in Vanarana had its level cap raised to 40. Floor 11 of the Spiral Abyss gave a 75 percent Dendro damage bonus, and the lunar phases were Thorn-Twisted Moon, Verdant Moon and Luxuriant Moon.
