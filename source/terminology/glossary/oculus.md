## summary
### LITE
An Oculus (plural Oculi) is a small orb of concentrated elemental energy found in the open world. People with Visions, and the Traveler, collect them and offer them to a Statue of The Seven to restore the statue's lost power and receive its blessing. Each element has its own kind, tied to a nation: Anemoculus (Mondstadt), Geoculus (Liyue), Electroculus (Inazuma), Dendroculus (Sumeru), Hydroculus (Fontaine), Pyroculus (Natlan) and Cryoculus (Snezhnaya).
### STANDARD +
Nod-Krai has Lunoculi instead, offered to the Statues of the New Moon. The item text is the same in every region: a substance that has accumulated intense elemental energy, to be offered to the statue to help reinstate the power it has lost over the years.
### LARGE +
Oculi are finite: they exist only in the open world, are not found in chests, and do not return once taken.
### ULTRA +
The name joins the element with "oculi", reflecting the idea of eyes for the statues. In several languages the term means "pupil" of the god; the Chinese and Japanese names are literally forms of "Missing Pupil of the [element] God".

## in_universe
### LITE
In Mondstadt, Paimon explains in the tutorial quest The Blessings of The Seven (1.0) that Anemoculi are consolidated wind that only Vision holders and the Traveler can see. They are offered to the Statues of The Seven in exchange for blessings.
### STANDARD +
The name comes from the fact that they can be fitted into the statues' eyes. A fairy tale says the statues once had gem eyes that swallows carried off, but Paimon stresses that Anemoculi never had a physical form to begin with. She notes that the Traveler has no Vision yet still receives the blessings.
### LARGE +
The in-world reason an Oculus works for the Traveler, who has no Vision, is not explained; Paimon only remarks that the usual rules of the world do not apply to them.

## regions
### STANDARD
Counts below are gameplay figures from the wiki as of 7.1, each enough to raise that nation's statues to maximum level with one to spare. Anemoculus: 66 in Mondstadt (65 in the open world and one given by the quest Sparks Amongst the Pages), introduced 1.0. Geoculus: 131 in Liyue (1.0). Electroculus: 181 in Inazuma (2.0). Dendroculus: 271 in Sumeru (3.0). Hydroculus: 271 in Fontaine and the Sea of Bygone Eras (4.0). Pyroculus: 271 in Natlan and the Ancient Sacred Mountain (5.0).
### LARGE +
Lunoculus: 271 in Nod-Krai and on the Frost Moon (6.0), offered to the Statues of the New Moon, which the wiki says depict Kuutar, the Moon Goddess, and were made by the Frostmoon Scions. They serve the same functions as the Statues of The Seven but do not let the Traveler resonate with an element. Cryoculus: 140 in Snezhnaya (7.0).
### ULTRA +
The Cryoculus count differs from the rest: the wiki states that Snezhnaya's 140 Cryoculi raise its statues only to level 6, not maximum. This is treated here as a gameplay note, not lore.

## usage
### STANDARD
Use "Oculus" for the general class and the element-prefixed name for a specific kind. The Lunoculus is a Nod-Krai variant with its own statues, not an Archon's Oculus.
### LARGE +
Gameplay, clearly labelled: collecting one gives one Oculus plus Adventure EXP, the minimap shows an icon and sound when one is near, and an element-specific Oculus Resonance Stone (or, for Lunoculi, the Luna Treasure Compass) can mark their locations. Offering them levels the statue and yields rewards such as Primogems.

## limits
### STANDARD
Do not claim canon says what Oculi physically are, or that Archons personally collect or need them. Do not mix regions: a Geoculus is a Liyue item and an Anemoculus a Mondstadt one.

## entry: Anemoculus
@keys: Anemoculus, Anemoculi
@version: 1.0
### STANDARD
Mondstadt's Oculus: consolidated wind, 66 in total. Its item text describes intense Anemo energy to be offered to the Statue. The quest The Blessings of The Seven teaches the offering ritual.

## entry: Lunoculus
@keys: Lunoculus, Lunoculi
@version: 6.0
### STANDARD
Nod-Krai's counterpart (271 in all, including the Frost Moon). Its item text speaks of intense kuuvahki energy offered to the Statue of the New Moon. Statues of the New Moon are said to depict Kuutar.
