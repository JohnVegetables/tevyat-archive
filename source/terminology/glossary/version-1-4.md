## summary
### LITE
Version 1.4, "Invitation of Windblume", was released on 2021-03-17, after Version 1.3. It added Rosaria, a flagship event for Mondstadt's Windblume Festival, and Archon Quest Chapter I Act IV. It opened no new region.
### STANDARD +
The Archon Quest Act, "We Will Be Reunited", follows the Traveler and Dainsleif tracking the Abyss Order in Liyue and Mondstadt. The festival event, Invitation of Windblume, is a light-hearted story set in Mondstadt.
### LARGE +
The version also brought the first Hangout Events (Barbara, Bennett, Chongyun and Noelle, Act I) and two system changes: the option to lower the World Level once the player reaches World Level 5, and conversion of Adventure EXP above Adventure Rank 60 into Mora.
### ULTRA +
This entry is a patch-note summary, not character lore. The Act IV finale is a major spoiler for the Traveler's sibling and Dainsleif and is described in a version-tagged sub-entry.

## entry: Version 1.4 Invitation of Windblume event
@keys: Invitation of Windblume, Windblume Star, Festive Anecdotes, Windblume Ode
@version: 1.4
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
This flagship event celebrated the Windblume Festival from 2021-03-19 to 2021-04-05 (shop to 2021-04-12). It needed Adventure Rank 20 and the Prologue Act III, "Song of the Dragon and Freedom". Its weapon reward was the bow Windblume Ode. The story, "Festive Anecdotes", has four parts: Ode to Flower and Cloud, Missive of Cloud and Fog, Realm of Fog and Wind and Dream of Wind and Flowers.
### LARGE +
In the story the Traveler and Paimon find Mondstadt decorated for the festival, where the custom is not to give or take commissions. Venti teaches a class on writing love poems and Kaeya has a homework poem that ends up saying "I love you" in Hilichurlian. Sucrose's "Windblume" turns out to be a giant ancient Sweet Flower, Bennett and Razor are freed from a trap in the Thousand Winds Temple, and the Traveler is chosen as that year's "Windblume Star".
### ULTRA +
In the final part Venti has the Traveler help calm the wind currents in Stormterror's Lair before Dvalin passes, and he explains what the Windblume flowers and the festival mean. This is limited event content and is not commonly known in-world.

## entry: Version 1.4 We Will Be Reunited
@keys: We Will Be Reunited, Chapter I Act IV
@keys2: Dainsleif, Abyss Order
@version: 1.4
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
"We Will Be Reunited" is the fourth Act of Chapter I. It needs Adventure Rank 28 and the quests "Bough Keeper: Dainsleif" and "The Meaning of Lupical". Its four quests are Involuntary Sacrifice, A Herald Without Adherents, Dishonorable Trial and A Soul Set Apart.
### LARGE +
The Traveler and Dainsleif investigate ruins near Jueyun Karst, where a Defiled Statue of Barbatos hangs upside down. Dainsleif says the Abyss Order plans to build a mechanised god from the statue, the limbs of Osial, the sea monster the Traveler fought in Liyue and the eye of the first Field Tiller. They follow the trail to the Favonius Cathedral, Wolvendom (where Boreas is attacked by an Abyss Herald) and a Field Tiller in Stormterror's Lair, whose eye Dainsleif takes.
### ULTRA +
In the finale a figure interrupts Dainsleif's attack on the Herald. The Traveler discovers it is their sibling, who asks why the Traveler is with Dainsleif and calls him the Twilight Sword, a Khaenri'ah royal guard who failed to protect his nation and was cursed with immortality. Earlier Dainsleif tells the Traveler that Khaenri'ah never had gods and was destroyed by gods about five centuries ago, and that Ruin Guards were originally Field Tillers. These are statements by the characters, not narrator facts.
