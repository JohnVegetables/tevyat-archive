## summary
### LITE
Version 6.3, officially "Luna IV" (Song of the Welkin Moon: Postlude), was released on 2026-01-14 as "A Traveler on a Winter's Night". It added the Nod-Krai area The Lament of the Cliff-Shadowed Hollow, Archon Quest Acts VII and VIII, and the characters Columbina, Zibai and Illuga.
### STANDARD +
It follows Luna III (6.2) and precedes Luna V (6.4). The Archon Quest follows the fight against the Fatui Harbinger Dottore, who has trapped Columbina and then the Traveler, and ends with the Moon Goddess's return.
### LARGE +
Act VII is "A Traveler on a Winter's Night" and Act VIII, "True Moon", carries the story's climax. The version also adds the Lunar-Crystallize reaction, a Meeting Point system, Dottore as a new weekly boss and the Story Quest "A Lanternlit Ode to the Silver Moon".
### ULTRA +
Its Song of the Welkin Moon subtitles run Segue (6.0), Reprise (6.1), Interlude (6.2) and Postlude (6.3). In the story the Frost Moon is brought down into Teyvat and remains there after the battle.

## release
@version: 6.3
### LITE
Released 2026-01-14, after Luna III (6.2) and before Luna V (6.4).
### STANDARD +
Phase I banners: Somnias a Luna (Columbina) and Astral Actuation (Ineffa). Phase II, from 2026-02-03: Harmonious Abundance (Zibai and Illuga) and Decree of the Deeps (Neuvillette and Illuga).
### LARGE +
New weapons were Nocturne's Curtain Call (5-star Catalyst) and Lightbearing Moonshard (5-star Sword). New artifacts were Aubade of Morningstar and Moon and A Day Carved From Rising Winds. New outfits were As Heaven and Earth Are Made Anew (Traveler), Rainlit Bamboo Reverie (Yaoyao) and Melusent Gift (Neuvillette). Events included A Lanternlit Ode to the Silver Moon, Moonlit Patrol Exercise, Shutterflash Memory Hunt, May Fortune Find You: Springs Premonition, Ley Line Overflow and New Year's Gift.
### ULTRA +
New enemies include The Doctor, Lord of the Hidden Depths: Whisperer of Nightmares, Fishers of Hidden Depths, three Frostnight Scions (Surging Currents, Veiled Cloudchaser and Gilded Sunshard) and the Radiant Glacial Wolf. The Doctor drops Mask of the Virtuous Doctor, Madman's Restraint and Elixir of the Heretic. New domains are Moonchild's Treasures and False Moon Institute. A new Imaginarium Theater season began on 2026-02-01 and the second Spiral Abyss phase of the version updated on 2026-02-16. The Genius Invokation TCG gained Ororon and Skirk cards.

## region
@version: 6.3
### STANDARD
New area: Nod-Krai: The Lament of the Cliff-Shadowed Hollow, with Voidsea Outlook, Wavechaser Plain and Ashveil Peak.
### LARGE +
The version also adds a Meeting Point system with three locations, the Witch's Garden, Favonius Keep and Cliffwatch Camp. New wildlife includes the Cinderneck Stork, Crowned Eagle and Thornback Gecko, and new materials are Pine Amber and Winter Icelea.

## characters
@version: 6.3
### STANDARD
Columbina ("Welkin Moon's Homecoming") is a 5-star Hydro Catalyst, Zibai ("White Horse's Fleeting Spring") a 5-star Geo Sword, and Illuga ("Nightmare's Burning Heart") a 4-star Geo Polearm.
### LARGE +
Columbina is the Moon Goddess at the centre of the Archon Quest and is playable from this version. Illuga appears as a younger friend of Flins and, at the end of Act VIII, is encouraged to become the Lightkeepers' representative in the new League. Their own files carry the detail.

## archon_quest
@version: 6.3
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Song of the Welkin Moon Act VII, "A Traveler on a Winter's Night", and Act VIII, "True Moon". Both need Adventure Rank 40.
### LARGE +
Act VII (Moonless Night, Where Our Space and Time Intersect, Tracing the Footsteps of the Past): Dottore, the Fatui Harbinger, has made an artificial Moon Marrow and a false moon. He captures the Traveler and offers them an alliance. Meanwhile Columbina, trapped in the moon's reflection, travels back in time to leave clues. Nod-Krai is partly evacuated and the Wanderer, Albedo and Durin study the energy fields. Act VIII (The First Sliver of Moonlight, Descending Moon, Welkin Moon's Homecoming): the Traveler is rescued, Columbina meets the three moon goddesses, and the final battle destroys Dottore's false-moon form but costs Sandrone her life.
### ULTRA +
Act VII: the group is "cursed" by Dottore, so that leaving Nod-Krai would activate it. Dottore admits having knocked the Traveler out for over a month, mocks their relationship to Teyvat and offers to restore their rightful Descender status. Travelling back in time, Columbina creates the Luonnotar from a part of her soul to follow the Traveler and guide them to her. Act VIII: Rerir, now calm after rejecting the abyssal energy in the moon's reflection, asks Columbina to carry a message to Tholindis. The three Moon Sisters, Sonnet, Canon and Aria, help Columbina by lending her their authorities; she disperses into moonlight so that her spirit can return. Sandrone's world-formula needs the Wanderer's Irminsul core. At the battle Sandrone shields the Traveler from a killing blow and is killed; her real formula had been hidden in Pulonia. The Frost Moon is pulled into Teyvat, the Trilune Goddess opens her eyes and the combined fighters break Dottore's artificial Marrow. Afterward the Frost Moon stays in Teyvat, a new League of Nod-Krai's factions is formed, and the Tsaritsa revokes the Palestar Edict, Columbina's arrest warrant.

## entry: A Traveler on a Winter's Night
@keys: A Traveler on a Winter's Night, Moonless Night, Where Our Space and Time Intersect, Tracing the Footsteps of the Past
@version: 6.3
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Act VII of Song of the Welkin Moon and the title of Version 6.3. A few days after the Dottore incident, the Traveler and friends go to Dottore's lab, where Sandrone and Arlecchino had gone first. Dottore, armed with an artificial Moon Marrow that heals his wounds, captures the Traveler and sends the rest away. Dottore's false moon replaces the Frost Moon, energy fields appear across the islands, and everyone who entered his lab is cursed. Columbina, alive in the moon's reflection, leaves messages in the past.
### LARGE +
The group arranges an evacuation: Lauma takes the Southern Isles and Hiisi Island toward Natlan, and Arlecchino and the Fatui take everything north of the design bureau toward Snezhnaya. Albedo discovers that the energy fields are Light Realm energy and that Dottore has swapped human minds into animals.

## entry: True Moon
@keys: True Moon, The First Sliver of Moonlight, Descending Moon, Welkin Moon's Homecoming
@version: 6.3
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Act VIII of Song of the Welkin Moon. The Wanderer rescues the Traveler. Columbina meets the Moon Sisters in their prison and, with their help, returns. In the final battle against Dottore she and the Traveler overcome his Heretic of the False Moon form, but Sandrone dies saving the Traveler. Afterwards Columbina lives with the Frostmoon Scions, shows the Traveler their spaceship on the Frost Moon, and gives the Traveler a gift that loosens some of Teyvat's limits on the elements.
### LARGE +
In the spaceship, flight logs show it passed Teyvat once thousands of years earlier. Columbina says that it was placed on the Frost Moon by the Heavenly Principles. Varka writes to Jean that the expedition will return to Mondstadt in waves.
