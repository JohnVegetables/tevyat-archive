## summary
### LITE
Version 2.7, "Hidden Dreams in the Depths", was released on 2022-05-31. It added the playable Yelan (5-star Hydro Bow) and Kuki Shinobu (4-star Electro Sword) and Archon Quest Interlude Chapter Act II, "Perilous Trail", in which a party is trapped in the depths of The Chasm. It opened no new region.

### STANDARD +
Yelan's Story Quest (Umbrabilis Orchis Chapter Act I, "Calculated Gambit") and a Kuki Shinobu Hangout Event also released with the version.

## contents
### STANDARD
New characters: Yelan ("Valley Orchid", 5-star Hydro Bow) and Kuki Shinobu ("Mender of Tribulations", 4-star Electro Sword). New weapons: Aqua Simulacra (5-star Bow) and Fading Twilight (4-star Bow). Event wishes: Discerner of Enigmas (Yelan) and a rerun of Invitation to Mundane Life (Xiao) in phase 1; Oni's Royale (Arataki Itto and Kuki Shinobu) in phase 2.
### LARGE +
Quests: Interlude Chapter Act II "Perilous Trail"; Yelan's Story Quest "Calculated Gambit"; Kuki Shinobu's Hangout Event Act I "The Gang's Daily Deeds"; World Quests "On the Stage, Behind the Stage", "The Tales Behind the Fan" and "Garcia's Paean". New recipes were Dew-Dipped Shrimp and Omurice Waltz.
### ULTRA +
Events: Perilous Trail (event), A Muddy Bizarre Adventure, The Almighty Arataki Great and Glorious Drumalong Festival and Core of the Apparatus. Other additions were character level-up guides with talent and artifact main-attribute recommendations, and the Floral Zither gadget becoming purchasable from Granny Shan in Liyue Harbor.

## story
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
In "Perilous Trail" (Interlude Chapter Act II), the Traveler and Paimon return to the Chasm after earlier events there and meet Yanfei, Arataki Itto, Kuki Shinobu and Yelan. A crack in the ground drops the group into The Chasm's Bed, where they enter a strange domain that turns out to prey on the fears and weaknesses of whoever enters. Xiao appears, saying he is looking for someone.
### LARGE +
Xiao explains that he seeks his missing brother-in-arms Bosacius, and the domain proves to be a chaotic, illusion-filled space. The party works through it using Yanfei's theory that its own rules can be turned against it, and finds a Fantastic Compass that helps them get back to the surface. In "Calculated Gambit", Yelan is helping Uncle Tian, the Tianshu of the Liyue Qixing, assess three candidates for his successor.

## placement
### STANDARD
Follows 2.6 and precedes 2.8. "Perilous Trail" requires earlier quests including the Interlude Chapter's first act, and leads into the Interlude Chapter's next act, "Inversion of Genesis".
