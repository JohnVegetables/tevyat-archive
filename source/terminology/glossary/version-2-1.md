## summary
### LITE
Version 2.1, "Floating World Under the Moonlight", was released on 2021-09-01. It added Watatsumi Island and Seirai Island, the Archon Quest Chapter II Act III "Omnipresence Over Mortals", and the Raiden Shogun, Sangonomiya Kokomi and Kujou Sara. Aloy was PlayStation-only.
### STANDARD +
Act III ends the Shogun confrontation begun in 2.0: the Traveler exposes the Fatui's hand in the Vision Hunt war, duels Signora, and defeats the Raiden Shogun in the Plane of Euthymia, after which Ei agrees to abolish the Vision Hunt Decree.
### ULTRA +
The patch also added fishing, the Phase Gate and the Thunder Manifestation boss. Hyakunin Ikki and Moonlight Merriment events were limited-time.

## contents
### STANDARD
Characters: Raiden Shogun (5-star Electro polearm), Sangonomiya Kokomi (5-star Hydro catalyst), Kujou Sara (4-star Electro bow), Aloy (5-star Cryo bow, PlayStation only). Weapons: Engulfing Lightning, Everlasting Moonglow, Luxurious Sea-Lord, "The Catch", Predator (PlayStation).
### LARGE +
Banners: "Reign of Serenity" (Raiden Shogun and Kujou Sara) in phase 1 and "Drifting Luminescence" (Kokomi) from 2021-09-21. Bosses: La Signora (weekly, at Tenshukaku), the Hydro Hypostasis and the Thunder Manifestation. Story Quests: Imperatrix Umbrosa Chapter Act I "Reflections of Mortality" (Raiden Shogun) and Dracaena Somnolenta Chapter Act I (Kokomi). The Seirai Stormchasers world quest series covers Seirai Island.
### ULTRA +
Limited events: Moonlight Merriment, Passage of Clouds and Stars, Hyakunin Ikki, Lunar Realm and Spectral Secrets.

## story
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
After Kokomi names the Traveler captain of a Resistance unit, the Fatui turn out to be the Resistance's mysterious sponsor and are supplying Delusions, which drain the user's life. The Traveler duels Signora before the Shogun, the Shogun kills her, and Kaedehara Kazuha helps block the Shogun's strike. In the Plane of Euthymia, Yae Miko persuades Ei to abolish the Vision Hunt Decree.
### LARGE +
Teppei, a Resistance soldier, uses a Delusion and dies prematurely aged. Chapter II also shows Kujou Takayuki, head of the Kujou Clan, selling out the Shogunate to the Fatui. Yae Miko saved the Traveler from the Balladeer by giving him Ei's Electro Gnosis.
### ULTRA +
Ei's reasons for eternity are the deaths of companions and her sister Makoto, and watching Khaenri'ah's destruction by the Heavenly Principles. Miko also tells the Traveler that knowledge is managed as a resource in Sumeru, and suggests the Traveler lacks a Vision because they have not yet found an ambition strong enough.

## placement
### STANDARD
Follows 2.0 and precedes 2.2. The Interlude Chapter Act I is 2.4. Sakoku ended in 2.5; the Archon Quest continues in Chapter II Act IV (2.6), then Sumeru in 3.0.

## entry: Version 2.1 Archon Quest Chapter II Act III
@keys: Version 2.1, Update 2.1, Patch 2.1, Omnipresence Over Mortals
@keys2: Archon Quest, Chapter II, Act III, Resistance, Delusion, Signora, Plane of Euthymia
@version: 2.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Act III has nine quests. Kokomi makes the Traveler captain of "Swordfish II". An anonymous sponsor has been supplying the Resistance, and the Traveler recognises one of its weapons as a Delusion. Teppei dies from using it. The Traveler dismantles the factory, where a Fatui mole named Nathan is revealed and where the Balladeer appears. Yae Miko rescues the Traveler and takes them to the Grand Narukami Shrine. She explains that the Shogun on the throne is a puppet, while Ei dwells in the Plane of Euthymia.
### LARGE +
Miko trains the Traveler for the confrontation. To split the Tenryou Commission, the Traveler, with Yoimiya as a distraction and the Shuumatsuban ninja Sayu infiltrating, obtains Takayuki's falsified reports and correspondence with the Fatui. Kujou Sara reads them, confronts her adoptive father, and then races to Tenshukaku, where she is beaten by Signora. The Traveler duels Signora in the Shogun's presence, wins, and Signora is executed by the Shogun's Musou no Hitotachi. Kazuha resonates with his late friend's Masterless Vision to parry the Shogun's strike.
### ULTRA +
In the Plane, Ei confesses she had known about the Fatui. Miko enters the Plane through the omamori she gave the Traveler, and the light of the confiscated Visions outside brightens it. Ei concedes and promises to abolish the decree but says she needs time to reconsider how to rule. Miko discloses the Electro Gnosis trade, confirms the Balladeer was Ei's prototype puppet turned Sixth Harbinger (her claim), and speaks of Sumeru.

### STANDARD
Version 2.1 introduced the Raiden Shogun, Sangonomiya Kokomi and Kujou Sara, with Aloy as a PlayStation-only reward delivered by mail to Adventure Rank 20+ players. Kokomi is the Resistance's commander and Sara the Shogun's Army general; the Raiden Shogun is the Electro Archon. Banners were limited-time and have been rerun.

## entry: Version 2.1 Watatsumi and Seirai Islands
@keys: Version 2.1, Update 2.1, Patch 2.1
@keys2: Watatsumi Island, Seirai Island, Sangonomiya Shrine, Tatarigami, Seirai Stormchasers
@version: 2.1
### STANDARD
Watatsumi Island is the Sangonomiya Clan's island at the far end of Inazuma from Narukami Island. It has dense, differently coloured foliage and a long-standing poor relationship with the Shogun's government. The Watatsumi Army rose against the Vision Hunt Decree. In Act III, the Tatarigami phenomenon, tied to the death of the god Orobashi in the Archon War, is mentioned near Fort Mumei. Seirai Island is a storm-ravaged island with Balethunder; in the Seirai Stormchasers series the Traveler seals four Warding Stones, meets the talking cat Neko at Asase Shrine, defeats a Thunder Manifestation, and finds a Peculiar Pinion.
