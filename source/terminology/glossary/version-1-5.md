## summary
### LITE
Version 1.5, Beneath the Light of Jadeite, was released on 2021-04-28. It brought the Serenitea Pot homeland system and two characters, Eula and Yanfei. It had no Archon Quest and no new region.
### STANDARD +
The official blurb mixes images of moonlight on branches, a dragon's roar from beneath the roots, deals sealed in scarlet flame and a realm of serenity where a gentle melody always plays. It was also the first version to support the PlayStation 5.
### LARGE +
The first banner phase began on 2021-04-28 and the second, for Eula, on 2021-05-18. The version centred on homemaking and side content rather than a main-story chapter.
### ULTRA +
The Spiral Abyss was updated, with new Lunar Phases Blade-Dance Moon, Thorny Moon and Rebellious Moon, and a changed Ley Line Disorder on Floor 11. These are gameplay notes, not lore.

## characters_and_story
### STANDARD
New characters were Eula ("Dance of the Shimmering Wave", 5-star Cryo claymore) and Yanfei ("Wise Innocence", 4-star Pyro catalyst). The weapon Song of Broken Pines was added. Banners were Gentry of Hermitage (Zhongli and Yanfei) and, in phase 2, Born of Ocean Swell (Eula).
### LARGE +
Story Quests: Zhongli's Historia Antiqua Chapter, Act II, No Mere Stone; and Eula's Aphros Delos Chapter, Act I, The Spindrift Shall Never Return to the Sea. Hangout Events Series II: Noelle's Act II, Knightly Exam Prep, and Diona's Act I, The Cat and the Cocktail.
### ULTRA +
World Quests included A Teapot to Call Home Parts I and II and When the Trail Goes Cold. Event quests included A Timeless Classic, Mr. Melancholy and Mimi Tomo. The new artifact sets were Tenacity of the Millelith and Pale Flame.

## new_content
### LARGE
Serenitea Pot, with furnishings, a teapot spirit and a new inventory category, was added as a gadget and as a homeland. New bosses and monsters were Azhdaha, Cryo Hypostasis and Abyss Lector: Violet Lightning, and the new domains were Ridge Watch and Beneath the Dragon-Queller.
### ULTRA +
Events in the version were Energy Amplifier Initiation, Battlefront: Misty Dungeon, Windtrace, Mimi Tomo and Overflowing Mastery. Combat voice lines were added to character profiles, and open-world characters received voiced interactions.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
A 1.5 scene can use the new Serenitea Pot as a place to invite others, and Eula and Yanfei are new faces. There is no Archon Quest in this version to anchor knowledge, so rely on the earlier chapters.
