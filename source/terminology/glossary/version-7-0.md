## summary
### LITE
Version 7.0, "Everwinter Without Mercy", was released on 2026-08-12. It opened Snezhnaya, the Cryo nation, as a new playable region and began Archon Quest Chapter VII with Act I (Everwinter Without Mercy) and Act II (Wraith's Nocturne). It added Odette, a five-star Cryo Sword user, and Alyosha, a four-star Electro Polearm user, and the Traveler gained a Cryo form.
### STANDARD +
It follows the version the wiki numbers 6.7 (Luna VIII) and precedes 7.1. The story takes the Traveler and Paimon to the frozen north, the nation where the Tsaritsa has been gathering the Gnoses, where an energy shortage, a theater trapped in a snowstorm and the Ruler of Death all collide.
### LARGE +
Chapter VII is the first Archon Quest set in Snezhnaya. The wiki notes that it was first designated "Act VI" in the Teyvat chapter storyline preview and was numbered Chapter VII after the Song of the Welkin Moon was added, though the Song of the Welkin Moon was never officially called Chapter VI. The release date was first revealed by an old promotional code, Snezhnaya20260812, according to the wiki trivia.
### ULTRA +
The wiki's version blurb reads that death visits every cold, unguarded night, leaving souls weeping for everything they held dear. The two acts released here end with the Tsaritsa returning from slaying the monster behind the blizzards; the story continues in 7.1, which is the canon cutoff of this archive.

## release
@version: 7.0
### LITE
Released 2026-08-12. New region: Snezhnaya, with Volkodlak Tundra, Everfrozen Earth, Fellfrost Peak, Flamefeather Valley and White Birch Snowgrave.
### STANDARD +
Phase I banners were Swan's Shadow in Silken Ice (Odette and Alyosha) and The Hearth's Ashen Shadow (Arlecchino and Alyosha). Phase II banners were The Lone Light Knocks at Night (Flins) and Astral Actuation (Ineffa). The new five-star weapon was Whitelake Frostfeather (Sword). New artifact sets were Scarlet Proof and Heart of the Furnace. The new Traveler weapon was the Exaiphanes Blade, and the new outfit was Hunt in the Snow.
### LARGE +
New systems included the Stellar Swirl elemental reaction, the Meeting Point locations (Korolevskiy Theater for Odette, Huntsman's Cabin for Alyosha and Tidesong Cavern for Vodyanitsa), the third-person-shooter Shadow Realm with the Special Recruitment Project Cycle Training, the Eye of Graeae gadget and the Snezhnaya Adventure Journal. New events were Great Expeditionist Challenge, Mutual Aid in Bloom: Into the Frostlands, Trial of the Bastion and Overflowing Abundance.
### ULTRA +
New enemies included Volkodlak, chimeric monsters, Wildwood Leshies, Immortal Constructs and other fae. New materials included Rimecurrant, Glaciafruit, Frostfairy Flower and Lumifluorite. New Snezhnayan dishes included Borscht, Medovik, Stroganina and Glupov Rye Bread. Genius Invokation TCG gained Jahoda, Durin and Hydro Hypostasis character cards and a Snezhnaya card back. Imaginarium Theater opened on 2026-09-01 requiring Hydro, Electro and Dendro, and the Spiral Abyss was refreshed on 2026-08-16 with Stellar Swirl and Stellar-Conduct themed Ley Line Disorders.

## characters
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
Odette ("Swirling Snow") is a five-star Cryo Sword character; in the story she is a Fatui ballerina who is also the outlaw known as Lelek. Alyosha ("Swift-Striding Hound") is a four-star Electro Polearm character, a hunter from Sretomorozsk who keeps a hunting dog named Tugarin. Vodyanitsa appears as a singer of the Korolevskiy Troupe in 7.0 and becomes playable in 7.1. Their own files carry their profiles.
### LARGE +
The Traveler also gains a Cryo form in this version. Returning characters with banners were Arlecchino, Flins and Ineffa.

## archon_quest
@version: 7.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Chapter VII Act I, Everwinter Without Mercy, requires the Prologue and has four quests: Into the Wind, Into the Frigid North; Gunfire in the Silent Lands; Great Deeds on the Tundra; and Silence Alone is Disfavored. Act II, Wraith's Nocturne, has five parts: The White Curtain Falls, Lelek's Confession, Winter's Mysterious Silence, Wraith's Nocturne and Mistilteinn.
### LARGE +
In Act I, Paimon falls ill soon after crossing into Snezhnaya, and the Traveler is helped by the ancient Eye of Graeae, which recognises them as a Descender. The Traveler meets Pierro by chance, then Alyosha, who explains Project Stuzha, the state plan to cut energy use, and the Kresnik Kristall shortages it causes. They expose corruption in Sretomorozsk, are pulled briefly into the Shadow Realm, reach Snezhnograd, meet Sandrone, Tartaglia and Odette, and are invited by Alyosha to join the secret group Rokot.
### ULTRA +
Act II traps the Traveler, Alyosha, Odette, Vodyanitsa and other theatergoers in the Korolevskiy Theater during a blizzard, where two people die each night. The deaths are caused by the Curse of Death of Ronova, the Ruler of Death, who treats the theater as an afterlife. Along the way ghosts such as Shram and Ptashka are identified, and Odette is revealed to be Lelek. Afterward Pierro takes Paimon to the Armory Palace; he explains that Project Mistilteinn, an anti-Celestia weapon, was making her ill because she is a being of Celestia, and that he once presented Paimon as a gift to the Khaenri'ahn king Irmin. Pierro and Mitya agree to pause the project until Ronova is dealt with. The act ends with the Tsaritsa returning from her fight and being greeted at Zapolyarny Palace, accompanied by the Druzhna, its commander Vesna and the head maid Danica.

## entry: Version 7.0
@keys: Version 7.0, Everwinter Without Mercy
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
Version 7.0 (2026-08-12) is the release that opened Snezhnaya and started Archon Quest Chapter VII. New characters were Odette and Alyosha, the Traveler gained a Cryo form, and the Stellar Swirl reaction and the Shadow Realm shooter mode were introduced.
### LARGE +
The following version, 7.1, continues Chapter VII with Acts III and IV.

## entry: Chapter VII
@keys: Chapter VII, Archon Quest Chapter VII
@version: 7.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Chapter VII is the Archon Quest set in Snezhnaya. Acts I and II came in 7.0 and Acts III and IV in 7.1, which is the canon cutoff for this archive. Its acts are Everwinter Without Mercy, Wraith's Nocturne, White Night, Like a Dream Upon Waking, and A Rekviem for the Underworld.
### LARGE +
Act I requires only the Prologue. The chapter number follows the Song of the Welkin Moon, which was never officially called Chapter VI.

## entry: Wraith's Nocturne
@keys: Wraith's Nocturne, Curse of Death
@version: 7.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Wraith's Nocturne is Chapter VII Act II. A blizzard keeps theatergoers in the Korolevskiy Theater, and each night two of them die in a recurring dream. The cause turns out to be Ronova's Curse of Death, which strikes whenever Fatui research threatens a breakthrough against Celestia.
### LARGE +
Those who die are revived with no memory of the event. Odette finds that her Cor Ignis can help a person wake from the afterlife faster. The Traveler chooses to side with Pierro and the Tsaritsa's faction because Ronova wants to take Paimon.
