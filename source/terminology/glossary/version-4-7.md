## summary
### LITE
Version 4.7, "An Everlasting Dream Intertwined", was released on 2024-06-05. It added Clorinde (5-star Electro Sword), Sigewinne (5-star Hydro Bow) and Sethos (4-star Electro Bow), and the Imaginarium Theater. It finished Archon Quest Chapter IV with Act VI, Bedtime Story.
### STANDARD +
Act VI is an epilogue set in Sumeru, not Fontaine. The Traveler takes a commission to find a villager whom nobody remembers well, and meets Dainsleif and then Caribert. The Traveler's sibling appears in a memory.

## new_content
### LITE
New weapons: Absolution (5-star Sword), Silvershower Heartstrings (5-star Bow) and Cloudforged (4-star Bow). No new region was added.
### STANDARD +
Banners ran as Illuminating Lightning (Clorinde and Sethos), Caution in Confidence (Alhaitham and Sethos), Romaritime Meluserenity (Sigewinne) and Chanson of Many Waters (Furina). Clorinde's Story Quest is Rapperia Chapter Act I - Silent Night, and Sigewinne's is Nereides Chapter Act I - The Warmth of Lies.
### LARGE +
Imaginarium Theater, a permanent combat challenge in the Theater Lobby in Mondstadt, opens at Adventure Rank 35 after the world quest Unbegun, Unending Story. Characters have limited Vigor and the season picks three elements as the main cast. Events included Mutual Security Enhancing Simulation, Endless Forms Most Martial, Spino Doubleblaster and Record of Reflective Writing.

## story
@spoiler: major
### LITE
Archon Quest Chapter IV, Act VI: Bedtime Story. It follows three quests: Cold Case Commission, Memories That Should Not Exist and World-Order Narration.
### STANDARD +
A joint commission from the people of Vimara Village asks for a missing man whom none of them can name. Atossa remembers sitting with him at dusk beneath a tree, when the sun never seemed to move. Dainsleif is also investigating the Abyss Order and tells the Traveler that the mysterious voice from their last meeting belongs to his elder brother, Vedrfolnir, one of the Five Sinners of Khaenri'ah.
### LARGE +
Dainsleif suspects the missing man has the power to implant memories, and that the Abyss Order is hunting the eye of the first Field Tiller. The Traveler and Paimon are sent back to the village while Dainsleif faces the Traveler's sibling, who holds a golden sword and wants a duel first. The missing man is Caribert, who says his father told him he was the foundation of the Loom of Fate. He built the false memories himself to have a chance at the life he was denied.
### ULTRA +
Caribert explains that the Loom of Fate began as a device that could create and implant memories and, when fully unlocked, creates Ley Lines of its own and can change the world. He says the Loom has been completed. The Traveler meets the sibling inside his memory but cannot touch them. The sibling says they are racing to unlock the Loom before the Heavenly Principles awaken, since those did not stir even after the Hydro throne was destroyed. The Traveler forgets the conversation on waking and finds only a photo of the three of them. The villagers remember Caribert as someone who left with two others.

## characters_introduced
### STANDARD
Clorinde is Fontaine's strongest Champion Duelist and has been in the story since 4.0. Sigewinne is the Fortress of Meropide's head nurse. Sethos is new in this version. Caribert appears only in Bedtime Story.

## placement
### LITE
Follows 4.6 and precedes 4.8.
### STANDARD +
Bedtime Story takes place after Masquerade of the Guilty and is the first Chapter IV act set outside Fontaine. Details of the sibling's goals and the Loom of Fate should not be known to the Traveler's companions who were not present.
