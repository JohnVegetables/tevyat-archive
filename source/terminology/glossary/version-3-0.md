## summary
### LITE
Version 3.0, "The Morn a Thousand Roses Brings", was released on 2022-08-24. It is the update that opened Sumeru: the rainforest half of the new nation, the Dendro element, and the start of Archon Quest Chapter III. It added the playable characters Tighnari, Collei and Dori.
### STANDARD +
It follows Version 2.8 and precedes Version 3.1, which added Sumeru's desert. The Dendro Traveler and the new Dendro reactions arrived with it, so Dendro became the last of the seven elements to be playable.
### LARGE +
The official tagline invites the reader to see the luxuriant verdure of the mountains and smell the roses on the meadows, then to hear the birds at daybreak "unveil wisdom silenced for centuries". Sumeru's story is framed from the first act as one of knowledge, dreams and a god who cannot be found.
### ULTRA +
Everything introduced here is permanent except the limited events listed in the events section; recollections of those belong only to players who took part during their run.

## release
@version: 3.0
### LITE
Released 2022-08-24, between Version 2.8 and Version 3.1.
### STANDARD +
Banner Phase I (2022-08-24) was Viridescent Vigil, featuring Tighnari and Collei, alongside Gentry of Hermitage, a rerun with Zhongli and Collei. Phase II (2022-09-09) featured Adrift in the Harbor, with Ganyu and Dori, and Drifting Luminescence, with Sangonomiya Kokomi and Dori.
### LARGE +
New weapons were the 5-star bow Hunter's Path and the 4-star bow End of the Line. Forgeable additions were King's Squire, Sapwood Blade, Moonpiercer, Forest Regalia and Fruit of Fulfillment. New artifact sets were Deepwood Memories and Gilded Dreams.

## characters
@version: 3.0
### LITE
Three playable characters debuted: Tighnari ("Verdant Strider", a 5-star Dendro bow user), Collei ("Sprout of Rebirth", a 4-star Dendro bow user) and Dori ("Treasure of Dream Garden", a 4-star Electro claymore user). The Traveler also gained the Dendro element.
### STANDARD +
Tighnari and Collei are tied to the Avidya Forest rangers and to the Archon Quest's opening act. Collei could also be invited to the party through the Spiral Abyss challenge Curtain of Leaves, which became available in Version 3.1.

## entry: Sumeru rainforest areas
@keys: Avidya Forest, Lokapala Jungle, Ardravi Valley, Ashavan Realm, Vissudha Field, Lost Nursery
@version: 3.0
### STANDARD
The new region was Sumeru's rainforest. The wiki lists these areas as new: Avidya Forest, Lokapala Jungle, Ardravi Valley, Ashavan Realm, Vissudha Field, Vanarana and Lost Nursery. The desert part of Sumeru, the Great Red Sand, did not exist yet and arrived in Version 3.1.
### LARGE +
Region systems introduced in the version include Vanarana's Favor, the Dendrogranum, the Withering as a gameplay element, and Tri-Lakshana creatures. The rainforest brought the Eremite and fungus enemy families, Ruin Drakes, Dendro Specters and many new wildlife entries.

## entry: Dendro reactions in 3.0
@keys: Bloom, Hyperbloom, Burgeon, Aggravate, Spread, Catalyze
@version: 3.0
### STANDARD
Dendro arrived together with its reactions: Bloom (with its follow-ups Burgeon and Hyperbloom) and Catalyze (with Aggravate and Spread). A new Dendro Elemental Resonance, "Sprawling Greenery", raises Elemental Mastery and rewards triggering several of those reactions. This is a gameplay summary, not in-world lore.
### LARGE +
The first Spiral Abyss changes tied to Sumeru favoured these reactions: Floor 9 boosted Bloom, Burgeon and Hyperbloom damage, Floor 10 boosted Spread and Aggravate, and Floor 11 gave all characters a Dendro damage bonus. The two lunar phases that began after the update were Efflorescent Moon and Reviving Moon.

## quests
@version: 3.0
### STANDARD
Archon Quest Chapter III began with Act I, "Through Mists of Smoke and Forests Dark", and Act II, "The Morn a Thousand Roses Brings". Tighnari's Story Quest, Vulpes Zerda Chapter Act I, "The Unanswerable Problems", also arrived.
### LARGE +
The version added many World Quests, notably the Aranyaka quest chain (set in the Lost Nursery), the Vimana Agama chain, the Varuna Gatha chain, the Agnihotra Sutra chain and the An Unwavering Culinary Dream chain. "Gourmet Supremos: Within Our Duties" was released in 3.0 but could only be accessed from Version 3.1.

## entry: Through Mists of Smoke and Forests Dark
@keys: Through Mists of Smoke and Forests Dark, Chapter III Act I
@version: 3.0
### STANDARD
Chapter III Act I is the opening of the Sumeru story. The Traveler and Paimon, seeking the Dendro Archon, meet Tighnari and Collei in the rainforest and follow a stolen Akademiya object toward Sumeru City.
### LARGE +
Act II, "The Morn a Thousand Roses Brings", takes place during the Sabzeruz Festival and revolves around a repeating day and the girl named Nahida. See their own quest entries for the plot.

## entry: Dendro element release
@keys: Dendro Traveler, Sprawling Greenery, Dendrogranum
@version: 3.0
### STANDARD
Dendro was introduced in Version 3.0 as the seventh playable element. The Traveler could take it as a new element, and the new Dendro Elemental Resonance and Bloom and Catalyze reactions were added.

## entry: Version 3.0 events
@keys: Graven Innocence, Fayz Trials, Tablet Analytics, Lost Riches
@version: 3.0
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
New limited-time events were Graven Innocence, Fayz Trials, Tablet Analytics, Lost Riches and a Ley Line Overflow. They are gameplay events, known only to people who played them.
### LARGE +
Quality-of-life additions in the same update included raising the saved party setups to 10, raising the friend limit from 45 to 60, and showing artifact sources with links to the corresponding domains.
