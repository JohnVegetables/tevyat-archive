## summary
### LITE
Version 4.3, "Roses and Muskets", was released on 2023-12-20. It added Navia (5-star Geo Claymore) and Chevreuse (4-star Pyro Polearm). It has no new region and no Archon Quest.
### STANDARD +
Its centrepiece is the limited-time Fontinalia film-festival event. The version follows the Archon Quest finale of 4.2 and has no main-story chapter of its own.

## new_content
### LITE
New weapons: Verdict (5-star Claymore) and Ultimate Overlord's Mega Magic Sword (4-star Claymore, an event reward). New artifact sets: Nighttime Whispers in the Echoing Woods and Song of Days Past.
### STANDARD +
Banners ran as In the Name of the Rosula (Navia), The Heron's Court (Kamisato Ayaka), Reign of Serenity (Raiden Shogun and Chevreuse) and Tapestry of Golden Flames (Yoimiya and Chevreuse). Navia's Story Quest, Rosa Multiflora Chapter Act I - Braving the Tides Together, arrived this version.
### LARGE +
A new domain, Waterfall Wen, was added, along with the Swirling Isles Serenitea Pot layout, a new book called The Two Musketeers and the item "Swirling Scene in a Bottle". Quality-of-life additions included a Recommended Sets function and Artifact Auto-Lock.
### ULTRA +
Other limited-time events included Arataki Blazing Armor Beetle Battle Boot Camp, Lost Riches, Dance of Resolute Will, Marvelous Merchandise and Gazing Up at Eternity, Looking Down at the World.

## event
@availability: limited
### LITE
Roses and Muskets was a flagship event, running from 2023-12-21 to 2024-01-08, with its shop open until 2024-01-15.
### STANDARD +
The Traveler and Paimon join the Fontinalia Film Festival. The filmmaker Xavier loses an investor and the group, including Chiori, Ayaka, Ayato and Yoimiya, recruit Furina as director for a film of "The Two Musketeers" and consult Chevreuse on muskets. Its four story acts were followed by an afterword.
### LARGE +
The acts were titled The Crack of Muskets Breaking the Silence, Fiction and Reality Inside and Outside the Frame, The Lone Isle Glimpsed Through Fog, Where the Roses Bloom, and an afterword The Two Musketeers' Triumph. The four gameplay modes were the Thousand-Pace Interdiction Arcminute Sharpshooting Zone, Trick Shots Tricky Lights, Xtreme Drive and Into the Frame. The event reward weapon is Ultimate Overlord's Mega Magic Sword.
### ULTRA +
The recommended prerequisites were Archon Quest Chapter IV Act V, Furina's Animula Choragi Chapter Act I and the Raiden Shogun's Imperatrix Umbrosa Chapter Act II.

## placement
### LITE
Follows 4.2 and precedes 4.4.
### STANDARD +
The event is a limited-time story, so characters should know of it only if they took part or heard about it.
