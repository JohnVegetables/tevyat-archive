## summary
### LITE
Version 4.1, "To the Stars Shining in the Depths", was released on 2023-09-27. It added Wriothesley (5-star Cryo Catalyst) and Neuvillette (5-star Hydro Catalyst) and continued Fontaine's Archon Quest with Acts III and IV.
### STANDARD +
Both acts take place largely in the Fortress of Meropide, the underwater prison that Wriothesley administers. The search for the missing Childe leads to a failing sluice gate that holds back the Primordial Sea.
### LARGE +
Neuvillette's secret, that he is the Hydro Sovereign, is first shown at the end of Act IV, and the Traveler is asked to keep it quiet. The giant creature Childe meets in the Primordial Sea is first glimpsed in the final scene.

## new_content
### LITE
New areas: the Liffey Region and the Fontaine Research Institute of Kinetic Energy Engineering Region. New weapons: Tome of the Eternal Flow and Cashflow Supervision (both 5-star Catalysts).
### STANDARD +
Banners ran as Decree of the Deeps (Neuvillette), Moment of Bloom (Hu Tao), Tempestuous Destiny (Wriothesley) and Ballad in Goblets (Venti). Four-star weapons were The Dockhand's Assistant, Portable Power Saw, Prospector's Drill, Range Gauge and Ballad of the Boundless Blue.
### LARGE +
Neuvillette's Story Quest, Diluvies Chapter Act I - The Remains of the Past Day, and Wriothesley's, Cerberus Chapter Act I - Reborn in the Land of Grievances, arrived this version. The Adventure Encounters system and the weekly Sennight Gauntlet (replacing the Guest Challenge) were added, and the Fountain of Lucine level cap rose to 30.
### ULTRA +
Limited-time events included Waterborne Poetry, Dodoco's Bomb-Tastic Adventure, Radiant Harvest, The Peaks and Troughs of Life and The Forge Realm's Temper: Clever Strategems. World quest chains included the Fontaine Research Institute Chronicles.

## story
@spoiler: major
### LITE
Archon Quest Chapter IV, Act III (To the Stars Shining in the Depths) and Act IV (Cataclysm's Quickening).
### STANDARD +
Childe has vanished from the Fortress of Meropide, so Neuvillette arranges for the Traveler and Paimon to be sentenced on false charges to 45 days there and investigate quietly. Inside, they meet the administrator Wriothesley, the head nurse Sigewinne and the prison's strange "hidden rules", and they find that Lyney, Lynette and Freminet are also looking for Childe. Freminet nearly dies from Primordial Seawater in the flooded tunnel Childe used.
### LARGE +
Wriothesley shows the Traveler a sluice gate in the forbidden zone. It holds back the Primordial Sea and its gauge has been rising for a year. He explains the fortress began as a place where Egeria, the previous Hydro Archon, let repentant exiles guard her secret. When the gate fails, Wriothesley and Clorinde hold it briefly until Neuvillette arrives, pushes the water back and reseals it. Meanwhile Arlecchino, at a tea meeting with Furina that the Traveler attends, learns Furina does not hold the Hydro Gnosis.
### ULTRA +
Arlecchino tells the Traveler she once sought the Gnosis from Furina directly and found no Gnosis and no real threat. After the seal holds, Neuvillette confirms to the Traveler and Paimon that he is the Hydro Sovereign and explains that the First Usurper took part of the Seven Sovereigns' power to make the Archons' Authorities. He says he cannot stop the prophecy unless the Hydro Archon returns his Authority. The act ends with Childe drifting in the Primordial Sea and waking beneath a massive narwhal.

## characters_introduced
### STANDARD
Wriothesley is the administrator of the Fortress of Meropide, called the Duke. Neuvillette is the chief justice of Fontaine. Sigewinne is the prison's head nurse. Wriothesley first appears in this version; Neuvillette was already seen in 4.0.

## placement
### LITE
Follows 4.0 and precedes 4.2.
### STANDARD +
Act III picks up days after the Act II verdicts. The Fortress of Meropide, the sluice gate and Neuvillette's identity are 4.1 reveals and are not known to anyone who has only seen 4.0.
