## summary
### LITE
Version 1.6, Midsummer Island Adventure, was released on 2021-06-09. It introduced Kaedehara Kazuha, a limited-time island area, and the Archon Quest Chapter II Prologue, Autumn Winds, Scarlet Leaves.
### STANDARD +
The summer event ran on the Golden Apple Archipelago, a temporary area, and centred on Klee. The Chapter II Prologue turns the story towards Inazuma, the next nation.
### LARGE +
The version added the Wind-Blessed Harpastum gadget, new furnishings, new outfits for Jean and Barbara, and a Serenitea Pot feature called Companion Move-In.
### ULTRA +
Midsummer Island Adventure was available until 2021-06-28 and its shop closed a day later; the Golden Apple Archipelago later returned in Version 2.8 with a different look.

## chapter_ii_prologue
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Autumn Winds, Scarlet Leaves (Chapter II Prologue, Inazuma) has three quests: A Path Through the Storm, The Crux Clash and Follow the Wind. The Traveler wants to reach Inazuma to confront the Unknown God and learn about their sibling, and Atsuko sends them to Beidou, whose ship is the Alcor.
### LARGE +
Beidou holds the Crux Clash, a tournament for fighters without Visions, and Kazuha, a temporary crew member, supplies the prize: a Masterless Vision. If the Traveler wins, they can choose that or passage to Inazuma. When Fei the Flyer, a Treasure Hoarder, steals the Vision and is caught, he is disqualified and the Traveler is crowned champion.
### ULTRA +
Kazuha explains why the Vision matters: when the Raiden Shogun enacted the Vision Hunt Decree, a friend of his challenged her and died in the duel, and Kazuha took the friend's Vision and fled. He hopes someone can reawaken it. He warns the Traveler that Inazuma is dangerous and tells them the Alcor will be notified when it is ready, while he travels across Liyue.

## midsummer_island_adventure
@availability: limited
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
During the limited-time event Midsummer Island Adventure, Klee receives a letter from a "Dodo-King" who threatens to take Dodoco from her. The Traveler, Paimon, Jean and Barbara go with her, carried by Dvalin at Venti's summons, and Kaeya, Diluc, Albedo and Razor follow separately.
### ULTRA +
According to the archipelago's history, the islands were formerly called the Haar Islands and were made when Barbatos reshaped Mondstadt. In the story it is revealed that Alice, Klee's mother, arranged the trip so her daughter could have a memorable summer. The event needed Adventure Rank 21 and the Archon Quest Song of the Dragon and Freedom.

## characters_and_content
### STANDARD
Kaedehara Kazuha ("Scarlet Leaves Pursue Wild Waves") is a 5-star Anemo sword user. Banners were Sparkling Steps (Klee) in phase 1 and Leaves in the Wind (Kazuha, from 2021-06-29) in phase 2. New weapons were Freedom-Sworn, Mitternachts Waltz and Dodoco Tales.
### LARGE +
Maguu Kenki was added as a monster. Other events were Never-Ending Battle, Kaboomball Kombat, Legend of the Vagabond Sword and Echoing Tales. The wiki also lists limited-time World Quests such as They Who Hear the Sea and Who Wields the Wild Wind?.
### ULTRA +
New outfits were Sea Breeze Dandelion for Jean and Summertime Sparkle for Barbara. The Spiral Abyss had new Lunar Phases themed on Swirl: Whirlpool Moon, Breezecall Moon and Brumous Moon.
