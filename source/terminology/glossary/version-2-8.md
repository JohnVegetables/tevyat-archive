## summary
### LITE
Version 2.8, Summer Fantasia, was released on 2022-07-13. It introduced Shikanoin Heizou and brought back the limited-time Golden Apple Archipelago for the summer event Summertime Odyssey. It had no Archon Quest and no new region.
### STANDARD +
Summertime Odyssey ran from 2022-07-15 until the end of the version and offered Fischl as its event reward. The official blurb mentions a cursed blade of unknown origin, an unsolved case and "familiar but not too familiar" islands at sea.
### LARGE +
The archipelago returned with a different look from its first appearance in Version 1.6; the 2.8 map includes Minacious Isle.
### ULTRA +
New outfits were Ein Immernachtstraum for Fischl and Red Dead of Night for Diluc.

## characters_and_banners
### STANDARD
Shikanoin Heizou ("Analytical Harmony") is a 4-star Anemo catalyst user. Phase 1 banners were Leaves in the Wind (Kaedehara Kazuha and Heizou) and Sparkling Steps (Klee and Heizou); phase 2, from 2022-08-02, was Tapestry of Golden Flames (Yoimiya).
### LARGE +
New weapons were Kagotsurube Isshin (4-star sword) and two quest-exclusive 4-star swords, Prized Isshin Blade (Awakened) and Prized Isshin Blade (Shattered).
### ULTRA +
Story and hangout quests: Kazuha's Acer Palmatum Chapter Act I, A Strange and Friendless Road, and Heizou's Hangout Event Series VII Act I, Trap 'Em by Storm. New content also included the recipe The Only Truth and the namecard Shikanoin Heizou: Temari.

## summertime_odyssey
@availability: limited
### LARGE
The flagship event Summertime Odyssey required Adventure Rank 32, the Chapter II Prologue Autumn Winds, Scarlet Leaves, and Mona's Astrolabos Chapter Act I (or a Quick Start). It had two parts: Surfpiercer and a series of Phantom Realm challenges (Misty Hills, Foggy Vales; The Islands' Stirring Strings; Immernachtreich Keep; Temple of the Star Latitudes).
### ULTRA +
In the event, Fischl receives an important missive and sets out with everyone in town on another sojourn. Players collect Iridescent Flotsam to invite Fischl and to buy rewards. Limited-time World Quests such as The Treasure of the Four Skerries, Treasure Voyage and Drama Phantasmagoria: Tale of the Sword-Wielding Princess! ran during the version.

## other_content
### LARGE
Other events were Reminiscent Regimen, Resonating Visions, Hidden Strife and Evermotion Mechanical Painting. A Film Notes quest series was added as a World Quest, and the Adventurer Handbook gained Guide and Embattle pages.
### ULTRA +
Spiral Abyss floor 11 granted all party members a 75% Pyro DMG Bonus, and the Lunar Phases were Valiant Moon, Windswept Moon and Shivered Moon. These are gameplay notes, not lore.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
The archipelago and Summertime Odyssey are limited-time canon: only those who joined should mention them. Heizou is a newcomer here and has not yet had later chapters.
