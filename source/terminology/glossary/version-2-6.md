## summary
### LITE
Version 2.6, "Zephyr of the Violet Garden", was released on 2022-03-30. Its headline content was the playable Inazuman Kamisato Ayato, The Chasm as a Liyue subregion, and Archon Quest Chapter II Act IV, "Requiem of the Echoing Depths", set in the Chasm's depths.

### STANDARD +
The Archon Quest sends the Traveler and Dainsleif into the underground mines, where the Abyss Order is at work in an inverted city and the Black Serpent Knights still guard the people of Khaenri'ah.

## contents
### STANDARD
New character: Kamisato Ayato ("Pillar of Fortitude", 5-star Hydro Sword). New weapon: Haran Geppaku Futsu (5-star Sword). New artifact sets: Vermillion Hereafter and Echoes of an Offering. New subregion: The Chasm, with a new domain, The Lost Valley. Event wishes: Azure Excursion (Ayato) and Ballad in Goblets (Venti) in phase 1, The Heron's Court (Kamisato Ayaka) in phases 2 and 3.
### LARGE +
New enemies: Ruin Serpent, Black Serpent Knight: Windcutter and Floating Hydro Fungus; new wildlife included the Bluethunder Weasel and Lucklight Fly. The Lumenstone Adjuvant gadget and the Starshroom item were added, and Radiant Spincrystals appeared in Mondstadt, Liyue and Inazuma. World Quest series included The Chasm Delvers and Valor's Afterglow.
### ULTRA +
Events were Hues of the Violet Garden and Outside the Canvas, Inside the Lens (phase 1), Vibro-Crystal Research, Ley Line Overflow and Marvelous Merchandise (phase 2), and Spices From the West and Overflowing Mastery (phase 3). Other changes included Tutorials improvements, alternate-outfit voice lines for Jean, Amber, Rosaria and Mona, and a separate "Chasmlighter" achievement category for exploring the Chasm.

## story
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Archon Quest Chapter II Act IV, "Requiem of the Echoing Depths", has the Traveler and Paimon take a miner's request about odd hilichurl activity in the Chasm. Underground they find an Inverted City and meet Dainsleif, who explains that "Twilight Sword" was his title as captain of Khaenri'ah's Royal Guard five hundred years earlier. Hilichurls gather in the area to live out their last days, and the place seems to ease the Khaenri'ahn curse.
### LARGE +
Black Serpent Knights, once Dainsleif's subordinates and now mindless from the curse, guard those hilichurls. One knight, whom Dainsleif believes to be Halfdan, restrains the others and leads the party to a hilichurl camp. There the Traveler sees a vision, through an Inteyvat flower, of their sibling and an Abyss Herald planning to amplify a water pool's properties to lift the curse from the gathered hilichurls. Dainsleif judges this futile and harmful, and the Traveler chooses to help him stop it.
### ULTRA +
An Abyss Lector triggers an amplification device that causes agony to those afflicted by the curse. Halfdan sacrifices himself to halt it long enough for the Traveler to defeat the Lector and destroy the mechanism. His soul briefly speaks with Dainsleif before departing. Dainsleif then leaves to recover and to look into the Abyss Order's Loom of Fate project. The Story Quest Cypressus Custos Chapter Act I, "The Firmiana Leaf Falls", was also added in this version.

## placement
### STANDARD
Follows 2.5 and precedes 2.7. The Chasm's story continues in the 2.7 Interlude Chapter Act II, "Perilous Trail".
