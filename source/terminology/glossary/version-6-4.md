## summary
### LITE
Version Luna V (numbered 6.4 on the wiki, with the Archon Quest subtitle "Song of the Welkin Moon: Variation") was released on 2026-02-25, after Luna IV. It added Varka, Grand Master of the Knights of Favonius, his first Story Quest and a Mondstadt homecoming event. It opened no new area.
### STANDARD +
The Story Quest, Lupus Majoris Chapter Act I, follows Varka's last expedition in Nod-Krai against a corrupted fragment of the wolf god Andrius, while Mondstadt waits for the Knights to come home.
### LARGE +
The event, "Homeward, He Who Caught the Wind", is a Mondstadt welcome ceremony for the returning Knights and plays out in four modes. The Serenitea Pot also received a Nod-Krai realm layout.
### ULTRA +
This entry is a patch-note summary, not character lore. The Story Quest ends with major revelations about Varka and the Bloodstained Knight and is described in a version-tagged sub-entry.

## release
@version: 6.4
### LITE
Released 2026-02-25. Previous: Luna IV. Next: Luna VI.
### STANDARD +
New character: Varka ("Knight of Boreas", 5-star Anemo claymore). New weapon: Gest of the Mighty Wolf (5-star claymore). New weapon appearances: Unbreakable: Durandarte and Phantasmal Gift: Star Omen.
### LARGE +
Phase one wishes: "The Northerly Wind's Song of Triumph" (Varka), "The Lone Light Knocks at Night" (Flins) and "Ode to the Dawn Breeze" (Mondstadt Chronicled Wish). Phase two: "Void Star's Advent" (Skirk) and "La Chanson Cerise" (Escoffier). Events included Sightseeing With Friends, Operation Stronghold Defense and Overflowing Abundance.
### ULTRA +
Additions included the Shadowflit Archipelago Serenitea Pot realm layout, Nod-Krai card back and box for the Genius Invokation TCG, the Harmonic Keys gadget, the Radiant Moongecko monster and the new World Quests "Nameless Bloodstained Monument" and "Stories to Sate the Glass and Scroll". Imaginarium Theater seasons began on 2026-03-01 and 2026-04-01.

## entry: Version 6.4 Homeward event
@keys: Homeward He Who Caught the Wind, Windscape Splendor, Phantasmal Gift Star Omen
@keys2: Version Luna V
@version: 6.4
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
This flagship event ran from 2026-02-27 to 2026-03-16. It needed Adventure Rank 20 and Prologue Act III, "Song of the Dragon and Freedom", and players are advised to finish "True Moon" and Lupus Majoris Chapter Act I first. The Traveler and Paimon are invited to Mondstadt for a welcome ceremony from the Knights of Favonius.
### LARGE +
The event has four modes: Echoes of Memory (finding differences with the Spotter Kamera), A Chat Over Fine Drinks (running the Knights' drink stand), Lionfang's Radiant Onslaught (a stage-play battle) and Favonius Falcon's Recon Log (flying a falcon on scouting routes). Completing them earns Windscape Splendor, with the weapon appearance "Phantasmal Gift: Star Omen" as a reward. This is limited event content.

## entry: Version 6.4 Lupus Majoris Chapter
@keys: Lupus Majoris Chapter, To Those Who Embark on the Expedition, Triumphant Warrior, Fated Warrior, Lonely Warrior
@keys2: Varka, Andrius, Roland
@version: 6.4
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
"To Those Who Embark on the Expedition" has three quests: Triumphant Warrior, Fated Warrior and Lonely Warrior. It begins with Venti, who learns that Varka's expedition has ended and that Lohen, Vice Captain of the 5th Company, is delivering Varka's letters to friends of the Knights.
### LARGE +
The Traveler learns that Andrius's soul was split in two long ago: one part stayed in Nod-Krai, and the other, Boreas, settled in Mondstadt. The Abyss has replaced the vanished Rerir with a figure calling itself Roland, who corrupted Andrius's remains. Boreas gives the Traveler a blessed claymore to bring to Varka, and Varka sets out to recover scattered Wolf Spirit fragments with the Traveler, his knights and allies in Nod-Krai.
### ULTRA +
Roland swallows Varka after destroying his sword, and Varka enters a space inside the wolf, collecting the longings of Rostam, Ravenwood and the Wandering Knight. He reveals that his letters were meant to anchor him to the living, and that he had hidden Boreas's soul in himself, not in the sword. He frees Andrius to return to the ley lines, treats the Roland he meets as a false copy and leaves Rostam's letter at Roland's sword before waking at Windrise, where his friends are waiting. Earlier in the quest Jean tells the Traveler that if Varka failed to return she would become Grand Master and train his successor. These are story events, and the status of the real Roland is left open.
