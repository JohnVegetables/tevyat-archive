## summary
### LITE
Version 3.6, "A Parade of Providence", was released on 2023-04-12. It opened two new areas in Sumeru's Girdle of the Sands, Gavireh Lajavard and the Realm of Farakhkert, and ran the flagship Akademiya event of the same name. Baizhu and Kaveh were added as playable characters in the second half of the version. There was no Archon Quest.
### STANDARD +
It follows Version 3.5 and precedes Version 3.7. The tagline reflects on joy and sorrow as constant companions and on history as a long river that never stops flowing.
### LARGE +
Baizhu and Kaveh arrived on 2023-05-02 in Phase II, three weeks after the version itself began, so the Phase I banners were Nahida and Nilou reruns.
### ULTRA +
The wiki's etymology note says the name Farakhkert likely derives from a Pahlavi word; canon does not explain the name.

## release
@version: 3.6
### LITE
Released 2023-04-12, between Version 3.5 and Version 3.7.
### STANDARD +
Phase I had reruns of The Moongrass' Enlightenment (Nahida) and Twirling Lotus (Nilou). Phase II had Immaculate Pulse (Baizhu and Kaveh) and Adrift in the Harbor (Ganyu and Kaveh).
### LARGE +
The new weapon was the 5-star catalyst Jadefall's Splendor, and the new artifact sets were Nymph's Dream and Vourukasha's Glow.

## characters
@version: 3.6
### LITE
Baizhu ("Beyond Mortality", a 5-star Dendro catalyst user, owner of Bubu Pharmacy) and Kaveh ("Empyrean Reflection", a 4-star Dendro claymore user, a Sumeru architect) were the new playable characters.
### STANDARD +
Baizhu's Story Quest is the Lagenaria Chapter, Act I, "The Heart of Healing". Nahida's Sapientia Oromasdis Chapter Act II, "Homecoming", and Layla's Hangout Event Act I, "Ever Silent Stars", also arrived.

## quests
@version: 3.6
### STANDARD
There was no Archon Quest. World Quests included "An Artist Adrift", "Another Home There May Yet Be", "Heart of Amrita", "Lightcall Resonance", and the chains Khvarena of Good and Evil, Monumental Study and Pale Fire.
### LARGE +
The Khvarena of Good and Evil chain has parts with the titles "The Splendorous Sky That Day", "Asipattravana Itihasa", "Awakening's Real Sound" and two-part "The Hymn of Tir Yazad". A new hidden exploration objective was The Flowing Spring of Life.

## entry: Gavireh Lajavard and Realm of Farakhkert
@keys: Gavireh Lajavard, Realm of Farakhkert, Girdle of the Sands
@version: 3.6
### STANDARD
Two new areas, Gavireh Lajavard and the Realm of Farakhkert, were added to the Girdle of the Sands in Sumeru. The wiki lists shared exploration features including Fravashi Trees, Amrita Mayflies and Khvarena Inscription Fragments.
### LARGE +
The Realm of Farakhkert includes an underground Khaenri'ah ruin, where an interactable order signed by a Schwanenritter garrison commander was found. The new system The Gift of Amrita and domains Molten Iron Fortress, Somalata Inland Sea, Purification Spring and The Realm of Beginnings arrived with the areas.

## entry: A Parade of Providence
@keys: A Parade of Providence, Akademiya Extravaganza
@version: 3.6
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
A Parade of Providence was the flagship event, held during the Akademiya Extravaganza and running 2023-04-27 to 2023-05-15, with its three story acts unlocking across the first days. It required Adventure Rank 30 and the Archon Quest "A New Star Approaches". Completing the Darshans' games filled a Gala Excitement meter, and enough of it invited Faruzan to the party.
### LARGE +
It had six gameplay modes: Gathering of Stars, Project Connectivity, Antiquity Hunt, In Truth's Steps, Concocted Reaction and Mimetic Replication. The other limited events were Fulminating Sandstorm, Brewing Developments, Overflowing Mastery and The Recollector's Path.

## entry: Version 3.6 system changes
@keys: Dendro Sigil, Reverberating Moon, Erupting Moon, Flourishing Moon
@version: 3.6
### STANDARD
Gameplay note: the Tree of Dreams level cap rose to 50 and Dendro Sigils could be exchanged with Khalid. Genius Invokation TCG gained Arataki Itto, Kamisato Ayato and Tighnari cards and a duel replay function. Floor 11 of the Spiral Abyss gave a 75 percent Dendro damage bonus, and the lunar phases were Reverberating Moon, Erupting Moon and Flourishing Moon.
