## summary
### LITE
Version 4.8, "Summertide Scales and Tales", was released on 2024-07-17. It added Emilie (5-star Dendro Polearm) and the limited-time summer event in Simulanka. It has no Archon Quest and is the last version before Natlan in 5.0.
### STANDARD +
The version has no main-story chapter; its story content is the limited-time event and Emilie's Story Quest.

## new_content
### LITE
New weapon: Lumidouce Elegy (5-star Polearm). Emilie's Story Quest is Pomum de Ambra Chapter Act I - Floral Debt, Blood Due.
### STANDARD +
Banners ran as In the Name of the Rosula (Navia), Twirling Lotus (Nilou), Ambrosial Essence (Emilie) and Discerner of Enigmas (Yelan). The official blurb for Emilie's quest says the legendary flower Auguste, once popular in Fontaine, was lost in a fire and rumours of it returning in Sumeru draw people to Port Ormos.
### LARGE +
The Imaginarium Theater gained Envisaged Echoes, Adventure Encounters gained Longer-Term Encounter Points, and a Hide UI function and a Dialogue Review screen were added. Other limited-time events were Bing-Bang Finchball, Outside the Canvas, Inside the Lens: Dew-Kissed Chapter and Energy Amplifier: Lemma.

## event
@availability: limited
### LITE
Summertide Scales and Tales was a flagship event running from 2024-07-17 to 2024-08-28, set in the limited-time area Simulanka.
### STANDARD +
Simulanka is a world created by Alice, Barbeloth and Andersdotter of the Hexenzirkel, whom its people call the Goddesses of Creation, Prophecy and Fate. Alice brought the Traveler, Paimon, Nilou, Kirara, Navia and the Wanderer there to experience its story. Visions do not work in Simulanka.
### LARGE +
Its subareas include the Forest of Blessings, the Constellation Metropole and the Broken Sea. The story ran in Page I, Page II and Page III with an Appendix, plus limited-time world quests such as The Lotus Leaf and the Champion and The Forest and the Princess. The gameplay modes were Boreal Flurry, Flying Hatter's Trick, Metropole Trials and Figurine Fabrication. Kirara could be invited to the team from the event.
### ULTRA +
The wiki recommends having finished Interlude Chapter Act III and Chapter IV Act V first.

## placement
### LITE
Follows 4.7 and precedes 5.0.
### STANDARD +
Version 4.8 closes the 4.x series. Characters should know the event only if they took part.
