## summary
### LITE
Version 5.7, "A Space and Time for You", was released on 2025-06-18. It added no new area. Its main content was Chapter V Act VI of the Archon Quest, set in Natlan, and two characters: Skirk (five-star Cryo Sword) and Dahlia (four-star Hydro Sword).
### STANDARD +
It follows Version 5.6 and precedes 5.8. Act VI returns to the thread of the Traveler's search for their sibling, who is revealed to lead the Abyss Order's activities in Natlan.
### LARGE +
Skirk's Story Quest, Crystallina Chapter, began with Act I, Whispers Betwixt Stars and Night. A limited-time combat event, Stygian Onslaught: Incursion Repeller, also opened.
### ULTRA +
Phase II reran Mavuika and Emilie.

## release
@version: 5.7
### LITE
Released 2025-06-18, between 5.6 and 5.8.
### STANDARD +
Phase I banners were Void Star's Advent (Skirk and Dahlia) and The Transcendent One Returns (Shenhe and Dahlia). Phase II banners were Ancient Flame Ablaze (Mavuika) and Ambrosial Essence (Emilie).
### LARGE +
The new five-star weapon was Azurelight (Sword). Five existing weapons gained new appearances (Serpent Devourer, Ardent Storm, Shattered Moon, Hallowed Fetters and Starpiercer) with matching Sublimation versions. Artifact Reshape, using Dust of Enlightenment, was added.
### ULTRA +
Spiral Abyss refreshed on 2025-07-16. On mobile, players now choose between Full Resources and Basic Resources downloads at first launch, and the latter leaves out some optional Archon, Story and Hangout quests. Genius Invokation TCG gained cards for Chasca, Citlali and Mavuika.

## characters
@version: 5.7
### STANDARD
Skirk ("Void Star") is a five-star Cryo Sword character. Dahlia ("Ode and Oblation") is a four-star Hydro Sword character. Their own files carry the profiles.
### LARGE +
The namecards were Skirk: Lone Shadow and Dahlia: Sacred Duty, with Travel Notes: Speculation.

## archon_quest
@version: 5.7
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Chapter V Act VI, A Space and Time for You, has three quests: The Key That Unlocks Fate, "Savior" and A Space and Time Without You. It needs Adventure Rank 40.
### LARGE +
The Traveler finds the key to the Hibernation Chamber on the spaceship that brought the twins to Teyvat, which they know belongs to their sibling. The Abyss Order attacks every tribe at once. The Traveler befriends a woman called Direidyth, who is seeking a Savior; the Traveler can see the monsters that attack her only while holding her hand. Dainsleif arrives and joins the Traveler in assaulting the Order's base at Ochkanatlan.
### ULTRA +
At the base, an Iniquitous Baptist claims the Order's wishes have been fulfilled. Dainsleif recognises the Baptist as his former master Haden, and learns that Haden's talk of reviving Khaenri'ah was a bluff meant to extract his memories. The Traveler's sibling then appears to explain that the attack was a feint so he could speak with the Lord of the Night, and that he had woven Khaenri'ah's Ley Lines over Natlan to create an Atlas of the New World. He hopes to meet the Traveler again in the new world.

## entry: Direidyth
@keys: Direidyth
@version: 5.7
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
A woman from a faraway village, sent by her grandmother to find a Savior who could stop a prophesied doomsday. The Traveler can see the monsters stalking her only while holding her hand. She and the Traveler later learn they are from different points in time.

## entry: Iniquitous Baptist
@keys: Iniquitous Baptist
@version: 5.7
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
An Abyss Order leader met at the Order's stronghold in Ochkanatlan, after the Order attacked every Natlan tribe at once, which Iansan calls its first coordinated attack there. Dainsleif identifies him as Haden, his former master.
