## summary
### LITE
Version 5.8, "Sunspray Summer Resort", was released on 2025-07-30. It added the Easybreeze Holiday Resort, a summer-themed area, the prelude act of the Song of the Welkin Moon Archon Quest, The Journey Home, the Lunar-Charged elemental reaction and one new character, the five-star Electro Polearm user Ineffa.
### STANDARD +
It follows Version 5.7 and was the last version before 6.0, which moves the story to Nod-Krai. The Journey Home is set in Natlan, bridges Chapter V and the next chapter, and brings in visitors from Nod-Krai.
### LARGE +
Easybreeze Holiday Resort, also known as Tenochtzitoc, is a sparsely inhabited island off Natlan's westernmost edge, and is the first summer-themed map to become permanent. The Sunspray Summer Resort Story Quest, with four parts, is set there.
### ULTRA +
The wiki marks the Sunspray Summer Resort quest as a stub, so its plot is not recorded here.

## release
@version: 5.8
### LITE
Released 2025-07-30, between 5.7 and 6.0.
### STANDARD +
Phase I banners were Astral Actuation (Ineffa) and a rerun of Starry Night's Whispers (Citlali). Phase II banners were Sharktacular Surfari (Mualani) and Piercing Shot's Crimson Wake (Chasca).
### LARGE +
New weapons were Fractured Halo (five-star Polearm) and Flame-Forged Insight (four-star Claymore). Outfits were Adventures in Blazing Hue (Bennett) and Tranquil Banquet (Yelan). Events included Sunspray Summer Resort, Tracing Vanishing Trails, Mementos of Teyvat: Prelude of the Frozen Veil and An Easy-Breezy Natlan in an Easy-Breezy Summer!
### ULTRA +
Spiral Abyss refreshed on 2025-08-16. The system additions included the Asha Saurian companion, the Natlan: Plains of Flowing Flames music album and the Leaping Spirit Piano. Hidden exploration objectives at the resort included Ayar Heroes' Cup and Easybreeze Speed Champions Tournament.

Her namecard is Ineffa: Command.

## lunar_charged
@version: 5.8
### STANDARD
Lunar-Charged is a new elemental reaction added in 5.8 and is labelled as game mechanics: when Electro meets Hydro and certain other conditions are met, a thundercloud forms and repeatedly strikes nearby targets affected by both elements for Electro damage.
### LARGE +
The damage can critically hit, and it is based on every character who applied an element during the reaction. See the system entry for details.

## archon_quest
@version: 5.8
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The Journey Home is the prelude act of Song of the Welkin Moon and has three quests: Flamelets, Smoldering and Searing. It needs Adventure Rank 40, with a Quick Start from Adventure Rank 28 after A New Star Approaches, and is followed by A Dance of Snowy Tides and Hoarfrost Groves in 6.0.
### LARGE +
The Traveler, Paimon, Chasca and Ifa find an unconscious automaton washed ashore, who calls for the Traveler and Paimon; merchants from Nod-Krai name her Ineffa. She has lost her memories and carries a sealed cryptex. Merchants from Nod-Krai say they fled the Fatui's actions there and the tension between religious groups, and the Traveler's group helps them. The Fatui then manipulate Secret Source automatons to attack and seek a hidden facility.
### ULTRA +
The facility is the Temple of Cascading Colors. There an automaton that is the Eighth Lord of the Thirteen Sovereign Lords tells Ineffa that she split into two selves after the war, one that sought a new home and became Ineffa and one hostile to humanity, the Eleventh Lord. The hostile self lures Ineffa to the core and tries to reclaim it. Ineffa crushes that core, then builds a new one and gains an Electro Moon Wheel, and the Eleventh Lord is destroyed. The cryptex holds a letter from Varka telling the Traveler to prepare for Nod-Krai and to meet him at a tavern called The Flagship. Ineffa plans to stay in Natlan for now.

## entry: Easybreeze Holiday Resort
@keys: Easybreeze Holiday Resort, Tenochtzitoc
@version: 5.8
### STANDARD
A sparsely inhabited island off the westernmost edge of Natlan, which the wiki says is where the long war had begun and met its end. Its name Tenochtzitoc translates as a cavern of stone and prickly pears, though prickly pears no longer grow there. It is the setting of the Sunspray Summer Resort Story Quest.
