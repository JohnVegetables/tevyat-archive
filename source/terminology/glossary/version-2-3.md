## summary
### LITE
Version 2.3, Shadows Amidst Snowstorms, was released on 2021-11-24. It brought two new characters, Arataki Itto and Gorou. It had no Archon Quest and no new region.
### STANDARD +
Its flagship event, also called Shadows Amidst Snowstorms, was set on Dragonspine. The official blurb is mock-mysterious and ends by saying it has "absolutely nothing to do" with Itto, leader of the Arataki Gang.
### LARGE +
The event was a limited-time flagship event, and it gave away a 4-star sword, Cinnabar Spindle, as its reward.
### ULTRA +
Because the version has no Archon Quest, it does not move the main story; its events are canon but limited.

## characters
### STANDARD
Arataki Itto ("Hanamizaka Heroics", 5-star Geo claymore) and Gorou ("Canine Warrior", 4-star Geo bow) were released with the Oni's Royale banner in phase 2, starting 2021-12-14. Phase 1 reran Secretum Secretorum (Albedo) and Born of Ocean Swell (Eula).
### LARGE +
New equipment: Redhorn Stonethresher (5-star claymore), Cinnabar Spindle (4-star sword) and the artifact sets Husk of Opulent Dreams and Ocean-Hued Clam.
### ULTRA +
Story quests and hangouts: Itto's Taurus Iracundus Chapter Act I, Rise Up, Golden Soul; Gorou's Hangout Event Act I, The Canine General's Special Operations; and Beidou's Hangout Event Act I, When the Crux Shines Bright. The Golden Wolflord was added as a monster, with a new gadget, Omni-Ubiquity Net, and a new domain, Slumbering Court.

## shadows_amidst_snowstorms
@availability: limited
### LARGE
This flagship event ran from 2021-11-25 to 2021-12-13 on Dragonspine, and the event shop stayed open until 2021-12-20. Its three acts were The Snowy Past, The Shadows Deepen and A Secret Born From Ashes, unlocked on 2021-11-25, 2021-12-01 and 2021-12-04. It required Adventure Rank 20, For a Tomorrow Without Tears, Albedo's Princeps Cretaceus Chapter Act I and Joel's Lost in the Snow.
### ULTRA +
Gameplay was built around three modes: Born of the Snow, in which players build puffy snowmen at the camp; Dragonspine Special Training; and Rise of the Fell Flower. Snowman parts were the event's cosmetic reward, and they could be taken into the Serenitea Pot afterwards.

## other_content
### LARGE
Other events were Bantan Sango Case Files: The Warrior Dog, Misty Dungeon: Realm of Light, Energy Amplifier Fruition and a Marvelous Merchandise rerun. World Quests included Omni-Ubiquity Net and Snowy Silhouette: Hope and Reunion. Companion furnishings were added for Paimon, Itto and Gorou.
### ULTRA +
The Spiral Abyss floor 11 Ley Line Disorder was changed to increase Geo DMG and Physical DMG for all party members. These are gameplay notes, not lore.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Dragonspine winter events are canon but limited-time, so only characters who joined in should refer to them. Itto and Gorou are new faces in this version and should not be given Archon Quest knowledge they did not witness.
