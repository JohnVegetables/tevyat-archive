## summary
### LITE
Version 4.5, "Blades Weaving Betwixt Brocade", was released on 2024-03-13. It added Chiori (5-star Geo Sword), the owner of Chioriya Boutique in Fontaine. It has no new region and no Archon Quest.
### STANDARD +
The version is built around Chiori's own Story Quest and a Mondstadt-based alchemy event. Its official tagline pairs a booming potion trade in Mondstadt with Chiori's unorthodox work in fashion.

## new_content
### LITE
New weapons: Uraku Misugiri (5-star Sword) and Dialogues of the Desert Sages (4-star Polearm, an event reward). A new book, Perinheri, was added.
### STANDARD +
Banners ran as Of Silken Clouds Woven (Chiori), Oni's Royale (Arataki Itto), Ode to the Dawn Breeze (a Chronicled Wish for Mondstadt characters and weapons), Decree of the Deeps (Neuvillette) and Leaves in the Wind (Kaedehara Kazuha).
### LARGE +
Chiori's Story Quest is Cisoria Chapter Act I - When They Talk About Tonight, and the Lynette Hangout Event is Act I - Checks & Cats. A Training Guide function was added, offering build suggestions from recently active players.
### ULTRA +
Other limited-time events included Feline Fortress Furrdyssey, Rolling Crossfire and The Great Fayz Reaction Debate.

## story_content
### LITE
Chiori's Story Quest is about the owner of Chioriya Boutique, called "The Thundering Seamstress".
### STANDARD +
The quest is listed as requiring the Fontaine Archon Quest Act V first. The official blurb describes her as hard to read, a designer whose blades slice fabric as if only one choice existed.

## event
@availability: limited
### LITE
Alchemical Ascension was a flagship event running from 2024-03-14 to 2024-04-01.
### STANDARD +
Set in Mondstadt, it has Lisa invite the Traveler and Paimon to help the Knights of Favonius promote alchemy. Players cultivate ingredients, concoct potions and sell them to grow a shop. Its recommended prerequisites were Archon Quest Chapter IV Act V and Eula's Story Quest Aphros Delos Chapter Act I.
### LARGE +
Its three gameplay strands were Cultivating Ingredients, Concocting Potions and Selling Potions; the reward weapon was Dialogues of the Desert Sages.

## placement
### LITE
Follows 4.4 and precedes 4.6.
### STANDARD +
This version has no Archon Quest. Its events are limited-time, so characters should know of them only if they took part.
