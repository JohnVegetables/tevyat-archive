## summary
### LITE
Version 5.1, "The Rainbow Destined to Burn", was released on 2024-10-09. It added no new region. Its main content was Chapter V Acts III and IV of the Archon Quest and one new character, the five-star Geo Sword user Xilonen.
### STANDARD +
It follows Version 5.0 and precedes 5.2. The two acts turn the story from a Pilgrimage tournament into an open war: the Fatui Harbinger Capitano pursues a plan of his own and the Abyss launches a nationwide invasion.
### LARGE +
Act III is about an Ancient Name for the Traveler and a hunt for Ororon, who has joined the Fatui. Act IV is a six-quest act that ends with the Abyss invasion repelled and with Mavuika's price for her victory unresolved.
### ULTRA +
A separate event with the same title as Act IV ran during the version. It is distinct from the Archon Quest act.

## release
@version: 5.1
### LITE
Released 2024-10-09, between 5.0 and 5.2.
### STANDARD +
Phase I banners were Forgefire's Blessing (Xilonen) and Of Silken Clouds Woven (Chiori). Phase II banners were The Moongrass' Enlightenment (Nahida) and Moment of Bloom (Hu Tao).
### LARGE +
The new five-star weapon was Peak Patrol Song (Sword), with the four-star Sturdy Bone (Sword), Mountain-Bracing Bolt (Polearm) and Fruitful Hook (Claymore). Events included Chromatic Ode of Candies and Roses, Feast of Pursuit, Reminiscent Regimen: Thrill, Aphid Treasure Trace, Marvelous Merchandise and a Tribal Chronicles event. The Imaginarium Theater season opened on 2024-11-01.
### ULTRA +
New enemies were the Secret Source Automaton: Configuration Device and the Tenebrous Mimiflora. Spiral Abyss updated on 2024-10-16 and 2024-11-16, with a skip function that lets a player claim lower floors' rewards after clearing higher ones. Genius Invokation TCG added Chiori and Eremite Floral Ring-Dancer cards.

## archon_quest
@version: 5.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Act III, Beyond the Smoke and Mirrors, has the quests All Names are Noble, To Whence the Smoke Drifts and The Flickering Light Splits in Twain. Act IV, The Rainbow Destined to Burn, has Beneath the Secret Source, As One We Watch the Setting Sun, The Surging Darkness, Despair Engulfs the Heavens, No One Fights Alone and A Fuel Named "Fate". Both need Adventure Rank 40.
### LARGE +
Act III: Xilonen is set to forge the Traveler a Name, which no outsider has ever had. Citlali agrees to help if they prove Ororon, a member of her tribe, has not truly sided with the Fatui. Capitano meets the Traveler through Ororon and warns that Mavuika's plan may not be enough, and the Abyss then attacks the whole nation at once. Act IV: Capitano and Ororon use a device from the Secret Source to wake the Lord of the Night. Mavuika persuades Capitano to join an alliance, an Abyssal invasion follows and ends only when six heroes stand with their Archon.
### ULTRA +
Capitano explains that his plan, to rebuild the Ley Lines, would erase the Night Kingdom's memory of Natlan, and Mavuika refuses because it would be no different from using the Gnosis. Chuychu, Chasca's adoptive younger sister, is infected by Abyssal energy and dies, at which point Chasca's Ancient Name appears and she becomes the sixth hero. Mavuika declares a Night Warden War in which the Ode of Resurrection protects everyone. The act closes with her using the power of Ronova, the Ruler of Death, which means she is slated to die, and with the Traveler's Name still unfinished. Capitano also reveals himself to be a survivor of Khaenri'ah.

## the_invasion
@version: 5.1
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
The Abyss answers Ororon's awakening by dropping tumours across the nation, flooding Natlan with pylons and monsters. Several tribes fall back to the Stadium of the Sacred Flame, with the Flower-Feather Clan and the Scions of the Canopy hit hardest. The war ends when Mavuika and the six heroes destroy the tumours and the rift at the stadium.
### ULTRA +
Mavuika's attack briefly exposes a false sky, which no one at the time pays much attention to. After the victory the Natlanese hold a feast, mourn the dead and plan a funeral for the fallen.

## entry: Source Mechanism
@keys: Source Mechanism, Secret Source Mechanism
@version: 5.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
A device from the Secret Source that Capitano and Ororon located with the help of three scholars, hoping to use it to rebuild Natlan's Ley Lines. When it was activated it woke Yohualtecuhtin, the Lord of the Night, who explained it was the source of the nation's Ley Lines.
### LARGE +
The Lord of the Night says the dragons once used it to strengthen the Ley Lines but could not work with their intricate structure, and that the Night Kingdom was built from fragments of the old lines after the Descender fought the dragons. She offers to rebuild the lines at the cost of her own existence, an offer Mavuika refuses.
