## summary
### LITE
Version 6.0, officially "Luna I" (Song of the Welkin Moon: Segue), was released on 2025-09-10. It opened the new region Nod-Krai and added Archon Quest Acts I-II, with the new characters Lauma, Flins and Aino.
### STANDARD +
It follows Version 5.8 and precedes Luna II (6.1). The wiki notes it is the first version not officially referred to by its number, being the first update of the "Luna" cycle.
### LARGE +
The Archon Quest takes the Traveler, Paimon and the automaton Ineffa from Natlan to Nod-Krai, where moon-power called kuuvahki, the Frostmoon Scions, the Lightkeepers and a new Fatui presence meet. New systems include Moonsign, the Lunar-Bloom reaction, the Luna Treasure Compass, the Silvermoon Hall: Selenic Chronicles reputation line, Travelers' Tales and the Repertoire of Myriad Melodies' first album.
### ULTRA +
Spiral Abyss blessings already refer to Lunar-Bloom and Lunar-Charged damage. The version title and the Act I title are the same, "A Dance of Snowy Tides and Hoarfrost Groves"; Act II is "Elegy of Dust and Lamplight". Together the two acts end with the Moon Goddess Columbina saying she wants to return to the moon, and with Varka introducing himself to the villain Rerir.

## release
@version: 6.0
### LITE
Released 2025-09-10, after 5.8 and before Luna II (6.1).
### STANDARD +
Phase I banners: Moonsong of the Groves (Lauma), The Moongrass' Enlightenment (Nahida), Roving Chalice of Dewgrass (Chronicled Wish). Phase II, from 2025-09-30: The Lone Light Knocks at Night (Flins and Aino) and Discerner of Enigmas (Yelan and Aino).
### LARGE +
New weapons were Nightweaver's Looking Glass (5-star Catalyst) and Bloodsoaked Ruins (5-star Polearm) from the event wish; Serenity's Call, Master Key, Prospector's Shovel, Blackmarrow Lantern and Snare Hook as forgeable 4-star weapons; the event weapon Etherlight Spindlelute; and the quest weapon Moonweaver's Dawn. New artifacts were Silken Moon's Serenade and Night of the Sky's Unveiling.
### ULTRA +
Events included Clink Clank Clash, Lightgleaner's Journey, Dodoco's Blow-Up-Erative Bomb-Nanza, Overflowing Favor, Rainbow's End: Silvery Flux and Uncover Lunar Realms, Hone the Eventide Radiance. The Spiral Abyss updated on 2025-09-16 and 2025-10-16. A new Imaginarium Theater season, with a Lunar Mode and Lunar Arcana, began on 2025-10-01. Quality-of-life changes included more inventory space, a revised Condensed Resin recipe, a Preset Teams feature for Spiral Abyss and Stygian Onslaught, and Adventure EXP for Oculi and chests.

## region
@version: 6.0
### STANDARD
New region: Nod-Krai, with Lempo Isle, Hiisi Island and Paha Isle. In the story it is a northern archipelago rich in kuuvahki, the pure energy of the moon, where Fatui forces have recently arrived.
### LARGE +
Nasha Town, the port, has the Adventurers' Guild branch, the tavern The Flagship, the Curatorium of Secrets run by Nefer, and Aino's workshop. Hiisi Island holds the Frostmoon Enclave, and Silvermoon Hall, where Columbina stays, lies nearby. The Wild Hunt is fought in the graveyards to the north, and the showdown with Rerir takes place at Starsand Shoal.
### ULTRA +
New monsters include the Frostnight Scions, Radiant Beasts, Wilderness Ghouls, Fatui Oprichniki and a family of Landcruisers. New wildlife includes the Frostfin Whale, Rimehorn Deer and Moonglow Firefly; new materials include Moonfall Silver and Frostlamp Flower.

## characters
@version: 6.0
### STANDARD
Lauma ("Evermoon's Sacrament Song") is a 5-star Dendro Catalyst, Flins ("Shadowy Lights, Stranger Wights") a 5-star Electro Polearm, Aino ("Clinky-Clank Gadgets-A-Gogo") a 4-star Hydro Claymore.
### LARGE +
In the story Lauma is a Moonchanter of the Frostmoon Scions, Flins a Ratnik who patrols the northern graveyards against the Wild Hunt, and Aino Nod-Krai's young inventor, looked after by Ineffa. Story figures introduced include Nefer, boss of the Curatorium of Secrets, her employee Jahoda, the Moon Goddess Columbina and the villain Rerir.
### ULTRA +
The World Quest Lightkeepers' Oath needs a Lightkeeper such as Flins or Illuga as active character, and The Lost Child's Tale needs Flins. Columbina is not playable in this version; she appears through the Silvermoon Hall: Selenic Chronicles reputation quests.

## archon_quest
@version: 6.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Song of the Welkin Moon Act I, "A Dance of Snowy Tides and Hoarfrost Groves", and Act II, "Elegy of Dust and Lamplight".
### LARGE +
Act I (Where the Moon Rises, Reunion Beneath the Moonlight, In the Shadows of Night) needs Adventure Rank 40 and the Interlude Chapter act Paralogism; a Quick Start allows entry at Adventure Rank 28 after A New Star Approaches. The Traveler arrives in Nasha Town and recognises the moon-power as the same they felt after landing on Teyvat. Searching for their spaceship, they meet Flins, Lauma, Aino, Jahoda and Nefer, are warned off by the Harbinger Marionette, and meet a young woman called both Columbina and Kuutar. Lauma explains the Three Moons and the "Moon Marrow". After a sanctum is entered and the Marrow stolen, Lauma tells the Frostmoon Scions the sky is false. Act II (five quests, from Fulminations and Undercurrents to Rest Beyond Reach) reveals the Wild Hunt as Rerir's work.
### ULTRA +
In Act I the Traveler and the sibling enter a hidden sanctum under the statue of Kuutar; the Marrow is later taken by Marionette to Starsand Shoal and won back after a Wild Hunt attack, and the Traveler stores it in their own body. Lauma cuts her hand to mix her silver blood into medicine for a sick child and asks the Traveler to keep it secret. In Act II, Rerir passes as the Ratnik Sousi, who is found dead, and Flins is accused. Rerir tricks the town into charging Aino's Boom and Blast Kuuvahki Cannon to regain part of a body. Flins's lantern holds a fragment of Rerir's heart. When Sandrone's robot Pulonia fails to stop him, Columbina teleports the group to Silvermoon Hall, then realises her strength is not returning. Columbina says she wants to return to the moon, where she would find her answers and "home". Rerir is one of the Five Sinners of Khaenri'ah, called the "Rächer of Solnari", who fell into the moon's reflection.

## entry: A Dance of Snowy Tides and Hoarfrost Groves
@keys: A Dance of Snowy Tides and Hoarfrost Groves, Where the Moon Rises, Reunion Beneath the Moonlight, In the Shadows of Night
@version: 6.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Act I of Song of the Welkin Moon and the title of Version 6.0. The Traveler, Paimon and Ineffa leave Natlan by boat for Nasha Town in Nod-Krai. The Traveler recognises the moon-power as the same they felt after landing on Teyvat and searches for their spaceship. They meet the Ratnik Flins, the Moonchanter Lauma, the inventor Aino, the Curatorium's Nefer and her employee Jahoda, are warned by the Harbinger Marionette, and meet Columbina. The act ends with the Moon Marrow stored inside the Traveler and Lauma telling her people that the sky is false.
### LARGE +
The sibling appears twice: once leading the Traveler into the sanctum, and once as an illusion in the fog produced by the Wild Hunt. The Traveler sees through the illusion because it contradicts what their sibling had said about finding them in hypersleep.

## entry: Elegy of Dust and Lamplight
@keys: Elegy of Dust and Lamplight, Fulminations and Undercurrents, The Dark Side of Memory, The Ashen-White Order Burns, Rest Beyond Reach
@version: 6.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Act II of Song of the Welkin Moon. The Wild Hunt grows bolder, and Rerir, who is behind it, manipulates Aino's cannon to restore part of his body. The Traveler and Flins, who is accused of killing Sousi, stop the shot only in part; Columbina saves the group from Rerir.
### LARGE +
Columbina uses the Moon Marrow to show the Traveler a memory of the crash in which the sibling stands outside the ship, although the Traveler knows they never left it. She teaches the Traveler to hide the Marrow they carry, and says two moons cannot rise at once and that a Frost Moon Marrow cannot exist unless that moon is destroyed.

## entry: Moon Marrow
@keys: Moon Marrow, Moon Marrows, Eternal Marrow, Iridescent Marrow
@version: 6.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The Frostmoon Scions' relic in Version 6.0 is the Moon Marrow, used in a ritual held when the moon is closest to Teyvat. Columbina says this one is a piece of the remains of a moon goddess, Aria. It was stolen from the sanctum, held by Marionette, and recovered by the Traveler.
### LARGE +
Kuuvahki and abyssal energy are polar opposites, and whichever side wins absorbs the other's energy, which is how Rerir's plan is worked out in Act II. Lauma describes carrying a Marrow in one's body as a grueling trial of will; an earlier Moonchanter did it but lasted only two days.
