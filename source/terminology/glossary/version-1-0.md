## summary
### LITE
Version 1.0, Welcome To Teyvat, is the launch of Genshin Impact on 2020-09-28. It offers Mondstadt and Liyue, the Prologue and the first two acts of Chapter I, and 22 playable characters.
### STANDARD +
The Traveler arrives in Teyvat separated from their twin sibling, meets Paimon, and sets out to meet the Seven Archons, beginning in Mondstadt and ending the version in Liyue. A first patch, 1.0.1, followed on 2020-10-22.
### LARGE +
Everything in the game's early story, from the first hilichurl camp to the death of Rex Lapis, is set in this version, which is why many characters' knowledge is anchored at 1.0.
### ULTRA +
Later versions add a great deal that a 1.0 character could not know. The archive tags such material with the version that revealed it.

## regions
### STANDARD
Mondstadt opens with Brightcrown Mountains, Galesong Hill, Starfell Valley and Windwail Highland. Liyue opens with Bishui Plain, Lisha, Minlin, Qiongji Estuary and the Sea of Clouds.
### LARGE +
Early bosses included the Anemo, Electro and Geo Hypostases, Pyro and Cryo Regisvines and the Oceanid. The Trounce Domain Confront Stormterror and the Spiral Abyss were also present at launch.

## story
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The Prologue is the Mondstadt Archon Quest, in three acts: The Outlander Who Caught the Wind, For a Tomorrow Without Tears, and Song of the Dragon and Freedom. Chapter I, Liyue, opens with Of the Land Amidst Monoliths and continues with Farewell, Archaic Lord.
### LARGE +
In the Prologue the Traveler is sealed in Teyvat by an unknown entity, rescues Paimon from the sea and meets Amber, Kaeya, Jean and Lisa of the Knights of Favonius. The dragon Stormterror, really Dvalin, one of the Four Winds, is troubled by Abyss Order corruption. The bard Venti is revealed to be Barbatos, the Anemo Archon, and Signora of the Fatui tears his Gnosis from him.
### ULTRA +
In Chapter I the Traveler reaches Liyue Harbor in time for Rex Lapis' Rite of Descension, where the Geo Archon appears dead. The Fatui Harbinger Childe offers help, the Traveler seeks out the adepti around Liyue, and in Farewell, Archaic Lord the Traveler and Zhongli, a consultant of the Wangsheng Funeral Parlor, prepare the funeral items for the Rite of Parting. The version ends before the Archon Quest's third act.

## characters
### STANDARD
The playable roster was the Traveler (Anemo and Geo options) plus Amber, Kaeya, Lisa, Barbara, Noelle, Bennett, Razor, Beidou, Fischl, Xiangling, Xingqiu, Chongyun, Ningguang, Sucrose, Venti, Klee, Jean, Diluc, Keqing, Mona and Qiqi.
### LARGE +
Venti, Klee, Jean, Diluc, Keqing, Mona, Qiqi and the Traveler were 5-star; the others were 4-star. Mona, Sucrose, Keqing and Qiqi had not been in the closed beta tests and were added for launch.
### ULTRA +
Launch Story Quests included Kaeya's Pavo Ocellus Chapter, Amber's Lepus Chapter, Xiangling's Trulla Chapter, Lisa's Tempus Fugit Chapter, Diluc's Noctua Chapter, Razor's Lupus Minor Chapter, Xingqiu's Fabulae Textile Chapter, Klee's Trifolium Chapter, Jean's Leo Minor Chapter, Venti's Carmen Dei Chapter and Mona's Astrolabos Chapter.

## launch_events_and_wishes
### LARGE
The first character banners were Ballad in Goblets (Venti) and, in phase 2 from 2020-10-20, Sparkling Steps (Klee). Launch events included Fate Foretold, Barbara's Shining Debut, Spiral Abyss People's Choice, Seize the Day, Elemental Crucible and Marvelous Merchandise.
### ULTRA +
The launch was global and simultaneous on PS4, iOS, Android and PC on 2020-09-28. The wiki records that PS4 account data was not cross-playable with other platforms at launch, though PS4 players could still join other platforms' worlds in co-op.
