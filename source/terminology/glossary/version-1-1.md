## summary
### LITE
Version 1.1, A New Star Approaches, was released on 2020-11-11. It adds the third act of Chapter I and four characters: Tartaglia, Diona, Zhongli and Xinyan. No new region was added.
### STANDARD +
The Archon Quest act concludes the Liyue chapter that began at launch. The version also introduced the city reputation system, the Archive and Character Archive systems and Exploration Progress.
### LARGE +
Its official blurb describes the Seven victors of the Archon War once gathering to dine in Liyue, and the spirit of the sixth, Rex Lapis, returning to the heavens as an ancient god encroaches on Liyue.
### ULTRA +
Version 1.0 ended on the funeral preparations; 1.1 resolves them. A character who has not completed this act should not know what Zhongli did.

## story
@spoiler: major
@version: 1.1
### STANDARD
Chapter I Act III, A New Star Approaches, has six quests: The Floating Palace, Equilibrium, Solitary Fragrance, Heart of Glaze, Turning Point and The Fond Farewell. It is set in Liyue and centres on the Rite of Parting for the dead Geo Archon.
### LARGE +
Ningguang invites the Traveler to the Jade Chamber, and the Fatui Harbinger Childe duels the Traveler at the Golden House, believing the Traveler has the Gnosis. Childe then uses the replicated Sigils of Permission to summon Osial, an ancient god sealed by Rex Lapis, and the adepti and the Liyue Qixing stop quarrelling to fight it. Ningguang sacrifices her Jade Chamber to crush Osial into the sea.
### ULTRA +
Zhongli is revealed to be the Geo Archon himself, who staged his own death as a test of whether Liyue could continue without him, and who forged a contract with the Tsaritsa in exchange for an as yet unstated goal. He hands his Gnosis to Signora. In the closing Rite of Parting, the Qixing give an official account that omits the truth, and Zhongli tells the Traveler that Inazuma is sealed off and that its Archon, Baal, has issued a Vision Hunt Decree.

## characters
### STANDARD
New characters were Tartaglia ("Childe", 5-star Hydro bow), Diona (4-star Cryo bow), Zhongli (5-star Geo polearm) and Xinyan (4-star Pyro claymore). Their first banners were Farewell of Snezhnaya (Tartaglia and Diona) and, in phase 2, Gentry of Hermitage (Zhongli and Xinyan).
### LARGE +
Story Quests added were Tartaglia's Monoceros Caeli Chapter, Act I, Mighty Cyclops' Adventure!, and Zhongli's Historia Antiqua Chapter, Act I, Sal Flore, which became available later.
### ULTRA +
New weapons were Memory of Dust, Vortex Vanquisher, The Unforged and Royal Spear. Phase 2 of the wishes began on 2020-12-01.

## systems_and_events
### LARGE
City Reputation was introduced with unlockable gadgets, recipes, namecards and wind gliders. The Archive and Character Archive systems and an Exploration Progress system also arrived, along with the domain Enter the Golden House and the events Unreconciled Stars, Baptism of Song, Gliding Challenge and While It's Warm.
### ULTRA +
Quality-of-life changes in this version included the Original Resin limit rising from 120 to 160, inventory limits changing to slot-based categories, and an equipment locking function. These are gameplay notes, not lore.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Characters set at 1.1 know the Rite of Descension ended in apparent tragedy and may not yet know that Zhongli is the Geo Archon. Tartaglia is a known Harbinger who has just fought the Traveler. Keep the revelation about Zhongli out of conversations that precede it.
