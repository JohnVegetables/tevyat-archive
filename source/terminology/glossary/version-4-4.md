## summary
### LITE
Version 4.4, "Vibrant Harriers Aloft in Spring Breeze", was released on 2024-01-31. It opened Chenyu Vale, a new mountain-and-river area of Liyue, and added Xianyun (5-star Anemo Catalyst) and Gaming (4-star Pyro Claymore). It had no Archon Quest.

### STANDARD +
The new area centres on tea-growing settlements such as Qiaoying Village and the port of Yilong Wharf, and ties into old Liyue adepti stories.

## contents
### STANDARD
New characters: Xianyun ("Passerine Herald", 5-star Anemo Catalyst) and Gaming ("Leonine Vanguard", 4-star Pyro Claymore). New weapon: Crane's Echoing Call (5-star Catalyst). New area: Chenyu Vale, with Chenyu Vale: Upper Vale, Chenyu Vale: Southern Mountain and Mt. Laixin. Event wishes: The Crane Soars Skyward (Xianyun and Gaming) and The Moongrass' Enlightenment (Nahida and Gaming) in phase I; Invitation to Mundane Life (Xiao) and Everbloom Violet (Yae Miko) in phase II.
### LARGE +
Named locations: Qiaoying Village, Yilong Wharf, Jademouth, Mt. Lingmeng, Yaodie Valley, Mt. Xuanlian and Chizhang Wall. New outfits: Frostflower Dew (Shenhe), Twilight Blossom (Ganyu) and Bamboo Rain (Xingqiu). New enemies were the Solitary Suanni and Xuanwen Beast. The Rainjade's Benediction system and the Chenyu Adeptea material were added.
### ULTRA +
Quests: Xianyun's Story Quest Grus Serena Chapter Act I, "A Thousand Moonlit Miles", and World Quests including the Chenyu's Blessings of Sunken Jade chain, "The Cloud-Padded Path to the Chiwang Repose", "Threefold Expectations", "A Wangshan Walk to Remember", "The Roaming Abode" and "Qiaoying, the Village of Many Tales". Events included Vibrant Harriers Aloft in Spring Breeze, Of Kites and New Sights, Receiver of Friends From Afar, Journey Through Hilinigmatic Terrain and Triumphant Frenzy. A Fast Equip option for artifacts was added as a system feature.

## story
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Chenyu Vale lies around Mt. Yaojun and was once under an unnamed mistress who presided over wild spirits and animals; during the Archon War she fought Morax, and the adepti Fujin, Lingyuan and the Herblord sided against her flood plan. Many present-day inhabitants descend from refugees who fled the Chasm at the time of the cataclysm. In the present the suanni adeptus Lingyuan tampers with the vale's spirit lines, tainting the soil and water and the village's tea, until the Traveler helps Fujin restore balance.
### LARGE +
Fujin, a golden carp adeptus, spends much of her power to undo the damage and returns to her carp form afterward. Xianyun's Story Quest "A Thousand Moonlit Miles" begins with the Traveler and Paimon visiting Mt. Aocang, where Xianyun, here in human form, explains she is visiting her disciples Ganyu and Shenhe, and Ganyu suggests the human name that she adopts.

## placement
### STANDARD
Follows 4.3 and precedes 4.5. Chenyu Vale's history draws on events set far before the game's present (the Archon War), while the present-day quest chain happens in Liyue.
