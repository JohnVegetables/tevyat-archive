## summary
### LITE
Version 5.4, "Moonlight Amidst Dreams", was released on 2025-02-12. It added Yumemizuki Mizuki, a dream-eating baku who treats patients at the Aisa Bathhouse in Inazuma City, and her Story Quest "Dream Eater's Melancholia". It had no Archon Quest.
### STANDARD +
Mizuki's quest is the first act of the Tapirus Somniator Chapter and needs Chapter II Act III, "Transient Dreams" and Adventure Rank 40. The bathhouse is a Natlan-inspired spa that offers psychotherapy by baku staff.
### ULTRA +
The patch also launched the first Imaginarium Theater season of 2025 (on 2025-03-01), Genius Invokation TCG cards, a character specialty dish and Paimon's Paintings emoji set 38.

## contents
### STANDARD
Character: Yumemizuki Mizuki (5-star Anemo catalyst). Weapons: Sunny Morning Sleep-In (5-star catalyst) and Tamayuratei no Ohanashi (4-star polearm). New location: Aisa Bathhouse (instance).
### LARGE +
Banners: phase I "Dawn's Drifting Reverie" (Mizuki) and "Romaritime Meluserenity" (Sigewinne); phase II "Chanson of Many Waters" (Furina) and "Tempestuous Destiny" (Wriothesley). The character dish is "Dreams of Healing".
### ULTRA +
Events were Enchanted Tales of the Mikawa Festival, Travelers' Tales: Anthology Chapter, Realm of Tempered Valor, Invasive Fish Wrangler, Reel Ad-Venture, a Ley Line Overflow and Tapirus Somniator Chapter (Event), all limited-time. The first Imaginarium Theater season featured Mizuki as a guest star.

## story
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
On a night walk the Traveler and Paimon stumble into Mizuki's dream-eating. She is a baku, a youkai that feeds on dreams and nightmares, and works as a clinical psychologist and major investor at Aisa Bathhouse. The bathhouse is struggling financially while a rival psychotherapy clinic promises better results, and the Traveler investigates.
### LARGE +
The rival is run by a man called Emura Ichigensai, really Emura Chuuya, a former patient. His clients feel only happiness, but their troubles are only hidden. The cause is Kuroyume, a nightmare monster possessing him. The Traveler and Mizuki enter his nightmare, fight Kuroyume, and rescue Chuuya from drowning. The baku then clear the nightmare bubbles over Narukami Island.
### ULTRA +
Yae Miko, a friend of Mizuki from before she became Guuji, secretly gives the Traveler Chuuya's address because she worries about her. Chuuya is saved but regresses to a young child's mind. Mizuki thanks Miko, saying her feelings of inferiority gave her the strength to persevere.

## placement
### STANDARD
Follows 5.3 and precedes 5.5. The Aisa Bathhouse page says the bathhouse was renovated after the Natlan quest "Incandescent Ode of Resurrection", under Mizuki's direction.

## entry: Version 5.4 Aisa Bathhouse
@keys: Version 5.4, Update 5.4, Patch 5.4, Aisa Bathhouse
@keys2: Mizuki, baku, bathhouse, psychotherapy, hot spring, Aikawa Susumu
@version: 5.4
### STANDARD
Aisa Bathhouse is an indoor bathhouse in Inazuma City, inspired by the hot springs of Natlan. It offers a spa, fine dining and psychotherapy from resident baku, including Mizuki. Its owner is Aikawa Susumu, who returned from Natlan, and its baku staff are Hyakujitsuhaku Shion and Shiba Murasaki. Many Tenryou Commission samurai visit.
### LARGE +
Shion takes a food-based approach to therapy. Mizuki works to expand the brand to Watatsumi Island and Natlan. Treating bad dreams can leave baku in pain: Shion and Murasaki suffer indigestion after eating two clients' nightmares.

## entry: Version 5.4 Dream Eater's Melancholia
@keys: Version 5.4, Update 5.4, Patch 5.4, Dream Eater's Melancholia
@keys2: Tapirus Somniator Chapter, Mizuki, Kuroyume, Emura, nightmare
@version: 5.4
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Dream Eater's Melancholia has five quests: "Encounter in the Realm of Dreams", "Crisis at the Aisa Bathhouse", "A Deceit Called Happiness", "Descendant of Nightmares" and "Afterword". Mizuki explains that she cries during the last stage of digesting nightmares. She identifies nightmare monsters as the natural enemy of baku, and Kuroyume says he is the last of his kind.
### LARGE +
Inside Chuuya's nightmare the Traveler sees fragments of his rejected ambitions to be a writer. In the borderland of Inazuma's collective subconscious, the Traveler offers a sweet dream to restore Mizuki's strength. Kuroyume survives wounded.
