## summary
### LITE
Version 7.1, "A Rekviem for the Underworld", was released on 2026-09-23 and is the canon cutoff of this archive. The wiki lists no new area. Its main content was Archon Quest Chapter VII Act III (White Night, Like a Dream Upon Waking) and Act IV (A Rekviem for the Underworld), and two new five-star characters: Vesna, an Anemo Sword user, and Vodyanitsa, a Hydro Catalyst user.
### STANDARD +
It follows Version 7.0. Act III has the Traveler meet the Tsaritsa at court and learn why she seeks the Gnoses, while the Fatui look for a way to counter the Curse of Death. Act IV ends with the Ruler of Death Ronova losing her Authority and being mortally wounded, and with a Nail above the Cryo Dragon's corpse beginning to crumble.
### LARGE +
Alongside the Archon Quest the version added a Travelers' Tales series, Snowstride Chapter, with short stories for Alyosha, Odette, Vesna, Vodyanitsa, Tartaglia, Sandrone, Jahoda, Charlotte, Clorinde and Lyney. The Genshin Impact 6th Anniversary profile background appears in the version gallery.
### ULTRA +
A known issue noted at launch was an English voice-over line in the cutscene of the quest One Move Left to Make that did not match the text, including an incorrect pronoun. On 2026-09-24 a patch fixed a problem that blocked progress in the Black Feathers Scattered at Parting domain and corrected an English description of Vodyanitsa's Elemental Skill.

## release
@version: 7.1
### LITE
Released 2026-09-23, after 7.0. No new area.
### STANDARD +
Phase I banners were When Warm Winds Cavort (Vesna) and Surging Ballad (Vodyanitsa). Phase II banners were Void Star's Advent (Skirk) and La Chanson Cerise (Escoffier). New four-star weapons were New Bough (Sword), Breezeborne Refrain (Bow), Winter's Heavy Heart (Catalyst) and Silver Light (Sword).

Edge of Survival gained two stages, Fiery Feather Pursuit and Ice-Imprisoned Citadel, and the Special Recruitment Project: Consultant to the Tsaritsa was added. The new enemy was the Guardian Blade of Drifting Snow.
### ULTRA +
Imaginarium Theater was scheduled to open on 2026-10-01 requiring Hydro, Cryo and Anemo, with a second season on 2026-11-01 requiring Hydro, Electro and Geo. Genius Invokation TCG gained Columbina and Illuga cards and the Lunar-Crystallize reaction card Moondrift Harmony. New dishes were Celestial Tea Crisp, Mors and Royal Grilled Fish.

## characters
@version: 7.1
### STANDARD
Vesna ("Snowy Banquet's Sharp Blade") is a five-star Anemo Sword character; in the story she commands the Druzhna and carries the mark of life. Vodyanitsa ("Lingering Siren-Song") is a five-star Hydro Catalyst character; she is the lead singer of the Korolevskiy Troupe and, like Alyosha, a member of the secret group Rokot. Their own files carry their profiles.
### LARGE +
New namecards were Vesna: Revelry, Vodyanitsa: Whirlpool, Travel Notes: Moontrace and Snezhnaya: Frostfall. Character specialty dishes were Iridescent Aria and Hoarfrost Soup.

## archon_quest_act_iii
@version: 7.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
White Night, Like a Dream Upon Waking (Chapter VII Act III) has three quests: Light and Shadow, Intertwined; Sparks Converging Upon the Tundra; and One Move Left to Make. The Traveler is approved for an audience with the Tsaritsa, who sets aside investigations into them and grants unrestricted access to the palace.
### LARGE +
The Tsaritsa, Anastasya, explains that she wants the Gnoses to gain enough power to face the Heavenly Principles, since Snezhnaya's growth has brought it to the limit of what they permit and left it under threat of a Celestial Nail. A crystalline seal left by the Third Descender lies in a hidden chamber and reacts only to the Gnoses. She says her Authority is love but refuses to use that identity. She records each day in a transcription prism because she distrusts memory. Project Mistilteinn is halted until Ronova can be dealt with.
### ULTRA +
At the Armory Palace, Pierro uses a prism called the Mirror of Remembrance to review memories and finds that Ronova had been working with Rhinedottir, who revived the people Ronova killed before the Shades could notice. Vesna, who carries a small Authority of life inherited through the first Vila, extracts "Seals of Life", bubbles holding an impactful memory, as a way to counter death. When the seals are collected from the public and officials, a theft is traced to Northland Bank, where the Traveler and Vesna face a creature made by the Ruler of Life with Pantalone's help.

## archon_quest_act_iv
@version: 7.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
A Rekviem for the Underworld (Chapter VII Act IV) has three quests: Delusion, Etched in Time and Black Feathers Scattered at Parting. The Fatui prepare a new Delusion with Arlecchino's help, recover the notebook of Hroptatyr, and set a trap for Ronova.
### LARGE +
Pierro explains that the authorities of Life, Death, Time and Space originally belonged to the Three Moons, and that the Crimson Moon held death. Arlecchino, as a Crimson Moon descendant, is therefore needed, and she says the House of the Hearth will become independent of the Fatui and stop training children as spies. The Harbingers vote unanimously to open Hroptatyr's notebook, which gives hints for the Delusion work, and the Traveler learns that Hroptatyr summoned their sibling at the same time Pierro summoned Paimon.
### ULTRA +
Dainsleif joins the plan. Rhinedottir visits the Tsaritsa's palace while Ronova's curse strikes at Rankova Zorya. The Tsaritsa uses the six Gnoses to manipulate the time and space around Rhinedottir, so that the window to revive Ronova's victims is missed, and Celestia seizes Ronova's Authority for abuse. Pierro's new Delusion then crushes Ronova. Project Mistilteinn is no longer restricted. Ronova, mortally wounded, reaches the Nail above the Cryo Dragon's corpse and fuses it with Eitr as a parting gift, so that the Nail begins to crumble and threatens to release Holy Soil over Teyvat.

## entry: A Rekviem for the Underworld
@keys: A Rekviem for the Underworld, Black Feathers Scattered at Parting
@version: 7.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
A Rekviem for the Underworld is Chapter VII Act IV. It follows the Fatui plan to stop Ronova, with Pierro's new Delusion, Dainsleif's help and the Tsaritsa's manipulation of time during Rhinedottir's visit. Ronova loses her Authority and is crushed, but she reaches the Nail above the Cryo Dragon's corpse and damages it.
### LARGE +
Dainsleif says the Snezhnayan victory over the Ruler of Death marks the start of a new, tumultuous era, and he meets Hroptatyr's remnants but is disappointed. The Tsaritsa personally commends all participants.

## entry: White Night, Like a Dream Upon Waking
@keys: White Night Like a Dream Upon Waking, Seal of Life, Seals of Life
@version: 7.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
White Night, Like a Dream Upon Waking is Chapter VII Act III. The Traveler meets the Tsaritsa, and Vesna's memory-bubble Seals of Life are gathered as a way to counter the Curse of Death.

Danica tests the Traveler with a Mora game in Podvorye Restaurant before apologising later, and the leaked news about the seals brings eager volunteers. Pantalone describes how he built Northland Bank by combining human and fae traditions.
