## summary
### LITE
Version 5.6, "Paralogism", was released on 2025-05-07. It added no new area. Its main content was the Archon Quest Interlude Chapter Act IV, also titled Paralogism, and two new characters: Escoffier, a five-star Cryo Polearm user, and Ifa, a four-star Anemo Catalyst user.
### STANDARD +
It follows Version 5.5 and precedes 5.7. The Archon Quest takes place in Mondstadt during the Windblume Festival and deals with a murder trial against Albedo, which turns out to be a trap for a monster that imitates other people.
### LARGE +
Escoffier's Story Quest, Dulciaria Structura Chapter Act I (Treasured Above All), opened at the same time and sends the Traveler to Hotel Debord in Fontaine. The wiki notes that "Paralogism" is the shortest version title.
### ULTRA +
Paralogism is the final act of the Interlude Chapter as of the cutoff. Interlude acts are stand-alone follow-ups to earlier quests rather than parts of the main Archon Quest chain.

## release
@version: 5.6
### LITE
Released 2025-05-07, between 5.5 and 5.7.
### STANDARD +
Phase I banners were La Chanson Cerise (Escoffier and Ifa) and In the Name of the Rosula (Navia and Ifa). Phase II, from 2025-05-27, reran Kinich, the Raiden Shogun and a Chronicled Wish.
### LARGE +
New weapons were Symphonist of Scents (five-star Polearm) and Sequence of Solitude (four-star Bow). New events included Whirling Waltz, Operation Downpour Simulation, Legends Ablaze: Cross-Border Brawl and Chronicle of Shifting Stratagems. The first Imaginarium Theater season opened on 2025-06-01 with Electro, Cryo and Dendro required.
### ULTRA +
New enemies were the Secret Source Automaton: Overseer Device and the chess-themed trial The Game Before the Gate, with matching Ascended Sample materials (Knight, Rook and Queen). Genius Invokation TCG gained Sethos and Xilonen cards.

## characters
@version: 5.6
### STANDARD
Escoffier ("Tasteful Excellence") is a five-star Cryo Polearm character; the wiki describes her as a renowned Fontainian chef, formerly of Hotel Debord, with exacting standards. Ifa ("In the Wake of Wandering Winds") is a four-star Anemo Catalyst character. Their own files cover their profiles.
### LARGE +
New dishes of the version included Gateau Debord: Magnifique and Emotional Support, and the namecards were Escoffier: Charm, Ifa: Bro and Travel Notes: Balletica.

## archon_quest
@version: 5.6
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Paralogism (Interlude Chapter Act IV) needs Adventure Rank 40 and the earlier acts Inversion of Genesis and Traveler Observation Report. During 5.6 up to before 6.0 a Quick Start let players enter it at Adventure Rank 28 after A New Star Approaches. It has three quests: Crimes One and Two, The Hunger of Many and The Gardener.
### LARGE +
During the Windblume Festival, people in Mondstadt act oddly and Albedo is tried for murder and illegal disposal of human remains. The Knights and Church of Favonius had staged the trial months ahead to flush out an imitator. Albedo explains that the imitator is a spawn of the dragon Durin, which can copy others. The imposter Hertha flees and monsters attack the city.
### ULTRA +
Albedo reveals he had been stationed in Dragonspine to watch Durin, whose remains were stirring, and that he had killed and buried people who were really monsters wearing human identities. He and the Traveler get reagents from the Hexenzirkel after a chess trial involving Barbeloth, then extract energy from Durin's Core. At the festival, with Venti singing as Windblume Star, Albedo's alchemy turns Mini Durin human, taking the fallen Durin's place. Albedo also mentions that Rhinedottir had devoured Naberius, the Ruler of Life.

## entry: Paralogism
@keys: Paralogism, Interlude Chapter Act IV
@version: 5.6
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Paralogism is the fourth act of the Interlude Chapter, set at Mondstadt's Windblume Festival. It centres on Albedo's trial, a trap for a shapeshifting spawn of Durin, and ends with Mini Durin becoming human. Characters include Albedo, Venti, Jean, Eula, Kaeya, Diluc, Rosaria, the Hexenzirkel's Alice and Barbeloth.
### LARGE +
Dahlia, a deacon of the Church, is revealed to also be Barbatos's herald. Phonia, missing for over half a year, is taken into custody, and Albedo promises to save her.
