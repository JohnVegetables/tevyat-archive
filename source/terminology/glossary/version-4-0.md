## summary
### LITE
Version 4.0, "As Light Rain Falls Without Reason", was released on 2023-08-16. It opened Fontaine, the Hydro nation, as a playable region and started Archon Quest Chapter IV. It added the characters Lyney, Lynette and Freminet and gave the Traveler the Hydro element.
### STANDARD +
The story is a run of public trials in the Court of Fontaine: first a death and disappearance at the Opera Epiclese, then Navia's hunt for whoever is behind a series of vanishings of young women.
### LARGE +
This is the version to cite for the first appearance of Fontaine's courtroom culture, Arkhe, diving and the Fountain of Lucine. It is also the version in which the Fatui connection of the magician siblings and Childe's trouble with his Hydro abilities are first shown.
### ULTRA +
In story order, 4.0 covers the Traveler's arrival at Romaritime Harbor through the aftermath of the Sinthe case, ending a few days after the verdicts. Furina is still the public Hydro Archon and Neuvillette's nature is not yet revealed. Anything about the Fortress of Meropide, the Primordial Sea breach, Focalors or the true meaning of the prophecy belongs to 4.1 and 4.2 and should not be known to a character who has only lived through 4.0.

## new_content
### LITE
New characters: Lyney (5-star Pyro Bow), Lynette (4-star Anemo Sword) and Freminet (4-star Cryo Claymore). New region: Fontaine, with the Belleau Region, Beryl Region and Court of Fontaine Region. The Traveler can now be Hydro.
### STANDARD +
New weapon on the weapon banner: The First Great Magic (5-star Bow). New artifact sets: Golden Troupe and Marechaussee Hunter. New systems: Arkhe (the Pneuma and Ousia energies), diving, the Fountain of Lucine and the Fontaine reputation system. New enemy families include Clockwork Meka, Fontemer Aberrants, Tainted Hydro Phantasms and Breacher Primuses.
### LARGE +
Character banners ran as Conjuring Chiaroscuro (Lyney and Lynette), Discerner of Enigmas (Yelan and Lynette), Gentry of Hermitage (Zhongli and Freminet) and Farewell of Snezhnaya (Tartaglia and Freminet). Lyney's Story Quest, Felis Fuscus Chapter Act I - The Forgotten Thief, arrived in this version. The Multi-Layered Map function was also added.
### ULTRA +
Fontaine's new domains were Denouement of Sin, Echoes of the Deep Tides and Pale Forgotten Glory. Spiral Abyss artifact rewards were swapped to Marechaussee Hunter and Golden Troupe, and the artifact inventory limit rose from 1,500 to 1,800.

## story
@spoiler: major
### LITE
Archon Quest Chapter IV, Act I (Prelude of Blancheur and Noirceur) and Act II (As Light Rain Falls Without Reason).
### STANDARD +
In Act I the Traveler and Paimon reach Romaritime Harbor, meet Lyney and Lynette, and hear the prophecy that Fontaine will be flooded and its people washed of their sin. At the twins' opera performance a trick goes wrong, a stagehand is found dead and an audience member vanishes. Lyney is tried, the Traveler defends him, and the twins' Fatui membership comes out in court.
### LARGE +
The trial in Act I ends with a Not Guilty verdict, with the missing woman reappearing and confessing what actually happened in the magic box. The witness Vaughn, who had given false evidence, dissolves into water before naming the "Boss". Lyney tells the Traveler that he and Lynette serve the House of the Hearth, led by the Knave Arlecchino, who believes in the prophecy. In Act II Navia, head of Spina di Rosula, takes the Traveler and Paimon into her investigation of the disappearances. Childe appears in Fontaine with his Hydro weapons slipping out of his control and gives his Vision to the Traveler for safekeeping before a duel.
### ULTRA +
Act II ties the case to the death of Navia's father Callas, who died in a duel with Clorinde three years earlier after being accused of killing Jacques. The Traveler traces the cause to Sinthe, a drug made with Primordial Seawater, and exposes the man behind it, Marcel, who is revealed to be Vacher, the man the Oceanid in the Fountain of Lucine had been calling for. Marcel dies in the dream realm at the Fountain, and doctors rule a panic attack. Childe is tried over the case and the Oratrice delivers a Guilty verdict, the first time it differs from Neuvillette's own, and Childe is taken away. Neuvillette says afterwards that he will investigate himself.

## characters_introduced
### STANDARD
Lyney and Lynette are magician siblings of the House of the Hearth, and Freminet is their shy younger brother. Navia (head of Spina di Rosula), Clorinde (the Champion Duelist) and Charlotte (a reporter) become the Traveler's allies in the Archon Quest. Neuvillette is chief justice of the Court and Furina is Fontaine's public Archon.
### ULTRA +
Childe (Tartaglia) is in Fontaine during the Archon Quest. He mentions his master Skirk, who does not appear in person until the Act V finale.
