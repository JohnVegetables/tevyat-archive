## summary
### LITE
Version 3.1, "King Deshret and the Three Magi", was released on 2022-09-28. It opened the desert half of Sumeru, the Great Red Sand, and continued Archon Quest Chapter III with Acts III and IV. It added the playable characters Cyno, Nilou and Candace.
### STANDARD +
It follows Version 3.0 and precedes Version 3.2. Where 3.0 was the rainforest and the Akademiya's world of scholars, 3.1 turned to the desert people, the Eremites, and the buried history of King Deshret.
### LARGE +
The official tagline contrasts the king who once commanded the sand with his burial in it, and promises that hidden ruins will welcome visitors and "reveal ancient, buried truths".
### ULTRA +
The Desert of Hadramaveth, a separate desert area of Sumeru, was added later in Version 3.4.

## release
@version: 3.1
### LITE
Released 2022-09-28, between Version 3.0 and Version 3.2.
### STANDARD +
Phase I (2022-09-28) had the banners Twilight Arbiter (Cyno and Candace) and Ballad in Goblets (Venti and Candace). Phase II (2022-10-14) had Twirling Lotus (Nilou) and Secretum Secretorum (Albedo).
### LARGE +
New weapons were the 5-star sword Key of Khaj-Nisut, the 5-star polearm Staff of the Scarlet Sands, the 4-star sword Xiphos' Moonlight, the 4-star claymore Makhaira Aquamarine, the 4-star catalyst Wandering Evenstar and the event polearm Missive Windspear.

## characters
@version: 3.1
### LITE
Cyno ("Judicator of Secrets", 5-star Electro polearm), Nilou ("Dance of Lotuslight", 5-star Hydro sword) and Candace ("Golden Vow", 4-star Hydro polearm) joined the game.
### STANDARD +
Cyno and Candace appear in the Archon Quest acts of this version; Nilou's role was already set up in Act II of 3.0 and carried into her Story Quest, Lotos Somno Chapter Act I, "To the Wise".

## quests
@version: 3.1
### STANDARD
Archon Quest Chapter III Act III is "Dreams, Emptiness, Deception" and Act IV is "King Deshret and the Three Magi". Cyno's Story Quest Lupus Aureus Chapter Act I, "Sands of Solitude", and Nilou's Lotos Somno Chapter Act I, "To the Wise", also arrived.
### LARGE +
World Quest chains included Golden Slumber, Old Notes and New Friends, The Exile, and A Gifted Rose, along with Afratu's Dilemma, Blooming Sands and a quest about joining the Eremites. "Gourmet Supremos: Within Our Duties", released in 3.0, became accessible and "Gourmet Supremos: Cleanup" arrived.

## entry: Great Red Sand
@keys: Great Red Sand, Hypostyle Desert, Land of Lower Setekh, Land of Upper Setekh
@version: 3.1
### STANDARD
The new area was Sumeru's desert, the Great Red Sand, with three listed subareas: the Hypostyle Desert, the Land of Lower Setekh and the Land of Upper Setekh. It added desert wildlife such as scorpions, desert foxes and scarabs, and enemies such as Aeonblight Drakes, Primal Constructs and Eremite types.
### LARGE +
Hidden exploration objectives of the version included Abbas' Escape, Eleazar Hospital Notes, Golden Life, From Yesteryear Awoken, and The Dead End and the Glinting Architects.

## entry: Chapter III Acts III and IV
@keys: Dreams Emptiness Deception, King Deshret and the Three Magi, Village Keepers
@version: 3.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Act III begins after the Sabzeruz samsara: the Traveler and Nahida plan to turn the Grand Sage's secretary, the Fatui Harbinger Dottore springs a trap, and the trail leads to Aaru Village in the desert, where Alhaitham, Cyno, Dehya and Candace meet. In Act IV, missing Village Keepers lead the group to the ruins of King Deshret's civilisation, where an inscription recalls how forbidden knowledge harmed his people and how Greater Lord Rukkhadevata helped them.
### LARGE +
In the same act, two scholars connected to the Akademiya confirm that the sages plan to create a new god with Dottore's encouragement. The two captured scholars describe the dream samsara and the harvesting of divine knowledge as steps of that plan.

## entry: Version 3.1 events
@keys: Of Ballads and Brews, Wind Chaser, Star-Seeker's Sojourn, Hyakunin Ikki, Path of Gleaming Jade
@version: 3.1
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
New limited-time events were Of Ballads and Brews, Wind Chaser, Star-Seeker's Sojourn, Hyakunin Ikki, Path of Gleaming Jade and a thank-you gift event. The permanent Spiral Abyss challenge Curtain of Leaves, which lets players invite Collei, came with the version.

## entry: Spiral Abyss changes in 3.1
@keys: Pruning Moon, Ascending Moon, Rupturing Moon
@version: 3.1
### STANDARD
Gameplay note: Floor 11 gave every party member a 75 percent Electro damage bonus, and the lunar phases that began after the update were Pruning Moon, Ascending Moon and Rupturing Moon, which rewarded Bloom, Hyperbloom and Burgeon and Dendro Core damage. This is game mechanics, not in-world lore.
