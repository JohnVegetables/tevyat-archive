## summary
### LITE
Version 5.5, "Day of the Flame's Return", was released on 2025-03-26. It had no Archon Quest. It added two Natlan areas, Atocpan and the Ancient Sacred Mountain, and two characters: Varesa (five-star Electro Catalyst) and Iansan (four-star Electro Polearm).
### STANDARD +
It follows Version 5.4 and precedes 5.6. The version's main story was a Tribal Chronicles chapter for the Collective of Plenty, and its main event, Tournament of Glory in Bloom, staged war games between the six tribes.
### LARGE +
Atocpan holds the Great Volcano of Tollan and the Collective of Plenty's home. The Ancient Sacred Mountain lies inside that volcano and holds the Sacred City of Tollan, capital of the ancient dragon civilisation.
### ULTRA +
The version also raised the level cap of the Tona's Flame system to 40 and added the Tatankasaur as a Saurian companion, the Land of Tleyaoyotl album, and the domain Derelict Masonry Dock.

## release
@version: 5.5
### LITE
Released 2025-03-26, between 5.4 and 5.6.
### STANDARD +
Phase I banners were Cornucopia of Contention (Varesa and Iansan) and The Crane Soars Skyward (Xianyun and Iansan). Phase II banners were Forgefire's Blessing (Xilonen) and Ballad in Goblets (Venti).
### LARGE +
The new five-star weapon was Vivid Notions (Catalyst), and the new artifact sets were Long Night's Oath and Finale of the Deep Galleries. World quest chains included A Finale Emberforged, Chronicler of the Crumbling City and Path to the Flaming Peaks.
### ULTRA +
Spiral Abyss refreshed on 2025-04-16. Genius Invokation TCG added Emilie and Kachina character cards. The hidden objectives included An Audience With the Nightsoul Pillar and Little Lost Saurians.

## characters
@version: 5.5
### STANDARD
Varesa ("Strength in Serenity") is a five-star Electro Catalyst character. Iansan ("Tempered in Molten Stone") is a four-star Electro Polearm character. Their own files carry the profiles.
### LARGE +
The namecards were Varesa: Powerhouse and Iansan: Unyielding.

## story
@version: 5.5
### STANDARD
The Tribal Chronicles chapter for the Collective of Plenty is Mushroom Realm Mystery. In its first act the Traveler and Paimon meet Varesa near the Great Volcano of Tollan and she guides them to her tribe. In the second she shows them the tribe's camp and her family's orchard.
### LARGE +
The chapter has three acts: Ardent Land of Plenty, Orchard Repose and Field Fungal Traces. The first two require the Pilgrimage quest from Act I of Chapter V, and the third needs Incandescent Ode of Resurrection.

## events
@availability: limited
@version: 5.5
### STANDARD
Limited-time events of the version included Tournament of Glory in Bloom, Quirky Quaking Arena, Rhythm Ball Meztli, Unlimited Fighting Championship, Overflowing Favor, The Sacred Mountain and Sanctified Land and a Mushroom Realm Mystery event. They ran only during the version and are not universal knowledge.
### LARGE +
Tournament of Glory in Bloom ran 2025-03-28 to 2025-04-14 and unlocked the character Ororon as a reward. It had two modes: Bloomflower Trials, in which players fight war games between the six tribes, and Saurian Treasure Seeker, a route-planning game with Saurian whelps.

## entry: Ancient Sacred Mountain
@keys: Ancient Sacred Mountain, Sacred City of Tollan
@version: 5.5
### STANDARD
An area inside the Great Volcano of Tollan, containing the Sacred City of Tollan, the capital of the dragon civilisation that once ruled Natlan. It opens through the quest Nahuatzin's Leap, and its lower levels open as the quests progress.
