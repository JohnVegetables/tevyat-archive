## summary
### LITE
Fontaine's faith is devotion to its God of Justice, the Hydro Archon. Egeria, "Mistress of Many Waters", ruled until the Cataclysm and was succeeded by Focalors. For five centuries Fontainians lived under the Prophecy that their sin would one day dissolve them in a great flood. In 4.2 Focalors had herself executed, which destroyed the Hydro Archon's divine throne, and the Hydro Dragon Neuvillette absolved the people. There is now no Hydro Archon.
### STANDARD +
Belief here runs through justice rather than ritual. The courtroom is Fontaine's cathedral, the people's faith in a fair verdict powered the city, and the Fontinalia Festival recalls the quest to bring Egeria back. Furina, who played the Hydro Archon for five centuries, abdicated after 4.2, yet her birthday remains a public holiday.
### LARGE +
Three layers coexist: the public cult of a goddess of justice (Egeria and Focalors), the older Oceanid devotion to Egeria that outlived her, and the dread of the Prophecy, a judgement from Celestia on the origin of Fontaine's people. The Prophecy was resolved in 4.2, so faith today is closer to memory and custom than to expectation.
### ULTRA +
A careful speaker keeps apart what the public was told (an Archon who loved trials and a Prophecy she could not stop) and what Archon Quest Chapter IV revealed (a divided god, a hidden Oratrice, a staged trial and a deliberate destruction of the throne). Most citizens know only part of the second layer.

## practice
### LITE
Fontainians do not appear to worship through priests or temples. Respect for the God of Justice shows in how seriously the law is taken. The people's belief in justice is felt at every verdict. Fontaine's Statue of the Seven is a monumental figure said to be sculpted in the Hydro Archon's image, and Fontinalia is the festival tied to Egeria.
### STANDARD +
- Trials as devotion. Court cases are staged in the Opera Epiclese and watched by paying audiences. The Oratrice Mecanique d'Analyse Cardinale, built by Focalors, turned the public's faith in justice into Indemnitium energy. In effect, the nation's belief powered its lamps and transport.
- Statue of the Seven. The Fontaine statue is described as a vast stone figure watching over the country, said to be made in the likeness of the Hydro Archon. Its inscription calls the law a fortress above all waters that neither sin nor flood can bring down.
- Fontinalia. A festival marking the Lochknights' search for the Oceanids and their welcoming of Egeria. Traditionally people dress up, raise golden cups and go door to door asking for pure water. Furina changed it into a carnival with sweets, and children call "Trial or treat!" at each house.
- The Fortress of Meropide. Egeria exiled criminals; when some begged forgiveness she gave them the duty of guarding her secret, the opening to the Primordial Sea. That is the origin of the prison and a quiet piece of religious history.
### LARGE +
- Egeria's followers. The Oceanids willingly served Egeria and spread across Teyvat on her order to connect everyone. After her death many refused to accept Focalors as Hydro Archon, found what remained of Egeria at the Harvisptokhm in Sumeru and chose exile. This is why Oceanids rarely appear in Fontaine.
- Lochknight romance. The Lochknights are told of in plays, poetry and film. The farewell of the knights Drest and Adsiltia is regarded as a classic of Fontainian romance, and their helms inspired the feather ornaments once fashionable among opera composers.
- Law as inheritance. Fontaine's law is famously intricate and punishes even small infractions, and Egeria is the one credited with establishing its laws after Remuria fell.
### ULTRA +
- The death penalty as a myth. Fontainians treated capital punishment as something that never happens. The only sentence of death the Oratrice ever issued was the one it passed on the Hydro Archon's throne in 4.2.
- The Oratrice as sacred object. It was publicly an impartial judge, and Focalors' hidden divinity was inside it. After 4.2 it stopped working, and Neuvillette now presides without it.

## history
@version: 4.0
### LARGE
After the Heavenly Principles took the Hydro authority from the Hydro Dragon, Naberius made Egeria as the Primordial Sea's new heart. Remuria, the nation of the god-king Remus, fell. Celestia then released Egeria from her confinement, gave her a Gnosis and set her over Fontaine, where she gathered survivors, settled them around springs and began the era of law. She is one of the Seven, and after the Archon War she attended their gatherings in Liyue. She died fighting at Tunigi Hollow in the Cataclysm 500 years ago. Her body became the Amrita, and the Harvisptokhm in Sumeru was grown from it to hold her consciousness. Focalors, one of her Oceanids who had been made human, succeeded her.
### ULTRA +
Egeria is said to have shed a first tear that gave rise to the Oceanids. Some Oceanids envied human life, so she poured Primordial Sea water into their blood vessels and gave them human form. Celestia judged this a sin because no new human race had been sanctioned. Egeria was imprisoned in the Primordial Sea, and her people were branded with Original Sin, which set the Prophecy in motion. Egeria did not find a solution before she died and left the problem to Focalors. Her titles include Mistress of Many Waters, Noble Navigator, Ruler of Rivers and Seas and Queen of All Waters, and Focalors was styled Regina of All Waters, Kindreds, Peoples and Laws.
The Harvisptokhm episode ties Fontainian religion to Sumeru. Greater Lord Rukkhadevata anchored Egeria's consciousness there so she could seal the defilement at Tunigi Hollow. Later the Khvarena bird Simurgh drank from the Amrita and became the Pari, who worship the Harvisptokhm as their god. In the Sumeru Archon Quest, the Traveler and Sorush awaken Egeria's consciousness, so Egeria's remnant is an object of care for people far from Fontaine.

## prophecy
@version: 4.0
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
The Prophecy says that every Fontainian is born with sin that cannot be absolved. One day the waters will rise and dissolve the sinful, leaving the Hydro Archon alone and weeping on her throne, and the sin of the people will be washed away. Four stone slates south of Poisson show the stages: Egeria creating Fontainians from Oceanids, Celestia punishing Egeria and her kin, the Hydro Archon on trial, and a flooded Fontaine with the Archon weeping. Many Fontainians lived with it for 500 years. After 4.2, people no longer worry about it.
### LARGE +
The sin is the creation of people who dissolve back into the sea when exposed to enough Primordial Sea water, since their blood is a borrowed substitute. Wriothesley knew of a sluice gate in the Forbidden Zone of the Fortress of Meropide that held the sea back, and built his ship, the Wingalet, as a precaution inspired by the legend of Remus's Golden Bee. Before 4.0's story, the traveller Liben noted that locals were uneasy and expected some judgement.
### ULTRA +
Neuvillette described the slate of the trial as an allegory in which water stands for justice. The Prophecy was not a vague omen: it named a flood, a trial and a weeping Archon, and Focalors' plan was to let it appear fulfilled while the people were saved.

## resolution
@version: 4.2
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
In Archon Quest Chapter IV (4.2, Masquerade of the Guilty), Furina was put on trial and the Oratrice declared the Hydro Archon guilty and sentenced her to death. The sentence fell on Focalors, the divinity who had been hiding in the Oratrice while Furina played the Archon in public. Focalors, using five centuries of stored Indemnitium, executed herself. This destroyed the Hydro Archon's divine throne and returned the Hydro authority and her Gnosis to Neuvillette, the Hydro Dragon. Neuvillette absolved the Fontainians of Original Sin and made them true humans. In the flood that followed the battle with the All-Devouring Narwhal they no longer dissolved, though those who had dissolved earlier were not restored. Furina later abdicated.
### LARGE +
Focalors had separated her divinity from her humanity, called the human half Furina, and cursed her to be immortal only while Focalors lived. She chose Neuvillette as Iudex by sending him a letter, hoping he would learn to care for humanity. Her last words thanked Furina and blessed her to live as a human. She admitted she feared death. People already dissolved before the absolution were not restored.
### ULTRA +
Because the throne no longer exists, no new Hydro Archon can arise, and the Archons number six. Neuvillette handles the duties once tied to the Archon's position.

## aftermath
@version: 4.2
### LARGE
Furina legally abdicated, left the Palais Mermonia and lives as an ordinary actress, later receiving a Hydro Vision. According to Neuvillette, the public knows only that her death sentence was not carried out and that she abdicated. Her birthday, 13 October, remains a public holiday. In Neuvillette's birthday mail (2023-12-18) he says her birthday is a holiday that remains so to this day. The Furina Award of the Fontinalia Film Festival kept its name after her abdication.
### ULTRA +
The Fontinalia Film Festival is run by the Fontaine Film Association, and the award goes to the highest-scoring entry. Furina directed the first winning film, The Two Musketeers (Musketeer Pictures). The film festival belongs to the limited-time event Roses and Muskets.
Canon shows no cult built around Neuvillette. He is Chief Justice and, in dragon terms, the Hydro Sovereign.

## scene_use
### LITE
Treat faith in Fontaine as civic and theatrical. A character might invoke "justice" the way others invoke a god, mention Furina's birthday holiday, or speak of the Prophecy as something now over.
### STANDARD +
- After 4.2 the Prophecy is past and the Oratrice is silent; before 4.2 people half-believed it.
### LARGE +
Keep what a person knows consistent with their position. A Poisson resident, a Meropide inmate, a Melusine and an Opera Epiclese regular will not all have the same view of Furina's trial. Oceanids and the Pari bring an older Egeria-centred devotion with them.
### ULTRA +
Never put Archon Quest Chapter IV revelations in the mouth of someone who was not there or who was not told.

## entry: Hydro Archon (God of Justice)
@keys: Hydro Archon, God of Justice, Regina of All Waters
@keys2: Fontaine, Fontainians
@version: 4.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The Hydro Archon was the God of Justice among the Seven. Egeria was the first, and Focalors the second and last. Focalors' execution in 4.2 destroyed the throne, so the title can never be held again. Furina publicly played the Archon for 499 years and was the human body that Focalors had separated from her divinity.

## entry: Egeria
@keys: Egeria, Mistress of Many Waters, Noble Navigator
@keys2: Hydro Archon, Fontaine, Oceanid
@version: 4.0
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Egeria was the first Hydro Archon and ruled Fontaine from about 2,000 to 500 years ago, after Remuria's fall. She gave some Oceanids human form and was punished for it by Celestia. She exiled criminals and later gave them the duty that created the Fortress of Meropide. She was killed at Tunigi Hollow in the Cataclysm, and her body turned into the Amrita. The Oceanids loved her and rejected her successor.
### LARGE +
She chose Focalors as successor in the hope of a plan to deceive Celestia. She is dead, but a remnant of her consciousness was held in the Harvisptokhm in Sumeru.

## entry: Prophecy of Fontaine
@keys: Prophecy of Fontaine, Fontaine prophecy, Original Sin
@keys2: Fontaine, Fontainians, flood
@version: 4.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Every Fontainian bears sin, the waters will rise and dissolve them, and the Hydro Archon will weep alone. It was resolved in 4.2 when Focalors' death returned the Hydro authority to Neuvillette, who absolved the people. The people were not dissolved in the flood that followed.
### LARGE +
The slates stand south of Poisson. The author is unproven; see uncertainties.

## entry: Fontinalia and Furina's birthday
@keys: Fontinalia, Fontinalia Festival, Trial or treat, Furina's birthday
@keys2: Fontaine, festival, holiday
@version: 4.0
### STANDARD
The Fontinalia Festival commemorates the Lochknights' search for the Oceanids and Egeria's welcome. Furina's birthday (13 October) is a public holiday, and still is after her abdication. The Fontinalia Film Festival and its Furina Award appeared in a limited-time event (Roses and Muskets).

## entry: Oceanids and Egeria's devotees
@keys: Oceanid, Oceanids, Lochfolk
@keys2: Egeria, Focalors, Hydro Archon
@version: 4.0
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
The Oceanids are beings of pure Hydro who served Egeria and spread across Teyvat. After her death many refused to recognise Focalors and left Fontaine. The descendants of those she made human were made true humans in 4.2.
