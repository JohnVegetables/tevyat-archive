## summary
### LITE
The Rite of Parting is Liyue's ceremonial funeral for a departed adeptus or god, an extreme rarity: only a handful of adepti have gone this way in three thousand years. The Wangsheng Funeral Parlor conducts it, with offerings and tools such as Glaze Lilies, a Cleansing Bell and Everlasting Incense. The one the player sees is held for Rex Lapis in Chapter I, although the "dead" god is alive as Zhongli, who helped plan it.
### STANDARD +
Liyue holds that receiving the adepti and sending them off are equally important. Hu Tao, 77th director of the Parlor, mostly handles mortals' funerals and calls on her colleague Zhongli for adeptus rites. Everything must be flawless: costumes, hour, place, items, weather, length, audience size, and the age, profession and standing of the spectators. Unlike the Rite of Descension, the Rite of Parting commemorates a departure and has no annual form.
### LARGE +
The rite for Rex Lapis was a real ceremony even though the god was alive: it marked the end of his reign and fixed the public narrative that his soul returned to the heavens after a trial. Ningguang called it the end of the contract and of an era, and the Qixing pledged to rebuild contracts for a new age.
### ULTRA +
Because the old texts are the only record, the rite's preparation in Chapter I turns up tied-in history: the Cleansing Bell was made by the goddess Guizhong and has been used at earlier rites; Everlasting Incense was bought at the price of 2,990,000 Mora; and the ceremonial kites stand for the Seven. Zhongli's contract with the Tsaritsa, under which Signora took the Geo Gnosis, was settled around the same time (see uncertainties).

## practice
### STANDARD
Canon lists the main items: Glaze Lilies (an ancient Liyue flower said to turn the land's memories into fragrance), the Cleansing Bell and Everlasting Incense. The bell, a mechanical instrument, rings the deity free of impurities from the mortal world as it is sent off, and it is among the vital implements. Zhongli's preparations in Chapter I add Noctilucous Jade of at least radiant grade, a perfume of Silk Flower matching Rex Lapis's preferred scent, and ceremonial kites. Requirements also include helpers hired to take part.
### LARGE +
At the Chapter I rite, held before the altar near Yiyan Temple in Liyue Harbor after a date was fixed, attendees listened to a Millelith herald read the Qixing's announcement and then to speeches by Ningguang and Keqing, with Ganyu introducing the speakers. Keqing publicly offered the Traveler any reward; the Traveler asked for help with missing-person posters.
### ULTRA +
Madame Ping said that in Liyue's history departures of adepti had always been honored with the rite before any other matter, and she lent the bell willingly. Cloud Retainer explains that the bell, one of Guizhong's proudest creations, can compose and perform, and that Madame Ping (Streetward Rambler) did not take it to play funeral tunes; each time she rang it, she played the melody Guizhong had composed.

## regional_variation
### STANDARD
This is a Liyue custom for adepti and gods. Mortal funerals are handled separately by the Wangsheng Funeral Parlor, and rites for human dead differ from rites for adepti. The Parlor also handles the practical side of laying Liyue Harbor's dead to rest.
### LARGE +
Hu Tao's family business is mainly the sending-off of mortals; this rite is the exception her colleague Zhongli helps with.

## scene_use
### STANDARD
Treat it as a precise, solemn spectacle from a nearly lost tradition. A character involved in preparation is fussy about details; a character who knows the truth about Rex Lapis finds the pageantry bittersweet, while others mourn sincerely.
### LARGE +
Zhongli says he laid down a burden of 3,700 years and treats the rite as an opportunity to experience Liyue's age-old traditions in mortal form. Do not let characters who lack that secret treat the ceremony as a prank.

## examples
### STANDARD
- The Fond Farewell: a date is fixed after the crisis, Fengyan reads the announcement, Ningguang and Keqing speak, and Zhongli says that there is no journey that does not end.
- The Realm Within and Guizhong: the Traveler borrows the Cleansing Bell from Madame Ping and helps buy Everlasting Incense and kites.

## entry: Cleansing Bell
@keys: Cleansing Bell
@version: 1.0
### STANDARD
A bell prepared specifically for adepti, whose sound lingers and is said to purify the mortal realm. Guizhong made it, and after her death Rex Lapis gave it to Madame Ping for safekeeping. It was used on several rites of parting. Zhongli sent the Traveler to borrow it from her for the Chapter I rite.

## entry: Everlasting Incense
@keys: Everlasting Incense
@version: 1.0
### STANDARD
Traditional incense fit for the Rite of Parting, bought from Bubu Pharmacy (through Baizhu) after a drawn-out negotiation for 2,990,000 Mora. Its item text hints that its value is understood only by those who understand it. The Traveler remarks that it is a great deal of Mora to go up in smoke.

## entry: Wangsheng Funeral Parlor and adeptus rites
@keys: Rite of Parting, send-off of adepti, rites of parting
@keys2: Wangsheng Funeral Parlor, Hu Tao, Zhongli
@version: 1.0
### STANDARD
The Parlor has run funerals in Liyue for 77 generations of the Hu family. Hu Tao, the present director, usually relies on Zhongli for the rare rites of adepti, which are performed from texts. Zhongli is a consultant there and organized the Chapter I rite.
