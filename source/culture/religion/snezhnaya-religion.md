## summary
### LITE
Snezhnaya has no known church. Its devotion is aimed at one living person, the Tsaritsa (Anastasya Feodorovna Snezhnaya), Cryo Archon and God of Love, who also rules the nation directly. Loyalists address her with sacral titles ("Holy One of All Winters", "Sovereign of Grace and Absolution") and say that all in Snezhnaya is offered to her throne. Older loyalties survive: the Pale Star of the Belyi Tsar's court, and fae saints and songs. The Tsaritsa herself rejects the "love" her divine office is supposed to carry. Nothing here is a priesthood or a holy calendar; do not invent one.
### STANDARD +
Faith and politics are the same thing in Snezhnaya. The Fatui, the Druzhna and the nobles serve "Her Majesty" in devotional language, while the Tsaritsa's own stance, as the wiki records it, is closer to refusal than to being worshipped. Her predecessor, the Belyi Tsar, was also God of Love and led the Pale Star Court. The Fatui's Palestar Banner and the Palestar Edict (issued, then rescinded, during the Nod-Krai crisis) have names that echo it; the wiki does not state that they honour him.
### LARGE +
Several layers coexist: loyalist devotion to the living Tsaritsa, nostalgia and gratitude among some fae for the Belyi Tsar, Vila reverence for Saint Strivozha, and the separate moon-and-ancestor faith of Nod-Krai. Ordinary Snezhnayans are more often described through loyalty, debt and survival than through prayer.
### ULTRA +
A careful speaker keeps apart three things: the devotional language people use about the Tsaritsa, what she actually thinks of divinity and love (stated in several quests, with the Consultant scene in 7.1), and the Khaenri'ah-related truths of her past with Saarelainen, which the Heavenly Principles made her forget. Most Snezhnayans do not know the last two.

## practice
### LITE
Devotion is mostly spoken and civic. People refer to "Her Majesty" with reverence, the Fatui exist to carry out her will, and the Statues of the Seven in Snezhnaya show the Cryo Archon. No regular rites, clergy or temples for her are known.
### STANDARD +
- Titles. The dedication of the General History of Snezhnaya addresses her as Tsaritsa of All Snezhnaya, Eternal Sovereign Under the Pale Star, Protectress of the Fae Kindreds, Holy One of All Winters, Sovereign of Grace and Absolution and Devoted Bestower of Boundless Grace. This is courtly writing, not a liturgy.
- Offering to the throne. A Snezhnaya loading-screen tip says that in Snezhnaya all shall be offered unto the throne of Her Majesty the Tsaritsa.
- Loyalist speech. The Druzhna officer Akvilina talks of Her Majesty's glory and boundless grace and calls fae bandits traitors. The councillor Golitsyn says he does his duty "in Her Majesty's name", closes with "May Her Majesty's glory endure", and calls fae and humans fuel for Her Majesty's flame.
### LARGE +
- Statues of the Seven. The Snezhnayan statues are described as massive, said to be sculpted in the likeness of the Cryo Archon, with an inscription on the harsh winter and on grace that is given to those chosen and earned by the recipient.
- Devotion abroad. Snezhnayans sent overseas, even those who have grown fond of their posting, say they would never betray the Tsaritsa.
- Funeral at the Sanctuary of Grief. After the Harbinger Signora died in Inazuma, the Tsaritsa held a funeral for her at the Sanctuary of Grief, a domed sanctuary in the Everfrozen Earth. After the Harbingers paid their respects (all but the missing Scaramouche) she froze the tomb and the whole mausoleum in thick ice. The wiki notes the building's resemblance to Neo-Byzantine church architecture; that is an observation, not a stated religious function.
- Korolevskiy Theater. A loading-screen tip calls the Korolevskiy Theater a "virtual site of pilgrimage" and holy land for singers and dancers. This is metaphor for artists' devotion, not a shrine of the Archon.
- Oath-like exclamations. Serialised fiction in the Snezhnaya Vestnik has characters say "may Her Majesty preserve my soul" and "His Majesty preserve us".
- Nation of the pinkie promise. Betrayal and unpaid debt are treated as personal affronts, and the nursery rhyme about a broken pinkie promise being thrown on the ice shows a moral code more than a creed.
### ULTRA +
- Devotion by group: the Fatui are the instrument of her will and the Harbingers are appointed by her; the Druzhna are personal guards who answer only to her; the Veche (a council of interest groups) was set up by her to replace the fae aristocracy; officials such as Akvilina and Golitsyn speak in loyalist terms.
- Krsnik Noc is a Snezhnayan festival known only by name from an NPC during the Lantern Rite; nothing of its meaning is stated.

## the_tsaritsa_and_love
@version: 7.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The Tsaritsa holds the office of God of Love, as every Cryo Archon has, but she refuses to identify with it. The wiki records (citing the Light and Shadow quest) that the Heavenly Principles laid a curse on the gods requiring them to love humanity, and that she regards any love she feels as false and coerced. In 7.1 she told the Traveler that she needed to understand the nation she is "supposed to love", and appointed them Consultant to the Tsaritsa because she could not be seen to favour any minister. Subordinates such as Vesna and Danica insist she is more sincerely kind than she believes.
### LARGE +
Dainsleif's chapter preview describes her as a god with no love left for her people, and no love left for her among them, whose followers hope only to be on her side when she rebels against the divine. Loading screens say many have entrusted their wishes to her in the hope she will raise the banner of rebellion against the heavens. Her aim, the overthrow of the Heavenly Principles, is covered by lore.tsaritsas-plan.
### ULTRA +
Wanderer treats her "love" as another kind of sin. Some Chinese and Japanese item names play on homophones between "grief" and "love"; this is a wiki translation note, not an in-world statement.

## history
@version: 7.0
### LARGE
The first Cryo Archon was the Belyi Tsar (personal name Monomakh Snezhnyi), God of Love, who led the Snowland Fae from the Pale Star Court and was Cryo Archon from about 2,000 years ago until 500 years ago. He founded Kitezh, the first city, defeated King Miscislav, brought House Strivozha and the Vila under his banner, and later moved his capital to the new Snezhnograd and pardoned humanity. He died in the Cataclysm 500 years ago. The Tsaritsa, who had been a herald of his court, rose to power in its aftermath, demanded that fae princes give up their titles (many died before answering), founded the Snezhnograd Veche and had the Fatui, which she established, build Glupov. Fae lost their monopoly over politics.
### ULTRA +
Before this, Anastasya lived in the unified civilization and, according to the accounts, loved the Third Descender Saarelainen. The Belyi Tsar incited them to open a gate, the Heavenly Principles erased her existence as punishment, and Saarelainen ascended to Celestia to secure her return and protect their descendants, the Khaenri'ahns. Her memory of him was removed. The Khaenri'ahns remember her as "Mother of Khaenri'ah". Details come from Black Feathers Scattered at Parting and from Hymns of the Far North, whose account the wiki flags as unreliable.

## regional_variation
### STANDARD
- Fae. The Snowland Fae were the aristocracy of the Belyi Tsar. Many now work ordinary jobs, some regret the old order, and loyalist fae like Akvilina denounce those who do. Vila tradition honours Saint Strivozha, "The Pure One", to whom the Songbook quoted in the General History is attributed (it tells the legend of the King of Light, his daughter Koitar and the King of Hyperborea).
- Nod-Krai. This autonomous borderland keeps a different faith centred on the moons and the Moon Maiden, with Moonchanters and the Frostmoon Scions on Hiisi Island. It is covered in culture.nod-krai-religion.
### LARGE +
Hymns of the Far North, a hymnbook of the Frostmoon Scions compiled by the priestess Ehrnrooth, contains prayer songs for several figures, including the stranger Saarelainen and the dawnstar goddess Koitar. It is a Nod-Krai text, not a Snezhnayan state creed.
### ULTRA +
The "Pale Star" of the old court is echoed in the names Palestar Banner and Palestar Edict. Whether anyone venerates it as a sacred symbol is not stated.

## scene_use
### LITE
Treat Snezhnayan faith as loyalty with a halo. A character may swear by Her Majesty, say "May Her Majesty's glory endure", or keep silent about doubts. Never put a church, bishop or hymn in a Snezhnayan town.
### STANDARD +
- Druzhna officers and Veche officials talk about the Tsaritsa with reverence.
- A fae character may mention the Belyi Tsar fondly or bitterly; a Vila may mention Saint Strivozha.
### LARGE +
Keep knowledge consistent with position. A noble, a Fatui family member, a fae outcast and a Nod-Krai Lightkeeper will not share a view of the Tsaritsa's divinity. Do not give ordinary citizens knowledge of her love-curse or of Saarelainen.
### ULTRA +
Never claim the Tsaritsa wants worship; canon shows her disclaiming the love-based divinity of her office.

## entry: The Tsaritsa as Cryo Archon
@keys: Tsaritsa, Cryo Archon, Anastasya, Her Majesty
@keys2: Snezhnaya, Snezhnayans, worship, faith
@version: 7.0
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Anastasya Feodorovna Snezhnaya, divine name Vassaga, is the current Cryo Archon and God of Love. She rules Snezhnaya directly from Zapolyarny Palace through Edicts. The Fatui carry out her will, and citizens speak of her in devotional titles. She rejects the divine love she was supposed to feel.
### LARGE +
Her regnal title in the General History of Snezhnaya includes "Holy One of All Winters" and "Sovereign of Grace and Absolution".

## entry: The Belyi Tsar and the Pale Star
@keys: Belyi Tsar, Pale Star, Palestar, Monomakh Snezhnyi
@keys2: Snezhnaya, fae, Cryo Archon
@version: 7.0
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
The Belyi Tsar was the first Cryo Archon and God of Love, White Emperor of the Pale Star Court. He united the fae, founded Kitezh and died in the Cataclysm. His Pale Star Court is echoed in the names of the Palestar Banner and Palestar Edict. Some fae remember him as the ruler who granted them amnesty to live with humans.

## entry: Saint Strivozha
@keys: Saint Strivozha, Vaselisa Strivozha, The Pure One, Songbook of Saint Strivozha
@keys2: Vila, fae, House Strivozha
@version: 7.0
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Vaselisa von Strivozha, called Saint Strivozha and "The Pure One", led the Vila of House Strivozha after Hyperborea fell and later swore to the Belyi Tsar after losing a duel. The Songbook ascribed to her preserves a legend of the King of Light and his daughter Koitar. The General History of Snezhnaya notes that her true deeds are uncertain.

## entry: Sanctuary of Grief
@keys: Sanctuary of Grief, Signora's tomb, Signora's funeral
@keys2: Snezhnaya, Tsaritsa, Harbingers
@version: 7.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The Sanctuary of Grief is the tomb of the Harbinger Signora, in the Everfrozen Earth. After her death in Inazuma the Tsaritsa held a funeral there, played chess with Pierro using the Anemo and Geo Gnoses, and froze the tomb in thick ice once the Harbingers had paid respects. Its domed architecture resembles Neo-Byzantine churches.
