## summary
### LITE
Faith is visible in daily Inazuma life. The Shogunate islands worship the Raiden Shogun (the Electro Archon) through the Grand Narukami Shrine and the Sacred Sakura; Watatsumi worships the serpent god Orobashi; Tsurumi's Thunderbird cult died out long ago.
### STANDARD +
Shrine maidens, torii gates, fortune slips and seasonal rites are everyday sights. The faith is tied to politics: the Shogunate's decrees, the Vision Hunt and the Sakoku, made worship of the Shogun and loyalty to the state hard to separate, while Watatsumi's separate devotion fed the island's quarrel with the Shogun. Since the decrees were lifted in the Archon Quest, shrines have kept their ordinary duties; the practices below describe the state of things as of 7.1.
### LARGE +
Three different relationships with the divine coexist in the archipelago. The Shogunate islands venerate a living Archon whose protection is symbolised by a tree. Watatsumi honours a god that Ei killed, and treats it as a guardian because it taught its ancestors farming and smelting after finding them in the Dark Sea. Tsurumi's tribe fed a being that never asked for worship. Canon leaves the second and third cases unflattering to the gods concerned.

## practice
### LITE
The Grand Narukami Shrine, Inazuma's largest, crowns Mt. Yougou beneath the Sacred Sakura and is dedicated to the Raiden Shogun. Yae Miko leads it. Visitors pray and draw fortune slips.
### STANDARD +
The shrine is outside the Shogunate's jurisdiction and answers to the Electro Archon; military force near it is not permitted because it would dishonour the Shogun. Its duties include receiving worshippers, guarding the Sacred Sakura and the Thunder Sakura, and holding rituals and ceremonies, above all the Sacred Sakura Cleansing Ritual. The sakura is read as the Archon's watch over Inazuma. Its branches, the Thunder Sakura, absorb filth into the roots, and because the roots cannot purge themselves the filth must be cleansed at intervals. A five-petalled sakura flower is the shrine's emblem and appears on Yae Miko's clothing. Most shrine maidens in Inazuma (not Watatsumi) are trained at the Grand Narukami Shrine, and tending local shrines keeps them aligned with it; the Kuki Clan has long supplied shrine maidens there. While the Sakoku was in force, a shrine maiden recounted that many visitors came to pray for it to end because it had hurt their livelihoods. The shrine maiden's outfit is a kosode with hakama, white socks and okobo sandals, with red hakama on Narukami Island.
### LARGE +
Fortune slips: a visitor shakes a bamboo slip from the box and exchanges it with the shrine maiden Gendou Ringo for a fortune slip; one per day, and the previous day's slip loses meaning. Ill-omened slips (Misfortune, Great Misfortune) may be tied to a hanger as a prayer for a turnaround; the item text adds that fate is best forged in one's own hands.

Goshintou Ceremony: a solemn event honouring the arrival of the Shogun, said to have brought swordsmithing to Inazuma, at which master smiths offer their finest blades (goshintou) to the Grand Narukami Shrine (book Treasured Tales of the Chouken Shinkageuchi).

Warrior's Spirit Swordfighting Exhibition: in the past champions faced the Shogun herself with an "Emissary of the Archon"; today she sends an emissary instead (most recently Kamisato Ayaka). It is a martial rather than priestly event, linked to the Archon's honour.

Hyakkiyakou: the "night parade of a hundred demons", once a gathering of youkai organised by the Kitsune Saiguu that grew from a storytelling party; it had not been held for centuries until Yae Miko held a farewell version in The Great Narukami Offering (see its entry below).
### ULTRA +
History of the shrine: built before the Archon War, with the kitsune Hakushin as first attendant. About 500 years ago it was led by the Kitsune Saiguu and the Great Tengu, chieftain of the Yougou tengu; the priestess Asase Hibiki dwelt there for a time and took over after Saiguu's death, before returning to the Asase Shrine during Ako Domeki's rebellion. Yae Miko now oversees it. The wiki's member list includes the shrine maidens Inagi Hotomi, Kano Nana, Maki and Miyuki, and names Kuki Shinobu as a former member.

Doctrine is not systematised in any text: Inazuman faith appears as inherited practice, local custom and ritual duty rather than scripture. Do not invent creeds, prayers or commandments.

## regional_variation
### LITE
Shogunate islands: Shogun and Sacred Sakura. Watatsumi Island: Orobashi, the Watatsumi Omikami. Tsurumi Island: the ancient Thunderbird cult, now extinct.
### STANDARD +
Watatsumi: the island was created by Orobashi after the Archon War, its giant corals are said to be branches broken from Orobashi's body, and the people of Enkanomiya who climbed them to the surface are the ancestors of the Watatsumi people. They worship Orobashi instead of the Raiden Shogun, under the Sangonomiya Clan. Orobashi attacked the archipelago, which tradition ties to the island's scarcity of resources, though the deeper cause given is that the Heavenly Principles had condemned it for learning forbidden knowledge; Ei killed it on Yashiori Island about 2,000 years ago, and the Sangonomiya Clan sought peace. Ei allowed Watatsumi to keep honouring it. Shrine maidens there are trained at the Sangonomiya Shrine and wear blue rather than red. Sangonomiya Kokomi is the Divine Priestess and also leads the Watatsumi Army.
### LARGE +
Orobashi's legacy: on Yashiori Island its slaying left the Musoujin Gorge and its remains, and its lingering will and power became the Tatarigami, a curse that causes storms, illness and madness when the wards over it fail. This is why worship of Orobashi is not purely benign in canon: the same god is a guardian to Watatsumi and a calamity on Yashiori.

Watatsumi Goryou Matsuri (Mitama Omatsuri): a ritual that holds off the "Holy Soil" bleaching and erosion of Watatsumi's land (which legend attributes to the depths of Enkanomiya drawing on the island's spirit), requiring a Jade Coral Branch from Enkanomiya, itself made from Orobashi's body. After the Three Realms Gateway Offering, Kokomi decided to look for a way to avoid disturbing the vishaps and ease the need for it.

Under the Vision Hunt Decree hostility rose between the Sangonomiya Clan and the Shogun's followers, particularly the Tenryou Commission; after it was abolished, talks on returning the island to Shogunate rule began and then stalled when the Fatui's influence on the Shogun's Army came to light.
### ULTRA +
Tsurumi: a tribe on Tsurumi Island, cut off by dense fog, believed that leaving meant certain death. They treated Kanna Kapatcir, the Thunderbird, as a guardian deity: they read her actions as revelations, chose shamans, offered blood sacrifices for protection, and used an amethyst hourglass to predict her arrival, holding a festival then. The young Ruu, who had befriended her through song and named her, offered himself as a sacrifice to bring his departing friends back, and the priest Mata also thought him favoured. When she found his blood in the goblet she saw murder and a broken promise, destroyed the tribe and cursed the island to replay its end. She was later killed by Ei, and the remnants of her power became the Thunder Manifestation. The Traveler's quest The Sun-Wheel and Mt. Kanna led Ruu to sing to her remains on Seirai Island, ending the curse. The people used Ishine Script and made the Maushiro instrument from her feathers. The item text on the matter says the Thunderbird "cared not for the worship of mankind".

## scene_use
### LITE
Shrines give atmosphere: torii, red-clad maidens, fortune slips. Watatsumi feels different: blue-clad maidens, coral, reverence for Orobashi.
### STANDARD +
Watatsumi's feeling toward the Shogunate's old rule has softened but not vanished. Characters from the Shogunate islands usually speak of the Shogun with respect and distance; Watatsumi characters do not credit the Shogun with their protection. Do not turn Orobashi worship into villainy: locals revere it, while the Tatarigami suffering on Yashiori is a separate consequence. Fortune slips are a light, repeatable scene beat; the Cleansing Ritual is a heavier one involving the Grand Narukami Shrine and Hanachirusato.
### LARGE +
Avoid giving the Raiden Shogun a modern-day sermon or a miraculous response to prayer; the shrine's daily business is run by Yae Miko. Ei's own view of her godhood and of Eternity is covered in her character entries, so keep this entry to the practice of worship.

## entry: Grand Narukami Shrine Worship
@keys: Grand Narukami Shrine, Narukami Ogosho, Mt. Yougou
@version: 2.0
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
The Grand Narukami Shrine at the summit of Mt. Yougou is Inazuma's largest shrine, dedicated to the Raiden Shogun as the Almighty Narukami Ogosho. Yae Miko oversees it; worshippers pray, draw fortune slips and seek the Archon's blessing. It is independent of the Shogunate's jurisdiction and answers only to the Electro Archon. Shrine maidens from across the Shogunate islands are trained there. See the faction entry for its people.
### LARGE +
Its history reaches before the Archon War; its first attendant was the kitsune Hakushin, and about 500 years ago it was led by the Kitsune Saiguu and the Great Tengu.

## entry: Sacred Sakura
@keys: Sacred Sakura, Thunder Sakura, Giant Thunder Sakura, Primeval Sakura, Crimson Sakura
@version: 2.0
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
A huge sakura on Mt. Yougou that purifies Inazuma's ley lines and keeps Abyssal corruption out. Sakura Bloom petals across Narukami Island fall from it, and its protection is said to extend wherever they rest. Its roots interconnect all of Inazuma's sakura into one organism; the Thunder Sakura (side branches) absorb filth that must be cleansed in the Sacred Sakura Cleansing Ritual. Locals once believed it cleansed the land of impurity.
### LARGE +
According to Ei, the tree began from a seed Makoto gave her in a realm of consciousness, and took root in the past; Ei came home from Khaenri'ah to find it already standing on Mt. Yougou and everyone saying it had always been there. Yae Miko's altered memory kept Ei from convincing her. The tree has probably fused its roots with the Irminsul's (a domain description), which would explain its glow. Its fox-like form is attributed by Yae Miko to centuries of care by kitsune. Origin is disputed in the uncertainties.

## entry: Sacred Sakura Cleansing Ritual
@keys: Sacred Sakura Cleansing Ritual, Cleansing Ritual, Hanachirusato, Miasmic Tumor
@version: 2.0
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
Ordinarily the Grand Narukami Shrine's job. In the world quest series the Traveler and the masked shrine maiden Hanachirusato lift five barriers and purge the filth in each, then destroy a tumor under the sakura. Hanachirusato is revealed to be filth condensed from the Kitsune Saiguu's memory, and she disperses with the cleansing. Without cleansing, the interconnected roots corrode and form tumors.

## entry: Watatsumi Omikami and Sangonomiya Faith
@keys: Watatsumi Omikami, Orobashi, Sangonomiya Shrine, Divine Priestess
@version: 2.1
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
Watatsumi worships Orobashi, the serpent god it calls the Watatsumi Omikami, as its guardian because it taught the first islanders to farm and smelt. Sangonomiya Kokomi serves as Divine Priestess. Shrine maidens train at the Sangonomiya Shrine and wear blue. The shrine now standing was built after Orobashi's death. Orobashi died around 2,000 years ago, slain by Ei.
### LARGE +
The Goryou Matsuri holds off the erosion of Watatsumi's soil using a Jade Coral Branch made from Orobashi's body.

## entry: Tatarigami
@keys: Tatarigami, Curse God
@version: 2.2
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
The curse born from Orobashi's will and power after its death on Yashiori Island. With the wards broken it brings perpetual storms, withered crops, mysterious illness and hallucinations. Vision-bearers and those with elemental sensitivity are somewhat protected. Its victims often speak of a terrifying god called "Him".

## entry: Thunderbird Cult of Tsurumi
@keys: Kanna Kapatcir, Thunderbird, Tsurumi tribe, Thunderbird Civilization, Ruu
@version: 2.8
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
Tsurumi Island's tribe worshipped Kanna Kapatcir with blood sacrifices. After the youth Ruu was sacrificed, she destroyed the tribe in rage and cursed the island to repeat the event. Ei later killed her. The curse was broken in the Traveler's Tsurumi quests (The Sun-Wheel and Mt. Kanna) when Ruu sang to her remains.
### LARGE +
The tribe used Ishine Script and a Maushiro made of her feathers. The wiki names Kama as the sole living descendant; check the quest text before using him.

## entry: Hyakkiyakou and the Kitsune Saiguu
@keys: Hyakkiyakou, night parade, Kitsune Saiguu
@version: 2.1
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
This is event material (The Great Narukami Offering). Yae Miko holds a last Hyakkiyakou, a spirit-sending ritual for youkai memories, for her old friend Urakusai and others, after Transient Dreams damaged the ley lines. The Kitsune Saiguu originally organised the gatherings; she died about 500 years ago during the Cataclysm.
