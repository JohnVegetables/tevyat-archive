## summary
### LITE
Fontaine Fashion Week is a large, recurring social event in Fontaine, held once a year, where fashion designers present new work to the public, sponsors and critics. It gives designers wide exposure and can make or break a name. Chiori of Chioriya Boutique owes her fame to an early Fashion Week yet is not fond of the event as a social occasion.
### STANDARD +
Designers are expected to present something new each time, since in Chiori's view customers will not patronise a house that releases nothing. Sponsors, suppliers, models and accessory makers all take part, so a designer's reputation in the weeks before the show matters as much as the clothes. The event is a backdrop in Chiori's Story Quest (4.5), where it is the stage for her rivalry with the designer Uther.
### LARGE +
Canon gives no organiser, venue rules or judging system. What it shows is the ecosystem around the show: newspapers and tabloids that shape opinion, suppliers who may withdraw, booked models, and friends who lend hands.

## practice
@version: 4.5
### STANDARD
Chiori describes Fashion Week as an annual event that is a major opportunity for designers because of the exposure it brings, and says it helped make her success. She regards taking part as the natural duty of a house that wants to stay relevant. Preparation involves finished garments, fabric shipped in (hers comes from Inazuma via Komaniya Express), accessory suppliers and booked models.
### LARGE +
In the 4.5 Story Quest the week is "of prime importance" because many new suppliers intend to take part and catching their attention would help her brand. Uther's smear campaign works by attacking the preparation chain: customers cancel orders, her accessory supplier Wenger withdraws his goods after seeing the planted rumours, and the models booked for her boutique pull out. Chiori refuses Wenger's advice to sit the year out. She replaces accessories with shells and natural finds from the sea, salvages fabric from her own ready-to-wear stock, as she once did at her first Fashion Week, and borrows hands for sewing from Navia's Spina di Rosula.
### ULTRA +
The show itself is staged as a spectacle. In the 4.5 quest's cinematic Navia and Kirara descend to the stage on an open umbrella, followed by Lyney and Lynette, Navia introduces the Chioriya brand to the audience, and Chiori arrives in time to present it and closes by wishing "fellow dreamers" a happy Fashion Week. The Lyney and Lynette appearance is returned kindness: Chiori had made their outfits and sponsored one of their shows.

## scene_use
### STANDARD
Use it as a reason for bustle in Fontaine's fashion circles: designers frantic over deadlines, suppliers and models being courted, gossip, tabloids and rival houses jockeying. Chiori treats it as a business necessity and speaks of it with a mix of professional respect and distaste for the social side. Do not invent official rules, prizes or a fixed venue; keep those vague.
### LARGE +
Good hooks: a designer whose accessory supplier has just withdrawn, a rumour planted in cheap tabloids days before the show, a friend asked to model, or a last-minute improvised collection. Fontaine's papers differ in credibility: the Steambird is trusted, the tabloids in the quest were paid to run the same story.

## examples
### STANDARD
- Chiori's first Fashion Week made her name; she improvised with fabric from her own Inazuman clothes.
- 4.3 Roses and Muskets (limited event): Chiori says a premiere is still better than Fashion Week, and later says she hopes to see the Traveler and Paimon at the upcoming one, while demand at her boutique has grown.
- 4.5 Cisoria Chapter: the week is where Chiori beats Uther's sabotage.

## entry: Fashion Week in Chiori's Story Quest
@keys: Fontaine Fashion Week, Fashion Week
@keys2: Chiori, Uther, Chioriya Boutique
@version: 4.5
### STANDARD
In the Cisoria Chapter (When They Talk About Tonight, 4.5), Chiori's rival Uther, a former partner who gave up before a past Fashion Week while she persisted, hires thugs against her courier Kirara, plants press smears and pushes her suppliers and models away. With the Traveler, Navia, Lyney, Lynette, Charlotte and Chevreuse she exposes his Fleuve Cendre factory and stages her show anyway. This is the one depiction of the show itself in canon through 7.1 that the research found.

### STANDARD
