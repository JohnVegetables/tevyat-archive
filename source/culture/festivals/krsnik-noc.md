## summary
### LITE
Krsnik Noc is a festival celebrated in Snezhnaya. Canon gives no details of what it involves. The one known remark is from Ivanovich, a Snezhnayan merchant in Liyue Harbor: during Lantern Rite the glow of the Xiao Lanterns reminded him of it.

### STANDARD +
He called it "our Krsnik Noc" and, feeling the same warmth in a foreign city, urged the Traveler to visit Snezhnaya and join one of its festivals. That is the full extent of what canon says. Nothing in the game through Version 7.1, including the Snezhnaya content, states its date, origin, rituals or meaning.

## practice
### STANDARD
No practices are established. The only inference from canon is the mood Ivanovich ties to it: lit lanterns in the night, and an impression of warmth and communal celebration that reminded him of home. Treat everything else as unwritten.

### LARGE +
Ivanovich's comparison is the sole in-game reference, made during the Lantern Rite event, which began in Version 1.3. That is why the festival is dated to 1.3 here. Its Chinese name translates as the Ice Hearth Festival, and the French and Russian names also say "icy furnace" or "ice hearth". Treat these as clues to naming only, not as evidence of what the festival does.

### ULTRA +
Most Snezhnayan cities contain large Kresnik's Torches, heat sources that keep out the cold, and the wiki notes the similar-sounding name. Canon never says the festival relates to them. The English spelling "Krsnik" is not a direct rendering of the Chinese name, and some languages (Turkish, Italian) use "Kresnik" instead.

## regional_variation
### LARGE
Canon records no regional variation, and nothing places the festival in a particular Snezhnayan city. It is described only as Snezhnayan. No Nod-Krai (6.x) or 7.0-7.1 source found describes it either.

## scene_use
### STANDARD
Use it lightly. A Snezhnayan character can mention it with homesick fondness, comparing lanterns, firelight or a warm crowd on a cold night to it, as Ivanovich does. Keep details vague or let the speaker be vague. Do not invent a canonical date, ritual, food, song or origin story and present it as fact.

### LARGE +
If a scene needs specifics, make it clear the speaker is describing their own family or town custom, not a universal rule. Characters who are not Snezhnayan should know it only by name or by what a traveller told them.

## examples
### ULTRA
Ivanovich's remark during Lantern Rite in Liyue Harbor is the only canonical example: a merchant far from home, warmed by the Xiao Lanterns, who compares the night to Krsnik Noc and invites the Traveler to see Snezhnaya's festivals for themselves. His other festival-time idle lines are about selling Xiao Lanterns in Snezhnaya, not about Krsnik Noc.
