## summary
### LITE
The Sabzeruz Festival is Sumeru's festival for the birth of Lesser Lord Kusanali (Nahida), counted as the day the sages found her. It is said to have begun as a birthday party for her predecessor, Greater Lord Rukkhadevata, thrown by the Goddess of Flowers, and it became Kusanali's festival after Rukkhadevata died. Its heart is the Dance of Sabzeruz, based on the Goddess of Flowers' own dance. Since Rukkhadevata's death the Akademiya has stopped organising it and treats it as almost non-existent, so it survives through ordinary people, above all around the Grand Bazaar.
### STANDARD +
Other traditions are the Haft-Mewa Feast, Yalda Candies, flower carriages and Farris, the Knight of Flowers, a children's favourite. The festival dimmed under the Akademiya's neglect, but after Kusanali's return it regained standing, and the 5.1 event shows a grand birthday celebration for her.
### LARGE +
Keep the festival's tone warm, a little wistful and deliberately unofficial: it is a people's festival, loved by Kusanali's followers and ignored by the scholarly establishment.

## practice
### LITE
Celebrants hold the Dance of Sabzeruz, set out a Haft-Mewa Feast, hand out Yalda Candies and (in the old days) watch flower carriages parade. Volunteers run the stalls.
### STANDARD +
- The Dance of Sabzeruz: the festival's most important performance, based on the Goddess of Flowers' dance. In the Archon Quest Nilou of the Zubayr Theater is to perform it, and the older Bazaar dancer Shrimati says she danced it as a child.
- The Haft-Mewa Feast: tables are set with seven different foods, which Dunyarzad says stand for the seven virtues of the Dendro Archon. The stall in the Archon Quest sells only mushrooms, flowers and fruit, and its vendor Amal says few people today know the custom.
- Yalda Candies: a festival staple, sold in numbered boxes that each hold a random flavour. Lavender Melon, Harra Fruit and Sunsettia are pleasant; Lizard Tail and Onikabuto are the dreaded ones.
- Farris, the Knight of Flowers: a figure popular with children, played by an actor during the festival. In the past the actor also sat on a flower carriage.
### LARGE +
- The Carriage Parade: Dunyarzad says large flower carriages once rolled through the city towards Port Ormos while people threw flowers, candy and liquor.
- Volunteer spirit: in the Archon Quest vendors cover their own costs, saying a lively atmosphere matters more than money. Dunyarzad privately funded the Grand Bazaar's stage.
### ULTRA +
Vihar, who plays Farris in the Archon Quest, gives candy to the children with parade-ground patter, calling them soldiers and telling them to stay loyal to Lesser Lord Kusanali. A child nearby, Kumari, says Farris handing out candy is the best part.

## origins
### LITE
The festival is attributed to the Goddess of Flowers, a friend of Greater Lord Rukkhadevata, who is said to have first held it for Rukkhadevata's birthday. Its present meaning is the birth of Lesser Lord Kusanali.
### STANDARD +
Nilou's version of the legend: at a banquet thrown by Rukkhadevata's friends, some gods got drunk, one began playing music, Rukkhadevata sang, and the Goddess of Flowers danced. Padisarah flowers bloomed wherever she stepped. That dance is the Dance of Sabzeruz, and the banquet is called the festival's origin.
### LARGE +
A second legend, mentioned in the limited 5.1 event (Give Her Memories), has King Deshret and Nabu Malikata planning a secret birthday for Rukkhadevata. They lured her with a trail of candies, King Deshret played an instrument and Lady Malikata danced. Rukkhadevata received the Khvarena from Malikata and the Wedjat Eye from Deshret. Neither legend is confirmed.
### ULTRA +
After Rukkhadevata's death the festival turned into a celebration of Lesser Lord Kusanali's birthday, the day the sages found her. Dunyarzad says it was a huge occasion when Kusanali was first welcomed back, and that people later lost interest under the Akademiya's influence. In the Chinese and Japanese versions only, the festival is plainly called the birthday festival of the Goddess of Flowers. Records of the Ancient Oases and a Primal Obelisk text place it in the ancient city of Gurabad.

## the_akademiya_and_the_festival
@version: 3.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The Akademiya takes part in many holidays honouring Greater Lord Rukkhadevata, but has stopped organising Sabzeruz and withholds funding. Dunyarzad guesses the sages see Kusanali's birth as confirmation of Rukkhadevata's death. The festival was not openly forbidden; it was left to survive on its own.
### LARGE +
Inside the dream world of the Archon Quest, Grand Sage Azar and his secretary Setaria shut down Nilou's rehearsal, citing laws against unlicensed performances and private hosting of large religious festivals, and Azar ordered an ordinance banning public art performances before the next Jnagarbha Day. The scene belongs to the dream, but it expresses the sages' real contempt for the festival and for the arts.
### ULTRA +
After Nahida's return the position reversed. In the 5.1 event Tighnari says he heard from his master that the Akademiya is planning a grand birthday celebration for the Dendro Archon, perhaps as a small attempt to make amends.

## in_the_archon_quest
@version: 3.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Archon Quest Chapter III Act II, "The Morn a Thousand Roses Brings," is built around the festival. Dunyarzad invites the Traveler and Paimon, Eremites try to kidnap her, and Azar stops Nilou's dance. Then the morning of the festival starts to repeat. This is a samsara, a collective dream loop in which the Akasha holds its users.
### LARGE +
Nahida, who is Lesser Lord Kusanali herself, helps the Traveler look for the cause. Recorded messages show the Akasha gathers its wisdom by harvesting users' dreams. The loop ends when Nilou realises she is the dream's host and performs the Dance of Sabzeruz as the dreamscape dissolves; Dunyarzad's spirit appears in the crowd, and she is later found alive in the Bimarstan.
### ULTRA +
Dunyarzad's wish to see Nilou's dance is the regret that shows the Traveler how to end the loop. See the quest entries for the full plot.

## depictions
@availability: limited
@version: 5.1
### STANDARD
The 5.1 flagship event Chromatic Ode of Candies and Roses (23 October to 7 November 2024) is set during the festival. Its three acts, "Give Her Surprises," "Give Her Sweetness" and "Give Her Memories," follow a secret birthday party for Nahida prepared by Tighnari, Collei, Dehya, Candace and others, with Kaveh handling specifics.
### LARGE +
The idea came from Dehya, who told Dunyarzad she wanted a change from the usual festival formula. The friends plan candy, a Haft-Mewa Feast and a surprise after the Carriage Parade. In the third act Nilou goes missing on the morning of preparations, which first looks like sabotage tied to a robbery case Cyno and Candace are investigating. The party goes ahead, the crowd calls out happy birthday together, and Nahida later tells the Traveler it felt like a little bird tucked in a nest.
### ULTRA +
The gameplay modes were Rocking Carriage (steering a flower carriage), Interreflection of Reality and Dreams, and Shadow of the Knight's Blade (a battle mode recreating the Knight of Flowers). Candace was the event character. In post-quest scenes Sethos persuades Wanderer to attend the Akademiya party, and Arasaka and Sorush talk at Pardis Dhyai.

## scene_use
### LITE
Use it for Sumeru scenes with stalls, candy boxes, the smell of the Haft-Mewa tables, children lining up for Farris, and a dancer in the Grand Bazaar. It is a good setting for bringing Nahida's followers together.
### STANDARD +
Characters differ about it: Nilou and Dunyarzad treat it as precious, Akademiya scholars dismiss it, and Bazaar traders keep it going.
### LARGE +
Do not state a calendar date. Before Chapter III the Akademiya neglects the festival; afterward it supports it. Keep 5.1 event details out of earlier-dated scenes, since that event is limited.

## entry: Dance of Sabzeruz
@keys: Dance of Sabzeruz, Goddess of Flowers' dance
### STANDARD
The festival's most important performance, based on the Goddess of Flowers' dance at the legendary first banquet, when Padisarah flowers bloomed where she stepped. Nilou is the dancer in the Archon Quest, on the Zubayr Theater stage in the Grand Bazaar. She says she would be thrilled to have even two real Padisarahs on that stage. Shrimati danced it as a child and was taught that both Archons are loved.

## entry: Haft-Mewa Feast
@keys: Haft-Mewa, Haft-Mewa Feast
### STANDARD
A festival meal of seven different foods set on the table, said to stand for the seven virtues of the Dendro Archon. The stall in the Archon Quest sells only mushrooms, flowers and fruit.

## entry: Yalda Candies
@keys: Yalda Candy, Yalda Candies
### STANDARD
Festival candies sold in boxes with a random flavour. Lavender Melon, Harra Fruit and Sunsettia are known flavours, along with the fearsome Lizard Tail and Onikabuto. In the 5.1 event Nahida compares the banquet's dishes to guessing a Yalda Candy's flavour before opening the box.

## entry: Farris, the Knight of Flowers
@keys: Farris, Knight of Flowers
### STANDARD
A festival figure played by an actor and especially popular with children. In the Archon Quest the stall-keeper Vihar plays him with a military-style patter. The performer traditionally rode a flower carriage. The 5.1 event's battle mode recreates the Knight of Flowers.

## entry: Dunyarzad
@keys: Dunyarzad
### STANDARD
A young woman from the Homayani family and the festival's benefactor in the Archon Quest. She gave her savings to the Bazaar's organisers, spent the festival with the Traveler and most looked forward to Nilou's dance. Dehya works as her bodyguard. Her own entry carries her illness and story.
