## summary
### LITE
The Windblume Festival is Mondstadt's once-yearly festival of love and freedom, and Klee calls it the city's biggest celebration. The city is decorated, and people offer Windblumes to the Anemo Archon Barbatos and to anyone they love or admire. Gifts and love poems can go with them, but the flowers are not only romantic. People also use the festival to mend or deepen any relationship. "Windblume" (German for "wind flower") began as a secret code word among the rebels against the tyrant god Decarabian. Today nobody agrees which flower the Windblume really is.
### STANDARD +
Each year one person is named the Windblume Star and offers a Windblume of their choice to Barbatos for all of Mondstadt at the Windblume Ceremony. Custom says everyone should do things for themselves during the festival, so there are far fewer commissions. The Adventurers' Guild has little work to give out.

## practice
### LITE
People offer Windblumes informally at the great statue of Barbatos all through the festival, and give flowers to loved ones. Florists such as Flora have more customers than they can handle.
### STANDARD +
- Do it yourself: Venti says the festival belongs both to the Anemo Archon and to every Mondstadter looking for love, and anyone who wants love must do everything themselves. Many people also use the time to settle matters of the heart. Katheryne and shopkeepers turn away offers of help, and Marjorie of the souvenir shop laughs at the idea.
- The Windblume Star: the Star is someone who has truly proven themselves during the festival or made great sacrifices for Mondstadt. Acting Grand Master Jean announces the choice and attends the ceremony with them.
- Festivities: taverns sell limited-edition wines (Venti names Applebloom Cider and saves his Mora for them). There are games, balloons, music and decorations across the city and at Statues of The Seven such as Windrise, Starfell Lake and the Dawn Winery.
### LARGE +
- Love poems are a festival habit. Wordsmiths can even sell lessons in writing them, as Venti did.
- Cape Oath is a popular date spot.
- The Adventurers' Guild treats the festival as a recruiting opportunity. Branch Master Cyrus sends adventurers to look for traces of the Windblume in Stormterror's Lair, and says the festival should carry on the rebellious spirit of the heroes who resisted Decarabian.

## origins
### LITE
In Old Mondstadt, Decarabian ruled from his tower. People who planned to overthrow him used "Windblume" as their secret sign. The festival grew out of that rebellion; when it was first held is unknown.
### STANDARD +
The original Windblume was a nameless, "floaty" flower from the dreams of Decarabian's people. It was said to thrive in the harshest wind and cold and to root more firmly the harder the wind blew, which is why the rebels made it their symbol. Venti confirms that the word dates from Old Mondstadt and was a code word for keeping in touch and resisting in secret. Few people today remember this meaning.
### LARGE +
The weapon text of Windblume Ode keeps the old greeting in spirit: the rebels offered each other "this nameless flower" and hoped for the day the storms would stop. It names the forgotten red-haired warrior, who turned away from the newborn god after the victory, as the first to pass the sign. It links the Windblume to his descendants, the Ragnvindr Clan, who keep bringing light in dark times. In peacetime, the text says, the flower came to mean love and gladness, which is surely what those rebels wanted. Few people know about the Ragnvindr link.

## the_windblume
@availability: limited
@version: 1.4
### STANDARD
During the limited event Invitation of Windblume (1.4), the city argued about which flower is the "true" Windblume. Most people favour the dandelion; others name the Windwheel Aster, which Mondstadters call "the visible winds."
### LARGE +
The two big camps are the Dandelionites, the most popular, who see the dandelion as the Windblume and the festival as one for lovers, and the Asterists ("Windblume Traditionalists"), who choose the Windwheel Aster and say the festival should remember Old Mondstadt's liberation. The guard Lawrence and the scholar Dr. Edith describe them. Other candidates in that festival included the Cecilia (Beatrice's pick), the Sweet Flower, Small Lamp Grass and the Wolfhook (Razor and Bennett's). Sucrose took an alchemically recreated giant Sweet Flower from antiquity, the "Tetratanic Sweet Flower," as her own.
### ULTRA +
Barbatos will not name any flower. Speaking privately in Stormterror's Lair after the ceremony, Venti told the Traveler that Windblumes "don't exist," yet are everywhere: they are the longing for freedom, and everyone has the right to define their own flowers of blessing, respect and love. In Windblume's Breath (3.5) Collei named two Sumeran flowers, the Padisarah and the Sumeru Rose, as her Windblumes.

## regional_variation
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
In Springvale, young hunters looking for love go into Wolvendom during the festival to catch foxes, then release them with flower wreaths on their heads. The hunter Allan admits he failed to catch one last year and doubts he will this time. Mentioned in event dialogue (1.4, 3.5).

## depictions
@availability: limited
@spoiler: minor
### LARGE
Festivals shown in limited events:
- Invitation of Windblume (1.4): Venti runs a paid love-poem class (Kaeya, Timaeus and Bennett among the students) and the Traveler helps him collect the poems. The Traveler is chosen Windblume Star and offers their chosen flower at the statue with Jean. Afterwards Venti and the Traveler tidy the wind currents of Stormterror's Lair as a gift for Dvalin, who soon flies past.
- Windblume's Breath (3.5): the Knights name Sucrose Windblume Festival Special Ambassador, to do good deeds for people. With the Traveler and Collei, who is visiting from Sumeru, she chases a riddle-slip found in the children's book "The Boar Princess." It leads to the Lantern of Utmost Joy on a floating island above Falcon Coast, a Hexenzirkel device that connects Klee's mother Alice and Scarlett with the finders. The "supreme blessing" turns out to be the joy of being with friends.
### ULTRA +
Other details of the 3.5 festival: Timaeus sends handpicked Windblumes and a letter to Ying'er in Liyue. Klee and Mona also take part in the search, and Alice wishes everyone a happy festival "of love and freedom."

## recent
@version: 5.6
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
The Archon Quest Paralogism (Interlude Chapter Act IV, 5.6) takes place as a new Windblume Festival begins. Under cover of the festival, the Knights stage the trial of Albedo to expose creatures born of Durin's lingering life force, and then defend Mondstadt against their attack on the city. When the festival opens, Jean names Venti the Windblume Star. He sings while Albedo's alchemy turns Mini Durin into a human, reborn in place of the fallen dragon.
### ULTRA +
In the same quest, ordinary townsfolk (Donna, Beatrice, Miles) behave strangely in the festival build-up, and the oddities turn out to be tied to the Durin crisis. As of 7.1, this is the most recent Windblume Festival shown on screen; 6.x and 7.0-7.1 content shows no new one.

## scene_use
### LITE
Use it for light-hearted Mondstadt scenes: decorated streets, flower stalls, music, wine, couples at Cape Oath, people working up courage to give someone flowers. Shopkeepers and the Guild may turn down offers of help, saying people do things themselves at Windblume.
### STANDARD +
Arguments over the "real" Windblume make easy small talk. A traditionalist may say the festival is about freedom from Decarabian rather than romance. Windblumes need not be romantic: friends, family, mentors and the Archon all receive them.
### LARGE +
Mondstadters revere Barbatos at the festival but do not know the bard Venti is their god. Jean naming Venti as Windblume Star in 5.6 does not mean the public knows. Keep the tone affectionate rather than solemn: Windblume Ode notes that the old rebellion is now remembered through festive jests.

## entry: Windblume Star and Windblume Ceremony
@keys: Windblume Star, Windblume Ceremony
### STANDARD
At the Windblume Ceremony, held in the plaza before the great statue of Barbatos, the year's Windblume Star offers a Windblume of their own choosing to the Anemo Archon for all of Mondstadt. The Star is someone who has truly proven themselves during the festival or made great sacrifices for the city. The Acting Grand Master announces the choice and attends the ceremony. Known Stars: the Traveler (shown in the 1.4 event) and Venti (in the 5.6 Archon Quest Paralogism). Many townsfolk wonder aloud who will get the honour.

## entry: Dandelionites and Asterists
@keys: Dandelionite, Dandelionites, Asterist, Asterists, Windblume Traditionalists
@availability: limited
@version: 1.4
### STANDARD
These are the two main schools of thought on the "true" Windblume. Dandelionites, the larger group, say it is the dandelion and that the Windblume Festival is for lovers. Asterists, or Windblume Traditionalists, say it is the Windwheel Aster ("the visible winds") and that the festival should remember Old Mondstadt's liberation from Decarabian. Most people have forgotten that meaning, so the Asterists are a minority. Some people hold neither view. Barbatos refuses to settle the question.

## entry: Lantern of Utmost Joy
@keys: Lantern of Utmost Joy
@keys2: Windblume, Hexenzirkel, Alice, Falcon Coast
@availability: limited
@version: 3.5
### LARGE
During the Windblume Festival of the event Windblume's Breath (3.5), a "prophecy" slip tucked into a children's book sent Sucrose, Collei and the Traveler looking for "a flower not of this world, a guide who never gets lost, one who would never lie and a legend that never ends." The answers lit the Lantern of Utmost Joy on Hexenzirkel's Island in the Sky above Falcon Coast. The witches had left the lantern in Barbatos's care, and lighting it put the group in contact with Alice and Scarlett. The island became permanently accessible after Paralogism (5.6).
