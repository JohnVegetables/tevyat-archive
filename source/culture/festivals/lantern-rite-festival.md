## summary
### LITE
Lantern Rite is Liyue's biggest festival of the year, held over five days around the first full moon of the year and celebrated across the nation, above all in Liyue Harbor. People pray for peace, ward off evil, eat seasonal food and release Xiao Lanterns carrying written wishes into the night sky. On the final night the city releases the Mingxiao Lantern, a huge floating lantern built from donated materials and shaped after a fallen hero, usually an adeptus who died protecting Liyue. According to Zhongli, the custom began in a war long ago as lanterns lit to guide soldiers home; after the Archon War it became a festival of remembrance.
### LARGE +
For the yaksha Xiao the festival is anything but restful: the lingering hatred of gods Morax defeated erupts most violently during Lantern Rite, so he spends the festival fighting unseen outside the city.

## practice
### STANDARD
- **Xiao Lanterns**: small floating lanterns made from a wrapping of lantern fiber, a wick and a shard of Plaustrite, the Liyue mineral that floats once activated. People write wishes on wish sticks, insert them and release the lantern.
- **Mingxiao Lantern**: the festival's centerpiece, a giant lantern built by the whole city and released on the last night.
- **Feasting and gatherings**: families and friends meet over a holiday meal and see out the old year together.
### LARGE +
- **Mingxiao Lantern details** (per the 1.3 festival planner Jiangzhou): everyone who can gives materials or money, and much of the funding comes from market vendors. Its size depends on the largest piece of Plaustrite unearthed in the preceding year, which sits at the lantern's core. Its design honors the adeptus form of a hero who died for Liyue, and Yuehai Pavilion chooses the subject partly by popular demand.
- **Xiao Market**: the festival market of stalls and vendors in Liyue Harbor; Jiangzhou, a planner at the Lantern Rite Gala, lists it among the festival's main traditions alongside the two kinds of lantern.
- **Organisation**: the festival has several planners, because the whole of Liyue Harbor turns out (Jiangzhou was one in 1.3); the Ministry of Civil Affairs handles lantern clean-up, and in later years the Qixing set each festival's theme.
- **Clean-up**: Xiao Lanterns do not simply vanish. Badly made ones break up in the air and leave loose, flying Plaustrite; others snag in trees and corners. The Ministry of Civil Affairs assigns someone, in 1.3 the Millelith soldier Hongyu, to gather fallen lanterns as the festival goes on.
### ULTRA +
- **Prayers to the adepti**: during festival seasons Liyue people burn incense and pray to the adepti of Jueyun Karst for protection. Xiao's profile notes that nobody prays to him, since a demon-slaying yaksha brings no luck or fortune. Hu Tao (6.3) observes that at Lantern Rite adepti and ancestors are venerated alike, and goes to pay respects to her ancestors.
- **Exorcism roots**: Xiao's profile says many human festivals mark days when the adepti defeated man-eating monsters; people imitated those deeds in exorcism rituals that grew into celebrations. The festival's prayers to "ward off evil" echo this.
- **Seasonal food**: in 6.3 Xiangling names Triple-Layered Consommé and "Come and Get It" as Wanmin Restaurant's Lantern Rite specialties.
- **Recent additions**: each year the Qixing add a theme. Fireworks were added in 2.4 and kites (mixing Liyue kite craft with Fontaine's Mechanical Vertical Lifting Device) in 4.4. In 3.4 a concert of the Fontaine musician Dvorak's Iridescence Tour was held, and in 5.3 Yun Jin staged the opera "The Sanctification of Tao Dou".

## history
### STANDARD
Zhongli says that in an ancient war the people of Liyue released lanterns to remind their soldiers of the way home and to never lose sight of themselves, and that few people today remember this. After the Archon War the custom became a way to commemorate those who gave their lives, and grew into a full festival of remembrance and unity.
### LARGE +
Jiangzhou explains the belief that the fire's light "keeps wisdom alive" and guides fallen heroes home along the sea, so they can be reunited with the living. The communal Mingxiao Lantern has been built since peace was restored, to keep alive the spirit of unity.
### ULTRA +
Xiao's character story ties the festival to the gods Morax defeated: their hatred and leftover power, half-awake and half-dreaming, sometimes erupt with abnormal force, and the eruption during Lantern Rite is the largest. Xiao must perform the "Nuo Dance of Evil Conquering" and fights day and night through the festival, so he has come to hate it, though he feels something he cannot name (loneliness, peace or fear for the future) when the lantern-light shines over the sea.

## regional_variation
### LARGE
Liyue Harbor is the heart of the festival: the Lantern Rite Gala, decorated streets and stalls up Feiyun Slope, the Mingxiao Lantern at the harbor and fireworks or kites over the water. Smaller celebrations happen elsewhere: Qingce Village takes part (one 1.3 request is titled "Qingce's Lanterns"), and in 1.3 the staff of Wangshu Inn brought the festival to the inn for Xiao. The adepti mark it in their own way: in 2.4 Keqing delivered festive gifts to Cloud Retainer, Madame Ping and Xiao, and the adepti appear watching the fireworks.
### ULTRA +
Visitors join in. Mondstadters visiting Liyue appear in the 1.3 festival requests, and foreign guests feature in later years: Dvorak of Fontaine in 3.4, Charlotte, Navia, Clorinde, Furina and Neuvillette around 4.4. In 6.3 Yanfei compares Liyue's moon legends with records she found among Nod-Krai's Frostmoon Scions.

## scene_use
### STANDARD
Use Lantern Rite for a warm, crowded, festive Liyue scene: lantern-lit streets, crowded stalls, the smell of festival dishes, people writing wishes on wish sticks, and the moment when the whole harbor looks up as the Mingxiao Lantern rises. It suits reunions, confessions of wishes, family meals and seeing out the old year. A quieter angle is grief and remembrance: the lanterns were meant to guide the dead home.
### LARGE +
Practical notes for roleplay:
- Typical greeting: "Happy Lantern Rite!"
- Liyue characters treat it as the year's big holiday; officials (the Qixing, the Ministry of Civil Affairs, the Millelith) are swamped with work.
- Xiao avoids crowds and is usually absent, fighting outside the city; drawing him into the festival is a recurring kindness his friends attempt, and he watches from a distance at most.
- The adepti are honored, but the festival is run by humans; Keqing notes in 4.4 that with Liyue now ruled by people, the Qixing update the festival to keep up with the times.
- Don't invent a specific Mingxiao Lantern subject for a year canon has not named.

## examples

### LARGE
Lantern Rites the Traveler has attended (each was a limited-time event; dates are release versions):
- **1.3**: the Mingxiao Lantern honored Skybracer. A would-be thief's absurd plan to steal its Plaustrite was foiled, and the Wangshu Inn staff brought the festival to Xiao, who watched the lantern rise from outside the city.
- **2.4**: fireworks joined the festival under Ningguang and Keqing; Keqing, overworked, was coaxed into resting and watching the fireworks.
- **3.4**: the Mingxiao Lantern was modeled on Sea Gazer; a Lantern Rite concert was held with Dvorak.
- **4.4**: a kite theme; the Mingxiao Lantern depicted the yaksha Menogias.
- **5.3**: the festival coincided with a crisis of fetor (death energy) around Wuwang Hill.
- **6.3**: the Mingxiao Lantern honored the White Horse Adeptus.
### ULTRA +
- **1.3 details**: the 1.3 Lantern's Plaustrite was the largest found in twenty years. Skybracer, by legend, had his antlers cut off to prop up the collapsing Mt. Tianheng and kept fighting until he died. The Lantern Rite Tales requests that year included Hongyu's lantern clean-up.
- **2.4 details**: Ningguang had fireworks added after the year's upheavals; stolen firework shipments to Qingce Village were traced to Treasure Hoarders. The festival also followed Ningguang's rebuilding of the Jade Chamber.
- **3.4 details**: Dvorak came looking for the adeptus who had once saved his ancestor, who turned out to be Madame Ping. Hu Tao's opening act with Yun Jin and Xinyan preceded the concert, and the year closed with a get-together at Xinyue Kiosk that drew Zhongli, Xiao and Venti.
- **4.4 details**: Xianyun used her adeptal power to send up her own kite, catching the released kites in its wake and lifting them all higher, and Gaming's wushou performance won over his father.
- **5.3 details**: Hu Tao led the "Sanctification of Tao Dou" ritual, with Xiao, Baizhu, Qiqi, Ningguang and Lan Yan covering the other Qimen gates while she took the Gate of Death; the Traveler pulled her back from the border of death. Yun Jin then performed an opera of the same name at Yilong Wharf.

## entry: Mingxiao Lantern
@keys: Mingxiao Lantern, Mingxiao
@version: 1.3
### STANDARD
The Mingxiao Lantern is the giant communal lantern at the center of Liyue's Lantern Rite, released over Liyue Harbor on the festival's final night. Citizens and visitors donate materials and money, vendors at the market cover much of the cost, and its size depends on the largest Plaustrite found in the previous year. Its design is normally the adeptus form of a hero who died protecting Liyue, picked by Yuehai Pavilion with public input. Subjects in canon: Skybracer (1.3), Sea Gazer (3.4), the yaksha Menogias (4.4), reportedly the god Tao Dou (5.3), and the White Horse Adeptus (6.3, a horse-shaped lantern).

## entry: Xiao Lanterns
@keys: Xiao Lantern, Xiao Lanterns
### STANDARD
Xiao Lanterns are the small personal floating lanterns of Lantern Rite. Their "Xiao" means sky (霄), not the adeptus Xiao (魈), though the two are pronounced alike. Each is made from lantern-fiber wrapping, a wick and a Plaustrite shard to lift it. People write wishes on wish sticks, slot them in and let the lantern carry the wish into the night. Item text: "It floats away carrying beautiful wishes." Children learn to make them (Yaoyao teaches Qiqi in 6.3), and stalls in Liyue Harbor help visitors craft their own. The Ministry of Civil Affairs has stray and broken ones collected because loose Plaustrite is a hazard.

## entry: Xiao and Lantern Rite
@keys: Xiao, Alatus, Vigilant Yaksha, Conqueror of Demons
@keys2: Lantern Rite, Xiao Lantern, Mingxiao
### STANDARD
For Xiao, Lantern Rite means the worst of his duty. The resentment of the gods Morax defeated erupts most strongly during the festival, and he performs the "Nuo Dance of Evil Conquering" day and night to keep Liyue safe, so he has come to hate the festival. He avoids the crowds. His friends keep trying to share it with him: in 1.3 Verr Goldet, Huai'an and the Traveler held a festival at Wangshu Inn and led him close enough to see the Mingxiao Lantern rise. In later years he gradually joins small gatherings, like the 3.4 dinner at Xinyue Kiosk, which made him visibly nervous.

## entry: White Horse Adeptus lantern (6.3)
@keys: White Horse Adeptus, Zibai, Ziqiao, Lanternlit Ode
@keys2: Lantern Rite, Mingxiao, Xiao Lantern
@version: 6.3
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
At the Lantern Rite of Version 6.3 ("Luna IV", story quest "A Lanternlit Ode to the Silver Moon"), the Mingxiao Lantern honored the White Horse Adeptus. Liyue legend says she lives in a palace on the moon, descends to grant people's wishes, and once aided Rex Lapis in the Archon War. Xingqiu links this to why wishes are written on Xiao Lanterns. The adeptus, Zibai, turned out to be real: Zhongli revealed that she had long since died, but one of the Three Deadly Selves she severed was trapped in the shadowmoon, and he estimated that the many wishes released on Xiao Lanterns on the festival night would trigger the shadowmoon's collapse. Another of her selves, Ziqiao, whom Paimon passed off as someone "cosplaying" the adeptus, laughed at the lantern's inaccurate horse gait and was moved that her story had not been forgotten. Ziqiao also remarked on how Xiao Lanterns resemble the Moon Lanterns once offered to the heavens; Zhongli insists they are different, and the link is unconfirmed. The story quest stayed permanently available after the event.

## status
### STANDARD
As of Version 7.1, Lantern Rite remains an annual, living Liyue tradition. The most recent one shown in canon is the 6.3 festival (in-game event January-February 2026); the 7.0-7.1 Snezhnaya content contains no Lantern Rite.
