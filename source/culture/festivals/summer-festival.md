## summary
### LITE
In Inazuma, fireworks mean summer. The summer festival on Narukami Island is a night of masks, stalls, fortune slips, darts and water balloons, ending with the Naganohara Fireworks Show over Inazuma City, which Naganohara Fireworks supplies. Its owner Yoimiya is called the "Queen of the Summer Festival", and people say summer on Narukami Island would not be the same without her.
### STANDARD +
The show is a communal event: families light their own favourite fireworks from paper slips the Naganoharas prepare, and Yoimiya chooses the moment for the grand finale.

## practice
### STANDARD
Festival-goers wear masks, an old custom that is now only optional, and browse stalls for fortune slips, prayer plaques, throwing darts and water balloons. The fireworks show is the one constant across Inazuma's recurring festivals. The Naganohara family prepares fireworks for each participating family, with paper slips holding the recipes for what they ordered; people light their chosen fireworks, and the owner of Naganohara Fireworks stages the grandest display at the right moment. In summer people sit on Amakane Island to watch the display over Inazuma City.
### LARGE +
Fireworks mean different things to different people: friendship for some, marriage for others, belief for others still. Yoimiya says she has never settled what they mean to her but that people's feelings give them purpose. Yoimiya herself falls silent while watching the show, calling it a sacred ceremony. Yashiro Commission staff, with shrine maidens, handle fire precautions so that she can work freely, and Takami of the Commission helps manage the show on Amakane Island.
### ULTRA +
Naganohara Fireworks, in Hanamizaka, also sells toys such as Scatterbloom Marbles and Leaping Tops, and children of Hanamizaka are fond of Yoimiya. A folk song goes, in effect, that rainbows become fireworks and carry wishes into the sky; local children have taken it to mean that a shiny trinket traded to Yoimiya earns a firework of their own. The show's by-products can be mixed with plant extract to make cheap scentless candles.

## history
### STANDARD
Over a century ago the Yashiro Commission organised a commemorative fireworks display made by the best pyrotechnicians of the time, which is regarded as the origin of the Naganohara Fireworks Show. As fireworks became common, families began joining in with their own favourites, and it became a cherished tradition.
### LARGE +
The year Yoimiya took over the family business, Kamisato Ayaka asked her to include the Raiden Shogun's emblem, the Electro Mitsudomoe, because the Shogun would be watching from Tenshukaku. Yoimiya ran launch tubes all over Inazuma City, producing an enormous aerial picture of the city that formed the emblem only when seen from Tenshukaku. This earned her the nickname "Queen of the Summer Festival". Some fifty years ago, the wiki says, Imatani Saburou proposed to his wife at the show.
### ULTRA +
In the Yoimiya Story Quest (2.0), Yoimiya races to finish orders while helping an Inazuman fugitive named Sakujirou and his friend Keisuke the Tenryou Commission officer. She lets Keisuke light the firework meant for the two as they say farewell, then shows the Traveler the show from a cliff on Amakane Island and gives them a paper slip for the golden firework she had made for them. Yoimiya notes that fireworks vanish in an instant, unlike the eternity the Shogun seeks, but the feelings tied to them endure.

## story_ayaka
@version: 2.0
### STANDARD
In Kamisato Ayaka's Story Quest the Traveler and Ayaka visit a festival on Amakane Island on its final day, as stalls are closing. It is Ayaka's first time ever at a festival, and the vendors, recognising her, stay open longer. They get masks and fortune slips, share a prayer-plaque wish and end the night with Ayaka dancing. Ayaka says she loves festivals' lack of rules, their friendly smiles and carefree happiness. The wiki counts this as the Summer Festival.

## folklore
### LARGE
The Retracing Bolide artifact texts are ghost stories set at a summer festival: a man separated from his parents as a boy during the procession of the divine palanquin, a young man with a stopped pocket watch, and a fox mask and a never-wilting flower. Each describes a beautiful fox-eyed woman who takes someone's hand, offers to watch fireworks, throw darts or play fusen, and disappears. They also mention masks in the likeness of a legendary fox deity, as part of the custom of wearing fox masks.
### ULTRA +
The stories are first-person recollections, not confirmed history. The wiki suggests the woman is Kitsune Saiguu and the vantage point Amakane Island, but the texts leave both open.

## scene_use
### STANDARD
Use the festival for warm, lively evening scenes: bargaining at stalls, children chasing Yoimiya, fortune slips, and the shared hush when a firework rises. It suits first-time festival visitors, reluctant officials seeing normal life, or quiet confessions beneath the display. For strangeness, use the folklore thread: a stranger with fox-like eyes offering the best view.
### LARGE +
Keep fireworks associated with Yoimiya's craft: loud, friendly, and quietly sincere. Do not assign a fixed date to the festival, and avoid claiming the Shogun attends in person unless the scene says so.

## status
### STANDARD
The show is presented in canon as an ongoing Inazuman tradition with Yoimiya as its star. No source read records a change to the festival after the 2.0 material.

## entry: Naganohara Fireworks Show
@keys: Naganohara Fireworks Show, Naganohara fireworks display, Queen of the Summer Festival
@version: 2.0
### STANDARD
The Naganohara Fireworks Show is run by Naganohara Fireworks and performed at various Inazuman festivals throughout the year, best seen from Amakane Island over Inazuma City. It began over a century ago as a Yashiro Commission commemoration and grew into a community tradition. Families light fireworks from paper slips, and Yoimiya sets off a grand finale. Her title, Queen of the Summer Festival, comes from the first show she ran, when the emblem of the Raiden Shogun appeared in the sky only from Tenshukaku.
