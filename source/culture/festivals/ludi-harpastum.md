## summary
### LITE
Ludi Harpastum is one of Mondstadt's oldest festivals, held every year and lasting fifteen days. It is a carnival of songs, wine and flight, the very things Mondstadt takes pride in, and it is especially loved by children. The champion of the games chooses a maiden who, at the climax, throws the Harpastum, a round ball wrapped in red cloth, into the crowd. Whoever catches it is said to enjoy a year of fortune and happiness.
### STANDARD +
It was founded to remember how the Anemo Archon Barbatos taught people to brew wine and to live freely, and it was meant for everyone. The Latin name means roughly "the Harpastum Games".
### LARGE +
Long ago the aristocracy hijacked it, and after Vennessa's rebellion it was restored. In the present day it is a free, public festival again; the manga shows it taking place roughly a year before the main story begins.

## practice
### LITE
Fifteen days of games, music, food and drink. At the climax a maiden chosen by the champion throws the red-cloth Harpastum ball to the crowd; the person who picks it up is blessed with a year of fortune.
### STANDARD +
- Games and flight: a gliding championship takes place in the last days of the festival, in the plaza by the Cathedral, according to the Teyvat Travel Highlights text written by a visiting bard.
- Households: each family hangs a handmade Harpastum on the door, made by the lady of the house.
- Food and drink: taverns and restaurants set their best dishes at their doors, Dawn Winery delivers many barrels of beer and cider, and cheese, grilled meat and fresh fruit scent the streets.
- Music: bards come from across the continent and sing poems, and the festivities are said to last all night long.
### LARGE +
The same visiting bard says most people have forgotten the original sacred aspect of the festival and treat it as a lively succession of events, but still finds that it shows off Mondstadt's feeling of freedom and independence.

## history
@version: 1.0
### LITE
Founded to mark Barbatos teaching his people to brew wine and live freely. About a thousand years before the main story the aristocracy that ruled Mondstadt turned it into an elite affair, until Vennessa's rebellion restored it.
### STANDARD +
Barbatos had left by then, to avoid becoming a tyrant like Decarabian, and the aristocracy had grown corrupt. In the manga's account, the head of the Lawrence Clan, the leading aristocratic family, cancelled all the games and kept only the climax; only his son Barca Lawrence was allowed to touch the ball, anyone else who approached it faced torture, and Barca also claimed the right to take the maiden home. Venti (Barbatos in his travelling form) arrived during the climax. At some point the aristocracy had built a tall tower in the Anemo statue plaza for the maiden to throw from. After the rebellion and the banishment of the aristocracy, the festival returned to its original glory and the tower was taken down.
### LARGE +
In the manga, Barca was about to take the ball for the fifth year in a row; the fates of the four earlier maidens are unknown. Venti took the ball himself, fled, and met Vennessa, then an undefeated gladiator belonging to another noble house, who stood up to Barca. The Lawrence Clan later bought Vennessa and her clan and forced her into a gladiator tournament. In the end Venti revealed himself as Barbatos, drove off Ursa the Drake and told Lord Lawrence to hand the city to Vennessa as promised. This is how the manga links the festival to the rebellion; it is not covered in the game's own quests, so treat the details as manga-sourced.
### ULTRA +
The Teyvat Travel Highlights text also relays a merchant's story that Mondstadt, when still a small village, was graced by a feather of a free bird dropped by Barbatos, and that a yearly commemoration thanking the Archon grew into the festival. It is secondhand and told by a drunk, so present it as hearsay alongside the wine-teaching origin.

## scene_use
### LITE
Use it for crowded, joyful scenes in Mondstadt: music in the streets, wine and food stalls, children running about, a gliding contest or the red Harpastum ball thrown to the crowd.
### STANDARD +
Characters may bring up the tradition that catching the ball means a lucky year. In the manga the festival also frames plot: the Fatui are invited to attend, which draws Dottore and Krupp, and Amber takes Collei around the festival after it begins.
### LARGE +
Most Mondstadters revere Barbatos without knowing that the bard Venti is the Archon. Do not let a character state the Barca episode as local common knowledge; it is a thousand-year-old story and the aristocracy-era festival is remembered only vaguely, if at all.

## entry: Harpastum Ball and Maiden
@keys: Harpastum ball, Harpastum maiden, red-cloth ball
### STANDARD
The Harpastum is a round ball wrapped in red cloth. The champion of the games chooses a maiden, who throws it into the crowd at the climax of Ludi Harpastum, and whoever picks it up is blessed with a year of fortune and happiness. Families also hang handmade Harpastums on their doors. Not to be confused with the Latin ball game harpastum (the name's source) or the Harpastum exploration mechanic.

## entry: Barca Lawrence
@keys: Barca Lawrence, Lord Lawrence
@version: 1.0
### LARGE
Barca Lawrence was the son of Lord Lawrence, head of the aristocratic Lawrence Clan, around the time of Vennessa's rebellion, about a thousand years before the main story. He appears only in the manga Prologue, as an arrogant, spoiled youth who monopolised the Harpastum ball at Ludi Harpastum and took the maiden for himself. After Venti seized the ball, Barca backed down from Vennessa, then the clan took revenge by buying her and her clan. His fate is unknown but presumably he was exiled with his clan.
