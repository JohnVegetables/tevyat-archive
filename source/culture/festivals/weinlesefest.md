## summary
### LITE
Weinlesefest ("wine harvest festival" in German) is held in Mondstadt every autumn, when the west wind returns with the harvest. Citizens brew fresh wine and keep it sealed until Windcoming Day, when it is uncasked to invite the Anemo Archon to share a drink. If the Archon is pleased with the wine, a gentle breeze is summoned to bless the people.
### STANDARD +
Jean describes it as an ancient festival that began as a welcome for Lord Barbatos's return, alongside Ludi Harpastum and the Windblume Festival, and the most important part of the fall. In the 3.1 event the festival is organised by the Knights of Favonius, the Church of Favonius and the Adventurers' Guild, with its main venue a festival market outside Springvale.

## practice
### LITE
New wine is brewed and sealed during the harvest season, then opened on Windcoming Day in a toast to the Anemo Archon. A breeze afterwards is read as his blessing.
### STANDARD +
- Preparations: the harvest season brings a rush of requests to the Adventurers' Guild, mostly deliveries and escorts of goods, and the Knights plan the festival ahead.
- Venue (as shown in the 3.1 event): a festival market with stalls, a stage and a Knights of Favonius charity shop; Angel's Share runs a stall there.
- Sealing: Jean says households once brewed fresh wine each year and kept it sealed at least until Windcoming Day of the following year's Weinlesefest.
- Toast: on Windcoming Day everyone gathers at the market for a toasting ceremony, and a bard sings before the wines are opened. Wine may be offered to the Archon.
### LARGE +
Wines are made with whatever ingredients their makers choose, so no two brews taste alike. Jean adds that the festival is when Mondstadters living away often come home, and when those who cannot return miss family most; in 3.1 the Knights used the market to raise funds for needy children and elderly people. The event's closing scene shows several Mondstadters spending the festival with family or remembering family members who mattered to them.

## the_event
@availability: limited
@version: 3.1
### STANDARD
In the limited event Of Ballads and Brews (3.1), the Traveler and Paimon help Razor, whose lost parents left him a bottle of Thousand-Wind Wine. They gather ingredients for his own bottle. Bennett gives Small Lamp Grass for the Adventurers' Guild's explorer's courage, Rosaria gives Mist Flower Corolla for the Church's tender and true love, and Noelle and Sucrose supply Quadruple-Sweetness Sunsettia for the Knights' defender's will. Diluc provides a barrel, Diona mixes the brew, and Venti adds Dandelion Seeds last to catch the wind's memory. Razor's wine is buried under the great tree at Windrise, and Razor says he will open it with everyone once he is grown up. The next day is Windcoming Day, when the toast is held with other wines, including bottles Bennett and Klee brought as an offering.
### LARGE +
Venti sings "Thousand-Wind Wine" at the toast. Klee first worries that the Archon did not come because she feels no wind, but the wind rises soon after, with Venti quietly using his power. Razor smells his parents' bottle and sees a vision of them, adventurers sharing the wine on a cliff and choosing his name. The event also had gameplay phases (Autumn Crisis, Highwind Trial, Charity and Creativity, Fecund Blessings) and gave the weapon Missive Windspear.
### ULTRA +
In the first act, Sounds From Afar, Mika reads out Varka's letter from the expedition, which wishes the Knights well for Weinlesefest and mentions the Captain. Later in the quest Lisa says Thousand-Wind Wine was, so they say, the first wine Mondstadters ever learned to make.

## scene_use
### LITE
Use it for warm autumn scenes: stalls at Springvale, barrels, fruit carts, a stage with music and the question of whether the wind will come. It suits quiet talks over wine and family reminiscence.
### STANDARD +
A character may put a bottle aside for Windcoming Day, argue about the best ingredients, or simply wait to feel the first breeze. Anyone can brew their own wine for the occasion.
### LARGE +
Do not state that Venti caused the wind unless the speaker is Venti or someone he told; most Mondstadters take the breeze as the Archon's answer.

## examples
### STANDARD
- A brewer seals a cask of new wine and writes the day it will be opened.
- A courier hurries fruit carts to the market while hilichurls lurk on the road.
### LARGE +
- A child asks whether the Archon dislikes the wine when no wind blows; an adult replies that he may come in other forms.

## entry: Windcoming Day
@keys: Windcoming Day
### STANDARD
The day on which wine sealed during the harvest is uncasked, as a symbolic invitation for the Anemo Archon to share a drink. If he approves the wine, a gentle breeze blesses the people. In the 3.1 event it is the day after the group buries Razor's wine.

## entry: Thousand-Wind Wine
@keys: Thousand-Wind Wine, Dandelion Wine
@availability: limited
@version: 3.1
### LARGE
Lisa says it was, so they say, the first kind of wine Mondstadters learned to make; Venti calls it the original Dandelion Wine. It tastes different from brew to brew, because everyone is free to add what they like, which makes it impractical to sell, so it is almost never seen in markets or taverns. Venti says dandelion seeds should be added at the last moment to capture the wind and keep its memory in the wine. Lisa judges that Razor's parents brewed the bottle they left him.
