## summary
### LITE
The Moonchase Festival is Liyue's autumn festival, celebrated by moonlight when many ingredients are ripe. Today it is mostly a time for friends and family to reunite, feast and enjoy themselves. Each year the Qixing choose a theme, and the festival has long been tied to food and cooking contests, such as the Masterful Chefs competition. It grew from an old rite once observed only by the adepti, and later festivals, including a seasonal tribute to the Stove God, were folded into it.
### STANDARD +
Moonchase is understood in Liyue as a popular festival that nobody dictates: new customs join it over time and later come to be called tradition. It is distinct from Lantern Rite, Liyue's turn-of-the-year festival, and from Nod-Krai's Moon-Prayer Night.
### LARGE +
Canon shows it in two Traveler-attended seasons: the 2.1 Moonlight Merriment, built around Masterful Chefs, and the 7.1 Silverwing in Pursuit of the Moon, centered on Yilong Wharf in Chenyu Vale and a World Food Fair.

## practice
### STANDARD
- **Seasonal feasting and reunions**: the modern festival is for gathering with friends and family, eating well, and admiring the autumn moon. Cloud Retainer notes it falls when cooking ingredients are ripe, which makes it a fitting time for fine food.
- **Yearly theme**: the Qixing decide a theme each year. The one in canon for 2.1 was "Feast of the Bounteous Land", for which they organized the Masterful Chefs cooking competition at Yujing Terrace.
- **Decorations**: Liyue Harbor is dressed up for the occasion, including its entrance, the stairs to Feiyun Slope, the Ministry of Civil Affairs, Yuehai Pavilion and Yujing Terrace.
### LARGE +
- **Masterful Chefs** (2.1): a cooking contest organized by the Qixing. Xiangling and Smiley Yanxiao of Wangshu Inn were the finalists, and Xiangling won the final cook-off.
- **Adventurers' Guild activities** (2.1): Linyang of the Liyue guild said the Guild had hidden Moonchase Charms across the land and placed special treasure chests, and added monster-camp challenges because many adventurers had gone off to enjoy the festival. The Charm's item text calls it a jade talisman made of Plaustrite shards that holds people's memories of past festivals.
- **World Food Fair** (7.1): a different format at Yilong Wharf, with food stalls from Liyue, Mondstadt, Fontaine and Sumeru. Escoffier represented Fontaine's culinary scene and Xiangling ran a Liyue stall. It replaced Masterful Chefs that year.
- **Culinary duel**: at the end of the 7.1 festival Lan Yan says Xiangling and Escoffier have agreed to hold a culinary duel at Yilong Wharf every year.
### ULTRA +
- **Annual security work**: Ningguang says that around this time each year, malevolent forces gather and trouble Liyue; their energy can possess monsters and make them more dangerous. The Millelith mobilize, and in 7.1 she traveled to Yilong Wharf to coordinate with the Lan clan of thaumaturges. Lan Yu, head of the Lan clan, helped the Millelith with the evil spirits.
- **Organisation by associations**: in 7.1 the associations of Chenyu Vale organized the festival. Lan Yan, who belongs to one of them, says her clan carries weight there and its elders worked out that year's format, including the food fair.
- **No official rules**: Ningguang stresses that no rules ever dictated how Moonchase must be celebrated and that most customs began with ordinary people. Masterful Chefs is, in her words, one such custom later regarded as tradition.

## history
### STANDARD
In ancient times, the people of Liyue paid seasonal tribute to the Stove God, Marchosius, who taught the people of the Guili Assembly to make fire and cook. Keqing's grandfather called this practice the Stove God Festival and thought it a forerunner of Moonchase. Madame Ping says Moonchase was originally a rite observed by the adepti alone, which ordinary people imitated, producing various festivals with the Moonchase name; Rex Lapis later unified them under that name to honor an old friend of his. Ping describes the shared meaning as vowing together under the moon to unite joyfully, remember the past and reflect on the present.
### LARGE +
Xiangling relays that old folk say the autumn moon was the season when the adepti sought "the Way" thousands of years ago. In the 2.1 story the Stove God, long sought through old texts at Qingce Village, Wangshu Inn and Wanmin Restaurant, turns out to be Guoba, formerly Marchosius, who gave up his power to protect Liyue Harbor from disaster and plague. This is why the festival honors him. See the sub-entry on the Stove God.
### ULTRA +
In 6.3, during Lantern Rite, Ganyu reports what Cloud Retainer told her: the Moonchase Rite began as a way to communicate with the moon and was always performed in person by Rex Lapis, who never received any reply, after which the adepti stopped performing it. After Keqing showed Cloud Retainer a photograph of the moon covered by a dark hole, Cloud Retainer realized it might have been an attempt to reach the White Horse Adeptus all along. Ziqiao, listening, murmured about the Geo Archon striving to awaken her.

In 7.1 Iron Tongue Tian narrates the origin as the adepti host chasing the moon to pursue the adeptal path. Zhongli replies that the adeptal path is distant from mortal life, but the word "Moonchase" suited the season and the imagination, so later generations adopted it as a festival motif. Zibai adds that in ancient Lang-Gan a similar holiday existed, when people brewed grain wine, gazed at the full moon, offered wine to her in hope of a good harvest, and she taught them farming and mathematics. Zhongli says offering prayers to the immortals is no longer common: in present-day Liyue people eat, drink, admire the moon and gather with loved ones.

## regional_variation
### LARGE
Liyue Harbor is the traditional stage: in 2.1 Masterful Chefs took place at Yujing Terrace, and the whole harbor was decorated. In 7.1 the centre moved to Yilong Wharf in Chenyu Vale, where the associations organized a food street and a World Food Fair. Cooking contests are therefore a variable feature, not a fixed requirement of the festival.
### ULTRA +
Old customs may survive elsewhere. In 7.1 Lan Yan suggests that some places in Liyue may still keep Moonchase customs from before the Stove God Festival was folded in. The adepti observe the season in their own way: in 7.1 Madame Ping, Moon Carver, Mountain Shaper and Cloud Retainer gather at Mt. Aocang, and Ping says they would have invited Silverwing had they been able to reach her.

## scene_use
### STANDARD
Use Moonchase for a warm autumn scene: moonlit streets, stalls and cooking contests, friends reuniting, relatives gathering, judging and tasting food. It suits reunions, friendly rivalry, learning about local history from an elder, and gentle melancholy about change; Paimon voices this in 7.1, saying the festival returns each year while the people around it change.
### LARGE +
Practical notes for roleplay:
- Liyue characters treat it as a seasonal gathering rather than a solemn occasion; food is central, but people disagree on how important it is (Paimon in 7.1: good food and good friends are what make Moonchase).
- Do not state a fixed theme for a year canon has not named, and do not claim it has always involved cooking contests: Ningguang and Lan Yan stress that formats change.
- The Millelith and thaumaturge clans may be busy with evil spirits while everyone else celebrates.
- Keep the Moonchase Rite and the White Horse Adeptus link as relayed reports, not common knowledge.

## examples

- **2.1, Moonlight Merriment (2021-09-27 to 2021-10-11)**: Xiangling, preparing for Masterful Chefs, asks friends for inspiration, and the group research the Stove God's identity. Guoba had lost the ability to speak but still remembered familiar people, and was visibly drawn to Zhongli on his arrival at Wanmin Restaurant.
- **7.1 spoiler summary** (major spoiler): a silver-haired man who keeps saying "Moonchase ought not to be like this" is a remnant soul bound to a rattan figure by the Lan clan's forbidden Soul-Binding Art. The quest names him Lan Junming, the Lan clan's "Grandmaster" and the Wind Reader. He needs to see the adeptus Silverwing, whose power comes from swallows, to keep an old promise. The story ends with Lan Yan casting a new version of her own art, which releases his soul; Zhongli later says more swallows will surely return to Chenyu Vale.

## entry: Stove God
@keys: Stove God, Marchosius, Stove God Festival
@version: 2.1
### STANDARD
The Stove God is the old god of cooking fire honored by the Moonchase Festival. Marchosius taught the people of the Guili Assembly to make fire and cook, and later sacrificed his divine power to protect Liyue Harbor from disaster and plague. In the 2.1 story he turns out to be the small guardian Guoba, who can no longer speak and is less intelligent than before, though he remembers those familiar to him. Keqing's grandfather called the seasonal tribute to him the "Stove God Festival" and saw it as a precursor to Moonchase; Madame Ping says it was likely one of the festivals Rex Lapis later gathered under Moonchase to honor an old friend.

## entry: Masterful Chefs
@keys: Masterful Chefs, Moonlight Merriment, World Food Fair
@keys2: Moonchase, Moonchase Festival, Xiangling
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
Masterful Chefs is the Moonchase cooking contest, organized by the Qixing; the 2.1 edition coincided with the "Feast of the Bounteous Land" theme and ended in a cook-off between Xiangling and Smiley Yanxiao. In 7.1 the contest was not held again; the Lan clan elders and Chenyu Vale associations instead hosted the World Food Fair at Yilong Wharf with food from Liyue, Mondstadt, Fontaine and Sumeru. Lan Yan says Xiangling and Escoffier have agreed to hold a culinary duel at Yilong Wharf every year.

## entry: Moonchase Rite
@keys: Moonchase Rite
@keys2: Rex Lapis, White Horse Adeptus, Cloud Retainer, Zibai
@version: 6.3
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
The Moonchase Rite is the adeptal ritual the festival grew from, said to be a way of communicating with the moon, always performed in person by Rex Lapis. According to Ganyu (relaying Cloud Retainer, 6.3), he never got any response and the adepti stopped performing it. After seeing Keqing's photograph of the moon obscured by a dark hole, Cloud Retainer wondered whether the rite had been an attempt to reach the White Horse Adeptus all along. Most people know only the festival, not the rite behind it.

## status
### STANDARD
As of Version 7.1 the Moonchase Festival is a living annual Liyue tradition. The most recent canon edition is the 7.1 festival at Yilong Wharf (event from 2026-09-24 to 2026-10-12). The earlier edition in canon is the 2.1 Masterful Chefs festival.
