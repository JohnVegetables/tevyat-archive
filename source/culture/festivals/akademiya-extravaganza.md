## summary
### LITE
The Akademiya Extravaganza is the Sumeru Akademiya's festival. It has two parts: the Wisdom Gala, held every year, when the six Darshans set up booths around Sumeru City to attract students, and the Interdarshan Championship, held once every four years, when each Darshan sends one representative to compete for the title of champion, rewards and the right to wear the Diadem of Knowledge. The Championship held in version 3.6 (the event A Parade of Providence) took place after the change in Sumeru's leadership and was of great importance to the Akademiya, which invited many desert-dwellers to enjoy its atmosphere.
### STANDARD +
Past Championships were far harsher. Events where competitors fought to the death were used until the one twenty years before A Parade of Providence, and the Akademiya later removed many of them.
### LARGE +
Keep the tone festive on the surface and thoughtful underneath: the Extravaganza showcases the Akademiya at its most open, but the Diadem of Knowledge and the past Championship show the cost of its competitive culture.

## wisdom_gala
### LITE
The Wisdom Gala is held every year. All six Darshans set up booths across Sumeru City to show off what they do and attract potential students. Booths often have minigames and activities.
### STANDARD +
Choosing what to showcase is a point of contention in some Darshans, who have trouble finding activities that are both fitting and appealing. In A Parade of Providence the booths became the event's puzzle games, such as Gathering of Stars, Project Connectivity, Antiquity Hunt, In Truth's Steps, Concocted Reaction and Mimetic Replication, which earned Gala Excitement.
### LARGE +
The Darshans are Amurta, Haravatat, Kshahrewar, Rtawahist, Spantamad and Vahumana. Each Darshan competes to look appealing to prospective students.

## interdarshan_championship
### LITE
Held every four years. Each Darshan picks one representative, and they compete to become champion of the entire Championship and earn rewards. For the twenty years up to and including A Parade of Providence, a top prize was the right to wear the Diadem of Knowledge, a valuable treasure widely thought of as a work of art.
### STANDARD +
Participants must belong to a Darshan, as current students or alumni. They can nominate themselves or be nominated by more than half of their Darshan, and nominees may decline. Sages may not take part, and a person may enter only one Championship.
### LARGE +
Per Huvishka, the Spantamad Darshan has the highest share of Championship winners, and the most thrilling Championship was the one twenty years ago. Known participants: Kaveh's father (Rtawahist, twenty years ago, finished second), a friend of Huvishka (Darshan unknown, twenty years ago), and in 3.6 Tighnari (Amurta), Faruzan (Haravatat), Kaveh (Kshahrewar, champion), Layla (Rtawahist), Cyno (Spantamad) and Wanderer (Vahumana, using the name Hat Guy).

## history
@version: 3.6
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Not much is known about the Wisdom Gala or Extravaganza before twenty years ago. Shortly before an Extravaganza that year, Sachin entrusted all his assets to the Akademiya, including the Diadem of Knowledge, which he said should be worn only by an Interdarshan Championship winner. The Extravaganza after his donation was larger than usual.
### LARGE +
Kaveh's father became a favourite to win that Championship but did not. The quests imply that in the final round, in Avidya Forest, he met the fragment of Sachin's consciousness stored in the Diadem and was badly affected; Arakasyapa, who remembered him, hid the Diadem in a tree hollow, where an Akademiya employee later found it. Kaveh's father fell into depression. What exactly happened is unknown, because no one dared approach the participants. The Akademiya then shut the competition down and later removed many of the events that had competitors fight to the death.
### ULTRA +
That history is why the Diadem of Knowledge matters in 3.6: Sachin's son Jiwani and the Diadem are tied to the Championship's darkest chapter, and the secret the Diadem carries is only understood through Kaveh's own experience of it.

## a_parade_of_providence
@version: 3.6
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
The Extravaganza of version 3.6 (event dates 27 April to 15 May 2023) mattered greatly to the Akademiya after the change in Sumeru's leadership in Chapter III. Lesser Lord Kusanali and the sages decided to invite desert-dwellers, who had been oppressed, among them Candace and Dehya, in the hope they would enjoy the Akademiya's atmosphere. Nilou, the Traveler and Paimon, none of them from the Akademiya, were recruited as guest commentators together with Alhaitham, so the Championship would appeal to more people.
### LARGE +
Participants were Tighnari, Faruzan, Kaveh, Layla, Cyno and Wanderer. Rewards were money, research funding, a limited-edition Genius Invokation TCG card and the right to wear the Diadem. The three rounds were: find the fast Swiftflies among butterflies released in Sumeru City; find Ley Line Disruptors, devices imitating Ley Line disruptions, buried around Aaru Village; and find the Diadem of Knowledge and put it on a stand in Mawtiyima Forest.
### ULTRA +
During the Championship Jiwani, who resented his father Sachin and thought him still alive, hired Eremites to kidnap him; Wanderer and Alhaitham stopped the plan. After Kaveh won, the fragment of Sachin in the Diadem appeared, but his words upset Kaveh, who smashed the Diadem so nobody else would suffer from it, and Sachin's image faded smiling. Kaveh, heir to Sachin's wealth, decided to donate it to help others, refused all the rewards except the TCG card, and gave that to Cyno, who bought it from him for one million Mora. Nahida arrived to seal Sachin's research, honouring Kaveh's wish, but said that if someone later wanted to take it up, she would not stop them.

## scene_use
### LITE
Use it for bustling Sumeru City scenes: Darshan booths with games, students recruiting, banners, spectators gathered around the Championship's commentators. It is a place where scholars, desert-dwellers and outlanders mingle.
### STANDARD +
Rivalry between Darshans is good-natured. Booth staff worry about attracting students; Championship contestants can be hopeful, proud or anxious. Remember the Gala is yearly while the Championship is every four years.
### LARGE +
After 3.6 the Diadem is destroyed, so scenes set later should not have a winner wearing it. Many of the fight-to-the-death events were removed after the Championship twenty years before 3.6. Do not name a winner for the Championship twenty years ago.

## entry: Interdarshan Championship
@keys: Interdarshan Championship, Interdarshan
### STANDARD
Held every four years. Each of the six Darshans enters one student or alumnus, who competes for the title of champion, money, research funding and, for twenty years until it was broken, the Diadem of Knowledge. Contestants cannot be sages and can enter once. The 3.6 Championship had three rounds: Swiftflies in Sumeru City, Ley Line Disruptors near Aaru Village, and the Diadem in Mawtiyima Forest.

## entry: Wisdom Gala
@keys: Wisdom Gala
### STANDARD
The yearly part of the Extravaganza. Each Darshan runs booths in Sumeru City, usually with minigames, to attract prospective students.

## entry: Diadem of Knowledge
@keys: Diadem of Knowledge
@version: 3.6
### STANDARD
A treasure Sachin gave to the Akademiya and declared would be worn only by the winner of an Interdarshan Championship; widely seen as a work of art. It holds a fragment of Sachin's consciousness, which harmed Kaveh's father twenty years earlier. Kaveh destroyed it after winning in 3.6 so no one would hear Sachin's words again.

## entry: Sachin and Jiwani
@keys: Sachin, Jiwani
@version: 3.6
### STANDARD
Sachin was a wealthy man who gave all his assets, including the Diadem of Knowledge, to the Akademiya shortly before the Extravaganza twenty years before 3.6. His son Jiwani resented him and hired Eremites to kidnap him during the 3.6 Championship, believing he was still alive. Wanderer and Alhaitham foiled the plan. Kaveh inherited Sachin's wealth and chose to give it away.

## entry: Kaveh's father and the old Championship
@keys: Kaveh's father
@version: 3.6
### STANDARD
A Rtawahist participant in the Championship twenty years before 3.6, finished second after being a favourite. Quests imply that he was harmed by the fragment of Sachin in the Diadem during the final round and later fell into depression. Arakasyapa, who remembered him, hid the Diadem in a tree hollow.
