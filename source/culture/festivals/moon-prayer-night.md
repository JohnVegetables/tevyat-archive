## summary
### LITE
Moon-Prayer Night (Kuunrukous, "moon-prayer") is a festival in Nod-Krai held on the night the moon comes closest to Teyvat. The Frostmoon Scions began it to celebrate the birth of the Moon Goddess; over time it became Nod-Krai's liveliest festival. Today most townsfolk treat it as a local custom and a chance to do business rather than a religious rite.

### STANDARD +
Its timing is never fixed in advance. People guess roughly, then wait for signs: the crescent at the top of the Verdant Crests turns into a full moon and the Moon-Prayer Blossoms bloom with a special radiance, which Nod-Kraians take as blessed omens. Because the moon is so close, kuuvahki reaches its peak. The waiting is part of the fun.

### LARGE +
The festival is a story setting in Version 6.2, when the Traveler and Paimon prepare and attend it in Nasha Town with Columbina, who has never joined a Moon-Prayer Night before.

## practice
### LITE
Stalls, food, gifts and decorations in Nasha Town, with song and dance in the Frostmoon Scions' tradition.

### STANDARD
In the Scions' past tradition, participants wore Moon-Prayer Blossoms, celebrated the Moon Goddess's birth with song and dance, and prepared food and handicrafts to give to others. In Nasha Town today, Moonveils are hung from buildings so that the night sky looks misty through them, Verdant Crests are set up, and stalls sell festival treats such as Moon-Prayer Candy in many flavours.

### LARGE +
In the Version 6.2 celebration, the Frostmoon Scions' area has Lauma's wishing slips, where people hang written wishes, plus sugar sculptures and balloon animals; Lauma also invites friendly animals. The Hexenzirkel runs a section of games and prizes with Alice, Barbeloth and Nicole, with Varka and Albedo taking part. Nasha Town's own stalls include candy, a frostfin whale game and a concentration game involving Kuuhenki. Katheryne wishes adventurers a happy Moon-Prayer Night.

### ULTRA +
Preparation is a joint effort: Varka helps oversee Nasha Town's decorations and custom cloth designs with Lauma, Sarakka checks a Moonveil for translucency, and Dori is asked to help source supplies. The Version 6.2 quest also shows the festival as a social outing for the Traveler's circle, including Nefer, Durin and the Wanderer. Stallholders greet Columbina as the Moon Goddess.

## regional_variation
### LARGE
The festival is tied to Nod-Krai and described mainly in Nasha Town. Katheryne notes that most people there see it as a business opportunity and do not concern themselves with the religious element, while the Frostmoon Scions keep a closer tie to the Moon Goddess. Lauma calls it a long-held tradition of Nod-Krai.

### ULTRA +
The name Kuunrukous is Finnish-derived ("kuu" moon, "rukous" prayer), matching the Finnish-flavoured naming across Nod-Krai. This is a naming note, not a statement about canon history.

## scene_use
### STANDARD
Use it for lively, warm scenes: crowds, stalls, misty Moonveils over the night sky, candy, wishes pinned up by children, mage games. A Nasha Town local may mention it casually or commercially; a Frostmoon Scion may speak of it with more reverence. Do not give it a fixed date or ritual set beyond what is stated here.

### LARGE +
Before the festival, expect talk of waiting for the omens and of mirage-like phantoms appearing around Nod-Krai. After the Version 6.2 events, it can carry bittersweet weight for characters connected to Columbina, who says she wishes it could last longer than one night.

## history
@version: 6.2
### LARGE
The Frostmoon Scions first celebrated it, and it has been a long-held tradition in Nod-Krai since. It is celebrated in the Version 6.2 Archon Quests In the Shifting Light of the Moon and The Night the Moon Returns Home, part of the Song of the Welkin Moon chapter, where the festival and the phantoms around it drive the plot.

## examples
@version: 6.2
### ULTRA
Columbina's wish slip in 6.2 hopes that everyone in the moonlit world finds a place to belong and that she will one day see her friends again. At the candy stall she suggests flavours to share with her friends, and the stallholder, caught up in the new ideas, calls the rush of business a blessing from the Moon Goddess.

## entry: Moon-Prayer Blossoms and Verdant Crests
@keys: Moon-Prayer Blossom, Moon-Prayer Blossoms, Verdant Crest, Verdant Crests
@version: 6.2
### STANDARD
At the festival's peak, the crescent moon at the top branches of the Verdant Crest turns into a full moon and Moon-Prayer Blossoms bloom with unusual radiance. Nod-Kraians read these as blessed omens, and they guide the timing of the festival. Canon links the effect to the moon's closeness and to kuuvahki reaching its peak.

## entry: Moonveil
@keys: Moonveil, Moonveils
@version: 6.2
### STANDARD
Cloth hung from buildings in Nasha Town during the festival. Looking at the night sky through it gives a lovely misty effect. Sarakka is seen checking whether one is translucent enough.
