## summary
### LITE
The Watatsumi Goryou Matsuri is a ritual of Watatsumi Island meant to stop its soil from bleaching and eroding. Local legend says the island's spirit is drawn down toward Enkanomiya; the ritual, performed with a Jade Coral Branch retrieved from the depths, calls the sunken spirit back. It is held only once every several centuries, and the Sangonomiya Shrine is responsible for it.
### STANDARD +
The affected soil turns white and is known as Holy Soil. The ritual only halts the erosion temporarily. Tsuyuko, the shrine maiden who raises the matter with the Traveler, is a descendant of Eboshi, a shrine maiden of Byakuyakoku's history, and is in charge of the ritual's affairs.

## practice
### STANDARD
Per Tsuyuko, every few centuries, at a moment when the spirit veins flow smoothly, the barrier between Enkanomiya and Sangonomiya must be broken so someone can dive down and retrieve a Jade Coral Branch left by the Omikami (Orobashi). The branch is then used to perform the ritual, causing the sunken "spirit" to return and stopping the erosion. Entering Enkanomiya requires breaking the seals on four Goshou Rocks around the island (the Eye, Tooth, Fin and Tail) to gain Spirit Pearls, which open the fifth rock, the Heart; solving its puzzle yields the Key of the Moon-bathed Deep.
### LARGE +
Near each of the four outer seals stands a tablet recording the ritual's matters, but the writing is badly worn after the ages and the rite for breaking the seals is now unknown to most people. The Jade Coral Branch was made from Orobashi's own body and also serves to suppress the Dragonheir of the Depths.

## explanations
### STANDARD
Three accounts exist. The local legend: Enkanomiya's depths try to absorb the island's spirit, bleaching the soil and leaving it infertile. Tsuyuko, schooled at the Sumeru Akademiya, offers a scientific reading: seawater salinity and alkalinity leach nutrients, and the Jade Coral Branches, being full of Orobashi's power, replenish them.
### LARGE +
The third account comes with Three Realms Gateway Offering (2.5), per the wiki's Holy Soil page. There the ritual's real purpose is to awaken Orobashi's second familiars, the Sangacorallia, which break the primitive elemental energy of the Light Realm into the milder energy of the Human Realm. The legend and Tsuyuko's science are therefore both partial, and canon does not reconcile the accounts.

## history
@version: 2.1
### STANDARD
In The Moon-Bathed Deep (2.1), Tsuyuko, a shrine maiden of the Sangonomiya Shrine, explains the ritual to the Traveler and why the Goshou Rock seals must be broken to reach Enkanomiya.
### LARGE +
Per the Holy Soil page's account of the 2.5 event, the ritual, performed by Sangonomiya Kokomi, did not reverse the bleaching but halted it, which had never happened before. An investigation found that the Void Realm had invaded Enkanomiya and was suppressing interactions between the Light and Human Realms. The situation was resolved, but the supply of Jade Coral Branches was cut off because the vishaps rescued their sealed kin.
### ULTRA +
The 2.1 quest text renders the name as Watatsumi Mitama Omatsuri, while the wiki gives the Japanese reading as Watatsumi Goryou Matsuri. The 2.5 event uses a different term in its Chinese and Japanese titles, Michiae-no-Matsuri, a historical Shinto rite name, for its own offering.

## scene_use
### STANDARD
Use it as a grave, communal rite tied to the island's survival and overseen by the Sangonomiya Shrine. Characters may disagree whether it works through myth or through soil chemistry.
### LARGE +
Do not state a date or say the ritual is annual. Its effect is temporary, and it needs Orobashi's coral, whose source was cut off after the events of 2.5.

## status
### STANDARD
As of 7.1 the ritual has been performed once in the Traveler's story, with Kokomi officiating, and only stopped the erosion. The vishaps' retrieval of their kin has cut off the source of Jade Coral Branches.

## entry: Goshou Rocks
@keys: Goshou Rocks, Goshou Rock, Spirit Pearls
@version: 2.1
### STANDARD
Five rocks around Watatsumi Island seal the entrance to Enkanomiya: Eye, Tooth, Fin, Tail and Heart. The first four are broken to retrieve Spirit Pearls, which unlock the Heart. The structure echoes other five-seal legends in Inazuma and Liyue.

## entry: Tsuyuko
@keys: Tsuyuko
@keys2: Watatsumi, Sangonomiya, Goryou Matsuri, Enkanomiya
