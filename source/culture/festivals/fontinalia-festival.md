## summary
### LITE
The Fontinalia Festival is Fontaine's festival remembering the legendary Lochknights, who searched for the Oceanids and welcomed the Hydro Archon Egeria to Fontaine. Traditionally people wore costumes, raised golden cups and went door to door asking for pure water. Furina reshaped it into a carnival in which children collect candy and cry "Trial or treat!". It now also includes the Fontinalia Film Festival.
### STANDARD +
The old commemoration is a founding-legend holiday, but in practice it is a lively, public, slightly theatrical celebration of Fontaine's city life. The film festival strand, run by the Fontaine Film Association, gives it a modern art-and-commerce side that suits Fontaine's love of performance.

## practice
### LITE
Legend says the Lochknights went in search of the Oceanids and in the end welcomed Egeria, the Hydro Archon, to Fontaine. The festival commemorates this: celebrants dress in special costumes, raise golden cups, and visit homes asking for pure water.
### STANDARD +
In recent years Furina changed the festivities so that they resemble a carnival. The segment about "pure water" was replaced with candy, and the festival became popular with children, who go from house to house saying "Trial or treat!". Fontinalia Mousse, shaped like the bud of a Lakelight Lily, became a favourite festival dessert once the customs leaned towards sweets; its in-game text asks people to share it with the trial-or-treaters at their door.
### LARGE +
The old and new forms overlap: the costumes and golden cups come from the Lochknight commemoration, while the asking for water became asking for sweets. The change is credited to Furina; her later abdication after Masquerade of the Guilty did not undo it. As the festival tells it, the Lochknights' search is a legend; do not present it as documented history beyond Fontaine's own tradition.

## film_festival
@version: 4.3
### STANDARD
The Fontinalia Film Festival is organised by the Fontaine Film Association to bring the art of cinematography to the public. Anyone can submit a film to be judged, and the highest-scoring entry receives the Furina Award. The award was named while Furina still ruled Fontaine, and nobody moved to rename it after she abdicated following the events of Masquerade of the Guilty.
### LARGE +
Winners seen in canon. The first Furina Award went to The Two Musketeers, made by Musketeer Pictures, produced by Xavier and directed by Furina herself (Roses and Muskets, 4.3). In the Afterword quest, The Two Musketeers' Triumph, Xavier has Furina carry the trophy because of her help as director, and she does so reluctantly. The latest iteration, in Sunny Summer Fontinalia, was won by Great Detective Hurlock, directed by Lepine-Pauline with Charlotte as lead actress. Musketeer Pictures entered A Homecoming, directed by Furina with Columbina in the lead role. Chiori's explanation of the loss: artistic films rarely beat commercial ones, and Great Detective Hurlock adapts a popular light novel. New awards were added that year: Best Director, Best Costume Design and Best Kamera Operation.
### ULTRA +
Roses and Muskets framing. In The Crack of Muskets Breaking the Silence the Steambird announces the film festival to the Traveler and Paimon. Xavier, a film producer short of funds because an investor could not back his project, assembles a cast with Chiori's help: Kamisato Ayaka, Kamisato Ayato and Yoimiya, who agree to work for free to strengthen ties between nations. Furina agrees to direct on condition of full freedom over the script, and Chevreuse is brought in to advise on musket use. A murder case echoing the source novel runs alongside; it belongs to the Roses and Muskets quest entry.

## regional_variation
### STANDARD
The festival is a Fontaine custom, with the film strand centred on the capital, the Court of Fontaine. Visitors from other nations (Inazuma in particular, in Roses and Muskets) take part as guests and actors.

## scene_use
### STANDARD
Use the festival for lively, low-stakes scenes: costumed children at a door asking for treats, golden cups raised in a toast, stalls selling Fontinalia Mousse, film posters in a plaza. It is a natural place for performers, filmmakers and nostalgic locals to meet.
### LARGE +
Mentioning Furina's carnival version is accurate common knowledge in Fontaine. The truth about her being the former Hydro Archon's public figurehead is a spoiler tied to the 4.2 Archon Quest and must follow each character's knowledge. In film-festival scenes, remember that artistic and commercial entries compete and that the Furina Award name is a holdover from her reign.
### ULTRA +
Anachronism guard: before 4.3 canon does not show the film festival, and the carnival version is described only as a change of recent years. Sunny Summer Fontinalia is a limited-time event whose story quest can still be played afterwards; characters who were not involved should not know its details.

## examples
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
Roses and Muskets (4.3 flagship event, 2023-12-21 to 2024-01-08) added the Fontinalia Film Festival story and the dish Fontinalia Mousse. Sunny Summer Fontinalia (Version Luna VIII, 6.7, flagship event, running from 2026-07-01 to 2026-08-11) returns to the film festival, with Charlotte, Chiori, Furina, Xavier and others, and also features Sandrone and Citlali aboard the Wingalet.

In Sunny Summer Fontinalia the event gameplay has the Traveler help run a special aquarium (Operation Deep-Dive Treasure, Kaleido-Catch, Curio Ledger), and the story quest follows a film shoot, a dive beneath Fontaine's waters with Sandrone, a visit to a hidden dwelling linked to Alain Guillotin, and a final gathering and photo with friends. Characters listed in the quests include Columbina, Lepine-Pauline, Neuvillette, Sigewinne and Wriothesley.

## entry: Furina Award
@keys: Furina Award, Fontinalia Film Festival, Fontaine Film Association
@version: 4.3
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
The Furina Award is the top prize of the Fontinalia Film Festival, given by the Fontaine Film Association to the highest-scoring submitted film. It kept Furina's name after she abdicated. Winners in canon: The Two Musketeers (first award, Roses and Muskets) and Great Detective Hurlock (latest, Sunny Summer Fontinalia). New awards added in the latest iteration: Best Director, Best Costume Design and Best Kamera Operation.

## entry: Trial or treat
@keys: trial or treat, trial-or-treaters, Fontinalia Mousse
@version: 4.3
### STANDARD
"Trial or treat!" is what children say at each house during the modern Fontinalia, after Furina swapped the old request for pure water for candy. Fontinalia Mousse, shaped like a Lakelight Lily bud, is the festival dessert and is meant to be shared with these visitors.
