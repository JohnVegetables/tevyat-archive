## summary
### LITE
Natlan has no single populace: everyone belongs to one of six tribes, each with its own chief, saurian species, territory and ancestral Wayob. They are the Children of Echoes, Scions of the Canopy, People of the Springs, Flower-Feather Clan, Masters of the Night-Wind and Collective of Plenty. The Pyro Archon handles national war, ritual and defence.
### STANDARD +
Chiefs set and change their tribe's customs, and even the Pyro Archon cannot overrule them while their tribe agrees. Each tribe has a trade niche, its own tribal warriors who imitate their saurian, and Ancient Name bearers chosen by the Wayob. Conflict is treated in Natlan as a way of reaching "reason": a decisive, sincere settling of scores.
### LARGE +
The structure is old (the wiki dates the six tribes to Och-Kan's era, roughly 2,000 years ago) and was shaped by 500 years of constant Abyss war, which is why leaving the nation, the Pilgrimage and the Ancient Names work the way they do. After the war ended (5.x) the system stayed, but the pressures behind it eased.

## practice
### LITE
- Each tribe has a chief, a homeland, a bonded saurian species, a Wayob and an elemental affinity; tribal warriors dress and fight like their saurian.
- The dead normally go to the Night Kingdom, Natlan's repurposed ley lines.
- Disputes are settled by open contest; peacemakers (Chasca is an example) step in when a conflict cannot be resolved.
### STANDARD
**Chiefs.** A chief runs the tribe on its own terms, overseeing customs and able to change them. There is no age requirement: being picked by the previous chief and coping with the duties is enough. Pacal (Children of Echoes) found other ways to support his tribe through the post; Amina (People of the Springs) is a young girl chosen by her grandfather and voted in by the elders, and she handles many tasks without trouble. The current chiefs are on good terms, though their duties leave little time together.

**The Archon's role.** The Pyro Archon does not manage tribal internal affairs. They are responsible for national events (the Pilgrimage, the Night Warden Wars) and defence. In the past the tribes have been bitterly divided, especially when a new Archon was appointed, to the point that the Archon could not intervene.

**Wayob and Ancient Names.** Each tribe's Wayob grants an Ancient Name to a tribe member it judges worthy. Bearers are revered; their deeds are recorded with the name, and a bearer who fully embodies the Wayob's aspirations can tap the knowledge of earlier bearers. Only name bearers can fight in the Night Warden Wars, because the Pyro Archon's Ode of Resurrection does not work for others.

### LARGE +
**The Pilgrimage.** The tribes hold tournaments to fuel the Sacred Flame (see the dedicated Pilgrimage entry): a team round, then individual duels whose winners form the team sent to the Night Warden Wars. Winners are honoured, and non-bearers who took part in the tournaments share the esteem.

**Leaving Natlan.** During the 500-year war Natlanese generally did not leave, because the Wayob shielded them from constant Abyssal influence at home. Someone who wished to leave asked their chief to seek the Wayob's permission, and success was slim. Outside the borders the protection weakened and long exposure to Abyssal energy followed; Bennett, taken out of Natlan, is the notable case of unexplained bad luck. Once the Night Kingdom was purified, people could leave without harm and the Night Kingdom began accepting people from other nations. The wiki says a chief's permission is still needed; see uncertainties.

**Warriors.** Natlan has no formal military. Each tribe fields its own warriors, who often copy their saurian's look and fighting style, use its element and abilities to a degree set by skill, and are skilled in unarmed combat and in weapons such as swords, clubs, spears, javelins, hammers, saw-like weapons and hand cannons. Because of the war, many able-bodied Natlanese have at least some familiarity with fighting.
**Language and saurians.** Saurians understand human speech but humans cannot understand them, although some, like Chasca, read their body language. Few can communicate in both directions; Ajaw is one who can.

**Defunct tribe.** The wiki lists an exiled Anemo tribe: some became the Muratans who left for Mondstadt, the rest were banished to Tenochtzitoc (where the Easybreeze Holiday Resort now stands) and died in the Cataclysm.

**Older festival.** The Bloomflower Trials also fed the Sacred Flame and could help heal sick saurians, but ended after a past attempt brought bloodshed and division, and because the Pilgrimage yielded more Contending Flame. They resumed after the Abyss war ended.
### ULTRA +
**Pilgrimage suspension.** After Kachina failed to return from a Night Warden War even though her team won, Mavuika suspended future pilgrimages because she could not find Kachina's Ancient Name. The wiki also says this prevented further casualties and division, as she knew the Contending Fire produced was not enough to sustain the Sacred Flame. After the Abyss was purged, Mavuika recast the Pilgrimage as athletic competitions.

**Fate of the dead.** The deceased usually go to the Night Kingdom, but a few exceptions risk being forgotten for good. A team that loses a Night Warden War cannot be revived by the Ode; their souls linger in the Night Kingdom and slowly fade.

## regional_variation
### LITE
Each tribe differs by territory, trade and saurian; the six are listed with chiefs and saurians at STANDARD.
### STANDARD
| Tribe | Chief | Saurian | Element | Ancient Name |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Children of Echoes (Nanatzcayan) | Pacal | Tepetlisaurus | Geo | Baraka (Ixquieh, Sundjatta, Xilonen) |
| Scions of the Canopy (Huitztlan) | Wayna | Yumkasaurus | Dendro | Malipo (Yupanqui, Burkina, Kinich) |
| People of the Springs (Meztli) | Amina | Koholasaurus | Hydro | Umoja (Uenuku, Tupac, Mualani) |
| Flower-Feather Clan (Tlalocan) | Mutota | Qucusaurus | Pyro | Vuka (Lianca, Menilek, Chasca) |
| Masters of the Night-Wind (Mictlan) | Biram | Iktomisaurus | Cryo | Bidii (Sanhaj, Ororon) |
| Collective of Plenty (Teteocan) | Acatl | Tatankasaurus | Electro | Uwezo (Ahpub, Wanjiru, Iansan) |
### LARGE +
Economic niches: the Nanatzcayan mine, smith and appraise gems, are Natlan's only source of obsidian (used for ritual, such as communing with the Night Kingdom) and engrave Ancient Names. The Meztli live on tourism, notably their hot springs, which Inazuma admired enough to imitate with Snezhnayan boilers. The Huitztlan take delivery and hunting commissions and host extreme sports. The Mictlan offer fortune-telling, blessings of tools and weapons, artisanal goods and the weaving of historical scrolls. The Teteocan grow high-quality produce, often giving or trading surplus, and some sell abroad (Varesa's family farm). The Tlalocan are elite warriors.

Craft and technology by tribe: the Nanatzcayan build many of Natlan's phlogiston-powered vehicles and drills; Xilonen made nearly everything of the kind apart from the Phlogiston Wings, which Tepal designed and his mother Allpa finished after Tepal died in the process. The Meztli make record discs compatible with Fontainian players, and artificial springs cooler than the natural ones. The Mictlan scroll weavers are appointed by the Iktomisaurus Elder Tlapo. The Tlalocan use hot air balloons to hold platforms aloft. Teteocan wrestling has a masked tradition: the loser of a duel between mask wearers must unmask while the winner stays masked.

## scene_use
### LITE
Use a tribe's identity to flavour an encounter: ask which tribe a Natlanese character comes from, who their chief is, and which saurian walks beside them. Expect directness: grievances are settled in open contest, not smoothed over.
### STANDARD
- A visitor is introduced through a tribe, never to "Natlan" in general; a chief's word binds that tribe only.
- Name bearers get respect and the recorded deeds of their predecessors; other tribe members are not belittled for lacking a name.
- Before the war ended (pre-5.x endgame), characters would rarely have met anyone from outside Natlan; afterwards free travel and a visiting Natlanese are plausible.
- Do not give the Archon authority over a tribe's private customs.
### LARGE +
Colour: an NPC at Bayda Harbor describes Natlanese as energetic people with souls forged for battle who are not barbarians, fond of street graffiti, showing off fashionable trinkets, dancing and music. This is one character's view, not a rule. The Natlanese practice of shadow boxing, seen by the Inazuman traveller Shousen in his youth, inspired Inazuma's Shinryuu.

## examples
### LITE
Pacal and Amina show that chiefs can be young or reshape their role; Bennett shows what leaving cost; Kachina's case led to the Pilgrimage's suspension.
### STANDARD +
Examples tied to people: Xilonen (Name Engraver of the Children of Echoes), Kinich (current Malipo bearer, Scions of the Canopy), Mualani (Umoja, People of the Springs), Chasca (Vuka, peacemaker), Ororon (Bidii), Iansan (Uwezo), Varesa (Collective of Plenty family farm).

### STANDARD
Pacal leads the Children of Echoes, Wayna the Scions of the Canopy, Amina the People of the Springs (a young girl chosen by her grandfather and voted in by the elders), Mutota the Flower-Feather Clan, Biram the Masters of the Night-Wind and Acatl the Collective of Plenty. A chief oversees and may change tribal customs; the Pyro Archon cannot overrule them while the tribe agrees.

## entry: Natlan Leaving Custom
@keys: leave Natlan, Wayob protection, outside Natlan
@keys2: Natlan, Natlanese, tribe
@version: 5.0
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
During the 500-year Abyss war, Natlanese seldom left, because the Wayob protected them from Abyssal influence at home; leaving required the chief to seek the Wayob's permission, rarely granted. Outside, protection weakened. After the Night Kingdom was purified, travel became safe. See uncertainties on whether a chief's permission is still required.

### STANDARD
An Anemo tribe exiled at some past time. One part left Natlan and later settled in Mondstadt as the Muratans (their fate is unknown); the rest were banished to Tenochtzitoc, away from the other tribes, and all died in the Cataclysm. The Easybreeze Holiday Resort now stands there.
