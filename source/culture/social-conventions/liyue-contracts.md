## summary
### LITE
Contracts are the heart of Liyue. Its god, Morax (Rex Lapis, now Zhongli), is the God of Contracts, and the nation treats agreements as binding in everything from a merchant's invoice to the ancient laws he laid down. Those laws are policed by the Liyue Qixing. The god's own warning is that whoever breaks their word will suffer the Wrath of the Rock. Fairness is the point: in Zhongli's words, a contract that has lost fairness is only proof of deception.
### STANDARD +
The idea reaches beyond commerce. Morax made contracts with the adepti to defend Liyue Harbor, with the dragon Azhdaha to spare humanity, and finally with the Tsaritsa, the "contract to end all contracts" by which he gave up his Gnosis. Ordinary people live under the same logic through trade rules, the Qixing's law book and the habit of settling matters in explicit terms.
### LARGE +
Contracts in Liyue are both law and moral idea. The legal side is a very old rulebook that every Tianquan amends. The moral side is reciprocity, "equivalent exchange": a contract binds both parties, and exploiting its letter against its spirit fails the principle.

## practice
### LITE
Merchants live by deadlines, invoices and shipping destinations. Harbor trade runs under what the in-game description of Liyue Harbor calls countless contracts and regulations. The Qixing punish violators.
### STANDARD +
Morax's ancient laws touch every part of city life, from simple monetary exchange and agreements between merchants to the framework of the city itself. Outlanders, as Linlang remarks to Paimon, like to say that Mondstadt is to freedom as Liyue is to contracts.

Zhongli says the God of Contracts remembers the name of every person he meets and the sailing date of every ship, because holding so many contracts requires it, and adds that not every memory is a happy one (voice-over about the God of Contracts).
### LARGE +
Fairness is the test of a good contract. Zhongli says that asking people to follow a contract is only asking them to respect fairness, and that when fairness is lost the contract becomes proof of one's deception. He also says contracts cannot define friendship or measure sentiment, and that a Vision is itself a kind of contract: power comes with responsibility.

The law book is a living document. Each generation of Qixing interprets the old laws and adds subtle amendments to close loopholes. Merchants treat undiscovered loopholes as "permissible if unaddressed" and exploit them until the Qixing patch them. Zhongli's Character Story says the amendment book had reached 279 pages, and that Ningguang, the Tianquan who maintains it, is jokingly nicknamed the "Tailor of Liyue" for patching so quickly. The Qixing divide the work: the Tianquan keeps the law book and foreign relations, and per Keqing's voice-over the Yuheng's post is subject to over a hundred contractual obligations.
### ULTRA +
Beyond the state, small agreements govern daily trade, such as hired escorts. Independent Local Security Agencies guard goods on the roads, and the wiki notes that because they are privately run the Qixing have struggled to standardise them. At the Rite of Descension the local Linlang tells the Traveler that the Qixing are mere mortals while Rex Lapis is forever with Liyue, a sign that ordinary people still ground the whole system in the god's promise.

## regional_variation
### STANDARD
Outside Liyue, the nation is known for this ideal: Paimon tells the Traveler that Mondstadt is to freedom as Liyue is to contracts. People from all seven nations come to trade at Liyue Harbor, and in practice foreign merchants work under Liyue's rules there.
### LARGE +
Zhongli's voice-over on Furina respects that she made a contract with herself and held to it for over five centuries, a case from another nation that he judges by the same standard: justice that makes exacting demands on oneself, not only on others.

## scene_use
### STANDARD
Use contracts as the natural way for Liyue characters to settle trust: terms are stated, and a breach has a stated price. Merchants and officials can quote clauses, deadlines and amendments. Zhongli especially frames companionship and favours as a contract with fair terms (in his voice-overs he asks what name to sign under and notes who pays travel expenses). Do not make everyone legalistic; the depth of the principle shows mainly in how seriously a promise is taken once given.
### LARGE +
Good scene hooks: a loophole being exploited and the Qixing patching it; a merchant who has missed a deadline; an adept or old servant who still honours an ancient agreement; a foreigner who treats words lightly. Fairness is what to dramatise: a one-sided clause is shameful, not clever. Treat the Wrath of the Rock as a moral warning rather than an everyday mechanic.

## examples
### STANDARD
- Morax and the adepti: at the founding of Liyue Harbor he contracted the adepti to defend it.
- Morax and Azhdaha: the Geo dragon agreed not to harm humanity or Liyue, and to be sealed underground again if he did.
- Morax and the Tsaritsa: he handed over his Gnosis under the "contract to end all contracts" (revealed in A New Star Approaches).
### LARGE +
- Ganyu serves the Qixing as secretary in honour of her contract with Rex Lapis; its dating is disputed (see uncertainties).
- Xiao is one of the adepti the wiki lists among those who signed a contract with Morax to defend Liyue, and he is the one most actively hunting demons.
- Sal Flore: Zhongli set a contract for two treasure-seekers in Havria's ruins, to take turns claiming finds. Both broke it in turn and he dealt with each.
- Azhdaha's erosion: over time the dragon forgot Morax's face and the contract, then turned on the people he had sworn to spare, a case of a contract failing through memory loss rather than malice.

## entry: Wrath of the Rock
@keys: Wrath of the Rock, Rex Lapis' wrath, wrath of Rex Lapis
@version: 1.0
@spoiler: none
### STANDARD
The saying attributed to Rex Lapis is that the one who reneges on their words shall suffer the Wrath of the Rock. Zhongli's Character Story quotes it as the one law standing above all mortal amendments, and it is the sanction behind Liyue's contract culture.
### LARGE +
In the Chinese text the line is also a pun, since the word for breaking one's word (shiyan) sounds like the word for being swallowed by rock, according to the wiki's translation notes. The sources do not describe a mechanical curse; treat it as moral and legal authority.

## entry: Contract to End All Contracts
@keys: Contract to End All Contracts, contract with the Tsaritsa
@version: 1.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
In A New Star Approaches, Zhongli reveals that he staged his own apparent death at the Rite of Descension to test whether Liyue could do without him, and that he had contracted with the Tsaritsa to give up his Gnosis. Signora collected it. He calls it the contract to end all contracts.
### LARGE +
His Character Story 5 notes that losing his divine ability to defend Liyue is a heavy price, and that in an equivalent exchange the Cryo Archon must have wagered something to balance the scales. What she gave is not spelled out in the sources checked (see uncertainties).

## entry: The Book of Liyue's Laws
@keys: Qixing law book, Liyue's law book, Tailor of Liyue, Liyue loophole
@version: 1.0
@spoiler: none
### STANDARD
The Tianquan keeps the collected ancient laws of Morax and every amendment. Zhongli's Character Story gives the book as 279 pages and calls Ningguang the "Tailor of Liyue" for patching loopholes quickly.
### LARGE +
The Qixing as a whole interpret the law and punish violators, and other members help draft laws. For example, livelihood matters like water supply are the Yuheng's job, yet the Urban Areas Sanitation Act, against sabotage of water sources, was amended by the Tianquan.

## entry: Azhdaha's Contract
@keys: Azhdaha's contract, Azhdaha contract, Geo dragon contract
@version: 1.0
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Morax brought Azhdaha up from Nantianmen and gave him sight; the dragon agreed never to harm humanity or Liyue and to be sealed underground again if he did. Erosion later made him forget Morax and the contract.
### LARGE +
Azhdaha's later attacks forced Morax and the adepti to intervene and seal him. The sources frame this as the cost of a contract with an eroding being.

## entry: Adepti Contracts
@keys: adepti contract, adepti contracts, contract with Rex Lapis
@version: 1.0
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
At the founding of Liyue Harbor, Morax contracted the adepti to defend it while he continued fighting. Ganyu, Xiao, Madame Ping, Cloud Retainer, Moon Carver, Mountain Shaper and Yanfei's father are the adepti the wiki lists as still defending Liyue.
### LARGE +
Zhongli credits Liyue's prosperity not only to Rex Lapis but to the servants who honoured their end of the contract, and says Ganyu has served the longest. Accounts of when each contract began disagree (see uncertainties).
