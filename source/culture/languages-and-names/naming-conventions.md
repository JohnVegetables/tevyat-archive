## summary
### LITE
Names in Teyvat vary by nation and mostly reflect regional flavour: Mondstadt names sound German, Liyue names are Chinese-style, Inazuma places clan first, Fontaine names are French, and Natlan has the Ancient Name, an inherited title that is also a stone. In scenes, keep each nation's names consistent and avoid mixing styles inside one family.
### STANDARD +
The Fatui Harbingers have Italian commedia dell'arte names that double as their titles. Archons carry several names for the same person: Venti is Barbatos and Zhongli is Morax or Rex Lapis. Most of the real-language origins are an out-of-universe observation, not stated lore; the in-world conventions are Natlan's Ancient Names, Inazuma's clan names and a few remarks in books.
### LARGE +
Common patterns to follow: a Mondstadt family name such as Gunnhildr, Ragnvindr or Lawrence marks an old lineage; Inazuma's clans (Kamisato, Kujou, Hiiragi) lead commissions; Natlan characters have a personal name plus an Ancient Name; Snezhnayan figures take Russian-style given name plus patronymic.
### ULTRA +
Naming is also used for tone. Chosen or invented names exist alongside real ones, such as Fischl's self-chosen full name, and an official name does not always tell you what someone is called by friends.

## practice
### LITE
Use each nation's naming style for new characters; use titles and clan names as the world does; and never assume everyone has a family name.
### STANDARD +
Mondstadt: English with German touches. Titles and special names are often German-flavoured, for example Fischl's chosen full name Fischl von Luftschloss Narfidort. Families like the Gunnhildr, Lawrence, Ragnvindr and Imunlaukr clans hold the nation's old noble names.
Liyue: names have meaning in the local language, and the culture page notes that in-game books explain the significance of some. Qixing and Adepti titles are separate from personal names. Archon names: Morax, Rex Lapis and Zhongli all refer to the same person at different points.
Inazuma: people are written with the clan or family name first (Kamisato Ayaka, Kujou Sara). Clans are identified by a seal called a mon, and the Tri-Commission is led by the Kamisato, Kujou and Hiiragi clans.
Sumeru: some surnames add Pur (son of) in front of an ancestor's name; Anisa Purbiruni derives from the sage Biruni. Treat this as common, not universal.
Fontaine: French-style names such as Neuvillette, and French phrases like c'est la vie are said to be in use.
### LARGE +
Natlan: a person has a personal name plus, for some, an Ancient Name (tocaitl): a title tied to a quality, inherited from earlier bearers, granted by a tribe's Wayob or commissioned by the Pyro Archon, and recorded on a feather-shaped obsidian stone inscribed in the Ancient Teyvat Script. Natlan's names draw on several traditions, which matches the wiki's list of Mayan, Yoruba, Quechua and Polynesian sources.
Snezhnaya: Russian-style names such as Ioanna Ivanovna N. (a given name, a patronymic and an initial) and Yalyane Morevna (a patronymic-style second name). Names in Nod-Krai lean toward Finnish and Slavic sounds in the wiki's analysis.
The Fatui: the Harbingers carry Italian names from commedia dell'arte (Pantalone, Il Dottore, Pierro, Columbina, Arlecchino, Tartaglia, and so on) that serve both as their names and titles. The Frostmoon Scions of Nod-Krai know Columbina, the Moon Goddess, as Kuutar, and her full name is given as Columbina Hyposelenia; canon sources do not call Kuutar a birth name.
### ULTRA +
Khaenri'ah, per the wiki's list, drew on Germanic and Frankish sources (Chlothar, Caribert); Varka's name is listed as Old Persian, and Hyperborean names are tied to Old Persian too. All of these are out-of-universe observations.

## regional_variation
### STANDARD
Mondstadt: German or English touches; clans with family names. Liyue: Chinese-style given names, titles for Qixing and Adepti. Inazuma: clan name first, as in Kamisato Ayato. Sumeru: Persian, Arabic and Indian-sounding names, and Pur- surnames. Fontaine: French. Natlan: a mix of Mesoamerican, African, Andean and Polynesian-sounding names, and Ancient Names. Snezhnaya: Russian forms, with Uralic and Slavic elements for some figures. Nod-Krai: Finnish and Slavic sounds.
### LARGE +
The wiki's Real Life Languages table lists, by nation, the real languages seen in names: Japanese and Ainu for Inazuma, Arabic, Persian and Sanskrit for Sumeru, French and Celtic sources for Fontaine, Italian for the Harbingers and some Fontaine names, Russian and Uralic for Snezhnaya, and numerous Mayan, Bantu, Yoruba, Quechua and Polynesian sources for Natlan. These are community observations.

## scene_use
### LITE
Introduce a new Inazuman with the clan name first, a Natlan character possibly with an Ancient Name, and a Fatui agent with an Italian title. Do not give an in-world explanation for why the names look the way they do.
### STANDARD +
Use titles correctly. Archons and the Seven are known by several names; allow a character to know only some of them (Venti is just a bard to most Mondstadt citizens). Do not give every nation family names, and do not give an Ancient Name to a non-Natlanese character except the Traveler, who receives one at the end of the Natlan story.
### LARGE +
In Natlan scenes the Ancient Name is used as an honorific title ("bearer of ..."); a person can lose it if it is not passed on. In Inazuma scenes, a clan name signals loyalty and standing.

## examples
### STANDARD
Mondstadt: Fischl von Luftschloss Narfidort. Inazuma: Kamisato Ayaka, Kujou Sara. Sumeru: Anisa Purbiruni. Natlan: the Traveler's Ancient Name Tumaini. Fatui: Il Dottore; Columbina. Snezhnaya: Ioanna Ivanovna N.
### LARGE +
Archon names: Barbatos is Venti; Morax and Rex Lapis are Zhongli; Baal is the name of the Raiden Shogun.

## entry: Ancient Name
@keys: Ancient Name, Ancient Names, tocaitl, Name Engraver, obsidian Ancient Name
@version: 5.0
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
An Ancient Name (tocaitl) is a title borne by certain members of Natlan's six tribes, plus the obsidian feather-shaped stone that represents it. It is tied to a quality such as resilience, passed on from bearer to bearer, and carries the memory of its past bearers' deeds. The Wayob grant them to worthy tribe members; the Pyro Archon can commission a new one, forged by a Name Engraver such as Xilonen, who pays for it with part of her lifespan.
### LARGE +
Bearers were required to join the Pilgrimage of the Return of the Sacred Flame and could be sent to fight in the Night Warden Wars; a lost team lost its names. After the Wars ended, the requirement was removed. The Traveler receives the Ancient Name Tumaini after the Abyss invasion of Natlan.

## entry: Fatui Harbinger names
@keys: Harbinger names, commedia dell'arte, Fatui titles, Eleven Fatui Harbingers names
@keys2: Harbinger, Fatui
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
The Harbingers' names come from Italian commedia dell'arte stock characters, and in English the name and the title are often used interchangeably (for example The Captain or Capitano). The Chinese and Japanese versions translate the Italian name as the title. The names are what the Harbingers call each other in the Fatui.

## entry: Archon names
@keys: Archon names, Barbatos, Morax, Rex Lapis, Baal
@keys2: Archon, name
@spoiler: major
@version: 1.0
### STANDARD
Archons are known by a divine name plus the name they use among mortals, so Barbatos is Venti, Morax and Rex Lapis are Zhongli, and Baal is Raiden Shogun. Many characters know only one of these. The archive has a separate entry on Archon names.

## entry: Sumeru Pur- surnames
@keys: Purbiruni, Pursina, Pur surname
@keys2: Sumeru
@spoiler: none
### STANDARD
Per the wiki, some Sumeru surnames are formed by placing Pur (son of) in front of an ancestor's name; Anisa Purbiruni takes hers from the great sage Biruni of 500 years ago. The wiki guesses that Pursina follows the same pattern, which is unconfirmed.
