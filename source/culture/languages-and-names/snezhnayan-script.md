## summary
### LITE
The Snezhnayan Script is the lettering found on signs, shopfronts, books and documents in Snezhnaya and Nod-Krai. It looks very much like the common script seen elsewhere in Teyvat, with no separate upper and lower case and no punctuation, and it is used almost entirely to spell English and Russian words. Characters just read it as ordinary writing.
### STANDARD +
The name is the wiki's label rather than an in-world term. It is occasionally also used to write Latin. An item description for a bunker access card, obtained in the world quest Return to Sender, refers to neat Snezhnayan text printed on the card, which gives it a small in-game anchor.
### LARGE +
The wiki treats it as the writing system of Snezhnaya including Nod-Krai, and sets it apart from the common script mainly by region and by its Russian-flavoured vocabulary. It is a close relative in look, not a separate language.

### ULTRA +
The wiki notes that its transcriptions may differ from any official English rendering and that unclear letters are filled in by educated guesses, so treat individual sign readings as likely rather than certain.

## practice
### LITE
Seen on business signs and labels in Nod-Krai: Voynich Chamber of Commerce, Speranza, Nuts 'N' Nuts, the Flagship, Mead-Boy, and the Rossum Workshop sign, which reads Maszyna.
### STANDARD +
Deciphered readings from community work include a Northland Bank branch sign in Nod-Krai that appears to read BSK Bank (the wiki suggests this stands for the bank's Russian name), the Curatorium of Secrets sign reading Nefer's, and a protest sign near Omesov. The engraving at the Final Night Cemetery is a tribute to Chudomir the Aarnivalkea. The Nasha Town event signage lists categories such as roulette, candy, ticket and gadget.
### LARGE +
Printed items use it too. The Moon-Prayer Night Pamphlet carries a title reading Kuunrukous Night, the book A Brief History of the Pale Starborn has a cover reading V Lyudyakh (likely Russian for 'in people', per the wiki), and the Tier II Bunker Access Card shows the letters MAST and the name A. Z. Kotelnikov, matching the item text that names him as a senior maintenance technician. The statue of the Torchforger at Piramida carries a Latin line, one of the few non-English, non-Russian uses listed.

## regional_variation
### STANDARD
No dialect or regional variants are documented. It is tied to Snezhnaya and Nod-Krai, while the common script dominates Mondstadt, Liyue and Natlan.
### LARGE +
The wiki's listed examples come from Nod-Krai content and items. Do not assume a different hand or style between Snezhnaya proper and Nod-Krai.

## scene_use
### LITE
Use it as set dressing: a sign, label or pamphlet in neat northern lettering, with shop names and short Russian-flavoured words.
### STANDARD +
Do not invent a grammar, alphabet or sound values for it. If a character reads a sign, keep to what canon supports: it is Snezhnaya's ordinary writing, mostly English and Russian words, and a reader from another nation can generally follow it as they would any foreign shop sign.
