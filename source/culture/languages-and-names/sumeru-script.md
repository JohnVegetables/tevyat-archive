## summary
### LITE
The Sumeru script is the community's name for the lettering on Sumeru's shop signs, bulletin boards, books and monuments. It resembles Devanagari, with the horizontal line along the top of letters, and is built on the Teyvat alphabet. It mostly writes English, with a number of romanised Arabic, Persian, Sanskrit and Greek texts. Its in-world name is probably Vedanagari, mentioned in Alhaitham's 2024 birthday mail.
### STANDARD +
Vedanagari is tied to Sumeru City: in the mail, Alhaitham says Isak is quickly learning to read and write it and has begun to receive primers from the Akademiya.

## practice
### LITE
Signs use initials, like most Teyvat scripts: KB for Lambad's Tavern, MK for Menakeri's Treasure Shop, FRG for Sumeru General Goods, DS for the Bimarstan. Writing has no spaces, capitals or punctuation.
### STANDARD +
- Fans read abbreviations as initials of Persian or Arabic words: AG (a mirrored sign) for a blacksmith at Shamshir's Smithy, QK for a coffeehouse at Puspa Cafe, DS as a possible "house of healing". These are guesses.
- Most texts do not match the official English transcription where one exists.
### LARGE +
- Etchings in the Dar al-Shifa in the quest Abbas' Escape repeat warnings such as "Do not panic" and "God forgives all".
- Layla's and Faruzan's idle animations carry scrambled titles of medieval Arabic-language works on astronomy and algebra.
- Alhaitham's Elemental Burst shows Greek philosophical terms (OUSIA, ERGON, TELOS, HULE, THEORIA, KINOUN); a monument in the quest Monumental Study shows Sanskrit terms of non-cessation, non-origination and non-permanence.

## regional_variation
### LITE
It is the script of Sumeru's cities, especially Sumeru City. The desert ruins use a different writing, covered under culture.deshret-script, and the Aranara have their own words.
### STANDARD +
Naming: the wiki links Vedanagari to Sanskrit veda (knowledge) and nagari (of the city). The Chinese name means roughly academic-city script, echoing phrases used for Sumeru City in the English localisation ("City of Wisdom", "academy city").

### ULTRA +
The Isak mail is the only in-universe naming source read: it describes the script as one Isak is rapidly learning and for which the Akademiya supplies primers. The wiki adds that the Vedanagari name parallels Devanagari (deva, divine, versus veda, knowledge, both plus nagari), and that the Chinese name means academic-city script; these are out-of-universe naming observations. Many signs are mirrored or unclear, so even initials like AS on a tailor's sign remain unread.

## scene_use
### LITE
Use it for city colour: signs with a few letters, titles on book spines. A resident reads it without thinking; an outlander guesses.
### STANDARD +
- Mention Vedanagari by name only where a character plausibly would, such as an Akademiya scholar.
- Keep decoded signs short and plausible: a trade or a name.
- Do not invent grammar or a long translated text.

## examples
### STANDARD
KB on the tavern sign, MK on the treasure shop and AS on a tailor's sign illustrate the initials-only style.

## status
### STANDARD
As of 7.1 the script is unchanged and still unexplained in the sources read. No 6.x or 7.0-7.1 content found changes this.
