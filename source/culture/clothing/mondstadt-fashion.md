## summary
### LITE
Mondstadt dress is practical folk clothing. Men wear wide trousers tucked into tall boots, women wear dirndl-style dresses or blouses with bodices, and children dress as small versions of the adults. Distinct uniforms mark bards (green), maids, Church of Favonius sisters (black habits, rarely white) and the Knights of Favonius (black clothing under white plate armour with wing motifs).
### STANDARD +
Visual design in the wiki is likened to German-speaking regional folk dress, but in-world no text explains the styles; they are simply what Mondstadters wear.

## practice
### LITE
- Boys: short-sleeved tunics with short vests and short trousers.
- Men: wide trousers tucked into tall boots; several shirt and vest variants exist, including a distinct style for elderly men.
- Girls and women: dirndl-style dresses, often pink or blue for girls and red, orange or blue for women; other women's outfits use detached sleeves, bloomers and short boots.
### STANDARD +
- Women's variants: the first has a blouse and bodice in white-and-blue, white-and-green or two browns; the second is similar but has no bodice; the third is a double-breasted puff-sleeved jacket over a knee-length dress, either a gray jacket with a blue dress and short gloves or a dark purple jacket with a white dress and long gloves.
- Bards: men wear a green jacket over a white-and-green striped shirt with green trousers, white boots and a green cravat; women wear a white blouse and brown skirt under a red dress. Only one style per gender is known.
- Maids: Mondstadt's maid uniforms are admired abroad; in Liyue, Master Hui had such uniforms made for his servant Xi, who calls it a curious hobby of his.
- Church of Favonius: sisters wear a habit, mostly black, with a white version seen on only a few sisters.
### LARGE +
- Knights of Favonius, men: black long-sleeved shirt, riding trousers, gloves and brown leather shoulder pads under white plate armour, in plain or brass-accented versions. Wing motifs decorate the armour and the order's crest sits on the breastplate (on custom uniforms, around the midsection). The sword hangs sheathed at the left hip.
- Knights, women: black long-sleeved shirt, close-fitting trousers and a gray knee-length skirt under white plate armour, with greaves but no knee guards. The sword hangs at the right hip.
- Not every knight carries a sword and only a few carry a blowing horn. The standard sword resembles the Traveler's Handy Sword in shape.
- Kaeya is the noted exception to the crest on his uniform; the shield version of the crest is a shortened form of the uniform one.

### ULTRA +
Examples from the wiki's NPC survey: the pink girls' dress is worn by Flora and the blue by Tia; red women's dirndls by Margaret, orange by Myrana and blue by Donna; the gray-jacket outfit by Marjorie, Dr. Edith and Freki, and the dark purple one by Patchi the Curious and Esther. Bard outfits are shown on Alfred and Sansa, maid uniforms on Adelinde and Hillie, and the black and white habits on Grace and Vind. Knights' uniforms appear on Swan, Lawrence and Hertha. The wiki also records two crest versions: a full one on uniforms and a truncated one with the lower part missing on shields.

## regional_variation
### STANDARD
Dress here is Mondstadt-specific; visitors from Liyue, Inazuma and elsewhere wear their own styles. The maid uniform is the one local style noted as a foreign fancy.

## scene_use
### LITE
Describe townsfolk in vests, boots and dirndls, knights in white armour with wing motifs, bards in green and sisters in black habits when you need Mondstadt to feel recognisable at a glance.
### STANDARD +
Do not give meaning to habit colour, researcher outfits or rank markings that canon does not state. Keep descriptions of children's clothing plain and practical.

## examples
### STANDARD
- A bard in a green jacket and cravat tunes a lyre outside a tavern.
- A knight on patrol with plate armour over a black shirt, sword at the hip.
- A sister in a black habit crossing the Cathedral square.

## entry: Knights of Favonius Uniform
@keys: Knights of Favonius uniform, knight's uniform, Favonius armour, Favonius armor
### STANDARD
Standard Knights dress is a black shirt with trousers (men) or a gray skirt (women), worn under white plate armour decorated with wing motifs and the order's crest on the breastplate. Men carry swords on the left, women on the right. Individual knights may wear custom uniforms, with the crest placed lower on the midsection.

## entry: Church of Favonius Habit
@keys: Church of Favonius habit, sister's habit
### STANDARD
Sisters of the Church of Favonius wear a habit that is usually black; a white version has been seen on only a few sisters. Canon does not say what the colours signify, and the design for male clergy is not shown.
