## summary
### LITE
Natlanese dress is shaped by heat and by the warrior culture of the six tribes. Clothes are light and loose, many adults carry body markings, and warriors like to dress in imitation of their saurian companions. Gems are prized by the Children of Echoes, and masks belong to the wrestling tradition of the Collective of Plenty.
### STANDARD
Dress in Natlan responds to a hot climate and a culture where fighting is part of daily life. Loose, light clothing is common (a visitor from Fontaine finds even her lightest dress too warm), and many adults wear markings on their bodies. Warriors in particular favour looks that echo their saurian companions, a habit also reflected in the Sauroform Tribal Warrior enemy groups. Each of the six tribes has its own NPC attire in the game, but canon gives few written details of what separates one tribe's dress from another's; the clearest tribe-level traits are the Children of Echoes' love of gemstone decoration and the masks of the Collective of Plenty wrestlers.
### LARGE +
Because the wiki documents Natlanese fashion mostly through NPC model galleries, treat it as a visual vocabulary rather than a rulebook: bare midriffs, open chests, full coverage for children and the elderly, markings and tribe-specific details are patterns people have observed, not announced customs. Easybreeze Holiday Resort also has its own holiday-style NPC attire, distinct from the tribal mainland.

## practice
### LITE
- Light, loose clothing suits the heat.
- Many adults have body markings.
- Warriors often dress to resemble their saurians.
- Gems decorate tools, weapons and accessories among the Children of Echoes.
- Some Collective of Plenty wrestlers wear masks.
### STANDARD
**Climate.** Natlan is hot, and clothing reflects it. The Fontainian traveller Baret says she was warned to dress light before setting out, and that among the People of the Springs even her lightest dress is not enough.

**Saurian imitation.** Natlanese people, especially warriors, like to wear things that make them look like their saurian companions. The Sauroform Tribal Warrior enemy groups show this: the Adventurer Handbook says warriors who live and fight beside saurians have gradually taken on their traits. The Search Chronicle event (Burning Up!) shows a hat inspired by this fashion, and the event character Ranjit is the source of the remark that warriors dress in imitation of their saurians.

**Masks.** Some members of the Collective of Plenty (Teteocan) wear masks as part of a long-standing wrestling tradition. According to the wiki, when two masked wrestlers duel, the loser removes the mask and the winner keeps theirs, so the winner stays a figure of mystery who inspires challengers. The tribe also wrestles its saurians, the Tatankasaurus, as an old tradition.

**Body markings and coverage.** Many adults wear markings on their bodies. Community observation of NPC models adds that children and the elderly are usually fully covered, that men may wear loose clothing leaving the chest and midriff bare or full coverage like other nations, and that women usually show the midriff. None of this is stated in game text.

**Gems.** The Children of Echoes treat stone as one of their symbols, and some describe gemstones as a second layer of skin. Members often decorate tools, weapons and accessories with gems, which some regard as a sign of skill or status.
### LARGE +
**Ceremonial materials.** Obsidian is described as a very important ceremonial material in Natlan, and a character named Vichama says the Nanatzcayan (Children of Echoes) are its sole producers. This ties the tribe's stonework to ritual objects, though the sources read do not say it is worn as clothing.

**Easybreeze Holiday Resort.** The wiki lists separate NPC attire for the resort island, also called Tenochtzitoc, a sparsely inhabited island off the westernmost edge of Natlan. This is holiday-style dress, not a tribe's costume.

**Warrior types.** The Sauroform Tribal Warriors come from all six tribes, and each fights with its tribe's element. The saurian-crowned warriors are the clearest in-game view of the imitation fashion.
### ULTRA +
**NPC attire by tribe.** The wiki lists model galleries of boys', girls', men's, women's and, for several tribes, elderly attire for each of the six tribes plus the resort. The galleries show variety within each tribe (several men's and women's versions), but without text naming or explaining the garments, a roleplay description should stay at the level of tribal variation instead of inventing garment names.

**Character cards.** The Natlan Character Cards use a large circle with six tribal emblems around it. This is interface design, not clothing.

## regional_variation
### LITE
The six tribes dress differently in game, but few differences are described in text. Gems for the Children of Echoes; masks for the Collective of Plenty.
### STANDARD
- **Children of Echoes (Nanatzcayan):** miners and gem appraisers who decorate tools, weapons and accessories with gems.
- **Collective of Plenty (Teteocan):** known for strength and agriculture; some wrestlers wear masks, and wrestling is a tribal tradition.
- **Scions of the Canopy (Huitztlan):** explorers, messengers and hunters living with Yumkasaurs; no dress detail in the text read.
- **People of the Springs (Meztli):** guides and cartographers; Baret finds the heat too much for her lightest dress there.
- **Flower-Feather Clan (Tlalocan):** Qucusaur riders and aerial warriors; no written dress detail beyond their riding role.
- **Masters of the Night-Wind (Mictlan):** shamans and people close to the Night Kingdom; no written dress detail.
### LARGE +
The tribal emblems appear consistently on Natlan Character Cards, but the sources read give no list of tribal colours, patterns or garments. Do not assign colours to tribes unless canon is checked.

## scene_use
### LITE
Use heat, markings, saurian-inspired looks, gems and masks to give a Natlanese scene local colour, and keep garment detail light.
### STANDARD
- A traveller from another nation may be overheated and underprepared, as Baret is.
- A warrior might wear something that evokes their saurian companion's look.
- A Children of Echoes character might show off a gem-decorated tool or accessory as a point of pride.
- A Collective of Plenty wrestling match can involve masks: the loser unmasks, the winner stays masked.
- Avoid inventing named garments, tribal colours or meanings for body markings; canon does not give them.
