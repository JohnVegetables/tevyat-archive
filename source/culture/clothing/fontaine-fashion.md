## summary
### LITE
Fontainian clothing splits along the line between the city and everywhere else. In the Court of Fontaine people wear formal, decorated outfits, many recognisably Belle Epoque in spirit (cravats, waistcoats, lace blouses, hats), while Fleuve Cendre and areas outside the city favour simpler, practical wear. Most civilian clothes come in many colour schemes, mostly blues, purples, yellows, greens, reds and browns. Many trades have their own uniform, and the best-known name in high fashion is the Inazuman designer Chiori of Chioriya Boutique.
### STANDARD +
Style is a public matter in Fontaine: fashion has an annual showcase (Fontaine Fashion Week), emblems and uniforms mark the law, the Spina di Rosula, the Steambird and the Fortress of Meropide, and a customer, as Chiori puts it, pays attention not just to clothes but to who wears them.

## practice
### STANDARD
Everyday dress in the Court: men wear waistcoats with cravats or bow ties, in one common type a jabot and a longer coat; women wear decorated hats, lace blouses and straight skirts, or more fanciful pieces. Boys and girls have their own versions, and elderly people have distinct ones. Outfits come in several colour schemes, and a recolour sometimes changes a pattern slightly. Maid outfits in Fontaine match the Mondstadt style, making Fontaine the only other nation where that look is standard.
### LARGE +
Wiki observers compare the men's styles to the mid-to-late 19th century (waistcoat and cravat) and the late 18th to early 19th century (jabot, long coat), and the women's to the Belle Epoque, steampunk and lolita fashion. That is commentary on design rather than something the game states, so treat it as a guide to the look, not as lore. Working dogs wear outfits too.
### ULTRA +
Chiori says people copy the styles of those they look up to, so a designer's fortunes follow who is seen in her clothes. Her own breakthrough came when an ensemble of hers appeared on the front page of a Fontainian fashion review, according to her character profile.

## regional_variation
### STANDARD
The Court of Fontaine's clothing is more formal and decorated. Fleuve Cendre, the settlement beneath the city, and other areas outside it wear plainer, practical clothes. Divers need gear: a Fontainian without a Vision uses a large, bulky diving suit underwater, while Vision holders from any nation can go in without special equipment.
### LARGE +
Foreign fashion arrives through trade and travellers. Chiori, who came from Inazuma, builds her shop around imported Inazuman fabric delivered by the Komaniya Express courier Kirara. Notes on her desk say she plans to look into a new clothing trend in Natlan and to use Snezhnayan designs as a reference for winter wear.

## entry: Uniforms of Fontaine
@keys: Fontaine uniform, Fontainian uniforms, Garde uniform, Fontaine chef uniform, Fontaine sailor uniform, Aquabus uniform, Champion Duelist attire
@keys2: Fontaine, Fontainian
### STANDARD
Many roles have recognisable uniforms: gardes, Fortress of Meropide guards, chefs (men's and women's versions), sailors and sea captains (with separate captain's dress), Aquabus operators, Spina di Rosula attendants and Champion Duelists. Divers wear diving suits. Melusines who work as gardes or Aquabus guides wear uniforms cut for them.
### LARGE +
Champion Duelist attire is the outfit of the arena fighters whom the accused may face instead of a trial; Clorinde wears the women's version. The Special Security and Surveillance Patrol, led by Chevreuse, is an exception: its members are not required to wear a standard uniform, though the uniform cap is a kept tradition, since colleagues hold on to their caps when a member falls in the line of duty.

## entry: Gardes, Maison Gardiennage and Their Emblem
@keys: Maison Gardiennage emblem, Garde emblem, Garde hat, Marechaussee Phantom uniform
@keys2: Fontaine, Garde, Maison Gardiennage
### STANDARD
The emblem of Fontaine's law enforcement shows two Melusines with mermaid-style tails standing back to back beneath stylised scales. It is worn on the hats of the Gardes' uniform and by members of the Marechaussee Phantom. The scales alone symbolised the law at first; the Melusines were added later to commemorate Carole's sacrifice, according to the wiki's citation of the quest Ever-Surging Memories.
### LARGE +
Six Fontaine-wide emblems appear on Fontaine character cards: the Gardes, the Spina di Rosula, the Clockwork Meka gears (possibly the Research Institute), the Steambird, the Fortress of Meropide, and the scales of Furina, the former Hydro Archon.

## entry: Melusine Attire
@keys: Melusine clothing, Melusine attire, Melusine dress

### STANDARD
Melusines are short, bipedal creatures with large tails, mitten-like hands and stubby feet. They typically wear simple shirts or dresses with a small neck bandana or choker, and special uniforms when working as gardes or Aquabus guides.

## entry: Emblems and Motifs of Fontaine
@keys: Spina di Rosula emblem, Spina di Rosula uniform, Steambird emblem, Sir Arthur, Fortress of Meropide emblem, Cerberus emblem, fin motif
@keys2: Fontaine, Spina, Meropide
### STANDARD
The Spina di Rosula are commonly shown with an anchor bound in rose thorns above a ship's wheel; pieces of that imagery recur on members' clothes, such as an anchor on Callas's eyepatch and a ship's wheel on his lapel. Navia, however, says the logo is shaped like a heart, so it is unclear whether these are one design. The Steambird's emblem is its clockwork mascot Sir Arthur, a bird in a top hat, set inside a gear. The Fortress of Meropide's emblem is a three-headed wolf, which is also tied to its administrator Wriothesley.
### LARGE +
Clockwork Meka carry a figure-eight symbol of two gear parts and often a fin motif, seen on specialist meks' hats and knee guards and in the dress of Coppelia, the Icewind Suite piece. These are design vocabularies of Fontaine's machine culture rather than clothing, but they appear on garments and props.

## entry: Chioriya Boutique and the Fashion Trade
@keys: Chioriya Boutique, Chioriya, Uther
@keys2: Fontaine, Chiori, fashion
@version: 4.5
### STANDARD
Chioriya Boutique, in the Court of Fontaine, is run by the Inazuman designer Chiori, who wants her brand known across Teyvat. In the Cisoria Chapter (4.5) her former partner Uther tries to wreck her before Fashion Week with false claims about underground-factory fabric, a hired attack on Kirara and a hostile newspaper story. The Traveler helps expose his own illicit factory, and Chiori's show goes ahead with Navia, Kirara, Lyney and Lynette taking part. Letters on her table show the shop works with Emilie's perfumes, with Charlotte, and with the Special Patrol, to which she passes on what she overhears.
### LARGE +
Chiori says fashion means releasing something new, that shows give designers exposure, and that her own first Fashion Week was done on a shoestring: she tore up the Inazuman clothes she had brought and used the scraps for her entries. Uther argues that fashion is only public opinion and a farce; Chiori answers that a designer should focus on her own work and decide what fashion means. After the show a new sign goes up at the shop. Ready-to-wear is arranged by type and style so customers see something different each day.

## entry: Fontaine Fashion Week
@keys: Fontaine Fashion Week, Fashion Week
@keys2: Fontaine, Chiori, Chioriya
@version: 4.3
### STANDARD
Fontaine Fashion Week is a large recurring event, described in the Cisoria Chapter as held once a year and a major chance for designers to be seen; Chiori is not fond of it. One was due after the Roses and Muskets event (4.3). In 4.5 Chioriya's show opens with Navia and Kirara descending on an open umbrella, Lyney and Lynette following, and the brand introduced to the crowd before Chiori appears.
