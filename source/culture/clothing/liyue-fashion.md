## summary
### LITE
Liyue dress draws on Chinese clothing traditions such as the hanfu, qipao and tangzhuang, without following any single historical dynasty. Ordinary people wear vests, shirts and long or knee-length dresses; some groups wear recognisable uniforms, among them the Millelith, restaurant waitstaff, the Wangsheng Funeral Parlor and exorcists.
### STANDARD +
Canon never names Liyue's styles; the comparisons to real-world dress come from how the game's NPC designs look. In the sources read, clothing mainly signals age, trade and affiliation rather than a formal dress code.

## practice
### LITE
Boys and men wear shirts and vests; girls wear short dresses, and women in Liyue Harbor wear long dresses with qipao-like details. Elderly characters have their own cuts. Several jobs have uniforms.
### STANDARD +
Men's dress appears in variants for boys, adult men and older men. Some shirts are based on the tangzhuang, visible in the decoration across the chest. Suling is a customised variant: he wears the vest without a shirt beneath and binds his torso with bandages. Girls tend to wear short, knee-length dresses resembling the qipao. Women who live in Liyue Harbor wear long dresses with similar details. Madame Ping's clothes seem to be inspired by the hanfu, which the wiki reads as a nod to her great age.
### LARGE +
Uniforms mark jobs. The Millelith have separate soldier and officer uniforms; waitstaff at Liyue restaurants appear in matching outfits in brown or red; and the Wangsheng Funeral Parlor has male and female uniforms worn by undertakers such as Ferrylady and Meng. Exorcists and thaumaturges have recognisable attire of their own. Chongyun's default outfit, Pure Spirit, is described in game as a traditional thaumaturge's trappings: a symmetrical white hooded jacket with navy embroidery over a blue shirt and white pants with gold cuffs, with bandages at his right wrist and ankle. Shenhe, from the same clan, is shown in a variation of the same style. No ordinary NPC wears exorcist attire.
### ULTRA +
Equipment and clothing connect to Liyue Harbor's trades. The Millelith's equipment is supplied by the Blackcliff Forge, and Turenna said they were developing armor of ceramic blocks wedged between pieces of hardened fabric (see the limited-time entry below). Fabric work is one of the trade sectors that Liyue Harbor's character-card emblems represent, through the Silk Flower, which stands for fabric trades such as the Feiyun Commerce Guild as well as herbal and pharmaceutical trades such as Bubu Pharmacy.

## regional_variation
### STANDARD
The wiki's Fashion section covers Liyue Harbor and nearby NPCs. Harbor women are the group specifically said to wear long qipao-style dresses; the sources do not describe separate dress for the villages, the mountains or the adepti's abodes beyond individual characters.
### LARGE +
Characters show individual variation rather than regional uniforms: Xinyan's sleeves carry a triple-diamond pattern, and the back of Zhongli's coat carries a Geo Archon motif of a central diamond with smaller diamonds at the corners, the same symbol found at Yuehai Pavilion. Whether these patterns have an in-world meaning on clothing is not stated.

## motifs
@version: 1.0
### STANDARD
Liyue design favours geometric decoration. Meanders and diamonds recur across emblems, and a stylised cloud pattern (xiangyun, "auspicious clouds") is a traditional motif. The multi-diamond shapes are likely based on a traditional Chinese motif (fangsheng), per the wiki. Zhongli's coat and Xinyan's sleeves are the clothing examples.
### LARGE +
The character-card frames use Liyue emblems that are interface art rather than clothing: adepti-related characters (Ganyu, Qiqi, Xianyun, Xiao, Yanfei, Zhongli and Zibai) carry a dragon, stag, qilin and crane, while other Liyue characters carry trade emblems (Mora, Silk Flower, ship, crossed sword and polearm). They are noted only because they show how the game groups Liyue's trades.

## scene_use
### LITE
Use clothing to place a character quickly: a vest and rolled sleeves for a laborer, a long qipao-style dress for a Harbor woman, a hooded white jacket for an exorcist, a plain uniform for an undertaker at night. Avoid naming real-world dynasties in character dialogue.
### STANDARD +
Add small, concrete details: bandages on a wrist, a Vision worn on a belt, embroidered trim. Remember the custom Ferrylady describes: in Liyue it is generally not customary to speak to Wangsheng staff, who work silently around mourners at night, so a character in that uniform may be formal and reserved by habit.
### LARGE +
Do not claim fixed rank colours or insignia for the Millelith, and do not invent specific tailors, fabrics or guilds beyond those named in canon.

## examples
### STANDARD
Chongyun's Pure Spirit outfit (exorcist attire); Ferrylady and Meng (Wangsheng Funeral Parlor uniforms); Hongyu and Zhenhai (Millelith soldier and officer uniforms, shown as images on the wiki); Madame Ping (hanfu-inspired elderly dress); Suling (bandaged variation of the vest).

## entry: Character Outfits of Liyue
@keys: Pure Spirit, Orchid's Evening Gown, Opulent Splendor, Twilight Blossom
@version: 2.4
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
Game cosmetics rather than in-world clothing, but they show how Liyue designs are imagined. Chongyun's default Pure Spirit is described as a traditional thaumaturge's trappings. Ningguang's Orchid's Evening Gown is formal wear with a long cyan skirt and butterfly ornaments at the ankles. Keqing's Opulent Splendor is described as formal wear woven from Silk Flower petals and violetgrass dye, fusing Liyue and exotic styles. Ganyu's Twilight Blossom (4.4) is a festive outfit in nightshade black and cerulean blue with Qingxin-inspired ornaments.
### LARGE +
Orchid's Evening Gown was a free reward during the Fleeting Colors in Flight event in version 2.4 (limited then) and has been sold in the outfit shop since 2.5. Treat these as optional cosmetics in roleplay, not default dress.

## entry: Wangsheng Funeral Parlor Uniform
@keys: Wangsheng uniform, undertaker attire
@keys2: Wangsheng, Ferrylady, Meng
@version: 1.3
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
Undertakers of the Wangsheng Funeral Parlor, such as Ferrylady and Meng, wear a recognisable parlor uniform (the wiki shows male and female versions). The parlor is run by the Hu family and has served Liyue Harbor for generations. Ferrylady says it is generally not customary in Liyue to speak to its staff. See the faction entry for the parlor itself.

## entry: Millelith Uniform

### STANDARD
The Millelith have standard soldier and officer uniforms (wiki examples: Hongyu and Zhenhai). Their standard weapon is the White Tassel, and Blackcliff Forge supplies their equipment. Their uniform colours and insignia are not described in the sources read.
