## summary
### LITE
Sumeru dress marks people by role more than by region. Akademiya students wear uniforms carrying the Akademiya emblem, and each of the six Darshans has a designated colour. Forest Rangers wear green, with higher-ranking Forest Watchers in lighter green. Eremites wear red scarves; the Corps of Thirty, who guard Sumeru City, wear teal-green ones instead.
### STANDARD +
Ordinary townsfolk wear a range of everyday outfits for boys, men, girls, women and the elderly. The wiki shows them only as pictures, so canon gives no description of their cut or fabric.

## practice
### LITE
- Akademiya students: men's and women's uniforms bearing the Akademiya emblem. The Sage's attire (worn by Grand Sage Azar) is a separate style.
- Forest Rangers: uniforms for men and women; Forest Watchers wear lighter green than ordinary Rangers.
- Eremites: crimson-red scarves; low-ranking Eremites bear the Eremite Crest (a sun motif over two crossed scimitars in a triangle) on the scarf.
- Corps of Thirty: Eremite-style weapons and uniforms with teal-green scarves marked with a petal-like emblem similar to the Akademiya's.
### STANDARD +
- Darshan colours: Amurta green, Rtawahist blue, Spantamad red, Haravatat black, Vahumana yellow, Kshahrewar white. Each Darshan also has its own emblem, sometimes worn on students' hats.
- Eremite eyes: mid- and high-tier Eremites wear the sun motif on their eye coverings. A material description says the King of the Desert's people once covered their eyes because they dared not look on his brilliance. Other Eremite item descriptions refer to red silk and red brocade woven with gold thread.
- Rangers: Forest Watchers are the senior ranks; Gandharva Ville in Avidya Forest is their main outpost.
- Everyday clothing: the wiki shows seven men's variants and six women's variants, plus distinct boys', girls' and elderly styles.
### LARGE +
- Card symbolism: character-card backgrounds echo the Akademiya (six circles for the Darshans) for rainforest-affiliated characters and four circles (crocodile head, ram, bird, scorpion) for desert characters, matching King Deshret's four ministers; Cyno uses the rainforest design because he was raised in the Akademiya.
- King Deshret is commonly represented by the sun and the eye, and the sun motif appears on Eremite gear and on Primal Constructs.
- The Corps of Thirty keep the Eremite crest and colours on the banners near their barracks at the Citadel of Regzar.
### ULTRA +
Examples from the wiki's NPC survey: Hunayn, Ashk, Hawa and Setaria wear Akademiya student uniforms and Azar the Sage's attire; Ashpazi, Iraj, Shirin and Nasrin wear Forest Ranger uniforms; Mendas, Hisham, Hamasa, Masseira and Lunja wear Eremite attire; Shayan, Layth, Luxembarbo, Bakr and Dwarkanath wear Corps of Thirty uniforms. Named Eremite leaders (Jebrael, Azariq, Tadhla, Babel, Hidimbi) have individual outfits.

## regional_variation
### STANDARD
The rainforest and desert are marked chiefly by affiliation: Akademiya and Ranger uniforms belong to the rainforest, Eremite attire and the sun motif to the desert, while the Corps of Thirty combine desert-born Eremite gear with the Akademiya-like emblem of their employers. The Eremite Crest and scarf colours are the clearest visual divide.
### LARGE +
The Corps of Thirty are a long-standing Eremite brigade hired by the Akademiya, and their uniforms are near-identical to other Eremites' apart from the scarf colour and emblem. Alhaitham notes their poor relations with the Ayn Al-Ahmar, devout followers of King Deshret who denounce Greater Lord Rukkhadevata.

## scene_use
### LITE
Mention a student's Darshan colour, a ranger's green, a red Eremite scarf or the teal scarf of a Corps of Thirty guard to place a scene quickly in Sumeru.
### STANDARD +
Do not invent meaning for scarf colours beyond what canon gives, or specific fabrics and cuts for everyday dress. Keep the sun-and-eye motif tied to Deshret and the Eremites, not to ordinary citizens.

## entry: Akademiya Darshan Colours
@keys: Darshan colours, Darshan colors, Darshan emblem, Akademiya uniform
### STANDARD
The Akademiya designates a colour per Darshan: Amurta (biology, ecology, medicine) green; Rtawahist (astronomy, astrology) blue; Spantamad (alchemy, elemental science, ley line studies) red; Haravatat (linguistics, rune studies) black; Vahumana (history, sociology) yellow; Kshahrewar (architecture, mechanical skills) white. Each Darshan also has an emblem, and the Akademiya's own emblem is on student uniforms.

## entry: Eremite Crest
@keys: Eremite Crest, Eremite scarf, sun motif
@keys2: Sumeru, Eremites
### STANDARD
The Eremite Crest is a sun motif over two crossed scimitars within a triangle, seen on low-tier Eremites' red scarves and on Eremite buildings and banners. The sun motif alone appears on mid- and high-tier Eremites' eye coverings and on Primal Constructs.
