## summary
### LITE
Snezhnayan and Nod-Krai dress is best known by what marks a person's group. Ordinary people (boys, men, girls and women) wear civilian outfits; bounty hunters have their own look; Fatui members wear standard uniforms marked with the organisation's Palestar emblem; the Frostmoon Scions have moonchanter, priestess and acolyte attire; and the Lightkeepers of Nod-Krai wear ranked uniforms and carry Oath Lanterns.
### STANDARD +
Canon gives no written description of how Snezhnayans tailor clothes for the cold or what fabrics they use, so scenes should lean on group markers rather than invented detail.

## practice
### LITE
- Civilians: the wiki distinguishes three boys' outfits, seven men's styles, one girl's outfit and four women's styles, all worn by ordinary NPCs.
- Bounty hunters: three men's outfits and one woman's outfit form a category separate from civilian wear.
- Fatui: standard uniforms for men and for women, plus at least one alternate men's uniform.
### STANDARD +
- The Fatui emblem, the Palestar Banner, is a stylised filled-in Solomon's knot over a circular emblem. It is typically seen on Fatui masks and clothing and shows the wearer's affiliation.
- Frostmoon Scions: three tiers of dress are listed, moonchanter (worn by Lauma), priestess and acolyte. The Scions say the sacred Hyperborean bloodline carries antler crowns, so antlers may appear on those of that line.
- Lightkeepers: the Starshyna, Ratniki Squad Leaders and ordinary Ratniki (male and female) each have a listed uniform. The order's traditional insignia is a nightingale holding a burning wick in its beak, and every Ratnik must carry an Oath Lantern that lets other Lightkeepers and the Starshyna detect them.
### LARGE +
- Rank structure behind the Lightkeeper uniforms: the Starshyna leads, under them are Sergeant Majors (sometimes addressed as Captains), then Squad Leaders, then Ratniki. Nikita is the current Starshyna, having inherited the post from Sigurd.
- Nod-Krai is a mixed region where several groups coexist (Lightkeepers, Frostmoon Scions, the Voynich Guild in Nasha Town, and a Fatui presence), so a crowd there can be read by affiliation markers: Fatui emblem, Lightkeeper lantern, Scion dress.
### ULTRA +
NPC examples from the wiki gallery: boys' outfits on Teucer, Mikko and Jalo; men's on Ivanovich, Viljami, Pentti, Przybor, Hintsa, Nurmio and Reko; girl's on Maisie; women's on Perasa, Liouba, Mahla and Gorchakova; bounty hunters on Tapani, Terho, Leszek and Zinaida; Fatui uniforms on Vlad, Nadia (the Liyue NPC) and Polonsky; Scions dress on Lauma, Ehrnrooth and Verna; Lightkeeper uniforms on Nikita, Olav, Sukhovykh and Zvoni.

## regional_variation
### STANDARD
Mainland Snezhnaya and Nod-Krai are treated together on the source page. Nod-Krai is described as warmer and more independent, and its dress is tied more to the group (Lightkeeper, Scion, Voynich, Fatui) than to a stated regional costume. Whether civilian dress differs between the two is not stated.
### LARGE +
Character Cards for Snezhnayan figures use faction emblems (Fatui, Druzhna, Snowland Fae, the Korolevskiy Theater, Zapolyarny Palace). That is a design convention of the game's UI rather than clothing.

## scene_use
### LITE
Signal affiliation with one or two concrete markers: a Fatui mask with the Palestar emblem, a Lightkeeper's Oath Lantern, a Scion's antler crown, a bounty hunter's distinct kit.
### STANDARD +
Do not invent fabrics, colours or cold-weather gear that canon does not describe. Keep children's clothing plain and practical. Do not give the Lightkeeper insignia to Fatui or vice versa, and do not claim all Frostmoon Scions have antlers; the trait belongs to the pure Hyperborean bloodline.

## entry: Palestar Banner
@keys: Palestar Banner, Palestar, Fatui emblem, Fatui insignia
### STANDARD
The Palestar Banner is the Fatui's emblem: a stylised, filled-in Solomon's knot on top of another circular emblem. It is typically seen on Fatui masks and clothing and shows who the wearer serves.

## entry: Lightkeeper Uniform and Oath Lantern
@keys: Lightkeeper uniform, Oath Lantern, Ratnik uniform, Ratniki, nightingale insignia
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Lightkeepers (Nod-Krai's defenders against the Wild Hunt) have separate uniforms for the Starshyna, Squad Leaders and the Ratniki rank and file, male and female. Their traditional insignia is a nightingale holding a burning wick in its beak. Every Ratnik must carry an Oath Lantern so other Lightkeepers and the Starshyna can detect them.

## entry: Frostmoon Scions Dress
@keys: Frostmoon Scions attire, Moonchanter attire, Scion priestess, antler crown
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
The Frostmoon Scions of Hiisi Island have named tiers of dress: moonchanter (Lauma's role), priestess and acolyte. The Scions hold that the sacred Hyperborean bloodline carries silver blood, kuuvahki sensitivity, antler crowns said to be a gift of the Frost Moon, and the ability to speak with all living things. The wiki records the dress tiers without describing garments.
