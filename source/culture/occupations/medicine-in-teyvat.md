## summary
### LITE
Medicine in Teyvat is a patchwork rather than one system. Herbal remedies are the everyday base, sold by pharmacists and herbalists, most famously at Liyue's Bubu Pharmacy. Sumeru runs an Akademiya hospital, the Bimarstan, offering free care. Surgery, therapy and medication all exist. Elemental power is also used for healing, Hydro and Anemo most visibly: Barbara and Jean are known healers. Practice differs a great deal between nations.
### STANDARD +
There is no single standard of care. Liyue leans on prescribed herbal blends, Sumeru on institutional and scholarly treatment, and Fontaine on specialist care in a place like the Fortress of Meropide, where a Melusine, Sigewinne, is head nurse. Medicine ties into politics too: the chronic illness Eleazar was a Sumeru matter involving the Akademiya, the Fatui and the Withering.

## practice
### LITE
Common treatments are plant-based: bitter herbs, teas, balms and brews prepared to a prescription. Minor injuries get bandages and wound dressings; dislocations are set by hand. Healers with elemental power can mend injuries directly.
### STANDARD +
Ingredients are specific. Qingxin, Glaze Lily, Violetgrass, Lotus Head and Mint appear in Liyue remedies; Windwheel Aster and Wolfhook are used in Mondstadt and Inazuma. Herbal knowledge is practical and local. Tighnari, a Sumeru forest ranger, looks for antidotes to poisonous fungi, and Lisa mentions Calla Lilies as an antidote. Some remedies are held to be odd or unconventional: ground Onikabuto as a wound dressing is called a "weird" remedy, while Wolfhook is the accepted method.

Beyond herbs: syringes and injected medicines are referenced, dementia is understood as a change in neural function (a record in the Bimarstan), and mental illness is treated with therapy and medication. In Fontaine's Loneliness Syndrome, a therapist uses "Guidance Therapy", walking a patient through their delusions to a point where they can process what set off the illness; a drug labelled Solamenaline is also associated with it.
### LARGE +
Elemental healing is common but not universal. Hydro is the element most often paired with restoration (Barbara is a known healer in Mondstadt), though Jean has used Anemo to heal as well. Elemental control is also used clinically: in Sumeru, symptoms of Eleazar were controlled by reducing the elemental content of a patient's body. That was an experimental treatment, not common practice.

Procedures include surgery (amputations done aboard the Crux Fleet's ships by a physician named Yinxing; a surgery mentioned for facial ulcers in Fontaine), autopsy with toxicological and forensic reasoning (as in Inazuma's Long-Sealed Mystery and in Sumeru's Zandik's Legacy), organ transplants, skin grafts and immune-system restoration. The last examples come from Fatui records: Pantalone is said to have had lung and liver transplants, and clinical autopsy data on Dottore went into anti-aging treatments. How accessible such procedures are to ordinary people is unknown.
### ULTRA +
Some Fatui research relates to psychotropic compounds that can stimulate or sedate, and to neurostimulation that shifts mood. Dottore's work is the source of those references. They show knowledge of neurological medicine in the Fatui, not common care.

## regional_variation
### STANDARD
Liyue: pharmacy and herbalism. The Bubu Pharmacy on Yujing Terrace in Liyue Harbor is called the finest in Liyue and is busy during and after the Lantern Rite. Baizhu is its owner, with the staff Qiqi and the herb-seller Herbalist Gui. Its name means roughly "not divining", which fans read as favouring method over fortune-telling.

Sumeru: institutional care. The Bimarstan in Sumeru City is an Akademiya-run hospital giving free healthcare to residents; its name echoes the historical Islamic-world term for a hospital.

Fontaine: the Fortress of Meropide has a Melusine head nurse, Sigewinne, with a Romaritime Flower tea for exhaustion.

Mondstadt and Inazuma: folk herbal use (Windwheel Aster, Wolfhook, Naku Weed) and healers like Barbara and Jean; Inazuma's Naku Weed reduces wound swelling.
### LARGE +
Liyue herbs, in the 3.6 Herbal Remedies web event, are Baizhu's prescriptions: Uncle Tian's Relieving Pellets, Master Lu's Muscle Relief Ointment, Hongdou's chronic-illness relief, Xiangling's Stomach Soothing Brew, Keqing's Invigorating Balm and Yelan's Wound Salve. Nothing shows them used outside Liyue. Sumeru remedies in quests include a stomach medicine of Ajilenakh Nut and Henna Berry, a headache medicine of Mint and Henna Berry, and a vision medicine using Scarab and Starshroom; Sumeru Rose is used for heatstroke and stomachaches.

## scene_use
### STANDARD
In a scene, medicine works best as texture: a bitter tea pressed on a tired traveller, a pharmacist asking about symptoms before naming a remedy, a hospital that charges nothing, a knight healing with Anemo. Characters should name regionally real ingredients rather than generic "potions". Keep certainty low: most Teyvat doctors prescribe and watch, rather than guarantee cures.
### LARGE +
Do not treat any elemental healer as a universal medic. Eleazar had no true cure before it vanished, so a Sumeru character should speak of it as controllable but incurable, and as gone since the Withering was cleansed from Irminsul.

## examples
### STANDARD
Baizhu's treatment for Anna combines Glaze Lily with Violetgrass and Starconch for fast recovery; the recipe finally used instead takes longer to work and uses a Dendro-processed berry with a Wind-Caressed Aster and Thick Hydro Slime Condensate. A fallback recipe boils Frog skins and Lizard Tails with a few drops of Changsheng's venom.
### LARGE +
Sumeru's Dunyarzad and Collei were both born with Eleazar. Collei's Fatui treatment used a god's remains and left her traumatised before Cyno sealed it; Tighnari later cared for her. Nilotpala Lotus helps patients regain energy.

## entry: Eleazar and the Eleazar Hospital
@keys: Eleazar, Eleazar Hospital, Dar al-Shifa hospital
@keys2: Sumeru, Akademiya, Withering, illness
@spoiler: major
@version: 3.0
### STANDARD
Eleazar was a chronic illness unique to Sumeru and a bodily expression of the Withering. It showed as dark, hardened scales and numbness, advancing to fatigue, ulcers, necrosis and paralysis, and sometimes coma. Patients have a higher elemental content than normal, and controlling that content eased symptoms. It had no cure until the tainted consciousness of Greater Lord Rukkhadevata was cleansed from Irminsul in the Archon Quest; then every symptom disappeared and patients recovered in days. Cases had been rising in Sumeru City, where sufferers were urged to visit the Bimarstan.
### LARGE +
The Akademiya built a hospital in Dar al-Shifa in the Great Red Sand to study Eleazar. A doctor whose name is struck from the records (implied to be Zandik) found a treatment that removed a late-stage patient's symptoms, but at a terrible price: the farmer Abbas survived by being given body parts taken from three other patients. Appalled, he tunnelled out of the hospital. The Akademiya later abandoned the site and barred access to its ruins. Separately, Fatui staff lured the parents of afflicted children with a promised cure; Collei's parents were among them. The Sumeru forest was said to mitigate symptoms, though the Withering made things worse.

## entry: Bubu Pharmacy
@keys: Bubu Pharmacy, Bubu-lu
@keys2: Liyue, Baizhu, Qiqi, Herbalist Gui
@version: 1.0
### STANDARD
The Bubu Pharmacy on Yujing Terrace in Liyue Harbor is called the finest pharmacy in Liyue. Its owner is Baizhu (Dendro), the staff are Qiqi and Herbalist Gui, who sells herbs from the shop; the back room is living quarters and the roof holds the Yujing Terrace viewpoint. Business booms around the Lantern Rite.

## entry: Bimarstan
@keys: Bimarstan
@keys2: Sumeru, hospital, Akademiya
@version: 3.0
### STANDARD
The Bimarstan in Sumeru City is an Akademiya-run hospital that gives free healthcare to all Sumeru residents. Its staff includes the doctor Zakariya and the intern doctors Golshan and Tamara. A record there shows the memory disease of the elderly scholar Giyath Khayyam, and the record says a sage, Siman Farrokhzad, would see to his treatment.

## entry: Sigewinne and Melusine Nursing
@keys: Sigewinne, Melusine head nurse, Fortress of Meropide nurse
@spoiler: minor
@version: 4.0
### STANDARD
Sigewinne is the resident head nurse of the Fortress of Meropide, a Melusine who took a human form to save a child she befriended, breaking Fontaine law and being convicted. She is a playable Hydro character (released 4.7). A character demo shows her using the bitter Romaritime Flower, a Fontaine remedy for exhaustion.
