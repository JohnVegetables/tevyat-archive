## summary
### LITE
Exorcists, also called thaumaturges, are Liyue practitioners who have sworn to cleanse the land of ghosts, spirits and demons. They combine spells and talismans with martial arts, and most come from families that pass the trade down. Chongyun, heir of the Tianheng Thaumaturges, is the best-known; Shenhe, born into a branch of the same clan but trained by an adeptus, is called by him an adept exorcist.
### STANDARD +
Canon names only a few groups: the Tianheng Thaumaturges (Chongyun's clan), the family of Yelan, and the Lan clan of Chenyu Vale. Exorcists are mortals, distinct from the adepti, though their techniques resemble adepti arts and some exorcists have had adepti friends.

## practice
### LITE
Exorcism is learned inside families and treated as a duty to protect Liyue. Methods mix mantras, talismans and sword work; Chongyun holds that thaumaturgy plus martial arts is the true way.
### STANDARD +
Clans keep records. The Tianheng Thaumaturges hold generational books of the evil spirits they have defeated, and Chongyun's illustrations in them are imprecise because he has never met a spirit. A mantra attributed to the clan runs "Heart be pure, evil be erased. Mind be purged, world be saved", which Chongyun admits he tends to forget the end of. Timing and weather matter to him: he treats noon as the peak of spiritual energy and rain as the purest water, and he trusts thunder to make evil cower.

Tools are individual. Chongyun fights with an ice-made spirit blade; Shenhe's mother carried an exorcism dagger that Shenhe later inherited. Exorcists also perform prayer rituals: on Chongyun's birthday he prays with clan elders on Mt. Tianheng.

Work is commission-based and often anticlimactic. Rumours of hauntings frequently turn out false, and Chongyun usually charges only a small fee. Haunted houses are typical jobs (in one tale he cleared a wealthy woman's home in Liyue Harbor simply by sitting in it).
### LARGE +
The craft overlaps with Liyue's other mystic traditions. Adeptus arts include talismans (sigils) that store adeptal power, and Qimen arts, practised by the Lan clan, cover fortune-telling, illusion-making and warding off evil. The Fantastic Compass shows another overlap: made by the adeptus Sea Gazer to seal monsters, it was given to a mortal thaumaturge friend, and the two later used it together against great demons in the mountains.

Whether exorcism needs a Vision is open. Everyone seen casting in play has one, yet Yelan says her family has practised magic for generations and built catalysts only they can use, which implies the art is not limited to Vision-bearers.
### ULTRA +
Limits and failure modes recur in character material: Chongyun's pure yang constitution (a "congenital positivity" in the official introduction) repels evil so strongly that he has performed hundreds of exorcisms without ever seeing a ghost. He regards this as an unearned crutch and trains in orthodox technique to prove himself. This is an individual quirk, not a general trait of exorcists.

## regional_variation
### LITE
Exorcism is a Liyue profession. Other nations have their own supernatural arts under other names; none is called exorcism in canon.
### STANDARD +
Within Liyue, practice differs by clan. The Tianheng Thaumaturges (Mt. Tianheng) are shown through Chongyun with mantras, talismans, an ice-made spirit blade and generational records of defeated spirits. The Lan clan of Chenyu Vale, descended from the brothers of Lan Junming (the Wind Reader among the Eight Adepts), practise Qimen arts, using them for duty or hobby crafts, and serve as consultants to the Moonchase Festival at Yilong Wharf.
### LARGE +
In Inazuma, onmyoudou was created 500 years ago by Kamuna Harunosuke, who combined Liyue adepti techniques with an art he learned from the Yougou Tengu. Yae Miko's yakan kitsune spirits are implied to be shikigami of that tradition. This is related to, but not described in canon as, exorcism.

## scene_use
### LITE
Use exorcists as polite local specialists called in for hauntings, who often find the problem smaller than the rumour.
### STANDARD +
A scene might feature a hired exorcist reading the room for yin and yang imbalance, setting talismans, and chanting a mantra before facing a haunting. Keep results grounded: many cases are mundane or fraudulent, and Liyue folk may respect exorcists while also doubting them. Chongyun admires Xiao as Conqueror of Demons but resents Xiao's remarks that exorcists' repertoire is lacking and their power trivial.
### LARGE +
Tone guidance: exorcists take their oath seriously, hold family tradition dear, and speak in measured, courteous terms. A lineage story (a clan reunion, a missing relative, a catalyst heirloom) fits better than a free-standing monster hunt.

## examples
### LITE
Chongyun, who sits motionless in a dark corner until spirits flee, is the standard example.
### STANDARD +
- Chongyun: heir of the Tianheng Thaumaturges; a pure yang spirit makes spirits flee, but he wants to earn the title by technique.
- Shenhe: from a Tianheng branch family, never taught exorcism by her parents, later the disciple of Cloud Retainer; her adepti techniques resemble exorcist ones.
- Yelan: descendant of the exorcist brothers Boyang and Rongzhao; she is not known to practise the family trade.
- Lan Yan and Lan Ying: Lan clan members who make rattan Warding Figures with built-in Qimen mechanisms.

## entry: Tianheng Thaumaturges
@keys: Tianheng Thaumaturges, Tianheng Thaumaturge, Tianheng clan
@version: 1.0
### STANDARD
The Tianheng Thaumaturges are an exorcist clan of Liyue with many exorcists in its ranks, associated with Mt. Tianheng. Chongyun is a clan heir; Shenhe, his aunt, came from a branch family. The clan elders were delighted to learn that Shenhe was alive and prepared gifts and an auspicious day for a reunion, though she is hard to find. Yuandai, a crane disciple of Cloud Retainer who lived as a human, is recorded as having fallen in love with an exorcist from Mt. Tianheng and had a son; her granddaughter is Shuyu. The clan keeps generational records of the spirits it has defeated.
### LARGE +
Shenhe's parents belong to the clan's recent history. Her mother died when Shenhe was small, and her father, maddened, tried to sacrifice her to an evil being when she was about six, so Shenhe was never trained by the clan.

## entry: Yelan's Ancestors and the Fantastic Compass
@keys: Fantastic Compass, Boyang, Rongzhao
@version: 2.7
### STANDARD
The Fantastic Compass was made by the adeptus Sea Gazer to seal monsters, and then passed to a mortal thaumaturge friend. Roughly 500 years ago, Yelan's ancestors Boyang and Rongzhao, both exorcists, brought it to the battle at the Chasm against the Abyss alongside the Yaksha Bosacius. Boyang and other Millelith lured monsters into a space that could suppress the Abyss and sealed them in with the compass, while Rongzhao helped from outside (the Fantastic Compass page credits Boyang and Bosacius with the seal). Rongzhao returned alone and driven mad by Bosacius' karma. Yelan's clan survived and developed catalysts only they can operate.

## entry: Lan Clan and Qimen Arts
@keys: Lan clan, Lan family, Qimen arts, Qimen Art
@version: 4.4
### STANDARD
The Lan clan of Chenyu Vale descends from the brothers of Lan Junming, the Wind Reader of the Eight Adepts, who taught them Qimen arts after returning from wandering. He encouraged pursuing the arts both as hobby and duty and also taught a Soul-Binding Art that was later outlawed as violating the natural order. Over centuries the clan became integral to Chenyu Vale's commissions and the Moonchase Festival. Some members make silver ornaments of the Jade-Plumed Silverwing. Lan Yu is the current head; Lan Yan, Lan Ying, Aunt Lan and Uncle Lan are other known members.

## entry: Lan Ying and the Warding Figures
@keys: Lan Ying, Warding Figure, Warding Figures
@version: 7.1
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
During the Silverwing in Pursuit of the Moon event (limited-time, 7.1), Lan Ying, Lan Yan's second cousin, explained that clan thaumaturges are too busy with large spirits to clear the small clumps of evil energy that can sicken ordinary people. With Lan Yan she devised rattan Warding Figures containing a Qimen core that absorbs and seals such energy.

## entry: Chongyun's Exorcism Creed
@keys: Chongyun
@keys2: exorcist, exorcism, pure yang, thaumaturgy
@version: 1.0
### STANDARD
Chongyun considers exorcists protectors of Liyue since ancient times, with techniques handed down from generation to generation. He opposes using his inborn yang energy as a substitute for technique, and in his Hangout Event he follows rumours that mostly prove baseless. He thinks Xiao is disparaging toward exorcists and finds the Wangsheng Funeral Parlor's easy smile irritating, though he praises the parlor's integrity and respects Xiao as the Conqueror of Demons.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 exorcists remain active in Liyue. The Lan clan featured in the Silverwing in Pursuit of the Moon event (7.1, limited-time). No exorcist-related content in Nod-Krai or Snezhnaya content (6.x to 7.1) was found.
