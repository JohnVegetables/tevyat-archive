## summary
### LITE
The Iridescence Tour is a music festival that originated in Fontaine. Its organizers, chiefly the travelling musician Dvorak, want to hold it in every nation of Teyvat. It has a poor record on the road: stagings planned for Inazuma and again for the Golden Apple Archipelago were cancelled, leaving the rocker Xinyan stood up twice. It finally happened in Liyue as the Lantern Rite Grand Concert and on Watatsumi Island as Arataki Itto's own rock party. Everything shown of it is limited-time event story.
### STANDARD +
Dvorak, one of its main organizers, calls it one of the biggest music festivals in Fontaine, though outside Fontaine hardly anyone has heard of it. In the sources it works as a recurring opportunity for performers: getting stage time is said to carry a musician's work far and wide. Because the tour is always seen through Dvorak's attempts to bring it somewhere new, it tends to appear as a half-promised, chaotic and warm-hearted production rather than a polished institution.
### LARGE +
It is also a thread linking several stories. Xinyan, who first signed up for it, ends up opening the Liyue concert alongside Hu Tao, and Dvorak reappears in Inazuma as Itto's co-host. Each staging is adapted to the host nation: opera singer Yun Jin and the Liyue Qixing's officials in Liyue, and the Arataki Gang's dog-and-Onikabuto rock theme in Inazuma.

## practice
### LITE
It is a touring festival of live music with a changing line-up. The organizers recruit performers, secure a venue and funding, plan rehearsals and a programme, then stage a show. In practice the schedule, funding and local approvals are the weak points.
### STANDARD +
Dvorak says staging a festival takes a lot of funding and staff, and that he had never worked with the Liyue authorities before. When he first advertised a Liyue stop he was approached by few; Xinyan was one of the few to give him time. He promotes the tour while travelling Teyvat, though he also travels for a private reason: a family legend of an ancestor saved from drowning by a woman playing a haunting tune.
### LARGE +
The tour's pattern is that it is assembled locally. In Liyue, Ganyu declines to replace or cancel any existing festival programme and instead proposes to the Qixing that the Iridescence Tour show become the final act of that year's celebrations, then discusses musician numbers, catering and lodging with Dvorak. In Inazuma, Itto helps find a venue and the Arataki Gang's local standing supports the project, but the gang's attention drifts to a stray-dog matter so Kuki Shinobu delivers the invitations while the Traveler works through Dvorak's checklist. Dvorak sometimes also adapts material to local taste: he reworked his song for the Inazuma show so it suits the Arataki Gang and light-novel fans.
### ULTRA +
Dvorak gives the Watatsumi planning a five-day schedule leading to the party, and the event includes a Jam Zone stocked with scores and instruments from several regions, including a horn he carried from Fontaine. He also treats the stage as meaningful rather than simply commercial: he insists the key quality is the spirit of the music, hearing "your own story" in it, and declines to give an official definition of rock 'n' roll.

## regional_variation
### STANDARD
The sources show three host settings. In Inazuma (2.2) the tour was to run at Inazuma City but was cancelled; in the Golden Apple Archipelago (2.8) a further plan also fell through. In Liyue (3.4) it was held as the Lantern Rite Grand Concert, a special performance joining the festival. In Inazuma again (4.6) it became the Iridescent Arataki Rockin' for Life Tour de Force of Awesomeness on Watatsumi Island. No source read shows a staging in Fontaine itself, Mondstadt, Sumeru, Natlan, Snezhnaya or Nod-Krai.
### LARGE +
Xinyan complains that she signed for the Inazuma date and her travel permit arranged through the organizers was lost when it was cancelled; Beidou had ferried her there early to prepare. Liyue's date works differently because it plugs into an existing festival and local authorities.

## scene_use
### LITE
Use it as a hook for music-minded characters: an earnest touring organizer, a call for performers, a rehearsal, or an apology for a cancellation. Keep it small-scale and good-humoured.
### STANDARD
Good beats are Dvorak anxiously rehearsing, Xinyan ready to be an opening act, and an organizer who keeps running into bad luck. Do not claim the tour is a huge, well-oiled international operation: canon shows it as widely advertised but patchily delivered. Do not invent further tour dates, line-ups or venues.
### LARGE +
Characters may know the tour differently. Xinyan, Hu Tao and Yun Jin know the Liyue concert first-hand; Ganyu knows it as a proposal she took to the Qixing; Itto thinks of it as the inspiration for his rock party. Most ordinary Liyue residents would not know it before 3.4.

## examples
### STANDARD
The Liyue concert opened with Xinyan and Hu Tao performing a song called "The Blaze Lilies", rehearsed at Wangsheng Funeral Parlor with Yun Jin as a mentor for lyrics. On Watatsumi Island, Itto and Paimon performed a new song written for the night, "I Got Reincarnated as an Onikabuto... And Flipped the World Upside Down". Dvorak calls the Inazuma performance soft rock.
### LARGE +
Hu Tao also composes a small limerick about the touring musician going door to door looking for acts to audition. This and the song titles are event-only material.

## entry: Dvorak
@keys: Dvorak
@keys2: Iridescence Tour, musician, Fontaine, organizer
@version: 3.4
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
Dvorak is a musician from Fontaine and one of the main organizers of the Iridescence Tour, a travelling promoter of the festival and, in the 3.4 and 4.6 events, an event-only NPC. He wears a maroon coat and top hat with musical motifs, a gold monocle and carries a black acoustic guitar. He is polite, easily flustered and anxious about performances, and is rather clumsy: he got lost in Liyue's scenery and slipped into water before Yaoyao helped him. He was in Ritou during the Inazuma war, per his own account in 4.6. His name evokes the composer Dvorak per the wiki's trivia.
### LARGE +
His personal quest is to trace the descendants of a woman his ancestor met: a traveller nearly drowned in a lake, rescued and led to shore after hearing a wonderful tune, who followed the woman a dozen paces without ever catching up. The family tale, "The Lady Overlooking the Lake", became a Fontaine legend. After praying at Mondstadt's cathedral and following leaves blown west, he concluded the story belonged to Liyue. With Ganyu and the Traveler, he learned the player was an adeptus remembering a late friend, now an elderly woman. He decided to thank her in person after the concert.

## entry: The Exquisite Night Chimes

### STANDARD
The 3.4 Lantern Rite flagship event (19 January to 6 February 2023). The Traveler and Paimon meet Dvorak in Liyue Harbor and help him find the adeptus from his ancestor's story. Xinyan persuades him to try a Lantern Rite show; Ganyu proposes it to the Qixing as a closing act; the event ends with the Iridescence Tour: Lantern Rite Grand Concert. Event gameplay includes Radiant Sparks, Paper Theater, Vigilance at Sea and Behind the Scenes. This is limited-time content.
### LARGE +
Xinyan insists that nobody else should perform the opening act and Dvorak says he will judge it by the strictest standards. Her act is a duet with Hu Tao; Yun Jin helps with lyrics.

## entry: Iridescent Arataki Rockin' for Life Tour de Force of Awesomeness
@keys: Iridescent Arataki Rockin' for Life Tour de Force of Awesomeness, Arataki Rockin' for Life Tour, Xtreme Carnival Ensemble
@keys2: Iridescence Tour, Itto, Dvorak
@version: 4.6
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
The 4.6 flagship event (6 to 27 May 2024) in which Arataki Itto and Dvorak host a rock party on Watatsumi Island. Dvorak's inspiration was a local legend of a dog that rescued injured animals during the Inazuma war; Itto's gang drifted onto the dog matter, so the Traveler and Paimon work through Dvorak's checklist, decorate the venue and take part. The event's gameplay is Xtreme Carnival Ensemble (rhythm music), Trillion Trinket Trawl and A Meeting of Melodies. Rewards are earned as Iridescence Tour Tickets, which the item text describes as symbolising the spirit of rock 'n' roll. Limited-time content.
### LARGE +
The finale brings many Inazuma figures to the party, including Kokomi, Thoma, Yae Miko, Yoimiya, Ei, Gorou and Heizou. Dvorak changes the song's dog protagonist to an Onikabuto. Characters debate whether the dog legend is true; Thoma suspects Dvorak invented it to catch the imagination. Dvorak is not sure the spirit of rock 'n' roll can be defined and says it matters more than the details.
