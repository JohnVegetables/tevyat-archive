## summary
### LITE
Mondstadt is known as a land of poetry, song and wine, and bards are woven into daily life: they play in the plaza, in taverns and at festivals, and they are paid in Mora or in drinks. The tradition is tied to freedom. In the days of the tyrant Decarabian, songs of resistance were sung in cellars and alleys, and a young bard of Old Mondstadt played his lyre in the rebellion. Barbatos, the Anemo Archon, later took that bard's name and appearance and still wanders the city as the bard Venti.
### STANDARD +
Bards are ordinary working people in Mondstadt, not courtly figures. They have their own customary dress, rely on audiences' generosity, and compete for good stages. Their songs are also one way Mondstadt keeps its history: the story of the slave Vennessa and the bard god who helped her defeat a drake, and the rebellion against Decarabian, survive in ballads, drinking songs and the text of festival gifts such as Windblume Ode.
### LARGE +
Music also has a sacred side. The Church of Favonius keeps the Holy Lyre der Himmel, the instrument Barbatos used, and it is used at Ludi Harpastum. The lyre, the poem and the toast are therefore both entertainment and worship, which is why a bard in Mondstadt can be treated lightly in a tavern and reverently in the cathedral.

## practice
### LITE
Bards perform on the streets, in squares and in taverns, often for tips or drinks. Inspired travelling bards come to Mondstadt, especially around Ludi Harpastum, and sing poems in the streets.
### STANDARD +
- Dress: canon shows one bard style for each gender. Men wear a white-and-green striped shirt under a green jacket with green trousers, white boots and a green cravat. Women wear a white blouse and brown skirt under a red dress. Venti's own outfit is a variation on the theme.
- Getting paid: bards earn Mora from onlookers. Venti, as a new arrival, found that no tavern would serve a child-looking customer, so he began playing with a wine cup in his mouth and asking listeners to buy him a drink from the bar instead of giving him Mora. The novelty made him popular. He has a firm rule never to perform where a cat is nearby, since he sneezes at them.
### LARGE +
- Popularity: Venti is said to have won the title of Mondstadt's most popular bard three times (recorded on the wiki from the quest That Green Fellow, not traced further).
- Stages: Diluc says bards compete to play at the Angel's Share, his tavern, and takes offence when Venti implies it is not good enough for the Holy Lyre in its worn state.
- Travellers' view: an unnamed bard's diary in the Teyvat Travel Highlights calls Mondstadt the 'Romantic City of Pastorals' and says poetry, music, wine and festivities are the sustenance of a bard's life. The diarist packs 'Select Works of Alan the Bard' and notebooks and pens for the muse, and stayed far longer than planned because of Ludi Harpastum, when bards from all over the continent stop in their tracks to sing poems.
- Taverns as a second home: the same diarist says tavern tales of the Anemo Archon and his lyre are heard across the continent, since taverns are a second home to bards and musicians.
- Language: German appears in names, such as Venti's lyres Der Himmel and Der Frühling. In-game text itself is in English.

## songs_and_lore
### LITE
Songs carry Mondstadt's story. A song of resistance was whispered in dark places under Decarabian and later under the corrupt nobility, and became an anthem for the rebels.
### STANDARD +
- The song of resistance is described in the story text of the sword Freedom-Sworn. It is a drinking song whose verses say that even if someone silences you or takes your sight you can still sing or see another way, and that only the theft of your song and your freedom shall never do. The text says the weak who could not climb the tower sang it from below, and that it spurred on those who did. It names the group before the tower as a youth, a spirit, an archer, a knight and a flame-haired warrior.
### LARGE +
- Windblume Ode, a bow from the Invitation of Windblume event, keeps the rebels' greeting of a nameless flower, and says the old rebellion is now remembered in festive jests and in the lilting lyre-tines of histories long gone by.
- The bard of Old Mondstadt: the artifact text of A Note in Spring's Leich says that when Decarabian fell, the bard strummed a tune that belonged to Istaroth to shield the people from the chaos of a god's death, flinging the tower into another time. A mortal body cannot bear a nation's melody, so the bard fell with the tower and his name was lost to the winds of time, until the wind spirit who became Barbatos claimed it.
- Venti's own songs: his profile has him singing both old songs and new ones nobody has heard. One character story is a praise-poem to the West, East and North winds and the animals of Mondstadt, ending with a joking claim that the credit really belongs to the bard who writes the songs.
- Sea and storm: the wiki reports from NPC dialogue that a Church of Favonius family, the ancestors of Vind, were set to watch for storms off the eastern coast, and that Barbatos sang to the sea, after which no more storms came.
### ULTRA +
- Hexenzirkel: the wiki, citing a Hexenzirkel story teaser, says Barbatos once persuaded the witches who challenged him to make music, not war, and that they now meet at formal tea parties to settle differences and vent their troubles to the wind. Treat this as lightly sourced.

## instruments
### STANDARD
The lyre is the signature instrument of Mondstadt. The Windsong Lyre, a playable gadget sold during Invitation of Windblume (1.4) and afterwards in Marjorie's shop in Mondstadt, is described as a traditional instrument; its text says Mondstadt has a long-standing tradition of poetry and song and so a long history of making instruments.
### LARGE +
- The Holy Lyre der Himmel: a rosewood lyre with a pattern of flowing wind carved on it, once played by Barbatos. In the Prologue's State of the Holy Lyre der Himmel, Venti says it has lost its Anemo power after a thousand years, and Dvalin's Teardrop Crystal restores some of it. The wiki states it is used at Ludi Harpastum and that lending it otherwise needs signed documents from the Grand Master, the Seneschal and a community representative. At the end of the Prologue it is returned to the Church in poor condition; Venti casts an illusion over it so that it seems repaired.
- Der Frühling: Venti's wooden lyre with Cecilia flowers and blue strings, which the glass 'Vision' he carries to avoid suspicion turns into during combat.

## notable_bards
### LARGE
Bards shown in the open world and quests:
- Venti, the Windborne Bard (his in-game title), is the best known. He is secretly Barbatos, the Anemo Archon.
- Alfred is a Mondstadt bard who once performed in Liyue. His young daughter fell ill and died while he was away, and he can be found at night in the graveyard behind Favonius Cathedral, singing her favourite nursery rhymes at her grave.
- Sansa performs in front of the fountain in the city plaza and has collected instruments from across Teyvat. She suffers stage fright and stammers; the world quest To the Winds of Freedom helps her gain confidence.
### ULTRA +
Alfred and Sansa show two sides of a bard's life: the cost of a career spent on the road, and the struggle to take it up. Neither is shown as a celebrity. Sansa's quest unlocks the Repertoire of Myriad Melodies, which lets players try different instruments.

## scene_use
### LITE
Use a bard as colour: a lyre in a tavern, a song in the plaza, a poem sold for a drink. Songs in Mondstadt are about wind, wine, freedom and its history.
### STANDARD +
- Treat bards as friendly, informal and slightly scruffy, in keeping with a city that values freedom. A bard may ask to be paid in a drink.
- Songs about the dragon, Vennessa and the rebellion are familiar; few listeners know the Anemo Archon is the wandering bard in the crowd.
### LARGE +
- Keep the sacred register separate: the Holy Lyre, the Sisters of the Church and Ludi Harpastum are solemn as well as festive.
- Do not invent the words of named songs beyond what the item texts give.

## entry: Bards of Mondstadt
@keys: Mondstadt bard, Mondstadt bards, bardic tradition
### STANDARD
Bards are a normal part of Mondstadt's life. They wear green or red-and-brown outfits, play lyres, sing for Mora or drinks, and bring poems to the festivals. They preserve the city's legends and songs of resistance. The most famous is Venti, secretly the Anemo Archon Barbatos.

## entry: Alfred the Bard
@keys: Alfred
@keys2: bard, Mondstadt, graveyard, daughter
### STANDARD
Alfred is a Mondstadt bard who used to play in Liyue. His daughter fell ill and died while he was away on a performance, and she had wished to hear his songs. Now he is found at her grave behind the Favonius Cathedral, singing her favourite nursery rhymes at night. He is courteous to strangers and sympathises with anyone parted from family.

## entry: Sansa the Bard
@keys: Sansa
@keys2: bard, Mondstadt, instruments, lyre
### STANDARD
Sansa is a young bard who performs in front of the fountain in Mondstadt City. She wants to be an exceptional musician and has collected instruments from across Teyvat. Her parents did not approve of the career and she fell out with them over it. She has studied music rigorously, but anxiety makes her stammer and lose her words in front of an audience. After Venti, the Traveler and a cat named Charcoal helped her in the quest To the Winds of Freedom, she stammers much less.

## entry: Holy Lyre der Himmel
@keys: Holy Lyre, Der Himmel, Holy Lyre der Himmel
### STANDARD
The Church of Favonius keeps the Holy Lyre der Himmel, a rosewood lyre with a flowing-wind pattern that Barbatos played. It is used at Ludi Harpastum, and lending it otherwise needs the signed approval of the Grand Master, the Seneschal and a community representative. In the Prologue it was taken by the Fatui and recovered; Dvalin's Teardrop Crystal restored some of its Anemo power, and it was later returned to the Church in poor condition.

## entry: Song of Resistance
@keys: song of resistance, drinking song of the rebels
### LARGE
According to the story text of the sword Freedom-Sworn, a drinking song was sung in cellars, alleys and taverns in the time of Decarabian, and again against the corrupt nobility. Its verses say you can still sing without a tongue and see with your ears without eyes, and that stealing your song and your freedom shall never do. In the tale it was sung by those who could not climb Decarabian's tower. The wiki notes the song may be the one the bard played to break the storm barrier, but this is only implied.
