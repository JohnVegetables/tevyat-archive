## summary
### LITE
Snezhnaya's stage arts are centred on the Korolevskiy Troupe and its home, the Korolevskiy Theater in Snezhnograd. The Troupe performs opera, theater and musical shows, and its stars are the prima ballerina Odette and the prima donna Vodyanitsa. Snezhnayans must be coaxed out of their warm homes to attend, but a Troupe poster draws crowds, and the company is especially popular among the Fatui Harbingers and the official Mitya.
### STANDARD +
The works shown in canon are the ballets Black Snow Swan, The Seabird, The Envoy and The Firebird, plus the family ballet "Spirit Seeker". The Troupe's artists often hold second lives: Odette is a Fatui agent and Vodyanitsa a Water Imp, and both are secretly in the Rokot, so the Theater doubles as a meeting place for people with public and private roles.
### LARGE +
The Theater is a working community as much as a venue: an entrance clerk, hosts, a greenroom, a cellar, hunters such as Alyosha taking odd jobs, and critics whose reviews fill the arts pages. Because stage artists in canon also have duties elsewhere, rehearsal schedules repeatedly collide with Fatui work.
### ULTRA +
The excerpted ballets keep returning to outcasts, fate, ambition and the price of perfection, and Odette's own ballet life (a family ballet tradition, a lost father, a mother's career-ending injury) echoes some of this. That is a reading rather than a stated canon theme; canon gives no scholarly history of the art.

## practice
### LITE
Companies perform in Snezhnograd's Korolevskiy Theater. Stars such as Odette appear on posters, fans queue at the ticket window, and many attend more than once. Ballet is physically severe work: rehearsals at dawn, pointe shoes, a strict diet and constant stretching.
### STANDARD +
Odette is the model of the discipline. Her rehearsals start early in the morning and she treats each as seriously as a performance. She plans her meals around her work (a hard-boiled egg before going on stage, a real meal afterward) and has a very high pain tolerance. She almost never smiles at audiences, leaves the stage immediately after the curtain, and uses side doors, though autographs and fan meetings are part of the job. Vodyanitsa treats performing as a social art: she rehearses roles aloud, teases colleagues and insists that friends attend her shows.
### LARGE +
The Troupe sells tickets at the door. After the Archon Quest Everwinter Without Mercy, the player can buy a ticket from Ksenia and watch one of four short performances that rotate daily; Odette is the focus of at least two of them, with Vodyanitsa onstage or in the wings in others. Audiences differ: Arlecchino, Vesna, Mitya, Sandrone and Tartaglia each attend one. Between shows the Troupe teaches, rehearses, films promotional videos for new ballets, and holds meet-and-greets.
### ULTRA +
Dance training is described through Odette's family. Her mother Violetta was an acclaimed ballerina from a family of dancers and taught her young, and her father served in the Fatui. When he died on duty, Odette says, Violetta was devastated and broke her leg during a performance, ending her career. Odette credits her mentor's harsh training and says that, by her mother's account, her exact technique lacked the emotion that ties the steps together. A critic quoted in her profile compares her dancing to a perfectly honed dagger: beautiful to watch, not to touch.

## repertoire
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
Four ballets are excerpted in the Polestar Ballet Anthology: Black Snow Swan, The Seabird, The Envoy and The Firebird. The Troupe prepares the Black Snow Swan premiere during the World Quest Variations on Belyi and Chernyi, and the Archon Quest chapter Wraith's Nocturne is framed by a preview of "Spirit Seeker". Details appear in the entries below.
### LARGE +
The excerpts range from The Firebird (a prince, a flame-feathered bird and a Demon King) to The Envoy (an emperor's dispatch halts a nobleman's banquet) and The Seabird (a composer and a singer meeting again in a country estate). Black Snow Swan is the most symbolic: a black swan, a swan queen and three visiting phantoms.

## regional_variation
### STANDARD
Snezhnayan theater is shown in Snezhnograd and, in passing, in the mining town of Svetloledovka, where a former Troupe member now works as safety officer and a miner sneers at the Troupe as a fancy opera house. Fontaine and Liyue have their own stage cultures (see Liyue Opera); the sources read make no direct comparison.
### LARGE +
Snezhnaya's climate shapes theater-going. Odette's profile notes that coaxing people to an opera house on freezing days takes effort, yet her name on a poster fills the ticket queue. In the Seabird excerpt, the singer Zarechnaya plans to winter in Svetloledovka, singing to audiences who understand nothing of opera.

## scene_use
### LITE
Use the Theater for staged scenes: a hushed audience, the greenroom, a cold-faced ballerina, a singer with a mischievous streak, a clerk checking tickets. Treat the Troupe as prestigious and strictly run.
### STANDARD +
Good beats: Odette repairing her music box at night; Vodyanitsa nagging colleagues to attend her shows; a Harbinger or senior official in the audience; Alyosha earning wages backstage; rumours of a ghost in the empty theater at night. Keep Odette disciplined, formal and tired rather than gushing toward fans. Do not invent new ballet plots beyond the excerpted ones.
### LARGE +
Tensions that suit canon: Fatui duty against rehearsal time, public persona against private grief, star status against loneliness. Rokot ties and the events of Wraith's Nocturne are spoilers; keep them out of scenes set before the user has reached them.

## entry: Korolevskiy Troupe
@keys: Korolevskiy Troupe, Royal Troupe, Troupe of Snezhnograd
@version: 7.0
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
A performance troupe based in Snezhnograd that specialises in opera, theater and musical performances. It is very popular in Snezhnayan circles, most notably among the Fatui Harbingers and Mitya. Its named stars are Odette (prima ballerina) and Vodyanitsa (prima donna). Dorofeya, Pashkov and Violetta are listed as former members: Violetta danced, Dorofeya was a singer who retired early, and Pashkov left to be the safety officer of Svetloledovka.
### LARGE +
Odette and Vodyanitsa are close friends and colleagues, and the Troupe gives each a daytime role. Their friendly bickering over Odette's overwork is a running thread of the World Quest Variations on Belyi and Chernyi. Open-world NPCs at the Theater include hosts and the ticket-checker Kulish, who seems to be a fan of Odette.
### ULTRA +
The name has not been explained in canon. Korolevskiy is Russian for royal. See uncertainties for the possible link to Nepla Korolevitsna.

## entry: The Korolevskiy Theater
@keys: Korolevskiy Theater, Korolevskiy Theatre, Royal Theater
@version: 7.0
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
A theater in Snezhnograd, in the Everfrozen Earth region of Snezhnaya, where the Korolevskiy Troupe gives its performances. The official map text says that backstage toil matters less here than whether the show can grant the audience a moment's escape into a beautiful illusion. A Meeting Point sits in the dressing room behind the stage. Other spaces include a greenroom, a lobby, an administrative room, Odette's private lounge and a cellar that appears during Winter's Mysterious Silence.
### LARGE +
The auditorium holds 312 seats: 44 blue sofas of three, plus four purple three-seat sofas in the lobby. Open-world dialogue is richer after the Mistilteinn quest and once the Theater Meeting Point reaches level 5; characters seen there include Odette, Vodyanitsa, Alyosha, Mitya, Valeriy, Vesna, Tartaglia, Arlecchino and Sandrone.
### ULTRA +
The Theater is the starting point of several Snezhnayan stories: the ghost rumour that leads to Odette's lounge, the double-schedule World Quest, and the Archon Quest episode in which the Traveler and other guests spend a night there. Details of the last are in the Winter's Mysterious Silence entry.

## entry: Black Snow Swan
@keys: Black Snow Swan, Myrtha
@keys2: ballet, Odette, Odile, premiere, Troupe
@version: 7.0
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
A Korolevskiy Troupe ballet with Odette in the lead; its script excerpts form Polestar Ballet Anthology Vol. 1. Odile, a black snow swan, is ashamed of her dark feathers and begs to be as pure as her white sisters. Their queen, Myrtha, says the colour comes from human love and passion, a poison that binds one to fate, and that tenderness is weakness. Three phantoms will visit Odile on successive nights. In the excerpted ending Odile refuses Myrtha's creed: love, she says, is the blade that cuts fate's snare. At dawn she soars higher than any snow swan.
### LARGE +
In Variations on Belyi and Chernyi, a World Quest, Odette and Vodyanitsa discuss the premiere, two weeks away. Odette practised the role as a child but never performed it on stage. She once disliked the black swan, whom she did not want to resemble, but now sympathises and says that in the story the black swan stops trying to fit the flock, flies higher than any white swan and is embraced by the clouds. Because she wants to balance the part with Fatui missions, Vodyanitsa persuades her to let the Traveler plan her schedule as her manager for two weeks.
### ULTRA +
The quest's endings are recorded in the Black Snow Swan Archive. Their titles include Lost Amidst the Applause (technically flawless, but Odette cannot find herself in it), A Solo Atop a Still Lake (she is determined to be herself, at a price), Still Dark the Snowy Night (she wants too much but will not give up), Dawn (a late light as warm as in her childhood) and The Wasting Daylight (self-doubt). The Anthology's first volume is obtained by finishing the quest for the first time.

## entry: Polestar Ballet Anthology
@keys: Polestar Ballet Anthology
@keys2: ballet, Korolevskiy Troupe, script, anthology
@version: 7.0
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
A four-volume collection of script anthologies from the Korolevskiy Troupe. Vol. 1 contains Black Snow Swan; Vol. 2 The Seabird; Vol. 3 The Envoy; Vol. 4 The Firebird. Vol. 1 comes from finishing Variations on Belyi and Chernyi for the first time; Vols. 2-4 are sold by Moes at Torchlight Books.
### LARGE +
The Seabird (Act IV) is set in a country-estate banquet hall: the composer Kostya, dissatisfied with fashionable music, meets Zarechnaya, a singer who left for Snezhnograd with Boris, lost her stage there and has come back only to say goodbye. She has learned to sing for the joy of song. The Envoy opens in the reign of Emperor Monomakh, where a manservant bursts into Viscount Schpekin's banquet with a sealed dispatch from the Zapolyarny Palace and the dancers freeze. The Firebird follows Khramov, third son of Grand Duke Vernov, who guards the golden apple tree and chases the fire-feathered bird Sirin; the Demon King Berstuk and Princess Vaselisa are in the cast.
### ULTRA +
The volumes are scripts, not a history of the Troupe, so they do not say when the ballets premiered. The Seabird excerpt mentions Snezhnograd and Svetloledovka, and The Envoy names an emperor, Monomakh.

## entry: Spirit Seeker
@keys: Spirit Seeker
@keys2: ballet, Violetta, preview, Korolevskiy Theater, Odette
@version: 7.0
@availability: permanent
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
A classic Snezhnayan ballet inherited by Violetta Spessiva's family; Violetta was an acclaimed ballerina at the Theater until a fall left her unable to dance. The sources read do not summarise its plot. In the Archon Quest chapter after Everwinter Without Mercy, a preview of it is held at the Theater, and a group photo of those present, the "Spirit Seeker" Preview Commemorative Photo, becomes a clue item.
### LARGE +
The photo's entries describe Theater staff and guests: the ticket-checker Kulish, the host Rampana, the hunter Alyosha, the mercenary Shram, the factory owner Chugunov, Tonia, the Traveler, Odette, Valeriy, Vodyanitsa, Mitya, and the businesspeople Soboleva and Torgov. As the quest proceeds, several entries are updated to record who died in the night. Odette's own entry says she is suspected of having a second identity, Lelek.
### ULTRA +
How far Odette is trying to revive the family ballet is unclear: Violetta's involvement in that revival is not stated in the sources read.

## entry: Winter's Mysterious Silence
@keys: Winter's Mysterious Silence, Wraith's Nocturne
@keys2: Theater, Odette, Vodyanitsa, Ronova, Spirit Seeker
@version: 7.0
@availability: permanent
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
In Archon Quest Chapter VII (Wraith's Nocturne, Act II) the Korolevskiy Theater becomes the setting of a closed, wintry mystery. After the preview of "Spirit Seeker", the Traveler and other guests, including Odette, Vodyanitsa, Mitya, Valeriy and Chugunov, wake to find people dead or gone, and a black mist drives them through the building. According to the wiki's summaries Ronova's curse is behind the deaths, and Vodyanitsa's Requiem helps save the Traveler and Mitya.
### LARGE +
Afterwards Odette, as Lelek, breaks into the Armory Palace; Ronova's curse takes hold of her and she places her Cor Ignis on the Traveler. The Rokot negotiate clemency but suspend her. She later dances at Signora's tomb and breaks the Cor Ignis. Odette, Vodyanitsa and Alyosha then renovate a place called Rankova Zorya. Treat all of this as Archon Quest spoilers; each character's own entry holds the detail.

## entry: One Yet to Take a Bow
@keys: One Yet to Take a Bow, Odette music box, theater ghost
@keys2: Odette, music box, midnight, lounge
@version: 7.0
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
A World Quest about a rumour that eerie music plays in the Korolevskiy Theater at midnight and a ghost's shadow dances on the wall. At night the Traveler finds Odette in the greenroom: the ghost is a broken swan-shaped music box, a childhood gift from her mother, which she repairs in secret in her private lounge because her days are full. She asks for help gathering parts and for silence. She offers the Traveler a reward of redecorating the lounge, but only after repairs, because the ghost keeps visitors away. In the end the box plays again with a ballerina figurine on top.
### LARGE +
Post-quest dialogue shows Odette in rehearsal and with Vodyanitsa. For example, she squeezes Vodyanitsa's shows into a packed schedule because Vodyanitsa would otherwise turn up unannounced in her makeup room, and at Podvorye Restaurant she cannot always get her Snowfield dessert. Most such dialogue appears only after the Mistilteinn quest and Theater Meeting Point level 5.

## entry: Korolevskiy Theater's Performance
@keys: Korolevskiy Theater's Performance
@keys2: Ksenia, Odette, Vodyanitsa, ticket, show
@version: 7.0
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
A repeatable exploration objective available after Everwinter Without Mercy. Ksenia sells the ticket at the entrance, then a short show plays. Four versions rotate at daily reset: Odette alone, Odette with domovoy dancers, Vodyanitsa preparing her voice, and Odette and Vodyanitsa together. Audience members vary: Arlecchino, Vesna, Mitya, Sandrone and Tartaglia are shown.
### LARGE +
Backstage lines show how seriously the Troupe works. Odette plans improvised steps in case a domovoy group number goes wrong, and says a little nervousness helps focus. Vodyanitsa predicts a flawless show. Tartaglia wishes Tonia could have come, and Sandrone remarks that attending was not a waste of time.

## entry: Former Troupe Members
@keys: Dorofeya, Pashkov
@keys2: Troupe, Svetloledovka, Snezhnograd
@version: 7.0
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
Dorofeya was a rising star and singer with the Korolevskiy Troupe who retired early; she now lives in Snezhnograd on her husband's land holdings and admires Odette's ballet. She warns that audiences adore the flawless stage illusion rather than the performer. Pashkov, a young man, left the Troupe to become the safety officer of Svetloledovka, where he is known for strictness about Prime Ice safety rules.
