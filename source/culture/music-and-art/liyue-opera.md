## summary
### LITE
Liyue Opera is a traditional art of Liyue: plays told through singing, acting, dance and spoken parts, performed by troupes, with sophisticated vocals and richly varied melodies. The best-known troupe is the Yun-Han Opera Troupe of Liyue Harbor, run by the playwright and singer Yun Jin, who performs from time to time at Heyu Tea House. Many traditional plays dramatise the adepti and Rex Lapis, such as "The Divine Damsel of Devastation".
### STANDARD +
Canon calls it one of Liyue's traditional arts, passed down for generations and much changed from its original form while keeping its refined vocals and varied melodies. Performers normally work in troupes. It is a living, popular entertainment in Liyue Harbor: fans ask at Heyu Tea House about performance schedules, wealthy patrons such as Ningguang donate to troupes, and outlanders such as Kaedehara Kazuha come to listen.

## practice
### LITE
Performers sing, act and dance, and training is long and physical. Full tradition calls for wrapped hair, full-face makeup and an elaborate costume. A troupe is led by a director, who may also be its lead performer and playwright.
### STANDARD +
Training begins in childhood and is harsh: stretching exercises, memorising lyrics, and whole days of dance practice in the sun. Yun Jin learned from her mother, a former star of Liyue Harbor, whom she calls a very strict teacher, and her father is a playwright. Daily voice rehearsal is a core habit: she says that if you skip a day only you know, but skip three and the audience knows. Stage combat is part of the work, which is why she took up the polearm. A troupe keeps veteran members, a manager, and wardrobe and props staff, while the director makes the major decisions.
### LARGE +
Costume and makeup are demanding. Yun Jin remarks that full dress is spectacular but exhausting, so she performs without full makeup or hair-wrapping whenever she has the option. She describes opera customs as very particular and handed down through generations, so performers sometimes know how a convention is done without knowing why; she is delighted that Zhongli knows the origins of almost everything. A well-regarded performer can fill a house: Fan Er'ye, boss of Heyu Tea House, says one Yun Jin performance earns enough Mora that the house need not open for a month.
### ULTRA +
Repertoire and taste are in tension. Yun Jin's profile says adeptal and Rex Lapis stories make up the majority of the traditional repertoire, because people love fantasies about the adepti. Early in her career she grew dissatisfied when pitch and tempo rose or fell the same way in every play and characters began to blur together; she compares the girl in "The Divine Damsel of Devastation" with the fisherman's daughter in "String of Pearls". She credits a monodrama, "Snow Treading", with teaching her to leave herself behind and walk in a character's shoes; her Vision turned up in her sleeve after that performance. She now writes plays about ordinary people's loves, angers and obsessions. Troupe elders, by contrast, are strongly traditional and frown on other music such as rock 'n' roll, so she attends Xinyan's gigs discreetly.

## repertoire
@version: 2.4
### STANDARD
Named plays in canon: "The Divine Damsel of Devastation", a traditional adeptal play the Yun-Han troupe has performed, with a new ending written by Yun Jin; "String of Pearls", written by Yun Jin, in which she plays the lead Zixin; and "Snow Treading", a monodrama about a strenuous walk in the snow. Yun Jin has written all the other new plays the troupe has staged in recent years.
### LARGE +
Because Liyue legends centre on gods and adepti, supernatural subjects dominate the repertoire. Yun Jin wishes to sing of human stories instead, and once dreamed of performing in an adeptus's abode, perhaps at Jueyun Karst, so that adepti and people alike could hear her.

## regional_variation
### STANDARD
In these sources Liyue Opera is a Liyue Harbor art that also travels. The Yun-Han troupe performs at Heyu Tea House and, per a later anecdote, on a stage at Yilong Wharf. During the Lantern Rite event Fleeting Colors in Flight, Granny Ruoxin of Qingce Village invited Yun Jin to perform at the village. Other nations' equivalents are not described in the sources read.

## scene_use
### LITE
Use it for refined entertainment: a tea house, tea going cold because the audience is captivated, fans trading opinions on past shows, tea between acts.
### STANDARD
Good scene beats: patrons asking at the tea house when Yun Jin will appear; backstage rituals such as cleaning a lucky lock before curtain; traditionalist troupe elders versus a modernising director; Zhongli attending as a connoisseur. Keep Yun Jin polished in public and friendly in private. Do not invent plots for operas beyond the titles and themes canon gives.
### LARGE +
Performers treat practice as discipline and even pleasure rather than burden. Audiences reward spectacle but also sincerity.

## examples
### STANDARD
Yun Jin performing at Heyu Tea House, and Zhongli watching her at Yilong Wharf, are the main canon examples. In the Zhongli anecdote (Travelers' Tales, added 5.4) he praises her command of the folk art and regrets letting his tea go cold.

## entry: Yun-Han Opera Troupe
@keys: Yun-Han Opera Troupe, Yun-Han Troupe, Yunhan Troupe
@version: 2.4
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
Liyue Harbor's most famous opera troupe, directed by Yun Jin and currently performing at Heyu Tea House. The Yun family were famous weaponsmiths about five hundred years ago before turning to opera as a family tradition; the troupe was established by the time of Yun Jin's paternal grandfather, and many veterans date from his directorship. Yunning is the manager and Yinggong handles wardrobe and props. The "Han" in the name is unrelated to the Han family of weaponsmiths; the character differs.
### LARGE +
Troupe elders fuss over Yun Jin's diet and voice and disapprove of rock 'n' roll. Ningguang has made several generous donations to the troupe, according to Yun Jin.

### STANDARD
A traditional Liyue opera about the adepti, named in Yun Jin's profile as a prime example of the genre. The Yun-Han troupe has performed it recently with a new ending written by Yun Jin. The sources read do not give its plot.

## entry: Heyu Tea House Performances
@keys: Heyu Tea House
@keys2: opera, Yun Jin, troupe, performance, stage
@version: 1.0
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
Heyu Tea House in Liyue Harbor is owned by Fan Er'ye, a former performer. It draws crowds with a top storyteller, Tea Master Liu Su, and with the Yun-Han troupe. Yun Jin takes the stage from time to time, so fans gather hoping to catch her, and nine in ten patrons are said to be her fans.
