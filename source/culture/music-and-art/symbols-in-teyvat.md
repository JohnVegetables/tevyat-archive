## summary
### LITE
Teyvat has a handful of shared visual motifs that recur across nations: the triquetra (on the Mora and Domain doors), Solomon's knot, the triskelion, a four-pointed star tied to Primogems and the Traveler, and a Celestia-linked "celestial" motif. Each nation and many organisations also carry their own heraldry, such as the Knights of Favonius crest, the Akademiya emblem and the Fatui's Palestar. Canon rarely explains what the shared symbols mean.
### STANDARD +
Many motif names used here (four-pointed star, celestial motif, domain seals, Old Mondstadt symbol) are wiki labels, not in-game terms. Treat them as descriptive shorthand for what is drawn, not as lore the characters know.

## practice
### LITE
Shared motifs appear on currency, doors, ruins, clothing and gadgets rather than being taught or explained. Organisations use crests to mark membership: on uniforms, hats, scarves, masks and banners.
### STANDARD +
Shared motifs include the triquetra, Solomon's knot, triskelion, four-pointed star, celestial motif and domain seals. Details of each are in the sub-entries and at higher tiers.
### LARGE +
- Triquetra: three interlaced arcs. Upright on the Mora and on Domain entrance doors; inverted on Paimon's clothing and on some abilities tied to Tartaglia. Double versions exist, such as the large inverted triquetra sealing the Spiral Abyss gateway before Adventure Rank 20, and Kaeya's Glacial Heart talent icon. Its meaning in Teyvat is not stated.
- Solomon's knot: a four-lobed knot over most Domain entrances, on some ruin mechanisms and Lightning Strike Probes in Inazuma, on Geo Lamps, and on the bases of two Teleport Waypoints in Liyue Harbor. The Fatui's Palestar is a filled-in, knotless variant of the same four-arc shape.
- Triskelion: a triple spiral, seen on the wall behind Irminsul trees inside Domains, on Ruins Torch platforms and on the shards of the Skyfrost Nail and Chasm Nail. Some ruins use a six-swirl version, such as doors in Dragonspine and the door leading down to Araumi's ruins.
- Four-pointed star: associated with Primogems, the Traveler (whose element symbol is based on it), Paimon's hairclip and Albedo's neck mark. Pure-blooded Khaenri'ahns have star-shaped pupils. It is also tied to Asmoday, whose portals form a line of three stars and whose domain, the Temple of Space, uses the star heavily. The Treasure Hoarders' logo combines a star with a ravenwing motif.
- Celestial motif: a semicircular accent of three or five petals, found on objects connected with Celestia: upgraded Statues of The Seven, the Skyfrost Nail and Paimon's clothing.
- Domain seals: ground patterns sealing domains of old gods. They form the floor of Decarabian's Tower in Stormterror's Lair and seal the Hidden Palace of Guizang Formula (with Guizhong's domain) at Luhua Pool and Havria's domain in Sal Terrae. A simplified version appears in Venti's Wind Domain skill.
### ULTRA +
In-game uses extend into the interface: the Omni-Elemental Dice and card back in the Genius Invokation TCG use triquetras, and Genesis Crystals and Welkin Moon art combine the triquetra with the four-pointed star. Elemental Mastery's attribute icon is a triskelion. These are production-design observations and carry no stated in-world meaning.

## regional_variation
### LITE
Each nation has local heraldry, and Character Cards use national ornament to show affiliation.
### STANDARD +
Mondstadt favours winged and wind-related emblems, Liyue diamonds and geometry, Inazuma tomoe and mon, Sumeru Darshan and desert-court animals, Fontaine melusines, scales and gears, Natlan tribal emblems, and Snezhnaya the Palestar.
### LARGE +
- Mondstadt: the Four Winds appear on cards as the Falcon of the West, Wolf of the North, Lion of the South and Dragon of the East, beside dandelion and Windwheel Aster icons. The Old Mondstadt symbol, a pair of outstretched wings with a tail, links to Decarabian's rule and the aristocracy and is found on Royal Series weapons and the Noblesse Oblige set; the Lawrence Clan's Glacial Seal is a variant. Decarabian's cercelee (a cross with curled ends) decorates Stormterror's Lair. The Knights of Favonius crest sits on uniform breastplates, and the Church of Favonius wears a fleur-de-lis-like headwear symbol (Rosaria is an exception).
- Liyue: geometric designs, meanders, diamonds, auspicious-cloud patterns and scales. Adepti-linked Character Cards show a dragon, stag, qilin and crane; other characters show emblems of Liyue Harbor trades (the Mora triquetra for the Golden House mint, a silk flower, a ship, crossed sword and polearm). A cluster of square diamonds marks places tied to Morax, such as Yuehai Pavilion, and appears on Zhongli's clothing.
- Inazuma: the Electro symbol is formed from the negative space of three tomoe, standing for the Raiden Shogun's rule. Five-petaled blossoms mark the Sacred Sakura at the Grand Narukami Shrine and Yae Miko's clothing, and clans may carry a mon (crest).
- Sumeru: Akademiya students wear the Akademiya emblem, and each Darshan has its own emblem. Rainforest-affiliated Character Cards carry six animal icons for the Six Darshans; desert-affiliated cards carry four, matching King Deshret's four ministers. King Deshret is represented by a sun and eye, and the Eremite Crest is a sun motif above crossed scimitars.
- Fontaine: Maison Gardiennage's emblem is two back-to-back melusines beneath stylised scales, with the melusines added to commemorate Carole. Other cards show Spina di Rosula, the Steambird (its clockwork mascot in a gear), the three-headed wolf of the Fortress of Meropide (also tied to Wriothesley's constellation) and Furina's scales.
- Natlan: Character Cards bear emblems for the six tribes: Huitztlan, Nanatzcayan, Teteocan, Meztli, Tlalocan and Mictlan.
### ULTRA +
- Snezhnaya and Nod-Krai: Fatui-affiliated cards display eleven Harbinger emblems that double as constellation symbols. Nod-Krai cards show emblems for the Lightkeepers, Hexenzirkel, Clink-Clank Krumkake Craftshop, Voynich Guild, Snowland Fae, Knights of Favonius, Frostmoon Scions and Fatui. Snezhnayan cards (confirmed as of 7.1) show the Fatui, Druzhna, Snowland Fae, Korolevskiy Theater and Zapolyarny Palace.

## scene_use
### STANDARD
Use motifs as set dressing that a perceptive character notices without explaining: a triquetra stamped on every coin, a knot above a Domain door. Do not invent meanings for the shared symbols; let characters wonder or shrug. Organisational crests are safe identification cues: a Fatui mask with the Palestar, a Knights of Favonius breastplate, an Akademiya student's emblem.
### LARGE +
Ordinary people do not read the four-pointed star as Khaenri'ahn or Traveler-related; that connection is a viewer's observation. Avoid claiming that the triquetra, knot and triskelion form a single ancient writing system or religion, since canon says nothing of the sort.

## examples
### STANDARD
A guard's Garde hat showing two melusines under scales; Zhongli's coat carrying the Geo diamond cluster; the sealed Spiral Abyss gate showing a double triquetra; Venti's Wind Domain echoing the seal pattern of Decarabian's Tower.

## entry: Triquetra and Solomon's knot
@keys: triquetra, Solomon's knot, triskelion
### STANDARD
The triquetra (three interlaced arcs) appears on the Mora and Domain doors; Solomon's knot appears above most Domain entrances and on ruin devices; the triskelion (triple spiral) decorates Domains and the Skyfrost Nail. Canon gives no meaning for any of them. The Fatui's Palestar resembles a filled-in Solomon's knot.

## entry: Palestar
@keys: Palestar, pale star, Fatui emblem
### STANDARD
The Palestar is the Fatui logo, seen on masks and clothing to show membership. It depicts a stylised, filled-in Solomon's knot above another circular emblem. Fatui-affiliated Character Cards add eleven Harbinger emblems.

## entry: Four-pointed star
@keys: four-pointed star, Primogem star
### STANDARD
A recurring star motif tied to Primogems, the Traveler, Paimon's hairclip, Albedo's neck mark and the star-shaped pupils of pure-blooded Khaenri'ahns. Asmoday's portals and the Temple of Space also use it. The name is unofficial and its in-world significance is unstated.

## entry: Domain seals
@keys: domain seal, domain seals
### STANDARD
Ground seals mark domains of old gods: the floor of Decarabian's Tower in Stormterror's Lair, the sealed Hidden Palace of Guizang Formula at Luhua Pool (including Guizhong's domain) and Havria's domain in Sal Terrae. Venti's Wind Domain uses a simplified version. The term is a wiki label.

## entry: Old Mondstadt symbol
@keys: Old Mondstadt symbol, Glacial Seal, Decarabian's cercelee
### STANDARD
A winged emblem tied to Decarabian's era and the Mondstadt aristocracy, found on Royal Series weapons and the Noblesse Oblige set. The Lawrence Clan's Glacial Seal is a variant, seen on Eula's talent icons. Which version is original, and what it meant, is unknown. Decarabian's cercelee is a separate curl-ended cross used in Stormterror's Lair.
