## summary
### LITE
Fontaine is a nation that loves the stage. Theatre, opera, music and magic acts are ordinary life there: per Navia's Character Story, saying you grew up watching the theatre is the same as saying you were raised drinking water. Most shows are held at the Opera Epiclese, which is also the main courthouse. Famous names include the old playwright Coppelius, the Great Magician Lyney and, in recent years, the new film scene.
### STANDARD +
Under the former Hydro Archon Furina, citizens treated judicial trials like performances, and the belief in justice they produced was turned into Indemnitium by the Oratrice. Film is the newest art form, promoted by the Fontaine Film Association and its Fontinalia Film Festival. Mystery novels are also hugely popular.
### LARGE +
The tradition has deep roots: Coppelius wrote romantic, often legend-based plays about 500 years ago, and his last opera, "Clockwork Coppelia", ended in catastrophe at its premiere. Modern Fontaine mixes that heritage with clockwork technology (dancing meka, Kamera cameras) and a public that expects drama from everything from a trial to a magic act.
### ULTRA +
Many parts of the scene overlap: the same Opera Epiclese stage hosts trials, a famous magician's shows and film screenings; Furina moved from playing a god to being an actress and artistic consultant; and the film awards still carry her name. Nothing here is a single organised institution: canon shows a culture, not a ministry of the arts.

## practice
### LITE
- Theatre and opera are part of daily life. Most shows are staged at the Opera Epiclese.
- Music festivals and film festivals are held yearly; the large touring concert is the Iridescence Tour.
- Magic shows are popular, with Lyney as the nation's best-known magician.
- Mystery novels such as "Great Detective Hurlock" and "The Case Files of Miss Orith" are widely read.
### STANDARD +
- **The Opera Epiclese.** On the island of Erinnyes, it is an opera house built to serve as Fontaine's main courthouse. Its official description says the true and the fantastical, the comedic and the tragic share the stage, and quotes an ancient Fontainian playwright: all the world is a stage. Besides trials it hosts other performances, among them Lyney's magic shows. The Oratrice Mecanique d'Analyse Cardinale sits at the back of the stage, below the seat from which Neuvillette presides. Other judges from the Palais Mermonia can be assigned to cases, so the Iudex does not hear every trial. Furina had a private VIP seat.
- **Trials as spectacle.** Under Furina the citizens treated trials like shows. The Oratrice harvests the people's belief in justice and converts it into Indemnitium, so a gripping trial was also a way of feeding the nation's power supply.
- **Film.** The Fontaine Film Association created the Fontinalia Film Festival (4.3) to introduce more people to the art. Anyone may submit a film; the highest-scoring entry receives the Furina Award. The name was kept after Furina abdicated because nobody moved to change it. The Kamera comes from Fontaine and has a film variant, the Film Kamera.
- **Magic.** Fontaine has popular magicians who perform for crowds, and Lyney is the Great Magician of the Court of Fontaine (see the entry on Lyney's magic and Cesar).
- **Fiction.** Mystery novels are popular enough that "Great Detective Hurlock" was adapted into an award-winning film (see the 6.7 event below).
### LARGE +
- **Coppelius and the old repertoire.** Coppelius lived about 500 years ago and showed a gift for playwriting as a child. His plays often reworked legends and historical figures, and critics already noted that his later works kept returning to eternal beauty and eternal love. Titles named in canon include "Golden Hyperborea", "Coppelia" and "Masque of the Great". His plays are still performed.
- **Clockwork art.** The Icewind Suite, a combat challenge in the Court of Fontaine region, was devised by Bossuet, a Research Institute technician inspired by "Clockwork Coppelia". He wanted art that transcends the "era of mechanical reproduction". After he left, Maillardet took over and added combat abilities, so the dancing meka Coppelia and Coppelius are now also fighters.
- **Festival culture.** The Fontinalia Festival commemorates the Lochknights' search for the Oceanids and the welcome of Egeria; Furina made it carnival-like (children shout "Trial or treat!"). The Film Festival is a newer addition by the film association. Fontaine also holds annual music festivals, and the Iridescence Tour, begun in Fontaine, tours all seven nations.
### ULTRA +
- **The Film Festival over time.** In 4.3 (Roses and Muskets, limited event) the first Furina Award went to "The Two Musketeers", from Musketeer Pictures, produced by Xavier and directed by Furina herself. In the 6.7 event Sunny Summer Fontinalia (limited event), "Great Detective Hurlock", directed by Lepine-Pauline with Charlotte in the lead, won the latest award, beating "A Homecoming" (directed by Furina, with Columbina in the lead). Chiori says the loss was expected: artistic films rarely beat commercial ones, and Hurlock adapts a popular light novel. New awards that year included Best Director, Best Costume Design and Best Kamera Operation. The films were screened in the Opera Epiclese, and Furina remarked that an opera house is the true way to enjoy a film.
- **Chevalmarin Film Fantasyland.** An amusement park (seen in the limited-time event Whirling Waltz, 5.6) built around crowd-favourite films, from Lepine-Pauline's idea, built by Navia leading the Spina di Rosula. Its map description calls it a stage for film fans to share their passion.
- **Lyney as performer.** Lyney's magic show at the Opera Epiclese in the 4.0 Archon Quest ended in a trial after his assistant Cowell was killed; later, in 4.2, a Giant Magic Box built by the Fatui to copy his trick was part of the plan that forced Furina onto the Opera Epiclese stage for her own trial. His teacher Cesar performed for the public, especially children, and took Lyney and Lynette as pupils for ten days.
- **Spread abroad.** The Iridescence Tour reached Liyue as the Lantern Rite Grand Concert, where Xinyan and Hu Tao performed the opening act. Xinyan also says rock 'n' roll music came to Liyue from Fontaine.

## regional_variation
### STANDARD
The performing arts are strongest in the Court of Fontaine, the capital, where clothing and manners are more formal and decorated. Outside the city, in Fleuve Cendre and other areas, dress and life are simpler and more practical. The Opera Epiclese and the Palais Mermonia are both in the Court of Fontaine. Other nations meet Fontaine's arts through exports and tours, such as the Steambird newspaper and the Iridescence Tour.
### LARGE +
Snezhnaya has its own theatre tradition (see its separate entry); canon compares the two nations' technology, not their stage traditions, so do not blend them. Fontaine's technology feeds its performance culture: clockwork meka built for entertainment and combat, and the Kamera used in film production.

## scene_use
### STANDARD
- A show at the Opera Epiclese is an easy hook: the building is both theatre and court, so a performance can slide into a trial or the reverse.
- Locals talk about the stage casually and with strong opinions. A Fontainian may mention a play, a film or a mystery novel rather than the news.
- Magic acts reward spectators who watch for the trick; patter and misdirection suit comedic or tense scenes.
- Film talk is current: festivals, the Furina Award, who directed what, which novel is being adapted.
### LARGE +
- Take care with the Oratrice after 4.2. Focalors said the Indemnitium stored over the centuries would have powered Fontaine for millennia had it not been spent on her own execution, so the trial-as-spectacle system belongs mostly to the Furina era.
- Characters who lived through the old system may joke about it (the audience was part of the machine) and also miss the drama.
- Pre-4.2 scenes should keep the audience loud and engaged; post-4.2 scenes can show a calmer court beside a growing film and theatre scene.
### ULTRA +
- For historical colour, Coppelius is a safe reference: well known, loved by romantics, still staged. Details of his last opera are second-hand, so characters may disagree about what happened.
- Mystery fiction is a common conversation topic. A fan of Hurlock may spot "clues" in real events; playing along does not confirm any theory.

## examples
### STANDARD
- A long evening at the Opera Epiclese: a trial, a magic show or a screening, with a lively audience.
- A film shoot with Kamera operators and clapper loaders, as in the Fontinalia Film Festival.
### LARGE +
- A debate over whether an artistic film should beat a commercial one.
- A revival of a Coppelius play, or a stop of the Iridescence Tour.

## entry: Opera Epiclese as a Stage
@keys: Opera Epiclese, Epiclese, Erinnyes opera house
@keys2: stage, show, performance, audience, seat, premiere, theatre, theater
@version: 4.0
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
The Opera Epiclese stands on the island of Erinnyes and works as Fontaine's main courthouse. It has raised seating, a private VIP section for Furina, and the Oratrice at the back of the stage below the judge's seat. Besides trials it hosts magic shows and other performances, and in the Sunny Summer Fontinalia event (6.7) it screened films. The Fortress of Meropide can be reached by a lift behind the building that descends to the River Talionis (unlocked in the quest A Tea Party Most Thorny). Luzerne is the receptionist there. The name is French for Epiclesis, an invocation used as an epithet in Greek religion.
### LARGE +
The Palais Mermonia has several judges, so a case can be assigned to someone other than Neuvillette; in the limited-time event quest An Overnight Fantasyland (Whirling Waltz, 5.6), Charlotte explains that the Iudex did not preside over a trial because it was assigned to another judge from the start.

## entry: Trials as Public Entertainment
@keys: Indemnitium, Oratrice, Oratrice Mecanique d'Analyse Cardinale
@keys2: trial, spectacle, audience, belief in justice
@version: 4.0
@spoiler: major
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
Under Furina, Fontaine's trials were treated like shows. The Oratrice Mecanique d'Analyse Cardinale, created by Focalors, harvests the people's belief in justice and converts it into energy called Indemnitium. Focalors said the stored Indemnitium would have powered Fontaine for millennia had it not been used for her own execution. The system was therefore both a legal process and a power plant, and the audience played a part in it.
### LARGE +
Neuvillette presides from the seat directly above the Oratrice. The public's love of drama in the courtroom frames both Lyney's trial and the staged trial of Furina in 4.2.

## entry: Coppelius and Clockwork Coppelia
@keys: Coppelius, Clockwork Coppelia, Golden Hyperborea, Icewind Suite, Coppelia
@version: 4.0
@availability: permanent
### LARGE
Coppelius was a Fontainian playwright who lived about 500 years ago. He wrote from childhood, often reworking legends and historical people, and later returned again and again to eternal beauty and eternal love. His works include "Golden Hyperborea", about a homesick Lumidouce Bell whose beauty is preserved by icy winds, "Coppelia" and "Masque of the Great". Weapon text says one play loosely used the fallen noble Parsifal and the Blue-Eyed Spear Witch and inspired the stage names Parsifal the Great and Josephine.

His last opera, "Clockwork Coppelia", was never published in its original form: the opera house was destroyed on the day of its premiere, after the intermission, and Coppelius is presumed to have died there. A later script was reconstructed by an unnamed company from survivors' memories of the scenes before the intermission. In the published snippets Coppelius himself appears as a character: his plan is exposed to the Marechaussee Hunters, he is arrested and parted from his creation Coppelia, and in another scene Coppelia speaks of her weariness with the world.
### ULTRA +
Bossuet, a former Senior Technician of the Fontaine Research Institute, drew on the opera for the Icewind Suite, and Maillardet later added combat modes. The challenge has two versions, "Dirge of Coppelia" and "Nemesis of Coppelius". Notes at the Institute of Natural Philosophy, probably by Dwight Lasker, speak warmly of Coppelius's new work and suggest the premiere was before the cataclysm, but give no exact date.

## entry: Fontinalia Film Festival and the Furina Award
@keys: Fontinalia Film Festival, Furina Award, Fontaine Film Association, Musketeer Pictures, The Two Musketeers
@version: 4.3
@availability: limited
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
The Fontinalia Film Festival is a competition run by the Fontaine Film Association to introduce more people to film. It was shown during Roses and Muskets (4.3), a limited-time event. Entries are evaluated and the highest-scoring film gets the Furina Award, a name nobody changed after Furina abdicated. "The Two Musketeers", produced by Xavier's Musketeer Pictures and directed by Furina, won the first. The festival returned in Sunny Summer Fontinalia (6.7, also a limited-time event), where "Great Detective Hurlock" won and new awards for Best Director, Best Costume Design and Best Kamera Operation were added.
### LARGE +
In Roses and Muskets, Xavier's investor withdrew and a contract tied to the festival kept him from working with other investors until it ended. The event includes a film-editing challenge, Into the Frame. In 6.7 the films were screened at the Opera Epiclese, and characters such as Chiori, Clorinde, Citlali and Lyney appear in the quests.

## entry: Great Detective Hurlock and Mystery Fiction
@keys: Great Detective Hurlock, Hurlock, Case Files of Miss Orith, Miss Orith
@version: 4.0
@availability: permanent
### LARGE
Mystery novels such as "Great Detective Hurlock" and "The Case Files of Miss Orith" are very popular in Fontaine, according to the NPC Angelique. In the 6.7 limited event Sunny Summer Fontinalia, Lepine-Pauline, prompted by an apprentice named Ninette, adapted "Great Detective Hurlock" into a film with Charlotte as the lead. It won the latest Furina Award. Chiori says it beat the more artistic "A Homecoming" because it adapts a popular light novel. Charlotte wears a Hurlock detective costume.
### ULTRA +
Citlali says she came to Fontaine just for the premiere, which shows the film's pull beyond Fontaine.

## entry: Lyney's Magic and Cesar
@keys: Lyney's magic show, Great Magician, magic box, Cesar
@version: 4.0
@availability: permanent
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Lyney is known in Fontaine as the Great Magician. His show at the Opera Epiclese in the 4.0 Archon Quest ended in a trial after one of his assistants, Cowell, was killed when a water tank fell on the box he was in. In 4.2 a Giant Magic Box, built by the Fatui to replicate Lyney's original trick, was part of the plan that forced Furina onto the stage for her own trial. Cesar, a well-known Fontainian magician who enjoyed performing for the masses and was adored by children, taught Lyney and Lynette for ten days. He did not know they belonged to the House of the Hearth, and canon records that he died ten years before the game's events (see the quest The Forgotten Thief).
### LARGE +
Lyney's profile affiliation is the Hotel Bouffes d'ete, the House of the Hearth's orphanage, which is separate from his public fame as a stage magician.

## entry: Film Technology and Fantasyland
@keys: Kamera, Film Kamera, Chevalmarin Film Fantasyland, Film Fantasyland
@version: 5.6
@availability: limited
### LARGE
The Kamera originated in Fontaine, and there is a Film Kamera for moving pictures. Chevalmarin Film Fantasyland, an area tied to the limited-time event Whirling Waltz (5.6), is an amusement park themed on beloved films, built by Navia and the Spina di Rosula from an idea by Lepine-Pauline. Its official description says it is a stage for film fans to express their passion and for casual visitors to enjoy the sights, lights and sounds.
