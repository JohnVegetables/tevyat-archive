## summary
### LITE
Everyday life in Natlan, the Pyro nation of war, is hot, loud and physical. People belong to one of six tribes, and each tribe is bonded with its own kind of Saurian, the evolved dragons that live alongside humans. Conflict is treated as an honest way to settle matters, many adults can fight, and music, dancing and showing off are everyday pleasures. Hot springs draw tourists, miners work phlogiston-rich ore, and loose clothing is the answer to the climate.
### STANDARD +
The six tribes are the Children of Echoes (Nanatzcayan), Scions of the Canopy (Huitztlan), People of the Springs (Meztli), Flower-Feather Clan (Tlalocan), Masters of the Night-Wind (Mictlan) and Collective of Plenty (Teteocan). Each is run by its own Chief, and no single Natlanese populace answers to one ruler; the Pyro Archon handles national matters rather than tribal affairs. Natlan has no formal army: each tribe fields its own warriors.

Outsiders' remarks give the flavour. The Inazuman Aikawa Susumu, who soaked in its hot springs, called Natlan a country of red-hot passion in name and climate. The Fontainian sailor Lectoure would choose it as the first place to settle down, while worrying about its many tribes and rules. A man named Adel at Bayda Harbor praises its people as energetic and forged for battle from birth, and stresses that they are not barbarians.
### LARGE +
For roughly five hundred years the daily threat of the Abyss shaped ordinary life, and many able-bodied Natlanese are at least familiar with combat. Until the Night Kingdom was cleansed, people generally did not leave the nation at all, because their tribe's Wayob protected them from constant Abyssal influence. Since then travel abroad has been possible. That change is recent, so scenes set now can show a society getting used to open borders.

## practice
### LITE
Common routines: tribal work (mining, delivering, guiding, patrolling, shamanic watching, farming), training with weapons, caring for a Saurian companion, music and dance, soaking in hot springs, and settling disputes by contest.
### STANDARD +
Each tribe has its own economic niche. The Children of Echoes mine, smith and appraise gems, and are Natlan's only source of obsidian, used in rituals such as communing with the Night Kingdom. The People of the Springs live largely from tourism, especially their famous hot springs. The Scions of the Canopy take delivery and hunting commissions and host visitors for extreme sports such as rock climbing, rappelling, bungee jumping and volcano parkour. The Collective of Plenty farm fertile land, trading or giving away surplus to other tribes; Varesa's family farm sells produce abroad, for example to the Fontainian chefs Escoffier and Dori. The Flower-Feather Clan are rangers and riders, and the Masters of the Night-Wind are priests and shamans who watch the ley lines and phlogiston.

Saurians are partners, not pets. They can choose their human companion and refuse anyone they do not accept. They understand human speech but humans cannot understand theirs; long association lets tribe members read a Saurian's body language and mood. Saurians have territories whose borders other Saurians respect, and these borders largely mark the Natlanese tribal lands as well.

Conflict is called "reason" in Natlan: parties are expected to face their own desires honestly, then decide the outcome by contest. When a dispute cannot be resolved, "peacemakers" such as Chasca may step in.
### LARGE +
Technology builds partly on the ancient dragon civilisation. Phlogiston, a natural high-temperature energy, powers many tools, and Phlogiston Engravings imitate the Iridescent Inscriptions the dragons used; they can store information and create Spiritways. Much dragon technology (called the "Secret Source") is lost or undeciphered, and survives mainly in automatons. Many phlogiston vehicles come from the Children of Echoes: Kachina's drill, Chasca's flying cannon, Mavuika's all-terrain motorcycle and Xilonen's portable DJ set; the wiki credits Xilonen with building all of these. Jetpacks called Phlogiston Wings were designed by Tepal and finished by his mother Allpa after he died in the effort; they are rarely distributed, so most people use more traditional transport.

Tribal crafts: the Children of Echoes use drills at many scales and keep an enormous one above their settlement; the People of the Springs press circular record discs (also playable on Fontainian record players), make string and percussion instruments, and build artificial hot springs deliberately cooler than natural ones; the Masters of the Night-Wind weave scrolls recording events, with weavers for major events appointed by the Iktomisaur Elder Tlapo; the Flower-Feather Clan use hot air balloons to hold wooden platforms aloft.
### ULTRA +
Tribal warriors favour swords, clubs, spears, javelins, giant hammers, saw-like weapons and hand cannons, plus oversized gear such as collapsible slingshots, circular saws and portable cannons. Many dress or fight in imitation of their tribe's Saurian, and can use some of its elemental or special abilities, such as levitation, telekinesis or "surfing" on land, to a degree that depends on skill.

An Inazuman character, Shousen, remembered Natlanese boxers practising by imagining an opponent's moves, a method that inspired Shousen to develop Shinryuu, the Inazuman martial art featured in the limited-time Phantom Flow event. Saurians are poached by criminal groups in the modern day, and during Och-Kan's era humans subjugated them.

## regional_variation
### STANDARD
Tribal homes differ. The Children of Echoes live in Tequemecan Valley in the east; the Scions of the Canopy on Coatepec Mountain in the southeast; the People of the Springs at Toyac Springs in the south; the Flower-Feather Clan on Quahuacan Cliff; the Masters of the Night-Wind in the Tezcatepetonco Range; and the Collective of Plenty at Atocpan. Their Chiefs are Pacal, Wayna, Amina, Mutota, Biram and Acatl respectively. Each tribe pairs with a Saurian: Tepetlisaurus, Yumkasaurus, Koholasaurus, Qucusaurus, Iktomisaurus and Tatankasaurus.
### LARGE +
Chiefs oversee their tribe's customs and can change them, and even the Pyro Archon cannot overrule them provided their tribe agrees. There is no age requirement: being chosen by the previous Chief and able to bear the duties is enough. Amina, Chief of the People of the Springs, is a young girl chosen by her grandfather and confirmed by the elders. The present Chiefs are on good terms but busy.

The Easybreeze Holiday Resort (also Tenochtzitoc, said to mean "cavern of stone and prickly pears") is a sparsely inhabited island off Natlan's westernmost edge, added in 5.8 as the first permanent summer-themed map. The wiki describes it as where the war began and met its end. The Tenochtzitoc were an Anemo tribe exiled in the past, while other exiled Muratans went on to settle in Mondstadt. The resort has a market and a leisure atmosphere distinct from the tribal homelands.
### ULTRA +
Saurian traits differ: young Yumkasaurs like having their ears touched but dislike tail grooming, and adults are proud to show off groomed tails; Qucusaurs are proud and, particularly with the Flower-Feather Clan, will carry a rider only if they sense no hesitation, since a rider's mistake could badly hurt both; Iktomisaurs can live over two centuries and meditate often; Tatankasaurs are docile until angered, then unreasonable until spent.

## scene_use
### STANDARD
Good hooks: a hot-spring visit where an outsider must first acclimate in a cooler artificial pool; a tribal contest or dance-off; a Saurian that refuses a new companion; a miner showing obsidian; a courier from the Scions; a friendly argument settled as a bout. Tone is warm, boisterous and competitive, with open pride in tribe.
### LARGE +
Time sensitivity matters. Before the Night Kingdom was cleansed, the Natlanese seldom left and the Pilgrimage of the Return of the Sacred Flame (the tournaments tied to the Night Warden Wars) was an ever-present, costly institution. After it, the Pilgrimage was revised into athletic competitions, the old Bloomflower Trials returned, and the Natlanese could travel. Do not place Natlanese characters abroad in earlier-era scenes without a reason.

## entry: Natlanese Tribes in Daily Life
@keys: Nanatzcayan, Huitztlan, Meztli, Tlalocan, Mictlan, Teteocan
@version: 5.0
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
The six tribes use Nahuatl-style names: Nanatzcayan (Children of Echoes, Geo, miners), Huitztlan (Scions of the Canopy, Dendro, couriers and explorers), Meztli (People of the Springs, Hydro, guides), Tlalocan (Flower-Feather Clan, Pyro, rangers and riders), Mictlan (Masters of the Night-Wind, Cryo, shamans) and Teteocan (Collective of Plenty, Electro, farmers and wrestlers). Each tribe's Wayob grants Ancient Names to worthy members, who are revered and have their deeds recorded.

## entry: Saurian Companions
@keys: Saurian, Saurians, Yumkasaur, Yumkasaurus, Tepetlisaur, Koholasaur, Qucusaur, Iktomisaur, Tatankasaur
@version: 5.0
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
Saurians are the evolved descendants of Natlan's Pyro vishaps, who lost their ancient wisdom after the Heavenly Principles' conquest (Kukulkan and Och-Kan saw this as devolution). They cannot speak human language, except Cacucu, who copies a few phrases of Ifa's; Ajaw, a surviving older dragon, can communicate with both humans and Saurians. Saurians choose their companions and follow emotion and trust. Large wild flocks of Qucusaurs live in Ochkanatlan and the Great Volcano of Tollan. Little One, a Saurian with a red mark called the Flamelord's Blessing, shows that marked Saurians have stronger powers.

## entry: Phlogiston Tools and Vehicles
@keys: Phlogiston Wings, Phlogiston Engraving, Phlogiston Engravings, Secret Source
@version: 5.0
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
Phlogiston Engravings imitate dragon Iridescent Inscriptions to store information and create Spiritways. "Secret Source" refers to undeciphered draconic technology, mostly automatons (Dragon Cannons, Dragon Idols, combat constructs) that react to Nightsoul's Blessing. Phlogiston Wings jetpacks were designed by Tepal and finished by Allpa after his death; few exist. Xilonen built the other well-known phlogiston vehicles and her DJ set.

## entry: Easybreeze Holiday Resort Life
@keys: Easybreeze Holiday Resort, Easybreeze, Tenochtzitoc
@version: 5.8
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
A sparsely inhabited island off Natlan's westernmost edge, also called Tenochtzitoc, the first summer-themed map permanently added to the game (5.8). Its subareas include the Concealed Land of Enigmas, Colorfall Cliffs, Easybreeze Market and Tete Isle. Prickly pears, which the name refers to, no longer grow there.
