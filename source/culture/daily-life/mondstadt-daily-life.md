## summary
### LITE
Mondstadt is a laid-back city-state of farms, windmills, vineyards and taverns where freedom, wine and song are the shared ideals. No monarch rules it. The Knights of Favonius keep order, the Church of Favonius handles worship, and the Adventurers' Guild takes on odd jobs. Ordinary life is peaceful, social and open to outsiders.
### STANDARD +
The mild climate under Barbatos' blessing, the high literacy and the lack of strong social rank make Mondstadt feel informal. People talk to each other freely and strangers such as the Traveler are welcomed. Wine is both the main industry and the main weakness of the city.
### LARGE +
Under the easy surface there is real friction: heavy drinking harms some families and the Church can do little about it. Mondstadters are also open to the unusual, as their acceptance of Eula, of Albedo's alchemy and of Durin shows.

## practice
### LITE
- **Work.** Farming, winemaking, hunting, smithing, trading and serving in taverns are the usual trades. Apples, grapes and sunsettias are plentiful, and most farms have a windmill.
- **Drink.** Dandelion wine and apple cider are the best-known drinks. Alcohol is part of social life, from the Angel's Share tavern to family dinners.
- **Order.** The Knights patrol the city and countryside, settle disputes and fight hilichurls. Most people expect help from them rather than fear them.
### STANDARD +
- **Education.** Most people are tutored rather than schooled, and those who choose a trade apprentice to a master, like Schulz with the smith Wagner or Sucrose and Timaeus with Albedo. Mondstadters are generally literate. Razor, raised by wolves, is the one known present-day exception.
- **Gliding.** Many people enjoy gliding with a Wind Glider. By the wiki's account a licence is required, taken after an exam with a fee that can sometimes be waived. Instructors such as Amber teach, and serious violations can cost a glider their licence. Springvale's low houses make it a popular practice ground.
### LARGE +
- **Library.** The city's library was once the largest in northern Teyvat. After the Great Fire of Fall Equinox, at an unknown date, only a sixth of it remained, and people still read there.
- **Homes.** The city is built in a half-timbered, Gothic style; villages have smaller country houses. Fireplaces are less necessary because the climate stays moderate, but house styles have not changed much.
- **Symbolism.** The rose stands for keeping secrets, not love, in Mondstadt: as wine bottles are corked, so are lips sealed.
- **Social rank.** Class differences have been small since the aristocracy ended long ago. A few families with long lineages, such as the Gunnhildr and Ragnvindr, still have good reputations, but this brings respect rather than privilege.
- **Agriculture.** Fresh fruit and grapes are plentiful, and carrots and radishes grow in small gardens at Springvale and the Dawn Winery. Philanemo mushrooms grow near homes and are part of the local diet. Dairy foods show up in recipes even though cattle are never seen.
- **Alcohol and families.** Nimrod, a relapsing drinker, mentions the "Four Drunkards" as the city's biggest drinkers; his wife Eury and Sister Victoria worry, and the bartender Charles contacts Eury when Nimrod drinks too much. Diona dislikes alcohol because her father Draff changes when he is drunk, though she does not link his behaviour to his drinking. After children complained to Grace about drunk fathers stumbling out of wardrobes, deaconesses began blessing newly built wardrobes. Confessors like Victoria are frustrated that prayer cannot address the cause.
- **Trade.** Wine is the main export. The Dawn Winery has over a thousand years of history and is the best-known supplier, and outsiders have tried, honestly and not, to learn how its dandelion wine is made. Liyue merchants buy alcohol and resell it elsewhere, usually arriving through the Stone Gate, while foreign visitors mostly dock at Dornman Port. Dornman Port itself is less developed because it was built inside ruins.
- **Travel.** The Adventurers' Guild's Teyvat Travel Highlights (author unknown) describe a visiting bard who met Guild members often on the road, and whose packing list includes a tent, maps, rations and a first-aid kit.
### ULTRA +
- **Variety of drink.** Besides grape wine there are hard ciders from apples and cherries, beer served in tankards at Angel's Share, and a spirit called jagerschnapps mentioned by name. The community wiki compares dandelion wine's strength to absinthe, but canon gives no figure.
- **Cuisine.** Alcohol ingredients like apples often appear in dishes. Familiar dishes include Moon Pie, Mondstadt Grilled Fish and Mondstadt Hash Browns. Cuisine has its own entry.
- **Reputation abroad.** Outsiders such as the Liyue Qixing and the Fatui treat the Knights as the ruling party, even though the Knights are not an explicit ruling entity.

## regional_variation
### STANDARD
- **Mondstadt City.** On an island in Cider Lake, with a central plaza, the Cathedral and the Knights' headquarters. Busy market streets and taverns.
- **Springvale and the Dawn Winery.** Rural life: hunting, orchard work, vegetable gardens and glider practice at Springvale, vineyards and cellars at the winery.
- **Dornman Port.** The trading and fishing harbour in the north, more rustic than the city.
- **Wolvendom.** Wild country, not settled life. It is where Razor grew up among wolves.
### LARGE +
Rural and city people share the same easy manner. Farms rely on windmills, and many people outside the city live in older and smaller houses.

## politics
@version: 1.0
### STANDARD
Mondstadt has no monarch. Before Vennessa's rebellion the Lawrence Clan governed, first justly and later with greed and arrogance. After the rebellion the Knights of Favonius took over keeping order. Barbatos asked that people be left free, so the Knights do not formally rule, though outsiders such as the Qixing and the Fatui treat them as the ruling party. Their Grand Master is Varka, who took a group of Knights to Nod-Krai; in his absence Jean serves as Acting Grand Master and meets visiting delegations. The Knights are based in the city and patrol the countryside.
### LARGE +
Other bodies fill in the gaps: the Church of Favonius hears confessions and leads prayer but has little practical power, the Adventurers' Guild posts commissions, and the Dawn Winery dominates the economy.

## festivals
@version: 1.0
### STANDARD
The year's main festivals are the Windblume Festival (a celebration of the Thousand Winds, about offering windblumes to Barbatos and loved ones; its origins lie in the rebels against Decarabian who used the flower as a code), Ludi Harpastum (fifteen days of food, games and music celebrating Barbatos' gift of wine and freedom) and Weinlesefest (new wine and the return of the Anemo Archon each autumn). Ordinary people take part through cooking, decorating and attending, and each has its own entry.
### LARGE +
During Ludi Harpastum, taverns put their best dishes outside, the Dawn Winery sends out barrels of beer and cider, and families hang a handmade Harpastum ball on their door. A visiting bard in the Travel Highlights notes that people have mostly forgotten the festival's sacred origins. A gliding championship is held in the last days. At the Windblume Festival, which wildflower was the "real" Windblume has been forgotten and is a subject of friendly debate.

## scene_use
### LITE
Set scenes in a warm, breezy city: windmills turning, wine and apples at stalls, bards in the plaza, Knights in white armour on patrol and gliders overhead. Mondstadters are friendly, informal and curious about travellers.
### STANDARD +
- Typical settings are a market street, Angel's Share, a sunny orchard, a farm with a windmill or the library.
- Characters will mention wine, the Knights and the weather far more than rank or ceremony.
- Do not depict a monarch, a strict law code or an official caste system.
- Drinking is common but not harmless; a scene can include a worried family member or a heavy drinker without turning it into a joke.
### LARGE +
Treat Varka's absence and the Knights' workload as background, not as a crisis. Keep references at or before Version 7.1 and use only the details above; prices, laws and holidays beyond those here are not established.

## entry: Gliding in Mondstadt
@keys: gliding licence, glider licence, gliding championship, gliding exam
@version: 1.0
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
Gliding with a Wind Glider is a widely loved Mondstadt pastime. According to the community wiki, a licence is required and given after an exam with a fee, and instructors such as Amber give lessons. Violations can bring fines and, in serious cases, licence suspension. Springvale's low houses make it a popular place to practise. A gliding championship is held during Ludi Harpastum.

## entry: Drinking Culture
@keys: Four Drunkards, drunk fathers, wardrobe blessing
@keys2: Mondstadt, Nimrod, Victoria, Grace
@version: 1.0
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
Alcohol is central to Mondstadt's economy and social life, and a number of people struggle with it. Nimrod mentions the "Four Drunkards" as the city's biggest drinkers, and is himself a relapsing drinker, which worries his wife Eury and Sister Victoria. Diona dislikes alcohol because of her father Draff's changes when drunk. After children told Grace about drunk fathers stumbling out of wardrobes, deaconesses began blessing new wardrobes.

## entry: Mondstadt Library
@keys: Mondstadt Library, Great Fire of Fall Equinox
@version: 1.0
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
Once the largest library in northern Teyvat, the Mondstadt Library was reduced to a sixth of its size by the Great Fire of Fall Equinox, at an unknown date. It still holds many books, and people read there.
