## summary
@spoiler: major
### LITE
Daily life in Nod-Krai, the moon-blessed autonomous borderland at Snezhnaya's southern edge, is a frontier life. There is no Archon and no real government. Nasha Town, a port built mostly of scrap metal and crossed by cargo rails, is both a paradise of freedom and a lawless jungle, and people from many nations end up there. The Voynich Guild keeps trade orderly, the volunteer Lightkeepers fight the Wild Hunt, and the Frostmoon Scions keep the old moon customs. Days are short, nights are long, and kuuvahki machines are everywhere.
### STANDARD +
Visitors call the region an "Elysium," and the word cuts both ways: it promises opportunity and escape, but the land is also hostile, with limited resources and few protective or regulating forces apart from the Voynich Guild. Anyone suspected of carrying a lot of Mora becomes a target, and newcomers are warned to be wary.

For scenes, the best tone is practical, wry and a little patched together: improvised machines, a deal struck under a guild stamp, a story told over a meal, and a quiet awareness that the Wild Hunt is never far.
### LARGE +
Three facts shape everyday attitudes. First, nobody can rely on a state: the Voynich Guild, the Curatorium of Secrets, the restaurant Speranza and the Fatui all influence Nasha Town, and none governs it. Second, everyone lives close to kuuvahki, the moon-power that predates the seven elements, so machines and customs revolve around it. Third, the Abyssal Wild Hunt has plagued the land since the Cataclysm, so ordinary people lean on the Lightkeepers even when they do not join.
### ULTRA +
By the end of the Song of the Welkin Moon arc (6.x, still the case as of 7.1) the mood has shifted. The Fatui had moved in under Dottore and Sandrone with their "Palestar Edict" and the Kuuvahki Experimental Design Bureau, and locals resented them. After Dottore's defeat the Edict was revoked, Sandrone apologised to the Frostmoon Scions, and a new League formed jointly by the region's factions was announced in Nasha Town's plaza. Life in town is described as returning after an evacuation and siege, but the details are mostly not spelled out, so use them lightly.

## practice
### LITE
Common routines: trade and haggling in Nasha Town, repairing and building kuuvahki machines, buying and selling information, patrol and watch duty for the Lightkeepers, and keeping lanterns and Tideseal Stones in good order against the Wild Hunt.
### STANDARD +
Nasha Town is the centre of ordinary life. It sits on Lempo Isle as Nod-Krai's main port and commercial centre. Because resources are limited it is built largely of scrap metal; it is also highly industrialised, with cargo rails overhead and kuuvahki-powered machines on every corner. Everyday places include the Adventurers' Guild branch (Katheryne at reception, Sarin as branch master), Mimisbrunnr Books (owner Dhabit; the stock does not refresh and each book can be bought once), the Rossum Workshop smithy (owner Arkhyp, now operated by his daughter Lyulka), the restaurant Speranza (run by Katya, the region's main restaurant and the home base of the "Conch Gang"), the tavern The Flagship and a Northland Bank branch.

Authority is informal. The Voynich Guild, a guild of local merchants based in Nasha Town, is the nearest thing Nod-Krai has to a regulator: it manages trade deals, imports and exports, issues business certificates, investigates and settles disputes, and backs the development of certain technologies. Beside it stands the Curatorium of Secrets, a commission agency that fronts the Northern Intelligence Network, an underground volunteer forum where information is traded for payment.

The Lightkeepers are the defence. They are volunteers who swear a shared oath, carry Oath Lanterns that other members can detect, and answer Wild Hunt incursions wherever they appear. Their headquarters, Piramida, is a city on top of an ancient castle, with a mead hall on its top level where banquets follow hard-won victories. They have set Tideseal Stones across the region to push back the Wild Hunt.
### LARGE +
Machines are part of the household. Kuuvahki appears in two forms: polarised, shown as blue or red, whose objects attract or repel each other, and unpolarised, found in the north, which opposes Abyssal energy. People cope by building. The Clink-Clank Krumkake Craftshop on Lempo Isle, home of the young inventor Aino and her automaton Ineffa, is the source of small bots and of the cannon that guards Nasha Town. Amazing 'Bots roam Lempo Isle and cleaning bots can be controlled from panels on Paha Isle.

Information is a currency. The Curatorium takes commissions from anyone; the network behind it is loosely structured with few rules, and many members gave up positions, reputations, even names to join. Nefer, the Curatorium's owner, is also a member of the Voynich Guild, and the Curatorium's headquarters stands directly behind the Guild's. Adventurers such as Nefer and Jahoda can gather intel on Nasha Town's people and trade it at the Adventurers' Guild.

Lightkeeper life: the group is led by the Starshyna and organised into Sergeant Majors, Squad Leaders and ordinary fighters called Ratniki, who are all volunteers. A Ratnik named Dori says not all members share the same obligations, so many consider early retirement or quitting. Ratniki who break direct orders can be reprimanded, and a member too injured for field duty may be given another role if they still want to serve, as with the doctor Ivar. Squads have names, for example the "Nightmare Orioles," who keep watch over Kipumaki Cliff from Cliffwatch Camp.
### ULTRA +
Foodways and faith have their own entries; briefly, the Frostmoon Scions appear to abstain from meat, favouring spring water, fruit and vegetables, and the Lightkeepers' banquets keep a Snezhnayan court custom of offering the first pour to the dead.

The Frostmoon Scions run on rank: a Moonchanter leads, followed by Priestesses, Acolytes and Adherents (ordinary followers, who must pass an exam on hymns and blessings). Males of the tribe cannot become Acolytes or Priests. Adherents also live and work in Nasha Town's central plaza. After Lauma told them the sky of Teyvat is false and that they had worshipped the wrong moon, some left the Scions for good, and some former Scions joined the Lightkeepers shortly before Moon-Prayer Night. Those who stayed agreed to treat Columbina as herself rather than as a goddess.

Small details from the pages read: Illuga's Piramida mailbox holds letters from people who want to join the Lightkeepers, including a child (he tells them to return when older), and he says recruitment has recently grown; Nasha Town's merchants, who normally ignore the "public interest," are said to be beginning to see why the Lightkeepers fight; Speranza has a "Lucky Spin" wheel that gives one of several proverb-style fortunes; and townsfolk often mistake Paimon for a fae.

## regional_variation
### STANDARD
Nasha Town on Lempo Isle: busy, industrial and mixed. Hiisi Island, a short way across the water: forest, the Frostmoon Enclave and the Scions' quiet, devout community. Piramida: a fortress-city of volunteers and veterans. Paha Isle: home of the Fatui-built Kuuvahki Experimental Design Bureau. The north (Voidsea Outlook, Wavechaser Plain, Ashveil Peak, Kipumaki Cliff): Abyssal blight and hazards that need lanterns, spirit blooms and Lightkeeper cover.
### LARGE +
South and north differ in how kuuvahki behaves and in how people live with the Abyss. In the north, Oath Lanterns charged with kuuvahki currents reduce the effects of Abyssal Decay, and so do allied Lightkeepers standing nearby. In the southern isles (Lempo, Hiisi, Paha) the lanterns are tied to the Lightkeepers' Oath quest. The Wild Hunt rarely travels to Hiisi Island because the moon's power deters it.
### ULTRA +
Snezhnaya's influence is mild. Nod-Krai is formally part of Snezhnaya as an autonomous region under the Lightkeepers' jurisdiction by an edict of the Tsaritsa after the Cataclysm, and locals rarely use Snezhnayan forms of address for her. The Snowland Fae, who once ruled the Northlands, mostly stay in Snezhnaya because many cannot tolerate warmer climates; only a few fae live in Nod-Krai, such as the Lantern Fae Flins (living as a human Ratnik) and the Augury Bird Linnea.

## scene_use
### STANDARD
Good scene hooks: a scrap-metal market stall, a Voynich stamp someone needs before a deal, a quiet commission at the Curatorium, a Lightkeeper patrol returning to Piramida for a meal, an engineer testing a new kuuvahki gadget, a fortune wheel at Speranza, a boat to Hiisi Island, lanterns lit against a misty night.
### LARGE +
Keep the tone wry rather than grim. People joke, haggle and trade favours, and they do not expect officials because there are few. Do not turn Nod-Krai into a place where everyone is a criminal: most residents are merchants, workers, engineers, adventurers and volunteers who chose a hard land. Keep the Wild Hunt as a background danger, and do not give the Lightkeepers or the Scions a single collective personality beyond what canon shows.
### ULTRA +
Spoiler handling: while the Fatui hold the Design Bureau and the Palestar Edict stands, the mood is tense and resentful. After the Welkin Moon arc the Edict is revoked, so scenes set "now" (as of 7.1) should use the calmer post-crisis mood. Do not let characters mention Moon-Prayer Night's secret events or Columbina's identity unless they would know them.

## examples
### STANDARD
A merchant stamps a certificate and slides it across the counter without looking up. A Ratnik hangs an Oath Lantern by her door before the evening mist. A young engineer tests a hand-built bot on the quay while a neighbour watches. A Scions adherent in a street draped with Moonveils hands over a handmade gift.

## entry: Nasha Town
@keys: Nasha Town, The Flagship, Speranza, Mimisbrunnr Books
@version: 6.0
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
Nasha Town is the seaside port and commercial heart of Nod-Krai, on Lempo Isle. It has no official governing body and is influenced by the Voynich Guild, the Curatorium of Secrets, Speranza and the Fatui. It is mostly built of scrap metal, heavily industrial, with cargo rails overhead and kuuvahki machines throughout. Residents and visitors include merchants, sailors, repairmen, engineers, adventurers, Frostmoon Scions adherents, Fatui soldiers and a Fontaine Research Institute researcher. "Nasha" comes from a Slavic word meaning "our."
### LARGE +
Notable places: the Adventurers' Guild branch, Mimisbrunnr Books, Rossum Workshop, Northland Bank, Speranza (run by Katya), the tavern The Flagship, Nuts 'N' Nuts and the Vesnici Proleca Greenhouse. The central plaza has merchants, specialty sellers and Scions adherents. The wiki intro describes it as both a paradise of freedom and a lawless jungle.

## entry: Voynich Guild
@keys: Voynich Guild, Voynich, Tarno
@version: 6.0
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
A guild of local merchants based in Nasha Town and one of Nod-Krai's most powerful factions. It is the nearest thing the region has to a regulatory authority, managing trade deals, imports and exports, business certificates and dispute investigation, and backing some technological development. It works closely with the Curatorium of Secrets; Nefer belongs to both. Tarno is a Guild employee.

## entry: Curatorium of Secrets and the Northern Intelligence Network
@keys: Curatorium of Secrets, Northern Intelligence Network, Curatorium
@version: 6.0
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
The Curatorium of Secrets is a commission agency in Nasha Town run by Nefer, with Jahoda as an employee. Behind it is the Northern Intelligence Network, an underground volunteer forum for well-connected people who sell information for payment. The network is loosely structured with few rules, and many members gave up their positions, reputations or names to join. Listed informants include Iratxa, Sarin and Tarno; listed clients include Yelan and Varka.
### LARGE +
The Curatorium served as the heroes' base during Song of the Welkin Moon. Its permanent front-desk employee Jahoda is a former Seahook Gang member with a mechanical arm built by Aino, and a lazy black cat called Ashru lounges on the counter.

## entry: Lightkeepers
@keys: Lightkeepers, Lightkeeper, Ratnik, Ratniki, Starshyna, Piramida
@version: 6.0
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
The Lightkeepers are Nod-Krai's volunteer defenders against the Wild Hunt, founded five hundred years ago by Solovei, the first Starshyna, under a royal edict of the Tsaritsa after the Cataclysm. Their insignia is a nightingale holding a burning wick. They are led by a Starshyna (Nikita, per the wiki), with Sergeant Majors, Squad Leaders and ordinary fighters called Ratniki, and are headquartered at Piramida. They are short of members. Illuga leads the "Nightmare Orioles" squad and Flins serves as a Ratnik.
### LARGE +
Piramida's top-level mead hall holds banquets after victories, with the first pour offered to the dead. A statue of Solovei the Torchforger stands in the city. Members swear an oath on joining and carry Oath Lanterns so other Lightkeepers can detect them.

## entry: Frostmoon Scions
@keys: Frostmoon Scions, Frostmoon Scion, Moonchildren, Moonchanter, Frostmoon Enclave
@spoiler: major
@version: 6.0
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
The Frostmoon Scions are the indigenous tribe of Nod-Krai, descended from survivors of the golden realm of Hyperborea. They live around Hiisi Island and the Frostmoon Enclave, led by the Moonchanter Lauma, and are organised by rank (Moonchanter, Priestess, Acolyte, Adherent). They appear to abstain from meat and they carved the Statues of the New Moon.
### LARGE +
After Lauma revealed that the visible moon was false, some members left the group. Those who stayed agreed to treat Columbina as herself, not as a goddess. Sandrone later apologised to the Scions for the Fatui's actions and the two sides agreed to take steps to reconcile.

## entry: Moon-Prayer Night
@keys: Moon-Prayer Night, Kuunrukous, Moon-Prayer Blossoms, Moonveils
@spoiler: minor
@version: 6.0
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
Moon-Prayer Night is Nod-Krai's liveliest festival, held on the night the moon is closest to Teyvat, when kuuvahki peaks. The date is not known in advance: people estimate it and then wait for the Verdant Crests and Moon-Prayer Blossoms to change, and the waiting is part of the fun. Scions once wore the blossoms, sang and danced to celebrate the Moon Goddess's birth, and gave away food and handicrafts. Today they hang Moonveils on Nasha Town's buildings so the night sky looks misty through them, and most people treat it as a chance to do business. Phantoms of the past appear around the festival.

## entry: Wild Hunt
@keys: Wild Hunt, Wasteland Wild Hunt, Tideseal Stone, Tideseal Stones
@spoiler: major
@version: 6.0
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
The Wild Hunt is an Abyssal phenomenon, strongest in Nod-Krai, that turns the dead into ghouls. Locals tell folk stories about it, such as a demonic host harvesting souls or the phantoms of an ancient nightmare. The Lightkeepers fight it and have placed Tideseal Stones to repel it. Fae can understand the ghouls' speech.
### LARGE +
The Wild Hunt began when Rerir, one of Khaenri'ah's Five Sinners, was torn apart trying to follow his lost lover through a moon gate. After he was expelled from Teyvat, the Abyss made an imitation of Roland to lead it; that form expired during Varka's story quest, and as of 7.1 the Hunt is leaderless but still dangerous.

## entry: Statues of the New Moon
@keys: Statues of the New Moon, Statue of the New Moon, Idol of the New Moon
@version: 6.0
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
Giant stone statues placed across Nod-Krai by the Frostmoon Scions in the image of their newborn Moon Goddess. Travellers pray to them for protection. Some were caged in steel by the Fatui.
