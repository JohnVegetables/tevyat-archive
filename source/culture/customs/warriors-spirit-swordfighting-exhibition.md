## summary
### LITE
The Warrior's Spirit Swordfighting Exhibition is a traditional Inazuman swordsmanship tournament. By legend, the champion earned the honour of crossing swords with the Almighty Shogun herself, with one of her familiars sent to aid and protect the human fighter. Today the Shogun does not attend: the contest ends with an exhibition match in which the champion fights beside a swordsman playing the "Archon's Emissary" against an envoy standing for the Shogun's divine power. The only edition shown in canon was held on Ritou in Version 3.4, hosted by the Yashiro Commission.
### STANDARD +
The name carries an ideal as much as a sport: the capacity to bear the weight of charity and mercy, and the capability to wield both blade and might. The Shogun is said to have wished to pass on her skill and her will to protect Inazuma's people through martial arts, and the event is presented as a way to spread the elegance of the sword to more people.
### LARGE +
The 3.4 edition doubled as a public spectacle with entry tickets, spectators' ballots and a popular figure (Kamisato Ayaka) as its face. In-world this was a deliberate effort by the organisers to widen the audience.

## practice
### STANDARD
As Ayaka explains it, the event comes from ancient Inazuman legends in which the martial artist crowned champion was granted a duel with the Almighty Shogun. Since the Shogun was far stronger than any human, the duel would have ended instantly, so she sent a familiar as an emissary who assisted and protected the champion. Later the event became a swordsmanship competition and kept the divine duel as its closing "exhibition match", so that later generations can understand where Warrior's Spirit comes from and what martial arts mean. Today the Shogun sends a special envoy to fight on her behalf. The champion takes the "Martial Artist" role, and the "Divine Familiar" (the Archon's Emissary) is filled by a true swordsman.
### LARGE +
Entry in the 3.4 edition was by numbered entry ticket. Madarame Hyakubei of the Kamisato household says applications were so many that every ticket had been issued a week before the event, and describes the contest as both a chance for martial artists to test themselves and a form of honour. The Kono Ittou Art's two disciples, who held a single ticket between them, treated it as so important that they were ready to settle by duel which of them would use it. The vote results were announced on the third day of the competition, and the exhibition match followed the final round.
### ULTRA +
Gameplay framing (a game abstraction, not a description of in-world rounds): the event is played as six duels, one unlocked each day, against Ibuki, Hirotatsu, Younan, Taroumaru, Akatsuki Saemon Juuzou and, last, Maguu Kengou. Elemental Skills and Bursts are disabled, and the player instead gets a Parry and temporary Honed Techniques. The event ran from 2023-02-09 to 2023-02-20.

## regional_variation
### STANDARD
Rules were relaxed in 3.4. Previously competitors had to practise one of Inazuma's schools of swordsmanship. Onodera of Yae Publishing House, who helped organise the revival, says anyone with a passion for martial arts could now enter, regardless of nationality or status, and weapons were no longer limited to one-handed swords: any close-range weapon within the length and weight requirements was allowed. The field accordingly included a polearm fighter from the desert (Younan) alongside Inazuman swordsmen.
### LARGE +
The exhibition match also changed in presentation. In the past the Shogun herself chose what the Archon's Emissary wore; in 3.4, with her support, the choice was handed to the audience. Ayaka notes that in earlier times only the most skilled swordsmen of reputable clans were eligible for the Emissary's role.

## scene_use
### STANDARD
Use it as a source of pride and rivalry among Inazuma's swordsmen, a conversation topic for anyone who trains with a blade, and a bridge between tradition and a modern, crowd-pleasing show. Inazumans know it as a famous contest with a ceremonial duel at its end, and the Kamisato household and the Kujou are closely tied to its present form.
### LARGE +
Keep the tone respectful of the sword arts but friendly: contestants may be earnest to the point of overreaction (the Kono Ittou Art disciples nearly fought each other over one ticket), while organisers worry about spectacle and public opinion. Do not invent winners, past emissaries or a regular calendar. Because the 3.4 edition was a limited-time event, characters who were not there should know the tradition but not the details of that edition.

## examples
@availability: limited
@version: 3.4
### STANDARD
The 3.4 edition took place on Ritou in the indoor training area of the Kanjou Commission, hosted by the Yashiro Commission, with Hiiragi Chisato helping with the venue and Onodera of Yae Publishing House planning the event. Its champion was the Traveler. The finale paired the Traveler and Kamisato Ayaka against the Maguu Kengou, presented as the Almighty Shogun's chosen envoy, and they won. Kujou Sara attended on the Shogun's orders to observe and then returned to the Tenshukaku to report.
### LARGE +
Hirotatsu, a retainer at the Kamisato estate, belittled the Traveler for hesitating to enter, then lost fairly and apologised. Ayaka, who had never before acted as ambassador for an event or fought in an elaborate costume, had worried about the role. After the match she said she had long wanted to spar with the Traveler.

## status
### STANDARD
As of 7.1 only the 3.4 edition (February 2023) is shown in canon. The Shogun's involvement remains limited to sending an envoy, and Ayaka is recorded as the most recent Archon's Emissary.

## entry: Archon's Emissary
@keys: Archon's Emissary, Emissary of the Archon, Divine Familiar, Warrior's Spirit
@version: 3.4
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
The Archon's Emissary is the figure in the exhibition match who stands in for the Almighty Shogun. In the legend, a familiar of the Shogun aided the champion in the duel against her; today the role is played by a true swordsman while a special envoy shows the Shogun's divine strength. In the 3.4 edition Kamisato Ayaka was the Emissary, fighting beside the champion against the Maguu Kengou. The idea of making Ayaka the Emissary came from Onodera, and she accepted it as an honour and a chance to promote the sword arts.
### LARGE +
Ayaka was nervous because she had never been the public face of such an event and was to fight in a showy outfit. She had also considered writing to the Traveler for advice, but did not send the letter, saying she did not want to depend on the Traveler for everything.

## entry: Emissary outfit vote
@keys: Emissary's outfit, outfit vote, Case Files of Miss Orith, Pretty Please Kitsune Guuji
@version: 3.4
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
For the 3.4 edition, ballots were meant to let the public choose among four seasonal Inazuman styles for Ayaka's exhibition outfit. The ballot boxes for Warrior's Spirit and for the other events were mixed up by a staff member, Mizuta, while Onodera was away, so the announced results named outfits from popular novels instead: "The Case Files of Miss Orith" and "Pretty Please, Kitsune Guuji?". The Fontaine-style outfit of Miss Orith won by a wide margin. Rather than rerun the vote, Ayaka decided to wear it, reasoning that the public had taken the result as a pleasant surprise. The organisers tailored it to her measurements; it bore the Kamisato crest and allowed more freedom of movement than she had expected.
### LARGE +
Ayaka saw it as a way to present something new in a traditional form, and as a way to fulfil her late mother's curiosity about other nations' clothing.
