## summary
### LITE
The Hot Pot Game is a light guessing game played at a hot pot meal. Each person secretly adds one ingredient to the shared pot. Players then take turns closing their eyes, fishing out and tasting an ingredient, and guessing whose it was. It is played for laughs among friends, and the stakes are small.
### STANDARD +
In canon it is introduced by Thoma at the Kamisato Estate in Inazuma and later improvised by Yun Jin in Liyue. The fun comes from how strange the ingredients get: edible is the only real requirement.

## practice
### LITE
Rules as Thoma gives them: everyone prepares an ingredient and the ingredients all go into the pot when the meal begins. Players take turns to close their eyes, pick something out, taste it and name its owner. A correct guess earns a point and another turn. The player with the most points when the pot is finished wins.
### STANDARD +
Tradition, according to Thoma, says the winner receives the blessing of the God of Hot Pot. Almost anything edible counts as an ingredient, so sensible choices (fish fillets, shrimp, bamboo shoots, crab roe, tofu) sit beside deliberately nasty ones. The game rewards knowing your friends' tastes and humour as much as palate.
### LARGE +
Ingredients named across the canon scenes include:
- sensible: fresh fish fillets, shrimp, bamboo shoots, tofu, crab roe;
- harsh: "raging demon-level" Jueyun Chili, very salty ham, salt-crusted herring, unripe Lavender Melon, flash-boiled dango, Mist Flower corollas;
- sweet or unexpected: little cakes, cream, sugar, jam, mint, pepper, plain salt, Slime Condensate.

The danger is that the person who guesses well is the one who has to eat the worst items, since you keep your turn only by tasting.
### ULTRA +
In the Inazuma scene the choice of ingredients changes the mood: a "safe" pot leaves only Thoma having added anything odd, while a deliberately outrageous pot leaves him eating his own salt-crusted herring and unripe melon and wearily declaring himself the winner. Players are not obliged to keep a turn running: a wrong guess ends it, which Paimon points out Thoma could have used to spare himself.

## regional_variation
### STANDARD
No regional versions are established. The game appears in Inazuma (Thoma's scene, 2.0) and Liyue (Yun Jin's, 2.4), but canon does not say it belongs to either nation. Yun Jin's version is a two-person variant played with drinks instead of soup: she adds extra ingredients to leftover drinks, the Traveler guesses them, and no winner is declared.

## scene_use
### STANDARD
Use it as a low-stakes social set piece: a rest between crises, a bonding scene, or a way for characters to reveal what they would put in the pot. Keep the tone playful. Characters can tease each other over ingredient choices, bluff, or take the game too seriously.
### LARGE +
Good beats: a player bluffing a bland face while swallowing something awful (Yun Jin's "facial expression control"); a character winning at their own cost; someone bringing an ingredient that seemed sensible and was not (Ayaka's boiled cakes). Do not invent a fixed ritual, a named god, or a forfeit system; canon gives only the rules above.
### ULTRA +
The game sits between two real moments: Thoma proposes it as a celebration after the Kamisato side's rescue of Masakatsu in Chapter II, and Yun Jin uses it to get through too-bland drinks from a stall while relaxing after a performance. Both frame it as a way to unwind.

## examples
### LARGE
Thoma's table: Thoma, Ayaka, Paimon and the Traveler gather around the hot pot, each with a secret ingredient. Yun Jin's cup: she doctors leftover drinks with ingredients from a neighbouring shop and the Traveler guesses combinations such as crab roe, tofu and salt, or cream, pepper and sugar; the right answer depends on which drink is served, and a wrong guess draws a gentle "close, but not quite".

## entry: Thoma's Hot Pot Game
@keys: hot pot game, God of Hot Pot
@keys2: Thoma, Kamisato Estate, Ayaka, Paimon
@version: 2.0
### STANDARD
After the Kamisato side frees Master Masakatsu in Chapter II, Act I (A Flower Blooms in a Prison), Thoma suggests a celebratory hot pot evening and a game he recently learned. He prepared his own ingredients in advance. Ayaka, Paimon and the Traveler join; whoever wins the most points earns the God of Hot Pot's blessing. Thoma wins in either version of the scene, having eaten the odd items he added himself, and cheerfully plans a rematch.

## entry: Yun Jin's Drink Version
@keys: hot pot game, Half a Day's Leisure
@keys2: Yun Jin, Chen the Sharp, Chihu Rock
@version: 2.4
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
In Yun Jin's hangout Half a Day's Leisure, after barbecue at Chen the Sharp's stand, the leftover milk tea, milk and tea are judged too bland. Yun Jin turns them into a hot pot game: she adds extra ingredients by her own tastes, drinks half herself so she will not add anything harmful, and challenges the Traveler to name them. She is delighted by a correct guess ("a well-developed palate") and proposes trying traditional teas such as Pine Nectar or Snow Blossom next time. The Traveler questions whether this is how the game is played; she says the exact details do not matter.
