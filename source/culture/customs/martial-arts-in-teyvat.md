## summary
### LITE
Teyvat's martial arts are named fighting schools, each tied to a place, a clan or a founder, and each usually built around particular weapons. Mondstadt's Knights of Favonius teach Favonius Bladework with sword and claymore. Liyue favours swords and polearms, with the Guhua Clan's arts, the yaksha's demon-slaying dance, the Wangsheng spear arts and Yunlai Swordsmanship. Inazuma has the most formally named schools: Iwakura, Kitain, Kamisato, Kaedehara, Meikyou Shisui, Kujirai, Shinryuu and the Raiden Shogun's Origin. Natlan is known for boxing, and Khaenri'ah's Black Serpent Knights had their own bladework.
### STANDARD +
Canon does not offer one unified system. What exists is a patchwork of schools that characters name in profiles, weapon texts and quests, with founders, lineages and ranks that differ from place to place. A school is a way of fighting that anyone can train in, which is separate from a Vision. Several schools are tied to clan identity, so asking someone about their style is also asking about their family.

## practice
### LITE
Schools are built around a weapon or technique, handed from founder to students, and often kept alive by one clan or dojo. Masters, rivals and duels are normal parts of the world, especially in Inazuma.
### STANDARD +
Fighting schools are passed down within knightly orders, clans, funeral parlors and dojos. A style usually names its weapon (sword, claymore, polearm, bow) and sometimes its philosophy, such as Meikyou Shisui's stillness of mind. Some schools are famous for a legendary founder and a signature technique; others survive only as a name. Rank systems exist: the Iwakura Art has several proficiency levels.
### LARGE +
Mondstadt. Favonius Bladework was created for the Knights of Favonius about five hundred years ago. Two forms were developed: the single-handed sword form of Rostam, the Wolf Pup, which still exists and is described as light, swift and precise in defence of Mondstadt, and the dual form of Grand Master Arundolyn, the Lion of Light, who fought with a sword and a claymore together. Arundolyn's style demanded so much strength that it died out; item text says he was not a Vision bearer. Knights and former knights known for their own variants include Jean, Albedo, Eula, Dahlia, Noelle and Varka. The Kaetzlein Style of the Kaetzlein bloodline appears to be a hunting art, perhaps used by the hunters of Springvale, whose head hunter is Draff; Diona uses it.

Liyue. Swords and polearms are the usual weapons. Morax is lauded as the Warrior God, since the Chinese word behind the title covers both warrior and martial arts; whether other nations know him as such is not stated. The Guhua Clan's three arts are Raincutter for swords, Light Piercer for polearms and Life Ender for those who master both; the Guhua Brotherhood was set up by an associate of Guhua, who himself used the claymore Rainslasher, and Xingqiu, Chen the Sharp, Little Que'er and Sun Yu are named practitioners. The Nuo Dance of Evil Conquering is the yaksha's technique for slaying demons, and Xiao uses it. The Wangsheng Spear Arts are the polearm and purifying-flame techniques of the Hu family of the Wangsheng Funeral Parlor, which has run for 77 generations; the first wielder of the Staff of Homa is strongly implied to be from that family, and Hu Tao is a practitioner. Yunlai Swordsmanship, still taught, is said by Keqing to descend from the rod-and-line technique of the Yunlai Angler, one of the Eight Adepts; Qiqi uses the more ancient variant, the Ancient Sword Art.

Inazuma. Characters speak of four famous martial arts schools and four famous historical martial artists. The schools include the Iwakura Art, Kitain Arts and Arataki Art; the historical masters include Takamine the Mistsplitter. Other named schools: Kamisato Art, Kaedehara Sword Art, Kono Ittou Art, Kujirai Art, Meikyou Shisui, Yoohoo Arts (ninjutsu), Origin, Shinryuu, Souran Isshin Art and Touzannou's sword arts.

Natlan and Khaenri'ah. Boxing is a known art in Natlan, trained by shadow boxing, sparring against imagined opponents. Black Serpent Bladework is a sword and claymore style named for the Royal Guard of Khaenri'ah.
Inazuman schools in more detail. The Iwakura Art was founded by Iwakura Michihiro after his victory against Teruyo of the Yougou Tengu, about five hundred years ago, and is remembered in an Inazuman folk song as "Iwakura the Successor". The Iwakura Clan specialises in swordsmanship, and many members use the title Iwakura Art to show affiliation with the clan dojo; it is unclear whether clan and art are distinct. Kitain Arts is a polearm style developed by Kitain Bunsou, and the Kitain Cross Spear is tied to him. Hirotatsu is a master-of-arms of the Kitain Art, so the school may include sword techniques. Takamine the Mistsplitter trained with the Yougou Tengu and mastered archery and swordsmanship; he passed archery to Asase Hibiki, but his sword style died with him in the Cataclysm.

The Kamisato Art is the style of the Kamisato Clan, with named techniques by Ayaka (Kabuki, Hyouka, Soumetsu, Senho) and Ayato. The Kaedehara Sword Art is Kazuha's informal name for a style unique to his family of bladesmiths, originally techniques for rigorous blade-testing; Kazuha notes that its forms differ greatly from those of typical samurai. Meikyou Shisui, whose present master is Domon, teaches stillness of mind and freedom from agitation as its main tenets. The Souran Isshin Art was developed by the samurai Yanagibashi Takuto while duelling the youkai Kamai Kenji and passed to Tominaga Masanari. The Kujirai Art was created by Kid Kujirai with help from Shousen, using explosive temari balls; Alice calls herself its originator in the form of "Temari Jutsu". The Yoohoo Arts are a ninjutsu style of escape, clone and concealment techniques whose last successor is Sayu; she says the name comes from taunting the enemy, and the techniques have little use in real combat but a performance-like flair.

Origin is the school founded by Ei, the Raiden Shogun. In her voice-over she says Inazuma's naginata and swordsmanship, and its swordsmithing, trace back to her. Its signature technique is Musou no Hitotachi, used in divine punishments; Kazuha's friend died after it was his last wish to witness it. Touzannou, called Akuou, a mortal lieutenant of Orobashi, developed Getsumouun and Yuushio, which remain popular with the warriors of Watatsumi Island, where swordsmanship was not otherwise a strong tradition.

Khaenri'ah. Black Serpent Bladework is the sword and claymore style of the Black Serpent Knights. Their Truthseeker art uses transforming weapons, and its principles are said to apply to other weapon types. Named practitioners are Haden, Surtalogi, Dainsleif, Skirk and Halfdan.
### ULTRA +
Details and cross-checks worth keeping straight. Rostam received the title Wolf Pup at twenty-seven, and since him no practitioner is said to have matched his ability. Arundolyn and Rostam were close companions growing up: Arundolyn from a long line of knights, Rostam from a peasant family. Arundolyn never displayed his strength again after Rostam's death. These details come from weapon texts, not from a manual of techniques, so do not invent specific moves.

Shinryuu and the Origin school sit at opposite ends of the Inazuman scene. Shinryuu is a school of fighting imagined opponents, whereas the Iwakura, Kitain and Favonius schools use real weapons against real opponents (Shousen draws exactly that contrast). Shousen studied martial arts abroad and drew on Natlan's shadow boxing; the school is dangerous because imagined blades and musket shots can wound the practitioner, and many students left or were expelled. The Kujirai Art is implied to have been created using Shinryuu.

The Kono Ittou Art is known only from the Warrior's Spirit event quest Unexpected Battle (see culture.warriors-spirit-swordfighting-exhibition): two students, Tadaaki and Tadatsune, planned to duel over an entry ticket, were beaten by the Traveler, handed over the ticket and left to train further, remembering their master's saying that there is always someone stronger.

Mystic arts are a related but separate subject (see system.mystic-arts). The schools here are about weapon and body technique.

## scene_use
### STANDARD
Use schools as colour and as a way to read a fighter. Naming a style gives a character a lineage and a rival, and an Inazuman duelist may well ask a stranger which school they belong to. Keep techniques vague: canon names techniques and philosophies but does not describe stances, so avoid inventing detailed moves. Do not assume a character belongs to a school just because of their weapon; check their own entry first.
### LARGE +
Reasonable scene hooks: a Knights of Favonius drill in single-handed Favonius Bladework; a funeral-parlor member using a polearm; a swordsmith testing a blade in the way Kazuha describes; a Shinryuu student seeking a sparring partner; a Kamisato retainer mentioning the clan's art with pride. Respect that schools have pride and ranks, and that some, like Takamine's swordsmanship and Arundolyn's dual style, are lost and mourned.
### ULTRA +
Common errors to avoid: treating Origin as a general Inazuman school anyone can learn; calling Favonius Bladework a Mondstadt-wide style rather than a Knights of Favonius one; assuming Mistsplitter's sword style survives; giving Natlan boxing lore beyond shadow boxing and the Shinryuu link; and crediting schools to Sumeru, Fontaine, Snezhnaya or Nod-Krai that canon does not name.

## entry: Favonius Bladework
@keys: Favonius Bladework, Rostam's bladework, Arundolyn's dual style
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
Favonius Bladework is the sword and claymore fighting style made for the Knights of Favonius. About five hundred years ago two forms were created: Rostam's single-handed form, which survives and is the basis of the tradition (light, swift, precise, used to defend Mondstadt), and Arundolyn's dual-wield form of sword and claymore, which died out because it required extraordinary strength. Jean, Albedo, Eula, Dahlia, Noelle and Varka each have their own named variant; see their entries.

## entry: Guhua Arts
@keys: Guhua Arts, Raincutter, Light Piercer, Life Ender, Guhua Brotherhood
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
The Guhua Clan's three arts are Raincutter (sword), Light Piercer (polearm) and Life Ender (mastery of both). The Guhua Brotherhood, the clan's original form, was founded by an associate of Guhua, who used the claymore Rainslasher. Xingqiu is the best-known practitioner; Chen the Sharp, Little Que'er and Sun Yu are also named.

## entry: Iwakura Art
@keys: Iwakura Art, Iwakura Clan, Tengu Sweeper
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
The Iwakura Art is an Inazuman sword school founded by Iwakura Michihiro about five hundred years ago, after his victory over Teruyo of the Yougou Tengu. Clan members are remembered in a folk song as "Iwakura the Successor". Several proficiency levels exist, and the Tengu Sweeper is its notable technique. Eiin and Iwakura Mitsunari are named members.

## entry: Kitain Arts
@keys: Kitain Arts, Kitain Art, Kitain School, Kitain Cross Spear
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
Kitain Arts is a polearm school from Inazuma, developed by Kitain Bunsou, who is credited with the cross spear. Nobumori and Hirotatsu are named practitioners; Hirotatsu is a master-of-arms, so the school may also teach swordwork.

## entry: Origin school
@keys: Origin school, Musou no Hitotachi
@keys2: Raiden Shogun, Ei, Inazuma
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
Origin is the martial school founded by Ei, the Raiden Shogun. Ei says Inazuman naginata and sword arts, and its swordsmithing, originate with her. Its signature sword art, Musou no Hitotachi, is performed when she delivers divine punishment. The Guardian of Eternity is also listed as a practitioner.

## entry: Shinryuu
@keys: Shinryuu, Phantom Flow, shadow boxing
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
Shinryuu is an Inazuman school invented by Shousen, built on fighting imagined opponents. It was inspired by the shadow boxing he saw in Natlan. It is dangerous: imagined blades and musket shots can wound the trainee, and many students left or were expelled for recklessness. Eiin has learned it, and the Traveler took part in Shousen's tournament during the Phantom Flow event. Kid Kujirai's Kujirai Art is implied to be derived from it.

## entry: Black Serpent Bladework
@keys: Black Serpent Bladework, Truthseeker
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
Black Serpent Bladework is the sword and claymore style of Khaenri'ah, named after the Royal Guard. It may include or coincide with the Truthseeker art of the Black Serpent Knights, which uses transforming weapons and whose principles are said to apply to other weapons. Named practitioners are Haden, Surtalogi, Dainsleif, Skirk and Halfdan.
