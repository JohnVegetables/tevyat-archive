## summary
### LITE
Inazuman food is light and built on fresh ingredients. Rice is the staple grain, eaten steamed or pounded into rice cakes that can be sweet or savoury. As on any island nation, fish, shrimp, crab, eel and seaweed fill many traditional dishes. Noodles (udon, soba, ramen) are loved, and sakura, a flavour tied to Inazuma, appears in both sweets and savouries. The best-known eating place is Kiminami Restaurant in Inazuma City.
### STANDARD +
Red meat and poultry mostly appear in imported dishes rather than traditional ones. Food is also a lively part of street and festival life: stalls sell snacks such as dango, and the Kamisato Clan has long ordered meals from the same city restaurant. Poor soil on some islands limits what can be grown locally.
### LARGE +
The Vision Hunt and Sakoku decrees strained supplies and trade for everyone in Inazuma, and the end of the sakoku policy reopened contact with foreign ingredients and dishes. Since then foreign dishes such as pizza have been adapted by local cooks.
### ULTRA +
Culinary talk in Inazuma links back to specific characters, shops and events, covered in the entries below. Regional variation (Narukami Island, Watatsumi Island, Ritou) is only lightly documented in canon: most named dishes and shops are on Narukami Island.

## practice
### LITE
Rice is central. Tricolor Dango is a soft steamed rice-flour sweet; Rice Cake Soup is a savoury city dish with a light broth. Noodles include udon (light-flavoured, springy), soba (cheap buckwheat noodles, very popular) and ramen (dense-flavoured). Sashimi and other seafood plates are prized for freshness. Sakura Mochi and Sakura Tempura use sakura blossom flavour.
### STANDARD +
Item text describes soba as made from inexpensive ingredients with a simple method, which is why ordinary Inazumans like it. Udon is contrasted with ramen: where ramen prizes dense flavours, udon uses light ones. Tonkotsu Ramen is described as slow-prepared meat over springy noodles in a bone-based soup that is fragrant but not greasy. Rice Cake Soup is said to have started with few ingredients and grown more elaborate over time, and its text calls it a warming dish for a snowy night. Sashimi Platter is presented as an art of knife work and freshness. Sakura Mochi is a delicate sakura-scented sweet with a leaf wrapper. In local folklore, bake-danuki pretend to offer food and shelter while really handing over leaves and mud.
### LARGE +
Tricolor Dango is coloured with Sakura Bloom and Snapdragon, and its text links it to watching blossoms and hoping for a good year. Dango Milk, a street-stall creation sold by Tomoki in Inazuma City, is dango added to milk; its text says customers who tried it love it but warns that too much may spoil the appetite. Sakura Blooms are a local specialty gathered from petals around the Grand Narukami Shrine, and travelling cooks consider them excellent for cooking. Crops are hard to grow on Watatsumi Island and in Konda Village.
### ULTRA +
The culture page names katsu sandwiches and imported poultry as foreign dishes found in Inazuma. Chongyun's voice-over praises the "ice-cold freshness" of an authentic Inazuman meal (with imported ingredients) for taking away the exhaustion of hard training. Yoimiya's voice-over has her going from stall to stall at festivals, eating as she goes. Gorou says he loves cake and Tricolor Dango because sugar is rare in field rations. Ayaka says she is fascinated by food from overseas; if she had to pick something from Inazuma it would be chazuke, but she says so only in confidence. Other favourites: Kirara likes Sashimi Platters, Thoma Rice Cake Soup, Kokomi Bird Egg Sushi.

## regional_variation
### STANDARD
Documented food culture is concentrated in Inazuma City on Narukami Island (Kiminami Restaurant in Hanamizaka, Shimura's, Tomoki's stall) and relies on seafood from the surrounding waters. Watatsumi Island's poor soil and Konda Village's farming difficulty appear as scarcity rather than as named regional dishes. No source read names a distinct Tsurumi Island cuisine; the island has no living inhabitants after its former civilisation was destroyed.
### LARGE +
Characters from elsewhere in the archipelago have their own taste notes: Kazuha, a seafaring wanderer, says fish is the easiest food to get at sea and cares about cooking it well; Kokomi of Watatsumi favours simple Bird Egg Sushi, which she calls well suited to army life; Thoma likes cooking Rice Cake Soup in a big pot to share.

## entry: Kiminami Restaurant
@keys: Kiminami Restaurant, Kiminami Anna
@version: 2.0
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
Kiminami Restaurant is a well-known restaurant in the Hanamizaka area of Inazuma City, run by its owner Kiminami Anna. It sells Inazuman classics and recipes such as Soba Noodles and, since Ayaka's story quest Food From Afar, a Mushroom Pizza from abroad; the in-game notice asks customers to thank a friend who helped bring it. Anna says the Kamisato siblings have long sent staff to order her food, and that Ayaka had never before booked a dinner with friends. The owner's home adjoins the restaurant.
### LARGE +
In the follow-up quest Pizza From Another Land, Anna asks the Traveler for help because customers are bored of one pizza and want other flavours, and her own attempts have not satisfied them. The pizza version the Traveler did not make in Food From Afar (Mondstadt-style or one using Inazuman ingredients) is then developed and added to her menu after feedback from a customer. It is a clear example of foreign cooking being adopted into Inazuman food culture.

## entry: Shimura's
@keys: Shimura's, Shimura Kanbei
@version: 2.0
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
Shimura's is a popular eatery in Inazuma City's shopping district, run by Shimura Kanbei. The map description calls him a poor but self-made samurai who finally gained a small fief of his own there. The shop sells simple food, ingredients and recipes such as udon, ramen, miso soup, egg roll and sushi.
### LARGE +
The shop description invites passers-by to take food to go if they lack time for tea. Its advertisement board is a running joke that the drinks are good.

## entry: Tricolor Dango and Dango Milk
@keys: Tricolor Dango, Dango Milk, Tomoki
@version: 2.0
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
Tricolor Dango is a steamed ball of glutinous rice flour coloured with Sakura Bloom and Snapdragon; its recipe is sold by Kiminami Anna. Gorou lists it as a favourite. Dango Milk, sold by the street vendor Tomoki, who advertises creative cooking, combines dango with milk.
### LARGE +
Yoimiya says she prefers snacks to full meals and offers homemade dango to friends. The Dango Milk text says customers who tried it all love it but cautions against drinking too much.

## entry: Sakura Bloom and sakura dishes
@keys: Sakura Bloom, Sakura Mochi, Sakura Tempura
@version: 2.0
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
Sakura Blooms are petals fallen from the Sacred Sakura at the Grand Narukami Shrine, floating in the air under the effect of Electro energy. Cooks use them in sweets such as Sakura Mochi and in savouries such as Sakura Tempura, whose recipe comes from the Gourmet Supremos quest line.
### LARGE +
The Gourmet Supremos begin their Inazuma storyline trying to get past a barrier to collect Sakura Blooms, which Xudong has heard have the best mouthfeel for cooking. The culture page calls sakura a flavour unique to Inazuma.

## entry: Noodles and Rice Cake Soup
@keys: Udon Noodles, Soba Noodles, Tonkotsu Ramen, Rice Cake Soup
@version: 2.0
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
Udon, soba and ramen are staples of Inazuma City's eateries. Soba is cheap and popular, udon is light and soothing, ramen is rich. Rice Cake Soup is a separate savoury rice-cake dish and is Thoma's favourite food.
### LARGE +
Udon's text says it uses light flavours to heal the Inazuman soul. Tonkotsu Ramen's text praises the springy noodles and a soup that goes down gently.

## entry: The Gourmet Supremos
@keys: Gourmet Supremos
@version: 2.0
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
Xudong (a cook from Liyue), Parvaneh (a scholar from the Sumeru Akademiya) and Julie (an adventurer from Mondstadt) are travelling food-lovers who call themselves the Gourmet Supremos and arrive in Inazuma to improve their cooking with local ingredients. Their quest series gives the Traveler recipes such as Sakura Tempura and Sashimi Platter, and later brings in Kamei Munehisa, a former samurai of the Shogun's Army, as their bodyguard.
### LARGE +
Their Inazuma line is unlocked after Ritou Escape Plan. When Parvaneh suggests putting up a commission as Xudong would have in Liyue, Xudong replies that they are strangers in Inazuma; the Traveler happens along instead.
