## summary
### LITE
Mondstadt cooking is simple, hearty country food shaped by the nation's two great loves, wine and song. Apples, potatoes, cheese, butter, milk and cream turn up everywhere. Signature dishes include Mondstadt Hash Browns eaten with jam, peppered Mondstadt Grilled Fish, the festival meat pie called Moon Pie, honey-roasted Sweet Madame and the steak-and-cheese stack "Pile 'Em Up". The famous drink is dandelion wine, followed by apple cider, alcoholic or not. Drinking is a big part of social life, at taverns such as Angel's Share and the Cat's Tail, and the popular restaurant is Good Hunter.
### STANDARD +
Because wine is central to Mondstadt's economy and identity, alcohol is a defining part of the cuisine rather than a side note: ingredients used in brewing, above all apples, are common in food too.

## practice
### STANDARD
Mondstadt's gentle climate, credited to Barbatos's blessing, lets fruit and vegetables flourish: sunsettias and apples are abundant, grapes grow for wine, and carrots and radishes are grown in small gardens at Springvale and the Dawn Winery. Philanemo Mushrooms are not farmed but grow in residential areas and have entered local diets. Milk, butter, cheese and cream are common in recipes, so a dairy industry is implied.

Signature dishes, as item text describes them: the Mondstadt Hash Brown, a fried cake of mashed potato with a little pinecone for crunch, eaten with jam and "loved by people of all ages"; Mondstadt Grilled Fish, fish grilled over moderate fire and sprinkled with pepper; Moon Pie, a "traditional staple", a small meat pie of pickled meat under baked meringue and a fixture of festivals; Sweet Madame, fowl roasted with honey and sweet flowers; and "Pile 'Em Up", originally a dish of steaks, potatoes and cheese. Barbatos Ratatouille is a plain vegetable chowder with a long history, and Sticky Honey Roast is meat in a sweet honey sauce.

Dandelion wine is the nation's most famous drink and the Dawn Winery's signature product. Apple cider, alcoholic and non-alcoholic, comes next; hard ciders of apple and cherry, beer by the tankard and jagerschnapps are also mentioned in dialogue.
### LARGE +
Heavy drinking is a social fact, with consequences. Mondstadt has a large drinking culture and relatively many alcoholics. Nimrod talks of the "Four Drunkards" while struggling with the habit himself, and the bartender Charles has to contact Nimrod's wife when he gets out of hand. Diona dislikes alcohol because her father Draff changes when drunk. The Church of Favonius now sends deaconesses to pray over newly built wardrobes after children complained to Grace about drunk fathers stumbling out of them.

Dandelion wine has fans and imitators. Merchants from other nations have tried to learn the Dawn Winery's methods, honestly and otherwise. Connor, the winery's wine maker, describes it as sweetish and refreshing with a rich cereal aroma. Eula's voice-over says aged dandelion wine should technically be poured into a silver goblet, left to breathe for 12 minutes and given ice cubes so that 60% of the ice is submerged, a ritual she refuses to bother with. The community wiki treats Thousand-Wind Wine as the first dandelion wine. Dandelion wine is Venti's favourite wine; the wiki guesses he is the friend who brought bottles when visiting Zhongli, which canon does not state.

Dishes are tied to people: Amber's favourite is the Sticky Honey Roast from Good Hunter, Bennett likes Sweet Madame (which Fischl likes least), and Jean's favourite is the pizza at the Cat's Tail.
### ULTRA +
Dishes carry local lore. The White Pigeon's archive description says many Mondstadters believe an authentic Sweet Madame uses pigeon meat, which is not true. Outsiders can garble the name: in the quest "Old Tastes Die Hard" Mr. Zhu calls it "Sweetened Mistress". Pile 'Em Up is now closely tied to Ludi Harpastum, where a game of the same name has players stack as high as they can. The Sticky Honey Roast recipe is a reward in the Dawn Winery version of the Food Delivery quest. The real-world Teyvat Food Notes publishes recipes for several of these dishes, using absinthe where dandelion wine is called for; that is an out-of-universe aid, not lore.

## regional_variation
### STANDARD
Within Mondstadt: the capital leans on taverns and Good Hunter; Springvale supplies game meat to Good Hunter; the Dawn Winery supplies wine and cider from its own orchards and vineyard and sells part of each year's output locally and the rest along the trade route south of the city.
### LARGE +
The wiki's Mondstadt dish list also includes "Millhaven's Morning Plate", "Northern Apple Stew", "Northern Smoked Chicken" and "Sunshine Sprat"; canon gives little beyond their in-game descriptions, so do not invent regional traditions for them.

## scene_use
### STANDARD
Scene use (this project's suggestion): a Mondstadt meal is warm, unfussy and sociable. Put jam beside the hash brown, fruit on the table and a bottle of dandelion wine or a pitcher of cider within reach. Taverns are noisy with bards and regulars; Good Hunter is busy and struggles to keep up with take-out orders. A guest who declines alcohol can be offered apple cider, which has a reputation as a sobering last drink of the night.
### LARGE +
Keep the winery's pride and Diluc's own habits straight: he holds the wine trade but is not fond of wine himself, and asked the winery to develop non-alcoholic drinks so people who cannot drink can still join in. Do not have ordinary Mondstadters know that the bard drinking dandelion wine in the corner is Barbatos.

## entry: Dandelion Wine
@keys: Dandelion Wine, Thousand-Wind Wine
@spoiler: none
### STANDARD
The signature product of the Dawn Winery and Mondstadt's best-known specialty, said to be sweetish, refreshing and rich in cereal aroma. It is Venti's favourite. Thousand-Wind Wine is held to have been the first. Fans compare its strength to absinthe, but canon gives no figure.
### LARGE +
Foreign merchants have tried to copy it; Dawn Orchard furnishing text and the quest "Wine, with a Taste of Freedom" touch on attempts to learn the winery's methods. Eula's voice-over describes how aged dandelion wine should technically be served (silver goblet, 12 minutes to breathe, ice 60% submerged) and says she will not bother.

## entry: Angel's Share
@keys: Angel's Share
@keys2: tavern, Charles, Diluc, Dawn Winery
@spoiler: none
### STANDARD
A tavern in Mondstadt City established and funded by the Dawn Winery; the bartender is Charles. He says the name refers to the portion of liquor that barrels absorb and distillation evaporates, which brewers call "the share of angels". It once held a monopoly over Mondstadt's wine trade. It sells drinks such as Apple Cider and does not accept tabs (Diluc's story quest).
### LARGE +
Its occasional bartending academy was restricted to non-alcoholic drinks after an attendee mixed a drink of three hard liquors (limited-time event "Of Drink A-Dreaming").

## entry: The Cat's Tail
@keys: Cat's Tail, The Cat's Tail
@keys2: Diona, Margaret, tavern, bartender
@spoiler: none
### STANDARD
A tavern owned by Margaret, famed for its cats and for Diona, its bartender, whose drinks are said to taste delicious whatever the ingredients. Her arrival ended the monopoly Angel's Share held; Elzer tried to headhunt her and got bitten. It also serves pizza, Jean's favourite when she has time to order one, and has become a gathering place for card players.

## entry: Good Hunter
@keys: Good Hunter
@keys2: Sara, restaurant, Mondstadt
@spoiler: none
### STANDARD
The most popular restaurant in Mondstadt City apart from the tavern, with the waitress Sara. It stresses local ingredients: meat from Springvale hunters and homegrown Mondstadt apples. Sara says it is known for venison steak and satisfying salad, and its daily recommendation board advertises a cold cut platter. Take-out is so popular that the service is understaffed (quest "Food Delivery").

## entry: Apple Cider
@keys: Apple Cider
@keys2: Dawn Winery, Diluc, non-alcoholic, Mondstadt
@spoiler: none
### STANDARD
A freshly squeezed, fashionable, fruity non-alcoholic drink (per the item text), reputed to sober people up and often ordered as the last of the night. In Diluc's character story the Dawn Winery developed several non-alcoholic drinks at his request, which pleased those who could not drink but wished to join in; the cider's monthly sales rival dandelion wine. Hard ciders also exist.
### LARGE +
The story adds that some think Diluc never drinks the public beverages because his standards are so high; the text leaves the reason open.
