## summary
### LITE
Snezhnayan cooking is hearty, warming food built for a land of eternal winter, and it clearly echoes Slavic cuisines. Staples are rye bread, smetana (sour cream), beetroot borscht, cold soups, grilled skewers, thinly shaved frozen fish and cured sausage. The drink everyone mentions is Fire-Water, a famously potent spirit; downing a whole glass in one go is the respectful norm. Dairy, preserved meat and seafood from coastal towns matter more than fresh produce.
### STANDARD +
The cuisine became detailed in game with Version 7.0, when Snezhnograd gained the Podvorye Restaurant and a set of regional dishes with item text describing how they are made and eaten. Before that it was known mostly through Tartaglia's remarks and the Fire-Water anecdotes told in Mondstadt.

## practice
### STANDARD
Climate shapes the table. Wheat is hard to grow in the cold, so rye serves in its place (rye bread, rye flour), and some milk is turned into smetana rather than ordinary cream. Seaside regions eat mostly seafood, according to Tartaglia's character story as summarised by the wiki; whether the rest of the country does is not stated. Dishes named in canon:
- Borscht: a red beetroot soup, slow-simmered in stages, finished with a spoonful of sour cream; the item text says it keeps the bitter cold at bay. Tartaglia calls it authentic and says he can make it from specialty ingredients of his hometown.
- Snowrealm Okroshkas: a cold soup of cooked ingredients under a sour-cream broth. Some Snezhnayans down a large bowl in one go on the coldest days to prove they can defeat the cold.
- Stroganina: raw frozen fish shaved into thin curling strips with a sharp blade and served with coarse salt, a common cold platter.
- Snezhnaya Shashliks: marinated meat grilled on iron skewers over coals, eaten at courtyard gatherings, on hunts, or on a patch of cleared snow wherever a fire can be lit.
- Glupov Rye Bread: a dense, long-fermented loaf that keeps well, a common ration for travelling merchants, soldiers and hunters.
- Medovik: a layered honey cake with sour-cream frosting.
- Podvorye Smoked Sausage: sliced smoked sausage, a signature dish of the Podvorye Restaurant that nobody at the table turns down.
- Kvass: a mildly fizzy, alcohol-free drink with a sour rye-bread aroma, sold at the Kvasnik beverage shop and described as a drink no Snezhnayan would refuse.
### LARGE +
Fire-Water is the national spirit. Tartaglia says most people love it, and that anyone who will stand out in the cold with you drinking it and talking has earned your complete trust (he adds that such a person is the kind you want on the battlefield). Draining the entire glass in one go is customary and counts as showing respect (open-world NPC and Ororon's story). A Dawn Winery anecdote told by the Mondstadt NPC Vile shows the cost to outsiders: during a banquet for a Snezhnayan diplomatic and merchant party, Diluc followed the custom, emptied his glass of Fire-Water, then slept for three days and refused the proposed partnership, saying Mondstadt's people could not handle it. In Snezhnograd a hidden exploration objective has the Traveler waking a drunk named Vanya, who is repeatedly found passed out by the restaurant district after too much Fire-Water.
### ULTRA +
The Podvorye Restaurant in Snezhnograd, in front of Snezhnograd Square, is a long-established Snezhnayan restaurant that offers refined versions of traditional local dishes and fine Fire-Water. Its owner and entire staff are Volkodlak and fae, which the map description counts as one of its attractions. Its server Buturlina sells Borscht, Medovik and the Smoked Sausage recipe, plus flour, cream and other stock; the shop restocks daily and weekly. Restaurant Zorya's Tolstaya sells Glupov Rye Bread and the Shashliks recipe, among other items. The Volkodlak Tundra and Everfrozen Earth hold the okroshka, rye bread and stroganina recipes in chests, so these dishes are tied to ordinary hunting country, not just restaurants.

## regional_variation
### STANDARD
Seaside Snezhnaya leans on seafood. Nod-Krai, nominally Snezhnayan but warmer and more independent, has its own cooking: the Meat-Filled Rye Bun, a fish-and-bacon filling baked inside thick rye dough and sold in Nasha Town, is classed as a Nod-Krai dish and the in-game rye ingredient lists Nod-Krai as a source. See the Nod-Krai cuisine entry for that region. Fae and Volkodlak communities keep their own tables, as the Podvorye shows.
### LARGE +
Camaraderie at the table is part of Snezhnayan culture generally: the Culture page notes closeness to family and a habit of building trust through shared drinking in the cold. Outsiders in Mondstadt and Liyue know the cuisine mostly through Fire-Water and Fatui diplomats.

## scene_use
### STANDARD
Use Snezhnayan meals to convey warmth against cold: a bowl of borscht with a spoonful of smetana, a chunk of heavy rye bread, skewers sizzling over coals in a snowy yard, or a shot of Fire-Water drained in one swallow to show respect. A host who offers Fire-Water expects the guest to finish the glass. Do not invent specific recipes, prices or feast traditions beyond the item texts.
### LARGE +
Contrast settings: a Snezhnograd restaurant with fae and Volkodlak staff and polite service; a soldier's or hunter's field meal of rye bread and skewers; a coastal fishing town's seafood table; a Nod-Krai bakery stall with fish-stuffed rye. A Mondstadter or Liyuean guest is likely to find Fire-Water overpowering. Treat the alcohol-free Kvass as a reasonable choice for anyone who declines.

## examples
### LARGE
Borscht (red beet soup with sour cream), Snowrealm Okroshkas (cold sour-cream soup), Stroganina (shaved frozen fish with salt), Snezhnaya Shashliks (grilled skewers), Glupov Rye Bread (long-fermented rye loaf), Medovik (layered honey cake), Podvorye Smoked Sausage, Kvass (non-alcoholic rye drink) and Fire-Water (potent spirit).

## entry: Fire-Water
@keys: Fire-Water, Fire Water
@version: 1.6
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
Fire-Water is Snezhnaya's best-known alcoholic drink, described as an extremely strong blend. Tartaglia holds that drinking it and talking with someone out in the cold builds complete trust. Emptying a full glass at once is the polite custom. Diluc did so at a Dawn Winery banquet for Snezhnayan merchants and passed out for three days (Vile's anecdote). The recipe, proof and ingredients are not given in canon.

## entry: Podvorye Restaurant
@keys: Podvorye Restaurant, Podvorye, Buturlina, Restaurant Zorya
@version: 7.0
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
A long-established restaurant in Snezhnograd, in front of Snezhnograd Square, offering refined takes on traditional Snezhnayan food and fine Fire-Water. Its owner and whole staff are Volkodlak and fae. The word podvorye means courtyard in Russian. Buturlina serves as its shopkeeper; Tolstaya sells similar fare at the separate Restaurant Zorya.

## entry: Special dishes of Vesna and Alyosha
@keys: Hoarfrost Soup, The Bullseye Roast
@keys2: Vesna, Alyosha
@version: 7.1
@availability: permanent
### LARGE
Gameplay-adjacent flavour. Cooking Snowrealm Okroshkas with Vesna may yield her special dish, Hoarfrost Soup: the item text has her declaring a "secret Vila snow flurry soup", inviting everyone to add what they like, and eating most of it herself after Paimon contributes the ingredients. Cooking Snezhnaya Shashliks with Alyosha may yield The Bullseye Roast, a massive gamey skewer he grills with the same confidence as his marksmanship while Tugarin barks excitedly. Both are portrayal hints about those characters, not general Snezhnayan practice.

## status
### STANDARD
As of 7.1 the cuisine is a stable, fully documented tradition in Snezhnograd with dishes, shops and recipes; no story event changes it. Fire-Water remains the national spirit.
