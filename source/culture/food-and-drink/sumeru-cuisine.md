## summary
### LITE
Sumerian cooking is fragrant, spicy and colourful. The rainforest and desert supply many edible flowers, mushrooms, fruits and nuts, and the local saying is that Spice, a seasoning made from dried Harra Fruit, goes well with anything. Flatbread wraps, bean soups, grilled fish and a long list of syrupy, nutty or floral sweets are everyday fare, and coffee is a social drink. Two Sumeru City landmarks define the scene: Lambad's Tavern, the main restaurant, and Puspa Cafe, a long-established coffee shop.
### STANDARD +
Compared with Mondstadt or Fontaine, Sumeru food leans on aroma and spice rather than delicacy. Sweets are especially prominent and are often tied to roses, Padisarah petals or Ajilenakh Nuts.
### LARGE +
Food in Sumeru also carries its two halves: forest ingredients (Sumeru Roses, Harra Fruit, mushrooms) and desert ones (Ajilenakh Nuts from oasis palms). The scholarly culture of the Akademiya shows in small ways, such as researchers ordering a heavy sweet before an exam.

## practice
### STANDARD
The culture page describes Sumeru cooking as built around fragrant, spicy dishes. Common seasonings are Spice and the Padisarah plant, whose flower buds are processed into valuable spices; sweeter dishes often use Sumeru Rose or Ajilenakh Nut. The cuisine includes meats, dairy products and grains, and two ingredients are described as fairly unique to the region: Mysterious Meat (dropped by Flying Serpents) and Glabrous Beans. Typical dishes from the shops and item texts:
- Savoury: Lambad Fish Roll (grilled fish with rose petals), Shawarma Wrap (meat shaved from a vertical skewer, wrapped in flatbread with vegetables and sauces; a simple Sumeru street food), Minty Bean Soup (mint and beans boiled, ground and thickened; good hot or cold).
- Sweet: Baklava (nutty puff pastry with butter and syrup), Tulumba (fried shaped dough dipped in syrup while hot), Rose Custard (sweetened fermented milk with dried roses and nuts), Padisarah Pudding (milk dessert flavoured with Padisarah and Sumeru Rose petals), Candied Ajilenakh Nut (syrup, filling and sesame paste set in a mould), and Charcoal-Baked Ajilenakh Cake (a dark, roasted-nut pastry).
### LARGE +
Item texts give small social details. Baklava is the Akademiya's favourite dessert, ordered by some researchers to restore energy before exams. Rose Custard started as fare for adventurers and travelling merchants and is now breakfast and tea-time food across Sumeru. Padisarah Pudding, cute and sweet, is common at celebratory events. Tulumba is called an inseparable companion to many Sumerians. Sumeru Roses have long been used for fragrant essences and perfumes; markets fill with their scent at festival time, and in Sumerian poetry the rose is compared with fine wine and is a classic literary symbol.
### ULTRA +
Spice comes from the Harra Fruit, the most valued spice plant in Sumeru. Its archive text jokes that foreign legends of Harra growing on cliffs guarded by flying snakes and giant birds are tall tales told by local merchants to raise spice prices. The Ajilenakh Nut grows on trees in desert oases and is a major food source for desert peoples; its extreme sweetness is attributed to the desert, it is eaten dried or in sweets, and it features in old songs. Spices and essential oils are among Sumeru's traded goods, and Eremite tribes in the Desert of Hadramaveth trade with merchants who carry goods to the city.

## regional_variation
### STANDARD
Forest and desert supply different staples. Roses, mushrooms, Harra Fruit and Padisarah are rainforest and city plants; the Ajilenakh Nut is an oasis fruit of the Great Red Sand. Sumeru City, where Lambad's Tavern and Puspa Cafe stand, is where the two meet through trade.
### LARGE +
Foreigners buy and sell at Port Ormos, the main port, so Sumerian ingredients reach other nations. The Ajilenakh Nut text notes that Snezhnaya has a similar candied sweet with a different flavour, and that both sides think theirs is the best in Teyvat; the Snezhnayan dish is not named.

## scene_use
### STANDARD
Use Sumeru meals for warm, talkative scenes: a bowl of bean soup and a grilled fish roll at the tavern, a shawarma wrap bought on the move, or coffee and syrupy sweets at Puspa Cafe. Do not invent prices, rituals or recipes beyond the item texts.
### LARGE +
Puspa Cafe is a social meeting place for people from all walks of life, and its acting manager Enteka reads fortunes from coffee grounds; the map text claims her readings are more accurate than those of professional diviners at the bazaar. Lambad's Tavern is near Treasures Street, the liveliest street in Sumeru City, and its owner is known for tall tales of sea voyages. The tavern's second-floor booth seating was redesigned and refitted by Kaveh (per his character story).

## examples
### LARGE
Sweets: Baklava, Tulumba, Rose Custard, Padisarah Pudding, Candied Ajilenakh Nut. Savoury: Lambad Fish Roll (called "the Romance of Sailors" in its text), Shawarma Wrap, Minty Bean Soup, Chicken-Mushroom Skewer. Staple ingredients: Spice, Sumeru Rose, Padisarah, Ajilenakh Nut, Glabrous Beans, Coffee Beans.

## entry: Lambad's Tavern
@keys: Lambad's Tavern, Lambad, Lambad Fish Roll
@version: 3.0
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
A tavern in Sumeru City near Treasures Street, run by Lambad and described as the main restaurant of Sumeru. Lambad claims to be a legendary sailor who travelled far and wide; the map text says it is hard to tell how exaggerated that is, but the food makes the cooking part of the claim believable. His Lambad Fish Roll is said to have been invented because grilled fish, which he had tasted in taverns everywhere he travelled, was not a signature food of Sumeru. A noticeboard outside advertises the fish roll as the day's specialty.
### LARGE +
The tavern sells ingredients such as flour, sausage, bacon, Spice, sugar, fish, crab, mushrooms and Glabrous Beans, and recipes including the fish roll, Minty Bean Soup and Shawarma Wrap. Its patrons include researchers, mercenaries and a boat captain.

## entry: Puspa Cafe
@keys: Puspa Cafe, Puspa Café, Enteka
@version: 3.0
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
A coffee shop in Sumeru City with a fairly long history. It serves authentic Sumerian-style coffee, and people also come to have their fortunes told from coffee grounds or to play the Genius Invokation TCG. Its acting manager, Enteka, sells Baklava, Candied Ajilenakh Nut, Padisarah Pudding and Coffee Beans, plus recipes for those and for Rose Custard and Tulumba. A staff member named Maddah is a storyteller there.
### LARGE +
A message-board notice states that the cafe's original owner went missing on a desert expedition three years before the notice. The name Puspa is a Sanskrit word meaning flower. Regulars include Akademiya researchers, students and veterans of the Corps of Thirty.

## entry: Candied Ajilenakh Nut and Halvamazd
@keys: Candied Ajilenakh Nut, Ajilenakh Nut, Halvamazd
@version: 3.0
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
A crunchy, rich candied sweet made from boiled syrup, filling and Sumeru sesame paste poured into a mould. Nahida names it her favourite food: in her voice-over she says one small bite makes her brain run at top speed, and warns that the jar should not be left within easy reach. Her special dish Halvamazd is a variant, with item text playing on "mazd" meaning wisdom in Sumeru.
### LARGE +
The ingredient has a long reputation: legend says candied Ajilenakh Nuts were the first appetizer at the nightly banquets of the Lord of the Blazing Sun. Food dislikes tied to Sumeru dishes include Dori's for the Charcoal-Baked Ajilenakh Cake and Faruzan's for Padisarah Pudding; Nahida's own least favourite food is sea creatures, which she says feel like swallowing an unknown storm cloud.

## status
### STANDARD
As of 7.1, Sumeru cuisine is a living, well-known regional tradition. Lambad's Tavern and Puspa Cafe remain the two Sumeru City food landmarks, and the dishes above are established canon.
