## summary
### LITE
Liyue cuisine is the food culture of the Geo nation of contracts, built around two long-rival styles. Li style is bold and rich, using ingredients from the mountains; Yue style is lighter and seafood-based. In Liyue Harbor the best-regarded houses of each are Liuli Pavilion (Li) and Xinyue Kiosk (Yue), both on Feiyun Slope. At Chihu Rock, the humbler side of the city, Wanmin Restaurant, run by Chef Mao with his daughter Xiangling as head chef, ignores the quarrel and cooks inventive, spicy, everyday food. Tea is very popular, with Qiaoying Village famous for its leaves.
### STANDARD +
Food is a point of pride and of argument in Liyue: fans of one style clash with fans of the other, and the two rival restaurants need booking months ahead (Xinyue Kiosk is noted as expensive). Locals value good ingredients, spice and presentation; the Liyue gourmet Xi'er judges Liyue's seasoning and presentation a cut above Mondstadt's.

## practice
### STANDARD
Liyue has two main culinary styles, as the gourmet Xi'er explains. Li style favours bold, rich, indulgent flavours and draws on mountain ingredients. Yue style favours lighter, brighter flavours and leans heavily on seafood. The two have a long history and a bitter, equally long rivalry. In Liyue Harbor's shopping district, Feiyun Slope, Liuli Pavilion is the leading Li-style house and Xinyue Kiosk the most authentic Yue-style one; Xinyue Kiosk's menu is mentioned as including Crystal Shrimp, Golden Crab and Fullmoon Egg. Xinyue Kiosk is noted as expensive; both need booking months ahead. Chihu Rock, the poorer side of the city, leans towards street food, with up-and-coming restaurants such as Wanmin Restaurant. Dishes in item text include Zhongyuan Chop Suey (a strong-flavoured comfort dish, described as the authentic "Liyue taste" for older folk), Jueyun Guoba (a crisp rice-and-horsetail crust topped with ham and Jueyun Chili, the snack Xiangling's companion Guoba is named after), Almond Tofu (a chilled almond dessert named tofu only for its shape) and Jade Parcels (ham and lotus seeds wrapped in vegetable leaves with a spicy broth). Tea is very popular: Qiaoying Village, in the north-west bordering Fontaine, is known for high-quality leaves that are served as drinks or made into tea cakes, and every tea establishment has its own way of serving. Dim sum is a popular breakfast; served with tea it is yum cha.
### LARGE +
Farming in rocky Liyue is limited, so crops are concentrated where terraces allow: Qingce Village grows terraced rice that goes to Liyue Harbor to be sold, and sunsettia trees grow across the land. Liyue exports specialties such as Chilibrew and silk; Dawn Winery sells its drinks to Liyue trading unions and merchants who resell them abroad, and Qiaoying's tea supplies local and foreign markets alike. Trade also brings foreign ideas: Xiangling's map notes mention Mondstadt's valberries and a Mondstadt restaurant's matsutake dish, and Xi'er says Xiangling sources some ingredients from Mondstadt.

Wanmin Restaurant (Wanmin-tang) is a deliberate outsider. Chef Mao says Li and Yue are all "Zhongyuan Chop Suey" to him: his kitchen cares about delicious food from everyday ingredients, universally loved flavours and excellent cooking, not systems and frameworks, and he calls those who impose them overcompensating amateurs. Its signature dish is Black-Back Perch Stew, fish cooked with chili oil made from Jueyun Chili and Violetgrass (powdered Violetgrass in the heated oil, per the item text). Xiangling's character story says the dish combines Li richness and strong colour with Yue freshness and nutrition; the Violetgrass touch is the restaurant's secret, which Zhongli mentions in the Stone Harbor Treasure Journal event (limited), with Chef Mao looking baffled in the background. The restaurant sells simple food, ingredients and cooking recipes.
### ULTRA +
Xiangling's story is the sharpest expression of Liyue's kitchen politics. She learned cooking from her father, who gave her his handwritten journal bearing the line that the most important secret of cooking is passion. At the time the Li-Yue conflict was white-hot and many restaurants, Wanmin included, felt the pressure; she was angered by the monopoly on luxury ingredients like matsutake and crab, and took over the kitchen to make super-delicious dishes out of any ingredient. Her methods are unorthodox: she has used slimes and lizards in recipes, took the hilichurls' clubs for Hilichurl Club BBQ Fish, and wrecks woks in the process. Her Cold-Dressed Fowl left her father on plain congee for two days, since mint and Qingxin flowers did not agree with him, though she herself never seemed affected. Chef Mao says she is constantly inventing dishes and that he sent her off to explore so the restaurant would not confine her; if anyone revolutionises Liyue cuisine, he says, it will be her. Her cooking won acclaim in both Mondstadt and Liyue.

Customer culture at Wanmin is warm and informal: regulars are recognised by their first words, anyone asking whether Xiangling is cooking that day is plainly a regular, and she offers to cook what is not on the menu. Chef Mao notes a young woman with a strange headdress (Ganyu) who often orders only small vegetarian portions; Shenhe later works there as a server after Ganyu's referral, and a Ningguang voice-over notes she has financed the restaurant. During the Moonlight Merriment event (limited) Chef Mao says Xiangling is that year's Masterful Chefs champion.

## regional_variation
### STANDARD
Within Liyue the contrast is between styles and districts rather than provinces: Li (mountain, rich) versus Yue (sea, light), the polished restaurants of Feiyun Slope versus the street-food leaning Chihu Rock, and the tea villages of the north-west. Qingce Village is rice country; Liyue Harbor draws in every nation's trade.
### LARGE +
Compared with Mondstadt, Xi'er likes the fresh, quality produce there but finds Liyue better on seasoning and presentation, and misses spicy food. In an event conversation (Silverwing in Pursuit of the Moon, limited), her older sister Da Xi'er remarks that Snezhnaya's food is made to keep people energetic in the cold and might taste heavy in Liyue, and that Liyue dishes taken north unchanged might seem light there, since environment affects taste. See also Mondstadt and Fontaine cuisine.

## scene_use
### STANDARD
Use Liyue food for lively, opinionated scenes: a quarrel between a Li-style and a Yue-style loyalist, a crowded Wanmin table with Xiangling at the stove, Xi'er sniffing out a dish's origin, a tea tasting, or a business dinner at an expensive, fully booked restaurant. Characters should talk about ingredients, spice and technique; do not invent prices, menus or recipes beyond the item text.
### LARGE +
Contrast settings: diners at the Feiyun Slope houses Liuli Pavilion and Xinyue Kiosk, working people at Chihu Rock, villagers in tea or rice country. Wanmin staff are friendly and informal, so avoid portraying Xiangling as a partisan of Li or Yue; her principle is that good food is good food. Xiangling's experiments with slimes and lizards are known in her circle but not something every diner assumes.

## examples
### LARGE
Black-Back Perch Stew: Wanmin's signature, fish fillets in heated oil with Jueyun Chili and powdered Violetgrass. Jueyun Guoba: crisp rice-and-horsetail crust with ham and Jueyun Chili. Zhongyuan Chop Suey: hearty comfort dish. Almond Tofu: silky chilled almond dessert. Jade Parcels: ham and lotus seeds in vegetable wrappers with a spicy broth. Xinyue Kiosk menu: Crystal Shrimp, Golden Crab, Fullmoon Egg. Tea and tea cakes from Qiaoying Village; dim sum with tea (yum cha) at breakfast.

## status
### STANDARD
As of 7.1, Liyue cuisine remains a well-established tradition. Li and Yue still compete, with Liuli Pavilion and Xinyue Kiosk the leading houses, while Wanmin Restaurant has made its mark through Xiangling's innovations, recognised in the Masterful Chefs contest of the Moonlight Merriment event (limited).

## entry: Xinyue Kiosk

@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
Renowned as the most authentic Yue-style (seafood-led) restaurant in Liyue, on Feiyun Slope. It is expensive with a long waitlist (about three months per the wiki; about half a year per Xi'er). Its dishes mentioned include Crystal Shrimp, Golden Crab and Fullmoon Egg. During the quest Know Thyself, Know Thy Enemy, the gentry member Maocai meets a Fatui Pyro Agent there.

## entry: Wanmin Restaurant
@keys: Wanmin Restaurant, Wanmin-tang, Chef Mao
@version: 1.0
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
A famous restaurant in Chihu Rock, run by Chef Mao, whose daughter Xiangling is head chef. Its map description says some believe its goal is to keep everyone fed, others that it hopes to unify the world's regional cuisines. It sells simple food, ingredients and cooking recipes. Shenhe works there as a server and Ningguang has financed it. Its signature is Black-Back Perch Stew (a dish Xiangling's story credits her with creating).
