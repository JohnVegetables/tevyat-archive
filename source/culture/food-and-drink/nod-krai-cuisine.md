## summary
### LITE
Nod-Krai food is warming, practical frontier cooking with a clearly Nordic flavour. Staples are salmon chowder (Lohikeitto), rye bread and crispbread, smoked fish and meat, sausages in bread (the Nod-Krai Hot Dog), and sweets built on wild berries, above all pale Lakkaberries in krumkakes and pies. The region's main restaurant is Speranza in Nasha Town, run by Katya.
### STANDARD +
The cuisine is known in game through item descriptions, shop pages and a few quests rather than a single cultural write-up, so this entry sticks to what those texts say.

## practice
### STANDARD
Cold, short days and a scrap-built frontier shape the menu: food is filling, keeps well, and leans on fish, cured and smoked meat, rye and berries. Dishes named in canon:
- Lohikeitto: described as a classic soup of the frigid regions. Salmon chunks and fresh vegetables are cooked together, and cream goes in just before serving for a silky chowder.
- Nod-Krai Hot Dog: a sausage in bread with fried onions and sauce, called classic convenience food. Katya's stall call advertises it fresh out of the oven.
- Lakkaberry Krumkakes: a classic dessert; batter is cooked on a griddle and rolled up fast, with butter and fruit added. Item text says it is very popular among children.
- Lakkaberry Madame: a sweet-and-sour chopped chicken dish, said to have become really popular in Nod-Krai though its recipe was imported. Its name hints at the Mondstadt dish Sweet Madame.
- Lakkaberry Pie: once made from Lakkaberries alone, later enhanced with Midsommar Berries, but the name never changed. It is a beloved staple on Nod-Krai tables.
- Midsommar Torte: a small baked cake with berry jam, Smetana and a berry on top, a popular surprise on festival days.
- Smoked Fish Steak: fish cured, laid on a wooden slat and smoked, so it keeps its shape and gains a smoky taste.
- Meat-Filled Rye Bun: a boned fish wrapped in bacon, sealed in thick rye dough and baked; the thick bread keeps the fish juicy.
### LARGE +
More dishes:
- Crispy Roast: scored, marinated meat baked in an oven, with simple ingredients and authentic flavour.
- Borderland Crispbread: dry rye crispbread stamped with Icy Pebbles, with a central hole said to imitate them; it has almost no moisture and stores well.
- Birch-Steamed Pork Ribs: ribs marinated, air-dried and steamed on birch branches until tender with a woody scent; the text calls it one of Nod-Krai's traditional festive dishes.
- Berries and Fried Meat: thin fried-then-stewed meat over mashed potatoes, usually eaten with crushed berries.
- Veggie Pot Soup: a mild vegetable soup with light seasoning.
- Berry Herbal Candy: a translucent candy of local flowers, herbs and berries that brings a little brightness to the frozen plains.

Fireside Merriment is a platter of hearty meat cuts that mixes Snezhnayan cured meats with Nod-Krai ham and sausage; many older locals reportedly enjoy it by the fire with a drink. Frontier Land is a layered non-alcoholic drink sold at The Flagship, said to be inspired by Nod-Krai itself, icy at first, then smoky and astringent; the text adds that the alcoholic version is a daily staple of many veteran adventurers. Lakkaberries and Midsommar Berries are wild cooking ingredients (see the berry entry), so many sweets depend on foraging in the warmer parts of the islands.
### ULTRA +
Item texts are consistent about what the food is for: Birch-Steamed Pork Ribs and the Midsommar Torte are tied to festivals, Meat-Filled Rye Buns and Borderland Crispbread to travel and storage, and Lohikeitto, soups and roasts to warmth against the wind. Everyday dishes also show borrowing: the imported Lakkaberry Madame, Snezhnayan meats in Fireside Merriment and Smetana on the torte. Where these are sold: Katya's Speranza stocks the hot dog, Lakkaberry Madame, Midsommar Torte and Crispy Roast, with recipes for Lohikeitto, pie, candy and ribs; the Meat-Filled Rye Bun recipe comes from Limppu in Nasha Town, and Berries and Fried Meat is sold by Demyan at The Flagship.

## regional_variation
### STANDARD
Nod-Krai is nominally Snezhnayan and its food overlaps with Snezhnaya's rye, Smetana and cured-meat tradition (see the Snezhnaya cuisine entry), but the islands have their own fish, salmon soup, smoked steaks and wild berries. The Frostmoon Scions tradition touches the table as well: Lakkaberry item text says that in their legends the fruit is a gift of the Moon Maiden, like kuuhenki, and that sharing and eating Lakkaberries is a way of worshipping Kuutar. Midsommar Berries symbolise life and abundance in the same legacy because they flourish only in spring and summer. Lakkaberries grow on Hiisi Island and Lempo Isle, particularly near Starsand Shoal; Midsommar Berries mostly in the south of Lempo Isle. Per the wiki, an NPC named Gorchakova indicates they are rare in Snezhnaya.
### LARGE +
The Midsommar Berry text also mentions a tale of the King of Winter Icelea and the King of Midsommar Oak, twin monarchs who slay each other as seasons change; the text says most Snezhnayan scholars treat it as a primitive metaphor for the seasons. Use it as folklore, not as a documented festival.

## scene_use
### STANDARD
Use Nod-Krai meals to show a rough but welcoming borderland: a bowl of creamy Lohikeitto after wind and snow, a hot dog bought from Speranza's counter, a rye bun or crispbread in a travel pack, a plate of Lakkaberry Krumkakes offered by a friend. Katya's restaurant is also a refuge for street children, and meals there come with work and shelter. Do not invent prices, rituals or recipes beyond the item texts.
### LARGE +
Contrast settings: the Flagship's rowdy drinks and Frontier Land; Speranza's family-like dining room and Conch Gang kids; a Lightkeeper's or Ratnik's plain field ration; a craftshop where fresh Lakkaberries matter more than market ones. Meals are good vehicles for small kindnesses, such as Katya treating friends of Maite, or Ineffa fetching fresh berries for Aino.

## entry: Lohikeitto
@keys: Lohikeitto
@version: 6.0
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
A classic soup of the frigid regions: salmon chunks and fresh vegetables cooked together, with cream and seasoning added just before serving for a smooth, warming chowder. Katya sells the recipe at Speranza. A Knights of Favonius member, Lohen, remembers eating it in Nod-Krai and wonders if he will taste it again after returning to Mondstadt.

## entry: Lakkaberry Krumkakes
@keys: Lakkaberry Krumkakes, Lakkaberry Krumkake, krumkake, krumkakes
@version: 6.0
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
A classic Nod-Krai dessert: griddle-cooked batter rolled quickly, then buttered and topped with fruit, crisp and sweet. In the anecdote Ineffa: A Matter of Krumkakes, Ineffa picks Lakkaberries at the Clink-Clank Krumkake Craftshop because Aino prefers krumkakes made with fresher berries than the market's that day. Finishing the anecdote gives the recipe. The craftshop itself began with Aino's research on the world's best krumkake machine before becoming a renowned machine workshop.
### LARGE +
Per the item text, the pastry is especially popular with children.

## entry: Speranza
@keys: Speranza
@keys2: Katya, restaurant, Nasha Town, Conch Gang
@version: 6.0
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
Speranza, in Nasha Town on Lempo Isle, is the main restaurant of Nod-Krai and the home base of the Conch Gang. Its owner Katya is a former Treasure Hoarders adventurer who retired after injuries. She takes in children left on the streets, giving them shelter, food and work; Aino was once one of them. Speranza also has a chef, Jurva, hosts such as Maisie and Mikko, and a luck wheel whose fortunes are told by "Hot Dog". Katya sells the hot dog, Lakkaberry Madame, torte and roast daily, plus recipes and ingredients weekly. A 10% discount applies once the quest Rest Beyond Reach is completed.

## entry: The Chef's Tale
@keys: The Chef's Tale, Food-Obsessed Phantom
@version: 6.0
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
An unmarked world quest at Final Night Cemetery, Paha Isle. A peculiar cooking pot there hosts a food-obsessed phantom chef who says he is making rations for the island's Ratniki. Over three real-time days he sets the Traveler to cook a Nod-Krai Hot Dog (make the bread thicker, add more meat), Berries and Fried Meat (balance meat with berries) and Lakkaberry Madame. At the end he admits he had prepared for the Wild Hunt's arrival but the food was spilled before it could be sent. The reward is the Smoked Fish Steak recipe. His complaints state the priority: plentiful, balanced food matters more than fancy dishes.

## entry: Lakkaberry and Midsommar Berry
@keys: Lakkaberry, Lakkaberries, Midsommar Berry, Midsommar Berries
@version: 6.0
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
Lakkaberries are pearl-white, refreshingly sweet berries growing in threes; in Frostmoon Scions legend they are a gift of the Moon Maiden, a symbol blessed by the new moon, so sharing and eating them is a way to worship Kuutar. Midsommar Berries grow in warmer parts of Nod-Krai and are loved by animals coming out of winter and by children; in the Frostmoon legacy they stand for life and abundance since they flourish only in warm seasons.

## status
### STANDARD
As of 7.1 these dishes are an established part of Nod-Krai life in game. Nothing in canon through 7.1 turns the cuisine into a formal tradition.
