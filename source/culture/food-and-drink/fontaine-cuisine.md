## summary
### LITE
Fontainian cuisine is the refined, elegant, French-style cooking of Fontaine, the nation of justice and theatre. Afternoon tea with desserts is popular among the upper classes, rich dishes like foie gras sit beside humble ones like fish and chips, and Fonta, a bright sweet soda, is the favourite drink. The most famous kitchen is Hotel Debord in the Court of Fontaine, home of the legendary Gateau Debord and, until recently, the chef Escoffier. As in Mondstadt, people there generally dislike spicy food.
### STANDARD +
Fontainian food is regarded across Teyvat as a sophisticated, popular cuisine, and the Hotel Debord is its showpiece restaurant: it also hosts artists and performers, in keeping with Fontaine's love of the stage. Escoffier, its former head chef, is considered a leading figure of modern Fontainian cooking ("precision gastronomy").

## practice
### STANDARD
Afternoon tea is fairly common among the wealthy and is served with the nation's many desserts: macarons, coffee custard (Coffee Bavarois), mousse and fruit tarts. The range runs from casual dishes such as fish and chips to lavish ones such as Fontainian Foie Gras, a pan-fried foie gras dish whose item text calls it traditional and stresses careful temperature control. Other dishes sold at the Hotel Debord include ile flottante, cream of mushroom soup and garlic baguette (duck confit is a recipe sold there). Fontainians generally do not like spicy food, much like Mondstadters. Melusine cuisine is unusual: since Melusines perceive taste differently, humans usually find it unfit to eat, much like the cooking of the Aranara.
### LARGE +
Fonta is the everyday drink: sweet, colourful and drunk for the fun of it (the in-game item only creates sparkles and bubbles around the drinker). It was developed by a team at the Fontaine Research Institute led by Heinry; Denichere joined later. After it sold well, the Institute let the team set up the Fonta Company (Fonta Co.), which the Institute still manages. Heinry would like to rename it "Fonta: Original" once new flavours are marketed. Sugar, which in the Debord's early days was scarce and costly, is far more available now, so rich sweets are a common luxury.
### ULTRA +
Escoffier's character story describes how the cuisine's fine-dining culture shaped her: the tastes and "food culture" of well-off clients limited what she could serve, so while still at the Debord she began quietly blending new flavours, aiming for a world where plain dishes could reach fine-dining tables and luxury food could lose its airs. Food in the Fortress of Meropide's Coupon Cafeteria is the opposite of haute cuisine and is described as a lottery; she used it to rethink precision gastronomy.

## entry: Hotel Debord
@keys: Hotel Debord, Debord, the Debord, Codex Debord
@version: 4.0
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
A famous restaurant in Quartier Narbonnais, Court of Fontaine. Its owner holds that "art is the best seasoning for good food", and it invites artists to perform; the interior is designed so that all guests can see the stage. The wiki lore says it was founded by the Fleuve Cendre chefs Modeste and Savelli, whose use of ingredients found on their expeditions made Fontainian cooking popular, and that it was an instant hit with the wealthy. Its Codex Debord, a book of Modeste's recipes, is reserved for current and former head chefs, with copies made as needed. The host Sanguinetti sells food, ingredients and recipes there.
### LARGE +
About ten years after its founding, the founders found a container in sunken Remurian ruins whose contents greatly sharpened the user's taste; Modeste used it to create the original Gateau Debord, the dessert considered the peak of Fontaine at the time, limited to sixteen orders a day because sugar was scarce. Modeste eventually realised that long use of the ingredient had destroyed his sense of taste, and he died; Savelli, having received a letter from him, took the blame to protect both their reputations. The hotel's standing collapsed after Savelli's death and recovered over the following decades through careful management. (Source: Hotel Debord wiki lore; this history is not a quest scene.)

## entry: Gateau Debord
@keys: Gateau Debord, Gâteau Debord, Debord cake
@version: 5.6
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
The Hotel Debord's legendary dessert, created by Modeste. Its recipe vanished after his death, and later chefs made many imitations; the Spina di Rosula once arranged a Debord tasting of a replica rumoured to be almost identical, limited to sixteen pieces a day (an event from Escoffier's childhood). Escoffier recreated it by trial and derivation and is credited with restoring the restaurant's glory. In the Treasured Above All story, a rumour about its secret ingredient drives the plot.
### LARGE +
In the 5.6 Story Quest Treasured Above All (Escoffier's Dulciaria Structura chapter; a same-named 5.6 event also existed, @availability limited), the Traveler helps Escoffier after the current head chef Savanna, drugged with lumiline by the chef Monica, mistreats staff. Monica had learned of the secret ingredient and wanted to sell it as a military asset; she needed Escoffier's original Codex. The conflict ends in the Remurian ruins, with Savanna asking Escoffier to come back; Escoffier declines the post but says she will visit periodically to check standards.

## entry: Escoffier and Precision Gastronomy
@keys: Escoffier, precision gastronomy
@version: 5.6
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
Escoffier, the former head chef of the Hotel Debord, is called the founder of "precision gastronomy": measuring flavours with scientific rigour to create vivid taste experiences. She became Furina's personal pastry chef and then the youngest head chef in the Debord's history. After a food safety incident involving lumiline (placed by another chef, Monica) she accepted responsibility and served a short sentence in the Fortress of Meropide. She has her own entry.

## scene_use
### STANDARD
Use Fontainian meals for refined scenes: tea and macarons at an upper-class table, a Hotel Debord dinner with a performance in view, a cheap Fonta from a street vendor, or a quarrel over sweetness versus spice. Locals take food seriously and talk about technique; do not invent specific menu prices or recipes beyond the item text.
### LARGE +
Contrast classes: wealthy diners at the Debord, ordinary Fontainians with Fonta and fish and chips, and Meropide inmates with the Coupon Cafeteria. A visitor from Mondstadt or Sumeru finds the spice level mild. Keep the Debord secret ingredient and the lumiline plot for scenes set after Treasured Above All (5.6) only.

## examples

Fonta: a sweet, colourful soda from the Research Institute's Fonta Company. Fontainian Foie Gras: pan-fried foie gras, a "traditional dish of Fontaine". Gateau Debord: the legendary cake; Gateau Debord: Magnifique is Escoffier's special dish in the game. Afternoon tea sweets: macarons, coffee custard, mousse, fruit tarts.

## status
### STANDARD
As of 7.1, Fontainian cuisine is a well-established, famous tradition. Escoffier no longer runs the Debord's kitchen (Savanna does), though she checks its standards from time to time, and the Gateau Debord is again a sought-after dish.
