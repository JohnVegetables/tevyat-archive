## summary
### LITE
Natlanese food is hearty and filling, heavy on starch and meat. Grainfruit, a plant named for the giant grains along its cob, is the staple and the base of many dishes, including tatacos, grainfruit meat soup and the Hot Spring O'Clock pizza. Cacahuatl, bitter and complex, flavours sweets and drinks such as volcano cake, saurus crackers and xocoatl. The main restaurant is Zakan's Street Bites, a food stall run by the innkeeper Chanca.
### STANDARD +
Food in Natlan sits in everyday tribal life: warriors, pilgrims and travellers eat at the stadium inn, and individual tribes have their own snacks. Saurians also figure in the food culture, appearing as shapes on crackers and as a motif on pizza.
### LARGE +
Most signature dishes are simple enough that, according to Chanca, almost everyone in Natlan knows how to make tatacos. Item texts treat food as comfort and good cheer, with sayings, shared meals and snacks that calm the nerves.
### ULTRA +
Detail on specific dishes, ingredients and shops is in the sections and entries below. Documented regional variation is thin and mostly limited to a few tribe-linked snacks.

## practice
### LITE
Grainfruit is the base of tatacos, grainfruit meat soup and Hot Spring O'Clock. Tatacos are thin tacos baked first, filled with vegetables and grilled shrimp, then topped with a sauce. Grainfruit meat soup comes with a choice of broths and side dishes, but must contain lots of grainfruit. Cacahuatl goes into sweeter things: volcano cake, saurus crackers and the drink xocoatl.
### STANDARD +
Tatacos' item text says it is hard to say what is more common in Natlan, phlogiston or tatacos, and quotes a Natlanese saying that life might disappoint but tatacos never will. Xocoatl is made from cacahuatl ground and blended with spices; the name means "bitter water" and stayed after added sugar made it sweet, fragrant and popular. Saurus crackers are light, crispy, sweet crackers stamped with saurian patterns; the text wonders whether saurians really eat crackers. Volcano cake is a baked cake with a hard outer layer and a soft molten filling, served with something cold on top to play the "ash cloud".
### LARGE +
Cacahuatl's seeds, once fermented and baked, are made into delicacies and are considered the most valuable part; its white flesh can also be pressed for juice. Quenepa berries (blue, juicy, growing in large numbers near the Scions of the Canopy and shaped like flamegranates) are also eaten, and flamegranates less so; the culture page says flamegranate fills whatever it goes into with phlogiston. Embercore flower nectar is extremely sweet and leaves a burning sensation on the lips and tongue, a flavour popular among the tribes. Hot Spring O'Clock is a grainfruit-and-cheese pizza with a little Koholasaurus picture drawn on it, meant to be eaten hot for the feeling of a soak in a hot spring.
### ULTRA +
Grainfruit meat soup, per its text, delivers soft meat, crunchy vegetables and a lively spiciness. Puff pops take the "pop" in their name from grainfruit kernels bursting under high heat; their unpredictable flavours are said to raise the heart rate, which is why they are popular. The xocoatl text also describes a rich cacahuatl scent and a silky, creamy sweetness. The Nahuatl word meaning "bitter water" behind the name is the wiki's observation, not an in-world statement.

## regional_variation
### STANDARD
The Scions of the Canopy are linked to puff pops, and the People of the Springs to the Hot Spring O'Clock pizza. Both are cooking recipes unlocked through reputation with those tribes. The culture page adds that the Collective of Plenty, with its plentiful crops, is fond of mushrooms because Varesa's family orchard came to grow berryshrooms after an unforeseen incident involving Bifrons.
### LARGE +
Volcano cake's recipe is found in the Tezcatepetonco Range and saurus crackers' in the Toyac Springs area, so dishes are spread across regions, but their texts do not claim tribal ownership. No source read names signature dishes for the Children of Echoes, Flower-Feather Clan or Masters of the Night-Wind.

## entry: Zakan's Street Bites and Chanca
@keys: Zakan's Street Bites, Chanca, Weary Inn
@version: 5.0
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
Zakan's Street Bites is the main restaurant for Natlan, a food stall at the entrance of the Weary Inn in the Stadium of the Sacred Flame area. It is run by Chanca, an Ancient Name bearer and once a top-ranked warrior in the Pilgrimage of the Return of the Sacred Flame. A leg injury in her last battles in the Night Warden Wars forced her to retire, and she later took over the inn. The stall sells meals, ingredients and recipes such as grainfruit meat soup and xocoatl.
### LARGE +
Chanca calls tatacos her personal specialty and teaches the Traveler to make them. She says she stayed at the inn free as a contestant, and tells pilgrims that friends of contestants can eat and sleep there at no charge. She says that after the battles ended many old comrades stop by for meals, injured but seemingly happy, and that she can only treat them to food now. A permanent 10% shop discount for players at maximum reputation with two of the six tribes is a gameplay detail.

## entry: Tatacos
@keys: Tatacos, Tataco
@version: 5.0
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
Tatacos are thin baked tacos, filled with vegetables and grilled shrimp and drizzled with sauce. They are Chanca's specialty and are described as a familiar part of Natlan life.
### LARGE +
Item text includes the Natlanese saying that tatacos never disappoint. Citlali has a special version of the dish in gameplay.

## entry: Xocoatl and Cacahuatl
@keys: Xocoatl, Cacahuatl
@version: 5.1
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
Cacahuatl is a Natlan crop with a bitter, flavourful fleshy part and seeds that, fermented and baked, become delicacies. Xocoatl is the drink made from it, originally a bitter spiced water and now a sweet, creamy, widely loved beverage that kept its "bitter water" name.
### LARGE +
Ifa has a special version of xocoatl in gameplay. Its recipe is sold by Chanca.

## entry: Grainfruit and grainfruit dishes
@keys: Grainfruit, Grainfruit Meat Soup, Hot Spring O'Clock, Puff Pops
@version: 5.0
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
Grainfruit is found all over Natlan and is the basis of grainfruit meat soup, Hot Spring O'Clock and puff pops. Grainfruit meat soup is a spicy mix of meat and vegetables with many broth and side-dish choices.
### LARGE +
Hot Spring O'Clock is the People of the Springs' pizza topped with grainfruit and cheese, and puff pops are a Scions of the Canopy snack.

## entry: Volcano Cake
@keys: Volcano Cake
@version: 5.2
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
Volcano cake is a sweet with a soft molten centre and a cold topping.
### LARGE +
Its recipe comes from an Exquisite Chest in the Tezcatepetonco Range. The cake's text emphasises satisfaction and comfort.

## entry: Saurus Crackers
@keys: Saurus Crackers
@version: 5.0
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
Saurus crackers are buttery, crisp, sweet crackers made with cacahuatl and grainfruit and shaped like little saurians.
### LARGE +
Their recipe comes from an Exquisite Chest in the Toyac Springs area; the wiki also lists a dialogue option with Sorimana and a shop run by Cipac as sources. The cracker text jokes that saurians themselves seem to like them.
