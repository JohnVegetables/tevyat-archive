## summary
### LITE
Project Stuzha ("severe cold" in Russian) is the package of energy-saving measures passed by the Snezhnograd Veche that forced Snezhnaya's factories and households to use less Kresnik Kristall, so the saved energy could go to research at the Armory Palace. Announced as temporary, it was extended again and again, leaving much of the country cold, short of goods and angry. Its hidden purpose is to feed Project Mistilteinn. It was put on hold in 7.0, when Ronova's curse stalled the research.
### STANDARD +
It was first mentioned in 4.6 and became the backdrop to Snezhnaya in 7.0. It is the clearest example of the Tsaritsa's grand plan reaching ordinary lives: the people pay in warmth and wages for a goal they were never told.

## established
### LITE
- The Veche passed a resolution requiring all factories and households to cut energy use. Kresnik Kristall, the crystal fuel Snezhnayans burn to survive the cold, is the energy in question.
- The saved energy was diverted to the Armory Palace in Glupov.
- Alyosha says the project was announced for six months but has been extended repeatedly, and that no one outside knows where the energy goes.
- The extensions need a majority vote in the Veche. Pulcinella, the Veche's chair, steers those votes; Alyosha says detractors cave to his political pressure.
### STANDARD +
- **Effects.** Businesses closed, trains stopped serving some towns, and Kristall deliveries became fixed rations. In Sretomorozsk the Fatui delivered Kristall twice a month. In the village of Alatyr, deliveries stopped entirely, according to Shishkin (the act summary elsewhere calls Alatyr an abandoned town). Shishkin and the town's mayor were caught skimming Kristall for resale; Shishkin said he did it for his home village.
- **Who is involved.** Lyney says Pulcinella and Pantalone work on the project together (4.6), and that the House of the Hearth was given a key and dangerous role after winning the Hydro Gnosis. Tartaglia was summoned back from Fontaine for it. Pierro, in 7.0, explains its real purpose.
- **Real purpose.** Pierro says Stuzha is a set of measures to consolidate the nation's resources whose greater aim is to serve Project Mistilteinn, a weapon for the Tsaritsa's fight against the Heavenly Principles.
- **Suspension.** The curse of Ronova prevented any breakthroughs at the Armory Palace, so in 7.0 the project is suspended. Citizens and factory owners are told so.
### LARGE +
- **Politics.** Opposition grows through 7.0. Tartaglia says a mock vote went badly and Pulcinella was furious. In the real vote the project passes with 61 percent, a smaller majority than before, after the factory owner Chugunov unexpectedly votes against.
- **Resistance.** The Rokot, a covert organisation of anonymous members, works against it, mostly through evidence and official channels. Its most radical member, Lelek, flips Veche votes by coercion; in Mistilteinn Odette reveals she is Lelek. Alyosha says the Veche would crack down on the Rokot if it crossed a line.
- **The Traveler's question.** In Great Deeds on the Tundra the Traveler, with Odette present, bluntly asks Tartaglia and Sandrone why they will not stand up for ordinary people. Tartaglia says he would vote against it if the Tsaritsa allowed him, but calls the hardship a sacrifice worth making for her long-term vision; he and Sandrone keep raising concerns in the Veche while speaking for those they represent.
- **After the pause.** In 7.1 Tartaglia says the atmosphere in the Veche had become lighter because more energy could go to the people.

## hardship_and_rationing
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Daily life under Stuzha is shown most in Sretomorozsk (7.0). Shops are shut, the inn is unavailable, and a Fatui officer, Polonsky, arrives to hand out the town's Kristall under watch. A near-fight breaks out over the amount. Shishkin mediates and later confesses that he and the mayor were diverting shipments. A villager, Zolotova, speaks of falling sales and tightened belts; in Snezhnograd a guild trader, Savva, says his guild's trade was halved even as strong liquor sold better.
### LARGE +
- Alyosha does not blame ordinary Fatui: he says they always act in the Tsaritsa's interest, and notes that Sretomorozsk still gets Kristall because of a relief proposal from Pulcinella himself, while the Northland Bank gave some Mora for disaster relief.
- Vodyanitsa describes Kristall rations as barely enough to survive on. She says Chugunov of the Iskra Manufacturing Plant drew energy illegally and underpaid workers, which Mitya confirms is rumoured; Mitya adds that Pulcinella is now demanding strict tracking of every Kristall batch.
- Tartaglia reports that Fatui families in remote areas have around 15 percent less Kristall than a typical Snezhnograd family and calls for clear allocation rules. Pantalone replies that fairness is relative.

## purpose
@spoiler: major
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
Mitya, the Royal Energy Commission researcher, tells Vodyanitsa that the Armory Palace needs a brand-new energy source that is neither the Light Realm's power nor elemental energy. Only the power in Stellar Linchpins qualifies, but harnessing it burns enormous amounts of Kristall with little progress. In the Armory Palace itself the Traveler wonders aloud whether the whole point of Stuzha was to support this research, and Pierro confirms it.
### LARGE +
Pierro says the Kuuvahki Experimental Design Bureau in Nod-Krai was part of the same effort. Columbina recalls that, in the past, she noticed the word Mistilteinn coming up together with Stuzha but kept her distance. Mitya says that when Ronova's curse struck, the materials and energy gathered through Stuzha were wasted.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 Stuzha is described as suspended since the 7.0 events. Mistilteinn's restrictions have been lifted, but canon does not yet show whether the rationing returns.
### STANDARD +
When Chugunov's factory is told in 7.0 that Stuzha is suspended, his staff propose expanding production; Chugunov instead orders production halted and the workers compensated, leaving the energy for those who need it. The Rokot celebrates an interim victory but warns against complacency.

## open_questions
### STANDARD
- Whether and how the rationing returns now that Mistilteinn can move forward.
- What the Tsaritsa's people will do about the damage caused over the project's long run.
### LARGE +
- The wiki says the project resumes after Ronova's authority is revoked; the quests read for this entry do not show it.

### ULTRA +
- Zolotova, a villager, says things only got worse once Stuzha began and, after Mistilteinn, that it is over and life may improve; Savva, a guild trader, says it is merely on the back burner. Their differing words show that ordinary people did not know its true status.

## entry: Veche Vote on Stuzha
@keys: Stuzha vote, Veche session, Veche vote
@version: 7.0
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
In Silence Alone is Disfavored the Traveler watches a Veche session. Pulcinella presents energy statistics and declares that Stuzha will continue. Tartaglia protests the Kristall gap for Fatui families in remote areas; Pantalone says fairness is relative; Sandrone steers debate back to data. The motion passes with sixty-one percent, narrower than before, after Chugunov votes against. Vodyanitsa suspects Lelek pressured him.
### LARGE +
A reporter, Mironova, turns the debate into a sensational article. Vodyanitsa explains that elites usually back the project because ending it would force their factories to absorb the Tsaritsa's unchanged energy demand.

## entry: Rokot and Stuzha
@keys: Rokot
@keys2: Stuzha, Veche, Kristall
@version: 7.0
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
The Rokot is a covert organisation of anonymous, codenamed members who choose their own methods within its principles. Members such as Vodyanitsa oppose Stuzha's abuses by gathering evidence of corruption and leaking it so officials investigate; Lelek used coercion instead. The Traveler joins it in Silence Alone is Disfavored; one application option reads that they oppose Stuzha. A Fatui officer, Polonsky, says Rokot members hide under false identities working against Stuzha.

## entry: Sretomorozsk Kristall Scheme
@keys: Sretomorozsk, Shishkin, Polonsky
@keys2: Kristall, Stuzha
@version: 7.0
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Sretomorozsk depends on twice-monthly Kristall deliveries from the Fatui under Polonsky. Alyosha and the Traveler discover that Shishkin and the mayor are siphoning part of each shipment. They are arrested and sent to Snezhnograd; Shishkin says he wanted to help his home village of Alatyr, which no longer got deliveries. Afterward the Traveler and Alyosha persuade Polonsky to help get their train onto the tracks.

## entry: Stuzha Suspension
@keys: Stuzha suspended, Stuzha on hold
@keys2: Mistilteinn, Ronova, curse
@version: 7.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
After Mitya's experiment triggers Ronova's curse and everyone present 'dies', he recommends halting the work, and Pierro agrees to hold Mistilteinn until the Ruler of Death is dealt with. Stuzha is reported suspended to factory owners and villagers. In 7.1 Tartaglia says the Veche has become lighter. Mistilteinn can move on after Ronova's authority is revoked; Stuzha's own fate is not shown.
