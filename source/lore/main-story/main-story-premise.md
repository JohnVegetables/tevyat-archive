## summary
### LITE
The Archon Quest is Teyvat's main story. Twin travellers from another world try to leave Teyvat, and an unknown god (later named as Asmoday) blocks their way. She takes one twin and seals the other, the Traveler. On waking, the Traveler fishes the floating companion Paimon out of the sea, and the two cross the nations in turn: Mondstadt, Liyue, Inazuma, Sumeru, Fontaine, Natlan, then Nod-Krai and Snezhnaya. In each, they meet that nation's Archon, one of The Seven, hoping the gods know where the lost twin is. In Liyue they learn the twin now leads the Abyss Order in a war against the gods. From then on the journey is also about understanding why.
### STANDARD +
Two threads run through every chapter. The Fatui, servants of the Tsaritsa, collect the Archons' Gnoses. The fallen kingdom of Khaenri'ah and its Cataclysm, about 500 years ago, link the sibling, Dainsleif, the hilichurls and the Harbingers. As of 7.1 the Traveler and Paimon are in Snezhnaya, the last nation of the twin's journey.

## established
### STANDARD
Players choose which twin they play, so the Traveler is Aether and the sibling Lumine, or the reverse. Paimon is the guide and constant companion. The Traveler needs no Vision. They take up each nation's element, mostly by resonating with its Statue of The Seven. In Mondstadt, Venti remarks that he knew the Tsaritsa well five hundred years ago, but that after a catastrophe at that time she cut off all ties with him. That is the first hint of the Cataclysm.
### LARGE +
The Traveler asked Mondstadt and Liyue to put up missing-person posters for the sibling. They stopped asking once they learned the sibling leads the Abyss Order. Later chapters stress that the Traveler is an outsider. Irminsul cannot record them, the Eye of Graeae in Snezhnaya recognises them as a Descender, and Ronova says a Descender's soul cannot enter the Ley Lines.

## structure
### STANDARD
Story order (release versions): Prologue, Mondstadt (1.0); Chapter I, Liyue (1.0-1.4); Chapter II, Inazuma (1.6-2.6); Chapter III, Sumeru (3.0-3.5); Chapter IV, Fontaine (4.0-4.7); Chapter V, Natlan (5.0-5.7); Song of the Welkin Moon, Nod-Krai and later Sumeru (5.8-6.6); Chapter VII, "Everwinter Without Mercy", Snezhnaya (7.0-7.1).
### LARGE +
From Chapter I on, each chapter ends with a "Traveler" act about the sibling and Khaenri'ah. These are "We Will Be Reunited", "Requiem of the Echoing Depths", "Caribert", "Bedtime Story" and "A Space and Time for You". A separate Interlude Chapter holds stand-alone follow-ups: "The Crane Returns on the Wind", "Perilous Trail", "Inversion of Genesis" and "Paralogism".
### ULTRA +
Act releases: Prologue 1.0. Chapter I Acts I-III 1.0-1.1, "Bough Keeper: Dainsleif" 1.3, "We Will Be Reunited" 1.4. Chapter II prologue "Autumn Winds, Scarlet Leaves" 1.6, Acts I-III 2.0-2.1, Act IV 2.6. Chapter III 3.0-3.2, Act VI 3.5. Chapter IV 4.0-4.2, Act VI 4.7. Chapter V 5.0-5.3, Act VI 5.7. Interlude acts 2.4, 2.7, 3.3 and 5.6. The 6.x versions carry "Luna" names (Luna I is 6.0, Luna VII is 6.6, Luna VIII is 6.7; the Song of the Welkin Moon Archon acts end in 6.6).

## journey
### LARGE
Chapter by chapter, in one line each:
- Mondstadt: the Traveler and Venti (Barbatos) free the dragon Dvalin from the Abyss Order, and Signora takes Venti's Gnosis.
- Liyue: Zhongli (Morax) stages his own death, and the Fatui release Osial. In Act IV the sibling appears as leader of the Abyss Order.
- Inazuma: the Vision Hunt Decree, Fatui Delusions, Signora's defeat and execution, and Ei's change of heart.
- Sumeru: Nahida is freed, the artificial god made from Scaramouche falls, and Rukkhadevata is forgotten.
- Fontaine: the prophecy, the truth about Furina, and Focalors' self-judgement.
- Natlan: Mavuika's war against the Abyss, Ancient Names, and Capitano's sacrifice.
- Nod-Krai: Columbina against Dottore. Dottore is finally destroyed when Irminsul is burned.
- Snezhnaya: Paimon's past, the Curse of Death, the Tsaritsa, and the fall of Ronova.
Each chapter's sub-entry has the details.

## sibling_thread
### LARGE
The sibling is the story's hidden second protagonist. They lead the Abyss Order, still love the Traveler, and promise a reunion "at this journey's end". Khaenri'ah, a nation without gods, was destroyed about 500 years ago, and the gods cursed its people. The sibling was once its crowned heir, called "Savior". Dainsleif, once of its royal guard, travelled with them after the Cataclysm. He now opposes the Order's methods but grieves for the same people. As of 7.1 the sibling holds the Atlas of the New World, which carries the Ley Lines of a New Khaenri'ah, and is seen with the Shade Asmoday. Their goal is to stand before the Heavenly Principles as an equal, with a world behind them.

## recurring_threads
### LARGE
- The Gnoses, links between each Archon and Celestia, which the Fatui gather.
- Celestia and the Heavenly Principles: the false sky, the Nails, Descenders, and the Shades (the Four Great Rulers).
- Khaenri'ah: the Cataclysm, the curse, the Abyss and the Five Sinners.
- Paimon: she seems a simple companion, but she came from Celestia, and Pierro pulled her down to Khaenri'ah.
- Each Archon's burden: freedom, contracts, eternity, wisdom, justice, war.
- The Fatui are not simply villains. The Tsaritsa aims to challenge the Heavenly Principles, and in 7.1 the Traveler fights alongside Harbingers.

## status
@version: 7.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
As of 7.1, after "A Rekviem for the Underworld", the Traveler and Paimon are in Snezhnaya, on cooperative terms with the Tsaritsa and the Fatui. Ronova has lost her Authority and is dying. Before her end she made a Nail of Retribution crumble, which threatens to release Holy Soil. The sibling has not been found. The story has not yet reached Khaenri'ah.
### LARGE +
The Tsaritsa holds six Gnoses. The Pyro Gnosis burned with Irminsul. Dottore and Signora are dead. Capitano is merged with the Lord of the Night.

## accounts
### LARGE
- The sibling: the gods are the enemy, and the Abyss can do what the Seven could not.
- Dainsleif, in the 2020 preview "Travail": the war against the gods "has already begun".
- The Tsaritsa and Pierro: Snezhnaya is preparing to rebel against the Heavenly Principles.
- Asmoday, at the start: she calls herself a sustainer of the Heavenly Principles. Her later alignment with the sibling is unexplained.

## open_questions
### LARGE
Still open: what lies at the "journey's end"; what the sibling and Asmoday will do with the Atlas; whether and when the Heavenly Principles will wake; what follows the Nail's collapse; why the sibling was in Celestia. The Khaenri'ah chapter and "The Sea of Flowers at the End" are unreleased. Treat them as unknown.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay guidance (this project's advice): what characters know depends on where a scene falls in this order. Most residents never learn Archon Quest secrets. A default "present day" scene is set after 7.1.

Do not resolve the sibling's endgame, the Heavenly Principles or Khaenri'ah's fate, and do not invent a reunion. Do not make the sibling a simple villain, or simply in the right.

## entry: Prologue and Chapter I
@keys: The Outlander Who Caught the Wind, Archaic Lord, We Will Be Reunited, Wanderer's Trail
@version: 1.4
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Prologue (Mondstadt, 1.0). The dragon Dvalin, called Stormterror, attacks Mondstadt after the Abyss Order poisons him and turns him against it. The bard Venti is Barbatos, the Anemo Archon. With the Knights of Favonius, Venti and the Traveler free Dvalin. Afterwards the Harbinger Signora tears Venti's Gnosis out of him for the Tsaritsa, and Venti sends the Traveler on to Liyue.

Chapter I (Liyue). Rex Lapis is found dead at the Rite of Descension, and the Traveler is suspected. Tartaglia (Childe) leads them to Zhongli. The Fatui free the sealed god Osial, and Ningguang crushes him with her Jade Chamber. Zhongli then reveals he is Morax. He staged his death to test Liyue, and under a contract with the Tsaritsa he gives his Gnosis to Signora.
### LARGE +
Act IV (1.3-1.4). With Dainsleif, the Traveler foils an Abyss plan to build a mechanical god. The sibling steps out of an Abyss portal as Prince or Princess of the Abyss Order. They say that Dainsleif and the Abyss monsters come from Khaenri'ah and that hilichurls are cursed Khaenri'ahns. They refuse to come home until their war with destiny is won, tell the Traveler to keep travelling and see the truth, and leave.

## entry: Chapters II and III
@keys: Omnipresence Over Mortals, Requiem of the Echoing Depths, Truth Amongst the Pages of Purana, Kalpa Flame
@version: 3.5
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Chapter II (Inazuma). Under the Sakoku and Vision Hunt Decrees, the Traveler joins the Watatsumi resistance. The Fatui arm both sides and give the rebels Delusions that kill their users. Teppei dies of one. The Traveler defeats Signora in a duel, and the Shogun executes her. Then they persuade Ei, inside the Plane of Euthymia, to give up her rigid Eternity. Yae Miko had given the Electro Gnosis to the Harbinger Scaramouche to keep the Traveler safe.

Chapter III (Sumeru). The sages had imprisoned Nahida for 500 years, and with Dottore they try to make a god from Scaramouche and the Electro Gnosis. The Traveler and allies free Nahida and defeat the false god. Nahida cleanses Irminsul of forbidden knowledge. Only the Traveler remembers Rukkhadevata. ## entry: Traveler's elements
@keys: Statue of The Seven, resonate, resonance
@keys2: Traveler, outlander, Aether, Lumine
### STANDARD
The Traveler takes up each nation's element: Anemo in Mondstadt, Geo in Liyue, Electro in Inazuma, Dendro in Sumeru, Hydro in Fontaine and Cryo in Snezhnaya, mostly by resonating with a Statue of The Seven. In Natlan the Pyro statue did not respond, and the Lord of the Night granted Pyro during the final battle against the Abyss.
### LARGE +
In 6.3 Columbina removed some of Teyvat's restrictions on the Traveler, letting them use the elements more freely.
