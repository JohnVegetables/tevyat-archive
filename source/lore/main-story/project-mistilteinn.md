## summary
### LITE
Project Mistilteinn is the Fatui's secret weapon project in Snezhnaya, overseen by Pierro and carried out at the Armory Palace. Its aim is a weapon that would let the Tsaritsa fight the Heavenly Principles, powered by the energy of Stellar Linchpins. It needs enormous energy, which is why Project Stuzha rations Snezhnaya's supply. Ronova's Curse of Death stalled it until her authority was revoked in 7.1; it has since resumed.
### STANDARD +
It is the hidden purpose behind much that the Traveler meets in Snezhnaya and Nod-Krai, from public energy rationing to the Kuuvahki research bureau. The Fatui describe the Linchpin-derived power it uses as "anti-Celestia energy".

## established
### LITE
- It is a Fatui project run by Pierro together with the Armory Palace, a sister project to Project Stuzha.
- Purpose: a weapon for the Tsaritsa's final battle against the Heavenly Principles.
- Power source: energy extracted from Stellar Linchpins, which the Fatui link to the Third Descender.
- Obstacle: Ronova's Curse of Death, which killed everyone near a technological breakthrough. It lifted in 7.1 when her authority was revoked.
### STANDARD +
- Why ordinary elements will not do: Pierro explains that elemental energy is the power of the Human Realm in a form the Heavenly Principles themselves converted it into, so turning it on them would be futile. Kuuvahki alone cannot breach Celestia's defences either, and the Heavenly Principles is used to fighting Light Realm power. The Fatui therefore turned to the energy associated with the Third Descender and the Stellar Linchpins.
- The method: Mitya, a Royal Energy Commission researcher and himself a Linchpin user, is in charge of the energy research that reverse-engineers the Linchpins' energy into a source the Fatui call "anti-Celestia energy".
- Energy cost: the project needs vast power. Pierro states that Project Stuzha, the energy reforms and rationing that Snezhnaya endured, exists to serve Mistilteinn.
- Hidden aims behind public projects: Pierro says the Kuuvahki Experimental Design Bureau in Nod-Krai was a facade for studying kuuvahki; its core objective was to use local resources and findings to build Mistilteinn itself.
- Protection: Pierro says the technology, though not fully developed, has reached a stage where Celestia's creations cannot damage it.
### LARGE +
- Where: the research is done deep beneath the Armory Palace in Glupov. Odette remarks that the facility seems to use structures requisitioned from a past civilisation, which also serves as a disguise; this is her guess.
- The curse's rule, as Mitya works it out in 7.0: when a moment of technological progress occurs, all life nearby is taken. Researchers, technicians and witnesses "die", then come back later without any memory of the time they were dead. Materials and energy gathered through Stuzha were lost each time.
- The 7.0 pause: Mitya's final-phase energy experiment, begun ahead of schedule, triggered the curse. He recommends suspending the experiments and Mistilteinn, and Pierro agrees to put the project on hold until the Ruler of Death has been eliminated.
- The 7.1 resumption: after Ronova's authority is taken back, the curse is undone with it, because Ronova imposed it on her own initiative and the Heavenly Principles never authorised it. Mitya says the blockade on Snezhnayan technology is lifted and the project can finally move forward; research staff of the Armory Palace confirm the restrictions are gone.
### ULTRA +
- Mitya also stresses that Ronova cannot simply do as she pleases, since he is standing there alive after being killed by her curse.
- In Black Feathers Scattered at Parting the last batch of newly purchased equipment for Mitya's new lab is being delivered, with military support arranged through Valeriy and Vesna. Afterwards Pierro tells Dainsleif that in the struggle against the gods no one can see the full picture of fate.
- Paimon's reaction to the project's emissions is what first exposes the link to Celestia (see accounts).

## accounts
### STANDARD
- Pierro (7.0): many of the things the Traveler has encountered so far exist to advance the project, including Stuzha and the Nod-Krai bureau. He tells the Traveler openly, speaking of allies.
- Mitya (7.0): he stresses that he speaks purely as a scientist: with the curse active no breakthrough is possible, so the project must stop.
- Columbina (6.3): she says the word "Mistilteinn" often seemed to come up in connection with Project Stuzha when she lived in Snezhnaya, but she kept her distance and did not know more.
- The Traveler (7.0): when Pierro's description comes up, the Traveler says they were given some intel and made their own deductions, and does not name a source in the text read.
### LARGE +
- Odette (7.0), as Lelek, enemy of Project Stuzha, enters the Armory Palace to rescue Paimon and sees the large machinery; the Traveler wonders aloud whether the whole reason for Stuzha was to support the research there.
- The quests read show the Tsaritsa's goal of overthrowing the Heavenly Principles, but she is not shown describing Mistilteinn herself in these texts; the link to her plan comes from Pierro and the wiki.

## open_questions
### STANDARD
- What exactly the weapon is, how it will be used and when. Canon shows only a sword-like form in the machinery.
- Whether the project can be completed, and what the Heavenly Principles, currently dormant, would do about it.
- Whether Project Stuzha's rationing returns now that Mistilteinn is unrestricted.

## entry: Anti-Celestia Energy
@keys: Anti-Celestia Energy, anti-Celestia energy
@version: 7.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Anti-Celestia energy is the Fatui's name for the power obtained by reverse-engineering Stellar Linchpins, which Pierro describes as associated with the Third Descender. It is meant to give Mistilteinn a chance against the Heavenly Principles, since ordinary elemental energy and kuuvahki are judged useless against Celestia. Mitya leads the research. Its emissions made Paimon ill, even from far away, until Mitya and Pierro used another energy on her that cancelled its effect.
### LARGE +
Mitya says the effect on Paimon was reversed and that Pierro specifically instructed him to do so. Paimon later tells the Traveler she could feel one energy cancelling out the other. Pierro explains the cause: Paimon is a creation of Celestia.

## entry: Mistilteinn Pause and Resumption
@keys: Mistilteinn, Project Mistilteinn
@keys2: suspended, resumed, resume, paused, pause, curse, restrictions
@version: 7.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
In 7.0 the project is put on hold because Ronova's Curse of Death makes breakthroughs impossible. In 7.1, after her authority is revoked, the curse is undone and Mitya declares that Project Mistilteinn can finally move forward.
### LARGE +
The 7.0 suspension follows Mitya's early final-phase experiment, which killed all who were present; the 7.1 resumption follows the fight at Rankova Zorya and Celestia's revocation of Ronova's authority. Pierro still warns that retaliation is possible, since Ronova may have escaped unseen.

## entry: Pierro's Briefing in the Armory Palace
@keys: Pierro, The Jester
@keys2: Mistilteinn, Armory Palace, weapon
@version: 7.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
After saving Paimon, Pierro offers the Traveler "hospitality", answers questions about Mistilteinn and the Tsaritsa, and explains that Stuzha serves the project. He proposes openly joining forces against Ronova. At Paimon's request he activates the machinery, and a large sword-like form appears in it.
### LARGE +
He says the Fatui need not hide anything from allies given the circumstances; the project, in his words, cannot move on until the Ruler of Death has been dealt with.
