## summary
### LITE
"Savior" was the title Khaenri'ah's last king, Irmin, and his court gave to the Traveler's twin when he made them royal heir about five hundred years ago. The Khaenri'ahn people were told the heir would bring the kingdom prosperity and rescue it. Instead Irmin used the heir as a vessel for Abyssal power, and the kingdom was destroyed. The title lingers among descendants who still await a golden-haired Savior.
### STANDARD +
In the Natlan chapter (5.7) Direidyth, a young Khaenri'ahn from the time of the Cataclysm, is shown still searching for the Savior and calls the Traveler by the title; the Abyss Order's Haden recalls that everyone prayed for the Savior when the disaster struck and the heir was nowhere to be found.

## established
@spoiler: major
### LITE
- The Traveler's twin fell into Teyvat separately from the Traveler, wandered the world looking for them, and arrived in the underground kingdom of Khaenri'ah.
- King Irmin, the last Vinster King of the Eclipse Dynasty, declared the twin his heir. They became the Princess (if the Traveler is male, Lumine) or Prince (if female, Aether) of Khaenri'ah, and a great celebration was held.
- The court of Khaenri'ah proclaimed the heir a source of hope who would bring the kingdom prosperity; "the Savior" was the celebratory title that grew from this. Dainsleif says in 5.7 that it was a title given by those who believed the court's rhetoric.
- Soon after, Irmin led the heir to the kingdom's deepest depths and used them as a vessel to absorb "unlimited" Abyssal power.
### STANDARD +
- The Wings of Fate's Course Intertwined glider text places a then-ordinary young knight, Dainsleif, in the plaza crowd at the announcement. The heir looked out over a populace they had not realised lived underground, having already lost their home world.
- Dainsleif was later promoted to captain of the Royal Guard with the title "Twilight Sword". He and the later Five Sinners stormed the palace to rescue his imprisoned brother Vedrfolnir, and the Abyssal power in the heir was divided among the five; Skirk's character story gives one-sixth each.
- The glider text calls the heir the key to the world's near-annihilation.
### LARGE +
- The Savior role was never one the heir chose. In the 5.7 quests the heir says that when the Cataclysm began they were preparing to fight back, but news that the Traveler was alive led them to leave Khaenri'ah at its most critical moment; they say the choice haunted them for five hundred years and that it drives their devotion to restoring the nation.
- Irmin's advisers who distrusted forbidden knowledge did not persuade him to stop, according to Pierro's artifact text as summarised by the wiki.
### ULTRA +
- Direidyth says her grandmother begged her to leave her village and find the Savior before doomsday. The one detail she notes as not matching the Traveler is gender: her village's Savior is described as a young person of the opposite gender to the Traveler, which fits the Traveler's twin.

## the_announcement
@version: 2.8
### STANDARD
The heir was presented to the people at a large celebration in the plaza before the palace gate. Irmin and the Court of Khaenri'ah proclaimed that the heir would bring great prosperity and began promoting them as a "Savior". The glider text says the heir, who had lost their own world, was now asked to carry the hopes of a single nation; Dainsleif watched from the crowd as an ordinary young knight and helped tidy the plaza afterwards.
### LARGE +
The wiki gives the sequence as arrival in Khaenri'ah, appointment as heir and Prince/Princess, the celebration, then the vessel ritual soon after. How long the heir served before the ritual is not stated precisely; one wiki passage mentions "several years" as heir.
### ULTRA +
The Universitas Magistrorum is tied to the arrival only by a namecard line (Achievement: Orbits, 4.0) addressed to an "Almighty Sovereign", reporting predictions of two stars captured by the world's gravity whose paths would intertwine again. The wiki reads this as implying the Universitas predicted the twins' arrival at Irmin's request; the text itself names neither the twins nor Irmin directly.

## the_vessel
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Irmin later led the heir to the kingdom's deepest depths and used them as a vessel for unlimited Abyssal power. His obsession with the Abyss was growing: the Universitas Magistrorum was reassigned to Abyssal research and the Crimson Moon bloodline was hunted. When Vedrfolnir warned that this path would end in the kingdom's fall, Irmin had his eyes gouged out and imprisoned him.
### LARGE +
Dainsleif gathered Hroptatyr, Rhinedottir, Rerir and Surtalogi to free Vedrfolnir, and the rescue became a coup. The Abyssal power in the heir was split among Rhinedottir, Rerir, Vedrfolnir, Surtalogi and Hroptatyr, who became the Five Sinners of Khaenri'ah. Rhinedottir's creations then attacked Khaenri'ah and the nations of Teyvat, and the Heavenly Principles intervened.
### ULTRA +
The glider text frames the meeting of Dainsleif and the heir as an intersection of two paths: a noble falling from the light into the Abyss, and a humble knight climbing from the dark. After the Cataclysm the two travelled Teyvat together for a time, until the sibling realised the "truth of this world" and became Dainsleif's enemy, aligning with the Abyss Order.

## the_title_after_the_fall
@version: 5.7
### LARGE
In the Natlan chapter, Direidyth meets the Traveler and calls them the Savior; Iansan finds it troubling that she seems to regard the Abyss Order's leader, the Traveler's sibling, as a Savior too. Dainsleif is startled that anyone still believes the myth after the Cataclysm and explains the title's origin. Haden says everyone prayed for the Savior when the Cataclysm first struck, but the heir was nowhere to be found.
### ULTRA +
In a time-crossing scene Direidyth, still in her own time near the Cataclysm, asks the Traveler for a memento to prove they are searching for their twin. The sibling later says Direidyth did bring them news of the Traveler and a sight of the Hibernation Chamber key, which is why they left Khaenri'ah; they suspect Istaroth, Ruler of Time, had a hand in the meeting.

## accounts
### LARGE
- Irmin and the court: the heir is a Savior who will bring prosperity (as presented at the ceremony).
- Dainsleif (5.7): "Savior" was a celebratory title given by believers in the court's rhetoric.
- Haden (Abyss Order, 5.7): the Savior was prayed for and absent when the Cataclysm began.
- Direidyth (5.7): a village sage told of a Savior appearing near doomsday; she believes the Traveler could be that Savior.
### ULTRA +
- The heir (5.7): they were preparing to fight the Cataclysm when they received news of the Traveler and chose to go to them; they cite this as the reason they have devoted themselves to restoring the nation.

## open_questions
### LARGE
- Whether the Savior idea predates the heir's arrival or was framed to fit it is not stated.
- How or why the Universitas Magistrorum is said to have predicted the twins' arrival is unresolved.
- Why Direidyth's village kept the Savior story is not explained.

### STANDARD
Khaenri'ah's "Savior" is the celebratory title given to the Traveler's twin, the crown Prince/Princess of Khaenri'ah, by those who believed the court's rhetoric that the heir would bring prosperity and rescue the kingdom. It failed: Irmin used the heir as a vessel for Abyssal power, and the Cataclysm followed. Khaenri'ahn descendants such as Direidyth still look for a golden-haired Savior.
### LARGE +

## entry: Direidyth
@keys: Direidyth
@version: 5.7
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
Direidyth is a young Khaenri'ahn woman, a quest-exclusive NPC in the Natlan chapter, who sets out from her village to find the Savior her grandmother and a sage described. She calls the Traveler the Savior. In a time-crossing scene she asks for a memento to show the Traveler's twin that the Traveler is searching for them; the sibling says her news pushed them to leave Khaenri'ah when it needed them.
