## summary
### LITE
The Sacred Heir Prophecy is the Frostmoon Scions' old belief that a chosen child of their purified Hyperborean bloodline would one day be born to rule Elysium, an age of abundance, and the generations-long breeding programme (the Sacred Heir Project) that tried to bring that child about. About 500 years ago the last Archpriestess, Lovia, pushed it to murderous extremes. It failed: the chosen maiden eloped, her son became the outlaw Avrelian, and Lovia's granddaughter Aila killed her and ended the project.
### STANDARD
The Sacred Heir Prophecy is the Frostmoon Scions' old belief that a chosen child of their purified Hyperborean bloodline would one day be born to rule Elysium, an age of abundance and peace. The Sacred Heir Project is the long programme of controlled breeding and rules on marriage that was meant to produce that child. Its decrees survived in a sealed chamber on Hiisi Island, the Court of the First Edict, until the Traveler and Verna opened it in 6.0. The project ended about 500 years ago, when the maiden chosen to bear the heir eloped, and the Archpriestess Lovia's desperate replacements ended in murder, rebellion and her own death at the hands of her granddaughter Aila.

## established
### LITE
The Scions believed their moon-blessed bloodline would produce a Sacred Heir. The project ran for generations and failed about 500 years ago.
### STANDARD
- **The prophecy.** The decrees in the Court of the First Edict quote it. In the fullness of time Elysium will come, and with it a sovereign ordained to rule it. The "false sevenfold radiance" of the high heavens will fall at that ruler's feet, the bones of the earth will rise to follow, and from the ruler will spring union with the world and the rebuilding of the first great cause.
- **The Sacred Heir Project.** A protocol written after Hyperborea's fall, by Spargapiris of Haxathrausa as Acting Archpriestess of Hyperborea, says the project is the old Seed of Life Project of the Hyperborea era, renamed. The original relied on Tekhneikos processes (gene editing and the work of the fae), which were lost in the fall, so the Scions fell back on slow artificial selection: recorded generations, chosen pairings, persuasion rather than force, and a ban on pairs sharing ancestry within five generations. The expected result was a Sacred Heir who would command pure, unaltered elemental forces at will, without ritual tools or loss of soul, and who would at last be fused with the world.
- **The collapse, about 500 years ago.** The Archpriestess Lovia was driven by fading kuuvahki and failing rites. A maiden of exceptionally pure blood, chosen to bear the heir, fell in love with a Snezhnayan officer and left. Lovia had Rauthvangi retrieve her; the officer died and the maiden killed herself, leaving a son, Avrelian. Lovia then raised the twins Alia and Aila in isolation as replacements and had them kill other Scions and drink their blood to raise their purity.
### LARGE +
- **Marriage law.** Decree III forbids marriage to blood relatives, to clans guilty of blasphemy, murder of the innocent, oath-breaking or parricide, to clans without antlered crowns, to the mentally disturbed, to descendants of the fae, to people outside the faith and to outlanders. Marriage needs the Archpriestess's permission. Breakers are cursed for seven generations, then seven more, and are to have their horns cut off with a silver blade.
- **The Sampo Mill.** Decree VI says only the prophesied heir can rouse the SAMPO (Subatomic Assembling Mass-Energy Patterning Orb), the sacred relic forged by the apostle of the Golden City. Followers below priestess rank were forbidden to enter the sanctum where it was sealed. A separate protocol records that its control module was lost with Hyperborea, so it could not be properly activated even by force.
- **Phasing out.** The same protocol planned to end widespread artificial breeding after about seven generations, after which limited intermarriage with outlanders could be allowed. The wiki notes that later priestesses instead leaned harder on the heir, which raised the importance of bloodline purity.
- **Items as sources.** Most of the story of the collapse is told by artifact and weapon texts rather than dialogue. Frost Devotee's Delirium tells of the bloodline "purified for over a thousand years" and of the chosen girl who cut off her bloodstained antler crown for a young officer. Pristine Plume of the Blessed tells of the twins, the faded storybook of white birds and their promise to leave together. Moonweaver's Dawn quotes Lovia ordering Aila not to marry the son of an outlander because she is "destined to bear the heir to Elysium."
- **The Court's documents.** Decree CMLXIII, "Alternative Plan for the Sacred Heir's Selection," is approved by Lovia, but its content has been erased, so the details of her replacement scheme are not recorded there. The older decrees are issued by Saredne, called the 13th Archpriestess since Hyperborea fell. The protocols name Vinda of Hithamthrausa as the priest who led the original project.
- **How it ended.** Alia fled Hiisi Island. Aila, now the sole focus, obeyed until Solovei told her Lovia was lying, then killed Lovia with the ritual sword Moonweaver's Dawn. As first Moonchanter she abolished the Archpriestess office and the Hyperborean rites, revised the hymns, and sealed the Court of the First Edict with the project's records and evidence of Lovia's deeds so there would be no second Lovia.
- **Aila's verdict.** In her recorded notes Aila writes that even if the maiden had borne the heir as foretold, the ruler would only have brought greater chaos to Snezhnaya, and that the new moon (Kuutar, later Columbina) had nothing to do with Lovia's schemes.
### ULTRA +
- **Version tracking.** The Court of the First Edict, the Silken Moon's Serenade and Night of the Sky's Unveiling artifact texts and Moonweaver's Dawn arrived in 6.0. Sacrificer's Staff and Dawning Frost (6.1) add Lovia's own backstory and the maiden's last dreams. Alia's confrontation with Rauthvangi in Nightingale's Song and the Treasure Hoarders legend in The Raven's Legacy are 6.3.
- **Dawning Frost.** In its text the maiden, now a mother, warns her unborn child never to set foot in Nod-Krai and never to be taken in by "that fool's dream of Elysium," saying no earthly paradise under the moon has been built for mortals. She is troubled by dream visions of Hyperborea's rise and fall and a voice vowing to raise a boundless Elysium "not to be king, but so that no other can ever claim that role."
- **Sacrificer's Staff.** This text presents Lovia as an orphan from another land, unaware of it, who was adopted by the previous Archpriestess after faith had waned and the moon no longer answered. After her adoptive sister, the purest maiden of the far north, died protecting her, she hardened her resolve; the text ties her path to the day her late sister's orphan would be born the sacred heir.

## accounts
### STANDARD
- **The Scions' official doctrine** (decrees, protocols): a prophesied child of pure blood will rule Elysium and wake the SAMPO. The project aims at a being of pure elemental power fused with the world.
- **Lovia** (through Moonweaver's Dawn and Sacrificer's Staff): the project and the Heir are the Scions' hope and duty, justifying even killing. Her certainty is undercut by the reveal that she is not herself of Scion blood and bleeds red.
- **Rauthvangi** (Crown of the Befallen, The Last Night, the First Light): he calls Avrelian the prophesied Ruler of Elysium and says the two of them are "chosen by fate", and he treats the Sampo Mill as a treasure to seize. The Elysium he speaks of himself lies beyond the sky's veil, reached by getting out of the false sky. Alia says he used Avrelian from the start.
### LARGE +
- **Aila** (recorded notes): Lovia's quest was futile and the Heir's rule would have been harmful; the faith should be rebuilt without the Hyperborean rites.
- **Avrelian's legend** (Treasure Hoarders): he wanted an Elysium "for the hungry and the poor" funded by the wealth of the haughty. How far this relates to the title Rauthvangi gave him is not stated.
- Item texts differ on the chosen one: the Silken Moon's Serenade texts say the girl was meant to "bear the sacred heir," and the decree speaks of a ruler to whom the heavens bow "at her feet," while Aureate Radiance of the Far-North Scions calls the maiden's son the "prophesied son." Preserve that mismatch rather than resolving it.

## open_questions
### STANDARD
- What the Heir would have been in practice, and whether it was ever a real possibility rather than a belief the Scions inherited.
- Whether the Heir, the Ruler of Elysium and the Primordial Human are the same thing. The wiki links them; the in-game texts read do not say so.
- Who the "other three" failures are that Rauthvangi mentions.
### LARGE +
- Lovia's exact relation to the twins and to the maiden is given differently in different texts.
- Whether Lauma's rare sensitivity to kuuvahki is meaningful to the old prophecy. No source says it is; the wiki only notes her unusual purity.

## connections
### STANDARD
Lovia, Aila, Alia and Avrelian are the people it touches most directly. It is the backstory for the Moonchanter title, the Court of the First Edict, the Sampo Mill, the Treasure Hoarders and Reed Miller's legend, and for Rauthvangi's schemes at the Pillar of Embla during the Cataclysm. The Hyperborean Primordial Human experiment on the Cryo Dragon's remains (see the Primordial Human entry) is a related but separate Hyperborean project that produced the Belyi Tsar.
### LARGE +
Do not merge the Scions' project with the Tsar's: the Scions' version is a human bloodline project, while the Tsar's origin was an experiment on a dragon's corpse.

## entry: The Maiden Who Fled
@keys: Avrelian's Mother, chosen maiden, Maiden of the Frostmoon Scions
@keys2: Sacred Heir, Frostmoon Scions, Lovia
@version: 6.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The unnamed maiden of exceptionally pure blood was chosen to bear the Sacred Heir about 500 years ago. She met a young Snezhnayan officer, cut off her antler crown and left with him, bearing a son, Avrelian. A spy sent by Rauthvangi, at Lovia's request according to the wiki, caught her; the officer died, and she took her own life with a dagger. Her name is not given in the texts read.
### LARGE +
Item texts treat her as the orphaned daughter of Lovia's late sister. Dawning Frost shows her as a mother haunted by visions, hoping her child would live in peace and keep away from Nod-Krai. Frost Devotee's Delirium says her death forestalled the prophecy.

## entry: Lovia's Replacements
@keys: Alia and Aila, Lovia's replacements, blood-drinking regime
@keys2: Sacred Heir, Lovia
@version: 6.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
After the maiden's death, Lovia took the twins Alia and Aila from a lesser branch of the bloodline and raised them on Hiisi Island. To make up for their thinner blood she had them kill other Scions and drink their blood. The sisters promised each other, from a faded storybook of white birds, to leave together. Alia broke the promise by escaping, leaving Aila to take the full weight of Lovia's attention. Treat this as a harrowing, abusive upbringing.
### LARGE +
Aila's notes say she lied to Solovei when he asked whether she felt hate, as Alia and Lovia had also lied. The wiki describes her as destined to bear the heir only as a replacement, and as forbidden to marry the son of an outlander. Alia later became the second Reed Miller and died at the Pillar of Embla.

## entry: Court of the First Edict
@keys: Court of the First Edict, Sanctum of the Oathbearer
@keys2: Sacred Heir, decree, Aila, Verna
@version: 6.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The sealed chamber on Hiisi Island where the Scions' decrees, protocols and evidence of Lovia's crimes were kept. Aila sealed it about 500 years ago to bury the evil, and the Traveler and Verna broke the seal in the world quest Echoes of an Unfinished Past. Its documents are the main in-game source for the prophecy's exact wording and the project's rules.
### LARGE +
Aila's recording for the Traveler is also kept there. She did not want the Scions' obsession with Hyperborea to survive her and believed a Traveler would one day come to Nod-Krai and finish Rauthvangi for good.

## entry: Rauthvangi and the Ruler of Elysium
@keys: Rauthvangi, mad monk, the Professor, Ruler of Elysium, King of Elysium
@keys2: Avrelian, Reed Miller, Sampo Mill
@version: 6.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Rauthvangi (Rotwang), the Belyi Tsar's envoy, told the outlaw Avrelian that he was the prophesied Ruler of Elysium and that they were both chosen by fate, and used him to steal the Sampo Mill from the Scions. Rauthvangi himself was after the Elysium beyond the sky, not the Scions' one. Avrelian believed him; Alia later saw through the lie.
### LARGE +
In The Last Night, the First Light, a fading Rauthvangi mistakes Alia for Avrelian and urges her to wield the Sampo Mill "with the sacred heir's blood burning inside you", calling "those other three" failures. Alia refuses him and takes up the Reed Miller name against him. This is Rauthvangi's claim, not an independent confirmation that Avrelian or Alia carried an Heir's blood.

## entry: The Sampo Mill and the Heir
@keys: Sampo Mill, SAMPO, Subatomic Assembling Mass-Energy Patterning Orb
@keys2: Sacred Heir, Hyperborea
@version: 6.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The Hyperborean relic was held to belong to the prophesied Heir, who alone could rouse it, and was sealed from lower-ranking Scions. Records say its control module was lost with Hyperborea, so it is nonfunctional. Rauthvangi sought to use it at the Pillar of Embla; Alia ended that by throwing herself into it and igniting it with her Scion blood.
### LARGE +
The Mill's decree promised unending gold, grain and salt and required offerings of wheat, goat's milk and salt each spring. A protocol decided to seal it indefinitely and not to reassess its activation for many generations.

## entry: Lauma and the Bloodline Today
@keys: silver blood, antler crown, bloodline purity
@keys2: Lauma, Frostmoon Scions, Sacred Heir
@version: 6.0
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Silver blood, sensitivity to kuuvahki and an antler crown are the marks of the Hyperborean bloodline. The wiki records only two Scions of unusual purity: Avrelian's mother and, today, Lauma, whose sensitivity is said to appear once a century. Lauma is the Scions' Moonchanter, and no text read calls her the Sacred Heir.
