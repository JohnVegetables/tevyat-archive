## summary
### LITE
The return of the Frost Moon is the climax of the Nod-Krai Archon Quest, Song of the Welkin Moon (Acts VII and VIII, Version 6.3, January 2026). The Fatui Harbinger Dottore seized the powers of the Three Moons and styled himself a god. Columbina's friends, led by Sandrone's world-formula, reached her across time and pulled the real Frost Moon down through the broken false sky. She returned as the Trilune Goddess and seemingly destroyed him. The Frost Moon now stays in Teyvat's sky.
### STANDARD
The return of the Frost Moon is the climax of Song of the Welkin Moon, the Nod-Krai Archon Quest chapter (Act VIII, "True Moon", Version 6.3). Dottore seized the Moon Marrows, drove Columbina into hiding, built an artificial Marrow and became the "Heretic of the False Moon". Columbina had fled into the moon's reflection, where she met the imprisoned Moon Sisters and took their authorities. To bring her back, Sandrone built a world-formula, Pulonia and the Wanderer's Irminsul core carried the computation, and Arlecchino, Varka and Nicole hauled the Frost Moon through the false sky. Columbina returned as the Trilune Goddess and vaporised Dottore (he was later revealed to have survived). Sandrone died protecting the Traveler. Afterwards the Frost Moon stayed within Teyvat, the Eternal Moon Marrow went to Lauma and the Iridescent to Arlecchino, and the Palestar Edict against Columbina was revoked.

## established
### STANDARD
**Place in the story.** Song of the Welkin Moon Act VIII, "True Moon" (The First Sliver of Moonlight, Descending Moon, Welkin Moon's Homecoming), released with Version 6.3 on 2026-01-14, after Act VII.

**Setup.** On Moon-Prayer Night, as Columbina tried to return to the Frost Moon with the help of the Moon Marrows, Dottore diverted her with a decoy bearing the Frost Moon's signature, held the Marrows and began drawing on the Three Moons' power. Columbina removed herself by falling into the moon's reflection; he built an artificial Marrow and captured the Traveler.

**What happened.**
- In the reflection, where time flows backwards, Columbina met the imprisoned Moon Sisters (Canon, Aria, Sonnet), who passed her their authorities. She "died" there so her moonlit body could be reconstituted in the present.
- Sandrone built a world-formula, run on the Wanderer's Irminsul core hidden in Pulonia, covering about ninety percent of reviving her. The rest required bringing the Frost Moon down.
- Dottore, now the "Heretic of the False Moon", tore the false sky open and isolated the Traveler's companions. Sandrone took a blow meant for the Traveler and died.
- Arlecchino, Varka and Nicole pulled the Frost Moon through the false sky, shattering it. Columbina became the Trilune Goddess, opened her eyes, and with the Traveler destroyed Dottore and his artificial Marrow.

**Aftermath.** The Frost Moon stayed in Teyvat. The Tsaritsa revoked the Palestar Edict. Columbina gave the Eternal Moon Marrow to Lauma and the Iridescent to Arlecchino, and moved to Hiisi Island among the Frostmoon Scions.
### LARGE +
**The reflection.** Columbina meets Rerir and Tholindis before the sisters. The sisters are made mortal by the transfer, so at their request she returns them to the real world first. In the present, Nefer, aided by Lauma, reads Luonnotar's memory of Columbina's past, and the Wanderer offers his core at the risk of being reset or lost. Sandrone drew on research by the Remurians and the Narzissenkreuz Ordo and used Luonnotar as an anchor.

**The reasoning behind the plan.** Dottore's trilune authority came from the Marrows, which Columbina and the Traveler describe as inferior to what she gained directly from the sisters. His artificial Marrow was cracked and shattered by her final beam.

**Details of the siege.** Before the fight the Traveler checks the group's Dodocommunication Devices with Sandrone, who has added a channel to track the formula's progress. The Traveler's companions split to defend Nasha Town. Sandrone and Pulonia attack Dottore in the arena and are beaten. Sandrone's last words to him are defiance ("go to hell"); Dottore then destroys her heart. The formula, which Dottore believes is stalled at ninety percent, keeps advancing in Pulonia, and the Frost Moon appears in the gash he opened.

**Departures after the battle.** Arlecchino takes Sandrone's and Pulonia's cores to the Fontaine Research Institute, following Sandrone's verbal will, and keeps her notes at the House of the Hearth, with one book sent to the Traveler's room on the Flagship. Sandrone had asked that Columbina not be told, which Arlecchino carries out without comment. The Wanderer returns to Sumeru to help Nahida with Dottore's damage. Nicole warns that the disaster has made Teyvat easier for the Abyss to detect. Varka writes to Jean that the Knights' expedition will return to Mondstadt in waves.

**The Frost Moon visit.** Columbina opens a portal from Hiisi Island to the Frost Moon, where the Traveler's ship waits. The flight logs show it passed Teyvat once thousands of years before and returned later, after which the Traveler was forced to crash-land at Nod-Krai. The Traveler tells her Teyvat feels like home, though different from what they knew. Columbina, who says she will return part of the Frost Moon to Nod-Krai as kuuvahki, lifts some of Teyvat's restrictions on the Traveler's use of the elements and gives them a Moon-Prayer Blossom.
### ULTRA +
**Order of participants' roles.** Arlecchino, Varka and Nicole pull down the moon, working with a plan in which Nicole and Alice, her Hexenzirkel colleague, had agreed to help Varka. Flins, Lauma and Nefer are held in extradimensional spaces by Dottore and are freed only when Columbina uses the Iridescent Marrow taken from him. Jahoda minds the Curatorium for Nefer under an earlier agreement. Arlecchino organised the evacuation of civilians from the area so the fight could happen without bystanders.

**The Moon Sisters in the reflection.** The sisters tell Columbina they were imprisoned by their creator Nibelung and explain the Heavenly Principles to her. They say they knew what was coming. They had never heard of kuuvahki, which suggests it did not exist in their time. They warn her that taking their authorities could tear her apart and that the prison would not let her leave alive; she asks whether she could leave while dead. Canon asks why her eyes are always closed. Columbina says she wanted to avoid lies, from the false moon to what others wanted from her, and Canon asks her to open them in the future and see the true smiles of her friends.

**Rerir and Tholindis.** Rerir tells Columbina that Dottore revived him and planned it to coincide with her weakness, and asks her to pass a message to Tholindis. Tholindis, once a Moon Marrow researcher, says she entered the reflection hoping to change her past and was torn apart, leaving only her soul. She forgives Rerir.

**The Frost Moon as a place.** After its return the Frost Moon is a playable region of Nod-Krai, reached through the Moon Gazing quest chain, where Archaeolune energy affects gravity. Its first exploration is in Version 6.7 content.

## accounts
### STANDARD
- **Who pulled the Frost Moon down.** The quest shows Arlecchino, Varka and Nicole doing the physical pulling. The computation that made it possible is credited to Sandrone's formula, run on the Wanderer's Irminsul core hidden inside Pulonia. Treat it as a joint effort.
- **Why Sandrone died.** Characters present attribute it to her choosing to take a blow meant for the Traveler. Arlecchino later explains that this was also a deliberate deception: the formula was in Pulonia and a false version in Sandrone's own body. The Traveler's statement to Dottore that Sandrone was part of the team is the party's view; Dottore's view was that her inability to trust caused her fall.
- **Why the Heavenly Principles put the ship on the Frost Moon.** Columbina offers two possible reasons and does not claim to know. See uncertainties.
### LARGE +
- **Dottore's account.** He frames his plan as becoming Teyvat's new "creator-god", and calls Columbina's rescue futile. He is an antagonist and an unreliable narrator on motives.
- **Rerir's account of the Abyss in the reflection.** He says some Abyssal energy was already there before he arrived and that rejecting it silenced the voices in his head. Columbina notes he only partly denies responsibility.

## connections
### STANDARD
This event ends Dottore's hold over Nod-Krai and leads to the revocation of the Palestar Edict, restores the Frost Moon that the Heavenly Principles flung beyond the false sky during the war of funerary flame, and ends the shadowmoon's purpose. It leads into Act IX, As All Falls to Emptiness, where Dottore's story continues. See Columbina, Sandrone, Dottore, the Three Moons, Moon Marrow, the false sky and Nod-Krai.
### LARGE +
Later, in Version 6.6, Act X reveals that Dottore survived: before dying he had one half of his soul recorded by the Irminsul and uploaded another half as data, and he returned as the Exalted Master of the Heretical Path. @version tags apply to the sub-entries below.

## entry: Moon Marrows after the battle
@keys: Eternal Moon Marrow, Iridescent Moon Marrow, Moon Marrow, Marrows, artificial Moon Marrow
@keys2: Lauma, Arlecchino, Columbina, Dottore
@version: 6.3
@availability: permanent
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
After the fight Columbina gave the Eternal Moon Marrow (from Aria) to Lauma of the Frostmoon Scions, and the Iridescent Moon Marrow (from Sonnet, once corrupted by Abyssal energy and purified earlier by the Traveler) to Arlecchino. Dottore's artificial Marrow was destroyed. A Marrow forms only after a Moon Goddess has died; Columbina is the living Frost Moon goddess and has none of her own. Each Marrow is tied to its goddess: one goddess cannot use another's power through a Marrow or hold two Marrows at once, though a Moon Goddess can draw on one to restore some of her own power.
### LARGE +
Before the battle, the Traveler and Arlecchino had used the Marrows to guide Columbina's return to the Frost Moon, which Dottore subverted; during the battle Columbina and the Traveler wrested both Marrows from him. Columbina has implied a Marrow is a symbol of godhood comparable to a Gnosis. Lauma restored the Eternal Moon Marrow to its place in the Frostmoon Scions' secret chamber.

## entry: The Frost Moon after its return
@keys: Frost Moon, Moon Gazing
@keys2: Columbina, spaceship, false sky, kuuvahki
@version: 6.3
@availability: permanent
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
After Dottore's defeat the Frost Moon remained within Teyvat's sky rather than returning beyond the false sky. Columbina can move between Teyvat and the Frost Moon by portal and has said she will give part of it back to Nod-Krai as kuuvahki. The Traveler's spaceship is there. Columbina says the Heavenly Principles put it there.
### LARGE +
The Frost Moon later became an explorable region, entered by the Moon Gazing quest chain (Version 6.7). There, Archaeolune energy, a form of energy very like kuuvahki, lowers effective gravity.

## entry: Sandrone's sacrifice and return
@keys: Sandrone death, Sandrone's sacrifice, world-formula, Pulonia formula
@keys2: Dottore, Columbina, Traveler
@version: 6.3
@availability: permanent
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Sandrone built the world-formula with the Wanderer's Irminsul core and was killed by Dottore while shielding the Traveler. Her last words to him were defiance. The true formula was in Pulonia and the version in her body was a decoy. Columbina mourned her alone, and Sandrone had asked that Columbina not be told of her arrangements. Her cores and Pulonia's went to the Fontaine Research Institute.
### LARGE +
In later content Sandrone appears alive again and reunites with Columbina. This is covered in the Sunny Summer Fontinalia story quest, introduced with the limited-time event of the same name (Version 6.7), and in Sandrone's own entry.

### STANDARD
In the Sunny Summer Fontinalia story quest (introduced with the Version 6.7 limited-time event of the same name; the quest stays available afterwards), Columbina goes to Fontaine looking for Sandrone during the Fontinalia Film Festival, where she is cast as lead in the film "A Homecoming". Sandrone, alive again after her death, had been avoiding her; once Columbina finds her, Sandrone hugs her. The two visit Alain's grave with the Traveler and attend the party on the Wingalet.

## entry: Dottore's survival after the battle
@keys: Dottore survived, Exalted Master of the Heretical Path, Heretic of the False Moon
@keys2: Irminsul, Nahida, Sumeru
@version: 6.6
@availability: permanent

### STANDARD
Dottore was widely believed dead after the Nod-Krai battle. Act X of Song of the Welkin Moon reveals he had planned his death: one half of his soul was recorded by the Irminsul and the other half uploaded as data, and he returned as the Exalted Master of the Heretical Path. He is finally killed when Nahida burns the Irminsul. Characters in Nod-Krai before this revelation believe he is dead.

## entry: Aftermath in Nod-Krai

@keys2: Tarno, Illuga, Flins, Nasha Town
@version: 6.3
@availability: permanent

### STANDARD
The Tsaritsa revoked the Palestar Edict, Columbina's arrest warrant, once Dottore's intentions were known to her. A new League was formed jointly by Nod-Krai's factions. Flins urged Illuga to represent the Lightkeepers in it, and Tarno addressed the public in Nasha Town. Aino and Ineffa helped distribute food on behalf of the Frostmoon Scions. Columbina took Lauma's suggestion to live on Hiisi Island, and hopes to attend the next Moon-Prayer Night openly as herself.
