## summary
@version: 6.6
@spoiler: major
### LITE
The destruction of Irminsul is the climax of the Sumeru part of Song of the Welkin Moon (Archon Quest Acts IX and X, Version 6.6). After his body died in Nod-Krai, Dottore reached Irminsul through the Ley Lines and took it over from inside, putting Nahida into a coma and making a giant Irminsul tree rise in Sumeru City. Sumeru moved the world's information into Aaru. Nahida then invoked her authority as Irminsul's keeper and burned the tree with the Pyro Gnosis, which Mavuika had given her. The Shades Ronova, Istaroth and Naberius consented. Dottore died in the fire, and as of 7.1 Aaru does Irminsul's work.
### STANDARD +
The fire burned for seven days and seven nights, and only the underground parts of the tree remained. Nahida says Teyvat's most vital information was largely preserved. Her own link was moved from Irminsul to Aaru by Nicole, so she was not harmed. The Pyro Gnosis vanished in the fire. Nahida says that with Irminsul burned, the flow of fate now "carries many more possibilities."

## established
@version: 6.6
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
- Irminsul is older than the Heavenly Principles. In the age of dragons it had a different role. After the Heavenly Principles came, they remade it into a device that records and keeps all information. Istaroth calls it "the original tree of Teyvat" and the means by which the Heavenly Principles maintain fate.
- Nahida calls Irminsul both a national treasure and a major threat to Sumeru. As Dendro Archon she is bound to protect it.
- Under a rule set by the Heavenly Principles, when Irminsul is in grave danger part of its power seals Sumeru off, so that the rest of the world has time even if Sumeru falls. Earlier pollution (the forbidden knowledge) never triggered it; Dottore's direct invasion did.
### ULTRA +
- The Traveler has no records in Irminsul, so the tree cannot hinder them. They walked through the barrier, and their meeting with Mavuika could not be recorded. Mavuika's records go to the Night Kingdom rather than the Ley Lines while she is in Natlan.
- Apep says Irminsul was among the things the Heavenly Principles usurped from ancient dragonkind and repurposed.

## causes
@version: 6.6
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
Dottore's plan was to split his soul in two. One half would reach the Ley Lines through death and the other would be fed in as pure data, and they would meet inside Irminsul and make him whole again. His body was killed in Nod-Krai, by Columbina according to the wiki. Pantalone, bound by contract, then placed Dottore's "final data backup" into the Ley Lines; it had been kept in the Northland Bank's most secure vault. Nahida had guarded every pathway by which an outsider could transmit themselves into Irminsul, but not the one route that cannot be guarded: death. Closing it would have meant stopping the souls of the dead from returning to the Ley Lines.
### ULTRA +
Inside the tree, Dottore restricted Nahida's authority and the tree began refusing all connections. He told Pantalone that he meant to "create a new world inside the existing one" and to experiment freely in the afterlife. Dottore said both of them despise the world's existing laws; Pantalone asked whether it was really so greedy to want the world to run by new rules, and said he backed Dottore as a venture capitalist. Fatui agents entered Sumeru beforehand; Cyno noticed a suspicious number of "merchant caravans" coming in through Bayda Harbor.
The Anomalous Tree Marrow, an item said to be resin from Irminsul's incinerated trunk, holds Dottore's digitized memories of the plan: he first experimented on animals and other people's souls, settled on digitization as the transfer method, and imagined an "immortal record" as something more eternal than eternal life.

## course
@version: 6.6
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
- Warning: Collei dreamed that everyone in Sumeru City had swapped identities and only she remembered the truth. Nicole spoke into her mind and called it a premonition. Nahida and the new Sages evacuated the rainforest to Caravan Ribat.
- Collapse: Irminsul stopped recording and processing information. Nahida took over the work herself until she fell into a coma. She was moved away to weaken her link, and Apep sheltered her at the Heart of Oasis and shared Dendro energy with her.
- Spreading madness: civilians who tried to return near the Yasna Monument fell into psychosis and killed one another, and abnormal Ley Line Blossoms appeared. The Jinn rose around Gurabad until Liloupar calmed them, and a barrier sealed Sumeru off.
- Aaru: the Seven Pillars agreed to open Aaru after Nefer answered Deshret's three questions ("time"), freeing Thoth, Sobek and Heryshaf. The Akademiya's scholars, led by Alhaitham and Kaveh, rebuilt Aaru and merged it with the Akasha.
- The tree: a colossal silver Irminsul tree burst up in Sumeru City. Nahida called it "a tree of unbridled greed" and took it as proof that Dottore was behind the crisis.
### LARGE +
- The Pyro Gnosis: Nahida, speaking through the Traveler at the sealed Natlan border, asked Mavuika to give the Pyro Gnosis to Sumeru outright. Mavuika agreed. The Traveler walked through the barrier, absorbed the Gnosis into their body through their mastery of Pyro, and carried it back in.
- The overload: Aaru-linked Akasha terminals were handed out to everyone in Sumeru. Together their connections overloaded Irminsul and shut it down, which stopped the tree's growth and hid the wearers from Dottore's tracking.
- The assault: Cyno, Dehya, Candace, Jeht and others drew the Fatui off at the city gates, while Tighnari, Wanderer, Paimon and the Traveler climbed to the Sanctuary of Surasthana. Wanderer and the Traveler entered Irminsul's depths, and Nicole guided them from the Eternal Oasis until Dottore cut her off.
- Inside: Dottore revealed that he had worked out Wanderer's erased identity as Scaramouche. The Traveler attached Nahida's device to the Ley Lines, and Aaru began pulling the world's information out of Irminsul, draining Dottore's power. Nahida joined the fight from the Eternal Oasis.

## the_burning
@version: 6.6
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Dottore broke the ground under them and the three fell into Irminsul's depths. Nahida drew the Pyro Gnosis out of the Traveler and declared, "I invoke my supreme authority as the keeper of Irminsul," asking the Shades to commend or condemn her. Ronova, the Ruler of Death, asked whether she would destroy what was both her roots and her finest achievement. Nahida answered that Irminsul was corrupted beyond redemption and that she honoured it by fighting for the humanity it exists to protect. Istaroth reminded her that the Archons owe their authority to the Heavenly Principles. Naberius gave the verdict: since Nahida had become "a great tree" herself, the loss of another tree was nothing to fear. The Pyro Gnosis burst into flame and set Irminsul's roots alight.
### LARGE +
Ronova addressed Nahida by her title Mahakusaladhamma, and Naberius parted from her as "Lord of Dendro." As the fire spread, Mavuika's voice spoke of "the first flame". Nahida carried the Traveler and Wanderer upward. Dottore chained the Traveler to drag them back, but Wanderer broke the chain, and Dottore himself was pulled down into the flames by shadowy figures: the Segments he had deleted years before.

## dottores_end
@version: 6.6
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
Bound to the burning tree, Dottore faded away. Before he died he spoke with Pantalone, who watched the fire from outside, and called him by his real name, Feofan. He warned Pantalone that his elixir of immortality would run out within a few years with no one left to make it. To Nahida he admitted that setting fire to the thing her own survival depended on was brave, and that her earlier threat had been empty but this one was real. Nahida told him the "experiment" was never his and parted with "We will never meet again."
### ULTRA +
Dottore called the outcome "certainly not the worst" and said he had concluded his "experiment in blasphemy." Pantalone told him he was "a truly rare talent." Pantalone was not caught and remains alive as of 7.1.

## aftermath
@version: 6.6
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
- Aaru, reconfigured, now collects and stores the information the Ley Lines carry. Nahida asks the Traveler to think of it as "the new Irminsul", and her existence is bound to it.
- Sumeru's people went home and rebuilt. Thoth and Heryshaf chose to stay at the Temple of Silence.
- Snezhnaya sent a letter through Pulcinella, the Rooster: Dottore was a rogue element already under internal censure after Nod-Krai, Snezhnaya had lost a Harbinger to him, his subordinates would face Snezhnayan law, and relief funds were on the way. Naphis called it diplomatically watertight. Nahida said Snezhnaya must keep a tight rein on its actions.
### ULTRA +
Nahida and the Traveler went to Natlan to thank Mavuika and tell her the Pyro Gnosis had vanished. Mavuika had already sensed its loss. She said this was always meant as giving, not lending. She wondered whether its disappearance was a knock-on effect on "a certain strand of fate," given how large the fallout of Irminsul's loss might be.

## pyro_gnosis_status
@version: 7.1
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
In Light and Shadow, Intertwined (Archon Quest Chapter VII, 7.1), Anastasya asks the Traveler where the Pyro Gnosis is, since it is the only Gnosis whose whereabouts are unknown. The Traveler says it burned to ashes with Irminsul and that, according to Mavuika, it has returned to the Ley Lines. Anastasya answers that Gnoses come from the Third Descender's remains and cannot coexist with the Ley Lines for long, so it will one day be excised. This is not the same as what Nahida and Mavuika said in 6.6 (no trace, not even ash).

## accounts
### LARGE
- Nahida: Irminsul exists to protect humanity, so humanity, and she as one of its protectors, must come first. Afterwards she says fate now carries many more possibilities and that she will watch carefully.
- The Shades: Ronova questioned the choice, Istaroth spoke for the tree's role in fate, and Naberius approved.
- Dottore: he called his work an experiment in blasphemy and called the result not the worst. He told Nahida that he had "unwittingly" been her arch-enemy all along.
- Pantalone: he never thought Dottore could win, but honoured the contract as "a reputable partner."
- The Traveler (7.1) and Mavuika give different accounts of the Pyro Gnosis's end; see above.
### ULTRA +
The Binding Field of Universal Nirvana domain text describes Irminsul as the tree "that once sustained memory", now turned to ash, and calls Dottore a "heretic" who claimed enlightenment after seizing the axis of the world's memories.

## open_questions
### LARGE
Canon leaves open where the Pyro Gnosis is, how far the Heavenly Principles' grip on fate has weakened, whether the silver-white trees and domains elsewhere in Teyvat changed, and what the Heavenly Principles make of Irminsul's loss. Do not resolve these.

## connections
### LARGE
Irminsul and the Ley Lines, Aaru, Nahida, Dottore and Pantalone, the Pyro Gnosis and Mavuika, the Four Shades, fate and the Heavenly Principles. Also the Akasha (repurposed for the overload), the Seven Pillars and Nabu Malikata's legacy, Nicole, the Wanderer (who fought inside the tree), the Nod-Krai chapter (where Dottore's body died), and the Tsaritsa's search for the Gnoses (7.1).

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay interpretation (this project's recommendation, not canon text): set after 6.6, Sumeru is rebuilding from a crisis everyone in the nation lived through: evacuation, the sealed border, the madness near the city, the giant tree and its burning. Most Sumerians know Irminsul burned and that their Archon is recovering. The details (Dottore's soul, the Shades, the Pyro Gnosis) are known only to those who were inside or in the leadership.
### LARGE +
Outside Sumeru, most people would know only that the border was sealed and that some catastrophe was stopped. Aaru now holds the world's memory, so Nahida still has her link to it; do not portray her as cut off or weakened by the loss. Dottore is dead for good as of 7.1; Pantalone survives.

## entry: Dottore inside Irminsul
@keys: Dottore, Il Dottore, The Doctor, Zandik
@keys2: Irminsul, Aaru, Ley Lines, Nahida, burned
@version: 6.6
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
After his body died in Nod-Krai, Dottore entered Irminsul through death. Pantalone placed his final data backup into the Ley Lines, and the two halves of him merged inside the tree. From there he restricted Nahida's authority, watched the world through Irminsul, and grew a giant Irminsul tree in Sumeru City. He died when Nahida burned Irminsul with the Pyro Gnosis in 6.6, pulled into the fire by his own deleted Segments.

## entry: Akasha overload
@keys: Akasha overload, Aaru Renovation Project
@keys2: Irminsul, Akasha, Aaru, terminal, terminals
@version: 6.6
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
To stop the corrupted Irminsul tree, Sumeru's scholars merged the reopened Aaru with the Akasha System, built new terminals and gave them to everyone they could reach. All those connections together overloaded Irminsul and shut it down, stopping the tree's growth. Thoth said he never thought he would see Irminsul shut down. The terminals also kept their wearers from being tracked accurately by Irminsul, so Dottore could not see them clearly. (Inside the tree, Dottore later claimed he had only played along with their belief that he could not track them.)

## entry: Nahida's verdict before the Shades
@keys: keeper of Irminsul, Mahakusaladhamma
@keys2: Ronova, Istaroth, Naberius, Shades, Pyro Gnosis
@version: 6.6
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
To burn Irminsul, Nahida invoked her "supreme authority as the keeper of Irminsul" and stood before the symbols of Ronova (Ruler of Death), Istaroth (Ruler of Time) and Naberius (Ruler of Life). Ronova asked if she would abandon what she had always fought for. Istaroth stressed that the tree maintains fate and that the Archons' authority comes from the Heavenly Principles. Nahida said that wisdom means weighing the options, and that if they cared for the world's fortunes they should preserve her. Naberius agreed and said goodbye: "Till we meet again, Lord of Dendro."

## entry: Pyro Gnosis after the burning
@keys: Pyro Gnosis
@keys2: Irminsul, burned, ashes, missing, vanished, whereabouts
@version: 7.1
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
Mavuika gave the Pyro Gnosis to Sumeru in 6.6, and Nahida used it to burn Irminsul. Afterwards it could not be found: Nahida found no trace of it, not even ash, and Mavuika only sensed its loss. In 7.1 the Traveler told Anastasya it had burned to ashes and that, according to Mavuika, it had returned to the Ley Lines. Anastasya expects that it will one day be excised from them. It is the only Gnosis whose whereabouts are unknown.

## entry: Irminsul barrier
@keys: Irminsul barrier
@keys2: Sumeru, sealed, border, isolate
@version: 6.6
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
When Dottore's invasion put Irminsul in grave danger, a rule laid down by the Heavenly Principles sealed Sumeru inside a barrier so that the rest of the world could survive even if Sumeru fell. Nicole and Nefer got in just before it closed. Only the Traveler, who has no records in Irminsul, could walk through it, which is how the Pyro Gnosis was brought in from Natlan.

## entry: Anomalous Tree Marrow
@keys: Anomalous Tree Marrow, Binding Field of Universal Nirvana
@version: 6.6
@spoiler: major
### ULTRA
Anomalous Tree Marrow is a strange resin thought to have oozed from Irminsul's incinerated trunk, with a baleful light inside. Touching it floods the mind with Dottore's digitized memories: Harbinger meetings, his Segments, and the steps of his soul-transfer experiment. It is gained from the Binding Field of Universal Nirvana, a domain where Dottore is fought "one last time".
