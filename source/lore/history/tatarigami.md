## summary
### LITE
The Tatarigami ("curse god") is the curse left behind by the serpent god Orobashi, slain by Ei on Yashiori Island near the end of the Archon War. Suppressed for ages by wards called Narukami Pillars, it broke loose shortly before the Traveler's arrival, when the wards on the island were destroyed. It brings perpetual thunderstorms, withered crops, and untreatable sickness and madness. The Traveler helps repair the wards in Orobashi's Legacy.
### STANDARD +
It is the reason Yashiori Island is in chaos in Chapter II and a stalemate between the Shogun's Army and the Watatsumi Army. It also shows up in the Mikage Furnace at Tatarasuna and in the sword Kagotsurube Isshin.

## established
### LITE
- The Tatarigami formed from Orobashi's will and power after its divine body was shattered. The item text for the Golden Branch of a Distant Sea says the god's ichor turned into plasma and its will became a curse that could never be extinguished.
- When the wards suppressing it are disrupted, it causes sickness and calamity around the island.
- It appears as a black aura on some enemies and as a persistent storm over Yashiori Island.
### STANDARD +
- Affected areas have unending thunderstorms, and crops grown in tainted soil wither.
- In people it causes illness that cannot be treated: bleeding, fevers, migraines, rashes, dizziness and weakness. Mental effects include voices and madness; many afflicted talk about a terrifying god called 'Him'. Washizu, Higi Village's chief, describes it as having eighteen pairs of red eyes. Vision bearers and others sensitive to elements are less affected.
- After Orobashi's death, the Raiden Shogun placed wards, the Narukami Pillars, to subdue it. The Yashiori Guardians, led by Kitain Bunsou and the Kitain Clan, took up the work of suppressing it.
- Before the Traveler's arrival the wards were destroyed, the Tatarigami returned, and the conflict between the Shogun's Army and the Watatsumi Army stalled. Higi Village was almost abandoned: only Yasumoto and Chouji remained unharmed, and the chief had gone mad.
### LARGE +
- The Kitain bloodline had died out by then (per Orobashi's Legacy Part V), and Kujou Takayuki ignored Kujou Masahito's reports about how bad the situation was. A Kujou expedition sent to investigate was lost, its Lost Texts at Fort Fujitou, Musoujin Gorge, Serpent's Head, Higi Village and Fort Mumei describing its members slowly succumbing.
- In Orobashi's Legacy, Kaji, a resident, and the Traveler repair the Narukami Pillars at Fort Fujitou, Serpent's Head, Jakotsu Mine and Fort Mumei. Each repair clears the Tatarigami in its region; the last, at Fort Mumei, ends the island's constant storm.
- In recent years the Tenryou Commission had the Mikage Furnace built at Tatarasuna using Fontaine technology that harnesses Tatarigami energy to smelt Jade Steel. The Grand Narukami Shrine's priestesses objected that the Tatarigami is too dangerous. In Tatara Tales, the Traveler helps Xavier repair the furnace's damaged core and fend off Fatui who want to use it.
### ULTRA +
- Objects can carry it. Akame Kanenaga used the Tatarigami to put his consciousness into Kagotsurube Isshin, a blade he made in exile in Snezhnaya, wishing to prove the Isshin Art still deserved the Shogun's trust. The blade's hatred twisted that wish into rivalling the gods and it possessed many people on its way back to Inazuma (Ere the End, a Glance Back).
- Chouji's mother stayed clear-minded despite falling ill; Yasumoto believed the Tatarigami's energy flowed freely within her instead of corrupting her.
- Real-world note (wiki trivia): in Shinto, tatarigami are gods or spirits that bring calamity unless appeased. This is an etymology, not an in-universe fact.

## accounts
### STANDARD
- **Orobashi's end.** According to the Orobashi sources, it invaded Yashiori Island out of the desperation of its starving followers and was slain by Ei with the Musou no Hitotachi; the blow opened Musoujin Gorge. The curse followed from its death.
- **How the wards fell.** A note found on the island shows a radical group within the Watatsumi Army, without Kokomi's knowledge, disguised as Shogunate troops and broke the wards, hoping to restore Orobashi's dignity. They instead set loose the Tatarigami.
### LARGE +
- **Fatui involvement.** The wiki states that a Fatui mole named Nathan, acting on the First Lord Harbinger's orders to prolong the war, pushed the Orobashi fanatics. This rests on a few quest texts; see `uncertainties`.
- **The Narukami Shrine's view.** Its priestesses hold the Tatarigami to be too dangerous to use; the Tenryou Commission treated it as an energy source.
- **Akame's view.** An item text for the blade quotes a view that the Tatarigami is pure malice against mortal desire.

## open_questions
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
Canon does not explain what the Tatarigami is beyond Orobashi's lingering will and power, who 'Him' is, or why some people resist it. It does not link the curse to any other phenomenon. See `uncertainties`. No sources read show the Tatarigami returning in 6.x or 7.0-7.1.

## connections
### STANDARD
- **Orobashi**: the god whose remains and will it is.
- **Yashiori Island**: where its effects are felt, including Higi Village and Fort Mumei.
- **Sakoku Decree and Vision Hunt Decree**: the tension from the decrees fed the war that led to the wards being broken.
- **Narukami Pillars**: the wards that hold it back.
### LARGE +
- **Mikage Furnace and Tatarasuna**: the site where its energy was put to industrial use.
- **Kagotsurube Isshin and Akame Kanenaga**: the cursed blade.

## timeline
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
- Near the end of the Archon War: Orobashi is slain on Yashiori Island; the Tatarigami arises. Ei places the Narukami Pillars; the Kitain Clan guard the island.
- Later: the Mikage Furnace is built at Tatarasuna using Fontaine technology.
- Shortly before 2.0: the wards are broken, the Tatarigami returns, and Higi Village is abandoned.
- 2.0: Orobashi's Legacy (wards repaired) and Tatara Tales (furnace saved).
- 2.8: the sword Kagotsurube Isshin is released with its Tatarigami-tainted backstory.

## entry: Narukami Pillar
@keys: Narukami Pillar, Narukami Pillars, warding stone
@version: 2.0
### STANDARD
Stone lantern wards on Yashiori Island created to suppress the Tatarigami. They crackle with black and purple energy when broken. Parts I to V of Orobashi's Legacy repair the pillars at Fort Fujitou, Serpent's Head, Jakotsu Mine and Fort Mumei.

## entry: Higi Village
@keys: Higi Village, Washizu
@version: 2.0
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
The village on Yashiori Island devastated by the Tatarigami. Most inhabitants fled to neighbouring islands or died of illness or madness; Yasumoto and Chouji remain, and the chief Washizu has lost his mind.

## entry: Mikage Furnace
@keys: Mikage Furnace, Tatarasuna furnace
@version: 2.0
### LARGE
Built at Tatarasuna by the Tenryou Commission with Fontaine technology that uses Tatarigami energy to smelt Jade Steel. Its core was close to meltdown before the Traveler helped repair it in Tatara Tales; the Grand Narukami Shrine opposed its use.

## entry: Kagotsurube Isshin
@keys: Kagotsurube Isshin, Akame Kanenaga
@version: 2.8
@spoiler: minor
### LARGE
A blade forged in Snezhnaya by the exiled swordsmith Akame Kanenaga, who failed to become head blacksmith. His wish was imbued into the sword by the Tatarigami, which twisted it into a hatred that possessed people on its journey back to Inazuma.
