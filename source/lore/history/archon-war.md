## summary
### LITE
The Archon War (Chinese and Japanese: War of the Demon Gods) was the long age of fighting between gods that followed the war of funerary flame about 6,000 years ago and ended about 2,000 years ago. The Heavenly Principles set up seven divine thrones in Celestia, one per element, and let the gods fight over them and their Gnoses. The victors became The Seven, the Archons who founded and shaped the seven nations. Of the original victors, only Barbatos (Venti) and Morax (Zhongli) remain as of 7.1; the other five seats have changed hands.
### STANDARD +
The war was not one conflict but many regional struggles that ended at different times. Mondstadt's was settled 2,600 years ago when Decarabian fell; Morax was already fighting for Liyue before 3,700 years ago; the era as a whole closed about 2,000 years ago, when the last seat was claimed. Natlan was never part of it. Most defeated gods who survived fled beyond Teyvat into the Dark Sea.

## established
### STANDARD
- After the war of funerary flame, the Heavenly Principles (the "usurper" of Neuvillette's Vision story) were too badly wounded to suppress the world's original order directly. They dismantled the unified civilization, banned its history and decided on a "new order".
- The angels who had guided humanity were cursed and cast out of Celestia, and a new class of gods emerged to take that role.
- Seven divine thrones were set up in Celestia, each holding part of an elemental Authority taken from the Seven Sovereigns. Gods fought for them and the matching Gnoses.
- Ningguang tells the Traveler that many gods walked the earth and fought long wars that did not abate until 2,000 years ago, with much blood shed.
- Zhongli's character story: Rex Lapis remembers the moment the final Archon took their seat, ending the Archon War and the era of warring gods.
### LARGE +
- Neuvillette's Vision story describes the result bluntly: humans were left with only "seven remembrances," and all fragments of the primordial were driven to devour each other.
- Nicole's character story (from the original Chinese, "demon gods") says that once mankind lost the angels, the land fell to strange beings who gathered followers with a wave of the hand and a trace of a miracle.
- The war also unleashed monsters: Zhongli's own stories say every corner of Teyvat burned and "wicked things" sought to expand their domains alongside the gods.
- After the war, the victors founded the seven nations, setting their governance by their ideals and reshaping the land with their elemental power.

## theatres
### STANDARD
- Mondstadt: Barbatos and the rebels overthrew Decarabian, God of Storms, 2,600 years ago; Boreas declined the throne.
- Liyue: Morax, after Guizhong's death, founded Liyue Harbor and sealed many gods (Osial, Chi, the mistress of Chenyu Vale); Havria and Marchosius fell or gave up their godhood.
- Inazuma: Makoto and Ei united the islands; Orobashi fled into Enkanomiya.
- Sumeru: King Deshret, Rukkhadevata and Nabu Malikata ruled together until Deshret's pursuit of forbidden knowledge; Rukkhadevata is presumed to have ascended afterwards.
- Fontaine: Remus' Remuria rose and sank; Celestia then made Egeria the Hydro Archon.
- Natlan: never part of the war; Xbalanque defeated the Pyro Sovereign and became the first Pyro Archon.
- Snezhnaya: Monomakh united the fae clans and became the Belyi Tsar.

## mondstadt
### LARGE
About 2,600 years ago, Old Mondstadt stood where Stormterror's Lair is now. Decarabian, God of Storms, walled it in a storm barrier against Boreas, the King of the North Wind, whose blizzards made the land almost uninhabitable. Neither could defeat the other. Barbatos was then only a wisp of the thousand winds. He befriended a young bard who longed to see birds, and with the bard, Decarabian's estranged lover Amos, a red-haired warrior and Gunnhildr, he led a rebellion that killed Decarabian. Amos and the bard died. Barbatos took a seat in Celestia as the Anemo Archon, took the bard's form, reshaped the land and left the people to rule themselves.
### ULTRA +
Boreas judged himself unfit to be the Anemo Archon, since he had little love for humanity, and withdrew to Nod-Krai. About a thousand years later he let his body die, turned his power into a blessing on Mondstadt's Ley Lines and remained as a spirit, the Wolf of the North among the Four Winds. Boreas' godhood came from an unwitting blessing by Koitar in Nod-Krai (revealed in 6.x content).

## liyue
### LARGE
Morax, Guizhong and Marchosius ruled the Guili Assembly together with the adepti. Guizhong, God of Dust, died in a battle that also flooded the Guili Plains, and Morax led the people south to found Liyue Harbor. He went on to defeat and seal many gods and sea monsters: Osial and others under the stone lances of Guyun Stone Forest, the beast-god Chi at Qingce, the mistress of Chenyu Vale, and the serpent Orobashi, who fled to Inazuma.
### ULTRA +
- Marchosius, God of the Stove, gave up his power and godhood to stop the war's calamities and plagues from destroying Liyue. Morax set the Yakshas to destroy the demons the war's dead gods left behind; the karmic debt killed or scattered nearly all of them, and Xiao is the last active Yaksha.
- Havria, the gentle Salt Goddess, kept giving ground until only Sal Terrae was left; her own king killed her, and the power released turned those nearby to salt. Survivors later founded Yinyuan Hall with a distorted account of events.
- In Chenyu Vale, the mistress' adepti Fujin and the Herblord turned against her, with the suanni Lingyuan, to save the vale's people when she tried to flood the Bishui River; she was sealed.

## inazuma
### LARGE
Makoto and Ei fought through the war together, conquering and uniting Inazuma's youkai; the youkai built Inazuma City on Narukami Island. Ei defeated the region's other gods and unified the archipelago under the Inazuma Shogunate. Ei was the stronger twin but judged Makoto fitter to rule, gave up her own body so Makoto could ascend, and was restored by Makoto to serve as her kagemusha. Orobashi, realising he could best neither Morax nor "Narukami", fled into Enkanomiya, where he became the people's god.
### ULTRA +
About 2,000 years ago Celestia sentenced Orobashi to death for reading Before Sun and Moon, a history of the age before the war of funerary flame. He prepared his people to return to the surface and raised Watatsumi Island from coral. After a period of peace, famine drove Watatsumi to invade Yashiori Island; Ei killed Orobashi and Akuou with one Musou no Hitotachi strike, splitting the island, after Akuou had killed her friend Sasayuri. Orobashi's resentment became the Tatarigami. This conflict came after Makoto's ascension and is told as an aftermath of the war.

## sumeru
### LARGE
Three God-Kings, King Deshret, Greater Lord Rukkhadevata and Nabu Malikata, shared rule of Ay-Khanoum peacefully. Deshret defeated other gods such as Heryshaf, Sobek and Thoth, who entered his service among the Seven Pillars; Remus refused and left. Deshret declined a "gift" from Celestia to pursue his ambition to rebel against it. Rukkhadevata left the desert and created the rainforest.
### ULTRA +
Nabu Malikata died guiding Deshret toward forbidden knowledge, and its corruption later brought Eleazar and the Withering. Rukkhadevata spent much of her power to contain it, shrinking to a child-like form, and Deshret sacrificed himself to end the disaster. Rukkhadevata is presumed to have then become the Dendro Archon. A minor god, the fungal Bifrons, fed starving people with clones of itself during the war and was later buried by an eruption in Natlan; when it woke in the present, it still thought the war was going on.

## fontaine
### LARGE
Fontaine had no native god at the start. Remus, after his defeat by Deshret, founded the empire of Remuria on the sea with the aid of the angel Sybilla and expanded it by diplomacy and conquest. In the north, Erinnyes united the warrior tribes of Aremorica in Egeria's name against Remuria. Remuria fell after Remus' death and a great flood. Celestia then ordered Egeria to succeed him; she gathered the survivors, founded a law-governed civilisation around Fontaine's springs, received a Gnosis and became the Hydro Archon.

## natlan
### LARGE
Natlan was never part of the Archon War: in-fighting among the dragons left no room for gods there (Acatl, Field Fungal Traces). Xbalanque defeated the Pyro Sovereign Xiuhcoatl, became the first Pyro Archon and founded Natlan's system, borrowing the power of Death from Ronova after ascending.

## snezhnaya
@version: 7.1
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
Fae clans held small principalities in the north. Monomakh Snezhnyi, a wandering hero, defeated or befriended fae princes such as Filipov and Vaselisa, founded Kitezh, and after the Volkodlak lord Miscislav and his allies were killed attacking it, the fae swore allegiance to him and he declared himself the Belyi Tsar of the Pale Star Court.
### ULTRA +
In his reign, by the 7.1 account, Saarelainen and Anastasya opened a passage into the Shadow Realm. The Heavenly Principles erased Anastasya; Saarelainen ascended to Celestia and let his body be split into the seven Gnoses, used to consolidate the Archon system, in exchange for her resurrection and a promise never to harm their descendants, the Khaenri'ahns, sealed with a curse that would put the Heavenly Principles to sleep. Anastasya later became the Tsaritsa.

## aftermath
### STANDARD
The war closed about 2,000 years ago with the last seat claimed. Most defeated gods who survived refused the new order and fled to remote islands in the Dark Sea, where they became "evil gods"; Bit of Aerosiderite notes their power came from the same source as Rex Lapis'. Some gods instead cooperated with The Seven, such as Boreas (as one of the Four Winds) and Marchosius. The original Seven used to drink together in Liyue.
### LARGE +
Five of the first Seven seats have since changed hands, four of them in the Cataclysm 500 years ago: Makoto, Rukkhadevata, Egeria and the Belyi Tsar died and were succeeded by Ei, Nahida, Focalors and Anastasya. The Pyro seat passes between mortal Archons, most recently Mavuika. Since 3.2 the world believes Nahida has been Dendro Archon since the war's end; Zhongli's revised story says only two of the first Seven remain "in positions of rulership".

## accounts
### LARGE
- Zhongli: the first Seven were a diverse lot but all shouldered the burden of guiding humanity; "the seven seats changed and again were changed," and his story laments that the new Archons would not honour that duty as the first did.
- Venti: his stories tell Mondstadt's war as a liberation from a god who mistook fear for devotion, and he insists the Anemo Archon cannot take credit for everything in Mondstadt.
- Ningguang: many gods once walked the earth and fought long, bloody wars that did not abate until 2,000 years ago.
- Bifrons (Field Fungal Traces, set in Natlan): an unreliable, self-glorifying god who sees the war as still under way; Paimon dismisses it as "old news."
### ULTRA +
- Neuvillette, after regaining his power: the war was the means by which the wounded usurper kept the world's resentments subdued.
- Nicole: people always want gods to hear prayers and bear the world's weight, which is why the demon gods so easily gathered followers after the angels were gone.

## open_questions
### LARGE
Canon leaves open how many gods fought, the war's exact start, how the Gnoses' creation fits its timeline, who the many defeated gods in the Dark Sea are, and when Rukkhadevata ascended. Do not resolve these in roleplay.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay interpretation (this project's recommendation, not canon text): to people in the present the Archon War is ancient history and legend, known through local tales, operas and ruins rather than memory. Only long-lived beings remember it: Zhongli, Venti, the adepti, Ei, Boreas and certain dragons. Its scars are physical: Guyun Stone Forest, Sal Terrae, the split Yashiori Island, Stormterror's Lair. Liyue's opera and storytellers favour the "Warrior God" Morax; Mondstadt tells its version as the tale of freedom won from a tyrant. In Natlan, people barely know of it.
### LARGE +
Do not portray the gods of the war as uniformly evil: Guizhong, Havria and Marchosius were kindly, and Decarabian's tyranny is partly disputed. "Evil god" is what coastal nations call the exiles in the Dark Sea. Never give an ordinary person knowledge of the Gnoses' origin (a 7.1 revelation) or of the forbidden pre-war history.

### STANDARD

The god fell, his storm wall broke, the bard and Amos died, and Barbatos became the Anemo Archon. Boreas, who had besieged the city for ages, judged himself unfit for the throne.

He sealed Osial and other sea gods at Guyun Stone Forest, slew Chi at Qingce, defeated the mistress of Chenyu Vale and drove Orobashi to Inazuma. Havria was killed by her own king; Marchosius gave up his godhood to protect Liyue. Xiao is the last active Yaksha of those who cleared the dead gods' demons.

## entry: Saarelainen's bargain
@keys: Saarelainen, Third Descender
@keys2: Gnosis, Gnoses, Archon War, Archon system
@version: 7.1
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
In the era of the war, Saarelainen let his body be split into the seven Gnoses to win Anastasya's resurrection and a promise that the Heavenly Principles would never harm their descendants, the Khaenri'ahns. The Gnoses were used to consolidate the Archon system. All memory of him was removed from Irminsul.
