## summary
### LITE
The Night Warden Wars were the continuous war Natlan fought against the Abyss, which began with the Cataclysm five hundred years ago. Natlan's warriors, led by bearers of Ancient Names, fought the Abyss in the Night Kingdom, the realm of the dead and of the Wayob, while the Pyro Archon could raise fallen Ancient Name bearers through the Ode of Resurrection. The war ended in the Natlan Archon Quest, when Mavuika and the Traveler defeated Gosoythoth, the heart of the Abyss.
### STANDARD +
The war shaped almost everything in modern Natlan: the Pilgrimage tournament, the Sacred Flame fed by Contending Fire, the system of inherited Ancient Names and the tribes' bond with their Wayob ancestors all exist to wage or sustain it.

## established
### LITE
- The Abyss invaded Teyvat in the Cataclysm 500 years ago. In Natlan it first appeared on Tenochtzitoc, an island on the far western edge. Natlan, without stable, deep-rooted ley lines, suffered more than any other nation.
- The tribeless warrior Tenoch gathered six warriors, one from each tribe; they became the original Heroes of Natlan. The Abyss was driven out of the overworld but kept attacking the Night Kingdom, weakening the tribes' ties to their Wayob.
- Natlan's warriors fought the war as teams sent from the Pilgrimage of the Return of the Sacred Flame. Fallen Ancient Name bearers could be revived by the Pyro Archon's Ode of Resurrection if at least one teammate won.
- The war ended in the Natlan Archon Quest with the defeat of Gosoythoth.
### STANDARD +
- After the Cataclysm the tribes were shattered and hopeless. The Pyro Archon Mavuika gathered the Heroes and reasoned that the Abyss had been repelled only because the tribes had united. Her plan was to restore each tribe's strength; when a tribe's bond with its Wayob was whole again, the Wayob would acknowledge a new hero who inherited the Ancient Name of one of the original six. When six were gathered, Natlan would be strong enough to expel the Abyss from the Night Kingdom for good.
- Because an Archon's life is of ordinary human length and the plan would take generations, Mavuika placed her life in the Sacred Flame so she could return near the time the tribes were ready.
- The Pilgrimage selected the strongest warriors. Ancient Name bearers among the winners were sent to the Night Warden Wars; the Pilgrimage also generated Contending Fire, which fuels the Sacred Flame. Only Ancient Name bearers were sent, since only they could be revived.
- The Ode of Resurrection needed two conditions: the dead warrior bore an Ancient Name, and at least one member of the team returned victorious. If the whole team fell, no revival was possible. Souls not brought back stayed in the Night Kingdom and their Ancient Names were forgotten. Warriors who survived retired when too old or badly hurt, and the name passed to a successor.
### LARGE +
- By the time the Traveler arrived, Mavuika had been back for some time, but only three of the modern heroes (Iansan, Kinich and Xilonen) had been acknowledged, because the Abyss had infected the Wayob and weakened their link to the tribes.
- Kachina's Ode failed after her team won: Mavuika could not find Kachina or her Ancient Name in the Night Kingdom, because Abyssal unrest there blocked the rite. She suspended the Pilgrimage and stopped sending anyone to the war, falling back to defend the overworld. The Sacred Flame, short of Contending Fire, risked going out; as a stopgap Mavuika fed her Archon power into it.
- With the Traveler's help Kachina was found in the Night Kingdom and Mualani was acknowledged, then Ororon. In response the Abyss launched a nation-wide invasion, and Gosoythoth, described as the heart of the Abyss, appeared in the sky and rained Abyssal tumors across Natlan. Many died, including Chasca's sister Chuychu; her death led to Chasca's acknowledgement as the sixth hero.
- The six heroes gathered at the Stadium of the Sacred Flame and, through Ronova, the Ruler of Death, empowered Mavuika and temporarily extended the Ode's blessing to every Natlanese warrior, with or without an Ancient Name. Casualties effectively dropped to zero, the overworld was cleared, and Gosoythoth was banished back to the Night Kingdom.
- Mavuika forbade other warriors from following, since the Abyssal energy near Gosoythoth would kill them instantly. She and the Traveler went alone into the Night Kingdom, helped by souls there (including the original six Heroes and the Lord of the Night). Gosoythoth took the form of the dragon Xiuhcoatl; the Lord of the Night awakened the Traveler's Pyro power, and when the Sacred Flame faltered Kachina led the gathered Natlanese in an Ode that returned its strength and unlocked the Nightsoul's Blessing of the Traveler's Ancient Name. They drew the weapon A Thousand Blazing Suns from the Sacred Flame and defeated Gosoythoth, ending the Night Warden Wars.
### ULTRA +
- Afterward the Ode was discontinued, since no warriors were sent to the Night Kingdom any longer. The Pilgrimage was retooled into a purely athletic tournament with outsiders able to take part; the Traveler served as its supervisor, and Kinich won the first of the new kind.
- Five years before the Traveler's arrival, Vichama and Mallko of the Scions of the Canopy won the Pilgrimage. An Abyssal attack on their tribe the day before the war injured Vichama, who stayed behind with Mavuika's permission; the rest of the team, Mallko included, was overwhelmed and killed, and their Ancient Names were lost. Mualani had prevailed in three Night Warden Wars without ever needing the Ode.

## connections
### STANDARD
Part of the wider Cataclysm. The Night Kingdom was built as Natlan's replacement for its war-damaged ley lines, see the War of Funerary Flame. The Ode's power is attributed to Ronova (see the Fall of Ronova). The tribes' Wayob and Ancient Names, the Pilgrimage, and Mavuika's Sacred Flame each have their own entries.

## entry: Heroes of the Cataclysm
@keys: Heroes of Natlan, Six Heroes, original six Heroes, Tenoyollotzin
@version: 5.0
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Tenoch, a tribeless, battle-hardened Muratan warrior exiled before the Cataclysm, wielded a great obsidian club called the Talking Stick and recruited one warrior from each of six tribes against the Abyss. The Cataclysm-era six, with the Ancient Names they carried, were Sundjatta (Baraka, Children of Echoes), Sanhaj (Bidii, Masters of the Night-Wind), Burkina (Malipo, Scions of the Canopy), Tupac (Umoja, People of the Springs), Wanjiru (Uwezo, Collective of Plenty) and Menilek (Vuka, Flower-Feather Clan). Mavuika, Capitano, Tenoch, the Traveler and Paimon are also counted as Heroes of Natlan for their part in the war.
### LARGE +
The names themselves go back to Xbalanque's day; each Cataclysm Hero inherited one. Deaths: Burkina died when his saurian companion Kongamato, corrupted by the Abyss, turned on him; Sanhaj and Tenoch died in the Mare Jivari when Sanhaj summoned a miniature Night Kingdom inside the monster Bakunawa and destroyed it; Menilek and Sundjatta fell in a final battle beside Mavuika; Wanjiru is assumed to have died afterward; Tupac carried the unconscious Mavuika to the Stadium of the Sacred Flame for her sacrifice and died standing. Tenoch could not be revived because he had already lost his Ancient Name.

## entry: Ode of Resurrection in the war
@keys: Ode of Resurrection, Rite of Resurrection
@keys2: Night Warden Wars, Ancient Name, Pyro Archon
@version: 5.0
### STANDARD
Performed at the Stadium of the Sacred Flame. After an officiant's eulogy, the Pyro Archon steps into the Sacred Flame to find the dead warrior and their Ancient Name in the Night Kingdom; those gathered bow and sing, and the Archon emerges with the revived warrior. It restores only fatal injuries, not old wounds, and works only if the team achieved victory. Xbalanque, the first Pyro Archon, borrowed Ronova's power to set down the rules of Natlan that the Pilgrimage and the Ode belong to.

## entry: Pilgrimage and the Night Warden Wars
@keys: Pilgrimage of the Return of the Sacred Flame, Contending Fire
@keys2: Night Warden Wars
@version: 5.0
### STANDARD
The Pilgrimage was held as needed and more often as the war worsened. Teams, usually led by an Ancient Name bearer, battle for Contending Sparks; winners proceed to one-on-one matches. Top Ancient Name bearers were sent into the war. It also feeds the Sacred Flame with Contending Fire, produced only by fights between Natlanese, so outsiders could not take part until it was reformed as a sport.

## entry: The Abyssal invasion and Gosoythoth
@keys: Gosoythoth, Reaper of the Abyss, heart of the Abyss
@version: 5.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
After the Sacred Flame was snuffed out, the heart of the Abyss manifested above the Stadium of the Sacred Flame and Mavuika reignited the Flame with a Gnosis. Gosoythoth then spread Abyssal tumors and monsters across Natlan. In the Night Kingdom it assumed the form of the dragon Xiuhcoatl and was defeated by Mavuika and the Traveler, with the whole nation's support through the Ode.

## entry: Tenochtzitoc and the first invasion
@keys: Tenochtzitoc, Bakunawa, Mare Jivari
@keys2: Abyss, Cataclysm, Night Warden Wars
@version: 5.0
### STANDARD
The Abyss first appeared in Natlan on the island of Tenochtzitoc at its far western edge. An Abyssal creature named Bakunawa devoured the island's tribespeople. Tenoch and Sanhaj later destroyed it from within, and successive Pyro Archons built a temple to contain what remained (the wiki suggests four Archons were involved). The Local Legend The Last Survivor of Tenochtzitoc and the Mare Jivari battle preserve this history. The Easybreeze Holiday Resort now stands where this occurred.
