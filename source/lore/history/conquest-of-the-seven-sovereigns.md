## summary
### LITE
The oldest conflict in Teyvat's recorded lore. An Enkanomiyan chronicle calls the era "When the Doves Held Branches". The eternal throne of the heavens, the Heavenly Principles or Primordial One, came to the world, made four shining shades of itself and fought the Seven Sovereigns, the dragon-lords of the old world. After forty years the Sovereigns were vanquished. The invader took part of their elemental Authorities and reshaped the old Light Realm into the Human Realm. Followers of the defeated Sovereigns fled into the sea, and the stolen power later became the basis of the Archons' thrones.
### STANDARD +
This conquest is distinct from the later war of funerary flame, when the Dragon King Nibelung returned with Abyssal power to fight the same usurper, and from the Archon War, fought over the thrones made from the stolen power. It explains why surviving Sovereigns such as Dvalin, Apep and Neuvillette cannot use their full power while an Archon holds the matching Authority.

## established
### STANDARD
- Before the Heavenly Principles arrived, the world belonged to the dragons. The Dragon King Nibelung and the Seven Sovereigns led it, and each Sovereign held one elemental Authority and a region attuned to that element (Seven Sovereigns wiki, drawing on Before Sun and Moon).
- The chronicle Before Sun and Moon (The Byakuyakoku Collection, Vol. 2) says the eternal throne of the heavens came and the world was made anew. The Primordial One then fought the seven "terrifying sovereigns" and made four shining shades of itself.
- Later quests and the Four Shades page name those Shades as Naberius (Life), Ronova (Death), Istaroth (Time) and Asmoday (Space).
- The chronicle says forty winters entombed the flames and forty summers churned the seas, after which the Sovereigns were vanquished and "the seven nations submitted to the heavens".
- The victor then began making heaven and earth for humanity. The seas accepted those who rebelled and would not kneel, so the Sovereigns' followers came to live in the Dark Sea and deep water.
- In Calamitous Tread (4.1) Neuvillette tells the Traveler and Paimon that when the First Usurper arrived on Teyvat it seized a part of the dragons' power, and that this stolen power is the basis of the Archons' Authorities. Seven elemental Archons correspond to seven Sovereigns.
### LARGE +
- The Light Realm, the original form of Teyvat's energy and the home of pure elemental beings, was narrowed and refracted into the seven elements to make the Human Realm. Light Realm creatures and the Human Realm are incompatible unless adapted.
- Neuvillette's Vision story says the usurper had to keep subduing the "resentments and loathing of the world" to uphold its order, and after being wounded in the great war of vengeance created the Gnoses with "one who came after" for that purpose. The Light Realm wiki page links this resentment to the narrowing of the Light Realm, whose overflow the Belyi Tsar and Aksinya call "love".
- About 400 years after the Held Branches the world was ready: animals, plants and then humans were created, and humanity made a covenant with the Primordial One, beginning the unified civilization.
- The chronicle also suggests the Primordial One "may have been Phanes": a winged, crowned, androgynous being born from an egg whose shell it used as the false sky, dividing the "universe" from the "microcosm of the world".
- A Sovereign's dragonhood is bound up with its Authority. Because the Authorities became the basis of the Archons' powers, no living Sovereign has its full power or full memory of the old world unless the matching divine throne is destroyed and the Authority returned.
### ULTRA +
- The wiki's reconstructed order: Nibelung makes the Three Moons and leaves; the Heavenly Principles descend, seize part of the Authorities and create the Shades; the fighting lasts forty years; the world is remade; humans appear after 400 years; the war of funerary flame follows at an unstated later date, ending about 6,000 years ago.
- The Heavenly Principles also took control of the Three Moons after the conquest, according to the Timeline page.
- By the chronicle's account, the people of the Seven Sovereigns found refuge in the oceans. Enkanomiya's ancestors later fell into the dark water during the war of funerary flame, met a ruling people there called the Dragonheirs of the Depths, and fought them.

## accounts
### STANDARD
- The Enkanomiyan chronicle tells it as a divine conquest from a scribe's point of view: heaven's will taking shape on earth, with the Primordial One as the true lord.
- Neuvillette calls the invader the "First Usurper" and treats the Authorities as stolen. This is a dragon's view.
- Apep, in the quest Homecoming, is at first hostile to anyone tied to the old world's usurpers and remains dissatisfied with the current order even after calming down.
- The Heavenly Principles give no account of the event in anything the Traveler hears.
### LARGE +
- Hydro vishap legend calls the Hydro Dragon the "beating heart" that ruled the Primordial Sea. It was defeated in the war and later withered away, and part of its Authority was later bound into the Hydro Archon's throne, until Focalors destroyed it. Neuvillette was reborn as its successor in human form.
- Natlan's accounts (An Omen of Annihilation and the Final Entreaty, summarised on the Xiuhcoatl and Natlan history pages) say part of Xiuhcoatl's Pyro Authority was taken when the heavens came and that he was left frozen in lava in a state of "false death". He woke when Nibelung returned and was later slain by Xbalanque, who became the first Pyro Archon.
- The Sumeru history page says Apep's Dendro Authority was stolen and that Irminsul was then taken over and refashioned.
- Wiki summaries say the Cryo Dragon's corpse was later used by Hyperborea in an attempt to make a Primordial Human, which left Snezhnaya covered in primordial ice. That happened long after the conquest.

## open_questions
### STANDARD
- How exactly the Authorities were divided: how much stayed with the Sovereigns and how much went to the thrones.
- Whether there was an Electro Sovereign and what became of it.
- Who the original Anemo and Hydro Sovereigns were.
- Why the Heavenly Principles chose Teyvat, beyond a home for humanity, and what became of the Sovereigns' domains.
- Whether the forty years are literal.

## connections
### STANDARD
- Aftermath: the Heavenly Principles create humanity about 400 years later and the unified civilization begins (lore.unified-civilization).
- Second conflict: Nibelung returns with Abyssal power and is killed by the Heavenly Principles; that war ended about 6,000 years ago (lore.war-of-funerary-flame).
- The stolen Authorities become the divine thrones that gods fight over in the Archon War (lore.archon-war, cosmology.gnosis).
- Present day as of 7.1: Neuvillette (Hydro, Fontaine, Authority restored after Focalors' death), Dvalin (Anemo, Mondstadt), Apep (Dendro, Sumeru) and Azhdaha (Geo, Liyue) survive; Xiuhcoatl was killed by Xbalanque and the Cryo Sovereign is dead.

## entry: When the Doves Held Branches
@keys: When the Doves Held Branches, Doves Held Branches, Forty Years After the Held Branches
@keys2: Heavenly Principles, Seven Sovereigns, Primordial One, Sovereigns
@version: 2.4
### STANDARD
The Enkanomiyan chronicle Before Sun and Moon names the age of the Heavenly Principles' war on the Seven Sovereigns "When the Doves Held Branches". It records that the Primordial One made four shades of itself, that forty winters and forty summers followed, and that the Sovereigns were then vanquished and the seven nations submitted to the heavens. The same book dates the creation of humans to 400 years after the Held Branches.

## entry: The Seven Sovereigns
@keys: Seven Sovereigns, Dragon Sovereign, Dragon Sovereigns, elemental dragons
@keys2: Heavenly Principles, Authority, Authorities, Archon
### STANDARD
The Seven Sovereigns were the mightiest dragons of the Light Realm, each holding one elemental Authority and ruling a region. Canon names Dvalin (Anemo, successor), Azhdaha (Geo), Apep (Dendro), Neuvillette (Hydro, successor to the first Hydro Dragon), Xiuhcoatl (Pyro, killed) and an unnamed Cryo Dragon (dead). No Electro Sovereign is identified. Part of their Authorities became the Archons' powers.

## entry: Remaking of the Light Realm
@keys: Light Realm, Human Realm, Vishap Realm
@keys2: Heavenly Principles, Sovereigns, Authorities
### LARGE
After the Sovereigns fell, the Heavenly Principles repurposed the Light Realm. Its primordial energy was narrowed and divided into the seven elements, forming the Human Realm. Creatures of the old and new realms are incompatible unless adapted, as with vishaps modified to accept Orobashi's coral or the Sangacorallia that break raw elemental energy down. Neuvillette's Vision story speaks of the "resentments and loathing of the world" that the usurper must keep subdued.

## entry: The Dark Sea and the Defeated
@keys: Dark Sea, Dragonheirs of the Depths, Enkanomiya
@keys2: Sovereigns, Heavenly Principles
### LARGE
Those who would not kneel were taken in by the seas. The chronicle says the people of the Seven Sovereigns found refuge in the oceans, and that the Dragonheirs of the Depths ruled the dark water where Enkanomiya's ancestors fell during the later war. It tells this from Enkanomiya's side, so the Sovereigns' followers appear as hunters in the shadows.
