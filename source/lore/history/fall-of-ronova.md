## summary
@version: 7.1
@spoiler: major
### LITE
The fall of Ronova is the climax of the Snezhnaya Archon Quest act "A Rekviem for the Underworld" (7.1). Ronova, the Shade who rules death, had been suppressing Snezhnaya's research with her Curse of Death. While the Tsaritsa held Rhinedottir in conversation and secretly slowed time around her with six Gnoses, Ronova's victims could not be revived in time; Celestia punished the violation by taking away Ronova's Authority of Death. The Traveler, Dainsleif and Pierro then defeated her. Mortally wounded, she fled and, dying, fused the Eitr with the Celestial Nail above the Cryo Dragon's corpse as a "parting gift". She is the first of the Four Shades shown dying.
### STANDARD +
Pierro landed the decisive blow with a new, untested Delusion called the "Eye of the Fatui", at a heavy cost to his soul and life force. The Curse of Death lifted with her Authority, freeing Project Mistilteinn, but the Curse of Immortality on the Khaenri'ahns remains. After her act, all of Teyvat's Celestial Nails began to cast strange pillars of light into the sky.

## established
@version: 7.1
@spoiler: major
### LITE
- Ronova, the Ruler of Death, is one of the Four Shades created by the Heavenly Principles.
- In Snezhnaya she had imposed a Curse of Death: when someone conceived technology threatening the Heavenly Principles, they and those around them briefly died and forgot the idea.
- Those deaths broke the rules she served, so she relied on Rhinedottir, Ruler of Life, to revive the victims before Celestia's "monitoring cycle" noticed.
- The Tsaritsa broke that loophole; Celestia reclaimed Ronova's Authority, and she was defeated in battle.
### STANDARD +
- Earlier in Chapter VII (7.0), the curse caused unexplained memory loss among Armory Palace researchers working on Project Mistilteinn, including Mitya. A whole audience at the Korolevskiy Theater, the Traveler among them, was trapped in the afterlife until the Traveler and Mitya unmasked Ronova, who had disguised herself as Alyosha. Later she tried to take Paimon by force at the Armory Palace before Pierro stopped her.
- The Fatui treated a direct confrontation as inevitable and built a new Delusion for it, using Arlecchino as the base because she descends from the Crimson Moon, which once held the authority over death.
- Snezhnaya's victory, as Pierro puts it, came at the moment Celestia reclaimed her Authority; the battle afterward was the old feud of the Curse of Immortality playing out.
### LARGE +
- After the battle, the Traveler concludes that the Curse of Immortality was a permanent curse laid under the Heavenly Principles' judgment, which even Ronova could not simply undo, while the Curse of Death was her own unauthorised initiative and ended when her power was taken away. Dainsleif and Pierro therefore remain immortal.
- Naberius judged the Tsaritsa's act beyond Celestia's power to judge as "rebellion" and below the threshold for a Nail of Retribution, though he wanted her punished for overstepping her authority. Rhinedottir declined.

## the_plan
@version: 7.1
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
The Tsaritsa had arranged to receive Rhinedottir at Zapolyarny Palace, offering news of Asmoday in exchange for information. On the same day Mitya was to move equipment from Rankova Zorya to Snezhnograd for a scheme to turn the Korolevskiy Theater into a laboratory, a project Ronova could not ignore. Pierro's instruction to the Traveler was simple: wait until Ronova appears and kills everyone, then keep her from leaving.
### ULTRA +
Pierro called in Dainsleif, who came partly for revenge on the Shade who cursed him and partly to speak with a past incarnation of Hroptatyr; Pierro asked him to set his own agenda aside and focus on Ronova. Pierro admitted to the Traveler that a plan existed whose full scope even he, as Director of the Fatui, did not know, because Ronova might be listening anywhere. Before the day, Sandrone and Arlecchino shared a tea party with the Traveler and Paimon; Sandrone spent the time fortifying positions and upgrading equipment. Ronova appeared earlier than expected and at Rankova Zorya rather than the theater; Mitya sent Vodyanitsa to fetch Valeriy and Vesna and had the Rokot evacuate the locals. Mitya's theater scheme rested on the Korolevskiy Theater's damaged Ley Lines, which briefly trap the souls of the dead there, and on his own soul bearing Ronova's mark, which lets him remember what happens while dead. Neither he nor anyone else could be sure it would work, and that was the point: Ronova could not be sure it would fail.

## battle_of_rankova_zorya
@version: 7.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Ronova struck Rankova Zorya and triggered the Curse of Death. The Traveler fought her (in gameplay, with Odette and Vesna as trial party members), was killed and escaped the afterlife first, drawing on Vesna's use of the Authority of Life and memories of every nation they had visited. Waking among the dead, the Traveler stalled Ronova with taunts: she was bound by more rules than she controlled, a tool of the Heavenly Principles, while Paimon was writing her own destiny. Ronova, angered, promised torment instead of death, and waited for Rhinedottir to revive the dead. The revival never came.
### LARGE +
When Ronova tried to leave, Dainsleif snared her and Pierro arrived with the completed Eye of the Fatui. Ronova said no mortal body or mind could withstand such power. She cast the Curse of Death again; the Traveler raced back through the afterlife, combined all the elements into the power of the Light Realm and destroyed her escape portal. At that point Celestia reclaimed her Authority. Stripped of it, she declared she could now kill without consequence and fought on with the full power of a Shade.
### ULTRA +
In the final clash, Dainsleif carried an exhausted Pierro away from Ronova's attacks while he and the Traveler bought time. Pierro, recalling how she had looked on without pity or pleasure as she cursed the Khaenri'ahns, called down a vast elemental nail, its force amplified by Abyssal energy surging from the Delusion. The Traveler shielded the others with a Geo construct. When the dust cleared, only a closing portal remained in the crater. In the quest, Ronova's boss titles change from "Arbiter of the Heavenly Principles" to "Paralogism's Outcast" once her power is stripped.

## the_tsaritsas_gambit
@version: 7.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
At the palace, the Tsaritsa offered her account of sighting Asmoday, persuaded Rhinedottir to stay and finish the tea, and kept her talking. Rhinedottir eventually noticed her drink had not cooled: the Tsaritsa had used the six Gnoses as nodes to slow time in the hall, so the window for reviving Ronova's victims passed unnoticed outside. Rhinedottir said Cryo amplified by the Gnoses could affect the bounds of Reason, slowing time though not stopping it.
### LARGE +
Celestia's directive then ordered Rhinedottir to correct Ronova's mistake, and she revived the dead, as the Tsaritsa, who said she did not sacrifice pawns, had expected. Rhinedottir spared the Tsaritsa, saying Celestia was watching her and Naberius as possible accomplices, and spoke as "Gold" of Khaenri'ah in admiration of Snezhnaya. Naberius was displeased and expected Snezhnaya to escalate. The Tsaritsa credited her own insight from the past thousand years as well as the Gnoses, and said the trick would not work on Rhinedottir twice.

## parting_gift
@version: 7.1
@spoiler: major
### LITE
Ronova had secretly teleported away. In a final cutscene she emerges, dying, before the Nail of Retribution over the Cryo Dragon's corpse and fuses the Eitr with it as her "parting gift"; the Nail begins to crumble and she disintegrates.
### STANDARD +
The Eitr (Embryonic-Ichoric-Telluric-Rudiment) is a forbidden creation of Hyperborea hidden in the Shadow Realm. The cutscene description says Ronova, feeling death for the first time, meant all of Teyvat to share that sensation as Holy Soil spread across the land. After the quest, pillars of light rise above Teyvat's Celestial Nails.
### LARGE +
In her last words she reflects that she who controls death cannot know dying, and that she need no longer despise humans, gods and one unnamed third party. In the world quest series "The Anomaly" (7.1), the Traveler finds the Snezhnayan nail glowing and making sounds, with Alyosha keeping watch; Paimon wonders if this is Ronova's revenge. Elsewhere Albedo observes the Skyfrost Nail, Xiao watches the Chasm's nail on Rex Lapis's orders, Yae Miko senses a faint power she cannot place and means to bring Kokomi to look at it, Candace and Dehya's mercenaries keep watch in Sumeru, and Mavuika warns Natlan's people to stay alert.

## aftermath
@version: 7.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Within days Rankova Zorya was being repaired, Armory Palace staff confirmed the restrictions on Project Mistilteinn were gone, and the Tsaritsa granted an audience commending everyone involved. Dainsleif attended out of respect for the "Mother of Khaenri'ah" and called the victory the start of a new era that would not be peaceful. Pierro was not seen; Dainsleif said the battle had taken an irrecoverable toll on his mind and soul.
### LARGE +
Dainsleif also met the young Hroptatyr preserved from the notebook and found it disappointing, since that incarnation knew nothing of Khaenri'ah's dark side. He noted that Pierro seemed eager to discuss his work with him, possibly as a way to resist erosion.

## accounts
### LARGE
- Pierro: the real victory was the reclaiming of her Authority; the fight was a feud born from the Curse of Immortality. Unless she escaped undetected, he said, she had taken fatal, irreversible damage; either way he warned of retaliation.
- Rhinedottir: called Ronova capricious beneath a serious manner, and said anyone should be grateful not to have to face her.
- Dainsleif: five hundred years ago, Khaenri'ahns saw her as an untouchable god; Snezhnaya may count the victory absolute.
- Ronova: earlier, she said she could finish the Traveler only if Asmoday returned so the Four Shades acted jointly, if she found conclusive evidence for a conviction, or when the Heavenly Principles awaken.
- Vodyanitsa, after the battle, wondered aloud whether the Ruler of Death can die at all.

## entry: Eye of the Fatui
@keys: Eye of the Fatui
@version: 7.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The Eye of the Fatui is the new Delusion completed for Pierro just in time for the battle with Ronova, developed with Arlecchino as the base for her Crimson Moon heritage. Untested in combat, it let him call down a vast elemental nail that mortally wounded Ronova, at the price of his soul and life force; Ronova said no mortal could withstand that power. Dainsleif said the toll on Pierro was irrecoverable.

## entry: Ronova's Curse of Death
@keys: Curse of Death
@keys2: Ronova, Snezhnaya, Mistilteinn, Rhinedottir
@version: 7.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Ronova's Curse of Death killed anyone in Snezhnaya who conceived technology threatening the Heavenly Principles, together with those near them, and erased the idea. Rhinedottir then revived them before Celestia's monitoring cycle could notice, a loophole around rules the killings broke. When the Tsaritsa delayed the revival in 7.1, the curse was exposed, Ronova lost her Authority and the curse ended with it.

## entry: Tsaritsa's time trick
@keys: monitoring cycle, Rhinedottir's visit
@keys2: Tsaritsa, Anastasya, Rhinedottir, Gnoses, Gnosis
@version: 7.1
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
During Rhinedottir's visit to Zapolyarny Palace, the Tsaritsa used her Cryo power, amplified by the six Gnoses as nodes of a field, to slow time inside the hall. Rhinedottir missed the agreed window to revive Ronova's victims, and Celestia's monitoring cycle flagged the deaths. Rhinedottir noted that the Tsaritsa had also uncovered the monitoring cycle itself.

## entry: The Anomaly (Celestial Nails)
@keys: The Anomaly, pillars of light
@keys2: Nail, Nails, Celestial Nail, Ronova, Eitr
@version: 7.1
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
After Ronova fused the Eitr with the nail over the Cryo Dragon's corpse east of Snezhnograd, Celestial Nails across Teyvat began to cast strange pillars of light into the sky. As of 7.1 their meaning is unknown; Liyue (Xiao reports to Rex Lapis), Inazuma's Yae Miko, Sumeru's desert folk and Natlan's Mavuika all treat the lights as a threat to watch.
