## summary
### LITE
The war of funerary flame was a world-shaking war fought about 6,000 years ago, in the "Funerary Year", between the Dragon King Nibelung and the Heavenly Principles for dominion over Teyvat. Nibelung returned from the sea of stars carrying the power of the Abyss and led the surviving dragons against the heavens. The Heavenly Principles won and killed him, but were badly wounded. In the fighting the Eternal and Iridescent Moons were shattered and the Frost Moon was flung beyond the false sky. Celestial Nails then fell across the land, and the old unified civilization was destroyed.
### STANDARD +
Most of what the people of Teyvat know about it survives only as fragments: Celestia banned knowledge of the history before this war, so it appears in ruins, item texts and the memories of ancient beings such as Apep, Nicole and the moon goddesses. Its scars are everywhere in the modern world: Dragonspine, the Chasm, the deserts of Sumeru, Enkanomiya and the shattered remnants of the moons all date from it.

## established
### LITE
- Sides: Nibelung and the dragons (including the surviving Dragon Sovereigns) against the Heavenly Principles, the First Descender who had conquered the Seven Sovereigns long before.
- Nibelung brought the Abyss and forbidden knowledge into Teyvat; the corruption it spread nearly brought the world to collapse.
- Outcome: Nibelung was killed; the Heavenly Principles won but were left crippled.
- The Three Moons were destroyed or banished and their goddesses later died.
### STANDARD +
- Before the war, Nibelung had left Teyvat to travel the sea of stars and created the Three Moons and their goddesses (Aria, Canon and Sonnet) to watch the world in his place. The Heavenly Principles arrived while he was gone, defeated the Seven Sovereigns in a 40-year war, created the Four Shades and raised the false sky. The moon goddesses, too weak to resist, kept an uneasy peace with them.
- When Nibelung returned, the moon sisters disagreed over whether to side with him. He judged them disloyal, sealed them in a prison called the "moon's reflection", and turned their moons into weapons against the heavens. The Heavenly Principles answered by shattering the Eternal and Iridescent Moons and throwing the Frost Moon beyond the false sky. The surviving dragons later cheered the moons' fall, seeing the goddesses as traitors to their kind.
- After the war the Heavenly Principles cast down Celestial Nails to purge the Abyssal corruption. The Nails cleansed it, but they ruined landscapes, damaged ley lines and wiped out most of the unified civilization.
### LARGE +
- The Heavenly Principles' wounds were lasting. In Neuvillette's Vision story, the "usurper", hurt in the "great war of vengeance", had its functions ruined and could no longer use absolute authority to suppress the world's original order. To keep control, it made the seven Gnoses with "one who came after" and built the new order of the Seven Archons.
- The Angels, Celestia's envoys to humanity, were punished afterwards for the archangel Koitar's part in the Hyperborean rebellion: Koitar lost her memories, and the others lost either their power (becoming Seelie) or their wisdom (becoming mindless beasts).
- Knowledge of the era before the war was forbidden. Before Sun and Moon, the Enkanomiya text that records it, is the book for which Celestia found Orobashi and Watatsumi guilty; Orobashi took the guilt on itself and died about 2,000 years ago.
- With the moons gone, the Moon Envoys who ferried souls to the moon were no longer needed, and souls of the dead were sent into the ley lines instead.

## course_of_the_war
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
Canon gives no full battle history; it is pieced together from several accounts:
- Hyperborea, a golden city in what is now Snezhnaya, had rebelled against Celestia under the angel Koitar and the star-voyager Anacharsis. A Celestial Nail destroyed it. Dragons had joined that rebellion and kept fighting.
- Nibelung then returned, tore open the false sky and led the dragons against the Heavenly Principles. Apep says he "acquired the power of darkness from outside of this world".
- The moons were turned into weapons and then destroyed, as described above.
- The heavens and the earth were "rent asunder". Delphi Pytho sank into the depths and became Enkanomiya.
- Nibelung was finally destroyed. Kukulkan says the "reaver" annihilated him "at great cost".
- Apep, the Dendro Sovereign, aided Nibelung with her hoard of forbidden knowledge. She says she was stopped by a "giant spike" from the sky while gathering more from a collapsing world; that Nail turned her lands into desert and her emerald body to a sand-coloured grey.
- In Natlan, the Pyro Sovereign Xiuhcoatl, whom the Heavenly Principles had frozen in lava in a false death, woke when Nibelung returned. The dragons destroyed the local ley lines and devoured the region's angels, and the human tribes fell back into barbarism. When Nibelung died, Xiuhcoatl, already steeped in the Abyss, had a brief, grim moment of clarity.
- The moon goddesses had already decided to take countermeasures against the Heavenly Principles' harsh rule when Nibelung returned and interrupted them. Aria says Nibelung built the moon's reflection specifically to stop them from interfering in his war.
### ULTRA +
- Before Sun and Moon calls Nibelung "the second throne of the heavens", and says war was "rekindled, as it was in the world's creation". This Enkanomiyan view casts the war as a second contest for the heavens after the Heavenly Principles' original conquest.
- Lauma calls the war a conflict brought about by Descenders. The Heavenly Principles are counted as the First Descender. Treat any label for Nibelung as a Descender as the wiki's synthesis of these remarks.
- Before Nibelung left Teyvat, the star-voyager Anacharsis met him and warned that his world was doomed. The Deep Gallery artifact texts tell this, and say that when Anacharsis returned to Teyvat she found him gone and the heavens in control.

## aftermath
### STANDARD
After the war:
- Celestial Nails fell across Teyvat. Known or implied impacts include Sal Vindagnyr (the Skyfrost Nail on Dragonspine), Lang-Gan (the Spiritstone, which carved out the Chasm in Liyue), Ys and Mt. Damavand in Sumeru (desertification), and probably Tsurumi Island in Inazuma and the Basin of Unnumbered Flames in Natlan.
- The unified civilization was dismantled and its history forbidden.
- The Heavenly Principles hid the shattered moons and the Frost Moon beyond the false sky and hung an illusory moon in the night sky.
- The Angels were cursed and cast out; Hyperborean survivors, guided by Canon, became the Frostmoon Scions.
- The Archon War, which began within a century or two of the war's end and lasted until about 2,000 years ago, placed the Gnoses in the hands of the Seven.
### LARGE +
- Sal Vindagnyr's Nail destroyed its Irminsul tree and froze the verdant mountain into today's Dragonspine; its people did not survive.
- In Lang-Gan, Zibai, the city's divine envoy, slowed the Nail long enough for survivors to escape to Chenyu Vale.
- In Sumeru, Greater Lord Rukkhadevata later raised the Wall of Samiel to stop the desert spreading into the rainforest, and King Deshret drew on the Nail's power to build his Eternal Oasis.
- In Natlan, Kukulkan says the sky-nails destroyed the phlogiston-rich soil, so the dragons regressed to survive and most eventually became saurians. Ronova had the Lord of the Night build the Night Kingdom to repair Natlan's ley lines, an act the Heavenly Principles saw as overreach.
- Enkanomiya was barred from returning to the surface. Only Istaroth did not forsake its people, who were eventually led up to Watatsumi Island by Orobashi.
- Asmoday's watchers carried some people of that era, such as Istoria, to the Temple of Space as living memory.
### ULTRA +
- The moon goddesses survived in their prison but lost their place in the world. When they returned to Teyvat, Aria soon died, Sonnet fused with the Abyss and became the Crimson Moon, and Canon guided the Hyperborean survivors to Hiisi Island before fading. In the True Moon act (6.3), Columbina visits them in the moon's reflection from the future before this happens, and they pass the Trilune authority to her.
- Dragons that survived the war cheered the moons' destruction, as Fractured Halo records.
- Arcadia, an ancient nation in Mondstadt's lands, was buried under endless winter when "Eternity" ended with the war's outbreak.

## present_day
@version: 7.1
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
The war still shapes the world as of 7.1. The Abyss that Nibelung brought remains Teyvat's great threat. The Celestial Nails still stand, and ley line disorders persist around them, though at the end of the 7.1 Archon Quest the dying Ronova fused the Eitr with Snezhnaya's nail of retribution, which began to crumble. The Heavenly Principles have been dormant since the Cataclysm 500 years ago. The Frost Moon returned to Teyvat's sky in 6.3 with Columbina as its goddess.
### ULTRA +
7.x revelations about the Third Descender Saarelainen tie into the war's aftermath. The Heavenly Principles made the Gnoses from his body in exchange for restoring his lover Anastasya, whom they had erased, and never harming their Khaenri'ahn descendants. A curse he laid on that bargain is what put the Heavenly Principles into their slumber when they broke it during the Cataclysm. In the 7.1 Archon Quest, beams of light rise into the sky over the six Celestial Nails.

## accounts
### LARGE
- Nicole, an ancient angel, tells the Traveler and Lauma the war's history in Where Does Her Name Rest? (6.2). She notes that the Heavenly Principles may not have known the goddesses were imprisoned.
- The moon sisters' own version, given to Columbina in True Moon (6.3), stresses the Heavenly Principles' harshness and the goddesses' failure to act. When Columbina calls it the "war of funerary flame", Canon remarks that this must be the name later ages gave it, and Sonnet asks who won.
- Apep and Kukulkan speak as dragons who lived through it, and both describe Nibelung as the dragons' rightful king.
- Neuvillette, Dragon Sovereign of Hydro, learned of the war only after regaining his full power; his Vision story calls it a great war of vengeance and the Heavenly Principles "the usurper".
- The Liyue moon legends (Moonlit Bamboo Forest) speak vaguely of disasters overturning the moon goddesses' carriage and ruining "the halls of the stars".
- Before Sun and Moon is written from Enkanomiya's point of view: ancestors who fell into the dark during "the Funerary Year", forsaken by three of the four Shades.
- Nabu Malikata's artifact text (Amethyst Crown) says the invaders brought war to her angelic kin, and the master of the heavens, fearing "delusion and breakthroughs", sent down nails "to mend the land".

## entry: Funerary Year (Enkanomiya)
@keys: Before Sun and Moon, second throne of the heavens, Delphi Pytho
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
In Enkanomiya's book Before Sun and Moon, "the Funerary Year" is the day the "second throne of the heavens" (Nibelung) came and war was rekindled as at the world's creation. Heaven collapsed and the earth split, and the ancestors of Enkanomiya fell with their land, Delphi Pytho, into the depths. Of the four Shades, only Istaroth did not forsake them. Celestia later forbade the history this book records. The sage Aberaku, a pupil of Istoria, became trapped there when the kingdom sank.

## entry: The moons in the war
@keys: moon's reflection, Moon Sisters, moon sisters, Three Moon Goddesses
@keys2: Nibelung, funerary, imprisoned, shattered
@spoiler: major
@version: 6.3
### STANDARD
Created by Nibelung, the three moon goddesses split over whether to follow him when he returned: one argued they should follow the one who had hung the moons in the sky, another that taking sides would only bring the world closer to ruin, since he had returned bearing the Abyss. Nibelung judged them disloyal and sealed them in the moon's reflection, a prison of warped space and time, then used their moons as weapons. The Heavenly Principles shattered the Eternal and Iridescent Moons and flung the Frost Moon beyond the false sky. Rejected by a world they no longer belonged to, the goddesses later died. The Frost Moon's survival is why a new moon goddess, Columbina, could later be born in Nod-Krai.

## entry: Hyperborean rebellion
@keys: Hyperborean rebellion, Golden City of Hyperborea
@keys2: Koitar, Anacharsis, rebellion, Nail
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
The archangel Koitar and the star-voyager Anacharsis ruled Hyperborea for thirty years in open defiance of the Heavenly Principles. When their experiments with the Cryo Dragon's corpse froze the region, the heavens sent a Celestial Nail; Hyperborea rose against Celestia, joined by some Angels and dragons, but the city was destroyed and sank into the Shadow Realm. The fighting dragons were then joined by the returning Nibelung. Afterwards Koitar was stripped of her memories and all Angels were cursed.

In Natlan, the Pyro Sovereign Xiuhcoatl woke from a false death in Tollan's frozen lava when Nibelung returned, restored the Sacred City under the Thirteen Dragonlords, and joined the war. The dragons destroyed Natlantea's ley lines and devoured its angels, and the local human civilisation fell. After Nibelung's death Xiuhcoatl, corrupted by the Abyss, briefly regained clarity and used the last of his time and wisdom to transform the dragon city and create the Tzitzimimeh and Huitzilopochtli. The sky-nails ruined Natlan's phlogiston-rich soil and most dragons regressed into saurians. Ronova later had the Lord of the Night create the Night Kingdom to mend the ley lines.

## entry: Apep in the war of funerary flame
@keys: Apep
@keys2: Nibelung, funerary, war, giant spike
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
Apep, the Dendro Dragon Sovereign, fought for Nibelung and gathered forbidden knowledge for him. She tells the Traveler and Nahida that the war's destruction nearly made the world collapse. A Celestial Nail stopped her, turned her domain around Mt. Damavand into desert, and turned her body from emerald to sand-grey.
