## summary
@spoiler: major
### LITE
Five hundred years ago Khaenri'ah, an underground kingdom built entirely by humans with no god, was destroyed. Its last king, the Vinster King Irmin, grew obsessed with the power of the Abyss. He named the Traveler's twin his heir and "Savior", then used them as a vessel to absorb Abyssal power. A palace coup led by Dainsleif ended with five of Khaenri'ah's greatest figures, the Five Sinners, dividing that power among themselves. Then the gods came: the Heavenly Principles and The Seven laid the kingdom waste, and Ronova cursed its people. The fall is the core of the cataclysm, and its survivors went on to found the Abyss Order and help found the Fatui.
### STANDARD +
Version 7.1 revealed a final twist. The Khaenri'ahns descend from the Tsaritsa Anastasya and the Third Descender, Saarelainen, who long ago bound the Heavenly Principles with a curse in case they ever harmed his descendants. Destroying Khaenri'ah triggered it, and the Heavenly Principles have been asleep ever since.

## established
@spoiler: major
### LITE
- Khaenri'ah was a godless kingdom of humans, called by Dainsleif "the pride of humankind". It lay underground beneath what is now Sumeru.
- About 500 years ago it was destroyed "by gods". The Traveler and their sibling, newly fallen into Teyvat, saw it burn.
- Pure-blooded survivors were cursed with immortality by Ronova, the Ruler of Death. Many others became monsters, and some of their descendants are the hilichurls and the Abyss Order.
### STANDARD +
- Under the Eclipse Dynasty, Khaenri'ah built machines meant to fight Celestia. Ruin Guards were once called "Field Tillers", after the saying that land is won with "steel and blood", not farming tools. A letter found in the Gavireh Lajavard ruins says the kingdom's "four pillars" prospered by "spying upon secrets from beyond the skies". This let their machines escape the world's laws.
- Every Archon except Rukkhadevata was summoned to Khaenri'ah during the disaster. She stayed to guard Irminsul from the forbidden knowledge flooding it.
- Ei says Makoto, the Electro Archon of the time, went to Khaenri'ah alone without telling her and never came back.
### LARGE +
- Pierro's artifact text (Mocking Mask) says he failed to win the former ruler's favour and failed to stop "the sages" from "tearing away the veil of sin". He describes the result as a tide of divine wrath, destruction and foolishness.
- Capitano said most Khaenri'ahns knew nothing of the Five Sinners' use of Abyssal power, yet all were cursed alike.

## the_road_to_ruin
@version: 6.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Irmin, the last Vinster King of the Eclipse Dynasty, began as a sane ruler. His closest counsellors were Vedrfolnir, "The Visionary", and Hroptatyr, "The Wise". Over time he became fixated on the Abyss. The Universitas Magistrorum moved almost all its staff into Abyssal Energy research. He also ordered every descendant of the earlier Crimson Moon Dynasty hunted down, as threats to his line.
### LARGE +
When the Traveler's sibling reached Khaenri'ah, Irmin and the Court proclaimed them heir to the throne and the nation's "Savior" in a great public celebration. A young Dainsleif kept order in the palace plaza. Later, the king led the heir "down, down, down" to the kingdom's deepest depths and made them a vessel to absorb "unlimited Abyssal power" (glider Wings of Fate's Course Intertwined, 5.2). Dainsleif later said the title "Savior" really stood for "the Vinster King's lust for power" (5.7).
### ULTRA +
Rerir's memories, explored in All Is Foretold (6.1), show the purge from inside. As a secret Eclipse agent, Rerir worked through case files on Crimson Moon descendants, including orphaned children, each marked as a threat to the dynasty. A voice he took to be Vedrfolnir's whispered to him. Tholindis, his would-be fiancée, quietly belonged to a diluted Crimson Moon branch. She was the only researcher left working on Moon Marrow, and she warned in her reports against staking everything on one poorly understood power. Rebels hiding from the purge called the king "a blind fool, his eyes clouded by powers from beyond this world".

## the_coup
@version: 6.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Vedrfolnir warned Irmin that if he kept on this path, Khaenri'ah would fall. The king had his eyes gouged out and threw him into a cell. Dainsleif, captain of the royal guard and Vedrfolnir's younger brother, gathered Hroptatyr, Rhinedottir ("Gold"), Surtalogi ("The Foul") and Rerir to storm the palace that night and free him. Dainsleif said he did not want treason; he meant to make the king see his error with his sword. But once inside, the others turned on his plan. Each took a share of the Abyssal power the king had poured into the sibling, and they became the Five Sinners of Khaenri'ah. Dainsleif says they each fell to their own desire: Vedrfolnir to foresee the future, Hroptatyr to limitless knowledge, Rhinedottir to creating life, Surtalogi to supreme strength.
### LARGE +
The plan was that Dainsleif's group would hold off the palace troops, Hroptatyr and Rhinedottir would disarm the security measures and Rerir would reach the prison. Vedrfolnir told Rerir he had foreseen the night and had briefly seen "something new". Then he sent him to the Universitas Magistrorum. A strange wave of energy swept the city, and monsters appeared in the streets and the laboratory. Rerir remembered rumours that the king had hidden Abyss-powered "secret mechanisms" in the palace and city. In the lab, Tholindis, trapped by monsters, stepped into a portal to an unknown space and vanished. Broken, Rerir returned to the palace, where Vedrfolnir invited him to "take your share". Dainsleif watched the last of his allies abandon him. Skirk's character story says each Sinner took one sixth of the power.
### ULTRA +
Dainsleif calls these five, with himself, the six who should have stopped the Vinster King. That night still haunts him, and he says even the brother he came to save betrayed him. When the gods attacked, not one Sinner defended Khaenri'ah, and he will never forgive them. Vedrfolnir also gave Dainsleif a ring that keeps resetting his body's condition, which is why his memory has held up better than that of other cursed survivors.

## the_destruction
@spoiler: major
### LITE
The gods descended on Khaenri'ah and destroyed it. The Traveler remembers a sea of flames and a god, Asmoday, who sealed the twins as they tried to leave the world.
### STANDARD +
The sibling woke first, saw the kingdom fall, then woke the Traveler and told them the destruction of Khaenri'ah had thrown the world into chaos and they should leave. Asmoday, the Ruler of Space, stopped them, calling them "outlanders", and sealed both twins. Ronova then laid the Curse of Immortality on pure-blooded Khaenri'ahns. Dainsleif was among them, condemned, in the sibling's words, to wander forever while his people became monsters of the Abyss.
### LARGE +
Accounts of the assault describe a red sky full of crimson cubes and the kingdom's land torn apart and flooded with lava. In the Inazuma Archon Quest (2.1) Ei recalls seeing "a nation stride forward and lose everything to the Heavenly Principles". Among the Archons, Makoto died in Khaenri'ah; Rukkhadevata died fighting forbidden knowledge in Irminsul; and Egeria fell at Tunigi Hollow in Sumeru's desert, fighting the Abyss.
### ULTRA +
In 7.1 Pierro remembers Ronova's face as she cast the curse: no pity and no pleasure, only indifference while people begged for mercy. Dainsleif recalls that the Khaenri'ahns who looked up at her saw an untouchable god. The Traveler concludes that the Curse of Immortality was laid under the Heavenly Principles' judgement and is permanent; even Ronova could not simply lift it.

## the_third_descenders_curse
@version: 7.1
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
The Archon Quest "A Rekviem for the Underworld" (7.1) revealed why the gods' victory cost them. Rhinedottir, drawing on Celestia's records, told Anastasya that all Khaenri'ahns descend from Anastasya and the Third Descender, Saarelainen. After Anastasya was erased as punishment for opening a gate to the Abyss, Saarelainen bargained with the Heavenly Principles. He gave his body to be split into the seven Gnoses in exchange for her return and a promise never to lay a hand on his descendants. He also bound the promise with a curse that would only take effect if it was broken. Anastasya concluded that the cataclysm in Khaenri'ah triggered that curse, which is why the Heavenly Principles are asleep.
### ULTRA +
Rhinedottir called Anastasya "Mother of Khaenri'ah", and Dainsleif attends her audience out of courtesy to that title. In this telling, the Heavenly Principles also demanded that every trace of Saarelainen be erased from the world through Irminsul. Only the Belyi Tsar, who fled into the Shadow Realm, kept any memory of him. Rhinedottir insisted she had told "just a story" and had not answered Anastasya's question about the Heavenly Principles' condition.

## aftermath
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
With King Irmin "indisposed", Knight Marshal Anfortas of the Schwanenritter proclaimed himself Regent and led the final kingdom council, the Regnum Concilium Ultimum. Survivors able to travel went up to Sumeru with the Schwanenritter and their Ruin Golems. On the surface, many refugees were struck by a disease that turned them into monsters, and some who had not yet fallen ill went back underground. The Schwanenritter fought the Abyss to the last knight.
### LARGE +
Notes left in the Gavireh Lajavard ruins record the Regent's bulletin and a survivor's journal from a family that stayed behind. The Sumeru scholars later known as the Order of Skeptics honoured the Schwanenritter as "heroes of Dahri" and took up their fight. Of the last two knights, Hadura betrayed the order and Anfortas lost his left eye executing them. Anfortas's final fate is unknown.
### ULTRA +
The fall shaped the present. Chlothar Alberich later founded the Abyss Order, and the sibling, who travelled with Dainsleif after waking, eventually took it over. Pierro joined the Cryo Archon and co-founded the Fatui. Capitano, cursed with immortality, sought revenge on Ronova for the innocent. Pure-blooded survivors still endure the curse and its erosion.

## accounts
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
- Dainsleif (1.4 onward): a glorious human nation "uprooted and crushed, like a weed removed from the garden of the gods". He still warns the Traveler not to repeat "the failings of my forebears" by trying to overthrow the gods.
- The sibling: Dainsleif failed to prevent the destruction. The sibling now wages war on "destiny" until the Abyss engulfs the thrones.
- Chlothar (3.5): Khaenri'ah saw the Prince/Princess as the Abyss itself and hoped that by controlling the Abyss it could control everything.
- Pierro: the sages invited divine wrath by tearing away the veil of sin.
- Capitano (5.3): the people were punished for the Sinners' crimes, though most were innocent.
- Ei: a nation strode forward and lost everything to the Heavenly Principles.
### ULTRA +
- Rhinedottir (7.1), herself a Sinner and now Ruler of Life, presents the Heavenly Principles' attack as the breach of an ancient promise. Her account comes from Celestia's own records.
- Nefer, viewing Rerir's memories (6.1), sums up Khaenri'ah's story as small lies piling up until they destroy everything.

## connections
### LARGE
The fall of Khaenri'ah is the centre of the wider cataclysm (Durin's attack on Mondstadt, the Abyss in Inazuma, Sumeru, Fontaine, Natlan and Nod-Krai). Its people and their descendants include Dainsleif, Kaeya, Pierro, Capitano and the Abyss Order. Several of the Five Sinners are still at large as of 7.1, and Rhinedottir now serves Celestia as the Ruler of Life.
### ULTRA +
According to notes found in Nod-Krai, the Belyi Tsar led Snezhnayan forces toward Khaenri'ah to stop an advancing dark host. After a week of fighting they were routed and his consciousness was destroyed. In 7.1 Snezhnaya brought down Ronova, the deity who cursed Khaenri'ah, with Dainsleif and Pierro among those who fought her.

## entry: Vinster King Irmin
@keys: Irmin, Vinster King
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Irmin was the last Vinster King of Khaenri'ah's Eclipse Dynasty. He trusted Vedrfolnir and Hroptatyr above all, and over time became obsessed with Abyssal power. He approved technology built on "secrets from beyond the skies", purged the Crimson Moon bloodline, named the Traveler's sibling heir and "Savior", and made them a vessel for Abyssal power. He blinded Vedrfolnir for warning that Khaenri'ah would fall, which set off the palace coup.
### LARGE +
When the cataclysm struck he was declared "indisposed" and Anfortas became Regent. Whether he died that night is unconfirmed; the glider text speaks of "regicide".

## entry: The storming of the palace
@keys: stormed the palace, storm the palace, Twilight Sword
@keys2: Khaenri'ah, Dainsleif, Vedrfolnir, Sinners, Irmin
@version: 6.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
On the night Vedrfolnir was blinded and imprisoned, Dainsleif, then captain of the royal guard, the "Twilight Sword", led Hroptatyr, Rhinedottir, Surtalogi and Rerir into the palace to free him. It was meant as a rescue that would force the king to see reason. Instead the five took shares of the Abyssal power held in the sibling and became the Five Sinners, leaving Dainsleif betrayed. Monsters spread through the city the same night.

## entry: Regent Anfortas and the exodus
@keys: Anfortas, Regnum Concilium Ultimum, Marshal Regent
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Knight Marshal Anfortas of the Schwanenritter became Regent when King Irmin was "indisposed". He led the evacuation of Khaenri'ahn survivors up into Sumeru, where the knights and their Ruin Golems fought the Abyss until none were left. Many refugees on the surface fell to a disease that turned them into monsters. Sumeru's scholars remembered the knights as the "heroes of Dahri".

## entry: The Third Descender's curse
@keys: Third Descender's curse, Saarelainen's curse
@version: 7.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Saarelainen, the Third Descender, gave his body to become the seven Gnoses on condition that the Heavenly Principles restore Anastasya and never harm his descendants, the Khaenri'ahns. He secured the promise with a curse that would trigger if it was broken. When the Heavenly Principles destroyed Khaenri'ah, the curse took effect, and they have slept ever since (revealed in 7.1).

## entry: Purge of the Crimson Moon
@keys: Crimson Moon descendants, Crimson Moon purge
@keys2: Irmin, Vinster King, Eclipse, Rerir, Tholindis
@version: 6.1
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
Before the fall, Irmin ordered every descendant of the deposed Crimson Moon Dynasty eliminated as a threat to his line, from the direct bloodline down to distant offshoots, children included. Rerir carried out these orders as a secret agent while hiding that role from Tholindis, the woman he hoped to marry, who herself belonged to a diluted Crimson Moon branch. Some descendants survived; Arlecchino is one of the line's later heirs.
