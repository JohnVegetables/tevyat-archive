## summary
@spoiler: major
### LITE
When the gods destroyed Khaenri'ah about 500 years ago, Ronova, the Ruler of Death, laid a curse on its people. Those of pure Khaenri'ahn blood received the Curse of Immortality: they cannot die, yet their bodies and minds keep eroding. Those whose ancestry came from the lands of other gods received the "curse of the wilderness" and became monsters, hilichurls among them.
### STANDARD +
Ronova herself was defeated in 7.1, but the Curse of Immortality was not lifted: the Traveler concludes it was a permanent curse set under the Heavenly Principles' judgment, which she could not undo at will. Her separate Curse of Death over Snezhnaya ended with her Authority.

## established
@spoiler: major
### LITE
- Cast as Khaenri'ah fell, by Ronova, one of the Four Shades.
- Pure-blooded Khaenri'ahns, the "greater sinners", were made immortal; people of other gods' ancestry were turned into monsters.
- Immortal does not mean unharmed: body and soul keep eroding.
### STANDARD +
- Eide (Chlothar Alberich), whom the Traveler meets through their sibling's memories of Avidya Forest in the Caribert act (3.5), called the curse a "gift" from the gods that took away any chance of release. His son Caribert, whose mother came from Mondstadt, turned into a hilichurl.
- People with strong Khaenri'ahn blood can be recognised by their eyes. In 7.1 Mitya also sensed the "signature" of the Curse of Immortality on Dainsleif and on Pierro.
### LARGE +
- Capitano said most Khaenri'ahns knew nothing of the Five Sinners' use of Abyssal power, yet all were cursed alike.
- The Khaenri'ahns descend from the Tsaritsa Anastasya and the Third Descender Saarelainen (revealed in 7.1); Anastasya reasons that the cataclysm triggered the Third Descender's curse, which is why the Heavenly Principles sleep.
- The Schwanenritter evacuated survivors up to Sumeru and fought the Abyss there until only Anfortas remained; one of them, Ynghildr, was corrupted into a Shadowy Husk.
- Dainsleif ordered the elite guard Halfdan to protect the people of Khaenri'ah at all costs; Halfdan and the other Royal Guards became Black Serpent Knights, cursed husks, yet Halfdan kept part of his mind and the order.

## immortality
@spoiler: major
### LITE
The Curse of Immortality is laid on pure-blooded Khaenri'ahns. Capitano calls it "the curse of absolute life". They cannot die, but Dainsleif says that over time both body and soul wear down until they are nearly nonexistent.
### STANDARD +
Dainsleif is the clearest case, and he carries a ring from his brother Vedrfolnir that keeps resetting his body's condition, which has preserved him far better than other immortals such as Capitano, whose decay was severe after centuries. Even so, his mind is still affected by erosion.
### LARGE +
Eide describes his hands failing as if decaying from within, and in 7.1 Dainsleif calls Ronova the architect of his torment over the past five hundred years. Capitano put the cost in terms of endless physical torment and of witnessing the demise of everything the cursed held dear. Even so, as the First Harbinger he was considered the strongest of the Fatui, with strength said to rival a god, despite the decay. The Lord of the Night said that for those cursed with immortality, release from the mortal coil is the greatest form of peace, a reminder of what the curse withholds.
### ULTRA +
Relation to erosion. Erosion, the wearing of mind, body, will and spirit with time, is part of the world's natural order imposed by the Heavenly Principles and tied to Irminsul. The cursed Khaenri'ahns are a case where the wear continues while death is refused. How exactly the curse and erosion interact is not explained in canon.

## wilderness
@spoiler: major
### LITE
The curse of the wilderness falls on Khaenri'ahns whose ancestry belonged to other gods' lands. It turns them into monsters, including hilichurls: they lose their language and most of their memory and sink into a painful, prolonged existence.
### STANDARD +
Eide explains the rule: Khaenri'ah began as a single bloodline but welcomed any human who forsook their gods, and those people were punished with the curse of the wilderness as they fled. Caribert, whose mother was from Mondstadt, was one such case. Dainsleif confirms that the hilichurls in the Chasm are former citizens of Khaenri'ah.
### LARGE +
The wiki also records that only Khaenri'ahns with descent from surface humans can turn into hilichurls. Hilichurls are known to have existed for over a thousand years, so this curse did not create all of them. The wiki treats the curse of the wilderness as a broader divine affliction, and canon notes that hilichurl numbers surged across Teyvat after the cataclysm. Cursed hilichurls grow forgetful, wear masks, and some seek out the Chasm to wait for the end. Humanity can be partly restored: after the encounter with Vedrfolnir's Abyssal power, a second dose of medicine woke Caribert and he could speak again, and Narzissenkreuz gave an Anemo Hilichurl Rogue sentience in Fontaine.
### ULTRA +
Many Abyss Order mages, lectors and heralds are thought to have been Khaenri'ahns too. Paimon concluded from the sibling's account that the Abyss monsters were once the people of Khaenri'ah. Cleansing the curse from hilichurls is a goal of the Abyss Order and the Traveler's sibling: so that they may return to the natural cycle of life and death, or, according to Dainsleif, become the foundation of a rebuilt Khaenri'ah.

## ronova_and_the_curse
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Ronova is the Ruler of Death, one of the four gods the Heavenly Principles created. In Pierro's memory she cast the Curse of Immortality with no pity and no pleasure, only indifference, while people begged for mercy. In 5.3 Capitano used the curse against her (see Capitano's Gambit); she chose to keep it.
### LARGE +
After Ronova was defeated in 7.1, the Traveler concluded in the quest that the Curse of Immortality was permanent, laid under the Heavenly Principles' judgment, and could not be undone at her will. By contrast, her Curse of Death over Snezhnaya was her own act, and ended when Celestia took her Authority. Pierro said the feud born of the Curse of Immortality was perhaps destined never to die.
### ULTRA +
Pierro's own immortality counteracted her Curse of Death in 7.0, letting him wake sooner than others. Whether Ronova's presumed death changes anything about the curse remains unanswered.

## bearers
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Known cursed Khaenri'ahns as of 7.1:
- Dainsleif, the Bough Keeper, who wanders Teyvat and hunts the Abyss Order.
- Capitano (Thrain), First of the Fatui Harbingers, whose body now lies seated on the Throne of the Primal Fire after he merged his life force with the Lord of the Night.
- Pierro, Director of the Fatui, who in his earlier life as Eftirvit was among those cursed as he looked on Ronova.
- Eide, who is Chlothar Alberich, pure-blooded and cursed with immortality; the skeletons later found in Avidya Forest leave his end uncertain.
- Halfdan, the Black Serpent Knights and the hilichurls of the Chasm, cursed in the wilderness form.
### LARGE +
Rhinedottir and Kaeya are listed by the wiki as people who appear to have avoided the curse, but their circumstances are not explained.

## in_the_world_today
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The Abyss Order learned of a pool in the Chasm that eases the curse. In the world quest series including Requiem of the Echoing Depths (2.6), the sibling authorised an experimental device to amplify it, despite a low chance of success and a high chance of causing suffering. Dainsleif and the Traveler stopped it, with Halfdan sacrificing himself to halt the device. Dainsleif told Halfdan's spirit a comforting lie, that Khaenri'ah still stood, so that he could pass on in peace.
### LARGE +
Dainsleif regards the curse as altering reality itself and so considers the Order's attempts futile. He no longer views hilichurls and Abyss monsters as human, but he honours the guards who protected their dying kin.
### ULTRA +
Chlothar, believing the Abyssal "sinner" had answered pleas the Seven ignored, concluded that only a sinner could save another sinner and resolved to use the power of the Abyss against the gods; he went on to found the Abyss Order. The Traveler learned of this through the sibling's memories in Avidya Forest.

## accounts
### LARGE
- Dainsleif: the curse is a punishment he has endured for five centuries; Ronova is its architect. He considers the Abyss Order's attempts to cure it futile, because the curse alters reality.
- Eide: the gods' "gift", indiscriminate, robbing the people of release.
- Capitano: an injustice done to innocents, the reason he sought revenge and used the curse as a weapon.
- Pierro: the curse as a feud that was perhaps destined never to die.
- The Traveler (7.1): a ruling of the Heavenly Principles that Ronova carried out and could not cancel.

## connections
### STANDARD
See Fall of Khaenri'ah, Cataclysm, Fall of Ronova, Ronova, the Four Shades, Dainsleif, Capitano, Pierro, hilichurls, the Abyss Order, Erosion and the Heavenly Principles' dormancy.
### LARGE +
Roleplay guidance (this project's recommendation, not canon text): a cursed character should show weariness, grief and patience rather than power, and speak of the gods with bitterness. Do not state that the curse is broken, that a particular character is cured, or that all hilichurls are Khaenri'ahns. Most people in Teyvat know nothing of the curse; do not let an uninformed commoner explain it.

## entry: Dainsleif's Ring
@keys: Dainsleif's ring, ring of Vedrfolnir
@keys2: Dainsleif, Vedrfolnir, erosion, curse
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
Vedrfolnir gave his younger brother Dainsleif a ring that keeps resetting his body's condition. It is why Dainsleif looks like an ordinary man after five centuries, unlike the badly decayed Capitano, and why his memory has held up better than that of other immortals. His mind is still touched by erosion.

## entry: Capitano's Gambit
@keys: Capitano's gambit, the Captain's gambit
@keys2: Ronova, Mavuika, Lord of the Night, Thrain
@version: 5.3
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
In When All Becomes a Monument, Capitano offered his life to Ronova as the payment she demanded for Mavuika's use of her powers. Because the Curse of Immortality forbade him from dying, taking his death would break her own rule. Ronova upheld the curse; the Lord of the Night absorbed his life force and became essentially immortal. His body remains seated on the Throne of the Primal Fire.

## entry: Eide and Caribert
@keys: Eide, Caribert
@keys2: Chlothar, hilichurl, Avidya, curse
@version: 3.5
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
Eide is the name Chlothar Alberich used when the Traveler's sibling met him in Avidya Forest. He was pure-blooded and cursed with immortality; his son Caribert, whose mother was from Mondstadt, became a hilichurl and sank into torpor. A forbidden medicine had no effect at first; after Chlothar fell under the sway of Vedrfolnir's Abyssal voice in a hidden sanctuary, a second dose woke Caribert. Chlothar came to see the Abyss as a power to turn against the gods, and he went on to found the Abyss Order. In the present day Dainsleif and the Traveler find the skeletons of a man and a woman in the field there, and Dainsleif supposes that Chlothar found a way out of the curse.

## entry: Chasm Water Pool
@keys: Chasm water pool, Inverted City pool
@keys2: Chasm, hilichurls, Halfdan, Black Serpent Knights
@version: 2.6
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
In the Chasm's underground, a relic pool eases the effects of the curse. Dying hilichurls come there to wait for the end, guarded by Halfdan and other Black Serpent Knights. The Abyss Order tried to amplify its power with a device, which caused agonizing pain to those cursed. Halfdan stopped it with his life.
