## summary
### LITE
The cataclysm was a disaster about five hundred years ago that struck all of Teyvat. Abyssal monsters poured out of Khaenri'ah, the underground kingdom that lived without gods, and ravaged every nation. The gods and the Heavenly Principles destroyed Khaenri'ah. Its people were cursed: pure-blooded Khaenri'ahns became immortal, and those of mixed blood became hilichurls and Abyss monsters. Several Archons died. Canon gives it no single proper name; people call it "the cataclysm", "the disaster" or "the Dark Calamity".
### STANDARD +
The cataclysm shaped the present world. The Traveler and their twin were separated by the god Asmoday as they tried to flee it. Three Archon seats changed hands: Inazuma (Makoto to Ei), Sumeru (Rukkhadevata to Nahida) and Fontaine (Egeria to Focalors). In Snezhnaya, the Belyi Tsar died and Anastasya, the Tsaritsa, took his place. Mavuika of Natlan sealed her own life in the Sacred Flame. The Abyss Order and the Fatui both arose in response to Celestia's actions. Since the cataclysm the Heavenly Principles have been asleep. Much about the disaster is still unresolved in canon, so do not fill the gaps with invention.

## established
### LITE
Confirmed: about five hundred years ago, monsters carrying Abyssal power spread from Khaenri'ah across Teyvat. The gods and the Heavenly Principles struck Khaenri'ah and destroyed it; The Seven were summoned there. Ronova, the Ruler of Death, cursed its people. Most present-day people know little of it and treat much of it as legend.
### STANDARD +
- **Trigger:** King Irmin, the last Vinster King of the Eclipse Dynasty, named the Traveler's sibling his heir and "Savior". He then made them a vessel for unlimited Abyssal power. Five of his most esteemed subjects each took a sixth of that power and became the Five Sinners of Khaenri'ah. None of them defended their homeland when the end came.
- **Who attacked:** all of The Seven were summoned to Khaenri'ah except Greater Lord Rukkhadevata, who stayed to guard Irminsul. The skies over Khaenri'ah turned red and filled with crimson cubes, and the land was torn apart and flooded with lava.
- **The curse:** Ronova cursed pure-blooded Khaenri'ahns with immortality and turned those of mixed or foreign descent into hilichurls and monsters. Hilichurl numbers surged across Teyvat afterwards. The hilichurl archive calls the disaster a "pitch-black calamity".
- **Rhinedottir's creatures:** monsters made by "Gold" Rhinedottir, among them the dragon Durin, the Riftwolves and Bakunawa, rampaged through the nations.
### LARGE +
- **Ruin machines:** Khaenri'ah's automatons, built to fight Celestia, wandered Teyvat without masters afterwards. They gathered at ruins, hence the name "Ruin Machines".
- **Survivors:** some Khaenri'ahns escaped or resisted the curse. Dainsleif is immortal; a ring from his brother Vedrfolnir keeps resetting his body, though his mind still suffers erosion. Halfdan kept part of his mind as a Shadowy Husk. Thrain (later Il Capitano) and his men retreated to Natlan. Pierro co-founded the Fatui with the Tsaritsa. Knight Marshal Anfortas, who declared himself Regent when King Irmin became "indisposed", led the Schwanenritter in evacuating survivors up into Sumeru, where they helped fight the Abyss and were remembered as the "heroes of Dahri".
- **The twins:** the sibling witnessed Khaenri'ah's destruction, woke the Traveler and tried to leave Teyvat with them. Asmoday intercepted them, sealed the sibling first and then the Traveler, who slept for five hundred years.

## accounts
### LARGE
Witnesses and survivors disagree about blame:
- **Dainsleif** despises the gods, yet he warns the Traveler not to try to overthrow or hunt them, so as not to repeat "the failings of my forebears". He holds that he and the Five could have stopped Irmin had they worked together.
- **Pierro** (Mocking Mask artifact text) says the sages of his homeland tore away "the veil of sin" and brought divine wrath on themselves.
- **Capitano** (5.3) stresses that most Khaenri'ahns were innocent and knew nothing of the Sinners' use of Abyssal power, yet Ronova cursed them all without distinction.
- **Ei** saw "a nation stride forward and lose everything to the Heavenly Principles". The memory fed her fear and her pursuit of Eternity.
- **Chlothar Alberich** blamed the Traveler's sibling as the catalyst of the disaster: the sages believed that controlling the Abyss meant controlling everything.

- **Rhinedottir** (7.1) traced the Khaenri'ahns' misfortune further back, to the Belyi Tsar inciting Anastasya and Saarelainen to open a gate to the Abyss. That act led to Saarelainen's bargain with the Heavenly Principles and to the curse that put them to sleep when they harmed Khaenri'ah.
- **Dainsleif** (7.1) recalls that during the cataclysm the Khaenri'ahns looked up at Ronova and saw only "an untouchable god". Pierro says he saw neither pity nor pleasure in her eyes when she cast the Curse of Immortality.
- **Zhongli** is bound by a contract not to speak of the cataclysm, and the adepti will not explain why Dunyu was abandoned.

## history
### LARGE
The broad shape, nation by nation (details in the sub-entries):
- **Mondstadt:** the Knights of Favonius under Grand Master Arundolyn marched out against the monsters. Rhinedottir's dragon Durin attacked Mondstadt, and Barbatos woke and fought him with Dvalin. Durin fell on Dragonspine, and Dvalin was poisoned by his blood.
- **Liyue:** monsters poured out of the Chasm. The yaksha Bosacius, the exorcist Boyang and a Millelith vanguard sealed them in the Chasm's Bed at the cost of their lives. Dunyu was abandoned.
- **Inazuma:** Makoto, the Electro Archon, died in Khaenri'ah. Mikoshi Chiyo was corrupted, the Kitsune Saiguu was consumed by the dark forces, and Takamine vanished. Ei became Archon.
- **Sumeru:** forbidden knowledge surged into Irminsul. Rukkhadevata died purging it and left Nahida as her successor. Abyssal monsters tore open Tunigi Hollow in the desert.
- **Fontaine:** Egeria fell at Tunigi Hollow and Focalors succeeded her. The beast Elynas polluted Fontaine's land and sea.
- **Natlan:** hit hardest because of its shallow Ley Lines. Tenoch and the first six heroes held the line at what became the Mare Jivari, and the Night Warden Wars began.
- **Snezhnaya:** Nod-Krai was the first land struck after Khaenri'ah. The Belyi Tsar died leading an expedition into Khaenri'ah, and Anastasya was crowned Tsaritsa.

The disaster's roots run deep. Nibelung first brought Abyssal power and forbidden knowledge into Teyvat in the war of funerary flame. Khaenri'ah was founded as a godless nation around the time Sal Vindagnyr fell, and over centuries it built war machines meant for Celestia. More than 2,000 years ago its envoys tried to steal the forbidden book Before Sun and Moon from Enkanomiya. In Inazuma, the kitsune Urakusai did something connected with the coming Abyssal attack, and the Kitsune Saiguu banished him for it. Makoto said that Khaenri'ah was "not somewhere that any archon could afford to ignore".

## consequences
### LARGE
- **Celestia:** the Heavenly Principles fell dormant after the cataclysm and remain asleep as of 7.1. Celestia's system still functions, and the Shades still act.
- **New powers:** the Tsaritsa, embittered, cut ties with Venti (and possibly the other Archons) and founded the Fatui with Pierro to oppose both Celestia and the Abyss Order. Chlothar Alberich later founded the Abyss Order to take revenge on Celestia and The Seven. The Traveler's sibling travelled Teyvat with Dainsleif, learned "the truth of this world" and came to lead the Order.
- **Inazuma:** grief and fear drove Ei to pursue Eternity. She built the Shogun puppet, withdrew into the Plane of Euthymia, and later gave her Gnosis to Yae Miko.
- **Sumeru:** Nahida was born as the new Dendro Archon. Rukkhadevata's lingering corruption caused the Withering and Eleazar for five centuries.
- **Natlan:** Mavuika placed her life in the Sacred Flame and planned a five-hundred-year return. The Night Warden Wars kept the Abyss at bay meanwhile.

- **Fontaine:** the Oceanids refused to serve Focalors and left Fontaine. The Melusines were born from Elynas's remains.
- **Liyue:** the monster war spurred new weaponsmithing (Prototype and Blackcliff weapons) once old weapons proved unsuited to the creatures.
- **Mondstadt:** Dvalin slept off Durin's poison in Old Mondstadt. Durin's blood seeped into Dragonspine. Rostam's lover Rosalyne became the Crimson Witch of Flames to avenge him and was later recruited by Pierro as La Signora. Roland, Rostam's student, became the Bloodstained Knight and eventually pledged himself to the Abyss; his fate is unknown.
- **Nod-Krai:** the Tsaritsa's edict made it autonomous under the Lightkeepers. The Wild Hunt has haunted it ever since.
- **Snezhnaya:** many Snowland Fae nobles refused to accept Anastasya; the Dalegrad clan broke away and Flins went into self-exile.
### ULTRA +
Riftwolves, Gold's creations, dwindled after the cataclysm but have reappeared in Inazuma and Mondstadt in the present. Ei treats them as harbingers of worse to come. Some of Rhinedottir's creatures were never fully destroyed: Bakunawa's rebirth in Natlan was only suppressed, and Durin's heart is still said to beat in Wyrmrest Valley.

## present
@version: 7.1
### LITE
As of 7.1: Khaenri'ah's fate and King Irmin's are still unknown. The Curse of Immortality still binds Dainsleif and other pure-blooded survivors. The Heavenly Principles still sleep. In Snezhnaya, Ronova, who cast the curse, has lost her Authority and apparently died.
### STANDARD +
In 7.1 (Archon Quest Chapter VII Act IV), Rhinedottir revealed that the Third Descender, Saarelainen, had bound the Heavenly Principles with a curse that would trigger if they ever harmed his and Anastasya's descendants, the Khaenri'ahns. Anastasya concluded that the cataclysm triggered it, which explains the Principles' sleep. The same quest presents the Curse of Immortality as a permanent judgment laid under the Heavenly Principles' authority. Unlike Ronova's Curse of Death on Snezhnaya, it was not undone when her Authority was revoked.
### LARGE +
Natlan's half-millennium war with the Abyss ended in Archon Quest Chapter V (5.0-5.3) when Mavuika and the Traveler destroyed Gosoythoth. In Nod-Krai (6.x) the Wild Hunt, an Abyssal plague dating from the cataclysm, lost Rerir when he was driven from Teyvat; the Abyss then raised an imitation of the Bloodstained Knight Roland to lead it, and once that form expired the Wild Hunt was left leaderless.

## knowledge
### LARGE
Who knows what (for roleplay): ordinary people across Teyvat know almost nothing. Few books mention the cataclysm, and much of it is dismissed as legend. Even some Fatui fighting the Abyss doubt the conflict is 500 years old. Jean knows only the tale of Dvalin and Durin, which she learned from Diluc. The long-lived know more: Venti, Zhongli (bound to silence), Ei, Nahida, the Tsaritsa, the adepti, Dainsleif, Pierro, Capitano, Rhinedottir and other Khaenri'ahn survivors.

The Traveler has searched old books for information with little success. They learn the most from Dainsleif and their sibling's memories, and in Snezhnaya from Rhinedottir and Anastasya. In 3.2 Rukkhadevata was erased from the memory of everyone in Teyvat except the Traveler, so Sumerians no longer know that their old Archon died in the cataclysm.

## roleplay
### LARGE
Roleplay interpretation (this project's recommendation, not canon text): treat the cataclysm as deep, half-forgotten history for most people, like a war five centuries past known through ruins, monsters and folk tales. Only the long-lived and Khaenri'ahn survivors speak of it with first-hand pain. Do not let ordinary NPCs name the Five Sinners, the Third Descender's curse or Irmin's ritual. Use "the cataclysm", "five hundred years ago" or "the disaster", not a proper-noun title.
### ULTRA +
Keep moral judgment contested: Dainsleif, Pierro, Capitano, Ei, Chlothar and Rhinedottir each frame the guilt differently. A Khaenri'ahn survivor's grief is centuries old and often eroded.

## entry: Durin's attack on Mondstadt
@keys: Durin, Dvalin, Stormterror
@keys2: Cataclysm, five hundred years, Dragonspine, Rhinedottir, Gold
@version: 1.0
### STANDARD
During the cataclysm, while the Knights of Favonius were away on Grand Master Arundolyn's expedition against the monsters, the dark dragon Durin attacked Mondstadt. Rhinedottir had created him and sent him to Teyvat to "display his beauty". He seems to have been unaware of the destruction he caused. Mondstadt's people prayed, and Barbatos woke and called on Dvalin. Together they fought Durin over Dragonspine, which Barbatos chose as his burial place to contain his poison. Durin fell there and Dvalin swallowed his Abyss-tainted blood. Dvalin then slept in Old Mondstadt to recover.
### LARGE +
Rostam held the valley against the monsters and died there, relieved that his lover was safe in Sumeru. Durin's blood seeped into Dragonspine, reviving the Frostbearing Tree, and his heart is said to still beat in Wyrmrest Valley. This historical dragon should not be confused with the playable Durin of 6.x, whose own entry covers his relation to Rhinedottir's creation.

## entry: Sumeru and the desert in the cataclysm

Rukkhadevata stayed behind to guard Irminsul while the rest of The Seven went to Khaenri'ah, but forbidden knowledge surged into Irminsul and into her. Ruin machines and Riftwolves also attacked Old Vanarana, scarring the land and giving rise to Marana's Avatar, the source of the Withering in the forest. The huntress Viridescent was corrupted after avenging the Bloodstained Knight's blind brother, who was killed by monsters in Sumeru.

## entry: Natlan in the cataclysm

@keys2: Cataclysm, five hundred years, Abyss, Mavuika
@version: 5.0
### STANDARD
Natlan suffered more than any nation because its Ley Lines were shallow. Rhinedottir's creature Bakunawa wiped out Tenochtzitoc. ## entry: The twins in the cataclysm
@keys: Asmoday, unknown god
@keys2: Cataclysm, twins, Traveler, sibling, Aether, Lumine
@version: 1.0
### STANDARD
The twins had crash-landed in Teyvat. The sibling woke first, reached Khaenri'ah and was named heir, then witnessed its destruction. They woke the Traveler and tried to leave the world. ## entry: Third Descender's curse and the slumber
@keys: Third Descender's curse, Saarelainen
@keys2: Cataclysm, Heavenly Principles, slumber, asleep, dormant
@spoiler: major
@version: 7.1
### STANDARD
Long before the cataclysm, Saarelainen agreed to be split into the seven Gnoses in exchange for Anastasya's resurrection and a promise that the Heavenly Principles would never harm their descendants, the Khaenri'ahns. He bound the promise with a curse that would trigger if it were broken. Revealing this in 7.1, Rhinedottir led Anastasya to conclude that the Principles' attack in the cataclysm set the curse off. That is why they have slept for five hundred years.
