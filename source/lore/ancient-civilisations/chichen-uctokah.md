## summary
### LITE
Chichen Uctokah, called the Land of Seven Flames, was a city built by dragons in what is now Natlan. Its sovereign was the Dragonlord Ix Uxul Tz'ib Bolon Ch'ule-L, whom Paimon nicknames Ixlel. The human usurper Och-Kan later seized the city and tried to found his empire, Ochkanatlan, on it.
### STANDARD +
The dragons also built it as the base of a "stairway", more precisely a cable, meant to reach beyond Teyvat's sky, working together with the satellite later known as the Tonatiuh. Its ruler survives only as a voice trapped in a statue.
### LARGE +
Almost everything known comes from two 5.2 quests: Ixlel's own speech in a world quest, and Kukulkan's account of the cable at the Tonatiuh in the Archon Quest. Treat the city as a sketched backdrop rather than a fully mapped place.

## established
### STANDARD
- Chichen Uctokah is named by its ruler as the "Land of Seven Flames", and she calls herself its sovereign, a Dragonlord.
- Its ruler was the Dragonlord Ix Uxul Tz'ib Bolon Ch'ule-L, who gives a longer technical-sounding designation as well. Paimon shortens the name to Ixlel and the Dragonlord accepts it grudgingly.
- Och-Kan, called the usurper, tried to build his nation Ochkanatlan upon the city.
- Ixlel says that her authority was stolen and that her memories were used to entrap her. She says he forced her to serve his empire because he did not know how to run the city. Her heart was broken apart and hidden in devices.
- The empire and its delusions are said to have fallen to ash, while Ixlel remained imprisoned in a crude statue in the Old Core of Chu'ulel, her life nearly spent.
### LARGE +
- Kukulkan, speaking in the Archon Quest, describes a stairway, or better a cable, put up by the dragons to reach beyond their skies while searching for light in the dark outer realms. This was before the false sky covered the world.
- The Tonatiuh was later modified from a part of that cable, and had been meant to hang higher in the sky. Kukulkan says Och-Kan turned it around, and that Och-Kan hid a Golden Entreaty there. The city beneath it, he says, once rivalled Tollan.
- The wiki states that Chichen Uctokah served as the base of this cable and worked alongside the Tonatiuh.
### ULTRA +
- Ixlel claims her stored memories are everything she was, and that for long-lived beings such memories are chains. She asks the Traveler to gather the fragments of her heart and then remove the restored heart, so she may die free. She says her consciousness would be spent within thirty winters regardless.
- She addresses the Traveler as the "Chosen of Dragons" and mentions colleagues in rulership who are lost, implying other Dragonlords of her kind. The text does not identify them.

## accounts
### STANDARD
- Ixlel's version: a great Dragonlord betrayed and enslaved by a traitor who stole her authority. She is proud, theatrical and bitter, so her tale carries her own grievance.
- Kukulkan's version: the city and its cable belonged to the dragons' project to reach beyond the sky. He treats Och-Kan as a failed experiment in merging human and dragon, and his words serve his own plan.
### LARGE +
- Neither account says how Och-Kan took control. Ixlel gives only the memory-binding and the stolen authority. The Ochkanatlan entry covers other, more sympathetic views of Och-Kan.

## open_questions
### STANDARD
- How large the city was, who lived in it, and when it fell are not stated.
- What the Land of Seven Flames refers to (seven flames or seven districts) is never explained.
- The identity of the other lost rulers Ixlel mentions is not given.

## connections
### STANDARD
- Ochkanatlan was raised over this city; see the Ochkanatlan civilisation entry, and the Old Core of Chu'ulel where Ixlel is kept.
- The Tonatiuh, the sky-ship tied to the cable, and the Abyss Order's use of Ochkanatlan's ruins are part of the same story.
- The Thirteen Sovereign Lords and the false sky are the wider setting for the Dragonlords' attempts to reach beyond the sky.

### STANDARD
Chichen Uctokah, the Land of Seven Flames, is the dragon city that Ixlel (Ix Uxul Tz'ib Bolon Ch'ule-L) once ruled. Och-Kan seized it and built Ochkanatlan upon it. The dragons had also built it as the base of a sky cable reaching beyond Teyvat, alongside the satellite Tonatiuh. Details of the city itself are sparse in canon.
