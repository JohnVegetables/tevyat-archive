## summary
### LITE
The Tribes of Fontaine were the many chiefdoms that lived in Fontaine after Meropis was drowned and before Egeria founded the modern nation. Remuria's records call them the Northern Barbarians. Most were clan societies ruled by councils of chiefs, though some had kings, castles and wealthy cities. Under Erinnyes, a singer who ruled Aremorica, they united in the name of the Mistress of Many Waters and rebelled against the Remurian empire.
### STANDARD +
Their story is known almost entirely through Remurian documents and through the knightly songs of La Chanson d'Erinnyes, so who counts as a tribe, and how barbaric they were, depends on who is speaking. After Remuria fell, Egeria gathered the survivors of both sides and had them build new cities around springs.

## established
### LITE
- After Meropis was destroyed by huge waves and a hundred days of rain, survivors lost their inherited knowledge and lived among forests and rivers. Over time many tribes arose across Fontaine.
- Remuria, founded by the God King Remus, set out to conquer and pacify the northern chiefdoms and called them barbarians.
- Erinnyes united the northern tribes under the Mistress of Many Waters and led the resistance that ended Remuria's rule.
- Afterward Celestia ordered Egeria to return from the Primordial Sea and rule Fontaine as the Hydro Archon.
### STANDARD +
- **Social structure.** A Remurian field report by a legion officer, found in the Faded Castle, says most chiefdoms were rudimentary clan societies whose highest authority was a council of clan chiefs. It also admits that for some of them "tribe" was no longer an accurate word, since they had left primal barbarism behind.
- **Remurian conquest.** Early on the empire relied on diplomacy. The Harmost Aurelius finished a northern campaign without violence: legionnaires announced the conquest and many insular chiefdoms submitted and were absorbed. After Aremorica was taken, such easily pacified tribes became rare, and the Iron Legions met organised resistance and needed different methods.
- **Named tribes.** Aremorica; Lugdunensis; a tribe ruled by a council of astrologists; and the tribe of Erinnyes and Cassiodor (see the entries below). La Chanson d'Erinnyes also names Atrebatis, Bellouacoi, Gellone, Lyonnais, Rhenanie and Uiromanduoi; NPC dialogue names Dunnaer and Uxellobrogi; Peiregord and Aisias are named too but may have been Remurian.
- **Erinnyes's rise.** Her own tribe was massacred by a Remurian army, leaving her and Cassiodor as the only survivors. Cassiodor joined Remuria under the Harmost Boethius, while Erinnyes was taken in by the chieftain of Aremorica, a northern border tribe, to guard waters not yet seized by the God King. She later became its ruler, raised an army that united the warring tribes, and led it, the later Lochknights, in open rebellion.
- **Aftermath.** Egeria mediated the feuds of the survivors and told them to found cities around flowing springs, beginning an era of law that has lasted to the present.
### LARGE +
- **Dates.** No year counts are given in the sources read. Remuria's history was written down a century after its destruction, and the Remurian report says Aremorica had been under Remuria for two decades before the uprising.
- **Faith and oaths.** The Mistress of Many Waters became the common object of loyalty. In the knightly songs Erinnyes tells Remus's emissary that she kneels to no one but the Mistress, and the princes who accepted her did so with sworn allegiance.
- **Sack of Petrocorii.** According to a text in the Pillar Hall, Erinnyes's forces went as far as sacking Cassiodor's palace in Petrocorii, the site of present-day Petrichor.
### ULTRA +
- **Remuria's final war.** Remuria's own history says that when Boethius ordered all barbarian tribes destroyed, he plunged the empire into ceaseless war and rebellion, which ended its golden age. The same account has Remus waking to the tribes' suffering, realising his error and giving a last order, but too late. A tsunami then swept Remuria away. See the Remuria and Remus entries for that side of the story; this file concerns the tribes.
- **Two pictures of the north.** Remurian history describes the post-flood peoples as living no differently from wildlife, while the Remurian report and the knightly songs show chiefdoms with kings, castles and cities. Both pictures appear in the sources, and the sources do not reconcile them.

## accounts
### STANDARD
- **Remurian view.** The northern peoples were savages to be civilised, and their devotion to the "Mistress of All Waters" a false, even evil, faith. The Faded Castle report itself calls that picture a distortion born of the conquest of Aremorica, because its ruler forced other chiefdoms to say that all their gods were aspects of the Mistress.
- **Tribal and knightly view.** In La Chanson d'Erinnyes the lords of the north are knights who swear to Erinnyes and the Mistress of Many Waters, and who see Remus's demand for water and earth as a threat dressed as mercy.
- **Cassiodor's view.** The emissary of Remus (Cassiodor) argues that unity under one ruler is wisdom and that Remus's orders come from pity. He is a survivor of the same massacred tribe as Erinnyes, which sets their two paths against each other.
### LARGE +
The Remurian report is written by a vicarius under the authority of Harmost Euergetia, and a note in it records that the astrologists' city was razed by Boethius and its name erased. So the empire both studied the tribes and deliberately wiped some of them out of memory.

## open_questions
### STANDARD
- The astrologists' tribe has no recorded name. Whether any of its elementally gifted warriors survived is not stated.
- The full list of tribes, and where each lay, is not given.
- It is not stated how long the tribes existed before Remuria, or how their gods were worshipped before Erinnyes's unification.
### LARGE +
- How much of La Chanson d'Erinnyes is history and how much poetic embellishment is not established; it is a knightly song cycle and its place names do not all map onto known locations.
- The relationship between the pre-Remurian tribes and the unified civilisation recorded elsewhere in Teyvat lore is not explained in canon.

## connections
### STANDARD
- Erinnyes, Cassiodor, Boethius, Aurelius, Euergetia and Remus are the main named individuals touching the tribes; each has their own entry.
- The Lochknights descend from Erinnyes's army; the Mistress of Many Waters is Egeria, the earlier Hydro Archon.
- Remuria and the Sea of Bygone Eras are the setting of the empire the tribes opposed; Petrichor stands on the old Petrocorii.
- Scylla and the vishaps appear in Remurian history among the "barbarian" forces Remus feared.
### LARGE +
- Present-day Fontaine's law-centred society is presented in the Remurian history as beginning when Egeria settled the survivors' feuds and ordered cities built around springs.
- Fontaine's prophecy-centred history is a later matter; see the prophecy entry.

## entry: Aremorica
@keys: Aremorica, Aremori, Aremori Castle, Cunoricus
@version: 4.0
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
Aremorica, also written Aremori, was a northern border tribe, at first obscure. Its ruler Cunoricus voluntarily submitted it to Remuria, and it stayed under Remurian rule for about two decades. Later, after Erinnyes was taken in by its chieftain and became its ruler, Aremorica became the political centre of the resistance and the place from which the northern tribes were united in the faith of the Mistress of Many Waters. Remus's emissary is shown visiting Aremori Castle in La Chanson d'Erinnyes.

## entry: Lugdunensis
@keys: Lugdunensis, Holy King of Lugdunensis
@version: 4.0
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
Lugdunensis was a city-state and the main trade hub of the northern tribes, wealthy enough to compare with Remurian cities. Its ruler took the title Holy King rather than chieftain and held that his authority came from a sword given by the Mother of Many Springs, a claim the Remurian report mocks. The city was eventually razed by Legio V.

## entry: The astrologists' tribe

@version: 4.0
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
A tribe governed by a council of astrologists, who picked out children with an elemental affinity and trained them into warriors nearly able to rival the Remurian legions. The Harmost Boethius conquered and razed it and had its name erased from memory, so canon does not give the name.

## entry: Erinnyes's tribe and the rebellion
@keys: Erinnyes's tribe, tribe of Erinnyes, Mistress of Many Waters
@keys2: tribe, Fontaine, Remuria, rebellion
@version: 4.0
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
Erinnyes and Cassiodor came from a tribe that was among the first destroyed by Remuria, leaving only the two of them alive. Cassiodor went over to Remuria, and Erinnyes, taken in by Aremorica's chieftain, eventually led the northern tribes under the Mistress of Many Waters, who is Egeria. Her army was the forerunner of the Lochknights.
### LARGE +
The book cycle La Chanson d'Erinnyes shows her receiving the princes of Bellouacoi, Atrebatis and Uiromanduoi and then answering Remus's emissary, who asked for water and earth and submission to the Sumphonia Capitolii. She refused, ordering that the emissary not be harmed. Knights named in the song include Guillaume of Gellone, Otkerus of Rhenanie and Drest, prince of Lyonnais.

### STANDARD
