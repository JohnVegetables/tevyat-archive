## summary
### LITE
The Guili Assembly was an ancient Liyue civilisation founded and ruled jointly by the God of Dust, Guizhong, and the God of Geo, Morax (later Rex Lapis, today the mortal consultant Zhongli), with the god Marchosius and the adepti at their side. Its heart was Guili Plains north of Mt. Tianheng, a land of farms and fields. About 3,700 years ago a battle of the Archon War killed Guizhong and a flood destroyed the Assembly; Morax led the survivors south and founded Liyue Harbor.
### STANDARD +
Its name joins one character each from Guizhong's name and from the name Morax then used. Today it survives as ruins, stone tablets and the memories of the long-lived.

## established
@version: 1.0
### STANDARD
When Morax first descended, according to the tablets, he lowered the tides, raised Mt. Tianheng and calmed the seas, and the people mined jade from the mountain and built a settlement. Guizhong, called the Lord of Dust, became his ally. She and the adepti, Cloud Retainer among them, raised the Guizhong Ballista, an automatic crossbow guarding the land around Mt. Tianheng, and she brought her people north of the mountain, where they learned to farm. The fields and farming towns spread so far that one could reputedly walk to the Stone Gate along the paths between them. She named the land the Plains of the Returning and Departing (Guili Plains): her people had left their old home and, content here, had in a sense returned home. The tablets say the Lord of Rock praised her work. Marchosius, God of the Stove, is named as a third ruler and supporter in Madame Ping's retelling.
### LARGE +
The Assembly's rulers had complementary strengths. Guizhong is described as kind and wise but not especially powerful, and her own words in the Memory of Dust text say she meant to use technique and wisdom where she lacked strength, "your brawn and my brains". She is credited with four commandments: unite in ambition, let wisdom nourish like water, fortify the bones so movement stays supple, and let virtue grow tall like a tree. Her interest in machines went beyond the Ballista: she is said to have collected ancient Ruin Machines for study and to have made the Realm of Clouds in Luhua Pool. Guizhong, Morax and Cloud Retainer are said to have shared meals at Cloud Retainer's abode on Mt. Aocang. These details come from quest text as summarised by the wiki.
### ULTRA +
The Ballista's design was Guizhong's, under the name "Obscuro Vulpes Mechanism". The wiki reports from the Archon Quest that Rex Lapis judged it better than Cloud Retainer's own creation, that the two differed on ideals, and that after Guizhong died Cloud Retainer added finishing touches in her memory. A maintenance notice at the Ballista still asks that spare parts be kept for a future war and a worthy craftsman. The Memory of Dust, a stone dumbbell puzzle Guizhong gave Morax at their first meeting among Glaze Lilies, is the personal token of this alliance, even though its text insists no formal contract bound them.

## fall
@version: 1.0
### STANDARD
When the gods of Teyvat fought for dominance, the adepti defended Guili Plains but could not stop the tide of war, which ravaged the plains and killed Guizhong. A great flood followed that swept through the area, including Dihua Marsh. The people were scattered; Morax took them south of Mt. Tianheng, and with Marchosius and the adepti they travelled for ten days or more through a storm. They settled at what became Liyue Harbor, about 3,700 years ago. The plains were left a wilderness; a viewpoint description calls the place a once-thriving city where a certain flower bloomed in abundance, an earthly paradise consumed by war.
### LARGE +
The Memory of Dust text gives the human side of the end: Guizhong's form dissolved into dust among the Glaze Lilies, and she asked Morax to forget the stone dumbbell. Morax never managed to open it, and the wild lilies later dwindled until none remained. The wiki also notes that after her death yakshas can be seen containing leftover energy she expelled. A Moonlight Merriment event quest (limited-time) has Madame Ping tell how Marchosius, the God of the Stove, who loved humanity, went into homes as small helpers and stayed with the people during the flight south; this is a legend told within an event and is not common knowledge.

## legacy
@version: 1.0
### STANDARD
The Assembly is the direct predecessor of Liyue Harbor, founded by the same survivors and the same god. Guili Plains still holds ruins, stone tablets and jade plates, and the Guizhong Ballista still stands at Mt. Tianheng. The tablets record that, after Rex Lapis pacified the warring gods, merchants and craftsmen formed the Qixing and the Millelith guarded the land. Zhongli, in the present, is a living memory of this era.
### LARGE +
Because few remain who lived through it, the Assembly reaches modern Liyue mostly through texts, machines and the Memory of Dust. Guizhong has no known worship today.

## open_questions
### STANDARD
The sources reviewed leave unanswered who killed Guizhong and how, what Morax was called at the time, why Guizhong moved to Morax's settlement, and how large the Assembly really was. Treat them as open; do not supply an invented villain or battle.

## entry: Guili Plains
@keys: Guili Plains, Plains of the Returning and Departing
@version: 1.0
### STANDARD
Guili Plains, in the Qiongji Estuary area of Liyue, was the central region of the Guili Assembly. Today it is wild country with ruins, hilichurls and treasure hoarders, stone tablets and jade plates bearing writing, and a viewpoint called the Ruins of Guili. The name means Plains of the Returning and Departing.

## entry: Guizhong
@keys: Guizhong, Haagentus, God of Dust, Lord of Dust
@version: 1.0
### STANDARD
Guizhong, divine name Haagentus, was the God of Dust and co-ruler of the Guili Assembly. She appeared as a young woman with long billowing sleeves, was a mechanist who taught her people farming and built the Guizhong Ballista, and died in the Archon War.

## entry: Marchosius
@keys: Marchosius, God of the Stove, Stove God
@version: 1.0
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
Marchosius, the God of the Stove, is named as an ally who supported the Assembly and, in a Moonlight Merriment event telling by Madame Ping, as a god devoted to the people who stayed with them during the flight to Liyue Harbor. His story is told in an event and is not common knowledge.
