## summary
### LITE
Project Moongazer was the work of the Moongazers, a group of dragon researchers on the Frost Moon, who set out to keep sending into the universe the message once carried by a creature called the Stellar Blackbody. That message came from a distant world and warned against pitch-black annihilation. The project was completed by passing the Moongazers' data into the moon's core, from which new information-based beings are reborn and repeat the transmission through the Eye of Ibni-Belum. The latest of them is Eggie, in the Version 6.7 Moon Gazing quests.
### STANDARD +
Its safeguard, Project Lunar Veil, was meant to hide the moon so that the transmission would not draw a hostile listener. A reply to an earlier signal once reached the Traveler and their sibling, who were inspired by it.

## established
@version: 6.7
### STANDARD
- The Stellar Blackbody: many years after Ibni-Belum's death a second signal and coordinates were captured. Ungien flew the spaceship Moongazer to them, although the destination was too far for a safe return. On the way he met an unknown creature, the Stellar Blackbody, and chose to fuse with it so it could deliver a message to the dragons. Years later the cloaked ship crashed into the capture station. His wife Maagees-Geti was first to enter and found a round, black, spiky creature whose data signature matched Ungien's. After delivering its message it began to die.
- The message, recorded in Moongazer's Engraving, came from a distant world and warned others against pitch-black annihilation, which the wiki reads as probably the Abyss.
- The project: inspired by the message, Maagees-Geti began Project Moongazer to send it back into space and inspire other civilisations as it had inspired her. With her former student Lusalim she widened the research. Because some researchers feared the signal would invite disaster from malicious aliens, she and Lusalim also began Project Lunar Veil.
- The arrival of the Heavenly Principles: Lusalim's Lunar Veil Laboratory and generator were destroyed, and he surrendered his research to them to protect the moon's facilities. He entrusted Project Moongazer, one step from completion, to Nin-Kiggia, whose last step was to merge the data into the moon's core. She did so.
- Legacy: the beings born from the project consume each other to acquire more data until their mission is clear, then return to the moon's core, open the Moongazer module in the Eye of Ibni-Belum and send the message again. Columbina says this cycle may have occurred more than once. One signal reached the Traveler and their sibling, who did not know its origin but were inspired by it.
- The present: Eggie, a black kuuhenki born from the moon's will, is found unconscious by Columbina in Maagees's Greenhouse and slowly awakens by merging with others of her kind. With the Traveler and Paimon she collects data from terminals across the Frost Moon, receives the key from the moon's core, opens the Moongazer module and sends the message once more. Her energy runs out and she returns to the cycle of life.
### LARGE +
- Origins: Ibni-Belum, the first Moongazer, received a signal from deep space. The researchers could not decipher it, but Ibni-Belum believed Nibelung, who had once journeyed into the sea of stars, might have sent it, and built the Eye of Ibni-Belum to catch it again. The researcher Ungien thought it came from a creature beyond the stars and built Ungien's Circle and the Space Lifeform Capture Station to catch alien lifeforms. Ibni-Belum later expelled Ungien as an oath breaker.
- Blackbody Matter: Maagees-Geti studied the creature in her greenhouse as "Containment Object-1" and extracted a black, filamentous, information-bearing substance she named Blackbody Matter. Hoping to preserve what remained of Ungien, she turned it into a living being of Archaeolune Energy and Blackbody Matter, Ungie, an enormous artificial kuuhenki. Ungien's consciousness was not preserved.
- Information-based life: Lusalim came to want to engrave the full measure of a researcher's affections, desires and ideals into data and create a life-form that could be reborn from it repeatedly, so the message would be broadcast across the ages. Nu-ur-Gestug told Xiuhcoatl, the Dragon King, about the plan, and the king offered practical suggestions that were passed back to Lusalim. Lusalim succeeded in the dragon-like Nin-Kiggia and raised her as his "daughter".
- Maagees-Geti's end: she used her own body to feed Ungie, declined and fell into a coma. Her final wish was to send the Stellar Blackbody's message out through the Eye of Ibni-Belum, and the transmission was made. She also asked Lusalim to perform on her the same technique she used on Ungien, and she became the Mega Kuuhenki Maggie.
- Lusalim's plan: he planned to collect the Moongazers' ancient moon energy data and merge it in the moon's core. He asked the Moon Goddesses to lend him the primordial power of creation, and they agreed, so the Moongazers' data could be reborn as information-based life.
- Ibni-Belum, according to the wiki's note, did not approve of the Moongazers' aim and expelled them as traitors; the title "First Moongazer" was given to him afterward for paving the way.
- Moon Gazing, third act: the Traveler, Paimon, Eggie and Columbina enter a dragon-built base on the Dark Side of the Moon, where Eggie reads that the base was built by a "Lord of Darkness" as a space-borne weapon powered by the moon's core. The group suspects this is the returning Nibelung. Columbina is drawn into the core and later reappears, explaining that the last step must be done at the Eye of Ibni-Belum since Ninnin cannot survive inside the core. She retrieves the key with the Traveler.
- At the core, the Traveler and Columbina defeat a guardian called the Warden of the Flame and find the seat of the moon's will. A recording of Nin-Kiggia, called Ninnin, tells them she left an imprint to guide future inheritors, and that the Traveler has met earlier incarnations of the moon's researchers, among them Ibni-Belum, Ungien and Maagees.
- At the Eye of Ibni-Belum, Eggie accepts that sending the message may expose Teyvat to the darkness of space, and is willing to give herself up for it, echoing the earlier Moongazers who held that courage and light must persist in a dark forest universe. Columbina proposes to shield the signal as it passes the false sky with her power as a Moon Goddess and erase its traces if danger approaches. Eggie then broadcasts the names of all the Moongazers before her, and her body dissolves, leaving her hat.
- The transmission unseals a stored reply: the Traveler's sibling, thousands of years earlier, thanked the moon's people for their bravery, compared their signal to a bonfire in a dark forest and promised to find them when their own troubles were over. In the aftermath, at the Traveler's Crater ship, the Traveler tells Columbina they once received a message too corrupted by distance to make out.
### ULTRA +
- Known Moongazers (wiki table): Ibni-Belum (first Moongazer, built the Eye); Ungien (life sciences researcher, built the Circle and capture station, flew the Moongazer); Maagees-Geti (researcher, professor, mistress of the greenhouse; created Ungie); Lusalim (executor of Project Moongazer and chief architect of Project Lunar Veil); Nin-Kiggia (final inheritor, left a recording at the core); Eggie (successor who opens the Moongazer module and sends the message).
- Related terminal sites: Nuur Stone Circle (Nu-ur-Gestug's Ia-Ilimmu Storage Array, which holds the account of the Dragon King's advice), Lusalim's Laboratory, Maagees's Greenhouse, the Curtain-Form Blackbody Containment Lab and the Archaeolune Storm Research Center.
- The Lunar Veil's role in the larger plan: see Project Lunar Veil. Lusalim designed the Veil as a crucial part of Project Moongazer, and it had to be suspended after the explosion.

## accounts
@version: 6.7
### STANDARD
- Ibni-Belum: believed the signal might be Nibelung's; later refused the Moongazers' aim.
- Ungien: believed the signal came from a creature beyond the stars and went to meet it.
- Maagees-Geti: saw the message as inspiration and wished to pass it on.
- Lusalim: feared disaster from an unknown listener, but carried the work forward and eventually entrusted it to Nin-Kiggia.
- Nu-ur-Gestug: sceptical of the Lunar Veil as a stopgap.
- Eggie: willing to be consumed to complete the mission, and chooses to send it.
- Columbina: accepts the transmission with a plan to shield it.
### LARGE +
- The Stellar Blackbody message uses a dark-forest framing: a civilisation that shows its light may be hunted, yet those who send it do so out of courage and hope. Different characters stress different halves of this.

## open_questions

### STANDARD
- What created the first signal Ibni-Belum received is not stated.
- Whether the reply the Traveler and sibling got came from the earliest transmission, or another in the cycle, is not stated beyond the stored reply.
- Who or what may be listening beyond the stars is left open.
### LARGE +
- How the Moon Goddesses lent "the primordial power of creation" is described only in those words.
- The relationship of Eggie to Nin-Kiggia is read differently in different sources (see uncertainties).
