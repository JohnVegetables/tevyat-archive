## summary
### LITE
Project Lunar Veil was a research project of the dragon scholars living on the Frost Moon. Professor Maagees-Geti and her student Lusalim set out to build a veil that could hide the entire moon from outside eyes. It was meant as a safeguard for Project Moongazer, the plan to send the Stellar Blackbody's message back into space. It was never finished: the Heavenly Principles arrived, Lusalim's Lunar Veil Laboratory was destroyed, and he handed his research over to them.
### STANDARD +
The project is learned about in the Version 6.7 Moon Gazing world quests, from terminal records left across the Frost Moon. Columbina points out that a veil that hides the moon resembles the false sky. Whether one became the other is not settled by canon.

## established
@version: 6.7
### STANDARD
- Purpose: Project Moongazer aimed to transmit the message of the Stellar Blackbody into the universe. Lusalim saw no evidence that whatever received such signals would be friendly, and feared the transmission could invite disaster. The Lunar Veil was meant to hide the moon so that this risk could be reduced.
- People: it was run by Professor Maagees-Geti with her student Lusalim, with a research team that included Nu-ur-Gestug. Lusalim is named as its chief architect.
- Basis: Maagees-Geti's studies of Blackbody Matter, the black filamentous substance taken from the Stellar Blackbody creature. Matter found on the hull of the spaceship Moongazer reflected in an unusual way, which probably explains why the ship was cloaked when it hit the Space Lifeform Capture Station. Experiments also showed that Blackbody Matter could stimulate and be stimulated by Archaeolune Energy and could fuse with plants and animals.
- First stage: researchers chose Moonshade Trees compatible with Blackbody Matter. Once stimulated with Archaeolune Energy, these gave off reflective waves, and stronger stimulation formed a stable "curtain-form" containment that spread beyond the whole Curtain-Form Blackbody Containment Lab.
- Phase I: a miniature Lunar Veil was laid over Ungien's Circle, which looked empty from outside but was quite different inside. Lusalim wrote that the project name actually came from his "daughter" Nin-Kiggia, who could hardly speak at the time.
- Phase II: Maagees-Geti's health kept failing. The team at the Eye of Ibni-Belum carried out the signal transmission as her last wish. Lusalim, still uneasy about it, wanted the full-scale Veil immediately despite the much larger energy cost, and asked the Moon Goddesses for help.
- End: Kalag, the moon's Security Chief, took control of Ungien's Circle, so Lusalim continued at his Lunar Veil Laboratory in what became the Moontide Sea, where he built a huge Veil generator able to conceal the whole moon. Before it took shape the Heavenly Principles arrived; the laboratory and generator were destroyed in an explosion that created the Moontide Sea. Lusalim moved to his laboratory on the Dark Side of the Moon and eventually surrendered his research to the Heavenly Principles, probably to protect the moon's facilities from further destruction.
### LARGE +
- Reaction of colleagues: Nu-ur-Gestug suggested larger-scale trials and Maagees-Geti agreed, which led to Phase I. He later called the project an uninspired, short-term fix that would only buy time before the Dark Destroyer inevitably arrived.
- Eggie, narrating records to the Traveler and Paimon, says the success of the miniature Veil pushed the project to its next phase and that Lusalim designed the Veil as a crucial part of Project Moongazer, which had to be suspended after the explosion.
- Eggie's account of the end is that an entity from beyond the stars pierced the Veil before it could take shape, the device went haywire and the laboratory exploded. The Traveler and Columbina conclude the culprit was likely the ruler of the firmament.
### ULTRA +
- In the Moon Gazing quests Columbina compares the Veil to the false sky, and Paimon jokingly calls it a baby version. This is a character observation, not a statement of how the false sky was made.
- The sequence of locations matters in a scene: Maagees's Greenhouse and the Containment Lab (Blackbody Matter and the curtain-form stage), Ungien's Circle (the miniature Veil), the Archaeolune Storm Research Center and Kalag's grounds (the moon's security took over), Lusalim's Lunar Veil Laboratory (the full-scale generator, now the Moontide Sea) and finally Lusalim's Laboratory on the Dark Side of the Moon.
- Later, in the final act of Moon Gazing, Columbina offers to use her power as a Moon Goddess to shield Eggie's transmission as it passes through the false sky. This is a separate, later act and not part of the old project.

## accounts
@version: 6.7
### STANDARD
- Lusalim's records: cautious and fearful of the unknown listener; he treats the Veil as protection for the Moongazers' work.
- Maagees-Geti's side: she wanted the message sent out to inspire other civilisations. Her final wish was the transmission, which was carried out while the Veil was still unfinished.
- Nu-ur-Gestug: critical of the Veil as a stopgap that does not answer the coming Dark Destroyer.
- Columbina and the Traveler: see the explosion as the work of the ruler of the firmament and compare the Veil to the false sky.
### LARGE +
- Eggie reports what she reads from the records and states some parts hesitantly (she says the exact principles are unclear). Keep that hedging when she speaks of it.

## open_questions
@version: 6.7
### STANDARD
- Whether the Heavenly Principles used Lusalim's research to make the false sky is not stated. Treat Columbina's comparison as a suspicion.
- Why Lusalim gave his research to the Heavenly Principles is given only as a probable motive: to spare the moon's facilities.
- What became of Lusalim after surrendering the research is not stated in the sources read.
### LARGE +
- The exact principle by which the Veil conceals the moon is described only in terms of reflective Blackbody Matter and Archaeolune Energy.

## entry: Lusalim
@keys: Lusalim
@keys2: Lunar Veil, Moongazer, Nin-Kiggia, Frost Moon
@version: 6.7
### STANDARD
Student of Ungien and Maagees-Geti and chief architect of Project Lunar Veil and executor of Project Moongazer. He feared that sending the signal could draw disaster, built the Veil to hide the moon, raised Nin-Kiggia as his "daughter", and eventually gave his research to the Heavenly Principles.

## entry: Maagees-Geti
@keys: Maagees-Geti, Professor Maagees
@keys2: Lunar Veil, Moongazer, Blackbody Matter, Frost Moon
@version: 6.7
### STANDARD
Dragon professor and Mistress of the Greenhouse. Her research on Blackbody Matter was the basis of the Lunar Veil. Her health declined and her last wish was to send the Stellar Blackbody's message out through the Eye of Ibni-Belum.

## entry: Blackbody Matter
@keys: Blackbody Matter
@keys2: Lunar Veil, Maagees, Moongazer
@version: 6.7
### STANDARD
Black, filamentous, information-bearing substance extracted from the Stellar Blackbody creature by Maagees-Geti. It has a reflective property, interacts with Archaeolune Energy, and can fuse with plants and animals; this is what makes the curtain-form containment and the Veil possible.

## entry: Moontide Sea
@keys: Moontide Sea
@keys2: Lunar Veil, Lusalim, laboratory, Frost Moon
@version: 6.7
### STANDARD
A lunar sea on the Frost Moon that formed where Lusalim's Lunar Veil Laboratory and its generator exploded when the Heavenly Principles arrived.
