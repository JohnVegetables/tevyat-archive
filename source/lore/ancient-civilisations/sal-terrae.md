## summary
### LITE
Sal Terrae ("Salt of the Earth") was the small civilisation of the gentle God of Salt, Havria, in what is now Liyue's Bishui Plain. During the Archon War she refused to fight and gave up land to other gods again and again, until only one hidden haven remained. After some centuries, the city's first and last king killed her with a sword. Her released power turned the people nearby into salt and the place was sealed. Survivors fled to Liyue Harbor, under Morax's protection.
### STANDARD +
Its descendants include the salt-trade guild Yinyuan Hall, which keeps a version of the story that Zhongli's quest shows to be distorted.

## established
@version: 1.0
### STANDARD
The civilisation was ruled by Havria, God of Salt, who is described as the gentlest of the gods and a weak one. She would not take part in the Archon War and tried to buy peace by giving up land. According to Roald, she sheltered people dispossessed by the war, and they settled under her care and lived humbly. Zhongli adds that she searched all of Liyue for a haven for them, and the one she found is the place now called Sal Terrae. The settlement stood for some centuries, in Roald's account. By the end its territory had shrunk to a small underground refuge.
### LARGE +
Roald's diary, written as a modern traveller's reading of the ruin, says the city centred on a temple and that most of the old city seems to have sunk into the Bishui River. Havria had two relics, a bottomless salt chalice that stays half-full and a ruler that covers the ground in salt when it is driven into the earth, both found in the sealed ruin during the story quest. A tradition recorded on the wiki, not re-read in the book text, links the Flower Ball custom to her habit of handing out flowers to her people.

## fall
@version: 1.1
### STANDARD
The civilisation's first and last king killed Havria. Roald's diary says he had once loved her but sought military might and wanted to show that gentleness was unfit for the times. Zhongli tells it differently: some of her people reasoned that a quick release was kinder than being beaten, since she could not protect them. When a god dies, the power released is more than mortals can bear. Her body dissipated, leaving only a single flower of salt, and the people who could not escape were turned to salt, caught in postures of flight. The king's figure remains in the pose of the blow. The people outside the blast scattered across Liyue, and many went to Liyue Harbor, then under Morax's protection.
### LARGE +
The ruin's inner rooms show the moment frozen in place. Salinised adherents are caught running toward the way out, and in the central room a figure that once held something upraised stands over the place where Havria died. A broken sword in two pieces lay in the ruin. Zhongli identifies it as the murder weapon, not a weapon of Havria's, since she had no blade left to defend her people with. Roald's diary adds a rumour that her salt body still keeps the posture of the instant she was struck; the quest shows nothing of her but the flower.

## aftermath
@version: 1.1
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
Descendants of the survivors feared that Havria had cursed them. They returned to the ruin, broke the sword, set it with two of her relics on a pedestal, and resealed the area, hoping for forgiveness. Zhongli says she bore them no hatred and would have understood their act. Over thousands of years the story was passed down in different forms. Yinyuan Hall, the Liyue Qixing's salt trade hall, now holds that Morax murdered Havria out of envy, as stated by its member Wanyan. Other families preserve the true tale, as Roald records. A stone stele at the entrance says that salt seals the ruin and that no uninvited person may enter. In the quest the seal opens only after a mechanism and four elemental monuments are activated in the order south, east, west, north, following an old riddle about Liyue's mountains.
### LARGE +
Zhongli says Havria's fate served as a warning to him during the Archon War. At the end of the quest he tells Wanyan that faith in a god who has already died does her no good, and that this holds equally for Havria and for Morax. For ordinary scenes, Sal Terrae's people exist only as survivors' descendants and as the ruin; there is no living civilisation.

## open_questions
@version: 1.1
### STANDARD
Canon does not name the king, give the population or date the founding or fall of the city in years, beyond placing it in the Archon War several millennia ago (Roald). It also leaves open which account of the king's motive is accurate: Roald's ambitious king or Zhongli's mercy-minded followers. Canon does not describe the fate of the king beyond his frozen figure. Nothing from Nod-Krai or Snezhnaya content (Versions 6.x to 7.1) changes this entry.

## expedition
@version: 1.1
@availability: permanent
### ULTRA
The ruin was opened by an expedition to Sal Terrae in Zhongli's story quest: the Snezhnayan Fatuus archaeologist Kliment hoped for treasure, and Wanyan of Yinyuan Hall hoped for proof of Morax's guilt. Zhongli served as consultant under a contract that let them claim relics in turn, one object per claim. Kliment took the chalice and then tried to break the contract over the ruler, and Zhongli removed him from the expedition. Wanyan broke the contract over the two sword halves and was given the truth as her penalty.

## connections
### STANDARD
Related to Havria, the Sal Terrae location in Bishui Plain, Zhongli's Historia Antiqua story quest, Yinyuan Hall and the Eight Trades, Liyue Harbor, and the Archon War. Compare the Guili Assembly, another Liyue civilisation ended in the same war whose survivors also founded Liyue Harbor.

## entry: Sal Terrae Civilisation
@keys: Sal Terrae, God of Salt, Salt of the Earth, salinised adherents
@version: 1.0
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
Sal Terrae was the refuge of Havria, the gentle God of Salt, in Liyue's Bishui Plain. Her king killed her; the power released turned most of her people to salt, and survivors fled to Liyue Harbor. Its ruin is sealed and was opened in Zhongli's Sal Flore story quest (1.1).
### LARGE +
Only Havria's remains in the form of a salt flower, two relics, a broken sword and the frozen figures of her adherents are left. Yinyuan Hall descends from the survivors.

## entry: Wanyan and the Yinyuan Hall legend
@keys: Wanyan, Sal Flore, Yinyuan Hall
@keys2: Havria, Sal Terrae, Morax
@version: 1.1
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
In the story quest, Wanyan of Yinyuan Hall believes that Morax killed the God of Salt out of envy and hopes to prove it. Zhongli's evidence shows the king killed her. Wanyan cannot accept this at first; Zhongli tells her it is the price of breaking their expedition contract, and the quest leaves her struggling with it.
