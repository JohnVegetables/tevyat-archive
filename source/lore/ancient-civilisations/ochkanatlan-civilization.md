## summary
### LITE
Ochkanatlan, called the Cinder City and, in a vision, the "Eternal Empire", was an ancient human-ruled city-state of Natlan. Followers of Xbalanque, the first Pyro Archon, founded it on the ruins of a dragon city, and Och-Kan, a half-dragon "Python King", became its sovereign. He turned into a tyrant, and was overthrown by a scarlet-eyed youth and his companions, who then became the second Pyro Archon. The city was abandoned, and today its ruins on an island north of Quahuacan Cliff are considered cursed, fouled by the Abyss and guarded by a dragon that was once Och-Kan's own body.
### STANDARD +
It features in the 5.2 world quest series Lost Traveler in the Ashen Realm, in the Scroll of the Hero of Cinder City artifact set, and in the Archon Quest as a base of the Abyss Order. Its ruins and the sky-ship Tonatiuh draw adventurers with a legend of treasure and a "Jade of Return" said to repel the Abyss. That legend is a lure.

## established
@version: 5.2
### STANDARD
- Founding: Ochkanatlan was built thousands of years ago by followers of Xbalanque, with Och-Kan as its king. It stood on the ruins of Chichen Uctokah, a city of dragons once ruled by the Dragonlord Ix Uxul Tz'ib Bolon Ch'ule-L (Paimon shortens her name to Ixlel).
- Seizure of the dragon city: Och-Kan took Ixlel's authority and imprisoned her in a statue inside the Old Core of Chu'ulel. The city's Source Mechanisms answered only to draconic power, but Ixlel refused to operate them for him.
- A new core: being half-dragon, Och-Kan chose to bind his own draconic half and placed his mind inside a new Core of Chu'ulel, a power source meant to serve humans alone. His body and mind were thereafter separate.
- Fall: the Scarlet-Eyed Youth and his party overthrew Och-Kan, after which the city was abandoned. The first-glimpsed city is remembered as having palatial courtyards and noble feasts.
- Aftermath: Kukulkan turned Och-Kan's body into an Abyssal conduit, the Dragon of the City of Flowing Ash, and spread the legend of the Tonatiuh's treasure to lure adventurers in.
- Present: the tribes forbid entry. The Flower-Feather Clan flies patrols over it, and the Saurian Relics Association avoids it after an investigating group vanished.
### LARGE +
- The Eternal Empire: in Revelations from the Past (5.2) the Traveler touches a stone slate and hears a voice proclaim "Glory, glory, the Eternal Empire, Ochkanatlan" while one of two Golden Entreaties lies in shadow under a dragon's raised wings. This is how the Traveler learns to look for the second Entreaty in the ruins.
- Layout and remains: Khaenri'ahn Ruin Machines, including disabled Aeonblight Drakes, litter the island, and different animals and Saurians occupy different parts of it. Beneath it lie the Core of Chu'ulel, once the power source of the whole city, and the deeper Old Core of Chu'ulel. After the Traveler cleanses the Abyssal energy in the Palace of the Vision Serpent, the core loses the last of its vitality and only a piece of Pyrophosphorite remains.
- The Tonatiuh: originally a dragon-built satellite anchoring a stairway or cable that rose from Chichen Uctokah beyond Teyvat, it was turned on its side after Och-Kan seized the city. The Jade of Return aboard it is really a Golden Entreaty.
- Abyss Order base: in the Archon Quest act A Space and Time for You (5.7), the Scions of the Canopy locate the Order's headquarters at Ochkanatlan. The Traveler and Dainsleif fight through it and meet an Iniquitous Baptist inside.
### ULTRA +
- Quest placement: the Lost Traveler in the Ashen Realm chapter has Act I City Buried By Ash, Act II A Dream of Gazing Upon the Distant Sky, Act III The Tonatiuh Quivers (with Moment of Awakening) and the epilogue The Chosen One's Promise. Open Your Heart to Me, a separate world quest, frees Ixlel in the Old Core.
- The Old Core quest lets the Traveler return Ixlel's heart fragments and free her from her bindings after millennia of captivity. She still speaks in the old sovereign's grand manner and names herself sovereign of the Land of Seven Flames (Chichen Uctokah).
- Evidence of Abyssal decay: notes in the area show that by the time of the adventurers the city was already corroded and some people had been replaced by Tenebrous Mimiflora in disguise.

## accounts
@version: 5.2
### STANDARD
- Tribal memory: Ochkanatlan is remembered as a tyrant's city where Saurian sympathisers were persecuted and the tribes had to hand their authority to the Grand Alliance. The Scarlet-Eyed Youth, from the Flower-Feather Clan, was banished by his elder for sympathising with Saurians and later led the revolt.
- Och-Kan's own memories (shown in Moment of Awakening): he saw himself as protecting humans and Xbalanque's legacy. He banned live sacrifice and worship of the Night Kingdom, used the Turnfire against opponents and broke Xbalanque's commandments on peaceful coexistence with dragons.
- Kukulkan's version: he reproached his son for breaking Xbalanque's oath and opening the door to malevolent forces, and demanded the Golden Entreaty, which Och-Kan said he had hidden. Kukulkan warned he would come back to collect Och-Kan's soul.
### LARGE +
- Yupanqui, Och-Kan's friend, disagreed with his rule but accepted a post as ordnance officer in the Grand Alliance, before the Scarlet-Eyed Youth persuaded him to join the rebels.
- The Ochkanatlan Adventure Team (unofficial name) went up about ten years after the Cataclysm, to find a weapon said to defeat the Abyss. Its members came from several tribes; only the young mystic Liriwu returned. Bona, daughter of the leader Taika, followed a couple of decades later and died too, fighting Kukulkan.
- Och-Kan's last words as the dragon: after the fight he said the Entreaty is "as important as it is dangerous", gave it to Little One, whom he compared to himself as a bearer of the Flamelord's Blessing, and shared memories with the Traveler before dying. His spirit returned to the Night Kingdom and later guided the Traveler through its corroded depths.

## open_questions
@version: 5.2
### STANDARD
- Exactly when Ochkanatlan fell and how long it stood is not stated.
- What Och-Kan unearthed that made him burn the excavation is never named.
- Why Kukulkan wanted adventurers' souls and what his "experiment" finally achieved is given only in part, through the wiki's reading of the quests.
### LARGE +
- The wiki notes the Scarlet-Eyed Youth asked Chief Sakkuk to remove his name from history, so his real name is not recorded.
- Whether the Abyss Order's headquarters here was destroyed or abandoned after the Traveler's visit is not made clear in the summary used for this entry.

## connections
@version: 5.2
### STANDARD
- Founder-god: Xbalanque, the first Pyro Archon, who left commandments on coexistence that Och-Kan broke.
- Ruler: Och-Kan, son of Kukulkan; also known as the Sacred Lord and Python King.
- Conqueror: the Scarlet-Eyed Youth, who became the second Pyro Archon.
- Parent site: Chichen Uctokah, the dragon city beneath it, and the Tonatiuh above it.
- Later users: the Abyss Order, who made a base here.
### LARGE +
- Tribes: the Flower-Feather Clan and Scions of the Canopy watch the ruins; the Grand Alliance of tribes arose in its era.
- Artifact sets: Scroll of the Hero of Cinder City (the Ochkanatlan Adventure Team) and Unfinished Reverie (the youth's rebellion).

## entry: Och-Kan, the Python King
@keys: Och-Kan, Python King, Sacred Lord, Cocouik
@keys2: Ochkanatlan, Cinder City
@version: 5.2
### STANDARD
Son of the dragon Kukulkan with a human soul, raised among tribes and then made a test subject by dragons in his childhood. He served Xbalanque and, after Xbalanque entered the Sacred Flame, ruled Ochkanatlan as the Sacred Lord. His rule became tyrannical. His mind lived on in the Core of Chu'ulel and woke in the form of Cocouik, the companion of the adventurer Bona; his body became the fell dragon of the city. He regained himself and died in Moment of Awakening.

## entry: Dragon of the City of Flowing Ash
@keys: Dragon of the City of Flowing Ash, Tainted One, fell dragon
@keys2: Ochkanatlan, Och-Kan, Tonatiuh
@version: 5.2
### STANDARD
The Abyss-tainted body of Och-Kan, the guardian that attacks everyone entering the ruins. Kukulkan made it an Abyssal conduit, and its victims' souls were used for masks. It stole the Jade of Return from the Traveler in Lost Traveler in the Ashen Realm and was ended in the Moment of Awakening fight.

## entry: Ochkanatlan Adventure Team
@keys: Ochkanatlan Adventure Team, Jade of Return, Liriwu, Taika
@keys2: Ochkanatlan, Tonatiuh
@version: 5.0
### STANDARD
An unofficial name for the adventurers, drawn from several tribes, who went to Ochkanatlan after the Cataclysm hoping to find a weapon against the Abyss. The Tonatiuh was not what they thought and most perished. Only Liriwu came home. Their stories appear in the Scroll of the Hero of Cinder City artifact set.

## entry: The Tonatiuh
@keys: Tonatiuh, Skyserpent Ship, Legendary Tonatiuh
@keys2: Ochkanatlan, Chichen Uctokah
@version: 5.2
### STANDARD
A sky-faring ship built by dragons as a satellite for a stairway beyond Teyvat, later modified by Och-Kan. Rumoured to be full of treasure, a legend fabricated by Kukulkan. In truth it holds Pyrophosphorite and the hidden Golden Entreaty.

## entry: Ixlel in the Old Core
@keys: Ixlel, Ix Uxul Tz'ib Bolon Ch'ule-L, Old Core of Chu'ulel
@keys2: Ochkanatlan, Chichen Uctokah, Och-Kan
@version: 5.2
### STANDARD
The Dragonlord who ruled Chichen Uctokah before Och-Kan took her authority. She was trapped in a draconic statue and her heart was split so her strength could power the city. The Traveler frees her in the quest Open Your Heart to Me.
