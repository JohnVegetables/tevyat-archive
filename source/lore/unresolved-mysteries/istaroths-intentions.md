## summary
### LITE
Istaroth, the Ruler of Time, is the Shade that few remember, and she has repeatedly nudged history: the Nameless Bard, Zibai's soul, Enkanomiya's sun, Makoto's tree, and the meeting that made the Traveler's sibling leave Khaenri'ah. Canon never tells us what she is trying to achieve. The only reason she gives on screen is that she needs to pass the time.
### STANDARD +
Her interventions help individuals and sometimes work around the Heavenly Principles' wishes, yet she also warns people not to defy them. She is not shown as an opponent of Celestia or as a loyal servant. Treat her goals as unknown.
### LARGE +
The pattern is that she acts quietly and often after a disaster, in ways others only notice later, leaving the people she touched to carry the consequences.

## established
### LITE
- In the teaser The Gods' Limits (2025-07-09), Ronova asks why Istaroth bestowed history upon "them". Istaroth answers that she had to find some way to pass the time.
- In A Space and Time Without You (5.7), the Traveler's sibling concludes that Istaroth made possible the meeting between the Traveler and the Khaenri'ahn girl Direidyth, which led the sibling to abandon Khaenri'ah to seek the Traveler. The sibling asks whether she is mocking them.
### STANDARD +
- Zibai: Istaroth gave her a Moon Wheel, making her a Moon Envoy, and warned her that blocking the Celestial Nail at Lang-Gan would cost her life. Zibai chose to protect her people anyway. Istaroth then held her at the moment of death and placed the fragments of her soul in her three selves, Ziju, Zizhi and Zijiao.
- Enkanomiya: after the island sank and its prayers went unanswered by the other Shades, Istaroth alone answered and gave Aberaku the knowledge for the artificial sun later called the Dainichi Mikoshi. She became Tokoyo Ookami there.
- Mondstadt: according to the wiki, the Nameless Bard played a melody that belonged to her at Decarabian's fall, and she preserved his lost name, Venti, when Barbatos ascended.
- Inazuma: Ei suspects that Makoto's plan to plant the Sacred Sakura through time involved a higher power; the wiki identifies it as Istaroth.
- Sumeru: in the scene where Nahida burns Irminsul (6.6), Istaroth appears with Ronova and Naberius and questions Nahida before they vanish.
### LARGE +
- Hyperborea honoured her at winter solstice, and her worship survives in Mondstadt only in phrases about the "winds of time". The wiki says the Khaenri'ahn girl Direidyth was caught in a superimposition of Khaenri'ah over Natlan that Istaroth created.
- Wiki readings of artifact texts hold that she drew nostalgic delusions toward King Deshret after the death of Nabu Malikata; this is an interpretation, not a statement.
- In 7.1, Naberius says the idea that Asmoday has a new master was probably something Istaroth thought of on a whim, and that she barely considered it.

### ULTRA +
- The wiki says she once taught the Frost Moon goddess Canon the meaning of "hope", telling her that only humans can claim it and that where life exists, hope remains. This comes from a wiki summary I could not trace to a transcript, so treat it as medium confidence.
- Mondstadt's sundial inscriptions about stories carried by wind and grown by time are read by the wiki as her legacy. They are not an explicit statement of intent.

## accounts
### STANDARD
- **Istaroth herself**: gives boredom as her reason (teaser). She advises Ronova that hunting for Asmoday would do more harm than good, and tells her Pierro is watching.
- **The Traveler's sibling** (5.7): treats her as the architect of the meeting with Direidyth and asks if she is mocking them.
- **Ei** (wiki summary): believes Makoto's plan relied on Istaroth.
- **Wiki** (Profile): says she considers every life equal in value and importance, and that it is unclear whether she acts out of favour for mortals or boredom.
### LARGE +
- **Naberius** (7.1): dismisses her ideas as whims.
- **Enkanomiya's account** (wiki): she was the only Shade who answered when the Heavenly Principles did not.
- **Zibai's story** (wiki): Istaroth warned her, then froze the moment of her death; the sequence is the clearest case of advice followed by mercy.

## entry: Direidyth and the Key
@keys: Direidyth
@keys2: Istaroth, key, Khaenri'ah, sibling
@version: 5.7
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
Direidyth was a Khaenri'ahn girl from the time of the Cataclysm who believed in the Savior. According to the wiki, she became caught in a superimposition of Khaenri'ah over Natlan created through Istaroth when the sibling activated the Loom of Fate. She met the Traveler there, and her account to the sibling in her own time led the sibling to leave Khaenri'ah to find the Traveler. In A Space and Time Without You, the sibling says Istaroth must have had a hand in it and asks whether she is mocking them. No reason is given.

## entry: Zibai's Last Hour

### STANDARD
Istaroth gave Zibai a Moon Wheel and later warned her that blocking the Celestial Nail over Lang-Gan would defy the Heavenly Principles and cost her life. Zibai blocked it so that her people could flee. Istaroth then froze her at the moment of death and sealed her soul in Ziju, Zizhi and Zijiao. The reason for the rescue is not given.
