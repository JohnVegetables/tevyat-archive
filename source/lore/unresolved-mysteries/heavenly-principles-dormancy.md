## summary
### LITE
The Heavenly Principles, the ruler of Celestia and First Descender, have been dormant since the Cataclysm in Khaenri'ah about 500 years ago. The best-supported explanation is a curse laid by the Third Descender, Saarelainen, which triggered when they harmed his descendants the Khaenri'ahns. Their rules and Celestia's machinery still operate, and nobody in canon knows whether they will wake.
### STANDARD +
Powers across Teyvat have used the silence: the Fatui and the Abyss Order both plot against Celestia, and the Shades act cautiously "within the rules" rather than freely. The cause is not fully confirmed in-game: the 7.1 account is partly an inference voiced by Anastasya.

## established
### LITE
- About 500 years ago, during the Cataclysm in Khaenri'ah, the Heavenly Principles became involved, sending the Seven (except the Dendro Archon) and the Four Shades. Ronova cursed pure-blooded Khaenri'ahns with immortality, which the Traveler in 7.1 reads as done under the Heavenly Principles' judgment.
- Since then they have lain dormant, and no quest shows them acting directly.
### STANDARD +
- Earlier, the Heavenly Principles consolidated their rule after the War of Funerary Flame by sacrificing the Third Descender, Saarelainen, whose body became the seven Gnoses and whose existence was erased from memory.
- According to the wiki's account, Saarelainen bargained for protection of his descendants, the Khaenri'ahns, and cast a curse to guard the deal: should the Heavenly Principles harm them, they would fall into slumber.
- In 7.1 Anastasya concludes that the Cataclysm triggered that curse, which is why the Heavenly Principles are asleep. Several events since (the Hydro throne's destruction, the burning of Irminsul) have not roused them.
### LARGE +
- The dormancy is not total inactivity. Ronova says that during the slumber Celestia only monitors the Shades' actions periodically, and in 7.1 a resurrection that came too late was detected and her Authority of Death was reclaimed. The Celestial Nails remain in place; after the dying Ronova fused the Eitr with Snezhnaya's Nail of Retribution, the other Nails glitched and emitted pillars of light.
- In the teaser The Gods' Limits, Ronova suggested to the other Shades that, given the Heavenly Principles' prolonged inactivity, they should intervene against the Tsaritsa and the Fatui within the extent the "rules" permit.
### ULTRA +
- Because the Heavenly Principles built and wield the laws of Teyvat (including fate anchored in Irminsul), their absence from direct decisions leaves a gap that human and divine schemes alike target, from the Tsaritsa's campaign to Abyss Order plots.
- Rhinedottir and Ronova colluded to revive victims of the Curse of Death between monitoring periods, which shows that the rules are enforced mechanically even when their author sleeps.

## accounts
@version: 7.1
### STANDARD
- Wiki summary of the 7.1 quest Black Feathers Scattered at Parting: the Cataclysm intervention triggered Saarelainen's curse, so the Heavenly Principles sleep.
- In that quest Anastasya, the Tsaritsa, infers the link after hearing Rhinedottir's account ("That's why the Heavenly Principles are asleep"); Rhinedottir answers that it was "just a story" and that she was not answering any question.
### LARGE +
- Ronova, in 7.1, says that failing other means she can simply bide her time until the Heavenly Principles awaken, so the Shades themselves expect the slumber to end. Anastasya asked Rhinedottir directly when they will awaken and was refused an answer.
- Anastasya tells the Traveler that the gods' love for humanity is the result of a curse by the Heavenly Principles, part of the rules they impose on the world, an example of how characters frame those rules.
### ULTRA +
- In a memory shown in Light and Shadow, Intertwined, Naberius says the Shades "must face the reality that the Heavenly Principles slumbers" and must eliminate risks within the established rules, which presents the slumber as a constraint on the Shades as well as an opening for their enemies.

## open_questions
### STANDARD
- Will the Heavenly Principles wake, and what would break the curse?
- What exactly did Saarelainen promise, and what is in his sealed legacy (see the Third Descender's seal)?
- Which functions of Celestia run without its ruler?
### LARGE +
- Is the sleep a punishment, a safeguard, or a side effect of the curse? Canon does not say.
- Whether the Tsaritsa's plan assumes the Heavenly Principles stay asleep or wake is unrevealed. Do not resolve these questions in narration.

## connections
### STANDARD
The slumber links the Cataclysm and the fall of Khaenri'ah, the origin of the Gnoses, the Tsaritsa's campaign against the Heavenly Principles, the dealings of the Four Shades, and the Traveler's sibling's long-term goal.

## entry: Heavenly Principles' slumber
@keys: Heavenly Principles dormant, Heavenly Principles asleep, slumber of the Heavenly Principles, Heavenly Principles awaken
@version: 7.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
After the Cataclysm about 500 years ago the Heavenly Principles stopped acting directly. The wiki attributes this to Saarelainen's curse, triggered when they harmed Khaenri'ah. Anastasya only infers the link in 7.1 and Rhinedottir does not confirm it. The Shades call this period a slumber, during which Celestia only monitors them periodically. Celestia's rules and monitoring still run, so characters speak of the slumber as real but incompletely understood. Never show the Heavenly Principles waking unless a scene is explicitly non-canon.
### LARGE +
Ronova expects the Heavenly Principles to awaken eventually, and Anastasya wants to know when. Factions such as the Fatui and the Abyss Order act on the assumption that now is their chance.

## entry: Saarelainen's curse
@keys: Saarelainen's curse, Third Descender's curse, curse of the Third Descender
@keys2: Heavenly Principles, Celestia, Khaenri'ah, Khaenriah
@version: 7.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Per Rhinedottir in 7.1, Saarelainen laid a curse on the Heavenly Principles when he bargained his body for Anastasya's resurrection and a promise never to harm his descendants, the Khaenri'ahns; it would take effect only if the promise was broken. The wiki says its effect is slumber, and Anastasya infers the Cataclysm triggered it. Its precise terms are not given in canon as of 7.1.
