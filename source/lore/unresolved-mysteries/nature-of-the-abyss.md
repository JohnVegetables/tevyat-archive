## summary
### LITE
The Abyss is a dark, destructive power that comes from the sea of stars beyond Teyvat. It corrupts living things, spawns monsters and wants to force its way into the world. What it fundamentally is remains unresolved as of 7.1. The nearest thing to a definition is the Hexenzirkel's reading, relayed by Alice: its essence is the hatred carried by all the fates that will not come to pass.
### STANDARD +
Do not confuse the Abyss with the Abyss Order. The Order is a group of cursed Khaenri'ahns and monsters who use Abyssal power. Mavuika calls the Abyss itself a far greater threat.

## established
### LITE
- The Abyss lies beyond the world the Heavenly Principles shaped. Canon calls it a power from the sea of stars and says it also exists on other worlds.
- It was brought into Teyvat during the war of funerary flame, according to the Abyss page's reading of quest and artifact text. Nibelung is blamed for bringing it in.
- It corrupts. Long exposure changes a person's nature or rots them, and large doses kill. The Traveler is immune and can cleanse Abyssal energy to a degree.
### STANDARD +
- Wiki summaries of quest text describe a Void Realm (the Abyss), a Human Realm (Teyvat, under the Heavenly Principles) and a Light Realm. The Abyss is said to be diametrically opposed to the Light Realm's power and to corrupt Human Realm beings, and its dark currents are likened to ley lines and elemental currents.
- Elemental beings, such as vishaps and dragons, are especially harmed by it. Humans who stay near it for long decay.
- Abyss monsters can use elemental power without Visions. Khaenri'ahn practitioners such as Skirk and Surtalogi use a technique called the Seven-Shifting Serpent (originally "Chaos Permeation") to make Abyssal power pass for elemental energy.
- Time passes differently inside it. Tartaglia spent about three months there and returned after only three days in Teyvat, and Skirk does not dispute Paimon's guess that a month there is a day outside.
### LARGE +
- The Five Sinners of Khaenri'ah took the Abyss's power for themselves. The Abyss-linked monsters that devastated Teyvat in the Cataclysm were made by Rhinedottir, "Gold."
- In Natlan the Abyss adapted to each tribe and escalated as Mavuika's plan advanced, with Gosoythoth at the head of the 5.x invasion. It was beaten back by the Traveler, Mavuika and the souls of the fallen. Mavuika separates "the true Abyss" from the human organisation that bears its name (5.7).
- Nod-Krai has the Wild Hunt, led by Rerir, who is one of the Five Sinners. The Abyss imitated Roland to revive the Wild Hunt after Rerir's defeat. In the same region Kipumaki Cliff is described as the most heavily corrupted place.
- Nahida has said forbidden knowledge is a manifestation of the Abyss that hit Sumeru hardest. Greater Lord Rukkhadevata is said to have confirmed that it came from the very bottom of the Abyss, which does not belong to Teyvat.
### ULTRA +
- In the 7.0 notes of the Belyi Tsar, the Shadow Realm created by Anacharsis is described as a film of oil between Teyvat and the Abyss. When the Tsar opened the "passage" through it, a gash in time let the Abyss seep in without end. The Shadow Realm itself was untouched, since it "teems with possibilities yet unobserved". He suspected the Abyss and Shadow Realm repel one another.
- The Tsar also wrote that the Heavenly Principles confined the light's boundless possibilities to a narrow band in the name of certainty, and that "whenever we lay down an anchor into fate, the Abyss will naturally lay an anchor into us at the end of time". His fuller conclusion, that a few centuries remain, is covered under accounts.

## accounts
### STANDARD
- **Alice and Barbeloth (6.4 event):** the Hexenzirkel has watched the Abyss's energies for a long time. In Alice's words, Barbeloth's reading is the most accurate. Every event that happens means a different possibility is gone, and those lost fates carry hatred. As an example Alice names Rhinedottir tapping the Abyss, which ended the tale of a future where she stayed pure. This hatred does not seek existence but the destruction of everything, and the Abyss is obsessed with entering the world at all costs. Alice also calls it cunning and obsessive, and jokes that it has "quite the victim complex".
### LARGE +
- **Paimon, listening:** summarised it as hatred from not being chosen. This was a lighter and more personal gloss, not an additional fact.
- **Belyi Tsar (notes, 7.0):** treats the Abyss as something that anchors into a world that narrowed its own possibilities, not as an invader from nowhere. He holds that the Abyss will end everything under the false sky within a few centuries, and says Rotwang's calculations agree.
- **The Traveler:** after hearing Alice, reflects that fighting the Abyss many times has made them agree that existing at all is a spark of luck. During Project Moongazer (6.7) the Traveler also worries that a transmission might draw "the darkness that dwells amidst the stars" to the moon.
### ULTRA +
- **Item text (1.0, PlayStation-exclusive):** the Sword of Descension's description speaks of facing a "star-devouring darkness" that ends worlds. The Wings of Descension text names a "boundless darkness" and "all-consuming radiance" as forces that can destroy "one, or even two worlds".
- **Wiki readers:** note that Neuvillette's Vision text, Aksinya's talk of a world of love run dry, and Barbeloth's line all describe the world's original order turning into resentment. They infer the Human Realm's creation produced the Abyss in Teyvat. No quest confirms this.

## open_questions
### STANDARD
- What is the Abyss at its root? Is it a force, an enemy, a will, or the leftover of possibilities removed from a world?
- Why does it seek only destruction? Alice describes this as hatred, but not where the hatred is aimed.
- What is the "true Abyss" that Mavuika contrasts with the Abyss Order, and is the Order's power the same as its power?
### LARGE +
- Did the Abyss end the Traveler and their sibling's world? Item text says a "star-devouring darkness" did, but no scene names it.
- Does the Abyss answer to anything? It adapts, speaks in Natlan, and imitates figures such as Roland, which suggests a mind, but canon has not said whose.
- If the Tsar is right, how long does Teyvat have?
### ULTRA +
- How can Hexenzirkel astrology see the Abyss's source in "unchosen fates" while the Belyi Tsar sees an anchor laid by the Principles? Are these the same claim?
- The Abyss destroyed Throgg, a world known only from a message the Moongazer Nin-Kiggia received. It is unclear whether the whole planet or only its civilisation ended.

## connections
### LARGE
The Abyss runs through the Abyss Order and its Loom of Fate, the Cataclysm and the Curse of the Khaenri'ahns, the Five Sinners, Natlan's wars and the Night Kingdom, the Wild Hunt of Nod-Krai, and forbidden knowledge in Sumeru. Cosmologically it is tied to the Light Realm, the Shadow Realm, the false sky and the Celestial Nails, which the Heavenly Principles used to burn away Abyssal corruption. For the Traveler it bears on the destruction of the twins' homeworld and on what the sibling has been doing with Abyssal power. See the twins' origin and the sibling's goal entries.
### ULTRA +
Individuals tied to the Abyss include Skirk, who lived within it and trained Tartaglia there, and Tartaglia, whose time in the Abyss changed how conflict follows him. Other characters met its power through Dvalin's corrupted blood, Chiyo's "beast of sin", and the sealed Enkanomiya.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay guidance (this project's recommendation): treat the Abyss as dangerous and unexplained. A character may say what they actually know or believe, and should say who told them. Most people in Teyvat have only met Abyss monsters and the Abyss Order. Only Hexenzirkel members and a few others have words for the Abyss's essence. Do not turn the Hexenzirkel reading into a settled fact in the mouth of someone who has no way of knowing it.
### LARGE +
Who could plausibly speak to the Abyss's nature: Alice and Barbeloth (hexenzirkel), the Belyi Tsar (dead; only his notes survive), Skirk, the Natlan leaders for their own war, and the Traveler for what they have seen. Dainsleif and Abyss Order members know it as a power they use, not a theory. Never have a character state who or what the Abyss "really" is.
### ULTRA +
Keep a clear line between three different things: Abyssal power, which can be used by individuals and corrupts them; the Abyss Order, a human faction; and the Abyss itself, the cosmic source nobody has explained. Characters often blur these in speech. That is fine, but do not make narration blur them.

## entry: The Abyss's essence
@keys: essence of the Abyss, hatred of unrealized fates, fates that will not come to pass, unchosen fates
@version: 6.4
@availability: limited
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
In the Homeward, He Who Caught the Wind event (6.4), Alice tells the Traveler that the Hexenzirkel has long studied the Abyss and that she finds Barbeloth's interpretation most accurate: its essence is the hatred carried by all the fates that will not come to pass. She says every event removes alternatives, from a lunch choice to Rhinedottir's decision to take Abyssal power, and that the Abyss resents being unchosen. She adds that it seeks the end of everything, not existence, and urges not to lose sleep over "what ifs".
### LARGE +
The context was an accident in Alice and Nicole's magic bubble, a recreation of the past in which a nascent form of Abyssal power developed with no outside source. Alice said that some added variables had let a certain number of "unchosen fates" come close to reality. The Abyss "showed its teeth", and the bubble's Dvalin was corrupted.

## entry: Abyss versus Abyss Order
@keys: true Abyss, Abyssal power
@keys2: Abyss Order, Mavuika, Natlan
@version: 5.7
@spoiler: minor
### LARGE
Mavuika tells the Traveler in 5.7 that the Abyss Order is a smaller threat than "the true Abyss" Natlan's warriors have just beaten, because the Order cannot replenish its forces endlessly. The Order is made of former Khaenri'ahns and monsters who wield Abyssal power. Its members are not the Abyss itself.

## entry: The Belyi Tsar's notes on the Abyss
@keys: Notes by a Certain Noble Individual, Shadow Realm passage, anchor into fate
@version: 7.0
@spoiler: major
### ULTRA
The notes at the Pale Crown Palace record the Belyi Tsar's conclusions after many expeditions into the ruins of Hyperborea. The Shadow Realm acts as a film between the world and the Abyss. His opening of the passage tore a hole in time, and the Abyss has been seeping in since. He also suggests that whenever people anchor themselves in fate, the Abyss will anchor in them at the end of time. He writes that the Abyss's seepage will end everything under the false sky within a few centuries, and that Rotwang has reached a similar figure independently.

## entry: Time in the Abyss
@keys: time in the Abyss, three months in the Abyss
@keys2: Tartaglia, Skirk
@spoiler: minor
### LARGE
Tartaglia fell into a hole at fourteen and lived about three months in the Abyss, learning from Skirk, only for three days to have passed in Teyvat. In the Skirk story quest (Sinister Undercurrent) Paimon asks whether a month there is a day outside and Skirk does not contradict her. Takamine the Mistsplitter, in weapon lore, returned from it to find Asase Hibiki no longer young. The wiki notes it is unclear whether the ratio is fixed.
