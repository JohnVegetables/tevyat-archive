## summary
### LITE
At the end of the Chapter VII, Act IV Archon Quest (7.1), the dying Ronova fused the Eitr with the Nail of Retribution above the Cryo Dragon's corpse in Snezhnaya as a "parting gift". The Nail began to crumble, threatening to unleash Holy Soil upon all of Teyvat, and every other Celestial Nail began to shine with a strange pillar of light. As of 7.1 nobody knows what comes next.
### STANDARD +
Characters in Snezhnaya, Mondstadt, Liyue, Inazuma, Sumeru and Natlan are watching their own Nails. Fontaine has none, and so no mark of its own.

## established
@version: 7.1
@spoiler: major
### LITE
- Ronova, mortally wounded after her Authority of Death was taken from her, went to Snezhnaya's Celestial Nail, east of Snezhnograd, above the corpse of the Cryo Dragon Sovereign.
- She fused the Eitr with the Nail. It began to crumble.
- Afterwards the Celestial Nails across Teyvat each emit a strange pillar of light.
### STANDARD +
- **The Eitr.** Its full name is Embryonic-Ichoric-Telluric-Rudiment. It is a forbidden Hyperborean creation holding Light Realm power, once used to alter organisms. The Belyi Tsar found it in the Shadow Realm; later it was studied at the Hvergelmir institute.
- **The Nail.** The wiki describes Celestial Nails as seemingly indestructible (the Skyfrost Nail had splintered into shards and the Spiritstone shed a fragment, but neither crumbled away). Ronova's act is the first time one is shown crumbling apart. In the cutscene Ronova herself begins to disintegrate as she does it.
- **What was threatened.** The wiki's summary of the scene says the crumbling Nail threatens to unleash Holy Soil upon all of Teyvat, and its description of the cutscene says Ronova intended Teyvat to share the feeling of death. That intent is the narration's, not a statement made aloud by the character.
- **The world reacts.** The world quest series "The Anomaly" opens after the Archon Quest. It has one quest per region with a Nail: Snezhnaya, Mondstadt, Liyue, Inazuma, Sumeru and Natlan. In each, a known character is found watching the Nail.
### LARGE +
- **Snezhnaya.** The Nail east of Snezhnograd glows and, as Paimon notes, sometimes makes a sound. Alyosha, out hunting nearby, may have been one of the first witnesses. He reported it to the local Druzhna, so the Tsaritsa and Fatui should know. He says it does not seem harmful so far and hopes the military's keenest minds can get to the bottom of it.
- **Mondstadt.** Albedo observes the Skyfrost Nail on Dragonspine, which has changed in the same way. He always assumed it had finished its work after destroying Sal Vindagnyr, and is now studying how the change affects the local ecosystem.
- **Liyue.** Xiao keeps watch at The Chasm on Rex Lapis's orders so he can report any new change at once. Extra Millelith patrols are posted and Cloud Retainer and other adepti have begun investigating.
- **Inazuma.** Yae Miko came to Tsurumi Island after a report. A fortune slip she drew read "Great Misfortune". She can faintly sense a power of some sort and means to bring Kokomi when she gets the chance, since Kokomi may be more familiar with it.
- **Sumeru.** Candace and Dehya camp near the desert Nail. Dehya's mercenaries have partly come to sightsee. Candace says the Desertfolk should not leave the Dendro Archon to carry the burden alone.
- **Natlan.** Mavuika watches the pillar near the Stadium of the Sacred Flame and calls its position precarious; Paimon points out that Natlan's Nail is right near where everyone lives. Mavuika resolves to tell the local population to watch for signs of danger.
### ULTRA +
- **Visibility.** The wiki notes that some of the marks can be seen from neighbouring regions (Snezhnaya's from Fontaine, Liyue's from Mondstadt City and Sumeru City), and that Fontaine, apparently never struck by a Nail, has no mark of its own. These are observations of the world map, not stated in dialogue.
- **Old Nails.** The wiki lists Nails or their traces at Dragonspine, The Chasm, Tsurumi Island, Mt. Damavand/Eternal Oasis, the Night Kingdom/Stadium in Natlan, and Hyperborea. Several have withered Irminsul trees nearby.

## holy_soil
@version: 7.1
### STANDARD
Holy Soil is soil rich in primordial elemental power from the three realms. Dragons can thrive in it, but it is unsuitable for human life. The process of land turning into Holy Soil is the Holy Soil phenomenon. Canon gives three older accounts.
### LARGE +
- **Hyperborea (Snezhnaya).** According to Aksinya, the Hyperboreans tried to create the Primordial Human from the dead Cryo Dragon Sovereign. The experiment went out of control, and the Sovereign's unchecked authority turned the whole of Snezhnaya into Holy Soil of primordial ice, even changing the Ley Lines into crystal veins. The Heavenly Principles then sent a Nail crashing down. By the time the Belyi Tsar awoke a thousand years later, Hyperborea had sunk into the Shadow Realm. The corpse and the Nail remained above ground.
- **Natlan.** Per the dragon "One Who Speaks Coldly" (identified by the wiki as Kukulkan) in the Natlan Archon Quest, the Heavenly Principles regarded Natlan's phlogiston-rich soils as a form of the Holy Soil phenomenon and cast "sky-nails" down to destroy the land. Countless Dragonborn could survive only by devolving, becoming the saurians.
- **Watatsumi Island (Inazuma).** The island's soil is bleaching and losing nutrients. Local folklore blames the island's spirit being drawn toward the depths of Enkanomiya. The shrine maiden Tsuyuko, who studied at the Sumeru Akademiya, thinks seawater leaches nutrients instead (world quest series "The Moon-Bathed Deep", 2.1).
### ULTRA +
In all three cases the pattern is the same: overflowing primordial power, a land unfit for people, and the Heavenly Principles answering with a Nail. That is why the Eitr's sabotage of a Nail alarms those who know this history; the old defence against Holy Soil is the thing now breaking. This reading is the entry's connection, not a statement made by a character.

## accounts
@version: 7.1
### STANDARD
- **Paimon and the Traveler:** suspect the whole situation may be Ronova's revenge, and say they are not in a mood to celebrate.
- **Alyosha:** the Nail started behaving strangely only after the Ruler of Death fell; however it is read, it cannot be good.
- **Mavuika:** whatever happens, the Traveler's party will not be to blame. She says Ronova and the Heavenly Principles have long used Natlan as a tool against the Abyss and the world's destruction, and most of Natlan's past disasters came from them.
### LARGE +
- **Albedo:** says the Skyfrost Nail was thought to have finished its task, and calls this change something he has never witnessed on Dragonspine.
- **Yae Miko:** unsure what trick the Heavenly Principles might be playing; senses a power.
- **Xiao:** says Rex Lapis himself is taking the phenomenon very seriously.
- **Ronova's own view:** the narration of the final scene gives only her last words about the unfamiliar sensation of death and the idea of a "parting gift".

## entry: Eitr and the Broken Nail
@keys: Eitr, Embryonic-Ichoric-Telluric-Rudiment, parting gift
@keys2: Ronova, Nail, Holy Soil
@version: 7.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The Eitr is a Hyperborean product holding Light Realm power. The Belyi Tsar found it in the Shadow Realm on his 446th expedition. Ronova, dying, fused it with the Nail above the Cryo Dragon's corpse east of Snezhnograd, and the Nail began to crumble. Other Nails then began to shine.
### LARGE +
Researchers Koshchei and Pavlina at the Hvergelmir institute experimented with it; it turned a snegovik named Donskoy into a snow giant, and fed into the decision to make the Wildwood Leshy. None of this explains its effect on a Nail.

## entry: The Anomaly (Nail Pillars of Light)
@keys: the Anomaly, pillar of light, pillars of light, Nails of Retribution
@keys2: Nail, Celestial Nail, Skyfrost Nail
@version: 7.1
### STANDARD
After the Archon Quest, the Celestial Nails in Snezhnaya, Mondstadt, Liyue, Inazuma, Sumeru and Natlan each emit a strange pillar of light. Locals and notable figures watch them: Alyosha, Albedo, Xiao, Yae Miko, Candace and Dehya, and Mavuika. No harm is shown as of 7.1.
### LARGE +
In the world quests, Albedo is struck that the Skyfrost Nail was not simply finished after Sal Vindagnyr's end, Yae Miko's fortune slip reads "Great Misfortune", and Xiao says Rex Lapis is taking it very seriously. Mavuika calls Natlan's Nail precariously placed, and Paimon notes it is near where people live.

## entry: Hyperborea's Holy Soil
@keys: Holy Soil of primordial ice, Hyperborean Holy Soil
@keys2: Cryo Dragon, Hyperborea, Nail
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
Aksinya says the out-of-control attempt to make a Primordial Human from the Cryo Dragon Sovereign's corpse turned all of Snezhnaya into Holy Soil of primordial ice, crystallising even the Ley Lines, until the Heavenly Principles sent their Nail crashing down; Hyperborea later sank into the Shadow Realm. A thousand years later the Belyi Tsar awoke from the dragon's remains.
### LARGE +
The Nail and the corpse stayed above ground. They are the same Nail and the same corpse that Ronova chose for her last act.

## entry: Watatsumi Goryou Matsuri and the Holy Soil
@keys: Goryou Matsuri, Bloodbranch Coral, Watatsumi Holy Soil
@keys2: Holy Soil, Watatsumi, Sangonomiya
@version: 2.5
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
During the limited-time event Three Realms Gateway Offering (2.5), Sangonomiya Shrine's answer to Watatsumi's Holy Soil phenomenon is shown: every few centuries someone retrieves a Bloodbranch Coral from Enkanomiya for the Watatsumi Goryou Matsuri. When Kokomi performed the rite in that event it only halted the bleaching instead of reversing it, which had never happened before; the cause was traced to the Void Realm's intrusion into Enkanomiya.
