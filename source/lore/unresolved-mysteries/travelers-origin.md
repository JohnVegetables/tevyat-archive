## summary
### LITE
The Traveler and their twin once ruled a world in the sea of stars. After it was destroyed they crossed the cosmos in a spaceship, asleep for most of the trip, looking for a world with "potential". The ship passed Teyvat once, thousands of years ago, then came back and crash-landed in Nod-Krai. Item text blames a "star-devouring darkness" for their lost world. Almost nothing else about their origin is known as of 7.1.
### STANDARD +
Everything here is seen through item text, the 6.x Nod-Krai quests and the flight logs on the Frost Moon. The twins themselves do not remember most of it. For the sibling's later choices see the sibling-goals entry.

## established
### LITE
- The twins were the rulers of a world, which was destroyed. The glider text of Wings of Fate's Course Intertwined says the sibling went "from the ruler of a world to the hope of but a single nation" in Khaenri'ah.
- They travelled between worlds in a spaceship. The Traveler slept aboard until the sibling woke them 500 years ago, during the Cataclysm.
- The Traveler is the fourth known Descender. A Descender is someone from outside Teyvat whose power can affect its fate.
### STANDARD +
- The Sword of Descension text says humankind once tried to preserve a "doomed world" that was its "last and only home" and drew a sword against "the star-devouring darkness". The Wings of Descension text speaks of watching the birth and death of stars on a long journey.
- The ship was built to carry a "spark of civilization" to a new world. The 6.3 outfit text says it searched for a world with potential for thousands of years, without success. It was run by a navigational intelligence which kept the twins asleep. They were left in stasis pods beside a genetic vault. The ship could remake terrain, air and ecosystems.
- Khaenri'ah's Universitas Magistrorum tracked the ship. The Achievement: Orbits namecard (4.0) quotes its report to the king that the "two stars" had been captured by the world's gravity.
- The ship fell at Starsand Shoal in Nod-Krai and stayed there so long that it was overgrown and seen by several generations. People tried to strip it for parts but could not even dent it.
### LARGE +
- In 6.3 (Welkin Moon's Homecoming) the Traveler, guided by Columbina, finds the ship on the Frost Moon and reads its flight logs. The logs show it flew over Teyvat once before, a very long time ago, without landing, then travelled on for what the Traveler thinks was thousands of years. Some data is corrupted. The Traveler had assumed Teyvat was their first stop.
- In 6.0 (The Dark Side of Memory) the Traveler, helped by Lauma and Columbina, sees a vision of the ship's landing in Nod-Krai. The sibling appears outside the ship and tells the Traveler to leave with them, though the ship's door was never opened.
- In 5.7 (The Key That Unlocks Fate) the Traveler finds in a Natlan cave a second key identical to their own key to the ship's Hibernation Chamber. Paimon concludes the sibling must have lost it nearby.
### ULTRA +
- In 6.7 (Project Moongazer) a sealed data segment in the Moongazers' terminal plays a recorded reply in the twins' voices to a signal they once received. They say they have crossed the universe alone for a long time, that the signal felt like a bonfire in a dark forest, that they will try to find the sender's planet in the future, but that they are dealing with trouble for now. The Traveler is troubled that the new transmission might attract the darkness that dwells among the stars.
- The Traveler remembers the sibling saying that on arriving in Khaenri'ah they could not recall their past. In 6.3 Columbina adds that the sibling rarely came to the surface and, in the early days of the journey, often watched the Traveler from a distance with a guilty look.
- In 7.1 (Etched in Time) Pierro says Hroptatyr summoned someone from Celestia at the moment he summoned Paimon, and Hroptatyr's earlier prophecy spoke of a golden-haired crowned heir answering his call. See the sibling-goals entry for what this means for the sibling.

## accounts
### STANDARD
- **Item text (second person):** the Sword of Descension and Wings of Descension descriptions address the Traveler as "you" and say they came by special means, as one who challenged the world. They are not narrated by a character and cannot be traced to a speaker.
- **The outfit text:** written from the ship intelligence's viewpoint. It says the supernova that killed the planet it passed erupted the very year the twins were born, and then insists the two events are unrelated. This does not say what ended the twins' own world.
### LARGE +
- **Traveler:** assumes Teyvat was their first stop, then learns from the logs it was not. They suspect the sibling left the ship during the first pass.
- **Columbina:** can sense the Traveler's and sibling's stories as a Trilune goddess. She says she saw little of the sibling's and chooses not to reveal more.
- **Wiki editors:** treat the "star-devouring darkness" as the Abyss. See the Nature of the Abyss entry. They also treat the cloth-giver in Wings of Descension as the ship intelligence.
### ULTRA +
- **The glider text:** says the Khaenri'ahn heir was "used as a vessel by which unlimited Abyssal power could be absorbed" and became the "key to the world's near-annihilation". It also says they longed for "a single realm" after losing their own world. It is the only source linking the loss of the first world to their later choices.

## connections
### LARGE
The origin ties into the Descender theme (the Third Descender, Nibelung and the Heavenly Principles), the Abyss's reach across worlds, the Frost Moon and Project Moongazer, and the sibling's story in Khaenri'ah and the Abyss Order. It also bears on the Cataclysm, since Asmoday blocked the twins' attempt to leave.
### ULTRA +
Irminsul keeps no record of Descenders, which is one reason the Traveler remembers things others forget. Paimon's own origin in Celestia is a separate thread, though Pierro links her summoning to the sibling's in 7.1.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay guidance (this project's recommendation): treat the Traveler's origin as sparsely known. The Traveler remembers little of the voyage and knows only what the ship's logs and item text show. A Traveler written after 6.3 can mention the flight logs and the earlier pass over Teyvat. One written before 6.3 should not. Teyvat natives know only that the Traveler is an outlander.
### LARGE +
Who could plausibly know parts of this: Paimon (who has heard about the ship), Columbina (for the Moon and the ship), Lauma and Nod-Krai locals who saw the wreck, Dainsleif, and the sibling. Fatui and the gods know the word Descender. Nobody knows the name of the homeworld.
### ULTRA +
Do not give the twins a backstory the sources do not support: no named kingdom, family, titles or ages for the homeworld, and no confirmed "Abyss destroyed it". Also do not decide that the sibling is the Traveler's elder or younger; canon does not say.

## entry: The twins' spaceship
@keys: twins' spaceship, Hibernation Chamber, ship's flight logs, pioneer vessel
@version: 6.3
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The spaceship that carried the twins is a pioneer vessel shaped like a four-pointed star, white with gold lines and a blue cockpit dome. It is made of a material nothing in Teyvat could damage. It is equipped with a genetic vault and a Hibernation Chamber that opens only with two special keys, one belonging to each twin. A navigational intelligence handled the course while the twins slept. After the Cataclysm it was hidden on the Frost Moon, where Columbina took the Traveler to find it in 6.3.
### LARGE +
The ship can reshape a planet's terrain, air and living systems so a civilisation can start there. It made protective clothing for the sleepers. The Traveler's alternate outfit "As Heaven and Earth Are Made Anew" came from a cabinet aboard, and the Traveler notes it is a matching pair with the sibling's, which the sibling liked more.

## entry: The ship's first pass over Teyvat
@keys: first pass over Teyvat, ship's first visit, flight logs
@keys2: Traveler, Columbina, Frost Moon
@version: 6.3
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
The flight logs the Traveler reads on the Moon show that the ship visited Teyvat a very long time before the Cataclysm (the wiki says thousands of years). It came close and moved on without landing, then travelled for a long time before a forced landing in Nod-Krai. The Traveler suspects the sibling left during that first pass. Canon does not confirm this.

## entry: The star-devouring darkness
@keys: star-devouring darkness, homeworld's destruction
@version: 1.0
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
The Sword of Descension text, a PlayStation-exclusive item, says the twins' world was one humankind tried to preserve against destruction and that facing a "star-devouring darkness" required a sword. The wiki presumes this means the Abyss, which is described elsewhere as ending worlds. The text never names it. Treat the identification as an inference.

## entry: The twins and the Moongazers
@keys: Moongazer signal, twins' reply, Stellar Blackbody
@keys2: Traveler, Columbina, Frost Moon
@version: 6.7
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
The Moongazers were dragon researchers on the Frost Moon who passed on a message from the Stellar Blackbody into space. According to the Project Moongazer page, one of the signals reached the twins, who were inspired by it. In 6.7 a hidden data segment in the Moongazers' terminal plays their reply: they promise to look for the sender one day. The Traveler worries that the signal might draw the darkness among the stars to Teyvat.

## entry: The Universitas Magistrorum's prediction
@keys: Universitas Magistrorum prediction, two stars captured by gravity
@version: 4.0
@spoiler: minor
### ULTRA
A namecard description (Achievement: Orbits) quotes the Universitas Magistrorum's report to the "Almighty Sovereign", Irmin, that the "two stars" had been captured by the world's gravity and, after a lengthy orbit, would meet again that day. The wiki reads this as the twins' ship returning to Teyvat. The text names no ship or twins.
