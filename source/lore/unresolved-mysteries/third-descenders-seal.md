## summary
### LITE
The Third Descender's Seal is a sealed secret kept in a hidden basement of Zapolyarny Palace in Snezhnaya. It is the last thing the Third Descender (Saarelainen) left behind before the Heavenly Principles killed him. Every attempt to open it has failed; only a Gnosis seems to respond to it. What is inside is unknown as of 7.1.
### STANDARD +
It was first looked after by the Belyi Tsar and is now guarded by the Tsaritsa, Anastasya, who hopes that once the Gnoses (the Third Descender's scattered remains) are gathered, its secret can finally be seen. The Traveler and Paimon were shown it in 7.1 when she granted them access to the palace.
### LARGE +
The seal ties together three threads: the Third Descender's bargain with the Heavenly Principles, the Tsaritsa's campaign to collect the Gnoses, and the question of what the Heavenly Principles failed to notice. Canon shows only the room and Anastasya's description, so everything beyond that must be treated as open.

## established
@version: 7.1
@spoiler: major
### LITE
- It lies in a basement room in Zapolyarny Palace, reached through a hidden door that the Tsaritsa had opened for the Traveler as part of the information she was willing to share.
- Anastasya calls it a seal left behind by the Third Descender and says it holds a secret yet to be revealed.
- Its original custodian was her predecessor, the Belyi Tsar, who said it was the last thing the Third Descender left behind before the Heavenly Principles killed him.
- It seems to have gone unnoticed by the Heavenly Principles.
- Every attempt to open it over the centuries has failed. The only thing that seems to respond to it is a Gnosis.
### STANDARD +
- Anastasya reasons that whatever the Third Descender sought to protect at the very end must be of vital importance. She suggests that only once his remains are no longer scattered across the world might the legacy he sealed away be seen.
- Paimon concludes that Anastasya has two reasons to collect the Gnoses: to amplify her power against the Heavenly Principles, and to break the seal. Anastasya confirms this, and agrees that it will be a while before the answer is known because the Pyro Gnosis is still missing.
- The Gnoses were made from the Third Descender's body, so the seal and the Gnoses are two legacies of the same person.
### LARGE +
- By 7.1 the Fatui hold six Gnoses (the Cryo Gnosis plus five collected from other nations); the Pyro Gnosis is the only one whose whereabouts are unknown. The Traveler tells her it was reduced to ashes with the burning of Irminsul and that Mavuika says it returned to the Ley Lines; Anastasya replies that, being formed from the Third Descender's remains, it cannot coexist with the Ley Lines for long and will one day be excised.
- The scene follows her decision to stop investigating the Traveler and give them unrestricted access to the palace. The game's text frames the seal as compensation for their cooperation.
- She is open that she cannot read the secret herself, even though she guards it.
### ULTRA +
- The Belyi Tsar knew of the Third Descender because he had fled into the Shadow Realm before the Heavenly Principles erased all trace of him from Irminsul, and he later told Anastasya about her forgotten past with him. She did not feel it as her own memory and did not trust the Tsar, so what she knows about the seal comes through that same chain. Canon does not say how much the Tsar knew of its purpose.
- In a corner of the same room Anastasya keeps a transcription prism in which she records each day's memories. Pierro explains that, like a Stellar Linchpin, it contains the power of the Third Descender and is therefore free from the influence of certain laws of Teyvat, calling such objects variables from beyond this world brought by the Third Descender. It is a separate object from the seal.

## the_third_descender
@version: 7.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Saarelainen, the Third Descender, came to Teyvat from the sea of stars and was the lover of Anastasya and ancestor of the Khaenri'ahns. In the account Rhinedottir gives Anastasya in Act IV, the Belyi Tsar incited the two to open a gate to the Abyss, and the Heavenly Principles erased Anastasya as punishment. He negotiated with them: his body would become the seven Gnoses, in exchange for her resurrection and a promise never to harm their descendants. As a guarantee he laid a curse that would send the Heavenly Principles to sleep if they broke the promise. Everything about him was erased in return. The seal is the one thing from this story still physically present in Snezhnaya.
### LARGE +
Rhinedottir says the Heavenly Principles saw his power, knowledge and last acts as dangerous and tolerated no uncertain variables, which is why the erasure was part of the bargain. Whether the seal was among those last acts is not stated, although Anastasya suggests it holds something he judged vital.

## accounts
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
- Anastasya (7.1): the seal was entrusted to the Belyi Tsar, who said it was the Third Descender's last act before his death; she does not know how those events unfolded. The Gnosis response and the hope that it may open once the remains are gathered are her own reasoning.
- The Belyi Tsar (as reported by Anastasya and Rhinedottir): he retained his memory of the Third Descender by having fled into the Shadow Realm. Anastasya distrusts him, but Rhinedottir judges his account probably true.
- Rhinedottir (7.1): tells the bargain, the erasure and the curse, and does not mention the seal.
- Hymns of the Far North: a hymn collection of the Frostmoon Scions with a version of the Third Descender's story, including his journey to court Anastasya and the demand by Pakkaisukko (the Belyi Tsar) that he open the "gates of Pohjola". The Heavenly Principles' modification of Irminsul makes parts of it unreliable, and some claims in it are known to be wrong. It does not mention a seal.
### ULTRA +
- Skirk (4.2, relayed by Neuvillette): tells him that the Gnosis he holds is the remains of the Third Descender and that it would bring misfortune. This is how the player learns that the Gnoses come from a person.

## open_questions
@version: 7.1
@spoiler: major
### LITE
- What is in the seal? Canon has not said.
- Will the Gnoses open it, and are all seven required?
### STANDARD +
- Why would the Third Descender make something that only a Gnosis answers, when the Gnoses were also the means of the Heavenly Principles' system?
- Why did the Heavenly Principles not notice it?
### LARGE +
- Is it connected to his curse on the Heavenly Principles or to his bargain? Canon does not say.
- Is it meant for Anastasya, for the Khaenri'ahns, or for no one in particular? No canon answer.
- Has Anastasya already tried the Gnoses she holds? Her wording, that every attempt to open it has failed, does not specify.
### ULTRA +
A character may offer a theory about the seal but should be shown to be guessing.

## connections
### LARGE
- The Tsaritsa's plan: the seal is one of the two reasons Anastasya gives for collecting the Gnoses.
- The Gnoses and Saarelainen: the seal's only known point of response is the material of the Third Descender's remains.
- The dormancy of the Heavenly Principles: Anastasya infers in 7.1 that his curse is why they sleep.
- The Belyi Tsar: the seal's first custodian.
### ULTRA +
- Stellar Linchpins: Fatui research ties them to Saarelainen's remains. No canon says the seal is a Stellar Linchpin or related to them.
- Zapolyarny Palace: the seal lies in a hidden basement below the palace where the Tsaritsa rules.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Archive recommendation, not canon: treat the seal as a guarded matter that Anastasya shares only with those she trusts. It was shown to the Traveler and Paimon in 7.1; do not assume other characters know it exists. Do not invent its contents, an inscription, a voice, a shape or any sign that it is opening. Keep to the canon description of a seal in a hidden basement room.

### STANDARD

## entry: Remains of the Third Descender
@keys: remains of the Third Descender, Third Descender's remains
@keys2: seal, Gnosis, Gnoses
@version: 7.1
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
Skirk revealed in 4.2 that a Gnosis is part of the Third Descender's remains. In 7.1 Anastasya connects the Gnoses with the seal: since only a Gnosis seems to react to it, she hopes that gathering them all will let its secret be seen. This is her reasoning, not a proven fact.
