## summary
### LITE
The Tsaritsa (Anastasya, the Cryo Archon) intends to confront and overthrow the Heavenly Principles. To that end she founded the Fatui and sent her Harbingers to collect the other Archons' Gnoses. As of 7.1 she holds six of the seven; only the Pyro Gnosis is missing. She also backs Project Mistilteinn, a weapon for that fight. Her goal was long kept secret even from her own people, and her endgame is still unresolved.
### STANDARD +
Venti put the question in the Prologue: he had not seen her in five hundred years and wondered what she was planning. Snezhnaya answered it openly in 7.0 and 7.1.

## established
### LITE
- The aim was stated openly by Pierro (7.0) and by Anastasya herself (7.1).
- Besides her own Cryo Gnosis she holds the Anemo, Geo, Electro, Dendro and Hydro Gnoses.
- She keeps a seal left by the Third Descender in Zapolyarny Palace. Only a Gnosis seems to respond to it.
### STANDARD +
- **Why the Gnoses.** They are made from the Third Descender's remains and amplify their bearer. Anastasya says that, fully exploited, they could let a bearer transcend the limits of their authority, a necessary step towards facing the Heavenly Principles. Pierro calls them a key bargaining chip.
- **Why the fight.** Snezhnaya long ago reached the technological boundary the Heavenly Principles permit and lives under threat of a Nail of Retribution. To her, civilization as it stands can be negated at any moment.
- **Mistilteinn.** Project Stuzha, the Veche's energy rationing, exists to feed Project Mistilteinn.
### LARGE +
- **More on the Gnoses.** They do not come from Teyvat's own system. The Seven can raise a storm, Anastasya says, but cannot shake the world's foundations; that would need a supreme authority that has always been out of reach.
- **The seal.** It came to her from her predecessor, the Belyi Tsar, who said it was the Third Descender's last legacy before the Heavenly Principles killed him. Every attempt to open it has failed. She hopes it may open once the Third Descender's remains are no longer scattered.
- **Pierro's framing.** Unless civilization stops being something the Heavenly Principles can "negate" on a whim, the fates of Khaenri'ah and Hyperborea will repeat. The Kuuvahki Experimental Design Bureau in Nod-Krai was a front for building Mistilteinn itself.
- **The love she rejects.** Anastasya names her authority as "love" but refuses to identify with it or use it. She says the gods' love for humanity is a curse the Heavenly Principles carved into them, and that this cheapened love. She chooses instead to be her true self and do her duty as Tsaritsa. Pierro calls the same curse one of many "rules" of the world that are really curses, and says she has ample reason to hate the Heavenly Principles.
- **Energy.** Pierro says elemental energy is Human Realm power converted by the Heavenly Principles, so turning it against them would be futile. Mistilteinn therefore relies on energy reverse-engineered from Stellar Linchpins, which carry the Third Descender's power. He calls it "anti-Celestia energy".
- **The Harbingers' latitude.** The Harbingers were given wide autonomy in retrieving the Gnoses, and Pierro says their actions do not fully represent Snezhnaya's official stance.

## gnosis_campaign
### LITE
The Gnoses were gathered one by one across the Traveler's journey: Anemo by force from Venti (Signora, 1.0), Geo by contract with Zhongli (1.1), Electro and Dendro from Nahida (Dottore, 3.2), Hydro as Neuvillette's diplomatic gift (Arlecchino, 4.2).
### LARGE
The Gnoses were gathered one by one across the Traveler's journey.
- **Anemo (1.0):** Signora ambushed Venti in Mondstadt and tore the Gnosis from his chest.
- **Geo (1.1):** Zhongli handed it over as his "contract to end all contracts" with the Cryo Archon. He never said what she offered in return.
- **Electro and Dendro (3.2):** Dottore's segment received the Electro Gnosis from Nahida, who had taken it from Scaramouche. In return he agreed to erase his other segments. He received the Dendro Gnosis in exchange for information about the false sky.
- **Hydro (4.2):** Arlecchino got the Tsaritsa's permission to use the Hydro Gnosis first to try to avert Fontaine's prophecy. The prophecy was resolved by other means, and Neuvillette's diplomatic gift put the Gnosis in her hands.
- **Pyro (5.3-6.6):** Capitano dueled Mavuika but would not exploit her weakness. He asked to be relieved of the mission, and the Tsaritsa agreed; after his sacrifice she kept his rank intact. In 6.6 Nahida used the Pyro Gnosis, which the Traveler had borrowed from Mavuika, to burn the corrupted Irminsul. The Gnosis vanished afterwards.
### ULTRA +
In 7.1 Anastasya's only question to the Traveler was where the Pyro Gnosis is. Told it had been lost to the Ley Lines, she was surprised but calm. Because Gnoses are made from the Third Descender's remains, she reasoned, it cannot stay in the Ley Lines for long and will one day be expelled, so Snezhnaya will find it eventually.

## the_ruler_of_death
@version: 7.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Ronova's Curse of Death halted Mistilteinn in 7.0. In 7.1 Anastasya used the six Gnoses to outmaneuver the Shades, and Celestia stripped Ronova of her authority. The project then resumed.
### LARGE +
The curse kills everyone nearby at moments of technological progress. Anastasya said the Heavenly Principles could not be faced until Ronova was dealt with, and she made life-force research, starting from the sliver of the authority of life inherited by Vesna, Snezhnaya's single most important task. She then baited Rhinedottir, the Ruler of Life. Snezhnaya collected Seals of Life knowing the scale of it would draw her out, and Anastasya used what she had seen of Asmoday as her bargaining chip to invite Rhinedottir to tea at Zapolyarny Palace. Over the tea she used the six Gnoses, amplifying her Cryo power with her own insight of the past thousand years, to slow time in the hall. Rhinedottir missed the window she and Ronova had arranged to revive Ronova's victims without the Heavenly Principles' monitoring cycle noticing. Celestia then reclaimed Ronova's Authority of Death for abuse and ordered Rhinedottir to revive the dead. Pierro, Dainsleif and the Traveler defeated the weakened Ronova, and the restrictions on Mistilteinn were lifted.
### ULTRA +
Naberius conceded that the act could not be judged by Celestia and fell far short of the threshold for a Nail of Retribution, but wanted Anastasya punished for overstepping her authority. Rhinedottir declined, since Celestia was watching her closely, and called the Tsaritsa a worthy rival. Speaking as a descendant of Khaenri'ah, she added that Snezhnaya might achieve monumental things. Asked whether she had counted on all this, Anastasya replied that she does not sacrifice pawns to reach an end. When the Traveler called it the beginning of a new era, Dainsleif agreed, adding that it would not be a peaceful one. In the act's final cutscene the dying Ronova fused the Eitr with Snezhnaya's Nail of Retribution, which began to crumble and threatened to spread Holy Soil across Teyvat.

## history
### LARGE
After the Cataclysm 500 years ago Anastasya succeeded the Belyi Tsar as Cryo Archon; Venti says that after that catastrophe she cut off all ties with him. In the third year of her reign she founded the Fatui with Pierro as its first member and leader. Over the following centuries the two of them recruited the Eleven Fatui Harbingers, and she gave them Delusions, a power separate from Visions. Snezhnaya became the most militarily and technologically developed nation in Teyvat.
### ULTRA +
In the animated short "The Final Gift" (released in the 6.6 period, set before the Traveler's journey), Pierro briefs the Harbingers that the Gnoses are an indispensable part of Her Majesty's grand design. He assigns Pantalone to finances and Sandrone to technical needs. Afterwards Signora, about to leave for Mondstadt and Inazuma, says she shares aspects of the Tsaritsa's vision, and that is enough for her. After Signora's death in Inazuma, the teaser "A Winter Night's Lazzo" shows Pierro playing chess with the unseen Tsaritsa. The Anemo and Geo Gnoses serve as pieces on the board, and her ice later seals Signora's mausoleum.

In the teaser "The Gods' Limits" (2025), Ronova scoffs that the Cryo Archon dares set her gaze on the Heavenly Principles with "paltry powers". Rhinedottir observes that the Heavenly Principles have yet to awaken and that in the Tsaritsa's place she would not pass up the opportunity either. Pierro answers, on the Tsaritsa's behalf, that even the gods have their limits.

## accounts
### LARGE
- **Pierro (7.0):** the Gnoses are a key bargaining chip; Mistilteinn is the weapon; the reason is that civilization is an illusion that can be negated at any time.
- **Anastasya (7.1):** the Gnoses are a step to transcending authority; civilization is "a lie told to us by fate"; Snezhnaya lives under threat of a Nail of Retribution.
- **Dainsleif (Teyvat Chapter Storyline Preview):** she is a god with no love left for her people, and her followers hope only to be on her side when her rebellion against the divine comes.
### ULTRA +
- **Rhinedottir (7.1):** after the tea she concludes that Anastasya's hostility comes less from past grievance than from Snezhnaya's ideals and future. In the teaser "Sudden Snow" she says over a chessboard that she hopes the Tsaritsa understands the nature of the game she is playing.
- **The Snezhnayan public (7.0):** people endure Project Stuzha because it is Her Majesty's will. No one knows where the gathered energy goes, since it has never been made public.
- **Several of her own subordinates,** among them Vesna and Danica, believe she is kinder than she admits.

## open_questions
### LITE
What lies inside the Third Descender's seal; whether the Pyro Gnosis will resurface; what Mistilteinn is and whether it can harm the Heavenly Principles; and how and when Snezhnaya will finally confront them. None of these is answered as of 7.1.
### LARGE +
Also open: what the Tsaritsa gave Zhongli for the Geo Gnosis; when the dormant Heavenly Principles will awaken (Rhinedottir refused to say); and what Ronova's final act at Snezhnaya's Nail of Retribution will bring.
### ULTRA +
Other loose threads: what the hooded figure seen with Asmoday beside the blizzard-beast that Anastasya killed in 7.0 wanted there (the wiki identifies the figure as the Traveler's sibling; Rhinedottir does not know who it was); how the Third Descender's curse binds the Heavenly Principles; and what the Tsaritsa's own memory records (her transcription prism) show of a past she cannot trust.

## connections
### LARGE
The plan ties together the Gnoses and the Third Descender (Saarelainen), whose remains became them; the Fatui and the Harbingers; Projects Stuzha and Mistilteinn; Stellar Linchpins; the Four Shades of the Heavenly Principles; and the Traveler, whom Anastasya treats as a potential ally from 7.1.
### ULTRA +
It also connects to Khaenri'ah. The Khaenri'ahns descend from Anastasya and the Third Descender, and Rhinedottir and Dainsleif address her as "Mother of Khaenri'ah". Pierro, once of Khaenri'ah, carries a grudge against Ronova from the Cataclysm.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
For roleplay: before 7.0, outsiders and ordinary Snezhnayans should not know the Tsaritsa's aim; the Harbingers' Gnosis thefts looked like naked aggression. From 7.1 the aim is open among her inner circle and known to the Traveler. Do not invent the plan's final step, the contents of the seal, the shape of Mistilteinn, or a resolution with the Heavenly Principles. Canon leaves all of these unrevealed. Do not present the Tsaritsa as acting out of "love" for humanity; she explicitly rejects that.

## entry: The Third Descender's Seal
@keys: Third Descender's seal, seal left behind by the Third Descender
@version: 7.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
An object kept in Zapolyarny Palace, inherited from the Belyi Tsar, who called it the Third Descender's last legacy. It went unnoticed by the Heavenly Principles and has resisted every attempt to open it. Only a Gnosis seems to respond to it. Anastasya hopes that once all of the Third Descender's remains (the Gnoses) are gathered, its secret can be seen. Its contents are unknown as of 7.1.

## entry: Transcription prism
@keys: transcription prism, memory recording, memory prism
@keys2: Tsaritsa, Anastasya, Zapolyarny
@version: 7.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Pierro explains that Her Majesty believes memory cannot be completely trusted, so long ago she began storing each day's memories in a transcription prism. (Act IV of 7.1 later reveals that the Heavenly Principles did alter her memories of the Third Descender.) Like a Stellar Linchpin, he says, it holds the Third Descender's power and so is free from certain laws of Teyvat, which makes it an indispensable tool against the Heavenly Principles.

## entry: Gnoses in Snezhnaya's hands

### STANDARD
As of 7.1 the Tsaritsa holds six Gnoses: Cryo (her own), Anemo (Signora, from Venti), Geo (Signora, by Zhongli's contract), Electro and Dendro (Dottore, from Nahida) and Hydro (Arlecchino, from Neuvillette).
