## summary
### LITE
Paimon is the small, floating, talkative companion who has stayed at the Traveler's side since the start of the game, and for years nobody knew what she was. She says she was fished out of the sea by the Traveler and remembers almost nothing before that. In Version 7.0 (Snezhnaya, Chapter VII) the Fatui Director Pierro revealed that he drew her down from Celestia in Khaenri'ah more than 500 years ago, and Ronova, the Ruler of Death, called her a creation of Celestia with none of its power. Paimon has no memory of any of this, and what she is and why she was sent remain open.
### STANDARD +
The answer is partial. Canon now fixes where she came from and roughly when, but not what she is, who intended her or what she is for. The Traveler's reply, that she is still Paimon to them, is the story's current resting point.

## established
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
These points are stated in canon by the 7.0-7.1 Archon Quest and earlier scenes.

- Paimon has been the Traveler's guide and companion since the opening of the story. She says she was exhausted, caught in a strange whirlpool and swept out to sea, and would have drowned had the Traveler not fished her out. She says nothing worse ever happened to her.
- In Chapter VII Paimon fell ill in Snezhnograd. The cause was the energy being developed for Project Mistilteinn, the Fatui's "anti-Celestia energy": her reaction to it points to her Celestial origin. Pierro personally carried her to the Armory Palace and had the researcher Mitya adjust the project so its energies would no longer affect her. Afterwards she was well again.
- Pierro, whose Khaenri'ahn name is Eftirvit, told the Traveler that in his Khaenri'ah days he used technology upon the heavens and the response was Paimon descending from Celestia. She then lived in his palace until the Cataclysm. Paimon, who was told this with the Traveler, pointed out that this was about five hundred years ago and that she remembers none of it, and that she has memories only from the past few years.
- Ronova, the Shade who rules over death, called Paimon a creation of Celestia that possesses none of Celestia's power, let alone its authority. She tried to take Paimon by force and was stopped by Pierro, then fought by the Traveler, and said she would "reclaim" Paimon next time.
- Paimon still feels oddly close to Pierro, though she remembers nothing about him.
### LARGE +
- She has no Vision and no element of her own and does not fight, yet she is unharmed by things that normally need an elemental affinity: dense Ley Line energy, Abyssal corruption, and diving in Fontaine, where she can even talk underwater. Mavuika kept her away from the final battle in the Night Kingdom because the Abyss would likely target a non-combatant, not because the corruption would injure her.
- When Dr. Luneva in Snezhnaya asked whether she was a fae, Paimon said she was not sure, only that she was not the same kind as the fae at the train station, and insisted she is basically the same as a human. Luneva found her vital signs normal and took the illness for a cold.
- Further Khaenri'ah details, all from Pierro: he presented Paimon to the Vinster King Irmin as a gift, but Irmin took no interest in her, so she lived in Eftirvit's palace; he completely ignored her, being wholly focused on his struggle against Hroptatyr "the Wise", president of the Universitas Magistrorum. When she arrived her consciousness was like a newborn's, and none could say what kind of being she had been in Celestia; her personality then was no different from now. In 7.1 (Etched in Time) Pierro added that when he summoned Paimon from Celestia, Hroptatyr summoned someone else at the same time: the Traveler's sibling.
- Pierro's stated reason for protecting her now is that she seems to be the only sane thing that survived what he called that era of insanity. Ronova found his obsession baffling.
- Paimon is one of the beings who is affected when Irminsul is rewritten, as only those native to Teyvat are (the wiki cites Where the Boat of Consciousness Lies and The Kabukimono's Finale for this, and a Scaramouche-related memory loss in which she forgot while the Traveler did not; the exact scenes were not re-read). This fits her being bound to Teyvat in practice, whatever her origin.

## accounts
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
- **Paimon's own account.** She remembers falling asleep or being exhausted, being swept out to sea and rescued. She is shown to have no memory of Khaenri'ah, of Pierro or of the five centuries between. She decided that being a creation of Celestia changes nothing and joked she may be "the fairy of food and fun". She also asked the Traveler whether they would abandon her; they said no.
- **Pierro's account.** He says his device on the heavens produced Paimon, and that she lived in his palace until the Cataclysm. He says she arrived with a newborn's consciousness and that none could say what she had been in Celestia, and he guesses her memory loss may be some curse from living through the Cataclysm. He told the Traveler that this was their first official meeting; he had already met Paimon when they first arrived in Snezhnaya and told her they had once spent time together in the distant past.
- **Ronova's account.** A being that may be a creation of Celestia but possesses none of its power, let alone authority; she called it worthless, yet said she would reclaim it next time. She says this while Pierro is the one standing against her, and she treats his attachment as a puzzle.
### LARGE +
- **Kukulkan's remark (Natlan).** He speaks of the Traveler and the "little emissary" by their side, without saying whose emissary.
- **Barbeloth's reading.** The Seer-Witch wondered whether Paimon wanted to know her own fate, then fell silent. Most seers cannot read Paimon's fate at all, according to the wiki.
- **Melusine sight.** The Melusine Canotila (in the quest Book of Esoteric Revelations, per the Paimon page) described Paimon as a little rainbow balloon with a string leading upward to somewhere beyond the sky, and insisted that Melusines can see what humans cannot. Paimon found this baffling. It is a poetic image, not an explanation.
- **Teyvatian impressions.** Most characters take Paimon for a flying pet, a fairy or a sprite. Verr Goldet calls her a floating elf; Starsnatcher a talking fairy; Vseslav mistook her for a young Vila. These are guesses by people with no inside knowledge.

## open_questions
### STANDARD
- What kind of being is Paimon, and was she made, born or sent?
- Why did Celestia's response to Pierro's device take the form of Paimon?
- Where was she, and in what state, from the Cataclysm to the time the Traveler met her, and why does she remember only the last few years?
### LARGE +
- Does she hold any hidden power? Ronova says she holds none of Celestia's, yet she shrugs off Abyssal corruption and Ley Line energy.
- What did Barbeloth see, and what did Kukulkan mean by "emissary"?
- How does she connect to the Traveler's sibling, whom Pierro says Hroptatyr summoned from Celestia at the same time he summoned Paimon? Beyond that simultaneity, canon has not tied the two together.
Canon so far gives no hint that Paimon is dangerous, hostile or a plant; she behaves exactly as she did before the reveal. Keep her this way in portrayal. Do not state a purpose, a hidden power or a destiny for her.

## connections
### STANDARD
Pierro and Ronova (and, through Ronova, the Heavenly Principles) are the two parties in canon who have spoken about Paimon's origin. The reveal is tied to Project Mistilteinn and the Fatui's plan against the Heavenly Principles, to the Cataclysm in Khaenri'ah, and to the Traveler's own origin and the sibling thread. In 7.1 (A Rekviem for the Underworld) Paimon is still with the Traveler through the Fatui's preparations; Ronova had her authority confiscated by Celestia for her transgression in Snezhnaya and was mortally wounded by Pierro's new Delusion; she spent her last act shattering a Celestial Nail with Eitr and is presumed dead (the wiki lists her as having died), so her threat to reclaim Paimon is no longer active and her reason for wanting Paimon was never explained.
### LARGE +
See the entries for Pierro, Ronova, Hroptatyr, Irmin, the Traveler's origin and the Traveler's sibling for those figures' own accounts; do not copy their biographies here.
### ULTRA +
Usable framing: Paimon is the one fixed companion who has been present at every major reveal without being part of any, and the story treats her loss of memory as unexplained rather than as a hidden clue. Anyone portraying her should keep her cheerful, loyal and unaware, and let other characters supply any speculation.

## entry: Pierro's Revelation (7.0)
@keys: Eftirvit, Pierro and Paimon, Paimon from Celestia
@keys2: Paimon, Khaenri'ah, Celestia
@version: 7.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
In the Archon Quest Mistilteinn, after Pierro (the Jester) rescues the unwell Paimon and Ronova is driven off, he explains why the Fatui's anti-Celestia energy affected her: in Khaenri'ah, centuries ago, he used technology upon the heavens and Paimon descended from Celestia in answer. She stayed in his palace until the Cataclysm. Paimon, who remembers none of it, realises this happened five hundred years ago (the Traveler can remark that she must be at least that old), asks whether she was super smart back then, and says she still feels weirdly close to him, though he is basically a stranger to her. Pierro answers that she had a newborn's consciousness and that he ignored her entirely, but that she now seems the only sane thing to survive that era. Asked whether they will abandon her, the Traveler can answer that she is still Paimon to them.
### LARGE +
This is the first time canon names Paimon's origin and gives her a place in Khaenri'ah's last days. In 7.1 Pierro adds that Hroptatyr summoned the Traveler's sibling from Celestia at the same time. It does not say why Pierro called, what he hoped for, or what she was before she arrived.

## entry: Ronova's Interest in Paimon
@keys: Ronova Paimon, Ruler of Death Paimon, reclaim Paimon
@keys2: Paimon, Ronova, Celestia
@version: 7.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Seeing Paimon, Ronova said "That thing... is coming with me" and fired at her; Pierro blocked the attack. She then said that thing may be a creation of Celestia but possesses none of its power, let alone authority, that Pierro's obsession with it baffled her, and that it was worthless, yet she would reclaim it next time. The Traveler, hearing this and that she wanted to retrieve Paimon, treated Ronova as an enemy. In Snezhnaya her Curse of Death was a barrier to Project Mistilteinn; in 7.1 she lost her authority, was mortally wounded by Pierro and is presumed dead, so she is no longer pursuing Paimon.
### LARGE +
Ronova did not explain why a creation of Celestia without its authority should be taken back, nor what she would do with Paimon.

## entry: Paimon's Illness in Snezhnaya
@keys: Paimon sick, Paimon fever, Paimon illness, Paimon unwell
@keys2: Snezhnaya, Mistilteinn, Podvorye Restaurant, Snezhnograd
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
In Snezhnograd Paimon grew dizzy and feverish. The doctor Luneva, after Paimon insisted she was basically the same as a human, found her vital signs normal and took it for a cold; the real cause was Project Mistilteinn's anti-Celestia energy, which affected her even from far away. When the Traveler was away at the theatre, Pierro carried her off, still asleep, to the Armory Palace; the Traveler took it for an abduction. Paimon woke there and told the Traveler that the Jester and Mitya had used some kind of energy on her that she could feel cancelling out what was making her unwell; Mitya said they had reversed the experimental energy's effects on Pierro's instructions. Afterwards her head stopped spinning and the fever was gone.
### LARGE +
Pierro explicitly links the illness to her being a creation of Celestia. Mitya and Pierro treated it as a side effect to be avoided, not a threat.

## entry: Paimon and Irminsul Changes
@keys: Paimon forgot, Paimon and Irminsul, Paimon memory
@keys2: Irminsul, Scaramouche, Wanderer
@spoiler: minor
### LARGE
According to the wiki, Paimon is affected by Irminsul modifications that only affect those native to Teyvat: when Scaramouche's record was altered in Irminsul, she forgot him while the Traveler remembered. This is as summarised by the wiki; the primary scenes were not re-read here. It shows she is bound to Teyvat's Irminsul record despite her Celestial origin.

## entry: Barbeloth's Silence
@keys: Barbeloth fortune, Seer-Witch Paimon
@keys2: Paimon, Barbeloth, Hexenzirkel
### LARGE
When Paimon asked the Seer-Witch Barbeloth to read her fortune, Barbeloth hesitated, asked in a serious tone whether she truly wished to know, and then fell silent. The scene is a hint, not an answer. Treat it as such and do not invent what she saw.
