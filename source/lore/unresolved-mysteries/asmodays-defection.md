## summary
### LITE
Asmoday, the Ruler of Space and "Sustainer of the Heavenly Principles", once stopped the Traveler and their twin from leaving Teyvat and tore them apart. Five hundred years later she no longer attends the meetings of the four Shades and has left her own Temple of Space. Hints point to her now working with the Traveler's sibling. Canon does not say why, or on whose terms.
### STANDARD +
The word "defection" is the other Shades' interpretation, not a confession. Istaroth floats the idea that she "switched sides" and says looking for her would do more harm than good. Ronova calls such a move unthinkable unless Asmoday loved a new master more than herself. In 6.5 her abandoned attendant Melanta asks why she chose the sibling over him. In 7.1 a hooded figure is seen beside her.
### LARGE +
Everything known comes from outside observers: other gods, a servant she left behind, and a ruler who saw her briefly in a storm. Asmoday has not spoken since the opening of the game. Her reasons, her goals and the nature of her bond with the sibling are open.

## established
### LITE
- In the prologue (1.0), a white-clad god calls herself the sustainer of the Heavenly Principles, says "the arrogation of mankind ends now", and traps both twins in swarms of red-black cubes. The Traveler's sibling is taken first; the Traveler is sealed and sleeps.
- Later canon names her Asmoday, Ruler of Space, one of the four Shades the Heavenly Principles created.
### STANDARD +
- In the Gods' Limits teaser (2025-07-09) Ronova, Rhinedottir and Istaroth meet in Celestia without Asmoday. Ronova notes that not one of the Shades has ever missed a meeting and wants her found.
- Asmoday's Temple of Space, the private domain where she kept chosen people and things from Teyvat, was left without its owner. Its rules began to fail; her attendant Nihilita walked away, and Melanta, the other attendant, tightened the rules until he began to destroy himself.
- In 6.5 (A Long Day in the Mountains, final chapter) Melanta asks his creator why she would choose the Traveler's sibling and abandon him. The portals she had opened nearby close as the fight ends.
- In 7.1 the Tsaritsa tells Rhinedottir she recently saw Asmoday with a hooded figure at the heart of a storm in Snezhnaya, near a monster that had come in with the snow. The hooded figure said it was worth witnessing the end of the Third Descender's story. Asmoday then used space to take them both away.
### LARGE +
- The Fatui also treat her as a danger. In Etched in Time (7.1) the Rooster says one reason a notebook was sealed was fear that Snezhnaya could become a "Second Khaenri'ah", drawing the notice of the Heavenly Principles and the sustainer thereof.
- In Black Feathers Scattered at Parting (7.1), Ronova says she need only wait until Asmoday returns so the four Shades can use their authority together, or until the Heavenly Principles awake.
### ULTRA +
- According to the wiki, the sibling was kept in a coffin as an exhibit in the Temple of Space after the Cataclysm and was later released, eventually leading the Abyss Order. This comes from the wiki summary rather than a transcript, so treat the detail as medium confidence.

## accounts
### STANDARD
- **Istaroth** (teaser): the meetings are only a formality, and Asmoday may simply have "switched sides". She adds that finding her would do more harm than good, then draws attention to Pierro's gaze when Ronova will not let the matter go.
- **Ronova** (teaser): wants her found, because no Shade has ever missed a meeting; says a change of side would be unthinkable unless she "loves her new master more than her own self".
- **Rhinedottir** (teaser; 7.1): finds the idea interesting. In 7.1 she asks whether the hooded figure is Asmoday's "new master". Naberius replies that this is more likely an idea Istaroth had on a whim.
### LARGE +
- **Melanta** (6.5): believes he was abandoned in favour of the sibling, and says he only wanted her praise on her return. He supplies the strongest direct hint that she chose the sibling, but it is his bitter reading of her departure.
- **Anastasya, the Tsaritsa** (7.1): reports what she saw and admits she could not identify the hooded figure. She offered the information in exchange for facts about the Heavenly Principles and said she was not trying to win favour with them.
- **Wiki synthesis**: says Asmoday has "aligned herself with the Traveler's sibling and, by extension, the Abyss Order". It is a fair reading of the above, but no character says the second half.

## open_questions
### LITE
- Why did the Shade who sealed the twins end up apparently helping one of them?
- Who, if anyone, is her "new master", and did she choose to follow them?
### STANDARD +
- Did the sibling free her, persuade her, or was it her own decision?
- Do the Heavenly Principles know? They have been dormant, and Ronova names their waking as a condition for dealing with the Fatui.
- Why was she watching a storm monster in Snezhnaya, and why did a hooded figure speak of the end of the Third Descender's story?
### LARGE +
- Does her absence explain why the Temple of Space's rules fail, or was the Temple always built on rules that she herself ignored?

## entry: Asmoday Switches Sides

@keys2: defect, defected, switched sides, new master, absence
@version: 6.5
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
Asmoday, the Ruler of Space, once sealed the Traveler and their twin and now appears to be working with the twin. The other Shades suspect that she has defected, and Ronova suggests she might love a new master more than herself. In 6.5 her attendant Melanta asks why she chose the Traveler's sibling over him. No scene explains why. Do not name her master or her motive as fact.
### LARGE +
She has not spoken in canon since the prologue. Everything about her present loyalty is inferred from sightings and from the words of others.

## entry: Gods' Limits Meeting
@keys: Gods' Limits, Shades' meeting, Four Shades meeting
@keys2: Asmoday, Ronova, Istaroth, Rhinedottir
@version: 5.8
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
In the interlude teaser The Gods' Limits (2025-07-09), the Shades meet in Celestia with Asmoday missing. Ronova insists she be found because no Shade has ever skipped a meeting. Istaroth wonders whether she simply "switched sides" and thinks finding her would do more harm than good. Ronova says the idea is unthinkable unless Asmoday loves a new master above herself. When she raises it again, Istaroth tells her Pierro is watching them. The scene gives no answer.

## entry: Hooded Figure With Asmoday
@keys: hooded figure, Asmoday's hooded companion
@keys2: Asmoday, Anastasya, storm, monster
@version: 7.1
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
In Black Feathers Scattered at Parting (7.1), Anastasya tells Rhinedottir she saw Asmoday with a hooded figure beside a storm monster. The figure said it was worth witnessing the end of the Third Descender's story. Asmoday used space and both vanished. Anastasya cannot say who the figure was; the quest stills and the wiki suggest it was the Traveler's sibling. Rhinedottir wonders whether it was Asmoday's "new master". Naberius dismisses the theory as one of Istaroth's passing ideas.

## entry: Melanta's Abandonment
@keys: Melanta, Melanta's abandonment
@keys2: Asmoday, Temple of Space, sibling
@version: 6.5
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
In the last chapter of A Long Day in the Mountains, Melanta defends the Temple of Space's rules after Asmoday's departure. Defeated, he asks why she chose the Traveler's sibling and abandoned him, admits he hoped for her praise, and ends in self-destruction. As he dies, Asmoday's portals nearby close, suggesting she may be watching. It is his own interpretation of her motives.
