## summary
### LITE
The Traveler's twin (Lumine or Aether, whichever twin the player did not choose) leads the Abyss Order as its Prince or Princess. They have said they will not go home with the Traveler until their "war with destiny" against the Heavenly Principles is over. Their two confirmed aims are reviving the fallen nation of Khaenri'ah and gaining enough power to face the Heavenly Principles as an equal. Their final purpose, and the "truth of this world" that set them on this path, are still unrevealed as of 7.1.
### STANDARD +
Their methods have changed from quest to quest: a corrupted Dvalin, a mechanical god, a curse-cleansing device, the Loom of Fate. By 5.7 the sibling had woven a complete Ley Line network for a "New Khaenri'ah" and sealed it in the Atlas of the New World. Asmoday, the Shade of Space, has apparently left Celestia to follow them.

## established
### LITE
- In 1.4 (A Soul Set Apart) the sibling asks the Traveler not to stop them or the Abyss. They say they cannot leave for a new world until the Abyss has "engulfed the thrones", and that they have already travelled this world once and seen its true nature.
- In 5.7 (A Space and Time Without You) they explain that guilt over abandoning Khaenri'ah drives the revival, but is only part of their motive. To speak with the Heavenly Principles as an equal, they say, they need the might of an entire world behind them.
### STANDARD +
- They regard Khaenri'ah as a second home. They arrived there with no memory, were made royal heir and "Savior", and only recovered their memories of the Traveler after years as Prince or Princess.
- When the Cataclysm began, the sibling was preparing to fight for Khaenri'ah. Then Direidyth brought news that the Traveler was looking for them, and they chose their twin over the nation, knowing they would regret it whichever way they chose.
- In 4.7 they said they still had time to unlock the Loom of Fate's full potential before the Heavenly Principles "awaken". The Principles had shown no sign of activity since the Cataclysm, and Celestia did nothing when Focalors destroyed her own divine throne.
### LARGE +
- In 2.6 (seen as a memory in Memories of Inteyvat), they told an Abyss Herald that the Order had focused so single-mindedly on confronting the Heavenly Principles that it had neglected "our original mission", the revival of the homeland. They approved a risky device anyway, saying even a one-percent chance was enough: the cursed would rather return to the natural cycle of life and death than go on paying for "so-called sins."
- The Atlas of the New World holds New Khaenri'ah's entire Ley Line network. The sibling says they will one day find a "suitable host location" for it.
### ULTRA +
- The sibling still loves the Traveler. In 4.7 they say they miss their twin too, but that while the war continues they do not even know how to face themselves, let alone their twin. In 5.7, asked what the plan has to do with the two of them, they answer that it has "only ever been about us" and do not explain.

## accounts
### LARGE
- **The sibling**: frames it as a war with "destiny" and the Heavenly Principles. In 4.7 they point to Caribert, a cursed Khaenri'ahn denied any place in the world, and ask the Traveler who took that right from him. They add that their feelings about the Principles are too complicated to explain briefly.
- **Abyss Order members**: Haden, leading the Natlan operation, called reviving the homeland the Order's "greatest wish". He trusted that the sibling's regret was sincere, and asked whom else proud Khaenri'ahns could place their faith in, since they could hardly turn to the gods.
- **Dainsleif**: hunts the Order and doubts Khaenri'ah can be revived so easily. He believes the curse cannot be undone and read the Chasm device as an attempt to make cleansed hilichurls the foundation of a rebuilt Khaenri'ah.
- **Chlothar Alberich** (3.5, in a memory from centuries ago): told the sibling that Khaenri'ahns once believed they would bring "new strength and hope" and saw them as the Abyss itself. Dainsleif suspects this encounter was where the sibling's path began, but notes they never told him about it.
- **The Traveler** wonders privately whether the sibling is so set on reviving Khaenri'ah because they feel they failed as its Savior.
### ULTRA +
- **Celestia's side** (all speculative, and none of these speakers knows who the sibling is): in the "Gods' Limits" teaser, Istaroth suggests that the absent Asmoday may simply have "switched sides", and Ronova says that would be unthinkable unless Asmoday loved a new master more than herself. In 7.1 Rhinedottir still wonders whether the hooded figure beside Asmoday is that "new master". Naberius calls the idea Istaroth's passing whim.

## history
### LARGE
How the stated goals developed, in release order:
- 1.0, Prologue: Asmoday seals both twins in cubes as they try to flee Teyvat during the Cataclysm. The Abyss Order is exploiting Dvalin. In the "We Will Be Reunited" story teaser the sibling is shown overseeing that operation.
- 1.4, We Will Be Reunited: the Order plans to build a mechanized god from a Defiled Statue, Osial's limbs and the eye of the first Field Tiller. The sibling appears, reveals who Dainsleif is and declares their war with destiny.
- 2.6, Requiem of the Echoing Depths: the Chasm device meant to cleanse the hilichurls' curse backfires and is destroyed.
- 3.5, Caribert: through disturbed Ley Lines, the Traveler relives the sibling's past with Chlothar and the cursed Caribert in Sumeru.
- 4.7, Bedtime Story: the sibling recovers the Field Tiller's eye and pushes on with the Loom of Fate.
- 5.7, A Space and Time for You: the sibling weaves New Khaenri'ah into the Atlas of the New World.
- 6.5, A Long Day in the Mountains: Melanta reveals Asmoday chose the sibling.
- 7.1, A Rekviem for the Underworld: the sibling's Khaenri'ahn origin story gains a Celestia link, and they are seen in Snezhnaya with Asmoday.
### ULTRA +
Background to the goals: the Vinster King Irmin named the golden-haired newcomer heir to the throne and later used them as a vessel for unlimited Abyssal power. According to the glider text Wings of Fate's Course Intertwined, the Five Sinners' storming of the palace was tied to this, and it made the heir "the key to the world's near-annihilation". The same text says that, having lost their own world, they came to long for "a single realm".

## present
@version: 7.1
@spoiler: major
### LITE
As of 7.1 the sibling holds the Atlas of the New World, with no host location chosen. Asmoday appears to be with them, and the Heavenly Principles are still dormant.
### LARGE +
In Black Feathers Scattered at Parting (7.1), the Tsaritsa tells Rhinedottir about a monster that brought blizzards into Snezhnaya. Deep in the storm she saw Asmoday with a hooded figure, and the quest's stills show this figure is the sibling. The figure said it was worth witnessing "the end of the Third Descender's story". Asmoday then opened portals and both vanished, and the Tsaritsa killed the half-conscious monster.

In the same quest Ronova tells the Traveler she is waiting for Asmoday to return so the Four Shades can jointly activate their authority, or for conclusive evidence for a conviction, or failing that for the Heavenly Principles to awaken. In Etched in Time, Pierro reveals that his colleague Hroptatyr summoned the sibling from Celestia at the same moment Pierro summoned Paimon. Vedrfolnir had foretold that a blond "crowned heir" would answer Hroptatyr's call.

## connections
### LARGE
The goals tie into the Abyss Order and its Loom of Fate, the curse of the Khaenri'ahns, the Savior of Khaenri'ah, the dormancy of the Heavenly Principles, Asmoday's defection, and the Descender idea, since a Descender's power is described elsewhere in canon in terms of the backing of a world. The Traveler's own goal of reuniting with their twin is the other half of this thread.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay guidance (this project's recommendation): present the sibling's goals only as far as canon states them. Never give a definitive answer to what the "truth of this world" is, what they want from the Heavenly Principles, or where New Khaenri'ah will be placed. Ordinary Teyvat residents know nothing of this. Most do not even know that the Abyss Order has a Prince or Princess, let alone that it is the Traveler's twin.
### LARGE +
Characters who could plausibly know parts of this: Dainsleif, Paimon, the Traveler, and senior Abyss Order members such as Heralds and Haden. Citlali knows of the Atlas through the Lord of the Night, and Pierro knows of the Celestia summoning. The Tsaritsa and Rhinedottir saw or heard about a hooded figure, but do not know who it is.
### ULTRA +
Keep the sibling's tone as canon shows it: affectionate and regretful toward the Traveler, resolute about the war, and unwilling to explain everything. Do not portray them as wanting to destroy Teyvat's nations or as a simple villain; canon has not shown that. Equally, do not soften what they have done: they authorised the Chasm device knowing it would likely cause great suffering if it failed.

## entry: Atlas of the New World
@keys: Atlas of the New World, New Khaenri'ah, Loom of Fate
@version: 5.7
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The Atlas of the New World is what the Loom of Fate produced. It holds the full Ley Line network of a "New Khaenri'ah". The sibling believed Teyvat's Ley Lines were locked by the Heavenly Principles, so that none could be added or replaced, but learned that Natlan's Night Kingdom seemed to be an exception. They had the Abyss Order fake an invasion of Natlan to buy time to negotiate with the Lord of the Night, who gave room for the weaving but no new Ley Lines.
### LARGE +
With an Inteyvat flower as the base, the sibling grew a realm modelled on the Khaenri'ah of five hundred years ago and superimposed it on present-day Natlan's spacetime. This is how the Traveler met Direidyth. Order members meditating to recall the old nation supplied memories as raw material. The operation also drew in Dainsleif, whose memories of Khaenri'ah Haden called the most complete of any survivor. According to Citlali, the Lord of the Night allowed this because preserving a whole nation as an "Atlas" seemed a possible third option between Mavuika's view, that culture and memory should be protected, and the Captain's, that Ley Lines should be preserved above all. She did so once satisfied it would not affect Natlan, and probably on an undisclosed condition.

## entry: The war with destiny
@keys: war with destiny, engulfed the thrones
@version: 1.4
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
At the 1.4 reunion the sibling says home is wherever the twins are together, but that they cannot go to the next world yet. Until the Abyss has engulfed the thrones, their war with destiny (meaning the Heavenly Principles) will not end. They tell the Traveler that, by the end of their own journey, they too will see the world's true nature, and add that they have more than enough time to wait.

## entry: The Chasm cleansing device
@keys: Chasm cleansing device, curse-cleansing device
@keys2: Abyss Order, hilichurl, Chasm
@version: 2.6
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
In the Chasm the sibling found a pool whose water eased the curse, where dying hilichurls had gathered. They approved a device to amplify its effect a hundredfold, saying even a one-percent chance of success was enough. Dainsleif warned that failure would mean pointless agony for the hilichurls. Halfdan, Dainsleif and the Traveler destroyed it when it went wrong.

## entry: Asmoday and the sibling
@keys: Asmoday's new master, Melanta
@keys2: Asmoday, sibling, Abyss Order
@version: 6.5
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
Asmoday sealed the twins during the Cataclysm and kept the sibling in a coffin in the Temple of Space. She later stopped attending the Shades' meetings. In 6.5 her abandoned Domain Keeper Melanta demanded to know why Asmoday had chosen the Traveler's sibling and abandoned him. Her portals are seen closing after the sibling's 5.7 conversation with the Traveler, and again after Melanta's defeat. In 7.1 she escorts the hooded sibling away from the Tsaritsa. Her reasons remain unknown.

## entry: The hooded figure in Snezhnaya
@keys: hooded figure, end of the Third Descender's story
@keys2: Asmoday, Tsaritsa, Snezhnaya, sibling
@version: 7.1
@spoiler: major
### ULTRA
In 7.1 the Tsaritsa went alone into the heart of a snowstorm brought by an unknown monster and found Asmoday and a hooded figure beside it. Quest stills show the figure is the sibling. The figure remarked that it was worth witnessing "the end of the Third Descender's story" before Asmoday's portals took them both away. Rhinedottir wondered whether they came to witness the monster's existence or its death. Neither she nor the Tsaritsa identified the figure.
