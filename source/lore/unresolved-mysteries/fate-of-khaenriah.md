## summary
### LITE
Khaenri'ah, the godless underground kingdom, fell about five hundred years ago, and canon has never said cleanly what became of it. Its last king, Irmin, was reported "indisposed" and the Knight Marshal Anfortas took over as regent; neither man's end is stated. Most of its pure-blooded people are cursed to live on, and the Traveler's sibling says the Abyss Order is building a "New Khaenri'ah". As of 7.1, even a Khaenri'ahn alchemist like Rhinedottir says the old kingdom "no longer exists".
### STANDARD +
This entry gathers the open threads about the kingdom's future: the fate of its rulers, the survivors and their curses, and the competing hopes of reviving it. It does not resolve any of them.

## established
### LITE
- Khaenri'ah fell in the Cataclysm five hundred years ago. The gods and the Heavenly Principles were behind its destruction, the wiki's summary of canon says, as punishment for using forbidden knowledge.
- A notice found in the underground Khaenri'ahn ruins (Gavireh Lajavard, Sumeru) says King Irmin was in an "indisposed state" and the Knight Marshal Anfortas proclaimed himself temporary Regent. That is the last word on the monarchy.
- In 7.1 Rhinedottir says it is a shame that Khaenri'ah no longer exists, and she and Dainsleif both call the Tsaritsa the "Mother of Khaenri'ah".
### STANDARD +
- **The throne.** Irmin is called the last Vinster King of the Eclipse Dynasty and is known to have had one eye. The Alberich family teaching that Kaeya kept (Hidden Strife, 2.8, a limited-time event) says the Alberich Clan, which had no royal blood, "stepped in as regents when the strength of the one-eyed king Irmin failed". Anfortas is not named in that text; the link to him is the wiki's deduction.
- **The regent.** Anfortas led the Schwanenritter. The knights evacuated the able upward into Sumeru with their Ruin Golems. After surfacing, the evacuees began to sicken with an affliction that turned them into monsters, and some chose to go back underground. A coded message in Ruin Guards near Dharma Forest says the Schwanenritters fought to the last one.
- **The survivors.** Pure-blooded Khaenri'ahns were made immortal and eroded rather than dying; those with ancestry from other gods' lands became monsters, including hilichurls. The royal guards, the Black Serpent Knights, became husks or remained as changed Black Serpents.
- **The people.** In 7.1 Rhinedottir tells the Tsaritsa that all Khaenri'ahns descend from a bloodline begun with her and the Third Descender. The Tsaritsa says she was aware of this but has no memory of it.
### LARGE +
- **A nation of unusual survivors.** Some of the five hundred year old Khaenri'ahns became Abyss Mages, Heralds and Lectors; the Abyss Order, founded by Chlothar Alberich, grew out of the kingdom's destruction.
- **The Order's aim.** The wiki's account of canon is that the Order seeks to destroy the nations under the Seven in revenge and to complete the Traveler's sibling's goal of reviving Khaenri'ah.
- **What was built.** In 5.7 the sibling says they have woven the entire Ley Line network of New Khaenri'ah into the Atlas of the New World, and that "the nation once known as Khaenri'ah can rise again". They add that they will one day find a suitable host location. The nation does not yet physically exist.
### ULTRA +
- **The real site.** Khaenri'ah lay deep underground in the region of Sumeru (Kaeya's words, and the wiki). Sumeru's older scholars know the kingdom as "Dahri" and revere the Schwanenritter as "heroes of Dahri" (via the Order of Skeptics).
- **Present-day echoes.** In 7.1 Dainsleif reports that Snezhnaya has a way to speak with a past incarnation of The Wise (Hroptatyr). He found it disappointing: the young Wise has not been exposed to Khaenri'ah's dark side. That past echo is the closest thing to a living Khaenri'ah that 7.1 shows.

## accounts
### LARGE
- **Rhinedottir (7.1):** says that Khaenri'ah "would have been proud" to have a mother like the Tsaritsa, but that it is a shame the kingdom no longer exists. She speaks as a descendant of Khaenri'ah and a "mother" herself.
- **Dainsleif:** hunts the Abyss Order and doubts Khaenri'ah can be revived so easily. He says he could never refuse "the Mother of Khaenri'ah" the courtesy of an audience. In 7.1 he calls Snezhnaya's victory over Ronova the start of a new era and adds that it will not be peaceful.
- **The Traveler's sibling (5.7):** the revival is driven partly by guilt and partly by something else; the Ley Lines for New Khaenri'ah are complete, and a host place will come later.
- **Haden (5.7):** first claimed the homeland had already been revived, then admitted this was false once Dainsleif pressed him: the Ley Lines were not finished at that moment. His claim is a lie, not a revelation.
### ULTRA +
- **The Order's rank and file:** Haden says the revived homeland would let comrades forget five hundred years of struggle and begin their lives anew. This is his promise, not a shown outcome.
- **The Tsaritsa (7.1):** says she has no memory of being their ancestor and has never known how to face them.

## open_questions
### STANDARD
- Is Irmin dead, and if so how? Is he the "indisposed" king of the bulletin, or did the reports hide something?
- What became of Anfortas after the last knights fell, and did the Alberich regency outlast him?
- Can Khaenri'ah be revived, and if so where, as what, and at what cost to the people already living there?
- Does the Curse of Immortality end, and what would that mean for the surviving pure-bloods?
### LARGE +
- What will the surviving Khaenri'ahns want now that the Heavenly Principles are dormant and the Ruler of Death is gone?
- Rhinedottir says all Khaenri'ahns descend from the Tsaritsa and the Third Descender. What does that make the kingdom's "godless" claim, and does the Tsaritsa accept any duty to its people?
### ULTRA +
- The Order is described as seeking both a revived Khaenri'ah and vengeance on the nations under the Seven. Canon has not said how the sibling reconciles the two, or what a revived nation would do toward the surface nations.

## connections
### STANDARD
This is the forward-looking companion to the fall: see the Fall of Khaenri'ah and the Cataclysm for what happened, the Curse of the Khaenri'ahns for what the survivors carry, and the Traveler's Sibling's goals for the revival plan. The Heavenly Principles' dormancy and the Tsaritsa's plan explain why Snezhnaya is suddenly relevant to the kingdom's descendants.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Treat every statement about Khaenri'ah's future as belief or hope. Do not state that Irmin died, that Anfortas lived or died, that Khaenri'ah has been revived, or where New Khaenri'ah will stand. Characters differ: Dainsleif is sceptical, the Order is determined, and Rhinedottir is wistful. Do not use unreleased story content.

## entry: Irmin's Indisposition
@keys: indisposed king, Irmin's fate, King Irmin indisposed
@version: 3.6
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
A weathered notice in the underground Khaenri'ahn ruins beneath Sumeru says that, given King Irmin's "indisposed state" and the unknown threats facing the Kingdom, Knight Marshal Anfortas proclaimed himself temporary Regent. It is the only in-world statement on the king's condition; his death, removal or survival is never stated.
### LARGE +
The same ruin holds an unfinished letter urging the Knight Marshal to petition the king to preserve machines made to the older standard, trusting "human will" over otherworldly methods. The wiki reads it as addressed to Anfortas. Irmin is also called "one-eyed" in the Alberich family teaching from the 2.8 event.

## entry: Regent Anfortas
@keys: Anfortas, Regent Anfortas, Marshal Regent, Regnum Concilium Ultimum
@version: 3.6
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Knight Marshal of the Schwanenritter. When the Cataclysm struck and Irmin was "indisposed", he declared himself Regent and took charge of the Regnum Concilium Ultimum, the Final Kingdom Council. The knights then led survivors up to Sumeru. His own fate is not stated; he appears only in documents and a hidden coded message ("In the Name of Anfortas") saying the knights fought to the last.
### LARGE +
The wiki treats him as an Alberich because the family teaching credits the clan with serving as regents, but that is a deduction, not stated canon. His connection to Chlothar Alberich, founder of the Abyss Order, is likewise not made explicit.

## entry: Mother of Khaenri'ah
@keys: Mother of Khaenri'ah
@version: 7.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
In 7.1 Rhinedottir greets the Tsaritsa Anastasya with this title and says all Khaenri'ahns descend from a bloodline begun with her and the Third Descender. Anastasya says she was aware of it, although she has no memory of it, and has never known how to face them. Dainsleif later uses the same title when he says he could not refuse her an audience.
### LARGE +
Rhinedottir recounts that the Belyi Tsar incited Anastasya, then only a herald of the court, to open a gate to the Abyss with the Third Descender, and that the Heavenly Principles erased her existence as punishment. The Third Descender traded his remains to bring her back, with a curse that would bite if the bargain were broken. Anastasya infers that the Cataclysm triggered that curse, but Rhinedottir declines to confirm it.

## entry: New Khaenri'ah
@keys: New Khaenri'ah, Atlas of the New World
@version: 5.7
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The Traveler's sibling says the Loom of Fate has finished weaving the Ley Line network of a restored Khaenri'ah and that it is held in the Atlas of the New World. They plan to find a suitable host location for it one day, and say the nation once known as Khaenri'ah can rise again. Haden had earlier claimed the homeland was already revived and then admitted the Ley Lines were not yet complete.
### LARGE +
Dainsleif calls the notion that a nation could be revived this way preposterous until he sees it himself. In the final cutscene the sibling folds the space where Khaenri'ah once stood into the Atlas. The scene also involves Direidyth, and what exactly the vanishing space represents is not explained.

## entry: The Cursed Survivors
@keys: Khaenri'ah survivors, surviving Khaenri'ahns, pure-blooded Khaenri'ahns
@version: 7.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Pure-blooded Khaenri'ahns live on under the Curse of Immortality, eroding rather than dying. Dainsleif, Pierro and Chlothar (as Eide) are the clearest cases, along with the cursed remnants such as the Black Serpent Knights. People of mixed ancestry became monsters. As of 7.1, no cursed Khaenri'ahn has been shown freed, even after Ronova lost her authority.
