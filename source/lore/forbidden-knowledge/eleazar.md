## summary
### LITE
Eleazar was a chronic illness found only in Sumeru. Patients grew dark, hardened scales, felt numbness and clumsiness, and in the end lost control of their bodies. It had no cure; at best its symptoms could be managed. Canon presents it as the Withering showing itself in the human body. After Rukkhadevata's tainted consciousness was cleansed from Irminsul in the Sumeru Archon Quest, every case recovered within days and the disease ceased to exist.
### STANDARD
Treat Eleazar as a past-tense disease: before that Archon Quest it was a feared, incurable condition tied to forbidden knowledge, and it has since disappeared. Hospital records note that some patients (the "Specimens") believed it was divine punishment, and desperate families sometimes grasped at false promises of a cure.
### LARGE +
Because it correlated with a person's elemental content, it was both a medical puzzle for Akademiya researchers and an opening for those who wanted to exploit the sick, notably Fatui agents working under Dottore.

## established
@version: 3.0
### LITE
- Symptoms: dark, hardened scales and slight numbness (causing clumsiness), then fatigue and nerve damage; scales progress to ulcers and necrosis; late-stage patients are immobile and may fall into a coma.
- It is a manifestation of the Withering on the body and is unique to Sumeru.
- No true cure existed; it was eradicated when Rukkhadevata's tainted consciousness was purged from Irminsul.
### STANDARD
- Hospital research notes say sufferers carry a higher elemental content than ordinary people, and that managing and reducing it can control the disease. The unnamed researcher found one treatment that made a late-stage patient's symptoms vanish entirely.
- Nilotpala Lotus can help patients regain energy, and Sumeru's forests are said to ease the illness, though the Withering made the disease noticeably worse.
- Some people were born with it: Collei and Dunyarzad are given as examples. A boy named Arbaaz, described as "little", is recorded at the hospital.
- Cases were increasing in Sumeru City, and residents with symptoms were urged to go to the Bimarstan.
### LARGE +
- The Chinese name means roughly "Demon Scale Disease"; the English name's possible Hebrew origin is only an etymological guess.
- Named cases listed by the wiki include Abbas, Arbaaz, Avin, Collei, Dunyarzad, Gulab, Hakim, Kumar and Emira; Arbaaz, Avin, Gulab, Hakim and Emira died from the illness or from complications of treatment.
### ULTRA +
- Collei's treatment side effects (trauma, unstable control over "black fire", plague-like symptoms) reportedly faded after Cyno sealed the god's remains inside her, but her Eleazar symptoms then returned, which is why she was taken to Tighnari at Gandharva Ville for care.

## accounts
@version: 3.0
### STANDARD
- The Akademiya built a hospital at Dar al-Shifa in the Great Red Sand to study and treat Eleazar. Its doctor found a working treatment using an unknown material. The records mention "Specimens" who called the treatment blasphemous, because they believed the illness was divine punishment. The hospital was later abandoned for undisclosed reasons and the Akademiya barred access to the ruins.
- The Fatui under Il Dottore also researched treatments using a god's remains. One of his subordinates falsely told parents the Fatui had a cure, collecting afflicted children as test subjects; Collei was among those handed over (Tighnari, in Chronic Illness, says her mother did so after a Fatui member lied about having a cure).
### LARGE +
- Abbas, a 23-year-old patient, was the lone survivor of a group of four at the hospital: the doctor cured him using body parts taken from the other three. Horrified, he tunnelled out and escaped. This is told through hospital notes and etchings, so it reflects what the documents say rather than a witnessed scene.
### ULTRA +
- Collei's treatment left her deeply traumatised according to her voice-overs and the Chronic Illness quest; her story is told across the Sumeru Archon Quest and the Collei manga rather than in one account.

## open_questions

## connections
@version: 3.2
### LITE
Eleazar is linked to the Withering, to forbidden knowledge, and to Irminsul.
### STANDARD
- The Withering: Eleazar is the form it takes in human bodies.
- King Deshret: Kasala's memories show a strikingly similar affliction in his day: darkened scales and crazed whispers in people's minds after forbidden knowledge reached them. Rukkhadevata helped erase the knowledge, and Deshret sealed it away at the cost of his life and his civilisation.
- Irminsul: once Rukkhadevata's tainted consciousness was removed, the symptoms disappeared and patients recovered within days.
### LARGE +
- In the 3.2 Archon Quest Where the Boat of Consciousness Lies, the cleansing is shown as green petals drifting over Sumeru and falling on people afflicted by Eleazar, and on those driven mad by connecting their minds to Irminsul. As of the 7.1 cutoff no canon source shows Eleazar returning.

## entry: Eleazar Hospital
@keys: Eleazar Hospital, Dar al-Shifa, Eleazar Hospital Notes
@version: 3.0
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
An Akademiya hospital in the Great Red Sand built to study and treat Eleazar. It is now abandoned and closed to the public by the Akademiya. Documents left behind include a diagnostic report linking Eleazar to high elemental content, a record of a treatment that worked, and Abbas's account of fleeing after surviving it.

## entry: Abbas
@keys: Abbas, Abbas' Escape
@version: 3.0
@spoiler: minor
### LARGE
Abbas was a 23-year-old patient at the Eleazar Hospital and the only survivor of four. He was cured with body parts taken from the other three and fled by digging underground. Known from documents, not as a living character in the story.

## entry: Collei and Eleazar
@keys: Collei
@keys2: Eleazar, scales, Fatui cure, black fire
@version: 3.0
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
Collei was born with Eleazar. Her mother, like other parents, handed her to the Fatui after one of Dottore's subordinates lied that they had a cure. She was treated using a god's remains, which suppressed her symptoms but left her traumatised and with unstable "black fire". After Cyno sealed the remains in her body, her Eleazar symptoms returned, and she was taken to Tighnari in Gandharva Ville. Her full biography belongs in her own entry.
