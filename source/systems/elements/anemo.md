## summary
### LITE
Anemo is the element of wind, one of Teyvat's seven elements. Its Archon is Barbatos, who lives in Mondstadt as the bard Venti; Mondstadt is the nation of wind and freedom. People with Anemo Visions raise gusts and updrafts, pull or push things with vortices, and glide on the wind. The Anemo Dragon Sovereign is Dvalin, once called Stormterror. The Harbinger Signora took the Anemo Gnosis from Venti for the Tsaritsa.
### STANDARD +
Neuvillette calls Barbatos "the God of Breeze and Hope." Anemo ascension gems are Vayuda Turquoise.

## in_universe
### LITE
Most people cannot use Anemo. Those who can usually hold an Anemo Vision. Venti draws on the Anemo Authority of his divine throne in Celestia instead; the "Vision" on his hip is, in his words, "a glowing glass ball I carry around to avoid suspicion."
### STANDARD +
Legend says that after Barbatos became Archon about 2,600 years ago, he blew away the ice and snow left by the Archon War, scattered mountains, flattened cliffs into plains and guided warm monsoons into Mondstadt. Legend also credits his blessing with making the first wind gliders fly. Mondstadters call their wine a "gift from the Anemo Archon."
### ULTRA +
Anemo is tied to the old idea of "the thousand winds." In Mondstadt the phrase means the named, personified winds said to protect the land. Venti was once "a single thread" of them, a tiny nameless wind spirit, before he gathered power and rose to godhood, and his power over wind is often described as calling on the thousand winds. In Enkanomiya belief, though, "the thousand winds of time" means Istaroth, the Ruler of Time. Sacrificial Bow text records that ancestors on the eastern cliffs worshipped "the masters of Time and Anemo together", with the saying "Anemo brings stories while Time nurtures them." Canon leaves the exact relationship between wind and time open.

## origins
@version: 5.1
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
Origins, as canon tells them. The elemental Authorities first belonged to the Seven Sovereigns, the elemental dragons of the old world. The Heavenly Principles stole part of that power and tied it to the Divine Thrones, which became the Archons' Authorities (revealed in the Fontaine Archon Quest, 4.1); Visions also derive from these Authorities. In the Natlan Archon Quest (5.1), the Lord of the Night reveals that the Heavenly Principles created elemental energy by dividing the raw elemental currents of the Light Realm into seven, using Phlogiston, Teyvat's primordial energy, as a base; the result was easier to control and meant to better counter the Abyss. How an Archon relates to their element, and why a nation takes on its element's character, is still unexplained.

## archon_and_nation
### LITE
Archon: Barbatos (Venti), the God of Freedom, who with Morax is one of the two surviving original members of The Seven. Nation: Mondstadt, which he deliberately leaves to govern itself.
### STANDARD +
He became Archon about 2,600 years ago after the rebellion against Decarabian, the God of Storms, then withdrew from rule for fear of becoming a tyrant. Few Mondstadters know that the bard Venti is their god. Venti calls himself the weakest Archon, because Archons draw power from how much control they hold over their nations.
### LARGE +
The Anemo throne was contested. In the Age of Kings, Decarabian, behind his storm wall, and Boreas, a wolf-god from the north whose blizzards engulfed the land, were locked in a stalemate. After Vennessa's rebellion against the aristocracy a thousand years ago, Barbatos established the Four Winds as Mondstadt's protectors: the Wolf of the North, the Lion of the South, the Dragon of the East and the Falcon of the West. They were worshipped for about 500 years before people forgot them. Mondstadt is governed by the Knights of Favonius, and the Church of Favonius tends his worship. According to the Biography of Gunnhildr, the wind spirit who would become Barbatos answered the prayers of the Gunnhildr Clan (Jean's family line) in the blizzards, sheltered them and gave Gunnhildr "the power to protect."

## succession
@version: 6.4
@spoiler: major
### ULTRA
Varka's Story Quest (Version Luna V, 6.4) reveals that after Decarabian fell, Boreas judged himself unworthy to be the Anemo Archon because he lacked a god's love for humanity. Boreas was a wolf from Snezhnaya made a god by Koitar's blessing in Nod-Krai. He returned to the Royal Court of the Seelie in Nod-Krai and went into slumber. The wind spirit became Barbatos and (per Venti's character stories) took the form and name of his fallen friend, the bard Venti. Boreas's later manifestation at Wolvendom, Andrius, is a temporary form made of ice and wind.

## gnosis_and_authority
### STANDARD
In Ending Note (Prologue, 1.0), Signora ambushed a weakened Venti and pulled the Anemo Gnosis from his chest. A Gnosis, Venti explained, is an Archon's internal focus that resonates directly with Celestia. Later canon shows that Archons keep their divine thrones and their elemental power without a Gnosis.
### LARGE +
Venti also told the Traveler that Vision holders are "allogenes" who may one day ascend. Besides her own Cryo Gnosis, the Tsaritsa gained the Anemo and Geo Gnoses first, then the Electro, Dendro and Hydro Gnoses; the Pyro Gnosis never reached her. In the official teaser "A Winter Night's Lazzo", in which the Harbingers gather to mourn Signora, the Anemo and Geo Gnoses appear as pieces on Pierro's chessboard.

## elemental_beings
### LITE
Dvalin, the Anemo Dragon Sovereign, and Anemo elemental lifeforms such as slimes, specters and the Anemo Hypostasis.
### STANDARD +
Dvalin, Dragon of the East, was born from a convergence of elemental energy in the high heavens and succeeded an unnamed, dead Anemo Sovereign. Anemo Samachurls are hilichurl shamans who command the winds.
### LARGE +
- Dvalin befriended Barbatos and has full command of sky and wind.
- Anemo Slimes form when Anemo scattered through nature coalesces, and float on the wind. Anemo Specters live on Seirai and Watatsumi in Inazuma. The Samachurls' power over the flowing winds "stems from dark, forgotten memories."
- Gods of wind: Barbatos himself began as an elemental wind spirit. Decarabian, the God of Storms, ruled Old Mondstadt behind a storm wall.
- Anemoculi: consolidated winds that, according to The Blessings of The Seven, only allogenes and the Traveler can see.

## known_uses
### LITE
Gusts and updrafts, gliding, whirlwinds that gather foes or debris, and pushing things away. Anemo also scatters other elements.
### STANDARD +
Anemo also raises barriers: Decarabian's storm wall resisted even Boreas, and a storm barrier sealed Stormterror's Lair in the Prologue. It can sharpen the senses: Kazuha reads the sounds of nature to sense rain or snow from a single gust.
### LARGE +
- Reshaping land and climate: Barbatos moved mountains and brought warm monsoons to Mondstadt.
- The Wanderer first "could hear the wind's voice" at the moment his Vision came.
- The Traveler: in Bird's Eye View, Paimon takes the Traveler to the Statue of The Seven of the god who "controls the wind", and Anemo is the first element they wield. In the Prologue their Anemo power suddenly flares near Venti and Dvalin, startling the dragon and breaking Venti's attempt to heal him.
- Recovery: Venti purged Abyssal magic he had caught from Dvalin with the help of the great oak at Windrise, and returned there to recover after losing his Gnosis.
### ULTRA +
- Visions are personal: Sucrose's Anemo Vision appeared in her cauldron during her 159th dandelion-seed simmering experiment, and Kazuha, who holds an Anemo Vision, once briefly awakened his late friend's Masterless Electro Vision.

## limits
### LITE
Anemo does not react with Geo or Dendro.
### STANDARD +
Swirl spreads an element that is already present; it does not create one. A Vision fixes its holder to one element: Kazuha's brief Electro use came from a second, borrowed Vision.
### LARGE +
Gameplay: an Anemo aura cannot be applied by attacks; the exception is the Anemo Hypostasis, which coats itself in Anemo while its core is exposed. Masterless Visions lose their element and power when their holder dies, though some later resonate with a new person.

## interactions
### LITE
Swirl: when Anemo meets Pyro, Hydro, Electro or Cryo it spreads that element to nearby targets (own entry).
### STANDARD +
Elemental Absorption: many Anemo skills take on Pyro, Hydro, Electro or Cryo on first contact and deal that element's damage as well.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not literal lore): Anemo is a damage type and the element of characters such as Venti, Jean, Sucrose, Xiao, Kaedehara Kazuha, the Wanderer and Xianyun.
### LARGE +
Anemo elemental enemies are immune to Anemo damage, and Anemo Samachurls resist it. The Elemental Resonance "Impetuous Winds" (two or more Anemo characters) cuts stamina use by 15%, raises movement speed by 10% and shortens skill cooldowns by 5%. Many Anemo talents create Vacuum Fields that pull or push targets. Anemoculi raise the Statues of The Seven in Mondstadt, and Anemo Sigils are a Mondstadt currency. Exploration includes Wind Currents (updrafts), Storm Barriers around objects and Anemo Ambers that release an updraft when broken. Swirl damage scales with the Anemo user's level and Elemental Mastery rather than ATK. Every Traveler begins as Anemo, resonating with a Statue of The Seven in Mondstadt in Bird's Eye View (Prologue Act I).

## gameplay_recent
@version: 7.1
### ULTRA
Gameplay, recent additions: Stellar Swirl (7.0) is a Stellar Glimmer reaction pairing Anemo with Cryo. It deals Anemo damage and leaves a Stellar Vortex that bursts for Cryo area damage, grows stronger with repeated triggers and briefly boosts jump height (own entry). Recent Anemo characters include Chasca, Varka (6.4) and Vesna (7.1); the Elements page lists Vesna's element source as a Stellar Linchpin rather than a Vision.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay interpretation (this project's recommendation, not canon text): show Anemo as pressure, sound and motion: cloaks snapping, grass flattening in a ring, dust spiralling. Teal or aqua-green light is a safe cue; Venti's braids glow Anemo-aqua when he uses his power. Keep scale proportionate: a Vision holder can knock people down or glide from a cliff, but reshaping mountains is an Archon's work.
### LARGE +
Wind users can plausibly notice weather, scents or sounds carried on a breeze, as Kazuha does. Narratively, Anemo carries things: other elements, voices, seeds, songs, news. A Swirl should visibly carry flame, water, sparks or frost outward. Mondstadters associate the wind with freedom and their god, so a sudden favourable breeze reads as a good omen. Do not have an ordinary Mondstadter recognise Venti as Barbatos.
### ULTRA +
Avoid portraying Anemo as weather control for any Vision holder, or as able to suffocate people by removing air; canon shows neither. Do not give the Anemo Gnosis back to Venti or place it anywhere except with the Tsaritsa as of 7.1.

## entry: Anemo Dragon Sovereign
@keys: Anemo Sovereign, Anemo Dragon Sovereign, Wind Dragon
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
The Anemo Dragon Sovereign today is Dvalin, who succeeded an earlier, unnamed Anemo dragon who died before the game's events. Dvalin was born from a convergence of elemental energy in the high heavens and drawn to Barbatos's lyre. He became the Dragon of the East of the Four Winds after Vennessa's rebellion.
### LARGE +
500 years ago, during the Cataclysm, Dvalin killed the black dragon Durin over Dragonspine but swallowed its poisoned blood. He slept in Decarabian's Tower, and the Abyss Order exploited his pain. The Traveler, Venti, Jean and Diluc freed him in the Prologue, and he is now Mondstadt's ally again.

## entry: Anemoculus
@keys: Anemoculus, Anemoculi
### STANDARD
Anemoculi are consolidated winds found across Mondstadt. According to The Blessings of The Seven, only allogenes and the Traveler can see them. They are offered at Statues of The Seven, which legend says once had gem eyes that swallows carried off, in exchange for The Seven's blessing. Item text describes the Anemoculus as a substance "that has accumulated intense Anemo energy", offered to help a statue regain power it has lost.

## entry: Thousand Winds
@keys: thousand winds, thousand threads, Thousand Winds Temple
### LARGE
"The thousand winds" is an old phrase with competing meanings. In Mondstadt it names the personified winds that protect the land; Venti was once a single nameless thread among them. In Enkanomiya belief, "the thousand winds of time" refers to Istaroth, the Ruler of Time, and the Thousand Winds Temple keeps carvings linking the gods of Time and Anemo. Legend says the original Skyward Atlas recorded the names of all the thousand winds. Treat the link between wind and time as unresolved.

## entry: Anemo elemental lifeforms
@keys: Anemo Slime, Anemo Specter, Anemo Hypostasis, Anemo Samachurl
### LARGE
Anemo Slimes form when Anemo scattered through nature coalesces. They puff up and drift down when knocked into the air. Anemo Specters haunt Seirai Island and Watatsumi Island in Inazuma. The Anemo Hypostasis is a boss made of shell pieces around a core; it builds vortices and tornadoes that absorb other elements. Anemo Samachurls are hilichurl shamans who call up small tornadoes and vacuum traps. In gameplay, the elemental lifeforms are immune to Anemo, and Samachurls resist it.
