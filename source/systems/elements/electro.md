## summary
### LITE
Electro is the element of lightning, one of Teyvat's seven elements. Its Archon is Beelzebul, publicly the Raiden Shogun, the God of Eternity who rules Inazuma, the land of thunder. Electro Vision holders conjure sparks, bolts and charged storms, and Electro reacts with every other element but itself. As of 7.1 the Electro Gnosis is no longer with the Archon: it passed through several hands and now belongs to the Tsaritsa.
### STANDARD +
The people of Inazuma associate Electro with their god, with storms and with the Shogunate. Vajrada Amethyst is the ascension gem of Electro characters, named after the vajra, a thunderbolt weapon of Indra.

## in_universe
### LITE
Most people cannot use Electro. Those who can usually hold an Electro Vision. The current Electro Archon, Ei, draws on the Electro Authority of her divine throne in Celestia; she no longer holds the Electro Gnosis.
### STANDARD +
Electro appears in nature as lightning during a thunderstorm. Lightning can strike a person or the body of water they stand in.
### LARGE +
Electro also has everyday uses: Keqing uses hers to stun fish for a meal (her "Survival Hairpin" character story). Ei's Musou Isshin story ties the sword to lightning: it says "the aesthetic of lightning is precisely to capture that which is precious amidst fleeting transience."

Electro and storms are bound together across Inazuma's history. Raiden Makoto and Ei came to power in the Archon War and ruled jointly; after Makoto died in the cataclysm 500 years ago, Ei succeeded her. Seirai Island, where the Thunderbird Kapatcir once raged, still has storms that the Adventurers' Guild finds dangerous to cross (Seirai Stormchasers, 2.1). Thundering Fury's artifact text describes ancient peoples who feared the Thunderbird's power and thanked it for blessings, while the monster itself paid them no heed.
### ULTRA +
Canon uses the vajra as the element's emblem in item text: the Vajrada Amethyst's full description is a paraphrase of what Ei says in the Chapter II finale, that her body is the noblest in the world and had promised her people an unchanging "eternity". The wiki traces the name to the vajra of Indra, a god of rain and thunder; it is the only gem group named after a weapon rather than a deity.

## origins
@version: 5.1
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
Origins, as canon tells them. The elemental Authorities first belonged to the Seven Sovereigns, the elemental dragons of the old world. The Heavenly Principles stole part of that power and tied it to the Divine Thrones, which became the Archons' Authorities (revealed in the Fontaine Archon Quest, 4.1); Visions also derive from these Authorities. In the Natlan Archon Quest (5.1), the Lord of the Night reveals that the Heavenly Principles created elemental energy by dividing the raw elemental currents of the Light Realm into seven, using Phlogiston, Teyvat's primordial energy, as a base; the result was easier to control and meant to better counter the Abyss. How an Archon relates to their element, and why a nation takes on its element's character, is still unexplained.
### ULTRA +
Canon never names an Electro Dragon Sovereign. The wiki's table of known Sovereigns lists Anemo, Geo, Hydro, Pyro and Cryo, and Apep is the Dendro Sovereign, but there is nothing for Electro; treat the old Electro Authority as unattributed. Bathysmal Vishaps are the one dragon-kin tie to Electro that canon describes: The book The Byakuyakoku Collection records that they strayed from "pure" Hydro forms into "impure" Cryo and Electro variants, which is why the next Hydro Sovereign was not born from them.

## archon_and_nation
### LITE
Archon: Ei, whose divine name is Beelzebul, the God of Eternity, publicly known as the Raiden Shogun. "Baal", the divine name of her late twin Makoto, and the title Raiden Shogun were long understood to refer to both twins. Nation: Inazuma, ruled by the Inazuma Shogunate.
### STANDARD +
Ei is the second holder of the title; the first was her twin Makoto, who died in the cataclysm 500 years ago. About 400 years ago Ei built the Shogun, a puppet in her image that governs Inazuma, and placed her own mind in the Plane of Euthymia, sealed inside the sword Musou Isshin. Most Inazumans do not know the Shogun is a puppet or that Makoto existed.
### LARGE +
Ei had served as Makoto's kagemusha, or body double, after Makoto ascended through Ei's sacrifice, so most people never learned there were two. Ei and Makoto were identical twins who lived at the start of the Archon War, about 6,000 years ago by the wiki's reckoning. About 2,000 years ago Ei defeated the last gods of the archipelago and, with Makoto, united the islands under the Inazuma Shogunate. Ei met Morax and Barbatos at one of the Seven's reunions in Liyue after the war ended. She keeps the title despite having passed the Electro Gnosis to Yae Miko, which shows that an Archon's seat in Celestia is independent of the Gnosis. Neuvillette's voice-over about the Electro Archon says that by taking the Authority of Electro "the Narukami placed herself on the list of the usurpers", and that he will visit her in a few centuries since she rarely appears.
### ULTRA +
Chapter II (Inazuma Archon Quest, 2.1) ends with the Traveler and Ei facing the Vision Hunt Decree. Fed by the Fatui-compromised Tri-Commission, the Shogun puppet had enforced the Sakoku Decree and Vision Hunt Decree with Ei's tacit approval. On the steps of Tenshukaku, the Traveler, helped by Yae Miko and drawing on the light of the seized Visions, convinced Ei to rescind the Vision Hunt Decree. In her later story quests she dueled the puppet over a new idea of Eternity, the Sakoku Decree was lifted and she began to take a more active part in ruling Inazuma. See the Raiden Shogun's own entry for the person; this entry covers only her role as the element's Archon.

## gnosis_and_authority
@version: 2.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Ei gave the Electro Gnosis to Yae Miko about 400 years ago, before retreating into the Plane of Euthymia. She remains the Electro Archon without it.
### LARGE +
A Gnosis is an Archon's focus resonating directly with Celestia. Ei had no use for hers, and none of her techniques could modify it. The Gnosis's later route: Yae Miko traded it to Scaramouche (the Wanderer) in exchange for the Traveler's safety; Nahida took it from him in Sumeru (Archon Quest Chapter III, 3.x) and later gave it to Dottore in exchange for his agreeing to erase his segments. By the time of Truth Amongst the Pages of Purana it was one of six Gnoses in the Fatui's hands. Scaramouche could manipulate lightning and weather while connected to it, but only after heavy modification with Sumeru Akademiya's help; Ei's own attempts to link him to it had failed. Nahida, by contrast, could not use it in battle against Dottore, though she did use it to open Greater Lord Rukkhadevata's realm of consciousness. According to Dottore, a Gnosis's use by anyone but its Archon may normally be restricted.
### ULTRA +
Besides her own Cryo Gnosis, the Tsaritsa gained the Anemo and Geo Gnoses first, then the Electro, Dendro and Hydro Gnoses; the Pyro Gnosis never reached her. In the teaser "A Winter Night's Lazzo", after Capitano asks about Scaramouche and the Electro Gnosis, Dottore replies that "Divine Knowledge cannot be rationally comprehended."

## gnosis_status
@version: 7.1
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
In the 7.1 Archon Quest "A Rekviem for the Underworld", Anastasya (the Tsaritsa) used her Cryo mastery together with the six Gnoses, the Electro Gnosis among them, to slow time within a room of her palace and stall Rhinedottir. As of 7.1 the Electro Gnosis remains in her possession; Ei does not have it.

## vision_hunt_note
@version: 2.1
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
For about a year before the Traveler reached Inazuma, no new Electro Visions appeared. Ei was surprised to hear of it, so the cause remains unknown.
### LARGE +
Sumeru Akademiya scholars concluded that the Archons' wills govern Vision distribution, and Kazuha guessed it reflected the Shogun's doubts. Electro Visions were also among those confiscated. The Vision of Kazuha's friend, an Electro holder killed during the Vision Hunt Decree, became Masterless, and Kazuha briefly awakened it. A Masterless Vision loses its element and power when its holder dies, though one may resonate with a new person. Tartaglia, a Harbinger, has an Electro Delusion that Pierro pinned on him when he became one; as a playable character he uses only Hydro. During the Vision Hunt Decree the Shogunate laid the confiscated Visions into the Statue of the Omnipresent God.

## elemental_beings
### LITE
Electro elemental lifeforms such as Electro Slimes, Electro Specters and the Electro Hypostasis, and the Thunder Manifestation of Amakumo Peak.
### STANDARD +
Electro Slimes form from Electro coalescing in nature. Electro Specters are born of highly concentrated Electro and float. The Electro Hypostasis (code name Aleph) is a high-purity Electro entity at Cape Oath in Mondstadt. The Electro Regisvine of Sumeru's Lokapala Jungle is a giant plant that releases charge stored in the ley lines.
### LARGE +
- Slimes: the Archive says their jumps reflect the electric potential difference in the ground, a behaviour that can be used to avoid danger in Electro-rich areas.
- Hypostases are the highest forms of elemental structures, formed at places bursting with elemental energy or at a clogged ley line.
- Thunder Manifestation (Amakumo Peak, Seirai Island, introduced 2.1): the Archive calls it an Electro life form that weaves thunder and lightning as a song to its resentment. It resembles an Oceanid but has none of her intelligence or memories, being driven by primal fury. Such beings are said to descend only where great lingering resentment remains, and as long as the grudges of the land do not fade, the thunder persists. Its origin is the remnant of the Thunderbird Kapatcir's power, kept in check at the Asase Shrine until Hibiki released it.
- Electro Specters are also found in Inazuma and Enkanomiya. One Archive entry jokes about a researcher who believed they must be Hypostases and was later investigated for academic corruption.
- The Regisvine entry says Electro awakens plants and boosts soil fertility, and that Kshahrewar researchers once proposed a circuit matrix around one to carry electricity to the city; Spantamad scholars opposed this and sacrificed ten Shroomboars to make their point.
### ULTRA +
Gods of lightning: Makoto and Ei are the gods of Electro in canon. Kapatcir was a monster rather than a god, though the Thunderbird's tribe worshipped it and read its actions as revelations.

## known_uses
### LITE
Lightning strikes, sparks that chain between foes, charged fields, and fast bursts of speed. Electro also sets off reactions with Pyro, Hydro, Cryo, Anemo, Geo and Dendro.
### STANDARD +
Electro also drives devices in Inazuma: Lightning Strike Probes respond to anyone affected by Electro.
### LARGE +
- Electro Crystals and lanterns shock whoever touches them.
- Ei's sword stroke Musou no Hitotachi split Musoujin Gorge; the Follow the Wind cutscene says it is seen only during "divine punishment."
- The Musou Isshin story recalls the blade's first battle amid "wild winds and blazing thunder".
- The Traveler resonates with Electro at an Inazuma Statue of The Seven, and fights with lightning in the cinematics of Amidst Stormy Judgment and The Omnipresent God.
- Razor's Vision story notes that, though he had obtained a Vision, he was still unable to manipulate Electro energy with finesse: a Vision grants power but not instant skill.
### ULTRA +
- Visions are personal: Fischl, Keqing and Beidou also hold Electro Visions, and Kazuha briefly used a Masterless Electro Vision from his late friend.
- Kapatcir's lightning razed Tsurumi Island's mountain in her grief over a murdered boy; a curse she left on the land would only lift if she heard his song again (Thundering Fury artifact lore).

## limits
### LITE
Electro does not react with itself. It has no effect of its own beyond enabling reactions.
### STANDARD +
Electro on its own does not change the target; it only enables reactions. A Vision fixes its holder to one element: Kazuha's brief Electro use came from a second Vision.
### LARGE +
Gameplay: Electro Slimes and Specters are immune to Electro; Hypostases are too. Masterless Visions lose their element and power when their holder dies.

## interactions
### LITE
Overloaded (with Pyro) explodes. Superconduct (with Cryo) damages and weakens Physical resistance. Electro-Charged (with Hydro) damages over time. Swirl spreads Electro. Crystallize with Geo forms a shield-granting shard. Quicken with Dendro enables Aggravate and Spread.
### LARGE +
- Overloaded causes an explosion that deals AoE Pyro damage with high knockback.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not literal lore): Electro is a damage type and the element of characters such as Fischl, Keqing, Raiden Shogun, Yae Miko, Cyno, Flins and Ineffa. Its ascension gems are Vajrada Amethyst.
### LARGE +
Other Electro characters include Lisa, Beidou, Razor, Kujou Sara, Kuki Shinobu, Dori, Clorinde, Sethos, Varesa, Iansan, Ororon and Alyosha. Elemental Resonance "High Voltage" (two or more Electro characters): affected by Hydro for 40% less time; Superconduct, Overloaded, Electro-Charged, Lunar-Charged, Quicken, Aggravate or Hyperbloom have a 100% chance to generate an Electro Elemental Particle (5s cooldown). Electroculi (181 can be found in the overworld) raise the Statues of The Seven in Inazuma, and Electro Sigils, which come from chests, one-time domains and the Statues, raise the Sacred Sakura's Favor and upgrade Electrograna. The 4-piece Thundering Fury artifact set boosts Electro reaction damage; Thundersoother resists Electro damage. Exploration includes Electro Crystals, Electro Current Puzzles, Electro Rock Piles and Electro lanterns that apply Electro to the active character. The Traveler can resonate with Electro only if they unlock an Inazuma Statue of The Seven, and the game never forces this.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay interpretation (this project's recommendation, not canon text): show Electro as light, sound and sudden force: a bright flash, a crack and rumble, hair lifting from static, the metallic smell of a storm. Violet is the usual colour cue, matching the Vajrada Amethyst and the Thunderbird's purple thunder; the wiki notes Electro can occasionally be seen as yellow. Keep scale proportionate: a Vision holder can arc sparks, not summon a storm.
### LARGE +
Narratively, Electro is the element of sudden change and of reactions: it sets things off. It also carries the Inazuma themes of eternity versus fleeting moments: her Musou Isshin story says the aesthetic of lightning is to capture something precious amid fleeting transience. Scenes in Inazuma can plausibly include storms, lightning-struck trees and thunder rolling in over the sea. Most Inazumans do not know that the Shogun is a puppet, that Makoto existed, or that Ei is a second Archon; the Raiden Shogun is the face of the god.
### ULTRA +
Avoid portraying Electro as an unlimited battery: canon shows Scaramouche needed heavy modification to channel the Electro Gnosis, and Nahida could not use it in battle. Do not give the Electro Gnosis back to Ei or leave it with Dottore; as of 7.1 it is with the Tsaritsa. Do not describe any named Electro Dragon Sovereign.

## entry: Electro Archon
@keys: Electro Archon, Great Narukami, God of Eternity, Beelzebul
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The Electro Archon is the God of Eternity among the Seven and ruler of Inazuma. There have been two holders: Raiden Makoto, who died in the cataclysm 500 years ago, and her twin sister Ei, who succeeded her and has held the Electro Authority since. The Shogun puppet, built in the twins' image, is now the public face of the Archon. Titles such as "Raiden Shogun", "Narukami" and "Electro Archon" in older texts may refer to any of the three or to the general idea of Inazuma's god.
### LARGE +
Makoto's own ideal was also eternity, but understood as time extended into infinity with dreams lighting each moment within it; Ei later remarked that her sister's understanding was far deeper than hers. Ei's own version was a stillness that never changes; after the story quest Radiant Sakura she no longer pursues eternity as stasis. See the Raiden Shogun's entry for her story.

## entry: Electro Gnosis
@keys: Electro Gnosis
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The Electro Gnosis was Ei's, entrusted to Yae Miko about 400 years ago, then traded to Scaramouche for the Traveler's safety, taken by Nahida in Sumeru, handed to Dottore, and now belongs to the Tsaritsa along with five other Gnoses (as of 7.1). No one outside the Archon can easily use it: Scaramouche could, but only with heavy modification, and Nahida could not use it in battle.

## entry: Thunderbird Kapatcir
@keys: Kapatcir, Kanna Kapatcir, Thunderbird, Great Eagle of the Storm
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Kapatcir ("Great Eagle of the Storm") was a monster, a huge violet bird of prey in storm and thunder, worshipped as a guardian by the tribe of Tsurumi Island. The boy Ruu named her; when the tribe sacrificed him, she destroyed the mountain in grief and rage, killing the tribe, and left a curse on the land. Years later Ei hunted her down as "an obstacle to progress" and sealed the remnants of her power at the Asase Shrine on Seirai Island. A thousand years on, Hibiki's release of that seal let the Thunder Manifestation loose.
### LARGE +
The Seirai Stormchasers world quest series and the Thundering Fury artifact lore (including the Thunder Summoner's Crown) tell her story. Her relationship to the Thunder Manifestation is a "remnant of power", not a rebirth: the Archive describes the Manifestation as a being of regret with no memories of its own.

## entry: Electro Visions
@keys: Electro Vision, Electro Visions
@keys2: Vision, Inazuma, Electro
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Electro Visions are the external apparatuses by which people wield lightning. No new Electro Visions appeared for about a year before the Traveler reached Inazuma, and the reason is unknown. Many were taken by the Vision Hunt Decree and returned once it was rescinded. Known Electro Vision holders include Keqing, Fischl, Beidou, Razor, Kazuha's late friend and, 500 years ago, Fukami.

## entry: Electro elemental lifeforms
@keys: Electro Slime, Electro Specter, Electro Hypostasis, Electro Regisvine, Thunder Manifestation
@spoiler: none
### LARGE
Electro Slimes form from Electro coalescing in nature and carry stored charge. Electro Specters, born of dense Electro, float, and grow larger and gain stronger attacks after taking heavy blows. The Electro Hypostasis (Aleph) is a boss of shell pieces and a core; the Electro Regisvine is a giant plant that releases ley line charge. The Thunder Manifestation on Amakumo Peak is a being of lingering regret. In gameplay, all these are immune to Electro damage.

## entry: Electroculus
@keys: Electroculus, Electroculi
@spoiler: none
### STANDARD
Electroculi are consolidated Electro energy scattered across Inazuma. Item text describes them as a substance that "has accumulated intense Electro energy", offered at a Statue of The Seven to help it regain lost power. The wiki counts 181 in the overworld.
