## summary
### LITE
Cryo is the element of ice and frost, one of Teyvat's seven elements. Its Archon is Anastasya, the Tsaritsa (divine name Vassaga), God of Love, who rules Snezhnaya, the land of eternal winter. Cryo users chill, freeze and slow their enemies. The Tsaritsa also holds the Cryo Gnosis and, as of 7.1, five of the other six.
### STANDARD +
Cryo ascension gems are Shivada Jade. The Cryo Dragon Sovereign is long dead, and its corpse is tied to Snezhnaya's ice and crystal ley lines. Cryoculi were added in Snezhnaya in Version 7.0.

## in_universe
### LITE
Most people cannot use Cryo. Those who can usually hold a Cryo Vision, a crystal that grants control of the element. The Tsaritsa draws instead on the Cryo Authority of her divine throne in Celestia, and on her Gnosis.
### STANDARD +
Snezhnaya is the element's home in the world. In the far north is Morepesok, the only port city on the northern coast, known for its birch forests and old forestry industry. In gameplay the Cryo status can coat certain objects as well as creatures, such as ore, Thunderwood and Amber's Baron Bunny, so reactions can occur on them. Frozen is a sibling element carried by enemy ice shields and some Cryo creatures; the Frozen reaction (Cryo with Hydro) holds a target in place.

## origins
@version: 7.1
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
Origins, as canon tells them. Like all the elemental Authorities, Cryo first belonged to one of the Seven Sovereigns, the elemental dragons of the old world. The Heavenly Principles stole part of those powers and tied them to the Divine Thrones, which became the Archons' Authorities (revealed in the Fontaine Archon Quest, 4.1); Visions derive from them too. In the Natlan Archon Quest (5.1), the Lord of the Night says the Heavenly Principles created elemental energy by dividing the Light Realm's raw currents into seven with Phlogiston as a base. How an Archon relates to their element, and why a nation takes on its element's character, remains unexplained.
### ULTRA +
The Cryo Dragon went to war against Celestia with the other sovereigns and was defeated. According to the wiki's account of the 7.x story, the people of Hyperborea then tried to use its corpse to create a Primordial Human with an authority equal to Nibelung's. The attempt went out of control. The corpse's unchecked authority left Snezhnaya with a form of Holy Soil phenomenon, covering the land in primordial ice and turning ley lines into veins of crystal, and Hyperborea was struck by a Celestial Nail. The experiment still produced a living being: the Belyi Tsar emerged a thousand years later from the dragon's remains, which had stayed above ground with their authority suppressed by the nail. In the 7.1 Archon Quest, Ronova, her authority revoked and mortally wounded by Pierro, placed Eitr on the nail, which crumbled and left the dragon's corpse free to hallow the soil of the whole world.

## archon_and_nation
### LITE
Archon: Anastasya Feodorovna Snezhnaya, the Tsaritsa of All Snezhnaya, Cryo Archon of the Seven and God of Love. Her divine name is Vassaga. Nation: Snezhnaya, which she governs directly from Zapolyarny Palace.
### STANDARD +
She became Cryo Archon about 500 years ago, during the Cataclysm, succeeding the Belyi Tsar after his death on an expedition. Unlike most Archons she runs her nation day to day: her Edicts are the highest authority, and the Fatui, who handle most governance, carry out her will abroad. After the Cataclysm she cut ties with the other Archons, including Venti.
### LARGE +
Early in her reign some Snowland Fae nobles refused to accept a non-fae ruler. She stripped rebellious princes of their titles, had the Fatui build the fortress Glupov, set up the Snezhnograd Veche (headed by a human) in place of fae rule, and approved Nod-Krai's autonomy under the Lightkeepers. She appoints the Eleven Fatui Harbingers. She is not a fae despite her pointed ears. As God of Love she refuses to use that authority, saying the love the Heavenly Principles force on the gods is false and coerced.
### ULTRA +
In the 7.1 Archon Quest she is shown directly: she ends the investigation of the Traveler and explains why she wants the Gnoses. In the follow-up World Quest A Letter From Zapolyarny Palace she names the Traveler "Consultant to the Tsaritsa". Her subordinates, including Vesna and Danica, believe she is kinder than she thinks herself, while others describe her as cold and stern. Her private history belongs in her own entry.

## gnosis_and_authority
### STANDARD
In Ending Note (Prologue, 1.0), Venti explained that a Gnosis is an Archon's internal focus that resonates directly with Celestia, and that the Fatui were gathering them. The Cryo Gnosis is the Tsaritsa's own; her power comes from both it and the Cryo Authority of her Divine Throne.
### LARGE +
Besides the Cryo Gnosis, the Tsaritsa gained the Anemo and Geo Gnoses, then the Electro, Dendro and Hydro Gnoses; the Pyro Gnosis never reached her. In the teaser "A Winter Night's Lazzo", the Harbingers gather to mourn Signora while the Anemo and Geo Gnoses sit as chess pieces on Pierro's board and an unseen cold moves the opposing pieces; the mausoleum is then frozen over.
### ULTRA +
In the 7.1 Archon Quest the Gnoses are shown to be the remains of the Third Descender, Saarelainen, split into seven when the Heavenly Principles agreed to resurrect Anastasya after erasing her. Using her Cryo mastery together with six Gnoses, she created a realm of slowed time inside a room of her palace to stall Rhinedottir, so the Shade could not finish reviving Ronova's victims before Celestia's next monitoring cycle. This is the clearest canon example of Cryo reaching beyond ice into the laws of Reason. She also keeps a seal left by the Third Descender, which she believes needs all seven Gnoses to open.

## snezhnaya_present
@version: 7.0
### ULTRA
Version 7.0 added Snezhnaya as a playable region, with Cryoculi, Statues of The Seven and terrain that players can freeze, shatter and reshape. By the time the Traveler arrived in inner Snezhnaya, the Tsaritsa was away on a campaign in the east against a monster causing endless snowstorms; she ended up going alone, and returned after eliminating it. Nod-Krai (6.x) remains autonomous territory within Snezhnaya.

## elemental_beings
### LITE
Cryo Slimes, Specters, Cicins, Whopperflowers, Regisvines, Abyss Mages and the Cryo Hypostasis are Cryo creatures. The Cryo Dragon Sovereign is dead.
### STANDARD +
Archive entries describe Cryo Slimes as small monsters formed where Cryo coagulates in nature; in gameplay they freeze the water beneath them, so they cannot drown. Cryo Specters are floating monsters of concentrated Cryo. Cryo Regisvines are vines imbued with biting frost from the ley lines.
### LARGE +
- Cryo Hypostasis (code name Daleth): a high-purity Cryo entity on Dragonspine, where a great Skyfrost Nail fell in a bygone era and has been pouring energy into the ley lines since.
- Cryo Abyss Mages: Abyss creatures that condense atmospheric vapour into icicles; fragile once their barrier is broken.
- Cryo Cicins: weak creatures that manipulate Cryo only slightly. Cryo Whopperflowers: ambush plants that can grow into Regisvines over years.
- A certain alchemist who studied Cryo Specters speculated they might be a kind of Slime, or like the Traveler, since none needed a Vision; his teacher reprimanded him.

## known_uses
### LITE
Chilling and freezing foes, raising ice, and slowing movement.
### STANDARD +
In Snezhnaya the ice can be broken, shattered and rebuilt to change terrain and enemy movement. Dragonspine's Sheer Cold is an environmental hazard of its climate, separate from the Cryo element.
### LARGE +
- The Tsaritsa froze Signora's whole mausoleum, and in 7.1 froze a monster created by the Ruler of Life in the Northland Bank vaults before Rhinedottir stopped her.
- Skirk, who is not a Vision holder, disguises Abyssal power as Cryo with the Seven-Shifting Serpent technique learned from Surtalogi.
- Cryo Vision holders include Ganyu, Kaeya, Qiqi, Chongyun, Diona, Eula, Rosaria, Shenhe, Kamisato Ayaka, Layla, Wriothesley, Mika, Charlotte, Freminet, Citlali and Escoffier; the origin of each Vision belongs in that character's file.
### ULTRA +
Venti told the Traveler in Ending Note that Vision holders are "allogenes" who may one day ascend to godhood in Celestia. A dead holder's Vision becomes Masterless and loses its power.

## limits
### LITE
Cryo does not react with Dendro.
### STANDARD +
A Vision fixes its holder to one element. Masterless Visions lose their element and power when their holder dies.
### LARGE +
Gameplay: Cryo slimes, specters and the Cryo Hypostasis are immune to Cryo, and Cryo-shielded enemies carry the Frozen element, which does not react with Hydro. The Cryo status slows animation speed of affected targets by 15%. Cryo is not Sheer Cold: it is a status applied to characters and objects.

## interactions
### LITE
Cryo reacts with Electro (Superconduct), Hydro (Frozen), Pyro (Melt), Anemo (Swirl, and Stellar Swirl since 7.0) and Geo (Crystallize). Each has its own entry.
### STANDARD +
Frozen holds a target in place, and a blunt or Geo hit shatters it. Superconduct lowers Physical resistance. Melt amplifies Cryo hits on Pyro-affected targets. Swirl spreads Cryo to nearby targets, and Crystallize leaves a Cryo shard that gives a shield.
### LARGE +
Stellar-Conduct (Cryo with Electro) and Stellar Swirl (Cryo with Anemo) are the Stellar Glimmer versions of Superconduct and Swirl, added in 7.0; each has its own entry.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not literal lore): Cryo is a damage type and the element of characters such as Ganyu, Kaeya, Qiqi, Chongyun, Diona, Eula, Rosaria, Shenhe, Kamisato Ayaka, Layla, Wriothesley, Mika, Charlotte, Freminet, Citlali, Escoffier, Skirk and Aloy. The Traveler becomes Cryo by resonating with a Statue of The Seven in Snezhnaya.
### LARGE +
The Elemental Resonance "Shattering Ice" (two or more Cryo characters) shortens the Electro status on the party by 40% and raises CRIT Rate by 15% against targets that are Frozen or affected by Cryo. Cryoculi are offered at Statues of The Seven in Snezhnaya (about 140 in the overworld; they do not respawn). Ascension gems are Shivada Jade.

## gameplay_recent
@version: 7.0
### ULTRA
Gameplay, recent additions: Stellar Swirl (Anemo with Cryo) and Stellar-Conduct (Cryo with Electro) joined the Stellar Glimmer reactions in 7.0. In Snezhnaya, Cryo can break, shatter and reshape terrain. The Elements page lists some Snezhnayan characters' element source as a Stellar Linchpin rather than a Vision.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay interpretation (this project's recommendation, not canon text): show Cryo as stillness, pale blue-white light and falling temperature: breath fogging, frost creeping from a hand, ice that rings or cracks. Keep scale proportionate: a Vision holder can chill a drink, freeze a puddle or raise an ice barrier, but sealing a whole mausoleum in ice is the Archon's work.
### LARGE +
Cryo can preserve and restrain as well as hurt: frosted windows, a cooled cellar, a held breath. A Snezhnayan can speak of the cold as ordinary rather than hostile. The Tsaritsa rules in person, so she is a visible head of state, not a distant myth.
### ULTRA +
Do not give every Cryo user control over time. Do not have a Cryo Vision holder know the story of the Cryo Dragon, Saarelainen or the Gnoses unless the scene establishes how they learned it. As of 7.1 the Cryo Gnosis and five others are with the Tsaritsa; the Pyro Gnosis is not.

## entry: Cryo Archon
@keys: Cryo Archon, Vassaga, Tsaritsa, God of Love
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
The Cryo Archon is Anastasya Feodorovna Snezhnaya, the Tsaritsa of All Snezhnaya, called the God of Love among the Seven. Her divine name is Vassaga. She succeeded the Belyi Tsar about 500 years ago, rules Snezhnaya from Zapolyarny Palace and appoints the Eleven Fatui Harbingers. She holds the Cryo Gnosis and refuses to use her authority of love.
### LARGE +
As of 7.1 she holds six Gnoses (all but Pyro), and in a 7.1 World Quest she appoints the Traveler "Consultant to the Tsaritsa". Her own entry carries her biography and relationships.

## entry: Cryo Dragon Sovereign
@keys: Cryo Dragon, Cryo Sovereign, Cryo Dragon Sovereign
@spoiler: major
@version: 7.1
### STANDARD
The Cryo Dragon was one of the Seven Sovereigns, the dragon-lords who ruled Teyvat before the Heavenly Principles. It fought Celestia and was defeated. Its corpse is the source of Snezhnaya's primordial ice and crystal ley lines.
### LARGE +
Hyperborea used the corpse in an attempt to make a Primordial Human, and the Belyi Tsar emerged from its remains a thousand years later. A Celestial Nail suppressed its authority until it crumbled in the 7.1 Archon Quest, freeing the corpse to hallow the soil of the world.

## entry: Cryoculus
@keys: Cryoculus, Cryoculi
@spoiler: none
### STANDARD
Cryoculi are found scattered across Snezhnaya and are offered at Statues of The Seven there. Item text calls the Cryoculus a substance "that has accumulated intense Cryo energy", offered to help a Statue regain power lost over the years. They appeared in 7.0.

## entry: Cryo elemental lifeforms
@keys: Cryo Slime, Cryo Specter, Cryo Hypostasis, Cryo Regisvine, Cryo Abyss Mage, Cryo Cicin, Cryo Whopperflower
### LARGE
Cryo Slimes form where Cryo coagulates in nature and, in gameplay, freeze the water under them so they cannot drown. Cryo Specters are floating monsters of concentrated Cryo that, in gameplay, grow bigger and gain enhanced attacks after taking heavy hits. The Cryo Hypostasis (code name Daleth) is a high-purity Cryo boss on Dragonspine, where a fallen Skyfrost Nail feeds the ley lines. Cryo Regisvines are monstrous vines that absorbed frost from the ley lines and may grow from Cryo Whopperflowers. Cryo Abyss Mages call on Cryo in battle, and Cryo Cicins manipulate it only slightly. In gameplay these creatures are immune to Cryo.
