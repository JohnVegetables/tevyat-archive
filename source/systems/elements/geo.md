## summary
### LITE
Geo is the element of earth and stone, one of Teyvat's seven elements, seen in stone constructs, crystal shields and shaking ground. Its Archon is Morax (Rex Lapis), God of Contracts, patron of Liyue. He staged his own "death", gave up his Gnosis and now lives as Zhongli in Liyue Harbor.
### STANDARD +
Neuvillette addresses the Geo Archon as Deus Auri. Geo ascension gems are Prithiva Topaz.

## in_universe
### LITE
Few people can use Geo, usually through a Geo Vision. Morax draws on the Geo Authority of his divine throne in Celestia instead; the Geo "Vision" at Zhongli's back is not a real one.
### STANDARD +
Geo's gifts to people are practical. A Liyue legend credits the Lord of Rock with the first settlers' stone stoves and struck-stone fires, which is why taverns once worshipped him as "God of the Stove"; the Moonlight Merriment event later shows that legend is false, as the true God of the Stove was Marchosius (now Guoba), whose tales merged into Morax's over time. Legend credits Rex Lapis with raising Mt. Tianheng and imprisoning the sea god Osial under the stone lances now called the Guyun Stone Forest. The Geo Archon's power was also what minted Mora, the currency of all Teyvat, at the Golden House.
### LARGE +
Geo energy is said to saturate the crust of the earth, and it condenses into creatures: the Archive describes Geo Slimes as small monsters built from Geo gathered in the earth, and Geo Specters as monsters born from a high concentration of Geo that float in the air and resemble fruit when their wings close. Because Morax slew or sealed many ancient gods, legend holds that their bodies sank into the earth while their grudges seeped out as miasma, plague and monster attacks, which is why the Yakshas were called to keep subduing them.
### ULTRA +
Rex Lapis is the God of Contracts and Liyue's economy rests on his Mora. The Prithiva Topaz item text, a first-person line the wiki considers likely spoken by Morax, says that the currencies flowing through the land are "my flesh and blood", presenting Mora as part of himself. Canon does not explain how the Geo Authority and his contract role are connected.

## origins
@version: 4.1
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
Origins, as canon tells them. The elemental Authorities first belonged to the Seven Sovereigns, the elemental dragons of the old world; the Geo Dragon Sovereign was Azhdaha, who ruled a vast territory that included what is now Liyue. The Heavenly Principles stole part of that power and tied it to the Divine Thrones, which became the Archons' Authorities (revealed in the Fontaine Archon Quest, 4.1); Visions also derive from these Authorities. How an Archon relates to their element and why a nation takes on its element's character is still unexplained.

## archon_and_nation
### LITE
Archon: Morax (Rex Lapis, Zhongli), oldest of The Seven. Nation: Liyue, now governed by humans.
### STANDARD +
Morax was born more than six thousand years ago and became Archon by winning the Archon War in the region. After faking his death in the Rite of Descension, he arranged a test of whether Liyue could manage without him, with the help of the Tsaritsa and her Fatui, and retired. He now works as a consultant at the Wangsheng Funeral Parlor under Hu Tao. Few know he is the Archon. Along with Barbatos he is one of the two original members of The Seven who remain.
### LARGE +
Liyue is governed by the Liyue Qixing, who run the city from the Jade Chamber; the Golden House has been shut since Morax's retirement, and Mora circulation depends on the Geo Gnosis he gave away. Morax told the Qixing, through Ningguang, that he was alive but had stepped down, and left the official explanation of his "death" to them. He forged contracts with the adepti, including a long-standing one with Ganyu, to protect Liyue Harbor, and called the Yakshas to subdue the ancient gods; one of them, Xiao, still serves him.
### ULTRA +
Earlier, he and Guizhong, the God of Dust, led the Guili Assembly at Guili Plains; Guizhong died in a battle that destroyed Guili Plains and Dihua Marsh, after which Morax moved their people south to found Liyue Harbor. Near the end of the Archon War he also defeated the mistress of Chenyu Vale, whose adepti servants sealed her away. Before he became Rex Lapis, in the era of the unified civilization, he met Zibai, then ruler of Lang-Gan, and taught her the art of Tri-Factor Excision.

## gnosis_and_authority
@version: 1.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
After the Rite of Parting in Archon Quest Chapter I, Act III (1.0), Signora, as the Tsaritsa's envoy, collected the Geo Gnosis from Zhongli peacefully under the contract he called the "contract to end all contracts". The Tsaritsa's end of the bargain is still unexplained.
### LARGE +
Zhongli later told Azhdaha that he was no longer the Geo Archon, and Azhdaha, who said he could sense it, accepted that. Even so, the Archons' Divine Thrones and Authorities exist independently of their Gnoses, and Neuvillette, speaking of the Geo Archon as Deus Auri, expects that Morax will one day have to answer for the Authority he won. Ever since the Gnosis left Liyue, the Golden House stopped minting Mora.
### ULTRA +
The Gnosis page lists the Geo Gnosis as one of the six in the Fatui's possession as of Truth Amongst the Pages of Purana, along with the Cryo, Anemo, Electro, Dendro and Hydro Gnoses. Anastasya, the Tsaritsa, later used the six Gnoses together with her Cryo mastery to slow time within a large room in the 7.1 Archon Quest (Black Feathers Scattered at Parting).

## elemental_beings
### LITE
Azhdaha, the Geo Dragon Sovereign, sealed beneath the Dragon-Queller; Geo elemental lifeforms such as Geo Slimes, Geo Specters and the Geo Hypostasis; and the Geovishap line of dragons.
### STANDARD +
Azhdaha was a friend of Morax whom Morax once gave sight, and who suffered from erosion until he lashed out at people. He was sealed in the Chasm and beneath the Dragon-Queller at Nantianmen. Geo Slimes are small and drifting, Geo Specters float and burst into earthquake-like attacks, and the Geo Hypostasis (code name Gimel) is a boss in the Guyun Stone Forest with a high-density solid shell around a reactive core. It raises pillars and drops them as stone seals.
### LARGE +
- The Geo Hypostasis is the third of the Hebrew-letter-named hypostases (Gimel); only its exposed core reacts to elements.
- Geo Specters haunt Seirai Island and Watatsumi Island in Inazuma.
- Azhdaha holds command over the Ley Lines; he has sent a vessel in the form of a girl, Jiu, to free him, and his benevolent fragment possessed the human Kun Jun to warn Morax.
- Geoculi: crystallised Geo energy scattered across Liyue; the item text says offering one to a Statue of The Seven restores power the statue lost over the years.
### ULTRA +
In the quest Amidst Chaos, the Rock Is Unmoved (1.5), the Traveler, Zhongli and Kun Jun fight Azhdaha, who is resealed; his body is held under the Dragon-Queller and the Mountain Shaper and the Moon Carver helped build the domain that first sealed him. Azhdaha's benevolent side, speaking through Kun Jun, bids Morax farewell.

## known_uses
### LITE
Barriers and shields, summoned stone constructs, pillars of rock, shaking the ground, and petrifying or fixing a target in place.
### STANDARD +
Geo shapes land as well as fights. Morax is said to have raised mountains, lowered tides and bound a sea god with stone lances. Geo Vision holders in play raise constructs and crystalline shields.
### LARGE +
- Zhongli's burst (Planet Befall) petrifies enemies and places a persistent Geo status on them for the duration.
- Geo characters in play, such as Ningguang, Albedo, Chiori and Gorou, summon constructs or shields; the wiki lists Geo Constructs as a category of talents.
- Zibai (6.3) and Illuga are Moonsign-era Geo characters.
- The Traveler: resonating with a Statue of The Seven in Liyue changes the Traveler's element to Geo, and in the Heart of Glaze chapter a cutscene shows them using both Anemo and Geo.
### ULTRA +
- Visions remain personal. Zhongli's hair-tips glow amber when he uses his skill or burst, and the Geo "Vision" at his back is not a real Vision.
- Kachina (Natlan) and Linnea are among the more recent Geo characters, as are Illuga and Zibai.

## limits
### LITE
Geo does not react with Anemo or Dendro; it crystallises Pyro, Hydro, Electro and Cryo.
### STANDARD +
Crystallize turns Pyro, Hydro, Electro or Cryo into a shard of that element that grants a shield when picked up; it does not create an element.
### LARGE +
Gameplay: a Geo aura cannot normally be applied by elemental attacks. Exceptions are the Geo Hypostasis, Stonehide Lawachurls and Large Geo Slimes while their shields are up (which cannot react), and Zhongli's burst, which applies a persistent Geo status of 0.04 gauge units that does not decay until the petrification ends and does not react. Geo elemental lifeforms are immune to Geo damage.

## interactions
### LITE
Crystallize: Geo meeting Pyro, Hydro, Electro or Cryo makes an elemental shard whose pickup gives a shield (own entry). Geo also gives Shatter, in which hitting a frozen target deals Physical damage and ends the freeze.
### STANDARD +
Lunar-Crystallize (Geo plus Hydro, 6.3): creates three Moondrifts near the target, Geo constructs that resonate after enough Lunar-Crystallize reactions, firing Moondrift Harmony for Geo damage.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not literal lore): Geo is a damage type and the element of characters such as Zhongli, Ningguang, Albedo, Noelle, Gorou, Yun Jin, Arataki Itto, Navia, Chiori, Xilonen, Kachina, Zibai, Illuga and Linnea, plus the Geo Traveler. Geoculi are scattered across Liyue.
### LARGE +
The Elemental Resonance "Enduring Rock" (two or more Geo characters) increases shield strength by 15%. Characters protected by a shield, or near Moondrifts from Lunar-Crystallize, gain a 15% damage bonus and reduce enemies' Geo RES by 20% for 15 seconds. Crystallize shield strength rises with the user's level and Elemental Mastery; shards last 15 seconds, only one Crystallize shield can be active and each shield absorbs more against its own element. There are 131 Geoculi in the overworld, which allow the Liyue Statues of The Seven to be fully levelled with one to spare, and the Geoculus Resonance Stone marks nearby Geoculi. Geo ascension gems are Prithiva Topaz.

## gameplay_recent
@version: 6.3
### ULTRA
Gameplay, recent additions: Lunar-Crystallize joined Crystallize and Shatter as a Geo-based reaction in 6.3 and is part of the Moonsign system. Recent Geo characters include Zibai (6.3, a 5-star sword user tied to Lang-Gan, Zhongli and the Lantern Rite plot), Illuga (4-star polearm) and Linnea (5-star bow).

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay interpretation (this project's recommendation, not canon text): show Geo as weight, stillness and pattern: amber-gold light, hexagonal or crystal facets, dust settling, a faint hum in stone. It protects more often than it strikes; a Geo defender plants themselves, and a shield that appears should read as solid, not glowing energy. Keep scale proportionate: a Vision holder can raise a pillar or a crystalline barrier, but reshaping mountains and binding a god is an Archon's work.
### LARGE +
Geo suits ideas of endurance and promises kept through time (an interpretation, not canon). Liyue's people associate stone with the Geo Archon, so a sudden rumble or a stone that does not give way can be read as an omen. Do not have an ordinary Liyue resident recognise Zhongli as Morax.
### ULTRA +
Avoid portraying Geo as earthquake or lava control for ordinary users; large-scale earth-shaping is shown only for the Geo Archon and the Geo Dragon Sovereign. Do not describe Zhongli as having regained his Gnosis, and do not describe the Geo Archon as still ruling Liyue.

## entry: Geo Archon
@keys: Geo Archon, Rex Lapis, Deus Auri, God of Contracts
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The Geo Archon is Morax, called Rex Lapis in Liyue and by many other titles. He faked his death during the Rite of Descension and gave his Gnosis to the Tsaritsa, leaving Liyue to humans. He lives as Zhongli, a funeral-parlor consultant in Liyue Harbor. His own character entry carries his biography, so this entry only points at the element.
### LARGE +
Neuvillette says he intends to bring each of the Seven to trial and speaks of Deus Auri as the Archon who won the Authority of Geo. The Geo Archon is also the subject of a Liyue folk image, a stage hero in opera, and wrongly credited in folk legend as the God of the Stove (a title that belonged to Marchosius); the person Zhongli and the legend do not always agree.

## entry: Geo Dragon Sovereign
@keys: Geo Dragon, Geo Dragon Sovereign, Earth Dragon, Azhdaha
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Azhdaha is the Geo Dragon Sovereign, an ancient Geovishap ancestor who once ruled a territory including what is now Liyue. Morax stopped the earthquakes that his movements caused, gave him sight, and made a contract with him. Erosion and human mining in the Chasm eventually drove him to attack the people he had loved, and he was sealed under the Dragon-Queller at Nantianmen. He is incapacitated, not dead, as of Amidst Chaos, the Rock Is Unmoved.
### LARGE +
The English "Geo Dragon" title comes from the Chinese "dragon king" wording, while his design notes and the Chinese text of the quest call him an Earth Dragon instead. His character page holds his full account.

## entry: Geoculus
@keys: Geoculus, Geoculi
### STANDARD
Geoculi are found scattered across Liyue. The item text calls a Geoculus a substance that has accumulated intense Geo energy, to be offered at a Statue of The Seven to reinstate power it has lost over the years. In gameplay there are 131 in the overworld.

## entry: Crystallize
@keys: Crystallize, Crystallise, Elemental Shard
@spoiler: none
### STANDARD
When Geo meets Pyro, Hydro, Electro or Cryo, Crystallize leaves a shard of that element on the ground. Picking it up gives the character a shield of that element. Shards last 15 seconds and only the three newest remain. Lunar-Crystallize is the Hydro version introduced in 6.3. Crystallize is a gameplay mechanic with only metaphorical support in lore.

## entry: Geo elemental lifeforms
@keys: Geo Slime, Geo Specter, Geo Hypostasis, Gimel
### LARGE
Geo Slimes are small monsters formed by the build-up of Geo within the earth; the Archive notes their "down-to-earth" feel. Geo Specters float, burst into earthquakes when enraged and haunt Seirai and Watatsumi in Inazuma; some tales treat them as the fruit of a giant sky tree with medicinal value. The Geo Hypostasis, code name Gimel, is a boss in Guyun Stone Forest: a solid shell of concentrated Geo around a reactive core. In gameplay these lifeforms are immune to Geo.
