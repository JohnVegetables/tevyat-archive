## summary
### LITE
Hydro is the element of water, one of Teyvat's seven elements. Its homeland is Fontaine, the nation of justice. Two gods held the title of Hydro Archon, Egeria and then Focalors. Focalors executed herself in 4.2 and destroyed the Hydro Archon's divine throne, so Fontaine has had no Hydro Archon since. The Hydro Authority now belongs to Neuvillette, the reborn Hydro Dragon Sovereign.
### STANDARD +
Hydro ascension gems are Varunada Lazurite. Hydro users appear well beyond Fontaine; the Fatui Harbinger Tartaglia and Columbina of Nod-Krai both wield Hydro.

## in_universe
### LITE
Most people use Hydro through a Vision. Neuvillette needs none, because the Hydro Authority is his own. In how it interacts with other elements, rain, rivers, the sea and the Primordial Sea beneath Fontaine all count as Hydro.
### STANDARD +
Fontaine was where the old Hydro Dragon ruled the Primordial Sea; Hydro vishap legend calls the Dragon its "beating heart". After the Dragon fell, Egeria became the god of Fontaine and gave it its laws. Hydro in Fontaine is entwined with justice, trials and the Oratrice Mecanique d'Analyse Cardinale.
### ULTRA +
The Fontainians descend from Oceanids that Egeria made human with water from the Primordial Sea, an act the Heavenly Principles branded as an original sin. The Prophecy of Fontaine foretold that the people would dissolve into water and leave the Hydro Archon weeping alone on her throne.

## origins
@version: 5.1
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
Origins, as canon tells them. The elemental Authorities first belonged to the Seven Sovereigns, the elemental dragons of the old world. The Heavenly Principles stole part of that power and tied it to the Divine Thrones, which became the Archons' Authorities (revealed in the 4.1 Archon Quest); Visions also derive from these Authorities. In the Natlan Archon Quest (5.1), the Lord of the Night reveals that elemental energy was made by dividing the raw currents of the Light Realm into seven, using Phlogiston as a base. Neuvillette's Vision character story adds that the Heavenly Principles, gravely wounded in a great war, made the Gnoses together with another entity as a substitute for their authority, and that the Seven had to grant a shard of their mastery to anyone whose wish reached the heavens. He says he obeys no edict from the heavens but acknowledges human will, so he too set parts of himself aside, like a dragon's hoard, for worthy humans to claim.

## archon_and_nation
### LITE
Hydro Archon: none as of 7.1. The divine throne was destroyed in 4.2. The two former Archons were Egeria and Focalors, both Gods of Justice among The Seven. Nation: Fontaine.
### STANDARD +
Egeria presided over Fontaine from about 2,000 to 500 years ago, after the fall of Remuria, uniting the surviving tribes and establishing its laws. She died in battle at Tunigi Hollow during the Cataclysm and her body became the waters of the Amrita. Focalors, originally an Oceanid familiar of Egeria, succeeded her and governed for 499 years.
### LARGE +
Focalors separated her divinity from her humanity. The body and spirit she left behind became Furina, who played the Hydro Archon in public for about five centuries to hide the real god's plan from the Heavenly Principles. In the 4.2 Archon Quest Focalors had the Oratrice sentence her to death, which destroyed the throne and returned its Authority to Neuvillette. The Fontainians were then absolved of the original sin and made true humans. Furina gave up the political post of Hydro Archon and now lives as an ordinary Fontainian. Neuvillette now handles the affairs of the title from the Palais Mermonia, with the duties of Hydro Sovereign and Iudex coexisting in him. The wiki infers there will be no further Hydro Archons, since the throne no longer exists.

## the_hydro_dragon
@version: 4.2
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
The Hydro Dragon Sovereign, the Dragon of Water, was one of the Seven Sovereigns and ruler of the vishaps with sovereignty over water. It fought the Heavenly Principles, was defeated and withered away. Cassiodor says no new Hydro Dragon could be born while Egeria hibernated in the deep sea. An Enkanomiya prophecy held that a new Hydro Dragon would come in human form, because the Bathysmal Vishaps, originally Hydro, had become impure by learning Electro and Cryo. That came true in Neuvillette, the Iudex of Fontaine. Before 4.2 he had lost many memories and much of his hold on the Primordial Sea; with the Authority restored he regained both and used it to make the Fontainians fully human.
### ULTRA +
A Fontainian saying, told by Freminet, claims the Hydro Dragon weeps when it rains and that children should shout for it to stop. In canon the sky does rain when Neuvillette grieves, and Focalors repeated the saying to him at their last meeting. Neuvillette has chosen to keep giving out Visions, in recognition of human will.

## gnosis_and_authority
### STANDARD
The Hydro Gnosis powered the Oratrice, which was secretly merged with Focalors; only a little of the Indemnitium gathered from trials at the Opera Epiclese ran the Court, and the rest was stored so she could execute herself. When Focalors died the Oratrice stopped working, but the Hydro Gnosis was unaffected. Archons keep their divine thrones and elemental power whether or not they hold their Gnosis.
### LARGE +
Neuvillette obtained the Hydro Gnosis after Focalors's death. On Skirk's advice he wanted it out of Fontaine, and it went to the Fatui through Arlecchino as a "diplomatic gift" for their help against the Prophecy; Before that, Arlecchino had the Tsaritsa's permission to try using the Hydro Gnosis against the Prophecy herself, a plan Lyney and Lynette knew of; the chance never came, and she later handed the Gnosis to the Tsaritsa.
### ULTRA +
Besides her own Cryo Gnosis, the Tsaritsa gained the Anemo and Geo Gnoses first, then the Electro, Dendro and Hydro Gnoses; the Pyro Gnosis never reached her.

## gnosis_status
@version: 7.1
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
In the 7.1 Archon Quest "A Rekviem for the Underworld", Anastasya (the Tsaritsa) used her Cryo mastery together with the six Gnoses, the Hydro Gnosis among them, to slow time within a room of her palace and stall Rhinedottir. As of 7.1 the Hydro Gnosis remains in her possession.

## elemental_beings
### LITE
Hydro Slimes, Hydro Samachurls, Hydro Specters, Hydro Mimics (summoned by Oceanids) and the Hydro Hypostasis. The Hydro Dragon Sovereign is Neuvillette.
### STANDARD +
Oceanids, occasionally called the Lochfolk, are beings of pure Hydro energy from Fontaine who float through the air, command water and make Hydro Mimics, water creatures that copy animals. Hydro Slimes form from Hydro dispersed through nature; legend says travellers once packed them as emergency water, though the dense Hydro makes drinking one harmful. Hydro Samachurls are aged hilichurl shamans who chant to the power of Hydro and call rain that heals allies.
### LARGE +
- Hydro Hypostasis: Code Name: He, a high-purity Hydro entity that violently expels those who test it. It is found on Watatsumi Island in Inazuma.
- Bathysmal Vishaps: originally Hydro dragon-kin of the Primordial Sea, they later evolved to use Electro and Cryo.
- Since Egeria's death most of Fontaine's waters can no longer naturally create Oceanids.
- Powerful, wise Oceanids can make copies of people, as Idyia did with her past companions in the Veluriyam Mirage.

## known_uses
### LITE
Currents, jets, bubbles, rain and tides, healing water, and soaking things so that they react to Electro and Cryo.
### STANDARD +
Neuvillette commands the Primordial Sea's waters and can draw out the memories stored in water. Hydro Samachurls heal allies with rain.
### LARGE +
- Furina's Vision appeared in her character story during a scene of the musical "The Little Oceanid" at the Opera Epiclese, when her heroine vanishes; she then studied it all night and flooded her building.
- Columbina, a Hydro character from Nod-Krai, is listed with a Tri-Lunar Convergence instead of a Vision and enables Lunar reactions (Lunar-Charged, Lunar-Bloom, Lunar-Crystallize).
- Under Elemental Sight, Oceanids in human form show as Hydro.
### ULTRA +
- Tartaglia holds a Hydro Vision and also an Electro Delusion; as a playable character he only wields Hydro.

## limits
### LITE
Hydro alone only soaks; it needs another element to do something special.
### STANDARD +
Wet itself has no special effect; it only allows Hydro reactions. A Vision fixes its holder to one element, while Archons and Sovereigns hold an Authority rather than a Vision.
### LARGE +
The Hydro Hypostasis is immune to Hydro damage. In lore, the Hydro Archon's throne cannot be restored after 4.2, and Neuvillette's mastery of the sea is the full Authority only because Focalors returned it.

## interactions
### LITE
Hydro reacts with Pyro (Vaporize), Cryo (Frozen), Electro (Electro-Charged), Anemo (Swirl), Geo (Crystallize) and Dendro (Bloom).
### STANDARD +
Bloom extends to Hyperbloom (Electro on a Dendro Core) and Burgeon (Pyro on a Dendro Core). Lunar reactions add Lunar-Charged (Hydro with Electro), Lunar-Bloom (Hydro with Dendro) and Lunar-Crystallize (Hydro with Geo).

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not literal lore): Hydro is a damage type and the element of characters such as Neuvillette, Furina, Sigewinne, Tartaglia, Columbina, Vodyanitsa (added in 7.1) and the Hydro Traveler.
### LARGE +
The Elemental Resonance "Soothing Water" (two or more Hydro characters) cuts Pyro duration by 40% and raises Max HP by 25%. Any Hydro damage, rain or contact with water applies Wet. Hydroculi, 271 of them in Fontaine (225 in the overworld and 46 in the Sea of Bygone Eras), are offered at Statues of The Seven. Neuvillette is Pneuma-aligned.
### ULTRA +
Lunar-Charged is the Moon Wheel variant of Electro-Charged: it makes a thundercloud that keeps damaging enemies affected by both Hydro and Electro. The Traveler becomes Hydro by resonating with a Statue of The Seven in Fontaine.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay interpretation (this project's recommendation, not canon text): show Hydro as flow, weight and sound: drips, condensation on glass, spray, a hush before rain. Blue and pale teal are safe visual cues. Keep scale proportionate: a Vision holder can raise a jet, bubble or wave; moving the sea is a Sovereign's work.
### LARGE +
In Fontaine, rain can mirror mood, especially around Neuvillette. Wet clothing and pooling water are natural prompts for Electro or Cryo reactions.
### ULTRA +
Do not claim there is a current Hydro Archon, or that Furina is a god; since 4.2 she is an ordinary human. Do not place the Hydro Gnosis anywhere except with the Tsaritsa as of 7.1.

## entry: Hydro Archon
@keys: Hydro Archon, Archon of Hydro, Regina of All Waters
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Two gods held the Hydro Archon title: Egeria, the Mistress of Many Waters, and Focalors, the Regina of All Waters, Kindreds, Peoples and Laws. Focalors's death in 4.2 destroyed the divine throne, so as of 7.1 no Hydro Archon exists.
### LARGE +
Furina played the public Hydro Archon for about 500 years, as Focalors planned, then abdicated. The Authority now rests with Neuvillette as the Hydro Sovereign.

## entry: Hydro Dragon Sovereign
@keys: Hydro Dragon, Hydro Sovereign, Dragon of Water
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The Hydro Dragon Sovereign was the old ruler of water and the vishaps, one of the Seven Sovereigns. It died in the war against the Heavenly Principles and was reborn in human form as Neuvillette, who regained its Authority in 4.2.
### LARGE +
A children's saying in Fontaine claims the Hydro Dragon weeps when it rains.

## entry: Hydroculus
@keys: Hydroculus, Hydroculi
### STANDARD
Hydroculi are collectible substances of intense Hydro energy found across Fontaine and the Sea of Bygone Eras. Offered at a Statue of The Seven, they help restore power the statue has lost over the years. There are 271 in total.

## entry: Varunada Lazurite
@keys: Varunada Lazurite, Varunada
### STANDARD
Varunada Lazurites are the ascension gems for Hydro characters. The gemstone's description quotes an unnamed speaker on ideals without stain and on laws and the Tribunal being able to judge even the speaker; the wiki reads the lines as fitting the 4.2 Archon Quest and the Oratrice's sentence on Focalors.

## entry: Oceanids
@keys: Oceanid, Oceanids, Lochfolk
### STANDARD
Oceanids are beings of pure Hydro from Fontaine who followed Egeria. Legend says her first tear created the first Oceanid. She gave some of them human forms with water from the Primordial Sea, which the Heavenly Principles counted as a sin, and many Oceanids left Fontaine after her death.
### LARGE +
Focalors was an Oceanid who became Hydro Archon. After the Prophecy was broken, the descendants of the transformed Oceanids, the Fontainians, were made true humans.
