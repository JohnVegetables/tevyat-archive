## summary
### LITE
Dendro is the element of plants and growth, one of Teyvat's seven elements. Its Archon is the God of Wisdom, whose divine name is Buer and who today is Lesser Lord Kusanali (Nahida); her nation is Sumeru, a land of rainforest and desert. Dendro Visions let their holders call up vines, leaves and seeds, and it is the element behind Quicken and Bloom reactions. The Dendro Dragon Sovereign is Apep, who once ruled all of Sumeru. Nahida traded her Dendro Gnosis to the Harbinger Dottore in Chapter III, and as of 7.1 the Tsaritsa holds six Gnoses.
### STANDARD +
Dendro ascension gems are Nagadus Emeralds. Dendro was the last element to gain a playable character, arriving only in Version 3.0 with Tighnari and Collei and the Dendro-aligned Traveler. The Traveler's resonance with Dendro is remarked on by several characters in Chapter III.

## in_universe
### LITE
Most people can only use Dendro through a Dendro Vision; Nahida draws on the Dendro Authority of her divine throne in Celestia. In Sumeru, Dendro is bound up with life, growth and dreams: the rainforest, its Aranara, and the Akasha network all trace back to the God of Wisdom.
### STANDARD +
Greater Lord Rukkhadevata, the first Dendro Archon, is credited with reviving the rainforest from desert, creating the Aranara, and guiding the people who founded the Sumeru Akademiya. Dendro is also the element of her "wisdom": the Akasha System she created used the Dendro Gnosis to link minds. Nahida, her successor, is hardly a fighter; her power is chiefly over dreams, consciousness and plants.
### LARGE +
Dendro and "growth" are linked in a notable way. Rukkhadevata shrank to a more child-like form each time she poured her divine strength into the land, once to suppress forbidden knowledge among King Deshret's people and again during the cataclysm. Kusanali says she too could release power beyond her limits by de-aging herself, possibly down to the "blade of grass or a twig" she began as. Canon does not say whether this is true of every Dendro Archon.
### ULTRA +
Sumeru's two halves reflect the Dendro-versus-desert story. After the war of funerary flame, a Celestial Nail from the Heavenly Principles turned most of verdant Sumeru into the Great Red Sand and forced the Dendro Dragon to adapt to the dunes; the forest survived only where Rukkhadevata and her followers revived and defended it, with the Wall of Samiel marking the line. Most Sumeru residents now associate Dendro with the rainforest and the god in the Divine Tree, but ancient desert kingdoms such as King Deshret's also lived under her protection.

## origins
@version: 5.1
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
Origins, as canon tells them. The elemental Authorities first belonged to the Seven Sovereigns, the elemental dragons of the old world. The Heavenly Principles stole part of that power and tied it to the Divine Thrones, which became the Archons' Authorities (revealed in the Fontaine Archon Quest, 4.1); Visions also derive from these Authorities. The Natlan Archon Quest (5.1) reveals that the Heavenly Principles made elemental energy by dividing the raw elemental currents of the Light Realm into seven, using Phlogiston as a base. Why an Archon is tied to their element, and why a nation takes on its element's character, remains unexplained; Sumeru is the former domain of the Dendro Dragon Sovereign Apep.

## archon_and_nation
### LITE
Archon: the God of Wisdom, divine name Buer. The current holder is Lesser Lord Kusanali (Nahida); before her it was Greater Lord Rukkhadevata. Nation: Sumeru, whose capital Sumeru City is built on the Divine Tree.
### STANDARD +
Rukkhadevata perished in the cataclysm about 500 years ago. Before dying she created Kusanali as her next incarnation, but she had exhausted herself fighting the Abyss, so Kusanali was born as a child with no memories. In Chapter III's finale (Where the Boat of Consciousness Lies), Nahida removed all memory of Rukkhadevata from Irminsul at Rukkhadevata's own request, to eradicate forbidden knowledge from the world. As a result everyone, Nahida included, now believes Kusanali has always been the sole Dendro Archon; only the Traveler remembers the truth.
### LARGE +
The Sumeru Akademiya's sages shut the young god in the Sanctuary of Surasthana for centuries, disappointed that she lacked her predecessor's wisdom, and ran Sumeru through the Akasha System instead. Before the 2.1 update, Chinese text never gave the Dendro Archon pronouns, which led some localisations to call the God of Wisdom male; 2.1 confirmed Kusanali as female and corrected the English text.
### ULTRA +
Canon also records an older sovereignty over the same land: the Dendro Dragon Sovereign Apep ruled all Sumeru before the Heavenly Principles arrived, and nurtured the first elemental lifeforms. In the sands, Rukkhadevata once co-reigned with Nabu Malikata and King Deshret as the "Three Eternal Companion-Gods", before breaking with them when they planned to turn against Celestia; she then founded her own domain by nurturing a rainforest.

## gnosis_and_authority
### STANDARD
Dendro's Gnosis belonged first to Rukkhadevata and powered the Akasha System, then to Nahida. In Chapter III Nahida gave Dottore the Dendro Gnosis in exchange for knowledge about the false sky. She also took the Electro Gnosis from Scaramouche's fallen "Shouki no Kami" and later gave it to Dottore in exchange for his agreeing to erase his Segments. A Gnosis is not required for an Archon to hold their power: Nahida stays the Dendro Archon with no Dendro Gnosis.
### LARGE +
The Gnosis connects its bearer directly to Celestia. Nahida needed an additional Gnosis (the Electro one) to enter Rukkhadevata's realm of consciousness, and later used the Pyro Gnosis, brought by the Traveler from Mavuika, to burn the corrupted Irminsul. Dottore remarked that she could not use the Electro Gnosis in battle, so usage of another element's Gnosis may be restricted.
### ULTRA +
Gnosis count as of 7.1: Anastasya has collected six Gnoses (all but the Pyro Gnosis, which vanished in the aftermath of the Irminsul fire). In the 7.1 Archon Quest "A Rekviem for the Underworld" she used her Cryo mastery and the six Gnoses together to slow time in a room of her palace and stall Rhinedottir.

## gnosis_event_note
@version: 6.6
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
In the Version 6.6 Archon Quest act "Truth Amongst the Pages of Purana", Nahida and her allies, with Mavuika's Pyro Gnosis carried by the Traveler, overloaded Irminsul using the Akasha System and then burned it. Dottore, who had attacked Irminsul from within, died in the fire. The Akasha System that Rukkhadevata had made to fight forbidden knowledge was used one last time in the plan.

## elemental_beings
### LITE
Apep, the Dendro Dragon Sovereign, and Dendro elemental lifeforms such as slimes, specters and the Dendro Hypostasis.
### STANDARD +
Apep survived the old world's wars and is still alive; the Traveler and Nahida helped her in the 3.6 quest Homecoming. Dendro Samachurls are hilichurl shamans who attack with vines and poison; Dendro Slimes are slimes shaped by abundant Dendro in their environment, which also gives them Dendro's weaknesses.
### LARGE +
- Apep contains an entire world inside her, the Heart of Oasis, in which she bore the first elemental lifeforms. Her attempt to gather forbidden knowledge to fight the Heavenly Principles contaminated both her and the Heart, and her creatures took on corrupted forms; Tumors of the Withering are what they become at the end of their lives. After Nahida erased forbidden knowledge, the creatures lost memories and helped restore the Heart, and Apep chose to rest and watch rather than pursue revenge.
- Dendro Specters have appeared since Version 3.0. One archive note records a researcher's conclusion that the Aranara are Dendro Specters, which quest text does not confirm.
- The Dendro Hypostasis is a boss that shoots Dendro orbs and transforms into a whip.
### ULTRA +
- The Aranara are small plant-like beings who can shape dreams. Rukkhadevata made them, and in the cataclysm they preserved the forest with the help of the Traveler's sibling.
- Dendroculi are consolidated Dendro energy; offered to Sumeru's Statues of The Seven they restore power the Statue lost over the years.

## known_uses
### LITE
Vines, leaves, seeds and plants grown or controlled, and Dendro Cores from Bloom. Some Dendro enemies, such as Large Dendro Slimes, inflict a poison status.
### STANDARD +
Nahida's power over dreams and consciousness is part of her Dendro Authority. Tighnari and Collei, Sumeru's rainforest rangers, hold Dendro Visions. The Sumeru rainforest itself is Rukkhadevata's work.
### LARGE +
- The Akasha System: Rukkhadevata's design to link minds, powered by the Dendro Gnosis; the sages later repurposed it into a knowledge network accessed through Akasha Terminals.
- Dreams: Nahida can enter, shape and send dreams as part of the Dendro Authority, and Sabzeruz Festival dream loops were abused by the sages.
- Purging: when Nahida erased forbidden knowledge from Irminsul, it was also purged from Apep and her Oasis.
### ULTRA +
- Visions are personal: Tighnari's Vision fell from his clothes after a conference in which he corrected a speaker. Collei's Vision appeared in her hand after a night on a cliff beside a sleeping girl, when Tighnari feared she had used power she should not.
- The Traveler: Dendro is likely the fourth element the Traveler resonated with, and its use is remarked on in Chapter III. Later canon has the Traveler wielding all seven elements.

## limits
### LITE
Dendro does not react with Anemo or Geo.
### STANDARD +
Dendro reacts with Hydro, Electro and Pyro only. A Vision fixes its bearer to one element.
### LARGE +
Gameplay: "Wood" objects (vines, bushes, shields) behave like a lesser sibling element that can burn but cannot trigger Quicken or Bloom. Dendro Slimes, Hypostases and elemental enemies are immune to Dendro.

## interactions
### LITE
Quicken (Dendro + Electro) leads to Aggravate and Spread. Bloom (Dendro + Hydro) creates a Dendro Core, which can become Hyperbloom with Electro or Burgeon with Pyro. Burning (Dendro + Pyro) ignites. Each is its own entry.
### STANDARD +
Lunar-Bloom (6.0) is a Lunar reaction version of Bloom: with certain characters in the party, Bloom becomes Lunar-Bloom and grants the party Verdant Dew.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not literal lore): Dendro is a damage type and the element of Nahida, Tighnari, Collei, Baizhu, Alhaitham, Kaveh, Yaoyao, Kirara, Emilie, Lauma, Nefer and others. The Traveler can switch to Dendro after resonating with a Statue of The Seven in Sumeru.
### LARGE +
The Elemental Resonance "Sprawling Greenery" (two or more Dendro characters) raises Elemental Mastery and gives additional Elemental Mastery after Burning, Quicken, Bloom, Aggravate, Spread, Hyperbloom and Burgeon reactions. Dendroculi raise the Statues of The Seven in Sumeru (271 in the overworld), and Dendro Sigils are the currency for the Tree of Dreams' Vanarana's Favor.

## gameplay_recent
@version: 6.1
### ULTRA
Gameplay, recent additions: Lunar-Bloom (6.0, Luna I) arrived with Lauma, and Nefer (6.1, Luna II) is another Dendro character who enables it.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay interpretation (this project's recommendation, not canon text): show Dendro as growth, scent and gentle light: leaves unfurling, vines tightening, fresh-cut grass, green glow with golden accents. Keep scale proportionate: a Vision holder can summon vines and seeds, not raise a forest. Dream-shaping and consciousness are Nahida's Authority, not general Dendro skills.
### LARGE +
Narratively, Dendro grows things, including knowledge and memory: leaves, ideas, seeds, dreams. A Bloom should visibly sprout something, and Burning should show the plant catching fire. Sumeru characters often treat plants and learning as sacred.
### ULTRA +
Avoid ascribing sleep control or mind reading to any Vision holder. Do not state that Rukkhadevata exists in anyone's memory except the Traveler's. Do not give the Dendro Gnosis back to Nahida; as of 7.1 it is in the Tsaritsa's hands.

## entry: Dendro Archon
@keys: Dendro Archon, God of Wisdom, Lesser Lord Kusanali, Greater Lord Rukkhadevata
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The Dendro Archon is the God of Wisdom, protector of Sumeru. There have been two holders: Greater Lord Rukkhadevata, who died in the cataclysm about 500 years ago, and her reincarnation Lesser Lord Kusanali (Nahida). In Chapter III Nahida erased Rukkhadevata from Irminsul, so the world remembers only Kusanali.
### LARGE +
Rukkhadevata created the Aranara, revived the rainforest and made the Akasha System. Kusanali was born a child with no memories because her predecessor overexerted herself fighting the Abyss.

## entry: Dendro Dragon Sovereign
@keys: Apep, Dendro Dragon, Dendro Sovereign, Heart of Oasis
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
Apep is the Dendro Dragon Sovereign. She ruled all Sumeru and created the first elemental lifeforms in an inner world, the Heart of Oasis. Attempting to gather forbidden knowledge to resist the Heavenly Principles corrupted her and her creatures. The Celestial Nail that turned Sumeru to desert forced her to adapt. After Nahida purged forbidden knowledge, the Traveler and Nahida helped restore the Heart (3.6, Homecoming), and Apep chose to rest and observe humanity and the gods.

## entry: Dendroculus
@keys: Dendroculus, Dendroculi
### STANDARD
A Dendroculus is an Adventure Item scattered across Sumeru; item text calls it a substance "that has accumulated intense Dendro energy" which can be offered to a Statue of The Seven to restore power it has lost.

## entry: Dendro elemental lifeforms
@keys: Dendro Slime, Dendro Specter, Dendro Samachurl, Dendro Hypostasis
### LARGE
Dendro Slimes have changed through ecological adaptation to abundant Dendro and burn intensely in bright flame. Dendro Samachurls are hilichurl shamans whose archive note says such shamans often peak in old age, and describes a grudge beneath the mask and poison ivy sprouting from hateful whispers. Dendro Specters have appeared since Version 3.0. The Dendro Hypostasis is a boss that shoots Dendro orbs and whips. In gameplay these are immune to Dendro (Samachurls resist it).

## entry: Nagadus Emerald
@keys: Nagadus Emerald, Nagadus Emeralds
### STANDARD
Nagadus Emeralds are the ascension gems used for Dendro characters.
