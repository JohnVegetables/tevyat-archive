## summary
### LITE
The Elements are Teyvat's seven forms of elemental power: Anemo (wind), Geo (earth), Electro (lightning), Dendro (plants), Hydro (water), Pyro (fire) and Cryo (ice). Each is bound to one Archon and that Archon's nation. Ordinary people wield an element through a Vision, a jewel-like focus granted to those with strong desires.
### STANDARD +
Behind the modern order lie the Seven Sovereigns, the elemental dragons of the old world, whose Authorities were taken by the Heavenly Principles and tied to the Archons' divine thrones. Archons can use their element without a Gnosis, while mortals normally need a Vision.
### LARGE +
As of 7.1, one divine throne has been destroyed (Hydro) and its Authority returned to the Hydro Sovereign, six Gnoses are known to have been gathered by the Fatui and the Tsaritsa, and the Pyro Gnosis has vanished. Two newer Vision-like objects exist alongside Visions: Moon Wheels, associated with Nod-Krai and the moon, and Stellar Linchpins from Snezhnaya.

## in_universe
### LITE
Each element pairs with one Archon and one nation. Canon does not explain why.
### STANDARD +
Canon does not say how an Archon relates to their element, nor how a region is shaped by its element.
### LARGE +
Some modern nations sit on the former land of the matching Dragon Sovereign. Sumeru was once the domain of the Dendro dragon Apep, the Primordial Sea beneath Fontaine was once governed by the Hydro Dragon, and Natlan was dominated by the Pyro dragon Xiuhcoatl and his draconic kin. Canon does not turn this pattern into an explanation. Most Archons already had some mastery of their element before they ascended: Barbatos began as a wind spirit, Morax was already called a god of Geo long before he became the Geo Archon, and Ei is described as an incarnation of lightning. Pyro is the exception in kind, since Pyro Archons are mortals rather than gods.
### ULTRA +
An Archon's power is attributed to three sources: ruling their region, their people's faith, and the Authority taken from the Sovereigns. Focalors is a rare case, having given up her innate Hydro ability as an Oceanid when she became a Fontainian human. Because no source explains the deeper relationship, treat claims that "the nation causes the element" or the reverse as open.

## the_seven_elements
### LITE
Anemo: Mondstadt, Barbatos (Venti). Geo: Liyue, Morax (Zhongli). Electro: Inazuma, the Raiden Shogun (Ei). Dendro: Sumeru, Nahida. Hydro: Fontaine, Focalors. Pyro: Natlan, Mavuika. Cryo: Snezhnaya, the Tsaritsa (Anastasya).
### STANDARD +
Barbatos and Morax are the only two original Archons still living; the rest of the original Seven have died and been replaced. After the Cataclysm 500 years ago, Ei replaced Makoto, Nahida replaced Greater Lord Rukkhadevata, Focalors replaced Egeria, and Anastasya replaced the Belyi Tsar as Cryo Archon. Mavuika placed her life in the Sacred Flame so that she could return after 500 years.
### LARGE +
Hydro is now unusual. In the Fontaine Archon Quest (Masquerade of the Guilty) Focalors destroyed the Hydro Archon's divine throne and returned its Authority to the Hydro Sovereign, so only six thrones remain. The Hydro Dragon Sovereign is now Neuvillette, born in human form. The Element page lists Neuvillette's source of power as an Ancient Dragon's Authority rather than a Vision.

## origins
@version: 4.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The seven elemental Authorities were originally wielded by the Seven Sovereigns, the most powerful dragons of the old world, who ruled alongside the Dragon King Nibelung. When the Heavenly Principles came to Teyvat they took part of those Authorities and later tied them to the Divine Thrones, which became the Archons' Authorities (Calamitous Tread, 4.1). Visions are also derived from these Authorities.
### LARGE +
Elemental energy as the modern world knows it was made later, as the Natlan Archon Quest (5.1, Beneath the Secret Source) reveals. The Heavenly Principles divided the raw elemental currents of the Light Realm into seven, using Phlogiston, Teyvat's primordial energy, as a base. The result was easier to control and more compatible with the Human Realm, and was meant to counter the Abyss (the Void Realm) better. Elemental energy is therefore regarded as the modern counterpart of Phlogiston.
### ULTRA +
After the war of the Funerary Flame, the Heavenly Principles were badly wounded and could no longer suppress the old order by their own absolute authority. With an unidentified entity ("the one who came after") they carved seven Gnoses from the remains of the Third Descender and stole part of the Sovereigns' Authorities, then set the gods against one another in the Archon War to choose seven victors. Details of this episode come from several late quests and the Gnosis and Seven Sovereigns pages; it is among the most spoiler-heavy material in the archive.

## gnosis_origin
@version: 7.1
@spoiler: major
### ULTRA
The 7.1 Archon Quest (Black Feathers Scattered at Parting) reveals that the Third Descender was Saarelainen, Anastasya's lover and the ancestor of the Khaenri'ahns. He consented to having his remains split into the seven Gnoses in exchange for Anastasya's resurrection and a promise that the Heavenly Principles would never harm his descendants. In the same quest, Anastasya used her Cryo mastery together with the six Gnoses she holds to slow time within a confined space.

## sovereigns
@version: 4.1
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
Each element has a Dragon Sovereign. The Sovereigns lost their status and most of their power when their Authorities were taken, and the living ones cannot use their full abilities, or recall the old world, unless their Archon dies or the throne is destroyed and the Authority returned. A new generation is being born as the original Sovereigns fade.
### ULTRA +
- Anemo: Dvalin, the new Anemo Sovereign, born from a convergence of elemental energy in the high heavens, friend of Barbatos.
- Geo: Azhdaha, once befriended by Morax and later sealed by him under the Dragon-Queller after erosion made him forget his love of humanity.
- Dendro: Apep, who held Sumeru's lush land and nurtured the ancient Dendro lifeforms.
- Hydro: the Hydro Dragon presided over the Primordial Sea; the current Sovereign is Neuvillette, whose Authority was fully restored with the end of Masquerade of the Guilty.
- Pyro: Xiuhcoatl, who held Pyro and presided over light, heat and wisdom; his power is said to show up in saurians directly descended from him.
- Cryo: a Cryo Sovereign whose corpse was used by Hyperborea in a failed Primordial Human project and which afflicted Snezhnaya with primordial ice.
- Electro: the sources read for this entry do not name the Electro Sovereign.
Sovereigns can only be born from elementally pure dragons. The Hydro Sovereign was not born from the Bathysmal Vishaps, who had strayed into impure Cryo and Electro variants, and so took human form. Canon also records that, in the Funerary Year, Nibelung returned bringing Abyssal power, which woke the slumbering Sovereigns for a great war against the Heavenly Principles; the Heavenly Principles dropped Celestial Nails and were left severely weakened.

## gnoses
@version: 1.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
A Gnosis is a chess-piece-like focus that, according to Venti in Ending Note (Prologue, 1.0), resonates directly with Celestia and is far more advanced than a Vision. Seven were made, one per element, and each belongs to an Archon. Archons can use their element without a Gnosis, and an Archon's divine throne exists independently of whether they hold it.
### LARGE +
The Fatui, under the Tsaritsa, collect the Gnoses. Their known holders as of Truth Amongst the Pages of Purana were the Cryo Gnosis (the Tsaritsa), the Anemo Gnosis (taken from Venti by Signora), the Geo Gnosis (obtained from Zhongli under his contract), the Electro and Dendro Gnoses (held by Dottore after dealings with Nahida) and the Hydro Gnosis (given to Arlecchino by Neuvillette). The Pyro Gnosis was never retrieved by the Fatui and vanished after Nahida used it to burn the Irminsul. As of 7.1 the Tsaritsa holds six Gnoses, every one except Pyro.
### ULTRA +
Skirk, who learned the macabre history of the Gnoses from her master Surtalogi, regards them as objects of misfortune. Like Visions, Gnoses can hold their owner's ambitions, and losing one can take strong obsessions with it. The Gnoses are the subject of their own entries, which carry the per-element detail.

## visions
@version: 1.0
### STANDARD
A Vision is a jewel-like amulet that lets its wearer channel one element directly. Visions are given to "allogenes", people with strong desires and the potential to become gods, and take the shape of a gem with the element's symbol, often in a regional setting. A Vision does not grant instant mastery: using the element takes practice, so skill varies widely. Visions can be channeled through mundane objects and put to everyday tasks. Those who hold one have some sensitivity to elemental energy (Elemental Sight). When a holder dies, their Vision becomes a Masterless Vision, losing its power and element indicator, though some later resonate with a new person.
### LARGE +
The Archons do not choose the holders: they have no control over who receives a Vision and know nothing about the recipient, and are only obliged to contribute a portion of their Authority when notified. Evidence suggests that a person deprived of their Vision loses the ambition as well, and the gods may collect a "gift" when a holder's goal is achieved. Details remain deliberately ambiguous.

## non_vision_sources
@version: 5.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Not every character draws on a Vision. The Element page notes: the Traveler and the Wonderland Manekin have none; Venti, Zhongli, the Raiden Shogun, Nahida and Mavuika draw on a Gnosis or Archon power; Neuvillette on an Ancient Dragon's Authority; Skirk on the Seven-Shifting Serpent; Columbina on the Tri-Lunar Convergence.
### LARGE +
- Moon Wheels (associated with Nod-Krai): objects formed in a pact between the Heavenly Principles and the Moon Sisters, using kuuvahki, an older, purer form of elemental power. Their users enable Lunar Reactions. Known users include Ineffa, Lauma, Flins, Nefer, Linnea and Zibai.
- Stellar Linchpins (Snezhnaya): Vision-like objects tied by Fatui research to the deceased Third Descender, whose users enable Stellar Glimmer Reactions. Known users are Sandrone, Odette and Vesna, among others.
- The Seven-Shifting Serpent (Chaos Permeation) is a Khaenri'ahn spell that disguises Abyssal power as an element; Skirk uses it to pass as Cryo.
### ULTRA +
Moon Wheels were distributed to the Moon Sisters' followers as a means of communication, and the wiki suggests Visions were formed on the same principle with modern elemental energy. The Fatui seek to use Stellar Linchpin energy in Project Mistilteinn, aimed at the Heavenly Principles, who would find Light Realm energy futile to fight.

## reactions
### LITE
When a second element is applied to a target already carrying one, the two can react, for example Vaporize (Pyro with Hydro), Melt (Pyro with Cryo), Overloaded (Pyro with Electro) or Swirl (Anemo spreading another element).
### STANDARD +
Further examples: Hydro and Electro trigger Electro-Charged; Electro and Cryo, Superconduct; Hydro and Cryo freeze the target; Dendro with Hydro, Bloom; Geo produces Crystallize.
### LARGE +
Reactions fall into groups. Transformative reactions (Overloaded, Shattered, Electro-Charged, Superconduct, Swirl, Burning, Bloom, Hyperbloom, Burgeon) deal their own damage. Amplifying reactions (Vaporize, Melt) multiply the triggering hit. Additive reactions (Spread, Aggravate) add to it. Elemental Mastery strengthens them.
### ULTRA +
Newer families extend the table. Lunar Reactions (Lunar-Bloom, Lunar-Charged, Lunar-Crystallize) need a character with a Moonsign Benediction passive, are tied to Hydro and use kuuvahki, the power associated with Nod-Krai (added from 5.8). Stellar Glimmer Reactions (Stellar-Conduct, Stellar Swirl), tied to Cryo, arrived with Stellar-Conduct in 6.7 and Stellar Swirl in 7.0, and need a character with the matching passive; the wiki notes Saarelainen introduced them to ancient Snezhnaya.

## elemental_resonance
@availability: permanent
### LARGE
Gameplay: when a full party includes two or more characters of the same element, an Elemental Resonance applies. Examples: Fervent Flames (Pyro) raises ATK, Soothing Water (Hydro) raises Max HP, Impetuous Winds (Anemo) lowers stamina use and shortens cooldowns, and Protective Canopy (four distinct elements) raises Elemental and Physical RES by 15%. Every other element has its own named resonance.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay, not literal lore: the Elements are seven of the eight damage types, the eighth being Physical. Each character has one element, shown by a Vision-style icon.
### LARGE +
Enemies can carry elemental auras, Elemental Nodes imbue enemies in some domains, and characters have Elemental DMG Bonus and Elemental RES stats.
Only the Traveler and Wonderland Manekin can change element, by resonating with a Statue of The Seven in a given nation. Characters who use more than one element in the story, such as Tartaglia (Hydro Vision, Electro Delusion) or Kazuha (briefly reawakening a Masterless Electro Vision), use only one in play. Dendro had no playable character until Version 3.0.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay interpretation (this project's recommendation, not canon text): treat the element as the colour and texture of a power, not a switch that controls the world. Show it through sensory cues: pressure and sound for Anemo, tremors and stone for Geo, crackle and static for Electro, growth and spores for Dendro, flow and chill for Hydro, heat and light for Pyro, frost and silence for Cryo.
### LARGE +
Keep scale proportionate. A Vision holder channels a modest slice; reshaping a climate or a mountain is Archon-level work. Distinguish who is using what: Vision, Gnosis, Moon Wheel, Stellar Linchpin and Dragon Authority are different sources and ordinary people mostly know only Visions.
### ULTRA +
Do not have a common NPC understand the Sovereigns, Gnoses or the Heavenly Principles. Most Mondstadters, for example, would not know a bard is their god. Do not let Visions be "given by the Archons", since canon says the Archons do not choose recipients; if characters believe this, record it as their belief.

## limits
### STANDARD
A Vision fixes its holder to one element and cannot ordinarily be destroyed or tampered with by human methods. Elemental energy can run out: a Vision holder can burn through too much at once and need rest. Masterless Visions go dull when their holder dies. Gnoses held by non-owners may be restricted in what they can do.
### LARGE +
Spoiler boundary: the origin of the Gnoses, the Sovereigns' history and the Heavenly Principles are late-game revelations (4.1, 5.1 and later). A character should not know them unless they were present.

## entry: Seven Sovereigns
@keys: Seven Sovereigns, Dragon Sovereign, Dragon Sovereigns, elemental dragons, elemental dragon sovereigns
@version: 4.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The Seven Sovereigns are the seven most powerful elemental lifeforms of the Light Realm and ruled the old world alongside Nibelung. The Heavenly Principles took their Authorities and gave them to the Archons' thrones. Living Sovereigns cannot use their full power until their Authority is returned. The Sovereign dragons are Dvalin (Anemo), Azhdaha (Geo), Apep (Dendro), Neuvillette (Hydro) and Xiuhcoatl (Pyro).
### LARGE +
Each Sovereign's land was attuned to its element. The Sovereigns were defeated by the Heavenly Principles and the Four Shades; some fled into the ocean depths. Nibelung's later return with Abyssal power woke them for a failed war of vengeance. The Cryo Sovereign is dead; the Electro Sovereign is not named in the sources read here.

## entry: Allogenes
@keys: allogene, allogenes
@version: 1.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
"Allogenes" is the gods' secret term for Vision holders: people with strong desires who could one day ascend to Celestia and become gods. Venti told the Traveler this in Ending Note (Prologue). Most holders and ordinary Teyvatians do not know the word.

## entry: Masterless Vision
@keys: Masterless Vision, Masterless Visions
@version: 1.0
### STANDARD
When a Vision's holder dies, it loses its power and element and becomes a Masterless Vision. Sometimes one resonates with a new person. The very rich seek them as commodities, according to Ningguang's account. Kaedehara Kazuha once briefly used a late friend's Masterless Electro Vision.

## entry: Moon Wheel
@keys: Moon Wheel, Moon Wheels
@version: 5.8
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Moon Wheels are Vision-like objects associated with Nod-Krai and the moon, formed from the stuff of the soul in a pact between the Heavenly Principles and the Moon Sisters. They use kuuvahki, an older, purer elemental power, and enable Lunar Reactions in combat. Nod-Krai Moon Wheels look identical to Nod-Krai Visions; Zibai, the only Moon Wheel user from outside Nod-Krai, has a differently shaped frame.

## entry: Stellar Linchpin
@keys: Stellar Linchpin, Stellar Linchpins
@version: 6.7
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Stellar Linchpins are Vision-like objects from Snezhnaya, whose users have a power that seems to come from beyond Teyvat. Fatui research links them to the dead Third Descender, Saarelainen: impurities from its remains fix in place when a Linchpin manifests. Known users include Sandrone, Odette and Vesna; they enable Stellar Glimmer Reactions.

## entry: Elemental Energy
@keys: elemental energy origin, Light Realm energy, raw elemental currents
@version: 5.1
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
The Lord of the Night's account in the Natlan Archon Quest (5.1) is that the Heavenly Principles divided the Light Realm's raw elemental currents into seven, built on Phlogiston, producing the elemental energy that people now use. This made the energy easier to control and better suited to the Human Realm, to counter the Abyss.
