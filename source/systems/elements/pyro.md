## summary
### LITE
Pyro is the element of fire, one of Teyvat's seven elements. Its Archon is the God of War and bears the divine name Haborym; the current Pyro Archon is Mavuika, who rules Natlan, the land of Pyro. Unlike other Archons, Pyro Archons are mortals who win the throne in battle. People with Pyro Visions ignite, burn and explode things. The Pyro Gnosis never reached the Fatui; as of 7.1 it has vanished.
### STANDARD +
Pyro ascension gems are Agnidus Agates. Natlan's Statues of The Seven take Pyroculi. The Pyro Archon looks after the Sacred Flame, the fire that protects Natlan and its warriors.

## in_universe
### LITE
Most people use Pyro through a Vision. A Pyro Archon draws on the Sacred Flame and the Pyro Archon's divine throne instead. In Natlan the element is bound up with war, oaths and the idea that humans can become gods.
### STANDARD +
Natlan's culture treats fire as something earned in contest. Contending Fire, the energy produced when Natlanese warriors fight each other, fuels the Sacred Flame, especially through the Pilgrimage of the Return of the Sacred Flame. Objects carried in battle can also become soaked with Contending Fire and be burned to feed it. Elsewhere in Teyvat, Pyro is the ordinary element of campfires, forges, kitchens and fireworks.
### LARGE +
Pyro is also linked to Phlogiston, Teyvat's primordial form of energy. Xiuhcoatl, the Pyro Dragon Sovereign, could manipulate Phlogiston and presided over light, heat and wisdom. According to the Archon Quest as summarised on the wiki, he was born from the primal flame on the Sacred Mountain and used it to make his younger brother Kukulkan and the other Pyro dragons of Natlan.

## origins
@version: 5.1
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
Origins, as canon tells them. The elemental Authorities first belonged to the Seven Sovereigns, the great elemental dragons of the old world. The Heavenly Principles stole portions of those powers and tied them to the Divine Thrones, which became the Archons' Authorities (revealed in the Fontaine Archon Quest, 4.1). Xiuhcoatl's Pyro Authority was among them: the Heavenly Principles took part of it and froze him in lava, a "false death". Visions derive from these Authorities. Later, in the Natlan Archon Quest (5.1), the Lord of the Night says the Heavenly Principles made elemental energy by splitting the raw currents of the Light Realm into seven on a base of Phlogiston, to be easier to control and to counter the Abyss. How an Archon relates to their element is not explained.

## archon_and_nation
### LITE
Archon: the Pyro Archon, God of War among The Seven, divine name Haborym. Current holder: Mavuika. Nation: Natlan.
### STANDARD +
Every Pyro Archon begins as a human and wins the position through battle. They stay mortal: they age and die, so Natlan has had many Pyro Archons. When a new one takes the divine throne, an inner flame awakens and the Sacred Flame hands them deep knowledge and memory of the land. They are also charged with protecting and fuelling the Sacred Flame.
### LARGE +
The first Pyro Archon was Xbalanque, who established the "rules" by which ordinary people can ascend; he is said to have killed Xiuhcoatl about 2,000 years ago and returned to life wreathed in fire. A second, scarlet-eyed youth of the Tlalocan tribe is credited with leading the tribes against Och-Kan and asked to be erased from history. Other named Archons include Ndenge and Xumucul (implied by their naming alongside Pyro Archons), Cochanina of the Meztli tribe (who drove the Volcano Lord out of the land of the People of the Springs and worked to contain the Bakunawa), Gloharry, Shulevitz and Isaja. An unnamed Pyro Archon who knew Venti is linked to the Muratans in the manga prologue. The Pyro Archon originally fated by the Heavenly Principles was Och-Kan, a half-dragon, not a human; the Belyi Tsar reads Xbalanque's usurpation as one of the long chain of rebellions against Celestia.
### ULTRA +
Mavuika, a Scion of the Canopy (Huitztlan), was Pyro Archon during the Cataclysm about 500 years ago. She placed her life within the Sacred Flame so she could die early and wake again in the present, and she holds a unique right to wield the power of the Ruler of Death, Ronova, once, at the cost of her fated death. Her Ancient Name is Kiongozi. The wiki suggests, by the same logic as Haborym, that all Pyro Archons may bear it; canon does not say so outright.

## gnosis_and_authority
@version: 5.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
A Gnosis is a device that resonates directly with Celestia and is more advanced than a Vision. Seven were made, one per element. The Pyro Gnosis was the one Capitano of the Fatui first pursued, but his duel with Mavuika ended inconclusively and he changed course, so the Fatui never retrieved it. Mavuika had long refused to use it, because rewriting the ley lines with it would erase Natlan's history.
### LARGE +
Archons keep their elemental power and divine thrones without a Gnosis. Mavuika still had the Pyro Gnosis during the fighting against Gosoythoth: she used it to relight the Sacred Flame after Gosoythoth's manifestation put it out, and to displace the Abyssal beast from above the Stadium.

## gnosis_status
@version: 6.6
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
In Song of the Welkin Moon Act X, "Truth Amongst the Pages of Purana" (6.6), the Traveler obtained the Pyro Gnosis from Mavuika so Nahida could burn down the corrupted Irminsul. The flames burned for seven days and nights, and afterwards the Gnosis vanished. Mavuika felt it go before Nahida confirmed it. Neither found remains. Later the Traveler says its destruction was confirmed and that Mavuika told them it had returned to the ley lines. Archons' thrones do not depend on the Gnosis, so Mavuika remains Pyro Archon as of 7.1.

## elemental_beings
### LITE
Xiuhcoatl, the Pyro Dragon Sovereign, and Pyro lifeforms such as Pyro slimes, specters, Abyss Mages, the Pyro Hypostasis and the Pyro Regisvine.
### STANDARD +
Pyro Slimes are described as small monsters formed by Pyro settling throughout nature. Pyro Specters are born from highly concentrated Pyro and float in the air; an Akademiya student reportedly argued they were a kind of Crystalfly, and has not graduated. Pyro Abyss Mages command Pyro and can communicate with hilichurls, so they manipulate them easily.
### LARGE +
- Pyro Hypostasis (code name Ayin): a high-purity Pyro entity found in Inazuma that can revive itself in an emergency restart phase.
- Pyro Regisvine: a giant vine that has absorbed the ancient flame raging in the ley lines, found in Liyue and described as restless and furious.
- Xiuhcoatl: the Flamelord, Pyro Dragon of the old world and creator of the Pyro dragons of Natlan. The Heavenly Principles froze him in lava; Abyssal corruption later wore down his clarity; Xbalanque killed him about 2,000 years ago. Some in Natlan believe he still sleeps in the volcano.
- Some Saurians are born with the Flamelord's Blessing, a mark tied to Xiuhcoatl that grants more power than modern Saurians can bear.
- Gosoythoth, the Abyssal beast that took Xiuhcoatl's form, has its own entry.
### ULTRA +
- Escoffier says the condensate from Pyro Slimes tastes spicy, like Jueyun Chili, with a small amount of an extremely sweet substance.
- Ranjit says Pyro Regisvine fruit is "pretty good" and tastes of Pyro.

## known_uses
### LITE
Igniting and burning things, explosions, heating, cooking and forging, and fire-based attacks. Pyro also melts ice and boils water in many fights.
### STANDARD +
In Natlan, Pyro is also a way to carry names and promises: objects carried in battle become imbued with Contending Fire and can be burned as fuel, as Mavuika does with her keepsakes, and Pyro Archons move the dead through the Sacred Flame during the Ode of Resurrection, stepping into the flame and returning with the fallen warrior.
### LARGE +
- Outside Natlan, Pyro Visions are held by a wide range of people, among them Klee, Diluc, Amber, Bennett, Xiangling, Xinyan, Hu Tao, Yanfei, Thoma, Yoimiya, Dehya, Gaming, Chevreuse, Lyney and Arlecchino.
- Durin (6.2) is a playable Pyro Sword user and Nicole (6.6) a playable Pyro Catalyst user. Neither 7.0 nor 7.1 added a Pyro character.

## limits
### LITE
Pyro does not react with Pyro. It does little on its own against water or ice.
### STANDARD +
Pyro needs a Vision or an Archon's authority; ordinary fire is just fire. Keep scale proportionate to the source.
### LARGE +
Gameplay: a Pyro aura on its own has no inherent effect; it only enables reactions. The reactions are covered in their own entries.

## interactions
### LITE
Vaporize with Hydro, Melt with Cryo, Overloaded with Electro, Burning with Dendro, Swirl with Anemo and Crystallize with Geo.
### STANDARD +
Vaporize amplifies damage when Pyro or Hydro triggers it; Melt does so with Cryo. Overloaded is an explosion with strong knockback. Burning keeps Pyro damage ticking in a small area around a target while a Dendro aura exists. Swirl spreads Pyro to nearby enemies. Crystallize makes a Pyro shard that grants a Pyro shield. Burgeon is Pyro detonating a Dendro Core created by Bloom.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not literal lore): Pyro is a damage type and the element of Pyro Vision holders. The Elemental Resonance Fervent Flames (two or more Pyro characters in a party) shortens Cryo's effect by 40% and raises ATK by 25%.
### LARGE +
Pyro is the only element the Traveler cannot resonate with immediately upon touching its Statue; in Natlan, switching to Pyro talents requires finishing a step of the quest "When All Becomes a Monument". Pyroculi are found only in Natlan, where 249 are in the open world and 22 in the Ancient Sacred Mountain; they are limited and do not respawn. Pyro Sigils are Natlan's currency, offered at the Tablet of Tona. Agnidus Agate is the ascension gem. Objects in the world that count as Pyro include campfires, torches, Pyro exploding barrels and flaming flowers. Pyro Slimes can be put out by one gauge of Hydro, two of Cryo or Electro, or four of Anemo or Geo, but they re-ignite whenever hit by Pyro damage.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay interpretation (this project's recommendation, not canon text): show Pyro as heat, light and sound: air shimmering, embers drifting, sudden warmth, crackle. Warm orange and red are safe visual cues. In Natlan, fire reads as honour, contest and shared meals rather than just destruction.
### LARGE +
A Pyro Vision holder can scorch, ignite or boil water, but reshaping a landscape is an Archon-level act. Keep Mavuika's fire friendly by default. Remember that a Pyro Archon is mortal and human-born, and can be ordinary company, not a remote deity.
### ULTRA +
Avoid portraying Pyro as uncontrollably violent; canon shows deliberate control. Do not give the Pyro Gnosis back to Mavuika or place it in the Fatui's hands as of 7.1.

## entry: Pyro Archon
@keys: Pyro Archon, Haborym, God of War
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The Pyro Archon is the God of War among The Seven and the ruler of Natlan. The divine name Haborym is linked to the position. Every Pyro Archon starts as a human who wins the position by battle and remains mortal. Mavuika, who is both the current Pyro Archon and the one who held the throne at the Cataclysm, is covered by her own entry.
### LARGE +
Earlier Archons include Xbalanque (the first), a scarlet-eyed youth, Cochanina, Gloharry, Shulevitz and Isaja. The Pyro Archon looks after the Sacred Flame, which is a conduit to the Night Kingdom and the Wayob.

## entry: Pyro Gnosis
@keys: Pyro Gnosis
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The Pyro Gnosis was never collected by the Fatui. Mavuika, who had always refused to use it, passed it through the Traveler so Nahida could burn the corrupted Irminsul in 6.6. It then disappeared. Mavuika sensed it go; its exact fate is not stated.

## entry: Xiuhcoatl
@keys: Xiuhcoatl, Flamelord, Pyro Dragon
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
Xiuhcoatl, also called the Flamelord, was the Pyro Dragon of the Seven Sovereigns and ruled Natlan before the Archons. The Heavenly Principles stole part of his Authority and froze him in lava. Abyssal corruption later wore down his clarity. About 2,000 years ago Xbalanque fought him at the Great Volcano of Tollan; both fell and died, and Xbalanque revived in flames. Some say Xiuhcoatl still sleeps in the volcano, while others say only a husk is left.

## entry: Sacred Flame
@keys: Sacred Flame, K'ahk', Contending Fire
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
The Sacred Flame is a fire that burns continually in the Stadium of the Sacred Flame. It is a conduit to the Night Kingdom and the Wayob, and a base of Natlan's resistance to the Abyss. It is fuelled by Contending Fire from combat between Natlanese warriors. Smaller flames split from it are used in rituals such as the Scions of the Canopy's Turnfire Night.
### LARGE +
During the Ode of Resurrection the Pyro Archon steps into the flame, fetches a fallen warrior from the Night Kingdom and walks out with them. As Natlan's condition worsened, Mavuika gave her own power to keep it alight.

## entry: Pyro lifeforms
@keys: Pyro Slime, Pyro Specter, Pyro Hypostasis, Pyro Regisvine, Pyro Abyss Mage
### LARGE
Pyro Slimes are small monsters made when Pyro settles throughout nature. Pyro Specters are born from concentrated Pyro and float. Pyro Abyss Mages command Pyro and can influence hilichurls. The Pyro Hypostasis is a high-purity Pyro entity, in Inazuma, that can revive in an emergency restart; the Pyro Regisvine is a giant vine fed by ley-line flame, found in Liyue.
