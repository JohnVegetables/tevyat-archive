## summary
### LITE
Fetor is the energy that leaks into the living world when the border between life and death is disturbed. It is a sign of death pressing in on the living and harms those it touches. It was revealed in the limited-time Liyue event "Springtime Charms" (Lantern Rite, 5.3).
### STANDARD +
Canon says it is not Abyssal and not divine in origin, and Chongyun distinguishes it from the "evil spirits" exorcists hunt.

## in_universe
@version: 5.3
@availability: limited
### LITE
Small leaks happen whenever the border is disturbed. The Wangsheng Funeral Parlor is responsible for burning them away with its secret technique, the Art of Homa. In the event, Chongyun senses a strange chill around a dying elder and names it the "fetor of death."
### STANDARD +
The body absorbs fetor easily and, like Abyssal energy, it seems to linger. In humans it sharply speeds up ageing, leading to fatigue, weakness, coma and eventually death. In monsters such as hilichurls it drives a frenzy. Adepti such as Xiao can perceive it directly; ordinary people mostly cannot, though they may feel unnaturally cold near it. Xiao can share his perception by marking a person with a Witness Sigil.
### LARGE +
Baizhu's account is that Changsheng's secret art does not affect fetor. Hu Tao explains that minor leaks are easy work for the Parlor, but a major one cannot be solved by burning, because the problem is the border itself, and the only remedy may be to purify the whole border. In the event this is done with the Seven-and-Eight-Gates Method, in which Homa altars at the gates burn the fetor away across Liyue. Qiqi absorbed a dangerous amount at the border yet survived, and Lan Yan remarks that she herself would probably not have; the wiki reads Qiqi as somewhat resistant, which may hold for other zombies.
### ULTRA +
Hu Tao says that about a dozen years before, a border incident at Wuwang Hill killed her father when he tried to burn off the fetor with the Art of Homa and was fatally exposed. In the climax the Traveler draws fetor out of Hu Tao and into themselves to save her; it fades from them afterwards. The event's setting is limited-time content: it is canon, but not every Liyue resident knows the details.

## gameplay
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not literal lore): during the event, fetor appears as balls of dark energy and as Fetor Nodes that can be temporarily dispelled with physical attacks.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay interpretation (this project's recommendation, not canon text): show fetor through cold, a heavy hush, tired limbs and people who look suddenly older. Only adepti and those marked by one see it clearly. Keep it Liyue-specific and distinct from Abyssal taint, curses and ghosts.
### LARGE +
Do not treat fetor as a curse that an exorcist can simply banish; Chongyun says it is not an evil spirit. Do not extend it to other nations without canon, and do not use Homa as a casual spell. Hu Tao treats it as serious professional work.

## entry: Fetor Node
@keys: Fetor Node, Fetor Nodes
@version: 5.3
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
Fetor can accumulate into Fetor Nodes. In the Springtime Charms event they are dispelled for a time by physical attacks, which opens the way past them.

## entry: Border between life and death
@keys: border between life and death, Wuwang Hill border
@keys2: fetor, Hu Tao, Wangsheng Funeral Parlor
@version: 5.3
@availability: limited
### LARGE
Liyue's border between life and death is a cold, dark place reached through a rift at Wuwang Hill during the event. When it is disturbed, fetor leaks out. Hu Tao, Qiqi and Lan Yan were trapped in it, and Xiao and the Traveler went in to bring them out. The border seems peculiar to Liyue.
