## summary
### LITE
Abyssal Corrosion Syndrome is an illness caused by direct contact with high concentrations of Abyssal power. It harms body and mind, can leave irreversible mental trauma, and mostly appears as hallucinations. It is named in Natlan during the Abyssal invasion. The worst cases are treated with the Draught of Lucidity.
### STANDARD +
The name is given by "The Captain" (Capitano) in the Archon Quest interlude "For the Same Land." Iansan later keeps patient records of the many people she finds affected.

## in_universe
### LITE
Symptoms include psychosis, often with sight and sound hallucinations and the reliving of past traumas. Severity follows the patient's level of trauma. In "For the Same Land," Atoco relives a childhood near-drowning, and Cochitta's fear of losing her loved ones is triggered by the memory of her parents' deaths.
### STANDARD +
Mild cases keep some awareness of the outside world and can be broken out of the hallucination by speaking to the person and by physical contact. Deep cases, such as the warrior Ocotlan, are lost in the hallucination and respond only to medicine.
### LARGE +
The Captain says the draught was developed for exactly such cases. Later, Paimon compares another episode in Natlan to the syndrome, noting that it can cause hallucinations and mental breakdowns. Saurians may also be susceptible: Ayo was melancholy after the invasion and the deaths of his grandparents, and a small dose of the draught lifted his mood.
### ULTRA +
During the Cataclysm, endless battles against the Abyss gave Khaenri'ahn soldiers hallucinations, to the point that some attacked each other. That experience led to the draught. See the Abyssal Corruption and Nature of the Abyss entries for other Abyssal harm.

## treatment
### LITE
Verbal and physical contact for light cases; the Draught of Lucidity for severe ones.
### STANDARD +
The draught dispels the psychological effects of the Abyss, but its side effects are severe, including chronic migraines for life. It is therefore reserved for the worst cases. It was made for humans and also works on saurians.
### LARGE +
Guthred, a respected Khaenri'ahn military doctor and the Captain's trusted second-in-command, developed the draught and used himself as the test subject to hurry the cure. He died shortly after completing it. In Natlan, the Captain supplied doses to the Flower-Feather Clan to cure their afflicted warriors. Ororon says Guthred's sacrifice for Natlan is a well-known tribal legend, and Mavuika believes the figure the Traveler saw in the Captain was Guthred's soul.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay interpretation (this project's recommendation, not canon text): portray a patient as sincere but locked in the past, answering people who are not there. Treat the draught as a harsh remedy, not a casual cure. Gentle talk and touch are canon for mild cases.
### LARGE +
Do not make the syndrome contagious, a Vision effect or a curse. Do not cure it with a simple potion for all patients. Do not claim Guthred still lives as an independent person.

## entry: Draught of Lucidity
@keys: Draught of Lucidity
### STANDARD
A medicine developed by the Khaenri'ahn military doctor Guthred to treat Abyssal Corrosion Syndrome. It dispels the psychological impact of the Abyss but can cause chronic migraines for life, so it is used only for severe cases. It works on saurians as well as humans.
### LARGE +
After the Abyssal invasion of Natlan, the Captain brought it to the Flower-Feather Clan at a critical moment and cured the most severely affected patients.
