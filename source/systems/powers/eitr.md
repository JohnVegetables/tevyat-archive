## summary
### LITE
The Eitr, full name Embryonic-Ichoric-Telluric-Rudiment, is a forbidden creation of Hyperborea, the lost golden city of Snezhnaya. It contains the power of the Light Realm and can be used to alter living bodies. In 7.1 the dying Ronova fused an orb of it to Snezhnaya's Celestial Nail.
### STANDARD +
The Belyi Tsar found it in the Shadow Realm, and the Hvergelmir institute later experimented with it, most visibly on a Snegovik.

## in_universe
### LITE
Canon describes the Eitr as the final result of forbidden technology that let Hyperborea's master crafters modify the very essence of life. It carries Light Realm power. Used on a body, it can give a creature power beyond what its kind normally has.
### STANDARD +
According to the Eye of Graeae, the Belyi Tsar obtained the Eitr not long after delving into the Shadow Realm, but began Eitr research only some time afterwards; no one knows why. A note left by a noble figure at the Pale Crown Palace records that during his 446th foray into the Shadow Realm the writer found the Eitr together with records about it. Those records concern Hyperborea's fate and its attempt to reach the original light of the world.
### LARGE +
Eitr alteration is said to leave a result similar to the makeup of the Belyi Tsar himself. The Eitr eventually came into the hands of Hvergelmir, the research institute of old Snezhnaya, where Koshchei and Pavlina experimented with it. Pavlina, princess of the Snegurochka, is said to have used Hvergelmir's techniques to modify Snegovik, giving their bodies power beyond their race's due. The wiki also credits Eitr research with influencing the decision to create the Wildwood Leshy; that link comes from alchemist's notes in the Ruined Hvergelmir.
### ULTRA +
The Eye of Graeae also notes that Pavlina was a primary researcher whose Prime Ice skill let her build facilities cold enough for research, and that she preserved many Hyperborean finds with Prime Ice at the Tsar's request. This places the Eitr among artifacts kept and studied in secrecy rather than a weapon used openly.

## known_uses
### STANDARD
Two uses are shown in canon. First, experimentation on living beings: the Snegovik Donskoy was turned into a snow giant by the Eitr (World Quest "A Great Heart Fettered by Slender Chains", 7.0), and the hidden objective "A Certain Noble's Relics" has the Traveler fight a Snegovik described as Eitr-altered and bound in ice. Second, Ronova's final act (see the 7.1 section below).
### LARGE +
Wildlife-like creatures altered this way are described as gaining power surpassing their race's due, not as becoming a different kind of being.

## ronova_fusion
@version: 7.1
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
In "Black Feathers Scattered at Parting" (Archon Quest, 7.1), after Pierro, Dainsleif and the Traveler defeated her, a mortally wounded Ronova, the Ruler of Death, felt death approach for the first time. She summoned an orb of light, which she described as hidden by Hyperborea in the depths of the Shadow Realm, and fused it to the Nail of Retribution hovering above the Cryo Dragon's remains over the ruins of Hyperborea, east of Snezhnograd. The Nail began to crumble as she disintegrated. She called it her parting gift and spoke of passing the sensation of death on to the world along with the hallowing of the soil.
### ULTRA +
After the cinematic, huge beams of light rose above the positions of the six Celestial Nails across Teyvat and the World Quest series "The Anomaly" became available in 7.1. The wiki ties the pillars to the fusion; canon does not yet say what they will cause.

## limits
### STANDARD
Do not turn the Eitr into a generic power-up or an item anyone can carry. Canon shows it only in Hyperborean and Hvergelmir research and in Ronova's hands, and it is tied to forbidden knowledge and Light Realm power. Its mechanism is unexplained.
### LARGE +
It is not a Gnosis, a Vision or an elemental energy source. Do not claim the Tsaritsa, the Fatui or any Harbinger uses it. Do not state what the light pillars will do.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay interpretation (this project's recommendation, not canon text): treat the Eitr as something spoken of in hushed, uneasy terms, if at all. Hvergelmir records, old researchers and the Eye of Graeae are natural sources. Ordinary Snezhnayans would not know the name.
### LARGE +
Keep the tone clinical and sorrowful when it comes up in connection with Donskoy or other altered Snegovik. Do not let a character who was not present for the 7.1 Archon Quest know what Ronova did.

## entry: Ronova's fusion of the Eitr
@keys: Nail of Retribution, Ronova's parting gift
@keys2: Eitr, Ronova
@version: 7.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
In "Black Feathers Scattered at Parting" (7.1), Ronova fused the Eitr to Snezhnaya's Celestial Nail as she died. She spoke of making Teyvat feel what dying is like as the Holy Soil spreads. Afterwards, pillars of light loomed over the six Nails.
### LARGE +
The Nail hangs above the remains of the Cryo Dragon over Hyperborea's ruins. Ronova's own entry carries her story; this entry only covers the Eitr.

## entry: Hvergelmir experiments
@keys: Hvergelmir experiments, Pavlina's experiments
@keys2: Eitr, Embryonic-Ichoric-Telluric-Rudiment
### STANDARD
Hvergelmir, the old research institute, held the Eitr after the Belyi Tsar found it. Koshchei and Pavlina used it in experiments on living beings. Pavlina, the Snegovik princess, modified Snegovik with it; Donskoy is the best-known subject.
### LARGE +
The Eye of Graeae records an old verdict, 530 years old, charging a defendant named Koshchei with using living fae for alchemical experiments. It says no record of an execution exists and that this may be someone who merely shares the name.

## entry: Donskoy
@keys: Donskoy, snow giant
@keys2: Eitr, Snegovik
### STANDARD
Donskoy was a Snegovik transformed into a snow giant by the Eitr. In the 7.0 World Quest "A Great Heart Fettered by Slender Chains" a gentle voice says his spirit was damaged during the war and that Pavlina ordered that a human girl not be allowed to meet him; Pavlina's own voice says he needs long, peaceful sleep. His own entry carries the rest.
