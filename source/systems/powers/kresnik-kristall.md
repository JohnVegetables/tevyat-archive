## summary
### LITE
Kresnik Kristall is the crystalline ore that keeps Snezhnaya warm. Burned in furnaces called Kresnik's Torches, it gives off heat efficiently, and every Snezhnayan city is built around one such torch. The ore is mined chiefly in Novokitezhgrad and shipped across the nation by rail.
### STANDARD +
The Royal Energy Commission of Snezhnaya oversees both the torches and the Kristall's transport and use. In gameplay the Eye of Graeae turns Kresnik Energy from Kristall and torches into protection against the cold.

## in_universe
### LITE
Most Snezhnayan settlements need Kresnik Kristall to survive. Before the torches existed, the Eye of Graeae says, the land was made only of Frigid and Subzero Zones and unfit for human life.
### STANDARD +
A torch holds a Normal Temperature Zone within a certain range and shields a city from snowstorms. Small civilian-model torches also stand across the countryside. Villages without a rail stop depend on deliveries; in "Gunfire in the Silent Lands" a character from the village of Alatyr fears that if Kristall deliveries have stopped there, the villagers will freeze.
### LARGE +
Snezhnaya's most important torch is in Kresnik's Burning Heart, a chamber under the central plaza of Snezhnograd. The wiki, citing an NPC of the Energy Commission, says it was built almost immediately after the Belyi Tsar completed his experiments in the Pale Crown Palace, where prototype torches remain. Loading-screen text states the Belyi Tsar created Kresnik's Torch to help humans survive the cold.
### ULTRA +
Zones form a scale: Normal Temperature, Cold, Frigid and Subzero. Settlements and warm places such as Flamefeather Valley are Normal; the edges of the map are Subzero. Who tended the earliest torches, and how the Commission arose, is not covered in canon read here (the Commission's history is blank on the wiki).

## origin
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
The Kristall is said to come from power released when the Cryo Dragon Sovereign lost control in Snezhnaya. A potion formula found in a world quest calls the ore the ideal material, as power refined from the authority released by a colossal beast of ages past that lost control.
### LARGE +
In "An Ark That Used to Be a Soul" (7.0), Aksinya explains that after the Cryo Dragon's corpse and unchecked authority turned Snezhnaya into Holy Soil of primordial ice, even the ley lines became veins of pure crystal, until the Heavenly Principles sent down a Celestial Nail. The wiki dates the event to about 6,000 years ago and infers that today's large deposits are remnants of that change.
### ULTRA +
Legend, as told by the Kristall Bug's archive entry, says early Snezhnayans found the rich ore beneath the frozen land by following the trails of Kristall Bugs, whose shells resemble the ore, and built a civilisation from it.

## known_uses
### STANDARD
Burned in a Kresnik's Torch, Kristall provides heat. Gameplay (not literal lore): each ore deposit and many torches release Kresnik Energy that the Eye of Graeae uses to keep the Traveler from Sheer Cold.
### LARGE +
Gameplay: mini torches release energy orbs at intervals and need a small amount of Kresnik Energy to activate; medium torches only release pickup orbs, and some are decorative. Kristall ore is mined by attacks, yields a fixed amount of energy and respawns after a short time. Catching a Kristall Bug also gives energy.

## limits
### STANDARD
Do not treat the ore as a source of elemental power for combat or as Cryo itself; canon describes its use as heat and energy for survival. Do not claim a character can make Kristall by Cryo or Pyro power.
### LARGE +
Do not state that the Fatui, the Tsaritsa or the Harbingers command the supply directly; oversight belongs to the Royal Energy Commission and the rail bureau transports it.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay interpretation (this project's recommendation, not canon text): in Snezhnaya, warmth is a civic fact. Characters notice how far a torch reaches, speak of stove-fuel and coal-like shipments, and feel a rail delay as a danger rather than an inconvenience. Foreigners are surprised that a Snezhnayan town is built around a heat source.
### LARGE +
Villages without a station worry about deliveries; city-dwellers take the torch for granted. Do not make the torches magical wonders to locals; they are everyday infrastructure.

## entry: Kresnik's Torch
@keys: Kresnik's Torch, Kresnik Torch, Kresnik Torches
### STANDARD
A furnace that burns Kresnik Kristall for heat. Large torches anchor cities, small civilian ones stand across the land, and the most important sits in Kresnik's Burning Heart beneath Snezhnograd. Prototype torches remain at the Pale Crown Palace. The Royal Energy Commission maintains them.

## entry: Royal Energy Commission of Snezhnaya
@keys: Royal Energy Commission, Energy Commission
@keys2: Kresnik, Kristall, Torch
### STANDARD
The Commission, seated in Snezhnograd, maintains the torches and manages the transport and use of the Kristall. Its acting chair is Zarechnaya; members include Mitya, Yelagina and Zaretsky. Its history is not documented in canon read here.

## entry: Kristall Bug
@keys: Kristall Bug, Kristall Bugs
### STANDARD
A beetle found near Snezhnayan mines whose shell resembles the ore. Local lore says people first found the deposits by following these bugs' trails.
