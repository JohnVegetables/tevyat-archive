## summary
### LITE
Archaeolune Energy is an ancient, primordial power found on the Frost Moon, the one intact moon above Nod-Krai. It is closely tied to kuuvahki, the moonlight-power of Nod-Krai, and is considered kuuvahki's primordial form; characters in the story usually call it "ancient moon energy". It fills the moon's core, powers the dragon-built machines on the moon, and can create life. It first appeared in the Moon Gazing quests (Version 6.7).
### STANDARD +
In game terms it is the basis of exploration on the Frost Moon: absorbing it puts the Traveler into a state called Moonvein Suffusion, which makes the body lighter and jumps higher and lets them fire Archaeolune Ripples. The wiki reports that Columbina simply calls it "Blue Kuuvahki".
### LARGE +
Its loading-screen description calls it an ancient power found on the Frost Moon, deeply connected to kuuvahki. Concentrated or stormy surges of it are dangerous to living things other than kuuhenki, the moon spirits.

## in_universe
### LITE
Kuuvahki, the power of the Three Moons, is a primordial form of elemental energy that belongs to the Light Realm. Columbina says kuuvahki and the ancient moon's energy are both, in essence, the power of the Light Realm. The wiki describes Archaeolune Energy as the primordial form of kuuvahki that shares the properties of two other primordial energies: phlogiston (programmable matter, an energy source, and a medium for storing and recording information) and the Primordial Sea (the power of creation, including the creation of life).
### STANDARD +
Dragon-era engineers built facilities on the moon around it. Pioneer Source Structures, said to be constructs of the dragons of Natlantea, store it. Ancient Moon Pearls are spherical energy vessels, pearl power stations feed it through energy pathways to doors and machines, and Archaeolune Energy Barriers guard dragon facilities linked to the moons. A deep base on the moon is powered by a stream of the energy rising from a fissure that leads to the moon's core. Eggie explains that this base was meant to turn the moon into a space-based weapon; Columbina describes it as the work of the returning dragon king Nibelung, who reforged the moon into a weapon whose core energy would become artillery fire aimed at Teyvat. That ruler was defeated by the Heavenly Principles, and Columbina says the site is no longer a threat.
### LARGE +
Creation and life. In the Moon Gazing finale, the recorded mark of the researcher Nin-Kiggia explains that the Three Moon Sisters allowed the researchers to borrow "the power of creation from the moon's very origin". When that primordial power of creation was triggered in the sea at the moon's core, a black kuuhenki was born, which is how Eggie came to exist. In an earlier episode, the scientist Maagees used an experimental technique to convert the dying Ungien into a being of pure ancient moon energy, creating Ungie, the first artificially birthed kuuhenki on the moon. Paimon compares this to the dragons' phlogistonization.
### ULTRA +
Information. Because the energy can store and record information (the phlogiston-like property), the researchers' data was collated into the moon's core. Nin-Kiggia's recording and Columbina explain that the researchers' data merged into this ocean and that, by the will of the moon, they are reborn in forms akin to Eggie, the cycle having possibly repeated more than once. Columbina also says she can infuse her own Light Realm power into transmitted messages to shield them. Do not present the cycle as fully explained: canon leaves its mechanism open.

## dangers
### STANDARD
Archaeolune Storms. When the lunar sea beneath Ungien's Circle is disturbed, highly concentrated energy leaks out as an Archaeolune Storm, which Columbina says harms all living beings other than kuuhenki. An occlusion field can temporarily hold the storms back. Blue areas of high concentration are best avoided.
### LARGE +
Knotted Moonlight Vines grow where the ancient moon's power has been thrown into turmoil, according to Eggie, and the Moonlight Dash can break them. Gameplay: standing in a storm or disturbance zone fills a bar, after which the player is incapacitated and returned to the last safe zone without HP or Energy loss.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not literal lore). Absorbing Archaeolune Energy from a source puts the Traveler in the Moonvein Suffusion state, which raises jump height, grants Archaeolune Ripples, and (per the wiki, flagged for verification) after a certain point in Pioneers (Part 2) also grants Moonlight Dash charges. Sources: Ancient Moon Pearls, Archaeolune Energy Nodes, Archaeolune Energy Surges, Pioneer Source Structures that are not on alert, and Wondrous Black Kuuhenki.
### LARGE +
Archaeolune Ripples can be aimed and detonated to damage objects in the blast area and to change the gravity acting on Archaeolune-affinitive materials. Energy Nodes work like quick-travel points and refill Ripple and Dash charges. Moonlight Dash turns the player into a black kuuhenki to dash quickly and ram Knotted Moonlit Vines. The Ripples can also hit Archaeolune Energy Barriers to open a temporary hole, activate Backup Power Generators, and make Archaeolune Energy-Affinitive Materials float or fall. Outside Ungien's Circle, the Frost Moon's turbulent energy makes jumps higher and falls slower, which lowers fall damage, although skill jumps and gliding are unaffected.

## limits
### STANDARD
Pearls cannot move once packed into a container, and barriers regenerate shortly after a hole is made. The energy is not one of the seven elements, and canon gives it no Vision or elemental reactions. Pioneer Source Structures can be drained only when not on alert, and then power back up after a short time.
### LARGE +
Kuuvahki and Archaeolune Energy are distinct in use: Wondrous Black Kuuhenki draw their power from Archaeolune Energy, not kuuvahki, while ordinary kuuhenki and Moon Wheel wielders work with kuuvahki.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay interpretation (this project's recommendation, not canon text). Archaeolune Energy is quiet and cold: blue-lit machinery, floating stones, slow falls and a faint sense of lightness. It belongs to the Frost Moon, not to ordinary Teyvat, and outsiders should treat it as unfamiliar and potentially harmful in high concentration.
### LARGE +
A character in a scene should not casually wield it unless they are Columbina, a moon-born being, or the Traveler with her grant. Scenes can use it as a clue source: pearls, power stations and terminals hint at a long-lost dragon-era project. Keep its Light Realm kinship with kuuvahki and phlogiston as hints, not a stated rule, and do not give it an element.

## examples
### LARGE
- Columbina gives the Traveler the power to fire Ripples at a dormant generator on the Frost Moon, reactivating a broken energy pathway (Pioneers, Part 1).
- Eggie explains that Knotted Moonlight Vines grow where the ancient moon's power has been disturbed, and that a sea of concentrated energy beneath Ungien's Circle powers the facility (Visitors From the Stars, Part 1).
- Maagees converts Ungien's failing body into pure ancient moon energy, creating the first artificial kuuhenki (Visitors From the Stars, Part 2).
- Nin-Kiggia's recording reveals that the primordial power of creation made Eggie (Project Moongazer, Part 2).

## entry: Moonvein Suffusion
@keys: Moonvein Suffusion, Archaeolune Ripples, Archaeolune Ripple, Moonlight Dash
@version: 6.7
### STANDARD
Moonvein Suffusion is the gameplay state entered by absorbing an object filled with Archaeolune Energy: the body feels lighter and jumps are higher, and the player can fire Archaeolune Ripples that detonate to damage objects and alter their gravity. The wiki notes that Moonlight Dash, which turns the player into a black kuuhenki for fast movement, is also granted after a certain quest step. Pressing the Elemental Burst button exits the state.

## entry: Ancient Moon Pearl
@keys: Ancient Moon Pearl, Ancient Moon Pearls, Pioneer Source Structure
@version: 6.7
### STANDARD
Ancient Moon Pearls are energy vessels for Archaeolune Energy, found on the Frost Moon and in Dunanna Pit. Their loading-screen description calls them wondrous spherical devices that roll, whose true function is to store energy. Pushing a pearl into a power station transfers its energy, which flows along pathways to power machinery; a launcher can throw a pearl (or a standing character) to a distant spot. Pioneer Source Structures, built by the dragons of Natlantea according to the wiki, are fed by the same energy and have modules for edict, stasis, transmission and annihilation.

## entry: Archaeolune Storm
@keys: Archaeolune Storm, Archaeolune Storms, lunar sea
@version: 6.7
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
An Archaeolune Storm is a surge of highly concentrated ancient moon energy that spreads when the lunar sea at the moon's core is disturbed. Columbina says it harms all living beings other than kuuhenki. Eggie notes that an occlusion field can hold such storms back for a while, and that the facility beneath Ungien's Circle draws its power from the lunar sea.
