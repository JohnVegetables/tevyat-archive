## summary
### LITE
Karma, or karmic debt, is the corruptive residue left by the wills and powers of ancient gods slain in the Archon War. It seeps into land, monsters and people, and can drive humans mad. The yakshas were called by Rex Lapis to destroy it, and absorbed so much of it that most died or went insane. As of 7.1 the sole surviving yaksha, Xiao, still bears it.
### STANDARD +
The term is used mainly in Liyue. Adepti can make a medicine that temporarily eases karma, but like most adeptal medicines it is lethal to mortals.

## in_universe
### LITE
During the Archon War, Morax subdued many gods. Their hatred and powers lingered in the world as half-awake remnants that corrupted land, spawned monsters and caused pestilence, miasma and mutations. Contact with karma can make humans insane and makes monsters stronger.
### STANDARD +
Morax summoned the yakshas, especially fierce illuminated beasts, to fight these remnants. As Zhongli narrates in the cinematic of Glimmers of the Vigilant Yaksha, they swore to restore order through slaughter, but each kill left a mark: the debt wore into their souls, and they went mad with fear, turned on each other or were lost to the darkness. Of the five foremost yakshas, three died and Bosacius vanished, leaving Xiao as the only one.
### LARGE +
Xiao's profile gives the most personal account. When he cut down the manifestations of slain gods' revenge, he fractured their hatred, and the fragments eventually tainted his own soul. The only way to remove such hatred is to accept the karmic debt it brings, and the debt he has accumulated is enough to consume flesh, bone and soul. His story says he feels no hatred himself: after more than two thousand years no single debt is more than a fleeting memory. Because his surroundings are tainted too, he avoids people, since human souls are less robust than adepti ones.
### ULTRA +
The gods' hatred does not stay quiet. It sometimes erupts with abnormal force in its half-dreaming state, and the worst eruption of all is at the Lantern Rite, so Xiao fights day and night then, performing the Nuo Dance of Evil Conquering, and has come to dislike the festival. Many human festivals recall days when adepti defeated man-eating monsters, and later generations turned the exorcism rites into celebrations. A chapter called "Ode to Windborne Wraith" in a Sumeru scholar's folklore book records that the yakshas were bound by their own karma to suffer horrors, and lists comforts such as food offerings and holy chants.

## effects_and_resistance
### STANDARD
Karma corrupts monsters and strengthens them, and can send humans insane. Yelan's clan story adds that her ancestor found temporary relief from his insanity only near the Wangshu Inn. Vision holders have heightened resistance, as do the Traveler and illuminated beasts; in the adepti's view, a Vision is a valid form of illumination.
### LARGE +
The hazard persists in places: the Karma-Heavy Cavern is a quest domain in The Purging of Evil, named for the heavy karma it holds. Karma also wore at the yakshas' minds; Bosacius saw several comrades kill each other and went mad himself, forgetting his own name and fighting beside the Millelith at the Chasm in the cataclysm as the "Nameless Yaksha."

## relief_and_remedies
### STANDARD
Remedies are partial. Adepti alchemy yields Remedium Tertiorum, which Zhongli once asked a traveler to pass to Xiao, but it can be lethal to mortals. Venti once relieved Xiao's pain by playing the flute through the night, which Xiao's profile describes as quieting his wild senses; the Liyue legend of the sound of a flute among the reeds near Dihua Marsh is tied to this. The Qixing built Wangshu Inn at Dihua Marsh to give Xiao a place to rest, and he eats Almond Tofu there.
### LARGE +
A fragment of Yelan's family tradition says Xiao's presence near the inn eased her ancestor's madness, and her voice-over says she would fight beside him with her focus on the mission, not on his status. The Nuo Dance, which purges demons and divine karma, costs its user great suffering in return for the strength to subdue a god's might, according to the wiki.

## status
@version: 7.1
### STANDARD
As of 7.1, Xiao remains the last yaksha and still carries the karmic debt. No release up to the cutoff resolves or removes karma, and no canon source says it has been cured.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not literal lore): karma appears in Xiao's character materials and quests such as The Purging of Evil, the Karma-Heavy Cavern domain, and the idle quotes of Xiao as a companion, one of which is simply "Karma..." There is no general karma mechanic in the open world.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay interpretation (this project's recommendation, not canon text): treat karma as a heavy, quiet burden rather than a spell. Xiao keeps people away, speaks little of it and does not ask for pity; the yaksha's own line in the profile says yakshas need no sympathy or tears. Show it through weariness, sudden pain, cold sweat or a distant, haunted look, not melodrama.
### LARGE +
Do not make everyone in Liyue aware of karma or the yakshas; the story says few know Xiao's role. Do not have ordinary Visions "cure" it. The only canon relief is Remedium Tertiorum and a flute song.
### ULTRA +
Avoid suggesting Xiao hates humans; canon says he is gentle and curious about people but avoids them for their sake. Keep his guilt tied to the yakshas' oath and his own deeds. Do not extend karma into Natlan, Nod-Krai or Snezhnaya scenes unless a scene is explicitly Liyue-related.

## entry: Yakshas and karma
@keys: yaksha, yakshas, Five Yakshas, Vigilant Yaksha
@keys2: karma, karmic debt
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
The yakshas were fierce illuminated beasts summoned by Morax to destroy the lingering hatred of defeated gods. Five stood foremost: Bosacius the eldest and leader, Indarias, Bonanus, Menogias and Alatus (Xiao). Accumulated karma destroyed them. Bonanus, Indarias and Menogias died, Bosacius went mad and vanished, and Alatus alone remains.

## entry: Remedium Tertiorum
@keys: Remedium Tertiorum
@spoiler: none
### STANDARD
An adeptal medicine that Zhongli gave to the Traveler to pass to Xiao. It can temporarily ease karma, but nothing in the world can withstand its power, and like other adeptal medicines it is lethal to mortals, so he warned that Paimon must not eat them.

## entry: Wangshu Inn
@keys: Wangshu Inn
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
An inn at Dihua Marsh built at least five hundred years ago by the Liyue Qixing to give Xiao somewhere to stay and rest while he does his duty. It is run by Qixing agents Verr Goldet and Huai'an.
