## summary
### LITE
Exorcists, also called thaumaturges, are Liyue people who have sworn an oath to cleanse the land of ghosts, spirits and demons. They are skilled in combat, magic and martial arts, and most come from families that keep exorcism as the family trade.
### STANDARD +
Canon names few groups: the Tianheng Thaumaturges (Chongyun's clan), the family of Yelan and the Lan Clan. This entry covers the tradition; the Exorcists culture entry covers the people.

## in_universe
### LITE
Chongyun's clan motto, as recorded in his and Shenhe's voice-overs, is to keep the heart pure and evil erased. The Tianheng clan keeps handed-down books recording the spirits it has defeated.
### STANDARD +
Little is known of origins. The wiki says Liyue thaumaturgy took its modern form around 500 years ago after the Cataclysm, when a revolution in weapon and catalyst making shifted favour toward supernatural arts over traditional martial arts, though thaumaturges still practise both. Exorcist arts seem close to adeptal arts: Chongyun counts Shenhe, who trained under the adepti, as a master exorcist.
### LARGE +
- Tianheng Thaumaturges: Chongyun's clan, with Shenhe from a branch family. Chongyun carries a spirit blade of ice; his abundance of yang energy means he has never actually met a spirit. Shenhe's mother used a dagger in exorcism.
- Yelan's clan: her ancestors held the Fantastic Compass, a device that seals away evil creatures, originally owned by the adeptus Sea Gazer and given to a thaumaturge friend. The clan's catalysts, usable only by its members, are said to be built on it. Boyang and Rongzhao, ancestors of Yelan, were exorcists at the battle at the Chasm 500 years ago with the Yaksha Bosacius.
- Lan Clan: descendants of the brothers of Lan Junming, the Wind Reader of the Eight Adepts, notable for Qimen Arts that may serve duty or craft.
### ULTRA +
Fetor is a different matter. In Springtime Charms (5.3, limited event) Chongyun reads a dying elder's chill as fetor, not an evil spirit, and the Wangsheng Funeral Parlor, not exorcists, burns it with the Art of Homa. Lan Junming also taught a Soul-Binding Art, later outlawed for violating the natural order.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay interpretation (this project's recommendation, not canon text): exorcists favour talismans, wards, mantras and dedicated discipline over spectacle. A clan trade means family loyalty and inherited books and relics. They are not equal to adepti or Archons.
### LARGE +
Do not make every Liyue spirit a "demon," and do not confuse exorcism with the Wangsheng Funeral Parlor's rites. Do not state how Vision-less exorcists perform magic.

## entry: Fantastic Compass
@keys: Fantastic Compass
@keys2: Yelan, Sea Gazer, exorcist
### LARGE
A device that can seal away evil creatures, said to have been invented by the adeptus Sea Gazer and gifted to a thaumaturge friend. It later reached Yelan's ancestors, who used it at the Chasm to seal in Abyssal monsters; her clan built catalysts on it.
