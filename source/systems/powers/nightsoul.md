## summary
### LITE
Nightsoul's Blessing is the power state of Natlan's warriors. Natlan's dead enter the Night Kingdom, a realm of souls beneath the land; the souls there are called Nightsouls, and Natlanese fighters borrow their strength to run, climb, swim, leap and fight beyond what they could alone. The Wayob, spirits venerated by the six tribes, live in that realm, which the Lord of the Night made. It is a Natlan power tied to the land's own rules, not an element.
### STANDARD +
In play, the state is entered mostly through a character's Elemental Skill, runs on a pool of Nightsoul points, and is stronger inside Natlan. It is separate from the Vision system, though many Natlanese fighters use both.

## in_universe
### LITE
In story terms, the Night Kingdom is "a liminal space between the body and mind, life and death," deep underground. Natlanese people go there when they die and slowly lose themselves into a sea of souls. Living warriors can draw on those Nightsouls to enhance their abilities, which is Nightsoul's Blessing.
### STANDARD +
The Night Kingdom effectively works as Natlan's Ley Lines. Natlan lacks stable, deep Ley Lines, and the original ones were badly damaged in the War of Funerary Flame. The Lord of the Night, guided by Ronova, rebuilt a similar network from the fragments; the first Pyro Archon, Xbalanque, strengthened it later. Memories gained in Natlan are recorded there. Experiences outside Natlan are not, which is why Xilonen needed the Lord of the Night's help to forge an Ancient Name for the Traveler.
### LARGE +
Item text in the Obsidian Codex artifact set tells how the realm's contact with the living began: the human Chaac, the last of his companions, climbed a mountain on his final night, lit the first flame and called out; the god of the night realm answered, and the first Wayob was born in that moment. Natlan's tribes later each had their own Wayob, to whom they pray for protection and whose will they read in the dreams of their chiefs.
### ULTRA +
The Abyss's long effects can leave Natlanese people without the Wayob's protection prone to memory loss, stress and bad luck, which is why most stay inside Natlan; leaving needs the approval of the Wayob through the tribal chief. The Wayob cannot extend their protection abroad without spending power reserved for fighting the Abyss.

## wayob_and_night_kingdom
### STANDARD
The Wayob are described by the Dreamseeker Priest as non-corporeal "will without form or thought." They live in the Night Kingdom and can see the past and future. Each tribe has its own Wayob, and the Wayob choose who receives an Ancient Name. They are not physically in Natlan, but they can appear through obsidian totems and Nightsouls as Wayob Manifestations. Natlan's people do not know why these appear; the common belief is that they are sent to solve some problem.
### LARGE +
The Night Kingdom was created by Yohualtecuhtin, the Lord of the Night, who was once an angel. After the cataclysm 500 years ago, Abyssal forces overran it. For centuries the Wayob fought them without rest, and that war wore down their strength and their link to the people. By the time of Natlan's Archon Quests, Mavuika estimated the realm would be destroyed in under a year.
### ULTRA +
The Weaver of Secrets, a Dragonlord, merged her consciousness into the Night Kingdom, making a "dream labyrinth" where the souls of dead dragons and humans can rest, and keeps a pact of protection with the Masters of the Night-Wind (per weapon item text).

## ancient_names_and_death
### STANDARD
Ancient Names are obsidian-stone titles carried by chosen members of the tribes. Their deeds become part of the Name's legacy. Each is granted by a tribe's Wayob to someone who has lived out its spirit, or is forged on commission of the Pyro Archon by a Name Engraver at a cost to the smith's lifespan. A bearer who fell in the Night Warden Wars can be returned by the Ode of Resurrection if their group won against the Abyss. If no one returned victorious, the Name is lost for good. A bearer who dies in some other way is reborn in Natlan eventually, in another form.
### LARGE +
Ronova, Ruler of Death, is the source of the Ode's power: she let Xbalanque borrow it. The Night Kingdom records Natlan's memories, which is why only Natlan's deeds could serve as the basis for a Name until the Lord of the Night made the Pilgrim's Chronicle for the Traveler.

## last_changes
@spoiler: major
### ULTRA
After the Abyss was repelled from Natlan, the Fatui Harbinger Capitano exchanged his own death for Mavuika's, merging his life force with the Lord of the Night to rewrite the Ley Lines' rules. Afterwards, people from outside Natlan are accepted into the Night Kingdom, and the Lord of the Night returned to maintain it. The Traveler's Sibling later met with her and obtained the Ley Lines for New Khaenri'ah, stored in an Atlas.

## known_uses
### LITE
Entering the state boosts mobility and fighting power; characters often move in ways ordinary people cannot. Kachina, for instance, rides Turbo Twirly while she has points left, and the state ends when they run out.
### STANDARD +
Nightsoul Transmission lets a Natlan character swiftly run, climb, swim or jump after a swap. Nightsoul Burst is a periodic surge triggered by elemental damage when a Natlanese character is in the party. Nightsoul points are gained or spent by each character in their own way; when they run out or when certain abilities are used, Phlogiston is drawn instead.
### LARGE +
- Nightsoul-aligned damage is better at breaking Void Wards (three times as effective) and the shields summoned by Wayob Manifestations.
- Nightsoul Totems, obsidian totems around Natlan, send the player into the Night Kingdom to attempt combat trials.
- Characters tied to Natlan with the state include the Pyro Archon Mavuika and fighters such as Kinich, Mualani, Xilonen, Chasca, Ororon, Citlali, Kachina, Iansan, Varesa and Ifa. How each uses it differs by character.

## limits
### LITE
It is a Natlan power: it works best in Natlan and relies on the Night Kingdom's support.
### STANDARD +
The state ends when a character's Nightsoul points are exhausted. The Night Kingdom has no record of deeds outside Natlan, and in canon the Wayob protect their people poorly abroad.
### LARGE +
Nightsoul-aligned damage has no special effect in ordinary fights, only against Void Wards and Wayob Manifestation shields. Nightsoul Burst does nothing by itself; only certain talents and artifacts react to it.

## interactions
### LITE
Phlogiston: Natlan's primordial energy, a form of Pyro, powers many Natlan devices and Saurian abilities, and is drawn on when Nightsoul points are gone.
### STANDARD +
Visions and the Elements: Nightsoul's Blessing is not an element, so it rides on whatever element a character wields. Burning is a fast way to wear down a Void Ward without the state.
### LARGE +
The Abyss is the common enemy of the Wayob and the Night Kingdom. Per Beneath the Secret Source, the Heavenly Principles modified elemental energy on a Phlogiston basis to counter the Abyss.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not literal lore): the state is entered mainly through Elemental Skills, enhances movement and combat, and is boosted in Natlan. Nightsoul Burst triggers on elemental damage with an interval of 18 seconds for one Natlanese character in the party, 12 for two and 9 for three or more, marked by a white screen outline. The mechanics arrived with Natlan in Version 5.0.
### LARGE +
Night Realm's Gift passive talents give each Natlan character their Transmission movement. An achievement, Imperishable Night, asks the player to hold the state for 18 seconds.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay interpretation (this project's recommendation, not canon text): show it as quiet weight: ember-lit patterns of soul-light, a sense of ancestors close by, a body suddenly lighter. Natlanese characters treat it as ordinary inheritance and take it with humour or pride. Keep respect for the dead.
### LARGE +
Do not let an outsider simply use it, and do not give a non-Natlanese character a clear grasp of the mechanism. Let characters mention the Wayob as guardians who fight the Abyss. Do not turn the Night Kingdom into a generic afterlife of punishment.
### ULTRA +
Avoid claiming Nightsoul's Blessing appears in Nod-Krai or Snezhnaya; as of 7.1 canon has not shown it there.

## entry: Night Kingdom
@keys: Night Kingdom, Lord of the Night, Nightsoul, Nightsouls
### STANDARD
The Night Kingdom is the realm of the Wayob and the souls of Natlan's dead, made by Yohualtecuhtin, the Lord of the Night, from fragments of the old Ley Lines, and it serves as Natlan's Ley Lines. Natlanese souls lose themselves there into a sea of souls. Souls of warriors who die in Natlan's ritual wars can be recalled by the Ode of Resurrection if their group wins.
### LARGE +
The Abyss invaded it after the cataclysm and was driven out in the Archon Quest; Gosoythoth fled to its deepest parts and was destroyed there by the Traveler and Mavuika.

## entry: Wayob
@keys: Wayob, Wayob Manifestation, Wayob Manifestations
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
The Wayob are the spirits of the six tribes, believed to embody the will of tribal ancestors. They guard their people and choose who bears an Ancient Name. They can appear in Natlan as obsidian-tied Wayob Manifestations, which are also elite enemies in play.

## entry: Ancient Name
@keys: Ancient Name, Ancient Names, tocaitl
### STANDARD
An Ancient Name is a title and a stone representing the deeds of all its past bearers, chosen by a Wayob or forged on the Pyro Archon's commission. If a bearer dies in the Night Warden Wars and nobody in the group returns victorious, the Name is lost.

## entry: Nightsoul Burst
@keys: Nightsoul Burst, Nightsoul Bursts
### STANDARD
A periodic surge that triggers when a party containing a Natlanese character deals elemental damage. On its own it does nothing; some talents and artifacts add effects.
