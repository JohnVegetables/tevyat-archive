## summary
### LITE
Azosite is an ancient form of energy used by the Khaenri'ahns. It was made by extracting pure elemental energy from ley lines and smelting it into solid Energy Blocks, and it powered everything from ordinary ruin machines to large weapons-testing facilities. Over time the Khaenri'ahns found more effective sources, such as Abyssal energy, which gradually replaced it.
### STANDARD +
Azosite is described as convenient, stable and safe, but, in the view of a Fontainian researcher who studied it, lacking "will." Players first meet it in the Khaenri'ahn ruins beneath Sumeru's desert (Version 3.6).

## in_universe
### LITE
The Khaenri'ahns refined elemental energy out of the ley lines and processed it into Energy Blocks. Azosite is not a separate force: notes found in the ruins say it is not fundamentally different from elemental energy and cannot break the rules that elemental energy must follow.
### STANDARD +
Because it is elemental energy, Azosite shares its properties, including the ability to reverse Abyssal influence. The same notes place the Abyss higher in the order of rules, since it can distort and pollute the elements of the gods. Energy Blocks fuel the backup power systems of Ruin Golems, and a quest item, the Mysterious Energy Block, is described in-game as a refined Azosite block formed by condensing pure elemental energy, usable for large-scale power systems.
### LARGE +
One Khaenri'ahn engineering log describes smelting runs pushing the Azosite concentration of a batch past 60%, and forming the resulting energy alloys into a matrix, which was thought enough to meet the energy demands of the large "Mechanical Wardens" (Ruin Golems). The same log admits it compared poorly with a newly found "perpetual" source. The author also frames the work as relying on human ingenuity rather than "dogma from beyond the heavens."
### ULTRA +
Azosite, as these notes present it, is a processing method rather than a material gathered from the ground: the ley lines hold elemental energy, and smelting extracts and concentrates it. Which elements, in what proportions, or how Energy Blocks differ by element is not stated in canon.

## history
@version: 3.6
### STANDARD
The Khaenri'ahns used Azosite to power machines from common Ruin Guards up to sites such as Madinat al-Nuhas. Research from the Sumeru Akademiya suggests the technology may predate them: Jazari reports that Azosite power systems were probably in widespread use long before the first ruin machine was built in ancient Khaenri'ah, so the Khaenri'ahns need not have invented it.
### LARGE +
Later Khaenri'ahn engineers found more effective power sources, including Abyssal energy, and these gradually replaced Azosite systems. The change shows in the machines: early ruin machines carry Azosite backup power, later ones do not. Jazari reads this as a transition period in which the Khaenri'ahns had not yet mastered Abyssal energy and kept Azosite as a safety net. Worn notes in the ruins call the factory Hangeh Afrasiyab the last workshop in the kingdom with a complete Azosite-powered system, after the smelting furnace of the Royal Machine Workshop "Gnade" was officially abandoned. They say its efficiency problems were hard to solve and that even small mass-produced machines such as Field Tillers found a power core more convenient. The author predicted the workshop would soon close too, and lamented that people are shortsighted about technology.
### ULTRA +
Another ruin note, an unfinished letter to a marshal of the kingdom, asks that some machinery built to the "old standard" be kept under his ministry's care so that it might spark future generations to explore technology by their own will. Whether the request was granted is not recorded.

## usage_sites
### STANDARD
Hangeh Afrasiyab, an abandoned underground factory in Gavireh Lajavard (Sumeru), was where Azosite was processed; a sealed gate to Khaenri'ah lies in its depths. In the quest "As the Khvarena's Light Shows," the Traveler and companions retrieved a Great Song of Khvarena there.
### LARGE +
Madinat al-Nuhas is named as a large weapons-testing facility powered by Azosite. The Khaenri'ahn ruins beneath Gavireh Lajavard and the Realm of Farakhkert hold the notes that explain the process, and the Mysterious Energy Block appears in the quest "As the Khvarena's Light Shows: Nirodha."

## rene_observations
@version: 3.6
### LARGE
Around the time of the cataclysm, Rene, a researcher from Fontaine, visited Hangeh Afrasiyab and studied Azosite. He judged it convenient, stable and safe, yet lacking "will" because it is pure elemental force, comparing the gap to that between a Hydro Slime and an Oceanid. He thought the Khaenri'ahns' use of the Abyss too primitive, steering their "evolution" in an uncontrollable direction. He also wondered whether Khvarena, which can reverse Abyssal influence, might be smelted by the Azosite method; he thought a true perpetual energy source of the kind the Khaenri'ahns idealised might result, and that learning the origin of Amrita, which he thought might be influencing Khvarena, could point to a way to raise the mortal to "supreme purity." These are speculations, not demonstrated results.

## limits
### STANDARD
Azosite is pure elemental energy and so obeys the rules of elemental energy; it has no will of its own and sits beneath Abyssal power in the order of rules. Canon shows it only as Khaenri'ahn-era infrastructure and notes, not as a power anyone wields today.
### LARGE +
Azosite is not a substitute for a Vision, a Gnosis or an Archon's Authority, and canon never shows it granting abilities to a person. Do not treat it as a separate sixth kind of element.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay interpretation (this project's recommendation, not canon text): treat Azosite as old industrial infrastructure. Scenes can feature sealed smelting furnaces, stacked Energy Blocks, dim Ruin Golems on backup power, and worn engineers' notes. Scholars in Sumeru can plausibly discuss it as a curiosity from the Khaenri'ahn ruins.
### LARGE +
A character who has read Rene's or Jazari's findings can cite them, with the caveats above. Ordinary people do not know the term; it is mostly known to scholars of Khaenri'ah and Akademiya researchers.

## entry: Energy Block
@keys: Energy Block, Energy Blocks, Mysterious Energy Block, Lost Energy Block
### STANDARD
Energy Blocks are solid, refined Azosite used to fuel the backup power systems of Ruin Golems. The Mysterious Energy Block is a quest item from "As the Khvarena's Light Shows: Nirodha," described as a block of refined Azosite formed by condensing pure elemental energy for large-scale power systems. The wiki also lists a Lost Energy Block as another form of Azosite.

## entry: Hangeh Afrasiyab
@keys: Hangeh Afrasiyab
### STANDARD
An abandoned underground factory in Gavireh Lajavard where the Khaenri'ahns processed Azosite, described in notes as the last workshop in the kingdom with an entirely Azosite-powered system. A sealed gate to Khaenri'ah lies within, and the Traveler recovered a Great Song of Khvarena there in the quest "As the Khvarena's Light Shows."
