## summary
### LITE
The Art of Khemia is the alchemy of Khaenri'ah. Because Khaenri'ah was an underground nation with little natural fauna, its alchemy centred on creating life, and it is quite different from the alchemy practised in the Seven Nations. Its master was "Gold" Rhinedottir, who made the dragon Durin, the synthetic human Albedo and many monsters. The Art played a major role in the fall of Khaenri'ah in the Cataclysm 500 years ago.
### STANDARD +
Albedo, Rhinedottir's creation and student, is the main living practitioner shown. Venti and Dainsleif, who were present at Khaenri'ah's fall, are uneasy about Albedo's use of it.
### LARGE +
The name comes from the Greek root for "black earth", reflected in the Chinese and Japanese names (Art of Black Earth). Soil and chalk are the central images in Rhinedottir's lessons to Albedo.

## in_universe
### LITE
Khemia sits at the far end of alchemy: those who combine the pinnacle of alchemical skill with vast learning can create synthetic life, which Albedo calls the "apex achievement". Canon names only Rhinedottir as having reached that level. Its inner workings and history are not explained in canon.
### STANDARD +
Rhinedottir's demonstration to the young Albedo (Character Story 3) ties life to earth: the universe is the dark essence of the starry sky, the earth is the accumulated memories of time and lives, and the "chalk" is the one being taught. Albedo learned of the Art from his master's notes and was taught that after "soil" comes "chalk", the spotless soil used to make primordial man. His star-shaped neck mark is left where life force was sealed into his vessel; he compares it to a birthmark and to a pontil mark on blown glass.
### LARGE +
Rhinedottir sorted creations into pedigrees that appear to name the "soil" they came from: Alfisol for the riftwolves, Humus for Durin, and Cretaceus (chalk) for Albedo, the last said to be of highest quality. Her creations appear to draw on the power of the Ruler of Life among the Four Shades (now Naberius's function, which Rhinedottir took over), giving them a degree of immortality; some, like the Bakunawa, are further strengthened with Abyssal power.
### ULTRA +
Albedo uses the Art in Mondstadt in ways he describes in plain terms, such as building a human body for Durin from the flesh of the immortal Bakunawa, blood taken from Durin's Core, and a portion of the embryo material his mother left behind. Alice remarks that little of that raw material remains. Khemia is distinct from ordinary alchemy as a game system (crafting, conversion), which the Alchemy entry covers.

## history
@version: 1.2
### STANDARD
The Art of Khemia grew in Khaenri'ah and was the alchemy that sustained the nation. Rhinedottir, an alchemist who served the Eclipse Dynasty in its closing years, became one of the Five Sinners of Khaenri'ah. Tempted by the Abyss, she took a share of the Abyssal power Irmin had given the Traveler's Sibling. Her monster armies then invaded Khaenri'ah and the seven nations in the Cataclysm.
### LARGE +
Not all of her creations were malicious. Durin and Elynas show an almost childlike delight in the new world and, in their memories, were unaware of or distressed by the harm they caused. Durin claims Rhinedottir sent him from Khaenri'ah to "display his beauty", with other motives left ambiguous, and the riftwolves invaded of their own will. After the Cataclysm she made her breakthrough in the Primordial Human Project and produced Albedo, then left him a last assignment (to find the truth and meaning of the world) after devouring the Heart of Naberius.
### ULTRA +
Earlier iterations of the project produced failures. Subject Two was an abandoned experiment, apparently left in Durin's stomach, who later escaped on Dragonspine and taught himself from Albedo's stolen research materials. His work with Durin's blood mutated a Whopperflower into Fellflower, which Albedo says can still count as Rhinedottir's creation. The Bakunawa slipped her control and swallowed part of her body, though she survived. Rhinedottir later takes part in recent Snezhnaya events (intervening over the Seals of Life and meeting the Tsaritsa), which show she and her Art are still active as of 7.1.

## known_creations
### LITE
Durin (dragon), Albedo (synthetic human), the riftwolves, Elynas, the Bakunawa, Sebas and the False Horned Steed.
### STANDARD +
Rhinedottir called only two of her works "miraculous beings": Durin and Albedo. Durin terrorised Mondstadt's skies until Dvalin killed him over Dragonspine; Elynas polluted Fontaine until its armada wounded him fatally; the Bakunawa devoured the Tenochtzitoc tribe in Natlan before Tenoch and Sanhaj's sacrifice broke it apart.
### LARGE +
- Unintended: the Golden Wolflord and its Abyss-corroded form (killed by the Traveler and Paimon in Revelations from the Past).
- Related lifeforms, not made directly by Rhinedottir: Breacher Primus (indirect creations of Elynas), melusines, Fellflower (made by Subject Two).
- The Durin who lives in Mondstadt today was created by the witch Andersdotter in the storybook world of Simulanka, modelled on the original; Albedo gave him a synthetic human body via the Art.

## practitioners
### STANDARD
Main practitioner: Rhinedottir (Gold). Her creation and student Albedo (Cretaceus) is the active practitioner. Sucrose (bio-alchemy) and Timaeus are Albedo's assistants and students but are not described as practising Khemia itself. Jakob Baker of the Narzissenkreuz Ordo is a possible practitioner: he researched alchemy extensively, with special interest in Khaenri'ahn Khemia and Rhinedottir's work.
### LARGE +
Subject Two taught himself from stolen notes and was killed by Albedo after The Shadows Deepen; the wiki records that he had been resurrected before Paralogism and that his current fate is unknown.

## limits_and_cautions
### STANDARD
Do not treat Khemia as ordinary Mondstadt alchemy or as a Vision power. It is not an element. It is slow, material and dangerous: Venti calls its power ancient and unmistakable and says he fears what would happen if someone lost control of it in the city.
### LARGE +
Canon does not give a method, so do not invent formulae or "recipes" beyond soil, chalk, embryo material, arrays and the stages above. Albedo's Khemia is not fully trusted by those who lived through Khaenri'ah's fall: Dainsleif and Venti both voice concern.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay interpretation (this project's recommendation, not canon text): show Khemia as quiet, exact craft, with chalk-drawn or glowing arrays, specimen notes, and a clinical calm. Its practitioners (Albedo especially) speak of creation as study, not conquest. Avoid portraying it as flashy combat magic.
### LARGE +
Keep the Cataclysm's shadow: anyone who knows Khaenri'ah's history may react to the word "Khemia" with unease. Ordinary Mondstadters know Albedo as the Chief Alchemist and Kreideprinz, not the lineage of his art.
### ULTRA +
Do not make Albedo the Art's inventor or Rhinedottir a villain without nuance: Albedo says she did not always have a "penchant for madness", and her creations vary in intent.

## entry: Rhinedottir (Gold)
@keys: Rhinedottir, Gold, Ruler of Life
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Rhinedottir, called Gold, is a Khaenri'ahn alchemist, one of the Five Sinners of Khaenri'ah and a Hexenzirkel mage ("R"). She is the foremost known master of the Art of Khemia. After the Cataclysm she created Albedo and, after devouring the Heart of Naberius, took over Naberius's function as the Ruler of Life.
### LARGE +
Her own entity entry carries her full biography. Skirk knows her name only through her master Surtalogi, another of the Five Sinners.

## entry: Primordial Human Project
@keys: Primordial Human Project, Subject Two, Cretaceus
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Rhinedottir's long project to create a human being through alchemy. Subject Two was an earlier failed result; Albedo, classed as Cretaceus, was the success. Albedo says the apex of alchemy is the creation of human life.
### LARGE +
The project's discarded product, Subject Two, later wished to live among humans as Albedo does and was killed by Albedo; see The Shadows Deepen and The Hunger of Many.

## entry: Durin's synthetic body
@keys: Durin human body, synthetic body, Durin's Core
@keys2: Albedo, alchemy
### LARGE
Albedo made a human form for Durin using the Art of Khemia: Bakunawa flesh from Mare Jivari, blood extracted from Durin's Core, and part of the embryo material left behind by Rhinedottir. Durin then used the body to live in Mondstadt. This Durin was made by Andersdotter in Simulanka and is distinct from the original Durin killed by Dvalin.

## entry: Narzissenkreuz Ordo and Khemia
@keys: Narzissenkreuz Ordo, Jakob Baker, Magnum Opus
@spoiler: minor
### LARGE
The Ordo's leader Jakob Baker left notes on Khaenri'ahn Khemia and the importance of studying Rhinedottir's work. Notes in the Tower of Ipsissimus criticise the Eclipse Dynasty's alchemists for moving the final stage of the Magnum Opus from red to yellow, saying yellow is bait and red is the true goal. Albedo's voice-over puts yellow after red. Treat the dispute as open.
