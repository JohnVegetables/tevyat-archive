## summary
### LITE
Arkhium is a yellow, green and blue crystalline ore that occurs in parts of Fontaine and works as an extremely potent energy source. The Fontaine Research Institute tried to use it to counter gravity so that a "city in the sky" could ride out the prophesied flood. A mechanical malfunction overloaded the Arkhium Kinetic Core, and the explosion destroyed much of the old Institute grounds.
### STANDARD +
Arkhium is distinct from Arkhe, the Pneuma and Ousia energy pair, though the Institute's researchers compared the two. Its energy potential far surpasses the controlled Pneuma-Ousia Annihilation reactions that power Clockwork Meka.

## properties
### LITE
Arkhium separates matter, or certain things outside of matter, according to archive text; the separation can apparently be reversed, though there has been little interest in doing so. It is either inert, stable and giving off no energy, or activated, unstable and emitting powerful pulses.
### STANDARD +
Inert Arkhium can be activated in a catalytic environment, and activated Arkhium can be made inert with a specialised inhibitor. Activated crystals can set off a resonance that activates other crystals in range, risking an explosive chain reaction. After enough activation, a crystal's energy-generating potential runs out and it becomes permanently inert.
### LARGE +
Residual Arkhium energy in the environment does not dissipate quickly. Refined ore may give off heat, light or a buzzing sound. Under particular conditions raw ore can fuse naturally. Item text on Arkhium Stock describes it as the raw form that can come together naturally, and the Arkhium Lumenite made from it as emitting an unusual energy and light.
### ULTRA +
Direct exposure is dangerous. Reported symptoms include nausea, vomiting, subcutaneous bleeding and even coma, and most people need medication or other precautions. The Traveler and Paimon are notably resistant. A memo in the abandoned Meropide production zone mentions Arkhium powder having a "rich distinctive flavor", but the consequences of consuming it are unclear.

## fontaine_research_institute
@version: 4.1
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
The Fontaine Research Institute became interested in Arkhium because its energy potential far exceeded Pneuma-Ousia Annihilation. The goal was to counteract gravity and build a city in the sky so that Fontaine's people could survive the prophesied flood. Another proposed use was a ship lift at Romaritime Harbor. The Arkhium Research Project was led by Edwin Eastinghouse, a senior researcher specialising in Arkhium and gravimeters, with Nacker and Raimondo as collaborators.
### LARGE +
Edwin built the Experimental Field Generator, a huge device that creates a Gravity Reduction Field. He and Raimondo developed the Arkhium Kinetic Core, an energy source powered by a large chunk of ore, plus a suppression device to control it. Edwin personally trained Nacker to operate it. At some point a mechanical malfunction overloaded the core and the resulting explosion destroyed much of the Institute grounds. It also created the Allogravity-Condensed Water Bodies, large cubes of water suspended in the sky around the Central Laboratory Ruins.
### ULTRA +
Afterwards nearly all Arkhium crystals nearby shattered into fragments. Because fragments can still be activated by resonance, the Institute used the Experimental Field Generator to gather and restrain them, and placed Survey Anchors to monitor residual radiation. Arkhium mining in the Geode Mine Shaft beneath the Fortress of Meropide was terminated. The Institute relocated to a new site near the old ruins.

## chronicles
@version: 4.1
@spoiler: minor
### LARGE
In the world quest series Fontaine Research Institute Chronicles, the Traveler helps the Institute. Nacker caused a smaller explosion, which Raimondo suspected was a bid to sell the Arkhium core for a fortune, and his tampering reactivated the prototype generator. The Traveler shut it down and installed inhibitors to stabilise the traces of Arkhium in the ruins. The Traveler was named Honorary Senior Researcher, with Paimon as their assistant.
### ULTRA +
The Traveler later tracked Nacker to a new benefactor named Concini, and discovered that Edwin was also alive, both men still pursuing Arkhium research. Their plans were foiled, and both were returned to the Institute, fired and sent to the Fortress of Meropide.

## locations
### STANDARD
Arkhium occurs naturally in parts of Fontaine, such as the Geode Mine Shaft beneath the Fortress of Meropide, which holds an inert vein. The ruined Central Laboratory Ruins, with their floating water cubes, mark where the Institute's core exploded.
### LARGE +
The Fortress of Meropide's resources were used to build The Wingalet, a giant flying ship. Edwin speculates that it is powered by Arkhium, and the ship was designed by one of his former assistants. This is not confirmed.

## quest_items
@version: 4.1
### STANDARD
A world-quest chain has the Traveler gather seven Mysterious Ore pieces across Fontaine, which combine into Arkhium Stock. Forged at Bravais' Press Works, the stock becomes Arkhium Lumenite, which powers a door mechanism for the hidden objective "Arkhium Forge".

## limits
### LITE
Arkhium is not a Vision element, and not the same thing as Arkhe.
### STANDARD +
Canon does not explain its origin or why it is concentrated at particular sites. It is dangerous without protection, and a depleted crystal is permanently dead.
### LARGE +
Do not claim that Arkhium powers all of Fontaine's machines; Clockwork Meka run on controlled Pneuma-Ousia Annihilation, not Arkhium. Do not treat the Wingalet's Arkhium power source as established.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay interpretation (this project's recommendation, not canon text): present Arkhium as an unstable laboratory hazard, with a dull buzz, warm glow and a sense of restrained danger, and as something researchers handle with detectors, inhibitors and nerves rather than bravado. Researchers might talk about "resonance", "inhibitors" and "radiation levels".
### LARGE +
Most Fontainians know of the explosion and the floating water cubes, not the details. Edwin Eastinghouse's attitude to risk is part of the canon account. An ordinary NPC should not give a casual speech on how to activate or weaponise Arkhium.

## entry: Arkhium Kinetic Core
@keys: Arkhium Kinetic Core, Arkhium core
### STANDARD
The Arkhium Kinetic Core was a concentrated energy source built around a large chunk of Arkhium ore, developed by Edwin Eastinghouse and Raimondo with a suppression device to control it. It fed the Experimental Field Generator. Its overload caused the explosion that destroyed much of the Fontaine Research Institute.

## entry: Experimental Field Generator
@keys: Experimental Field Generator, Prototype Cal. Breguet, Tourbillon Device
### STANDARD
The Experimental Field Generator is a huge device created by Edwin Eastinghouse that produces a Gravity Reduction Field. The wiki lists the machine as Prototype Cal. Breguet, a Clockwork Meka. In combat the field greatly raises jump height, and three Pneuma-aligned hits end it.
### LARGE +
After the explosion the Institute reused it to gather and restrain shattered Arkhium. During the Chronicles quests Nacker's tampering reactivated it, and the Traveler shut it down and installed inhibitors.

## entry: Allogravity-Condensed Water Bodies
@keys: Allogravity-Condensed Water Bodies, floating water cubes
@keys2: Fontaine, Institute, Arkhium
### STANDARD
Large cubes of water suspended in the sky around the Central Laboratory Ruins. They were created by the Arkhium explosion that destroyed the old Institute grounds.

## entry: Geode Mine Shaft
@keys: Geode Mine Shaft
@keys2: Arkhium, Meropide, Fortress of Meropide
### STANDARD
A mine shaft beneath the Fortress of Meropide that contains a vein of inert Arkhium. Mining there ended after the Institute disaster. A Meropide inmate named Lechlade complains of headaches, palpitations and cold extremities near it, but the doctor dismisses this; it is unconfirmed whether this is exposure.

## entry: Edwin Eastinghouse
@keys: Edwin Eastinghouse, Edwin
@keys2: Arkhium, Institute, Fontaine
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Edwin is a former Senior Technician of the Fontaine Research Institute, widely acknowledged as a genius and obsessively committed to research results, including at heavy cost to people. He led the Arkhium Research Project and built the Experimental Field Generator. He was presumed dead after the explosion; the Traveler later found him alive and still continuing Arkhium work, and he was fired and sent to the Fortress of Meropide.
