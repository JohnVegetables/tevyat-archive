## summary
### LITE
Alchemy in Teyvat is the art of transmuting and combining matter. In its everyday form it mends, brews and refines; at its peak it can reshape living things, even create them. Mondstadt practises it openly (the Knights of Favonius employ a Chief Alchemist, Albedo), Sumeru's Akademiya studies it in its Spantamad Darshan, Liyue's adepti keep a private version, and Snezhnaya forbids it. The pinnacle is Khaenri'ah's Art of Khemia, which creates life and is tied to the Cataclysm.
### STANDARD +
This entry covers alchemy as a whole. The Art of Khemia has its own entry, as do individual characters such as Albedo and Sucrose. The same word also names the game's crafting system, which is only a loose reflection of the lore (see gameplay).

## in_universe
### LITE
Alchemy is a body of techniques for transforming materials and living things by controlled processes: potions, solutions, transmutation arrays, cauldrons. It does not require a Vision; Sucrose received hers in the middle of an alchemy experiment, not before it. Alchemy comes in levels: ordinary alchemy is limited, while the deepest forms touch the nature of life itself.
### STANDARD +
Albedo's Character Details say that, to most Mondstadters, alchemy meant little more than mending broken odds and ends, until Albedo arrived and showed what it can do, bringing a corpus of knowledge the Sumeru Akademiya did not have. He describes dust as the most basic form of complex life and demonstrated this by growing a Cecilia from the burnt ash of a flower. The sub-categories most often named are bio-alchemy (modifying or transforming existing life), the Art of Khemia (creating new life) and, in Sumeru, alchemical divination and potion-craft.
### LARGE +
Alchemy and the wider mystic arts overlap. The same people often practise astrology, engineering and magic alongside it (Alice is credited with alchemy, astrology, engineering, magic and medicine). The Akademiya treats alchemy as one discipline among many, while Liyue's adepti and Khaenri'ah's alchemists treated it as something closer to a guarded craft.

## mondstadt
### LITE
Mondstadt practises alchemy openly. The Knights of Favonius count a Chief Alchemist among their ranks; Albedo holds that post, with Sucrose and Timaeus as his assistants. Sucrose practises bio-alchemy, working to modify existing life.
### STANDARD +
Albedo came to Mondstadt with a recommendation letter from his master Rhinedottir, addressed to Alice, who placed him with the Knights of Favonius, believing they could provide the large laboratory he needed. He prefers fundamental questions: the nature of things and the creation of life. Sucrose's interest runs the other way: using alchemy to modify existing life and add variety and colour to the world. Her results include a special potion that raised the nectar output of Sweet Flowers by 70 percent and a spray that keeps Sunsettias fresh for a month; she treats these as incidental steps toward a larger, still secret, goal.
### LARGE +
Sucrose's work is methodical to the point of comedy: she devised thirty-odd rearing plans for the Sweet Flower alone, with control groups for temperature and other conditions, and each result raised more questions. She collects fresh bones from butchers and hunters for skeleton-assembly projects, and her Mist Flower Project (numbered versions of enlarged, tagged specimens) is her private paradise of plants.
### ULTRA +
Lisa, the Knights' Librarian, is a former member of Sumeru's Spantamad Darshan (the wiki records her as the Akademiya's most distinguished graduate in two centuries). Albedo's Character Story 1 says he is sincerely disappointed that so gifted a person chose to remain a librarian.

## liyue
### STANDARD
Liyue's adepti practise their own alchemy, a closely guarded secret that most people do not know exists. The adepti use it to make elixirs. Item text for the Mist Veiled Mercury Elixir says the power in these elixirs is lethal to anyone who is not an adeptus, and the wiki records that Mist Veiled Gold Elixir text says such elixirs can give weapons transcendent power.
### LARGE +
Adeptal alchemy is one of the Adeptus Arts, taught to the adepti by Rex Lapis according to the wiki. See the Mystic Arts and Adeptal Arts entries for how it sits among the adepti's other techniques. Canon does not say how it relates to the alchemy of Mondstadt or Sumeru.

## sumeru
### STANDARD
At the Sumeru Akademiya, alchemy is one of the fields of the Spantamad Darshan, which specialises in subjects connected with ley lines and the elements. Some scholars of the Amurta Darshan are also skilled in potion-craft.
### LARGE +
Two Sumeru developments are named. Alchemical divination is studied by Kimiya of Spantamad; he says Lesser Lord Kusanali invented it, and that it reads the ashes left by a failed, random transmutation as one might read tea leaves, with results such as "the Moon" standing for illusions and lies. The Remelting Effect is a battlefield alchemy invented by the Spantamad alchemist Hajanad, using explosive "Remelting Tablets" that the wiki compares to the Smoldersleet Elixir, which originated in Liyue.

## khaenriah
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The Art of Khemia is Khaenri'ah's form of alchemy. Because Khaenri'ah was an underground nation with little natural life, it focused on creating life, and it began as a way to make plants grow without sunlight. It later grew advanced enough to create far more complex beings, and it contributed to the nation's downfall in the Cataclysm 500 years ago. See its own entry for the details.
### LARGE +
Albedo learned the art from his master Rhinedottir (Gold), who created him as her masterpiece, a synthetic human, and who taught him in the field. The art can also make ordinary objects such as chairs, replacing the usual manufacturing process. Venti and Dainsleif, who were present when Khaenri'ah fell, are uneasy about Albedo's practice of it.

## snezhnaya
@version: 7.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Snezhnaya once practised alchemy freely under the Belyi Tsar, and later banned it. The Tsaritsa now forbids the populace from using alchemy, and the Harbingers enforce the ban. The wiki connects the old freedom to the creation of the Aberrant Chimeric Monsters, which alchemists made by recklessly combining forms of life.
### LARGE +
Item text for the 7.0 artifact "Moment That Ceased Upon Waking From Grand Dreams" describes the period before the Tsaritsa took the throne as one in which alchemy was still free, under the long shadow of the Belyi Tsar. The ban therefore dates from after his era, but canon gives no more precise date.

## koshchei_and_hvergelmir
@version: 7.0
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
Koshchei (the Deathless) was a Snezhnayan bio-alchemist and a member of Hvergelmir, the Belyi Tsar's institute for bio-alchemy and Shadow Realm research. He regarded life as a puzzle to be taken apart and reassembled at will. By the account of item text and the wiki, he created the first Aberrant Chimeric Monsters by joining animals, which earned him praise; unsatisfied, he then turned to the Snowland fae, a crime that brought him to the Belyi Tsar's attention, and the Tsar put him to work at Hvergelmir instead of executing him.
### ULTRA +
At Hvergelmir he met Aksinya, to whom the institute had been entrusted. When he answered her gentleness with a cruel prank (growing an ear in her palm), she replied with calm instructions to submit a request first and gave him a timepiece for his schedule. The encounter left him in a daze and stirred an unfamiliar longing. The wiki's summary of the 7.0 world quests "The Stone Beasts, Like Ghouls Seated" and "Chorus of Solace" credits him with the intelligent Chimeric Winged Lion Zver's origin among his abandoned creations and with the Immortal Constructs, and with a later experiment in the village of Okurov in Flamefeather Valley under the name Doctor Moreau. These are summarised from a wiki page; check them against the quests before relying on specifics.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not literal lore): Alchemy is a crafting system used at a Crafting Bench. It combines three lower-tier materials with Mora to make one higher-tier material, and it also produces some potions and gadgets such as Condensed Resin. Adventure Rank unlocks certain recipes.
### LARGE +
The Dust of Azoth converts a gem or fragment of one element into another of the same quality. The Dream Solvent converts weekly-boss drops from the same boss into one another, and converts Northlander Billets into Midlander or Borderland Billets and back. The Parametric Transformer, a device found in the Dunyu Ruins and powered by elemental energy, can also transform materials but not in a wholly controllable way. The game's three crafting methods are Crafting, Converting and Mystic Offering.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay interpretation (this project's recommendation, not canon text): show alchemy as patient, hands-on process: weighing, dissolving, simmering, chalked arrays, cauldron steam and notebooks of failed trials. Mondstadt alchemists are scholars, not villains; their danger is curiosity, not malice. A Snezhnayan scene should treat alchemy as forbidden and spoken of carefully.
### LARGE +
Keep the levels distinct: a common alchemist can mend, brew or purify; modifying living things (bio-alchemy) is a rare skill; creating a life form belongs to Albedo and Rhinedottir. Do not let ordinary alchemists produce homunculi or revive the dead on request. Alchemy is not elemental power and needs no Vision.
### ULTRA +
Do not say that Albedo makes golems at will or that Khemia was a common Khaenri'ahn skill. Do not blur the adepti's lethal elixirs with Mondstadt's potions. Do not have Sucrose reveal her secret aim, which canon never states.

## limits
### STANDARD
Canon never lists exactly what alchemy cannot do. It is shown as skilled, not omnipotent: Sucrose's experiments often fail (her Vision came from one that burned her dandelion seeds to a crisp), and the Remelting Effect and alchemical divination have narrow uses.
### LARGE +
The highest feats are tied to one master (Rhinedottir) and her student. The Art of Khemia's creations are not guaranteed to be safe: Rhinedottir's creations helped bring about the Cataclysm, and failed experiments existed alongside her masterpieces.

## entry: Bio-Alchemy
@keys: bio-alchemy, bio-alchemist, biological alchemy
### STANDARD
Bio-alchemy focuses on changing existing life. In Mondstadt its practitioners are Sucrose and, according to the wiki, Rhinedottir; Sucrose most likely learned it from Albedo. Sucrose uses it to improve plants and preservation, not to create new beings. Snezhnaya's bio-alchemists, such as Koshchei, went much further and were suppressed.
### LARGE +
Sucrose's approach is curiosity-led: she asks why a living thing behaves as it does, then designs experiments. Canon does not say that she has attempted anything like creating life.

## entry: Aberrant Chimeric Monsters
@keys: Aberrant Chimeric Monster, Aberrant Chimeric Monsters, chimeric monster, Chimeric Winged Lion, Immortal Construct
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
The Aberrant Chimeric Monsters are an enemy group found in Snezhnaya. The Adventure Handbook calls them creations of the biological alchemy that once flourished there before it was forbidden. They were made in the Belyi Tsar's reign by alchemists, above all Koshchei, who combined many forms of life. Most were consumed in the war against the Onslaught of Dark Beasts, and the few survivors are rare and dangerous. Named types include the Chimeric Armored Beast, Burrowbeast, Winged Elephant, Horned Bear and Winged Lion, and the Immortal Construct.
### LARGE +
Item text describes the earliest creations as winged four-legged beasts, and insect-winged, casqued tigers and leopards. Many were deemed failures and abandoned. One Chimeric Winged Lion gained a degree of intelligence and was taken in by the Belyi Tsar, who named it Zver.

## entry: Alchemical Divination
@keys: alchemical divination, Kimiya, Remelting Tablets, Remelting Effect, Hajanad
### STANDARD
Two Sumeru alchemies from Spantamad: alchemical divination (reading the ashes from a failed transmutation, said to have been invented by Lesser Lord Kusanali) and the Remelting Effect, which uses explosive Remelting Tablets invented by Hajanad. Both appear in Akademiya-related quests.
