## summary
### LITE
Phlogiston is the primal energy of Natlan. People there can sense and use it: it powers machines, written signs and the Nightsoul's Blessing state of Natlan's warriors. It exists as liquid, solid and gaseous forms and flows along Spiritways. Canon calls it Teyvat's primordial energy and a form of "computronium", programmable matter that stores information. The dragon Kukulkan stole it and taught humans to use it.
### STANDARD +
The Heavenly Principles later reworked elemental energy using phlogiston as a base, so elemental energy is described as phlogiston's modern counterpart. Natlanese treat phlogiston as a symbol of an indomitable spirit.
### LARGE +
Phlogiston is also the reason Natlan is a target: in earlier ages the Heavenly Principles treated Natlan's phlogiston-rich soil as a "Holy Soil" phenomenon and cast sky-nails down at it, according to Kukulkan.

## in_universe
### LITE
In the world: Natlan's people use phlogiston for devices, signs, paints and warrior abilities. It carries heat and is dangerous to those who cannot handle it, but the Natlanese, nearly all of whom can use it, read it and work with it directly.
### STANDARD +
Phlogiston can appear as a gas that leaks from the ground and flows in currents, as a flowing liquid, and as solid crystals or blocks. Natlanese craft solid phlogiston into barrels, targets and nails. Item text describes it as a high-temperature thermal energy found throughout Natlan "in all shapes and sizes", and warriors switch to drawing on it to keep their Nightsoul's Blessing going once their Nightsoul runs out.
### LARGE +
Those who can use phlogiston can read words recorded in Phlogiston Engravings by sensing the phlogiston inside them, so formal study of the writing is partly seen as obsolete. Two prominent modern uses of engraving are Ancient Name forging by the Children of Echoes, who carve names into obsidian, and Nightspirit graffiti made by the Masters of the Night-Wind with painted engravings that only their spiritspeakers can activate. According to Ororon, painted engravings can be activated with either phlogiston or elemental energy.
### ULTRA +
Saurians also draw on phlogiston: in gameplay indwelling one grants it extra phlogiston storage, and a soldier's diary links the Fatui's capture of Saurians to phlogiston and their special constitution. Item text notes that Qucusaurs and some members of the Flower-Feather Clan have learned to use gaseous phlogiston without being burned. Phlogiston Chiseliths are devices that can be infused with phlogiston to open mechanisms, based on the older inscriptions.

## origins
@version: 5.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Phlogiston is one of Teyvat's ancient forms of energy. In the Archon Quest "Beneath the Secret Source" (5.1), the Lord of the Night explains that phlogiston is Teyvat's primordial energy, that it belongs to the Light Realm, and that elemental energy is built on it: as he puts it, light splits into seven colours like a rainbow, and elemental energy is a similar idea, the modern counterpart of phlogiston. The Heavenly Principles developed it to counter the Abyss better.
### LARGE +
The wiki flags that the English line is somewhat misleading; the Chinese text is read as the Principles modifying elemental energy to share phlogiston's properties rather than making it from phlogiston. The Primordial Sea and kuuvahki, the power of Nod-Krai's moons, are cited as other ancient, primordial forms of energy alongside phlogiston.
### ULTRA +
How elemental energy relates to the seven elements, Visions and the Archons' Authorities is told elsewhere (see the elements entry); this entry covers phlogiston itself.

## dragons_and_computronium
@version: 5.5
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
In the world quest "An Omen of Annihilation and the Final Entreaty" (5.5), Kukulkan tells the Traveler that phlogiston is an atom on which information can be written, a form of "computronium" that can imitate the essence of anything through the weaving of language, which is why it takes on different forms. The ancient dragons used it this way. Their golden script, Iridescent Inscriptions, works as a programming language for phlogiston: it records information, controls Secret Source mechanisms, converts phlogiston between forms, creates Spiritways and lets dragons reshape their own appearance.
### LARGE +
Natlan's ruins still hold dragon-era phlogiston recordings. Modern researchers have made little progress deciphering the inscriptions, and the Saurian Relics Association has posted bounties for it. Human Phlogiston Engravings are a simpler imitation, and a considerable gap in complexity separates the two.
### ULTRA +
Kukulkan also describes Huitzilopochtli, the Natlan Phlogistonization Array built by the dragon Xiuhcoatl, as a device meant to turn all of Natlan into phlogiston or matter made of it, so that information is transcribed into phlogiston and the Abyss destroyed, at the cost of all life dying or becoming phlogiston lifeforms. The wiki's account says Kukulkan and Xbalanque split its key, the two Golden Entreaties, to keep it from ever being activated.

## kukulkan_and_humans
@version: 5.0
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
The dragon Kukulkan, called the Sage of the Stolen Flame, took phlogiston from his kin and taught humans in Natlan to use it. Natlanese tradition names him the origin of their ability to use it.
### LARGE +
Per the wiki's account of Natlan's history, he foresaw the dragons' fall and believed humanity would find salvation if knowledge were shared. He taught his first human disciple, and the first wielders formed Natlan's first settled tribe. The dragons later accused him of betrayal and the Lords and Princes moved to impeach him. When tribes began using phlogiston as a weapon against each other, he withdrew from the world. Some Natlanese believe he was in fact human.
## forms
### LITE
Gaseous, liquid and solid phlogiston, and Spiritways.
### STANDARD +
- Gaseous: free-flowing heat, leaking from the ground or forming currents and wind tunnels. It burns those who cannot handle it.
- Liquid: a molten flow that ignites those who touch it, but is safe for Saurians of certain kinds and certain characters.
- Solid: orange crystals or blocks used for tools and barriers.
- Spiritway: a flowing channel carrying travellers in the Nightsoul's Blessing state.
### LARGE +
Spiritways are a phlogiston-based channel type created by the dragons through inscription. Modern humans can stabilise or create new ones with Phlogiston Engraving in the form of Spiritdoors. Spiritways may be one-way or bidirectional.
### ULTRA +
In the world quest at the Heart of the Sacred Mountain, the Traveler describes immersion in liquid phlogiston as an experience that gave a flood of images and wisdom, and Kukulkan afterward confirms that the information and wisdom of ancient times lie within the primal flame.

## gameplay
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not literal lore): Phlogiston is a shared party meter in Natlan, capped at 100. It is consumed once Nightsoul points run out and by Saurian Indwelling abilities, and it is refilled by collecting items, breaking objects and standing near certain features. It is not available in domains.
### LARGE +
Liquid phlogiston drains 5% of max HP every 0.25 seconds and applies Pyro to those it touches; gaseous phlogiston drains 2.5% every 0.5 seconds. Solid phlogiston can be passed through only by certain Saurian forms, Children of Echoes characters and Mavuika. Filling one level of a Phlogiston Chiselith costs 25 phlogiston. Natlan characters such as Xilonen, Mualani and Chasca have talents that generate it.
### ULTRA +
The wiki lists the common sources: the Pyro Crystalfly, Embercore Flower, Phlogiston Aphid and Candlecap Mushroom, Dracolite, Pulverite, Phlogiston Piles, Recon Targets and Rings, Statues of The Seven and Teleport Waypoints.

## fatui_extraction
@version: 5.0
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
The Fatui run a secret underground base in Natlan's Coatepec Mountain, the Phlogiston Extraction Research Center, where they extract phlogiston from captured Saurians. Fontaine Research Institute equipment is used there, though it is unclear how much the Institute knows about what it is used for. A soldier's diary in the base says the soldiers do not know the purpose of the research.
### ULTRA +
The base's emergency manual mentions plans to adapt its machinery to run on Natlan's lava. These are minor details from item text and carry no larger revelation.

## status
@version: 7.1
### STANDARD
As of 7.1, phlogiston remains Natlan's central energy resource and symbol. No later canon found in the Nod-Krai or Snezhnaya chapters changes what is said above. Huitzilopochtli is covered in its own entry.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay interpretation (this project's recommendation, not canon text): treat phlogiston as the everyday lifeblood of Natlan: warm, flowing, taken for granted by locals and alarming to outsiders who step into it. Natlanese characters sense it the way others sense a smell, so they notice engravings, leaks and Saurians' condition without tools. Outsiders from other nations should treat liquid or gaseous phlogiston as dangerous.
### LARGE +
Keep phlogiston distinct from Pyro in scenes: it can be liquid, gas or solid, is a source of information as well as heat, and is associated with ancient dragon writing. Do not have characters casually reprogram matter with it; only dragons and the Iridescent Inscriptions did so in canon, and modern engraving is a limited imitation.
### ULTRA +
Do not claim the Heavenly Principles simply created elemental energy out of phlogiston without noting that the exact wording is disputed. Do not invent a Nod-Krai or Snezhnaya use of phlogiston.

## entry: Iridescent Inscription
@keys: Iridescent Inscription, Iridescent Inscriptions, Phlogiston Engraving, Phlogiston Engravings
@version: 5.0
### STANDARD
Iridescent Inscriptions are the golden script the ancient dragons of Natlan used to wield phlogiston, essentially a programming language for matter. Humans imitated it as Phlogiston Engravings, which can store text, build Spiritdoors and Phlogiston Chiseliths, and underlie Ancient Name carving and Nightspirit graffiti.
### LARGE +
Surviving inscriptions are scattered and mostly undeciphered; the Saurian Relics Association has issued research bounties. Phlogiston users can read engravings by sensing the phlogiston inside them.

## entry: Natlan Phlogistonization Array
@keys: Huitzilopochtli, Phlogistonization Array, Sweeping Skyfire, Sunquake
@version: 5.5
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
Huitzilopochtli is a device built by Xiuhcoatl to convert Natlan into phlogiston and destroy the Abyss. According to Kukulkan in the 5.5 world quest, it works by transcribing information into phlogiston, with life either dying or becoming phlogiston lifeforms. It needs the two Golden Entreaties to be reunited to activate. Kukulkan and Xbalanque are said to have split them to prevent that.

## entry: Phlogiston Extraction Research Center
@keys: Phlogiston Extraction Research Center, Coatepec Mountain Fatui base
@version: 5.0
### STANDARD
A secret Fatui base under Coatepec Mountain where phlogiston is extracted from captured Saurians. It uses Fontaine Research Institute equipment. Staff there write that the purpose of the research was never explained to them.

## entry: Liquid and gaseous Phlogiston hazards
@keys: Liquid Phlogiston, Gaseous Phlogiston, Solid Phlogiston, Phlogiston Wind Tunnel, Scorchwind Conflux
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
Liquid phlogiston burns those who enter it, gaseous phlogiston burns those who stand in its currents, and solid phlogiston forms crystals and blocks used for devices. Qucusaurs and some Flower-Feather Clan members have mastered gaseous phlogiston without being burned, and Koholasaurs and certain characters move through liquid phlogiston safely.
