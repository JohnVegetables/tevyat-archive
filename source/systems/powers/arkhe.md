## summary
### LITE
Arkhe is a pair of opposing energies native to Fontaine: Pneuma and Ousia. When one meets the other, the two annihilate and release a greater burst of energy. This Pneuma-Ousia Annihilation is what powers most of Fontaine's Clockwork Meka. Arkhe is not one of the seven elements. It is a separate, Fontaine-specific energy, and most Fontainian characters carry one alignment of it.
### STANDARD +
The two names are often joined as "Pneumousia" in Fontaine's engineering and environment text. A hit of one alignment on something charged with the other disrupts it, so a machine's own power supply can be turned against it.

## in_universe
### LITE
In-game tutorials describe Pneuma and Ousia as properties found in the life-forms, earth, air and watery veins of Fontaine. When energies of different properties collide they annihilate and yield greater energy. This is the principle that powers many Clockwork Meka.
### STANDARD +
Meka power supplies are unstable and settle into a Pneuma-aligned or Ousia-aligned state. An attack of the opposite alignment overloads the supply and disables the machine for a time. Combat-oriented meka keep a backup system for low-end fighting, while utility meka lose their combat functions entirely.
### LARGE +
Meka refine the naturally occurring pneumousia around them using components called Repeaters. That is convenient inside Fontaine and a limitation elsewhere. Pneumousia Blocks can stand in as a substitute energy source, but only for brief periods. Fontaine loading-screen text credits Alain Guillotin with inventing artificial blocks more than 400 years ago.
### ULTRA +
Ancient Logs described on the wiki place Alain and his peers, around 400 to 500 years ago, turning what had been seen as a latent environmental threat into a controlled but unstable power source, the kinetic core. Arkhe in nature is therefore both a hazard and a resource. The Fontaine Research Institute, which Alain founded, still leads meka development today.

## character_alignment
@version: 4.0
### STANDARD
Most Fontainian characters, and the Hydro Traveler, have an innate Arkhe alignment, reflected in the shape of their Vision. Pneuma belongs to Neuvillette, Lyney, Freminet, Charlotte and Emilie, and to the Hydro Traveler. Ousia belongs to Navia, Clorinde, Wriothesley, Sigewinne, Lynette, Chevreuse and Escoffier. Furina is listed under both and can alternate between the two.
### LARGE +
An aligned character can release a follow-up from one of their combat talents: Pneuma users spawn Spiritbreath Thorns and Ousia users spawn Surging Blades. These do not apply an element and have their own fixed cooldown, separate from the talent's. Alignment is a fact of design and not a personality trait; canon draws no link between temperament and alignment.

## known_uses
### LITE
Disabling Clockwork Meka by striking with the opposite alignment, solving Fontaine puzzles, and stunning Breacher Primuses in their stressed state.
### STANDARD +
Pneumousia Blocks found in the wild can be picked up and released on the next attack. The underwater tutorial notes that most Fontainian characters can trigger their own alignment underwater without blocks. Enhanced Pneumousia Amplifiers apply an elemental aura to nearby enemies, and an opposite-Arkhe hit disables one for the rest of the fight.
### LARGE +
- Prototype Cal. Breguet, the Experimental Field Generator, ends its Gravity Reduction Field after taking three Pneuma-aligned hits (see Arkhium).
- Meka are colour-coded: bright white clockwork is Pneuma, dark blue is Ousia. Stronger meka take more opposite hits to weaken: one for common meks, two for specialist meks, three for boss-class meka.
- Outside Fontaine, meka lean on Pneumousia Blocks because local pneumousia is unavailable.

## limits
### LITE
Arkhe is not an element and does not react like one.
### STANDARD +
An Arkhe hit does not apply elemental effects. Annihilation needs two opposite alignments; matching alignments do nothing special.
### LARGE +
Canon does not say what Arkhe is at root or where it comes from. Do not tie Pneuma and Ousia to Hydro, the Heavenly Principles or the Prophecy of Fontaine unless a scene is clearly speculation.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not literal lore): Arkhe is a combat and exploration mechanic introduced in Version 4.0. Pneuma and Ousia talents trigger on a fixed Arkhe interval, and some weapons have passives that depend on Arkhe alignment. While diving, a character's attacks default to their own alignment, since they have no access to their combat talents.
### LARGE +
Normal, Charged and Plunging attacks carry the energy of a picked-up block. The Genius Invokation TCG also uses a Pneuma-Ousia Annihilation reaction, and Furina's TCG passive "Arkhe: Seats Sacred and Secular" (4.7) is named for her Arkhe.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay interpretation (this project's recommendation, not canon text): treat Arkhe as a Fontainian thing, a quiet hum of mutual opposition. Pneuma reads as sharp, thorn-like and spirit-like; Ousia reads as heavy, surging and substantial, echoing the in-game thorn and blade effects. Keep it distinct from elemental power: a Fontainian can have a Vision element and an Arkhe alignment at once.
### LARGE +
Visitors from outside Fontaine will often not know the terms. A Fontainian engineer might say "pneumousia" casually, while a visitor needs it explained as two opposing energies that cancel into power. Do not assign alignments to non-Fontainians, and do not present Pneuma as good and Ousia as bad.

## entry: Pneuma
@keys: Pneuma, Pneuma-aligned
@spoiler: none
### STANDARD
Pneuma is one of the two Arkhe energies. In gameplay it appears as the Spiritbreath Thorn follow-up and as white-clockwork meka. Characters aligned with Pneuma include Neuvillette, Lyney, Freminet, Charlotte, Emilie and the Hydro Traveler, and Furina can use Pneuma as well. A Pneuma meka is weakened by an Ousia hit.
### LARGE +
The Chinese name for Pneuma translates roughly as "thorniness", and the English name comes from the Greek word for "breath" or "spirit". This is etymology, not in-game explanation.

## entry: Ousia
@keys: Ousia, Ousia-aligned
@spoiler: none
### STANDARD
Ousia is the other Arkhe energy. In gameplay it appears as the Surging Blade follow-up and as dark-blue-clockwork meka. Characters aligned with Ousia include Navia, Clorinde, Wriothesley, Sigewinne, Lynette, Chevreuse and Escoffier, and Furina can use Ousia as well. An Ousia meka is weakened by a Pneuma hit.
### LARGE +
The Chinese name for Ousia translates roughly as "wildness", and the English name comes from the Greek word usually rendered "essence" or "substance".

## entry: Pneumousia Block
@keys: Pneumousia Block, Pneumousia Blocks, Pneuma Block, Ousia Block
### STANDARD
Pneumousia Blocks are energy blocks found in Fontaine's wilderness, in Pneuma and Ousia varieties. Once picked up, the next Normal, Charged or Plunging attack releases the stored energy, including underwater. Alain Guillotin is credited with inventing artificial blocks over 400 years ago, which he used to power his meka. Blocks also let meka run outside Fontaine, though only briefly.

## entry: Pneuma-Ousia Annihilation
@keys: Pneuma-Ousia Annihilation, mutual annihilation, Annihilation reaction
@keys2: Pneuma, Ousia, Arkhe, Pneumousia
### STANDARD
When Pneuma and Ousia meet, they annihilate and produce a greater energy. Fontainian engineering harnesses this in controlled form inside kinetic cores, and it can overload an opposite-aligned meka. Fontaine Research Institute researchers found Arkhium's energy potential to be far greater than this reaction's.
