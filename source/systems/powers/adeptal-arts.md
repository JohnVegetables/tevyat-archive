## summary
### LITE
The Adeptal Arts are the techniques of Liyue's adepti, the illuminated beasts and gods led by Rex Lapis (Morax). They include alchemy for making elixirs, paper sigils that store adeptal power, the creation of pocket-world abodes, and dream arts. The power is generally harmful or lethal to ordinary mortals, and the adepti guard their methods closely.
### STANDARD +
Rex Lapis is said to have taught these arts to the adepti. Their elemental abilities come with them, since all adepti can command elemental energy. Mortals who learn a technique do not become adepti.

## in_universe
### LITE
Adepti can manipulate elemental energy without a Vision, which is why Liyue's people call them the "three-eyed" mighty and illuminated adepti; their Visions, when worn, are shown as a human convention. Beyond that, each adeptus carries adeptal energy with an effect particular to them, which is dangerous to mortals who spend too long near it.
### STANDARD +
Core techniques named in canon:
- Alchemy (Dandi), used to refine elixirs. The item text on the Mist Veiled Mercury Elixir says that anyone who swallows an elixir meets certain death, since the power in them is lethal to non-adepti.
- Sigils (Fulu), paper talismans that hold adeptal power as symbols.
- Sub-Space Creation (Waijing), the making of autonomous pocket worlds through imagination. Rex Lapis granted this ability.
- Dream Trawler, the giving and receiving of dreams.
- Mortal Lingering, taking a temporary human form after the original body has died.
- The Nuo Dance of Evil Conquering, a yaksha technique that uses the power of a yaksha mask (see System: Karma).
### LARGE +
Sacred names are another feature of adeptal culture: most adepti have a four-character name followed by the honorific "Perfected Lord", of which the English game text usually keeps only the first half, as in Moon Carver or Mountain Shaper. Rex Lapis and Xiao's titles follow other patterns. Not every adeptus uses a sacred name; Madame Ping and Ganyu live under ordinary names.

According to Liyue belief, the adepti shaped the landscape, so that rivers are their saliva and grass their hair. Mountain Shaper calls those claims nonsense and explains that the amber of Mt. Hulao comes from a plant called the karst crawler, though he can control it somewhat.
### ULTRA +
Other Liyue techniques sit near the Adeptal Arts without being the same thing: the Art of Homa, a secret technique for purifying the border between life and death, descends from a member of the Eight Adepts and is carried on by the Wangsheng Funeral Parlor, and the Qimen arts of Chenyu Vale's Lan Clan trace to the adeptus Wind Reader. The wiki also treats Liyue exorcist methods, such as Chongyun's, as derived from adeptal arts, though canon says little more.

## alchemy_and_elixirs
### STANDARD
Adeptal alchemy produces elixirs of great power that kill mortals who swallow them. The Mist Veiled Mercury Elixir is the item the game uses to describe this, and the Mist Veiled Gold Elixir is known for giving weapons transcendent powers. Remedium Tertiorum, a medicine Zhongli once offered for Xiao, temporarily eases the pain of karma and follows the same rule: in his words, nothing of this world can withstand it.
### LARGE +
In gameplay, the Parametric Transformer in the Dunyu Ruins turns items into other items; canon says nothing about how it works or where it came from.

## sigils
### STANDARD
Sigils (Fulu) store adeptal power in symbols and typically appear as paper talismans. Qiqi wears talismans that hold in the adeptal power inside her body; without them, her profile says it would send her into a berserk state.
### LARGE +
Sigils of Permission (Talisman of Nonrestriction) were made by Rex Lapis and carried adeptal power. During the Archon War, mortals used them to channel that power in battle; today most of the power has faded, but adepti still refrain from harming the holder, and Cloud Retainer remarks on them in Custodian of Clouds. In the Archon Quest, the Fatui studied the patterns and made copies; Tartaglia used many at once, together with his Harbinger power, to wake Osial for a short time.

## abodes_and_dreams
### STANDARD
Sub-Space Creation lets an adeptus build a small pocket world, usually a home for themselves. Madame Ping's teapot and the Serenitea Pot are examples; larger abodes such as the Realm of Clouds and the Sea Gazer's Abode were made the same way. Adepti may also make teapot spirits to run an abode, and the spirits receive adeptus names.
### LARGE +
The ability has limits: the Serenitea Pot is not a true world, so a whole city cannot be built inside it. It is also regarded as lesser than Rex Lapis's power to move mountains and seas. Energy from Sub-Space Creation gives objects special properties, including soil that stays fertile and warm water all year. The Traveler can use it too, and the wiki suggests this may be because the adepti lent power to them in Turning Point, though canon does not say.

Dream Trawler lets the user enter another person's dream or give them a dream of the user's design. Known practitioners are Rex Lapis and Xiao. Mortal Lingering is attributed to Pervases. In The Purging of Evil, Xiao and the Traveler use Dream Trawler to call up the spirit of the fraud Starsnatcher.

## adeptal_energy_and_mortals
### STANDARD
Adeptal energy gives each adeptus a particular effect. In Turning Point, when the Traveler was lent power from Madame Ping, Ganyu and Xiao, Ping's gave shockwaves with attacks, Ganyu's gave healing and Xiao's gave extra speed. Ordinary mortals cannot endure too much of it: their souls are not as robust, and with enough time it overwhelms them.
### LARGE +
Qiqi's story shows the danger. When a herb-picking girl was mortally wounded in a clash between adepti and demons, the adepti each gave her a portion of their strength to bring her back. She revived as a zombie, but could not control the adeptal energy raging in her and went berserk, so Mountain Shaper sealed her in amber for hundreds of years. The same profile says a Vision appeared to her as she was dying, and that the adepti accepted this as a valid form of illumination. Zibai's voice-over later calls her an odd case, a girl with the body of an adeptus but not the soul.
### ULTRA +
The wiki notes that Qiqi can still manipulate the adeptal energy centuries later. Do not describe her as an adeptus; canon calls her a zombie who was kept from death by adeptal power.

## devices_and_compass
### STANDARD
Adepti have also put their arts into devices. Cloud Retainer (Xianyun) believes the laws of nature can be invested in ingenious devices, so she builds mechanisms for the people of Liyue; Zibai notes that she would rather tinker than use her powers. The Fantastic Compass, made by the adeptus Sea Gazer to seal away monsters, is a catalyst that shines when an adeptus and a mortal use it together.
### LARGE +
The book that comes with the Compass says that when mortal and adeptal power combine, one can move the heavens and shake the earth. Ancestors of Yelan used it with the yaksha Bosacius to seal the Chasm's Bed during the cataclysm. In Perilous Trail (Interlude Chapter, Act II), Yelan and Xiao activate a conjured version to escape the Chasm's Bed, and Yanfei, who has both human and adeptal ancestry, makes it flicker.

## limits
### LITE
Adeptal power harms mortals and is not given freely. Humans can learn some adeptal arts, but they do not become adepti, since canon applies the word to non-humans (half-humans can qualify).
### STANDARD +
Adeptal abilities do not remove limits: the Serenitea Pot cannot hold a city, and not even Sub-Space Creation can restore Padisarah flowers that died out when the Goddess of Flowers fell. Dream arts and sigils take effort and are often kept secret from the public.
### LARGE +
Most adepti withdrew to Jueyun Karst after the Archon War, so ordinary people rarely see these arts. Only mortals with a Sigil of Permission may approach the adepti's land without offence, and the Sigil's power has mostly worn off.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not literal lore): adeptal-themed content includes the Serenitea Pot realm, adeptal-themed furnishings, the Mist Veiled elixirs as weapon-ascension materials, adeptal-themed talents such as Qiqi's Adeptus Art: Preserver of Fortune, and Xiao's Bane of All Evil.
### LARGE +
Ganyu, Xiao, Yanfei, Xianyun, Qiqi and other Liyue characters use these themes in their kits. Treat kit names as flavor, not as a full account of the canon arts.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay interpretation (this project's recommendation, not canon text): show adeptal power as quiet and costly rather than flashy. A simple talisman glow, a hush, a chill or warmth in the air, or a hint of old incense fits. Adepti guard their methods, so a character who asks about the arts should usually get a partial or indirect answer.
### LARGE +
Remind mortals of the danger: elixirs are not for drinking, and long contact with an adeptus leaves the weak-souled uneasy. Do not let an ordinary human perform the highest arts such as Sub-Space Creation unless canon supports it (the Traveler is an unexplained case).
### ULTRA +
Avoid portraying adeptal energy as an element of its own with fixed rules, or giving every adeptus the same arts; canon shows different effects for Madame Ping, Ganyu and Xiao and says only a few adepti use certain techniques. Keep Nod-Krai and Snezhnaya material out unless a scene needs it.

## entry: Sub-Space Creation
@keys: Sub-Space Creation, Waijing, adeptus abode
@spoiler: none
### STANDARD
Waijing is the adeptal ability to build small, self-contained pocket worlds through imagination. Rex Lapis granted it, and it has been used for abodes: Madame Ping's teapot, Cloud Retainer's Realm of Clouds, Sea Gazer's Abode and the Serenitea Pot. The result is not a true world and cannot hold a whole city, but its energy can make soil fertile and water warm and clear. Adepti also make teapot spirits to manage these homes.

## entry: Sigil of Permission
@keys: Sigil of Permission, Sigils of Permission, Talisman of Nonrestriction
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
A talisman made by Rex Lapis and charged with adeptal power. In the Archon War mortals used such sigils to channel power in battle; by the present most of the power has worn off, yet adepti will not harm a holder, and a holder is said to be able to enter Jueyun Karst without offending them. The Fatui copied the design and used the copies to briefly free Osial during the Archon Quest.

## entry: Fantastic Compass
@keys: Fantastic Compass
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
A catalyst made by the adeptus Sea Gazer to seal away monsters, later passed to a human friend and used to exorcise demons together. In the cataclysm, Bosacius and Boyang used it to seal the Chasm's Bed. Its power needs both an adeptus and a mortal; Yanfei, who has both ancestries, can briefly wake it.
