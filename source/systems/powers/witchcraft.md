## summary
### LITE
Witchcraft in Teyvat is not a single taught discipline. The word points at the witches and mages of the Hexenzirkel, the circle of powerful women founded by the adventurer Alice, whose members practise astrology, alchemy and other arts, and at titles such as Lisa's "Witch of Purple Rose". Their powers are shown through individuals, from a near-omnipotent sorceress to members with no magical training at all. The game also uses "Hexerei" as a team-bonus label for characters tied to the circle.
### STANDARD +
This entry describes the magic the witches use. The circle itself (history, members, island) belongs in the Hexenzirkel faction entry, and the individual practices have their own entries: Alchemy, the Art of Khemia and Mystic Arts in Teyvat (for astrology and hydromancy).

## in_universe
### LITE
Hexenzirkel members are called witches or mages, but the label is loose. Alice, the founder, is a long-lived polymath with alchemy, astrology, engineering, magic and medicine among her skills. Barbeloth reads fate by astrology. Rhinedottir practises the Art of Khemia. Nicole is an angel who survived the fall of her kind. Octavia travels the Sea of Stars. Two members, Andersdotter and I. Ivanovna N., never had any magical or combat training.
### STANDARD +
Members use code letters and epithets: Alice (A, the Merry-Witch), Barbeloth (B, the Seer-Witch), I. Ivanovna N. (J, the Tricksy-Witch), Andersdotter (M, the Story-Witch), Nicole (N, the Memory-Witch), Octavia (O, the Wander-Witch) and Rhinedottir (R, the Sage-Witch). Alice's criterion for inviting members was that they be "interesting"; moral virtue was not required. Members appear in several forms, notably as Dodoco-shaped dolls in witch hats with individually coloured tails, and some voices reach the world through phonographs, teacups or lanterns.
### LARGE +
The circle's work is world-scale. It is credited with creating Simulanka (a mirror of Teyvat) and the Imaginarium Theater. It explores the Irminsul, and Alice is described as the Sustainer of the World's Borders who maintains Teyvat's borders and is wary of anyone interfering too much in its affairs. The Hexenzirkel once challenged the Anemo Archon Barbatos, who managed to pacify them and bring the conflict to a peaceful resolution. More recently, at Grand Master Varka's request, Barbatos arranged the Tripartite Conference between himself, the Hexenzirkel and Mondstadt's Knights and Church, and the circle agreed to help Mondstadt in its time of greatest need.
### ULTRA +
Mystic arts and witchcraft overlap but differ in scope: the Mystic Arts overview groups regional traditions, whereas witchcraft is tied to one circle of individuals whose powers are not regional and some of whom rival gods. Alice is the member most often shown doing physical wonders (lifting or shaping island systems on command) while Barbeloth's reach is informational: she reads the fortunes of nations, Teyvat and the world.

## members
### STANDARD
Seven founding members are named in canon. Alice and Barbeloth are the circle's best-known active practitioners; Nicole is a playable angel; Octavia created the pocket realm Miliastra Wonderland and has visited at least 1,896 planets; Rhinedottir is one of the Five Sinners of Khaenri'ah and, after merging with Naberius, the Ruler of Life among the Four Shades. Andersdotter (deceased) was an acclaimed writer; I. Ivanovna N. (deceased) became a powerful Snezhnayan politician and the first Mayor of Snezhnograd.
### LARGE +
- Successors: becoming a member's successor does not guarantee membership. Scarlett is the successor of J.
- Mona calls Barbeloth her master and Scarlett calls her Barbeloth's student, not successor; after Alice's explanation in the 3.5 event Mona was surprised that so many mysterious women all knew one another, so her own membership is unclear.
- Not all witches are members: Lisa denies interest, and Signora, once the Crimson Witch of Flame, has no known ties and takes offence at being called a witch.
- Klee is Alice's daughter but has not learned Alice's magic; Alice has hinted that she will teach her when she is ready.

## practices
### STANDARD
- Divination: Barbeloth pioneered hydromancy (a subclass of astrology) and is said to be able to read the fates of whole nations and the world; Mona practises it as her student. Barbeloth's case of oracles is tied to the Lunar Arcana.
- Alchemy and life-making: Alice practises alchemy; Rhinedottir's Art of Khemia creates life and does not entirely conform to Teyvat's rules.
- Life extension: Alice can extend a human's lifespan through magic (Andersdotter refused).
- Realm-making: Simulanka, the Imaginarium Theater and Miliastra Wonderland are all creations of members, and members are said to rewrite fate in some cases. Andersdotter helped Simulanka's creation and rewrote fate for her child, Durin.
### LARGE +
Rhinedottir's creations were involved in the Cataclysm and she served the Khaenri'ahn dynasty; she is a Hexenzirkel mage and a Sinner at once. Members also invent: Alice's inventions include a phonograph, the Temari game, explosive Dodoco dolls and a long-distance instant communication device.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not literal lore): "Hexerei" is a team-bonus label. After completing Witch's Homework, a character becomes a Hexerei character, and the Hexerei: Secret Rite team bonus applies when the party has two or more of them. The Little Witch's Dictionary, received from Alice, is the item that issues the homework. The wiki lists Albedo, Klee, Mona, Venti, Durin, Varka, Nicole, Lohen, Fischl, Razor, Sucrose and Prune among them.
### LARGE +
The unlock requires owning the character at level 70 or higher, then completing their quest. Each Hexerei character gets a Witch's Eve Rite Passive. The Witch's Revelation system is separate: it uses Barbeloth's case of oracles, tied to the Lunar Arcana, to give some characters (such as Wriothesley, Yae Miko, Cyno, Mizuki, Qiqi, Diona and Beidou) additional passives.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay interpretation (this project's recommendation, not canon text): present witchcraft as personality plus craft, not a spell list. A Hexenzirkel scene is playful on the surface, with teacups, tea parties and Dodoco-shaped messengers, while the power underneath is ancient and world-scale. Different witches should feel different: Alice's enthusiasm, Barbeloth's teasing way with fate, Nicole's silence, Rhinedottir's coldness and strictness (as Albedo remembers her).
### LARGE +
Members rarely act in the open; they send messages, dolls and cryptic readings. Use hints (a tail-tip colour, a hat, a tea cup) to signal them. Magic has costs and consequences in fate, such as astrologers entangling their own destiny.
### ULTRA +
Do not turn Klee into a trained witch, treat every Mondstadt scholar as a Hexenzirkel member, or call Signora a witch to her face without consequence. Do not resolve the circle's goals or number of members. Do not let Hexerei, the gameplay label, imply a character is in the circle.

## limits
### STANDARD
No unified rules exist. Each member's feats are personal, from Alice's island-moving to Barbeloth's readings; some members have no magic at all. Even the powerful are limited: Barbeloth, for instance, warns that revealing fate too readily spoils the future.
### LARGE +
The circle acts by agreement, not command, and its members mostly pursue their own goals. Rhinedottir's merger with Naberius shows that even a member's choices have consequences: Naberius still voices her disapproval within their shared mind.

## entry: Hexenzirkel Members
@keys: Hexenzirkel, Merry-Witch, Seer-Witch, Sage-Witch, Tricksy-Witch, Story-Witch, Memory-Witch, Wander-Witch
### STANDARD
The Hexenzirkel is the circle of witches that Alice founded; its members use code letters A, B, J, M, N, O and R and epithets such as Merry-Witch, Seer-Witch and Sage-Witch. They gather for "formal tea parties" and have used a secret island above Falcon Coast. Their purpose is mysterious. See the Hexenzirkel faction entry.

## entry: Barbeloth and Hydromancy
@keys: Barbeloth, hydromancy, Seer-Witch
### STANDARD
Astromancer Barbeloth Trismegistus, the Seer-Witch (B), is the master hydromancer who taught Mona. She is Alice's friend and rival, and an elderly woman in blue witch attire. She mapped the fates of all living things in Simulanka and, according to Mona, can read the fortunes of nations and the world.

## entry: Alice
@keys: Alice, Merry-Witch, Teyvat Travel Guide
### STANDARD
Alice (A) is a travel writer, adventurer and the founder of the Hexenzirkel, a long-lived elf-eared woman at least 500 years old, and Klee's mother. Albedo calls her a near-omnipotent sorceress. She appears chiefly as a voice in a Dodoco, phonograph or teacup, and first appeared in person in the 6.2 Nod-Krai quest. See her own entry.

## entry: Hexerei Team Bonus
@keys: Hexerei, Secret Rite, Witch's Homework, Little Witch's Dictionary
@spoiler: none
### STANDARD
Gameplay: the Hexerei team bonus rewards parties of characters who have completed Witch's Homework. Alice gives the player the Little Witch's Dictionary in the quest "Unexpected Commission". This is a game label, not proof of Hexenzirkel membership.
