## summary
### LITE
"Mystic arts" is the wiki's umbrella for the magical, ritual and supernatural traditions of Teyvat that are not just a Vision's elemental power. Mondstadt has openly practised alchemy and astrology; Liyue has the adepti's arts, exorcism, the Art of Homa and the Qimen arts; Inazuma has kitsune and bake-danuki magic, onmyoudou and tengu arts; Sumeru has the Akasha, Akademiya alchemy and the Aranara's Ararakalari. A parallel circle of powerful mages, the Hexenzirkel, practises witchcraft (its own entry).
### STANDARD +
The grouping is the wiki's, not the game's: each tradition keeps its own in-world name and rules. The mystic arts are mostly shown by what particular people can do, not by a rulebook; canon explains very little about how any of it works. Nation entries and the Alchemy, Art of Khemia, Adeptal Arts, Exorcism and Witchcraft entries carry the detail.

## in_universe
### LITE
Magic is not limited to Vision-bearers: Yelan says her clan has practised it for generations, and kitsune, adepti, tengu and aranara work wonders without Visions. Arts include divination, talismans, transformation, dream-magic, pocket dimensions, purification, alchemy and spirit-summoning.
### STANDARD +
Many traditions inherit from each other. The adepti's arts were taught to them by Rex Lapis. Liyue's exorcist arts look like derivatives of adeptal arts. Inazuma's onmyoudou was built by Kamuna Harunosuke 500 years ago from adeptal techniques and the tengu art of transcension. The Fantastic Compass, invented by the adeptus Sea Gazer, was handed to a mortal exorcist friend and later reached Yelan's family, which developed its own catalysts.
### LARGE +
The same expertise is often cumulative in one person: Alice is credited with alchemy, astrology, engineering, magic and medicine; Rhinedottir mixed Khemia with the Hexenzirkel's wider learning. Tools and devices (talismans, catalysts, compasses, nets, orbs) carry much of the work, often needing a specific trained user, such as an adeptus working with a mortal on the Fantastic Compass.

## mondstadt
### LITE
Alchemy is openly practised (bio-alchemy and Albedo's Art of Khemia). Astrology, including hydromancy, is practised by Barbeloth and Mona.
### LARGE +
Astrology rests on the belief that the stars record the destinies of all Teyvat's inhabitants, and Vision-bearers' destinies are thought to be tied to particular constellations. Those who come from outside Teyvat, such as the Traveler, cannot be read this way. Mona warns that reading your own fortune makes fate turn in on itself, and that giving advice to the subject risks entangling your fate with theirs. Hydromancy, a subclass practised by Barbeloth and Mona, reads the inverted reflection of the stars on water; Mona says the best water is the pure water of melted snow. Pyromancy, presumably a fire-based divination possibly connected with Phlogiston, is known only from a single diary.
### ULTRA +
Albedo's Khemia can also produce ordinary objects such as chairs. The Alchemy entry covers Mondstadt, Liyue, Sumeru and Snezhnayan alchemy in detail. Witchcraft (the Hexenzirkel) is also linked to Mondstadt, where several members have appeared, but it has its own entry.

## liyue
### LITE
The adepti's Adeptus Arts, taught to them by Rex Lapis (adeptal alchemy, dream-magic, talismans, pocket dimensions), plus exorcism, the Art of Homa and the Qimen arts.
### LARGE +
- Dream Trawler: also called the giving and receiving of dreams; Rex Lapis and Xiao are known users.
- Mortal Lingering: lets the user take a human form even after their original body has died. Pervases used it.
- Nuo Dance of Evil Conquering: known to be used by yakshas; the mask lets the wearer subdue a god's might, at the cost of great suffering.
- Sigils: Qiqi's body is full of adeptal power, and the talismans on her clothing keep it from making her berserk. Rex Lapis also made Sigils of Permission that let mortals channel adeptal power; Starsnatcher used one against evil spirits, and the Fatui later duplicated them.
- Sub-Space Creation is considered inferior to Rex Lapis's power to move mountains and seas. Adepti can also make Teapot Spirits as butlers for their abodes.
### ULTRA +
- Art of Homa: a secret technique to purify the border between life and death and burn away fetor leaking into the living world, using Homa altars and the Staff of Homa. Hu Tao says it demands great focus and no hesitation in the face of death; doing it wrongly can shorten or end one's life. The wiki says it was first used by the Lone Butterfly of the Crimson Flame, one of the Eight Adepts, whose sacrifice in the Seven-and-Eight-Gates Method purified Tao Dou; Wuwang Kid then handed her methods to the founders of the Wangsheng Funeral Parlor.
- Exorcism: Liyue's exorcists (thaumaturges) drive off ghosts, spirits and demons, and often learn the trade from their families. Known groups are the Tianheng Thaumaturges (Chongyun's clan), Yelan's clan and the Lan Clan (see the Exorcism entry).
- Fantastic Compass: a catalyst made by Sea Gazer to seal monsters; "one can move the heavens and shake the earth" when activated by an adeptus and a mortal together. Past users include Boyang and Bosacius, Yanfei (briefly), and Xiao with Yelan.
- Parametric Transformer: an elemental-powered device from the Dunyu Ruins that transmutes items into other items; its origin is unknown.
- Qimen arts: originated with the Wind Reader of the Eight Adepts, ancestor of Chenyu Vale's Twelve Tribes; fortune conveyance, fate transference, divination, illusion and warding. The Lan Clan of Chenyu Vale has practised them for generations; one art uses rattan figures and is Lan Yan's specialty.

## inazuma
### LITE
Inazuma has the kitsune and bake-danuki's shapeshifting magic (Yae Miko is a master of kitsune spells), onmyoudou (paper shikigami and shikifuda, largely lost) and the tengu's feather-fan arts.
### LARGE
- Bake-danuki magic: bake-danuki are tanuki with mystical knowledge, known for transforming into people or objects. Kichiboushi's trick "Eight tatami Kapow" made an air cushion he floated on in Dragonspine's cold winds.
- Kitsune magic: past kitsune were said to shapeshift. After the passing of the Kitsune Saiguu, Sky and Earth kitsune became stone statues and the race lost its power. Yae Miko, a Sky Kitsune of the Hakushin line, remains masterful. In "A Centuries-Long Dream" she performed a summoning ritual that made the Traveler a host for the spirit of another kitsune, Urakusai.
- Tengu arts: include the art of transcension, which was used in creating onmyoudou. Tengu warriors such as Kujou Sara use feather fans with magical effects.
### ULTRA +
- Onmyoudou: the Kamuna art Harunosuke created 500 years ago during the Cataclysm by combining Liyue's adeptal techniques with the art of transcension, which he learned under the Yougou Tengu. Most of it is lost. It centres on shikigami (paper cut-outs with magical power, such as Shiki Taishou) and shikifuda (paper seals focused on elemental power). The Kairagi, former samurai, still use shikifuda to put elements in their blades without a Vision.
- Mystic Onmyou Chamber: a shifting-room domain built with onmyoudou as a samurai training ground, now abandoned.
- Ubiquity Nets: made with Kamuna onmyoudou to capture small animals; the Omni-Ubiquity Net, Wakamurasaki's improvement, can hold the essence of a creature and recreate it, but neither net affects humans.

## sumeru
### LITE
Sumeru has the Akasha knowledge system, Akademiya alchemy and the Aranara's memory-powered Ararakalari.
### LARGE
- The Akasha: a Gnosis-powered knowledge system made by the former Dendro Archon Greater Lord Rukkhadevata and developed by the Akademiya. Akasha Terminals give users knowledge; Lesser Lord Kusanali calls herself the first Akasha Terminal. Terminals can also run without the Akasha from knowledge stored as "Canned Knowledge" in Knowledge Capsules.
- Alchemy: studied in the Spantamad Darshan, with alchemical divination and the Remelting Effect (see the Alchemy entry).
- Ararakalari: special abilities of the Aranara, drawn from their memories and powered by forest plants. They come from the "Mahamaitri Kusa Stotra", an Aranara song in praise of Queen Aranyani, humans and themselves.
### ULTRA +
- Each Aranara has a unique Ararakalari. Strong ones can attack, teleport or shield against the Withering, but each use costs some of the Aranara's memories. They can gain new abilities from new memories, including borrowed ones: Arama obtained Aramuhukunda's teleportation by receiving his memories. A Kusava seed strengthens an Ararakalari and lets humans, including the Traveler, use one briefly.
- Eremite Spirits of Omen: Eremite Stone Enchanters and Galehunters can summon spirits sealed in their weapons (the Stone Drake and the Wind Glede).
- Wisdom Orb: a Fungus-capturing device invented by the Amurta researcher Kautilya for the Nilotpala Cup; the wiki says it was repurposed from earlier experimental devices and concealed a backdoor command. It resembles the Omni-Ubiquity Net, which the Akademiya investigators noted, tracing both to Liyue's adeptal arts.

## other_regions
### STANDARD
The wiki page covers only Mondstadt, Liyue, Inazuma and Sumeru. Other nations have their own non-Vision traditions that live in separate entries: Natlan's Nightsoul, Nod-Krai's Moonsign and Moonchanters, and Khaenri'ah's Khemia. Fontaine's mystic traditions are not covered by the wiki page.
### LARGE +
Phlogiston, the Delusions, the Gnoses and Abyssal power are treated as cosmological powers and have their own entries, not as "mystic arts" in the wiki sense.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay interpretation (this project's recommendation, not canon text): treat each tradition as belonging to a place, a lineage and a way of thinking, not as a generic spell list. Liyue's arts are talismans, formulas and discipline; Inazuma's are paper charms and transformations; Sumeru's are knowledge, memory and lectures; Mondstadt's are lab benches and star charts. Magic should cost something: focus, memory, health, a rare tool or a partner.
### LARGE +
Reserve godlike feats for Archons, adepti and the Hexenzirkel. A mortal exorcist can seal, ward and banish; they cannot reshape land. Magic does not need a Vision, and a Vision is not a spell: keep elemental power and mystic arts distinct in description.
### ULTRA +
Do not let the Traveler or any other character casually learn secret arts (Homa, Khemia, onmyoudou) without canon support. Do not give ordinary kitsune or tanuki Yae Miko's power. Do not treat the wiki grouping as a single magic system.

## limits
### STANDARD
Canon never defines a unified magic system. Arts are tied to teachers and lineages and some are lost (most onmyoudou; kitsune power after the Saiguu). Some carry serious risks: adeptal elixirs kill non-adepti, the Nuo Dance inflicts great suffering, Homa can shorten a life, and astrologers risk entangling their own fate.
### LARGE +
The question of how a non-Vision user performs magic is unanswered; every exorcist seen using exorcist arts in-game holds a Vision.

## entry: Astrology and Hydromancy
@keys: astrology, astrologist, astromancy, hydromancy, hydromancer, pyromancy
### STANDARD
Astrology infers fate from the movements of stars. Barbeloth and her student Mona practise it; hydromancy is the subclass in which the starlight's inverted reflection on water is read. Mona says reading your own fate makes it turn in on itself and advising a subject can entangle your fates. The Traveler's fate cannot be read this way. Pyromancy is a presumed fire divination known only from a single note.
### LARGE +
In the Witch's Revelation system, Barbeloth's case of oracles and the Lunar Arcana are tied to her readings (see the Witchcraft entry).

## entry: Adeptus Arts
@keys: Adeptus Arts, adeptal arts, Dream Trawler, Mortal Lingering, Nuo Dance of Evil Conquering, Sub-Space Creation, Teapot Spirit, Sigils of Permission
### STANDARD
The adepti's arts were taught to them by Rex Lapis. Besides controlling the elements, adepti can make elixirs, give or receive dreams, remain in human form after death, use yaksha masks, store power in talismans, and create pocket dimensions with teapot-spirit servants. See the Adeptal Arts entry.
### LARGE +
The Fatui duplicated Sigils of Permission through research, and Tartaglia used many of them with his Harbinger power to reawaken Osial.

## entry: Art of Homa
@keys: Art of Homa, Homa altar, Homa Altars
### STANDARD
A secret technique of the Wangsheng Funeral Parlor that purifies fetor, the leakage of death into the world of the living. Hu Tao says it needs great focus and no hesitation; mistakes can shorten the practitioner's life. Wuwang Kid passed it to the Parlor's founders after the Lone Butterfly of the Crimson Flame's death.

## entry: Qimen Arts
@keys: Qimen Arts, Qimen Divination, Pentad Fortune Conveyance, Lan Clan
### STANDARD
A set of Liyue techniques including fortune-telling, fate transference, illusion-making and warding off evil, originated by the Wind Reader of the Eight Adepts. The Lan Clan of Chenyu Vale uses them for both duty and craft; one form uses rattan figures. Lan Junming also taught a Soul-Binding Art that was later outlawed.

## entry: Exorcists
@keys: exorcist, exorcists, thaumaturge, thaumaturges, Tianheng Thaumaturges
### STANDARD
Liyue's exorcists (thaumaturges) swear to cleanse the land of ghosts, spirits and demons and are skilled in combat, magic and martial arts. Most come from families that hold the trade, among them the Tianheng Thaumaturges (Chongyun, and Shenhe's mother), Yelan's clan and the Lan Clan. Their arts seem derived from adeptal arts; every exorcist seen using them in-game has a Vision. See the Exorcism entry.

## entry: Onmyoudou
@keys: onmyoudou, shikigami, shikifuda, Kamuna Harunosuke, Mystic Onmyou Chamber, Ubiquity Net
### STANDARD
Inazuma's Kamuna art of paper-based charm magic, created by Kamuna Harunosuke 500 years ago from adeptal techniques and the tengu art of transcension. Shikigami are enchanted paper cut-outs; shikifuda are elemental seals. It is largely lost, though the Kairagi use shikifuda, and its techniques survive in devices like the Ubiquity Nets and the Mystic Onmyou Chamber.

## entry: Ararakalari
@keys: Ararakalari, Kusava, Mahamaitri Kusa Stotra
### STANDARD
The Aranara's memory-fuelled abilities. Each Aranara has a unique power that can attack, teleport or shield against the Withering, spends their memories when used, and can grow with new memories. A Kusava seed amplifies it and lets humans use it briefly.

## entry: Akasha System
@keys: Akasha, Akasha Terminal, Canned Knowledge, Knowledge Capsule
### STANDARD
Sumeru's Gnosis-powered knowledge network, created by Greater Lord Rukkhadevata and developed by the Akademiya. A terminal gives its user any knowledge they seek; Lesser Lord Kusanali calls herself the first Akasha Terminal. Terminals can also work without the Akasha, from stored Canned Knowledge in Knowledge Capsules.
