## summary
### LITE
A Stellar Linchpin is a Vision-like object from Snezhnaya. Like a Vision, it lets its bearer channel elemental power, but Stellar Linchpin users often show a kind of power that seems to come from beyond Teyvat. The Fatui believe Linchpins are tied to the remains of the Third Descender, and they study them for their weapon against the Heavenly Principles (Project Mistilteinn). Known users are Sandrone (Cryo), Odette (Cryo), Vesna (Anemo), Danica (Electro) and Mitya (Electro). Linchpin bearers can enable the Stellar Glimmer reactions.
### STANDARD +
It is not a Vision, a Gnosis or a Moon Wheel, and should not be called a Vision in narration. Sandrone, introducing the idea in 2026's Sunny Summer Fontinalia event (6.7), describes it as "a rare kind of Vision."

## in_universe
### LITE
Visions are said to be a means of channelling elemental power for those who hold them. In Sandrone's explanation (as Marionette, in the 6.7 event), the same is true of Linchpins, with one difference: Linchpin users often exhibit a unique kind of power that seems to come from beyond Teyvat. What makes a Linchpin appear is not known.
### STANDARD +
Appearance in canon is gradual and personal. Sandrone found hers waiting on her workbench after her damaged body rebooted. Odette's formed in the "cold glimmer" backstage after a performance. Vesna's voice-over treats Linchpins as pretty collectible objects. Mitya wears his on a golden garter on the side of his left thigh, according to the wiki.
### LARGE +
Sandrone says the Fatui work from the idea that Visions came from the same system that produced the Gnoses (themselves made from the Third Descender's remains), so a Vision's manifestation is also, to some extent, related to the Third Descender. Linchpins share that origin in stronger form. In Fatui research as Sandrone relays it, when a Linchpin manifests, impurities from the remains get fixed in place, which is where the name comes from and also what is thought to be the source of their strange power. This is Fatui belief, not something shown directly in the story.
### ULTRA +
Paimon and the Traveler had not heard of Linchpins before this conversation, though they already knew the remains of the Third Descender were used for the Gnoses. Sandrone is unsure whether the Linchpin has any lasting use for her: her character story has her deciding to use its Cryo as a cooling well for herself and her mechanism Fagio during heavy computing, and she is grateful that it set off Alain's gift, her second life.

## the_third_descender_link
@version: 6.7
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
In Fatui research as relayed by Sandrone (Marionette), Stellar Linchpins are related to the Third Descender. When one manifests, impurities from his remains get fixed in place. In canon, the Heavenly Principles used his remains to create the seven Gnoses, which consolidated the Archon system; Visions come from that same system.
### LARGE +
The Third Descender's name is Saarelainen, and the wiki credits the introduction of Stellar Glimmer reactions to ancient Snezhnaya to him. Treat the Linchpin link as a belief of Snezhnayan researchers and some in-world voices, not a proven mechanism.

## origins_in_users
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
How each known user got a Linchpin, as the sources tell it:
- Sandrone: she was badly damaged in a fight with Il Dottore, and a Linchpin appeared and activated a built-in "Final Gift" left by her creator Alain Guillotin, a second chance for a body that would otherwise have shut down. After her core rebooted, she repaired herself and imported her old numerological array and some surviving algorithms from Pulonia, who was beyond repair. She says she has no idea why it appeared for her.
- Odette: in her character story she had trained until she felt no pain and, after a performance, found an ice-blue crystal forming in the cold glimmer backstage. She recognises it as a Linchpin from what she heard among the Fatui, and accepts it. In her voice-over she describes waking from near-unconsciousness with frostbite and seeing a light from a Linchpin in front of her, and wonders whether it was her will to survive that woke her.
- Vesna: her "Stellar Linchpin" character story is about the death of her teacher, the Captain, and her grief; the text I reviewed does not say how her Linchpin came to her.
- Danica and Mitya: wiki infoboxes list Linchpins as their element source (Electro for both). Mitya also reverse-engineered Linchpin energy for Project Mistilteinn.
### ULTRA +
Sandrone's story also says the Linchpin was necessary for the gift to trigger: the damage to her core was too severe, and without it the gift would not have fired. She says it was almost wasted on the fight with Dottore.

## project_mistilteinn
@version: 7.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Pierro of the Fatui explains in the Archon Quest "Wraith's Nocturne" (Act II, "Mistilteinn", 7.0) that elemental energy is the Human Realm's power, converted by the Heavenly Principles themselves, so using it against its creator would be futile. The Fatui therefore turned to the energy associated with the Third Descender and the Linchpins. By reverse-engineering Linchpins to extract their energy, they have what Pierro calls a source of "anti-Celestia energy."
### LARGE +
The wiki describes Project Mistilteinn as a weapon allowing the Tsaritsa to fight the Heavenly Principles, overseen by Pierro and the Armory Palace, with Project Stuzha regulating Snezhnaya's energy consumption to feed it. Mitya, a scientist of the Royal Energy Commission of Snezhnaya, is the one in charge of energy research and the extraction of Linchpin energy into Anti-Celestia Energy.

## appearance
### STANDARD
A Stellar Linchpin is shaped like a four-pointed star, similar to the shape of a Primogem, set in a star-shaped frame. Its casing resembles a Snezhnayan Vision's, with two rhombuses beneath the star. Mitya's is Electro and sits on his left thigh; Odette's was described as ice-blue, cold and almost weightless, no heavier than a frozen tear.
### LARGE +
Aloy's Vision has a similar four-pointed star in a gold circular frame, but the wiki says it is not established as a Stellar Linchpin and works like a normal Vision. Vesna's voice-over calls Linchpins "beautifully designed" and "absolutely a collector's item," adding that Moon Wheels aren't bad either and that she is not interested in Visions at the moment.

## known_users
### LITE
Sandrone (Cryo), Odette (Cryo), Vesna (Anemo), Danica (Electro), Mitya (Electro).
### STANDARD +
- Sandrone: Seventh of the Eleven Fatui Harbingers, artificer; her Linchpin appeared in the 6.7 event.
- Odette: prima ballerina of the Korolevskiy Troupe, Fatui; released 7.0.
- Vesna: Commander-in-chief of the Druzhna; Anemo user, released 7.1.
- Danica: head housemaid of the royal house.
- Mitya: scientist of the Royal Energy Commission.
Their personal stories belong to their own entries.
### LARGE +
Linchpin users appear mostly in Snezhnayan service: the Fatui, the Druzhna, the royal household and its research organisations. Sandrone, Odette and the Traveler (Cryo) are the Cryo set. Vesna is the Anemo user, and the Electro users Danica and Mitya appear in the Archon Quest (Mitya is named a Linchpin user in the 7.0 known-issues note).

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not literal lore): Characters with a Linchpin have a Stellar Jubilee Passive, which lets them enable Stellar Glimmer reactions. The Traveler (Cryo) has them too.
### LARGE +
Stellar-Conduct (Superconduct converted; introduced 6.7 with Sandrone) is enabled by Sandrone and Odette. Stellar Swirl (Cryo Swirl converted; introduced 7.0) is enabled by Odette and Vesna. Stellar Glimmer reactions are a subset of elemental reactions. A talent that converts the matching Cryo-related reaction must be present in the party. The wiki credits ancient Snezhnaya as the setting where Saarelainen introduced these reactions.

## limits
### STANDARD
A Stellar Linchpin is not a Vision, and is not a substitute for a Gnosis. Sandrone herself uses it modestly: it supplies Cryo, which she uses to cool her systems, and she does not find that it solves problems for her. No canon text says a Linchpin makes its holder more than a skilled Vision-like elemental user.
### LARGE +
Linchpins are said to carry a power from beyond Teyvat, yet no source quantifies it. The Fatui treat them as raw material for the anti-Celestia energy that is meant to threaten the Heavenly Principles, but whether Mistilteinn works is not shown as of 7.1.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay interpretation (this project's recommendation, not canon text): describe the Linchpin as a small, cold-looking star carried on the body like a Vision. Do not have an ordinary bystander name it correctly; it is a Snezhnayan matter, and Paimon needed Sandrone to explain it.
### LARGE +
Characters who know Fatui research may mention the Third Descender link, but should treat it as theory. Let users react personally: Sandrone with dry practicality, Odette with quiet acceptance, Vesna with an aesthete's curiosity.
### ULTRA +
Avoid inventing wishes that "caused" a Linchpin for a character whose story does not say so, and avoid giving other nations' Vision holders a Linchpin.
