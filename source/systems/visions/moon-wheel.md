## summary
### LITE
A Moon Wheel is a Vision-like gem associated with Nod-Krai and the Three Moons. It is "formed from the stuff of the soul" and lets its bearer manipulate the elements like a Vision does, but it draws on kuuvahki, an ancient and purer form of elemental power, instead of ordinary elemental energy. In Nod-Krai a Moon Wheel looks identical to a Vision.
### STANDARD +
All playable Moon Wheel users hold a Moonsign Benediction passive, the talent that links them to the Moonsign mechanic and to Lunar Reactions (own entries). The known users are Ineffa, Flins, Lauma, Linnea, Nefer and Zibai.

## in_universe
### LITE
Moon Wheels work like Visions in practice: the bearer wears the gem and channels one element. The difference is the energy behind it. Kuuvahki is described as a primordial form of elemental power, the power of the Three Moons.
### STANDARD +
The wiki's lore summary is that the Heavenly Principles and the Three Moon Sisters originally created Moon Wheels, and that they were given to the Moon Goddesses' human followers so the Goddesses could talk with them during rituals. A Natlan-era note in Ineffa's story calls the Moon Wheel a plaything the "traitors" (the Three Moons) gave their human followers to communicate with, and says it relies on a purer power rather than on elemental energy "tainted" by the usurper, the Heavenly Principles.
### LARGE +
The same note records an experiment on a Vision, concluding that, like the northern ritual items, it is "formed from the stuff of the soul", with its form fixed through wishes so that it can call forth elemental power. The writer asks whether this is why Visions have recently appeared alongside human wishes. The writer served the Sovereign Lords of Natlan and worked from a Vision taken at Ek' Pulinik, so this is one in-world researcher's hypothesis. Lauma's story adds a human-scale puzzle: she wonders whether Moon Wheels even existed when the three moons stood in the sky.

## appearance
### STANDARD
A Nod-Krai Moon Wheel is an orb-shaped elemental gem set in the upper part of a circular frame, with a crescent-like small circle and four rhombuses on the lower half. It is visually identical to a Nod-Krai Vision.
### LARGE +
Zibai, the only known user from outside Nod-Krai (she is from Liyue), has a slightly different frame: the crescent-like circle has an inverted colour scheme and the four rhombuses are missing. Her story says Istaroth, the shade of Time, gave her the Moon Wheel, which may explain the difference; the wiki lists that as one possible reason, not a stated one.

## origins
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Item text from the Aubade of Morningstar and Moon artifact set tells of an age of three moons when people, frightened by questions the gods' envoys would not answer, turned to the Three Moons, who answered with madness and inspiration beneath the moon. An archpriestess then returned from a mountain summit with a small circular talisman, called the "Moon Wheel", the new pact between the three moons and the people.
### LARGE +
Moon Envoys were servants of the Heavenly Principles who also worked with the Moon Goddesses. In Zibai's case, a Moon Wheel marked her new post: she then served both Celestia and the Moons, travelling between the Lunar Palace, Celestia and her home Lang-Gan. Her story says she sensed that the Moons' love for the world differed from that of the morning stars (the Heavenly Principles): moonlight settled gently on each scorched plant rather than only on the sacred ones.

## users
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Playable Moon Wheel users: Ineffa (Electro), Flins (Electro), Lauma (Dendro), Linnea (Geo), Nefer (Dendro) and Zibai (Geo). Sigurd's Electro gem might also be a Moon Wheel; this is unknown.
### LARGE +
- Ineffa: the only user the player sees receive a Moon Wheel on screen. Her character story says the Moon Wheel inside her core, when it began to glow, brought back her memories.
- Flins: his story says a Moon Wheel appeared suddenly in his showroom one night, a gift that matched his coat and served as cover for his power and flames. He first told Illuga he got it at an auction. As a Lantern Fae he does not need one for his power.
- Lauma: has had hers since birth; it reportedly fell from her swaddling clothes. It troubles her that she cannot remember how she got it, and she regards it as a symbol of vigilance, restraint and responsibility.
- Linnea: found a plain gray stone on a seabed-ruin expedition; the glutton Lumi swallowed it, and afterwards it turned out to be her Moon Wheel. She keeps it as an ornament.
- Nefer: it appeared at the bottom of a gift box from a Northern Intelligence Network emissary, in the quest "An Elegy for Faded Moonlight". She links it to Thoth, who told her tales of labouring across the three moons, although she believes none of his claims.
- Zibai: see origins; a Moon Envoy's mark.
### ULTRA +
Release notes for the wiki's infobox dates: Ineffa, the first user, released on 2025-07-30 (Version 5.8), Zibai on 2026-02-03 and Linnea on 2026-04-08. The wiki also notes that the Moon Wheel users the player actually watches receiving a gem on screen are limited to Ineffa; with Visions included, Rana, the Wanderer and Furina also receive theirs on screen. Nothing in canon as of 7.1 says a Moon Wheel has been taken from a user or destroyed, and Ineffa's story is the only one that places a Moon Wheel inside a machine rather than on a person.

## kuuvahki_link
### STANDARD
Kuuvahki is found only in Nod-Krai and is described as the power of the Light Realm in its purest form. Lunar Reactions use kuuvahki, and Moon Wheel users enable them through their Moonsign Benediction passives.
### LARGE +
Per the wiki, kuuvahki exists in Nod-Krai either because Columbina poured her power out over the land, or because two of the Three Moons shattered above it. Kuuvahki of sufficient strength can counter or convert Abyssal power, but a stronger source of Abyssal energy will suppress the weaker kuuvahki instead (Flins's remark in "The Ashen-White Order Burns"). Moon Wheels and kuuvahki are separate things: the wheel is the gem, kuuvahki the energy.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay, not literal lore: Moon Wheel users are playable characters from Nod-Krai (plus Zibai) who carry a Moonsign Benediction passive. Each passive raises the party's Moonsign level by 1, and Moon Wheel users enable and strengthen Lunar Reactions.
### LARGE +
Lunar Reactions are a subset of elemental reactions (Lunar-Bloom, Lunar-Charged, Lunar-Crystallize) which, unlike their base reactions, can CRIT. A character with a Moonsign Benediction passive that converts specific Hydro-related reactions must be in the party to trigger them.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay interpretation (this project's recommendation, not canon text): treat a Moon Wheel in a scene like a Vision, a gem at the hip or back that glows with its element. Pale, silvery light is a safe cue for kuuvahki.
### LARGE +
Most Nod-Krai citizens would not tell a Vision from a Moon Wheel by sight; the difference matters to scholars and to people who know the Moons' history. A character who learns what a Moon Wheel is might be uneasy about its origin, as Lauma is. Do not claim a given character's wheel was a gift from a specific being unless their story says so.

## limits
### STANDARD
Canon does not say who can receive a Moon Wheel, whether it needs a particular wish or desire (as Visions are said to), or whether a Moon Wheel and a Vision can be held together. It does not say an ordinary person can buy one.
### LARGE +
Do not state that Columbina hands out Moon Wheels, or that the Moon Goddesses personally chose any current user. Only Zibai's story ties her wheel to a Moon-related figure (Istaroth).

## entry: Zibai's Moon Wheel
@keys: Zibai's Moon Wheel, Moon Envoy of Lang-Gan
@keys2: Moon Wheel
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
Zibai, the White Horse Adeptus, was an Angel who guided Lang-Gan before Liyue. Her wiki history says Istaroth gave her a Moon Wheel as a symbol of becoming a Moon Envoy of the Lunar Palace, assigned to Sonnet, goddess of the Iridescent Moon. Her own story tells it more ambiguously: the shade of Time placed before her a crest engraved with the moons' sigil, and she at first treated it as unnecessary to her mission. Her Moon Wheel has a different frame from Nod-Krai ones.

## entry: Ineffa's Moon Wheel
@keys: Ineffa's Moon Wheel
@keys2: Moon Wheel
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
In Ineffa's story the Moon Wheel in her core glows and her memories return. A Natlan-era note describes a Moon Wheel acquired for study, and a scene in the same story has Lianca fit a gem that once belonged to her mother, a gem that suited the core's phlogiston power. Treat the exact chain of ownership as only partly told.
