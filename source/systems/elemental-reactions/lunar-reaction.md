## summary
### LITE
Lunar Reactions are a family of three elemental reactions that appear when a party contains a character whose Moonsign Benediction passive allows them. Each one takes a normal Hydro-related reaction (Electro-Charged, Bloom, Crystallize) and replaces it with a Moon-themed version: Lunar-Charged, Lunar-Bloom and Lunar-Crystallize. Unlike their base versions, Lunar Reactions can land critical hits. They belong to the Nod-Krai era of the game.
### STANDARD +
They arrived in stages: Lunar-Charged in Version 5.8, Lunar-Bloom in Version 6.0 (Luna I) and Lunar-Crystallize in Version 6.3 (Luna IV). Lunar-Charged and Lunar-Crystallize can be set off by simply applying the right elements; Lunar-Bloom does no damage by itself and works through character talents and constellations.

## in_universe
### LITE
Canon offers no in-world explanation. Lunar Reactions are presented as tutorial and combat material. Treat them as a gameplay abstraction: a scene may borrow the look (moonlit clouds, drifting stones, glowing dew) without naming an in-world law.
### STANDARD +
Related setting, from the wiki: the reactions are tied to Moonsign, a party bonus keyed to characters of Nod-Krai (plus Zibai), and their enabling passives are called Moonsign Benedictions. Columbina, the new Moon Goddess in the wiki's account, and users of the Moon Wheel are the ones whose passives turn the reactions on. Nod-Krai is also where kuuvahki, described as the power of the Three Moons, is found.
### LARGE +
The wiki's Kuuvahki page describes that power as a primordial form of elemental energy tied to the Three Moons, found only in Nod-Krai. It does not say that Lunar Reactions are kuuvahki, so this entry does not either; see the uncertainties for the project brief's wording.

## gameplay
### LITE
Gameplay (not literal lore): while a character with a matching Moonsign Benediction is in the party, three Hydro-related reactions are replaced. Electro and Hydro give Lunar-Charged instead of Electro-Charged; Dendro and Hydro give Lunar-Bloom instead of Bloom; Geo and Hydro give Lunar-Crystallize instead of Hydro Crystallize.
### STANDARD +
- Lunar Reactions can land critical hits, which their base versions cannot.
- Lunar-Charged makes a thundercloud, Lunar-Bloom gives Verdant Dew, and Lunar-Crystallize makes Moondrifts (each has its own entry).
- Lunar-Charged and Lunar-Crystallize can deal damage through a separate instance when elements are applied, using the stats of every character that contributed an element. Lunar-Bloom damage comes only from talents and constellations.
### LARGE +
- Damage uses a shared formula with the Stellar Glimmer reactions. Its elemental mastery bonus is 6 x EM / (EM + 2000), and the base reaction coefficient is 3 for Lunar-Charged, 1.6 for Lunar-Crystallize and 1 for Lunar-Bloom (the last only when a talent deals it directly).
- Only Moonsign Benediction passives of Moon Wheel users and Columbina give a Lunar Reaction Base DMG Bonus: up to 7% for Columbina and up to 14% for the others the wiki lists.
- Moonsign levels: one Moonsign character gives Nascent Gleam; two give Ascendant Gleam, which strengthens Moonsign characters and lets non-Moonsign characters raise Lunar Reaction damage for 20 seconds after a skill or burst, up to 36%, scaled by an element-specific stat.
### ULTRA +
- Elemental resonances also react to the family: High Voltage (two Electro) can generate Electro particles on Lunar-Charged; Enduring Rock (two Geo) buffs allies near Moondrifts; Sprawling Greenery (two Dendro) grants Elemental Mastery after Lunar-Bloom.
- The 6.0 and 6.3 patch notes show the reactions in limited modes: limited-time modes and a Spiral Abyss ley line disorder raised Lunar Reaction damage (50% or 75% in the cases noted), and a 6.3 fix corrected an issue where the 50% Lunar boost on Floors 9 and 10 did not apply to Lunar-Crystallize.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay interpretation (this project's recommendation, not canon text): give Lunar Reactions a cool, silvery cast. A thundercloud drifting between fighters, a ring of floating stones, dew beading on leaves under moonlight. Keep them tied to someone with a Moonsign Benediction, since that is what the game requires.
### LARGE +
Characters in Nod-Krai may talk about moonlight and kuuvahki, but no named character in the sources read calls these "Lunar Reactions". Use the term in out-of-character or system-minded narration, not as an in-world discipline taught to ordinary people.
### ULTRA +
Do not claim Lunar Reactions are kuuvahki powered, caused by Columbina's will, or that ordinary Hydro reactions stop working in Nod-Krai. Canon says none of these.

## limits
### LITE
Without a Moonsign Benediction character in the party, nothing changes and the ordinary reactions occur.
### STANDARD +
Lunar-Bloom does not make its own damage when it triggers; the Dendro Cores it creates are ordinary Bloom cores and do not count as Lunar-Bloom damage.
### LARGE +
The family is Hydro-based only. Pyro, Cryo and Anemo have their own Stellar Glimmer family (Stellar-Conduct, Stellar Swirl), which is a separate set of reactions.
