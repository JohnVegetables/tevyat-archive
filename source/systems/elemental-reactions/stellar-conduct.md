## summary
### LITE
Stellar-Conduct is a Stellar Glimmer reaction from Snezhnaya. It happens when Cryo is applied to a target already affected by Electro, or the other way round, while a character with the matching Stellar Jubilee passive is in the party. It then replaces Superconduct. The reaction creates a Starlight Prism, which briefly turns the surrounding area into a Polestar Field.
### STANDARD +
It was added in Version Luna VIII (6.7), listed among the new systems of that version, and is the first of the two Stellar Glimmer reactions; Stellar Swirl followed in 7.0.

## in_universe
### LITE
Canon says nothing in-world about the prism or the field. The only lore note concerns the whole family: Stellar Glimmer reactions are said to have been introduced to ancient Snezhnaya by Saarelainen, the Third Descender, and the wiki cites a Version 7.0 livestream for that. Do not treat it as detailed history.

## gameplay
### LITE
Gameplay (not literal lore): Stellar-Conduct does no damage by itself. Damage comes only from character talents and constellations, and may be Cryo or Electro depending on the source.
### STANDARD +
After the reaction, the Starlight Prism lasts 7 seconds and keeps a Polestar Field around it. Inside the field, Cryo and Electro hits on enemies are recorded by the Prism; the hit that created it does not count. Every 4 seconds the count is reset and buffs lasting 4 seconds are given. Characters inside the field gain a Cryo and Electro damage bonus (20%, or 28% plus 1% per recorded hit if any hit was recorded, up to 12 hits). Enemies in the field lose 40% Physical RES. The reaction's base coefficient rises by 40% plus 5% per recorded hit, up to 12 hits.
### LARGE +
In the Snezhnayan open world, characters inside the field lose no heat and no Kresnik Energy, and Kresnik Energy under 30% is restored to 30%. Prime Ice Constructs and firearms get extra effects inside a Polestar Field. A Prism left over while the player is out of combat turns into a Prism Mirage after it expires. The player can activate it again, and it then lasts indefinitely, behaves like a Prime Ice Construct (placement limits, co-op construct, likes) and can be removed by interacting with it or from the map.
### ULTRA +
Version 7.0's Spiral Abyss gave one Blessing of the Abyssal Moon that made attacks inside a Polestar Field release a True DMG shockwave once every 4 seconds, and floor effects raised Stellar-Conduct damage by 50% and 75% in different halves. Version 7.0 also lowered the visual effects of the reaction at Low graphics settings. These are patch details, not canon.

## interactions
### STANDARD
Stellar-Conduct replaces Superconduct when the matching passive is active, so Superconduct's usual outcome does not happen in that party. Stellar Swirl is the other Stellar Glimmer reaction.
### LARGE +
Stellar Glimmer reactions also disable certain attacks of Snowland Fae and Aberrant Chimeric Monsters. The reaction deals damage only through talents and constellations; the wiki lists the talents, constellations, weapons and artifacts that enhance it.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay interpretation (this project's recommendation, not canon text): show the Prism as a small star-like shard of light that hangs in the air above a frozen, crackling spot, with a faint ring of light around it. Allies inside it should feel steadier, and cold or heat should be easier to bear in Snezhnaya. Keep the scale at the size of a fight.
### LARGE +
Do not treat Stellar-Conduct as something an ordinary Cryo or Electro Vision holder can do. It needs a Stellar Linchpin user, or the Cryo Traveler, present. Do not give it a made-up lore origin beyond what the family page says.

## limits
### LITE
As of 7.1, no canon explains the reaction beyond its name and the family-level Saarelainen note.
### STANDARD +
Stellar-Conduct needs a Stellar Jubilee passive in the party. It is not a Lunar reaction.

## entry: Starlight Prism and Polestar Field
@keys: Starlight Prism, Polestar Field, Prism Mirage
@version: 6.7
### STANDARD
The Starlight Prism is the object created by Stellar-Conduct. It lasts 7 seconds and sustains a Polestar Field, in which Cryo and Electro hits are recorded and then turned into party buffs. In the Snezhnayan open world, an unused Prism turns into a Prism Mirage that can be reactivated and then behaves like a Prime Ice Construct.
