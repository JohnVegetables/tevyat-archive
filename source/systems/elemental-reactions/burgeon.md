## summary
### LITE
Burgeon is a Dendro elemental reaction. A Dendro Core (the seed-like orb a Bloom leaves behind) that is struck by Pyro explodes immediately, in a bigger burst of Dendro damage than the core would deal on its own. It is one of the two ways to cash in a Bloom early, the other being Hyperbloom.
### STANDARD +
It debuted in Version 3.0 together with Bloom, Hyperbloom, Quicken, Spread and Aggravate, the reactions introduced for Dendro. The reaction cannot happen on its own: a Hydro and Dendro Bloom has to make the core first.

## in_universe
### LITE
Canon gives no in-world explanation for Burgeon. The pages read treat it purely as a combat rule, so it is best understood as what fire does to a ripe seed of plant energy.

## gameplay
### LITE
Gameplay: when Pyro is applied to a Dendro Core, Burgeon triggers. It uses up the core and deals Dendro area damage, wider and heavier than an unaided core burst.
### STANDARD +
- A Dendro Core normally explodes by itself when its time runs out, but only if the character who made it is still in the party and alive. Burgeon detonates it early, in a 5 m radius.
- The wiki says the damage hits enemies and players alike; damage to players from Bloom-related reactions is 2% of the damage to enemies.
- It can be triggered by a party other than the one that made the Bloom, including the environment, and its damage scales with the party that applied the Pyro.
- Burgeon is a transformative reaction: it does its own damage, based on the triggering character's level and Elemental Mastery, and ignores ATK and cannot critically strike unless a talent or constellation allows it.
### LARGE +
- A target can take only two instances of damage from each Bloom-related reaction per source every 0.5 seconds, and that limit is separate for each triggering character. Dendro Core explosions and Bountiful Core explosions are counted separately.
- Burgeon cannot be triggered on Bountiful Cores, which are the quick-bursting cores some abilities and event buffs make instead of ordinary Dendro Cores. They share the limit of five cores with normal ones.
- Hyperbloom, the Electro version, sends the core as a homing Sprawling Shot instead of exploding it where it lies. At most five Dendro Cores can exist at once.
### ULTRA +
- Version 3.0 added the Dendro Elemental Resonance Sprawling Greenery, which gives nearby party members 20 Elemental Mastery for 6 seconds after Burgeon (or Spread, Aggravate, Hyperbloom), against 30 for Burning, Quicken or Bloom.
- The wiki lists talents, constellations and artifacts that raise Burgeon damage, let it critically strike or add flat damage; these are character-specific and not detailed here.
- Lunar-Bloom (the Moonsign version of the Dendro-Hydro reaction, which the wiki says replaces Bloom) also spawns a core. Treat Lunar-Bloom as a separate reaction.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay interpretation (this project's recommendation, not canon text): show a green, lumpy seed of energy hanging in the air, then a flame touching it and the seed bursting outward in a flare of leaves, sap and heat. Keep it a sudden, loud, fairly local blast.
### LARGE +
It reads best as a team move: one person coaxes the seed out with water and plants, another flicks fire at it. Make the Pyro user's contribution matter; the burst is theirs. Scale it to a skirmish, not a firestorm that levels a village.
### ULTRA +
Avoid having Burgeon work without a core being present, avoid claiming a Dendro-only or Pyro-only user can trigger it alone, and avoid giving it an origin story. Do not describe it as destroying forests; the effect is a short, small blast.

## limits
### LITE
Burgeon needs a Dendro Core, so it needs Bloom first. It does nothing to a Bountiful Core.
### STANDARD +
The explosion can hurt the party too, though far less than it hurts enemies. The core vanishes after its duration if its creator has left the party.

## examples
### STANDARD
Gameplay example: Hydro and Dendro together leave a Dendro Core. A Pyro attack on it triggers Burgeon at once and the core blows up in a wide Dendro burst, catching enemies close together.

## entry: Dendro Core

### STANDARD
A Dendro Core is what the Bloom reaction (Dendro with Hydro) leaves behind. If left alone it bursts after about six seconds. Pyro on it triggers Burgeon, Electro triggers Hyperbloom. No more than five can exist at a time.
