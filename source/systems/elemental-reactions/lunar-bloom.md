## summary
### LITE
Lunar-Bloom is the Lunar Reaction between Dendro and Hydro, in either order. When the party includes a character whose Moonsign Benediction passive enables it, ordinary Bloom is replaced by Lunar-Bloom. By itself it does no damage. Instead it gives the party Verdant Dew, a stored resource certain characters spend to deal Lunar-Bloom damage, which can crit. It arrived in Version 6.0 (Luna I) with Nod-Krai.
### STANDARD +
The Dendro Cores and Bountiful Cores that the reaction still creates are identical to those from Bloom. Damage from those cores is not counted as Lunar-Bloom damage.

## in_universe
### LITE
Canon offers no in-world explanation. Verdant Dew and Lunar-Bloom are shown in tutorials and combat, not discussed by characters in the sources read. Treat them as a gameplay abstraction.
### STANDARD +
Related setting: the reaction came with the Nod-Krai region and the Moonsign party bonus. It replaces Bloom, the Dendro and Hydro reaction from Version 3.0.

## gameplay
### LITE
Gameplay (not literal lore): Lunar-Bloom does no damage on its own. The only ways to deal Lunar-Bloom damage are certain character talents and constellations, which spend Verdant Dew.
### STANDARD +
- After Lunar-Bloom triggers, the party gains 1 Verdant Dew every 2.5 seconds for 2.5 seconds. The party can hold up to 3.
- Triggering Lunar-Bloom again while dew is being gained resets the duration.
- Verdant Dew lasts indefinitely. It persists through party changes, teleports between maps and domains, daily resets and game sessions.
### LARGE +
- Lunar-Bloom damage uses the shared Lunar formula with a base reaction coefficient of 1. It applies only when a talent deals it directly, so the elemental mastery bonus is 6 x EM / (EM + 2000) on top of any reaction bonus.
- The wiki lists Lauma (Nature's Chorus, a burst that deals Lunar-Bloom in up to 36 instances, and Constellation 2), Nefer (Dusklit Eaves) and Columbina as Moonsign sources of Lunar-Bloom bonus, and Baizhu and Aino as providing reaction bonus.
- Increases come from artifacts and weapons such as Flower of Paradise Lost, Night of the Sky's Unveiling, Silken Moon's Serenade, Blackmarrow Lantern and Nightweaver's Looking Glass.
### ULTRA +
- Sprawling Greenery resonance (two Dendro characters) gives party members Elemental Mastery for a short time after Lunar-Bloom triggers.
- Event and mode boosts have applied to it: the 6.0 notes list 75% Lunar-Bloom boosts alongside 200% Bloom boosts in the same half of a limited-time mode.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay interpretation (this project's recommendation, not canon text): present Lunar-Bloom as the same seed-like burst as Bloom with an added pale shine, and Verdant Dew as small drops that gather and are spent. Keep it tied to a team that includes a Moonsign Benediction user.
### LARGE +
Because the dew is stored and carried between fights and sessions, a scene may show characters keeping a little of it after a battle. This is an interpretation of the gameplay rule, not something canon narrates.
### ULTRA +
Do not claim Verdant Dew is a real substance sold or gathered by Nod-Krai residents, or that Lauma invented the reaction. Neither is stated in the sources read.

## limits
### LITE
Lunar-Bloom does not deal damage when it triggers; the dew must be spent by a character that can use it.
### STANDARD +
Dew caps at 3. Dendro Core and Bountiful Core damage is not Lunar-Bloom damage.
### LARGE +
Without a Moonsign Benediction character, Dendro meeting Hydro is plain Bloom.
