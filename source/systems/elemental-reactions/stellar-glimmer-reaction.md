## summary
### LITE
Stellar Glimmer reactions are a small family of Cryo-related elemental reactions tied to Snezhnaya. There are two: Stellar-Conduct (Cryo with Electro) and Stellar Swirl (Anemo with Cryo). They take the place of Superconduct and Cryo Swirl only when a character with a matching Stellar Jubilee passive is in the party, and those characters are Stellar Linchpin users from Snezhnaya, or the Cryo Traveler.
### STANDARD +
The family was introduced in Version Luna VIII (6.7) with Stellar-Conduct, and Stellar Swirl followed in 7.0. Stellar Glimmer reactions also interrupt a specific attack from Snowland Fae and Aberrant Chimeric Monsters.

## in_universe
### LITE
Canon gives the reactions almost no in-world explanation. The only lore note is that Stellar Glimmer reactions were introduced to ancient Snezhnaya by Saarelainen, the Third Descender. The wiki sources this to the Version 7.0 Special Program livestream rather than to quest text.
### STANDARD +
Stellar Linchpins are Vision-like objects from Snezhnaya that let users manipulate the elements. Fatui research, as shown in the quest "Should We Meet Again", links them to the remains of Saarelainen. The Fatui's idea is that impurities from his remains become fixed in place when a Linchpin manifests, hence the name. Users of elemental energy through a Linchpin often show a power that seems to come from beyond Teyvat.
### LARGE +
The Fatui want that otherworldly energy for Project Mistilteinn, a weapon meant to threaten the Heavenly Principles, which are used to fighting Light Realm energy and are the source of the Human Realm's elemental energy. Canon does not connect Mistilteinn to the Stellar Glimmer reactions directly. Do not claim that the reactions are the weapon.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not literal lore): a Stellar Glimmer reaction needs a character in the party with a passive talent that converts specific Cryo-related reactions. The two reactions are Stellar-Conduct, which replaces Superconduct, and Stellar Swirl, which replaces Cryo Swirl. The passives are called Stellar Jubilee passives.
### LARGE +
Known Stellar Linchpin users are Sandrone (Cryo, 6.7), Odette (Cryo, 7.0), Vesna (Anemo, 7.1), Danica and Mitya (Electro, per the wiki's user list); the Cryo Traveler also has Stellar Jubilee passives. Artifacts, weapons and talents exist that raise Stellar Glimmer damage, and a Version 7.1 Spiral Abyss disorder boosts "Stellar Glimmer DMG" as a whole.
### ULTRA +
In the Spiral Abyss, Version 7.0 floors raised Stellar Swirl and Stellar-Conduct damage separately, and one Blessing of the Abyssal Moon rewarded attacks made inside a Polestar Field. These are event-style modifiers, not rules of the reactions themselves.

## enemies
### STANDARD
Gameplay: Stellar Glimmer reactions disable certain attacks of Snowland Fae (Snegoviks, Vilas, Volkodlak Brutes) and Aberrant Chimeric Monsters, and a Leshy that is rooted and healing has its heart further exposed when a Stellar Glimmer reaction hits it.
### LARGE +
The Snowland Fae are creatures of crystalline flesh from the biological alchemy of ancient Snezhnaya, as are the Aberrant Chimeric Monsters, which the wiki's text says were created under the Belyi Tsar. Canon does not explain why the Stellar reactions suit these creatures.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay interpretation (this project's recommendation, not canon text): treat these reactions as a Snezhnayan quirk of elemental power, visible in the company of a Linchpin user. Frost, static or wind react in an unusually bright, starlike way, with sparkling light and a sense of weight. Scale it to a skirmish: no Stellar reaction should be shown as a world-ending force.
### LARGE +
A character who knows Snezhnayan lore might link the effect to the Third Descender, but ordinary people have no reason to. Do not make the reactions a secret Fatui project or a Mistilteinn component unless canon says so.

## limits
### LITE
There are only two Stellar Glimmer reactions as of 7.1: Stellar-Conduct and Stellar Swirl. Do not invent further ones.
### STANDARD +
The reactions replace, not add to, the ordinary reactions, and require a Stellar Jubilee passive in the party. They are separate from the Lunar reactions, which depend on a Moonsign passive.

## entry: Stellar Linchpin

### STANDARD
A Stellar Linchpin is a Vision-like object from Snezhnaya that lets its user manipulate the elements. Fatui research connects Linchpins to the dead Third Descender, Saarelainen, whose remains left impurities that fix in place when a Linchpin forms. What makes one appear is unknown. Linchpin users are the ones who enable Stellar Glimmer reactions.
