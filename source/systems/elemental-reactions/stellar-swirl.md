## summary
### LITE
Stellar Swirl is a Stellar Glimmer reaction from Snezhnaya. It happens when Anemo is applied to a target already affected by Cryo, while a character with the matching Stellar Jubilee passive is in the party. It then replaces Cryo Swirl. It deals Anemo damage and leaves a Stellar Vortex that later bursts for Cryo damage.
### STANDARD +
It was added in Version 7.0 ("Everwinter Without Mercy"), after Stellar-Conduct in 6.7. The announcement presented it as a reaction that disturbs opponents and also helps exploration.

## in_universe
### LITE
Canon says nothing in-world about the vortex. The only lore note concerns the family: Stellar Glimmer reactions are said to have been introduced to ancient Snezhnaya by Saarelainen, the Third Descender, citing a 7.0 livestream.

## gameplay
### LITE
Gameplay (not literal lore): on triggering, one hit of Anemo damage is dealt to the target, and a Level 1 Stellar Vortex appears for 3 seconds. After a delay the vortex bursts, dealing area Cryo damage.
### STANDARD +
Triggering Stellar Swirl a third time raises the vortex to Level 2, so that it bursts for more damage over a wider area. Three further triggers make it burst at once. For 5 seconds after any vortex bursts, the player's next jump is higher, which the announcement framed as an exploration aid.
### LARGE +
The Version 7.1 patch notes fixed an error where the reaction's damage could leave out Cryo characters involved in the reaction. Spiral Abyss floors in 7.0 raised Stellar Swirl damage by 50% and, in the later phase, by 75% in one half, and one blessing created a True DMG shockwave from Swirl or Stellar Swirl damage once every 4 seconds. These are patch details, not canon.

## interactions
### STANDARD
Stellar Swirl replaces Cryo Swirl in a party with a matching passive, so Anemo on a Cryo target no longer spreads Cryo to nearby targets. It does not replace Swirl with Pyro, Hydro or Electro. Stellar-Conduct is the other Stellar Glimmer reaction.
### LARGE +
Stellar Glimmer reactions also disable certain attacks of Snowland Fae and Aberrant Chimeric Monsters. Known Stellar Linchpin users include Vesna, the Anemo user introduced in 7.1; Odette in 7.0 is a Cryo user.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay interpretation (this project's recommendation, not canon text): show Stellar Swirl as a small, bright whirl of frost and starlight that spins in place for a breath before it bursts outward. Anemo moves it, Cryo is what it leaves behind. Keep it to skirmish scale.
### LARGE +
Do not describe it as an ordinary Anemo Vision holder's Swirl. It needs a Stellar Linchpin user, or the Cryo Traveler, to be present. Do not invent a lore origin beyond the family note.

## limits
### LITE
As of 7.1, no canon explains the reaction beyond its name and the family-level Saarelainen note.
### STANDARD +
Stellar Swirl needs a Stellar Jubilee passive in the party and an Anemo-Cryo pairing. It does not apply to other elements and is not a Lunar reaction.

## history_note
@version: 7.0
### ULTRA
The Hoyolab announcement for 7.0 described Stellar Swirl as following Stellar-Conduct, introduced in Version Luna VIII, and listed its features as the vortex, disrupting opponents, and supporting exploration. It also noted a Starlight Prism effect that provides warmth and enhances constructs, which belongs to Stellar-Conduct's own entry.

## entry: Stellar Vortex
@keys: Stellar Vortex, Stellar Vortices
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
The Stellar Vortex is the object left by Stellar Swirl. It lasts 3 seconds, bursts for area Cryo damage, levels up with repeated triggers, and boosts the next jump after it bursts.
