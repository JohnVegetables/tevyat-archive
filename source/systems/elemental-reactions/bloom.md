## summary
### LITE
Bloom is what happens when Dendro and Hydro meet on a target, in either order. The result is a Dendro Core: a small bud-like object that sits on the field for a few seconds and then bursts in a spray of Dendro damage around it. It arrived in Version 3.0 with the Dendro element.
### STANDARD +
A Dendro Core can also be hit with a third element before it bursts. Pyro turns it into Burgeon, a bigger blast. Electro turns it into Hyperbloom, a homing projectile (Sprawling Shot). Both are summarised below and have their own entries.

## in_universe
### LITE
Canon offers no in-world explanation. Bloom is shown in tutorials and combat, not discussed by characters in the sources read. Treat it as a gameplay abstraction: a scene may borrow its look (a seed forming from water and verdant energy) but should not present a named in-world law.
### STANDARD +
Related setting: Dendro is the element associated with Sumeru. The wiki also notes that creatures made of Dendro, the Tri-Lakshana creatures, react with Hydro and Electro as Bloom and Quicken.

## gameplay
### LITE
Gameplay (not literal lore): Dendro applied to a Hydro-affected target, or Hydro applied to a Dendro-affected one, creates a Dendro Core. It lasts up to 6 seconds. If it expires while the character who made it is still in the party and alive, it explodes for area Dendro damage in a 5 m radius, hurting enemies and players alike. Otherwise it simply disappears.
### STANDARD +
- At most 5 Dendro Cores exist at once; making a sixth makes the oldest expire immediately.
- Burgeon: Pyro on a Dendro Core consumes it and causes a larger Dendro explosion, also in a 5 m radius, to enemies and players.
- Hyperbloom: Electro on a Dendro Core turns it into a Sprawling Shot that homes in on the closest enemy and hits for increased Dendro area damage in a 1 m radius. If an enemy or the environment triggers it, it homes on the closest ally instead.
- Damage to players from every Bloom-related reaction is 2% of what the same reaction deals to enemies, whoever triggers it.
- Burgeon and Hyperbloom may be triggered by a different party from the one that made the Core, including the environment, and they scale with the stats of whoever applied the Pyro or Electro.
### LARGE +
- Bountiful Cores: certain character abilities and event buffs make Bloom produce Bountiful Cores instead. These explode 0.4 seconds after creation in a 6.5 m radius, again dealing 2% to players. Burgeon and Hyperbloom cannot be triggered on them, and they share the limit of 5 with ordinary Dendro Cores.
- Damage limit: one target can take only 2 instances of damage from each Bloom-related reaction per source every 0.5 seconds. Reactions triggered by one character do not share this limit with another's, and Dendro Core and Bountiful Core explosions are counted separately.
- Damage is Transformative: it comes from the triggering character's level and Elemental Mastery, and the wiki gives separate tables for Bloom and for Burgeon/Hyperbloom.
- Trivia from the wiki: the Lea Lotus Lamp made by the Dendro Traveler's Elemental Burst counts as a Hyperbloom target when an enemy or the environment triggers the reaction.
### ULTRA +
Several talents, constellations and artifact sets raise Bloom, Burgeon or Hyperbloom damage, make Dendro Cores burst at once, let these reactions deal critical hits, or add flat base damage; the wiki keeps them in category tables, which this entry does not copy.

## lunar_bloom
@version: 6.0
### LARGE
Lunar-Bloom (own entry) is the Moonsign form of Bloom, which the wiki records as introduced in Version Luna I. It needs a party member with a qualifying Moonsign Benediction passive, so it is not available to every team. According to the wiki it deals no damage itself; damage comes only through character talents and constellations. Triggering it grants the player Verdant Dew, one every 2.5 seconds for up to 3, and the Dew persists across party changes, teleports, domains, daily resets and game sessions.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay interpretation (this project's recommendation, not canon text): show Bloom as a bud or seed of green energy that swells out of damp air and then bursts into leaves and spores. Keep the idea of waiting: the Core gives bystanders a moment to move, strike it, or step away.
### LARGE +
A fight can be described in terms of a third element changing the outcome: fire makes a swollen, violent blast, lightning sends the seed flying at the nearest foe. Do not have characters speak of seconds, caps of five cores, or percentages. Cores harming allies is a good dramatic hazard, though a minor one.

## examples
### STANDARD
A Hydro slime is soaked by a Hydro attack; a Dendro arrow then lands, and a glowing seed forms at its feet. Left alone it bursts in a green shower. If a spark of lightning hits it first, it darts across the field at the nearest enemy.
### LARGE +
Because Hyperbloom can home in on an ally when an enemy or the environment sets it off, an enemy with Electro can plausibly send a seed toward the party.

## limits
### STANDARD
Do not claim Bloom can happen without Dendro and Hydro, or that Anemo, Geo or Cryo triggers it. Do not describe Burgeon or Hyperbloom as separate elements; they are second-stage reactions of a Dendro Core.
### LARGE +
Do not move Lunar-Bloom earlier than Version Luna I, and do not give it direct damage on its own. As of 7.1 nothing newer than Luna I is recorded here for Bloom itself.

## entry: Dendro Core
@keys: Dendro Core, Dendro Cores
### STANDARD
A Dendro Core is the bud-like object created by Bloom. It lasts up to 6 seconds and bursts for Dendro area damage within 5 m if the party member who made it is still present and alive. At most 5 can exist at a time. Pyro on it causes Burgeon; Electro on it causes Hyperbloom.

## entry: Burgeon
@keys: Burgeon
@keys2: Dendro Core, Bloom, reaction
### STANDARD
Burgeon is the reaction that follows when Pyro hits a Dendro Core. It consumes the Core and deals stronger area Dendro damage within 5 m to enemies and players. It can be triggered by a different party, or by the environment, and uses the Pyro applier's stats.

## entry: Hyperbloom
@keys: Hyperbloom
@keys2: Dendro Core, Bloom, reaction
### STANDARD
Hyperbloom is the reaction that follows when Electro hits a Dendro Core. The Core becomes a Sprawling Shot that homes in on the nearest enemy (or ally, if an enemy or the environment triggered it) and deals stronger Dendro area damage in a 1 m radius.

## entry: Bountiful Core
@keys: Bountiful Core, Bountiful Cores
### LARGE
A Bountiful Core is a Bloom variant created by certain talents and event buffs. It bursts 0.4 seconds after it appears, with a 6.5 m radius, and cannot be turned into Burgeon or Hyperbloom. Like a Dendro Core, it harms players for only 2% of its damage to enemies.
