## summary
### LITE
Lunar-Crystallize is the Lunar Reaction between Geo and Hydro. When the party includes a character whose Moonsign Benediction passive enables it, Hydro Crystallize is replaced by Lunar-Crystallize, which creates three Moondrifts around the target. Every third Lunar-Crystallize while Moondrifts are present sets them resonating in Moondrift Harmony, a tracking projectile that deals Geo damage and can crit. It arrived in Version 6.3 (Luna IV).
### STANDARD +
As with the other Lunar Reactions, the damage is based on every character who applied an element during the reaction, and it can also be dealt directly by a talent. The wiki treats Moondrifts as Geo constructs.

## in_universe
### LITE
Canon offers no in-world explanation. Moondrifts and Moondrift Harmony appear in tutorial and combat material only. Treat them as a gameplay abstraction.
### STANDARD +
Related setting: the reaction came to the Nod-Krai-themed Moonsign family later than Lunar-Charged and Lunar-Bloom. The wiki's announcement images for it describe it as Hydro and Geo meeting again under the moonlight; that wording is the announcement's, not a statement about the story world.

## gameplay
### LITE
Gameplay (not literal lore): triggering Lunar-Crystallize makes three Moondrifts near the target. While at least one is on the field, each third Lunar-Crystallize makes them resonate and fire a projectile that tracks the target for Geo damage.
### STANDARD +
- The wiki classes Moondrifts as Geo constructs.
- The tutorial says triggering the reaction three times starts Moondrift Harmony, which hits nearby opponents with Geo damage.
- Damage can crit and is shared across contributing characters who applied Geo or Hydro.
### LARGE +
- Lunar-Crystallize uses the shared Lunar formula. Its base reaction coefficient is 1.6 in both indirect and direct damage, and the elemental mastery bonus is 6 x EM / (EM + 2000).
- The wiki lists Zibai (The Coursing Sun and Moon) and Linnea (Habitat Survey) for up to 14% Lunar-Crystallize base bonus, Columbina for up to 7%, and Aino's Constellation 6 for a reaction bonus.
- The wiki keeps category tables of artifacts, weapons, talents and constellations that raise Lunar-Crystallize damage; they are not copied here.
### ULTRA +
- Enduring Rock resonance (two Geo characters) gives extra benefits when Moondrifts are nearby: 15% more damage dealt and a 20% Geo RES reduction on enemies for 15 seconds after damaging them.
- Limited-time mode boosts in 6.3 reached Lunar-Crystallize: the notes list a 75% Lunar-Crystallize boost for one half of a mode and a True DMG shockwave when a character deals Lunar-Crystallize damage, once every 3 seconds. A fix in the same notes corrected an earlier case where the Floor 9 and 10 Lunar boost of 50% missed Lunar-Crystallize.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay interpretation (this project's recommendation, not canon text): show three small floating stones circling a foe that glow pale and then fly together in a single strike. Keep them small; they are summoned by a reaction, not a landslide.
### LARGE +
The three-then-strike rhythm suits a team pattern: two hits to set the stones turning, the third to release them. Narrate who contributed Geo and who contributed water, since both count.
### ULTRA +
Do not claim Moondrifts are fragments of a moon or related to the Three Moons. The sources read say neither, and the name is only a game term.

## limits
### LITE
Without a Moonsign Benediction character who enables it, Geo meeting Hydro is ordinary Hydro Crystallize.
### STANDARD +
Moondrift Harmony needs Moondrifts on the field and fires only on every third Lunar-Crystallize.
### LARGE +
Moondrifts are Geo constructs for the game's rules but have no stated in-world nature; do not give them one.
