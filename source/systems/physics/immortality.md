## summary
### LITE
Teyvat has no single rule for avoiding death. Canon shows separate, bounded cases: the Curse of Immortality that Ronova, Ruler of Death, laid on pure-blooded Khaenri'ahns 500 years ago; long-lived adepti, gods and dragons, who still die or erode; zombies such as Qiqi; and Natlan's conditional Ode of Resurrection.
### STANDARD +
Each case has a cost: the curse stops dying and ageing but not mental and spiritual decay; zombies lose memory or feeling; the Ode needs a victory and heals only fatal wounds. The Authorities of Life and Death, held by two of the Four Shades, are what Celestia polices.
### LARGE +
As of 7.1, Ronova's Authority of Death has been reclaimed by Celestia and she is defeated, with her fate unconfirmed. Her Snezhnayan Curse of Death also showed that death in Teyvat is a process involving souls and the Ley Lines (see the Souls entry), not only the end of a body.

## in_universe
### LITE
- Cursed immortality: pure-blooded Khaenri'ahns, by Ronova.
- Longevity: adepti, gods and dragons live for millennia but can die or be worn away.
- Zombification: adeptal energy (Qiqi) or an Elixir of Immortality (Jialiang).
- Resurrection: Natlan's Ode, for fallen Ancient Name bearers.
### STANDARD +
Ordinary people age and die; no source gives a human lifespan.
### LARGE +
Ronova could define the form of death for any being, or deny it. Rhinedottir, who merged with Naberius, holds the Authority of Life among the Four Shades. Gods are not exempt from wearing down: erosion, which the Heavenly Principles built into the natural order and which tracks the flow of the Ley Lines, affects bodies, memory, will and spirit. Zhongli says the more powerful a being is, the more dangerous it becomes under erosion. Azhdaha, Ei (who built the Shogun to withstand it) and the Khaenri'ahns are the standard examples.

## curse_of_immortality
@version: 4.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
During the Cataclysm 500 years ago, Ronova cursed Khaenri'ah's pure-blooded people with immortality as a punishment. They cannot die or age, but they still erode. Khaenri'ahns of mixed or foreign descent were instead turned into monsters, per the wiki's reading of the story.
### LARGE +
- Canon describes the curse as falling on the whole population, including those who knew nothing of the Five Sinners' use of Abyssal power.
- Many survivors, notably most of the Abyss Order, eventually became Abyss mages, heralds or lectors.
- The royal guards, the Black Serpents, became Shadowy Husks or stayed Black Serpents in a different form, still eroding. Halfdan kept his captain's face and orders for centuries and passed on only after Dainsleif acknowledged him in the Chasm.
- Rates of erosion vary. Some hilichurls knew of a fountain in the Chasm where they could "die" peacefully.
- Chlothar was driven fully insane by the curse and, the wiki says, is presumed dead in Avidya Forest after finding a way to break it. How is not given.
### ULTRA +
Pierro (the Jester) says that in the cataclysm he saw neither pity nor pleasure in Ronova's eyes while people begged for mercy, and that the curse created a feud "destined never to die." Dainsleif calls Ronova the architect of his torment over the last five hundred years.

## erosion_and_the_curse
### STANDARD
Dainsleif says the curse denies death but does not promise that those under it will never die: body and soul keep eroding until they are nearly nonexistent, even if death is not the form that erosion takes. His ring, a gift from his brother Vedrfolnir, continually resets his body's condition and lets him keep his memories better than other Khaenri'ahns, though his mind still erodes.
### LARGE +
Erosion has four canon forms: mental (progressive loss of memory, clearest in Azhdaha), physical (deterioration, which led Ei to replace her body with a puppet), of the will, and of the spirit (loss of the sense of self, leading to feral behaviour or madness). It is imposed by the Heavenly Principles and may be connected to Istaroth, the Ruler of Time. Two attempts to stall it are recorded: Zhongli sharing power with Azhdaha, which failed, and Ei's Shogun.
### ULTRA +
In 7.1 Dainsleif says the battle with Ronova took an exceptionally heavy toll on the Jester's mind and soul, and that wielding such power always costs something irrecoverable. He also suggests that a fellow Khaenri'ahn's forgiving manner might be an attempt to resist erosion.

## capitano_case
@version: 5.3
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
In Natlan's Archon Quest Ronova demanded Mavuika's life as the price for using her power against the Abyssal invasion. Capitano, a cursed Khaenri'ahn and the First Harbinger, offered his death instead, aiming to exploit the paradox: a being made unable to die by Ronova's own curse could not pay her price without forcing her to break one of her rules. Ronova chose to keep the Curse of Immortality, and Capitano's life force went into the Lord of the Night, who became essentially immortal. His body stayed on the Throne of the Primal Fire. As a result, souls from outside Natlan can now enter the Night Kingdom.
### ULTRA +
The wiki records that Capitano also aimed to save the souls of the dead by this substitution. How his own curse now stands is unstated. Do not extend the outcome to other Harbingers.

## ronova_and_authority
@version: 5.0
### LARGE
Ronova was the Ruler of Death among the Four Shades and Arbiter of the Heavenly Principles, a god whose existence predated any Archon. She let Xbalanque, the first Pyro Archon, borrow her power to set the rules of Natlan, on the understanding that she would deny involvement if the Heavenly Principles found out. Anyone who uses her power must eventually die. After Natlan's victory over the Abyss she honoured a bet by letting Xbalanque see present-day Natlan.
### ULTRA +
She also created the Maranasati Caskets that guard the Apathic Interval. The wiki further reads the Staff of Homa, which purified miasma in Liyue's Archon War era, as her gift; that is an inference from item text.

## curse_of_death
@version: 7.0
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
In Snezhnaya, Ronova spread a Curse of Death to stop technology that might threaten the Heavenly Principles, especially Project Mistilteinn. Whenever someone conceived such an idea, the curse killed them and everyone nearby for a short time. They revived with short-term memory loss, so researchers at the Armory Palace reported lost time, and Odette once found Mitya's body only to see him alive days later. Such killings broke the Heavenly Principles' rules, so Ronova and Rhinedottir used a loophole: kill, then resurrect within a window before Celestia's monitoring cycle noticed.
### ULTRA +
In the Wraith's Nocturne quest (7.0) the Curse of Death killed everyone in the Korolevskiy Theater. Its Ley Lines had been modified by angels so that they could take only two souls a day, which left the dead stranded in an afterlife that felt like a blizzard outside the theater. Ronova, disguised as the dead Alyosha, killed two people a night. When the Traveler and Mitya exposed her and destroyed the disguise, everyone in the theater woke up alive. In 7.1 Rhinedottir's resurrection could not be carried out in time because the Tsaritsa had used her six Gnoses to tamper with space and time around Rhinedottir. Celestia detected the aberration, reclaimed Ronova's Authority of Death and undid the unauthorised curse. Rhinedottir noted she would rather not lose her Authority of Life too.

## ronova_status
@version: 7.1
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
As of 7.1 Ronova has been defeated. Pierro (the Jester), the Traveler and Dainsleif fought her after Celestia took back her Authority. The Jester spent his own soul and life force on the final strike, a summoned elemental nail. A portal was closing in the crater afterwards. Even without her Authority she still had power befitting a Shade. The Fatui count it as their victory, and the Snezhnayan blockade on technology ended with it.

## zombies
### STANDARD
A zombie is a person brought back from death, usually cold and stiff, with talismans to hold the state. Qiqi was never awakened by anyone and gives herself orders. She has poor memory, little expression, no sense of smell, prefers cold, and her flesh decays in heat. Jialiang, revived by Baizhu's Elixir of Immortality, is the only other known zombie.
### LARGE +
Qiqi's story: an ordinary herb-picker caught in a clash between adepti and demons in Liyue, near death, who received a Vision in her last moments. The adepti each gave her a portion of strength to keep her alive. It was too much for a mortal, so she went berserk and Mountain Shaper sealed her in amber. Hundreds of years later humans found the amber and carried it toward the Wangsheng Funeral Parlor; the seal weakened on the road and she woke, escaped and met Baizhu, who took her in.
- She does calisthenics daily so that rigor mortis does not make her hop.
- In combat she drops her restraint and her undead strength and speed come out.
- She forgets a person within about three days unless she reinforces the memory; she writes things in a notebook and sometimes forgets to read it. Her self-given orders can be cancelled by an honest hug.
- Her appearance is fixed at the moment of her death, so her age cannot be estimated reliably.
### ULTRA +
During Baizhu's Story Quest "The Heart of Healing" (3.6), after Jiangli's god-remains treatment for Jialiang's rare heart condition was ended, Baizhu used the elixir on him. He woke at Bubu Pharmacy under talismans and, unlike Qiqi, kept his memory and speech. Baizhu did not know how long it would hold; the family left for Natlan hoping its hot springs would help. Baizhu's long pursuit of eternal life is said to be so that he can protect himself and Changsheng; Qiqi's Character Story ties his taking her in to his own pursuits.

## adepti_and_longevity
### STANDARD
The adepti are very long-lived but not immortal. Liyue still holds the Rite of Parting, a memorial service run by Wangsheng Funeral Parlor, for each adeptus who passes, though it has been needed only a handful of times in three thousand years.
### LARGE +
Legend says some adepti, tired of waiting for a natural end, asked Morax to turn them into stone statues. Adeptal energy endangers mortals because their souls are less robust, which is also what overwhelmed Qiqi. Adepti arts include Dream Trawler, which separates the soul from the body, and Pervases's Mortal Lingering, which lets a spirit regain a physical vessel for a short time.

## ode_of_resurrection
@version: 5.0
### STANDARD
In Natlan the Ode of Resurrection, a ceremony at the Stadium of the Sacred Flame, raises Ancient Name bearers who died fighting the Abyss in the Night Warden Wars, provided their group achieved victory. The Pyro Archon enters the Sacred Flame to seek the deceased and their name in the Night Kingdom while the crowd sings. It heals fatal injuries only, and its power comes from Ronova.
### LARGE +
Kachina's Ode failed: Mavuika came out alone and said neither Kachina nor her Ancient Name could be found in the Night Kingdom. Abyssal unrest was blocking it, so she suspended the Pilgrimage. A bearer who dies in defeat and is not resurrected loses the name; one who dies otherwise is eventually reborn in Natlan in a different form. After the six Heroes of Natlan gathered, Mavuika briefly called on the Lord of the Night's power to extend the Ode to every Natlanese warrior, nameless or not, and casualties effectively dropped to zero. When the Sacred Flame faltered during the assault on the Abyss, Kachina began the Ode spontaneously to send the nation's support, re-empowering the Flame. The ceremony was discontinued after the Night Wars.
### ULTRA +
The melody and lyrics are an excerpt of the Natlan soundtrack and are sung in Swahili. After he defeated Xiuhcoatl, Xbalanque was said to have been "revived from the flames" (legend). Pyro Archons begin as ordinary humans who win the position by combat, so they remain mortal.

## other_returns
@version: 6.3
### LARGE
In the Liyue Story Quest "A Lanternlit Ode to the Silver Moon" (Version Luna IV, 6.3), once the shadowmoon vanished, the Traveler and Zhongli reunited the three fragments of Zibai's divided soul and restored her. This is a unique, quest-specific act, not a general method.
### ULTRA +
In 7.1 the Traveler is sent to the afterlife by Ronova's curse and escapes with a Seal of Life formed from their memories, used by Vesna through the Authority of Life. This is a bounded act within one story scene, not a general rule.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not literal lore): fallen party members can be revived at a Statue of The Seven or with a dish. No story source treats these as literal in-world resurrection.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay interpretation (this project's recommendation, not canon text): treat any return from death as costly and conditional. A Khaenri'ahn survivor should feel tired, not triumphant; a zombie should show cold, stiffness or forgetfulness; an adeptus's death is a rare, formal event.
### LARGE +
Do not offer the Ode as a service outside Natlan. Do not let an ordinary character cast the Curse of Immortality or Death, which are Authority-level acts.
### ULTRA +
Avoid portraying the curse as invulnerability: the cursed can be hurt, exhausted and defeated; canon says only that they do not die. Do not claim that Dainsleif or any named Khaenri'ahn has a way to end the curse unless the scene's canon says so.

## limits
### LITE
No source describes a way to undo the Curse of Immortality, and no resurrection is unconditional.
### STANDARD +
Ronova could not simply break her own rules (Capitano's paradox), and when she exceeded the limits Celestia permitted, it reclaimed her Authority. The Ode needs victory, a name, a fatal wound and a Pyro Archon willing to enter the flame.
### LARGE +
Zombies need orders to act (Qiqi's self-given ones can lock her into a task), and adeptal energy that revives a mortal can overwhelm her. Canon records no proven remedy for erosion, so every long-lived being, god or mortal, faces a limit.

## entry: Curse of Immortality
@keys: Curse of Immortality, curse of immortality, cursed with immortality
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Ronova, the Ruler of Death, cursed the pure-blooded Khaenri'ahns with immortality during the Cataclysm 500 years ago. They cannot die or age but are not shielded from erosion, which gradually dissolves memory, will and spirit. Dainsleif and Capitano are among the cursed. As of 7.1, Ronova's Authority is reclaimed, but the characters who live with the curse still treat it as active.

## entry: Curse of Death
@keys: Curse of Death, Ronova's curse
@spoiler: major
@version: 7.0
### LARGE
Ronova's Snezhnaya curse that killed anyone developing dangerous technology and their surroundings, then returned them to life with memory loss. It worked with Rhinedottir's Authority of Life and a resurrection window before Celestia's monitoring cycle. In 7.1 the window passed, Celestia reclaimed the Authority of Death and the curse was undone.

## entry: Elixir of Immortality
@keys: Elixir of Immortality
@keys2: Baizhu, Jialiang, zombie
### LARGE
Baizhu's elixir turned the dying Jialiang into a zombie in the 3.6 Story Quest "The Heart of Healing". Unlike Qiqi, Jialiang kept his speech and memory; the wiki guesses this is because the elixir does not use adeptus arts. Its duration is unknown.

## entry: Rite of Parting
@keys: Rite of Parting
@keys2: adeptus, adepti, Wangsheng
### LARGE
A Liyue memorial service for the passing of an adeptus, run by Wangsheng Funeral Parlor. It has been simplified over time and has been needed only a few times in three thousand years, so adepti are best described as long-lived rather than immortal.

## entry: Authority of Life
@keys: Authority of Life, Ruler of Life
@keys2: Rhinedottir, Naberius, resurrect
### LARGE
Rhinedottir merged with Naberius and holds the Authority of Life among the Four Shades. The wiki says her creations draw on it and gain a degree of immortality. In 7.1 she says she would rather not have the Authority reclaimed too, and complies with Celestia's directive to rectify Ronova's mistake by resurrecting the people Ronova killed.
