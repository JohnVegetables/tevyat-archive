## summary
### LITE
Canon has no single doctrine of the soul. The recurring picture: the soul is what outlasts the body. After death it is meant to return to the Ley Lines, or in Natlan to the Night Kingdom, where it eventually loses its sense of self. A soul or memory that fails to go can linger as a ghost. Older belief sent the noble dead to cities on the moons.
### STANDARD +
Souls can also be split, marked, shared or stored: Zibai's soul was divided into fragments, Columbina left a piece of hers in a Luonnotar, Mitya's soul bears Ronova's mark, and Moon Wheels are said to be formed of soul-stuff. The Snegovik, Snezhnaya's fae, are said to be born with incomplete souls.
### LARGE +
These are separate local accounts, not one system. Treat any soul rule as belonging to its region and source.

## in_universe
### LITE
- Ley Lines: the standard destination. Ronova says that after the body dies the soul must return to the Ley Lines for death to be complete.
- Night Kingdom: in Natlan the dead go there; souls become Nightsouls and eventually merge into a sea of souls.
- Ghosts: souls or echoes that did not make the transition and linger on the surface.
### STANDARD +
- Irminsul: a record of everything in Teyvat; Nahida says the one way to enter it that she could not guard was through death.
- The moons: in an older age, boatmen ferried the noble dead to cities on the moons.
### LARGE +
Ronova claims that death gives mortals wisdom: souls connect to all things and see the chain of cause and effect. Mortals experiencing the afterlife in the Korolevskiy Theater looked and felt physical, which Odette noted as odd for something non-physical; Mitya concluded their bodies there were shells in the afterlife.

## ley_lines_and_death
@version: 7.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
In the Wraith's Nocturne quest (7.0), Ronova explains that after the physical body dies, the soul must return to the Ley Lines for that person's death to be considered complete, and that her Authority covers the whole process, including guiding souls there. The Ley Lines of the Korolevskiy Theater had been modified by angels to make room for the souls of special creations, which hindered their ability to take ordinary souls: they could accept only two a day. Those killed by the Curse of Death were stuck in an afterlife that looked like the theater trapped in a blizzard.
### LARGE +
Souls that die from the Curse of Death must return within a short window or miss their chance for good. Ronova said that the Ley Lines wanted back two long-lost souls, so each night they took an equal number to fill the gap. The Traveler and Mitya were able to help the two lost souls find clarity and "go to where they belong," which Mitya read as returning to the Ley Lines.
### ULTRA +
In 7.1 Mitya explains that because his soul bears Ronova's mark, he remembers everything that happens while he is dead, and that the recent theater incident showed a soul can be held briefly in the afterlife. He therefore proposes to do an experiment there. Vodyanitsa warns that the afterlife is Ronova's territory. Mitya also says he can sense the Curse of Immortality's signature on others.

## natlan_night_kingdom
@version: 5.0
### STANDARD
When Natlan's people die they enter the Night Kingdom, a realm created by the Lord of the Night with Ronova's guidance that functions as Natlan's Ley Lines. There they eventually lose their sense of self and become one with the sea of souls. The souls in it are called Nightsouls, and Natlanese warriors draw on them for Nightsoul's Blessing.
### LARGE +
Abyssal corrosion limited the Night Kingdom's capacity, so some souls were stuck in Natlan and would eventually and painfully disperse. If the soul had not been dead long and still held its form, a shaman of the Masters of the Night-Wind such as Citlali could "open the gate" for it, though the soul could refuse. These shamans can see Phlogiston and souls. Ancient Name bearers who fall in the Night Warden Wars can be raised by the Ode of Resurrection; a bearer who dies otherwise will eventually be reborn in Natlan in a different form. The Night Kingdom records only Natlanese memories.
### ULTRA +
The Weaver of Secrets, a Dragonlord, merged her consciousness into the Night Kingdom to create a "dream labyrinth" where the souls of deceased dragons and humans could rest in peace.

## natlan_aftermath
@version: 5.3
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
After Capitano's sacrifice the Lord of the Night absorbed his life force and became essentially immortal, and souls from outside Natlan are now accepted into the Night Kingdom. Xbalanque, the first Pyro Archon, briefly returned to present-day Natlan in the body of a young boy, Mausau, because his own body could not be restored; he stayed one day.

## ghosts_and_echoes
### STANDARD
Ghosts, also called spirits, appear throughout Teyvat. Flins, a Lightkeeper, says they are echoes of living souls: a portion of thoughts and memories that for certain reasons failed to enter the Ley Lines at death and so linger on the surface. They stay near fixed places and repeat words and phrases that mattered in life, and they are hard to reason with. In time their memories and yearning fade and slowly return to the Ley Lines.
### LARGE +
The wiki lists ghosts in Mondstadt (Boreas), Liyue (Wuwang Hill, Mingyun Village), Inazuma (Tsurumi Island), Enkanomiya and Nod-Krai's Final Night Cemetery. Most keep their living appearance. The Shades of Tokoyo in Enkanomiya are projections of powerful emotions, the "Sins of Tokoyo," that look like featureless ghosts; one that realises it is a shade soon vanishes. Tsurumi's ghosts are featureless blobs, and it is unknown whether that is damage from the Ley Line Disorder there or a local belief. In Nod-Krai, Lightkeeper ghosts react strongly when the lighthouse is relit because it is deep in their memories.
### ULTRA +
Hanachirusato in Inazuma looks like a ghost but is a physical memory recreation of Kitsune Saiguu made by the Sacred Sakura from "filth" and is a different entity from the original. Flins stresses that the ghosts are but the memories of people that once were; those to whom they belonged are long gone. A Snezhnayan legend in Flins's lore describes Kyryll the Azure Flame as a blue flame that guided fallen souls across the icy plains.

## moons_and_irminsul
@version: 6.3
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
Diotima of Meropis, now in the Temple of Space, says she was once a boatman who sent wandering souls on silver ships to the Moon Capital above the clouds. In her time, three moons hung in the sky and each held Moon Envoys. Noble souls did not go to the ancient tree but followed the envoys to sleep eternally in cities on the moons. The wiki reads the Chanson of the Boatman, found in the Golden Hall, as suggesting this worked alongside Irminsul. Tsurumi's "Boatman" ghost is a similar figure, per the wiki.
### ULTRA +
When Zibai died protecting the civilization of Lang-Gan, Istaroth used the shadowmoon to preserve a portion of her soul. When the Frost Moon returned and the shadowmoon vanished, Zhongli and the Traveler reunited it with the other fragments in the Liyue Story Quest "A Lanternlit Ode to the Silver Moon" and restored her. Columbina split herself into three: body to moonlight poured over Nod-Krai (the origin of kuuvahki), soul left in the "moon's reflection" with a fragment anchoring her in a Luonnotar, and her Trilune Authority hidden in the Frost Moon.

## irminsul_and_the_dead
@version: 6.6
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
In Version Luna VII, Nahida says a major problem arose within Irminsul because an intruder entered it through death, the one route she could not guard. She would not stop deceased souls from returning to the Ley Lines to guard against an invasion, because the souls of the departed must be allowed to find peace. Dottore, who was thought dead, is said by the wiki to have survived by planning his death so that half his soul would be recorded by Irminsul and the other half uploaded as data.
### ULTRA +
Aranara use "Sarva" for Irminsul and "return to Sarva" for passing away, which hints at an afterlife, though they also say they come from Sarva and travel through it, so its meaning is unsettled.

## soul_stuff
### LARGE
A research note read in Ineffa's Character Story (6.0), written from Nephilheim's perspective, says a Moon Wheel, a ritual item given by the Three Moons to their followers so they could communicate, relies on a purer power (kuuvahki) than elemental energy and is "formed from the stuff of the soul." The same note says an acquired Vision sample, too, is soul-formed and wonders if Visions recently appeared alongside human wishes for that reason. Moon Wheels look like Nod-Krai Visions and allow Lunar Reactions.
### ULTRA +
This is an in-world researcher's hypothesis and partly redacted. The wiki's own comment is that Visions were probably formed from the same principle, with modern elemental energy in place of kuuvahki. Treat Visions as soul-linked only if the scene asks for that hypothesis.

## snegovik_souls
@version: 4.0
### LARGE
Snowland fae such as the Snegovik, made for "companionship" with the people of Hyperborea, are incomplete in soul by nature: only by falling in love with a human and taking that person's soul can they become whole, according to texts cited by the wiki. Male Snegovik are knightly; female Snegurochka are mages. The Grand Duchess of the Snegurochka rules them from the House of Hesperides. Today they are among the most numerous fae in Snezhnaya.

## adepti_and_mortal_souls
### LARGE
In Liyue, adepti arts include Dream Trawler, used to separate the soul from the body for self-cultivation and to summon another's spirit, as Xiao and the Traveler did with Starsnatcher. Pervases uses Mortal Lingering to regain a physical vessel temporarily. Mortals' souls are less robust, so prolonged contact with adepti, or a large infusion of adeptal energy, can overwhelm them, as it did with Qiqi.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not literal lore): Natlan characters use Nightsoul's Blessing, Nightsoul Points and Nightsoul Bursts, mechanics drawn from the Nightsouls of the Night Kingdom. Moon Wheel users in Nod-Krai trigger Lunar Reactions through Moonsign passives, built on kuuvahki. Ghost NPCs are quest and world features.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay interpretation (this project's recommendation, not canon text): keep soul talk regional. A Natlanese character thinks of the Night Kingdom and Nightsouls; a Nod-Krai Lightkeeper of ghosts and the Ley Lines; a Liyue adeptus of arts and parting. Do not blend them into a single universal rule.
### LARGE +
Ghosts speak in fixed, repeated phrases about what mattered in life and rarely hold a conversation. A character who feels the dead near them should not claim certain knowledge about the afterlife. Ronova's account is her own claim.
### ULTRA +
Avoid presenting any one explanation as settled: whether souls are reincarnated, return to the Ley Lines, sleep on the moons or join a sea of souls depends on region and era. Do not give a living character the ability to see souls unless canon does, as it does for the Masters of the Night-Wind.

## limits
### LITE
No source describes how reincarnation works, and no source lets an ordinary person talk to or free a soul at will.
### STANDARD +
A soul that has been dead too long or has lost its form is hard or impossible to help: in Natlan, dispersal is painful and unrecoverable, and in the Korolevskiy Theater, souls missing their window were lost for good.
### LARGE +
Natlan's Night Kingdom recorded only Natlanese memories until Capitano's sacrifice; the Ode works only for Ancient Name bearers after a victory.

## entry: Night Kingdom souls
@keys: Nightsoul, Nightsouls, sea of souls
@keys2: Natlan, Night Kingdom
### STANDARD
In Natlan, the dead enter the Night Kingdom, where they eventually lose their sense of self and join a sea of souls. Their remnants are the Nightsouls that warriors draw on for Nightsoul's Blessing. Abyssal corrosion limits the kingdom's capacity, and shamans such as Citlali can open the gate for a lingering soul, which may refuse to enter.

## entry: Ghosts
@keys: ghost, ghosts, phantom, Lightkeeper ghosts
@keys2: Nod-Krai, Final Night Cemetery, lighthouse
### STANDARD
Flins describes ghosts as echoes of the living: portions of thoughts and memories that failed to enter the Ley Lines at death. They repeat phrases from life, stay near fixed places and may respond strongly to anything tied to their memories, like the relit lighthouse in Nod-Krai. In time they fade and return to the Ley Lines.

## entry: Moon afterlife
@keys: Moon Capital, cities upon the moon, boatman, Diotima
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
In an older age the noble dead were ferried by boatmen to cities on the moons, where they slept eternally. Diotima of Meropis, once such a boatman, now guides newcomers in the Temple of Space. The wiki reads another text as suggesting this ran alongside Irminsul.

## entry: Curse of Death souls
@keys: Curse of Death, two souls a day, the afterlife
@keys2: Ronova, Mitya, theater
@version: 7.0
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
In the Korolevskiy Theater, Ley Lines modified by angels accepted only two souls a day, so the cursed dead were stranded in an afterlife that felt like a blizzard. Ronova, who guided the souls, said a soul must return to the Ley Lines to complete death. Mitya, whose soul bears her mark, remembers what happens while he is dead.

## entry: Soul fragments
@keys: soul fragment, fragment of her soul, divided soul
@keys2: Zibai, Columbina, Luonnotar
### LARGE
Zibai's soul was divided into three fragments, one held in the shadowmoon by Istaroth, and was reunited by Zhongli and the Traveler. Columbina left part of her soul as an anchor in a Luonnotar when she split her being. Both are one-off, quest-specific cases.

## entry: Snegovik souls
@keys: Snegovik, Snegurochka, incomplete soul
@keys2: Snowland Fae, Hyperborea, fae
### LARGE
Snegovik are said to be born with incomplete souls, made for companionship with humans in Hyperborea. They become whole only by falling in love with a human and taking that person's soul. This comes from texts the wiki cites, not a stated rule of the world.

## entry: Moon Wheel soul-stuff
@keys: Moon Wheel, Moon Wheels, soul-stuff, stuff of the soul
@spoiler: minor
@version: 6.0
### LARGE
A research note in Ineffa's Character Story calls the Moon Wheel "formed from the stuff of the soul," powered by kuuvahki rather than elemental energy, and suggests a Vision is similar. It is an in-world hypothesis, not confirmed canon.
