## summary
### LITE
Moonsign is a Team Bonus that sits beside Elemental Resonance. Some characters, mostly from Nod-Krai, carry a Moonsign Benediction passive, and each one in the party raises the party's Moonsign Level by one. One Moonsign character gives Nascent Gleam; two or more give Ascendant Gleam, which strengthens those characters and rewards non-Moonsign teammates for their skills. It is a game system, not a thing characters talk about, and it is tied to the moon-themed power of Nod-Krai.
### STANDARD +
It first appeared in Version Luna I (6.0), the first Nod-Krai version. The Lunar Reactions it supports (Lunar-Charged from 5.8, Lunar-Bloom from 6.0, Lunar-Crystallize later) are separate systems.

## in_universe
### LITE
Canon never explains Moonsign inside the story. The only in-world frame is the one the game gives: Nod-Krai is where "the new moon rises", and the tutorial says there are other ways to raise a party's Moonsign Level there.
### STANDARD +
The related lore is concrete, though. Moon Wheels are Vision-like gems that channel kuuvahki, the moon-linked primordial elemental power found in Nod-Krai, and every playable Moon Wheel user has a Moonsign Benediction passive. Kuuvahki is described as the power of the Three Moons, spread over Nod-Krai by the new Moon Goddess Columbina. Moonsign should be read as the game's way of showing a team gathering around that moonlight; canon does not describe it as an in-world ritual or ranking.
### LARGE +
Not every Moonsign character uses a Moon Wheel. Per the wiki, the Moonsign roster is: Ineffa, Flins, Lauma, Aino, Nefer, Jahoda, Columbina, Illuga, Zibai and Linnea. Of these the wiki lists Flins, Ineffa, Lauma, Linnea, Nefer and Zibai as Moon Wheel users; Aino, Jahoda, Illuga and Columbina are not on that list. Zibai is from Liyue and is the only roster member whose region is not Nod-Krai.

## mechanics
### LITE
Gameplay (not literal lore): the party gets a Moonsign Level equal to the number of Moonsign characters in it. One gives the Nascent Gleam effect. Two or more give Ascendant Gleam, which also includes Nascent Gleam's effects.
### STANDARD +
At Ascendant Gleam, certain Talents and Skills of the Moonsign characters themselves are enhanced. In addition, for 20 seconds after a non-Moonsign character uses an Elemental Skill or Elemental Burst, nearby characters deal more Lunar Reaction DMG, up to 36% and not stacking. How much depends on the caster's element: for Pyro, Electro and Cryo, 0.9% per 100 ATK; for Hydro, 0.6% per 1,000 Max HP; for Geo, 1% per 100 DEF; for Anemo and Dendro, 2.25% per 100 Elemental Mastery.
### LARGE +
The stats needed to hit the 36% cap are 4,000 ATK (Pyro, Electro, Cryo), 60,000 Max HP (Hydro), 3,600 DEF (Geo) and 1,600 Elemental Mastery (Anemo, Dendro). The tutorial adds that a higher Moonsign Level also improves some Weapons and Artifacts. Gameplay behaviour in Co-Op Mode is not settled (see uncertainties).
### ULTRA +
Moonsign Benediction passives of the Lunar-Reaction users also change the reactions themselves (see the separate entry). The passives of Illuga, Jahoda and Aino are the plain form: their wiki text states only the +1 Moonsign Level effect and no reaction conversion.

## moonsign_benediction
### STANDARD
A Moonsign Benediction passive is the Talent that adds one Moonsign Level while its owner is in the party. Gameplay: the passives are Ineffa's Assemblage Hub, Flins's Old World Secrets, Lauma's Nature's Chorus, Aino's Force Limit Analysis, Nefer's Dusklit Eaves, Jahoda's Rooftop Dash, Columbina's Moonlight, Lent Unto You, Illuga's Unwithering in Winter, Zibai's The Coursing Sun and Moon and Linnea's Habitat Survey.
### LARGE +
Per the wiki, Columbina's passive and the Moon Wheel users' passives also convert a base reaction into its Lunar form and add Base DMG scaling to a stat of the owner. Ineffa and Flins convert Electro-Charged into Lunar-Charged (0.7% Base DMG per 100 ATK, capped at 14%). Lauma and Nefer convert Bloom into Lunar-Bloom (0.0175% per point of Elemental Mastery, capped at 14%). Zibai and Linnea convert Hydro Crystallize into Lunar-Crystallize (0.7% per 100 DEF, capped at 14%). Columbina converts all three (0.2% per 1,000 Max HP, capped at 7%).

## lunar_reactions
@version: 6.0
### LARGE
Lunar Reactions are a subset of Elemental Reactions that only happen when a character with a fitting Moonsign Benediction passive is in the party. Unlike their base versions they can land CRIT hits, and they use kuuvahki. Lunar-Charged (Electro + Hydro) is a thundercloud that strikes enemies affected by both Wet and Electro; it came with Ineffa in 5.8. Lunar-Bloom (Dendro + Hydro) does no damage by itself and grants Verdant Dew. Lunar-Crystallize (Geo + Hydro) creates Moondrifts that fire Moondrift Harmony strikes. Moonsign Level matters because several Lunar Reaction bonuses key off Ascendant Gleam.

## sources_of_ascendant_gleam
@version: 6.0
@availability: limited
### LARGE
Only inside Nod-Krai: completing a quest of Silvermoon Hall: Selenic Chronicles (Columbina's reputation system, unlocked by "The Dark Side of Memory") gives the New Moon's Embrace effect until the seventh daily reset after it. While it lasts, the party's Moonsign Level is always Ascendant Gleam when in Nod-Krai, and it cannot be switched off. The Selenic Chronicles quests become unavailable once "A Nocturne of the Far North" starts after Act IV of the Archon Quest and return after "True Moon". Treat this as gameplay-system context, not a character-visible event; its availability depends on quest progress.

## equipment
### ULTRA
Gameplay, the equipment that reads Moonsign Level: the Silken Moon's Serenade artifact set (Luna I) gives party Elemental Mastery of 60 at Nascent Gleam and 120 at Ascendant Gleam for 8 seconds after the wearer deals Elemental DMG, plus extra Lunar Reaction DMG for each different Gleaming Moon effect in the party. The Aubade of Morningstar and Moon artifact set (Luna IV) gives Lunar Reaction DMG while the wearer is off-field, with a further large bonus at Ascendant Gleam or higher.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay interpretation (this project's recommendation, not canon text): Moonsign is a gameplay label and ordinary characters should not mention Moonsign Levels, "Nascent Gleam" or "Ascendant Gleam". If a scene wants the flavour, use soft silver or pale light, quiet confidence and a sense of people drawing strength from one another near Nod-Krai's moonlight, without claiming characters can feel a numbered level.
### LARGE +
A party of Nod-Krai characters with Moon Wheels can be played as sharing a recognisable "moon-touched" connection, but canon does not say that their wheels link to each other. Do not give a non-Moonsign character a Moon Wheel or kuuvahki power because they stand near a Moonsign character.
### ULTRA +
Do not present Moonsign as a formal title or rank in Nod-Krai society, as something only Columbina grants, or as a power that exists outside the moon-linked themes the wiki ties to kuuvahki. Zibai's membership shows it is not limited to Nod-Krai natives.

## entry: Nascent Gleam
@keys: Nascent Gleam, Moonsign: Nascent Gleam
@version: 6.0
### STANDARD
Nascent Gleam is the Moonsign effect for a party with one Moonsign character; every party member gains it. It is the lower of the two named levels. Ascendant Gleam includes Nascent Gleam's effects.

## entry: Ascendant Gleam
@keys: Ascendant Gleam, Moonsign: Ascendant Gleam
@version: 6.0
### STANDARD
Ascendant Gleam is the Moonsign effect for a party with two or more Moonsign characters. It enhances Moonsign characters' Talents and Skills and lets non-Moonsign characters boost nearby Lunar Reaction DMG for 20 seconds after using a skill or burst, up to a 36% cap. In Genius Invokation TCG, "Moonsign: Ascendant Gleam" is also an Elemental Resonance event card, with the in-card line "Come night or day, may the moonlight connect us."

## entry: Moonsign Benediction passives
@keys: Moonsign Benediction, Moonsign Benediction Passive
### STANDARD
The Talent type that adds a Moonsign Level. Characters who have one: Ineffa, Flins, Lauma, Aino, Nefer, Jahoda, Columbina, Illuga, Zibai and Linnea. Passives of Columbina and the Moon Wheel users also enable Lunar Reactions.
### LARGE +
The passive names, in the order above, are Assemblage Hub, Old World Secrets, Nature's Chorus, Force Limit Analysis, Dusklit Eaves, Rooftop Dash, Moonlight, Lent Unto You, Unwithering in Winter, The Coursing Sun and Moon and Habitat Survey.

## entry: New Moon's Embrace
@keys: New Moon's Embrace
@version: 6.0
@availability: limited
### LARGE
New Moon's Embrace is the temporary effect gained from completing Silvermoon Hall: Selenic Chronicles quests. In Nod-Krai the party's Moonsign Level is always Ascendant Gleam while it lasts (up to the seventh daily reset after the quest), and it cannot be disabled. It is tied to the Selenic Chronicles quest line and is not a standing feature of the system.

## entry: Silvermoon Hall Selenic Chronicles
@keys: Selenic Chronicles, Silvermoon Hall Selenic Chronicles
@version: 6.0
### LARGE
The reputation system for Columbina (called "the Damselette") at Silvermoon Hall in Nod-Krai. Unlocked by "The Dark Side of Memory" in Version Luna I, it unlocks a quest every seven daily resets and gives rewards after set quest counts. Its link to Moonsign is the New Moon's Embrace effect.
