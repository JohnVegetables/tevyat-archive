## summary
### LITE
Bond of Life is a combat mechanic that prevents healing. Some enemy, weapon or talent effects place it on a character; while it lasts it soaks up incoming healing instead of restoring health. It is a gameplay abstraction, not a story concept.
### STANDARD +
It was introduced in version 4.0. In the game it appears as a red outline over the character's HP bar.

## gameplay
### LITE
Gameplay (not literal lore): while Bond of Life is active, healing the character is absorbed by the bond according to its value rather than restoring HP. It clears once healing equal to its value has been taken in, or when the player visits a Statue of The Seven.
### STANDARD +
- Maximum value: 200% of the character's Max HP.
- Display: the red outline covers half as much of the HP bar as its value, so a 100% bond overlays 50% of the bar.
- Timeout: if not cleared sooner, it clears 600 seconds (10 minutes) after its last increase.
- After the bond clears, some sources apply additional effects, which depend on whatever applied it.
### LARGE +
- Sources: certain enemies, weapons, artifacts and character talents apply or interact with it, and some talents and constellations scale with its value (the wiki keeps category lists of these).
- A weapon wielder does not receive that weapon's buff if the bond is cleared by a Statue of The Seven rather than by healing.
- Interface quirk: when a character with the bond eats healing foods such as apples, sunsettia, starshroom variants or bulle fruit, the notification reads "decreased" rather than "increased".

### ULTRA +
The wiki sorts related content by category: talents and constellations that scale with the bond, talents related to it, artifacts, weapons with passives that involve it, and enemies that apply it. Exact members change between versions, so check the in-game text of a specific item before citing it.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay interpretation (this project's recommendation, not canon text): portray it as a debt or curse on the body that makes recovery feel useless for a while, with relief at the statue. Do not quote the percentages in character or invent a lore origin for it.
### LARGE +
Because healing goes to paying off the bond, a healer in a scene may find their power seemingly wasted on a bonded ally. Pairing this with the Statue of The Seven as the place where it lifts matches the mechanic but is an interpretation, not stated canon.
