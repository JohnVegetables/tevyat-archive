## summary
### LITE
The Elemental Skill, or just Skill, is a Talent every playable character has from the start. It costs no Energy; instead it goes on cooldown after use. It deals Elemental DMG, never Physical, and usually produces elemental particles that recharge the party's Energy.
### STANDARD +
It is the everyday counterpart of the Elemental Burst, which needs full Energy.

## in_universe
### LITE
This is a gameplay category. In the world, the skill is simply what a character does with their element or training, such as a technique, a summoned construct or a stance. The game fixes one such signature move per character.

## gameplay
### LITE
Gameplay: using the Skill starts a cooldown whose length differs per character and which cannot be used again until it ends. Cooldown can be shortened, reset or extended by Talents, Constellations and other effects.
### STANDARD +
Some skills accept more than one input before the cooldown begins, such as Diluc's and Fischl's, and they differ in whether the cooldown starts at once or after the effect expires. Single-input skills, including held ones, start cooldown straight away, with held skills starting it after the hold ends. Some skills have several Charges, shown as small squares on the icon. Many have different tap and hold versions; the hold is usually stronger but has a longer cooldown. Most Skills generate Energy particles when they damage an enemy.
### LARGE +
Tartaglia is a special case. His Skill switches him between ranged and melee stances; in melee stance the Skill's stats apply to his Normal Attack, so all of its Skill DMG comes through Normal Attacks, and the cooldown depends on how long he stays in that stance. Talents, Constellations, weapons and artifact sets can raise Skill level, increase Skill DMG or react to its use.

### ULTRA +
The Skill's cooldown stat, number of charges and particle output differ for each character and are listed in the game's Talents menu and on each character's own entry rather than here. A small number of characters generate no particles from their Skill at all, and particle generation can have its own cooldown separate from the Skill's.

## limits
### STANDARD
The Skill is not the same as the Elemental Burst or as a Normal Attack. It is a game category, so characters do not name it in dialogue.
