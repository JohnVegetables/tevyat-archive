## summary
### LITE
A Moonsign Benediction passive is a Passive Talent carried by certain characters, mostly from Nod-Krai plus Zibai of Liyue. While its owner is in the party, the party's Moonsign Level rises by one. On Columbina and on Moon Wheel users, the passive also lets a base reaction turn into a Lunar Reaction. It is a game-mechanic label, not something characters discuss in the story.
### STANDARD +
Official wiki phrasing ties the passive to the Moonsign Team Bonus, which sits beside Elemental Resonance: one Moonsign character gives Nascent Gleam, two or more give Ascendant Gleam.

## gameplay
### LITE
Game mechanics, not lore. Every Benediction passive adds +1 Moonsign Level while its owner is in the party. Illuga, Jahoda and Aino have only this plain form.
### STANDARD +
The Lunar-enabling passives convert a base reaction triggered by any party member and add Base DMG that scales with a stat of the owner, per the wiki. Ineffa and Flins convert Electro-Charged into Lunar-Charged (0.7% Base DMG per 100 ATK, up to 14%). Lauma and Nefer convert Bloom into Lunar-Bloom (0.0175% per point of Elemental Mastery, up to 14%). Zibai and Linnea convert Hydro Crystallize into Lunar-Crystallize (0.7% per 100 DEF, up to 14%). Columbina converts all three (Electro-Charged, Bloom and Hydro Crystallize) into their Lunar forms, adding 0.2% Base DMG per 1,000 Max HP, up to 7%.
### LARGE +
Moonsign levels: one Moonsign character in the party gives Nascent Gleam; two give Ascendant Gleam, which enhances certain Talents and Skills of Moonsign characters. Non-Moonsign teammates also help once Ascendant Gleam is reached: for 20 seconds after casting a Skill or Burst, they raise nearby characters' Lunar Reaction DMG according to a stat tied to their element, to a maximum of 36%. See the Moonsign entry for details.

## roster
@version: 6.3
### STANDARD
Passive, owner and release as listed by the wiki:
- Assemblage Hub: Ineffa (5.8), Electro-Charged to Lunar-Charged.
- Old World Secrets: Flins (Luna I, 6.0), Electro-Charged to Lunar-Charged.
- Nature's Chorus: Lauma (Luna I, 6.0), Bloom to Lunar-Bloom.
- Force Limit Analysis: Aino (Luna I, 6.0), Moonsign level only.
- Dusklit Eaves: Nefer (Luna II, 6.1), Bloom to Lunar-Bloom.
- Rooftop Dash: Jahoda (Luna III, 6.2), Moonsign level only.
- Moonlight, Lent Unto You: Columbina (Luna IV, 6.3), all three conversions.
- The Coursing Sun and Moon: Zibai (Luna IV, 6.3), Hydro Crystallize to Lunar-Crystallize.
- Unwithering in Winter: Illuga (Luna IV, 6.3), Moonsign level only.
- Habitat Survey: Linnea (Luna VI, 6.5), Hydro Crystallize to Lunar-Crystallize.
### LARGE +
Reading the roster: the three reaction families map to the three Lunar Reactions, each with two Moon Wheel-style converters, and Columbina covers every family. The wiki's description says that those belonging to Columbina and Moon Wheel users additionally enable and enhance Lunar Reactions. Aino, Jahoda and Illuga add a level but change no reaction.

## in_universe
### STANDARD
Nothing found presents the Benediction as an in-world ritual. The surrounding themes come from Nod-Krai lore: kuuvahki, the moon-linked primordial energy, the Moon Wheels that channel it, and the Moon Goddess Columbina. Treat the passive as the game's way of showing a team gathering under that moonlight, not as a title or rank.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Project-recommended narrative interpretation, not canon. Characters should not say "my Moonsign Benediction passive" or quote numbers. If a scene needs the idea, show moonlight making a shared effort stronger: the owner steadies the party's reactions, and other teammates feed it through their own skills.

## limits
### STANDARD
Do not state that Zibai or any non-Nod-Krai character is a Nod-Krai native because they carry the passive. Do not claim a Moonsign rank exists in society. Do not assume Illuga, Jahoda or Aino alter reactions. Co-Op behaviour of Moonsign is unverified.

### ULTRA +
Release order, per the wiki change histories: Ineffa's passive came first in 5.8 (the version that introduced Lunar-Charged), then Flins, Lauma and Aino in Luna I (6.0), Nefer in Luna II (6.1), Jahoda in Luna III (6.2), Columbina, Zibai and Illuga in Luna IV (6.3), and Linnea in Luna VI (6.5). The wiki dates the Moonsign Team Bonus itself to Luna I, so how Ineffa's passive counted a level before 6.0 is not documented here. The in-game passive names differ across languages (the Chinese title is Yuezhao Zhuci, roughly Moonsign Blessing), and the English name is the only one this archive uses as a key.

## entry: Moonsign Benediction passives
@keys: Moonsign Benediction, Moonsign Benediction Passive, Benediction passive

The Talent type that adds one Moonsign Level while its owner is in the party. Owners: Ineffa, Flins, Lauma, Aino, Nefer, Jahoda, Columbina, Illuga, Zibai and Linnea. The passives of Columbina and the Moon Wheel users also enable Lunar Reactions.
