## summary
### LITE
Elemental Resonance is a game bonus granted when a full party contains certain element combinations. Two or more characters of the same element among the first four party slots trigger that element's resonance. Four different elements trigger Protective Canopy. It is a gameplay mechanic, not an in-world phenomenon.
### STANDARD +
Resonances depend only on the first four party members, work in Co-Op Mode, and do not need all members alive. A third or fourth character of the same element adds nothing more.

## gameplay
### LITE
Gameplay (not literal lore), as of 7.1:
- Fervent Flames (two Pyro): 40% shorter Cryo effect, ATK +25%.
- Soothing Water (two Hydro): 40% shorter Pyro effect, Max HP +25%.
- High Voltage (two Electro): 40% shorter Hydro effect; certain Electro reactions have a 100% chance to produce an Electro particle (5 s cooldown).
- Shattering Ice (two Cryo): 40% shorter Electro effect, CRIT Rate +15% against Frozen or Cryo-affected enemies.
- Impetuous Winds (two Anemo): stamina use -15%, movement speed +10%, skill cooldowns -5%.
- Enduring Rock (two Geo): shield strength +15%, plus damage and Geo RES reduction effects when shields or Moondrifts are nearby.
- Sprawling Greenery (two Dendro): Elemental Mastery +50 and reaction-triggered boosts.
- Protective Canopy (four unique elements): all Elemental RES +15% and Physical RES +15%.
### STANDARD +
- Enduring Rock details: when a shielded character or Moondrifts from Lunar-Crystallize are nearby, damage dealt rises 15%, and dealing damage lowers enemy Geo RES by 20% for 15 s.
- Sprawling Greenery details: after Burning, Quicken, Bloom or Lunar-Bloom, nearby party members gain 30 Elemental Mastery for 6 s; after Aggravate, Spread, Hyperbloom or Burgeon, they gain 20 for 6 s. The durations count independently.
- High Voltage triggers: Superconduct, Stellar-Conduct, Overloaded, Electro-Charged, Lunar-Charged, Quicken, Aggravate or Hyperbloom.
- Co-op rule: if some of a co-op party is inside a Domain, only the characters in the Domain determine resonance and benefit from it.
### LARGE +
Elemental Resonance is one of several team bonuses on the Team Bonus screen:
- Moonsign: Moonsign characters in the party give a Moonsign Level. One gives Nascent Gleam; two give Ascendant Gleam, which enhances Moonsign characters' talents and lets non-Moonsign characters raise Lunar reaction damage by up to 36% for 20 s after casting a Skill or Burst, scaling with an element-specific stat. The thresholds are 4,000 ATK (Pyro, Electro, Cryo), 60,000 Max HP (Hydro), 3,600 DEF (Geo), and 1,600 Elemental Mastery (Anemo, Dendro). Introduced with version 6.0.
- Hexerei: Secret Rite: two or more Hexerei characters in the party further enhance them, including activating their Witch's Eve Rite passive. Characters become Hexerei by completing Witch's Homework.
- Nightsoul Burst (region-based): with at least one Natlanese playable character, a Nightsoul Burst triggers at intervals of 18 s for one, 12 s for two, and 9 s for three or more Natlanese characters. Bennett and the Pyro Traveler do not count.
### ULTRA +
Region-based effects are not listed on the Party Setup menu, and several weapons and talents depend on party regions. Version notes: Elemental Resonance dates from 1.0; Moonsign 6.0; Hexerei 6.2 (per the archive's entries); Nightsoul Burst with Natlan 5.0. Values may shift in later patches.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay interpretation (this project's recommendation, not canon text): represent a resonance as companions who work especially well together, such as two Pyro users feeding each other's flames, with a visible shared glow of their element. Do not quote percentages in character. Protective Canopy can be shown as a diverse team covering one another's weaknesses.
### LARGE +
Canon does not say characters feel or name a "resonance", so do not have anyone announce it. Anemo pairs may move more freely, Geo pairs hold firmer shields, and Dendro pairs sense reactions more keenly. Keep these as flavour, not canon facts.

## limits
### LITE
Only a full party triggers resonance, except that Protective Canopy needs four unique elements. Extra same-element characters add nothing.
### STANDARD +
Unlisted: this entry does not cover individual talents or weapons that depend on the party's elements.
