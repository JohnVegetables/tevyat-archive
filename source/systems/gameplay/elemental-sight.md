## summary
### LITE
Elemental Sight is the ability to perceive the elements around you. Toggling it makes elemental traces visible, shows which nearby things are affected by an element and highlights what can be interacted with. In Teyvat it is bound up with owning a Vision, or at least having elemental sensitivity, which is rare but known.
### STANDARD +
The Traveler is among those canon confirms to have it, and the wiki also lists Amber, Lisa, Rosaria and Yanfei.

## in_universe
### LITE
Elemental traces, also called elemental trails, are left behind by almost anything elemental, from slimes to gods to the wind itself, and can also be left by elemental magic or Vision holders. Everyone knows they exist, but those without elemental affinity find them hard to sense.
### STANDARD +
Sensitivity varies even between people who can wield the elements, and it can be trained. Lisa says she heard the Traveler's sensitivity is very strong, and later only calls a drawing clear once the Traveler has copied what they saw with Elemental Sight. Bennett is implied to have weak sensitivity: he wanted to swap his goggles for "Alchemist's Goggles", an item rumoured to let anyone see even the faintest elemental energy. Timaeus dismissed those goggles as impossible. An allogene who gives up their Vision may stay sensitive, as Jiangxue does.
### LARGE +
Because a Vision helps field work and magical research, including this sight, scholars without one are seen as handicapped. The Fatui-run Northland Bank has some means of detecting elemental energy, which it uses to tell its certified bags of Mora apart. Dr. Edith has no Vision and so no Elemental Sight, yet says the Anemo-imbued leaves the Traveler brought back from the Symbol of Mondstadt's Hero really let one feel the wind, which hints that close proximity may let non-sensitive people notice the elements. Hurdle states that hilichurl bodies cannot withstand high elemental concentrations, so they normally avoid such areas, which suggests at least an instinctive sense for them.

## gameplay
### LITE
Gameplay: Elemental Sight is toggled with a button beside the minimap (middle mouse button by default on PC). A white range indicator spreads from the active character; background objects turn dark and muted while interactable ones are highlighted.
### STANDARD +
It shows elemental trails for bounties, between Seelie and their gardens, between Monetoo and their resting places and in certain quests and hidden exploration objectives, and it shows possible routes between a Phase Gate and the gates it connects to. It also shows the element affecting enemies and most interactable objects, and reveals enemy name tags. Highlight colours follow the element: white for nothing, red for fire, torches and explosive barrels, blue for things made Wet, light blue for recently deactivated flamethrowers and Mist Flowers, purple for unbroken Electro crystals, yellow for Geo constructs and ore, turquoise for Anemo ambers and green for Dendro vines and flammable plant matter.
### LARGE +
The sight switches itself off after the character moves a short distance, and its range depends on where the character stood when toggling, so far objects may not show. For bounties and some quests only the traces nearest the character appear. Enemies are highlighted even when inactive; shielded enemies take their shield's element while it holds. Not every elemental enemy is inherently affected by its element: Anemo Slimes never apply Anemo to themselves. Some things are not highlighted: most signs and tablets, bushes and small trees, trees yielding wood, and doors into areas of the same instance. It can be captured with the Kamera and is disabled during Third-Person Shooter gameplay.
### ULTRA +
A Special Elemental Sight exists for a character indwelling an Iktomisaurus, for Masters of the Night-Wind characters and for Mavuika. It widens the field of view and shows hidden details such as certain graffiti arrows and invisible barriers.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay (this project's interpretation, not canon): treat it as a sense, not a menu. A Vision holder can say they feel or see lingering elemental traces, noticing a trail, an enchanted thing or a hidden mechanism, but should not talk about toggles, cooldowns or colour codes. Characters without a Vision or elemental affinity should not perceive traces.

## limits
### STANDARD
Canon gives no rule for how far elemental senses reach, and does not say that every Vision holder perceives the same things. Do not assume that all allogenes see equally well, or that people without a Vision cannot sense strong elements at all.
