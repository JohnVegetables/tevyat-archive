## summary
### LITE
Diving is how the Traveler explores underwater in Fontaine. Fontaine's lakes can be dived in once the first Statue of The Seven there is activated; a power called the Transoceanic Sourcewater keeps Vision holders, the Traveler and Paimon from drowning and lets them breathe and speak underwater. Ordinary people without Visions need diving gear and training. Fontaine is the only nation where diving is a game mechanic.
### STANDARD +
Diving is a recognised profession in Fontaine, and a diving certificate can be earned there, according to Vasco. It arrived in Version 4.0 with Fontaine.

## in_universe
### LITE
The tutorial calls the power the "Transoceanic Sourcewater" and says it flows out from a Statue of The Seven to bless those moving through Fontaine's lakes. In the text of the world, it lets Vision holders (and certain others, such as the Traveler and Paimon) breathe and speak underwater.
### STANDARD +
People without Visions rely on diving gear and must complete specialised training. The diver Archenoul, hired by Spina di Rosula, says such newcomers must first go down with a professional, and warns that cramps or ringing in the ears can follow if a diver skips a warm-up or fails to adapt to water pressure. The Traveler also dives comfortably in the Sea of Bygone Eras, said to lie beneath depths untouched by sunlight, so cold and darkness do not seem to trouble them either.
### LARGE +
Fontainians often call the great lake that lies on top of their land a "sea", although geographically it is a lake, so "underwater" in Fontaine usually means the lakes of the raised mainland. The Sea of Bygone Eras, the waters south of that lake, once belonged to the same high sea and holds the ruins of Remuria; a commission such as Days Beneath the Waves shows divers doing ordinary salvage work.
### ULTRA +
Canon does not say what the Sourcewater is. It is presented as a blessing carried by the statues, and nothing read here ties it to the Hydro Archon or to Pneuma and Ousia. Keep it a mystery rather than assigning it a source.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not literal lore): Diving unlocks after activating the first Statue of The Seven in Fontaine, once the quest The Outlander Who Caught the Wind is complete. While diving the party cannot drown and uses Aquatic Stamina for fast swimming instead of ordinary Stamina; Aquatic Stamina cannot be increased or unlocked further. Characters share the same underwater abilities and can still be switched. Diving works in the lakes of the raised mainland and the Sea of Bygone Eras, but not in the lower surrounding waters or around the Nostoi region (the sea around Petrichor, per the Fontaine page).
### LARGE +
Normal Attacks, Elemental Skills and Bursts are replaced underwater. A water string stands in for the Normal Attack, and borrowed Xenochromatic Creature abilities stand in for the Skill; their damage is the same for every character regardless of level, equipment or stats. Xenochromatic Creatures are Fontemer Aberrants that never attack and cannot be harmed; striking one lets the party borrow an ability (for example the Armored Crab's shield and shockwave, or the Hunter's Ray's water blades) that lasts until the party fully leaves the water. Recovery Orbs found near schools of fish restore Aquatic Stamina and half of each character's maximum HP for the whole party. Underwater currents carry the party along set paths, and Echoing Conches or Sonar Waves reveal hidden objects. In Co-Op each ability applies only to the player who got it.
### ULTRA +
- Fontaine's Blessing is the in-game tutorial name for the Diving ability; the second tutorial explains how Sourcewater clusters beside the character let the player unleash borrowed powers.
- Characters with an Arkhe alignment show a matching aura around the Sourcewater bubble beside the active character's shoulder.
- Furina's utility passive, The Sea Is My Stage, reduces the cooldown of Xenochromatic abilities by 30%; it is the only character Talent that affects them.
- Fontemer Aberrants and specialised Clockwork Meka are met underwater, and passive Talents whose conditions can be met while diving still trigger.
- Exiting the water by sprinting makes Fontainian characters and the Traveler leap out; other characters surface slowly into ordinary Swimming.

## limits
### LITE
Diving is a Fontaine mechanic and does not apply in other nations.
### STANDARD +
Ordinary Swimming outside Fontaine uses Stamina, and the party cannot fight while swimming. Non-Vision holders in the story cannot dive freely without gear and training.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay interpretation (this project's recommendation, not canon text): treat the Sourcewater as a quiet, protective blessing. Vision holders, the Traveler and Paimon can talk and breathe underwater as if in air, while ordinary NPCs wear divesuits and take pre-dive precautions. Do not let an ordinary Fontainian simply dive without gear.
### LARGE +
Underwater scenes suit slow, bright, wondering description: light fading with depth, schools of fish, currents. Keep Xenochromatic creatures non-hostile, since they never attack. Do not name the Sourcewater's origin.

### STANDARD
The Transoceanic Sourcewater is the power behind diving in Fontaine. Tutorial text says it flows from a Statue of The Seven, protects those moving through the lakes and lets them breathe and speak underwater if they hold a Vision (and for the Traveler and Paimon). Its origin is not explained.

## entry: Xenochromatic Creature
@keys: Xenochromatic Creature, Xenochromatic Creatures, Xenochromatic
@keys2: Fontaine, diving, underwater

### STANDARD
Xenochromatic Creatures are special Fontemer Aberrants met underwater. They do not attack and cannot be damaged, but striking one lets a diver borrow its ability, such as a shield, water blades or a thrown hat-shaped bomb, until they leave the water.

## entry: Archenoul
@keys: Archenoul
@version: 4.0
### STANDARD
Archenoul is a diver in Poisson, Fontaine, employed by Spina di Rosula, who says they seldom take outside commissions. They explain that people without Visions need specialised training and a professional's company on first dives, and warn about cramps and pressure-related symptoms.
