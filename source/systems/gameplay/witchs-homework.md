## summary
### LITE
Witch's Homework is a system from Version 6.2 in which The Little Witch's Dictionary hands out tasks, made by the Hexenzirkel's mages, to friends of the Traveler. Finishing a character's task makes them a Hexerei character and unlocks a bonus Talent for them, the Witch's Eve Rite Passive. In story terms the "homework" is the witches' long-range preparation to help Mondstadt in a future time of need.
### STANDARD +
The Traveler gets the dictionary in the world quest "Unexpected Commission", set at the Knights of Favonius Headquarters in Mondstadt.

## in_universe
### LITE
Alice, a Hexenzirkel mage, wrote the dictionary with other mages. The title is a disguise: it claims to explain the storybook "The Little Witch and the Undying Fire", but the two books are unrelated and the dictionary is really full of tasks tailored to individual readers. The item text says it was made by the witches and is "actually filled with homework tailored by the Hexenzirkel for each reader."
### STANDARD +
Alice explains the reason in "Unexpected Commission": the Hexenzirkel promised Varka at the Tripartite Conference that in Mondstadt's greatest time of need the mages would return and help. Rather than appear out of nowhere, they chose to lay groundwork over time (a line she attributes to "M", the Hexenzirkel's Andersdotter). The tasks, drawn up after looking into Barbeloth's scryglass, are about striving toward a common goal, overcoming obstacles with trusted companions and becoming better people. Alice stresses this is preparation, not a warning that something is about to happen. Item and event text add the motto "To defy the current is to learn."
### LARGE +
Alice hid the dictionary among a large donation of books to the Mondstadt library, because bringing it into Teyvat openly was risky; the donation was also what brought the Traveler and Paimon to the library, where Lisa asked them to sort it. When Paimon opens the dictionary the library turns grey and she and the Traveler enter a mage's domain, where Alice, in her Dodoco form, explains it. Paimon accepts on the understanding that the tasks go to the Traveler's friends, because the pool of people who count as "a friend to all in Mondstadt" is the Traveler.

## gameplay
### LITE
Gameplay (not literal lore): each Witch's Homework quest belongs to one character. The player must own that character and have them at level 70 or higher. Finishing it makes the character a Hexerei character and unlocks their Witch's Eve Rite Passive, which also changes some of their Talents and Constellations.
### STANDARD +
Most of these enhancements work only when at least two Hexerei characters are in the party, which switches on the Hexerei: Secret Rite Team Bonus (own entry, Hexerei). The dictionary is a gadget that lets the player view and accept the tasks; the same quest also invites Razor, Fischl or Sucrose to the party from the event menu.

### ULTRA +
The wiki infobox files Witch's Homework under Mondstadt, and the system's quests are structured as one act and one quest per character, so each homework is a self-contained story. The list also includes characters with no particular Mondstadt link in the table, so treat the region label as a setting for the dictionary, not a requirement.

## release_note
@version: 6.2
### STANDARD
Witch's Homework arrived in Version 6.2, "A Nocturne of the Far North" (2025-12-03), together with the Hexerei Team Bonus. A later system with the same flavour, Witch's Revelation, followed in 6.7 (own entry).

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay interpretation (this project's recommendation, not canon text): treat the homework as the Hexenzirkel's gentle, slightly mischievous coaching, framed by Alice's cheer. A character who "has done their homework" has simply taken on a task from the dictionary with companions. Do not suggest Hexerei characters are all witches or Hexenzirkel members, or that a specific disaster is coming to Mondstadt.

## limits
### STANDARD
Canon text seen names only the dictionary's framing, the Hexenzirkel's promise and the broad themes of the tasks. The system does not say who is a witch, and "Hexerei" is a gameplay label. Do not invent additional rules for what each homework contains beyond what each character's quest shows.

## entry: The Little Witch's Dictionary
@keys: Little Witch's Dictionary, Witch's Homework
### STANDARD
A 5-star gadget received from Alice in "Unexpected Commission" and reusable. Its item text calls it a dictionary created by the witches that claims to explain the storybook "The Little Witch and the Undying Fire" but is in fact filled with homework tailored by the Hexenzirkel for each reader, and ends with the words "To defy the current is to learn. Embark on your journey with your companions and seek the crown of elevation!" The dictionary's contents are not related to the storybook.

## entry: Witch's Eve Rite Passive
@keys: Witch's Eve Rite Passive, Witch's Eve Rite
### STANDARD
The additional Talent a Hexerei character gains after finishing their Witch's Homework. Most of what it enhances, in the character's Talents and Constellations, applies when at least two Hexerei characters are in the party under Hexerei: Secret Rite.
