## summary
### LITE
Spiritways are pathways of constantly flowing Phlogiston in Natlan. Anyone who can ride one moves swiftly in the direction of the flow. They first appeared in Version 5.0.
### STANDARD +
Only Koholasaurs, or people who have mastered the relevant technique, can use them freely, and the in-game text warns that not all Spiritways are complete or completely safe.

## in_universe
### LITE
In-game text describes a Spiritway as a pathway formed by flowing Phlogiston. Riding it carries the traveller along the flow.
### STANDARD +
Spiritways may run one way or both ways; a route with portal-shaped ends is one-way, one without is bidirectional. A rider can join anywhere along the route and can leave at any point.
### LARGE +
Modern Natlanese cannot simply make Spiritways. Their ancestors built Spiritdoors using Phlogiston Engraving, a technique that imitates the methods of those who made the Spiritways. A Spiritdoor, when interacted with, creates a Spiritway or makes an unstable one manifest. Canon does not name the original makers; see uncertainties.
### ULTRA +
Spiritways glow brighter and more vividly when the one riding is a People of the Springs character, Mavuika, or someone indwelling a Koholasaur, which suggests the flow responds to its rider.

## who_can_ride
### STANDARD
A rider must be in the Nightsoul's Blessing state. The People of the Springs and Koholasaurs (through Saurian indwelling) can ride naturally. Mavuika is not of the People of the Springs; after a fourteenth round of modifications Xilonen made at her request, Mavuika's flamestrider gained the ability to manipulate Phlogiston and use Spiritways.
### LARGE +
Leaving the Nightsoul's Blessing state, or ejecting from an indwelled Koholasaur, takes the rider off the Spiritway.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not literal lore): riding a Spiritway consumes no Nightsoul points, Phlogiston or Stamina.
### LARGE +
Collectibles and hazards appear along the route: Flowlight Orbs and Flowlight Targets to collect or break, and Flowlight Rifts to avoid. A rider can jump to dodge hazards, and Flowlight Leap carries them further.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay interpretation (this project's recommendation, not canon text): present a Spiritway as a glowing current of Phlogiston that sweeps a rider along at speed, bright when a Natlanese traveller uses it. Do not let an ordinary visitor ride one without a Nightsoul-based method, and do not invent a Spiritway network or origins beyond what canon states.

## entry: Spiritdoor
@keys: Spiritdoor, Spiritdoors, Phlogiston Engraving
@keys2: Spiritway, Natlan
### STANDARD
Spiritdoors are devices made by the ancestors of modern Natlanese using Phlogiston Engraving to imitate the techniques of the Spiritway makers. Interacting with one creates a Spiritway or brings an unstable one into being.
