## summary
### LITE
"Constellation" means two linked things in Teyvat. In lore, the stars of a constellation stand for a person's fate, and the Heavenly Principles are said to pull those stars like strings on the people of the mortal world. In gameplay, each playable character has six Constellation levels, upgrades unlocked one at a time with that character's Stella Fortuna.
### STANDARD +
Every Vision holder has a personal star sign: Jean's is Leo Minor, which canon reads as strength and responsibility shouldered too young. The Traveler, Archons, adepti and a few others have special constellations that depict them personally.

## in_universe
### LITE
The stars of Teyvat are tied to Fate. Constellations are an extension of that idea: patterns of Stella Fortuna that trace what a person will be, and strings by which the Heavenly Principles direct mortals.
### STANDARD +
Each Vision holder's fate is linked to a specific constellation, and each constellation represents particular characteristics. Jean's Leo Minor represents "strength and responsibility shouldered too young." People without Visions have fates that are also reflected in the stars, but they do not seem to be tied to named constellations. The stars themselves are physical celestial bodies called Stella Fortuna.
### LARGE +
Dragons, which predate the Heavenly Principles' control over Teyvat, and Descenders are not subject to this puppeteering, per Neuvillette's character story and the Sovereigns' council records the wiki cites. Beings who are "overseers of the mortal realm" or who "defy the world itself" are instead given special constellations that depict them. The wiki's list of such cases includes the Traveler, the Seven Archons (Furina among them), the adepti, Albedo, Durin, Yae Miko, the Wanderer, Neuvillette, Flins, Columbina and Sandrone. Their constellation names often translate into their own titles: Venti's is Carmen Dei ("God's Song"), Zhongli's is Lapis Dei ("God's Stone"), Durin's is Draco Rubedo ("Red Dragon"), Albedo's is Princeps Cretaceus ("Chalk Prince") and the Wanderer's is Peregrinus ("Wanderer").
Such beings can still be given a constellation later: Ineffa, a part of Ix Raq'lapuj Chue'q Buluk's mind that was given its own consciousness, has one.
### ULTRA +
Some people can handle "inauspicious stars". Raiden Ei's Elemental Skill, Transcendence: Baleful Omen, refers to them, and Citlali says she can bind her own Stella Fortuna into objects. She also notes that her Stella Fortuna "return" every year on her birthday, which the wiki reads as a Vision holder's constellation being unusually reachable that day. In the limited-time event Unreconciled Stars, a constellation was shown falling as meteors; canon does not say why.
The Little Witch and the Undying Fire likens the stars to strings by which the Heavenly Principles puppeteer humans. In Competition on the Sands, Layla wonders whether Ley Lines are the reflection of the constellations seen from above, and Teleport Waypoints are said to follow "a certain order"; treat this as a hint, not a fact.

## fate_and_changes
@version: 6.6
@spoiler: major
### ULTRA
The wiki's Fate page says that after Irminsul was destroyed in the Archon Quest "Truth Amongst the Pages of Purana" (6.6), the artificial system of Fate cannot be as easily directed by Celestia. It also records that Barbeloth, inspecting Durin after his fate was rewritten, found that his fate no longer existed among the stars. Do not generalise these two cases to all constellations.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not literal lore): each playable character (the Traveler is handled separately) has six Constellation levels, unlocked in order by spending one Stella Fortuna per level. Constellation effects work like extra passive talents: they raise effectiveness or add capabilities to existing abilities. The third and fifth levels are normally a three-level boost to one specific Combat Talent.
### LARGE +
Stella Fortuna comes from getting duplicates of a character from Wishes, and the 4-star kind can also come from Paimon's Bargains or events. Some 5-star characters' Stella Fortuna can be earned through the periodic event "To Temper Thyself and Journey Far". A character's Stella Fortuna cannot be used for anyone else. Once a character is at Constellation level 6, further duplicates are converted into Masterless Stella Fortuna. Talent info screens do not show the changes that Constellation effects make to talents, except the direct talent-level increases. Lyney's third level (Version 4.0) was the first to raise the Normal Attack level instead of the Skill. Aloy's Constellation levels cannot be unlocked and have no listed effects.
### ULTRA +
The Traveler has no Constellation until they align with an element. Their levels are not bought with Stella Fortuna: they come from Archon Quests, Souvenir Shops, Adventure Rank rewards (Anemo only), Statue of The Seven upgrades (Electro, Dendro, Hydro and Cryo) and Primal Flame Resonance through Tribe Reputation (Pyro only).

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay interpretation (this project's recommendation, not canon text): characters rarely discuss "Constellation 3" as a number; in-world, a constellation is a star sign and a hint of fate. Use it for atmosphere (a character glancing at the stars, an astrologer reading a pattern) rather than game talk. Mona is the natural voice for this, since she reads fortunes.
### LARGE +
Keep the gameplay system out of the fiction unless the user asks about it. Keep the fate theme ambiguous: canon says the Heavenly Principles impose Fate, and that Descenders and dragons are not bound by it, but it does not say the stars dictate every choice.

## entry: Stella Fortuna
@keys: Stella Fortuna, Masterless Stella Fortuna
### STANDARD
Stella Fortuna are the physical stars that make up a constellation in lore. In gameplay, they are the item that unlocks one Constellation level for the matching character; extra copies at level 6 convert into Masterless Stella Fortuna.

## entry: Constellation names
@keys: Leo Minor, Draco Rubedo, Carmen Dei, Lapis Dei, Princeps Cretaceus
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Constellations are named in Latin-style terms that sometimes match the character: Jean's Leo Minor, Venti's Carmen Dei ("God's Song"), Zhongli's Lapis Dei ("God's Stone"), Albedo's Princeps Cretaceus ("Chalk Prince"), Durin's Draco Rubedo ("Red Dragon") and Xiao's Alatus Nemeseos ("Winged Nemesis"). Columbina is the only character whose constellation name equals her own name.
