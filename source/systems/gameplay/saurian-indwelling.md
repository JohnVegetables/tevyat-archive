## summary
### LITE
Saurian Indwelling is an ability of the Traveler in Natlan: their spirit slips into a Saurian (one of Natlan's dragon-kin) and acts through its body, so they can run, climb, glide or swim as that creature does. The in-game tutorial credits "a mysterious power" with the gift. It is not presented as a Vision or an Archon's power, and almost nobody else in Natlan seems to know it exists.
### STANDARD +
It works on Saurians of the six tribal species and on small totem-like markers called Spiritsconces, and it ends when the Traveler chooses to leave or the body is lost. Each species brings its own movement and elemental strike, so indwelling is how the Traveler crosses ground that a human on foot could not.

## in_universe
### LITE
Natlan's Saurians are descendants of Pyro vishaps that lost their ancient intelligence; they follow emotion and trust their human companions. Tribe members learn to read a Saurian's moods through long companionship. Indwelling is different: the Traveler's spirit enters the animal, and while inside, the speech of Saurians becomes understandable to them. Nobody is shown teaching the Traveler this; it simply works.
### STANDARD +
The Traveler first indwells a Saurian in the World Quest "Lost in the Woods" (5.0). Brought into a strange golden space on arrival in Natlan, they and Paimon find a weakened adult Tepetlisaurus and a whelp, later named Little One, caught in trouble. The Traveler's body vanishes and the adult rises with new energy; Paimon realises the Traveler is "inside" it. The Traveler uses it to free the whelp. Later, people who notice the effect do not understand it: Chasca sensed that an indwelt Qucusaur had the Traveler's demeanour but chose not to ask, and Coyllur found the Traveler oddly familiar after first meeting them as a Qucusaur.
### LARGE +
Reactions show how little is known. The Saurian Antu recognised the Traveler inside a Qucusaur but believed it was a Qucusaur that could turn into a human. In the Night Kingdom, the spirit Chuychu gathered Saurian spirits for the Traveler to indwell, knowledge she did not have in life. The Traveler also indwells Saurians to carry out errands: an adult Qucusaur to return to Atoco with Paimon and Iansan while looking for a cure for Abyssal Corrosion Syndrome, adult Tepetlisaurs while searching for Antu, and an adult Iktomisaur while helping Mualani with Wavey Bay's attractions.

### ULTRA +
Related Saurian background that frames the ability: Saurians have their own language that only other Saurians (and surviving pre-devolution dragons such as Ajaw) understand, and most cannot speak human words. Some are born with the Flamelord's Blessing, a mark of much stronger ancestral power that tribes fear because its bearers may sicken or lose their minds; Kukulkan is later revealed to have shaped how it appears, to pass power and wisdom on to future dragons. Tribes traditionally refuse bearers of the mark, and the volcano ritual to be accepted again was rarely successful.

## the_chosen_of_dragons
@version: 5.0
@spoiler: minor
### LARGE
Kukulkan, while hiding his identity, called the Tepetlisaurus whelp Little One the "Chosen of Dragons" and said hope lay with him. The same title names the World Quest series "In the Footsteps of the Chosen of Dragons", in which the Traveler helps Little One with its dangerous red mark, the Flamelord's Blessing, and finally takes it to the Great Volcano of Tollan to lift the curse. The wiki links the title to the Traveler's indwelling ability; canon does not state that link outright (see uncertainties).

## spiritsconces
### STANDARD
Spiritsconces are small, colourful totems found across Natlan. Saurians are described as able to enter a "Spiritsconce" state under particular circumstances; indwelling one lets the Traveler take on the Saurian's original form. When the Traveler leaves a Saurian without killing it, a Spiritsconce is left where they stood, and defeated adult Saurians leave one too.
### LARGE +
Gameplay detail: leftover Spiritsconces from ended indwellings are limited (four, or three while indwelling, with the oldest vanishing first), natural ones return after 15 seconds, and an indwelt enemy Saurian respawns at its place after a minute. A 4-second cooldown separates one indwelling from the next. These are mechanics, not lore.

## species_and_abilities
### STANDARD
Each tribe's Saurian offers its own way of moving, tied to an element and to its tribe's land.
- Tepetlisaurus (Geo): burrows and climbs rock at speed; home of the Children of Echoes.
- Yumkasaurus (Dendro): grapples and glides, grabs and spits out objects; Scions of the Canopy.
- Koholasaurus (Hydro): swims and dashes through water and Liquid Phlogiston; People of the Springs.
- Qucusaurus (Pyro): flies and dives; Flower-Feather Clan.
- Iktomisaurus (Cryo): glides, high jumps, and can scan and place Nightspirit Graffiti objects; Masters of the Night-Wind.
- Tatankasaurus (Electro): charges, smashes Pulverite and pushes heavy objects; Collective of Plenty.
### LARGE +
Further detail: a burrowing Tepetlisaur cannot be left manually and spends Phlogiston as it moves. Yumkasaurs also take half the usual fall damage. Qucusaurs can change altitude in flight, while Iktomisaurs only glide downward. A Tatankasaur that charges hard into an obstacle, or taps its skill, enters a "Spirited" state, letting it leap off terrain and briefly cross water or Liquid Phlogiston. Little One, as a Tepetlisaurus, has a quest-only version of the Tepetlisaurus kit.

## asha
@version: 5.0
### LARGE
At the Easybreeze Holiday Resort (Tenochtzitoc) the same mechanic works on Asha, friendly paint-spraying mascot creatures described as a kind of Monetoo. Asha can walk up walls and on water, grapple and throw paintballs without using Phlogiston, and indwelling one ends if the Traveler leaves the resort.

## limits
### LITE
Some creatures refuse. A few quest-linked Saurians, such as "Half-Baked" in "Special Friends", reject the attempt with a message that a strong will refuses the indwelling. Indwelt Saurians are poor fighters and cannot dash to dodge attacks.
### STANDARD +
A Saurian is forced out if it tries to enter the main settlement of a tribe that is not its own: the screen warns that outside Saurians may not enter, and the Traveler is ejected. Only the matching species is allowed in each tribe's home (Tepetlisaurus for the Children of Echoes, and so on, as listed above). Per the wiki, runes bar outsiders, and who made them is unexplained. Leaving Natlan's boundaries, teleporting or entering instance rooms also ends the indwelling. While indwelling, the Traveler cannot cook, use a Statue of The Seven, or use a Tablet of Tona.
### LARGE +
Deep water drowns most Saurians; only Koholasaurs swim freely, and Tatankasaurs can cross water briefly in a charge. Indwelling an enemy Saurian is unrewarding: killing it grants no EXP, materials or Mora. Other Saurians may be curious when an indwelt one approaches but will not aggravate unless attacked.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not literal lore): indwelling is an exploration mechanic introduced with Natlan in 5.0. An indwelt Saurian uses its own Phlogiston meter before the player's. Phlogiston is refilled near Everflame Seeds, Teleport Waypoints and Statues of The Seven, by touching Flaming Seeds of Phlogiston, and by collecting or smashing certain items. All indwelt Saurians share the same stats. While indwelling, NPCs other than Saurians usually say only "..." except for quest-specific dialogue. Indwelling counts as leaving combat only if the indwelt enemy was the sole aggravated one.
### LARGE +
Exiting is done by holding the exit button, like leaving the Waverider.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay interpretation (this project's recommendation, not canon text): treat indwelling as a quiet, uncanny act. The Traveler's body goes still or seems to vanish and the Saurian moves with unusual purpose and awareness. Natlanese who see it should be puzzled rather than certain; they might sense something familiar about the animal without naming it.
### LARGE +
Tribe members read Saurians by body language, so a Saurian that behaves oddly and shows a human's habits invites curiosity, not alarm. Do not give any other character the ability, do not make the Saurian speak human language, and do not claim the power's origin is known. Remember the Traveler cannot bring an indwelt Saurian into a rival tribe's village.

## entry: Spiritsconce
@keys: Spiritsconce, Spiritsconces
### STANDARD
A Spiritsconce is a small, colourful totem-like marker left behind in Natlan. Saurians can enter a "Spiritsconce" state in particular circumstances, and the Traveler can indwell one to take on the Saurian's original form. Leaving a Saurian alive, or defeating an adult one, leaves a Spiritsconce behind.

## entry: Saurian tribal borders
@keys: External Saurians, Saurian borders
@keys2: Natlan, tribe, Saurian
### STANDARD
Each Saurian group has its own main territory, which neighbouring Saurians respect; entry is by a local Saurian's invitation and escort. The same borders broadly mark the lands of the Natlanese tribes. In play, an indwelt Saurian tries to enter a rival tribe's village and the Traveler is ejected; the wiki says canon explains this with runes whose origin is unknown. Little One, the Tepetlisaurus whelp, alone slips through those of the Collective of Plenty, perhaps because of the volcano's instability or the Flamelord's Blessing (unconfirmed).
