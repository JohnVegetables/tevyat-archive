## summary
### LITE
A party is a team of up to four playable characters. The player controls one at a time and can swap to another with a short delay. Team bonuses such as Elemental Resonance apply to the party as a whole.
### STANDARD +
The ability to set up parties unlocks after the quest "Unexpected Power".

## in_universe
### LITE
Gameplay abstraction: in the story the Traveler travels with a handful of friends, but canon never states a four-person limit or an instant swap between them. Treat party swapping as the game's way of presenting who is fighting rather than a rule of the world.

## gameplay
### LITE
Gameplay: switching characters has a 1-second cooldown. Up to four characters are deployed, added left to right, and a team with none cannot be deployed. Trial-character stages can add a fifth, the trial character, to a full party of four.
### STANDARD +
Party Setup is opened from the Paimon Menu or with L on PC. There are four default teams ("Party 1" to "Party 4"), which can be renamed but not disbanded, and a maximum of 15 teams. The deployed team cannot be disbanded. Characters can be reordered by dragging, and the Configure Team and Quick Setup menus help manage them. The Party Setup background can change by region, with variants for Nod-Krai (also depending on Moonsign characters) and Snezhnaya (depending on Stellar Linchpin characters), if the player enables that setting.
### LARGE +
Some talents, Constellations, weapon passives and artifacts only work while the character stays on the field, so effects can end after a swap; others apply to the whole party. Co-Op Mode keeps the total at four: two players get 2 each; three players get 2, 1 and 1; four players get 1 each. Most food effects do not apply to characters owned by another player.
### ULTRA +
In Version 1.0 a bug let a player field five characters through revival, related to the fact that the party system supports five to fit a trial character. Many characters show an object, elemental effect or companion in their Party Setup animation; Venti, Zhongli, the Raiden Shogun and Nahida keep a persistent elemental effect after it finishes, and Charlotte and Tartaglia are the only ones whose outfits stay changed.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay (this project's interpretation, not canon): in fiction, the Traveler's friends take turns in a fight and cover for each other. Avoid telling characters they have been "swapped out"; treat it as people stepping in and out of a battle.

## limits
### STANDARD
The four-character limit, cooldown and menu are mechanics. Canon does not limit how many friends the Traveler may have around them.
