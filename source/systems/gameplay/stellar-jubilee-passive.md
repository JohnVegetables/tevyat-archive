## summary
### LITE
A Stellar Jubilee Passive is a passive Talent held by characters with a Stellar Linchpin, plus the Cryo Traveler. It is what lets Stellar Glimmer reactions happen at all: with a holder in the party, the matching Cryo reactions turn into Snezhnayan Stellar Glimmer reactions. Sandrone, Odette and Vesna each have one, as does the Cryo Traveler.
### STANDARD +
This is a gameplay label, not a term used by characters in the story.

## gameplay
### LITE
Gameplay, not literal lore. With a holder in the party, a Superconduct triggered by any party member becomes Stellar-Conduct, and a Cryo Swirl becomes Stellar Swirl. The passive also raises the base damage of the converted reaction in proportion to the holder's ATK, and puts the holder into a "Radiance" state.
### STANDARD +
- Radiance: Stellar-Conduct applies when the holder is inside a Polestar Field, the area a Stellar-Conduct creates around its Starlight Prism.
- Radiance: Stellar Swirl applies for 8 seconds after a nearby party member triggers Stellar Swirl.
- Sandrone (Light of Rationalisme), Odette (Dance of Aurore) and the Cryo Traveler (Illusory Frostmirror) convert both reactions. Vesna (Splendid Prelude) converts Cryo Swirl only.
### LARGE +
- Bonus per 100 ATK: Sandrone and Odette add 0.7% base reaction damage, capped at 14%. The Cryo Traveler adds 0.35%, capped at 7%. Vesna adds 0.7% to Stellar Swirl, capped at 14%.
- A character can only be in one Radiance state at a time; where both could apply, Radiance: Stellar-Conduct takes priority (stated in Sandrone's separate talent text).
- The Radiance states make some skills deal their damage as Stellar-Conduct or Stellar Swirl damage, for example Vesna's Spirit Blades in Radiance: Stellar Swirl.
### ULTRA +
Stellar Glimmer reactions are a subset of elemental reactions that require a holder of a passive like this in the party. They also interrupt a particular attack of Snowland Fae and Aberrant Chimeric Monsters, and the Starlight Prism of a Stellar-Conduct matters in the Snezhnayan open world (see its own entry).

## in_universe
### STANDARD
Gameplay abstraction; the story gives no in-world name for this passive. Canon context: Stellar Linchpins are Vision-like objects from Snezhnaya, and the Fatui's research ties them to the dead Third Descender, with users showing a power that seems to come from beyond Teyvat. In the Version 7.0 Special Program it was said that Stellar Glimmer reactions were introduced to ancient Snezhnaya by Saarelainen.
### LARGE +
The Fatui seek the energy of Stellar Linchpins for Project Mistilteinn, a weapon against the Heavenly Principles (per the quest "Mistilteinn", as cited by the wiki). Why the Cryo Traveler can use the same reactions without a Linchpin is not explained in the pages read.

## holders
### STANDARD
- Sandrone, Light of Rationalisme (released 6.7; the first holder, with Stellar-Conduct).
- Odette, Dance of Aurore (released 7.0).
- Vesna, Splendid Prelude (released 7.1).
- Traveler (Cryo), Illusory Frostmirror (version not verified).
### LARGE +
The wiki's list of Stellar Linchpin users also names Danica and Mitya, who are not playable here, so they have no passive of this kind.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay interpretation (this project's recommendation, not canon text): treat the passive only as the reason that, around these characters, frost and lightning or frost and wind combine into a Snezhnayan star-like effect. Do not have characters name or discuss a "Stellar Jubilee Passive". Describe the result instead: a prism of light and a field of cold glittering light for Stellar-Conduct, a drifting vortex that bursts into frost for Stellar Swirl.

## limits
### STANDARD
It enables only Stellar-Conduct (Cryo with Electro) and Stellar Swirl (Anemo with Cryo). Vesna's version covers Stellar Swirl alone. Do not extend it to other element pairs or to people without a Linchpin, apart from the Cryo Traveler.
