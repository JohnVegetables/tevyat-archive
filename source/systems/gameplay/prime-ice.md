## summary
### LITE
Prime Ice is a pale ice found across Snezhnaya that does not melt like ordinary frost. The Traveler can harvest it with the Fab-Vision function of the Eye of Graeae to gain Prime Ice Resource, then spend that resource, plus Kresnik Energy, to build Prime Ice Constructs: heat sources, grappling hooks, launch pads and more. Constructs can be taken down again for a refund of the ice.
### STANDARD +
It is introduced in the Archon Quest "Into the Wind, Into the Frigid North" (7.0), where the Eye of Graeae teaches the Traveler to clear blocking ice and prop up a door with a cube. It is an exploration tool, not a combat element.

## in_universe
### LITE
In-world, Prime Ice is a durable ice native to Snezhnaya. The townsfolk of Svetloledovka make a living by harvesting it. Ordinary humans cannot shape it, but Snowland Fae can manipulate it naturally.
### STANDARD +
The Eye of Graeae, a device-bound entity, is the Traveler's way of working it. It tells the Traveler that constructs last only briefly and cannot be stabilised during the development period, so it was allowed to use Kresnik Kristall as a heat source. The Eye also says it has forgotten what warmth feels like. Item text describes the glaciafruit, a star-shaped fruit, as born from Prime Ice after a peculiar change in the permafrost, "for reasons unknown".
### LARGE +
Prime Ice also appears as scenery that people cannot rebuild: ornaments on city buildings, vases in Svetloledovka, windows in White Birch Snowgrave, floors in the House of Hesperides, hats on Jack Frosts. These show the material is part of Snezhnayan architecture and craft, not only a puzzle obstacle. Canon does not link Prime Ice to Cryo power, a Cryo Gnosis or a Vision.
### ULTRA +
The Eye of Graeae is described on the wiki as a creation of Aksinya and the Hvergelmir, split into three segments before the Cataclysm for the "prototype human" experiment; the Traveler restores its scattered power in the 7.0 Archon Quest and later sends it and Aksinya into the Shadow Realm. The device the Traveler keeps retains every function, Fab-Vision included, but loses the personality. Whether Prime Ice itself was part of that experiment is not stated.

## gameplay
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not literal lore): Fab-Vision is a mode of the Eye of Graeae gadget, usable in Snezhnaya while running, falling or fighting but not while gliding or swimming. Harvesting a deposit gives Prime Ice Resource (most deposits roughly 15 to 70 units; the largest are rare, often do not respawn and fill the 400-unit storage). Building spends resource and a percentage of Kresnik Energy; removing a construct refunds the resource.
### LARGE +
- Each construct type has a per-area placement limit. By default the oldest of that type is replaced and refunded; this can be switched off in Construct Details.
- Constructs placed in an invalid spot are destroyed and the resource refunded, but the Kresnik Energy spent is not. Most are destroyed (and refunded) if a train hits them.
- Standard constructs and their roles: Cube (raises or blocks things), Basic Heat Source (warms the player so Kresnik Energy is not drained), Link Mechanism (paired grappling line between points), Grappling Gun (pulls to hooks), Launching Platform (higher ground; also launches juvenile Jack Frosts), Storage Device (item storage), Ambient Bollard (periodic buff), Sign: Icon and Sign: Text (messages for other explorers).
- Blueprints: Cube and Basic Heat Source are unlocked in "Into the Wind, Into the Frigid North"; the rest after "Gunfire in the Silent Lands".
- Inside a Polestar Field, usually from a Starlight Prism, most constructs get extra effects: the Cube applies Cryo, the Basic Heat Source generates Kresnik Energy, the Grappling Gun and Launching Platform reach further, and Ambient Bollard buffs are stronger.
### ULTRA +
- Temporary Blueprints are short-lived constructs copied from a quest or location (matryoshka doll, snowball launcher, blockade spikes, phonograph, animal statues, resonant pillar, seats, gift box, wall pieces and others). Only one can be held, and it expires once the player travels far from where it was copied. Several come from limited-time or quest content.
- Co-op constructs: constructs built by other players can appear in your world, can be liked by players and NPCs, can be removed or anchored from the map, and sharing can be switched off in permission settings.
- Alternate appearances for some constructs unlock through exploration: Jack Frost (bought at the Jack Frosts' Goodies Shop), Polyarbear (a quest reward), and Ballerina, Hound and Tides bollard looks from Meeting Point rewards.
- Starlight Prisms made by Stellar-Conduct behave much like constructs once reactivated from a Prism Mirage.

## roleplay
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
Roleplay interpretation (this project's recommendation, not canon text): treat the Eye of Graeae's tools as a craft, not magic anyone can learn. The Traveler can spend the Eye's stored resource to raise a wall, a stair or a warm brazier, and gives the material back by taking the thing down. Describe pale, hard ice that shapes quickly under Fab-Vision and stays cold to the touch. Keep scale small: bridges, hooks and braziers, not fortresses.
### LARGE +
- A Prime Ice heat source works because of Kresnik Kristall; without that, it is simply ice. Do not let characters warm themselves on Prime Ice alone.
- Snowland Fae can shape Prime Ice by instinct. A Fae watching the Traveler build can be impressed or amused, since a human needs a gadget.
- Because constructs are short-lived in canon, a lasting Prime Ice structure should have a stated reason.
### ULTRA +
- Do not give Prime Ice shaping to Cryo Vision holders as a general ability, and do not imply the Traveler does it without the Eye.
- Do not say Prime Ice is the Cryo Archon's power, a Gnosis or a Vision; canon makes no such link.

## examples
### LARGE
Scene seeds: the Traveler places a cube to hold up a sagging door while a Jack Frost slips under it; a Basic Heat Source keeps a group of travellers from freezing at a train station; a grappling line crosses a gorge; a storage device left at a camp lets a later traveller find supplies. All of these use gadget mechanics from the 7.0 content.

## limits
### LITE
Prime Ice constructs can only be built in Snezhnaya using the Eye of Graeae. Each type has a limit per area, and the Traveler can only carry 400 units of resource.
### STANDARD +
Fab-Vision cannot be used while gliding or swimming. Kresnik Energy is needed to build, and is used up faster in colder zones. Constructs cannot be stacked or placed where the player is standing.
### LARGE +
Items left in a storage device are not refunded if the device is removed, and withdrawals are capped each day. Treat all of these as mechanics, not world rules.

## entry: Prime Ice Construct
@keys: Prime Ice Construct, Prime Ice Constructs, Construct Blueprint, Fab-Vision
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
A Prime Ice Construct is any object the Traveler builds from Prime Ice Resource with the Eye of Graeae, unlocked by blueprints. They are short-lived by design, can be removed for a refund, and many gain extra effects inside a Polestar Field. The first constructs the Traveler learns are the Cube and the Basic Heat Source.
### LARGE +
The Eye of Graeae explains that Prime Ice Constructs last only briefly and cannot be stabilised during the development period, so it was authorised to rely on Kresnik Kristall. See the main Prime Ice entry for each construct's role.

## entry: Prime Ice Resource
@keys: Prime Ice Resource, Prime Ice unit, Prime Ice units
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
Prime Ice Resource is the material stored in the Eye of Graeae's internal reservoir. Item text says it can be used to build the mechanisms and constructs in the blueprints. It is gained by harvesting Prime Ice or deconstructing constructs, and the gadget holds at most 400 units.

## entry: Glaciafruit
@keys: Glaciafruit
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
Glaciafruit is a star-shaped fruit that grows from a Prime Ice branch on the Snezhnayan tundra. Taking the branch away with the Eye of Graeae drops the fruit into the snow and returns a little Prime Ice Resource. Item text says it forms from Prime Ice for reasons unknown; it is edible and used in cooking.

## entry: Starlight Prism and Polestar Field
@keys: Starlight Prism, Polestar Field, Prism Mirage
@version: 7.0
### LARGE
A Starlight Prism is the result of Stellar-Conduct, a Stellar Glimmer reaction between Cryo and Electro. It sustains a Polestar Field around it, which gives nearby constructs and characters bonuses and keeps characters from losing heat or Kresnik Energy in Snezhnaya. In the open world, a prism left standing can become a Prism Mirage that the player can reactivate to last indefinitely, like a construct.
