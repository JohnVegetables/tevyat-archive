## summary
### LITE
Hexerei (German for witchcraft) is a party Team Bonus introduced in Version 6.2. Certain characters become "Hexerei characters" by completing their Witch's Homework quests. Bring two or more into the party and the bonus Hexerei: Secret Rite activates, strengthening them.
### STANDARD +
The system is built around the Hexenzirkel's "homework" for the Traveler. The Hexerei characters at launch were Sucrose, Mona, Durin, Klee, Albedo, Venti, Fischl and Razor; Varka, Nicole, Lohen and Prune were added later.

## in_universe
### STANDARD
In the fiction, the Witch's Homework is set by witches. In the quest Unexpected Commission, Lisa of the Knights of Favonius library and the mage Alice have the Traveler sort a huge anonymous donation of books, among them banned ones, and the Traveler receives The Little Witch's Dictionary. Its description says it was made by the witches and "claims to explain" the storybook The Little Witch and the Undying Fire, while really containing homework tailored by the Hexenzirkel for each reader.
### LARGE +
The Hexenzirkel was founded by Alice and is described as a mysterious group of powerful witches pursuing mostly personal goals. Not every Hexerei character is shown as a member: Nicole lists the Hexenzirkel as an affiliation, and Mona is a student of its astrologer Barbeloth, but canon does not make the whole roster members.

## gameplay
### LITE
Gameplay: finishing a character's Witch's Homework makes them a Hexerei character. With at least two Hexerei characters in the party, Hexerei: Secret Rite is active.
### STANDARD +
Requirements: complete the Prologue Archon Quest Act III, finish Witch's Homework: Solution (the quest begins in Mondstadt City) to receive The Little Witch's Dictionary, own the character and have them at level 70 or higher. Each character then has their own homework quest.
In Witch's Homework: Solution, the Traveler may also invite one of Fischl, Sucrose or Razor to the party; only one can be chosen.
The Secret Rite gives each Hexerei character their Witch's Eve Rite Passive, an additional talent, and enhances some of their other talents and Constellations. Most of those effects work only while two or more Hexerei characters are in the party. Two weapons, Athame Artis and The Daybreak Chronicles, show their own effects when the party has the bonus.
### LARGE +
The roster in the wiki's Witch's Homework table (character: quest "act" then follow-up quest, passive):
- Albedo: Of Questions Beyond Questions / Old Friends and New Knowledge; passive Book of Blinding Light.
- Klee: Of Leaves That Spark / The Great Mage's Journey of Trials; Sparkborne Magic.
- Mona: Of Untested Insight / A Dry Well Doth Not Reflect the Stars; Genesis of Starsigns.
- Venti: Of the Waking of Wind / Tales of Time and Wind; Temporal Wind's Eulogy.
- Durin: Of This World / A Child Wandering the World; Ode to Ascension.
- Varka: The Untitled Question / Stories to Sate the Glass and Scroll; Dawn's Return.
- Nicole: The Role of a Guide...? / Witch's Homework: The Role of a Guide...?; Light in the Darkness.
- Lohen: Of Various Foes / Proof of Strength; Unhealing Thorn.
- Fischl: Of the Unexpected / Alan Smithee, Author of Fischl; Phantasmal Nocturne.
- Razor: Of the Little Wolf Pack / With Lupical...; Surge of Lightning.
- Sucrose: Of Wonderland Flowers / The Law of Boundaries; Sevenfold Transmutation.
- Prune: Of What Is Contested / A Proposition Beyond Refutation; Witchseeker's Vow.
Hexerei sits beside Elemental Resonance and Moonsign as a Team Bonus type, and all three have their own tab on the Party Setup screen.
### ULTRA +
Version history: Version 6.2 "Luna III" (2025-12-03) added Witch's Homework as a permanent event and launched Hexerei: Secret Rite with Sucrose, Mona, Durin, Klee, Albedo, Venti, Fischl and Razor. Version 6.6 "Luna VII" (2026-05-20) added the homework quests for Prune, Lohen and Nicole alongside those characters, and a Hexerei-themed album for Repertoire of Myriad Melodies. Later patch notes (6.3, 6.6) mention fixes to the weapons' Hexerei visual effects.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay interpretation (this project's recommendation, not canon text): treat "Hexerei" as a loose bond of witch-adjacent people drawn together by the Traveler's homework, not a formal guild. A scene with two or more Hexerei characters can feel charged or unusually in tune, but avoid giving them powers beyond their own.
### LARGE +
Do not assume any Hexerei character is a Hexenzirkel member unless canon says so, and do not claim the Witch's Homework reveals Hexenzirkel plans.

## entry: Witch's Eve Rite Passive
@keys: Witch's Eve Rite, Witch's Eve Rite Passive, Secret Rite
### STANDARD
The Witch's Eve Rite Passive is an additional talent unlocked for a character once they finish their Witch's Homework. It is part of Hexerei: Secret Rite and enhances other talents and Constellations of that character.

## entry: The Little Witch's Dictionary
@keys: The Little Witch's Dictionary, Little Witch's Dictionary
### STANDARD
A gadget received from Alice in the quest Unexpected Commission. It lists and starts the Witch's Homework quests. Its description says the witches made it and that it is "actually filled with homework tailored by the Hexenzirkel."
