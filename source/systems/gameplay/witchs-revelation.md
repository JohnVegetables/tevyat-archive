## summary
### LITE
Witch's Revelation is a system from Version 6.7 in which the fortune-teller Barbeloth's Witch's Revelation Case holds oracles for particular characters. Completing a character's Witch's Revelation quest unlocks an extra Talent, the Witch's Revelation Passive, and enhances some of their Talents and Constellations. It is the sequel in spirit to Witch's Homework: Barbeloth and Alice are again behind it.
### STANDARD +
The Traveler gets the case in the Fontaine world quest "Revelations by Chance", near the Court of Fontaine.

## in_universe
### LITE
Barbeloth, a powerful fortune teller, is running a small divination stand just outside the Court of Fontaine, and tells the Traveler that as a Descender from beyond the world the Traveler's own fate cannot be predicted by most fortune tellers, herself included. She recently glimpsed an omen, followed it and caught threads of fate and the faces of their owners, who are all people the Traveler knows.
### STANDARD +
Alice, who is helping with the divination, joins through Barbeloth's Dodoco-shaped stand-in and wants to hold a party for everyone involved. Barbeloth considers invitations delivered by messenger birds likely to disturb fate and refuses, but allows the Traveler to bring the people to the stall because their fates are already tied to the Traveler's. The case itself, per item text, once belonged to Barbeloth and contains every oracle, those already revealed and those still to come; she says she prefers a future with secrets still to unfold.
### LARGE +
The Version 6.7 promotional material frames it as a new reading by the seer-witch: the warp and weft of fate emerge through an ancient spread of cards (the Lunar Arcanum). Barbeloth insists, in the promotional text, that hydromancy is "the one true legitimate art", and remarks that what used to be "fate" has long been disrupted by unforeseen events. After the quest, Barbeloth stays at the stand with her research and says a reminder will reach her when the person involved in an oracle arrives.

## gameplay
### LITE
Gameplay (not literal lore): each quest belongs to one character. The player must own that character at level 70 or higher. Finishing it unlocks the Witch's Revelation Passive and changes some Talents and Constellations. The Passive also lets the character enter the Radiance state under certain conditions.
### STANDARD +
The Passive allows a character to enter Radiance: Stellar-Conduct while inside a Polestar Field. A Polestar Field is created by the Starlight Prism that forms when the Stellar-Conduct reaction (Cryo with Electro, a Stellar Glimmer reaction) is triggered. The "Revelations by Chance" quest follows "Song of the Dragon and Freedom" and gives the player the case, 20 Primogems, Hero's Wit and Mora.
### LARGE +
The seven eligible characters, with the quest and the Lunar Arcanum card attached to each, as of 7.1:
- Wriothesley: "Witch's Revelation: Warden's Instruction"; The Emperor (Upright); There Shall Be an Unveiling for Injustice.
- Yae Miko: "Witch's Revelation: Great Youkai's Instruction"; Justice (Reversed); Edict of Cleansing.
- Cyno: "Witch's Revelation: Duty's Instruction"; Temperance (Reversed); A Star With Which to Start the Journey.
- Yumemizuki Mizuki: "Witch's Revelation: Instruction Beyond Dreams"; The Moon (Reversed); Vast Be the Dream.
- Qiqi: "Witch's Revelation: Instruction of Curious Form"; The Hanged Man (Upright); Seven Sacred Treasures.
- Diona: "Witch's Revelation: Special Blend's Instruction"; The High Priestess (Reversed); Choice Treasures.
- Beidou: "Witch's Revelation: Voyaging Instruction"; Strength (Upright); Polaris.

### ULTRA +
The wiki's own description for the map marker reads "The witch's revelation awaits the one who will uncover it", and the event overview describes the seer-witch beginning "a new reading". Each quest title ends in "Instruction" and names its character by role or trait (Warden, Great Youkai, Duty, Beyond Dreams, Curious Form, Special Blend, Voyaging).

## release_note
@version: 6.7
### STANDARD
Witch's Revelation was introduced in Version 6.7, "Sunny Summer Fontinalia" (Luna VIII, 2026-07-01). Its announcements presented it as the witch's new divination, with an accompanying new reaction and Talents. Witch's Homework (6.2) is the earlier system of this kind.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay interpretation (this project's recommendation, not canon text): play Barbeloth as dry, teasing and unwilling to explain fate in full, and Alice as warmly enthusiastic about parties. A character with a Revelation has simply been "read" by Barbeloth; do not claim the reading predicts their death or doom, since canon seen here states no specific outcome. Do not claim Barbeloth can read the Traveler's fate.

## limits
### STANDARD
Canon gives only the stand, the case, a rough sense that fate is being nudged, and the seven character quests. Do not invent contents of the oracles, the cause of the prophecy, or the nature of Radiance. Barbeloth stays out of the way of the Traveler's future, which she says she cannot see.

## entry: Witch's Revelation Case
@keys: Witch's Revelation Case, Revelations by Chance
### STANDARD
A 5-star reusable gadget obtained in "Revelations by Chance", used to view and accept Witch's Revelation tasks. Its text says the case once carried by Barbeloth has reached the Traveler, and holds all the oracles, both revealed and not yet opened, with Barbeloth declining to say whether they could steer fate.

## entry: Witch's Revelation Passive
@keys: Witch's Revelation Passive
### STANDARD
The additional Talent unlocked by a Witch's Revelation quest. It enhances some of the character's Talents and Constellations and allows entry to the Radiance: Stellar-Conduct state within a Polestar Field.
