## summary
### LITE
Ascension Phase is a game-mechanics term: the number of times a character or weapon has been ascended. It determines the maximum level the character or weapon can reach; the highest phase, 6, allows level 90. It is not something characters experience in the story.
### STANDARD +
Each phase must be unlocked by reaching a required Adventure Rank (the player's account progress) and by spending materials. It has existed since version 1.0.

## gameplay
### LITE
Gameplay (not literal lore): ascending raises the level cap. Characters need level-up materials, enhancement materials and local specialties; weapons need weapon ascension materials and enhancement materials.
### STANDARD +
For characters, each Ascension Phase also unlocks new Passive Talents, raises the maximum level of Combat Talents, and unlocks further Character Stories and voice-overs in their profile. This is why a character's deeper backstory is revealed in stages in the game.
### LARGE +
The prerequisite is player-side: the Adventure Rank gate belongs to the account, not to the character. Because ascension needs local specialties from specific regions, each character is loosely tied to a region's resources, a gameplay convention rather than a lore statement.

### ULTRA +
Ascension is listed among the game's systems alongside Talents and Constellations. Its page records only that it came in with 1.0 and has no later change history; nothing about it changed through 7.1 on the wiki.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay interpretation (this project's recommendation, not canon text): do not have characters talk about their own "Ascension Phase" or "level 90". If a scene needs the idea, describe it as growth through training and experience. Do not conflate this mechanic with a mortal ascending to godhood, which is an in-world concept.
### LARGE +
Passive Talents may be portrayed as skills a character picks up with experience, but avoid assigning specific phase numbers to story events.

## limits
### LITE
Applies to playable characters and weapons only; it has no canonical in-world explanation.
### STANDARD +
The cap of 6 phases and level 90 are the figures given for the mechanic as of 7.1; individual material costs are not recorded here.
