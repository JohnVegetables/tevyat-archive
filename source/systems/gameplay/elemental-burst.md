## summary
### LITE
The Elemental Burst, or Burst, is a Talent that needs Energy and is usually stronger than the Elemental Skill. Fans call it the ultimate, or "Q" after its default PC key. Every character has one unlocked from the start. This is a gameplay mechanic; the narrative equivalent is a character unleashing their strongest technique.
### STANDARD +
Casting grants a character a few seconds of invincibility frames (the exact time varies per character), during which the HP bar is locked and no damage from enemies or debuffs is taken.

## gameplay
### LITE
Gameplay (not literal lore): a Burst can be used when (1) it is off cooldown and (2) the character holds at least the Burst's Energy Cost. Energy beyond the cost is ignored. Using it spends all Energy and starts a short cooldown, though Energy can still be gathered during the cooldown.
### STANDARD +
- Readiness: the HUD icon glows with an audio cue; off-field characters show a ready icon beside their portrait, and co-op players show a marker beside their names.
- Typical costs and cooldowns: 40 Energy with a 12 s cooldown; 50 with 13.5 s; 60 with 15 s; 70 with 18 s; 80 with 20 s; 90 with 18 s or 22 s.
- Exceptions: Hour of Burning Skies and Havoc: Ruin do not use Energy. The first spends Fighting Spirit (100-200) gathered from the party's Normal Attacks and Nightsoul point use in combat. The second uses Skirk's Serpent's Subtlety points (50-100) and has a 15 s cooldown.
### LARGE +
- Increasing Burst damage can come from certain Talents, constellations, weapons and artifacts, each listed on the wiki by category; constellations can also raise a Burst's talent level.
- Barbara is the only 4-star character with a Burst animation like those of 5-star characters, and hers neither buffs nor damages.
- The Traveler, Varesa, Skirk, Flins and the Wonderland Manekin are the 5-star playable characters whose Bursts lack the cut-in animation.
- Kinich is the only character whose Burst-linked voice-overs keep playing while he is off-field.
### ULTRA +
- Tartaglia, Varesa, Skirk and Flins have two distinct Bursts, with separate animations, voice-overs, Energy timing, cooldown timing and targeting. The special versions for Varesa, Skirk and Flins have separate costs and no cut-in; Varesa's and Flins's special versions also have their own cooldown (the wiki flags some of these timing details as needing verification).
- The Wanderer changes his Burst voice-overs depending on whether he is in the Windfavored State, though the Burst itself is unchanged.
- Other Bursts behave differently depending on conditions (for example Albedo, Hu Tao and Yae Miko), but these do not count as two separate Bursts.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay interpretation (this project's recommendation, not canon text): present a Burst as a character's signature, high-effort moment, a surge of power they can only call on after the fight has built momentum. Avoid quoting Energy costs or cooldowns in character. Match the character's element and weapon in the description.
### LARGE +
Narratively, the brief invincibility frames can read as the character being untouchable in the instant they unleash it. Use the character's own technique names where canon provides them, and keep the Burst proportionate to the character's established power.

## limits
### LITE
The Energy meter, key binding and cooldown are interface concepts, not things characters mention.
### STANDARD +
Figures are those listed on the wiki as of 7.1.

## entry: Hour of Burning Skies
@keys: Hour of Burning Skies, Havoc: Ruin

### LARGE
Hour of Burning Skies and Havoc: Ruin are the only Elemental Bursts that do not use Energy. Hour of Burning Skies uses Fighting Spirit gathered from the party's Normal Attacks and Nightsoul point consumption; Havoc: Ruin uses Skirk's Serpent's Subtlety points. Both have minimum and maximum costs rather than a fixed one.
