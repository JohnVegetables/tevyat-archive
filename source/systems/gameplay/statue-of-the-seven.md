## summary
### LITE
Statues of The Seven are large stone statues of each nation's Archon, found across Teyvat. Legends say they were sculpted in the image of The Seven who sustain the world, and travellers pray to them for protection. In play they unlock the map, act as teleport waypoints, heal and revive the party, and swap the Traveler's element.
### STANDARD +
Offering oculi to a nation's statues rewards the player and raises the party's maximum Stamina.

## in_universe
### LITE
Mondstadt's statue honours the Anemo Archon, Liyue's the Geo Archon, Inazuma's the Electro Archon, Sumeru's the Dendro Archon, Fontaine's the Hydro Archon, Natlan's the Pyro Archon and Snezhnaya's the Cryo Archon. Each statue shows a hooded figure holding an object shaped like the region's Vision. The in-game map text calls them monumental statues that "watch over" the land.
### STANDARD +
Statues seem to depict the Archon currently in office. The game's description reads: "Legends say they were sculpted in the images of The Seven who sustain this world. Travelers would pray for the statue's protection should they come across one, while it silently anticipates the arrival of a noble soul."
According to fairytales cited in The Blessings of The Seven, the statues originally had gem eyes that swallows carried off. Item text on the Anemoculus describes offering consolidated winds to a statue so that it can regain power it has lost.
### LARGE +
Areas that no Archon rules, such as Enkanomiya and the Golden Apple Archipelago, have no Statues of The Seven. Watatsumi Island was under Orobashi, not the Electro Archon, so it had none until Orobashi was slain; after that it gained a statue (a claim attributed to Mochizuki). Fontaine's statues match Focalors and Furina's Pneuma stance, not Egeria. Mondstadt also once lost a statue: the Traveler later finds a Defiled Statue hanging upside down in the ruins in Call of the Abyss. In Nod-Krai, the Frostmoon Scions built statues of Columbina called Statues of the New Moon.
### ULTRA +
Each nation's map text adds a motto. Mondstadt: "Seeds brought by the wind will grow over time". Liyue: its history is one of enduring stability. Inazuma: "Glory to the Shogun and her everlasting reign!" Sumeru: "There is nothing new under the sun." Fontaine: "The law is a fortress above all the waters of the world." Natlan: "Just as the perpetual living flame burns on without end, so too are struggle and change the true sovereigns of all things." Snezhnaya: "Only in the bitterest depths of winter does the steadfast pine reveal the warrior's resilience and unyielding strength."

## gameplay
### LITE
Gameplay: each region's statues fill in map areas when first touched, unlock expeditions and teleport waypoints, revive fallen characters and heal the party. Interacting with a statue while playing the Traveler lets them change element to match that region.
### STANDARD +
Statue's Blessing: all statues share a pool of HP called Restorative Power. It refills at 1% of its maximum per 15 seconds (25 minutes for a full recharge). The maximum is 5,000 times the number of Statues of The Seven and Statues of the New Moon unlocked; the wiki gives 310,000 as the possible maximum as of Version 7.0 (55 statues of the Seven and 7 Statues of the New Moon). Each manual click heals a character by 10% of their maximum HP.
With "Auto-recover when nearby" enabled, fallen characters are revived at 35% HP for free, and party members below a chosen HP threshold are healed from the pool if you stay close for about 2 seconds. Statues also cleanse Bonds of Life and act as a source of warmth in Subzero Climate.
### LARGE +
Oculi offerings: a nation's statues share a level. Offering the region's oculi gives Adventure EXP, Primogems and Sigils, and the first levels raise maximum Stamina (see the Stamina entry); Liyue statues also give Stones of Remembrance, and Inazuma, Sumeru, Fontaine and Snezhnaya statues give Shrines of Depths Keys and Memories. Natlan statues give Acquaint Fates. Snezhnaya's statues also let a player apply Cryo to enhance the Exaiphanes Blade. Natlan statues replenish Phlogiston where it is enabled.
First touch of a new statue gives 50 Adventure EXP and 5 Primogems. Mondstadt statues take 1 to 15 oculi per level; Liyue's take 2 to 30; Inazuma's take 10 to 30. Snezhnaya's take 20 to 32 across six levels. Switching back to Anemo, Paimon says, is done at a Mondstadt statue.
Only the Starfell Lake statue (during Bird's Eye View) and the Galesong Hill statue near Windrise are available at the very start. Others need obstacles cleared: a Windwail Highland statue guarded by an Eye of the Storm, a frozen statue on Dragonspine, and the Ochkanatlan statue (an activated switch).
### ULTRA +
Statue scenes with Paimon vary by region and story: she reacts to the Geo power at the first Liyue statue and to the light and humid air at the first Fontaine statue, and in Natlan she remarks that the statues feel odd compared with earlier nations'.
A statue grows more ornate as oculi are offered: broken stones are replaced at level 2, gold detail is added and cracks fixed at level 6, and a floating gold ring and Celestial-motif designs appear at level 10.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay interpretation (this project's recommendation, not canon text): statues are public landmarks where travellers rest, pray and wait for a safe teleport. Characters can say "pray to the statue" or "rest at the statue" without knowing about Restorative Power or oculi counts. Healing at a statue can be described as light and warmth rather than numbers.
### LARGE +
Do not have an ordinary character explain the game's oculi or healing mechanics. Oculi are the exception: according to The Blessings of The Seven, only allogenes and the Traveler can see them, so an ordinary person would not be offering them.

In Nod-Krai, the Frostmoon Scions, who worship the Moon Goddess, built statues in the image of Columbina and placed them across the region, called Statues of the New Moon. They add to the Restorative Power pool like the Seven's statues in play.
