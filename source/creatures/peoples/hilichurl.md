## summary
### LITE
Hilichurls are the masked, tribal creatures that attack travellers in the wilds of every nation, and most people take them for monsters. Many are in fact cursed people: Khaenri'ahns of mixed or foreign ancestry struck with the "curse of the wilderness" in the cataclysm 500 years ago. It wears away memory, speech and appearance but not life, leaving them in slow, painful decline.
### STANDARD +
This entry covers hilichurls as a people with a past. The monster family (Samachurls, Mitachurls, Lawachurls, gameplay) is in the Hilichurls entry. Few in Teyvat know the truth; Dainsleif, the Traveler and the Traveler's sibling do, as did Chlothar Alberich.

## nature
### STANDARD
A hilichurl is bare-chested and barefoot, with a heavy mane, long ears, a loincloth and bandaged limbs, and always wears a mask. The Archive says they look much like humans but seem to have lost their intellect and spirituality. Dainsleif explains in The Grave of the Guarded (2.6) that the mask is so they never see their own reflection in water: compared with how they remember themselves, the sight brings them great despair. So they still remember something of what they were.
### LARGE +
The Archive says hilichurls have been recorded for over a thousand years and have spread in large numbers since the "pitch-black calamity" of 500 years ago. They live in many climates but avoid places dense with elemental energy, which burdens their bodies (Venti, Hurdle).
### ULTRA +
Jacob Musk thought they live only in the present: they store no food on purpose and do not mark their dead. Yet they camp in ruins and copy glyphs from old walls, which he called an "inexplicable affinity" for the remains of lost civilisations.

## origin
@version: 2.6
@spoiler: major
### LITE
After the cataclysm the gods cursed Khaenri'ahns in two ways. Pure-blood Khaenri'ahns received the curse of immortality. People whose ancestry belonged to other gods' lands received the curse of the wilderness, which turned them into monsters.
### STANDARD +
Dainsleif confirmed in 2.6 that the hilichurls drawn to The Chasm in Liyue come from Khaenri'ah. In Fortune-Mocking Pedigree (3.5) Chlothar Alberich, living as "Eide", explains: Khaenri'ah began as one bloodline but welcomed anyone who forsook their god, and when the cataclysm came the pure-bloods were named the "greater sinners" and made immortal, while those from other gods' domains were punished as they fled. His own son Caribert, whose mother's roots were in Mondstadt, became a hilichurl as a small child.
### LARGE +
The folk tale Perinheri has a comparable rule: a knight descended from god-forsakers begins to howl like a beast on crossing the kingdom's border.
### ULTRA +
Hilichurls have been recorded for over a thousand years, so the cataclysm cannot explain all of them. Canon is silent on the older ones. Fan guesses (Sal Vindagnyr, Gurabad) are in the uncertainties and should not be given to a character as fact.

## curse
@version: 3.5
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The curse degrades the mind: memories fade, speech and understanding of Common are lost, and the body outlives its time. A hilichurl cannot simply die of age; erosion wears body and mind toward something like death, and old hilichurls are said to be in constant pain. Dainsleif says immortality does not mean one never dies, only that body and soul keep eroding until almost nothing is left.
### LARGE +
Ronova, the Ruler of Death, is tied to the curses at the cataclysm: Capitano says she cursed the full-blooded Khaenri'ahns with immortality, and the wiki attributes the curse of the wilderness to her too (the wiki's reading). Dainsleif holds that the curse alters reality itself and calls any attempt to cleanse it hopeless. In 7.1 (Black Feathers Scattered at Parting) the curse of immortality outlasts Ronova losing her Authority because the Heavenly Principles authorised it; canon says nothing equivalent about the curse of the wilderness.

## decline_and_end
@version: 2.6
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Old hilichurls that sense their end seek a calm, dark corner to say goodbye to centuries of suffering. Part of The Chasm, near the Inverted City, eases the curse, so many go there and ignore food and danger.
### LARGE +
Cursed Black Serpent Knights, Khaenri'ah's former Royal Guard, guard the dying hilichurls under Halfdan, whom Dainsleif once ordered to protect the people of Khaenri'ah "at all costs". Halfdan later died stopping the Abyss Order's device (Requiem of the Echoing Depths, 2.6). Their growls are not threats but the old Khaenri'ahn word "Run".
### ULTRA +
The Traveler's sibling and an Abyss Herald planned a device to amplify the pool there, to lift the curse from the gathered hilichurls and "restore the homeland". Dainsleif said it would only bring them more suffering; the Traveler decided the sibling had no right to risk such pain for a tiny chance of redemption. After the Herald was beaten, an Abyss Lector activated the device and the hilichurls writhed in pain; Halfdan sacrificed himself to halt it long enough for the Traveler to defeat the Lector and destroy it.

## society
### LARGE
Hilichurls live in small tribes that work like extended families. The oldest member, a Samachurl (shaman), leads worship and decisions, and food is shared by need. They worship elemental power, not an Archon, with song and dance, and "unu" ("one") is their holy word for gods and the origin of life. Tribe structure and variants are in the Hilichurls entry.
### ULTRA +
Jacob Musk toured every tribe he could reach for his Hilichurl Ballad Selection: battle chants, festival hymns around totems and a melancholy song from an elderly Samachurl whose words he could not translate but whose grief was unmistakable.

## abilities
### LARGE
Some hilichurls, above all Samachurls, use elemental energy without a Vision, which scholars cannot explain. Many use Slimes as tools and weapons. Nothing suggests a hilichurl keeps skills from its human life.

## relations
### STANDARD
Most hilichurls are hostile to travellers. Abyss Mages speak Hilichurlian and often command them. The Abyss Order and the Traveler's sibling regard them as Khaenri'ah's lost citizens, to be cleansed so they die naturally or become the base of a rebuilt homeland (Dainsleif). Hilichurls elsewhere are not always hostile: during the limited-time Mimi Tomo event (1.5), hilichurls in several regions, including Starfell Valley, asked the Traveler for help in fights against Treasure Hoarders and Abyss Mages.
### LARGE +
Dainsleif no longer regards hilichurls as human, per the wiki's reading of Memories of Inteyvat. Chlothar cared for his hilichurl son Caribert and fought to restore his mind. Ella and Jacob Musk study them, and Jacob Musk recorded their songs as poetry.

## individuals
### LARGE
- Caribert Alberich: Chlothar's son (entry below).
- Caterpillar: an Anemo Hilichurl Rogue given speech by Narzissenkreuz (entry below).
- Halfdan: not a hilichurl but a cursed Black Serpent Knight who guarded them and died in 2.6.
### ULTRA +
- Ukko: a Frostarm Lawachurl on Dragonspine whose name matches a Sal Vindagnyr scribe (unconfirmed link).
- The Eclipse Tribe of Dadaupa Gorge, whose shamans are stronger than other tribes' and who worship an eclipsed-sun symbol.

## status
@version: 7.1
### STANDARD
As of 7.1 no one has been shown lifting the curse of the wilderness, and hilichurls remain common across Teyvat. No Nod-Krai (6.x) or Snezhnaya (7.0-7.1) content read for this entry changes this picture.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay guidance (this project's recommendation, not canon text): when the Khaenri'ah link matters, play hilichurls as pitiable and dangerous at once. They cannot say what they were, yet still flinch at reflections and mourn in song. Do not let one speak Common or recall Khaenri'ah. A character who knows the truth (Dainsleif, the Traveler, Chlothar) says so quietly, because the knowledge is rare and painful.
### LARGE +
Ordinary Teyvat folk see monsters; do not let a Mondstadter casually know hilichurls were people. Never show an unmasked face as a casual detail. Do not turn tribes into a nation with an Archon or king.

## mistakes
### STANDARD
- Not all hilichurls are Khaenri'ahns: only many are, and the older ones are unexplained.
- The curse of the wilderness did not hit pure-blood Khaenri'ahns; they got immortality.
- The Traveler cured no one. In 2.6 they only stopped a cleansing device; Caribert and Caterpillar regained speech through Abyssal power and Narzissenkreuz's blade.
- Abyss Mages, Heralds and Lectors are Abyss Order monsters, not hilichurls, even though the wiki reads the Order's monsters as also originating from Khaenri'ah's people.

## entry: Curse of the wilderness
@keys: curse of the wilderness, wilderness curse, cursed Khaenri'ahns
@version: 3.5
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The curse of the wilderness is one of two curses the gods placed on Khaenri'ah at the cataclysm. Chlothar Alberich (as Eide) says pure-blood Khaenri'ahns were judged the "greater sinners" and given the curse of immortality, while those whose ancestry belonged to other gods' domains were punished with the curse of the wilderness as they fled, turning them into monsters. The effects are loss of memory, speech and appearance, plus a long, prolonged decline. The Archive and Dainsleif describe hilichurls as the result.
### LARGE +
Chlothar says that Khaenri'ah was welcoming to anyone who forsook their gods, which is why so many of its people had surface ancestry. He pleads for his hilichurl son in his prayer to the God of Wisdom: a child turned into a monster before he saw anything beautiful.

## entry: Masks and reflections
@keys: hilichurl mask, hilichurl masks, hilichurls reflection
@keys2: hilichurl, hilichurls
@version: 2.6
### STANDARD
Dainsleif says hilichurls wear masks to avoid seeing their reflection in water. Compared with how they remember themselves, the sight is a terrible one that causes great despair. This shows hilichurls still remember their human faces. Masks also show a hilichurl's element in their designs, and shamans' masks are horned.
### LARGE +
Caribert says that after his mind was restored he suffered indescribable mental anguish. The Traveler's sibling says that even as a hilichurl, seeing the terrible sight in the mirror did not taint his spirit (World-Order Narration, 4.7).

## entry: Hilichurls in The Chasm
@keys: Chasm hilichurls, Inverted City hilichurls, Grave of the Guarded
@keys2: hilichurl, hilichurls
@version: 2.6
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
In Requiem of the Echoing Depths (2.6) the miner Yuehui commissions the Traveler to look into strange hilichurl activity in The Chasm. The Traveler and Paimon meet Dainsleif underground. He explains that these hilichurls come to the area, where the curse is eased, to spend their final years. Black Serpent Knights guard them, led by Halfdan. A pool at the summit is a relic of an older civilisation and is the source of the effect.
### LARGE +
A hilichurl camp nearby holds an Inteyvat, Khaenri'ah's national flower. Touching it shows the Traveler a vision of their sibling and an Abyss Herald planning to amplify the pool. Dainsleif and the Traveler stop the plan and then fight the Herald sent to delay them.

## entry: Black Serpent Knights guarding the dying
@keys: Black Serpent Knight, Black Serpent Knights, Halfdan
@keys2: hilichurl, hilichurls, Chasm
@version: 2.6
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The Black Serpent Knights are Khaenri'ah's former Royal Guard, now cursed and mindless. In The Chasm they protect hilichurls, and Dainsleif thinks they are still following the last order to protect the people of Khaenri'ah. He recognises one as Halfdan, an elite he knew as a young man, whose silent order makes the others retreat. The knights repeat a single word of the old language: "Run". Halfdan later sacrificed himself to halt the Abyss Order's device, and his soul briefly spoke with Dainsleif before passing on (2.6).

## entry: Caribert
@keys: Caribert, Caribert Alberich
@keys2: hilichurl, hilichurls, Chlothar, Eide
@version: 3.5
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Caribert was the son of Chlothar Alberich and a mother with Mondstadt roots, so the curse of the wilderness turned him into a hilichurl while he was still a young child. Chlothar cared for him while he lay in torpor. With the help of the Traveler's sibling he tried a forbidden medicine, and after Vedrfolnir's power was given to Chlothar a later dose gave Caribert back his mind and speech.
### LARGE +
In Bedtime Story and World-Order Narration (4.7) it emerges that Caribert's father meant him to become the Loom of Fate but he was only used as a means for its construction; a remnant of his consciousness remained within the unfinished Loom, and he died aged eight by his own account. In World-Order Narration he used the Loom, while it could still create memories, to implant in the hilichurls a comforting memory in the form of a story, as his father once told him one. The agitated hilichurls around Vimara Village grew calm. He says it was the most worthwhile thing he could offer.

## entry: Caterpillar
@keys: Caterpillar
@keys2: hilichurl, Narzissenkreuz, Meropide
@version: 4.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Caterpillar ("Cater") looks like a child, but he is not one; he was held in the Fortress of Meropide for centuries and later joined the Ordo's efforts against Jakob's world. He is a former Anemo Hilichurl Rogue. Narzissenkreuz's miracle gave him sentience and human speech, and he was inducted into the Narzissenkreuz Ordo. A log says remains of Carter Scherbius were grafted on "those who have lost their intellect", which is read to mean hilichurls. He calls himself a monster in human skin.

## entry: Hilichurls and the Abyss Order
@keys: Abyss Order hilichurls, Order hilichurls
@keys2: hilichurl, hilichurls
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Abyss Mages speak fluent Hilichurlian and can direct tribes, so hilichurls often fight for the Abyss Order. In the Order's view they are Khaenri'ah's lost citizens. In Hurdle (1.0), when hilichurls turn up near Stormterror's Lair, Venti notes that they avoid dense elemental energy and Diluc concludes the Order must be manipulating them.

## entry: Older hilichurls
@keys: Ukko, Frostarm Lawachurl Ukko, Gurabad hilichurls
@keys2: hilichurl, hilichurls, Lawachurl
@spoiler: minor
### LARGE
Ukko is a Frostarm Lawachurl on Dragonspine who shares a name with a person of Sal Vindagnyr, probably its scribe and the last survivor of that fallen city. The wiki treats this as a hint that other civilisations lost to catastrophe also produced hilichurls, but no quest confirms it. The book The Tale of Shiruyeh and Shirin describes a similar fate for Gurabad, whose scattered people became wild creatures without language or faces. Desert records call hilichurls "majuj". These are parallels, not answers.
