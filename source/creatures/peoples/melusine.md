## summary
### LITE
Melusines are a small, amphibious, all-female people of Fontaine, born from the remains of the dead great beast Elynas, whom they call "Father". Most live in Merusea Village, underwater inside Elynas's body in the Beryl Region; many others work in the Court of Fontaine, especially in the Marechaussee Phantom. Neuvillette is their protector and their "ideal of the perfect father".
### STANDARD +
Humans once feared them as monsters. Today Fontaine's law protects them (by Neuvillette's own clause a Melusine is "she", never "it"), and nearly every Fontainian would defend one. The playable Melusine is Sigewinne, head nurse of the Fortress of Meropide.

## nature
### LITE
Short, two-legged and almost faun-like, with large tails, mitten-like hands, stubby feet, sea-slug-like head "feelers" and ordinary hair. They swim well, live for centuries and do age. Each is born with a unique "token".
### STANDARD +
There are two main variants: a fluffy tail, curled feelers and flower-patterned skin, or a long tufted tail, ear-like feelers and stripes. Some have small wing-like back appendages. They see colours humans cannot, and their tastes in food and art often baffle humans.
### LARGE +
Every Melusine combines variant, skin colours, pupil shape and hairstyle in her own way. They usually wear simple shirts or dresses with a small bandana or choker, and uniforms when they work as Gardes or aquabus guides. Sigewinne's restorative cooking is called "mystery meat" by inmates of the Fortress of Meropide, Wriothesley calls her milkshakes "desolation" and Escoffier calls them "absolutely deadly", though Escoffier does not criticise such food as a chef, since it was never made for human tastes. Mamere's paintings are incomprehensible to most humans. Melusines find Breacher Primuses adorable. Even their view of Neuvillette differs from one Melusine to the next: when they made a signboard of him they had to "split the difference". They eat tidalga as well as all kinds of human food, need sleep, and react to coffee and tea as humans do.
### ULTRA +
Tokens: Aeval, the Clementine Line aquabus guide, explains that the token is a Melusine's first memento of the world and that no two are alike; hers is a sword shard, which she takes to mean courage. Mamere's token was a clockwork relay module with runes on it, taken from the damaged mek Seymour, and she used it to repair him. Age: during limited-time events (6.7), Enghiene, a veteran Garde and former Special Security and Surveillance Patrol officer, admitted that age had impaired her shooting, and she retired after 150 years with the Maison Gardiennage.

## origin
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
About 400 years ago, Marechaussee Hunters fought the Narzissenkreuz Ordo inside Elynas's remains. An explosion killed many and drove clockwork mek parts into his flesh; the Melusines grew from those wounds, the parts becoming their tokens. Elynas's lingering consciousness raised them as daughters, but in time only the painter Mamere could hear him.
### LARGE +
Elynas was a vast, Abyss-linked creature made by Rhinedottir. He appeared around the time of the cataclysm and polluted land and sea; after the Fontaine Armada pursued him he bled out and died, but his consciousness lingered in his body. He taught the Melusines how to build a home and watched them flourish. Mamere's kin took her "Father" for an imaginary friend. In Ancient Colors (4.0) the Iniquitous Baptist Jakob tried to restart Elynas's heart with his blood, meaning to make transcendent "neohumans"; a revived Elynas would have crushed the Melusines' homes. Elynas refused life at that price, helped the Traveler stop the revival and, in his brief "time of life", asked them to pass his blessing to Mamere. Afterwards Mamere could no longer hear him either. The Breacher Primuses were made from Elynas's cells at about the same time and act like his white blood cells, defending his body and his children; they do not attack Melusines, though they are not clever and do not always tell friend from foe where others are concerned.

## society
### STANDARD
Merusea Village has no official leader; the older Melusine Serene acts as everyone's "big sister". Dutiful, just and perceptive, Melusines make up most of the Marechaussee Phantom and also work as receptionists (Sedene at the Palais Mermonia) and aquabus guides (Aeval, Elphane).
### LARGE +
Those who work in the Court often live there for convenience and return to the village on holiday. The Melusine Philharmonic Orchestra is a well-known orchestra made up entirely of Melusine musicians. Merusea Village can be reached only underwater, by diving near Elynas's skull or into the pool above the Beryl Region's Statue of The Seven. Few humans visit, but those who do enjoy it. According to a Steambird reporter, the village's wooden street lamps were made for guests who found it darker than they were used to, though the Melusines themselves no longer remember the lamps' origin. One Melusine built her own swing scaled to Melusine height, since human swings did not suit her. Scrap mechanical "components", common there and pretty to look at, serve as currency: they pay for goods from the vendor Xana and are given as thanks between neighbours. Some Melusines, Sigewinne in particular, love astrology columns and draw Neuvillette into them too.

## abilities
### LITE
Small and not very dexterous, they fight poorly but investigate superbly.
### STANDARD +
Their hands cannot work pistolets, yet some are expert riflewomen.
### LARGE +
Marine, a former Marechaussee agent who did reconnaissance and sentry duty, reads people from their walk, expressions and choice of words, and says Elphane, the "Scarlet Specter", has almost perfect grades in the main professional skills (both from limited-time event dialogue). Some have special crafts: Sigewinne is a doctor, and Mamere paints, once using Elynas's blood as paint. They are not helpless: during limited-time events, Marine cleared out monster nests during a visit to Natlan (6.7), and Nadia, a former elite Marechaussee agent, is revealed to have become one of the Adventurers' Guild's fifteen S-ranked adventurers (7.0).

## relations
### STANDARD
Neuvillette tells people to befriend Melusines and never harm them, calling them "the pride of Fontaine"; Fontainians read his warmth toward them as a father's love.
### LARGE +
He personally speaks out against rumours that disrespect them, such as the claim that Sigewinne has mixed blood. As the Hydro Dragon, he sees the Melusines as his dependents and successors and the finest example of a new sort of Hydro Vishap species. Asked why he won them rights among humans, he answered only that Melusines simply prefer being around humans. Pahsiv, a unique Primordial Bathysmal Vishap, can take a Melusine form at will, though her skin patterns and pupils stay a vishap's. Outsiders find them irresistible: Linnea, the Adventurers' Guild advisor, longs to live among them in Merusea Village and is always refused, and after Lauma politely refused to let her touch her antlers, she remarked that the Frostmoon Scions are even stricter than the Melusines.

## history
### STANDARD
After almost twenty years in Merusea Village, the Melusines met Neuvillette, who brought them to the Court of Fontaine, where many humans called them monsters. Trust came only after Carole, an early Marechaussee Melusine, was framed by Neuvillette's opponents and gave her life to a mob to prove her innocence.
### LARGE +
Carole had worked for five years beside Vautrin, captain of the Special Security and Surveillance Patrol. Vautrin's later trial and Neuvillette's evident impartiality won trust for both the Iudex and the Melusines. The Gardes' hat emblem was redesigned to include a Melusine in her memory. Neuvillette then wrote the pronoun law into the codex, and harsh penalties were set for treating Melusines as monsters. In the early years many Melusines gave gifts to humans as goodwill, and bad actors exploited it, so Neuvillette strictly forbade the practice. Faced with human wariness, many early Melusines went back to the water and gave up trying to understand humans. Sigewinne's curiosity kept her ashore, and she learned medicine from a travelling doctor whom children called "the witch".
### ULTRA +
Neuvillette's Story Quest (4.1) shows the threat repeating 400 years later: a criminal threatened the Melusine officer Kiara, who had come to the Court with Carole. Neuvillette investigated personally so that Carole's tragedy would not happen again, and so many people volunteered to protect Kiara that Clorinde moved her into the Palais Mermonia. There Kiara dreamt that Carole was glad their dream had come true.

## status
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
As of 7.1 they are a protected people of Fontaine; Merusea Village is intact and Elynas still at rest. A few now travel to Nod-Krai and Snezhnaya.
### LARGE +
Mamere and Seymour have painted their way to Nod-Krai, the Marechaussee agent Iratxa is chasing a suspect through Nasha Town, and Nadia competed in Snezhnaya during the limited-time Great Expeditionist Challenge event (7.0).

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay guidance (project recommendation): write Melusines as earnest, perceptive adult professionals, not pets or children; always "she".
### LARGE +
They tend to describe people by colours and feelings humans cannot see. Fontainians treat them with courtesy; rudeness to a Melusine in the Court draws stares or a Gardes' attention. Avoid giving them pistols or strong combat power by default, making any Melusine male, inventing a Melusine queen or chief, or having any Melusine other than Mamere hear Elynas (and even she no longer can). Sigewinne's human-like body is unique, not a sign of mixed ancestry.

## entry: Melusine tokens
@keys: Melusine token, Melusine tokens
### STANDARD
Every Melusine is born with a "token", a memento formed from the mechanical debris that was driven into Elynas's flesh when the Melusines were born. No two are alike. Aeval's is a sword shard, which she takes to mean courage. Mamere's was Seymour's artificed clockwork relay module, and with it she could repair him; she later returned it to him.

## entry: Carole and Melusine rights
@keys: Carole
@keys2: Melusine, Melusines, Vautrin, Neuvillette
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Carole was among the first Melusines brought to the Court of Fontaine, and one of the first to join the Marechaussee Phantom. After five years of service beside Vautrin, hardliners opposed to Neuvillette framed her for murder. To prove her innocence she gave herself up to the mob, and her death turned public opinion. Neuvillette then wrote the pronoun law into Fontaine's codex, and the Gardes' emblem now honours her with a Melusine.

## entry: Elynas and his daughters
@keys: Elynas
@keys2: Melusine, Melusines, Father, daughters
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The Melusines call Elynas "Father". He was an Abyss-linked giant beast made by Rhinedottir who appeared around the time of the cataclysm and later bled to death, and his consciousness lived on in his body. He rejoiced at the Melusines' birth, taught them to make a home and asked nothing of them. In Ancient Colors he refused to be revived, because his body would crush their homes, and he sent his blessing to Mamere through the Traveler.

## entry: Sigewinne's human-like form
@keys: Sigewinne
@keys2: Melusine, human form, potion, witch
@version: 4.7
@spoiler: minor
### LARGE
Sigewinne is a Melusine whose face, hands and feet look human. In the days when Melusines were still distrusted, a little girl befriended her, and when the girl fell gravely ill her parents would not let a Melusine doctor in. Sigewinne got a "potion" from her teacher, a travelling doctor called "the witch", took human form and saved her friend, and she has kept that form since. Her teacher later revealed the bottle had been a final gift left after the teacher dissolved herself.
