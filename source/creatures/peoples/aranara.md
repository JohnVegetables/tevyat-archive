## summary
### LITE
The Aranara are small, plantlike forest spirits of Sumeru, living mainly in Vanarana, a village hidden inside a dream in Dharma Forest. The Dendro Archon Greater Lord Rukkhadevata, whom they called Queen Aranyani, made them to guard the forest and its creatures. They are friendly, curious and easily frightened, shy of adults, close to children, and they treasure memories and songs.
### STANDARD +
Most adults think them a myth or a child's imaginary friend. The Akademiya's Amurta darshan looked into them and, since its sensors cannot detect them as children can, filed them as "unidentified lifeforms". Their central story is the Aranyaka World Quest series (3.0), in which the Traveler helps them make a Bija fruit to save a Forest Watcher named Rana.

## nature
### LITE
Aranara come in many shapes, colours and hats (a mushroom cap, a pair of leaves) and always seem to smile. Many have a leaf on top of the head that works as a small propeller and lets them fly. They do not need to sleep or eat, are very afraid of fire and salt, and can be revitalised with Dendro energy.
### STANDARD +
Like plants, an Aranara in great danger can turn into a seed and hibernate; once the crisis passes the seed sprouts back into an Aranara, which takes a lot of energy. The last stage of an Aranara's life is growing into a Vasara Tree, whose size and growth speed depend on the memories it holds. They can hide from human eyes, talk to plants and mushrooms, and befriend wild animals such as Rishboland Tigers. They are long-lived and sentimental.
### LARGE +
Aranara have adopted many human practices that serve them no practical purpose: cooking (they do not eat and fear fire), houses (they do not rest), trade (they do not need goods) and siblinghood (they do not reproduce). They sprinkle their speech with words from their own language, whose vocabulary is built on Sanskrit roots: "Nara" for a human, "Vana" for the forest and, by extension, Sumeru's forest half, "Valuka" for sand and the desert half.
### ULTRA +
Each Aranara has a personal tune by which the others recognise it, and some tunes carry information that apparently only Aranara can read. The nearer a tune is to the legendary "Sourcesong", the stronger that Aranara's Ararakalari; Queen Aranyani's song was said to be the closest. Song gatherers collect other Aranara's songs, a craft they learned from humans, partly to keep memories and partly to look for the Sourcesong; a few even collect human songs, which the others find unusual. Songs can be played by non-Aranara too, including the Traveler on a Vintage Lyre, to wake a Silapna, open a Gloomy Path or call up a Dendrograna.

## abilities
@version: 3.0
### LITE
Each Aranara has its own Ararakalari, powers drawn from its memories and channelled through the plants of the forest. They can fight, restrain or weaken enemies, shield against the Withering, teleport over great distances in an instant and seal off areas.
### STANDARD +
Ararakalari burn memories as fuel, so using one costs the Aranara something of itself. Aranara can also selectively create and remove dreams and memories. An Aranara's Ararakalari can grow when it gains new memories, its own or someone else's: Aramuhukunda gained the ability to counter the Withering after seeing Queen Aranyani do so, and Arama received his teleportation by inheriting his memories. Channelling the power through a Kusava seed strengthens it and can let a human use Ararakalari briefly.
### LARGE +
Arama says the powers come from the "Mahamaitri Kusa Stotra", a song praising Queen Aranyani, humans and the Aranara themselves. In the Aranyaka quests, Arana bubbled the boy Iotham in the Withering cave and then Rana, to shield them from corrosion, and Arama used the Ararakalari of Aradasha to wipe out a regenerating Ruin Grader.

## society
### LITE
The Aranara live in Vanarana, a hidden village. Their elder Araja and the lively Arana are two of the main figures; most Aranara have names beginning "Ara-".
### STANDARD +
Aranara love festivals. In the Aranyaka story the village holds a Festival Utsava, where a Bija can be formed, and Aramuhukunda recalls an earlier Festival Utsava shared with Nara Varuna. Many practise crafts learned from humans: cooking, shopkeeping, song gathering, mending the Varuna Contraption, and tending the Vanarana nursery. Named Aranara across Sumeru also offer small tasks and games.
### LARGE +
Beyond the named residents the Traveler can help 64 unnamed Aranara scattered across Dharma Forest (Confused, Material-Collecting, Mischievous, Music-Loving and Reckless Aranara). Each gives a reward, then moves to Mahavanaranapna, the dreamlike version of Vanarana. Aranara give flowers to friends so they will be remembered if they meet again.
### ULTRA +
Named groups in the Aranyaka story include those who fetched the Vasoma Fruits (Arakavi, Arayasa, Aradasa, Arasudraka), those who repaired the Varuna Contraption (Arapandu, Aranakula, Arabalika, Arakunti), those who cleaned Mawtiyima (Aranakin, Aragaru, Aranaga), Ararycan on Devantaka Mountain, and Arapacati's culinary trainees (Arasaka, Arachatora, Araphala, Arapas). Araminali keeps a set of chests and rewards the Traveler for helping many Aranara. Outside the Aranara themselves, Port Ormos' Acara Crafts sells toy Aranara carvings made by artisans who had never seen a real one but wanted to bring children joy (Graven Innocence event, 3.0).

## origin
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Rukkhadevata created the Aranara shortly after she left the desert to make Dharma Forest. She sang a song that made the Ashvattha Tree grow "from Sarva"; it bore a pomegranate, and each seed became an Aranara, Aramuhukunda being the first. The Aranara guarded the forest, befriended its creatures and for a time lived alongside humans.
### LARGE +
Item text on the Laurel Coronet says a jungle ruler named Viaghara blessed the first Aranara under the first Vasara Tree, and that his pact with the divine kept the forest's birds and beasts from harming them.

## history
@version: 3.0
### STANDARD
The Aranara first lived in Old Vanarana and were friendly with Sumeru's humans, many of whom visited them in Vanarana and the Ashvattha's Concourses. When the Cataclysm struck, many Aranara fought the invaders from the Abyss before leaving Old Vanarana, and built a new Vanarana to the north. The true Vanarana was hidden in a dream, anchored to the real world by the Tree of Dreams. When Sumeru's people stopped dreaming, the Aranara grew wary of grown humans.
### LARGE +
Some time after the Cataclysm the Traveler's Sibling passed through Sumeru, helped repair the Varuna Contraption with Aramuhukunda and Aradasha, and was named "Nara Varuna" by the Aranara. Before leaving, they told the Aranara to expect another golden-haired human who would give them even better memories. The Aranara cherish the memory and keep murals and monuments of Nara Varuna.
### ULTRA +
The Withering, which the Aranara call Marana, spread after Rukkhadevata "returned to Sarva". Aramuhukunda became a Vasara Tree in Old Vanarana to suppress Marana's Avatar, and Araji, Aramaha and Arayama became the three Vasara Trees that seal the Ashvattha's Concourses. In the Aranyaka quests the Traveler helped the Aranara cleanse those trees and destroy Marana's Avatar; Arama then became the new Ashvattha Tree, which bore the Bija, and Arana gave up her own memories to bring it about.

## relations
### STANDARD
Aranara trust children and few adults. The golden-haired Traveler is accepted as kin of Nara Varuna, and the Forest Watcher Rana is trusted as an exception. After Rana is saved, Arana leaves Vanarana to travel with her. The Aranara also gave the Tighnarians the name Valuka Shuna when they came to the rainforest.
### LARGE +
According to the wiki's quest summary, Yoimiya could also see Aranara because of her pure, warm heart, although no encounter is shown. The current Dendro Archon is Nahida; since Rukkhadevata was edited out of the Irminsul's memory in the 3.2 Archon Quest, the Aranara regard her as their parent and call her Lord of Verdure. They fear the Withering, and the Fatui who, in the Aranyaka story, plot to lure and capture an Aranara.
### ULTRA +
The Akademiya's Amurta darshan studied whether Aranara exist, but adults generally dismiss them as imaginary friends. The Aranara themselves call the Eremites "Nara Valuka", people of the sand.

## status
@version: 3.0
### STANDARD
As of 7.1 the Aranara still live in Vanarana and its dream, Mahavanaranapna. Arama stands as the new Ashvattha Tree in Old Vanarana, Marana's Avatar is destroyed and Old Vanarana is recovering, though the Withering is still present elsewhere in Sumeru.
### LARGE +
Aramuhukunda returned to Sarva at the end of the Aranyaka story. Araja, as the Tree of Dreams, still sustains the dream-village but has lost many old memories. Arana left to journey with Rana, who received a Dendro Vision, and Aranakula and Arakara tend the Vanarana nursery.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay guidance (project recommendation): write Aranara as gentle, shy and curious, speaking simply and warmly and using words such as "Nara"; they hide or freeze when frightened.
### LARGE +
Avoid making them cynical, worldly or combat-minded by default, and do not give them human family ties; they do not reproduce. Remember their fear of fire, salt and the Withering, and that flowers, songs and memories mean a great deal to them. Only Aramuhukunda is the first Aranara.

## entry: Ararakalari
@keys: Ararakalari, Aranara abilities
@version: 3.0
### STANDARD
Ararakalari are the memory-fed powers of the Aranara. Each Aranara has a unique one, which may attack, restrain, teleport, seal an area or guard against the Withering. Using it consumes memories, but new memories can add new abilities. Arama says they originate in the "Mahamaitri Kusa Stotra", a song praising Queen Aranyani, humans and the Aranara. A Kusava seed makes them stronger.

## entry: Vasara Tree
@keys: Vasara Tree, Vasara Trees, Vasoma Fruit, Ashvattha Tree
@version: 3.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
A Vasara Tree is what an Aranara can choose to become at the end of its life; it keeps dreams and memories, and even some awareness, and other Aranara still treat it as the Aranara it was. The first Vasara Tree is the Ashvattha Tree that Rukkhadevata made. In Aranyaka the Traveler cleanses three Vasara Trees (Araji, Aramaha, Arayama), each of which bears a Vasoma Fruit to feed the corroded Ashvattha Tree. It can no longer bear a Bija, so Arama becomes the new Ashvattha Tree in Old Vanarana. Arana, though ancient, refuses to become a Vasara Tree while she still wants to play with humans.

## entry: Marana
@keys: Marana, Marana's Avatar
@version: 3.0
### STANDARD
Marana is the Aranara name for the Withering. In Araja's telling it began after Rukkhadevata returned to Sarva, and urges everything toward death. The Aranara say it can turn Vana (forest) into Valuka (desert). Its avatars take many forms. In the Aranyaka story the Traveler and Arama destroy Marana's Avatar in the Land of Grounded Dreams beneath Old Vanarana, after which Old Vanarana begins to rejuvenate.

## entry: Vanarana and Mahavanaranapna
@keys: Vanarana, Mahavanaranapna, Old Vanarana
@version: 3.0
### STANDARD
Vanarana is the Aranara's village in Dharma Forest. Old Vanarana, the original home, was abandoned in the Cataclysm and became the heart of the Withering. The Aranara built a new Vanarana to the north and hid the true one in a dream, Mahavanaranapna, held to the real world by the Tree of Dreams (Araja). It is entered by playing the "Rhythm of the Great Dream" on a Vintage Lyre at the Phantasmal Gate or a Silapna.

## entry: Nara Varuna
@keys: Nara Varuna, Golden Nara
@version: 3.0
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Nara Varuna was the Aranara's name for the Traveler's Sibling, a golden-haired human who helped repair the Varuna Contraption and shared the last Festival Utsava with Aramuhukunda and his friends. Before leaving, they told the Aranara to expect another golden-haired human who would bring even better memories. The Aranara therefore welcome the Traveler as kin of Nara Varuna.

## entry: Arana
@keys: Arana
@version: 3.0
### STANDARD
Arana is an Aranara of Vimara Village, with a pale mint body, floppy ear-like structures, a dark green conical hat decorated with a blue butterfly, and two orange leaves that act as a propeller. She was Rana's childhood friend but forgot her. In the Aranyaka quests she shielded Iotham and then Rana from the Withering, and she gave up her memories to the new Ashvattha Tree to produce the Bija. She then befriended Rana again and left to travel with her.

## entry: Arama, Aramuhukunda and Araja
@keys: Arama, Aramuhukunda, Araja
@version: 3.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Arama is a pale green Aranara with a yellow flower on its head and a red scarf, and the protagonist of the Aranyaka story. It wins the Traveler's trust, helps destroy Marana's Avatar and then grows into the new Ashvattha Tree. Aramuhukunda is the oldest Aranara, first to sprout from the Ashvattha Tree; he became a Vasara Tree to hold back Marana's Avatar and, at the end of the story, happily returned to Sarva. Araja is the village elder, who became the Tree of Dreams and created Mahavanaranapna.

## entry: Queen Aranyani
@keys: Queen Aranyani, Aranyani, Lord of Verdure
@version: 3.2
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Queen Aranyani is what the Aranara called Greater Lord Rukkhadevata, the Dendro Archon who made them and led them until she disappeared. Her song was closest to the Sourcesong. After her memory was removed from the Irminsul in the 3.2 Archon Quest, the Aranara are said no longer to remember her under that name and instead speak of the Lord of Verdure.

## entry: Aranara songs
@keys: Rhythm of the Great Dream, Sourcesong, song gatherer, song gatherers
@version: 3.0
### STANDARD
Aranara songs are tied to power and identity. Playing the right song on a Vintage Lyre can open a Silapna or a Gloomy Path, or call a Dendrograna. Song gatherers collect other Aranara's songs and look for the Sourcesong; the closer a song is to it, the stronger the Ararakalari.
