## summary
### LITE
The Tighnarians, called Valuka Shuna ("large desert dog") by the Aranara, are a humanoid, foxlike people of Sumeru. They came from Tighnar and served King Deshret in the Great Red Sand. After his fall they lived in Tulaytullah and later moved to the rainforest. Tighnari, the Forest Watcher, is one of their descendants.
### STANDARD +
Tighnarians are remembered for wisdom, and for the Temple of Silence, which they founded in Tulaytullah to keep King Deshret's knowledge. Their most famous member, Hermanubis, was one of Deshret's Seven Pillars.

## nature
### LITE
Hermanubis, the only "true" Tighnarian shown, had a jackal-like head and a humanlike body. Mortal descendants such as Tighnari are mostly human, with big fennec-like ears and bushy tails.
### STANDARD +
In the desert their hair and fur were pale, which helped with the heat. Over time in the rainforest it turned darker green, and some descendants now tolerate heat badly; Tighnari's own childhood trip toward the desert ended with him fainting from the heat. The Aranara named them Valuka Shuna even though they looked more like foxes than dogs.
### LARGE +
Tighnari's Character Story 5 says that his ancestors lived alongside the Valuka Shuna and inherited some of their traits, so descendants most likely share a human ancestry. Hermanubis wielded Electro-based power while alive, though it is not known whether other Tighnarians could.

## history
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Tighnarians lived in the Great Red Sand during King Deshret's era and mostly served him as priests. Deshret chose the sage Hermanubis from among them as his familiar. When Deshret died, Hermanubis led the Tighnarian priests to Tulaytullah, where they founded the Temple of Silence to guard knowledge from his fallen civilisation.
### LARGE +
About a century later the desert city-states fell into war. A king of Gurabad besieged Tulaytullah and demanded the Temple of Silence as proof of victory, and the city's ruling elite sided with him and blamed the Temple. Hermanubis had worn himself out holding back the forbidden knowledge Deshret had brought and shattered himself into Ba fragments, giving his power to Temple members, who drove the invaders away. The people of Tulaytullah then shunned the Temple, and the Temple and Tulaytullah's Tighnarians left the desert for the rainforest.
### ULTRA +
In Sumeru they allied with the Akademiya to preserve their knowledge and lived there in peace for several centuries. After Rukkhadevata's death in the Cataclysm about 500 years ago, the Sages of the Akademiya grew corrupt, and a century later the Temple of Silence quietly ended the partnership. Some members returned to the desert (to a large temple near Thamud Oasis) but most Tighnarians stayed in the rainforest. In The Dead Past is the Living Present (Cyno's Story Quest "Oathkeeper", 4.6) Sethos told Tighnari that he was a descendant of the Valuka Shuna and that the Temple were the heirs to the will of Hermanubis; Bamoun had already recognised him as a descendant. Tighnari did not know before that his forebears had such ties to the Temple of Silence.

## society
### STANDARD
While Deshret lived, most of his priests were Tighnarians, and Hermanubis was their leader and a revered guide of priests. Later, Tulaytullah prospered through their wisdom, and people came to link the Tighnarians with the city although they came from Tighnar.
### LARGE +
Their traditions claim that their survival after Deshret's fall was thanks to Greater Lord Rukkhadevata's grace, which also made their fur green. Tighnari's family records included a manuscript left by an ancestor about the Valuka Shuna. His father studied insects and his mother studied fossils.

## relations
### STANDARD
The Aranara gave them the name Valuka Shuna after they moved to the forest. They worked with the Akademiya before leaving it. The Temple of Silence they founded still exists under Sethos, and Cyno, one of its Ba fragment bearers, is Tighnari's close friend.
### LARGE +
Bamoun and Sethos recognise Tighnari as a descendant of the Valuka Shuna. Cyno and Tighnari were close friends at the Akademiya before the events of Oathkeeper. Valuka is the Aranara word for sand; Valuka Shuna means "large desert dog".

## status
@version: 4.6
### STANDARD
As of 7.1 no full Tighnarian remains known; Hermanubis is long dead, though Ba fragments of his power live on in Temple members. Tighnari and his family are the only known descendants, living in Sumeru's rainforest.
### LARGE +
Because the wiki notes only Tighnari and his parents as descendants, canon does not show how many other families descend from the Tighnarians.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay guidance (project recommendation): treat Tighnarians as a long-gone people, known now through Hermanubis and descendants. A descendant is human, with fox-like ears and tail, not a beastman, and may be sensitive to desert heat.
### LARGE +
Do not make every foxlike person in Sumeru a Tighnarian; only Tighnari's family is established. Avoid giving descendants Electro powers merely for their ancestry.

## entry: Hermanubis
@keys: Hermanubis, Greatest of Sages
@version: 4.6
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Hermanubis was a warrior of Tighnar who served King Deshret and became his familiar, the only one of the Seven Pillars to rise from humanity to the rank of a god's familiar. Known as the Greatest of Sages, he was sincere, steadfast, kind and respected even by the other Pillars. After Deshret's death he founded the Temple of Silence in Tulaytullah. When Tulaytullah was betrayed, he divided himself into Ba fragments to protect the Temple, and those fragments still exist.

## entry: Tulaytullah and the Temple of Silence
@keys: Tulaytullah, Temple of Silence
@version: 4.6
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Tulaytullah was a desert city that prospered while Hermanubis' Tighnarian priests kept Deshret's knowledge there as the Temple of Silence. A Gurabad king besieged it and its elite sided with him, and the Temple left for the rainforest and later returned to the desert near Thamud Oasis. An empty Akademiya office of the same name now handles sealed information.

## entry: Valuka Shuna
@keys: Valuka Shuna, large desert dog
@version: 3.0
### STANDARD
Valuka Shuna is the name the Aranara gave the Tighnarians after they moved to the rainforest. It means "large desert dog" in their language, though the Tighnarians looked more like foxes. Tighnari's Character Story 5 tells how, as a child, he found the name in a family manuscript as a child and was startled to learn he was a "large desert dog".
