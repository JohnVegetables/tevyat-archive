## summary
### LITE
Snezhnaya has three open-world bosses as of 7.1. Two are discarded creations of the alchemist Koshchei: the Immortal Construct, a feral winged beast with crimson leaf-like wings, and the Chimeric Winged Lion, a two-headed lion-and-bird chimera. The third is the Guardian Blade of Drifting Snow, an ancient Snegovik knight who has left her people's mountain manor and still trains alone in the snow.
### STANDARD +
The first two belong to the Aberrant Chimeric Monsters, the by-products of bio-alchemy from the age of the Belyi Tsar. The Guardian Blade belongs to the fae of crystalline flesh. In the game they are classed as "Mystical Beasts".

## immortal_construct
@version: 7.0
@spoiler: minor
### LITE
A mindless winged beast in Everfrozen Earth, titled "The Remnant Body of the Eternal Flamefeather". It is a by-product, made only to serve someone else's plan, and now wanders the wilds with no purpose left.
### STANDARD +
The Archive says Koshchei made it while creating the individual called the Firebird. He is said to have kept the one success from each batch and thrown away or discarded the rest. This derivative should have fused with some form of power to be completed. Without that, it only wanders the mountain gorges as an incomplete, mindless beast. The Event Menu text speaks of an unfinished grand ritual and "hollow dreams" locked in its shell.
### LARGE +
Appearance and game behaviour (labelled as such): it has crimson, leaf-like wings and fights with Physical and Pyro damage. Its signature move, Lifeflare, spends a large part of its health to raise a segmented ward and makes its blows heal it for a while; breaking the ward stuns it. This is a gameplay expression of the "immortal" in its name, not a stated lore fact. It drops the boss material Unscorched Blossom Branch.
### ULTRA +
The wiki's Zhara page describes Zhara, the Firebird, as an Immortal Construct, the same kind of creature. She has a humanoid upper body but is otherwise similar; she guarded Okurov in Flamefeather Valley for centuries and was subdued by the Traveler in the quest Chorus of Solace and then dissolved by Aksinya (the wiki dates her removal to the quest To Rest in a Forgotten Field). Koshchei's work on the "Perfect Tree" at Okurov is the context for the constructs.

## chimeric_winged_lion
@version: 7.0
### LITE
A two-headed chimera, one head a lion and one a falcon, titled "Solo Accompanist", found in the Volkodlak Tundra. It commands Anemo and Electro power far beyond ordinary man-made monsters.
### STANDARD +
The Archive calls it a product of bio-alchemy and one of the man-made monsters that were abandoned. To bring a certain great wish to fruition, an alchemist (Koshchei) created many primordial bodies, "Prototypes", by a technique that must never be retold. Only one grew into the perfect creation he planned. The others, close to perfection but failures, were carelessly remade into two-headed monsters, and these still have tremendous power; among chimeras nothing can stand against them. The Handbook adds that anomalous materials fused into its shell may explain the power.
### LARGE +
In the game it fights with Anemo swipes and arrows and Electro swords and orbs, and opens with an Electro ward that stuns it when broken. It drops the boss material Severed Tail of the Sky-Roamer. The Volkodlak Tundra's Expedition text calls it the "Tundra of Ancient Beasts" and says the Belyi Tsar razed and rebuilt the former capital of the Miscislavli Alliance there.
### ULTRA +
One of the Winged Lions gained a degree of intelligence; the Belyi Tsar took it in rather than let Koshchei dispose of it, and gave it the name Zver. Zver is a separate character (see npc.zver): the Tsar's companion and commander of the chimeric monsters, who after the Cataclysm guarded his body in the Pale Crown Palace. The ordinary Winged Lion boss is a different, unthinking specimen.

## guardian_blade
@version: 7.1
@spoiler: minor
### LITE
An ancient Snegovik swordswoman titled "Outcast Ice Shadow", met at Fellfrost Peak in southern Snezhnaya. She left the House of Hesperides and trains alone, awaiting a day she may return.
### STANDARD +
The Handbook says she is held in high esteem among her kin. After the current Snegurochka princess came to power she left the clan's mountain manor, perhaps because their ideals no longer aligned. The Archive adds that she once served the legendary Great Snegurochka, Yalyane Morevna, and was entrusted for life with protecting the Snegovik clan. Seven were given this charge; she alone remains, so every succession of the Snegovik sovereign must take place under her watch. The current princess is Pavlina.
### LARGE +
Her first encounter of each day carries a spoken challenge: a cold voice asks who dares enter uninvited. If the player has finished the quest Bell Ringing at Dusk, she instead remarks that she senses Pavlina's aura and wonders whether Pavlina failed. In that quest Pavlina is defeated and dies. In combat she wields ice blades and icicles and, partway through, encases herself in an ice coffin (ability names unofficial). Her voice lines reuse Pavlina's. She drops the boss material Vagabond's Cracked Armor.
### ULTRA +
Snegovik and Snegurochka are among the Snowland Fae; their souls are incomplete and become whole only through love of a human, which is the background of the Hesperides rituals in the quest series Hesperides of Love and Hate. The Guardian Blade is not shown taking part in that story.

## origins
### STANDARD
The Immortal Construct and Chimeric Winged Lion come from the same cause: the unregulated alchemy of the Belyi Tsar's age, when alchemists such as Koshchei combined every form of life. The Handbook says most such monsters died in the war against the Onslaught of Dark Beasts and the survivors are rare exceptions. The Guardian Blade has no such link; she is a fae knight from a much older clan.
### LARGE +
Koshchei served in Hvergelmir with Pavlina, Alvis and Aksinya and later hid in Flamefeather Valley under the name Doctor Moreau, where he made immortal constructs while pursuing an endless life. The wiki's Koshchei page also says he was recruited after experimenting on Snowland Fae.

## placement
### STANDARD
Immortal Construct: Everfrozen Earth (northern Snezhnaya); Chimeric Winged Lion: Volkodlak Tundra; Guardian Blade of Drifting Snow: Fellfrost Peak, south of Snezhnograd and above the town of Svetloledovka, whose Prime Ice mining belongs to the Snegurochka's mountain. Versions: 7.0 for the first two, 7.1 for the third.
### LARGE +
The Flamefeather Valley Expedition text mentions bird-shaped monsters with pinions like crimson leaves, but the Construct's wiki page lists Everfrozen Earth as its location; see uncertainties.

## relations
### STANDARD
The Construct and the Lion are linked through Koshchei, Hvergelmir and Zver; the Guardian Blade is linked to Pavlina, Yalyane Morevna and the House of Hesperides. Nothing connects the knight to Koshchei.

## status
### STANDARD
As of 7.1 all three can be fought in the open world. No source says what becomes of them or whether more Snezhnaya bosses will follow in later versions; nothing beyond 7.1 is recorded here.

## chronology
### ULTRA
- 7.0: Immortal Construct and Chimeric Winged Lion arrive with the Snezhnaya release, with the drops Unscorched Blossom Branch and Severed Tail of the Sky-Roamer.
- 7.1: the Guardian Blade of Drifting Snow arrives as the single new monster, with a new Teleport Waypoint at Fellfrost Peak and the drop Vagabond's Cracked Armor.

## entry: Immortal Construct
@keys: Immortal Construct, Remnant Body of the Eternal Flamefeather, Lifeflare
@keys2: Snezhnaya, Koshchei, Everfrozen Earth, Flamefeather Valley
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
A feral, mindless winged beast with crimson leaf-like wings, titled "The Remnant Body of the Eternal Flamefeather". The Archive says Koshchei made it as a by-product while creating the Firebird; with no purpose left, it roams the mountain gorges as an incomplete beast. Its Pyro-edged Lifeflare state is a game mechanic.

## entry: Chimeric Winged Lion
@keys: Chimeric Winged Lion, Solo Accompanist, Sky-Roamer
@keys2: Snezhnaya, Volkodlak Tundra, Koshchei, chimera
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
A two-headed chimera with a lion head and a falcon head, titled "Solo Accompanist". A failed Prototype remade by Koshchei, it is among the strongest chimeras. It is found in the Volkodlak Tundra and uses Anemo and Electro power. Zver, the Belyi Tsar's companion, was a rare sentient member of the kind.

## entry: Guardian Blade of Drifting Snow
@keys: Guardian Blade of Drifting Snow, Outcast Ice Shadow, Fellfrost Peak
@keys2: Snezhnaya, Snegovik, Snegurochka, Pavlina
@version: 7.1
### STANDARD
An ancient female Snegovik knight who once served Yalyane Morevna and guarded the Snegovik clan's successions. She left the House of Hesperides after Pavlina became Grand Duchess and now trains in the snow at Fellfrost Peak. Her name is not given; she fights with Cryo blades, icicles and an ice coffin.
