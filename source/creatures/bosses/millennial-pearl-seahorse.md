## summary
### LITE
The Millennial Pearl Seahorse is a very large, ancient Fontemer Aberrant that dwells in a cavern in the Liffey Region of Fontaine. It is an Electro boss, and its Archive title is the Nameless Lord of the Great Lake. Legend names it one of the two sovereigns of the Fontemer Aberrants; the Archive insists the legend is romantic fancy.
### STANDARD +
It first appeared in Version 4.1 as a normal (open-world) boss. In game text it is also catalogued as the Chasm Seahorse, and it is a different creature from the other Fontemer sovereign, the Emperor of Fire and Iron.

## nature
### LITE
The Adventurer Handbook calls it an ancient suzerain of the Fontemer Aberrants, with a body of phantasmal beauty and a temper fiercer than a raging thunderstorm. It is a seahorse-like beast with a single horn, and it carries an Electro-charged pearl. Its title reads as: the great tyrant of the Aberrants lives in a deep cavern, and few have withstood its violet, electrifying glare.
### STANDARD +
The Archive says it is one of the two sovereigns at the top of the Fontemer Aberrants. Writers praise it as proud and noble because it lives alone, but the real reason for the praise is its elegant, slender body; hence it is a "Pearl Seahorse" while the Armored Crabs' sovereign is an "Emperor".
### LARGE +
The same Archive text then deflates the legend. It says the Fontemer Aberrants are simply aquatic animals with no courts, no fallen kingdoms and no words to exalt or curse, so the picture of two rulers splitting the lake is attributed to people unwilling to accept the plain reality of the world.

### ULTRA +
The wiki notes an Enraged variant of the boss exists, that its Chinese name contains a character alluding to the mythical qilin, and that its Electro drops release as its health falls. These are catalogue details, not story.

## legend
@spoiler: none
### STANDARD
In the legends and rumours of Fontaine, a brutal war was fought among the Aberrants of the Fontemer. Only the Millennial Pearl Seahorse and the Emperor of Fire and Iron survived, dividing the great lake into two courts, one ruling the north and one the south. The "Song of Dobharcu" is cited for the outcome: the great Hunter's Ray called the Stormlord churned huge waves in its death throes, which people call the "first diluvian period". The Archive treats all of this as imagination, not fact.

## abilities
### LITE
Electro attacks while its pearl is intact: it leaps and slams the ground, charges with its horn, marks a target with a circle of fish, sends homing fish-shaped missiles and swipes with its tail, leaving electrified ground behind.
### STANDARD +
The Xenomare Pearl it holds in front of its legs is Electro-infused. While it is intact the seahorse resists damage far more and uses its stronger attacks. Destroying it with elemental reactions leaves the beast paralysed for a time. Without the pearl the seahorse falls back on plain Physical tail swipes and charges.
### LARGE +
Once the pearl is badly weakened, the seahorse rises into the air and summons three Resonant Coral Orbs, then draws on them to call lightning over a wide area; destroying the orbs breaks the pearl. This happens once per fight. It can also regrow the pearl with a shockwave, but the regrown pearl is weaker. Its drop is the Fontemer Unihorn, and it has a weak point.

## status
### STANDARD
As of 7.1 it remains a boss in the Liffey Region. Canon gives it no named personal history and no role in the Archon Quests.

## entry: Millennial Pearl Seahorse
@keys: Millennial Pearl Seahorse, Pearl Seahorse, Nameless Lord of the Great Lake, Chasm Seahorse
@version: 4.1
### STANDARD
The Millennial Pearl Seahorse is an ancient Electro-wielding Fontemer Aberrant living in a cavern in Fontaine's Liffey Region. Legend makes it one of two sovereigns of the great lake, ruling the north or south alongside the Emperor of Fire and Iron; the Archive says this is romantic imagination and that Fontemer Aberrants are only aquatic animals. Its fight centres on a charged Xenomare Pearl: break it with elemental reactions and the beast is stunned and loses its Electro attacks.

## entry: Fontemer Aberrants and the sovereign legend
@keys: Fontemer Aberrant, Emperor of Fire and Iron, Song of Dobharcu
@keys2: Seahorse, great lake
@version: 4.1
### STANDARD
Fontaine's tales say the Fontemer Aberrants once warred until only a seahorse and a crab Emperor remained, splitting the great lake between them. The "Song of Dobharcu" blames the Stormlord (a Hunter's Ray) dying in the war for the first diluvian period. The Archive entry dismisses this as romantic writing and describes the Aberrants as ordinary animals.
