## summary
### LITE
The Thunder Manifestation is an unusual Electro lifeform, a giant violet bird of lightning that haunts Amakumo Peak on Seirai Island in Inazuma. It looks somewhat like an Oceanid but lacks her intelligence or memories. What drives it is raw, primal fury that the texts variously call resentment, hatred or regret.
### STANDARD +
Its title is "Raging Thunder of Amakumo Peak". The Archive says such beings come down like lightning only on places that host great lingering resentment, like the fragmented Amakumo Peak under its always-roiling clouds, and that while the land's grudges do not fade, neither will the roaring thunder. The boss unlocks when the world quest series Seirai Stormchasers is completed. It first appeared in Version 2.1.
### LARGE +
The wiki treats the Seirai Thunder Manifestation as the congealed regret of the Thunderbird Kanna Kapatcir, whose story is told through the Thundering Fury artifact set, the Hamayumi bow and the Tsurumi Island quests. See the uncertainties for how firmly that link is supported.

## nature
### LITE
An Electro elemental lifeform of lightning and storm. It does not speak or reason like an Oceanid, and acts from a single overwhelming emotion.
### STANDARD +
The Handbook, once the boss is unlocked, says it weaves thunder and lightning as a song to the resentment that drives its every move, and is "an elemental being driven by pure regret". The Archive adds that it has no Oceanid-like intelligence or memories. The Storm Beads it leaves behind are described as crackling Electro pearls, and the item text says the singers hold that the unbearable disappointments and sufferings of life drive these beings to wander the broken earth, scattering sky-searing lightning.
### LARGE +
The two accounts use different language: the Handbook stresses regret, the Archive stresses a primal fury, and the item text hatred and disappointment. Canon does not choose between them.

## abilities
### STANDARD
Game mechanics (labelled as such): all attacks are Electro. At first it sets lightning-strike probes around the arena that pulse when it has noticed a player, sweeps in a spinning wing attack, and calls waves of bolts. A pulse or shockwave that hits a player enrages it. While enraged, it teleports across the arena, slashes with a wing, closes two walls of lightning around a target, shoots electric feathers, dives across the arena and fires a homing thunder cage. Most move names are unofficial.
### LARGE +
It has a weak point, drops Vajrada Amethyst gems and the unique Storm Beads, and releases an arena-wide shockwave around 74% HP. Its shape is that of a huge bird of prey, in keeping with the Thunderbird of the local tale.

## history
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The ancient Thunderbird Civilization of Tsurumi Island worshipped Kanna Kapatcir, a powerful Thunderbird the wiki's Kapatcir page calls a monster misunderstood as the island's guardian. The boy Ruu was sacrificed in her name; when she found him dead, her grief became lightning that destroyed the tribe.
### LARGE +
Item lore and the wiki say she was later hunted down as a monster, reportedly by Ei. The wiki further reports that the remains of her power were sealed on Seirai, and that Asase Hibiki unsealed them to unleash the resentment on ships flying the Electro Archon's banner. In Seirai Stormchasters, Neko says Hibiki sealed and unsealed the island's Warding Stones, meant to hold a bird of immense size, and that Hibiki and Ako Domeki never returned after a vast storm.

## quests
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
In Seirai Stormchasters: Part IV the Traveler seals the final Warding Stones, then defeats the Thunder Manifestation, and afterwards finds a Peculiar Pinion, a feather. Taisuke judges from its dimensions that the bird must have been as large as the floating stone platform above. Completing the series unlocks the boss for repeat challenges.
### LARGE +
Per the wiki's Kapatcir page, another Thunder Manifestation appears in the hollow of Mt. Kanna on Tsurumi Island after the Traveler and Paimon bring Ruu back to the place of his sacrifice, born of Kapatcir's rage. Ruu then goes to Seirai Island and sings to the Thunder Manifestation, which fulfils the old promise and lifts the island's curse, with Ruu given another feather as a sign. This summary is wiki-sourced and should be checked against the Tsurumi quests.

## event_variant
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
The Crackling Thunder Manifestation, titled "Stormy Resentment", is an event-only enemy in Feast of the Departed Warriors (Version 3.7, June 2023), with a stronger moveset built around lightning probes.

## chronology
### ULTRA
- 2.0: the Hamayumi bow, whose story names the freed resentment of the Thunderbird, is released.
- 2.1: the Thunder Manifestation arrives on Seirai Island with Seirai Stormchasters.
- 3.7: the Crackling Thunder Manifestation variant appears in Feast of the Departed Warriors.

## status
### STANDARD
As of Version 7.1 the Thunder Manifestation remains a permanent boss on Seirai Island. No Nod-Krai (6.x) or Snezhnaya (7.0-7.1) content adds to its lore.

## entry: Storm Beads
@keys: Storm Beads
@version: 2.1
### STANDARD
The Thunder Manifestation's unique boss drop: crackling Electro pearls. The item text says such beings only descend upon lands where powerful hatred dwells, driven forever across the broken earth by life's unbearable disappointments and sufferings.

## entry: Seirai Warding Stones
@keys: Warding Stone, Warding Stones
@keys2: Seirai, Thunder Manifestation
@version: 2.1
### STANDARD
Four stones on Seirai Island that the quest Seirai Stormchasters has the Traveler seal. Neko says Asase Hibiki once sealed and unsealed them and that they were meant to seal a bird of enormous size. Sealing the last two, up in the sky, forms a platform of floating stones where the Thunder Manifestation is fought.
