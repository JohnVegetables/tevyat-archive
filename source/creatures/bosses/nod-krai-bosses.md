## summary
### LITE
Seven big enemies arrived with Nod-Krai between versions 6.0 and 6.7. Knuckle Duckle is a duck-shaped kuuvahki robot built by the Krumkake Craftshop's owner. The Radiant Moonfly and Radiant Moongecko are beasts steeped in ancient moonlight. Frostnight Herra is a proud moon-touched Frostnight Scion. The Super-Heavy Landrover: Mechanized Fortress is a Fatui war machine. The Lord of the Hidden Depths is an Abyssal creature watching from a rift. The Precursor's Secret Source Matrix of Overseer Network is an ancient machine left on the Frost Moon.
### STANDARD +
They are open-world fights on the Nod-Krai islands (Lempo Isle, Hiisi Island, Paha Isle, Ashveil Peak, Wavechaser Plain) and on the Frost Moon. Three themes recur in their Archive texts: the lingering power of the old moon and its kuuvahki, intruders and machines straying from what their makers intended, and the stability of the world beneath the Heavenly Principles.

## nature
### STANDARD
The seven fall into five families. Automatons: Knuckle Duckle, the Landrover (a Fatui Landcruiser) and the Secret Source Matrix. Radiant Beasts, a group of mystical beasts empowered by kuuvahki: the Moonfly and the Moongecko. Elemental lifeforms: Frostnight Herra, a Frostnight Scion. The Abyss: the Lord of the Hidden Depths.
### LARGE +
Radiant beasts are ordinary wildlife warped by long exposure to the ancient moon's power and kuuvahki; most are attuned to one element. Frostnight Scions are elemental lifeforms said to be born of the Frost Moon's blessings. Landcruisers are autonomous machines from the Armory Palace in Snezhnaya, and the Landrover is its super-heavy member. Secret Source Automatons are strange old machines, mostly found in Natlan, with some on the Frost Moon.

## abilities
### STANDARD
Game mechanics, as described in the in-game tutorials. Knuckle Duckle fights with heavy punches, and in "Duckstruction Mode" gains a Ward and summons floating Stamping Devices; frying those with Electro-Charged or Lunar-Charged damage makes them hit its Ward. The radiant beasts can trap the party in a Radiant Cocoon, escaped by healing to full HP. Frostnight Herra enters a Gloomveiled state; damaging it forces it out and it takes on the element that hurt it most. The Landrover overheats under Pyro and then raises a Cryo Ward to cool down. The Lord of the Hidden Depths deploys a Deepdark Shield and calls Fishers of Hidden Depths. The Secret Source Matrix raises a Convergence Field, broken by freezing it and shattering the ice.
### LARGE +
The Moongecko can prepare "Voidstep Reverberation", cancelled by destroying the Moonstone Anchor it leaves behind; escaping its Cocoon also lowers its Geo RES. If Herra's Gloomveiled damage was mainly Lunar reaction damage it becomes Elementless. The Lord's shield is best broken with Lunar reaction damage, and killing a summoned Fisher also damages it. The Local Legend version of the Matrix, Mushteshir, adds a Suppression Field with Detection Devices that fire water beams. Elemental types: Knuckle Duckle Electro; Moonfly Dendro and Pyro; Herra all elements plus Physical; Landrover Physical, Cryo and Pyro; Moongecko Geo; Lord Electro; Matrix Hydro.

## individuals
### LITE
Seven encounters, by release: Knuckle Duckle and Radiant Moonfly (6.0), Frostnight Herra (6.1), Super-Heavy Landrover (6.2), Lord of the Hidden Depths (6.3), Radiant Moongecko (6.4), Secret Source Matrix (6.7).
### STANDARD +
Wiki boss titles: Creator's Pride (Knuckle Duckle), Shifting Shadow Beneath the Moon (Moonfly), Guardian of the Holy Light (Herra), All-Terrain Combat Mek (Landrover), Faceless, Boundless Overlord (Lord of the Hidden Depths), Moonseeker Drake (Moongecko). Locations: Clink-Clank Krumkake Craftshop on Lempo Isle; Barrowmoss Barrens on Lempo Isle; Hiisi Island; Paha Isle; Ashveil Peak; Afterglow Cavern in Wavechaser Plain; Kalag Guarding Grounds on the Frost Moon.

### ULTRA +
Wiki-listed boss materials (game items, useful as scene props): Precision Kuuvahki Stamping Die (Knuckle Duckle), Lightbearing Scale-Feather (Moonfly), Radiant Antler (Herra), Cyclic Military Kuuvahki Core (Landrover), Remnant of the Dreadwing (Lord of the Hidden Depths), Prismatic Severed Tail (Moongecko). Release order by version name: Luna I (6.0, 2025-09-10), Luna II (6.1), Luna III (6.2), Luna IV (6.3), Luna V (6.4) and Luna VIII (6.7, 2026-07-01). Names: the Moonfly's Chinese name means roughly "Radiant Phantom Moon Butterfly"; the Matrix's old name "Ur-Namrasit-Tilimtu-Babbar" echoes epithets of the Mesopotamian moon god Sin, a real-world reference noted on the wiki, not in-game lore.

## relations
### STANDARD
Knuckle Duckle is tied to Aino and, jokingly, to Ineffa. The Landrover belongs to the Fatui and to Snezhnaya's war effort. Frostnight Herra is honoured as a sacred beast by the Frostmoon Scions. The Lord of the Hidden Depths commands the Fishers of Hidden Depths. The Matrix is a relic of a lost ancient civilisation.
### LARGE +
The creatures of the Frost Moon and the old moon tie into the Heavenly Principles: several Archive texts stress that primeval power was tamed by the Heavenly Principles (the "heavenly ruler" or "Lord of the Sky"), yet the moon's blessing let some beasts keep their old forms. Outsiders such as the Abyss are described as obeying this world's rules and unable to do more than linger at its edges. None of the seven is a recurring character with a personal story; they are encountered in the open world.

## roleplay
### LARGE
Use these as scene elements, not people. Knuckle Duckle, however, speaks (see its entry) in a dry, bureaucratic voice and can appear as a comic security robot at the workshop. The beasts are wild and act on instinct; a scene with Herra can feel solemn and ceremonial; the Landrover should feel like an unthinking Fatui machine; the Lord of the Hidden Depths should be a distant, eerie watcher, not a talker.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 all seven remain in the Nod-Krai open world as repeatable bosses.
### STANDARD +
Nothing in canon through 7.1 ends or changes any of them. Knuckle Duckle remains at the craftshop; the Frost Moon machine is still stranded in its ruins.

## entry: Knuckle Duckle
@keys: Knuckle Duckle, Krumpanion, Krumkake Friendship Guide, Duster Mode, Workshop's Trove
@version: 6.0
### STANDARD
Knuckle Duckle is a kuuvahki mechanism built from scrap metal and mechanical bearings by the owner of the Clink-Clank Krumkake Craftshop on Lempo Isle, Aino, and it partly resembles a duck because of its creator's tastes. The Archive says its output and durability match the Fatui's best weapons, yet it is simply a toy duck that can punch extremely hard. It guards the workshop, greets visitors as "Krumpanions" and offers two options: a sparring fight ("Duster Mode") or the Workshop's Trove shop, which opens only for those with Aino's key (when Aino or Ineffa is in the party).
### LARGE +
It describes itself as a multipurpose armed robot for domestic use, combining security, surveillance, storage, dusting, cleaning and krumkake baking, and it records trusted people in the Krumkake Friendship Guide, a database kept by its master. It is competitive with Ineffa, whom it calls a multifunctional robot for domestic application: it claims its production date is earlier and its code name better, and refuses her lakkaberry krumkake recipe. In weekly reports it can list passers-by (in one dialogue: 3 Treasure Hoarder scavenger groups, 2 Fatui squads, 1 Frostmoon Scions congregation, 1 adventure team and 5 Voynich Guild caravans, risk low). The Archive's commentary compares the duck to the world itself, shaped by its makers' odd tastes, and adds that Ineffa would never say so to Aino.

## entry: Radiant Moonfly
@keys: Radiant Moonfly, Shifting Shadow Beneath the Moon, Barrowmoss Barrens
@version: 6.0
### STANDARD
The Radiant Moonfly is a butterfly-shaped monster, the strongest species of radiant beast according to its tutorial, found at Barrowmoss Barrens on Lempo Isle. The Archive says that after the Heavenly Principles tamed the wild primeval power, such a creature should have dwindled in size and strength, yet the moon's blessing lets it remain in its prime as a remnant of an older world, when the ancient moon lit the land. It is described as dancing in moonlight, beautiful and dangerous.
### LARGE +
It fights with Dendro and Pyro and can wrap the party in a Radiant Cocoon. Its tutorial adds that radiant beasts are rumoured to change element under the right conditions, which is not confirmed.

## entry: Frostnight Herra
@keys: Frostnight Herra, Guardian of the Holy Light, Frostnight Scion, Hiisi Island
@version: 6.1
### STANDARD
Frostnight Herra is a majestic Frostnight Scion on Hiisi Island, wielding ancient powers long ago tamed by the Lord of the Sky. Its Archive text says legend places such beings in the age before the Frost Moon was broken, favoured by the divine emissary so that daylight would never taint them. Among the Frostmoon Scions it is regarded as a sacred beast lost to the world, passing along secret lunar paths and, as it has for centuries, guarding lands coveted by calamity and watching over those who live there, in fulfilment of an oath to the apostle of the golden city.
### LARGE +
The boss starts Elementless and can enter a Gloomveiled state; damage then decides which element it takes on. Before it was released in 6.1, its arena held an Icy Crystal Frostnight Scion. The name "Herra" means "lord" in Finnish and related languages.

## entry: Super-Heavy Landrover: Mechanized Fortress
@keys: Super-Heavy Landrover, Mechanized Fortress, Type Drzislav, Landcruiser, All-Terrain Combat Mek
@version: 6.2
### STANDARD
The Super-Heavy Landrover, internally the "Type Drzislav", is a super-heavy war machine jointly developed by the Armory Palace and the Kuuvahki Experimental Design Bureau and fielded by the Fatui on Paha Isle. The Archive says it was originally designed as a civilian all-terrain heavy transport, with drills and cutters, but later models were militarised with heavy weapons. The in-game blurb says it now roars only for the Fatui and Snezhnaya's will against those who stand in the way of the "new world"; the Archive reflects that even the best designs stray from their original purpose.
### LARGE +
It belongs to the Landcruiser group, which the Armory Palace made as "armed ships that cruise on land" and now deploys at the leading edge of the Fatui's conquests across Nod-Krai and Snezhnaya. In combat it regulates its own heat: Pyro damage builds heat, and a Cryo Ward cools it.

## entry: Lord of the Hidden Depths
@keys: Lord of the Hidden Depths, Whisperer of Nightmares, Faceless Boundless Overlord, Rift of Hidden Depths
@version: 6.3
### STANDARD
The Lord of the Hidden Depths: Whisperer of Nightmares is an Abyssal creature that creeps through the rift of the hidden depths near Ashveil Peak and quietly watches the world. Its faceless form is said to be merely a projection; the true body is a formless mass of pure Abyssal energy. It leads the swarm of monsters that came through the Rift of Hidden Depths, and fights with Electro, summoning Fishers of Hidden Depths.
### LARGE +
The Archive notes that invaders from outside the world must obey its rules to a degree and can do little more than linger at its fringes, whispering madness, unless the heavens split and the earth's defences fall; it asks how long such order can last. The in-game blurb says the guardian shadows that once held the frontier have vanished and a miasma is about to encroach beneath an ever-withering throne. The Lord defends itself with the Deepdark Shield, as the Fishers do.

## entry: Radiant Moongecko
@keys: Radiant Moongecko, Moonseeker Drake, Afterglow Cavern
@version: 6.4
### STANDARD
The Radiant Moongecko is a Geo radiant beast, an ancient monster drawn to moonlight that lives deep in layers of rock, found in Afterglow Cavern in Wavechaser Plain. The Handbook says that though the memory of true moonlight has faded, instinct remains: on full-moon nights it gnaws rock and soil suffused with lunar radiance to preserve its bloodline. The in-game blurb calls the geckos fiercely territorial and unwilling to share the limited moonlight that reaches their caves.
### LARGE +
The Archive adds that accumulating kuuvahki has crystallised its body into segments as precise as machine parts, which can harden into stone spikes. Some scholars think its ancestors once lived on the surface and were driven underground by sweeping changes in the world over the ages.

## entry: Precursor's Secret Source Matrix of Overseer Network
@keys: Precursor's Secret Source Matrix, Secret Source Matrix, Mushteshir, Kalag Guarding Grounds, Ur-Namrasit-Tilimtu-Babbar
@version: 6.7
### STANDARD
An ancient machine stranded in the lunar ruins of the Frost Moon, a Secret Source Automaton that attacks anything stepping onto the ground it guards. Its Local Legend version, Mushteshir, "The Pioneer That Gazes at the Stars", waits in Kalag Guarding Grounds after two combat challenges. The Archive says that in the records of a lost ancient civilisation these machines were called "Ur-Namrasit-Tilimtu-Babbar", "the one who brings light to the darkness"; the pioneers' leaders engraved earnest words in golden sigils, hoping their successors would keep the light shining with the machines' help, but the machine named "overseer" never met the one it was meant to serve.
### LARGE +
It uses Archaeolune energy to raise a Convergence Field, gaining a Hydro aura and resistance until frozen and shattered. A terminal in Kalag Guarding Grounds names a Security Chief Kalag and suspends two researchers after the Stellar Blackbody is reported to the seven Kings; the machine's place in that story is not explained.
