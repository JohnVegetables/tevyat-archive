## summary
### LITE
Prototype Cal. Breguet is a large machine from the Fontaine Research Institute of Kinetic Energy Engineering, listed in the Archive as the Experimental Field Generator. It was meant to counteract gravity, but it went out of control after an accident. It now sits in the ruined Academic Assembly Hall as a Geo-attacking boss.
### STANDARD +
It is catalogued in the Clockwork Meka family of automatons and first appeared in Version 4.1.

## nature
### LITE
The Adventurer Handbook calls it a product of the Institute that has gone out of control because of an accident. The Archive describes it as an exquisite mechanical construct that once embodied an engineer's lofty ideals.
### STANDARD +
The Archive says it was designed by the late Edwin Eastinghouse to "counteract" gravity. It was the first step in a plan to turn Fontaine into a city in the sky, so people would never again fear the waves that once swept the land; even if all seven nations were swallowed by the deeps, the Court of Fontaine would float above them, like Celestia. The secrets of the universe would no longer seem so distant.
### LARGE +
The Arkhium in the device's core has "splitting" or "precipitation" properties, separating matter, or certain things outside matter. The Archive says the process can reportedly be reversed, but the Institute was interested only in its physical effects. The Gravity Field entry adds that the machine waits quietly in the centre of the ruined hall for the gravity field to act again, a moment when it will no longer be seen as a mere memento of disgrace.

## abilities
### LITE
Physical attacks: sweeping laser beams from two rotating emitters, carpet bombing from the air followed by a slam, and homing missiles.
### STANDARD +
It teleports to the arena centre and covers it in a Gravity Reduction Field that greatly raises how high characters jump. While it lasts the machine uses Geo shockwaves that can be jumped over. Landing three Pneuma-aligned hits interrupts the field.
### LARGE +
Under the field it also detonates explosions that throw rocks at the player, raises rocks from marked ground, and charges a large explosion. Its drop is the "Tourbillon Device", and it has no weak point.

### ULTRA +
The machine also appears in the Stygian Onslaught challenge mode, where the Gravity Ripple attack is boosted by stacks that build over time and are reduced by plunge attacks. This is a gameplay variant, not story. The wiki's trivia guesses its name honours a real-world horologist; the game does not confirm that.

## status
### STANDARD
As of 7.1 it remains a boss in the Institute's ruined hall. Canon gives no further story for it.

### STANDARD
Prototype Cal. Breguet, called the Experimental Field Generator in the Archive, is a Clockwork Meka boss in the ruined Academic Assembly Hall of the Fontaine Research Institute of Kinetic Energy Engineering. Edwin Eastinghouse designed it to cancel gravity so that Fontaine could one day float above any flood. An accident sent it out of control. It fights with lasers, bombs and missiles, and with Geo attacks inside a field that lowers gravity.
