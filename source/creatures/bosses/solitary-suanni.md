## summary
### LITE
The Solitary Suanni is an illuminated beast of Chenyu Vale, Liyue, called the Ancient Lord of the Mountains. Its in-game text says it is an elegant, proud creature that lives apart from the material world in mountains shrouded in adeptal energy. As a boss it wields Anemo and Hydro against intruders on the Southern Mountain, at Chizhang Wall.
### STANDARD +
Suanni are more often treated as symbols of good fortune than as monsters, and the Handbook says they are oft thought to exist only in fantasy. The Mist-Cloaked Paths description says it still drives away outsiders from fog-hidden mountains with wild mists and furious waves.
### LARGE +
The wiki's Lingyuan page says the suanni Lingyuan takes on the form of the Solitary Suanni. This entry treats the boss as a suanni of the Mountain Lord kind and leaves the identification open.

## nature
### LITE
A beast of graceful form and lonesome, proud nature, in the Mystical Beasts family and attuned to Anemo and Hydro.
### STANDARD +
The Archive says suanni live in mountains shrouded in adeptal energy and have been admired for generations for their preference for living aloof from the material world. They are regarded as a sign of good fortune.
### LARGE +
Its title and the Mist-Cloaked Paths text describe a creature that once roamed freely among wild mountains and looked down on other life, now concealed in fog and defending its territory from all outsiders.

## lore
### STANDARD
In Chenyu Vale legends, the leader of the suanni was called the Mountain Lord and was ranked with the adepti. The Archive records that the well-known Wushou Dance of Chenyu Vale came from the movements of suanni. Since only a handful of people have seen one, the dance costume head has changed over thousands of years of artistic interpretation into its present style.
### LARGE +
The Archive also reports a theory that the Xuanwen Beasts that roam the mountains today are suanni descendants whose blood thinned over the years until they devolved into violent creatures. It notes that this contradicts another theory of the Xuanwen Beasts' origin, though the few records of suanni often show them attended by many Xuanwen Beasts. The Xuanwen Beast text itself says folklore holds they were servants of an adeptus, tasked with defeating evil and protecting the region, who devolved after the adeptus died; the legends cannot be verified and the adeptus is unnamed.
### ULTRA +
Xuanwen Beasts are named for the dark patterns on their bodies, and over a thousand years they have lost much of their territory to human encroachment. They use Anemo and Hydro like the Solitary Suanni. The wiki's Lingyuan page describes Lingyuan as a suanni who presides over Chenyu Vale, was friend to the adepti Fujin and the Herblord, and never grew close to humanity, preferring life as a wild beast in the mountains.

## abilities
### STANDARD
Game mechanics (labelled as such): the boss uses Anemo and Hydro. Its attacks include a tail sweep followed by a plunge that sends out a wave, water blades from backflips, a vortex and splash, a barrage of homing scales, and a stomp. It lunges while imbued with Anemo and Hydro.
### LARGE +
Two charged attacks are used when its health falls to set thresholds. In Spirittide Deluge it gathers adeptal energy while many water splashes burst around it, and in Spiritwind Storm it spawns Spiritwind Pearls that orbit it and become tornadoes that pull players in. While charging it gains great resistance. It can be Frozen and then shattered, melted, superconducted, swirled or crystallised to paralyse it, and destroying all three pearls also paralyses it. It drops the Cloudseam Scale and Anemo and Hydro ascension gems.

## individuals
### STANDARD
The Solitary Suanni is found at Chizhang Wall on the Southern Mountain of Chenyu Vale. It is a single named boss, not a group.
### LARGE +
The wiki's Lingyuan page places Lingyuan, who takes on the Solitary Suanni's appearance, near Chizhang Wall and Mt. Lingmeng after the Chenyu Vale world quests. Gaming's suanni companion, Man Chai, is a separate individual.

## relations
### STANDARD
Suanni are one of the illuminated beast kinds of Liyue, alongside qilin, cranes and deer. Local legends rank the Mountain Lord with the adepti. Xuanwen Beasts live in the same mountains and may be linked to them.
### LARGE +
Lingyuan, Fujin and the Herblord served the mistress of Chenyu Vale in the Archon War, according to the wiki's Lingyuan page; Fujin and the Herblord defected from her, and Lingyuan helped herd the people of Chenyu Vale to safety. The boss entry itself does not mention any of this.

## chronology
### ULTRA
- 4.4: the Solitary Suanni and the Xuanwen Beast are added with Chenyu Vale.

## status
### STANDARD
As of 7.1, the Solitary Suanni remains a boss in Chenyu Vale. Whether it is Lingyuan or another suanni is not stated by the boss's own texts.

## entry: Xuanwen Beasts
@keys: Xuanwen Beast, Xuanwen Beasts
@version: 4.4
### STANDARD
Xuanwen Beasts, named for the dark patterns on their bodies, are fierce Anemo and Hydro creatures of the mountains of Chenyu Vale. Folklore says they were servants of an adeptus and devolved after the adeptus died; the Archive also reports a theory that they are the thinned descendants of suanni. Neither can be verified.
### LARGE +
For over a thousand years human settlement has taken much of their old territory, and they are now seen only in remote mountains.
