## summary
### LITE
The Icewind Suite is a ballet-like boss fight in Fontaine: two clockwork meka dancers, Coppelia and Coppelius, built as Artificed Dancers by the Fontaine Research Institute. They wait on a garden dance floor in the Court of Fontaine Region and fight only when someone speaks to the engineer Maillardet and asks for a performance. Coppelia fights with Anemo and Coppelius with Cryo.
### STANDARD +
The Adventurer Handbook calls the Suite "a gift from the Fontaine Research Institute", composed of two pieces: Dirge of Coppelia and Nemesis of Coppelius. The dialogue choices made with Maillardet decide which one is performed and which Ley Line Blossom reward is earned.

## nature
### LITE
Clockwork meka, not living creatures: two artificed dancers shaped like a ballerina and her partner, built to perform. They belong to the Clockwork Meka group of Automatons. Their performance is described as an eternal waltz.
### STANDARD +
The Archive tells their history in two steps. Bossuet originally intended the Artificed Dancers to be works of art that transcended the era of mechanical reproduction. After Bossuet was forced to resign, Maillardet took the project over with his own understanding of what he wanted to achieve. Their features were perfected during development, but they gradually strayed from Bossuet's vision.

The in-game ballet is said by the wiki's trivia to be Bossuet's, with Coppelia and Coppelius modelled on the roles of Claire and Lothair. The names also match the ballet Coppelia, in which a mechanical doll is mistaken for a real woman.
### LARGE +
Fontaine's historical Coppelius was a playwright who lived about 500 years ago; his last opera, "Clockwork Coppelia", was never published because the opera house was destroyed on the day it premiered. The boss texts do not connect him to the dancers; see uncertainties.
### ULTRA +
The Suite is the first Normal Boss that does not attack on sight: the fight starts only when Maillardet is spoken to. It is also the only Normal Boss with an in-world reason for "respawning". If a player asks for a rematch too soon, Maillardet says the pair are undergoing repairs.

## abilities
### LITE
Skating, spinning and fan-and-cape attacks. Coppelius makes trails of ice and fires icicles from his cane; Coppelia makes pulses and orbs of wind. They can fight together or separately.
### STANDARD +
Every few tens of seconds, or at a health threshold, they move to the centre of the dance floor for a "Climax". In Dirge of Coppelia, Coppelia exposes a clockwork component charged with ousia and summons whirlwinds of dirge that turn Cryo if struck by Coppelius's ice; the tutorial says using pneuma-imbued attacks reduces her output and overloads her. In Nemesis of Coppelius, Coppelius exposes a pneuma-charged component, gains a shield and creates three retributive Frosthearts; ousia-imbued attacks break the shield and overload him.
### LARGE +
Both versions use the same two dancers, each with a different set of attacks, and each drops a different Artificed Spare Clockwork Component. Defeated together, Coppelia falls into Coppelius's arms; defeated apart, she drops to the ground. Ability names and damage figures are game mechanics.

## relations
### STANDARD
Created under the Fontaine Research Institute; Bossuet started the project, and Maillardet, listed on the wiki as a Clockwork Meka Engineer of the Institute, took the project over and is the NPC who starts the challenge. The place is the garden dance floor on the north side of Erinnyes in the Court of Fontaine Region.

## status
### LITE
In operation as of 7.1. I found no mention of the Icewind Suite in 6.x (Nod-Krai) or 7.0-7.1 (Snezhnaya) story content. The wiki lists Anemo and Cryo as the Suite's elements.

The ousia version of the fight, led by Coppelia. Her Climax summons whirlwinds of dirge; the tutorial advises pneuma-imbued attacks to overload her. Its reward leans toward Vayuda Turquoise and the Artificed Spare Clockwork Component, Coppelia.

@keys: Nemesis of Coppelius
@version: 4.0
### STANDARD
The pneuma version of the fight, led by Coppelius. His Climax gives him a Cryo shield and three retributive Frosthearts; ousia-imbued attacks break the shield. Its reward leans toward Shivada Jade and the Artificed Spare Clockwork Component, Coppelius.
