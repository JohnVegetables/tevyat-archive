## summary
### LITE
The Emperor of Fire and Iron is a colossal Armored Crab that lives in a cavern beneath a lake in Fontaine's Belleau Region. It is an ancient ruler of the Fontemer Aberrants, the strange sea creatures of Fontaine, and wears twin horns that burn with Pyro. It does not hunt travellers; it is an enormous, armoured creature that fights when disturbed. Sailors tell tales about it.
### STANDARD +
The in-game Archive calls it one of two sovereigns who once stood at the apex of the aberrant species, and its event panel titles it the "Lone Suzerain of Land and Sea". It is a repeatable world boss, and the crab's size, age and armour are the point of the entry: a creature that simply never stopped growing.

## nature
### LITE
An Armored Crab (the small shell-borrowing crabs found across Fontaine) that grew far beyond its kin. The Adventurer Handbook calls it an ancient ruler of the Fontemer Aberrants, said to have witnessed Fontaine's seas before the first diluvian period. The tutorial calls it an Armored Crablord that has outlived and outgrown almost every other aberrant.
### STANDARD +
Ordinary Armored Crabs are gentle: they spend their lives looking for shells that fit as they grow, and the Archive says they show little of the aggression of wild animals because their territory is only the small home on their backs. The Archive explains the Emperor as the end of that story. A crab that avoids predation and keeps hunting and eating keeps growing and seeking new shells, until it became simply too gargantuan. Its armour is its nature rather than a borrowed shell.

Its title in the world is half-joking and half in awe. The Archive says that is how people speak of it, and imagines a Teyvat emptied of humanity in which no one would be left to call it "Emperor of Fire and Iron" at all.
### LARGE +
The twin molten horns on its body are described as Pyro-aligned organs that allow more vicious elemental attacks and stronger defence. Destroying the Pyro in them (with elemental reactions) leaves the Emperor staggered for a time, and after a while it digs into the ground and comes back with a fresh pair. Fontemer Aberrants in general deal mostly Physical and Hydro damage, so its Pyro is unusual among them.
### ULTRA +
As a game feature it is a Normal Boss in the Mystical Beasts family and the Fontemer Aberrant group. Its event panel text ("In the depths of the cavern beneath the roiling waters of the lake, there dwells a blazing Emperor clad in armor") frame the fight as an audience with a ruler "in place of its dull courtiers", whom players are told to calm rather than slay.

## abilities
### LITE
Heavy claw strikes, charges and leaping slams (Physical), and Pyro attacks from the horns: volleys of fireballs that scorch the ground, trails of explosions and a large explosion at the arena centre. While its horns are intact it uses its strongest attacks.
### STANDARD +
Breaking the horns with elemental reactions paralyses it for a period. After they are destroyed it regenerates them by burrowing and resurfacing. The horns are low and exposed to melee during certain big attacks (for example the leaping slam and the central explosion).
### LARGE +
In the harder challenge modes it summons Sternshield Crabs to assist; these are adult Fontemer Aberrants whose Pyro aura makes them lose control when depleted, slamming into the Emperor. All attack figures in game are mechanics, not lore.

## relations
### STANDARD
Among the aberrants it belongs to the same group as the Armored Crab and the Sternshield Crab. The Archive records one human encounter: before the first diluvian period, a massive Armored Crab attacked a ship and was driven back into the depths by Basil Elton, who had not yet been reinstated as a naval commander. The text says "a massive Armored Crab", so whether that was this very creature is not stated.
### LARGE +
The Palais Mermonia is invoked only in the Archive's thought experiment; no source read ties the Emperor to any other named place beyond its cavern in the Belleau Region.

## status
### LITE
Alive and unchanged as of 7.1: it remains in its cavern as a repeatable boss. I found no mention of it in 6.x (Nod-Krai) or 7.0-7.1 (Snezhnaya) story content.
### STANDARD +
A different version, the Tyrant of Fire and Iron ("Indomitable Lord"), appeared only in the limited-time event On the Trail of Behemoths (5.3, January 2025). It is a variant for that event, not a separate canonical creature.

## entry: Tyrant of Fire and Iron
@keys: Tyrant of Fire and Iron
@version: 5.3
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
The Tyrant of Fire and Iron, titled "Indomitable Lord", is an event-exclusive variant of the Emperor of Fire and Iron met in the 5.3 limited-time challenge event On the Trail of Behemoths (14-27 January 2025). It reuses the Emperor's body and Pyro horns with event-specific mechanics, and the event's challenges were run through an NPC named Helka. Treat it as a gameplay variant, not as a second giant crab in the world.

## entry: Armored Crablord
@keys: Armored Crablord, Armored Crab, Sternshield Crab
@keys2: Fontaine, Emperor of Fire and Iron
### STANDARD
Armored Crabs are shell-borrowing crabs found throughout Fontaine; the Archive notes a popular Fontaine storybook about one crab's search for a shell that fit, which touched adults deeply. Sternshield Crabs are the adult form that carry a Pyro aura on land. The Emperor is described as an Armored Crablord that has outlived and surpassed almost all other Fontemer Aberrants.
