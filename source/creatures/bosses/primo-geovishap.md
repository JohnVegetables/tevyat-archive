## summary
### LITE
The Primo Geovishap is the largest and most ancient form of Geovishap, a dragon-like vishap of Liyue. It is met as a world boss in the cavern ruin of Tianqiu Valley, where it rises from long slumber. It is a Geo creature that always carries one extra element, shown in the colour of its scales: Pyro, Hydro, Cryo or Electro. The Adventurer Handbook calls it awesomely powerful.
### STANDARD +
Local folktales tie it to the great draconic calamity that ruined Tianqiu Valley long ago. It is a remnant of the age when the Geo dragon ruled the mountains, not a creature of the present Liyue.
### LARGE +
It is one of the vishap line, the same dragon-kin family as the common Geovishaps, and the game treats it as the top of that family among normal bosses.

## nature
### LITE
A massive, scaled, lizard-like vishap of Geo, belonging to the Mystical Beasts. After many years it has grown accustomed to changes in its elemental surroundings, which is why it carries a second element drawn from the world around it.
### STANDARD +
Its epithets are given in game as the Geo dragon who once raged with the king and the long-slumbering earthshaker dragon. It burrows through rock and appears from beneath the ground, and it is always found with one extra element in its scales.
### LARGE +
The description of the Revived form says that even in its long dreams it kept drawing energy from the ley lines, waiting for the day it could rise to meet the sun and unleash its long-festering fury. This is flavour text for an event form and shows how Liyue's writers picture the species, not a record of what the wild boss does.

## lore
### STANDARD
Both in-game texts say folktales hold that after the great draconic calamity that ruined Tianqiu Valley, Geovishaps and Primo Geovishaps dug deep into the earth to await a chance to rise again.
### LARGE +
The Archive text adds that the overlord of the Geovishaps and Primo Geovishaps was imprisoned deep under the earth, and that this is why they burrowed into deep, unseen places. The overlord is the Geo dragon Azhdaha. In the world quest No Restoring This Past Land of Beauty, Zhiping of the Adventurers' Guild reads from old texts that Tianqiu Valley was a prosperous settlement before the dragon king's rampage, that Rex Lapis and his adepti imprisoned him, and that his servants fled underground to sleep until he returned. Azhdaha's own story is told in his entry; this entry keeps only what concerns his servants.
### ULTRA +
Zhiping notes this tradition as old text, not a witnessed event. The quest links the valley's ruin to the dragon king only through legend, and no source states when the Primo Geovishap woke or whether it woke on its own.

## abilities
### STANDARD
Game mechanics (labelled as such): the boss is always idle in one of four infusions at the start, Pyro, Hydro, Cryo or Electro, and its Geo attacks are joined by attacks of that element. It claws and spins, burrows toward distant targets, and scatters elemental stones that burst after a short delay. It breathes a cone of its infused element and unleashes a large burst of elemental power. If that burst is absorbed by a shield, it rebounds and wounds the boss. At low health it replaces the burst with a barrage of elemental stones. It drops Juvenile Jade and ascension gems that depend on its infusion.
### LARGE +
Ordinary Geovishaps only convert their rolling and jump attacks to one element at a time, while the Primo Geovishap is infused from the start of the fight and gains extra resistance to its infused element. Those details are mechanics, not lore.

## individuals
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
The open-world Primo Geovishap is in the cavern ruin of Tianqiu Valley in Minlin, Liyue. The valley holds the ruins of the old settlement, three ruined towers on the western side and the cavern the boss sleeps in.
### LARGE +
Two event-only forms exist. The Revived Primo Geovishap, Fury of the Cliffs, appeared in the Legend of the Vagabond Sword event (1.6) as a showdown enemy.
The Wintry Primo Geovishap appeared in the Specially-Shaped Saurian Search event (4.6).

## relations
### STANDARD
Ordinary Geovishaps live in Liyue's mountains and are kin of the Primo Geovishap, with Azhdaha as their lord in the folktales. Humans of Liyue treat them as dangerous beasts, and Adventurers' Guild reports ask for investigation when they stir.
### LARGE +
In No Restoring This Past Land of Beauty, Zhiping asks the Traveler to investigate earthquakes and monster activity in Tianqiu Valley and Nantianmen and to look into the cavern; the Traveler defeats the Primo Geovishap there. Zhiping's later account of the valley's history is the quest's explanation for it. Local Legend Geovishaps such as Yiji and Tianyu at Chizhang Wall are separate creatures.

## status
### STANDARD
As of 7.1, the Primo Geovishap remains a world boss in Tianqiu Valley. No later canon changes its nature or explains its origin further.

## entry: Tianqiu Valley's Dragon King Legend
@keys: Tianqiu Valley, draconic calamity, dragon king
@keys2: Primo Geovishap, Geovishap
@version: 1.3
### STANDARD
Folktale of Liyue: Tianqiu Valley was a thriving settlement until a dragon king's rampage ruined it. Rex Lapis and his adepti imprisoned him, and his servants, the Geovishaps and Primo Geovishaps, fled underground to sleep until he returned. The Handbook gives a shorter version without naming the dragon king. This is Zhiping's account in a world quest, drawn from old texts.
### LARGE +
The Primo Geovishap in the cavern ruin is the survivor most often tied to this tale. The quest never confirms that it is the same creature as the legend's servants, only that such beasts lie in the valley.
