## summary
### LITE
The Aeonblight Drake is a huge dragon-shaped combat machine, one of the Ruin Machines, found in Sumeru in the Ardravi Valley area of Devantaka Mountain. Its boss title is Perpetual Motion GL-Faf. It is a normal boss that fights on the ground and in the air, and it can take on and use the element that has harmed it most.
### STANDARD +
Its Adventurer Handbook entry calls it a being that inspires fear, as the lord who once ruled these lands did, and proof that a now-destroyed realm reached heights mortals should never have reached.
### LARGE +
It belongs to the Ruin Drake group, which also includes the lesser Ruin Drake: Earthguard and Ruin Drake: Skywatch. Those two occupied the Aeonblight Drake's arena before it was added.

## nature
### LITE
A tireless machine built in the shape of a dragon, with thrusters, a tail, a head it can strike with, and an element-storing core in its neck.
### STANDARD +
It is an automaton of the Ruin Drake group. The group is described as modelled on vishaps, the ancient creatures able to absorb or counteract elemental power, with structural changes and an unusual energy system that strengthen that ability. The group page says the colour of the Chaos Storage in the neck reflects the element currently absorbed.
### LARGE +
Its Archive text says the destroyed realm sought forbidden knowledge and tried to build perpetual-motion machines that could match or surpass primordial lifeforms. The Handbook calls the Drake a mysterious, tireless mechanical monstrosity.

## lore
@version: 3.1
### STANDARD
The Handbook for the Ruin Drake group calls the machines war machines said to have been left by a now-destroyed nation, Khaenri'ah, that imitate the vishaps' ability to counteract elements. The Aeonblight Drake's own Archive entry speaks of that realm's attempts at perpetual motion.
### LARGE +
The boss description card, titled Perpetual Motion Dragon, speaks of a machine drake that once left deep scars across the earth and begins to stir after sleeping for many long years.
### ULTRA +
Inactive Aeonblight Drakes can be found around the Furnace of Fiery Embers in Ochkanatlan (Natlan), per the wiki trivia. They are scenery rather than the Sumeru boss, and they show the machine type is not unique to Sumeru.

## abilities
### STANDARD
Game mechanics (labelled as such): it deals Physical damage and has high Physical resistance. Grounded it uses tail sweeps, thruster smashes, nose strikes and a charge; airborne it uses a machine gun volley, a missile barrage and a charge. Its Energy Surge scatters bombs that land as Physical or as the absorbed element.
### LARGE +
After Energy Surge it absorbs the element that has hurt it most since the last surge, gains resistance to that element (60 percent for the normal boss) and uses it on the next surge; if Physical damage dominated, it stays Physical. It switches between grounded and airborne after the surge. Weak points on the head (during Energy Surge and charges), the chest (during the missile barrage) and, while airborne, the two thrusters can be hit to make it fall and be paralysed, which also clears the absorbed element. Its signature drop is Perpetual Caliber.
### ULTRA +
In Stygian Onslaught the boss gains 200 percent resistance to the absorbed element, and at health thresholds locks its health and raises resistance to the element that hurt it most in the previous cycle. The numbers come from the wiki and are game values rather than lore.

## individuals
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
The Aeonblight Drake of Ardravi Valley is the open-world boss. Its event form, Aeonblight Drake (Heavy Assault Pack), appeared in the limited-time event Feast of the Departed Warriors.
### LARGE +
The event version has a permanent attack bonus and an extra chest-weak-point attack, according to the wiki; it is a different encounter, not a second creature in the story.

## relations
### STANDARD
It is a Ruin Machine boss alongside the Ruin Serpent and the Perpetual Mechanical Array. It is not part of King Deshret's machinery: the Primal Constructs of the desert are a separate line of automata.
### LARGE +
Its resemblance to a vishap links it in theme to dragons and to the ancient lifeforms the Archive says its designers tried to equal.

## chronology
### ULTRA
- 3.1: the Aeonblight Drake is added to Sumeru; the wiki trivia notes that its arena was previously held by a Ruin Drake: Earthguard and a Ruin Drake: Skywatch.
- 3.7: the Heavy Assault Pack variant appears in the event Feast of the Departed Warriors (version per the event page's change history).

## status
### STANDARD
As of 7.1, the Aeonblight Drake remains a boss in Sumeru. Its builders' purpose and the identity of the lord the Handbook mentions are not explained.

## entry: Ruin Drake
@keys: Ruin Drake, Ruin Drakes
@version: 3.1
### STANDARD
Ruin Drakes are Ruin Machines modelled on vishaps, found in Sumeru and Fontaine. They use Physical attacks, have high Physical resistance and adapt to elements through their Energy Surge attack. The Aeonblight Drake is the boss of the group.
### LARGE +
The Handbook says the machines mimic vishaps, which can absorb or counteract elements, and were given structural and energy-system changes to improve this.

## entry: Perpetual Caliber
@keys: Perpetual Caliber
@version: 3.1
### STANDARD
Perpetual Caliber is the exclusive drop of the Aeonblight Drake.
