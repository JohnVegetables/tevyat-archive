## summary
### LITE
The Hydro Tulpa is a human-shaped monster formed from the agglomeration of countless drops of water. It stands as a world boss in Fontaine's Beryl Region and fights with water-formed fists, spears, hammers and swords. During the fight it keeps producing smaller Half-Tulpas and absorbs them to grow stronger. Its event panel is titled "Awakening Water".
### STANDARD +
The panel text reads: "A new life has awakened as the world turns, near where many destinies intersect." It is the 4.2 world boss of Fontaine. A quest boss with the same form, Narzissenkreuz, is a separate matter described below.

## nature
### LITE
A humanoid monster made of water. The Adventurer Handbook describes it as formed from the agglomeration of countless drops. It belongs to the Elemental Lifeform family (not the Fontemer Aberrants) and its title is "Awakened Egregore".
### STANDARD +
Before the quest Rowboat's Wake, its Archive entry only repeats the description above. After the quest, the entry adds a reflection: countless wills dissolved in the water, which, like the universe, was full of souls wishing to be born. The text asks whether newborn Hydro Tulpas come from "the former or the latter" and answers that they are not so different. The sun, time, the surging tides and even the pervasive darkness cannot tell them apart. The entry never states a mechanism.
### LARGE +
The name comes from the idea of a tulpa (a being materialised by concentrated thought) and its title "Egregore" from the concept of a thought-form arising from a group's collective thoughts and emotions, per the wiki's etymology notes. These are naming notes, not canon statements about how it works.

### ULTRA +
Its event panel says the new life awakened near where many destinies intersect. The panel does not name those destinies. Its Adventurer Handbook entry stays brief throughout, and the philosophical text appears only in the Archive after Rowboat's Wake.

## abilities
### LITE
Water-formed fist, spear, hammer and sword attacks, a barrage of lances falling across the arena, and the ability to absorb a Half-Tulpa into itself to become stronger. Everything is Hydro.
### STANDARD +
A Half-Tulpa spawns roughly every twelve seconds in the standard fight; each carries a Hydro shield and breaks when the shield does. The Hydro Tulpa periodically absorbs one to enter an enhanced state in which its reach and damage grow and it resists more. The tutorial advises players to disrupt the absorbing with attacks or elemental reactions.
### LARGE +
The harder game mode adds delayed ring bursts to some attacks, a larger Half-Tulpa summon and a charged finishing attack. Ability names on the wiki are unofficial, and all numbers are game mechanics, not canon.

## relations
### STANDARD
The Half-Tulpa is an enemy summoned by the Hydro Tulpa. The Archive links the boss with the Narzissenkreuz quest arc via its post-Rowboat's Wake text.
### LARGE +
Narzissenkreuz, leader of the Narzissenkreuz Ordo and known as the Master, appears as a quest-exclusive boss in Waking from the Great Dream (4.2, part of In the Wake of Narcissus). That boss has the same body and attacks as the world boss, with an extra phase in which he rains lances and can only be interrupted using the Sword of Narzissenkreuz. According to the wiki he disappeared in Rowboat's Wake. He has his own entity; this file covers the open-world creature only.

## status
### LITE
Still in the Beryl Region as a repeatable boss as of 7.1. I found no mention of it in 6.x (Nod-Krai) or 7.0-7.1 (Snezhnaya) story content.
### STANDARD +
Hydro Tulpa: Flashflood ("Manifest Will of the Waters") is an event-exclusive variant from the 5.3 limited-time event On the Trail of Behemoths (14-27 January 2025).

## entry: Half-Tulpa
@keys: Half-Tulpa, Half-Tulpas
@version: 4.2
### STANDARD
Half-Tulpas are smaller water-formed enemies summoned by the Hydro Tulpa during its fight. They are Hydro-immune, hit with an arm slam and homing bubbles, and have a Hydro shield; breaking the shield defeats them. The Hydro Tulpa absorbs them to power itself up.

## entry: Hydro Tulpa: Flashflood
@keys: Hydro Tulpa: Flashflood, Manifest Will of the Waters
@version: 5.3
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
An event-exclusive version of the Hydro Tulpa met in On the Trail of Behemoths (5.3, January 2025), with extra delayed bursts added to the standard attacks. It is a gameplay variant for the event, not a distinct creature in the story.
