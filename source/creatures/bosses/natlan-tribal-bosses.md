## summary
### LITE
Natlan has seven normal bosses, arena fights in the nation of Pyro. Two are saurian beasts: Kongamato, an ancient Yumkasaurus the Scions of the Canopy revere, and Holawaqa Ngoubou, a mutated Qucusaurus. Two are ancient machines from the Secret Source ruins: the Configuration Device and the Overseer Device. The others are the Abyssal Tenebrous Papilla, the hollow Lava Dragon Statue, and the Wayward Hermetic Spiritspeaker, a reclusive priestess of the Masters of the Night-Wind.
### STANDARD +
The Archive lists Kongamato as the "Gluttonous Yumkasaur Mountain King" and Holawaqa Ngoubou as the "Goldflame Qucusaur Tyrant". Their stories draw on the Abyss, the saurian past and the vanished civilisation of the Secret Source.
### LARGE +
Only Kongamato has a story of any length in quests; the rest are described almost entirely in Adventurer Handbook and Archive text.

## nature
### LITE
Each boss is a distinct kind of threat: living saurians, ancient machines, an Abyssal plant, a stone-hearted lava construct and a human priestess.
### STANDARD +
Grouped by origin:
- Saurians (Mystical Beasts, Natlan Saurians): Kongamato (Dendro and Pyro) and Holawaqa Ngoubou (Pyro).
- Secret Source Automatons: the Configuration Device (Electro) and the Overseer Device (Hydro).
- The Abyss: Tenebrous Papilla, one of the Tenebrous Mimesis.
- Avatars of Lava: the Lava Dragon Statue (Physical and Pyro).
- Humans: the Wayward Hermetic Spiritspeaker (Cryo), of the Masters of the Night-Wind.
### LARGE +
Game mechanics (labelled as such): several fights are built around Nightsoul. The Secret Source automatons build up countermeasures when characters enter Nightsoul's Blessing, Papilla's Void Ward breaks three times faster under Nightsoul attacks, and the Spiritspeaker's illusions can be frozen in place by scanning them with Spiritspeaking (an Iktomisaurus or a Masters of the Night-Wind character).

## individuals
### LITE
- Kongamato (Tequemecan Valley): ancient Yumkasaur king, Archived as the Gluttonous Yumkasaur Mountain King.
- Holawaqa Ngoubou (Huitztli Hill): golden-flame Qucusaur, Archived as the Goldflame Qucusaur Tyrant.
- Secret Source Automaton: Configuration Device (Toyac Springs).
- Tenebrous Papilla (Quahuacan Cliff).
- Wayward Hermetic Spiritspeaker (Tezcatepetonco Range).
- Lava Dragon Statue (Atocpan).
- Secret Source Automaton: Overseer Device (Atocpan).
### STANDARD +
Arrivals by version: Kongamato and Holawaqa Ngoubou 5.0, Configuration Device 5.1, Tenebrous Papilla 5.2, Wayward Hermetic Spiritspeaker 5.3, Lava Dragon Statue 5.5, Overseer Device 5.6.

## kongamato
@version: 5.0
### LARGE
Kongamato is an enormous Yumkasaurus who has lived since the ancient cataclysm and, per the Handbook, owes his huge size to rampant gluttony. The Archive says this Mountain King once joined the great heroes against the Black Tide and holds a special place for the Scions of the Canopy. Some tribespeople think he is big because of a special physique, like the Qucusaurus Tyrant, but the Archive says it is simply age and overeating. His in-game title is "Lord of the Mountain": an ancient king slumbering beneath the canyon, awaiting young warriors of the tribe. He fights with Dendro and with Pyro flamegranates he devours and spits.
### ULTRA +
His story is told in the Tribal Chronicle Yupanqui's Turnfire (as summarised by the wiki, not traced to dialogue). In the cataclysm he and his human companion, Burkina, fought the Abyss; its energies infested him, and in a fit of rage he killed Burkina, who passed some of his power and blood to him. Ashamed, Kongamato wished to be put out of his misery, though the Abyssal energy kept him alive well beyond a normal Yumkasaur's life. The Scions of the Canopy keep him in a cavern and hold a Turnfire Ritual to drain some of his energy so it cannot overwhelm him.

When the Traveler arrives, the energy is building faster than usual. Trinidad asks the Traveler to stand in for Kinich at the ritual. Kinich proposes a different answer: a Beastly Rift to hold Kongamato safely. Wayna eventually relents, but Trinidad, believing Kongamato will be killed, breaks Kinich's device and wakes him; Kongamato hurls a fireball at Trinidad, burning him badly. After a short fight Kongamato regains some sanity, smashes into the Turnfire brazier and begs to die to end the tribe's humiliation. Kinich refuses, telling him he must live with his shame. Engulfed in Turnfire, Kongamato resonates with Kinich, calls him an heir to the Turnfire, and vows to use his "ruined" vessel to train future heroes until the Abyss is gone from Natlan. A strange shell later forms around him, which the elders judge a scab that stops Abyssal energy leaking out and being absorbed; he still rouses if his territory is disturbed, but fights less aggressively. Before the descent to the Night Kingdom, a projection of Kongamato appears with Kinich and says he saw Burkina in dreams, who believed the coming battle would end all battles and urged him to support the Traveler, whose presence resembles the one who moved Burkina to their side.

## holawaqa_ngoubou
@version: 5.0
### LARGE
Holawaqa Ngoubou is a mutated Qucusaurus with the supernatural form and golden flames of an old bloodline, titled the "Feather of Proclamation" in game: a tyrant "cursed by ancient bloodlines" in a mist-shrouded valley of firewood. The Archive says his body took a form modern Saurians' bodies cannot control, and that the violent power in his blood pushes his mind toward insanity. He fights with Pyro, wings and, at times, a protective Golden Flame form.
### ULTRA +
Legend says that when young he followed the heroes who saved Natlan. One of them (the wiki links the text to Menilek) agreed to make a pilgrimage to the volcano with him, to pull his mind back from the brink. The Archive adds that his present title, "Tyrant", shows the story did not end happily. No quest tells this tale.

## secret_source_automatons
@version: 5.1
### LARGE
Two bosses belong to the Secret Source Automatons, masterless machines from a time long past, generally idle until disturbed. The Configuration Device (also called the Secret Source Constructor) is an ancient manufacturing machine, shaped like a giant claw and powered by golden patterns, which could transform into many shapes to build cities, even in a volcano's depths; it can also be used as a weapon and fights by its preset instructions. It fights with Electro. The Overseer Device (the Secret Source Overseer, "Ancient Ruins Master Terminal") is a domination mechanism with Hydro attacks, used to oversee a city's operation.
### ULTRA +
The Handbook says the Overseer was used by a mighty ancient race to oversee its cities. The Archive adds that this race built phlogiston-engineered intelligence into the machines, which once controlled the workings of myriad cities; the civilisation fell in wars with an emerging race, and its machines now keep only basic commands. The Event Menu text says its true purpose was to lord it over the Source Mechanisms, and that a stone dragon head's fury still lingers in its lair. The Configuration Device arrived in 5.1 and the Overseer in 5.6.

## tenebrous_papilla
@version: 5.2
### LARGE
Tenebrous Papilla, "Void Fantasy Plant", is an Abyssal, plant-like creature from another world. The Archive says such monsters push deep into the earth, extract memories to imitate and manipulate, and distort them into stranger, darker forms; it adds that they draw closer to Irminsul's core, threatening its existence, and that some say they corrupt the many colours of light. Its Event Menu text calls it a pitch-black monster from an alien world that plunges roots of corruption into the home of souls and memory.
### ULTRA +
Game mechanics (labelled as such): it mimics other normal bosses in three variants, none of which keep their original special mechanics, and it shields itself with a Void Ward that Nightsoul attacks wear down faster. Some attacks inflict Attrition, which steadily drains the active character's HP. Papilla belongs to the Tenebrous Mimesis, which according to the wiki's lore note can copy memories from ley lines into monster, animal or object forms, such as cranes or chests.

## lava_dragon_statue
@version: 5.5
### LARGE
The Lava Dragon Statue, "Stone-Hearted Final Form of Flowing Flame", is a monster that keeps generating heat to sustain itself, with lava at its core. It starts in a Geohuman form and shifts to a Molten Dragon form when it takes enough Pyro. According to the Archive, legend says such constructs came from a branch of research parallel to the Source Mechanisms: an attempt to use the primal flame to create a new human-based race. The Event Menu text describes a creature born in a geothermal fissure in an attempt to restore an ancient race, now only a senseless beast.
### ULTRA +
The Archive says the research failed: once the Heavenly Principles (the "reaver" with a throne from beyond the skies) arrived, the primal flame could only produce hollow shells with no more consciousness than a stone. The wiki groups it with the Avatars of Lava.

## wayward_hermetic_spiritspeaker
@version: 5.3
### LARGE
The Wayward Hermetic Spiritspeaker, titled "Sequestered Priestess", is a priestess of the Masters of the Night-Wind who lives apart from her tribe. Her Spiritspeaking surpasses that of ordinary priests and shamans. She attacks with Cryo, using a staff like a paintbrush, and her fight calls up painted illusions.
### ULTRA +
The Archive says her power came from the protection of the Masters of the Night-Wind's Wayob, both a blessing and a curse, because it was at first beyond her control. She went into isolation to master it, and now lives in seclusion, which has made her a reputed loner. Rumour says she will teach those who seek her, always on her own terms. Her Event Menu text describes a hermit contemplating changing forms and their metaphors in exile. The wiki notes she is the first human Normal Boss.

## relations
### STANDARD
Kongamato is tied to Kinich, Burkina, the Scions of the Canopy and the Turnfire. The Spiritspeaker belongs to the Masters of the Night-Wind. Papilla is part of the Abyss. The Lava Dragon Statue and the two automatons link to the Secret Source and the primal-fire past.
### LARGE +
Papilla can imitate Kongamato, among other bosses. Rewards (game): the bosses drop Natlan boss materials and ascension gems of their element, for example Kongamato's Overripe Flamegranate and Papilla's Ensnaring Gaze.

## status
### STANDARD
As of 7.1 all seven bosses remain open-world challenges in Natlan. No new Natlan boss was added in 6.x or 7.0-7.1 as far as the researched pages show; the Nod-Krai bosses are a separate group.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay interpretation (this project's recommendation, not canon text): treat Kongamato as weary and ashamed rather than villainous, Holawaqa Ngoubou as tormented, the automatons as idle and impersonal, and the Papilla as uncanny and wordless. Do not give the Lava Dragon Statue or Overseer speech or intent, and do not invent a spoken history for the Spiritspeaker beyond the Archive.
