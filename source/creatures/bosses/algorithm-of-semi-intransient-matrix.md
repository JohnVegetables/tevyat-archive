## summary
### LITE
The Algorithm of Semi-Intransient Matrix of Overseer Network is a large Primal Construct, an automaton left by the ancient civilisation of King Deshret. It is a normal boss found at the Dune of Elusion in the Hypostyle Desert of Sumeru, with the boss title Tomb Guard of the Desert King. It fights with lasers and by turning invisible behind deployed parts.
### STANDARD +
The Adventurer Handbook calls it a perpetual-motion device that governs the many ruin machines scattered through the desert and still seems to carry the will of its lonely ruler, King Deshret. Players often shorten its name to ASIMON; the abbreviation appears in official media only once, in a Spanish achievement name added in 4.0.
### LARGE +
It was introduced in 3.1 with the Hypostyle Desert. Its drop is the Light Guiding Tetrahedron.

## nature
### LITE
A perpetual-motion machine from an ancient desert civilisation, working as a governing node over lesser constructs.
### STANDARD +
It belongs to the Primal Constructs, the elite leftover technology of King Deshret's civilisation found across the Great Red Sand. The Primal Construct Handbook text calls such machines guardians of the ruins who punish those who disturb the pure dreams of their lord; their dreams and oaths survive now only as mantras inscribed upon them.
### LARGE +
It is tied to the desert king, not to Khaenri'ah. The Handbook and Archive texts describe no pilot, so it is treated as autonomous.

## lore
@version: 3.1
### STANDARD
The boss's description card, titled Matrix of Overseer Network, speaks of an ancient capital buried beneath the sands, where an autonomous machine was developed and built, and which still remembers the dreams of the fallen King Deshret.
### LARGE +
The Archive says that, perhaps through a friend's teachings in a dream, or perhaps from coveting the power of the holy ones in the skies (Celestia), the king created mysterious devices that should not exist in the world before founding his realm. Amid the ruins of the City of the Pillars the ancient crystals still reflect the glory of the golden paradise, though no one alive remembers the king's dreams.
### ULTRA +
The Archive's 'friend' is linked on the wiki to Nabu Malikata. The Archive itself does not name the friend or say which of its two guesses is true.

## abilities
### STANDARD
Game mechanics (labelled as such): it deals Physical damage. It opens every fight with a Laser Zone, then follows with Repulse, Reshape and Prospect attacks, each matching one of its part types, and with tracking lasers and a missile. Its signature behaviour is an invisible state: it leaves Repulsor, Reshaper and Prospector parts on the field and gains very high resistance while they attack the player.
### LARGE +
Destroying a part forces the Matrix to repair it. Hitting a part with Electro during the repair, or inflicting Quicken on the invisible Matrix directly, ends the state and paralyses it. While repairing, the parts carry Dendro, so Bloom or Burning can be triggered on them. If it stays invisible too long it gathers its parts for an Overclocked Attack, a shockwave over the whole arena.
### ULTRA +
The three part types are Repulsor (chases and strikes), Reshaper (lands and, when two Repulsors are present, a beam forms between them) and Prospector (fires periodic energy beams). Parts have a tenth of the Matrix's health and decay if not damaged for about a minute. These are game values from the wiki, not lore.

## individuals
### STANDARD
The open-world boss is at the Dune of Elusion. The same model is used as a stage boss in some quests, under other names: Field Annihilation Array (The Evincing Shadow) in the world quest Dual Evidence, and Anoushbord (Forgotten Mechanical Guardian) in The Temple Where Sand Flows Like Tears.
### LARGE +
In Dune-Entombed Fecundity: Part III it appears as an ordinary enemy because the main enemy there is Ferigees, Guardian of the Eternal Oasis, who has her own title and health bar. (From wiki trivia.)

## relations
### STANDARD
The Handbook says it governs the many ruin machines scattered through the desert. It belongs to King Deshret's technology, a separate line from the Khaenri'ahn Ruin Machines.
### LARGE +
Its Archive text ties it to the City of the Pillars and to the king's reaching for the power of Celestia.

## chronology
### ULTRA
- 3.1: the boss is added to Sumeru with the Hypostyle Desert.
- 4.0: the abbreviation ASIMON appears in the Spanish text of the achievement Ever So Slightly Inferior, per the wiki trivia.

## status
### STANDARD
As of 7.1, the Algorithm remains in the Hypostyle Desert. Whether it truly preserves the king's will is not established.

## entry: Primal Construct
@keys: Primal Construct, Primal Constructs
@version: 3.1
### STANDARD
Primal Constructs are elite automata left by King Deshret's civilisation, found mostly across the Great Red Sand. They deploy parts and may become invisible; Electro on a part being repaired, or Quicken, Frozen or Petrification on the construct itself, ends the state. The Algorithm is their boss form.
### LARGE +
Like Ruin Machines, some are dormant rather than patrolling. Their inscribed mantras are all that remains of the dreams and oaths of their lord.

## entry: Light Guiding Tetrahedron
@keys: Light Guiding Tetrahedron
@version: 3.1
### STANDARD
The Light Guiding Tetrahedron is the exclusive drop of the Algorithm of Semi-Intransient Matrix of Overseer Network.
