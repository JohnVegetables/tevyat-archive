## summary
### LITE
The Setekh Wenut is a giant eyeless sandworm living in the Wenut Tunnels of the Desert of Hadramaveth, in Sumeru's Great Red Sand. It is a normal boss with the title The Last Prince of the Sands. It finds prey by sound and vibration and fights with Anemo blasts and by drilling out of the ground.
### STANDARD +
It is a Mystical Beast. Its Handbook entry says Wenut sometimes take on forms very like plants and that some call them descendants of the lord of plants, while others say the form is a lure for prey.
### LARGE +
It was added in version 3.4. Its description card, Lord of the Desert, says the desert was once home to flowers, giant trees and countless birds and beasts, and that only the blind predators called Wenut remember it.

## nature
### LITE
A huge blind predator of the deep desert, hunting through sound and vibration.
### STANDARD +
The Archive says these creatures came from deep within the Great Red Sand. A branch of their genus survived by adapting to earth-based living and became known as the Wenut. Some desert folk wrongly think sandworms are the last stage of a quicksand eel's life cycle, because the two behave similarly.
### LARGE +
Wenut are also an exploration hazard in the Desert of Hadramaveth: in certain areas they rise from the ground to attack the player, and their attacks can be steered at Weathered Rocks. The wiki cites an item description saying high-end spices can be extracted from Wenut body fat, and notes that Sand Grease Pupae are sometimes ground up and passed off as it.

## lore
@version: 3.4
### STANDARD
Legend says that in the ancient past, before time itself existed, these creatures lived with their progenitors and offspring in a land of verdant grass and colossal trees, moving through slick soil as fish do through water.
### LARGE +
With the passage of eons the gifts of heaven, the Celestial Nails, turned the soil to sand and drove many creatures extinct, according to the Archive. The Wenut were a branch that survived. The Archive text links the progenitors to the Seven Sovereigns.
### ULTRA +
The names are drawn from Egyptian mythology according to the wiki's etymology notes: Setekh from the desert god and Wenut from the snake goddess Unut. These are naming sources, not in-world facts.

## abilities
### STANDARD
Game mechanics (labelled as such): its first phase uses a body sweep and Anemo spit; after a short opening (about 20 seconds, or the first sliver of its health) it switches to a drilling thrust from below, a Stream of Gales of Anemo projectiles, a Stormbreath sweep and a Pressurized Beam. It also digs out and back into the ground, exposing itself.
### LARGE +
In a later phase it floats and conjures six Windbite Bullets. Destroying two in succession with Cryo, Electro, Hydro or Pyro paralyses it for about ten seconds and lowers its resistance to the elements used. If it absorbs the bullets it launches rounds of projectiles over expanding circles. Its drop is Pseudo-Stamens.
### ULTRA +
In the Spiral Abyss the first phase is skipped. These details are game values from the wiki, not lore.

## individuals
@version: 3.6
### STANDARD
The Setekh Wenut of the Wenut Tunnels is the open-world boss. Wenut: Abnormal Turbulence is an event-only enemy from the limited-time event Fulminating Sandstorm.
### LARGE +
The event enemy fires Anemo projectiles and a large orb; it is a separate encounter, not another named creature in the story.

## relations
### STANDARD
It is the boss form of the Wenut that threaten desert travellers, and is related in the Handbook's account to Apep, the lord of plants, by the claim that Wenut were once his scions.

## chronology
### ULTRA
- 3.4: the boss and the Wenut exploration hazard are added to the Desert of Hadramaveth.
- 3.6: Wenut: Abnormal Turbulence appears in the event Fulminating Sandstorm (version per the event page's change history).

## status
### STANDARD
As of 7.1, the Setekh Wenut remains a boss in the Wenut Tunnels. Its ancient origin is known only as legend.

## entry: Wenut
@keys: Wenut, Wenuts, sandworms of Sumeru
@version: 3.4
### STANDARD
Wenut are giant sandworms of the Desert of Hadramaveth. They emerge from the ground in certain areas to attack travellers. The Setekh Wenut is the boss form.
### LARGE +
The Handbook says they may appear plant-like, either because they were once scions of Apep or to lure prey into their shade.
