## summary
### LITE
The "Statue of Marble and Brass", archived as the Legatus Golem, is a majestic centaur-shaped golem of marble and brass. It sleeps in the exhibition hall of the undersea Faded Castle, a relic of the sunken Remurian civilisation. Under the stone it has Ichor blood, and its shield and sword can "perform" a blazing melody of flames. Its title is "Legatus of Legion Athanatoi".
### STANDARD +
The panel describes it as a colossus displaying the immortality and glory of a great civilisation of old, awaiting anyone who dares challenge the majesty of its ancient ruler. The boss is unlocked by completing Petrichorror Dream, the first act of Canticles of Harmony (4.6).

## nature
### LITE
A golem in the form of a centaur, built of white stone and brass, towering like a commander of a legion. Its body is stone, but the Archive says Ichor blood flows beneath the hard exterior.
### STANDARD +
The Archive says the sunken Remurian civilisation believed that war was like music, an art of rhythms to be followed. A commander of legions must therefore be like a conductor: shield and sword become the strings and bow of a violin, performing a melody as blazing as the flames of a merciless battlefield. A Harmost who led the golem legions across the whole high sea is quoted: "War is a necessary evil on the path to all-encompassing peace."
### LARGE +
Its title has a Latin and Greek form: "Legatus" (a Roman officer or envoy) and "Athanatoi" (Greek for "the immortals"), which the wiki renders as "Lieutenant of the Legion of the Undying". That is a translation note, not a canon statement. The Adventurer Handbook entry changes after the boss is unlocked, from "a golem that once bore the weight of the ancients' dreams slumbers in the exhibition hall" to the fuller description of its body and weapons.

### ULTRA +
The event panels for this boss and the Sea of Bygone Eras were the first to alternate between two sound effects when switched. Its combat is presented as a performance with themed phases; Remuria's own view of war as music is the framing the Archive gives for that design.

## abilities
### LITE
A Geo ward over its stone body, flaming sword strikes, stomps, charging slashes, shield slams that cause cascading explosions, and Pyro attacks performed as music: orbs, rings of notes and heatblades.
### STANDARD +
The tutorial says it uses a sturdy marble body to defend and music and searing flames to obliterate foes. Geo-suited methods are needed to break the ward, and the golem sets up resonators in combat that assist its attacks; destroying them wears down the ward. Once the ward is broken the golem is paralysed for a short time and then restores it.
### LARGE +
Its finale is a sequence of three expanding explosions and a leaping strike with its raised sword. The wiki says most attack names are unofficial, and the damage figures are game mechanics.

## relations
### STANDARD
Remurian: the Archive links it to the sunken civilisation (Remuria) and to its Harmost, who led golem legions. The setting is the Faded Castle, in the Nostoi Region of the Sea of Bygone Eras.

## status
### LITE
Slumbering in the Faded Castle and available as a boss as of 7.1. I found no mention of it in 6.x (Nod-Krai) or 7.0-7.1 (Snezhnaya) story content.
### STANDARD +
Legatus Golem: Harmonizer ("Rhythm of Ruthless Flames") is an event-exclusive variant from On the Trail of Behemoths (5.3, 14-27 January 2025). The Geo ward there has different research-topic modifiers; it is a gameplay variant, not a separate golem in the world.
