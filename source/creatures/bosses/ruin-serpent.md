## summary
### LITE
The Ruin Serpent is a bizarrely shaped autonomous machine that prowls the underground Chasm of Liyue, in the Serpent's Cave of The Chasm's Underground Mines. It carries a huge drilling gear on its head that can carve through bedrock. Its designation is Excavator: SI/Glp. It is one of the Ruin Machines, the automatons left by Khaenri'ah, and it is a normal boss.
### STANDARD +
The Archive describes it as heavy-duty engineering equipment left behind by a destroyed nation, and notes that machines like it could once have bored tunnels for that nation's forces across the whole continent.
### LARGE +
A quest-only form, Haftvad the Worm, appears in the world quest Wherefore Did the Spiritstone Descend?

## nature
### LITE
A long, segmented serpent-shaped machine with a drilling gear on its head. The Handbook says it dug a tunnel to The Chasm from somewhere unknown.
### STANDARD +
Its designation follows the scheme of the other Khaenri'ahn bosses, such as Perpetual Resonance SI/Ald. Its glowing body segments are its weak points. The Archive and Handbook texts do not describe a pilot or controller, so it is treated as autonomous.
### LARGE +
The Handbook entry shown before the boss is unlocked speaks only of faint bursts of machine motion coming from the depths of The Chasm. The wiki notes that Muzhen first mentioned it in 2.0.

## lore
@version: 2.6
### STANDARD
Archive text calls it an ancient, bizarrely shaped autonomous machine and heavy-duty engineering equipment from a destroyed nation. If the nation had such machines, tunnelling out routes for its forces would have been easy. The nation is Khaenri'ah, as with the other Ruin Machines.
### LARGE +
In the world quest series The Chasm Delvers, Paimon recalls that Zhiqiong mentioned a tale of a giant serpent lurking in The Chasm. In Wherefore Did the Spiritstone Descend? a stone tablet at the end of a long tunnel shows a big worm and appears to warn of it; Paimon guesses the tunnel was dug by a giant serpent. The Traveler purifies large crystals with a Lumenstone Adjuvant and strikes the purified crystal, which awakens Haftvad the Worm, titled Giant Digging Device of the Lost Realm. Paimon calls it the one who dug out the place.
### ULTRA +
During the fight the Traveler cannot wear it down, and in a cutscene Yelan, watching from a vantage point, shoots the crystal so that it falls onto the serpent. The party escapes the collapse. Afterwards Paimon asks what the thing above them was, why a giant serpent came roaring out and where the dark mud and strange monsters came from; the quest leaves these open.

## abilities
### STANDARD
Game mechanics (labelled as such): it deals Physical damage. Its attacks include a rolling steamroll, a charge that ends with the drill planted in the ground, a burrowing Sand Tomb that pulls players toward its centre, a bomb barrage and a shockwave. Before the fight it drains the energy of a Lumenstone Adjuvant. It creates Oozing Concretions in the arena, which the player breaks with the Adjuvant.
### LARGE +
The barrage and shockwave charge from absorbing the Oozing Concretions, and while charging it gains great resistance to all damage and exposes four glowing weak points. Breaking all four paralyses it for a short time. In the Spiral Abyss it has no Oozing Concretions and is paralysed only by weak point hits. Its drop is the Runic Fang.
### ULTRA +
Haftvad the Worm creates only two sources of Oozing Concretions instead of four and does not use the Charge and Drill attack. The fight ends when its health drops to a low level, where a cutscene takes over.

## individuals
### STANDARD
The Ruin Serpent of the Serpent's Cave is the open boss. Haftvad the Worm is the quest-only variant in Wherefore Did the Spiritstone Descend?, the sixth and last part of The Chasm Delvers series.
### LARGE +
The Serpent's Cave becomes accessible after the clues about Uncle He are found in Chasm Spelunkers. The Chasm Exploration Team has its camp nearby, with Jinwu among those at it.

## relations
### STANDARD
It belongs to the Ruin Machines and is one of the Ruin Machine bosses, with the Perpetual Mechanical Array and the Aeonblight Drake. It is a Khaenri'ahn construct found in Liyue.
### LARGE +
It meets the Chasm Exploration Team only as a danger in their excavation. The Lumenstone Adjuvant is the tool used to break its Oozing Concretions.

## chronology
### ULTRA
- 2.0: Muzhen mentions the Ruin Serpent, per the wiki trivia.
- 2.6: the boss is added; The Chasm Delvers world quests include Chasm Spelunkers and Wherefore Did the Spiritstone Descend?, where Haftvad the Worm appears.

## status
### STANDARD
As of 7.1, the Ruin Serpent remains in The Chasm's Underground Mines as a boss. Its origin and the reason it dug there are not explained.

## entry: Haftvad the Worm
@keys: Haftvad, Haftvad the Worm
@version: 2.6
### STANDARD
Haftvad the Worm, Giant Digging Device of the Lost Realm, is the quest-only form of the Ruin Serpent in Wherefore Did the Spiritstone Descend? It is awakened by striking a purified crystal in a cavern behind a sealed gate. Yelan's shot ends the battle when the crystal falls on it.
### LARGE +
The quest leaves open why it was there and whether it is the same machine as the Ruin Serpent of the Serpent's Cave.
