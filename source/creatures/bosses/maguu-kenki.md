## summary
### LITE
The Maguu Kenki is a tall mechanical swordsman, a humanoid automaton wielding a blade and wearing a fearsome oni-style mask. It was reportedly built from the stored memory of the first master of a sword school famed for the Tengu Sweeper, went out of control and was thrown away. It cannot speak and only "talks" by crossing swords. The standard boss stands at Serpent's Head on Yashiori Island, Inazuma.
### STANDARD +
Its in-game title is "Ingenious Machine: Marionette Swordmaster". It is a boss of the Automaton family, defeated for gems and its unique drop, the Marionette Core. It first appeared in Version 1.6 on the Golden Apple Archipelago, where its title was "Humanoid Automaton of Unknown Origin", and was only given its fuller backstory in Version 2.0 with Inazuma.
### LARGE +
The text surrounding it is consistently elegiac: a discarded tool that keeps practising a dead man's art in the place where its fate ended. The archive makes no claim that it feels anything.

## nature
### LITE
A discarded puppet-like automaton with a disciplined, human-shaped body. Puppet strings seem attached to parts of its body, matching its name. It has neither the ability nor, apparently, the will to speak.
### STANDARD +
The Adventurer Handbook and Archive say it was made from the culminative memory of the first-generation master of a certain sword school, one known for its secret technique, the Tengu Sweeper. It malfunctioned, lost control and was ultimately discarded. The Marionette Core description says the core lets an inorganic body wield a blade like a living being, and muses on how flesh decays along with martial mastery and cherished memories, so that a person might only transcend that impermanence by turning limbs, body and finally heart into machinery.
### LARGE +
Its guard-mask is said to have been inspired by a famous oni from the same era. Some who sing of it say its spirit lingers at the place where its fate was severed. Treat "spirit" as folk phrasing: canon does not confirm a surviving mind.

## abilities
### STANDARD
Game mechanics (labelled as such): the boss fights with a sword in Physical damage, summons oni masks that block ranged attacks and burst or shove, and uses a gale-and-frost second phase in which it conjures a "Shadowsword" that attacks in Anemo or Cryo and drags players in with a vacuum-style pull. Its signature move, the Pseudo Tengu Sweeper, is a charged spinning slash. Most move names are unofficial. It is the only Automaton boss that drops gems matching its own elements.
### LARGE +
Hits during its second phase can make it teleport away; it does this when struck while idle, and a teleport back toward its spawn point acts as a leash. A taunt, a raised fist when the player stays at distance, leaves it open to attack. Its sword and movement style reportedly mirror the techniques attributed to Iwakura Michihiro, which makes the Maguu Kenki, in the wiki's phrase, the only remaining "practitioner" of the Iwakura Art.

## origin
### STANDARD
The maker, date and purpose are not given in canon. The machine's sword school is the Tengu Sweeper school; the wiki identifies it with the Iwakura Clan founded by Iwakura Michihiro, a former Mikoshi oni who took the Iwakura name from the Yougou Tengu Teruyo after the Cataclysm. The Iwakura Clan is now believed to be extinct, so the machine outlived its source.
### LARGE +
Per the wiki's Iwakura Clan page, "an unknown person or people" noted his techniques while he lived and built a Maguu Kenki series that malfunctioned; two such puppets reached Yashiori Island and a third wound up on the Golden Apple Archipelago after Ako Domeki's pirate crew stole it. This is the wiki's synthesis; canon text states none of it directly.

## individuals
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
Known units, per the wiki:
- Serpent's Head, Yashiori Island, Inazuma: the standard boss, active.
- Fort Mumei, the ruins of an ancient city on Yashiori Island that fell in a battle between the Shogunate and Orobashi's forces: a second unit listed as destroyed.
- Golden Apple Archipelago (Version 1.6): the first boss version, active.
### LARGE +
Event versions: in Legend of the Vagabond Sword (Version 1.6) a pair, Maguu Kenki: Lone Gale and Galloping Frost, fight together, sometimes joined by Maguu Kenki: Mask of Terror, as "a pair of mysterious puppets lost in the endless process of honing their combat skills" who finally meet a fearless wanderer. In Warrior's Spirit (Version 3.4) the Round 6 opponent is the Maguu Kengou, "The Instructor".

## quests
@availability: limited
### LARGE
The Maguu Kenki appears only as a combat encounter, never as a speaking character. The Echoing Tales story My Hero (a 2.x event) is linked on the wiki to a "General" who may be this machine, and Diluc's remark in Dodo-King of the Sea: Lying in Wait suggests it might have guarded the Archipelago's islanders from hilichurls, but both are interpretation.

## chronology
### ULTRA
- 1.6: first appears on the Golden Apple Archipelago; the Legend of the Vagabond Sword event features the paired event bosses.
- 2.0: placed on Yashiori Island with the Inazuma release; its Handbook entry is rewritten around the Tengu Sweeper master and it is added to the Archive.
- 2.7: its attack scaling is adjusted.
- 3.4: the Warrior's Spirit event adds the Maguu Kengou variant.

## status
### STANDARD
As of Version 7.1, the Maguu Kenki still stands as an open-world boss in Inazuma. No story in the Nod-Krai (6.x) or Snezhnaya (7.0-7.1) content adds to its backstory, so what is known is unchanged since 2.0.

## entry: Marionette Core
@keys: Marionette Core
@version: 2.0
### STANDARD
The Maguu Kenki's unique boss drop. Its description says it can power the inorganic swordsman to wield a blade like a living being, and reflects that flesh and memory decay, so perhaps only a person who replaced limbs, body and finally heart with machinery could escape the impermanence of the flesh.

## entry: Dual Maguu Kenki
@keys: Lone Gale, Galloping Frost, Mask of Terror, Dual Maguu Kenki
@version: 1.6
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
Event bosses from Legend of the Vagabond Sword. Maguu Kenki: Lone Gale and Maguu Kenki: Galloping Frost fight as a pair; Maguu Kenki: Mask of Terror sometimes joins them. The event text calls them puppets lost in the endless honing of their skill whose bodies and hearts may last a thousand years or more, raising the question of how many worthy foes they will find in that time.

## entry: Tengu Sweeper
@keys: Tengu Sweeper, Pseudo Tengu Sweeper
@keys2: Maguu Kenki, Iwakura
@version: 2.0
### STANDARD
The secret technique of the sword school whose first master's memory the Maguu Kenki was built from. In Iwakura lore it is a fast, powerful spinning slash fast enough to catch even a Tengu. The machine's version is called Pseudo Tengu Sweeper.
