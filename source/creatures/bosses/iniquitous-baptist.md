## summary
### LITE
The Iniquitous Baptist is a hulking, many-limbed creature of the Abyss that wields several elements at once. As a normal boss it fights with three of Cryo, Electro, Hydro and Pyro and hides behind elemental shields that rotate between those three. It speaks like a zealot, offering to cleanse the Traveler of sin and calling the Abyss a salvation. Two named individuals take this form: the Fontainian Jakob Ingold and Haden, the Abyss Order's former sword instructor.
### STANDARD +
It first appeared in Version 3.6 in Sumeru's desert, near the Gate of Zulqarnain in Gavireh Lajavard, and its defeat drops the Evergloom Ring. Its overworld epithet changes with the day's element combination, for example Invoker of Fire, Frost, and Fulmination.
### LARGE +
The Archive and Handbook describe a creature of the Abyss that has gained extra limbs through dark "evolution". Its item text calls it a four-armed envoy of dark tidings.

## nature
### LITE
An Abyss-family monster with additional appendages, able to bend several kinds of elemental power. The Archive adds a note left in Hangeh Afrasiyab by an unnamed scholar: the Abyss is irreconcilable with the elements, yet even gods' elements can be entrapped and distorted and made tools of abyssal will, so abyssal power looks much like an elemental item, from a Treasure Hoarder's container to a Vision.
### STANDARD +
Its voice is solemn and sermonising, with a religious vocabulary of sin, penance, redemption and blessing. In its lines it addresses the Traveler as a lost lamb and promises to cleanse their sins.
### LARGE +
Its in-game descriptor, Cleansing of Sin, reads as a seeker of truths who glimpsed the pitch-dark world beyond the sky and now whispers accursed prayers that guide mortals toward the "paradise of truth". The infobox title The Guide to the Dark Paradise is in the same vein. Its battle cries call the Abyss's order salvation and false laws something to be swept away. Treat the speech as the creature's own doctrine, not as a statement of what canon holds to be true.

## abilities
### LITE
Game mechanics (labelled as such): the boss wields three of four elements (Cryo, Electro, Hydro, Pyro), which changes daily in the overworld and is fixed in challenge modes such as the Spiral Abyss and Imaginarium Theater. It shields itself with an element and cycles through three shields. Breaking a shield paralyses it. Crystals behind it show which shield is active and which remain.
### STANDARD +
Each element has its own attacks, with side effects that drain resources: Cryo icicles drain Stamina, Electro orbs drain party Energy, Hydro blades lengthen cooldowns, and Pyro flames mark players with a status that later drains HP. An overworld-only attack conjures elemental nodes that form rings; if the nodes are not destroyed, the rings explode. It teleports away from players who get close in its Electro phase.
### LARGE +
Exact numbers belong to the wiki. Its drops are a boss material (the Evergloom Ring), element gems and artifact sets.

## individuals
### STANDARD
Two named Baptists are known. Jakob Ingold, a Fontainian and co-founder of the Narzissenkreuz Ordo, can transform into a Baptist through experiments with Abyssal power; he is fought in the Fontaine world quest Limner, Dreamer, and Robotic Dog. Haden, the former Khaenri'ah sword instructor now in the Abyss Order, takes the form in the Archon Quest A Space and Time Without You (5.7), under the epithet Wyrdsword of Heaven's End.
### LARGE +
In Jakob's fight the boss does not raise an elemental shield but still switches between Cryo, Hydro and Pyro. In Haden's fight it starts with a shield and cycles through Pyro, Cryo and Electro, and a broken shield sends it into full paralysis and resets the cycle to Pyro. The open-world Baptists are anonymous.

## chronology
### ULTRA
- 3.6: the Iniquitous Baptist is added as a Sumeru desert normal boss near the Gate of Zulqarnain.
- 4.0: a fix is made so it generates a shield after using its ring attack in certain circumstances.
- Fontaine (Ancient Colors, Blue Longing of Heart and Moon): Jakob fights as a Baptist in Limner, Dreamer, and Robotic Dog.
- 5.7: Haden appears as a Baptist in the Abyss Order's stronghold in the Cinder City, Natlan, in A Space and Time Without You.

## status
### STANDARD
As of 7.1 the Iniquitous Baptist remains an open-world and challenge boss. No new Baptist individuals beyond Jakob and Haden appear in the pages checked for Nod-Krai (6.x) or Snezhnaya (7.0-7.1).

## entry: Haden the Baptist
@keys: Haden, Wyrdsword of Heaven's End
@keys2: Iniquitous Baptist, Abyss Order, Dainsleif
@version: 5.7
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Haden was the sword instructor who taught Black Serpent Bladework to Khaenri'ahn knights, including Dainsleif and Surtalogi, five hundred years ago. In A Space and Time Without You, an Iniquitous Baptist is the final foe in the Abyss Order's stronghold at the Cinder City in Natlan. Before the fight it greets Dainsleif as the Twilight Sword and announces, falsely, that Khaenri'ah has been revived by the Traveler's sibling through the Night Kingdom and the Loom of Fate. After the Traveler leaves, the Baptist attacks Dainsleif, who recognises Haden. Neither can kill the other for good because of the curse of immortality.
### LARGE +
Haden says that his memories, emotions and personality have eroded and that he cannot recall the cruelty Dainsleif accuses him of: a benevolent mask, unjust corporal punishment and the mutilation of Surtalogi. He admits the earlier claim was a lie and that the ley lines were not fully rewoven; the Loom of Fate needs the Abyss Order's members to supply memories, and he used Dainsleif's own recollections of Khaenri'ah, which are better preserved than most. Haden still believes the Traveler's sibling is sincere in wishing to revive their homeland.

## entry: Jakob Ingold as a Baptist
@keys: Jakob Ingold, Jakob
@keys2: Iniquitous Baptist, Narzissenkreuz Ordo, Blue Longing of Heart and Moon
### STANDARD
In Limner, Dreamer, and Robotic Dog, the Traveler finds Jakob in the Beryl Region of Fontaine using Abyssal power to turn crystallised blood back to its living form, gathering what he calls paint. He denies belonging to the Abyss Order and calls its members benighted primitives, saying the Narzissenkreuz Ordo seeks true equality and liberation of the will. He fights as an Iniquitous Baptist.
### LARGE +
The Elynas profile says that Jakob and Rene entered Elynas's body repeatedly, that Jakob absorbed his blood and Abyssal power, and that Jakob eventually became a Baptist; the wiki cites the item Enigmatic Page (III). Elynas regarded Jakob as a friend even in that form.

## entry: Evergloom Ring
@keys: Evergloom Ring
@version: 3.6
### STANDARD
The character level-up material dropped by Lv. 30+ Iniquitous Baptists. Its text calls it a mystical weapon from the Four-Armed Envoy of dark tidings and notes that rings are shaped like passageways; arcane formations meant to produce a mystical effect are often ring-shaped, perhaps gateways to guide the universe itself.
