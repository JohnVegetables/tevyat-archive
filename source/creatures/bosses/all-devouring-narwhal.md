## summary
### LITE
The All-Devouring Narwhal is a colossal whale-like creature from the sea of stars, outside Teyvat's sky. It is the pet of Surtalogi the Foul, Skirk's master. It fed on the Primordial Sea beneath Fontaine, which made the sea level rise, and its rampage set off the flood the Prophecy of Fontaine foretold. Neuvillette and the Traveler defeated it in the Primordial Sea, and Skirk shrank it to an orb and took it away.
### STANDARD +
Poets liken the night sky to an ocean; this beast is the nightmare version of that image, a leviathan that swims between worlds. It is drawn to the "blood" of Fontainians and cannot resist its instinct to devour. After the story, the battle can be relived as the weekly boss fight in the Shadow of Another World domain.

## nature
### LITE
A vast, mysterious creature "from another world". Neuvillette describes it as a monster that crossed the stars weeping. It is not a native of Teyvat and belongs to no known species. It devours, and it craves energy and living things.
### STANDARD +
Its in-game Archive entry reads, before the finale, simply as a mysterious, gargantuan creature from another world. After the finale the entry adds a reflection: poets compare the starry sky to the sea, and the universe may be constantly trying to infiltrate Teyvat, or a higher power may have raised borders (the false sky) to protect it. The Archive does not say which.

The creature is a pet only in Surtalogi's eyes. Skirk calls it uncooperative, a glutton and a failure as a pet. Neuvillette names its instincts as the fight's key: it "cannot resist its instinct to devour", and so it can be provoked into swallowing someone.
### LARGE +
Its body is tied to Hydro in the fight (its attacks are Hydro and it is highly resistant to Hydro). Inside it lies a hostile "Dark Shadow" that wields Electro and fights as a claymore-armed armoured figure. Whispers Betwixt Stars and Night (5.7) shows the same armoured form in the Abyss, worn by Skofnungr, a Khaenri'ahn who grafted some of the Narwhal's flesh into his heart. It explains that the Dark Shadow takes Surtalogi's shape because those who crave his strength tend to imitate him.

## origins
### STANDARD
Its origin world is unknown. Surtalogi, one of the Five Sinners of Khaenri'ah, left Teyvat after the Cataclysm to seek stronger opponents among the stars, and the Narwhal became his pet. According to the quests it:
- entered Teyvat at some point and began drinking energy from the Primordial Sea, which raised sea levels around Fontaine;
- was, in the account Neuvillette investigated, awakened by chance by the young Tartaglia (then Ajax, who says he was fourteen) when he fell into an unknown chasm or abyss; and
- turned up in Fontaine's Primordial Sea, where it had been fighting Tartaglia since he escaped the Fortress of Meropide.
### LARGE +
Skirk says it is a strange use of a planet's primordial waters to raise an All-Devouring Narwhal, which implies Surtalogi may have brought it to the sea on purpose. Neuvillette instead stresses Tartaglia's accidental link to it. The wiki prefers the second reading, that it followed Tartaglia home from the Abyss, but canon never settles this.

A second thread comes from Whispers Betwixt Stars and Night (5.7). Before he left Teyvat, Surtalogi gave his friend Skofnungr a "gift" if he still wished to grow stronger. The gift was a weakened Narwhal left in the Primordial Sea, and Skofnungr cut away a piece of its flesh and fused it into himself.

## events
### STANDARD
During the Fontaine Archon Quest (4.2), the Narwhal appears first in the Traveler's dream and then in person. It bursts out of a rift in space into the Opera Epiclese during Furina's trial, and the crowd flees. Neuvillette's first attempts to stop it fail, and the creature turns toward the escapees. Tartaglia, in his Foul Legacy form, appears out of the rift and attacks it, which gives Neuvillette time to join him. Tartaglia then falls unconscious into the hole.
### LARGE +
Neuvillette tells the party that the whale does not belong to Teyvat and that it covets Fontainian "blood". He credits the Harbinger with buying time: without his long fight in the Primordial Sea, it would have come ashore far sooner. In "The Opera of Noirceur and Blancheur" Neuvillette and the Traveler follow it into the Primordial Sea. He has tried to separate the sea's power from the beast but cannot reach its source, so the Traveler provokes it until it devours them, and they defeat the Dark Shadow within.

Once it is beaten, its prior consumption and the power spent in the fight cause the upheaval above, which triggers the foretold flooding of Fontaine. A woman then appears, shrinks the Narwhal into a small orb in her palm, carries the unconscious Tartaglia by the scruff, and sends both through a teleporter. She is Skirk, who says she meant to deal with the beast herself until it began fighting her student. She also remarks that it had used up almost all its strength fighting the party.
### ULTRA +
Neuvillette's inquiry, reported in Finale, is that Tartaglia's part in the disaster is at most tangential liability, and that his imprisonment may have been used to buy time. Lyney in the same quest speaks of a gesture that could be an apology for the incident with Tartaglia or thanks for his help in tying the Narwhal down. These are characters' readings, not narrator statements.

## abilities
### LITE
It swims through solid ground as if it were water, rising to leap or dive at its target. It conjures falling crystals and beams of light, and it can swallow a fighter whole.
### STANDARD +
Game mechanics (labelled as such): it is a weekly boss. Most attacks are Hydro: leaps and dives that crack the ground and burst into splashes, a rain of crystals, beams of light, and an Eye of Maelstrom that pulls players inward and then explodes. The domain's text says Pneuma or Ousia (Arkhe) attacks can quickly destroy the whirlpool.

The signature mechanic is hostility. Damage dealt, and the Eye's destruction, fill a Fury meter that also rises with time. When it is full, the beast devours a character and sends them into its belly to fight the Dark Shadow. Beating the Shadow takes a large share of the Narwhal's HP, paralyses it and fills the party's Energy. Below 20% HP it stops devouring.
### LARGE +
The Dark Shadow carries a greatsword and uses Electro strikes that leave cracks in space. It also charges its own Eye of Maelstrom using Pneumousia energy: if an Arkhe attack interrupts the charge, the Shadow loses health. If the charge finishes, it summons creatures (Geovishaps, Consecrated Fanged Beasts or Hat Jellyfish) to attack. The Shadow's health is a separate white bar under the Narwhal's, and in the fight it is the only part with a weak point.

The domain's in-game text describes the same system in-world: dealing damage and destroying the Eye can draw the creature's hostility until it devours a character.

## relations
### LITE
Surtalogi owns it. Skirk, his disciple, defeated and shrank it when it threatened her student. Tartaglia has an old tie with it, and he built the whale shape into his own fighting style after meeting it.
### STANDARD +
Neuvillette and the Traveler are the ones who finish it in the story. Skofnungr carries a graft of its flesh, and the Dark Shadow in the Narwhal also wears Surtalogi's form, suggesting its power is bound up with imitations of him.
### LARGE +
Tartaglia: the wiki records that his Foul Legacy form's whale motif comes from meeting the beast in the Abyss at 14. The relation is also why Neuvillette's inquiry asks how much blame he bears.

Surtalogi: the creature is his pet, and he is described as roaming the cosmos for opponents. With him gone from Teyvat, Skirk is the one who handles the pet.
### ULTRA +
The Primordial Sea: the Narwhal's feeding is the physical cause of Fontaine's flood. Neuvillette says the sea's power must be separated from the beast to weaken it.

## status
### STANDARD
As of 7.1 it is shrunk to an orb that Skirk threw through a rift after the 4.2 battle, and it has not been shown since. The weekly domain Shadow of Another World (a Trounce Domain in Salacia Plain, Court of Fontaine region) lets the player relive the fight as a memory, and is unlocked after finishing the Fontaine Archon Quest. It is a replay, not a new appearance.

## entry: Dark Shadow of the Narwhal
@keys: Dark Shadow, Dark Shadow of the Narwhal
@keys2: Narwhal, Skirk, Skofnungr, Surtalogi
@version: 5.7
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The Dark Shadow is the armoured, claymore-wielding figure inside the All-Devouring Narwhal's belly. A hostile entity in Skirk's story quest (Whispers Betwixt Stars and Night, 5.7) wears the same form; Skirk explains that those longing for Surtalogi's strength imitate him on a subconscious level. When its armour breaks, the wearer is Skofnungr, a Khaenri'ahn friend and follower of Surtalogi who fused some Narwhal flesh into his heart.
### LARGE +
Skofnungr, cursed with immortality after the Cataclysm, refused Surtalogi's offer to lift the curse because he wanted to grow stronger. The Narwhal's power still brought him pain. He asked Skirk to end his life; she offered instead to destroy his consciousness so the borrowed power left him and the curse of immortality could slowly restore his body and mind, and he accepted.

## entry: Shadow of Another World
@keys: Shadow of Another World, Memories: The Titan Amidst the Stars
@version: 4.2
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
A Trounce Domain in the Court of Fontaine region that unlocks after the Fontaine Archon Quest's Finale. The player can relive the battle against the Narwhal there. Its description calls that battle the intersection point of this world's fate and says Fontaine now treads a brighter path. Rewards are weekly-boss materials: Lightless Silk String, Lightless Eye of the Maelstrom and Lightless Mass.

## entry: The whale and the Prophecy of Fontaine
@keys: Prophecy of Fontaine, Fontaine flood, whale prophecy
@keys2: Narwhal, whale
@version: 4.2
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
In "Apocalypse", Paimon concludes that the whale is the real cause of the disaster in the prophecy. Neuvillette later calls it the root of Fontaine's calamities and the beast that enacts the prophecy. Its drinking from the Primordial Sea raised the water and its defeat caused the final upheaval.

## entry: Tartaglia and the whale
@keys: Childe, Tartaglia, Ajax
@keys2: Narwhal, whale
@version: 4.2
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
In The City of Rains and Remembrance (4.0) Tartaglia says that at fourteen he fell into an unknown abyss, dreamed of a vast whale and had awakened "it"; Neuvillette's investigation in Finale says he by chance awakened the All-Devouring Narwhal after falling into an unknown chasm. He later fought it in the Primordial Sea after escaping the Fortress of Meropide, which delayed its landing. His Foul Legacy transformation uses a whale motif.
