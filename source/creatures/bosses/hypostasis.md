## summary
### LITE
Hypostases are elemental lifeforms that have given up their old forms and structures to reach the highest level of elemental purity. Each is a compact, dense, cube-shelled core of one element, and there is a normal boss for each of the seven: Anemo, Electro, Geo, Cryo, Hydro, Pyro and Dendro. They are invulnerable while the shell surrounds them and can be hurt only when the diamond-shaped core is exposed, usually during or after an attack.
### STANDARD +
They are the highest forms of elemental structure, found where elemental energy is concentrated or at clogged ley lines. Research on them is led by scholars of the Sumeru Akademiya, but because they are dangerous, very little is known beyond each one's scientific name and Hebrew-letter code name.

## nature
### LITE
Ultra-compact, high-mass bodies of pure element. A solid shell forms around the core, and only the core responds to elemental stimuli. They are immune to their own element and deal damage of that element.
### STANDARD +
The Archive and Handbook texts, together, say that Hypostases:
- are formed usually at a location bursting with elemental energy or at a clogged ley line;
- have subtle differences in physical composition from other elemental lifeforms, which may explain their high homeostasis and nearly mechanical movements;
- appear tied to anomalies in local elemental veins, though whether they cause the anomalies or mutated energy creates them is unconfirmed; and
- can "learn", according to the deductions of some researchers (the Dendro entry), using mimesis to start attacks.
### LARGE +
They show strong rejection behaviour: the Hydro Hypostasis's text says they expel any who test them, and that the behaviour even extends to natural elements, for reasons unknown. Close to defeat, they enter an "emergency restart" phase (except the Pyro one in its main mechanic) and revive, a regeneration rate not seen even among creatures for whom survival is first. In-game, this takes the form of a revival phase after 95% of their health is lost, in which elemental objects must be handled before a time limit to finish them.

## codenames
### STANDARD
Each Hypostasis has a code name drawn from the Hebrew alphabet: Electro is Aleph, Anemo is Beth, Geo is Gimel, Cryo is Daleth, Hydro is He, Dendro is Zayin and Pyro is Ayin. The sixth letter (Vav) is skipped and Ayin is used instead of it. Geo's code name was once printed as "Grimoire" in the Adventurer Handbook, which did not match its in-game title.
### LARGE +
Pure Hypostases use different code names: Anemo is Etar, Electro is Siras and Geo is Besanar. The wiki notes the three names appear in a spell in the grimoire text The Black Pullet; that is a trivia link, not canon.

## individuals
### STANDARD
Locations of each normal boss:
- Anemo (Beth): Stormbearer Mountains, Starfell Valley, Mondstadt.
- Electro (Aleph): Cape Oath, Galesong Hill, Mondstadt.
- Cryo (Daleth): Dragonspine, outskirts of the Entombed City, Mondstadt. Unlocked after the quest In the Mountains.
- Geo (Gimel): Guyun Stone Forest, Liyue.
- Pyro (Ayin): Kannazuka, Inazuma.
- Hydro (He): Watatsumi Island, Inazuma.
- Dendro (Zayin): Land of Lower Setekh, Sumeru; the last, added in 3.2.
### LARGE +
Notes from the entries:
- The Cryo Hypostasis sits where a great nail fell in a bygone era and has poured energy into the local ley lines ever since; the Handbook wonders what will happen if it is dislodged.
- The Pyro one can regenerate, and its shield must be broken to put out its flame.
- The Hydro one has the highest ATK of the group and no clap attack, as with the Dendro one.
- The Dendro one is the only Hypostasis whose fight aims to cleanse it, not destroy it.
- Anemo, Electro, Geo and Dendro have sharp, decayed-looking corners, while Cryo, Hydro and Pyro have rounded, intact ones. Canon gives no reason.

## pure_variants

### STANDARD
Pure Hypostases are quest- or event-exclusive variants of the normal bosses. Pure Anemo, Electro and Geo Hypostases appeared in the limited-time Hypostatic Symphony event (a recurring event first held in 1.2) as challenge-domain enemies.

## pure_electro_quest
@version: 2.0
@availability: permanent
### LARGE
The Pure Electro Hypostasis also appears in the world quest Sakura Arborism (2.0, Kannazuka, Inazuma): a slumbering monster there resembles the Electro Hypostasis of Mondstadt, and it is awakened and fought during the quest (epithet "Awakened Monster"). Before the quest it can be interacted with and does not react. The Treasure Hoarder Hideaki, who is allied with Fatui Skirmishers, sets it on the Traveler; Paimon guesses he got "this Electro Hypostasis-like thingy" from some dirty business. Separately, a group of Fatui Skirmishers stands on a cliff west of the ordinary Electro Hypostasis at Cape Oath; the wiki says they have had no story importance.

## abilities
### STANDARD
Game mechanics (labelled as such): each Hypostasis uses attacks of its own element and is immune to that element. Most have a healing phase and invulnerability phases. Examples include the Anemo one's giant crystalfly form that raises a wind eruption, the Geo one's summoned pillars and stone barrage, the Pyro one's molten forms and ignited state, the Hydro one's bubbles and water droplets, and the Dendro one's orbs and whip forms. The Cryo and Pyro ones can shield themselves.
### LARGE +
Players are generally told to strike the exposed core. When Hypostases are defeated they drop a character ascension gem of their element and a unique boss material: Hurricane Seed (Anemo), Lightning Prism (Electro), Basalt Pillar (Geo), Crystalline Bloom (Cryo), Dew of Repudiation (Hydro), Smoldering Pearl (Pyro) and Quelled Creeper (Dendro).

## relations
### STANDARD
Sucrose's elemental skill and burst are emulations of the Anemo Hypostasis. Her skill is Astable Anemohypostasis Creation - 6308, her burst is Forbidden Creation - Isomer 75 / Type II, and her second constellation is titled Beth: Unbound Form. This ties the Akademiya-trained alchemist's craft to the research on Hypostases.
### LARGE +
The Handbook says research on Hypostases is led by the Sumeru Akademiya. They belong to the larger group of elemental lifeforms, though they are described as an unusual, mechanically behaving kind of them.

## chronology
### ULTRA
- 1.0: Anemo, Electro and Geo Hypostases launch.
- 1.2: the Hypostatic Symphony event introduces Pure Hypostases (Anemo, Electro, Geo).
- 1.5: the Cryo Hypostasis arrives in Dragonspine.
- 2.0: the Pyro Hypostasis (Kannazuka) and the Sakura Arborism world quest with the Pure Electro variant arrive.
- 2.1: the Hydro Hypostasis arrives at Watatsumi Island.
- 3.2: the Dendro Hypostasis completes the set of seven.

## status
### STANDARD
As of 7.1, all seven normal Hypostases exist in Teyvat as open-world bosses. No Hypostasis appearance, Archon Quest plot or lore has been added in the Nod-Krai (6.x) or Snezhnaya (7.0-7.1) content as far as the researched pages show. Their nature and formation remain only partly explained.

## entry: Anemo Hypostasis
@keys: Anemo Hypostasis, Beth, Hypostasis of Wind
@keys2: Hypostasis, Anemo
@version: 1.0
### STANDARD
Code name Beth, a high-purity Anemo entity found in the Stormbearer Mountains of Starfell Valley, Mondstadt. Its text says Hypostases abandoned their former form and biology to reach elemental purity. Its crystalfly attack inspired Sucrose's Astable Anemohypostasis Creation - 6308 and the second constellation Beth: Unbound Form.

## entry: Electro Hypostasis
@keys: Electro Hypostasis, Aleph
@keys2: Hypostasis, Electro
@version: 1.0
### STANDARD
Code name Aleph, an Electro entity at Cape Oath in Galesong Hill, Mondstadt. Its Handbook text calls Hypostases the highest forms of elemental structures, formed in a place bursting with energy or at a clogged ley line. A Pure variant is the sleeping monster in Sakura Arborism.

## entry: Geo Hypostasis
@keys: Geo Hypostasis, Gimel
@keys2: Hypostasis, Geo
@version: 1.0
### STANDARD
Code name Gimel, a Geo entity in the Guyun Stone Forest of Liyue. Its text describes Hypostases as ultra-compact structures of high mass, whose concentrated energy forms a shell around a core that alone reacts to stimuli.

## entry: Cryo Hypostasis
@keys: Cryo Hypostasis, Daleth, Skyfrost Nail
@keys2: Hypostasis, Cryo, Dragonspine
@version: 1.5
### STANDARD
Code name Daleth, a Cryo entity in Dragonspine, near the Entombed City's outskirts. It appears only after the quest In the Mountains. The Handbook ties the area to a great nail that fell in a bygone era and pours energy into the ley lines. Its Archive text speculates that physical-composition differences explain its homeostasis and mechanical movement.

## entry: Hydro Hypostasis
@keys: Hydro Hypostasis, Mendacious Waves
@keys2: Hypostasis, Hydro, Watatsumi
@version: 2.1
### STANDARD
Code name He, a Hydro entity on Watatsumi Island, Inazuma. Its text says Hypostases have strong rejection properties and expel anything that tests them, even natural elements, for unknown reasons. In combat it has the highest ATK of the group and no clap attack.

## entry: Pyro Hypostasis
@keys: Pyro Hypostasis, Ayin
@keys2: Hypostasis, Pyro, Kannazuka
@version: 2.0
### STANDARD
Code name Ayin, a Pyro entity in Kannazuka, Inazuma. Its Handbook text speaks of the "emergency restart" phase by which Hypostases can revive. Its in-game name for its fire seeds is inconsistent: Tinder, Fire Seed and Fiery Shieldseed all appear in different texts.

## entry: Dendro Hypostasis
@keys: Dendro Hypostasis, Zayin, The Verdant Takeover
@keys2: Hypostasis, Dendro, Sumeru
@version: 3.2
### STANDARD
Code name Zayin, a Dendro entity in the Land of Lower Setekh, Sumeru; it completed the set of seven in 3.2. Its text says it behaves differently from the other Hypostases, and the Archive reports a researchers' theory that it learns and mimics, with the risk that its original purpose becomes distorted. It is the only one a player is meant to cleanse, not destroy.

Event- and quest-only versions: Pure Anemo (Etar), Pure Electro (Siras) and Pure Geo (Besanar) in the Hypostatic Symphony event, plus the Pure Electro variant in the Sakura Arborism world quest in Kannazuka. Each has only a short poetic flavour line and no lore of its own.
