## summary
### LITE
The Perpetual Mechanical Array is a strange, alien-looking war machine made of cubic parts, titled "Perpetual Resonance SI/Ald". It rests in the ruins beneath the water at Araumi, on the northern tip of Narukami Island in Inazuma. It can reshape itself into rockets, swords, blades and cubes to suit the fight.
### STANDARD +
The Adventurer Handbook and Archive describe it as a war machine left behind by a nation that has already been destroyed. They say its cubic construction makes it, on some level, very like the elemental hypostases. It first appeared in Version 2.0. The wiki identifies the nation as Khaenri'ah; the text does not.
### LARGE +
Players reach it by a portal on Jinren Island, or through a Teleport Waypoint at Araumi once that area's part of the Cleansing Defilement quest has been done. The machine sits under the sea in the ancient city's ruins.

## nature
### LITE
A modular automaton of several parts rather than a single body. It is a unique Ruin machine and a boss of the Automaton family.
### STANDARD +
Per the Perpetual Heart, its core powers its autonomous movement. Some theorize the symbols on these unusually large cores carry knowledge and logic written in a language the eye cannot see, and some go further, believing these machines chose to drop a lifelike (biomimetic) shape in favor of power of function. The wiki reads that as four small machines that merged by their own choice, an interpretation rather than stated canon.
### LARGE +
The group tag in the infobox is Ruin Sentinels, since four cubes it launches become Ruin Sentinels. The Archive does not explain how it relates to ordinary Ruin machines other than the shared look of ancient hardware.

## abilities
### STANDARD
Game mechanics (labelled as such): all attacks are Physical. It transforms into a rocket that slams down, a cube that rolls at range and ends with a shockwave, a giant sword and a spinning blade for melee, and it calls down targeted strikes and fires a tracking energy beam. Its defining phase is Defensive Mode, in which it launches four cubes that become Ruin Sentinels; only one highlighted sentinel can be hurt, and defeating it paralyzes the Array and lowers its resistances.
### LARGE +
Defensive Mode is guaranteed once its HP reaches 35% if it has not already used it. It has no weak point and a version 3.1 change raised its ATK by 20% while lowering its damage multipliers by one sixth. A version 2.1 fix addressed it getting stuck behind Geo constructs when splitting. Its drop is the Perpetual Heart, plus Prithiva Topaz and Shivada Jade gems.

## relations
### STANDARD
The Aranara of Sumeru call a large iron fighting thing in the Legends of the Stone Lock quest a "Bulky Iron Chunk"; the wiki treats this as their name for the Array. In the lines read, they say these are big, hard and sometimes shoot fire from their eyes, and that one broke free when a stone lock was shattered.
### LARGE +
The Archive compares its cubic form to the hypostases, which also behave in near-mechanical ways, but offers no claim of kinship.

## chronology
### ULTRA
- 2.0: the Perpetual Mechanical Array arrives at Araumi with Inazuma.
- 2.1: a bug with Geo constructs is fixed.
- 3.1: ATK is increased and damage multipliers reduced.

## status
### STANDARD
As of Version 7.1 the Array remains a permanent boss in Inazuma. No Nod-Krai (6.x) or Snezhnaya (7.0-7.1) story content adds to its lore, and its builders and purpose are still unstated.

## entry: Perpetual Heart
@keys: Perpetual Heart
@version: 2.0
### STANDARD
The Array's boss drop. The description calls it the core powering the Array's autonomous motion, and passes on theories that its engraved symbols record knowledge and logic in an unseen language, and that these Ruin machines chose strength of function over a biomimetic shape.

## entry: Araumi
@keys: Araumi
@keys2: Perpetual Mechanical Array, Narukami Island
@version: 2.0
### STANDARD
A promontory at the northern point of Narukami Island, cut off from the rest by Mt. Yougou and full of the ruins of an ancient city that extend under the earth and sea. Much of it is underwater; a path from here leads to the Perpetual Mechanical Array and the Empty Boat of a Thousand Gates domain.
