## summary
### LITE
The Jadeplume Terrorshroom is a large bird-like fungal beast, the lord of fungal beasts, living in Vissudha Field in Sumeru's rainforest. Its boss titles are The Beloved King of Fungi and Deepwood Shroomlord. It attacks with Dendro spores and its beak, and it is a normal boss.
### STANDARD +
Its Archive entry says the fungal beast has morphed over many long years into a unique form, hunts actively for food, and has a tail of jade plumage. It was added in version 3.0.
### LARGE +
Its description card, titled Murkwood Fungal Raptor, calls it the proud lord of fungal beasts and says it dreams of covering the whole world in spores.

## nature
### LITE
A fungal beast shaped like a large bird with a beak and a jade-coloured tail. It lives at the heart of the woods in the company of its kin.
### STANDARD +
The Handbook says fungi grow wherever it is damp and fertile, adapt to adversity and spread their spores wherever there is room. It remarks that the beast may, given time, evolve into something even more magnificent.
### LARGE +
The Archive says its intelligence has reached, and perhaps surpassed, that of real animals; it hunts prey actively rather than only feeding passively.

## lore
@version: 3.0
### STANDARD
The Archive notes that its jade tail plumage closely resembles that of the ancient sacred beast revered by the Spantamad school. Reasoning from mimesis, it suggests the two creatures may once have walked the earth together.
### LARGE +
It is a Mystical Beast of the Fungi group, a Dendro creature of the Sumeru rainforest.

## abilities
### STANDARD
Game mechanics (labelled as such): it deals Dendro damage. Its normal attacks are a three-hit combo, a barrage of homing projectiles from tail swipes and a front flip, and a Volatile Spore Cloud ignited by a spark from its beak.
### LARGE +
It has a Fury bar. Electro attacks and the Quicken, Aggravate and Spread reactions fill it, and Pyro lowers it. When full, it becomes activated and uses scattered homing spores, a double charge or rapid pecks, then is paralysed for a time with lowered resistance. Burning it while it is not activated makes it Scorched: it fires three spores that, unless destroyed, explode and become Scorched Floating Dendro Fungi to fight alongside it. Its drop is Majestic Hooked Beak.
### ULTRA +
Up to four summoned Fungi can exist at once. These mechanics and numbers come from the wiki and are game values rather than lore.

## individuals
@version: 4.6
### STANDARD
The Jadeplume Terrorshroom of Vissudha Field is the open-world boss. The Metastable Jadeplume Terrorshroom, titled Hates Pyro Regisvine Fruit, is a different encounter in the limited-time event Specially-Shaped Saurian Search.
### LARGE +
It also exists as a character card in the Genius Invokation TCG, which is a game mode rather than a story appearance.

## relations
### STANDARD
It rules the lesser Floating Dendro Fungi and other fungi in its forest, according to the Handbook's description of it as lord of the fungal beasts. Its tail is compared to the Spantamad school's sacred beast.

## chronology
### ULTRA
- 3.0: the boss is added to Sumeru with the rainforest.
- 4.6: the Metastable variant appears in the event Specially-Shaped Saurian Search (version per the event page's change history).

## status
### STANDARD
As of 7.1, the Jadeplume Terrorshroom remains a boss in Sumeru's rainforest. Its origin and any link to the Spantamad's sacred beast remain unconfirmed.

## entry: Fungi
@keys: Floating Dendro Fungus, Floating Dendro Fungi
@version: 3.0
### STANDARD
Floating Dendro Fungi are the small fungal creatures that accompany the Jadeplume Terrorshroom when it is Scorched. The Terrorshroom is described as their lord.
