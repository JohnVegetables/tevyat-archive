## summary
### LITE
Regisvines are giant plant monsters: Whopperflowers that grew huge and rooted in one place, trading their agility for a more refined store of one element. They are normal bosses, found as a Cryo Regisvine in Mondstadt, a Pyro Regisvine in Liyue and an Electro Regisvine in Sumeru. Each starts the fight inside an elemental shield at its roots or its corolla, which raises its resistances sharply until it is broken.
### STANDARD +
The name probably blends the Latin regis (of a king) with vine, meaning king's vine; this is the wiki's guess, not an official explanation. The three bosses drop their own core material and element gems. As Mystical Beasts, they are strongly affected by the element they embody but are not made purely of it, unlike elemental lifeforms.
### LARGE +
Their appearance in Teyvat is tied to ley lines. The Archive and Handbook texts present them as plants that soaked up ley line energy over many years, and some researchers suppose plants are the world's organs that harmonise turbulent elements.

## nature
### LITE
The Adventurer Handbook says a Whopperflower that survives many years with enough nutrition and the right environment could take root and grow into a giant tree that sways and spews elemental power. Regisvines are that stage: tall vines with a flower-like head (the corolla) and lashing tendrils, keeping to their roots instead of burrowing around as Whopperflowers do.
### STANDARD +
The Cryo text describes a vine imbued with the essence of biting frost in the ley lines. The Pyro text describes a giant vine that absorbed the ancient flame in the ley lines and seems restless, as if filled with endless fury. The Electro text describes a huge vine changed by high-voltage current conducted through the ley lines, with electricity surging from its canopy.
### LARGE +
The material item texts are written in a brooding, poetic mood. The Hoarfrost Core is described as a core of pure Cryo that shows how deathly silent frost can turn ordinary vines into vicious predators. The Everflame Seed fuels the Pyro Regisvine's unending flames and prompts the question of what desire could hold such fire. The Thunderclap Fruitcore is wrapped in razor-sharp leaves, and its text wonders whether the plant longs to inflict pain or is struggling toward its own end. Treat all of this as in-world musing, not a statement about what the plants think.

## abilities
### LITE
Game mechanics (labelled as such): a Regisvine's shield, or core, sits at its roots first. Breaking it with elemental reactions paralyses the Regisvine and strips its raised resistances. When it wakes, the core moves to the corolla, and breaking it again exposes a weak point. If the corolla core is left alone for too long, the core returns to the roots.
### STANDARD +
Each uses its own element. The Cryo Regisvine flings ice beams and homing icicles, slams down in a charge, spins to freeze the ground around it, and calls a blizzard if its core stays intact. The Pyro Regisvine slams its face in a locked-on charge that sends tremors through the ground, and fires fireballs from its buds. The Electro Regisvine is different: its single shield shifts between corolla and roots, and each shift produces a Waning or Waxing Stamen that attract each other into a large explosion if both are left standing. It also uses laser sweeps and thunderbolts. Pyro is the most effective element against the Cryo Regisvine's shield.
### LARGE +
Ability names are unofficial wiki labels. The Cryo Regisvine's rotary beam was weakened against repeat hits in 3.1.

## individuals
### STANDARD
Cryo Regisvine (1.0): a normal boss in Starfell Valley, Mondstadt, titled variously Giant Frostbreath Calamus, Evercold Iceflower and Silent Frost Bloom; it drops the Hoarfrost Core. Pyro Regisvine (1.0): a normal boss at Cuijue Slope in Minlin, Liyue, titled Giant Everblaze Calamus, Flickering Blaze Bloom and Raging Fireflower; it drops the Everflame Seed. Electro Regisvine (3.0): a normal boss at Mawtiyima Forest in Lokapala Jungle, Sumeru, titled Giant Sparksurge Calamus, Mournful Rumbleflower and Thunderous Blast Bloom; it drops the Thunderclap Fruitcore.
### LARGE +
Two event-only variants exist. The Resurgent Cryo Regisvine appeared in the limited-time event The Chalk Prince and the Dragon (1.2) and in the Uninvited Guests story quest: a sick Reckless Pallad stumbled to the sword, knocked it over, and its restorative power passed into the stump of a dead Cryo Regisvine, which revived it. The Sky-Searing Pyro Regisvine appeared in the limited-time event Specially-Shaped Saurian Search (4.6) as a Pyro boss with a nucleus at its corolla that is weakest to Hydro.

## relations
### STANDARD
They belong to the Mystical Beasts family alongside Whopperflowers, vishaps and others. The Archive notes that Kshahrewar researchers once proposed a circuit matrix around an Electro Regisvine to carry its electrical energy to the city, and that scholars of Spantamad, who opposed this, sacrificed ten Shroomboars to illustrate the danger of utilising electricity. The Electro Handbook text also hopes the Regisvine's presence reflects the earth's wish for lush forests in Sumeru.
### LARGE +
The Pyro Regisvine's fruit is "pretty good" and tastes like Pyro, according to Ranjit in the world quest Search Chronicle: Cold, Round Belly!, as the wiki reports. The Cryo Regisvine is nicknamed "We Could Make A Cold Dish?" in the quest Secret Ingredient.

## entry: Cryo Regisvine
@keys: Cryo Regisvine, Hoarfrost Core
@version: 1.0
### STANDARD
A Cryo normal boss in Starfell Valley, Mondstadt, born of a vine imbued with the essence of biting frost in the ley lines. Its core is first at the roots, then at the corolla. It drops the Hoarfrost Core and Shivada Jade gems.
### LARGE +
The Handbook says some studies suggest plants act as the world's organs, harmonising turbulent elements in ley lines, naming Mist Flowers and Whopperflowers as examples that brim with elemental energy.

## entry: Pyro Regisvine
@keys: Pyro Regisvine, Everflame Seed
@version: 1.0
### STANDARD
A Pyro normal boss at Cuijue Slope in Minlin, Liyue. Its text calls it restless and furious; poets, bards and some academics are said to believe that elements hold emotions and hopes. It drops the Everflame Seed and Agnidus Agate gems.

## entry: Electro Regisvine
@keys: Electro Regisvine, Thunderclap Fruitcore, Rumbleflower
@version: 3.0
### STANDARD
An Electro normal boss at Mawtiyima Forest in Lokapala Jungle, Sumeru. Its quest description tells of thunder rolling on a sunny day, leading to a strangely shaped plant that does not blend in. The Archive records the Kshahrewar proposal to harness it and the opposing Spantamad demonstration. It drops the Thunderclap Fruitcore and Vajrada Amethyst gems.

## entry: Resurgent Cryo Regisvine
@keys: Resurgent Cryo Regisvine, Reckless Pallad
@keys2: Regisvine, Chalk Prince, Dragonspine
@version: 1.2
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
A one-off event enemy near the Entombed City outskirts in Dragonspine. During the event The Chalk Prince and the Dragon, Sucrose, Albedo and the Traveler found Reckless Pallad suffering from hypothermia. He stumbled toward the sword thinking it a heat source and knocked it down, and its restorative power transferred to the stump of a dead Cryo Regisvine. The result is protected by Frost Thorns and releases Seeds of Winter that must be broken to produce protective shields.
