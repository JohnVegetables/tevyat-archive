## summary
### LITE
Slimes are pudgy elemental lifeforms found all over Teyvat, made of condensed elemental energy. They come in the seven elements and in small and large sizes, are immune to their own element, and bump, leap and slam. Canon calls them lowly creatures of minimal intelligence, but people find uses for them: hilichurls throw them, chefs cook with them, and engineers build with them.
### STANDARD
Slimes are the most common Elemental Lifeforms. The Adventurer Handbook calls them pudgy elemental life forms and notes that desserts made from slimes of different elements are said to taste distinctly different. Each is made of a single element (Anemo, Cryo, Dendro, Electro, Geo, Hydro or Pyro), is immune to it, and keeps it applied to itself. Small slimes mainly bump into things; large slimes have a wider range of attacks, all including a leap and slam. Since 6.7 (Luna VIII) a unique Prism Slime lives in the Lunar Highlands of Nod-Krai.
### LARGE +
In practice they are Teyvat's ubiquitous background fauna: dull, drawn to elemental sources, and mostly a nuisance until ignored too long. They show up in settings from open fields to the Ruin Guard factory, where Electro Slimes powered machinery.
### ULTRA +
Use them as everyday colour or a vehicle for Teyvat's elemental texture rather than as characters; no slime has a name, a voice or a recorded motive in canon.

## nature
### LITE
Slimes are made of condensed elemental energy. The kind of energy fixes the element, and its concentration fixes the size. They gather in groups in places that match their element.
### STANDARD
Slimes, like other Elemental Lifeforms, are made of condensed elemental energy: the type determines the element and the concentration the size (per the Large Anemo Slime Archive entry). They are usually found in environments matching their element and tend to gather in groups. They must take in elemental energy to survive, so they are drawn to potent sources such as Mist Flower extract, which characters in some quests use as "Elemental Bait". The Loading Screen text calls them lowly life forms of minimal intelligence, and Breeze Amidst the Forest (vol. I) says life that arises from the elements either descends into a slime or ascends into a crystalfly.
### LARGE +
Large slimes are denser: the Large Anemo Slime Archive text says their greater concentration of Anemo gives them stronger flotation than small Anemo Slimes. The Mutant Electro Slime is a variant that can discharge through Electro Slimes near it.
### ULTRA +
Anemo and Dendro slimes are mysteriously absent on Inazuma's main islands, though Large Anemo Slimes appear at Watatsumi Island commissions and some Ley Line Outcrops, and Dendro Slimes appear in Enkanomiya (considered apart from Inazuma). Canon offers no reason.

## abilities
### LITE
Slimes bump into people; large slimes also leap and slam. They shrug off their own element and carry it, so reactions with it can still happen. (Gameplay note.)
### STANDARD
Game mechanics, for reference: slimes are immune to their element and have it applied constantly. Small slimes primarily bump. Large slimes vary their attacks by element, and all share a leap-and-slam. In scene terms, a Pyro slime burns, a Cryo slime chills, an Anemo slime floats, an Electro slime shocks.
### LARGE +
Hilichurls use Large Anemo Slimes as cargo balloons because of their flotation; as a gameplay quirk, a player who jumps on one just before its Wind Blast is launched skyward. Mitachurls and grenadiers use slimes as projectiles and as elemental infusions (see relations).
### ULTRA +
The Prism Slime (6.7) is the exception: in its open-world encounter one slime of each element is placed near it and it inhales them at the start of the fight. It drains Energy from the active character to take on that element, becoming immune to it, and when charged it enters a Light-Gathering state in which it grows, hits harder, and loses its elemental alignment and resistance. All of this is gameplay description and should be translated into narration loosely.

## relations
### LITE
Hilichurls use slimes as weapons, balloons, and refrigerators. Chefs, engineers and tinkerers treat them as a material.
### STANDARD
Humans use slimes experimentally: Garcia made a slime-based lubricant for his Numbering Machine, and Akim uses slime as a main ingredient of his clay-like building material "Concresoil". Amber uses Pyro Slime Condensate for her Baron Bunny, and the operators of the ruin-guard factory used Electro Slimes to power machinery. Hilichurls use small slimes to make explosive barrels, Cryo slimes to preserve food, and Large Anemo Slimes as cargo balloons; grenadiers and mitachurls throw slimes, and lawachurls throw larger ones, switching to smaller ones if their elemental shield is broken.
### LARGE +
Slime is also used in cooking, though most people balk at eating it; slime-containing foods are often passed off as agar-based because they are extremely similar. Escoffier says the culinary world has been interested in elemental lifeforms for centuries, calling Hydro Slimes flavourless and Pyro Slimes spicy with a hint of intense sweetness. Xiangling often experiments with slime, her better-received results including Sugar-Frosted Slime and a "Salt and Pepper Slime" that inspired Kuki Shinobu's Roasted Lavender Melon.
### ULTRA +
Two playful details are worth knowing. Certain hilichurl rogues use Anemo slimes to float in the air to attack, or Hydro ones to launch projectiles before throwing them, and wooden shieldwall mitachurls build shields with Dendro slimes. In the Tears of Themis HoYoLand event (an out-of-game tie-in), slimes appear among the park's mascots; this is not game canon.

## entry: Prism Slime
@keys: Prism Slime, Creation's Afterglow
@version: 6.7
### STANDARD
The Prism Slime (title "Creation's Afterglow") was added in version 6.7 as a Local Legend in the Lunar Highlands of Nod-Krai; reaching it requires finishing the Pioneers (Part 2) quest. It is the only slime not found in Teyvat proper. It adopts the element of whoever fights it, inhales the seven elemental slimes around it, and, once charged, swells into a light-gathering state. Its Valiant Chronicles description says that the first element was once scattered like light through a prism until the Creator separated it into seven forms, and that on this corner of the moon elemental forms from the mortal world can be steeped in flawless source-light to retrace their origins and return to the primordial unity, "perhaps by the same principle" as the primordial light said to reverse destruction.
### LARGE +
The trivia note that it is the first slime without a landing indicator on its jump slam is gameplay-only. Whether the Prism Slime is a particular slime or a type is unclear; the wiki lists it as a single Local Legend.

## entry: Elemental Bait
@keys: Elemental Bait, Mist Flower extract
@keys2: slime, slimes
### STANDARD
Slimes need to extract elemental energy to survive and are irresistibly drawn to potent sources such as Mist Flower extract. Characters in the Darknight Hero's Alibi and Astrology and the 50-Year Pact quests use these as "Elemental Bait" to lure slimes.

## entry: Slime cuisine
@keys: Tasty Slime Condensate, Sugar-Frosted Slime, Salt and Pepper Slime, slime condensate
### STANDARD
Slime is sometimes used in cooking; the item text for Tasty Slime Condensate notes that slime-containing foods are usually passed off as agar-based because most people balk at eating it. Escoffier and Xiangling have both experimented with slime, and a "Salt and Pepper Slime" of Xiangling's inspired Kuki Shinobu's Roasted Lavender Melon (Shinobu's Character Story).

## entry: Hilichurls and slimes
@keys: hilichurl slime, slime barrel, explosive barrel, slime balloon
@keys2: hilichurl, hilichurls, Mitachurl, Lawachurl
### STANDARD
Hilichurls make explosive barrels from small slimes, keep food cold with Cryo slimes, ride Anemo slimes into the air, and carry cargo with Large Anemo Slimes used as balloons (as seen in the Dangerous Haul quest). In fights, grenadiers and mitachurls throw slimes; mitachurls infuse axes with Pyro or Electro slimes or build Dendro slime shields; lawachurls throw larger slimes and switch to smaller ones if their elemental shield is broken.
