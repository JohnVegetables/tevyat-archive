## summary
### LITE
Elemental Lifeforms are creatures made of pure, condensed elemental energy rather than flesh and blood. Each is bound to one element and is immune to it. The family covers the humble slimes, the floating specters, the cube-shelled hypostases (the purest of them), the Tainted Hydro Phantasms of Fontaine, and the Frostnight Scions of Nod-Krai. Canon treats them as dangerous phenomena of living element, not as people.
### STANDARD
Elemental Lifeforms are creatures made of condensed elemental energy rather than flesh. Each is tied to a single element, carries that element as part of its body, and cannot be harmed by it. The family includes slimes (small, common and found everywhere), specters (floating sprites that can fly into a rage), hypostases (ultra-dense, cube-shelled constructs at the highest level of elemental purity), the Tainted Hydro Phantasms that took the place of the Oceanids in Fontaine's waters, and the Frostnight Scions, born of the Frost Moon's blessings in Nod-Krai.
### LARGE +
They are distinct from "mystical beasts" such as Vishaps, Fungi and Whopperflowers, which are creatures strongly affected by an element but not made of pure elemental energy. Nothing in canon grants elemental lifeforms language or culture, though hilichurls, scholars and cooks have all found uses for them.
### ULTRA +
Because they are literally made of element, they show where elemental energy is dense: the Akademiya ties hypostases to places bursting with elemental energy or to clogged ley lines, and Fontaine's Phantasms are described as having replaced the Oceanids. Treat claims that they feel, remember or plan as unproven unless a source says so; the Dendro Hypostasis's Archive text only suggests that hypostases can "learn".

## nature
### LITE
Elemental Lifeforms are enemies made purely of one element, to which they are immune. Some apply their own element to themselves, so reactions with that element can still occur on them. Power and size track how much elemental energy went into them.
### STANDARD
Elemental Lifeforms are made purely of one element and are immune to it. Many keep that element applied to themselves at all times, so reactions with other elements still work on them. Their elemental energy is also their body: slimes are condensed elemental energy, with the type of energy fixing the element and the concentration fixing the size. An in-world book (Breeze Amidst the Forest, vol. I) observes that when life arises from the elements it either descends and becomes a slime or ascends and becomes a crystalfly. The loading-screen line calls slimes lowly life forms of minimal intelligence that become a bigger problem if ignored.

Higher forms are more structured: hypostases are said to have abandoned their former appearance and biological structure entirely, becoming compact, heavy structures with a shell of concentrated element around a reactive core.
### LARGE +
Specters (Adventurer Handbook) are monsters born from highly concentrated elements that can float, little sprites of gentle temperament, much like slimes, that can suddenly explode into rage. Frostnight Scions are described as elegant, born of the Frost Moon's blessings and symbolising the pure source and the first radiance, shaped by long exposure to high concentrations of elemental energy. Tainted Hydro Phantasms are described as elemental life that arose from Fontaine's waters and replaced the elegant Oceanids.
### ULTRA +
The Valiant Chronicles text for the Prism Slime (version 6.7) gives the family a cosmological frame: the first element was once dispersed like light through a prism until the Creator separated it into seven forms, and in one corner of the Frost Moon elemental forms from the mortal world can be steeped in "flawless source-light" and return to the primordial state in which all things were one. This is the only in-world statement linking elemental lifeforms to the original unity of the elements; handle it as an in-world legend, not a confirmed mechanism.

## abilities
### LITE
Each lifeform fights with its own element and shrugs off that element. Slimes bump, leap and slam; specters float and explode; hypostases hide in shells and expose a core only when they attack; Phantasms and Scions grow stronger when enraged.
### STANDARD
Game mechanics, for reference (gameplay, not lore): specters have a Fury meter that fills from heavy hits and turns them enraged and larger, and they charge up and explode when defeated; hypostases are invulnerable while their cube shell surrounds them and can be hurt only when the diamond core is exposed, usually after an attack, and at low health they enter a revival phase in which they spawn elemental objects that must be dealt with in time; Tainted Hydro Phantasms periodically enrage, grow larger, and then ignore Frozen; Frostnight Scions periodically enter a Gloomveiled state in which they swap immunity for strong resistance and must be forced out of it with their own element or Lunar reaction damage.
### LARGE +
In narration, translate these into scene terms: a Pyro lifeform leaves heat and scorched ground, a Cryo one frost, a hypostasis moves like a slow heavy machine that reveals its heart only mid-strike, a specter drifts and then flares. Reactions still make sense in scene (a Hydro creature frozen solid is plausible; a Hydro creature "drowned" is not).
### ULTRA +
Frostnight Herra, a normal boss on Hiisi Island in Nod-Krai, starts elementless and takes on immunity to the element it received the most damage from while Gloomveiled (unless the damage was mostly physical or Lunar), a rare case where a lifeform's alignment is shaped by the fight itself.

## types
### LITE
Slimes (small and large, all seven elements; the Prism Slime is a rare special case), specters, hypostases, Tainted Hydro Phantasms, Frostnight Scions.
### STANDARD
- Slimes: common enemies found all over Teyvat in Anemo, Cryo, Dendro, Electro, Geo, Hydro and Pyro forms, small and large. See their own entry.
- Specters: common enemies in Inazuma, Enkanomiya and Sumeru, first appearing in version 2.1. On Watatsumi Island they are considered pests; Kokomi has a Specter Eco-Management group (mentioned in a limited-time event quest).
- Hypostases: normal bosses at the highest elemental purity. All seven elements exist; the last, Dendro, arrived in version 3.2. They are found in Mondstadt, Liyue, Inazuma and Sumeru.
- Tainted Hydro Phantasms: elite enemies of Fontaine and Liyue, permanently Hydro, introduced in 4.0.
- Frostnight Scions: elite enemies of Nod-Krai in seven elemental forms, introduced in 6.0; the boss Frostnight Herra followed in 6.1.
### LARGE +
The Eye of the Storm, an Anemo elite that makes Vacuum Fields, is also listed in this family, and the wiki's family gallery also shows the Oceanid (Rhodeia of Loch), the Thunder Manifestation and the Hydro Tulpa. Mutant Electro Slimes are a variant that discharges through nearby Electro Slimes. The Prism Slime (6.7) is a Local Legend in the Lunar Highlands, unlocked through the Pioneers (Part 2) quest. It inhales slimes of all seven elements and, unlike other slimes, switches its alignment to the element of the active character; the wiki notes it is the only slime not found in Teyvat.
### ULTRA +
The wiki also lists "Pure" hypostases as quest- and event-exclusive variants. Hypostases carry codenames drawn from the Hebrew alphabet (see the codenames entry).

## relations
### LITE
Hilichurls throw and ride slimes and use them for shields and weapon infusions. Scholars, cooks and builders find uses for them. Fontaine's Oceanids were replaced by Phantasms.
### STANDARD
Hilichurls exploit lifeforms: grenadiers dig up and throw slimes; some mitachurls infuse axes with Pyro or Electro slimes or build wooden shields from Dendro slimes; rogues ride Anemo slimes or fling Hydro ones; Large Anemo Slimes serve as cargo balloons. Slimes are drawn to strong elemental sources, and Mist Flower extract works as "Elemental Bait". The Sumeru Akademiya leads research on hypostases, but because of their danger little is known beyond scientific names and code names.
### LARGE +
Archive text for the Dendro Hypostasis says some researchers deduce that hypostases can "learn" and use "mimesis" to attack, and warns that a being that learns risks information contamination. Chefs such as Escoffier and Xiangling treat slimes as ingredients; Escoffier calls Hydro Slimes flavourless and Pyro Slimes spicy with intense sweetness.
### ULTRA +
Fontaine's Tainted Hydro Phantasms are linked to the Oceanids only by the Adventurer Handbook line that they replaced them; do not make them former Oceanids.

### STANDARD
In the Adventurer Handbook each hypostasis carries a codename taken from a letter of the Hebrew alphabet: Electro is Aleph, Anemo is Beth, Geo is Gimel, Cryo is Daleth, Hydro is He, Dendro is Zayin and Pyro is Ayin. The sixth letter is skipped and Ayin used instead. Descriptions open with lines such as the Dendro Hypostasis's "Code Name: Zayin".
### LARGE +
The codenames are descriptive labels only; canon does not say who assigned them or why a letter is skipped.

## entry: Mystical Beasts versus Elemental Lifeforms
@keys: Mystical Beast, Mystical Beasts
@keys2: elemental lifeform, elemental lifeforms
### STANDARD
Mystical beasts are creatures strongly shaped by an element and generally more resistant to the element they represent, but they are not made of pure elemental energy. Examples in this group include Vishaps, Fungi, Whopperflowers, Consecrated Beasts and Fontemer Aberrants. Elemental Lifeforms, by contrast, are pure elemental energy given form.

## entry: Gloomveiled state
@keys: Gloomveiled, Gloomveiled state
@keys2: Frostnight Scion, Frostnight Herra, Scion, Herra
@version: 6.0
### STANDARD
Frostnight Scions and the boss Frostnight Herra periodically withdraw into a Gloomveiled state in Nod-Krai. In it they use stronger attacks but trade elemental immunity for a high resistance, and they can be broken out of it by their corresponding element or by Lunar reaction damage. The Fury meter shown in this state is a gameplay readout, not a lore concept.
### LARGE +
Frostnight Herra, the Guardian of the Holy Light in the boss listing, starts elementless and takes on immunity to the element it suffered most while Gloomveiled (unless that damage was mostly physical or Lunar).
