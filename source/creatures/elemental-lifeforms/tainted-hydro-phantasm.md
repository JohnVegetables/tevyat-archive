## summary
### LITE
Tainted Hydro Phantasms are elite Hydro elemental lifeforms, strange water spirits that arose in Fontaine's waters after the Oceanids were gone. The Archive says they lack the elegance and beauty of the Oceanids, which is why they are called tainted. They come in two forms, the Water-Splitting and the Water-Spouting Phantasm.
### STANDARD +
They are permanently imbued with Hydro and shrug off Hydro damage. They are hostile to travelers; a harbor request in Fontaine records a visitor from Liyue so scared of "water that attacks people" that he refused to leave his ship.
### LARGE +
Wiki sources place the group in both Fontaine and Liyue. The Archive's wording stresses that they replaced the Oceanids and are not a continuation of them.

## nature
### LITE
Elemental lifeforms made of Hydro, born from Fontaine's waters. Their Archive entries call them "tainted" because they came after the calamity and lack the beauty and grace of the Oceanids.
### STANDARD +
The Archive states that with the Oceanids gone from Fontaine, strange elemental lifeforms have appeared in the waters. It adds that the toxins once dissolved in the water have long been diluted and cleansed, yet unless containers of pure water still survive somewhere, Fontaine's waters will never again naturally give birth to Oceanids. A material description adds that the life in the water is formless, and that formless life takes the shape of the water that holds it.
### LARGE +
The second Archive text recalls Egeria's era, when Oceanids lived among humans in clear springs and linked the waters of the world; most died with their god, and for survivors no god they recognise remains. The Phantasms are the opposite image: lifeforms with no god or lineage in the record. Leftover Phantasm water still carries life force, according to the drop and scoop descriptions.
### ULTRA +
Canon never says why the waters yield Phantasms and not Oceanids, or whether the "toxins" mean the taint attributed to Elynas's death. Treat a Phantasm as a wild lifeform, with no known language, name or purpose.

## abilities
### LITE
They fire bubbles of water and, once enraged, grow larger and hit harder. They are permanently Hydro and immune to Hydro damage.
### STANDARD +
Gameplay (labelled, not lore): the Water-Splitting Phantasm uses fists, a spinning arm and, up close, an arm that becomes a fish to slap. The Water-Spouting Phantasm sends a stream of water and large projectiles, and inflates itself to burst outward when someone is close. Both periodically enter an enraged state, grow in size and become immune to Frozen; freezing one for too long forces the rage. In the Genius Invokation TCG, a Phantasm monster card also never freezes and is always Hydro-applied.
### LARGE +
Local Legend versions, Ninianne of the Lake (Water-Splitting, north of the skeletal remains in Elynas) and Vivianne of the Lake (Water-Spouting, northeast of the Echoes of the Deep Tides domain), stay permanently enraged and add splashes to their attacks.
### ULTRA +
The wiki's Local Legend names are the only individual names in the group. Canon does not say the creatures use them for themselves.

## relations
### LITE
They replaced the Oceanids in Fontaine's waters; the Archive explicitly contrasts the two. Sigewinne once needed an extract from a Water-Splitting Phantasm among the ingredients for a task.
### STANDARD +
In the quest "Beauty Expert" (4.0), Paimon and the Traveler seek a special ingredient on Sigewinne's list. It was a Tainted Hydro Phantasm Extract, obtained by defeating a Tainted Water-Splitting Phantasm. In the weekly request "Hydro Phantasm Havoc", Lectoure asks for the Phantasms to be dealt with so a frightened passenger will leave the ship.
### LARGE +
Hydro Eidolons of the limited-time Veluriyam Mirage area are a different creature, despite the similar name. The Phantasms are also distinct from the Hydro Mimics that Oceanids create and from the boss Hydro Tulpa.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 Tainted Hydro Phantasms remain ordinary hostile lifeforms in Fontaine's waters. No later content found reveals their origin, and none was found in Nod-Krai or Snezhnaya.

@keys2: Oceanid, Oceanids, Fontaine, Egeria

### STANDARD
The Archive presents the Phantasms as what filled the waters after the Oceanids were extirpated from Fontaine. They lack the Oceanids' elegance, and the waters will not give birth to Oceanids again unless pure water survives. Most Oceanids died with Egeria, and survivors left. No source says the two are related by descent.
