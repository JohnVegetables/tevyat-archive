## summary
### LITE
Oceanids, also called the Lochfolk, are beings of pure Hydro born in Fontaine. They are devotees of Egeria, the first Hydro Archon, and can float, command water, and shape it into lifelike mimics. Many took human form, and the Fontainians' own ancestry is tied to that change. Some Oceanids left Fontaine long ago and now live scattered across Teyvat, such as the Spring Fairy in Mondstadt.
### STANDARD +
Under Elemental Sight even a human-looking Oceanid shows as Hydro-afflicted. They are usually shown as female, but the species is treated as technically genderless.
### LARGE +
The English and Thai texts use both names. A Thai translation note says Oceanid is what outsiders call the Lochfolk, but the English text never explains the difference, so treat it as a naming nuance, not a division into two peoples.

## nature
### LITE
Oceanids are made of pure Hydro energy. They are born from the essence of water and are affected by the water they are tied to: hostility can turn their connected water bitter, while sweet water heals them.
### STANDARD +
Origin legend, from the Ancient Natural History Excerpts in the Institute of Natural Philosophy: Egeria shed a first tear, which became the first Oceanid, and every other Oceanid descends from it. Egeria sent Oceanids across Teyvat as spies, not out of malice but so that, like water linking all lands, everyone would be connected.

Growth works unlike other creatures. An Oceanid gains understanding by joining with water, whether a pond, a spring or even tears. One of the richest sources is water creatures made by another Oceanid. Gazing at the moon's reflection in water also raises their understanding. Leaving their homeland and the company of others dulls their intellect and power, though both can return with time.
### LARGE +
Oceanids may merge into a single, stronger Oceanid, which most regard as normal or even welcome, though Ann is upset by it. Rhodeia warns that a mind can be shattered if it joins with a smaller but wicked one. Reproduction is a secret kept by Oceanids; the Excerpts say they must ask the Archon or an elder for leave to claim the Helixsplit, which lets them split into little Oceanids. Caterpillar guesses that imagination and mimicry are involved, since Ann came into being when the merged minds of Lyris and Mary-Ann Guillotin dreamed of living forever in a simple fairytale world.
### ULTRA +
Like the Melusines, Oceanids are treated as a genderless people who are nearly always shown as female. Hydro Mimics and Eidolons appear male only when imitating a specific human. The Master of the Narzissenkreuz Ordo, an Oceanid created by a human, is the first known male-presenting one, and he may not be of the original race. The wiki notes that the design resembles real-world sea angels; that is a possible inspiration, not canon.

## society
### LITE
Oceanids revere Egeria, whom they regard as their master. Most no longer live in Fontaine.
### STANDARD +
The Lochfolk's story is also an origin story of Fontaine's people. Some Oceanids saw humans and envied them, and asked Egeria for the same life. She drew water from the Primordial Sea and injected it into their vessels, giving them human form. The Heavenly Principles counted this a sin and issued the Prophecy of Fontaine: that the people would dissolve into water and leave the Hydro Archon weeping alone on her throne.

After Egeria died in the Cataclysm, the Oceanids left Fontaine. Idyia says it was because the waters had turned full of pain and hatred. Rhodeia implies that she and some others left because they refused to accept Focalors as the new Archon and wished to keep the old dreams alive. The coming and death of Elynas is also said to have tainted Fontaine's land and water.
### LARGE +
During this exile the Oceanids came to Tunigi Hollow looking for their master, but found that the Harvisptokhm was not what they had hoped, and scattered in different directions. People later followed their trail and established the Purification Spring domain.

Oceanids who live away from Fontaine are solitary or in small groups: Callirhoe the Spring Fairy in Springvale, Rhodeia of Loch on the Bishui Plain in Liyue, Idyia in the Veluriyam Mirage, and Ann traveling with Mary-Ann and companions. Their wellbeing is tied to local water and to each other's moods, as in the quarrel between Rhodeia and Endora.
### ULTRA +
The Narzissenkreuz Ordo's legend mentions a fell dragon called Narcissus appearing as an Oceanid in the Annapausis; this is likely an illusion and not a true Oceanid.

## abilities
### LITE
Oceanids float through the air at considerable speed and manipulate water, firing Hydro attacks and creating water creatures (Hydro Mimics).
### STANDARD +
They can imitate a human form out of water, as Idyia and Callirhoe do, though they still read as Hydro under Elemental Sight. Very powerful Oceanids who have both wisdom and compassion can produce manifestations that replicate particular humans; Idyia did this with her past human companions in the Veluriyam Mirage.
### LARGE +
Callirhoe's blessing is the reason Diona's drinks always taste good, however strange their ingredients. It is unclear whether other Oceanids can bestow blessings, since none other is shown to.

Their limit is that they are strongly shaped by the water and company around them. Separation from the Lochfolk weakens them, hostility sours their water, and merging with a malicious mind risks their own.

## relations
### LITE
Close to Egeria in origin. Fontaine's people descend from transformed Oceanids; Focalors began as one of Egeria's Oceanid familiars before becoming Archon.
### STANDARD +
Focalors was among the Oceanids Egeria turned human, then rose to godhood as the second and last Hydro Archon, and was executed in the events of the Opera of Noirceur and Blancheur. After her divinity merged into the Oratrice Mecanique d'Analyse Cardinale, the body and spirit she left behind became Furina, who ruled Fontaine in her place.

When Focalors's death destroyed the Hydro Archon's divine throne, Neuvillette's Authority as the Hydro Dragon Sovereign was restored. He then absolved the descendants of the transformed Oceanids, including Furina, making them true humans. People caught in the primordial waters after his verdict did not dissolve, but those who dissolved before it were lost.
### LARGE +
Fontainians who dissolved in Primordial Sea water as it escaped their vessels returned to the Sea but kept their consciousness, taking Oceanid form. Victims of Vacher and of the Poisson flood merged into collective Oceanids this way. Whether they share any other Oceanid ability is not stated.

## individuals
### LITE
Callirhoe, the Spring Fairy of Springvale in Mondstadt; Rhodeia of Loch on the Bishui Plain in Liyue; Idyia in the Veluriyam Mirage; Endora, who accompanies the Traveler; Ann and Mary-Ann; and Focalors, the former Hydro Archon.
### STANDARD +
Others: Ianeira, said to have died in Natlan (per a weapon description); Lyris, who merged with Mary-Ann Guillotin to become Mary-Ann; Narzissenkreuz, who died in the Rowboat's Wake story; and Urania, the Hateful Oceanid who appeared in a Legend of the Vagabond Sword event domain at Yaoguang Shoal.
### LARGE +
Urania appeared only in a limited-time event domain, so most characters would not know of it.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 no new Oceanid story was found in the Nod-Krai (6.x) or Snezhnaya (7.0-7.1) content. Oceanids remain scattered across Teyvat, and Fontaine's people have been absolved as true humans.

### STANDARD

Neuvillette later absolved the descendants of those transformed Oceanids. This is knowledge from the Ancient Natural History Excerpts and the Archon Quest, not common knowledge in Fontaine.

### STANDARD
Oceanids learn by joining with water, tears or the creations of other Oceanids, and may merge together into one stronger Oceanid. Merging is usually seen as natural but can shatter a mind that joins with a smaller wicked one. Ann was born from the merged Lyris and Mary-Ann Guillotin.
