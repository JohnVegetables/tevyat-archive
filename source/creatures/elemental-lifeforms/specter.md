## summary
### LITE
Specters are small elemental lifeforms that float in the air. The Adventurer Handbook describes them as monsters born from highly concentrated elements, little sprites with a gentle temperament much like slimes, though they can sometimes explode into a rage. They exist in all seven elements and are immune to their own.
### STANDARD +
They are common in Inazuma, in Enkanomiya and, per the wiki, in Sumeru. Specters were added in Version 2.1. They are everyday pests rather than characters: no individual specter has a name, voice or recorded motive.
### LARGE +
On Watatsumi Island they are treated as a nuisance serious enough that Kokomi has a Specter Eco-Management group. The group is mentioned only in passing in dialogue during a limited-time event.

## nature
### LITE
Specters are made of concentrated elemental energy and drift above the ground. They are calm by default but fly into a rage if struck hard.
### STANDARD +
Like slimes they are classed as Elemental Lifeforms, and each carries one element: Anemo, Cryo, Dendro, Electro, Geo, Hydro or Pyro. Their Japanese name is Flime, which the wiki notes is likely a blend of "fly" and "slime". When struck hard in a single blow they grow larger and angrier, and when defeated they pause and then explode.
### LARGE +
The wiki lists the places: Anemo, Geo and Hydro specters on Seirai Island and Watatsumi Island; Cryo, Electro and Pyro specters in Enkanomiya; Dendro specters are listed without a place.
### ULTRA +
Because the Handbook calls them gentle while Watatsumi treats them as pests, one reasonable portrayal (a roleplay suggestion, not canon) is to show them as basically harmless unless provoked or crowded, not as predators.

## abilities
### LITE
They float, spit or throw elemental bolts, and have a rage state: after a very heavy hit they swell and use stronger attacks. On death they blow up. (Gameplay-derived.)
### STANDARD +
A specter's rage (Fury) builds only from a single heavy blow, in steps that depend on how much of its health the blow takes, and at full Fury it grows bigger and uses only enhanced attacks; its death-explosion is also stronger. Each element has its own moves: Anemo specters fire wind bolts and create a vacuum field, Hydro specters fire bubbles and heal nearby enemies, Geo specters spit rocks and cause a quake, Cryo specters throw ice and snow and resist freezing, Electro specters call lightning and bump into targets, Pyro specters throw fire. The wiki marks move names as mostly unofficial.
### LARGE +
A Hydro specter's final explosion releases bubbles that heal nearby enemies when it has full Fury. Specters do not count as normal kills for some on-kill weapon and talent effects, according to a community theorycrafting library cited on the wiki. These are gameplay details; in narration treat them as brief flares of temper and a loud pop.

## relations
### LITE
Specters have no leader or society. They share their family, the Elemental Lifeforms, with slimes.
### STANDARD +
Kokomi's Specter Eco-Management group on Watatsumi Island treats specters as pests. In event dialogue Kokomi jokes that she could swap the posts of two officials, Tsuyuko and Naki, which would put Tsuyuko in charge of the group; Tsuyuko later compares it to Liyue's programs against rats, cockroaches, flies and barnacles. Specters appear in Inazuma's Felonious Floaters daily commission as a group of wild specters threatening nearby areas.
### LARGE +
Specters also appear mixed with hilichurls and abyss mages in the Plug-and-Play Plan commission on Seirai Island, which suggests they are common enough to share ground with other hostile groups. That is gameplay placement, not a stated alliance.

## entry: Specter Eco-Management

### STANDARD
Specter Eco-Management is a Watatsumi Island group mentioned in dialogue during the limited-time event Three Realms Gateway Offering. Kokomi jokes that swapping posts would put Tsuyuko in charge of it, and Tsuyuko later compares it to Liyue's pest programs. It shows that Watatsumi treats specters as pests, but little else about the group is stated.
