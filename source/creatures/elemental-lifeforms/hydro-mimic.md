## summary
### LITE
Hydro Mimics are bodies of living water shaped like ordinary animals: boars, cranes, crabs, squirrels, finches, frogs, mallards and raptor-like birds. They are pure Hydro Elemental Lifeforms, summoned in numbers by the Oceanid Rhodeia of Loch, and some are giant-sized. They are not real animals and do not behave like them.
### STANDARD +
In a scene they sit beside slimes rather than beside wildlife: they are made of water, are immune to Hydro, and each kind is easier to hurt with one particular element. They are found mostly around Rhodeia in Liyue, but also appear on their own in some quests and other places in the world.
### LARGE +
No Hydro Mimic has a name, voice or recorded motive in canon. Treat them as a tool of the Oceanid who summons them, or as odd local colour where they turn up without one.

## nature
### LITE
Hydro Mimics are made entirely of Hydro and resemble normal wildlife. Like Hydro Slimes, they can walk on deep water and so cannot drown.
### STANDARD +
They are summoned by Oceanids, which is why most meetings with them happen at the Oceanid fight, but the wiki also records them appearing without an Oceanid present. Some are giant-sized. By the wiki's listing, each kind is vulnerable to one element other than Hydro: cranes and raptors to Electro, mallards and crabs to Cryo, boars and squirrels to Pyro, frogs and finches to Geo. That is a gameplay detail, not a statement canon makes about their bodies.
### LARGE +
When Rhodeia's arena is approached the weather turns to rain whatever the surrounding climate, and she sends mimics out in waves of three, two and one. The single kinds, the frog and the finch, burst into a wide Hydro blast when they fall (gameplay description).
### ULTRA +
In the Veluriyam Mirage the wiki records that nearly all mimics are neutral unless provoked, unlike the usual hostile kind, and that during the Capturing Light and Shadow quest a Hydro Eidolon takes the form of a mimic and fights beside the Traveler. The sources read give no further explanation.

## abilities
### LITE
They fight with water: rams and charges from boars, wave-flapping and bubble attacks from cranes and crabs, a whirling vortex under mallards, leaps from frogs and finches, and diving swoops from raptors. Boars and squirrels heal themselves continually. (Gameplay-derived.)
### STANDARD +
A boar mimic's self-healing is cut off for a few seconds after Pyro hits it. A mallard's vortex damages anyone standing in it, and cranes tend to back away rather than close in. The wiki says most attack names are unofficial, so describe the motions rather than quoting names.
### LARGE +
In narrative terms the waves (a trio, then a pair, then a single) let the Oceanid test visitors in stages rather than all at once. This is a reading of the fight structure, not stated canon.

## relations
### LITE
Rhodeia of Loch, the Oceanid at Wuwang Hill in Liyue, summons them. They are otherwise only loosely tied to any person or group.
### STANDARD +
Rhodeia was once an Oceanid scout who served the Hydro Archon Egeria in other nations, and settled by a lake east of Qingce Village after the Oceanids left Fontaine. Her hostility to the young Oceanid Endora turned the Bishui River bitter; with the Traveler's help Endora and Rhodeia reached an understanding in the Life Flows On quests, though Rhodeia stays unhappy.
### LARGE +
In Version 4.0 Tainted Hydro Phantasms were added at Wuwang Hill and Qingce Village; they do not actively attack. They are a separate group, and their relation to the mimics is not stated in the sources read.

## appearances
### LITE
Beyond Rhodeia, the wiki records mimics in Zhongli's Story Quest around the Guyun Stone Forest, in the Spiral Abyss during Versions 1.5 and 2.3, around Broken Isle in the Golden Apple Archipelago, and in the Chasm's underground mines.
### STANDARD +
They also show up in the final part of The Moon-Bathed Deep world quest series, in some rooms of the Mystic Onmyou Chamber during Labyrinth Warriors, and in the Veluriyam Mirage. Spiral Abyss use is gameplay only.
### LARGE +
During the limited-time event Wishful Drops (Version 1.4), mimics were captured for quest progress. In that event they deal minimal damage, and the mimic mallards do not carry their normal Hydro aura and flee when approached, much like ordinary mallards. This is event-only behaviour, not how mimics normally act.

## entry: Rhodeia of Loch
@keys: Rhodeia, Rhodeia of Loch
@version: 1.0
### STANDARD
Rhodeia of Loch, archived as Oceanid, is the Oceanid of Qingce and a normal-boss opponent at Wuwang Hill in Liyue. She summons Hydro Mimics in waves. Before the cataclysm 500 years ago she was the strongest of the Oceanid scouts who served the Hydro Archon Egeria in other nations. After Egeria and many Oceanids perished, the survivors disagreed with her successor Focalors, and Rhodeia settled by a lake far from Fontaine.
### LARGE +
Rhodeia sensed the young Oceanid Endora and believed her an assassin from Fontaine. Her enmity made the Bishui River bitter, harming the fish and the Dawn Winery, until Endora and the Traveler faced her in the Life Flows On quests. She is still waiting for pure waters to become corrupted.

## entry: Wishful Drops event
@keys: Wishful Drops, Inquisitive Endora
@version: 1.4
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
Wishful Drops was a limited-time event in Version 1.4 in which water along the Mondstadt-Liyue border turned bitter. Travelers used the gadget Inquisitive Endora to capture Oceanid creatures for event progress. Mimics in the event were gentler than in normal play.
