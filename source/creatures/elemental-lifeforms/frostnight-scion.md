## summary
### LITE
Frostnight Scions are elegant, antlered and winged elemental lifeforms found in Nod-Krai, introduced in Version 6.0 (four kinds; three more arrived in 6.3). Their Handbook text says long exposure to high concentrations of elemental energy gave them their present forms. Each kind carries one element (Cryo, Pyro, Hydro, Dendro, Electro, Geo or Anemo), is immune to it, and periodically slips into a stronger Gloomveiled state. Far-northern prayers say they were born from the Frost Moon's blessings.
### STANDARD
The Frostnight Scions are an elite enemy group of elemental lifeforms in Nod-Krai (Version 6.0, Luna I; the Hydro, Geo and Anemo kinds were added in 6.3, Luna IV). The Adventurer Handbook calls them elegant beings whose present form comes from long years of exposure to dense elemental energy. There are seven elemental kinds, one per element, plus a single boss, Frostnight Herra, who starts without a fixed element.

Far-northern prayers that still survive hold that they were born from the Frost Moon's blessings, a symbol of the pure source and the first radiance. Archive entries add that they cannot bear their pale purity being stained by the seven colours of heavenly light, so they reject any life that draws too near.

## nature
### STANDARD
Canon describes them as elemental lifeforms, in the same family as slimes: like slimes, each kind is immune to its own element. The Archive stresses refinement more than savagery, though several kinds are called violent when provoked.

Two origin accounts sit side by side. One is elemental: the Handbook says long exposure to high concentrations of elemental energy produced their present forms. The other is devotional: the Handbook states flatly that they were born from the Frost Moon's blessings, as a symbol of the pure source and the first radiance, while the Archive entries attribute this to still-extant prayers of the far north. The Archive for the Icy Crystal kind adds that this kind is said to be closest to the original form of a Frostnight Scion.
### LARGE +
The Herra's Archive text pushes the devotional account further. Legends say these graceful, mystical beasts were born in the age before the Frost Moon was broken, and that, favoured by the divine emissary, they would never be tainted by the light of day. They are described as proud beings roaming in the waning glow of the moon, wielding ancient powers long since tamed by the Lord of the Sky. Followers of the Frost Moon regard them as sacred beasts lost to this world, retreating along secret lunar paths beyond mortal sight. They wander in the shadow of the moon at the boundary between two worlds, guarding lands coveted by calamity and watching over the people who live there, which the text calls the oath they first swore to the apostle of the golden city.

These are legends and prayers, not a narrated history: treat the oath and the age before the broken moon as claimed by the text, not demonstrated by a quest.
### ULTRA +
The wording "tamed by the Lord of the Sky" ties their power to the Heavenly Principles in the same breath that the text opposes their pale purity to heavenly light's seven colours; the text itself does not resolve this tension. The Dendro kind's entry also mentions that one of the Moonchanters once made a contract with the wooded lands with its help, which the text presents only as something "some say".

## abilities
### STANDARD
Each kind keeps a lasting aura of its own element and is immune to it outside the Gloomveiled state. The seven kinds fight in distinct ways:
- Icy Crystal (Cryo): Cryo Aura, Swipe, Ice Ball, Stomp and a Frost Skirt attack.
- Blazing Brilliance (Pyro): Slash, Flying Slam, Stomp and a Fireball.
- Surging Currents (Hydro): Tail Swipe, Tail Slam, Stomp and a Bubble Shower.
- Tangled Vines (Dendro): Tail Swipe, Tail Strike, Stomp and a Bloomburst.
- Gleaming Charge (Electro): Tail Swipe, Tail Slam, Stomp and a Tri-Shot.
- Gilded Sunshard (Geo): Swipe, Crystal Ball, Stomp and a Crystal Hail.
- Veiled Cloudchaser (Anemo): Slash, Flying Slam, Stomp and a Dart Barrage.

(Attack names are the wiki's; the Archive text does not describe the moves.)

Gloomveiled state (game mechanic): they periodically enter it, gaining stronger attacks while losing their elemental immunity (replaced by a still-considerable resistance). A Scion is forced out of it by damage of its matching element or by Lunar reaction damage.
### LARGE +
The Archive text characterises the kinds. The Pyro and Electro kinds are called among the most violent in temperament (the Electro kind commands bolts that shatter steel). The Hydro kind is gentler than most, with a glass-clear body of pure water droplets, but can shape water into piercing blades when provoked. The Geo kind has antlers gleaming like polished amber. The Veiled Cloudchaser almost never leaves footprints and is also known as the one who walks with the wind. The Dendro kind resonates with the plant life of the groves. The Cryo kind is said to be closest to the original form of a Scion and uses icy mists and crystals to set up walls, perhaps to keep the flawless primeval light apart from the world beneath the moon.

Gameplay notes (not in-world lore): they are Elite Enemies, release elemental particles at health thresholds, and interact with Nod-Krai's Lunar reaction mechanics.

## relations
### STANDARD
Their lore ties them to the Frost Moon and to Nod-Krai's old northern devotion rather than to any modern faction. The Herra's Archive text says the followers of the Frost Moon (see the Frostmoon Scions faction) regard these beasts as sacred. In play they are fought as ordinary hostile enemies.
### LARGE +
The Herra text frames the creatures' purpose as protective and old: guarding the lands calamity covets and watching over those who live there, in keeping with an oath to the apostle of the golden city (the wiki links this to Anacharsis of Hyperborea). No current oath-bearer is shown. See the Frost Moon location and the Frostmoon Scions faction for the surrounding setting.

## individuals
### STANDARD
Frostnight Herra is the group's only named boss: a world boss on Hiisi Island, introduced in Version 6.1 (Luna II). Its in-game title is Guardian of the Holy Light. It starts without an element.
### LARGE +
Herra's Archive and Event Menu text describe a majestic being that strides with pride through the frost-laden night, with iridescent wings still gleaming with the glory of an ancient age and echoes of a bygone time in each flutter. In battle, while Gloomveiled, it absorbs the element that has damaged it most and becomes immune to it, unless the damage was exclusively Physical or mostly Lunar. Before Herra's release its arena was held by an Icy Crystal Frostnight Scion (wiki trivia).

## entry: Frostnight Herra
@keys: Frostnight Herra, Guardian of the Holy Light
@version: 6.1
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
Frostnight Herra is the one boss among the Frostnight Scions, found on Hiisi Island in Nod-Krai (Version 6.1). Its name, per the wiki's etymology note, means "lord" in Finnish, Icelandic and Old Norse. Archive text calls it a proud being of the waning moonlight with iridescent wings, wielding ancient powers tamed by the Lord of the Sky, bound by an oath to the apostle of the golden city. Its attacks include sweeps, moon projectiles and a plunging strike, and in the Gloomveiled state it takes on the element that hurt it most.

## entry: Gloomveiled state
@keys: Gloomveiled, Gloomveil
@version: 6.0
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
Gloomveiled is the shrouded, empowered state Frostnight Scions enter periodically. In it a Scion hits harder and with extra moves, but loses its elemental immunity (keeping a high resistance). It can be forced out by its matching element or by Lunar reaction damage. The text read does not explain the shrouding in-world.
