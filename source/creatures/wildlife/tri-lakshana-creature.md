## summary
### LITE
Tri-Lakshana Creatures are special plants and fungi of Sumeru that change when elemental energy touches them. Normally they sit in a Normal state; Pyro leaves them Scorched, and Electro makes them Activated. The Sumeru tutorial tells travellers to use their different forms to aid an adventure.
### STANDARD +
They are mostly an exploration aid (bouncing, revealing hidden things, clearing enemy attention) and, in a few cases, a hazard. Most appear first in version 3.0, with the Sumeru region.

## nature
### LITE
The in-game tutorial calls them creatures that can transform due to stimuli from external elemental energy. Contact with Electro puts them in an Activated state; contact with Pyro puts them in a Scorched state. Their Normal state is associated with Dendro.
### STANDARD +
Most kinds have a pile of white fluff beside them in the Normal and Activated states that blinks; scorching removes it for a time. Exceptions: Fungi have no fluff in any state, Seed Mirrors keep it even when Scorched, and some starshrooms and bouncy mushrooms (for example in The Chasm's underground mines) cannot change state at all. A Scorched creature can usually be restored with Dendro (Fungi cannot). Activated creatures generally return to Normal after a short while, except Fungi.
### LARGE +
When Activated by Electro, most kinds also release a Truesense Pulse that reveals certain hidden objects within a radius, which first appear as blue outlines and become solid once struck by the pulse. In Withering Zones the creatures remain Scorched until the zone is cleared. The name alludes to a Buddhist idea, the "three marks of existence", fitting a creature with three states.

## kinds
### STANDARD
The wiki lists these kinds: Bouncy Mushrooms, Dream Flowers, Fungi, Poisonous Mushrooms, Rocklifting Branches, Seed Mirrors, Starshrooms and Tri-Yana Seeds.
### LARGE +
Bouncy Mushrooms launch whoever lands on them (higher when Activated, not at all when Scorched). Dream Flowers appear in Phantasmal Gate challenges and clear nearby enemy attention in their Normal state; activated, they mark remaining Phantasmal Seeds instead. Seed Mirrors sit atop eight watchtowers in Dharma Forest and help locate nascent Withering Zones and a hidden Aranara when Activated. Tri-Yana Seeds move along a fixed path in the Normal state, stop when Scorched, and give a large Truesense Pulse when Activated. Poisonous Mushrooms (an unofficial name) burst with Dendro damage near the player in the Normal state, form a poisonous cloud when Activated, and are harmless when Scorched. Rocklifting Branches (also unofficial) rise under Dendro, wither under Pyro, and rise higher with Electro after Dendro, sometimes uncovering items or enemies beneath them. Starshrooms are an edible mushroom that can be Activated or Scorched in Sumeru.
### ULTRA +
Special yellow and green "floating" bouncy mushrooms in certain domains and Girdle of the Sands challenges are not Tri-Lakshana Creatures and cannot change state. Electro on an ordinary Bouncy Mushroom also lets it take part in the Quicken reaction.

## gameplay
@version: 3.0
### STANDARD
Gameplay note: everything about the three states, Truesense Pulses, bounce height and damage is game mechanics, not described in the world by any character. In roleplay treat them as odd Sumeru flora that visibly react to lightning or fire.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
This project's suggested use: in a scene they are local colour, such as a mushroom that springs a traveller upward after lightning strikes, or a flower that withers when a flame passes. Do not invent scholarly explanations, folklore or sentience for them; canon gives none.

Tri-Lakshana Creatures are Sumeru plants and fungi with a Normal state, an Electro-Activated state and a Pyro-Scorched state; the Sumeru tutorial introduces them as creatures that transform in response to external elemental energy. Gameplay-driven, with no deeper canon explanation.

## entry: Seed Mirror
@keys: Seed Mirror, Seed Mirrors
@keys2: Dharma Forest, watchtower, Withering
### LARGE
Seed Mirrors crown the eight watchtowers of Dharma Forest. Activated with Electro they can reveal a hidden Aranara, and they help find nascent Withering Zones; a Scorched or Withering-affected mirror does not work.

## entry: Tri-Yana Seed
@keys: Tri-Yana Seed, Tri-Yana Seeds
### LARGE
A moving seed that follows a fixed path, stops when Scorched and releases a large Truesense Pulse when Activated. The name combines Sanskrit words for three and for moving.
