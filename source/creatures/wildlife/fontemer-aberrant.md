## summary
### LITE
Fontemer Aberrants are Fontaine's native aquatic beasts: crabs, octopuses, jellyfish, seahorses, stingrays, sea hares and a whale-like mammal called the Blubberbeast. They live in the waters of Fontaine and along its shores, and they are not recorded anywhere else in Teyvat. Most fight by firing Hydro projectiles or pouncing. They are animals, not a kingdom: the Adventurer Handbook insists they have no courts, words or aristocracy.
### STANDARD +
The group name appears in the Adventurer Handbook as "Fontemer Aberrant". The Handbook says that, perhaps because of environmental requirements, the creatures can seemingly only be found in Fontaine, and it guesses that they developed from aquatic animals that once differed little from sea life elsewhere in Teyvat. Younger, smaller animals are usually tolerant of travelers; adults and the named "Local Legend" individuals are not.
### LARGE +
They first appeared in Version 4.0 with Fontaine itself; the great seahorse boss arrived in 4.1. Their drops form the Fontemer Aberrant material line (Transoceanic Pearl, Transoceanic Chunk, Xenochromatic Crystal), and their giants have unusual boss-level reward items. As of Version 7.1 nothing researched shows them outside Fontaine.

## nature
@version: 4.0
### LITE
Fontemer Aberrants are ordinary animals as canon describes them, not spirits or people. Most hunt for food and are unaggressive otherwise. Even the largest remain simple creatures that grew powerful by surviving.
### STANDARD +
Adventurer Handbook texts describe individual kinds this way. The Armored Crab lives in a borrowed shell and has no real territory beyond that shell on its back. Angelic Sea Hares, Hat Jellyfish, Bubbly Seahorses and similar small kinds hunt by firing watery projectiles. The Ball Octopus's water jets are strong enough to endanger careless tourist divers, which is one reason diving gear is thick. The Blubberbeast is a mammal that communicates with companions through melodious sounds; the Handbook notes that sound travels well in water and that a Research Institute scholar once took inspiration from this for an image-to-data transmission device that never spread beyond the Institute. The Hunter's Ray is a stingray that releases blades of water.
### LARGE +
Juveniles and adults can be quite different. Canon describes the Bubbler Seahorse as the extremely aggressive adult form of a male Bubbly Seahorse, the Cherubic Sea Hare as the adult Angelic Sea Hare whose "wings" are actually fat used to store elemental energy, and the Sternshield Crab as the grown form of the Armored Crab, forever searching for a shell that fits as it grows. The Cherubic Sea Hare, the Handbook adds, spends its life eating and hoarding energy beyond its needs, and may someday survive drying oceans or simply become food for something else. In gameplay terms, ordinary adults found on the surface wear an elemental ward that lets them use attacks other than Physical or Hydro; breaking it returns them to those two. This is a game-mechanical note, not lore.
### ULTRA +
Growth is the central idea in the great creatures' item texts. The Emperor's Resolution text says Fontemer Aberrants have no concept of aristocracy or inheritance: none is born superior, and one that survives predators and hardship while hunting for centuries can accumulate immense power, which is why such a creature is called an overlord. The Fontemer Unihorn text treats the Millennial Pearl Seahorse differently, saying it did not simply grow larger from a base form but acquired limbs, a horn, and altered muscle and skeleton that an ordinary seahorse lacks, so that it looks more like a creature from old legends.

## abilities
@version: 4.0
### LITE
Hydro projectiles and jets are the common attack: bubbles, water guns, water blades. Crabs and some adults are tougher and hit harder. Giants carry an elemental ward that must be broken before they can be hurt properly.
### STANDARD +
Named abilities from the Archive sources: Bubble Shot (Angelic Sea Hare), Water Gun and Pounce (Ball Octopus), Bubble Spit (Bubbly Seahorse). Adults gain elemental flavors, such as Electro and Hydro for the Bubbler Seahorse, Cryo for the Cherubic Sea Hare and Pyro for the Sternshield Crab. These are gameplay data and should be used only as colour in a scene.
### LARGE +
The two named giants each rely on an elemental focus. The Millennial Pearl Seahorse holds an Electro-infused Xenomare Pearl in front of its legs; destroying it paralyzes the creature. The Emperor of Fire and Iron wears a pair of Pyro-infused Twin Molten Horns that work the same way. Both are mechanics from the boss entries; their Handbook lore says nothing about how the pearl or horns work in the world.

## entry: Xenochromatic Creatures
@keys: Xenochromatic Creature, Xenochromatic Creatures, Xenochromatic Jellyfish, Xenochromatic Blubberbeast
@version: 4.0
### STANDARD
Xenochromatic Creatures are Fontemer Aberrants that the Traveler can borrow abilities from while diving in Fontaine to solve underwater puzzles. They do not attack and cannot be harmed. Five kinds are documented: Armored Crab, Hunter's Ray, Blubberbeast, Jellyfish and Ball Octopus. Borrowed powers last until the diver leaves the water entirely. The Hunter's Ray text notes a rumor that these are really ocean spirits that bless those accepted by, or able to control, the Fontemer, then dismisses it as groundless speculation from people without a grounding in natural philosophy.
### LARGE +
The three named abilities are Armored Shield (from the crab, a shield with a shockwave), Water Blades (from the ray) and Sonar Waves (from the Blubberbeast). Furina's passive "The Sea Is My Stage" shortens the cooldown for them by 30 percent. This is a gameplay detail.

## entry: Local Legends
@keys: Local Legend, Local Legends
@keys2: Fontemer Aberrant, Blubberbeast, Hat Jellyfish, Hunter's Ray, Sternshield Crab, Cherubic Sea Hare
@version: 4.0
### STANDARD
Local Legends are named, unusually strong individual Fontemer Aberrants found in particular spots of Fontaine; unlike ordinary animals they attack on sight. The Archive notes these: the Blubberbeasts Fading Veteran (Elton Trench) and Dobharcu, Lord of the Hidden (Chemin de L'Espoir); the Hat Jellyfish Chassanion (under the Sealed Ruined Tower, later inside the Tower of Ipsissimus after the quest In the Wake of Narcissus) and Mageblade Corrouge (Roots of Erinnyes, fightable only after The Wild Fairy of Erinnyes); the Hunter's Rays Angelica and Medoro (the Fairy Knight Twins, Salacia Plain) and Cortana and Murgleis (Swords of the Gorge, Thalatta Submarine Canyon); the Cherubic Sea Hare Luachra the Brilliant (north-west Liffey Region); and the Sternshield Crabs Iron Viscount (Beryl Region) and Ocean Circuit Judge (Elynas).
### LARGE +
Every legend is a stronger specimen of an ordinary species rather than a different kind of being. A song titled "Song of Dobharcu" is referenced in the Millennial Pearl Seahorse's Handbook text, but its content beyond that reference was not found.

## society
@version: 4.0
### LITE
There is no society. The Handbook calls Fontemer Aberrants simple aquatic animals with no courts, kingdoms or words, and says tales to the contrary are the romantic imagination of people unwilling to accept a bland world.
### STANDARD +
Fontainians still tell stories about them. A children's-style storybook about an Armored Crab seeking a fitting shell was, per the Handbook, very popular in Fontaine for a time and moved adults too. There is also a small, sparse fan community: Bubbly Seahorses are said to have many fans, and the text compares the more standoffish enthusiasts to some clockwork meka hobbyists.
### LARGE +
Legend names two rulers. Sailors' tales say a war among the Aberrants once left only the Millennial Pearl Seahorse and the Emperor of Fire and Iron, dividing the great lake into northern and southern courts and ending with a dying Stormlord Hunter's Ray whose thrashing caused the "first diluvian period." The Handbook treats this as invention. Treat it as folk story and attribute it.

## relations
@version: 4.0
### LITE
People in Fontaine regard them as wildlife to avoid or admire. They are also associated with Fontaine's local waters and, in speculation only, with the Primordial Sea.
### STANDARD +
A Handbook text about the Emperor of Fire and Iron says it fought machines, other Aberrants and curious hunters alike, and that a giant Armored Crab once attacked a ship before being driven back into the depths by Basil Elton, then not yet reinstated as a naval commander. That attack is dated to before the first diluvian period. The same entry imagines a future empty of people in which the Emperor wears the Palais Mermonia like a coat of white stone, which is a thought experiment, not a prediction.
### LARGE +
The prefix "Fontemer" is left unexplained. The wiki links it to the Primordial Sea and to the idea that the creatures evolved there; that remains an interpretation. Fontemer Aberrants should not be confused with Oceanids, which are Hydro lifeforms created by Egeria, or with clockwork meka, which are machines.

## individuals
@version: 4.0
### LITE
The two famous individuals are the Emperor of Fire and Iron, a giant Armored Crab, and the Millennial Pearl Seahorse, a violet-lit ancient seahorse. Both live in caverns.
### STANDARD +
The Emperor of Fire and Iron (title "Searing Tide Pulsing Beneath the Mountains") is found in the Belleau Region and is said to have seen Fontaine's seas before the first diluvian period. Its drop, Emperor's Resolution, is its war-hammer claw. The Millennial Pearl Seahorse ("Nameless Lord of the Great Lake"), described as having a phantasmal beauty and a temper fiercer than a thunderstorm, lives in the Liffey Region; its drop is the Fontemer Unihorn. The Handbook describes the pair as the two sovereigns at the apex of the Aberrants and notes the "Pearl Seahorse" versus "Emperor" naming reflects the seahorse's slender elegance versus the crab's heavy bulk.
### LARGE +
Each has its own Archive entry (creature.emperor-of-fire-and-iron and creature.millennial-pearl-seahorse), which should be preferred for detail. Both also have event-only stronger variants described below.

## entry: Event variants of the sovereigns
@keys: Tyrant of Fire and Iron, Enraged Millennial Pearl Seahorse
@version: 4.0
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
Two boss variants appear only in limited-time events: the Tyrant of Fire and Iron, called "Indomitable Lord" in the On the Trail of Behemoths event, and the Enraged Millennial Pearl Seahorse, called "Best Ride" in the Specially-Shaped Saurian Search event. They are event enemies and are not standing wildlife.

## status
### LITE
As of Version 7.1 the species is unchanged. Nothing found shows a new Fontemer Aberrant in 6.x or 7.0-7.1 or any appearance outside Fontaine.
