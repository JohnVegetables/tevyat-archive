## summary
### LITE
Wildlife are the ordinary animals of Teyvat: birds, beasts, fish, and small creatures such as frogs, crabs and fireflies. Most are harmless and flee when approached. Each region has its own species, and a few carry folk legends.
### STANDARD
Teyvat's wildlife includes birds (falcons, cranes, finches, pigeons and others), beasts (boars, foxes, deer, wolves, bears, and more), fish, and small catchable animals like frogs, crabs, lizards, fireflies and crystalflies. Most flee on approach and are harmless, though boars can accidentally hurt a traveller when they charge in a panic. In game terms many drop meat, fowl or fish when killed or caught. Several species have folk reputations: kitsune, bake-danuki, wolves and owls; whales are known as gentle despite their size.
### LARGE +
Behaviour varies by region. Most animals simply bolt when startled. Sumeru introduced animals that fight back when startled, and Chenyu Vale animals that are passive but flee if attacked (game behaviour). Snezhnaya has combat-capable predators such as the Polyarbear. Animals in Nod-Krai are the one place where specific characters change how wildlife acts.
### ULTRA +
Because the wiki sorts wildlife by what the player can do (drop items, be caught, or be left alone), do not carry those categories into narration: in scenes, treat animals as living creatures with ordinary habits.

## nature
### LITE
Wildlife are natural animals, not elemental creatures. Most are skittish; a few are dangerous.
### STANDARD
Wildlife are ordinary fauna, unlike Elemental Lifeforms and Cicins, which are made of or tied to elements. The wiki groups them into birds, beasts, fish and a miscellaneous group of small catchables (crystalflies, loaches, frogs, crabs, lizards, unagi, fireflies, scarabs and others). Nod-Krai wildlife include Frostfin Whales, which the Archive says are giant but have never actively attacked a human; they race ships and leap clear of the water as if playing. Snezhnaya has Nochhornowls (night-hunting owls), Pale-Furred Wolves and Polyarbears.
### LARGE +
Some creatures called wildlife are tied to folklore or to other beings. Bake-danuki are described as a type of youkai in Inazuma, small plump raccoon-like creatures with a leaf on their head and a bag round their neck. Kitsune are foxes that, by legend, were once family to the Kitsune Saiguu.

## regional_variation
### STANDARD
Examples from the Archive: Mondstadt, Nod-Krai and Snezhnaya share Pale-Furred Wolves, which the Archive says are mostly peaceful but warn intruders with long howls; Inazuma has kitsune and bake-danuki; Nod-Krai has the Frostfin Whale; Snezhnaya has the Nochhornowl and Polyarbear. Wolvendom's wolves do not flee on approach.
### LARGE +
The Nochhornowl is described as a wise-seeming night hunter that many hunters respect and believe has a spirit; the Polyarbear is an agile, solitary tundra hunter that some Snezhnayan regions treat as a symbol of strength.

## scene_use
### STANDARD
Use wildlife for atmosphere: a flock lifting from a field, a boar crashing through brush, fish in a stream, or a kitsune sitting calmly near a shrine. Predators like the Polyarbear can be a real hazard on the Snezhnayan tundra. Do not give animals speech or character-level motives; canon does not.

## entry: Nod-Krai wildlife and Lauma or Columbina

### STANDARD
When Lauma or Columbina is in the player's party, some of Nod-Krai's wildlife react warmly: they dig up resources for the player or even heal and restore energy, and animals that would normally flee do not unless attacked (a gameplay behaviour). Canon gives no in-world explanation for the pattern.

## entry: Kitsune
@keys: Kitsune, kitsune fox
@keys2: Inazuma, Grand Narukami Shrine, Sky Kitsune
@version: 2.0
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
The Archive describes kitsune as sly, prideful foxes said in urban legends to be very intelligent with long memories. They ignore people who come near, used to living beside humans. Legends say they were once family to the Kitsune Saiguu, and that after she disappeared the mightier Sky and Earth Kitsune turned to stone statues to await her return. As their kind dwindled they lost their mystical might and no longer speak. They are found near the Grand Narukami Shrine and just outside Inazuma City, and cannot be harmed.

## entry: Bake-danuki
@keys: Bake-danuki, tanuki
@keys2: Inazuma, Ioroi, prank
@version: 2.0
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
Bake-danuki, also called tanuki, are a type of youkai found on Inazuma's islands (except Tsurumi Island). They look like small plump raccoons, usually with a leaf on the head and a bag around the neck. Their leader, Ioroi, is sealed inside a large statue in Chinju Forest. In play they disguise themselves as objects to prank travellers; that is a gameplay behaviour, though pranks fit their reputation.

## entry: Frostfin Whale
@keys: Frostfin Whale, Frostfin Whales
@version: 6.0
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
Frostfin Whales live off Nod-Krai and are categorised as fish. The Archive calls them fierce predators to most animals, with tails that could sink a fishing boat, yet says no Frostfin Whale has ever actively attacked a human. They race ships and leap from the water, as if enjoying playing with humanity, and fisherfolk have waited hours in the cold for a glimpse. They are found south of Frostmoon Enclave, northwest of Starsand Shoal and west of South Harbor, each marked by a whale-shaped rock.

## entry: Pale-Furred Wolf
@keys: Pale-Furred Wolf, Pale-Furred Wolves, Wolf King
@keys2: wolf, wolves
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
Pale-Furred Wolves live in Mondstadt, Nod-Krai and Snezhnaya. The Archive calls them solemn, noble pack hunters that rarely start attacks and instead give long warning howls. Folk tales call them rather tame, and old stories hold they descend from the Northwind, guided by a Wolf King once equal to the gods; the text says that being has long passed on. Wolves in Wolvendom do not flee when approached.

## entry: Polyarbear
@keys: Polyarbear, Polyarbears
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
The Polyarbear is a large, thick-furred Snezhnayan animal that survives extreme cold. The Archive calls it agile despite its size, able to leap between ice floes and stalk prey silently, a top tundra hunter. It is no tame beast and some Snezhnayan regions see it as a symbol of strength. It attacks on sight and is found in Volkodlak Tundra, Everfrozen Earth and Fellfrost Peak.

## entry: Nochhornowl
@keys: Nochhornowl, Nochhornowls
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
Nochhornowls are Snezhnayan owls with night-sky feathers that hunt after dusk. The Archive calls them the most agile tundra hunters, skilled at disguise, observation and fast pursuit. Many hunters see them as symbols of wisdom, believe they have spirits, and say the owls sometimes guide them with messages from the forest depths. They are found across Snezhnaya.
