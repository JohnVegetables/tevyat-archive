## summary
### LITE
Cicins are small, bat-like creatures that can slightly manipulate an element (Electro, Hydro or Cryo). Individually weak, they fly nimbly, fire elemental bolts, and avoid predators by their agility. Fatui Cicin Mages command them in battle.
### STANDARD
Cicins are common creatures found across Teyvat that look like small bats. The Archive describes them as little creatures able to manipulate an element only slightly, individually very weak, and shaped by that weakness into evasive fliers. In numbers they can still do real damage. They are best known as the minions of Fatui Cicin Mages.
### LARGE +
They are background fauna rather than characters: no individual Cicin is named, speaks, or has a recorded motive in canon.

## nature
### LITE
Cicins are small flying creatures tied to one element each. Canon documents Electro, Hydro and Cryo varieties. They are fragile but quick.
### STANDARD
The Archive entries for all three varieties open the same way: little creatures that can ever so slightly manipulate their element, known collectively as Cicins. The Cryo entry says they are individually very weak and that this weakness is exactly why they evolved unique ways of moving to avoid predators. The Electro entry says that, though weak alone, they can cause significant damage under the right circumstances. The Hydro entry says they adore the rare Mist Grass plant. Electro and Hydro Cicins are met in several places, with Guyun Stone Forest named as a location for both; Cryo Cicins are found on Dragonspine (introduced in version 1.2). The English name comes from Latin cicindela, firefly or glowworm.
### LARGE +
The wiki places Cicins in the Mystical Beasts family, separate from Elemental Lifeforms such as slimes. The Chinese, Japanese and French names all mean firefly, which matches the Latin root. The Adventurer Handbook gives no habitat entry for them.
### ULTRA +
Version history: Electro and Hydro Cicins and the Electro mage date from 1.0; Cryo Cicins and the Cryo mage came with Dragonspine content in 1.2. No later Cicin variant is documented on the pages read, including in Nod-Krai (6.x) or Snezhnaya (7.0-7.1) content.

## abilities
### LITE
Cicins fly and shoot small bolts of their element at people. They are fragile; their danger is in agility and numbers.
### STANDARD
Game mechanics, for reference: a Cicin has low HP, hovers out of reach and fires a single elemental bolt or icicle (Electro, Hydro or Cryo) at its target. When summoned by a Fatui Cicin Mage they gain special group attacks they cannot use alone, such as converging on a target to burst together. For narration, treat a Cicin as a darting, flickering nuisance: a few stinging bolts, not a siege.
### LARGE +
In the Cryo Cicin Mage's kit, the mage also draws on her Cicins to build a Cryo shield, so killing the Cicins first weakens her. This is a gameplay detail; translate it loosely.

## relations
### LITE
The Fatui use Cicins: Fatui Cicin Mages summon and command them, using their fondness for Mist Grass.
### STANDARD
Fatui Cicin Mages are an elite group within the Fatui who use Delusions to command elements. The Hydro Cicin Archive entry says it is by exploiting the Cicins' love of Mist Grass that Fatui Mages have sought to commandeer them in battle. The Adventurer Handbook adds that, as Cicins go crazy for Mist Grass, the mages take pleasure in toying with their prey, and that they seem to wander the land aimlessly as if compelled by some unknown duty. In the wild, mages are most often found alone, and an Electro or Cryo mage always summons three Cicins of her element.
### LARGE +
Only Electro and Cryo mages are documented; the wiki lists no Hydro mage, so do not assume one. The Cryo mage appears on Dragonspine and in Inazuma. Her Chinese voice actor, Nuoya, also voices Diona, a trivia point only.

## individuals
### STANDARD
No individual Cicin is named in canon. Fatui Cicin Mages are likewise an unnamed enemy group.

## entry: Fatui Cicin Mage
@keys: Fatui Cicin Mage, Fatui Cicin Mages, Cicin Mage, Cicin Mages
@version: 1.0
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
Fatui Cicin Mages are an elite enemy group of the Fatui who use Delusions to command elements. They summon Cicins of their own element, and the Cicins fight beside them and help with defence and special attacks. The Adventurer Handbook describes them as taking pleasure in toying with prey, and as seeming to wander without purpose, as if compelled by some unknown duty. Cicins go crazy for Mist Grass, and the mages exploit that to command them. Electro and Cryo mages are documented; the Cryo mage was added in version 1.2.

## entry: Mist Grass and Cicins

### STANDARD
Per the Hydro Cicin Archive entry, Cicins truly adore the rare and wonderful Mist Grass plant, and the Fatui have sought to exploit this to commandeer them. The Adventurer Handbook entry for the Fatui mages phrases it as Cicins going crazy for Mist Grass. Nothing further is said about how the plant is used.
