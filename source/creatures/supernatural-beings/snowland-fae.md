## summary
### LITE
The Snowland Fae, or simply "fae", are Snezhnaya's nonhuman supernatural peoples. Hyperborean artisans created them thousands of years ago. They live alongside humans, can use elemental power without a Vision, and many can change their shape. The first Cryo Archon, the Belyi Tsar, united them, and under his rule they formed the ruling aristocracy of the Northlands. After his death in the Cataclysm about 500 years ago, the Tsaritsa took power and the fae lost most of their old privileges.
### STANDARD +
The six common kinds are the Vila, Domovoy, Leshy, Volkodlak, Snegovik and Jack Frost. Rarer kinds include the Lantern Fae, the Water Imps ("Sirens") and the Augury Birds. Four fae are playable: Flins (Lantern Fae), Linnea (Augury Bird), Vesna (Vila) and Vodyanitsa (Water Imp). The Harbinger Pulcinella is a Domovoy.

## nature
### LITE
The fae vary widely. Many look nearly human, often with blue crystalline markings; others resemble animals (the Volkodlak), little snowmen (Jack Frosts) or trees (Leshy). They have their own language and script.
### STANDARD +
Lifespans differ by kind. Flins reckons himself six or seven hundred years old "by human measure", Augury Birds live little longer than humans, and Domovoy may live indefinitely yet still grow old. Most prefer the cold. Linnea and Flins cope with Nod-Krai's milder climate, but Flins suspects some fae can no longer survive anywhere warm. Jack Frosts feel discomfort in heat but do not melt as fairy tales claim. The Fae language is not mutually intelligible with Snezhnayan: a Fatui private wrote that he "couldn't understand a word" of a fae's speech.
### LARGE +
Shapeshifting is common, and the Nod-Krai loading screen calls the fae "an ancient race ... that can take many forms". Flins's true form is a ball of blue flame, though he passes as a pale human. Water Imps can turn a fish tail into working legs. Augury Birds grow wings on their heads as adults and can hide them under headwear. Filipov, a fae of the Belyi Tsar's court, was famed as the most skilled at transformation and once spied inside an enemy army. This talent breeds fear. Folk tales tell of fae swapping a human child for one of their own, and in Whisper Beneath the Waves (6.0) a woman claims a girl is "the child of a fae". Linnea really was raised this way: her Augury Bird parents placed her in a human cradle.
### ULTRA +
Some kinds are said to have set roles or temperaments. Most Jack Frosts are about as clever as a bright cat, cannot speak human languages and love pranks, play and buttons. Snegovik men are described as natural-born knights who forge weapons from ice, and Snegurochka (Snegovik women) as born mages of ice and snow. Leshy are a reclusive kind of the deep forests, and the Adventurer Handbook guesses that the oldest may be as tall as ancient trees. The Vila fly on insect-like wings and are said to have an instinctive feel for plants, turning them into fragrances and elixirs.

## origin
@version: 7.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The fae began in Hyperborea, the "golden realm" that once stood where Snezhnaya is now. Using "forbidden methods" given to them by the rebel angel Koitar, its artisans forged fae spirits on the bones of giant beasts and spun the fallen light of the Frost Moon into their flesh. When the Celestial Nail destroyed Hyperborea, many fae shrieked and turned into "icy silver mist". Humans who survived struggled in the frozen land, and afterwards fae were the main inhabitants of the north.
### LARGE +
Their oldest name, found by the Belyi Tsar in Hyperborean ruins inside the Shadow Realm, is Filii Adamici Evoluti, "the evolved children of humanity". Legend calls them servants made for the humans of the golden realm. The Tsar wrote that this was a lie told by people who feared fae living among them, and that the fae had been entrusted with a far grander ambition. The artifact text says the "rebellious envoy" dreamed that these "little creatures" might one day create a perfect being that could merge with the world. Hyperborea's later Primordial Human experiment drew on what it had learned from making the fae. In its final days, Hyperborea is said to have armed even newborn fae for the war against the heavens.

## history
### STANDARD
After Hyperborea fell, the fae formed kingdoms and clans of their own. A being called Monomakh emerged from the remains of the Cryo Dragon believing himself a fae, founded Kitezh as the seat of the Pale Star Court and set out to unify the fae. He defeated King Miscislav of the Volkodlak and his allies at Kitezh, and the remaining clans submitted to him. He took the title Belyi Tsar, "protector of all fae", and later became the Cryo Archon. The fae became his nobility, with some posts open only to them, and he eventually welcomed "pardoned" humans into the realm and moved the capital to Snezhnograd.
### LARGE +
The Belyi Tsar died in the Cataclysm, leading an expedition against the forces of the Abyss. When Anastasya was crowned Tsaritsa, many fae princes refused to come and pay homage, and the Water Imps' Dalegrad clan broke away. In her third year she founded the Fatui, which took the lands of the Drevogorye, Volkhovan and Cirnareka clans. Grand Duke Drevogorye died fighting them. She then ordered the remaining princes to give up their authority in exchange for keeping honorary titles. In her sixth year the human Ioanna Ivanovna became the first chair of the new Snezhnograd Veche. In her seventh year, after the Imperial Order Reformation Edict, many princely fae renounced their titles and left Snezhnaya. Flins, then the noble Kyryll the Azure Flame, was one of the nobles who chose self-imposed exile.
### ULTRA +
The Snezhnayan chronicle that the fae elders are thought to have written records the decisive war. Monomakh's envoys persuaded the Vila to stop paying tribute to Miscislav. Miscislav answered by demanding thirty times that tribute from the Pale Star Court, and in the next year he attacked Kitezh with his clans and allies. Over three battles the Emperor slew Miscislav and the Grand Dukes Skoldyryt and Vodyanoy by hurling his spear across the field like an arrow. He returned their bodies to their clans for burial. During the Belyi Tsar's reign the fae nobles were famously fond of luxury. Banquets and dances in the rotating galleries of Zapolyarny Palace hardly ever stopped, and the nobles considered attending them their most important duty. A Reed Miller keepsake mockingly records the nobles' claim that they alone "bore the sin of fullness" for the people's sake.

## society
### STANDARD
Each kind has traditionally had its own rulers: a Grand Duke or Grand Duchess of the Vila, a Grand Duke of the Domovoy, a Grand Duchess or princess of the Snegurochka at the House of Hesperides, and in ancient times a king of the Volkodlak. Old noble houses survive. House Strivozha is an ancient Vila bloodline, and Vesna's grandfather Pyotr is the reigning Grand Duke of the Vila. Most of the Druzhna, the Tsaritsa's personal guard, are Vila.
### LARGE +
Today most fae live in Snezhnograd, Novokitezhgrad and other parts of Snezhnaya. Novokitezhgrad, built under the Domovoy Grand Duke Alvis, has a large fae population. Noble titles are now honorary, and fae work ordinary jobs, serve in the state, or join gangs. Prominent fae include the Domovoy Pulcinella, Fifth Harbinger and Mayor of Snezhnograd. The Snegurochka Grand Duchess Pavlina, princess of the House of Hesperides and once a Hvergelmir researcher, ruled her people from before the Cataclysm until the 7.0 story, when she tried to use the Eye of Graeae to give the Snegovik complete souls and was stopped and killed by Aksinya and the Traveler. The Vila Akvilina, a Druzhna officer and Privy Councillor, shows the loyalist view: she calls the Vila bandits of the tundra a "permanent shame" upon her people.
### ULTRA +
Some kinds keep their own codes. Since Vaselisa Vodyanovna's covenant with Monomakh, no Water Imp may be forced to bear arms, so Water Imps walked among armies without fighting. Snegovik are said by some to "live for love". A House of Hesperides document records that when Pavlina asked her rival Larisa whether she lived for love alone, Larisa answered that the greatest misfortune is to love no one at all, and that the Snegovik ancestor Yalyane Morevna decreed that the Grand Duchess must have a soul. Volkodlak once formed subgroups with different looks: the Nyholmgarthir were bovine, the Cirnareka wolf-like, and Miscislav's skull is said to be bear-shaped.

## abilities
### LITE
All fae can wield elemental energy and magic without a Vision or a Moon Wheel. Some kinds also fly, shapeshift or work ice.
### STANDARD +
Flins says plainly that fae "don't need Moon Wheels", but he wears one as cover for his Electro power. Linnea keeps hers only because Lumi likes how shiny it is. A fae's element is not fixed by kind: Jack Frosts are usually linked to ice, but Linnea's companion Lumi shows Geo after eating huge amounts of ore. Pulcinella, a Domovoy, uses Hydro. Vodyanitsa is unusual in holding a true Hydro Vision.
### LARGE +
Other gifts belong to particular kinds. Augury Birds can foresee fixed points in their own fate, though not in anyone else's. Vila can fly on their wings, and Vesna is the only member of her family in five hundred years to awaken the ancient power in the Vila bloodline, which her grandfather names Life. Snegurochka shape Prime Ice, and male Snegovik forge ice weapons. Some fae can tame monsters: Linnea's sister Celaeno tamed a young Radiant Bladehorn. The Vila were never known for combat prowess.

## relations
### STANDARD
Old divisions between fae and humans persist, but the two peoples live side by side, especially in Snezhnograd. Flins once thought fae and humans must share an ancestry, and he now believes a common spirit unites them. Ordinary people know the fae well enough that townsfolk in Nasha Town and Snezhnograd often take Paimon for one.
### LARGE +
- The Belyi Tsar: venerated by many fae as their lord. Akvilina credits him with granting the Vila amnesty and the right to live among humans.
- The Tsaritsa: most fae accept her rule. Small pockets, such as the rogue Vila of the tundra, want the old aristocratic order back. Vesna's grandfather, Grand Duke Pyotr, believes that since Gluvasha's time the Vila have never truly had the Tsaritsa's trust.
- Humans: the humans of Port Belovodye rebelled against the fae princes when Monomakh died. Today human and fae nobles sit side by side in Snezhnograd's politics.
- Nod-Krai: few fae live there. Netochka, arriving from Snezhnograd, was surprised not to meet a single one.

## status
@version: 7.1
### STANDARD
As of 7.1 the fae are subjects of the Tsaritsa. They are numerous across Snezhnaya, rare in Nod-Krai, and still influential through noble houses, the Druzhna and individuals such as Pulcinella. The Nod-Krai loading screen says Snezhnaya's fae aristocracy and their ruler held power "a long, long time ago".
### LARGE +
Snezhnaya's playable fae appear in 7.0-7.1 (Vesna, Vodyanitsa), and Nod-Krai's in 6.x (Flins, Linnea).

## tsar_revelation
@version: 7.0
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
In the 7.0 world quest An Ark That Used to Be a Soul, the Traveler learns that the Belyi Tsar was never a fae. He was an incomplete Primordial Human made in a Hyperborean experiment on the Cryo Dragon's remains, and he took fae form whenever he spoke or travelled with fae. Aksinya later poured the Primordial Human's power into his still-living remains at the sealed Pale Crown Palace; the Traveler defeated the resulting being, and the body crumbled to dust. Fae society at large knows nothing of this.

## welkin_blurb
@availability: limited
### LARGE
The Song of the Welkin Moon web event, which previewed Nod-Krai, listed the Snowland Fae among the powers on its "wheel of fate". It described them as the fae who once followed the Belyi Tsar and ruled the Northlands, now without their former glory but still influencing the nation "in their own ways".

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay guidance (project recommendation): treat "fae" as a family of distinct peoples, not one species. Give each fae the traits of their own kind, and remember that most look nearly human. In Snezhnaya a fae neighbour, clerk or noble is ordinary, while in Nod-Krai one is a curiosity.
### LARGE +
Elderly or noble fae may remember the Belyi Tsar's court firsthand. Some are nostalgic for it and others are glad it ended. Avoid portraying all fae as servants or as villains, giving them Visions by default, or treating their elements as fixed by kind. Do not reveal the Belyi Tsar's true nature through a fae who could not know it. Keep fae children and child-like Jack Frosts innocent and factual.

## entry: Filii Adamici Evoluti
@keys: Filii Adamici Evoluti, Evolved Children of Humanity
@version: 7.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
"Filii Adamici Evoluti", "the evolved children of humanity", is the earliest name of the fae. The Belyi Tsar found it in the oldest Hyperborean records during his forays into the Shadow Realm (Notes by a Certain Noble Individual III). He concluded that the fae were never meant to be human servants, as legend claims, but were entrusted with a far grander purpose. He did not write down what that purpose was.

## entry: Six common kinds of fae
@keys: common types of fae, kinds of fae, types of fae
### STANDARD
In Linnea's Story Quest (6.5), Linnea names the six most common kinds of fae: the Vila, Domovoy, Leshy, Volkodlak, Snegovik and Jack Frosts. Her sister Celaeno adds the rarer kinds: the legendary Lantern Fae, the "Sirens with their lilting songs" (Water Imps) and the Augury Birds. A Snezhnaya loading tip adds that many other lineages have since dwindled or withdrawn from the world.

## entry: War against Miscislav
@keys: Miscislav, Miscislavli, Skoldyryt, Seriviiki, Nyholmgarthir, Cirnareka
@spoiler: minor
### LARGE
According to A Brief History of the Pale Starborn, the Volkodlak King Miscislav, "the Blood-Devourer", led an alliance of clans that included the bovine Nyholmgarthir, the wolf-like Cirnareka and the Water Imps of Dalegrad. After the Pale Star Court lured away his Vila vassals, Miscislav attacked Kitezh. In three battles the Emperor Monomakh killed him and the Grand Dukes Skoldyryt and Vodyanoy. The Cirnareka leader Seriviiki escaped and went straight into the Pale Star Court as a minister, and some scholars suspect a secret deal. Afterwards the remaining clans accepted Monomakh as Belyi Tsar.

## entry: Fae princes and the Tsaritsa
@keys: fae princes, Drevogorye, Imperial Order Reformation Edict
@keys2: fae, Tsaritsa, Anastasya, Snezhnaya
@spoiler: minor
### LARGE
When Anastasya was crowned about 500 years ago, many fae princes would not acknowledge her. The Fatui, her new force, seized the lands of the Drevogorye, Volkhovan and Cirnareka clans, and Grand Duke Drevogorye fell fighting them. The other princes surrendered their authority in exchange for honorary titles. The Snezhnograd Veche took over their administrative role. After the Imperial Order Reformation Edict, many princely fae renounced their titles and left Snezhnaya, "deeply grateful", as the chronicle puts it, for the Tsaritsa's mercy.

## entry: Changelings
@keys: changeling, changelings
@keys2: fae, Snegurochka, Augury Bird, child
### LARGE
Because fae can change shape, Snezhnayan and Nod-Krai folklore fears a fae replacing a human child. The 4.0 weapon Ballad of the Fjords tells how the hero Ajax and a Snegurochka were parted by "the wicked tricks of the changelings". In Linnea's case something like it really happened: her Augury Bird parents left her in a human cradle, hoping humanity's ignorance of fate would shield her from premature grief.

## entry: Vila rebellions
@keys: Gluvasha, Photius, Burner of Cities
@keys2: Vila, fae, Strivozha, Tsaritsa
@version: 7.0
@spoiler: minor
### ULTRA
After the Belyi Tsar's death, rebel fae clans, Volkodlak lords among them, rose against the Tsaritsa, and some Vila descended from the old nobility led small-scale rebellions of their own. Vila raiders called the "Burner of Cities" used the railway to roam the frozen plains. In Vesna's character stories, Pulcinella (the Rooster) tells her that five hundred years ago Gluvasha of House Strivozha brewed the house's red fern into mead and poisoned the former Grand Duke Photius, openly joined the rebels, and became the keeper of the mutually distrustful clans' secrets. She then handed everything to the Tsaritsa, who crushed the rebellion in one stroke, and Gluvasha surrendered her lands and abdicated. Vesna's grandfather dates the Tsaritsa's distrust of the Vila to Gluvasha's time.

## entry: Water Imp covenant
@keys: Vaselisa Vodyanovna, Dalegrad, Vodyanoy
@version: 7.1
### ULTRA
Vodyanitsa's character stories say the Water Imps' Dalegrad clan fought for Miscislav under Vodyanoy Dombertiev, "the Lackland". His daughter Vaselisa Vodyanovna mourned their dead and refused crown, treasure and land from the Belyi Tsar. Instead she asked that no Water Imp be made to serve another's desire. The Emperor swore that no Water Imp would ever have to bear arms, even one who volunteered for war. Long afterwards, almost all of Snezhnograd's Water Imps left the city, and Vodyanitsa remains its last Water Imp still singing on stage.
