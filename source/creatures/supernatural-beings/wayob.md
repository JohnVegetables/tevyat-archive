## summary
### LITE
The Wayob are the great spirits venerated by Natlan's six tribes, believed to embody the will of each tribe's ancestors. They have no body and live in the Night Kingdom, from which they guard their people against the Abyss. Each tribe calls on its own Wayob for protection and blessing. The Abyss's invasion of the Night Kingdom infected them, but they recovered once it was cleansed.
### STANDARD +
The name is used for both singular and plural. In the Adventurer Handbook, obsidian totems called Wayob Manifestations are described as forms the Wayob take when they appear in Natlan. Wayob appear from 5.0.

## nature
### LITE
According to the Dreamseeker Priest, a Wayob is "will without form or thought". Wayob are not physically present in Natlan, but can manifest there through obsidian totems and Nightsouls. They can see the past and future.
### STANDARD +
Each tribe has its own Wayob, and the dreams of the tribe's chief carry its will. People do not know why manifestations appear; the belief is that a Wayob sends one to solve a problem.
### LARGE +
The origin is told two ways. The artifact text Myths of the Night Realm says the hero Chaac, who had received primal fire from the Sage of the Stolen Flame, called out on his last night from a high mountain, and the god of the night realm answered. People across the land heard voices from another world, and this was the birth-cry of the first Wayob of the Night Kingdom. Maghan's master, the last Holy Sovereign of a tribe in what became Mictlan about 2,000 years ago, is said to have become that tribe's Wayob after she died.

## society
@version: 5.0
### STANDARD
The Wayob are central to tribal life. They choose certain members of a tribe to inherit Ancient Names. Because of the Abyss's long-lasting effects, Natlanese people without a Wayob's protection can suffer memory loss, stress and bad luck, so the tribes depend on them.
### LARGE +
A Wayob's protection does not reach beyond Natlan's borders without spending power that is reserved for fighting the Abyss, so most locals choose not to leave. Those who do must first win the Wayob's approval through their tribal chief, which involves a complicated bureaucratic process.

## relations
### STANDARD
The Wayob serve their tribes; the Lord of the Night, as ruler of the Night Kingdom, is the god of the realm they inhabit. Known Wayob include Chaac, Angakuk, the Great Spirit of This Land (Children of Echoes), Maghan's master (Masters of the Night-Wind) and a Weakened Wayob Manifestation that appeared in the Biting-Cold form and, on the surface at Tecoloapan Bay, as an adult Iktomisaurus (5.2).
### LARGE +
The Dreamseeker Priest's chronicle calls Angakuk the collective consciousness and guardian of the past. The wiki also guesses that two other Night Kingdom beings may be Wayob, but canon does not confirm it.

## history
@version: 5.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
When the Abyss invaded the Night Kingdom, the Wayob became infected with Abyssal energy. This greatly weakened their bond with their tribes and put all of Natlan in danger (Past and Future). After the Night Kingdom was purged of its Abyssal influence, the Wayob went back to their normal duties. Shortly after that, they told Citlali that Xbalanque, the first Pyro Archon, was returning to the human world (After the Peace).

## status
### STANDARD
As of 7.1 the Wayob are active again and protect their tribes as before. The wiki page records nothing later than the events above.
### ULTRA +
The wiki page records no Wayob in 6.x or 7.0-7.1 content; its sources are the Natlan-era quests (5.0-5.2), the Dreamseeker Priest's chronicles and the Tecoloapan Beach quest.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay guidance (project recommendation): Wayob speak through dreams, totems and tribal chiefs, not as ordinary persons. Do not give them faces, names or personalities beyond what canon says of individual Wayob. Natlanese characters treat them as ancestral wills and protectors.

## entry: Wayob Manifestations
@keys: Wayob Manifestation, Wayob Manifestations, obsidian totems
@version: 5.0
### STANDARD
Wayob Manifestations are obsidian totems that the Wayob use to appear in Natlan. In the game they are elite enemies in six elemental kinds (Flow-Inverted, Foliar-Swift, Rock-Cavernous, Biting-Cold, Burning-Aflame, Thundering). The people of Natlan do not know why they appear.

## entry: Myths of the Night Realm
@keys: Myths of the Night Realm
@keys2: Chaac, first Wayob
@version: 5.0
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
This artifact text from the Obsidian Codex set tells how Chaac took fire from the Sage of the Stolen Flame, shared it with his tribe and defeated a wicked dragon. When he was old and alone, he lit the first flame on a mountain and called to his companions, and the god of the night realm answered. The text calls this the birth-cry of the first Wayob.
