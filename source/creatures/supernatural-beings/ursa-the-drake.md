## summary
### LITE
Ursa the Drake was an Angel that gave up wisdom for raw power and became a corrupted dragon, said to have no distinct element. A thousand years ago it hounded Vennessa's tribe and menaced Mondstadt until Barbatos helped drive it off, after which it was sealed. Four years before the game's story the Fatui and the Knights' inspector Eroch unsealed it for experiments, and its flesh was still being exploited in Mondstadt in the Lohen story quest.
### STANDARD +
In Mondstadt the name is tied to the death of Crepus, Diluc's father, though the beast involved may have been an imitation. Its remains caused trouble again in the A Knight's Resolve story quest (Lohen), where Varka, Diluc and the Traveler razed the hidden laboratory and destroyed Ursa's remaining power.

## nature
### LITE
A draconic devolved Angel. Wiki summaries describe it as having abandoned wisdom for power, and it is not tied to a particular element. It controlled hilichurls, and could speak or at least understand speech.
### STANDARD +
Angels are Celestia's servants, and the wiki's infobox cites Hymns of the Far North for their creation by the Heavenly Principles. Ursa's flesh and blood are potent and dangerous: in the story quest researchers refine them into a serum, and the experiments are treated as deadly and corrupting.
### LARGE +
Researchers in the hidden laboratory disagreed over why severed pieces of Ursa's flesh attacked each other. Some thought the flesh was trying to reform; others read it as the drake's own curse that no fragment of its power should survive. The log's author dismisses the second view as sentimental.

## history
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
A thousand years ago the wandering tribe of Vennessa fled Ursa and its hilichurls for four days before reaching Mondstadt, where the Lawrence aristocracy enslaved them. Lord Lawrence later forced Vennessa to fight twelve battles for her freedom, the last against Ursa, with the tribe fighting beside her. Venti revealed himself as Barbatos and helped her drive Ursa and its army away. These events come from the Vennessa manga. Ursa was later sealed, and the Knights of Favonius were responsible for guarding the seal.
### LARGE +
Four years before the game's story, Inspector Eroch, who maintained the seal and secretly worked with Dottore, unsealed Ursa. Diluc and Crepus were attacked by a beast taken for Ursa (the wiki, drawing on the manga, places this on the road from Angel's Share to Dawn Winery on Diluc's eighteenth birthday). Diluc's Character Story 3 says the monster overpowered him and Crepus defeated it with a Delusion whose backlash fatally wounded him. Inspector Eroch then ordered Diluc to conceal the truth, and Diluc resigned from the Knights; the wiki adds that Eroch took the credit himself, and Varka later had him investigated and ousted. Dottore later killed the imitation, leaving Mondstadt in the Fatui's debt.
### ULTRA +
In A Knight's Resolve, Theodore, who had been taken as a child for Dottore's experiments, rediscovered the Millhaven laboratory and used Ursa's flesh to make a serum for Adorno, whose health was failing. Lohen helped him and offered himself as a test subject. Varka, Diluc and the Traveler found the site, fought Ursa's residual power in its depths and razed the laboratory, while a voice in Lohen offered 'guidance' of corrupted angelic power. He refused, cut it out and destroyed the remaining serum. Adorno forgave Theodore.

## abilities
### STANDARD
Ursa overpowered even a Vision holder in the Crepus attack, and it commanded hilichurls. Its power can be extracted into a serum.
### LARGE +
Taking the serum into the body risks being addressed by what remains of Ursa. In Lohen it spoke in a demanding, contemptuous voice, mocking mortal weakness and offering strength. Whether this is Ursa's living mind is not stated.

## relations
### STANDARD
Ursa's story crosses Barbatos, Vennessa and the Lawrence Clan, then Eroch, Dottore and the Fatui, then Crepus and Diluc, and finally Lohen, Theodore, Adorno and Varka. Nicole, who learned Lohen's suffering stemmed from a former angel, tried to give him guidance, but this failed.

## individuals
### STANDARD
The original Ursa is the thousand-year-old drake. The beast that attacked Crepus and the one Dottore slew are described by the wiki as an imitation. Ursa's remaining flesh and power at the Millhaven site were destroyed by Varka, Diluc and the Traveler in A Knight's Resolve.

## entry: Ursa's Flesh and the Ursa serum
@keys: Ursa's Flesh, Ursa serum, Ursa the Drake
@keys2: serum, flesh, Millhaven
### STANDARD
A substance refined from Ursa's flesh and blood in the Millhaven laboratory. Theodore used low-concentration serum to ease Adorno's decline; high concentration required repeatedly stimulating the flesh. Taking it risks the corrupting voice that tempted Lohen. Varka and the others destroyed the laboratory so it could not be made again.
