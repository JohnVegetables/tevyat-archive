## summary
### LITE
The Snegovik, "snow fae", are one of the six common kinds of Snowland Fae. Their women are called Snegurochka. By several in-world accounts they were made for companionship and are born with incomplete souls, and only love for a human can make them whole. They live all over Snezhnaya, and their Grand Duchess traditionally resides at the House of Hesperides.
### STANDARD +
Male Snegovik are natural-born knights who forge weapons from ice and snow. Snegurochka are born ice-and-snow mages who can work Prime Ice. The Eye of Graeae cautions that the "made for love" idea is one research perspective, not established fact.

## nature
### LITE
Snegovik are said to be created as servants or companions for the Hyperboreans. They are among the most numerous fae in Snezhnaya today and live across the whole nation.
### STANDARD +
Archive text on the Glaciomancer says their souls were incomplete when they were first made, and that only by falling in love with humans could they make themselves whole. The Eye of Graeae adds that, once a Snegovik is complete, the constraint on them is gone, which it offers as the reason tales of them so often turn cruel or cold, while a Snegovik who cannot find love suffers and dies. It stresses this is a research perspective and may not be true. The Archive wonders if a whole Snegovik still needs love, and notes that many love stories between Snegovik and humans end in tragedy.
### LARGE +
"Snegurochka" is not a formal title. According to the Eye, it came from a romantic novel that was nonetheless well researched on Snegovik, and it summed them up as a people who live for love. The wiki says becoming whole means taking the human's soul, citing quest text. Treat that mechanism as the way the stories describe it.

## appearance
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
Snegovik are humanoid with pale skin, glowing ice-blue eyes and bluish-grey to white hair, and they bear blue crystalline markings on legs, faces, hair ends and necklines like other fae. Snegurochka have blue fingertips, often tear-shaped crystal marks on their faces, knee-length off-the-shoulder gowns in blue patterns and usually a kokoshnik headpiece. Male Snegovik are about twice human height, with blue crystal forming ears, a beard and a horn-like spike on the forehead, an ice-crystal gauntlet on the right forearm, quilted coats with fur collars and silver armour on the chest and left forearm.

## abilities
### STANDARD
Males are described as natural-born knights who forge weapons from ice and snow, and females as born mages of ice and snow who can also manipulate Prime Ice. The Eye of Graeae says Pavlina, the Snegurochka princess, was unmatched in Snezhnaya at shaping Prime Ice. Snegovik can faint in prolonged warmth like other cold-bound fae.
### LARGE +
A Glacial Warden Archive entry tells of Donskoy, a Snegovik guard of the Belyi Tsar who, in the Tsar's campaign against the dark tide, unleashed a power that did not belong to his kind and became a towering giant. His final fate is unknown, and the Archive suggests such a taboo broken could not end happily.

## society
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
They are ruled by a Grand Duchess of the Snegurochka at the House of Hesperides. The wiki lists Yalyane Morevna, called Knyazhna of Blood, as an ancestor, and her granddaughter as the first Grand Duchess. The wiki's Pavlina page says Yalyane Morevna decreed that the Grand Duchess must have a soul. Oksana, the lobby receptionist, says few humans are invited there now and most guests are Snegovik living elsewhere.
### LARGE +
Succession was contested. Pavlina and Larisa competed for the seat. Both were equal as Prime Ice artisans, but Larisa surpassed her in etiquette. Larisa brought a human man to the mansion to marry him, he abandoned the ritual midway and she died. Pavlina swore on Larisa's sword that her own wedding, to Ikhor, would succeed, and used Larisa's fate to justify sacrificing his power to give her kin souls. The Eye of Graeae says some Snegovik found Pavlina emotionally indifferent and not a proper Snegovik, unlike Larisa.
### ULTRA +
The ritual in the 7.0 story involved Ikhor, a shattered segment of the Eye of Graeae, whose power Pavlina took in her repeated wedding rituals. Pavlina is recorded as killed by Aksinya and the Traveler in the quest Bell Ringing at Dusk. Her own entry carries that story.

## relations
### STANDARD
Snegovik are one of the kinds of fae under the Pale Star Court. Their bond with humans is central to how they are described: they are said to seek human companionship, and the stories about them are often tragedies. They are one of the fae kinds that served under the Belyi Tsar.

## individuals
### STANDARD
Named Snegovik include Pavlina (princess), Larisa (a rival for the title), Yalyane Morevna (an ancestor), Oksana (House of Hesperides receptionist), Donskoy (a Snegovik guard of the Belyi Tsar) and Liubov, among others. The adventurer Viliana's mother is also a Snegovik.
