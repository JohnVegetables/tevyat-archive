## summary
### LITE
Augury Birds are a rare, short-lived kind of Snowland Fae from Snezhnaya. They grow a pair of small wings on their heads, which can pass for ordinary hair under a hat, and are born able to see "crossroads in the flow of fate" for themselves alone. Very few are alive. The two known ones are the sisters Celaeno and Linnea; Linnea was raised as a human child in Nod-Krai.
### STANDARD
Augury Birds are one of the less common kinds of Snowland Fae, set apart from the six common kinds (Vila, Domovoy, Leshy, Volkodlak, Snegovik and Jack Frost). They grow head-wings as they mature, shimmer faintly with a dusting of small crystals, and live only a little longer than humans. Their defining gift is a sight of their own fate, which they regard as a burden rather than a blessing. Celaeno says the living ones can be counted on one hand.

## nature
### STANDARD
Like other Snowland Fae, Augury Birds were part of the creation of the fae in Hyperborea and are native to Snezhnaya, though the two known individuals now spend time in Nod-Krai. Unlike many fae they are not long-lived. Celaeno says they do not live much longer than humans and treats death as part of the natural cycle.

Physically they look nearly human. As they grow up they develop a pair of working wings on the head. Linnea's profile describes this as two stubborn tufts of hair that eventually unfurl into tiny wings, accompanied by a shimmering dusting of small crystals on her skin. Hidden under a hat or headwear, an Augury Bird can pass as a human, which is exactly what Linnea did for years.
### LARGE +
The kind has its own verse, recited by Celaeno: they foretell disaster and delight, gazing on the past dawn and the future night, and while others fear and revere their might, they alone hate their plight. Linnea, raised outside fae society, had never heard it. Celaeno also speaks of "generation after generation" of Augury Birds living under this burden, which implies an older line of them, but canon names none.
### ULTRA +
Celaeno finds Snezhnograd far too loud for her liking and says Linnea is right to stay away from it. That is her personal opinion, not a stated trait of the kind. She also says that Augury Birds as a whole are people whom "others fear and revere", which matches the verse below.

## abilities
### STANDARD
Augury Birds are born with a prophetic sense. Celaeno describes it as seeing "crossroads in the flow of fate", meaning immutable future events that every path leads to. Two limits matter.
- It shows only the Augury Bird's own fate. They cannot foretell the future of a friend or of the world.
- What is seen cannot be changed, according to Celaeno. She says that trying to defy it only makes the suffering worse.

Linnea's first vision came after she touched Celaeno's hand during the Spirit Envoy Championship in Nod-Krai: she collapsed and saw her own future, including the end of her life, which left her numb and in tears. How the sight is triggered is not explained in the sources read.

Like other fae, Augury Birds use elemental power without a Vision. Linnea carries a Moon Wheel that her Jack Frost companion once swallowed, and her power is Geo.
### LARGE +
Celaeno's sight appears in play at the Spirit Envoy Championship. She reads her opponent's moves in her match against Jahoda, and Linnea notices that she has a similar intuition that helps her predict opponents. When Lumi unexpectedly grew to a huge size in the semi-final, Celaeno was too shocked to see what was coming, so her sight can be disrupted by sudden shocks. Celaeno also tamed Haemus, a young Radiant Bladehorn separated from its parents.
### ULTRA +
Celaeno says humanity has many ways of observing fate, such as the stars and the ley lines, and that the Augury Birds' gift is feeble beside them. In the Traveler's recollection, Vedrfolnir "the Visionary" once said something close to her view, that foresight without the power to change events only crushes the spirit. That parallel is the Traveler's observation, not a statement that the two abilities are related.

## society
### STANDARD
There is no recorded Augury Bird community in canon. The known family is a single household in the tundra of inland Snezhnaya: Celaeno, her parents, and Linnea, who is Celaeno's younger sister. Celaeno also helped raise the human girl her parents swapped Linnea with.
### LARGE +
The family's solution to the burden of sight is the changeling swap. In their lore, an Augury Bird sent far away to grow up among humans can have the awakening of its abilities delayed. Linnea was exchanged for a human child who had a rare disease that human doctors could not cure and who would have died if left among humans; the parents saved her life and raised her as their own. Linnea was raised in Nod-Krai by a human couple who loved her until her wings appeared, after which they grew distant, and they eventually left her on her own with money and the hat her mother had made. This is told in Linnea's profile and her Story Quest, and the swap is Celaeno's explanation for it.
### ULTRA +
Celaeno resented Linnea for escaping the fate she herself had to face and at first meant to show her the harsh reality of their kind, but she could not go through with it when she found how much Linnea had struggled. After the tournament she offered to keep in touch by letter and to invite Linnea home when she next returned to the tundra, and took gifts for both of Linnea's families back north. She also released Haemus into the wild after the competition.

## relations
### STANDARD
Linnea and Celaeno are sisters by birth. Lumi, a Geo Jack Frost, travels with Linnea. In Nod-Krai, the Adventurers' Guild and the children of Speranza know Linnea as an advisor, naturalist and teacher. Lumi's size-changing outburst, the Traveler and Paimon, and Aguara (the Traveler's Tepetlisaur Whelp, who found Celaeno by scent) all feature in the story that reveals the kind.
### LARGE +
Linnea's Story Quest is the only place the kind is explained to a human audience. Celaeno speaks of the six common kinds before naming the "legendary Lantern Fae", the "Sirens with their lilting songs" (Water Imps) and "the Augury Birds". Unlike Lantern Fae and Water Imps, the Augury Bird sisters are described as part of the same close family.

## individuals
### STANDARD
- Celaeno: the elder sister. A tall young woman with long, straight dark blue hair and lavender eyes. Blunt, prickly and protective of the human girl her family adopted; she fought in the Spirit Envoy Championship with Haemus, lost in the final to the Traveler, and afterwards returned to Snezhnaya. After the quest she can be found in Snezhnograd, where she says she is running errands and escorting the human girl, and where she finds the capital too loud.
- Linnea: the younger sister, raised by humans in Nod-Krai, now an Adventurers' Guild advisor and naturalist. Playable (Geo, Moon Wheel).
- Their parents: unnamed Augury Birds in the tundra of inland Snezhnaya.
### LARGE +
Linnea's adoptive human parents are Latkin and Zinovia in her profile, and they raised her until her wings appeared. Neither is an Augury Bird. The human girl swapped with Linnea is not named in the sources read.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
A recommended way to portray an Augury Bird, as this project's interpretation and not canon: treat the sight as private, unwelcome and personal, never as a fortune-telling service. A character may say that they know their own fate but cannot reveal anyone else's. Keep the wings hidden under a hat in public. Do not give the kind a catalogue of prophecies or let them predict world events.

### STANDARD
Celaeno is the elder Augury Bird sister, from the tundra of inland Snezhnaya. Her parents, wanting to spare their next child the burden of foresight, had Linnea raised by humans and brought up a human girl with a rare disease in her place. Celaeno came to Nod-Krai intending to hurt Linnea with the truth, found she could not, and became a wary relation. She tamed the Radiant Bladehorn Haemus and fought in the Spirit Envoy Championship before releasing him.
