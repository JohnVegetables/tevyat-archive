## summary
### LITE
The adepti (singular adeptus) are Liyue's immortal guardians. Most are illuminated beasts: ancient cranes, deer, qilin and similar creatures that speak, live for millennia and wield elemental power. A few gods are also counted among them. Most signed a contract with Rex Lapis (Morax), the "Prime of Adepti", to protect Liyue from demons and evil gods, and they fought beside him in the Archon War. Afterwards most withdrew to the mountains of Jueyun Karst. Well-known adepti include Cloud Retainer, Moon Carver, Mountain Shaper, Madame Ping, Xiao and the half-qilin Ganyu.
### STANDARD +
Their full title is "the Mighty and Illuminated Adepti." Mortals revere them almost as gods and leave offerings at the edge of Jueyun Karst, but rarely go further. Now that Liyue is under human rule, several adepti live quietly among humans in Liyue Harbor.

## nature
### STANDARD
Most adepti show mortals their true beast forms. Full-blooded adepti can also take human form, and some prefer it, like Madame Ping and Xiao. They are long-lived but can die. Children of an adeptus and a human, such as Ganyu and Yanfei, can be called adepti. Humans are not adepti, even when they master adeptal arts, as Shenhe has.
### LARGE +
- **Beast forms.** Cloud Retainer and Mountain Shaper are cranes, and Moon Carver and the late Skybracer are deer-like. Ganyu is half qilin, and Yanfei's father is an illuminated beast. Ivanovich says that at formal occasions Rex Lapis himself appears as a celestial cross between two of Liyue's mythical creatures (Rite of Descension, 1.0).
- **Human guises.** Madame Ping chooses to look like an old woman; her age does not cause it, and she has appeared young in the past. Moon Carver took pride in his beast form and refused human disguises for many years. During the limited-time 4.4 Lantern Rite event, he and Mountain Shaper came to Liyue Harbor in human guise, calling themselves Houzhang and Jiehu.
- **Half-blooded adepti** look human apart from a few traits like horns. Canon has not shown them shapeshifting.
- **Humans.** Shenhe, trained by Cloud Retainer, explicitly has no sacred name because she is human. Developer commentary says humans have a different, "higher" purpose in Teyvat.
- **Mortality.** Skybracer died protecting Liyue in the Archon War, and Sea Gazer died long before the game. Records of Jueyun tells of illuminated beasts who did not want to wait for a natural death and asked Rex Lapis to turn their bodies into stone.
- **Gods called adepti.** Morax is the "Prime of Adepti." The Stove God Marchosius, later reborn as Guoba, was also counted among them.
### ULTRA +
- **Origins.** Unknown. Madame Ping says the arts Rex Lapis taught them, such as making their own realms, were part of their "illumination", which suggests that not every illuminated beast is born illuminated. Some Liyue folk believe adepti came from Celestia and return there when they die. Legends also say humans once sought to join them by passing the "trials of heaven and earth": the earth trial in Taishan Mansion and the heaven trial at the Dwelling in the Clouds.

## society
### STANDARD
The adepti are a loose fellowship under Rex Lapis, not a government. Most live apart in mountain abodes and meet only in a crisis. Many go by sacred names such as Cloud Retainer, Moon Carver, Mountain Shaper and Streetward Rambler.
### LARGE +
In Chinese, sacred names are usually four characters followed by the honorific "Perfected Lord"; English shortens them. Rex Lapis's title uses the higher honorific "Sovereign Lord." Not every adeptus uses a sacred name: Madame Ping uses an ordinary alias, and Ganyu is not known to have one. Xiao tells the Traveler to call him "Adeptus Xiao."

Ordinary people hold their rituals only at the edges of Jueyun Karst. A Sigil of Permission, a talisman Rex Lapis made and filled with adeptal power, lets its holder enter the adepti's lands safely. Most of the original sigils' power has faded, and the Fatui have reverse-engineered copies. Travellers and herb-gatherers do wander there unharmed, but they get no audience.

Among themselves the adepti behave more like quarrelsome old friends than solemn deities. Cloud Retainer, Mountain Shaper and Moon Carver bicker constantly, and Cloud Retainer's dislike of Mountain Shaper's beloved mint is a running joke between them.
### ULTRA +
Liyue marks the death of an adeptus or a god with the Rite of Parting, which the Wangsheng Funeral Parlor conducts with offerings such as Glaze Lilies, a Cleansing Bell and Everlasting Incense. By Zhongli's account it has been needed only a handful of times in three thousand years, and the tradition has grown simpler and is fading. The rite prepared in Chapter I (1.0) was for Rex Lapis himself.

## abilities
### STANDARD
Adepti command elemental energy without needing a Vision. Each carries a personal "adeptal energy" that is dangerous to humans over long exposure. They create private pocket realms called abodes and practise adeptal arts. They are formidable fighters, and the yakshas were their elite demon-slayers.
### LARGE +
- **The "third eye."** Liyue calls the adepti "three-eyed": their elemental power is read as an inner eye, the equivalent of a Vision. Xiao wears an outward Vision because he lives in human form. His character story asks whether adepti receive Visions from Celestia as humans do, and leaves the question unanswered.
- **Adeptal energy.** In Turning Point (1.0), Madame Ping's energy made the Traveler's attacks release shockwaves, Ganyu's healed them steadily and Xiao's made them faster.
- **Abodes.** Madame Ping says Rex Lapis granted the power to make their own realms (domains), which can even take the form of a teapot, as part of their "illumination." The Serenitea Pot comes from this art.
- **Craft.** Cloud Retainer invents adeptal mechanisms. She upgraded the Guizhong Ballistae against Osial.
- **Limits.** Mortals cannot bear much adeptal energy. Qiqi was an herb-gatherer mortally wounded when she strayed into a battle between adepti and demons. Several adepti poured their energy into her to save her, but it was too much: she went on a rampage, and Mountain Shaper had to seal her in amber for centuries.
### ULTRA +
- **Esoteric arts.** Zhongli used the "gifting of dreams and visions" to tell the adepti and the Qixing that Morax had retired rather than died. Dream Trawler separates soul from body and can summon another's spirit. The yaksha Pervases used Mortal Lingering to take on a physical vessel again for a time.
- **Shaping the land.** Folk belief says the adepti formed Liyue's landscape, with rivers from their saliva and grass from their hair. Mountain Shaper calls this nonsense. He explains that the amber on Mt. Hulao comes from a plant, the karst crawler, which he can partly control.

## relations
### STANDARD
Adepti are bound to Rex Lapis by contract, and mortals worship them from a distance. Their relationship with the Liyue Qixing is guarded. When Rex Lapis appeared to die in Chapter I (1.0), they suspected the Qixing, until the threat of Osial forced the two sides into an alliance.
### LARGE +
Some adepti once stayed in Liyue Harbor to help the Qixing govern, but by Chapter I they had long been shut out of politics. The exception is Ganyu, who still serves as the Qixing's secretary. Zhongli later told the adepti in dreams that Morax had retired.
- **Disciples.** Shenhe and Shuyu are students of Cloud Retainer, and Yaoyao is Madame Ping's. Yuandai, a crane from Mt. Aocang who gained sentience, is also Cloud Retainer's disciple.
- **Teapot spirits.** The adepti created Tubby and Chubby to look after their realms. These spirits bear sacred names but are not adepti.
- **The Fatui.** Childe gave the Traveler a forged Sigil of Permission, and the Fatui used duplicates to free Osial from Rex Lapis's seal.
### ULTRA +
Outside Liyue, the onmyouji Kamuna Harunosuke travelled to Liyue after the cataclysm to learn the "more powerful way of the adepti," and combined it with tengu arts to develop the paper seals of Inazuma's Kairagi.

## history
### STANDARD
The adepti fought for Morax and Guizhong in the Archon War. After the Guili Assembly fell, Morax led his people south of Mt. Tianheng to found Liyue Harbor, and the adepti contracted with him to protect it. When the war ended about two thousand years ago, most withdrew to Jueyun Karst.
### LARGE +
During the war, Rex Lapis also summoned the yakshas, belligerent illuminated beasts, to purge the demons born from the resentment of fallen gods. Five were counted the strongest: Bosacius, Indarias, Bonanus, Menogias and Alatus (Xiao). Over centuries the karmic burden drove them to rage, madness and death; some turned on each other and others vanished. Bosacius, long missing, is now known to have died in The Chasm, and the other three are also dead. Only Alatus remains.
### ULTRA +
In Chenyu Vale, a god at war with Morax tried to flood the Bishui River. Her own adepti, the Herblord and Fujin, turned against her. Fujin threw the Rainjade Votive into the river to stop the flood, which exhausted her for thousands of years. The Herblord later became Changsheng, the white snake who accompanies Baizhu. Xiao's character story says many human festivals began as imitations of the adepti's victories over man-eating monsters, and that the resentful remnants of fallen gods surge most violently during Lantern Rite.

## status

### LITE
As of 7.1 the adepti still guard Liyue, mostly from seclusion. Rex Lapis lives on as Zhongli. Cloud Retainer (as Xianyun) and Madame Ping live in Liyue Harbor, and Xiao keeps watch from Wangshu Inn.
### LARGE +
In the 6.3 Lantern Rite story, Rex Lapis ordered the adepti to Liyue Harbor to stand watch, and they helped the Traveler deal with a Deadly Self of the White Horse Adeptus that had possessed Mountain Shaper; the Traveler and Zhongli then restored Zibai.

## entry: Sacred Names of the Adepti
@keys: sacred name, adeptus name, Perfected Lord, Streetward Rambler
### STANDARD
Adepti go by sacred names. In Chinese they are usually four characters plus the honorific "Perfected Lord" (zhenjun). English keeps a short form: Cloud Retainer, Moon Carver, Mountain Shaper, Streetward Rambler (Madame Ping), and the dead Sea Gazer and Skybracer. Rex Lapis ("Sovereign Lord Rock King") and Xiao ("Conqueror of Demons", literally "Great Sage who Subdues Demons") use higher or different forms. The names do not prove adeptus status. Teapot spirits like Tubby (Rain Calmer) have them, and humans have adopted grand fake ones to pose as adepti. Shenhe has none because she is human.

## entry: Jueyun Karst and the Seclusion of the Adepti
@keys: abode of the adepti, Sigil of Permission
@keys2: Jueyun Karst, adepti, adeptus
### STANDARD
After the Archon War most adepti withdrew to Jueyun Karst, the misty peaks west of Liyue Harbor, including Mt. Aocang, Mt. Hulao and Qingyun Peak. Locals hold their rituals at its edges and do not go further. A Sigil of Permission, a Rex Lapis-made talisman, lets its holder enter the adepti's lands without offence. In Chapter I the Traveler carried a Fatui copy from Childe there to seek the adepti's help.
### LARGE +
Each adeptus keeps an abode, often a pocket realm reached through their mountain. Cloud Retainer's is on Mt. Aocang. During the limited-time 4.4 Lantern Rite event, Moon Carver and Mountain Shaper said Jueyun Karst had grown unusually quiet since she moved to the city.

## entry: Adeptal Energy and Mortals
@keys: adeptal energy, adeptal power
### STANDARD
Adeptal energy is the distinct power each adeptus carries. Lent briefly, it can empower a mortal: three adepti infused the Traveler during the battle against Osial. Over longer periods it overwhelms human souls. Madame Ping warns that prolonged contact with adeptal treasures harms humans. Qiqi is the cautionary case: so much adeptal energy was poured into her to save her life that she could not bear it, and she had to be sealed in amber.

## entry: Stone Beasts of Liyue
@keys: stone beasts, Records of Jueyun
@keys2: Liyue, illuminated beast, adepti, Rex Lapis
### LARGE
Records of Jueyun relates a legend from Qingce Village elders. After the Archon War, some illuminated beasts missed their days of service, and knowing they would one day die, they asked Rex Lapis to turn their bodies to stone. He granted it. Fishermen, Silvergrass harvesters and miners say these stone beasts wake on cool autumn nights to patrol the land, and statues seem to shift position overnight. The book's author admits no one has actually seen one move.

## entry: The Adepti and the Osial Crisis
@keys: Osial, Overlord of the Vortex
@keys2: adepti, Cloud Retainer, Moon Carver, Mountain Shaper, Jade Chamber
@version: 1.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
After Rex Lapis's apparent death at the Rite of Descension, the adepti blamed the Qixing and the two sides faced off outside the city. When the Fatui freed the sealed god Osial, the adepti joined the Qixing and Millelith on the Jade Chamber. Cloud Retainer, Moon Carver and Mountain Shaper channelled their power into upgraded Guizhong Ballistae. Madame Ping, Ganyu and Xiao lent the Traveler adeptal energy. Ningguang finally brought the Jade Chamber down on Osial. Cloud Retainer remarked that the adepti had not fought a god in several millennia.

## entry: Adepti Among Mortals in Modern Liyue
@keys: Houzhang, Jiehu
@keys2: Moon Carver, Mountain Shaper, Liyue Harbor
@version: 4.4
@availability: limited
### LARGE
During the 4.4 Lantern Rite event, Moon Carver and Mountain Shaper visited Liyue Harbor disguised as humans named Houzhang and Jiehu, looking for Cloud Retainer, who had moved into the city as Xianyun and forgotten to tell them about the festivities. Paimon at first took them for impostors, since neither had ever cared for city life. Madame Ping (since long before), Xianyun and Yanfei show the same trend: adepti and their descendants are learning to live in a Liyue that now rules itself.

In the Lantern Rite story A Lanternlit Ode to the Silver Moon (6.3, first released with a limited-time event and later made a permanent Story Quest), Rex Lapis called the adepti to Liyue Harbor to stand watch. Zizhi, the Second "Deadly Self" and one fragment of the legendary White Horse Adeptus's divided soul, possessed Mountain Shaper. Moon Carver, Cloud Retainer, Madame Ping and Xiao cornered him at Mt. Aocang, where the possessed Mountain Shaper was planting mint all over Cloud Retainer's abode. The Traveler and Zhongli then reunited the soul's three fragments, restoring Zibai. Xianyun recalls the White Horse Adeptus as a mysterious white mare who aided Rex Lapis in the Archon War, and Madame Ping had heard mountain clans call her the daughter of Time. Zibai is counted among the Eight Adepts and was originally an Angel, so whether she is an adeptus in the strict sense is unclear.

## entry: Half-Blooded Adepti
@keys: half-adeptus, half qilin, adeptus blood
### LARGE
Full-blooded illuminated beasts can have children with humans. Ganyu, half qilin, witnessed the founding of Liyue Harbor thousands of years ago yet looks young. Yanfei's father is an illuminated-beast adeptus who, as Paimon puts it, "went back to get married" to her mortal mother, a merchant. Yanfei shrugs off the adeptus title in favour of being a legal adviser. Both obtained Visions in the ordinary human way. It is unknown how their heritage affects their aging or their powers.
