## summary
### LITE
In Liyue a zombie is a dead person brought back into suspended animation, by adeptal energy or by medicine. The body stays cold and stiff, and talismans help sustain the state. Two are known: Qiqi, the herb-gathering apprentice of Bubu Pharmacy, and Jialiang, a husband and father revived by Baizhu's Elixir of Immortality.
### STANDARD +
Zombies normally act on orders from whoever awakened them. Both known cases are exceptions: Qiqi gives herself orders, and Jialiang kept his own mind.

## nature
### LITE
A zombie can look like an ordinary person at a glance, but the body is cold and stiff, as in rigor mortis, so zombies are usually forced to move by hopping. Talismans on the body help sustain suspended animation. Zombies need orders before they act.
### STANDARD +
Normally the awakener becomes the master and the zombie obeys; the wiki infers that an awakener could also choose to set a zombie free, since neither Qiqi nor Jialiang is controlled by anyone. Qiqi avoids hopping with daily calisthenics, done at night so Hu Tao does not see. Her appearance stays fixed at the moment of death, so estimates of her age are unreliable.
### LARGE +
Memory suffers. Qiqi forgets a person within about three days unless she keeps reinforcing her impression of them, and she carries a notebook for anything important. Jialiang is the contrast: he kept his memory and ordinary speech after revival. Liyue's zombies correspond to the jiangshi of Chinese folklore, visible in Qiqi's hat and forehead talisman.
### ULTRA +
Qiqi's character story also records a quirk of her state: she dislikes heat, which reminds her of death, and her flesh begins to decay if she stays too long in hot places, so she prefers the cold. Her Vision, a Cryo one, was given in her last moment of life, born of her wish to stop time and live happily again.

## abilities
### STANDARD
In combat Qiqi drops the self-control she normally keeps and her body returns to its undead state, giving her unusual strength and speed. Her character story notes she once sent a whole crew of Treasure Hoarders flying. Her self-given orders persist until completed or until someone cancels them by hugging her from behind and sincerely saying something like "I love you the most"; a task too hard for her can leave her stuck below a cliff, unable to give up.
### LARGE +
Jialiang's case is different in kind: in The Formula for a Miracle Baizhu says the Elixir of Immortality suspends him between life and death, calls it a crude imitation of an adeptal art, and says he does not know how long it will last, only that it gives time to say goodbye. Qiqi gave Jialiang rules for living as a zombie, beginning with stretching regularly.

## society
@version: 3.6
### STANDARD
Zombies are not a people or community, only individual cases. Qiqi lives and works at Bubu Pharmacy under Baizhu. Hu Tao, director of the Wangsheng Funeral Parlor, wants to bury her, and Qiqi hides from her. After his revival Jialiang left Liyue with his family for Natlan, hoping the hot springs and plants there could help his condition.

## relations
### STANDARD
Qiqi's zombie state is tied to the adepti, whose energy revived her. Jialiang's is tied instead to Baizhu's medicine, and Baizhu's elixir is described as an imitation of adeptal arts.

## individuals
### LITE
Qiqi and Jialiang.
### STANDARD +
Qiqi is a playable Cryo character with a long history as a zombie. Jialiang appears only in the Heart of Healing story quest chain.

## entry: Qiqi's origin as a zombie
@keys: Qiqi's origin, Mountain Shaper, Qiqi's amber
@keys2: Qiqi, zombie, adepti
### STANDARD
Qiqi's Character Stories relate, as a long-forgotten ancient tale, that a herb-picking girl strayed into the adepti's realm during a clash between adepti and demons, was caught in it, and received a Vision as she was dying. The adepti would not let her die and each gave her some of their strength. She woke but went berserk, unable to control the energy, and Mountain Shaper sealed her in amber. Centuries later humans found the amber and took it to the Wangsheng Funeral Parlor for burial; the road was rough and the seal was worn, so she woke, broke the amber and fled. In the hills she met Baizhu, who took her in. Because it is told as legend, details are not independently confirmed.

## entry: Jialiang's revival
@keys: Jialiang, Elixir of Immortality
@version: 3.6
### STANDARD
In the story quest The Formula for a Miracle, Jialiang, a patient with a rare heart condition who had long been sustained by god remains, agreed to let those remains be destroyed even though it meant death. Baizhu had had Qiqi prepare an Elixir of Immortality, so Jialiang woke at Bubu Pharmacy covered in talismans and was told he was now more akin to a zombie than a human. Baizhu said it was only a crude imitation of an adeptal art. Hu Tao had made the suggestion and asked Jialiang to keep it secret.

## entry: Zombies and burial
@keys: Wangsheng Funeral Parlor, bury Qiqi
@keys2: Qiqi, zombie
### STANDARD
Hu Tao, the 77th-generation director of the Wangsheng Funeral Parlor, tells Qiqi it would be easier if she just let herself be buried. Qiqi has no wish to be buried, exercises when no one is about, and has learned hiding places to avoid her. Qiqi calls her face punchable and holds a grudge against her,.
