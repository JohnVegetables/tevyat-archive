## summary
### LITE
Bake-danuki, also just called tanuki, are youkai of Inazuma that look like small plump raccoon dogs, often with a leaf on the head and a bag around the neck. They can transform into objects and even people, and love pranking humans. Their leader is Ioroi, the Hidden Protector of the Grove, sealed inside a large statue in Chinju Forest.
### STANDARD +
The in-game Archive calls them carefree, leaving no trace when they go, with mystical arts known only to their own kind. They are not wicked and tease humans for fun. The one recorded exception was a past battle in Chinju Forest, after which most bake-danuki were sealed in stone statues.

## nature
### LITE
Youkai tricksters. They shapeshift into objects and people, and the Archive stresses they mean no harm.
### STANDARD +
In open-world play they disguise themselves as torches, vases, crates, rock piles, chests, explosive barrels and similar objects, or dance and teleport away to lead players on. When a prank ends they vanish in a puff of smoke. Kichiboushi is a rare brown-furred example. Ioroi's statue form shows a large, jolly bake-danuki in a conical straw hat holding a sake cup, surrounded by smaller statues.
### LARGE +
Ioroi's pranks could be large: he once turned the lavender melons of Konda Village into potatoes and sunsettias into tigers to draw out the Saiguu. In the Mikawa Festival event he scolds three younger tanuki for using external power and tells them not all humans are bad.

## abilities
### LITE
Transformation into objects and humans, and illusions. They are not fighters.
### STANDARD +
Their magic is passed down only among their kind. A folk tale in Glimpses of Mysterious Inazuma has the samurai Takehiko learn bake-danuki magic to hide himself inside a stone; this is in-world folklore.
### LARGE +
A tanuki impersonating the human Atsuko and the doppelganger incident in the Mikawa Festival event show how convincing human disguises can be. The Saiguu secretly learned some of Ioroi's magic, according to the kitsune entry.

## society
### LITE
Loose communities under one leader, Ioroi, who lives in Chinju Forest on Narukami Island.
### STANDARD +
Named bake-danuki include Ioroi, Kichiboushi, Shousei, Gyoubu Koban, Giichiro (a statue), Mamesuke, Shibasuke, Tsukumo, Tetsunosuke, Ginkaku and Kinkaku. Ioroi plans to make Kichiboushi his successor once the younger tanuki is grown.
### LARGE +
Ioroi was ordered by the Raiden Shogun to serve as Protector of the Grove after the Saiguu pleaded for his life. Because he was sealed, he learned about the wider world only from tanuki who stayed free.

## relations
### LITE
Rivals and sometime friends of the kitsune. They tangle with the Shogunate's officers when pranks go too far.
### STANDARD +
Ioroi and the Kitsune Saiguu competed for years: she dared him to steal a Naku Weed from the imperial garden, Ei nearly cut him down, and the Saiguu begged for his life. He still owes her, and calls her "that sneaky kitsune".
### LARGE +
During the Cataclysm the Saiguu tricked Ioroi into a game of hide-and-seek to keep him away from the fighting. He took it as a game that never ended, began breaking kitsune statues, and stole a comb used in the Sacred Sakura Cleansing Ritual. The Shogunate sent the onmyouji Kamuna Harunosuke, who sealed him and many bake-danuki in stone as a lesson.
### ULTRA +
Yae Miko treats the tanuki with a kitsune's teasing: in the Mikawa Festival event she goaded Ioroi into sending tanuki to help by claiming kitsune would do what tanuki would not. Hilichurls near Chinju Forest keep him company, though they cannot talk to him.

## individuals
### STANDARD
- Ioroi: leader, Hidden Protector of the Grove, sealed in Chinju Forest.
- Kichiboushi: a young, rare brown bake-danuki who guides the Traveler to Ioroi's comb and is Ioroi's intended successor.
### LARGE +
- Tetsunosuke, Ginkaku and Kinkaku: tanuki Ioroi had believed sealed, found behind the doppelganger incident at the Mikawa Festival (limited-time event).
- Shousei, Gyoubu Koban, Mamesuke and Shibasuke: smaller named tanuki met in the open world and the Tanuki-Bayashi quest.

## history
### STANDARD
Bake-danuki have long lived on Narukami's lands. About 500 years ago the Cataclysm and Ioroi's reaction to losing the Saiguu led to most of them being sealed in stone statues.
### LARGE +
After Tanuki-Bayashi in the Forest (2.0) the Traveler can talk to Ioroi's statue, and in Eight Locales Over Mountains and Seas (2.4) he asks the Traveler to take Kichiboushi around Teyvat for training.

## status
### STANDARD
As of 7.1 bake-danuki appear only in Inazuma. Ioroi remains in statue form; no Nod-Krai (6.x) or Snezhnaya (7.0-7.1) content features tanuki.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay interpretation (this project's recommendation, not canon): playful and boastful, quick to claim credit, secretly kind. A tanuki prank should be harmless and end in a puff of smoke.
### LARGE +
Do not make tanuki vicious, and do not invent their numbers, a hidden village or powers beyond transformation and illusion.

## entry: Ioroi
@keys: Ioroi, Hidden Protector of the Grove, Protector of the Grove
### STANDARD
Ioroi is the leader of the bake-danuki, sealed in a statue in Chinju Forest. He was spared by the Raiden Shogun at the Saiguu's request and made Protector of the Grove. He misread her disappearance during the Cataclysm as hide-and-seek, caused trouble to draw her out, took the Sacred Sakura comb, and was sealed by Kamuna Harunosuke. He deflects whenever her fate is mentioned.

## entry: Kichiboushi
@keys: Kichiboushi
### STANDARD
Kichiboushi is a rare brown-furred bake-danuki who helps the Traveler find Ioroi's comb and whom Ioroi intends as his successor.

## entry: Tanuki pranks
@keys: bake-danuki prank, tanuki prank
### STANDARD
Open-world tanuki lead players with dancing and teleporting or disguise themselves as nearby objects. Completing fifteen pranks earns an achievement; the spoils are ordinary goods such as produce.
