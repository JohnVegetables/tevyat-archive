## summary
### LITE
Illuminated beasts (xianshou) are animals of Liyue that have gained supernatural power: enormous lifespans, formidable elemental combat ability and the gift of human speech. Qilin, suanni, cranes and deer are the best known. Nearly all named examples are adepti who signed a contract with Rex Lapis to protect Liyue, so the terms overlap heavily. The full-blooded ones can take human form and can have children with mortals.
### STANDARD +
Liyue folk treat them as half legend, half neighbour. Many withdrew into the mountains after the Archon War, but a number still live among people, and some have left half-blooded descendants such as Ganyu and Yanfei.

## nature
### STANDARD
Canon says little about what makes a beast "illuminated". Madame Ping calls Sub-Space Creation, a blessing she and her fellow elders received from Rex Lapis, part of their "illumination"; the wiki reads this as a hint that illumination can be acquired and is not simply inborn. Cloud Retainer and Mountain Shaper are cranes, yet ordinary cranes show no sign of it. Full-blooded illuminated beasts can shapeshift into human form.
### LARGE +
- **Children with mortals.** Records of Jueyun tells a folktale of a young qilin who, after eating the clothes of an herb gatherer bathing in a pool, takes human form to make up for it and spends a night with him; versions differ on the ending, either a child left in a basket or the qilin living among humans. Half-blooded children are mostly human with a few beast traits, such as Ganyu's horns.
- **Lifespans.** Ganyu is old enough to remember the Guili Assembly's fall about 3,700 years ago yet looks like a teenager. Yanfei looks like a teenager, her mortal mother is alive and her father is a full-blooded illuminated beast, and she knows she may live for millennia. How heritage governs ageing is not stated.
- **Mortality.** Illuminated beasts can die: Skybracer and Sea Gazer are dead, and of the yakshas only Xiao (Alatus) remains. Records of Jueyun (Vol. 1) also relays the Qingce Village elders' story of illuminated beasts who missed serving Rex Lapis, knew life would end for them too, and begged him to turn them into everlasting stone (see creature.adepti).
### ULTRA +
The English phrase is flexible. Shenhe's character story mentions "odd passing illuminated beast" among her few visitors, where the Chinese text uses a different word meaning spirit or divine beast. Qiqi uses the playful term "adeptibeast" for the Cocogoat, a creature that turned out not to exist.

## society
### STANDARD
After the Archon War, under Rex Lapis's direction, many adepti withdrew to mountains and bamboo groves in seclusion and left human affairs alone. Records of Jueyun adds that some illuminated beasts with long friendships with humans chose to stay: they hid in mountain hamlets or walked the streets of the cities, quietly helping society with their powers.
### LARGE +
Some cultivated those friendships deliberately and left bloodlines in Liyue Harbor. Others stayed apart by temperament: Lingyuan, a suanni of Chenyu Vale, never grew close to humanity and preferred life as a wild mountain beast. Records of Jueyun says a qilin's every movement is dictated by an age-old etiquette unchanged for a thousand years.
### ULTRA +
Modern Liyue shows the range. Cloud Retainer now lives in Liyue Harbor as Xianyun, Madame Ping keeps a teapot realm, Ganyu serves the Qixing and Yanfei works as a legal adviser. Ganyu hides her horns by calling them ornamental heirlooms, because she fears people would keep their distance if they knew the truth (character story).

## types
### STANDARD
- **Qilin.** Gentle, vegetarian beasts. By folklore they drink spring water, eat fragrant herbs, never step on an insect or break a blade of grass, and fight only when peace is gone. Ganyu is half qilin, and her horns are sensitive.
- **Suanni.** Found mainly around Chenyu Vale and believed by locals to have been the land's original rulers, who secluded themselves once peace came. Known: the Solitary Suanni (called Ancient Lord of the Mountains), Lingyuan, and Man Chai, Gaming's companion.
- **Cranes.** Cloud Retainer, Mountain Shaper and the yaksha Pervases. Yuandai is a crane who gained sentience; she could take human form only through Cloud Retainer's potion, and it is unknown whether she counts as an illuminated beast.
- **Deer.** Moon Carver and the late Skybracer.
### LARGE +
- **Xiezhi.** Not described in-game. Yanfei's father is implied to be one, and a xiezhi appears on her legal stamper, depicted as goat- or deer-like with antlers behind the ears.
- **Birds of unknown kind.** Xiao's beast form is hinted at in his design, constellation and the name Alatus as an avian creature, perhaps the mythical garuda or peng; the species is not stated. Sea Gazer appeared goose-like in a Mingxiao lantern during the limited-time Lantern Rite event The Exquisite Night Chimes.
- **Yakshas.** Belligerent illuminated beasts summoned by Rex Lapis to fight demons. They prefer human form, and their true forms are believed to be animals. Indarias is implied to be a fire rat, Menogias ape-like and Pervases a crane. See faction.yakshas.

## relations
### STANDARD
Most are bound to Rex Lapis by contract. Their relationship with mortals ranges from deep friendship to avoidance. Cloud Retainer taught the human Shenhe; Gaming formed a bond with his suanni companion, Man Chai.
### LARGE +
At Chenyu Vale, Lingyuan, Fujin and the Herblord (Changsheng) once served the region's goddess. When she caused the Bishui River to flood to stop Morax, Fujin and the Herblord turned on her and Lingyuan helped herd the people to safety. Thousands of years later, Lingyuan disrupted the vale's spirit veins hoping to restore Fujin's power, until the Traveler and Fujin persuaded her to stop in Chenyu's Blessings of Sunken Jade (4.4). Fujin reverted to her original carp form to recover.
### ULTRA +
Man Chai gave Gaming a glittering stone from its mane after Gaming imitated its movements for a day on Mt. Lingmeng, founding the Mighty Mythical Beasts troupe, per Gaming's character story. Lingyuan is well aware that Man Chai chose to stay with Gaming.

## individuals

- **Moon Carver** and **Skybracer** (dead) - deer.
- **Lingyuan**, **Solitary Suanni**, **Man Chai** - suanni.

- **Yakshas** - Bosacius, Indarias, Bonanus, Menogias, Alatus (Xiao), Pervases.
- **Fujin** (carp) and **the Herblord** - former Chenyu Vale adepti.
### ULTRA +
Others: **Sea Gazer** (dead). **Yuandai** (sentient crane) is a borderline case.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 illuminated beasts are still part of Liyue life. Most live in seclusion or quietly among people. Xiao, Ganyu, Xianyun and Madame Ping appear in the world, and Chenyu Vale's suanni live in the mountains.

## entry: Qilin
@keys: qilin, qilins
@keys2: illuminated beast, Ganyu, Liyue
### STANDARD
In Liyue legend, qilin are the gentlest illuminated beasts. They roam mountain forests, appearing on nights when starlight touches clear dew, and live on spring water and fragrant herbs. They harm no living thing and keep an ancient etiquette. In a Records of Jueyun folktale a young qilin who meets an herb gatherer by a pool takes human form to make amends. Ganyu is half qilin and keeps the strict vegetarian diet, though she struggles with her love of Liyue food.
### LARGE +
Ganyu's character story says qilin are paragons of benevolence among the adepti, leisurely yet composed. A qilin's horns are sensitive, which is why Ganyu discourages people from touching hers. Cloud Retainer says Ganyu enjoyed having them stroked when she was young (The Illuminated Beast of the Sea of Clouds, 1.2).

## entry: Suanni of Chenyu Vale
@keys: suanni, Lingyuan, Solitary Suanni, Man Chai
@keys2: Chenyu Vale, Gaming, Qiaoying Village
@version: 4.4
### STANDARD
Suanni live mainly around Chenyu Vale, and locals believe they were the land's original rulers who retreated to the mountains once Liyue was at peace. Lingyuan is a blue suanni, the Beastlord of the Sunken Jade Realm. The Solitary Suanni is a mountain guardian fought as a boss at Chizhang Wall, and Man Chai is Gaming's red, flame-bodied companion.
### LARGE +
Lingyuan, a friend of Fujin and the Herblord, kept to the wild and never grew close to people. Her disruption of Chenyu Vale's spirit veins and the Traveler's part in resolving it are told in Chenyu's Blessings of Sunken Jade.

## entry: Half-Blooded Illuminated Beasts
@keys: half-blooded, half-qilin, half-xiezhi
@keys2: Ganyu, Yanfei, illuminated beast
### STANDARD
Ganyu (half qilin) and Yanfei (half xiezhi, inferred) are the two known children of an illuminated beast and a human. Both look like teenagers, and both carry Visions.
### LARGE +
Ganyu, who serves the Qixing, hides her horns and her heritage. Yanfei has little interest in her adeptal heritage and did not sign a contract with Rex Lapis, having been born in an era of peace; she serves Liyue through her work as a legal adviser. How their blood affects ageing is unknown.
