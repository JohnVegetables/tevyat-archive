## summary
### LITE
Ghosts, also called spirits, are spectral figures found in many parts of Teyvat. Per the wiki, they are the souls of the dead that linger rather than being reincarnated, though some are better described as memories projected by the Ley Lines. Most keep a human appearance. They are not a single species: each region has its own sort, from Wuwang Hill in Liyue to Enkanomiya, Tsurumi Island, Natlan and Nod-Krai.
### STANDARD +
Ghost NPCs stand in graveyards, ruins and places marked by disaster. Most are tied to a place. Flins says they are hard to reason with, and the Final Night Cemetery's ghosts react strongly to the lighthouse being relit.
### LARGE +
Because the causes differ by region, the rules of one place should not be applied to another: Natlan's stranded souls, Enkanomiya's Sinshades and Nod-Krai's lingering Lightkeepers are explained separately below.

## nature
### LITE
The wiki profile calls ghosts souls that remain in the world and do not get reincarnated for unknown reasons. The ghosts of Wuwang Hill and Tsurumi Island appear instead to be memory recreations made by the Ley Lines.
### STANDARD +
Appearance varies. Ghosts at Wuwang Hill and Mingyun Village keep clear features such as clothing, age and gender. Shades of Tokoyo in Enkanomiya are human-shaped but mostly featureless, resembling dark identical mannequins in robes. The ghosts of Tsurumi Island are blob-like and barely human; whether that is the Ley Line Disorder's effect or a local belief is not stated. Hanachirusato is an outlier: a physical, non-spectral recreation of Kitsune Saiguu made of filth by the Sacred Sakura.
### LARGE +
In Natlan the dead are meant to go to the Night Kingdom, but the Night Kingdom is weakening, especially in its capacity to accept souls, so many souls fail to return to the Ley Lines and wander as lost souls that fade away (As the Sun Rises and Sets, where Citlali explains this and says the final foe is corroding the Ley Lines from the Night Kingdom). Returning souls usually needs a complex ritual, and her people rely on soul-perceiving vessels such as Ororon. The wiki adds that a Night-Wind shaman can sometimes open the gate for a recently dead soul and that the soul may refuse; that detail was not found in the quest dialogue read and is listed as uncertain.
### ULTRA +
In Nod-Krai, Flins describes ghosts as echoes of living souls that failed to enter the Ley Lines at death and linger on the surface; they are hard to reason with, better given key information that might provoke a reaction, and in time their memories and yearning will fade and return to the Ley Lines (To the Lighthouse).

## places
### STANDARD
Wuwang Hill in Liyue has a permanent eerie fog and blue ghost lights, and is used for tests of courage. Liyue, Inazuma and Mondstadt each have a handful of ghost NPCs; Boreas and the historical Stanley are listed among Mondstadt's. Enkanomiya has the Shades of Tokoyo, seen during Evernight. Nod-Krai has Lightkeepers' ghosts at the Final Night Cemetery and others at the Sanctum of Departed Heroes.
### LARGE +
All the ghosts at the Final Night Cemetery react strongly when the lighthouse is relit, since it is deeply embedded in their memories. The cemetery is also Flins's home. The wiki lists named ghosts there, including Adina, Aimo, Dietrich, Eetu and Jaakob, each in their own entry.

## shades_of_tokoyo
### STANDARD
In Enkanomiya, Shades of Tokoyo (also Sinshades, afterimages, once called Eidolons and Ghostfire Phantasms) are projections of the past. Byakuyakoku, a nether realm, sometimes projects shadows based on remnant thoughts, and strong emotions called Sins of Tokoyo can become a ley line phenomenon of Sinshades. A Sinshade that realises it is one will soon disappear, as 'to know sin is to be free of it'. Aru, a "sinless" Shade created specifically to help with the Watatsumi Goryou Matsuri, is an exception.
### LARGE +
These ideas come from the Enkanomiya quest line and Byakuyakoku Collection book texts and apply to Enkanomiya only.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
This is the project's suggested interpretation. Ghosts are bound to a place, a role or a memory; speak plainly of what they last did and let them be caught up in it. Do not turn a ghost into a free-roaming person, and do not assert why any specific ghost remains unless canon states it.
### LARGE +
Characters with relevant knowledge, such as Citlali in Natlan or Flins in Nod-Krai, understand ghosts as a local phenomenon and can explain it; ordinary people in the same place may simply find the spot eerie, as Wuwang Hill is treated as a test-of-courage site.

The Final Night Cemetery on Paha Isle holds the graves of Lightkeepers, and their ghosts linger there with Flins. Flins describes the ghosts as echoes of living souls that failed to enter the Ley Lines at death; they are hard to reason with, and over time will return to the Ley Lines.

## entry: Natlan's Stranded Souls
@keys: stranded souls, Night Kingdom
@keys2: ghost, ghosts, Citlali
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
Natlan's dead are meant to enter the Night Kingdom, but it is growing weaker, especially at accepting souls, and many fail to reach the Ley Lines. Citlali says such lost souls wander and fade from existence for good, and that returning them usually takes a complex ritual or a soul-perceiving vessel like Ororon. (The wiki's added claim that a shaman can open the gate for a recently dead soul, which may refuse, is unconfirmed here.)
