## summary
### LITE
Oni are horned youkai of Inazuma, known for strength, pride, and explosive tempers. There are two tribes, the crimson oni and the blue oni. Their blood has thinned with every generation since the Cataclysm 500 years ago, so most descendants today look and act close to human. Arataki Itto, head of the Arataki Gang, is the present representative of the crimson oni.
### STANDARD +
Oni clans value their principles and pride very highly. Humans often fear or distrust them; Itto's family was driven from their village on suspicion simply because they were oni. The blue oni were believed extinct, but Itto's story quest reveals they still survive in poor conditions.

## nature
### LITE
Oni have long horns and larger builds than most humans. In ancient times they were wholly blue or wholly crimson with no markings; after generations of mixing with humans their skin became human-like, so oni now paint markings on their skin and horns to show their tribe.
### STANDARD +
The old oni are pictured with crooked faces and fangs, judging by the oni masks and the guard of the Maguu Kenki. They are famously allergic to beans, which can be lethal in large quantity. Most descendants today show only a mild reaction, but Itto's is severe: skin contact makes him itch and struggle to breathe, and he fears fried tofu most of all.
### LARGE +
Oni lack the flashier supernatural gifts of other youkai; Itto's story describes them as remembered chiefly for their horns, tempers and talent for trouble, though their raw strength is great. In-game item text says an oni mask contains immense power. Because of the thinning blood, such traits are weaker in modern oni.
### ULTRA +
Real-world note from the wiki: the allergy likely echoes Setsubun, a Japanese holiday where beans are thrown at someone dressed as an oni. Inside Inazuma, the cause is stated as a genuine allergy, not a curse.

## abilities
### LITE
Great physical strength and endurance. Itto's style is brawling and ritual combat with a heavy weapon, backed by sheer stamina.
### STANDARD +
Oni power is largely physical strength. The old oni masks are said to hold immense power, but no canon scene explains how they work.
### LARGE +
Oni can be skilled swordspeople as well as brawlers: Mikoshi Chiyo, an oni of the Mikoshi Clan, was a warrior and close ally of Ei, and her son Michihiro founded a sword school. The wiki lists a legendary martial artist, Arataki of the Front Gate, among the oni of unknown period.

## society
### LITE
Oni historically lived secluded because humans feared them. Today oni are rare and live alongside humans, often outsiders to Inazuman society.
### STANDARD +
Itto is a member of the crimson oni who lives in Hanamizaka and leads the Arataki Gang. Granny Oni, who is human, raised him after his parents died; the nickname comes from taking in an oni child when people shunned oni.
### LARGE +
The blue oni stayed secluded and were thought to have died out. In Rise Up, Golden Soul (2.3), the Traveler learns that they survive in hardship; Takuya, a blue oni, joined a ronin group for supplies, on condition that no innocents were killed. Itto resolved to help the blue oni, and Granny Oni began preparing aid. In the limited-time Enchanted Tales of the Mikawa Festival event (5.4), Takuya says a kitsune lady (Yae Miko) told the police station he might make amends for his crime, but that everything was already settled by the time he arrived.
### ULTRA +
Most oni descend only partly from oni. Itto's parents had oni blood and were suspected of crimes because of it. The wiki mentions Four Oni of Ogata, who trespassed on Chinju Forest and were deceived by Ioroi, and the Twin Oni of Tokaku, two bandit sisters beaten by Itaru the Black Fox and later recruited by her, both from stories in the books.

## relations
### LITE
Oni and humans have often distrusted each other, and oni remain wary outsiders in Inazuman society.
### STANDARD +
The oni wanted the Raiden Shogun's protection, which required good relations with humans. Oni allied with Ei in the old days, notably Mikoshi Chiyo, who fought the dark forces beside her. In the present Itto's brawling keeps him on the Tenryou Commission's radar, and he lost his Vision to Kujou Sara, a tengu, whose refusal to grant a rematch frustrates him.
### LARGE +
The tengu are the oni's counterparts. Itto once lost a sumo contest to a young tengu on Byakko Plain; the tengu carried him home with a sprained ankle. Michihiro, Chiyo's son, was given the name "Iwakura" by the tengu Teruyo to escape the oni curse and later became the patriarch of the Iwakura Clan.
### ULTRA +
Kitsune and oni intermingle in tales. The Kitsune Saiguu (in the Hakushin Ring's text) recalls Chiyo as an oni maiden lovely as the moon, and the kitsune Itaru recruited the Twin Oni of Tokaku. In modern Inazuma, Yae Miko and Itto once held a kitsune ramen eating contest despite the fried tofu.

## history
### STANDARD
The oni are some of Inazuma's oldest inhabitants. Humans did not tell oni tribes apart, and the blue oni's frequent clashes with humans soured everyone's view of them. To win the Shogun's protection the tribes agreed to a pact: the blue oni would help the crimson oni integrate, while the crimson oni would treat humans respectfully, never use their strength for evil, and earn their trust.
### LARGE +
In the Cataclysm, 500 years ago, Mikoshi Chiyo was swallowed by a beast of sin; though she slew it she was left corrupted, saw friends as enemies, and turned on the Raiden Shogun on Mt. Yougou. Ei cut off her sword arm and one horn, and she fled into the forest. The horn, buried, was later found by bake-danuki and spread nightmares. Her family's name was stained, and her biological son Michihiro lived in self-exile near Konda Village.
### ULTRA +
Michihiro met the Yougou tengu Teruyo, who renamed him Iwakura. After thirteen years of yearly duels he finally touched her (slicing off a black feather as his blade broke) and earned the title "Tengu Victor", then named his style the Tengu Sweeper and founded the Iwakura Art; the Maguu Kenki was built on it and modelled after him (details in the Maguu Kenki entry). Chiyo's adoptive son Mikoshi Nagamasa joined the Shogunate to clear the Mikoshi name. The wiki also lists Ako Domeki, a "deathless oni of Seirai", among the oni of that era.

## status
### STANDARD
As of 7.1, oni survive in Inazuma: Arataki Itto and the blue oni Takuya are named descendants, and Granny Oni is working to help the blue tribe. The wiki's Oni page lists no oni newer than Itto and Takuya, and no 6.x-7.1 oni content was found.
### LARGE +
Takuya reappears in the limited-time Enchanted Tales of the Mikawa Festival event (5.4), still serving his sentence but present at the festival; he asks the Traveler to keep the outing secret from Itto and says he would rather decide about joining the Arataki Gang after his sentence ends.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay interpretation (this project's recommendation, not canon): oni are proud and blunt, quick to stand up for their own and easily offended by assumptions. Many present-day oni are half-human in culture; do not make them monsters.
### LARGE +
Keep to the established limits: strength and endurance, a bean allergy, horns. Do not give oni elemental powers or revive Chiyo. Treat the Crimson and Blue Oni as a pact between tribes, not as a simple hero/villain tale.

## entry: Crimson and Blue Oni
@keys: Crimson Oni, Blue Oni, crimson oni, blue oni, oni pact
### STANDARD
The Crimson Oni and the Blue Oni are the two great oni tribes of Inazuma. The children's tale makes the gentle crimson oni the winner who earns human respect, but the truth among oni is a pact: the proud oni wanted the Shogun's protection, humans did not tell tribes apart, and the blue oni's clashes with humans made things worse. So the tribes agreed that the blue oni would help the crimson oni integrate, while the crimson oni would never use their strength for evil and would win human trust. The blue oni stayed apart and were thought extinct, until Itto's story quest revealed they survive.

## entry: Takuya
@keys: Takuya
@version: 2.3
### STANDARD
Takuya is a blue oni with blue horns and markings. Hurt by the oni fairytale and by his tribe's suffering, he abandoned his pride and joined a ronin group led by Nogata for supplies, insisting innocents not be killed and secretly giving captives extra food and water. He believed Itto had broken the old pact. When Nogata tried to blow up the hideout, Takuya turned on him, and Itto saved him. Both were arrested, and Itto promised the blue oni would be looked after. Takuya also appears in the limited-time Enchanted Tales of the Mikawa Festival event (5.4).

## entry: Mikoshi Chiyo
@keys: Mikoshi Chiyo, Chiyo, Torachiyo
### LARGE
Mikoshi Chiyo, also called Torachiyo, was an oni of the Mikoshi Clan and a close ally of Ei about 500 years ago. After being swallowed by a beast of sin she was corrupted and attacked the Shogun; Ei's strike cost her the sword arm and a horn, and she fled, her end unrecorded. The Mask of the Kijin text names her an oni, the Hakushin Ring calls her an oni maiden, and the wiki reads the later tale of a male rebel oni named Torachiyo as her story distorted over time. She was the biological mother of Iwakura Michihiro and adoptive mother of Mikoshi Nagamasa. The Hakushin Ring's text, in the Kitsune Saiguu's voice, recalls an oni maiden with a face as lovely as the moon; the wiki reads this as Chiyo.

## entry: Granny Oni
@keys: Granny Oni
@version: 2.3
### STANDARD
Granny Oni is not an oni; the nickname came from her taking in Arataki Itto as a child when people disparaged oni. She fed him porridge when he collapsed after a fight and has treated him as her own son since. During Itto's story quest she cared for the boy Daisuke after ronin kidnapped his parents, and began preparing help for the blue oni after the Traveler brought news.
