## summary
### LITE
Jack Frosts are one of the six common kinds of Snowland Fae, native to Snezhnaya. They look like short, squat snowmen with floating mitten-like hands, small orange eyes and blue cheeks, and they say little except "ya-ho". They are playful, mischievous and about as clever as cats. Lumi, the earthen-looking Jack Frost who travels with Linnea, is the one most travellers meet.
### STANDARD
Jack Frosts are among the six common kinds of Snowland Fae, along with Vila, Domovoy, Leshy, Volkodlak and Snegovik. They are small and round, communicate mainly in "ya-ho" calls and gestures, love buttons and pranks, and have no fixed element. Their hidden homeland is Jack Frost Village, north of Fellfrost Peak. Players of the 6.5-7.0 content mostly meet them as puzzle-givers, shopkeepers and the companion of Linnea.

## nature
### STANDARD
Jack Frosts were created by the Hyperboreans with the other Snowland Fae, as part of an effort to make a people that could surpass humans. They may look like snow, but they bear heat with at most some discomfort, and their bodies can be squashed hard without lasting harm.

The kind includes adults and juveniles. Adults have stubby legs and a small round tail and like to wear hats of ice, snow or fabric. Juveniles have no visible legs and sit inside a spherical shell, which closes up when they roll about so that they look like a big snowball. How they are born is not stated.

Most are said to be about as intelligent as cats. They cannot speak or write human languages, and they talk with "ya-ho", gestures and drawn shapes. A few, such as the Jack Frost Village Head, are cleverer and can write simple words.
### LARGE +
They are described as naturally playful and mischievous, enjoying pranks and play with humans. They prize buttons, which they call "bite-ons", because buttons are pretty, wearable and light, unlike heavy metal Mora, and they hand them as gifts to humans they like. In the open world, Jack Frosts run time-trial challenges, shown by the markings on the shovels they carry, and reward Intricately Patterned Buttons that can be traded at the Jack Frosts' Goodies Shop.
### ULTRA +
In the 7.0 quest "Small Jack Frost, Big Problems" the village head communicates with the Traveler in writing while the village is under attack. Lumi's own history shows what exclusion looks like inside the kind: other Jack Frosts bullied her, took her food and chased her from her sleeping spots because she looked different.

## abilities
### STANDARD
Like all Snowland Fae, Jack Frosts use elemental power without a Vision or Moon Wheel. They have no fixed element: the snowman look suggests Cryo, but Lumi shows Geo because, according to Linnea, she has eaten large amounts of ore and earthy things. Lumi can also stretch her body to a huge size, taking on Super Power and Ultimate Power forms, and she wields Geo hammers when she fights beside Linnea.
### LARGE +
In the Spirit Envoy Championship, Lumi grew to gigantic size and nearly crushed Celaeno and Haemus before Linnea stopped her. That accumulation of Geo energy showed Linnea that Lumi's quirks came from her diet. Lumi has also swallowed a rare fossil from the seafloor, which turned out to be a Moon Wheel, and Linnea kept the Moon Wheel as an ornament.

## society
### STANDARD
Jack Frosts live in small communities. Their homeland, Jack Frost Village, is a hidden settlement north of Fellfrost Peak. Volkodlak bandits, having heard of treasure the Jack Frosts kept, raided the village and ransacked it. With the Traveler's and Paimon's help the village repelled them, and the Traveler found that the "treasure" was in a well where the Jack Frosts had thrown it, since they had no interest in it.
### LARGE +
Jack Frost Village is also where Lumi lived before she met Linnea. A disguise she once made out of a cardboard box with eyes cut out and a blue front still lies there in a broken igloo, discovered by the Traveler as the "Strange Jack Frost's Paper Bag", and canon implies at least one Jack Frost was sad when she left.

## relations
### STANDARD
Jack Frosts are linked with human travellers through gifts and games rather than any political role. They are on friendly terms with Linnea and her companions. The Volkodlak raid shows that other fae are sometimes dangerous to them. As Snowland Fae, they share the history of the fae as a whole; see the Snowland Fae entry.

## individuals
### STANDARD
- Lumi: a small Geo Jack Frost with golden eyes and brown cheeks, an orange-yellow star hat and a bowtie on her back. She understands human speech, speaks mostly in "ya-ho", and travels in Linnea's backpack. She joined Linnea voluntarily after Linnea asked, and is a "gluttonous little Jack Frost" in Linnea's character story.
- The Jack Frost Village Head: a more intelligent individual who runs the village and communicates in writing.
- A thieving Jack Frost and a "Golden Jack Frost King" are listed among known Jack Frosts (the second from weapon text); the sources read give little about them.
### LARGE +
Lumi and Linnea wandered together, including to the desert of Sumeru, because Lumi tolerates heat without trouble. After the Spirit Envoy Championship, Linnea decided they should look for another Jack Frost with a similar nature, to give Lumi true company.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
As this project's interpretation: portray ordinary Jack Frosts with gestures, drawings and "ya-ho", never with fluent speech, and with a cat's mix of curiosity and mischief. They have no fixed element, so do not assume Cryo. Treat buttons as gifts and prank props. An exception is individuals like the Village Head or Lumi, who are clearly cleverer.

## entry: Jack Frost Village
@keys: Jack Frost Village, Fae's Hidden Settlement

### STANDARD
A hidden homeland of the Jack Frosts north of Fellfrost Peak, introduced in 7.0. Volkodlak bandits raided it for treasure; the Traveler helped to drive them off, and the treasure turned out to be at the bottom of a well where the Jack Frosts had dropped it for lack of interest. Linnea first met Lumi here.
