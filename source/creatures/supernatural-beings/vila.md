## summary
### LITE
The Vila are one of the six common kinds of Snowland Fae. They are small, pale fae with pointed, green-tipped ears and two pairs of insect-like wings that allow brief flight. They were among the first to follow the Belyi Tsar, supplied most of the Druzhna guard, and the playable Vesna, Commander of the Druzhna, is one.
### STANDARD +
They are traditionally led by a Grand Duke or Duchess of the Vila. Since the Cataclysm some have remained loyal to the Tsaritsa through the Druzhna, while others in the tundra cling to the privileges of the old Tsar's day.

## nature
### LITE
Vila are humanoid and small in stature, with pale white skin, pointed ears tipped in green and some with a green triangle under the eyes. They fly briefly on insect-like wings, which gives them exceptional mobility.
### STANDARD +
Like all fae they were created by Hyperborean artisans as part of a drive to surpass humans, and need no Vision for power. The Winged Soarer Knight Archive says their brief flight lets them scout formations and raid over long distances.
### LARGE +
The wiki's trivia notes that the Vila of Slavic folklore are female nature spirits akin to fairies, known mostly among South Slavs. The wiki's Chinese and Japanese names for the fae both contain the word for wind.

## history
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
After Hyperborea fell and the fae settled in Snezhnaya, the Vila were for an unknown period vassals of the Volkodlak under King Miscislav, paying tithes of grain, gold and slaves. When Monomakh, the later Belyi Tsar, set out to unite the fae, the Vila were among the first to follow. Their first Grand Duchess, Vaselisa Strivozha, was defeated alone by the Tsar. He asked them to stop their tribute, and they denounced Miscislav and submitted to the Pale Star Court willingly.
### LARGE +
The Tsar founded the Druzhna and led it himself as personal guard, and its ranks came largely from the Vila, as they do today. He also granted the Vila amnesty and let them live beside humans, a favour many remain grateful for and credit for their wealth.
### ULTRA +
After the Tsar died in the Cataclysm and the Tsaritsa became Cryo Archon, some Vila rebelled against her policy toward fae nobility. Vila raiders known as the Burner of Cities travelled the frozen plains by rail "like a shadow" (Foundryman's Magnanimity); the rebellions were soon suppressed. Their leaders were descendants of Vila nobility, which split the Vila people. The Beyond the Chrysalis weapon story tells how Gluvasha Strivozha, daughter of Vaselisa, held the Silver Court of Veneter after the death of Grand Duke Photius, and was urged by rebels to join them against the new sovereign. She denounced the Tsaritsa in public and raised a following without sanction, but the rebels were then caught and learned she had sided with the Tsaritsa; she later asked to be stripped of her fief, last name and title, and the Tsaritsa decreed her title and name be forgotten. In Vesna's Character Story 4, Mayor Pulcinella (the Rooster) tells Vesna that Gluvasha poisoned Photius with a mead brewed from the crimson fern and passed the rebels' secrets to the Tsaritsa, and that House Strivozha has never been wholly trusted; this is his account. The Tsaritsa took over the Druzhna as her own guard. Weapon and artifact narratives are literary accounts and may blur detail.

## society
### STANDARD
The Vila were traditionally led by a Grand Duke or Grand Duchess. Names recorded include Vaselisa Strivozha, Photius, Gluvasha, Benigna and Pyotr; Vesna's Character Stories make Pyotr the current Grand Duke and her grandfather, and Benigna her parent. House Strivozha is an ancient Vila bloodline whose name carries weight in Snezhnaya. Vesna's profile says the clan existed long before Snezhnaya's early days.
### LARGE +
The wiki says the Vila of the Druzhna regard Vila still living in the tundra and opposed to the Tsaritsa as traitors and a permanent shame to their people, and lists the Druzhna officer Akvilina among fae who view such opponents with contempt. The Traveler fights such rogue Vila as enemies in Snezhnaya.

## abilities
### STANDARD
Flight on four wings is brief rather than sustained, but it makes Vila fast scouts and raiders. The Druzhna's Winged Soarer Knights, a Vila enemy type, use this in the game's combat.

## relations
### STANDARD
Vila served the Volkodlak, then the Belyi Tsar, and now many serve the Tsaritsa. They interact with the other kinds as fellow fae nobility. In the New Bough story Vaselisa Strivozha is called the saint of the Vila and begs the Tsar to protect the Leshy forest, which shows their ties to other fae.

## individuals
### STANDARD
Known Vila include Vesna (playable, Druzhna Commander), Vaselisa Strivozha (first Grand Duchess), Gluvasha, Photius, Benigna, Pyotr, Akvilina (Druzhna officer), and the Burner of Cities (rebel).

## entry: Druzhna

### STANDARD
The Druzhna is the personal guard that the Belyi Tsar founded and led. Its ranks have always been drawn largely from the Vila. The Tsaritsa took it over as her own guard after the Cataclysm, and Vesna now commands it.

## entry: Burner of Cities
@keys: Burner of Cities
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
A female Vila of the old fae nobility who led a short-lived rebellion against the Tsaritsa, raiding by rail across the plains, and who was killed soon after according to item text. Her scheme to destroy the Kresnik's Torches was foiled by Izmaylov's heroes and the movement was suppressed.
