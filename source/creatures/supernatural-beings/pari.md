## summary
### LITE
The Pari are a race of small, sentient avian beings with flower-like features who live in the Vourukasha Oasis in the Girdle of the Sands, Sumeru. They descend from the divine bird Simurgh, whose Khvarena became countless motes when mixed with the Amrita, and the sentient ones are the Pari. Each is said to have a fated responsibility, and the Traveler meets them in the 3.6 quest Khvarena of Good and Evil.
### STANDARD +
Pari bond with humans whom they call Yasnapati. In Zurvan's words they exist to avert the catastrophe and cleanse the defilement. Their eldest member, Zurvan, was born in the Cataclysm; Sorush is the Bloomguard of the Pari.

## nature
### LITE
Pari look like birds with disproportionately large heads, tiny bodies and long wing-like arms, with flower-like details. They are the only form of Khvarena that is sentient. Each Pari lives to carry out one fated duty, which it must take on alone.
### STANDARD +
Sorush, a typical Pari, has a pale red body with mauve arm tips, pale orange eyes and a red cape with a heart-shaped emblem; the eldest, Zurvan, is pale purple, has closed, wrinkled eyes and wears a flower-like crown. At the end of their life cycle Pari turn into Plumes of Purifying Light that are returned to the Amrita Pool. Pari tainted by Abyssal corruption are rejected by the Oasis and transform into Fravashi Trees; the humans they have bonded with are responsible for bringing these lost Pari home.
### LARGE +
The Pari sometimes use flower-themed endearments, such as "good petal" in Zurvan's speech. Their power comes from the primordial Amrita, and Zurvan says that one day it will flow back to its source. They believe in Egeria, the former Hydro Archon, as their god.

## origin
@version: 3.6
### STANDARD
Simurgh began as a shard of Khvarena formed from Nabu Malikata, who gave it to Greater Lord Rukkhadevata to protect. After Nabu Malikata and King Deshret died, Rukkhadevata turned the Khvarena into a divine bird charged with guarding the border between new birth and death. In the Cataclysm the Amrita, made of the remains of Egeria, began to be corrupted by the Abyss. Simurgh flew into the pool and shattered her form into countless motes of Khvarena; the sentient motes became the Pari.
### LARGE +
Nabu Malikata is said to have foreseen both the Cataclysm and Simurgh's sacrifice. Zurvan was the very first Pari, born from the Amrita Pool; when she woke, all of Sumeru except the Oasis was ravaged by the Abyss and the sky was marked with holes like the Sign of Apaosha, and she followed Simurgh's last wish by gathering Khvarena and driving off the Abyssal demons.

## society
### STANDARD
The Pari live in the Vourukasha Oasis, with Zurvan as the elder: she refused Sorush's request to become Bloomguard until Sorush had proved herself, then allowed her to be judged by their god, and later set her trials. The Bloomguard is the Pari recognised by their god as the one allowed to touch the five Great Songs of Khvarena. Pari take human partners, Yasnapati, meaning bonded comrades in arms, and Sorush gives the Traveler and Paimon that title.
### LARGE +
Zurvan disapproved of Sorush fraternising with humans, regarding her as immature at first; Sorush's later trials tested her skill, her knowledge of Pari history and her reflection on her own rashness. After the Sabzeruz Festival, the Lesser Lord Kusanali offered the Pari a place in Sumeru City, which Sorush declined.

## history
@version: 3.6
### STANDARD
In the Cataclysm the Pari fought alongside Khaenri'ahn warriors, a masked sword-wielder and a one-armed sage, and a group of Akademiya scholars who later became the Order of Skeptics. Zurvan found Dainsleif after Khaenri'ah's destruction and brought him to the Oasis; together with Nagarjuna they found the Harvisptokhm and performed the first Rite of Ab-Zohr, which purified the land and created the other Pari. These Pari bonded with the Skeptics and helped destroy the monsters.
### LARGE +
Centuries later the Sign of Apaosha appeared above the Harvisptokhm. In Khvarena of Good and Evil, Sorush, believing the Skeptic Nasejuna, collected the five Great Songs of Khvarena, only to learn that he wanted them to break the seal on the Abyss. The Traveler defeated him and his collaborator Klingsor. With her Yasnapati's help Sorush then performed the Rite of Ab-Zohr again and the Rite of Chinvat, which dispelled the Sign.
### ULTRA +
The Sign of Apaosha is a result of constant defilement building up; Zurvan warns that if the filth erodes the Harvisptokhm's structure the rift of Tunigi Hollow could reopen. The defilement can never be fully destroyed, only delayed. The Pari took the chance to rest afterwards. Zurvan later set Sorush a series of trials in The Recollector's Path, and Sorush performed the Dance of Sabzeruz at the festival for Kusanali after her release.

## abilities
### STANDARD
Pari wield Khvarena, the power of their descent from Simurgh. They can gather it to fight the Abyss and to power the rites of Ab-Zohr and Chinvat. Sorush, as Bloomguard, can touch the Great Songs of Khvarena, which others cannot. She can also drop bombs from above, as she did to scare off bandits.
### LARGE +
Nasejuna told Sorush that the Rite of Chinvat required her to sacrifice herself; the wiki account records that she performed the rite after learning his true motive and the Sign was dispelled, without stating that she gave her life. How exactly Khvarena works beyond these uses is not explained.

## relations
### STANDARD
The Pari revere Egeria and are tied to the Harvisptokhm. They allied with the Order of Skeptics, Khaenri'ahn survivors such as Dainsleif and Nagarjuna, and later the Traveler. Nasejuna, a Skeptic, used Sorush's trust to pursue his own plan. The Lesser Lord Kusanali, the Dendro Archon, is honoured at the Sabzeruz Festival.

## individuals
### STANDARD
Known Pari: Zurvan, the First Pari; Sorush, Bloomguard of the Pari; Mihir; Rashnu, called Familiar of the Barsom; Fedhri; Sefana; Jarjar; and Residual Pari of the Forest. Canon gives profile detail only for Zurvan and Sorush.

## entry: Zurvan
@keys: Zurvan, First Pari
@version: 3.6
### STANDARD
Zurvan is the First Pari, born in the Cataclysm when Simurgh became Khvarena. She protected the Oasis with Dainsleif and helped enact the first Rite of Ab-Zohr. She is the eldest Pari, wary of outsiders at first ("This isn't a place anyone can just wander into, trespasser") yet fond of Sorush ("a good petal at heart"), and, per the wiki (which flags this as unverified), she appears in the open world only after Sorush is obtained in The Splendorous Sky That Day. Her own entry covers her in full.

## entry: Sorush
@keys: Sorush, Bloomguard
@version: 3.6
### STANDARD
Sorush is the Pari Bloomguard, born after the Cataclysm. She was initially impulsive, trusted Nasejuna, and later grew from her mistakes. The Traveler and Paimon are her Yasnapati. Her own entry covers her in full.

## entry: Sign of Apaosha
@keys: Sign of Apaosha
@keys2: Pari, Harvisptokhm
@version: 3.6
### STANDARD
The Sign of Apaosha is a defilement-driven mark in the sky above the Harvisptokhm. It appeared in the Cataclysm and again in the present day; the Pari remove it through the rites of Ab-Zohr and Chinvat. If defilement erodes the Harvisptokhm, Tunigi Hollow's rift might reopen.

## entry: Plume of Purifying Light
@keys: Plume of Purifying Light, Amrita Pool
@keys2: Pari
### STANDARD
When a Pari's life cycle ends it becomes a Plume of Purifying Light that must be returned to the Amrita Pool in Vourukasha Oasis. If a Pari has been tainted, the Oasis rejects it and it becomes a Fravashi Tree. Its human Yasnapati is then responsible for bringing it home.
