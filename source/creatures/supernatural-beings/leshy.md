## summary
### LITE
The Leshy are one of the six common kinds of Snowland Fae, tree-like forest spirits of Snezhnaya. They are reclusive, rarely come near towns or high roads, and prefer the wilderness and mountain forests. The wild ones, the Wildwood Leshies, fight as elite enemies, rooting themselves to heal and strengthen.
### STANDARD +
They may be the longest-lived of all fae. The Adventurer Handbook suspects the oldest are as tall as ancient, sky-piercing trees.

## nature
### LITE
The Adventurer Handbook calls them a "reclusive breed" of Snezhnaya's fae that favours deep wilderness over towns. They grow in forests and wilds and are typically seen as stands of living wood with a visible heart.
### STANDARD +
The Archive says wild Leshies have only fragmentary sentience and cannot speak. They might look like Hyperborea's early experiments, but according to records they were made later, after the artisans had almost fully mastered authority over life. It adds that perhaps there is a purpose in this that neither fae nor humans of the present fully understand. The Archive also notes that most people would struggle to tell one Leshy from another.
### LARGE +
Two stages appear as enemies. The Juvenile Tree Segment is a small Leshy, and the Rugged Elder Leshy is the large, old form. The Archive calls the kind possibly the longest-lived among all fae, and says few other long-lived peoples have ever watched a Leshy's life from beginning to end.

## abilities
### STANDARD
In battle a Leshy roots itself to heal and strengthen. The exposed heart is its weak point. Breaking the rooting disrupts the effect, and a Stellar Glimmer reaction while it is rooted exposes the heart further. After the rooting is broken, Pyro hurts it a little extra. They deal Geo and physical damage in game; this is a gameplay detail rather than a lore statement.

## history
@version: 7.1
### STANDARD
The New Bough weapon story tells of an age of peace in the ancient forest, broken when Miscislav, the "Blood-Devourer", pressed every nation to bow or perish. The "treeborn" refused to take sides and refused the Volkodlak's summons, and the holy forest was burned. By the time the Belyi Tsar arrived only a tenth of them survived. Vaselisa of the Water Imps mourned in song, and the Vila saint Strivozha begged him to protect the forest. The Tsar then addressed the venerable King Lesnik, grandsire of the wilderness, asking only for healing herbs and leaves to tend the earth's wounds.
### LARGE +
The story is told by a bird and is a folk-style account, so it should be read as in-world legend.
### ULTRA +
The Tsar introduced himself to Lesnik as Monomakh, "heir to the Pale Star and bearer of the Pale Crown", who wished to found a realm where no living creature would be harmed. The canopy murmured and sighed, and a venerable bough broke from the tree and was laid before him by the bird. The unattributed words that follow, evidently Lesnik's, give the young king the bough's life, bid him not to mourn the dead, and promise that the forest will remember. The story adds that even the tyrannical Knyazhna of Blood had never tried to wipe out the treeborn, and that they trusted the Volkodlak who bore the name of wisdom would not either. The forest was burned all the same, for declining the summons. It also says the treeborn remembered "gracious lords of old" in the ordering of their leaves, a nod to Hyperborea.

## relations
### STANDARD
Leshy are fae but live apart from the Pale Star Court's cities. Their link to the Belyi Tsar is the story above. Wild Leshies are a threat to travellers in Snezhnaya rather than part of its society.

## individuals
### STANDARD
King Lesnik is named as a Leshy ruler only in the New Bough story. No Leshy is a speaking character in the open world.

## entry: King Lesnik
@keys: Lesnik, King Lesnik
@version: 7.1
### STANDARD
Lesnik is called the venerable grandsire of the wilderness and the myriad forests in the New Bough weapon story, where the Belyi Tsar addresses him after the holy forest was burned. He answers by giving the Tsar a bough. Nothing else of him is known.
