## summary
### LITE
The Volkodlak are one of the six common kinds of Snowland Fae, the non-human spirits of Snezhnaya. They are beast-like but humanoid, strong and keen-sensed, and the Adventure Handbook calls them the most populous fae race in the nation. About 3,000 years ago their kings led the Miscislavli Alliance, which the first Cryo Archon, the Belyi Tsar, broke in war. Today they appear as a nation's worth of clans, a few mild or loyal, and as the Volkodlak Brutes that attack travellers in the Snezhnayan wilds.
### STANDARD +
They vary a great deal in shape: wolfish, bovine, canine, long-eared, elephant or mammoth-like. The wiki shows the race has no single look, and the history book notes subcategories, so treat "Volkodlak" as a family of clans rather than one animal.

## nature
### LITE
Like other fae, Volkodlak were made by the artisans of Hyperborea, the "Golden City", as part of an effort to build a race that could surpass humans. They stand and work on two legs with beast features and the strength of animals. They are long-lived, though lifespans differ across fae kinds.
### STANDARD +
A noble's notes found at the Pale Crown Palace give the general origin of all fae: the Evolved Children of Humanity, made with forbidden methods. The Adventure Handbook adds a guess about the Volkodlak specifically: that they may have been made to find out what makes a human human. The Long-Eared Striker's Archive entry gives a separate account for one lineage, saying it was made to test the limits of reproductive capability, and that its great numbers let it survive the fall of the golden realm.
The Handbook also says the crystalline growths on fighting fae come from the surge of energy released as they were made; see the Fae of Crystalline Flesh.
### LARGE +
Their senses are sharper than a human's: Archive texts credit Sharpshooters with judging wind speed and seeing well in poor conditions, and Raiders with instinctive evasion. Archive texts say Volkodlak do not generally excel at shaping Prime Ice or wielding elemental power, but few fae lineages match their physical strength. Large clans are numerous, while the most powerful bloodlines, like the elephantine one, now find it hard to form clans at all and are rarer.

## society
### LITE
Historically the Volkodlak were ruled by a king. Their great confederation was the Miscislavli Alliance. Since the Belyi Tsar absorbed the fae kingdoms into the Pale Star Court, they have no king of their own, and clans have different loyalties and roles.
### STANDARD +
Volkodlak clans are described by animal aspect: the Nyholmgarthir were bovine, the Cirnareka wolf-like (a Cirnareka name is rendered "Black Hand"), and the Archive speaks of canine clans with a reputation for loyalty and mildness. Some are go-betweens: Channel Contact types are entrusted with carrying goods and messages.
### LARGE +
The Miscislavli confederation was loose, drawing in Volkodlak clans, the water imp Dalegrad, and the Nyholmgarthir and Cirnareka. The Vila, who paid tribute of grain, gold and slaves, were its vassals. The Water Imps under their Grand Duke Vodyanoy also fought for it. The Tundra of Ancient Beasts is said to have been the site of the alliance's capital.
### ULTRA +
Miscislav's line survived the war, and the open-world NPC Bogatov is described as one of his descendants (the wiki cites this through the Miscislav page; Bogatov himself has no profile text). Miscislav's family once held some power under the Belyi Tsar.

## history
### LITE
About 3,000 years ago King Miscislav, the "Blood-Devourer", ruled the Miscislavli Alliance as a rival of the first Emperor, Monomakh Snezhnyi. After a short war the Emperor killed Miscislav in battle and became the Belyi Tsar.
### STANDARD +
In the 428th Cycle, the 76th year after Monomakh founded Kitezh, the Emperor's envoys told the Vila to stop paying tribute to Miscislav because it was "unlovely". The Vila did so. Three years later Miscislav accused the Pale Star Court of breaking the peace and demanded ten years of grain, gold and slaves, thirty times what the Vila had paid, which the Emperor rejected. The book's footnotes say this was likely a provocation: Miscislav feared the Pale Star Court's rise. A year after that he marched on Kitezh. Three times the armies met (the foothills, the frozen lake, the plains), and in the last the Emperor slew him with a spear through the skull. Grand Dukes Skoldyryt and Vodyanoy were also slain, and many princes who followed him.
### LARGE +
Footnote accounts add details: the Grand Duke of the Nyholmgarthir, Skoldyryt (the "Deepwood Bull"), had his flanking plan stolen by the fae spy Filipov, was himself slain and was later returned to his clan for burial. The Cirnareka Grand Duke Seriviiki (the "Gray Wolf") escaped and was the only fae grand duke to join the Pale Star's ministers afterwards; some scholars have suspected his Schpekin Legion's failure to cut off the imperial armies was no accident, though the book says the evidence is insufficient and attributes the failure to a mysterious icy fog. After the war the remaining clans came to the Emperor, and he named himself the Belyi Tsar. Miscislav's bear-shaped skull, as large as a chariot, is held in the Zapolyarny Palace Treasury and shows the spear's mark.
### ULTRA +
The Archive says the canine clans fought the Tsar to the last in defence of their king and earned his respect for it, and that the elephantine clan once produced war leaders who served Miscislav as close retainers. A legend says Miscislav tried to call on the Shadow Realm in the final battle. This failed but changed the Emperor's later choices. The Leshy weapon story "New Bough" separately recalls Miscislav pressing every nation to bow, and the burning of the holy forest when the Leshy refused; see the Leshy entry. After unification, humanity was pardoned and the capital moved from Kitezhgrad to Snezhnograd about five centuries ago, and the Tsaritsa was crowned around the time of the Cataclysm.

## relations
### LARGE
Vila: once vassals paying tribute, they changed sides willingly after their first Grand Duchess was defeated by the Tsar. Belyi Tsar: conqueror of the Volkodlak, remembered as merciful toward his enemies. Other fae: the Domovoy under Alvis helped defeat Miscislav's allies, per the Domovoy entry. Humans: the Archive treats a Volkodlak Basher as dangerous to an untrained human, and other Archive entries treat them as threats for travellers crossing the snow.
### ULTRA +
Under the Tsaritsa the fae lost their monopoly on rank, and Volkodlak are one of the common fae kinds living in the Snezhnayan lands; no source read gives a general Volkodlak stance toward her rule.

## abilities
### STANDARD
Gameplay, labelled as such: the Volkodlak Brute enemies use weapons and can imbue themselves with elemental energy, then attack in combos. Low-tier brutes (Basher, Lobber, Raider, Sharpshooter) mostly deal Physical damage with a special elemental attack; high-tier ones (Channel Contact, Fanged Zealot, Leomastiff Guard, Long-Eared Striker, Voywolf Hunter) lean on elemental power. Every brute has a special attack that a Stellar Glimmer reaction can interrupt or backfire. In-world: great physical strength, keen senses, agility on snow, and skill with bows, crossbows, cannons and thrown objects (Archive texts).
### LARGE +
Shown types: Bashers are large and powerful (Cryo, Geo or Pyro, shown by brown, teal or orange markings); Lobbers have strong upper bodies for throwing; Raiders are small and agile (Dendro, Electro or Pyro); Sharpshooters fire from long range with great accuracy; Channel Contact types hide a hand cannon as a tree trunk and may fire signal rounds disguised as fireworks; Fanged Zealots resemble elephants or mammoths and hold some of the noblest bloodlines; Leomastiff Guards belong to the canine clans; Long-Eared Strikers hide in cloaks and move in secrecy; Voywolf Hunters are Cryo bow users (the Local Legend Furiosa is one). The brute enemy drops are called Volkodlak Brute Materials (Accreted Fragment, Mass and Growth).

## individuals
### STANDARD
Miscislav (dead; see his own entry). Seriviiki, Skoldyryt: grand dukes recorded in the Pale Starborn book. Bogatov: open-world NPC in Snezhnaya (exact location not recorded), a descendant of Miscislav per the wiki. Furiosa: a Volkodlak archer, a quest NPC and a Local Legend enemy (Voywolf Hunter), described in her own entry.
### LARGE +
Furiosa nearly died of an arm wound; the scientist Koshchei, under the name "Doctor Moreau" in Okurov, altered her with bioalchemy so that her strength passed the limits of living beings and she gained some authority over trees. She was meant to cast off her body and join the "Perfect Tree" collective Koshchei planned for Okurov's villagers, but refused at the last moment and was left in a state like being parasitized by trees. She appears in the quest "Forbidding Doors of Melancholy" (7.0 content), where she tries to stop the Traveler and Nastya from entering the Hall of Prayers and then warns Nastya she may regret it. She wears the look of a brown-furred Voywolf Hunter with red roots in her hair and a branch growing from her horn.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 the Volkodlak are an ordinary part of Snezhnayan life and wildlife-like threats, with no ruler. Their Tundra of Ancient Beasts is a region of the Snezhnayan map.
### STANDARD +
The Volkodlak Tundra, opened in 7.0, is said to be where the Miscislavli capital stood; after Miscislav's defeat the site was demolished and rebuilt, with the Tsar leaving no trace of the old city. A boss, the Chimeric Winged Lion, is fought there (see Aberrant Chimeric Monster). Nothing about the Volkodlak was found in 7.1 sources.

## roleplay
### LARGE
Roleplay use (this project's suggestion, not canon): treat Volkodlak as people with clan culture, not mindless beasts. A Volkodlak NPC could be gruff or friendly according to clan, proud of an ancestor who fought the Tsar, and wary of the Pale Star Court's legacy. Do not give them romances, catchphrases or private stories that no source states.
### ULTRA +
Keep canon limits in view: do not claim a single Volkodlak homeland beyond the tundra tradition, do not state one purpose for their creation, and keep the Brute enemies' gameplay elements separate from narrative claims about the race.

## entry: Miscislavli Alliance
@keys: Miscislavli, Miscislavli Alliance, Blood-Devourer, Gorynishche
@keys2: Volkodlak, Miscislav, Belyi Tsar, Kitezh
@version: 6.3
### STANDARD
The Miscislavli Alliance was the Volkodlak-led confederation of fae clans under King Miscislav, the Blood-Devourer, whose vassals included the Vila and in whose host marched the Nyholmgarthir, Cirnareka, and Dalegrad water imps. Its war with Monomakh Snezhnyi's Pale Star Court ended with Miscislav's death at Kitezh and the Emperor's rise as the Belyi Tsar. See Miscislav's own entry for the man.
### LARGE +
Accounts differ on blame: the book calls Miscislav's accusations preposterous but reasoned, since he feared Pale Star growth; Pale Star footnotes also record clandestine dealings of Seriviiki with the court's finance minister. The Tundra's description says the alliance's capital was demolished after defeat.

## entry: Volkodlak Brutes
@keys: Volkodlak Brute, Volkodlak Brutes, Volkodlak Basher, Volkodlak Lobber, Volkodlak Raider, Volkodlak Sharpshooter, Channel Contact, Fanged Zealot, Leomastiff Guard, Long-Eared Striker, Voywolf Hunter
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
The Volkodlak Brutes are the enemy group of hostile Volkodlak in Snezhnaya, part of the Fae of Crystalline Flesh. Four low-tier types (Basher, Lobber, Raider, Sharpshooter) and five high-tier types (Channel Contact, Fanged Zealot, Leomastiff Guard, Long-Eared Striker, Voywolf Hunter) are known. Their special attacks can be disrupted by Stellar Glimmer reactions. Archive entries describe them as beast-strong and keen-sensed, dangerous to anyone untrained.

## entry: Volkodlak Tundra
@keys: Volkodlak Tundra, Tundra of Ancient Beasts
@keys2: Miscislavli, Miscislav, Snezhnaya
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
The Volkodlak Tundra, "Tundra of Ancient Beasts", is a Snezhnayan region said to have held the capital of the Miscislavli Alliance. After the Tsar's spear pierced Miscislav's head and his iron hooves scattered the confederation, the city was razed and rebuilt, and the usually merciful ruler seemed afraid something might rise from the place, leaving no trace of the old city (Expedition description). The Chimeric Winged Lion is a boss there.

## entry: Furiosa
@keys: Furiosa
@keys2: Volkodlak, Koshchei, Okurov, Nastya
@version: 7.0
@spoiler: minor
### LARGE
Furiosa is a Volkodlak archer who was altered by Koshchei in Okurov and refused, at the last moment, to join his collective "Perfect Tree". She told Nastya about the wider world of Snezhnaya while hoping she would never learn what became of the villagers. Her Local Legend version is the Voywolf Hunter "Furiosa, Guardian of What Is No More" near the Scars of Cursed Obsession domain in the Everfrozen Earth. She has her own entry; this one only places her among the Volkodlak.

## entry: Volkodlak Origins and the Golden City
@keys: Filii Adamici Evoluti, Evolved Children of Humanity, Golden City
@keys2: Volkodlak, fae, Hyperborea
@spoiler: minor
### LARGE
The fae, originally called Filii Adamici Evoluti, were made by Hyperborean artisans using forbidden methods taught by Koitar and built from Frost Moon light upon the bones of giant beasts, according to item and notes texts cited by the Snowland Fae page. The Celestial Nail's fall turned many to silvery mist, leaving the survivors to dominate the frozen land. The Volkodlak are among the six common kinds that survived. Which Hyperborean purpose produced them is not settled.
