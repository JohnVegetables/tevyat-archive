## summary
### LITE
Yumekui-baku, or baku, are lesser youkai of Inazuma who enter, shape and eat dreams. In their original form they are small, round and squishy with curly tails. Only some can take human form. Yumemizuki Mizuki is the best-known baku and works as a clinical psychologist at Aisa Bathhouse.
### STANDARD +
Baku are less famous than kitsune, oni or tengu and are traditionally seen as benevolent, carefree helpers. Today they are gradually joining human society.

## nature
### LITE
Small floating dream-eaters, gentle and mostly unable to fight.
### STANDARD +
Their bodies are soft and dough-like with colourful skin, small eyes, short limbs, drooping noses and small ears, which some compare to tapirs or boars; they usually float. Those in human form tend to have large pointed ears. Prune calls them cute with dough-like bodies. The wiki notes a tapir-like belly stripe on all of them.
### LARGE +
Digesting dreams takes time and energy, and a baku must be in its own dream to do it. Continuous eating brings fullness; nightmares can cause indigestion. An eaten dream is forgotten by its owner.

## abilities
### LITE
Enter and influence the dreams of humans and other youkai, eat them, and rarely fight.
### STANDARD +
Positive dreams taste warm and mildly sweet; nightmares taste bitter, acidic or metallic. A particularly strong nightmare acts like a toxin, causing nausea, fatigue, sharp pain, coma and possibly death. Baku can also create dreams, implant thoughts, and even enter the subconscious of a waking host whose mind is dissociated.
### LARGE +
Mizuki, who studied the human mind for years, feels every negative emotion she consumes, while most baku barely notice them. An unwritten rule forbids eating a good dream without its owner's permission, because it can destabilise them, so most baku do not know what good dreams taste like.
### ULTRA +
Mizuki says baku cannot reach dreams in Sumeru, where the Dendro Archon holds authority over dreams within the nation's borders.

## society
### LITE
Quiet, helpful folk with no known central leadership; rarely seen in groups.
### STANDARD +
In the youkai heyday they lived modestly, farming and weaving while offering dream-eating to those in need. At the Hyakumonogatari Taikai they served drinks or listened attentively. In the present several baku work with Mizuki at the bathhouse.

## relations
### LITE
Gentle toward humans and other youkai, who rely on them for relief from nightmares.
### STANDARD +
Mizuki is a major shareholder of Aisa Bathhouse, where baku and their kin staff psychological care. Little Baku accompanies her as a companion.

## individuals
### STANDARD
- Yumemizuki Mizuki: playable baku, clinical psychologist at Aisa Bathhouse.
- Little Baku: a baku companion of Mizuki.

## history
### STANDARD
When the Cataclysm struck, the baku hid because they were ill-suited for battle, and some still feel guilty about it. Afterwards they emerged to help with recovery, extracting the nightmares of those who had fought the Abyss and sometimes injuring themselves by overwork.

## status
### STANDARD
As of 7.1 baku appear in Inazuma content from 5.4 onward, centred on Mizuki and Aisa Bathhouse; no Nod-Krai (6.x) or Snezhnaya (7.0-7.1) content adds baku lore.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay interpretation (this project's recommendation, not canon): portray baku as soft-spoken and modest, taking little for themselves. Never have a baku eat a good dream without consent.
### LARGE +
Keep them non-combatants; do not invent abilities such as mind control or telepathy beyond dream entry and shaping.

## entry: Dream eating
@keys: dream eating, nightmare eating, dream-eating, dream eater
### STANDARD
Baku consume dreams for sustenance. Eaten dreams are forgotten by the dreamer. Mild nightmares are acid and bitter; strong ones are toxic. The unwritten baku rule forbids eating good dreams without permission.

## entry: Little Baku
@keys: Little Baku
### STANDARD
Little Baku is a small baku companion of Mizuki, shown in its original dough-bodied form.
