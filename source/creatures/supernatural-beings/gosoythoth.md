## summary
### LITE
Gosoythoth, titled the Reaper of the Abyss, was a powerful sapient entity that Natlan's defenders called the heart of the Abyss. When the Abyss put out the Sacred Flame, it manifested over the Stadium of the Sacred Flame as a black rift and led an all-out invasion of Natlan. It was driven back by Mavuika's Night War, then hunted into the Night Kingdom and killed there by Mavuika and the Traveler in the Version 5.3 finale of the Natlan Archon Quest. It is dead as of 7.1.
### STANDARD +
It was a quest-exclusive character, first seen in The Surging Darkness (5.1) and killed in Where All Hopes Lie (5.3); the Night War was declared in No One Fights Alone (5.1). Its battle form is the weekly boss Lord of Eroded Primal Fire.

## nature
### LITE
Gosoythoth had no fixed body. It was an entity of ineffable form that took shape from the Abyss's power once the Sacred Flame was gone. Unlike its monsters it could think: it worked out Mavuika's plan to awaken a last hero and moved to crush Natlan before that could happen.
### STANDARD +
It referred to itself as an individual and showed confidence, arrogance and, in the end, fear. Xilonen sums up the danger in Despair Engulfs the Heavens: the heart of the Abyss had joined the invasion, which was a power the defenders could not match.
### LARGE +
Its presence alone turned the sky black. It rained down Abyss Tumors that grew Abyssal Pylons, and the pylons kept summoning Abyssal monsters to corrode the land. It could also copy a foe in the way Tenebrous Mimiflora and Tenebrous Papilla do, adopting an alternate form that imitates an enemy.

## appearance
@version: 5.1
### STANDARD
Over Natlan it looked like a huge dark rift in the sky: a spiral of dark purple cubes, a green-black eclipse-like light at the centre that resembles an eye, and a galaxy-like aura. It could briefly shrink to a dark purple orb with the same star and sphere patterns. In the Night Kingdom it took the shape of a giant Abyss-corroded dragon (see the Xiuhcoatl form).
### LARGE +
That dragon form has a reptilian or serpent-like body, large wings, jagged feather-like growths and a pair of arms wielding huge swords made of Pyro and Abyssal energy.

## history
@version: 5.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
After the Abyss snuffed out the Sacred Flame, Gosoythoth manifested its heart above the Stadium of the Sacred Flame. Mavuika used the Gnosis to relight the Sacred Flame and briefly held the incursion back. Gosoythoth retreated into the sky and hung over Natlan, staining land and sky black.
### LARGE +
Once the six heroes had gathered (Version 5.1), Mavuika awakened the power of the Divine Throne and declared the Night War, in which Natlan's warriors would keep resurrecting and wear down the Abyss's forces. She then poured her strength into a blast of flame that pierced Gosoythoth and the False Sky, ending the invasion and sending it back into the Abyss.

In the Version 5.3 finale, she and the Traveler followed it into the Night Kingdom and split up. Mavuika destroyed a core like those of the tumors while the Traveler entered Gosoythoth's lair, where Gosoythoth was already fighting Mavuika. Gosoythoth tried to stop the Traveler from joining and failed. It then drew the memories of the fallen Pyro Dragon Xiuhcoatl out of the ley lines and took his form, becoming the Lord of Eroded Primal Fire, and it spoke in distorted voices of earlier Pyro Archons to sow despair.
### ULTRA +
The Traveler withstood the attacks, and Mavuika pointed out that Gosoythoth was frightened because victory was no longer certain. Together they killed it and purged the Night Kingdom of Abyssal influence for the time being. In Gurabad the Abyss subsided afterwards (A Divergence Leading to Oneself), though canon does not explain the link.

## abilities
### STANDARD
Rained Abyss Tumors that spawned Abyssal Pylons and monsters; mimicry of enemies; and in the Xiuhcoatl form, Pyro and Abyssal flame attacks, which the weekly boss version also uses to corrode the ground.
### LARGE +
Its battle form also appears in the Stone Stele Records as the Lord of Eroded Primal Fire, whose attacks include Abyssal flame and Void Wards. These are gameplay details and are not described in quest text.

## status
### LITE
Dead. It was killed in the Night Kingdom by Mavuika and the Traveler (Where All Hopes Lie, 5.3).
### STANDARD +
Canon says only that the Night Kingdom was purged "for the time being", so a return is not ruled out but is not stated either.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay guidance (project recommendation): Gosoythoth is a villain of one arc, not a recurring character. Show it as sapient, proud and cruel, and as afraid of the Traveler. Do not give it a backstory, name for its origin, or ties to other Abyss figures that canon does not state. It should not be alive in any scene set after Where All Hopes Lie.

### STANDARD
In the Night Kingdom, Gosoythoth took the shape of the dead Pyro Dragon Sovereign Xiuhcoatl by pulling his memories out of the ley lines. The result is the Lord of Eroded Primal Fire. It is Gosoythoth imitating a dead dragon, not Xiuhcoatl returned.

## entry: Night War
@keys: Night War
@keys2: Gosoythoth, Abyss, Mavuika
@version: 5.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Mavuika declared the Night War after awakening the Divine Throne's power. For its duration the warriors of Natlan kept resurrecting, which destroyed the Abyssal army. It ended Gosoythoth's invasion of the surface and pushed it back into the Abyss.
