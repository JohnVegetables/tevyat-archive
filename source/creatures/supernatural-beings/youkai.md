## summary
### LITE
Youkai are Inazuma's supernatural beings. They outlive humans, have their own natures, and once powerful enough can take human form. Kinds include kitsune, tengu, oni, bake-danuki (tanuki), nekomata, yumekui-baku, kappa and tsukumogami. Many died fighting the Abyss in the Cataclysm 500 years ago; today they are few and their bloodlines thin. Known present-day youkai include Yae Miko (kitsune), Yumemizuki Mizuki (baku), Kirara (nekomata), Kujou Sara (tengu) and Arataki Itto (part oni).
### STANDARD +
The most powerful are called Great Youkai; the only ones known to be alive are Yae Miko, Mizuki and the petrified bake-danuki Ioroi.

## nature
### LITE
Many youkai have a true form and a human one; tails, horns or wings often show even in human shape. Inazumans believe everything has a spirit and honour local spirits at small shrines.
### STANDARD +
Not all are born youkai: Kirara was an ordinary kitten who became a two-tailed nekomata, and tsukumogami are objects that gained awareness from long exposure to strong youkai power.
### LARGE +
A tsukumogami draws power from its original object, weakens the farther it strays from it, and dies if the object is destroyed. The witch Barbeloth told Yae Miko (Witch's Revelation, 6.7) that a youkai's birth is bound up with desire: a longing, unfinished dream or strong emotion can take root at the slightest prompt and grow in unexpected directions, and a Great Youkai likely carries a larger desire than most. This is a fortune-teller's reading, not a full explanation.
### ULTRA +
Youkai leave traces after death. In Yae Miko's story quest (2.5), memories of departed youkai leaked from damaged Ley Lines; Yae explained that youkai memories outlast human ones and can still choose whether to be seen. The kitsune Urakusai, once a Daiyouko, could even possess a living human for a while, which ordinary youkai memories cannot.

## history
### STANDARD
Before Inazuma's founding, youkai roamed the islands freely (the Youkai Era). In the Archon War, Ei subdued many youkai races; those under her banner helped humans shelter in the mountains and build a city by the sea. Later the Kitsune Saiguu hosted nights of drink and stories that became the Hyakkiyakou, and the Mikawa Flower Festival also dates from this era. In the Cataclysm humans and youkai fought the Abyss together; the Kitsune Saiguu and many others died or vanished.
### LARGE +
- Yae Miko's character story pictures that age: tengu ruling the skies, oni on the battlefield, tanuki on the back roads, kitsune walking among humans, with the Hakushin line of kitsune the most revered. The Hyakkiyakou got its name when drunken youkai competed to cover the moonlit sky with their powers.
- Oni tribes: humans did not tell them apart. Because the blue oni clashed with humans while the crimson oni sought peace, the tribes made a pact: the blue oni would help the crimson oni be accepted, and the crimson oni would never use their strength for evil. Itto honours it.
- Tanuki legend (Toki Alley Tales) says Inazuma belonged to the tanuki until the kitsune arrived by sea; a war of 800 and then another 800 years ended in peace talks, with the tanuki, never admitting defeat, ceding the great Sacred Sakura. The same book claims humans began as youkai who shape-shifted so often they forgot what they were; it is a collection of boastful tales, so treat this as folklore.
### ULTRA +
After the islands' unification, Toki Alley Tales says, youkai either lived in seclusion or faced destruction and suppression. After the Kitsune Saiguu's death, the bake-danuki Ioroi, believing she was only hiding from him, made trouble on Narukami Island until the onmyouji Kamuna Harunosuke sealed him and many bake-danuki in stone statues in Chinju Forest.

## society
### STANDARD
There is no single youkai society. Some live in mountains and forests; others live openly among humans: Yae Miko is Guuji of the Grand Narukami Shrine, Kirara delivers for Komaniya Express, and Mizuki is a clinical psychologist and major shareholder of the Aisa Bathhouse.
### LARGE +
The old community had great and lesser youkai; by Mizuki's account the baku were bit-part players at gatherings where the powerful held forth. Living among humans takes effort: Kirara learned rules such as not climbing roofs or eating ornamental koi, and learned "how to be a good youkai in human society" from Yae Miko. Kujou Sara, a young tengu, was adopted into the Kujou Clan after the Shogun saved her life in battle, and serves the Shogun with unbreakable devotion.
### ULTRA +
Toki Alley Tales describes youkai neighbours, unwritten youkai etiquette, and Yae Miko giving a house to the ameonna "Granny Ame", though as fiction its details are unreliable. Itto's parents were driven out though they had done nothing wrong, while Kirara's honesty and good work won over customers who first remarked on her youkai nature.

## abilities
### LITE
Shape-shifting, long life, and powers by kind: kitsune and tanuki trickery, tengu flight and martial skill, oni strength, baku eating nightmares.
### LARGE +
- Yumekui-baku slip into dreams; Mizuki combines this with hot springs and fragrance in treatment.
- Bake-danuki change shape freely; Ioroi hid as an Onikabuto beetle.
- Powers fade with bloodlines. Itto, only part oni, has horns and a severe bean allergy, while most oni descendants now react only mildly. A kitsune furnishing description cited by the wiki says ordinary kitsune lost their mystical abilities as their numbers fell and can no longer speak.

## relations
### STANDARD
Relations with humans range from friendship to wariness. The gap in lifespans is a recurring sorrow; Yae Miko holds that friendships with short-lived humans are not broken but carried on in a new form.
### LARGE +
In the Akitsu Kimodameshi event (3.3), the tsukumogami Hanyuuda Chizuru recalled how the youkai Kamai Kenji befriended the samurai Yanagibashi Takuto at a Mikawa Flower Festival and grieved as his friend aged; Yae's answer above comes from that quest. The Raiden Shogun still commands deep youkai loyalty, notably Kujou Sara's. Onmyouji such as Kamuna Harunosuke, one of the Yougou Three, could seal troublesome youkai.

## individuals
### STANDARD
Besides the playable youkai: the late Kitsune Saiguu and Urakusai (kitsune), Ioroi (bake-danuki). Sayu is called a mujina but says she is fully human, and Gorou was identified as human by a bake-danuki; neither is a youkai.
### LARGE +
Great Youkai named in canon include Hakushin, the Kitsune Saiguu, Urakusai, Sasayuri and Reizenbou, most long dead. In the limited-time Akitsu Kimodameshi event (3.3), the young kitsune Youko, a kappa and a hitotsume kozou tried to revive the Mikawa Flower Festival with Itto's help.

## kinds
### LARGE
Kinds named in canon (mostly from Japanese folklore): kitsune (also yakan), tengu (including karasu tengu), oni (and jikininki), bake-danuki, bake-neko and nekomata, yumekui-baku, kappa, hitotsume kozou, kawauso, mujina, nue, ameonna, gashadokuro, inugami, kasha, kurodama ("black jade"), minobi ("raging flame"), tofu boys, shinkirou, umibouzu and tsukumogami. Kitsune, tengu, oni, bake-danuki, yumekui-baku and umibouzu have their own entries.

## status
@version: 6.7
### STANDARD
As of 7.1, youkai survive mainly in Inazuma, few and with thinning bloodlines. Ioroi remains a stone statue in Chinju Forest: during the limited-time Enchanted Tales of the Mikawa Festival event (5.4) he told Yae Miko he preferred staying petrified and that the seal's power was nearly gone anyway.
### LARGE +
In 6.7, Yae Miko's Witch's Revelation quest took her to Fontaine, where Barbeloth read her fortune as reversed Justice and spoke of the desire behind every youkai.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay interpretation (this project's recommendation, not canon): youkai feel old, playful and out of step with human time; many enjoy festivals, food and novels. Most present-day youkai are known to their neighbours, not hidden monsters.
### LARGE +
Keep powers proportionate: a lesser youkai can shape-shift or play tricks, not move mountains. An unexplained prank may be blamed on a tanuki or kitsune. Do not invent new Great Youkai, revive dead ones, or place youkai in Nod-Krai or Snezhnaya events canon does not show.

## entry: Great Youkai
@keys: Great Youkai, Daiyouko
### STANDARD
"Great Youkai" is a title for exceptionally powerful youkai. Holders named in canon include the kitsune Hakushin, the Kitsune Saiguu and Urakusai (who called himself a Daiyouko), the tengu Sasayuri and Reizenbou, and the umibouzu Daikengyou. Most are dead. As of 7.1 the living Great Youkai are Yae Miko, Yumemizuki Mizuki and Ioroi. Kirara could reportedly become one if she returned to the wild, but says she never wants to.

## entry: Youkai Era
@keys: Youkai Era
### LARGE
The Youkai Era is the time before Inazuma's founding, when youkai roamed the archipelago's mountains and forests. Tanuki legend in Toki Alley Tales claims the islands belonged to the tanuki until the kitsune arrived and a 1,600-year war ended with the tanuki ceding the great Sacred Sakura. The era ended when youkai submitted to, or allied with, the Raiden Shogun during the Archon War.

## entry: Tsukumogami
@keys: Tsukumogami, tsukumogami, Hagoita Spirit
@version: 3.3
@availability: limited
### LARGE
Tsukumogami are objects that slowly gain awareness after long exposure to strong youkai power and become youkai. In the Akitsu Kimodameshi event (3.3), Shikanoin Heizou identified Hanyuuda Chizuru as one, born from a pair of hagoita paddles from Kamai Kenji's duels at the Mikawa Flower Festival. Such a spirit can use youkai power near its original object, weakens away from it, and perishes if the object is destroyed. The weapon Tamayuratei no Ohanashi is also listed by the wiki as a tsukumogami.

## entry: Oni pact
@keys: oni pact, blue oni, crimson oni, Blue Oni, Crimson Oni
### LARGE
In the past humans did not distinguish between oni tribes. The blue oni often clashed with humans while the crimson oni wanted peace, so the tribes made a pact: the blue oni would help the crimson oni integrate, and the crimson oni would respect humans, never use their strength for evil and earn human trust. Arataki Itto, descended from the crimson oni, keeps this pact and is grateful for the blue oni's sacrifice.
