## summary
### LITE
Umibouzu are huge, intelligent sea creatures known in Inazuma, extinct in most waters. Sailors regard them as foes and many were hunted; the people of Watatsumi Island instead treat each as a lonely individual. They appear only through the world quest Solitary Sea-Beast (2.1).
### STANDARD +
"Umibouzu" is the name used on Narukami Island. Canon has never shown a living one, and its remarks suggest they resemble whales.

## nature
### LITE
Huge, intelligent, docile and solitary sea animals.
### STANDARD +
A partial skeleton on Watatsumi Island shows their scale; Kumi describes them as lonely and indifferent to curiosity or hostility from outside their own world. Their biology is otherwise unspecified.
### LARGE +
Anisa, a researcher, reads Kuntira's deformed pelvic bones as evidence she bore many young, and her spine as showing she died at sea before washing ashore.

## society
### LITE
Mostly solitary. Watatsumi's people give each creature its own name rather than a species name.
### STANDARD +
Narukami Island folk say they attack cargo ships on long voyages and steal fishermen's catch, so they are hunted. Near Watatsumi Island they have grown close to humans. Traces may also exist on Yashiori Island, per an in-world note.
### ULTRA +
The two attitudes meet in Kumi, whose father came from Narukami Island and whose mother came from Watatsumi. She and Kuntira bonded over shared loneliness rather than trying to cure it. The wiki speculates that the name Kuntira may echo an old Japanese word for whale; this is the wiki's guess, not canon.

## individuals
### STANDARD
- Kuntira: a female umibouzu befriended in childhood by Kumi, who later found her remains on a beach of Watatsumi Island.
### LARGE +
- Kuntira's children: Anisa learns they exist and resolves to befriend them while staying on Watatsumi Island.

## history
### STANDARD
Umibouzu once ranged more widely but are now gone from most waters. Kumi believed Kuntira the last of her kind; the quest overturns this.

## status
### STANDARD
As of 7.1 only Kuntira's offspring are implied to remain; nothing in Nod-Krai (6.x) or Snezhnaya (7.0-7.1) content adds to this.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay interpretation (this project's recommendation, not canon): treat them as rarely seen and mourned by those who knew one. Do not invent sightings or powers.

## entry: Kuntira
@keys: Kuntira
