## summary
### LITE
Tengu are youkai of Inazuma with black wings and crow-faced masks. They are swift, skilled with the bow and sword, and strong beyond their slim frames. Most lived in seclusion; the elite Yougou Tengu guarded Mt. Yougou and the Grand Narukami Shrine. The tengu most people meet today is Kujou Sara, a general of the Tenryou Commission who was adopted into the Kujou Clan and serves the Raiden Shogun with unbroken devotion.
### STANDARD +
The chieftain of the Yougou Tengu bore the title Great Tengu. As of 7.1 the only tengu known from the present day are Sara and an unnamed tengu who once wrestled Arataki Itto.

## nature
### LITE
Black wings are a common trait (Teruyo, Sara), and every known tengu wears a crow mask. Sara has two pairs of raven-black wings and a red crow mask at the side of her head.
### STANDARD +
Tengu are slimmer than oni but much stronger than they look. The wiki observes that Akuou kept the mask of Sasayuri as a trophy, suggesting masks carry honour or importance; canon does not confirm what they mean.
### LARGE +
Tengu speed is said to be nearly unrivalled: the Kitsune Saiguu "technically" won a race against the Yougou Tengu chieftain but believed the tengu let her win, and Iwakura Michihiro earned the title Tengu Victor merely by grazing Teruyo's wing. Tengu warriors carry feather fans, items that produce various effects in tengu hands; in human hands, the Red Feather Fan only makes the body lighter in the air (it speeds gliding).
### ULTRA +
Sara still shares tengu habits: she often goes to the mountains, knows them like the back of her hand, and has seen strange creatures rarely told of in human tales. Her story also notes the loneliness of being a non-human adopted child among humans.

## abilities
### LITE
Flight, archery, swordsmanship and speed. Tengu arts are taught beyond their own clans.
### STANDARD +
The Great Tengu taught the human Takamine the Mistsplitter her archery, which Takamine later taught the shrine maiden Asase Hibiki. Another of the Yougou Three, the onmyouji Kamuna Harunosuke, learned the tengu art of transcension from the Yougou Tengu and combined it with Liyue adepti arts to create onmyoudou.
### LARGE +
According to Teruyo, a name given by the Yougou Tengu holds divine power; she renamed Michihiro Iwakura to loosen the hold of his oni bloodline. Whether other tengu can do this is unknown. Sara believes the Raiden Shogun saved her life by granting her an Electro Vision as she fell from a cliff.
### ULTRA +
Red Feather Fan text says the first successor of the Iwakura Art had an annual duel with a Yougou tengu warrior, but his blade could not touch a tengu who could fly freely, so the duels were closer to pranking sessions. Michihiro's own style was later named the Tengu Sweeper.

## society
### LITE
Tengu live mostly in seclusion in forests and mountains. The Yougou Tengu are an elite group who protected Mt. Yougou, and so the Grand Narukami Shrine on it.
### STANDARD +
The chieftain of the Yougou Tengu bears the title of Great Tengu. Sasayuri and Reizenbou are the two known holders. The Yougou Tengu also created the Empty Boat of a Thousand Gates at the end of the Araumi labyrinth, where the Yougou Three trained under them.
### LARGE +
Sasayuri, a cherished general of the Raiden Shogun, was killed in the war between the Shogun and Orobashi's people on Watatsumi Island; Akuou fought Sasayuri on Yashiori Island and Sasayuri's mask shattered. Reizenbou later led the Yougou Tengu.
### ULTRA +
Reizenbou was rambunctious, impatient and fond of fighting; she became the Kitsune Saiguu's bodyguard after losing a race to her, and mentored the Yougou Three. Her bow Thundering Pulse was a gift from the Shogun; a bet with Takamine the Mistsplitter ended in a draw, and he became her servant in exchange for the bow.

## relations
### LITE
Tengu have served or allied with the Raiden Shogun and the Narukami kitsune. They appear alongside the oni in Inazuman tales and duels.
### STANDARD +
The Great Tengu Reizenbou guarded the Kitsune Saiguu, and Sasayuri served the Shogun as a general. In the present Sara serves the Shogun as a general of the Tenryou Commission. Itto once had an unsettled sumo match with a young tengu on Byakko Plain years ago, which ended with both of them rolling down a mountain and the tengu carrying the ankle-sprained Itto home; he also lost his Vision to Sara in a duel.
### LARGE +
Teruyo's bond with the oni Michihiro is the best-documented tengu and oni friendship: she renamed him, duelled him for thirteen years, and parted forever after he grazed her wing. In the Raiden Shogun's "Good Evening" voice-over the Traveler says they used to be a shooting star, and Ei asks whether they might be a tengu warrior.
### ULTRA +
Sara sometimes brings melons and fruit to the bake-danuki on the mountain after catching them thieving, as a sort of consolation, never framing it as a gift.

## individuals
### STANDARD
- Kujou Sara (playable): Tenryou Commission general who enforces the Vision Hunt Decree; adopted by Kujou Takayuki after a young tengu was found with a Vision.
- Sasayuri (killed in the Orobashi war): Great Tengu of Yougou, a general of the Shogun.
- Reizenbou: Great Tengu 500 years ago, Saiguu's protector and mentor of the Yougou Three; went into exile after the Cataclysm.
### LARGE +
- Teruyo: Yougou Tengu, Reizenbou's child; disappeared after parting from Michihiro.
- Yoichi: a name the wiki lists as a tengu of unknown period.
- The unnamed young tengu who wrestled Itto on Byakko Plain.

## history
### STANDARD
Tengu were among Inazuma's oldest inhabitants and lived in seclusion. Sasayuri served Ei and died fighting Orobashi's people. Reizenbou later led the Yougou Tengu and was given the bow Thundering Pulse by the Shogun.
### LARGE +
In the Cataclysm Reizenbou fought the Abyssal monsters; after the Kitsune Saiguu vanished, she went into self-exile in shame and rage, her final abode being the Empty Boat of a Thousand Gates. Teruyo remained on Narukami Island and met Michihiro, who was living in self-exile near Konda Village. After thirteen years of duelling he grazed her wing; she granted him the title Tengu Victor and left, asking him to forget her.
### ULTRA +
Sara's own history: living in a mountain forest plagued by great evils, a young, nameless tengu with damaged wings was hurled off a cliff by monsters; as she fell, a Vision appeared in her hand. She was found unconscious and unharmed the next morning, and Kujou Takayuki, noticing the Vision, took her in and named her Sara. Her story quest and Vision Hunt Decree role are in her own entry.

## status
### STANDARD
As of 7.1, tengu remain few and secluded. Kujou Sara is the only known tengu in human society; Reizenbou and Teruyo disappeared 500 years ago. No tengu were found in the sources checked for Nod-Krai (6.x) or Snezhnaya (7.0-7.1).

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay interpretation (this project's recommendation, not canon): tengu are disciplined, direct and fast, with a duelist's sense of honour. They are rare and reserved with humans.
### LARGE +
Do not invent tengu clans beyond the Yougou Tengu, or give them new elemental powers. Treat the "Great Tengu" as a title, not a species rank.

## entry: Yougou Tengu
@keys: Yougou Tengu, Great Tengu, tengu of Yougou
### STANDARD
The Yougou Tengu are an elite group of tengu tied to Mt. Yougou, responsible for protecting the mountain and the Grand Narukami Shrine on it. Their chieftain holds the title Great Tengu, held by Sasayuri and later Reizenbou. They created the Empty Boat of a Thousand Gates in the Araumi labyrinth, where the Yougou Three trained under them. Their names carry divine power, according to Teruyo.

## entry: Sasayuri
@keys: Sasayuri
### STANDARD
Sasayuri, the Great Tengu of Yougou, was a general of the Raiden Shogun who died in the war with Orobashi's people. The duel Akuou planned over Tenshukaku never happened; they fought on Yashiori Island, and Sasayuri's mask broke. The name is Japanese for the bamboo lily.

## entry: Reizenbou
@keys: Reizenbou
### STANDARD
Reizenbou was the Great Tengu of the Yougou Tengu five hundred years ago and bodyguard to the Kitsune Saiguu, whom she had let win a race. She trained the Yougou Three, taught Takamine archery, and gave him the bow Thundering Pulse after a bet that ended in a draw. The wiki treats her as Teruyo's mother. After the Saiguu was lost in the Cataclysm she went into self-imposed exile; the Empty Boat of a Thousand Gates was her last home.

## entry: Teruyo
@keys: Teruyo
### STANDARD
Teruyo was a Yougou Tengu who gave Mikoshi Michihiro the name Iwakura, hoping to free him from the curse of oni blood, and challenged him to a duel that lasted thirteen years. After he grazed her wing she named him Tengu Victor and left forever. Her fate is unknown.

## entry: Feather fans
@keys: feather fan, feather fans, hauchiwa, Red Feather Fan
### STANDARD
Tengu warriors carry magical feather fans (hauchiwa) with various effects. For ordinary people, the Red Feather Fan merely makes the body lighter in the air, speeding gliding.
