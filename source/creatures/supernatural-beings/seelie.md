## summary
### LITE
Seelie are small, softly glowing, wisp-like creatures that drift through Teyvat's wilds, each with a pointed lower body, horn-like head parts, a flowing scarf-like trail and two trailing whiskers. A Seelie leads anyone who follows it back to its Seelie Court, a small statue, where it settles and a treasure appears. Canon explains Seelie as the devolved form of Angels, envoys of Celestia who were cursed to lose their bodies and minds. They are wordless and harmless.
### STANDARD +
Seelie appear in nearly every region, and the variants fit their climates: red Warming Seelie in Dragonspine, fast Electro Seelie in Inazuma, Luminous Seelie in the Chasm's mines, snowflake-marked Seelie in Snezhnaya. Most of the Courts they return to are broken or ruined.
### LARGE +
Loading-screen text describes them as once a mighty race that lived to guide mankind, who now offer only a little treasure to willing followers. The curse behind that fall is set out in the nature section.

## nature
### LITE
Angels were envoys of Celestia, created by the Heavenly Principles and commanded to love all creatures equally. After the war of funerary flame they were cursed: any Angel who loved one individual above all others would lose body and mind. Many of the cursed now exist only as Seelie. In the Chapter V Archon Quest Beneath the Secret Source, Yohualtecuhtin, Lord of the Night, tells the Traveler that Seelie are the devolved form of Angels.
### STANDARD +
A Seelie does not speak and does not fight. It waits, then heads for its own Court when a traveller approaches, and if not led home within some time it drifts back to where it started. Settled in, it may repair the damaged Court, and a chest appears. Elemental Sight shows a trail from a Seelie to its Court and from a Court to its Seelie. Electro Seelie differ by creating their own Court on arrival.
### LARGE +
Angel lore splits the cursed in two: Angels who forsook wisdom for power were stripped of wisdom and became beasts and dragons without distinct elemental traits, while those who abandoned power for wisdom were stripped of form, soul and memory and degraded into Seelie. The horn-like head parts of a Seelie match the horns of the Angel Nabu Malikata, and a text of the ancient song of Seelie speaks of exiles singing their last tune in the halls of angels.
### ULTRA +
Angels are described as immortal and long-lived even after losing their powers, but no text gives a Seelie a lifespan, and none shows one dying. Do not give Seelie speech, names or plans of their own; the narrative reading is a living echo of something lost, which the Traveler simply treats kindly.

## types
### STANDARD
Mysterious Seelie are the ordinary kind, in the open or hidden behind breakable rocks. Warming Seelie of Dragonspine are red and flame-like and give off enough heat to keep nearby travellers warm against Sheer Cold, and they keep radiating once settled. Electro Seelie in Inazuma dash away at speed and release a harmless Electro shockwave when approached; some serve as puzzle guides, tracing a path and vanishing. Luminous Seelie in the Chasm's Underground Mines recharge the Lumenstone Adjuvant and wake Lumentorches. Seelie in Snezhnaya carry snowflake patterns instead of swirls.
### LARGE +
Mini Seelie are infant-like forms with only the head part, none of the tail, scarf or whiskers, and none of the chiming sound; they squeak when excited. They are cosmetic gadgets from the Lost Riches event (limited-time event), where a Treasure-Seeking Seelie hunts coins instead of returning to a Court. Monetoo of Natlan work like Seelie in play but are not Seelie in lore.

## places
### STANDARD
Seelie and Courts are found in Mondstadt, Liyue, Inazuma, Enkanomiya, the Chasm, Sumeru, Fontaine, Chenyu Vale, the Sea of Bygone Eras and Snezhnaya. Courts in Enkanomiya, the Chasm and its Underground Mines, the Great Red Sand, the Desert of Hadramaveth and the Girdle of the Sands are still intact, while most Courts elsewhere are broken.
### LARGE +
Natlan is a special case. Its Angels and Seelie were nearly all killed by dragons in the great war of vengeance, leaving only one final envoy, implied to be Yohualtecuhtin, Lord of the Night, who built the Night Kingdom. Natlan's Monetoo fill the gameplay role of Seelie but are not described as Angels.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
This is the project's suggested interpretation. A Seelie in a scene is quiet and gentle: it drifts, glows, chimes, and never talks. Followed, it goes to its Court and a small gift appears.
### LARGE +
Canon does not show ordinary residents knowing that Seelie were Angels; the link is stated in Archon Quest and item text (in the Chapter V quest Beneath the Secret Source, Yohualtecuhtin says the Angels' devolved form is the Seelie), so treat it as specialist knowledge. Let a Seelie stay gentle and mysterious rather than a talking pet.

### STANDARD
A Seelie Court is a small stone statue, usually damaged or moss-covered, where a Seelie rests when led home. Elemental Sight shows the trail between Court and Seelie. When a Seelie settles in, a chest appears and the Court may be repaired. Electro Seelie create their own Court on arrival.

## entry: Seelie and the Angels
@keys: Seelie, Seelies, cursed Angels
@keys2: Angel, Angels, curse, Celestia
@version: 5.1
@availability: permanent
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Angels were Celestia's envoys, bound to love all equally. After the war of funerary flame they were collectively cursed: loving one above the rest stripped body and mind. Those who gave up power for wisdom lost form, soul and memory and became Seelie; those who clung to power became beasts and dragons. A few, such as Nicole, kept their form at a price; their own entries carry the detail.

## entry: Warming Seelie
@keys: Warming Seelie, Warming Bottle

### STANDARD
Warming Seelie live in Dragonspine, are red and flame-like, and give off heat that cancels Sheer Cold. The Warming Bottle gadget was crafted by those struggling in the snowy mountains, in imitation of them.

## entry: Electro Seelie
@keys: Electro Seelie
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
Electro Seelie are the Inazuma variant. They move fast when approached, release a harmless Electro shockwave, and generate their own Court on arrival. Some act as puzzle guides that trace a path and vanish.

## entry: Luminous Seelie

### STANDARD
Luminous Seelie in the Chasm's Underground Mines recharge the Lumenstone Adjuvant and activate Lumentorches nearby. Loading-screen text says the Chasm's unique climate mutated them.
