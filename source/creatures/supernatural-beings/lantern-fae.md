## summary
### LITE
Lantern Fae are one of the rarer kinds of Snowland Fae. Their true form is a floating blue flame, which they can hide behind a human-looking shape, and they live for an indefinite time without visibly ageing. In canon the only one is Flins, who serves as a Ratnik of the Lightkeepers and keeps a lonely lighthouse in Nod-Krai.
### STANDARD
Lantern Fae are listed among the less common Snowland Fae, with the Augury Birds and the Water Imps, and Celaeno calls them "legendary". They normally look like a floating blue flame and can take a convincing human shape. Their lifespans have no visible limit: Flins is six or seven hundred years old by his own reckoning. Snezhnayan legend links them with blue flames that guide lost souls over the ice, and Flins, the only one shown, wears the story like a family crest.

## nature
### STANDARD
As Snowland Fae, Lantern Fae descend from the creation of the fae in Hyperborea and belong to Snezhnaya. Unlike Augury Birds, they do not seem to age. Flins says that he is forced to "show his age" and that he must be six or seven hundred years old.

The true form is a ball or figure of blue flame. In Nod-Krai, Flins keeps a human appearance, with a lantern at his belt that burns with a cold blue flame. He also shows no visible breath in Dragonspine's Sheer Cold, which the wiki attributes to his fae nature. Most of the fae shown have humanlike forms, but a few, such as Lantern Fae, rely on shapeshifting to fit into human society.
### LARGE +
Flins does not eat human food in any ordinary sense. His profile describes him as not fully integrated into human customs, so he feeds the food he is given to the flame in his lantern. The same text says he is curious about humans and joined the Lightkeepers out of fascination with them. Fae of every kind can use elemental energy without a Vision or Moon Wheel; Flins wears a Moon Wheel only as a cover story.
### ULTRA +
Legend in Flins's profile says his namesake, Kyryll the Azure Flame, was originally a blue flame guiding fallen souls across the icy plains, and Snezhnayan rumours say wandering souls are carried to the far shore by blue ghost-fires. The same profile says the old blue flames were gradually replaced by red ones as power changed hands. Whether these are statements about Lantern Fae as a kind or only about Flins is not settled.

## abilities
### STANDARD
Lantern Fae have the shapeshifting common to the fae, taking a human shape or their flame form, and the free use of elemental energy. Flins channels Electro and calls lightning with a polearm. His profile also credits him with ancient fae rites: he can seal himself away with them, and fae script written in blood on a stone altar was enough to wake him from centuries of sleep.
### LARGE +
Flins's character file credits him with a manifest-flame form and with the ability to understand and seal the Wild Hunt. These are personal abilities of one individual, not established traits of the whole kind.

## society
### STANDARD
Nothing in canon describes a Lantern Fae society. Flins was a fae noble at the court of the Belyi Tsar, which the Snowland Fae overview says the fae filled with aristocrats living in luxury and with some posts reserved for them. He had no followers of his own. After the Tsar's fall and the Tsaritsa's rise he withdrew into self-imposed exile and came south.
### LARGE +
Flins was present for the handover of power from the Belyi Tsar to the Tsaritsa. He once gave away a gem, the "Tear of the North", by deliberately losing a bet at the Deepwinter Cocktail Ball, then vanished. His family crest and surname survive in the register of Snezhnayan nobility. Many fae went into exile or lost status when the Tsaritsa purged dissenting fae princes.
### ULTRA +
He sealed himself under a stone altar on a small island in Nod-Krai, intending to sleep forever, although he says he did not mean it as a suicide. His sleep was broken when Lightkeepers fought the Abyssal Wild Hunt above him and a dying warrior traced fae script in blood on the altar. He later joined the Lightkeepers under the name Flins, which is plausible as an ordinary surname.

## relations
### STANDARD
Lantern Fae are in the same wider fae family as the Augury Birds, Jack Frosts and Water Imps, and Flins has been among the few fae in Nod-Krai alongside Linnea. His closest ties in the sources read are with his fellow Lightkeepers, such as Illuga, and with the dead of the island he tends.
### LARGE +
Few of the people around Flins know what he is. He told the Traveler during the Song of the Welkin Moon story, and he says he wants to see how long he can keep up the pretence of being human.

## individuals
### STANDARD
- Flins (Kyryll Chudomirovich Flins): the only Lantern Fae shown in canon. He is a Ratnik of the Lightkeepers and the lone keeper of the lighthouse and graveyard on the Final Night Cemetery isle. See his character file for personality, voice and events.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
As this project's interpretation: portray a Lantern Fae as old, courteous and slow to reveal itself. Its secret is the flame behind the human shape, so avoid making every Lantern Fae a ghost-guide or an Electro user; Flins is the only example. Blue flame, lantern and graveyard imagery suit the kind, but keep claims about the kind's nature to what Flins's text supports.

### LARGE
Kyryll Chudomirovich Flins, "Kyryll the Azure Flame", is a fae noble of the Belyi Tsar's court. Legend says he began as a blue flame that guided fallen souls across the ice. He gave away the gem "Tear of the North" by deliberately losing a bet at the Deepwinter Cocktail Ball, and then vanished into self-imposed exile. He later became the Lightkeeper Flins in Nod-Krai.

### ULTRA
Flins's profile says the blue flames that once lit Snezhnaya were replaced by red ones, and that the regions which enshrined blue flames fell one by one until people accepted red flames as they accepted the new regime. Read it as the text's own metaphor for the change of rulers; it does not claim a literal replacement of fires.
