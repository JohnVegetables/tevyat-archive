## summary
### LITE
The Domovoy, "household fae", are one of the six common kinds of Snowland Fae. They are short, very long-lived people native to Snezhnaya. They were traditionally ruled by a Grand Duke of the Domovoy. The Fifth Harbinger Pulcinella (Mirko Radovidovich Veleshek) is a Domovoy, as were the late Grand Duke Alvis and Izmaylov, the president of the Royal Energy Commission.
### STANDARD +
Like all fae they were made by Hyperborean artisans and later joined the Belyi Tsar's realm; under the Tsaritsa many entered the human world as officials, craftsmen and politicians.

## nature
### LITE
Domovoy are known for their small stature. They count among the six common kinds of Snowland Fae, alongside Vila, Leshy, Volkodlak, Snegovik and Jack Frost. Their name is the Russian word for a house spirit.
### STANDARD +
Artifact text says they can supposedly live indefinitely, but they still age and suffer for it: one lore piece shows an old Domovoy white-haired and frail after centuries. Pulcinella is considered tall for his people, roughly a head above other Domovoy. Like other fae they use elemental power without a Vision, and they share the cold-loving habits of their kin.
### LARGE +
The fae in general were created by Hyperborean artisans, who forged them with forbidden methods and the light of the Frost Moon. The Belyi Tsar's own notes at the Pale Crown Palace say the fae were meant to surpass humans, and he called the "servants of humans" story a myth. Nothing in canon sets the Domovoy apart in origin from the other kinds.

## society
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
The kind was traditionally led by a Grand Duke of the Domovoy. Alvis held that title from before the Belyi Tsar unified Snezhnaya. He ruled Kitezhgrad and its mining trade until the Cataclysm destroyed the city about 500 years ago, then founded Novokitezhgrad. The title has since lost its old power, since fae lost their monopoly on rank under the Tsaritsa, and Domovoy now work as officials, engineers and politicians.
### LARGE +
Domovoy sat on both sides of the same political fight. Artifact lore describes Grand Duke Alvis and Izmaylov, the Royal Energy Commission's president, as fierce rivals at the Snezhnograd Veche. Alvis, a noble of the old order, favoured fae of noble birth for posts, while Izmaylov, a younger Domovoy, backed humans chosen by the Tsaritsa's wishes. In the Foundryman artifact stories Izmaylov made repeated trips into the Shadow Realm hoping to find his teacher Aksinya, and eventually took over the Energy Commission. These are artifact narratives and do not name every detail outright.
### ULTRA +
Many of the Domovoy in canon are long-lived enough to bridge eras. Pulcinella entered the human world after the Cataclysm to secure a place in Anastasya's plans, joined the Fatui and was named a Harbinger, then learned politics as protege of the human mayor of Snezhnograd, Ioanna Ivanovna The arrangement was seen as humiliating for a fae. After her death he became Mayor of Snezhnograd, Speaker of the Snezhnograd Veche and a major organiser of Project Stuzha.

## abilities
### STANDARD
Canon gives no kind-wide combat traits for the Domovoy. Individuals are known for craft and statecraft rather than martial display: Alvis monopolised mining, Izmaylov forged weapons and ran the Energy Commission, and Pulcinella is a career politician.

## relations
### STANDARD
The Domovoy were among the fae who accepted the Belyi Tsar. Alvis was a staunch ally even before the unification and taught Aksinya, though he disliked her at first. After the Tsar's death the Domovoy adapted to the Tsaritsa's rule, and Pulcinella serves her directly. They sit beside the other fae kinds as part of the old fae aristocracy of the Pale Star Court.
### LARGE +
Alvis's loyalty failed Aksinya in the end. According to the Eye of Graeae, he and others were persuaded to betray her by sending her into the Shadow Realm. In the 7.0 world quest series that includes "The Stone Beasts, Like Ghouls Seated" she returned and ensured he met an unwelcome fate, which the wiki records as her killing him.

## individuals
### STANDARD
Known Domovoy include Pulcinella (Fifth Harbinger), the late Grand Duke Alvis, and Izmaylov (Energy Commission president). Their own entries carry the detail.

## entry: Grand Duke Alvis

@version: 7.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Alvis was Grand Duke of the Domovoy from before the Belyi Tsar unified Snezhnaya and Lord of Kitezhgrad until the Cataclysm, after which he founded Novokitezhgrad. He helped the Tsar defeat the Volkodlak king Miscislav's allies, mentored the human girl Aksinya, and later joined the betrayal that sent her into the Shadow Realm. He died in the 7.0 world quests (the wiki records Aksinya as his killer). See his own entry.
