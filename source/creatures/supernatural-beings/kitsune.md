## summary
### LITE
Kitsune are fox youkai of Inazuma. They live far longer than humans, can shapeshift, and keep fox features such as ears or tails in human form. Kitsune are graded: Sky Kitsune rank highest and yakan sit two grades below. The Hakushin line was the most revered; its member the Kitsune Saiguu was worshipped as a kitsune goddess and was lost in the Cataclysm 500 years ago. As of 7.1 the best-known living kitsune is Yae Miko, Guuji of the Grand Narukami Shrine.
### STANDARD +
The only other kitsune shown alive in the present is the young Youko from the limited-time Akitsu Kimodameshi event (3.3). The kitsune known in canon are clever and mischievous, and the two best-known, Yae Miko and the Kitsune Saiguu, are close to the Raiden Shogun.

## nature
### LITE
Kitsune are youkai, not animals: intelligent, long-lived and able to walk among humans. Shapeshifting is their defining talent.
### STANDARD +
Yae Miko has pink fox ears; five translucent tails show when she uses her Burst and one when she uses her skill, and she also has a fox form which she refuses to show the Traveler. The Kitsune Saiguu is described as having white hair and fur. Miko loves fried tofu and despises pickled foods.
### LARGE +
The wiki notes that kitsune may be able to move their consciousness into inanimate objects, inferred from Yae Miko having passed that skill to Ei; this is an implication drawn from a quest, not a stated rule. Kitsune also leave traces after death: the Saiguu's memories outlasted her, and Urakusai's lingered in the Ley Lines in the Sacred Sakura's roots until he possessed a living novelist and later the Traveler (The Great Narukami Offering, 2.5).
### ULTRA +
Yae Miko's character story pictures the age before Inazuma's founding: tengu ruling the skies, oni in battle, tanuki on the back roads, and kitsune walking among humans. Kitsune can start young: Miko was a young kitsune perched on the Saiguu's shoulder at the Hyakkiyakou gatherings, and is now a mighty youkai herself.

## abilities
### LITE
Shapeshifting and long life. Yae Miko's skill calls sealed yakan spirits out of sakura branches to strike with lightning.
### STANDARD +
Miko's talent text says the spirits sealed in the sakura branches are yakan that preyed on humans, and that she feels free to command them since they rank two grades below Sky Kitsune. This is the one place canon states the grading system directly.
### LARGE +
The Hakushin apprentices were "greatly skilled in magic and shape-shifting" and helped run the shrine and defend Mt. Yougou. The Kitsune Saiguu is also shown outwitting the bake-danuki Ioroi, and she secretly learned some of his magic.
### ULTRA +
Sky Kitsune Statues stand at Mt. Yougou, Konda Village, Araumi and near the Kamisato Estate; they respond to Electro and appear in the Sacred Sakura Cleansing Ritual quests. The wiki notes that no lore is attached to them yet.

## society
### LITE
There is no single kitsune society in the present; kitsune are few. In the past the Hakushin Clan was the most prominent kitsune family and was tied to the Grand Narukami Shrine.
### STANDARD +
Hakushin was matriarch of the Hakushin Clan, which dwelt around Mt. Yougou; the Grand Narukami Shrine is implied to have existed in her day, and she managed it with six apprentices, the eldest being the unruly Black Fox Itaru. The Kitsune Saiguu belonged to the same lineage. Yae Miko is described as the Saiguu's kin and a descendant of Hakushin.
### LARGE +
The Chronicles of the Six Kitsune (Urakusai's original, a hit 500 years ago, and the present-day New Chronicles adaptation sold at Yae Publishing House) tell of Itaru: banished from the mountain for wrecking a shintai of the Shogun while drunk, she defeated and recruited the bandit Twin Oni of Tokaku, travelled helping those in need, and unmasked a tanuki posing as the swindling melon merchant Dozaemon. They show kitsune as larger than life, fond of drink and quick with their fists.
### ULTRA +
In the human world, Yae Miko is both Guuji of the Grand Narukami Shrine and editor-in-chief of Yae Publishing House, which publishes light novels. The young kitsune Youko appears in the limited-time Akitsu Kimodameshi event (3.3); the wiki describes her as a girl in a black fox mask who is friends with a kappa and a hitotsume kozou, and gives no further profile.

## relations
### LITE
The kitsune known in canon, Yae Miko and the Kitsune Saiguu, are close to the Raiden Shogun and made friends among humans. Canon pairs kitsune with the bake-danuki as tricksters and rivals (the Saiguu and Ioroi, Itaru and the tanuki merchant Dozaemon).
### STANDARD +
The Kitsune Saiguu was a close ally and friend of Ei. Her parting words to Ei were not to be blinded, not to waver, and to keep walking the path she believed in. Reizenbou, the Great Tengu, became the Saiguu's bodyguard after losing a race to her, and the Saiguu knew the oni Chiyo, whose beauty and sword skill she praised. Yae Miko remains the Shogun's servant and friend.
### LARGE +
The Saiguu tricked Ioroi into surrendering to the Shogun and then persuaded Ei to spare him and take him under her command, while Itaru exposed a tanuki merchant. Toki Alley Tales claims the two species once fought a war lasting centuries over Inazuma; this is folklore from an in-world book.
### ULTRA +
Urakusai, a close associate of the Saiguu, was sent away by her for an unnamed grave mistake before the Cataclysm. He returned only as a spirit in 2.5. He is also described as having been a grandfather figure to Yae Miko in her youth.

## individuals
### STANDARD
- Yae Miko (playable): Guuji of the Grand Narukami Shrine, editor-in-chief of Yae Publishing House, and the only major kitsune alive in the present.
- Kitsune Saiguu (lost in the Cataclysm): the Hakushin kitsune worshipped as a goddess and Ei's friend.
- Hakushin (long dead): matriarch of the Hakushin Clan.
### LARGE +
- Itaru the Black Fox: eldest of Hakushin's six apprentices, big as a bear and strong as a tiger; hero of New Chronicles of the Six Kitsune.
- Urakusai: scholar and author of the original Chronicles; died, with his memories resting in the Sacred Sakura Ley Lines until 2.5.
- Youko: young kitsune from Akitsu Kimodameshi (3.3), a limited-time event.

## history
### STANDARD
A kitsune lineage shaped Inazuma's religious life: the Hakushin line looked after a shrine on Mt. Yougou and supported the Raiden Shogun. About 500 years ago the Saiguu left her shrine for Tenshukaku to stay close to Ei, foreseeing a disaster that would engulf heaven and earth. She was lost in the Cataclysm.
### LARGE +
The Hakushin Ring text, narrated by the Saiguu in her last moments, recalls her joys: a race with Reizenbou (she won, suspecting the tengu let her), a contest with Ioroi that ended with him serving the Shogun, a moonlit night in the imperial garden with Ei, and friendships with humans including the shrine maiden Asase Hibiki, the kanjou Hiiragi Hiroshi and the craftsman Mikoshi Nagamasa. In the Yougou Cleansing quest her purest remnant survived as the spirit Hanachirusato.
### ULTRA +
Yae Miko was not yet born when the Saiguu first left the Hakushin lands for the Grand Narukami Shrine, and was a young girl when the Saiguu left again for Tenshukaku; the Saiguu doted on her, but Miko speaks little of those times. In the Witch's Revelation quest (6.7), Miko appears in Fontaine as a great youkai of Inazuma being read by the witch Barbeloth.

## status
### STANDARD
As of 7.1, kitsune appear only in Inazuma content. Yae Miko is the sole known major living kitsune; nothing in Nod-Krai (6.x) or Snezhnaya (7.0-7.1) content shows other kitsune.
### LARGE +
Miko's trip to Fontaine for Witch's Revelation (6.7) took her out of Inazuma but added no lore about kitsune as a species; the witch Barbeloth only remarks that a youkai's birth is tied to desire, and calls Miko a great youkai of Inazuma who likely holds a greater desire than most.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay interpretation (this project's recommendation, not canon): portray kitsune as playful, perceptive and used to being underestimated, who tease in elaborate, indirect ways. They are rare, so a stranger claiming to be a kitsune needs a believable reason.
### LARGE +
Do not give kitsune powers beyond what is shown: shapeshifting, long life, spirit-binding and Electro shrine magic fit; mind control or world-shaping do not. Do not invent new Great Kitsune, extra Saiguu scenes or Hakushin descendants.

## entry: Hakushin Clan
@keys: Hakushin, Hakushin Clan, Hakushin Kitsune, Hakushin lands
### STANDARD
Hakushin was the matriarch and namesake of the most revered kitsune lineage in Inazuma, which dwelt around Mt. Yougou and looked after a shrine implied to be the Grand Narukami Shrine. She trained six apprentices, the eldest being Black Fox Itaru. The Kitsune Saiguu and Yae Miko are among her descendants. Nothing is known of her death or personal life beyond New Chronicles of the Six Kitsune.

## entry: Black Fox Itaru
@keys: Itaru, Black Fox, Six Kitsune, Twin Oni of Tokaku
### STANDARD
Itaru was the eldest of the Six Kitsune and the central hero of Urakusai's Chronicles, retold in New Chronicles of the Six Kitsune. She is described as bear-broad and tiger-strong, wild, fond of drink and prone to breaking things. After being banished from Mt. Yougou for smashing the Shogun's shintai she beat and recruited the Twin Oni of Tokaku, two bandit sisters, and went about helping those in need. She also exposed Dozaemon, a Konda Village melon merchant who was a tanuki in disguise, and spared his life. This is an in-world adaptation, so details may be embellished.

## entry: Yakan and Sky Kitsune grades
@keys: Sky Kitsune, Yakan, yakan kitsune
### STANDARD
Yae Miko says kitsune are ranked in grades: she is a Sky Kitsune and the yakan are two grades beneath. The yakan sealed in her sakura branches had preyed on humans. Canon does not detail the grades.

## entry: Urakusai
@keys: Urakusai
@version: 2.5
### LARGE
Urakusai was a famous kitsune writer and tea connoisseur, praised as one of the most cultured of all kitsune, and a friend of the Kitsune Saiguu. About 500 years ago he made an unnamed mistake that angered her, and she sent him away; his deeds may have been connected to the Abyss's later incursion, though no text says how. He created the blade Uraku Misugiri but never used it on a living being. He is also described as a grandfather figure to the young Yae Miko. His memories later lay in the Sacred Sakura's Ley Lines, and in The Great Narukami Offering (2.5) he briefly possessed the Traveler to talk with Miko.

## entry: Kitsune Saiguu
@keys: Kitsune Saiguu, Saiguu, Lady Saiguu
@version: 2.0
### LARGE
The Kitsune Saiguu was a kitsune of the Hakushin line who served at the Grand Narukami Shrine and was worshipped as a goddess. She was Ei's close friend, warned of a coming disaster, moved to Tenshukaku, and was lost in the Cataclysm. A dark will consumed her; the unsullied part of her memory became the spirit Hanachirusato.
