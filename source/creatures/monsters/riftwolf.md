## summary
### LITE
Riftwolves, also called Rifthounds or Wolves of the Rift, are pack-hunting beasts of the Abyss that travel together like wolves and are said to be able to erode the borders of the world. They were made by the alchemist Gold. They first appeared in Version 2.2, mainly on Tsurumi Island in Inazuma, and are ruled by the Golden Wolflord boss (2.3). Their attacks inflict a party-wide Corrosion that drains HP.
### STANDARD +
They are not new: they ran across the continent during the Cataclysm, were driven to low numbers by hunts and resistance, and began to re-emerge in recent years. Their Archive text compares them to real wolves and even suggests a jealous urge to replace their "next of kin".
### LARGE +
Wiki-listed sightings: Tsurumi Island, the Chasm, Enkanomiya, Sumeru, Fontaine, Natlan and Nod-Krai, and Wolvendom after Razor's Lupus Minor Chapter begins. Natlan's unstable ley lines and Abyss attacks draw them in, and they were among the abyssal forces in Gosoythoth's invasion when the Sacred Flame went out.

## nature
### LITE
Dull-coloured, alien beasts with blood strong enough to dissolve the world's boundaries. They act like wolf packs, but the Abyss, not nature, is their origin.
### STANDARD +
Created by Gold (Rhinedottir), who classified them as "Alfisol" according to the Whelps' Archive text, though the Golden Wolflord itself was an unintentional creation. Archive text says they show certain biological behaviours like real wolves.
### LARGE +
"Alfisol" is a real soil order; the wiki notes this as trivia and canon gives no further meaning. The Wolflord's Archive entry says it has no name because it was one of Gold's unintentional creations, which is why it is obsessed with invading worlds that are not its own and making a name for itself.

## abilities
### LITE
The elites use Electro (Thundercraven) or Geo (Rockfond). Any contact, even absorbed by a shield, applies Corrosion to the whole party, draining HP over time. Smaller Whelps run with them.
### STANDARD +
Game mechanics (not narrative lore): their Fury gauge fills when struck by their own element; when full they enter an Elemental Devour state, hitting harder but with reduced resistance to that element. Thundercraven can teleport.
### LARGE +
The Golden Wolflord uses a shield, Corrosion, a pushing vacuum field, a tornado, a mouth beam and skull attacks. At 70 percent HP it enters its Elemental Devour state and calls Rifthound Skulls that provide its shield until they are broken; its heavy dive attack appears only in that state.

## society
### STANDARD
They hunt as a pack under a leader. The Archive says the Wolflord commands its followers, its "hidden claws", to dissolve space and forge a rift through which it can enter, and that after a defeat in a previous invasion the pack chose an island with no humans or protectors to prepare for their lord's arrival.
### LARGE +
Elsewhere the same effect appears as Beastly Rifts, which the Traveler removed on Elynas in Fontaine. A large nest in the Roots of Erinnyes, together with a Root-Consuming Tumor, sickened the tree until the Traveler and a Primordial Bathysmal Vishap cleared it.

## relations
### STANDARD
Their maker is Gold; they belong to the Abyss and turn up alongside abyssal invaders. In Sumeru the quest Khvarena of Good and Evil calls them echoes left from the battles of the Cataclysm. In Wolvendom, a group began to cause havoc in the surrounding area during Razor's story quest.

## individuals
### STANDARD
Thundercraven Rifthound and Rockfond Rifthound (and their Whelps) are the common elites. The Golden Wolflord, "Nameless King of the Rift", is the Tsurumi Island world boss and drops Riftborn Regalia.
### LARGE +
An Abyss-Corroded Golden Wolflord exists as a quest-exclusive variant; it is not covered here.

## scene_use
### STANDARD
Use Riftwolves as an eerie, relentless pack threat in places where borders are weak (ley-line disorder, Abyss incursions). They do not speak or bargain. Treat them as echoes of the Cataclysm rather than as a modern army.
### ULTRA +
Keep the two layers apart: Rifthounds are common beasts in the wild, while the Wolflord is a singular ruler with a motive (a name and a world of its own). Do not give the Rifthounds speech, and do not confuse them with the ordinary wolves of Wolvendom.

## entry: Golden Wolflord
@keys: Golden Wolflord, Nameless King of the Rift
@version: 2.3
### STANDARD
The nameless ruler of the Riftwolves, a twisted beast from another world and one of Gold's unintended creations. It commands the pack to dissolve space so it can enter, and is obsessed with making a name for itself. Its Tsurumi Island arena is an island without humans or protectors.
