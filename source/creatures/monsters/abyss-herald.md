## summary
### LITE
Abyss Heralds are elite melee fighters of the Abyss Order who command intense Abyssal power through blades. They come in two kinds: Wicked Torrents (Hydro) and Frost Fall (Cryo). The Archive calls them silent vanguards and envoys of the Abyss, counterparts to the singing, ranged Abyss Lectors. Most are anonymous. Klingsor, a one-time Sumeru Skeptic who embraced the Abyss, is the best-known named Herald.
### STANDARD +
Wicked Torrents debuted in 1.4, with the Presager of the Abyss in Archon Quest Chapter I Act IV; Frost Fall followed in 3.5. Unlike mages, Heralds are individual threats and several have personal roles in the story.

## nature
### LITE
A Herald is a person-sized, humanoid creature of the Abyss, armed with a sword of water or ice. Archive text for Wicked Torrents calls the type silent figures at the Abyss's vanguard whose tidal blades "engulf everything in an instant"; the Frost Fall entry says that just as saints spread the gospel, there are envoys to correct twisted strains and remove dissonance, and that their dark frost is said to freeze even the soul.
### STANDARD +
Klingsor shows how a Herald can come to be: a human Skeptic who found a Khaenri'ahn ruin and its gospel, "embraced the defilement's blessings" and was transformed. His current form is a Hydro Wicked Torrents Herald. Whether every Herald was once a person is not stated.
### LARGE +
Heralds are not all in the Abyss Order. The Cryo Herald the Traveler's Sibling met in the Caribert quest was encountered before the Order was founded. The wiki also notes that East Asian versions use the Christian word "apostle" rather than "herald".

## abilities
### LITE
Fast, aggressive melee attackers, with some ranged attacks. In the Hydro form the blades throw torrents of water; in the Cryo form, bursts of ice. At low health a Herald raises a tough elemental shield and falls the moment that shield breaks.
### STANDARD +
Tutorial text: Wicked Torrents commands "warped torrents", and some attacks lengthen the cooldowns of skills already on cooldown. Frost Fall commands ice and frost, and some attacks drain stamina. Fans note that some Wicked Torrents attack names (Surging Tide, The Wicked Torrents) share wording with Tartaglia's talents; this is wiki trivia, not an in-world link.
### LARGE +
Gameplay (not literal lore): the shield is best broken with the right element. The Frost Fall Herald cannot be met in the open world outside quest-specific encounters (it otherwise appears in Spiral Abyss, Imaginarium Theater and certain time-limited events) and lacks entry and defeat voice lines.

## society
### STANDARD
Heralds are listed among the Abyss Order's sentient Abyss creatures, alongside Lectors, Iniquitous Baptists and mages. The Order's hierarchy is officially unknown: the Traveler's Sibling leads, an "inner circle" exists, and Haden, an Iniquitous Baptist, led its force in Natlan, but how Heralds rank is not stated.
### LARGE +
The wiki pairs the two: Heralds are the melee elites and Lectors the ranged ones, though Heralds also have some ranged attacks.

## relations
### LITE
Heralds serve the Abyss Order, and so, indirectly, its leader, the Traveler's Sibling. Their natural enemies are the Traveler, Dainsleif, and the guardians of the lands they attack.
### STANDARD +
A Frost Fall Herald stands with the sibling in the ambush where the sibling tampers with the Ley Lines and wounds Dainsleif (the Bedtime Story era, 4.7). Klingsor works with Nasejuna, then head of the Order of Skeptics, in Khvarena of Good and Evil.
### LARGE +
Enjou, an Abyss Lector, and Klingsor, a Herald, are the two best-known named figures of this pair.

## individuals
### STANDARD
The Presager of the Abyss is an unnamed Hydro Herald who works directly under the Traveler's Sibling and clashes with the Traveler four times in Archon Quests: first in the Call of the Abyss domain, then at Wolvendom (assisted by Hilichurl Shooters), again in the Call of the Abyss domain (assisted by Abyss Mages), and finally in the Chasm's underground mines, where he is killed. He flees after the first three fights. Klingsor is described in his own entry. The Fortune Lector, a Cryo Herald, guards the last chamber of the Caribert quest, where it tests the Traveler before the Sinner's crystal.
### LARGE +
Ansger the Frigid Glare is a Cryo Herald who defends Haden during the Archon Quest A Space and Time Without You (Chapter V, Act VI, 5.7), and a Hydro Herald, V. Haldher the Corrupt, defends him in the same quest. A Frost Fall Herald, the Emissary of Frigid Blades, is fought in the Night Kingdom during Journey of Stars and Flames.
### ULTRA +
The Presager returns in Silent Seeker of Knowledge as a dream manifestation, the "Confidante" to the "Omen", which the wiki reads as an image of the Traveler's fears and not a sign that he survived.

## scene_use
### STANDARD
This project's suggestion (not canon): treat a Herald as a silent, disciplined opponent who does not parley, in contrast to a Lector or a mage. Heralds do bark short battle cries (Frost Fall: "Foolish mortals!"), but full conversation is not shown. If a scene needs a Herald who talks, remember that Klingsor is a Herald with a human history and a mission, not a typical one.

## entry: Klingsor

@keys2: Abyss Herald, Skeptics, Nasejuna, Khvarena
@version: 3.6
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Klingsor was once a ranger of the Order of Skeptics in the Girdle of the Sands, a descendant of the Khaenri'ahn Hadura. Resenting the shame attached to Khaenri'ahns, he came to suspect the Skeptics hid the truth. Sent to supervise visiting Akademiya scholars in the ruins, he found material that confirmed his doubts and embraced the Abyss, becoming a Herald. His first attack on the Vourukasha Oasis was repelled by the Pari and the Skeptics. In the present he persuades Nasejuna to help seize the Great Songs of Khvarena in order to break the seal at Tunigi Hollow; the Traveler stops them. He is covered further in his own entry.

### STANDARD
An unnamed Hydro Wicked Torrents Herald who works directly under the Traveler's Sibling. The wiki counts four encounters across Archon Quests: the Call of the Abyss domain, Wolvendom (with Hilichurl Shooters), the Call of the Abyss domain again (with Abyss Mages) and finally the Chasm's underground mines during Memories of Inteyvat, where the Traveler and Dainsleif defeat him. He appears again in Silent Seeker of Knowledge only as a dream.
