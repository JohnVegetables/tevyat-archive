## summary
### LITE
Secret Source Automatons are machines left in Natlan by its ancient dragon civilization, and a few are also found on the Frost Moon. Most sit idle among ruins until disturbed. They bear golden inscriptions, and the fighting kinds react to a traveler's Nightsoul power. They first appeared in Version 5.0.
### STANDARD +
"Secret Source" is the in-game name for the old technology of the ancient dragon civilization. Not every Secret Source machine is a fighter: the group also covers work machines, guards, servants and machines that once supervised whole cities. The fighting models are Hunter-Seeker, Configuration Device, Overseer Device and, on the Frost Moon, the Precursor's Matrix.

## nature
### STANDARD
Natlan's enemy group is described as strange machines from a time long past, usually found in an idle state. Their bodies glow with golden patterns akin to the inscriptions on the ruins. They are tied to Secret Source technology and to phlogiston-engineered intelligence, not to living creatures. They have no clans or leaders of their own. The sturdier ones are described as having lost, or keeping only fragments of, the purpose their makers gave them.
### LARGE +
Their elements are not uniform: the Hunter-Seeker uses Pyro, the Configuration Device Electro, and the Overseer Device and the Precursor's Matrix Hydro. Intelligence varies with the model. Most Servants communicate through gestures, though some highly intelligent models have advanced speech and memory; the overseer-class machines are described as retaining only basic commands.

## origins
### STANDARD
The wiki's group and Servant pages credit the ancient dragons, and the Overseer Device and Precursor's Matrix texts link their makers to the dragons. The Overseer Device's Handbook entry says a once-mighty ancient race used it to oversee the running of their cities, and the Archive says that race put part of its power into phlogiston-engineered intelligence for this role. The Hunter-Seeker's Archive text adds a caution: the machines were reportedly made for work such as collecting samples, not for war, and rumor says humans did not build them.
### LARGE +
The Overseer Device's Archive entry says its civilization fell through wars with an emerging race, leaving the machines with little more than basic commands. The Configuration Device is described as a manufacturing machine able to change shape, once used to build cities even in extreme places such as the depths of a volcano, and also capable of battle through preset instructions. The wiki's Three Moons page adds that the Dragon King Nibelung used Secret Source technology to raise the Three Moons at modern-day Nod-Krai; that lore comes from Nod-Krai-era quests, not from the enemy texts.

## models
### STANDARD
Hunter-Seeker (Pyro, Version 5.0): an elite enemy built for gathering specimens; combat was an afterthought. Configuration Device (Electro, 5.1): a normal boss in Toyac Springs, in game called Ancient Ruins Constructor and also Secret Source Constructor. Overseer Device (Hydro, 5.6): a normal boss in Atocpan, in game called Ancient Ruins Master Terminal and also Secret Source Overseer. Precursor's Secret Source Matrix of Overseer Network (Hydro, added in 6.7, Luna VIII): an elite enemy, and also a Local Legend, Mushteshir, the Pioneer That Gazes at the Stars, in Kalag Guarding Grounds on the Frost Moon.
### LARGE +
Non-combat machines in the same group include Secret Source Servants (small, tailed, dragon-faced helpers), Secret Source Sentinels (guards, record keepers and transport machines), Secret Source Dragon Idols (large serpentine statues made of stone segments that can be ridden or fitted with cannons) and Secret Source Scout Sweepers (small fish-like machines caught with Emberglow Bait in Natlan). A variant of the Configuration Device, Secret Source Automaton: Firepower Enhanced, appeared only in the On the Trail of Behemoths event.

## abilities
### STANDARD
Roleplay interpretation of the in-game fights: these machines fire beams and energy orbs, strike with claw-like limbs, and generate repeated attacks over a fight. Elemental type matches the model (see Models). In game they react to Nightsoul's Blessing: once a character holds the state for long enough, the machine counters with a stronger attack and becomes easier to hurt for a time (gameplay detail, not lore).
### LARGE +
Model specifics from the gameplay pages. The Hunter-Seeker uses laser and clamping attacks, and a cluster laser once its counter meter fills. The Configuration Device sweeps lasers, strikes with a transformed claw, and charges a Boltsphere Cannon on pillars of solid phlogiston; the pillars can be climbed only by Children of Echoes abilities or by indwelling a Tepetlisaurus. The Overseer Device uses water blades and vortices, and its Sweeper Mode builds up momentum that Cryo attacks reduce. The Matrix creates a Convergence Field with reduced gravity. The Local Legend version adds a Suppression Field that closes the arena and summons four Detection Devices.
### ULTRA +
The Archive text for the Matrix says the leaders of the "pioneers" engraved their most earnest words in golden sigils, hoping their successors, with the help of these machines, would keep the light that illuminates the universe shining. This hints at a purpose beyond guarding, but canon does not describe the machines' purpose more exactly.

## society
### STANDARD
Canon presents these machines as relics of an earlier civilization, not something Natlan's people built (the Hunter-Seeker text says rumor holds they were not made by humans). The combat machines are ruin dwellers, found at ruins, springs and old sites, and they attack travelers who come near or disturb them. Secret Source Servants are the exception: they cooperate with people on small tasks, activate spiritways and dispel barriers.
### LARGE +
Servants are described as able to control other Secret Source technologies, such as barriers and spiritways, and to communicate with other Secret Source machines. Some highly intelligent ones were assigned to particular masters. One in the quest In Search of a Hidden Heart says it is the only intelligent Servant without a master, and that it kept basic city upkeep and records going even after Lord Xiuhcoatl fell.

## individuals
### STANDARD
Mushteshir, the Pioneer That Gazes at the Stars, is the only named combat machine, a stronger Local Legend form of the Precursor's Matrix. The Archive records the old name of these out-of-control machines as Ur-Namrasit-Tilimtu-Babbar, "the one who brings light to the darkness", and calls them overseers that did not live to serve the one they were meant for. Named servants (Amaru, Awanyu, Ehecatl and "Kukulkan") are NPCs and are not described here.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay interpretation (this project's recommendation, not canon text): combat machines do not talk, bargain or lead; they react to intruders with violence or lie dormant. They should feel old and worn, not malicious. Servants may speak and help, but only the ones canon presents that way. Do not give any of these machines a secret agenda, a modern faction or knowledge of current events.
### LARGE +
In a scene, use the golden glow, stone and metal bodies, and sudden activation when someone steps into a ruin. Machines near Nightsoul users react more fiercely. Do not describe the Frost Moon machines as controlled by the Frostmoon Scions or by Nod-Krai's governments; canon does not say so.

### STANDARD
An elite Pyro enemy and the first of the group to appear. The Handbook calls it an ancient machine used in object collection. Its Archive entry says it was meant for work such as capturing and collecting samples, with only basic combat function, and mentions rumor that a more ancient civilization than the humans of Natlan built it.

## entry: Configuration Device
@keys: Configuration Device, Secret Source Constructor, Ancient Ruins Constructor
@keys2: Toyac Springs, Natlan, automaton
@version: 5.1
### STANDARD
An Electro world boss at Toyac Springs. The Archive calls it a shape-changing manufacturing machine from the ancient ruins, used to build cities, even in extreme settings such as a volcano's depths, and still able to fight through preset instructions. It drops a Gold-Inscribed Secret Source Core.

## entry: Overseer Device
@keys: Overseer Device, Secret Source Overseer, Ancient Ruins Master Terminal
@keys2: Atocpan, Natlan, automaton
@version: 5.6
### STANDARD
A Hydro world boss in Atocpan, described as a domination mechanism that retained great authority long after its makers fell. Its in-game description says its true purpose was to lord it over the Source Mechanisms, and that its stone dragon head still roars for anyone entering its lair. It drops a Secret Source Airflow Accumulator.

## entry: Mushteshir
@keys: Mushteshir, Precursor's Matrix, Pioneer That Gazes at the Stars, Kalag Guarding Grounds
@keys2: Frost Moon, Lunar Highlands, Nod-Krai, machine
@version: 6.7
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Mushteshir is a Local Legend form of the Precursor's Secret Source Matrix of Overseer Network, introduced in the Luna VIII update, Version 6.7. The Handbook describes an ancient machine trapped among the lunar ruins, now attacking whatever sets foot on the ground it guards. The Archive says it never met the one it was meant to serve, and calls it the last out-of-control remnant of a long-lost ideal. It stands in Kalag Guarding Grounds, in the Frost Moon's Lunar Highlands, after two combat challenges.

## entry: Secret Source Servants and Sentinels
@keys: Secret Source Servant, Secret Source Sentinel, Secret Source Dragon Idol, Scout Sweeper
@keys2: Natlan, automaton, dragon, spiritway
@version: 5.0
### STANDARD
Servants help people with small tasks and, when active, glow with golden lines; asleep, they curl up. Sentinels are guard and record-keeping machines that can carry heavy loads, with a center disc that can serve as a display. Dragon Idols are rideable stone serpents. Scout Sweepers are fish-like heat-seeking machines caught on Emberglow Bait. All are classed with the group but are not enemies like the bosses above.
