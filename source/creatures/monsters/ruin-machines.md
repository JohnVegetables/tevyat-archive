## summary
### LITE
Ruin Machines are war machines built by the lost underground nation of Khaenri'ah, whose people called the common Ruin Guard a "Field Tiller". Masterless since Khaenri'ah fell 500 years ago, they roam or lie dormant in ruins all over Teyvat, wake when people come near, and attack with fists, blades, lasers and homing missiles. "Ruin Guard" is a modern name: people mistook them for the ruins' guardians.
### STANDARD +
The family includes the cyclops-like Ruin Guards, Ruin Hunters and Ruin Graders, the animal-shaped Ruin Sentinels, the vishap-like Ruin Drakes, and bosses such as the Perpetual Mechanical Array, the Ruin Serpent and the Aeonblight Drake. Dainsleif says the Abyss Order and the Ruin Guards grew from the same root: Khaenri'ah.

## nature
### LITE
Automatons, not living creatures: they have no element of their own and fight mostly with physical force. A glowing eye or core is each one's weak point.
### STANDARD +
Dainsleif says the first Field Tiller is not powered by the elements, which is why Elemental Sight shows nothing inside it. Archive text notes that the machines and the ruins they haunt can be thousands of years apart in age.
### LARGE +
Item text praises their exquisite engineering, says no one can make sense of their logic circuits, and suggests that whoever could reproduce a Chaos Core "could perhaps change the world". Their power lasts extraordinarily long: they still run centuries after Khaenri'ah fell. The Akademiya scholar Jazari says the cores supply almost unlimited energy, and that early models such as the Ruin Golems also carried backup relays fed by blocks of Ley Line energy, because the technique of harnessing "this dark power" was still new. Rene de Petrichor's notes call Khaenri'ah's use of the Abyss primitive and say a true perpetual energy source remained their unrealised ideal. Jazari adds that the Akademiya once could not handle the cores at all: a single mistake could cause a core meltdown.
### ULTRA +
Khaenri'ahn design was driven by mimesis. Ruin Drake item text describes people "obsessed with the idea of mimesis" who believed the pure order of the Hypostases and the primal power of dragons could be copied and improved on, so that a superior, unsullied body could replace a decaying order. The Ruin Sentinels copy animals, the Ruin Drakes copy vishaps, the Ruin Serpent echoes the sand-burrowing Setekh Wenut, and the Perpetual Mechanical Array resembles a Hypostasis. Some Akademiya opinion holds that the Sentinels are worth more to the study of biomimesis than the common Ruin Guard.

## origins
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Dainsleif explains the codename with a Khaenri'ahn saying: land is not to be tilled with farm tools but fought for with steel and blood. After Khaenri'ah's destruction the masterless Field Tillers went out of control and, over centuries, spread to every corner of Teyvat, coming to rest among ruins.
### LARGE +
Dainsleif suggests they were perhaps drawn by the sorrow of other lost civilisations. They are older than the Cataclysm. More than 2,000 years ago, envoys from Khaenri'ah came to Enkanomiya with a Ruin Guard to seize the forbidden book Before Sun and Moon; Antigonus (Antei) stopped them and destroyed the machine at the cost of his life (Antigonus, 2.4). A realm the adepti made to hold "ancient and evil artifacts" that Guizhong collected while studying mechanics also contains Ruin Guards and a Ruin Hunter. Ruin Serpent Archive text calls it heavy engineering equipment able to grind through bedrock, so that tunnels could carry the nation's forces anywhere on the continent.

## models
### STANDARD
- Ruin Guard: the common, slow humanoid; stomps, spins and fires homing missiles. Ruin Hunter: fast, with bladed arms, it flies to bomb and fire beams. Ruin Grader (1.2): larger, distorted and silent, with lasers and leg cores.
- Ruin Sentinels (2.0) and Ruin Drakes (3.0): see their sub-entry.
- Bosses: the Perpetual Mechanical Array (2.0), the Ruin Serpent (2.6) and the Aeonblight Drake (3.1).
### LARGE +
- The Perpetual Mechanical Array is four Sentinels joined into one Hypostasis-like machine on Araumi, Inazuma; its core's item text records a theory that such machines, with their especially large and complexly inscribed cores, autonomously chose to forsake biomimetic form in pursuit of function. The Ruin Serpent is a tunnelling excavator in The Chasm; the Aeonblight Drake is a giant drake at Devantaka Mountain in Sumeru.
- Ruin Golems ("Mechanical Wardens"): enormous crewed machines (own sub-entry). An unfinished prototype lies in Madinat al-Nuhas.
- The Ruin Hunter's description says its makers dropped the "low-performing" humanoid design for combat effectiveness; the Ruin Grader's says its leg cores seem to power an overly heavy body.

## abilities
### LITE
Heavy armour, crushing melee attacks, homing missiles, lasers and, for some models, flight. Hitting the exposed weak point can stun a machine for a while.
### STANDARD +
A Ruin Guard collapses if its eye or back socket is struck twice in quick succession; protective runes then shield the weak points for a while.
### LARGE +
Ruin Hunters charge and slash with arm-blades and, airborne, rain missiles, fire Pyro beams and blast lasers downward; their core is exposed only in flight. Ruin Graders fire sweeping and tracking lasers and drop mines, with weak points in the eye and both feet. Sentinels expose their oculi during certain attacks; Ruin Drakes adapt to elements. Boreas recalled that the first Field Tiller could spin, leap high and shoot "fireballs", and was stronger than many Vision holders though no match for a god.
Gameplay (not literal lore): high Physical resistance, so elemental attacks are favoured; bows hit weak points best. Inactive Ruin Guards that are real enemies glow in Elemental Sight, scenery wrecks do not. Drops include Chaos Device/Circuit/Core (humanoids), Chaos Gear/Axis/Oculus (Sentinels) and Chaos Storage/Module/Bolt (Drakes).

## society
### LARGE
Ruin Machines have no society: they are tools whose masters are gone. They patrol fixed routes, wait dormant until approached, or stand broken. Except for Ruin Graders, the humanoid models speak an unknown language when attacking, and many carry serial codes: Hu- for Ruin Guards, Fo- for Ruin Hunters, Ra- for Ruin Graders, Ald- for Sentinels and Faf- for Drakes.
### ULTRA +
Boss designations follow the same scheme: the Ruin Grader's title is "Ominous Field Tiller", the Perpetual Mechanical Array is "Perpetual Resonance SI/Ald", the Ruin Serpent "Excavator: SI/Glp" and the Aeonblight Drake "Perpetual Motion GL-Faf". The Ruin Guard at the Thousand Winds Temple is always GN/Hu-24601.

## distribution
### STANDARD
Found in nearly every region: Mondstadt, Liyue (including The Chasm), Inazuma and Enkanomiya, Sumeru, Fontaine and Natlan.
### LARGE +
In Mondstadt they sit in Brightcrown Canyon, Stormterror's Lair and on Dragonspine. Sentinels are concentrated in Inazuma, and Khaenri'ahns also brought machines into Enkanomiya. Ruin Drakes and the Ruin Golems are found almost only in Sumeru. Fontaine has only a few, on Elynas. Natlan keeps them out of sight: one machine in the underground ruins near the Flamegranate tree and many inactive or disabled ones, including several Aeonblight Drakes, in Ochkanatlan. Both humanoids and Sentinels are dug up in The Chasm.

## later_appearances
@version: 7.1
### LARGE
Nod-Krai and Snezhnaya (6.x-7.x): the wiki records a few Ruin Machines at Kipumaki Cliff in Nod-Krai, corrupted by the Abyss but inactive, and a Ruin Grader in Snezhnaya's Volkodlak Tundra with a Ruin Guard head lying in the snow nearby. Neither has a role in the Archon Quests.

## relations
### LITE
The Abyss Order scavenges them for Khaenri'ahn technology; Dainsleif, a Khaenri'ahn survivor, urges the Traveler to destroy them.
### STANDARD +
Childe says the Harbinger Dottore once ran a Ruin Guard research lab in Liyue, but that the machines were not built by the Fatui and are tied more closely to the Abyss.
### LARGE +
In A Herald Without Adherents, Dainsleif finds Abyss Mages picking through dead Ruin Guards for one particular valuable object; Paimon notices that Ruin Guards roam wherever there are Abyss Mage hideouts, and Dainsleif replies that the connection is no coincidence. In Tartaglia's story quest (1.1), Dottore has abandoned the lab out of boredom, and Childe's little brother Teucer adores the machines as "Mr. Cyclops" toys. In Sumeru they are called relics of the "Dahri", an archaic name for Khaenri'ah used in the Vahumana Darshan. When Khaenri'ah fell, many burst out of the desert; the Akademiya, unable to handle their cores, sealed them away. Jazari of the Kshahrewar studies Ruin Golems, and Mahamata Afratu suspects deeper, unknown links between Dahri machines and the Ley Lines.
### ULTRA +
The researcher Abattouy, who left the Akademiya after it banned research into mechanical lifeforms, modified a Ruin Defender into Karkata, a machine meant to prove that mechanical beings could become true lifeforms. After Abattouy's death Tighnari repaired Karkata and kept it at Pardis Dhyai (The Unanswerable Problems, 3.0). A torn note by Zandik (Dottore's earlier identity) found inside the Devantaka Ruin Golem records him collecting its parts and wishing he could learn how they were made.

## individuals
### LARGE
- The first Field Tiller: the prototype Ruin Guard, now shut down atop the tower of Stormterror's Lair (own sub-entry).
- The Ruin Golems of Sumeru, crewed by Schwanenritter knights during the Cataclysm (own sub-entry).
- The Anomalous Model Ruin Grader: an unusually strong Grader in the fog of Tsurumi Island, hunted for the scholar Hosseini during the limited-time event Shadow of the Ancients (2.2).
- Karkata: a modified Ruin Defender, now Tighnari's research assistant at Pardis Dhyai (see relations). Sandrone's companion Pulonia was a clockwork meka with Ruin Machine styling, not a confirmed Ruin Machine; Dottore destroyed his body in Descending Moon, and Sandrone now keeps it while trying to restore his core.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay interpretation (this project's recommendation, not canon text): Ruin Machines are mindless and hostile; they do not talk, negotiate or recognise anyone, Khaenri'ahns included. A dormant Ruin Guard slumped among mossy stones is a classic ambush. Adventurers salvage their parts, but ordinary people do not know they came from Khaenri'ah.
### LARGE +
Keep scale sensible: a Ruin Guard threatens a party of Vision holders, a Ruin Grader or Hunter more so, while Ruin Golems are immobile giants. Do not have them fight for the Abyss Order as a commanded army, which canon does not show, or give them elemental power beyond specific models (Hunters' beams, Drakes' absorbed elements).

## entry: Field Tiller
@keys: Field Tiller, Field Tillers, first Field Tiller, Weird Statue
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
"Field Tiller" was the Khaenri'ahn codename for the war machines now called Ruin Guards. The first Field Tiller was the prototype: every Ruin Guard is a replica of it, and as the prototype its combat power was unrestricted. It wandered into Boreas's proving ground at Wolvendom; Boreas mistook it for a challenger and badly damaged it. It fled into the ruins of Old Mondstadt (Stormterror's Lair) and, immobile, fired missiles at anyone approaching for about a year until it shut down. It still sits atop the central tower, labelled a "Weird Statue" until its identity is revealed.
### LARGE +
The Abyss Order planned to set the first Field Tiller's eye into the hands of the Defiled Statue, an inverted Statue of The Seven, attach the limbs of Osial and so build a mechanized god able to "topple the divine thrones of Celestia". In A Soul Set Apart, Elemental Sight could not find the eye because the machine is not elemental; Dainsleif drew it out with his own power and kept it himself.

## entry: Eye of the first Field Tiller
@keys: eye of the first Field Tiller, Field Tiller's Eye
@version: 4.7
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
In Bedtime Story (4.7), false memories appeared in Dainsleif's mind: first of handing the eye to a stranger, a trap to make him check the eye's hiding place, then, at that place, of having handed it to the Traveler. Caribert later explains that he implanted them and that Dainsleif had in fact carried the eye inside his body all along, planning to pass it to the Traveler; the Abyss Order took it, which allowed the Loom of Fate to be completed. Dainsleif also says the Osial plan had been only a technical experiment.

## entry: Tyrant's Final Fury
@keys: Tyrant's Final Fury, The Tyrant's Final Fury
### STANDARD
The Tyrant's Final Fury is an old Church of Favonius story, told by Barbara: for about a year, long ago, balls of fire rained on anyone approaching Old Mondstadt. People blamed the curse of the dead God of Storms, Decarabian, until the attacks stopped on their own. The Traveler and Dainsleif later worked out that the "fireballs" were missiles from the damaged first Field Tiller.

## entry: Ruin Golem
@keys: Ruin Golem, Ruin Golems, Mechanical Warden, Mechanical Wardens
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Ruin Golems are colossal Khaenri'ahn machines that dwarf every other model. Jazari says they may have been called "Mechanical Wardens" in Khaenri'ah, and unlike other Ruin Machines they were operated by a crew. During the Cataclysm the Schwanenritter brought them while evacuating Khaenri'ahn citizens into Sumeru and fought the dark beasts with them. Three stand in Sumeru: at Devantaka Mountain, the Valley of Dahri and the Desert of Hadramaveth.
### LARGE +
Logs inside the Devantaka Golem record failing cores, weapons that melted from overuse and a machine abandoned in the southern desert; memorial chronicles name knights who died, were executed for sabotage by Knight Marshal Anfortas, or went missing fighting the beasts. The Akademiya sealed the golems' cores. In Vimana Agama (3.0) the Traveler and Jazari restart the Devantaka Golem's control systems with backup power, and in Afratu's Dilemma (3.1) the Traveler shuts down a sealed golem that had begun attacking everything nearby; Afratu suspects a Ley Line anomaly.

## entry: Ruin Sentinels and Ruin Drakes
@keys: Ruin Sentinel, Ruin Sentinels, Ruin Cruiser, Ruin Destroyer, Ruin Defender, Ruin Scout, Ruin Drake, Ruin Drakes, Earthguard, Skywatch
### STANDARD
Ruin Sentinels (2.0) are Khaenri'ahn machines shaped after living things, adapted to different tasks: the hovering, hive-like Cruiser; the rooted Destroyer; the shielded, crab-like Defender; and the jellyfish-like Scout that lays energy orbs. They rest as cubes and are most common in Inazuma. Ruin Drakes (3.0), mainly in Sumeru, imitate vishaps: after an Energy Surge they absorb the element they took most damage from, show it in the Chaos Storage in their neck, resist it and turn it against their attackers. Hitting an exposed weak point paralyses them and clears the absorbed element.
