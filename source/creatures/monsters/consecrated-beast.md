## summary
### LITE
Consecrated Beasts are wild animals that fed on the remains of gods or similarly powerful beings and were warped by them. They grew huge and strong, sprouted bone-like growths they cannot shed, and now fight with elemental attacks. They are elite enemies, found mostly in Sumeru and rarely in Inazuma and Fontaine. Five kinds are known: fanged beast (Dendro), flying serpent (Anemo), horned crocodile (Hydro), red vulture (Pyro) and scorpion (Electro).
### STANDARD +
The Adventurer Handbook calls them lifeforms twisted by eating some great being. Surviving the change is rare and painful. They first appeared in Version 3.4.

## nature
### LITE
Ordinary animals, not a separate species: a predator, a reptile, a scavenger bird or an arthropod that ate the corpse of a far greater being and lived. They could not fully digest it, so the remains fused with their bodies into a bony shell.
### STANDARD +
Archive texts stress the cost. Most beasts that absorbed such power were killed by it; only a few evolved new forms. The devoured beings often have an almost immortal consciousness that cannot be silenced, and the power and will the beasts take in drives them toward more violence and carrion. The shell resembles a certain smelting material from Inazuma in composition.
### LARGE +
Item text for the shells treats them as relics of a calamity: the Desiccated Shell is an effigy of the "grand calamity that devoured the lands of yore", usually found where few dare go; the Sturdy Shell says that even beasts that sip a sliver of a god's power will meet the same end as the god; and the Marked Shell carries ancient script, the regrets of a bygone age, and will one day scatter like dust so the dead can rest.
### ULTRA +
Archive texts also read as warnings. The Fanged Beast entry observes that baits left by humans and the remains of immortals are traps, deliberate or not, and that coveting the strongest's gift costs more than a cage. The Horned Crocodile entry notes that humans sometimes revere majestic beasts as embodiments of a greater power. The Scorpion entry says such beasts were simply lucky to find a long-dead carcass before their rivals did.

## species
### STANDARD
- Consecrated Fanged Beast (Dendro, 3.6): a predator with claws, a tail blade and vine attacks.
- Consecrated Flying Serpent (Anemo, 3.4): a flying reptile that fires projectiles and creates vacuum fields.
- Consecrated Horned Crocodile (Hydro, 3.6): a reptile that fires water blades and burrows to ambush.
- Consecrated Red Vulture (Pyro, 3.4): a scavenging bird that bombs from the air.
- Consecrated Scorpion (Electro, 3.4): a burrowing arthropod with claws, a stinger and tail spikes.
### LARGE +
Each species is described in the Archive as a mutated animal of its kind. Sumeru holds the majority; the Red Vulture and Horned Crocodile are found on Elynas in Fontaine, and a Scorpion on Yashiori Island in Inazuma, near the remains of gods or equally powerful beings.

## abilities
### STANDARD
Aggressive, high-damage attacks of their own element. When they spot an intruder they enter a Phagocytic Form, a heavily armoured state that resists all elements, their own most of all. Periodically after a special attack they drop the form and release a Phagocytic Energy Block.
### LARGE +
Gameplay (not literal lore): destroying the block with elemental attacks, in one hit with the matching element, gives the active character a buff; striking the beast with it deals heavy damage and briefly staggers it. If the chance is missed, the beast re-enters Phagocytic Form. Most species have a weak point. Drops are Desiccated, Sturdy and Marked Shells, used for character and weapon enhancement.

## distribution
### STANDARD
Mostly Sumeru. Outside Sumeru, the wiki lists three: a Red Vulture and a Horned Crocodile on Elynas in Fontaine, and a Scorpion on Yashiori Island in Inazuma. The Fanged Beast has only one overworld spawn, at the mouth of Tunigi Hollow, and it appears only after the world quest The Hymn of Tir Yazad (Part 2).
### LARGE +
The pattern fits the description: they gather around the remains of gods and similarly powerful beings. The wiki's Elynas page suggests, with some hedging, that wildlife eating his remains may have become Consecrated Beasts.

## relations
### STANDARD
They are wildlife, not Abyss creatures, though the wiki's Elynas page suggests the Fontaine ones may owe their mutation to abyssal power in his remains. They have no known leaders, society or ties to Ruin Machines, and are hostile to anyone who comes near.
### LARGE +
No 6.x or 7.0-7.1 content (Nod-Krai, Snezhnaya) adds Consecrated Beasts or changes their lore.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay interpretation (this project's recommendation, not canon text): treat them as dangerous, animal-minded predators, not talking monsters. Do not give them speech, plans or loyalty. A scene near a god's remains or ruin can hint at one; the shell trophies are what adventurers take.
### LARGE +
Keep scale as an elite: a threat to a party of Vision holders, not a boss or world-ender. Avoid claiming which god a given beast ate unless the scene is Elynas or Yashiori Island.

## entry: Phagocytic Form
@keys: Phagocytic Form, Phagocytic Energy Block, Phagocytic Block
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
The state a Consecrated Beast enters as soon as it fights. The Archive tutorial says it is a powerful form, and that some attacks unleash Phagocytic Energy Blocks; destroying a block with the matching element absorbs its power, which can be released against the beast for heavy damage and a brief paralysis. In game terms it raises the beast's resistances; the lore meaning of the term is not explained beyond the devouring of a greater being.

## entry: Consecrated Shells
@keys: Desiccated Shell, Sturdy Shell, Marked Shell, Consecrated Beast shell
### STANDARD
The three enhancement materials dropped by the beasts, in rising quality. Their descriptions speak of a ruinous calamity, of beasts that gnaw at fallen gods only to share their end, and of ancient script on the pallid Marked Shell. A Marked Shell is crafted from three Sturdy Shells, and a Sturdy Shell from three Desiccated Shells.
