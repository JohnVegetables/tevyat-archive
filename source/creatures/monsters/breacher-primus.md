## summary
### LITE
Breacher Primuses are elite creatures of Fontaine, aligned with the Abyss and found mostly in and around the body of Elynas. The Archive calls them a strange life form with no known kin among Teyvat's creatures. According to Elynas himself they are his cells, made after his death, and they work like a body's defenders, driving harmful things away from him and his children.
### STANDARD +
They are not very intelligent. Elynas admits they sometimes attack the very people he would like them to spare, and Fontaine's earliest records after the Cataclysm show them both attacking and protecting humans. Known kinds include the Overgrown (Dendro) and Shatterstone (Geo) Breacher Primus, each in a small and a large form.

## nature
### LITE
Alien, Abyss-aligned lifeforms that behave like the defensive cells of a giant body. They are tied to Elynas, the great beast whom the Melusines call "Father".
### STANDARD +
The wiki says they were created from Elynas' body shortly after the Cataclysm, around the same time as the Melusines, through the Abyss's power and his remains by methods canon does not explain. Elynas in the quest "Limner, Dreamer, and Robotic Dog" calls them his cells and says he retains some control over them when needed. They are described as an elite enemy group, outside the ordinary ecology of Teyvat.
### LARGE +
Elynas was one of the creations of Rhinedottir, and he says he realised too late how harmful his existence was to others. His Melusine children grew from the wounds and debris of a great battle inside his body, while the Breacher Primuses serve a different purpose, protecting that body. The wiki infers a link to the Abyss because of their alignment and Elynas' origin; the text only says the Archive lists them in the Abyss family.

### ULTRA +
Elynas also says his blood is poisonous to humans, yet the Traveler was not harmed and became linked to his and his daughters' awareness; the same body that produces the Primuses is thus one where perception is shared. The Archive says the creatures mostly appear in Elynas, which fits their role as the body's defenders rather than roaming wildlife. Their name is only attested by the Archive and the quest; canon gives no individual Primus names.

## abilities
### STANDARD
In combat they use Physical attacks, a single projectile and melee strikes. Periodically a Primus enters a stressed state, gains a shield of its element and unleashes sustained elemental attacks. The Overgrown kind fires waves of Dendro missiles and the Shatterstone kind smashes the ground to cause Geo quakes. Breaking the shield, whether by elemental reactions or a single Arkhe attack, leaves it paralysed for a long time and exposes its core as a weak point (gameplay, labelled as such).
### LARGE +
The Large variants are bigger and tougher, and the Large Overgrown adds an Abyssal Barrage attack. Gameplay detail only: the Overgrown shield is most vulnerable to Electro.

## society
### STANDARD
They have no society. They act as Elynas' defenders, responding to threats near his body rather than to ordinary social cues, which is why they can strike friends and foes alike.

## relations
### STANDARD
Elynas is their source and the only figure shown steering them. The Melusines are his other creation and the ones the Primuses are meant to shield. During the quest, Jakob's actions endanger Elynas and the Traveler is trapped by a spell, and Elynas sends a Shatterstone Breacher Primus to free them, after which it fights beside the Traveler for a time. Elynas apologises that his cells are not clever enough to avoid attacking friends too.
### LARGE +
Elynas says the Breacher Primuses help repel harmful things from his body, and that his strength was returning because Jakob had restarted his heart briefly, which made the intervention possible. Elynas' influence over them is limited and temporary.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 they remain as hostile creatures in and around Elynas in Fontaine's depths. No later canon changes their nature.
### STANDARD +
Elynas' brief "time of life" ended after the Traveler stopped his revival, so the guiding voice behind them is again silent; the Primuses persist as instinctive guardians.

## entry: Elynas and his cells
@keys: Elynas, Breacher Primuses, Breacher Primus
@keys2: cells, Melusine, Father, Mamere
@version: 4.0
### STANDARD
Elynas is the great beast the Melusines call "Father". He says he gave life to his children by accident, from the wounds of a battle in his body, and that the Breacher Primuses are his cells, able to push harmful things away but not wise enough to spare friends. He does not want to be revived, knowing his living body would crush the Melusines' homes.

## entry: Overgrown and Shatterstone variants
@keys: Overgrown Breacher Primus, Shatterstone Breacher Primus, Lushmissile
@version: 4.0
### STANDARD
The Overgrown Breacher Primus uses Dendro: in its stressed state it gains a Dendro shield and fires waves of Lushmissiles. The Shatterstone Breacher Primus uses Geo and brings down Stonefalls that cause quakes. A Shatterstone one is the kind that frees the Traveler in "Limner, Dreamer, and Robotic Dog".
