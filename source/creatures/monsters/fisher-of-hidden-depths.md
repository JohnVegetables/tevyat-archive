## summary
### LITE
Fishers of Hidden Depths are Electro-aligned elite creatures of the Abyss family, first seen in Version 6.3 in the northern reaches of Nod-Krai. They are described as Abyssal creatures that slip through rifts from beyond this world. They come in groups, and their linked Deepdark Shields make a swarm hard to break unless the shield is shattered.
### STANDARD +
The in-game Archive describes them as deathless wanderers of the silent cosmic void, scaled rather than feathered, which use hooked beaks to draw biomass from world to world. The Adventure Handbook gives only the shorter line about slipping through rifts in the hidden depths.

## nature
### STANDARD
The Archive text compares them to an old philosopher's definition of humans as "featherless bipeds": scaled instead of feathered, these outsiders collect biomass with hooked beaks and travel between worlds without dying. The text uses this to suggest, wryly, that they might fit the same simplistic definition of "human". It does not say they are people, nor where they come from. An item they leave behind, the Hooked Beak of the Deep Shadow, is described as a birdlike hooked beak used by predators from beyond this world to store seized biomass.
### LARGE +
The tutorial text calls them a swarm that surged through the Rift of Hidden Depths. Canon does not explain that rift. They are classed in the Abyss enemy family, in which they appear alongside other Nod-Krai Abyssal threats.

## society
### STANDARD
Fishers are almost always met in groups. In the tutorial's words, their connections to one another form Deepdark Shields when several are present, and breaking one Fisher's shield shatters the whole swarm's shields at once and gathers the swarm around the Fisher whose shield broke. A lone survivor does not raise a shield.

## abilities
### STANDARD
Element: Electro. On entering a fight a Fisher roars and raises a Deepdark Shield that protects it and strengthens its attacks. Only elemental damage harms the shield, and Lunar reactions are especially effective against it. Kuuvahki currents, the pure non-polar kuuvahki waves released in northern Nod-Krai by plants and creatures such as Glimmerfruit, Mandragora, Spirebloom and Slothsheep, can also damage it; weak currents do little and strong ones damage it considerably.
### LARGE +
Attacks are a charge, a backflip pounce with claws and a head slam (when shielded), and a laser beam that several Fishers can fire in tandem. While the shield holds, these attacks reach further and hit harder. This is game mechanics rather than lore.
### ULTRA +
Used in a scene, the linked swarm reads best as a pack that fights as one mind: wound the group's shared protection and all of it breaks together, leaving them momentarily dazed and bunched. This is this archive's narrative reading of the mechanic, not a canon statement.

## relations
### STANDARD
The boss Lord of the Hidden Depths: Whisperer of Nightmares, who lurks at Ashveil Peak, summons four Fishers in its Deepdark Strike attack, and the Fishers are consumed by the boss's attack if left alive. Its Archive text uses similar wording about creatures that creep through rifts, but no text states a formal link beyond the shared name and mechanics.

## individuals
### STANDARD
No individual Fisher is named in canon.

## status
### STANDARD
As of Version 7.1 they remain regular elite enemies of northern Nod-Krai (Voidsea Outlook, Wavechaser Plain and Ashveil Peak are the northern areas where Abyssal contamination and kuuvahki currents are found), and no new information about them appeared in 6.x after their debut or in 7.0-7.1.
