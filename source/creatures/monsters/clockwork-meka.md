## summary
### LITE
Clockwork Meka (singular Mek) are Fontaine's machines: clockwork automatons that were built as laborers and surveyors and later modified for combat. They are powered by Arkhe, the Pneuma and Ousia energies native to Fontaine, which lets them deal elemental damage. Most are not truly intelligent. They are used by the Maison Gardiennage, sold to private citizens, and favored by thieves and brigands.
### STANDARD +
Each mek sits in one of two energy states, Pneuma-aligned or Ousia-aligned, and a hit from the opposite Arkhe throws it out of balance and cripples it for a time. The in-game Handbook calls them machines made possible by Fontaine's advanced engineering and its energy sources, and also excellent assistants to thieves and brigands. They first appeared as an enemy group in Version 4.0.

## nature
### LITE
Meka are powered by Arkhe. A mek is either Pneuma-aligned (its clockwork glows bright white) or Ousia-aligned (dark blue) and uses different attacks depending on which. Hit it with the opposite Arkhe and it temporarily loses its energy: it is reduced to physical attacks, or cannot attack at all. Some models deal the same damage type whichever way they are aligned.
### STANDARD +
Weaker meka are thrown off by one opposing Arkhe attack, specialist models need two, and boss-class meka three. The alignment is unstable by nature, so a mek tends to settle in one state or the other. Combat-oriented meka keep a backup power system that supports low-level fighting, while utility meka lose combat ability entirely until they recover. The Arithmetic Enhancer Mek has a self-repair function that restores it after a while.
### LARGE +
Modern meka draw on pneumousia in Fontaine's environment, refined by components called Repeaters. That makes them convenient inside Fontaine and much less useful elsewhere; Pneumousia Blocks can substitute as a fuel, but only briefly. Two models are exceptions to the alignment pattern: the Recon Log Mek always deals Electro and the Underwater Survey Mek always deals Hydro. The Prototype Cal. Breguet has no alternate alignment and is exclusively Ousia.

## origins
### STANDARD
Clockwork machinery was established in Fontaine long before the present, but traditional meka ran on elastic potential energy and were regarded as novelties. About 400-500 years ago Alain Guillotin and his peers learned to produce controlled Pneuma-Ousia annihilation reactions, turning a latent environmental danger into a powerful if unstable energy source. He also invented artificial Pneumousia Blocks. His new-type meka ran on a kinetic core using this technology, and were far more efficient and structurally tougher than the older designs, which could not withstand the energy.
### LARGE +
Alain's work made traditional clockwork research all but obsolete: funding dried up and researchers had to change fields or close their labs. One example is Butor, who shut down his Institute of Clockwork Applications. The wiki's trivia notes that the first meka Alain built were the Recon Log Mek and the Assault Specialist Mek, associated respectively with his sister Mary-Ann Guillotin and his late friend Carter Scherbius; the Archive itself says only that the Assault Specialist was named to commemorate a friend of its inventor, and that the Suppression Specialist is adapted from the "Cal. Scherbius" type. Since his time, meka researchers have advanced kinematics greatly but dynamics very little, so modern meka are versatile and inventive in design (the Underwater Survey Mek imitates living creatures) yet still generate energy at roughly the level of Alain's first inventions. The Arithmetic Enhancer Mek, which passes energy to other meka, was designed to compensate.

## models
### STANDARD
Normal meks (low health): Area Alert, Arithmetic Enhancer, Geological Survey, Nimble Harvester, Recon Log, Underwater Patrol and Underwater Survey. Specialist meks (tougher): Annihilation Specialist, Assault Specialist, Deepwater Assault, Construction Specialist and Suppression Specialist. Boss-class machines include the Icewind Suite and the Prototype Cal. Breguet. Geological Survey Meks can be set to detect specific minerals, which makes them popular with Treasure Hoarders.
### LARGE +
The humanoid models show how purposes have drifted toward combat: the Annihilation Specialist was designed to commemorate the inventor's lover, the Assault Specialist to assist humans, and the Icewind Suite as a dynamic art piece. Because they are used chiefly by the Maison Gardiennage, some citizens describe this as the state enforcing its monopoly on violence.

## society
### STANDARD
Meka are used mainly by the Maison Gardiennage, but citizens may buy them for personal use. By law each purchase must be registered and recorded. People get around this, for example by filing off serial numbers, and there is a black market in which old military models fetch high prices. Parts and whole models have been smuggled out of Fontaine, though there are also legitimate sales abroad.
### LARGE +
Research and development is done mostly at the Fontaine Research Institute, and most manufacturing at the Fortress of Meropide. Beyond patent law, building meka is unrestricted, so many private workshops design their own. According to Aeval, meka proved so useful in building the Poisson Tunnel that a new wave of clockwork technology followed, after which meka were made available to the public, built from cheaper materials and less efficient energy sources than the Institute's models. Some Fontainians worry that progress in artificial intelligence will replace humans and Melusines.
### ULTRA +
Personal ownership shows up among characters: Escoffier uses a custom-made set, the Experimental Multi-Purpose Gastromek Assembly (her floating serving dish and tail-like back prosthesis), for cooking and combat, and Jahoda keeps a stash of meka for fighting practice.

## individuals
### LARGE
Most meka cannot think for themselves. The notable exception is Seymour, a special Recon Log Mek created by Alain himself as proof of his skill in kinematics and material science. Seymour has a complex neural network that allows fast learning and autonomous decisions. Another is "Curve", a humanoid mek built by Mosso Lombroso that came to decide using emotion rather than logic (Questioning Melusine and Answering Machine).

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay interpretation (this project's recommendation, not canon text): ordinary meka are tools. They follow their programming and do not converse, with the rare exceptions above. In Fontaine a mek is a normal sight, a harvester in the field or a patrol unit on a quay, and not a menace. Hostile ones are typically owned or abused by thieves, or are military and security models.
### LARGE +
For a fight, use the Arkhe weakness: an opposite Arkhe strike staggers a mek, and a combat model still manages weak physical attacks. Do not give meka free-roaming sentience or an independent faction.
