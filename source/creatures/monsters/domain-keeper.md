## summary
### LITE
Domain Keepers are elite enemies of the automaton family, shadow constructs that guard the Temple of Space, the Pillar Hall floating above Windrest Peak in Mondstadt. They arrived with the Temple of Space in Version 6.5 (Luna VI). They fight in Geo with materialised swords and can briefly take on the element they are struck with.
### STANDARD +
Their Archive text calls them guardians born from the overflow of a mighty primordial being, entrusted with "eyes" yet able only to extol the ideal forms of an unseen perfect world. Because they cannot grasp the complex phenomenal world they are not counted among the angels.
### LARGE +
Wiki lore credits their creation to Asmoday, who also made the Watchers Nihilita and Melanta and several Segment-Domain Keeper types as lower-ranking guardian units. The Open-Eyed, a boss linked to the Temple, is the best-known fallen watcher but is a different creature.

## nature
### LITE
In the Archive they are shadow constructs "born of a flow" from the primordial being, the Heavenly Principles, and they stand guard over the Pillar Hall of the heavens. They are not living animals or angels.
### STANDARD +
The Archive observes a built-in contradiction: although given eyes as guardians, they can only praise the "Ideal Forms" of the noumenal world and cannot behold the phenomenal one. It leaves open whether this reflects their creator's rational design or her failure to grasp the intent of the being above her. A loading-screen tip notes the guards' differing appearances suggest differing roles.
### LARGE +
The Temple of Space was made by Asmoday, the Ruler of Space (Segment-Domain Tau), to preserve records of fallen civilisations. Within it time stands still and the "Uplifted" people it holds do not age, hunger or die. Keepers belong to this preservation order: they are muscle, not scholars, and serve rules rather than judgement.

## abilities
### LITE
Fights with Geo: hand and wing strikes, plus a sword that appears in its hand for spinning slashes, lunges and thrown blades. Its signature Deluded Eye state monitors Pyro, Hydro, Electro and Cryo hits; after enough hits of one element it transforms into that element with an immutable aura and strong resistance, then ends in a heavy ground slam.
### STANDARD +
Game mechanics (not narrative lore): it tracks all four elements at once, shows the leading one on a Fury bar, and transforms after three hits of the same element. If it does not transform it makes one slash and returns to normal. After four attacks in a transformed state it slams the ground in an area strike of that element and reverts.
### LARGE +
Roleplay reading: the Deluded Eye can be described as a guardian that "records" what strikes it and answers in kind, echoing the recording motif of the Open-Eyed's Supreme Overseer technique, whose recorded element decides its attack.

## relations
### STANDARD
Created by Asmoday and tied to the Watchers Nihilita and Melanta. Nihilita, called the Open-Eyed, opened his eye after Asmoday vanished and fell from the Temple to Windrest Peak, while Melanta stayed in the Cage of Suchness upholding the old rules. Which side individual Keepers favour is not stated; they behave as guardians of the status quo.

## scene_use
### STANDARD
Use Keepers as sentinel enemies in the still, windless halls of the Temple of Space. They do not speak. They respond to elemental provocation by briefly mirroring it. They should not be written as sapient, hostile to the Traveler by malice, or as the same being as the Open-Eyed.

## status
### STANDARD
Introduced in Version 6.5 (Luna VI) together with the Temple of Space, which is entered after the world quest The Wind Has Ceased. It is a permanent enemy in that area. No Domain Keeper appears in the Nod-Krai or Snezhnaya content or in Versions 7.0-7.1 as far as research found.
### ULTRA +
The Temple's three exhibition areas (Luyang Academy, the Mahavaipulya Chamber and the Desert Pavilion) and the Pillar Hall Central Zone are the Temple areas the wiki lists. Ranks named in wiki lore are Domain Keepers and the six Segment-Domain Keeper exploration types (Concealed, Organization, Patrol, Projectile, Suppression, Transport); how the two groups relate in canon beyond sharing a creator is not described.
