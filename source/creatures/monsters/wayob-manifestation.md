## summary
### LITE
Wayob Manifestations are elite enemies in Natlan, the form in which the Wayob revered by the six tribes show themselves on the land. They look like obsidian totems or statues and appear through totems and Nightsouls. There are six types, one for each tribe and element: Flow-Inverted (Hydro), Foliar-Swift (Dendro), Rock-Cavernous (Geo), Biting-Cold (Cryo), Burning-Aflame (Pyro) and Thundering (Electro).
### STANDARD +
The Adventurer Handbook says the Wayob worshipped by Natlan's tribes manifest themselves in these obsidian totems. Natlanese do not know why they appear; the belief is that a Manifestation is sent to solve some problem.

## nature
### LITE
Archive text says the Wayob is a belief that grew from the tribes and obsidian totems once Natlan began to communicate with the realm of the Lord of the Night. A Wayob is a consciousness of collective thought and the home of the ancestors' souls.
### STANDARD +
Wayob have no fixed form and attach themselves to things in order to guide lost tribespeople. Storytellers often cast Manifestations as messengers of trials or guides to the Night Kingdom; a Saurian-Crowned Warrior is said to have fought one in a trance and learnt from it. Each type is a tribe's own Wayob, which the tribe believes controls a related power.
### LARGE +
The Masters of the Night-Wind found that obsidian gave the steadiest link to the Night Kingdom, which may be why Manifestations resemble stone statues. The Flower-Feather Clan say Manifestations once looked rougher, like an obsidian statue, and have changed little since their outline was recorded in woven scrolls. The wiki's Wayob page adds that the Wayob are non-corporeal beings of will without form or thought, that they live in the Night Kingdom, and that they are not physically present in Natlan.

## society
### STANDARD
Each tribe worships its own Wayob. According to their Archive entries: the People of the Springs believe theirs controls tides and waves, flowing and still water; the Scions of the Canopy, growth of fruit and leaf and the veins of leaves; the Children of Echoes, mountains, valleys and echoes of stone; the Masters of the Night-Wind, freezing and crystallisation, fixedness and mutability; the Flower-Feather Clan, fire, heat and blazing winds; the Collective of Plenty, thunder and speed, and abundant harvests.
### LARGE +
Tribal legend of the Collective of Plenty says the Wayob has taken many forms and, in pre-dawn darkness, wrestled the mightiest warriors with neither side winning. The Children of Echoes' text says a Manifestation, revealed through the totems, often has an important purpose.

## abilities
### LITE
Gameplay (not literal lore): a Manifestation periodically raises a ward and a field around itself. The ward blocks all damage, and the field drains Energy from the whole party while the active character stands in it.
### STANDARD +
If the ward is not destroyed before charging finishes, it turns into an elemental shield that must be removed with the right reactions. Destroying the ward earlier yields more elemental particles. Either way, breaking a ward stuns the Manifestation briefly. Each type uses its own element.

## relations
### STANDARD
Manifestations are bound to the Wayob and the tribes, not to the Abyss. Hostile behaviour toward the Traveler is not explained by the Archive texts.
### LARGE +
The wiki's Wayob page, citing the quest Past and Future, says the Abyss's invasion of the Night Kingdom infected the Wayob with Abyssal energy and weakened their link to the people. After the Night Kingdom was cleansed they resumed their duties.

## individuals
### STANDARD
The wiki lists a Weakened Wayob Manifestation among known Wayob individuals; the six types here are otherwise unnamed group forms.
### ULTRA +
The same page lists other named Wayob: Chaac, Angakuk, "The Great Spirit of This Land" (tied to the Children of Echoes) and Maghan's Master (tied to the Masters of the Night-Wind). It also says that after the Night Kingdom was cleansed, the Wayob told Citlali that the first Pyro Archon, Xbalanque, was returning to the human world (quest After the Peace). These details come from the wiki's summary and were not traced to dialogue.

## scene_use
### STANDARD
This project's suggestion (not canon): a Manifestation suits a scene of tribal ritual, trial or guidance at a totem, treated with the respect the tribes show it.
