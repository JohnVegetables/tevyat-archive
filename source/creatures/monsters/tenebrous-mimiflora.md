## summary
### LITE
Tenebrous Mimifloras are plant-like monsters of the Abyss found in Natlan. They enter battle disguised as other creatures, protected by a Void Ward, and fall back to their true form when the ward breaks. Their largest form is the Tenebrous Papilla, a boss at Quahuacan Cliff that mimics other bosses. Their attacks inflict Attrition, a slow loss of health.
### STANDARD +
They first appeared in 5.1 as an elite enemy group, the Tenebrous Mimesis. Archive text says they bear only a surface resemblance to plants, having adopted that form to approach the tree-like world and copy its concepts.

## nature
### LITE
Archive text calls them Abyssal monsters that descended from a twisted, alien world. Where the corrosive waves of the dark tide lap, such plant-like monsters are said to grow.
### STANDARD +
The entry stresses that the plant shape is borrowed: they took it to come closer to the tree-like world, extract the concepts they needed, and analyse and imitate them. Inactive in the wild, a Mimiflora may sit as a closed bud that looks like a closed Embercore Flower.
### LARGE +
The Tenebrous Papilla's Archive text goes further. It describes plant-like Abyssal creatures that burrow deep into the earth, extract memories, and distort them into more abstruse and sordid forms. By doing so they inch toward the Irminsul's core and threaten its existence. It adds a hypothesis that they corrupt the many colours of light, leaving only black information, which fits the branches the Papilla thrusts out as if to pierce the void.
### ULTRA +
A Handbook line for the Papilla calls it a pitch-black monster that sinks roots of corruption into the home of souls and memory, with no one knowing what it gains or how its chaotic nature will show.

## abilities
### LITE
Mimicry: on entering battle it may take the shape of another enemy, and in that form it cannot be damaged until its Void Ward is destroyed. Its true form fires spikes and homing spheres.
### STANDARD +
Gameplay (not literal lore): the Void Ward wears down with each elemental hit, and faster under Nightsoul-aligned attacks. Once it is gone the Mimiflora returns to its true form, is briefly stunned, and later transforms again with a weaker ward. Fifteen variants copy creatures such as Saurian hatchlings and warriors, a Geovishap Hatchling, an Anemo Hilichurl Rogue, a Crackling Axe Mitachurl, a Ruin Guard and a Secret Source Automaton. A Mimiflora whose name is plain "Tenebrous Mimiflora" does not transform. Its attacks apply Attrition stacks: each stack removes a little health after a delay.
### LARGE +
The Papilla has three sets of forms and copies the shapes and attacks of ordinary bosses (for example the Aeonblight Drake, Regisvines, Maguu Kenki, Primo Geovishap and Hypostases) without their special mechanics. When beaten it returns as a different form. At intervals it retreats to the arena centre, raises a large Void Ward, and charges a huge explosion that inflicts heavy Attrition if the ward is not destroyed in time. In Stygian Onslaught the ward is stronger and the unbroken charge is lethal to the active character.

## society
### STANDARD
Nothing in the texts shows Mimifloras acting as a group or serving a master. They are described as individual predators of memory and concepts.

## relations
### STANDARD
They belong to the Abyss and so oppose everything of Teyvat. Their copying of Saurians, hilichurls and automata shows no loyalty to what they imitate.
### LARGE +
The Papilla is placed at Quahuacan Cliff in Natlan. Its text ties it to the Irminsul rather than to Natlan's tribes or the Lord of the Night.

## individuals
### STANDARD
The Tenebrous Papilla ("Void Fantasy Plant"), added in 5.2, is the sole named individual: a normal boss at Quahuacan Cliff with three mimic types.

## scene_use
### STANDARD
This project's suggestion (not canon): a Mimiflora is suited to uncanny scenes in which something familiar behaves slightly wrongly, with plant-like details visible through its disguise.

### STANDARD
A boss form of the Tenebrous Mimiflora, found at Quahuacan Cliff in Natlan and added in 5.2. It mimics other normal bosses in three possible sets of forms, summons a Void Ward and charges a large explosion that inflicts Attrition. Archive text says it infiltrates deep into the earth to extract memories and moves toward the Irminsul's core.
