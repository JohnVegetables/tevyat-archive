## summary
### LITE
The Wild Hunt is an Abyssal phenomenon, and the horde of monsters it produces, in Nod-Krai. It taints the dead with Abyssal energy and turns them into ghouls that wander the frozen wilderness, usually when dark mist rolls in. Its source is Rerir, one of the Five Sinners of Khaenri'ah, whose body was torn into fragments scattered across the land. It attacked Nod-Krai for years looking for the pieces of him and the power of the moons.
### STANDARD +
Most people do not know where it comes from, so folk tales fill the gap. After Rerir was expelled from Teyvat in Version 6.1, the Abyss put an imitation of Roland at its head, and when that imitation expired in 6.4 the Hunt was left leaderless and driven only by an urge to destroy.

## nature
### LITE
Not a species and not a single creature. It is a distributed Abyssal phenomenon: wherever a fragment of Rerir lies, monsters gather. The ghouls themselves are described on the wiki as former humans cursed by the Abyss and doomed to wander the frozen wastes.
### STANDARD +
Archive texts describe the ghouls as spirits that could not rest, from resentment, partings or unfulfilled wishes, which the Abyss corrupted into creatures lost in the Hunt's thick mist. The Wilderness Hunter's entry adds that buried champions of a lost ancient kingdom, awakened by Abyssal taint, now treat every trespasser as prey. Their emergence is said to presage worse calamities.
### LARGE +
Albedo concluded in Echoes of Fate that the Hunt in itself has nothing like a human personality, but that, taken as a collective, the medium through which it is summoned appears to have a soul. Flins says there that the Hunt proliferates wherever Rerir's scattered fragments lie.
### ULTRA +
The word "hunt" echoes a European folklore motif of a spectral chase led by a mythic figure, an omen of death. In-world tales are similar and unreliable: the Undying One's Mourning Bell text offers a demonic host from another world harvesting souls, or phantoms of an ancient nightmare. Treat these as rumour.

## origin
### STANDARD
Rerir was an intelligence officer of Khaenri'ah who, in despair over losing Tholindis, took Abyssal power with the other Sinners and entered a moon gate to find her; he had proposed to her but she had not yet accepted. The attempt tore his body into countless pieces scattered over Nod-Krai (All Is Foretold). The fragments became the monsters of the Wild Hunt. Nod-Krai was the first land struck by the cataclysm after Khaenri'ah, which is why the Hunt there is unusually strong.
### LARGE +
Rerir survived because of the transcendent Abyssal power he had taken, but was trapped in the moon's reflection. Through means canon does not explain, Dottore brought him back from there and gathered large numbers of his fragments, timing the revival for when Columbina was weakest. Rerir wanted his body back from the fragments and his strength back from the fragments of the Three Moons and the Moon Marrow, which is what drove the Hunt's attacks.
### ULTRA +
The Lightkeeper Flins once cleared a Wild Hunt anomaly and kept Rerir's heart out of curiosity, not knowing what it was. More fragments fused to it, and Rerir later attacked Flins repeatedly to retrieve it, growing stronger as Flins was weakened by Abyssal corrosion. The detailed story of Rerir belongs in his own entry.

## ghoul_types
### STANDARD
Two enemy forms are listed as the Wasteland Wild Hunt (also called Wilderness Ghouls), both elite enemies of the Abyss family with Physical attacks:
- Wilderness Hunter: the champion-like ghoul, tied in Archive text to buried warriors of a lost northern kingdom.
- Wilderness Exile: the restless-spirit ghoul, tied in Archive text to unfulfilled wishes and unresolved grief.
### LARGE +
Gameplay (not literal lore): they move slowly but burst forward or teleport to their target. At 0 HP a ghoul is not killed but paralysed in a Grief-Stricken state, and damage then lowers its maximum HP until it perishes. Moonsign: Ascendant Gleam skills can start that state immediately. Both types were added in 6.0 (Luna I).

## history
### STANDARD
Illuga says the Lightkeepers paid in blood, three years before the events of When War Songs Rise (6.3), for the Kipumaki Cliff Hunt behaving differently from elsewhere. In the Song of the Welkin Moon Archon Quest, Flins fights it, Albedo studies it, and Rerir's role is revealed (6.1); Columbina then seals Rerir away beyond the sky while Dainsleif holds the gate open.
### LARGE +
Rerir took the form of the Traveler's Sibling and later of Sousi and Flins to pursue the Moon Marrow and then empower himself with Aino's cannon. Those events are Rerir's story; for the Hunt they mean that after his expulsion the monsters had no master.
### ULTRA +
The Abyss then produced an imitation of Roland to lead the Hunt, which also infested Andrius' remains and twisted the wolf god's thoughts. In To Those Who Embark on the Expedition (6.4) the Hunt invades Mondstadt and Wolvendom, where Lohen tests the Traveler about letters to be sure the Hunt has not copied their forms. Varka seemingly dies to Roland in a duel in order to reach the wolf. When the imitation expired, the Hunt was left leaderless. Stragglers still roam, as Varka's knights note, aimlessly and bent on destruction.

## distribution
### STANDARD
Primarily Nod-Krai. The Lightkeepers also count the Abyssal phenomena at Kipumaki Cliff and elsewhere in the north as Wild Hunt, though that one behaves differently and is attributed to Rotwang and his disciples rather than Rerir. The Hunt rarely approaches Hiisi Island because the moon's power deters it.
### LARGE +
Outside Nod-Krai, Wild Hunt monsters attack Mondstadt and Wolvendom during the 6.4 story. As of the sources read, no Wild Hunt content was added in 7.0-7.1.

## abilities
### STANDARD
Ghouls fight physically and hit hard in short bursts. The Hunt's wider power is its numbers, its ability to spread through the whole land from scattered fragments, and its capacity for mind-corrupting anomalies that the Lightkeepers have to clear. The Hunt can be led (Rerir, then the Roland imitation) and was able to attack Mondstadt.
### LARGE +
Rerir himself shapeshifted into others' forms, and Lohen's letter test shows the Knights feared the Hunt could imitate people. The ghouls themselves are not shown doing so.

## relations
### STANDARD
Ranked as part of the Abyss. Master: Rerir, then the Roland imitation. Enemies: the Lightkeepers, the Knights of Favonius' expedition, and the Fatui, who hold the Moon Marrow Rerir wants. Dottore is the reason Rerir returned in the first place.
### LARGE +
Its drive to find the moons' power makes the Hunt a threat to places holding kuuvahki and the Moon Marrow. The moon's power deters it on Hiisi Island.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay interpretation (this project's recommendation, not canon text): ghouls are mindless and wordless; do not give them speech, plans or names. A scene works through omens: pitch-dark mist, a bell-like silence, then ghouls. Keep the larger tragedy, Rerir's love and loss, for scenes where it has been revealed.
### LARGE +
Because the Hunt is distributed, destroying a group does not end it while fragments remain. After Rerir's expulsion, treat it as an aimless remnant, not a coordinated army.

## entry: Rerir
@keys: Rerir, Rächer of Solnari, Master of the Wild Hunt
@version: 6.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Rerir, one of the Five Sinners of Khaenri'ah, was a normal intelligence officer who took Abyssal power after losing Tholindis, who entered a moon gate. His torn body became the Wild Hunt. After Dottore revived him he hunted for the Moon Marrow and the Three Moons' power, and he was expelled from Teyvat in Echoes of Fate. He later appears, at peace, in the moon's reflection (The First Sliver of Moonlight).

## entry: Wilderness Ghouls
@keys: Wilderness Ghoul, Wilderness Ghouls, Wilderness Hunter, Wilderness Exile, Grief-Stricken
@version: 6.0
### STANDARD
Wasteland Wild Hunt ghouls stalk the frozen wilderness when pitch-dark mist rolls in. The Hunter is bound to the champions of a lost kingdom; the Exile to restless spirits of grief. In the game they do not die at 0 HP but become Grief-Stricken and paralysed, a mechanic reflecting their sorrowful nature.

## entry: Roland Imitation
@keys: Roland, imitation of Roland
@keys2: Wild Hunt, Andrius, Varka
@version: 6.4
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
After Rerir was expelled, the Abyss produced an imitation of Roland to lead the Wild Hunt in Nod-Krai and Mondstadt. It also infested the remains of the wolf god Andrius and left his thoughts twisted. It duelled Varka, who seemed to die to reach the wolf, and expired in To Those Who Embark on the Expedition, leaving the Hunt leaderless.
