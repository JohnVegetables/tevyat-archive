## summary
### LITE
"Automatons" is the game's name for mechanical enemies. They are not one race or one invention: the family groups machines built by different, often long-dead makers. The biggest group is the Ruin Machines of Khaenri'ah (which have their own entry). The rest are Primal Constructs from King Deshret's desert, Clockwork Meka built in Fontaine, Secret Source Automatons found in Natlan and on the Frost Moon, and the Domain Keepers of the Temple of Space. Most fight with physical force and have no will of their own.
### STANDARD +
Two odd cases sit beside the groups: the Maguu Kenki, a masterless mechanical swordsman, and Knuckle Duckle, a duck-shaped home robot built by Aino. Nothing new was added in 7.0 or 7.1.

## nature
### LITE
Machines, not living creatures. Most still active are leftovers of lost civilisations with no master left; dormant ones wake when someone comes near.
### STANDARD +
The groups differ in how they work: Ruin Machines are mostly Physical fighters; Primal Constructs also use Physical attacks but can turn invisible; Clockwork Meka are imbued with Arkhe energy; Secret Source Automatons respond to Nightsoul; Domain Keepers watch the elements. The Adventurer Handbook says Clockwork Meka were also "an excellent assistant to thieves and brigands".
### LARGE +
The Primal Constructs and the Secret Source machines share a quiet theme in their Archive text: machines designed for something else (a paradise, collecting samples, running cities) that outlived the purpose and now only defend what is left. The Fontaine Meka are the reverse: they are recent, built for work, and then adapted for security and violence.

## groups
### LITE
- Ruin Machines (1.0): Khaenri'ah's, own entry.
- Primal Constructs (3.1): Deshret's desert guardians.
- Clockwork Meka (4.0): Fontaine's, charged with Arkhe.
- Secret Source Automatons (5.0): Natlan's ancient machines, plus a Frost Moon variant (6.7).
- Domain Keepers (6.5): Temple of Space guardians.
### STANDARD +
Two named machines have their own place: the Maguu Kenki (1.6, Golden Apple Archipelago and Inazuma) and Knuckle Duckle (6.0, Nod-Krai). Both are covered in sub-entries.

## abilities
### LITE
Mostly Physical damage and a weak point that stuns them when hit; several groups have a trick such as invisibility or retaliation.
### STANDARD +
Gameplay (not literal lore): Primal Constructs fire parts and turn invisible, which can be broken with Electro attacks or by triggering Quicken, Frozen or Petrification. Clockwork Meka are charged with Pneuma (bright white clockwork) or Ousia (dark blue); striking them with the opposite Arkhe overloads them. Secret Source Automatons prepare a stronger counter when a character uses Nightsoul's Blessing against them. A Domain Keeper switches into an elemental form after enough hits of one element.
### LARGE +
Unlike most automatons, the Maguu Kenki drops elemental gems matching its elements. Wiki trivia says Clockwork Meka are the first Automaton enemy group made up of Common Enemies.

## society
### STANDARD
Automatons have no society: they have routes, instructions and defence duties. The Overseer Device and the Matrix of Overseer Network were built to command or oversee other machines, not to rule people. The Algorithm of Semi-Intransient Matrix of Overseer Network governs the many constructs of the desert, and its Archive text says the lonely ruler's will still seems to dwell in it.

## relations
### STANDARD
Automatons relate to people through whoever built or now uses them. Fontaine's Maison Gardiennage uses humanoid meka for municipal security, and the Fontaine Research Institute of Kinetic Energy Engineering is the main body behind meka research. The Ruin Machines are tied to the Abyss Order and the Fatui by their own entry.
### LARGE +
Fontaine's Research Institute, Alain Guillotin's meka and the Institute's Icewind Suite are all part of Fontaine's clockwork meka work. The Natlan and Frost Moon machines belong to the ruins of earlier civilisations, while in Nod-Krai, Aino built Knuckle Duckle with kuuvahki technology.

## individuals
### STANDARD
Named machines: the Algorithm of Semi-Intransient Matrix of Overseer Network (Primal Constructs, 3.1), Icewind Suite (Coppelia and Coppelius, 4.0), Prototype Cal. Breguet (4.1), the Secret Source Automaton Hunter-Seeker (5.0), Configuration Device (5.1) and Overseer Device (5.6), the Precursor's Secret Source Matrix of Overseer Network and its Local Legend Mushteshir (6.7), the Maguu Kenki and Knuckle Duckle.

## scene_use
### STANDARD
This project's suggestion (not canon): present Automatons as relics that obey old instructions rather than monsters with motives. Pick the group by setting: the desert for dream-haunted guardians, Fontaine for tools turned to security, Natlan for forgotten work machines.

## entry: Primal Constructs
@keys: Primal Construct, Primal Constructs
@keys2: Great Red Sand, desert, Deshret
@version: 3.1
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Primal Constructs are elite automatons found mainly across the Great Red Sand, leftover technology of King Deshret's civilisation. Their three Archive entries (Repulsor, Reshaper, Prospector) all open with the same line: the guardians of the lost desert ruins have power enough to punish any who disturb the pure dreams of their lord. They add that the machines were not first built as guards but to rebirth the paradise that the "foolish desert ruler" dreamed of, that the lord of the blazing sun promised his people a utopia on the other side of existence that turned out to be a mirage, and that the master of the sands studied taboo knowledge to create a dream paradise, with these machines possibly being the fruits of it. Today only mantras inscribed on the machines preserve the dreams and oaths.
### LARGE +
Gameplay (not lore): a Primal Construct fires parts on to the field and turns invisible; destroying the parts forces it to repair them, and Electro hits then paralyze it. Like Ruin Machines, some stand dormant; Elemental Sight shows which are real enemies.
### ULTRA +
The Algorithm of Semi-Intransient Matrix of Overseer Network (3.1) is a perpetual-motion machine that governs the many constructs in the desert and still seems to carry its lonely ruler's will. Its Archive text says the king may have built these devices after a friend's teaching in a dream or from covetousness of the holy ones in the skies, before founding his realm, and that the crystals of the City of the Pillars still reflect the glory of the golden paradise. Players abbreviate its name as ASIMON; the abbreviation appears officially only in a Spanish achievement name.

## entry: Clockwork Meka
@keys: Clockwork Meka, Mek, Meka, clockwork meka
@keys2: Fontaine, Arkhe, Ousia, Pneuma
@version: 4.0
@spoiler: none
### STANDARD
Clockwork Meka are enemy machines made by Fontaine to serve as laborers and later modified for combat. They are charged with Arkhe, either Pneuma (bright white clockwork) or Ousia (dark blue clockwork), which lets them deal elemental damage. Hitting a meka with the opposite energy overloads it, so that it temporarily loses its Arkhe and can only attack physically or not at all. Common Meka (Normal and Specialist Meks) and bosses form the group; a Normal Mek takes one opposite-Arkhe hit to weaken, a Specialist Mek two, a boss three.
### LARGE +
Archive text on the Annihilation Specialist Mek says the Fontaine Research Institute of Kinetic Energy Engineering is the main body of meka design, but independent engineers and workshops also build them. The first machine of the Annihilation type was designed by an independent engineer to commemorate their lover and sponsor, and was eventually acquired by the Maison Gardiennage as a tool of municipal security. Wiki trivia says the first meka made by Alain Guillotin were the Recon Log Mek and the Assault Specialist Mek, associated with his sister Mary-Ann Guillotin and his late friend Carter Scherbius respectively (the Archive itself says only that the Assault Specialist was named for a friend of its inventor). Some citizens call this use of meka "state apparatus enforcing the monopoly on violence".
### ULTRA +
Icewind Suite (4.0) is a ballet of two "Artificed Dancers", Coppelia and Coppelius, developed by the Institute; the fight comes in an Ousia version ("Dirge of Coppelia") and a Pneuma version ("Nemesis of Coppelius"), chosen in dialogue with Maillardet. Bossuet intended them as art that transcended mechanical reproduction; after he was forced to resign, Maillardet took over the project, and they strayed from the original vision. Prototype Cal. Breguet (4.1) is a device by the late Edwin Eastinghouse that counteracts gravity, intended as the first step in making Fontaine a floating city that would never again fear flood; its Arkhium core "splits" matter, and the Institute is interested only in its physical effects. It ran out of control after an accident.

## entry: Secret Source Automatons
@keys: Secret Source Automaton, Secret Source Automatons, Hunter-Seeker, Configuration Device, Overseer Device
@keys2: Natlan, Nightsoul, Secret Source
@version: 5.0
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Secret Source Automatons are mainly found in Natlan and appear idle until disturbed. Archive text says they were created by a long-lost civilisation to carry out work: the Hunter-Seeker collected samples and had only basic combat functions; the Configuration Device was a manufacturing machine, shaped like a giant claw, that could transform to build colossal cities even inside a volcano; the Overseer Device was a domination mechanism by which a mighty ancient race oversaw the running of its cities through "phlogiston-engineered intelligence", and it keeps only basic commands since that civilisation fell in wars with an emerging race. Rumour says they were not built by humans.
### LARGE +
Gameplay (not lore): the Secret Source machines react to Nightsoul's Blessing, preparing stronger attacks. The Hunter-Seeker is Pyro; the Configuration Device (Toyac Springs, Electro) charges a Boltsphere Cannon, with Accretion Mechanisms set atop Solid Phlogiston pillars; the Overseer Device (Atocpan, Hydro) builds Flow Momentum in Sweeper Mode.
### ULTRA +
The wiki's overview attributes the Natlan machines to the dragon civilisation; the game text does not name a maker.

## entry: Precursor's Secret Source Matrix of Overseer Network
@keys: Precursor's Secret Source Matrix, Mushteshir, Matrix of Overseer Network
@keys2: Frost Moon, Archaeolune, Nod-Krai
@version: 6.7
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
A variant of the Secret Source machines found on the Frost Moon, which uses the moon's Archaeolune Energy to gather Hydro. Its Archive text calls it an ancient machine trapped in lunar ruins that has lost the purpose its makers gave it; it now attacks anything entering its ground. Records of a lost civilisation call these machines "Ur-Namrasit-Tilimtu-Babbar", "the one who brings light to the darkness"; the "pioneers" engraved their most earnest words in golden sigils, hoping their successors would keep the light of the universe shining, but the machine named "overseer" never found the one it should have served.
### LARGE +
A Local Legend version, "Mushteshir - The Pioneer That Gazes at the Stars", stands at the Kalag Guarding Grounds in Ungien's Circle, Lunar Highlands (Nod-Krai). It gathers Hydro through a Convergence Field; removing a Frozen state from it with Shatter, Melt or similar reactions interrupts the field.

## entry: Domain Keepers
@keys: Domain Keeper, Domain Keepers, Segment-Domain Keeper
@keys2: Temple of Space, Asmoday, Pillar Hall
@version: 6.5
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Domain Keepers are elite automatons that guard the Temple of Space (Segment-Domain Tau) in the sky above Windrest Peak. Their Archive text calls them shadow constructs born from the overflow of a mighty primordial being, guardians of the Pillar Hall of the heavens. Entrusted with "eyes" but able only to extol the "Ideal Forms" of the noumenal world, they cannot see the phenomenal world's complexity and so are not counted among the angels. The text wonders if this contradiction was a design of their creator or a failure of their master to grasp the primordial being's intent. The Adventurer Handbook says the guards' differing appearances suggest different roles. The wiki credits their making to Asmoday, the temple's former owner.
### LARGE +
Gameplay (not lore): a Domain Keeper enters a Deluded Eye state, counts Pyro, Hydro, Electro and Cryo hits, and after three of one element transforms into the matching elemental form with raised resistance to it. Other Segment-Domain Keeper types in the Temple of Space can be manipulated or destroyed with the juvenile Watcher Dex during exploration.

## entry: Maguu Kenki
@keys: Maguu Kenki
@keys2: mechanical swordsman, Tengu Sweeper, Inazuma
@version: 1.6
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
The Maguu Kenki is an autonomous humanoid swordsman of unknown make. Archive text says it was built from the memories of the first-generation master of a sword school (the Tengu Sweeper technique is mentioned), but malfunctioned, lost control and was discarded. It cannot or will not speak and seeks only to communicate with crossed swords; its vicious mask is said to be inspired by a famous oni. In Inazuma it stands at Serpent's Head on Yashiori Island. It first appeared in the limited-time Golden Apple Archipelago event (1.6) on Twinning Isle, where wiki trivia says Ako Domeki's crew had taken it and were shipwrecked 500 years ago; this trivia is not tied to a direct statement in the Archive.

## entry: Knuckle Duckle
@keys: Knuckle Duckle
@keys2: Aino, Krumkake, kuuvahki, Nod-Krai
@version: 6.0
@spoiler: none
### STANDARD
A duck-shaped kuuvahki mechanism made of scrap metal and mechanical bearings, and a boss. Its Archive text says it is as strong and tough as the Fatui's newest weapons, yet is simply a toy duck that can punch very hard, shaped by its creator's tastes. In its own dialogue it calls itself a multipurpose armed robot for domestic use created by Master Aino under the code name "Knuckle Duckle", with security, surveillance, storage, dusting, cleaning and krumkake baking functions. It guards the Workshop's Trove used by Aino and Ineffa.
