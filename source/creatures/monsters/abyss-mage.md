## summary
### LITE
Abyss Mages are the Abyss Order's everyday spellcasters: hooded, masked creatures who command one of four elements (Pyro, Hydro, Cryo, Electro) and shield themselves behind a barrier of that element. Adventurers say they sit at the lowest rung of the Order's ranks. They are usually found near hilichurls, whom they can talk to and manipulate, or with other mages. They are far weaker than Abyss Heralds and Lectors, but still dangerous.
### STANDARD +
They first appear in 1.0 and are among the first agents of the Abyss the Traveler meets, in Mondstadt, where a Cryo Abyss Mage ambushes the Traveler and a Hydro Abyss Mage is later tracked to the winery.

## nature
### LITE
Archive text calls them "Abyss creatures" that can command the elements to a limited extent. The Adventurer Handbook says that, for unknown reasons, they can speak with hilichurls and so bend their minds easily, and that the language they chant is long forgotten and the source of their magic.
### STANDARD +
Each mage is bound to one element. Pyro, Cryo and Hydro mages date from 1.0; the Electro mage was added in 2.0. In appearance the four are alike (robed, masked, floating casters), except that the Hydro mage wears a white mask and has no element emblem on its clothing, while the others wear a black mask and their emblem.
### LARGE +
Archive notes add small colour: the Cryo mage can condense water vapour into icicles but its body is frail once its barrier breaks; the Electro mage's entry says the Akademiya researcher Alva Nikola once studied how it manipulates Electro and sketched a theoretical weapon, the Nikola Coil, never built (some say his notes were eaten in a fungal infestation). The Hydro mage's entry notes that adventurers mock mages as being able to do little more than give someone a cold or rheumatism, then warns that their power is not to be underestimated.

## abilities
### LITE
Mages fire elemental bolts and cast area spells from range. Their key defence is an elemental shield, and breaking it leaves the mage stunned and exposed.
### STANDARD +
Mages are often seen performing a ritual or patrolling a set area. A mage in the middle of a ritual has no shield but raises one quickly if it survives the first blow; patrolling mages always have theirs up. When the shield is destroyed the mage is stunned for a long time and takes full damage; if it is not finished off, it tries to regenerate the shield.
### LARGE +
Gameplay (not literal lore): the in-game hints suggest breaking each shield with an element that reacts with it, for example Electro against a Pyro shield, Pyro against a Cryo shield and Cryo against a Hydro shield.
### ULTRA +
Mages are usually found with hilichurls or fellow mages, which fits the Archive claim that they can command hilichurls. They were used in the very first days of the story: a Cryo mage controlled Dvalin and reported to the Traveler's Sibling, and a Hydro mage behind the ambush in the Prologue was defeated by Diluc.

## society
### STANDARD
Mages are said to be the lowest-ranking Abyss creatures, wandering the lands to scheme small acts of destruction, a rumour reported by adventurers in the Hydro mage's entry. Many are seen in the open, unlike the more elusive Heralds and Lectors.
### LARGE +
Not every mage obeys a distant superior. Individual mages in side stories act on their own whims: a Cryo mage refused to return a book, a Pyro mage took a bomb to use against Mondstadt and mishandled it, and a Pyro mage tried to attack a sick Jean. Mages that appear in quests often bear random names such as Adalbert, Konrad or Thyla (assigned by the game rather than established characters).

## relations
### LITE
Mages serve the Abyss Order and, through it, the Order's leader, the Traveler's Sibling. They command hilichurls; Dainsleif says many hilichurls are cursed Khaenri'ahns whom the Abyss Order conscripts.
### STANDARD +
The Order's more dangerous sentient creatures include Abyss Heralds and Lectors. Diluc and Jean treat Abyss Mages as a lead to the Order in the Prologue (Abyss Mage quest): Diluc, with his sources, traces one to the Dawn Winery, and Venti notes that a certain energy dispersed from the fallen mage's body, which he reads as having been used to cut his connection to Dvalin.
### LARGE +
Octavia, a character from the Sea of Stars, dislikes Abyss Mages after she mistook one for an animal, tried to feed it and was attacked by a Cryo and a Pyro mage; costume sets based on mages were later made to play antagonists in Teyvat-themed attractions on her trip. This is minor trivia from cosmetic set text.

## individuals
### STANDARD
Named mages are minor. Cryochanter Abyss Mage Emery is the main antagonist of the Troublesome Work world quest and refuses to return a book before Lisa electrocutes him. Hydrochanter Abyss Mage Landrich is lit on fire by Diluc when interrogated about a threatening note to the Knights (Darknight Hero's Alibi). "Mr. Fluffball", a Pyro mage nicknamed by Klee, takes one of her bombs in True Treasure and accidentally kills itself with it.
### LARGE +
In the Prologue the mages behind the ambush are unnamed. A Cryo Abyss Mage appears in the story teaser "For a Tomorrow Without Tears" controlling Dvalin and reporting to the Traveler's Sibling.

## scene_use
### STANDARD
This project's suggestion for scenes (not canon): use a mage as an early or low-stakes Abyss threat near a hilichurl camp, or as a ritual interrupter. Keep them small and bound to one element; for real menace, use Heralds and Lectors.
