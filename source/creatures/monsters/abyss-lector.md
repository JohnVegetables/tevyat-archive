## summary
### LITE
Abyss Lectors are the ranged elites of the Abyss Order, spellcasters who chant over books and verses and fight with catalysts. They are the counterpart of the melee Abyss Heralds. There are two kinds: Violet Lightning (Electro, debuted 1.5) and Fathomless Flames (Pyro, added 2.4). The best-known individual is Enjou, a Pyro Lector who posed as a human clerk in Enkanomiya and kept turning up afterwards, as Sanka and under other names, in Natlan.
### STANDARD +
Their Archive entries say that the surface people slightly misread their name: "Lector" can also mean an exegete, someone who interprets scripture. Several Lectors are written as scholars of the Abyss rather than as soldiers.

## nature
### LITE
Archive text calls the Fathomless Flames Lector a wielder of abyssal fire who pursues the meaning behind texts and scriptures, and says that reading "the word of grace" grants them great power. Violet Lightning Lectors are called the evangelists and scholars of the Abyss, who sing the praises of darkness and strike the hearts of unbelievers with violent violet lightning.
### STANDARD +
A Lector is a sentient Abyss creature, not a beast. Some are capable of subtle behaviour. Enjou disguised himself as a human clerk for the entirety of a long trial and was friendly to the Traveler. Comonqui, a Lector in Natlan, was a "spectator" who came to watch the destructive power of the Abyss.
### LARGE +
Lectors are not all recent. The wiki notes that Burkina fought an Electro Lector during the Cataclysm (A Hero's Rite), which would put the type before the Order that now claims them. Enjou is said to have visited or witnessed many ancient civilisations, including Gurabad, Sal Vindagnyr and Enkanomiya, over a very long life.

## abilities
### LITE
Ranged elemental attacks with a catalyst, shielding themselves when wounded.
### STANDARD +
Tutorial text: the Fathomless Flames Lector sings of the warmth of the dark flame, and some attacks leave a brand of abyssal flame on a character that explodes after a short time and hurts the whole party. It defends itself with a shield of abyssal fire, which becomes easier to wear down if its Darkfire Furnace is destroyed. The Violet Lightning Lector calls thunder in praise of the darkness and can disrupt elemental power: some attacks drain Elemental Energy from characters they hit.
### LARGE +
Gameplay (not literal lore): as with Heralds, a Lector falls as soon as its low-HP shield is broken, which is easiest with the reacting element. Fathomless Flames Lectors in the overworld appear only in one-time encounters (Egill in a quest, one in the hidden exploration "Marana's Last Struggle" and one in Erinnyes Forest).

## society
### STANDARD
Lectors work in the Abyss Order's service, usually at a distance from the front line. Enjou is, in his words, a "lowly clerk" who studies ancient lore; he is aware of the Order's plans but not part of the inner circle, and he avoids fights when he can.
### LARGE +
Lectors serve the Order as scholars and ritualists as well as fighters. A Violet Lightning Lector in Requiem of the Echoing Depths manages a ritual meant to purge the curse from Khaenri'ahns that only causes them agony; Haden, an Iniquitous Baptist, led the Order's invasion of Natlan, a feint that let the Traveler's Sibling meet the Lord of the Night.

## relations
### LITE
Lectors serve the Abyss Order and its leader, the Traveler's Sibling. They are opposed by the Traveler, Dainsleif, and the people whose lands they invade.
### STANDARD +
Enjou has the most personal relationships: he is amicable toward the Traveler despite trying to kill them once to check whether they hold Before Sun and Moon, he negotiates with the vishaps, and in Natlan makes a deal with Kinich and Ajaw. Before leaving the Traveler, he remarks that Paimon could betray them.
### LARGE +
Lectors are paired with Heralds in the wiki's Order listing: the melee Heralds are their counterpart.

## individuals
### STANDARD
Enjou (Fathomless Flames) is the principal named Lector; see his entry. Egill ("One Who Calls The Living", Pyro) and Agnarr ("One Who Mourns the Dead", Electro) are twin bosses in the Perils in the Dark quest, after the dark fog is dispersed. Comonqui ("a burning flame" in Nahuatl, Pyro) is a boss in the Echoes of Life Archon Quest, defeated by Kachina and Mualani. The unnamed "Baptist of the Ruined Kingdom" (Electro) manages the failed purge ritual and is defeated by Halfdan, Dainsleif and the Traveler.
### LARGE +
Burkina's Electro Lector from the Cataclysm is mentioned in A Hero's Rite. Lectors seen in the overworld at the Marana's Last Struggle objective and in Erinnyes Forest are unnamed.
### ULTRA +
Enjou's aliases follow his travels: Enjou in Enkanomiya, Sanka ("beneath the mountain") with two Natlan children whom he questioned about the Turnfire, Kokuchuu in the Vision of Ashen Desolation, and Hayne when posing as "Tenoyollotzin". In The Last Survivor of Tenochtzitoc (5.8) he and the Traveler defeat the Bakunawa together inside a miniature Night Kingdom; he gives the Traveler the Lavawalker's Resolution, says they will likely not meet again, and goes on deeper in search of his homeland in the smouldering Mare Jivari. In the Natlan deal he agrees to summon a Beastly Rift for Kinich in return for uninterrupted research, while Ajaw asks him to claim Kinich's body; the wiki reads this as a trap Kinich and Ajaw set to bring Enjou to the Traveler.

## scene_use
### STANDARD
This project's suggestion (not canon): a Lector suits a scene about forbidden texts, ritual and temptation. Enjou is the exception that shows the range: soft-spoken, evasive and curious rather than cruel.

## entry: Enjou
@keys: Enjou, Sanka, Kokuchuu, Hayne
@keys2: Abyss Lector, Lavawalker, Enkanomiya, Natlan
@version: 2.4
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Enjou is the Pyro Abyss Lector the Traveler meets in Enkanomiya as a helpful human translator and clerk. After the Traveler wins the trial, he reveals himself, fights them, and explains that he sought Before Sun and Moon to prove that gods and Celestia came from beyond Teyvat. He then spares them, having grown fond of them. In Three Realms Gateway Offering (2.5, limited-time event) he restores the Towers of the Void and strikes a deal with vishaps. In Natlan he appears as Sanka, Kokuchuu and Hayne, bargaining with Kinich and Ajaw for time to do "historical research" and finally setting out deeper toward the burning Mare Jivari in search of his homeland; the wiki ties the Lavawalker title to him. He is described more fully in his own entry.
### LARGE +
Because the Mare Jivari is displaced from the normal flow of time, it is unclear whether Enjou was the historical Lavawalker or became him afterwards.
