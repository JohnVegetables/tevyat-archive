## summary
### LITE
Bathysmal Vishaps, called the Dragonheirs of the Depths by the people of Enkanomiya, are a race of vishaps that live in the deep sea. They are aggressive toward humans, refuse man-made light, and adapt so quickly that individuals can carry Hydro, Cryo or Electro. They terrorised Enkanomiya (Byakuyakoku) for thousands of years until the Watatsumi Omikami suppressed them under the sea with jade coral. In recent years a few have appeared in Fontaine.
### STANDARD +
Their best-known members are the Coral Defenders: a Cryo Rimebiter and an Electro Bolteater, bred and modified in Enkanomiya and grafted with fragments of Orobashi's coral.
### LARGE +
Official texts describe them as pure, primordial elemental creatures that never bent the knee to any god. Their evolution has also cost them a future: the Archive says the Dragon of Water will not be born from their ranks.

## nature
### LITE
Vishaps of the Light Realm, living in the deep sea and in the caverns of Enkanomiya. The Adventurer Handbook calls them a mighty race that hates light not from weak eyesight, but because the sunlight and the surface world were taken from them, so they refuse man-made light.
### STANDARD +
They are intelligent and unusually adaptable. They can choose which inherited traits to pass to their offspring and can even create new traits, including elemental affinities, so adaptation can happen within a single generation. The Byakuyakoku Collection gives an example: a heat-resistant vishap that matures in a cold place will have offspring with thick body fat, a tendency to sleep, and possibly a Cryo affinity. Before maturing, an individual can also choose which of its stored traits to activate, for instance heat resistance in a hot place or cold resistance in a cold one. One researcher remarked that they had already stored an armoury of abilities in their bodies before meeting the people of Enkanomiya.
### LARGE +
They have their own means of communicating, and they learn human language astonishingly well: within four generations their command can match that of a twelve-year-old student. The Vishap Research Lab stopped its experiments out of fear of this potential. The item text for Dragonheir's False Fin adds that mature individuals lose their eyesight, attributed there to their great hatred of humanity, and that they developed elemental polymorphism for combat; the fin itself has no function.

Like all vishaps they are elemental beings of the Light Realm, so they are fundamentally at odds with the Human Realm (where gods and mortals live) and with the Void Realm.
### ULTRA +
Sources frame them differently. The Byakuyakoku Collection says they follow their Dragon Sovereigns as the peak of their kind, while the Adventurer Handbook stresses wild spirits who never kneeled to any god or worldly power. Their name also changed with time: the people of Byakuyakoku used "Dragonheirs of the Depths" until Orobashi observed the experiments, after which "Bathysmal Vishaps" came into use.

## society
### STANDARD
Little is known of their social life beyond what Enkanomiya's records say. The Handbook says the strongest members of the Coral Defenders herd use different elements as a show of their hatred, and that the herd was selected and bred by the people of Enkanomiya. The Bolteater Archive text says its Electro form arose from social adaptation (division of labour).
### LARGE +
For the Primordial Bathysmal Vishap, the Archive notes that they were once the dominant race in the depths, and that dragons ruled the whole world in an even earlier age before their Seven Sovereigns were defeated by a power from the heavens and withered one by one.

## history
### STANDARD
For thousands of years the Dragonheirs of the Depths preyed on the people of Enkanomiya, then called Byakuyakoku. Learning that the dragons loved the dark and feared light, the people hung thousands of lanterns, which was not enough. They prayed until Istaroth, one of the Four Shades of the Heavenly Principles, answered by giving Abrax the knowledge to build the Dainichi Mikoshi, the artificial sun on which Enkanomiya relied for an untold span of years.

Several thousand years ago the Watatsumi Omikami (Orobashi) defeated them and suppressed them beneath the sea with jade corals, then brought the people of Enkanomiya to the surface and made Watatsumi Island their home.
### LARGE +
Later, Enkanomiya's people kept studying the vishaps. The Vishap Research Lab bred specially modified individuals that could accept grafted fragments of Orobashi's Bloodbranch Coral without ill effect, which made them very large and powerful. This was painstaking, because Light Realm beings naturally reject those of the Human Realm, which includes gods.
### ULTRA +
In recent years some have ventured to the surface. In Fontaine, a Primordial Bathysmal Vishap lives at Loch Urania before the tree in Erinnyes Forest is restored; afterwards a group of hatchlings appears there, which, unlike those in Enkanomiya, are not aggressive unless attacked. Most Fontainians are not aware of them. The Archive says that although they may struggle to fit into a world ruled by humans, it is still their world.

## legacy
### STANDARD
The spirit of Watatsumi Island is said to feel the calls of Enkanomiya in the depths, and the island's soil is slowly bleaching into Holy Soil. By tradition, every few centuries the shrine maidens of Sangonomiya Shrine break the barrier when the spirit veins flow smoothly, retrieve a Jade Coral Branch left to suppress the Dragonheirs, and perform the Watatsumi Goryou Matsuri so the sunken spirit can return.
### LARGE +
There are many Jade Coral Branches, but the World Quest The Moon-Bathed Deep warns that as the tradition continues the Dragonheirs may one day break the barrier and invade Watatsumi Island.

## abilities
### LITE
Individuals can carry Hydro, Cryo or Electro, and some wield more than one element. A signature attack, Cleansing Shower, drains Energy from its target, and a target without enough Energy loses HP instead.
### STANDARD +
Primordial Bathysmal Vishaps combine physical and Hydro attacks. The Rimebiter is Cryo, the Bolteater is Electro.

## variants
### STANDARD
- Primordial Bathysmal Vishap: a Hydro form found in Enkanomiya; one Local Legend named Cineas lives in Machimos in the Sea of Bygone Eras. One also appears in Fontaine.
- Bathysmal Vishap Hatchlings: young vishaps, including Rimebiter and Bolteater hatchlings, which appear in Enkanomiya and Fontaine.
- Rimebiter Bathysmal Vishap (Cryo) and Bolteater Bathysmal Vishap (Electro): together the Coral Defenders, also known as the Bathysmal Vishap Herd. The Bolteater is met only in this fight.
### LARGE +
The Bolteater's Archive entry says its Electro form shows that the Bathysmal Vishaps lost their purity, so the Dragon of Water would not be born among them but would descend in human form. The post-Finale text adds that returned Dragons of Water still regard them as kin but they are closer to another race, perhaps choosing to be near humans, perhaps seeing potential.

## relations
### STANDARD
Enemies of humans in Enkanomiya, with lanterns and the artificial sun as their historical deterrents. Watatsumi Island's Omikami subdued them. Neuvillette, the Hydro Dragon reborn as a human, is the Dragon of Water who would otherwise have been born from them.
### LARGE +
Pahsiv, a shapeshifting vishap living in Fontaine in the form of a Melusine, is mentioned alongside them on the wiki; the wiki does not class Pahsiv as a Bathysmal Vishap.

## status
### STANDARD
As of 7.1 they remain hostile in Enkanomiya and are quietly appearing in Fontaine, where most people do not know of them.

## gameplay
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
Game mechanics, not lore: the Coral Defenders are a normal boss challenge that requires completing the related Archon and World Quests; Cleansing Shower drains Energy (or HP when Energy is short).
### LARGE +
The fight was also reused with variants in the events Three Realms Gateway Offering and The Call of Ice and Thunder.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Treat them as proud wild creatures who distrust humans and light, not as mindless beasts: they are intelligent and can learn language. Fontaine's hatchlings are calm unless provoked. Canon does not show a Bathysmal Vishap speaking, so avoid inventing dialogue for one.
