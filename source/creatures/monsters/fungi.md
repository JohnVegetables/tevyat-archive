## summary
### LITE
Fungi are mushroom creatures found mainly in Sumeru and, in a few spots, in Liyue. The Adventurer Handbook calls a fungus "the congregation of overgrown and over-evolved spores." Most people treat them as monsters, though some are being tamed. They attack with every element, and Pyro or Electro changes how a fungus behaves.
### STANDARD +
Fungi count as Tri-Lakshana Creatures, so touching one with Pyro scorches it and touching it with Electro activates it. Their main boss is the Jadeplume Terrorshroom, the lord of the fungal beasts. Quests also show a gentler side: some fungi are the displaced survivors of the dragon Apep's corruption, and an ancient fungus god, Bifrons, claims to be their progenitor.

## nature
### LITE
A fungus is a spore-born creature that has grown far beyond an ordinary mushroom. It keeps one element, so a given fungus may be Anemo, Cryo, Dendro, Electro, Geo, Hydro or Pyro. Most are animal-like enough to float, stretch, spin or walk.
### STANDARD +
The Handbook says the fungi infestation is rumoured to be the result of a lab accident: a certain sage hoped to use spores in medicine and underestimated how well they adapt and reproduce. The Sumeru Preview Teaser gave a milder account, calling them mushrooms that evolved stronger ability to reproduce and to protect the flora. Even so, most people see them as monsters. Fungi use elemental power, but in Homecoming Nahida explains that the ordinary kind draw it from nature instead of from within themselves.
### LARGE +
Fungi are grouped by body plan: Floating (Anemo, Dendro, Hydro), Stretchy (Anemo, Electro, Geo, Pyro), Whirling (Cryo, Electro, Pyro), Grounded (Geoshroom, Hydroshroom) and Winged (Cryoshroom, Dendroshroom). In Homecoming a fungus survivor tells Nahida that its kind change shape to suit the environment they find themselves in.
### ULTRA +
Gameplay (not literal lore): a fungus that is Scorched attacks much more slowly but hits harder, and an Activated fungus attacks much faster. Unlike most other Tri-Lakshana Creatures, a Scorched or Activated fungus stays that way until it is killed, cannot be restored to normal with Dendro, and does not release a Truesense Pulse. Fungi never drop nucleus materials in the normal state; only Scorched or Activated ones do.

## habitat
### LITE
Fungi are common enemies across Sumeru. The Jadeplume Terrorshroom entry notes that fungi grow wherever it is damp and fertile. They do not appear in the Natlan overworld.
### STANDARD +
Two groups live in Liyue: a lone group in Yaodie Valley in Chenyu Vale, and the fungi of the Underground Waterway and the Glowing Narrows in the Chasm's Underground Mines. Small caged fungi sometimes sit in the camps of hilichurls, Treasure Hoarders or Eremites; they cannot be freed and can still be fought.
### LARGE +
Fungi also appear in Natlan only inside the Tribal Chronicles chapter Mushroom Realm Mystery (5.5), where they arrived because the fungus god Bifrons had relocated there. Natlan's overworld has no fungi enemies.

## abilities
### LITE
Each fungus fights with its own element: spore bursts, spinning, stretching strikes or flying charges. Pyro scorches them and Electro activates them, which changes their pace and damage.
### STANDARD +
Their fungus-specific drops are spores, pollen and dust, with nucleus materials added when one is Scorched or Activated. The Handbook warns that fungi adapt and spread quickly.
### LARGE +
The Jadeplume Terrorshroom shows the top of the scale: a Dendro boss in Vissudha Field with a Fury meter that Electro, Quicken, Aggravate and Spread fill, and a Scorched form that scatters spores that become Scorched Floating Dendro Fungi to fight beside it.

## relations
### LITE
People and fungi are mostly enemies, but there are exceptions. Most people consider fungi monsters, Sumeru scholars from Amurta take an interest in them, and some people raise them.
### STANDARD +
In the limited-time event Fabulous Fungus Frenzy (3.2), a Beast Tamer Tournament in Port Ormos, contestants caught and trained fungi. In the event quest Farewell, My Shroom Buddies, Layla hopes an improved Wisdom Orb might one day let people talk to fungi directly and plans to ask an Amurta scholar, and Yae Miko says the bond between the Traveler and the fungi turned out to be companionship rather than a master-servant arrangement.
### LARGE +
Varesa and the Collective of Plenty in Natlan grow fruity red and green berryshrooms in their orchard. The green ones turn out to be linked to Bifrons, and a visiting Sumeru scholar once told the tribe to eat any green mushroom at once. When Tighnari is told, he says Sumeru will send scholars to monitor them.

## individuals
### LITE
The Jadeplume Terrorshroom, the lord of fungal beasts in Sumeru. Bifrons, the Sage-Lord of Fungi in the Natlan questline. Xamaran, a giant glowing mushroom in the Chasm that can speak.
### STANDARD +
The Jadeplume Terrorshroom, a normal boss (3.0) in Vissudha Field, is a large bird-like fungal beast, titled in game as the Deepwood Shroomlord. The Archive says it hunts actively and is more intelligent than a real animal; its tail plumage resembles that of the ancient sacred beast the Spantamad school reveres, so the two may once have lived together.
### LARGE +
Xamaran is a mushroom creature from Sumeru who speaks by releasing spores and says its name came from an old friend. Paimon nicknamed it "Sheeshroom". Tighnari says it took a long time to reach its present form. Xamaran is described as an offshoot of Bifrons, but it no longer shares a link with him and calls him a foolish father.

## history
@version: 3.6
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
Homecoming (Sapientia Oromasdis Chapter Act II, 3.6): Nahida meets a fungus that turns out to be an elemental lifeform from Apep, the Dendro Dragon, rather than a spore. It says its people lived peacefully inside Apep until the Cataclysm twisted them, and that it forgot where home was after the forbidden knowledge was purged. Many of its kin became the Withering when they died. The fungus recalls an old promise from the Lord of Dendro that they would be brought home when the apocalypse ended. With a Fire Seed made long ago by Rukkhadevata and the fungi's kind, Nahida and the Traveler take the fungi to Apep's Resort. The two fungi give themselves up so that Nahida does not have to sacrifice her own form, and dissipate into basic elemental power inside Apep's Heart of Oasis. Afterwards Nahida keeps watch on the fungi who stayed near Sumeru City.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay interpretation (this project's recommendation, not canon text): treat wild fungi as animal-like and territorial rather than speaking. A speaking fungus is a special case: only Xamaran, the Homecoming survivors and Bifrons talk in the quests above. Keep their cartoonish, puffing look, and let Pyro and Electro visibly change their mood.
### LARGE +
Do not make every fungus intelligent, and do not claim that all fungi come from Apep or Bifrons. Canon tells us a few special ones do.

## entry: Bifrons
@keys: Bifrons, Sage-Lord of Fungi
@version: 5.5
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Bifrons is an ancient fungus god, titled the Sage-Lord of Fungi, who appears in the Tribal Chronicles chapter Mushroom Realm Mystery (5.5). According to the quest and the wiki's summary, during the Archon War he offered his "clones" as food to starving people in Sumeru, granting power in exchange; the larger his following, the thinner his power became. He ordered a human follower to carry him somewhere hot to reproduce, and an eruption near the Great Volcano of Tollan buried them. Over 2,000 years later the green berryshrooms in Varesa's orchard let him contact the Traveler, Paimon and Varesa. He still thought the Archon War was going on, and was beaten when the Collective of Plenty simply ate the mushrooms.
### LARGE +
The scholars of the Sumeru Akademiya, chiefly in Amurta, knew of Bifrons's role in Sumeru's ecological history and lost track of him once he left. Xamaran, a giant mushroom in the Chasm, is his offshoot.

## entry: Xamaran
@keys: Xamaran, Sheeshroom
### STANDARD
Xamaran is a giant glowing mushroom in the Glowing Narrows of the Chasm's Underground Mines in Liyue, and can speak through spores. Tighnari says it took a long time to reach its present form. It has an affinity for Lumenspar and can be energized with the Lumenstone Adjuvant. After the 5.5 Natlan questline it is revealed to be an offshoot of the fungus god Bifrons, though it long ago lost its mental link to him and speaks of him with disdain.

## entry: Apep's Creatures
@keys: Apep's Creatures, Proliferating Organism
@version: 3.6
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
"Apep's Creatures" is an unofficial wiki name for the elemental lifeforms born from the elemental energy inside Apep. Inside the dragon was a whole world, and the creatures believed it the best place ever. When the forbidden knowledge corrupted Apep, many could not leave the dragon and became Proliferating Organisms, while escapees took the shape of fungi and sought help from Rukkhadevata. In Homecoming, Nahida takes up their cause as the new Dendro Archon. Afterwards Nahida comforts a young Hydro fungus she calls Nur, and expects to meet the elemental fungus again.

## entry: Fabulous Fungus Frenzy
@keys: Fabulous Fungus Frenzy, Beast Tamer Tournament, Shroom Buddies
@version: 3.2
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
During the limited-time event Fabulous Fungus Frenzy (3.2), a researcher organizes a Beast Tamer Tournament at Port Ormos with the backing of a wealthy sponsor, and contestants capture and train fungi. In the sixth event quest, Farewell, My Shroom Buddies, the Traveler's fungus Bongo-Head, Layla's Twirly-Whirly and Yae Miko's Blitzara appear as companions at the end of the tournament. Yae Miko says the event also exposed a criminal conspiracy involving a man named Elchingen.
