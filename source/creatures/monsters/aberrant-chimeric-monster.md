## summary
### LITE
Aberrant Chimeric Monsters are the hybrid beasts of Snezhnaya, products of the bio-alchemy that flourished before it was forbidden. In the reign of the Belyi Tsar alchemists, above all Koshchei, freely combined living things into new animals. Most were consumed in the war against the Onslaught of Dark Beasts, and the handful that survive are rare, dangerous exceptions. They include armoured beasts, burrowing predators, winged elephants, horned bears, the two-headed Chimeric Winged Lion and the Immortal Construct.
### STANDARD +
They are not fae and not natural wildlife: each was designed, or assembled, for a purpose, and many have lost the purpose they were built for. Most have little awareness and follow a leader or simply hunt. A few are tied to named figures: Zver, the intelligent chimera who served the Belyi Tsar, and Zhara, an Immortal Construct.

## nature
### LITE
Man-made monsters from Snezhnayan bio-alchemy, combining the traits of different animals. In-game text describes them as pieces of life, scattered like puzzle parts, put together as the researchers pleased.
### STANDARD +
Archive and Loading Screen text say alchemy was practised freely across Snezhnaya during the Belyi Tsar's reign, before it was forbidden. Koshchei is named as the creator of the group. The wiki says he first combined animals, was praised for it, and later experimented on fae. Monsters built around one function rarely survive in the wild once their place or purpose is gone, and the Armored Beast is noted as an exception. Most of the creations broke the laws of the world and died; the Horned Bear is a stranger case, assembled spontaneously from life segments rather than to a design, and able to wield elemental power.
### LARGE +
The Chimeric Winged Lion's Archive entry adds that Koshchei made many primordial bodies called Prototypes by a method never retold. Only one grew into the perfect creation he intended; the near-perfect failures were casually remade into two-headed monsters, which still outclass any ordinary chimera. In The Stone Beasts, Like Ghouls Seated the Eye of Graeae says Zver and one other prototype were the only ones to show a trace of intelligence, and that Koshchei considered Zver half-formed and abandoned it. The Immortal Construct text repeats the pattern: when one subject of a batch succeeded the rest were discarded.

## society
### STANDARD
They have no society. Most possess little real awareness and simply obey a leader or follow instinct. Before the Cataclysm the chimeras fought the Abyss under Zver's command and the Tsar's banner, according to the Eye of Graeae; Chimeric Winged Elephants also worked with the Vila on intelligence and communications, carrying the Tsar's orders as his army advanced.
### LARGE +
Today the survivors live wild: Armored Beasts hunt in packs, Burrowbeasts move underground at night, and the Valiant Chronicles text calls the Burrowbeast perhaps the most cunning of the remaining chimeras. An Archive entry mentions a report that a worker in Svetloledovka once saw a giant Burrowbeast, but dismisses it as probably a wild rumour. Residual guard behaviour persists in places: the chimeric beasts near the Pale Crown Palace attack intruders on Zver's orders.

## abilities
### STANDARD
Chimeric Armored Beast: a four-legged beast with a carapace and a charging horn, built as a shock force; it fires crystals of Geo energy. Chimeric Burrowbeast: tunnels through earth, rock, ice and snow and sees in the dark, ambushing prey at night (Electro). Chimeric Winged Elephant: a short-legged flying elephant made to cross terrain freely and reach high fruit. Chimeric Horned Bear: a Pyro bear of spontaneous construction. Chimeric Winged Lion: a two-headed, winged boss with Anemo and Electro power, said to be unmatched among chimeras. Immortal Construct: a mindless, winged, red-leafed boss with Pyro power.
### LARGE +
Gameplay (labelled as such): the Winged Lion boss, "Solo Accompanist", is fought in the Volkodlak Tundra and shields itself with an Electro ward; the Immortal Construct, "The Remnant Body of the Eternal Flamefeather", is fought in the Everfrozen Earth and has a Lifeflare state that adds Pyro to its attacks. Stellar Glimmer reactions are the most effective way to deplete the Winged Lion's ward.

## individuals
### STANDARD
Zver: a two-headed chimera that gained intelligence. It was a loyal servant of the Belyi Tsar, dragged his body back from the battlefield at Khaenri'ah, and guards his resting place at the Pale Crown Palace; it died in 7.0 after the Traveler defeated it. Zhara: an Immortal Construct with a humanoid upper body and long fair hair, animated by Koshchei's fragment of the Eye of Graeae, who protected Okurov in Flamefeather Valley for centuries.
### LARGE +
The Chimeric Burrowbeast has a Local Legend variant under Svetloledovka (Bandersnatch, reached after part of the quest Great Deeds on the Tundra). That is gameplay content only.
### ULTRA +
Zver's loyalty had a dark side the quest shows in flashback. It once executed a human noble, which the wiki identifies as the eldest son of House Solovyov, for stumbling onto something not meant to be seen; the Tsar's private affairs were the reason it gave, and the wiki says this spared Koshchei trouble. In the same flashback Zver says it owes its mind and speech to its creator but that its loyalty to the Tsar was its own choice.

## relations
### STANDARD
Koshchei created them and treated life as a puzzle to be reassembled; he abandoned results he judged failures. The Belyi Tsar took Zver in and named it. Aksinya, who once worked with Koshchei at Hvergelmir, is spoken of with contempt by Zver. The Vila are the closest fae ally in the war record.
### LARGE +
Koshchei's work later moved from animals to fae and then toward the Perfect Tree; the Immortal Constructs belong to that last phase at Okurov. The chimeras are separate from the Snowland Fae, though the Tsar's armies used both against the Abyss.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 only scattered survivors remain across Snezhnaya, wild and hostile. Zver is dead.
### STANDARD +
Zhara was unable to revive after Aksinya adjusted her inert body, per the wiki's summary of Chorus of Solace. The Immortal Constructs that remain in Flamefeather Valley are the mindless leftovers of Koshchei's experiments. No new chimeras are shown being made.
### ULTRA +
The wiki's Koshchei page says that those who joined his Perfect Tree experiment dissolved into blue algae, including Koshchei himself, while a few experimented-on villagers such as Aunt LePuy and No-Nose Ove stayed on as Leshy. These details come from an interactive readable and a wiki summary and are medium confidence.

## entry: Koshchei and the origin of the chimeras
@keys: Koshchei, Doctor Moreau, bio-alchemist
@keys2: chimeras, chimeric, Hvergelmir, Snezhnaya
@version: 7.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Koshchei was a Snezhnayan bio-alchemist who saw life as a puzzle to be taken apart and reassembled. He built the first Aberrant Chimeras by combining animals and was praised; wanting more, he began experimenting on fae. Rather than executing him the Belyi Tsar recruited him to Hvergelmir. Later the Eye of Graeae persuaded him, Pavlina and Alvis to betray Aksinya; he took a third of the Graeae's power to Flamefeather Valley and lived in Okurov as "Doctor Moreau", pursuing eternal life through the Perfect Tree. The wiki's Koshchei page is the main source for this life and is of medium confidence.

## entry: Zver
@keys: Zver
@keys2: Belyi Tsar, chimera, Eye of Graeae, Pale Crown Palace
@version: 7.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Zver ("the Beast" in Russian) is the two-headed chimera that stood guard over the Belyi Tsar's body in the Pale Crown Palace. According to the Eye of Graeae it was one of Koshchei's prototypes, abandoned as half-formed, then named and valued by the Tsar. It followed the Tsar to every battlefield and brought back his body from Khaenri'ah. Fighting the Traveler it taunted the Eye of Graeae about its lost memory, its body split in three and Aksinya's banishment to the Shadow Realm, then died, saying it had watched over the body for five centuries and kept its vow. A flashback has the Tsar forgiving Zver for not following its creator's orders, after Zver complains of its maker's contempt; Zver says its loyalty is its own choice.

## entry: Chimeric Winged Lion
@keys: Chimeric Winged Lion, Solo Accompanist
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
A winged, two-headed beast fought as a boss in the Volkodlak Tundra. Its Archive entry calls it a byproduct of a grand ambition, one of the Prototypes that failed to become the intended perfect creation and was remade as a two-headed monster. It commands Anemo and Electro power, and the text suggests anomalous materials fused into its shell lend it unusual strength. The Adventurer Handbook says the entity is a byproduct engineered on the way to a certain great wish.

## entry: Immortal Construct
@keys: Immortal Construct, Eternal Flamefeather, Remnant Body of the Eternal Flamefeather
@version: 7.0
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
A winged, crimson-leaved construct that roams Flamefeather Valley and the Everfrozen Earth, now a mindless, soulless remnant. The Archive says it was abandoned before the grand ritual could be completed; its purpose as a biological vessel is lost. Zhara belongs to the same kind, animated by the Eye of Graeae's fragment.

## entry: Chimeric beasts of the frozen lands
@keys: Chimeric Armored Beast, Chimeric Burrowbeast, Chimeric Winged Elephant, Chimeric Horned Bear
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
Four common chimeras survive in Snezhnaya. The Armored Beast hunts in packs and fires crystalline energy. The Burrowbeast ambushes from shadow and, by one Vestnik report, may grow very large. The Winged Elephant once relayed orders for the Belyi Tsar's army with the Vila. The Horned Bear is the odd one out, formed from life segments without a plan and able to use elemental power freely.
