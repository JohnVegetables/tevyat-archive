## summary
### LITE
Geovishaps are large, armoured vishaps native to Liyue's mountains. They are Geo-attuned elite beasts that roll into a ball, slam, and send spikes along the ground. Adults can also soak up Cryo, Pyro, Electro or Hydro from their surroundings and fight with it. They are the grown form of Geovishap Hatchlings and the lesser kin of the great Primo Geovishap.
### STANDARD +
The Adventurer Handbook and Archive call them mature Geovishaps. Both they and the Primo Geovishaps spent many years hibernating under the mountains and only recently became active, which is why ancient texts were the only record of them until adventurers began meeting them in Liyue. They first appeared in Version 1.3.
### LARGE +
Canon never gives a single origin for their return. Folktales tie the Primo Geovishaps to a calamity at Tianqiu Valley, and the wiki connects that to the Geo Dragon Sovereign Azhdaha, whom Morax once befriended and later sealed. As of 7.1 Geovishaps play no part in the Nod-Krai or Snezhnaya story.

## nature
### LITE
Geovishaps are vishaps, dragon-like elemental creatures of the Light Realm, in their Geo line. They are heavily armoured and aggressive towards people. The name is Geo plus "vishap", an Armenian word for dragon.
### STANDARD +
Their armour grows over a hatchling's. Archive text says hatchlings are said to shed the shell that first protected them and become Geovishaps after many years. Sumeru Akademiya experts quoted in the hatchling's entry think vishaps become dragons when fully grown, and a hatchling that avoids predators and disasters might one day reign over a mountain.
### LARGE +
Nobody has observed this growth: the adult Geovishaps and the Primo Geovishaps lay dormant for a long time, so the idea rests on old accounts and expert belief. The wider vishap entry describes the Geo line as little known, presumed to have lived in Liyue for thousands of years.

### ULTRA +
Names in other languages stress the same idea: the Chinese name reads as "rock dragon-lizard", and the German and Spanish names combine Geo with dragon and lizard. The wiki's trivia gives the Armenian vishap as the root of the word.

## habitat
### LITE
Liyue's rocky country, often in caves and burrows. Known haunts include Tianqiu Valley in Minlin, the Chasm and the highlands of Chenyu Vale.
### STANDARD +
The Handbook can pinpoint Geovishap Hatchling locations on the map. In the opening quest of the event "Vishaps and Where to Find Them", the Adventurers' Guild treats lone adult sightings as a danger to travellers, caravans and lantern-watchers.
### LARGE +
Event expedition notes describe signs of their presence: armour-polishing marks, wide rolling ruts, hollowed-out stone and heavy sandstorms. These are Guild speculation inside the event, not narrated fact.

## abilities
### LITE
Geovishaps roll into a ball and charge, jump and land with a slam, slash and punch with their claws, and drag out lines of spikes from the ground. Their signature trick is Elemental Conversion: partway through a fight an adult absorbs Cryo, Pyro, Electro or Hydro and hits harder with it.
### STANDARD +
Which element they pick usually reflects the environment: for example a Pyro coating if Flaming Flowers grow nearby. In The Chasm, Khedive explains that a Geovishap which turned Hydro had fed on Hydro-rich waterweeds, matching the energy it takes from local plants.
### LARGE +
Each element changes the attacks: Cryo leaves ice mines, Pyro adds spike rings, Electro releases wandering orbs, and Hydro marks a spot that later bursts. A rolling charge that hits a shielded character without breaking the shield bounces the Geovishap off and leaves it stunned for a moment, a weakness the Handbook tutorial points out. The Local Legend pair at Chizhang Wall use Cryo and Pyro.

## relations
### LITE
Hatchlings are their young. Primo Geovishaps are the larger form. Azhdaha, Geo Dragon Sovereign, is an ancient ancestor of the species. People in Liyue treat them as dangerous beasts.
### STANDARD +
Folktales say the Primo Geovishaps and their kind retreated underground after a draconic calamity at Tianqiu Valley. The Archive version adds that the overlord of the Geovishaps was imprisoned deep beneath the earth, and that the servants burrowed away to wait. In the quest "No Restoring This Past Land of Beauty", Zhiping suggests the monsters reported in Tianqiu Valley are the Geovishaps and Primo Geovishaps that once terrorised Liyue.
### LARGE +
The wiki's account of the valley says an ancient dragon king rampaged there, Rex Lapis and his adepti imprisoned it, and its servants fled underground. It identifies the dragon king as Azhdaha, whose own entry holds the fuller story of his erosion and his seal under the Dragon-Queller at Nantianmen. Treat the identification as the wiki's reading rather than something characters state.

## individuals
### STANDARD
- Yiji and Tianyu: a Local Legend pair of Geovishaps called the Rulers of the Chizhang Mountains, found at Chizhang Wall in Chenyu Vale. One is Cryo and one Pyro. They were added in 4.4.
- Azhdaha: the Geo Dragon Sovereign, an ancient Geovishap ancestor (own entry).
### LARGE +
- Geovishap Hatchlings: the young, with a hard Geo shell, that carry and cherish bone shards.
- Primo Geovishap: the great boss form met in Tianqiu Valley (own entry).

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not literal lore): Geovishaps are elite enemies in the Vishaps group of the Mystical Beasts family, and drop bone shards. Defeating one in each of its elemental forms is an achievement ("...Geovishap, Solarvishap, Lunarvishap...").
### LARGE +
Absorbing an element after dropping to 40 percent health or after enough time gives it extra resistance to that element. A Geovishap can be stunned by letting its roll hit a shield. Revived and Wintry Primo Geovishaps are event-only enemies of the Primo form.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay interpretation (this project's recommendation, not canon text): a Geovishap is a territorial beast, not a talker. Show stone-grey armour, a heavy crouch before the roll, burrowing, and the sudden shift in colour when it takes on another element. Keep to mountain and cave scenes in Liyue.
### LARGE +
Do not make it a speaking dragon, and do not call it Azhdaha. Keep apart hatchlings (small, sharp, bone-shard hoarding), Geovishaps (adult, elite) and Primo Geovishaps (boss-sized). Do not claim a canon origin for their return or put them in Nod-Krai or Snezhnaya.

## entry: Geovishap Hatchling
@keys: Geovishap Hatchling, Geovishap Hatchlings
### STANDARD
A young vishap with a hard Geo exoskeleton. Handbook text says vishaps are agile and not to be underestimated. Hatchlings carry bone shards and dig in groups, and Sumeru Akademiya experts believe vishaps become dragons when fully grown. In gameplay they burrow often and use a spinning attack.

## entry: Tianqiu Valley Folktale
@keys: Tianqiu Valley, Tianqiu, draconic calamity
### STANDARD
Tianqiu Valley in Minlin, Liyue, was a settlement long ago and is now ruins with a Primo Geovishap in a cave. Folktales speak of a draconic calamity that ruined the valley, after which the Geovishaps and Primo Geovishaps burrowed deep into the earth to wait for another chance to rise. The wiki links this to Azhdaha's old rampage and his imprisonment by Rex Lapis and the adepti, but the in-game text does not name him.

## entry: Yiji and Tianyu
@keys: Yiji, Tianyu, Rulers of the Chizhang Mountains
@version: 4.4
### STANDARD
Yiji and Tianyu are a pair of Local Legend Geovishaps called the Rulers of the Chizhang Mountains. They live at Chizhang Wall in Chenyu Vale, Liyue, and fight as a Cryo and a Pyro partner. Chizhang was once the city governed by the Mistress of Chenyu Vale. The pair were added to the game in Version 4.4.

## entry: Vishaps and Where to Find Them
@keys: Vishaps and Where to Find Them
@availability: limited
@spoiler: none
### STANDARD
During a limited-time event, the Liyue Adventurers' Guild, through Katheryne, reports unusual Geovishap sightings across Liyue and asks adventurers to investigate. Until then the only known sightings were in ancient texts. The adults look like hatchlings but are far more aggressive and destructive. Because of the many lantern-watchers heading to remote spots, the Guild fears dire consequences from an encounter.
