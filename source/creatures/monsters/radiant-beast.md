## summary
### LITE
Radiant Beasts are ordinary Nod-Krai animals that spent ages immersed in the power of the ancient moon and were changed by it. Infused with kuuvahki, they gained otherworldly strength, and most are attuned to a single element. They are classed as Mystical Beasts in Nod-Krai: most are elite enemies, while the Radiant Moonfly and Radiant Moongecko are world bosses.
### STANDARD +
Six kinds are known as of 7.1: the Radiant Antelope (Electro), Bladehorn (Anemo), Lunapod (Hydro), Glacial Wolf (Cryo), Moonfly (Dendro and Pyro) and Moongecko (Geo). The group first appeared in Version 6.0.

## nature
### LITE
Not a new species: an animal reshaped by moonlight over a very long time. The Adventurer Handbook says kuuvahki warped it and gave it strength beyond the natural. They use kuuvahki to fortify themselves.
### STANDARD +
Kuuvahki is the primordial elemental energy and power of the Three Moons, found only in Nod-Krai, where it can fortify creatures, move machines and even influence weather and nature. The Archive texts describe the beasts as living remnants of an older world lit by an ancient moon, who survived where others vanished.
### LARGE +
The Moonfly text says that by the principles of evolution, after the heavenly ruler tamed the primeval power, such a monster should have shrunk and weakened, but the moon's blessing lets it stay in its prime. The Lunapod text says that animals that could not accept the moonlight into themselves vanished like foam when calamity waves struck the Forsaken Sea.
### ULTRA +
The Glacial Wolf's Archive entry says that after a dark calamity killed countless lives the frontier forests fell silent, broken only by Glacial Wolves howling to the moon night after night. These are told as legends. The wiki links the 'dark calamity' to the Cataclysm and the 'ancient moon' to the Frost Moon, but the in-game text itself names neither.

## species
### STANDARD
- Radiant Antelope (Electro): once ordinary antelopes given lightning by strange bolts on moonlit land; legend says some temper their bodies on cliffs at the heart of storms.
- Radiant Bladehorn (Anemo): a rhino-like beast whose charge, the Archive says, once restored the power that makes all things turn in the ancient borderlands.
- Radiant Lunapod (Hydro): its Archive text says that when waves of calamity rolled over the land, Forsaken Sea animals who could not accept the moonlight vanished like foam, implying the Lunapod's line is one that did.
- Radiant Glacial Wolf (Cryo): the howling wolf of the frontier forests; one Local Legend wolf, "The Homesick Lone Wolf", is found at Ashveil Peak.
### LARGE +
- Radiant Moonfly (Dendro, then Pyro; world boss, 6.0): a butterfly-shaped monster in the Barrowmoss Barrens of Lempo Isle whose wings hold the moon's power.
- Radiant Moongecko (Geo; world boss, 6.4): a rock-dwelling gecko in the Afterglow Cavern on Wavechaser Plain.
### ULTRA +
The Moongecko's Archive text says its body has crystallised into segments as precise as machine parts, which harden into stone spikes. Scholars suppose its ancestors once lived on the surface and were driven underground, where on full-moon nights an instinct compels the geckos to gnaw lunar-soaked rock and soil to preserve their bloodline. The Event Menu text says they are fiercely territorial and unwilling to share the little moonlight that reaches the caves.

## abilities
### STANDARD
Each beast attacks with its own element. Periodically they release a burst of kuuvahki, the Radiant Cocoon, around themselves.
### LARGE +
Gameplay (not literal lore): the Radiant Cocoon deals no damage but drains HP from all nearby party members and can incapacitate off-field characters; while wrapped, a character loses damage, CRIT Rate and incoming healing. Healing the character to full HP frees them and grants a Disentangled buff to CRIT Rate and DMG Bonus. The Moonfly changes between Dendro and Pyro states and, after the Cocoon, gains wings and stronger attacks; the Moongecko shields itself with a multi-layered ward in a dome and an anchor; the Glacial Wolf adds a Cryo claw follow-up. Drops include the Lightbearing Scale-Feather (Moonfly) and Prismatic Severed Tail (Moongecko).

## distribution
### STANDARD
Nod-Krai only. The Moonfly is at Barrowmoss Barrens on Lempo Isle, the Moongecko at Afterglow Cavern on Wavechaser Plain, and the Local Legend wolf at Ashveil Peak.
### LARGE +
Their habitat follows kuuvahki, which is found only in Nod-Krai. The wiki attributes this to Columbina, who turned her body to moonlight and spread her power over Nod-Krai (Song of the Welkin Moon); the beasts' own texts speak of far older moonlight, so the two should not be merged.

## relations
### STANDARD
Wildlife, not Abyss: the wiki lists them in the Mystical Beast family, apart from the Wild Hunt. They have no society, leader or speech and are hostile to travellers.
### LARGE +
They share kuuvahki with the Frostnight Scions and other moon-touched things of Nod-Krai, but the archive treats those as separate. No 7.0-7.1 content changed their lore (the 7.0 and 7.1 version pages list no Radiant Beast changes).

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay interpretation (this project's recommendation, not canon text): treat them as dangerous, animal-minded predators that glow and gleam with moonlight. Do not give them speech, plans or loyalty. They suit a night scene in the wilds or caves of Nod-Krai.
### LARGE +
Keep scale honest: antelopes, bladehorns, lunapods and wolves are elite threats; only the Moonfly and Moongecko are boss-scale. The texts hint at legend, so let local characters tell uncertain stories rather than stating facts.

## entry: Radiant Cocoon
@keys: Radiant Cocoon, Disentangled
@version: 6.0
### STANDARD
The kuuvahki burst a Radiant Beast releases, wrapping characters in a cocoon that drains HP and weakens them until healed to full, after which they are Disentangled and briefly empowered. It is a gameplay mechanic; canon does not describe it in the world beyond the beasts' use of kuuvahki.

## entry: Radiant Moonfly
@keys: Radiant Moonfly, Shifting Shadow Beneath the Moon
@version: 6.0
### STANDARD
A butterfly-shaped monster at Barrowmoss Barrens, Lempo Isle, steeped in ancient moon power. Its Archive text calls its dance mesmerising and says the line between graceful beauty and deadly peril is thin. Its Dendro and Pyro states alternate in combat.

## entry: Radiant Moongecko
@keys: Radiant Moongecko, Moonseeker Drake
@version: 6.4
### STANDARD
An ancient, territorial gecko of the Afterglow Cavern on Wavechaser Plain, drawn to moonlight and crystallised by gradual kuuvahki buildup. Its Geo ward and anchor attacks form a core of its fight.

## entry: Radiant Glacial Wolf
@keys: Radiant Glacial Wolf, Homesick Lone Wolf
@version: 6.3
### STANDARD
A Cryo wolf shaped by the ancient moon's power. A Local Legend one, "The Homesick Lone Wolf", can be found at Ashveil Peak. Its Archive entry recalls wolves howling at the moon in the silent forests after a dark calamity.
