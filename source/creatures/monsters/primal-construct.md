## summary
### LITE
Primal Constructs are the leftover machines of King Deshret's desert civilization. They are elite guardians that stand among the ruins of the Great Red Sand and inside the Mausoleum of King Deshret. The Adventurer Handbook says they guard the ruins and can punish anyone who disturbs their lord's "pure dreams." What remains of those dreams survives only in the mantras written on the machines.
### STANDARD +
Three types are known: the Prospector, the Repulsor and the Reshaper, whose in-game title is "Architect of the Desert Palace." They are classed under the Automatons family and attack with Physical damage. In the 6.6 Archon Quest A Tower Unto Wisdom and Folly, Cyno's team dismantles excess Primal Constructs in the Mausoleum to make Akasha Terminals for people who lack them.

## nature
### LITE
These are automatons rather than animals or spirits, made for King Deshret's kingdom in Sumeru's desert. Like Ruin Machines, some stand dormant while others patrol set routes, and some simply serve as scenery among the ruins.
### STANDARD +
Archive text says the machines were not first built to be temple guards or palace defenders. Their original purpose was to rebirth the paradise Deshret dreamed of. The Prospector entry adds that the king studied taboo knowledge to create that paradise and that the machines "may or may not" be the result. His utopia is gone, and the Archive says the "dreams and oaths" he made survive only as mantras on the machines. The Reshaper entry calls the promised utopia, on the other side of existence, a mirage made of folly.
### LARGE +
Elemental Sight lets a traveler tell dormant constructs from ones that are part of the scenery. Their components were advanced enough that a 6.6 quest item describes them as ancient technology and high-grade components, to be broken down into microscopic parts and reconfigured for a new environment.

## abilities
### LITE
They fight with Physical attacks. A Primal Construct can deploy parts onto the battlefield and turn invisible, leaving the parts to attack in its place.
### STANDARD +
While it is invisible, the deployed parts can be destroyed; this forces the Construct to repair them before it reappears. If an Electro attack hits a part during repairs, the Construct is paralyzed. Inflicting Quicken, Frozen or Petrification on an invisible construct ends the state immediately. While being repaired the parts carry Dendro, so Bloom or Burning can be triggered.
### LARGE +
The wiki's part names match the three types. On the Algorithm, which shares the mechanic, Prospector parts fire energy beams, Repulsor parts chase the player and Reshaper parts land with force and, with two Repulsor parts present, link with a beam.

### ULTRA +
Gameplay (not literal lore): each deployed part carries about a tenth of the Construct's HP and slowly loses health, and the invisible Construct is far more resistant to damage. Paralysis lasts about nine seconds. Destroyed parts produce Energy particles.

## location
### LITE
Mainly the Great Red Sand in southern Sumeru, in its desert ruins, and the Mausoleum of King Deshret.
### STANDARD +
They appear as elite enemies in the ruins of the desert. In the 6.6 quest the Mausoleum holds several groups of them, plus an Algorithm of Semi-Intransient Matrix of Overseer Network as a larger opponent.
### LARGE +
In the Archon Quest A Tower Unto Wisdom and Folly (6.6), Cyno, Sethos, the Wanderer and others enter the Mausoleum to find "excess" Primal Constructs. Cyno says Thoth had agreed that they might as well be used if they were just sitting there doing nothing.

## relations
### LITE
They are bound to the will of King Deshret, and canon names no other master.
### STANDARD +
The Algorithm of Semi-Intransient Matrix of Overseer Network is a perpetual-motion device that governs the many ruin machines scattered through the desert and still seems to carry its lonely ruler's will. It is the boss version of this group, first appearing in 3.1, and shares the invisible-parts mechanic.
### LARGE +
Heryshaf, one of Deshret's Seven Pillars, calls Kaveh's suitcase Mehrak "King Deshret's favorite Primal Construct" in the 6.6 quest, a remark that other text does not confirm. Kaveh, with Alhaitham's help in reading Deshret Script, goes on to work on the merger of Aaru; the parts taken from the Constructs are for that effort and for new Akasha Terminals.

## individuals
### LITE
No individual Primal Construct has a name in canon. The Algorithm of Semi-Intransient Matrix of Overseer Network is the named boss variant.
### STANDARD +
In the quest Dual Evidence an Algorithm appears under the name "Field Annihilation Array" and in The Temple Where Sand Flows Like Tears as "Anoushbord", a "Forgotten Mechanical Guardian". In Dune-Entombed Fecundity: Part III it appears as an ordinary enemy because the main foe is Ferigees.

## history
@version: 6.6
@spoiler: minor
### LARGE
In the Archon Quest A Tower Unto Wisdom and Folly (6.6, part of Song of the Welkin Moon Act X), Nahida and Grand Sage Naphis commission Alhaitham and Kaveh to build many new Akasha Terminals. Cyno's team removes the Primal Constructs in the Mausoleum and brings back components, including after defeating the Algorithm. After this, the components are reconfigured for the new terminals and for renovating Aaru.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay interpretation (this project's recommendation, not canon text): portray a Primal Construct as silent, ancient and unhurried, with desert-sand dust, faint glyphs and a low mechanical hum. It does not speak, bargain or show emotion. Rely on the Archive's wording: it enforces oaths, not personal malice.
### LARGE +
Do not give a Primal Construct a personality or tie it to a named builder. Canon says only that Deshret's dream of paradise lies behind it, and even that is hedged.

## entry: Algorithm of Semi-Intransient Matrix of Overseer Network
@keys: Algorithm of Semi-Intransient Matrix of Overseer Network, Matrix of Overseer Network, ASIMON
@version: 3.1
### STANDARD
This boss is a perpetual-motion machine from the ancient civilization, ruling over the many ruin machines that litter the desert. Its Archive entry says it seems to carry the will of its lonely ruler, and that Deshret may have built devices that should not exist in this world because a friend taught him in a dream or because he coveted the power of the holy ones in the sky. Amid the City of the Pillars, ancient crystals still reflect the glory of the golden paradise. "ASIMON" is a fan abbreviation that was added in-game only in a Spanish achievement name.
### LARGE +
It leaves two kinds of parts on the battlefield and turns invisible, as the Primal Constructs do. The parts can be destroyed, Electro on a part under repair paralyzes it, and Quicken applied directly to it does the same.

## entry: King Deshret's machines
@keys: Primal Constructs, Primal Construct Prospector, Primal Construct Repulsor, Primal Construct Reshaper
### STANDARD
Archive text for the three Primal Constructs is the main canon for them. The Prospector entry says the master of the sands studied taboo knowledge in an effort to create a dream paradise and that the machines may or may not be its fruits. The Repulsor entry says they were built to rebirth the paradise. The Reshaper entry recalls the lord of the blazing sun who promised his people a utopia on the other side of existence. All three end with the same idea: the utopia is gone, and its oaths now live only in the mantras on the machines.
