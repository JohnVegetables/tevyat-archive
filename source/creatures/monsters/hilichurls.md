## summary
### LITE
Hilichurls are primitive, masked humanoid monsters found in wild places all over Teyvat. They live in small tribes, speak their own language (Hilichurlian), love meat, hate vegetables, and are usually hostile to humans. Most people believe they cannot be reasoned with. Many of them are former people of Khaenri'ah who were cursed into this form during the cataclysm 500 years ago.
### STANDARD +
A hilichurl tribe works like an extended family. The usual figures are ordinary hilichurls, Samachurls (shamans), Mitachurls (big warriors), Lawachurls (huge, solitary "kings") and tribeless Hilichurl Rogues.

## nature
### LITE
Hilichurls are bare-chested and barefoot, with a large mane, long ears, a loincloth, bandaged limbs, greyish-black skin and always a mask. The Archive says they resemble humans but have lost "both their intellect and spirituality".
### STANDARD +
The masks have a reason. In The Grave of the Guarded (2.6), Dainsleif explains that hilichurls cover their faces so they never see their reflection in water: compared with how they remember themselves, the sight drives them to despair. Hilichurls have been recorded for over a thousand years, but their numbers spread sharply after the "pitch-black calamity" of 500 years ago. They can live in many environments, but they avoid places with very dense elemental energy, which strains their bodies.
### LARGE +
The curse. In Fortune-Mocking Pedigree (Sumeru Archon Quest, 3.5), Chlothar Alberich, living as "Eide", explains that Khaenri'ah welcomed people who had forsaken their gods. During the cataclysm the gods named the pure-blood Khaenri'ahns the "greater sinners" and cursed them with immortality. Citizens whose ancestry belonged to other gods' lands were struck with the "curse of the wilderness" as they fled, and became monsters. Under this curse, memory, speech and intellect wear away over time. Dainsleif adds that the curse denies death without truly sparing anyone: body and soul keep eroding until almost nothing is left.
### ULTRA +
Growth. Young hilichurls dream of becoming Mitachurls, because fighting and eating meat make them stronger. A Mitachurl that absorbs enough of its surroundings' elemental energy mutates into a Lawachurl. Canon does not say how long these changes take.

## curse_and_end
@version: 2.6
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
Dainsleif confirms that the hilichurls gathering in The Chasm in Liyue are former Khaenri'ahns. When old hilichurls sense their end coming, they seek a calm, dark place to say goodbye to centuries of suffering. Part of The Chasm, around the Inverted City, eases the curse's effects, so they go there to fade away, ignoring food and danger. In Requiem of the Echoing Depths the Traveler's sibling and an Abyss Herald planned to amplify a pool there to lift the curse and "restore the homeland". Dainsleif judged this futile and likely to cause the hilichurls only more suffering, and the Traveler helped him stop it.
### ULTRA +
In the same quest the Black Serpent Knights, Khaenri'ah's cursed former soldiers, were found guarding the dying hilichurls. Dainsleif recognised their leader as Halfdan, a Royal Guard elite. He came to understand that the knights' growls repeated one word of old Khaenri'ahn, "Run", as if they were still protecting their people after 500 years. Some hilichurls dancing near the Thousand Winds Temple sundial and camping in ruins fit Jacob Musk's observation that they have an "inexplicable affinity" for remnants of the past.

## society
### LITE
Tribes are small and scattered through the wilderness. The Samachurl (shaman) is usually the oldest member and acts like a parent: leading worship, deciding policy and defending the tribe with elemental power. Food and goods are shared according to need.
### STANDARD +
In some tribes, a stronger Mitachurl overthrows the shaman; these tribes become more aggressive. Every member gathers and fights. Lawachurls are honoured with the title "Lawa" (roughly "king" or "chief") but do not actually rule and prefer to live alone. Hilichurls keep contact with other tribes, some over long distances. Mondstadt has many tribes; remote regions such as Natlan and Nod-Krai have few.
### LARGE +
Daily life observed by scholars and adventurers includes patrols, guard duty, sparring, dancing when happy, napping, mining with rocks, staring out from cliff edges, and building sturdy wood-and-stone huts, watchtowers, fences and totems. Some structures use "long-lost techniques" no one can explain. Hilichurls keep Forest Boars in pens, eat a big midday meal, boil water over Flaming Flowers or Pyro Slimes, and cook in large cauldrons. According to Jacob Musk, they seem to live only in the present: they neither store food deliberately nor commemorate their dead. Marks in their camps are crude copies of what they have seen in ancient ruins.
### ULTRA +
Their language, Hilichurlian (own entry), is barely studied. Jacob Musk, a Mondstadt ecologist dubbed the "Poet Laureate of Hilichurlian" (a title he disliked), and his descendant Ella Musk are almost the only scholars. Phrases like "mosi mita" (eat meat) express happiness and "mosi gusha" (eat grass) sadness. Kaeya and Venti know some Hilichurlian; Venti says he learned it for songwriting. Mika's Character Story recalls an outing with Ella Musk that ended in a hilichurl attack after she made a mistake in conversation.

## religion
### LARGE
Hilichurls worship elemental power itself, not any Archon. Some Mondstadt tribes revere Anemo but do not recognise Barbatos. Members with different elemental beliefs live together. Mask designs and body paint show which element each worships, and shamans dye their hair and skin in its colour. Worship is song and dance led by the shaman. Leftover raw meat is laid on the altar as an offering; treasure such as Mora and gems never is.
### ULTRA +
The Handy Handbook of Hilichurlian gives "Unu" as a holy word for the hilichurls' idea of gods and the origin of life (own entry). The word also means "one".

## abilities
### LITE
They fight with fists, clubs, rocks, crossbows and shields. Some, above all Samachurls, channel elements without a Vision; Jacob Musk calls this unexplained.
### STANDARD +
Hilichurls make heavy use of Slimes. They keep food fresh with Cryo Slimes, cook with Pyro Slimes, lift supply balloons with Anemo Slimes, and dig slimes out of the ground to throw. Mitachurls slam slimes into their axes to give them an element, Dendro Slimes reinforce wooden shields, and Anemo Rogues ride Anemo Slimes into the air.
### LARGE +
Samachurl lore attributes their power to experience and "dark, forgotten memories", peaking in old age. Stonehide Lawachurls are shaped by Geo until it becomes their "flesh, blood, and skin", and Electro seeps through the bodies of Thunderhelm Lawachurls as crystal growths. Electro Hilichurl Shooters carve their bolts from Electro Crystals. Crossbows are suspected to come from the Abyss Order. The giant axes suggest at least basic smithing skill.

## variants
### LITE
Common hilichurls (fighters, guards, shooters, grenadiers); Samachurls (elemental shamans); Mitachurls (big warriors with axes or shields); Lawachurls (mutated elemental giants: Stonehide, Frostarm, Thunderhelm); Hilichurl Rogues (tribeless Anemo or Hydro wanderers).
### LARGE +
"Mitachurl" comes from Hilichurlian "mita", meat. Shield Mitachurls carry wooden, rock or ice shieldwalls; axe Mitachurls carry Blazing (Pyro) or Crackling (Electro) axes. Some Mitachurls appear under Hilichurlian titles such as "Dada Mita" or "Mosi Mita".

## relations
### LITE
Hilichurls usually attack outsiders on sight, and adventurers and the Knights of Favonius fight them routinely; a few individuals will talk.
### STANDARD +
Many hilichurl groups are allied with the Abyss Order, whose Abyss Mages speak fluent Hilichurlian and can persuade them to fight. Abyss Mages are sometimes seen conducting hilichurl dances. The Abyss also drove hilichurls into battle during its invasion of Natlan. Not every hilichurl obeys the Order: a cautious hilichurl in Starfell Valley once asked the Traveler to drive off Abyss Mages and Hilichurl Shooters.
### LARGE +
Ella Musk's language-exchange commissions show both sides. If the Traveler uses the right words, hilichurls dance; the wrong word starts a fight. People in Sumeru's desert historically called hilichurls "majuj".

## individuals
### LARGE
- Caribert Alberich: Chlothar's son, cursed into a hilichurl as a child (own quest).
- Caterpillar ("Cater"): an Anemo Hilichurl Rogue given human form and speech by Narzissenkreuz in Fontaine.
- Churldric, "Ladler of Broth": a legendary Samachurl at Windrest Peak, Mondstadt (6.5).
### ULTRA +
- The three Dadaupa Gorge tribes: Meaty, Sleeper and Eclipse (own entry).
- Ukko: a Frostarm Lawachurl on Dragonspine whose title is a name from Sal Vindagnyr (see uncertainties).

## status
@version: 7.1
### STANDARD
As of 7.1 hilichurls remain common across Teyvat. The curse has not been lifted for anyone; the only known exceptions to their speechlessness (Caribert, Caterpillar) involved Abyssal power or Narzissenkreuz's miracle, not a cure. Frostarm Lawachurls also roam Snezhnaya (7.0).

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not literal lore): hilichurls are one of the first enemy families the player meets. Their drops are masks, scrolls, arrowheads, horns and similar materials. Rogues are the only group that does not drop hilichurl materials.
### LARGE +
Lawachurls gain an elemental Infused Form shield that makes them far more dangerous until it decays or is broken. Shield guards block frontal attacks. Samachurls cast from range and buff or shield allies depending on element.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay interpretation (this project's recommendation, not canon text): portray hilichurls as dangerous but pitiable. They growl, chant and shout in Hilichurlian rather than speaking the common tongue. They are territorial and protective of camp, meat and tribe, and they dance when pleased. Ordinary Teyvat people see monsters; only those who know Khaenri'ah's history, such as Dainsleif, the Traveler or Chlothar, see cursed people.
### LARGE +
Don't let ordinary hilichurls speak human language or remember Khaenri'ah clearly. Never show their faces unmasked as a casual detail. Treat a mask falling off as deeply distressing for them. Don't make them a unified nation or give them an Archon. Don't let an ordinary Mondstadter casually know that hilichurls were once Khaenri'ahns: that truth is known to very few.

## entry: Samachurl
@keys: Samachurl, Samachurls, hilichurl shaman
### STANDARD
Samachurls are hilichurl shamans: usually the oldest members of their tribes, leading worship and making decisions. They wear horned, smiling-face masks, carry rough staffs and chant hymns no human has deciphered. Jacob Musk notes the masks are meant to frighten but look more charming than frightening. Each dyes hair and skin the colour of the element it worships (Anemo, Cryo, Dendro, Electro, Geo or Hydro). They are the only hilichurls heard vocalising outside combat. Mitachurls sometimes overthrow them.

## entry: Mitachurl
@keys: Mitachurl, Mitachurls
### STANDARD
Mitachurls are the strongest warriors of hilichurl tribes, the size every young hilichurl dreams of reaching so it can eat more meat and fight more battles. The name comes from Hilichurlian "mita", meat. They wield giant axes (infused with Pyro or Electro by slamming a slime into them) or wooden, rock or ice shieldwalls, and throw slimes at distant foes. Tribes led by a Mitachurl tend to be more aggressive, and Mitachurls can stir infighting.

## entry: Lawachurl
@keys: Lawachurl, Lawachurls, Hilichurl Chieftain, Lawa
### STANDARD
Lawachurls are Mitachurls mutated by absorbing large amounts of elemental energy: bigger, stronger and ferocious. Other hilichurls call them "Lawa", a respectful word for "king" or "chief", yet they do not rule. They prefer solitude and avoid the smaller hilichurls' reverence, although some admirers settle near them. Known kinds: Stonehide (Geo), Frostarm (Cryo; found only on Dragonspine and in Snezhnaya) and Thunderhelm (Electro). Seasoned adventurers avoid areas where they roam.

## entry: Hilichurl Rogue
@keys: Hilichurl Rogue, Hilichurl Rogues
### STANDARD
Hilichurl Rogues are tribeless wanderers, as tall as Mitachurls and said to be clever by hilichurl standards. The Archive says they seem to be searching for the origin of their kind and some "bygone splendor" of their people, and that if hilichurls could write epics, the rogues would be the heroes. They sometimes help other hilichurls for a while and then move on. Anemo Rogues ride Anemo Slimes; Hydro Rogues launch mist bubbles from Hydro Slimes.

## entry: Dadaupa Gorge tribes
@keys: Meaty Tribe, Sleeper Tribe, Sleepy Tribe, Eclipse Tribe
### STANDARD
Jacob Musk's fourth volume describes three tribes in Dadaupa Gorge, Mondstadt. The Meaty Tribe loves feasting: boars in a central pen with a swineherd, a stew pot over a Pyro Slime, and a combat arena where anyone thrown out is mocked by all. The Sleeper Tribe sleeps whenever it can in huts with soft hide beds; Musk calls it the most cunning tribe and jokes that good sleep is the reason. The Eclipse Tribe is the most occult: it worships a crude eclipsed-sun symbol instead of an element, its shamans are unusually powerful, and the strongest shaman sits on a grand throne.
### LARGE +
The eclipse symbol may refer to Khaenri'ah's Eclipse Dynasty; this is unconfirmed.

## entry: Hilinigmatic Terrain event
@keys: Hilinigmatic, Journey Through Hilinigmatic Terrain, Eye of Upano, Mystifying Megachurl
@version: 4.4
@availability: limited
### LARGE
During the limited-time event "Journey Through Hilinigmatic Terrain" (4.4), strange statues appeared in Dadaupa Gorge and the tribes behaved oddly. The Traveler infiltrated their camps past watchful "Eyes of Upano" and fought a domain-dwelling Samachurl, the Mystifying Megachurl, described as a shaman who "accidentally gained Domainlightenment". In the later event quest "Sightlines and Camera Tricks" (6.7), Hedwig reports the Investigation Company's finding: the statue was the work of an ancient Samachurl, the "Ladler of Broth" Churldric, who had turned an unused Domain into a drill ground. The Honorary Knight's victory over one of his "lingering shadows" collapsed it.

## entry: Churldric
@keys: Churldric, Ladler of Broth
@version: 6.5
### STANDARD
Churldric is a Local Legend Samachurl at Windrest Peak, Mondstadt (added in 6.5), famous for delicious fish soup and respected among the local tribes. His Valiant Chronicles text says that in an ancient underground kingdom where food was scarce, the cooking pot symbolised the Praetorian Legion, whose commander was called the "Ladler of Broth". The kingdom is long gone, but the tradition survives among hilichurls in a distorted form. In battle he rains cabbages and sunsettias on foes and stirs a great cooking pot; letting the meal finish satisfies and strengthens him.

## entry: Caribert
@keys: Caribert, Caribert Alberich
@keys2: hilichurl, hilichurls, Chlothar, Eide
@version: 3.5
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Caribert was the son of Chlothar Alberich and a mother with Mondstadt roots, so the curse of the wilderness turned him into a hilichurl as a child. Chlothar, with the help of the Traveler's sibling, gave him a forbidden medicine; after Chlothar received Abyssal power from Vedrfolnir, a second dose restored Caribert's mind and speech. Seeing his reflection in a mirror tormented him. He was later engulfed by Abyssal energy and meant to become the Abyss Order's "Loom of Fate".
### LARGE +
In Bedtime Story (4.7) it emerges that he died at eight, used as fodder for building the Loom. A fragment of his consciousness survived inside it. Through the Loom's power to implant memories, he gave hilichurls across Teyvat a comforting bedtime story like the one his father told him, and the agitated hilichurls around Vimara Village grew calm. His consciousness then faded away.

## entry: Caterpillar
@keys: Caterpillar, Cater
@keys2: hilichurl, Narzissenkreuz, Meropide
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Caterpillar ("Cater") is a resident of the Fortress of Meropide in Fontaine who looks like a child but is not one. He was first imprisoned there 400 years ago after serving his master Narzissenkreuz. He is really an Anemo Hilichurl Rogue: a log says Jakob Ingold grafted remains of Carter Scherbius onto "those who have lost their intellect", and Narzissenkreuz's holy blade gave him sentience and human speech. Whether any of the original hilichurl's mind survives in him is unknown, and he does not remember Carter.
