## summary
### LITE
"Fae of Crystalline Flesh" is the game's label for the Snowland Fae that fight the Traveler in Snezhnaya. The group covers Snegovik (the ice-mage Glaciomancers, the ice-knight Glacial Wardens and the boss Guardian Blade of Drifting Snow), winged Vila (Halberd-Knight and Soarer Knight), the wolf-like Volkodlak brutes and the tree-like Wildwood Leshies. The Handbook says the crystals on their bodies formed from a surge of energy as they were being crafted into living beings.
### STANDARD +
The label is a game category, not a name the fae use for themselves. Most of these fae are curious rather than aggressive at first and leave travellers alone until provoked; Leshies are the exception. The people behind them, with their history, are covered under Snowland Fae.

## nature
### LITE
Hostile members of the Snowland Fae, artificial peoples made long ago in Hyperborea and later gathered under the Belyi Tsar. Crystalline growths mark their bodies.
### STANDARD +
The group's Handbook text ties the crystals on these fae to volatile energy that surfaced as they were being made; Snegovik bear blue crystalline markings, Vila a green-tipped ear. The Snegovik appear as pale humanoids with ice-blue eyes; the male Snegovik are roughly twice human height. Vila have insect-like wings and pointed ears. Volkodlak, per the Handbook, are the most populous race of fae in Snezhnaya, a family of varied creatures. Leshies are tree-fae that may be the longest-lived of all.
### LARGE +
Behaviour in play (labelled as gameplay): like Natlan's saurians, most of these fae notice the player and watch, only becoming aggressive if attacked, approached too closely or watched too long; Wildwood Leshies do not behave this way. Stellar Glimmer reactions are the standard counter to their special attacks.
### ULTRA +
Appearance of the Snegovik, per the wiki: pale skin, glowing ice-blue eyes and bluish-grey to white hair, with blue crystal markings on the lower legs, face, hair ends and neckline. Snegurochka often have tear-shaped facial crystals and wear gowns and a kokoshnik headdress; male Snegovik have crystal ears, beards and a forehead spike, an ice-crystal gauntlet on the right forearm and heavy silver armour. Vila markings include a green tip on the pointed ear and sometimes a green triangle under the eye.

## society
### STANDARD
They are ordinary Snezhnayan fae who happen to be met as enemies, often as soldiers, guards or wilderness dwellers. Vila were among the clans that followed the Belyi Tsar from the start of his unification campaign; most of the Druzhna, his personal guard, were Vila. Snegovik are led by the Snegurochka princess, with the Great Snegurochka of the past, Yalyane Morevna, remembered as a figure of "crimson infamy". Leshies keep to forests and mountain wilds and are seldom seen near towns.
### LARGE +
The Archive tells of Vila turbulence in the century after the Tsaritsa took the throne: small rebellions by fae angry at her policy toward the fae nobility, quickly put down, but their leaders were descendants of the old Vila nobility and the affair split the Vila people.

## abilities
### STANDARD
Snegovik use ice: Glaciomancers cast frost spikes and shelter in ice coffins; Glacial Wardens forge ice maces and shards. Winged Vila fight with Anemo-aligned halberds, swords and short flights. Volkodlak brutes fight with weapons and can imbue strikes with Dendro, Electro or Pyro (green, purple and red markings in the Raider's case); high-tier ones lean on elemental power, including the Voywolf Hunter and Fanged Zealot. Wildwood Leshies are Geo-aligned and root themselves to recover.
### LARGE +
Gameplay (labelled as such): the Ice Coffin is a Cryo ward that Snegovik cast on themselves at low health and that Stellar Glimmer reactions break quickly. The Guardian Blade is a Cryo swordswoman and boss.

## individuals
### STANDARD
Guardian Blade of Drifting Snow, boss title "Outcast Ice Shadow" (7.1): an ancient Snegovik held in high esteem who once served the Great Snegurochka and was charged with protecting the Snegovik clan for life; seven were given the duty, and only she remains. Every succession of the Snegovik sovereign must therefore happen under her watch. After the current Snegurochka princess took power she left the clan manor in the mountains, perhaps over differing ideals, and she is fought on Fellfrost Peak.
### LARGE +
The Handbook says she has left the manor and abandoned an ancient oath while still honing her swordsmanship in the snow, awaiting the day she can return. On first meeting each day she speaks differently depending on whether the player has finished the quest Bell Ringing at Dusk (7.0): in one version she senses Pavlina's aura on the Traveler and wonders whether Pavlina failed. The Glacial Warden's Archive tells of a Snegovik guard of the Belyi Tsar who turned giant during the war against the dark tide.
### ULTRA +
The Volkodlak Raider's markings tell its element: green for Dendro, purple for Electro, red for Pyro. A Glaciomancer, unlike the other Snowland Fae enemies, has a spoken line when defeated and knocked down, similar to Treasure Hoarders (a gameplay detail).

## relations
### STANDARD
They are the hostile face of the Snowland Fae, met by the Traveler as enemies in the Snezhnayan wilds. The sources read give no shared cause behind their hostility; the Guardian Blade has left her clan's manor, perhaps over differing ideals. The Vila and Chimeric Winged Elephants cooperated in the Belyi Tsar's war. The Snegovik have a conflicted relationship with humans, as their Archive stories of love and tragedy suggest.
### LARGE +
These fae are distinct from the Aberrant Chimeric Monsters, which are animals made by alchemists; both appear on the Tsar's side in the war against the Abyss.

## status
### LITE
As of 7.1 these fae wander Snezhnaya, and the Guardian Blade (new in 7.1) has left her clan's manor.
### STANDARD +
The group first appeared in 7.0; only the Guardian Blade of Drifting Snow was added in 7.1. No canon event wipes them out or reunites them.

## entry: Snegovik enemies

@keys2: Snegovik, fae, Snezhnaya
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
Female Snegovik, called Snegurochka, are born as ice mages: the Glaciomancers. Male Snegovik are born as ice knights who forge weapons from snow, like the Glacial Warden. Archive text says Snegovik souls were first made incomplete and become whole only through love of a human, which may be why so many love stories between Snegovik and humans end sadly; the Eye of Graeae treats that as a research perspective, not fact.

## entry: Vila knights
@keys: Winged Halberd-Knight, Winged Soarer Knight, Vila
@keys2: fae, Snezhnaya, Belyi Tsar
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
Winged Vila warriors. The Soarer Knight's Archive entry says Vila followed the Belyi Tsar from the beginning of his unification campaign, scouted enemy formations by brief flight, and made up most of the Druzhna guard. The Halberd-Knight's entry recalls the unrest among the Vila during the Tsaritsa's first century.

## entry: Volkodlak brutes

@keys2: fae, Snezhnaya

### STANDARD
Brutes come in low and high tiers: Basher, Lobber, Raider and Sharpshooter are low-tier; Channel Contact, Fanged Zealot, Leomastiff Guard, Long-Eared Striker and Voywolf Hunter are high-tier. The Handbook says they may have been created to answer what makes a human human. The Raider's Archive entry describes small, agile Volkodlak with senses keener than a human's.

## entry: Wildwood Leshies
@keys: Wildwood Leshy, Wildwood Leshies, Juvenile Tree Segment, Rugged Elder Leshy
@keys2: fae, Snezhnaya
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
Reclusive tree-fae that live deep in forests and mountains. Archive text says Leshies have only fragmentary sentience and cannot speak, yet records show they were made late, after Hyperborea's artisans had nearly mastered authority over life. They may be the longest-lived fae, and few outsiders have seen one live a full life.

### STANDARD
An ancient Snegovik swordswoman who left the House of Hesperides' manor after the current princess took power. She is the last of seven guardians sworn to protect the clan in the age of the Great Snegurochka, and every succession must occur under her eye.
