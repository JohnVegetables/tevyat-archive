## summary
### LITE
Whopperflowers are elemental plant monsters found all over Teyvat. They hide as Sweet Flowers, Mint or Small Lamp Grass and burst out when someone tries to pick them, then fight with Cryo, Pyro or Electro. They have high resistance to every damage type, and given enough time and food they grow into huge Regisvines.
### STANDARD +
Cryo and Pyro Whopperflowers are present from Version 1.0; the Electro one was added in 2.0. The Adventurer Handbook calls them "a bizarre mimetic plant monster". The Aranara call them Vanagni.
### LARGE +
They are common enough that only two areas are noted as free of them: the Sea of Bygone Eras (entirely underwater) and the Ancient Sacred Mountain (the wiki guesses they could not survive the dive into the Great Volcano of Tollan).

## nature
### LITE
Mimetic plants that lurk beneath the surface and ambush unsuspecting prey, taking years, plentiful nutrition and the right environment to put down roots and become a giant tree that sways and spews elemental power.
### STANDARD +
Archive text for the Pyro one says Teyvat hosts plants infused with the elements and that, having gained a measure of intellect, their "nutritional needs" have also greatly increased. The Electro one's text says that, fully charged, it can unleash a strong current from its crown, and that some researchers have tried to apply them in other fields.
### LARGE +
Regisvines are Whopperflowers that have fed for a long time without interruption: their elemental properties are more refined but they lose the mobility of their brethren. Regisvines are listed as a separate normal-boss group.

## abilities
### LITE
They attack with their element, burrow underground to reposition, and periodically raise an elemental shield while charging up a special attack. Breaking the shield in time paralyses them and lowers their resistance.
### STANDARD +
Game mechanics (not narrative lore): the disguise can be told apart because a disguised plant does not sparkle, has no Elemental Sight silhouette, shows a dialogue bubble instead of a pickup icon, and does not glow at night when posing as Small Lamp Grass. Seed Dispensary and Nahida's All Schemes to Know do not pick them up. Unusually large clusters of one plant are often a clue.

## relations
### STANDARD
Canon gives them no allies or masters; they are simply a hazard to travellers and farmers. The Aranara have their own name for them, Vanagni, from the quest The World of Aranara.

## individuals
### STANDARD
Notable cases: during an Archon Quest a group posed as Glaze Lilies; in Sumeru commissions a single Whopperflower disguised as a budding Sumeru Rose freeloaded off the farmers' watering, then fled rather than attacked when revealed.

## scene_use
### STANDARD
Use Whopperflowers as a harmless-looking roadside trap: a too-perfect patch of flowers that does not sparkle. Keep them animal-like, without speech or malice.
### ULTRA +
In a quiet farming scene, a lone out-of-place plant is a classic gag; as a hazard, an unusually dense cluster of one herb. They should not talk, and the Fellflower copy of Albedo is a special event case, not a habit of ordinary Whopperflowers.

### STANDARD
Whopperflowers that feed long and uninterrupted become Regisvines: immobile, with a shield at the roots or corolla that greatly raises resistance. Breaking the shield with the right elemental reactions paralyses the Regisvine.

### STANDARD
During the limited-time Dragonspine event Shadows Amidst Snowstorms, a mutated plant called the Fellflower took the form of Albedo. The wiki notes that mutated Whopperflowers can occasionally pose as humans; this is a special event case and not a habit of ordinary Whopperflowers.
