## summary
### LITE
Avatars of Lava are elite creatures of Natlan with a core of lava. They must keep absorbing heat to stay active, and are often found dormant until the Traveler uses Elemental Sight. Archive text treats them as hollow leftovers of very old attempts to make life from fire and phlogiston, with no more will than a stone. Two ordinary types exist (Eroding and Fluid) and one boss form, the Lava Dragon Statue.
### STANDARD +
The Adventurer Handbook says their traits depend on the circumstances of their formation: the heat of the core, which it calls the "Seeing Eye", and the rock around it.

## nature
### LITE
A strange monster that needs to absorb thermal energy continually or it goes inert. Its heart is a core of molten rock and the body is built from stone around it.
### STANDARD +
The Eroding type's Archive entry says the ancient civilisation drove huge constructs and cities with golden patterns that guided the flow of phlogiston. Besides machines with fixed structures, it says, Avatars of Lava are badly damaged lifeforms once made the same way, and after the flames of the sacred mountain went out most of them lost purpose and direction.
### LARGE +
The Fluid type's entry looks further back: a deeper, older fire once burned across all Natlan in many forms, giving light, heat and wisdom, and the early people believed life and soul took shape from the sparks of struck flint. The embers inside an Avatar of Lava are described as the cold, ash-like remnants of that era. If a core burns forever, even stubborn stone can be given life.
### ULTRA +
The Lava Dragon Statue entry gives a third angle. Legends say lava-cored monsters came from a separate line of research that followed the Source Mechanisms, asking whether the primal flame could build a new race based on humans. The text calls the research a failure: once the reaver arrived with a throne from beyond the sky, the primal flame could only produce hollow shells whose nominal life force gave them no more consciousness than a stone.

## abilities
### LITE
They fight in melee and with eruptions of heat. Eroding types are of the Geo kind; Fluid types are Pyro.
### STANDARD +
Gameplay (not literal lore): the Eroding Avatar of Lava and the Lava Dragon Statue carry a "Fury" gauge that fills when they are hit with Pyro attacks. At set intervals they take damage equal to a share of their HP; filling the gauge outright defeats the Eroding type, while the boss shifts into a different state depending on its form. The Fluid type has no Fury gauge but resists Pyro strongly, as the boss does while it is not in a weakened state.
### LARGE +
Dormant Avatars of Lava can be found motionless in the field and revealed with Elemental Sight, much as dormant automatons are. The wiki compares this behaviour to Automatons in its group overview.

## relations
### STANDARD
They are hostile to anyone who comes near. Archive text places them among the leftovers of Natlan's ancient fire-and-phlogiston age, alongside constructs such as the Primal Constructs, and not within any tribe or order. No tribe or faction is said to command them.
### LARGE +
Their link to the Heavenly Principles is only through the legend above: the "reaver" and her throne ended the age that made them. Archive and quest sources do not name any living maker.

## individuals
### STANDARD
The Lava Dragon Statue is the one distinctive individual form: a boss in the Atocpan area of Natlan, introduced in 5.5, called "Stone-Hearted Final Form of Flowing Flame". Its Archive entry calls it a creature raised in a geothermal fissure to restore an ancient race, now only a senseless beast that punishes anyone who comes near.
### LARGE +
Its drop is the Sparkless Statue Core. Sparkless is the word used for its dead core, in contrast to the "Seeing Eye" cores of the ordinary types.

## scene_use
### STANDARD
This project's suggestion (not canon): use an Avatar of Lava as a mute, instinct-driven guardian of a hot cavern, or as a quiet reminder of the ancient age that burned out. Do not give it speech, goals or a master.

### STANDARD
A boss form of the Avatar of Lava found in Atocpan, first seen in 5.5. Archive text says it was created in the depths of a geothermal fissure in an attempt to restore an ancient race but became a senseless beast acting only on primal instinct. It carries a Fury gauge that fills under Pyro attacks and is in a weakened state when its lava body is exposed.
