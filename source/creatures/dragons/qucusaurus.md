## summary
### LITE
Qucusaurs are the Pyro Saurians of Natlan: red, feathered, winged dragons of the northwestern peaks, living beside the Flower-Feather Clan. They fly, and they are proud. They are animals, not talkers, acting mostly on emotion and trust in their human companions.
### STANDARD +
The Archive describes Saurians with large, supple wings that can glide through the air for long periods. They first appeared as common enemies in 5.2, and some can be seen wheeling over Natlan's great ruins and mountains.

## nature
### STANDARD
Wiki descriptions give a slim, red, feathered, two-legged body with large wings. Qucusaurs, like Iktomisaurs, are stated to have feathers. They are very prideful. Their young are called chicks.
### LARGE +
Their Archive entry preserves a legend: the ancestors of the Qucusaurs were the closest in form to the mighty winged dragons and could soar like clouds, and when the greatest of them spread its wings the whole sky was blotted out. Today's Qucusaurs no longer have such forms, but the claws hidden in their downy feather coats are as sharp as ever. This is storytellers' legend, not confirmed lineage.

## society
### LITE
Qucusaurs live on the mountain peaks of northwestern Natlan and are closely tied to the Flower-Feather Clan, whose people ride them.
### STANDARD +
Riding takes deep mutual trust. A Qucusaur refuses a rider who shows even slight hesitation, because a mistake in the air could maim or kill both. As with every Saurian, a Qucusaur chooses its partner.
### LARGE +
Not all Qucusaurs are tribal. Large flocks live in Ochkanatlan and around the Great Volcano of Tollan, likely wild and with little contact with humans. Around Ochkanatlan, the Skyfire Circlet and the Ancient Sacred Mountain, some Qucusaurs can be seen flying fixed routes and ignore the player even when attacked, while others circle a small area and drop to fight when approached (gameplay observations from the wiki).

## abilities
### STANDARD
They fly through air and through Gaseous Phlogiston, and fight with claws, spat fireballs and a diving charge, at both close and long range with Pyro.
### LARGE +
Gameplay (not literal lore): Qucusaurs are common enemies of the "Natlan Saurians" group and resist Pyro. When indwelled by the Traveler they can fly and, unlike Iktomisaurs, change their altitude; they recover Phlogiston from Phlogiston Currents.
### ULTRA +
Wiki notes on flight behaviour: Qucusaurs on fixed routes can be killed and still give drops, but their drops fall all the way to the ground or water; those in a limited area are only attackable once they land. The Chinese name means roughly "velvet-winged dragon". The Goldflame Qucusaur Tyrant is a boss-tier member of the group, listed with the Natlan tribal bosses.

## individuals
### STANDARD
- Cacucu: Ifa's small pink Qucusaur assistant with an eyepatch, and the only Saurian known to say human words (a few phrases, limited by his vocal cords).
### LARGE +
- Holawaqa Ngoubou, the "Goldflame Qucusaur Tyrant": a Qucusaur bearing the Flamelord's Blessing, companion of Menilek, a hero of Tenoch's alliance 500 years ago, per its Archive description as relayed by the wiki. It is a boss rather than a common Qucusaur.

## relations
### STANDARD
Their tribe is the Flower-Feather Clan. For the wider picture of Saurians, the Flamelord's Blessing and the devolution of the Pyro vishaps, see the Saurian group entry.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay interpretation (this project's recommendation, not canon text): portray a Qucusaur as a proud, perceptive animal that will not carry anyone who doubts. Communicate by crests, wingbeats and posture, with its rider translating. Ordinary Qucusaurs do not speak; Cacucu is the exception.

## entry: Qucusaurus
@keys: Qucusaurus, Qucusaur, Qucusaurs
### STANDARD
Qucusaurs are the red, feathered, winged Pyro Saurians of northwestern Natlan, partners of the Flower-Feather Clan. They fly through air and Gaseous Phlogiston, are very proud, and carry a rider only if the rider shows no hesitation. Legend ties them to great winged dragons of old.
### LARGE +
Introduced as enemies in 5.2. Wild flocks also fly over Ochkanatlan and the Great Volcano of Tollan.
