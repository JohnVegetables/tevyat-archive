## summary
### LITE
Tepetlisaurs are the Geo Saurians of Natlan: bipedal, lizard-like dragons with large brown bodies, rocky protrusions and a drill-shaped horn on the forehead. They live in the mines of eastern Natlan alongside the Children of Echoes, and can tunnel through rock and even solid Phlogiston.
### STANDARD +
They are one of six common Saurian species, each tied to a Natlan tribe. In the game's bestiary they are a common enemy of the Natlan Saurians group, fighting close-range with Geo attacks.

## nature
### LITE
Tepetlisaurs are strong-clawed, rock-armoured animals. Their drill-horn and claws let them dig through stone and solid Phlogiston, and they climb crystals of solid Phlogiston easily.
### STANDARD +
The Archive entry gives their legendary origin: the ancestor of all Tepetlisaurs was a drake as large as a mountain with an exceptionally long life. When it slept, mountains rose along its back; when it woke and shook off dirt and stone, gems and jade were left shining among its scales. The huge drill-shaped objects in the Children of Echoes' lands are said to be the remains of its great horn. Like all Saurian ancestor stories, this is legend, not recorded history.
### LARGE +
The name combines the Nahuatl word tepetl, "mountain", with the Greek suffix -saurus, "lizard". Young Tepetlisaurs are called whelps; both adults and whelps appear in Natlan's world.

## society
### LITE
Tepetlisaurs belong to the Children of Echoes and live in the mines of eastern Natlan. Like other Saurians they choose their human partners and keep to their own tribe's territory.
### STANDARD +
Saurians do not enter another species' tribal settlement unless invited and escorted; in the game, an outside Saurian entering a settlement is expelled, and the world story explains this with protective runes whose origin is unknown. A Tepetlisaur belongs among the Children of Echoes, and the other tribes' Saurians belong in their own lands.
### LARGE +
In Linnea's Story Quest "Tread the Right Path" (6.5, set partly in Nod-Krai), the Traveler partners a young Tepetlisaur named Aguara in a Spirit Envoy contest and takes it to Natlan for training. Little One, the marked Tepetlisaur whelp from the Natlan world story, secretly teaches it overnight to form Geo maces.

## abilities
### LITE
Tepetlisaurs fight with their drill-horn and claws, striking with Geo force. They burrow into the ground and travel under it, then burst out to slam down on a target.
### STANDARD +
Gameplay (not literal lore): as enemies they use a drill-horn combo with an optional jumping smash, and a burrowing strike that surfaces to taunt before leaping out. They resist Geo strongly. When the Traveler indwells one, burrowing spends Phlogiston while moving, and it can scale walls at speed while burrowing; it is the best climber among the indwellable Saurians.

### ULTRA +
Gameplay classification (not lore): the bestiary files Tepetlisaurs under Mystical Beasts, in the Natlan Saurians group, and they have been in the game since version 5.0. Their Geo resistance is high, and defeating one yields Natlan Saurian materials and Geo energy.

## relations
### LITE
Their human partners are the Children of Echoes. Among the other species they are the miners' counterpart to the forest Yumkasaurs of the Scions of the Canopy.
### STANDARD +
The Traveler indwelt adult Tepetlisaurs several times in the quest "Medicine Born of Guilt" while looking for the young Qucusaur Antu.

## individuals
### LITE
Little One, a Tepetlisaur whelp marked by the Flamelord's Blessing and called the "Chosen of Dragons" by Kukulkan, accompanies the Traveler in the Natlan world story. The young Tepetlisaur Aguara appears in Linnea's Story Quest.
### STANDARD +
Little One is described under the Saurians entry. He later sets off on his own adventures. In the Natlan story the Blessing was removed through an old rite at the Great Volcano of Tollan during version 5.5.
