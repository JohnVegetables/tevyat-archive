## summary
### LITE
Yumkasaurs are the Dendro Saurians of Natlan: bipedal, lizard- or chameleon-like dragons with slim green bodies, feathery ears, large cheeks and a round, fluffy tail. They live in the forests of southeastern Natlan alongside the Scions of the Canopy.
### STANDARD +
They are one of six common Saurian species, each tied to a Natlan tribe. In the bestiary they are a common enemy of the Natlan Saurians group, fighting close-range with Dendro attacks.

## nature
### LITE
Yumkasaurs have strong hind legs and a fluffy tail. They use their tongues as grappling hooks to swing through the air and scale walls, and keep Flamegranate pectin in their cheek pouches to spit at distant objects and wrap around them.
### STANDARD +
The Archive entry gives a legend: the original Yumkasaurs were far smaller than today's, so small they were hard to see. They roamed on the wind, clung to massive dragons, or gathered in groups that moved like clouds. After their abilities degenerated they had to take their current shape to keep their form. This is legend, not recorded history.
### LARGE +
Normally a Yumkasaur lives less than a century; one that lives longer is considered unnatural. Their name joins Yumka, a Maya forest deity whose name means roughly "Forest Lord", with the Greek -saurus, "lizard".

## society
### LITE
Yumkasaurs belong to the Scions of the Canopy and live in the trees of the south-east. Like other Saurians they choose their human partners and stay in their own tribe's territory.
### STANDARD +
Signboards in the Scions' village give care advice: young Yumkasaurs like having their ears touched but dislike tail grooming, while adults enjoy tail grooming and show their tails off proudly. Saurians enter another tribe's settlement only when invited and escorted; in the game an outside Saurian is expelled by protective runes of unknown origin.

## abilities
### LITE
Yumkasaurs fight with their claws, tail and tongue, using Dendro power. They grapple with the tongue to swing forward and can climb cliffs, though less efficiently than Tepetlisaurs.
### STANDARD +
Gameplay (not literal lore): as enemies they whirl their tail, strike with claws after bouncing off their tail, and do a front flip using the tongue. They resist Dendro strongly. When indwelt, a Yumkasaur grapples, grabs and throws or spits objects, and takes reduced fall damage; carrying a Spore of Repose lets it make a temporary leaf to stand on.

### ULTRA +
Gameplay classification (not lore): the bestiary files Yumkasaurs under Mystical Beasts, in the Natlan Saurians group, and they have been in the game since version 5.0. Their Dendro resistance is high, and defeating one yields Natlan Saurian materials and Dendro energy.

## relations
### LITE
Their human partners are the Scions of the Canopy. They are the forest counterpart of the mining Tepetlisaurs of the Children of Echoes.

## individuals
### LITE
Kongamato, the ancient "Mountain King", is a Yumkasaur revered by the Scions of the Canopy. He fought beside the hero Burkina during the Cataclysm.
### STANDARD +
Characters in "A Hero's Rite" note that Yumkasaurs normally never live past a century, and suspect that Abyssal power explains his unnaturally long life. See the Saurians entry for more on him.

Yumkasaurs are Natlan's Dendro Saurians: green, slim and bushy-tailed, living in the south-eastern forests with the Scions of the Canopy. They swing on their tongues, spit Flamegranate pectin from their cheek pouches, and normally live under a century. Legend says they were once tiny and nearly invisible.
