## summary
### LITE
Vishaps are dragon-like elemental creatures of the Light Realm. They once followed each of the Seven Sovereigns by element and were the dominant race of the world; over long ages they are said to evolve into true dragons. Three lines are known today: the Geovishaps of Liyue, the Bathysmal Vishaps of Enkanomiya (with newcomers in Fontaine) and the Pyro vishaps of Natlan, who lost their intelligence and became Saurians.
### STANDARD +
The word names a kind, not one animal: a Geovishap in a Liyue valley and a Bathysmal Vishap in the dark sea of Enkanomiya are cousins, not the same creature. Their history is mostly known through the three places where they survive.
### LARGE +
Each Sovereign is said to be born anew from its own vishap line, but only while that line stays biologically pure. Azhdaha, the Geo Sovereign, is an ancient Geovishap ancestor. The Hydro Dragon would have come from the Bathysmal line had its evolution not become impure; instead the Hydro Dragon was reborn as Neuvillette. In Natlan the Pyro line was broken by the Heavenly Principles' conquest.

## nature
### LITE
Vishaps are elemental creatures of the Light Realm, also called the Vishap Realm or Elemental Realm. They are reptilian, dragon-like beasts rather than people, and where players meet them they are hostile to humans.
### STANDARD +
Vishaps are fundamentally at odds with the Human Realm, where gods and mortals live, and with the Void Realm. Neuvillette says their ancestry runs back to the Fontemer, which is why they adapt so well. In his companion chat lines he adds that vishaps lose the ability to tell colours apart over the course of their evolution.
### LARGE +
Bathysmal Vishaps are the best studied. They choose which traits to pass on and can even create new ones, including elemental affinities, so a line can change within one generation. A heat-resistant vishap raised in the cold may have young with thick fat, a tendency to sleep and a Cryo affinity. Before maturing, a vishap can switch traits on or off to suit its surroundings; Enkanomiya's researchers wrote that they had already stored "a veritable armory" in their bodies. They also learn human speech quickly: within four generations their language skill matched that of a 12-year-old human student, and fear of this is why the Vishap Research Lab stopped its experiments.
### ULTRA +
The wiki's trivia says Bathysmal Vishaps lose all visual acuity once mature, apparently "because of their great hatred for humanity"; the wiki notes it is unclear what this means. Neuvillette's voice-over mentions an old dragon custom of sipping the blood of dead young, tied to the tides and the water in Hydro bodies. The wiki describes them as pure, primordial elemental creatures, wild spirits that never bent the knee to any god or worldly power.

## geovishaps
### LITE
Geovishaps live in Liyue's mountains and have done so since a bygone era, together with their lord Azhdaha. They attune to Geo; adults can also take on Cryo, Electro, Pyro or Hydro one at a time during a fight, drawing on nearby elemental energy.
### STANDARD +
The Primo Geovishap is the great form met as a world boss in the Tianqiu Valley area of Liyue, always carrying one extra element shown in its scales. Local Legend Geovishaps such as the pair Yiji and Tianyu at Chizhang Wall are called rulers of their mountains. Geovishaps and Primo Geovishaps first appeared in 1.3.
### LARGE +
Azhdaha's design notes place him within the Geovishap species, as an ancient ancestor. He commands the ley lines and can show several elements. His own story is in the Azhdaha sub-entry below and in his own entry.

## bathysmal_vishaps
@version: 2.1
### LITE
Bathysmal Vishaps, also called the Dragonheirs of the Depths or Hydro Vishaps, once ruled Enkanomiya and the depths below it. Their attacks can drain Energy. They hate artificial light.
### STANDARD +
The people of Byakuyakoku, as Enkanomiya was once called, feared them for thousands of years. Thousands of lanterns and the light of the Dainichi Mikoshi, built with knowledge from Istaroth, kept them at bay. Several thousand years ago the Watatsumi Omikami (Orobashi) defeated them and suppressed them under the sea with jade corals, then brought the people to the surface and made Watatsumi Island for them.
### LARGE +
The wiki says the name "Bathysmal Vishap" replaced the older "Dragonheirs of the Depths" only after experiments on them were observed by Orobashi. The Adventurer Handbook says their hatred of man-made light comes from having lost the sun and the surface. Some bargained with Enjou to free kin trapped beneath the Dainichi Mikoshi, though Enjou never meant to help. Watatsumi tradition holds that every several centuries the Sangonomiya shrine maidens must break the barrier to Enkanomiya, take a Jade Coral Branch left by the Omikami and perform the Watatsumi Goryou Matsuri so that the sunken spirit returns, which stops the Holy Soil bleaching the island. With each branch used the vishaps' barrier weakens, and they may one day break through.
### ULTRA +
In Fontaine, Bathysmal Vishaps have begun to come to the surface in recent years. Before the Erinnyes tree is restored, a Primordial Bathysmal Vishap lives at Loch Urania; afterwards, hatchlings appear there that do not attack unless provoked. Most Fontainians do not know they are there.

## pyro_vishaps
@version: 5.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The Pyro vishaps lived in Natlantea, ruled by Xiuhcoatl and the Thirteen Sovereign Lords, and slaughtered many angels in their territory. After the empire fell and the Heavenly Principles conquered the land, they adapted to survive and lost their intelligence, becoming the Saurians of Natlan. Kukulkan and Och-Kan both called this a devolution, because the descendants had lost their ancestors' wisdom and power.
### LARGE +
The wiki ties this to Holy Soil: Natlan's Phlogiston-rich soil was counted as part of that phenomenon, said to help dragons spread and to hinder farming, and divine nails were cast down to disrupt it. The devolved Pyro vishaps no longer look like other vishaps and split into other elements, giving six Saurian species: Tepetlisaurs, Yumkasaurs, Koholasaurs, Qucusaurs, Iktomisaurs and Tatankasaurs. See the Saurian entry.

## relations
### LITE
Vishaps follow the Dragon Sovereigns, whom Bathysmal Vishaps see as the peak of their kind. Liyue's Geovishaps follow Azhdaha. Humans meet them as threats, though Liyue has lived beside them for ages.
### STANDARD +
Azhdaha, once Morax's friend, was sealed under the Dragon-Queller at Nantianmen after erosion made him attack the humans he had sworn to protect. Neuvillette, the Hydro Dragon reborn in human form, speaks of vishaps as kin. Melusines are the "daughters" of Elynas, born from the remains of an Abyssal beast; Neuvillette's third Character Story treats them as a new form of Hydro vishap or dragonheir in some sense, while the wiki doubts they count as dragons.

## individuals
### STANDARD
- Azhdaha: Geo Dragon Sovereign and Geovishap ancestor, sealed under the Dragon-Queller (own entry).
- Jiu: the child-shaped vessel Azhdaha made from part of his power (own entry).
- Pahsiv: a Hydro vishap who takes Melusine form, living in Erinnyes Forest and later seen in the Liffey region of Fontaine; first seen in 4.2.
### LARGE +
- Yiji and Tianyu: the Local Legend Geovishap pair at Chizhang Wall in Liyue.
- The Primordial Bathysmal Vishap at Loch Urania, in Fontaine.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay interpretation (this project's recommendation, not canon text): treat wild vishaps as dangerous, territorial beasts, with Bathysmal Vishaps shunning light, rather than as talkers. Do not make Geovishaps and Bathysmal Vishaps interchangeable: the first belong to Liyue's rock and ley lines, the second to the dark sea. Pahsiv is the rare friendly exception and speaks only a few words.
### LARGE +
Do not call Azhdaha or Neuvillette simply "a vishap" without noting that they are dragons. Saurians descend from Pyro vishaps but are not vishaps themselves.

## entry: Azhdaha and the Dragon-Queller
@keys: Azhdaha, Dragon-Queller, Nantianmen
@version: 1.5
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Azhdaha is an ancient Geovishap ancestor and Geo Dragon Sovereign. He slept for untold years under Nantianmen until Morax unearthed him and gave him sight, and he became a friend of Morax and of humanity. Erosion made him forget Morax and their contract, and mining in the Chasm damaged the ley lines and caused him pain. He attacked humans and was sealed under the Dragon-Queller. Later he tried to break free through his vessel Jiu, but the Traveler, Zhongli and his own benevolent consciousness stopped him. His own entry holds the full story.

## entry: Pahsiv
@keys: Pahsiv
@version: 4.2
### STANDARD
Pahsiv is a vishap who can take Melusine form and is called a "fairy" of Erinnyes Forest. She says "pahsiv" for yes and "Melusine" for no. In The Wild Fairy of Erinnyes the Traveler helps her cleanse the Weeping Willow of the Lake. Her only earlier acquaintance was Neuvillette, whom she called "New Valet". In A Gift From the Sea Spirits she rescues the Melusine Puca and later returns to the sea with Puca's signboard.

## entry: Dragonheirs of the Depths
@keys: Dragonheirs of the Depths, Dragonheir of the Depths, Hydro Vishap
@version: 2.1
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
The Byakuyakoku name for the Bathysmal Vishaps. Their dislike of light is why Enkanomiya burned thousands of lanterns and built the Dainichi Mikoshi. The Watatsumi Omikami later suppressed them with jade corals.
