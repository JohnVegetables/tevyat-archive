## summary
### LITE
Koholasaurs are the Hydro Saurians of Natlan, dragons of the southern springs. They are four-legged, finned and slim, and the only Saurian species that can swim. They live beside the People of the Springs. They are animals, not talkers: they act on emotion and trust in their human companions.
### STANDARD +
The Archive describes Saurians with thick, strong flippers and a long tail that can swim in water and in liquid Phlogiston. Like all Saurians they belong to the line of Natlan dragons that adapted rather than vanished.

## nature
### STANDARD
Wiki descriptions give a slim, quadruped, reptile-like body in white, black and blue, with several fins and horned protrusions. They are built for swimming in water, liquid Phlogiston and the spirit-roads called spiritways. As with other Saurians, they have a language only Saurians understand; tribe members learn to read their Saurians' intent from body language and mood.
### LARGE +
Their Archive entry preserves a legend about their ancestry: older dragons adapted far better to their surroundings, and the largest dragon in the ocean of its age, floating at the surface, could be mistaken for an island. Koholasaurs today are said to descend from it, and the colossal forms of those ancient sea beasts may still be glimpsed in their broad backs. This is told as a story ("it is said"), not confirmed lineage.

## society
### LITE
Koholasaurs live in the springs of southern Natlan, alongside the People of the Springs.
### STANDARD +
Each Saurian species keeps a home territory that largely defines the lands of its tribe; other Saurians cross into it only when invited and escorted by one that lives there. The Natlanese also take Saurians as companions, and the Saurian chooses its partner.
### LARGE +
In Saurian Indwelling, the game blocks entry to the "People of the Springs" area when the Traveler is indwelling a Yumkasaur (a gameplay restriction shown by an in-game message, not a lore statement).

## abilities
### STANDARD
In the world they fight with a tail whirl, a fired jet of water and, in water, a fast charge. Wiki notes say they swim at high speed through water, liquid Phlogiston and spiritways, and strike at both short and long range with Hydro.
### LARGE +
Gameplay (not literal lore): Koholasaurs are common enemies of the "Natlan Saurians" group. They resist Hydro, use Physical tail strikes on land and a charge that is used only in water. When the Traveler indwells an adult Koholasaur, it is the only Saurian that can swim in water and liquid Phlogiston; other Saurians drown in deep water.
### ULTRA +
Indwelling is a Traveler-only exploration mechanic: an indwelled Koholasaur gives no EXP or materials, respawns after a minute, and leaves a Spiritsconce when the Traveler leaves it. Its Phlogiston meter is spent before the Traveler's own. Wiki trivia notes the Chinese name means roughly "finned swimming dragon".

## relations
### STANDARD
Their tribe is the People of the Springs. For the wider picture of Saurians and their ancestry, see the Saurian group entry.
### LARGE +
Koholasaurs share the dragon-descent theme of all Saurians: Natlan's dragons once had a civilisation, Natlantea, and the Pyro vishaps of the Great Volcano lost their power to survive. The Koholasaur legend points instead to the sea dragons of older ages; the sources read do not reconcile the two accounts.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay interpretation (this project's recommendation, not canon text): treat a Koholasaur as an intelligent water animal that communicates by posture and sound, most at ease in or beside water, with tribe members interpreting. Do not give it human speech.

## entry: Koholasaurus
@keys: Koholasaurus, Koholasaur, Koholasaurs
### STANDARD
Koholasaurs are the Hydro Saurians of southern Natlan: finned, quadruped swimmers living in the springs with the People of the Springs. They can swim in water, liquid Phlogiston and spiritways, and legend makes them heirs of a sea dragon so large it was mistaken for an island. They are animals and do not speak.
### LARGE +
They were introduced in 5.0 with Natlan's first Saurians. Their name combines the Hawaiian word for whale with the Greek word for lizard.
