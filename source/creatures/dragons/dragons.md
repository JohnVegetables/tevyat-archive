## summary
### LITE
Dragons are enormous elemental beings of the Light Realm and the original masters of Teyvat. The old world was ruled by the Dragon King Nibelung and the Seven Sovereigns, one elemental dragon per element, until the Heavenly Principles arrived, defeated them, took part of their Authorities and remade the world for humanity. Today most dragonkind survives as vishaps, Natlan's saurians and a few great individuals such as Dvalin (Anemo), Azhdaha (Geo), Apep (Dendro) and Neuvillette (Hydro).
### STANDARD +
Ancient gods and dragons have "never seen eye to eye", and many dragons long resented gods and humans as usurpers. The Archons' powers rest partly on Authority taken from the Sovereigns, so a living Sovereign cannot regain full power while the matching Archon's throne stands.
### LARGE +
The history of dragonkind is told piecemeal across many regions: Mondstadt's Prologue (1.0), Liyue's sealed Azhdaha, Enkanomiya's Bathysmal Vishaps (2.4), Apep in Sumeru (3.6), Neuvillette in Fontaine (4.2), Natlan's lost dragon civilisation (5.x), Nod-Krai's moon history and dragon researchers (6.x) and Snezhnaya's Cryo Dragon (7.0-7.1).

## nature
### LITE
Dragons are born from the elements and live far longer than mortals or even gods. They usually grow stronger with age, and their size tracks their strength: Azhdaha at full power was as large as a mountain range. Their bodies reflect their element, like Azhdaha's stony hide or Dvalin's sleek wind-dragon shape.
### STANDARD +
Some are four-legged, some serpentine, some winged, some wingless. Dragons belong to the Light Realm, Teyvat's original form of energy, and thrive on "Holy Soil", land rich in primordial elemental power that is hostile to human farming. They are not immune to erosion and remain bound by many of the Heavenly Principles' rules, though canon says some fully fledged dragons (Neuvillette, Natlan's Thirteen Sovereign Lords) stand outside rules such as destiny. Vishaps and elemental dragons are especially vulnerable to the darkness of the Void Realm (the Abyss).
### LARGE +
- Vishaps are believed to grow into dragons over long ages; Azhdaha's design notes place him in the Geovishap species. Neuvillette traces the vishaps' ancestry back to the Fontemer, which is why they evolve so readily.
- Adaptability is the race's great survival trait and its weakness: lineages that evolve new elements (Bathysmal Vishaps turning Cryo or Electro, saurians splitting into six elements) become "impure", and a new Sovereign can only be born from a pure lineage.
- When the Heavenly Principles stripped phlogiston from Natlan's soil, its dragons survived by regressing into smaller forms that lost human speech and the ability to organise.
### ULTRA +
Neuvillette recalls an old dragon custom of sipping the blood of young kin who had died; when the tides surge with the moon and stars, the water in Hydro beings rises, a reminder that the dead kin will be revived. He also notes that vishaps lose the ability to tell colours apart as they evolve.

## society
### STANDARD
In the old world dragons were the dominant race, led by Nibelung and the Seven Sovereigns, each of whom ruled a portion of the world attuned to their element. Vishaps follow and obey their Sovereign as the peak of their kind.
### LARGE +
Natlan's dragon empire, Natlantea, shows how advanced their civilisation became: the Pyro Sovereign Xiuhcoatl and the Thirteen Sovereign Lords ruled from the Ancient Sacred Mountain, using the Secret Source (phlogiston technology) for automatons, observatories, artificial intelligences, megacities and a space elevator. Nibelung, warned by the star-voyager Anacharsis that Teyvat was doomed, chose to lead his whole people to the stars rather than leave them, which drove this technology.
### ULTRA +
After Nibelung left, a group of dragon scientists led by Ibni-Belum set up a research base on the Frost Moon. Their work, told in the Moon Gazing world quests (6.7), produced Project Moongazer and Project Lunar Veil: an attempt to keep broadcasting a warning about "pitch-black annihilation" received from deep space, and research that turned dead researchers into giant artificial kuuhenki.

## history
@spoiler: major
### LITE
Long ago the Heavenly Principles came to Teyvat, defeated the Seven Sovereigns with the help of the Four Shades and remade the world for humanity. Dragons who refused to submit, such as the Bathysmal Vishaps, fled into the deep sea.
### STANDARD +
Later, Nibelung returned bearing the power of the Abyss. The surviving Sovereigns woke and joined him in a great war of vengeance (the war of funerary flame), which nearly destroyed the world. The dragons lost; the Heavenly Principles cast down Celestial Nails to mend and suppress the land, turning Apep's forests into desert. Weakened, the Heavenly Principles then used part of the Sovereigns' Authorities to make the Gnoses and the Archons' divine thrones, which began the Archon War.
### LARGE +
- Natlan: Kukulkan, brother of Xiuhcoatl, taught humans to use phlogiston and planned a human-dragon heir. Instead the human Xbalanque slew the abyss-corrupted Xiuhcoatl and became the first Pyro Archon; most of the Thirteen Sovereign Lords fell in the siege of Tollan.
- Liyue: Azhdaha befriended Morax and fought for him in the Archon War, but erosion and mining in the Chasm turned him against humans, and Morax and the adepti sealed him beneath the Dragon-Queller.
- Inazuma: the serpent god Orobashi subdued Enkanomiya's Bathysmal Vishaps and studied them.
- Fontaine: the original Hydro Dragon died in the war against the Heavenly Principles; Egeria replaced it as heart of the Primordial Sea.
- Mondstadt: the newborn Anemo dragon Dvalin befriended Barbatos. 500 years ago he and Barbatos killed Durin, an artificial dragon made by the Khaenri'ahn alchemist Rhinedottir, over what became Dragonspine, and was poisoned by its blood.
### ULTRA +
Nibelung had earlier created the Three Moons and their goddesses to rule in his absence (revealed in the Nod-Krai storyline, 6.x); when the Moon Sisters accepted the Heavenly Principles, he treated them as traitors, imprisoned them in the "moon's reflection" and turned the moons into weapons. Some time after the Cryo Sovereign's death, the people of Hyperborea used its corpse to try to make a new Primordial Human. The corpse's unchecked Authority covered Snezhnaya in primordial ice, the Heavenly Principles struck Hyperborea with a nail, and a millennium later the Belyi Tsar emerged from the remains (7.0).

## abilities
### LITE
A great dragon commands its element on a national scale and can rival an Archon: Azhdaha, Dvalin and Durin are all described in such terms. Lesser dragons can be killed by strong Vision or Delusion users.
### STANDARD +
A Sovereign's dragonhood is bound up with its Authority. Neuvillette only gained absolute control of Hydro, and the power to declare Fontaine's people truly human, after Focalors destroyed the Hydro Archon's throne in 4.2. Dragons draw on ley lines; Azhdaha planned to absorb the world's ley-line energy, and Dvalin's rampage threw Mondstadt's elemental flow into disorder.
### LARGE +
Bathysmal Vishaps can choose which inherited traits to pass on or activate, can develop new elements within a generation and learn human language with alarming speed. Phlogiston, which Natlan's dragons regarded as a high manifestation of the light from which dragon souls arise, was the basis of their technology; Xiuhcoatl built the Huitzilopochtli array, which could have turned all of Natlan into pure phlogiston to make it immune to the Abyss.

## relations
### LITE
Most dragons and the gods have been enemies since the Heavenly Principles' conquest, though exceptions exist: Dvalin is Mondstadt's protector, and Azhdaha was once Morax's friend.
### STANDARD +
Canon notes that most reborn dragons no longer carry the old hatred of gods and humans. Apep herself remains distrustful but chose to watch humanity rather than destroy it after Nahida and the Traveler saved her in 3.6. In Natlan, saurians live as equal companions of the human tribes.
### LARGE +
Neuvillette believes Nibelung was wrong and that every kind of life is needed to stand against the Abyss. In Natlan, the saurian Little One was given the chance to inherit Xiuhcoatl's Pyro Authority through Huitzilopochtli and refused, trusting his human companions; the Pyro Authority stays with Mavuika (5.5).

## individuals
### LITE
- Nibelung: the Dragon King, killed by the Heavenly Principles (own entry).
- Living Sovereigns: Dvalin (Anemo), Azhdaha (Geo, sealed), Apep (Dendro), Neuvillette (Hydro, in human form).
### STANDARD +
- Dead Sovereigns: the original Anemo and Hydro dragons, Xiuhcoatl (Pyro) and the Cryo Dragon. No Electro Sovereign has been named.
- Others: Durin, Rhinedottir's artificial abyssal dragon; Ajaw, a surviving Sovereign Lord of Natlan bound to Kinich; the Fell Dragon Prince Scylla of Fontaine; Kukulkan, Xiuhcoatl's brother.
### LARGE +
- Och-Kan, Kukulkan's half-human son who turned on dragonkind. Wenut, the sand-serpent scions of Apep in the Great Red Sand (Setekh Wenut is called the last prince of the sands). Ursa the Drake, an element-less "corrupted dragon".
- The playable Durin (6.2) is a dragon written into being in Simulanka by the Hexenzirkel witch Andersdotter, modelled on the original Durin and later given a human body by Albedo; he is a product of witch magic, not a Light Realm dragon.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay interpretation (this project's recommendation, not canon text): great dragons are rare, ancient and politically significant; an ordinary Teyvat resident knows vishaps as dangerous beasts and the Sovereigns only as legends. Wild vishaps should be dangerous animals, not conversational characters. Saurians in Natlan are everyday companions.
### LARGE +
Do not give Neuvillette, Apep or Dvalin knowledge of each other's histories they have not shown, and do not resolve open questions (the Electro Sovereign, what the 7.1 Holy Soil will do). Keep the old-world history as something most humans do not know: it is learned from Apep, Kukulkan, Neuvillette's memories, ruins and quests.

## entry: Holy Soil and the dragons' fall
@keys: Holy Soil, hallowing of soil
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Holy Soil is land saturated with primordial elemental power. Dragons thrive on it; humans cannot farm it. Kukulkan says the Heavenly Principles treated Natlan's phlogiston-rich soil as Holy Soil and removed it, forcing the dragons there to devolve into saurians. Watatsumi Island's bleaching soil is another case, held back by the Watatsumi Goryou Matsuri. In Snezhnaya, the Cryo Dragon's corpse turned the land into Holy Soil of primordial ice.
### LARGE +
At the end of 7.1 Ronova released the Cryo Dragon's corpse from its nail so that it would hallow the soil of the entire world; the outcome is not yet shown.

## entry: Dragon Sovereign authority
@keys: Dragon Sovereign, Dragon Sovereigns, elemental dragon, elemental dragons
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
"Elemental dragon" in canon refers to the Seven Sovereigns, the mightiest dragons, each holding one element's Authority. The Heavenly Principles stole part of these Authorities and later placed them in the Archons' divine thrones, so a living Sovereign stays incomplete until its Archon dies and the throne is destroyed. This happened for Hydro in Fontaine (4.2). A Sovereign can be reborn, normally from a pure vishap lineage; the impure Bathysmal Vishaps forced the Hydro Sovereign to be reborn in human form as Neuvillette.

## entry: Frost Moon dragon researchers
@keys: Moongazers, Ibni-Belum, Lusalim, Maagees-Geti, Ungien, Nin-Kiggia
@version: 6.7
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
In the Moon Gazing world quests (6.7) the Traveler explores the Frost Moon, where dragon scientists once worked. Ibni-Belum built a transceiver after receiving a signal from deep space he thought might be from Nibelung. Ungien later fused with an alien "Stellar Blackbody" carrying a warning of pitch-black annihilation. Maagees-Geti and Lusalim began Project Moongazer to rebroadcast that message, and the dead Ungien and Maagees became the giant artificial kuuhenki Ungie and Maggie. Lusalim's research base was destroyed when the Heavenly Principles struck the Frost Moon.

## entry: Corrupted dragons
@keys: corrupted dragon, corrupted dragons, Ursa the Drake
### LARGE
Nicole says some angels who gave up wisdom for power became corrupted dragons without distinct elemental traits, such as Ursa the Drake, and that heroes have hunted them nearly to extinction. Treat them as separate from the elemental dragons of the Light Realm.
