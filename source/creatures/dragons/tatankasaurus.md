## summary
### LITE
Tatankasaurs are the Electro Saurians of Natlan: huge, horned, buffalo-like dragons of the volcanic southwest, living beside the Collective of Plenty. They are usually docile, but an angered Tatankasaur cannot be reasoned with until its energy runs out. They are animals and do not speak.
### STANDARD +
The Archive describes a Saurian with a hard, bony crest and a powerful body suited to dashing around volcanic landscapes.

## nature
### STANDARD
Wiki descriptions give a quadruped, massive, fluffy body in purple and beige, with crystal protrusions along the body and great horns atop the head. Their young are called whelps.
### LARGE +
Their Archive entry tells a legend: the ancestors of the Tatankasaurs were so large that they could move mountains with their horns, but lacked the agility of their smaller modern relatives. The entry suggests that the great power Tatankasaurs still have may be a gift passed down their bloodline. It is legend and speculation, not stated fact.

## society
### LITE
Tatankasaurs live in the volcanic regions of Natlan's southwest, around Atocpan, with the Collective of Plenty.
### STANDARD +
Like each Saurian species, they hold a home territory that largely defines their tribe's lands; others enter only by invitation and escort. A Tatankasaur, like any Saurian, chooses its human partner.
### LARGE +
The wiki names a Tatankasaur called Kayeke as the companion of Wanjiru, a hero of the Cataclysm, from Talking Stick weapon lore (see uncertainties).

## abilities
### STANDARD
They stomp and charge, dashing across large gaps and smashing massive rocks and boulders. The wiki describes them as close-range Electro fighters.
### LARGE +
Gameplay (not literal lore): Tatankasaurs are common enemies of the "Natlan Saurians" group resisting Electro; they stomp and, if the player is far away, charge, and terrain can stop the charge. A Local Legend version called "The Peak" is found in Atocpan. When indwelled, a Tatankasaur can charge across water and break Pulverite.
### ULTRA +
Wiki trivia notes Tatankasaurus is among the few enemies with no frontal Archive icon. The Chinese name means roughly "protruding-horn dragon". In the Saurian Indwelling mechanic, a Tatankasaur's charge both crosses water and shatters Pulverite, which other Saurians cannot do.

## relations
### STANDARD
Their tribe is the Collective of Plenty. For the wider picture of Saurians, see the Saurian group entry.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay interpretation (this project's recommendation, not canon text): portray a Tatankasaur as a heavy, steady animal that tolerates a lot, then breaks into a blind charge only when truly angered. Do not give it speech.

## entry: Tatankasaurus
@keys: Tatankasaurus, Tatankasaur, Tatankasaurs
### STANDARD
Tatankasaurs are the huge, horned Electro Saurians of Natlan's volcanic southwest, partners of the Collective of Plenty. They are fairly docile, but once angered become unreasonable until their energy is spent. They charge, stomp and shatter boulders. Legend says their ancestors could move mountains with their horns.
### LARGE +
Introduced as enemies in 5.2, with a later change in 5.5. A Local Legend challenge called "The Peak" is in Atocpan. The name combines the Lakota word for buffalo with the Greek word for lizard.
