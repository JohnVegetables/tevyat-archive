## summary
### LITE
Saurians are the dragons of Natlan. Unlike dragons elsewhere in Teyvat, they are common and live closely with humans. There are six common species, each tied to one of Natlan's six tribes: Tepetlisaurs (Geo), Yumkasaurs (Dendro), Koholasaurs (Hydro), Qucusaurs (Pyro), Iktomisaurs (Cryo) and Tatankasaurs (Electro). They are animals, not sages: most cannot speak, and they act mostly on emotion and trust in their human companions.
### STANDARD +
Neuvillette describes them as dragons that, unlike ancient dragons such as himself, went through long years of development and evolution.

## society
### LITE
Natlanese take Saurians as companions, and the Saurian chooses its human partner. Each species keeps a home territory that largely defines its tribe's lands, and Saurians do not cross into another species' territory uninvited.
### STANDARD +
A Saurian enters another territory only when invited and escorted by a Saurian who lives there. Warriors often dress in imitation of their Saurians, as Ranjit explained during a limited-time event. In the tale of Kuntur, told by Ranjit in a limited-time event, he was shunned by humans and Saurians alike until a great deed won him an Iktomisaur comrade.
### LARGE +
Qucusaurs let riders, especially from the Flower-Feather Clan, fly on their backs, but only with deep mutual trust. A Qucusaur refuses a rider who hesitates at all, because a mistake in the air can maim or kill both. Signboards in the Scions of the Canopy's village give grooming advice: young Yumkasaurs like their ears touched and dislike having their tails groomed, while adults enjoy tail grooming and show their tails off proudly.
### ULTRA +
Harmony was not always the rule. Unfinished Reverie artifact text (Faded Emerald Tail) records a man of the Children of Echoes condemned to death for protecting his tribe's Saurians, which the wiki places in Och-Kan's era, when Saurians were subjugated. Saurian poachers still operate in the present day.

## species
### STANDARD
- Tepetlisaurus (Geo): rocky, with a drill-horn; tunnels through rock and solid Phlogiston. Lives in the eastern mines with the Children of Echoes.
- Yumkasaurus (Dendro): green and bushy-tailed; swings on its tongue and spits objects from its cheek pouches. Lives in the southeastern forests with the Scions of the Canopy.
- Koholasaurus (Hydro): four-legged and finned; swims through water, liquid Phlogiston and spiritways. Lives in the southern springs with the People of the Springs.
- Qucusaurus (Pyro): red, feathered and winged; flies. Lives on the northwestern peaks with the Flower-Feather Clan.
- Iktomisaurus (Cryo): blue, furry and beaked; floats and glides, and alone can enter the "Spiritspeaker" state. Lives on the western cliffs with the Masters of the Night-Wind.
- Tatankasaurus (Electro): huge, horned, buffalo-like; charges through boulders. Lives in the southwestern volcanic lands with the Collective of Plenty.
### LARGE +
Each species' Archive entry repeats a legend of a greater ancestor. Koholasaurs descend from a sea dragon so vast it could be mistaken for an island. The first Tepetlisaur was a drake as large as a mountain whose sleeping back raised mountain ranges. The huge drill-shaped objects in the Children of Echoes' lands are said to be what is left of its horn. Early Yumkasaurs were tiny enough to drift on the wind and cling to giant dragons, and took their present shape only after their abilities declined. Qucusaurs' ancestors were winged dragons, and the greatest could blot out the sky. Iktomisaurs' ancestors had no physical body and drifted between reality and the spiritual realm. The Tatankasaur's ancestors could move mountains with their horns but lacked their descendants' agility.

Wild Saurians also live outside tribal lands: large flocks of Qucusaurs fly over Ochkanatlan and the Great Volcano of Tollan, with little contact with humans. Long-necked rhinos are not Saurians. A further species, the Stegosaurus, is known only from weapon lore: one named Muhuru fought beside the hero Sundjatta in the Cataclysm. Its appearance and abilities are unknown.

## nature
### STANDARD
Saurians have their own language, which other Saurians understand but humans do not; tribe members learn to read their tribe's Saurians from body language and mood. Cacucu, Ifa's Qucusaur companion, is the only Saurian known to say human words, a few phrases borrowed from Ifa. Saurians have fur; Iktomisaurs and Qucusaurs also have feathers.
### LARGE +
- Wayna calls them simple creatures that cannot easily tell right from wrong.
- Dragons from before the Pyro vishaps' decline can understand Saurians: Ajaw, one of the Thirteen Sovereign Lords, is one example. Cacucu's speech is limited by his vocal cords, and he sometimes translates for other Saurians.
- Lifespans differ. A Yumkasaur normally lives less than a century. The Yumkasaur Kongamato has lived since the Cataclysm, which characters in "A Hero's Rite" suspect is due to Abyssal power. The Iktomisaur Elder Tlapo has lived for several centuries and has been the Masters of the Night-Wind's elder for some two hundred years; he was once the companion of Huitzilin and watches over Citlali.
- Temperaments: Qucusaurs are proud. Tatankasaurs are usually docile, but once angered they cannot be reasoned with until their energy runs out. Iktomisaurs are often seen meditating alone or in small groups.
- Young Saurians are called whelps (Qucusaur and Iktomisaur young are chicks). Saurians eat a bit of everything, as Paimon remarks in "Lies and Promises".

## flamelords_blessing
@spoiler: major
@version: 5.5
### STANDARD
A few Saurians are born with a red mark, the Flamelord's Blessing, linked to the ancient Pyro dragon Xiuhcoatl. It gives power modern bodies cannot bear: marked Saurians are often killed or driven mad by it, and the Wayob and the Night Kingdom reject them. In 5.5 the Traveler helped one marked Tepetlisaur whelp, "Little One", through an old rite at the Great Volcano of Tollan, and his Blessing was removed (see sub-entry).
### LARGE +
Tradition offered one remedy: carry two Golden Entreaties to the Great Volcano with the Saurian's companion and wash away the Saurian's name as Xiuhcoatl remembers it. No tribal warrior was known to have succeeded, so the tribes abandoned the rite and usually care for a marked Saurian until it dies. According to the Natlan NPC Mposo, the Blessing appears every dozen or several dozen years, more often when the Abyss draws near. Some marked Saurians enter the volcano alone and are never seen again, and others turn violent and are killed by saurian-hunters.
### ULTRA +
In the world quest series ending with "An Omen of Annihilation and the Final Entreaty" (5.5), the rite's true purpose turns out to be different. Kukulkan, the dragon sage, had long guided how the Blessing was expressed so as to pass the power and wisdom of ancient dragons to a future dragon, and he devised the rite to bring the Golden Entreaties to the Huitzilopochtli array. In the canonical outcome, Little One refuses to activate the array to protect his friends, and the Blessing's warmth leaves his horn. The red mark itself stays.

## individuals
### STANDARD
- Little One: the Traveler's marked Tepetlisaur companion in the Natlan world story, called the "Chosen of Dragons" by Kukulkan. He later set off on his own adventures.
- Cacucu: Ifa's small pink Qucusaur assistant with an eyepatch.
### LARGE +
- Kongamato, the "Mountain King": an ancient, enormously fat Yumkasaur revered by the Scions of the Canopy. He fought beside the hero Burkina against the Black Tide during the Cataclysm, and in that war killed his partner in an Abyss-tainted rage.
- Holawaqa Ngoubou, the "Goldflame Qucusaur Tyrant": a Qucusaur with the Flamelord's Blessing. His companion Menilek, of Tenoch's alliance 500 years ago, failed in the pilgrimage to save him, and he survives as a "Tyrant".
- Heroes of the Cataclysm with named companions, from Talking Stick weapon lore: Sundjatta with the Stegosaurus Muhuru, Wanjiru with the Tatankasaur Kayeke, and Sanhaj with the Iktomisaur Mahamba, who had also gone with Sanhaj into the Night Kingdom.
- Elder Tlapo: an Iktomisaur several centuries old, elder of the Masters of the Night-Wind for some two hundred years, once Huitzilin's companion and close to Citlali.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay interpretation (this project's recommendation, not canon text): portray Saurians as intelligent animals with strong personalities, closer to loyal mounts or dogs than to talking dragons. Communicate through growls, posture and nudges, and let human companions interpret. Match species to setting: Tepetlisaurs in mines, Koholasaurs at springs, Qucusaurs on peaks.
### LARGE +
Do not make Saurians fluent speakers. Cacucu's handful of borrowed phrases is the exception, and a Saurian's thoughts become clear only through Saurian Indwelling. A Saurian wandering alone into another species' land is unusual. A red mark on a Saurian is serious and frightening to Natlanese, not a lucky charm. Do not treat the ancient dragons of Natlan, such as Kukulkan or Ajaw, as ordinary Saurians.

## origins
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
Saurians are what Natlan's dragons became. Natlan was once home to Natlantea, an advanced dragon civilisation, until the Heavenly Principles conquered it and humans took the dragons' place. The Pyro vishaps of the Great Volcano adapted to survive and lost their intelligence and power in the process. In his notes on the Tonatiuh in Ochkanatlan, Och-Kan calls this a "devolution": the vishaps who once thought themselves masters of evolution became beasts for humans to slaughter, and their descendants had lost the primal flame's purity, so the sovereign of fire would never again be born among them. Kukulkan's hidden Phlogiston replies blame the divine punishment that divided his race and cut off its evolution, and call evolution a mixed thing rather than a pure one.
### ULTRA +
The wiki pieces together a further link from scattered quest and NPC lines. The new world order counted Natlan's Phlogiston-rich land as part of the "Holy Soil" phenomenon, which helped dragons spread and made farming harder for humans, and divine nails were cast down to disrupt it. Treat this as a reconstruction, not a single canon statement.

## abilities
### LARGE
Each species is attuned to one element and moves in its own way, many drawing on Phlogiston, Natlan's primordial energy. The Traveler can project their spirit into adult Saurians ("Saurian Indwelling") to borrow these skills; Saurians tied to a quest can refuse it, and while indwelling the Traveler can talk with Saurians fluently.
### ULTRA +
Gameplay (not literal lore): all six species are common enemies in the "Natlan Saurians" group and resist their own element. Indwelled Saurians spend a Phlogiston meter on their skills. Only Koholasaurs can swim. Tatankasaurs can cross water while charging and break Pulverite. Qucusaurs can climb in flight, while Iktomisaurs can only glide downward. Yumkasaurs grapple and climb, but Tepetlisaurs climb better. The Asha of the Easybreeze Holiday Resort can also be indwelled, but they are a kind of Monetoo, not Saurians.

## relations
### LARGE
Ruins across Natlan are thought to have been left by even older Saurians, and the Saurian Relics Association studies objects dug from them, though its interests go well beyond Saurians. In Och-Kan's account, the decline of the Pyro vishaps means the next Pyro Sovereign will not be born among them; Kukulkan, by contrast, worked through the Flamelord's Blessing to make one Saurian heir to the old power.

## later_appearances
@version: 6.5
### LARGE
In Linnea's Story Quest "Tread the Right Path" (6.5), the Traveler takes Aguara, a young Tepetlisaur they are partnering in a Spirit Envoy contest in Nod-Krai, to Natlan for training. Little One secretly teaches Aguara overnight to form Geo maces, and the Traveler glimpses him on a nearby cliff but keeps his presence to themselves.

## entry: Flamelord's Blessing
@keys: Flamelord's Blessing, Flamelord Blessing
@spoiler: major
@version: 5.5
### STANDARD
The Flamelord's Blessing is a red mark some Saurians are born with, linked to the Pyro dragon Xiuhcoatl. It is an atavism: it carries the power of far older dragons, which modern Saurians cannot withstand. Marked Saurians grow powerful but often die or go mad, and the Wayob and the Night Kingdom reject them. The old cure was to bring two Golden Entreaties to the Great Volcano of Tollan and wash away the Saurian's name as Xiuhcoatl remembers it, but no one was known to have succeeded, so tribes now care for marked Saurians until they die.
### LARGE +
The Traveler and Little One completed the journey in 5.5 and learned that Kukulkan had shaped the Blessing and devised the rite for his own plans. Little One's Blessing was removed when he rejected Kukulkan's request; the red mark remains but no longer feels warm.

## entry: Saurian companions and territories
@keys: Saurian companion, saurian companions, Saurian territory, saurian territories
### STANDARD
Natlanese pair with Saurians, and the Saurian does the choosing: it will not partner with someone it does not acknowledge. Warriors often dress in the style of their Saurian. Each species keeps a home territory matching its tribe's (Tepetlisaurs with the Children of Echoes, Yumkasaurs with the Scions of the Canopy, Koholasaurs with the People of the Springs, Qucusaurs with the Flower-Feather Clan, Iktomisaurs with the Masters of the Night-Wind, Tatankasaurs with the Collective of Plenty). A Saurian crosses into another species' territory only by invitation and under escort.

## entry: Devolution of the Pyro vishaps
@keys: devolution, Pyro vishaps, Pyro Vishap
@keys2: Saurian, Saurians, Natlan, Natlantea
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
Natlan's dragons once had their own civilisation, Natlantea. After the Heavenly Principles conquered the Dragon Sovereigns, the Pyro vishaps of the Great Volcano adapted and lost their wisdom and power, becoming Saurians. Och-Kan's notes in Ochkanatlan call this devolution and say the sovereign of fire will never again be born among them. Kukulkan's hidden replies blame divine punishment for cutting off dragon evolution, and he looked to humanity, and later to a single marked Saurian, for new possibilities.
