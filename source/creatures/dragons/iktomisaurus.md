## summary
### LITE
Iktomisaurs are the Cryo Saurians of Natlan: blue, furry, beaked, bat-like dragons of the western cliffs, living beside the Masters of the Night-Wind. They float and glide, are often seen meditating, and are the only Saurians said to have grasped the "Spiritspeaker" state. They are animals and do not speak.
### STANDARD +
The Archive describes Saurians able to use their plumage for leaping. They first appeared as common enemies in 5.2.

## nature
### STANDARD
Wiki descriptions give a large, fuzzy, blue body with a beak, large ears and wings that are separate from the arms; Iktomisaurs are stated to have feathers. Their young are called chicks. They can float and make themselves as light as a feather, so a rider feels no weight (per the character Chikya in a quest). They are frequently seen meditating alone or in small groups, and meditating wild ones ignore a player unless attacked.
### LARGE +
Legend gives them an odd past. According to the Archive entry, their ancestors had no physical form and drifted like spirits between reality and the spiritual realm, appearing through the darkness of night. Some also say the first Iktomisaur to leave the Night Kingdom was not a true Saurian but a distinct race. Both claims are told as hearsay and are unconfirmed.
### ULTRA +
Iktomisaurs can live long: the Iktomisaur Elder Tlapo is a bit over two centuries old, grew up alongside Huitzilin and Citlali, and is the elder of the Masters of the Night-Wind.

## society
### LITE
Iktomisaurs live on the cliffs of western Natlan, in the Tezcatepetonco Range, alongside the Masters of the Night-Wind.
### STANDARD +
Like all Saurians, an Iktomisaur chooses its human partner. In the tale of Kuntur, told in a limited-time event (Search Chronicle: For Childhood Dreams!), Kuntur was shunned by humans and Saurians alike until a great deed won him an Iktomisaur comrade.
### LARGE +
Natlan's Saurian territories are respected between species; an Iktomisaur crosses into another's land only by invitation and escort.

## abilities
### STANDARD
They float and glide, can enter the Spiritspeaker state to store and move certain objects and attributes, and attack at long range with Cryo.
### LARGE +
Gameplay (not literal lore): Iktomisaurs are common enemies of the "Natlan Saurians" group resisting Cryo; they can rise on a self-made updraft, fire cold projectiles and mark circles of frost blasts. When indwelled they can only glide downward, unlike Qucusaurs, which can change altitude.
### ULTRA +
A Local Legend variant, "Spirit of the Fallen Dawnstar", appears in the Tezcatepetonco Range as a Nightsoul Totem challenge; in that fight it lights torches, flies up and shields itself in ice with a freezing storm (gameplay only).

## individuals
### STANDARD
- Elder Tlapo: an Iktomisaur a bit over two centuries old, elder of the Masters of the Night-Wind and close to Citlali.
### LARGE +
- Mahamba: named by the wiki as the Iktomisaur companion of the hero Sanhaj, who went into the Night Kingdom with him and later joined him in the Cataclysm, per Calamity of Eshu and Talking Stick weapon lore (not read directly here).

## relations
### STANDARD
Their tribe is the Masters of the Night-Wind. For the Flamelord's Blessing, the vishap devolution and the other Saurian species, see the Saurian group entry.
### LARGE +
Marked Saurians, those with the Flamelord's Blessing, are rejected by the Wayob and the Night Kingdom, which bears on the Night Kingdom's role in Iktomisaur legend only in that both appear in the same sources; canon draws no explicit link.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay interpretation (this project's recommendation, not canon text): portray an Iktomisaur as a quiet, light, watchful animal, often sitting still, drifting a little above the ground when it moves. It does not talk; its tribe members read its mood.
