## summary
### LITE
Teyvat's recorded past runs in ordered eras. Before the Heavenly Principles came, dragons led by the Seven Sovereigns held the world. The Heavenly Principles and their four Shades defeated them and made a world for humanity. Around 6,000 years ago a second war ended with the Celestial Nails cast down to purge Abyssal corruption. Gods then fought over seven divine seats in the Archon War (6,000 to 2,000 years ago), which ended with the first Seven. About 500 years ago the Cataclysm destroyed Khaenri'ah and let the Abyss spill across the world. The Traveler's story begins after that: the twins fall into Teyvat, one is sealed for 500 years, and the Archon Quests follow the search region by region up to Snezhnaya in Version 7.1. Dates before the Archon War are approximate and partly reconstructed by editors.
### STANDARD +
Use this entry for "what came before what" and for rough "how long ago". For depth on any era or nation, defer to the regional history entries and the lore entries for the Archon War, the Cataclysm and the fall of Khaenri'ah. Order of eras: Old World, When the Doves Held Branches, Unified Civilization, War of Funerary Flame, Interim Period, Archon War, Post-Archon War, Pre-Cataclysm, Cataclysm, Post-Cataclysm, Recent Past, then the story-present.

## eras_overview
### LITE
Old World; When the Doves Held Branches; Unified Civilization; War of Funerary Flame (about 6,000 years ago); Interim Period; Archon War (6,000 to 2,000 years ago); Post-Archon War (2,000 to 500 years ago); Pre-Cataclysm; Cataclysm (500 years ago); Post-Cataclysm (500 to 100 years ago); Recent Past (under 100 years ago); the present (the Archon Quests).
### STANDARD +
Round figures the wiki gives: the Archon War runs "6000 to 2000 years ago"; the Post-Archon War period runs 2,000 to 500 years ago, across which the original Cryo, Dendro, Electro, Hydro and Pyro Archons gradually died out; the Cataclysm is 500 years ago; the Post-Cataclysm period is 500 to 100 years ago; the Recent Past is less than 100 years ago. Mondstadt's own refounding by Barbatos is placed about 2,600 years ago in this archive's Mondstadt entry. Spans overlap: the end of the War of Funerary Flame blends into the start of the Archon War, and the wiki's Archon War section itself includes events after the Celestial Nails fell.

## old_world
@version: 1.0
### ULTRA
In the Old World the elemental beings came from the Light Realm (also called the Vishap Realm), where dragons and vishaps dominated, led by the Seven Sovereigns under Nibelung. Each Sovereign held one elemental Authority and was the strongest dragon of that element, and their lands were attuned to it. Nibelung raised the Three Moons from the planet and then left Teyvat, setting the three Moon Goddesses to govern in his absence. The voyager Anacharsis sensed Nibelung's presence, warned him that the world would be destroyed and urged him to leave with her; he refused to abandon his people and instead tried to lead the dragons to the stars, which spurred their advanced space technology. Dragon pioneers on the Frost Moon received a distant signal; the Eye of Ibni-Belum was built to recapture it, and later Project Moongazer was launched to send warnings to other civilisations while research began on cloaking Teyvat from the universe.
Several of these details (Anacharsis, the Frost Moon signal, the cloaking research, Project Moongazer and the Lunar Veil) are assembled by the wiki from Ancient Moon, Nod-Krai and Hyperborea material. Treat the sequence as ordered but not dated.

## heavenly_principles_arrive
### LARGE
The Heavenly Principles descended on Teyvat and took a portion of the Seven Sovereigns' elemental Authorities, creating four Shades of themselves to fight: Naberius, Ruler of Life; Ronova, Ruler of Death; Istaroth, Ruler of Time; and Asmoday, Ruler of Space. The dragons' Lunar Veil, meant to hide the Frost Moon, was destroyed before it was finished and Project Moongazer was suspended. The cloaking research was surrendered to the Heavenly Principles, which is implied to be the basis of the False Sky wrapped around Teyvat, isolating it from the wider universe. About 40 years after their arrival the Heavenly Principles and Shades won, the Sovereigns' subjects fled to the oceans, and the heavens began creating the earth and the Human Realm for humanity and took control of the three moons.
### ULTRA +
The 40-year figure and the False Sky link are the wiki editors' deductions from item text and later revelations. In the Sumeru Archon Quest (Version 3.2) Dottore traded information about the False Sky to Nahida in exchange for the Dendro Gnosis.

## unified_civilization
### LARGE
Around 400 years after the Heavenly Principles appeared, the world was ready for humanity. The heavens (with Naberius) made animal life and humanity entered a covenant with them, beginning a sacred plan sometimes called the "Ark's Opening". Artifact texts (the Prayer sets) describe a world once covered in raw ice, flame, lightning and water, which envoys of the gods made hospitable; humans could hear Celestia directly, white Irminsul trees tied to the ley lines were central to ritual, and divine envoys, including the Shades, lived among people. Enkanomiyans remember this as the Year of Jubilee. The world was cyclical: humanity would flourish, end, and be renewed, and the envoys stayed silent when asked how long prosperity would last. Chief priests wearing Irminsul-branch crowns went to the depths of the world for answers. Eventually humanity grew proud and challenged the gods' authority, angering Celestia. During this time the Angels, a wise humanoid race, guided humanity and built palaces across the world. Regional footholds include Arcadia and Elysium in Mondstadt and Sal Vindagnyr on Dragonspine.
### ULTRA +
These events happened at an unknown time before roughly 6,000 years ago; the wiki cites Morax's birth as a loose upper bound. It marks the section as a rough reconstruction.

## war_of_funerary_flame
@version: 4.0
### LARGE
After the dragons' defeat, Anacharsis returned to Teyvat to find Nibelung missing and the Heavenly Principles in charge. She cast her consciousness into the body of a boy in Hyperborea; Koitar fell in love with her and they swore an oath to rebel. Nibelung found Abyssal power and brought it to Teyvat, starting the War of Funerary Flame, and turned the imprisoned Three Moons into weapons. The Heavenly Principles broke the Eternal and Iridescent Moons and threw the Frost Moon beyond the False Sky. Around 6,000 years ago the Heavenly Principles won and cast down the Celestial Nails to purify Teyvat of Abyssal corruption, forbade knowledge of what came before, and punished the survivors: Koitar lost her memories, and the remaining Angels became the small spirit-like Seelie that guide travellers to Seelie Courts.
### ULTRA +
The wiki ties the Three Moons' deaths and the wolves' mourning to this period. Two links are its own inference: that a surviving Angel befriended the wolf-king (possibly Boreas), and that a survivor heavily implied to be Nabu Malikata later befriended Rukkhadevata and King Deshret.
The Nod-Krai arc (Song of the Welkin Moon, from its 5.8 Prelude and Version Luna I, numbered 6.0) showed more of the Three Moons' history. Khaenri'ah's wiki history places its foundation as a godless nation around the time of Sal Vindagnyr's fall, about 6,000 years ago.

## interim_period
### ULTRA
The Interim Period is not an officially named era. Liyue lore points to a stretch of relative peace between the Great War of Vengeance and the Archon War. Regional footholds include Old Mondstadt, the Guili Assembly, the Lisha civilisations and Chenyu Vale in Liyue, plus early civilisation in Sumeru and Fontaine. In Mondstadt the wiki places Boreas arriving from Nod-Krai and blizzards turning the region into a wasteland, which the wind spirit later known as Barbatos heard.
Because the period is deduced, not named in-game, treat any mention of "during the peace" as a loose gesture and prefer vague phrasing in roleplay.

## archon_war
@availability: permanent
### ULTRA
About 6,000 years ago seven divine seats in Celestia opened and gods began a bitter struggle over them. Many fought for their ideals of a prosperous humanity, and other creatures expanded their own domains in the chaos. Regional strands include the Age of Kings in Mondstadt, the fall of the Guili Assembly in Liyue, Inazuma's Archon War, Sumeru's Ay-Khanoum, Dharma Forest, King Deshret's civilisation and Gurabad, and conflicts in Fontaine, Snezhnaya and Khaenri'ah. Natlan did not take part and was instead consumed by fighting among dragons; about 2,000 years ago Xbalanque and his army invaded the Sacred City of Tollan. The war ended about 2,000 years ago when the final seat was claimed and the original Seven were fixed. Defeated gods who refused the Seven's rule fled to islands in the Dark Sea and became evil gods. After Barbatos visited Morax in Liyue, the first Seven would often gather in Liyue Harbor.
Two undated folk tales (the Thundersoother and the Berserker) are listed by the wiki under unknown regions; canon gives no "years ago" for them, so they stay undated here.

## post_archon_war
### ULTRA
From 2,000 to 500 years ago the original Seven's tenure unfolded. The original Cryo, Dendro, Electro, Hydro and Pyro Archons died out over roughly 1,500 years, leaving Barbatos and Morax as the remnants of the original Seven. They were succeeded by the Tsaritsa, the Lesser Lord Kusanali, Ei, Focalors and Mavuika respectively, though it is unknown how many times those seats changed hands. Kusanali is the youngest, born after the Cataclysm; Mavuika is a human born before it and reborn more recently. Some newer Archons no longer honour the first Seven's oath to guide humanity. Mondstadt passed through an aristocratic period before being reborn.
Natlan notes from the wiki's history: about 1,000 years ago a civil war exiled the Muratans, some of whom settled in Mondstadt, and a Pyro Archon named Cochanina drove the Volcano Lord from the People of the Springs.

## cataclysm
@version: 5.7
### LITE
About 500 years ago the Kingdom of Khaenri'ah was destroyed by the Heavenly Principles and Abyssal monsters invaded the surface world: the Cataclysm.
### STANDARD
The Cataclysm began when the Abyss invaded the surface world and devastated much of Teyvat. Teyvat's people blame one of the Five Sinners of Khaenri'ah, "Gold" Rhinedottir, whose Alfisol were the monsters' vanguard. The Kingdom of Khaenri'ah was destroyed by the Heavenly Principles, ending the Eclipse Dynasty and its civilisation and sending ripples across the world; the Seven became involved after the Abyss invaded. Natlan suffered more than any other region because its ley lines were weakened, and the Night Warden Wars began there. Hilichurl numbers rose sharply as Khaenri'ahns of foreign ancestry were turned into hilichurls as punishment, while pure-blooded Khaenri'ahns were cursed with immortality and slow erosion.
### LARGE +
The Traveler's story overlaps this time. The twins fell into Teyvat while travelling between worlds and were separated; the sibling woke first and was named heir and "Savior" by King Irmin. Chapter V Act VI (Version 5.7) showed the sibling abandoning Khaenri'ah during the disaster after the Khaenri'ahn girl Direidyth showed them the key to the Traveler's hibernation chamber, which she had obtained from 500 years in the future through Istaroth's intervention; the sibling then went back and woke the Traveler. The twins then tried to leave Teyvat and fought Asmoday, who sealed the Traveler for 500 years.
### ULTRA +
In Fontaine, Egeria died and Focalors took over 500 years ago, and about 499 years before Chapter IV Focalors split her divinity from her body, leaving Furina (revealed in Chapter IV Act V). The wiki's Natlan history puts the Ochkanatlan Adventure Team's doomed search for the Legendary Tonatiuh about 490 years ago.

## post_cataclysm
### LARGE
During the 500 to 100 years ago span the Traveler's sibling woke again and travelled with Dainsleif until meeting Chlothar Alberich in Sumeru, who was trying to cure his hilichurl-cursed son Caribert. After witnessing the miracle granted by the Sinner Vedrfolnir, which temporarily brought Caribert back to sentience, Chlothar declared the founding of the Abyss Order (more than 100 years ago); the sibling later parted from Dainsleif and joined the Order (Chapter III Act VI, Version 3.5). Rhinedottir created Albedo after Khaenri'ah's destruction. About 400 years ago came the Tatarasuna incident, in which Dottore, posing as the Fontaine mechanic Escher, tampered with the Mikage Furnace and murdered Niwa (as rewritten by Inversion of Genesis, Version 3.3). The wiki also places Kuutar of the Frostmoon Scions joining the Fatui as Columbina, the Third Harbinger, about 400 to 350 years ago.
### ULTRA +
The wiki puts the sibling taking leadership of the Abyss Order roughly 100 years after its founding, but this is inferred. Also in this span, Pierro co-founded the Fatui with the Tsaritsa, and Thrain, a Khaenri'ahn who fled to Natlan with his platoon not long after the Cataclysm and fought the Abyss there, later joined as the First Harbinger, Capitano. (Flins, by contrast, was born 600 to 700 years ago, before the Cataclysm.)

## recent_past
### ULTRA
The last 100 years holds events the wiki orders by characters' apparent ages rather than dates, for example the fall of the Raiden Gokaden about 100 years ago (the Wanderer's revenge), Thoma gaining his Vision about 10 years ago, and the collapse of the Kaedehara Clan some years ago. Fontaine's Callas affair, about 3 years ago, leads into Chapter IV. In Mondstadt, Crepus's death and the resignation of Amber's grandfather from the Knights are about four years before the story-present.
The wiki also lists Collei's birth with the Eleazar disease in Sumeru, the Seahook Gang of Nasha Town disbanding at most two decades ago, and Bennett surviving the deaths of his parents' adventuring team in the Mare Jivari before it vanished about 20 to 30 years ago. Order within this era is guesswork and should not be stated as fact.

## story_present
@version: 1.0
### STANDARD
The Traveler arrives in Teyvat in Mondstadt and follows the Archon Quests: Prologue (Mondstadt), Chapter I (Liyue), Chapter II (Inazuma), Chapter III (Sumeru), Chapter IV (Fontaine), Chapter V (Natlan), the Song of the Welkin Moon arc (Nod-Krai) and Chapter VII (Snezhnaya, Versions 7.0 and 7.1).
### LARGE +
Chapter V was released across Versions 5.0 to 5.7 and the Song of the Welkin Moon arc began with a Prelude in 5.8 and continued through the Luna versions. Chapter VII Acts I and II came in 7.0 and Acts III and IV in 7.1. Interlude Chapter quests were released between main chapters: Acts I and II in Liyue (2.4 and 2.7), Act III "Inversion of Genesis" (3.3) and Act IV "Paralogism" (5.6). A numbered Chapter VI is not used; the Nod-Krai Song of the Welkin Moon arc fills that slot.
### ULTRA +
By Version 7.1 the Traveler is in Snezhnaya, an anonymous member of the Rokot (joined in Chapter VII Act I) who holds the Natlan Ancient Name Tumaini; the Tsaritsa has called them a formidable adversary worthy of respect and says she looks forward to working with them. ## accounts
### ULTRA
Different sources frame the same history differently. The Heavenly Principles forbade knowledge of what came before the War of Funerary Flame, so most mortal myths are second hand. Celestia-aligned voices tell a story of purification and protection, Khaenri'ahn and Abyss-aligned voices tell of divine cruelty, and Dottore and Pierro claim secret knowledge. The wiki's editors add their own deductions and mark them as such.
For roleplay, a character should usually give only the era-slice they could plausibly know: Mondstadt folk know the Archon War through legend; Sumeru's scholars know more but from censored sources; Fatui and Khaenri'ahn survivors know most, with bias.
