## summary
### LITE
Snezhnaya's history runs from the golden city of Hyperborea (destroyed by a Celestial Nail about 6,000 years ago) through the Pale Star Court of the Belyi Tsar, first Cryo Archon, who ruled until he fell fighting the Abyss in the Cataclysm 500 years ago. Anastasya then became Tsaritsa, broke the fae aristocracy, founded the Fatui with Pierro, and let Nod-Krai become an autonomous land guarded by the Lightkeepers. As of 7.1 she holds six Gnoses and is waging a hidden war on Celestia.
### STANDARD +
Use this entry for "what came before what" in Snezhnaya and Nod-Krai. Order: Hyperborea and the first fae; the Pale Star Court (Kitezh, Belyi Tsar); the Abyssal gate and Anastasya's erasure and return; the Belyi Tsar as Cryo Archon and the pioneering age of Nod-Krai; the Cataclysm; the Tsaritsa's accession and reforms; the Fatui and Harbingers; Nod-Krai in the Song of the Welkin Moon; and 7.0-7.1. Absolute dates are mostly round figures from item texts and are partly wiki reconstruction.
### LARGE +
Canon and the wiki disagree or stay silent on several early dates (see uncertainties); where a number is given it is attributed. Nod-Krai's history is told here because the wiki has no separate page for it.

## hyperborea
@version: 6.0
### STANDARD
Over 6,000 years ago, in the Unified Civilization era, the Golden City of Hyperborea stood in ancient Snezhnaya, then a warm, green land. It drew on the power of the Three Moons. According to artifact texts, its people angered the angels by questioning the progress of knowledge; the voyager Anacharsis, living in the guise of a Hyperborean boy, was sent before the first angel Koitar, and the two became lovers who resolved to oppose the Heavenly Principles. Hyperborean artisans made the Snowland Fae using forbidden methods Koitar taught.
### LARGE +
About thirty years after Koitar and Anacharsis met, a Celestial Nail destroyed Hyperborea, around the War of Funerary Flame. Only Canon, goddess of the Frost Moon, took pity on the survivors and led them to Nod-Krai, where they settled on Hiisi Island and called themselves the Frostmoon Scions. Over centuries the Scions kept to their faith and ran a bloodline project to produce a sacred heir for their goddess. A fallen remnant of a fourth moon that never fully formed became the land of Nod-Krai, steeped in kuuvahki.
### ULTRA +
Hyperborean experiments also tried to create a Primordial Human from the remains of the Cryo Dragon; the Dragon's released power afflicted the land with Holy Soil and froze Snezhnaya, and, per the wiki, turned its Ley Lines into Kresnik Kristall veins. Hyperborea's ruins lie within the Shadow Realm. Most of the Hyperborean content was revealed in the Nod-Krai arc (6.x) and item texts; the pieces are assembled by wiki editors.

## saarelainen_and_anastasya
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
Over 6,000 years ago the Third Descender Saarelainen came from the sea of stars to ancient Snezhnaya and entered a relationship with Anastasya; they had a son, and their descendants became the Khaenri'ahns. Thousands of years later, as a herald of the Belyi Tsar's Pale Star Court, Anastasya and Saarelainen were incited by the Tsar to open a gate to the Abyss (the Hymns of the Far North place it on Pohjola). The Abyss began seeping into the world and the Heavenly Principles erased Anastasya.
### LARGE +
Saarelainen ascended to Celestia and offered his own remains, which were split into the seven Gnoses, in return for Anastasya's revival and a promise never to harm their descendants. Anastasya was revived, but all memory of him was removed through Irminsul. The Belyi Tsar, having fled into the Shadow Realm, kept the memory and later told her, though she never fully trusted him. She then began recording her days in a memory prism immune to Irminsul changes.
### ULTRA +
The wiki dates the gate episode loosely to between 3,200 and 2,000 years ago, an editors' inference from her service to the Tsar. These events were revealed in the Version 7.0 Archon Quest material and the Version 7.0 special program.

## pale_star_court
@version: 6.0
### STANDARD
The Belyi Tsar (Monomakh Snezhnyi) is said to have emerged around 5,000 years ago from the Cryo Dragon's remains and to have believed himself a fae. He founded Kitezh, the first city of Snezhnaya and capital of the Pale Star Court, and about 3,000 years ago won the war against King Miscislav of the Volkodlak Alliance by killing him and two fae Grand Dukes with a thrown spear. All fae clans then swore fealty and he took the title Belyi Tsar. Snezhnaya was an imperial aristocracy led by the Snowland Fae, living in luxury.
### LARGE +
Kitezh remained the capital for about 1,000 years. Around 2,000 years ago, after exploring Hyperborea's ruins through the Shadow Realm and learning he was an incomplete Primordial Human, he accepted Celestia's offer to become Cryo Archon, opened his realm to humans, moved the court to Snezhnograd and renamed the nation Snezhnaya. His court sat at Zapolyarny Palace. He attended the gatherings of the first Seven in Liyue when the Archon War ended.
### ULTRA +
He also pursued the Hyperboreans' aim of completing his ascension and overthrowing the Heavenly Principles, supported by the Hvergelmir institute, and raised the infant Aksinya, whom he found in a time capsule. Folk tales (a twin brother, a final immortal enemy) are legends the wiki doubts. Sources are chiefly the books A Brief History of the Pale Starborn and Notes by a Certain Noble Individual.

## pioneering_age_of_nod_krai
@version: 6.0
### LARGE
More than 500 years ago the Belyi Tsar ordered the borderland of Nod-Krai explored and developed in the "pioneering age": forests were cleared for cities, ports and factories. A Khaenri'ahn exile, Rauthvangi, arrived a few years in, took the name Professor Rotwang, and became a favourite at court for his alchemy and astrology. Per item texts, the Tsar began plotting a revolt the "sublunary order" could not tolerate; Rauthvangi pushed him to aim higher, toward what lay beyond the false sky, and acted as his envoy to Nod-Krai on a secret errand for a hidden treasure. Many fae nobles distrusted him as the "mad monk."
### ULTRA +
Meanwhile Lovia, last Archpriestess of the Frostmoon Scions, bred "pure" heirs; a maiden fled with a Snezhnayan officer and bore Avrelian, later called Reed Miller, who grew up as a friend of Solovei. After the maiden's death, Lovia raised twins Alia and Aila in seclusion and forced them to drink their kin's blood. This is told in weapon and artifact texts and the wiki's reading of them.

## cataclysm_in_the_north
@version: 6.0
### STANDARD
In the Cataclysm 500 years ago, Nod-Krai was the first land corrupted by the Abyss, which appeared as the Wild Hunt. Neither the royal court nor any god defended it, and Abyssal monsters razed it. The Belyi Tsar fell in a battle against the Abyss during his expedition. Solovei, called the first Torchforger, and Aila, the Scions' first Moonchanter, turned the tide with the moons' power, and Solovei and his allies founded the Lightkeepers, swearing to defend Nod-Krai.
### LARGE +
Aila gave Solovei the Arrow of Terpikeraunas, a Frostmoon relic that dispels the Abyss. Aila then reformed the Scions, cutting references to Hyperborea and the heir project in favour of nature, kindness to outsiders and the coming New Moon, and a new Frost Moon goddess, Kuutar, was born on Hiisi Island afterward. The Lightkeepers later pledged fidelity to the Tsaritsa. Reed Miller became a famed thief and founded the Treasure Hoarders on the dream of an "Elysium" for the poor; whether his execution succeeded is disputed.

## tsaritsas_accession
@version: 1.0
### STANDARD
Anastasya succeeded the Belyi Tsar during the Cataclysm and was crowned Tsaritsa of All Snezhnaya. Some fae nobles, furious a non-fae ruled, refused to recognise her; the Dalegrad clan broke ties with Snezhnograd and Flins left in self-imposed exile. She was left bitter and cut ties with other Archons, including Barbatos (reason unclear). She approved Solovei's request to make Nod-Krai an autonomous region under the Lightkeepers by Edict.
### LARGE +
In the second year of her reign the humans of Port Belovodye, exploiting the succession crisis, declared self-governance; Grand Duke Drevogorye failed to put it down. In the third year she founded the Fatui with Pierro as its first member and sent them to retake the port. The Fatui seized lands of several fae clans (Drevogorye, Volkhovan, Cirnareka) for negligence; Drevogorye accused her of treason and died fighting. The rebels were offered clemency for handing over instigators and accepted.
### ULTRA +
She then demanded absent fae princes give up their titles, had the Fatui build the fortress Glupov, and replaced fae aristocratic rule with the Snezhnograd Veche, first headed by the human I. Ivanovna N. In her seventh year the Imperial Order Reformation Edict led many princes to renounce titles and leave; many settled in Novokitezhgrad, rebuilt after Kitezhgrad was destroyed by the Abyss. The fae lost most of their political influence. The year numbers come from the wiki's Anastasya page.

## fatui_and_the_harbingers
@version: 1.0
### LARGE
The wiki timeline places Pierro, a former Khaenri'ahn court mage, in a meeting with the Tsaritsa 500 years ago, after which they founded the Fatui and its Harbingers with Pierro as Director. Over five centuries the pair recruited the Eleven Harbingers; the Fatui came to run most of Snezhnaya's government and army. Snezhnaya became the most militarily and technologically advanced nation. Around 400 to 350 years ago the Frost Moon goddess Kuutar joined as Third Harbinger "Columbina," and later fulfilled a promise to the Tsaritsa that used up most of her power. More than a decade ago Anastasya pardoned House of the Hearth orphan Peruere, who became Arlecchino; Ajax became the youngest Harbinger, Tartaglia, afterward (see his own entry).
### ULTRA +
Shortly before the Traveler's journey the Tsaritsa launched her plan to collect the Gnoses, which Pierro explained to the Harbingers. After Signora's death in Inazuma, a funeral was held at the Sanctuary of Grief. Individual Harbinger histories belong to their own entries.

## nod_krai_in_the_welkin_moon_arc
@version: 6.0
### LARGE
A few months before the Traveler reached Nod-Krai, the Fatui pulled fragments of the Eternal Moon from the sky; the two largest fell on Paha Isle and Hiisi Island, and Sandrone and Dottore stayed at the Kuuvahki Experimental Design Bureau built on Paha. Columbina, disillusioned, fled to Hiisi Island, and the Tsaritsa issued the Palestar Edict, her highest command, to bring her back. Lauma, the current Moonchanter, sheltered her at Silvermoon Hall. Dottore defied the Tsaritsa's will in a bid to seize the power of the Three Moons. After he was dealt with, the Palestar Edict was rescinded and the Tsaritsa questioned Pantalone and Pulcinella over the affair. The Frost Moon returned to Teyvat's sky; as of 7.1 it remains. This arc is Version 6.x content (Song of the Welkin Moon and its Archon Quest chapters).

## snezhnaya_in_7_0_and_7_1
@version: 7.0
### LARGE
By 7.0 the Tsaritsa was overseeing Project Stuzha, an energy-saving policy tied to the secret Project Mistilteinn, an anti-Celestia weapon. Before the Traveler arrived she campaigned east against a monster causing endless snowstorms, saw Asmoday with a hooded figure, and killed the beast. In 7.1 she ended investigations of the Traveler, explained her aims and appointed them Consultant to the Tsaritsa. In 7.1 she used her six Gnoses to slow time around the Shade Rhinedottir, so the revival of the victims of Ronova's curse of death was delayed past Celestia's monitoring cycle; Celestia revoked Ronova's authority for abuse, and Pierro mortally wounded her with his new Delusion, the "Eye of the Fatui". Project Mistilteinn, stalled by the curse, resumed. The dying Ronova fused the Eitr with the Nail of Retribution hovering over the Cryo Dragon's corpse, which began to crumble as she disintegrated; the quest frames this as her attempt to let Holy Soil spread across Teyvat, and Celestial Nails everywhere glitched and sent pillars of light into the sky.
### ULTRA +
Pierro was left depleted after using the Eye of the Fatui; Arlecchino's Crimson Moon lineage served as the base for that Delusion, and in return Pierro agreed the House of the Hearth would no longer answer to the Fatui or train children as spies; corruption purges based on lists from Pulcinella and the House of the Hearth are under way. Whether these promises are carried out is not yet shown. Status is as of 7.1; nothing later is covered.

## entry: Hyperborea
@keys: Hyperborea, Hyperborean, Golden City of Hyperborea, Frostmoon Scions origin
@version: 6.0
### STANDARD
Hyperborea was the golden civilization of ancient Snezhnaya, over 6,000 years ago, that powered its magic with the Three Moons' tides. It created the Snowland Fae with Koitar's forbidden methods, and was destroyed by a Celestial Nail. Survivors fled to Hiisi Island and became the Frostmoon Scions.

## entry: Pale Star Court and Kitezh
@keys: Pale Star Court, Kitezh, Kitezhgrad, Monomakh Snezhnyi
@version: 6.0
### STANDARD
The Pale Star Court was the fae empire of the Belyi Tsar (Monomakh Snezhnyi), centred on Kitezh, the first city of Snezhnaya. Kitezhgrad was later destroyed by the Abyss and the survivors founded Novokitezhgrad. After the Tsar became Cryo Archon, the court moved to Snezhnograd and the nation was renamed Snezhnaya.

## entry: Pioneering Age
@keys: pioneering age, Rotwang, Professor Rotwang
@keys2: Nod-Krai, Belyi Tsar, Rauthvangi
@version: 6.0
### STANDARD
The Belyi Tsar's exploration and industrial development of Nod-Krai over 500 years ago. The Khaenri'ahn exile Rauthvangi came to court as "Rotwang" and encouraged the Tsar's rebellion against the Heavenly Principles.

## entry: Founding of the Lightkeepers
@keys: Lightkeepers founding, first Torchforger, Arrow of Terpikeraunas, Pillar of Embla
@version: 6.0
### STANDARD
After the Cataclysm Solovei, the first Torchforger, and Aila used the moons' power against the Wild Hunt, and Solovei founded the Lightkeepers. The Tsaritsa's Edict approved their jurisdiction and Nod-Krai's autonomy. Aila gave Solovei the Arrow of Terpikeraunas. Their insignia is a nightingale holding a burning wick; headquarters are at Piramida.
### LARGE +
Accounts differ on who raised the first beam of light, Aila at the Pillar of Embla or Solovei with Aila's borrowed power. Their oath is sworn by every member on joining.

## entry: Reed Miller and the Treasure Hoarders
@keys: Reed Miller, Avrelian, Treasure Hoarders founder
@version: 6.0
### STANDARD
Reed Miller, born Avrelian to a Frostmoon maiden and a Snezhnayan officer, became an infamous thief across Teyvat who dreamed of an Elysium for the poor and founded the Treasure Hoarders. The royal court sentenced him to death; whether he died is disputed between item texts.

## entry: Tsaritsa's Accession and the Fae Princes
@keys: Drevogorye, Imperial Order Reformation Edict, Port Belovodye rebellion, Dalegrad clan
@version: 1.0
### STANDARD
Early in her reign Anastasya crushed fae opposition: the Port Belovodye rebellion, the seizure of Drevogorye's lands, the Veche replacing fae rule, and the Imperial Order Reformation Edict of her seventh year, after which many princes renounced their titles. Many settled in Novokitezhgrad.

## entry: Founding of the Fatui
@keys: Fatui founding, founding of the Fatui, Pierro first member
@keys2: Tsaritsa, Anastasya
@version: 1.0
### STANDARD
The Fatui were founded about 500 years ago by the Tsaritsa with Pierro, who served as its first member and Director. Their first campaign retook Port Belovodye. Individual Harbingers are covered in their own entries.

## entry: Palestar Edict
@keys: Palestar Edict
