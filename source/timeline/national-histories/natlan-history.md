## summary
### LITE
Natlan's history in order: a dragon civilisation (Natlantea) under the Pyro Dragon Sovereign Xiuhcoatl; its fall about 2,000 years ago to Xbalanque, the first mortal Pyro Archon; the Eternal Empire of the half-dragon tyrant Och-Kan and his overthrow; and the Cataclysm 500 years ago, when the Abyss invaded and the Night Warden Wars began. They ended in Version 5.3 when the Traveler and Mavuika purged the Night Kingdom and Capitano gave his life.
### STANDARD +
Natlan never joined the Archon War; dragon infighting filled that age. Its distinct feature is a throne won by mortals: Pyro Archons are humans who prove themselves in battle. Use this entry for sequence and cause; use the linked entries for people, tribes and places.

## old_world
@version: 5.0
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Xiuhcoatl was born from the primal flame as the Pyro Dragon Sovereign and, the same day, split off a portion of that flame to create his younger brother Kukulkan. He hollowed out a space inside the Great Volcano of Tollan, founding the Sacred City of Tollan, and made the Thirteen Sovereign Lords, artificial intelligences, to govern Natlantea. The Legendary Tonatiuh was built to anchor a "cable" at Chichen Uctokah that leads beyond Teyvat's sky.
### LARGE +
The Heavenly Principles later took Xiuhcoatl's Pyro Authority and left him frozen in lava in a state of false death. During his absence Kukulkan acted as Regent of the dragon civilisation. In the war of funerary flame, which ravaged Natlan and damaged its ley lines, Xiuhcoatl woke when Nibelung returned to Teyvat. Kukulkan and the Lords tried to repeat the miracle of moon forging to reach worlds beyond Teyvat; Xiuhcoatl stopped them.
### ULTRA +
About 6,000 years ago Yohualtecuhtin, Lord of the Night, rebuilt Natlan's ley lines as the Night Kingdom under the guidance of Ronova, and a Celestial Nail fell into the Night Kingdom. The wiki's note says all Celestial Nails are implied to have fallen at the same time, when the Heavenly Principles won the great war, so the 6,000-year figure is an inference. The dragon civilisation then reclaimed Natlan from a collapsed human one.

## ancient_dragon_era
@version: 5.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
From roughly 6,000 to 2,000 years ago the dragons of Natlantea ruled and humans were subordinate. Natlan took no part in the Archon War, because in-fighting among dragons absorbed everything; as a result its modern people know little about that war. Natlantea slowly declined and Kukulkan foresaw its end. He gave Phlogiston to the "primitive" humans and fathered Och-Kan, half dragon and half human, to unite the two peoples and rise to a new throne. Near the end of this age the tribes were founded, worshipping the Wayob (ancestors) and the Night Kingdom.
### LARGE +
Kukulkan's scheme was discovered; the Thirteen Lords and the Three Princes tried to impeach him, but the trial halted when Xiuhcoatl appeared. Most of Natlan's angels were "devoured" by the dragons, according to item text. Xiuhcoatl also modified one of his pupils to serve as a control hub for Huitzilopochtli. The Sumeru fungus god Bifrons travelled to Natlan to recover and was buried with his attendant Chatraka by a volcanic eruption at the Great Volcano of Tollan.
### ULTRA +
About 2,000 years ago the human twins Ixquieh and Ahpub sealed Aj Imuch' Paxlaq Lahun, the Tenth Lord, in a wristband and buried it in a ruin. Other Natlan material makes Ixquieh the first bearer of the Ancient Name Baraka and founder of the Children of Echoes, and Ahpub founder of the Collective of Plenty; this entry only notes their link to the sealing.

## xbalanque_and_the_first_pyro_archon
@version: 5.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
About 2,000 years ago Xbalanque and his army invaded the Sacred City of Tollan. He killed Xiuhcoatl and became the first Pyro Archon of Natlan, ending the civilisation of Natlantea. The Three Princes and ten of the Thirteen Lords died in the fighting.
### LARGE +
Xbalanque and Kukulkan each took one of Xiuhcoatl's eyes, the Golden Entreaty, swearing to keep peace between humans and dragons. Xbalanque borrowed Ronova's power to set up rules by which mortal humans can ascend as the Pyro Archon, and the Ode of Resurrection. Chichen Uctokah, the territory of the Ninth Lord Ixlel, fell to ruins at about this time.
### ULTRA +
Ronova's price for this power is the thread that pays off at the war's end (see end_of_the_war): she kept a promise to Xbalanque of one last day among the living once Natlan had defeated the Abyss.

## ochkanatlan_and_och_kan
@version: 5.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
About 2,000 years ago followers of Xbalanque founded Ochkanatlan, the Eternal Empire. After Xbalanque entered the Sacred Flame, Och-Kan became king. The seven tribes formed the Grand Alliance and put their authority in his hands. Och-Kan betrayed Xbalanque's commandments and became a tyrant: he sought to wipe out the dragons and their descendants, the Saurians, mistreated his people and banned ancestor worship and the Night Kingdom.
### LARGE +
Och-Kan built Ochkanatlan on the remains of Chichen Uctokah. Ixlel first helped him, but when she resisted he trapped her in the Old Core of Chu'ulel and made a new Core of Chu'ulel from his own draconic half, one he could fully control. The Scarlet-Eyed Youth, who sympathised with the Saurians, was banished by his elder in an attempt to help him escape capture. In exile he ascended as the second Pyro Archon and gathered those who opposed Och-Kan.
### ULTRA +
Och-Kan was overthrown and Ochkanatlan became abandoned and "cursed". He survived by separating mind and body: his mind sat in the Core of Chu'ulel, and his body became the Dragon of the City of Flowing Ash. Sovereignty returned to the six tribes, who reconnected with the Night Kingdom and made peace with the Saurians. The wiki here says seven tribes joined the Alliance but six regained sovereignty, and does not explain the difference (see uncertainties).

## post_archon_war
@version: 5.0
@spoiler: minor
### LARGE
Later Pyro Archons held tournaments between tribes. More than 1,600 years ago (editors' estimate) an unnamed female Pyro Archon, whom Venti knew, held martial arts contests, as the manga prologue shows. More than 1,000 years ago a civil war in Natlan split the Muratans: some left Natlan and eventually settled in Mondstadt, and the rest were forced to live apart at Tenochtzitoc, where the Easybreeze Holiday Resort now stands. Also over 1,000 years ago the Pyro Archon Cochanina drove the Volcano Lord out of the People of the Springs. About 500 years ago Mavuika was born, before the Cataclysm.
### ULTRA +
The name 'Murata' for the unnamed Archon comes from the English manga and is probably a place or people name; the Chinese original does not name her. The date of the Muratan exile is inferred from a character's remark that they lived in neglect for millennia until darkness returned.

## cataclysm_and_night_warden_wars
@version: 5.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Five hundred years ago the Abyss invaded, and Natlan, with weakened ley lines, suffered more than any other region. This began the Night Warden Wars. Great infighting among the tribes occurred as well. Bakunawa, a creature made by Rhinedottir, entered Tenochtzitoc and killed almost all the Muratans there except Tenoch, who had been exiled before the disaster. Several Pyro Archons (Cochanina, Shulevitz, Gloharry and Isaja) worked together to contain it; the first three died doing so and Isaja contained it.
### LARGE +
Tenoch, known for his giant obsidian club, recruited allies from each of the six major tribes to defend Natlan against a "turbid black tide" (the Abyss) and they became the Heroes of Natlan. Mavuika ascended as Pyro Archon and joined the party. Their battle site became the Mare Jivari. Traore led the Children of Echoes against an Abyssal invasion in their territory, and with the Khaenri'ahn warriors Zephaniah and Irizar trapped the Abyss deep underground using the Shadow Pins.
### ULTRA +
Around 490 years ago the Ochkanatlan Adventure Team led by Taika went to the ruins in search of the Legendary Tonatiuh, rumoured to hold the key to beating the Abyss. It was a trap set by Kukulkan, who killed almost all of them and took their souls. Taika's daughter Bona followed, freed Och-Kan's mind from the Core of Chu'ulel and named it Cocouik; she met the same fate as her father at the Tonatiuh. By the war's end the tribes were decimated and their bonds with the Wayob nearly severed, and the Abyss rooted itself permanently in the Night Kingdom.

## mavuikas_plan
@version: 5.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Mavuika's plan was that the tribes would spend 500 years regaining their strength, after which the Wayob would choose six new heroes to drive the Abyss out for good. She sacrificed her life to the Sacred Flame so that she could reawaken 500 years later to oversee it. Because of long exposure to the Abyss, Natlanese people had to remain within their borders, where the Wayob could protect them.
### LARGE +
Her sister Hine helped rebuild the Scions of the Canopy. Not long after the Cataclysm, Capitano (then called Thrain) and his platoon fled Khaenri'ah to Natlan and helped the tribes fight the Abyss for a time. Menilek tried to remove the Flamelord's Blessing from his Saurian companion Holawaqa Ngoubou, and failed.

## recent_past
@version: 5.0
@spoiler: minor
### LARGE
Under 100 years ago, Bennett's parents' adventuring team wandered into the Mare Jivari and died there; Bennett survived and an adventurer took him to Mondstadt. Some 20 to 30 years ago visitors reported a mountain-sized dark creature in the Mare Jivari, which was then said to have "disappeared", blown off Teyvat by Barbatos, with several departed souls unable to pass on. A Pilgrimage of the Return of the Sacred Flame was held with Vichama, Mallko, Pisac and others; an Abyss attack on the eve of a Night Warden War wounded Vichama, and the rest lost their Ancient Names for good.
### ULTRA +
Mualani won three Night Warden Wars without needing the Ode of Resurrection. Chasca was abandoned as a child because of a rare disease, raised by a qucusaur named Chimpu, found by Allpa and adopted by Chuychu's family. Kinich found Ajaw in a ruin and made a contract with him, and was revived by a Dendro Vision after dying in a Night Warden War. By then three of six tribes (Kinich, Iansan, Xilonen) had heroes ready. The wiki dates Capitano's arrival in Natlan with his subordinates to Version 3.1 (around Mondstadt's Weinlesefest). His subordinates, including the Polychrome Tri-Stars, captured Saurians for Phlogiston experiments until the Traveler shut them down, as the permanent hidden exploration objective at the Phlogiston Extraction Research Center on Coatepec Mountain shows.

## present_day_5_0_to_5_3
@version: 5.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
In Version 5.0 a Pilgrimage was held and Kachina beat Mualani in the finals; she, Chasca, Iansan and others fought in a Night Warden War, but the Ode of Resurrection did not revive Kachina when she fell. Mavuika suspended the Pilgrimage and the wars. With Vichama's help Kachina's Ancient Name was recovered, and the Traveler, Chasca, Iansan and Mualani entered the Night Kingdom and rescued her, then were trapped until Mavuika freed them by giving up keepsakes people had offered her in exchange for Contending Fire. Mualani was acknowledged by her Ancient Name, leaving only the Flower-Feather Clan and the Masters of the Night-Wind without heroes.
### LARGE +
In 5.1 the party learned Capitano and Ororon intended to rewrite the ley lines, which would erase Natlan's history, and tried to stop them. The plan failed in its intended form but awoke Yohualtecuhtin, whose power destabilised Ororon's damaged soul and let the Khaenri'ahn Guthred possess him; Ororon regained control and was acknowledged as an Ancient Name bearer, and Capitano agreed to work with Mavuika. A nationwide Abyss invasion followed that cost thousands of lives and briefly put out the Sacred Flame. Chuychu died of Abyssal contamination, and Chasca, after her grief, was acknowledged by her Ancient Name as the sixth hero. The six channelled Ronova's power into Mavuika, who drove the Abyss back deep into the Night Kingdom.
### ULTRA +
In 5.2 the Traveler and Paimon went to Ochkanatlan, met Cocouik and Bona's spirit, and reached the Tonatiuh, where Kukulkan admitted luring souls with its legend for centuries. The Dragon of the City of Flowing Ash took the Golden Entreaty; in the Nursery of Nightmares Cocouik merged with the dragon, showing it to be Och-Kan, whom the Traveler defeated, taking the Golden Entreaty. The Traveler also freed Ixlel from the Old Core of Chu'ulel at her request.

## end_of_the_war
@version: 5.3
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
In 5.3 Xilonen forged the Traveler an Ancient Name, and the Traveler and Mavuika entered the corroded Night Kingdom to purge Gosoythoth's influence. With Yohualtecuhtin's blessing the Traveler gained Pyro power. Gosoythoth became the Lord of Eroded Primal Fire and seemed to win, but the support of the Natlanese restored the Traveler and Mavuika, and they defeated it, ending the Night Warden Wars.
### LARGE +
It then emerged that Ronova had demanded Mavuika's death as payment for the power lent to her. Mavuika meant to use her life force to repair the Night Kingdom, but Capitano took her place: as a Khaenri'ahn cursed with immortality, he forced Ronova to choose between keeping his deathless "life" (denying her death) or lifting the curse on his people. She upheld the curse, and Capitano gave his life for the Night Kingdom.
### ULTRA +
With the war over, the Natlanese may leave the nation freely and the Pilgrimage became a sporting competition. Ronova kept her promise to Xbalanque and let him return to the living for one last day; Mavuika and the Traveler showed him the Natlan his sacrifice made possible, and he left smiling.

## status
### LITE
As of Version 7.1 the Abyss war is over; Mavuika survived its end, while Capitano gave his life and merged with the Lord of the Night, his body left on the Throne of the Primal Fire. The wiki's Natlan history records no later events.

## open_questions
### STANDARD
How the seven ancient tribes became six, who all the Pyro Archons were and when they ruled, and the precise dating of events before the Cataclysm are not settled by canon (see uncertainties).

## entry: Ochkanatlan Adventure Team and Bona
@keys: Taika, Bona, Cocouik, Ochkanatlan Adventure Team
@version: 5.2
### STANDARD
The Ochkanatlan Adventure Team under Taika went to the ruins of Ochkanatlan about 490 years ago seeking the Legendary Tonatiuh, hoping it would help against the Abyss. Kukulkan had laid a trap and took nearly everyone's souls. Taika's daughter Bona later found Och-Kan's mind in the Core of Chu'ulel, named it Cocouik and travelled with it to the Tonatiuh, where she died too.

## entry: Tenoch and the Heroes of Natlan
@keys: Tenoch, Heroes of Natlan, Talking Stick
@version: 5.0
### STANDARD
Tenoch was an exile from Tenochtzitoc who survived Bakunawa. He gathered fighters from the six tribes to defend Natlan from the Abyss; they are the Heroes of Natlan, and Mavuika joined them as a new Pyro Archon. The Mare Jivari is the site of their battle.

## entry: Pyro Archons Who Contained Bakunawa
@keys: Cochanina, Shulevitz, Gloharry, Isaja, Bakunawa
@version: 5.0
### STANDARD
When Bakunawa destroyed Tenochtzitoc, four Pyro Archons (Cochanina, Shulevitz, Gloharry and Isaja) united to restrain it. Three died in the effort; Isaja held it. Pyro Archons are mortal, so their deaths pass the title on.

## entry: Night Warden Wars
@keys: Night Warden Wars, Night Warden War
@version: 5.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The Night Warden Wars were the long war against the Abyss in the Night Kingdom, beginning with the Cataclysm 500 years ago. The Pilgrimage selected warriors to fight them, and the Ode of Resurrection revived the fallen. They ended in Version 5.3.
