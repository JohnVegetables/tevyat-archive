## summary
### LITE
Liyue's recorded past, in order. Lang-Gan on the western coast was destroyed by a Celestial Nail about 6,000 years ago and its survivors went to Chenyu Vale. Morax and the Goddess of Dust Guizhong then founded the Guili Assembly, which was ruined in an Archon War battle about 3,700 years ago. Morax led the survivors south of Mt. Tianheng to found Liyue Harbor, waged war on rival gods, and became the Geo Archon more than 2,000 years ago. Adepti and Yakshas guarded the land, the Cataclysm sent monsters out of the Chasm about 500 years ago, and the Liyue Qixing now govern in public. Most dates are approximate.
### STANDARD +
Use this entry for "what came before what" in Liyue. Fine detail on the Archon War, Cataclysm and the Unified Civilization belongs to their own entries, and the individual places and people have their own entries.

## lang_gan_and_chenyu_vale
@version: 6.3
### STANDARD
The wiki places the civilisation of Lang-Gan in what is now the Chasm, part of the unified civilisation of Teyvat, with the angel Zibai as Celestia's divine envoy. About 6,000 years ago, while Morax was still young, the Heavenly Principles sent a Celestial Nail down on Lang-Gan to cleanse Abyssal corruption. Zibai held the Nail back for a time so that the people could flee, and died doing so. Lang-Gan was destroyed, the impact created the Chasm, and the Nail's remains lie deep beneath it as the Spiritstone. The survivors settled in Chenyu Vale.
### LARGE +
Istaroth is said to have preserved Zibai's soul by freezing her at the moment of death and placing its shattered pieces in her Three Deadly Selves. One alternative account in the wiki credits the Chasm to the fall of a Solar Chariot instead of a Nail. Whether that chariot existed, or how it relates to the Nail, is unknown.
### ULTRA +
The wiki says Zibai later became one of the adepti. Her restoration is recorded in the entry on Zibai's soul below.

## guili_assembly
### STANDARD
In a period of peace before the Archon War, Morax descended on the land, raised Mt. Tianheng, calmed the seas and defeated sea monsters such as Baqiu. Guizhong, the Lord of Dust, befriended him, and together they founded the Guili Assembly between Mt. Tianheng and the Stone Gate. Its name takes the first character of Guizhong's name and one character of the name Morax then used (the name itself is unknown). It prospered through farming, mining and trade. Guizhong set four commandments for her people, about unity, wisdom, a supple body and growing virtue. She and the Cloud Retainer, among other adepti, built the Guizhong Ballistas to defend it. The wiki says the sources disagree about which god came first.
### LARGE +
Its fall is dated about 3,700 years ago. One battle of the Archon War ravaged Guili Plains and set off landslides that turned Dihua Marsh from a fertile islet into a swamp, and Guizhong was killed. The cause is not stated and folklore holds several theories. More than ten days later Morax, Marchosius and the adepti led the people south of Mt. Tianheng to found Liyue Harbor.

## lisha_and_other_ancient_sites
### LARGE
The Lisha region had early civilisations at Dunyu Ruins, Lingju Pass and Qingxu Pool. Those at Lingju Pass and Qingxu Pool were submerged, either before or during the Archon War, and emerged when the long war ended and the tides withdrew. Local legend says the ruins predate Geo Archon rule and were left by an evil god whose name has been erased. A separate legend tells of a Sea God who traded a year free of storms for a human bride; a village was destroyed after one such bride called on him to stop her daughter being taken. The wiki notes it is unknown whether the Sea God was real or tied to Lisha.
### ULTRA +
Elsewhere, the god Havria set up her domain, which included Sal Terrae, and the village at Wuwang Hill was founded. Both are covered below. These stories come from legends and in-game notes; the wiki gives no years.

## archon_war_morax
### STANDARD
After the Guili Assembly fell, Liyue Harbor was founded about 3,700 years ago. Morax created the Millelith to guard the people against monsters, taught people to build houses using Mora as a model, set minted Mora as the currency and made a contract with the adepti to defend the harbor. He then decided to make war on the other gods, as the only way to restore order and protect humanity. The adepti and Yakshas fought for him. Over the next centuries, by the wiki's tentative ordering, he beat many gods and sea monsters. He became the Geo Archon, ending the war in Liyue, more than 2,000 years ago.
### LARGE +
Notable episodes in the wiki: at Guyun Stone Forest he pinned many gods and monsters, including Osial, to the seabed with huge stone spears and sealed them with Sigils of Permission. Around 2,600 years ago he subdued the beast Chi at Mt. Qingce and sealed its remains in five places. When a rival god slashed Mt. Tianheng, the adeptus Skybracer propped it up with his antlers, a gift made of Morax's divine essence, and died from blood loss; the Bishui River is said to come from his blood. More than 2,000 years ago Morax took pity on the blind dragon Azhdaha, gave him sight and made a contract that he would not endanger humans. The wiki's order of these events is tentative.
### ULTRA +
Xiao is said to have been born about 4,000 years ago and enslaved by an evil god who used his weakness. Morax defeated that god in the Archon War and named him Xiao; Xiao became a Yaksha to repay the debt, with the call sign Alatus. Ganyu, born on December 2 (year not stated), signed a contract with Morax to protect the harbor. The wiki also relays a story of the early war in which she accidentally choked a monster that tried to eat her. Stories in which Chi's body became the land around Mt. Qingce are legends; the wiki says Zhongli calls them products of imagination.

## havria_and_sal_terrae
### LARGE
Havria, the God of Salt, was gentle but weak, and she tried to settle the conflicts peacefully. She lost territory until only Sal Terrae remained, where she sheltered displaced humans. The wiki says her followers, seeing no chance she could survive, gave her a swift, painless end. The power released at her death turned the city and its people to salt. Survivors scattered; many came to Liyue Harbor and became Yinyuan Hall, part of the Eight Trades, which handles the salt trade. Some descendants entered Sal Terrae to break the sword used. The wiki says that Yinyuan Hall's story has changed: they now believe Havria was a powerful god murdered by Morax out of envy.

## yakshas_and_the_adepti
### LARGE
The bodies of the gods and monsters Morax defeated became part of the earth, but their hatred and resentment lingered as miasmas, curses and monsters. Morax summoned the Yakshas, a group of the adepti, to suppress them. The five strongest were Bosacius, Bonanus, Menogias, Indarias and Alatus (Xiao). Pervases was a lesser Yaksha who died in the Archon War. After the war some adepti withdrew to Jueyun Karst and others asked Morax to turn their bodies to stone, producing statues around Liyue. The Yakshas suffered: tainted by hatred and the karmic debt of unending slaughter, they went mad, died or disappeared one by one, leaving Xiao as the only known Yaksha.
### ULTRA +
Karmic disease: the wiki says an unknown doctor led efforts against the pestilence from the dead gods' corpses. The Rite of Homa, cremation, stopped the spread, and its first practitioners founded what is now Wangsheng Funeral Parlor. After the war the first Seven met at Liyue Harbor, after Barbatos visited Morax out of curiosity about a neighbouring land. The Liyue Qixing were set up around 2,000 years ago by the wiki's dating, and Ganyu became their secretary.

## post_archon_war_events
### LARGE
Other episodes the wiki places in the centuries after the war, with vague dates. A wandering fighter named Guhua became a famous figure, perhaps an adeptus, and the Guhua Brotherhood honoured him. Qiqi, a herb gatherer, was caught up in a fight between adepti and demons and her wish to live gave her a Vision. The adepti shared their energy to save her but turned her into a zombie who went berserk, so Mountain Shaper trapped her in amber for several hundred years.
### ULTRA +
More than 1,000 years ago Azhdaha began to forget those around him and grew irritable. Around 1,000 years ago, mining disturbed the ley lines that nourish him; in pain and unable to recognise loved ones, he attacked the Chasm and destroyed the settlement at Tianqiu Valley. Morax, Mountain Shaper, Moon Carver and a third, unnamed adeptus sealed him in a pocket dimension at Nantianmen.

## cataclysm_in_liyue
### LARGE
About 500 years ago Morax signed a contract agreeing not to disclose what was about to happen. Of the five Yakshas only Bosacius and Alatus remained; Bosacius went mad and later wandered to the Chasm. During the Cataclysm an iron meteorite struck the Chasm, monsters poured out, and Morax sent the Millelith to defend and evacuate the miners. Bosacius, the mortal thaumaturge Boyang and the Millelith vanguard sealed the beasts into the depths using the Fantastic Compass, which needed a mortal and an adeptus for constant energy, and all died. Survivors were few. Rongzhao, Boyang's brother, went mad; a Millelith who lost all four limbs later became a famed doctor and set up a memorial. What occurred at Dunyu Ruins is unknown; its people left for Liyue Harbor, and the wiki guesses the Chasm's monsters or Abyssal influence were the cause.
### ULTRA +
Afterwards, a martial arts renaissance grew as people sought ways to fight the monsters still appearing. Yun Hui of the Yun family of blacksmiths and the craftsman Han Wu produced the weapon designs known as the prototypes, and their children Yun Huang and Han Ce made the Blackcliff series.

## recent_past
### LARGE
The Recent Past is under 100 years, ordered by the wiki by apparent age. About 50 to 40 years ago, Amber's grandfather, a Liyue mercenary leader, lost his companions in a monster attack and moved to Mondstadt, where he joined the Knights of Favonius and founded the Outriders. About 30 years ago, the Cloud Retainer last took human form before moving to Liyue Harbor. About ten years ago, Hu Tao's grandfather and father died in a failed Art of Homa, and she became the 77th director of Wangsheng Funeral Parlor at 13; she received her Pyro Vision after visiting Wuwang Hill. Wuwang Hill's inhabitants left for Liyue Harbor after a disaster 20 to 40 years ago. Qiqi was found in amber, taken for burial and escaped, then was hired by Baizhu.
### ULTRA +
More than two years before the Liyue Archon Quests, the Liyue Qixing sealed the Chasm with the Seven-Star Array after accidents, a harmful artefact and strange earthquakes, though some miners stayed on. Treasure Hoarders also dug there illegally. Shortly afterwards La Signora signed trade agreements with the Qixing, under which the Fatui would send an advance team and share results. The Wangshu Inn was built at Dihua Marsh by the Qixing to covertly support Xiao. Shortly before the Liyue Archon Quests, Tartaglia obtained a Sigil of Permission by collecting a debt from the owner of Yanshang Teahouse; Yelan later took the teahouse over.

## liyue_archon_quest_present
@version: 1.0
### LARGE
The wiki's Liyue history ends with the Recent Past; the Liyue Archon Quests that follow are covered in the Liyue nation and quest entries. In brief, at the Rite of Descension the Traveler saw the exuvia of the Geo Archon fall, which proved to be Morax's own staging. He gave his Gnosis to the Tsaritsa's envoy Signora under contract, after which the Liyue Qixing governed. Morax lives on as Zhongli.

## entry: Guili Assembly (History)
@keys: Guili Assembly, Guili Plains, Guizhong Ballista, Guizhong Ballistas
@version: 1.0
### STANDARD
The Guili Assembly was an ancient Liyue civilisation founded by Morax and the Lord of Dust Guizhong between Mt. Tianheng and the Stone Gate. It was prosperous, a trading hub with strong engineering, and was protected by the adepti and the Guizhong Ballistas. A battle of the Archon War about 3,700 years ago destroyed it and killed Guizhong; the survivors went south to found Liyue Harbor.

## entry: Lang-Gan (History)
@keys: Lang-Gan, Langgan
@version: 6.3
### STANDARD
Lang-Gan was an ancient civilisation on the western coast of Liyue, in the area that became the Chasm, governed by the angel Zibai. A Celestial Nail destroyed it about 6,000 years ago; Zibai died holding it back so her people could flee. Survivors went to Chenyu Vale.

@keys: Zibai, White Horse Adeptus

During the Lantern Rite after the True Moon act of the Song of the Welkin Moon, the Traveler and Zhongli reunited the three fragments of Zibai's divided soul and restored her original form, according to the wiki. This was a limited-time Lantern Rite storyline (A Lanternlit Ode to the Silver Moon, Version 6.3) and is not common knowledge in Liyue.

## entry: Havria and Sal Terrae
@keys: Havria, God of Salt
@version: 1.0
### STANDARD
Havria was a gentle, militarily weak god of salt who lost her lands in the Archon War until only Sal Terrae remained. Her followers ended her life painlessly, and the power released turned the city to salt. Yinyuan Hall's descendants now misremember her as killed by Morax.

## entry: Sea God of Lisha
@keys: Sea God, Lisha legend, Qingxu Pool
@keys2: Lisha, bride, Liyue
@version: 1.0
### STANDARD
A Liyue legend of a Sea God who traded a year without storms for a human bride. After a bride asked for his help when her daughter was to be taken, he destroyed her village. Whether he existed, or relates to the ruins at Qingxu Pool, is unknown.

## entry: Yakshas of Liyue
@keys: Yaksha, Yakshas, Bosacius, Bonanus, Menogias, Indarias
@version: 1.0
### STANDARD
Yakshas were adepti summoned by Morax to suppress the lingering hatred of defeated gods. The five strongest were Bosacius, Bonanus, Menogias, Indarias and Alatus (Xiao). Karma, madness and the Cataclysm left Xiao the only known Yaksha.

## entry: Azhdaha's Fall
@keys: Azhdaha, Tianqiu Valley, Nantianmen
@version: 1.0
### STANDARD
The Geo dragon Azhdaha, once blind and healed by Morax, lost his memory and, about 1,000 years ago, rampaged after mining disturbed the ley lines. Morax and the adepti sealed him in a pocket dimension at Nantianmen.

## entry: The Chasm Cataclysm
@keys: Fantastic Compass, Boyang, Chasm Cataclysm
@version: 1.0
### STANDARD
In the Cataclysm a meteorite struck the Chasm and monsters poured out. Bosacius, the thaumaturge Boyang and the Millelith vanguard used the Fantastic Compass to seal the beasts at the cost of their lives.

## entry: Wangsheng Funeral Parlor Origins
@keys: Rite of Homa, Art of Homa, Wangsheng Funeral Parlor
@keys2: history, origin, karmic, cremation
@version: 1.0
### STANDARD
The Rite of Homa began as cremation to stop a karmic plague spread by the corpses of slain gods. Its pioneers founded Wangsheng Funeral Parlor, whose family motto is that only an unbound flame can purify the world.
