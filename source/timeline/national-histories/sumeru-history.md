## summary
### LITE
Sumeru's past runs from the dragon Apep's forest and the first Celestial Nail's burying of it, through the three God-Kings (Nabu Malikata, King Deshret and Greater Lord Rukkhadevata), Deshret's fall to forbidden knowledge and the desert's age of city-states, to the cataclysm 500 years ago, when Rukkhadevata was replaced by Lesser Lord Kusanali (Nahida) and the sages came to rule in her name. Chapter III (3.0-3.2) freed Nahida; in 6.6 she burned Irminsul to end Dottore's attack, and Aaru took over its work.
### STANDARD +
Use this entry for the order of events and rough "how long ago". Canon seldom gives years for pre-cataclysm Sumeru. The rainforest and the desert have separate histories (Rukkhadevata's and Deshret's) that were later tangled and distorted by the Akademiya's misinformation.
### LARGE +
The periods used below follow the wiki: Old World, the Heavenly Principles, the Unified Civilization, the War of Funerary Flame, the Archon War (Ay-Khanoum, Dharma Forest, King Deshret's civilization, Gurabad), the Post-Archon War age of kingdoms, the Pre-Cataclysm, the Cataclysm, the Post-Cataclysm, the Recent Past and the Present Day.

## old_world_to_war
@version: 3.0
### STANDARD
In the Old World the Dendro Dragon Sovereign Apep ruled the land that became Sumeru and nursed ancient Dendro lifeforms. Irminsul first grew there as a fern in her court. The Heavenly Principles took Apep's Dendro Authority and remade Irminsul into the device that records Teyvat's information and keeps the cycle of erosion running. In the Unified Civilization the ancient city of Ys stood in what is now the Sumeru desert, and Rukkhadevata, Irminsul's avatar, already existed.
### LARGE +
Apep joined Nibelung in the War of Funerary Flame against the Heavenly Principles. Nabu Malikata survived the downfall of the Angels. About 6,000 years ago the first Celestial Nail fell on Sumeru, buried the rainforest under the Great Red Sand and destroyed Ys. The wiki says the nail was used to combat the forbidden knowledge Apep had accumulated.
### ULTRA +
The wiki ties Mt. Damavand and the permanent storm around the Desert of Hadramaveth to the nail's impact, and legend says Deshret later found the nail. The timing of all the Celestial Nails right after the Funerary Flame is the wiki's inference from the Chasm's creation 6,000 years ago.

## three_god_kings
@version: 3.0
### STANDARD
During the Archon War Nabu Malikata, a surviving Angel later called the Goddess of Flowers, befriended King Deshret and Greater Lord Rukkhadevata. The three became known as the Three Eternal Companion-Gods of Sumeru and each held a share of the realm: the banquet, authority and life. Nabu Malikata and Deshret built Ay-Khanoum, the City of Amphitheaters, whose name means "City of the Moon Maiden" in the Jinn tongue. Deshret defeated many gods, among them Heryshaf, Sobek and Thoth, who entered his service as the Seven Pillars; gods who refused, such as Remus, went wandering.
### LARGE +
Nabu Malikata is said to have created the Jinn and Khvarena. Foreseeing the cataclysm she made a shard of Khvarena, called her daughter, and gave it to Rukkhadevata, who later turned it into the divine bird Simurgh and charged her with guarding the border between birth and death. Nabu Malikata warned Deshret of Celestia's wrath but agreed to help him seek forbidden knowledge. Rukkhadevata refused and left. Nabu Malikata used her lingering link to Celestia to grant Deshret divine knowledge and was "burned to ashes by the flames of wrath" for it. The wiki suggests she knew she would die and wanted the bitterness of her death to preserve her legacy, which Deshret never realised.
### ULTRA +
Her remains were scattered; Deshret buried what he found in the Eternal Oasis, and some fragments were lost. The Jinn were told she was only asleep and served him in exchange for help finding her. The Orchard of Pairidaeza was made as her cemetery and Gurabad was charged to protect it. Ay-Khanoum was then lost to sandstorms and abandoned. Dottore later gathered the missing fragments, and one ended up in Collei. Whether Deshret was "a son of the sky" is told by legend, but he seems to have been born after the Angels fell.

## dharma_forest
@version: 3.0
### LARGE
After parting from Deshret, Rukkhadevata went east of the Great Red Sand and used the Varuna Contraption to turn part of the desert into rainforest. The Wall of Samiel was raised at the border. The forest's first lord, the tiger Viaghara, was born from moonlight on the water. With followers who founded the Amurta Darshan she grew the forest, and Sumeru City was built on the Divine Tree. She also created the Aranara.
### ULTRA +
Her devotees became the Sumeru Akademiya's scholars, who helped her govern. These events are assumed by the wiki to be roughly concurrent with Deshret's kingdom and Gurabad.

## deshret_and_gurabad
@version: 3.0
### STANDARD
Deshret ruled the desert alone after Nabu Malikata's death, having made a deal with Apep that it would consume him after his death to take his knowledge. He chose vassal kings and priests, with the Jinn as messengers. The shepherd Ormazd founded Gurabad with help from the Jinni Liloupar, whom he married. Liloupar felt betrayed by his later tyranny and spent generations arranging the city's ruin. The folk book The Tale of Shiruyeh and Shirin blames Shirin and Shiruyeh; Liloupar's memories show otherwise. Deshret was furious on learning of it and split her soul into seven pieces, each guarded by a sage.
### LARGE +
The book's version: Liloupar's son Kisra was raised by a great bird and adopted by Ormazd as Parvez. Shirin, under Liloupar's influence, poisoned Ormazd and his 300 heirs; Kisra took the crown as Parvezravan. Their son Shiruyeh was banished, returned with Liloupar's help, and Gurabad was destroyed by the power of the abyss with the king turned into a monster. Shirin and Shiruyeh fell into an abyss, where Liloupar says they remain.
### ULTRA +
The Eremites remember Shiruyeh as a coward and a traitor. Deshret's people built the Primal Constructs and the Khaj-Nisut mechanism, meant to carry people into a "golden slumber".

## fall_of_deshret
@version: 3.1
### STANDARD
Deshret's research released forbidden knowledge. People heard whispers, dark scales (Eleazar) spread over them and the land withered. He asked Rukkhadevata for help. She came back to the desert, had priests build temples she filled with the power of life, and created the Akasha System to unite people's wisdom against the poison. The priest Kasala's record says the embers of Deshret's civilisation were preserved in Aaru Village, though other in-game accounts place the village's founding centuries later under the Old Blind Poet. This exhausted her and shrank her to a child's form. It was not enough, and Deshret sacrificed himself to end it. His civilisation broke into short-lived city-states.
### LARGE +
Apep consumed his body, as agreed, and was corrupted by the knowledge. Rukkhadevata later promised one of Apep's creatures to erase the "apocalypse" and the two made the Fire Seed to cure Apep, though it was unstable. The Valuka Shuna, once Deshret's subjects, were given refuge in the rainforest. Rukkhadevata turned the Khvarena shard into Simurgh after the city-state age ended. The Traveler learns this in Chapter III (3.1).
### ULTRA +
Many people long thought Rukkhadevata and Deshret fought and that she betrayed him, a story kept alive by the Akademiya. The record of Deshret's priest Kasala, left in a device at his grave and projected by Alhaitham in Secret of the Scorching Desert, showed her as the desert's saviour.

## post_deshret_kingdoms
### LARGE
Between about 2,000 and 500 years ago desert history is told through weapon texts and wall inscriptions. Shah Ramshahr was credited with building seven towers (the "Towers of Betrayal") raised when forbidden knowledge was purged, and seven cities, among them a rebuilt Ay-Khanoum, a rebuilt Gurabad, Saleh and Tulaytullah, grew around them. Ramshahr's son Artaxir died young. The regent Turan Khagan was executed. Power passed among the free states.
### ULTRA +
Later, kings such as Humayun Gurkan of Tulaytullah, the Khagan Garshasp, and the rebuilt Gurabad's king Saleb Dowleh (born Goudarz, who answered a riddle of Thoth) ruled in turn. Saleb Dowleh was betrayed and killed by Muzaffar, a Dey from Port Ormos, who then built his city elsewhere. Muzaffar's son Zumurud had 99 children, and under them Sumeru had a golden age. The tale of Xiphos and the Jinni Makhaira, whom Thoth also questioned, covers the fall of Tulaytullah; Makhaira was killed by the prince Faramarz. The Old Blind Poet, Xiphos's master, took desert folk to Aaru Village to await Deshret's return. The realms were eventually buried by sand. Thoth's three questions (what causes rise and fall, what obliterates love and hate, what determines truth and falsehood) were attempted in turn by Goudarz, Makhaira and, in 6.6, Nefer, whose answer was the intended one and freed Thoth, Sobek and Heryshaf from Aaru. Xiphos's end is told in two incompatible versions, and the wiki doubts the mercenary-duel one.

## pre_cataclysm
### STANDARD
Slightly more than 500 years ago, Rosalyne-Kruzchka Lohefalter (later the Fatui Harbinger Signora) came from Mondstadt to study at the Sumeru Akademiya.

## cataclysm_in_sumeru
@version: 3.0
### STANDARD
Five hundred years ago Rukkhadevata was not summoned to Khaenri'ah like the other Archons, because she was to protect Irminsul. Forbidden knowledge contaminated Irminsul and, since she was its avatar, her too. She used the Akasha System to borrow her people's wisdom and purge it, but could not erase herself, so she created Lesser Lord Kusanali from the purest branch of Irminsul to be her successor. She then left on a final journey and never returned. The forest's king Viaghara died defending the forest.
### LARGE +
The Withering (the Black Tide) spread through both forest and desert. The Aranara saved the forest with help from the Traveler's sibling, whom they call Nara Varuna. The huntress Viridescent, after her duty, was eroded by the monsters' black blood and killed by the Bloodstained Knight.
### ULTRA +
The wiki suggests Rukkhadevata died before Simurgh finished her sacrifice. The phrase "Let the world completely forget me" is the start of the trail she left for Nahida.

## girdle_of_the_sands
@version: 3.6
### LARGE
The Gate of Everlasting Mourning was the centre of the Abyssal invasion. Monsters tore a great rift at Tunigi Hollow, where the Hydro Archon Egeria fell and her body became the Amrita of the Vourukasha Oasis. Rukkhadevata grew the Harvisptokhm from it to anchor Egeria's remaining consciousness. Simurgh awoke and drank the Amrita, shattered into Khvarena, and her motes became the Pari. The Schwanenritter brought Khaenri'ahn refugees up into the Girdle and used their Ruin Golems against the Abyss, and are remembered as the heroes of Dahri.
### ULTRA +
The First Pari Zurvan found a weakened Dainsleif and brought him to the oasis. The Darshan of Nagarjuna arrived soon after. Together they performed the Rite of Chinvat, Zurvan purified the Harvisptokhm's seal and made the five Great Songs of Khvarena, four of which were scattered; some Darshan members stayed as the Order of Skeptics. Dainsleif later left with the Traveler's sibling. Later, the Skeptic Klingsor joined an Akademiya expedition into the Dahrian ruins, fell to the Abyss and became an Abyss Herald. Zurvan then sealed the oasis, and the Order lost its records and neglected its duty.

## post_cataclysm
@version: 3.0
### STANDARD
After the cataclysm Lesser Lord Kusanali was found and brought to Sumeru City. The people rejoiced and the Sabzeruz Festival moved to her birthday. The sages found her no wiser than an ordinary child and shut her in the Sanctuary of Surasthana, while espousing Rukkhadevata's legacy and building the Akasha into a public information network. Over 400 years ago the student Zandik, who became the Fatui Harbinger Dottore, studied at the Akademiya, and the Temple of Silence left the corrupt sages for a temple at Thamud Oasis.
### LARGE +
Two hundred years ago the Akademiya graduated a scholar the wiki calls its most talented before Lisa. Faruzan was born over 120 years ago and was trapped in Deshret-era ruins for a century. Misinformation then pitted forest against desert.
### ULTRA +
After Chapter III Act V (3.2) Rukkhadevata's memory was erased from the world: Nahida is now believed, even by herself, to have always been the Dendro Archon who lost her memories in the cataclysm. Only the Traveler remembers.

## recent_past
@version: 3.0
### LARGE
Roughly 20 years ago Kaveh's father, a scholar who had entered the Interdarshan Championship, left for Aaru Village to help its people and died saving a caravan from quicksand. About 10 years ago Cyrus took Cyno away from the Temple of Silence. Collei, born with Eleazar, was handed to a seer who was a Fatui agent and used her in Archon-residue experiments. Lisa graduated from Spantamad after two years and left for Mondstadt. Kaveh and Alhaitham became friends, started a joint study of Deshret's ruins and later fell out. In the last few years the sages grew more hostile to the arts, and Kaveh built the Palace of Alcazarzaray at the cost of his savings.
### ULTRA +
Faruzan escaped the ruins and was found with an Anemo Vision. In the manga, set shortly before the game, Collei leaves Mondstadt with Cyno for Sumeru. The precise order of these events is only estimated by the wiki from characters' ages.

## present_day
@version: 3.0
### STANDARD
In Chapter III (3.0 to 3.2) the sages' plan to build an artificial god with Dottore and the Fatui was defeated, Nahida was freed, the Akasha shut down, and Nahida purged Rukkhadevata from Irminsul, removing the forbidden knowledge and ending Eleazar. In Khvarena of Good and Evil the ex-Skeptic Klingsor tried with Nasejuna to break the Harvisptokhm's seal, and the Traveler and the Pari Sorush stopped them. In 6.6 Dottore attacked Irminsul from within and Sumeru City was evacuated; Nahida burned the tree with the Pyro Gnosis and Aaru, reprogrammed by Alhaitham and Kaveh, took over its role.
### LARGE +
Limited-time events add small notes about this period; see the entry on Sumeru in limited events.
### ULTRA +
In 6.6 Nahida had collapsed, and Apep sheltered her. The Seven Pillars' freeing and the Irminsul burning for seven days are detailed in the lore files on the destruction of Irminsul. As of 7.1 Sumeru is at peace and rebuilding, the Archon Quest has moved on to Snezhnaya, and Sumeru's Celestial Nail began, like the other nails, to emit a strange light into the sky after the Hyperborea nail collapsed.

## entry: The Three God-Kings and Ay-Khanoum
@keys: Three Eternal Companion-Gods, God-Kings of Sumeru, Ay-Khanoum, City of Amphitheaters, City of the Moon Maiden
@version: 3.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Nabu Malikata, King Deshret and Greater Lord Rukkhadevata ruled Sumeru together for a time and built Ay-Khanoum as an earthly paradise. Each valued a different kind of wisdom and they debated often. The partnership broke over Deshret's quest for forbidden knowledge. Ay-Khanoum was later abandoned and then rebuilt among the seven cities of the post-Deshret era.
### LARGE +
A chair crafted as a memorial to Rukkhadevata stands in the Orchard of Pairidaeza. Accounts differ on whether she left before or after Nabu Malikata died.

## entry: Gurabad's Fall
@keys: Gurabad, Ormazd, Parvezravan, Shirin, Shiruyeh, Tale of Shiruyeh and Shirin
@version: 3.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Gurabad was a vassal state of Deshret founded by the shepherd Ormazd with the Jinni Liloupar. Liloupar, seeing her husband become a tyrant, planned its destruction and used her children and grandson. The in-world book blames Shirin; Liloupar's own memories show she was the planner. Deshret punished her by splitting her soul into seven parts.
### LARGE +
Liloupar says Shirin and Shiruyeh fell into an abyss with the city. The Eremites still use Shiruyeh as a word for a traitor.

## entry: Rukkhadevata's Rainforest
@keys: Varuna Contraption, Wall of Samiel, Dharma Forest origin

## entry: Khvarena, Simurgh and the Pari
@keys: Simurgh, Khvarena, Pari, Great Songs of Khvarena, Rite of Chinvat, Sign of Apaosha
### LARGE
Nabu Malikata's shard of Khvarena became Simurgh, who sacrificed herself during the cataclysm to purify the Amrita at the Vourukasha Oasis. The Pari are her motes. In Khvarena of Good and Evil the Sign of Apaosha returned over the Harvisptokhm for the first time since the cataclysm and the Traveler helped the Pari Sorush complete the Rite of Chinvat again.

## entry: Irminsul's Burning
@keys: burning of Irminsul, Aaru and Irminsul, Dottore's attack on Irminsul
@keys2: Sumeru, Nahida, history
@version: 6.6
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
In 6.6 Dottore corrupted Irminsul from within. Sumeru City was evacuated. Nahida, invoking her authority as keeper of Irminsul before the three Shades, burned it with the Pyro Gnosis that Mavuika had given the Traveler, and Dottore died in the fire, which burned for seven days and nights. Aaru, modified under Alhaitham and Kaveh's plan with help from the freed Pillars Thoth, Sobek and Heryshaf, now holds the world's records. The Pyro Gnosis is gone.
### LARGE +
The Belyi Tsar's notes suggest Aaru was the Goddess of Flowers' intended replacement for the Ley Lines. The wiki infers this, and the claim that Celestia no longer fully controls fate, from Nahida's remark that fate now has many more possibilities.

## entry: Recent Past of Sumeru
@keys: Kaveh's father, Interdarshan Championship, Palace of Alcazarzaray
@keys2: Sumeru, history
### LARGE
Kaveh's father died about 20 years ago helping caravans near Aaru Village. Kaveh built the Palace of Alcazarzaray for Dori and went bankrupt, then moved in with Alhaitham after meeting him again at Lambad's Tavern. These details come from Kaveh's character stories and the 3.6 limited event A Parade of Providence.
