## summary
### LITE
Inazuma's recorded history runs from islands flooded after the War of Funerary Flame, through the Archon War, when Ei (the Raiden Shogun) conquered the archipelago and slew Orobashi, to the Cataclysm 500 years ago, when her twin Makoto died in Khaenri'ah. Grieving, Ei spent centuries pursuing "Eternity" through a puppet Shogun while she meditated in the Plane of Euthymia. Under Fatui influence the Shogunate issued the Sakoku and Vision Hunt decrees about a year before the Traveler arrived. The Traveler helped end both, and Inazuma is open and quiet as of 7.1.
### STANDARD +
Use this entry for "what came before what" in Inazuma. For depth on a single episode (the Vision Hunt, the Tatarigami, the Byakuyakoku, the Thunderbird) defer to that episode's own entry. The sequence below follows the wiki's editor-assembled chronology, so round dates are approximate.

## unified_civilization_and_funerary_flame
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
In the oldest era the Inazuma region belonged to a single human civilisation. Enkanomiya (Byakuyakoku) was part of it, as was a Celestia-linked people on Tsurumi Island who worshipped the Three Moons and a sand-built kingdom north of Narukami Island. A great Electro eagle, later called the Thunderbird (Kapatcir), was also born. The War of Funerary Flame killed the Three Moons, and a flood swept Inazuma, leaving few islands.
### LARGE +
The wiki attributes the flood to the war and says the sand kingdom was drowned or buried. Enkanomiya fell into the ocean and was cut off from the surface; its people clashed with the vishaps (the Dragonheir of the Depths) who had fled into the sea and who held the advantage in the lightless deep. A Celestial Nail is said to have landed on Tsurumi Island, destroying its first civilisation and leaving it wreathed in mist.
### ULTRA +
The Violet Court, an altar for the Sacred Sakura, is recorded as separated from the other islands. Most of Watatsumi Island does not yet exist; Orobashi creates it later. The wiki places the Thunderbird's choosing Tsurumi Island as her home about 6,000 years ago.

## enkanomiya_and_interim
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
After the war, Enkanomiya's people prayed to the Heavenly Principles and were answered only by Istaroth. A sage called Abrax (Aberaku) built the Dainichi Mikoshi as an artificial sun to drive off the vishaps. Nobles later made the Sunchild a puppet ruler.
### LARGE +
When the people tried to leave a year after the Mikoshi was built they could not find a way out, and read this as the Heavenly Principles' victory. Corrupt nobles taught them to worship the Mikoshi itself as a god and chose a child, the Sunchild, to rule; Abrax and later Spartacus (Supada no Hiko) opposed them and were imprisoned.
### ULTRA +
Much later Orobashi arrived in Enkanomiya fleeing the Archon War, and a child begged him to be their god. He overthrew the Sunchild's rule, protected the people from the vishaps, supervised research on them, and strengthened the Mikoshi's light. Celestia held Orobashi and Watatsumi guilty of profanity and of deceiving living souls, and he took the sentence on himself so his people could be spared. The Divine Priestess line (origin of the Sangonomiya) was chosen from the pearl-gathering sea-daughters, and other clans such as the Yuna also came from Enkanomiya.

Orobashi had his people learn Narukami Island's language and culture before they left. On the last day, envoys from Khaenri'ah with a Ruin Guard stole the banned book Before Sun and Moon; Antei fought them and kept it, but was gravely hurt. Afterwards Orobashi raised Watatsumi Island from coral grown from his own body, and his people named it Sangonomiya and themselves the Sangonomiya Clan, calling Byakuyakoku "Enkanomiya" in contrast. This is told through Enkanomiya's quests and books.

## archon_war_in_inazuma
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Ei conquered many youkai races; they helped humans reach the mountains and build a city by the sea, which was Inazuma's beginning. More than 2,000 years ago she killed the gods she had defeated and united the islands. She believed her twin Makoto the better ruler, gave up her body so Makoto could ascend to Celestia, and became her kagemusha. Most of Teyvat came to believe there was a single god, Baal, the Raiden Shogun.
### LARGE +
Makoto used her new power to recall Ei's will and rebuild her body. Makoto became the first Electro Archon, the God of Eternity. During the brief peace that followed, Ei, Makoto, the Kitsune Saiguu, Mikoshi Chiyo and Sasayuri are recorded gathering for picnics and celebrations.
### ULTRA +
Orobashi's people, driven by famine, urged him to invade fertile Yashiori Island. After refusing, he gave in, seeing it as a way to fulfil his sentence. The Watatsumi forces under Orobashi, Akuou and the twin generals Mouun and Ayame took the ancient city of Mumei, and Akuou ruled it. War with the Shogunate followed. Akuou killed the Great Tengu Sasayuri, Ei's close friend. Enraged, Ei used Musou no Hitotachi to slay Orobashi and Akuou with one stroke and split Yashiori Island in two. The sea ran red and Mumei fell; Mouun and the whale Daikengyou were ambushed by tengu, and Ayame vanished into the sea of blood.

Orobashi's resentment became the Tatarigami. Ei set Narukami Pillars as wards to hold his soul, the Kitain clan's Yashiori Guardians began their watch from Higi Village, the Jakotsu Mine was dug to extract Crystal Marrow from his remains, and the Mikage Furnace in Kannazuka was built to use it for Jade Steel. Watatsumi came under the Electro Archon's rule, though Ei let its people keep worshipping their god. The wiki dates the end of the Archon War to about 2,000 years ago, when the last seat in Celestia was claimed; defeated gods who refused The Seven fled to islands of the Dark Sea, and since Inazuma lies near it the Shogunate fought many wars there.

## tsurumi_and_seirai
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
On Tsurumi Island a man named Ruu befriended the Thunderbird, who had forgotten her own name; he offered her "Kanna Kapatcir". After Ruu was sacrificed in a misguided rite, she destroyed his people and the island relived its last day under a ley line disorder. On Seirai Island she was later killed by Ei about 1,500 years ago.
### LARGE +
Tsurumi islanders at first gathered her shed feathers and used the maushiro to find each other in the fog, while she paid them little mind. Many islanders who "vanished" had in truth settled on other islands. Ruu volunteered to be sacrificed to please her; she saw it as the killing of an innocent and a broken promise. After the massacre she flew to Seirai, devastated the city of Hiraumi, and Ei hunted her on Amakumo Peak to protect the people.
### ULTRA +
Her death raised a vast thunderstorm, held by the Warding Stones; the Asase Shrine was built to quiet the lingering hatred and Koseki Village was founded. The "Thunder Manifestation" is that remnant. Much later the pirate Ako Domeki rebelled against the Shogunate, and Asase Hibiki, reminded of Takamine, released the seals; the storm shattered Seirai and the Shogunate evacuated its people to Narukami Island. The wiki gives the date of this rebellion only as "? years ago".

## cataclysm_and_ei_eternity
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Around 500 years ago the Cataclysm struck. Makoto left Ei to defend Inazuma and went alone to Khaenri'ah, where she was killed; Ei found her dying and carried her consciousness home. The Sacred Sakura appeared on Mt. Yougou and helped Ei end the monster crisis. Ei, shattered, resolved to find Eternity.
### LARGE +
The Kitsune Saiguu was consumed by the dark forces (her surviving memory became Hanachirusato); Takamine the Mistsplitter vanished; Mikoshi Chiyo was swallowed by a beast of sin and, tainted, turned on Ei and was driven away. Kujou Shigeyori founded the Kujou Encampment at the front in Kannazuka. Ei suspected Istaroth was behind the Sakura's sudden appearance.
### ULTRA +
Ei took up the title of Electro Archon after Makoto's death, and few knew Makoto was gone. She set up the Tri-Commission (Tenryou, Kanjou, Yashiro) to run Inazuma and planted Thunder Sakura around Narukami Island from Sacred Sakura branches and her own spirit to draw out filth. Ritou's port rose under Hiiragi Hiroshi. The survivors of Araumi chose not to return and it was abandoned. Yae Miko, then young, met Alice when she visited Inazuma.

Disillusioned with Celestia and with her losses, Ei decided to reach "Eternity" by making a puppet of herself. A first prototype took the form of a young male vessel; seeing him weep, she judged him too fragile for the Gnosis, sealed his power and let him live his own life. Several failed puppets followed, including the Maguu Kenki dolls modelled on the swordsman Iwakura Michihiro. About 400 years ago she completed the Shogun, entered the Plane of Euthymia to meditate, and gave the Electro Gnosis to Yae Miko. The Archon Quest reveals this in Chapter II.

## tatarasuna_and_gokaden
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Ei's first prototype puppet woke in Shakkei Pavilion and lived among Tatarasuna's smiths as "Kabukimono". In the Tatarasuna incident, Il Dottore (as Escher) poisoned the town with Tatarigami; the puppet came to hate gods and humans and later took the name Kunikuzushi (Scaramouche, the Wanderer).
### LARGE +
Escher, a supposed Fontaine mechanic, altered the Mikage Furnace and released the Tatarigami. He killed the armory officer Niwa Hisahide and used his heart. Nagamasa cleaved Katsuragi to answer for Niwa; Nozomu and Kinjiro saved the blade's remains. Kabukimono sought help from the Shogunate, which was occupied by the Shogun's activation, and took the silence for abandonment.
### ULTRA +
About 100 years ago Kunikuzushi plotted the fall of the Raiden Gokaden, ruining three of its five schools by pushing the smith Akame Kanenaga to betray Inazuma. Swordsmithing falls under the Yashiro Commission, so the Kamisato clan was harmed until Yae Miko intervened at their judgement. The Kaedehara fortunes declined, and by Kazuha's time the clan had collapsed. After Scaramouche erased himself from the Irminsul in Inversion of Genesis, records name a Hyakume swordsmith and descendant instead; see uncertainties.

## recent_past_to_present
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
About a year before the Traveler arrived, the Fatui steered the Tenryou and Kanjou commissions, the Shogun issued the Sakoku Decree to close the borders and the Vision Hunt Decree to seize Visions, and the Sangonomiya Resistance rose. The Traveler, Yae Miko and the Watatsumi Army had the Vision Hunt abolished; a ceasefire followed and Sakoku was lifted after Transient Dreams.
### LARGE +
The decrees came with the building of the Statue of the Omnipresent God. Kaedehara Kazuha, whose friend lost a duel before the Shogun, fled as a branded criminal with the friend's Vision. Orobashi's wards were broken by fanatics aided by a Fatui infiltrator, the Tatarigami spread, and the Mikage Furnace failed, halting Tatarasuna. Signora lost her duel to the Traveler and was executed by the Raiden Shogun.
### ULTRA +
Kujou Sara and Sangonomiya Kokomi agreed the ceasefire with the Traveler as witness. In Transient Dreams, Ei and the Shogun settled a new meaning of Eternity, the Sacred Sakura was planted into the past and Sakoku ended. Afterwards the Traveler took part in the Sacred Sakura Cleansing Ritual, restored the Mikage Furnace with Xavier, repaired the wards on Yashiori, resealed the Thunder Manifestation, freed Tsurumi from its fog, and fetched Bloodbranch Coral from Enkanomiya at Tsuyuko's request. This woke the Sangacorallia, though the Abyss Order and the Bathysmal Vishaps also exploited the trip.

Nothing in 6.x or 7.0-7.1 is recorded as altering Inazuma's situation. The Electro Gnosis, which Yae Miko had guarded, left Inazuma with the Fatui during the 2.x story and later passed to Il Dottore in Sumeru; its fate belongs to other entries.

## entry: Orobashi and the Yashiori War
@keys: Orobashi, Watatsumi Omikami, Orobashi no Mikoto, Akuou, Mouun
@keys2: Yashiori, Ei, Raiden, Mumei, Tatarigami
@version: 2.0
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
Orobashi, the sea serpent god of Watatsumi, fled the Archon War, took in Enkanomiya's people and made Watatsumi Island from coral grown from his body. Famine drove him to invade Yashiori Island with Akuou, Mouun and Ayame. After Akuou killed the Great Tengu Sasayuri, Ei struck down Orobashi and Akuou with Musou no Hitotachi. His lingering resentment became the Tatarigami, held back by Narukami Pillars.

## entry: Makoto's death and the Sacred Sakura
@keys: Makoto, Raiden Makoto, kagemusha, Sacred Sakura, Mt. Yougou
@keys2: Khaenri'ah, Ei, Cataclysm, twin
@version: 2.0
### STANDARD
Makoto, Ei's twin and the first Electro Archon, went alone to Khaenri'ah during the Cataclysm and died in Ei's arms. Ei took her consciousness back to Inazuma, where the Sacred Sakura appeared on Mt. Yougou and helped end the crisis. Ei, who had been Makoto's kagemusha, took Makoto's place as the world's only known Electro Archon.

## entry: Tatarasuna incident
@keys: Tatarasuna incident, Kabukimono, Kunikuzushi, Escher, Mikage Furnace
@keys2: Dottore, Scaramouche, Niwa, Nagamasa
@version: 2.0
### STANDARD
Roughly 400 years ago, Il Dottore, posing as the Fontaine mechanic Escher, modified the Mikage Furnace and released Tatarigami, poisoning Tatarasuna and killing the armory officer Niwa. Ei's first prototype puppet, called Kabukimono, believed the Shogunate had abandoned them. He later called himself Kunikuzushi.

## entry: Sakoku and the Vision Hunt
@keys: Sakoku Decree, Vision Hunt, Statue of the Omnipresent God, Sangonomiya Resistance
@keys2: Inazuma, Fatui, Tenryou, Shogun, Traveler
@version: 2.0
### STANDARD
About a year before the Traveler's arrival, Fatui influence over the Tenryou and Kanjou commissions led the Shogun to close Inazuma's borders and seize Visions. The Watatsumi Army resisted and a stalemate followed. The Traveler and Yae Miko ended the Vision Hunt; Sakoku lapsed after Transient Dreams.

## entry: Thunderbird and Ruu
@keys: Thunderbird, Kapatcir, Kanna Kapatcir, Ruu, Tsurumi Island
@keys2: fog, Seirai, Amakumo Peak, song
@version: 2.0
### STANDARD
Ruu befriended the forgetful Thunderbird by singing and named her Kanna Kapatcir. When he was sacrificed she massacred his tribe and trapped Tsurumi in a repeating day, then devastated Seirai until Ei killed her on Amakumo Peak. The Traveler later helped Ruu's spirit keep his promise.
