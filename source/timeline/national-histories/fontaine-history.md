## summary
### LITE
Fontaine's past runs from the Primordial Sea, the source of all life, ruled in the Old World by the Hydro Dragon Sovereign. The Heavenly Principles took that Authority and made Egeria the sea's new heart. The god Remus founded the musical empire of Remuria, which sank about 2,000 years ago, and Egeria then became Hydro Archon. Her act of giving Oceanids human form was branded a sin and a prophecy of dissolving floods followed. Egeria died in the Cataclysm about 500 years ago; her successor Focalors spent five centuries on a plan that ended in Version 4.2, when the prophecy was fulfilled in form and defused in fact.
### STANDARD +
Use this entry to place events in order and give rough "how long ago". Order of eras: Old World, the Heavenly Principles take the Hydro Authority, the great flood that ends the first era, Remuria (Archon War period), Egeria's rule, the Cataclysm and Focalors' succession, the Melusines and Neuvillette's appointment, the Recent Past, and the resolution of the prophecy in 4.2. For the underlying mysteries defer to the prophecy, Egeria, Focalors and Remuria entries.

## old_world
### LITE
In the Seven Sovereigns' era the Primordial Sea lay on the world's surface. Most of Teyvat's life traces back to it, and the Hydro Dragon Sovereign was its "beating heart".
### STANDARD +
The Hydro Dragon is sometimes called the original god of life, and the sea its "birthing waters" or "amniotic fluid". Life within the Primordial Sea was a collective rather than separate individuals. The wiki treats these as lore statements from item and book text, not dated events.
### LARGE +
When the Heavenly Principles arrived they removed the Hydro Dragon from its role as the sea's heart and took its Hydro Authority (the "When the Doves Held Branches" stage on the wiki's timeline). The Hydro Dragon then withered away at some point after its defeat. According to the Oceanids, Naberius, Ruler of Life, then created Egeria as the sea's new heart, shaped entirely from material native to Teyvat. No new Hydro Dragon could be born while Egeria held that place.

## first_era_flood
### STANDARD
Near the end of what a Remurian history calls the "first era", likely the unified civilisation, humanity began to covet what belonged to Celestia and to resist its fate. Cities were destroyed by tsunamis and buried after 100 days of rain that "drowned all sin and arrogation"; humanity had to rebuild. The envoy Sybilla, who guarded an Irminsul tree, survived as the only one left from that age.
### LARGE +
In Fontaine no true civilisation formed for a very long time afterwards, long enough that Gurabad in Sumeru rose and fell. This is the sequence given by The History of the Decline and Fall of Remuria, which was written about a century after Remuria's own destruction.

## egeria_and_the_oceanids
### STANDARD
The wiki places this in the interim between the great wars. Egeria's first tear is said to have given birth to the first Oceanid, and the Oceanids were told to understand every kind of creature and to love them. They spread across Teyvat's waters as watchers. When some of them longed to live on the surface among humans, Egeria took Primordial Seawater and poured it into their vessels, giving them humanoid forms. Celestia judged this an unauthorised creation of life, a sin, and sentenced her to be sealed. Fontaine's people learned of a prophecy that the waters would rise and dissolve them, washing away the sin.
### LARGE +
The humanised Oceanids became the tribes of Fontaine, who worshipped Egeria as the Mistress of Many Waters. The catch was that if they ever met Primordial Seawater of high enough concentration, their borrowed blood would seep back into it and return them to plain Oceanids without self or individuality. Rhodeia of Loch, the strongest Oceanid, travelled Teyvat on Egeria's behalf; her spokesperson Endora denies that "spy" describes Egeria's motives. A fourth stone slate shows the end: a flooded Fontaine and the Hydro Archon weeping alone on her throne. Slates showing the stages stand in ruins south of Poisson.

## remuria
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
Some time after Gurabad fell in Sumeru, the god Remus came to the High Seas looking for a place to die and found a forgotten city whose last inhabitants were Sybilla and the other Golden Bees. They told him he would found a prosperous nation whose desire for justice would doom it. Remus accepted, the Irminsul became a golden ship, and he established the empire of Remuria over the waters. Its golden age brought agriculture, great cities and temples, music and art. He called other tribes into the empire through the Fortuna and conquered those who refused, such as the tribe of Erinnyes and Cassiodor. Worried by the prophecy of its fall, Remus went to Egeria's prison in the Primordial Sea; Scylla, her guardian, fought him for thirty days and nights before letting him pass. Egeria could not help, but gave him a goblet of primordial water, and Scylla left the sea to join his cause.
### LARGE +
Remus mixed the primordial water with immortal stone to make Golden Ichor, placed the Remurians' souls and minds into stone golems, and tried to replace the sea's water with it. Later he made the Grand Symphony, Phobos, from Sybilla's life; Phobos inherited her lack of a will of its own and served only to satisfy people's desires, including bloodshed. The people grew arrogant and bloodthirsty, believing this was their Imperator's wish. Seeing their degradation, Remus plotted in secret with Scylla to destroy Phobos. Scylla led vishaps into the capital Capitolium, and Remus died unleashing the power of the Symphony. The Harmost Boethius took this for betrayal, stole the Golden Ichor and sealed Scylla using chains forged by the Harmost Euergetia. Remuria sank beneath the sea as Scylla was sealed; Primordial Sea water corroded the Ichor and the survivors reverted to "Barbarism and Annihilation".
### ULTRA +
The wiki records that the Golden Troupe existed in this era, but its records were largely lost in the sudden destruction; research by Rene suggests that Jakob's unusual constitution is related to it. Cassiodor later founded the Marechaussee Phantom to oppose the Golden Troupe. The sunken ruins lie in the Sea of Bygone Eras.

## egeria_archon
### STANDARD
After Remuria sank, Celestia ordered Egeria out of her prison to take Remus' place. She gathered the survivors, settled their feuds, and told them to found new cities around flowing springs, beginning an era of law that lasts to the present. She received a Gnosis, became the Hydro Archon, and one of the Seven. After the Archon War she travelled to Liyue for the gatherings of the original Seven. She exiled criminals from her nation; those who begged forgiveness she heard in the waters, and she ordered them to guard her secret, the way to the Primordial Sea, from beneath the waves. That led to the Fortress of Meropide, whose real purpose was later forgotten.
### LARGE +
Knowing she could not oppose the divine and that her followers were doomed by the prophecy, Egeria named the Oceanid Focalors her successor, hoping Focalors could devise a way to deceive Celestia. The wiki lists the Marechaussee Phantom's foundation by Cassiodor in this period too.
### ULTRA +
Before the Cataclysm the Fleuve Cendre outlaw Eduardo Baker clashed with the Court of Fontaine's bureaucracy; he was arrested, freed on the way into exile in the Great Red Sand, and in the ensuing Siege of Poisson the town was razed, probably killing its mayor Renault de Petrichor. Their sons, Rene and Jakob, were sent to the Narzissenkreuz Institute. The wiki places this shortly before the Cataclysm.

## cataclysm_and_succession
@version: 4.0
### STANDARD
About 500 years ago Egeria died fighting the Abyss at Tunigi Hollow in the Cataclysm, and Focalors took over. The Oceanids refused to obey Focalors and left Fontaine on self-imposed exile; Fontaine's waters turned bitter. In Sumeru the wiki reports that Egeria's body became the pure waters of the Amrita. The same period saw the great beast Elynas spreading pollution; the retired admiral Basil Elton led the Fontaine Armada in a 35-day pursuit and rammed it, destroying the fleet and mortally wounding Elynas, who died while keeping consciousness. A great flood, the first diluvian period, submerged much of Fontaine including the Narzissenkreuz Institute, which closed.
### LARGE +
The Institute's director Lyris, herself an Oceanid, disappeared. The orphaned boys Rene and Jakob were taken in by Karl Ingold, and Alain and Mary-Ann Guillotin by Emanuel Guillotin. The Oceanids later found what had happened to Egeria at the Harvisptokhm in Sumeru, according to the Focalors page, and chose not to return.

## focalors_plan
@version: 4.2
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
About 499 years before the Masquerade of the Guilty, Focalors separated her divinity from her body and spirit; the human she left behind was Furina, who took the public role of Hydro Archon. Focalors then sealed herself in the Oratrice Mecanique d'Analyse Cardinale as its verdict-giving mind. Trials at the Opera Epiclese produced Indemnitium from public belief in justice. A small share powered Fontaine; the rest was stored for the day the prophecy's trial of the Hydro Archon was held. Focalors also kept the Hydro Gnosis with her divine half and cursed Furina to live as long as Focalors existed, without chasing her own happiness.
### LARGE +
Her plan: stage the prophesied judgement, have the Oratrice sentence the Archon, and use the stored energy to execute herself and destroy the Hydro Archon's divine throne, returning the stolen Hydro Authority to the Hydro Dragon. To prepare, she invited Neuvillette, the current Hydro Dragon, to become Chief Justice so he might come to understand and protect humanity. Lyney, investigating the Oratrice, heard a voice that was Focalors'.
### ULTRA +
Furina's exhaustion after five centuries of acting as the Archon is why, after the sentence, she left the Palais Mermonia and Opera Epiclese and settled in an ordinary apartment, according to the wiki.

## melusines_and_neuvillette
### STANDARD
About 400 years ago Neuvillette became Iudex. In the same period the Melusines were born from wounds in Elynas' remains, caused by shrapnel from an explosion of destroyed clockwork meka during the Marechaussee operation against the Narzissenkreuz Ordo. Neuvillette brought them into the rest of Fontaine, where many people distrusted them, partly because of "strange incidents" and his own fresh appointment.
### LARGE +
The Melusine Carole and Vautrin, then leader of the Special Security and Surveillance Patrol, worked for goodwill between the species, and five years later Neuvillette gave them the Medal of Peace. Hardliners including Thibert and Suavegothe then framed Carole for a murder and stirred up a crowd; she took her own life to calm the situation. Vautrin killed the hardliners in revenge and was exiled to the Fortress of Meropide, where he became a model prisoner and founded the Mutual Aid Society. About 400 years later a similar threat to the Melusine Kiara prompted Neuvillette to investigate in person. The Melusine Sigewinne accepted a potion giving her a more human-like appearance in order to save a human friend, broke an old law against turning other species human, and ended up in Meropide, where she stayed on as medic after her sentence was lifted.

## narzissenkreuz_ordo
@version: 4.1
### LARGE
Rene and Jakob, by then in the Institute of Natural Philosophy, explored Khaenri'ahn ruins with Karl and deduced a world-formula predicting a second flood that would destroy Fontaine within a few centuries. Rene founded the Narzissenkreuz Ordo to prepare for it. After a failed experiment on Alain's research assistant Carter Scherbius, Alain cut ties; the Guillotin siblings joined the Marechaussee Phantom. Rene dissolved himself into a pure personality with the Holy Blade and Lyris' essence; Jakob made a Hydro Tulpa to hold his consciousness, named Narzissenkreuz. He created the sentient hilichurl Caterpillar.
### ULTRA +
Narzissenkreuz absorbed Emanuel Guillotin and the Champion Duelist Marfisa after they investigated the Ordo. About 400 years ago he sent Jakob, Caterpillar and Lyris into Elynas' remains to draw the Marechaussee Hunters away from himself; Mary-Ann was fatally wounded, her personality absorbed by Lyris for unknown reasons, and the Melusines were born. Narzissenkreuz fell into the Primordial Sea hidden beneath the Tower of Ipsissimus, and Caterpillar was imprisoned in Meropide. Later Alain left the Phantom and founded the Fontaine Research Institute of Kinetic Energy Engineering. These steps are the wiki's reconstruction.

## recent_past
### STANDARD
The "Two Musketeers" incident: an assassin hired by Morris killed Elisa to hide an illegitimate relationship; their children Baptiste and Veronique witnessed it and sought revenge. About three years before the story, Callas, president of the Spina di Rosula and Navia's father, was accused of murdering the double agent Jacques; he chose a duel over a trial and died at Clorinde's hands, asking her to look after Navia. Wriothesley was adopted into a trafficking scheme, killed the couple and freed the children, was sent to Meropide and later took over as its administrator by winning a duel against the previous administrator.
### LARGE +
Before his death Callas had built the Aquabus network with his wife Clementine, parting ways with Romeuf. His associates held back information on Sinthe. A few months before the story the Kamera was redeveloped in Fontaine and spread to other nations. Skirk fell into Fontaine's seas like a shooting star, seen by a crowd including Furina and Neuvillette; her arrival, assisted by Surtalogi, is undated.

## present_day
@version: 4.2
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
In the Archon Quest the prophecy comes true on the day Furina is put on trial before the people, Neuvillette and the Oratrice and judged guilty; the Oratrice sentences "the Hydro Archon". A giant whale, the All-Devouring Narwhal, breaks into the Opera Epiclese and Childe holds it back. Focalors executes herself with the stored Indemnitium, destroying the Hydro Archon's throne and freeing Furina from her curse. Neuvillette, with the Hydro Authority fully his, forgives all Fontainians their original sin and makes them true humans, so the Primordial Sea can no longer dissolve them. In the battle against the Narwhal, the flood foretold by the prophecy arrives but recedes as quickly; those already dissolved are not restored, and the Wingalet rescues many from drowning.
### LARGE +
Wriothesley had shown the Traveler the sluice gate locking the Primordial Sea beneath Meropide and built the Wingalet airship as a precaution, inspired by the legend of Remus and the Golden Bee. There is no longer a Hydro Archon; the Oratrice has stopped working and Neuvillette governs as Chief Justice. Furina lives as an ordinary person.

## accounts
### STANDARD
The Oceanids' account (Egeria made them by a tear and Celestia punished her) differs from later researchers' view on who created them. Neuvillette reports rumours that the prophecy is rooted in Egeria's last words before her death; the words themselves are not recorded. Remurian history comes mostly from The History of the Decline and Fall of Remuria, written a century after the fall.

## open_questions
### STANDARD
How long Fontaine's civilisation existed before Remuria, the prophecy's true author, how the Golden Troupe relates to Jakob, and whether Egeria survives in any form are not resolved in Fontaine's story. Do not date items the wiki leaves loose.

## connections
### STANDARD
This history ties into the Master Timeline, Khaenri'ah's history (the Cataclysm and the Ordo's ruins), and Sumeru through Gurabad, Remus' origin and the Harvisptokhm.

## status
### STANDARD
As of 7.1, the prophecy is resolved, the Hydro Archon's throne is destroyed, there is no Hydro Archon, Neuvillette is Chief Justice and Furina lives as an ordinary person. No later Fontaine upheaval is recorded in the sources used. In 6.7 the Harbinger Sandrone was revived at the Fontaine Research Institute and reappeared in public during a Fontinalia Festival before returning to Snezhnaya (see the sub-entry).

## entry: Original Sin and the Prophecy of Fontaine
@keys: Prophecy of Fontaine, original sin, Fontainian prophecy
@version: 4.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Fontaine's people are said to be born with an original sin: the unauthorised creation of humans from Oceanids by Egeria. The prophecy says the waters will rise, those with sin will dissolve, and only the Hydro Archon will remain, weeping on her throne. Stone slates south of Poisson show the stages. In Version 4.2 it was fulfilled in form, with a trial and a flood, but defused in fact when Neuvillette absolved the people.

## entry: Fall of Remuria
@keys: Remuria, Capitolium, Golden Ichor, Harmost
@version: 4.0
### STANDARD
Remus' empire sank about 2,000 years ago after he turned his people's souls into stone-bodied golems, built Phobos from Sybilla's life, and conspired with Scylla to destroy it; the Harmost Boethius sealed Scylla and the empire drowned. The ruins now lie in the Sea of Bygone Eras.

## entry: Egeria's Reign
@keys: Egeria, Mistress of Many Waters, Hydro Archon Egeria
@version: 4.0
### STANDARD
Egeria ruled from about 2,000 to 500 years ago, founded Fontaine's law, and exiled criminals who later built the Fortress of Meropide over the path to the Primordial Sea. She died at Tunigi Hollow in the Cataclysm and named Focalors her successor.

## entry: Focalors' 500-Year Plan
@keys: Focalors, Regina of All Waters, Oratrice sentence
@version: 4.2
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Focalors split her divinity from Furina, hid in the Oratrice, stored Indemnitium from the Opera Epiclese's trials, and executed herself in 4.2 to destroy the Hydro Archon's throne and return the Authority to Neuvillette.

## entry: Melusines and the Medal of Peace
@keys: Carole, Vautrin, Medal of Peace, Mutual Aid Society
### STANDARD
About 400 years ago the Melusine Carole and Vautrin of the Special Security and Surveillance Patrol earned the Medal of Peace for improving relations between species. Hardliners framed Carole and she took her own life; Vautrin avenged her, was exiled to Meropide and founded the Mutual Aid Society.

## entry: The Callas Affair
@keys: Callas, Spina di Rosula, Aquabus, Sinthe trade
### STANDARD
About three years before the story, Callas chose a duel against Clorinde rather than endanger Navia by testifying against Sinthe producers, after being accused of murdering Jacques. He asked Clorinde to look after Navia.

## entry: Sandrone's revival in Fontaine (6.7)
@keys: Sunny Summer Fontinalia, Stellar Linchpin, Fagio
@keys2: Sandrone, Marionette, Fontaine Research Institute
@version: 6.7
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
After Sandrone (Marionette) died fighting Dottore in Nod-Krai (6.3), Arlecchino sent her and Pulonia's bodies to the Fontaine Research Institute, as Sandrone had asked, so their cores could be salvaged. In the 6.7 story quest Sunny Summer Fontinalia, first released alongside an event of the same name and kept as a permanent Story Quest, she reactivated there. A Stellar Linchpin and a hidden fail-safe left by her creator Alain Guillotin made it possible. Alain had built her about 450 years ago in the image of his late sister Mary-Ann. Pulonia was beyond repair, so she built a new assistant, Fagio. She rented the Wingalet from Wriothesley and wandered the Court of Fontaine incognito as the Fontinalia Festival began. After her public return during the festival she resumed her Harbinger duties in Snezhnaya. These details come from the wiki's Sandrone/Storyline, since the quest pages were still unsummarised when checked.
