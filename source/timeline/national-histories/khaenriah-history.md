## summary
### LITE
Khaenri'ah was a godless nation built underground beneath what is now the Sumeru desert, founded about 6,000 years ago and later ruled by two dynasties, the Crimson Moon and then the Eclipse. About 500 years ago its last king, Irmin, pursued Abyssal power. Five of his greatest subjects, the Five Sinners, seized that power, the Abyss poured into Teyvat, and the gods and the Heavenly Principles destroyed the kingdom. Its pure-blooded survivors were cursed with immortality and still wander the world.
### STANDARD +
The history runs in rough order: founding, the Crimson Moon Dynasty, the coup that brought the Eclipse Dynasty, the long build-up of machines and forbidden knowledge, Irmin's reign and the Traveler's sibling's arrival, the palace raid and the Five Sinners, the Cataclysm and the evacuation to Sumeru, and then the survivors' scattered story (the Abyss Order, the Fatui, Kaeya, Arlecchino, Dainsleif) through 7.1. Canon never gives a full sequence of the Cataclysm, so many links are the wiki's reconstruction.
### LARGE +
Most Khaenri'ah history reaches the player through fragments: item and book texts, a legend (Perinheri), memory sequences in Archon Quests and the Nod-Krai and Snezhnaya chapters, and the survivors' own accounts. Those accounts disagree on who is to blame, and this entry keeps that disagreement open. The Khaenri'ah Archon Quest has not been released as of 7.1.
### ULTRA +
Three points to carry into any scene. First, speakers know different slices: a Mondstadt knight knows almost nothing, Sumeru scholars know the Dahri legend, Fatui and Abyss Order members know more but with bias. Second, the end of Khaenri'ah is a divine punishment in the survivors' story but a self-inflicted wound in Dainsleif's and Pierro's. Third, the 6.x and 7.x chapters only added detail about individual Sinners and the Khaenri'ahns' origin; they did not release the nation's own chapter.

## founding_and_early_kingdom
### LARGE
Around 6,000 years ago, about when the ancient civilisation of Sal Vindagnyr fell, the Kingdom of Khaenri'ah was established as a nation without gods. An item text (the Scribe's Box) is the wiki's source for this date. Its founders once lived aboveground, though Khaenri'ah itself lay deep underground, under what is now the Sumeru desert. The kingdom is also called Dahri in Sumeru, where the name survives as an archaic term used in the Vahumana Darshan. Khaenri'ahns saw themselves as the pride of humankind, and people from the Seven Nations joined the original founders over the millennia. Because the land was barren of natural life, Khaenri'ah's alchemy focused on creating life, an art called the Art of Khemia. It also developed war automatons, later known as Ruin Machines, with Ruin Guards given the code name Field Tillers. The wiki dates Ruin Machines in Teyvat to as early as about 3,700 years ago. More than 2,000 years ago, diplomats from Khaenri'ah, including Oogura and Ooshima, went to Enkanomiya shortly before its people finished moving to Watatsumi Island; they tried to steal the forbidden book Before Sun and Moon and were thwarted by Antei, who destroyed their Ruin Guard at the cost of his life. Khaenri'ahns suspected that the gods and the Heavenly Principles were alien to the world and had usurped it, and knew the rumour that Teyvat's starry sky is false, which the second God of Wisdom had not known until Dottore told her.
### ULTRA +
The wiki notes that no god ever moved against Khaenri'ah over its long history, and treats Celestia's later action as a response to its growing involvement with the Abyss and forbidden knowledge. The national flower was the Inteyvat, and large subterranean lakes there could reflect the sea of stars when the Sign of Apaosha was drawn on them, according to the mage Barbeloth, which made space there less stable.

## crimson_moon_dynasty
### LARGE
The first known rulers were the Crimson Moon Dynasty. Early in this period priests convinced a "muddle-minded king" that the Crimson Moon, a fusion of the Iridescent Moon's remains and the Abyss, was an all-powerful being, and encouraged its worship, probably because they wanted a link to the Abyss. Royal descendants developed the Balemoon Bloodfire. The Crimson Moon and its marrow were later sealed in a domain reachable only with that bloodfire. The legend of Perinheri, an outlander who arrived in Khaenri'ah from another world and grew up in an orphanage, is set in this era, and Perinheri became one of the kingdom's mightiest figures. The dynasty was eventually toppled in a coup. In the legend a sage proposed an organisation for children who came from beyond the world, hoping one would have the power to transcend the gods; Pierro implied it was the first form of the House of the Hearth. Archaeology records that alchemy and the Beastmaster Knights were at their peak under this dynasty, and the "Darksprites" in the legend were the first alchemically made black wolf warbeasts. Moon Marrow research established that the Crimson Moon's origin relates to a god's power and that it could serve as a portal to an unknown place.
### ULTRA +
Arlecchino speculated that the usurpers opposed a direct link to the Abyss or were driven by an ideological divide, but this is her guess. Crimson Moon descendants have red X-shaped pupils within black irises. Arlecchino is one such descendant, and Tholindis belonged to a distant branch. The wiki treats the legend's accuracy as unverified.

## eclipse_dynasty
### LARGE
The Crimson Moon Dynasty was replaced by the Eclipse Dynasty, probably during the Archon War, and its crimson moon gave way to a dark sun. The Eclipse court favoured mechanical engineering and "secrets from beyond the skies" over alchemy and the Beastmaster Knights. Some Khaenri'ahns distrusted this approach as less reliable than human will, but it won. The Universitas Magistrorum, the Alberich Clan and a royal guard called the Black Serpent Knights belonged to this era's institutions. The last king was Irmin, the final Vinster King. The weapons programme that produced Field Tillers and other Ruin Machines was likely aimed at fighting Celestia. In Gavireh Lajavard an abandoned letter pleads that older machinery made before the new knowledge be preserved, and an old bulletin shows the Knight Marshal Anfortas became Regent. The royal guard included "Twilight Sword" Dainsleif and Halfdan.
### ULTRA +
Khaenri'ahn eyes mark the people: ordinary Khaenri'ahns have pupils shaped like a four-pointed star. Pierro, Dainsleif and Kaeya are all shown covering the right side of their face, a habit the wiki notes without explanation.

## irmin_and_the_sibling
### STANDARD
More than 500 years ago Vedrfolnir was born, and later his younger brother Dainsleif. Irmin ascended the throne, and his most trusted advisers became Hroptatyr and Vedrfolnir, with Rhinedottir, Surtalogi, Rerir and Dainsleif rising to prestige as well. The Traveler and their sibling fell to Teyvat in their ship after their home world was destroyed and were separated. The sibling woke first, searched for the Traveler, reached Khaenri'ah and was declared Irmin's heir and "Savior", Princess or Prince of Khaenri'ah. Shortly after, Irmin used the sibling as a vessel to absorb "unlimited Abyssal power".
### LARGE +
Irmin turned increasingly erratic and obsessed with the Abyss; he had originally been sane. He reassigned the Universitas Magistrorum to Abyssal research, except for Tholindis, a Moon Marrow researcher; Professor Mimameith had caught an incurable illness from the earlier Abyss work and died. He also decreed that all Crimson Moon descendants be hunted down, and Rerir served as one of his secret agents without telling Tholindis, his fiancée and herself a distant descendant. Dainsleif was promoted to captain of the royal guard around then. In the 7.1 quest Etched in Time (as the wiki summarises it), Hroptatyr summoned the sibling to please Irmin while Eftirvit, the future Pierro, summoned Paimon from Celestia.
### ULTRA +
Irmin had one eye. Vedrfolnir warned him that pursuing the Abyss would end in the kingdom's fall; Irmin had his eyes gouged out and cast him into the dungeon. The wiki says the Universitas is implied to have predicted the twins' arrival at Irmin's request. Because the sibling is recorded in Irminsul despite being from another world, the Fatui do not count the sibling as a Descender, which the wiki flags as unexplained.

## five_sinners_and_the_raid
### STANDARD
When Dainsleif heard of Vedrfolnir's imprisonment he led a rescue that was also a coup, with Rerir, Hroptatyr, Rhinedottir and Surtalogi. Irmin triggered secret mechanisms that attracted Abyssal monsters to the palace. Rerir saw Tholindis flee through a portal, rejoined the group and, with Hroptatyr, Rhinedottir, Surtalogi and Vedrfolnir, divided the Abyssal energy the sibling had absorbed, one-sixth each. They gained "world-shattering" power and became the Five Sinners of Khaenri'ah. Dainsleif claims that together with the five he could have stopped Irmin.
### LARGE +
Titles: Rhinedottir "Gold", Rerir "Rächer of Solnari", Vedrfolnir "The Visionary", Surtalogi "The Foul", Hroptatyr "The Wise". Rerir's motive was first a pardon to protect Tholindis, then to follow her through her moon gate, which tore his body into pieces across Nod-Krai. Hroptatyr, president of the Universitas and Chief Royal Mage, wanted infinite knowledge. When the Cataclysm came, none of the Sinners defended Khaenri'ah.
### ULTRA +
Later history is thin: Rhinedottir is alive and merged with Naberius; Rerir was revived by Dottore in Nod-Krai and sealed away in Song of the Welkin Moon; Hroptatyr left for the sea of stars to merge with the Abyss, and the memories he engraved into a prism in his notebook surfaced in 7.1 (Etched in Time); Vedrfolnir and Surtalogi are unresolved. Skirk describes three of the Sinners (Rhinedottir, Vedrfolnir, Surtalogi) as pursuing some form of perfection.

## cataclysm
### STANDARD
About 500 years ago Abyssal monsters, including creations of Rhinedottir such as the dragon Durin, poured out of Khaenri'ah across all seven nations. The Heavenly Principles and the gods destroyed Khaenri'ah for its use of forbidden knowledge. Ronova, the Ruler of Death, cursed all pure-blooded Khaenri'ahns with immortality, which ends death and aging but not erosion. Dainsleif was cursed too, and keeps a ring from Vedrfolnir that resets his body but not the erosion of his mind. The sibling saw Khaenri'ah fall, woke the Traveler, and tried to leave Teyvat but was stopped by Asmoday, who separated and sealed them.
### LARGE +
Irmin became "indisposed" and Knight Marshal Anfortas of the Alberich Clan became Regent at the Regnum Concilium Ultimum. Anfortas led the Schwanenritter to evacuate survivors aboveground to Sumeru, where a strange disease turned many into monsters. Per the wiki, the Schwanenritter and their Ruin Golems fought the Abyss in Dharma Forest, and Nagarjuna's Akademiya Darshan named them "heroes of Dahri". Hadura betrayed the group and Anfortas lost his left eye executing them; his own fate is unknown. In the 7.1 quest Etched in Time, as the wiki summarises it, Hroptatyr had found a way to extract the Authority of Death from the Crimson Moon and rejected it as a curse.
### ULTRA +
Regional effects shown in the wiki include Mondstadt (Knights' expedition, Durin buried at Dragonspine), Liyue (the Chasm), Inazuma (Makoto's allies fought the Abyss) and Sumeru, where forbidden knowledge infected Irminsul. The wiki states that Ei saw a scorched battlefield in Khaenri'ah caused by Mavuika, and that the Hydro Archon Egeria was slain at Tunigi Hollow suppressing the defilement seeping from below. Capitano said Ronova cursed without distinguishing the guilty from the innocent.

## post_cataclysm_and_survivors
### STANDARD
Both the Crimson Moon Dynasty and the Alberich Clan survived and produced descendants such as Arlecchino and Kaeya. Pierro, embittered at the world's foundational principles, met the Cryo Archon and co-founded the Fatui. Over 100 years ago, after the events of Caribert, Chlothar Alberich founded the Abyss Order to avenge Khaenri'ah on Celestia and the Seven. The sibling travelled Teyvat with Dainsleif, then learned the "truth of this world", parted ways with him and joined the Order. Chlothar vanished and was presumed dead in Avidya Forest, and the sibling came to lead it.
### LARGE +
More than 20 years ago Peruere, a Crimson Moon descendant, was taken in by the House of the Hearth and became Arlecchino. About 10 years ago Kaeya's father brought him from Khaenri'ah to Mondstadt and left him near Dawn Winery for an "ancient plot", calling him a last hope. During Requiem of the Echoing Depths, Halfdan, a cursed Black Serpent Knight eroded into a Shadowy Husk, protected hilichurls entering the Chasm and was acknowledged by Dainsleif before dying.
### ULTRA +
The wiki's dating of the sibling leading the Order at around a century after its founding is inferred. Per the wiki's summary of A Space and Time Without You, the sibling meant to fight in Khaenri'ah's defence until Direidyth showed them the key to their spaceship's Hibernation Chamber, which she had obtained from 500 years in the future through Istaroth's intervention; the sibling chose to go back for the Traveler.

## nod_krai_and_snezhnaya_revelations
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
From Version 6.0 the Song of the Welkin Moon chapter showed Rerir's history: he was an intelligence officer and Tholindis' fiancé, left Khaenri'ah to follow her through a moon gate, and survived as fragments that became the Wild Hunt monsters of Nod-Krai. Dottore revived him; Columbina, empowered with his own siphoned power, flung him through a gate beyond the sky that Dainsleif held open and then closed, sealing him away.
### LARGE +
The 7.x Snezhnaya chapters added that Khaenri'ahns are descended from the Cryo Archon Anastasya and Saarelainen, which is why her title is "Mother of Khaenri'ah". Their son had four-pointed star pupils. Saarelainen later bargained for her revival and his descendants' safety after the Heavenly Principles erased her. The account is told in item text and quests, and reaches different characters in different ways.
### ULTRA +
In 7.1, Pierro (Eftirvit) opened Hroptatyr's sealed notebook seeking an answer to Ronova's curse of death, Ronova, her authority confiscated by Celestia and mortally wounded by Pierro, died in Black Feathers Scattered at Parting (7.1). The Khaenri'ah Archon Quest has not been released as of 7.1.

### STANDARD
Khaenri'ah was founded about 6,000 years ago, around the time of Sal Vindagnyr's fall, as a nation without gods. It lay underground beneath what is now the Sumeru desert; its founders once lived aboveground.

## entry: Crimson Moon Dynasty era
@keys: Crimson Moon Dynasty, Balemoon Bloodfire, Perinheri
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
The Crimson Moon Dynasty was Khaenri'ah's first known ruling house, associated with worship of the Crimson Moon. Its descendants carry the Balemoon Bloodfire. The legend of Perinheri is set in this period. The dynasty fell in a coup and the Eclipse Dynasty took over; Irmin later hunted its descendants.

## entry: Eclipse Dynasty era
@keys: Eclipse Dynasty, Vinster King, Black Serpent Knights
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
The Eclipse Dynasty ruled from about the Archon War to the Cataclysm, embracing machines and "secrets from beyond the skies". Its last ruler was Irmin, the final Vinster King. Dainsleif and Halfdan served in its royal guard, the Black Serpent Knights.

### STANDARD
Irmin named the Traveler's sibling heir and "Savior", then used them as a vessel to absorb unlimited Abyssal power. When Vedrfolnir warned against it, Irmin blinded and jailed him. The palace raid that followed produced the Five Sinners.

Dainsleif organised a raid with Rerir, Hroptatyr, Rhinedottir and Surtalogi to free his brother Vedrfolnir. The Abyssal power in the sibling, left unguarded amid the monsters Irmin's mechanisms attracted, led the Sinners to take a sixth each. None of them defended the kingdom later.
