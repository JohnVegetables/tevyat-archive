## summary
### LITE
Mondstadt's history runs from the lost northern kingdom of Arcadia and the snow-buried mountain city of Sal Vindagnyr, through the tyrant god Decarabian's walled city, which fell about 2,600 years ago when his people rebelled and the wind spirit Barbatos became the Anemo Archon. The noble clans who followed turned tyrannical until the gladiator Vennessa, about 1,000 years ago, overthrew them and founded the Knights of Favonius. About 500 years ago the Cataclysm brought the dragon Durin down on Mondstadt before Barbatos and Dvalin killed him. The recent past (to 7.1) covers the Ragnvindr family tragedy, the Stormterror crisis and Varka's expedition to Nod-Krai.
### STANDARD +
The sections below follow the order the wiki timeline uses: Unified Civilization, War of Funerary Flame, the Interim Period, the Archon War and Age of Kings, the aristocratic period, Mondstadt Reborn, the Cataclysm, the post-Cataclysm centuries, and the recent past. Many dates are only "unknown years ago", and the ordering is partly an editors' reconstruction.

## unified_civilization
### ULTRA
In Teyvat's unified civilization, the people of Arcadia lived in the vast oak forests of what became Mondstadt. Their city-state Elysium stood in Windrest Peak under the Gunnhildr Clan. They built the Temple of Space for Asmoday and the Thousand Winds Temple for Istaroth, and worshipped the Three Moons, especially the Frost Moon goddess under the name Prusmene. After a disaster the Temple of Space was lifted into the sky, where Asmoday made it "Segment-Domain Tau", while the ruins left on the ground became her sanatorium.

Separately, refugees from a land of snow and strife found a green mountain, raised a monument on its peak and called their civilisation Sal Vindagnyr and the mountain Vindagnyr. They built a large castle there. Starsilver ore is native to the mountain. A great white tree stood at the heart of the city; its princess, born under it, inherited her priestess mother's gift of foresight and once dreamed of Durin spreading poison across the land, which her people feared as a curse. Celestia was moored over the mountain for an unknown span.

## war_of_funerary_flame
### ULTRA
About 6,000 years ago, according to the wiki timeline, an unknown calamity buried Arcadia under endless winter and the civilisation collapsed; the Thousand Winds Temple fell to ruin. Gunnhildr, the Knight of the Golden Bough, travelled the frozen north to find the legendary Golden Bough, a branch of Irminsul, hoping to restore the kingdom. He met the self-styled fae king Jack Frost King, fought him, and found the bough withered. Accepting that Arcadia could not return, he gathered its survivors and led them south to the tower where the wicked dragon had once been cast down, seeking shelter under Decarabian.

On Dragonspine, the Skyfrost Nail fell and shrouded Sal Vindagnyr in cloud and snow, then broke into three fragments, one of which turned the sacred tree into the Frostbearing Tree. The princess gave the outlander Imunlaukr a starsilver greatsword, and he left to seek a branch of Irminsul. While he was away the trees withered, the princess failed to finish her frescoes, tried to graft a branch onto another tree and died beside it when it withered. Imunlaukr returned to find everyone dead, left the sword between the frescoes and went to war, destroying three thriving kingdoms. The survivors hunted him, and he eventually gave up the life of a warrior. (The Imunlaukr Clan later bore his name.)

## interim_period
### ULTRA
Between the Great War and the Archon War the wiki places an unnamed stretch of peace. At an unknown point Boreas, the Wolf of the North, came to Mondstadt from Nod-Krai hoping to bring prosperity. Instead the region filled with blizzards and became inhospitable; canon is ambiguous about whether this was intentional. To shelter his city, Decarabian raised a storm wall around it.

## age_of_kings
### LARGE
More than 2,600 years ago Boreas declared war on Decarabian, but could not scratch the walled city, so the war between the two "Kings of Ice and Frost" became a stalemate. Decarabian's people began to see his rule as despotism. Gunnhildr's clan sought refuge in his city, but Gunnhildr's father led them out again; they built a shrine in the Thousand Winds Temple and prayed to Istaroth, and a tiny wind spirit, later Barbatos, answered and gained strength from their faith. A boy known only as the Nameless Bard befriended the spirit; he dreamed of birds, green grass and the world outside the walls. Inside the city a resistance used "Windblume" as its code word, the Red-Haired Warrior among the first to say it.
### ULTRA +
Further notes from this period: the first wine was supposedly brewed when the wind spirit in fox form fermented apples left with a careless guard; a wine for forgetting the past was also brewed so people could keep going. Leonard the adventurer invented the wind glider and failed to climb Pilos Peak (now Musk Reef), writing "Of Mountains and Seas" about the attempt. Boreas realised that blizzards could not nurture life and that he lacked the qualifications to be an Archon, so (in this placement) he let his body die and sent his power into the land; his spirit remains and can manifest a body of ice and wind. This placement is disputed, as noted in the uncertainties.

## decarabians_fall
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
About 2,600 years ago the people rose, led by the Nameless Bard, the wind spirit Barbatos, Decarabian's former lover Amos, Gunnhildr and the Red-Haired Warrior, with Gunnhildr's clan fighting too. Decarabian and Amos died, and his death unleashed a violent tempest.
### LARGE +
The Nameless Bard played his lyre with a melody that belonged to Istaroth, turning the storm into the song that became Mondstadt, but could not bear that power, died as the tower fell and was forgotten. Barbatos rose to godhood as the Anemo Archon and quietly kept the bard's lost name, Venti. The Red-Haired Warrior turned from Barbatos for reasons unclear; his line eventually produced Ragnvindr and the Ragnvindr family.
### ULTRA +
Barbatos then reshaped the land: his winds cleared snow and ice, Pilos Peak was levelled into Musk Reef, and the people moved from Decarabian's old city to Cider Lake to build the present Mondstadt. Prominent clans were the Gunnhildr Clan under Gunnhildr, the Imunlaukr Clan (leader at the time unknown) and the Lawrence Clan under Venerare. The Thousand Winds Temple was rebuilt for both Barbatos and Istaroth until Istaroth was forgotten, for unknown reasons. Seeing the people strong and wise enough to live without him, Barbatos left so he could never become a tyrant, and the Windblume Festival began in memory of those who fought for freedom; the code word later became tangled with real flowers and the festival became one of love.

## early_aristocracy
### ULTRA
From about 2,600 to 1,600 years ago aristocrats blessed with divine power led Mondstadt after Barbatos left, their clans descended from heroes of his early city (Lawrence, Gunnhildr, Imunlaukr and others), and at first they ruled for the people's good. Venerare of the Lawrence Clan built the statue of Barbatos before the Favonius Cathedral, with an inscription by the clan leaders pledging to defend Mondstadt. Many customs took shape: communal hunting, Ludi Harpastum (fifteen days celebrating wine, song and flight), Windblume and Weinlesefest.

Dvalin, the new Anemo sovereign, was born from converging elemental energy, was curious about the world and frightened humans by accident. Drawn by the Holy Lyre der Himmel, he landed beside Barbatos, who stayed calm and sang of the dragon's beauty; Dvalin stayed, learned human speech and Barbatos' singing. The Gunnhildr Clan returned to its ancient fortress in the north, the future Dornman Port, and founded Mondstadt's first sanctuary to Barbatos there; the port is said to be named for an ancestor called the Thorn King. At an unknown time the Hexenzirkel challenged Barbatos to a fight, he offered a song instead, and they became allies. Some time between 2,600 and 500 years ago the "Tyrant's Final Fury" occurred: for about a year a damaged Field Tiller fired from Decarabian's abandoned tower at anyone approaching, after Boreas, mistaking it for a challenger, had damaged it.

## late_aristocracy
### ULTRA
Around 1,600 years ago the Lawrence Clan became the foremost clan and degenerated into tyrants who enslaved and tormented commoners. Opponents were enslaved for labour or sport, historical knowledge was suppressed, the "starlit knights" served the nobles, royal mages kept people ignorant and the Church of Favonius split, its senior clergy backing the nobility. The Gunnhildrs were said to keep their honour but had little influence; at Dornman Port they threw a bishop sent from the capital out of the sanctuary tower and tore it down, and the port has had no church since. At some point Ursa the Drake began terrorising Mondstadt.

About 1,010 years ago Vennessa, born with a younger sister, Lind, into the Muratan tribe of the southern plains (exiled from Natlan by civil war), crossed Ursa and fled to Mondstadt, only to be enslaved. Her strength made her a gladiator; what bound her was her duty to her people. Around the same time the manipulative Eberhart, an illegitimate heir of Landrich's clan, plotted the aristocracy's downfall, secretly mastering the spear and sabotaging an aristocratic expedition to Sal Vindagnyr; the party never returned, and his fate is unknown. The Wanderer's Troupe, a musical and military company, tried and failed to overthrow the aristocrats in Mondstadt; Kreuzlied, a Lawrence who abandoned his clan, was the only known survivor and founded an underground organisation, and the Dawnlight Swordswoman's defiance inspired Ragnvindr, who later took the title Dawn Knight.

## mondstadt_reborn
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
About 1,000 years ago, after Ludi Harpastum, the cries of the oppressed woke Barbatos, who came down as the bard Venti and befriended Vennessa after she angered Barca Lawrence, heir of the Lawrence Clan. She was sentenced to gladiator matches for her people's freedom, the last against Ursa the Drake itself, which Lord Lawrence had bartered with to buy peace.
### LARGE +
Barbatos revealed himself on the field and gave Vennessa the power to win. Afterwards most aristocrats and their collaborators, including the royal mages, were exiled; the uncorrupted Gunnhildrs stayed. Vennessa founded the Knights of Favonius as its first Dandelion Knight and Grand Master and restored the Church of Favonius so tyrants could not return.
### ULTRA +
The recorded history is only the start of a longer rebellion. Barbatos forged a contract claiming the aristocrats had sold Mondstadt, and their own troops, to Morax; discovered, this "treachery against the wind" turned the soldiers against them, and scholars learned only much later that it was fake. Ragnvindr, Kreuzlied and Church faithful mobilised by the Nameless Shepherd helped. Afterwards the Gunnhildr Clan pushed to abolish hereditary fiefs. Ragnvindr turned the aristocrats' bathhouse into the first Mondstadt Library, which became the largest in northern Teyvat until the Great Fire of Fall Equinox cut it to one-sixth; the Barbatos statue was restored, its original inscription lost. A Knight of Boreas came to the city and joined the Knights, though his wolf companion preferred the wild; when it died he buried it and became a wanderer. The Four Winds were established, with Boreas as Wolf of the North, Dvalin as Dragon of the East and Vennessa both Lion of the South and Falcon of the West; Vennessa later ascended to Celestia at Windrise, where a great oak grew. Boreas' pack moved to Wolvendom at an unknown time.

## cataclysm
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
About 500 years ago Arundolyn, Grand Master and the "Lion of Light", and his friend Rostam, the "Wolf Pup", led the Knights to fight monsters in Khaenri'ah during the Cataclysm; Rostam died there, and Arundolyn gave up fighting.
### LARGE +
Rostam had mentored a man called the White Knight, possibly Roland, who fought the monsters until people feared him as the Bloodstained Knight and who later joined the Abyss Order. Meanwhile the alchemist Rhinedottir sent her creation Durin to Teyvat, where he devastated Mondstadt; with no Lionfang Knight strong enough to stop him, Barbatos called on Dvalin and together they killed Durin. Dvalin swallowed Durin's poisoned, Abyss-tainted blood and slept in Decarabian's tower.
### ULTRA +
Durin's body fell on Dragonspine, his blood seeped into the mountain and revived the Frostbearing Tree, and his heart still beats in Wyrmrest Valley; his blood set as Scarlet Quartz. Rostam's lover Rosalyne-Kruzchka Lohefalter, who had left to study at the Akademiya just before the Cataclysm, returned, learned of his death and turned herself into living flame to avenge him, becoming the Crimson Witch of Flames. Dornman Port nearly fell and many knights died defending it; their graves lie beneath the city. Worship of the Four Winds faded afterwards.

## post_cataclysm
### ULTRA
Before the Crimson Witch burned away, Pierro of the Fatui found her and gave her a Cryo Delusion to quench the flames; she became Signora, eighth of the Fatui Harbingers. About 400 years ago a text on Mondstadt's customs, quoted in an in-game book, was written by an ancestor of Ella Musk and Dr. Edith. Around 300 years ago Olaf of Springvale was frozen by a Cryo Regisvine while hunting on Dragonspine; Springvale grew around a spring of melted Dragonspine snow. Less than 200 years ago a Lochfolk named Callirhoe, the Spring Fairy, left Fontaine and blessed Springvale's waters.

## recent_past
### LARGE
Roughly 50 years ago the Spring Fairy befriended Old Finch as a child, the basis for a book. Amber's grandfather, a Liyue mercenary leader, moved to Mondstadt, joined the Knights and founded the Outriders. Seamus Pegg settled in Mondstadt, rose to Cardinal of Daybreak and married Frederica Gunnhildr; their daughters Jean and Barbara were later born, and the couple separated, with Jean staying with her mother. About a decade ago Kaeya was left near the Dawn Winery and raised by Crepus Ragnvindr alongside Diluc. Diluc became Cavalry Captain at 14, the youngest captain on record, and Jean became Dandelion Knight at 15.
### ULTRA +
Four years before the Traveler's arrival Crepus Ragnvindr died on Diluc's eighteenth birthday after using a Delusion against an attacker everyone took for Ursa the Drake (lured by a Fatui metal bug; later revealed as an imitation bred from Eroch and Dottore's experiments on the unsealed drake). Inspector Eroch ordered the cause covered up and Diluc resigned in fury; the same night Kaeya revealed his origins to him. Afterwards a Segment of Dottore slew the imitation, publicly credited as Ursa, leaving Mondstadt in the Fatui's debt. Eroch was later exposed as a traitor and purged, and Jean was promoted from Captain to Master of the Knights. Amber's grandfather resigned and vanished. Albedo arrived about three years ago with a note from Rhinedottir and became Chief Alchemist, and Klee was entrusted to the Knights. About six months before the Traveler arrived, Varka took the Knights' elite and the cavalry on an expedition, with Jean as Acting Grand Master; Diluc returned and began his night patrols as the "Darknight Hero". The official manga's Ludi Harpastum story, listed on the wiki timeline as the last Ludi Harpastum, involves Dottore's schemes, Collei and a Fatui death; it is a side medium.

## story_present
@version: 1.0
### LARGE
In the Prologue the Traveler arrived after being attacked by the Unknown God and meeting Paimon, and with Venti and the Knights freed the dragon "Stormterror", Dvalin, from the Abyss Order's hold. Immediately afterwards the Fatui Harbinger Signora took Venti's Anemo Gnosis.
### ULTRA +
Dvalin had been poisoned by Durin's blood, and the Abyss Order had persuaded him that Mondstadt had abandoned him; he is honoured again since the Prologue. Venti healed by drawing on the Windrise oak's connection to Celestia.

## recent_events_5_to_7
@version: 5.6
@spoiler: major
### ULTRA
At a recent Windblume Festival (Paralogism, 5.6) Albedo's closed trial for murder was a long-planned trap that exposed beings born of Durin's lingering life force. A full assault on the city followed, repelled by the Knights, Diluc, the Church and the Traveler. Albedo convened a Tripartite Conference of the Knights and Church, the Anemo Archon and the Hexenzirkel, drained Durin's Core and, with Venti singing as Windblume Star, transmuted a kindly storybook dragon into a synthetic human body, so that Durin was reborn as a human in Mondstadt.

In 6.4 Varka's expedition, which had ended in Nod-Krai after the Knights fought the Wild Hunt from Favonius Keep, came home. Windrest Peak, with Dornman Port and Millhaven, opened in 6.5. In 6.6 Lohen's story quest found the real Ursa still held in Eroch's hidden experiment site, where Theodore had restarted serum experiments on its flesh; Varka, Diluc and the Traveler killed it, and Jean rescinded Lohen's commendation and planned promotion to Captain of the 5th Company. As of 7.1 Mondstadt is at peace and the Archon Quest is in Snezhnaya; Frederica Gunnhildr is on a secret mission known only to Varka and Jean.

## entry: Arcadia and Sal Vindagnyr Timeline
@keys: Arcadia, Sal Vindagnyr, Vindagnyr, Elysium, Golden Bough, Skyfrost Nail
@keys2: Mondstadt, history, Gunnhildr, Imunlaukr
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Arcadia was the oak-forest civilisation of old Mondstadt, with Elysium in Windrest Peak under the Gunnhildr Clan. About 6,000 years ago an unknown calamity buried it in winter; Gunnhildr, the Knight of the Golden Bough, failed to find a living branch of Irminsul and led survivors south to shelter under Decarabian. On Vindagnyr (Dragonspine) the city of Sal Vindagnyr died beneath snow after the Skyfrost Nail fell, while the outlander Imunlaukr was away seeking Irminsul.

## entry: Fall of Decarabian
@keys: Decarabian, Nameless Bard, Windblume code word, Kings of Ice and Frost, Age of Kings
@keys2: Mondstadt, Barbatos, Venti, rebellion
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
About 2,600 years ago, with Boreas' blizzards beating on his walls, Decarabian's people rose in rebellion under the code word Windblume. Decarabian and Amos died; the Nameless Bard's last song turned the tempest into Mondstadt's wind but cost the bard his life. The wind spirit became the Anemo Archon and kept the bard's name, Venti, then built the new city on Cider Lake and later withdrew.

## entry: Vennessa's Rebellion Timeline
@keys: Vennessa, Barca Lawrence, Lord Lawrence, Lawrence Clan, Ursa the Drake
@keys2: Mondstadt, aristocracy, gladiator, Knights of Favonius
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
About 1,000 years ago the gladiator Vennessa, a Muratan enslaved by the aristocrats, fought Ursa the Drake with Barbatos' help; the nobles were exiled and she founded the Knights of Favonius and restored the Church. A longer rebellion also involved a forged treaty selling Mondstadt to Morax.

## entry: The Cataclysm in Mondstadt Timeline
@keys: Durin, Arundolyn, Rostam, Crimson Witch of Flames, Lion of Light
@keys2: Mondstadt, cataclysm, Dvalin, Dragonspine
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
About 500 years ago Rostam died in Khaenri'ah, Arundolyn stopped fighting, and the dragon Durin devastated Mondstadt until Barbatos and Dvalin killed him on Dragonspine. Rostam's lover became the Crimson Witch of Flames and later Signora.

## entry: Recent Past of Mondstadt
@keys: Crepus' death, Eroch's cover-up, Darknight Hero, Varka's expedition
@keys2: Mondstadt, Diluc, Knights of Favonius, Jean
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
Four years before the Traveler, Crepus Ragnvindr died fighting what was taken for Ursa (later revealed as an imitation), Diluc left the Knights and Kaeya revealed his past; Eroch was later purged and Jean became Master.
