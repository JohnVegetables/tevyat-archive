## summary
### LITE
The Three Moons are three ancient moons and the three goddesses tied to them, also called the Three Moon Sisters: Aria of the Eternal Moon, Canon of the Frost Moon and Sonnet of the Iridescent Moon. The Dragon King Nibelung raised them from the earth of what is now Nod-Krai to watch over Teyvat in his place. In the war of funerary flame the Heavenly Principles shattered the Eternal and Iridescent Moons and threw the Frost Moon beyond the false sky, and all three goddesses died. Columbina, born in Nod-Krai as the new Frost Moon goddess, now holds the power of all three moons as the Trilune Goddess.
### STANDARD
The Three Moons are three ancient moons and the goddesses bound to them, also called the Three Moon Goddesses or Three Moon Sisters: Aria of the Eternal Moon, Canon of the Frost Moon and Sonnet of the Iridescent Moon. The Dragon King Nibelung raised them from the planet's crust, at what is now Nod-Krai, to oversee Teyvat and wield his authority while he travelled the sea of stars. Their power is called the Trilunar Authority or Trilune. In the war of funerary flame the Heavenly Principles shattered the Eternal and Iridescent Moons and flung the Frost Moon beyond the false sky. All three goddesses then died, because Teyvat no longer accepted them. To hide this, the Heavenly Principles hung an illusory "shadowmoon" in the night sky.

Their story is the core of the Nod-Krai Archon Quest, Song of the Welkin Moon (5.8-6.6). There, Columbina, born in Nod-Krai as the new Frost Moon goddess (Kuutar), receives the authority of all three moons and becomes the Trilune Goddess. She defeats Dottore, who had stolen the moons' power, and the Frost Moon returns inside Teyvat's sky.

## established
### LITE
- Eternal Moon (Aria): the first and largest moon. Its gravity was said to raise the tides. It was shattered.
- Frost Moon (Canon): the second moon and the brightest. It was banished beyond the false sky, but has now returned to Teyvat's sky with Columbina as its goddess.
- Iridescent Moon (Sonnet): the third and smallest moon, with a faint light that made fleeting halos. It was shattered, and its remains became the Crimson Moon.
### STANDARD +
- The sisters lived in the Lunar Palace. One at a time, they ruled the night sky, riding through the heavens in a silver carriage or chariot and changing places about three times a month.
- When a goddess dies, her remains can condense into a Moon Marrow. The Moon Marrows hold what is left of the sisters' power and are a source of Light Realm energy.
- Before the war, many civilisations worshipped the moons rather than the Heavenly Principles. Some accounts also say the moons received the dead, with "boatmen" ferrying souls to cities upon the moon.
### LARGE +
- Nibelung made the moons with draconic Secret Source technology. The Eternal Moon in particular is called a piece of that technology (Natlan Archon Quest, 5.3). Nicole describes the goddesses as "shadows of the planet", extensions of Nibelung's will, created to keep the world alive and working.
- The sisters agreed to share their power equally so that none of them would grow too strong. They saw their role as protecting Teyvat, not ruling it.
- A Frost Moon map description says the ancient spirits of the frostbound reaches called the three silver lights "Dilimbabbar", and that the lights first marked the waxing and waning of light, the seasons and the passing years.

## history
@version: 6.3
@spoiler: major
### LITE
Nibelung left Teyvat and the moons kept it for him. When the Heavenly Principles arrived, the sisters let them stay. They then watched the Heavenly Principles remake the world's rules and destroy the dragons. When Nibelung returned, corrupted by the Abyss, the sisters could not agree whether to side with him. He imprisoned them and turned their moons into weapons. The Heavenly Principles answered by destroying or banishing the moons, and the goddesses died.
### LARGE +
The Heavenly Principles, the First Descender, came several millennia after Nibelung left. Canon calls them "a guest in our home". They reshaped Teyvat down to its foundations, partly by using the sisters' authorities. Sonnet opposed this. Aria and Canon chose to wait and see whether the new order would help the world. After a 40-year war, the Heavenly Principles and the Four Shades overthrew the Seven Sovereigns and raised the false sky. Because the moons, also creations of Nibelung, never stepped in, the dragons came to see the sisters as traitors. The Heavenly Principles let the moons remain because their task of maintaining the planet did not conflict with heavenly aims. When celestial nails began falling on civilisations that pushed against the rules, the sisters were horrified. Canon tried to help some of them, but the sisters obeyed in the end, since their duty was to keep Teyvat intact until Nibelung returned.

In the Funerary Year Nibelung came back carrying the Abyss. He took the sisters' disagreement for disloyalty and sealed them inside the "moon's reflection", a prison of warped space and time. Then he turned the moons into weapons against Celestia. The Heavenly Principles treated this as a breach of their peace: they shattered the Eternal and Iridescent Moons and flung the Frost Moon beyond the false sky. Most dragons celebrated.

Cut off from moons that were destroyed or exiled, the goddesses lost their "rationale" for existing in Teyvat. Alice later explains that the world itself then rejected them and drained their strength. Inside the prison they were shielded from all of this, but could neither escape nor learn what was happening outside.

Their ends came separately:
- Aria died soon after the war. Part of her remains condensed into the Eternal Moon Marrow, which the Frostmoon Scions later kept as a sacred relic. Other pieces of the Eternal Moon rained onto Teyvat, and the rest was hidden beyond the false sky.
- Sonnet, in agony over her shattered moon, fused her soul with the Abyss to stay alive. She became the baleful Crimson Moon, which Khaenri'ah's Crimson Moon Dynasty revered as a source of Abyssal power.
- Canon lived longest because the Frost Moon was still intact. She guided the survivors of Hyperborea to Hiisi Island, and by their tradition gave them hereditary gifts such as kuuvahki sensitivity and their antler crowns. Then she too faded. The survivors took the name Frostmoon Scions in her honour.
### ULTRA +
The time loop: in True Moon (6.3), Columbina flees into the moon's reflection, travels backwards through time and meets the three sisters during their imprisonment. When they learn their fate, they decide to make up for their earlier indecision. Canon, though her Frost Moon survives, agrees, saying she could never go on living without her sisters. They transfer the authority of all three moons to Columbina, and Canon declares her "the one and only moon goddess of this world". Now mortal, the sisters feel the prison dissolving their bodies, and Aria comforts the frightened Sonnet. At their request Columbina sends them back to the real world, where they die as history recorded. To get back to her own time, Columbina splits herself in three. Her body becomes moonlight poured over Nod-Krai (the origin of kuuvahki). Her soul stays in the reflection, anchored by Luonnotar. Her Trilune Authority is hidden inside the Frost Moon. This is why, in the present, Sonnet's dying spirit greets Columbina fondly although Columbina has never met her.

## present_day
@version: 6.3
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
In the present (5.1-6.3), the false sky briefly cracked over Natlan, Dottore stole the moons' power in Nod-Krai and was defeated by Columbina as the Trilune Goddess, and the Frost Moon returned inside Teyvat's sky, ending the shadowmoon illusion.
### LARGE +
- During the Abyss's assault on Natlan (5.1), Mavuika's strike broke the false sky for a moment, revealing the true sky and the Eternal Moon's shattered remnants. The crack closed, and the public was told it was an astronomical anomaly.
- The Fatui pulled Eternal Moon fragments down into Nod-Krai and built the Kuuvahki Experimental Design Bureau around one of them.
- Dottore schemed for the moons' power. On Moon-Prayer Night (6.2) he seized Columbina's Moon Marrows and later built an artificial one, briefly making himself a false god with trilunar authority. Columbina returned as the Trilune Goddess with the sisters' greater authority and defeated him (6.3). Sandrone sacrificed herself to complete the plan that revived Columbina. She was later reactivated and is active again as of 7.1.
- The Frost Moon was drawn back inside the false sky and lights Teyvat's night again. The shadowmoon lost its purpose, faded, and vanished during Lantern Rite, releasing the fragment of Zibai's soul held within it.
- Lauma keeps the Eternal Moon Marrow and Arlecchino keeps the Iridescent Moon Marrow.

As of 7.1, Columbina is the sole moon goddess and holds the Trilune, and the Frost Moon remains in Teyvat's sky. The Traveler can visit it: Columbina took them there, where the Heavenly Principles had hidden the twins' spaceship after the cataclysm. Dottore survived his defeat inside Irminsul as the "Exalted Master of the Heretical Path". He was finally killed when Nahida burned Irminsul (6.6), and he did not use moon power in that last fight. No 7.0-7.1 Snezhnaya content found in this research changes the sisters' fate.

## accounts
### LARGE
After the moons disappeared, the people of Teyvat passed down conflicting legends:
- Liyue books (Moonlit Bamboo Forest, Records of Jueyun, Heart's Desire) tell of three sister goddesses in a Lunar Palace who took turns crossing the sky in a silver carriage. Some versions say the sisters betrayed and killed one another. Others say only two died and the moon now in the sky is one of their corpses.
- One weapon legend (Moonpiercer) says the last surviving goddess grieved so much that she never left her palace again.
- Dragon-side accounts (Fractured Halo) treat the sisters as traitors to Nibelung and record that the dragons cheered their deaths.
- The sisters' own account, given to Columbina, describes naive acceptance rather than coercion.
- Nilotpala Lotuses are said to be tied to the three sisters, which is why they bloom at night.
- The angel Nabu Malikata told King Deshret stories of the three moons, and from them he deduced that the false sky exists.
- Ziqiao, one of Zibai's Deadly Selves, told the moons' story to people she met across Liyue. The Liyue books grew from her tales and are not always faithful to them. Zibai herself once served as a Moon Envoy, sent by the Heavenly Principles to help the sisters.
- Nicole and another angel, writing together as "Mr. Nine", put the Three Moons into Flowers for Princess Fischl. The Mitternachts Waltz text tells how two of three bright moons were "shredded by a sword".
- Tsurumi Island murals address the moons in Latin as "Trivia". Columbina's Elemental Burst calls the moon goddesses "Iynx".

## open_questions
### STANDARD
- Canon does not settle whether the sisters were coerced partners or willing observers of the Heavenly Principles' order. Both views appear in-game.
- It is not clear whether the Heavenly Principles knew Nibelung had imprisoned the goddesses before they struck the moons.
### LARGE +
- Exactly how far the Trilunar Authority reaches: the wiki groups it as rule over Life, Death, Time and Space, later passed to the Four Shades. Treat this as summary, not a verbatim definition.
- The meaning of Moonlit Bamboo Forest's "one love", the morning stars, and of the names "Aralos", "Solnari" and "Cannon" on Alice's note in a teaser.
- How long the sisters were imprisoned, given the prison's distorted flow of time.

## connections
### STANDARD
Nibelung (creator); the Heavenly Principles and Four Shades (rivals and inheritors of their authority); the false sky and shadowmoon (the cover-up); the Moon Marrows, kuuvahki and the Moon Wheel (what remains of them); the Frostmoon Scions and Hyperborea (their worshippers); Khaenri'ah's Crimson Moon Dynasty (Sonnet's corruption); Columbina (successor and Trilune Goddess).
### LARGE +
Gameplay: Nod-Krai's Lunar reactions and Moonsign mechanics are presented in-world as drawing on the moons' power (kuuvahki). See the system entries for the rules. The Spiral Abyss shows lunar imagery, including an Abyssal Moon Spire and phase shifts called the "Moment of Syzygy". Whether these connect directly to the Three Moons is unconfirmed.

## entry: Moon's Reflection
@keys: moon's reflection, moons reflection, lunar prison
@version: 6.3
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The "moon's reflection" is a prison of warped space and time that Nibelung made to keep the Three Moon Sisters out of the war of funerary flame. Every path inside it leads back to the moment of their imprisonment, so they could never escape or learn what had happened outside. In Song of the Welkin Moon, Columbina banishes herself there to escape Dottore. Travelling backwards through it, she meets the imprisoned sisters, who give her their authority. She then leaves her soul there, anchored by Luonnotar, until she is revived in the present (6.3).

## entry: Trilune Authority
@keys: Trilune, Trilunar Authority, trilunar authority, Trilune Goddess
@version: 6.3
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The Trilune, or Trilunar Authority, is the combined power of the three moons. Aria says it is bound to Teyvat's most fundamental rules. Dottore took its raw power by capturing the Moon Marrows and later made an artificial marrow. The Traveler was unaffected by the authority he gained, and authority drawn from marrows proved weaker than what the living sisters had handed to Columbina. Since True Moon (6.3), Columbina is the Trilune Goddess, the one moon goddess of Teyvat. Lauma and Arlecchino keep the surviving Moon Marrows.

## entry: Moon Sisters and the Heavenly Principles
@keys: Moon Sisters, Moon Goddesses, Three Moon Sisters
@keys2: Heavenly Principles, First Descender, false sky, celestial nail
@version: 6.7
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
The sisters first welcomed the Heavenly Principles as a guest and let their authorities be used to rewrite Teyvat's laws. Sonnet objected, while Aria and Canon waited to see what would happen. Under the new order, angels guided humanity. The sisters got on well with the angels and, by some accounts, even with the Four Shades. Many peoples worshipped the moons for comfort against the harsh rules. On the wiki's reading of the 6.7 world quest The Unmanned Vessel, around this time the sisters gave up their old forms for human ones. They also witnessed the union of the angel Koitar and the voyager Anacharsis about thirty years before Nibelung's return. The celestial nails horrified them. They had begun preparing countermeasures after Hyperborea fell, but Nibelung returned before they could act.

## entry: Xiuhcoatl and the silent moons
@keys: Xiuhcoatl
@keys2: Three Moons, Moon Sisters, moon goddesses
@spoiler: minor
### LARGE
According to the weapon text of Fractured Halo, the Pyro Sovereign Xiuhcoatl went to each of the three moons in search of Nibelung during a time of upheaval. "Shadows poured over the sky", snowfields turned black and volcanoes clogged with ice. None of the sisters answered him. Canon does not say whether their silence was chosen.
