## summary
### LITE
Angels were beautiful winged beings made by the Heavenly Principles to serve as Celestia's divine envoys during the era of the unified civilization. They guided mortals, taught them language and philosophies of nature, and carried oracles, and were commanded from birth to love every creature equally. After the war of funerary flame the Heavenly Principles cursed them: an angel who loves one being above all others loses body and mind. Almost all angels have since fallen into Seelie; only a few, such as Nicole and Zibai, keep their minds and forms.
### STANDARD +
Angels are tied to the Irminsul trees and the Ley Lines, and a few survivors were later worshipped as gods (Nabu Malikata in Sumeru, the Lord of the Night in Natlan, Zibai in Lang-Gan).

## established
### LITE
- Creators: the Heavenly Principles made the angels; they served Celestia as envoys.
- Era: the unified civilization, long before the Archon War.
- Duty: guide humanity, teach languages and "philosophies of nature", bring oracles about the future, and judge fairly.
- Law: love all creatures of Teyvat equally.
- Fate: after the war of funerary flame a curse struck the angels, and most became Seelie.
### STANDARD +
- Every angel named in canon is female. Their wings are pale silver, and they once wore crowns of sevenfold radiance, probably an allusion to the seven elements; the crowns were too bright for mortals to look at directly.
- They have a limited ability to foresee the future, even far ahead, and are immortal and long-lived even after their powers and form are lost.
- Koitar was the first angel and Nicole the last.
- Angels were assigned to Irminsul and Ley Line duties: two are known to have been charged with protecting Irminsul trees and Ley Lines (Sybilla guarded the tree of Meropis).
### LARGE +
- The artifact set Finale of the Deep Galleries records the angels' creed: love every being on earth with all heart, soul and might, as morning dew longs for the dawn. It calls them the heavenly court's most loyal servants and "flawless arbiters of justice", who wove a veil for the ruler of the firmament and carried sacred revelations to all realms.
- Mortals could seek angels out by descending into the Irminsul altars found within Domains.
- The same text says the name written on the crowns was the love promised to all creatures, or perhaps a mandate to rule the nations of the earth. Koitar's crown also served as a shackle binding her to her duty.

## appearance
### LARGE
Pale silver wings that shimmer like moonlit flame, and a distinct headpiece for each angel: a halo for Nicole, crowns for Zibai, Koitar and the Lord of the Night, horns for Nabu Malikata. Nabu Malikata also wore an amethyst crown. Murals in Dragonspine and in the Lost Palace show angels with crests or headdresses, usually larger than humans, though Nicole and Zibai match tall human women.
### ULTRA +
The crowns were wrought of the bones of the earth and the stars of the sky, according to the artifact text, and they were shackles as well as ornaments. A Seelie's horn-like crest echoes Nabu Malikata's horns; the wiki suggests the headdress on murals may be the same feature, which is an interpretation, not a statement.

## history
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Koitar, the first angel and envoy to Hyperborea, met a youth beneath the Irminsul in the far north. He was Anacharsis, a voyager from beyond Teyvat in a Hyperborean boy's body, and he told her the forbidden origins of the world. Her crown broke, she felt love and freedom she had never known, and they married and rebelled against the Heavenly Principles. When Hyperborea was struck down with Celestial Nails in the war of funerary flame, the Heavenly Principles wiped Koitar's memory and cursed her kin. Those who clung to power over wisdom were corrupted into dragon-like beasts; those who chose wisdom over power lost form, soul and memory and became Seelie.
### LARGE +
- Koitar urged other angels to join her and warned them of the fate waiting for them. With Anacharsis she ruled Hyperborea; the length of that rule is stated inconsistently (see uncertainties).
- Artisans of Hyperborea, using a portion of the Authority of Life Koitar took from Naberius, forged Snowland fae upon the bones of giant beasts, hoping they might one day create a perfect being able to merge with the world.
- A less reliable account, Volume 4 of Records of Jueyun, says the disaster came thirty days after their union and that the pair fled into exile.
- After the war, Nabu Malikata tried to hide beneath the Great Red Sand; whether this let her escape the curse is told inconsistently (see uncertainties). Nicole gave up her voice and half her wisdom and power rather than give up either entirely, which let her keep her preferred form.
- In Natlan, the angels and Seelie were nearly all killed by dragons after the great war of vengeance, leaving one envoy alive.
### ULTRA +
Zibai was appointed a Moon Envoy by Istaroth, carrying news between the Three Moons and the Heavenly Principles. When a Celestial Nail was sent against Lang-Gan after the Eternal and Iridescent Moons were destroyed, she ignored Istaroth's warning and sacrificed herself so her people could flee east. At her death she split into the Three Deadly Selves, and Istaroth divided her soul among them: the Heavenly Soul in the First Deadly Self, Ziju, held in the false moon; the Human Soul in the Third, Ziqiao, who walked among humans and adepti and gave rise to the White Horse Adeptus tale. After the Frost Moon's return, the Traveler and Zhongli reunited the three parts during Lantern Rite and restored her.

## curse
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The Moment of Oblivion text states the curse plainly: an angel who gazes into another's eyes and gives to one alone the love meant for all will lose her divine beauty to the wind and her mind to dust, until a Seelie rises from her remains to drift in search of fading memories. The Heavenly Principles imposed it on the whole race because of Koitar's betrayal, and stripped Koitar herself of name and form.
### LARGE +
The curse is conditional: it falls on those who forsake the commandment of universal love. Angels who gave up wisdom for power became beasts and dragons without a distinct element (Ursa the Drake is one); angels who gave up power for wisdom decayed into Seelie, who now offer only small treasures to those who follow them. Angels who kept their forms but lost their power are no longer counted as angels, according to the wiki, apart from some who died before the punishment and were later resurrected.

## known_angels
### LITE
- Koitar: the first angel, Goddess of the Dawnstar, rebel of Hyperborea; status unknown.
- Nicole: the last angel; voiceless, alive, a member of the Hexenzirkel.
- Zibai: a Moon Envoy of Lang-Gan; the White Horse Adeptus of Liyue, restored to her full self.
- Nabu Malikata: Goddess of Flowers in Sumeru; dead.
### STANDARD +
- Yohualtecuhtin, the Lord of the Night: Natlan's Night Kingdom god; alive as of 7.1.
- Sybilla: the Golden Bee of Remuria; died to create Phobos.
### LARGE +
- Mr. Nine: a Seelie, once an angel, who recovered speech by possessing authors.
- Ursa the Drake: a corrupted dragon-form angel; dead.
### ULTRA +
The wiki's table lists Koitar as an archangel with unknown status; Ursa the Drake, Nabu Malikata and Sybilla as deceased; Zibai, Mr. Nine, the Lord of the Night and Nicole as alive. Nabu Malikata, Zibai and the Lord of the Night are called gods in their regions. Individual angels fell differently: Sybilla was worn down to a golden bee while guarding a tree; Ursa chose power; Mr. Nine decayed into a Seelie but regained speech.

## legacy
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
Angels shaped the nations that survived the Archon War. Nabu Malikata co-ruled Sumeru with King Deshret and Greater Lord Rukkhadevata and created the Jinn and Khvarena. The Lord of the Night aided the tribes of Natlan, brokered the agreement among Xbalanque, Ronova and Kukulkan, and still guards the Night Kingdom and its Ley Lines. Sybilla's sacrifice produced the Symphony Phobos for Remus. The fae begun by Koitar's artisans still seem active in Snezhnaya.
### ULTRA +
Sybilla's rebellion partly worked: Remurian arts spread in secret through orders such as the Golden Troupe and the Marechaussee Hunters, and the Seal of Chymical Marriage she taught humans was later used to restore the Moon Goddess in the Archon Quest True Moon. Phobos failed to free Remuria, because Sybilla's will was vague: she wished happiness for all who are called human, so Phobos tried to grant everyone's wishes, harmful ones included.

## accounts
### LARGE
Sources tell the angels' story differently. The Finale of the Deep Galleries artifact texts, written in an archaic voice, treat the angels as loyal servants undone by one angel's love. Records of Jueyun gives a shorter version in which the disaster sends Koitar and her lover into exile. The wiki calls the Hyperborean account more reliable. Nicole, herself an angel, says that once they were tasked with guiding human beings, but because some chose a different path the whole race was punished.
### ULTRA +
Mr. Nine's partnership with Nicole shows another face of the survivors: Nicole, who finds writing tedious, supplied story ideas, and Mr. Nine possessed authors in their sleep to write them, using this to regain her ability to speak.

## open_questions
### STANDARD
- Whether the curse can be lifted or a Seelie restored.
- Koitar's exact status; through Boreas she remembers Anacharsis and awaits Anacharsis's freedom.
- Why a few angels keep their forms.
- How many angels remain.
### LARGE +
- Whether the crowns' sevenfold radiance really refers to the seven elements.
- What the angels who lived in Snezhnaya did beyond the Hyperborean episode; canon leaves this open.

## connections
### LITE
Linked to the Heavenly Principles, Celestia, Seelie, the Irminsul and Ley Lines, the Three Moons and their Moon Envoys, Hyperborea and the war of funerary flame.
### STANDARD +
Boreas, a wolf Koitar named "North Wind", was unknowingly blessed by her and became a god; he fought Decarabian in the Archon War but judged himself unworthy to become the Anemo Archon, and later became the North Wind of Mondstadt's Four Winds. Seelie are the visible survivors of the curse, found with courts across Teyvat, including the Royal Court of the Seelie in Nod-Krai.

## roleplay
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Angels who still hold their form are rare, long-lived and formal about duty, yet marked by the punishment; portray them as watchful and grave rather than saccharine. A Seelie is not a personality: it is a mindless echo that follows memories and treasure, so avoid giving ordinary Seelie speech.
### LARGE +
Keep the curse's trigger central: love for one above all is dangerous to an angel. A surviving angel should not discuss it casually; the subject is old, painful and forbidden. Do not show Seelie as chatty companions or bring fallen angels back without canon support.

## entry: Koitar, the first angel
@keys: Koitar, Goddess of the Dawnstar, Dawn's Navigator, Mother of Dawn, Princess of the Morning Star
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Koitar was the first angel; the Hymns of the Far North call her the first and fairest daughter of the creator god. She was envoy to Hyperborea and lived beneath its Irminsul. Anacharsis told her the forbidden origins of Teyvat; her crown broke; they married and ruled Hyperborea until the Celestial Nails fell. The Heavenly Principles wiped her memory and confined her to the Royal Court of the Seelie, where she grieved among kin she could not recognise. She named a wolf Boreas, and through him recovered the memory of Anacharsis; she now waits in Hyperborea.
### LARGE +
Before her rebellion she carried out the Heavenly Principles' punishments on nations. She warned the other angels of their fate. The Hymns of the Far North still invoke her as "wife of Seutervoinen", the Frostmoon Scions' name for Anacharsis.

## entry: Nicole Reeyn
@keys: Nicole Reeyn
@keys2: angel, Hexenzirkel, voiceless, halo, Seelie
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Nicole is the last and youngest angel, a voiceless Pyro catalyst-user and Hexenzirkel member with the codename "N". Other angels hid and shielded her during Koitar's rebellion. She paid for the curse by giving up her voice and half her wisdom and power, which let her keep her form and halo. A statue of an angel stands at her Witch's Lodge.
### LARGE +
She is the friend of the Seelie Mr. Nine, and the "Mr. Nine" author persona was her invention so stories could be written without her speaking. Her own entry covers her personality; here she matters as proof that an angel can survive the curse by compromise.

## entry: Zibai
@keys: Zibai, White Horse Adeptus, Ziju, Ziqiao, Three Deadly Selves
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Zibai was an angel and Moon Envoy who governed Lang-Gan as the God of Soil and Grain, then became the White Horse Adeptus among Liyue's Eight Adepts. She sacrificed herself so Lang-Gan's people could flee, splitting into three Deadly Selves; the Traveler and Zhongli reunited them after the Archon Quest True Moon. She is among the few angels alive and whole by 7.1.

## entry: Nabu Malikata
@keys: Nabu Malikata, Goddess of Flowers, Lord of Flowers
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Nabu Malikata, Goddess of Flowers, was an angel (the wiki also counts her a Moon Envoy, from her title "Moon Maiden") who made the Jinn and Khvarena, and one of Sumeru's three companion-gods with Greater Lord Rukkhadevata and King Deshret. She joined Koitar's rebellion, helped hide the youngest sister, and warned Deshret against forbidden knowledge before agreeing to help him. She was killed by the Heavenly Principles; a portion of her remains was kept inside Collei until the Archon Quest "Your Past Is Like a Garden" removed it.

## entry: The Lord of the Night as an angel
@keys: Yohualtecuhtin, Lord of the Night, Lord of the Crossroads
@version: 5.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Yohualtecuhtin, the Lord of the Night, is identified as an angel in Beneath the Secret Source. She rebuilt a Ley Line network from the broken lines of Natlan into the Night Kingdom, and the tribes communicated with her through obsidian and sacrifice. She is among the angels still alive in 7.1.

## entry: Sybilla and Ursa the Drake
@keys: Sybilla, Golden Bee, Ursa the Drake
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Sybilla, the Golden Bee, guarded the tree of Meropis until her form wore down to a golden bee, left with Remus to build Remuria, and died to create Phobos. Ursa the Drake is the opposite fate: an angel that chose power, became a dragon without an element, and plagued Mondstadt a thousand years ago until Barbatos drove it off and it was sealed. Eroch unsealed it for experiments with Dottore, and Varka, Diluc and the Traveler eventually killed it.

## entry: Mr. Nine
@keys: Mr. Nine
@keys2: Seelie, author, Fischl
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Mr. Nine, author of "Legend of the Shattered Halberd" and "Flowers for Princess Fischl", is in truth a female Seelie and former angel who possessed authors in their sleep to write Nicole's story ideas. She regained speech this way, and Nicole later sent her to watch the situation in Nod-Krai.
