## summary
### LITE
Irminsul is the primordial silver-white world tree of Teyvat. It grows downward in a vast cavern deep underground, and the Ley Lines are its roots. Through them it gathered the world's memories into one repository. The Dendro Archon's consciousness is bound to it, and outsiders such as the Traveler have no records in it. In Version 6.6, after Dottore hijacked and corrupted it, Nahida burned Irminsul with the Pyro Gnosis, and Aaru took over its role.
### STANDARD +
Irminsul is older than the Heavenly Principles. They took it over and remade it into a recorder of all information and an anchor for the world's fate.

## established
### LITE
Tighnari (3.0) says the Dendro Archon is called the God of Wisdom because of this link, and that her power is said to be a manifestation of Irminsul.
### STANDARD +
Silver-white trees with glowing blue streaks, known as "withered trees", Petrified Trees or Irminsul trees, are parts of Irminsul across Teyvat. Domains, Ley Line Outcrops and Ley Line Blossoms belong to the same network. A mind can connect to Irminsul: the Dendro Archon does so by nature, and the Traveler briefly did in Chronic Illness. Fate is written into the tree; Nahida says the Fontaine prophecy was "carved into Irminsul long ago".
### LARGE +
- Original Resin text says the silver-white trees and blossoms intertwine in the deepest place in the earth, and that the pattern of their roots defines the Ley Lines.
- Scholars also connect to Irminsul to produce the Akasha's Knowledge Capsules. Some forest dwellers, such as Haypasia, try through meditation and Spirit Borneol, but many who attempt such a connection go mad.
- The innermost region is made only of torrents of information. Small saplings sprout there as packets of data; touching one carries a visitor elsewhere in the space. It is easy to get lost. The "heart" is a huge glowing tree whose branches look like circuitry (The Night-Bird Falls at the Curtain's Call, 3.3).
- The Raiden Shogun said that as long as the Ley Lines flow, the erosion of time never stops. Wiki analysis links Irminsul to that flow.
- Lisa's voice-over about Mona mentions that the Hexenzirkel go on Irminsul expeditions.

## origins
@version: 6.6
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Apep (6.6) says the Heavenly Principles did not plant Irminsul. It existed in the age of the dragons, looked and worked very differently, and its Ley Lines carried the power of the Light Realm. The Heavenly Principles took it over, made it serve humanity and used it to anchor fate.
### LARGE +
Apep adds that in the end humanity failed to protect it. The Worldspan Fern item says the first fern sprouted in Apep's verdant court and that later plants came from those first fronds; the wiki identifies that first frond as Irminsul. In the 6.6 Archon Quest, Istaroth calls Irminsul "the original tree of Teyvat" and the means by which the Heavenly Principles maintain fate.

## memory_and_erasure
@version: 3.2
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Purging information from Irminsul takes power on the level of a god. A purge rewrites everything recorded about its subject, including memories, books and letters. The events and their consequences remain, but people remember different causes or different actors. Natives of Teyvat never notice the change, Archons and Paimon included. Only beings with no records, such as the Traveler, keep the original memories.
### LARGE +
A being cannot fully erase itself: Scaramouche erased his past (3.3) but went on existing as the Wanderer. Erased information can be kept if it is abstracted so that it still resonates with what Irminsul records. Nahida gave Scaramouche a memory block for this purpose. Once restored, information does not vanish again. The wiki notes that Weapon and Artifact descriptions never change after a purge.
### ULTRA +
Irminsul can also refuse something it cannot place. In Durin's Story Quest (6.2), the world would not record Andersdotter's Durin as different from the historical dragon Durin. Strangers began to see him as the evil dragon and his friends started to forget him, until the Hexenzirkel and the Traveler had him accepted as his own entity. The Wanderer, who is not affected by Irminsul's changes, noticed that whatever he wrote about Durin turned into the Durin of history.

## history
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
- About 6,000 years ago (per item text), Celestia's Skyfrost Nail shattered an Irminsul tree on Dragonspine. Durin's blood later revived it as the Frostbearing Tree.
- In the distant past, the Heavenly Principles used Irminsul to erase the Third Descender, Saarelainen (revealed 7.1).
- Greater Lord Rukkhadevata, who died about 500 years ago, was polluted by forbidden knowledge. Through her link she went on polluting Irminsul after her death, and the Withering was a sign of that corruption.
- 3.2: Nahida removed Rukkhadevata from Irminsul. With the Traveler, she erased the forbidden knowledge. Nahida's Irminsul search found records of the Traveler's sibling, which is unexpected for an outsider, and they grow fuzzy near the end of the sibling's journey.
- 3.3: Scaramouche tried to erase himself.
- 6.2: In Nod-Krai, the Wanderer says he was built from a branch once connected to the main Irminsul tree.
- 6.6: Dottore hijacked Irminsul, and Nahida burned it.
### ULTRA +
After Rukkhadevata's removal, no native remembers her. Nahida believes she was always the Dendro Archon and lost her strength in the Cataclysm, and the Aranara now credit "the Dendro Archon" in place of Queen Aranyani. Scaramouche's erasure rewrote the histories of Tatarasuna and the Raiden Gokaden: the deaths still happened, but people now give other causes. Dottore's Segments were built with the same Khaenri'ahn technique as the Wanderer.

## status
@version: 6.6
@spoiler: major
### LITE
As of 7.1, Irminsul is destroyed; only its underground parts remain. Aaru, King Deshret's unfinished afterlife, was rebuilt to collect and store the world's information in its place.
### STANDARD +
Nahida says the fire burned for seven days and nights, and most of Teyvat's vital information was saved. Nicole moved Nahida's connection to Aaru, so the loss did her no harm. With Irminsul burned, Nahida says, fate "now carries many more possibilities." The Pyro Gnosis vanished in the fire.
### ULTRA +
In 7.1 (Etched in Time), the Traveler tells the Harbingers that they and Nahida erased forbidden knowledge from Irminsul. When Hroptatyr's sealed notebook is opened, most of its pages are blank, and Pierro assumes the erased text was forbidden knowledge. The same Archon Quest reveals the much older erasure of Saarelainen. The Belyi Tsar kept some memories of him only because he was hiding in the Shadow Realm at the time.

## language_and_minor_details
### ULTRA
"Irminsul" is a name from the English localisation. Chinese, Japanese and Korean texts call it the "world tree" in Sumeru quests and the "silver-white ancient tree" elsewhere, and some event text just says "Ley Line". So not every English line that says "Irminsul" necessarily means the one great tree. Prototype Crescent's bowstring is said to be made of Irminsul branches brought back from the abyss. Wiki observers note that after its purification in Sumeru, Irminsul's inner space is shown with a clear sky above it.

## irminsul_fruit
@availability: limited
### ULTRA
In the limited-time event Energy Amplifier Initiation, Irminsul trees produced crystalline fruits, called Irminsul Fruit in English and "Ley Line Fruit" in East Asian languages, which the Sumerian researcher Hosseini studies. This happened during an event and is not common knowledge.

## accounts
### LARGE
- Tighnari's ancestors learned from Rukkhadevata's familiars that Irminsul was sick, but even those creatures knew no cure.
- Apep says the tree is older than the Heavenly Principles, who usurped it. Istaroth says it is how they maintain fate. Nahida says it exists to protect humanity and can therefore be sacrificed for humanity.
- The Belyi Tsar's notes say the Goddess of Flowers in Sumeru sought to build a system independent of the Ley Lines and Heimarmene.
- Albedo says Truly Ancient Petrified Trees grow one or two miles high, with a large spider living around their roots.

## open_questions
### LARGE
Canon leaves several things open: where the main tree stood relative to the Abyss (Prototype Crescent mentions Irminsul branches brought back from the abyss); why the Traveler's sibling was recorded; whether the silver-white trees across Teyvat changed after the burning; where the Pyro Gnosis went; and how far the Heavenly Principles' hold on fate has loosened.

## connections
### LITE
Ley Lines (its roots), Nahida, Aaru (its successor), the Heavenly Principles and fate.
### LARGE +
Also the Akasha, Descenders (who are not recorded), the Withering and forbidden knowledge (its corruption), Dottore (who hijacked it), the Wanderer (made from one of its branches) and the domains.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay interpretation (this project's recommendation, not canon text): after 6.6, Irminsul is gone and Aaru holds the world's memory. Natives must not remember erased people or events, such as Greater Lord Rukkhadevata or Scaramouche's original history; only the Traveler, other outsiders and those whose records were restored know the old versions.
### LARGE +
Outside Sumeru, most people know at most about the crisis and the sealed border. Silver-white trees, Ley Line Blossoms and domains are still part of the landscape; canon has not said they changed. Treat a mind link to the world's memory as dangerous for anyone but a Dendro Archon.

## entry: Burning of Irminsul
@keys: destruction of Irminsul, burning of Irminsul, burned Irminsul, keeper of Irminsul
@version: 6.6
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
In Version 6.6 (Song of the Welkin Moon, Acts IX and X), Nahida fell into a coma, chaos spread through Sumeru from the Ley Lines, and finally a giant Irminsul tree rose in Sumeru City. Dottore had entered Irminsul through death, a route Nahida could have closed only by cutting every soul off from the Ley Lines. Once he was inside, her authority was quickly restricted, and Pantalone had placed Dottore's last backup in the Ley Lines. Sumeru's defenders overloaded Irminsul with the Akasha to stop its growth. Wanderer and the Traveler went inside while the world's information was moved into Aaru. Nahida invoked her "supreme authority as the keeper of Irminsul" and lit the Pyro Gnosis, which Mavuika had given her. Ronova, Istaroth and Naberius consented, and the flames burned the tree and Dottore with it.
### LARGE +
Ronova asked whether Nahida would really abandon what she had always fought for. Nahida answered that Irminsul exists to protect humanity, so humanity, and she as one of its protectors, come first. Naberius replied that since she had become "a great tree" herself, she need not fear losing another. Dottore tried to drag the Traveler into the flames, but the Segments he had deleted dragged him down instead.

## entry: Irminsul barrier
@keys: Irminsul barrier
@keys2: Sumeru, sealed border, Mavuika, Nahida
@version: 6.6
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
The Heavenly Principles set a rule: if Irminsul is in grave danger, part of its power seals off Sumeru to protect the rest of Teyvat. Pollution alone never set it off; Dottore's direct invasion in 6.6 did. The barrier cut Sumeru off and stopped trade with Natlan. Only the Traveler, who has no record in Irminsul, could walk through it. They absorbed Mavuika's Pyro Gnosis with their Pyro mastery and carried it in. The meeting was held so that Irminsul could not record it.

## entry: Aaru as the new Irminsul
@keys: Aaru
@keys2: Irminsul, Ley Lines, repository, Nahida
@version: 6.6
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
King Deshret built Aaru with illicit celestial technology as an unfinished afterlife, and it once held three of his Seven Pillars prisoner. In 6.6 Sumeru's scholars reprogrammed it, and during the battle the world's information was moved into it out of Irminsul. Aaru now collects and stores what the Ley Lines carry. Nahida calls it the new Irminsul, and her own existence is now bound to it.

## entry: Erasure from Irminsul
@keys: erased from Irminsul, removed from Irminsul, purged from Irminsul
### STANDARD
Known erasures: the Third Descender Saarelainen, erased by the Heavenly Principles long ago (revealed 7.1); Greater Lord Rukkhadevata, removed by Nahida in 3.2; Scaramouche's past, erased by himself in 3.3; and forbidden knowledge, erased by Nahida and the Traveler. Each time, natives' memories and records were rewritten while the consequences remained. Memory of Saarelainen survived only with the Belyi Tsar, who was hiding in the Shadow Realm at the time, and in Celestia's own records, which Rhinedottir says she has read.

## entry: Irminsul trees
@keys: withered tree, withered trees, Petrified Tree, Petrified Trees, Irminsul roots
### STANDARD
Silver-white trees with glowing blue streaks stand across Teyvat as parts or root-ends of Irminsul: on Dragonspine (the Frostbearing Tree, plus one on a small island further east), in The Chasm, on Tsurumi Island near the "Boatman", and in the Orchard of Pairidaeza on Mt. Damavand. Ley Line disorders warp the land around them. The wiki notes that they stand where Celestial Nails are thought to have struck. The Dragon-Queller, the Sacred Sakura and the Weeping Willow of the Lake have similar streaks but brown bark, and are not counted as Irminsul trees.

## entry: Frostbearing Tree
@keys: Frostbearing Tree, Frostbearer
### LARGE
On Dragonspine stands the sprout of an ancient Irminsul tree that Sal Vindagnyr revered. Item text says the kingdom's last princess was born under it. Celestia's Skyfrost Nail shattered and froze the old tree, and the princess failed to save it by grafting a branch. Five hundred years ago, the dragon Durin's blood seeped into the Ley Lines there. It revived the tree with red streaks in place of blue and a "thirst for more power". Fed with Crimson Agates, the tree bears a single fruit, the catalyst Frostbearer.

## entry: Sarva
@keys: Sarva

### LARGE
"Sarva" is the Aranara's word for Irminsul. They speak of returning to Sarva when someone dies, but also of coming from, returning from and travelling through it. After Greater Lord Rukkhadevata's removal, Araja said Sarva no longer remembers Marana, the Aranara name for the Withering.
