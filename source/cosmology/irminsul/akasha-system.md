## summary
### LITE
The Akasha System (usually "the Akasha") is Sumeru's information network. Greater Lord Rukkhadevata created it with her Dendro Gnosis, and the Sumeru Akademiya later ran it. People connect through a leaf-shaped earpiece, the Akasha Terminal, and receive information on whatever they concentrate on, as far as their clearance allows. It secretly draws on the dreams of its users. After Chapter III, Nahida shut it down and gave away her Dendro Gnosis. In Version 6.6 the Akasha was reconnected through Aaru to overload and stop Dottore's corrupted Irminsul.
### STANDARD +
The name is Sanskrit-derived (akasha: sky, space, aether); the wiki notes that the Akashic records are a likely inspiration. Sumeru's people grew up relying on it, and overuse dulls independent thinking.

## established
### LITE
- Created by Greater Lord Rukkhadevata with her Gnosis, then expanded and controlled by the Akademiya's sages after her death.
- Compiles and distributes information to users with an Akasha Terminal.
- Powered by the Dendro Gnosis; fed by users' dreams as Jnana Energy.
- Shut down at the end of Chapter III, when Nahida gave the Dendro Gnosis to Dottore.
### STANDARD +
- Clearance depends on identity, age, demographic and other unnamed criteria. If clearance is too low, the user gets silence instead of an answer. It works anywhere in Sumeru except Devantaka Mountain, where there is no signal, and is strongest in big settlements such as Port Ormos and Sumeru City.
- Besides lookups, it carries announcements (replacing some bulletin boards), messages and file transfers, which the sages monitor closely. With enough data it can build predictive models of a person or subject; the Akademiya used this to anticipate Cyno's investigation until he learned of it and changed his habits.
- Prolonged use makes Akasha-derived knowledge feel true and hard to correct. Grand Sage Azar, who rightly doubted that Kusanali had escaped, still failed to see her at first because he had checked the Akasha's announcement and expected her absence.
- Most of its content comes from Knowledge Capsules written by scholars in Satyavada Life, who gain knowledge by linking their minds to Irminsul.
### LARGE +
- Kusanali is the "First Akasha Terminal": her consciousness has always been able to reach the network, which let her perceive the outside world while confined and move between people's terminals. She can also transfer consciousnesses, hers and the Traveler's, through it in ways the sages cannot monitor.
- Originally only the Archon could operate it. The sages gained permissions after Rukkhadevata's death, and by imprisoning Kusanali they in effect monopolised it. After she was freed she revoked their access so that she alone controls it.
- Dottore exploited the same connection to trap Kusanali's consciousness inside the Akasha during the Chapter III arc, which incapacitated her.
- A detailed system manual exists and few have read it. Alhaitham, as scribe, had; his Character Story says he used it to learn how to alter the Akasha's status signal.

## terminals
### STANDARD
An Akasha Terminal is a leaf-shaped earpiece that hovers over the user's left ear. Reciting the activation phrase ("May the Mighty God bless us with their voice of wisdom") raises a holographic green visor over the eyes, which disappears when the Akasha is not in use. Terminals were originally issued only to Sumeru's citizens and long-term residents; shortly before the Sumeru Archon Quests the sages opened them to all visitors.
### LARGE +
The visor gives some physical protection and its position can be adjusted, but only people who have read the manual know how. The sages' reason for opening terminals to visitors is not stated outright, but the quest "Secret of the Scorching Desert" implies it served to maximise dream-harvesting during the Sabzeruz Festival samsara.
### ULTRA +
After the sages were removed, Nahida built a weaponised upgrade, the Neo Akasha Terminal, and gave it to the Traveler. Once she had battle data from the samsara in which Scaramouche, as the false god Shouki no Kami, fought repeatedly, she sent it to everyone in Sumeru through their terminals, so the people's combined knowledge could process his combat abilities and find a way to beat him.

## entry: Knowledge Capsules (Canned Knowledge)
@keys: Knowledge Capsule, Knowledge Capsules, Canned Knowledge
@version: 3.0
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Knowledge Capsules hold a fixed amount of information and can be used by anyone with a terminal, bypassing clearance levels. They were meant to put new information into the Akasha and be destroyed after Jnagarbha Day, but many leaked and have been sold on a black market over the last century. The Akademiya banned possessing, selling or using them, partly from fear of harm and partly to protect its monopoly on information; offenders can lose their right to study. Eremites, commoners and some students use them when the Akasha gives them too little.
### LARGE +
Richer capsules contain more Dendro energy and glow brighter under Elemental Sight, though that is an unreliable guide, since once-useful knowledge may still shine after it has gone out of date. Topics range from obscure academic studies to brewing techniques; much leaked Canned Knowledge originates inside the Akademiya. Most capsules are made from written material, but a secret method extracts knowledge directly from a person's mind. Divine Knowledge Capsules glow bright red and supposedly hold the wisdom of the gods: they actually contain knowledge taken from scholars who went mad linking their minds to Irminsul, which was contaminated by forbidden knowledge. Their users go mad too, as seen with Mizri; the sages planned to infuse them into Scaramouche.

## entry: Jnagarbha Day
@keys: Jnagarbha Day
@version: 3.0
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Jnagarbha Day is the occasion, several times a year on an unknown but regular schedule, when the Akademiya enters new information into the Akasha. The capsules are linked to the Grand Sage's console in his office above the House of Daena. It acts as a deadline and is a major event for Akademiya staff, though reports can be added outside it.

## in_universe_cost
@version: 3.2
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
The Akasha's computing power comes from its users. It harvests their dreams, called Jnana Energy, because dreams occur when brain activity is high, and joins them into something likened to one massive brain. This is why Sumeru's people had a reputation for not dreaming. The sages after the Cataclysm stigmatised dreams as irrational, and "outgrowing" dreams came to be seen as an honour. Few people knew this was an abnormal effect caused by the Akasha. Its dream-taking is a manifestation of Rukkhadevata's power, and it runs on the Dendro Gnosis.
### ULTRA +
The harvesting was pushed to an extreme in the Sabzeruz Festival samsara, a looping dream that fed energy to the sages' plan to raise Scaramouche as an artificial God of Wisdom. When the Akasha was turned off, people could dream again.

## history
@version: 3.2
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
During the Cataclysm, forbidden knowledge infected Irminsul. Rukkhadevata judged her own power insufficient and, drawing on her experience of forbidden knowledge in King Deshret's time, built the original Akasha with her Gnosis so that the knowledge of all Sumeru's people could be used to find a solution. After her death and Kusanali's imprisonment in the Sanctuary of Surasthana, the sages took over and made the Akasha part of everyday life.
### LARGE +
At the end of Chapter III (3.2), after the sages were arrested and the forbidden knowledge purged from Irminsul, Nahida chose to stop using the Akasha: it had served its purpose and she did not think it good for her people, even though she accepted some might resent that. She traded her Dendro Gnosis to Dottore for information. Before this, Moseis had uploaded his whole consciousness into the Akasha to run hyper-realistic dream models on a tiny share of its Jnana Energy supply. When the system was switched off he became trapped in the network he had built, and he kept feeding on his dreamers through implanted signal devices (3.2, "Dream of Falling").

## six_six_reuse
@version: 6.6
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
In Luna VII (6.6), when Dottore entered Irminsul and began corrupting it, the Akasha was brought back as a tool. Thoth told the Sumeru scholars that Aaru and the Akasha are closely linked. Kaveh and Alhaitham modified Aaru to reboot the Akasha, intending to use it to reach Irminsul. Nahida said Irminsul was too damaged to be accessed by normal means, so the plan was changed.
### LARGE +
The new plan required terminals for everyone in Sumeru, including mercenaries and desert folk who never had one; the Akasha main terminal had been evacuated from the House of Daena along with other assets. The terminals were not for acquiring knowledge, only for linking minds to Irminsul. Since Irminsul's function for connecting minds had been used only by scholars, adding the whole population would stack up a heavy load on a system already under strain. Nahida's reasoning was that it might be shut down completely by overloading it. Everyone took part and the tree stopped growing, while Dottore, inside it, said he still had all the time he needed. Aaru's interference also meant no one wearing a terminal could be accurately tracked by Irminsul. Shortly after, Nahida ignited the Pyro Gnosis to burn Irminsul down; Aaru then assumed its responsibilities.

## accounts
### STANDARD
- The Akademiya presented the Akasha as the most powerful and comprehensive wisdom dissemination system in history (Cyno's description). In Sumeru it was a point of national pride.
- Alhaitham says that people had come to rely on it so much that they were slaves to its orders.
- Thoth, one of the Seven Pillars of Deshret's court, says Aaru inspired the Akasha, which in turn drew from Celestia's technology; Alhaitham says the manual supports this.
### LARGE +
The Akasha is described both as Rukkhadevata's invention and as a derivative of older, desert-born design. Treat both as claims made by in-world speakers.

## open_questions
### STANDARD
- Does the Akasha still exist in any working form after 6.6? Canon has not said.
- How the Dendro Gnosis relates technically to the terminals, and how much of the design was Rukkhadevata's own, is not explained.

## connections
### STANDARD
Irminsul: the Akasha's content is drawn from scholars who link to Irminsul, and in 6.6 the Akasha was turned back on it. Aaru: a similar architecture; Aaru later took over Irminsul's storage role. Nahida: she is the First Akasha Terminal. Forbidden knowledge: the reason for its creation. The Akademiya: its operator after Rukkhadevata's death.
### LARGE +
Characters shaped by it: Alhaitham (scribe who read the manual), Cyno (once tracked by its predictive modelling), Kaveh (6.6 modifications), Azar (Grand Sage who oversaw Jnagarbha Day), Moseis (trapped in it in 3.2), and Dottore (who trapped Kusanali in it and obtained the Dendro Gnosis).
