## summary
### LITE
Irminsul, the silver-white tree that stores Teyvat's information, can be edited. A god-level power can purge a person or fact from it, and memories, books and records across Teyvat then change to match, while the events themselves still happened and their results remain. Three purges are known: the Greater Lord Rukkhadevata (by Nahida, 3.2), Scaramouche (by himself, 3.3) and the Third Descender Saarelainen (by the Heavenly Principles, revealed 7.1). In 6.6 Dottore hijacked Irminsul and Nahida had it burned down.
### STANDARD +
The edits touch people's memories and physical records (books, letters, item and character texts), but not the underlying events, so rainforests Rukkhadevata grew, or crimes Scaramouche committed, remain and are simply remembered with other causes or actors. People native to Teyvat, including gods, do not notice they were altered. Descenders and other outlanders have no records in Irminsul and so keep their memories.

## established
### LITE
Rules shown in canon: only god-level power can purge; a being cannot purge itself completely; changes rewrite memory and records but not events or consequences; Descenders, being outside the record, remember what others lose.
### STANDARD
Irminsul collects the world's information through the Ley Lines and acts as the record of Teyvat, so removing something there removes it from the shared understanding of the world. Rewriting changes whatever counts as Teyvat's information: what people remember and what books and letters say. Natives, including the Archons, do not notice. Paimon is affected too, while the Traveler is not. Removal appears to happen only once: information that natives of Teyvat preserve or retrieve afterward does not disappear again, though retrieval is presumably hard without the help of someone who never lost it, such as the Traveler.
### LARGE +
Weapon and artifact descriptions are not changed by these edits, which is why some texts still name Rukkhadevata or Scaramouche. Ordinary item descriptions and books are changed. Natives can sometimes shield their memory: according to Rhinedottir, the Belyi Tsar fled into the Shadow Realm and so was the only one to retain some memories of Saarelainen. Information can also be stored in a form that still resonates with what Irminsul holds, which let Scaramouche's past be recovered later. Irminsul can also refuse an entity: Andersdotter's Durin was at first rejected by Irminsul and, once he physically entered Teyvat, scripted toward the fate of Rhinedottir's earlier Durin, until steps taken by the Hexenzirkel and the Traveler let Irminsul accept him as a different entity.
### ULTRA +
A purge of a whole being leaves traces: Nahida still felt sorrow immediately after removing the Greater Lord, and later sensed a "warmth" that had supported her, which suggests an echo of the lost being. Some names survive in places with special properties, such as the throne inscription in the Orchard of Pairidaeza reading RUKDVTA, and a Geoshroom's memories in What Shape Does the Self Hold, whose attempt to say the name came out distorted.

## accounts
@version: 3.2
### STANDARD
Rukkhadevata: In Where the Boat of Consciousness Lies Nahida removes the Greater Lord, whose existence had been contaminated by forbidden knowledge and kept polluting Irminsul after her death. Afterward Nahida believes she was always the original Dendro Archon who lost her memories fighting the darkness in the cataclysm. The other Archons and the people of Sumeru remember only Lesser Lord Kusanali (in the voice-overs of Venti, Zhongli and Ei, "Buer"), and the Akademiya's reasons for confining Nahida change to disappointment in her weakness.

Scaramouche: In The Night-Bird Falls at the Curtain's Call (Interlude Chapter, Inversion of Genesis) Scaramouche erased his own past from Irminsul. He could not erase himself entirely and continued to exist as the Wanderer, but he lost the memory of his earlier identities. Records elsewhere were rewritten: Ei and Yae Miko lose their Kunikuzushi voice-overs and Tartaglia his Balladeer voice-over, Fatui NPC lines are changed, and Inazuma's account of Tatarasuna and the fall of the Raiden Gokaden is retold without him.

Saarelainen: In the 7.1 Archon Quest (Black Feathers Scattered at Parting) Rhinedottir, who says she read Celestia's records of the incident, tells Anastasya that Anastasya was first erased by the Heavenly Principles as punishment after the Belyi Tsar incited her and Saarelainen to open the gate to the Abyss. Saarelainen then ascended to Celestia to bargain for her return, offering his own life. The Heavenly Principles agreed on condition that everything about him be forgotten: they erased him and all related memories from Anastasya's mind and used Irminsul to erase every trace of the Third Descender from the world.
### LARGE +
Other changes: Sumeru NPC dialogue that named the Greater Lord now names Lesser Lord Kusanali or the Dendro Archon; Aranara and Aranyaka voice-lines that named Queen Aranyani now say "Dendro Archon" or "Lord of Verdure"; the descriptions of Kalpalata Lotus, Rukkhashava Mushrooms, Harra Fruit and The Boon of the Elder Tree, and the book The Folio of Foliage, are rewritten. Nahida's Character Details and Character Stories 1 and 3, and Zhongli's Character Story 5, are altered. Weapons and artifacts such as Viridescent Venerer's Determination and Fruit of Fulfillment keep references to her. The Traveler's voice-over about Rukkhadevata disappears after A Toast to Victory, since Paimon no longer remembers her.

Tatarasuna: the swordsmith Niwa Hisahide is remembered as dying with another smith in the furnace to purify Tatarasuna, rather than being murdered by Dottore, who in the original history took his heart to power a device. Aqaba's report names a mechanic from Fontaine (Dottore as Escher) instead of a puppet. The fall of the Futsu, Hyakume and Senju schools is blamed on a murder spree by the head of the Hyakume clan, and Kaedehara Yoshinori's letter is altered accordingly. The Traveler checks many of these changes in A Dance of Destruction. Nahida's own Balladeer voice-over is not removed.

Saarelainen was not completely erased: the Frostmoon Scions kept some of his tales as hymns, though these are unreliable because they were written under the modified record (they claim Anastasya was the Belyi Tsar's daughter, which is shown to be false). Objects tied to him, such as a Gnosis, a Stellar Linchpin and a prism that stores memories independently of Irminsul, remain, and the Belyi Tsar retained some memories of him because he had fled into the Shadow Realm.

## open_questions
### STANDARD
Canon does not explain how the purge power works in detail, why self-purging fails, how much else the Heavenly Principles have edited without anyone noticing, or why the Traveler's sibling has records in Irminsul while the Traveler has none.
### LARGE +
Because anything that happened before the Traveler's arrival could have been edited with nobody (except other Descenders) remembering, canon invites caution about claiming a record is original. Do not invent additional purges or say who else has been erased.

## connections
### STANDARD
Irminsul, the Heavenly Principles, the Ley Lines, the Akasha System and Aaru, Descenders, Fate, the Shades, and the destruction of Irminsul in 6.6 are the neighbouring entries. Characters most affected are Nahida, the Wanderer, Dottore, the Belyi Tsar and the Traveler.

## entry: Rukkhadevata's Erasure
@keys: Rukkhadevata erased, Greater Lord Rukkhadevata, Rukkhadevata removal, forgotten Rukkhadevata, RUKDVTA
@version: 3.2
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Nahida removed the Greater Lord Rukkhadevata from Irminsul in Where the Boat of Consciousness Lies to cleanse the pollution of forbidden knowledge. Gods, mortals and Nahida herself now remember only Lesser Lord Kusanali, who believes she lost her memories in the cataclysm. The forests she grew and Sumeru's traditions remain.
### LARGE +
Traces: the throne inscription RUKDVTA in the Orchard of Pairidaeza, a Geoshroom's distorted memory, and the artifact set Viridescent Venerer's Determination and weapon Fruit of Fulfillment. After the purge the Rukkhashava Mushrooms archive text says people take "Rukkhadevata" to be the name of a giant tree that once stood in Sumeru.

## entry: Scaramouche's Self-Erasure
@keys: Scaramouche erased, Scaramouche purge, Kunikuzushi erased, Balladeer erased
@version: 3.3
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Scaramouche tried to remove himself from Irminsul in the Interlude Chapter and succeeded in erasing nearly all records of his past, but he could not remove himself and lives on as the Wanderer, no longer recalling his earlier identities. Before this, Nahida had prepared a memory block that would remind him of his past regardless of changes to Irminsul, and gave it to him after the Traveler recognised his new identity as Wanderer.
### LARGE +
Effects included Tatarasuna and Raiden Gokaden histories rewritten (see main entry), and Fatui NPC dialogue about him altered to mention Tartaglia or Signora. Weapons and artifacts keep references to him (Surpassing Cup, Husk of Opulent Dreams, Kagura's Verity, Kagotsurube Isshin). The Shakkei Pavilion domain description lost its reference in 3.3 for everyone.
### ULTRA +
In the 6.6 Archon Quest (Truth Amongst the Pages of Purana) Dottore, inside Irminsul, greeted the Wanderer as Scaramouche: seeing him in Nod-Krai had been enough for him to connect the dots. He said he had no intention of restoring the records the Wanderer deleted, adding that it would be exceptionally difficult anyway, and taunted him that he would never have the chance to make up for his mistakes.

## entry: Saarelainen's Erasure
@keys: Saarelainen erased, Third Descender erased, Saarelainen purge, Saarelainen Irminsul
@version: 7.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
According to Rhinedottir in the 7.1 Archon Quest, Saarelainen ascended to Celestia and traded his own life to have Anastasya restored; the Heavenly Principles' condition was that everything about him be forgotten, and they used Irminsul to erase every trace of him. Memories of him were wiped and records altered, but traces remain.
### LARGE +
Survivors: hymns of the Frostmoon Scions (unreliable), his Gnosis, a Stellar Linchpin, and a memory-storing prism. The Belyi Tsar kept some memories of him by fleeing into the Shadow Realm.

## entry: Dottore's Hijack
@keys: Dottore Irminsul, Irminsul hijack, Irminsul tree Sumeru City, Omega Build
@version: 6.6
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
After the Doctor's Nod-Krai half was destroyed, part of his Omega Build stored in Northland Bank infiltrated Irminsul and overrode Nahida's authority for a time. It produced red mist that drove people mad, unusual Ley Line blossoms, and a giant Irminsul tree in Sumeru City that Nahida described as a sign of unchecked greed.
### LARGE +
With the Seven Pillars of King Deshret helping them take control of Aaru, Nahida's allies merged Aaru with the Akasha System and used it to overload Irminsul. Nahida diverted its stored data to Aaru and used the Pyro Gnosis, which Mavuika had handed over to her through the Traveler, inside Irminsul. The Heavenly Principles had reserved enough of its power to isolate Sumeru if Irminsul were lost.

## entry: Irminsul's Destruction
@keys: Irminsul burned, Irminsul destroyed, Irminsul destruction, Pyro Gnosis Irminsul
@version: 6.6
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Nahida, within Irminsul, invoked her right as its keeper and faced the Shades Ronova, Istaroth and Naberius, who asked whether she was certain she wished to use her authority to destroy Irminsul, the tree through which the Heavenly Principles maintain fate. She said humanity's survival came before Irminsul. They allowed it, and the Pyro Gnosis burned the tree; the Doctor died, dragged into the flames by the Segments he had deleted. Aaru took over Irminsul's role and Nicole's reliance on Irminsul was transplanted there.
### LARGE +
The burning lasted seven days and nights, and afterward the Pyro Gnosis vanished without a trace, which Mavuika accepted. See the dedicated entry for the destruction of Irminsul for fuller detail.

## entry: Additions and Records of Outlanders
@keys: Irminsul record, Irminsul added, Doctor's memory, anonymous data
@version: 3.3
### STANDARD
Additions are rarer. Nahida arranged for Dottore's recorded memory of killing Niwa Hisahide to be preserved and findable when Scaramouche searched for Descender records, and admitted to the Wanderer in As Though Morning Dew that she planned this deliberately. Beings not from Teyvat, such as Descenders, do not normally appear in Irminsul, but the Traveler's sibling has records there, which characters link to Khaenri'ah.
