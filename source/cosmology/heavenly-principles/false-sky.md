## summary
### LITE
The false sky, also called the firmament, is a real, physical barrier wrapped around the world of Teyvat. The Heavenly Principles made it to shut Teyvat off from the sea of stars and hide it from the wider universe. From inside it looks like an ordinary sky with an ordinary moon and stars; most people in Teyvat have no idea it is not real. The Heavenly Principles themselves are called "the great ruler of the firmament".
### STANDARD +
For centuries the false sky was a whispered theory. Scaramouche, the Doctor, King Deshret and Khaenri'ahn astrologers hinted at it. Since then it has been visibly broken several times by "split-sky phenomena": over Natlan in 5.1, and over Nod-Krai in 6.3, when the Frost Moon broke through and took the false moon's place in the night sky.

## established
### LITE
- It surrounds the whole planet and separates Teyvat from the cosmos beyond, which canon calls the sea of stars.
- Its makers are the Heavenly Principles (also named Phanes), the First Descender, who rule Teyvat from Celestia.
- From inside, it projects the image of a normal sky. The stars and the single moon that Teyvat saw for millennia were part of that image.
- It can be breached. Only enormous power has done so on screen, and so far each breach has closed again.
### STANDARD +
- The book Before Sun and Moon (The Byakuyakoku Collection, vol. 2) describes Phanes using the shell of the egg it hatched from to split "the universe" from "the microcosm of the world." Lore readers take this eggshell to be the false sky.
- Canon calls the boundary "the world's borders". Alice, called the "Sustainer of the World's Borders" in Skirk's voice-overs, writes in the Wings of Feasting item text that Teyvat's borders "have grown fragile these past two years".
- After Dottore's defeat in 6.3, Nicole says that the crisis at the world's borders has made Teyvat easier to detect from the outside universe.
### LARGE +
- The old moons were hidden behind it. After the War of Funerary Flame, the remains of the shattered Eternal Moon and the darkened Frost Moon lay beyond the false sky. The Heavenly Principles put an illusory "shadowmoon" onto the false sky to cover up what had happened to the Three Moons (wiki summary of the Three Moons and the 6.3 Archon Quest).
- Columbina reveals that the Traveler's spaceship had been hidden all along on the Frost Moon, "which lay beyond the sky" (Welkin Moon's Homecoming, 6.3).

## origin
@version: 6.7
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
The Moon Gazing world quests (6.7) tie the false sky to dragon research on the Frost Moon. The dragon researcher Lusalim led Project Lunar Veil, a plan to hide the moon behind a reflective veil made from Blackbody Matter. He feared that the message Project Moongazer was beaming into space might draw a hostile answer. A small Lunar Veil worked on Ungien's Circle. But before the full-sized veil generator could hide the whole moon, an entity from beyond the stars pierced it, and the laboratory exploded. The Traveler and Columbina suspect that entity was "the great ruler of the firmament", the Heavenly Principles.
### ULTRA +
Lusalim's last terminal record in his laboratory on the Dark Side of the Moon says he "capitulated" to a terrifying sovereign not of this world and handed over the results of his research. He called this a sin against dragonkind. Hearing this, Columbina remarks that a veil able to hide the moon "sounds very similar to the false sky." Canon strongly suggests the Heavenly Principles built the false sky from Lusalim's veil technology but never states it outright, so treat that as inference. The destroyed Lunar Veil Laboratory is now the Moontide Sea.

## before_proof
### LITE
Scaramouche, visibly shaken by something he had just discovered in his research, told Mona and the Traveler that "the stars, the sky" are "a gigantic hoax". It happened during the 1.1 event Unreconciled Stars.
### STANDARD +
- Mona had been taught to speak of the "false sky" in her astrological readings. Scaramouche's outburst made her wonder whether the phrase was more than a figure of speech. Her teacher is Barbeloth.
- In Where the Boat of Consciousness Lies (3.2), the Doctor asked Nahida whether she had heard the rumour that "the skies of Teyvat are fake", and called it the secret about the world's "truth" hidden in Irminsul. He told her this as part of the deal that gave him the Dendro Gnosis.
### LARGE +
- King Deshret's account in the Staff of the Scarlet Sands story says the angel Nabu Malikata described a night sky with three moons, and insists there should be three. He adds "even if the celestial sphere was just an illusion".
- Khaenri'ahn astrologers, branded heretics, saw in the Sign of Apaosha an "inverted image" of the false sky that revealed the origin of fate (Crimson Moon's Semblance). In Sumeru, the Sign of Apaosha shows the sea of stars beyond the false sky. The wiki records Barbeloth's view that it makes space unstable and easier to "break through the firmament".
- In The Splendorous Sky That Day (3.6), the Order of Skeptics' leader Nasejuna compares the sky to a reflection on water: an illusion that still hides the truth within it. This suggests the false sky mirrors something real.

## split_sky_phenomena
### LITE
Visible breaches of the false sky are called split-sky phenomena. In 5.1, Mavuika's final strike against the Abyss over Natlan broke through the sky. In 6.3 the Doctor tore it open over Nod-Krai, and the returning Frost Moon then smashed through it.
### STANDARD +
- Natlan (5.1, The Rainbow Destined to Burn): in No One Fights Alone, Mavuika gathered the strength of the six modern heroes and Ronova's power and struck down the Abyssal entity Gosoythoth. The blow pierced the sky and briefly showed space beyond, with the shattered remains of the Eternal Moon. The breach soon closed. Mavuika said she had heard the phrase "false sky" before, but this was the first proof she had seen. The Adventurers' Guild knew of no such phenomenon in any other nation. She told the public it was an "astronomical anomaly".
- Nod-Krai (6.3, True Moon): as the "Heretic of the False Moon", Dottore used the power of the Moon Marrows to draw a line across the false sky. It split open to show the Frost Moon, and he rained attacks down from it. Using that opening, Nicole channelled her power through Varka and the Knave to drag the Frost Moon in. Dottore sealed the rift, but the moon's momentum shattered the barrier anyway, and Columbina came down with it. This completed Sandrone's plan to bring her back.
### LARGE +
- Faint fragments still show in Natlan's sky north of the Great Volcano. The NPC Mtwa confirms they can be seen only from inside Natlan. In When All Becomes a Monument (5.3), Mavuika identifies them as "Ancient Moon's Remnants", relics of Secret Source technology from the age of the ancient dragons, and links them to a project of the Doctor's.
- After 6.3 the Frost Moon remains inside the false sky and has replaced the false moon. Columbina declares that the Frost Moon "has now entered the false sky" and that she is the Trilune Goddess. Ordinary people noticed the moon looked different, but most carried on as usual.

## skirk_crossing
@version: 5.7
@spoiler: minor
### LARGE
The animated short Star Odyssey (June 2025, announced in the 5.7 special programme) shows the first known crossing. Surtalogi, one of Skirk's mentors, opened the border so Skirk could descend into Teyvat, and Skirk is shown looking at the hole in the false sky. People in Teyvat saw her descend; whether anyone saw the rift itself is unknown.

## shadowmoon
@version: 6.3
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
The false moon on the false sky, called the shadowmoon or moon's reflection, was the Heavenly Principles' cover story for the lost Three Moons. Istaroth used it to hold Ziju, one of the Three Deadly Selves keeping a part of Zibai's soul, after Zibai died stalling a Celestial Nail aimed at Lang-Gan. Once the Frost Moon was back inside the false sky, the shadowmoon went invisible except during eclipses. Zhongli expected the wishes made at Lantern Rite to speed up its collapse. In A Lanternlit Ode to the Silver Moon (6.3), he and the Traveler reunited Zibai's three selves on Lantern Rite night. The shadowmoon fell apart moments later.

## accounts
### LARGE
- As a gift or a shelter: Before Sun and Moon presents the eggshell separation as part of Phanes's creation of the world and of humanity.
- As a cage: in the Springs of Hidden Jade item text, Zibai feels "trapped within the shell of the starry sky". In his friendship dialogue, Neuvillette, the Hydro Dragon Sovereign, says a world you cannot break out of is "a prison" and tells the Traveler not to fear the desolation outside it.
- As a lie: Scaramouche calls the sky a hoax. The Doctor treats it as Irminsul's hidden truth.
### ULTRA +
- As protection from outside: the archive entry for the All-Devouring Narwhal hints that a higher power's borders isolate Teyvat and that the universe keeps trying to get in. Wiki summaries go further and say the false sky hides Teyvat from the Abyss ravaging the universe. This archive treats that motive as a reading of canon, not a quoted line.
- From the dragons' side: Lusalim saw handing over his research as a betrayal of dragonkind.

## open_questions
### LARGE
- Is the false sky built directly on Lusalim's Lunar Veil? Strongly suggested, not stated.
- How large is it, and how far out? The Star Odyssey short shows it hugging the planet, yet the Frost Moon now orbits inside it, apparently much farther out.
- What exactly do Alice's "world's borders" mean, and how does her role relate to the Heavenly Principles?
### ULTRA +
- How fragile is it now? Two breaches in quick succession, and Nicole's warning that Teyvat has become easier to detect, leave this open as of 7.1. The 7.0-7.1 Snezhnaya Archon Quests say nothing new about the barrier. They refer to the Heavenly Principles only by the epithet "great ruler of the firmament".

## connections
### STANDARD
The Heavenly Principles and Celestia made it and rule beneath it. The sea of stars lies beyond it, along with the Descenders who came through it: the Traveler, Skirk and others. Inside it sit the Three Moons story, the shadowmoon, the Frost Moon's return and Columbina, and the Natlan war against the Abyss.

### ULTRA +
- Simulanka, the storybook world made by the Hexenzirkel members Alice, Barbeloth and Andersdotter, is a parallel the wiki points out. When Mona read its stars she found its fates copied from Teyvat's, and said its creator had made it "inside a mirror, or a lake", with that world as the reflection. That is an echo of the false sky's "reflection" imagery, not proof of a link.
- The wiki records that when Mona tried to open a Sign of Apaosha on Barbeloth's instructions, Abyssal creatures "that roam beyond the false sky" came through. This suggests weak points in the firmament can act as doorways.
- Visual depictions disagree. The space beyond looks red-tinted in Star Odyssey and over Natlan but blue during the fight with the Heretic of the False Moon.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay interpretation (this project's recommendation, not canon text): for almost everyone in Teyvat the sky is simply the sky. Scholars, Fatui researchers and the gods involved know better, and Natlanese and Nod-Krai witnesses saw something strange. Ordinary Natlanese accept the "astronomical anomaly" story. Do not have common folk talk about the false sky as a known fact.
### LARGE +
Since 6.3 the moon over Teyvat is the real Frost Moon, not the old image. A character who looks up after that point sees a moon that has changed. Avoid scenes where mortals casually travel through or past the false sky. Canon breaches needed an Archon's full strength, Moon Marrow power or a Descender's mentor.

## entry: Split-sky phenomenon
@keys: split-sky phenomenon, split-sky phenomena, split sky, astronomical anomaly
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
A split-sky phenomenon is a visible break in the false sky. One opened over Natlan in 5.1, when Mavuika's strike against Gosoythoth showed the Eternal Moon's fragments, which can still be faintly seen from Natlan. Another opened over Nod-Krai in 6.3, when Dottore tore the sky and the Frost Moon broke through. Both closed afterwards. Mavuika publicly called the first one an "astronomical anomaly".

## entry: Project Lunar Veil and the false sky
@keys: Lunar Veil, Project Lunar Veil, Lusalim
@version: 6.7
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
Lusalim, a dragon researcher on the Frost Moon, designed a veil to hide the moon from anyone who might answer Project Moongazer's broadcast. An entity from beyond the stars, which Columbina suspects was the Heavenly Principles, pierced his full-scale veil generator and destroyed the laboratory. Lusalim later surrendered his research to them, which he recorded as a sin against dragonkind. Columbina notes how closely the veil resembles the false sky; that it was the false sky's basis is inferred, not stated.

## entry: Great ruler of the firmament
@keys: great ruler of the firmament, Sovereign of the Firmament, ruler of the firmament
### STANDARD
"The great ruler of the firmament" is an epithet for the Heavenly Principles. Columbina uses it in the Nod-Krai story (Welkin Moon's Homecoming, 6.3) and again in the Moon Gazing world quests (6.7), and Danica uses it in the 7.0 Snezhnaya Archon Quest. It names them as the power behind the false sky.
