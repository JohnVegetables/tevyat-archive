## summary
### LITE
The Abyss, also called the Void Realm, is a dark and destructive force that comes from the sea of stars, outside Teyvat. It manifests as Abyssal monsters, corrupting energy and Abyssal domains, and it is hostile to the elemental order the Heavenly Principles maintain. The Abyss Order, the Five Sinners of Khaenri'ah, the Wild Hunt of Nod-Krai and the forbidden knowledge of Sumeru all draw on it. As of Version 7.1 it remains a live threat across Teyvat.
### STANDARD +
Most accounts say the Dragon King Nibelung brought it into the world during the war of funerary flame, thousands of years ago. A later Snezhnayan account adds a "gate" opened by Anastasya and Saarelainen at the Belyi Tsar's urging, through which contamination has seeped ever since. Prolonged exposure decays the body and warps the mind. The Traveler is unusually resistant and can partly purify it.

## established
### LITE
Confirmed: the Abyss is foreign to Teyvat and exists elsewhere in the sea of stars, where it has destroyed other worlds. Its power is opposed to the Light Realm. It corrupts beings of the Human Realm, the world under Celestia's rule. Abyss monsters can wield elemental energy without Visions.
### STANDARD +
Enkanomiyan learning names three realms: the Human Realm, the Light Realm and the Void Realm. The Void Realm's dark currents are its counterpart to ley lines and to the Light Realm's elemental currents. Its darkness is especially harmful to elemental beings such as vishaps and elemental dragons, though they can repel it by drawing on the Light Realm. Item text says Abyssal darkness is the only power that could stain Morax's stone pillars black.
### LARGE +
The Abyss is drawn to ambitious people, such as Rhinedottir and Tartaglia. Those tied to it can gain an uncanny talent for stirring up conflict. Exposed humans slowly decay, and large doses kill even with treatment. Exposure can also change personality, magnifying desires and fears. Time runs differently inside it: Tartaglia trained there for three months while only three days passed outside. Abyss Mages use an Enochian-style script.
### ULTRA +
A few Khaenri'ahn-trained fighters, including Skirk and Surtalogi, use a technique called the "Seven-Shifting Serpent" (originally "Chaos Permeation"), which disguises Abyssal power as ordinary elemental energy. Venti was poisoned by the Abyss-laced blood of the dragon Durin when his link with Dvalin was disrupted, and he purged it at Windrise. The rest of Durin's blood soaked Dragonspine and crystallised as Crimson Agate.

## accounts
### STANDARD
Different speakers explain the Abyss in different ways, and none is confirmed as complete:
- **Anacharsis**, a voyager of the sea of stars, warned Nibelung that the Abyss would one day destroy Teyvat, as it had other worlds.
- **Barbeloth of the Hexenzirkel**, as relayed by Alice during a limited-time event, ties the Abyss to unchosen fates (see the sub-entry on her interpretation).
- **The Belyi Tsar's notes** suggest that when the Heavenly Principles narrowed the Light Realm's boundless possibilities to make the Human Realm, the Abyss in turn "anchors" into the world at the end of time.
### LARGE +
Aksinya and Neuvillette describe a related idea: the love or original order of ancient Teyvat was ground down into envy, hatred and resentment. The wiki notes that this resembles Barbeloth's view, but the link is interpretation. Greater Lord Rukkhadevata said forbidden knowledge came from the very bottom of the Abyss. Nahida later called it the manifestation of the Abyss that hit Sumeru hardest. Tsumi believed the Abyss Order finds Void Realm power addictive.
### ULTRA +
Characters who have only heard folk tales, such as Nod-Krai's legends of the Wild Hunt as a demonic host or ancient phantoms, would not know any of these theories.

## history
### STANDARD
**Before the Heavenly Principles:** the Abyss already existed in the sea of stars. Warned by Anacharsis, Nibelung pushed the dragons toward spacefaring technology, then left Teyvat to seek a solution and was corrupted by the Abyss instead. The Heavenly Principles, who also feared it, raised the false sky to keep outside influences out.
**War of funerary flame:** Nibelung returned bearing Abyssal power to overthrow the Heavenly Principles. Apep says he believed it would restore the dragons' rule. He was destroyed. The Principles then cast down Celestial Nails, partly to purge the corruption, but Abyssal remnants survived.
**The Cataclysm, 500 years ago:** Khaenri'ah fell. Rhinedottir loosed a flood of Abyssal monsters across Teyvat, and Khaenri'ahns of mixed blood were cursed into hilichurls and Abyss creatures.
### LARGE +
Before the Cataclysm, five Khaenri'ahns (Surtalogi, Rhinedottir, Vedrfolnir, Hroptatyr and Rerir) answered the Abyss's call. They split a vast power taken from it among themselves and became the Five Sinners. Natlan suffered badly because its ley lines were shallow. Mavuika and the ancient heroes drove the Abyss back, but it infiltrated the Night Kingdom and corrupted the Wayob. Ochkanatlan was overrun and sealed. Fontaine was hit when Elynas arrived with Abyssal sea creatures. The Belyi Tsar died leading an expedition against Abyssal forces in Khaenri'ah, which a Snezhnayan source calls "the hounds of the God of Darkness."
### ULTRA +
At some point after the war of funerary flame, the remains of the Iridescent Moon sank into an Abyssal domain beneath Khaenri'ah. There the goddess's fading soul fused with Abyssal power and became the Crimson Moon. The Crimson Moon Dynasty used it as a conduit to the Abyss. The Abyss also destroyed an alien civilisation called Throgg in the sea of stars at an unknown date.

## present
@version: 7.1
### LITE
As of 7.1: Natlan's war with the Abyss ended with Gosoythoth's destruction. The Wild Hunt in Nod-Krai is weakened and leaderless. Abyssal influence is reported to be growing in Snezhnaya. The Abyss Order, led by the Traveler's sibling, remains active.
### STANDARD +
In Natlan (Archon Quest Chapter V, 5.0-5.3) the Abyss launched a full-scale invasion led by its "heart", Gosoythoth. Once all six heroes were found, Mavuika channelled their power to end the invasion; she and the Traveler then descended into the depths of the Night Kingdom and destroyed Gosoythoth, and the Traveler gained Pyro in that battle. In Nod-Krai (6.x), the Lightkeepers and the Traveler cleansed Kipumaki Cliff and defeated Rauthvangi. After Rerir was driven back into the moon's reflection, the Abyss made an imitation of the knight Roland, which infested Boreas's remains. That threat ended in Varka's story quest (6.4).
### LARGE +
In Snezhnaya (7.1), Rhinedottir told the Tsaritsa that the Belyi Tsar had incited her, then a mere herald, and the Third Descender to open the gate to the Abyss. Rhinedottir blamed that act for the many Abyssal disasters since and said the true blame lay with the Tsar. An NPC, Helka, says Katya warned her that the Abyss's influence is growing in Snezhnaya and told her to avoid the northern paths into the city.

## effects
### LARGE
The Abyss corrupts land, creatures and people (see the separate entry on Abyssal corruption). Physically it causes weakness, nausea, coughing and gradual decay. Mentally it causes hallucinations, agitation, memory loss and amplified desires or fears. Natural elemental beings are especially vulnerable. Corrupted places show darkened, decaying ground and Abyssal growths.

The Traveler is not harmed the way others are and can purify Abyssal energy to some degree: Lisa remarks on their immunity in Mondstadt, and Mualani watches them cleanse it in Natlan. Abyss creatures in combat use the elements freely. The Abyss also produces sapient leaders, such as Gosoythoth, which manifested the Lord of Eroded Primal Fire from the memories of the late Pyro Sovereign Xiuhcoatl, and the imitation of Roland in Nod-Krai.

## open_questions
### STANDARD
Unresolved in canon as of 7.1: what the Abyss fundamentally is; whether it is a single will or a force that spawns minds; whether Nibelung's return and the Pohjola gate are the same entry point or separate breaches; how the twins' homeworld was destroyed; and what the Traveler's sibling and the Abyss Order ultimately intend. Do not resolve these in narration.
### LARGE +
Also open: whether the time difference inside the Abyss is constant; how exactly the Shadow Realm repels the Abyss (the Belyi Tsar only hypothesised that its unobserved possibilities make the two mutually repellent); and whether the Belyi Tsar's notes, which treat the seepage as irreversible but "manageable for the present", are correct.

## connections
### LARGE
Related subjects with their own entries: Abyssal corruption and erosion; the Light Realm, Shadow Realm and three realms; the Heavenly Principles and Celestial Nails; the war of funerary flame; the Cataclysm and the fall of Khaenri'ah; the Abyss Order and its monsters (Abyss Mages, Heralds, Lectors, hilichurls); the Five Sinners; the Wild Hunt; Gosoythoth; the Crimson Moon; forbidden knowledge and the Withering. The Spiral Abyss domain shares the name but is a separate game location.

## roleplay
### LARGE
Roleplay interpretation (this project's recommendation, not canon text): portray the Abyss through its effects, such as purple-black stains, wrongness in the air, sickness and whispers, rather than as a person who explains itself. Ordinary Teyvat folk know "the Abyss" mainly as monsters and the Abyss Order, not cosmology. Scholars, Archons, Hexenzirkel members and Khaenri'ahns know more. Prolonged contact should cost something.
### ULTRA +
Keep the theories as theories: a character may voice Barbeloth's or the Tsar's view only if they could plausibly have heard it. Do not let Vision holders casually wield Abyssal power. In canon, those who do (Skirk, Tartaglia, the Sinners, the Abyss Order) have specific histories with it.

## entry: Void Realm and the Towers of the Three Realms
@keys: Three Realms, Towers of the Three Realms, Aphotic Diffusal, Human Realm
@version: 2.5
### STANDARD
In Enkanomiya, the people stranded under the sea after the war of funerary flame developed a theory of three realms: the Human Realm, the Light Realm and the Void Realm (the Abyss). They built three towers (Kunado's Locus, Yachimatahiko's Locus and Yachimatahime's Locus) to regulate the three powers. In Inazuma's Aphotic Diffusal story, Enjou persuaded the Bathysmal Vishaps to tip the towers toward the Void Realm. The vishaps proved even more vulnerable to it. Tsumi helped push the darkness back.

## entry: The gate to the Abyss in Pohjola
@keys: gates of Pohjola, gate to the Abyss, Pohjola passage
@spoiler: major
@version: 7.1
### STANDARD
Long before the Cataclysm, the Belyi Tsar incited Anastasya, then a herald of his Pale Star Court, and the Third Descender Saarelainen to open a gate to the Abyss. The folk book Hymns of the Far North places it in Pohjola. The opening also created a passage into the Shadow Realm, and Abyssal contamination has seeped into Teyvat through it ever since. The Heavenly Principles erased Anastasya as punishment. Saarelainen bargained for her return by letting himself be split into the seven Gnoses.
### LARGE +
In his own notes the Tsar describes the Shadow Realm as a "film of oil" between Teyvat and the Abyss. He says the opening tore a gash through it and admits he "foolishly" opened the passage. The resulting seepage is slow and irreversible but, he judged, manageable for now. The Hymns' version, in which Anastasya is the Tsar's daughter and Saarelainen opens the gates as a bride-price, conflicts with other sources' chronology and should be treated as folklore.

## entry: Time in the Abyss
@keys: time in the Abyss
@keys2: Skirk, Tartaglia, Childe, Ajax, Takamine
### STANDARD
Time inside the Abyss runs far slower relative to Teyvat. Tartaglia (Ajax) fell into the Abyss at fourteen and trained under Skirk for three months, but only three days passed at home. In 5.7, Skirk took the Traveler to her "home" in the Abyss for that reason. She did not dispute Paimon's estimate that a month there is a day outside. Takamine the Mistsplitter, by contrast, emerged after so long that Asase Hibiki was "no longer young", so the effect may not be uniform.

## entry: The Abyss and the Five Sinners
@keys: Five Sinners
@keys2: Abyss, Void Realm
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The five Sinners of Khaenri'ah (Surtalogi, Rhinedottir "Gold", Vedrfolnir, Hroptatyr and Rerir) were drawn by the Abyss's call and split a world-shattering power taken from it among themselves. Rhinedottir created Abyss-linked life, including the dragon Durin and the Riftwolves. Surtalogi taught Skirk to wield Abyssal power. Vedrfolnir inspired Chlothar to found the Abyss Order. Rerir leads the Wild Hunt in Nod-Krai.

## entry: The Abyss in Natlan
@keys: Abyssal invasion, Night Kingdom
@keys2: Abyss, Gosoythoth, Night Warden
@version: 5.0
### LARGE
Natlan fought the Abyss for centuries. The Sacred Flame, fed by Contending Fire from pilgrimages and Night Warden Wars, held most of it back, and the Abyss adapted its tactics tribe by tribe. After the Cataclysm it infiltrated the Night Kingdom and corrupted the Wayob. In the Archon Quest it trapped Kachina in the Night Kingdom and then launched a full invasion led by Gosoythoth, the "heart of the Abyss". With all six heroes found, Mavuika and the Traveler descended and destroyed Gosoythoth, ending Natlan's war against the Abyss.

## entry: The Abyss in Nod-Krai
@keys: Kipumaki Cliff, Evil Eye, Rauthvangi
@keys2: Abyss, Wild Hunt, Nod-Krai
@version: 6.0
### LARGE
Nod-Krai was among the first lands struck in the Cataclysm. The Wild Hunt, corrupted dead under Rerir hunting for moon fragments, has roamed it ever since. Abyssal influence is strongest at Kipumaki Cliff, under a giant Evil Eye, where explorers are slowly corrupted. The Lightkeepers and the Traveler cleansed it and killed Rauthvangi, its mastermind. Rauthvangi's agents included Morozov, who became the "Ultimate Calamity" in a sealed bunker, and Raimo of Dreadshade Mire.

## entry: Barbeloth's interpretation of the Abyss
@keys: fates that will not come to pass, unchosen fates
@keys2: Abyss, Barbeloth, Alice, Hexenzirkel
@availability: limited
@version: 6.4
### STANDARD
During the limited-time event Homeward, He Who Caught the Wind (6.4), a magical recreation of Mondstadt's past grew a "nascent" Abyssal power. Afterwards, Alice told the Traveler that the Hexenzirkel had long observed the Abyss and that Barbeloth's reading was the most accurate. On that reading, the essence of the Abyss is the hatred carried by every fate that will not come to pass: each time one thing happens, other possibilities are eliminated. Alice's example was Rhinedottir's choice to tap the Abyss, which erased the future in which she stayed pure. Out of that resentment the Abyss seeks the destruction of everything and is obsessed with entering the world.
### LARGE +
Alice called the Abyss perilous, cunning, obsessive and "a complete and utter mystery", and treated such incidents as common on the "border" she travels. Only those who heard this conversation, or know Hexenzirkel thinking, would know the theory.
