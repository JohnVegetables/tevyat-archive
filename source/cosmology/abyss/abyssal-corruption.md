## summary
### LITE
Abyssal corruption (also called Abyssal corrosion or contamination) is the general term for the harm the Abyss does to the world. The Abyss holds the power of the Void Realm, which is directly opposed to the Light Realm and the Human Realm that Teyvat belongs to, so contact with it damages people, creatures, land and objects. Its most notable surge so far was the Cataclysm 500 years ago, when monsters made from Abyssal power attacked from Khaenri'ah.
### STANDARD +
The corruption poisons body and mind, can be removed only with difficulty, and in advanced cases fuses with its host. The power of Celestia and the Light Realm, including the seven elements, repels it.

## established
@spoiler: minor
### LITE
Even brief exposure can cause dizziness, weakness, nausea or a worsening cough, and mental effects such as hallucinations, agitation, jumbled thoughts and memory loss. Long contact can cause severe, permanent damage and can warp a person's personality by amplifying their desires and fears.
### STANDARD +
Chasca says that once a lot of corruption has entered a body it fuses irreversibly with the internal organs. Beyond a certain point it cannot be removed without killing the host. Mualani notes that soaking in hot water speeds up some physical symptoms. Chasca's long-running, compulsive urge to fight is also linked to it.
### LARGE +
Paradoxically, corruption can greatly extend life. Jakob and the Yumkasaur Kongamato were both born more than 500 years ago and lived to the present by absorbing Abyssal energy. The goddess Sonnet survived the shattering of the Iridescent Moon in the war of funerary flame by fusing its remnants with the Abyss, and passed that influence to the Crimson Moon Dynasty's descendants as the Balemoon Bloodfire, an ability that inflicts cumulative corrosion on its user. Arlecchino, a modern bearer, says the corruption hungers for her soul and would change her completely if it reached her heart.
Abyssal blood is a lasting source of taint. Monsters of Khaenri'ah, many of them made by the alchemist Rhinedottir, carry blood tainted by the Abyss, and that blood lingers in the land where it is spilled.
### ULTRA +
Removing the corruption does not always heal the mind. After the Abyssal invasion of Natlan, some warriors kept PTSD-like symptoms even after the Traveler purified their corrosion. Capitano named this Abyssal Corrosion Syndrome, which he said he had also seen during the Cataclysm, and gave them the Draught of Lucidity.

## effects_on_creatures
@version: 5.2
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Abyssal Corrosion Syndrome is the named illness for the lasting form of this harm, from direct contact with high concentrations of Abyssal power. Its symptoms include psychosis with visual and auditory hallucinations and reliving past trauma. In Natlan, Atoco relived a near-drowning from his childhood, and Cochitta relived the death of her parents, which stoked her fear of losing loved ones. Mild cases respond to talking and physical contact. Extreme cases such as Ocotlan respond only to the Draught of Lucidity. Saurians can suffer too: Ayo sank into melancholy after the invasion, and a small dose of the draught lifted his mood.

## purification
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
The power of Celestia and the Light Realm repels Abyssal corruption, and so do the seven elements derived from it. People tied to the elements, such as Vision holders, resist Abyssal energy better, including Forbidden Knowledge. Destroying the source of a corruption can halt it, permanently or only for a time depending on the source's strength. The Fatui built the Mokosh Device, which clears Abyssal corruption to a limited degree.
### LARGE +
Local remedies exist. In the Chasm of Liyue, unearthing a fragment of a Chasm Nail made Abyssal Dark Mud and Celestial Lumenstone both spread; a large Lumenstone, the Lumenstone Adjuvant, can clear Dark Mud. In the Girdle of the Sands, the Amrita derived from the Hydro Archon Egeria defends the land from defilement; her familiars the Pari, using Khvarena (made jointly by the angel Nabu Malikata, the Dendro Archon Rukkhadevata and the Amrita), can purify or restrain corruption. Gray Crystals are Abyssal remnants of the Cataclysm that were sealed with Khvarena. In Nod-Krai, kuuvahki, the primordial power of the moons, can cleanse corruption, hurt Abyssal enemies and resist Abyssal Decay.
Venti is said to recover at the Symbol of Mondstadt's Hero, which is held to have grown where Vennessa ascended and carries Anemo power. He used it after Abyssal poison from Dvalin's curse hit him in Mondstadt.
### ULTRA +
The Traveler can take in Abyssal energy without evident harm. They purified one of Dvalin's tears, which stung Lisa when she touched it, and later cleared corruption from the Festering Desire sword, Atea, Vichama, Chuychu and other Natlan warriors, a group of Bake-Danuki, the Iridescent Moon's marrow, Durin, and the Wayob of the Children of Echoes. In Fated Warrior they also reversed an Abyssal assimilation, separating the Wolf Spirit's soul from an Abyssal imitation of Roland. Whether this is real purification is unsettled (see open_questions).

## accounts
### STANDARD
Different characters describe the same force differently. Capitano frames the long-term psychological damage as a syndrome and a medical problem. Chasca describes how it fuses irreversibly to the organs, and her own chronic, compulsive urge to fight is linked to it. Khedive, speaking about the Chasm, pictures humanity as beans on a mill, crushed between the powers of Celestia from above and the Abyss from below. Skirk questions whether 'purification' is even the right word, since power does not just vanish.

## open_questions
### STANDARD
Canon has not explained how the Traveler is unharmed by the Abyss, whether Natlan's underground sludge matches the Dark Mud, or what became of the stolen Defiled Statue. The Abyss itself is covered in the Abyss entry and its own uncertainties.

## connections
### STANDARD
This entry is the effects side of the Abyss; the Abyss entry covers its nature and origin. The Cataclysm and the fall of Khaenri'ah were the largest surge. The Abyss Order, made up in part of people turned into monsters, can spread corruption. Natlan's war with the Abyss and Nod-Krai's Abyssal Decay are regional cases.

## entry: Draught of Lucidity
@keys: Draught of Lucidity, Guthred
@version: 5.2
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
A medicine made by Guthred, a Khaenri'ahn military doctor during the Cataclysm, to treat Abyssal Corrosion Syndrome. Endless fighting against the Abyss had caused hallucinations so bad that some Khaenri'ahn soldiers tried to kill one another. Guthred tested it on himself and died soon after finishing it. It dispels the psychological effects but causes severe side effects, including chronic migraines for life, so it is reserved for the worst cases. It works on Saurians as well as humans. After the Abyssal invasion of Natlan, Capitano gave doses to the Flower-Feather Clan to cure their afflicted warriors.

## entry: Dark Mud
@keys: Dark Mud, Oozing Concretion, Oozing Core
@keys2: Chasm, Abyssal, Abyss, corruption
### STANDARD
Dark Mud is a form of Abyssal corruption in the Chasm in Liyue, usually with an Oozing Concretion at its heart. Unearthing a Chasm Nail fragment made it spread, while Celestial Lumenstone grew too, as though the two powers were at war. A large Lumenstone Adjuvant clears the mud. The quest What Shape Does the Self Hold, in the Homecoming event, reveals the mud to be Forbidden Knowledge that the Celestial Nail changed into this form. Similar corruption is the dark mud of Natlan's Ancient Sacred Mountain, found with Oozing Cores, and the Blightseep Fields and Abyssal Riftlines of Nod-Krai.

## entry: Miasmic Tumor
@keys: Miasmic Tumor, Sacred Sakura, Hanachirusato
@keys2: Abyssal, filth, corruption
@version: 2.0
### STANDARD
The Miasmic Tumor was filth absorbed by the Sacred Sakura, which Makoto made and Ei planted in Inazuma to dampen the Abyss's attack during the Cataclysm. In the Inazuma World Quest series Sacred Sakura Cleansing Ritual, the Traveler helps the shrine maiden Hanachirusato, herself a resurgence of filth formed from the memories of the late Kitsune Saiguu, cleanse the roots and purge the tumor. Other 'tumors' in canon are the Abyss Tumors Gosoythoth summoned in Natlan and the Root-Consuming Tumors infesting the Roots of Erinnyes in Fontaine.

## entry: Tainted Blood and Durin
@keys: Abyssal blood, Frostbearing Tree, Crimson Agate
@keys2: Durin, Dragonspine, Dvalin
### STANDARD
When the Abyssal dragon Durin was slain over Dragonspine, its blood seeped into the land, revived the Frostbearing Tree and crystallized into Crimson Agate, which can be offered to the tree to make it grow, defying the Skyfrost Nail's freezing of the region. Dvalin, who killed Durin with a bite to the neck, swallowed some of the blood. The poison and his wounds tormented him, and the Abyss Order exploited this after he woke 500 years later. When Venti tried to purify Dvalin, the Traveler and Paimon interrupted by accident; the attempt failed and Venti was poisoned.

## entry: Dvalin in the Homeward simulation
@keys: Homeward, He Who Caught the Wind, Variations on the Song of the Dragon and Freedom
@keys2: Dvalin, Abyss, Abyssal
@version: 6.4
@availability: limited
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
In the limited-time event Homeward, He Who Caught the Wind (6.4), the Traveler sees an alternate Mondstadt where the Honorary Knight never arrived. There Dvalin was sealed rather than saved, and the Abyss kept eroding his mind until he broke free. Alice says the power of the Abyss has fused with his flesh and bones, so purifying him in that state would be no different from killing him. This illustrates the point that advanced corruption cannot be removed without killing the host.

## entry: Defiled Statue
@keys: Defiled Statue, Loom of Fate
### STANDARD
A Statue of The Seven in Barbatos's image, taken from Mondstadt by the Abyss Order for a project called the Loom of Fate. The Order meant to join it with the power of the ancient god Osial and the core of the First Field Tiller to make a weapon able to destroy Celestia. Dainsleif and the Traveler's intervention left the project at a standstill, but the statue's fate has not been addressed.

## entry: Balemoon Bloodfire
@keys: Balemoon Bloodfire, Iridescent Moon
@keys2: Sonnet, Arlecchino, Crimson Moon
### STANDARD
Sonnet, goddess of the Iridescent Moon, fused her shattered moon with the Abyss to survive the war of funerary flame and gave that influence to the Khaenri'ahn Crimson Moon Dynasty's descendants as the Balemoon Bloodfire. Using it inflicts cumulative Abyssal corrosion on the user.

## entry: Foul Legacy
@keys: Foul Legacy, Foul Legacy Transformation
@keys2: Tartaglia, Childe, Skirk, Surtalogi
### STANDARD
Tartaglia's Foul Legacy Transformation is an Abyss-derived combat technique, not a power of his Delusion. He learned it from Skirk after falling into the Abyss, before joining the Fatui, and it is named for Surtalogi 'the Foul', Skirk's own master. He grows to twice his size in black and violet armour and wields his Hydro Vision and Electro Delusion together, but it badly drains and lastingly harms him. He used it in the Golden House fight, against the All-Devouring Narwhal in Fontaine and in other scenes. Within the story the name refers only to this form.

## entry: Abyssal Decay
@keys: Abyssal Decay
@keys2: Nod-Krai, Kipumaki Cliff, Wild Hunt, Oath Lantern
### STANDARD
In Nod-Krai, exposure to the Wild Hunt's influence at Kipumaki Cliff builds up Abyssal Decay, which drains health and narrows vision unless the person is protected by an Oath Lantern or other kuuvahki. This is mostly a gameplay gauge; the in-world point is that kuuvahki resists Abyssal influence.
