## summary
### LITE
The Crimson Moon is the baleful red moon of Khaenri'ah's old legends. It is in truth the shattered remnant of the Iridescent Moon, left when its goddess Sonnet fused with the Abyss to stay alive. Khaenri'ah's Crimson Moon Dynasty revered it, and the Balemoon Bloodfire of its surviving descendants, such as Arlecchino, comes from it.
### STANDARD
The Crimson Moon is the baleful red moon of Khaenri'ah's old legends. It is in truth the shattered remnant of the Iridescent Moon, the third of the Three Moons, left when its goddess Sonnet fused with the Abyss to stay alive. Khaenri'ah's Crimson Moon Dynasty revered it and drew a power from its reflection. Its Balemoon Bloodfire still burns in the bloodline's last descendants, such as Arlecchino. Sonnet's marrow lay sealed in an Abyssal domain until the Traveler and Columbina entered it in the Nod-Krai Archon Quest (6.1), where Sonnet died for good.

## established
@version: 6.1
@spoiler: major
### LITE
- Arlecchino states that the Crimson Moon is only the shattered Iridescent Moon, fused with the Abyss to prolong its life. Paimon checks her understanding: the Crimson Moon the Khaenri'ahns worshipped was really the Iridescent Moon, one of the Three Moon Sisters.
- Khaenri'ah's Crimson Moon Dynasty worshipped it. Columbina relates that a coup later saw it "eclipsed", and the Eclipse Dynasty took its place. Arlecchino suspects the Khaenri'ahns used the moon's remains mainly as their link to the Abyss rather than out of deep devotion.
### STANDARD +
- After the war of funerary flame the Iridescent Moon was shattered. Sonnet could not bear dying and fused her fading soul with the Abyss. This kept her barely alive as a broken husk, in lasting torment, and turned her moon into the Crimson Moon.
- The Iridescent Moon's marrow and remains were sealed in an Abyssal domain. Columbina cannot sense them anywhere in Teyvat. Lauma says Sonnet's remains vanished in an instant, so unlike Aria's they were never found in the overworld.
- The domain opens only briefly, through a ritual that needs Balemoon Bloodfire. The sages of the Crimson Moon Dynasty used the same rite to brand children received in their orphanage with the Crimson Moon's light (Arlecchino's reading of the Perinheri legend).
### LARGE +
- The Balemoon Bloodfire is a fusion of the Iridescent Moon's former power and Abyssal corruption. It is also called the "Two Worlds' Flames", and Arlecchino calls herself one of its last surviving embers. It uses Abyssal energy, and she knows that if the corruption reached her heart she would become someone else, so she uses it with care.
- Arlecchino says she is an inheritor of the Crimson Moon's royal bloodline. She first saw her fire only as a curse; once she became a Harbinger, the Jester (Pierro) told her the truth, and she pieced together more through her own investigations. She does not know why the dynasty fell, and guesses that a rival faction found a way to touch the Abyss directly, or that an ideological split led to a coup that drove out the royal heirs.
- The Traveler can purify the Abyssal corruption on the marrow. In the domain they find the Iridescent Moon's broken husk hanging in the sky and its marrow hidden in the husk's reflection.
- The Universitas Magistrorum in Khaenri'ah kept a Moon Marrow laboratory devoted to studying the Crimson Moon's remains, where the researcher Tholindis worked.

## history
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Under the Crimson Moon Dynasty, Khaenri'ah revered the Crimson Moon. Its only direct account is the legend of Perinheri, which cannot be verified. In that legend a "crimson moon" shone over the subterranean realm, and a sage proposed an institution to house children from beyond the world, hoping one would have power to rise above the gods. The legend places a "dark sun" in the moon's stead later. Canon calls the dynasty that replaced the Crimson Moon Dynasty the Eclipse Dynasty, and it ruled until the Cataclysm 500 years ago.
### LARGE +
Alchemy and the Beastmaster Knights were mainstays of the Crimson Moon era (Perinheri's footnotes), before the Eclipse Dynasty turned to mechanical engineering. The Crimson Moon sages wanted beings with powers beyond the gods, but feared losing control, so they put the children through a process of "refinement". Arlecchino reads that as branding, a mark by which they would live always in the Crimson Moon's light.

After the coup, the royal line of the Crimson Moon was driven out. Much later the last Eclipse ruler, the Vinster King (Irmin), still coveted the secrets it left behind and sent Rerir (the "Rächer of Solnari") to hunt its remaining followers, whom Jahoda calls the "Dregs of the Crimson Moon". The bloodline was never wholly wiped out. Tholindis, a distant descendant from a sub-branch whose family had cut ties long before, was Rerir's would-be fiancee: he had proposed, and she had deferred her answer. Before the Cataclysm she gave a piece of the Crimson Moon to Frithr, another descendant, so that the line would not fade. Frithr was captured, but Rerir spared her and told her to stay hidden.
### ULTRA +
Surviving descendants carried the bloodline into the present. Arlecchino, the head of the House of the Hearth, holds the Balemoon Bloodfire. In the Nod-Krai Archon Quest (6.1) she opens the ritual path for the Traveler and Columbina, who want the Iridescent Moon's marrow to restore Columbina's kuuvahki against the revived Rerir. As the Traveler purifies the marrow, Sonnet briefly regains her lucidity. She greets Columbina as a dear friend, calling her by the name "Hypo-", which belongs to a future Columbina she met in the moon's reflection (see Three Moons), thanks her, and dies. The Abyssal will left in the domain then tries to keep the marrow and the party in, as the husk falls like meteors. Arlecchino clears their way with the Crimson Moon's power. Columbina gives Arlecchino the marrow as agreed; Dottore later steals it, and after his defeat Columbina returns it to her. See the Moon Marrow and Three Moons entries for that fight.

## authority_of_death
@version: 7.1
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
In the 7.1 Archon Quest "A Rekviem for the Underworld", Pierro explains that before the Four Great Rulers (the Four Shades), the authorities of Life, Death, Time and Space belonged to the Three Moons. The Crimson Moon took up the responsibilities of death, and after the goddesses died, the broken Crimson Moon merged with the Abyss and acted as though its power had never been taken from it. Pierro and Arlecchino use this in a project to create more Delusions for the coming clash with Ronova, and Arlecchino's descent from the Crimson Moon is the reason she is wanted. In a test, researchers say the new Delusions also drew from "another authority" under the Crimson Moon's jurisdiction, adding an unstable variable, and the Traveler must pull Arlecchino out as the corrosion hits its maximum. The quest does not say what that authority was.

## open_questions
### STANDARD
- Canon does not say how Khaenri'ah first found the Crimson Moon, when the dynasty began, or why it fell.
- Perinheri is a legend; the wiki's reading of its "crimson moon" and "dark sun" as the two dynasties is interpretation.
### LARGE +
- The exact reach of the Crimson Moon's authority over death is not defined, and "another authority" in the Delusion test is not named.
- Where the sealed domain lies, and how the marrow was taken there, is not explained.
- Whether "Solnari" is another name for the Crimson Moon or Sonnet is the wiki's inference only.

## connections
### STANDARD
Sonnet and the Iridescent Moon (origin); the Three Moons and the Moon Marrows; the Abyss (the force it fused with); Khaenri'ah's Crimson Moon and Eclipse Dynasties; Arlecchino, Tholindis, Frithr and Rerir; the weapon Crimson Moon's Semblance, whose story speaks of priests who prayed to a long-dead Crimson Moon and of the epithet "Balemoon" that outlived its name; Columbina.
### LARGE +
Gameplay: Arlecchino's Elemental Burst is named "Balemoon Rising", and the Archon Quest title "Everlasting As the Moon" echoes one of her burst lines. Roleplay note (this project's guidance): treat the Balemoon Bloodfire as costly and corrupting, never as free power, and do not have an ordinary Khaenri'ahn descendant know the moon's true identity.

## entry: Balemoon Bloodfire
@keys: Balemoon Bloodfire, Bloodfire, Two Worlds' Flames, Balemoon
@keys2: Arlecchino, Crimson Moon
@version: 6.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Balemoon Bloodfire is the flame carried by descendants of the Crimson Moon's royal bloodline. It is Sonnet's former power fused with Abyssal corruption, also called the "Two Worlds' Flames". It can leave afterimages of what it burns, and it is the key for opening the sealed domain of the Iridescent Moon's marrow. Using it slowly corrupts its holder. Arlecchino is among the last who carry it.

## entry: Crimson Moon Dynasty
@keys: Crimson Moon Dynasty, Crimson Moon sages, Dregs of the Crimson Moon
@version: 6.1
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
The Crimson Moon Dynasty is the earlier of Khaenri'ah's two known ruling dynasties, named for the moon it revered and overthrown in a coup by the Eclipse Dynasty. Little survives about it. Its sages operated an institution for children from beyond the world, which Pierro implies was the original House of the Hearth. Its surviving descendants, such as Tholindis and Frithr, were hunted under the Vinster King; Arlecchino is a present-day descendant. See the faction entry for fuller detail.

## entry: Crimson Moon's Semblance
@keys: Crimson Moon's Semblance
@version: 4.6
@spoiler: minor
### LARGE
Crimson Moon's Semblance is a 5-star polearm. Its in-game description calls it a keen weapon whose luster dazzles like the crimson moon, once regarded by an ancient dynasty as a ritual object bridging two worlds. Its weapon story tells of dogmatic priests who convinced a king that the remnants of the Crimson Moon ruled all, and says that by the time of the "blackened sun" only the epithet "Balemoon" remained. In the Archon Quest, Arlecchino's scythe changes to an unascended Crimson Moon's Semblance when she opens the passage into the marrow's resting place.
