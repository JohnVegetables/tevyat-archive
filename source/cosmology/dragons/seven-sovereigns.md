## summary
### LITE
The Seven Sovereigns are the seven elemental dragons, one per element, who ruled the old world of Teyvat under the Dragon King Nibelung. The Heavenly Principles defeated them and stole part of their power, the elemental Authorities, which became the basis of the Archons' Authorities. Living Sovereigns therefore lack full "dragonhood" until an Archon's throne is destroyed and the Authority returned, which has happened only for Hydro. Current Sovereigns: Dvalin (Anemo, Mondstadt), Azhdaha (Geo, sealed in Liyue), Apep (Dendro, Sumeru) and Neuvillette (Hydro, Fontaine, reborn in human form). Xiuhcoatl (Pyro) and the Cryo Dragon are dead with no successor; no Electro Sovereign is known.
### STANDARD +
The Chinese title means "Dragon King", which English sometimes renders "Dragon Lord". They are not Natlan's Thirteen Sovereign Lords, who served Xiuhcoatl.

## established
### STANDARD
- They were the dragon-lords of the old world. Each held one Authority and a domain attuned to its element.
- In Calamitous Tread (4.1), Neuvillette says no living Sovereign, himself included, holds full dragonhood, and that the power the "First Usurper" seized is the basis of the Archons' Authorities.
- A Sovereign is born only from an elementally pure lineage. Bathysmal Vishaps, which diverged into Electro and Cryo forms, are impure. A Sovereign may instead be reborn in human form, like Neuvillette.
### LARGE +
- A new generation replaces the fading originals: Dvalin, born from a convergence of elemental energy high in the heavens, succeeded a dead Anemo dragon; Neuvillette succeeded the Hydro Dragon.
- Lost dragonhood also means lost memory: Neuvillette did not remember his link to the Primordial Sea until his Authority came back in 4.2.

## sovereigns
### STANDARD
- Anemo: Dvalin (Mondstadt), alive and Venti's ally again since the Prologue; his unnamed predecessor is dead.
- Geo: Azhdaha (Liyue), once Morax's friend, eroded and sealed beneath the Dragon-Queller; resealed in Zhongli's Story Quest (1.5).
- Electro: unknown.
- Dendro: Apep (Sumeru), purged of forbidden knowledge in Homecoming (3.6); now merely observes gods and humans.
- Hydro: Neuvillette (Fontaine), the only Sovereign with his full Authority back (4.2).
- Pyro: Xiuhcoatl (Natlan), killed about 2,000 years ago by Xbalanque, the first Pyro Archon.
- Cryo: the Cryo Dragon (Snezhnaya), long dead; its remains lie beneath a Celestial Nail by the ruins of Hyperborea.
### LARGE +
Details:
- Dvalin was drawn to Barbatos by the Holy Lyre der Himmel, became the Dragon of the East of the Four Winds, and was poisoned by Durin's blood 500 years ago.
- Azhdaha fought for Morax in the Archon War, but erosion and mining turned him against humans and he destroyed Tianqiu Valley. Morax needed three adepti to seal him, and Azhdaha allowed it.
- Apep ruled a forested land and its primordial Dendro lifeforms. She let King Deshret build a kingdom in her domain on condition that all the knowledge he gathered would belong to her after his death; that knowledge was forbidden and corrupted her.
- The Hydro Dragon was the heart of the Primordial Sea, from which all life on the planet first came, and so could be called the original "God of Life". It withered after the defeat; the Heavenly Principles made Egeria as a replacement heart, and no new Hydro Dragon could be born while she hibernated.
- Xiuhcoatl presided over light, heat and wisdom and commanded Phlogiston; at his birth he created his "brother" Kukulkan.

## history
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
The world belonged to the dragons from the dawn of time. The Byakuyakoku Collection (2.4) records that when the "eternal throne of the heavens" came, the Primordial One fought "the seven terrifying sovereigns, dragon-lords of the old world" with four shades of itself; forty years later the Sovereigns were vanquished and the seven nations submitted. Later, the seas took in those who rebelled and would not kneel. Surviving Sovereigns fell into deep slumber; Xiuhcoatl lay frozen in liquid Phlogiston in a "false death".

In the Funerary Year Nibelung returned bearing the power of the Abyss. The Sovereigns could not resist his call and joined his "great war of vengeance". They lost again and the world nearly collapsed, but the Heavenly Principles was so damaged it could no longer suppress the old order alone. It and "one who came after" created the seven Gnoses, which set off the Archon War for seven new divine thrones (Neuvillette's Vision story).
### ULTRA +
Apep's account (Homecoming, 3.6): Nibelung took the power of darkness from outside the world and led the dragons against the outsiders' order. After his death Apep kept gathering forbidden knowledge until a giant spike from the sky (a Celestial Nail) stopped her and turned her forest into dunes. By the time the Dragon King returned, she said, some dragons had grown close to humans and forgotten their hatred, some had died, and others fled.

Earlier still, Nibelung met the star-voyager Anacharsis, who warned that Teyvat would be destroyed. He refused to abandon his people, tried to lead the dragons to the stars, and eventually left Teyvat, creating the Three Moons to govern in his place (Deep Gallery's Distant Pact artifact text, 5.5; Fractured Halo weapon lore, 5.8; Song of the Welkin Moon, 6.x). During the war, Nibelung and other dragons burned Natlan's ley lines and devoured all of its angels except the Lord of the Night.

After Nibelung's defeat, a briefly lucid Xiuhcoatl built the Tzitzimimeh and the device Huitzilopochtli, which would turn Natlan into pure Phlogiston to make it immune to the Abyss, then sealed himself beneath the Great Volcano. Kukulkan opposed this and made the human-dragon hybrid Och-Kan to slay Xiuhcoatl, but Och-Kan sided with the human Xbalanque, who killed the Pyro Dragon and, resurrected by Ronova, became the first Pyro Archon.

## current_state
@version: 7.1
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
As of 7.1: Neuvillette holds the full Hydro Authority, returned when Focalors arranged her own execution and destroyed the Hydro throne (4.2). Acknowledging human will, he set aside parts of his own power so that Visions can still be granted (Vision character story). Dvalin, Apep and Neuvillette are at peace with the present world, and Dvalin still helps Venti (in Varka's Story Quest, 6.4, he carried the Traveler toward Nod-Krai). Azhdaha remains sealed. The other Authorities remain tied to the Archons' thrones; in A Finale Emberforged (5.5) Kukulkan arranged for the Saurian Little One, a descendant of Xiuhcoatl, to inherit the Pyro Authority, but Little One refused, Kukulkan passed away, and the Authority stayed with Mavuika.
### ULTRA +
In Black Feathers Scattered at Parting (Archon Quest Chapter VII, 7.1), a mortally wounded Ronova appeared above the Cryo Dragon's corpse and fused the Eitr, retrieved from Hyperborea's hiding place in the Shadow Realm, with the Nail of Retribution hovering there, hoping the Holy Soil would spread across Teyvat so that all would feel death. The Nail was undone and pillars of light rose over the six Celestial Nails of Teyvat. What this means for the Cryo Sovereign's power is unresolved at the cutoff.

## accounts
### LARGE
- Apep calls the Heavenly Principles the "usurper from beyond the heavens"; her hatred remains, though she sees no purpose in destroying humans, and Nibelung's scales and will "still shine bright" in her heart.
- Neuvillette recalls the usurper appointing its own "God of Life" and creating Egeria to suppress the planet's original vital force. He believes Nibelung was wrong: all kinds of life together are needed against the Abyss.
- The Byakuyakoku chronicle, written by worshippers of the Primordial One, presents the conquest as the rightful creation of a world for humanity.
### ULTRA +
- Aksinya (7.0) calls Nibelung "a will born alongside Teyvat", master of the Light Realm's power and "King of all Dragon Lords".
- Kukulkan foresaw his kin falling because they enforced their might through violence, and hoped to merge the human and dragon races so that dragonkind could resist the Abyss by subtler means.
- Fontaine's children's rhyme says it rains when the Hydro Dragon weeps; Neuvillette doubts whoever wrote it knew the Hydro Dragon well.

## open_questions
### LARGE
- Who was (or is) the Electro Sovereign? Who was the original Anemo Sovereign, and is Azhdaha an original or a successor?
- What happens to an Authority if an Archon dies without destroying the throne? Canon shows only the Hydro case.
### ULTRA +
- Whether Pyro or Cryo can ever produce new Sovereigns: Little One refused the Pyro inheritance, and the Cryo Dragon's remains gave rise to the Belyi Tsar instead.
- What the undoing of the Nail over the Cryo Dragon will unleash (7.1).

## connections
### LARGE
- The Seven (Archons): their divine thrones rest on the stolen Authorities. Venti and Zhongli each befriended a dragon (Dvalin, Azhdaha).
- Gnosis: created by the Heavenly Principles with "one who came after" (Saarelainen) after Nibelung's war.
- Four Shades: the Heavenly Principles' four shades, including Ronova, fought the Sovereigns.
### ULTRA +
- Hyperborea used the Cryo Dragon's corpse to try to create a new Primordial Human with authority equal to Nibelung's; the Sovereign's unchecked power turned Snezhnaya from green land into the Holy Soil of primordial ice, and a Celestial Nail sent Hyperborea into the Shadow Realm. A thousand years later Monomakh Snezhnyi, the Belyi Tsar, emerged from the remains (7.0).
- Enkanomiya's prophecy foretold the Hydro Dragon's rebirth in human form. In the Byakuyakoku chronicle, the Sovereigns' people sheltered in the oceans as the Dragonheirs of the Depths and warred with the Byakuyakoku in the dark.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay interpretation (this project's recommendation, not canon text): Sovereigns are ancient, proud and alien, and may see Archons as the usurper's appointees. Ordinary people do not know the term or the history of the Authorities. Do not invent an Electro Sovereign or a Cryo successor; keep Azhdaha sealed and Dvalin, Apep and Neuvillette non-hostile as of 7.1.

## entry: Authority (elemental)
@keys: elemental Authority, elemental Authorities, dragonhood, Sovereign's Authority
@spoiler: major
@version: 4.1
### STANDARD
An elemental Authority is a Sovereign's dominion over its element. The Heavenly Principles seized part of each Sovereign's power, and it became the basis of the Archons' Authorities and divine thrones. A living Sovereign cannot reach full dragonhood, or its memories of the old world, until the Archon's throne is destroyed and the Authority returned. This has happened once: Focalors arranged her own execution in 4.2, destroying the Hydro throne and returning the Hydro Authority to Neuvillette.

## entry: Hydro Dragon
@keys: Hydro Dragon, Dragon of Water, Hydro Sovereign, Dragon Sovereign of Water
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The original Hydro Sovereign was the heart of the Primordial Sea and ruled the vishaps of the waters. It perished after the war against the Heavenly Principles. Fontaine's children's rhyme says that when the Hydro Dragon weeps, it rains; Neuvillette, its human-form successor, is puzzled by the idea that the dragon could cry.

## entry: Cryo Dragon
@keys: Cryo Dragon, Cryo Sovereign, Cryo Dragon Sovereign, Primordial Cryo Dragon
@spoiler: major
@version: 7.0
### LARGE
The Cryo Sovereign died in the war with the heavens. Hyperborea later used its flesh and blood to try to forge a Primordial Human. Its unchecked authority turned Snezhnaya into the Holy Soil of primordial ice, and Hyperborea was struck by a Celestial Nail. The corpse and the Nail stayed above ground, and a thousand years later the Belyi Tsar emerged from the remains. In 7.1 a dying Ronova fused Eitr with that Nail.

## entry: Xiuhcoatl and the Pyro seat
@keys: Xiuhcoatl, Pyro Sovereign, Pyro Dragon Sovereign, Huitzilopochtli
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
Xiuhcoatl, the Pyro Sovereign of ancient Natlan, was frozen in liquid Phlogiston in a "false death" after the first defeat. Woken by Nibelung, he was later corrupted by the Abyss. In lucid moments he built the Tzitzimimeh and Huitzilopochtli, a device that would turn Natlan into pure Phlogiston, then sealed himself beneath the Great Volcano. Xbalanque slew him about 2,000 years ago and became the first Pyro Archon. In A Finale Emberforged (5.5), Little One, a Saurian descendant of Xiuhcoatl, refused to inherit the Pyro Authority, which stayed with Mavuika.

## entry: Byakuyakoku chronicle of the Sovereigns
@keys: When the Doves Held Branches, Forty Years After the Held Branches, seven terrifying sovereigns
@spoiler: minor
@version: 2.4
### ULTRA
The Byakuyakoku Collection, Vol. 2 ("Before Sun and Moon") dates the conquest in the chronicle's own era names. In "When the Doves Held Branches", the eternal throne of the heavens arrives and the Primordial One battles the Sovereigns with four shades of itself. By "Forty Years After the Held Branches" ("forty winters entombed the flames, and forty summers churned the seas") the Sovereigns are vanquished. In "The First Year of Darkness" the Sovereigns' people, sheltered in the oceans as the Dragonheirs of the Depths, war with the fallen Byakuyakoku in the dark. The chronicle is partisan scripture, not neutral history.
