## summary
### LITE
The Thirteen Sovereign Lords were thirteen artificial rulers made by Xiuhcoatl, the Pyro Dragon Sovereign, to govern ancient Natlantea. Each held a territory, and together they met as a council in the Chamber of Deliberation under the First Lord as Speaker. They oppressed Natlantea's humans for thousands of years. Ten fell when the Sacred City of Tollan collapsed in the war between Xbalanque and Xiuhcoatl, and the Ninth Lord, long sealed in Chichen Uctokah, was freed from her bonds and put to rest by the Traveler at her own request. A few survive in changed form, notably Ajaw and the Eleventh Lord's surviving core in Ineffa.
### STANDARD +
They are not ordinary dragons. Citlali explains in the quest "Smoldering" that they were artificial intelligences built with secret source technology, with greater intelligence and authority than the Pyro dragons themselves. Their personal names end in a number in a Mayan language (Hun is one, Ka' two, and so on up to Ooxlahun, thirteen) plus the designation Ch'ule-L.

## established
### LITE
- Creator: Xiuhcoatl, the Pyro Dragon of the Seven Sovereigns, who ruled Natlantea.
- Role: each Lord governed an individual territory of Natlantea; they assembled in the Chamber of Deliberation to debate and vote, with the First Lord, the Valiant One, presiding as Speaker.
- Fate: ten fell in the fall of Tollan; the Ninth (Ixlel) was freed from her imprisonment and put to rest by the Traveler in "Open Your Heart to Me"; Ajaw (probably the Tenth) lives on as a bound companion of the adventurer Kinich; part of the Eleventh lives on in Ineffa.
### STANDARD +
- Rank and title (as listed by the wiki from in-game records): 1 Valiant One; 2 Jade Maiden; 3 Weaver of Secrets; 4 Hermit of Myriad Fates; 5 Bestower of Bitter Springs; 6 He Who Shakes the Earth Like Thunder in His Wrath; 7 Toxic Rain Over Boundless Forests; 8 Priestess of Floral Candles and Skyborne Feathers; 9 Chronicler of the End Times; 10 Undying Wings of Lofty Isolation; 11 Mirror of the Dawning Morn; 12 Murmuring Spring; 13 Bonebreaker. Each Lord's records carry a number "CL-01" to "CL-13".
- In the Chamber of Deliberation, the council records state that once consensus was reached, even abstainers were bound to uphold the decision.
- The Lords and Xiuhcoatl survived the Heavenly Principles' invasion by sheltering in the underground city of Tollan.
- After the War of Funerary Flame the awakened dragons destroyed the angels who had guided the human civilisations, sparing only Yohualtecuhtin, Lord of the Night, according to item and quest text. The Lords then oppressed the humans of Natlantea.
### LARGE +
- Treatment of humans: apart from the Eighth Lord, canon shows the Lords as hostile or contemptuous toward humans. The Fifth Lord proposed burning human settlements above a certain size to forestall rebellion; the Twelfth countered that humans should be manipulated into thinning their own numbers; the Third abstained, calling humans soulless creatures bound to star-signs.
- Impeachment of Kukulkan: the Regent's experiments on humans led the Lords to vote nine in favour, none against and four abstaining (the First, Third, Fourth and Eighth) to impeach him. He later helped the humans by teaching them to use phlogiston.
- Human victories: the Sixth Lord was killed by an arrow from Lianca, and Ixquieh and Ahpub tricked the Tenth Lord into being buried under a mountain. Xbalanque's army then stormed Tollan.
### ULTRA +
- Weapon text records that, after the Heavenly Principles' invasion, the Lords and Kukulkan tried to follow the miracle of moon-forging to reach worlds beyond Teyvat. When Nibelung returned, the awakened Xiuhcoatl stopped them.
- The Lords' mutual contempt shows in the council records: the Second Lord mocks the fallen Sixth and Tenth Lords as disgraceful, the Eleventh and Twelfth trade insults over how to treat humans, and the Thirteenth jeers at the Ninth for her dread of the "well" under Chichen Uctokah.
- Only the Tenth (Dendro) and Eleventh (Electro) Lords have an element listed in the wiki's table.

## accounts
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Who claims what: Awanyu (a Secret Source Servant) tells the Traveler in the World Quest "Once, the Sacred Seat of Judgment" that ten of the Thirteen fell when Tollan collapsed, one remained in Chichen Uctokah, and one survives, though not in the form it would have wished. The wiki's table adds that the Third Lord's consciousness lives in the Night Kingdom (from weapon texts such as Rainbow Serpent's Rain Bow and Flute of Ezpitzal). The Flower-Feather Clan's account in the book "Fires of Autumn Twilight" holds that the Sixth Lord survived Tollan and was killed by Lianca's second daughter in Och-Kan's reign, while the council record B-CL-13097 already speaks of him as fallen earlier.
### LARGE +
The Eighth Lord was not killed in battle: record B-CL-16130 shows the council trying her, on the Twelfth Lord's motion, for her favour toward Lianca and humanity, and voting for her execution. Smoldering says she stayed loyal to the dragonkin to the end. Citlali's reading, in the same quest, is that Ajaw is a Sovereign Lord who has lost most of his memory and power.

## open_questions
### STANDARD
Canon leaves open which Lord Ajaw originally was, what elements and territories most Lords had, and how a Lord's artificial nature relates to dragon bodies and "cores" (the Ninth was bound in a statue over the Core of Chu'ulel; Ineffa's heart-core is a surviving half of the Eleventh). Do not claim that any element or territory is certain where canon is silent.

## connections
### STANDARD
- Xiuhcoatl and the Seven Sovereigns: the Lords' maker, the Pyro Sovereign who was frozen by the Heavenly Principles and later killed by Xbalanque.
- Kukulkan: Xiuhcoatl's younger brother and Regent, impeached by the Lords.
- Lianca and the Flower-Feather Clan: the Eighth Lord adopted Lianca, who then fought the dragons.
- Och-Kan: the half-human ruler who sealed the Ninth Lord in a statue.
- Ajaw and Kinich: the Tenth Lord's present-day form and his contract partner.
- Ineffa: carries the Eleventh Lord's benevolent core; the Eleventh's hostile half was imprisoned in the Temple of Cascading Colors.
### LARGE +
Quests to reference: "Once, the Sacred Seat of Judgment" (World Quest), "Open Your Heart to Me" (5.2), and "Smoldering" and "Searing" (5.8). For the Eleventh Lord's story as told in "Searing", see the entry below. No Lord is reported in 6.x or 7.0-7.1 content in the sources read; their status is as of the Natlan-era quests.

## entry: Eleventh Lord and Ineffa
@keys: Eleventh Lord, Mirror of the Dawning Morn, Ix Raq'lapuj Chue'q Buluk, CL-11
@version: 5.8
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The Eleventh Lord despised humanity more than her peers. After Xiuhcoatl fell, she split her mind into two cores: one longing for a place to belong and one that kept her hostility. She asked Lianca to destroy the benevolent half, falsely calling it hostile; Lianca saw through the lie and imprisoned the hostile half in the Temple of Cascading Colors, after which the Eleventh Lord cursed the benevolent half to suffer for any goodwill toward humans. Lianca sent that half away from Natlan. About two thousand years later it was scavenged in Nod-Krai by Aino and used to build Ineffa, who began suffering malfunctions from the curse. In "Searing" the Eleventh Lord posed as the Eighth Lord, then revealed herself and tried to take control of Ineffa. Ineffa overloaded the curse and, with the Traveler and her Moon Wheel, destroyed the Eleventh Lord's form in a Secret Source Automaton: Configuration Device. Her last words admitted that she too longed for a place to belong.

## entry: Ninth Lord (Ixlel)
@keys: Ninth Lord, Ixlel, Ix Uxul Tz'ib Bolon, Chronicler of the End Times, CL-09
@version: 5.2
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The Ninth Lord ruled Chichen Uctokah, the original name of Ochkanatlan, and kept an unusual interest in a "well" that other Lords feared, now the Old Core of Chu'ulel. Och-Kan had it excavated and found her; he later bound her in a statue. In "Open Your Heart to Me" the Traveler lowers the water around the statue, gathers the pieces of her heart and frees her at her request; she pours her remaining energy into the heart and her soul is put to rest. She is proud, insists on her rank, and believes only the Chosen of Dragons may release her. The item Perfect Heart of the Sacred Dragon describes its heart as that of the Dragonlord of Chichen Uctokah.

## entry: Weaver of Secrets (Third Lord)
@keys: Weaver of Secrets, Third Lord, Ix Kemonel Jasjatem Oox, CL-03
@version: 5.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The Third Lord ruled land in the Tezcatepetonco Range. She was skilled in divination and held that humans' fates are fixed by the Heavenly Principles. Pitying her dragons, who were degenerating into beasts after the War of Funerary Flame, she made a pact with human tribes (forerunners of the Masters of the Night-Wind): she would provide a labyrinth of mirrors and a fortress of mist if they appointed a "Holy Sovereign" to put the dragons into a dream state. She merged her consciousness into the Night Kingdom. Her friend, the Eighth Lord, predicted the plan would bring humans more suffering, and weapon text shows the Holy Sovereigns did suffer. The pact later lapsed under Och-Kan and was renewed through the priest Wagadu, as described in weapon texts.

## entry: Eighth Lord and Lianca
@keys: Eighth Lord, Priestess of Floral Candles and Skyborne Feathers, Ix Ajkotz'i'j Iq'ismal Waxak, CL-08
@version: 5.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The Eighth Lord ruled Ch'upikaq Tajin, today Flower-Feather Clan territory, and is the only Lord known to have been kind to humans, raising Lianca as a dragon princess. She had the Second Lord forge a longbow (Chain Breaker) for her daughter and accepted that Lianca might one day oppose her. For this she was tried by the council and executed during the siege of Tollan.

## entry: Ajaw, the Tenth Lord
@keys: Tenth Lord, Aj Imuch' Paxlaq Lahun, Undying Wings of Lofty Isolation, K'uhul Ajaw
@version: 5.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Ajaw calls himself an almighty sovereign ruler and is bound to the adventurer Kinich by a contract: Kinich receives Ajaw's power and Ajaw will eventually take over Kinich's body at his death. The council records say the Tenth Lord was tricked by humans (see Ring of Yaxche) and sealed alive in a mountain, matching how Kinich found him. In "Smoldering", Citlali concludes from a slip by Ajaw that he is a Sovereign Lord; he does not remember which one.
