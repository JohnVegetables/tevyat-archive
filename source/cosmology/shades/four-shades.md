## summary
@spoiler: major
### LITE
The Four Shades, or Four Great Rulers, are four gods the Heavenly Principles created as "shades" of itself during its war against the Seven Sovereigns. Each governs one law of the world: Naberius (Life), Ronova (Death), Istaroth (Time) and Asmoday (Space). They are older and stronger than The Seven and act from Celestia as the Heavenly Principles' emissaries. As of 7.1, Naberius survives only fused with the alchemist Rhinedottir, Ronova has lost her Authority and died, Istaroth is active but elusive, and Asmoday has defected.
### STANDARD +
Few mortals know they exist. They dislike being named by any living being, prefer to stay in the shadows, and usually act through proxies, curses and lent power rather than open rule.

## established
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
- Origin: Enkanomiya's chronicle Before Sun and Moon says that when the Primordial One fought the dragon-lords of the old world, it created shining shades of itself, "and the number of these shades was four". Forty years later the Sovereigns were vanquished.
- Role and rank: they govern Teyvat's fundamental laws for the Heavenly Principles. Mavuika calls Ronova a god "whose existence predates any Archon", a kind of emissary of the Heavenly Principles. The Jester calls the Shades "beings of an altogether higher order".
- Command: they receive directives from Celestia and do not appear able to act wholly on their own initiative, so they look for ways to act "within the rules".
### LARGE +
- Appearance: the Shades share a family likeness, each a tall woman with pale skin and long white hair; the three whose faces are shown have golden eyes whose pupils suit their domains (Istaroth's resemble a clock face without hands, Ronova's a seven-petalled flower, Asmoday's a target). Naberius's original face has never been clearly shown.
- Ronova dismisses the Tsaritsa's powers as "paltry"; Istaroth is called a "higher power" than Makoto. Paimon describes the Shades as having "the power to enforce the rules of the world itself".
- Lent and stolen power: a Shade can grant part of her Authority to others, and it can also be stolen or usurped without her consent (sub-entry "Borrowed and stolen Authority").
- Joint power: Ronova says that if Asmoday returned, the Four Shades could "jointly activate" their Authority.
### ULTRA +
- Creation: Before Sun and Moon says the Primordial One and one of its shades (Naberius) made the birds, beasts, fish and plants, and finally humans, in the age after the Sovereigns' defeat.
- Ronova told mortals they do not even have the right to speak with the Shades or see their true forms. Rhinedottir, though she acts as a Shade, preferred to deal with Albedo and Durin through her servant Sebas.

## members
@spoiler: major
### LITE
Naberius, Ruler of Life (now inside Rhinedottir); Ronova, Ruler of Death and Arbiter of the Heavenly Principles (dead as of 7.1); Istaroth, Ruler of Time; Asmoday, Ruler of Space and Sustainer of the Heavenly Principles (defected).
### STANDARD +
Each has her own entry. Naberius, whose compassion was revered from Hyperborea to Sal Vindagnyr, had her heart devoured by Rhinedottir after the Cataclysm; she lost her agency but still objects from within their shared mind. Ronova could define or deny any death, as when she cursed full-blooded Khaenri'ahns with immortality. Istaroth is bored and quietly bends history. Asmoday stopped the twin travelers leaving Teyvat 500 years ago and is now seen with the Traveler's sibling.
### LARGE +
Other names: Naberius is Mother of Life and, to the Frostmoon Scions, Maadteraahka. Ronova is Mother of the Underworld, Grim Reaper and, to the Frostmoon Scions, Tuonetar. Istaroth was Kairos and Tokoyo Ookami in Enkanomiya and is said to remember everything while letting everything be forgotten. Asmoday kept a private domain, the Temple of Space.

## present_day
@version: 7.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
As of 7.1 the Heavenly Principles is still dormant. The Shades meet in Celestia, but Asmoday has missed every meeting since the Cataclysm. In Snezhnaya, Ronova broke the rules with a curse of death; Celestia reclaimed her Authority, Pierro mortally wounded her, and she died.
### LARGE +
- Natlan (Natlan Archon Quest): Mavuika spent the power Xbalanque had borrowed from Ronova to defeat the Abyss. Ronova came to take Mavuika's life as payment, but Capitano offered his own, and Ronova chose to keep her curse of immortality on him.
- Sumeru (Truth Amongst the Pages of Purana, 6.6): when Nahida invoked her right as Irminsul's keeper to burn the corrupted tree with the Pyro Gnosis, Ronova, Istaroth and Naberius appeared, questioned her and allowed it. Naberius said Nahida had herself become "a great tree".
- Snezhnaya (7.0-7.1): Ronova cursed people working toward technology that threatened the Heavenly Principles, starting with Project Mistilteinn's researchers. Dying, she placed the Eitr on the Celestial Nail above the Cryo Dragon's corpse; the nail began to crumble as she disintegrated, calling it her "parting gift", and the other Celestial Nails across Teyvat reacted.
### ULTRA +
Dainsleif reflected that 500 years ago the Khaenri'ahns saw Ronova as "an untouchable god", and that her defeat begins a new era that will not be peaceful. Earlier in the same quest the Tsaritsa told Rhinedottir and Naberius that she had seen Asmoday in Snezhnaya with a hooded figure, and Ronova told the Traveler she could finish them once Asmoday returned or the Heavenly Principles awoke.

## rules_and_monitoring
@version: 7.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The Shades are watched by a "monitoring cycle" the Heavenly Principles set up to catch violations; breaking the rules can cost a Shade her Authority.
### LARGE +
Ronova's curse of death killed people and needed Rhinedottir's Authority of Life to revive them before a cycle ended. When the Tsaritsa delayed the revival, Celestia detected it and reclaimed Ronova's Authority of Death. A directive then "echoed" in Rhinedottir's mind ordering her to put Ronova's mistake right. Rhinedottir noted Celestia was watching her and Naberius closely and that she did not want to lose the Authority of Life too. Even without her Authority, Ronova kept power befitting a Shade until Pierro wounded her.
### ULTRA +
Ronova had been censured long before: her help in repairing Natlan was judged an overreach, after which she "succumbed to self-pity" and grew indifferent to the land.

## authorities
@version: 7.1
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
In Delusion (Chapter VII) (7.1), The Knave explains that the Authorities of Life, Death, Time and Space once belonged to the Three Moons, the Iridescent Moon holding the largest share of Death. After the moons' destruction in the War of Funerary Flame, the Heavenly Principles reclaimed them and redistributed them among the Four Shades "under a new system of rules". The Unified Civilization had already worshipped the Shades as masters of these domains.
### ULTRA +
The Knave adds that the broken Crimson Moon merged with the Abyss, which simulates its old Authority, so Death still exists in another form in the Crimson Moon and its bloodline. In Where Our Space and Time Intersect (6.3), Dottore names the Four Great Rulers of Life, Death, Time and Space, then claims the Trilune's power is older than all four.

## history
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
In the ancient world the Shades were worshipped openly. Arcadia built the Thousand Winds Temple for Istaroth and the Temple of Space for Asmoday. After the War of Funerary Flame most of humanity forgot them.
- Natlan: Ronova guided the Lord of the Night in creating the Night Kingdom to mend Natlan's ley lines. Later Xbalanque, the first Pyro Archon, borrowed her power to create Natlan's rules; they agreed she would deny involvement if the Heavenly Principles found out.
- Mondstadt: about 2,600 years ago Istaroth met the wind spirit who became Barbatos and entrusted him with fragments of her power.
- The Cataclysm: Ronova cursed the full-blooded Khaenri'ahns with immortality without separating guilty from innocent. Asmoday blocked and sealed the twin travelers.
### ULTRA +
- Hyperborea: newborns were blessed with a pinch of sea salt offered to Naberius; autumn animal sacrifices in boat-shaped vessels were burned for Ronova; at the Winter Solstice a cloth recording the year's events was burned for Istaroth.
- Enkanomiya: when the realm sank, only Istaroth answered its prayers, giving Aberaku the knowledge to build the Dainichi Mikoshi.
- Fontaine: after the first Hydro Dragon was deposed, Naberius created Egeria as the Primordial Sea's new heart.
- Mondstadt: when Decarabian fell, the Nameless Bard usurped Istaroth's power, playing strings that belonged to the "Master of Time", to turn the dying god's tempest into song.
- The Temple of Space: Asmoday turned the fallen temple into a store for things she wished to preserve and left Domain Keepers such as Melanta and Nihilita to guard it. In her absence the other Shades added exhibits; Ronova made the Maranasati Caskets, and together they commissioned the recreated Desert Pavilion.
- The Cataclysm: Istaroth let Direidyth meet the Traveler 500 years in the future, which helped persuade the sibling to leave Khaenri'ah.

## the_gods_limits
@version: 5.7
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
The official teaser "The Gods' Limits" (2025-07-09) was the first time all four Shades were shown. Ronova, Istaroth and Rhinedottir (attending for Naberius) meet in Celestia. Ronova says no Shade has missed a meeting since the Heavenly Principles made them, until Asmoday. Istaroth calls the meetings a formality and suggests Asmoday "switched sides"; Ronova finds that unthinkable unless Asmoday loves a new master more than herself.
### ULTRA +
Before the meeting the three inspect a ruined space made by Asmoday. Istaroth then warns Ronova that Pierro is watching from Nod-Krai; Ronova recalls his eyes from the moment she cursed the Khaenri'ahns. When Rhinedottir says the Tsaritsa would be wise to use the Heavenly Principles' sleep, Naberius forbids the thought. Ronova concludes that, lacking a direct order, they should intervene "to the extent permitted by the rules".

## accounts
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
- Lord of the Night: the Shades do not like being named by any living being, even The Seven, and prefer to remain in the shadows as Shades. He calls the one he knows only "the Ruler of Death".
- Ronova to her peers: "We are Shades, Shadows of the Heavenly Principles."
- Istaroth explains her interventions as a way to pass the time.
### ULTRA +
- Deep Gallery's Distant Pact (artifact text): after the Heavenly Principles' victory, a returning voyager found the bones of the earth "bound beneath fourfold shackles" and the sky's light split into seven hues.
- The wiki states that the other Shades acknowledged Rhinedottir's takeover of Naberius and did not act against her; in canon she attends their Celestia meeting in Naberius's place.

## open_questions
### STANDARD
Canon leaves open who now holds the Authority of Death, what Asmoday intends with the Traveler's sibling, and whether Istaroth truly serves the Heavenly Principles.
### LARGE +
Also unexplained: how Naberius's heart ended up in a domain after the Cataclysm, and how the Shades will act when the Heavenly Principles wakes.

## connections
### LARGE
Heavenly Principles (creator and master); Celestia (their seat and source of directives); The Seven (younger gods who sometimes borrow their power); the Three Moons (former holders of the four Authorities); the Abyss and the Crimson Moon (a rival form of Death); Paimon (Ronova tried to seize her as a creation of Celestia).
### ULTRA +
The wiki notes the four Authorities match Flower of Life, Plume of Death, Sands of Eon (Chinese "Sands of Time") and Goblet of Eonothem (Chinese "Cup of Space"); in Truth Amongst the Pages of Purana the three present Shades appear in the matching artifact shapes.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay interpretation (this project's recommendation, not canon text): treat the Shades as remote, rule-bound powers. Ordinary people do not know their names; Archons, Fatui leaders and Hexenzirkel mages might. A Shade appearing is a world-altering event. As of 7.1, do not portray Ronova alive, Naberius independent of Rhinedottir, or Asmoday back in Celestia.
### LARGE +
Keep them distinct: Istaroth is idle, playful and evasive; Ronova was severe, contemptuous of mortals and bound by her own rules; Naberius is a disapproving inner voice; Asmoday is cold and absent.

## entry: Rulers of Life, Death, Time and Space
@keys: Ruler of Life, Ruler of Death, Ruler of Time, Ruler of Space, Great Rulers
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The four titles of the Shades. Ruler of Life: Naberius, whose power and duties passed to Rhinedottir when Rhinedottir devoured her heart. Ruler of Death: Ronova, whose Authority Celestia reclaimed in 7.1 before she died. Ruler of Time: Istaroth. Ruler of Space: Asmoday, who has abandoned her post. The Knave says these four Authorities once belonged to the Three Moons.

## entry: Monitoring cycle
@keys: monitoring cycle, monitoring cycles, monitoring system
@keys2: Celestia, Heavenly Principles, Ronova, Rhinedottir, Shades
@version: 7.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
A system the Heavenly Principles set up to check that the Shades obey the rules. Ronova and Rhinedottir agreed on a "window of resurrection" so that Ronova's victims would be revived before a cycle concluded. The Tsaritsa used her Cryo mastery and six Gnoses to slow time around Rhinedottir in Zapolyarny Palace, so the window passed, Celestia detected the aberration and reclaimed Ronova's Authority of Death.

## entry: Borrowed and stolen Authority
@keys: Authority of Life, Authority of Death, Authority of Time, Authority of Space
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
Shades can lend fragments of their Authority, and it can be stolen. Istaroth entrusted fragments of her power to Barbatos. The Nameless Bard usurped Istaroth's power to save Mondstadt and died of it. Xbalanque borrowed Ronova's power to create Natlan's rules, at the price that its users would die. Koitar stole part of Naberius's Authority of Life for Hyperborea's artisans to make the Snowland Fae; it passed down to the Vila and to Vesna, until Rhinedottir took it back in 7.1.

## entry: Asmoday's absence from Celestia
@keys: Asmoday's new master, Sustainer of Heavenly Principles, Sustainer of the Heavenly Principles
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
Since the Cataclysm Asmoday has missed every Shade meeting in Celestia, and her Temple of Space has been left to its keepers. Istaroth guessed she had switched sides; Ronova wanted her found. The Tsaritsa later saw her in Snezhnaya with a hooded figure, and Asmoday opened portals to escape. Her portals were also seen closing near the Traveler's meeting with their sibling in Natlan.
