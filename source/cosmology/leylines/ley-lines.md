## summary
### LITE
The Ley Lines are a hidden network that links all of Teyvat. Elemental energy flows through them, and they carry the memories of everything that happens in the world, from the surface to the deepest depths. They were tied to Irminsul, the world tree, which stored what they gathered. Disturbed Ley Lines can make people see memories that are not their own.
### STANDARD +
Ley Lines existed in the age of the dragons, but the Heavenly Principles refashioned them, along with Irminsul, into a defence for the world against the Abyss and an anchor for fate. Natlan is the great exception: its own Ley Lines were destroyed in an ancient war, and the Night Kingdom serves in their place.
### LARGE +
Since the end of the Sumeru crisis in 6.6, Irminsul has been burned away, and Aaru now stores the information carried by the Ley Lines.

## established
### LITE
- Elemental energy flows through the Ley Lines, and loading-screen lore says they "remember all things" that happen in the world.
- Tighnari compares them to the roots of Irminsul. They reach up to the surface from a vast cavern deep underground, and they keep absorbing the world's memories and funnelling them into the tree.
- Where the flow of energy is blocked, deposits form at the surface and Ley Line Blossoms bloom. All Domains are said to be built on Ley Lines, around silver-white Petrified Trees that are parts of the Irminsul root system.
### STANDARD +
- Original Resin item text says the roots of all the Irminsul trees and blossoms intertwine at the deepest, most hidden place in the earth, and that the pattern they make defines the Ley Lines. Condensed Resin text says these trees are connected to Ley Lines that have become blocked over time, and that resin energy can purify the blockage.
- Disturbances carry memories. In the Interlude act Caribert (3.5), the Abyss Order tampered with the Ley Lines near Avidya Forest, and the Traveler fell unconscious and saw their sibling's memories from centuries before. Dainsleif later observed that Abyss Order activity is usually followed by Ley Line trouble.
- The Ley Lines are linked to erosion. The Raiden Shogun says that as long as they flow, the relentless erosion of time will never stop (Farewell to the Past, 2.5).
### LARGE +
- Souls and the dead. In Nod-Krai (To the Lighthouse, 6.0), Flins describes ghosts as the echoes of living souls whose thoughts and memories failed to enter the Ley Lines at the moment of death and so linger on the surface. In time they fade and return to the Ley Lines. Ei likewise calls ghosts "disembodied memories held in the Ley Lines."
- Not everyone is recorded. The Traveler is not recorded in the Ley Lines, and in 7.0 Ronova says that a Descender's soul cannot be brought into them.
- Damage and healing. In Song of the Welkin Moon Act IX (6.6), Cyno explains that overloading and the Withering both harm the Ley Lines' vitality and can do irreversible damage, but that the Ley Lines heal well once blockages or pollution are dealt with. His teacher Cyrus says Ley Line Blossoms bloom from openings in the Ley Lines and signal some anomaly; dissecting anomalous blossoms let scholars judge the state of Irminsul.
### ULTRA +
- Ley Lines can be imitated and studied. Sumeru's Ley Line Disruptors (Competition on the Sands, 3.6) imitate Ley Line disruption and leave people dizzy and lost in circles. Cyno says that Spantamad, the Darshan he came from, knows a great deal about Ley Lines. Layla notes that their hidden underground parts can be traced through elemental energy, sound and other signs.
- Khaenri'ah's Azosite fuel was made by extracting pure elemental energy from Ley Lines and processing it into energy blocks (per in-world notes summarised by the wiki).
- Abyss Mages drop withered "Ley Line" branches and sprouts. Their item text describes branches from deep within the earth, part of a great tree whose roots once reached every corner of the world, that still pulse with energy.

## origins
@version: 6.6
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Before the Heavenly Principles arrived, the Ley Lines and Irminsul already existed. According to Apep (6.6), the Ley Lines then carried an abundant, primitive form of elemental energy: the power of the Light Realm. The Heavenly Principles usurped and refashioned Irminsul, made it serve humanity and used it to anchor the fate of the world. The Light Realm's power was diluted into the elemental energy of the human world.
### LARGE +
The Lord of the Night (5.3) says the Heavenly Principles modified the Ley Lines to help counter the Abyss, which is why their stability mattered above all. Kukulkan, the ancient Natlan ruler, once hoped to learn how "the one seated upon heaven's throne" had turned the Ley Lines into "a girdle that defends the planet" (A Finale Emberforged, 5.5). According to the Lord of the Night, the ancient dragons once tried to strengthen the Ley Lines with the Source Mechanism, a device under Natlan, but failed because they could not navigate the Ley Lines' intricate structure; that structure has since completely changed.

## accounts
### STANDARD
- Mavuika (5.3): the forces of the Abyss always seek to corrode Teyvat; the Ley Lines were created by the Heavenly Principles, and only their protection lets Teyvat nurture life.
- Apep (6.6) and Kukulkan (5.5): the Heavenly Principles took an existing network and converted it, rather than making it from nothing.
### LARGE +
- Caribert (4.7): Teyvat's Ley Line system is deeply entrenched in the planet, so new Ley Lines can neither replace nor extend the existing ones.
- Layla (3.6) wonders, without evidence, whether the Ley Lines follow fixed courses like the orbits of celestial bodies and are reflections of the stars upon the earth.

## natlan_and_the_night_kingdom
@version: 5.3
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Natlan lacks stable Ley Lines of the ordinary kind. Mavuika (5.1) says its Ley Lines took the worst damage in the ancient war between the Descender and the dragons, which let the Abyss in. Guided by Ronova, the angel Yohualtecuhtin, the Lord of the Night, rebuilt a similar network from the fragments: the Night Kingdom, which functions as Natlan's Ley Lines.
### LARGE +
Because of the rules needed for the Ode of Resurrection, Natlan's network could not be reconnected to the other nations' Ley Lines, so memories made in Natlan and elsewhere do not mix. Capitano at first sought to "reconstruct" the Ley Lines through the Source Mechanism (5.1), which would have cost the Lord of the Night her existence and wiped the memories in the old network. In 5.3 the Ley Lines were instead restored after the war against the Abyss: Capitano died in Mavuika's place and merged with the Lord of the Night to change the rules, letting the Khaenri'ahn souls he carried enter. The Night Kingdom has since been open to the souls of outlanders who die in Natlan.
### ULTRA +
In 6.6, Mavuika notes that her records are kept in the Night Kingdom rather than the Ley Lines. While the Night Kingdom stays disconnected from the Ley Line cycle, Dottore cannot track her movements through it (own entry: Night Kingdom).

## sumeru_crisis_6_6
@version: 6.6
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
In Song of the Welkin Moon Acts IX and X (6.6), Dottore's corruption of Irminsul spread a red mist of corrupted Ley Lines over central Sumeru. Apep sensed the disorder from afar, and strange, never-before-seen Ley Line Blossoms appeared. Dottore had entered Irminsul through death; Nahida says her only possible safeguard would have been to block every soul's free access to the Ley Lines. Pantalone placed Dottore's last backup into the Ley Lines. To stop him, Nahida and her allies moved the world's information out of Irminsul and redirected it to Aaru. Nahida then burned Irminsul with the Pyro Gnosis, with the permission of the Shades Ronova, Istaroth and Naberius. The burning took seven days and nights. The reconfigured Aaru now collects and stores the Ley Lines' information.

## connections
### LITE
Irminsul (the memory store), the Heavenly Principles (who refashioned the network), the Abyss (which it protects against), Domains and Ley Line Blossoms (surface outlets) and Ley Line Disorders (unstable energy in Domains).
### STANDARD +
Erosion and fate both flow through this network. Natlan's Night Kingdom is a stand-in network, and Aaru is the repository since 6.6.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not literal lore): Ley Line Outcrops are open-world challenges. Defeating the enemies reveals a Ley Line Blossom (a Blossom of Revelation for character EXP materials, or of Wealth for Mora), claimed with Original Resin. Domains and their Petrified Trees and Ley Line Disorder effects are tied to the Ley Lines in the same way.
### LARGE +
Loading screens describe a "recognition" by the Ley Lines that boosts some characters in Ley Line Disturbance domains, and say that disturbed Ley Lines can carry those who touch them to hidden Domains where foes from their memories appear.

## gameplay_event
@availability: limited
### ULTRA
Gameplay: Ley Line Overflow is a recurring limited-time event. During it, Ley Line Outcrops give double rewards up to three times a day when claimed with Original Resin.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay interpretation (this project's recommendation, not canon text): treat the Ley Lines as an unseen, underground current. Most people only know them through Domains, blossoms, scholars' talk or strange memories and ghosts. A disturbance can plausibly cause dizziness, disorientation, odd elemental behaviour or flashes of another person's memory.
### LARGE +
Experts such as Spantamad scholars, Sumeru forest rangers, Natlan shamans like Citlali, Dainsleif or the Lord of the Night can sense or reason about Ley Lines. Ordinary people cannot read them at will. Nobody can simply create new Ley Lines from nothing: even the Lord of the Night only reassembled intact fragments, and the Loom of Fate is a unique Khaenri'ahn device.
### ULTRA +
Keep the time frame straight. Before 6.6, memories feed Irminsul; from 6.6, they feed Aaru. Natlan's dead go to the Night Kingdom, not the general network. Do not give the Traveler a record in the Ley Lines.

## entry: Loom of Fate
@keys: Loom of Fate
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The Loom of Fate is a Khaenri'ahn device made by Caribert and the Abyss Order that can weave Ley Lines. In its incomplete form it could only create and implant memories. According to Caribert (Bedtime Story, 4.7), once complete it gains the power to weave real Ley Lines and loses its lesser power over memories. He also said new Ley Lines can neither replace nor extend Teyvat's existing ones.
### LARGE +
In A Space and Time for You (5.7), the Iniquitous Baptist Haden claimed that the Traveler's sibling had used the Loom to re-weave Khaenri'ah's Ley Lines; he later admitted he was bluffing about Khaenri'ah's revival being complete, since the Order still needed memories of Khaenri'ah. The sibling explained that the Abyss Order's attack on Natlan was a feint to reach the Lord of the Night. She gave them space to weave new Ley Lines, which were superimposed on Natlan; according to Citlali, she agreed once assured that Natlan would not be affected, and set another condition she did not reveal. The result is the "Atlas of the New World", holding New Khaenri'ah's Ley Lines, which still needs a place to be laid down; the sibling sees this as necessary to face the Heavenly Principles as equals.

## entry: Ley Line Blossoms and Petrified Trees
@keys: Ley Line Blossom, Ley Line Blossoms, Ley Line Outcrop, Petrified Tree, Petrified Trees
### STANDARD
Ley Line Blossoms grow from deposits where the flow of elemental energy is impeded, at the ends of the Ley Lines. They are said to take root in people's wills: a "Revelation" blossom answers a desire for battle, a "Wealth" blossom a desire for money. Petrified Trees, silver-white extensions of Irminsul, stand at the heart of Domains and channel the Ley Lines; the surge of energy through them causes Ley Line Disorders. Both can be revitalised with resin.
### LARGE +
Scholars treat blossoms as diagnostic. In 6.6, Cyrus taught that they bloom from openings in the Ley Lines and signal an anomaly, and anomalous blossoms helped Sumeru judge Irminsul's condition during Dottore's corruption.

## entry: Ley Lines and the dead
@keys: ghost, ghosts, soul, souls
@keys2: Ley Line, Ley Lines
@spoiler: minor
### LARGE
Several sources tie the dead to the Ley Lines. Flins (Nod-Krai, 6.0) says ghosts are fragments of souls that failed to enter the Ley Lines at death and will slowly fade back into them. Ei calls ghosts memories held in the Ley Lines. In 6.6 Nahida says the only defence against Dottore's entry through death would have been closing the Ley Lines to all souls. In Wraith's Nocturne (7.0), Ronova explains that the angels' renovation of the Korolevskiy Theater's Ley Lines hampers her soul-ferrying and allows only two deaths a day there, and that a Descender's soul cannot be brought into the Ley Lines. Natlan's dead go to the Night Kingdom instead.
