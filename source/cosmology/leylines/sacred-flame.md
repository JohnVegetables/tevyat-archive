## summary
### LITE
The Sacred Flame is the fire that never stops burning above the arena of the Stadium of the Sacred Flame, Natlan's central landmark. It is the conduit to the Night Kingdom, the realm of the dead and of the Wayob, and the means of speaking with the Wayob. Mavuika calls it the foundation of Natlan's resistance against the Abyss. Battles between Natlanese warriors produce Contending Fire, which fuels it. The Pyro Archon steps into it during the Ode of Resurrection to bring fallen heroes back.
### STANDARD +
Its main outlet, the column of fire above the arena, can be seen from far outside the Stadium. A second outlet is the large fire pit in the Speaker's Chamber, where the tribal chiefs meet under the Archon's eye. Mavuika put her life into the flame at the end of the Cataclysm 500 years ago and returned from it in the present day.

## established
### LITE
- The flame is a continuous fire at the Stadium of the Sacred Flame and a conduit to the Night Kingdom and the Wayob.
- Contending Fire, produced only in combat between people of Natlan, keeps it burning. Cival explains that Natlan is safe from the Abyss only while the flame burns eternal.
- The Pyro Archon performs the Ode of Resurrection through it.
### STANDARD +
- When a new Pyro Archon ascends the divine throne, their "inner flame" awakens, and the Sacred Flame grants them broad knowledge and memory of Natlan. Mavuika says this is how she knows the history of Natlan's rules (Echoes of Life, 5.0). In a scene from Mavuika's past shown in A Fuel Named "Fate" (5.1), her friend Wanjiru repeats the idea and worries that inheriting the flame's knowledge can change a person's personality.
- Objects carried into battle by Natlanese heroes slowly become imbued with Contending Fire. Burning them releases that fire as fuel, though crudely and all at once.
- Mavuika says that no one but her could sustain the flame, and that it "must never go out": it frightens the Abyss and is "the pillar of Natlan's stability".
### LARGE +
- Smaller flames split from the Sacred Flame are carried to braziers for the Turnfire Night ritual of the Scions of the Canopy. A tutorial describes them as holding a blessed power that dispels darkness and scatters the Abyss's power.
- Ochkanatlan-era bulletins call the flame K'ahk' and give the tyrant Och-Kan the title "Guardian of the Sacred Flame" (Yajaw K'ahk').

## ode
### LITE
In the Ode of Resurrection, an officiator recites a eulogy, the Pyro Archon walks into the flame to seek the fallen warrior and their Ancient Name in the Night Kingdom, and the crowd bows and sings until the Archon walks back out with the revived warrior.
### STANDARD +
Only Ancient Name bearers who fell in the Night Warden Wars qualify, and only if their unit "achieved victory", meaning at least one member came back victorious. The rite reverses only the fatal wound; injuries built up earlier in the battle and existing illnesses remain. Mavuika's character stories call the return a "Rite of Rekindling", treated like a victory feast. As a child she watched her father come back through the flame alive but so badly wounded he could not stand.
### LARGE +
The power behind the Ode traces back to Ronova, the Shade of Death, who let the first Pyro Archon, Xbalanque, borrow her power on condition that he keep the bargain secret. See the Ode of Resurrection's own entry for the full rite.

## history
@spoiler: major
@version: 5.0
### LITE
Mavuika was Pyro Archon during the Cataclysm. After the war against the Abyss she placed her remaining life inside the Sacred Flame so that she could reawaken 500 years later and oversee her plan to drive the Abyss out of Natlan. She woke some years before the Traveler arrived and won the Pilgrimage to become Archon again.
### STANDARD +
In the Natlan Archon Quest (5.0), the Ode for Kachina failed: Mavuika came out of the flame alone, unable to find her or her Ancient Name, because the Abyss had invaded the Night Kingdom and blocked communication between the flame and the Wayob. Mavuika suspended the Pilgrimage, even though fewer battles meant less Contending Fire. Since Contending Fire alone was "far from sufficient", she then transferred her own power into the flame to keep it alight, giving up, among other things, the ability to turn her hair to flame. To reach Kachina and the Traveler in the Night Kingdom, she burned the keepsakes of past heroes stored in the Speaker's Chamber, using their Contending Fire to open the boundary between the worlds.
### LARGE +
During the Abyss's full invasion (Archon Quest Act III, 5.1), the manifestation of the Abyssal being Gosoythoth put the flame out. Mavuika used the might of the Pyro Gnosis to reignite it and hold the incursion back, and refugees fell back to the Stadium because the flame repels the Abyss. At the flame the Traveler witnessed a scene from the distant past in which Ronova and Xbalanque struck their bargain. Once the six heroes were gathered, Mavuika borrowed the Lord of the Night's power to extend the Ode's protection to every Natlanese warrior, with or without an Ancient Name.
### ULTRA +
In the final battle (Act V, 5.3), the Sacred Flame connected Mavuika and the Traveler in the depths of the Night Kingdom with the people in the Stadium. When it began to gutter, the crowd, led by Kachina, sang the Ode on their own initiative. The flame roared back to life, and the Traveler's Ancient Name awakened. Mavuika and the Traveler then put their hands into a flame in the Night Kingdom and drew out a weapon able to resist the Abyss, the greatsword later known as A Thousand Blazing Suns. With Gosoythoth destroyed, the two burst out of the Stadium's flame together. After the war the Ode was no longer needed, and the Pilgrimage was refounded as a purely athletic contest.

## accounts
### LARGE
- Mavuika (Echoes of Life): the first Pyro Archon, a mortal with no special power, used the divine throne to borrow power from the heavens (Ronova) and set "the rules" of Natlan, a framework through which ordinary people can become Archon. The flame's gift of memory is part of that framework.
- A material description: the first human to ascend "arose from the throne to enter the Sacred Flame" while the world waited for a successor; Och-Kan then seized power and founded Ochkanatlan.
- A Thousand Blazing Suns (weapon text): Xbalanque reached for "the primal flame" in the ineffable city. Under the name "Haborym", the divine name Pyro Archons bear, "life had become the sacred flame unquenchable" and the rules became an ever-blazing divine throne. The text also credits an artisan and the Scarlet-Eyed Youth, the second Pyro Archon, with ensuring that the Sacred Flame would endure. Its closing line is "So long as the flame does not die, there shall still be 'hope.'"
- Mavuika's own imagery (Character Story 5): in meditation she pictured her plan as a long road toward a high tower with the Sacred Flame burning at its summit.

## open_questions
### LARGE
- How the flame came into being. The poetic weapon text ties it to Xbalanque and the name Haborym, but no literal account exists.
- What sustains it now that the Abyss war is over, and whether Contending Fire is still collected for it.
- How the knowledge passed to new Archons is stored, and whether it really changes the inheritor (Wanjiru's worry in Mavuika's memories, not a confirmed fact).
- Whether the flame outlet in the Night Kingdom is a true exit as well as an entrance.

## connections
### LITE
The Night Kingdom, which stands in for Ley Lines in Natlan, lies on the other side of the flame. The flame is not itself a Ley Line.
### LARGE +
- Pilgrimage of the Return of the Sacred Flame: the tournament that produced Contending Fire, chose heroes for the Night Warden Wars and chose the Archon.
- Ode of Resurrection and Ancient Names: the rite performed through the flame, and the names it restores.
- The Pyro Archon (Mavuika as of 7.1) is responsible for protecting and fuelling the flame. The Pyro Gnosis rekindled it in 5.1.
- Ronova and the Lord of the Night are the powers behind Natlan's rules of resurrection. Capitano's later sacrifice, which merged his life force with the Lord of the Night, rewrote the rules of the Ley Lines; canon frames that through the Night Kingdom rather than the flame.

## gameplay
### LARGE
Gameplay (not literal lore): during the 5.1 Archon Quest defence, Pyro DMG is increased within the light of the Sacred Flame. Lit braziers carrying split-off Sacred Flame grant protection that lets the player light other braziers during Turnfire Night content. In the final 5.3 boss fight, "the Sacred Flame will ignite your will to persevere", fully restoring Mavuika and the Traveler instead of letting them fall. The Pyro Traveler's sixth constellation is named "The Sacred Flame Imperishable".

## roleplay
### LARGE
Roleplay interpretation (this project's recommendation, not canon text): treat the flame as Natlan's civic and spiritual heart. Crowds face it during the Ode, chiefs negotiate beside its fire pit, and warriors speak of feeding it through honourable combat. Outlanders could not fight in the old Pilgrimage because their battles produce no Contending Fire.
Only the Pyro Archon walks into the flame in canon, apart from the Traveler at Mavuika's side. Do not let ordinary characters step through it to the Night Kingdom. Set after 5.3, the war is won and the Ode is no longer performed; a scene showing a resurrection rite as current should be set in the past.

## entry: Contending Fire
@keys: Contending Fire
@version: 5.0
### STANDARD
Contending Fire is the energy produced when people of Natlan fight one another, and the fuel of the Sacred Flame. The Pilgrimage of the Return of the Sacred Flame existed largely to generate it, which is why outlanders were barred: their participation would hinder its production. Items carried in battle by heroes absorb some of it over time. Mavuika burned such keepsakes in 5.0 to force open the way into the Night Kingdom, and their power was spent in an instant.

## entry: Mavuika's sleep in the Sacred Flame
@keys: Mavuika
@keys2: Sacred Flame, five hundred years, 500 years, reawaken
@spoiler: major
@version: 5.0
### STANDARD
After the Cataclysm, Natlan was ruined and its tribes broken. The Archon and the tribal heroes agreed on a 500-year plan to rebuild the tribes until each Wayob could choose a new hero. Because Natlan had no gods able to extend a human life, Mavuika placed her life in the Sacred Flame, dying before her time so that she could wake again when the plan matured. She revealed this in Past and Future (5.0).

## entry: Guardian of the Sacred Flame (K'ahk')
@keys: K'ahk', Yajaw K'ahk', Guardian of the Sacred Flame
### LARGE
In Ochkanatlan-era Ancient Bulletins found in the Old Core of Chu'ulel, the Sacred Flame is called K'ahk', and Och-Kan, the Holy Sovereign of the Eternal Empire, is styled Yajaw K'ahk', "Guardian of the Sacred Flame". One bulletin uses that title to seal off a "cursed land". The title shows how Ochkanatlan's ruler claimed the flame's authority after Xbalanque entered it.
