## summary
### LITE
Ley Line Disorder is the in-game term for the odd conditions inside Domains and some Spiral Abyss chambers. Depending on the place, the surroundings may hurt or hinder fighters (falling icicles, lightning, a creeping cold, drained energy) or boost them (stronger reactions, higher damage). It sits on the border between game rule and setting: Domains are said to stand on Ley Lines, but canon never explains each effect as a story event.
### STANDARD +
Treat it mainly as a gameplay label with an in-world flavour: Domains are built on the Ley Lines, and energy surging through the Petrified Tree at a Domain's heart is how the Ley Lines entry accounts for such effects. The same word is also used for a few Trounce Domains that replay old battles as "Memories".

## established
### LITE
- The wiki defines Ley Line Disorders as effects inside the Spiral Abyss and certain Domains that help or hinder characters, separate from enemy auras and elemental nodes. It has existed since 1.0.
- Domains are tied to the Ley Lines: item and loading text say Domains stand on Ley Lines around Petrified Trees (see the Ley Lines entry).
### STANDARD +
- Hazards and debuffs seen in Domains include Sheer Cold (a temperature bar that drains HP), periodic lightning bolts that drain Energy, falling icicles, longer Frozen duration, unstable High-Energy Crystal Cores dropped by defeated foes, a spreading Plasma Field, a pursuing Chaos Cluster, enemy Attendants that bolster a leader, and alternating Energy Tides.
- Four elemental debuffs exist: Smoldering Flames (Pyro damage over time), Slowing Water (longer skill cooldowns), Engulfing Storm (draining Energy), and Condensed Ice (higher Stamina use). Each lasts while the matching element stays on the character and can be cleansed by applying a self-aura of an element that dispels it.
- Beneficial Disorders include raised elemental damage, boosted reactions (Overloaded, Electro-Charged, Swirl, Superconduct), HP-threshold bonuses, CRIT damage, Plunging Attack, melee and ranged buffs, Shield and Elemental Mastery buffs, and shockwaves that trigger on certain actions.
### LARGE +
- Trounce Domains that carry a Ley Line Disorder include Beneath the Dragon-Queller, Narukami Island: Tenshukaku, End of the Oneiric Euthymia, Joururi Workshop, The Realm of Beginnings, Shadow of Another World and Scattered Ruins. Several are "Memories" challenges whose in-game text invites the player to relive an earlier battle, for example the sealed dragon Azhdaha in Beneath the Dragon-Queller and the duel with the Knave (Arlecchino) in Scattered Ruins.
- Damage from Disorders such as icicles, lightning and blasts scales with the Domain's level and the target's resistances. Specific values vary by Domain and are mechanics, not lore.
### ULTRA +
- Related objects: Ley Line Monoliths, found in some Domains, quests and Abyss floors, are energy-filled objects that attract monsters and spectral beings, which tend to attack them; a challenge there is to protect the monolith. The wiki says the reason for the attacks is unknown.
- Ley Line Overflow, a recurring limited-time event, doubles Ley Line Outcrop rewards up to three times a day when claimed with Original Resin. It is a rewards event and has no story content.

## accounts
### STANDARD
- Game text: the Ley Lines entry records loading-screen lines saying some characters are "recognised" by disturbed Ley Lines and boosted in Disorder Domains, and that those touching disturbed Ley Lines may be carried to hidden Domains where foes from their memories appear. That is a rule-of-play description, not a witnessed event.
- Story quests: separate Ley Line disturbances (Abyss Order tampering in 3.5, corruption in Sumeru in 6.6) are narrated as real events in the Ley Lines entry. None of them names a "Ley Line Disorder" in the Domain sense.
### LARGE +
- Characters such as Dainsleif, Nahida, Nicole and Thoth treat the Ley Lines as a real network. In Caribert (3.5) Dainsleif says the Abyss had interfered with the Ley Lines in Avidya Forest and that he repaired it. No one in canon says Domain Disorders are a deliberate act.

## open_questions
### STANDARD
- Whether the hazards in Domains are natural properties of the Ley Lines beneath them, a product of their builders, or only a gameplay abstraction.
- Whether "Memories" Domains are the Ley Lines replaying recorded events; their descriptions suggest it, but canon does not state a mechanism.

## connections
### LITE
Ley Lines, Irminsul and its Petrified Trees, Domains, the Spiral Abyss, elemental reactions and auras.
### STANDARD +
Ley Line Blossoms and Outcrops are the open-world counterpart. Disorder effects draw on the elements and reactions, so they connect to the element and reaction entries.

## gameplay
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not lore): in Domains and Abyss chambers, Disorders are the environmental rule set for the challenge. A Domain can show one or several, and values differ by Domain and difficulty. Elemental debuffs apply to each party member independently (Smoldering Flames affects only the active character), pulse on a fixed interval, and are shortened by artifacts and elemental resonance when the Domain applies them. Some self-applied auras can cleanse them.
### LARGE +
Examples of tuning in the wiki: Cryo icicles and Electro lightning bolts deal damage scaled by Domain level, and the Energy Blast and Powerful Blast types depend on reactions.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay interpretation (this project's recommendation, not canon text): characters can describe a Domain's strange cold, lightning or surging energy as the Ley Lines acting up beneath it. Most travellers would treat it as a known hazard of Domains, not a mystery. Do not have a character explain its cause as settled fact; canon does not.
### LARGE +
Keep it separate from story-level Ley Line trouble such as the 6.6 Sumeru crisis. The words overlap but canon never joins them.
