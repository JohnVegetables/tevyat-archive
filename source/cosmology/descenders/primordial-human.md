## summary
### LITE
The Primordial Human, also called Narayana, is a title: the ruler of Teyvat in "the court of the stars". Nibelung, the dragon king, held it before the Heavenly Principles destroyed him, and the seat has been vacant since. Later efforts tried to create a new bearer: the Hyperboreans' project that produced the Belyi Tsar, the Frostmoon Scions' Sacred Heir Project, and Rhinedottir's Primordial Human Project, which produced Albedo.
### STANDARD +
In 7.0 the Pale Crown Palace quest line shows Aksinya's attempt to complete the Belyi Tsar's ascension to the title at the cost of the world as it is. The term also appears in a Nicole voice-over, where in another possibility Albedo was the "Primordial Human" who traveled through worlds "recording and creating all".
### LARGE +
The term gathers several separate efforts under one name, and canon never defines it in one place. Treat it as a title whose meaning is shown by what people tried to do to obtain it.

## established
### LITE
- Narayana is another name for the title (Aksinya, 7.0). An artifact text speaks of a "vacant seat in the court of the stars", which the wiki identifies with it.
- Nibelung, called "the One" by Aksinya, is tied to the seat; after his destruction it has sat empty.
- Hyperborea tried to produce a new bearer from the remains of the Cryo Dragon. The result, born about a thousand years after the experiment, was the Belyi Tsar, the first Cryo Archon.
### STANDARD +
- Because Nibelung was still alive when Hyperborea ran its experiment, notes in the Pale Crown Palace say the seat was still occupied and the experiment could not reach its goal.
- The experiment ran out of control. The Cryo Dragon's unchecked authority turned the land into the Holy Soil of primordial ice, and the Heavenly Principles dropped a nail on it, which sank Hyperborea into the Shadow Realm.
- Aksinya says the Belyi Tsar knew he was the failed product of that project, yet accepted it, and with the Hvergelmir institute set out to become the true Primordial Human. The Eye of Graeae was the "Proto-Man" built to complete this.
- In the world quest An Ark That Used to Be a Soul (7.0) the Traveler stops Aksinya's plan to open the Court of Bliss, in which the Tsar would ascend and a "Courtyard of Carefree Light" would replace the world.
### LARGE +
- Aksinya describes the intended outcome as covering Snezhnaya, then the whole world, in the Shadow Realm so that love stays unchanging. The Eye of Graeae says the authority of the Primordial Human is like a poison to humans and fae, who carry mixed desires, and that completing it would mean the world was about to undergo an inexorable revolution.
- Nibelung is the only past bearer canon points to. According to the wiki, Aksinya and the Belyi Tsar describe the Light Realm's overflow from him as hope and love, which the Heavenly Principles later distorted.
### ULTRA +
- The Belyi Tsar ruled as Cryo Archon from about 2,000 years ago until the Cataclysm 500 years ago, having chosen to take on the purpose the Hyperboreans intended for him. Per Aksinya, his body stayed biologically alive after his death, spreading as a network of "nerves", a physical form of his longing.
- After the fight, the Primordial Human, whose core is the Eye of Graeae's consciousness, falls with Aksinya into the depths of the Shadow Realm, and per the wiki the Tsar's body crumbles to dust. The Eye device the Traveler carries keeps its functions but not its personality.

## accounts
### STANDARD
- **Aksinya (7.0):** the seat of Primordial Human, or Narayana, has been empty since Nibelung's destruction, and the Belyi Tsar was meant to fill it.
- **Artifact text (Disenchantment in Deep Shadow):** the Snow-King, burdened by sin and remorse, assembled the Hvergelmir to seek the stairway to the vacant seat in the court of the stars.
- **Nicole (voice-over, About Albedo):** in another converged possibility, Albedo was the "Primordial Human" who traveled through worlds, "recording and creating all". She adds that once that journey ended, no one could guarantee whether he would choose destruction or salvation. The wiki generalises this into a description of Primordial Humans.
### LARGE +
- **The Eye of Graeae (7.0):** it says it persuaded Koshchei, Pavlina and Alvis to stop Aksinya and asked to be divided, because it feared both the title's power and the world it would produce.
- **Hyperborea's notes:** the seat was described as occupied while Nibelung lived, so the experiment could never have succeeded in his lifetime.
### ULTRA +
- **Wiki on the Frostmoon Scions:** in the generations after Hyperborea's fall, the survivors' Sacred Heir Project aimed at a being able to wield the Light Realm's energy at will, for whom the elements would "fall at their feet". Weapon and artifact texts call that being the Ruler or King of Elysium. The wiki compares this with the Court of Bliss, but canon does not say the two projects share a source.
- **The wiki's Venti note:** in the Chinese manga text Venti calls Pangu, Purusha and Ymir "primordial humans" of other worlds' songs. The English text reads "primeval ones". It is a wiki observation, not a confirmed link.

## open_questions
### STANDARD
- Whether the office has any holder now. No canon says it does.
- Whether Nibelung's title and Narayana's seat in "the court of the stars" are the same idea as being a Descender.
### LARGE +
- What authority the title grants beyond being equal to the One, and why the Heavenly Principles would have treated a new bearer as a threat.
- What the Courtyard of Carefree Light would have done to the world, beyond what Aksinya and the Eye of Graeae say.
### ULTRA +
- Whether Albedo holds or could hold the office. Nicole's remark concerns another possibility she can no longer see.
- Whether the Sacred Heir Project and the Primordial Human Project were ever meant to reach the same seat.

## entry: Nibelung and the vacant seat
@keys: Nibelung, Narayana, vacant seat, court of the stars
@keys2: Primordial Human
@version: 7.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Aksinya calls Nibelung "the One": a will born alongside Teyvat, master of the Light Realm's power in the beginning and king of the Dragon Lords. She says the place of Primordial Human, or Narayana, has been vacant since his destruction. The wiki, citing the same quest, lists "Primordial Human" among his titles. His rank as Second Descender rests on the war of funerary flame, covered in the Descenders entry.
### LARGE +
Per the wiki's summary of the Pale Crown Palace notes, his seat was still held while he lived, which is why the Hyperborean experiment could not give anyone the title.

## entry: Hyperborea's project and the Belyi Tsar
@keys: Primordial Human, Seed of Life Project, Proto-Man
@keys2: Belyi Tsar, Hyperborea, Cryo Dragon, Aksinya, Hvergelmir
@version: 7.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Hyperborea tried to forge a new Primordial Human from the flesh and blood of the Cryo Dragon. The experiment went out of control and the land became the Holy Soil of primordial ice; the Heavenly Principles dropped a nail on the region, and Hyperborea sank into the Shadow Realm. A thousand years later the Belyi Tsar awoke from the remains. Unaware of what he was, he led the Snowland Fae, later learned his origin and, as Cryo Archon, chose to pursue the goal set for him.
### LARGE +
He founded the Hvergelmir institute to help. Aksinya and the Hvergelmir built the Eye of Graeae to complete his transformation. The Tsar fell fighting the Abyss in the Cataclysm 500 years ago, and Anastasya succeeded him as the Tsaritsa.

## entry: The Eye of Graeae as Proto-Man
@keys: Eye of Graeae, Proto-Man, Svarod, Velikan of Light
@version: 7.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Aksinya describes the Eye of Graeae as the "Proto-Man" created through countless experiments, the key that would let the Belyi Tsar become the Primordial Human; its face was modelled on the Tsar's human form. It is the quest NPC Paimon calls Goggles, found by the Traveler in an abandoned train in Chapter VII (7.0). Before the final fight it asked the Traveler what they would do if their sibling had become a different person; the Traveler answered that they would learn to love them again.
### LARGE +
Before the Cataclysm it was divided into three parts and persuaded Koshchei, Pavlina and Alvis to stop Aksinya. Once the Traveler recovers its three fragments, Aksinya transfers it into the Tsar's remains, and its consciousness becomes the core of the being fought as the Primordial Human in the Court of Eternal Bliss. After the fight, it and Aksinya fall into the Shadow Realm together.

## entry: The Sacred Heir Project and Avrelian
@keys: Sacred Heir Project, Ruler of Elysium, King of Elysium, Avrelian
@keys2: Frostmoon Scions, Hyperborea, Reed Miller
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The Frostmoon Scions, Hyperborean survivors, ran a Sacred Heir Project instead of the Seed of Life Project: breeding to purify moon-blessed blood until a Maiden bore a being able to wield the Light Realm's energy at will. The prophesied child was called the Ruler or King of Elysium. The wiki lists Avrelian, the first Reed Miller, as the failed outcome: his mother fled the role, and he became a legendary thief and founder of the Treasure Hoarders, executed 500 years ago.
### LARGE +
Lovia, the last Archpriestess, tried again with the twins Aila and Alia. Both rebelled, and Aila, as the new Moonchanter, ended the project. The link between this project and the title Primordial Human is the wiki's reading of item texts.

## entry: Rhinedottir's Primordial Human Project
@keys: Primordial Human Project, Subject Two, Cretaceus
@keys2: Rhinedottir, Albedo
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Rhinedottir's Primordial Human Project aimed to create a human through alchemy. It first produced failures such as Subject Two, who took Albedo's form and was discarded in Durin's stomach, and finally the "perfect" Albedo, whom she classed as Cretaceus. Nicole says that in another converged possibility Albedo was the Primordial Human who traveled through worlds.
### LARGE +
Nicole adds that she cannot observe how that version ended, and that the journey now is the player's own.
