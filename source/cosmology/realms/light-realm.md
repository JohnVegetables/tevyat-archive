## summary
### LITE
The Light Realm, also called the realm of the elements or the Vishap Realm, is one of Teyvat's three realms. It is the home and origin of pure, primordial elemental beings, above all dragons and elemental lifeforms, and the raw source from which the seven elements were later drawn. It stands directly opposed to the Void Realm (the Abyss) and is not naturally compatible with the Human Realm.
### STANDARD +
Canon treats it as the elemental energy of the world before it was divided, like white light before it is split into seven colours. Its energy vanquishes the Void Realm's darkness. Modern Elemental Energy is a narrowed, more easily controlled and human-compatible form of it.

## established
@version: 2.4
### LITE
- The Light Realm hosts creatures that wield pure, raw elements. Dragons and elemental lifeforms are its best-known inhabitants.
- It is diametrically opposed to the Void Realm and incompatible with the Human Realm unless adjustments are made.
### STANDARD +
- Its dragons were ruled by the Seven Sovereigns under the Dragon King Nibelung, who were later succeeded in rulership by the Three Moons.
- When the Heavenly Principles arrived, it and its shades defeated the Seven Sovereigns, founded the Human Realm and narrowed and refracted the Light Realm into the seven elements (per the banned book "Before Sun and Moon").
- It then used phlogiston as inspiration to modify the Light Realm's power into modern Elemental Energy, easier for humans to control and better suited to fight the Abyss (Beneath the Secret Source, as relayed by the wiki).
- The Human Realm was at some point interposed between the Light and Void Realms (The Subterranean Trials of Drake and Serpent).
- Realm-crossing adaptations exist: vishaps were modified to accept Orobashi's Bloodbranch Coral, Sangacorallia break the Light Realm's pure elemental energy into something the Human Realm can bear, and Natlan's Holy Soil was similarly rendered tolerable with Grainfruit and the Essence of Sacrifice. The Void Realm, by contrast, is incompatible with both and dangerous to both.
### LARGE +
- Descriptions of its nature: the "Notes by a Certain Noble Individual" in the Pale Crown Palace call the light from the genesis of the world "undissipated hope itself", able to "illuminate the entire cosmos", and say the never-refracted light signified "a boundless array of possibilities in its pure whiteness" until man confined it to a narrower band of hues in the name of certainty. The same notes say that whenever we lay an anchor into fate, the Abyss lays an anchor into us at the end of time.
- In An Ark That Used to Be a Soul, Aksinya names Nibelung the master of the Light Realm's power at the very beginning, relays the Belyi Tsar's vision of a world where all love one another "as in the very beginning", and calls that "love" a kind of spiritual overflow that runs dry under sorrow.
- Neuvillette's Vision story says the Heavenly Principles, crippled in the war of vengeance, created the Gnoses to keep subduing "the resentments and loathing of the world". The wiki reads these sources together as meaning that narrowing the Light Realm and anchoring Fate turned the primordial "love" into envy, hatred and selfish desire; that link is the wiki's synthesis, not a single canon statement.
- Apep, a being of the Light Realm, adapted to abyssal forbidden knowledge after consuming the corrupted corpse of King Deshret, but suffered great infirmity for millennia.
### ULTRA +
- The wiki notes that vishaps are the only confirmed Light Realm creatures yet are listed under Mystical Beasts rather than Elemental Lifeforms in the in-game Archive.
- Phlogiston (Natlan) and kuuvahki (Nod-Krai) are described as forms of Light Realm power; the Version 6.0 "Luna I" special program called kuuvahki light before dispersion. See connections.

## accounts
@version: 2.4
### STANDARD
- The banned book "Before Sun and Moon" presents the Heavenly Principles' refraction as founding the Human Realm. The Pale Crown Palace noble's notes present the same narrowing as a loss: boundless possibility confined in the name of certainty. Both are in-world views; canon does not name one the whole truth.

## event_accounts
@version: 2.4
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
- During the limited-time 2.4 event Three Realms Gateway Offering, Tsumi, the Snake-Masked Shrine Maiden, describes the three realms' analogous currents (ley lines for the Human Realm, elemental currents for the Light Realm, dark currents for the Void Realm) and says the Light Realm's energy vanquishes the Void Realm's darkness.
### LARGE +
- In the same event, Enjou had tricked the vishaps into disrupting the balance of the three realms in Enkanomiya. Afterwards the vishaps, despite their hatred of humanity, lent humans aid against the encroaching Void Realm (Three Realms Gateway Offering: Aftermath; also stated on the Light Realm loading screen).

## open_questions
@version: 2.4
### STANDARD
Canon does not say what the Light Realm physically is, where it lies, or how its currents work. The three realms appear to be concurrent facets of the world rather than separate places, with some areas dominated by one realm's power.
### LARGE +
Fan theories connect the Light Realm to the Abyss or to the Shadow Realm. They are speculation only. Treat them as unresolved.

## entry: Light Realm
@keys: Light Realm, realm of the elements, Vishap Realm, Koukai
@version: 2.4
### STANDARD
The Light Realm (realm of the elements, Vishap Realm) is the primordial source of pure elemental energy and the home of dragons and elemental beings. Opposed to the Void Realm and incompatible with the Human Realm without adaptation. The Heavenly Principles defeated its Seven Sovereigns and narrowed it into the seven elements.

## entry: Three Realms
@keys: three realms, Human Realm
@keys2: Light Realm, Void Realm, Abyss
@version: 2.4
### STANDARD
Teyvat's three realms are the Light Realm, the Void Realm and the Human Realm made by the Heavenly Principles. They appear to coexist as facets of the same world rather than separate places. The Light Realm's energy overcomes the Void Realm's darkness. The Void Realm threatens both others.
