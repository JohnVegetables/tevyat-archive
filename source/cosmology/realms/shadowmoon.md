## summary
### LITE
The shadowmoon, also called the false moon or the moon's shadow, was an illusory moon that occupied the sky of Teyvat for thousands of years after the real Three Moons were destroyed or banished. It also served as a prison for one fragment of the dead Zibai's soul. When the Traveler and Columbina brought the Frost Moon back in Nod-Krai, the shadowmoon lost its purpose and began to disintegrate. It vanished completely on the night of Lantern Rite in Liyue Harbor, in version 6.3.
### STANDARD +
Until then it was invisible to the eye except as occasional dark patches, which people took for lunar eclipses. Zhongli worked out that mortals' wishes seemed to trigger each stage of its breakdown.

## established
@version: 6.3
### LITE
- It was an illusory "false" moon, a projection of the moon rather than a body of its own.
- The Frost Moon's return left it without a place in the heavens, so it began to disintegrate bit by bit.
- It held Zibai's First Deadly Self, Ziju, and with her the Heavenly Soul (Taiguang) of Zibai's divided soul.
- It disappeared entirely on Lantern Rite night.
### STANDARD +
- Disintegration comes and goes. While it happens the real moon looks as if a black shadow covers it, which Zhongli identifies as the recent lunar eclipse; when it pauses the shadow cannot be seen at all.
- Zhongli suspected that wishes, such as those made on Xiao Lanterns, trigger the episodes. In The Realm of Clouds the Traveler experiences one exactly when Linlang speaks her wish.
- Because the soul fragment and the shadowmoon had spent countless years together they had merged, so when the shadowmoon was gone Ziju would cease to exist in the world unless Zibai's other two fragments were reunited with her first.
- Zhongli had been waiting for such an opportunity for thousands of years; he used the moment to reunite the three fragments.
### LARGE +
- Zibai's soul had been shattered when she died; Istaroth preserved the pieces in the moment before her death and placed them in her Three Deadly Selves. Taiguang, the Heavenly Soul, was held in Ziju and kept in the shadowmoon; Shuangling, the Earth Soul, was held in Zizhi, who slept beneath the earth in what is now the Chasm; Youjing, the Human Soul, was held in Zijiao, sealed in a painting.
- The Traveler gained access to Ziju's "celestial prison" through a broken jade token that served as a medium. Ziju led them out, but stayed behind, saying her time was nearly up.
- The Traveler received the Earth Soul from Zizhi (Act II) and the Human Soul from Zijiao (Act III). On Lantern Rite night the wishes of the people fully disintegrated the shadowmoon, Zibai's souls merged and she came back, with hazy memories.
### ULTRA +
- Ziju's eyes are blue, Zizhi's red and Zijiao's gold; whichever Self is dominant shows in the colour (per the wiki's Zibai page).
- Zibai's later amnesia and her walk through Liyue are told in the epilogue White Horse's Leisurely Journey.
- This content ran as a time-gated event in version 6.3 (Luna IV) and was later made a permanent Story Quest.

## accounts
@version: 6.3
### STANDARD
- Zhongli (The Realm of Clouds): the shadowmoon is a false projection of the moon, the prison confining Zibai's Heavenly Soul, and its collapse is both a danger and an opening.
- The wiki's Three Moons page: the Heavenly Principles projected the shadowmoon to cover up what happened to the Three Moons after the war of funerary flame; Istaroth used it to preserve a portion of Zibai's soul. Only the second half is shown directly in the quest.
### LARGE +
- Zibai's own wiki summary credits Istaroth with freezing her at the moment of death and storing her soul in the Selves; Zhongli says the same.

## open_questions
@version: 6.3
### STANDARD
Canon does not say what the shadowmoon was made of, whether it ever existed apart from the sky it replaced, or who made it. The Heavenly Principles are the attributed maker (via the wiki) but the quest text read does not state this outright. Do not describe the Heavenly Principles as having sworn to it or as having destroyed it.
### LARGE +
It is also unstated how much of the old night-sky cycle (the three sisters' alternating rule over the sky) it imitated.

## connections
@version: 6.3
### STANDARD
Replaced the Three Moons after the war of funerary flame, when the Eternal and Iridescent Moons were destroyed and the Frost Moon banished beyond the false sky. Ended by the return of the Frost Moon (Archon Quest True Moon, version 6.3). Linked to Zibai, the Moon Envoy and one-time god of Lang-Gan, to Istaroth of the Four Shades, to Zhongli, and to Liyue's Lantern Rite.
### LARGE +
Related lore: the Chasm (where Lang-Gan stood), the Heavenly Principles' false sky, and the Crimson Moon (a different moon, not the shadowmoon).

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Treat it as a hidden, fading thing after 6.3: it is now gone, and only characters who followed the Lantern Rite events know of it. Ordinary Liyue residents may remember the odd eclipses or the legend of the White Horse Adeptus without knowing the connection. (Suggested use, not canon.)

## entry: Shadowmoon disintegration
@keys: shadowmoon, false moon, fake moon, moon's shadow
@version: 6.3
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
After the Frost Moon returned to Teyvat, the shadowmoon broke down bit by bit. To observers, the moon looked briefly blocked by a black shadow (the "lunar eclipse"), then clear. Zhongli suspected wishes triggered it; on Lantern Rite night, with countless Xiao Lanterns released, it fully collapsed. The story was told in the A Lanternlit Ode to the Silver Moon event, a limited-time event when it began.

## entry: Zibai's Three Deadly Selves
@keys: Ziju, Zizhi, Zijiao, Three Deadly Selves
@keys2: Zibai, shadowmoon, soul
@version: 6.3
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
Zibai split off Ziju, Zizhi and Zijiao, her Three Deadly Selves, before dying. Istaroth placed her shattered soul in them: the Heavenly Soul (Taiguang) in Ziju, imprisoned in the shadowmoon; the Earth Soul (Shuangling) in Zizhi, who slept in the Chasm; and the Human Soul (Youjing) in Zijiao, who was sealed in a painting and later followed Rex Lapis during the Archon War. The Traveler and Zhongli recovered all three before the shadowmoon vanished, and Zibai returned.
