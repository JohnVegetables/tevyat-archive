## summary
### LITE
A Moon Marrow is the energy core that condenses from a Moon Goddess's remains after she dies, carrying an imprint of her divine presence. Two formed naturally, from Aria of the Eternal Moon and Sonnet of the Iridescent Moon. Dottore built a third, artificial one. Columbina likens a Marrow to an Archon's Gnosis: a symbol and core of godhood. After the events of Song of the Welkin Moon the Eternal Marrow is with Lauma and the Iridescent Marrow with Arlecchino.
### STANDARD
A Moon Marrow is a divine artifact: an energy core formed from a Moon Goddess's remains after her death. Moonchanters in Nod-Krai long sensed a divine presence in the Eternal one and named it the Marrow. It exists in three forms: the Eternal Moon Marrow (Aria), the Iridescent Moon Marrow (Sonnet, corrupted by the Abyss) and an Artificial Marrow that Fatui Harbinger Dottore made himself. Columbina compares the Marrow to an Archon's Gnosis, and it can only form once a goddess is dead.

## established
### LITE
- Formed from the remains of a dead Moon Goddess; it holds her power and divine imprint.
- Eternal Moon Marrow: from Aria, a miniature moon ringed with debris. Sealed away by the Frostmoon Scions and able to show past events.
- Iridescent Moon Marrow: from Sonnet, a red crescent wreathed in crimson flame when uncorrupted. Corrupted by the Abyss.
- Artificial Marrow: made by Dottore, a light-blue mechanical sphere with esoteric patterns.
### STANDARD +
- Columbina, the living Frost Moon goddess, says a Marrow can only form after the goddess is dead, so she does not carry one herself. She compares it to a Gnosis, which was formed from the body of the Third Descender; a god who bears a Gnosis is an Archon.
- A Marrow is a source of primordial Light Realm energy that predates the Heavenly Principles.
- Each Marrow belongs to its own goddess. Columbina says the three moons are not one and the same and that, as the Frost Moon goddess, she cannot wield the others' powers. She also says one body cannot contain the Marrows of two moons.
- A goddess can still restore her own strength by drawing kuuvahki from a Marrow. Columbina does this with the Eternal Marrow, saying moonlight blends and each moon can reflect the others' glow.
- Dottore's Artificial Marrow counted as a genuine article for completing the Trilune.
### LARGE +
- The Marrows came into being after the war of funerary flame, once the Three Moons were destroyed or exiled and their goddesses had died.
- Canon's death is confirmed but no Marrow of hers is attested. Her circumstances differed: her moon was banished beyond the false sky rather than shattered, and a new goddess (Columbina) was born to succeed her. Whether a Frost Moon Marrow exists is not stated in canon.

## accounts
@version: 6.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Eternal Moon Marrow: it coalesced from Aria's remains and was found and kept as a sacred relic by the Frostmoon Scions. They sealed it in a secret chamber that opens only once a year, on Moon-Prayer Night, when the moon is nearest Teyvat and kuuvahki in Nod-Krai peaks. Its power can reveal past events, especially when a Moon Goddess wields it.
### LARGE +
Iridescent Moon Marrow: it came from Sonnet. At the moment of death she was overcome by fear and surrendered to the Abyss to prolong her life, which left her suspended in agony and her Marrow corrupted with Abyssal energy. That corruption shows as the Balemoon Bloodfire, an accursed flame that leaves ghostly images of those it burns. As the Iridescent Moon sank into the Abyss it became the Crimson Moon, venerated by Khaenri'ah's Crimson Moon Dynasty. They hid the Marrow in a domain reachable only for brief periods through a ritual that uses Bloodfire. Later Khaenri'ahn dynasties kept researching the Crimson Moon, and the Universitas Magistrorum had a Moon Marrow laboratory devoted to its powers.

Artificial Marrow: Dottore made it much later in his False Moon Institute in Nod-Krai, using the other two Marrows as a substrate (per the wiki). Arlecchino says his contact with Hyperborean phantoms gave him knowledge of the moons and Hyperborean technology, which he used to fashion it as a replacement for the Frost Moon. Dottore boasts that his artificial moon is not rejected by Teyvat, unlike Columbina, whose Old World identity and lineage kept her from integrating into Teyvat.

## history
@version: 6.0
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
The Traveler came to Nod-Krai and, wanting to recover memories of their spaceship, sought the Eternal Marrow's ability to glimpse the past through the Frostmoon Scions. Dottore wanted it to gain the Three Moons' power and become a god. The resurrected Sinner Rerir wanted it to reunite with his lover in the moon's reflection. Rerir broke into the Scions' secret chamber alongside the Traveler, disguised as the Traveler's sibling. Before the Traveler could take the Marrow, Vilemina stole it and brought it to Sandrone, who later returned it to the Scions after a fight with Rerir at the Kuuvahki Experimental Design Bureau. Lauma then used a secret rite to store the Marrow in the Traveler's body, which took it easily because the Traveler is a Descender, and the Traveler became more sensitive to kuuvahki. Once friends with Columbina, the Traveler let her draw on it from within; even a single ray of its moonlight greatly restored her strength.
### ULTRA +
The Traveler and Columbina then enlisted Arlecchino to enter the Crimson Moon's burial ground to retrieve Sonnet's Marrow in order to oppose a revived Rerir (Everlasting As the Moon, 6.1). The Traveler freed the Marrow from its Abyssal fetters and purified it. Sonnet briefly regained lucidity and spoke parting words to a puzzled Columbina before passing away. The space destabilised as the Abyss tried to take the Marrow back, but both escaped. As agreed, Columbina gave the Iridescent Marrow to Arlecchino, who stored it in her body. Her power grew greatly, the wiki notes, because she already descends from the Crimson Moon bloodline.

Later the Traveler and Arlecchino used the Marrows to guide Columbina's return to the Frost Moon by a Hyperborean altar (Return to the Moon, 6.2). Dottore had made a decoy with the Frost Moon's exact signature, diverted Columbina into it and stole the Three Moons' power. He froze time for everyone but the Traveler. Columbina summoned a portal and banished herself into the moon's reflection so Dottore could not control the power with only two Marrows. In Act VII (6.3) it emerged that Dottore had made the Artificial Marrow as a surrogate for her strength, giving him full control. He warped space and time into experimental zones in Nod-Krai for his experiments, such as swapping the minds of humans and animals, and told the Traveler he meant to build a world where even gods would serve the living.

In the final confrontation (Descending Moon, 6.3) Dottore tore a hole in the false sky and became the Heretic of the False Moon. Columbina, fully restored as the Trilune Goddess, and the Traveler fought him for the Marrows and took the Eternal and Iridescent ones from him. The Nod-Krai cast then overwhelmed him, the Artificial Marrow was destroyed and he apparently died. Columbina returned the Eternal Marrow to Lauma, who put it back in the Scions' secret chamber, and handed the Iridescent Marrow to Arlecchino again.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Treat a Marrow as a sacred relic, not a trinket. Nod-Krai residents and the Scions regard the Eternal one with reverence. Only characters who were present for the Song of the Welkin Moon should know the details of Sonnet's purification, Dottore's artificial Marrow or Columbina's role.

## entry: Eternal Moon Marrow
@keys: Eternal Moon Marrow, Marrow of the Eternal Moon, Aria's Marrow
@version: 6.0
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
The remains of Aria, goddess of the Eternal Moon, condensed into a miniature moon ringed with debris. The Frostmoon Scions kept it as a sacred relic in a chamber that opens once a year, on Moon-Prayer Night. It can show past events, especially in a goddess's hands. Lauma stored it in the Traveler for a time, and after Dottore's defeat she restored it to the chamber.

## entry: Iridescent Moon Marrow
@keys: Iridescent Moon Marrow, Marrow of the Iridescent Moon, Sonnet's Marrow
@version: 6.1
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
Sonnet's Marrow, a red crescent in flames when uncorrupted. Abyssal energy twisted it into the Balemoon Bloodfire and the Crimson Moon Dynasty hid it in a domain opened only by a Bloodfire ritual. The Traveler freed and purified it during the 6.1 story, and Arlecchino now holds it.

## entry: Artificial Moon Marrow
@keys: Artificial Moon Marrow, Artificial Marrow, Dottore's Marrow
@version: 6.3
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Dottore's creation, a light-blue mechanical sphere with esoteric patterns, built in the False Moon Institute. It stood in for Columbina's strength after she banished herself and let him control the Trilune power. It was destroyed when he was defeated.
