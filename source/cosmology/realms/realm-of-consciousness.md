## summary
### LITE
A realm of consciousness is an abstract space built on a person's mind. It lies outside the world of Teyvat, yet what happens inside it can change the outside world. Its entrance takes a shape tied to its owner, and its interior is coloured by the owner's memories. The Traveler visits three: Makoto's (Inazuma), Lesser Lord Kusanali's and Greater Lord Rukkhadevata's (Sumeru). Canon says very little about how they work.
### STANDARD +
A realm can outlast its owner's body: the owner keeps a sense of self after death, but cannot perceive the outside world or the passage of time, and the realm cannot be preserved forever. Yae Miko calls the whole idea "very abstract" and surrounded by chaos.

## established
@version: 2.5
### LITE
- It is a domain built on one person's consciousness, separate from Teyvat.
- Events inside can affect the real world.
- The entrance takes a form related to its owner: a torii gate for Makoto, a newly sprouted tree branch for Kusanali, a Knowledge Capsule for Rukkhadevata.
- Its interior is shaped by the owner's memories.
### STANDARD +
- A realm can exist after the owner's body dies, and the owner can keep their sense of self; but the owner cannot sense the outside world or time passing, and the realm cannot be kept indefinitely.
- Someone else may seal or open the entrance: Ei sealed Makoto's, and the Shogun later unlocked it; Kusanali opened Rukkhadevata's with the Electro Gnosis.
- A realm can be closed from within by a living owner: Kusanali shut hers to keep Il Dottore from reaching her mind through the Akasha.
### LARGE +
- Makoto deliberately used hers as a place to show Ei her vision of Eternity, with a seed of the Sacred Sakura waiting to be given to Ei once she understood. Ei did not know this.
- The Dream Sakura the Traveler took from Makoto's realm persists afterwards in a quasi-physical form that can be seen but not touched.
### ULTRA +
- The Gnosis gave some protection against the forbidden knowledge corrupting Rukkhadevata's remaining consciousness, which is why Kusanali could open and enter it.
- Terminology note: item text in the English localisation calls Makoto's realm a "Plane of Euthymia", but the quests do not, and Ei's own Plane of Euthymia is a different thing.

## accounts
@version: 3.2
### STANDARD
- Yae Miko (Radiant Sakura) gives the only general description: a very abstract concept, surrounded by chaos.
- Kusanali treated Rukkhadevata's realm as a preserved image of Irminsul as it was five hundred years earlier; this is her belief at the time, stated in the quest.
### LARGE +
- The wiki's overview of how a realm is accessed and what it contains is a summary of these three cases rather than a statement by any one character.

## open_questions
### STANDARD
Canon does not explain the mechanics of realms of consciousness, who can make one, or what happens to a realm once its owner's consciousness is gone. It does not say whether Focalors' and Furina's spaces in The Opera of Noirceur and Blancheur are realms of consciousness; they merely resemble them in how time behaves. Do not state either as fact.
### LARGE +
Whether realms of consciousness differ from Ei's Plane of Euthymia only in how they are made is also unstated; the wiki merely notes that they are different.

## connections
### STANDARD
Related to Ei's Plane of Euthymia (a mind-world she placed in the blade Musou Isshin), to the Sacred Sakura and Inazuma's cataclysm cleansing, to Irminsul and forbidden knowledge in Sumeru, and to the Akasha system that Dottore used against Kusanali. Adepti personal abodes (sub-space creation) are a separate technique.
### LARGE +
Makoto's realm links to the Sacred Sakura Cleansing Ritual and the Dream Sakura; Rukkhadevata's links to the erasure of Eleazar and the healing of Irminsul.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Use as a narrative device, clearly dreamlike and personal: the entrance looks like something the owner would make, the inside echoes their memories, and time outside need not match time inside. Keep explanations vague, as in canon. (This is the archive's suggested use, not a canon rule.)

## entry: Makoto's realm of consciousness
@keys: Makoto
@keys2: realm of consciousness, Sacred Sakura, Dream Sakura, torii gate
@version: 2.5
### STANDARD
After Makoto died in the cataclysm, Ei preserved her realm of consciousness; its entrance is a torii gate. Ei sealed it, but the Shogun unlocked it when their wills opposed each other so they could fight at full strength, Ei having no body of her own. Makoto had prepared the realm so that Ei could eventually grasp her vision of Eternity, after which Makoto's remaining consciousness in Musou Isshin would give Ei the seed of the Sacred Sakura. Ei planted it in the realm, where it took root in Inazuma's past and absorbed the filth of the cataclysm. Makoto's strength and consciousness were then spent and the realm vanished. The Dream Sakura it yielded remains, visible but intangible.
### LARGE +
Quests: Farewell to the Past and Radiant Sakura (Transient Dreams, Imperatrix Umbrosa Chapter, version 2.5).

## entry: Kusanali's realm of consciousness
@keys: Kusanali, Lesser Lord Kusanali
@keys2: realm of consciousness, consciousness, sprouted branch
@version: 3.2
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
While Lesser Lord Kusanali was imprisoned in the Sanctuary of Surasthana, she closed off her consciousness so Il Dottore could not read her mind through the Akasha. As part of the plan to free her, the Traveler entered her realm of consciousness; the entrance appeared as a newly sprouted tree branch (quest Jnagarbha Day, Archon Quest Chapter III Act V).

## entry: Rukkhadevata's realm of consciousness
@keys: Rukkhadevata, Greater Lord Rukkhadevata
@keys2: realm of consciousness, Knowledge Capsule, remaining consciousness
@version: 3.2
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Greater Lord Rukkhadevata's realm was left behind when she died in the cataclysm. Its entrance was a Knowledge Capsule, opened when Kusanali used the Electro Gnosis, which also shielded her somewhat from the forbidden knowledge that had corrupted Rukkhadevata's remaining consciousness. Because Rukkhadevata had been contaminated and was bound to Irminsul, Irminsul could not heal while her memory lasted. Kusanali erased Rukkhadevata's existence and remaining consciousness; the realm, which Kusanali took to be Irminsul as of five hundred years earlier, disappeared. Irminsul was healed and things caused by its contamination, such as Eleazar, vanished (quest Where the Boat of Consciousness Lies, version 3.2).
