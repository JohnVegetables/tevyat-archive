## summary
### LITE
The Shadow Realm is a strange pocket-space that floats like a bubble over the frozen land of Snezhnaya and swallows those who stumble in. Inside there is no wind, no light and no usable elemental energy, so Vision-holders cannot channel their powers and must fight with firearms that carry elemental power in their ammunition. The ruins of Hyperborea, the Golden City, lie inside it. Fatui soldiers and travellers have been lost to it; the Druzhna contain and clear breaches, with Royal Energy Commission officials posted outside them.
### STANDARD +
Snezhnayans also speak of it as the "world of shadow." Its origin is uncertain: the Belyi Tsar suspected that it was made with technology from beyond Teyvat and that it shields the world from the Abyss.
### LARGE +
The Traveler first enters it by accident in the Chapter VII Archon Quest, when the train bound for Snezhnograd is dragged in. The Belyi Tsar's connection is central: his expeditions into its ruins taught him what he was, and the passage he opened let the Abyss begin to seep into the world.

## established
### LITE
- No wind, no light, no elemental energy; as Alyosha puts it, even time does not exist there and everything seems to come to a standstill. Most people pulled in are never seen again, and the few who return are never quite the same.
- Those who fail to escape are devoured: in Alyosha's words their shadows are devoured and they are no longer among the living; Druzhna briefings describe them warped into monsters lost to reason.
- Breaches open suddenly in Snezhnaya, like bubbles rising to the water's surface; the Eye of Graeae says the Realm intersects the world of the Light Realm only rarely, in just that way.
- The ruins of Hyperborea lie inside it.
### STANDARD +
- Vision-holders cannot call on their power there. Firearms, whose ammunition holds and channels elemental power from outside, were found by someone after much research to be the most effective means of attack, according to the Eye of Graeae.
- The Eye of Graeae, a device found in an abandoned station, calls the Shadow Realm the darkest space in the "shell," where even the most high is loath to cast its gaze. It helps the Traveler by marking exits and issuing equipment.
- Breaches can appear anywhere, not only in expected places: one swallowed the Traveler's train on the railway itself, which Alyosha calls almost unheard of. Falling off an edge into the untethered depths may mean never leaving again.
- A breach can "sink back down"; the Traveler escapes through a core that collapses behind them.
### LARGE +
- The Belyi Tsar's notes describe the interior as the reverse of what he expected: no foul Abyssal blood, only fine white dust in the air as if all existence had been ground by winds, and beyond the storm a castle that proved to be Hyperborea.
- He writes that the Realm lies at the intersection of the physical, temporal and spiritual planes; that matter has no stable form inside it ("no true form"); and that it teems with possibilities yet unobserved.
- He compares it to a film of oil lying over the known world, separating Teyvat from the Abyss beyond. By his reckoning the science behind it is not of this world and exceeds even Celestia's.
- Hyperborea's sunken ruins have stayed uncorrupted by the Abyss for millennia, which he takes as a sign the two repel one another.
### ULTRA +
- His notes count his eighth foray (Note II), the 86th (Note III) and the 446th (Note IV). He restored a castle in the ruins, and on his first visit he found a long-standing acquaintance already there, whom the notes never name; a figure whose name is redacted ("■■■■") counsels him in later notes.
- The Realm's third-person shooter mode has its own fixed Traveler stats and a shooting range, but those are mechanics, not lore (see gameplay).

## accounts
### STANDARD
- The Belyi Tsar (Pale Crown Palace notes): the Realm was probably made by Anacharsis, "The Saint," directly or as a by-product of the memories he left in the eternal void in defiance of his fate; its science is not Teyvat's; it works as a film protecting the world from the Abyss. He presents these as deductions.
- Alyosha, a Snezhnayan traveller: knows it as a deadly place from stories and a few sightings, and says this is his first time inside. He recalls a story of a "World of Shadows" with a way out.
- The Eye of Graeae, an ancient device: names it the darkest space in the shell and says its own creation was likely connected to it. Its words on this are partly broken off.
### LARGE +
- Aksinya (An Ark That Used to Be a Soul): a Hvergelmir scholar who intends to blanket Snezhnaya and then the world in the Shadow Realm to create a "Courtyard of Carefree Light." She says the Hvergelmir recovered records in it showing that Hyperborea sank into the Shadow Realm and only the fae remained above, and credits Izmaylov with helping her escape from it.
- The Primordial Human in that quest says that in the Shadow Realm the binding of fate's threads is dispelled and infinite possibilities remain. This is a mystical statement from an unreliable speaker, not a verdict.
- Popular interpretations link the Realm to the Light Realm or place Nod-Krai's Mysterious Space inside it; canon does not state either (see uncertainties).

## history
### STANDARD
- Hyperborea, the Golden City of the unified civilization, was destroyed by a Celestial Nail roughly 6,000 years ago and plunged into the Shadow Realm; survivors fled toward Nod-Krai and became the Frostmoon Scions.
- Much later the Belyi Tsar, driven by dark dreams and a search for his origin, opened a "passage" into the Shadow Realm; his notes say the innocent were ensnared at a price heavier than he imagined, and the Abyss began to seep through the rift he had made. In Black Feathers Scattered at Parting (7.1), Rhinedottir tells the Tsaritsa that the Tsar incited her (then a herald of the court) and Saarelainen, the Third Descender, to open the gate to the Abyss, and that he alone escaped the consequences by fleeing into the Shadow Realm, which also let him keep his memories of the Third.
- Inside he found Hyperborea's ruins and records, learned what he was (an incomplete Primordial Human made from the Cryo Dragon), and eventually accepted Celestia's offer of the Cryo throne about 2,000 years ago.
### LARGE +
- He first assumed Anacharsis had destroyed Hyperborea through folly. Records found alongside the Eitr in his 446th foray convinced him the city's fate was sealed beforehand and that Anacharsis and Koitar had only delayed it by thirty years. He also wondered why the Heavenly Principles never purged the Realm the moment it appeared.
- In his last note he wrote that the passage stood open anew and that the Abyss would end everything within the celestial shell within a few centuries; Rotwang reached a similar conclusion by his own calculation.
- The Tsar died in the Cataclysm 500 years ago; the Pale Crown Palace is his mausoleum.
### ULTRA +
- The Eitr he recovered there passed to the Hvergelmir institute and was used in experiments by Koshchei and Pavlina. In the Archon Quest (Black Feathers Scattered at Parting, 7.1), the dying Ronova, naming it something Hyperborea hid in the depths of the Shadow Realm, fuses the Eitr to the Celestial Nail over the Cryo Dragon's remains in the ruins of Hyperborea.
- In An Ark That Used to Be a Soul (7.0), Aksinya and the Primordial Human fall into the Realm's depths together after the Traveler stops them; Aksinya says she escaped it once before with Izmaylov's help.

## connections
### STANDARD
- Hyperborea, Anacharsis and Koitar: the Realm holds the city's ruins and records.
- The Belyi Tsar, Anastasya and Saarelainen: the passage he opened.
- The Abyss: the Realm is described as holding it back, yet the opened passage lets it seep in.
- The Heavenly Principles and Celestia: the Tsar wonders why they never destroyed the Realm.
- The Eye of Graeae, Aksinya and the Hvergelmir: they researched or used Realm technology and records.
### LARGE +
- Eitr: a Hyperborean creation tied to the Light Realm, recovered from the Shadow Realm.
- Druzhna and the Royal Energy Commission: the Druzhna contain and clear breaches, drawing on Commission intelligence.
- Nod-Krai: a similar substance appears in its Mysterious Space; canon does not say they are connected.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not lore): the Realm is a third-person shooter mode introduced in 7.0 and first unlocked in the Archon Quest "Gunfire in the Silent Lands." Elemental Sight, food and some gadgets are unavailable; the Traveler uses firearms such as Shatanaya's Frostsilver, Balsag's Sunwheel and Ashamez's Thunder and throws Frag Crystals. Enemies are usually Fatui Oprichniki, Skirmishers and Landcruisers marked by a purple aura.
### LARGE +
Mini Shadow Realms (Facility, Floating Base, Maintenance Center, Ice Quarry, Ruins) are instanced breaches with a boss at the end; Pale Crown Palace and Flamefeather Valley are shooter-style areas; the Edge of Survival is an end-game mode with three strong Realms; the Shadow Realm Analyzer on the personal train turns samples from Shadow Wingdrops into rewards; the Special Recruitment Project's Cycle Training has the Druzhna officer Yarovaya send the Traveler to clear weekly Realms. Version 7.1 mainly adjusted bugs and minimap display.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay interpretation (this project's recommendation, not canon text): treat the Shadow Realm as eerily still and quiet; light is flat or absent, sound dull, the air full of pale dust. Characters rely on guns and gear, not Visions. Do not let a Vision holder cast elemental spells inside it. Keep its origin and purpose open.
### LARGE +
Its dangers are loss and erasure: people vanish, shadows are "eaten," survivors return changed or not at all. Fatui and Druzhna personnel treat it as a hazard to contain, not a place to explore for fun.
### ULTRA +
Do not state as fact that Anacharsis made it, that it is part of the Light Realm, or that Nod-Krai's Mysterious Space is inside it. Do not have ordinary Snezhnayans know the Belyi Tsar's private findings.

## entry: Shadow Realm breaches and containment
@keys: Shadow Realm breach, Mini Shadow Realm, Shadow Realm rift
@keys2: Snezhnaya, Fatui, Druzhna, Energy Commission
### STANDARD
Breaches open suddenly in Snezhnaya's tundra and, rarely, on its railways. Royal Energy Commission officials stand by known breaches; the Druzhna, whose duties include containing them, set up perimeters, enter and clear them. Fatui soldiers have been lost inside; those trapped are devoured and warped into monsters lost to reason. The Special Recruitment Project's descriptions say that clearing a Realm releases the lost souls, human and fae.
### LARGE +
The high-ranking Druzhna officer Yarovaya oversees these operations from the Druzhna headquarters: verifying public sightings, processing intelligence from the Energy Commission, establishing containment perimeters and leading clearing operations. Rewards and the Tsaritsa's favour are held out to those who help.

## entry: Hyperborea within the Shadow Realm
@keys: Hyperborea, Golden City, Golden Hyperborea
@keys2: Shadow Realm, ruins, Belyi Tsar
### LARGE
The ruins of the Golden City lie inside the Realm and remain free of Abyssal corruption. They hold records of Anacharsis and Koitar's rebellion, of the fae's earliest name ("Filii Adamici Evoluti," "the evolved children of humanity"), and the Eitr. The Belyi Tsar explored them over hundreds of forays and restored a castle there.

## entry: The Belyi Tsar's Notes
@keys: Notes by a Certain Noble Individual, Belyi Tsar's notes, Noble Individual
@version: 7.0

### LARGE
Six notes in the Pale Crown Palace record the Tsar's work: the opened passage (I), doubts about Anacharsis (II), the film image and the Abyss's slow seepage (III), the Eitr records and Hyperborea's sealed fate (IV), his acceptance of the Cryo throne and the Gnosis (V), and the passage opened anew with time running short (VI).

## entry: Aksinya and the Shadow Realm
@keys: Aksinya, Courtyard of Carefree Light
@keys2: Shadow Realm, Snezhnaya, Hvergelmir
@version: 7.0
@spoiler: major
### ULTRA
In An Ark That Used to Be a Soul (7.0), Aksinya resolves to spread the Shadow Realm over Snezhnaya and then the whole world in order to bring about a "Courtyard of Carefree Light." She flees into a Realm portal, the Traveler follows and stops her with the Eye of Graeae's help, and she and the Primordial Human tumble into its depths together. The Traveler and Paimon return through a portal.
