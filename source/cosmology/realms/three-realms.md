## summary
### LITE
The three realms are the Light Realm, the Void Realm and the Human Realm. The Light Realm is the home of pure elemental beings such as dragons and elemental lifeforms. The Void Realm, better known as the Abyss, is a destructive force that corrupts and unmakes. The Human Realm is the realm of gods and humanity, created by the Heavenly Principles. They are not separate places but concurrent facets of one world that can occupy the same space.
### STANDARD +
Each has its own current: ley lines for the Human Realm, elemental currents for the Light Realm and dark currents for the Void Realm. The Light Realm and Void Realm are opposed, the Human Realm sits between them, and the Abyss Order and other Abyssal monsters seek to tear down the human world.

## established
@version: 2.4
### LITE
- The Light Realm (elemental), the Void Realm (abyssal) and the Human Realm (created by the Heavenly Principles) coexist in the world of Teyvat as facets of it, not as distant locations.
- An area may be dominated by one realm's power, while elsewhere the three keep their influence in balance.
### STANDARD +
- When the Heavenly Principles descended, it vanquished the Seven Sovereigns of the Light Realm, subjugated nature and the Light Realm, and used phlogiston as the basis for the seven elements' Elemental Energy. It then created the Human Realm, including gods and humanity (per the books "Before Sun and Moon" and the Bathysmal Vishap records in The Byakuyakoku Collection).
- The Human Realm was at some point interposed between the Light Realm and the Void Realm.
- Creatures of the Human Realm and of the Light Realm (elemental beings) share the continent, though the two tend to conflict. The Void Realm threatens both, so the other two may cooperate to halt its encroachment.
- Elemental beings and Human Realm creatures can be made compatible with each other. Vishaps were modified to accept Orobashi's coral despite its Human Realm origin, and Sangacorallia break the Light Realm's pure elemental energy into something the Human Realm tolerates. The Void Realm's power, by contrast, has only been shown to destroy or corrupt.
### LARGE +
- The Void Realm originates from the sea of stars and is opposed to the Light Realm. Its corruption affects creatures of the Human Realm under Celestia's dominion and is especially harmful to elemental beings, who can repel it with the Light Realm's power.
- The Light Realm is called "undissipated hope itself" by the Belyi Tsar and Aksinya. Narrowing its possibilities into the seven elements and anchoring Fate is said, by Neuvillette's Vision profile and the Tsar's notes, to have turned its "love" into resentment that must be suppressed to maintain the Human Realm, and to have let the Abyss anchor into Teyvat at the end of time.
- Barbeloth describes the Abyss as the hatred carried by every possibility of fate that never came to pass.
### ULTRA +
- Nibelung, the Dragon King of the Light Realm, left Teyvat to seek an answer to the Abyss and was corrupted. Meanwhile the Heavenly Principles, which also feared the Abyss, shrouded the planet under a false sky against outside infiltration. On returning, Nibelung fought the Heavenly Principles in the war of funerary flame, and his return let the Abyss into the world. After defeating him, the Heavenly Principles sent Celestial Nails to the surface to purify the corruption.
- Enkanomiya sank in this era; its people developed their understanding of the three realms and built the Towers of the Three Realms (Kunado's, Yachimatahiko's and Yachimatahime's Locus) to regulate the three powers.
- In the cataclysm 500 years ago the Abyss ravaged the human world. In Natlan it tailored its attacks to each tribe, and Abyssal presence persists elsewhere, such as the Wild Hunt in Nod-Krai.
- Examples of crossing between realms: Apep, a Light Realm being, adapted to abyssal forbidden knowledge by consuming the corrupted corpse of King Deshret and was left infirm for millennia; Natlan's Holy Soil was rendered tolerable with Grainfruit and the Essence of Sacrifice.

## accounts
@version: 2.4
### STANDARD
- Tsumi, in the Three Realms Gateway Offering quest, says the people of Byakuyakoku believe in three realms, the Vishap Realm (called the Light Realm in most books), the Human Realm and the Void Realm. She names the three currents and presents the Light Realm's energy as able to vanquish the Void Realm's darkness. She also believes the Abyss Order finds the Void Realm's power addictive.
- The Heavenly Principles' account in "Before Sun and Moon" presents the refraction of the Light Realm as founding the Human Realm. Later voices, the Belyi Tsar and Aksinya, present it as a loss of possibility and hope.
### LARGE +
- After Enjou tricked the vishaps into upsetting the balance of the three realms in Enkanomiya, the vishaps, despite hating humanity, aided humans against the encroaching Void Realm (Three Realms Gateway Offering: Aftermath).
- The wiki reads the volume of elemental power each realm can handle as the main difference between the Light and Human Realms, inferred from wording ("elemental flow" versus "elemental currents"). This is interpretation, not an in-game statement.

## open_questions
@version: 2.4
### STANDARD
Canon does not say what the realms physically are, how they overlap, or how their currents behave. Do not resolve this with theory.
### LARGE +
Fan theories that the Light Realm became the Abyss, that the Shadow Realm belongs with the Light Realm, or that the Human Realm was literally built on the Light Realm are unconfirmed. Nor does canon reconcile Nibelung's role with the Tsar's claim that narrowing the Light Realm anchored the Abyss.

## roleplay
@version: 2.4
### STANDARD
This is the project's suggested handling, not canon. Few ordinary people use the term "three realms"; scholars, Enkanomiya natives, vishaps, dragons or those close to the Abyss might. Keep it mysterious and avoid stating fan theories as fact.

## entry: Three Realms
@keys: three realms, Human Realm, Three Realms Gateway
@version: 2.4
### STANDARD
The three realms are the Light Realm, the Void Realm (Abyss) and the Human Realm made by the Heavenly Principles. They are concurrent facets of Teyvat, with analogous currents: elemental currents, dark currents and ley lines. The Light Realm's energy overcomes the Void Realm's darkness, and the Void Realm threatens both others.

## entry: Void Realm
@keys: Void Realm
@keys2: three realms, Light Realm, Human Realm
@version: 2.4
### STANDARD
The Void Realm, better known as the Abyss, is an unworldly destructive force from the sea of stars, opposed to the Light Realm and corrosive to the Human Realm. It corrupts people and creatures and is harmful to elemental beings.
