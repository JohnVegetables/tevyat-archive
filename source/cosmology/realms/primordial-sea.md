## summary
@spoiler: major
### LITE
The Primordial Sea is an ancient sea hidden beneath Fontaine. Most life in Teyvat first arose in it, and it once lay on the surface of the world. Its water gives life, and it can also take it away: the people of Fontaine were made from Oceanids using its water, so pure Primordial Seawater dissolved them back into water. This was the root of Fontaine's "original sin" and of the prophecy that a rising flood would dissolve every Fontainian. In the 4.2 Archon Quest, Neuvillette, restored as the Hydro Sovereign, made Fontainians true humans. The flood came, but it no longer dissolved them.
### STANDARD +
The main known entrance lies beneath the Fortress of Meropide, behind a sluice gate in its Forbidden Zone. Smaller passages and pools exist in other underground places, such as beneath the Tower of Ipsissimus and in an underwater Sinthe production base. People from other nations are not dissolved by it; it only makes them more sensitive to Hydro.

## established
@version: 4.2
@spoiler: major
### LITE
- Pure Primordial Seawater dissolves a Fontainian of Egeria's era or later into water, and their memories merge into the sea. Non-Fontainians are not dissolved.
- Diluted water does not dissolve people but still harms them. Exposed Fontainians show flushed skin, hyperventilation and exhaustion.
- Since Neuvillette's absolution (4.2), Fontainians are no longer dissolved by it.
### STANDARD +
- Water from the sea is described as having "higher quality and density" than ordinary seawater, and it can be weakened by mixing or dilution.
- In the 4.0 Archon Quest, Neuvillette says the Primordial Sea no longer exists on Teyvat's surface.
- Ordinary people cannot normally enter the sea; Childe reached its depths only after following a voice out of Meropide.
- Sigewinne can diagnose Primordial Seawater exposure from its symptoms (she did so for Freminet, and later testified about Furina).
### LARGE +
- The dissolved are not simply erased. Their consciousness seems to keep some individuality: an "Oceanid" took shape in the Fountain of Lucine from Vigneire and Vacher's later victims, whose dissolved remains flowed back there with the water used on them.
- After the prophecy was fulfilled, much less Primordial Water remained on the surface, and what remained seems to be impure. Vacher's old gang still tries to exploit it while the Fontainian authorities pursue them.

## history
@spoiler: major
### LITE
Before the Archons, the sea's "heart" was the first Hydro Dragon, one of the Seven Sovereigns. After the Heavenly Principles removed it, the Hydro Archon Egeria became the sea's new heart. Egeria used the sea's water to give her Oceanids human bodies. The Heavenly Principles judged this a sin, and the Fontaine prophecy followed.
### STANDARD +
Egeria's people became the tribes of Fontaine. In her reign, repentant exiles were sent below the waves to guard her secret, the entrance to the sea. They built the first Fortress of Meropide over it, and later wardens added gauges to monitor the water level. In time most of the prison's inmates forgot what the gate was for. The Remurian god-king Remus received a goblet of water from Egeria at the sea and used it to build Remuria (see the Ichor entry).
### LARGE +
Present day, in the 4.x Fontaine Archon Quest:
- 4.0: Marcel, whose real name is Vacher, has been dissolving young women for about twenty years in experiments to bring back his dissolved lover, and has framed others each time. The court learns that water from the sea can dissolve Fontainians.
- 4.1: For about a year Wriothesley had watched the gauges in Meropide's Forbidden Zone climb. Freminet collapses after swimming through diluted Primordial Seawater along Childe's route, and Clorinde rescues him. The sluice gate bursts. Wriothesley and Clorinde hold the water back until Neuvillette arrives, pushes it back and reseals the gate. Childe, meanwhile, wakes in the depths of the sea facing a colossal narwhal-like beast.
- 4.2: The water rises at Poisson after a tremor, and people there dissolve, including Navia's attendants Melus and Silver. Navia nearly dissolves in a flooded ruin; Melus and Silver, their consciousness now formed into Oceanids, shield her from the other victims for the few seconds Neuvillette needs to save her. The narwhal surfaces at the Opera Epiclese to devour Fontainians. Focalors's death on the Oratrice returns the Hydro Authority to Neuvillette. He absolves Fontainians of their sin and turns the sea-derived "blood" in their bodies into real blood. With the Traveler he subdues the beast inside the sea, and Skirk sends it, with the unconscious Tartaglia, back into the Abyss. The energy the beast had consumed returns to the sea, which floods Fontaine. No one dissolves, and the water then recedes.
### ULTRA +
The Cataclysm of 500 years ago tainted Fontaine's waters with the Abyss. The taint has since been diluted, but Oceanids now rarely arise naturally outside the few remaining pure waters. The Narzissenkreuz Ordo studied the sea more than 400 years ago. Jakob Ingold's conclusions became the basis of Vacher's experiments, and the Ordo's Master, Narzissenkreuz (Rene de Petrichor), descended into the sea to await the birth of his own world (see the Narzissenkreuz entry).

## accounts
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
- Hydro Vishaps (glider text, which itself calls them unreliable): the first sea was like blood, and life within it was not separate individuals. Creatures that moved onto land or into the sky grew blood vessels to carry the sea inside them, and the first Hydro Dragon was its beating heart.
- Oceanid tales (same text): when that heart was removed, Celestia's envoy, the leader charged with creating life, came to the sea and made another heart, Egeria. She had a dragon's nobility without its form and a god's majesty without a divine duty, and her compassion led her into the sins that beings of pure water must beware.
- Remurian legend (Dross of Pure Sacred Dewdrop): the dense primordial sea is the purest origin of all things and will one day swallow all life; whatever did not arise from it will be filtered out as floating detritus.
### LARGE +
- The Remurian Harmost Boethius (Sublimation of Pure Sacred Dewdrop): life born of the sea is fated to return to it, but humans have endless ways to transcend, and even the sea cannot fully dissolve them.
- Hyperborean prayer preserved in Nod-Krai (Hymns of the Far North): the hymn to Maadteraahka, Mother of Life, praises her as the "mother of the waters" who birthed a heart for "the roaring primordial ocean"; the wiki identifies Maadteraahka as Naberius, Ruler of Life, and the heart as Egeria.
### ULTRA +
- Moon lore (Moonlit Offering's Final Hour, Aubade of Morningstar and Moon set, added in 6.3): a "perfumed sea" nurtured the primeval dragon Nibelung before he sailed into the mists among the stars. The same text calls the sea the "wellspring" that sustains the world's existence, and says the winged Descender did not seem intent on plundering it.

## open_questions
### STANDARD
- Who, if anyone, now acts as the sea's "heart"? Canon says only that Neuvillette, fully restored, could command it.
- Is the Amrita Pool, Egeria's body in Sumeru, made of the same water? The question is unresolved.
### LARGE +
- Is Fontemer, Fontaine's sea and its aberrant creatures, the same thing as the Primordial Sea? The names suggest a link that the English text never states.
- What does the Remurian prophecy mean by "detritus", things not born of the sea that will be filtered out? Boethius posed this question to Remus; the item text records no answer.

## connections
### STANDARD
Prophecy of Fontaine (the flood it caused); Egeria, Focalors and Furina (the Hydro Archon's role); Neuvillette and the Hydro Dragon (the sea's original sovereign); Oceanids and the tribes of Fontaine (the people it shaped); the Fortress of Meropide (built to guard its entrance); Remuria (built on water refined from it).
### LARGE +
The All-Devouring Narwhal fed on the sea's energy. The Narzissenkreuz Ordo studied it. The Sea of Stars and Nibelung appear in the moon-age lore that the sea predates.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay interpretation (this project's recommendation, not canon text): after 4.2 the sea is no longer a danger to Fontainians, so a modern scene should not show Fontainians dissolving. Primordial Water can still be shown as a rare, illicit substance tied to Vacher's remnant gang. Before 4.2 it is lethal to Fontainians and almost unknown to the public; most people learned of its power only from Marcel's trial.
### LARGE +
Ordinary people cannot visit the sea. Its only known gates are sealed: Meropide's sluice gate and the Tower of Ipsissimus, which Ann resealed. Do not have non-Fontainians dissolve, and do not treat the sea as a place where the dissolved can be revived; Vacher never succeeded.

## entry: Primordial Seawater and Sinthe
@keys: Primordial Seawater, Primordial Water, Water from the Primordial Sea, Sinthe
@version: 4.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Water from the Primordial Sea dissolves Fontainians whose bodies came from it. Diluted, it does not dissolve them. Vacher (Marcel) found that weakened water made Sinthe, an addictive drink that brings euphoria. He sold it through dealers in Poisson to fund his research. Long use leaves people unable to focus or control their emotions, and withdrawal brings paranoia, anxiety and severe fatigue. Callas banned it in Poisson. Vacher also used the water to dissolve a man after a shooting, leaving Callas to be blamed. Callas kept silent to protect his daughter Navia, and later died in a duel with Clorinde.
### LARGE +
Cowell, a conspirator against the House of the Hearth, hid a balloon of the water in Lyney's magic box to dissolve the woman posing as Halsey. She was really Liliane, a thief from Mondstadt, so the water did not affect her. At Furina's trial, a deliberately diluted sample produced the same symptoms in her as in other humans, which supported the charge that she was human.

## entry: Meropide's sluice gate
@keys: Forbidden Zone sluice gate, Meropide sluice gate
@keys2: Primordial Sea, Primordial Seawater, Meropide
@version: 4.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Deep in the Fortress of Meropide's Forbidden Zone, a sluice gate seals the largest known passage from the Primordial Sea to the surface. Only the warden and trusted people knew of it. Wriothesley worked out that its gauges measured Primordial Water, and that the prison existed to stop the sea from engulfing Fontaine. When the gate burst in 4.1, Neuvillette pushed the water back and resealed it.

## entry: Original sin of Fontaine
@keys: original sin
@keys2: Fontaine, Fontainian, Fontainians, Egeria, Oceanid, Oceanids, Primordial Sea
@version: 4.2
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Egeria's Oceanids longed to live as humans, so she poured Primordial Seawater into their "blood vessels" as a substitute for blood, giving them human bodies. Creating human life was beyond her authority, and the Heavenly Principles branded her and her people with an original sin. The decree followed: the waters would rise, every Fontainian would dissolve, and only the Archon would remain, weeping on her throne. Neuvillette's absolution in 4.2 replaced that substitute blood with real blood.

## entry: Ichor of Remuria
@keys: Ichor, golden Ichor
@keys2: Remus, Remuria, Primordial Sea, Phobos
@version: 4.0
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
Legend says that when Remus came to the Primordial Sea, Egeria gave him a goblet of water. He refined it into golden Ichor, which he hoped would replace the sea itself. He mixed the insoluble Ichor with pure water, believing it could hold memories and wisdom, so that his subjects could leave their bodies and live forever in golem bodies that the sea could not dissolve. Item text says ordinary life cannot bear the separation of soul and body. The torn souls turned the Ichor black, and the attempt helped destroy Remuria.

## entry: Narzissenkreuz in the Primordial Sea
@keys: Narzissenkreuz, Genesis of a Certain Micro-Universe
@keys2: Primordial Sea, Rene de Petrichor, Master
@version: 4.2
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
In the world quest series "Ann of the Narzissenkreuz", the Ordo's Master, Narzissenkreuz (Rene de Petrichor), waited in the Primordial Sea to give birth to a new world of his own design. He had foreseen an apocalypse and meant to escape it this way. The Traveler, Ann, Seymour and Caterpillar forged the Holy Blade of Narzissenkreuz and defeated his follower Jakob Ingold at the Tower of Ipsissimus. When the tower collapsed into the sea, it broke a seal leading to the Primordial Sea. Inside, the Traveler and Ann defeated the Master, who lost control of the sea, accepted that his world-formula was wrong and left with them. Ann then resealed the waters so they would not flood Fontaine.
