## summary
### LITE
Holy Soil is land that holds a high concentration of the primordial elemental power of the three realms. Dragons can thrive in it; humans cannot live or farm on it. The process of land becoming Holy Soil is called the Holy Soil phenomenon. Canon shows it on Watatsumi Island, in ancient Natlan and across ancient Snezhnaya, and at the end of Version 7.1 Ronova tries to give it to the whole world.
### STANDARD +
The term is used by different speakers for different places, so a character should say which land they mean. It is a lore term, not a gameplay status or zone.

## established
### LITE
- Definition (Holy Soil page, citing The Three Great Martial Trials): soil with high concentrations of primordial elemental power from the three realms. It suits dragons and is unsuitable for human habitation.
- Watatsumi Island: its soil bleaches, loses nutrients and becomes infertile, which would eventually turn the island to desert if left alone.
- Natlan and Snezhnaya: in both lands the Heavenly Principles treated Holy Soil as a problem and answered with a celestial nail.
### STANDARD
- Watatsumi: local folklore says the island's spirit hears the call of Enkanomiya and sinks toward its depths. The Sangonomiya Shrine sends someone to Enkanomiya every few centuries to fetch a Bloodbranch Coral left by Orobashi to seal the vishaps, then performs the Watatsumi Goryou Matsuri to make the sunken spirit return.
- What the ritual really does (Tsumi, in the limited-time Three Realms Gateway Offering event): it awakens Orobashi's second familiars, the Sangacorallia, creatures that break the Light Realm's primitive elemental energy down into the milder energy of the Human Realm.
- During the limited-time Three Realms Gateway Offering event (Version 2.5) the ritual did not reverse the phenomenon but only stopped it, which Kokomi says had never happened before. Investigation showed the Void Realm had invaded Enkanomiya and suppressed interaction between the Light Realm and the Human Realm. The matter was resolved, but the source of the Bloodbranch Coral was cut off when the vishaps rescued their sealed kin.
- Natlan: the speaker the wiki identifies as Kukulkan says that, in an earlier eon, the Heavenly Principles regarded the phlogiston-rich soil that sustained Natlan as Holy Soil and cast sky-nails down to destroy the land. Dragons, being adaptable, were forced to devolve to survive in the new Natlan and became the saurians, though their bloodline did not change.
- Snezhnaya: Aksinya, citing record fragments from the Shadow Realm, says that when Hyperborea tried to make the Primordial Human from the Cryo Dragon Sovereign's corpse, the experiment ran out of control, and the corpse's unchecked authority turned the whole of Snezhnaya into the Holy Soil of primordial ice. Ley lines there became veins of pure crystal. The Heavenly Principles then sent a nail down on Hyperborea, which sank into the Shadow Realm.
### LARGE +
- Dragons and the vishaps belong to the Light Realm, the realm of pure primordial elemental beings, so land close to its power suits them.
- Eboshi (The Three Great Martial Trials) tells the Traveler that the phenomenon is the expansion of the Bathysmal Vishaps' habitat, meaning primordial power is growing stronger, and that only Watatsumi Omikami's life force awakening a second servant lets the Sangonomiya convert this raw power.
- Version 7.1: Ronova, dying and stripped of her authority, fuses the Eitr with the nail above the Cryo Dragon's corpse. The cutscene text of her last words describes giving the feeling of death and the hallowing of soil to the world. The nail crumbles and light rises from the other nails (see Celestial Nail).
### ULTRA +
- Canon states the Natlan and Snezhnaya outcomes separately; the shared pattern (a source of primordial power, a nail from the Heavenly Principles, a changed land) is an observation of this archive, not a stated law.
- In Watatsumi the remedy is the opposite approach to a nail: a Divine Priestess' ritual with a coral carrying an Omikami's power, converting the energy rather than destroying the land.

## accounts
### STANDARD
- Folklore: Enkanomiya's depths draw away Watatsumi's spirit, bleaching the soil.
- Tsuyuko (a shrine maiden who attended the Sumeru Akademiya): seawater leaches nutrients; Orobashi's Jade Coral Branch replenishes the missing elements and holds off the bleaching.
- Eboshi: the soil is the vishaps' habitat growing, a sign that primordial power is strengthening.
- Kokomi: the Matsuri is meant to reverse the process; during the limited-time 2.5 event it only paused it, and once she learned how the Bloodbranch Coral works she said she may need to think of a new way to counteract the phenomenon.
### LARGE +
- Kukulkan (Natlan) and Aksinya (Snezhnaya) both describe the Heavenly Principles as ending Holy Soil with a nail, each from their own side of events; the heavenly motive is never given in the Principles' own words.

## open_questions
### LITE
Canon does not say what Holy Soil is at a physical level, or whether the nails cure or merely suppress it.
### STANDARD +
It is unresolved whether the Holy Soil Ronova meant to release in 7.1 is actually spreading, whether it helps or harms living dragons and vishaps, and what it would do to farmland.
### LARGE +
Whether the Watatsumi bleaching and the nail-ended cases are the same process is never stated; the shared name is the only direct link in canon.

## connections
### STANDARD
The three realms and the Light Realm supply the power. Celestial nails and the Heavenly Principles are the response in Natlan and Snezhnaya. Dragons, vishaps and saurians are the beings tied to it. Watatsumi Island, the Sangonomiya Shrine, Enkanomiya, Hyperborea, the Cryo Dragon, the Eitr and Ronova are the places and figures involved.

## entry: Holy Soil on Watatsumi Island
@keys: Holy Soil, Holy Soil phenomenon, Watatsumi Goryou Matsuri
@keys2: Watatsumi, Bloodbranch Coral, Jade Coral Branch, Tsuyuko
@version: 2.1
### STANDARD
Watatsumi's soil is bleaching and losing nutrients. In The Moon-Bathed Deep, Tsuyuko explains that a Jade Coral Branch left by the Omikami must be retrieved from Enkanomiya for the shrine's ritual (called the Watatsumi Mitama Omatsuri in that quest). Tsuyuko privately attributes the bleaching to seawater leaching nutrients from the soil.

## entry: Holy Soil and the Three Realms Gateway Offering
@keys: Bloodbranch Coral, Sangacorallia, Three Realms Gateway Offering
@keys2: Holy Soil, Watatsumi, Kokomi, Enkanomiya
@version: 2.5
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
During the limited-time Three Realms Gateway Offering event (Version 2.5), Kokomi's Bloodbranch Coral ritual only paused the Holy Soil phenomenon instead of reversing it, which she says had never happened before. Tsumi explains that the ritual really awakens the Sangacorallia, which convert raw primordial energy into the milder energy of the Human Realm, and that darkness from the Void Realm was stalling them. The problem was resolved, but the coral supply ended, and Kokomi says she may need a new way to counteract the phenomenon.

## entry: Holy Soil in Natlan and Snezhnaya
@keys: Holy Soil, hallowing of soil, Holy Soil of primordial ice
@keys2: nail, Natlan, Snezhnaya, saurians, Hyperborea
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Kukulkan says a nail was cast on phlogiston-rich Natlan and forced the dragons there to devolve into saurians. Aksinya says the Cryo Dragon's corpse turned Snezhnaya into the Holy Soil of primordial ice until a nail struck Hyperborea. At the end of 7.1 Ronova breaks the Snezhnaya nail with the Eitr, meaning to give the world the feeling of death and the hallowing of soil.
