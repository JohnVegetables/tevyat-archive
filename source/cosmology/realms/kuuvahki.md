## summary
### LITE
Kuuvahki is a moon-power found only in Nod-Krai: a pure, primordial form of elemental energy belonging to the Light Realm, comparable to phlogiston. It flows through Nod-Krai's air, land, plants and animals. The Frostmoon Scions call it "the blessing of the Moon Goddess"; Snezhnograd researchers named it "kuuvahki." It powers Moon Wheels, Lunar Reactions and Nod-Krai machines.
### STANDARD +
Ineffa also compares it to the Primordial Sea. The Song of the Welkin Moon Archon Quest (6.3) reveals that kuuvahki is the body of Columbina, the Moon Goddess, poured out over Nod-Krai about 6,000 years ago.

## established
### LITE
- It is linked to the Three Moons and is strongest when the Frost Moon comes closest to Teyvat. Many creatures grow stronger or weaker with its tides.
- Kuuvahki strong enough can counter the Abyss and even turn Abyssal power into something usable.
### STANDARD +
- It has two opposite polarities, red and blue: like colours repel, opposite colours attract. Where it is very strong, "the rules by which objects move and operate" change.
- It drives Aino's "Boom and Blast Kuuvahki Energy Cannon" over Nasha Town, which fires when the Wild Hunt threat gets too high, and the Fatui's Kuuvahki Experimental Design Bureau on Paha Isle, a source of new technology and weapons.
- Radiant Beasts are ordinary animals warped over ages by kuuvahki into stronger, element-attuned creatures. Some plants store kuuvahki.
### LARGE +
- The Frostmoon Scions hold it sacred. Lauma says their statues of rare Lunasoul Stone brim with kuuvahki, which drew the Fatui's interest, and the Fatui's efforts to use kuuvahki have worsened tensions between the two groups.
- Abyssal power can also win. Flins observes that when kuuvahki and Abyssal energy meet, the stronger one suppresses the weaker; a strong enough Abyssal source consumes and changes the kuuvahki.
- Under the influence of the Pillar of Embla in northern Nod-Krai, kuuvahki exists in a "pure energetic form" that has no polarity and spreads as waves (kuuvahki currents). These waves can purify Abyssal contamination and melt Lunar Conflux Barriers, the barriers that form when pure kuuvahki condenses.
- Bodily sensitivity varies. Overexposure can cause nosebleeds, dizziness, headaches, nausea or full-body itching. People with chronic, severe symptoms have congenital kuuvahki sensitivity, which worsens if untreated. Snezhnayan doctors created a medicine for it generations ago, but some Frostmoon Scions refuse Fatui-linked help. In Where the Moon Rises (6.0), Lauma treats a flare-up suffered by Kaarina, whose family distrusts the Snezhnayan doctors in Nasha Town. Powdered lakkaberries ease symptoms for a while. The silver blood of Frostmoon Scions with strong Hyperborean ancestry, such as Lauma, can counter them.
### ULTRA +
- The Frostmoon acolyte priestess Verna built a suit to keep out excess kuuvahki, but it also blocks her own use of kuuvahki in blessings.
- Kuuhenki ("moon spirits"), the small moon-spirit lifeforms of Nod-Krai, live in this energy. Red and blue kuuhenki carry the matching polarity.
- On the Frost Moon there is Archaeolune Energy, described as kuuvahki's primordial form. It has all of kuuvahki's properties plus traits of phlogiston (programmable matter, energy source, information storage) and of the Primordial Sea (creation, including of life).
- A furnishing description credits Nod-Krai's abundant kuuvahki for its brightly coloured plants, oddly behaving creatures and strangely shaped landforms.

## accounts
### LITE
Before the truth came out, locals explained kuuvahki either as the power of the moons that Nibelung raised from Nod-Krai, or as the remains of two shattered moons whose fragments fell on Nod-Krai.
### LARGE +
Ineffa says geological surveys and meteorite studies support the legend that the moon once rose from Nod-Krai. Lauma tells the shattered-moon version: the fragments rained down "like stardust", which is why kuuvahki is so plentiful there. Frostmoon legend also credits the immense kuuvahki of the Eternal Moon, the largest moon, with creating the ocean tides. Hyperborean survivors on Hiisi Island are said to have received hereditary gifts from the Moon Goddess Canon, including kuuvahki sensitivity, silver blood, animal speech and white antlers.

## origin
@version: 6.3
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
In The First Sliver of Moonlight (6.3), Columbina, fleeing Dottore through the "moon's reflection", travels back to the War of Funerary Flame and meets the Moon Sisters Aria, Canon and Sonnet. They tell her kuuvahki did not exist in their time: it is simply her power. Made Trilune Goddess by their authorities, she turns her body into moonlight poured over Nod-Krai alone, and that moonlight becomes kuuvahki. It answers her because it is her.
### LARGE +
The reflection could hold divinity and authority but not a body, which is why she gave up her body. She left her soul in the reflection, anchored to the present by a Luonnotar, and hid her Trilune Authority within the Frost Moon. Because the Heavenly Principles destroyed the Eternal and Iridescent Moons and banished the Frost Moon in the same era, later generations believed kuuvahki came from the shattered moons. To close the loop, Columbina willed herself to be reborn about 500 years before the present, with no memory except a lullaby.
### ULTRA +
Before reaching the sisters, Columbina had used kuuvahki through passages linking the reflection to reality to make small changes there, leaving her friends clues. These explain some events in Nod-Krai that once seemed inexplicable. In Descending Moon (6.3), the restored Columbina brought down a great beam of kuuvahki on Dottore. His artificial Moon Marrow cracked and failed against her Trilune Authority, and he was destroyed. Earlier, on Moon-Prayer Night, Dottore had gathered enough kuuvahki to trick a teleportation device into treating him as the moon, letting him capture Columbina and the Moon Marrows. The Rächer of Solnari (Rerir) said that absorbing kuuvahki in Nod-Krai helped him survive inside the moon's reflection.

## moon_wheels_and_reactions
### LITE
Moon Wheels, the Vision-like gems of Ineffa, Lauma, Flins and others, channel kuuvahki instead of ordinary elemental energy. Lunar Reactions also harness kuuvahki.
### STANDARD +
The Lunar Reactions are Lunar-Charged, Lunar-Bloom and Lunar-Crystallize. Zibai of Liyue is the only known Moon Wheel user from outside Nod-Krai; Istaroth gave her hers.
### LARGE +
Ineffa's Moon Wheel character story records an ancient researcher's notes describing the Moon Wheel as a ritual item from Nephilheim: a "plaything" the "traitors" (the Moon Goddesses) gave their human followers so the goddesses could communicate with them. Like the wisdom of the researcher's Sovereign, it relies on "a purer form of power" rather than elemental energy, which the notes call tainted by the usurper (the Heavenly Principles). Later canon says the Heavenly Principles and the Three Moon Sisters made the Moon Wheels.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not literal lore): kuuvahki is Nod-Krai's main exploration mechanic. Kuuvahki plants, red or blue kuuhenki and similar sources give a red or blue "empowered state" for lifting, throwing and smashing objects, disabling Fatui machines and riding Moonlanes.
### LARGE +
Other sources are struck Atapetra Conches, some bots and Strong Kuuvahki Environments (such as Thunderclap Reef), which keep the player empowered. The player carries one object at a time and can also disable Fatui Oprichniki, Landcruisers and Recon Bots and use Unipolar Fields. Unipolar objects respond to one colour, bipolar ones to both. Near kuuvahki plants the player can briefly turn into a kuuhenki to move quickly along the ground. In co-op, a guest cannot use the empowered state or kuuvahki currents. In combat, Lunar Reactions need a Moon Wheel holder or Columbina in the party, whose Moonsign Benediction passive enables them, and unlike ordinary reactions they can CRIT. Radiant Beasts can trap the party in a draining Radiant Cocoon.

## open_questions
### STANDARD
- How kuuvahki relates to ordinary elemental energy, and how Zibai's Moon Wheel works outside Nod-Krai, are not explained.
### LARGE +
- In The First Sliver of Moonlight, Varka mentions Albedo's theory that elemental energy can dilute the Light Realm's power, but its consequences for kuuvahki are not spelled out.
- Canon has not stated how kuuvahki changed after Columbina's three parts were reunited and the Frost Moon was brought within the False Sky at the end of Descending Moon.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay interpretation (this project's recommendation, not canon text): in Nod-Krai kuuvahki is part of the landscape. Plants glow or lean toward passers-by, animals are livelier or more sluggish with its tides, and red or blue glows suit polarity effects.
### LARGE +
Ordinary Nod-Krai people still believe the shattered-moon or "moon rose from Nod-Krai" explanations. Only those close to the Song of the Welkin Moon events, such as the Traveler and Columbina's companions, know kuuvahki is Columbina's body, so keep that knowledge limited. Frostmoon Scions speak of it reverently as the Moon Goddess's blessing; Fatui researchers and Nasha Town engineers treat it as an energy resource. A local might casually mention a neighbour's kuuvahki sensitivity or nosebleeds. Do not give kuuvahki powers outside Nod-Krai without a canon reason.

## entry: Kuuvahki sensitivity
@keys: kuuvahki sensitivity, congenital kuuvahki sensitivity, kuuvahki hypersensitivity
### LARGE
Kuuvahki sensitivity is how strongly a person's body reacts to kuuvahki. Overexposure brings nosebleeds, dizziness, headaches, nausea or full-body itching. Congenital kuuvahki sensitivity is a chronic, worsening form diagnosed in people with severe symptoms. Treatments are a Snezhnayan medicine developed generations ago (some Frostmoon Scions refuse it out of distrust of the Fatui), powdered lakkaberries for short-term relief, and the silver blood of Frostmoon Scions with strong Hyperborean lineage, such as Lauma. Verna's protective suit and Czcibor's unexplained recovery after vanishing in Barrowmoss Barrens are known cases.

## entry: Kuuvahki current
@keys: kuuvahki current, kuuvahki currents, Lunar Conflux Barrier
@version: 6.3
### LARGE
In northern Nod-Krai (Voidsea Outlook, Wavechaser Plain, Ashveil Peak), under the influence of the Pillar of Embla, kuuvahki exists in a pure energetic form without polarity and spreads as waves. Glimmerfruit, Mandragoras, Slothsheep and Spireblooms give off these currents, which suffuse Mandragoras, Slothsheep and Spirit Blooms, light Oath Lanterns, melt Lunar Conflux Barriers and purify Abyssal contamination. Mandragoras are plant-like organisms that live in symbiosis with pure kuuvahki.

## entry: Archaeolune Energy
@keys: Archaeolune Energy, Archaeolune, Blue Kuuvahki
### ULTRA
Archaeolune Energy is an ancient moon-power found on the Frost Moon, described as kuuvahki's primordial form. It has all of kuuvahki's properties, plus phlogiston-like traits (programmable matter, energy source, information storage) and the Primordial Sea's power of creation, including creating life. Columbina calls it "Blue Kuuvahki." Its own entry carries the detail.
