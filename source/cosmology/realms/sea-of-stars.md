## summary
### LITE
The Sea of Stars is the universe beyond Teyvat: thousands of planets, stars and civilisations lying past the false sky and the Dark Sea. Canon describes it as a dangerous "black forest". The Heavenly Principles, Saarelainen and the Traveler all came from it, and unknown threats and cosmic calamities such as Surtalogi, the All-Devouring Narwhal and the Abyss reside there. Teyvat is one planet among them.
### STANDARD +
Most Teyvatians cannot see it at all, because the false sky hides the cosmos from the planet. It is not an accessible location in the game and appears in animated shorts, weapon and book text and character stories.

## established
### LITE
- Teyvat's planet sits on the edge of a spiral arm of an unnamed galaxy in the sea of stars.
- Three of the four Descenders came from beyond Teyvat (the wiki counts the Heavenly Principles as the first Descender, Saarelainen as the third and the Traveler as the fourth). The Abyss is counted among the cosmic calamities that reside there.
- Unknown cosmic calamities live there too.
### STANDARD +
- In the Old World, Nibelung made the Eternal, Frost and Iridescent Moons from the planet's crust to govern it while he travelled the sea of stars. During his absence the Heavenly Principles descended on Teyvat, took control and raised the false sky, which kept the universe hidden.
- During the Funerary Year Nibelung returned, brought the Abyss to the world and fought the Heavenly Principles, who won and still rule Teyvat. The Eternal and Iridescent Moons were shattered, and the Frost Moon was thrown out beyond the false sky into the sea of stars. By the Archon Quest "True Moon" it had been revitalised and drawn back inside the false sky.
- After the Cataclysm, Surtalogi brought Skirk to Teyvat from her homeworld, and gave the weakened All-Devouring Narwhal to Skofnungr in the Primordial Sea.
### LARGE +
Wiki-listed arrivals from beyond Teyvat, roughly in order: the Stellar Blackbody (brought to the Frost Moon and fused with Ungien before the Funerary Year); the Heavenly Principles; Anacharsis (before the Funerary Year); the Abyss (Funerary Year, brought by Nibelung); Saarelainen (after the war of funerary flame); the Traveler and their sibling, who passed over Teyvat's sky in the distant past and later made a forced landing at Starsand Shoal before the Cataclysm; the Narwhal and Skirk (after the Cataclysm, via Surtalogi). The Traveler's sibling emerged during the Cataclysm from outside the ship, and the Traveler speculates they left long before.

## worlds
### STANDARD
Known worlds beyond Teyvat. Teyvat itself. The Traveler's unnamed planet, which the Traveler and their sibling once ruled before a "star-devouring darkness" destroyed it; they then began travelling between worlds in a spaceship. Skirk's unnamed planet, home to humans who can adapt to any environment or energy source; a merchant ship from a neighbouring world judged them a potential threat and an army attacked, and Skirk escaped with Surtalogi's help. Throgg, an empire of the immortal Ysna'qurel, child of Ghel'vur, which was destroyed by the "Dark Destroyer" after sending a warning into space; it is unclear whether the world or only its civilisation died. The warning was received by the Moongazer Nin-Kiggia.
### LARGE +
Octavia's travels, from cosmetic-set and collection text, name many numbered planets: for example the 66th, which resembles Liyue and lives under a purple-black haze called "Misfortune"; the 67th, whose extinct dragons survive as fossils; the 93rd, the Academy Planet, which can reshape its surface into one academic collective; the 311th, where flowers express the planet's will; the 503rd, a perpetually hot ocean world where she introduced the lemon; the 666th, ravaged by an "Evil Star"; the 777th, a magic-saturated world whose scholars imbue meteorite dust with spells to counter a threat from deep space; and the 1896th, a bronze-age world of city-states that united against Abyssal enemies. These are told through outfits and shop events, so treat the details as light flavour rather than central lore.
### ULTRA +
Further entries include the 31st (a world fearing a threat from the stars, kept in order by two phantom thieves), the 118th (an Iron Forest world with a strict work culture and a virtual "carrier pigeon" network), the 206th (a young, savannah world that Octavia asked the Academy Planet to guard), the 273rd (an icy world of extreme skiing), the 507th (spirits that form pacts with humans, at a busy interstellar crossroads), the 712th (a theme-park world where Octavia worked as consultant and spread tales of Teyvat), the 722nd (a medical world), the 1217th (humans without flight who chart the sky) and a few with no description (921st and an unspecified world). Event-linked set descriptions are limited-time material.

## accounts
### STANDARD
The Heavenly Principles themselves are presented, in the Byakuyakoku Collection, as the first Descender. Alice describes the Abyss as the hatred carried by fates that will not come to pass, which is an account of its nature rather than a home world. The wiki does not state where it came from before Nibelung brought it. Weapon text for the Sword of Descension supplies the "star-devouring darkness" that ended the Traveler's world.
### LARGE +
Aloy, a collaboration character, is said to come from a post-apocalyptic world through a portal; the wiki notes this is not treated as canon. Alice shows familiarity with real-world Earth ideas (idol culture, the Rubicon), but because she is also able to break the fourth wall it is unclear whether this means an Earth exists in the sea of stars.

## open_questions
### STANDARD
Canon does not say what lies beyond the Dark Sea in detail, where the Heavenly Principles or the Abyss originated, what the star-devouring darkness is, how many Descenders exist overall, or how the numbered worlds Octavia visited relate to the main plot. Do not invent a map of the cosmos.

## connections
### LITE
Linked to the Heavenly Principles and the false sky (the barrier), the Descenders, the Abyss, the Three Moons, the Traveler and their sibling, Skirk and Surtalogi, Nibelung, Octavia and the Moongazers.
### STANDARD +
Roleplay note (this project's guidance): characters inside Teyvat generally have no idea the sea of stars exists, since the false sky hid it. Only Descenders, the Traveler, beings tied to the Moongazers or Surtalogi's circle, and a few others would speak of it, and even they know it only in fragments.

## entry: Sea of Stars
@keys: Sea of Stars
@version: 1.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The universe beyond Teyvat's false sky. Teyvat sits on the edge of a spiral arm of an unnamed galaxy. It is described as a dangerous "black forest" full of planets, civilisations and unknown calamities. The Heavenly Principles, Saarelainen and the Traveler came from it, and the Abyss is counted among its calamities. Teyvatians cannot normally perceive it.

## entry: Skirk's Planet
@keys: Skirk's planet, Skirk's homeworld
@keys2: Skirk, Surtalogi
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Skirk comes from an unnamed planet whose humans could adapt to any environment or energy source. A merchant ship from a neighbouring world saw them as a potential threat, and an army was sent. She escaped with Surtalogi's help and was brought to Teyvat after the Cataclysm. What became of the planet is unknown.

## entry: Throgg
@keys: Throgg, Ysna'qurel, Dark Destroyer
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
A world once ruled by the immortal Ysna'qurel, child of Ghel'vur, and their empire. It was destroyed by the "Dark Destroyer" and broadcast a warning into the cosmos, which the Moongazer Nin-Kiggia received. It is unclear whether the whole world or only its civilisation was lost.
