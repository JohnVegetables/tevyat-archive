## summary
### LITE
The Eternal Moon was the first and largest of the Three Moons, ruled by the goddess Aria. Legend says its great gravity raised the ocean tides. The Heavenly Principles shattered it in the war of funerary flame, and Aria died. Its remnants were hidden beyond the false sky, and Aria's remains survive as the Eternal Moon Marrow.
### STANDARD
The Eternal Moon was the first and largest of the Three Moons, raised by the Dragon King Nibelung and governed by the goddess Aria, eldest of the Three Moon Sisters. Legend says its immense gravity, or Aria's immense kuuvahki, raised the ocean tides. In the war of funerary flame the Heavenly Principles shattered it; Aria, unable to live without her moon, died. Pieces fell to Teyvat like stardust, the rest was hidden behind the false sky, and a part of Aria's power condensed into the Eternal Moon Marrow. Unlike the Frost Moon, it has not returned.

## established
### LITE
- Largest and first of the Three Moons; its goddess was Aria.
- Said to have caused the tides.
- Destroyed by the Heavenly Principles; Aria did not survive.
### STANDARD +
- Nibelung made the moons with Secret Source technology and raised them from the planet's crust at what is now Nod-Krai. In the Natlan Archon Quest (5.3), Mavuika described the fragment that appeared in the sky as an "Ancient Moon's Remnant", a relic of Secret Source technology from the age of the Ancient Dragons; the wiki reads this as the Eternal Moon.
- Its goddess shared sovereignty of the night sky with her sisters, one at a time, riding in a silver carriage. The English text says they switched about three times a month; the Chinese uses the unit xun, which is looser.
- Part of Aria's power condensed into the Eternal Moon Marrow after her death. The Frostmoon Scions found it and kept it as a sacred relic.
### LARGE +
- When it fell, heaven and earth were thrown into chaos, according to an artifact text (Moonlit Offering's Silver Crown). Some of its remains rained down onto Teyvat.
- Aria is shown as a tall, fair woman with long blonde hair in white and turquoise robes, with a soft yellow crescent behind her to mark her moon.

## history
@version: 6.3
@spoiler: major
### LITE
Nibelung raised the moon and left. When the Heavenly Principles arrived, Aria welcomed them as a guest and waited to see whether they would help the world. After Nibelung returned and imprisoned the sisters, the Heavenly Principles shattered the moon.
### STANDARD +
Before the war, Xiuhcoatl visited the moons looking for Nibelung and got only silence from each; canon does not say whether the sisters chose it. After the Heavenly Principles overthrew the Seven Sovereigns, Aria and her sisters entered a precarious peace with them, which the dragons took as betrayal. Aria, like her sisters, was appalled by the Heavenly Principles' treatment of civilisations that challenged the new rules.

When Nibelung returned from the Abyss, the sisters disagreed about taking sides. He read it as disloyalty, sealed them in the moon's reflection and made their moons into weapons. The Heavenly Principles shattered the Eternal and Iridescent Moons and threw the Frost Moon beyond the false sky. The dragons celebrated.
### LARGE +
Aria survived inside the moon's reflection even after her moon was destroyed, because of the prison's warped properties, and knew nothing of the outside war. In True Moon (6.3) a future Columbina reached her and her sisters and told them what had happened. Aria accepted her death calmly and tried to comfort Sonnet. The three gave Columbina the authority of all three moons, and at their request she sent their now-mortal bodies back to Teyvat, where, as history records, they died. Aria's remains then formed the Marrow.
### ULTRA +
The wiki pages disagree on how long before the disaster the sisters witnessed the union of the angel Koitar and the voyager Anacharsis (thirty days on the Aria and Sonnet pages, thirty years before Nibelung's return on the Three Moons page). The detail comes from artifact and book texts and the wiki's reading of them, not from a quest scene, so no interval is asserted here.

## the_marrow
@version: 6.2
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The Eternal Moon Marrow was kept by the Frostmoon Scions as a relic. In Song of the Welkin Moon it was stolen from them and then lodged in the Traveler's body for safekeeping, until Dottore took it after Moon-Prayer Night (6.2). Columbina retrieved it in the final battle (6.3) and returned it to the Scions under the Moonchanter Lauma.
### LARGE +
Dottore combined the Eternal and Iridescent Marrows with an artificial marrow he built, briefly becoming a false moon god. Authority drawn from marrows proved weaker than the sisters' own gift to Columbina, and the artificial marrow was destroyed when he was beaten.

## present_day
@version: 5.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
In the Natlan Archon Quest (5.1), Mavuika's strike broke the false sky for a moment and revealed the Eternal Moon's shattered remnants; the sky closed again and the public was told it was an astronomical anomaly. Later the Fatui pulled fragments of the Eternal Moon down into Nod-Krai and built the Kuuvahki Experimental Design Bureau around one of them, for a project for the Tsaritsa that canon leaves unspecified. As of 7.1 the moon itself remains shattered, though its Marrow is held by Lauma.
### LARGE +
The shadowmoon, an illusion the Heavenly Principles hung in the false sky to hide the moons' fates, faded once the Frost Moon returned to Teyvat's sky (6.3). The Eternal Moon had no such return.

## accounts
### STANDARD
- Liyue books give a legend of three sister goddesses in a Lunar Palace; some versions say the sisters killed one another or that only two died. Treat these as folk memory.
- Dragon-side accounts treat the sisters as traitors to Nibelung.
- The sisters' own account (given to Columbina) is that they chose to wait and see the new order.
### LARGE +
- Mavuika treats the moon fragment as a made object, a relic of Secret Source technology; the sisters and the Three Moons wiki account speak of living moons that are extensions of Nibelung's will.
- Where the moon's remnants lie is told two ways: pieces fell on Teyvat, while the rest was hidden beyond the false sky.

## open_questions
### STANDARD
- How Aria died is not told.
- Canon does not say what the Fatui weapon built around the moon fragment is.
### LARGE +
- Whether "Aralos" on the teaser note is another name for Aria is unconfirmed.
- Whether the moon's draconic technology and its nature as Aria's seat are the same thing.

## connections
### STANDARD
Aria (its goddess); Nibelung (creator); the Heavenly Principles (destroyers); the false sky and shadowmoon (what hid it); the Eternal Moon Marrow, kuuvahki and the Frostmoon Scions; Columbina, Lauma and Dottore (what became of its power); the Frost Moon and the Iridescent Moon (siblings).
