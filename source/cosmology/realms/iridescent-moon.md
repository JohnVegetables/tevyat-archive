## summary
### LITE
The Iridescent Moon was the third and smallest of the Three Moons, ruled by the goddess Sonnet. Its faint light made fleeting halos. The Heavenly Principles shattered it in the war of funerary flame. In agony, Sonnet fused its remains with the Abyss, and it became the Crimson Moon that Khaenri'ah's Crimson Moon Dynasty revered. In Version 6.1 Columbina and the Traveler reached it, and Sonnet died.
### STANDARD
The Iridescent Moon was the third and smallest of the Three Moons, raised by the Dragon King Nibelung and governed by Sonnet, the youngest of the Three Moon Sisters. Its light was the faintest of the three, barely visible to the naked eye, but it produced fleeting halos when it shone. The Heavenly Principles shattered it in the war of funerary flame. Sonnet, in agony over her broken moon, fused her fading soul with the Abyss to stay alive, and the Iridescent Moon became the baleful Crimson Moon. Khaenri'ah's Crimson Moon Dynasty later worshipped it as a source of Abyssal power. Its husk and Abyss-corrupted Moon Marrow were hidden in an otherworldly domain, which the Traveler and Columbina entered in the Nod-Krai Archon Quest (6.1).

## established
### LITE
- Smallest and last-made of the Three Moons; its goddess was Sonnet.
- Faint light, fleeting halos.
- Shattered, then fused with the Abyss as the Crimson Moon.
### STANDARD +
- Nibelung made the moons with Secret Source technology and raised them from the planet's crust at what is now Nod-Krai.
- Sonnet shared sovereignty of the night sky with her sisters, one at a time, riding in a silver carriage. The English text says they switched about three times a month; the Chinese uses the looser unit xun.
- When the moon fell, the sisters' carriage was overturned and the palace of the stars ransacked, according to the Liyue book Moonlit Bamboo Forest.
### LARGE +
- Sonnet is shown as a tall, fair woman with long pink hair and pink eyes, in white robes lined with purple and pink, with a pink crescent behind her and a laurel-like circlet. Everlasting As the Moon also gives her the title "Mistress of the Seven Hues".
- Sonnet was the one sister who spoke against the Heavenly Principles' new order, though she deferred when Aria and Canon chose to watch and wait.
- The dragons treated the sisters as traitors and cheered the moons' destruction.

## history
@version: 6.3
@spoiler: major
### LITE
Nibelung left, the sisters kept Teyvat for him, and then the Heavenly Principles arrived and were received as guests. After Nibelung returned and imprisoned the sisters, the Heavenly Principles shattered the moon. Sonnet fused the remains with the Abyss.
### STANDARD +
Sonnet was horrified at the Heavenly Principles' treatment of anyone who challenged them, and thought it excessive for the offences. After the Heavenly Principles overthrew the Seven Sovereigns, the sisters entered a precarious peace with them, which the dragons saw as betrayal. When Nibelung returned from the Abyss, the sisters disagreed about whether to take sides. He took it for disloyalty, sealed them in the moon's reflection and made their moons into weapons. The Heavenly Principles shattered the Iridescent and Eternal Moons and threw the Frost Moon beyond the false sky.

Sonnet stayed imprisoned in the reflection after her moon was shattered, ignorant of the war. In True Moon (6.3) a future Columbina reached her, and the truth shocked her. Of the three sisters she feared death most and looked to the others for comfort as the prison began to wear down her body. The sisters gave Columbina the authority of all three moons and asked to be sent back to Teyvat, mortal, where history records they died.
### LARGE +
Leaving the prison meant death, because a moon goddess cannot live without her moon. Seeing the shattered Iridescent Moon was an agony and a terror for Sonnet. To prolong her life she fused her fading soul with the Abyss (A Nation That Doesn't Exist). This kept her barely alive, in perpetual torment, and turned her into the Crimson Moon. Light Realm power and Abyssal power were mixed in her.

Her Marrow and the moon's husk then ended up sealed in an Abyssal domain, by means canon does not explain, so unlike Aria's remains they were never found in the overworld. Lauma says Sonnet's remains vanished in an instant.
### ULTRA +
The wiki pages disagree on how long before the disaster the sisters witnessed the union of the angel Koitar and the voyager Anacharsis (thirty days on the Aria and Sonnet pages, thirty years before Nibelung's return on the Three Moons page). The detail comes from artifact and book texts and the wiki's reading of them, not a quest scene, so no interval is asserted here.

## crimson_moon_era
@version: 6.1
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
Khaenri'ah's Crimson Moon Dynasty found the Crimson Moon and used it as a conduit to the Abyss. The domain holding its Marrow was sealed and could be opened only temporarily by a ritual. The dynasty's sages used the ritual in an induction ceremony in their otherworldly orphanage, branding the children with the Crimson Moon's light. Its power appears in the royal bloodline as the Balemoon Bloodfire, which is Sonnet's former power fused with Abyssal energies and can leave afterimages of whoever it burns. Hence the name "Two Worlds' Flames".
### ULTRA +
When the Eclipse Dynasty overthrew the Crimson Moon Dynasty, much knowledge of the era was lost or destroyed and the bloodline was purged under the Vinster King. It was never completely wiped out; survivors, including Tholindis and Frithr, entrusted pieces of the Crimson Moon to one another as a failsafe. See the Crimson Moon entry for the dynasty and bloodline.

## everlasting_as_the_moon
@version: 6.1
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
About 500 years after the Cataclysm, Columbina and the Traveler wanted the Iridescent Moon Marrow to restore Columbina's kuuvahki against the revived Rerir in Nod-Krai. Arlecchino, one of the last descendants of the Crimson Moon bloodline, used her Balemoon Bloodfire to open the path to the moon's burial grounds. Inside they found the broken husk hanging in the sky and the Abyss-corrupted Marrow in its reflection. The Traveler absorbed the Abyssal power and restored the Marrow to its pure essence.
### ULTRA +
Sonnet briefly regained lucidity. She spoke to Columbina like a long-lost friend, called her "Hypo-", thanked her for the rescue, said a sad farewell and died for good. Columbina, who had not yet met the sisters in the loop, supposed Sonnet had mistaken her similar aura for Canon's. As the moon's will faded, a dark presence in the domain awakened and tried to take the Marrow, and remnants rained like meteors, but with Arlecchino's help they escaped. Columbina used the Marrow to regain strength and, as agreed, gave it to Arlecchino, whose Crimson Moon blood made it fit her.

After Moon-Prayer Night, Dottore stole the Marrow and Columbina escaped into the moon's reflection (6.2). Dottore later became a false moon god using both Marrows and an artificial one, until Columbina, reborn as the Trilune Goddess, defeated him (6.3). The artificial marrow was destroyed and Columbina returned the Iridescent Marrow to Arlecchino.

## present_day
@version: 6.3
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
As of 7.1 the Iridescent Moon is a broken husk in a sealed Abyssal domain, Sonnet is dead, and its Marrow is held by Arlecchino. The shadowmoon illusion is gone and only the Frost Moon, returned under the false sky, lights the night.

### LARGE +
- The Mitternachts Waltz text says two of three bright moons were "shredded by a sword".

## entry: Iridescent Moon Marrow
@keys: Iridescent Moon Marrow, Iridescent Moon's marrow, Sonnet's marrow
@version: 6.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The Iridescent Moon Marrow was sealed in the Crimson Moon's domain, corrupted by the Abyss. The Traveler cleansed it (6.1) and Columbina gave it to Arlecchino. Dottore stole it, and after his defeat Columbina returned it to her again.

## entry: Balemoon Bloodfire
@keys: Balemoon Bloodfire, Balemoon, Two Worlds' Flames
@version: 6.1
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
The Balemoon Bloodfire is the form the Crimson Moon's power takes in the bloodline of Khaenri'ah's Crimson Moon Dynasty: Sonnet's old power mixed with Abyssal energy, able to leave afterimages of what it burns. Arlecchino used it to open the way to the moon's burial grounds.
