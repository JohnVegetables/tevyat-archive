## summary
### LITE
A Gnosis (plural Gnoses) is a gleaming object shaped like a chess piece that an Archon of The Seven carries inside their chest. There are seven, one per element. Venti calls it an Archon's internal focus that resonates directly with Celestia and marks them as one of The Seven. Gnoses amplify elemental power and symbolise the Heavenly Principles' control over Teyvat. Since the Prologue the Tsaritsa's Fatui have been collecting them; as of 7.1 she holds six, and only the Pyro Gnosis is missing.
### STANDARD +
In Chinese and Japanese the name means "Heart of God", paired with Visions as the "Eye of God". Their origin was kept secret for millennia: they were made from the body of the Third Descender, Saarelainen (revealed in stages in 4.2 and 7.1).

## established
### LITE
- An Archon does not need a Gnosis to use their element or keep their divine throne. Ei gave hers away centuries ago and is still the Electro Archon.
- A Gnosis can be torn out by force (Signora from Venti) or handed over willingly (Zhongli, Yae Miko, Neuvillette, Mavuika).
### STANDARD +
- Venti calls Visions "primitive tools" next to a Gnosis, which he describes as "a higher-order nexus of elemental manipulation". Mavuika calls it "basically an Elemental energy enhancer" that she rarely used. Nahida saw it as a core that gathers elemental power at her will.
- A Gnosis resists tampering: Ei tried and failed to modify hers into an energy supply device.
- Someone other than its own Archon normally struggles to use a Gnosis. Dottore told Nahida she could not fight with the Electro Gnosis. Still, Nahida used it to reach Greater Lord Rukkhadevata's consciousness, and the Wanderer could wield lightning only through heavy modifications made with Dottore and the Akademiya.
- A Gnosis can hold its bearer's obsessions. When Nahida pulled the Electro Gnosis out of Shouki no Kami, the Balladeer fell from the machine in despair.
### LARGE +
- Archons may keep some sense of their Gnosis after giving it away: Mavuika "sensed its loss" when the Pyro Gnosis vanished.
- Beings partly outside the Heavenly Principles' laws, such as Neuvillette and the Traveler, have handled Gnoses without obvious trouble. The Traveler carried the Pyro Gnosis through Sumeru's barrier when it sank into their body "just like phlogiston".
- Nahida warned Dottore that destroying a Gnosis might wake the Heavenly Principles. Canon has not shown what would happen.

## origin
@version: 7.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Origin, as canon now tells it. Neuvillette's character story (4.1) says the "usurper", the Heavenly Principles, was badly wounded in the war against Nibelung and could no longer suppress the world's original order by its own authority. With "one who came after" it created the seven Gnoses to keep that order, and the gods were driven to fight over them. In Finale (4.2), Skirk tells Neuvillette the Gnoses are remains of the Third Descender and calls them objects of misfortune, warning that once a person dies their bonds to the world turn into curses.
### LARGE +
Black Feathers Scattered at Parting (7.1) gives the full account, told by Rhinedottir to the Tsaritsa from Celestia's records. Incited by the Belyi Tsar, Anastasya and the Third Descender, Saarelainen, opened a gate to the Abyss. The Heavenly Principles erased Anastasya from existence. Saarelainen went up to Celestia and offered his own life: he had an unmatched affinity for the Human Realm's power and could amplify the elements. He agreed to let his remains be split into seven pieces to consolidate the Heavenly Principles' rule. In return they would bring Anastasya back and never harm his descendants, the Khaenri'ahns. He bound the promise with a curse that would only work if it were broken. The Heavenly Principles also required that he be forgotten, and erased him from Irminsul.
### ULTRA +
Anastasya concludes, and Rhinedottir does not dispute, that the Cataclysm in Khaenri'ah triggered this curse, which is why the Heavenly Principles have slept for five centuries. Earlier, in Light and Shadow, Intertwined (7.1), the Tsaritsa had told the Traveler that the Gnoses come from the Third Descender rather than from Teyvat's own system, so used to the full they could let a bearer go beyond the limits of their authority. Fatui research also links Stellar Linchpins to Saarelainen's remains.

## holders
### LITE
As of 7.1: the Cryo Gnosis is the Tsaritsa's own. Signora took the Anemo and Geo Gnoses; Dottore got the Electro and Dendro; Arlecchino brought the Hydro. The Pyro Gnosis burned with Irminsul in 6.6 and has vanished.
### LARGE +
- Anemo: Signora tore it from Venti's chest outside the Cathedral in Mondstadt (Prologue, 1.0).
- Geo: Zhongli handed it to Signora after his Rite of Parting, fulfilling a secret contract with the Tsaritsa, his "Contract to End All Contracts" (1.1). What she gave him in return is unrevealed.
- Electro: Ei left it with Yae Miko before retreating into the Plane of Euthymia. In 2.1 Miko traded it to Scaramouche (the Balladeer) for the Traveler's safety. Scaramouche built it into Shouki no Kami. Nahida took it from him in 3.2, then gave it to Dottore in exchange for his erasing his other segments.
- Dendro: Nahida gave her own Gnosis to Dottore in exchange for information about the false sky (3.2).
- Hydro: Focalors passed it to Neuvillette as her divinity faded. Neuvillette, now wary of it after Skirk's warning, gave it to Arlecchino as a "diplomatic gift" (4.2).
- Pyro: Capitano dueled Mavuika for it but then asked to be relieved of that mission, and the Tsaritsa agreed (5.3). In 6.6 Mavuika gave it to the Traveler for Nahida, who used it to burn the corrupted Irminsul.

## uses
### LARGE
Archons have used their Gnoses as power sources for national infrastructure.
- Liyue: the Golden House minted the world's Mora with the Geo Archon's power and has been shut since Zhongli gave up the Geo Gnosis.
- Sumeru: the Dendro Gnosis powered the Akasha, which Nahida shut down after taking back control (3.2).
- Fontaine: the Hydro Gnosis powered the Oratrice Mecanique d'Analyse Cardinale. Focalors used its stored energy to execute herself and destroy the Hydro throne (4.2).
### ULTRA +
- Natlan: Mavuika revealed the Pyro Gnosis could save Natlan from Abyssal corrosion, but only by draining every soul and memory in the Night Kingdom into a net around the Ley Lines, erasing Natlan's history (5.1). Ayizu had urged her to use it and she always refused; on his deathbed he told Capitano the secret.
- Sumeru again: Nahida used the power of the Electro and Dendro Gnoses together to reach the Irminsul consciousness from five hundred years before (3.2), and in 6.6 the Pyro Gnosis set Irminsul's roots ablaze. Per Nahida, the fire burned for seven days and nights.
- Snezhnaya: in 7.1 the Tsaritsa placed her six Gnoses as nodes around a hall of her palace. Their amplification of her Cryo let her slow time inside it, stalling Rhinedottir until the window she and Ronova had agreed for resurrecting Ronova's victims had passed. Celestia then took back Ronova's authority. Rhinedottir called this power beyond what a god should have; the Tsaritsa credited her own insight as well.

## accounts
### LITE
Most mortals know little or nothing about Gnoses. Venti said his was not something he was meant to discuss with ordinary people, and rank-and-file Fatui seem unaware of the Harbingers' mission.
### LARGE +
Paimon describes Gnoses as what keeps The Seven connected to Celestia. Yae Miko said Ei had severed her ties with Celestia by giving hers up. Even so, canon treats Ei as the Electro Archon, Focalors's destruction of the Hydro throne left the Hydro Gnosis unharmed, and Nahida was never called anything but the Dendro Archon while holding other Gnoses. Zhongli told Azhdaha he was no longer the Geo Archon after losing his Gnosis, and Azhdaha said he could sense it. Canon leaves open how far Gnosis, throne and title depend on each other.
### ULTRA +
The Balladeer as Shouki no Kami says strife is "engraved upon every god and every Gnosis", which Nahida takes to mean the Archon War. Skirk sees them as cursed. The Tsaritsa sees them as tools of survival and rebellion: she wants them to fight the Heavenly Principles, whose Shades and Nails of Retribution threaten Snezhnaya, and to open the Third Descender's seal.

## open_questions
### STANDARD
- Where is the Pyro Gnosis? The Tsaritsa expects the Ley Lines to cast it out eventually.
- What is sealed beneath Zapolyarny Palace, and will it open with all seven Gnoses?
- What did the Tsaritsa give Zhongli for the Geo Gnosis?
### LARGE +
- How exactly do the Gnoses maintain the Heavenly Principles' order, and what would happen if one were destroyed or all seven were gathered?
- Is Saarelainen's remaining power or will still present in the Gnoses? Canon hints at a link between them and Stellar Linchpins but explains nothing more.

## connections
### STANDARD
The Seven and their divine thrones (Archon Authority is separate from the Gnosis); Visions (Neuvillette's story places their appearance in the same era, and Venti calls them primitive by comparison); the Tsaritsa's plan and the Fatui Harbingers; the Third Descender and the Khaenri'ahn bloodline; the dormancy of the Heavenly Principles.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay interpretation (this project's recommendation, not canon text): treat Gnoses as state secrets. Ordinary people, soldiers and most Fatui agents should not know what one is, let alone where they are. Archons speak of them guardedly. Mavuika and Zhongli were relaxed about parting with theirs, while the Wanderer was desperate to have one.
### LARGE +
In scenes set after 7.1, the Tsaritsa holds six Gnoses in Zapolyarny Palace, no Archon carries their own except her, and nobody knows where the Pyro Gnosis is. Do not return Gnoses to Venti, Zhongli, Nahida or anyone else, and do not show an Archon losing their element without one.

## entry: Gnoses on the chessboard
@keys: Winter Night's Lazzo, Gnosis chess, Gnosis chess piece
@version: 2.8
### LARGE
In the official teaser "A Winter Night's Lazzo", made for Signora's funeral in the Sanctuary of Grief, Pierro plays chess against an unseen opponent whose pieces move by themselves, implied to be the Tsaritsa. The Anemo and Geo Gnoses stand on the board as pieces. Later Snezhnaya trailers such as "Sudden Snow" use more Gnoses the same way. Which piece each represents is fan analysis, not canon. In Sumeru Dottore called the Gnosis "another chess piece".

## entry: Pyro Gnosis disappearance
@keys: Pyro Gnosis
@version: 7.1
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
In 6.6 Mavuika gave the Pyro Gnosis to the Traveler. At the edge of Sumeru's barrier it sank into their body, and Nahida drew it out during the battle against Dottore. It burst into flame that consumed the corrupted Irminsul. Afterwards Nahida and Mavuika found no trace of it, not even ash, and Mavuika felt it had gone. In 7.1 the Traveler told the Tsaritsa it had burned and, per Mavuika, returned to the Ley Lines. The Tsaritsa doubted that remains of the Third Descender could coexist with the Ley Lines for long, and expects it to resurface. Its fate is open.

## entry: The Third Descender's bargain
@keys: Saarelainen, Third Descender
@keys2: Gnosis, Gnoses
@version: 7.1
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
Rhinedottir's account (7.1): Saarelainen let the Heavenly Principles split his remains into the seven Gnoses. In exchange they revived the erased Anastasya and swore never to harm the Khaenri'ahns, a promise guarded by a curse. Every memory of him was then erased from Irminsul. Only the Belyi Tsar, who had fled into the Shadow Realm, remembered. Anastasya does not remember Saarelainen and says she chooses to live in the present.

## entry: Third Descender's seal
@keys: Third Descender's seal, seal of the Third Descender
@version: 7.1
@spoiler: major
### ULTRA
Beneath Zapolyarny Palace lies a seal said to be the last thing the Third Descender left before his death. The Belyi Tsar guarded it first, and the Heavenly Principles seem never to have noticed it. Every attempt to open it has failed, and only a Gnosis seems to make it respond. The Tsaritsa believes it may open once the Descender's remains are no longer scattered, which gives her a second reason to collect all seven. She showed it to the Traveler in 7.1.
