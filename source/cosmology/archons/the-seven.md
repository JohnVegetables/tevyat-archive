## summary
### LITE
The Seven (the Seven Archons) are the gods who each preside over one of Teyvat's seven nations and one element. They are the victors of the Archon War, which ended about 2,000 years ago. Each Archon holds a divine throne in Celestia and was given a Gnosis, a chess-piece-like focus. Each also embodies an ideal that shapes their nation. As of 7.1: Venti (Barbatos, Anemo, Freedom, Mondstadt), Zhongli (Morax, Geo, Contracts, Liyue), Raiden Ei (Electro, Eternity, Inazuma), Nahida (Lesser Lord Kusanali, Dendro, Wisdom, Sumeru), Mavuika (Pyro, War, Natlan) and the Tsaritsa, Anastasya (Cryo, Love, Snezhnaya). The Hydro throne (Justice, Fontaine) no longer exists.
### STANDARD +
Only Barbatos and Morax remain from the original Seven; the other seats have changed hands, most of them during the Cataclysm 500 years ago. The Tsaritsa's Fatui have taken the Gnoses one by one: as of 7.1 she holds six, and the seventh, the Pyro Gnosis, was lost when Irminsul burned.

## established
### STANDARD
- Archons are gods, not mortals with Visions. Venti calls Visions "primitive" next to a Gnosis.
- Each Archon's power over their element comes from their divine throne. The thrones hold part of the Authorities the Heavenly Principles took from the Seven Sovereigns, the elemental dragons.
- Archons can die. Their thrones then pass to a successor.
- Venti says an Archon's strength depends on how much they govern their land. He calls himself the weakest of The Seven because he refuses to rule Mondstadt.
- The wiki also names faith as a source of power, alongside rule over a nation and the throne's Authority.
- Archons do not choose who gets a Vision and know nothing about the recipients. They are only obliged to give up part of their Authority to form one when notified to do so (Raiden Shogun's voice-overs, Neuvillette's character story).
- People in Teyvat commonly believe the Archons grant Visions.
### LARGE +
- Archons choose their successors, and most chose someone close to them: Ei was Makoto's younger twin, Nahida was created by Rukkhadevata from Irminsul's purest branch, and Focalors was one of Egeria's Oceanids.
- Pyro Archons are mortal humans who age and die. They win the throne through the Pilgrimage, and on ascending the Sacred Flame gives them much of the land's knowledge and memory.
- A divine throne can be destroyed. This takes enormous power and the death of the Archon seated on it, and the Authority inside returns to its Dragon Sovereign. Focalors did this in Fontaine (4.2).
### ULTRA +
- Many Archons had elemental power before ascending. Barbatos was a wind spirit, Morax was called the God of Geo long before he was Geo Archon, Ei calls herself an incarnation of lightning, and Rukkhadevata was an incarnation of Irminsul.
- Focalors is an exception: she gave up her Oceanid Hydro power to become a Fontainian human. Mavuika had a Pyro Vision before ascending and no longer wears one.
- Some Archons have remade bodies with divine power. Barbatos took the form of his dead friend, the Nameless Bard. Makoto reforged Ei's body after Ei gave hers up so Makoto could ascend. Rukkhadevata shrank to a child-like form each time she spent divine power against forbidden knowledge. Focalors split her divinity from her body, and the human left behind became Furina.
- Vessels an Archon makes have finite lives. Venti's body cannot be healed by ordinary means, including a Vision holder's healing.

## members
### STANDARD
Current holders of the six surviving thrones (7.1):
- Anemo, Mondstadt: Barbatos (Venti). God of Freedom, ascended 2,600 years ago.
- Geo, Liyue: Morax (Zhongli), called Rex Lapis. God of Contracts and eldest of The Seven. Retired as a mortal in 1.1.
- Electro, Inazuma: Beelzebul (Raiden Ei), who rules as the Raiden Shogun. God of Eternity, Archon since 500 years ago.
- Dendro, Sumeru: Buer (Nahida), Lesser Lord Kusanali. God of Wisdom and the youngest Archon, about 500 years old.
- Pyro, Natlan: Mavuika, holder of the shared divine name Haborym. God of War.
- Cryo, Snezhnaya: Vassaga (Anastasya), the Tsaritsa. God of Love, Archon since 500 years ago.
Hydro, Fontaine (Justice): Egeria, then Focalors. The throne was destroyed in 4.2, so there will be no more Hydro Archons.
### LARGE +
Predecessors:
- Electro: Makoto (Baal), who died in the Cataclysm. Ei served as her kagemusha, so Inazumans saw the twins as one Archon.
- Dendro: Greater Lord Rukkhadevata, who died in the Cataclysm and was later erased from Irminsul.
- Hydro: Egeria, the "Mistress of Many Waters", who died in the Cataclysm.
- Cryo: the Belyi Tsar, Monomakh Snezhnyi, who died in the Cataclysm.
- Pyro: a long line of mortal Archons, beginning with Xbalanque about 2,000 years ago.

Regnal titles: Rex Lapis (Morax); Raiden Shogun (Makoto, then Ei); Lesser Lord Kusanali (Nahida) and Greater Lord Rukkhadevata before her; "Regina of All Waters, Kindreds, Peoples and Laws" (the title Furina used while ruling in Focalors' name); Tsaritsa of All Snezhnaya (Anastasya) and Belyi Tsar before her. Pyro Archons inherit Ancient Names: Xbalanque's "Kiongozi" passed to Mavuika. Venti uses no regnal title. Neuvillette calls Barbatos "the God of Breeze and Hope." The divine names Baal and Beelzebul come from the 2.1 quest Wishes, Buer from the 3.2 Sumeru Archon Quest, and Vassaga from the 7.1 Snezhnaya Archon Quest.
### ULTRA +
Known Pyro Archons besides Xbalanque and Mavuika (all dead): an unnamed scarlet-eyed youth, Ndenge and Xumucul, an unnamed woman Venti knew personally, Cochanina, and Gloharry, Shulevitz and Isaja. Mavuika ascended before the Cataclysm, placed her life in the Sacred Flame 500 years ago, and has returned in the present. Every Pyro Archon carries the divine name Haborym. Xbalanque, the first, was briefly resurrected in the body of Mausau during the Natlan Archon Quest before returning to the Night Kingdom.

## powers
### STANDARD
A divine throne gives an Archon authority over their element. A Gnosis, normally kept inside the Archon's chest, amplifies elemental power and links its holder directly to Celestia. Archons keep their elemental power and their thrones without a Gnosis.
### LARGE +
Gnoses can be pulled out by force, as Signora did to Venti, or handed over willingly, as Zhongli and Ei did. Those who are not Archons usually cannot use another element's Gnosis freely. Dottore said Nahida could not fight with the Electro Gnosis, though she used it to enter Rukkhadevata's realm of consciousness and later used the Pyro Gnosis to burn Irminsul. The Wanderer could wield the Electro Gnosis only after heavy modification by Dottore and the Akademiya. Neuvillette and the Traveler have each used a Gnosis briefly without that limit.

Archons have used their Gnoses to power national infrastructure:
- Liyue: Morax's power minted Mora at the Golden House, which has stood idle since he gave up the Geo Gnosis.
- Sumeru: the Dendro Gnosis powered the Akasha System.
- Fontaine: the Hydro Gnosis powered the Oratrice Mecanique d'Analyse Cardinale, which was secretly merged with Focalors.
- Natlan: Mavuika kept the Pyro Gnosis in reserve and used it to sustain the Sacred Flame during the Abyss's attack.
Ei tried and failed to turn the Electro Gnosis into an energy source for her puppets. Gnoses, like Visions, can hold their bearer's obsessions, and the Wanderer lost his ambition when his Gnosis was taken.

## gnosis_amplification
@version: 7.1
@spoiler: major
### ULTRA
With six Gnoses, the Tsaritsa can push her Cryo power far enough to affect "the bounds of Reason", as Rhinedottir puts it. In 7.1 she slowed time in a hall of Zapolyarny Palace to stall Rhinedottir. Earlier she told the Traveler that the Gnoses do not originate within Teyvat's system but from the Third Descender, and that, exploited to the fullest, they could let a bearer transcend the limits of their own authority. She says the power of The Seven "can create a storm" but cannot shake the foundations of the world.

## history
### STANDARD
After the war of funerary flame about 6,000 years ago, the Heavenly Principles set up seven divine thrones in Celestia and had gods fight for them and their Gnoses. This was the Archon War. Many gods died. The last seat was claimed about 2,000 years ago, and the victors founded the seven nations. The original Seven were fairly close and shared the duty of guiding humanity; they used to gather in Liyue to drink with Rex Lapis.
### LARGE +
Different regions settled their wars at different times. Morax held Liyue from its founding 3,700 years ago. Barbatos rose after Decarabian fell 2,600 years ago. Makoto, Egeria, Xbalanque and the Belyi Tsar took their seats around 2,000 years ago. Natlan's gods never took part, because the land was consumed by war between dragons and humans; Xbalanque became the first Pyro Archon by defeating the Pyro Sovereign Xiuhcoatl. In Fontaine, Celestia ordered Egeria to succeed the fallen Remus.

The Cataclysm 500 years ago, when Khaenri'ah fell and the Abyss spread, killed Makoto, Rukkhadevata, Egeria and the Belyi Tsar. Ei, Nahida, Focalors and Anastasya replaced them. Mavuika placed her life in the Sacred Flame so she could return 500 years later. Venti says the Tsaritsa changed after that catastrophe; he knew her well 500 years ago but has not seen her since. She cut ties with the other Archons.

Afterwards, Ei built the Shogun puppet, finished about 400 years ago, gave her Gnosis to Yae Miko and withdrew into the Plane of Euthymia. Nahida was confined in the Sanctuary of Surasthana for 500 years while the sages promoted Rukkhadevata's legacy. Focalors split from her body, leaving Furina to play the Hydro Archon for 500 years. Anastasya took in Pierro and founded the Fatui, whose aims are to collect all seven Gnoses and rebel against Celestia.

Losses of the Gnoses, in story order:
- 1.0: Signora tore the Anemo Gnosis from Venti.
- 1.1: Zhongli handed the Geo Gnosis to Signora under a contract with the Tsaritsa.
- 2.1: Yae Miko traded the Electro Gnosis to Scaramouche for the Traveler's safety.
- 3.2: Nahida recovered the Electro Gnosis from Scaramouche. Dottore then received the Electro Gnosis in exchange for erasing his segments, as Nahida demanded, and the Dendro Gnosis in exchange for information about the false sky.
- 4.2: Focalors destroyed the Hydro throne, and Neuvillette gave the Hydro Gnosis to Arlecchino as a "diplomatic gift".
- 5.x: Capitano failed to seize the Pyro Gnosis from Mavuika by force, acknowledged his defeat and abandoned that mission.
- 6.6: The Traveler took the Pyro Gnosis from Mavuika so Nahida could burn the corrupted Irminsul, and it disappeared.

## rukkhadevata_erasure
@version: 3.2
@spoiler: major
### ULTRA
In the Sumeru Archon Quest (3.2) Rukkhadevata was erased from Irminsul. In the rewritten history, Nahida has been the Dendro Archon since the Archon War ended and is one of the original Seven, imprisoned after losing her memories in the Cataclysm. Only the Traveler remembers the original history. Even in Zhongli's revised character story, Barbatos remains the second eldest.

## gnosis_origin
@version: 7.1
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
The 7.1 Archon Quest revealed where the Gnoses came from. The war of funerary flame left the Heavenly Principles too weakened to enforce their order directly. The Third Descender, Saarelainen, was Anastasya's lover and an ancestor of the Khaenri'ahns. After the Heavenly Principles erased Anastasya, he let his body be split into the seven Gnoses. In return they resurrected her and promised never to harm their descendants, and his curse sends them into slumber if they break that promise. They harmed the Khaenri'ahns in the Cataclysm and have been dormant since. The Gnoses were used to consolidate the Archon system, and Visions, which derive from it, are linked to him too. All memory of Saarelainen was removed from Irminsul, so Anastasya does not remember him.
### ULTRA +
Earlier hints: in Fontaine (4.2) Skirk warned Neuvillette that Gnoses are objects of "misfortune" with a grim history. Anastasya kept watch over a seal the Third Descender left under Zapolyarny Palace, which the Belyi Tsar guarded before her. Only a Gnosis seems to affect it, which is her second reason for wanting all seven.

## status
@version: 7.1
@spoiler: major
### LITE
As of 7.1, six thrones remain, since the Hydro throne is gone. All six current Archons are alive. The Tsaritsa holds six Gnoses (Cryo, Anemo, Geo, Electro, Dendro, Hydro). The Pyro Gnosis is missing.
### LARGE +
- Venti lives freely in Mondstadt.
- Zhongli lives as a consultant at Wangsheng Funeral Parlor in Liyue, which now governs itself.
- Ei co-rules Inazuma with the Shogun puppet.
- Nahida rules Sumeru openly.
- Mavuika leads a recovering Natlan.
- Furina lives as an ordinary Fontainian, and Neuvillette, the Hydro Dragon, remains Chief Justice.
- Anastasya rules Snezhnaya directly and in 7.1 cooperated with the Traveler against Ronova.
On the missing Pyro Gnosis, the Tsaritsa says that, being formed from the Third Descender's remains, it cannot coexist with the Ley Lines for long and will one day be excised from them.

Most of The Seven are known to the Traveler personally, but few ordinary people know that Venti, Zhongli or Nahida is a god. Ei and Nahida are publicly worshipped. In Liyue, the public believes Rex Lapis died at the 1.1 Rite of Descension.

## accounts
### LARGE
- Venti, in Ending Note (1.0): The Seven "don't always get along well", but he never expected the Tsaritsa to plot against another Archon. He says a Gnosis marks an Archon's status as one of The Seven.
- Zhongli, in his character story: Rex Lapis remembers the moment the last Archon took their seat. The Seven were scattered and diverse but all bore the burden of guiding humanity, and many titles have since changed hands.
- Ganyu: the youngest current Archon is Sumeru's god of Dendro, "merely five hundred years old."
### ULTRA +
- The Tsaritsa (7.1): the gods' love for humanity is a curse carved into them by the Heavenly Principles, so she does not identify with her own authority, though when asked she names it as "love".
- Dainsleif's chapter preview calls her a "god with no love left".
- In We Will Be Reunited (1.x), the Traveler's sibling, who leads the Abyss Order, refused to come home and told the Traveler they would see the truth about the world on their journey.

## open_questions
### LARGE
Whether the Archon title depends on a Gnosis; what a Gnosis does for the Heavenly Principles; where the Pyro Gnosis is now; what the Third Descender's seal holds. Canon leaves all of these open as of 7.1, and none should be resolved in roleplay.
### ULTRA +
Also open: whether Fontaine will ever have another elemental god (canon says no further Hydro Archons); and how Celestia will react to the Tsaritsa holding six Gnoses, now that Ronova lost her Authority and a Celestial Nail began to crumble in 7.1.

## connections
### LARGE
Separate entries cover each Archon, each Gnosis, the Archon War, Celestia, the Heavenly Principles, the Seven Sovereigns, Visions, and the Statues of The Seven (the Archons' roadside statues, where the Traveler resonates with each element).
### ULTRA +
The name follows Gnostic myth, in which seven archons rule the material world under a false creator, and gnosis is the knowledge that frees people from them; a developer interview (PASH, May 2021) named Gnosticism as the game's main mythological basis. Most divine names come from demons of the Ars Goetia: Barbatos, Morax, Baal, Buer and Haborym directly; Focalors and Vassaga in altered form.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay interpretation (this project's recommendation, not canon text): Archons are rarely recognised in person. Venti, Zhongli and Nahida move among people unnoticed, and many people know their god only from statues, temples and stories. Keep them distinct: each embodies a different ideal and governs differently. Venti refuses to rule, Zhongli gave up rule, Ei and the Shogun guard Eternity, Nahida rules gently, Mavuika leads in war, and Anastasya rules absolutely. When elemental power runs through an Archon, the ends of their hair glow in their element's colour; Mavuika's whole hair lights up.
### LARGE +
Ordinary people believe the Archons hand out Visions; a scholar or an Archon may know otherwise. No one but the Traveler remembers Rukkhadevata. Fontaine has no living Hydro Archon, and Furina should not be treated as a god. Do not have Venti or Zhongli get their Gnoses back. Do not invent a council of Archons or friendships canon does not show: as of 7.1 the Tsaritsa has had no contact with Venti for 500 years.
### ULTRA +
The Archons' trouble with Mora is a running joke. Venti has listeners buy him drinks instead of paying him, and Zhongli never checked prices because his Gnosis could make Mora. Ei has the Tri-Commission bring her whatever she needs, and Nahida struggles to price things after 500 years of confinement. Mavuika breaks the pattern and is careful with her nation's scarce resources.

## entry: Divine Thrones of The Seven
@keys: divine throne, divine thrones, Archon's throne
@keys2: Archon, Archons, Celestia, The Seven, Authority
@version: 4.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Celestia holds seven divine thrones, one per element, each containing part of an Authority the Heavenly Principles took from the Seven Sovereigns. Neuvillette says the First Usurper "seized a part of the dragons' power" and that it is now the basis of the Archons' Authorities (4.1). A throne passes to a successor when its Archon dies. Destroying a throne takes immense power and the seated Archon's death, and returns the Authority to its dragon. Focalors destroyed the Hydro throne this way in 4.2, so only six remain.
### LARGE +
Before the Archon War ended, phrases like "divine throne" did not necessarily mean these Celestial seats. Decarabian's death, for example, is called the crumbling of "an ancient seat of divinity."

## entry: The original Seven
@keys: original Seven, first Seven, first iteration of The Seven
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The first Seven were Barbatos, Morax, Baal (Makoto), Rukkhadevata, Egeria, Xbalanque and the Belyi Tsar. They took their seats at different times, ending about 2,000 years ago, and would gather in Liyue to drink with Rex Lapis. As of 7.1 only Barbatos and Morax survive. Morax is the eldest and Barbatos the second eldest. Ei fought through the Archon War beside Makoto but only became Archon after her twin's death.
### LARGE +
Because of the 3.2 erasure, the world believes Nahida was one of the first Seven and does not remember Rukkhadevata.

## entry: Hydro Archon's end
@keys: Hydro Archon, Hydro throne
@version: 4.2
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Egeria became Hydro Archon about 2,000 years ago, after Remus fell, and died in the Cataclysm. Her Oceanid Focalors succeeded her, split her divinity from her body, and left that body, Furina, to play the Hydro Archon for 500 years. In Masquerade of the Guilty (4.2) Focalors had herself executed by the Oratrice, which destroyed the throne and returned the full Hydro Authority to Neuvillette, the Hydro Sovereign. Furina abdicated and lives as an ordinary Fontainian. Neuvillette gave the Hydro Gnosis, unaffected by the throne's destruction, to Arlecchino.

## entry: Pyro Archons and the Pilgrimage
@keys: Pyro Archon, Pyro Archons, Haborym
@version: 5.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Natlan's Archons are mortals who age and die. Each new Pyro Archon wins the seat through the Pilgrimage, gains the Sacred Flame's knowledge on ascending, and carries the divine name Haborym. Xbalanque, the first, defeated the Pyro Sovereign Xiuhcoatl and established Natlan's system. The current Pyro Archon, Mavuika, placed her life in the Sacred Flame 500 years ago so she could return to finish the war against the Abyss, and she has returned.
### LARGE +
Capitano's attempt to take her Gnosis ended without a result, and the Fatui never obtained it. Mavuika later gave it, through the Traveler, to Nahida to burn Irminsul (6.6), and it was lost. She said she sensed its loss even after handing it away.

## entry: The Tsaritsa's Gnosis collection
@keys: six Gnoses, Gnosis collection, collecting the Gnoses
@version: 7.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The Fatui have collected Gnoses for the Tsaritsa since 1.0. As of 7.1 she holds six: her own Cryo Gnosis, plus the Anemo, Geo, Electro, Dendro and Hydro Gnoses. Before Signora's funeral (teaser "A Winter Night's Lazzo"), she played chess with Pierro with the Anemo and Geo Gnoses on the board as pieces. In 7.1 she told the Traveler her two reasons: to amplify her power beyond a single Archon's against the Heavenly Principles, and to open the Third Descender's seal. She asked the Traveler where the Pyro Gnosis was, the only one unaccounted for.
### LARGE +
In Black Feathers Scattered at Parting (7.1), the Tsaritsa combined her Cryo mastery with the six Gnoses to slow time around Rhinedottir over tea. This made Rhinedottir miss the window to revive Ronova's victims, and Celestia stripped Ronova of her Authority. Rhinedottir chose not to fight her and called her "quite a worthy rival".
