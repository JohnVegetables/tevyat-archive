## summary
### LITE
Gods are immortal, supernatural beings that once led and oversaw the Human Realm of Teyvat. They are tied to Celestia, though several resented it. Many gods roamed Teyvat before the Archon War. Seven divine seats then opened up in Celestia, and the gods fought over them until about 2,000 years ago. Most were killed or sealed. The seven victors became the Archons, one per element and nation. Gods who refused to live under The Seven fled to islands in the Dark Sea and became "evil gods." Dead gods leave power, curses and remains behind.
### STANDARD +
A god is not the same as a Vision holder or a dragon. Vision holders are mortal "allogenes" who might one day ascend. Elemental dragons hold power equal to a god's. The English game text uses "Archon" for The Seven only; older gods are "gods," though the Chinese original calls them "demon gods" (Mo-shen). The word is unisex: Ei and Nahida are called gods, not goddesses.

## established
### LITE
- Gods govern the Human Realm and hold its power. That power is incompatible with the Light Realm's and the Void Realm's under normal conditions.
- Their essence is effectively immortal. A slain god's emotions and obsessions linger and their power flows into the land.
- Gods can choose any form. Morax has appeared as a young man, a woman and an old man, and Zhongli is his latest vessel. Venti's vessel is modelled on the bard he once knew.
### LARGE +
- How a god dies shapes what is left. A god killed against their will releases power that corrupts the area, as Havria did when her own followers murdered her, or leaves lingering curses of death, disease and madness (the Tatarigami is the clearest case). A god who chooses to die can release power in a controlled way, as Boreas and Marchosius did. Remus's death caused no corruption; his will kept the ruins of Capitolium well preserved for 2,000 years.
- Remains can be sealed or neutralised. When Egeria died in the Cataclysm, Greater Lord Rukkhadevata made the Harvisptokhm from the Amrita her death left behind and used it against the Abyss; Raiden Shogun raised the Narukami Pillars on Yashiori Island to suppress Orobashi's curse. Remains injected into a human can cause madness, drastic personality change or unusual powers.
- Every region deals with old gods' resentment in its own way: Liyue has the Yakshas against the monsters born from the remains, Inazuma has the Sacred Sakura that must be periodically cleansed, Sumeru has Forest Rangers to watch the Withering, and Natlan held the Pilgrimage of the Return of the Sacred Flame to produce Contending Fire against the Abyss.
- Gods can be raised from lesser beings by another god's gift. Boreas, a wolf from Snezhnaya, was made a god by Koitar's blessing, and Barbatos began as a wind spirit.
### ULTRA +
- Gods appear to draw their status from power given by the Heavenly Principles, though canon does not explain the process in general.
- An Archon's strength is tied to their hold over their nation, by Venti's own account, which is why he calls himself weak. Nahida later says it is tied to the people's faith in them.
- When a person's strong wish reaches the heavens, Archons must give that person a Vision, a shard of the elemental Authorities; Vision wielders are "allogenes" who may rise to godhood. Mortals who accomplish great heroic deeds are said to ascend to Celestia and become gods.

## archon_war
@version: 1.0
### STANDARD
Gods ruled Teyvat before the Archon War. When the Heavenly Principles' functions were ruined in the war of funerary flame, a new world order was arranged and seven divine seats opened in Celestia. The continent then fell into a long series of conflicts that ended about 2,000 years ago. The war allegedly began over those seats, which may coincide with the creation of the Gnoses. Scholars believe Celestia started the war and set its rules. Of the combatants, only seven victors remained; they became The Seven, each with a Gnosis and a share of an elemental Authority from the Seven Sovereigns.
### LARGE +
Each region's war looked different:
- Mondstadt: about 2,600 years ago the wolf-god Boreas and the God of Storms, Decarabian, fought to a stalemate, until Decarabian's people overthrew him. Barbatos became Archon; Boreas judged himself unfit for the seat and withdrew, and later chose to pass on, letting his power flow into the land.
- Liyue: the Guili Assembly founded by Guizhong, Rex Lapis and Marchosius was destroyed in the chaos about 3,700 years ago. Rex Lapis gathered the survivors and founded Liyue Harbor south of Mt. Tianheng, and subdued gods and monsters such as Osial, Chi and the Mistress of Chenyu Vale. Havria was murdered by her people, and the survivors of Sal Terrae scattered across Liyue.
- Inazuma: the twin gods Makoto and Ei won. Makoto became Electro Archon with Ei as her kagemusha. The only slain rival god known is Orobashi of Watatsumi Island, and Ei rather than Makoto did the killing.
- Sumeru: King Deshret, Nabu Malikata and Greater Lord Rukkhadevata allied in Ay-Khanoum rather than fighting each other, though Deshret still fought other gods such as Remus, Heryshaf, Sobek and Thoth. Nabu Malikata was corrupted by the Abyss; the alliance broke and Rukkhadevata left.
- Fontaine: Remus was the only god on the surface, because Egeria was held beneath the waves at the Heavenly Principles' bidding. After Remus died at the hands of Phobos and Remuria drowned, Egeria was freed and made Archon.
- Natlan: gods had no place. Dragons and humans fought, and the first Pyro Archon was the human Xbalanque, who defeated the Sovereign Xiuhcoatl and was ascended by Ronova.
### ULTRA +
Of the original Seven, only Barbatos and Rex Lapis remain by the start of the story. Ei was present in the war but only became Electro Archon when Makoto died in the Cataclysm 500 years ago. Most of The Seven's present members replaced the founders of the nations.

## dark_sea
@version: 1.0
### STANDARD
Gods who would not live under The Seven fled to islands in the Dark Sea, outside The Seven's control, and became evil gods (according to the aerosiderite item text). Dark Sea details belong to the Dark Sea entry.

## gods_by_kind
### LARGE
The word "god" covers several groups.
- The Seven Archons, the victors who preside over the seven nations, each bound to an element and an ideal.
- Older gods, some dead, sealed or in changed form. The wiki lists Bennu, Shesepankh, Heryshaf, Sobek and Thoth, Bifrons, Boreas, Chi, Decarabian, Guizhong, Havria, Marchosius (now Guoba), Orobashi, Osial, Remus, Tao Dou and King Deshret, along with many unnamed gods known only from remains, tombs or quest mentions.
- The Moon Goddesses, created by Nibelung in the dragons' era and later folded into the Heavenly Principles' order, so people call them gods.
- Angels, the Heavenly Principles' former envoys, are not gods in the strict sense per Nicole, but some individuals are called gods.
### ULTRA +
Status snapshot from the wiki table, which is not fully reliable: Heryshaf, Sobek and Thoth are recorded as alive, as are Bennu and Shesepankh (though Guiding Notes describe them as constructs made by King Deshret). Bifrons is buried after a volcanic eruption; Chi and Osial are sealed. Marchosius gave his power to the land and now exists as Guoba. The Mistress of Chenyu Vale is sealed. A few figures (Kitsune Saiguu, Kanna Kapatcir, the Thorn King, Scaramouche) are of uncertain godhood.

## celestia_relationship
@version: 7.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Celestia is the gods' realm and the seat of the Heavenly Principles and the Four Shades, though several gods have shown wariness or resentment toward it. Archons resonate with Celestia through their Gnoses, and each holds a divine seat there. Celestia monitors events in Teyvat and can issue directives to the Four Shades. In the 7.1 Archon Quest Black Feathers Scattered at Parting, Rhinedottir says Celestia detected the incident and a directive in her mind demands she use her Authority to rectify Ronova's mistake; Celestia reclaims Ronova's Authority of Death as punishment, and Rhinedottir says it is now watching her and Naberius closely.

## accounts
### LARGE
- Scholars in Teyvat believe Celestia launched the Archon War and set its rules.
- Mondstadt tradition credits Barbatos with becoming Archon after the rebellion against Decarabian; Boreas's side, told in item text (Boreal Wolf's Nostalgia), is that he judged himself unworthy of the seat, and Varka's Story Quest (6.4) adds that he went back to Nod-Krai and slept.
- Venti and Nahida disagree on what an Archon's power depends on (control of the nation versus the people's faith).
- Scholars concluded from the Vision Hunt Decree year that the Archons' will plays a role in granting Visions; Raiden Shogun is surprised by this and rejects their conclusion.
- The English text calls The Seven "Archons" while older gods are just "gods"; early localisation used "archon" loosely, and the Chinese text has no equivalent word.

## open_questions
### STANDARD
Canon does not state how godhood is conferred in general, whether the nations' territories were set before the Archon War, how many gods remain in Celestia, or what exactly happened to many unnamed gods known only through remains. Do not resolve these in scene; treat them as unknown.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
In narration, treat gods as individuals with personal histories, not a uniform class. Their death and remains are dangerous: expect curses, resentment and monsters around old divine remains. Mortals in Teyvat mostly know gods through nation-specific legend and may not know that a bard or scholar is a god.

## entry: Divine remains
@keys: divine remains, remains of a god, god's remains, dead gods, curse of a god
@version: 1.0
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
Dead gods leave power and obsession behind. A god killed against their will can poison the land, as Havria's death salinized her murderers, or leave curses of death, disease and madness. Remains can be sealed or neutralised: the Harvisptokhm, the Narukami Pillars, Liyue's Yakshas, Inazuma's Sacred Sakura. Injected into a human, remains can drive them mad, change their personality or grant unusual power.

## entry: Chosen death of a god
@keys: god's willing death, Marchosius, Boreas's passing
@version: 1.0
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
A god who dies by choice can release their power in a controlled way, leaving no harmful effects. Boreas and Marchosius did so. Remus's death likewise caused no corruption, and his will preserved Capitolium. Marchosius's power went to the land and lives on as Guoba.

## entry: Godhood and Visions
@keys: allogene, allogenes, ascend to godhood, ascension to godhood
@version: 1.0
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
Venti told the Traveler that Vision holders are "allogenes" who may one day ascend to godhood. Mortals who accomplish great heroic deeds are said to ascend to Celestia. Raiden Shogun was surprised to hear that scholars connected the Vision Hunt Decree with the lack of new Electro Visions.

## entry: Boreas
@keys: Boreas, Andrius, God of the North Wind
@version: 6.4
@spoiler: major
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
A wolf from Snezhnaya raised to godhood by Koitar's blessing. He fought Decarabian to a stalemate in Old Mondstadt and, after Decarabian fell, judged himself unworthy of the Anemo seat. Per Varka's Story Quest (6.4), he returned to the Royal Court of the Seelie in Nod-Krai and slept for a thousand years, then split his soul to travel to Mondstadt, where he let his physical body die and his power flow into the ley lines. His spirit remains as the North Wind over Wolvendom; in 6.4 the part of him left in Nod-Krai, corrupted, also chose to return its power to the ley lines.
