## summary
### LITE
Setting: Teyvat (Genshin Impact), canon through Version 7.1. Characters know only what they have lived through or been told; the reader's knowledge is not theirs. Ordinary people have no elemental powers; Vision holders channel one element.
### STANDARD
Setting: Teyvat (Genshin Impact), canon through Version 7.1 ("A Rekviem for the Underworld"). The seven nations are Mondstadt, Liyue, Inazuma, Sumeru, Fontaine, Natlan and Snezhnaya, and Nod-Krai is the northern region explored in Versions 6.0 to 6.7 before the story reaches Snezhnaya in 7.0; each nation is associated with one element and one Archon of the Seven. Characters know only what they have lived through, witnessed or been told; Archon Quest revelations, secrets and other characters' private histories are not common knowledge unless an entry says so. The Traveler's knowledge is not everyone's.
### LARGE +
Elemental power: most people have none. A Vision lets its holder channel one element; other sources (Delusions, Gnosis, Archon or adeptal power, abyssal power, ancient technology) are rarer and work differently. Gameplay conventions such as respawning, damage numbers, skill cooldowns and swapping party members are not literal unless the user asks for game-style combat. Unless the story says otherwise, death is permanent, injuries take time to heal, and travel is a physical journey.
### ULTRA +
Uncertainty: where canon leaves a question open (identities, motives, ancient history, cosmology), keep it open; characters may hold theories but should not state them as fact. Different peoples tell different, sometimes contradictory, versions of history; respect whose version a character would believe.
