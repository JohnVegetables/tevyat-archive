## summary
### LITE
Emblem of Severed Fate is a 4 and 5 star Inazuma artifact set. Its five item texts tell, in fragments, of the half-oni swordsman Iwakura Michihiro, also called Douin, and of the black-winged tengu Teruyo of Yougou who gave him his new name and fought him in thirteen yearly duels. Mechanically it rewards Energy Recharge with Elemental Burst damage.
### STANDARD +
The set is dropped by the Momiji-Dyed Court domain on Yashiori Island. The texts read as memory and farewell: a mother lost to a failed bid for glory under the Shogun's banner, a son starting over under a new name, a tengu who cannot keep a promise, and a friend's broken seal cage. Several of the narrated people also appear in the Amethyst Series weapons Amenoma Kageuchi, Katsuragikiri Nagamasa and Hakushin Ring.
### LARGE +
Reading the five pieces together gives a compact tragedy in two strands. One is Michihiro's: disgrace of his house, exile in a village, renaming, duels, and the final strike that ends the duels and makes his career. The other is the Storm Cage's, a separate and more melancholy memory of a gambler, a seal cage and a promise that could not be kept, told by an unnamed narrator who is not Michihiro.

## appearance
### LARGE
Five pieces: Magnificent Tsuba (flower, an ornate sword hand guard said to belong to a blade given to an oni who betrayed the Shogun), Sundered Feather (plume, the black feather of a tengu warrior kept as a swordsman's souvenir), Storm Cage (sands, an inro-style seal cage with pansies on a black ground, inlaid shell and gold work), Scarlet Vessel (goblet, a finely made wine vessel once drunk from by a famous martial artist), and Ornate Kabuto (circlet, a sturdy noble samurai's helmet).

## lore
### LITE
The texts follow Michihiro, the son of an oni mother who lost her standing with the Shogun. After his family's fall he lived in seclusion; a black-winged tengu, Teruyo of Yougou, renamed him "Iwakura" (a seat of rock) to free him from the burden of oni blood and challenged him to yearly duels.
### STANDARD +
The pieces dwell on a fallen oni house, a renaming and a long rivalry: the mother of the Mikoshi line turned on the Shogun and was ruined; her son withdrew from the clan; the tengu renamed him Iwakura, and after thirteen years of duels he finally touched her, breaking his sword and her shrine. He then became the Kujou Clan's sword instructor, "Douin".
### LARGE +
Magnificent Tsuba: the mother, of the war-oni line, had been shown kindness by the Shogun and given a prized sword. She turned on the Shogun and ended in ruin, and only the sword's guard came back to the Mikoshi Clan. Her eldest son, who should have inherited, withdrew to a village outside the city. A tengu with black wings gave him the name Iwakura, told him that names given by the Yougou Tengu carry divine blessing, and set a duel for the next cherry-blossom season, promising that if he could touch her he could name his secret blade for a victory over a tengu.

Sundered Feather and Scarlet Vessel: after thirteen years of duels in the courtyard of a small branch shrine, Michihiro finally cut her black feathers and caught her. His sword broke from his own strength, and the shrine was wrecked. She said the tengu had duties to her clan, told him to forget her, and urged him to use his sword for the Iwakura line and make a path of his own. He became the Kujou Clan's sword instructor, earned the title "Douin" and founded a successful school, and later walked into the ruined branch shrine remembering her and the thirteen years that passed like a dream.

Ornate Kabuto is a brief exchange with a young head of the Kanjou Commission, Hiroshi, who built up Ritou, asking Douin whether his sword could cut lightning. Douin says at most it could strike a tengu from the air, though no tengu has ever been brought down. Hiroshi says he would have recruited him had the old Kujou not moved first, and that with such a sword even Ako Domeki of Seirai would be no match. The passage closes on Douin's memory of the tengu who gave him a new name and a new life, throwing him a rusty blade and telling him to strike her down, and the words she spoke to him after the blade broke.

Storm Cage is separate in tone. It describes a time before Seirai Island was wrapped in storm clouds, and a seal cage that holds thunderstorms and tremors and could not be delivered to the one it was promised to. A rough but affectionate exchange follows between the narrator and a gambling, wandering samurai who is also a Hatamoto and a married man; the narrator mended his broken seal cage, he offered to teach tengu archery, spoke of Teruyo of the shrine and of nightmares in which he cut off his own head, and took the narrator to watch ships in the harbour. Long afterward the narrator stood on the heights again, holding the cage she had made, gathering thunder in hope of retrieving those memories and praying for the gambler's safety. The text mentions "Lady Saiguu", linking it to the Kitsune Saiguu's circle.
### ULTRA +
Trivia and cross-references: the Amethyst weapon Amenoma Kageuchi (Michihiro's blade line, with his Tengu Sweeper technique said to need a heart without confusion), Katsuragikiri Nagamasa (belonging to Mikoshi Nagamasa, adoptive brother of Michihiro, though he is not named in this set), and Hakushin Ring (narrated by the Kitsune Saiguu) all intersect with these texts. The weapon text for Amenoma Kageuchi says the Tengu Sweeper was first performed in the courtyard of a small shrine where incense no longer burned, broke Douin's sword in two, and that the Iwakura Art grew from it.

## owners
### LARGE
No named owner holds the set. The pieces are described as belonging to or associated with Michihiro (hand guard, feather, vessel), the Kitsune Saiguu's circle (seal cage), and an unnamed noble samurai (helmet).

## gameplay
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
Gameplay, not lore: the 2-piece bonus is Energy Recharge +20%. The 4-piece bonus increases Elemental Burst damage by 25% of the wearer's Energy Recharge, up to 75%. The set drops from the Momiji-Dyed Court domain on Yashiori Island, and a strongbox variant exists. It first appeared in Version 2.0.

## entry: Iwakura Michihiro (Douin) in the artifact texts
@keys: Iwakura Michihiro, Douin, Tengu Sweeper, Iwakura Art
@version: 2.0
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
In the Emblem of Severed Fate texts, Michihiro was born into the Mikoshi line of war-oni, withdrew to a village after his mother's disgrace, and was renamed "Iwakura" by the tengu Teruyo to free him from his oni heritage. After thirteen years of duels he perfected the Tengu Sweeper, became the Kujou Clan's sword instructor with the title Douin, and founded a successful school. The weapon Amenoma Kageuchi, a copy of the sword Hakuen Michimitsu Amenoma, descends from his school; the weapon text says he gave a copy to the Saimon Clan for sheltering him in Konda Village.

## entry: Teruyo of Yougou
@keys: Teruyo, Teruyo of Yougou
@version: 2.0
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
A black-winged tengu who, in the set's texts, gave Michihiro the name Iwakura, fought him in yearly duels at a small branch shrine, and was finally touched by him after thirteen years. She told him tengu names carry blessings and that she had duties to her clan; she said goodbye and asked him to forget her. A line in the Storm Cage mentions "Teruyo of the shrine" inheriting her master's beauty and skill, but the set says no more of her.
