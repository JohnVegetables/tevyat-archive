## summary
### LITE
Maiden Beloved is a healing artifact set (4 and 5 star, added 1.0). Its pieces tell of a sheltered noblewoman rescued by a white knight, who fell in love with him and spent her years waiting for a man devoted to chivalry. She is the counterpart to the knight of the Bloodstained Chivalry set.
### STANDARD +
The pieces are Maiden's Distant Love (flower), Maiden's Heart-Stricken Infatuation (feather), Maiden's Passing Youth (sands), Maiden's Fleeting Leisure (goblet) and Maiden's Fading Beauty (circlet).

## appearance
### STANDARD
A maiden's keepsakes: a fresh, elegant flower of a species the books call extinct, an old delicate feathered ornament under layers of dust, a delicate timepiece, treasured teaware once filled with black tea, and a hat she never took off before her suitors.

## lore
### LITE
A maiden was saved by a white knight and gave her heart to him that day. He took only a white flower and went on his way; she waited for the rest of her life.
### STANDARD
Distant Love recalls the passage she read countless times, of the damsel saved by the white knight and the exchange of flowers as blessings; the flower stays constant because her heart stilled when they met. Heart-Stricken Infatuation says time stopped for her that day; nothing mattered, and she wandered looking for him like a bird without a nest, praying her love would reach him while he pursued chivalry in a distant land where ancient empires crumbled. Passing Youth shows the short hand chasing the long hand like her longing: her time would end, her waiting would not. Fleeting Leisure has her indulging in pastries and tea while recalling the knight saying that the flower would be his medal; her unfinished confession ('my heart is already...') was both her privilege and the chain of courtliness. Fading Beauty has her brush the dust off her hat each night and ignore the flowers of courters whose names and faces were not worth remembering, as fewer and fewer came.
### LARGE +
The set never says whether the knight knew her feelings or whether they met again. The wiki links the white knight to Roland and to Bloodstained Chivalry, where the same flower is his only reward and ends up dried and darkened with blood. Read together, one encounter became a symbol of purity for him and a whole life for her.

### ULTRA +
Per-piece details worth keeping: the Distant Love flower is described as a species the books say is extinct; the Fading Beauty hat is the object she brushes each night as the courters thin out; the Fleeting Leisure confession is the only direct quotation of her feelings and is left unfinished. The Heart-Stricken Infatuation text places the knight in a distant land where ancient empires crumbled, which the wiki links to the Cataclysm and Khaenri'ah; the set itself does not name either.

## owners
### STANDARD
No named owner; the maiden is anonymous and the pieces are her belongings.

## status
### STANDARD
The maiden is long gone and the texts leave her fate and name unstated. The set is background lore, unchanged by 6.x and 7.0-7.1.

## roleplay
### LARGE
Use for wistful, historical-register scenes. Do not name the maiden or write her knight as returning her feelings; the texts leave his side to the Bloodstained Chivalry set.

## gameplay
### LARGE
Gameplay (game mechanics, not in-world fact). A 4- and 5-star set from the Valley of Remembrance. 2-piece: Healing Bonus +15%. 4-piece: using an Elemental Skill or Burst raises healing received by all party members by 20% for 10s (in game terms Healing Bonus and Incoming Healing Bonus). The Adventurer Handbook gives a full 4-star set.
