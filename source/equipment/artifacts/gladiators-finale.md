## summary
### LITE
Gladiator's Finale is an ATK artifact set (4 and 5 star, 1.0) about the champion gladiator of aristocracy-era Mondstadt, a slave who nearly won his freedom, was undone by pride and a master's flattery, and fell to a young girl in the arena. The girl is Vennessa, the later founder of the Knights of Favonius. Its weapon companion is the polearm Deathmatch.
### STANDARD +
The pieces are Gladiator's Nostalgia (flower), Gladiator's Destiny (feather), Gladiator's Longing (hourglass), Gladiator's Intoxication (goblet) and Gladiator's Triumphus (helmet).

## appearance
### LARGE
A brooch-flower, a feather of a free bird, a hand-made hourglass, a gold goblet and a feathered gladiator's helmet.

## lore
### LITE
A slave gladiator rose to legend in the arena. His master flattered him into staying, and a girl defeated him in his last bout.
### STANDARD
Nostalgia shows the master giving a flower to his silent slave and making it a brooch; years later the master called such gifts whims. Destiny says he was one battle from freedom when a young girl defeated him but refused to execute him, and a bird left a feather on his chest. Longing is the hourglass he flipped before each fight; in his last fight the sand stopped. Intoxication is a gold cup from his master; Triumphus a helmet that made him a symbol of invincibility to the crowd.
### LARGE +
The pieces give conflicting endings. Destiny has the girl spare him so that he walks free as a bird. Longing says the girl's sword pierced his heart and the hourglass sand stopped. Intoxication and Triumphus argue that vanity and the master's care bound him; by lingering on victory he missed his chance to leave. Triumphus says he glimpsed the world outside his cell on the day of victory, but he was always only a worthless slave in his master's eyes.
### ULTRA +
The wiki reads the master as Eberhart, a noble of the aristocracy period, and the girl as Vennessa, a flame-touched slave gladiator of the Lawrence clan who later led the rebellion. It reads Destiny as a possible imagined ending. The Deathmatch weapon and Luther's expedition journal (which fears the gladiator's fate) are the other canon touches. The wiki also says that, as he died, he looked for Eberhart's seat and found it empty.

## owners
### LARGE
The unnamed gladiator owned the relics in the story; his master Eberhart gave the flower and cup.

## status
### LARGE
Both the gladiator and Eberhart's aristocracy-era world are long gone. Eberhart is recorded as the later mastermind behind the deaths of a Dragonspine expedition of young aristocrats; his ultimate fate is unknown.

## roleplay
### LARGE
Tone: tragic and moral, not glorifying. A character who knows the tale uses it as a warning about vanity and being used by one's master.

## gameplay
### LARGE
Gameplay (game mechanics, not in-world fact). 2-piece: ATK +18%. 4-piece: +35% Normal Attack DMG for Sword, Claymore and Polearm users. Dropped by Normal and Weekly Bosses and Domain Reliquary.
