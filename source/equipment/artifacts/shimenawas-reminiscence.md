## summary
### LITE
Shimenawa's Reminiscence is a 4 and 5 star Inazuma artifact set. Its five item texts are the first-person memories of the shrine maiden Hibiki of her teacher the Kitsune Saiguu at the Grand Narukami Shrine, of fortune slips and paper-cord charms, and of the friends who were lost. It boosts ATK and trades Energy for Normal, Charged and Plunging Attack damage.
### STANDARD +
It drops from the Momiji-Dyed Court domain on Yashiori Island and has existed since Version 2.0. The theme is love of someone after they are gone: the texts return again and again to the idea that moments cannot be held, but time spent with someone is not lost.

## appearance
### LARGE
Five pieces: Entangling Bloom (flower, a mizuhiki paper-cord omamori said to bind wishes and their reasons), Shaft of Remembrance (plume, an old demon-slaying arrow kept with care), Morning Dew's Moment (sands, a bronze pocket watch with a shrine bell and twisted paper cord, its hands stopped before dawn on an autumn day), Hopeful Heart (goblet, a cylinder of fortune slips with a mechanism for removing unwanted ones) and Capricious Visage (circlet, a well-kept festival fox mask with a small enigmatic smile).

## lore
### LITE
The narrator came to Narukami as a country shrine maiden and was trained by the Kitsune Saiguu, who spoke in riddles about entanglement, memory and loss. Years later Saiguu is gone, and the narrator holds the empty pipe and keeps a promise to someone who is not at her side.
### LARGE +
Entangling Bloom: Hibiki arrived on Narukami from a small fishing village as a young shrine maiden, blunt and curious, doubting Saiguu's elegant sayings. Saiguu said everything in the world is entangled, so that illusory visions come from concrete reality, and that an omamori cannot grant a wish but can make it eternal through that entanglement. She teased Hibiki about a "fated one", a rude reckless brute, and noted that darkness engulfed everything in the end.

Morning Dew's Moment: on a slope on an autumn night Saiguu told Hibiki that trying to keep a moment's beauty is like grasping morning dew and that she herself had already passed away, so that what Hibiki saw was a residual vision born of her wishes. Then she tapped Hibiki's head with her pipe and said the sun was about to rise.

Hopeful Heart and Capricious Visage: years pass in the shrine. Hibiki has learned to talk like a kitsune; the Yougou Tengu has a daughter; Konbumaru has become a Hatamoto of the Shogun and will marry a high-ranking samurai's daughter. Saiguu jokes that the shrine misses children, draws Hibiki a Great Fortune, and explains that it means the missing person will become part of Hibiki's memories for ever, so she must live long. In the mask text, Hibiki says she has grown and is less foolish, but Saiguu's face seems to cloud with reluctant sorrow on a festive day that feels like a farewell, and Saiguu says that losing memories is like death in darkness before turning the talk to the "idiot Konbumaru".
### ULTRA +
Shaft of Remembrance: Hibiki reflects that evil is not an objective thing but grows from fear in cold, numb hearts. Saiguu is long gone and Hibiki holds her empty pipe; the Great Tengu went into self-imposed exile, enraged at failing to protect Saiguu, leaving Teruyo behind; Harunosuke left for another country in grief and rage; Nagamasa joined the Shogunate to clear the Mikoshi name. The man who taught her archery and listened to her naive promise under red sakura boughs will return, even blinded by blood or turned into a beast, and she will save him with their bow and arrows and destroy evil, folly and needless obsession. She imagines saying "Come see me, you idiotic problem gambler" and "don't lose your way this time, Konbumaru", still wondering who won the last throw of the dice.

Context from other sources: wiki summaries of Hibiki's story say the Yougou Three (Hibiki, Harunosuke and Nagamasa) trained under the Yougou Tengu; that Takamine later went to the Abyss with Ei in the cataclysm; and that when he returned corrupted, Hibiki shot him, believing it would save him. That outcome is not stated in this set.

## owners
### LARGE
The pieces are described through Hibiki's eyes: the pipe, the fortune cylinder, the arrow and the mask are tied to the Kitsune Saiguu and the Grand Narukami Shrine, and the pocket watch and charm are personal to the narrator.

## gameplay
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
Gameplay, not lore: the 2-piece bonus is ATK +18%. The 4-piece bonus: when casting an Elemental Skill with 15 or more Energy, the wearer loses 15 Energy and gains +50% Normal, Charged and Plunging Attack DMG for 10 seconds; it does not retrigger during that time and does not trigger on characters that do not use Energy (such as Mavuika). It drops from the Momiji-Dyed Court.

## entry: Hibiki's memories of the Kitsune Saiguu
@keys: Hibiki, Asase Hibiki, Kitsune Saiguu, Lady Saiguu
@version: 2.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
In Shimenawa's Reminiscence the narrator, Hibiki, remembers the Kitsune Saiguu as a teacher who spoke in paradoxes, drank sakura wine, and tapped heads with a tobacco pipe. The texts show Saiguu worrying about memory and loss, giving Hibiki a Great Fortune, and warning that she herself has "already passed away like the morning dew". The Hakushin Ring catalyst is narrated from Saiguu's side.

## entry: Konbumaru
@keys: Konbumaru, Takamine
@version: 2.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
"The idiot Konbumaru" is Hibiki's teasing name in the set for a wandering, gambling man who becomes a Hatamoto of the Shogun, marries a samurai's daughter and taught her archery. The wiki names him Takamine, a servant of the tengu Reizenbou, and says he disappeared in the cataclysm; the item texts use only the nickname. His connection to the Storm Cage text of Emblem of Severed Fate is inferred from the shared setting.

## entry: The Yougou Three
@keys: Yougou Three, Harunosuke, Kamuna Harunosuke
@version: 2.0
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
The Shaft of Remembrance lists the aftermath for Hibiki's fellow trainees: the Great Tengu in exile, Harunosuke leaving Inazuma, Nagamasa joining the Shogunate to clear the Mikoshi name. Wiki summaries call Hibiki, Harunosuke and Nagamasa "the Yougou Three" for training under the Yougou Tengu; the set itself does not use that term.
