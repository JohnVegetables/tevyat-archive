## summary
### LITE
Scroll of the Hero of Cinder City is a 4- and 5-star artifact set from Natlan, added in Version 5.0. Its five pieces belong to members of one band of adventurers: a beast tamer, a mountain ranger, a young mystic, a scholar and a warrior who set out in the Cataclysm for a legendary Abyss-repelling treasure in a ruined ancient city. It boosts Nightsoul Burst and elemental-reaction team play.
### STANDARD +
The pieces are Beast Tamer's Talisman (flower), Mountain Ranger's Marker (plume), Mystic's Gold Dial (sands), Wandering Scholar's Claw Cup (goblet) and Demon-Warrior's Feather Mask (circlet).

## appearance
### LARGE
The flower is a talisman of flint and Drakite with a flint paw print. The plume is a bird-feather-shaped path marker. The sands is a golden sun-like disc with draconic patterns that seem to symbolise the cycle of life and death; its needle points home. The goblet is shaped strikingly like a dragon's claw. The circlet is a feather mask with falcon-like plumage.

## lore
@version: 5.0
@spoiler: major
### LITE
The pieces are the last belongings of people from different tribes who tried to reach a treasure in the ash-laden ancient city while the black tide ravaged Natlan, and most of them did not return.
### STANDARD
The five pieces, each with its own entry:
- Beast Tamer's Talisman: passed down among beast tamers of the Scions of the Canopy.
- Mountain Ranger's Marker: rangers from the spring's source left feather-shaped markers on their paths for future travellers.
- Mystic's Gold Dial: mystics from the canyon of night smoke normally train for decades to speak with the Wayob.
- Wandering Scholar's Claw Cup: a scholar who claimed to be President of the Saurian Relics Association spent his days with scrolls and strange mechanisms until war burned his work.
- Demon-Warrior's Feather Mask: Taika, once the mightiest warrior of the Flower-Feather Clan, said goodbye to his wife and unborn child to fight monsters, becoming as ferocious as a demon.
Each piece has its own entry with its full text.
### LARGE +
Wiki cross-references that the item text does not state: the "city up in the clouds" and the "treasure" are the legendary Tonatiuh; the Python King is Och-Kan; the team reached Ochkanatlan around the Cataclysm and only Liriwu came back alive; and Taika led the party, which also included Quenin of the Flower-Feather Clan, Copan of the Collective of Plenty and others. According to the wiki, the party's tamer belonged to the Scions of the Canopy and the ranger to the People of the Springs.
### ULTRA +
The wiki's Taika and Bona pages say that the adventurers died to Waxaklahun Ubah Kan (Kukulkan) and that Taika's daughter Bona later followed his path to the same ruins. Kukulkan also spread the legend of the Jade of Return, which Bona's page says was a falsehood meant to lure strong warriors into a trap. The Mystic's Gold Dial text is the only piece that says any of the party made it back to tell the tale. Of the set's many legends, none confirm whether the real prize could repel the Abyss.

## owners
### LARGE
No wearer. The pieces are keepsakes of members of a single adventuring party. Named figures are Liriwu (Masters of the Night-Wind), Taika (Flower-Feather Clan), and, in the text, Taika's unborn child, Bona. The beast tamer, mountain ranger and scholar are unnamed.

## gameplay
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not lore): the 2-piece bonus restores 6 Elemental Energy when a nearby party member triggers a Nightsoul Burst. The 4-piece bonus gives nearby party members a 12% Elemental DMG Bonus for the elements in a reaction triggered by the wearer, for 15s; if the wearer is in Nightsoul's Blessing, they gain a further 28% for 20s.
### LARGE +
The 4-piece effect works off-field and same-named bonuses do not stack. Obtained from the Sanctum of Rainbow Spirits domain in Toyac Springs, Natlan, from Tribal Secret Space (from 5.2) and from the artifact strongbox (from 5.3).
Per the wiki's gameplay notes, reactions that consume Dendro Cores give Dendro plus the triggering element's bonus (Burgeon gives Pyro and Dendro; Hyperbloom gives Electro and Dendro), Quicken and Aggravate give Electro and Dendro, Spread gives Dendro only, and Shatter gives none. A second reaction adds its own bonuses with separate timers. The same domain also drops the Obsidian Codex.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay interpretation (this project's recommendation, not canon text): these are the humble effects of ordinary tribespeople who walked into a lost cause; treat them with respect rather than as treasure. A Natlanian who finds one can plausibly recognise the tribe by its crafted style.
### LARGE +
Do not turn them into the story of one named hero; the set is a chorus of unnamed people.

In the set, Cinder City is the burned ancient city where the legendary treasure was said to lie; the beast tamer's and mountain ranger's notes call it filth-ridden and ash-laden. The wiki ties it to Ochkanatlan, which later canon shows as Och-Kan's domain.

## entry: Beast Tamer's Talisman
@keys: Beast Tamer's Talisman
@version: 5.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
a talisman passed down among beast tamers of the Scions of the Canopy, carved with the paw print said to be that of the first Yumkasaurus to bond with a human. Beast tamers befriend Saurians, even violent ones, and believe in patience. The young tamer who learned this believed beasts share human hearts until the black tide came, bringing monsters who could never be understood. He settled the survivors of his hometown and set out on a journey of no return, hoping to find a secret Abyss-repelling treasure the ancient Python King was said to have forged in the burned city.

## entry: Mountain Ranger's Marker
@keys: Mountain Ranger's Marker
@version: 5.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
rangers from the spring's source left feather-shaped markers on their paths for future travellers. When the black tide swept over their homeland, the last ranger, who knew every inch of Natlan, set out again to retake it, hoping for a golden wheel in the city of the foul dragon that could reverse time, even if it was another trap laid by a heartless spectator.

## entry: Mystic's Gold Dial
@keys: Mystic's Gold Dial
@version: 5.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
mystics from the canyon of night smoke normally train for decades to speak with the Wayob, but in this chaotic time even fresh disciples had to guide fallen warriors' spirits home. The dial belonged to a young mystic named Liriwu. Joining a team of veterans bound for the city above the clouds, she was left behind in a safe tower; when she reached their camp she found only shattered corpses and the remains of a dragon's meal. Resolved to take word of their end to waiting families, she set out on the road home.

## entry: Wandering Scholar's Claw Cup
@keys: Wandering Scholar's Claw Cup
@version: 5.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
a scholar who claimed to be President of the Saurian Relics Association spent his days with scrolls and strange mechanisms until war burned his work. Having read that ancient dragon ruins held weapons against demons, he rebuilt machines from the secret source to aid warriors, then found a secret in a forsaken city, hidden for thousands of years. Believing the legend that the Python King of Ochkanatlan built a sky-faring treasure ship holding a prize that could drive the Abyss out of the world, he joined an adventurers' team bound for the city in the clouds, despite public scorn after earlier expeditions never returned.
