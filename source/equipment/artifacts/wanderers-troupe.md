## summary
### LITE
Wanderer's Troupe is a 4- and 5-star artifact set (1.0) about a troupe of travelling warrior-musicians from about a thousand years ago, who used swords for flutes and bows for harps and played for both friends and foes. They recorded their travels in a book called The Widsith. The wiki connects them to a failed uprising against Mondstadt's aristocracy.
### STANDARD +
The pieces are Troupe's Dawnlight, Bard's Arrow Feather, Concert's Final Hour, Wanderer's String-Kettle and Conductor's Top Hat.

## appearance
### LARGE
A small flower-shaped insignia, an azure arrow fletching, an hourglass that is also a harp, a canteen fitted with harp strings, and a top hat.

## lore
### LITE
A troupe of swordswoman, harpist-archer and conductor wandered the world, playing music with weapons. Its members died one by one and their instruments were buried in dust.
### STANDARD
Troupe's Dawnlight recalls a charismatic swordswoman whose slashes hummed a tune and whose music and sword were both lethal and beautiful. Bard's Arrow Feather recalls a harpist-archer who lured birds with his harp and shot them down, never looking at the sky; companions said it was a hunter's mercy. Concert's Final Hour is a harp-hourglass played to end shows. Wanderer's String-Kettle plays as water flows out. Conductor's Top Hat recalls a troupe that owned no sheet music, whose conductor held The Widsith to the end.
### LARGE +
The performances had two audiences: friends, and foes, to whom the music was a weapon. The troupe crossed deserts and the blazing Mare Jivari. Each member met their fate and the instruments were left in dust; the last sound was sand sliding down the hourglass.

The wiki's faction page (drawn from this set, the Resolution of Sojourner set and the Wanderer's Troupe weapons) adds: the Conductor met the Sojourner, then the Dawnlight Swordswoman and Kreuzlied. The troupe ended its journey in Mondstadt around Ludi Harpastum. After the Sojourner fell in love with the maiden who would be claimed by a member of the tyrannical Lawrence clan, the troupe stormed the city. They failed: the Conductor and Sojourner died in battle, the Dawnlight was enslaved as a gladiator, and Kreuzlied's punishment was never made public.
### ULTRA +
Its legacy: the Dawnlight Swordswoman befriended the squire Ragnvindr and inspired his title Dawn Knight, and Kreuzlied founded an underground society that later aided Vennessa's rebellion. These details come from sources beyond this set's text, so characters who only know the artifact text will know only the troupe's wandering and music.

## owners
### LARGE
The troupe and its members. The Widsith is the conductor's book; The Flute, The Stringless and The Bell are weapons associated with the Dawnlight, the Sojourner and Kreuzlied.

## roleplay
### LARGE
Musical, wistful, a little defiant. Describe songs, weapons-as-instruments and the sense of a lost, free age.
