## summary
### LITE
Song of Days Past is a 4- and 5-star artifact set from Fontaine, added in Version 4.3. Its bonuses reward healing: it records healing as a Yearning effect and turns it into extra damage. Its pieces tell of the people the Remurians called the "people of bygone times": the rebellion of a songstress against the golden authority, her search for a wish-granting Pure Grail, and the verses that later turned her into legend.
### STANDARD +
The pieces are Forgotten Oath of Days Past (flower), Recollection of Days Past (plume), Echoing Sound From Days Past (sands), Promised Dream of Days Past (goblet) and Poetry of Days Past (circlet). Many details are told through unnamed figures.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Only a few images are given: a butterfly wing worn in the ear (Recollection), the tolling night bell (Echoing Sound), a golden grail brimming with primordial water (Promised Dream) and a silver flower and silver armor (Forgotten Oath and Poetry).

## lore
@version: 4.3
@spoiler: major
### LITE
Before the Many Waters converged, Remuria's golden order crushed the tribes of the north; a songstress united them against it, swore to Egeria and later sought the Pure Grail. Centuries later, only poems and plays remembered it.
### STANDARD
The pieces follow a northern songstress who unites the tribes against Remuria's golden authority as the first Lochknight, a doomed love between a musician and a woman of his tribe, a desperate search for the wish-granting Pure Grail, and later poems that turn her into legend.
### LARGE +
- Forgotten Oath of Days Past: the golden authority swept across the sea and the iron legion made tribes submit, believing the Harmosts' new order was the rightful symphony. In the savage north the tribes united under the heir of Aremorica, a woman later eulogized as the "Lochknight". She called herself an oracle of the Queen of All Waters, not a sovereign, and her army made fellow tribes pledge themselves by the sword. Two vows run through every telling: never draw the sword on those who pledge faith in Egeria, and never compromise with evil. The war that followed burned "proud gold and untarnished silver" alike.
- Recollection of Days Past: in a short-lived seagrass tribe, a youth who did not yet know the God-King's laws put a butterfly wing on a woman's ear, a vow in their tradition not to part until death. They met again years later in the capital. He had become a musician and, disguised as a traveler, won the tournament and an audience with the King; when his identity was revealed, blades greeted him. His "artificial body" was cut to ribbons, and she, thinking an Usurper had imprisoned his soul in stone, whispered that she would free him and that the pale butterfly would guide their souls to the Many Waters. Both names are blanked in the text.
- Echoing Sound From Days Past: the tribes tolled bells for sunrise, sunset, births and deaths, and at last for the destruction of the golden empire. Survivors split: some tried to replay the old music, some gave up their names to hunt ghosts in the forests, some hid their names in song. A woman, doubtful but desperate, says that if the Pure Grail truly grants every wish, she will "drown in such fantastical delusions one last time" before stepping alone into the valley as the night bell tolls.
- Promised Dream of Days Past: tribes told of the Pure Grail, a golden vessel brimming with primordial water, said to heal wounds, restore youth, revive the dead and give wisdom to the purest. After the golden city fell in one night, the vengeful songstress was urged by an Oceanid to seek the primordial chalice that grants every prayer, which spread the heterodox belief that it grants any wish. At twilight's end she realised what the chalice actually was.
- Poetry of Days Past: when the Many Waters converged, a gentle mistress crossed the ruins and built a new city, and the golden symphony would never play again. For more than a thousand years later generations have enjoyed the Lochknights' adventures: swearing on silver armor to fight beside the Oceanids, finding the Pure Grail and welcoming back the Queen of Many Waters. A hero called Erinnyes in these tales has nothing to do with the real woman who shared the name, and the ballads are "dreams of the present".
Cross-check with wider canon (not in these pieces): the Remurian empire was led by Remus with four human Harmosts (Boethius and Cassiodor among them), and Cassiodor shared a tribe with Erinnyes, who left for Egeria's side. This is why the identification of the songstress with Erinnyes and the musician with Cassiodor is common but remains interpretation. The Lochknights became a Fontainian legend, and the "Pure Grail" tale was heard in La Chanson d'Erinnyes.
### ULTRA +
The set deliberately includes the tension between what happened and how it is remembered: Poetry of Days Past warns that later heroic verse is not the true past, and Promised Dream shows the Grail belief to be hearsay from an ancient Oceanid's words. The vows of Forgotten Oath are presented as constants of every retelling, but the details of the pledge differ between versions.

## gameplay
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not lore): the 2-piece bonus is Healing Bonus +15%. The 4-piece effect creates a Yearning effect for 6s when the wearer heals a party member, recording total healing including overflow (up to 15,000), then converts it to Waves of Days Past, adding 8% of the recorded total to the active character's DMG for the next 5 hits or 10s. Standby wearers still trigger it. Obtained from the Waterfall Wen domain in Fontaine and the artifact strongbox. Added in Version 4.3.
### LARGE +
Waterfall Wen is a Domain of Blessing on the east slopes of Mont Automnequi in Fontaine's Morte Region: a sunken ruin once used by a secret order, now ruled by beasts without language.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay interpretation (this project's recommendation, not canon text): lean into the set's elegy: memorial verse, bells, silver and gold. Remind characters that stories of Lochknights are partly legend, as the circlet itself says.

## entry: Lochknight (set lore)
@keys: Lochknight, Lochknights
@keys2: Song of Days Past, Forgotten Oath of Days Past, Poetry of Days Past
@version: 4.3
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
In Forgotten Oath of Days Past, the first Lochknight is the unnamed oracle-songstress who united northern tribes against Remuria and swore to Egeria, with two vows: never draw the sword on those who pledge faith in Egeria, and never tolerate evil. Poetry of Days Past shows how later poems retold her, and the Lochknights have their own entry.

## entry: Pure Grail
@keys: Pure Grail, Golden Grail, primordial chalice
@keys2: Song of Days Past, Promised Dream of Days Past, Echoing Sound From Days Past
@version: 4.3
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
In this set the Pure Grail is a legendary vessel of primordial water said to heal, restore youth, revive the dead and grant any wish. No one in the pieces has seen it, the belief came from an Oceanid's words, and it is called a heterodoxy. Poetry of Days Past retells the quest as myth.
