## summary
### LITE
Tenacity of the Millelith is a 4- and 5-star artifact set for Liyue's soldiers. Its five pieces are a golden flower, a falcon-feather plume, a sun-and-moon time-dial, a golden oath cup and a general's helmet, and their item text commemorates the Millelith who held the Chasm against the Abyss alongside a nameless yaksha, dying there so civilians could escape.
### STANDARD +
Added in version 1.5. The text is Liyue folk memory about an event 500 years before the present, in the time of the Cataclysm. Rex Lapis is said to have sent the Millelith to defend the Chasm mines when the Abyss flooded in; the soldiers who stayed to cover the retreat are remembered in the Millelith's ceremonial dress.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Ceremonial military gear of Liyue: a flower made of gold leaf, a plume from a high-altitude bird of prey worn on parade, a bronze time-dial powered by sunlight and moonlight, a golden cup used to swear oaths, and a splendid ancient helmet which looks new again once the dust is cleaned away.

## lore
@spoiler: minor
### LITE
The pieces recount how Millelith soldiers and a nameless yaksha made a last stand against the Abyss in the Chasm, and how Liyue still honours them.
### STANDARD
Rex Lapis is said to have ordered the Millelith to defend the Chasm mines when the Abyss flooded in. Fighting beside a nameless yaksha, a rearguard stayed behind to cover the civilians' escape and fell there; Millelith ceremonial dress (golden flower, feather plume) honours them.
### LARGE +
Each piece adds an element. A meteorite fell into the Chasm, turning the ground to Glazed Sand Crystal; later, when the Abyss flooded out, Rex Lapis ordered the Millelith to defend the mines (Flower of Accolades). The Millelith take their feather ornaments from the yaksha, whose plumage was torn in battle and whose fallen feathers became a symbol of hope (Ceremonial War-Plume). Because fighting beside the yaksha exposed mortals to karma and the darkness of killing, soldiers kept a fixed marching rhythm with a time-dial, one squad taking the field as another withdrew (Orichalceous Time-Dial). The Millelith's first elders swore loyalty to Rex Lapis over a golden cup before marching into the abyss (Noble's Pledging Vessel). The front-line troops under a commander in helmets charged so the afflicted civilians could flee, and when the gloom cleared the yaksha had vanished and the soldiers lay at rest (General's Ancient Helm).
The set treats memory as its theme: the names of mortals and adepti alike are said to have been forgotten, yet their deeds are not. Today's Liyue Harbor is described as still peaceful because the Cataclysm was quelled, and soldiers wear the golden flower in honour of their forebears. The text also records two later episodes: a conceited adventurer who, centuries after, recovered the pledging cup from the Chasm and found it untarnished, and a miner who found a time-dial a hundred years after the battle.
### ULTRA +
The pieces include a deliberate ambiguity about the yaksha. Ceremonial War-Plume reports that people claim the yaksha was not acting under Rex Lapis's command but paying for an old sin of cowardice and neglected duty, and that Rex Lapis, moved by the sacrifice, kept a long silence; General's Ancient Helm, by contrast, says the yaksha fought at Rex Lapis's order. The text neither resolves this nor says what happened to the yaksha afterwards, only that it is thought to have returned to a free life among the clouds. Orichalceous Time-Dial adds an urban legend of a black-robed collector of curios who bought up every time-dial at a generous price and deflected all questions; the wiki connects him to Zhongli, but the text itself says only that time may eventually explain his reasons. Together, the pieces give a mortal-centred picture of the Cataclysm in Liyue: the heroes are a rearguard nobody remembers by name.

## gameplay
@availability: permanent
### LITE
Game mechanics: the 2-piece bonus raises HP; the 4-piece bonus gives the party an ATK and Shield Strength buff after an Elemental Skill hit.
### STANDARD
Game mechanics (not lore): obtained from the Ridge Watch domain and the Artifact Strongbox. 2-piece: HP +20%. 4-piece: when an Elemental Skill hits an opponent, all nearby party members gain ATK +20% and Shield Strength +30% for 3s, triggerable once per 0.5s, including while the wearer is off-field.

## entry: The Chasm stand (item-text account)
@keys: Chasm stand, nameless yaksha, Millelith last stand
@keys2: Millelith, Chasm
@version: 1.5
### STANDARD
In the folk account of the Tenacity of the Millelith set, a meteorite crater in the Chasm turned the ground to Glazed Sand Crystal. When the Abyss flooded in, Rex Lapis sent the Millelith to defend the mines; the troops escorted civilians out, and a rearguard stayed with a nameless yaksha and fell in the Chasm. The Abyss receded, the glaze sands shone again, and the yaksha vanished. Treat the details as legend: the text is partly contradictory about whether the yaksha acted on Rex Lapis's orders.

### STANDARD
The set explains Millelith ceremonial dress: the golden flower honours the dead of the Chasm, and the feather plume worn on ceremonial occasions is said to have been inspired by the yaksha's torn plumage. The plumes raise civilian morale and awe foreign visitors.
