## summary
### LITE
Bloodstained Chivalry is a Physical artifact set (4 and 5 star, added 1.0). Its five pieces tell the downfall of a righteous knight: he began as a white knight who rescued a maiden, killed so much in the name of justice that he came to see himself as a monster, and finally went to the fallen kingdom to die. The wiki names him as Roland; the texts say only 'the Bloodstained Knight'.
### STANDARD +
The pieces are Bloodstained Flower of Iron (flower), Bloodstained Black Plume (feather), Bloodstained Final Hour (sands), Bloodstained Chevalier's Goblet (goblet) and Bloodstained Iron Mask (circlet). It is the other half of the story told by the Maiden Beloved set.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Black-stained relics of a knight: a dried white flower hardened by blood to the toughness of steel, a raven feather dyed pitch black, a timepiece whose liquid has dried up, a silver goblet engraved with a knight's feats but blackened by smoke and blood, and a luxurious platinum iron mask with permanent dark stains.

## lore
### LITE
A white knight rescued a maiden, wanted no reward but chivalry, and later killed so much that he could not tell his own blood from his enemies'. He became the Bloodstained Knight and died in the lair of monsters in the ancient kingdom.
### STANDARD
The Flower of Iron tells how the rescued damsel offered a reward and he refused everything but one pure white flower, saying chivalry is its own reward; he wore it over his heart and his bloodshed dried and blackened it. The Black Plume says ravens followed him, he could no longer tell whose blood stained him, and he realised that his path had turned the white knight into a monster. The Goblet shows him returning from a battlefield to find that those awaiting rescue were gone among burning, collapsed houses; he took the smoke-blackened goblet and vowed to rid the world of monsters, poverty and evil. The Iron Mask describes a person he tried to help cowering from him, which made him realise he had become the monster, tainted by the cruelty of a drawn-out war; the mask shields him from the eyes of those he vowed to protect.
### LARGE +
The Final Hour finishes the story. In the abyss, where no celestial bodies cast light, time lost its meaning for him. He saw there was no place for him on earth and travelled into the fallen ancient kingdom, where he died fighting in the monsters' lair. At the bottom of the world he learned the origin of the monsters: the kingdom had been wrongfully cursed, turning its people into monsters. His code of chivalry could not tolerate that injustice, and he pledged loyalty to the Abyss, whatever its name. The wiki links the 'ancient kingdom' to Khaenri'ah and the 'drawn-out war' to the Cataclysm.
### ULTRA +
The wiki's Roland page adds context from other item texts: a noble-born Knight of Favonius taught chivalry and swordsmanship, obsessed with justice, who lost himself in cutting down foes, and the rescued maiden is the one of the Maiden Beloved set. Those details come from weapon and set texts rather than a quest, so treat them as wiki-compiled lore. The Iron Mask description notes that many have speculated about the face behind the mask; canon does not settle it.

## owners
### STANDARD
The Bloodstained Knight, whom the wiki identifies as Roland. The goblet and the mask are described as his; the pieces are remnants of his life.

## status
### STANDARD
The knight is long dead; the texts say he died in battle in the monsters' lair of the fallen kingdom. Nothing in 6.x or 7.0-7.1 changes this set.

## roleplay
### LARGE
Use as a tragedy about justice turning cruel. A character holding a piece might speak of ravens, stained iron or a white flower. Do not give the knight a living voice or invent dialogue; the quoted vows in the item texts are the only words attributed to him.

## gameplay
### LARGE
Gameplay (game mechanics, not in-world fact). A 4- and 5-star Physical set from the domain Clear Pool and Mountain Cavern. 2-piece: Physical DMG Bonus +25%. 4-piece: after defeating an opponent, Charged Attack DMG +50% and Charged Attack Stamina cost 0 for 10s. It persists when switching characters and can trigger on wildlife; kills by co-op or off-field teammates do not count. A 4-star goblet can be obtained from one of Sister Victoria's dialogue branches in the Mondstadt cathedral.

### STANDARD
The Bloodstained Knight is the figure of the Bloodstained Chivalry set texts: once a white knight, he became a killer in justice's name and died in the fallen kingdom. The wiki identifies him as Roland. See the set entry for the five piece stories.
### LARGE +
His white flower, raven feather, silver goblet and iron mask mark the stages of his fall. He and the rescued maiden are the two sides of the Maiden Beloved and Bloodstained Chivalry sets.

### LARGE
The Final Hour records the knight's end: he went to the fallen ancient kingdom, learned that its people were cursed into monsters, pledged loyalty to the Abyss, and died in battle. The Iron Mask text is the earlier moment when he understood he had become a monster to the very people he helped.
