## summary
### LITE
Viridescent Venerer is an Anemo artifact set from launch (Valley of Remembrance domain). Its five pieces tell of a legendary huntress, the "queen of hunters", who moved silently through the forest, stopped hunting animals at a boy's request after disaster struck an ancient kingdom, and hunted monsters instead.
### STANDARD +
The huntress is nameless apart from her title. The set's story continues in the lore of the bow The Viridescent Hunt.

## lore
### LITE
In the days when monsters had not yet appeared, the Viridescent Venerer hid her human scent with a white wild flower, and was sought by walking barefoot through the forest. The one who found her was a boy, when disaster had just befallen an ancient kingdom. She had once let the grass and mud tell her what birds and roots perceived, but when the God of the Woods died in the disaster the plants stopped speaking, so she used a wayfinding device. At the boy's request she stopped hunting birds and beasts and hunted the monsters that brought suffering instead.
### STANDARD +
Her arrows once brought death gently: she stroked her prey so its life force returned to nature, and believed she would meet the departed at an endless hunting ground. But she killed the monster that had slain the boy out of vengeance, and believed that hunting ground was then lost to her. The Chinese text ties this to avenging those the monsters hurt on the boy's behalf. Her vessel held the laughter of a campfire she once overheard; she had forgotten human speech, and even after joining the humans no one heard her say a word or saw her leave a trace.
### LARGE +
The English text says that "disaster" came, while the Chinese original specifies the ancient kingdom, and the wiki reads this as Khaenri'ah and the Cataclysm; the God of the Woods is said to have died with it. The diadem piece quotes the hunters' rule, "Never become the foe of the earth and the forest", says a bird nested in her hat, and notes none could crown her since only nature was above her. After the crisis her hunter's hat became a frightful sight for monsters.
### ULTRA +
She replayed the stored voices for years whenever she felt lonely. A translation note says the original gives her a believed place at a "fenceless range", lost because she took revenge, not because she saved the boy.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not lore): 4 and 5 star set from Valley of Remembrance and its strongbox. 2-piece: Anemo DMG Bonus +15%. 4-piece: Swirl DMG +60%, Stellar Swirl +20%; lowers the enemy's RES to the swirled element by 40% for 10s, and Cryo RES too on a Stellar Swirl; same-element debuffs don't stack. Introduced in 1.0.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay interpretation (this project's recommendation, not canon text): portray her as silent, gentle and grieving. Do not name her or the boy, and do not state more about the disaster than the items imply.

## entry: In Remembrance of Viridescent Fields
@keys: In Remembrance of Viridescent Fields
### STANDARD
The flower piece: a white wildflower that once covered the land, worn by the huntress to hide her scent. The silent huntress could be found by walking barefoot through the Dharma Forest following its fragrance; the one who found her was a boy, as the ancient kingdom suffered disaster.

## entry: Viridescent Arrow Feather
@keys: Viridescent Arrow Feather
### STANDARD
The plume piece: a falcon feather, once fletching on her arrow. She comforted prey so its life force returned to nature, expecting an endless hunting ground, but believed her vengeful killing of the monster that killed the boy cost her that.

## entry: Viridescent Venerer's Determination
@keys: Viridescent Venerer's Determination
### STANDARD
The sands piece: a wayfinding device that points to prey. She once learned what birds saw from the grass and what roots heard from the mud; after the God of the Woods died, the plants fell silent, and at a boy's request she began hunting monsters.

## entry: Viridescent Venerer's Vessel
@keys: Viridescent Venerer's Vessel
### STANDARD
The goblet piece: a leakproof water pouch she used to store the laughter of a campfire. She had forgotten human speech; no one ever heard her speak or saw her leave a trace.

## entry: Viridescent Venerer's Diadem
@keys: Viridescent Venerer's Diadem
### STANDARD
The circlet piece: a bloodless hunter's hat, from folklore of the uncrowned queen of hunters, who held to the rule never to become the foe of the earth and forest. It became a frightful sight for monsters in the crisis that followed.
