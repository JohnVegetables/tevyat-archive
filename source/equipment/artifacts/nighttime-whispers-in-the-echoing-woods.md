## summary
### LITE
Nighttime Whispers in the Echoing Woods is a Geo artifact set from Version 4.3. Its five pieces tell a bedtime-style fairy tale, read aloud in fragments by a little girl in an attic with her dog Pie: a witch in the Echoing Woods grants a youth's wish, and the girl he loves is given endless good luck that turns out to be a curse.
### STANDARD +
The tale is framed twice. The outer story is the attic scene (the girl, her dog Pie and her mother, who likes to tell stories); the inner story is the book. Each piece holds a page of the book and a short return to the attic.

## lore
### LITE
A girl follows her dog Pie into the attic and finds a storybook with golden-winged butterflies on its cover. It says a witch in the Echoing Woods grants any wish but hides her hut in fog and echoes, so few reach her. A youth, led by a golden-winged butterfly, finds the hut and asks that the girl he loves, who loves another, have all the happiness in the world. The witch agrees; the price will come later and need not be his soul.
### STANDARD +
The witch's magic is a pen, ink bottle and other items: whatever the dipped pen writes becomes real and the girl becomes fate's favourite. Everything goes her way, everyone adores her, and she grows spoiled.
### LARGE +
She scorns gifts, rebukes her worried mother and leaves home, living a life like an endless ball where all forgive her. Returning years later she learns her mother has long since died, and calls the gift a curse because a life where nothing is paid for feels worthless. The youth shows her a magic mirror borrowed from the witch. In the mirror she sees what follows when the luck ends: lost wealth, people turning cold, hardship, and every gift needing repayment tenfold. She still chooses the harder, real path, recalling her mother's stories in which heroes lose things on the way and so value their reward, and says she will love others sincerely even if they stop loving her. At that moment the witch's appointed time arrives, and she takes what she most wanted from what the youth had to give. The girl ends up trapped in the ink bottle; the witch muses she will make a good character in other stories, and that she may follow the girl into the bottle's many universes. In the attic the real girl decides to tell the story to her mother, while Pie runs off, perhaps hungry.
### ULTRA +
The set's recurring motifs are paying a price versus getting things free, the witch's line that "only a heart of gold may be weighed", and the attic girl's own reflection that souls seem cheap in stories while afternoon tea and her mother's bedtime tales are what she values. The pieces describe the witch's items as a butterfly brooch, a dip pen, a sand timepiece that runs very fast if the wrong incantation is spoken, an ink bottle holding everything the user can imagine, and a lady's hat that mixes solemnity and playfulness.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not lore): 4 and 5 star set from the Waterfall Wen domain and its strongbox. 2-piece: ATK +18%. 4-piece: after an Elemental Skill, +20% Geo DMG Bonus for 10s; increased by 150% under a Crystallize shield or near Moondrifts formed by Lunar-Crystallize, with the extra ending 1s after the condition lapses. Introduced in 4.3.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay interpretation (this project's recommendation, not canon text): treat the story as a fairy tale told through a storybook rather than as a historical event; do not assign it a nation, and do not decide the witch's or youth's fate beyond what the text says.

## entry: Selfless Floral Accessory
@keys: Selfless Floral Accessory
### STANDARD
The flower piece: a butterfly-shaped brooch worn by the witch. Its text opens the frame story (the girl, Pie, the attic, the butterfly-covered book) and the legend of the Echoing Woods witch whose fog confuses visitors with echoes.
### LARGE +
The youth had been looking for a blue flower when a golden-winged butterfly led him to the hut. The witch cuts him off with "That's what they all say" and warns that every wish has a different price. He asks only that the girl he loves, who loves another, be happy.

## entry: Honest Quill
@keys: Honest Quill
### STANDARD
The plume piece: the witch's dip pen. The girl wonders why souls are sold so easily in stories; the youth asks what could fulfil a higher happiness, and the witch says words written with the pen dipped in her ink come true and the girl will be fate's favourite.
### LARGE +
The girl's life changes: fortune follows her, everyone loves her, and she becomes used to praise though her looks and virtue are described as ordinary.

## entry: Faithful Hourglass
@keys: Faithful Hourglass
### STANDARD
The sands piece: the witch's sand timepiece, said to run very fast if the wrong incantation is spoken. In the tale, the girl scorns blue ribbons given by guests, tells her mother she does not need a mother who does not love her like the others, and leaves home.
### LARGE +
In the attic, the real girl muses about what she would write with a magic pen (lots of doggie biscuits for Pie). The tale notes friends forgive her rudeness the next day.

## entry: Magnanimous Ink Bottle
@keys: Magnanimous Ink Bottle
### STANDARD
The goblet piece: the witch's ink bottle. Its text has the girl return home to find her mother long dead, call her luck a curse and set out for the Echoing Woods; the youth shows her the witch's magic mirror.
### LARGE +
The mirror shows her future ruin once the luck ends. The witch says the magic's fortune has not yet abandoned her and none will mock her weakness.

## entry: Compassionate Ladies' Hat
@keys: Compassionate Ladies' Hat, Compassionate Ladies Hat
### STANDARD
The circlet piece: the witch's lady's hat. The girl chooses the harder path, the witch's appointed time arrives, the girl is trapped in the ink bottle, and the frame story ends with the girl leaving the storybook behind on the attic floor.
### LARGE +
The witch says she exists for the girl as the girl does for her, and hopes to find a place for them in another story within the bottle.
