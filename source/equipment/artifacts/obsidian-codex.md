## summary
### LITE
Obsidian Codex is a 4- and 5-star artifact set from Natlan, added in Version 5.0. Its five pieces are mythic fragments, each opening with the line that many stories "were never recorded or eulogized in any human history": a dragon's gift of fire to humans, the first tribe, a contest of heroes and the crowning of a king who ended the tribes' wars. It boosts characters in Nightsoul's Blessing.
### STANDARD +
The pieces are Reckoning of the Xenogenic (flower), Root of the Spirit-Marrow (plume), Myths of the Night Realm (sands), Pre-Banquet of the Contenders (goblet) and Crown of the Saints (circlet). Together they read as one long legend told in epithets, not names.

## appearance
### LARGE
Only item descriptions survive. The flower is carved from black crystal and glows mysteriously at night. The plume is a feathered accessory in imitation of an ancient dragon's wing. The sands is a ritual element that is neither timepiece nor compass, with no known use. The goblet is a stone vessel shaped in a seemingly whimsical way, leaving open who could have the strength to make it. The circlet is an obsidian crown, once used in coronations by noble tribal kings.

## lore
@version: 5.0
@spoiler: major
### LITE
The set tells, from several angles, how the old dragon civilization faded, how humans received fire and organised into tribes, and how a hero without a prophecy united them.
### STANDARD
Every piece opens with the same refrain, that many stories passed which no human history recorded. In order:
- Reckoning of the Xenogenic: a dispossessed divine envoy fell into lightless realms.
- Root of the Spirit-Marrow: the wisest of dragons carried a seed of the primal fire across a burning land and, finding no creation worthy of it, chose the weakest and most cherished of species, humans living in the shadow of ancient wings.
- Myths of the Night Realm: brave Chaac took the ever-bright kindling from the Sage of the Stolen Flame, shared it.
- Pre-Banquet of the Contenders: priests of smoke.
- Crown of the Saints: as a dark tide rose, warriors carried the Wayob's blessings into forbidden realms, the dragons were gone from the fields.
Each piece has its own entry with its full text.
### LARGE +
Cross-references used by the wiki's lore transcription (not stated inside the item text): the wisest of dragons and Sage of the Stolen Flame is Kukulkan; the ruler and "overlord of fire" is the Pyro Dragon Xiuhcoatl; the dispossessed envoy is the Lord of the Night (Yohualtecuhtin); the "first barbarian" is Chaac; the "high king among dragons" is Nibelung; and the unprophesied hero of Crown of the Saints is Xbalanque, the first Pyro Archon. The "black tide" and foul darkness are the Abyss, the fall of heaven and earth is the War of Funerary Flame, and the citadel destroyed is tied to the Unified Civilization.
### ULTRA +
Read side by side, the pieces describe a plan that mostly worked and partly failed. Kukulkan's gift of Phlogiston let humans found tribes and meet the Night Kingdom's gods, but "evolution" was meant to produce a single strong heir for the dragons' purposes. Pre-Banquet of the Contenders shows that plan crowning the strongest hero; Crown of the Saints shows a different hero ending the tribal wars through a pact instead. The pieces never say what became of the dragon afterwards, and they do not name the heir of the prophecy.

## owners
### LARGE
No named owner. The text is legend, not the biography of a wearer; no character is known to have carried these artifacts.

## gameplay
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not lore): the 2-piece bonus gives +15% DMG while the wearer is in Nightsoul's Blessing and on the field. The 4-piece bonus gives +40% CRIT Rate for 6s after the wearer consumes 1 Nightsoul point while on the field, at most once per second.
### LARGE +
The set is obtained from the Sanctum of Rainbow Spirits domain in Toyac Springs, Natlan, from Tribal Secret Space (from 5.2) and from the artifact strongbox (from 5.3).
The Sanctum of Rainbow Spirits' Ley Line Disorder unleashes a True DMG shockwave from an opponent's position whenever a character triggers a Pyro-related reaction on it, at most once every 5s. The same domain also drops the Scroll of the Hero of Cinder City.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay interpretation (this project's recommendation, not canon text): present the pieces as relics whose texts are half-understood. Ordinary Natlanians know the tribal legends of Chaac and the Wayob but not the dragons' side of the story; no one has a ready key to the epithets. Do not let a character confidently name every figure in the set.

### STANDARD
In Root of the Spirit-Marrow and its sister pieces, the wisest of dragons carries a seed of primal fire to a rugged land, grants humans the kindling of wisdom and foretells a savior of two worlds. The wiki's lore transcription links this figure to Kukulkan, Waxaklahun Ubah Kan. The pieces call his path "evolution" and note it will order human destinies as the price of civilising them.

### STANDARD
a dispossessed divine envoy fell into lightless realms, and the newly returned ruler destroyed a blasphemous citadel. The vishap kingdom survived the collapse of heaven and earth, isolated by a sea of red soil, but its fire-wisdom had been seized by foul darkness and only a gray corpse shambled on, while its blind heirs mistook violence for law. One "human" among them foresaw the ruler's deathbed and the fall of a great civilization, and carried off unextinguished primordial kindling from the palace beneath the magma.

## entry: Root of the Spirit-Marrow
@keys: Root of the Spirit-Marrow
@version: 5.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
the wisest of dragons carried a seed of the primal fire across a burning land and, finding no creation worthy of it, chose the weakest and most cherished of species, humans living in the shadow of ancient wings. The gift would civilise them but also order their destinies, since it was the profane path of "evolution" rather than the first divine plan. The dragon told the first barbarian who climbed the floating landmass that from his heirs would come a savior of two worlds who would slay the overlord of fire and take the oldest throne.

## entry: Myths of the Night Realm
@keys: Myths of the Night Realm
@version: 5.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
brave Chaac took the ever-bright kindling from the Sage of the Stolen Flame, shared it, and with his companions defeated a raging dragon and founded the first tribe. The Sage knew every answer but two: the end of the living and the homeward path of the dead. When his companions were gone, Chaac climbed a mountain on his last night, lit the first flame, and the god of the night realm answered; people heard what seemed a mother's song, the birth-cry of the first Wayob.

## entry: Pre-Banquet of the Contenders
@keys: Pre-Banquet of the Contenders
@version: 5.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
priests of smoke and mist fed bonfires with the blood of other species, a hero rode a tamed beast to a fortress of huge stones with the Wayob's guidance, and reptiles feared an ancient city. The wisest dragon watched, planning that the strongest tribal hero should rule all humans, make a covenant, raise the banner of the blazing sun and take the primal fire from the living corpse on the throne. It knew foes waited in the deepest night, and that neither the gods in their heavens nor the high king among dragons would be enough.

## entry: Crown of the Saints
@keys: Crown of the Saints
@version: 5.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
as a dark tide rose, warriors carried the Wayob's blessings into forbidden realms, the dragons were gone from the fields, and the dragon temples stood waiting for judgment. The tribes' war chiefs abandoned dragon-slaying to fight over supremacy. A hero no plan had accounted for, wearing a crown of golden flowers and carrying a great obsidian blade, visited tribe after tribe; the tribes made a pact of armistice and the sacred ones of the night realm offered him the crown. The dragon that thought itself master of evolution had not foreseen that strength would answer with something other than conquest.
