## summary
@spoiler: major
### LITE
Pale Flame is a 4- and 5-star artifact set added in version 1.5. Its five pieces read like origin stories of the Fatui: the first Fatuus (Pierro) approaches a woman consumed by fire, a heretical scholar, a moneyman and a discarded constructed being, and each takes a role in the Tsaritsa's cause. The last piece, the Mocking Mask, is Pierro's own declaration. In it he says the blood of his Khaenri'ahn compatriots cannot be washed away and that he will become a jester who mocks fate.
### STANDARD +
The set is an early long-form hint that the Fatui's founders each carry a grievance against the heavens or the old order. The first four figures are not named in the item text; the wiki reads them as Harbingers.

## appearance
### STANDARD
The five pieces are a flower (Stainless Bloom), a feather (Wise Doctor's Pinion), a clock-like sands piece (Moment of Cessation), a cup (Surpassing Cup) and a mask (Mocking Mask). Item text gives no further physical description.

## lore
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The five pieces dramatise how the first Fatuus, Pierro, recruited early Fatui: a woman consumed by fire (read as Signora), a heretical scholar (Dottore), a man seeking worldly power (Pantalone) and a discarded divine creation (the Balladeer). The Mocking Mask is Pierro's own vow: because his Khaenri'ah compatriots' blood cannot be cleansed, he will become a jester who mocks fate and serve the Tsaritsa.
### LARGE +
Item text, summarised:
- Stainless Bloom: the first Fatuus offers a deal to a young woman whose life-flame has all but burnt out and who feels she is made of fire. She asks that glacial ice replace her erased past and quench the fire, and that pain and sin be purified by silent ice. A pure white flame still burns in her heart, and she calls herself a tool stained by the ashes of the dead. The wiki identifies her as Signora.
- Wise Doctor's Pinion: a young researcher in the seat of learning argues that people are machines that can be improved, fears being cast out as a heretic, and notes that the Akademiya's way of working blocks research. The first Fatuus finds him in the desert, and he boasts that with enough resources he could make what the nation calls a god. He is accepted as a partner and given a title that amuses him bitterly. The wiki identifies him as Dottore.
- Moment of Cessation: a man not favoured by the gods can pursue only worldly power, and money is cast as the world's lifeblood. He vows that Snezhnaya will become the heart that pumps wealth around the world and that, when the time comes, it will stop by its own will. The wiki identifies him as Pantalone.
- Surpassing Cup: a being born with a face fairer than any other, divinely made and discarded as worthless dross, wakes through an unknowable error and wanders for countless years. He decides to join the mask-wearing Fatui because they are fun. The wiki identifies him as the Balladeer (Scaramouche, now the Wanderer).
- Mocking Mask: Pierro speaks in his own name. Because the blood of his Khaenri'ah compatriots cannot be cleansed, he will be the Jester who laughs at fate. He says he could not match the sages, failed to win the previous ruler's favour, and failed to stop others tearing away the veil of sin, which brought divine wrath and cataclysm. He will therefore become a fool and a Fatuus in service to the Tsaritsa, then calls on his comrades to put on masks in mockery and rewrite destiny.
### ULTRA +
Cross-references the wiki cites: Signora says "Pale Flame" at two points in her boss fight, which supports the Stainless Bloom link; the Balladeer's "worthless dross" phrasing echoes a line in the Inversion of Genesis interlude; Pantalone's wealth supports the Moment of Cessation link; and Dottore's earlier Ruin Machine facility matches the "manufacture a god" boast. These are community inferences from matching wording, not statements in the item text.

The Mocking Mask is the key canon claim: Pierro frames himself as Khaenri'ahn and as someone shaped by the causes of the Cataclysm, but he does not name the Five Sinners or say what he did. The phrase "tearing away the veil of sin" refers to events the set leaves unexplained.

The pieces mix an omniscient narrator with quoted speech, so the recruitment scenes are best treated as a dramatised, possibly unreliable account rather than minutes of real meetings. Later canon gave several of these figures more tragic or complicated stories, so use the set as flavour about how the Fatui present their own origins.

## owners
### STANDARD
No owner is named. The text is narrated from the perspective of the first Fatuus about his recruits and himself.

## gameplay
@availability: permanent
### LITE
Game mechanics: the 2-piece bonus is +25% Physical DMG; the 4-piece bonus boosts ATK after Elemental Skill hits.
### STANDARD
Game mechanics (not lore): 4- and 5-star; obtained from the Ridge Watch domain (Liyue) and an Artifact Strongbox. 4-piece: when an Elemental Skill hits an opponent, ATK +9% for 7s, up to 2 stacks, once per 0.3s. At 2 stacks the 2-piece Physical DMG Bonus increases by 100%. The wiki notes the effect applies only to the wearer, only for an on-field hit, and persists after swapping characters.

## entry: Mocking Mask
@keys: Mocking Mask
@version: 1.5
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Pierro's own piece. He says the stain of his Khaenri'ah compatriots' blood cannot be cleansed and that he will become The Jester who laughs at fate, a Fatuus devoted to Her Majesty (the Tsaritsa), who understands his pain. He addresses his Fatui comrades, saying they carry both the fire of rage and the cold of eternal winter, have seen the callousness of the world's foundational principles, and should wear masks in mockery to rewrite destiny.
