## summary
@spoiler: major
### LITE
Finale of the Deep Galleries is a 4- and 5-star Cryo artifact set. Its five Deep Gallery pieces carry item text about Skirk, following her master Surtalogi's guidance, walking the frozen north to find relics, with each piece telling part of a legend of the first angel, a stranger from beyond the stars and the Golden City's fall. Mechanically it rewards a character at zero Energy.
### STANDARD +
The text interleaves two layers: a short frame in which "the girl" (Skirk) searches northern ruins, and a long past in which a youth and the first angel rebel against the heavens. The pieces were released in version 5.5.

## appearance
### STANDARD
All pieces are made of northland jade: an icy flower whose petals crumbled, a shimmering feathered accessory, a pocket watch frozen at its moment of destruction, a ceremonial cup, and a helmet with jade ornamentation from an older age. Per the wiki Skirk wears a feather resembling the Distant Pact in her hair.

## lore
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Every piece begins with the girl's trek: following her master, she crosses the freezing wastelands and the corridor of fractured mirrors and kills monsters in the ruins before finding a treasure under snow beside a silver tree, Abyssal remnants, the golden tower, the Spiral Abyss ruins or a tattered veil. The legend:
Across the five pieces: a youth asks the first angel about forbidden origins, she breaks her bonds and loves him, the Golden City forges fae and dreams of a world without tears, the heavens destroy it with crystal spikes, and her kin are cursed so that an angel who loves one alone ends as a Seelie.
### LARGE +
Piece by piece:
- Echoing Song: a youth who ventured deep underground asked the first angel about the world's most forbidden origins. She was shaken but he revealed his own origin, and the fetters of the enthroned ruler broke for her; she told him the secret of creation. He swore to be her sword, shield and conspirator, and she kissed his forehead. The heavenly moons saw.
- Distant Pact: before the Frostmoon Scions' dream, a voyager (a scribe duty-bound not to commune with primordial civilisations) was drawn by a light from a small world and its primeval dragon. She offered the dragon-lord escape from a doomed world; he refused, calling his people the meaning of the universe and choosing to be their bulwark. When she returned the dragon was gone, the earth bound under four shackles and light split into seven hues; she placed her mind in a youth's body in the city of gold.
- Moment of Oblivion: the Golden City's furnaces forged fae spirits on giant bones with frostmoon light, a creative authority the rebellious envoy gave to mortals in hope of a perfect being. The first angel dreamed of a future with no tears or death. Blue crystal spikes fell, the fae became silver mist, and the envoy was stripped of name and form with a curse on her kin: love given to one alone would crumble beauty and mind until a Seelie rose from the remains.
- Bestowed Banquet: the priests of the Golden City blamed one another for the angels' wrath. A boy of humble birth slipped in and refuted them; the chief priest told him to drink the cup of wrath and take his case to the envoys at the ashen-silver tree.
- Lost Crown: angels, silver-winged, crowned with sevenfold radiance, were loyal servants of the sky until the first angel met the youth, loved him freely and cast her star-crown into the snow, vowing to build a city and tower so humans need weep no more.
The Seelie origin is the notable canon claim: it presents the Seelie as the remains of fallen angels cursed for singling out one beloved. The wiki notes overlap with Records of Jueyun Vol. 4 on an angel and a traveller from beyond Teyvat. The Golden City is tied by wiki links to Hyperborea; the curse and the crown scene are told from a loving-rebel perspective, not a neutral historian's.
### ULTRA +
The text keeps its voice strictly separate from Skirk's own knowledge: it never says what she learned, only that she found the objects. Names are withheld, so linking the youth/voyager to Anacharsis and the dragon-lord to the Nibelung is wiki-link inference (see uncertainties). The Distant Pact's mention of the Frostmoon Scions' "sweet dream of submergence" and the Echoing Song's mention of the war of funerary flame place the narrator in a later age than the events.

## owners
### STANDARD
No owner is named. The text implies Skirk finds the relics; otherwise they are described as lost objects of an ancient civilisation.

## gameplay
@availability: permanent
### LITE
Game mechanics: the 2-piece bonus gives +15% Cryo DMG; the 4-piece bonus boosts Normal Attack and Burst damage at 0 Energy.
### STANDARD
Game mechanics (not lore): 4- and 5-star; domain Derelict Masonry Dock in Natlan (5.5) and an Artifact Strongbox. 4-piece: while the wearer has 0 Elemental Energy, Normal Attack DMG +60% and Burst DMG +60%. Dealing Normal Attack DMG pauses the Burst bonus for 6s, and dealing Burst DMG pauses the Normal Attack bonus for 6s. It can trigger off-field.
