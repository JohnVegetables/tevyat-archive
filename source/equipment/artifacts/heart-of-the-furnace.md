## summary
@spoiler: major
### LITE
Heart of the Furnace is a 4- and 5-star artifact set for Stellar Glimmer teams, released in version 7.0. Its five pieces, all titled "Foundryman's...", tell the story of a young Domovoy from Kitezhgrad: his rise at the furnace core, his secret assignment, his apprenticeship under the human scholar Aksinya and his later power as president of the Royal Energy Commission.
### STANDARD +
Each piece starts with the same comparison: something true about the furnace's heart that does not hold for people, such as warmth, the outcome of events, friendship or resolve.

## appearance
### STANDARD
No physical appearance is described; the pieces are the personal articles of a furnace worker and later official.

## lore
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The Domovoy leaves Kitezhgrad young, with a letter of recommendation, and enters the furnace core where his keen hearing and speed set him apart. Grand Duke Alvis later orders him to join a secret project and watch the others. There, in the research institute Hvergelmir, he becomes the apprentice of the silver-haired human scholar Aksinya, who places a pen in his hand and changes his life. After the old sovereign falls, he takes control of the Energy Commission. In the end he returns the pen to his teacher.
### LARGE +
- Foundryman's Conjecture: born in Kitezhgrad, he left on a beast-drawn sled and never saw the city again. He passed through the gates of the ever-burning furnace core and was recognised for hearing keen enough to diagnose the furnace by sound, speed in the narrow iron conduits and skill with Kresnik Kristall and the city's energy supply. He hoped to become a supervisor or president of the torches. At a private banquet, the elderly Grand Duke of mining and energy ordered him, "Izmaylov", to join a project of utmost secrecy and observe everyone.
- Foundryman's Observation: he first thought the silver-haired woman behind the palace curtains was a Tsar's orphan, a symbol of mercy. Inside Hvergelmir he found how wrong he was: she answered ideas before he finished them and understood the duty that made him report to the Grand Duke. He went with her into the Shadow Realm, where she gave him a pen; he added strokes to the blueprint for "the world within the egg". The Tsar fell in a tide that swept away all things, the new monarch's gaze left the forbidden researchers unprotected, and news came that she had died in the shadows.
- Foundryman's Calculus: he grasped that great power must be divided in three before it can gather, and that he had no right to claim it. The lady who shaped wind and snow left with "the heart", and the somber alchemist turned to eternal life. When the Grand Duke announced Aksinya's death, the Domovoy recognised the lie, declined his invitation and swore to return to the Shadow Realm until he found her. When the dark tide destroyed Kitezhgrad, grief gave way to elation: he won an audience with the new sovereign through a fellow Domovoy and, at a price only those present know, became master of the Energy Commission while Alvis was building Novokitezhgrad.
### ULTRA +
- Foundryman's Magnanimity: as president, he and Alvis opposed each other at every council, possibly as an act, with malice "sweeter than honeyed wine". The Tsaritsa wanted a human to run the new rail transport body; Alvis suddenly yielded his right to nominate, because he believed Izmaylov could not quell a rebellion by descendants of the purged old nobility. Vila raiders called the Burner of Cities roamed the plains by rail. Izmaylov forged weapons for the heroes of Luchkins and crushed the plot; the rebel and the heroes' leader burned in the same flame. The renown went to Izmaylov, and in the narrow alleys he swore an oath with the surviving heroes, thereby founding the High Table.
- Foundryman's Legacy: years later, white-haired and frail, he met Aksinya again. She had been spared by the Shadow Realm. He answered all she had missed, bowed, removed his hat as he did as an apprentice and gave back her pen. He said he had spent five hundred years believing she would never abandon her dream or life, and that the debt between them was cleared. He ends by saying her name.
The set's tone is of a loyal, ordinary-seeming engineer who rises through patience and calculation. Whether every political step was truly selfless is left open.

## owners
### STANDARD
The set belongs to the Domovoy Izmaylov, who is addressed by that name in the text. No other owner is named.

## gameplay
@availability: permanent
### LITE
Game mechanics: 2-piece gives +18% ATK; the 4-piece boosts ATK and the party's Stellar Glimmer reaction damage.
### STANDARD
Game mechanics (not lore): 4- and 5-star set from the Inverted Glacier domain (Everfrozen Earth, Snezhnaya). 4-piece: when the wearer triggers a Stellar Glimmer reaction or deals Stellar Glimmer reaction DMG, ATK +12% for 12s; Stellar Glimmer reaction DMG dealt by all nearby party members +50%. It works off-field, and the DMG bonus from several sets with this name does not stack.

## entry: Izmaylov and Alvis
@keys: Izmaylov, Grand Duke Alvis
@keys2: Domovoy, Royal Energy Commission, Kitezhgrad

@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
In the Heart of the Furnace text, the Domovoy Alvis, Grand Duke of mining and energy in Kitezhgrad, orders the talented young Izmaylov to join a secret project. After the old court falls, Izmaylov takes the Royal Energy Commission while Alvis oversees the building of Novokitezhgrad, and the two oppose each other in council. The Magnanimity text says some believed it was an act and that only those involved knew the truth.
