## summary
### LITE
Vourukasha's Glow is a Sumeru artifact set (Version 3.6, Molten Iron Fortress domain). Its five pieces trace the Khvarena, a daughter-like shard of life made by the Goddess of Flowers: she is entrusted to the queen of grass and trees, forged into the divine bird Simurgh, and finally shatters into the countless flowers of the Vourukasha Oasis. The set is a farewell, not a chronicle.
### STANDARD +
Its images are verdant: a pilgrim's flower insignia, a feather alive with leaves, a pure liquid timepiece, a wine goblet that was once full of nectar, and earrings of a hundred flowers. The speakers address a daughter or a friend, never an audience.

## lore
### LITE
A mother warns her "innocent daughter", the Khvarena, of a faceless nightmare in her dreams and sends her to "another mother", a verdant queen of grass and trees whose light is brighter than her own. The Khvarena will face division and death before becoming immortal, and the lord of sweet dew and the lord of grass and trees will lose their divine forms and be forgotten.
### STANDARD +
Before the Cataclysm's dark tide, the queen of grass and trees forged the Khvarena into a divine bird to guard the border between birth and death. The bird slept among pines and snow-lotuses until disaster woke her. When the dark tide struck, the former leader of the water realm was slain and her body became a sea of pure dew, the Amrita, which would dry up under the Abyss. To cleanse it the divine bird, Simurgh, shattered her own form. The scattered Khvarena fell upon the water mistress's remains and became a sea of flowers; spirits dwell there, singing of the three mothers (verdure, sweet dew, flowers).
### LARGE +
Each piece speaks a different voice. In Ancient Abscission, a friend gives the queen of trees a shard of Khvarena to protect, fearing the "chaotic darkness" more than any predictable disaster. After the mistress of flowers withered and the master of the desert was led astray by a false dream, the queen fulfilled the pact and gave the Khvarena a beautiful form: Simurgh, whose song held countless flowers' songs and whose body hid the sea of flowers. In Feast of Boundless Joy, the queen of grass and trees cuts ties with the sea of sand after the mistress of the orchard dies and returns to the rainforest to defend life. Simurgh is born from the Khvarena of the flowers and declares herself "the final remnant of my mistress", ready to burst into rays of light like a pomegranate. The wiki identifies the mothers as Nabu Malikata, Greater Lord Rukkhadevata and the Hydro Archon Egeria, and says Simurgh's shattering gave rise to the Pari of the Vourukasha Oasis.
### ULTRA +
The Heart of Khvarena's Brilliance supplies the set's philosophy: life walks with death and forgetting is the lover of remembrance; without the threat of death no life would matter, and without waves of forgetfulness no history would be worth recording. It adds that one night "the daughter must leave the embrace of the three mothers" and sacrifice herself because the world's impurity has grown. The Plume says even the pure sea would dry in the Abyss-devastated land, so the queen of grass and trees, who had no time to mourn, had to tend the mother-tree of numberless seeds. The Stamen piece adds that the lord of sweet dew and the lord of grass and trees will be forgotten and only the memory of the daughter's sacrifice kept.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not lore): a 4- and 5-star set from the Molten Iron Fortress domain and the Vourukasha's Glow strongbox. 2-piece: HP +20%. 4-piece: Elemental Skill and Burst DMG +10%; after the wearer takes DMG, that bonus rises by 80% for 5s, up to 5 independent stacks, even off-field. Released in 3.6; no later change is listed.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay interpretation (this project's recommendation, not canon text): the set suits quiet, elegiac scenes about the Vourukasha Oasis. Keep the mothers unnamed unless a character knows the Khvarena story.

## entry: Stamen of Khvarena's Origin
@keys: Stamen of Khvarena's Origin
### STANDARD
The flower piece of Vourukasha's Glow: a small flower insignia once worn by pilgrims. A mother tells her daughter, the Khvarena, that she is being sent to another mother, because the speaker dreamed of a faceless nightmare and separated her daughter from her body to hold it off.
### LARGE +
The mother predicts division, death and a harder immortality, and says the lord of sweet dew and the lord of grass and trees will be forgotten. She asks her daughter not to let her Khvarena dim and not to disgrace her mother.

## entry: Vibrant Pinion
@keys: Vibrant Pinion
### STANDARD
The plume piece: a feather-shaped ornament whose colours shimmer like leaves and flowers. It tells of the night before the sandstorm of tragedy, the dark tide, the queen of grass and trees forging Khvarena into a divine bird, the slain water leader whose body became the Amrita, and Simurgh's decision to shatter her form and cleanse it.
### LARGE +
The text says new plants and beasts united with mortals to push back the dark tide. The queen of grass and trees had broken ties with the lord of the red sands in the "lonesome years" before. All beautiful things born of the Khvarena must wither and fall in the mud, then be reborn from the Amrita as beings of the flowery sea.

## entry: Ancient Abscission
@keys: Ancient Abscission
### STANDARD
The sands piece: an ancient timepiece with Khvarena's light and a living pure liquid inside. A friend entrusts a shard of Khvarena to the queen of grass and trees, calling it a drop of light in a sea of dew; later the queen shapes it into Simurgh, whose body hides the sea of flowers.
### LARGE +
The giver foresees mortals wandering lost like infants unswaddled, strong enough to defeat wind and flame and their own flaws, but threatened by chaotic darkness. When the mistress of flowers withered, the desert master was misled by a false dream, and the gilded sand devoured the old paradise, the queen kept the covenant.

## entry: Feast of Boundless Joy
@keys: Feast of Boundless Joy
### STANDARD
The goblet piece: an opulent wine goblet once full of nectar and honeydew, now empty. After the mistress of the orchard dies, the queen of grass and trees cuts ties with the sea of sand and returns to the rainforest to defend life. The Khvarena of flowers, born of joy, give birth to Simurgh.
### LARGE +
The poem names the Khvarena's fate as inescapable withering, which only the Lord of Wisdom could learn from. It ends with Simurgh staying beside her master for a time before flying to the flowery sea.

## entry: Heart of Khvarena's Brilliance
@keys: Heart of Khvarena's Brilliance
### STANDARD
The circlet piece: earrings in which the glow of a hundred flowers seems to flow. A speaker mourns a wise friend who died young and meditates on remembrance and oblivion. It tells how the verdant bird Simurgh blossomed into countless Khvarena flowers, which fell on the remains of the Amrita's mistress and became the sea of flowers.
### LARGE +
The spirits of the sea carry the wishes of grass, tree and dew and cleanse all filth, and they sing of the three mothers. The final lines say the daughter must leave their embrace and sacrifice herself one night, because the impurity of the world grows great.
