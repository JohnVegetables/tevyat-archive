## summary
@spoiler: major
### LITE
Celestial Gift is a 4- and 5-star artifact set from the Thorny Crown of the Mountain Wind domain in Mondstadt. Its Heavensent pieces tell of a star-faring civilisation that watched the cosmos die, a voyager who woke from the void to find a shrouded world, and the eldest angel's rebellion in the frozen north against the heavens. Mechanically it gives Energy Recharge and team Elemental DMG Bonus.
### STANDARD +
The legend picks up threads also told in Finale of the Deep Galleries and Aubade of Morningstar and Moon, but from the closing days of the northern revolt, not its beginning.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Each piece is described as a gift of the high heavens to their envoy, each turned into a temptation: an everlasting flower (greed), a feather of rebirth (forgiveness at fate's end), a sundial of counter-time (a prohibition that fed rage), nectar from an unending feast (desire) and a splendid crown (arrogance).

## lore
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Across the five pieces: a star-faring civilisation watches the cosmos die, a chronicler-voyager wakes and finds a shrouded world, and the eldest angel leads a last rebellion in the frozen north against the heavens as a falling nail ends it.
### LARGE +
Piece by piece:
- Heavensent Fragrance: a golden age when every choice was permitted, the first race to enter the void spread across the stars for billions of years until the cosmos began to unravel, civilisations vanishing and galaxies evaporating. Descendants of the ancients conquered time and space but found the end unavoidable; they sealed star paths, retreated to Giant Voids and sent chroniclers to the limits of matter, where only chaos remained. A sleeping voyager awoke, still dreaming of a dragon and a fragile world.
- Heavensent Demise: a stellar spike fell from the heavens while the great city of the frozen earth stood armed, its fae ready for war. The highest angel visited the first awakened one before the end, saying that hope rests with ordinary people and that time bought was too little; she urged her companion to go home. The nail of judgment fell, and the mind of the one who walked first lingered in an unfinished dream.
- Heavensent Decree: the voyager, a chronicler bound by law never to interfere, awoke to find the star chart matched except one missing point of light: a planet she remembered visiting its sovereign, now shrouded by fields and with a new Descender-built shell. Curiosity or change moved her to reach into the world within the shell.
- Heavensent Reward: a city on the frozen earth where peoples of every nation laboured together, breaking taboos, tracing origins and teaching once-blasphemous mysteries to children. The heavens judged that free will brings only calamity and that wayward mortals must be pruned, though the text asks whether miracles defy reason.
- Heavensent Crown: beneath the faint three moons, rebels gathered in the old capital. The eldest daughter once hailed as morning star, now enthroned in sin, proclaimed that she prefers hard-won freedom to comfortable shackles and would either overthrow the Heavenly Principles or perish with fate. Angels, ancient beasts and masters of the deep answered; she declared their peace already beyond the heavens' grace.
The set is the middle act of the northern legend: Finale of the Deep Galleries shows the rebellion's origin, Celestial Gift shows its last hours under the falling nail, and Aubade of Morningstar and Moon shows how the moons later fit into the order. The text frames the heavens as arguing that free will leads only to ruin, while the rebels frame defiance as meaning; the account is told from the rebels' side.
### ULTRA +
The wiki links the "stellar spike" to the Celestial Nail, the "great city" to Hyperborea, the "eldest daughter/highest angel" to Koitar and the voyager to Anacharsis. The Decree text says the voyager's earlier visit to the sovereign of the world happened before a new Descender built the shell, which agrees with the Distant Pact text from Finale of the Deep Galleries. The Crown's prayer-like speech has the rebel leader refer to the Heavenly Principles in the capitalised plural, which the text keeps.

## owners
### STANDARD
No owner is named; the pieces are presented as gifts bestowed by the heavens upon their envoys.

## gameplay
@availability: permanent
### LITE
Game mechanics: 2-piece +20% Energy Recharge; 4-piece gives a team Elemental DMG Bonus after an Elemental Skill, strengthened by Hexerei.
### STANDARD
Game mechanics (not lore): 4- and 5-star; domain Thorny Crown of the Mountain Wind (Windrest Peak, Mondstadt). 4-piece: if the wearer has completed Witch's Homework, after using an Elemental Skill they grant nearby party members "Light's Guidance", +20% Elemental DMG Bonus matching the wearer's element, for 20s, even off-field; same-name bonuses do not stack. With the Hexerei: Secret Rite effect it becomes "Mortal Hymn": +40% Elemental DMG Bonus for both the wearer's and the active character's elements (no stacking if the elements match).
