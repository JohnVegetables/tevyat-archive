## summary
### LITE
Deepwood Memories is a Sumeru rainforest artifact set (Version 3.0, Spire of Solitary Enlightenment domain). Its five pieces are fragments of old forest legends: a labyrinth of roots and water, a lord of the forest and the maiden who inherited his crown, a huntress with a white bow, and friends who fought forgetting.
### STANDARD +
Nothing in the set is dated. It reads as a mix of oral legend and a few personal confessions, and no piece names its speaker.

## lore
### LITE
When the lord of the forest was born, the king of trees gave them a crown, which passed to the first maiden to follow in the lord's footsteps. She took in lost children and taught them the hunt, the walk through dreams and love for the forest's plants and creatures. Some became guardians who patrolled the woods and lit bonfires against the dark. One stayed behind, hunting the shadows until she dreamed a great dream of the labyrinth and hunt that covered every other dream of the forest, and then she disappeared with it.
### STANDARD +
The Laurel Coronet gives the forest's origin: the god of trees made a forest among the sands and first built a device to call rain; moonlight on the water drew a labyrinth, and the labyrinth birthed a tiger named Viaghara, the first lord. Pomegranate seeds became forest spirits, who were allowed to share the labyrinth by a pact. The last lord died defending the nurseries of life and a large whiskered cat took the name. A Time of Insight records the immortal lord melting into the forest and friends who vowed to remind one another of their forms to defeat death, until forgetfulness struck. Lamp of the Lost tells of a desert prince who fled to the rainforest and was accepted by its dream.
### LARGE +
Scholar of Vines tells two stories. A servant girl, the lord's most virtuous, understood beasts and moonlight poetry; her teaching thinned like tiger blood. In the "years of the ominous moon" a blind swordsman followed his white-armoured brother into the dark forest, found a white relic of the woods, and lost his power to wish before the huntress reached him. The pieces also include the speaker of A Time of Insight, who plans to capture dream spirits and hire sellswords to give a friend with brain damage dreams of their shared past. The wiki ties the labyrinth and Viaghara to the forest god Rukkhadevata's rainforest and says the final lord died in the Cataclysm 500 years ago, but the item does not name those events.
### ULTRA +
The maiden's story ends with a doubled loss: the dreams she left behind spread through the people's tales in many forms, yet the best-known tales had nothing to do with her, and the name they use is the name of the crown, not hers. All she kept was her true name, "a handful of water that reflected the moonlight", and the golden flower from the crown. Laurel Coronet gives the laws of the forest as a cycle, in which fruit feeds beasts and beasts become fruit, and the cat who inherited the lord's title, though it "could not match even an iota" of the old lord's might, keeps the promise not to harm the tree spirits.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not lore): a set from the Spire of Solitary Enlightenment domain (4 and 5 star) and its strongbox. 2-piece: Dendro DMG Bonus +15%. 4-piece: after Elemental Skills or Bursts hit, the target's Dendro RES is lowered by 30% for 8s, even if the wearer is off-field. Released in 3.0; no later change is listed.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay interpretation (this project's recommendation, not canon text): present these as half-remembered rainforest legends, not as history. Treat the forest lords, the maiden and the huntress as unnamed.

## entry: Labyrinth Wayfarer
@keys: Labyrinth Wayfarer
### STANDARD
The flower piece of Deepwood Memories: a gold-plated flower from the crown of the lord of the forest. It tells of the first maiden who followed the lord, took in lost children, taught them the hunt and the walk through dreams, and whose lessons produced guardians who patrol the woods. One stayed until she eroded into a vast dream of the labyrinth and hunt.
### LARGE +
Only she and those who knew the forest lord's teaching could pass the maze to the endless hunting ground; the whispers that preach death were lost inside it. After she vanished, fragments of her dream entered the tales of her descendants and the crown's name replaced hers.

## entry: Scholar of Vines
@keys: Scholar of Vines
### STANDARD
The plume piece: an emerald leaf as fluffy as a feather, plucked from a forest scholar's raiment. Two verses: the years of the lord of the labyrinth, with a servant girl who read the poetry of moonlight, and the years of the ominous moon, with a blind swordsman, a white relic and a huntress.
### LARGE +
The girl's teaching: "that which comes from the forest must return to it", so whoever follows nature need not fear death. The blind swordsman is described as obsessed with swordsmanship, gentle, and dogmatic about justice. The verse ends with a glimpse in nightmare of a dark longsword and red blooming in the water.

## entry: A Time of Insight
@keys: A Time of Insight
### STANDARD
The sands piece: a timepiece that holds mustard seeds instead of sand, used by those following the way of the wise. Its verses say the lord of the forest was immortal, melting into the forest at death. Then a speaker whose friend suffers from forgetfulness plans to capture dream spirits to remind them of their shared past.
### LARGE +
The speaker argues that souls are void concepts and memories must return to the land, so the answer is for friends to remind one another and keep everyone's image alive, defeating the natural cycle. Their plan relies on a scientist expelled by the academy, on dream-controlling forest inhabitants, and on sellswords who "once did much for me".

## entry: Lamp of the Lost
@keys: Lamp of the Lost
### STANDARD
The goblet piece: a desert-style oil lamp that has sprouted glowing green leaves. It belonged to a prince of the decaying desert kingdoms. He tells of his father falling from a tower while chasing a falcon, of burying a lover, of becoming a refugee, and of a rainforest that accepted him after a huntress with a white bow hunted him each night.
### LARGE +
The text calls the time after the fall of "the lord of folly" an age of tiny tyrants raising temples and walls on ruins. The prince meant to conquer the rainforest "like a Rishboland Tiger" but was conquered by quiet moonlight, and learned the murmurs of trees and the whispering of tigers.

## entry: Laurel Coronet
@keys: Laurel Coronet
### STANDARD
The circlet piece: a crown given by the deity of plants, an heirloom of the royal house of the labyrinth, passed at last to the king's attendant. Its verses describe the cycle of life and death, the god of trees creating a forest among the sands, the first rain-calling device, the tiger Viaghara and the labyrinth, and the forest spirits' pact.
### LARGE +
Beneath the first Vasara Tree the lord of the forest made a pact with the divine to share the labyrinth with the forest spirits. Later the sun's light was covered and the water was corrupted; the final forest lord died defending life's nurseries, and a large whiskered cat took the title and kept the promise.
