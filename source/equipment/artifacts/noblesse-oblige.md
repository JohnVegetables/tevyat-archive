## summary
### LITE
Noblesse Oblige is a 4- and 5-star artifact set (1.0) about the old aristocracy of Mondstadt: nobles who began as heroes and guides of the people and decayed into idle, grasping rulers. Its name is French for 'nobility obliges'. It drops in the Clear Pool and Mountain Cavern domain.
### STANDARD +
The pieces are Royal Flora, Royal Plume, Royal Pocket Watch, Royal Silver Urn and Royal Masque.

## appearance
### LARGE
A blue satin lily headdress, a hunting-hat falcon feather, a sapphire pocket watch that still keeps time, a sapphire urn with a silver noble insignia, and a silver masquerade mask inlaid with gold and gems.

## lore
### LITE
The nobles of old Mondstadt were examples to the people, noble in conduct as well as birth. Later nobles let indulgence and power ruin them.
### STANDARD
Each piece pairs a virtue with its decay. Royal Flora: nobles guided subjects by conduct and wisdom. Royal Plume: hunts shared with common folk. Royal Pocket Watch: punctuality as self-discipline, a worthy noble more vigilant than his people. Royal Silver Urn: treasures that stood for the people's prosperity. Royal Masque: chieftains of humble origin who welcomed the people to banquets. Each ends with later nobles abandoning that virtue.
### LARGE +
The texts say the early nobles were heroes of humble origins who became rulers, and that their longevity was cut short by self-indulgence. Hunts were once shared festivities in which servants and common folk took part as a sign of the nobles' benevolence; later the nobles claimed everything as their right. The pocket watch's schedule was overturned by indolent descendants and their timepieces became extravagant but lost their function. The urn lost its treasures, and luxury became vanity. Later banquets were for the nobles' own pleasure.
### ULTRA +
Canon history (the Mondstadt Aristocracy) says the aristocracy ruled Mondstadt for about 1,600 years after the fall of Decarabian's Mondstadt, going from protectors to tyrants, until the rebellion of Vennessa. The set does not mention the rebellion; it is the earlier, nostalgic view of the class.

## owners
### LARGE
No named owner; the relics were left behind by nobles who once ruled Mondstadt.

## roleplay
### LARGE
Use as a tragic parable about duty decaying into privilege. A modern Mondstadt scholar or Knights of Favonius member could cite it as a reminder.

## gameplay
### LARGE
Gameplay (game mechanics, not in-world fact). 2-piece: Elemental Burst DMG +20%. 4-piece: using an Elemental Burst raises the whole party's ATK by 20% for 12 seconds (multiple copies refresh but do not stack). Sources include the Clear Pool and Mountain Cavern domain and the Nine Pillars of Peace quest.
