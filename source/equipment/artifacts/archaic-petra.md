## summary
### LITE
Archaic Petra is a 4- and 5-star Geo artifact set (1.0) made of Liyue folk legends about Rex Lapis, the Lord of Geo: flowers from stone, a giant hawk of rock and jade, sundials, wine vessels and a solemn basalt mask. Its Chinese name means roughly 'ancient boulder'.
### STANDARD +
The pieces are Flower of Creviced Cliff, Feather of Jagged Peaks, Sundial of Enduring Jade, Goblet of Chiseled Crag and Mask of Solitude Basalt. The set drops from the Domain of Guyun in the Guyun Stone Forest.

## appearance
### STANDARD
A flower grown in a cliff cleft whose petals seem alive in the wind; a hard seacliff-hawk feather with a black basalt tip; a sundial carved from one large piece of jade; a dignified wine goblet of stone; and a cold basalt mask with hollow eyes.

## lore
### LITE
Each piece retells a legend about Rex Lapis, the Lord of Geo, always framed as a saying ('they say') rather than recorded fact.
### STANDARD
Flower: told that barren stone holds no life, the Lord of Geo made golden flowers burst from rock; the text suggests Liyue Harbor, grown out of lifeless mountains, may be that flower. Feather: in the Archon War he uprooted mountains to carve a giant bird of rock and jade, which flattened peaks and dived into the sea, and the stone pillars there still draw birds. Sundial: he made the earth's glittering stars into time-keeping devices to teach early humans the value of every moment. Goblet: he raised bedrock and carved a wine vessel for himself; some say there were seven. Mask: during the Archon War his aspect was one of boundless slaughter and an unchanging stone face, laid down only once the dust settled.
### LARGE +
Details the texts add: the sundial passed to Kunwu, then a scholar in training, who recounts that he once dreamed of studying in Sumeru, but after long examining the dial and finding no flaw, he sought a master craftsman to learn a trade and challenge the dial's creator. The goblet text says that going incognito among mortals should be as exquisite as basalt is impregnable. The mask text says Rex Lapis knew right from wrong and showed no mercy even to friends-turned-foes, and that the face was worn to fulfil a contract. The Feather ties into the lore of the Primordial Jade Winged-Spear.
### ULTRA +
Taken together, the set blends creation myths (flowers, hawk, sundial, goblet) with the harsher memory of the Archon War, and ends with the contract motif that Liyue's later story develops. The pieces never confirm any legend; the Flower text explicitly says the tale may simply be one of many that shroud the ancient world.

## owners
### LARGE
No owner is named for the set. The Sundial's text names Kunwu as the one who prized it, and the Goblet and Mask pieces are attributed to Rex Lapis in legend only.

## roleplay
### LARGE
Treat these as in-world folklore: a Liyue storyteller, tea-house guest or antique dealer might recount them with a wink and not as proof. Characters who know Rex Lapis' later story may find the legends amusingly or poignantly close to the truth.

## gameplay
### LARGE
Gameplay (game mechanics, not in-world fact). 2-piece: Geo DMG Bonus +15%. 4-piece: when the equipped character picks up an Elemental Shard created by Crystallize (the wiki also lists the Lunar-Crystallize reaction), all party members gain a 35% DMG Bonus for that element for 10s; only one element's bonus applies at once, and in Co-Op it also affects other players' characters. Source: Domain of Guyun (a Blessing domain, recommended Electro) and the Archaic Petra strongbox.
