## summary
### LITE
Blizzard Strayer is a Cryo artifact set (4 and 5 star, added 1.2) from Dragonspine. Its pieces tell of the Icebreaker, a quiet hero from a mountain city lost to endless winter, who left with the hopes of its people and was never heard of again. The wiki connects it to Imunlaukr and the fallen nation of Sal Vindagnyr. It drops in the Peak of Vindagnyr domain.
### STANDARD +
The pieces are Snowswept Memory (flower), Icebreaker's Resolve (feather), Frozen Homeland's Demise (hourglass), Frost-Weaved Dignity (ice cup) and Broken Rime's Echo (crown).

## appearance
### LARGE
Frost-coated relics: a long-extinct flower covered in beads of frost, a falcon feather coated in pearls of ice, an hourglass of fine powdered snow instead of sand, a cup carved of ice for unfreezable liquor, and a winter crown.

## lore
### LITE
A hero left his snow-sealed city to find salvation. A maiden who painted frescoes waited for him. He did not return in time and was branded a deserter; his deeds were forgotten, though the frost-flower never withered.
### STANDARD
Snowswept Memory shows a maiden pinning a frost flower on the hero's chest and promising a fourth fresco with his likeness. Icebreaker's Resolve follows him through the storm toward a lost summer palace, carrying the hope of homeless children. Frozen Homeland's Demise says the mountain city believed cold from the sky could freeze time. Frost-Weaved Dignity portrays the silent warrior drinking bitter wine from a cup of ice while the painter begs him to live rather than die forgotten. Broken Rime's Echo has him donning the winter crown and vanishing into the snowstorm.
### LARGE +
The set ends with the maiden singing to comfort her people and believing he would bring back spring. But he did not return in time, and 'hateful words long ground to dust' accused him of flight. The texts treat him as an 'outlander' searching for hope on another's behalf. The ending is bitter: the heroism of the Icebreaker was forgotten, buried in northern storms, yet the flower never withered.
### ULTRA +
Sal Vindagnyr's own history supplies the context: an ancient nation hit by the Skyfrost Nail, its princess (Varuch's daughter) painting frescoes of the future, and Imunlaukr, a hero from another land and namesake of the Imunlaukr clan, one of Mondstadt's founding clans. According to the wiki the princess gave him the greatsword Snow-Tombed Starsilver before he left and said she would dedicate her fourth fresco to him, though the storm drowned out her words. The set text itself leaves all names out.

## owners
### LARGE
No canon holder is named. The wiki treats the Icebreaker as Imunlaukr.

## status
### LARGE
The hero, the princess and the nation are long dead; the relics are remnants found in Dragonspine's domain. Sal Vindagnyr's story is background lore, not a present-day location.

## roleplay
### LARGE
Use as melancholy lore. A character who touches the relics might hear storm-voices or feel the cold of the gone hero. Do not give the hero a name unless the speaker knows the Sal Vindagnyr history.

## gameplay
### LARGE
Gameplay (game mechanics, not in-world fact). A 4- and 5-star set from the Domain of Blessing Peak of Vindagnyr; a Cryo 2-piece (+15% Cryo DMG Bonus) and a 4-piece that raises CRIT Rate by 20% against Cryo-affected opponents and 20% more against Frozen ones. The bonus applies to the wearer only, and Frozen requires the Frozen reaction itself.

### STANDARD
The Icebreaker is the unnamed hero of the Blizzard Strayer texts, a silent warrior who left the snow-sealed mountain city to seek hope and was accused of flight when he did not return. The wiki identifies him as Imunlaukr.
### LARGE +
His likeness was to be painted on the maiden's fourth fresco. The texts never say where he went or how he fell.
