## summary
### LITE
Crimson Witch of Flames is a Pyro artifact set (4 and 5 star) built around the story of a young woman who lost her hopes and loved ones in a calamity and became a witch who burned monsters and demons. Its texts call her 'the Crimson Witch of Flame'. Later canon (Archon Quest, 2.1) identifies her as the Fatui Harbinger Signora, once a maiden of Mondstadt. The set is earned in the Hidden Palace of Zhou Formula.
### LARGE +
The five pieces are Witch's Flower of Blaze, Witch's Ever-Burning Plume, Witch's End Time, Witch's Heart Flames and Witch's Scorching Hat. Read in order, they sketch a single life: a student, a disaster, a path of fire, and a person who cannot see how much she has destroyed.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Pyro-themed relics: a flower that survives flame, a never-dying feather, a small timepiece holding a hot liquid, a flame-spitting urn holding liquid fire, and a witch's tall hat with a broad brim.
### LARGE +
The hat is the classic pointed witch's hat with a large brim; the text says the brim blocked her sight. The timepiece is described as a small vessel of high-temperature liquid, said to be the molten souls of evil spirits.

## lore
### LITE
The texts follow the 'Crimson Witch of Flame' from a maiden with a bright future to a hunter of monsters. A crisis centuries ago ended her hopes; from the ashes she burned away her pain with fire.
### STANDARD
Per the item texts, a crisis centuries ago took the maiden's loved ones, her past and her promised future. From it the Crimson Witch of Flame was born, dreaming of burning away all the demons in the world. Her fire touched only monsters and demons, yet people shut their windows and tried to drive her off. She wanted no comfort; only the birds beside her understood. Historians were loath to remember her.
### LARGE +
A common flower is described as strangely able to withstand her flames, a hint that painful and beautiful memories were two sides of one coin. The Heart Flames text says rumour held she gave up her mortal body to become liquid fire, and that love and longing once lived in her before the fire burned that part away. The Scorching Hat text ends on the image of a witch who burned on, blind to the scale of her destruction and unwilling to look at her burnt face in the water.

Two details tie the texts to named places. The End Time text says the timepiece was a Hydro timepiece given to her before the calamity; one cycle was the time needed to finish her study at the Akademiya (the Sumeru Akademiya), and by the time the countdown ended and she came home, its previous owner (the giver) had become a casualty of the crisis. The Scorching Hat text says the hat let her hone her Pyro skills while still an apprentice.

The set text itself never names her. The link to Signora comes from elsewhere: the Archon Quest 'Duel Before the Throne' (2.1) presents Signora as the Crimson Witch of Flame/Embers, and Signora's profile items tell of a Mondstadt maiden named Rosalyne who studied at the Akademiya and received a Hydro timepiece from Rostam of the Knights of Favonius before she left. The wiki's trivia for the set treats it as the story of La Signora, historically known as the Crimson Witch of Flame.
### ULTRA +
The texts and the Signora story agree on the broad arc: a student at the Akademiya, a gift to count the time away, a return to find the giver dead after a crisis, then a path of fire against monsters. Signora's profile items further say that the fire nearly consumed her until she met Pierro and received a Cryo Delusion, after which she became La Signora of the Fatui Harbingers. That later stage is not part of this set's text; keep it out of any scene that treats the set as only an old relic.

## owners
### LARGE
By the texts, the relics belonged to or were touched by the Crimson Witch of Flame. Canon (the 2.1 Archon Quest and Signora's profile) identifies her with Signora, whose original name was Rosalyne. The relics found in domains are treated in game as remnants; no canon scene shows Signora carrying them.

## status
@version: 2.1
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
Signora died in Inazuma during 'Duel Before the Throne' (2.1); her Harbinger seat remains vacant as of 7.1. Characters who know only the artifact texts know only the legend of a nameless 'Crimson Witch of Flame'; the identification with Signora is not common knowledge.

## roleplay
### LARGE
Treat these as relics with a tragic legend, not a trophy. A character who handles them may sense warmth or tell an old story of a maiden who became a witch. A character who does not know the Archon Quest revelation should not call the witch Signora.

## gameplay
### LARGE
Gameplay (game mechanics, not in-world fact). A 4-star and 5-star set from the Domain of Blessing Hidden Palace of Zhou Formula. The 2-piece bonus gives +15% Pyro DMG Bonus. The 4-piece bonus raises Overloaded, Burning and Burgeon DMG by 40% and Vaporize and Melt DMG by 15%, and each Elemental Skill use adds to the 2-piece Pyro bonus for 10 seconds, up to 3 stacks.
