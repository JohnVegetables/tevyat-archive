## summary
### LITE
Heart of Depth is a Hydro artifact set (4 and 5 star, added 1.2), also called Ocean Conqueror in the Adventurer Handbook. Its pieces show the last voyage of the Skipper's Chief Mate: a sardonic, hard-drinking exile from a gray aristocratic land, who joined a Liyue sea captain's hunt for the monster that haunted them both. The wiki names him as Parsifal.
### STANDARD +
The pieces are Gilded Corsage (flower), Gust of Nostalgia (feather), Copper Compass (sands), Goblet of Thundering Deep (goblet) and Wine-Stained Tricorne (circlet). The set text ties in with the Tales From the Waves books and the Serpent Spine weapon text.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Sea-worn keepsakes: a corsage faded by sea-salt wind, a red feather stained by blood and brine, a bronze seaman's compass, a faded finely crafted wine cup ground down by sands in the sea's depths, and a tricorne hat thick with the smell of alcohol.

## lore
### LITE
The Chief Mate was no native of Liyue but came from a gray land ruled by an aristocracy, rumoured to have disgraced his house. He drank to forget, followed the Skipper to sea, and met his end in the vengeful hunt for a giant sea creature.
### STANDARD
The Gilded Corsage shows the warship leaving harbour with the Skipper and the Chief Mate, who hums a shanty of his own that mocks his story: the scoundrel who gave up his family name and the witch who hunted him never reached the sea, and the step-brother who could not be an heir became head at last, 'or did he?'. The Gust of Nostalgia describes his arrival with only a slim sword and a sapphire-blue feather pinned to his mantle, the feather later stained red; at his last moment he remembers his drowned days. The Copper Compass is the Skipper's, guiding the ship over whirlpools and guiding the man whose hatred hid behind laughter; when he tries to leave, the Skipper reminds him he signed a contract aboard.
### LARGE +
The Goblet falls from the Chief Mate's hand into the ocean and recalls a woman with sapphire eyes and a scar who promised to repay him for the mark. The Tricorne shows the hard-drinking officer given important duties by the Skipper, who does not reprimand him; storm and sea hat lead to the creature that gnaws at his dreams, and the call to set sail. Key beats from the texts: both men are 'people without a thing to our names'; the Chief Mate's shanty names a half-brother and a witch, and his memories contain the witch's scar. In the Goblet he questions how many times he has forgotten the past and concludes that all deaths are in vain and that what has sunk cannot be saved. The wiki, using the Parsifal, Eberhart and Blue-Eyed Spear Witch pages, reads these as the Mondstadt story of the Phantom Thief Parsifal, his half-brother Eberhart and the spear witch, which the set only hints at.
### ULTRA +
The wiki's Skipper page, drawing on the Tales From the Waves books and Serpent Spine, says the Skipper was a Liyue monster hunter from about a thousand years ago who lost his crew and navigator to a huge sea monster, swore revenge, took in the exiled Parsifal and later died killing it. Those details come from books and a weapon text, not from the set, so treat them as wiki-compiled lore.

## owners
### STANDARD
The Chief Mate, whom the wiki identifies as Parsifal, and the Skipper (the compass is his). The wiki lists Parsifal as the set's subject.

## status
### STANDARD
Both men are dead; the set is background lore about events around a thousand years ago. Nothing in 6.x or 7.0-7.1 changes this set.

## roleplay
### LARGE
Use as salty, bittersweet shanty-tavern lore: bravado over grief, humming, wine. Do not name the Chief Mate in a speaker's mouth unless they know the Parsifal story, and do not invent songs beyond the lines in the texts.

## gameplay
### LARGE
Gameplay (game mechanics, not in-world fact). A 4- and 5-star Hydro set from the domain Peak of Vindagnyr on Dragonspine. 2-piece: Hydro DMG Bonus +15%. 4-piece: after using an Elemental Skill, Normal and Charged Attack DMG +30% for 15s.

### STANDARD
The Chief Mate of Heart of Depth is a cynical, hard-drinking exile from a gray land ruled by an aristocracy (the item text's link points to Mondstadt) who sailed under the Skipper. The wiki names him Parsifal. The texts show his drinking, his forgotten past and his blue feather, not his full story.
### LARGE +
He carries a slim sword and a faded blue feather, keeps the memory of a sapphire-eyed woman's scar, and is promised the chance for revenge against a sea creature.

### STANDARD
The Skipper is the rough, jovial captain of the great warship in the Heart of Depth texts; his real name is unknown. His compass guided the ship through whirlpools and his crew contract bound the Chief Mate. The wiki connects him to Serpent Spine and Tales From the Waves.
