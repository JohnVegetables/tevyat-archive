## summary
### LITE
Vermillion Hereafter is a 4- and 5-star artifact set that boosts ATK after an Elemental Burst. Its five pieces (Flowering Life, Feather of Nascent Light, Solar Relic, Moment of the Pact, Thundering Poise) have item text about the Chasm in Liyue: the mountain tribes' plea to a four-armed yaksha, the Millelith soldiers who marched into the abyss, and the battle that ended with the yaksha and many soldiers lost.
### STANDARD +
Added in version 2.6. The text is legend told by miners and merchants; no one is named, and the set is a companion to the older Tenacity of the Millelith set.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Relics of the Chasm story: a small flower given as a memento, a dim eagle feather, an ancient solar timepiece, a crystal-sand wine cup, and a fearsome mask.

## lore
@spoiler: minor
### LITE
The pieces follow a legend of the Chasm: a four-armed yaksha answers the tribes' plea and fights the darkness there, supported by Millelith soldiers who do not return.
### STANDARD
Mountain tribes ask a four-armed yaksha for help; he drinks their wine, promises nothing and goes east into the Chasm, while Millelith soldiers march into the abyss beside him. Both are lost, and the pieces insist the land will remember.
### LARGE +
Flowering Life: a Millelith soldier, sworn to Rex Lapis, tells a mountain tribe's daughter he will go with the four-armed yaksha into the depths and leaves her a small flower, asking not to be forgotten. Feather of Nascent Light: a hero who plucks an eagle's feather from the Chasm's highest cliff is said to earn the right to die beside the adepti; soldiers' doubts about being forgotten are described before the battle ends and the land remembers them. Solar Relic: a story about a fallen solar chariot and glowing sand crystal, said to come from Millelith who held out in the lightless abyss five hundred years ago. Moment of the Pact: mountain tribespeople offer wine to the four-armed yaksha and beg for help; he drinks it all, promises nothing and walks east, and the cup remains as a testament of the pact. Thundering Poise: a verse about the yaksha's battle at the Chasm, ending with the vortex and the lament of birds.
Details across the pieces. The tribes in Moment of the Pact say Rex Lapis's grace keeps their lives free of terror and that a dark shadow now clouds the blessing of the fallen star. Flowering Life says the Millelith stood guard when dark demons threatened, and the soldier speaks of braving crystal halls and twisted beasts. Solar Relic notes the Millelith gathered glowing sand to light their way, resembling pale moonlight, and kept a timepiece to mark their time in the abyss; it is described as proof that others later took up the duty of those who fell. Feather of Nascent Light states that the cries of the dark forces were silenced and many soldiers rest in the cavern.
### ULTRA +
Several pieces carry deliberate doubt. In Feather of Nascent Light the soldiers voice despair, saying that dying unremembered, like feathers in a whirlwind, makes saving and defending seem meaningless; the narrator answers that the land will remember. Solar Relic frames the sun-chariot story with a sceptical merchant's warning not to trust folktales, and ties Shenglu Hall's merchants to the sand crystal trade in the same hedged way. In Thundering Poise the yaksha is called a hero who sinks into the vortex, a loss mourned as a life spent for the dawn. Compared with Tenacity of the Millelith, which centres on the Millelith's ceremonial customs, Vermillion Hereafter centres on the yaksha and the tribes who asked for help.

## owners
### STANDARD
No owner is named. The pieces speak of a four-armed yaksha, Millelith soldiers and mountain tribespeople, all unnamed.

## gameplay
@availability: permanent
### LITE
Game mechanics: the 2-piece bonus raises ATK; the 4-piece bonus gives an ATK buff after an Elemental Burst that grows as the wearer loses HP.
### STANDARD
Game mechanics (not lore): obtained from The Lost Valley domain and the Artifact Strongbox. 2-piece: ATK +18%. 4-piece: after an Elemental Burst, the wearer gains Nascent Light (ATK +8% for 16s); each time their HP decreases, ATK rises another 10%, up to 4 times, at most once per 0.8s. It is dispelled when they leave the field, and a new Burst replaces it.

### STANDARD
In Moment of the Pact, the mountain tribes' elders offered wine and asked a lone four-armed traveller for help against a shadow troubling the Chasm. He drank it, promised nothing and went east; what followed is left to the listener's knowledge. In Thundering Poise he enters the Chasm with a blade and is lost in the collapse. The text never names him.
