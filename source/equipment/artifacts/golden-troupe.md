## summary
### LITE
Golden Troupe is a 4- and 5-star artifact set from Fontaine, added in Version 4.0. Its five pieces tell the story of Boethius: a boy from a conquered island tribe who became a musician and Harmost of Remuria, and, after the empire's fall, the troupe that clung to the dream of its golden age. Its bonuses boost Elemental Skill damage.
### STANDARD +
The pieces are Golden Song's Variation (flower), Golden Bird's Shedding (plume), Golden Era's Prelude (sands), Golden Night's Bustle (goblet) and Golden Troupe's Reward (circlet). The set and the Golden Troupe faction share a name: this entry is the artifact set, the faction is a separate entry.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Only names and lore text describe the pieces. The theatrical, musical and golden imagery (songs, birds, preludes, a golden dome, a theater) is the set's recurring motif.

## lore
@version: 4.0
@spoiler: major
### LITE
The pieces follow the rise and ruin of the Remurian golden order through the musician-Harmost Boethius and end in the troupe that waits for the symphony to play again.
### STANDARD
The pieces follow Boethius: a conquered child who becomes a musician and Harmost, a rift between two Harmosts over mercy toward the tribes, the God King's failed last orders, the people turned into golems, and the faded-castle troupe waiting for the symphony to return.
### LARGE +
- Golden Song's Variation: the "song of fate" spread civilization along Remuria's waterways. A young musician from a short-lived tribe befriended a conquered warrior-servant (later famous as Cassiodor); both rose in golden Capitolium to become proud masters under the God King. The musician urges his friend to abandon the humble past and trust the glorious kingdom, which only has eyes for a perfect future.
- Golden Bird's Shedding: the harmonious symphony closed as empires do, when progress turned to arrogance and exploitation. After the unconquered banded together, one Harmost pleads for compassion toward the "people of bygone times" while the other answers with scorn, calls for purging the tribes (blaming the Bane Dragon Scylla for destroying towers and killing musicians), and the golden era breaks into war. The God King wakes startled.
- Golden Era's Prelude: the musician says he came from a lone isle, was taken as a child to the capital after Remuria's warriors announced conquest, and escaped bondage through his fingers and voice. He gave up his name and tribe to be known only as Boethius.
- Golden Night's Bustle: terrified and remorseful, the God King gave his last orders to his guards and musicians to restore peace, but arrogance and prejudice could not be undone in a night. The Harmosts and rulers fell, the Fell Dragon Prince's armies and the king's own were swept away with the palace, and the people, without the guiding song, became disfigured golems. Boethius lay in the rubble muttering about a betrayal.
- Golden Troupe's Reward: beneath the sea a faded golden castle stands. Its musicians, who have lost their score, mourn the perfect order and wait with silent golems for the symphony to play again, when the loyal will win the future as their prize.
Wider canon (not in these pieces) fills in names: Boethius became one of Remus's four Harmosts, his friend Cassiodor another, and after Remuria's fall Boethius mustered survivors into the Golden Troupe, who gathered in the Faded Castle. Cassiodor later founded the Marechaussee Hunters to fight the troupe and its golems (see the Marechaussee Hunter set, whose Hunter's Brooch makes this link).
### ULTRA +
Note how the same event looks different by piece. Golden Bird's Shedding shows two Harmosts divided, Golden Night's Bustle blames "arrogance and prejudice" for the collapse, and Golden Troupe's Reward shows the survivors justifying themselves with hope. None of the pieces says exactly who betrayed whom; Boethius's own words in the goblet text point to a single act of treachery, which wider canon treats as his betrayal of the empire.

## owners
### STANDARD
No named owner; the set is narrative item text about Boethius, Cassiodor, Remus and their circle.

## gameplay
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not lore): the 2-piece bonus is Elemental Skill DMG +20%. The 4-piece bonus is Elemental Skill DMG +25%, with a further +25% while the wearer is off the field (cleared 2s after taking the field). Obtained from the Denouement of Sin domain in Fontaine's Belleau Region, near Romaritime Harbor, and the artifact strongbox. That domain's Ley Line Disorder gives a CRIT DMG bonus whenever a character's HP rises or falls; its enemies are out-of-control clockwork meka left after an accident at the Fontaine Research Institute of Kinetic Energy Engineering.
### LARGE +
The domain text mentions legends of the first Lochknights hiding in the wilderness with Lochfolk to atone for a sin beyond redemption.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay interpretation (this project's recommendation, not canon text): present the set as poetic, in-universe artifacts of Remurian memory, with a sadder, more faithful tone than the set's bonuses suggest. Characters should not casually know the Golden Troupe's real methods unless they have read the relevant Faded Castle materials.
