## summary
### LITE
Nymph's Dream is a 4- and 5-star artifact set that boosts Hydro damage and ATK. It was released in Version 3.6, long before Fontaine opened, and its lore tells a quiet story: orphans playing heroes, wicked mages and fell dragons, a tea party that pauses the adventure, and the later scattering of the "Narzissen champions" after a catastrophe.
### STANDARD +
The pieces are Odyssean Flower (flower), Wicked Mage's Plumule (plume), Nymph's Constancy (sands), Heroes' Tea Party (goblet) and Fell Dragon's Monocle (circlet). It is the one set here that is obtained in Sumeru but belongs to Fontaine's story.

## appearance
@version: 3.6
### STANDARD
A pocket watch (Nymph's Constancy) pieced from rejected parts of other devices, a dark green feather from a hat (Wicked Mage's Plumule) and a monocle are described, but no piece has an owner who is shown on-screen.

## lore
@version: 3.6
@spoiler: minor
### LITE
Told as children's play and reminiscence, the set follows the Narzissen champions from their games to their separation after a disaster.
### STANDARD
Told as children's play, the pieces move from a tea party that pauses the adventure, through stories of heroes, mages and fell dragons, to the Director's departure and the scattering of the champions after a catastrophe.
### LARGE +
- Heroes' Tea Party: between adventures the champions, mages, knights and fell dragons all pause for the Vice Director's very good refreshments. A little girl arrives at a home that sees neither sun nor moon, is embraced by the tall Director and led by the Vice Director to the others.
- Wicked Mage's Plumule: where there are heroes there must be wicked mages. The children gaze at a dark green feather pinned to the Vice Director's hat and are told to get along. Before leaving on business with the Director, the Vice Director gives the feather, as a loan, to one child, who is named only by a black bar. It never becomes a mage's staff but travels with its owner to the source of the calamity of parting, then returns.
- Nymph's Constancy: a pocket watch made from rejected parts, owned by a tiny champion who loved machines, which fell, together with the person it was gifted to, into the primordial liquid that dissolves all. It carries a bedtime story of a dark empire ruled by the fell dragon Narcissus, a sleeping princess, and a pearl of water from which a tiny life was born.
- Fell Dragon's Monocle: heroes and dragons depend on which story is told, and heroes who live too long become dragons. A dragon dreams of being reborn as a new universe, while the victorious hero loses the thing he valued and thereafter imagines a kingdom run on machines not powered by the elements.
- Odyssean Flower: the kingdom's shadow came not from the dragon beating the hero but from both being lost in the world. The Director and her sisters set out to defeat the evil at its source, her assistant boarded a battle ship and slept beneath the waters, and the champions scattered. Some joined the Marechaussee Phantom or the Special Security and Surveillance Patrol; some went with explorers; some sought a path with machinery and bodies of steel; some began again in the name of the nymphs.
The wiki's Trivia states that the set's lore relates to Fontaine and the Narzissenkreuz Institute, and its Institute and Marechaussee Phantom pages cite these pieces: the play-acting, tea parties and a Vice Director with good refreshments, and the Odyssean Flower for Institute members recruited by the Marechaussee Phantom after the Director and Vice Director vanished in the cataclysm 500 years ago.
### ULTRA +
Read together with the Marechaussee Hunter set, the games here echo the "hero" and "evil dragon" childhood play that set recalls (Emanuel as hero, Karl as Jabberwock). The set itself never states a link between the two sets, and the machine-minded ending of Fell Dragon's Monocle names no person.

## owners
### STANDARD
No named owner. The pieces speak of anonymous children and of the Director and Vice Director.

## gameplay
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not lore): the 2-piece bonus is Hydro DMG +15%. The 4-piece bonus grants a Mirrored Nymph stack (8s) after attacks, skills and bursts hit opponents; 1, 2 and 3+ stacks give ATK +7%/16%/25% and Hydro DMG +4%/9%/15%, and stacks from each attack type are independent. Obtained from Molten Iron Fortress (Gavireh Lajavard, Girdle of the Sands, Sumeru), whose Ley Line Disorder gives Elemental Mastery for Dendro-related reactions, and from the artifact strongbox. Added in 3.6.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay interpretation (this project's recommendation, not canon text): treat the set as nostalgic and wistful, a childhood game remembered after loss. Do not name the children or confirm which Fontainian figures they became unless a canon source for that scene is cited.
