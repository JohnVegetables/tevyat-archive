## summary
### LITE
Lavawalker is a Pyro-resistance artifact set (4 and 5 star, added 1.0). Its five pieces tell of the Lavawalker, a proud sage who wore a flame-flower brooch, a crimson agate circlet and a phoenix feather, walked onto the burning sea of the Mare Jivari and was never seen again. In Natlan, the Abyss Lector Enjou is linked to the name.
### STANDARD +
The pieces are Lavawalker's Resolution (flower), Salvation (feather), Torment (hourglass), Epiphany (goblet) and Wisdom (circlet). The set is found in the Hidden Palace of Zhou Formula.

## appearance
### LARGE
Heat-proof relics: a fiery-red flower like glowing agate that blooms only in flames, a feather from a bird that sings in fire, an hourglass of burning sand that leaves no mark on its glass, a goblet that still keeps a trace of warmth though empty, and a circlet of crimson agate.

## lore
### LITE
A sage called the Lavawalker defied the flames of the Mare Jivari. His teacher, jealous, sent him into the fire wearing the circlet; he strolled out over the lava and faded into the horizon.
### STANDARD
Lavawalker's Wisdom says the sage crafted a crimson agate circlet to resist the flames, and that his skill sparked fear and jealousy among peers and seniors, one of whom, his teacher, called it blasphemy and demanded he step into the flaming sea. He walked calmly onto the lava and vanished. Lavawalker's Resolution is his brooch: before the end he told his people they would see the flower's embers shining through dark smoke, and they followed its light to the edge of the Mare Jivari, finding flowers but not the sage. Lavawalker's Salvation is a feather he took from a solitary fire-bird revered as a totem and a sign of nobility; wearing it he withdrew from the world, and since then cries are heard over the Mare Jivari, which no one can tell from the bird's song or his sighs.
### LARGE +
Lavawalker's Epiphany presents him as a wise man who treated wine, even lava drunk from a heat-proof goblet, as only a spark for ingenuity, and says the goblet was proudest the moment before his final journey. Lavawalker's Torment continues his story beyond the Mare Jivari: he is said to have lived another hundred years as a hermit, made a burning-sand hourglass to mark time, and suffered most from loneliness, having lost his disciples and family, which burned him more than any flame.
### ULTRA +
The set texts never name the sage or say where he came from. The only identified link is Enjou's (see owners). The tellings do not fully agree: Resolution and Wisdom end with him disappearing into the fire, while Salvation and Torment leave him alive long after crossing it.

## owners
### STANDARD
The set texts name only the Lavawalker. The 5.8 world quest The Last Survivor of Tenochtzitoc connects the title to Enjou, an Abyss Lector who gives the Traveler a keepsake called Lavawalker's Resolution using the same words as the set text.
### LARGE +
The wiki reads this as Enjou having entered the burning Mare Jivari and earned the name, while noting that the area's displacement from normal time leaves open whether he was the sage of legend or is echoing it.

## status
### LARGE
The relics are legend and remnants; the Lavawalker's fate is unresolved. In 5.8 Enjou says he is heading deeper into the Mare Jivari to look for a homeland he cannot remember, and that the Traveler would likely not see him again.

## roleplay
### LARGE
Treat the set as a melancholic legend of pride, envy and loneliness. A character handling a piece might feel lingering warmth. Do not state as fact that Enjou is the original sage unless the speaker has the 5.8 events, and then present it as a strong hint rather than a proven identity.

## gameplay
### LARGE
Gameplay (game mechanics, not in-world fact). A 4- and 5-star set; the 2-piece raises Pyro RES by 40% and the 4-piece increases DMG against opponents affected by Pyro by 35%. It drops in the Hidden Palace of Zhou Formula; the 5-star can also come from the Lavawalker artifact strongbox.

### STANDARD
The Lavawalker is the sage of the artifact set's texts, who crossed the burning Mare Jivari, was resented by his teacher and peers, and disappeared leaving a flower, a feather, a circlet and a goblet behind. The set never gives his name.

### LARGE
In The Last Survivor of Tenochtzitoc (5.8), Enjou, who has been hiding behind disguises at Easybreeze Holiday Resort, leaves the Traveler a flower keepsake with words matching the set's Resolution text and says he serves the Abyss Order though it is not his home. The wiki says he earned the name Lavawalker by entering the burning Mare Jivari, but stresses that this is unclear because of the area's time displacement.
