## summary
### LITE
Husk of Opulent Dreams is a 4 and 5 star Inazuma artifact set that tells, in dream-like fragments, the life of a puppet made to hold a "heart": the figure once known as Scaramouche, Sixth of the Eleven Fatui Harbingers (the Balladeer), who now goes by the Wanderer. It is a defence and Geo-damage set.
### STANDARD +
It is dropped by the Slumbering Court domain on Seirai Island, and came with Version 2.3. The texts follow the puppet from creation and abandonment, through the faces and names he tried on, to the stage name he chose for himself, with the theme that he is still living out a story whose ending is not written.

## appearance
### LARGE
Five pieces: Bloom Times (flower, a golden six-petalled ornament that never wilts, symbol of the transience of mortal glories), Plume of Luxury (plume, a feather-shaped token from a secluded hall), Song of Life (sands, a mechanism with the heart removed and its hands no longer moving, a small overseas object to Inazuma eyes), Calabash of Awakening (goblet, a gourd gilded with powdered gold and black paint, original colour lost) and Skeletal Hat (circlet, a hat that shielded a wanderer from sun and rain and became a tool for hiding faces).

## lore
### LITE
The pieces read as snapshots of a wanderer: a vessel made for a heart and left to sleep, a gold ornament of the Shogun's, a young traveller turned away by a rain, and a name taken from the villains of Inazuma's theatre.
### LARGE +
Song of Life: the puppet was made to hold a "heart" but wept in his dreams; his maker judged him too fragile as a person or a tool, did not destroy him, and let him sleep, and in her later works left aside designs that could store such a heart. Later he awoke and wandered, watching hearts that were good, upright, strong and gentle, and he wanted one too. He eventually obtained one, the very purpose of his existence, but not what he wanted: it carried no blessing and was a sacrifice full of selfishness, hypocrisy, cunning and curses in an amiable husk. If he tore it out he would feel nothing at all.

Plume of Luxury: the wanderer no longer thinks about moonlit nights at Tatarasuna or the furnace fires. Four unnamed voices show the same gold ornament, a proof of identity from the Shogun, in different lights: a kind young deputy told him to keep his identity hidden on his travels; an upright Inspector explained that because he was neither man nor mechanism he had to be dealt with in a certain way; hopeful eyes saw it as something that could save people; and a shrine maiden said she would not abandon him and would send for help. In the end the golden arrow-feather lay in dust and all the tales burned in karmic flames.

Skeletal Hat: a child of a Tatarasuna craftsperson, a boatman, an underling who calls him "Lord Harbinger", and an old woman on Yashiori Island each ask where he is going. He tells the sick child he is bound for Inazuma City, gets onto a boat with a stranger's help, slaps the chatty underling, and tells him he is going east to Mondstadt; on returning, he says he is headed for an appointed meeting, and a lady in foreign garb throws him a small crystal sphere from the shore, which he lifts toward the dying sun.
### ULTRA +
Calabash of Awakening: the Raiden Gokaden had five branches (Amenoma, Futsu, Isshin, Hyakume, Senju), but only Amenoma still keeps its line, and Isshin barely has descendants. People think time did this, but the text says the wanderer did it as a revenge against the bladesmith, then dropped his plan halfway because it became dull, and would call it only a little experiment into human nature in a tone he learned from a researcher. Inazuman theatre has a stock role "Kunikuzushi" for schemers who topple nations; at the end of his wandering he chose that name himself, and no longer remembers the earlier one. Plays are named by joining the titles of their three acts, and the text ends with his third act still running.

Bloom Times: in a dream the wanderer sees a phantom dancing in moonlight, like a blank young man from long ago, and is told that the "heart" he once acquired was a prop for lies, that this false body can aspire to rule the world, and that it is only a passing dream of glory; he awakes, and what fades is the imagined future, not him.

## owners
### LARGE
The pieces are associated with the Wanderer; the Shogun's gold ornament in the Plume of Luxury is described as a token given by the Almighty Shogun, and the Skeletal Hat and the gourd are his travelling things.

## gameplay
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
Gameplay, not lore: the 2-piece bonus is DEF +30%. The 4-piece bonus grants "Curiosity" stacks: on the field, a stack per Geo attack (once per 0.3s); off the field, a stack every 3s; each of up to 4 stacks gives 6% DEF and 6% Geo DMG Bonus, and a stack drops after 6 seconds without gaining one. It drops from the Slumbering Court domain.

## entry: The puppet and the Shogun's gold ornament
@keys: Husk of Opulent Dreams, Plume of Luxury, Calabash of Awakening
@version: 2.3
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
In the Husk of Opulent Dreams texts, the wanderer is never named, but he is a puppet made to hold a "heart", has been a Lord Harbinger, lived at Tatarasuna, and carries a gold ornament that the Shogun gave as proof of identity. The texts describe his name "Kunikuzushi" as one he chose from Inazuman theatre, in which the name belongs to schemers who bring down states. The Raiden Shogun's voice-over line "About Kunikuzushi" and the Wishes quest are the wiki's cited corroboration.

## entry: Tatarasuna and the Kabukimono years
@keys: Tatarasuna, Kabukimono, Shakkei Pavilion
@version: 2.3
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The Plume of Luxury recalls moonlit nights over Tatarasuna and its furnace fires. Wiki summary pages on Mikoshi Nagamasa say a puppet found in the Shakkei Pavilion was introduced to the workers at Tatarasuna and welcomed as one of them under the name "Kabukimono", though the artifact texts do not use that name. Treat the link as wiki-derived rather than stated in the item text.
