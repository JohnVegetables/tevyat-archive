## summary
### LITE
The Exile is an Energy Recharge artifact set (3 and 4 star, added 1.0), called Legacy in the Adventurer Handbook. Its five pieces mourn an aristocrat's carefree childhood, a family feud that wrecked his house, and a lonely exile in which he kept only a few treasured objects. The wiki links him to Parsifal and the Eberhart feud; the texts name no one.
### STANDARD +
The pieces are Exile's Flower, Exile's Feather, Exile's Pocket Watch, Exile's Goblet and Exile's Circlet.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Heirloom and travel keepsakes: a faded flower, a sapphire-blue feather, a delicate childhood pocket watch, a broken silver family goblet with a carved-out crest, and his father's circlet.

## lore
### LITE
An aristocrat lost his childhood to a family feud, left his house in disgrace and wandered alone, carrying little more than a few mementos.
### STANDARD
The Flower belongs to the time before the first blood was shed in the feud; the exile, having seen human cruelty, left his gentleness in the past but treasured the faded flower and his old innocent smile. The Feather fell from a panicked bird fleeing a falcon onto his shoulder as he walked alone through plains and forests, a symbol of a nestless free bird like himself. The Pocket Watch recorded his carefree youth, ended by tragedy and long lonely exile. The Goblet is a silver family heirloom whose gem with the family crest he carved out after bringing shame to his family; it is broken and can hold no liquid, yet he carries it. The Circlet recalls a childhood in a silver palace, the father's circlet as a symbol of nobility, and a later feud that painted the collapsing palace blood-red; it became the evidence of the exile's crime.
### LARGE +
The set never states what the crime was or who was involved. The wiki links it to the Mondstadt aristocrat Parsifal and his half-brother Eberhart, and ties the sapphire-blue feather to the feather the Heart of Depth set describes pinned to the Chief Mate's mantle. A World Quest series called The Exile is a separate archive entry.
### ULTRA +
Read with Heart of Depth: the exile's blue feather and slim sword in that set continue the same image, a noble who gave up his name and ended up at sea. The wiki's Parsifal page, drawing on weapon and book texts rather than this set, says his chivalric play-acting and Eberhart's urging led him to vigilantism against fellow aristocrats and exile to Liyue. Treat that as wiki-compiled lore.

## owners
### STANDARD
The unnamed exile; the wiki identifies him as Parsifal.

## status
### STANDARD
The exile is a figure of about a thousand years ago and long dead. Nothing in 6.x or 7.0-7.1 changes this set.

## roleplay
### LARGE
Use as quiet melancholy about lost home and shame. A holder of a piece might mention a broken goblet or a blue feather. Do not invent the feud's details or name the exile unless the speaker knows his story.

## gameplay
### LARGE
Gameplay (game mechanics, not in-world fact). A 3- and 4-star set found as drops and in chests rather than a domain. 2-piece: Energy Recharge +20%. 4-piece: using an Elemental Burst regenerates 2 Energy for all other party members every 2s for 6s, three ticks starting after 2s; it does not stack and is not affected by Energy Recharge.
