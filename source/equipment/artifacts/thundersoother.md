## summary
### LITE
Thundersoother is an Electro artifact set from launch (Midsummer Courtyard domain). Its five pieces recount a legendary hero, "the Thundersoother", who conquered thunder and lightning, killed the tyrannical Beast of Thunder, and later lived and died alone.
### STANDARD +
The story is told in legend-like fragments with no names, dates or places.

## lore
### LITE
The Beast of Thunder once enslaved people with its lightning. The Thundersoother, who plucked the purple Flower of Thunder with reverence and wore it on his chest, fought the beast without wavering and defeated it in a lightning storm, beheading it and claiming a diadem as his trophy.
### STANDARD +
He made tools from amethyst and Electro shards: an insignia shaped like the plume of a falcon that fearlessly crossed storms and a burnt woodland, and an hourglass of Electro shards that shatter and merge as time passes. Watching it taught him about the cycle of life and death, which helped him defeat the beast. His goblet is said to have held the wine for the sacrificial slaying of his family, the courage for entering the beast's cave, and his only company in his last years of solitude.
### LARGE +
The Thundersoother is called a mere mortal with human feelings, and the goblet witnessed his sorrow as well as his deeds. The Heart piece says the flower grants courage to travellers in storms; for him, the text suggests, wearing it may have been mere whimsy. The Diadem piece ends: "the past can never be fully pacified". The beast's might vanished as quickly as its lightning.
### ULTRA +
The texts never name the family sacrificed, the nature of the sacrifice (a slaying, apparently ritual), or the cave where the beast lived. The Hourglass piece notes even he could not conquer the flash of time.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not lore): 4 and 5 star set from Midsummer Courtyard and its strongbox. 2-piece: Electro RES +40%. 4-piece: +35% DMG against Electro-affected opponents; it does not trigger on the hit that applies Electro, still works off-field, and stacks additively with other DMG bonuses. Introduced in 1.0.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay interpretation (this project's recommendation, not canon text): tell it as an anonymous legend of a solitary, grieving hero. Do not name him or connect him to a nation or to the Thundering Fury set.

## entry: Thundersoother's Heart
@keys: Thundersoother's Heart, Flower of Thunder
### STANDARD
The flower piece: a purple flower that blooms only in thunderstorms and never withers in the rain. The Thundersoother knelt to pluck it and wore it on his chest; for travellers, it gave unfaltering resolve against thunder.

## entry: Thundersoother's Plume
@keys: Thundersoother's Plume
### STANDARD
The plume piece: an amethyst insignia in the shape of a falcon's feather. The falcon left it in burnt woods after flying through storms and volcano; the Thundersoother, like it, conquered both thunder and flame.

## entry: Hour of Soothing Thunder
@keys: Hour of Soothing Thunder
### STANDARD
The sands piece: an amethyst hourglass filled with Electro shards that shatter and merge as time passes. The hero learned about the cycle of life and death from it.

## entry: Thundersoother's Goblet
@keys: Thundersoother's Goblet
### STANDARD
The goblet piece: his cup, said to have held the wine for the sacrificial slaying of his family, the courage for the beast's cave and the only company of his solitary final days.

## entry: Thundersoother's Diadem
@keys: Thundersoother's Diadem
### STANDARD
The circlet piece: a very ancient diadem that shines with the light of pacified lightning, taken as a trophy from the Beast of Thunder after the storm fight.
