## summary
@spoiler: major
### LITE
Aubade of Morningstar and Moon is a 4- and 5-star artifact set dropped in the Moonchild's Treasures domain in Nod-Krai. Its five pieces (Moonlit Offering's ...) carry item text about an ancient age when three moons guided the heavens, how mortals came to worship them, and how two of them fell, ending an age called Eternity. Mechanically it supports Lunar reactions.
## appearance
### STANDARD
The pieces are ornaments of ritual devotion to the moons: a carved floral ornament (Flower), a prayer quill (Plume), an hourglass for the final hour (Sands), a wine cup from a rite (Goblet) and a silver circlet worn by hymn poets (Circlet). Item descriptions say they were made in ancient times by artisans, priests, sages, believers and poets honouring the heavenly moons.

## lore
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Across the five pieces the legend runs: envoys of the sky, silent at human questions, lose mortals to worship of three moons; a Winged One finishes creation under a false veil and the moons accommodate Order; a primeval dragon had raised the moons and gone into the stars; the long sword at the edge of the world ends Eternity; two moons fall and the New Moon is awaited.
### LARGE +
Piece by piece:
- Opulent Dream: the gods' envoys walked among humans bringing prosperity and wisdom, which woke intellect and then doubt; people chose priests and priests anointed kings. The envoys stayed silent at human questions, so people turned to the three moons, whose watching daughters of dreams and poetry in the Arianrhod Realm answered with madness and inspiration. An archpriestess brought back a small circular talisman, the "Moon Wheel", as the new pact between the moons and the people.
- Parting Light: the Winged One became Lord of the Eternal Heavens and finished creation, a false veil was drawn to hide the true skies, and a place was kept in the sky for the Three Sisters of the Moonlit Night. The Lectors of Thirty Nights judged they could not save the planet alone; the ruler of the firmament spurned a destroyer veiled in black mist, and the moons chose Order, or made space for it. This was the age when the three moons shared governance with the morning star and Eternity was still Eternity.
- Final Hour: a primeval dragon nurtured by the perfumed sea sailed into the mists among the stars to seek an answer to the doom approaching the planet, raising three moons to bind the world with tides while he was gone. After a thousand years of waiting the moons met a winged Descender arriving on the morning star, who did not seem to be the great enemy; the guardians debated, but the Lord of Hosts would crush all resistance.
- Libation: a silver ship came to the harbour of Meropis to gather lost spirits; peoples honoured the moons as gentle mothers against a wrathful father, at Vindagnyr, Lang-Gan and Arcadia. Moonlight was only a strand of grace in the homeland the Lord of the Eternal Heavens chose for humankind, and probing the heavens was forbidden until the long sword at the edge of the world cut the sky and Eternity ended.
- Silver Crown: two moons have fallen. When the Eternal Moon fell, heaven and earth were thrown into chaos; the Iridescent Moon's shattering sank a crimson shadow into the abyssal sea; the Frost Moon ceased to turn and nations faltered. The perfumed sea still ripples with lunar quakes, and people await the New Moon.
Reading across the five texts: the moons are portrayed as sisters, guardians of the planet's will, first set by a departing dragon and later accommodated into the order of the Winged One. They are neither simply defeated nor victorious against Order; the account says they "made space" for it. Mortal devotion to them is shown as an alternative to silent envoys and a distant ruler. The set pairs naturally with the Nod-Krai Moonsign storyline, but the item text itself names no living character.
### ULTRA +
Place names in the Libation piece (Meropis, Vindagnyr, Lang-Gan, Arcadia) tie the legend to other regions, as do the Arianrhod Realm, the Three Realms and the Lectors of Thirty Nights mentioned in the other pieces. The texts do not say how the three moons relate beyond "sisters"; the Eternal, Iridescent and Frost Moon are named only in the Silver Crown, as the fallen. The "winged Descender" arriving on the morning star is linked by the wiki to the Heavenly Principles and the Descender cosmology; the text itself gives no name.

## owners
### STANDARD
No character is named as owner. The pieces are described as relics of ancient moon worship, dropped in a domain whose description speaks of Moonchildren priests hiding the remaining sparks of an ancient northern civilisation.

## gameplay
@availability: permanent
### LITE
Game mechanics: the 2-piece bonus gives +80 Elemental Mastery; the 4-piece bonus boosts Lunar reaction damage while the wearer is off-field.
### STANDARD
Game mechanics (not lore): obtained from Moonchild's Treasures (Nod-Krai), 4- and 5-star. 4-piece: while the wearer is off-field, Lunar reaction DMG is +20%; if the party Moonsign Level is at least Ascendant Gleam, a further +40%. The effect disappears 3s after the wearer is active. Per the wiki it also boosts direct Lunar damage (for example from Ineffa's Overclocking Circuit) and only applies to the equipping character.
