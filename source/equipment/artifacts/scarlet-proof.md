## summary
@spoiler: major
### LITE
Scarlet Proof is a 4- and 5-star artifact set for Stellar Swirl teams, released in version 7.0. Its five pieces are bright red Fatui keepsakes (a flower medal, a quill, a pocket watch, a wine bottle, a helmet), and each carries a short vignette about a Fatui member in the Tsaritsa's Snezhnaya: Sandrone, Pulcinella, Capitano, Dottore and Pantalone.
### STANDARD +
The tone is dry and personal: how members joined, what they quietly carry and how the organisation treats talent as fuel for its "grand ideal".

## appearance
### STANDARD
All five items are described as bright red: a flower ornament symbolising blazing glory, a quill, a pocket watch, a wine bottle and a helmet. The Fatui recruitment poster in the Flower bears a scarlet flower, which is the medal promised to those who devote themselves to the cause.

## lore
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
- Honor to Your Devotion: Miss Guillotin, who had come from Fontaine after Duke Alain Guillotin's death, sits in a Snezhnograd square. In Fontaine she had talked with a branch president who praised a foreign bank; in Snezhnaya she met the Director of the Fatui, who spoke of saving the world by destroying the old. She later became Marionette, or Sandrone, and took on a Harbinger's duties.
- Glory to Your Legacy: a fae named Mirko joined the Fatui, studied under Mayor Ioanna Ivanovna and as Pulcinella watched a young man, Ajax, grow into a Fatui officer who showed him an old recruitment quill.
### LARGE +
- Honor to Your Devotion: she watched the Adventurers' Guild window where Katheryne works, no longer surprised since learning of the Guild's deeper purpose in Snezhnaya. Sometimes she feels herself part of the Fatui's "voracious beast". The text says she had been given thermal and taste systems like a human's by Alain and wanted to test how well they would hold up in cold.
- Glory to Your Legacy: Mirko (Pulcinella) divides groups into the young, the old and the hopeless in between. He chose to apply to the Fatui to ensure a place in the coming world, and reports to Ioanna Ivanovna, Speaker of the Snezhnograd Veche, a human; observers read it as ambition or swallowed pride. He competes with Pantalone for control of the Adventurers' Guild, and eventually watches Ajax (Childe) grow. After a morning meeting, he talks to Capitano about it, and Tartaglia soon shows off a quill from the Fatui's earliest recruitment that Capitano gave him; it is several centuries old.
- Time Gifted Unto You: Pulcinella set up counselling rooms for the Fatui. Capitano, who took off his helmet there, listens to a former student, a soldier with one remaining hand, whose squad was sent to quell a disturbance in a remote region and came back shaken. Locals believed it was still the age of the Belyi Tsar. Dottore later judged that the area was suffering virus-induced mass hysteria linked to cannibalism, treated the soldiers and called it reasonable; Capitano questioned that word. The soldier leaves his medal, quill, bottle, helmet and watch with Capitano before retiring.
### ULTRA +
- Chalice of Your Blood and Sorrow: Dottore, under the persona Zandik, walks into an isolated, backward village in a birch forest to run a drug experiment, bringing animal scraps as a gift, and later notes that psychotropic drugs can control minds in either direction. A curious child asks about the butterfly on his bottle; he tells the child it is "a four-eyed monster", then loses interest. He then returns to the castle of Fatuus as Dottore.
- Testament to Your Faith: Pantalone sits alone in the Fatui's theatre box. He recalls being sold by his mother as a youth, being sold again, and learning how money multiplies from a foster father before reaching powers beyond the nobles. He notes the "newest Harbinger" (Sandrone) interrupts him, that he has ceded her share of the Adventurers' Guild's profits, and that Dottore, newly returned, needs a place for an experiment that Pantalone knows of.
Across the set the Fatui appear as people who joined for their own reasons, tolerated by a pragmatic system, not simply as a monolith. The Sands vignette is the set's own frame: the red items are a retired soldier's leftovers, and Capitano keeps them to pass on.

## owners
### STANDARD
No single owner. The items are linked with the Harbingers named in each vignette; the Sands says a retiring soldier left five red items with Capitano to give to those who come after.

## gameplay
@availability: permanent
### LITE
Game mechanics: 2-piece gives +18% ATK; the 4-piece boosts CRIT Rate and Stellar Swirl reaction damage after triggering Stellar Swirl.
### STANDARD
Game mechanics (not lore): 4- and 5-star set from the Inverted Glacier domain (Everfrozen Earth, Snezhnaya). 4-piece: for 10s after the wearer triggers a Stellar Swirl reaction, their CRIT Rate +16% and their Stellar Swirl reaction DMG +40%.

@keys: counseling room, counselling room, the little rooms

### STANDARD
According to the Time Gifted Unto You text, Pulcinella arranged quiet rooms for Fatui members to talk with a companion they trust. Capitano is described as a kind of walking counselling room because colleagues confide in him. The text says Lohefalter, Dottore and Pantalone each used the rooms, or did not, in small anecdotes. Treat these as item text.
