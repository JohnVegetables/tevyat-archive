## summary
### LITE
Marechaussee Hunter is a 4- and 5-star artifact set from Fontaine, added in Version 4.0. Its pieces are mementos of the old Marechaussee Hunters, chiefly Emanuel Guillotin, who directed the Siege of Poisson, retired scarred and drinking, and raised two children he never felt close to. The bonuses boost Normal and Charged Attacks and CRIT Rate when HP changes.
### STANDARD +
The pieces are Hunter's Brooch (flower), Masterpiece's Overture (plume), Moment of Judgment (sands), Forgotten Vessel (goblet) and Veteran's Visage (circlet). The set shares a name with the Marechaussee Hunters, the older monster-hunting group behind today's Marechaussee Phantom (see its own entry).

## appearance
@version: 4.0
### STANDARD
Each piece is a keepsake: an emblem for feats in Fontaine's defence (the brooch), a small tool for adjusting clockwork torque (the overture), a pocket watch given to Fontaine's enforcers that doubled as a duty badge (the moment of judgment), a wine vessel (the forgotten vessel) and a mask given to a Hunter whose face was wounded in service to the Court (the visage).

## lore
@version: 4.0
@spoiler: major
### LITE
The set tells, through objects, how the Marechaussee Hunters began, how one Hunter broke at Poisson, and how the people around him were changed by it.
### STANDARD
The pieces concern the Hunters' founding under Cassiodor, the Siege of Poisson, Alain Guillotin's clockwork legend, a famous trial by combat, and the retired Hunter (Emanuel Guillotin) who cared for two children he never felt close to.
### LARGE +
- Hunter's Brooch: the emblem was awarded for feats of arms in Fontaine's defence; some recipients hid it or sank it in the water. The name "Marechaussee Hunter" is credited to Cassiodor, who became the "Golden Hunter" and thought of the name as humiliating. The piece notes that heretics and demons are rare but people driven to become them are many, and that today the Marechaussee Phantom investigates crime more than it fights, with many Melusines in its ranks. The brooch's owner directed the Siege of Poisson, quit the force, and was drawn back among people at an old friend's request to make a world free of phantoms for his son and daughter. A peaceful ocean ended it.
- Masterpiece's Overture: a clockwork tool made outmoded by Alain Guillotin's "new model" meka. Alain organized energy research at the now-ruined Institute of Natural Philosophy, then joined and left the Marechaussee Phantom and founded the Research Institute of Kinetic Energy Engineering. He was close to nobody but his younger sister, a fellow Marechaussee. Two legends are told: that he built a thinking machine to help her, and that his final research vanished from his workshop, leaving only signs that something had been built.
- Moment of Judgment: a pocket watch of Fontaine's enforcers. The piece recalls the Great Magician "Parsifal" demanding a trial by combat and Champion Duelist Marfisa being chosen as prosecution's champion, to public fascination. Journalist Karl Ingold, who knew Parsifal, later photographed old friends of the Narzissenkreuz Institute at the Vice Director's invitation and regretted not recording a girl's last battle.
- Forgotten Vessel: the wine vessel of a servant of the Court of Fontaine who learned, near the end, that the rabbit hole led to a maelstrom. He remembers childhood games with Dwight, Basil and Karl (where he played the hero and Karl the evil dragon "Jabberwock"), the Director's embrace, the Poisson fires, and sending innocent children to the Narzissenkreuz Institute. He admits he never felt close to his "son" and "daughter," Alain and Mary-Ann, though he treasured watching them grow.
- Veteran's Visage: a mask for a soldier scarred in the Court's service. A friend who grew up with him says goodbye before an Institute battle, asking him to look after "those two kids," and calls him Guillotin, "my dear Emanuel". He dislikes children and company because seeing others only reminds him they have red blood too, but he takes the task on.
Wider canon (wiki) connects the pieces to Emanuel Guillotin: he was a Narzissenkreuz Institute orphan, later a Marechaussee Hunter who directed the Siege of Poisson, which may have caused the deaths of Rene de Petrichor's and Jakob Ingold's parents. After retiring he became Alain and Mary-Ann's guardian when Basil Elton asked, investigated the Narzissenkreuz Ordo for Dwight Lasker, and was the first person dissolved by the Master, with Marfisa the second.
### ULTRA +
The set deliberately gives separate pictures of the same man: the glory-seeking Hunter's emblem, the broken drinker's vessel, and the masked guardian. His real reasons for fighting, and the extent of his remorse for Poisson, are shown only through these private memories. The Hunters' original purpose, according to wider canon, was to fight the Golden Troupe's magic and golems (see the Golden Troupe set), and the group later lived on as family traditions rather than an organised force.

## owners
@version: 4.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Emanuel Guillotin is the set's main figure. The pieces also touch Cassiodor (the brooch's naming), Alain Guillotin (the overture), Karl Ingold and Marfisa (the watch) and Basil Elton, the unnamed friend of the mask.

## gameplay
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not lore): the 2-piece bonus is Normal and Charged Attack DMG +15%. The 4-piece bonus gives CRIT Rate +12% for 5s whenever current HP rises or falls, up to 3 stacks. Obtained from the Denouement of Sin domain in Fontaine and the artifact strongbox.
### LARGE +
The domain lies in Fontaine's Belleau Region; its Ley Line Disorder rewards HP changes with CRIT DMG, and its enemies are out-of-control clockwork meka.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay interpretation (this project's recommendation, not canon text): the pieces suit gentle, melancholic scenes about duty, regret and chosen family. A character who holds one should treat it as a keepsake, not as a weapon or token of rank.
