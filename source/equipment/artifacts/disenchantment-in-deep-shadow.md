## summary
@spoiler: major
### LITE
Disenchantment in Deep Shadow is a 4- and 5-star artifact set for Superconduct and Stellar-Conduct teams, released in version 6.6. Its five pieces are all about Aksinya, the silver-haired human scholar of the old Snezhnayan court: her friendship with a frost-wielding fae princess, her work among the ruins of the Shadow Realm, a long-haired alchemist who joined her research, and the Tsar who found her as an infant.
### STANDARD +
Every title begins "...That Ceased...", pointing at the dream of the Belyi Tsar's project that stopped when he fell. The items are keepsakes of Aksinya's life, given to her by friends, colleagues and her master.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Keepsakes: a silk flower, a feathered quill, a fleeting moment given as a reminder, a wine goblet, and a delicate piercing trinket.

## lore
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
All five stories centre on Aksinya, who is not named in some pieces but is addressed by name in the Flower and the Goblet. The Flower is her friendship with the Snegurochka princess in a fae manor; the Quill her teaching of a young Domovoy; the Sands her first meeting with a long-haired alchemist; the Goblet a toast from the old Domovoy lord Alvis; the Pendulum the Tsar's trinket, given in celebration of her birth.
### LARGE +
- Iridescence That Ceased Amidst Glory: Aksinya, then a court attendant following the Emperor of the North, slipped away from a banquet at the manor of the foremost Snegovik (the House of Hesperides per wiki) and got lost. The mistress of frost, a Snegurochka princess, found her, shared paintings, sculptures and puzzles and showed her a courtyard of ice that never melts. She warned that even that ice was magic and fleeting, engraved in the blood of former servants. She pinned a silk flower on Aksinya as proof of friendship, saying it is never the craft that is artful but the heart.
- Sharpness That Ceased Upon Wondrous Creation: in the Shadow Realm Aksinya and her apprentice, a young Domovoy, walked among the ruins of a golden civilisation struck down by the heavens' master. She sketched a grand design while he begged her to turn back. Aksinya answered that they sought to see by the fire's light, not to inherit it, and that their bones could be the next step for others to climb. She handed him her pen.
- Moment That Ceased Upon Waking From Grand Dreams: before the Tsaritsa took the throne, a young black-haired alchemist who had defied taboos, even on the fae, was sent by the Snow-King to labour where taboos are touched. At their first meeting he secretly made an ear grow in her palm out of spite. She treated it as an interesting trick, asked him to request permission next time and gave him his schedule. The encounter made him sense something above the material, and wounded and drew him at once.
- Ovations That Ceased Upon Festivity: a company gathered by the guilt-ridden Snow-King sought the stairway to a vacant seat in the court of the stars. After years of descending into ancient ruins, the Domovoy lord Alvis, who held Kitezhgrad's mines, toasted her, a human, for outpacing everything earlier generations left. Aksinya saw that he now cared for her like an elder.
### ULTRA +
- Pendulum That Ceased Amidst a Great Fall: the towering man on the throne had given her a trinket in celebration of her birth; in the scene she is grown and has a fresh ear piercing. He remembered walking the shadows, finding the broken golden homeland, "the mother he had never met", and the Hyperboreans' secrets about himself, which gave him pain, not freedom. In one ruined structure of life-sustaining devices he had found an unconscious silver-haired infant. When Aksinya asked what she could give in return, he told her of the vision he had held, perhaps for far too long.
Read together with Heart of the Furnace (Aksinya's apprentice) the picture is of a human woman who earned a Domovoy lord's respect, the Snegovik's friendship, and the Tsar's trust, and whose project fell with him.
