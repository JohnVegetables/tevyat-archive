## summary
### LITE
Retracing Bolide is an Inazuma artifact set from launch (Domain of Guyun). Its five "Summer Night's" pieces record, in a man's voice, meetings with a fox-eyed woman at summer festivals near Mt. Yougou: lost as a child, nursing a sprained ankle as a teenager, and as an adult hoping to see her again. The wiki links her to Kitsune Saiguu.
### STANDARD +
The set is framed as an Inazuma ghost story about meetings between the human and the inhuman. It is a memory of kindness and parting, not a monster tale.

## lore
### LITE
At a summer festival a lost child, separated from his parents by the crowd carrying the divine palanquin, cries by the torii gates on the road to the shrine. A beautiful woman with fox-like eyes takes his hand and offers to see fireworks, throw darts and play fusen. Years later, on a festival night, the same woman sits beside him on a rock by the wayside, unchanged after ten years, and invites him to watch the fireworks with wine.
### STANDARD +
The woman never ages: in the pocket-watch piece, the teenager (hurt, with a broken watch while the girl he admired fetched ointment) meets her after ten years and she has not changed. Later, in the dart piece, he is a man whose wife is pregnant; he climbs the mountain carrying the water balloon from age seven, the fox mask from seventeen and a flower that would not wilt, and thinks he sees her in white on the same rock, but it is only a sunbathing fox that flees, leaving an old throwing dart.
### LARGE +
The flower piece describes an undying summer flower as the last remembrance of a "foxy-eyed woman who eventually disappeared without a trace", set against the idea that life lies in change, meeting and parting. The mask piece describes masks cast in the image of a fox deity: people wear them hoping to gain her power of transformation. It says that in Inazuma's legends everything has a spirit but most spirits have been driven into the forests by the Shogun's suppression; many still believe thousand-year-old animals gain power. A note on the back, written in a lovely hand, says she left under cover of fireworks and that they will likely never meet again.
### ULTRA +
The narrator wonders why he expected to meet her again: no matchmaker introduced him to his wife, money was short and an heir took time, yet his days were full of happiness. The pieces keep it ambiguous whether the fox on the rock was her.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not lore): 4 and 5 star set from the Domain of Guyun and its strongbox. 2-piece: Shield Strength +35% (affects only the wearer). 4-piece: while shielded, +40% Normal and Charged Attack DMG. Introduced in 1.0.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay interpretation (this project's recommendation, not canon text): present it as a wistful Inazuma festival memory. The fox-eyed woman is kind, teasing and unexplained; do not state definitively that the fox on the rock was her.

## entry: Summer Night's Waterballoon
@keys: Summer Night's Waterballoon, Summer Nights Waterballoon
### STANDARD
The goblet piece: a finely made water balloon. The narrator as a child is separated from his parents at the summer festival, cries by the torii gates and is found by a fox-eyed lady who offers fireworks, darts and fusen.

## entry: Summer Night's Moment
@keys: Summer Night's Moment, Summer Nights Moment
### STANDARD
The sands piece: a stopped pocket watch. On a festival night, a young man walking to the shrine with a girl he admired hears a lost child crying, falls and breaks the watch. A masked woman sits by him on a rock and offers to watch the fireworks.
### LARGE +
She says few know the spot, notes he has grown, and has not aged in ten years. They are to watch the fireworks with wine.

## entry: Summer Night's Finale
@keys: Summer Night's Finale, Summer Nights Finale
### STANDARD
The plume piece: a wooden throwing dart. The man, whose wife is expecting, climbs Mt. Yougou with the water balloon, mask and flower; he sees what looks like the woman in white but finds only a fox that flees, leaving a dart.

## entry: Summer Night's Bloom
@keys: Summer Night's Bloom, Summer Nights Bloom
### STANDARD
The flower piece: an artificial summer festival flower that never wilts. It is the final thing left to remember the foxy-eyed woman who disappeared without a trace.

## entry: Summer Night's Mask
@keys: Summer Night's Mask, Summer Nights Mask
### STANDARD
The circlet piece: a mask in the image of a legendary fox deity. People wear such masks hoping to gain her transformation ability. A note on the back apologises for leaving under the fireworks and wishes the finder well.
