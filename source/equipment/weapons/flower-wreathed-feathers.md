## summary
### LITE
Flower-Wreathed Feathers is a 4-star longbow from Natlan that burns like a flame, adorned with feathers said to come from the ancient tyrant Ngoubou. It belongs to the Heroes of Natlan series.
### STANDARD +
Its story is the legend of the Flower-Feather Clan hero Menilek, who refused to kill his dying Saurian companion despite an old sage's warning.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Canon describes a longbow that burns like a flame, adorned with feathers. Keep to that; do not invent a colour scheme or carvings.

## lore
### LITE
A dying old sage tells the hero Menilek, "bearing the name Transcendence", that a calamity older than his name flows in the veins of the injured Saurian whelp in his arms, and that if he pities mortals he should sever its neck and give it freedom. Menilek ignores the oracle and tends the whelp.
### STANDARD +
Menilek declares that the descendants of the Flower-Feather Clan bow to neither tyrant nor curse, and that he will break the chain just as his glorious ancestors did. With his loyal companion Ngoubou he sets out on a pilgrimage. The Clan still sings of it: the hero tearing mountains apart with thunderous bowstrings, the whelp piercing a wicked python's throat, a forty-day fight with a huge monster won with a single pike, and a contest with Tenoch (before his exile from his tribe) that led to a lasting friendship.
### LARGE +
The legends are said to have been sung by poets for five hundred years across Natlan. The tone then darkens into a reflection on hope: well-intentioned hope can be the mould of many misfortunes, and Menilek kept comforting himself with "tomorrow". In the final stanza he asks Ngoubou to rest while he finishes his task, promising to wake it after the dark tide subsides and to see the new flowers on the high cliffs.
### ULTRA +
The text's last couplet, that all hope turned into betrayal and that tomorrow would never come again, signals tragedy but does not say how. Read alongside the Clan's stories, Menilek stands beside Tenoch against the tide of darkness many years after their duel. The wiki identifies the dying whelp of the opening as the Saurian Ngoubou; the weapon text does not detail its nature.

## owners
### LITE
The feathers are tied to Ngoubou; the bow itself is tied to Menilek by the legend.
### LARGE +
The text never says the bow was Menilek's explicitly, only that its feathers come from Ngoubou and that his tale is attached. Leave exact ownership vague in scenes.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not in-world): a 4-star bow of the Heroes of Natlan series, base ATK 42 at level 1, secondary stat ATK%. Weapon Event Wishes from Version 5.2.
### LARGE +
Passive "Inflorescence Unattainable": decreases Gliding Stamina consumption by 15%, and during Aimed Shots Charged Attack DMG increases every 0.5s, stacking up to 6 times; the effect is removed 10s after leaving Aiming Mode.

## roleplay
### LARGE
Flower-Feather Clan characters know this as a clan legend of a hero and a Saurian companion. Keep the tone reverent and bittersweet; do not give the story a stated ending.

Inflorescence Unattainable is the passive of the bow Flower-Wreathed Feathers (gameplay): cheaper gliding and, while aiming, stacking Charged Attack damage. Its name echoes the story's flowers on the high cliffs that Menilek never reaches.
