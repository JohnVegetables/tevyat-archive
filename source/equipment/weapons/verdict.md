## summary
### LITE
Verdict is a 5-star claymore of Fontaine, released in Version 4.3 alongside Navia. Its item text tells of Leticia, the eldest daughter of the noble House Randolph, who slipped into the underground Fleuve Cendre in disguise, fell in love with a young man there, and eventually abandoned her family name to live among its people. The weapon is described as stained with the blood of enemies and loved ones alike.
### STANDARD +
Its passive is named Many Oaths of Dawn and Dusk, echoing the oaths in the story. The weapon's text is one of several Fontaine item stories about the Fleuve Cendre and the events leading to the Siege of Poisson.

## appearance
### LARGE
Canon text does not describe the blade beyond calling it a claymore; the story mentions an axe in Leticia's hand years later, though the text does not say the axe and the weapon are the same. Unusually, the weapon's inventory icon shows the handle at the bottom left rather than the top right, unlike most claymores.

## lore
### LITE
A young noblewoman, Leticia, is told by her father that as the eldest daughter of House Randolph she must keep a composed, pure bearing, because when nobles fall they drag many common homes down with them. She still disguises herself and goes down to the Fleuve Cendre, the sunless subterranean city. A man there sees through the disguise and invites her to see how its people live.
### STANDARD +
Her guide is a young man she already knows by name; he explains that her father, brother and servants need not learn of this place, quoting the Boss's saying that the sun's light belongs to others and the Fleuve Cendre to them. He later tells her he adores her noble soul and that if he is ever lost, her hand will decide his fate. Years pass; when she meets her father again she has taken on the name of the rose and is used to the weight of an axe rather than fine dresses. She tells him she has made a pact with her beloved and an oath to those she loves, and that she will not let the Randolph name be stained on her account. He answers that he lights a candle for her every day, asks her to come home, says her child is innocent and he will not abandon it, and hints that Randolph influence could be used for her husband too.
### LARGE +
The text closes in a dream: she remembers that naval cannons had not shaken them, yet no outsiders should have known the secret paths the hunters used to infiltrate. This links the story to the Marechaussee Hunters' assault on the Fleuve Cendre and Poisson. The wiki deduces, from this text together with the Fleuve Cendre Ferryman text, that Leticia and her husband are the Rosa and Thompson Reed of Eduardo Baker's faction at the Siege of Poisson. Verdict does not state this outright (see uncertainties).
### ULTRA +
Reading notes: the text's timeline begins several years before the Siege of Poisson, which the wiki dates to over 500 years before the present, before the cataclysm. Her father's closing offer (return home, with her child sheltered) is made in the early days of the siege per the wiki's reading; the item text itself gives no dates. The Fleuve Cendre Ferryman text, by contrast, names Baker's companions in the faction, which is why the Reed identification rests on combining the two items. Keep the Boss's saying and the lovers' oaths as the emotional core; avoid stating how the story ends.

## owners
### LITE
The text names Leticia as the weapon's former user ("a young maiden who forsook her family name"). No present-day character is established as owning the original.
### LARGE +
Leticia appears by name only in her father's words; she herself later goes by the rose. Do not assume Navia, whose organisation, the Spina di Rosula, uses rose imagery, carries or inherited it.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not in-world): a 5-star claymore released 2023-12-20 (Version 4.3) in the weapon event Wish, later also in Chronicled Wishes. Base ATK 48 with CRIT Rate as its secondary stat.
### LARGE +
Passive Many Oaths of Dawn and Dusk raises ATK by 20% to 40% by refinement. When party members gain Elemental Shards from Crystallize, or trigger Lunar-Crystallize, the wielder gains a Seal that boosts Elemental Skill DMG (18% to 36% per Seal at refinements 1 to 5), lasting 15 seconds, with up to 2 Seals; Seals vanish shortly after the wielder's Elemental Skill deals damage, and Lunar-Crystallize grants at most one Seal per second. The Lunar-Crystallize clause belongs to the Nod-Krai era of the game and is not part of the weapon's original 4.3 text as far as verified. Ascension uses Ancient Chord materials, Rift Core line boss materials and Meshing Gear common materials.

## roleplay
### LARGE
For scenes: the weapon works as a Fontainian relic of an old, tragic love and defiance of class. Treat links to Navia, the Spina di Rosula or the Reeds as possibilities the user or scenario may introduce, not as canon fact.
