## summary
### LITE
The Prized Isshin Blade is a four-star sword that exists only inside Kaedehara Kazuha's Acer Palmatum Chapter, Act I ("A Strange and Friendless Road", Version 2.8). It is a blade full of Tatarigami malice that possesses a samurai and goes hunting for Kazuha, wanting to prove that the Isshin Art can beat even the Shogun. Kazuha defeats it, the curse drains out of it, and at the end of the quest it is remade into the ordinary, obtainable Kagotsurube Isshin.
### STANDARD +
It appears in two quest forms: an Awakened form while it is still dangerous, and a Shattered form once the curse has been worn down. It cannot be kept as a weapon; the lasting result of the quest is the Kagotsurube Isshin.

## appearance
### STANDARD
The Awakened description speaks of an ominous purple aura clinging to a bizarre blade, with the power to control its wielder's heart and mind. After the curse weakens, the text says the aura's wickedness and hostility have decreased greatly. The Shattered form is described as a battered cursed blade showing its true form, its innate endowment ground to dust by its journey.

## lore
### STANDARD
In the quest the Traveler and Paimon are attacked on Byakko Plain by a samurai who is really looking for Kazuha. Investigation leads through Ritou, the Tenryou Commission and the Amenoma Smithy to a small island off Inazuma City, where they find Amenoma Yuuya, a samurai of the Amenoma Clan, possessed by the sword. The sword speaks for itself. It says it was the prized weapon of an Isshin smith who failed the Shogun's tasks and exiled himself to Snezhnaya, and that after the smith died it began possessing people to make its way back to Inazuma and show that it was the ultimate weapon the Shogun had carelessly lost.
### LARGE +
Its aim now is Kazuha. News that he survived the Shogun's Musou no Hitotachi has spread, and the sword wants to possess him to show that the Isshin Art is the strongest. Kazuha beats it and tells it that it cannot prevail against the Shogun, because its values run against those of the Isshin Art and it has neglected itself over the years. When it refuses to accept this and threatens Yuuya, Kazuha proposes a wager: the sword may possess him and fight; if its strength proves real it keeps its claim, and if not it must let Yuuya go. The sword agrees, and Yuuya follows them in silence.
### ULTRA +
In the former hideout of the Takatsukasa Clan, Kazuha cuts through the ronin there while the sword, now in his hand, tries to imitate him rather than control him. He notes that the blade's lack of maintenance makes it hard to use. After a Kairagi falls, the last of the Tatarigami drains out, Paimon calls it a piece of junk, and the sword realises its mistakes and apologises. It releases Yuuya, who then asks for a formal duel with Kazuha and loses. Back at the Amenoma Smithy the sword's story is told to Amenoma Tougo, who expresses regret over the sword's life. The sword wants to control Kazuha once more but chooses to give up its own consciousness and be reborn, producing the Kagotsurube Isshin, its original name, to serve as reference material for the Isshin Art. Kazuha then tells Kujou Kamaji he means to study the Isshin Art and revive it as a bladesmith, though he declines an offer to stay in Inazuma. The Tenryou Commission decides not to charge Yuuya.

## owners
### LARGE
Original owner: an unnamed Isshin smith who fled Inazuma for Snezhnaya (no name is given in the quest synopsis). In the quest it passes through its possessed victims, notably Amenoma Yuuya, and the quest also mentions a collector, Nagato, who was injured and later found alive. Kazuha wields it for the wager fight. The Kagotsurube Isshin that results is a normal player weapon.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not lore): a four-star sword, base ATK 42, secondary stat ATK. Passive "Wandering Striker": Normal, Charged or Plunging Attacks that hit an opponent release an Accursed Spirit for AoE Physical DMG equal to 180% of ATK, once every 8s, and the wielder's DMG is reduced by 50%. The Awakened form also restores HP equal to 100% of ATK; the Shattered form does not. In the Awakened form the secondary stat is about 41.3% at Level 90 on entering the Occupied Hold and drops to about 20.7% after the second fight, making it the first weapon with different secondary-stat values at the same level.

## roleplay
### LARGE
Roleplay use (this project's suggestion, not canon text): the sword is a warning about a craft pursued only for lethality, a blade that mistook obsession for strength. A character who has met it will remember it as Kazuha's cursed opponent that chose to be reborn, not as a weapon they could own.

## entry: Kagotsurube Isshin
@keys: Kagotsurube Isshin
@keys2: Isshin, sword, blade
@version: 2.8
### STANDARD
The Kagotsurube Isshin is the four-star sword the cursed blade becomes at the end of "A Strange and Friendless Road". Its own item text credits the swordsmith Akame Kanenaga of the Akame School, which pursued the killer-blade ideal of the Isshin Art, and says it was forged in a snow-covered northern land rather than in Inazuma. The quest presents the rebirth as the sword giving up its consciousness so that it can serve as reference material for the Isshin Art.

## entry: Isshin Art and the Tatarigami
@keys: Isshin Art, Tatarigami
@keys2: Prized Isshin Blade, cursed blade
@version: 2.8
### LARGE
The Kagotsurube Isshin text explains the ideal this blade distorted: "Isshin" is a stillness of mind free of distraction that lets a smith forge tirelessly, but the Akame School pursued lethality to the point that its blades often carried some evil and the school was branded undesirable by the authorities. The Tatarigami, in the text's words, is malice by nature and resents mortal desire. The Prized Isshin Blade is the quest's example of that malice taking over a weapon.
