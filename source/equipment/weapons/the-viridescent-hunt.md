## summary
### LITE
The Viridescent Hunt is a 4-star bow from the Battle Pass Gnostic Hymn, in the game since Version 1.0. The in-game short description calls it a pure green hunting bow that once belonged to a hunter whose home was the forest. The full text is the tale of a nameless hunter taught never to loose an arrow for anything but survival.
### STANDARD +
According to the wiki, the lore of the Viridescent Venerer artifact set ties into this weapon.

## appearance
### STANDARD
A bow of a pure green so close to the natural environment that, per the text, it could blend in. No other design details are given.

## lore
### LITE
The hunter was raised, far from the city, to live by the laws of nature, never bearing ill will and loosing arrows only to survive. She left no traces and comforted wounded beasts until their lives returned to nature.
### STANDARD +
Her teaching said that those born of the greenery move unimpeded where there is grass and shade, that followers of nature need not fear death, and that at the last ebb of life they head to an endless plain.
### LARGE +
The text turns on what might have been had the calamity not happened: had she not tracked tainted blood on the leaves, and had she not met a dying, blind youth under the tree where she often napped, she might never have been shaken by thoughts of revenge, blood and fire. A voice reminds her that she belongs to the green and must never loose an arrow for war, revenge or glory, since the bloodstained will never find the hunting fields of endless viridescence.
### ULTRA +
The hunter's last words ask that the bow at least not be tainted by her mortal vengeance, say she cannot reach the other side to meet her master or the parents she never knew, and ask that the bow stay pure and carry her thoughts and regrets to them. The wiki links the master to Viaghara and the calamity to the Cataclysm; the text itself only says calamity.

## owners
### STANDARD
The previous owner is the nameless hunter of the text, a woman raised in the forest far from the city; the wiki links the forest to the Dharma Forest and the city to Sumeru City.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not literal lore): 4-star bow, base ATK 42, CRIT Rate secondary stat. Passive Verdant Wind: Normal and Aimed Shot hits have a 50% chance to create a Cyclone that draws in nearby opponents, dealing 40 to 80 per cent of ATK (by refinement) every 0.5s for 4s, with a cooldown of 14 to 10 seconds. Obtained through the Battle Pass Gnostic Hymn bundle.
