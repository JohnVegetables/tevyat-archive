## summary
### LITE
Tidal Shadow is a four-star claymore of the Beaumont series, forged in Fontaine. The description calls it an exquisitely crafted standard-model sword for the high-ranking officers and flagship captains of Fontaine's old navy. Its story recalls the white ironclad flagship Sponsian and Admiral Basil Elton, whose blue-and-white blade is all that remained of the fleet's legend.
### STANDARD +
The Navy was disbanded after the armada ceased to exist. The Admiral's First Mate, Nathaniel Pickman, spent his later years trapped by a false heroic reputation.

## appearance
### STANDARD
A blue and white blade (the text uses the word epee) of the old Fontaine navy's standard officer pattern. For wielders with Arkhe it shows Pneuma or Ousia alignment indicators.

## lore
### STANDARD
In the glorious past the pure white ironclad flagship Sponsian was the pride of Fontaine's Navy, and the Court of Fontaine's darlings were its metal warships with heavy cannons and ramming prows. Basil Elton could be seen on its bridge before her retirement. Afterwards Pickman petitioned the Court for years to salvage and rebuild the Sponsian, but he failed, and the families of the fallen suspected he was fishing for glory.
### LARGE +
A short exchange recalls the ship's naming: Elton calls Sponsian an awful name, Pickman half-jokes it was a usurper's, and Elton relents, saying she likes that sense of unreal reality. Legends of the Sponsian and the White Armada were staged at the newly rebuilt opera house on Erinnyes until an accident ended such shows, the best remembered being the armada's final battle with ten battleship cannons onstage, reconstructed from Pickman's records.
### ULTRA +
The text speculates that Pickman omitted an absurd detail, or that writers cut it as undermining the epic tragedy: Elton boarded the flagship once more after years away and told Pickman and the sailors that this time they could finally fire on their enemies. Only then does she smile and call out, as in the opera, to the sailors, promising tea later and telling them to first send the 'oversized punk' Elynas back to the bottom of the waterfall.

## owners
### STANDARD
Admiral Basil Elton, whose sword the story says it was.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Game mechanics, not story. Passive White Cruising Wave raises ATK for 8 seconds after the wielder is healed, and can trigger when the character is off-field. Base ATK 42, ATK 9% secondary stat.
