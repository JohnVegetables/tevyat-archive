## summary
### LITE
Cashflow Supervision is a 5-star catalyst: a floating device that can monitor certain phenomena. After being abandoned, it was picked up and modified for other purposes.
### STANDARD +
Its weapon story is about money. A certain person, linked to Pantalone, wants to prove that currency belongs to humanity and not to a god, and picks the Fortress of Meropide as the place for a test.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Canon describes a floating device with a large crystal in its upper part; the crystal glows with Pneuma or Ousia when an Arkhe-aligned character holds it. The text also calls it an overseer made of precision machinery that rumbles as it watches money move. Add nothing else.

## lore
### LITE
The text argues that the flow of money traces the arteries of the world and that humanity should master currency, not be enslaved by it. A certain person (the wiki links Pantalone) cannot accept that the corner of the world in which money flows was chosen and defined by a god, which the text points at as Liyue and Rex Lapis.
### STANDARD +
Since the heart of gold has been taken by a god from a distant land, that person decides the system must be overhauled: control of money should be given equally to all, even if mastering the heart of gold is impossible. He proposes to demonstrate it on a small isolated economy, a nation within a nation with minimal divine interference, which the wiki links to the Fortress of Meropide, and to introduce a new currency in place of Mora.
### LARGE +
In quoted speech, he says he dislikes naming things and calls the new currency the Credit Coupon, in honour of the credit that had been given him to run the experiment. The overseer machine then tirelessly records every amount of money, savings and spending, changes in value, and the number of times each piece circulates in a period. The only law in the experiment is the hoarder's private law, and the only sanctions are poverty or death: either become a rentier with power and control, or work to death. The text calls these rules always fair.
### ULTRA +
The closing lines state the intended consequence: humanity would meet gods on equal ground through ambition and wealth, the weak who fail to prevail swallowed by the flood of the mortal world, with divine power no longer used to embellish the rich before the poor, and divine wealth not coming down to save the poor from under the rich man's feet. The text sets this out as the logic of the plan; it does not say the experiment succeeded or what happened to the people inside.

## owners
### LITE
No owner is named. The device was abandoned, then picked up and modified.
### LARGE +
Wriothesley is the character it is usually paired with at release (see uncertainties). Do not narrate him owning it unless the user's scenario does.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not in-world): a 5-star catalyst, base ATK 48 at level 1, secondary stat CRIT Rate; Weapon Event Wishes; introduced in Version 4.1. Passive "Golden Blood-Tide": ATK is raised; when HP increases or decreases, Normal Attack DMG, Charged Attack DMG and Stellar-Conduct DMG rise for 4s (up to 3 stacks, once per 0.3s); at 3 stacks ATK SPD also rises. Values scale with refinement.

## roleplay
### LARGE
For scenes: a Meropide inmate or a Fatui-aware character would link the Credit Coupon to a closed economy. Do not make the Fatui plan sound successful or resolved.

## entry: Credit Coupon experiment
@keys: Credit Coupon, Credit Coupons, Cashflow Supervision
@keys2: Meropide, Pantalone, Mora
### STANDARD
In the weapon story of Cashflow Supervision, a certain person (linked to Pantalone) plans to test a new currency, the Credit Coupon, inside a closed economy, the Fortress of Meropide, to prove money should not be controlled by a god. A machine overseer records every transaction. The text does not state the result.
