## summary
### LITE
Lightbearing Moonshard is a five-star sword added in Version 6.3 (Luna IV). Its description calls it a legendary blade forged from glimmering moonlight gathered in a well by an adeptus hidden among clear springs, its true form revealed only where dusk meets dawn. The item story is a Liyue legend about a girl of the mining tribes who seeks that adeptus and the sword that could save her people's ancient kingdom.

## appearance
### STANDARD
The text gives no physical description of the blade beyond "glimmering moonlight" and a form that only shows at dusk and dawn. The sword is associated with moonlit water, a silvery shadow in a spring and half-formed phantoms behind bamboo curtains. Do not invent a hilt or blade design.

## lore
### STANDARD
At dusk a girl in climbing sandals reaches the hidden spring of a forgotten tomb that appears in no chronicle. She has followed a tale told only among the mining tribes, of an adeptus who forged swords from flowing light and then vanished into the watery moon beneath the depths. In the spring a silver shadow answers her with soft laughter.

The legend she carries is the history of Lang-Gan, a western-coast kingdom older than Mt. Tianheng, where people honoured a heavenly being who taught them to farm and weave. A blazing comet then fell from the east and the heavens, sea and earth were ruined, so that the people perished. In her own day, a mighty dragon who once stood with the Lord of Geo has been struck by sudden ruin and tramples human lands; the mountain folk take up their ancestors' weapons and follow the Yaksha under the Lord of Geo's banner.
### LARGE +
The shadowy figure is wry and unhurried about prophecy: fortune and woe are intertwined, and eternal prosperity is a reflection on water. She pulls a bamboo stalk from a cliff, the tip breaks and falls, and the girl catches it before it hits the ground. The figure then says that, as the waxing moon bears its light, one may chase forms and shadows, and that the girl has understood the sword's true essence, so why seek its physical form. The lesson is that the sword is not an object to be fetched.

Afterwards, the story says, the girl trained a hundred elite warriors in sword-and-halberd combat. With the Lord of Geo they subdued the dragon. After peace returned those warriors became merchants haggling in Shenglu Hall, and they speak of such tales now only when selling porcelain fired from Sand Crystals. The text also foresees the mountain fortresses crumbling into jade-coloured ruins that become Glazed Sand Crystals in merchants' hands.
### ULTRA +
The item text ties the dragon to Azhdaha only implicitly (the wiki links "the mighty dragon" to him and the "Heavenly being" to Zibai), and the Lord of Geo to Zhongli; the item itself uses titles, not these names. It ends on the harbour city beneath a cold moon that still lights every river as it did thousands of years ago.

## owners
### STANDARD
No owner is named. The legend says the girl sought the sword, but the text never says she obtained or wielded it, only that she understood its essence.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Game mechanics, not lore. The passive Legacy of Lang-Gan raises DEF and, for a short time after the wielder uses an Elemental Skill, increases damage from Lunar-Crystallize reactions. Base ATK 44; secondary stat CRIT DMG. Obtained from Weapon Event Wishes; featured in Version 6.3's second half.
