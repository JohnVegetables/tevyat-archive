## summary
### LITE
Clash of Kings is a 4-star catalyst: a golden box lavishly inlaid with precious jewels that houses a game much beloved by the kings of the sands. Its story is a legend of three gods who, bored in a formless world, create a new one and play a board game over it.
### STANDARD +
It is a Battle Pass (Gnostic Hymn) reward from Version 7.0 and belongs to the Gnostic Hymn 3 series. The story is comic in tone: the gods bicker over rules, and nobody wins.

## appearance
### STANDARD
A golden box set with precious jewels, holding the game. The description gives no further detail.

## lore
@version: 7.0
### LITE
The story is told as a legend. Before anything existed there were only three gods in a world without beginning or end: Valor, with unlimited power; Cunning, with unlimited wisdom and guile; and Nature. Valor and Cunning fought endlessly without result, so Nature proposed making a new world and deciding the winner with dice, since even gods cannot change the dice.
### STANDARD +
Valor flattened the land into a square chessboard and shaped mountains and rivers after the old world. Cunning carved the pieces: angels, humans, Jinn and monsters, and the dragons and magical beasts that Nature's will drove. Countless days and nights passed over the little world, and only the gods' dice could decide its fate.
### LARGE +
The tale ends with a game in progress. A mighty Hero is defeated by the Dark Lord on bad rolls. Valor's side tries to revive him through his divine armor, but Cunning, addressed as Ibis, invokes a rule from the Tome of Universal Laws, page 83, so that the Hero falls under the spirit of the previous Dark Lord's magical sword. Cunning wants the Hero as a piece on his own side, Nature objects that a Hero held by the previous Dark Lord belongs to the monsters under Nature's command, and the ancient demons take the world. No one wins, and the gods agree to create a new batch of adventurers and start again.
### ULTRA +
The comedy rests on rules-lawyering: each god accuses the others of unfair or unread rules, and Cunning's schemes are called backstabbing by Valor. The text presents the whole of creation as a tabletop pastime for gods who have endless time; this is a framing of the legend and is not stated as history.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not in-world): 4-star catalyst, level 1 base ATK 42, secondary stat CRIT Rate 6%. Passive Without Heed for Day nor Night: using an Elemental Skill grants Laws of the Board for 6 s, raising ATK and Elemental Mastery; it can trigger once every 12 s and does not stack. A Charged Attack hit during it extends the duration by up to 6 s.
### LARGE +
At refinement 1 the bonus is 20% ATK and 100 Elemental Mastery; at refinement 5 it is 40% ATK and 200 Elemental Mastery. Obtained as a Battle Pass (BP Bounty, Gnostic Hymn) reward.

## roleplay
### LARGE
Treat the story as a legend with a wry humour, told as hearsay. Do not use it to state who the gods Valor or Nature are in Teyvat's history.
