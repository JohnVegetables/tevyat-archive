## summary
### LITE
Royal Bow is a 4-star bow of the Royal series, an old royal longbow of the erstwhile rulers of Mondstadt. Countless generations later its string is still tight and fires with great force. Its text is about the fall of the ruling Lawrence Clan and the Knights of Favonius exiling them.
### STANDARD +
It is the Royal series' bow, made of premium materials with master craftsmanship, which the text gives as the reason it is still as powerful as ever.

## appearance
### STANDARD
A longbow of fine, long-lasting materials whose string is still taut after generations.

## lore
### LITE
Hunting was once a pastime of the nobles. They showed their strength in the natural world and shared the spoils with the people in benevolence. Benevolence was lost, and with it their reign.
### STANDARD +
After the uprising, the Lawrence Clan that had long ruled Mondstadt was overthrown. The newly established Knights of Favonius judged long investigations unnecessary in the name of benevolent governance, and the clan was promptly cast out, never to return.
### LARGE +
A first-person passage by a clan member recalls leaving: a father sighing at the betrayal of the people and the end of a glorious era, former subjects rejoicing in the open country, and the speaker's later realization that it was the Lawrence Clan who had betrayed the people and closed the history books.
### ULTRA +
The text names Vennessa as the last push that toppled a crumbling tower, and lists earlier flames of resistance: the phantom thief praised in secret by the people, the woman who could see death, and the Wanderer's Troupe intent on revolution. It closes that the spirit of resistance is in Mondstadters' blood. The wiki links these to Parsifal, the Blue-Eyed Spear Witch and the Wanderer's Troupe.

## owners
### LITE
No owner is named; the text says only a noble who ruled Mondstadt.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not in-world): 4-star bow, base ATK 42, secondary stat ATK 9%, sold in Paimon's Bargains. Passive Focus: dealing damage increases CRIT Rate by 8% to 16% by refinement per stack, up to 5 stacks, and a CRIT hit removes all stacks.
