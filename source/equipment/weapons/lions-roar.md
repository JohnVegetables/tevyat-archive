## summary
### LITE
Lion's Roar is a 4-star sword of the Kunwu series, a sharp blade with extravagant carvings that does not compromise on durability and sharpness and that roars like a lion as it cuts the air. It is rumoured to be a legendary sword of Liyue. It has been in the game since Version 1.0.
### STANDARD +
Its text tells how the craftsman Kunwu forged it in one day and then carved a sheath for it over a year, and how the sheath was lost.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Extravagant carvings. The lost sheath was said to be eaglewood decorated with mica and engraved with war imagery. Do not invent the blade's colour.

## lore
### LITE
The blade is so sharp that its victims realise they have been struck only an hour later.
### STANDARD +
Kunwu is said to have forged it in a day, working through the day and night. When his aged master saw the work he clenched his fists, sighed and struck the ground with his staff, calling it a pitiful thing, merciless in all the world.
### LARGE +
Kunwu pondered his master's words for three days without touching a blade, then spent a whole year carving the eaglewood sheath, hoping an elegant scabbard would subdue the blade's wildness and violence.
### ULTRA +
But the sword was lost among the common folk. The blade survived and only the story of the scabbard remained, because the blade, the text says, seeks only to bathe in its victims' blood and no sheath can curb that. To the blade the sheath was a prison that kept it from its true purpose. The account is rumour and never says who tells it.

## owners
### STANDARD
No owner is named in the text. Keqing is shown wielding it in cutscenes (the Turning Point and Where the Heart Finds Rest quests, her demo) and in promotional art, which is a cameo and not an ownership claim.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not literal lore): 4-star sword, base ATK 42, ATK% secondary stat (9 per cent). Passive Bane of Fire and Thunder: increases DMG against opponents affected by Pyro or Electro by 20 to 36 per cent by refinement. A Kunwu series weapon, shared with the 4-Star Bane series (see Dragon's Bane).

## entry: Kunwu
@keys: Kunwu
@keys2: Lion's Roar, Dragon's Bane, Solar Pearl
### STANDARD
Kunwu is the legendary master craftsman named in the item texts of Lion's Roar, Dragon's Bane and Solar Pearl. In Lion's Roar he is shown as an apprentice whose master was dismayed by the sword he made, and later as an artisan who spent a year on its sheath.
