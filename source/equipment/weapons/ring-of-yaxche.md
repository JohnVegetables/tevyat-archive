## summary
### LITE
Ring of Yaxche is a 4-star catalyst you forge: a jade vessel made by a hero of yesteryear, said to have shot down the giant dragon of the volcano. Its story belongs to the twin heroes Ahpub and Ixquieh.
### STANDARD +
Page 1 is their triumph and vow; page 2, unlocked later, is how the same bond ended.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Canon says only that it is a jade vessel. Do not add shapes or inscriptions beyond that.

## lore
### LITE
In the age of myth, when great dragons roamed Natlan, the lost twin heroes reunited before an evil dragon's corpse and swore, upon sacred Yaxche, to end death, destruction and pain, and to ensure the strong never again bully the weak.
### STANDARD +
Their father had died through a Dragonlord's whim and their mother sacrificed herself to protect them, and without the Collective of Plenty and the clans of the mines they could never have reunited.
### LARGE +
They ventured out on the paths of "strength" and "blessings", the young man hot-tempered and the young woman calm, each covering the other's shortcomings. To the conceited self-proclaimed sun god they offered a duel: three lightning strikes from him, and if he failed to kill them, three blows from them. With strength beyond mortal ken they weathered the lightning and shot the false sun from the sky with a blowdart.

For the earthquake-causing great dragon they used deceit instead: they offered it a healing salve and an imitation gem forged with fire unknown to mortals, which fooled the dragon into drinking an elixir that stripped its power, and it was buried alive in the mountains. Their adventures became the origin of the traditions of the Collective of Plenty and the mines. At the crossroads they met the warrior (the wiki gives Xbalanque) and walked with him into the night wind, vowing that Ahpub's blowdarts and Ixquieh's forge-fires would be his.
### ULTRA +
Page 2 (unlocked after Serenity's Reprise) is set when the tribal banners were not yet dusty and the new King of Cinder City had not yet seized the highest authority. The twins, once inseparable, were divided by their positions: Ahpub was a warrior of vengeance and blood, while Ixquieh the flame-forging artisan viewed dragonkind differently. Ahpub had never openly opposed the hero's prohibition, but his hatred of dragons was known. When the Lord of the Cinder City ordered the fleeing wicked dragons pursued, only Ahpub among the heroes answered; as Chief he led the Collective of Plenty's warriors to the mines and demanded that the Nanatzcayan ruler hand over the "ferocious beasts hidden in the mountains" or face their blades.

After days of standoff he invited his twin sister to talk. She told him the real oathbreakers were the usurper and the new King, and warned that harming these peace-loving Saurians would make Nanatzcayan treat him as an enemy of the Alliance. Enraged, he drew his blade to her throat to cow her, but blood spurted forth and ended the talk, and he walked into the incinerating fire holding her. The beacons of war were extinguished along with the disappearance of the two Chiefs. The King sent emissaries to ask the tribes to hand authority to the Alliance until new Chiefs were chosen, while secretly noting the names of those who sheltered dragons. Sakkuk, later proclaimed the tribe's agent, was not yet aware of her fate, and the young heir of the Flower-Feather Clan was already offering the usurper more secret counsel.

## owners
### LITE
No wielder is named. The ring is a jade vessel attributed to the heroes of the legend.
### LARGE +
The text links it to Ahpub and Ixquieh in spirit but names no specific owner; the story that it "shot the dragon" is legend, and scenes that put it in a character's hands are inventions.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not in-world): a forgeable 4-star catalyst of the Salutary series, base ATK 42 at level 1, secondary stat HP; introduced in Version 5.0.
### LARGE +
Forging: Borderland Catalyst Billet plus Condessence Crystal (50), White Iron Chunk (50) and Mora (500). Passive "Echoes of the Plentiful Land": using an Elemental Skill grants the Jade-Forged Crown effect for 10s, in which every 1,000 Max HP raises Normal Attack DMG by 0.6% at refinement 1 up to 1% at refinement 5, with a cap of 16% to 32%. Ascension uses Sacred Lord Delirious materials, Will-series boss materials and mask common materials.

## roleplay
### LARGE
For scenes: a Natlanese storyteller could tell page 1 as a triumph and hold back page 2. Do not claim the legend settles whether the twins died.

## entry: Ahpub and Ixquieh
@keys: Ahpub, Ixquieh
@keys2: Ring of Yaxche, twin heroes
### LARGE
Ahpub and Ixquieh are the twin heroes of the Ring of Yaxche legend: he a hot-tempered warrior who became Chief of the Collective of Plenty, she a calm artisan who ruled the Nanatzcayan mines. They defeated dragons together, then were divided over how to treat the peaceful Saurians, as told on page 2 of the ring's story.
