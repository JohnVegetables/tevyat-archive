## summary
### LITE
Gest of the Mighty Wolf is a five-star claymore of the Hexerei series, released in version 6.4. Its description says it was forged by Ravenwood, who inherited the name of Boreas, before he became a knight. The item story is a Mondstadt tale of a nameless northern knight and his wolf companion Andrius, and of the blacksmith's son who admired him.

## appearance
### STANDARD
No visual description is given in the item text beyond calling it a greatsword. The story speaks of a heavy, fearsome sword, but it is describing the weapon Ravenwood carried at the end, not necessarily this one.

## lore
### STANDARD
Mondstadt's tales praise knights' martial feats, and the "wolf" stories are the best loved, as a free-roaming wolf suits a poet more than a stately lion. They often begin with a wandering knight arriving from the north with his companion Andrius, visiting a city freshly freed. Together they killed beasts plaguing the countryside and drove back a great serpent threatening the coast. The homesick wolf eventually left his spirit in the emerald wilds, and the knight quietly followed his companion's steps. No one learned his name.
### LARGE +
Poets claimed the knight fought after drinking the snowlands' strong spirits, but he did not come from that snowy land. In Mondstadt he lived unassumingly; the Grand Master (Vennessa) never stopped trying to recruit him, while tavern guests thought him an ordinary drinker. Only a boy named Ravenwood, the city blacksmith's son, took notice. The knight had commissioned a sword from the boy's father, paying with unfamiliar coins, and the blacksmith, who had once forged only for nobles, simply gave him a heavy slab of iron (the Wolf's Gravestone), saying everyone should now take up arms in the new Mondstadt. Ravenwood followed him around, often watching him practise.
### ULTRA +
At sunset the tired knight sometimes spoke of a forgotten foreign land where flowers never withered and rivers ran with mead, and where a warrior kingdom once stood. Its warriors, who had sung of devotion and honour, bent to power and gold and offered their blades to a greedy tyrant; the law was abolished and virtue ruined until the enslavers were enslaved. The story ends on the knight's fate of wandering as a man with no homeland, nothing left to defend. Years later the tale of Andrius had become legend; the chain-breaking hero was gone, the knights' code was echoed but comrades had again sought the old aristocracy's embrace. Ravenwood took up a new longsword, quoted the knight's warning about freedom and courage, and set out into the wilds. Days later a knight in a tattered cloak appeared at a fortress, fighting as if guided by a god; an old man recognised the technique of "Andrius", the crowd cheered, and Ravenwood closed his eyes.

## owners
### STANDARD
Ravenwood, said by the description to have forged it before his ascension to knighthood. The wiki identifies him as the second Knight of Boreas. The wandering knight, the first Knight of Boreas, is an earlier figure from the same story.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not lore): five-star claymore from weapon event wishes. Base ATK 46, secondary stat CRIT Rate 7.2%. Passive "Indomitable Chivalry": raises ATK SPD by 10% and builds Four Winds' Hymn stacks (up to four) that raise DMG dealt; with the team bonus "Hexerei: Secret Rite" each stack also raises CRIT DMG.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay use (this project's suggestion, not canon text): the story suits scenes about borrowed names and legend outliving the person. Treat the connection between Ravenwood, Andrius and Boreas as something characters know only if they have read the item or Varka's quests.

### STANDARD
Ravenwood was the son of Mondstadt's blacksmith, later the second Knight of Boreas (wiki title). He wielded a greatsword in battle, a design later copied and used as a model for Ferrous Shadow. He is dead; he appears in canon only through Varka's quest and item text.

## entry: Andrius, the wandering knight
@keys: Andrius, Wolf Knight, wandering knight
@keys2: Mondstadt, wolf
@version: 6.4
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
The legend of Andrius mixes a wolf and a knight: the wolf Andrius ran with the nameless knight and, per the story, left his spirit in the wilds. Wolf's Gravestone, the heavy slab of iron given to the knight, is the companion weapon. The knight's arrival is dated by the wiki to about a thousand years before the game, after the fall of the aristocracy.

### LARGE
The Hexerei series is a group of five-star weapons whose passives are enhanced by the team bonus "Hexerei: Secret Rite". The wiki lists Gest of the Mighty Wolf as one of three that recount Mondstadt history, the others being Disaster and Remorse and The Daybreak Chronicles.
