## summary
### LITE
Dodoco Tales is a 4-star catalyst, a small children's book of cheerful adventure stories about the Dodoco, furry creatures from the Golden Apple Archipelago. Its text is told in a child's voice and says the book was given by the freest mother in the world to the luckiest daughter in the world. It was an event reward from Midsummer Island Adventure (Version 1.6), a limited-time event.
### STANDARD +
The wiki notes that Klee is shown holding this catalyst in her Genius Invokation TCG card. The book itself is not tied to a named owner.

## appearance
### STANDARD
A small, precious picture book. Its description says it is filled with childish short stories that make readers laugh and that even long-grown adults get absorbed in the innocent, naive little adventures.

## lore
@availability: limited
### LITE
The tale says there is a group of islands in a foggy sea called the Golden Apple Archipelago, where the cute, peace-loving Dodo Clan live without troubles. They call each other "Dodoco", which means "the best of friends".
### STANDARD +
Like dandelion seeds, every Dodoco must one day ride the wind and waves into the wider world to find a red, blazing four-leaf clover. Having lived together so long, the Dodocos have all become alike and cannot tell each other apart, with no "you" or "me". Only the clover, the story says, lets a Dodoco learn what "Dodoco" truly means and tell friends apart. The narrator adds that this is "what my mom says".
### LARGE +
A Dodoco tumbling into the sea or leaping into the wind and drifting away means a lucky child is about to meet their bestest friend. The closing paragraph says the book chronicles the Dodoco's adventures before meeting the daughter and the bigger, sparkly adventures since, with sparks, splashes, treasures and many friends. The wiki's trivia ties the catalyst to Klee through her TCG card; the text does not.
### ULTRA +
Tone: wholly warm and childlike, written as a child retelling a mother's story. When using it in a scene, keep the Dodoco legend as stories the book tells rather than as verified facts about the Golden Apple Archipelago.

## owners
### LITE
No owner is named in the text. The book is described as given by a mother to a daughter.

## gameplay
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not in-world): 4-star catalyst obtained from the Midsummer Island Adventure event (Version 1.6, 2021-06-17 per the wiki infobox), base ATK 41, secondary stat ATK 12%. Passive Dodoventure!: Normal Attack hits raise Charged Attack DMG for 6 s, and Charged Attack hits raise ATK for 6 s. It is refined with the event material Fragments of Innocence rather than duplicate weapons.
### LARGE +
The Charged Attack DMG bonus from Normal Attack hits is 16% at refinement 1 and 32% at refinement 5.
