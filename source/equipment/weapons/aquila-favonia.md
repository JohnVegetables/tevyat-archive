## summary
### LITE
Aquila Favonia is the sword of Vennessa, the enslaved foreign gladiator who led Mondstadt's revolt against its tyrannical old nobles about a thousand years ago and founded the Knights of Favonius and the Church of Favonius. Its item text calls it "the soul of the Knights of Favonius" and says it still calls on the winds of swift justice. Its name means "Eagle of the West Wind", echoing Vennessa's later title, the Falcon of the West.
### STANDARD +
The sword is a symbol of Mondstadt's founding ideals - justice, freedom and wisdom - rather than an artifact anyone in the present story is shown carrying. In game terms it is a 5-star sword.

## appearance
### LARGE
Canon text does not describe the sword's look in words; its in-game model is a knightly longsword. When describing it, keep to what is safe: a fine sword of Mondstadt's knightly tradition, associated with wind and with the falcon or eagle imagery of the Knights of Favonius (eagle crests decorate the walls of the Knights' headquarters).

## lore
### LITE
The sword's story says everyone knew "the falcon's" protection was a blessing of Favonius, but few knew that this blessing came from a distant tribe. Under the old nobles, an exiled foreign fighter was enslaved in Mondstadt; she refused to kneel to injustice, overthrew the nobles, founded a chivalric order and the Church, and was eventually blessed to walk with the gods. The sword witnessed all her hardships and deeds.
### STANDARD +
The text ends with the spirit the wielder is said to feel when swinging it, summarised as a creed: justice for the oppressed, freedom for the shackled, wisdom for the deceived, and to let the wind of justice and freedom lead. The Genius Invokation TCG card adds that the sword was always at the hip of "the warrior from another land" and bore witness to her spreading justice and freedom across Mondstadt.
### LARGE +
The "distant tribe" is Vennessa's own people. Other sources (the official manga, as summarised on the wiki) identify her as a Muratan, one of a wandering people of Natlanese descent who settled in Mondstadt and were enslaved by the aristocracy; so the "blessing of Favonius" that Mondstadt credits to its own wind is, in the sword's telling, partly a gift of foreigners. The phrase "walk with the gods" refers to the legend that Vennessa ascended to Celestia at Windrise at the end of her life, where the great oak now called the Symbol of Mondstadt's Hero stands.

## owners
### LITE
Vennessa (the Falcon of the West, first Grand Master and first Dandelion Knight of the Knights of Favonius) is the only named wielder.
### LARGE +
No present-day character is established as owning or carrying the original sword. Jean, the present Dandelion Knight, inherits Vennessa's office and ideals, not this weapon as far as canon states; do not portray her as wielding it by default.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not in-world): a 5-star sword released in Version 1.0, available in the standard Wish (Wanderlust Invocation) and in weapon event and Chronicled Wishes.
### LARGE +
Base ATK 48 with Physical DMG Bonus as its secondary stat. Its passive, Falcon's Defiance, raises ATK; when the wielder takes damage, "the soul of the Falcon of the West" awakens, healing the wielder based on ATK and damaging nearby enemies, at most once every 15 seconds. Refinement scales the passive from +20% to +40% ATK, healing from 100% to 160% of ATK, and the burst from 200% to 320% of ATK. Ascension uses Decarabian's-series weapon materials (Tile of Decarabian's Tower and its higher tiers), the Heavy Horn line, and arrowheads from hilichurl archers. A Genius Invokation TCG equipment card of the same name (added in 3.3) gives +1 damage and small self-healing.

## accounts
### ULTRA
Related accounts that frame the sword's story: Venti's character stories say the rebellion succeeded partly because a "secret treaty" showing the nobles meant to sell Mondstadt's people to Rex Lapis turned the aristocracy's soldiers against them - a forgery that, unknown to historians, Barbatos himself made. Dainsleif, in his Bough Keeper story quest, remarks that the Falcon of the West, though it can soar on the wind, only hovers beneath the light of the gods; he does not name Vennessa, so this reads as a skeptical view of her ascension rather than a fact about the sword. The sword's own text presents the ascension as a blessing, so keep both perspectives attributed.

## entry: Falcon's Defiance
@keys: Falcon's Defiance
@keys2: Aquila Favonia, Vennessa, Falcon of the West
### LARGE
Falcon's Defiance is the name of Aquila Favonia's weapon passive (gameplay). Its flavour: when the wielder is struck, the soul of the Falcon of the West awakens and raises the banner of resistance, healing the wielder and striking enemies around them. It frames Vennessa's legacy as defiance of oppression.
