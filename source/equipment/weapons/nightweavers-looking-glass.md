## summary
### LITE
A flawless silver mirror said to have witnessed the Moon Maiden's birth; the Frostmoon Scions hold it a relic of unrivalled provenance. A 5-star catalyst from Version 6.0.
### STANDARD +
Its item text is a Scion-side history of Hyperborea's Archpriestesses, the first Moonchanter Aila and the hoped-for new moon.

## appearance
### STANDARD
A mirror of flawless silver used as a catalyst; canon gives no further description.

## lore
### LITE
Its story opens with a prophecy-verse: the Eternal Moon fell, the Iridescent Moon shattered, the Frost Moon ceased to turn, and the world marches toward its end until Elysium dawns.
### STANDARD +
Descendants of the Golden City of Hyperborea built a lonely temple on the tundra, forged pale crowns of secret silver and chose Archpriestesses, though their gods had departed. The first Moonchanter, Aila, was a mortal who witnessed the birth of a god.
### LARGE +
The text presents the Scions' rituals as both a wall hiding them from the world and the sign of a people without a homeland, awaiting a sacred heir of a pure bloodline after which all sins would be forgiven as service to Elysium.

According to later generations, Aila foresaw the new moon (Columbina) and chose to abandon the old rites. The last Archpriestess, Lovia, admired her, handed her the authority to lead the people and let her seal the ancient sanctuary with her own hands. The title of Archpriestess of Hyperborea was never held again; only the Moonchanter remained.
### ULTRA +
After an ellipsis the text jumps forward through many moons. Those who dreamed of Elysium found the shadow of the axe, while those who longed for salvation were swept up in flowing light. It names the mad monk who reached for the heavens, the old master of the Pale Star throne (the Belyi Tsar), the White Emperor who fell into the black waves of the Abyss, and the silent nightingales (the Lightkeepers). As promised to the Torchforger (Solovei), the new moon's maiden spun a prayer song and laid it to rest alongside the sanctuary. The closing lines repeat the verse but end on hope: from the ashes the New Moon rises and spins the wheel of fate anew. The text is a Scion-side account with a partisan tone; each person it names has their own entry.

## owners
### LITE
No wielder is named in canon; the description ties the mirror to the Frostmoon Scions as a whole.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not in-world): 5-star catalyst with Elemental Mastery as secondary stat. Passive Millennial Hymn gives Elemental Mastery after the wielder's Hydro or Dendro Skill damage and when party members trigger Lunar-Bloom; with both active, the party gains Bloom, Hyperbloom, Burgeon and Lunar-Bloom bonuses, even off-field.

## roleplay
### LARGE
Treat it as a Scion heirloom with ritual weight: those who know Nod-Krai religion may recognise it as sacred, outsiders see a beautiful silver mirror. Do not assign an owner, and keep claims about Aila as what the Scions say, not settled history.

## entry: Millennial Hymn
@keys: Millennial Hymn, Prayer of the Far North, New Moon Verse
@version: 6.0
### STANDARD
Millennial Hymn is the weapon's passive. Its two named effects, Prayer of the Far North and New Moon Verse, echo the text's themes of the northern faith and the coming new moon. Mechanics are in the gameplay section.
