## summary
### LITE
Frostbearer is a 4-star catalyst of the Dragonspine series. Its in-game description is "a fruit that possesses a strange, frosty will," with a faint sense of agony. It is the one weapon of the series obtained only by forging, using a diagram from the Frostbearing Tree. Added in Version 1.2.
### STANDARD +
Its text recounts the Frostbearing Tree of Sal Vindagnyr from the princess's birth to the tree's revival by dragon blood, and ends with the tree's own voice offering a bitter fruit to whoever can deliver justice on a poisoned world.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Canon text calls it a crimson, icy fruit borne by a silver-white tree. No further physical description is given.

## lore
### LITE
When the daughter of the priest of Vindagnyr was born beneath the white tree, the verdant mountain kingdom rejoiced at her blessing. Those who wrote the kingdom's annals believed that Sal Vindagnyr's blessing, like the undying silver-white tree, would last forever.
### STANDARD +
Then the nail that froze the world fell from the sky and shattered the tree. The princess took the most complete branch and tried to graft it to give the tree new life, but the grafted life did not flourish, and the snowstorm covered the moonlight like a curtain of blades.
### LARGE +
Long afterward, when the deathmatch between the dragons of darkness and wind (Durin and Dvalin) ended and corrosive blood stained the ashen valley red, the tree remembered it had not died with the entombed city. It reached for the warm ichor in the soil. When a certain someone poured crimson essence on it, it bore one fruit with all its remaining strength.
### ULTRA +
The tree speaks last: it gives this icy fruit to those who dwelt in its shadow, the priests who eulogized it and the maiden who painted upon it, containing all the happiness they never had, and asks that it go to someone who can render recompense upon the poisonous world and carry its innocent, bitter fruit as they enact justice. Compare Snow-Tombed Starsilver, which tells the princess's and a hero's story in the same ruined kingdom.

## owners
### STANDARD
No owner is named in canon. It is a crafted weapon, made at the forge from the diagram for Frostbearer.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not literal lore): a 4-star catalyst with base ATK 42 and an ATK% secondary stat. The Dragonspine series passive Frost Burial gives Normal and Charged Attack hits a chance (60 to 100 per cent by refinement, once per 10s) of dropping an Everfrost Icicle that deals area damage, more against Cryo-affected enemies. The wiki notes it is the only Dragonspine weapon that can be obtained only by forging; its diagram comes from raising the Frostbearing Tree to level 10.

## entry: Frostbearing Tree

@keys2: Frostbearer, Sal Vindagnyr
@version: 1.2
### STANDARD
The Frostbearing Tree is the silver-white tree of Sal Vindagnyr whose roots pierced the earth. After the Skyfrost Nail shattered it, the princess tried to graft a branch to revive it; in much later times, dragon blood and crimson essence gave it one fruit, the source of Frostbearer. In game it is a Dragonspine tree the player can level up.
