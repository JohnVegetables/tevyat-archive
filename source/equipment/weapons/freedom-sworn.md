## summary
### LITE
Freedom-Sworn is a five-star sword released in version 1.6, a Millennial Movement weapon whose story is a Mondstadt resistance song. The song says that if someone takes your tongue you can still sing with your eyes, and that stealing your song and your freedom "shall never do". It was sung through Decarabian's reign and the aristocracy's, and when five rebels climbed Decarabian's tower.

## appearance
### STANDARD
The in-game description calls it a straight sword, "azure as antediluvian song, and as keen as the oaths of freedom taken in the Land of Wind". The text gives no further visual detail.

## lore
### STANDARD
The item's story begins with the drinking song itself: even if your tongue is plucked out you can sing with your eyes; even if you are blinded you can see with your ears; and if someone plots to destroy tomorrow, raise them a glass, for the song will live on. It then reflects on whether the Anemo Archon's love of freedom and wine shaped the people or the people shaped him; the text says this can no longer be answered.
### LARGE +
The song was sung in dark times: when Decarabian ruled from his gale-crowned tower, and again when the corrupt nobility (the Lawrence clan) overturned the Archon's statue. It was heard in cellars, alleys and taverns, seeped through the wind and the iron fist, and became an anthem for resisting heroes.
### ULTRA +
In the distant past a group stood before the tower, a youth, a spirit, an archer, a knight and a wandering flame-haired warrior, and swore to be free and to shatter the lone tyrant's rule. Those too weak to climb sang the song that had only been whispered where the wind did not reach, a lyre's sound finally piercing the prison of raging winds, and the chorus shook the city walls. The final verse changes the last line to: anyone who steals your song, the freedom you yearn for, will never be allowed. The wiki's series page ties the group to the Nameless Bard, Barbatos the wind spirit, Amos, Gunnhildr and the Red-Haired Warrior.

## owners
### STANDARD
No owner is named in the text. The sword stands for the song rather than a single wielder.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not lore): five-star sword from weapon event wishes. Base ATK 46, secondary stat Elemental Mastery 43. Passive "Revolutionary Chorale": raises DMG (10-20%); triggering Elemental Reactions gives a Sigil of Rebellion, and at two Sigils nearby party members get "Millennial Movement: Song of Resistance" for 12 seconds (more Normal, Charged and Plunging Attack DMG and ATK), followed by a 20-second lockout. Buffs of the same type do not stack.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay use (this project's suggestion, not canon text): the song works as a toast or defiance chant at taverns. Ordinary Mondstadters can know the song; the identity of the five rebels is more specialist history.

### STANDARD
The resistance drinking song of Mondstadt, quoted in Freedom-Sworn, is said to have been sung whenever the people were oppressed, under Decarabian and under the aristocracy. Its line "even if tomorrow dies, this song shall live on" frames music as the thing tyrants cannot remove.

### LARGE
Freedom-Sworn lists five rebels at the tower: a youth, a spirit, an archer, a knight and a flame-haired warrior. Amos' Bow tells the same climb from the huntress's side and names only four; the wiki records that the later Freedom-Sworn text established the Red-Haired Warrior as a fifth member. In this archive the group is the Nameless Bard, Barbatos, Amos, Gunnhildr and the Red-Haired Warrior.
