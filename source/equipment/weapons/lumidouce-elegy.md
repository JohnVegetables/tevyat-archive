## summary
### LITE
Lumidouce Elegy is a 5-star polearm adorned with azure crystal. It is said to have been the treasure of a legendary Champion Duelist, yet never once used.
### STANDARD +
The weapon story follows that duelist, Marfisa (the wiki's identification), from the undercity Fleuve Cendre to the stage of the opera house, to the court's service as an enforcer, and finally to the pure sourcewaters of Narzissenkreuz.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Canon describes a magnificent polearm adorned with azure crystal; the story gives its heroine sapphire eyes, but the text does not link that to the weapon. Arkhe-aligned wielders show a Pneuma or Ousia mark. Add nothing else.

## lore
### LITE
In the Fleuve Cendre undercity where there is neither rain nor sun, a girl scraped by for scraps and would kill for a slice of mouldy bread, accepting that the weak are prey. Her mother's teaching was ashes to ashes, dust to dust.
### STANDARD +
Facing death, she is instead shown kindness by a girl in strange attire (the wiki links her to Parsifal the Great), who tucks a Lumidouce Bell flower behind her ear, saying it means hope and matches her hair, and trades it for her heart. She later watches the magician and assistant from a VIP box in the Opera Epiclese and sees hope in the flowers blooming under the spotlight. With no teacher, she steps onto a stage of her own, her lance performing every spear technique she has seen.
### LARGE +
She is undefeated until a swordsman, whom the wiki links to Emanuel Guillotin, tricks her by slowing the rhythm and wounds her arm. He tells her that her stiff adherence to rules is her weakness and suggests she make something immutable, like justice, her rule. She answers angrily that surface-dwellers have no right to speak of justice, but concludes that crimes should be punished and lies exposed. She becomes a purple-clad enforcer, a Champion Duelist, serving justice until, after the Siege of Poisson, the criminal Parsifal is brought before her.
### ULTRA +
The wiki adds that Parsifal, or Josephine in her sister's name, requested trial by combat and presumably died in the duel against Marfisa, and that Marfisa later went hunting for the changed Emanuel, which is how she met the Narzissenkreuz Ordo. The weapon text itself ends differently, with her falling into the pure sourcewaters and the self-evident meaning of her life dissolving along with her memories. Before that, she never learned that the Lumidouce Bell's true meaning in the language of flowers was not hope. The final line says that even fate gave her no blossoming elegy.

## owners
### LITE
A legendary Champion Duelist, linked by the wiki to Marfisa. The weapon was never used.
### LARGE +
Emilie is paired with it only as a release convention (see uncertainties).

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not in-world): a 5-star polearm, base ATK 46 at level 1, secondary stat CRIT Rate; Weapon Event Wishes; introduced in Version 4.8. Passive "Bright Dawn Overture": raises ATK; after the wielder triggers Burning, or deals Dendro DMG to Burning enemies, their DMG rises for 8s (up to 2 stacks); at 2 stacks, or when refreshed at 2 stacks, Energy is restored, at most once per 12s. It can trigger off-field. Values scale with refinement.

## roleplay
### LARGE
For scenes: Marfisa is a past figure; modern Fontainians know the Champion Duelist title, not this polearm. The description says the polearm was never used, so do not narrate famous kills with it.

## entry: Marfisa
@keys: Marfisa, Lumidouce Elegy

### STANDARD
Marfisa was a Champion Duelist of the Court of Fontaine who came from the Fleuve Cendre. The wiki links her to the story of Lumidouce Elegy: self-taught lance-fighter, defeated once by a swordsman who convinced her to follow justice, and dissolved in the Narzissenkreuz's waters.
