## summary
### LITE
The Flute is a four-star sword of the Wanderer's Troupe series. It has a nimble, delicately engraved and perforated blade that sounded like a flute when swung with skill, the pitch depending on the swing angle. It is tied to a swordswoman of the Troupe later called the Dawnlight Swordswoman.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Item text: a thin blade with holes and delicate engraving, lavishly decorated beneath a rusty exterior. After the Troupe disbanded it was buried; unearthed years later it can no longer sing, though it remains lethal.

## lore
### STANDARD
Among the Wanderer's Troupe was a valiant sword-wielding dancer. After the Troupe's attempt to tear down the ruling class failed she was enslaved as a gladiator, and though she lost her hope and companions she fought bravely, her sword singing with the morning light; she was dubbed the Dawnlight Swordswoman.
### LARGE +
In his youth the Dawn Knight Ragnvindr served in a knight's retinue. He went with his master to a gladiator match and was moved by her splendid finale. He took the name Dawn Knight in her honour and knew in his heart what he must do next.
### ULTRA +
The text stops at Ragnvindr's resolve and does not say what he did, nor what happened to the swordswoman afterwards. Within the series the Flute is the sword counterpart to the Stringless (the Troupe's harp-bow) and the Widsith (their notebook).

## owners
### STANDARD
The Troupe's sword-wielding dancer, later the Dawnlight Swordswoman. The sword was buried when the Troupe disbanded.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Game mechanics: Wanderer's Troupe-series four-star sword, base ATK 42, ATK 9% secondary, passive 'Chord' (stacking harmonics). Standard and event wishes.
