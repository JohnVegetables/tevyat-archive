## summary
### LITE
Dragon's Bane is a 4-star polearm of the Kunwu series, described as a polearm with an entwining golden dragon, light and sharp, that may well kill dragons with ease. It is rumoured to be a legendary polearm of Liyue. It has been in the game since Version 1.0.
### STANDARD +
The item text says the master craftsman Kunwu forged it with a dragon's spine as the haft and a dragon's claw as the tip.

## appearance
### STANDARD
A dragon motif runs from handle to tip. It once had a dragon-shaped wooden sheath, but according to the text it no longer needs to contain its might. Do not invent colours beyond the golden dragon the in-game description mentions.

## lore
### LITE
It is called a legendary Liyue polearm that pierces anything in its way.
### STANDARD +
Because of its materials, the body is said to be unbreakable and the tip sharp beyond compare, gleaming with a dragon's malice.
### LARGE +
The text tells of a dragon slayer of immense strength and courage who defied the ocean's wrath and fought the monstrosities in it. Many years after the slayer vanished without a trace, a dragon's hopeless cry was still heard across the ocean.
### ULTRA +
The slayer became an urban legend, and in the end both the legend and the polearm were lost. Freed from the shackles of its past, the weapon is said to have shown its full ferocity once and for all, as if marking the dragon's return to the ocean where it belonged. The text is a tale told as rumour and never confirms it.

## owners
### STANDARD
Only the unnamed dragon slayer of the item text is tied to it in story. Yun Jin, Yaoyao and Xiangling are shown with it in promotional art, demos and trailers, which is a cameo and not an ownership claim.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not literal lore): 4-star polearm, base ATK 41, Elemental Mastery secondary stat (48). Passive Bane of Flame and Water: increases DMG against opponents affected by Hydro or Pyro by 20 to 36 per cent by refinement. A Kunwu series weapon, shared with the 4-Star Bane series (see Lion's Roar).
