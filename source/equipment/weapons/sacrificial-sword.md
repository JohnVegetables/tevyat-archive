## summary
### LITE
Sacrificial Sword is a 4-star sword of the Sacrificial series, in the game since Version 1.0. Its item text describes a ceremonial sword, originally a prop and sharpened by time, that belonged to the kindly Gunnhildr clan, who enacted the defense of Mondstadt in rituals for the winds of time.
### STANDARD +
Its story is one of protection, meant to show the courage to protect.

## appearance
### STANDARD
A sword with trinkets still visible, described in game as petrified over time. The text says its blade, originally just a prop, was sharpened by the passage of time. No other detail is given.

## lore
### LITE
The Gunnhildr clan used it in sacrificial ceremonies that enacted the defense of Mondstadt.
### STANDARD +
On the cliff facing the eastern sea, the ancestors are said to have worshipped the masters of Time and Anemo together; the item text quotes a saying that Anemo brings stories while Time nurtures them. The ceremony dedicated to the winds of time had three acts, and the final act told a tale of the protection of Mondstadt, of life, and of freedom.
### LARGE +
The text says the ceremony and its history are now lost, but that the Gunnhildr clan continues to act as guardians of Mondstadt to this day. This ties the item to the clan's real role in the city's history without adding named individuals.
### ULTRA +
Of the three Sacrificial weapons it is the hopeful one: where the Bow's Lawrence clan decays and the Greatsword's Imunlaukr clan seeks a cause, the Gunnhildr clan's ritual role and real role coincide. The Gunnhildr clan's history belongs in its own entry.

## owners
### STANDARD
The item text names the Gunnhildr clan as former owners. No individual wielder is named.

## trivia
### STANDARD
Albedo is seen wielding a Sacrificial Sword in his official art. This is an art detail, not a statement of ownership in the story.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not literal lore): 4-star sword, base ATK 41, Energy Recharge secondary stat. Passive Composed: after an Elemental Skill deals damage, it has a 40 to 80 per cent chance (by refinement) to end its own cooldown, at most once every 30 to 16 seconds. Obtainable from the Standard Wanderlust Invocation and from character and weapon event wishes; part of the Sacrificial series.
