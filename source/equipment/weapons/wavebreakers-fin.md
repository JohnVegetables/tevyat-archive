## summary
### LITE
Wavebreaker's Fin is a four-star polearm of the Watatsumi series. Item text calls it the naginata of "Umigozen", a mighty Watatsumi warrior, whose edge flows with the phosphorescence of the deep. Narukami's navies are recorded in the island's music as having quailed before her. It is the only proof that she once tried to change the tides of the world.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Item text says only that its edge flows with the phosphorescence of the depths and that it is still sharp.

## lore
### STANDARD
The text recalls that the twin Watatsumi shrine maidens sang whalesong that followed the tide into the island's dreams, and that Watatsumi's warriors rested their hopes on them. With nagamaki flashing like a white wave, the army advanced on the other islands, until Omikami's glory and that of his warriors were dimmed by the lightning's glow. Mouun was consumed by a dark storm of crow feathers, the great whale sank, and the vassal ruler, "determined as a child", vanished into a rift in the earth.
### LARGE +
Umigozen was lost to the waves and became a myth. One legend says she dived alone into the press of tengu warriors to recover her comrades' bodies and was lost in a valiant last stand; another says she hid afterwards and steered her flagship into the Dark Sea at the world's border. The text ends on the hope that as long as the seas are disturbed by waves her song will live on, and the legend that it still echoes in seashells and the bellies of great whales.
### ULTRA +
The wiki's links read "Umigozen" as the shrine maiden Ayame, the "vassal ruler" as Akuou, and Omikami as Orobashi; Mouun's Moon and Akuoumaru tell the same war from those characters' sides. Wavebreaker's Fin is the latest-in-time of the three, since it covers the defeat and the legends that followed.

## owners
### STANDARD
"Umigozen" (Ayame, historical.ayame). No later owner is named.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not lore): four-star polearm. Base ATK 45, secondary stat ATK 3%. Passive "Watatsumi Wavewalker" (shared with Akuoumaru and Mouun's Moon): for every point of the party's combined maximum Energy capacity, the wielder's Elemental Burst DMG rises, up to a cap (the maximum needs about 333 Energy cost in the party, which as of 2.5 could be reached with Yae Miko, Raiden Shogun and two more 80-cost characters). Obtained from weapon event wishes.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay use (this project's suggestion, not canon text): the naginata of a heroine who became legend, whose real end is a matter of competing stories.

### STANDARD
"Umigozen" is the title by which the mighty Watatsumi warrior Ayame is remembered in songs. One legend says she died in a last stand against the tengu warriors; another that she steered her flagship into the Dark Sea at the edge of the world. The item text leaves the question open.
