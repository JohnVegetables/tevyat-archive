## summary
### LITE
Wolf-Fang is a 4-star sword from the Battle Pass Gnostic Hymn of Version 4.0, in-game described as a longsword once used by a wandering knight. Its text explains how the nameless first Knight of Boreas became a legend in early Mondstadt. Released 2023-08-16.
### STANDARD +
The text separates the story told in the streets from what likely happened, and notes Mondstadt's two famous knightly titles: the Lion, from the first Grand Master, and the Wolf Knight, Boreas.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Canon gives no appearance for the sword. The knight in the text wore a dust-stained, tattered cloak over exquisitely crafted but battle-worn armor and paid in real gold and silver coins with unfamiliar symbols.

## lore
### LITE
Mondstadt's knights have, "by coincidence or fate," two distinguished titles and animal insignia: the Lion of the first Grand Master of the Knights of Favonius, and the Wolf Knight, Boreas, from almost the same period. In truth, the text says, there is no record at all of the Knight of Boreas; the name comes from stories circulating in the streets.
### STANDARD +
In the tale, a merchant or farmer whose daughter was saved asks the knight how to thank him; the knight says to toast his companion instead, "Boreas." A gust blows, bestial eyes shine in the dark, and the knight is gone. The daughter is always saved and the knight always nameless.
### LARGE +
Before the story the knight was a strange traveler in a dusty cloak. The tavern owner noticed he paid in real silver and gold and was not rowdy. The tale grew from a snake demon from beyond the sea; the knight left the city to hunt demons for a single silver coin, and the carrion on the beach drew thousands of falcons, which, the text says, gave Falcon Coast its real name rather than the legend of the Falcon of the West.
### ULTRA +
The Knights of Favonius tried repeatedly to recruit him and he refused. The text guesses that the two famous lines of the story were spoken when the first Grand Master, out of patience after countless refusals, had the knight surrounded in the street, and that the real exchange was far less civil. Talented bards merged that dialogue with other events into many stories; he stayed only a few years, but his shadow remains.

## owners
### STANDARD
Owner: the Wandering Knight, the first Knight of Boreas. The wiki cites this weapon and Wolf's Gravestone as evidence that he lived in Mondstadt during Vennessa's rebellion about a thousand years ago.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not literal lore): a 4-star sword with base ATK 42 and a CRIT Rate secondary stat. Its passive, Northwind Wolf, raises Elemental Skill and Burst damage; skill and burst hits each add CRIT Rate for 10s (separately, up to four stacks). The wiki notes the CRIT bonus works only while the wielder is on the field. Obtained from the Battle Pass BP Bounty.

### STANDARD
Wolf-Fang's text treats "Boreas" as a street legend. The first Knight of Boreas lived in Mondstadt only a few years, declined to join the Knights of Favonius, and was recorded nowhere; the stories grew from a demon hunt and a quarrel with the early Grand Master. Boreas himself is the Wolf of the North, whom the knight called his companion.
