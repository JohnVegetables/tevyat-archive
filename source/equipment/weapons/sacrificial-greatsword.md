## summary
### LITE
Sacrificial Greatsword is a 4-star claymore of the Sacrificial series, in the game since Version 1.0. Its item text describes a ceremonial greatsword that began as a prop and was hardened by time, once belonging to the Imunlaukr clan, brave and gifted warriors who fought hard and died young.
### STANDARD +
It tells a story of war and was used to enact war tales in sacrificial ceremonies dedicated to Time and Anemo.

## appearance
### STANDARD
A greatsword with trinkets still visible on it, described in game as petrified over time. The text says its blade, originally only a prop, was hardened by the passage of time. No other detail is given.

## lore
### LITE
The Imunlaukr clan used it in sacrificial ceremonies to enact duels between brave warriors stained black with blood.
### STANDARD +
On the cliff facing the eastern sea, the ancestors are said to have worshipped the masters of Time and Anemo together; the item text quotes a saying that Anemo brings stories while Time nurtures them. In the clan's eyes, combat existed neither for protection, glory nor territory but for the amusement of the gods high in the heavens. They fought monsters and outlaws alike with no regard for whether they would return to their loved ones, and the point was to roar loud and clear in the heat of battle.
### LARGE +
The text observes that such hot blood should not have allowed a long history, since their fight was endless and without hope of victory. Yet as the snow slowly melted and Mondstadt began to take shape, they gradually realized they had at last found something to fight for. It is a legend-style narration with no named narrator.
### ULTRA +
Compared with its siblings, the Greatsword is the one whose clan changes purpose: the Sword's Gunnhildr clan guard Mondstadt and the Bow's Lawrence clan decline, whereas the Imunlaukr move from fighting for the amusement of gods to fighting for a place. Specific people and events of the clan belong in the clan entries.

## owners
### STANDARD
The item text names the Imunlaukr clan as former owners. No individual wielder is named.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not literal lore): 4-star claymore, base ATK 44, Energy Recharge secondary stat. Passive Composed: after an Elemental Skill deals damage, it has a 40 to 80 per cent chance (by refinement) to end its own cooldown, at most once every 30 to 16 seconds. Obtainable from the Standard Wanderlust Invocation and from character and weapon event wishes; part of the Sacrificial series.
