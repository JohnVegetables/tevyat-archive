## summary
### LITE
Athame Artis is a 5-star sword in the Hexerei series, described as a precious sword the Day King gave to the brave hero in a little witch's story, only for the witch to take it and defeat the hero herself. Its text is a fairy-tale in which the witch destroys a perpetual motion machine to keep a dying planet alive. Added in Version 6.2.
### STANDARD +
The weapon is a story object. The wiki's Hexerei Series page notes that its mechanics are tailored to Durin, who also comes from a fairy tale. The name echoes the real-world ceremonial blade called an athame.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Canon gives no physical description; it is called a precious sword gifted by the Day King, carried on the hero's back in the story. Do not add decoration or materials.

## lore
### LITE
A little witch arrives at the Day King's palace as twilight takes the kingdom; the story follows her through the following night.
### STANDARD +
She had come answering a faint message about a planet feeling unwell, and when night falls the Day King's court dissolves into a black ocean. She flies to a lonely castle where a warrior invites her to see the perpetual motion machine they call the Holy Grail, which burns seawater to keep the dark at bay.
### LARGE +
The Day King greets the witch as one who traversed the cosmos, kindler of time's eternal flame, and tells her the Kingdom of Day would never harm the planet; he sends her to ask the Night King, then fades as the sun sets. The witch, who had been hiding in her workshop on the eve of the Night Carnival from a nemesis, had spent effort deciphering the message. The warrior explains that defeating the Night King changes nothing because the dark tides return after each sunset, and that the machine was built to break the cycle. The witch sighs that burning the sea will only drain the planet faster, since all tangible things on it are shadows cast by light, and that there is nothing holy about the grail. She takes the sword from the warrior's back and throws it into the machine; when the blade touches the black sea mist both warrior and fading light vanish, and the planet is swallowed by darkness. When daylight returns it is as though everything had been a dream. She calls it a temporary fix and leaves on her broom.
### ULTRA +
In the closing scene the warrior, shadow trailing behind him, is shown taking up the sword bestowed by the Day King and setting out again, with a line saying the cycle may go on for a long time and that he must keep living until they meet again. The wiki notes the story may connect to the book The Little Witch and the Undying Fire referenced in Durin's Story Quest, and that stories like this may be allegories of real history; neither point is stated in the item text. The witch's nemesis, the planet's black ocean and the Night Carnival are named only in this fable.

## owners
### LITE
The item belongs to the story's Day King, who gave it to a hero; no real-world owner is named in canon. The wiki says the weapon is tailored to Durin.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Game mechanics, not lore: a 5-star sword with base ATK 46 and a CRIT Rate secondary stat. Day King's Splendor Solis raises Elemental Burst CRIT DMG and, when a Burst hits, grants an ATK buff to the wielder and nearby party members; having a Hexerei: Secret Rite effect in the party increases it further. It was on the weapon event Wish Epitome Invocation (2025-12-03 to 2025-12-23) and again on the Version 6.6 banner (2026-05-20).

## roleplay
### LARGE
This archive's suggested use: a story-sword from a fairy tale about a witch who prefers a quiet fix to a great battle. Keep it tied to the fable; do not give it a real forger or a present-day owner.
