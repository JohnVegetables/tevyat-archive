## summary
### LITE
Everlasting Moonglow is a pearl-strung catalyst from the Sangonomiya and Watatsumi tradition. Its item text is a poetic history of Watatsumi Island: the people who dreamed with their god, the first Divine Priestess, the lightning that struck down their dream, and the memory that survives in the "pearl and waves" emblem.
### STANDARD +
It is a 5-star catalyst and, unusually, is not part of the Inazuma weapon series. Its description calls it a string of lovely jasper from the deep sea, shining with a pure radiance like the moon and "ever-distant".

## appearance
### STANDARD
A string or ring of pearls and jewelled beads, with a pale moonlit glow, set with the Sangonomiya emblem. The description speaks of a string of lovely jasper from the deep sea; canon text says nothing more of its design.

## lore
### LITE
The text begins with the "pearl and waves" emblem of the Sangonomiya, said to mean the waves that embrace Watatsumi and its bright pearls, though some say it shows the jewelled wheel of Watatsumi Omikami keeping eternal watch over the coral land like the moon. It then recalls how the descendants of their god passed hope from generation to generation.
### STANDARD +
The first Divine Priestess led her people with wisdom as precious as pearls and chose clergy from those newly introduced to sunlight, who comforted those who feared the day. The text also recalls the Umigozen, who made the navies of Narukami tremble and sang whale-songs with them, and says the emblem was drawn as they danced with the airborne kurage.
### LARGE +
Later "a single bolt of lightning" rejected the Watatsumi people's dream, and the serpent slithered into the storm to face its glow. The children of the god and the pearl-hearted shrine maidens did not forget: tales, gratitude and the ring of ocean gems were passed down. The text lists what the emblem commemorates: the tearing of the jewelled branches, the birth of pearls, the defeat of the vishaps in the depths to bring sunlight to the pale nation beneath the waves, and the young man who dreamed of standing atop Mt. Yougou, was named an "evil king", and fought his great duel with the tengu. The final lines promise that even if storm clouds gather and the violet lightning is perilous, the silver moon of Watatsumi will still pierce through.
### ULTRA +
The "single bolt of lightning" is linked on the wiki to Musou no Hitotachi, the Raiden Shogun's stroke; Orobashi's entry holds the story of the serpent who faced it. The young man who dreamed of Mt. Yougou is Akuou, a figure from Inazuma's past; his own story belongs to his entry. The text reads as Watatsumi's version of events, favourable to its own people and critical of the lightning that ended their dream.

## owners
### LITE
No wielder is named in canon. The weapon is tied to the Sangonomiya clan only by the emblem its text is built around.
### LARGE +
Sangonomiya Kokomi, the Divine Priestess of Watatsumi, is the most natural in-world fit and is commonly equipped with it in play, but nothing in the item text says she owns it; if a scene needs an owner, make that a scene decision.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not in-world): a 5-star catalyst released in Version 2.1 on the weapon event Wish. Base ATK 46 with HP% as secondary stat.
### LARGE +
Its passive, Byakuya Kougetsu, raises the wielder's Healing Bonus (10% at refinement 1, 20% at refinement 5) and adds Normal Attack damage equal to a percentage of the wielder's Max HP (1% at refinement 1, 3% at refinement 5). For 12 seconds after an Elemental Burst, Normal Attacks that hit opponents restore 0.6 Energy, at most once every 0.1 seconds. Refinement increases the values. Ascension uses the Distant Sea branch materials of Watatsumi, prism boss drops and the Spectral line of enemy drops.

## roleplay
### LARGE
For scenes: use it as a Watatsumi heirloom-style catalyst, an object that carries a people's memory. Characters from Watatsumi may recognise the emblem; others may see only an elegant pearl charm. Avoid asserting that it is Kokomi's own.

## entry: Pearl and Waves Emblem
@keys: pearl and waves, Sangonomiya emblem
@keys2: Watatsumi, Sangonomiya
### STANDARD
The "pearl and waves" emblem of the Sangonomiya clan is said to stand for the sea around Watatsumi and its pearls, though some say it shows the jewelled wheel of Watatsumi Omikami watching over the coral land like the moon. Everlasting Moonglow's text treats it as a symbol of memory that Watatsumi's people carry through loss.
