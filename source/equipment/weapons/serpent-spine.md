## summary
### LITE
Serpent Spine is a 4-star claymore from the Battle Pass Gnostic Hymn, in the game since Version 1.0. The in-game short description calls it a rare weapon from the ancient ocean whose swings carry the sound of ageless waves. The full text says it is a greatsword made from the spine of a great sea monster and tells of a singing sea captain who never returned from the deep.
### STANDARD +
The Skipper is the greatsword-wielding captain of the story, and the wiki notes that the book series Tales From the Waves covers the same character.

## appearance
### STANDARD
A greatsword made of a sea monster's spine, each eerie segment of bone described as a tale unto itself. Brilliant white bone is mentioned in the story of the beached monster. No hilt or other design details are given in text.

## lore
### LITE
In the stormy seas of the ancient past, those who set out often never returned. A greatsword-wielding captain, fond of drinking and merriment before voyages, sailed off on the day sea and wind aligned and neither he nor his ship came back.
### STANDARD +
The text says sailors once used the bones of sea monsters as figureheads to frighten the beasts of the deep sea, who envied land-dwellers. Carefree sea shanties of today were once farewell songs that left people silent. When questioned about his revelry the captain would laugh, say he knew himself, tell people not to worry and that once the sea and winds were right he would go, but never finish the thought, instead raising his mug.
### LARGE +
After many days a sea monster's corpse washed ashore, a great gash from a greatsword in it and its blood long washed clean. Words attributed to the skipper speak of avenging a woman charmed by the tidal song and of sending his shanties into the deep where she dwells even if he sleeps with the fishes.
### ULTRA +
The text closes in the present: giant creatures no longer rise from storms to ravage the waves, the custom of mounting monster skeletons on ships has been lost with their extinction, but far abroad one can still sometimes hear rumbling thunder in the depths. The Skipper's bond with his Chief Mate Parsifal is expanded in the Heart of Depth artifact set, and the Skipper appears in the Tales From the Waves book series, per the wiki's trivia.

## owners
### STANDARD
The previous owner is the Skipper, the greatsword-wielding captain of the item text. His relationship with his Chief Mate, Parsifal, is covered in the Heart of Depth artifact set.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not literal lore): 4-star claymore, base ATK 42, CRIT Rate secondary stat. Passive Wavesplitter: every 4s the wielder is on the field they deal 6 to 10 per cent more DMG and take 3 to 2 per cent more DMG (by refinement), up to 5 stacks. Stacks persist when leaving the field and drop by one when the wielder takes DMG. Obtained through the Battle Pass Gnostic Hymn bundle.
