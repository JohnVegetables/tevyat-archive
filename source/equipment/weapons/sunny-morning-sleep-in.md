## summary
### LITE
Sunny Morning Sleep-In is a five-star catalyst, a bell lantern forged from purple jade. The game description says that placed beside a pillow it brings sweet dreams. Its item story is a sad, quiet one about a shrine maiden who had the three auspicious "first dreams" of the new year before the Cataclysm, and whose dreams of bathhouses, hawks and the Narukami banner came to mean loss.
### STANDARD +
The story is told in fragments, each ending with a dream she would never have again, then closes on a warm bathhouse day that may have been only a lazy nap after the rain.

## appearance
### STANDARD
The in-game description calls it a bell lantern forged from purple jade. The text does not describe it further.

## lore
### STANDARD
The item story opens on a half-asleep shrine maiden explaining to her companions that she was late because she dreamed of a bathhouse and thought she had already reached the meeting place. In truth, on the first day of the new year she had simply overslept. A frivolous young man teases her and a young woman of Seirai glares at him; an apprentice who is the oni's foster son says nothing. The text explains the Inazuman saying "First, bathhouses, second, hawks, third, Narukami": the sights of the first dream of the year, standing for health and longevity, high achievement and wishes fulfilled.
### LARGE +
The story then moves through three losses. After a time of carefree years, bathhouse scenes never again appear in the maiden's dreams. In the second fragment a swordsman places his bow as a wager that he will return alive from the coming disaster; the text identifies this as the Thundering Pulse, and the maiden thinks him a hopeless gambler. The hawk of her dream, which she had taken as a sign that he would win, never returns either, leaving only drifting feathers. In the third, she dismisses omens as stories to fool children, a former friend having said that night's dreams reflect waking worries, until she dreams of the banner of Narukami rising over the sea like a storm of lightning. She laughs hollowly and, in the text's words, apologises to her master for squandering the art taught to her and for turning against the banner the master served, saying she cares nothing for her life or name so long as "he" lives on.
### ULTRA +
The final scene returns to the opening: the companion apologises for making light of the matter, and the maiden is urged not to stand daydreaming until the bathhouse closes. The text suggests that all the upheaval may have been a fleeting bathhouse dream and her awakening only a lazy nap after rain. The text never says how she regained a peaceful dream or whether the final scene is a dream, a memory or consolation. Read together with the Thundering Pulse text, the wiki's links identify the maiden as Asase Hibiki, the swordsman as Takamine the Mistsplitter, her master as Kitsune Saiguu, and the oni's foster son as Mikoshi Nagamasa. These identifications are the wiki's; the story itself leaves most of the figures unnamed.

## owners
### STANDARD
No owner is named in the item text. The wiki notes only the dreaming maiden and her companions as the story's characters.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not lore): a five-star catalyst, base ATK 44, secondary stat Elemental Mastery 58. Passive "Bathhouses, Hawks, and Narukami": Elemental Mastery increases by 120-240 for 6s after triggering Swirl, by 96-192 for 9s after the wielder's Elemental Skill hits an opponent, and by 32-64 for 30s after the Elemental Burst hits an opponent, at refinement 1-5. It was the event-exclusive weapon of the Version 5.4 Epitome Invocation (2025-02-12), alongside Silvershower Heartstrings. The three effects echo the three dreams in the saying.

## roleplay
### LARGE
Roleplay use (this project's suggestion, not canon text): it works as an object of wistful, gentle nostalgia, comfort offered by someone who has lost things. Keep the tone quiet; the story is sad but not melodramatic, and the maiden masks her feelings with indifference.

## entry: First dreams of the year
@keys: Bathhouses Hawks and Narukami, first dream of the year, hatsuyume
@keys2: Inazuma, saying, omen
@version: 5.4
### STANDARD
The Inazuman saying in the item story lists the good omens of a first dream of the new year: bathhouses for health and longevity, hawks for high achievements, and Narukami for wishes fulfilled. In the story each fades from the shrine maiden's dreams as the Cataclysm approaches and arrives.
