## summary
### LITE
Favonius Sword is a 4-star sword of the Favonius series, the standard-issue longsword of the Knights of Favonius. Its text credits Favonius Bladework to Rostam the Wolf Pup, childhood friend of Arundolyn, the Lion of Light, and says Rostam's skill has never been matched.
### STANDARD +
It is usually issued after approval of both the Master of the order and the Church. The Version 6.4 story quest for Varka features a Favonius Sword that speaks in Rostam's name.

## appearance
### STANDARD
A light, agile longsword of the Knights of Favonius. Jean is shown with the ascended version in most official artwork, her manga, her TCG card and her Collected Miscellany, per the wiki.

## lore
### LITE
Mondstadt's craftsmen studied Mondstadt's Anemo to reach elemental affinity, and the sword is both an honor to a knight and the fruit of the defenders' skill. The text warns that its sharp blade is for defending, not attacking.
### STANDARD +
Favonius Bladework began with Rostam, the childhood friend and spiritual counterpart of Arundolyn. The legend says he could cut every raindrop out of the sky and that the wind from his sword dance could split a rose and put out a torch. The style is light, swift and precise in the defense of Mondstadt.
### LARGE +
At twenty-seven, Rostam received the title of Wolf Pup. The Knights' tradition reserved the titles of Lion and Wolf for those with the leadership potential to become Grand Master someday. He served faithfully but did not live to see that day. No other practitioner has matched his legendary ability since, but his fervor and will to protect Mondstadt have stayed with the knights.
### ULTRA +
Version 6.4 detail: in Lonely Warrior, a part of Varka's Lupus Majoris Chapter, Varka comes upon a Favonius Sword and asks who left it there. The sword speaks, wishing aloud to be taken to see someone, and grants him a wind blessing called Rostam's Longing. The wiki labels the voice as Rostam's. Treat it as an echo of him in the setting, not as proof of a spirit.

## owners
### LITE
No owner is named in the text.
### LARGE +
Jean is the character most often shown with it, per the wiki; this is art, not a statement in the item text.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not in-world): 4-star sword, base ATK 41, secondary stat Energy Recharge 13.3%, obtainable from Wishes including the standard banner. Passive Windfall: CRIT hits have a chance (60% to 100% by refinement) to generate a small amount of Elemental Particles, restoring 6 Energy, once per 12 s at refinement 1 down to once per 6 s at refinement 5.

Windfall is the shared passive of the Favonius series (gameplay). It lets CRIT hits occasionally create Elemental Particles that restore Energy.

In the Version 6.4 Varka story quest Lonely Warrior, a Favonius Sword grants Varka a wind blessing named Rostam's Longing, which in the quest gives him a cooldown-reduction buff (gameplay) and later forms a shield against a gale. The wiki labels the sword's voice as Rostam.
