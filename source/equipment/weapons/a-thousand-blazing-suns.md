## summary
### LITE
A Thousand Blazing Suns is a 5-star claymore from Natlan, a greatsword that dazzles like the rising sun. Its story is a legend of the first human Pyro Archon, who took the Sacred Flame and chose to keep fighting the Abyss.
### STANDARD +
It belongs to the Pyro Archon weapon series. Its poem closes with a petition to the Lord of the Night to record the first sun and its Ancient Name.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Canon gives only the one-line description (a greatsword that dazzles like the rising sun). Keep descriptions to a radiant, sun-like claymore; do not invent engravings or materials.

## lore
### LITE
When the great dragon bowed its head, the leader of the mortals reached the primal flame, and the god who pronounced death showed him the Abyss devouring all life. He understood that a new war had begun.
### STANDARD +
The human Archon, whose domain was war, bartered with death for power and bent even the wisest of dragons to his plan, so that hope would burn on through generations.
### LARGE +
The dragon sage's foresight had named Natlan's future rulers as one like a manticore and one like a wild fox, but he had not expected to come to revere the wisdom and cunning of Xbalanque, the Pyro Archon. Xbalanque never meant to submit to the rules, nor to honour his oath to the one who stole the flame. Under the name "Haborym", life became the sacred flame that cannot be quenched, and the rules were crafted into a divine throne that always blazes. People would keep fighting with courage in the face of horror; short lives would burn out, but sunlight would disperse the dark again and again.

The first sun saw several figures in the primal flame and the ashes: the tainted one, defiled and devoured by the Abyss yet fighting to the last for humanity (the wiki links this to Och-Kan); the artisan whose eyes were dyed with golden tears and who, alongside the crimson-eyed youth, made sure the Sacred Flame would endure; and the lost tribe still wandering, whose warriors kept fighting for freedom (linked to the Muratans and to Vennessa). It also saw warm blood offered to the burning sparks that lit the long night, until a thousand suns had risen.
### ULTRA +
The closing lines are a petition to the Lord of the Night to chronicle the first sun, the first Ancient Name, "Kiongozi", followed by a pledge to leave hope for the future: so long as the flame does not die, there will still be hope. Kiongozi is glossed on the wiki as meaning "leader", which suits the poem's picture of the Archon as someone who leads by carrying the fire rather than by ruling. The poem reads as an origin story for the Natlanese Archon system: the flame is kept alive by mortal courage and sacrifice, and handed on.

## owners
### LITE
No wielder is named in the item text; association with any character is a gameplay convention.
### LARGE +
The text's central figure is Xbalanque, the first human Pyro Archon, and its themes also reach the later Archon Mavuika, who bears the same Ancient Name according to the wiki. Treat any scene in which a character holds "a Thousand Blazing Suns" as an invention of that scene; canon gives no in-world account of the blade's history.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not in-world): a 5-star claymore, base ATK 49 at level 1, secondary stat CRIT Rate; Weapon Event Wishes; introduced in Version 5.3.
### LARGE +
Passive "Sunset Reignites the Dawn": using an Elemental Skill or Burst grants "Scorching Brilliance" (CRIT DMG and ATK bonuses for 6s, once per 10s, scaling with refinement from 20% and 28% to 40% and 56%). While it is active, Normal or Charged Attacks that deal Elemental DMG extend it by 2s, up to a 6s total extension. In Nightsoul's Blessing the effects are increased by 75% and the timer does not run while the character is off-field. Ascension uses the Blazing Sacrificial Heart materials, the Secret Source boss materials and Fang common materials.

## roleplay
### LARGE
For scenes: treat the weapon as a Natlanese relic whose story is better known as a legend than as a documented object. A speaker could quote its themes of hope, sacrifice and rekindling, but should not claim to know the exact fate of the original blade.

### STANDARD
Sunset Reignites the Dawn is the passive of the claymore A Thousand Blazing Suns (gameplay): it grants a short CRIT DMG and ATK boost after using a Skill or Burst, amplified in Nightsoul's Blessing. Its name echoes the text's image of a sun that sets and is rekindled.
