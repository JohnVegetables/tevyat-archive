## summary
### LITE
Festering Desire is a creepy straight sword that almost seems to yearn for life, dripping with a shrivelling venom that could corrupt even a mighty dragon. Its blade was coated with powder made of the dragon Durin's remains. The Traveler received it as a memento from Albedo after the 1.2 event The Chalk Prince and the Dragon, once the corruption had been drawn out.

## appearance
### STANDARD
The in-game description calls it a creepy straight sword that seems to yearn for life and drips with a shrivelling venom. No other visual detail is given in text.

## lore
### STANDARD
The item text recalls a memory of the dragon Durin, as a child of Rhinedottir. Rhinedottir's words speak of unborn life and unfulfilled wishes at the edge of the universal darkness, asking them to dwell in her body and descend into the world, then go to a lovely place and show their own beauty. Durin answers: "Thank you, Mother, thank you." He says she gave him wings and a mighty form, and that he wishes to go to a land of lovely songs and tell them the place he was born is beautiful.
### LARGE +
Albedo explains the sword's history in the event quest Festering Desire. A blacksmith made it and vanished soon after. The blade was lost to time and ended up with Treasure Hoarders, from whom the Knights of Favonius recovered it with other stolen goods. As nobody claimed it, Albedo studied it and found that the blade had been coated with a powder of Durin's remains, especially his eyes, claws and scales. That gave it Durin's power and also his corruption, so ordinary mortals could not use it, since the corruption might seep through the handle into the wielder.
### ULTRA +
Albedo considers the sword almost a living being and wonders if the curse was why its maker disappeared. The Traveler was unaffected because they can purify abyssal corruption, as they had done with Dvalin's tears, so Albedo gave them the sword to experiment on. The Traveler purified it over time in the story ending with Raiders Amidst Snow Mist, though some of its power was accidentally transferred into the Resurgent Cryo Regisvine in Uninvited Guests. In the Afterword Albedo extracted what remained of the corruption into a bottle and gave the now safe sword to the Traveler as a keepsake of their research.

## owners
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
No lasting owner except the Traveler, who receives it from Albedo as a memento. Before that it passed from its unnamed maker to Treasure Hoarders and then into Knights of Favonius custody.

## gameplay
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not lore): four-star sword obtained from the event The Chalk Prince and the Dragon. Base ATK 42, secondary stat Energy Recharge 10%. Passive "Undying Admiration": raises Elemental Skill DMG and Elemental Skill CRIT Rate (16-32% and 6-12% at refinement 1-5). During the event it also gave Dragonspine boosts.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay use (this project's suggestion, not canon text): the sword suits scenes about inherited harm and the line between study and obsession. Albedo handles it with clinical care; do not have ordinary mortals wield it uncorrupted. By 7.1 canon the corruption in the sword itself is gone.
