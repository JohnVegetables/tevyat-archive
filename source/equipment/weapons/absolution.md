## summary
### LITE
Absolution is a 5-star rapier once wielded by a deceiver who had forsaken everything. In the past it served mainly as a tool when he had to disguise his identity.
### STANDARD +
The weapon story belongs to Thompson Reed, a man from the Fleuve Cendre undercity who rose by lying and ended by betraying the people he had joined.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Canon calls it a rapier and says nothing further about its looks. When held by a character with an Arkhe alignment it carries a Pneuma or Ousia mark. Do not add guard or blade details.

## lore
### LITE
Thompson Reed is a young man from the Fleuve Cendre who took his mother's lesson of sincerity and diligence as a lie, after learning she lay buried there. He decided that lying and surviving were what mattered, and used costumes and masks to climb.
### STANDARD +
He meets a noble girl, Leticia, who has wandered into the Fleuve Cendre in disguise, and spots her at once because her clothes bear no soot, oil or blood. He sees her as a way into House Randolph. The text then follows him through a period in which he courts her and speaks of a world with more sunlight, no segregation and no deception, a place where people could build a home with nothing to trouble them.
### LARGE +
Later he asks her to think of their children and says they should not be born amid cannon fire and filthy waters, and calls their comrades' goals confused dreams of unknown outlook. The narration reveals that he has already arranged his betrayal and feels a strange foreboding about it. By then he no longer walked among nobles; asked why he betrayed his comrades, he could not recall whether the pretext was a higher position or winning peace for her and the children. Per the wiki's Thompson Reed page, he fed the Marechaussee Hunters secret paths into Poisson, where the two had joined Eduardo Baker's resistance.
### ULTRA +
The last scene has his wife ask him that question once more; he still cannot answer, but remembers his promise to her, that the two of them at least could keep their initial contract. The passive's name, Deathly Pact, echoes this. The text also generalises: in any age there will be young people who grow up too fast, rise under the banner of survival of the fittest, and become monsters of the city above ground, for whom both cities, with sunlight or without, are hunting grounds.

## owners
### LITE
Thompson Reed, per the description. No present-day owner is named.
### LARGE +
Clorinde is the character it is released with (see uncertainties), but canon never says she holds this rapier.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not in-world): a 5-star sword, base ATK 48 at level 1, secondary stat CRIT DMG; Weapon Event Wishes; introduced in Version 4.7. Passive "Deathly Pact": raises CRIT DMG, and whenever the value of a Bond of Life increases the wielder's DMG rises for 6s (up to 3 stacks). Values scale with refinement.

## roleplay
### LARGE
For scenes: a character who knows the story speaks of it as the weapon of a liar who betrayed Poisson; most Fontainians would not know the sword by name. Keep Thompson's self-deceit ambiguous, as the text does.

## entry: Thompson Reed
@keys: Thompson Reed, Absolution

### STANDARD
Thompson Reed is a historical figure from the Fleuve Cendre who appears in the story of the weapon Absolution as a self-serving liar who joined Eduardo Baker's resistance and then betrayed it, with Leticia as his wife. Not enough is known to state what became of him.
