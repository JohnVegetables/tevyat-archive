## summary
### LITE
Wolf's Gravestone is a 5-star claymore and, per the wiki, the sword of the first Knight of Boreas, also called the Wandering Knight. Its text tells of a knight and the wolf Boreas who shared one spirit: when the wolf died the knight set the sword over its grave, left Mondstadt and lived as a lone wanderer. Added in Version 1.0.
### STANDARD +
Item text calls the sword a "longsword" and "a heavy sheet of iron" that became legendary through the knight's friendship with the wolves; in game it is a claymore.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Canon text describes it only as a heavy sheet of iron that a blacksmith from the city gave the knight. Its silhouette appears at the start location of the Wolf of the North Challenge.

## lore
### LITE
The Knight of Boreas's journey ended at the city of Barbatos. As he entered it, his wolf companion said farewell without a sound, since the free-spirited wolf disliked stone walls and lamplight and longed for the wilds.
### STANDARD +
While the Knight's body was in the city his heart stayed free, and the windborne wolf, roaming the forest, stayed loyal to him. When the Knight rode out to hunt again, the wolf followed his scent into battle; the text calls them one. But death comes sooner to beasts than men.
### LARGE +
The Knight buried the wolf in the remote countryside (Wolvendom, per the wiki link), laying his sword down as its gravestone, and left the city for the life of a lone wolf. The wolf's eternal spirit stayed behind, watching over the land the Knight once protected.
### ULTRA +
Story context from other canon: the wiki identifies the Knight as the first Knight of Boreas, who lived during Vennessa's rebellion about a thousand years ago and refused the Knights of Favonius's attempts to recruit him; Ravenwood was later approved by Boreas as the next Knight of Boreas. The wolf "Boreas" is the Wolf of the North, known as Andrius, whose spirit watches over Wolvendom. The Wolf-Fang item text calls Boreas the Knight's companion.

## owners
### STANDARD
Owner: the Wandering Knight, the first Knight of Boreas (per the wiki, from the Lonely Warrior quest and the weapon texts). The wiki notes that Diluc is shown with the ascended claymore in his Red Dead of Night outfit art and that Varka holds it in the Song of the Welkin Moon teaser; these are promotional appearances, not statements that either owns the relic.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not literal lore): a 5-star claymore with base ATK 46 and an ATK% secondary stat. Its passive, Wolfish Tracker, raises ATK, and hitting an opponent under 30% HP gives all party members an ATK bonus for 12s (once per 30s). It is on the Standard Wish and weapon event Wishes.

### STANDARD
The Knight of Boreas is the title of the Knights of Favonius' wolf-bonded warrior. The first holder, the unnamed Wandering Knight, wielded Wolf's Gravestone and left Mondstadt after his wolf companion died; the title was carried on by others, with Ravenwood next. Wolf-Fang's text says no record of the first knight survives and that his name comes only from street stories.
