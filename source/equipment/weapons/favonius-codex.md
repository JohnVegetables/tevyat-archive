## summary
### LITE
Favonius Codex is a 4-star catalyst, a secret tome that belonged to the scholars of the Knights of Favonius. It records the logic and power of elements and matter and is inlaid with crystallized elements. Every copy is hand-made, and its text is partly about the order's great library.
### STANDARD +
It is the one non-standard-issue weapon of the Favonius series, which otherwise holds the knights' swords, spear and bow.

## appearance
### STANDARD
A tome set with crystallized elements. The wiki notes that the Teyvat script on the inside reads "Zephyrus", the Greek counterpart of the Roman Favonius.

## lore
### LITE
The book holds the collective knowledge of the Knights' scholars. Its crystals are precious both in themselves and because each codex is made by hand; the text says only scholars who truly understand the workings of the elements can make such crystals.
### STANDARD +
When the Knights of Favonius were founded, the Dawn Knight Ragnvindr turned the aristocrats' indoor baths into a library. Contributions from bards, scholars and travelers made it the largest collection of writing in the northern half of the continent. The text reasons that songs are carried off by the wind and wine's pleasure is brief, but what is written down may shine for ever.
### LARGE +
The collection is now a fraction of its peak size (the Chinese text gives one sixth) because the library once burned in the "Great Fire of Fall Equinox". In the library basement stands a sturdy poplar-wood door said to predate the library and to have withstood the smoke and flames. The text adds that the door stood there long before the Knights of Favonius existed. The Knights officially say the restricted section lies behind it, but tales hint at something more profound hidden within.
### ULTRA +
The wiki links the hidden secret to the Imaginarium Theater, but the text itself stops at the hint. In a scene, keep the poplar door as rumor and the Knights' official line, and do not decide what is behind it.

## owners
### LITE
No owner is named. The text says it belonged to the scholars of the Knights of Favonius.
### LARGE +
The wiki notes the ascended codex is seen with Sucrose in her character card, Mona in her demo and Lisa in a 4-panel comic; this is art, not an ownership claim.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not in-world): 4-star catalyst, base ATK 42, secondary stat Energy Recharge 10.0%, obtainable from Wishes including the standard banner. Passive Windfall: CRIT hits have a chance (60% to 100% by refinement) to generate a small amount of Elemental Particles, restoring 6 Energy, once per 12 s at refinement 1 down to once per 6 s at refinement 5.
### LARGE +
The passive needs a CRIT hit that deals damage, and in co-op the particles go to all team members.

## entry: Favonius library

### LARGE
The Knights of Favonius' library was begun by Ragnvindr in the former aristocrats' baths, grew into the largest collection in the northern half of the continent, and was later reduced by a fire called the Great Fire of Fall Equinox. A poplar-wood door older than the Knights stands in its basement.
