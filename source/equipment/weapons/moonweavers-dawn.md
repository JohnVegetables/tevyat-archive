## summary
### LITE
An immaculate silver sword that slices armour like moonlight through an empty night; legend says it was blessed by the first Moonchanter, Aila. A 4-star sword of the Frostmoon Scion series from Version 6.0.
### STANDARD +
Its text shows Aila obeying, then ending, the last Archpriestess.

## appearance
### STANDARD
An immaculate, sharp silver sword; canon text gives no hilt or decorative detail.

## lore
### LITE
A guardian addresses her ward Aila as her heart's child and tells her the pure silver blood of their line alone is worthy of the Frost Moon's grace.
### STANDARD +
Aila is told she will bear the heir to Elysium and must not wed an outlander's son. The text says Lovia, the Archpriestess of the far north, repeated blasphemous deeds, and Aila obediently carried out her commands, including severing silver threads of fate with the blade and silencing singing voices, to purify the holy blood.
### LARGE +
Lovia then orders Aila to guide Solovei, a man she calls a treacherous murderer who dares seek the Scions' protection, to the sanctuary and to thrust the sword through his heart from behind. Lovia reads Aila's years of silence as consent and never doubts that she resents none of it.
### ULTRA +
The blade instead pierces Lovia's back. Aila's last words are that Mr. Solovei was right and that Lovia was lying: if not, why would she bleed dawn-light crimson rather than moonlit silver when her thread was cut? The scene is set before the pitch-black calamity swept the borderlands. The text keeps Aila's thoughts private and never says what Lovia was lying about.

## owners
### LITE
No wielder is named; the blade is attributed to Aila's blessing.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not in-world): 4-star sword, base ATK 44, ATK% secondary. Passive Secret Silver's Testament increases Elemental Burst DMG, with an extra bonus when the wielder's Energy Capacity is low (60 or 40 or less, depending on the tier of the effect). One copy is awarded by the quest Echoes of an Unfinished Past.

## roleplay
### LARGE
Present as a sacred, ominous blade whose legend is Scion-flavoured. Do not treat the stabbing scene as universally known; it is the text's depiction, and other weapon texts tell a different Aila-Lovia story.
