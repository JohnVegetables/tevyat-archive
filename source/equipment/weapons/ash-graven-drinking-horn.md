## summary
### LITE
The Ash-Graven Drinking Horn is a 4-star catalyst from Natlan: a ritual drinking vessel handed down among the Meztli, said to have been the treasure of the giant hero Tupac. Its story is a banquet song about him.
### STANDARD +
It belongs to the Meztli series and was given out as an event reward in Version 5.0. The song ends with a moral: unity and promises must never be forsaken.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Canon gives only the one-line description: a ritual vessel, a drinking horn. Keep descriptions to a drinking horn; do not invent carvings, inscriptions or colours.

## lore
### LITE
A minstrel sings of the ancient ancestor Tupac, huge as a mountain. The young hero Tenoch, furious, would have challenged him for the horn; Tupac, laughing, would have answered that it rightfully belonged to him and his homeland's kin.
### STANDARD +
The song adds tales of Tupac's strength: in a fit of rage he broke mountains with his bare hands and drove rocks into the earth so that hot springs welled up (the singer says those springs in the southwest are still visible), and he once sailed alone to fight a two-tailed whale and tore out its teeth. Years later, remembering the old contest with Tenoch over the horn, he offers the horn before the lord of the blazing flames, the Pyro Archon.
### LARGE +
The singer frames Tupac's gift as his last hope and his way of holding the flame high until his final breath, keeping his oath to the end. The performance ends with a warning to the young: the years of darkness have passed and there is again singing above the springs and feasting at dusk, but the new generation must not forget that unity and promises are the two things never to be abandoned. The singer says the song came from a father who sang it in a golden sea breeze and a mother who taught it in childhood.
### ULTRA +
The text is a layered retelling: a modern minstrel recites an ancestor's tale, conjuring a past quarrel between Tupac and Tenoch (who is called young, heroic and proud) in the conditional mood. It also has Tenoch ask why Tupac crossed the sea and say that Tupac's ancestor Uenuku would not have been so audacious, which frames the horn as a point of pride between two heroes. Read it alongside Tupac's other item story, Waveriding Whirl, which shows his restless sea voyage and the Lord of the Night's counsel.

## owners
### LITE
Legend ties it to Tupac, the giant of the Meztli, who never used it. Later holders are not named.
### LARGE +
The text says the horn was valued but unused by Tupac, contested by Tenoch in a story set decades before the song's telling, and finally offered by Tupac to the Pyro Archon. Any scene showing a character holding "the" horn should treat it as a relic or replica of a legend; canon records no continuous chain of ownership.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not in-world): a 4-star catalyst of the Meztli series, base ATK 42 at level 1, secondary stat HP%. Obtained from the Version 5.0 event Traces of Artistry; its refinement material, The Sea's Silent Shade, also came from that event.
### LARGE +
Passive "Tupac's Grip": when an attack hits an opponent, deals AoE DMG at the target location equal to a percentage of the wielder's Max HP (40% at refinement 1 up to 80% at refinement 5), once every 15s.

## roleplay
### LARGE
Natlanese characters may know the song as a tavern or hearth tale of Tupac, shared at banquets near the springs. Treat the exchange with Tenoch as a story told with relish, not documented fact, and keep its moral (unity and promises) in the tone of a storyteller.

### STANDARD
Tupac's Grip is the passive of the catalyst Ash-Graven Drinking Horn (gameplay): an attack that hits an opponent triggers an AoE blow scaled to the wielder's Max HP, on a 15 second cooldown. The name nods to Tupac holding the horn tightly in the song.
