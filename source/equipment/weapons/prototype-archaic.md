## summary
### LITE
Prototype Archaic is a forgeable 4-star claymore of the Prototype series. Its description calls it an ancient greatsword discovered in Blackcliff Forge that swings with such force it seems to cut through reality. It has been in the game since Version 1.0.
### STANDARD +
The item text credits the master craftsman Han Wu and the Yun family forge with building it as the first greatsword of a new line of designs called the prototypes.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Understated: dull, blotchy gold that hid its true strength. Its hilt is made from a rare ore and the blade from fine steel, and it weighs close to forty pounds according to the text. Swung, it shows blinding flashes of light.

## lore
### LITE
It is an ancient prototype greatsword from Liyue's arsenal. The batch number has faded and no records confirm the date of production.
### STANDARD +
After the great disaster, the Yun family forge joined forces with Han Wu to push past the limits of contemporary blade design. The result was a new series of weapon models simply called the prototypes, and this greatsword was the first built to the new design. It was weighty but not unwieldy.
### LARGE +
Han Wu travelled north through the night to hand it to a martial artist who followed the chivalric code. In a trial swing the blade howled like wind and levelled a whole bamboo forest. The martial artist praised its antique elegance, worth as both weapon and ornament, and named it Archaic.
### ULTRA +
Because of its strength, the sword became the model for thousands of greatswords in Liyue. In the Chinese text the martial artist names it Guhua ('ancient flower'), which is why its Chinese in-game name is Prototype Guhua; the wiki suggests the unnamed martial artist may be Guhua, but this is unconfirmed.

## owners
### STANDARD
Han Wu is its maker. The martial artist who named it is unnamed in the text.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not literal lore): 4-star claymore, base ATK 44, ATK% secondary stat (6 per cent). Passive Crush: Normal or Charged Attacks have a 50 per cent chance on hit to deal extra AoE Physical damage of 240 to 480 per cent ATK (by refinement), at most once every 15s. It is obtained by forging (Northlander Claymore Billet, 50 Crystal Chunk, 50 White Iron Chunk, 500 Mora). It is the claymore of the Prototype series, whose other weapons include Prototype Rancour (sword), Prototype Crescent (bow), Prototype Starglitter (polearm) and Prototype Amber (catalyst).
