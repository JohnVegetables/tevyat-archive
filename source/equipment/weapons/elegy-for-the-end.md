## summary
### LITE
Elegy for the End is a five-star bow released in version 1.4, one of the Millennial Movement weapons, each of which carries a song of Mondstadt's history. Its story tells of the Cataclysm, when a venomous dragon crushed the land, and of a dour knight who could be made to forget his duty only by a girl's song. He fell in the battle; she came home to find the song changed.

## appearance
### STANDARD
The item text describes no particular look for the bow beyond comparing it to a bard's lyre: "a bow as lovely as any bard's lyre", whose arrows pierce the heart like a lamenting sigh. Its in-game passive effect is shown as a lyre string lighting up.

## lore
### STANDARD
The story opens with a verse about the wind carrying wine's fragrance, news from the mountains and longing. Long ago there was a knight (Rostam) who was always grave, and only one song and one girl who sang it in the square could make him forget his burden. Then the Cataclysm came, and a venomous dragon, Durin, drowned the songs and ripped apart the cries and flames. The Anemo Archon, though he had refused to rule, heard the agony; to protect his old friends' dream he woke and flew into battle beside the sky dragon, Dvalin. The knight and his knights fought as well.
### LARGE +
When the venomous dragon fell on the ice-sealed peak (Dragonspine), the blue sky dragon fell asleep in the ancient tower (Stormterror's Lair). The knight lay spent in the valley, and as he fell he thought that she was studying abroad and so must be safe, and that he wished to hear her sing again; he also took comfort that Arundolyn and Roland were all right, believing the calamity would blow over before she returned.
### ULTRA +
Many songs praised the Archon and recorded the battle of the two dragons, but they were slowly lost. The girl's own song changed on her return: where it once spoke of summer wind, grain and the wind that fetched her love's gaze, it now ends with "no wind in any season" bringing him back. After her voice and tears ran dry she swore to use her life's flame to cleanse the world's crookedness. The Millennial Movement series page summarises this as the girl devoting her life to burning away the world's evil. Which historical girl this is rests on the wiki's link to Signora rather than a printed name.

## owners
### STANDARD
No canon owner is named. The story concerns Rostam and the girl but does not say the bow was theirs. The bow is a song-weapon in the series sense, not an inherited relic.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not lore): a five-star bow from weapon event wishes. Base ATK 46, secondary stat Energy Recharge 12%. Passive "The Parting Refrain": raises Elemental Mastery; Elemental Skill or Burst hits give a Sigil of Remembrance, and at four Sigils all are consumed and nearby party members get "Millennial Movement: Farewell Song" for 12 seconds (more Elemental Mastery and ATK), then a 20-second lockout. Buffs of the same Millennial Movement type do not stack.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay use (this project's suggestion, not canon text): suited to scenes about parting, songs that outlive their singers and the cost of the Cataclysm. Do not state the girl is Signora to a character who has no way to know it.

## entry: Millennial Movement series
@keys: Millennial Movement
@keys2: Sigil, Farewell Song, Song of Resistance, series
@version: 1.4
### LARGE
The Millennial Movement series is the wiki's (unofficial) name for five-star Mondstadt weapons built around songs of the country's history: Freedom-Sworn (the rebellion against Decarabian), Song of Broken Pines (the fight against the corrupt nobility) and Elegy for the End (the Cataclysm). Each grants party-wide effects after collecting Sigils, and same-type effects do not stack.
