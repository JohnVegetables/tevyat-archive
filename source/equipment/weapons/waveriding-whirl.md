## summary
### LITE
Waveriding Whirl is a 4-star catalyst from Natlan: a boomerang made from a giant sea serpent's fangs. It was a rare weapon that the giant Tupac called "trusty".
### STANDARD +
Its story follows Tupac, a Meztli giant with no equal on land, as he sails out to sea in search of a worthy feat.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Canon: a boomerang made from the fangs of a giant sea serpent. Do not invent size, colour or markings.

## lore
### LITE
Tupac of the Meztli towered like a mountain, with a sandstone-like body and a burning heart whose steam nearly hid the sun. In an age of peace, when even the ancient shadow-foe was silent, he was sullen, longing to have been born in the age of Xbalanque's dragon-slaying or among those who fought the Python King.
### STANDARD +
With no equal on land, he walks into the sea to find the bones of a titanic beast or treasures from times past. A winged dragon exiled by an old king gives him wings for sails; the bones of a whale that once drove back waves become his white boat; the fang of a voracious sea python becomes his weapon, and its flesh a meal to drink with.
### LARGE +
At the sea's edge a voice tells him to rest his arrogant waves, that he is sullen because the deathly path and dark gate have not yet revealed themselves, and that he must harden his flesh and bone like iron and a golden mace, keeping his true heart and his oath until the day the exile, once his foe, returns.
### ULTRA +
The final stanza: when the time came, Tupac understood his duty, carried the master of flames to the place where fire belonged with the Lord of the Night's admonishment in his ears, and saw death's door appear as he had once sworn to the deity. The text ties him to Natlan's heroes of legend. Compare the Ash-Graven Drinking Horn, whose song also has Tupac keeping an oath to the end.

## owners
### LITE
Tupac, the giant of the Meztli, by the text.
### LARGE +
The weapon is described as a boomerang from a fang; any in-world holder today would be an invention of the scene.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not in-world): a 4-star catalyst of the Heroes of Natlan series, base ATK 41 at level 1, secondary stat Energy Recharge. Weapon Event Wishes from Version 5.2.
### LARGE +
Passive "Fangs Flying To and Fro": decreases Swimming Stamina consumption by 15%; for 10s after an Elemental Skill, Max HP rises (20% at refinement 1 up to 40% at refinement 5) and rises further for each Hydro character in the party (12% to 24% each, capped at 24% to 48%), usable once every 15s.

## roleplay
### LARGE
Meztli characters may tell Tupac's voyage as a hearth tale of a hero who outgrew his age. Keep it a story, not a document.
