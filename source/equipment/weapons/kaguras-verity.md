## summary
### LITE
Kagura's Verity is the set of bells used for the Kagura Dance, blessed by Yae Miko, the Guuji of the Grand Narukami Shrine, and still carrying the scent of the Sacred Sakura. Its item text is Yae's voice, looking back on her long friendship with Ei and the people and spirits she has met across her life.
### STANDARD +
The Kagura bells are a short staff with tiered bells used in Shinto kagura dance. In-universe, the text says that where there were once dances before the throne, the sound of the bells still lingers.

## appearance
### STANDARD
A staff of tiered bells in the style of a kagura suzu, with the bells glowing in turn as the weapon's passive stacks up (top bells at one stack, top and bottom at two, the full weapon and a particle effect at three). The description adds that the scent of the Sacred Sakura clings to it.

## lore
### LITE
The text opens with the white silhouette of the past, which never returned and remains an unceasing dream. In Yae's recollection she was a silly little thing, nowhere near as clever as the great matriarch Hakushin. As a whelp foraging through snow she tried to catch Ei's attention; her foolhardy courage earned Ei's pity and she entered her service, warming her hands and feet, a "very great honor".
### STANDARD +
When Saiguu departed never to return and her seniors were lost for various reasons, the clumsy Yae took up the role of Miko. The unfortunate task of putting a smile on Ei's face fell to her. On the night she first performed the Kagura Dance she felt the weight of the past. The text then says the great silver kitsune, master and friend, vanished into the river of dreams; the daughter of the great kitsune lineage would take up the bells and dance for the vibrant "now".
### LARGE +
Yae recalls her dealings with others: she bluffed a young tengu warrior (Kujou Sara) into mountain asceticism and recommended her to the Kujou clan; she lost a contest to an obstinate oni (Arataki Itto) through his sheer perseverance, after adding tricks to make it interesting; she corresponded with a half-adeptus from Liyue (Ganyu) and gifted her fresh ocean plants, though she cannot understand her "almost foolish love", asking whether it is a bondage for adepti. She revisits the moonlit imperial garden scene that the Hakushin Ring also recalls.
### ULTRA +
Her reflections on the era: in a few hundred years she has walked the earth in many guises and come to know human beauty deeply. With Ei engrossed in her dream of Eternity, someone had to look after the common folk. Yae lists the incidents she handled: quelling the resentment of the wicked oni Kuroami with a hint of her ominous power, making sport of the disturbance caused by Kosanta the Bald Tanuki, betraying the marauding pirate Rinzou over a small discordance in plans, and hoping the "blank slate" immortal eccentric Scaramouche would find his way and not threaten them all. The darkened remains of the swordmaster's soul and the beasts of catastrophe hiding in the sacred forest were wiped out. Compared to Ei's dream, these were fleeting interludes, and Yae knew time was on her side. She ends with a mock-serious refusal of a Euthymia without wind or moon, with unwithering lotuses and udumbara: the loneliness would bore her to tears. She would rather snap Thunder Sakura branches in a drunken haze and cavort with impudent youkai, and asks whether she might join Ei to watch the first pale purple buds when the snow next melts.

## owners
### LITE
Yae Miko is the blesser and voice of the text. The bells are tied to the Guuji's duties at the Grand Narukami Shrine.
### LARGE +
The text says the bells were blessed by Yae, not that she carries these particular ones; avoid assuming that any item copy belongs to a particular shrine maiden.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not in-world): a 5-star catalyst in the Inazuma series released in Version 2.5 on the weapon event Wish. Base ATK 46 with CRIT DMG as secondary stat.
### LARGE +
Its passive, Kagura Dance of the Sacred Sakura, grants a stacking Elemental Skill DMG bonus (12% per stack at refinement 1, 24% at refinement 5, up to 3 stacks) for 24 seconds after the wielder uses an Elemental Skill; at 3 stacks the wielder also gains an all-element DMG Bonus of the same size. Gaining a stack refreshes the duration of all stacks. It has since also been offered in Chronicled Wishes and Lightrace Wishes.

## roleplay
### LARGE
For scenes: the text is the perfect register for Yae: playful, wry, and quietly devoted to Ei. A character handling the bells may find a lingering scent of sakura. Do not have the bells reveal Ei's secrets; Yae's lines here mock-complain more than they confess.

### LARGE
The Kagura Dance is the shrine dance for which Yae Miko's bells are used. Her item text says she first performed it on a night when she felt the weight of the past and the loss of Saiguu, taking up the bells to dance for the present. It is also the name of the weapon's passive in game.
