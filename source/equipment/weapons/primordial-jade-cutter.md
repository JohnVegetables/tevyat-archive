## summary
### LITE
Primordial Jade Cutter is a five-star sword of Liyue, a ceremonial blade masterfully carved from pure jade. Item text says that when Liyue first arose, Rex Lapis once walked the land with a sword hewn from jade. It was cut as a costly gift for peace and luxury, then bathed in blood in war. It is a legend rather than a quest object: no scene shows the sword.
### STANDARD +
It belongs to the Primordial Jade weapon series, the Liyue weapons tied to the Archon War era. The wiki's reading of the series is that the sword is the weapon Morax wielded after the gift never reached its recipient.

## appearance
### LARGE
The in-game description calls it a ceremonial sword carved from pure jade, so polished that it keeps a sheen as if new despite age and blood, and says there seems to be an audible sigh in the wind when it is swung. Item text gives no other detail (no hilt, length or inscriptions), so avoid inventing any.

## lore
### STANDARD
The item story is a lament. The jade edge, soaked in blood long ago, still looks new; rain washed the blood away over a thousand years, but the thoughts and grudges gathered around it linger. A friend, now long forgotten, once said that nephrite has the soul of the Bishui River's gentle heart and will in time cleanse itself of old grudges, and then asked who would ease the agony the jade feels at having become an instrument of slaughter.
### LARGE +
The text widens into a statement about the Archon War: over many years people who once feasted together became mortal foes, while those who betrayed or fought to the death later shared a drink with their hatred gone. The sword was carved as an expensive gift for a certain someone, cut from jade out of love of peace and luxury. When wine vessels fill with blood and tender feelings are torn apart by cold ambition, the story ends, gifts ungiven and bonds unspoken become sharp blades with which to cleave former friends. The text never says who gave or received the gift.
### ULTRA +
The wiki's Primordial Jade Series page ties the sword to Morax, who used it in the Archon War after the intended gift never came to pass. That is interpretation layered on the text, which says only that "Rex Lapis" once walked with a jade sword. In Chinese the weapon is named after a famous jade, Jielü, from the State of Song; the English translation drops that allusion. The jade connection to the Bishui River is the text's own.

## owners
### STANDARD
Item text names no owner beyond the legendary Rex Lapis (Morax, the Geo Archon, who as Zhongli appears in the game). It does not say the sword ever left his hands, and no playable character is attached to it by canon.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not lore): a five-star sword from weapon event wishes. Base ATK 44, secondary stat CRIT Rate 9.6%. Passive "Protector's Virtue": HP rises 20-40% and the wielder gains ATK equal to 1.2-2.4% of Max HP, at refinements 1 to 5. The bonus does not apply if the wielder has fallen or is not in the party.

## roleplay
### LARGE
Roleplay use (this project's suggestion, not canon text): the sword suits themes of a gift turned weapon, grudges outliving the people who held them and gentleness forced into violence. Treat it as a legend that Liyue scholars or antique dealers might repeat, not as a relic that anyone can show.

## entry: Primordial Jade series
@keys: Primordial Jade Series, Primordial Jade weapons
@keys2: Cutter, Winged-Spear, Jadefall's Splendor
@spoiler: none
### LARGE
The Primordial Jade series is an unofficial grouping of five-star weapons made of jade with a green look. The wiki notes the first two released, Primordial Jade Cutter and Primordial Jade Winged-Spear, retell weapons or objects Morax made from jade for the Archon War, and that Jadefall's Splendor is a later addition with a different focus. The series has no shared passive.
