## summary
### LITE
Sword of Descension is a 4-star sword in the game since Version 1.0 and given by mail to players who start on PS4 or PS5. Its description says it does not appear to belong to this world, and the item text calls it proof that the Traveler came to Teyvat by special means.
### STANDARD +
It can be wielded only by one who has challenged the "world", and it is addressed in second person to the Traveler as a weapon forged for one who challenges and pursues.

## appearance
### STANDARD
A sword of unique craftsmanship. The wiki notes that the PlayStation triangle, circle, square and cross symbols appear on the handle, a real-world platform reference rather than an in-world detail.

## lore
### LITE
The item text says only one who has challenged the "world" may wield such a sword, and that the Traveler wields it while searching for the answers hidden in this world.
### STANDARD +
It recalls the last time the sword was drawn: humankind tried to preserve a doomed world that was their last and only home, drawing steel against the law that all who exist must one day perish. Against the star-devouring darkness, the text says, the only fitting weapon is a sword, bearing the memory of those who faced their ends without fear.
### LARGE +
In this world, the text adds, such a weapon can be used without trouble, not because the universe casts no shadows here nor because it suits the world's aesthetics, but because the search is perhaps why the Traveler descended to this realm. It ends by urging the wielder to take it, walk the earth and cut open the challenges and mysteries it offers.
### ULTRA +
The wiki links the "one who challenged the world" to the Descender motif and the darkness to the Abyss. Treat these as the wiki's cross-references to the item text's evocative framing rather than a plotted story: the text names neither the doomed world nor the sword's earlier wielders, so do not assign it to any specific character.

## owners
### STANDARD
Addressed to the Traveler only. The text says only one who has challenged the "world" may wield it.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not literal lore): 4-star sword, base ATK 39, ATK secondary stat, fixed at refinement 1 with one copy given. Its Descension passive is effective only on PlayStation: Normal and Charged Attack hits have a 50% chance to deal 200% ATK as DMG in a small area (once every 10s), and if the Traveler equips it their ATK rises by 66. Players on other platforms can obtain the weapon through cross-save, but the effect applies only when playing on PlayStation.
