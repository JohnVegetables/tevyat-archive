## summary
### LITE
Tulaytullah's Remembrance is a 5-star catalyst: a bell of deep sapphire and sterling silver whose echoes are "as crisp as they are distant". Its story recalls the Sapphire City Tulaytullah, home of the Tighnarians, and the farewell of the court dancer Makhaira to her golden-eyed lover Xiphos, and what became of both of them.
### STANDARD +
The bell's design is based on a ritual bell, the Tibetan drilbu with a vajra handle (wiki trivia on design), which fits the City's ceremonial tone.

## appearance
### STANDARD
A catalyst in the form of a sapphire-and-silver bell with a vajra-style handle. The in-game description names only sapphire and sterling silver.

## lore
### LITE
Once, on the silver dunes, a woman danced with the three sisters of the moon; blood and tears mingled to form a garden spring. The Lord of Flowers grieved, and a sapphire city rose from her tears, where the Tighnarians grew proud and bred generations of wisdom. Tulaytullah was said to be her most precious jewel, and in the golden age even the moon looked amber.
### STANDARD +
In the gardens bloomed dream-violet Padisarahs and singing pomegranates, and canals shimmered; even passing deities would not see them buried in sandstorms. In a farewell speech Makhaira tells Xiphos, the exiled high-born and Blade of Tulaytullah, beloved of the Jinni, that tomorrow she must leave because the scholars have sold her to the court of Badanah, whose forefathers destroyed her homeland and enslaved her people. She will dance and speak honeyed words among her enemy and asks him to keep her name in his heart for the night.
### LARGE +
To please the waning vassal king, the scholars who ruled Tulaytullah paid tribute, and Makhaira, a court dancer, became part of it. The story says the rest has been diluted by too many tongues. She is said to have taken vengeance and brought low a kingdom thought invincible, but an adder devoured her life and she sleeps beneath gilded sand. Xiphos earned esteem among his kindred yet died in vain protecting nobles who feared him, killed by another exiled princeling whose banishment the serpent-hearted dancer, a Jinni's daughter, had delivered. All, good or evil, are ground by fate, and the sapphire city faded like tears in the sun.
### ULTRA +
In the surrounding wiki accounts (Xiphos, Faramarz), Makhaira is the concubine of King Badanah who conspired with his son Faramarz to kill the king, and later Faramarz poisoned her with a viper's venom. The bell's text is the dancer's side and Xiphos's side of that arc, ending before the wiki's detailed political narrative. Where sources differ in detail, keep both accounts attributed.

## owners
### LITE
No owner is named; the bell is a memory-object of Tulaytullah. Xiphos and Makhaira are the people the story is about.
### LARGE +
The wiki page records no character wielding it in promotional material, so avoid assigning it to a specific character in scenes.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not in-world): 5-star catalyst released in Version 3.3 (2022-12-07), level 1 base ATK 48, secondary stat CRIT DMG. Passive Bygone Azure Teardrop raises Normal Attack speed; after the wielder uses an Elemental Skill, Normal Attack DMG increases every second for 14 s and again with each Normal Attack hit (once every 0.3 s), up to a cap. The buff ends when the wielder leaves the field and resets with the next Elemental Skill.
### LARGE +
At refinement 1 Normal Attack SPD rises 10%, +4.8% per second, +9.6% per hit, capped at 48%; refinement 5 gives 20%, 9.6%, 19.2% and 96%. Ascension uses Inactivated, Dormant and Robust Fungal Nucleus with Fungal Spores, Luminescent Pollen and Crystalline Cyst Dust.

## roleplay
### LARGE
Use the bell as a mourning symbol of Tulaytullah's golden age. Speakers of the story should treat the details as legend, as the text itself does.
