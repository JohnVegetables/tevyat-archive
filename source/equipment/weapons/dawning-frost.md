## summary
### LITE
A silver-forged ritual vessel, pure and without blemish, said to be a sacred relic passed down among the priestesses of the far north. A 4-star catalyst of the Frostmoon Scion series from Version 6.1.
### STANDARD +
Its text follows a mother's prophetic dream.

## appearance
### STANDARD
A catalyst described as a silver-forged ritual vessel, unblemished. The text mentions a silvery-white talisman worn at the chest, but does not say it is this object.

## lore
### LITE
The item text is a warning and a dream: a mother tells her child, Avrelian, never to set foot in Nod-Krai and not to be taken in by "that fool's dream of Elysium".
### STANDARD +
She is the woman once worshipped as the Maiden, living far from her homeland (Hiisi Island). She says a thousand-year-old curse runs in his blood and no earthly paradise has been built for mortals under the moon. Pregnant, she is troubled by visions of the fate of her homeland.
### LARGE +
The dream cycles through an age: seeds of life fall from silver branches into a ruined city; featherless upright creatures enjoy wisdom, then greed, hatred and strife follow while divine envoys watch silently. The kings of the lands test the eternal heavens and their towers fall in flame. After a thousand years a golden city rises (Hyperborea) and mortals, longing for the vacant throne of the world, try to dissolve the line between man and god. The draconic kings of flame plunge into the Abyss and the tide swallows a white-stone empire.
### ULTRA +
Out of the dark she hears a familiar, compassionate voice that promises to end the warriors of the land and raise a boundless Elysium in their place, "not to be king, but so that no other can ever claim that role". The dream always shatters at that promise. She wakes in pale morning light, her fingers on the talisman, her hand on her belly, and asks her unborn child why it stirs, whether it senses her fear. The text does not say whose voice it is, nor resolve whether the dream is memory, prophecy or both.

## owners
### LITE
No wielder is named; the description ties it to the priestesses of the far north.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not in-world): 4-star catalyst, base ATK 42, CRIT DMG secondary. Passive Nocturnal Dreams gives Elemental Mastery for 10 seconds after a Charged Attack hits, and a separate boost after an Elemental Skill hits. Released in Version 6.1.

## roleplay
### LARGE
Use as an heirloom-style vessel with hushed religious weight. Nod-Krai folk and Scion descendants might recognise it; keep the dream as a text artefact, not a verified record of what happened.
