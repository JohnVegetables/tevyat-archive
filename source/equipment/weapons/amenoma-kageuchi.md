## summary
### LITE
Amenoma Kageuchi is a four-star sword forged in Inazuma, described as one of the "kageuchi" copies of the famed blade "Hakuen Michimitsu Amenoma". Iwakura Douin, founding patriarch of the Iwakura clan, gave it to the Saimon clan as thanks for caring for him while he lived in seclusion in Konda Village. Its item text is mostly about the Iwakura secret technique Tengu Sweeper.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Item text does not describe the blade's look. Its passive is shown in the game as small purple orbs floating above the hilt, a gameplay effect rather than a lore detail.

## lore
### STANDARD
The sword is a copy of Hakuen Michimitsu Amenoma, a blade Douin had the Amenoma smith of his day craft for the "In" line of Iwakura swordsmen. Douin gave this copy to the Saimon clan in thanks for their care. The text describes the Iwakura secret technique Tengu Sweeper, said to work only when there is no trace of confusion in the heart; for centuries the swordsmen who inherited the name "In" used it against evils across the archipelago.
### LARGE +
Legend says Tengu Sweeper was first created in the courtyard of a small shrine where incense no longer burned. It was so powerful it destroyed the building and broke Douin's sword in two. After that he founded the Iwakura Art and became instructor to the Kujou clan, and had the Amenoma master of that time forge the sword for the In line. The text adds that the original's edge was said to cut even mortal fate, and that the overlong name was specified by Douin himself. The English text says "Tengu Sweeper" was once homophonous with "Tengu Victor", a sword strike that could cut a soaring tengu from the sky.
### ULTRA +
In Japanese swordsmithing, a kageuchi ("shadow forging") is the second blade made alongside a commissioned one, usually the smith's own spare. The wiki also notes that a Japanese sword is called by its smith's name, so "Amenoma" names both the smithy and this weapon. The wiki reads the name Hakuen Michimitsu as a Buddhist-flavoured phrase and the claim of cutting "fate" as echoing the themes of the Raiden Shogun's own sword art; this is the wiki's etymological commentary rather than item text.

## owners
### STANDARD
Item text names Iwakura Douin as giver and the Saimon clan of Konda Village as recipients; no wielder is named after that. The Traveler can use it as an ordinary player weapon.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not lore): four-star sword, forged from the Saimon Heirloom Blade Diagram (obtained in the World Quest "The Farmer's Treasure"). Base ATK 41, secondary stat ATK 12%. Passive "Iwakura Succession": after an Elemental Skill, gain a Succession Seed (once per 5s, up to 3, lasting 30s); after an Elemental Burst, all seeds are consumed and 2s later the wielder regains Energy per seed (6 at refinement 1, 7.5 at refinement 2, 9 at refinement 3).

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay use (this project's suggestion, not canon text): a good prop for a village family heirloom, or for a swordsman who prizes a clear mind. Keep Iwakura details to what the text states.

## entry: Tengu Sweeper
@keys: Tengu Sweeper, Tengu Victor, Iwakura Art
@spoiler: none
### LARGE
Tengu Sweeper is the secret sword technique of the Iwakura clan, passed down by swordsmen who take the name "In". It is said to be performable only with a heart free of confusion. According to the Amenoma Kageuchi text, it was first made by Iwakura Douin in a shrine courtyard, wrecked the building and broke his sword, after which he founded the Iwakura Art and taught the Kujou clan.
