## summary
### LITE
Rainbow Serpent's Rain Bow is a 4-star bow, an ancient ceremonial weapon passed down by the Masters of the Night-Wind, dark and forever wreathed in an unshakable chill. Its story tells of a covenant between a young priest and a rainbow-winged dragon.
### STANDARD +
It was an event reward in Version 6.2 (Soulblazers' Snowbound Journey). The story picks up after Cinder City's fall and Maghan's death, and ends with the first Iktomisaurus appearing.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Canon description: a dark bow forever wreathed in chill. Keep to that; no added carvings or colours.

## lore
### LITE
Cinder City and its usurper's dream collapsed in a blaze when the scarlet-eyed hero called down the god's fiery wrath and burned the corrupt throne to ash. Decades earlier Maghan the dragon slayer had driven the old ruler from the deep valleys.
### STANDARD +
The young priest Wagadu laid Maghan to rest in a nameless grave as he had wished, took up his bow and walked into the mist-shrouded mountains. He did not understand what "renew the covenant" and "restore peace" meant; he thought he was returning the cherished bow to the Night Realm to soothe spirits abandoned since ancient sacrifices.
### LARGE +
Past a threefold stone stair and a labyrinthine hall with gilded runes, mist brought visions: a turquoise-eyed maiden, a serpent draped in starlight, a white spider weaving sun and moon, figures of black gold and white steel, a hare mocking a fox, a coyote struggling in tar. Then a mighty dragon with rainbow wings looked down. It said it knew Wagadu's purpose, that Maghan's blade had never been stained with ancient blood, that its king was long dead and it was weary of its kin lost in dreams. It offered secret rites so his tribes could prosper and hear the night's whispers without feeding blood to the fallen envoy of the underworld.
### ULTRA +
The price was two conditions: his descendants must treat the people it once protected as friends and equals and heed their counsel, never letting oaths become a mockery as under the Grand Alliance; and they must strike its name from every woven record so no self-proclaimed sage could uncover it and ruin the pact with foolish revenge. It added that the Wayob sheltering Wagadu had been its servant and had told him of this covenant long ago. Dazed, Wagadu nodded, the hall dissolved, and a cold, feather-light touch made him turn: legend says the first Iktomisaurus stood there, newly emerged from the Night Kingdom.

## owners
### LITE
Used by Maghan in the story; carried into the Night Realm by Wagadu. No modern bearer is named.
### LARGE +
The weapon description says only that the Masters of the Night-Wind pass it down. Do not assign it to a living character.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not in-world): a 4-star bow, base ATK 42 at level 1, secondary stat Energy Recharge (10%). Event reward of Soulblazers' Snowbound Journey (Version 6.2); refinement material is Refined Heart.
### LARGE +
Passive "Astral Whispers Beyond the Sacred Throne": ATK rises for 8s after the equipping character's attacks hit an opponent while they are off-field (28% at refinement 1 to 56% at refinement 5).

## roleplay
### LARGE
Masters of the Night-Wind characters treat this as tribal origin lore and may be careful about speaking the dragon's name, since the covenant asks that it be erased.
