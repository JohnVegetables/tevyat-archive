## summary
### LITE
Skyward Atlas is a five-star catalyst of the Mondstadt Skyward series, released in 1.0. It is a cloud atlas that symbolises Dvalin and his former master, the Anemo Archon, details the winds and clouds of the northern regions and holds the power of sky and wind. In its story it is a copy of a far older atlas of 100,000 odes, each naming one cloud or wind.

## appearance
### STANDARD
The item text describes an atlas presented as poems and images. It gives no cover or binding detail.

## lore
### STANDARD
The atlas pairs images with 100,000 odes, each dedicated to a single cloud or wind and calling it by name. The atlas gave the winds form and the odes gave them personality, so the formless winds became friends and family in Barbatos' eyes. Legend says that in ancient times Barbatos used the original to summon the four winds, thaw the snow, drive away vicious beasts, call rain and create Mondstadt.
### LARGE +
He let the atlas be shared and copied among the people under the name Cloud Atlas. Time was unkind: surviving copies lack certain drawings and odes, though the knowledge lived on in ballads and legends. The histories of the thousand winds were not lost, because Barbatos still holds them. When he stood against the shadowy wings of the evil dragon Durin, he sang to the thousand winds and called the Wind Dragon, Dvalin.
### ULTRA +
The text says whoever holds the atlas wields the true names of the thousand winds and all their powers. It ends in calm: the skies over Mondstadt are blue, Barbatos and Dvalin have found a new place to call home, and the atlas has been entrusted to one worthy of the honour. The Anemo entry in this archive notes that legend credits the original atlas with recording the names of all the thousand winds; the Thousand Winds Temple in Enkanomiya uses a similar phrase with a different meaning, and the link between the two is left unresolved.

## owners
### STANDARD
Original author or keeper: Barbatos. Copies passed among the people of Mondstadt. The item names no current holder. Mona is shown holding the catalyst in her 2022 birthday artwork, which is presentation rather than a canon statement of ownership.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not lore): five-star catalyst from the standard Wanderlust Invocation and weapon event wishes. Base ATK 48, secondary stat ATK 7.2%. Passive "Wandering Clouds": raises Elemental DMG Bonus (12-24%); Normal Attack hits have a 50% chance to summon clouds that seek out nearby opponents for 15 seconds, dealing 160-320% ATK DMG, at most once every 30 seconds.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay use (this project's suggestion, not canon text): treat it as lore-rich rather than as a god's personal spellbook. Weather-lore, names and odes suit scenes with scholars and bards.

### LARGE
The atlas ties to the idea of the thousand winds: the named, personified winds said to protect Mondstadt, whose true names its holder wields. The phrase carries other meanings elsewhere, as the Anemo entry explains.
