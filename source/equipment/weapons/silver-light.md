## summary
@availability: limited
### LITE
Silver Light is a four-star sword obtained from the limited-time Version 7.1 event Silverwing in Pursuit of the Moon (part of the Moonchase Festival). Its description says it is a longsword left by Wind Reader, one of the Three Hermits of Guizang. The item story shows him as a reckless young thaumaturge humbled and gently taken in by the swallow adeptus.

## appearance
### STANDARD
The text describes only a longsword, said to be forged from sacred silver from the heavens, that once cleaved sea water into two. Do not invent more detail.

## lore
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
The story begins in the youth of the man later revered as founder of Qimen Divination, before the name Wind Reader was known. He wandered as a vagabond, plucking talismans from the air, laying formations with poured wine, proud of his genius and answering to nothing but his whims. He once stole the bronze mirror of the Two Sages of Lianshan and struck down bandits. One night he burned shadow phantoms terrorising a mountain village, then mocked the villagers he had saved.

A flash of silver light followed, and the swallow adeptus (later one of the Three Beasts of Tao Dou) appeared, lamenting that he treated his foresight as a toy. He drew his sword and boasted with a rude verse, but was beaten and pinned under her foot. She did not rage. She lifted him up and invited him to walk the wanderer's path with her, since the world was bigger than the outlaws' squalor.
### LARGE +
He called her an old hag and refused to bow as her servant, yet stayed, because in her cold eyes he saw a tenderness he had not felt in a very long time, like the mother's embrace described in ancient texts. Years later the story shifts to a dream: she asks "Or perhaps...?" by lamplight and he, normally quick with banter, cannot speak. He wakes to a burned-out lamp, untouched wine and a lingering spirit. Another full moon has come, and she has still not returned. He resolves to keep waiting in dreams.

### ULTRA +
The verse he shouts at the adeptus is boastful and crude and is best summarised, not quoted. Its claim to command the Jueyun adepti and thunder gods is bravado that he promptly fails to back up. The founder of Qimen Divination is named only by that title and as Wind Reader here, and the swallow adeptus is linked on the wiki to Zibai, though the item text does not name her. The story is a limited-event item text from Silverwing in Pursuit of the Moon.

## owners

Left behind by Wind Reader, according to the description. No later owner is named.
