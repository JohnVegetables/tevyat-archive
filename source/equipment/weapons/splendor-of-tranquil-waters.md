## summary
### LITE
Splendor of Tranquil Waters is a 5-star sword: a scepter around which pure water swirls, once a symbol of the highest authority over the seas. Its story belongs to Erinnyes, a figure of the age of Remuria.
### STANDARD +
The description exists in two versions that disagree about what Erinnyes was and what she achieved, which makes the sword a good example of how Fontaine's legends are told in more than one way.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Canon gives only: a scepter wrapped in swirling pure water, water-blue in colour, with a pommel tip that glows with Pneuma or Ousia light when held by a character who has an Arkhe alignment. Add no further detail.

## lore
### LITE
A scepter that once symbolised supreme authority over the seas. It belonged to Erinnyes, who in the time of the great symphony united the people of the high waters against the gods.
### STANDARD +
The legend says her home was destroyed early by the God King, Remus, whose army from the Golden City enslaved or killed her family. Only two escaped. One met the proud Harmost during the war and rose to inherit his authority (the wiki links this person to Cassiodor and the Harmost to Boethius). The other was taken in by the Prince (page 1) or chieftain (page 2) of Aremorica to guard the waters the God King had not yet seized. The text frames them as two survivors of one home drifting apart, one toward good and one toward evil.
### LARGE +
Page 1 tells it as a knightly quest. Sworn in Egeria's name to find the Pure Grail and free her, Erinnyes and her fellow knights (linked to the Lochknights) suffer terrible trials; their pure hearts and the Grail, won through sacrifice, restore the Mistress of Many Waters. Burdened by the sins of her deeds, Erinnyes then asks Egeria to judge and expel her. The goddess, believing a fair verdict is the greatest mercy to a selfless soul, grants it. The sword sinks into the lake with Egeria's blessing, and Erinnyes departs with her head high, never seen again.

Page 2 tells it as a revenge story. The sword-singer swears to retrieve the Grail and break her mother goddess's shackles, but never finds it. The heavens themselves choose the Mistress of Many Waters, ordering her back from her prison to succeed the golden king and rule the sea-ruins. Erinnyes begs that goddess for wisdom on how the descendants of the seas can avoid disaster, saying she killed the unrighteous and toppled cities for her. The goddess answers with what she once told the King of Fortuna, and the plea ends in despair: the sword, stained with blood, falls away with Erinnyes's last thread of reason, and she is never seen again.
### ULTRA +
Page 2 closes on a note about memory: the chieftain who dreamed of honour and glory never saw the land of her dreams and never found absolution, like a goddess who shared similar hopes. Many years later the musician called the Golden Hunter (linked to Cassiodor) recalls the name and thinks neither of blood nor tears, only of a distant flute and a twisting melody under a watery moon. Page 1 by contrast ends on the departing knight and the sunk sword, with no mention of the flute.

The wiki credits the book La Chanson d'Erinnyes with the information that the sword was called Hauteclaire in Remurian times. Treat that as an alias attributed to the book, not as something the weapon text itself says.

## owners
### LITE
Erinnyes, per the description. No present-day wielder is named.
### LARGE +
Furina is the character this sword is usually paired with, but only as a gameplay and release-timing convention (see uncertainties). Do not state in narration that she inherited or found it unless the user's scenario does.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not in-world): a 5-star sword, base ATK 44 at level 1, secondary stat CRIT DMG; Weapon Event Wishes; introduced in Version 4.2. Passive "Dawn and Dusk by the Lake": when the wielder's current HP increases or decreases, their Elemental Skill DMG rises for a few seconds (stacking up to 3 times); when other party members' HP changes, the wielder's Max HP rises (stacking up to 2 times). Both can trigger off-field. Values scale with refinement.

## roleplay
### LARGE
For scenes: speak of the sword as a scepter tied to old legends of Egeria and the people of the seas, and keep the two accounts of Erinnyes both available. A character can know one version, a stage play or a song, without the other. Do not resolve which is true.
