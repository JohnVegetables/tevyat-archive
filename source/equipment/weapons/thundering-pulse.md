## summary
### LITE
Thundering Pulse is a five-star bow, a longbow with eternal lightning crackling around it that the game calls a gift from the Shogun. Its item story follows the samurai Takamine the Mistsplitter, who won it from a Great Tengu in a gamble, learned Tengu archery from it, and left it as a wager with a shrine maiden when he went to face the disaster from across the sea. He did not return as himself.
### STANDARD +
The bow keeps its sheen despite having been soaked in thick darkness, a detail the text uses to mark that it outlasted both the Cataclysm and its owners.

## appearance
### STANDARD
The text describes a bow that crackles with thunder and retains its luster even though it was soaked in thick darkness. The game description calls it a longbow with eternal lightning around it.

## lore
### STANDARD
When disaster came from a distant sea, the bow was the favourite weapon of a great swordsman, Takamine. In his youth he wandered the mountains and met a great Tengu, with whom he wagered his young, strong body against a bow given by the Shogun. The wager was a draw, three wins each; he became the Tengu's servant but won the bow. The Tengu gave him the ridiculous nickname Konbumaru and showed him Tengu archery, a fierce, unpredictable and elegant form of fighting, loosing thunderbolts with every bowstring's pulse.
### LARGE +
Years later the Tengu sent him to the Shogunate with a recommendation letter. He grew in skill, made friends and enemies, and kept his wanderlust, becoming more fearless with the bow. Before leaving for the Abyss he proposed another wager to the shrine maiden who had inherited the essence of his archery: if he returned alive, the bow would be his again; if he lost, she could keep it. When peace returned and the swordsman had not come back, the Shogun's bow went to the maiden as the prize. Later, in the forest where the Kitsune Saiguu would no longer appear, at their promised meeting place, a lone returnee stumbled back from the Abyss and met the shrine maiden again, though she could no longer be called young. His dull eyes, stained with dried blood and tears, regained their radiance and were pierced by a barbed arrow glowing with power.
### ULTRA +
The wiki's lore section puts the chain of ownership plainly: forged by the Shogun, given to the Great Tengu Reizenbou, won by Takamine, entrusted to Asase Hibiki as a pledge, and used by Hibiki against him when he came back corrupted by the Abyss. It also links the bow to Mistsplitter Reforged, both having been Takamine's. The same wager appears in the Sunny Morning Sleep-In story, told from the shrine maiden's side: the man bets his bow that he will return alive, she thinks him a hopeless gambler despite his fame as a swordmaster, and the hawk of her good dream is never seen again.

## owners
### STANDARD
Forged by the Shogun (not specified which one) and given to Reizenbou. Then Takamine the Mistsplitter, who won it in a gamble. Then Asase Hibiki, who received it as a wager prize and is its last known owner. Kujou Sara is depicted with it in artwork, which is not an ownership claim.
### LARGE +
It is the same type of bow as Hamayumi in gameplay terms, sharing ascension materials with it; the wiki notes this as trivia, not as lore.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not lore): a five-star bow from the Inazuma series, base ATK 46, secondary stat CRIT DMG 14.4%. Passive "Rule By Thunder": ATK +20-40%, plus the Thunder Emblem, which gains a stack on each Normal Attack hit (5s) and each Elemental Skill cast (10s) and disappears when Energy is full; stacks 1/2/3 boost Normal Attack DMG by 12/24/40% up to 24/48/80% at refinement 1-5. It was the event-exclusive weapon of the Version 2.0 Epitome Invocation, alongside Skyward Blade.

## roleplay
### LARGE
Roleplay use (this project's suggestion, not canon text): a sad inheritance, a bow that stayed whole while its people were lost. Anyone who knows its full story will treat the wager with wry fondness and the ending with restraint; most Inazumans know the bow only as a fabled weapon of the Shogun.

### STANDARD
Takamine is the great swordsman of the item stories, the one-time servant of the Tengu Reizenbou who won this bow and later owned Mistsplitter Reforged. The Thundering Pulse text gives him the nickname Konbumaru from his time as a Tengu's servant and tells of his wagers. His own entry carries the rest of his story.

## entry: Asase Hibiki

### LARGE
Hibiki is the shrine maiden who inherited Takamine's archery and holds the bow after the wager. The Thundering Pulse text leaves her unnamed in the story passage; the wiki links the figure to her name and gives her as the last known owner. Her dreams are the subject of the Sunny Morning Sleep-In story.
