## summary
### LITE
Oathsworn Eye is a jewel-like catalyst that glimmers like a snake's eye. Its description calls it a national treasure of Byakuyakoku, stored in the Dainichi Mikoshi, and says that when the Serpent God came, it was used to notarize great oaths and wishes. Its item text is a short fable of the oath a lonely serpent swore with a child and with his people in the depths.
### STANDARD +
It is a 4-star catalyst that players could obtain only as a reward in the Three Realms Gateway Offering event (Version 2.5), so its in-world material is tied to Enkanomiya.

## appearance
### STANDARD
A jewel that glimmers like snake-eyes, shown in the text as held out by the giant white serpent. Canon text describes it no further than a glittering jewel used as a witness to oaths.

## lore
### LITE
The text imagines the giant white snake looking down at a child who asks him to become their deity. The snake says he fled into waters unknown because he could best neither Deus Auri nor Narukami, and warns that anyone who keeps seeking the light will suffer loss. Yet he shows a snake-eyed jewel and says: swear it upon this eye, as he did when he made a pact with his coral servants.
### STANDARD +
A speaker quotes Master Spartacus's teaching that they must never worship a god and can rely only on themselves. The white serpent says nothing, since he respects the will of the people dwelling in the depths: if vain worship were replaced by new belief, would that not dishonour those who fought? The child swears before the eye anyway, declaring that all is as it was when everything was lost.
### LARGE +
Years pass. The island is complete, the vishaps are repelled and the Holy Soil may now be governed. The oath sworn on the eye has come to fruition, with the House of Sangonomiya, gathered Jibashiri and the vassals named as parties. The speaker sets the terms: if two or more parties are dissatisfied with the arrangement concerning Enkanomiya, or decide otherwise than they first did, the tower of the sun will hear their wishes and collapse of its own accord, destroying all that was once their past. Saying this, the serpent god took the remaining people to the surface, to fulfil his oath to the heavenly city.
### ULTRA +
The wiki identifies the white serpent as Orobashi, the island as Enkanomiya, the vishaps as the Bathysmal Vishaps, the tower of the sun as the Dainichi Mikoshi and "Master Spartacus" as Supada no Hiko; the text itself uses titles. The final line points to the serpent god's covenant with Celestia, which the Enkanomiya quests tell in more detail.

## owners
### LITE
No individual is named. The eye belongs to Byakuyakoku as a national treasure, and its home was the Dainichi Mikoshi.
### LARGE +
The text implies the original eye was the serpent's own witness-jewel for his pact with his coral servants and later with the people; do not assume that any player-obtained copy is that jewel.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not in-world): a 4-star catalyst, an event weapon of the Three Realms Gateway Offering (Version 2.5, limited-time event). Base ATK 44 with ATK% as secondary stat.
### LARGE +
Refinement uses the event material Ointment of Sight (four obtainable from the event) rather than duplicate weapons. Its passive, People of the Faltering Light, increases Energy Recharge for 10 seconds after using an Elemental Skill (24% at refinement 1, 48% at refinement 5).

## roleplay
### LARGE
For scenes: treat the eye as a witness to promises. Characters from Enkanomiya or those who know its history may be moved by it; others see only a snake-eyed jewel. Keep the oath solemn: breaking it, in the text's wording, destroys the past.
