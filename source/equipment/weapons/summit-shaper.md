## summary
### LITE
Summit Shaper is a five-star sword of Liyue and one of the Liyue series weapons tied to Rex Lapis in the Archon War. Item text says that in an age of turmoil, when people cried out for lost loved ones, the ruler of Geo carved a long blade from a slab of golden Cor Lapis, cut a corner off a mountain with one stroke, and on it swore a solemn oath: to gather the scattered and punish those who broke contracts.
### STANDARD +
The text says the oath still works in Liyue: to break a contract is to make an enemy of the land, and the mountain Rex Lapis cut will sooner or later fall on contract-breakers. A legend adds that the sword's true owner will one day return among mortals.

## appearance
### LARGE
Item text describes a long blade carved from a pure slab of golden Cor Lapis and says that when its owner returns it "will once again gleam gold". It gives no hilt or length details.

## lore
### STANDARD
The story begins with ordinary people asking where their lovers and children had gone, when the departed would return and how long the horrors would last. Even hearts hewn from bedrock cracked, and the eyes of those who kept faithful silence began to smolder.
### LARGE +
In answer the ruler of Geo (Morax) put forth his might, carved the blade, sliced a corner from a mountain and swore on it: scattered people would be gathered, contract-breakers punished, and those who lost loved ones, possessions or justice would receive recompense. The text admits this is but one of many tales about ancient Liyue, yet claims the oath is at work in the land: breaking a contract makes an enemy of the country where gods once ruled.
### ULTRA +
The closing legend says the weapon's true owner will return to walk among mortals; the sword will gleam gold again and its light will cut through the world's greatest injustices, as it did at the oath. The wiki's Liyue Series page uses the story as an example of Morax's weapons in the Archon War and does not claim the return has happened.

## owners
### STANDARD
The text credits the sword to the ruler of Geo, Morax or Rex Lapis. It records no later holder, and canon does not show Zhongli carrying it.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not lore): a five-star sword from weapon event wishes. Base ATK 46, secondary stat ATK 10.8%. Passive "Golden Majesty", shared by the Liyue series: Shield Strength +20-40% and scoring hits raises ATK 4-8% for 8 seconds, up to 5 stacks, doubled while under a shield (refinements 1 to 5).

## roleplay
### LARGE
Roleplay use (this project's suggestion, not canon text): Liyue's contract culture makes this a natural touchstone for talk of oaths, honesty and the cost of breaking a deal. Present it as a legend told in Liyue, not as an object a character can produce.

### LARGE
The Liyue series is an unofficial grouping of five-star weapons tied to Morax in the Archon War, sharing the passive "Golden Majesty" and the stats of base ATK 46 and ATK 10.8% (before levelling). The wiki lists Summit Shaper and Vortex Vanquisher among them and describes the series as weapons closely associated with Morax in the Archon War.
