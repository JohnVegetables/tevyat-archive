## summary
### LITE
The Alley Flash is a four-star sword of the Alley series: a straight, night-black longsword once carried by a thief who roamed Mondstadt's benighted streets. The item text tells of a young noble who stole it from his family's vault and used it, never staining it with blood, to rob the rich for the poor.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Item text calls it a mighty, noble longsword as black as night, its darkened blade blending into nights when people feared lighting fires. It is said many later knightly swords, such as the Favonius Sword, were modelled on this design. A bard's song says it was blackened during the age of the aristocracy.

## lore
### STANDARD
The young noble (identified by the wiki as Parsifal) read the tales and songs of his upright ancestors, which sowed rebellion in him. He left his venerable house, stole the sword, and hid in the streets, living among common folk in alleys and taverns and using his noble swordsmanship to rob the rich for the poor. The edge was never marred by blood, nor was the thief's heart.
### LARGE +
As wine, song and youth faded and events passed, he buried his beloved blade under moonlight and fled across the sea. He remembered the night he took it from his family's vault, and the vow he made to his family, past and present, to his land and to Eberhart: to use his strength, even if only a little, to turn the dark world around.
### ULTRA +
Related items fill in the legend: the Wine and Song songbook sings of the same gentleman thief, and the Alley Hunter is the black bow he carried. Eberhart is called a sworn brother in English and a half-brother in the Chinese original. The sword is thus a symbol of rebellion from within the aristocracy rather than from outside it.

## owners
### STANDARD
Item text refers to a young noble turned thief; the wiki identifies him as Parsifal. He took it from his house's vault and buried it before leaving.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Game mechanics: Alley-series four-star sword, base ATK 45, Elemental Mastery 12 secondary, passive 'Itinerant Hero'. Released in Version 1.4 via Weapon Event Wishes.
