## summary
### LITE
Beyond the Chrysalis is a 5-star sword. Its description calls it a longsword brimming with light and color, as weightless as a butterfly when brandished, once used by a certain individual to break bonds. Its text is the story of the eldest daughter of a Vila noble house in Snezhnaya who ended a rebellion against the Tsaritsa and then asked to lose her name. Added in Version 7.1.
### STANDARD +
Released with Version 7.1 (2026-09-23), the item belongs to Snezhnayan history rather than to a famous present-day wielder; canon does not say who carries it.

## appearance
### STANDARD
The only description is of a longsword brimming with light and color, weightless as a butterfly when brandished. The title plays on a chrysalis; the text does not describe any ornament.

## lore
### LITE
Before she lost her last name, the world knew her as the eldest daughter of the Strivozhas, a Vila noble house.
### STANDARD +
After Grand Duke Photius died of a sudden stroke, a counselor inherited his title and his planned rebellion with the Druzhna was buried. The Vila house stayed at the top of power, and the Silver Court of Veneter again fell to a Strivozha. Rebels from the fae clans urged the Vila princess to rise against the new Tsaritsa, and she swore to stand at their side.
### LARGE +
The rebels pressed her with the name of her mother, called The Pure One, who had been devoted to Tsar Monomakh. The princess, Gluvasha Strivozha, then denounced the Tsaritsa in public, expelled the palace-appointed observer from her lands and raised a following without royal sanction. The rebel clans, distrustful of each other, entrusted their secrets to her, until all were caught at once and learned she had long stood at the Tsaritsa's side. When they accused her of besmirching her mother's name, she replied coolly that the Vila had pledged to stand with them only if the Tsaritsa ought to be overthrown, and that Anastasya alone was the Vila sovereign. She was neither their vassal nor they her lieges, so she had betrayed nothing. The rebellion ended, and the Strivozhas were recorded as having upheld their honor and loyalty.
### ULTRA +
The story's last part is the Vila princess's little-known sequel. She asked the Tsaritsa to release her from the chain of a noble name and her fief, and, because she had raised a force without sanction even as a ruse, asked to be stripped of her last name and title and replaced. The throne decreed that from that day none would remember her by her last name, that her title would vanish from the histories, and that her gravestone would bear witness to her convictions. The text names the Tsaritsa, Tsar Monomakh and Anastasya without explaining their present status; for that, rely on their own entries and the Pale Star Court. The identity of the "certain individual" who used the blade to break bonds is not given.

## owners

Canon does not name an owner. The text tells the story of Gluvasha, a Vila who gave up her house name; a link to the sword is only inferred from the item description about breaking bonds.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Game mechanics, not lore: a 5-star sword, base ATK 48, CRIT DMG secondary stat. Dance of Wings Unbound runs a three-step sequence each time the wielder uses an Elemental Skill or Burst, giving CRIT DMG, then Stellar Swirl DMG, then Energy regeneration, and the sequence resets when the wielder leaves the field. It was on the weapon event Wish Epitome Invocation (2026-09-23 to 2026-10-13).
