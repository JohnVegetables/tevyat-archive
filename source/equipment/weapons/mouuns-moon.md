## summary
### LITE
Mouun's Moon is a four-star bow of the Watatsumi series, the longbow of the Watatsumi shrine maiden Mouun. Item text says it shines pure white like seafoam at the tip of a moonlit wave. She befriended the monsters of the distant seas and fought with them against the thundering clouds in the war of Orobashi (Watatsumi Omikami), a path of no return that ended in tragic obliteration.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Item text describes a longbow shining as pure white as seafoam at the tip of a moonlit wave. No further detail is given.

## lore
### STANDARD
The text says the shrine maiden was friends with the monsters of the distant seas, and together they fought the thundering clouds for the sake of Watatsumi's "transient dream", riding the waves in the spray at ships' bows. She is quoted as accepting that Orobashi's war may have been doomed from the start, but hoping that leaving memories and "seeds of sacrifice" would make it worthwhile. Songs eulogise her unspoken bond with her twin Ayame, "Umigozen", depicting them with bow drawn and spear raised in the wave-spray of a ship.
### LARGE +
Other songs recount her riding with Touzannou on a sea creature at night and whispering of broken tomorrows into the hero's ear. In peaceful days the twins sang in chorus with the great whale of the deep sea, telling of dismal days in the depths and the dark ever-night, and of Omikami and the radiant jade branches. She also "frolicked with that reckless young man, whose only strength was his strength". The text closes with a promised exchange: once the legendary Great Tengu's mask is brought back, "you have to keep our promise and finish that business", and the retort that a whale will raise a wave to wash that mouth clean.
### ULTRA +
Akuoumaru's text gives the other end of this exchange: its hero wished to bring the Great Tengu's mask back to Ayame and Mouun, and Wavebreaker's Fin says Mouun was consumed by a dark storm of crow feathers while the great whale sank to the sea floor. The three Watatsumi weapons therefore overlap: Mouun's Moon is the intimate account, Akuoumaru the hero's, Wavebreaker's Fin the aftermath. The nature of the "promise" and "business" is never stated; do not fill it in.

## owners
### STANDARD
The shrine maiden Mouun (historical.mouun). No later owner is named.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not lore): four-star bow. Base ATK 44, secondary stat ATK 6%. Passive "Watatsumi Wavewalker" is shared with Akuoumaru and Wavebreaker's Fin: for every point of the party's combined maximum Energy capacity, the wielder's Elemental Burst DMG rises, up to a cap. Obtained from weapon event wishes.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay use (this project's suggestion, not canon text): a bow of lost friends and a lost cause. Within Watatsumi, Mouun is remembered with affection; elsewhere she is mostly an unknown name.
