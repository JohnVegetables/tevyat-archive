## summary
### LITE
Engulfing Lightning is the polearm (a naginata) of Ei, the Electro Archon who rules Inazuma through her puppet, the Raiden Shogun. Its item text calls it a lethal weapon for cutting away "scattered dross" and speaks of Ei's vow to prune mortal obsession and unchecked desire in order to keep an unchanging, eternal world. Its Japanese name means roughly "grass-cutting lightning".
### STANDARD +
It is a 5-star polearm of the Inazuma weapon series. The Raiden Shogun uses a polearm in ordinary combat and switches to the sword Musou Isshin for her Elemental Burst.

## appearance
### STANDARD
A long-hafted naginata, a polearm with a curved blade, charged with Electro. Its in-game model gains a more ornate form at the second ascension phase. Canon text gives no materials or measurements beyond calling it a naginata; the writers have said only that the weapon Ei used to slay Orobashi weighed thousands of tons (see uncertainties).

## lore
### LITE
The weapon's story is told as Ei's reflection. From high above the storm clouds she looked on the mortal world she loved and saw petty disputes and fleeting obsessions that, to her, were enemies of eternity. Like weeds among crops, they had to be swept away by lightning, so that nothing needed to wither or be lost.
### STANDARD +
The text recalls a remembered scene under the sakura tree: Yae Miko, drinking wine with Ei, asked what kind of eternity Makoto's eyes would reflect. Ei dismisses it as a pointless question, though the answer she gave was lost to the alcohol. She had already settled the matter countless times in memory: just as fruit trees are thinned for quality, so not one speck of dross could be allowed in an eternal paradise.
### LARGE +
The passage then turns to what drives her. She imagines using a great blade of light to uproot all obsession and to remove the chance of any dream taking root and withering, producing a tranquil world with no dispute, gain or loss, but an "amnesiac world, lost to itself". Her old friends still appear clearly in the heart of eternity and the scent of the sakura is still fresh. Having watched the Cataclysm's pitch-dark annihilation consume those precious to her, she regards the impermanence of life and death, and destiny itself, as her mortal foe. Since no one can overturn impermanence, the text ends by saying the eternal euthymia in her heart should be carried forward into the nation she loves, Inazuma.
### ULTRA +
Read with the rest of the Inazuma story, this is the philosophy behind her pursuit of eternity and the Plane of Euthymia: eternity as protection against loss. The item text itself stops at her resolve and never shows the change of heart she later reaches in the Archon Quest; do not attach that resolution to the weapon's own text.

## owners
### LITE
Ei is the wielder the text names. The Raiden Shogun, her puppet, fights with the same style of polearm.
### LARGE +
Ei's other signature blade, Musou Isshin, belonged first to her sister Makoto and is a separate object (see the Musou Isshin entry). No present-day character other than Ei is established as owning Engulfing Lightning.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not in-world): a 5-star polearm in the Inazuma series, released in Version 2.1 and obtainable from the weapon event Wish. Base ATK 46 with Energy Recharge as its secondary stat.
### LARGE +
Its passive, Timeless Dream: Eternal Stove, raises ATK by a percentage of the wielder's Energy Recharge above the base 100% (capped), and grants extra Energy Recharge for 12 seconds after using an Elemental Burst. Refinement scales these values: at refinement 1 the conversion is 28% of surplus Energy Recharge, capped at 80% ATK, and at refinement 5 it is 56%, capped at 120%.

## roleplay
### LARGE
For scenes: treat the weapon as Ei's personal symbol, not as something handed out. A scene may invoke it as the instrument of her old pursuit of eternity; do not give it to other characters as an heirloom, and do not claim canon says where the physical weapon is kept.

## entry: Timeless Dream: Eternal Stove
@keys: Timeless Dream, Eternal Stove
@keys2: Engulfing Lightning, naginata
### LARGE
Timeless Dream: Eternal Stove is the weapon passive of Engulfing Lightning (gameplay). Mechanically it converts surplus Energy Recharge into ATK and adds Energy Recharge after an Elemental Burst; its title echoes the weapon's theme of an unchanging, timeless dream of eternity.
