## summary
### LITE
Tome of the Eternal Flow is a 5-star catalyst: a manual once passed down from generation to generation within an underwater priory. Today none know of its existence.
### STANDARD +
Its story is the oath of the priory's watchers, who guard the capital of water against a flood and a breach, and the slow decay of the order.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Canon gives only that the tip of the tome's spine glows with Pneuma or Ousia when an Arkhe-aligned character holds it. Add nothing else.

## lore
### LITE
The first watcher led new recruits to recite an oath: remember the oath of a thousand years, defend the foundation of the capital of water, and never forget the sin that was once tempting as honey nor the responsibility that may feel like fetters.
### STANDARD +
These shackles stand for the deity's honey-sweet grace and faith in them (the text links the deity to Egeria). The watchers are the shield against the flood, the line of defence against the breach. Those who join have succumbed to the euphoria of violence or to greed; the vow is the recompense of justice and their last chance at redemption. There is neither sunlight nor family, so they must find a semblance of joy.
### LARGE +
The first watcher sometimes wondered whether the sinners swore on their own accord or only uttered meaningless syllables, yet hoped the wild joy he felt when the deity entrusted him would light the way for his brothers and sisters. If the harbinger of the apocalypse, which the wiki links to the All-Devouring Narwhal, one day arrived, the flood would wash everything away, and the watchers would fight with duty and joy in their hearts. When his duty was done he left the duty to the others and was buried between splendid mineral veins, the place most filled with light.
### ULTRA +
The flood never came, as the text says fate rarely grants would-be heroes an honourable death. Instead an inaudible trickle began to corrode the priory like a thief in the shadows. The number of those forced to join and live underwater grew, while those who swore the oath, understood it and knew the teachings declined. Many years later a fortress rose from the seabed, and the last brother who called himself a watcher and bore a god's expectations left a grave known to none. The text ends on a hopeful note: the other suppressed breach was eventually found by someone who set out to reveal its secrets.

## owners

Neuvillette is paired with it only by convention (see uncertainties). Do not claim he owns or found it without a source.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not in-world): a 5-star catalyst, base ATK 44 at level 1, secondary stat CRIT DMG; Weapon Event Wishes; introduced in Version 4.1. Passive "Aeon Wave": raises HP; when current HP increases or decreases, Charged Attack DMG rises for 4s (up to 3 stacks, once per 0.3s); at 3 stacks, or when the third stack refreshes, Energy is restored, once per 12s. Values scale with refinement.

## roleplay
### LARGE
For scenes: the tome is a forgotten relic; characters would speak of the watchers as lost folklore, not as a known institution. Do not name the priory's location or leaders.

In the weapon story of Tome of the Eternal Flow, an underwater priory's watchers, sinners given a second chance by a deity, swear to guard the capital of water against a flood and a breach. The order decays over time, and the last watcher's grave is lost.
