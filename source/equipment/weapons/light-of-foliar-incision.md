## summary
### LITE
Light of Foliar Incision is a 5-star sword of the Sumeru series, a gilded blade made from precious white branches. Its description says it was once used to destroy countless poisons that infested the forest. The story follows an exiled prince from the golden desert who is spared by a huntress in Dharma Forest and told to break off a pale branch.
### STANDARD +
The sword is the blade counterpart of the bow Hunter's Path: both are made from the pale branches of the forest, and the huntress with the "moon-white bow" appears in this text.

## appearance
### STANDARD
A gilded blade of white branches. Canon text gives no other physical detail.

## lore
### LITE
A wanderer from the land of gold, once a prince, whose body and mind are scarred by battle, is lost in the green maze of the forest. The ancient forest king, tasting the scent of blood stemming from authority, sighs and summons a huntress with a moon-white bow to hunt the beast that has come from beyond the woods.
### STANDARD +
Death whispers through the maze following the exile; a curse spreads across the desert sands behind him. The huntress finds him by his strange scent, warns him that she has already struck him with one arrow and that the next will find his heart, and tells him the crown he seeks is not here and that he must not disturb the dreams of the children. Yet she lowers her bow and deliberately misses. The Aranara, the dream-hidden children, breathe out as no blood is spilled.
### LARGE +
The forest king judges the man: he comes from a sordid land with blood on his hands and delusions in his heart, and the forest keeps only dreams born of naivete. But if she believes he can reclaim his honour, she should guide him to break off the pale branch so that the moon and newborn stars can grant pure wisdom and help him shed bitter memories and desires. She drives him into the maze again. The ending is rumour: that he forged a blade from the pale branch that belonged to him alone, and that children claim in sleep that he forgot his home and his dream of kingship. Prince Faramarz's name vanishes in the rainforest and returns to the desert on windborne sand.
### ULTRA +
The wiki's Faramarz page places this scene in his exile after he killed his lover Makhaira, and calls him the creator and wielder of this sword. Treat the sword's text as the poetic version: it itself says the truth survives only in dreams. The forest king is Viaghara by the wiki's reading, but the text calls only "the ancient forest king".

## owners
### LITE
In the story, the exiled prince Faramarz is the one rumoured to have forged a blade from the pale branch; the wiki calls him its creator and wielder.
### LARGE +
No present-day owner is established. Do not state that any current character carries the original blade.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not in-world): 5-star sword released in Version 3.4 (2023-01-18), level 1 base ATK 44, secondary stat CRIT DMG. Passive Whitemoon Bristle increases CRIT Rate and, after Normal Attacks deal Elemental DMG, grants the Foliar Incision effect, increasing Normal Attack and Elemental Skill DMG by a percentage of Elemental Mastery for 28 hits or 12 s; it can be gained once every 12 s.
### LARGE +
At refinement 1 CRIT Rate rises 4% and the bonus is 120% of Elemental Mastery; refinement 5 gives 8% and 240%. Ascension uses Desiccated, Sturdy and Marked Shell with Faded Red Satin, Trimmed Red Silk and Rich Red Brocade. A Genius Invokation TCG equipment card of the same name exists.

## roleplay
### LARGE
Use it as a forest relic whose story is half rumour. Characters may repeat that the exiled prince forgot his home and kingship, and should say so as hearsay.
