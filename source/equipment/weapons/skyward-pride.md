## summary
### LITE
Skyward Pride is a five-star claymore of the Mondstadt Skyward series, released in 1.0. It symbolises the pride of Dvalin soaring through the skies and hums deeply when swung, with the force of his command of sky and wind. Its story tells of the battle with Durin, the poison, and Dvalin's fall to become Stormterror.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Only its sound is described, a deep hum when swung. The text says nothing of its look.

## lore
### STANDARD
A tale of sky-shaking valour: even the vile dragon Durin, brought into the world by a sinner of an ancient abyssal nation, lost its dark, metallic wings to "Anemo's pride and joy". Durin, jealous of Mondstadt's glory, terrorised it. The Anemo Archon, woken from his sylvan slumber by the victims' cries, summoned his faithful servant, and the two rode the wind to fight.
### LARGE +
In a battle that tore clouds from the sky, Dvalin spread his six blessed wings and cut through Durin's iron scales like a colossal blade, then struck with claws like swords of wind. He tore out Durin's throat with his teeth and the evil dragon fell. Dvalin saved Mondstadt, but the tainted blood he swallowed poisoned him and corruption seeped into his bones.
### ULTRA +
Though a hero, Dvalin suffered alone, and the stench of corruption drew evil creatures. Deceived by the Abyss, he fell and Stormterror was born. His master at last returned to calm his frenzied mind, and his faithful friend and a new companion fought to drive out the monsters and restore his honour. Over the century of his sleep his image faded from Mondstadt's memory, until the people recently saw his six-winged figure again. Now, blessed by the Archon and serenaded by Barbatos, Dvalin takes to the skies once more.

## owners
### STANDARD
The item names no wielder. The claymore stands for Dvalin's pride rather than being something he held.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not lore): five-star claymore from the standard Wanderlust Invocation and weapon event wishes. Base ATK 48, secondary stat Energy Recharge 8%. Passive "Sky-ripping Dragon Spine": raises all DMG (8-16%); after an Elemental Burst, Normal and Charged Attack hits create vacuum blades dealing 80-160% ATK DMG along their path, lasting 20 seconds or 8 blades.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay use (this project's suggestion, not canon text): suits a retelling of Dvalin as hero first and Stormterror second. Mention the poison honestly; the text insists he was a hero.
