## summary
### LITE
Wandering Evenstar is a 4-star catalyst: originally a device used by researchers to observe and calculate celestial phenomena, which now serves as a conduit and catalyst. Its legend is of a prince who lost his kingdom and wandered into the rainforest.
### STANDARD +
It belongs to the Tulaytullah series with Makhaira Aquamarine and Xiphos' Moonlight, and was released in Version 3.1 through weapon event Wishes.

## appearance
### STANDARD
A researcher's celestial observation device, now a catalyst. The description gives no more detail.

## lore
@version: 3.1
### LITE
The tale is set thousands of years ago, after the foolish God-King was buried by sandstorms. The wandering prince, having lost his kingdom, retreated into the rainforest, meaning to conquer new lands but pursued by a huntress with a white bow. Trapped by vine branches in cold moonlight, he heard a tiger growl around him.
### STANDARD +
Quoted lines say the rainforest trail was so treacherous that mortals could tell the way only by moonlight breaking through leaves. The flickering evening stars lit his way yet lured him into a deadly trap. The huntress drove the intruder away again and again, and with the tiger's roar ordered his expulsion, but did not take his life.
### LARGE +
An old blind poet repeats the story in a cracked voice, still turning his lost eyes toward the evening star beside the moon, which led the wanderer to new hope and a dead end. Years later, facing death with nothing to lose, the wanderer had to choose when the teachings of death were whispered to him. A warning says: he belongs to neither forest nor death, so he should keep far from the king's palace and not go deep into the dark and dangerous lands.
### ULTRA +
The wanderer answers with a refusal of the repeated warning: if his fate leads to the moon-white bow and the evening stars foretell his end, he would rather accept it than die pursuing a falcon.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not in-world): 4-star catalyst, level 1 base ATK 42, secondary stat Elemental Mastery 36. Passive Wildling Nightstar: every 10 s the wielder gains a percentage of their Elemental Mastery as ATK for 12 s, with nearby party members gaining 30% of it; it works off-field and stacks across copies.
### LARGE +
The ATK gain is 24% of Elemental Mastery at refinement 1 and 48% at refinement 5.

## roleplay
### LARGE
Use it as a melancholy legend recited by a blind poet. Do not identify the wanderer beyond the wiki's link to Faramarz, and treat that as the series' background rather than something the text says.
