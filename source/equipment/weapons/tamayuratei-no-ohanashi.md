## summary
### LITE
Tamayuratei no Ohanashi is a four-star polearm of the Youkai series, obtained during the Version 5.4 event Enchanted Tales of the Mikawa Festival. Its text tells of an invisible youkai who took the form of a spectral flame, stole stories at hearths and braziers and retold them at banquets of wandering spirits, and was named "Tamayuratei" by the kitsune chief Hakushin. After the Cataclysm it became a messenger of battle reports and wore itself away.

## appearance
### STANDARD
The weapon is a lantern-topped polearm. The wiki notes that its lantern has dynamic night-time lighting when effects are set high (a client detail, not lore); the item text does not describe its look.

## lore
### STANDARD
The text begins with spectral flames drifting through the wilds and an invisible, secret-loving youkai taking the form of such a flame, flitting from hearth to brazier to steal stories from people chatting over tea. It retold them at banquets of wandering spirits, for amusing the great while they drank was the purpose of the Otogishu. At one such evening Hakushin, chief of the kitsune, sighed that she wished time could stop, and raised her sake cup to give the lowly youkai on stage the title "Tamayuratei".
### LARGE +
Her melancholy proved prophetic, since the Cataclysm's dark, corrosive tide engulfed the land and the hundred-youkai revels gave way to weeping. Remembering the great one who named it, the frail youkai threw itself into the war, darting from fire to fire and turning its eloquence to the precise relaying of battle reports and enemy movements. The constant effort took a toll: it divided its essence again and again, nearly exhausting its spirit, could no longer speak from within its lantern, and lost even its cherished memories.
### ULTRA +
The closing legend is a gentle one: the bewildered youkai has forgotten everything and now appears only at the liveliest occasions, when crowds gather to hear fantastic tales, and perhaps the storytellers' atmosphere lets it recall, briefly, a past it cannot reclaim. This is event-sourced text (the weapon is only obtainable during the Mikawa festival event), so it should be treated as folklore told within Inazuma rather than universally known history.

## owners
### STANDARD
No human owner is named; the story is about an unnamed youkai who bears the title "Tamayuratei".

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not lore): four-star polearm of the Youkai series. Base ATK 44, secondary stat Energy Recharge 6.7%. Passive "Busybody's Running Light" raises ATK and Movement SPD by 10% for 10s after using an Elemental Skill. Obtained in the 5.4 event "Enchanted Tales of the Mikawa Festival".

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay use (this project's suggestion, not canon text): a storyteller's lantern: good for scenes about gossip, festival evenings and forgotten loyalty.
