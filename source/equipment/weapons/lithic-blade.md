## summary
### LITE
Lithic Blade is a 4-star claymore of the Lithic series, a greatsword carved and chiselled from the very bedrock of Liyue. The Millelith of old favoured it, but it is so heavy that no ordinary human can lift it. It was added in Version 1.3.
### STANDARD +
Its text ties the blade to the Millelith, the armed force first founded at the initiative of Rex Lapis' followers, and to their code about stone and guardians.

## appearance
### STANDARD
A plain stone greatsword; canon describes only that it is carved from bedrock. Do not invent decoration.

## lore
### LITE
It is made of the divine bedrock upon which Liyue Harbor was established, and it is incomparably heavy; records say the Millelith of old wielded it with ease.
### STANDARD +
The Millelith was founded on the initiative of the followers of Rex Lapis, and its history goes back to when the port city was first completed. They march under Rex Lapis' and Liyue's banner and their code is: while rock stays hard, the mountain shall never fail; while the Millelith stands guard, evil shall never prevail.
### LARGE +
They follow Rex Lapis' footsteps as best they can, slaying monsters, protecting citizens and keeping the peace; the text says the Millelith made its name not in slaughter but in defence, shielding the homeland with their bodies.
### ULTRA +
The greatsword mirrors the guardians' duty and will. Xing and Han, the two members who first carved stone into swords, must have known that fewer people would have the strength to use them, which also meant that peace had come and neither guardians nor swords would be needed.

## owners
### STANDARD
No individual owner is named. The text attributes use to the Millelith of old.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not literal lore): 4-star claymore, base ATK 42, ATK% secondary stat (9 per cent). Passive Lithic Axiom: Unity gives the wearer 7 to 11 per cent ATK and 3 to 7 per cent CRIT Rate (by refinement) for each Liyue character in the party, stacking up to 4 times. It was a Weapon Event Wish (Version 1.3) and has the same passive as Lithic Spear.
