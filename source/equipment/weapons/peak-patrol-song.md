## summary
### LITE
Peak Patrol Song is a 5-star sword: an obsidian blade of unparalleled sharpness and light as a song, modified by an artisan (linked to Xilonen) with a detachable Phlogiston device that plays records.
### STANDARD +
Its story is not about the blade but about Sakkuk, an old hero of the mines, and the songs of the Nanatzcayan.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Canon says an obsidian blade of unparalleled sharpness, with a detachable record-playing Phlogiston device. Add no further details such as pattern or colour.

## lore
### LITE
Every year, before the Embercore Flowers bloom, the mistress of the mountain mines comes alone to Tequemecan Valley to lay dew-kissed flowers for friends who never saw the dawn. Her once-hard heart has softened with age.
### STANDARD +
The sound of the mines and the artisans' songs shakes her from her bitter memories: the echoes of the Nanatzcayan have reached even this place, and she laughs.
### LARGE +
The woman is Sakkuk, representative of the Chiefs, who has crossed the mountain's spine more times than she can count. All who could receive her warmth have long since returned to the Sacred Flame, and the once empty hills now ring with laughter that the young dreamed of while hiding from usurpers and that belongs to the future of civilization.

She sings aloud the song that "he" once taught her but that she was too embarrassed ever to sing to him, calling it a mere prank in those days. Its verse speaks of stone engraved and peaks that echo, tempered in the forge, blessing all beings; of converging and dispersing, rebirth in ashes, and a blazing dawn.
### ULTRA +
Thousands of years after authority was returned to the tribes, the ballads once kept by Sakkuk, called "Peace-Reforger", are lost. What survives is the sharp, resolute rhythm passed on with each hammer stroke on chisel: the text calls it the blood that has flowed through the mines since ancient times, and the sparks from the hammer a song for those who resist. It closes by repeating the opening questions, whether a life must wither like fresh flowers, where the heart goes when all is chaos, and the answer that nobody stays forever in this world: "here, there were once flowers; here, there was once song". The wiki gives Sakkuk the Ancient Name Baraka (blessing), which the verse echoes, and records her as the last survivor of the first heroes to join the Scarlet-Eyed Youth.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not in-world): a 5-star sword, base ATK 44 at level 1, secondary stat DEF; Weapon Event Wishes; introduced in Version 5.1.
### LARGE +
Passive "Halcyon Years Unending": Normal or Plunging Attacks that hit give "Ode to Flowers" (DEF and an All Elemental DMG Bonus for 6s, up to 2 stacks, once per 0.1s). When it reaches 2 stacks or the 2nd stack is refreshed, nearby party members get an All Elemental DMG Bonus for 15s that scales with every 1,000 DEF the wielder has. Ascension uses Night-Wind's Mystic materials, the Secret Source boss line and Fang common materials.

## roleplay
### LARGE
For scenes: a miner or artisan in the Nanatzcayan could connect the blade's name to the rhythm of hammers and chisels. Speak of it as a sword that sings, not as a relic of a specific hero.
