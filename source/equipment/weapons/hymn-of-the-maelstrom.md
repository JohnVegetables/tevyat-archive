## summary
### LITE
Hymn of the Maelstrom is a 5-star catalyst, an exquisite lamp of blue chalcedony that looks like a treasure from a fairy tale. It is said that a song of praise, forgotten by all, slumbers sealed inside. Its text is a fairy tale a storyteller tells a young Water Imp about a child born of the waves. Added in Version 7.1.
### STANDARD +
The tale ends as a warning to the Water Imps to sing only for those who truly understand their song. The lamp's design and name are not tied to a character in canon.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Canon describes only an exquisite lamp crafted out of blue chalcedony. The tale mentions silk, golden robes and agate in the prince's gifts and a sacred artifact brought by the sisters, but the text does not say that artifact is this lamp.

## lore
### LITE
A young Water Imp who sings alone keeps close a fairy tale from her youth: a child born of the breaking waves, nobler than the daughters of the sea, whose voice made mariners sink into whirlpools and thawed frozen seas.
### STANDARD +
She followed a prince of the land despite her sisters' warnings and her father's call to stay in the sea, sang only for him, and was cherished like a jewel in a jewel-box palace. Her voice grew harsh, she could no longer sing the ancient hymns, and the prince grieved only that she had lost her worth.
### LARGE +
Her sisters rose to shore and urged her to find her voice or turn to foam. Her kin made a blood bargain with the fae of the wind and brought her a sacred artifact, saying the prince's indifference had imprisoned her song and that if his heart spilled blood the artifact would return her to them. She raised the blade above the sleeping prince, her hands trembling, but the strike faded into a sigh, and the ancient hymns were lost in the twilight of the story. The young Water Imp, who had expected a happy ending, asks what the story means, and the storyteller says the Water Imps should sing only for those who truly understand their song.
### ULTRA +
The wiki links the "fae of the wind" in the tale to the Vila. The storyteller says the tale has been told by the Water Imp people for a hundred years, so it is folklore rather than recorded history. The lamp's description about a forgotten hymn is separate from the tale, and the text does not connect the two.

## owners
### LITE
Canon names no owner. The text is about a Water Imp and her tale, not about a possessor.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Game mechanics, not lore: a 5-star catalyst with base ATK 44 and an HP secondary stat. Rondo of Slumber raises Healing Bonus and, after the wielder heals, grants Max HP and a party ATK buff that scales with Max HP above 40,000; Frozen or Stellar Swirl reactions by a nearby party member raise the bonuses further, and the effect works off-field. It was on the weapon event Wish Epitome Invocation (2026-09-23 to 2026-10-13).

## roleplay
### LARGE
This archive's suggested use: a lamp that keeps a story about a voice lost to indifference. Use it for scenes about whom one sings for. Do not give the lamp a wielder or say its sealed hymn is known.
