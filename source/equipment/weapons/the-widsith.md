## summary
### LITE
The Widsith is a four-star catalyst of the Wanderer's Troupe series: a heavy notebook of hand-written musical scores and travel journals, moth-eaten and worn, that the Troupe shared. It records their experiences and still carries power drawn from their performances.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Item text describes a heavy notebook filled with musical scores, suffering moth damage and wear but with much power in the hand-written words.

## lore
### STANDARD
The notebook was passed among the Troupe's members and holds scores and travel journals about the lands they roamed. It siphons power from the records of their performances and the cheering of the crowds. The Troupe's history was lost because it disbanded before Mondstadt was rebuilt.
### LARGE +
The Troupe was founded in the aristocracy's reign. Many called it the Swordsman's Troupe, some from hope and some from fear, in an era that denied even poems and songs freedom. For flutes they had swords and for harps they had bows, and their song was an anthem of rebellion. Their final act was an attempt to storm the city and execute its tyrannical rulers.
### ULTRA +
The text closes by saying the Troupe and the memory of its rebellion are gone, but the spirit of resistance they embodied runs in Mondstadters' veins. The name comes from the Old English poem Widsith ('far traveller'), and the passive's song names (Recitative, Aria, Interlude) are opera terms, per the wiki; these are real-world naming notes, not lore.

## owners
### STANDARD
Shared by the members of the Wanderer's Troupe; no single owner is named.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Game mechanics: Wanderer's Troupe-series four-star catalyst, base ATK 42, CRIT DMG 12% secondary, passive 'Debut' (Recitative/Aria/Interlude buff). Standard and event wishes.
