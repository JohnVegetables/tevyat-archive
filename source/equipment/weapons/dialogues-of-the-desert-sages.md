## summary
### LITE
Dialogues of the Desert Sages is a 4-star polearm: a caduceus symbolising the glory of alchemy, made from special alloys. Its story names the seven sages of King Deshret and tells how the Crocodile King and the Ibis King wagered on a transmutation, with the sage Hermanubis ruling that the two powers must balance.
### STANDARD +
It was a reward of the Version 4.5 event Alchemical Ascension. The description jokes that it is so rigid and heavy that it would hurt as a cudgel.

## appearance
### STANDARD
A caduceus of special alloys, exceedingly rigid and heavy. The description says it symbolises the glory of alchemy.

## lore
@version: 4.5
### LITE
King Deshret's seven sages vanished long ago; their deeds are blown away and only their names, carved in stone, remain, now eroded to golden grains of sand. The text lists four: the Goat King (Heryshaf), right hand of the King and head of the sages; the sun-bound bird that was the King's soul, Bennu; the human-headed lioness that was the King's will, Shesepankh; and the dragon Al-Apep, who obtained the King's flesh and blood and never counted himself among the Seven. These four have nothing to do with the story that follows.
### STANDARD +
The Crocodile King and the Ibis King (Sobek and Thoth) were arguing, as ever, about the changing of things. The Crocodile King produced a man blessed by an Oceanid, through whose hands any liquid became perfect wine. The Ibis King produced a man cursed after touching the tomb of an ancient god, whose touch slowly turned things to pure silver. The text adds that turning things to gold and Mora is the province of Deus Auri alone. They bet on which effect would win and asked Hermanubis, the last of the seven sages, to predict.
### LARGE +
Hermanubis was a desert sage, a warrior and leader of the priestly class, and learned in alchemy, so he was a fitting judge. He said the two powers clash like sharp spears against sturdy shields and each must yield a step: the principle of equilibrium. The result was a cup turned to pure silver and a fine liquor with suspended grains of silver. The two kings failed to take his admonition to heart and kept fighting.
### ULTRA +
In the end the kings devised a strange summoning magic, and the text says they now carry their battle across millennia onto the field of the Genius Invokation TCG, as part of this version of the tale "King of Invokations".

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not in-world): 4-star polearm, level 1 base ATK 42, secondary stat HP 9%. Passive Principle of Equilibrium: when the wielder heals, restore Energy, once every 10 s, even when off-field. Refinement uses the material Exalted Earth from Alchemical Ascension instead of duplicate weapons.
### LARGE +
Energy restored is 8 at refinement 1 and 16 at refinement 5.

## roleplay
### LARGE
Present the Crocodile King and Ibis King as a bickering pair of old rivals, and Hermanubis as measured and learned. Do not treat the wager tale as established history of Sobek and Thoth; the item frames it as a story.
