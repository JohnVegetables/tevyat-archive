## summary
### LITE
Fang of the Mountain King is a 5-star claymore, a giant sword forged from pure turquoise. It is named for Kongamato, the Mountain King, because it is far heavier than ordinary weapons.
### STANDARD +
The item text is less about the blade than about the beast: a Yumkasaur who once protected the tribes and now guards a fire it no longer remembers.

## appearance
### STANDARD
A giant sword of pure turquoise, heavier than ordinary weapons. Canon adds nothing about its shape; keep descriptions to a massive blue-green claymore.

## lore
### LITE
In the heart of Huitztlan sleeps Kongamato, the Mountain King, a creature as vast as the mountains around it. In its half-waking, searing pain it treats everything that approaches as a mortal enemy and attacks without distinction.
### STANDARD +
Tribespeople say it once protected them and was driven mad by the dark disaster of the Cataclysm, yet no one can explain why it only attacks what comes near.
### LARGE +
Its sanity was lost long ago, when its fangs pierced the flesh of Burkina (the wiki names him as its human companion). Legends speak of the person who made pacts with it and hunted at its side, but those memories are gone in the Mountain King's long nightmare, and even the name Kongamato is unfamiliar to the ancient lord of the Yumkasaurs.

Yet it must at least protect the fire, though it knows not whose it is. Its body, once its pride, is wracked by burning agony and bears the marks of unbearable shame, and its soul has been consumed by the filth of darkness. The text says it will not abandon this last shred of resolve.
### ULTRA +
The poem ends with three lines in a speaker's voice: that the Turnfire name will be returned to the Night Realm in exchange for the final kindling, that life marches on by past fuel and present flame, and that all will answer to the fire for their deeds and be reunited in the flames. In the wiki's account of Kinich's story, the Scions of the Canopy periodically drain Kongamato's Abyssal energy in the Turnfire Ritual; during one such ritual it begged to be put out of its misery, and was told by Kinich that it must live with its shame. That fits the item's themes of guilt and duty, but the item text does not mention Kinich.

## owners
### LITE
No wielder is named. The sword is named after Kongamato rather than owned by anyone.
### LARGE +
The only person tied to the legend is Burkina, the former partner of the Mountain King. Do not give the sword to a character without making it a scene invention.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not in-world): a 5-star claymore, base ATK 49 at level 1, secondary stat CRIT Rate; Weapon Event Wishes; introduced in Version 5.0.
### LARGE +
Passive "Turquoise Hunt": hitting an opponent with an Elemental Skill grants a stack of Canopy's Favor (once per 0.5s). When a nearby party member triggers Burning or Burgeon, the wielder gains 3 stacks (once per 2s, even if the trigger is off-field). Each stack raises Elemental Skill and Burst DMG by 10% at refinement 1 up to 20% at refinement 5, for 6s, to a maximum of 6 stacks counted independently. Ascension uses Sacred Lord Delirious materials, the Ignited boss materials and whistle common materials.

## roleplay
### LARGE
For scenes: a Scions of the Canopy speaker would treat the Mountain King with sorrow and respect, not as a mere monster. Avoid giving the sword a manufacturing story; canon only says it is turquoise and named for him.

### STANDARD
Turquoise Hunt is the passive of the claymore Fang of the Mountain King (gameplay): it builds stacks of Canopy's Favor to raise Elemental Skill and Burst damage, with extra stacks from nearby Burning or Burgeon reactions.

## entry: Kongamato
@keys: Kongamato, Mountain King
@keys2: Yumkasaur, Huitztlan, Turnfire
### LARGE
Kongamato, the Mountain King, is a gigantic Yumkasaur of Huitztlan, archived as the Gluttonous Yumkasaur Mountain King. It has lived since the Cataclysm, fought the Abyss at the side of its human companion Burkina, and was severely afflicted by Abyssal energy, part of which the Scions of the Canopy drain in their Turnfire Ritual. It roused when its territory was disturbed and appears in a world-boss fight; in the wiki's account it resonates with Kinich as an heir to the Turnfire.
