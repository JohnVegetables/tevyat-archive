## summary
### LITE
The Royal Spear is a four-star polearm of the Royal series. It was kept in the collection of a Mondstadt noble, remains as sharp as the day it was forged, and in the aristocracy's own time never left the dark. Its story contrasts noble sword-fighting with the spear as a commoner's weapon.

## appearance
### STANDARD
The text mentions only that the spear is premium work and that the legendary one was emblazoned with a noble house's crest. It bathed only in pale moonlight in the old days.

## lore
### STANDARD
The old nobles held that people of high blood fought with swords and that clashing blades were the chorus of lofty souls, whereas spears and bows were for plebeian gladiators and commoners. Commoners with pitchforks and sharpened poles could still threaten sword-wielding scions, which the rulers of Old Mondstadt found hard to accept, so the spear was kept out of sight.
### LARGE +
A legend tells of a noble youth who asked a smith to make this weapon, marked with the house crest, and who, like the weapon, never earned the family's favour without bloodshed. Wanting change, the youth chose power even through an ignoble weapon, accepting that the spear-wielding shadow would only ever be seen by moonlight.
### ULTRA +
The wiki links the youth to Eberhart, a figure of the same age of rebellion against the aristocracy. The item text never names them, so this tie is recorded in uncertainties. The spear thus pairs with the Royal Longsword and Royal Grimoire as another view of the old nobility's blind spots.

## owners
### STANDARD
No named owner. The legend refers only to a noble youth.
### LARGE +
Rosaria is shown wielding the base version in her character demo, Collected Miscellany and the Version 1.4 trailer, and an ascended one in a birthday artwork; that is presentation, not a canon ownership claim.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Game mechanics: Royal-series passive 'Focus'. Four-star polearm, base ATK 44, ATK 6% secondary. Obtained from Paimon's Bargains; released in Version 1.1.
