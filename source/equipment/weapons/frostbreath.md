## summary
### LITE
Frostbreath is a four-star polearm in the Gnostic Hymn 3 series, added in version 7.0 as a Battle Pass weapon. Its in-game description calls it a spear of solid ice imbued with a chill, said to have belonged to the Belyi Tsar who ruled Snezhnaya long ago. Its story follows the young Monomakh, trained by his master, who asked whether stronger spears exist.

## appearance
### STANDARD
The description gives only "a spear of solid ice imbued with an icy chill". The item has a base and a second-ascension look in the game, but the text describes no further detail such as shaft, tip or decoration.

## lore
### STANDARD
The story opens with the young Monomakh asking his master whether stronger spears exist, during training so harsh that even a fae could hardly imagine it. He is described as lean as a white-barked birch and gasping on the ice. Looking at him, the crimson princess of the snow, whose eyes had survived the ruin of the golden city, answers that a spear once existed that could shoot down the Lord of the Sky. She tells him he cannot do it, since he cannot yet even defeat her, and that he asked only whether stronger spears exist. He says he would like to learn it so as to protect more people, and she tells him to firm up his stance.
### LARGE +
The years pass and war sets the far north ablaze. Monomakh, who swore to become king, keeps honing the spear and throws it like a silver star at the Water Imp in a frozen lake, the bull Skoldyryt in the forest and the mountain-sized beast Miscislav. The text calls this his supreme spear art, taught by the Knyazhna of Blood who had slaughtered countless fae in an earlier age, and notes that it differs somewhat from her personal teaching. He becomes emperor and unifies the northern realms; fae and mortals alike sing his praises. A deliberately fawning passage lists his titles (conqueror of countless winters, most merciful father).
### ULTRA +
Yet he cannot reproduce what his master described, a spear that crossed countless snowfields at once; hurling his polearm like an arrow carries it only about half of Snezhnaya, and he blames his own lack of strength, trusting that his master would never lie. Thousands of winters later he visits a workshop where artisans mass-produce muskets from steel and Prime Ice, and falls silent. The story ends on his realisation that his master never said the golden city's spears were thrown. The reader is left to infer that she meant something other than a thrown spear (the text links the idea to the Arrow of Terpikeraunas; the item itself never names the weapon).

## owners
### STANDARD
Per the short description, the Belyi Tsar who ruled Snezhnaya in the distant past once owned it. The story's protagonist, Monomakh, is the only wielder shown, and the item text links him to the same ruler; his teacher is the Knyazhna of Blood (Yalyane Morevna).

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not lore): four-star polearm, obtained from the Battle Pass (Gnostic Hymn). Base ATK 42, secondary stat Energy Recharge 10.0%. Passive "A Cast Real Far": triggering a Cryo- or Hydro-related reaction raises the wielder's ATK for 15 seconds and restores Elemental Energy to the other party members, once per 16 seconds.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay use (this project's suggestion, not canon text): a northern soldier or scholar might know the polearm as a relic of the old Tsar. The tale's irony, a king who never doubted his teacher and learned too late what she had actually said, makes it a good prop for stories about misunderstood advice or loyalty.
