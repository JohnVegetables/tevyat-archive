## summary
### LITE
Windblume Ode is a four-star bow obtained during the limited-time Invitation of Windblume event (Version 1.4). It is adorned with nameless flowers and carries the hopes of an equally nameless person. Its item text recounts the Windblume as a flower of resistance in the days of the tyrant in the tower.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Item text describes only a bow adorned with nameless flowers; the wiki ties these to the Windblume Festival's flower. No further design is stated.

## lore
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
In Mondstadt's childhood dreams there was a floaty flower that grew lushly amid mighty winds, harsh winters and whipping ice, whose roots grew stronger as the wind grew wilder. Today the common people's rebellion against tyranny has been turned into festive jest and the flower's shape is faint in the lyre-tines of old histories. The text records a greeting: one offers the nameless flower and wishes the other hope and a smile, to stand together until the storms stop. The weapon was obtained during a limited-time event.
### LARGE +
In the days when a tyrant gazed down from his tower, those who nursed freedom used this as a secret sign. A lonely flower bloomed with the wind and spread over the wild mountains, and rootless subjects became fearless heroes while the frowning lord in the tower, Decarabian, shrank and could not stop the tide. Atop the ruins of the ancient tower a red-haired warrior turned his back on the newborn god, first among those who passed the secret sign; his name is lost but his deeds are sung.
### ULTRA +
A thousand years later, another red-haired knight (the wiki links this to Ragnvindr) followed in his footsteps and brought light to the nobility's dark history, and the text says the clan will always live in darkness and bring forth the flame of dawn. The ending notes that in peace the Windblume now stands for love and gladness, and quotes a wish that the flowers signal love and spring, no longer rebellion or raised banners.

## owners
### STANDARD
No individual owner is named; the text refers to a nameless person who bears the hope.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Game mechanics: four-star bow of the Festival series, base ATK 42, Elemental Mastery 36 secondary, passive 'Windblume Wish'. Obtained from the Invitation of Windblume event (limited availability); refined with 'The Visible Winds'.
