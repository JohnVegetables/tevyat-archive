## summary
### LITE
Akuoumaru is a four-star claymore, part of the Watatsumi series, and the beloved sword of the Watatsumi warrior known to his foes as "Akuou". Item text calls the blade huge and majestic yet surprisingly easy to wield. It belonged to a young man whom the people of Watatsumi named Touzannou, a champion of Orobashi's war against the Raiden Shogun's forces.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Item text only says the blade is huge and majestic and easy to handle despite its size. It gives no further description of its shape or decoration, so avoid inventing any.

## lore
### STANDARD
The item story calls the weapon the blade of a mighty Watatsumi general who knew only the self-taught "Getsumouun" and "Yuushio" styles, yet was never beaten by a mortal in duel or battle. When Orobashi (Omikami) led Watatsumi to war against the gathering storm clouds, this young man stood out; his fearless valour won him great love at home and the name Touzannou. That name was later forgotten and replaced by the epithet his enemies used: Akuou, the wicked fangs and claws of the Great Serpent, the savage invader of Yashiori Island.
### LARGE +
The slaughter hardened him into a warrior, but only one thing stayed eternal for him: a wish made before departing, upon the moon reflected in the water beside the shrine. He wished to stand above Mt. Yougou overlooking the palace of the Lord of Thunder, to have a joyful showdown with the legendary Great Tengu of Yougou on the roof of Tenshukaku, and to bring the tengu's mask back to Ayame and Mouun as a souvenir.

The ending is bleak. The tides washed the dreams away like towers in the sand. The tengu's mask, shining like a crimson star, was shattered amid the chaotic battle; the shrine maiden who had shone into his soul like moonlight never came back; and Akuou himself would meet the blinding ray of lightning alongside the Great Serpent. The wiki links that last image to Musou no Hitotachi. The same war is told from other sides in the companion Watatsumi items: Mouun's Moon (the shrine maiden Mouun) and Wavebreaker's Fin (Ayame, "Umigozen"), whose text says the "vassal ruler, determined as a child" vanished into a rift torn in the earth.
### ULTRA +
The wiki's links identify the "Great Tengu of Yougou" as Sasayuri. Mouun's Moon preserves the other half of the promise as banter: a young man says that once he brings the legendary Great Tengu's mask back she must keep their agreement, and she answers that if he is still so full of nonsense she will have a whale raise a wave to wash his mouth clean. Mouun's Moon also says she rode with Touzannou on the back of a sea creature at night. These exchanges are item flavour text, not quest dialogue. Together the texts present Touzannou/Akuou as young, reckless and sincere, loved at home, and feared elsewhere under a demonic title that was not how his own people saw him.

## owners
### STANDARD
Original owner: the Watatsumi warrior Touzannou, called Akuou (historical.akuou), described by item text as a vassal ruler and vanguard of Watatsumi's forces. No later owner is named; the Traveler may use it as an ordinary player weapon.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not lore): four-star claymore from the Watatsumi series, obtainable from weapon event wishes. Base ATK 42, secondary stat ATK 9%. Passive "Watatsumi Wavewalker": for every point of the party's combined maximum Energy capacity, the wielder's Elemental Burst DMG rises by 0.12% at refinement 1 up to 0.24% at refinement 5, to a cap of 40% at refinement 1 up to 80% at refinement 5. A character's maximum Energy is the cost of their burst. The passive is shared with Mouun's Moon and Wavebreaker's Fin.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay use (this project's suggestion, not canon text): the sword suits a story about a loved hero remembered by his enemies as a monster. Few people outside Watatsumi would know the name Touzannou; elsewhere the name "Akuou" carries the Shogunate-side memory of the war.

## entry: Akuou
@keys: Akuou, Touzannou
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Akuou is the epithet by which the Raiden Shogun's side remembered a Watatsumi warrior whom his own people called Touzannou. The item story presents him as a fearless young vassal of Orobashi, whose personal wish was a duel with the Great Tengu of Yougou and bringing the tengu's mask back to the shrine maidens Ayame and Mouun. The text does not say how old he was beyond calling him a young man.
