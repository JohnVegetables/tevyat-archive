## summary
### LITE
Skyward Harp is a five-star bow of the Mondstadt Skyward series, released in 1.0. The item calls it the sky-piercing lyre: its story is how Barbatos' lyre drew Dvalin to him, held back the shadow dragon, and then went unheard by a poisoned Dvalin.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Described as a greatbow whose sound when fired is music to the Anemo Archon's ears. The text gives no further look.

## lore
### STANDARD
The clear tune of the lyre still echoes in the hearts of the people and in the winds, and it is said the ancient evil dragon from the abyss was drawn there by it. In ancient times Barbatos softly strummed his lyre and summoned the pure thousand winds and songs. Charmed by the free winds and songs, the high dragon Dvalin descended and swore loyalty. Barbatos, glad of a new friend, entrusted the people of Mondstadt to Dvalin, and the Archon and the Wind Dragon together forged Mondstadt's dawn.
### LARGE +
In the final battle the lyre's floating tunes halted the shadow dragon's attack and gave Dvalin the chance to end its poisonous reign. Long afterwards Dvalin awoke to find his master gone and heard only the poisonous whispers of the Abyss Order. Unseen pain and unheard torture crushed his spirit.
### ULTRA +
Dvalin turned on what he had vowed to protect and cursed the one he had sworn loyalty to, crying that Barbatos was indifferent to his pain, a careless friend and the cruellest of the Archons. The text says the furious friend did not know that Barbatos was trying to save him, and that many memories may have been crushed by Dvalin's anguish but not that of the lyre, the Holy Lyre der Himmel. It closes expectantly: centuries of misunderstanding will soon pass, and Dvalin will hear his songs once more.

## owners
### STANDARD
The item names no wielder. Per the wiki's trivia, Venti wields the bow in his Character Demo and a Collected Miscellany video, and in the Dvalin fight of the Prologue (A Long Shot) he uses it to help the Traveler free Dvalin from the Abyss Order's control. Tartaglia used it as a trial character in an early Story Quest, since replaced by the Polar Star.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not lore): five-star bow from the standard Wanderlust Invocation and weapon event wishes. Base ATK 48, secondary stat CRIT Rate 4.8%. Passive "Echoing Ballad": raises CRIT DMG (20-40%); hits have a 60-100% chance of a small AoE dealing 125% Physical ATK DMG, at most once every 4 seconds at refinement 1, down to every 2 seconds at refinement 5.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay use (this project's suggestion, not canon text): a weapon about friends who misunderstand each other. The text only says Dvalin will soon hear the songs again; it does not show the reconciliation.
