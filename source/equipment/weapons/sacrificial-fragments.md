## summary
### LITE
Sacrificial Fragments is a 4-star catalyst in the Sacrificial series from Mondstadt. Its description calls it a weathered script whose text is no longer legible, a cursed item eroded by the winds of time. Present since Version 1.0.
### STANDARD +
The Sacrificial series are props from early Mondstadt's wind-worship rituals; the wiki notes this script is the only one described as cursed and with no clan named.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Canon describes only a weathered script whose text is illegible. As a catalyst it is shown as a book or script; no further detail is given.

## lore
### LITE
Early Mondstadt built theaters atop windy cliffs and performed rituals as stories and ballads, because they believed the gods enjoyed stories.
### STANDARD +
The script is millennia old and now illegible. A long-ago war between the Lord of Storm and the Great Wolf King of the North buried Mondstadt in blizzards; a group who could not bear the cold built a clifftop shrine in the east to pray for mercy. Later generations presumed the shrine had always been for the wind alone.
### LARGE +
The text says the wind blows for a moment, but the ravages of time are constant, unrelenting and irreversible. A god of the winds may move between pages of a book, but the merciless god of time will eat away at them until no legible word remains. Yet time's assault and the wind's often take their toll on the heart alike, which is perhaps why people forgot the shrine's older purpose.
### ULTRA +
The wiki reads the series as relics of the Thousand Winds Temple, associated with the new Anemo Archon's rise and with Istaroth, and notes that the Sacrificial Sword belonged to the Gunnhildr Clan, the Greatsword to the Imunlaukr Clan and the Bow to the Lawrence Clan, while Fragments names no clan. That interpretation is the wiki's reading of the item texts. The Lord of Storm in the text is Decarabian; the Great Wolf King of the North is Boreas, whose later Nod-Krai role is covered elsewhere.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Game mechanics, not lore: a 4-star catalyst, base ATK 41, Elemental Mastery secondary stat (48). Composed gives a chance for an Elemental Skill that damages an opponent to end its own cooldown, with a cooldown on the effect. It can be obtained from Wishes, including the standard Wanderlust Invocation and weapon event Wishes.
