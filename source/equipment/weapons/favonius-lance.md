## summary
### LITE
Favonius Lance is a 4-star polearm of the Favonius series, a spear of honor among the Knights of Favonius. It is used in parades and reviews yet is also a keen foe of monsters. Its text recounts how spears came to Mondstadt and how the noble Eberhart took one up.
### STANDARD +
The shaft is straight and the tip flows lightly like the wind. It is the weapon that tells of spear use in Mondstadt, from commoners' stakes to a noble's secret training.

## appearance
### STANDARD
A polearm in the Knights of Favonius' style: a straight shaft and a tip described as flowing lightly like the wind.

## lore
### LITE
The text calls it a crystallization of the work and artistry of Mondstadt's defenders. By studying the arbor that held firm in the wind, the city's craftsmen and scholars learned much about elemental affinities. In hand, it reminds the Knights to be disciplined and to defend Mondstadt's freedom.
### STANDARD +
Polearms of a kind have long been the weapon of those who lack martial training: even untrained commoners with sharpened wooden sticks might match trained soldiers' steel. To celebrate the overthrow of the aristocracy, long stakes, flagpoles and pitchforks were stuck all over the greenery around the city. Nobles were once required to study the sword, and the spear was left to foreign gladiators and traitors.
### LARGE +
One noble scion nonetheless wielded a spear: Eberhart, said to enjoy the night breeze while catching the first dew on his spear tip. An illegitimate child, he dreamed of recapturing the glory of ancient nobility and needed power to destroy its rotten foundations. The text offers two possible routes: inciting his legitimate brother to pursue a phantom thief's dream and then deceiving him into fleeing so that he could take the legitimacy, or pledging himself to a spear-wielding witch as a disciple to learn the fatal loophole in her art in order to slay her. The brother is linked by the wiki to Parsifal.
### ULTRA +
The entry ends on Eberhart's own defiant line that he will do anything for his goals even if later generations despise him. In a scene, present the two routes as alternatives, as the text does, and attribute any firm claim to the wiki's reading rather than the item.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not in-world): 4-star polearm, base ATK 44, secondary stat Energy Recharge 6.7%, obtainable from Wishes including the standard banner. Passive Windfall: CRIT hits have a chance (60% to 100% by refinement) to generate a small amount of Elemental Particles, restoring 6 Energy, once per 12 s at refinement 1 down to once per 6 s at refinement 5.

## entry: Eberhart's spear
@keys: Eberhart
@keys2: spear, Favonius Lance
@version: 1.0
### LARGE
Eberhart was the one noble known to wield a spear in the aristocratic age, an illegitimate child who dreamed of restoring ancient nobility. The Favonius Lance text leaves open whether he betrayed his legitimate brother, trained under a spear-witch in order to kill her, or both.
