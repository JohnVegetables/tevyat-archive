## summary
### LITE
The Unforged is a five-star claymore of the Liyue series, an edgeless blade whose description says it can drive away evil spirits and wicked people. Its item story is a legend of an ancient stone dragon in Liyue, and says the sword was planted as a seal beside the tree that subdued it.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Item text calls it an edgeless claymore that seems to possess divine might. The wiki connects the name (Sword of No Work, from the idiom about a heavy sword being edgeless) to the idea of power without showy technique.

## lore
### STANDARD
Long ago a dragon lived in Liyue, one of the mountains rather than the sky: an ancient dragon of stone as large as its home. Legend says it slept in Nantianmen, looking like the hills, and its smallest stretch shook the earth. Rex Lapis came before it to stop the quaking. The land then knew a long peace, and he gained another comrade.

The union of dragons, gods and men did not last. The dragon was sealed in the earth, and the Geovishaps that had caused havoc beside it fled to the roots of the mountains for fear of the gods and adepti. As millennia passed, the dragon began to stir again.
### LARGE +
Legend says those who triumphed over the dragon stabbed this sword into the ground by the giant tree, the Dragon-Queller, which subdued it. As one of the seals, it keeps monsters and malevolent mortals from touching it, and only those with hearts like clear mirrors can approach. The text ends by asking how, if so, the sword came to be lost. The wiki trivia identifies the dragon as Azhdaha.

### ULTRA +
The item text never names the dragon, the tree or the sword's finders; the wiki identifies the dragon as Azhdaha and calls the tree the Dragon-Queller. The structure of the story is a legend with a hole at the end: it claims the sword is a seal that only the pure of heart can approach, then asks how it was lost, so portray the sword's present location as unknown.

## owners
### STANDARD
No owner is named. Legend places the sword at the Dragon-Queller as a seal.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Game mechanics, not lore. The passive Golden Majesty raises Shield Strength and stacks ATK on hits, with a larger bonus while shielded. It shares this passive with Memory of Dust. Obtained from Weapon Event Wishes; added in Version 1.1.
