## summary
### LITE
Lost Prayer to the Sacred Winds is a 5-star catalyst: an educational tome by anonymous early worshippers of the wind, blessed by the wind for its faithfulness over the millennia. Its text follows the scripture through Mondstadt's ages and ends with a farewell note from a believer who joins the thousand winds. Added in Version 1.0.
### STANDARD +
Item text calls it scriptures of the ancient winds, passed from generation to generation among observers of ritual who served the Anemo Archon, its yellowing pages touched by countless hands whose prints the wind has carried away.

## appearance
### STANDARD
A book with yellowed pages and an elegantly handwritten title page, thicker with every era. Canon gives no cover or size.

## lore
### LITE
Under the Storm Lord, who reigned from his tower, the scriptures filled with desperate appeals. In the era when the sands of snow thawed, the joy of sprouting greenery flooded the pages.
### STANDARD +
Under the ruling aristocracy, who pulled puppet strings to control the masses, the scriptures in the hands of slaves told of their envy and their desire for the thousand winds. As the people lived, endured, fought, rejoiced and enjoyed freedom, the scriptures grew thicker and heavier.
### LARGE +
When Mondstadt was born anew and the Church was finally unshackled, the book could no longer bear to stay on a shelf, took flight and left the Church's treasury. It belonged, like the winds and like the people of Mondstadt, to freedom and the winds.
### ULTRA +
The title page carries a farewell from a believer: "From the winds we have come, and with the winds we shall go. Never, ever grieve for me," because only the flesh rests in the soil while the soul becomes one with the thousand winds. Where flowers bloom and leaves sway, the writer says, it is that soul singing songs of freedom. The text is a poetic book-history and never names the Church officials or wind spirits involved.

## owners
### STANDARD
No owner is named in canon. The book is said to have left the Church of Favonius's treasury on its own; no one has been shown holding it.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not literal lore): a 5-star catalyst with base ATK 46 and a CRIT Rate secondary stat. Its passive, Boundless Blessing, raises Movement SPD by 10 per cent and, in battle, adds an Elemental DMG Bonus every 4 seconds (up to four stacks), lasting until the character falls or leaves combat. It is on the Standard Wish and weapon event Wishes.
