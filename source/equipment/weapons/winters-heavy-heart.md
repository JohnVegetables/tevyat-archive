## summary
### LITE
Winter's Heavy Heart is a four-star catalyst in the Fae series, added in version 7.1. The in-game description calls it a ceremonial artifact formed from the purest ice and snow, which generations of Snegovik lords used in coronations until the Cataclysm five hundred years ago. Its story is about the first Snegovik, a blood-soaked ruler who did not know what love was, and the youth she trained.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Only "a ceremonial artifact formed from the purest ice and snow" is given. The text does not describe its shape beyond that.

## lore
### STANDARD
Snegovik are said to have been born with dawn's first gleam from virgin frost. They were more desolate than winter, and only love could mend them. A handmaiden of the Azurelight woke from deathlike sleep still carrying the warmth of her former lord; the Mother of Dawn had lifted her curse so her kind need not be bound to fate. But the golden city (Hyperborea) fell, and its survivors wandered the frozen wastes devouring one another. She resolved that blood must answer blood and that she alone would protect her lord's descendants.
### LARGE +
The first of the Snegovik, who had never known love, purged even Snegovik who shared her hatred of mankind and opened her frozen domain, the House of Hesperides, to mortal clans driven from their homes. Yet the frightened people who looked at her showed no love. The Volkodlak king of Cirnareka came to beg a truce for the fae of his kind, and she asked him what love is. He answered that love is fear, and that she kept mortals through fear just as he did, and she killed him.
### ULTRA +
Long afterwards a young man lost in the snow reached her realm with a company of exiles; she recognised his lineage and took him as her pupil. When she asked what he thought love was, he replied that it is bearing each other's burdens and granting forgiveness, as she had taught, and she struck the stone by his feet with a silver flash as he spoke. He then left to begin the journey remembered in songs and stories. Once the noise she had tired of faded, her longing faded too; her Lady and Mother had freed her from her bond to destiny but she had found no other tie to the world. For the first time she felt a fear deeper than any before, that without bloodshed even she would forget the dream her mother had cherished, so she believed slaughter must continue.

## owners
### STANDARD
No wielder is named. The short description says the Snegovik lords used it in coronation ceremonies until the Cataclysm, five hundred years ago.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not lore): four-star catalyst, Fae series, from weapon event wishes. Base ATK 42, secondary stat CRIT DMG 12%. Passive "Secrets of Frost": the wielder gains Elemental Mastery for each Cryo party member and ATK for each Electro party member (up to four each). In a Stellar Glimmer Radiance state the effect changes to Elemental Mastery and Stellar Glimmer reaction DMG per Cryo or Electro member.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay use (this project's suggestion, not canon text): a Snezhnayan fae scholar might cite it as a Snegovik regalia. The story fits scenes about cruelty born of grief and fear, and a teacher who asks what love is.

### STANDARD
In this item's story the first of the Snegovik is a scarlet ruler called the Knyazhna of Blood and, in the text, the blood-soaked Snegurochka and "mad queen", formerly the Azurelight's handmaiden. Freed from her curse by the Mother of Dawn, she purges the fae who hate mankind and shelters mortal clans in her frozen domain, yet cannot find love in their eyes.
### LARGE +
She fears that without bloodshed she will forget her mother's dream, as a soul with no bond to the world dissolves like fading snow.

She asked him what love is; he answered that it is fear, which mortals and fae use to bind each other, and that there is no reason for them to be enemies. He did not finish before she killed him.
