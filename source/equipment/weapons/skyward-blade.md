## summary
### LITE
Skyward Blade is a five-star sword of the Mondstadt Skyward series, released in 1.0. It is the sword of a knight and symbolises the restored honour of Dvalin, with the Anemo Archon's blessing on its fuller. Its story is Dvalin's fight with the shadow dragon Durin and the poison that followed.

## appearance
### STANDARD
The text puts the Archon's blessing on the fuller of the blade and calls it "the sky-piercing fang". No other visual detail is given.

## lore
### STANDARD
The story opens with a fang that pierced the black-gold scales of the abyssal kingdom and cut the throats of the sons of ancient sinners. Mondstadt was threatened by the shadow dragon Durin, whose jealousy of the city's prosperity bred evil and poisoned the land. Hearing the people cry, the Anemo Archon descended and woke Dvalin, who rose into the sky to fight for all life.
### LARGE +
Blessed by the Archon, Dvalin fought Durin; clouds broke and the sky burned red as if the end had come. Dvalin's blessed fangs pierced Durin's throat and Durin fell, but Dvalin was poisoned by the tainted blood and fell unconscious from the pain.
### ULTRA +
Centuries passed and Dvalin's sacrifice was nearly forgotten. When he awoke, his friend and the comfort of his lyre were gone, and those he had protected cowered at the sight of him. The text closes as a prayer: honour reclaimed, poison cleansed, ballads giving a people their forgotten memory, that Dvalin's name may be restored. The item only frames the restoration as a hope; the Prologue Archon Quest is where it plays out in the game.

## owners
### STANDARD
No wielder is named; the sword stands for Dvalin's honour. The Traveler is shown wielding it in the cinematic of the limited-time event Moonlight Merriment Part II, per the wiki's trivia.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not lore): five-star sword from the standard Wanderlust Invocation and weapon event wishes. Base ATK 46, secondary stat Energy Recharge 12%. Passive "Sky-Piercing Fang": raises CRIT Rate (4-8%); after an Elemental Burst you gain Skypiercing Might for 12 seconds (Movement SPD +10%, ATK SPD +10%, Normal and Charged hits deal extra damage of 20-40% ATK).

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay use (this project's suggestion, not canon text): a symbol of redeemed reputation. Do not say the blade is Dvalin's own fang; the text says it symbolises him.

### STANDARD
The Skyward weapons retell the Cataclysm-era battle from different angles. In the Blade's version Dvalin, blessed by the Archon, pierces Durin's throat, is poisoned by the blood, falls unconscious and is forgotten.
