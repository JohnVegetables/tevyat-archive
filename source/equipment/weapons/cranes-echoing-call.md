## summary
### LITE
Crane's Echoing Call is a five-star catalyst of Liyue, described in game as a fan carved from pure jade with an ancient aura of adeptal energy swirling around it. Its item story tells of a distant time before Liyue Harbor existed, when refugees begged the adepti for shelter while a horde of maddened monsters pursued them, and how the adeptus Cloud Retainer is said to have saved them. Xianyun, who is Cloud Retainer, wields it.
### STANDARD +
The story gives three competing versions of the rescue, and the speaker at the end teasingly refuses to say which is true.

## appearance
### LARGE
The description names only a fan of pure jade ringed by adeptal energy. The story's adeptus appears with azure and white feathers, as jade-carved cranes, or with a folding fan in hand, depending on the version. Xianyun's own crane form, described in her profile, is not described in the item text.

## lore
### STANDARD
In the distant past, before the harbor city existed, even the adepti's abodes were burned by the war of the gods. Survivors who lost everything fled to the mountains and begged the adepti for shelter, hounded by countless monsters that had lost their senses. These monsters came like a flood and were, in truth, mourning the fallen master they had once revered, the Mistress of Chenyu Vale. Only a few yaksha stood between the refugees and catastrophe, fighting for days until white clothes turned scarlet and scarlet turned black.
### LARGE +
The story says the tale has since branched into versions. In one, an adeptus with azure and white feathers burst from the clouds like a gale and, solitary and proud, vanquished the apparently endless tide. In another, two jade cranes hurtled down in a hail of sigils, then turned out to be an adeptus in the air with a folding fan. In a third, the ancestors only heard a crane's cry and the monsters turned to ash like dust in sunlight. All versions credit Cloud Retainer, and Liyue folk choose their words about her with respect.
### ULTRA +
The close shifts to a spoken scene: a character addresses a listener who asked about "such distant matters", says the tale would take eight or nine hours, tells them to pull up a chair, and then scolds them for trying to slink away while distracted. The voice is not named in the text; the pattern of speech fits Cloud Retainer, who refers to herself as "one", but treat that as an inference. For Xianyun's own history (her work with machines and her crane form) use her file.

## owners
### STANDARD
Wielder: Xianyun (Cloud Retainer), shown with the ascended version in her character demo and Collected Miscellany. The item text does not name an owner.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not lore): a five-star catalyst from weapon event wishes. Base ATK 49, secondary stat ATK 3.6%. Passive "Cloudfall Axiom": after the wielder lands a Plunging Attack, all nearby party members' Plunging Attacks deal 28-80% more DMG for 20 seconds, and their Plunging hits restore the wielder 2.5-3.5 Energy every 0.7 seconds, even off the field (refinements 1 to 5). The wiki lists its base ATK among the highest in the game.

### STANDARD
Cloud Retainer is an adeptus of Mt. Aocang and Jueyun Karst, said in legend to have rescued refugees from a monster tide long before Liyue Harbor. She is also known as Xianyun in the mortal world and can take a crane form. The item story gives three versions of the rescue and does not decide between them.
