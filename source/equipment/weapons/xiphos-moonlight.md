## summary
### LITE
Xiphos' Moonlight is a 4-star sword: an ancient double-edged blade that glimmers with moonlight, said to hold a now-silent Jinni. Its story is that of Xiphos, a warrior of Tulaytullah, and the exiled wanderer who killed him.
### STANDARD +
It belongs to the Tulaytullah series and was released in Version 3.1 through weapon event Wishes. The Jinni whispered to its master in the story; the passive name Jinni's Whisper refers to it.

## appearance
### STANDARD
A double-edged sword whose blade glimmers with moonlight, said to be sharpened rather than dulled by slaughter and brightened by blood, in the story's words.

## lore
@version: 3.1
### LITE
Thousands of years ago, after the foolish God-King was buried by sandstorms, mortal cities dotted the land. The most outstanding was Tulaytullah, where men and women of wisdom gathered, with sapphire-domed buildings and emerald gardens of blooming trees and fountains. A warrior named Xiphos lived there, adept with his double-edged sword.
### STANDARD +
He saved many maidens from monster attacks and took many treasures in the process. In ancient Tighnarian lore his sword was indwelt by a twisted, cruel Jinni, which is why the blade stayed sharp and bright with blood. The Jinni addresses its master in sinuous verse, offering its affection and promising he will be an unmatched warrior as long as his foes still love the world.
### LARGE +
Xiphos later met an exiled foreign wanderer who had accepted silver coins, blood money from kings opposed to Tulaytullah, and so shouldered their grudges. The two, who might have been friends at some bar, fought to the death in moonlight. The pale moon lit the winner and the defeated alike, and a saying notes that the three departed goddesses had determined the heroes' fate long before.
### ULTRA +
The wanderer took the moonlit sword and the bloodstained silver and walked silently toward the rainforest. Without Xiphos' protection, Tulaytullah lost its luster and withered; its gardens were ground into dirt and the desert gales consigned them to oblivion. The black key and the young prince's quest to rejuvenate his kingdom also sank into the sand.

## owners
### LITE
In the story the sword belonged to Xiphos and then to the exiled wanderer who killed him. The wiki notes Nilou, Layla and Alhaitham are shown with it in trailers and quest scenes (Alhaitham in his fight with Cyno), which is not ownership.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not in-world): 4-star sword, level 1 base ATK 42, secondary stat Elemental Mastery 36. Passive Jinni's Whisper: every 10 s the wielder gains Energy Recharge for each point of Elemental Mastery for 12 s, with nearby party members gaining 30% of it; it works off-field and stacks across copies.
### LARGE +
The bonus is 0.036% Energy Recharge per point of Elemental Mastery at refinement 1 and 0.072% at refinement 5.

## roleplay
### LARGE
Treat the Jinni as a silent, possibly malign presence in a legend; do not give it speech beyond the story's quoted lines.
