## summary
### LITE
Hunter's Path is a 5-star bow of the Sumeru series, a golden hunting bow made of precious white branches. Its description says it has felled countless wicked beasts trying to infiltrate the woods. The story is about a huntress who pursued a dark herd so long that she forgot the human tongue and became a beast herself.
### STANDARD +
The bow also appears, as a moon-white bow in the hands of a huntress, in the Light of Foliar Incision's text, tying the two weapons into one tale of the forest.

## appearance
### STANDARD
A gilded bow of white branches containing "the blessing of the forest". The text adds that trees with such white branches are now rare and that the bow's blessings were once covered by darkened blood that water has since washed away.

## lore
### LITE
A hunter pursues a dark herd in a hunt that never seems to end, waiting nights under rotten leaves and sleeping inside carcasses, always moving to the next target after the fatal arrow. She stops caring about the wind revealing her location or about camouflaging her scent with wildflowers, as her smell becomes like that of wild beasts. She forgot the human tongue before she became a hunter, and even the blind youth who first found her and gave her the white-branch bow was forgotten.
### STANDARD +
She is told that the bloodstained will never find the hunting grounds of endless viridescence, and replies that the world where barbarous beasts roam shall be her hunting grounds. Looking into clear water by moonlight, she sees that she too has turned into a wild beast. A black knight, whose form and sword are reflected in the water, follows the tracks of the beast and finds her at a loss. He says he took her for another beast to destroy, tempted by the illusory moon in the water, though for a moment he thought he saw a maiden lost in the forest, and states that he must continue west to settle the crime of twisting man into a beast's form.
### LARGE +
The black knight is Roland by the wiki's annotation, and the "beast's form" he speaks of refers to the hilichurls. The text does not say what becomes of the huntress. The same white bow is described in Light of Foliar Incision as held by a huntress who drove an exiled prince through the forest's maze and spared him, which the wiki reads as the same huntress.
### ULTRA +
Tone: the item moves from praise of the bow to a lament. The change is the central point of the story, a hunter slowly becoming what she hunts. When using it in a scene, avoid giving the huntress a name; canon does not.

## owners
### LITE
No owner is named. The huntress is the only wielder in the text.
### LARGE +
The wiki reads the Light of Foliar Incision text as showing her later, still wielding the moon-white bow in Dharma Forest. No present-day character is established as owning it.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not in-world): 5-star bow released in Version 3.0 (2022-08-24), level 1 base ATK 44, secondary stat CRIT Rate. Passive At the End of the Beast-Paths grants All Elemental DMG Bonus and, after the wielder hits with a Charged Attack, the Tireless Hunt effect, which raises Charged Attack DMG by a percentage of Elemental Mastery for 12 Charged Attacks or 10 s; it can be gained once every 12 s.
### LARGE +
At refinement 1 the Elemental DMG Bonus is 12% and Tireless Hunt adds 160% of Elemental Mastery; refinement 5 gives 24% and 320%. Ascension uses Inactivated, Dormant and Robust Fungal Nucleus with Faded Red Satin, Trimmed Red Silk and Rich Red Brocade.

## roleplay
### LARGE
For scenes: speak of the bow as a relic of the rainforest and of a forgotten huntress. Do not turn the huntress into a named, speaking character unless the user supplies one.
