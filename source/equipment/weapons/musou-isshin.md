## summary
### LITE
Musou Isshin is the legendary sword of the Raiden twins. It was created from Makoto's divine might and kept unsharpened and unused while she lived, as a symbol of true peace. When Makoto died in the Cataclysm it passed to her sister Ei, who began to use it in battle. It is the blade the Raiden Shogun draws from her heart during her Elemental Burst.
### STANDARD +
The name means roughly "a dream of one heart". Makoto gave the sword its name and wished it to witness an Inazuma as lovely as a dream, and the noble hearts of the people in it. Ei kept the name.

## appearance
### STANDARD
A tachi, a samurai sword with a slightly longer hilt and blade than the more familiar katana, according to the Burst's talent text. The Raiden Shogun produces it from her chest rather than from a sheath at her hip. Canon gives no further description of its fittings.

## lore
### LITE
The character story calls it a sword that has accompanied Ei to this day and has "witnessed both time and eternity". It was born from Makoto's divine might but never sharpened, because Makoto was not one for battle; it was a symbol, a sign of true peace.
### STANDARD +
On the day Makoto passed on, the sword was given to Ei. It was already stained with blood and, as the blade drew its first crimson, the ichor was blown aside by wild winds and blazing thunder. Ei did not change its name, because she too had looked out over the vast scene of Inazuma's beauty and it had given her a stronger, purer dream of her own. The text ends on the aesthetic of lightning: to capture what is precious amid fleeting transience.
### LARGE +
In the Archon Quest chapter Radiant Sakura, Ei says she feels something stirring in the sword and that the blade inherited from Makoto seems to guide her. She reasons that it belonged to Makoto and may only respond with its full power when she truly understands and believes in her sister; holding it, she felt Makoto beside her again. Makoto's voice answers from the light of the sword, saying she left a faint fragment of her will in it so that she too would be released when Ei unleashed its full power.
### ULTRA +
The wiki further states that Ei and the Shogun have performed the technique Musou no Hitotachi with this blade ever since it was passed down, and that after Ei created the Shogun she transferred her consciousness into the sword and created the Plane of Euthymia, with the blade kept within the puppet Shogun. This is a summary of Archon Quest events; treat the Plane of Euthymia and Ei's consciousness as detailed in their own entries.

## owners
### LITE
First Makoto, who never used it to fight, then Ei, who has used it in battle since Makoto's death. The Raiden Shogun, Ei's puppet, wields it in her Burst.
### LARGE +
The sword is not an item anyone can pick up; it is tied to the sisters. Do not portray other characters wielding it.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not in-world): the sword is not an obtainable weapon. The Raiden Shogun's Elemental Burst, Secret Art: Musou Shinsetsu, changes her stance from a polearm to the Musou Isshin, which uses sword animations. In the Inazuma Archon Quest boss fight, the playable Raiden Shogun stays permanently in her Musou Isshin state.

## roleplay
### LARGE
For scenes: Musou Isshin is intimate, tied to grief and to Makoto. Ei is unlikely to discuss it casually. A character who knows nothing of its history would see only a graceful, unmarked tachi drawn from nowhere; do not have strangers recognise it.
