## summary
### LITE
The Sword of Narzissenkreuz is a 4-star sword received after the Rowboat's Wake quest in Fontaine. In the story it is the holy blade of the Narzissenkreuz Ordo, its power faded when that story ended.
### STANDARD +
Its description calls it a sword that will now begin a new journey with the power remaining in it, commemorating a certain great dream. The Arkhe it carries, Pneuma or Ousia, can be switched at Annapausis.

## appearance
### STANDARD
The only canon visuals are two forms, a Pneuma-aligned and an Ousia-aligned blade. The wiki gives no further description; avoid inventing engraving or hilt details.

## lore
### LITE
A ceremonial sword of the Narzissenkreuz Ordo. It was used in a ritual to separate mind from body and, in the Ordo's story, held reason itself.
### STANDARD +
Narzissenkreuz himself used it in that ritual, for example to grant a certain hilichurl intelligence, per the wiki lore summary. The quest-exclusive version, shown during Waking from the Great Dream, is described as a sacred blade with enough reason and will to create and destroy a universe, or a dream. After Rowboat's Wake it is replaced by the ordinary weapon.
### LARGE +
The wiki reads the sword's makeup as based on ancient philosophies of the soul: the Ordo recreated Memory, Wish, Soul and Persona in the material world, each sealed in its Orthant, and their fusion is the reason that can cut or unite any will of water. Caterpillar says any object that holds reason can serve as the sword; Sandrone, on seeing the Traveler with it, calls it a savior's plaything. Narzissenkreuz's own closing line in the quest, as relayed by the wiki, is that the Holy Blade need not be a sword and could even be a gold-haired outlander.
### ULTRA +
The weapon description is a general passage about travellers: the sword in the hand and the dream in the heart as a traveller's trusted companions, and the eternal Traveler passing through many worlds and leaving a slightly brighter future. Its first paragraph repeats that of the Dull Blade. In the game's chain the blade is first the Holy Blade (Search in the Algae Sea), then the quest sword in Waking from the Great Dream, then this weapon in Rowboat's Wake.

## owners
### LITE
Ann carries the blade in the story; the player receives the weapon at the end of the quest.
### LARGE +
Narzissenkreuz is its original wielder. Sandrone's remark shows she knows the Traveler possesses it. Anyone else who sees it will not recognise it unless they know the Ordo.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not in-world): a 4-star sword, base ATK 42 at level 1, secondary stat ATK; obtained after Rowboat's Wake, version 4.2. Passive "Hero's Blade": for a wielder with no Arkhe of their own, Normal, Charged or Plunging Attacks that hit release a Pneuma or Ousia energy blast for a percentage of ATK, at most once every 12s. Refinement uses the material Surging Sacred Chalice, made from Drained Conch Cup at the Little Fountain.

## roleplay
### LARGE
Handle as a quest-end reward: someone who did not follow the Ordo's story will see only a plain, light blade. A character who knows Ann or Caterpillar may react with solemnity rather than treating it as a prize.
