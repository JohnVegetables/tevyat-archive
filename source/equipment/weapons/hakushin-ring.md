## summary
### LITE
The Hakushin Ring is a catalyst that carries the memory of the Kitsune Saiguu, a kitsune of the ancient Hakushin clan who lived in Narukami's mountains and walked among humans. Its short description admits that the device is "but an impoverished vessel" for the full breadth of her thought. Its text is her fond recollection of the friends she made and a final plea.
### STANDARD +
It is a 4-star catalyst of the Amethyst series that players forge, and it belongs to the Sacred Sakura Cleansing Ritual quest series.

## appearance
### STANDARD
A ring-like catalyst; canon text does not describe it further than as a device that stores Saiguu's memory. Its Japanese name, Hakushin no Wa, means "ring of the Hakushin".

## lore
### LITE
The speaker is the Hakushin Kitsune. She calls her life "simple and ordinary" and says she has lived it to the full, running with her agile and loveable comrades across the mountains and plains of Narukami. She hopes they will be able to run happily again once everything is over.
### STANDARD +
She remembers an oni maiden with a face as lovely as the moon, with whom she played divine music before the throne, and whose sword dance she applauded. She remembers racing the chieftain of the Yougou Tengu through the spirit mountains and, as a Hakushin, winning the contest, though she suspects the tengu let her off easy. She also remembers outwitting a bake-danuki who challenged her, making him surrender to the Shogun, and then tricking the Shogun into taking that great bake-danuki under her command. A moonlit night in the imperial garden still glows in her memory, and she hopes the Shogun will remember the motto she offered when they parted: do not be blinded, do not waver, keep walking the path you believe in. She hopes the tanuki will not hate her for her final deception.
### LARGE +
She lists friends from all walks of life, those she did not expect to make: a shrine maiden who came to train in Narukami for her hometown shrine, a child separated from the adults by the divine palanquin's procession during a summer festival, an easygoing young man who finally went to Liyue to practise the adepti arts, the Kanjou who worked to make the city prosperous, a craftsman obsessed with making extremely sharp swords, and a clan who used ingenious techniques to make man-made meteors bloom in the sky. She hopes the barrier that guards them will not be eroded by darkness.
### ULTRA +
The text closes with a plea addressed to a "dark will that gnaws at me". With her strength lost, she offers her Hakushin blood to it and asks, on behalf of those she treasures, that it return her ever-bright memory to the land she loves, so that she can still hope beautiful things endure after its rampage. The wiki links the place she speaks from to the Abyss and the "ever-bright memory" to Hanachirusato; neither is named by the item's own words.

## owners
### LITE
The Kitsune Saiguu, the Hakushin of the text, is the originating figure; the physical ring is a memory-bearing device rather than a known heirloom.
### LARGE +
In the text of Kagura's Verity, Yae Miko calls Saiguu her master and friend and says she departed never to return; this item does not state that Yae holds the ring.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not in-world): a 4-star catalyst in the Amethyst series, released in Version 2.0. Base ATK 44, Energy Recharge as secondary stat. Forged from a Northlander Catalyst Billet, 50 Amethyst Lump, 50 White Iron Chunk and 500 Mora; the diagram Mask of Memories comes from completing Yougou Cleansing.
### LARGE +
Its passive, Sakura Saiguu, gives nearby party members of an element involved in an Electro reaction triggered by the wielder an Elemental DMG Bonus for that element (10% at refinement 1, 20% at refinement 5) for 6 seconds, non-stacking. Wiki notes say it applies to Dendro and Electro allies on Aggravate, and to Dendro and Electro (not Hydro) allies on Hyperbloom, that Spread does not trigger it, and that it only triggers while the wielder is on the field.

## roleplay
### LARGE
For scenes: a narrative echo of the Saiguu. Characters who knew Yae Miko may react to it as a keepsake of a lost friend. Treat the ring's words as a record of her memories, not as a living voice, unless the scenario decides otherwise.
