## summary
### LITE
"The Catch" is a four-star polearm: the favoured barbed spear of a famous Inazuman bandit, Ako Domeki. It began as a harpoon for catching fish and proved handy in battle; item text says he even pierced the enchanted, sword-armed puppet with it before carrying it off. It is bought with fish from the Inazuma Fishing Association.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Item text calls it a barbed spear that was originally a fishing harpoon. No more detail is given.

## lore
### STANDARD
The text is a monologue by Ako Domeki, called Zaemon. He says he was once master of the ship Seiraimaru, commanded over ten ships and was called the Deathless Oni of Seirai, but is now "a leaf floating upon the waves". Without Janome and the people marooned on the islands he doubts he would have made it home, and he finds Seirai changed, with no place left for him in the Inazuman Islands; even the "naggy, fretful old shrine maiden" is gone.
### LARGE +
His voice thick with emotion, he then declares himself the freest man in the world. He tells Janome and the departed shrine maiden ("Aunt Shrine Maiden") that she had wanted to see the world, as that Kamuna fellow and the Konbumaru she always spoke of had, and that he, Ako Domeki Zaemon, will go in her stead to see the edge of the world. He promises they will meet again "where all ship routes end" and that he will tell her all about the distant lands then; the wiki glosses that phrase as a way of referring to death or the afterlife.
### ULTRA +
Read with Hamayumi, which shows the shrine maiden deciding to protect him as the fleet sets sail, this item is the sequel: the same crew's pride and resistance, but now a loss. The wiki's links identify Konbumaru with Takamine the Mistsplitter and the maiden as Asase Hibiki, but the text itself uses only nicknames.

## owners
### STANDARD
Ako Domeki, the bandit (historical.ako-domeki). No later owner is named.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not lore): four-star polearm. Base ATK 42, secondary stat Energy Recharge 10%. Passive "Shanty" raises Elemental Burst DMG and Elemental Burst CRIT Rate. Bought with fish from Kujirai Momiji of the Inazuma Fishing Association.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay use (this project's suggestion, not canon text): a keepsake of a boisterous, fond farewell; characters from Seirai may remember Ako Domeki warmly, while the Shogunate's side would remember him as a bandit.
