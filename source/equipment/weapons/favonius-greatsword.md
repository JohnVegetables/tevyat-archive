## summary
### LITE
Favonius Greatsword is a 4-star claymore of the Favonius series, a heavy ceremonial sword of the Knights of Favonius. It channels elemental power easily and is highly destructive, and its text tells of the Lion of Light Arundolyn and his childhood friend Rostam the Wolf Pup.
### STANDARD +
It is usually issued only after approval from both the Grand Master and the Church.

## appearance
### STANDARD
A heavy, ceremonial greatsword. The wiki's trivia remarks that the crossguard and pommel resemble a famous fantasy-film sword; this is a design observation, not canon.

## lore
### LITE
The text calls it an honor given to a Knight of Favonius and the fruit of the work and skills of Mondstadt's craftsmen, who achieved results in elemental affinity by studying the city's ancient artifacts. It reminds the bearer that destruction is only a means; the end is to defend what must be defended.
### STANDARD +
Only a single-handed form of Rostam's Favonius Bladework survives today. Another form is lost to history: that of Arundolyn, the Lion of Light, who could wield a longsword and a greatsword together in battle. It demanded such strength that only the extraordinarily gifted could try it, and it died out.
### LARGE +
Arundolyn came from a long line of knights and Rostam from a peasant family. They were close companions growing up, with a shared dream of heroism, became fellow knights as young men, and as mature adults one became Grand Master and the other his assistant. Arundolyn was no Vision bearer; his formidable strength came from natural gifts and intense training, and it was his pride and the pride of the Knights and all Mondstadt.
### ULTRA +
After Rostam met his fate, Arundolyn never displayed that strength again, because challenging ferocious monsters no longer brought glory. For fuller detail on either man, defer to their own entries.

## owners
### LITE
No owner is named in the text.
### LARGE +
The wiki notes the sword is shown with Noelle in her art, Hangout quest endings and Travelers' Tales, and with Diluc in several quests and videos; this is art, not an ownership claim.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not in-world): 4-star claymore, base ATK 41, secondary stat Energy Recharge 13.3%, obtainable from Wishes including the standard banner. Passive Windfall: CRIT hits have a chance (60% to 100% by refinement) to generate a small amount of Elemental Particles, restoring 6 Energy, once per 12 s at refinement 1 down to once per 6 s at refinement 5.

Favonius Bladework originated with Rostam, the Wolf Pup. Per the Favonius Greatsword text, only a single-handed form survives, because the dual longsword-and-greatsword form of Arundolyn, the Lion of Light, required extraordinary strength and died out.
