## summary
### LITE
Deathmatch is a four-star crimson polearm, part of the Gnostic Hymn series from the Battle Pass. Its item story follows a gladiator who fought for blood, then for the young master he loved, and who died by the blade of a red-haired maiden at his last performance. The wiki identifies that maiden as Vennessa and the master as Eberhart.
### STANDARD +
Item text calls it a gladiator's priceless treasure, with a point stained by the blood of countless beasts and men. It is a Mondstadt-linked story weapon, tied by the wiki to the Gladiator's Finale artifact set.

## appearance
### STANDARD
A sharp crimson polearm with an ice-cold tip. The text mentions nothing else about its shaft or decoration, so do not invent any.

## lore
### STANDARD
The story is a duelist's tragedy. The polearm watched the valor of a gladiator whose tip was often wet with the blood of his foes while the crowd applauded. Freedom always seemed near and never came. When his master told him the promised number of victories had been met, he offered the spear as a parting gift and asked him to fight on as a free man, for glory, for the gladiator and for himself. Over many years the beasts and men the spear felled were too many to count, and the weapon was called undefeated, but the gladiator's heart belonged to his young master.
### LARGE +
In his final performance, the gladiator's weapon fell to the sand when the red-haired maiden's burning blade pierced his heart. As he fell he looked toward his master's seat, which was empty apart from a toppled wine goblet and silver platter, and asked whether Master Eberhart had liked the performance. His dying words say that at first he fought for himself, for blood and battle-lust, and later killed for his master; he added that people can fight like wild beasts without care only for the sake of others, and that she, who fought for her people and not for herself, would surely understand.
### ULTRA +
The wiki links the maiden to Vennessa and the master to Eberhart; the item text itself gives only the master's name and calls the killer a red-haired maiden. It also notes that the story ties into the Gladiator's Finale artifact set. The empty seat is the story's cruelest detail: the man who gave the order to fight on is not even there to watch him die, and the text never says whether he fled or simply left.

## owners
### STANDARD
The gladiator of the item story (historical.unnamed-gladiator), who received it from his master as a parting gift. No later owner is named.
### LARGE +
In promotional material Chevreuse wields it in her character demo, her Collected Miscellany video and the Version 4.3 'Roses and Muskets' trailer; that is presentation, not a canon statement that she owns it.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not lore): obtained through the Battle Pass Gnostic Hymn. Base ATK 41, secondary stat CRIT Rate 8%. Passive 'Gladiator': with at least two opponents nearby, ATK and DEF each rise by 16-32% across refinements 1-5; with fewer than two, ATK rises by 24-48% instead.
### ULTRA +
The wiki notes that the passive state is updated only while its wielder is on the field and keeps the last state after they are switched out.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay use (this project's suggestion, not canon text): a symbol of loyalty that outlasts freedom, and of a person who stops fighting for himself and starts fighting for someone else. A character holding it can reasonably speak of arena life and of the cost of a master's applause.
