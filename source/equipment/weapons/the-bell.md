## summary
### LITE
The Bell is a four-star claymore of the Wanderer's Troupe series. It is a heavy greatsword with a clock set into it, whose internal mechanisms are long damaged. Its story belongs to Kreuzlied, a former Lawrence clan noble turned traitor who travelled with the Troupe.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Item text describes a strange greatsword decorated with an exquisite clock. After the Troupe disbanded it was soaked in acid, which rusted the gears; it is still a lethal weapon. Its clockwork once gave the wielder and friends a combat rhythm.

## lore
### STANDARD
Kreuzlied once belonged to the ruling Lawrence clan but became a traitor and outcast and travelled with the Wanderer's Troupe. In that era scholars and bards ignored history because the aristocracy preferred wilful ignorance of their own decay to history's judgement, yet they trembled at the sound of this blade, the toll of the bell of judgment.
### LARGE +
What punishment Kreuzlied suffered after the failed uprising was never made public, which the text takes as proof of his privilege. Legend says that after being dismissed from office he founded a secret society to bring down the aristocracy, and gave his full help to the enslaved gladiator Vennessa when she revolted.
### ULTRA +
A once-popular rumour held that the society kept working behind the scenes to help the Knights of Favonius protect Mondstadt through deeds no knight should take part in, and that the Wolf Pup Rostam secretly ran it behind the Grand Master Arundolyn's back. The text presents this only as rumour, so do not state it as fact.

## owners
### STANDARD
Kreuzlied, who travelled with the Troupe.
### LARGE +
Freminet is shown wielding the ascended version in his official art and in the Version 4.6 trailer, and the base version in the Into the Sunlight story; that is presentation, not a canon ownership claim.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Game mechanics: Wanderer's Troupe-series four-star claymore, base ATK 42, HP 9% secondary, passive 'Rebellious Guardian' (a shield; damage rises while any shield protects the wearer). Available in the standard Wanderlust Invocation and event wishes.
