## summary
### LITE
Sacrificial Bow is a 4-star bow of the Sacrificial series, in the game since Version 1.0. Its item text calls it a ceremonial hunting bow petrified by time, once the property of the proud Lawrence clan of old Mondstadt, who used it in ritual reenactments of their victory over the frozen wilderness.
### STANDARD +
The Sacrificial weapons are linked to ceremonies honouring Time and Anemo together; the Bow's story is the one in which a clan's self-image decays from conquerors to overlords to kings.

## appearance
### STANDARD
A hunting bow with trinkets still visible on it, which the in-game description says has been petrified over time. The text adds that it was once a prop with an immobile bowstring, but the string became flexible and sturdier with the passage of time. Do not invent further decoration.

## lore
### LITE
The Lawrence clan used the bow to act out their ancestors' conquest of the frozen wilderness in sacrificial ceremonies, and the bow itself was said to tell the story of the pioneers and their hardships.
### STANDARD +
On the cliff facing the eastern sea, the ancestors are said to have worshipped the masters of Time and Anemo together; the item text quotes a saying that Anemo brings stories while Time nurtures them. The ceremony's first act told how the forebears took the land through wisdom and strength. The ceremony was eventually lost, but the clan kept playing the same role.
### LARGE +
According to the item text the role became corrupted: the clan's view of itself shifted from conquerors to overlords and then to kings, and they strayed into depravity and lost the affection of Mondstadt's winds. The text is a legend-style narration with no named narrator and gives no dates.
### ULTRA +
Read with the other two Sacrificial weapons, the Bow is the cautionary member of the set: the Sword (Gunnhildr clan) keeps its guardian role, the Greatsword (Imunlaukr clan) finds something to fight for, while the Lawrence clan's tale ends in decline. How this corresponds to Mondstadt's recorded history (for example the clan's fall) is covered by the clan and Mondstadt history entries, not by this item text.

## owners
### STANDARD
The item text names the Lawrence clan as the bow's former owners. No individual wielder is named.

## trivia
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
Diona is briefly seen holding a Sacrificial Bow in her anecdote in the Travelers' Tales: Anthology Chapter event (a limited-time event). It is a cameo, not a story claim of ownership.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not literal lore): 4-star bow, base ATK 44, Energy Recharge secondary stat. Passive Composed: after an Elemental Skill deals damage, it has a 40 to 80 per cent chance (by refinement) to end its own cooldown, at most once every 30 to 16 seconds. Obtainable from the Standard Wanderlust Invocation and from character and weapon event wishes; part of the Sacrificial series.
