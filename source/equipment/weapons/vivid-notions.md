## summary
### LITE
Vivid Notions is a 5-star catalyst: a trophy of gemstones from the Collective of Plenty and crystals from the Flower-Feather Clan, said to have belonged to a legendary wrestler.
### STANDARD +
Its story concerns a woman of the Collective of Plenty who stepped into the hero's role when the tribes fell into a succession quarrel.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Canon says only that it is a trophy of gemstones and crystals. The story speaks of a hero's prop or mask, which is not the same object; keep to a glittering trophy.

## lore
### LITE
After the six heroes of the blazing sun had stepped into the night wind, the tribes quarrelled over the empty throne. A girl who had stood silently in Wanjiru's shadow burst into the council chamber and drove a half-broken bone blade into the long table, ending the quarrel.
### STANDARD +
Fietena, born of the Collective and married into the Flower-Feather Clan, then led the united tribes against the Abyss's black tide.
### LARGE +
The tribes had been scheming for the authority left behind while the calamity loomed. Fietena had never received an Ancient Name nor been expected to become a hero; she had huddled by campfires listening to Wanjiru's legends. She now wore a hero's mask and rallied the tribes. With Traore, an orphan of the mines, she carved through rocks to channel lava into protective rivers, and with Nzambe, a priest of the deep valleys, she set a trap that unleashed a hurricane across the corrupted swamp.
### ULTRA +
In the end a mortal is only a mortal: when the calamity finally waned, the withering tide had permeated Fietena's bones. As the last shadow crumbled she removed her mask and gave the prop she had played the hero with for many years to Taika, a young follower from the Flower-Feather Clan. Her final words, as told, are that when the storm passes a rainbow will shine, but its false colours are illusions in the mist; whether a true hero or merely acting the part, each person's turn to bow out comes, as with a wrestler; and Taika should let the mask witness the journey ahead. The wiki's Fietena page adds that she was a renowned wrestler and calls the mask the Demon-Warrior's Feather Mask.

## owners
### LITE
The description says the trophy once belonged to a legendary wrestler; the story is about Fietena.
### LARGE +
No wielder is named; Taika receives Fietena's mask, not necessarily the trophy. Treat any scene in which a character carries the trophy as invented.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not in-world): a 5-star catalyst, base ATK 48 at level 1, secondary stat CRIT DMG; Weapon Event Wishes; introduced in Version 5.5.
### LARGE +
Passive "Falling Rainbow's Wish": ATK is increased by 28% to 56%. A Plunging Attack grants "Dawn's First Hue" (Plunging Attack CRIT DMG up by 28% to 56%), and using an Elemental Skill or Burst grants "Twilight's Splendor" (up by 40% to 80%). Each lasts 15s and is cancelled 0.1s after the ground impact hits a target.

## roleplay
### LARGE
For scenes: a Natlanese wrestler or storyteller can use the story to talk about being a hero as a role that mortals take up and later put down. Avoid claiming the trophy's exact history.

Falling Rainbow's Wish is the passive of the catalyst Vivid Notions (gameplay): an ATK boost, plus Dawn's First Hue and Twilight's Splendor, which raise Plunging Attack CRIT DMG until the plunge lands.

## entry: Fietena
@keys: Fietena
@keys2: Vivid Notions, Taika, Demon-Warrior's Feather Mask
### LARGE
Fietena was a Collective of Plenty woman who married into the Flower-Feather Clan and, with Traore and Nzambe, protected Natlan for about a decade after the Pyro Archon went into the Sacred Flame. She wore a hero's mask, was a renowned wrestler, and handed the mask to Taika as she aged.
