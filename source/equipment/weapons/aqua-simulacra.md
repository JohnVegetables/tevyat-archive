## summary
### LITE
Aqua Simulacra is a five-star bow of Liyue whose color is unpredictable: under light it takes on a lustrous, watery blue. Its item text says it can summon water and uses water as a lesson, that water has no fixed form and is at once the gentlest and keenest of substances. The story ties the lesson to Yelan, who received it from an elderly senior before she worked for Ningguang of the Jade Chamber.
### STANDARD +
Yelan is seen with the unascended bow in her Story Quest, in Perilous Trail and in a cameo in The Chasm Delvers, as well as in her promotional material.

## appearance
### LARGE
The in-game description calls it a longbow whose color is unpredictable, taking on a lustrous, watery blue under light. The wiki notes the top and bottom of the bow glow while its damage bonus is active.

## lore
### STANDARD
The text opens with the idea that water can serve as blade or bow. A senior taught that water envelops what is impure and washes away filth yet stays pure, takes any form and imitates any life yet never loses itself, and, divided, is uncounted streams or, united, an unstoppable wave. Many secrets hide beneath its azure translucence, and a thousand minds may fail to grasp its shifts.
### LARGE +
Yelan heard this teaching before she worked for the noble mistress of the Jade Chamber. The text says that the world of commerce and power is full of hidden reefs and storms and ship skeletons on the seabed, and that those with great ambition and vast resources can sail through it. A first-person reply, naturally read as hers, says that she lacks that spirit and patience; she prefers a roomful of tea scent and the possibilities of six-sided dice. The speaker has infiltrated crooks' hideouts by night and broken enemies' schemes from faraway lands, but not to help the powerful crush dissidents: water, too, has its source.
### ULTRA +
The close of the story states that gentleness can become blade and bow in defense of the source's purity, and that conspiracy and coded language weave easily under a pale blue full moon, which it calls the wisdom of water's shape. The text gives no concrete operation or names beyond Ningguang's title (noble mistress of the Jade Chamber).

## owners
### STANDARD
Yelan is shown wielding the bow in many quests and cutscenes (her Story Quest, Perilous Trail, a cameo in The Chasm Delvers) and in her art and demo. No earlier owner is given.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not lore): a five-star bow from weapon event wishes. Base ATK 44, secondary stat CRIT DMG 19.2%. Passive "The Cleansing Form": HP rises 16-32% and DMG dealt rises 20-40% while at least one opponent is within 8 metres, whether the wielder is on the field or not (refinements 1 to 5).

## roleplay
### LARGE
Roleplay use (this project's suggestion, not canon text): water imagery (reflections, lingering mist, shifting blue) suits it. Use the text's philosophy as a perspective Yelan might share, not as dialogue to be recited.
