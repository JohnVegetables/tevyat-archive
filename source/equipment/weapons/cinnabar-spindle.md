## summary
### LITE
Cinnabar Spindle is a 4-star sword, in-game description "a sword made from materials that do not belong in this world," whose power might withstand the corruption of a venom that could corrode a mighty dragon. It was the reward weapon of the limited-time event Shadows Amidst Snowstorms in Version 2.3, a Dragonspine event in which Albedo appears; it ran 2021-11-25 to 2021-12-13.
### STANDARD +
Its item text reads like an alchemist's message handed down to a successor and is tied by the wiki to Albedo and his creator Rhinedottir. Its name is also alchemical: cinnabar is the ore of mercury, and the weapon is refined with an event material called Alkahest.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Canon gives no description of the blade beyond the "otherworldly materials". The wiki notes its colors match Albedo's design; avoid inventing other details.

## lore
### LITE
The text is an exhortation: separate the dust in the flames with joy and extract the exquisite from the crude, for all things in the universe come from a single source and may be derived from a single thought.
### STANDARD +
It tells the addressee to pursue what the elder brother, "the one-horned white horse," could not accomplish, to reach the far side of philosophy and to create a new destiny for the speaker and the addressee's brothers. The wiki reads this as Rhinedottir speaking to Albedo about the False Horned Steed and the Primordial Human, but the text itself names nobody.
### LARGE +
The wiki also notes that the sword's short description mirrors that of Festering Desire, another weapon the wiki calls related in many ways. The link is thematic; the item text draws no explicit connection.

### ULTRA +
Caution when using the text: it is an unsigned alchemical exhortation, so a roleplayer should not have anyone quote it as Rhinedottir's words in-world unless the scene supports that wiki reading. The sword's name (cinnabar as mercury ore) and refinement material (Alkahest, the alchemists' hypothetical universal solvent) are real-world alchemy references noted by the wiki, not Teyvat lore.

## owners
### STANDARD
No owner is named in item text. In the Shadows Amidst Snowstorms event cutscenes, Albedo is seen using a blade matching it.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not literal lore): a 4-star sword with base ATK 41 and a DEF% secondary stat. Its passive, Spotless Heart, adds a share of DEF to the wielder's Elemental Skill damage (once per 1.5s, cleared shortly after the skill deals damage), and it works even when the wielder is off-field. Instead of duplicate weapons, refinement uses Alkahest, four of which come from the event.

## entry: Shadows Amidst Snowstorms event

### STANDARD
Shadows Amidst Snowstorms was a limited-time Version 2.3 Flagship event (2021-11-25 to 2021-12-13) played in Dragonspine, with Albedo appearing in its story. Cinnabar Spindle was its reward weapon, and the event's cutscenes show Albedo using a matching blade. This entry does not cover the event's plot.
