## summary
### LITE
Mitternachts Waltz is a 4-star bow described as painted in the color of transgression and nights of illusion. Its text is a dramatic, German-flavored tale: the Prinzessin der Verurteilung and her Night-Severing Raven, Ozvaldo Hrafnavins, recount a long journey across space and time. The wiki notes Fischl is shown with this bow in her TCG card.
### STANDARD +
The name is German for "midnight waltz". The text never says in plain words who the Prinzessin is, but it addresses her as "mein Fräulein" and the wiki links the address to Fischl.

## appearance
### STANDARD
A bow painted in a transgressive, dream-like night color. Beyond that short description, canon gives no visual detail.

## lore
### LITE
The tale opens with Oz telling the Prinzessin that not one of her tears was shed in vain on her pilgrimage. The Prinzessin and her raven, in their journey across space and time, witnessed countless stories and their endings.
### STANDARD +
In the text, every young man's rage at injustice must turn to calm, and everything must pass through the doorway of its destruction into the Prinzessin's future kingdom, where it finds sleep in her pitch-dark Nachtgarten. When Droctulf fell to the Beast of the World, having fought for a young man's dreams, the Prinzessin began to tear up, and she declared that the Sovereign of the Immernachtreich weeps not.
### LARGE +
She then says everyone born into the world carries sin, and when the clock strikes judgment again, the struggles of men and beasts trapped under the skirt of night will be a midnight waltz. Oz asks if she still recalls a tale of two of three bright moons being shredded by a sword that tore the horizon. The text offers a second reading: the moons were reduced to dust, yet wished to stay in the Prinzessin's gaze. It ends with the Prinzessin insisting that a Prinzessin does not shed tears; the wetness was just secretion from eyes insulted by impudent grains of sand.
### ULTRA +
The piece is both epic and comic: grand talk about eternity, undercut by a proud denial of crying. Use it as in-character flavor from Fischl's persona, not as verified cosmology. For the moon and tree motifs, the wiki's links to the Three Moons and the Irminsul are interpretive.

## owners
### LITE
No owner is named in the text. The wiki notes Fischl is seen wielding this bow in her TCG character card.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not in-world): 4-star bow, base ATK 42, secondary stat Physical DMG Bonus 11.3%, obtained from weapon event Wishes (added in Version 1.6). Passive Evernight Duet: Normal Attack hits raise Elemental Skill DMG for 5 s, and Elemental Skill hits raise Normal Attack DMG for 5 s, by 20% at refinement 1 up to 40% at refinement 5.
### LARGE +
The wiki also notes it appeared in the Honkai Impact 3rd crossover event Outworld Traveler; that is out-of-universe trivia.
