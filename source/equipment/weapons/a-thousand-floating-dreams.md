## summary
### LITE
A Thousand Floating Dreams is a 5-star catalyst from Sumeru, a lamp whose emerald light carries "a song of ancient days". Its item story is a lyrical tale of the girl who had never known "light", who hears the Greater Lord Rukkhadevata's whisper in a dream as a palace crumbles and later walks out of the dreams into a real dawn. The wiki reads the girl as the Dendro Archon Nahida.
### STANDARD +
The short description says the lamp lights the dreams that float up "over a thousand nights". The weapon is a keepsake-like symbol of Sumeru's themes of dreams, stories and the passing of old ages.

## appearance
### STANDARD
A lamp-like catalyst. The description gives it an emerald-green light. Canon text gives no further physical detail such as material, size or inscriptions.

## lore
### LITE
At the moment the palace crumbled, the girl who had never known "light" heard the Greater Lord's whisper inside a dream. A verdant lamp then shows stories: an emerald-eyed dancer kissing nectar-soaked silk, brightly coloured fish in pearly water, a moonlight musician guiding the Jinn's torches, and a sailor seeking a voice from a dream and the blooming gardens of a far shore.
### STANDARD +
The child leaves the fallen palace on a soft forest breeze that guides lost children away from a place no longer tranquil. As dawn breaks, the lamp fades, and looking back the blind girl realises there is no return. The lamp never flickers again, yet birds still sing its tales, bringing courage, hope and happiness to children who never saw a dream. The closing verses show a traveller reaching the faraway homeland and the rose in the breeze, waiting for a new story.
### LARGE +
The text opens with a line that also appears in the description of the artifact Laurel Coronet, where it is attributed to the Greater Lord Rukkhadevata: there is a time appointed for all things to live and die, like the ceaseless cycles of night and dawn. This links the lamp's tone to her sayings. Read the verses as allegory rather than as a literal chronology: "dawn" is both a literal sunrise on a forest path and the end of a long seclusion.
### ULTRA +
Because the text names no one except by description, avoid assigning identities to the dancer, the musician or the sailor. The only explicit references are to "the girl who had never known light", "the blind girl" and the whispering Greater Lord, all of which the wiki ties to Nahida and Rukkhadevata.

## owners

No in-world owner is named. Nahida is shown wielding it in her Collected Miscellany video and her Genius Invokation TCG character card.
### LARGE +
Do not assume Nahida carries a lamp in ordinary scenes; her own entry governs what she holds. If a scene needs an object, the lamp works as a symbol of her story.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not in-world): 5-star catalyst released in Version 3.2 with the Sumeru series. Level 1 base ATK 44 with Elemental Mastery as its secondary stat. Its passive, A Thousand Nights' Dawnsong, gives the wielder Elemental Mastery for each other party member of the same Element and an Elemental DMG Bonus for each party member of a different Element, up to three stacks each, and gives all other nearby party members an Elemental Mastery boost.
### LARGE +
At refinement 1 the same-element bonus is 32 Elemental Mastery per stack, the different-element bonus is 10% per stack and the party boost is 40 Elemental Mastery; refinement 5 raises these to 64, 26% and 48. Copies of the weapon on several characters stack their party-wide effect. A Genius Invokation TCG equipment card of the same name exists. Ascension uses Damaged, Turbid and Radiant Prism with Fungal Spores, Luminescent Pollen and Crystalline Cyst Dust.
