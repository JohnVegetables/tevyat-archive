## summary
### LITE
Kagotsurube Isshin is a long blood-red sword bearing the Isshin name. It was forged by Akame Kanenaga, an Inazuman smith of the fallen Akame school, but made in a snowbound land to the far north rather than in Inazuma, and its journey home was marked by dark deeds. It figures in Kaedehara Kazuha's Story Quest, where the possessed sword is finally reforged and the player receives it.
### STANDARD +
Its description says it is remarkably sharp and vividly ominous, able to cleave a porous bamboo basket and the water within it before any leaks. The name is taken from Kagotsurube, a haunted sword in a kabuki play whose blade is said to leave no drop of blood on its edge.

## appearance
### STANDARD
A long blade the colour of blood. Its hamon, seen in moonlight, appears to flow like the blood and tears of a wayfaring wanderer. In the story it appears first as a menacing blade wreathed in an ominous purple aura (the Prized Isshin Blade), then shattered, and finally as the reforged sword.

## lore
### LITE
The text says people often think each blade holds a blade-spirit; a sword forged by the Tatarigami would then hold a demon of malice. Akame Kanenaga was a swordsmith who failed to become head smith. He made this blade in a snow-covered northern land, not in the land of thunder and Jade Steel.
### STANDARD +
The Akame school, in its zealous practice of "Isshin" (the stillness of mind free of distraction), had always sought a killer blade of extreme lethality. Its members grew withdrawn and venomous, and the authorities came to regard the school as undesirable: leadership of the Isshin Art, passed down from Mitsunaga, was rescinded before three generations had passed.
### LARGE +
After that, Kanenaga was involved in the Case of the Eccentric and committed treason, so he changed his name and went north to live humbly under the rule of "imperious ice". The text records his bitter words: he had spent half his life chasing the vanity of the Isshin Art and had become another "Kaedehara". The Kaedehara clan were master swordsmiths whose work best symbolised Masagomaru's style, and the Niwa clan were humble and diligent clay-temperers. Kanenaga died amid the snow, wishing only to forge a renowned blade that would restore the name of Kaedehara and win back the people's recognition. His last hope was that a sword born in ice and frost would not be as weak as its "useless title".
### ULTRA +
According to the wiki's synopsis of Kazuha's Story Quest, Kanenaga infused the sword with the power of the Tatarigami so that it carried its own consciousness, and told it his wish on his deathbed: to show Inazuma that the Isshin Art could still produce magnificent blades. The blade's long journey home was warped by Tatarigami hatred into a desire to rival the gods; it possessed people as tools and then discarded them. Arriving in Inazuma weakened, it met Kazuha, faced its lack of power and the truth of its corruption, and chose, with Kazuha's consent, to take hold of him so that it could be forged anew at the cost of its own consciousness, hoping the Isshin Art would one day regain the Raiden Shogun's regard. Before that consciousness faded, it gave Kazuha its name. This chain of events is wiki-summarised, not item text.

## owners
### LITE
Akame Kanenaga forged it. After the Story Quest, Kazuha holds the reforged sword.
### LARGE +
The Traveler's inventory contains the sword after the quest, but Kazuha's dialogue implies it stays with him in canon. He is also shown wielding it in his Genius Invokation TCG card.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not in-world): a 4-star sword obtained from the Story Quest A Strange and Friendless Road in Version 2.8; it has a single refinement rank. Base ATK 42 with ATK% as secondary stat. It was announced under the working name "Cursed Blade", which the game never uses.
### LARGE +
Its passive, Isshin Art Clarity, makes a Normal, Charged or Plunging Attack that hits whip up a Hewing Gale dealing AoE damage equal to 180% of ATK and increasing the wielder's ATK by 15% for 8 seconds, at most once every 8 seconds. In the quest, the Prized Isshin Blade (awakened) instead releases an Accursed Spirit that heals the wielder but halves the wielder's damage; the shattered version keeps the damage reduction without the heal.

## roleplay
### LARGE
For scenes: treat the sword as Kazuha's possession and a reminder of a personal mistake in the north. Its past owner is a bitter smith, not a hero, and its former corrupted self is gone; a scene should not revive its possession unless the scenario says so.

## entry: Akame school
@keys: Akame school, Akame Kanenaga, Isshin Art
@keys2: Kagotsurube Isshin, sword, swordsmith
### STANDARD
The Akame school pursued the Isshin Art's ideal of a distraction-free mind by forging killer blades of extreme lethality; their works were keen but carried an evil that led the authorities to rescind the school's leadership of the Isshin Art within three generations. Akame Kanenaga, a later member, fled north after the Case of the Eccentric and forged Kagotsurube Isshin there.
