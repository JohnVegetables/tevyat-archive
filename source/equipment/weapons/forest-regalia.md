## summary
### LITE
Forest Regalia is a 4-star claymore of the Aranara series. The description says it has taken the shape of a greatsword that shall cut down the foes of the forest. Its story tells, through an Aranara's memory, of the last king of the forest and the shifting maze of his palace.
### STANDARD +
It is forged from the diagram Tale of the Forest King, which the Aranara quest line lets a player buy from Aravinay in exchange for Stories of You and the Aranara.

## appearance
### STANDARD
A greatsword whose form the description attributes to the forest cutting down its foes. No further detail is given.

## lore
@version: 3.0
### LITE
The tale begins "The forest used to be a giant maze many, many moons ago..." The last king of the forest has returned to Sarva, leaving one who once waited upon him to inherit the name "Tiger". Where there was shade, there was the forest king's domain, and birds and beasts bowed as he passed.
### STANDARD +
Every king had a palace, and as each took the throne the forest reshaped itself to his image. The last king's palace lay behind a fence of trees and vines and a network of water and cliffs, like a disc of pearls made by moonlight through leaves on still water, where he basked alone. Legend says he traced the maze in his dappled stripes; successive palaces piled on one another so that paths wove from tree to tree and streams branched.
### LARGE +
Only the forest king, the Aranara and the people of the king tree's sanctuary could find their way through. The last king died protecting the forest, so no new palace would be made and the maze would vanish, or else a dark herd of beasts felled the trees like waves of wheat. The narrator heard this from a timid Aranara who dislikes being heard singing; one night he found the moon's reflection in the water of the maze and learned the story, before the current scattered the reflection.
### ULTRA +
The narrator concludes that even someone who has never met a king would get the impression that Tiger was a noble and powerful king of the forest, and that much has changed while much remains the same.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not in-world): 4-star claymore, level 1 base ATK 44, secondary stat Energy Recharge 6.7%. Passive Forest Sanctuary: after Burning, Quicken, Aggravate, Spread, Bloom, Lunar-Bloom, Hyperbloom or Burgeon, a Leaf of Consciousness appears for up to 10 s; picking it up grants Elemental Mastery for 12 s, once every 20 s, and works off-field. Forged from one Midlander Claymore Billet, 50 Crystal Chunk, 50 White Iron Chunk and 500 Mora.
### LARGE +
The Elemental Mastery bonus is 60 at refinement 1 and 120 at refinement 5.

## roleplay
### LARGE
Use it as a quiet woodland legend told by Aranara. Keep the two accounts of the king's death side by side rather than choosing one.
