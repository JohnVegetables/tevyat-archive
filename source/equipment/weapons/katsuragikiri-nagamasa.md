## summary
### LITE
Katsuragikiri Nagamasa is a four-star claymore, a tough, simple nagamaki-style blade designed by Mikoshi Nagamasa, an inspector of Tatarasuna. It was originally named the Daitatara Nagamasa. After he used it to execute his own subordinate Katsuragi, the sword and its owner's title were renamed for that act.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Item text calls it a nagamaki sword whose design is as simple and upright as the inspector himself, and a tough, simple sword. There is no further description of its look.

## lore
### STANDARD
The item story says the sword was designed by Tatarasuna's inspector, who was for a time obsessed with the grim beauty of steel and consulted the similarly inclined Vice Armory Officer Miyazaki. Nagamasa was only the foster son of the Mikoshi clan. His foster mother had stained the clan's name and the legitimate heir, Michihiro, had abandoned him and disappeared, yet his loyalty made him cherish the Mikoshi name. When his yoriki Katsuragi was connected with a single trace of misconduct, he had him executed without mercy, and from then on the sword's name and his title were changed.
### LARGE +
Before the outlander technology that built the Mikage Furnace, Tatarasuna smelted iron by the traditional Tatara method. The text frames Nagamasa as a man of self-admitted stupidity and loyalty who strove to enter the government and erase the family's bad reputation through diligence and righteousness. The wiki adds that he forged and wielded the sword at the Mikage Furnace, that "Daitatara" means "Great Tatara" and "Nagamasa" is his name, and that the new name "Katsuragikiri" plays on Nagamasa cutting Katsuragi. The Version 3.3 trailer briefly shows a man holding this weapon in what appears to be the Wanderer's memories, and the wiki points to the 3.3 interlude "Inversion of Genesis" for more lore tied to Tatarasuna.
### ULTRA +
The text reports only the outcome (an execution, a changed title) and not the evidence, so the rightness of Nagamasa's decision is left open; the item itself pairs his "righteousness" with the phrase "without mercy". Roleplayers should keep that ambivalence rather than resolving it. The weapon is also mentioned in the in-game notes called Rather Aged Notes.

## owners
### STANDARD
Mikoshi Nagamasa (historical.mikoshi-nagamasa) designed and used it. No other owner is named in the item text.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not lore): four-star claymore. Base ATK 42, secondary stat Energy Recharge 10%. Passive "Samurai Conduct" (shared with Kitain Cross Spear): raises Elemental Skill DMG, and after a skill hits an opponent the wielder loses 3 Energy but regains Energy every 2s for 6s (once per 10s), even off-field. Forged from a diagram found in a chest in Tatarasuna's arsenal.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay use (this project's suggestion, not canon text): the blade suits stories about duty against loyalty and the cost of defending a family name.
