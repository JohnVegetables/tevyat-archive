## summary
### LITE
A Teaspoon of Transcendence is a 5-star claymore. Its description calls it an object of fantasy with countless definitions in an endless sea of stories: no real difference between a hero's holy sword and a princess's teaspoon, or between a cold clockspring and the key to the gates of transcendence. Its text is a conversation between Sandrone and Alain. Added in Version 6.7.
### STANDARD +
The item is built on wordplay: the Chinese name's final character can mean both spoon and key, so the idea of a teaspoon and a key to transcendence are the same object. Sandrone is the weapon's most direct connection, as the wiki records her holding its unascended form in her character trailer.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Canon text gives no physical description beyond calling it an object of fantasy. In game it is a claymore with a teaspoon-and-key theme; keep to that and do not invent materials or measurements.

## lore
### LITE
The text opens with a line that heroes of different stories find different holy swords and face different nemeses, then shows Sandrone closing a storybook and asking Alain why heroes always win.
### STANDARD +
Alain answers that people prefer easy, happy stories, then offers a different tale: a kingdom ruled by a white dragon, a prophecy that the land would dry up and its princess wither, and a hero named Narzissenkreuz who sought the greatest holy sword and wanted to transcend the weakness of flesh. In it the hero falls into a chasm that dissolves all things, vowing to return as a new universe.
### LARGE +
The hero believed salvation required severing ordinary bonds, uniting everyone's wishes and crossing beyond good and evil; he dreamed of shouldering every sin, wiping away tears through a tearless magick and attaining transcendence, a leap compared to ape becoming man. The dragon, clinging to the old ways, scattered wicked mechanisms across the land, and the hero was driven into a corner before falling. Alain adds that the dragon could not understand the hero's choice and lost what he treasured most, while the hero could not accept human weakness. Sandrone, who says she does not like the story, calls both of them idiots who could not find another way; Alain replies that sacrificing oneself to save the world is little more than an unrealistic obsession. The text closes by repeating its opening line about heroes and swords.
### ULTRA +
The wiki links the white dragon to Alain Guillotin, the hero to Rene (Narzissenkreuz), the prophecy to the Prophecy of Fontaine, the holy sword to the Sword of Narzissenkreuz and the "chasm that dissolves all things" to the Primordial Sea, and notes the story's wording echoes the Nymph's Dream artifact Fell Dragon's Monocle with the labels hero and dragon reversed. Alain's reference to a friend who loved chess is a line in the item text itself. These are annotations on a fable told within the item, not claims that every detail is literal history.

## owners
### LITE
No in-game text names an owner. The wiki trivia notes that Sandrone is seen wielding the unascended claymore in her Character Trailer, which makes her the closest known association.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Game mechanics, not lore: a 5-star claymore, base ATK 48, CRIT DMG secondary stat. White Fairy's Queening adds an ATK bonus, and each Charged Attack hit grants a short Surmount stack that boosts the wielder's Stellar-Conduct and Stellar Swirl DMG. It was available on the weapon event Wish Epitome Invocation (2026-07-01 to 2026-07-21).

## roleplay
### LARGE
This archive's suggested use: a keepsake that blurs sword, spoon and key, tied to Sandrone and to Fontaine's old story of a hero and dragon. Characters who know the Fontaine and Narzissenkreuz history can quote the fable as a told story. Do not assert it belongs to Sandrone unless that is what the scene needs, and do not treat the fable as a verified record.
