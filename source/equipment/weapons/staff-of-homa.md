## summary
### LITE
The Staff of Homa is a five-star polearm of Liyue: a "devilishly red" ritual staff from the Archon War era, when fire rites were used to cleanse the miasmas of dead gods. Item text says a merciful doctor was given it by a whispering flame and used it to burn away impurity and release the dead from their ill karma. Today it is carried by Hu Tao, the Wangsheng Funeral Parlor's director, who inherited it through the Hu family.
### STANDARD +
Its name echoes homa, a fire-offering ritual. The wiki also notes the winged top of the staff, possibly a nod to the Homa bird of Persian myth. Nothing in the item text states the staff is a weapon first: Hu Tao's friend Xiangling does not know much about it beyond what Hu Tao has told her.

## appearance
### LARGE
Item text calls it a "devilishly red" staff and, in the doctor's vision, "scarlet firewood". The wiki records a winged motif at the top, which it says matches the wings of Ronova; the item text itself describes no wings.

## lore
### STANDARD
The item story: rites that cleanse by fire let impurity rise with the flames to the heavens. Fresh cinders were lit in rainy seasons and wolf smoke rose as prayers for blessings and the defeat of evil. These rites were common in the ancient period of strife, since the dreams and delusions of the silent corpses of gods bred evil miasmas that snatched away citizens.
### LARGE +
A merciful doctor then heard a whisper from within a flame as red as the sunset, from the crackling of withered grass: "Only an unbound flame can purify the filth of this world", and "Lift up this scarlet firewood. With it, you shall repel every demon." The doctor took up the red staff and burned all that was impure. The dead, who could not bear their sorrow and had been shrouded in ill karma and calamity, were reduced in the flame to ashen butterflies and found relief. Legend says the doctor, who lit countless flames, was likewise reduced to smoke shaped like a lovely butterfly. The rites were forgotten with the coming of peace, but those with fire in their hearts can still hear the whispered words when facing a great darkness.
### ULTRA +
The wiki reads the rites as the origin of the Wangsheng arts and the staff as a ceremonial tool, not an ordinary weapon, and describes the Wangsheng spear techniques as using polearms and purifying flames meant to destroy demons. It also suggests the staff was used to purify bodies during the plague after the Yaksha's accumulated karma killed many in Liyue (marked "likely"). Because the whisper and the staff's wing design match Ronova, the wiki's Ronova page suggests she was the voice; the Ronova connection is still only the wiki's inference. Treat all of these as readings of a poetic text.

## owners
### STANDARD
Current owner: Hu Tao, director of the Wangsheng Funeral Parlor, per the wiki's reading of the Lantern Rite event Springtime Charms (5.3, limited-time) as showing it passed down through the Hu family. The doctor of the legend is unnamed; the wiki treats the first wielder as heavily implied to be a Hu family ancestor.
### LARGE +
Zhongli temporarily holds the staff in some one-time story domains and trial runs, before returning to the Vortex Vanquisher; that is a gameplay stand-in, not a change of owner.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not lore): a five-star polearm from weapon event wishes. Base ATK 46, secondary stat CRIT DMG 14.4%. Passive "Reckless Cinnabar": HP rises 20-40% and the wielder gains ATK equal to 0.8-1.6% of Max HP, plus a further 1-1.8% of Max HP while HP is under 50% (refinements 1 to 5).

## roleplay
### LARGE
Roleplay use (this project's suggestion, not canon text): the staff is a funeral-rite object first, so its owners treat it with ritual seriousness even when playful. Sparks, drifting ember and butterfly motifs fit; avoid presenting it as a casual spear that any Liyue guard might carry.
