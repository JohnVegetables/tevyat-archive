## summary
### LITE
Solar Pearl is a 4-star catalyst of the Kunwu series, a dull golden pearl of unknown substance that harbours the light of the sun and moon and pulses with warm strength. It is rumoured to be a legendary orb of Liyue. It has been in the game since Version 1.0.
### STANDARD +
Its text gives Liyue's jewelers' tale of how the craftsman Kunwu made it, and Kunwu's own, more modest, account.

## appearance
### STANDARD
A dull, golden pearl. Its substance is unknown and it pulses with a warm strength. Do not invent a size or setting.

## lore
### LITE
The text says it once absorbed all the light in the world and was then kept in a wooden box for millennia, yet its power has not dissipated. Released from its container, it shines on, and a truly knowledgeable sage might unleash its power after so long.
### STANDARD +
A story long popular with Liyue's jewelers says Kunwu took the light of the heavenly bodies and sealed it in a wooden box, which he buried in a dark rocky chamber. After forty-nine days and twenty hours in pitch blackness, the pearl within had absorbed the shine of the sun and moon.
### LARGE +
Many in Liyue still believe in Kunwu's esoteric talents. When someone asked the ageing craftsman his secret, he laughed off the notion and said it was purely the product of prolonged polishing.
### ULTRA +
His apprentice told a different story in private: as Kunwu finished polishing the pearl, the splendour of the heavens shone down and a crimson moon filled the sky. The text leaves open whether this means Kunwu received divine help or whether his ambitions were great enough to send waves through the firmament and draw a response from the heavenly bodies.

## owners
### STANDARD
No owner is named in canon. Ningguang is shown with the ascended version in artwork of the Turning Point battle and Shikanoin Heizou in his character demo, which are cameos and not story claims.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not literal lore): 4-star catalyst, base ATK 42, CRIT Rate secondary stat (6 per cent). Passive Solar Shine: Normal Attack hits increase Elemental Skill and Elemental Burst DMG by 20 to 40 per cent (by refinement) for 6s, and Skill or Burst hits likewise increase Normal Attack DMG. Kunwu and Gnostic Hymn 1 series; it comes from the Battle Pass bounty.
