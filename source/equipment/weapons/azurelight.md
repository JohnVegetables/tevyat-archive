## summary
### LITE
Azurelight is a 5-star sword, once pure white like a dawn star and now tinted deep blue. Its item text is an angel's sermon urging the angels to defy the Heavenly Principles and entrust the world to humanity. Added in Version 5.7.
### STANDARD +
The sword is a story object rather than a famous artifact in the present-day plot: nobody in the story is shown carrying it, and its text is the only canon voice it has. Its passive is called Whitehill's Bestowal.

## appearance
### STANDARD
The in-game description gives only a colour story: the blade was originally white, like a dawn star, and has since been tinted a deep, dark blue. It is a straight sword in the game model; no other physical detail is given in canon text.

## lore
### LITE
The text is spoken by one who once taught that the heavens' laws must never be questioned, until starlight on a white stone mountain showed them a coming sunrise.
### STANDARD +
The speaker addresses angels who submitted to the primordial master and says begging, pleading and sacrifice will not loosen their bonds. The call is to cast it all aside, entrust the world to those who live in it and give humanity authority. The speech ends on the promise that once the veil is torn a new day will dawn.
### LARGE +
Before the call, the speaker lists four fates: one wanders a moonless night drinking a sea of tears for kin who will not return, one trudges over gilded sands never grasping scarlet sincerity or ardent admiration, one shrinks into the dark and bone-piercing hostility until promised tides bring a last melody, and one wanders in dreams awaiting an encounter in a stagnant foreign land. The sermon asks whether the angels will languish in an eternity with neither beginning nor end or commit atrocities in the name of false promises, and urges them to let lances of lunar light pierce the creator's delusions and reach a daybreak where nations need no longer beg leave to live. Read with the other angel-themed weapon text, it belongs to the angels' rebellion against the Heavenly Principles. The Angelos' Heptades text (Version 6.6) mentions a "cradle of tender Azurelight" shared by angel sisters before starlight from beyond ended their idyll, which echoes the sermon's starlight, though neither text explicitly calls itself part of the other.
### ULTRA +
The wiki's annotations match the four fates to the Lord of the Night, Nabu Malikata, Sybilla and Zibai. That match is the wiki's reading of the images. Treat it as strongly supported rather than stated. The wiki also notes that the diction resembles the item text of Polar Star, Tartaglia's signature bow.

## owners
### LITE
Canon gives no owner or wielder in the item text. It debuted in Version 5.7 during the same period as Skirk's character banner, but no source cited here says it belongs to her.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Game mechanics, not lore: a 5-star sword with base ATK 48 and a CRIT Rate secondary stat. Whitehill's Bestowal gives an ATK bonus for 12 seconds after an Elemental Skill is used, with a further ATK and CRIT DMG bonus while the wielder has 0 Energy. It was available from weapon event Wishes (Epitome Invocation, 2025-06-18 to 2025-07-08).
### LARGE +
The passive's name, Whitehill's Bestowal, fits the white-stone-mountain imagery of the text, but the passive is a gameplay effect and says nothing in canon about how the sword behaves in the world.

## roleplay
### LARGE
This archive's suggested use: treat Azurelight as an object that carries a rebel angel's creed, a blade turned from white to deep blue. Characters can read its sermon as a poem of defiance against fixed fate. Do not give it a wielder, a forging date or powers beyond what is listed.
