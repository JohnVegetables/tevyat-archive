## summary
### LITE
Wine and Song is a four-star catalyst of the Alley series: an elegantly bound songbook from the aristocratic era, scented with daisies and aged wine, whose composer is forgotten. It holds a popular tavern ballad about a heroic gentleman thief.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Item text: elegantly bound, with an indelible scent of daisies and finely aged wine on its pages.

## lore
### STANDARD
The ballad sings of a drunken gentleman thief (the wiki identifies him as Parsifal), no one knowing where he was from, who appeared in the slums like a ghost. He sings, drinks and wanders the rooftops, steals a great house's sword (the Alley Flash) and carries a black bow (the Alley Hunter), robs the rich and pays the poor.
### LARGE +
The maidens dream of him but he prefers a pint with his fellows. One night he ran amok in a mansion and took a thief's due plus an extra silver cup, and seeing a woman with sapphire-blue eyes at a lit window (the wiki links her to the Blue-Eyed Spear Witch) he pried the gem from the cup and gave it to her. When at last they toppled the indolent nobles, the song says, they left to explore distant lands together.
### ULTRA +
The item closes by saying that in the later age of righteous governance nobody sings of the gentleman thief any more nor needs one. It adds that such a carefree-opening, happy-ending tale is popular, and that however the real story went, wine and songs give the unfortunate courage to stand up to their oppressors. The ballad also jokes that he could drain Cider Lake were it filled with Death After Noon.

## owners
### STANDARD
A songbook; no owner is named. It was fashionable in the days of the ruling aristocracy.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Game mechanics: Alley-series four-star catalyst, base ATK 44, Energy Recharge 6.7% secondary, passive 'Ever-Changing'. Released in Version 1.4 via Weapon Event Wishes.
