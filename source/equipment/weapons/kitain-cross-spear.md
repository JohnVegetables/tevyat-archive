## summary
### LITE
Kitain Cross Spear is a four-star polearm: a strangely shaped, very heavy spear made by Kitain Bunsou for his own style of spearmanship. Item text says it is unwieldy for the untrained but destructive in expert hands. The Kitain family had long hunted the Tatarigami and served as the Prefects of Yashiori Island.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Item text calls the spear strangely shaped and massive. The wiki's trivia says its design is probably a reference to the cross spears of the Houzouin-ryuu, a real Japanese martial art school; that is a design note, not canon.

## lore
### STANDARD
The spear's weight makes it extremely unwieldy for the untrained, but in an expert's hands it can unleash great destructive power. The text says the Kitain family hunted the Tatarigami for many generations and held the office of Prefect of Yashiori Island.
### LARGE +
A children's song once spread across Inazuma: "Arataki of the Front Gate, Iwakura the Successor, Kitain the Serpent, Takamine the Mistsplitter". The text describes these as dazzling warriors who once lit the earth like comets, says many other names on the list have been lost to history, and ends by noting that someone who has been a demon hunter for so long may unavoidably take in some of the miasmic, tainted blood.
### ULTRA +
The song sets Kitain beside Arataki, Iwakura (the wiki links this to Iwakura Michihiro) and Takamine as one of the great fighters of an earlier age. The weapon text states no specific events; its diagram is a reward of the Orobashi's Legacy quest series, and the wiki ties no further story to it.

## owners
### STANDARD
Maker and user: Kitain Bunsou (historical.kitain-bunsou). The text gives no later owner. Thoma is shown wielding it in official art, which is presentation and not a claim of ownership.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not lore): four-star polearm. Base ATK 44, secondary stat Elemental Mastery 24. Passive "Samurai Conduct" (shared with Katsuragikiri Nagamasa): raises Elemental Skill DMG, and after a skill hits an opponent the wielder loses 3 Energy but regains Energy every 2s for 6s (once per 10s), even off-field. Forged from a diagram given on completing Orobashi's Legacy: Part V.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay use (this project's suggestion, not canon text): a fitting prop for an old guardian family whose long duty has left a mark on them.
