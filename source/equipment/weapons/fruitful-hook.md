## summary
### LITE
Fruitful Hook is a 4-star claymore from Natlan: a hooked blade made by the courier-hero Burkina of the Scions of the Canopy, with the hero Tenoch's help, to harvest the finest Flamegranates from the great mother tree.
### STANDARD +
Its story explains why the best Flamegranates are called "Mountain Kings" and how Burkina became companions with the Saurian Kongamato.

## appearance
### STANDARD
A hooked blade or sword with a hook, used to cut fruit loose and to block attacks. Do not add decoration.

## lore
### LITE
A young courier, Burkina, was royally annoyed at a Saurian living on the mountain's other side who mocked his movements, so he swore to climb to the peak and harvest the Flamegranates of the mother tree to show off to the most agile Yumkasaurus of all.
### STANDARD +
Skill alone did not help: winged Saurians harassed him at the summit and wild beasts waited under the tree. Burkina trained in patience: looking after the Saurians of the Flower-Feather Clan to learn their movements, hardening his body on boulders with the people of the mountain below the volcano, and seeking guidance from the Night-Wind high priests. Tenoch, travelling there, offered to join him; Burkina refused, since the training was the price of the result and he sensed that Tenoch's kindness might one day need to be repaid. Tenoch only said he could still do a small favour.
### LARGE +
On the day the fruit ripened Burkina climbed faster than a sprint over flat ground, waving the hooked sword to dodge or block the beasts. He had read the winged Saurians' tactics, and they lost their composure. As he reached the fruit a gust staggered him, and he nearly fell with the fruit in his arms; a green figure shot out its long tongue and pulled him back from death. It was the Yumkasaurus that had always tussled with him and had been following in the shadows. Burkina gave it the Flamegranates and earned its acknowledgement; this is how the Turnfire hero and The Mountain King Kongamato became companions.
### ULTRA +
Later the heroes of the Scions of the Canopy transplanted the mother tree to the foot of the mountain, and in memory the best Flamegranates were also named "Mountain Kings". The tree grows beneath Coatepec Mountain today. The text mixes pride and its cost: Burkina's complacency nearly kills him, and the rescue comes from the rival he scorned.

## owners
### LITE
Made by Burkina with Tenoch's help. No later wielder is named.
### LARGE +
Treat the story as the Scions of the Canopy's tale of how a rivalry became a friendship, not as a documented history of the blade.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not in-world): a 4-star claymore of the Heroes of Natlan series, base ATK 44 at level 1, secondary stat ATK%. Weapon Event Wishes from Version 5.1.
### LARGE +
Passive "The Weight of Falling Branches": raises Plunging Attack CRIT Rate (16% at refinement 1 up to 32% at refinement 5); after a Plunging Attack hits, Normal, Charged and Plunging Attack DMG rise by the same percentage for 10s.

## roleplay
### LARGE
Scions of the Canopy characters may tell it as a fond cautionary story: skill needs patience, and rivals may save you.

## entry: The Weight of Falling Branches
@keys: The Weight of Falling Branches
@keys2: Fruitful Hook, claymore
### STANDARD
The Weight of Falling Branches is the passive of the claymore Fruitful Hook (gameplay): it boosts Plunging Attack CRIT Rate and then Normal, Charged and Plunging Attack damage after a Plunging Attack lands, echoing Burkina's near-fall from the tree.
