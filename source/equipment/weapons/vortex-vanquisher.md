## summary
### LITE
Vortex Vanquisher is a five-star polearm of Liyue and the weapon Zhongli is shown with. Its item story is a sailors' legend from the days when ships sailed beside sea monsters: Rex Lapis once wielded a spear that pierced the rainbow and pinned a churning vortex in the middle of the deep sea, and whales and dolphins still gather there to sing.
### STANDARD +
Folklore adds that the vortex is not dead, only stilled, and that one day it will wake and bring a hurricane. The text asks who then will be able to subdue That Which Lies in the Deep.

## appearance
### LARGE
Item text gives no look for the spear apart from its power to pierce a rainbow. Zhongli appears with its ascended version in his portrait art and the Version 1.5 trailer, and the plain version in his demo.

## lore
### STANDARD
In ancient times Liyue Harbor was not at peace because of creatures from the deep. A great shadow dwelt in the oceanic abyss and used great vortices and whirlpools to smash even the strongest ships and drag prey into the sunless depths. Sailors also told of a divine island, rainbow-clad and fog-shrouded, that rises from the sea with treasure for whoever sets foot on it, though others said the island was itself a monster in torpor.
### LARGE +
Most such legends are bizarre, but sailors and skippers all believe one: Rex Lapis once wielded a spear that pierced the rainbow and pinned the vortex at the heart of the deep sea. Since then dolphins and whales are often seen gathering there, singing. Some say they mourn the god they revered; others that they are awed that he could pierce the rainbow and bring the sun down to them.
### ULTRA +
Folk legend adds that the vortex, held in a dead stillness by the spear, will one day wake; the omen is wind carrying the stench of the deep ashore, followed by a hurricane as big as a nine-headed hydra. The wiki links the great shadow to Osial, the Overlord of the Vortex, though the item text does not name him. The Chinese name, "Rainbow-Piercing Lance", is wordplay on an idiom about a white rainbow piercing the sun; in the lore it is Osial's rainbow that is pierced.

## owners
### STANDARD
The story credits the spear to Rex Lapis (Morax). In play it is Zhongli's weapon; the wiki notes that in some one-time story domains and trials he swaps it for the Staff of Homa, which is presentation only.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not lore): a five-star polearm from weapon event wishes. Base ATK 46, secondary stat ATK 10.8%. Passive "Golden Majesty", shared by the Liyue series: Shield Strength +20-40% and scoring hits raises ATK 4-8% for 8 seconds, up to 5 stacks, doubled while under a shield (refinements 1 to 5). A shield-friendly passive suits Zhongli's shielding.

## roleplay
### LARGE
Roleplay use (this project's suggestion, not canon text): the story is a sailors' tavern tale with deep-sea dread and awe at a god's power. Present it as legend; do not state that the vortex is about to wake.
