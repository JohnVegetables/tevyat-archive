## summary
### LITE
Finale of the Deep is a four-star sword of the Beaumont series, made by forging in Fontaine. Its description calls the longsword as silent as the ocean depths and older than its styling suggests. The item story says it once belonged to a musician whose name is forgotten, a proud figure of Remuria's golden era whose doctrine of music as authority ended in catastrophe.
### STANDARD +
The sword is covered in ostentatious decoration and gems, which the text takes as proof that its owner was no ordinary instrumentalist. Lynette is shown wielding it in the Version 4.0 trailer and other promotional material.

## appearance
### STANDARD
A ceremonial longsword with layer upon layer of decoration and shining gems. When wielded by a character who has Arkhe, the teardrop-shaped gem on the hilt glows with Pneuma or Ousia.
### LARGE +
The wiki lists the base and second-ascension looks as separate models. Lynette carries the ascended version in the 4.0 trailer, her character demo and her Collected Miscellany, and the unascended one in the Into the Sunlight event.

## lore
### STANDARD
Legend holds that the sword belonged to a musician, now nameless, who was fiercely proud of an ancient grand symphony he never performed for anyone, believing that no age of chaos would answer it with applause or lament, and that no one would transcribe so complex a work. An opera in Fontaine portrays his life: nobody knows his origins, but everyone understands his tragedy. Like many of humble birth, he decided his fate in the city to which all waterways lead.
### LARGE +
In the text he teaches at a towering theater academy, telling students that musicians must be like those who transcend, voicing the sublime with passion so that glory follows passion, unity follows glory and order follows unity. He casts music as the scepter and shield of authority and holds that the passion of vanquishing foes and loving comrades divides humanity into masters and slaves. This understanding raised him to the loftiest of halls, and in Remuria's golden era listeners were intoxicated by it.
### ULTRA +
The story ends when a mountainous wave destroys the academy. In the wreckage, the skeletal remains of people burned away by the musician's passion are exposed beneath the foundations. The text thus presents the sublime he preached as having been built on those who were consumed by it. The item text does not say who the musician was; the wiki links the places named (the Collegium Phonascorum, the Domus Aurea, Remuria) to the setting.

## owners
### STANDARD
The nameless musician of legend is the only former owner named in the text. In the current game it is associated with Lynette through trailer and promotional appearances.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Game mechanics, not story. Passive An End Sublime: using an Elemental Skill grants a percentage ATK increase for 15 seconds and a Bond of Life worth 25% of Max HP (once per 10 seconds). Clearing the Bond of Life gives extra flat ATK based on the amount cleared, for 15 seconds, and only if the Bond was increased by this weapon at some point. Base ATK is 44 with ATK 6% as its secondary stat.
