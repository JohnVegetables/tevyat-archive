## summary
### LITE
Crimson Moon's Semblance is a 5-star polearm (released in Version 4.6) whose text recalls an ancient Khaenri'ahn dynasty that revered the crimson moon. Its description calls it a keen-edged weapon with a luster as dazzling as the crimson moon, once regarded by that dynasty as a ritual object that could bridge two worlds. Arlecchino is its signature wielder in gameplay.
### STANDARD +
Nobody in the item text remembers the beliefs and ceremonies tied to the weapon; the dynasty is described as a bygone age. The story is a lament about the fall of the Crimson Moon's followers and the survival of only the epithet "Balemoon."

## appearance
### STANDARD
A polearm with a bright, keen edge, its sheen compared to the crimson moon. When Arlecchino wields it, it transforms into a scythe during her Normal Attacks, Charged Attack and Elemental Skill. In her "Masque of the Red Death" state it shows a red aura running down the staff (gameplay presentation).

## lore
### LITE
The item story is a sombre account of Khaenri'ah's old royal clan and the Crimson Moon, not of the weapon's own forging.
### STANDARD +
It opens with an invocation to the High Lord of the Nobles of Khaenri'ah: the crimson-red moon has long since fallen into the abyssal firmament, and the blindness of that bloodline has ended the line. A cup of wine is offered to the lord.
### LARGE +
Per the story, in an ancient era, before the dark sun lit the underground, an ancient and glorious clan ruled the kingdom. Dogmatic priests persuaded the muddle-minded king that the Crimson Moon's remnants in the sky ruled all, because the color of moonlight flows beneath mortal flesh and the darkness at the bottom of the abyss would also come from it. The king was therefore to take the name of the Crimson Moon and judge fate by the light and flame of two worlds. The court sought transcendent individuals to build countless glorious towers and prayed for the long-dead Crimson Moon to save them. Astrologers branded as heretics then glimpsed, in the inverted image of the false sky, the origin of the world's fate. Flames of doubt and fury spread through the dreamless realm and burned even the moonlight-hued palace.
### ULTRA +
By the time of the "blackened sun" (the Eclipse Dynasty), the Crimson Moon's name had faded along with the crimson that had flowed; only the epithet "Balemoon" survived. Neither those who suffered the curse nor those not yet tainted by fate would again call themselves followers of the moon. The few survivors hid in the shadows and longed for the Crimson Moon to repay their vengeance, but that never came, and the dark sun fell to the same ignorance and arrogance. When destruction (the Cataclysm) neared again, only mocking moonlight fell upon the shadow of a sun long burnt to ash. The closing verse addresses fate as submitting to a tyrannical usurper (the wiki links this to the Heavenly Principles) and speaks of mocking the destiny "she" wove (linked to Sonnet), until the last shadow of the ashen sun burns away the old world and the Crimson Moon witnesses an immaculate dawn. Those links are the wiki's annotations on the text, not stated outright by it.

## owners
### LITE
Arlecchino, the Fourth Fatui Harbinger and head of the House of the Hearth, is its signature wielder.
### STANDARD +
Arlecchino is a descendant of the Crimson Moon Dynasty and is titled "Dire Balemoon," echoing the story's word. The weapon's text does not mention her, and canon does not spell out how the weapon relates to her in story terms.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not literal lore): a 5-star Polearm with a base ATK of 48 and a secondary stat of CRIT Rate. Its passive, Ashen Sun's Shadow, grants a Bond of Life equal to 25% of Max HP when a Charged Attack hits an opponent (at most once every 14 seconds). While the wielder has a Bond of Life they gain a DMG Bonus, and a further bonus if the Bond of Life is at least 30% of Max HP. The bonuses rise with refinement, and Bonds of Life from this weapon can stack. It was released on 2024-04-24 through Weapon Event Wishes and is also available in Chronicled Wishes.
### LARGE +
Its ascension materials include the Goblet of the Pristine Sea items, Hilt materials from a boss, and gear-type common materials. Its passive name, "Ashen Sun's Shadow," echoes the burned-out sun in its story.
