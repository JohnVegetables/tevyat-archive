## summary
### LITE
Starcaller's Watch is a 5-star catalyst: a statue carved in the shape of an eagle, a sacred relic of the Masters of the Night-Wind passed down from generation to generation.
### STANDARD +
Its story is about the girl chosen to guide the people into night's slumber, and about the man who believed he could manipulate her.

## appearance
### STANDARD
A statue carved in the shape of an eagle; the story calls it a statuette and associates eagle feathers with the star-caller's crown. Add no other details.

## lore
### LITE
In an ancient, exalted contract the messenger of night chose a child with a star-bright soul, set a crown of eagle feathers on her brow, and charged her to guide a formless ancient people into night's slumber.
### STANDARD +
The text pictures her as a young woman, once kneeling before dragons, who later turned and led an outlander through a wall of fumes with flute and song.
### LARGE +
A self-proclaimed sage (the wiki identifies him with Waxaklahun Ubah Kan) schemed in the dragons' dreams. A covenant between the valley people and the noble scions cut off the war-chiefs' bloodlust and left only the silence of death on the shrouded paths, so that even the bravest warrior of the Tezcatepetonco valley served the star-caller. The sage disliked the decay of the fallen and used small tricks to incite her to rebel against the dragons, believing human hearts easy to steer: add fuel to suffering, speak of hope, and she hung on his words.

As he had predicted, she led an outlander the sage had never seen through the fumes, and sent her silent servant Dinga from the deep valley. He expected the flames of slaughter to be drawn to this isolated land.
### ULTRA +
Many years later, when the hero Maghan plunged the Python King's golden dagger into his former master's heart, the sage realized that the seeds of betrayal had long been planted: not aimed at the sleeping dragons as he had imagined, but carried alongside the song of the newborn Wayob from the deepest halls of the Night Realm. This secured the foundations of the tribe called "Mictlan" for a thousand years. The seven-layered curtains in the cell sealed the ancient, lightless land of disorder, and ants stole the secret rites the sage could never obtain.

The closing words are the girl's own, in the wiki's reading: do not put all hope in the night or the benevolence of saints, and above all do not trust the one who calls himself a sage; humans can rely only on themselves. Even if all goes as she intends she will only stabilize the boundary for a hundred years, so Dinga must tell the people of the tribes of their heroes, stretch their memories out on woven scrolls and anchor this home that will be named Natlan. She gives him the statuette and promises to watch over him, bound to his shoulder like a silken string, until their souls are entwined in profound peace.

## owners
### LITE
The Masters of the Night-Wind keep it as a sacred relic. The wiki names its first wielder as the Holy Sovereign who was Maghan's master.
### LARGE +
Maghan (earlier named Dinga) is given the statuette in the story's last lines. No source shown establishes who holds it now, and Citlali is not stated to carry it.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not in-world): a 5-star catalyst, base ATK 44 at level 1, secondary stat Elemental Mastery; Weapon Event Wishes; introduced in Version 5.3. The passive only triggers while the wielder is on-field.
### LARGE +
Passive "Offering Unto Wind and Sun": Elemental Mastery is increased (100 at refinement 1 up to 200 at refinement 5). Within 15s after the wielder creates a shield, "Mirror of Night" makes the active character deal 28% to 56% more DMG to nearby opponents, once every 14s. Ascension uses Sacred Lord Delirious materials, Will-series boss materials and whistle common materials.

## roleplay
### LARGE
For scenes: shamans of the Masters of the Night-Wind may regard the watch as a keepsake of the first Holy Sovereign. They should speak of the girl and Maghan with respect, and leave the sage's role as the text frames it (a schemer outplayed).

Offering Unto Wind and Sun is the passive of the catalyst Starcaller's Watch (gameplay): an Elemental Mastery boost, plus the Mirror of Night damage bonus for the active character after the wielder creates a shield.

## entry: Maghan's Master
@keys: Maghan's Master, Holy Sovereign
@keys2: Starcaller's Watch, Mictlan
### LARGE
Per the wiki, Maghan's master was the last Holy Sovereign of the tribe in the Tezcatepetonco Range that became Mictlan. Her servant Dinga later became Maghan; he killed her on Och-Kan's orders, and after her death she became the tribe's Wayob. The Starcaller's Watch text is told in her voice and is the source for her being its first wielder.
