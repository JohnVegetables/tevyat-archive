## summary
### LITE
The Royal Longsword is a four-star sword of the Royal series. It was the sword of a noble who ruled Mondstadt, made of premium materials and still as sharp as ever. Its item story laments that noble swordsmanship, once meant to refine wisdom and elegance, decayed into formality, and recalls an oath of protection sworn 2,600 years ago.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Item text calls it exquisitely crafted, with carvings and embellishments that testify to its owner's stature. No specific design is described.

## lore
### STANDARD
Swordsmanship was once a required skill for nobles, but as a discipline for wisdom and elegance rather than for combat. Both qualities were eventually all but forgotten and the skill became a formality, even though the sword itself stayed sharp through generations.
### LARGE +
The story then turns to 2,600 years ago, the era of Mondstadt's most ancient inhabitants, who swore a solemn oath after the new Anemo Archon descended and reformed the world. The oath has two parts: devotion to the plains, hills and forests of Mondstadt, and devotion to its lasting freedom from the blizzard (Boreas) and the tyrant (Decarabian), whose coldness and oppression the text calls one and the same.
### ULTRA +
The closing lines promise that whatever may come, human tyranny or monster infestation, and even if the tablet bearing the oath is destroyed, the oath will be carried by the winds and defend Mondstadt like a loving father. The 'as always' refrain matches the Gunnhildr clan's motto as quoted in Jean's voice-over, per the wiki; the item itself does not draw the connection.

## owners
### STANDARD
Item text names only a noble who ruled over Mondstadt.
### LARGE +
Albedo is shown wielding this sword in his official character demo and a Collected Miscellany video; that is presentation, not a canon ownership claim.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Game mechanics: Royal-series passive 'Focus' (stacking CRIT Rate, max 5 stacks, cleared by a CRIT). Four-star sword, base ATK 42, ATK 9% secondary. Obtained from Paimon's Bargains.
