## summary
### LITE
Etherlight Spindlelute is a 4-star catalyst: a lute whose golden strings shine like sunlight, which appears to be a relic from the Seelie of old.
### STANDARD +
It was a reward of the Clink Clank Clash event in Nod-Krai. Its story takes place in the hidden court of the Seelie by Lake Amsvartnir, where a woman's lament recalls a dream and the last Seelie play a final tune.

## appearance
### STANDARD
A catalyst: a lute with golden strings as radiant as sunlight, apparently a Seelie relic. Add no further detail.

## lore
@availability: limited
### LITE
Beyond the ruins of an ancient palace, at the edge of the Nod-Krai wasteland, stands the quiet sacred forest of Lake Amsvartnir. Legend says that whoever breaks the golden branch in the forest will find the royal court of the Seelie, hidden from mortal eyes by day.
### STANDARD +
The court is a kingdom of the past, forgotten by time and space and untouched by heavenly light. Its owner has long departed, leaving a neglected courtyard and a bare hall, yet a young woman's silent lament still echoes through the palace.
### LARGE +
The lament says that the titan craftsman of the skies once wove the destinies of all living beings, with the golden harp as its instrument of fate, and that this was the heavens' divine plan. Yet even the ignorant can dream, and in her dream a new future appeared: men raising a tower to the heavens whose apostle wages war on the sky, roaring beasts larger than mountains breaking from the earth's bonds, and three moons shining on the sea before an ancient dragon rose like a tide and eclipsed them. Everything vanished when she woke, leaving only the memory of a glance from star-like eyes.
### ULTRA +
She wonders whether that was love or only delusion woven from hope, ambition and a longing for death, and says it no longer matters: their story has ended and became Elysium's finale. The text then likens insects chirping on a long-gone autumn night to a chorus of exiles singing mankind's oldest song, people who lost the place of their bodies and souls and keep only songs and memories of a homeland. The last singers, the first Seelie, played their final tune in the hall of angels. A wolf who once broke into the place has long gone, and only unfulfilled dreams remain, chasing phantoms. The text never explains who the dreaming woman is.

## owners
### LITE
No owner is named. The Seelie are its implied makers or keepers, and the court's owner is described as long departed.
### LARGE +
On the wiki the item text's "owner" of the court and "first Seelie" are linked to Koitar, but the item itself does not name that figure. Do not state who played the lute without a source.

## gameplay
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not in-world): a 4-star catalyst, base ATK 42 at level 1, secondary stat Energy Recharge 10.0%; introduced in Version 6.0 as a reward from the Clink Clank Clash flagship event (2025-09-12 to 2025-09-29, requiring Adventure Rank 20 and the Prologue archon quest), not from Wishes. Refinement uses Golden Strings rather than duplicate weapons, and four can be obtained in the event. Passive "Last Singer": for 20s after using an Elemental Skill, the wielder's Elemental Mastery increases by 100 (refinement 1) up to 200 (refinement 5).

## roleplay

For scenes: treat it as a quiet Seelie-court legend from Nod-Krai, half-remembered and melancholy. Characters may know the golden-branch legend without knowing who the lamenting woman is, because the text leaves her unnamed. ## entry: The Royal Court of the Seelie
@keys: Seelie court, Royal Court of the Seelie, Etherlight Spindlelute
@keys2: Amsvartnir, golden branch, lute
### STANDARD
According to the Etherlight Spindlelute's story, the Seelie court by Lake Amsvartnir can be found by breaking the golden branch in the sacred forest, and is hidden from mortal eyes by day. Its owner has long departed. A woman's lament still echoes there, and the first Seelie played their last tune in its hall.
