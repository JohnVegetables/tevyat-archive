## summary
### LITE
A jade pearl wrapped in moonlight whose gentle warmth lingers like a song even on the coldest winter night. A 5-star catalyst from Version 6.3, its text a lullaby and a vision of the Moon Maiden.
### STANDARD +
It frames Columbina's memories of the three moons.

## appearance
### STANDARD
A jade pearl shrouded in moonlight. Canon text describes only the pearl and its warmth.

## lore
### LITE
The text is framed by a lullaby to a little dove, child of the moon: may the moon be with you in your slumber. Between its verses is Columbina's vision of the Moon Maiden drifting through the night.
### STANDARD +
A shattered husk falls toward Nod-Krai, the misty land that never became a moon. In a prison beyond the reach of Time and Wind, Columbina, called the one who traverses backward, watches the eternal slumber of three sisters.
### LARGE +
A silvery vessel once patrolled between mountains and seas at the meeting of day and night, ferrying the souls of the untainted across the corrupted mortal realm to rest on the dark side of the moon. The three mistresses of the golden hall could not meet the gaze of mortals: the Lord of the Seven Calamities would not tolerate gods pitying mortal suffering, so even the three sisters, spinning silver threads of fate, were bound in threefold silence.
### ULTRA +
The three moons each walked a different path: turning against their corrupted master of old to protect the living, looking away from one another so that the ember of defiance might survive, or staining themselves with darkness to take vengeance on a tyrant. At the end of every path lay oblivion, the future once lamented by the vanished moons in the era "when three were still three". Yet in her private memories Columbina sees a golden star not of this world, reflected in a cold courtyard under the silver moon, and believes it will light her way home. In the next instant "three are no longer three": the Lord of the Crossroads (unnamed in the text) looks back at the fourth path beyond the three-way fork, and waits among honeyed veils for her appointed meeting with her companions beneath the stars. The golden star is tied by the wiki to the Traveler; the text itself only says a golden star.

## owners
### LITE
No wielder is named; the text concerns Columbina but does not make her the owner.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not in-world): 5-star catalyst, CRIT DMG secondary. Passive Ballad of the Crossroads raises Max HP and, on triggering Lunar reactions, restores Energy and grants Bountiful Sea's Sacred Wine, a Max HP and Lunar CRIT DMG bonus.

## roleplay
### LARGE
Handle as a delicate, lullaby-like heirloom. Keep the three moons and the golden star as poetic memories belonging to Columbina's perspective, and do not state who owns it.
