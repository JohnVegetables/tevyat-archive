## summary
### LITE
Blackmarrow Lantern is a 4-star catalyst of the Rossum series: a lantern with a faint ghostly glow, held in a midnight-black alloy that looks like interlocking bones.
### STANDARD +
Its description says it was once revered as a precious treasure by a certain secret organization. Its story is a tale of the Godfather of Nod-Krai's black market, who binds three thieves to a creed that theft alone can restore balance, and of the butler who then takes the master thief's black spine bone.

## appearance
### STANDARD
A catalyst: a lantern giving off a faint, ghostly glow, contained by a midnight-black alloy resembling interlocking bones. Add no further detail.

## lore
### LITE
The item story opens with a parable: power is a great edifice, and sheer chance alone decides which brick becomes foundation and which becomes spire. A single brick that refuses the false order can bring the whole building down.
### STANDARD +
The Godfather's secret treasure is said to lie deep in the black market, and thieves gather to take it in a battle of all against all, where fellow thieves are the deadliest threat. Three get past the guards and into the vault: a fae who reads people like maps and avoids harm, a young lady who cuts every lock with her blade, and a hunter who treats the maze as a harsh hunting ground. All three are defeated by the vault, and a butler in black binds them and brings them before the Godfather.
### LARGE +
The Godfather, eating a Zaytun Peach, says he will reward them. He holds that order serves only its architects, that the rich get richer while the poor get less, and that thievery is the only way to restore balance. Those who will keep the thief's code must eat a fruit in the presence of the master thief's remains. The fae, tired since childhood of hollow noble luxury, eats the Bulle Fruit; the young lady, who never bowed to those above her, swallows the Wolfhook; the hunter, who once retreated into the cold forest rather than live with a corrupt world, takes the Valberry. The Godfather calls the pact sealed and names them the backbone of resistance against injustice.
### ULTRA +
Alone afterwards, the Godfather runs his hand over the bare vault walls, having removed the crucial brick from the edifice of order. He does not foresee what follows: he clutches his abdomen in pain while the butler smiles. She takes the master thief's black spine bone, leaves a Calla Lily to serve as an antidote, and tells him she spares his life because his words showed the master thief's ideals to the thieves. Her last line: true justice lies not in order itself but in the fact that no single person can impose it on others. The text does not say who she serves or why she acts.

The Rossum-series lore (and the wiki's Soini page) summarises the same episode: the Godfather lured three rival thieves into his vault and bound them to his creed, and his butler betrayed him by seizing the master thief's relic.

## owners

No owner is named. The lantern is tied to the Godfather, and the secret organization that revered it is not identified.
### LARGE +
The series lore identifies the Godfather as Soini, who founded the Fruity Order. That identification comes from series and wiki material, not from this item's own text.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not in-world): a 4-star catalyst, base ATK 41 at level 1, secondary stat Elemental Mastery 48; part of the Rossum series, introduced in Version 6.0. It is forged, not wished for: the recipe uses a Borderland Catalyst Billet, 50 Rainbowdrop Crystal, 50 White Iron Chunk and 500 Mora, and its diagram is sold by Lyulka at Rossum Workshop in Nod-Krai. Passive "Token of Covenant": increases Bloom DMG (48% at refinement 1, up to 96% at refinement 5) and Lunar-Bloom DMG (12% to 24%), with an additional Lunar-Bloom DMG bonus of the same size under Moonsign: Ascendant Gleam.

## roleplay
### LARGE
For scenes: the story works as black-market lore in Nod-Krai, a legend about a Godfather and a pact of thieves. Let characters disagree on what the butler was and what happened to the Godfather afterwards, as the text leaves both open.
