## summary
### LITE
Redhorn Stonethresher is a five-star claymore whose item story is a folk tale. Its full name is the "Mighty Redhorn Stoic Stonethreshing Gilded Goldcrushing Lion Lord", and it was the beloved blade of an eccentric known as the "Otogi Kongou Lion King". Because both names were too long, the village children called the weapon the Redhorn Pestle and its wielder the Otogi King.
### STANDARD +
The tale is told partly in affectionate mockery: the Otogi King was a boaster with real strength, and the weapon's legendary reputation outlived the man who never returned.

## appearance
### STANDARD
The item story describes the Redhorn Pestle as a weapon made from an enchanted monster's horn. It does not describe colour or shape beyond that; the in-game model is a very large blade. Avoid inventing further detail.

## lore
### STANDARD
In the children's telling, the weapon's bite made bake-kitsune, bake-danuki and evil demons beg for mercy, and even the Great Tengu Reizenbou of Mt. Yougou feared it. The children did not believe this. The text says the Otogi King really was strong, so strong that seven men together could not push him out of a sumo ring, and that he would kick a tree to bring down fruit for children who could not reach it. He also once kicked down a whole tree, a Lavender Melon tree, and was chased into the mountains by an old man.
### LARGE +
Other tales have him crash an official autumn leaf-viewing concert while shouting that the Lion King had arrived and demons should beware, then wrestle a small oni who happened to be in the mood for a bout, with a result the text calls too tragic to watch. He never earned the right to wear the Shogun's colours, so the children doubted he could ever tame the "youkai of song". He answered such doubts with excuses, such as having had the chills after a long night of drinking, and promised to cut off the horns of the monsters and to deal with the creatures from beyond the ocean. He also told the children to follow the fox envoy and hide, saying he would come back for wrestling bouts later.
### ULTRA +
The text then reports what became of the boasts. The oni who wrestled him, remembered by the children as having flung him into the sky, later fled with her arm and horn cut off; the Great Tengu withdrew into the mountains and woods and no longer appeared before humans; and so the empty boasts attached to the greatsword came true in the end, though the Lion King himself never returned. The Lavender Melon tree he kicked down was tended and bore fruit again. The tale sits against the larger historical background of the Cataclysm (the "creatures from beyond the ocean") and the Kitsune Saiguu, but the item text never names the Cataclysm or the Saiguu outright in connection with him. Uraku Misugiri's item text gives a more concrete picture of him as someone "of the Front Gate" who loved plays, toys and fine clothes, and whose usual weapon in anecdotes is a huge blade with a long, hard-to-pronounce name, which that text identifies as this one.

## owners
### STANDARD
Former owner: the Otogi Kongou Lion King, called the Otogi King by children. No other owner is named. The same figure is the recipient of Uraku Misugiri in that sword's text. The weapon is an ordinary player weapon in-game.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not lore): a five-star claymore from the Otogi King series, base ATK 44, secondary stat CRIT DMG 19.2%. Passive "Gokadaiou Otogibanashi": increases DEF by 28-56% and adds bonus damage to Normal and Charged Attacks equal to 40-80% of DEF at refinement 1-5. It was the event-exclusive weapon of the Version 2.3 Epitome Invocation, alongside Skyward Harp.

## roleplay
### LARGE
Roleplay use (this project's suggestion, not canon text): the weapon is a boast turned into a legend. It suits characters who tell the story with relish and a touch of teasing; anyone solemn about it will be missing the tone of the text. Do not give the Otogi King a death scene that the item does not supply.

## entry: Otogi King
@keys: Otogi King, Otogi Kongou Lion King, Lion King
@keys2: Redhorn, Stonethresher, Pestle, claymore
@version: 2.3
### STANDARD
The Otogi King is the nickname given by village children to the Otogi Kongou Lion King, a loud, eccentric Inazuma warrior who carried the Redhorn Pestle and bragged that he would send monsters packing. The Redhorn Stonethresher text portrays him as a boastful prankster; Uraku Misugiri's text adds that he loved plays, toys and clothes and went into battle in gold brocade with painted features. It also describes him as an oni who earned the villagers' nickname "The Great Idiot" and did not dislike it. His name remains unconfirmed beyond those titles.

## entry: Redhorn Pestle and Mikoshi Chiyo
@keys: Redhorn Pestle
@keys2: Chiyo, oni, wrestling
@version: 2.3
### LARGE
In the tale, the Otogi King wrestled a petite oni who, in the children's memory, tossed him into the sky. The wiki links the unnamed petite oni to Mikoshi Chiyo, who in the text later loses an arm and a horn and flees; the item text itself does not name her. Uraku Misugiri's text shows an Oni Chiyo dancing with a sword, linked to the same wiki page.
