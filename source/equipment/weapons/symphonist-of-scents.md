## summary
### LITE
Symphonist of Scents is a 5-star polearm: an elegantly built baton said to have been wielded by a great musician, Euergetia, to bridge tune and taste.
### STANDARD +
Its story is a quiet one about the Remurian Harmost Euergetia and her use of authority to comfort homesick conquered people.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Canon describes only an elegantly built polearm shaped like a conductor's baton. Arkhe-aligned wielders show a Pneuma or Ousia mark. Add nothing else.

## lore
### LITE
During the long conquest of the tribes and kingdoms of the High Sea by the Legion Athanatoi, the Harmost Euergetia listened to the pleas of the conquered. The homesick people, though awed by the golden capital, missed the cuisines they had once enjoyed.
### STANDARD +
Stone golems need neither food nor drink, but the people were frail flesh and would grow dejected at poor fare. The God King's directives were the supreme rule, yet Euergetia reasoned that fulfilling a tiny wish was no offence in a symphony meant to embrace all living things. Her golden-haired colleague (the wiki links Boethius) grumbled behind her back but never stopped her.
### LARGE +
She began an attunement in which hearing and taste could play in harmony: war drums and clashing swords could sing as one, and a melody with a simple meal could remind a slave of dishes they could only taste in memory, raising morale. The text admits it was mere illusory comfort, and asks whether illusions and dust are not the origin and end of all things.
### ULTRA +
Before emptying herself of desires, she had once been enchanted by gentle music and honey-sweet confections. Had she continued, the text says, even the uplifted ones might have enjoyed divine dishes and been anchored in a commonality. The golden empire never played the final notes: a veiled change overturned the fortress of white stone in a night, and both wicked and good plunged into the ocean depths. The simple wish to enjoy delicious food endured to present-day Fontaine. The wiki's Euergetia page adds that she died in the destruction of Remuria, that Boethius wished to revive Remuria with her but could not, and that she had a sweet tooth.

## owners
### LITE
Euergetia, per the description (she is called the benefactrix on Remurian engravings).
### LARGE +
Escoffier is paired with it at release only by convention (see uncertainties). Do not state that she holds the baton without a source.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not in-world): a 5-star polearm, base ATK 46 at level 1, secondary stat CRIT DMG; Weapon Event Wishes; introduced in Version 5.6. Passive "Seasoned Symphony": raises ATK, with an extra boost while the wielder is off-field; after the wielder initiates healing, they and those healed gain "Sweet Echoes" (ATK up for 3s), also while off-field. Values scale with refinement.

## roleplay
### LARGE
For scenes: the baton is an old tale of food and kindness; a chef or a Fontainian historian might know the story as a charming footnote. Keep the Remurians as conquerors, with the story's gentleness not erasing the slavery that frames it.

## entry: Euergetia
@keys: Euergetia, Symphonist of Scents
@keys2: Harmost, baton, benefactrix
### STANDARD
Euergetia was one of Remuria's four Harmosts, called the benefactrix. The weapon story of Symphonist of Scents shows her using her power to let conquered, homesick people taste their native dishes in their imaginations. She perished in Remuria's destruction.
