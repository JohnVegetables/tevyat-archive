## summary
### LITE
The Staff of the Scarlet Sands is a 5-star polearm of the Deshret series. The in-game description calls it one of a paired set of obsidian scepters that grant the right to lead the people of the desert, though no retainer now lives who can verify the proper appearance of these regalia. Its story is a monologue by King Deshret about how the world should be ordered, ending in his longing for a perfect world.
### STANDARD +
The staff is the polearm partner of the Key of Khaj-Nisut. Together the wiki treats them as keys to the Orchard of Pairidaeza, the eternal oasis made as Nabu Malikata's resting place.

## appearance
### STANDARD
An obsidian scepter or staff. Canon text gives no more detail. The in-game description stresses that no one is left alive who can confirm what the genuine regalia looked like, so depictions are uncertain.

## lore
### LITE
The narrator, speaking as a creator, lists how the world was made: the sun and three moons (as a woman once described them to him), weight so that sand sank into earth, and seven sage monks to rule the paths of earth, water and stars. After the original world's barriers fell and dark poison seeped in, spikes pierced the earth to heal it.
### STANDARD +
He then lists what must be removed for his ideal: the cycle of seven, the barrier between life and death, the sun, moons and weight, the original rules and verdicts, and all creatures, so that none steal wisdom. He pictures the Sand King sleeping alone, drawing up new theorems, and "our paradise" regained; that oasis was never a paradise for the many.
### LARGE +
The text ends with a request: the narrator does not want the poison taken in by mistake to remain in the world, and hopes "she" (the wiki identifies the Greater Lord Rukkhadevata) might, for old friendship, help; if not for his sake then for their mutual friend. Per the wiki, the text is Deshret speaking about his friend Nabu Malikata, an exiled Seelie, and the punishment placed on the Seelie after the war. The wiki ties the "spikes" to the Celestial Nails and the war to the one in which the Dragon King Nibelung brought the Abyss to Teyvat; this is its cross-reference of other sources.
### ULTRA +
Two things should be kept apart. The statements in the text are the narrator's own plan and wishes: he considers his rules "more elegant and precise" than the existing ones. The wiki additionally reads the later quest events (the spread of the forbidden knowledge as plagues in Deshret's civilisation and Rukkhadevata's intervention, which cost her power) as the answer to the request. Treat that last part as a reading of other quests, not as something the staff's text confirms.

## owners
### LITE
No owner is named in the text. Cyno appears with the ascended staff in his official art and character demo, and Candace with the unascended one in her wish art and TCG card (wiki trivia).
### LARGE +
Per the wiki, Cyno names the staff as his weapon in the After Repose quest. As canon is not explicit on whether the objects shown are the original regalia, do not claim any character holds the original scepter.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not in-world): 5-star polearm released in Version 3.1 (2022-09-28), level 1 base ATK 44, secondary stat CRIT Rate. Passive Heat Haze at Horizon's End gives bonus ATK equal to a percentage of the wielder's Elemental Mastery; when an Elemental Skill hits opponents, the Dream of the Scarlet Sands effect adds more for 10 s, up to 3 stacks. Multiple targets hit give multiple stacks, and the effect is not gained off-field.
### LARGE +
At refinement 1 the base conversion is 52% of Elemental Mastery and each stack adds 28%; refinement 5 gives 104% and 56%. Ascension uses Chaos Storage, Chaos Module and Chaos Bolt with Fungal Spores, Luminescent Pollen and Crystalline Cyst Dust.

## roleplay
### LARGE
The staff suits discussions of Deshret's age: scholars and priests may mention scepters that confer the right to lead the desert's people. Keep the uncertainty the description itself states: nobody can say what the regalia truly looked like.
