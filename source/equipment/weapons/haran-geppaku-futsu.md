## summary
### LITE
Haran Geppaku Futsu is a celebrated sword of the Futsu school of smiths in Inazuma. It is the only surviving blade forged by the great smith Masagomaru (Futsu Masayoshi). Its name pairs "Haran", meaning turmoil or roiling waves, with the Geppaku Futsu series, of which it is the "Rippling Upheaval" blade.
### STANDARD +
Its item text is drawn from the "Anthology of Inazuman Blades" by Inspector Mikoshi Nagamasa of Tatarasuna. Two Geppaku Futsu swords were made by order of the school: Hasui, the Wavespike, and Haran, the Rippling Upheaval.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Canon text describes the school's blades rather than this sword's fittings: the Hasui blade was pale blue with a hamon like rippling water, while Haran is said to be formidable as a driving hurricane. The two blades look very similar but differ greatly in quality, in the text's account.

## lore
### LITE
Two Geppaku Futsu swords were made. Hasui was forged by Futsu Minori, third-generation head of the school; its pale blue blade and water-like hamon were often seen at the waist of the Shogun's valet. It was later broken in a mortal duel to decide the fate of the oni people and sent back for reforging.
### STANDARD +
By then Minori was worn down by drink, old wounds and the lingering memory of Tatarigami, and her son Futsu Hiroyoshi, fourth-generation head, could not match her skill. The task fell to Hiroyoshi's sworn brother Futsu Masayoshi, called Masagomaru, an orphan taken in by Minori, illiterate and mute from birth. Ordered to revive Hasui's beauty, he inscribed its name as it had been before, and the reforging let the school's masterpiece re-enter the world. He named only one work of his own: Haran.
### LARGE +
Minori helped Masagomaru pass her techniques on to Hiroyoshi, and wished Masagomaru himself to inherit the school, but he declined repeatedly; the fame of Haran nearly affected Hiroyoshi's succession. Secure that his sworn brother was independent, he left for other lands and learned from other famed smiths. In his late years he had three pupils he was proudest of, Kaedehara Kagemitsu, Niwa Nagamitsu and Akame Mitsunaga, who created the Isshin Sansaku, the three great works of the Isshin Art.
### ULTRA +
The text includes Masagomaru's own recollections: a mute, abandoned child drawn like a moth to the warmth of the forge-fire, fed rice by the surly Minori, who named him after the metallic sand covering him. She told him of the old wound on one side of her body, her mother's and brother's wishes, the crimson hakama she could never wear and the giant wave that would devour everything. He once saw her weeping while hammering iron; she told him to forget it, then declared him a good keeper of secrets. He regrets not accepting her offer to drink together. Hasui was made as gently beautiful as the sea at night, while the mute Masayoshi's Haran was as fierce as a driving hurricane.

## owners
### LITE
Futsu Masayoshi (Masagomaru) is the smith, not an owner. No wielder is named in the item text.
### LARGE +
The Hasui blade was seen at the waist of the Shogun's valet; the wiki calls Hasui a currently unplayable sword. Nothing states that Haran passed to any specific clan, so avoid assigning it to the Kamisato.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not in-world): a 5-star sword released in Version 2.6 on the weapon event Wish. Base ATK 46 with CRIT Rate as secondary stat.
### LARGE +
Its passive, Honed Flow, grants an All Elemental DMG Bonus (12% at refinement 1, 24% at refinement 5). When other nearby party members use Elemental Skills, the wielder gains a Wavespike stack (maximum 2, once per 0.3 seconds). When the wielder uses an Elemental Skill, the stacks are consumed to grant Rippling Upheaval, increasing Normal Attack DMG for 8 seconds by 20% per stack consumed at refinement 1 (40% at refinement 5).

## roleplay
### LARGE
For scenes: a smith-craft legend. Inazuman smiths and swordsmen may know the Anthology's account; most would not recognise the blade. Keep Masagomaru's muteness and the Futsu school's family bonds in mind, and do not invent a wielder.

### STANDARD
The Futsu school of smiths made the Geppaku Futsu pair. Third-generation head Futsu Minori forged Hasui; her foster-son Futsu Masayoshi (Masagomaru), mute from birth, forged Haran and later taught three pupils who made the Isshin Sansaku. Her son Futsu Hiroyoshi became fourth-generation head.
