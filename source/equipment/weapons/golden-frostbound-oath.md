## summary
### LITE
Golden Frostbound Oath is a five-star bow released in version 6.5 (Luna VI). Its in-game description calls it a longbow carved from ancient whitewood, once wielded by the Jack Frost King of the northern lands. Its story follows the Knight of the Golden Bough, who walks into the frozen north seeking the Golden Bough to save fallen Arcadia and finds the sacred tree already ruined.

## appearance
### STANDARD
The description says only "a longbow carved from ancient whitewood". The wiki shows a base and second-ascension look, but the text gives no further detail.

## lore
### STANDARD
The story begins in an age when an idyllic land of oakwoods and plenty stood against even the fiercest storms. The Arcadians, exalted by the thousand winds, believed their holy city would last forever, but calamity struck and its spring was laid to rest in a deathly chill. The Knight of the Golden Bough, addressed in a prophecy as Gunnhildr, last light of the people, then walked alone into the endless frost. The prophecy says that only when the pure Golden Bough is severed can the withered wood live again and the sealed paradise endure.
### LARGE +
In the frozen wastes the knight reached another sacred grove spared from ruin, tended by no priests. In their place a Jack Frost named itself king and watched from the high boughs left by a vanished envoy. It was not a rightful lord but a warden bound to an old, fading command, which delighted in riddles and mockery and in leading the lost astray. It set capricious trials and gave answers that were not answers. Wordless, the two fought until the knight's blade struck the crown from the fae king's brow.
### ULTRA +
After the storm the truth came out: the frenzied aurora had taken the Golden Bough long before, and with each tide of the Light Realm the last vestiges of mortal reason and the quiet breath of plant life were stripped away, leaving a pale, withered remnant on the branch. The oath-bound fae did not understand that the holy tree was gone and still guarded what it thought was hallowed ground. The knight hurled the longsword down beneath the withered tree and laughed without sound, accepting that a paradise sealed against change cannot last, then went south. The text says that in later years the hero led willing Arcadian survivors to the tower where the wicked dragon had been cast down, to seek shelter from its lord, Decarabian. The Jack Frost King never knew this: it took its defeat calmly, placed the emptied Golden Bough into the traveller's hands, blessed the mortal desire for a new dawn, and hoped that whoever had set the Jack Frost there might find a way home.

## owners
### STANDARD
The short description names the Jack Frost King of the northern lands as the bow's wielder in ages past. The long story features the Knight of the Golden Bough as the traveller who confronts the king but does not give her or him the bow.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not lore): five-star bow from weapon event wishes. Base ATK 44, secondary stat CRIT DMG 19.2%. Passive "Dawn's Salutation Returned": raises DEF; after the wielder's Elemental Skill or Lunar-Crystallize attack hits, Geo DMG and Lunar-Crystallize Reaction DMG rise for 6 seconds. If Moondrifts are near the wielder, other nearby party members gain a smaller Geo DMG and Lunar-Crystallize bonus, even when the wielder is off-field.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay use (this project's suggestion, not canon text): the weapon fits scenes about duty to a lost homeland, hope that has outlived its object, and a guardian loyal to an order whose meaning is gone. Treat the Arcadian tale as ancient history, not common knowledge.
