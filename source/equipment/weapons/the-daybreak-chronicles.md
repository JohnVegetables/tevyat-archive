## summary
### LITE
The Daybreak Chronicles is a five-star bow released in version 6.2 (2025-12-03), part of the Hexerei series. Its description calls it a bow whose strings gather the "songs of resistance" carried on the wind, strong enough to tear boundless darkness apart with the light of daybreak. Its item story is the legend of Kreuzlied, a name that Mondstadt turned from a byword for failed rebels into a rallying cry.

## appearance
### STANDARD
The in-game text gives no visual description beyond the image of a bow strung with songs: the resistance songs settle "as gentle tones upon these strings". Artwork details are not described in canon text.

## lore
### STANDARD
The story opens with two comrades: a voice in the wind sings farewell, and the answer is that nobody will weep, since the night breeze will carry the tears away. It then tells of a traveling musical troupe that, as the tale goes, plotted to assassinate the ruling house; one member was a renegade noble called Kreuzlied, and a tune was written for him after the plot failed.

Kreuzlied was not his real name, which is lost. The word meant "The Quartering Song", after a cruel public punishment the aristocracy enjoyed watching. It became a byword for those who rose up and failed, and so a rallying cry for rebellion.
### LARGE +
The story says the name was soon carried by many: the Nameless Shepherd, Imunlaukr, the Midnight Rogue, Dawnlight, Gunnhildr and Ragnvindr are all listed among the monikers that "countless" songs gave to Kreuzlied. It ends the line with the final Kreuzlied, who would wake from a thousand-year slumber to answer the small cry of a foreign maiden who had drifted to the land, just as he had once been born from the wish of a certain youth. The wiki links these references to Barbatos, the Vennessa legend and the Nameless Bard; the item text itself does not give those names outright.
### ULTRA +
The ending reverses the opening. The songs of Kreuzlied were fashionable for a time and then left people's lips, since after the darkness there was no longer need for the name. The closing verses answer the opening ones: the horizon has turned white, a maiden sings farewell to the wind, the morning sun has kissed away her tears and told her dawn has broken, and the last line addresses a lover: the dawn has broken. The ordering matters for portrayal: the tale is told as lore ("they say"), not as verified history, and the closing lines pair the weapon's passive name with the idea of resistance giving way to morning.

## owners
### STANDARD
No owner is named in canon text. The bow belongs to the legend of Kreuzlied rather than to a particular wielder.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not lore): five-star bow, Base ATK 48, CRIT DMG secondary stat. Passive "Dawning Song of Daybreak": out of combat the wielder gains a large Normal Attack, Skill and Burst DMG bonus (60-120%) that decays in combat and is rebuilt by hits, even off-field; Hexerei: Secret Rite in the party makes hits rebuild it faster.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay use (this project's suggestion, not canon text): the bow suits scenes about folk songs, tavern ballads and the memory of failed uprisings in Mondstadt. Ordinary Mondstadters may know a verse of the song; the idea that many figures were each called Kreuzlied is specialist lore.

### STANDARD
In the Daybreak Chronicles, Kreuzlied is the name of a renegade noble in a traveling musical troupe that plotted against the ruling house and failed. The word means "The Quartering Song" in the old tongue, after a brutal execution, and became a byword for failed rebels and a rebel rallying cry. The man's real name is lost.
### LARGE +
Many figures are listed under the name in song, and the item calls Barbatos the final Kreuzlied. Treat this as the legend's own framing, not a claim of a single continuous person.
