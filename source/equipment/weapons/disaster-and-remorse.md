## summary
### LITE
Disaster and Remorse is a 5-star Hexerei-series polearm, described as forged from Whitestone and Starsilver, its point always wrapped in a bone-piercing chill. Its text tells how the outlander warrior Imunlaukr hurled this astral-silver lance to protect a maiden of Sal Vindagnyr, and the blow that legend names dolorous destroyed three kingdoms and killed her. Released in Version 6.6.
### STANDARD +
The Hexerei Series page reads the lance as Imunlaukr's weapon. The item is a record of the Mondstadt-region legend of his throw and its aftermath, and of how it ended his life as a warrior.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Canon says it is forged from Whitestone and Starsilver, with a keen point constantly enveloped in a bone-piercing chill; the text calls it an astral silver lance. Do not describe its hilt or length.

## lore
### LITE
In the moonless city of snow, an outlander warrior mourned a shattered realm and left his old greatsword to the frost.
### STANDARD +
He wandered the frozen plains believing he could forget his weakness in endless battle, but abandoned that pretense at the first moonfall after leaving the Mural Room. When he saw the maiden of Sal Vindagnyr attacked, instinct made him hurl the lance into a black tide of beasts, to save her.
### LARGE +
The text says he had buried a hope that would no longer bear fruit and left his unspoken longings beside the last mural. He thought he had long broken a warrior's oath and wanted only a diversion, and he never wished to see silver hair or a moonlike face buried in ice and snow again. The throw is called dolorous; the songs say he came for the joy of conflict and shattered the snow-covered mountains, leveling three thriving kingdoms and leaving multitudes to wander wastelands. The text then says the song is only a song: he stood dazed in the ruins, his spear had pierced the stone that held up the earth, and the maiden had died in the billowing darkness. Survivors named him Imunlaukr and hunted him without end. He had claimed to entertain the gods with strife, then sought redemption and brought only more grief, and so ceased to long for salvation and left the life of a warrior forever.
### ULTRA +
The wiki ties the setting to Sal Vindagnyr during the Funerary Year, where the city was destroyed, and reads the tide of beasts as abyssal monsters. The Sacrificial Series wiki page, discussing Dragonspine and the Imunlaukr Clan, treats the warrior as an ancestor figure; the item text itself does not. The lance in this text, the old greatsword he abandoned (Snow-Tombed Starsilver) and the mural room are items and places with their own entries.

## owners
### LITE
Imunlaukr is the warrior of the text; the wiki's Hexerei Series page states that the polearm once belonged to him.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Game mechanics, not lore: a 5-star polearm, base ATK 48, CRIT Rate secondary stat. After an Elemental Skill, the wielder gains Path of Conflict; its Unforgivable effect raises Normal and Charged Attack DMG, Irreparable raises Skill and Burst DMG, and hits of the matching kind extend each other. Hexerei: Secret Rite raises the boosts by 75%. It was on the weapon event Wish Epitome Invocation (2026-06-09 to 2026-06-30).

## roleplay
### LARGE
This archive's suggested use: a weapon of a tragic legend, where the story the songs tell and the grieving truth behind it differ. Keep both versions: the tale of ruin and the account of a man trying to save one person.
