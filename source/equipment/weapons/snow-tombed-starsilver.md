## summary
### LITE
Snow-Tombed Starsilver is a 4-star claymore of the Dragonspine series, in-game description "an ancient greatsword stored between frescoes" and forged from Starsilver. Its text tells of the princess of the snow-entombed mountain city of Sal Vindagnyr, who gave it to a hero from another land. Added in Version 1.2.
### STANDARD +
In game, one copy and its diagram lie in the Mural Room unlocked by the Hidden Exploration Objective Dragonspine's Glacial Secret; the weapon can also be forged.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Canon calls it an ancient greatsword of Starsilver that can cleave ice and snow. In the text it lies between frescoes in a great hall, with black blood once dripping from its blade.

## lore
### LITE
When the verdant city was sealed by cloud and mist and unending snowstorms blotted out the moonlight, a nail that fell from the azure skies pierced all life and every interrupted story. The priest's daughter gave the Starsilver greatsword to the hero from another land, Imunlaukr.
### STANDARD +
In the howling storm her words could not reach him. She told him a fourth fresco was prepared for him, where his likeness would remain, and promised to wait and pray for his return. She withered together with the grafted, barren silver branches while he was far away chasing answers.
### LARGE +
Her last words, which never reached him, said she had not seen blue sky and green grass for a long time, did not know which colours could capture the thawing landscape her father longed for, and wished to see him again. The wiki notes the same final words appear in the description of the Princess' Box, from Dragonspine's Last Trio.
### ULTRA +
The hero ended his fruitless journey, trudged back through foreign snowy paths with tainted black blood dripping from his blade, and found only deathly echoes in the great hall. Seeing nothing left to protect, he addressed those who dwell in the heavens, said they must only wish to watch the suffering below, and offered to pass their time with a song of iron and blood. He left the Starsilver between the frescoes and descended to search for a land of war to paint red with blood. The princess's gift was meant to shatter snow and wind; the hero's own account is not given.

## owners
### STANDARD
Owner: the hero Imunlaukr, who was given it by the princess and left it in the mural hall. Nobody is shown wielding it afterward.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not literal lore): a 4-star claymore with base ATK 44 and a Physical DMG Bonus secondary stat. The Dragonspine series passive Frost Burial gives Normal and Charged Attack hits a chance (60 to 100 per cent by refinement, once per 10s) to drop an Everfrost Icicle for area damage, with much higher damage against Cryo-affected enemies. Obtainable by forging or from the Mural Room.
