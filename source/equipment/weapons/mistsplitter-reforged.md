## summary
### LITE
Mistsplitter Reforged is a sword from Inazuma's past, one of the blades the Shogun bestowed on her Hatamoto. It was carried by the samurai Takamine the Mistsplitter, who used it against demons and Tatarigami, and who fought the dark army of the Cataclysm at the end of his life. The sword broke and was reforged from its fragments, so the "Reforged" in the name marks it as a successor bearing the old name.
### STANDARD +
Its item text says it can call on lightning to cut through mountain mist and night fog. The reforged blade has a hamon like flowing clouds. The original line of users ended with Takamine; the sword now survives mainly in stories.

## appearance
### STANDARD
A sword that blazes with a fierce violet light (the item description). The reforging left a hamon, the temper line on the blade, that resembles flowing clouds. In play, a purple aura surrounds the weapon while its passive is active and brightens with more stacks.

## lore
### LITE
The text says the sword was shattered into a thousand pieces and reforged. Its user, Takamine the Mistsplitter, appears in a children's rhyme listing famous martial artists, after Arataki of the Front Gate, Iwakura the Successor and Kitain the Serpent.
### STANDARD +
Takamine once accompanied a member of the clergy and used the blade to slay demons and Tatarigami. He learned archery from the Yougou Tengu and passed it on to a person he loved, Asase Hibiki. At the end of his life he became one of the Shogun's yoriki and fought a dark army.
### LARGE +
He wagered his beloved bow with Asase and promised to return to claim the future as his prize; the text imagines things might have gone differently had he not left the bow with her. As the foe flooded in like mist he kept slashing with the blade that could disperse the dark night, but the sword eventually gave out before the swordsman did. He was submerged in the thick dark mist, holding the hilt of the broken sword like a spider's thread, still reminding himself that the wager was undecided. Only some fragments were retrieved and reforged so that the new weapon could inherit the Mistsplitter name.
### ULTRA +
Takamine's story links to Asase Hibiki and the bow Thundering Pulse, to the text of Hamayumi and Shimenawa's Reminiscence, and to the Storm Cage piece of the Emblem of Severed Fate artifact set, which expand on the same pair. In Kagura's Verity, Yae Miko lists among the things wiped out in the Shogun's era "the darkened remains of the swordmaster's soul"; the swordmaster is linked on the wiki to Takamine, though that item never names him in the sentence.

## owners
### LITE
Takamine the Mistsplitter is the only named user. The Shogun is named as the one who gave such swords to her Hatamoto.
### LARGE +
The text says the sword "would have no successors"; do not portray any present-day character as inheriting it. Kamisato Ayaka is shown with it in an official sticker, but that does not make it canon property of the Kamisato clan.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not in-world): a 5-star sword in the Inazuma series, released in Version 2.0 on the weapon event Wish. Base ATK 48 with CRIT DMG as its secondary stat.
### LARGE +
Its passive, Mistsplitter's Edge, grants an Elemental DMG Bonus for all elements (12% at refinement 1, 24% at refinement 5) and up to three stacks of Mistsplitter's Emblem that add a bonus for the wielder's own element (8/16/28% at refinement 1, 16/32/56% at refinement 5). One stack comes from Normal Attacks (or Charged Attacks) dealing Elemental DMG (5 seconds), one from casting an Elemental Burst (10 seconds), and one while Energy is below 100%. Each stack's duration is tracked separately.

## roleplay
### LARGE
For scenes: Mistsplitter Reforged works as a half-remembered legend. Characters in Inazuma may know the rhyme or the story of Takamine, but nobody should treat the sword as a known, available weapon unless the scenario creates a copy explicitly.

### STANDARD
Takamine the Mistsplitter was a samurai of Inazuma's past, listed in a children's rhyme of famous martial artists. He used the sword Mistsplitter against demons and Tatarigami, learned archery from the Yougou Tengu, and made a wager with Asase Hibiki that involved leaving his bow with her. He died or vanished fighting the dark army of the Cataclysm; his own entry carries the rest.
