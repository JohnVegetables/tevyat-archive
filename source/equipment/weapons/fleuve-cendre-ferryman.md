## summary
### LITE
Fleuve Cendre Ferryman is a four-star sword from Fontaine that is, plainly, a handy length of abandoned copper pipe once used as a weapon, famous or infamous depending on the circle. Its item story belongs to Eduardo Baker, an outlaw leader of the Fleuve Cendre, who called it the Ferryman for the many who went into the grey river with it. It is obtained from the Fontaine Fishing Association rather than forged.
### STANDARD +
The story ends with a photograph of Eduardo and others taken in Poisson during a hostage incident, which the Marechaussee Hunters resolved.

## appearance
### STANDARD
A discarded copper (the text also says bronze) pipe, kept like a cane in the photograph in which Eduardo leans on it.

## lore
### STANDARD
The text says discarded components in the gutters can serve as a weapon in an emergency. This pipe's first opponent was a monster nourished by 'richly nutritious water', and it broke the knees and jaws of countless 'livestock' who threatened Eduardo's countrymen. The Court of Fontaine's bureaucracy could not keep order in the Fleuve Cendre, so the outlaw leader began his rise by beating back crocodiles with it, protecting merchants from gangs, settling disputes and blessing newlyweds, until it was said that the sun's light belonged to them and the Fleuve Cendre to 'us'.
### LARGE +
The story then turns to the city's drive for improvement, in which people could not be moved but their coordinates could, and the distinction between 'them' and 'us' became merely places to develop and trash to dispose of or 'move'. Eduardo was arrested in the purge of the Fleuve Cendre and was being taken to exile in the desert when his henchmen rescued him. With comrades near Mont Automnequi, they occupied Poisson, seized members of the Maison Gardiennage who were transporting them as hostages and made a series of unreasonable demands. The Marechaussee Hunters ended the incident.
### ULTRA +
The reporter Karl Ingold volunteered to deliver the demands and took a photograph in Poisson before things worsened. In it Eduardo rests his free hand on his son Jakob's shoulder, Jakob clutching the sleeve of Rene de Petrichor; the Great Magician Parsifal leans casually on the railing; Poisson's mayor Renault de Petrichor stands formally dressed, collar askew; and Rosa Reed with her infant, her husband Thompson and Tom Alter stand at the far end. The text says they all look ahead, 'on the future'.

## owners
### STANDARD
Eduardo Baker, whose hands gave it the nickname Ferryman.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Game mechanics, not story. Passive Ironbone raises CRIT Rate of Elemental Skill hits and grants Energy Recharge for 5 seconds after using an Elemental Skill. Base ATK 42, Energy Recharge 10% secondary stat. It is sold by Delaroche at the Fontaine Fishing Association for fish.
