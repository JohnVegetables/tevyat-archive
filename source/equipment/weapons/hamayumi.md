## summary
### LITE
Hamayumi is a four-star bow of the Amethyst series. Its text is told from a Seirai shrine maiden's point of view: she watches the bandit Ako Domeki (called Zaemon) refuse to bring her aboard the Seiraimaru, thinks of her late archery teacher, and secretly decides to use "true magic" learned on Mt. Yougou against the Electro Archon's ships. The name nods to the real hama yumi, a sacred evil-banishing bow carried by shrine maidens.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Item text calls it a warbow and gives no description of its look.

## lore
### STANDARD
Ako Domeki, the thief the shrine maiden knows as Zaemon, tells her to get off the Seiraimaru because women are a hindrance. His tone and the way he turns his back recall the person who taught her archery before going on campaign with the Shogun in the Cataclysm, and she reflects that had he not gone, their son would be Zaemon's age. She resolves not to let this person die. She had once secretly learned real magic on Mt. Yougou, from her Master, the tengu Reizenbou. She plans to undo the great barrier that had held for a thousand years and free the resentment of the dying violet thunderbird against the ships flying the Electro banner.
### LARGE +
The sea shanties of the departing crew, with spears and swords sharpened and a call to show the Shogunate Seirai's strength, are the sound she hears as she sets down her bow. She is sorry to turn her tengu master's teaching to this use, and hopes only that "that old cat" will not charge into the storm. The text thus places the story on the eve of Seirai's resistance to the Raiden Shogun's forces, a theme that continues in The Catch.
### ULTRA +
Compared with The Catch, the two texts present the same people from opposite ends: Hamayumi gives the shrine maiden's quiet private resolve, The Catch gives Ako Domeki's later farewell, in which he lists himself as the former master of the Seiraimaru, calls an "old shrine maiden" gone, and sets out to see the edge of the world. The identification of the maiden with Asase Hibiki is the wiki's link, not a statement in the item.

## owners
### STANDARD
The text's speaker is a Seirai shrine maiden, read by the wiki as Asase Hibiki. Promotional material shows Yoimiya and Kujou Sara with this bow; that is presentation, not canon ownership.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not lore): four-star bow. Base ATK 41, secondary stat ATK 12%. Passive "Full Draw" raises Normal and Charged Attack DMG, and the bonus is doubled once the wielder's Energy is at 100%. Forged from a diagram found as a reward from Takashi's Mysterious Conch chests, on the seventh opening.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay use (this project's suggestion, not canon text): a keepsake of quiet loyalty and of choosing to protect a stubborn friend despite being told to stay behind.

### LARGE
In the Hamayumi text, a great barrier over Seirai has held for a thousand years (the Chinese original can be read figuratively as "a very long time"). The shrine maiden plans to dissolve it and loose the resentment of the thunderbird against the Shogunate's ships.
