## summary
### LITE
Blade of Atonement is a 4-star claymore of Snezhnaya's Kuznets series, described as a greatsword passed down by the Luchkin and said to be fit only for the bravest warrior. Its story is that of Rafalovich, a silent warrior who once served the Burner of Cities and now carries the weight of what he did.
### STANDARD +
It is a forgeable weapon, with its diagram sold by the blacksmith Kuzmichev at the Kuznets shop in Snezhnograd.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Canon calls it a heavy greatsword and gives nothing further about its looks in the text. Do not invent engravings or a hilt.

## lore
### LITE
Rafalovich is a Volkodlak warrior who once believed the Burner of Cities' promise of a pure, joyful new land. He fought for her without mercy, then abandoned that life and now bears a face wound that never healed and a vow of silence and solitude.
### STANDARD +
The text tells of a wise one, a fae mentor with an enchanting voice who convinced the warrior that mercy toward foes would only bring ruin. After the warrior defected he followed a guiding one to atone, accepted a new greatsword and swore an oath, but the wise one still visits him in dreams to mock him. Frightened by the kindness of ordinary people who ask him questions, he chooses exile as the price of his sin.
### LARGE +
In the final section the guiding one has to die to defeat the certainty of destruction, and Rafalovich guards the wounded at the rear against old comrades. He cannot bring himself to kill, which lets the inflamer of hearts escape. A wolf fae, drunk on ruin, taunts him as a traitor with a softened heart and recalls the Domovoy friend who refused to kneel before Cirnareka. Rafalovich runs the fae through, names himself as once a blade for brigands and the Burner of Cities' sword, and says he has earned the right to repay him blood for blood.
### ULTRA +
The wiki's Kuznets Series page reads the text as one of five linked stories from Snezhnaya's past concerning the guiding one and the Luchkin against the Burner of Cities; this weapon is the one about a defector. The text itself frames redemption as an extravagant hope rather than a promise, and keeps Rafalovich's guilt unresolved.

## owners
### LITE
Rafalovich is the warrior of the story. The description says the greatsword was passed down by the Luchkin. No modern owner is named.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not in-world): a 4-star claymore from the Kuznets series, base ATK 44 at level 1, second stat ATK 6%; introduced in Version 7.0 and obtained by forging with a Borderland Claymore Billet, Rainbowdrop Crystal, White Iron Chunk and Mora. Passive "Repentance and Redemption": triggering an Elemental Reaction raises Elemental Mastery for 12s, and triggering a Stellar Glimmer reaction also raises ATK for 12s; it works off-field. Values scale with refinement.

## roleplay
### LARGE
For scenes: someone who knows the story would speak of a warrior who put down his blade out of shame, not glory. Keep the tone sombre and sparing, as the text does, and leave Rafalovich's ultimate redemption unresolved.
