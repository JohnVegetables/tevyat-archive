## summary
### LITE
The Stringless is a four-star bow of the Wanderer's Troupe series: an exquisite stringed instrument that is also a bow, once played by the Troupe's harpist from Fontaine. After the Troupe disbanded its harp strings were cut, leaving only the bowstring.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Item text: strings of different materials on every section, each trembling to make flowing music when struck. After the Troupe disbanded the strings were cut with a dull blade, creating ear-piercing noise; the lofty melodies are lost but the bow stays deadly. Per the wiki, the Resolution of Sojourner set says it once had four strings.

## lore
### STANDARD
The Troupe could make birds fall from the sky, sometimes with the harp's tune and sometimes with the arrows that followed it. The harpist (the Sojourner) was playful and determined, like the wind and the flowers of Starsnatch Cliff. After the failed uprising the Troupe fled, but he stayed to the end, playing and loosing arrows to protect his comrades until his repertoire and arrows ran out.
### LARGE +
He came from Fontaine and left to seek his true destiny, and it is said the ladies of the Court of Fontaine wailed that he left without goodbye. The story goes that he loved a humble Mondstadt woman who was then chosen for the miserable fate of the Ludi Harpastum princess.
### ULTRA +
It is claimed he did not regret dying anonymously in a foreign land; his only regret was finding true love and never getting to sing another love song. The text frames all of this as story, not recorded history. The wiki also notes the Chinese name may echo an idiom about a musician severing his strings after losing a friend; that is a translator's etymology, not in-game lore.

## owners
### STANDARD
The Troupe's harpist (the Sojourner).
### LARGE +
Diona is shown with this bow in her character demo and Faruzan with the ascended version in hers; that is presentation, not canon ownership.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Game mechanics: Wanderer's Troupe-series four-star bow, base ATK 42, Elemental Mastery 36, passive 'Arrowless Song'. Standard and event wishes.
