## summary
### LITE
Calamity Queller is a five-star polearm of Liyue, a keenly honed weapon forged from a strange aqua-colored crystal, whose faint blue light seems to whisper of countless past matters. Item text says it was made from materials not of this world, passed through many loyal but unfortunate hands and was lost with the Yaksha who wielded it at the end of the Cataclysm. Shenhe is shown wielding it in official art.
### STANDARD +
Its owners tend to fight without orders and protect without oaths, and to meet similar fates.

## appearance
### LARGE
The in-game description calls it a keenly honed weapon of strange crystal with a faint blue light. The crystal is described as ominous and aqua-colored. Nothing more about its shaft or blade is stated.

## lore
### STANDARD
The story says the long polearm was forged from materials not of this world. It has been through the hands of many loyal yet unfortunate souls, seen slaughters begin and end, and tasted the flesh and blood of foul beasts. An exorcist once came to the great chasm of crystal sands, retrieved an ominous aqua-colored crystal from its depths and hired someone to forge it into a weapon named Calamity Queller.
### LARGE +
The exorcist said that if disaster should befall, the spear would calm it. The tribespeople of the craggy mountains neither boasted nor criticised, and accepted the words since no contract was signed. Later, in the Cataclysm, monstrous tides scaled the bedrock and turned the cinnabar-colored earth black. The shield-wall of the Millelith clashed with the dark forces, stragglers were slain, and Calamity Queller flashed at the heart of the maelstrom like a cold star. When sunset split the clouds, the dark mud sank back into the chasm, and the spear was lost along with the Yaksha who wielded it.
### ULTRA +
The text goes on that later wielders mostly met similar fates, yet some fought without orders and protected people without oaths, for whom such a fate was no lament. It adds that the spear was once borrowed from someone and, in a deserted water-eroded cave, witnessed a clash between intimate friends; the wiki links the cave to Yaodie Valley. The wiki also notes it has the highest base ATK of any polearm (741 at level 90), tied among the game's highest base ATK weapons.

## owners
### STANDARD
Named in the story: a Yaksha lost with it during the Cataclysm (the wiki says Bosacius) and unnamed earlier hands. Shenhe is the character shown wielding it in her official art, which is presentation rather than a story claim.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not lore): a five-star polearm from weapon event wishes. Base ATK 49, secondary stat ATK 3.6%. Passive "Extinguishing Precept": All Elemental DMG +12-24%, and after an Elemental Skill the wielder gains "Consummation" for 20 seconds, raising ATK 3.2-6.4% per second up to 6 stacks, doubled while the wielder is off the field (refinements 1 to 5).

## roleplay
### LARGE
Roleplay use (this project's suggestion, not canon text): suits themes of duty carried without recognition and the lonely endings of those who guard others. Do not state that a specific Yaksha or friend wielded it beyond what the text says.
