## summary
### LITE
The Royal Grimoire is a four-star catalyst of the Royal series. It is a wax-treated scroll that once belonged to a royal mage of Mondstadt, holding both historical accounts and spells. Its item story is mainly a bitter note on how the nobility and their mage tutors failed Mondstadt, and on an oath whose wording was lost.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Item text describes a delicate scroll treated with wax against decay and moth damage; the catalyst is a scroll, not a bound volume. Nothing further about its look is stated, so avoid inventing detail.

## lore
### STANDARD
The text says royal mages were charged with nourishing the water and soil, fighting monsters and tutoring the nobles, so their research covers history, governance, management and culture, and is still described as forward-looking. When the people overthrew the royals, the mages were exiled too, because they had abandoned their duty to instil virtue in the nobles and become birds of a feather with them.
### LARGE +
The second half recalls early Mondstadt, when the Lawrence clan was led by Venerare. Under her the people raised a tall statue in the plaza to mark the Anemo Archon's descent; beneath it the leader of all the clans put an oath of protection for Mondstadt. Over the years the Lawrence clan abandoned its forebears' ideals and the statue was taken down, and the court's mages never spoke of the history or the oath again. In the age of the Knights of Favonius the statue was rebuilt, but the original wording of the oath was lost for good.
### ULTRA +
The story is told by a later chronicler and gives no date for the overthrow beyond the era of the aristocracy. It shares the oath-and-statue theme with the Royal Longsword, whose text records an ancient oath sworn after the Anemo Archon descended, but each item presents its own account and neither claims to be the lost inscription. Treat the scroll as an indictment of complicit scholarship: knowledge kept for the powerful and silence about the people's history.

## owners
### STANDARD
Item text names no individual owner, only a royal mage of Mondstadt.
### LARGE +
Klee is shown wielding this catalyst in her official character demo and in a Collected Miscellany video; that is presentation, not a canon statement of ownership.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Game mechanics: Royal-series passive 'Focus' stacks CRIT Rate on damaging hits (up to 5 stacks, reset by a CRIT). Four-star catalyst, base ATK 44, ATK 6% secondary. Obtained from Paimon's Bargains. This is gameplay, not in-world lore.
