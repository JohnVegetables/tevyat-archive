## summary
### LITE
Flame-Forged Insight is a 4-star claymore from Natlan. The People of the Springs are said to have forged a great blade to protect a child who would go on a distant journey, and this weapon is a replica of that legendary sword.
### STANDARD +
Its story is told through two old adventurers, Akamai and Kau Kau, who melted down the stones of a lifetime of travel to arm a descendant. It was an event reward in Version 5.8.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Canon gives only the description of a replica of a legendary great blade. Do not invent engravings. The story only notes that it was forged from crystal and ore gathered on the old friends' travels.

## lore
### LITE
Akamai and Kau Kau, two youths of the People of the Springs, once swore to explore all of Natlan and see their deeds recorded on woven scrolls. Their treasures turned out to be mostly ordinary ore and crystals, but they treasured them anyway.
### STANDARD +
Each stone held a memory: one taken from beneath the claws of a giant Tepetlisaurus in crystal mountains, one from the fiend that tripped them by a hot spring when they were drunk, one ash-covered stone that had kept watch on the edge of the burned ancient city of Ochkanatlan before they took it.
### LARGE +
When Akamai's descendant wanted to follow a forebear's path, now-aged Akamai and Kau Kau (one walking with a cane) had the blacksmith from the mountains melt the stones down and forge a blade to protect the traveller. They handed it over as a parting gift, saying the past accompanies the young in place of ancestors who no longer have the strength, and that the past flows through the blood like a fire passed down by rituals that never stop burning.
### ULTRA +
The story is told as inherited tribal tradition, with dialogue for the old friends. The reforging motif is stated in the story itself: as the smith melts each stone, the past stirs again with each memory spoken, and the closing lines liken the past flowing through the blood to a fire passed down by never-ending rituals.

## owners
### LITE
No owner is named. The wiki calls this particular weapon a replica of the legendary sword.
### LARGE +
Within the story the blade passes from Akamai and Kau Kau to Akamai's unnamed descendant. Any scene claiming the original still exists is an invention.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not in-world): a 4-star claymore of the Meztli series, base ATK 42 at level 1, secondary stat Elemental Mastery. Reward of the Version 5.8 event Sunspray Summer Resort; refinement material is Drifter's Crystal Marrow.
### LARGE +
Passive "Mind in Bloom": when Electro-Charged, Lunar-Charged, Bloom, Lunar-Bloom, Crystallize or Lunar-Crystallize is triggered, restores Elemental Energy and raises Elemental Mastery for 15s, once per 15s, even when the wielder is off-field.

## roleplay
### LARGE
For scenes: a keepsake-weapon with a sentimental legend. Natlanese elders may tell it as a story about friends, journeys and passing the past to the young.
