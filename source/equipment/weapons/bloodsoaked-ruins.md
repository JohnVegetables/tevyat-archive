## summary
### LITE
A long spear tainted black by unrelenting battle, glowing faintly beneath the ghostly blue light of a lantern-fire. A 5-star polearm from Version 6.0, shown in Flins's hands.
### STANDARD +
Its item text is a northern tale of two brothers, a snowbound court and a stained spear.

## appearance
### STANDARD
A long black spear with a lantern fixed to it. The description speaks of an ethereal glow and ghostly blue lantern-fire; its feathers were once silver-white but the spear now wears the black stain of noble blood. When Flins wields it the lantern shines red in combat (wiki trivia).

## lore
### LITE
A Snezhnayan folk tale says the King of Midsommar Oak, who brought warmth, was accidentally slain by his elder brother, the King of Winter Icelea, and so the world has known unrelenting winter. The spear's story sets a human tragedy beside it.
### STANDARD +
The White Emperor, ruler of the fae from the Pale Star throne, fell silent after returning from the ruins of the far north, then opened his doors to humans he had once turned away. A palace rose beside Snezhnograd's furnaces, where the wisest humans and fae gathered and the seeds of a "grand delusion" were sown.
### LARGE +
Guarding that court were fae and the mortal House Solovyov. Of two brothers, the elder inherited the nightingale emblem and guarded the sacred chariot; the younger gave up the family name and lived a life of obscurity on the frontier. The elder was consumed by beasts ("zver") for glimpsing the philosophers' secret. The younger, away quelling unrest, came back to a world under deep snow and found the spear, once silver-white feathered, stained black.
### ULTRA +
Years later the surviving brother, now a general who had taken the family name, left his homeland for the wild borderlands of Nod-Krai seeking vengeance. Each time he touched the spear he relived the snowy night outside the palace gates and the words of an old friend (Avrelian) spoken before the guillotine. The text ends with the Torchforger, bathed in eternal moonlight, lighting an everlasting flame and beginning his watch. The folk tale and the human story are linked only by mood: the text adds that the fae know the permafrost is far older than the Tsar's reign.

## owners
### LITE
Flins is shown wielding it in official art and trailers; the item text itself names no owner.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not in-world): 5-star polearm, CRIT Rate secondary. Passive Mournful Tribute boosts the wielder's Lunar-Charged damage after a Burst and, when Lunar-Charged is triggered, grants CRIT DMG and some Energy (limited to once every 14 seconds).

## roleplay
### LARGE
Present it as a weapon with memory rather than a heirloom of fame: those who know Flins may link it to him, but the in-text brothers are told as a tale and should not be asserted as his own history.

## entry: Mournful Tribute
@keys: Mournful Tribute, Requiem of Ruin
@version: 6.0
### STANDARD
Mournful Tribute is the weapon's passive. Its theme, mourning and a vigil, matches the closing image of the Torchforger beginning his watch.
