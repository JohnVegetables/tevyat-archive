## summary
### LITE
Dragonspine Spear is a 4-star polearm of the Dragonspine series, in-game description "a spear created from the fang of a dragon" that is oddly warm to the touch. It is made from teeth of the shadow dragon Durin and is the reward of the world quest The Festering Fang. Added in Version 1.2.
### STANDARD +
Its archive entry is Durin's own dying dream. The weapon shares its source dragon with Festering Desire.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Canon says only that it is a spear made from a dragon's fang, oddly warm to the touch. Do not invent a colour or length.

## lore
### LITE
The text narrates a very long dream. He dreamed that he and everyone had journeyed to a land of green grass and soulful songs, where he sang with the gentle people and danced in the skies with a dragon as beautiful as a jewel.
### STANDARD +
He woke in the sky above a mountain swept by roaring snowstorms. The green land was already painted crimson with fire and blood, the song of a sky-blue bard's lyre almost drowned out, and the jewel-like dragon had pierced his neck with sharp fangs.
### LARGE +
His last thoughts: "Farewell, Mother! My journey is ended." He will sleep beneath the white, shining silver and thinks that perhaps this too is good; he bids farewell to the bard and the dragon, wishes they had met at another time and place to sing and dance together, and gives them the blessing in his veins and the sight of the dark universe that bore him.
### ULTRA +
Canon context: the wiki identifies the dreamer as Durin, the mother as Rhinedottir, the bard as Venti and the dragon as Dvalin; the same battle is told from Dvalin's side in Skyward Spine, where Durin is a poisonous attacker. Take the dream as Durin's perception, not neutral history, and as a reason Dvalin's victory carries sorrow.

## owners
### STANDARD
No owner is named. In The Festering Fang the Traveler collects the materials and the craftsman Orban forges the spear; the weapon passes to the Traveler as the quest reward.

## quests
### STANDARD
In The Festering Fang (a Version 1.2 Dragonspine world quest), the Traveler picks up a Strange Tooth, asks around the adventurers' camp and learns from Orban that it may be dragon tooth. He asks for three more teeth, 50 Starsilver and a Northlander polearm prototype, then finds that ordinary tools cannot cut the tooth. A tooth must absorb dragon "vitality" first: either from the heart in Wyrmrest Valley (where Fatui skirmishers interrupt) or, if the Traveler has already progressed through The Gardener, via a Runestone that Albedo sent through Sucrose. Orban then forges the spear.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not literal lore): a 4-star polearm with base ATK 41 and a Physical DMG Bonus secondary stat. The Dragonspine series passive Frost Burial gives Normal and Charged Attack hits a chance (60 to 100 per cent by refinement, once per 10s) to drop an Everfrost Icicle that deals area ATK damage, much more against Cryo-affected enemies. It is a forgeable polearm obtained as a World Quest reward, with a diagram also given by the quest.

## entry: The Festering Fang
@keys: The Festering Fang, Dragonteeth, Dragontooth
@version: 1.2
### STANDARD
The Festering Fang is a Version 1.2 world quest in Dragonspine. Orban, an artisan, works with the Traveler to turn a strange dragon tooth into the Dragonspine Spear, after the tooth is first infused with dragon vitality. Festering Desire, a sword, is a related weapon from the same dragon.
