## summary
### LITE
A silver-white polearm, pure and without blemish, which legend says the Archpriestesses of Hyperborea used to guide their flock of Frostmoon Scions. A 4-star polearm of the Frostmoon Scion series from Version 6.1.
### STANDARD +
Its text is Lovia's turn from compassion to authority.

## appearance
### STANDARD
A silver-white staff-like polearm. The text mentions an antlered crown worn by Lovia later, but that is separate from the staff.

## lore
### LITE
A fading Archpriestess hands the staff to her kind adopted daughter Lovia, telling her only her mercy can open the eyes of the lost.
### STANDARD +
Lovia was an orphan from another land, unaware of it, and never blessed with the moon's light. The pale Frost Moon no longer gazed down and the faithful were turning away, so the frail Archpriestess put her adopted daughter on the fragile throne, hoping kindness would unite her followers.
### LARGE +
Lovia took "right" as absolute: feeding the hungry, tending the sick, mending suspicion with sincerity. Her faith was broken when the maiden of the purest bloodline, her sister by bond, was cut down by a traitor's blade while shielding her. Lovia could not even cry out.
### ULTRA +
She concluded that the kindness of the weak was an unforgivable delusion. If the true moon would withhold hope, lies would be spun into a gentle dawn; if the new moon never rose again, absolute authority would rule the mortal dream. Those who defied her were to be washed away in blood, until her late sister's orphan was born the sacred heir. She came to don the antlered crown, "pure, defiled and illusory", wrapping herself in it like a cocoon. The text keeps the traitor unnamed.

## owners
### LITE
The text passes the staff to Lovia, the last Archpriestess; no current wielder is named.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not in-world): 4-star polearm, base ATK 45, CRIT Rate secondary. Passive Untainted Desire grants ATK and Energy Recharge for 6 seconds after Skill hits, up to 3 stacks, even off-field.

## roleplay
### LARGE
Present it as a weapon of sad, devout rule. Do not assert that Lovia is the only portrayal; other texts give a different background.
