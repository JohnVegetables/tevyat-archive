## summary
### LITE
Makhaira Aquamarine is a 4-star claymore of the Tulaytullah series: an ancient greatsword that gleams like water, whose unnaturally sharp edge a thousand years of sand erosion has not dulled. Its legend belongs to the dancer Makhaira, once a tyrant's concubine.
### STANDARD +
It is one of three Tulaytullah-series weapons that share a story world with the five-star catalyst Tulaytullah's Remembrance, and it was released in Version 3.1 through weapon event Wishes.

## appearance
### STANDARD
A greatsword with a blade that gleams like water. The description stresses its unnaturally sharp edge and great age.

## lore
@version: 3.1
### LITE
A thousand years ago, when the foolish king (Deshret) was buried beneath the dunes, short-lived city-states were scattered across the gilded land. The sword belonged to the dancer Makhaira, once the tyrant's concubine, who won the king's favor and the prince's attention with a ruthless sword dance.
### STANDARD +
The noble king of a decaying kingdom was obese and haughty, spending his days on food, wine and hunting, and, like other kings of that decadent age, on conquest and hoarding loot. One night, as water-colored moonlight quieted the kingdom and the Seelie murmured, he fell into a deep sleep. Makhaira, who had seen the signs of destruction, polished her sword for one last dance, and the young prince, who had foreseen the same, came to her.
### LARGE +
A quoted exchange between the two does not label its speakers. It speaks of a little revenge lifting a burden from both their hearts, of the hunting-addict being executed by the falcon he spoils, and of a speaker whose mother, a Jinni, taught her a seven-fold sword dance, which fits Makhaira but is not stated outright. The conspiracy was drawn up in bed, and the lovers' gentle words were sharp blades. The tyrant fell, and the great cause of conquering kingdoms and harems vanished.
### ULTRA +
The city-state's rise and fall is called a drunken dream in which the kind and the evil alike were crushed like wheat husks by a millstone. Makhaira, adept at the sword dance, failed to become queen, and the wise old retainer who survived to tell the story lost his eyes. The displaced, swept into the sea of sand, became wanderers or mercenaries before the Dharma Forest swallowed them.

## owners
### LITE
The sword belongs to Makhaira in its story. No modern character is recorded wielding it.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not in-world): 4-star claymore, level 1 base ATK 42, secondary stat Elemental Mastery 36. Passive Desert Pavilion: every 10 s the wielder gains a percentage of their Elemental Mastery as ATK for 12 s, with nearby party members gaining 30% of it; it works off-field and stacks across copies.
### LARGE +
The ATK gain is 24% of Elemental Mastery at refinement 1 and 48% at refinement 5. The text also appears in Chronicled Wishes.

## roleplay
### LARGE
Present it as a legend with an unreliable edge: the speaker "has heard" it. Do not merge it with Tulaytullah's Remembrance as a single account.
