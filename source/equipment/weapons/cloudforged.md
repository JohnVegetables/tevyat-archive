## summary
### LITE
Cloudforged is a 4-star bow added in Version 4.7. Its in-game description calls it a prototype of a standard-issue weapon that the Yuheng commissioned from Blackcliff Forge, after analysing the ancient mechanisms that guarded the adeptal abode on Mount Tianheng. It was a reward of the limited-time event Mutual Security Enhancing Simulation.
### STANDARD +
Its archive text is not about the bow itself but about Blackcliff Forge and the semi-legendary founder of the Yun family of smiths.

## lore
### LITE
The item text says the ancients called Mount Tianheng full of gems and the Chasm rich in jade, and that Liyue's key mining and smelting works are in Blackcliff Forge, carved into Tianheng itself.
### STANDARD +
The Forge was built by hollowing out the mountain into countless workshops, furnaces and soot-blackened connecting tunnels. In the years of war the ancestors of the mountain tribes took shelter there, and long-forgotten passages are said to lead down to an ancient underground realm. According to the Stone Tablet Compilations, the founder of the famed Yun family first carved the dark paths through the mountains, and the Yun house of smiths has since been honoured by later artisans as their progenitor.
### LARGE +
Mechanisms and forging were once bound up with the adepti, so the founder was often taken as an adeptal apprentice. The best-known tale says the founder served beside Rex Lapis on campaign and was acknowledged and taught secret mechanical arts by three adepti in turn. Legend claims clouds could be plucked from the sky to mould manses, and decaying wood carved into lifelike birds and beasts; hence the Liyue saying 'the Blackcliff, forged from clouds'. A passage from the annals praises wooden birds that fly for three days without landing.
### ULTRA +
The text then deflates the legend: cloud-forging is a latter-day play on the family name Yun, 'cloud', though the founder devoted a whole life to the Forge and to teaching craft, and at life's end ascended in heavenly light to become the guardian star of artisanry. Yet the Forge was no adeptal art but was chiselled out hammerstroke by hammerstroke by ordinary artisans over centuries; Deus Auri's contract, it notes, never said mortal resolve could not surpass adeptal miracles. It ends with the founder's maxim that what benefits the people is clever and what does not is clumsy.

## owners
### STANDARD
No owner is named in canon. The description frames it as a prototype of an issue weapon for the Yuheng (the office Keqing holds).

Obtained through the limited-time Version 4.7 event Mutual Security Enhancing Simulation, which also gave the refinement material Cam and Cables of Law.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not literal lore): 4-star bow, base ATK 42, Elemental Mastery secondary stat (36). Passive Crag-Chiseled Forge: after Elemental Energy is decreased, the wearer gains 40 to 80 Elemental Mastery (by refinement) for 18s, up to 2 stacks. Refinement uses Cam and Cables of Law instead of duplicate weapons; four came from the event.

## entry: Blackcliff Forge and the Yun founder
@keys: Blackcliff Forge, Yun family founder

### STANDARD
Cloudforged's text portrays Blackcliff Forge as a mountain-carved complex of workshops and tunnels, a refuge in wartime and later Liyue's key mining and smelting centre, begun by the founder of the Yun family of smiths. The legend of adeptal teaching coexists with the claim that the Forge was built by ordinary labour.
