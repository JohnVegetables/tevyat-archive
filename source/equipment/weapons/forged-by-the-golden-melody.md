## summary
### LITE
Forged by the Golden Melody is a four-star claymore added in Version 7.0 through the Battle Pass Gnostic Hymn. The description calls it a greatsword decorated in gold and says legend holds it was the favored weapon of an exalted Harmost of the distant past. Its story is a conversation in the white-stone halls of Remuria between a musician and his golden-haired friend.
### STANDARD +
The musician is troubled by the burden of attunement and by his friend's contempt for ordinary people; he recalls a female companion who did not believe in gods.

## appearance
### STANDARD
A greatsword decorated in gold, as the in-game description puts it.

## lore
### STANDARD
In silent corridors of white stone, the golden-haired friend urges the musician to look at the 'benighted slaves' who mistake the labor of the high-ranking for luxury, and laments that a beautiful solo should fall to mortal ground. The musician sighs and does not argue. The duty of attunement, cleansing the bitter sorrow of all waters, was in effect burning one's soul like firewood, so that the God King in his golden palace could fulfil his great and tragic wish to save all souls. He rebukes his friend gently: they were raised up to guide and rescue those people, and one cannot play the Sebastos' perfect melody if one despises them.
### LARGE +
His friend scorns this as weakness and plays his lyre. The musician sees afterimages of his own past, when he too held weak hopes that faded among clamoring bugles, leaving only unfulfilled vows. He thinks he should never have become a Harmost, having no lofty delusions but only the old hope that descendants of the high sea's people might suffer less. He remembers his female companion, kind like an elder sister, saying that those who truly believed in gods would never commit atrocities in their name, and reflects that she would have bowed before the Sebastos had she ascended.
### ULTRA +
The musician always believed he could read hearts as he read hers. Only when he looked on the golden-haired traveler at the end did he understand that no one understands another's heart until the last hour; he had not fully understood his friend's fanaticism, his companion's compassion or his God King's design. The text closes on the image of all dreams, noble and despicable, flowing into the waters like raindrops and like the tears she shed embracing his broken body, vanishing into the symphony of night and day.

## owners
### STANDARD
Only an unnamed exalted Harmost of the distant past, according to legend.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Game mechanics, not story. Passive Day and Night in Counterpoint rotates a 10-second Harmonic Movement (ATK, Elemental Mastery, Stellar Glimmer reaction DMG) that works even when the wielder is off-field, and a Stellar Glimmer reaction adds a 12-second Contrapuntal instance. Base ATK 42, CRIT Rate 6% secondary stat.
