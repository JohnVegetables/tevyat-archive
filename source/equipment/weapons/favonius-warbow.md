## summary
### LITE
Favonius Warbow is a 4-star bow of the Favonius series, a recurve bow issued only to the Knights of Favonius' best, long-serving archers. Its text tells of the Outriders, the Knights' archer scouts, and how the team declined after their first member left.
### STANDARD +
It can be obtained as a reward for the A Long Shot quest and from Wishes.

## appearance
### STANDARD
An oak-framed recurve bow with metal parts that make it easy to use. The bowstring is described as enchanted with the secrets of alchemy and magic, protecting the archer's fingers.

## lore
### LITE
The bow is a reward for worthy guardians of Mondstadt and a tool for their continued guardianship. The text says the Knights once had a formidable team of archer scouts called the Outriders, founded by a mercenary leader from Liyue who passed on a lifetime of knowledge.
### STANDARD +
The Outriders gained tracking skills and a sense for approaching danger that were unique among the Knights and invaluable to the order. This lasted until the first of the Outriders left the order for reasons unknown to the Knights.
### LARGE +
After that departure, the team became a shell of its former self: its expertise was gone, though its structure and name were kept. The text ends by noting that there are still those who work tirelessly to maintain the dignity of the Outrider title; the wiki links that line to Amber.

In a scene, keep the Outriders' decline as the item states it. Do not name the founder or the departed member unless the user supplies a name.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not in-world): 4-star bow, base ATK 41, secondary stat Energy Recharge 13.3%, obtainable from Wishes including the standard banner and as a reward for the Archon quest A Long Shot. Passive Windfall: CRIT hits have a chance (60% to 100% by refinement) to generate a small amount of Elemental Particles, restoring 6 Energy, once per 12 s at refinement 1 down to once per 6 s at refinement 5.
