## summary
### LITE
Alley Hunter is a 4-star bow of the Alley series, an opulent black longbow. Its story belongs to Mondstadt's aristocratic age: a noble house's hunting bow fell into the hands of a gentleman thief who was never caught, and used it to shoot crowns off noble heads without a sound. The tale ends bittersweetly, with a witch he loved branded and vanished and the thief fled across the sea.
### STANDARD +
It belongs to the same set as the Alley Flash and shares its theme of a thief among the commoners. The Alley series has no shared passive, and the bow's text is a lament more than a boast.

## appearance
### STANDARD
An intricate, ornate longbow painted black so that it vanishes into the deepest night. The text says it was once the first choice of an opulent noble house for their hunting games.

## lore
### LITE
The bow passed from a noble house to a gentleman thief who was never caught. He shot crowns from noble heads in silence, cut through tightened bonds and disarmed pursuing soldiers. He once vowed to bring light to a darkened age and to bring justice, riches and gladness to the oppressed.
### STANDARD +
The text says he did deliver on this for a time: the powerful lost sleep in impotent rage, rooftops and terraces heard his light footsteps like rain, and bards in the squares gained a song. He pried a sapphire from a noble's regalia and gave it to a blue-eyed witch who hunted the vile with a spear. He never managed to draw a smile from her, and she was as cold as the sapphire. In the end, seeking death, she took a criminal's brand on her flower-like face, and nobody knew where she went. The thief heeded the advice of his brothers, abandoned his vow and sailed into the ocean that belonged to no one.
### LARGE +
The last lines are his own words, a question asked of nobody in particular: whether she still remembers his song and the wine-scented streets and the songs sung of her. The wiki links the thief to Parsifal and the witch to the Blue-Eyed Spear Witch, and the Alley Flash and Favonius Lance texts tell parts of the same story from other sides. Treat those identifications as the wiki's reading of linked texts.
### ULTRA +
Tone: the first half is celebratory and the second quietly tragic, with the key image being a gift that could not warm its recipient. For scene use, keep the thief unnamed unless the user supplies a name, and keep the ending open as canon leaves it.

## owners
### LITE
An unnamed noble house first; then the gentleman thief. No present-day character is established as owning it.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not in-world): 4-star bow, base ATK 44, secondary stat ATK 6%, obtained from weapon event Wishes (added in Version 1.4). Passive Oppidan Ambush: while the wielder is in the party but not on the field, their DMG increases every second up to a cap; once they have been on the field for more than 4 s, the bonus decays.
### LARGE +
At refinement 1 the bonus grows 2% per second up to 20% and decays 4% per second; at refinement 5 it grows 4% per second up to 40% and decays 8% per second. Its ambush-from-the-wings design echoes the thief theme.
