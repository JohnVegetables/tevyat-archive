## summary
### LITE
Lithic Spear is a 4-star polearm of the Lithic series, a spear forged from the rocks of the Guyun Stone Forest whose hardness knows no equal. It was favoured by the Millelith of old, and its great weight stops today's Millelith from equipping it. It was added in Version 1.3.
### STANDARD +
Its text says it is said to be able to pierce even dragon scales, and recounts how the Millelith used leftover ancient spears after an earthquake.

## appearance
### STANDARD
A spear forged from lith of the Guyun Stone Forest; canon gives no more detail. Do not invent a length or decoration.

## lore
### LITE
The Millelith conducted themselves in Rex Lapis' name by the axiom: while rock stays hard, the mountain shall never fail; while the Millelith stands guard, evil shall never prevail. In the past they went forth to subdue demons and protect roads and countryside; in peaceful times their duties have shifted to maintaining order.
### STANDARD +
At a certain point in the past the earth shook and the mountains trembled. The Millelith then gave equipment left over from the ancient wars to those among them of unusually great stature: the very heavy Lithic Spears, which were used like shovels to rescue the injured. The text says the spear pierces rock with ease and that a thousand such spears lined up would be unstoppable.
### LARGE +
In an even earlier and more turbulent era, every soldier of the Millelith could wield these colossal weapons, and a thousand spears in formation protected Rex Lapis' kingdom and the homeland.
### ULTRA +
Read together, the text sketches three stages: in the oldest era every Millelith soldier could wield the spear, after the earthquake only the unusually large were issued one and used it as a rescue tool, and today none of the Millelith can carry it because of its weight. It is a traditional account and does not name the earthquake or its date.

## owners
### STANDARD
No individual owner is named. Yaoyao is seen with the unascended spear in her Thrust and Parry anecdote and Xiangling with the ascended one in the Three to Get Ready, and Here We Go cinematic (cameos, not story claims).

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not literal lore): 4-star polearm, base ATK 44, ATK% secondary stat (6 per cent). Passive Lithic Axiom: Unity gives the wearer 7 to 11 per cent ATK and 3 to 7 per cent CRIT Rate (by refinement) for each Liyue character in the party, stacking up to 4 times. It was a Weapon Event Wish (Version 1.3) and has the same passive as Lithic Blade.
