## summary
### LITE
The Calamity of Eshu is a 4-star sword from Natlan: the curved ceremonial blade of Sanhaj, the great high priest of the Masters of the Night-Wind. Legend says he used it in rituals that blurred the boundary between the Night Kingdom and the living world.
### STANDARD +
Its story follows Sanhaj as a silent child who journeyed to find half of his soul and came back knowing the past. The tribe's chief keeps the blade today and lends it for ceremonies.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Canon says only that it is a curved ceremonial blade. In its quest uses it carries the power of a Wayob, which the game says can be called on to defend its wielder. Do not invent decoration.

## lore
### LITE
A child who barely spoke for years was told by a ritual elder that half his soul had been lost in the night flames. On his birthday he walked into the stony wastes to find it, with only the Iktomisaur Mahamba willing to go with him.
### STANDARD +
Crossing seven curtain layers to reach the Lord of Silence is a journey few return from. In an obsidian hall the boy took in the memories of an ancient era as if he had lived them: the first Pyro Archon swearing a vow before the volcano, and the Sacred Lord Och-Kan, fallen into delirium, beaten down by the crimson-eyed youth on the stairs before an empty throne. The memories gave him every word and its meaning, and let him see far along the threads of time.
### LARGE +
He realised that the master of the night had arranged all this, and then saw a shadow about to cover Natlan and monsters from beyond, including a black shape that engulfed a lonely isle and gnawed at every boundary. When the dark fog neared the tribes he cried "Stop!", his first word, and woke by a blue bonfire as Mahamba's eyes dimmed and the elder handed him a ceremonial staff.
### ULTRA +
The closing stanza turns grim: from then on Sanhaj, who knew all the past, used the ancient words to rebuke those who scorned future dangers, and also wove a great net of lies, making stacks of woven scrolls a veil over a past that should not be touched again. This frames the Masters of the Night-Wind's record-keeping as both preserving and concealing history. The wiki notes the name echoes Eshu, a trickster-messenger figure of Yoruba religion; that is an etymological observation, not an in-world claim.

## owners
### LITE
Sanhaj's ritual sword, now kept by the Masters of the Night-Wind's chief.
### STANDARD +
In The Spirit of Ah K'ulbatil (Iktomi Spiritseeking event quests), Citlali brings the chief's ceremonial sword and calls on it during a ritual. In the world quest The Last Survivor of Tenochtzitoc she tells the Traveler she borrowed it from the chief and lends it to them for the trip to Mare Jivari, where its Wayob-granted defensive power is used against Bakunawa.
### LARGE +
She stresses that she must return it to the chief afterwards. The wiki identifies the chief as Biram; the quest lines say only 'the chief'.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not in-world): a 4-star sword, base ATK 44 at level 1, secondary stat ATK%. Reward of the Version 5.2 event Iktomi Spiritseeking Scrolls; its refinement material is Shuttle of Odara.
### LARGE +
Passive "Diffusing Boundary": while the wielder is protected by a Shield, Normal and Charged Attack DMG and CRIT Rate increase. Inside The Last Survivor of Tenochtzitoc it is also a quest-only tool that grants a defensive skill and Soulshard Rain.

## roleplay
### LARGE
Masters of the Night-Wind characters treat the sword as ritual property of the tribe, not a personal weapon. Outsiders given it are trusted guests who must return it.

### STANDARD
Diffusing Boundary is the passive of the sword Calamity of Eshu (gameplay): while a Shield protects the wielder, Normal and Charged Attacks deal more damage and gain CRIT Rate. Its name evokes the blade's story of blurring the boundary between the Night Kingdom and the living world.
