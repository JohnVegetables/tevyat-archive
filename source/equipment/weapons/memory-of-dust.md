## summary
### LITE
Memory of Dust is a five-star catalyst of the Liyue series, a stone puzzle dumbbell. Its item story recounts Morax remembering Guizhong, who gave him the dumbbell as a token and a challenge: all her wisdom is hidden inside, if he can unlock it. He never could.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Item text calls it a stone dumbbell. The wiki notes its design is based on a Kongming/Luban lock burr puzzle, and its Chinese name means Mortal World's Lock.

## lore
### STANDARD
The story is told from Morax's memory. He recalls meeting a young woman with billowing sleeves among blooming Glaze Lilies; she acted solemn but seemed joyful as she handed him the dumbbell, calling it the mark of their pledge and her challenge to him. He insists there was no formal contract between them, just two people on the same path for their own reasons, yet he remembers the scene anyway.

At their end she said that the little people are as small and fragile as dust, and fear because they cannot know when disaster will take them, and so they work hard to grow clever. Since she and he differed so greatly in strength, she chose technique and wisdom, and with his brawn and her brains the city would become great. Her final smile was lonely as her form dissolved into fine dust. She told him to forget about the dumbbell.
### LARGE +
Guizhong's sentence ("If you can unlock it") breaks off, and Morax never learns how it would have ended. Over the years the wild Glaze Lilies dwindled until none were left. The wiki's trivia says the text is about the two gods who dwelled over Guili Plains and their first and final meeting.

### ULTRA +
Guizhong's words give a view of her motives that the item states directly: she thought mortals fragile and fearful, and chose wit over force because of the gulf in strength between her and Morax. The pledge is framed from his side as no formal contract, which preserves how each of them described the bond differently. The text is narrated through Morax's recollection, so it shows his memory rather than an objective record.

## owners
### STANDARD
Guizhong forged it by unknown methods and gave it to Morax (Zhongli). The wiki says he presumably still has it; the text never confirms this.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Game mechanics, not lore. The passive Golden Majesty raises Shield Strength and stacks ATK on hits, with a larger bonus while shielded. It shares this passive with the Liyue-series claymore The Unforged. Obtained from Weapon Event Wishes; added in Version 1.1.
