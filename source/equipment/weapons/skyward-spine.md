## summary
### LITE
Skyward Spine is a 5-star polearm, part of the Mondstadt series of weapons. Its item text is a short legend of the dragon Dvalin: he fought and killed the shadow dragon Durin in the skies over Mondstadt, but swallowed Durin's venom and spent the following centuries wounded in an ancient ruin. Added in Version 1.0.
### STANDARD +
The weapon is described as a polearm that symbolizes Dvalin's resolve, its shaft pointing at the heavens and clad in the might of sky and wind. It sits with Skyward Blade, Skyward Pride, Skyward Harp and Skyward Atlas in a set of Mondstadt weapons, but this entry covers only the spear.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Canon text gives only the symbolic picture: an upright shaft that points toward the heavens, wrapped in the power of sky and wind. Do not invent materials, a maker or measurements.

## lore
### LITE
In olden times the shadow dragon Durin envied Mondstadt's peace and came to defile it. His black wings darkened the sky and released toxic fumes that even the thousand winds could not hold back, so poisoned black rain fell on the people. The Anemo Archon heard their agony and called Dvalin to his side.
### STANDARD +
Cloaked in storm, Dvalin tore through the poisoned clouds and a whirlwind in his wake destroyed them. He seized Durin in his claws and carried him above the clouds, where the battle continued. In the end Dvalin's fangs pierced Durin's throat and his claws tore out its corrupted heart. The text calls Durin the sinners' creation and says it fell, full of regret, onto a snowy peak.
### LARGE +
Winning cost Dvalin dearly. Durin's venomous ichor ran between his teeth and into his body; the pain withered bone and marrow, and he coiled up in the depths of an ancient ruin. The text ends with Dvalin still believing he would be reawakened, scatter the darkness and sing again to the Anemo Archon's lyre, which he calls his dear friend.
### ULTRA +
The item itself names no names beyond Durin and Dvalin; the wiki links the sinners to Rhinedottir, the snowy peak to Dragonspine and the ancient ruin to Stormterror's Lair, where the Prologue finds Dvalin. The weapon is told in one voice (loyalty and resolve), while the Dragonspine Spear presents Durin's own dying dream of the same fight.

## owners
### STANDARD
No wielder is named in canon. The weapon is not tied to any playable character or to Venti, and no story shows Dvalin giving it to anyone.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not literal lore): a 5-star polearm with base ATK 48 and an Energy Recharge secondary stat. Its passive, Black Wing, raises CRIT Rate and Normal ATK SPD and gives Normal and Charged Attacks a chance of a vacuum blade that deals area damage (roughly once per two seconds). It is on the Standard Wish and weapon event Wishes.
