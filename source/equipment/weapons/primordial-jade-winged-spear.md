## summary
### LITE
Primordial Jade Winged-Spear is a five-star polearm of Liyue. Its item story is a creation legend: at Liyue's foundation a sea behemoth called Baqiu, which the Geo Archon's own Geo Whale could not beat, drowned settlements with its waves. The Geo Archon hand-forged a black kite from jade and stone, it dove into the sea, and Baqiu sank for good.
### STANDARD +
It is part of the Primordial Jade series, the Liyue weapons of the Archon War era. Despite its polearm form the text describes the origin object as a kite (a bird), not a spear.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Item text says only that the Geo Archon fashioned a black kite by hand from jade and stones, and that the stone bird took flight once forged. Nothing is said of the polearm's own look; the name's "Winged" and the kite are the only visual hints.

## lore
### STANDARD
Item story: when Liyue was founded, monsters and gods still ruled the seas and the forebears lived in fear, fighting the sea with all they could. Over many ages a behemoth rose that dominated the waters; even the Geo Archon's Geo Whale could not triumph. Liyue's people called it Baqiu, a beast without rival in the ocean's deepest abyss, whose enormous waves washed away whole settlements.
### LARGE +
The Geo Archon then made a black kite by hand from jade and stones. Once forged it took to the sky, dove into the heart of the sea and joined the battle against the behemoth and the whale. The behemoth sank into the depths to remain there, and Liyue's people were released from its thundering roars. The wiki adds that in Chinese the name Baqiu combines the characters for eight and young horned dragon, and that the weapon's Chinese name refers to a jade of the State of Chu (He's jade); the English text drops both allusions.
### ULTRA +
The wiki's series page frames the story as an object Morax made from jade for battle, plunging into the sea like a spear. Treat that as the wiki's reading: the item text describes a kite, and the connection between the kite and a polearm is not spelled out.

## owners
### STANDARD
The item text names no later owner. In promotional art Xiao wields the ascended version in his official art and character demo; that is presentation, not a canon statement that Xiao owns this exact weapon.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not lore): a five-star polearm in the standard Wanderlust Invocation and weapon event wishes. Base ATK 48, secondary stat CRIT Rate 4.8%. Passive "Eagle Spear of Justice": on hit, ATK rises 3.2-6.0% for 6 seconds, up to 7 stacks, once every 0.3 seconds; at max stacks DMG dealt rises 12-24% (refinements 1 to 5). The wielder must be on the field to gain stacks.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay use (this project's suggestion, not canon text): an old Liyue sailors' and coast-dwellers' legend of the time before the harbour, useful for themes of the sea as an enemy and of a god's ingenuity. Keep it a tale; no character in canon can confirm it.

## entry: Baqiu
@keys: Baqiu
@spoiler: none
### STANDARD
Baqiu is the sea behemoth named in the Primordial Jade Winged-Spear item text, a beast that dominated the seas around early Liyue, washed away settlements with its waves and defeated the Geo Whale of the Geo Archon. It was finally sunk by a black jade-and-stone kite the Geo Archon made. It appears only in that item text.
