## summary
### LITE
Song of Broken Pines is a 5-star claymore, part of the Millennial Movement series. Its item text is a history of Mondstadt's rebel songs: how bards and a split Church of Favonius stood against the tyrant Decarabian and the ruling aristocracy, and how a folk song gained a second, defiant half when the rebellion rose. Added in Version 1.5.
### STANDARD +
The Chinese name means roughly "when the pine-sound echoes", a phrase from the song's last line; this is wiki etymology, not an in-world title.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Canon text describes it as a greatsword as light as the sigh of grass in the breeze, yet as merciless to the corrupt as a typhoon. Nothing more is said of its look.

## lore
### LITE
The text opens with a folk song urging people to toss coins to the bard, give a bouquet to a fair maiden, drink bitter wine and sing for tomorrow. Mondstadt, the text says, is a land where songs travel on the wind, so tyrants such as Decarabian and the ruling aristocrats sometimes banned certain chords and tunes, sensing resistance in the bards' music.
### STANDARD +
Songs and hymns were also used by rebels to communicate. Under the aristocracy the Church that revered the Anemo Archon split in a schism: clergy who dined at the lords' table, wrote hymns of praise and even helped overturn the archon's statues, against saints without office who walked the streets and wine cellars, blessed slaves and commoners with the original holy manuscripts and wrote forbidden songs.
### LARGE +
When the gladiator from a foreign land, Vennessa, rose together with the re-awakened Anemo Archon and raised the banner of rebellion, the aged saint called the Nameless Shepherd mobilized the Church of Favonius's true adherents. Many shed martyr blood on the emerald fields, and together they sang the previously unsung second half of the old song: leave the keen steel to those who will die in the fight, prepare the thieves' gallows, sharpen rusted arrows, and shoot the beasts down when the music sounds.
### ULTRA +
Read this as one partial account from the rebels' side. It sets Church and aristocracy against each other and credits song with real organizing power, but it names no battles, dates or numbers. "Beasts" in the last line is a figure for the corrupt nobility; the original Chinese plays on a phrase for dressed-up beasts, per the wiki trivia, which is not an official gloss.

## owners
### STANDARD
No owner is named in canon. Eula carries it in promotional art only; do not treat it as an heirloom of the Lawrence Clan or of Vennessa.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not literal lore): a 5-star claymore with base ATK 49 and a Physical DMG Bonus secondary stat. Its passive, Rebel's Banner-Hymn, raises ATK and gives the wielder a Sigil of Whispers when Normal or Charged Attacks hit (at most once per 0.3s). With four Sigils, all are consumed and all nearby party members gain the Millennial Movement: Banner-Hymn effect for 12s (higher ATK and Normal ATK SPD), after which Sigils stop accruing for 20s. Different Millennial Movement buffs do not stack. Obtained from weapon event Wishes.

## entry: Nameless Shepherd

@version: 1.5

### STANDARD
The Nameless Shepherd is an aged saint of the Church of Favonius mentioned only in Song of Broken Pines. When Vennessa raised the banner of rebellion, the Shepherd mobilized the Church's true adherents, and many of them died on the fields singing the rebel half of an old folk song. Nothing else is known, including the Shepherd's real name or fate.
