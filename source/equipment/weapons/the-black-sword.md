## summary
### LITE
The Black Sword is a 4-star sword from the Battle Pass Gnostic Hymn, in the game since Version 1.0. The in-game short description calls it a pitch-black longsword that thirsts for violence and conflict. The full text is the parable of a bright white knight whose pursuit of justice slid into slaughter.
### STANDARD +
Its text ties to the Bloodstained Chivalry artifact set, and the wiki notes that the decoration atop the blade resembles that set's circlet.

## appearance
### STANDARD
A pitch-black longsword with a decoration on top of the blade. The wiki notes the decoration strongly resembles the circlet of the Bloodstained Chivalry artifact set. In the story the knight's sword once hung at his hip with a mirror sheen before the bloodshed blackened it.

## lore
### LITE
A blade that thirsts after fresh blood is awakened by its scent, and those who wield and nourish it keep killing until even a pure soul is blackened. The text tells of a white knight who rode to cut down injustice, beasts and fires, taught by the Wolf Pup in chivalry, justice and swordsmanship.
### STANDARD +
The knight recalls losing himself in cutting, slashing and piercing, feeling a tingle at the blade's meeting flesh and believing this was justice getting its due. A voice calls him a fool and says slaughter in the name of justice is still slaughter; he answers that it is justice.
### LARGE +
The narration urges him to go on though the white flowers the maidens give him are bloodied black, though his sword no longer gleams, his face turns hideous and he must wear an iron mask, and though those he defends do not understand him. The black-stained knight persists, pursuing monsters in the name of justice.
### ULTRA +
The text ends with him coming upon an ancient, ruined realm where he found the ultimate injustice, leaving the reader to supply what that was. The wiki links the realm to Khaenri'ah, the injustice to hilichurls, the knight to Roland and the Wolf Pup to Rostam, and the maidens' flowers and the iron mask to the Bloodstained Chivalry set's items. Treat these links as the wiki's cross-references.

## owners
### STANDARD
The item text names no owner. The wiki links the white knight to Roland; see his entry for the details.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not literal lore): 4-star sword, base ATK 42, CRIT Rate secondary stat. Passive Justice: Normal and Charged Attack DMG rises 20 to 40 per cent by refinement, and a CRIT hit from those attacks restores 60 to 100 per cent of ATK as HP, at most once every 5s. The healing can trigger even if the hit deals no damage to an invulnerable or shielded enemy. Obtained through the Battle Pass Gnostic Hymn bundle.
