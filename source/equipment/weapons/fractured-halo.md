## summary
### LITE
A spinning mechanical blade produced by the Clink-Clank Krumkake Craftshop, seeming to hide secrets from a bygone era. A 5-star polearm from Version 5.8, wielded by Ineffa.
### STANDARD +
Its item text is Ineffa's fragmentary memory of the age of the three moons.

## appearance
### STANDARD
A spinning, halo-like mechanical blade with a cyclonic mechanism whose centre holds a hidden remnant of the Moon. In her portrait art and demo Ineffa carries the unascended version.

## lore
### LITE
After her revival, Ineffa remembers an age even bards forget, but her memories feel hollow, like thoughts flaring and fading in a trance.
### STANDARD +
She recalls following the Master of Light and Flame to visit the moon-dwelling wills on their three jade thrones, at a time when the king who set the moons aloft had vanished into the fog between the stars. The Master received only three silences in three palace halls.
### LARGE +
The king had sought the world beyond, and the moons governed the cycles in his place. A reaver from beyond the skies replaced the primordial creator and took the vows that kept the world turning. When the king returned corrupted by darkness his fury was boundless; the Master of Light and Flame, waking from feigned death, stopped the lords and the regent from exploring the stars and replicating moon-forging. Whether the jade-crowned goddesses truly betrayed the dragonborn kingdom is left open, and none who knew the answer survived the war.
### ULTRA +
Ineffa tells the girl who rebuilt her body and forged her weapon (Aino) that these snapshots are all she can recall. Aino, clever, suppresses her own inquiring gaze, and it becomes Ineffa's private secret that when the heavenly ring shattered, a jade plate fell from the sky. The dragons cheered the death of the moons that had betrayed them, but she remembered the silent shadows in their palaces quietly hiding the fallen Moon's remnant. That remnant now sits at the centre of the cyclonic mechanism, hidden where no one else will know. The text describes the snowfields turning to blackened earth and volcanoes clogging with ice as an after-effect of the silences.

## owners
### LITE
Ineffa wields it; the text says Aino forged it for her.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not in-world): 5-star polearm, CRIT DMG secondary. Passive Purifying Crown raises ATK for 20 seconds after a Skill or Burst, and if the wielder creates a shield the party deals more Lunar-Charged damage.

## roleplay
### LARGE
Ineffa treats it as hers and as a keeper of a secret; she does not volunteer the hidden remnant to others. Keep the ancient-war memories as uncertain recollection.
