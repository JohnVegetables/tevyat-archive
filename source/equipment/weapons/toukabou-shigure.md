## summary
### LITE
Toukabou Shigure is a four-star sword of the Youkai series, designed as an umbrella and obtained during the Version 3.3 event Akitsu Kimodameshi. Its text is a ghost story ("kaidan") told by a woman from Fontaine: a dancer's cherished umbrella becomes cursed after two deaths, turns into a one-eyed, one-legged, long-tongued youkai, and is finally washed and enshrined by a young shrine maiden.

## appearance
### STANDARD
The sword is an umbrella, in the manner of the kasa-obake umbrella youkai. The wiki notes its icon and 3D model are oriented differently from other swords (handle at the bottom left); item text itself describes Inazuman umbrellas in general as exquisite artistry rather than describing this one.

## lore
### STANDARD
The narrator is introduced as a visitor who is moved by Inazuman umbrellas. Hundreds of years ago, at a festival season, a girl danced with an intricate umbrella and drank the Oni clan's wine, cheered by humans and youkai alike, until she went to war with the army and died; her subordinates offered the umbrella to a shrine. A martial family's daughter bought it at full price, but before she could use it a message announced her husband's death in battle; she fell ill and died, and her parents returned the umbrella to the shrine to be sealed as cursed.
### LARGE +
Months later a youkai was rumoured to stalk the streets on rainy nights: an umbrella taller than a man with a single eye, a single leg and a long tongue that licked passers-by. It seemed more of a prankster, but people worried, young women stayed home and the elderly said the wise and powerful would have dealt with it easily if they were not absent. A young shrine maiden washed the umbrella from the tip with a wooden spoon, wiped it with silk, and told it that tomorrow and the day after would still come even if yesterday's rains ceased, and that if its noble owner were alive it would surely not be in this state. She had it enshrined in an annex and it was never heard of again.
### ULTRA +
The narrator closes by saying that she searched the shrines of Narukami and found no mention of such an enshrined umbrella; her Inazuman friend laughs and asks whether she takes kaidan to be true. This frame matters: the text is explicitly folklore passed through a foreigner's retelling. The wiki's trivia says the design is based on the kasa-obake, a real Japanese umbrella yokai.

## owners
### STANDARD
No owner is named: the umbrella passes from the dancing girl to a martial family's daughter to a shrine, in an unverified story.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not lore): four-star sword of the Youkai series. Base ATK 42, secondary stat Elemental Mastery 36. Passive "Kaidan: Rainfall Earthbinder": after an attack hits opponents, one of them gains Cursed Parasol for 10s (once per 15s); the wielder deals extra damage to that enemy, and if it is defeated the cooldown refreshes. Obtained during the 3.3 event "Akitsu Kimodameshi".

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay use (this project's suggestion, not canon text): a prop for ghost-story nights; keep the "hearsay" framing, and let characters treat it as a spooky tale rather than fact.
