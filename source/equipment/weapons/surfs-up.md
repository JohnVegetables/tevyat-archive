## summary
### LITE
Surf's Up is a 5-star catalyst: a commemorative prize reserved for the champion of the First All-Tribe Surfing Championship, kept at the Leisurely Puffer while the tournament is being prepared.
### STANDARD +
Its story is a comic legend about the Pyro Archon Cochanina, a surfing champion, driving the Volcano Lord away from the People of the Springs.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Canon gives no physical description of the item beyond calling it a commemorative piece. Keep descriptions simple and festive; do not add decorations.

## lore
### LITE
A huge black Volcano Lord fouled the springs of the People of the Springs by dumping his filth into them. The Pyro Archon Cochanina, a surfing champion, left her famous blade at home, took only a surfboard, and tricked him out to sea.
### STANDARD +
She told him the tribe's treasure, the Iridescent Chalice left by the hero Uenuku, lay in the deep sea and that only he deserved it. When the clumsy Volcano Lord was dizzy, she tossed out a Drifting Pufferfish as the supposed key; he charged it, was struck, and fell off his board.
### LARGE +
The Volcano Lord, a black and purple salamander-like monster, took advantage of other dragons' absence to occupy the Great Volcano of Tollan and ran roughshod over the people. He had been beaten many times by earlier Pyro Archons but always slipped away to hide at the volcano's bottom. This time he wanted to wash his filth in the springs, and the smoke he belched made the Flowcurrent Birds leave. The elder reported to Cochanina, who left her trusted weapon, the renowned Unu Pachacuti blade, and brought only a surfboard.

She flattered him into joining her, and he followed her wake behind the tribe's surfing champion. Struggling in the sea, he latched onto reeds and drifted a long time before reaching shore. Cochanina did not manage to expel him from Natlan, but he never again dared bother the People of the Springs.
### ULTRA +
The description's framing says the prize is in temporary storage at the Leisurely Puffer, with the shop owner (Mualani) as custodian, because the First All-Tribe Surfing Championship is only being prepared. The tale is also a window onto the Pyro Archons as surfers and tricksters: the wiki's profile of Cochanina adds that she was a kind, fun-loving mortal, known by the divine name Haborym, and later one of the Pyro Archons who contained the Bakunawa. Those later facts come from other quests, not from this item.

## owners
### LITE
No wielder is named. Mualani is the custodian of the prize, not its holder.
### LARGE +
Cochanina is the hero of the story but carried a surfboard and left her own blade at home; the tournament champion is the intended future owner. A scene giving it to a particular character is an invention.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not in-world): a 5-star catalyst, base ATK 44 at level 1, secondary stat CRIT DMG; Weapon Event Wishes; introduced in Version 5.0.
### LARGE +
Passive "Aqua Remembrance": Max HP is increased by 20% to 40%. Once every 15s, for 14s after using an Elemental Skill, the wielder gains 4 Scorching Summer stacks, each increasing Normal Attack DMG by 12% to 24%. During the effect every 1.5s a stack is lost after a Normal Attack hits, and a stack is gained (once per 1.5s) after triggering Vaporize on an opponent. Ascension uses the Blazing Sacrificial Heart materials, Will-series boss materials and Fang common materials.

## roleplay
### LARGE
For scenes: Natlanese locals tell the Volcano Lord story as a children's tale. The tone is playful; keep Cochanina clever and good-humoured, not solemn.
