## summary
### LITE
Angelos' Heptades is a 5-star catalyst in the Hexerei series, described as a golden box as splendid as the light of the heavens that, according to legend, once belonged to a divine envoy of the Eternal Court. Its text tells how the youngest angel was hidden from the angels' rebellion, then chose to cast off divinity and wander among mortals. Added in Version 6.6.
### STANDARD +
The Hexerei Series is mostly tied to Mondstadt; the wiki points out that this catalyst is the exception.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Canon describes only a golden box as splendid as the light of the heavens; the item text does not describe its shape any further.

## lore
### LITE
The envoy was a newborn girl whose elder sisters protected her; she later gave up her divinity and her old powers, kept human form, and walked the mortal world alone.
### STANDARD +
Her sisters, including a mother of roses and lilies and a songstress, wrapped her in perfume and warm waters so the music of the world's mundane strings would never touch her. When starlight from beyond ended the paradise beneath the silver-white tree, the eldest daughter who overstepped fell from a mountain peak and armored emissaries perished, while the youngest was hidden far away in a palace in the wilds.
### LARGE +
The sisters decided to overthrow the throne of the Most High for the sake of the oppressed, but resolved not to harden the youngest's heart for war, saying the yoke of rebellion should not weigh on her wings. Though no one knew the capricious ruler's desire, her rule still let one choose a path, and the youngest chose to cast off divinity. She was miraculously allowed to keep a human form, and stepped into the mortal world with broken wings and a fragile body, wandering a land of sorrow for thousands of years while towers turned to sand. The text says the envoy met a witch and at last was relieved of the past, and that her crown, wrought from countless human joys and sorrows and the adventures of mortal heroes, became irreplaceable. The final line is her exclamation, "What a beautiful world this is!"
### ULTRA +
The wiki links the mother of roses and lilies to Nabu Malikata, the songstress to Sybilla, the eldest daughter to Koitar, the starlight to Anacharsis, the witch to Alice and the girl to Nicole. The text itself names none of them, so treat those identifications as strongly supported rather than stated. The "cradle of tender Azurelight" in the text lines up with the Azurelight sermon, released in Version 5.7, and "the chains of eternity" before they were shattered place the story in the era before the angels' fall.

## owners
### LITE
The weapon "once belonged to" a divine envoy of the Eternal Court. The wiki reads the envoy as Nicole, the youngest angel; Nicole was the featured character on the Version 6.6 banner running with this weapon, but no text declares she holds it now.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Game mechanics, not lore: a 5-star catalyst, base ATK 49, ATK secondary stat. Crown of the Final Scion raises ATK. After the wielder creates a Shield they gain Pathfinder's Light, which raises the active character's DMG by ATK scaling, plus Guide's Contentment, which restores Elemental Energy; opening a chest out of combat also triggers it. Under Hexerei: Secret Rite, a Hexerei character off-field gains half the bonus. It was on the weapon event Wish Epitome Invocation (2026-05-20 to 2026-06-09).

## roleplay
### LARGE
This archive's suggested use: a golden keepsake of the youngest angel's choice to be mortal. Frame it as a story about leaving the sky rather than a relic of power, and do not assign it to a specific present-day character beyond what is cited.
