## summary
### LITE
Amos' Bow is an extremely ancient five-star bow from Old Mondstadt, when the land was still a wasteland under the God of Storms Decarabian. It is named for Amos, a huntress who loved Decarabian, then turned against him and fired an arrow at him from the top of his tower. Item text says the bow still holds power although its owner is long gone, and that it draws strength from all things in the world.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Item text describes the bow only as extremely ancient and still powerful; it gives no detail of its look, so avoid inventing any.

## lore
### STANDARD
The item story is a short tragedy. In a barren age, before the land was green, a barefoot maiden followed the footprints of the Lord of the Tower through the snow. He had been her love and also her enemy, but her hunt was for more than simple vengeance. She told him of her dreams of ocean waves and sand, forests, boars playing in berry bushes and a towering spire, and he did not hear her. She came to see that only she had spoken sincerely: he talked of love but was accompanied only by razor winds, and he read his subjects' bent backs in the howling wind as devotion.
### LARGE +
The story places this in the era when the Tyrant of the North Wind (Boreas) warred with the Lord of the Tower. At the end of that war, when the wind of resistance first blew, the huntress, accompanied by a nameless young man, the wind spirit (Barbatos) and a knight, scaled the tower to face the eccentric lord. Her thought as she climbed was "Finally, I shall hold his gaze." The text ends at the moment her arrow flew and the piercing wind was about to tear her apart, when she finally understood how far apart they were. The Freedom-Sworn text names the same company as a youth, a spirit, an archer, a knight and a flame-haired warrior.
### ULTRA +
The wiki's Lore section states plainly that the bow was originally Amos' own and that she used it to hunt boars as well as in her attempt to kill Decarabian. Other item texts the wiki cites for Amos (the artifact set Minnesang of Love and Lament and related pieces) add that she was a renowned hunter given to Decarabian by her clan and made his executor, and that her turn against him was rooted in wanting his love; those accounts were not re-read here, so treat them as medium confidence. The bow's boar imagery and the huntress's story are told only through item text and the Venti Story Quest teaser The Boy and the Whirlwind.

## owners
### STANDARD
Original owner: Amos, the huntress (historical.amos), who died in the rebellion. The item text gives no later owners. In promotional art Ganyu holds this bow in her character demo and card, and Fischl is shown with it in her demo; those are presentation, not canon claims that either of them owns it.
### LARGE +
The Traveler can use it as an ordinary player weapon; it is not a quest item with a stated custodian.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not lore): a five-star bow, obtainable from the standard Wanderlust Invocation and weapon event wishes. Base ATK 46, secondary stat ATK 10.8%. Passive "Strong-Willed": raises Normal and Charged Attack DMG, and DMG rises again every 0.1 seconds the arrow is in flight, up to five times (12-24% base bonus and 8-16% per step at refinement 1-5). Fittingly for the story, its damage grows with distance from the target.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay use (this project's suggestion, not canon text): the bow works as a symbol of unreturned devotion and of seeing someone clearly too late. Characters who know Mondstadt history may associate it with the rebellion; ordinary Mondstadters are unlikely to know its full story.

## entry: Amos

@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Amos was a huntress of Old Mondstadt about 2,600 years ago and the lover or favourite of Decarabian, the God of Storms, before she joined the rebellion against him. The wiki records that she died in the final confrontation, torn apart by his winds as her arrow reached him. Her bow carries her name. The full account of her motives is only in item texts, not in quest dialogue.

### LARGE
The Boy and the Whirlwind is the teaser shown in Venti's Story Quest ("Should You Be Trapped in a Windless Land"). The wiki says it depicts a group of four climbing Decarabian's tower, including Amos with long white hair, and that the Red-Haired Warrior was later recorded as a fifth member by the Freedom-Sworn text. It is the only visual depiction of Amos.
