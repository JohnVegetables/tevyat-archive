## summary
### LITE
Royal Greatsword is a 4-star claymore of the Royal series, an old greatsword once used by the nobles who ruled Mondstadt, made of the finest materials and described as a weapon for royals only. Its text covers the noble duty to fight monsters and the history of gladiatorial combat.
### STANDARD +
Even after being passed down for generations it is described as good as new.

## appearance
### STANDARD
A large, finely made greatsword that has aged without losing its condition. The wiki notes Eula is shown with it in a character teaser and a Collected Miscellany video.

## lore
### LITE
Combat was once a responsibility of the nobles, who protected their people from monsters. They all but forgot that duty, which turned them into the very monsters they once sought to destroy.
### STANDARD +
The text says research suggests gladiatorial combat began in a religious context during the aristocracy's rule, as a sacrificial ceremony held jointly with prayers to the gods. It was later turned into a perverted sport for the Lawrence Clan's pleasure and abolished by the Knights of Favonius, who now outlaw it.
### LARGE +
Some texts of the original prayers survive. In one, the fighter asks the guardian winds of Mondstadt to look after themselves, their friends, their countrymen and their opponent, and hopes the clash of blades and offering of sweat and blood please them. In another, they ask the winds for strength when exhausted and wisdom to tell good from evil when uncertain.
### ULTRA +
The prayers are a useful scene detail for how Mondstadters once addressed wind in a martial setting. Keep them as surviving texts, not as a current practice, since the Knights ban gladiatorial combat.

## owners
### LITE
No owner is named; the text says only that nobles used such greatswords.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not in-world): 4-star claymore, base ATK 44, secondary stat ATK 6%, sold in Paimon's Bargains. Passive Focus: dealing damage increases CRIT Rate by 8% to 16% by refinement per stack, up to 5 stacks, and a CRIT hit removes all stacks.
