## summary
### LITE
Flowing Purity is a four-star catalyst of the Beaumont series, forged in Fontaine. The weapon is a strangely shaped anthology said to record all the scripts written by Coppelius, and the item text presents a reconstruction of fragments from his last opera, Clockwork Coppelia.
### STANDARD +
The script of Clockwork Coppelia was never publicized or published. The text is a compiled attempt, made with the consent of Coppelius's family and the survivors who recalled it, to reproduce a part of it.

## appearance
### STANDARD
A catalyst shaped like an anthology of scripts. For characters with Arkhe, the weapon shows Pneuma or Ousia alignment indicators.

## lore
### STANDARD
A preface-style note explains that the opera's script was never released, for reasons 'known to all and sundry'. Survivors of the day at the splendid opera theater wrote down parts and lines performed before the intermission, and a company collated them with the family's consent, hoping to prompt more capable efforts.
### LARGE +
The first excerpt is a scene in which Coppelius's plan is exposed before the Marechaussee Hunters and he takes his last farewell of Coppelia before punishment. He asks her to sing once more, calls her his love, sin and soul, and declares that from today he will bring happiness to those he chooses instead of seeking what pleases him. Coppelia answers by asking how she should sing of the sin and its inevitable punishment, warning that people prize a colorful facade and miss the poison inside, and that all he pursued is as hollow as stage scenery.
### ULTRA +
The second excerpt is set on a journey: Coppelia tells the youth Nathaniel that her creator has already died. She means to follow him, since when her rusted gears return to ash, sin will be forgotten, and for flesh and blood to be forgotten is to be forgiven. She no longer wishes to see the sun, hear birds or keep her heart beating in a world without the one who gave it to her, and asks where such a heart, stained with the same sin and tortured by regret, could find peace. Nathaniel calls it absurd and commonplace, remarking that the wise take orders from fools.

## owners
### STANDARD
The text names no owner; the scripts belong to the playwright Coppelius.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Game mechanics, not story. Passive Unfinished Masterpiece grants All Elemental DMG Bonus for 15 seconds after an Elemental Skill, with a Bond of Life worth 24% of Max HP (once per 10 seconds). Clearing the Bond adds more bonus per 1,000 HP cleared, up to a maximum, only when the Bond was increased by this weapon. Base ATK 44, ATK 6% secondary stat.
