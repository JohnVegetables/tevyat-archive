## summary
### LITE
Blackcliff Longsword is a 4-star sword of the Blackcliff series, described in game as a sword of blackstone with a dark crimson glow on its black blade. It was wrought by the master craftsman Han Wu from crystallized blackcliff, a rare stone he fetched from the Chasm. It has been in the game since Version 1.0.
### STANDARD +
Its item text tells how a cave-in trapped Han Wu's mining crew for four days, how a prototype blade carved them out, and how the sword shattered in the first sunlight and was later remade and named.

## appearance
### STANDARD
A black blade with a dark crimson line along its centre. According to the item text the strand is about the length of an outstretched hand and shows on a clear moonlit night, with a faint blood-red flicker in the dark. Do not invent hilt or guard details beyond this.

## lore
### LITE
Blackcliff is a rare stone; the longsword is cut from its crystallized form and is said to slice through gold and jade easily.
### STANDARD +
After new prototype designs appeared, Han Wu overhauled his whole catalogue. He believed the right blackcliff would give a superior blade, but final hardness and durability depend on subtle differences in forging temperature and cooling water, a challenge he felt he had to master to rise in his craft. Through a friend in the Yun family he joined a crew to the Chasm to collect the material himself.
### LARGE +
A cave-in trapped the crew in the shaft for four days. Their tools could not cut the rock, and without daylight they lost track of time; one by one they fell into madness and despair. Han Wu saw a faint flicker from the corner of the cave where the prototype sword lay, and with it he carved a way out. The moment the first sunlight touched the blade it shattered into countless pieces and the smith collapsed. Months later he remade the sword and named it Blackcliff Longsword because it is made of blackcliff.
### ULTRA +
Everyone who saw the finished sword admired its beauty, sharpness and tenacity, yet the crew never spoke of what they had lived through. The text closes with the image of Han Wu striking the cave wall in the darkness and the sword releasing a roar like thunder over the plains, and suggests the crew understood it foreshadowed an earthquake years later. It is a legend-style narration and never says who tells it.

## owners
### STANDARD
No wielder is named in the item text. Han Wu is its maker. Xingqiu is briefly shown with a Blackcliff Longsword in his Travelers' Tales anecdote (a cameo, not a story claim of ownership).

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not literal lore): 4-star sword, base ATK 44, CRIT DMG secondary stat. Passive Press the Advantage: after defeating an opponent, ATK rises for 30s (12 to 24 per cent by refinement), stacking to 3 with independent durations. It is a Paimon's Bargains item and belongs to the Blackcliff series of weapons.

### STANDARD
Han Wu is the master craftsman named in the item texts of the Blackcliff Longsword and Prototype Archaic. After the great disaster he worked with the Yun family forge on new weapon designs called the prototypes, and later turned to blackcliff for a superior blade.
