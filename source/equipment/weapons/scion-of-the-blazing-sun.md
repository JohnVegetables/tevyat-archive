## summary
### LITE
Scion of the Blazing Sun is a 4-star bow: an ancient longbow from King Deshret's era, carved with indecipherable ancient text and solemn patterns. Its story traces the priests of King Deshret's kingdom, their archery, and the descendants who still keep the old ceremonies in secret.
### STANDARD +
It is a Battle Pass (Gnostic Hymn) reward from Version 4.0 and belongs to the Gnostic Hymn 2 series.

## appearance
### STANDARD
An ancient longbow carved with indecipherable text and solemn patterns.

## lore
@version: 4.0
### LITE
In the era when the desert king ruled the land, authority covered it like sunlight over the dunes. The nomads' surviving songs sing of that golden age, and say the red lord once contended against the bright sun and fell to the earth like a meteorite. Some priests were not slain by the kings and princes who followed and passed down King Deshret's art of archery.
### STANDARD +
The priests' descendants call themselves the "Heirs of the Burning Sun" and continue the ceremonies of the old kingdom without ceasing. Few have the right to inherit the priests' legacy, few can read the ancient texts, and fewer still master the combat arts the priests prized: cudgel, spear and, above all, archery. The Eremites call such people "ascetics".
### LARGE +
Some ascetics have turned their loyalty to the only surviving God King of Sumeru, while most vanished into the desert among snakes, scorpions and specters. Those loyal to the rainforest keep the habits of being hidden and silent, repaying an ancient favor, yet mourn their ancestors and King Deshret, who met with disaster millennia before. The art and ceremony of archery survive in their hands even though the meaning of many texts and pictures has been lost or corrupted.
### ULTRA +
The text ends with a darker branch: ascetics who strayed from the priests' path, grew obsessed with mirages of authority, tried to mold the nomadic tribes, and used the strict old ceremonies to train lost children as "Falcons". To help bring about a new "king" they trained mindless Falcons and dogs. The text calls this another tale, saying those with great ambition often vanish into the desert without a sound.

## owners
### LITE
No in-world owner is named. The wiki notes Sethos wields the ascended bow in his character introduction, which is promotional use only.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not in-world): 4-star bow, level 1 base ATK 44, secondary stat CRIT Rate 4%. Passive The Way of Sunfire: after a Charged Attack hit, a Sunfire Arrow descends on the opponent hit, deals ATK-based damage and applies Heartsearer for 10 s, increasing Charged Attack DMG the opponent takes from the wielder; once every 10 s.
### LARGE +
At refinement 1 the Sunfire Arrow deals 60% ATK and Heartsearer adds 28% Charged Attack DMG taken; at refinement 5 these are 120% and 56%.

## roleplay

Use it as a relic of the priests' hidden tradition; keep the Falcon branch as rumor with no named culprit.
