## summary
### LITE
The Exaiphanes Blade is a 5-star sword given to the Traveler during the Snezhnaya Archon Quest (Chapter VII, Act I), described as a longsword that once shone with the light of the stars and in the voyager's hand becomes a new light unconnected to any past era. It reacts to Statues of The Seven in Snezhnaya and can only be strengthened at them. Added in Version 7.0.
### STANDARD +
Unlike other weapons it cannot be refined with ordinary materials. After the Traveler uses Cryo on a Statue of The Seven in Snezhnaya, the blade can be enhanced there once per Statue.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Canon describes it as a longsword that once shone with the light of the stars, and in the Traveler's hand as a new light. No further detail is given in the cited text.

## lore
### LITE
The blade is obtained in a special domain during Great Deeds on the Tundra, and it seems to react whenever the Traveler comes near a Statue of The Seven in Snezhnaya.
### STANDARD +
Each Statue of The Seven in Snezhnaya can bless the sword once. The in-game Function Details say that the blade can only be enhanced at those Statues, after the Traveler uses Cryo to awaken and connect to them. The quest places the first enhancement at a Statue in Zolnyy.
### LARGE +
Each enhancement stage has a short text. The first, Origin of Stellar Grace, speaks of a traveler who journeyed far into foreign lands for a slim chance of redemption. The second, Melody of the Astral Voices, says it is not known when the traveler gave up capturing sounds amid the eternal silence. The third, Boundless Journey to the Starry Sea, asks how long since someone looked at the stars hoping to find answers. The fourth, Solitary Drifting of the Astral Vessel, compares people to moths drawn to flame while an ordinary life still yearns to survive. The fifth, Grand Wish of the Shifting Stars, says darkness cannot dull the traveler's edge and that with swords and plowshares aboard a new beginning is within reach.
### ULTRA +
The wiki's table shows the blade's Level, ATK and CRIT Rate climbing with each Statue (for example ATK 122 after the first, 532 after the fourth, 608 after the fifth and sixth) with Refinement Rank rising to 4 at the sixth. The sixth stage is named Twilight of Fallen Stars; its description and the seventh stage were not filled in on the wiki. Treat the stage texts as item flavor: they do not say whom "the traveler" is.

## owners
### LITE
The blade is the player's Traveler's weapon, obtained during the Snezhnaya Archon Quest. Its Traveler's Path passive applies only when the Traveler equips it.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Game mechanics, not lore: a 5-star sword, base ATK 46, CRIT Rate secondary stat. The passive, Traveler's Path, grants CRIT DMG, ATK and Energy effects that scale with enhancement, can trigger even when the Traveler is off-field and has a cooldown. It needs no Mora or materials, and the Statue enhancement does not require the Traveler to be in the party. Obtained at step 18 of the quest, which grants the achievement Mystery Play of the Pale Star.

## roleplay
### LARGE
This archive's suggested use: a blade whose light belongs to the Traveler's own journey rather than any past age, strengthened by the Statues of The Seven in a frozen land. Do not explain its origin, because canon does not.
