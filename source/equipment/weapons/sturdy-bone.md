## summary
### LITE
Sturdy Bone is a 4-star longsword from Natlan, made of bone that has only grown harder with use. Legend says it was the hero Wanjiru's reliable weapon.
### STANDARD +
Its story shows Wanjiru, famous for strength, slipping into the great dragon's tomb for a weapon and coming back with a bone in her palm.

## appearance
### STANDARD
A longsword made of bleached bone, the spit of a giant beast's tail in the story. Do not invent engravings or a handle design beyond a hilt.

## lore
### LITE
The great dragon that once ruled the land of flame had a tomb of bleached bone ridges like giant claws, patrolled by secret machines. Hearing of it around the tribe's fire, Wanjiru leaps for joy: nothing in the world was more fearsome, and surely she could find there the weapon she wanted most.
### STANDARD +
The text lists her feats: shattering century-old ironwood with a swing, crushing raw ore to powder, bending boulders and toughest metal in her fingers. She jumps into the volcano from Tollan's peak, avoids the serpent-like guardian constructs and enters the ruins, following scars left by the heroes who once fought the dragon. She wishes she had been born in those days. The boneyard seems only a place of lonely corpses until a huge winged golden shadow throws itself at her.
### LARGE +
She fights it with a laugh in a bloody, eye-for-an-eye brawl and ends it by tearing off its shattered wing with her bare hands. The shadow vanishes with a wail as if it had never been real. She finds her hand still outstretched, her arm aching and a heavy dragon bone in her palm shaped like the tail of the beast she had grappled.
### ULTRA +
When she tells her story-loving friends, the High Priest of the Night-Wind scrunches his nose and says Tollan is forbidden ground and she was too young then; another friend holding a ceremonial blade says they have never even seen her draw the weapon. She replies that she just usually doesn't need to. On the eve of battle by the campfire, seeing the great black beast's silhouette on the shore, she grips the hilt of the bleached bone blade and waits for the battlefield where she will draw it once more. The wiki links the black beast to the Abyss; the text itself says only that she keeps the blade in reserve for that battle.

## owners
### LITE
Wanjiru, by the text's legend. No later wielder is named.
### LARGE +
Her friends doubt the dragon-tomb tale, so ownership rests on her word and on the weapon description rather than on witnessed events.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not in-world): a 4-star sword of the Heroes of Natlan series, base ATK 44 at level 1, secondary stat ATK%. Weapon Event Wishes from Version 5.1.
### LARGE +
Passive "Trapper's Pride": decreases Sprint or Alternate Sprint Stamina consumption by 15%; after sprinting, Normal Attack DMG is increased by a percentage of ATK (16% at refinement 1 up to 32% at refinement 5) until 18 triggers or 7s.

## roleplay
### LARGE
Natlanese storytellers may tell it as a boast: Wanjiru proved herself, though her friends teased her about the story.
