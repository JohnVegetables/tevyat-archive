## summary
### LITE
The Funerary Mask is a quest item the Traveler receives after defeating La Signora, Eighth of the Fatui Harbingers, in a duel before the Raiden Shogun's throne in Tenshukaku, Inazuma (Version 2.1). Its text calls it a trophy and uses it to reflect on why Fatui wear masks.
### STANDARD +
It is a memento: a keepsake of the duel with no use in play, tied to the end of Signora's story.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Canon gives no physical description of the mask. Its name suggests mourning, and its item text speaks of the faceless masks the Fatui present to the world.

## lore
@version: 2.1
### STANDARD
The Archive text first says it is a trophy for defeating Signora. It then says the Fatui often face the world from behind masks, and that this is said to be because everything they were counts for nothing once they become Fatui: they must give up their true names and share one face.

The text adds that giving up everything that makes up an "I", past and bonds included, is hard. It suggests those willing to join a group that rebels against the Heavenly Principles may have an obsession close to or beyond that of a Delusion holder, binding the rest of their days to the Delusion and burning like stars. It also notes that many Fatui are simply drunk on power or sadistic, and that the states are not mutually exclusive.
### LARGE +
The last paragraph turns to Signora herself, naming her as Rosalyne-Kruzchka Lohefalter, who may once have lived a more innocent, naive life. It says her eyes were filled with raging fire and her innocent days burned away, until an icy Delusion put out the flames that gnawed at her. The frozen power protecting her was then shattered by the Traveler, an "eternal outlander", whose determination matched hers, and the flames burst forth again. The text is written as a reflective narrator's voice rather than as a character's speech.
### ULTRA +
The item therefore ties together the Fatui practice of surrendering one's name, Signora's concealed past, and the cost of her Cryo Delusion. Her own biography is not told in the item and belongs in her entry.

## owners
@version: 2.1
### LARGE
Held by the Traveler. It came as a quest reward alongside experience, Primogems, Mora and materials.

## origin
@version: 2.1
### STANDARD
Obtained on completing Duel Before the Throne (Archon Quest, Chapter II, Act III), set in Tenshukaku in Inazuma City. After Kujou Sara stormed toward the throne, the Traveler found Signora already there before the Raiden Shogun and accused her of distributing Delusions and of the harm done in Liyue and to Venti's Gnosis. Signora answered as a mere Snezhnayan diplomat, mocking the Traveler's status as a wanted criminal. The Traveler then challenged her to a duel before the throne, which the Shogun allowed.
### LARGE +
Signora warned that the loser must die and accepted. During the fight she shed her Delusion's shield and emerged as the Crimson Witch of Embers. After the Traveler won, the rules of the duel left the loser to the Shogun: Signora threatened diplomatic consequences and then shouted orders for the Shogun and others to stop, but the Raiden Shogun executed her with the Musou no Hitotachi. The Shogun called her an enemy of eternity but, as the victor, let the Traveler leave Tenshukaku alive.

## gameplay
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
Gameplay note: a quest item with no function in play. The wiki lists it as a memento, so it can be viewed in the inventory but not used.

## roleplay
### LARGE
Roleplay use (project guidance, not canon): a good prop for scenes about Signora's fall, the Fatui's loss of self, or the Traveler reflecting on the duel. Do not give the mask powers, a known owner history, or a claim that it was physically worn by Signora; canon does not state these.

## status
### LITE
The Traveler holds it as of Version 7.1; nothing read says otherwise.
### STANDARD +
Signora died at the end of the duel; the mask is her only keepsake in the Traveler's possession.
