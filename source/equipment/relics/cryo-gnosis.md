## summary
### LITE
The Cryo Gnosis is the Gnosis of the Cryo Archon. It is held by Anastasya, the Tsaritsa of Snezhnaya, and is the only one of the seven Gnoses that never had to be collected by the Fatui. Like every Gnosis, it is a divine focus that resembles a chess piece and strengthens its bearer's elemental power. Each Archon's Gnosis also links them to Celestia.
### STANDARD +
As of Version 7.1, the Tsaritsa holds six Gnoses: her own Cryo Gnosis plus the Anemo, Geo, Electro, Dendro and Hydro Gnoses her Harbingers gathered. Only the Pyro Gnosis is missing. In 7.1 she revealed that all seven Gnoses were formed from the remains of the Third Descender. She wants them for two reasons: to amplify her power for a confrontation with the Heavenly Principles, and to open a seal the Third Descender left beneath Zapolyarny Palace.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Like every Gnosis it resembles a gleaming chess piece and normally rests within its Archon's chest. No canon text describes its individual shape or colour, so do not invent details.
### LARGE +
A Gnosis can be drawn out of an Archon by force or materialized at will.
In 7.1 the Tsaritsa's six Gnoses were shown outside her body, set as energy nodes marking the edges of a field she raised inside her palace. It is the first time the Cryo Gnosis is seen working alongside the others.

## lore
@version: 7.1
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
Origin: Saarelainen, the Third Descender, was Anastasya's lover and the ancestor of the Khaenri'ahns. After the Heavenly Principles erased her existence, he bargained with them for her resurrection and for the safety of their descendants. In exchange, he agreed to let his remains be split into seven pieces to consolidate the Heavenly Principles' rule. Those pieces are the Gnoses. The Cryo Gnosis held by the Cryo Archon is therefore part of the body of the man the Tsaritsa loved, though the Heavenly Principles erased him and every memory related to him from her mind (and, through Irminsul, from the world).
In Light and Shadow, Intertwined, the Tsaritsa tells the Traveler that the Gnoses have a powerful elemental affinity and amplify their bearers. Because they come from the Third Descender rather than from Teyvat's own system, she says, their full potential could let a god go beyond the limits of their authority. She calls this a necessary step toward facing the Heavenly Principles, since the power of The Seven alone "cannot shake the foundations of the world." She also says the Third Descender's seal beneath the palace responds only to a Gnosis. She guesses that it might open once all his scattered remains are gathered again.
### ULTRA +
The Tsaritsa reasons that the missing Pyro Gnosis, as a remnant of the Third Descender, cannot coexist with the Ley Lines for long and will eventually be ejected and found. Earlier, in Mistilteinn (7.0), The Jester (Pierro) described the Gnoses as a key bargaining chip for when she confronts the Heavenly Principles. In Delusion (7.1) he said she had already amassed enough power to strike at Teyvat's rules even without all seven. In Black Feathers Scattered at Parting, Ronova threatens the Traveler by pointing to the Third Descender's remains, "the playthings of the gods for centuries now", as a precedent.

## owners
### LITE
Current holder: Anastasya, the Tsaritsa and Cryo Archon, at Zapolyarny Palace in Snezhnograd.
### LARGE +
Canon implies that the seven Gnoses went to the original members of The Seven after the Archon War. The first Cryo Archon was the Belyi Tsar, who ruled Snezhnaya from about 2,000 to 500 years ago. Anastasya succeeded him when he died during the Cataclysm. Canon does not narrate how the Cryo Gnosis passed between them (see uncertainties).
### ULTRA +
The Tsaritsa added the other Gnoses to her own. Signora took the Anemo Gnosis from Venti by force and received the Geo Gnosis from Zhongli by contract. Dottore obtained the Electro and Dendro Gnoses from Nahida. Arlecchino brought the Hydro Gnosis, which Neuvillette gave her as a diplomatic gift. The Pyro Gnosis was never taken. It was lost when Irminsul burned; the Traveler told the Tsaritsa that, according to Mavuika, it has returned to the Ley Lines, and it has not been found.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay: the Cryo Gnosis is a story object only. Players cannot obtain or equip it, and it has no stats. It appears only in Archon Quest cutscenes and dialogue.

## roleplay
### LARGE
Roleplay guidance (this project's recommendation, not canon text): treat the Cryo Gnosis as a state secret and a divine organ, not a trinket. Ordinary Snezhnayans and rank-and-file Fatui would at most know that the Tsaritsa is gathering Gnoses. Very few people know that the Gnoses are a dead Descender's remains. Do not show the Gnosis being lent, stolen or casually displayed as of 7.1.
### ULTRA +
If a scene needs the Tsaritsa's full power, remember her own words: the Gnoses are only one part of it, and her own insight over a thousand years matters too. An Archon also keeps elemental power without a Gnosis, so taking it away would not leave her powerless.

## entry: Six-Gnosis time field
@keys: six Gnoses, Gnosis time field
@keys2: Tsaritsa, Anastasya, Rhinedottir, Cryo Archon
@version: 7.1
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
In Black Feathers Scattered at Parting (7.1), the Tsaritsa hosted Rhinedottir for tea while the Traveler, Dainsleif and Pierro fought Ronova. Unnoticed, she had placed her six Gnoses, the Cryo Gnosis among them, as nodes around the hall. Using Cryo amplified to its limit, she bent "the bounds of Reason" enough to slow time inside the room, though not to freeze it. Rhinedottir missed the window she and Ronova had arranged to undo Ronova's curse of death without the Heavenly Principles' monitoring noticing. Celestia then reclaimed Ronova's authority for abuse. Rhinedottir called this power beyond what a god should have. The Tsaritsa answered that the Gnoses were only one aspect, and that she knew the trick would not work on Rhinedottir twice.

## entry: Third Descender's seal and the Gnoses
@keys: Third Descender's seal, seal of the Third Descender
@keys2: Gnosis, Gnoses, Cryo Gnosis
@version: 7.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Beneath Zapolyarny Palace lies a large crystalline seal left by the Third Descender before the Heavenly Principles killed him. The Heavenly Principles seem never to have noticed it. The Belyi Tsar guarded it first, and the Tsaritsa inherited the duty. Every attempt to open it has failed, and only a Gnosis draws any response from it. The Tsaritsa believes all seven Gnoses, his reunited remains, are needed. She showed it to the Traveler in Light and Shadow, Intertwined. As of 7.1 it remains sealed because the Pyro Gnosis is missing.
