## summary
### LITE
The Dull Golden Bridle is a quest item found in Enkanomiya's library ruin during the world quest The Subterranean Trials of Drake and Serpent. Its description calls it the hereditary national treasure of Byakuyakoku, drained of all power.
### STANDARD +
The power was removed deliberately, so that thieves who entered by irregular means would not see its value. It is the disguised form of the Golden Bridle that the quest needs.

## appearance
### STANDARD
No in-world description of its look is given. Enjou, the guide, says the Golden Bridle is a device smaller than an armful and not a rope; the name comes from a Byakuyakoku legend in which the sun and moon were moved by the chariots of the gods.

## lore
@version: 2.4
### STANDARD
The item text says it is Byakuyakoku's hereditary national treasure and has lost all its own power, a measure meant to stop intruders from recognising its worth. The full Golden Bridle, per its own item text, represents the highest authority in Byakuyakoku and lets its wielder control Evernight and Whitenight.

In the quest Enjou, a self-described researcher, explains that the people of Byakuyakoku, saved by the Great Serpent, left the depths for the surface, and that the library was one of the facilities the people of Watatsumi archived afterwards. To ease later trials the Bridle was moved to the back of the library. Finding it is the first part of the trial and is needed to bring down the barrier around the Dainichi Mikoshi.
### LARGE +
Enjou also notes that ordinary intruders could not get far: the trial does not activate without the Key of the Moon-Bathed Deep, and they could not read Byakuyakoku's script. Later the Bridle serves as the core of the Reins, to which three other fragments must be fitted. Completing the quest gives the ability to switch freely between Whitenight and Evernight.

### ULTRA +
The quest belongs to the series From Dusk to Dawn in Byakuyakoku and was commissioned by Tsuyuko of Sangonomiya Shrine, who wanted the Bloodbranch Coral for Watatsumi Island. After the Bridle is handed over in part one, three side quests (The Trail of Drake and Serpent, The Heart of Ouroboros, Dreams in the Gaps) gather three fragments, which Aru says must be pieced with the Bridle as the core of the Reins. No later content through 7.1 was found to give the object new significance.

## owners
@version: 2.4
### LARGE
No named owner. It is held by the Traveler during the quest and handed to Enjou to disable the barrier. Its nominal owner is the nation of Byakuyakoku.

## gameplay
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
Game mechanics only: a quest item with no combat use. The quest skips a step if the Dull Golden Bridle was picked up before the Traveler met Enjou.

## entry: Dull Golden Bridle
@keys: Dull Golden Bridle, Golden Bridle
@version: 2.4
### STANDARD
Byakuyakoku's hereditary treasure, found in a drained, unremarkable form in the library of Byakuyakoku so thieves would not value it. It is used in The Subterranean Trials of Drake and Serpent to open the way to the Dainichi Mikoshi, and is tied to control of Evernight and Whitenight.
