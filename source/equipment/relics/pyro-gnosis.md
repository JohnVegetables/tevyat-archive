## summary
### LITE
The Pyro Gnosis is the Gnosis of Natlan's Pyro Archon, Mavuika, and the only one of the seven the Fatui never obtained. Capitano fought Mavuika for it and failed. In 6.6 she gave it away so Nahida could burn the corrupted Irminsul, and it vanished in the fire. As of 7.1 nobody knows where it is.
### STANDARD +
It also holds a terrible emergency function: it could drain every soul and memory in Natlan's Night Kingdom into a net shielding the Ley Lines from the Abyss, erasing Natlan's history. Mavuika always refused to use it.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Like every Gnosis, a small, gleaming chess-piece-like object carried inside its Archon's chest. Holding it, the Traveler found it strangely ordinary and not heavy. Canon gives no description of its specific design.

## lore
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Mavuika revealed the secret in 5.1: the Pyro Archons kept it as a worst-case contingency against the Abyss. The net would cost the Night Kingdom's dead, memories and legends, and could still corrode if the Ley Lines were not repaired.
### LARGE +
Capitano learned the secret from his dying friend Ayizu, a former chief of the Masters of the Night-Wind, who had urged Mavuika to use it several times; she refused each time. Capitano condemned her for holding a solution she would not use.

## history
### LARGE
- 5.0: Capitano storms the Stadium of the Sacred Flame for the Gnosis; Mavuika wounds him and he escapes.
- 5.1: Mavuika reveals its function; when the Abyss puts out the Sacred Flame, she uses the Gnosis to reignite it and hold the incursion back.
- 5.3: Mavuika learns from a letter Capitano left behind that, after their duel, he had asked the Tsaritsa to release him from the Gnosis mission, and she had agreed.
- 6.6: Mavuika gives it to the Traveler for Nahida; it sinks into the Traveler "just like phlogiston", Nahida draws it out inside Irminsul, and it becomes the fire that burns Irminsul for seven days and nights. No trace of it, not even ash, is found afterwards.
### ULTRA +
Nahida first meant only to borrow it and hoped to return it; Mavuika insisted it was a gift with no "returning". She said an Archon is not left powerless without a Gnosis, called a Gnosis "basically an Elemental energy enhancer" that she did not usually use, and said Irminsul matters to all of Teyvat. Afterwards she said she had sensed its loss and wondered whether it was a knock-on effect of Irminsul's loss on a strand of fate.

## owners
### STANDARD
Mavuika until 6.6; then briefly the Traveler and Nahida. No known holder since. Capitano sought it for the Tsaritsa but never held it.

## status
@version: 7.1
@spoiler: major
### LITE
Missing as of 7.1; the only Gnosis the Tsaritsa lacks.
### STANDARD
Missing as of 7.1; the Tsaritsa holds the other six. In 7.1 the Traveler told her it burned with Irminsul and, per Mavuika, returned to the Ley Lines. She replied that remains of the Third Descender cannot coexist with the Ley Lines for long and expects it to resurface.
### LARGE +
Its absence matters to her: she collects the Gnoses to fight the Heavenly Principles and to open the Third Descender's seal. The Traveler's account differs from the 6.6 dialogue (no ash found; Mavuika only sensed its loss), so its fate is open.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay note: a story object in Archon Quest cutscenes and dialogue only; not an obtainable item.

## roleplay
### LARGE
Roleplay interpretation (this project's recommendation, not canon text): after 6.6 nobody holds it. Mavuika keeps her full power without it. Its soul-net function was a closely kept secret. Do not invent its reappearance, a new holder or a confirmed destruction.

## entry: The Night Kingdom soul-net
@keys: soul net, net of souls, Gnosis contingency, Natlan backup plan
@keys2: Gnosis, Pyro Gnosis, Mavuika, Night Kingdom
@version: 5.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Natlan's secret last resort, revealed by Mavuika in The Flickering Light Splits in Twain (5.1): the Pyro Gnosis can drain every soul, memory and legend in the Night Kingdom and weave them into a giant net around Natlan's Ley Lines to hold back Abyssal corrosion. The cost is the erasure of Natlan's history and culture, and the net can still corrode unless the damaged Ley Lines are repaired in time. Mavuika said the Pyro Archons had to plan for the worst case given the Abyss, but she cared about how Natlan won and refused to use it except as a last resort.
### LARGE +
Capitano knew because Ayizu, chief of the Masters of the Night-Wind, who befriended him after he fled the fall of Khaenri'ah, told him as he died. Mavuika confirmed that Ayizu had recommended using the Gnosis several times, even before the tragedy five centuries ago. Capitano told her the Ley Lines had long been destined for ruin, that an oath made five centuries ago remained unfulfilled, and that she had not kept her promise. Unable to get the Gnosis, he had his men, helped by Ororon, search Natlan for another option; it led them to the Source Mechanism and a plan to reconstruct the Ley Lines themselves rather than weave a net around them.

## entry: Capitano's duel for the Gnosis
@keys: duel at the Stadium, Capitano's duel
@keys2: Gnosis, Pyro Gnosis, Mavuika
@version: 5.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
In Chapter V Act II (5.0), Capitano led the Fatui into the Stadium of the Sacred Flame to seize the Gnosis and fought Mavuika, who wounded him with one full-power strike before Night-Wind smoke carried him off. When a subordinate later suggested taking it from the weakened Archon, he refused, counting the duel as his defeat. He later said Mavuika had held back to protect the spectators, and she called them evenly matched. He said he wanted the Gnosis chiefly to save Natlan and would only have taken it to Snezhnaya afterwards. After their duel he asked the Tsaritsa to release him from the mission and she agreed (Mavuika reported this in 5.3 from a letter he left behind); Mavuika doubted the Tsaritsa would let the matter rest, since it was the last Gnosis the Fatui lacked.

## entry: The Pyro Gnosis and Irminsul
@keys: burning of Irminsul, Irminsul burned, Irminsul fire
@keys2: Gnosis, Pyro Gnosis
@version: 6.6
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
In A Tower Unto Wisdom and Folly (6.6), Nahida asked Mavuika to give the Pyro Gnosis to Sumeru, and Mavuika agreed at once, saying she had long toyed with giving it to someone who needed it more. She tossed it to the Traveler outside Irminsul's barrier. Thinking hard on their memories of Pyro and Natlan, the Traveler felt it sink into their body like phlogiston; Mavuika guessed it resonated with them because they had mastered phlogiston. In Truth Amongst the Pages of Purana, during the fight with Dottore inside Irminsul, Nahida drew it out of the Traveler and it vanished into a surge of Pyro that set Irminsul's roots ablaze, while Mavuika's voice spoke of the first, purifying flame. Per Nahida the fire burned seven days and nights, leaving only the underground parts; her connection moved to Aaru. Nahida and Mavuika found no trace of the Gnosis afterwards.

## entry: The Tsaritsa and the missing Pyro Gnosis
@keys: missing Pyro Gnosis, lost Pyro Gnosis, seventh Gnosis
@version: 7.1
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
In Light and Shadow, Intertwined (7.1), the Tsaritsa's one question to the Traveler was where the Pyro Gnosis is, as they were the last to see it. The Traveler said it was reduced to ashes with Irminsul and, according to Mavuika, returned to the Ley Lines. She said she did not doubt their honesty but was surprised: formed from the Third Descender's remains, it should not coexist with the Ley Lines for long and will one day be excised, so the Fatui will find it eventually. Paimon noted that without it she cannot yet open the Third Descender's seal.
