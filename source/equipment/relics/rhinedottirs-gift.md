## summary
### LITE
Rhinedottir's "Gift" is a quest item from the world quest Old Friends and New Knowledge (Version 6.2). It is the present that Rhinedottir sent to her creation Albedo through her cat Sebas, and it carries a short riddle about a tree, its perfect fruit and a seed of dreams. Albedo's only response is that he needs to think.
### STANDARD +
It follows the completion of the final task Rhinedottir set Albedo, and it works as the start of a new problem rather than a reward in the usual sense: the item's own description calls it a new gift and a new quest.
### LARGE +
The quest belongs to the Witch's Homework line in Dragonspine, set after Albedo has finished his master's last assignment and is finding idle time unfamiliar.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Canon does not describe the object. Sebas only says the gift is on the table at Albedo's campsite. What the item shows is its text: two lines in the voice of Rhinedottir.
### LARGE +
The wiki lists it as a 4-star Quest Item.

## lore
### LITE
Albedo's master Rhinedottir sends the gift via Sebas, her old cat-butler, together with a spoken message. The message says that once a problem is solved, dwelling on it loses its point, and urges Albedo to pose questions of his own instead of resting on answers. The gift's text then compares a tree proud of its most perfect fruit with a "seed of dreams" that refuses to be only the king of a universe confined to its flesh.
### STANDARD +
Rhinedottir also teases that Albedo may be bored, asks him to bring questions she has never considered, and warns him not to build a "self" whose only purpose is asking questions, since she tried it and it failed. She adds that the gift should be kept well and that Sebas need not be fed.
### LARGE +
Albedo calls Sebas a kind of family member, created by Rhinedottir long before himself, and says Sebas used to help him tidy vials and jars and sometimes modelled for his sketches. Sebas, for his part, says he runs Rhinedottir's large house as its butler. After the riddle is read, Albedo says he needs to think, and his post-quest line is simply "New questions...".
### ULTRA +
Sebas also mentions he has another message to deliver in Mondstadt to someone whose "birth" was recent and whose task Rhinedottir foresaw (a player who has completed A Child Wandering the World hears him refer to Durin instead). The riddle is never explained in the quest, so its link to Albedo's nature is left to the player.

## owners
### STANDARD
Albedo, to whom it is addressed and delivered. The Traveler receives it as a quest reward in the game's mechanics, but within the story it is Albedo's.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay note: a 4-star Quest Item received on completing Old Friends and New Knowledge. The quest also unlocks Albedo's Book of Blinding Light talent. It has no combat use.
