## summary
### LITE
The Great Songs of Khvarena are five large concentrations of Khvarena, the purifying power left by the divine bird Simurgh. The Pari elder Zurvan says she fused them long ago to cleanse the land of impurity. They hold immense power, but only a Pari who has earned the Khvarena's approval can unleash it, through a special ritual. In Vourukasha Oasis's quest series, Sorush the Pari and the Traveler gather all five, and Nasejuna, leader of the Order of Skeptics, is revealed to have been using them for his own plot.
### STANDARD +
They are needed to perform the Rite of Chinvat and open a path to the Harvisptokhm, so the Sign of Apaosha in the sky can be extinguished. Whoever holds them can seal or open lands: Zurvan once used them to seal Vourukasha Oasis and the nearby ruins against the Abyss Order.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Paimon describes them as much bigger than ordinary Khvarena motes, and "kinda cute" when one rises out of Sunyata Lake; she jokingly calls it "Great Khvarena". The Khvarena's power forms a barrier around each one.

## lore
### LITE
Zurvan says that after she scattered the monsters that invaded the sea of flowers, she fused the strongest Khvarena gathered into five Great Songs to purify the land. She hid one in Sunyata Lake for a time of need, and the rest were scattered. A Pari needs the Twin-Horned Chaplet, the symbol of the Khvarena's recognition, to touch them. Sorush earns it by being acknowledged as a Bloomguard.
### LARGE +
Collection (As the Khvarena's Light Shows). The first Song is raised from Sunyata Lake by Sorush. Nasejuna claims to know the other four sites. Three are found through the sub-quests As the Khvarena's Light Shows: Samudaya, Dukkha and Nirodha; in Samudaya a Song is found after lowering water levels with control devices, and in Dukkha the Song's effect on a petrified Setekh Wenut is weak, which Nasejuna says shows it affects only things tied to the Abyss. The final one lies deep in an underground factory of the Dahri (the name Nasejuna uses for Khaenri'ahn) once owned by the Schwanenritter, reached by unsealing relays with Farrwicks and riding an elevator.

Nasejuna's betrayal. At the bottom he demands the Songs, then drops his pose of reverence for Sorush, reveals that his plan is to use them to reverse the Rite of Chinvat and release the seal on the Abyss, and summons the Abyss Herald Klingsor. The Traveler defeats them, but Nasejuna slips away. Pyrrho, another Skeptic, only believes the news when shown Nasejuna's handwritten notes.

Use (The Hymn of Tir Yazad, Part 1). Zurvan explains that the Songs, placed at trees around the Tunigi Hollow that were originally cultivated with Khvarena, would restore their power and let the Pari Mihir and Rashnu lift the seal. Sorush then purifies the trees, and the cleansed Hollow opens the way to the Harvisptokhm for the Rite of Ab-Zohr.

Zurvan tells the Traveler that Klingsor was once of the Order of Skeptics: several hundred years ago he accompanied an investigation team into Dahrian ruins and returned changed, having taken the defilement's blessing, while the team was gone. Followers of the dark hollow then summoned demons to attack Vourukasha Oasis in pursuit of the Songs. They were beaten back with the help of many Pari and human warriors but not wiped out, and Zurvan sealed the Oasis and many ruins with the Songs' power. The Order lost its ancient archives to fire and declined afterwards.

### ULTRA +
Rene's Investigation Notes (the fragments Nasejuna gives Sorush) record a researcher's view that Khvarena resembles the Abyss in assimilating whatever it touches while being opposed to it. They also describe a Dahri site producing a block of energy called Azosite. The notes mention children named Jakob and Karl and a return journey to Fontaine; the archive does not interpret them beyond what the text shows. They are not about the Songs themselves but guided Nasejuna's search for Farrwicks.

## owners
### STANDARD
Fused by Zurvan (or by the first Pari, per the tutorial text), who hid one in Sunyata Lake. In the quests only Sorush, who has the Twin-Horned Chaplet, can pick them up. Nasejuna, with Klingsor and the Abyss Order, tried to take them; the Traveler and Sorush kept them and placed them at the Tunigi Hollow.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Quest items. The tutorial text says the Khvarena's power forms a barrier around each, that only a Pari with the Twin-Horned Chaplet can touch them, and that Sorush, with the Chaplet, may use her power to collect them. The Songs from Samudaya and Dukkha appear in inventory only once those sub-quests are started.

## entry: The Rite of Chinvat
@keys: Rite of Chinvat, Chinvat
@keys2: Great Song, Khvarena, Sorush, Nasejuna
@version: 3.6
### STANDARD
Nasejuna says the Rite of Chinvat requires collecting the Great Songs so the Sign of Apaosha can be extinguished; Zurvan confirms the rite is real and that she once performed it alongside human heroes to cross the darkness and cleanse the mother-tree. Nasejuna's actual plan is to reverse it with the Songs and release the Abyss. Zurvan says it opens the path into the Harvisptokhm, where the separate Rite of Ab-Zohr then cleanses the tree.

## entry: Nasejuna's Betrayal
@keys: Nasejuna's betrayal, Vijnanapati
@keys2: Great Song, Order of Skeptics, Sorush
@version: 3.6
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
Nasejuna, whom the Skeptics call Vijnanapati, flattered Sorush as the destined Envoy while quietly guiding the party to the Songs. Paimon grew suspicious of his familiarity with the factory's machines. He claims to have read lost documents and to have entered the ruins in secret with a lector. When the last Song was found he revealed that he had never intended to help, insulted Sorush, and summoned Klingsor. The Traveler defeated them, and Nasejuna slipped away. His own account of the Sign of Apaosha is that Dahri records call it the Sign of Truth, reflecting the reality beyond the sky; the quests do not confirm this.
