## summary
### LITE
The Geo Gnosis was the Gnosis of Morax, the Geo Archon, who now lives in Liyue as Zhongli. A Gnosis is a gleaming, chess-piece-like focus that an Archon carries in their chest. Zhongli gave his to the Fatui Harbinger Signora in Liyue Harbor (1.1), fulfilling a secret contract with the Tsaritsa that he called his "contract to end all contracts".
### STANDARD +
It was the second Gnosis the Fatui obtained, after the Anemo Gnosis, and the first one given up willingly. Canon has not revealed what the Tsaritsa gave in return.

## lore
### STANDARD
Zhongli staged his own death at the Rite of Descension to test whether Liyue could thrive without its god. Meanwhile Childe (Tartaglia), kept in the dark, tried to seize the Gnosis and finally freed the ancient god Osial. The Qixing, the adepti and the Traveler stopped Osial together. At the Northland Bank, Signora asked Morax to honour the agreement. Zhongli materialised the Gnosis and gave it to her, saying his promise was "solid as stone" and that the matter lay "solely between the Tsaritsa and I".
### LARGE +
Zhongli said he had kept the Gnosis as a fallback: had the chaos gone too far, he could have returned and used his divine power to restore order. Once Liyue proved itself, the Gnosis he had kept for so many years "suddenly seemed to have lost its meaning". At his request Signora had kept the plan secret, even from Childe, who complained that the two had deceived him. She left for Zapolyarny Palace with the Gnosis. His character story asks what the Cryo Archon could have wagered to balance a loss so great. His ascension voice line later recalls "letting go" of it.

## consequences
@version: 1.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The Golden House, Teyvat's only Mora mint, stopped working because minting needs the Geo Archon's power; existing Mora did not vanish.
### LARGE +
Zhongli later told the ancient dragon Azhdaha, "I am no longer the Geo Archon", and Azhdaha said he could sense it. Elsewhere canon shows an Archon's divine throne does not depend on holding a Gnosis, so the link stays unclear.

## owners
### LITE
Morax (Zhongli), then Signora, then the Tsaritsa (Anastasya), who holds it as of 7.1.

## status
@version: 7.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
As of 7.1 the Tsaritsa holds six Gnoses, the Geo Gnosis among them; only the Pyro Gnosis is missing. She collects them to amplify her power against the Heavenly Principles and to open a seal the Third Descender left beneath Zapolyarny Palace. In 7.1 she used all six to slow time around Rhinedottir.

## origin
@version: 7.1
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
Like every Gnosis it is made from the remains of the Third Descender, Saarelainen (hinted in 4.2, told in full in 7.1). Whether Morax knew this is not stated.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay note: a story object only, never an obtainable item. Zhongli's playable Geo powers are not tied to it.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay guidance (project recommendation, not canon): keep the Geo Gnosis with the Tsaritsa after 7.1. Zhongli remains powerful without it, declines to discuss the contract's terms and does not voice regret. Ordinary Liyue citizens believe Rex Lapis died and know nothing of the trade. Never invent the Tsaritsa's side of the bargain.

## entry: Contract to End All Contracts
@keys: contract to end all contracts, Zhongli's contract with the Tsaritsa, Morax's contract with the Tsaritsa
@version: 1.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Zhongli's final contract as Geo Archon was made with the Cryo Archon, the Tsaritsa. He would surrender the Geo Gnosis, and Signora would collect it as her emissary. Asked what could be worth a Gnosis, Zhongli said that realistically nothing was. Even so, as God of Contracts he would never agree to a deal that brought him no benefit. One clause forbids him to reveal the contract's terms. He told the Traveler to discover the Tsaritsa's "bargaining chip" on their own journey. As of 7.1 it remains unknown.

## entry: Childe's search of the Exuvia
@keys: Heart of Glaze, Exuvia Gnosis
@keys2: Childe, Tartaglia, Golden House
@version: 1.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
In Heart of Glaze (1.1), Childe used the Traveler's work on the Rite of Parting to learn where the Qixing had hidden the Exuvia, the god's shed body. At the Golden House, where the Millelith guards had been knocked unconscious, he reached into the Exuvia for Morax's Gnosis, but came out empty-handed. He fought the Traveler, believing they had taken it, then realised they had not. He guessed that the Exuvia was a diversion and freed Osial with a swarm of Sigils of Permission to force the god to reveal himself. The handover itself later took place at the Northland Bank, not the Golden House.

## entry: Signora and the Geo Gnosis
@keys: Signora Geo Gnosis
@keys2: Signora, La Signora, Fair Lady
@version: 1.1
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
Signora was the Tsaritsa's liaison to Zhongli in Liyue. She knew of the contract and kept it secret at his request. When she claimed the Gnosis, she taunted Childe that the pressure he created was what turned Liyue into "a dazzling diamond of a city". Childe refused to share a boat home with her, and she left alone for Zapolyarny Palace with the Gnosis. The Fatui's Ninth Company had been working in the Chasm under a separate deal with the Qixing, unaware of the Gnosis mission. The Qixing cancelled that deal after Osial's release. Signora died in Inazuma in 2.1, after the Gnosis was already in Snezhnaya.

## entry: The six Gnoses in Zapolyarny Palace
@keys: six Gnoses, Tsaritsa's Gnoses
@version: 7.1
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
In Black Feathers Scattered at Parting (7.1), the Tsaritsa placed her six Gnoses, the Geo Gnosis among them, as energy nodes around a hall of her palace. Her Cryo power, amplified by them, slowed time inside the hall. This stalled Rhinedottir until the resurrection window she had agreed with Ronova, meant to bypass the Heavenly Principles' monitoring, had passed, and Celestia then reclaimed Ronova's authority. Rhinedottir called this power beyond what a god should have; the Tsaritsa credited her own insight too. She had earlier told the Traveler that the Gnoses come from outside Teyvat's system and can let a bearer go beyond the limits of their authority, and that only a Gnosis responds to the Third Descender's seal.

## entry: Gnoses as chess pieces
@keys: A Winter Night's Lazzo, Sudden Snow, Gnosis chessboard
@spoiler: minor
### ULTRA
In the official teaser A Winter Night's Lazzo (2022), the Harbingers gather to mourn Signora. Before the ceremony the Tsaritsa plays chess with Pierro, and the Anemo and Geo Gnoses, the only two collected so far, sit on the board as pieces. The 2026 trailer Sudden Snow shows the Tsaritsa playing chess with Rhinedottir, with Gnoses among the pieces. Fans read the Geo Gnosis as a rook, but canon assigns no roles.

## entry: Snezhnaya Awaits recap
@keys: Snezhnaya Awaits
@availability: limited
@spoiler: minor
### ULTRA
The limited 2026 web event Snezhnaya Awaits, a recap before the Snezhnaya chapter, showed each collected Gnosis with a line from its handover. The Geo Gnosis appeared with Zhongli's words about the contract being fulfilled, from A New Star Approaches.
