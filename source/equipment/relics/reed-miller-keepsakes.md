## summary
### LITE
The Proofs of the Cognoscenti are five quest-item relics in Nod-Krai, each "said to be a keepsake from Reed Miller", founder of the Treasure Hoarders: a silver mask, a black feather, a stopped timepiece, a goblet and a rusted flower. Legend says they point to his Great Treasure. The Traveler collects all five in East of the Moon, West of the Sun.
### STANDARD +
"Reed Miller" was an alias worn by at least three people, so the legend the keepsakes carry is deliberately tangled. The collective name used here is this archive's label, not an in-game title.

## appearance
### STANDARD
- Stratagema-Cognoscenti's Mask: a cold, plain silver mask with no decoration but the frosty moonlight on its surface.
- Inscripta-Cognoscenti's Feather: a feather as dark as night.
- Devocina-Cognoscenti's Sorrow: a timepiece that stopped long ago.
- Weavenexa-Cognoscenti's Intoxication: a lavishly decorated goblet.
- Memorata-Cognoscenti's Jest: an artificial flower stained with rust.

## lore
### LITE
Folk retellings of the master thief, mixing legend and rumour, not eyewitness history.
### LARGE +
- The Feather recalls the old order in Snezhnaya, where nobles ruled under the Tsar, and how the master thief raised a black raven banner and called the poor and hungry to build an "Elysium" from the wealth of the high and haughty.
- The Mask describes a thief who wore a silver mask after surviving a failed execution, then took it off after the Cataclysm; rumour offers several reasons for the mask (hiding from searches, covering the executioner's marks, weariness of the crowd's love and hate). It ends with moonlight pouring from a high tower and destroying the silver and the resolve in "her" eyes.
- The Goblet tells of the thief after the mask came off: assuming many identities and soothing the people with song and wine, with a legend that grew until singer and subject blurred.
- The Timepiece (Sorrow) is a grieving, first-person meditation on fallen dreams: a prophesied ruler who never ruled, a would-be heir who could not break her bonds, a maid left with nothing, and an adopted son who failed to save the one he loved and took a commoner's forgotten name. It admits the tokens are the author's counterfeits that can still lead the finder to "our great treasure".
- The Flower records the master thief's most famous tricks (a fake medallion to empty a governor's palace, posing as the Tsar's envoy to drain a viscount's treasury), his promise to a partner named Alia not to take risks, and the execution that ended the first thief, followed by the woman who took his name and hid behind a mask. Its closing words are a defiant claim that "a murder of crows" would rise.
### ULTRA +
Read together with the Reed Miller lore page, the texts line up with three bearers of the alias. Avrelian, the original, founded the Treasure Hoarders and was executed after turning himself in. Alia, who had followed him, took the name and led the Hoarders, stealing Grand Duke Prokhor Trubetskoy's treasures in one night. Brahe, Trubetskoy's adopted son who loved her, took the name afterwards and spread contradictory legends so the name could not be used to justify harm. Tolfin, a character in the quests, estimates that about 20% of the legend comes from Alia, 30% from Brahe and the rest is invention.

The Flower names Solovei as the master thief's best friend, who regretted the execution. The Timepiece's lines on a "maid" singing beneath the nightingale's tower and a woman who "heralds the new moon" connect to Aila and the Frostmoon Scions only through the wiki's link annotations; the text itself names no one, so treat these as readings, not statements.

## owners_and_finding
### STANDARD
All five end up with the Traveler, one per quest in East of the Moon, West of the Sun. The first four can be done in any order; the Flower unlocks only after the other four.
### LARGE +
- Feather (Whisper Beneath the Waves): hidden in an Exquisite Chest beneath the Eye of Kratti, a whirlpool known for wrecking ships. Captain Grettir of the Atlas and others failed to get it and met misfortune; the Traveler restores the roots of a strange tree that grew there and reveals the chest.
- Mask (The Tale-Telling Heart): found in a treasure trove at Blue Amber Lake, kept by Papa Wrench (the new identity of the missing Mr. Shesternin). After Papa Wrench's death, Tolfin inherited it and used the Proof as bait to test Papa Wrench's son-in-law Zhorik; Zhorik overlooked the mask, and the Traveler took it.
- Goblet (The Shoemaker's Children Go Barefoot): Poruchik Raskolnikov of the Fatui's Kuuvahki Experimental Design Bureau had recovered it for Snezhnaya, but Omesov's drunken announcement in Nasha Town drew treasure-seekers into the Bureau. Raskolnikov "loses" it to the Traveler and Netochka, declaring the Fatui would spend no more resources on Proofs, and Netochka lets the Traveler keep it.
- Timepiece (Return to Sender): found in an abandoned bunker in Wavechaser Plain built against the "apocalypse" (the Abyss); an Abyssal entity inside calling itself the Ultimate Calamity believes the apocalypse still rages. The Timepiece had been left by Miller for Grazina.
- Flower (The Raven's Legacy): held by Hiljetta, Tolfin's grandmother, until a worthy successor appeared; she tests the Traveler in a friendly spar and hands it over, though she is displeased at Tolfin's withheld truth.
### ULTRA +
Before the Traveler reached Nod-Krai, the Fatui were already seeking the Proofs, which is why the Kuuvahki Bureau held the Goblet. Netochka fancies herself the next Reed Miller and gives the Traveler and Paimon roles from the legends, showing how the name has become a costume anyone can wear.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (labelled): the five are quest items with no combat or stat use; the wiki styles them as flower, plume, sands, goblet and circlet pieces only for presentation.

## entry: Proof of the Cognoscenti
@keys: Proof of the Cognoscenti, Proofs of the Cognoscenti, Cognoscenti keepsakes
@version: 6.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Five relics said to be keepsakes of Reed Miller, the Treasure Hoarders' founder, that legend claims point toward Reed Miller's Great Treasure: Memorata-Cognoscenti's Jest (flower), Inscripta-Cognoscenti's Feather (plume), Devocina-Cognoscenti's Sorrow (sands), Weavenexa-Cognoscenti's Intoxication (goblet) and Stratagema-Cognoscenti's Mask (circlet). The Traveler gathers them in East of the Moon, West of the Sun. Their texts retell the Reed Miller legend, which canon shows was shaped by several people using one alias, and one text calls the tokens counterfeits.
### LARGE +
Whether any of them is a real heirloom is left open. Do not have a character state that the Proofs are certainly authentic, or certainly fake.

## entry: Stratagema-Cognoscenti's Mask
@keys: Stratagema-Cognoscenti's Mask, Stratagema Cognoscenti, silver mask of the master thief
@version: 6.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
A plain silver mask under frost-pale light. Its item text says the master thief wore it after surviving a failed execution, then removed it for good after the Cataclysm; rumours say he wore it to hide from the searches in Snezhnaya, to cover executioner's marks, or from weariness of the crowd. The thief scorned both gods and nobles. The last line, which switches to "her" and moonlight from a high tower, suggests the wearer at the end was a woman (the wiki reads this as Alia), but the text does not say so directly. Obtained in The Tale-Telling Heart.

## entry: Inscripta-Cognoscenti's Feather
@keys: Inscripta-Cognoscenti's Feather, Inscripta Cognoscenti, black raven feather Proof
@version: 6.0
### STANDARD
A night-black feather whose text recalls Snezhnaya before the Cataclysm: nobles ruled for the Tsar, the poor were told the nobles bore "the sin of fullness" on their behalf, and the master thief raised a black raven emblem to gather the despised and promise an Elysium built on the wealth of the haughty. It is found beneath the Eye of Kratti in Whisper Beneath the Waves.

## entry: Devocina-Cognoscenti's Sorrow
@keys: Devocina-Cognoscenti's Sorrow, Devocina Cognoscenti
@version: 6.3
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
A timepiece that stopped long ago, left by Miller for Grazina in the bunker in Wavechaser Plain. Its text mourns the failed dreams of a prophesied ruler, a would-be heir, a maid and an adopted son, and admits the tokens are counterfeit work that can nevertheless guide a finder to the great treasure. Obtained in Return to Sender (listed as added in 6.3).

## entry: Weavenexa-Cognoscenti's Intoxication
@keys: Weavenexa-Cognoscenti's Intoxication, Weavenexa Cognoscenti
@version: 6.0
### STANDARD
A lavishly adorned goblet. Its text describes the master thief after the mask: no longer chasing fallen nobles' wealth, taking many identities and spreading song and wine among both nobles and commoners until the thief and the legend blurred. The Fatui held it first; the Traveler gets it in The Shoemaker's Children Go Barefoot.

## entry: Memorata-Cognoscenti's Jest
@keys: Memorata-Cognoscenti's Jest, Memorata Cognoscenti
@version: 6.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
A rusty artificial flower. Its text collects legendary tricks played on the powerful, notes that the first master thief "never lied" and promised his partner Alia he would not take risks, and ends with the execution and the woman who then took his name. It is the last Proof, given by Hiljetta in The Raven's Legacy.
