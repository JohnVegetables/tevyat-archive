## summary
### LITE
The Anemo Gnosis is the Gnosis of Barbatos (Venti), the Anemo Archon: a gleaming, chess-piece-like divine focus that sat inside his chest and, in his words, resonates directly with Celestia. At the end of the Prologue (1.0) the Fatui Harbinger Signora ambushed Venti in Mondstadt and tore it out. As of 7.1 it is one of six Gnoses held by the Tsaritsa in Snezhnaya.
### STANDARD +
Losing it did not end Venti's divinity: canon shows that Archons keep their divine thrones and elemental power without a Gnosis. During the limited-time 6.4 event Homeward, He Who Caught the Wind, Venti said it had been of no use to him. Few people know of the theft; he called Gnoses something he was not meant to discuss with ordinary people.

## owners
### STANDARD
Chain of possession: Barbatos/Venti (its Archon until 1.0) -> Signora, who took it for the Tsaritsa -> the Tsaritsa (Anastasya), who still holds it. It was the first Gnosis the Fatui seized; the Tsaritsa already had her own Cryo Gnosis. Canon implies the Gnoses went to the seven victors of the Archon War, but never shows how Barbatos, who became Archon after Decarabian's fall about 2,600 years ago, came to hold his. Oddly, after the theft Venti's in-game profile changes from "Vision: Anemo" to "Gnosis: Anemo".

## lore
### LARGE
The theft, in Ending Note (Prologue Act III, 1.0): just after the Stormterror crisis, Venti, the Traveler and Paimon left the Favonius Cathedral, where Venti had used a trick to make the broken Holy Lyre der Himmel look repaired. Fatui Pyro Agents held down the Traveler while Signora froze Venti's legs and blew Paimon away. She mocked him as Mondstadt's absent, powerless god, slapped him when he answered back, pulled the Gnosis from his chest and ordered that nothing be left for the Knights to find. Barbara found them unconscious; her healing barely affected Venti, who went to recover under the oak at Windrise. There he told the Traveler that Vision holders are allogenes who may ascend to Celestia, that Archons instead have an internal focus called a Gnosis, and that the glass orb on his hip is only a prop. He named Signora as the Eighth Harbinger and admitted he never expected the Tsaritsa, who had cut ties with him after the cataclysm 500 years ago, to plot to steal another Archon's Gnosis.
### ULTRA +
According to the official animated short "The Final Gift" (as summarised on Signora's wiki page), Pierro outlined the Tsaritsa's plan to collect the Gnoses at a Harbinger meeting shortly before the Traveler's journey began, and Signora was then sent to Mondstadt for the Anemo Gnosis. She went on to receive the Geo Gnosis peacefully from Zhongli under his contract with the Tsaritsa, then died in Inazuma without the Electro Gnosis. The Electro and Dendro Gnoses later reached Snezhnaya through Dottore and the Hydro Gnosis through Arlecchino; the Pyro Gnosis never did.

## appearance
### LARGE
Canon describes Gnoses in general as gleaming, chess-piece-shaped objects that normally rest inside an Archon's chest. No dialogue or item text describes the Anemo Gnosis's particular shape or colours; Signora only called it an "ugly thing" she would never wear in public. It is a different object from the glowing glass ball Venti wears as a fake Vision.

## status
@version: 7.1
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
As of 7.1 the Anemo Gnosis is in the Tsaritsa's possession at Zapolyarny Palace, one of six (all but the Pyro Gnosis). Venti has not recovered it and has shown no wish to.
### ULTRA +
In Light and Shadow, Intertwined (Chapter VII Act III, 7.1) the Tsaritsa explained that the Gnoses are formed from the remains of the Third Descender and amplify their bearer's power. She gathers them to strengthen herself against the Heavenly Principles and to break the Third Descender's seal. In Black Feathers Scattered at Parting (Act IV) the six Gnoses served as nodes bounding a field in which her Cryo power, greatly enhanced, distorted space and time around Rhinedottir. She added that her own thousand years of insight mattered as much as the Gnoses.

## roleplay
### LARGE
Roleplay interpretation (this project's recommendation, not canon text): treat the Anemo Gnosis as gone from Mondstadt. Most Mondstadters do not know it existed or was stolen. Venti, if asked, is more likely to be breezy than vengeful. Do not let him use it, sense where it is or win it back, and do not treat him as powerless without it.

## gameplay
### ULTRA
Gameplay (not literal lore): the Anemo Gnosis is a story object, never an item the player can obtain. Completing Song of the Dragon and Freedom unlocks it as the first entry in Gnosis recaps such as the Snezhnaya Awaits web event (July-August 2026), which pairs it with Signora's line from Ending Note.

## entry: Theft of the Anemo Gnosis

@version: 1.0
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
In Ending Note (Prologue, 1.0), outside the Favonius Cathedral, Signora pinned Venti with ice while her Pyro Agents subdued the Traveler. She tore the Anemo Gnosis from his chest, sneered at the "ugly thing" and left before the Knights of Favonius arrived. Venti, hurt, recovered at Windrise.

## entry: Venti on losing his Gnosis
@keys: Anemo Gnosis, Venti's Gnosis
@keys2: Varka, Homeward, Reunion Without Tears
@version: 6.4
@availability: limited
@spoiler: minor
### LARGE
During the limited-time event "Homeward, He Who Caught the Wind" (6.4, part "For a Reunion Without Tears"), Venti remarked that only the Pyro Gnosis was still free from the Tsaritsa's grasp. When Grand Master Varka noted that even the Captain could not take it by force, Venti joked that Varka must not be taking a dig at someone else's ability to hold onto a Gnosis. He then said the Gnosis had been of no use to him and he did not care who held it. Given a reason good enough to persuade Morax, he could have been persuaded too; Signora, he guessed, saw no need to negotiate because he had only just woken up, and he held back from fighting her because the Cathedral, perhaps the whole city, would have become collateral damage. He suggested her easy successes in Mondstadt and Liyue led her to overreach in Inazuma.
