## summary
### LITE
The Dendro Gnosis is Sumeru's Gnosis, one of seven chess-piece-like divine foci held by The Seven, carried inside the Archon's chest. Greater Lord Rukkhadevata used it to build and power the Akasha. In 3.2 Nahida (Lesser Lord Kusanali) gave it to the Fatui Harbinger Dottore in exchange for knowledge about the "false sky", and shut the Akasha down. Dottore brought it to the Tsaritsa in Snezhnaya, and as of 7.1 she holds it with five other Gnoses. Nahida is still the Dendro Archon without it.

## lore
### STANDARD
Rukkhadevata built the Akasha during the Cataclysm to pool human wisdom against the forbidden knowledge polluting Irminsul, and it ran on the Dendro Gnosis. In 3.2 the Balladeer tried again and again to tear the Gnosis from Nahida's chest. Nahida then combined it with the Electro Gnosis she won from him to reach Rukkhadevata's last memory. Afterwards Dottore asked for it, arguing that Sumeru would have no use for it once the Akasha was shut down, and offered knowledge in return. She accepted, because as God of Wisdom she would not choose ignorance.
### LARGE +
Nahida's character story "Gnosis" says Dottore counted on her curiosity and sense of duty. To her a Gnosis was only a core that gathers elemental power as she wills, so she wondered why a god would want them all. She later told the Traveler the deal was both a punishment for Dottore and knowledge she could not refuse. Dottore presented this Gnosis and the Electro one to the Tsaritsa as trophies (his memory in Anomalous Tree Marrow III, 6.6).

## consequences
### STANDARD
The Akasha could not run without the Gnosis, so Nahida shut it down for good. Akasha Terminals became useless and dreams came back to Sumeru. Nahida keeps her divine throne and the Dendro Authority. She has also used other Gnoses: the Electro Gnosis briefly in 3.2, and the Pyro Gnosis to burn Irminsul in 6.6.
### LARGE +
In 6.6, when Dottore corrupted Irminsul, Sumeru's scholars planned to restart the Akasha but had nothing to power it without the Dendro Gnosis, and looked for a substitute. In the end the reconfigured Aaru system took over Irminsul's role.

## status
@version: 7.1
@spoiler: major
### LITE
As of 7.1 the Tsaritsa (Anastasya) holds it in Snezhnaya, one of her six Gnoses; only the Pyro Gnosis is missing.
### STANDARD +
She collects the Gnoses to amplify her power against the Heavenly Principles and to open the Third Descender's seal. In 7.1 the Gnoses turned out to be remains of the Third Descender, Saarelainen.

## owners
### LARGE
Greater Lord Rukkhadevata, then Nahida, then Dottore, and now the Tsaritsa. Canon never shows how it passed from Rukkhadevata to Nahida, her purest branch and next incarnation. The wiki treats them as the same object, which Dottore calls "the Gnosis of Sumeru".

## appearance
### LARGE
Like every Gnosis it is a gleaming, chess-piece-shaped object kept inside the Archon's chest. No in-game text describes its specific design.

## roleplay
### LARGE
Roleplay interpretation (this project's recommendation, not canon text): after 3.2, Nahida does not have it and the Akasha is not running. She treats the trade as a deal she chose, not a robbery, and few people outside her circle know about it. Do not return it to her.

## gameplay
### ULTRA
Gameplay (not in-universe): a story object, never an inventory item. The Snezhnaya Awaits recap before 7.0 shows it beside Nahida's line about trading knowledge for the God of Wisdom's property.

## entry: Dottore's bargain for the Dendro Gnosis
@keys: Dendro Gnosis
@keys2: Dottore, Doctor, false sky, trade, bargain, exchange
@version: 3.2
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
In 3.2 Dottore (his Omega Build, segment 35) first traded Nahida the destruction of his other segments for the Electro Gnosis. Then he called the Gnosis "another chess piece" and asked for her Dendro Gnosis as a separate deal. Nahida called it arrogant, since people traditionally trade knowledge with the God of Wisdom, not for her property, but admitted she was interested. He told her the rumour that "the skies of Teyvat are fake", the secret Irminsul hides about the world's "truth", and she handed the Gnosis over.
### ULTRA +
In A Toast to Victory, Nahida says an important part of what she got "in return for my Gnosis" was the Fatui's record of the Descenders. By that record the Traveler is the Fourth Descender, and their twin is not counted as one. Her own hypothesis is that the First Descender was the Heavenly Principles. The Chinese text does not make clear whether she means the Dendro Gnosis, the Electro Gnosis or both.

## entry: Akasha and the Dendro Gnosis
@keys: Akasha
@keys2: Gnosis, Rukkhadevata, Dendro Archon, power
@version: 3.2
### LARGE
In her last memory, Rukkhadevata says she built the Akasha to compile people's wisdom, borrowing their dreams and joining all her own power to it. The Dendro Gnosis powered it. Only the Archon could run it at first; after her death the Akademiya sages took it over and stigmatised dreaming. Nahida shut it down in 3.2 after giving the Gnosis away.

## entry: The Tsaritsa's six Gnoses
@keys: six Gnoses
@keys2: Tsaritsa, Anastasya, Zapolyarny Palace, Rhinedottir
@version: 7.1
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
In Black Feathers Scattered at Parting (7.1), the Tsaritsa placed her six Gnoses, the Dendro Gnosis among them, as energy nodes around a hall of Zapolyarny Palace. Her Cryo power, amplified by them, slowed time inside and stalled the Shade Rhinedottir. Rhinedottir called it power beyond what a god should have; the Tsaritsa credited her own insight as well.
