## summary
### LITE
Tale of "Durin" is a quest item from the Draco Rubedo Chapter (6.2), Durin's Story Quest. It is a replica, made by the Hexenzirkel witch Alice, of the unfinished storybook left by Andersdotter ("M"), Durin's creator and mother. The original had to end up with Durin so he could write his own ending. The replica has no magical properties but keeps every twist of the story.
### STANDARD +
Alice gave the replica to the Traveler at the end of The Solution of Fate, saying that a witness to the tale deserves a keepsake. The player can use it to re-read all the storybook panels from The Magic Known as Storytelling; panels reflect the choices made during the quest.
### LARGE +
Its in-game description stresses that the original magic book was meant to be handed over to Durin so that he could continue writing the story.

## lore
### STANDARD
The original book was a joint work of the Hexenzirkel's witches, but Andersdotter died before finishing her part and left a great regret. She made sure Alice would give the book to Durin, whom she thought the only one who could resolve it. In the Story Quest the Traveler and Paimon help him fill the book's clues, entering the story world several times. The finale shows a simulated Simulanka-like world where Durin must break out of the story, and he receives his mother's pen and continues the tale. In post-quest dialogue Venti remarks that a line about Durin and Dvalin flying together over Mondstadt now describes a happy ending.
### LARGE +
The quest runs over nine parts spanning at least five real days: a book in Starsand Shoal brings the Traveler into a story space; clues lead to the Mondstadt library (with Lisa), Venti, a game at Good Hunter and Stormterror's Lair, then to Sumeru City where the Wanderer helps. Albedo summons them to Dragonspine, where the witches' plan is explained: it needs Barbeloth's astrology, Rhinedottir's alchemy and Alice's magic. Only the magic remained, and Alice supplies it by making the Traveler the reader and Durin the writer. In the story world the group can choose paths; the bad endings lead to fading characters, and Alice's recorded message lets them flip back pages. Each ending is written onto Durin, which is why she built that feature.
### ULTRA +
Outcome of the book. After Durin breaks free and takes up his mother's pen, Albedo finds his earlier symptoms gone, and Durin wears a freer outfit. Barbeloth, observing him, no longer sees his fate among the stars and evades his question, only telling him he was a lucky child. A fragment of Andersdotter's consciousness says farewell to Alice and delivers a letter to her child through a cinematic. Venti then sends Durin to fulfil a long-overdue wish, and Durin flies beside Dvalin and befriends him. As a reward Alice gives the Traveler the replica book.

## appearance
### STANDARD
The in-game icon is a storybook; no further description is given in item text. The original was a magic book, and the quest depicts the story world with a Dodoco narrator.

## owners
### STANDARD
Original: made by Hexenzirkel witches including Andersdotter, entrusted to Durin through Alice. Replica: Alice made it and gave it to the Traveler.

## status
@version: 6.2
### STANDARD
As of 7.1 it remains a quest item in the Traveler's possession. The story of the original book is complete; Durin continues writing his own story.

## roleplay
### LARGE
Roleplay interpretation (this project's recommendation, not canon text): the replica is a keepsake, not a magical object. Do not let it summon story worlds, alter fate or heal the Abyssal energy in Durin; those effects belonged to the original and Alice's magic. If Durin sees it he may be shy or touched, since the book is built from his mother's work.

## gameplay
### ULTRA
Gameplay (not literal lore): a quest item obtained from The Solution of Fate that opens a gallery of the book's panels reflecting the player's choices.
