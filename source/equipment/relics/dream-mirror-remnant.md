## summary
### LITE
The Dream-Mirror Remnant is an Abyss Crystal that Skirk gave the Traveler, received at the end of the quest Unbounded in Version 5.7. When worn, it can be used during sleep to "dream-mirror" with her; using it again lets the holder revisit certain scenes from what happened during that case.
### STANDARD +
It is a quest item (listed as 4-star), not equipment. It belongs to the closing stretch of Skirk's Crystallina Chapter, Act I (Whispers Betwixt Stars and Night), after the Traveler and Skirk beat the Khaenri'ahn man Skofnungr in the Abyssal space near the Chasm.
### LARGE +
The description frames it as a keepsake of a two-way exchange: to find the culprit, Skirk entered the Traveler's dreams, while the Traveler saw memories deep within her heart. The item preserves that experience as something that can be revisited.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Described only as an Abyss Crystal that is worn. No shape, colour or setting is given in canon text read. The item page shows five illustrated scenes, each with a short, poetic caption.
### LARGE +
The captions evoke a view of the universe far above the sky, a nest burning with hatchlings unable to fly, a broken frame under dim stars, a fledgling bound by cobwebs setting out on a new journey, and lost people far from home who may share a common solitude. These are the item's own words; whether each maps to a specific memory is not stated.

## lore
### LITE
Skirk, Surtalogi's disciple, is an outsider who visits Teyvat to grow stronger. In Whispers Betwixt Stars and Night she offers to dream-mirror with the Traveler and Paimon so she can experience the Traveler's stolen-soul dream herself. Later, while the Traveler sleeps, they wander through Skirk's inner world and see her memories.
### STANDARD +
In the quests, those memories include fragments of her childhood on her home planet, training under Surtalogi, and arriving in Teyvat. In Heart and Mind the Traveler reaches Skirk's inner world, a shattered river that stands for her emotions, and helps her accept the emotions she had suppressed. In Unbounded Skirk is restored to full strength and the Traveler and she defeat the culprit, Skofnungr, who stole part of her power.
### LARGE +
Skirk gave the Traveler the crystal as a gift after this. The description casts the memory-viewing as mutual: she infiltrated the Traveler's dreams to find the culprit, and the Traveler saw her heart in return. Skirk had earlier asked the Traveler to keep what they saw secret from Paimon, which gives the keepsake some weight as a shared secret, though the item text itself says nothing of that.
### ULTRA +
Context for the "case" the item commemorates: the culprit, Skofnungr, was a Khaenri'ahn friend of Surtalogi who had grafted flesh of the All-Devouring Narwhal into his heart and stole part of Skirk's power (and, per Skirk, a fragment of the Traveler's soul) by exploiting their ability to purify Abyssal energy. At the end Skirk offered to shatter his consciousness and the power inside it so that his mind and body could regenerate under the curse of immortality, and he accepted. The Traveler's dream-mirror view of Skirk's memories happened earlier in that same quest chain; the crystal is a remembrance of the whole episode rather than of the final fight alone.

## owners
### STANDARD
The Traveler holds it; Skirk is the giver.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay note (labelled as mechanics): quest reward in Unbounded alongside Primogems, Mora and other materials. It is a non-equippable quest item.

### STANDARD
The Dream-Mirror Remnant is the Abyss Crystal Skirk gave the Traveler after the events of Unbounded. Worn in sleep, it opens a "dream-mirror" with her and can replay certain scenes from the case in which she and the Traveler tracked down Skofnungr.
### LARGE +
Dream-mirroring first appears in Whispers Betwixt Stars and Night, when Skirk offers to experience the Traveler's dream herself. The Traveler then sees fragments of Skirk's inner world, which is why the crystal is described as the Traveler witnessing the memories deep within her heart.
