## summary
### LITE
The Electro Gnosis is the Gnosis of Inazuma's Electro Archon: a small object shaped like a chess piece that connects an Archon to Celestia. Ei, the Raiden Shogun, gave it up and entrusted it to Yae Miko. Yae traded it to the Fatui Harbinger the Balladeer (Scaramouche) to save the Traveler. In Sumeru, Nahida took it from him, then gave it to the Harbinger Dottore. As of 7.1 it is one of the six Gnoses held by the Tsaritsa.
### STANDARD +
Ei had no further use for it once she withdrew into the Plane of Euthymia and left Inazuma to her puppet, the Shogun. She told Yae she had cut her ties with Celestia. Paimon learned only after Signora's death that the Fatui had in fact got Inazuma's Gnosis.

## appearance
### LARGE
Like the other Gnoses it looks like a small, gleaming chess piece; Yae Miko calls it "the little thing that looks something like a chess piece." A Gnosis normally rests inside an Archon's chest and can be drawn out or made to appear. The source texts read for this entry do not describe how the Electro Gnosis differs in look from the others.

## lore
### LITE
A Gnosis grants and strengthens elemental power and links its holder to Celestia; Nahida calls the Gnoses symbols of the Heavenly Principles' control over Teyvat and its "laws." Archons keep their thrones and their power without one: Ei is still the Electro Archon after giving hers away.
### STANDARD +
Ei had first tried to turn the Gnosis into an energy supply device, but none of her techniques had any effect on it. She then gave it to Yae Miko, her old friend, knowing Yae might "sell" it but would only do so for something of equal value. Yae kept it at the Grand Narukami Shrine. When the Traveler fell into the Balladeer's trap at the Fatui's Delusion factory and collapsed, Yae gave him the Gnosis in exchange for the Traveler's safety, judging it useless to Ei and a likely source of conflict.
### LARGE +
The Balladeer was Ei's prototype puppet. Yae says Ei built him before her final puppet, then sealed his power instead of destroying him. The artifact Song of Life says he was made as a vessel for a "heart", that Ei later dropped designs able to hold such a heart, and that the "proof" which then had no home went to the great shrine. In the Fatui, the Balladeer took the Gnosis and then broke off contact. At Signora's funeral, Capitano asked Dottore about Scaramouche and the Gnosis; Dottore said only that he was waiting for Scaramouche to move first.
### ULTRA +
Yae's line to Paimon after the deal: she was not the sort to risk her life for "any old reason," and judging by the results she had struck a good bargain. Paimon agreed the Traveler was worth more than a Gnosis. The Song of Life text says the heart the puppet finally got held no blessings, only selfishness, hypocrisy, cunning and curses, yet tearing it out would leave him unable to feel anything at all.

## owners
### LITE
Ei (Raiden Shogun) -> Yae Miko -> the Balladeer (Scaramouche, later the Wanderer) -> Nahida -> Dottore -> the Tsaritsa (Anastasya).
### LARGE +
- Ei: original holder as Electro Archon. Canon does not say whether her twin Makoto, the earlier Raiden Shogun, ever held it.
- Yae Miko: kept it for Ei, then traded it away during the Inazuma Archon Quest (2.1).
- The Balladeer: used it as the core of the Sumeru god-creation plan (3.2).
- Nahida: took it from him after his defeat (3.2).
- Dottore: received it from Nahida in a bargain, and also got her Dendro Gnosis (3.2).
- The Tsaritsa: Dottore presented both Gnoses to her at Zapolyarny Palace.

## sumeru
@version: 3.2
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
In Sumeru, Dottore and four sages of the Akademiya altered the Balladeer so the Gnosis could power him as a new God of Wisdom. Joined to it, he could call down lightning and change the weather: he struck Fatui soldiers with a living-seeming lightning storm at Pardis Dhyai. Encased in a giant machine with the Gnosis as its power source, he became Shouki no Kami, the Everlasting Lord of Arcane Wisdom. He claimed that strife is "engraved upon every god and every Gnosis"; Nahida took this as a reference to the Archon War.
### ULTRA +
After the Traveler and Nahida defeated him, Nahida pulled the Gnosis from the machine's core. The Balladeer, whose obsessions it now held, begged her not to and fell from the shell in despair. With the power of another Gnosis, Nahida could reach the last memory of Greater Lord Rukkhadevata. Dottore's Omega segment then arrived. He pointed out that she could not use the Electro Gnosis in battle. Nahida threatened to destroy it and asked whether that would wake the Heavenly Principles. She judged that a foreign Gnosis would only bring disaster to Sumeru, and gave it to him on condition that he erase all his other segments. He agreed, then also traded knowledge of the "false sky" for her Dendro Gnosis.

Nahida's character story says she barely knew more about the Gnoses than about the truth of the world: to her a Gnosis was a core that gathers elemental power at will. That left her wondering why a god would want them all.

## status
@version: 7.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Dottore later recalled, in memories recovered from Irminsul's burnt trunk (6.6), presenting the two Gnoses to the Tsaritsa as battle trophies. As of 7.1 the Tsaritsa holds six Gnoses, Electro among them; only the Pyro Gnosis is missing.
### LARGE +
In "Light and Shadow, Intertwined" (7.1) the Tsaritsa told the Traveler that the Gnoses, formed from the Third Descender's remains, amplify their bearers' power, and that only a Gnosis responds to the seal he left in her palace; she hopes it may open once his remains are no longer scattered. In the 7.1 Archon Quest "A Rekviem for the Underworld", she arranged the six Gnoses as energy nodes around a hall of Zapolyarny Palace. Her Cryo power, greatly enhanced by them, slowed time there while she stalled Rhinedottir over tea. The resurrection window Rhinedottir had agreed with Ronova, meant to bypass the Heavenly Principles' monitoring, passed unnoticed, and Celestia reclaimed Ronova's Authority of Death. The Tsaritsa said the Gnoses were only one part of it; her own insights from the past thousand years mattered too.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay note: the Electro Gnosis is a story object. It cannot be obtained, equipped or used by the player.

## entry: Ei entrusts the Gnosis to Yae Miko
@keys: Ei's Gnosis, Electro Gnosis
@keys2: Yae Miko, Yae, Guuji, Grand Narukami Shrine
@version: 2.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Ei set the Gnosis aside before withdrawing into the Plane of Euthymia, after failing to turn it into an energy supply. She gave it to Yae Miko on the understanding that Yae might trade it, but never at a loss. Yae kept it at the Grand Narukami Shrine. Then the Traveler fell into the Balladeer's trap at the Fatui's Delusion factory, and Yae gave him the Gnosis in exchange for the Traveler's safety. She told the Traveler and Paimon only afterwards, in the Archon Quest "Wishes" (2.1).

## entry: Shouki no Kami and the Gnosis
@keys: Shouki no Kami, Everlasting Lord of Arcane Wisdom
@version: 3.2
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Shouki no Kami was the false god the Balladeer became in Sumeru, built around his puppet body and a giant machine powered by the Electro Gnosis. Nahida defeated him with the Traveler's help and extracted the Gnosis. Losing it left the Balladeer in despair, since it had come to hold his obsessions.

## entry: Nahida's bargain with Dottore
@keys: Electro Gnosis, Ei's Gnosis
@keys2: Dottore, Doctor, segments, Nahida, Kusanali
@version: 3.2
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
Nahida could not use the Electro Gnosis in battle and would not keep a foreign Gnosis in Sumeru. She used it as leverage, threatening to destroy it, and Dottore's Omega segment agreed to erase all his other segments to get it. He then traded knowledge of the false sky for her own Dendro Gnosis and took both Gnoses to the Tsaritsa.

## entry: The six Gnoses in Zapolyarny Palace
@keys: six Gnoses
@keys2: Tsaritsa, Anastasya, Rhinedottir, Zapolyarny
@version: 7.1
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
In "A Rekviem for the Underworld" (7.1), the Tsaritsa placed her six Gnoses, the Electro Gnosis among them, as nodes of a field in her palace. Her Cryo power, enhanced by them, slowed time in that hall. This kept Rhinedottir from completing the resurrection she had agreed on with Ronova before the Heavenly Principles' monitoring cycle came around.
