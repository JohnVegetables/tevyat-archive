## summary
### LITE
The Oratrice is a huge mechanical scale in the Opera Epiclese, Fontaine's court-theatre. At trials it weighed the arguments, returned the verdict the Iudex had to honour, and turned judgment into Indemnitium, the energy that powered the Court of Fontaine. Fontaine believed it was Furina's creation; in truth Focalors, the real Hydro Archon, built it and hid inside it. In 4.2 it sentenced "the Hydro Archon" to death and executed Focalors. It has not functioned since.
### STANDARD +
Its name is French, roughly "Mechanical Oratress of Cardinal Analysis".

## appearance
### STANDARD
A towering set of scales at the back of the stage, below the judge's seat, with four lights that come on as a trial progresses. Its core sits in a chamber in the opera house basement.
### LARGE +
A great blade hung above it; in Focalors's execution the spinning energy above her stopped and came slicing down. At the end of a trial a ticket is inserted and the Oratrice ejects its verdict.

## lore
### STANDARD
Navia explains a trial (4.0): the arguments of both sides, testimony and even the audience's emotions are projected onto the Oratrice, whose scales tip toward whoever is winning. The Iudex's decision is fed in as a reference, and the result is treated as the will of "justice" itself.
### LARGE +
Neuvillette's character story says children think of the judge as the Oratrice's "messenger pigeon". It acquitted Lynette and Lyney in their 4.0 trial, but later found Tartaglia guilty even after the real culprit was caught, the first time it ever disagreed with the Iudex; Furina covered the error with bravado. Lyney, sent by Arlecchino, slipped into the core chamber during his show and fled when a voice there seemed to recognise him; even approaching the core contaminated him with a large amount of Indemnitium. In 4.1 Arlecchino asked Furina what the vast store of Indemnitium was for, and Furina had no idea.
### ULTRA +
Indemnitium lit lamps and ran transport in the Court of Fontaine and powered the island of Erinnyes (4.0 Special Program). Fontaine's energy supply had long worried its people; about 400 to 500 years ago a looming energy crisis led to Alain Guillotin's Pneuma-Ousia Arkhe system for the Clockwork Meka, but the Oratrice was never wholly replaced, and Lyney believed its secrets were key to Fontaine's troubles. The viewpoint text asks how, without law, sins could be forgiven in the name of sin.

## true_purpose
@version: 4.2
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Focalors revealed (4.2) that the Oratrice was no enactor of justice but a device built to kill the God of Justice and her divine throne. For 500 years she stored almost all its Indemnitium inside it; only a small fraction ever powered Fontaine.
### LARGE +
The voice Lyney heard was hers: she had merged with the machine, taking Fontaine's Gnosis with her. At Furina's trial the Traveler and Neuvillette proved Furina was not a god; the Oratrice agreed she was guilty but sentenced "the Hydro Archon" to death, a sentence many had thought a myth. Neuvillette realised it was aimed at neither Furina nor Focalors but at the Hydro Archon's throne. Focalors said the stored power could have sustained Fontaine for millennia, but only that much could break a throne established by Celestia. Destroying it returned the Hydro authority to the Hydro Dragon, Neuvillette, whose verdict then made the Fontainians true humans, safe from the Primordial Sea.
### ULTRA +
Focalors admitted to Neuvillette that she was a little afraid of death, danced on her stage inside the Oratrice while Furina danced the same dance elsewhere, bowed, and the blade fell. She had also arranged for Neuvillette to be invited to Fontaine as Iudex, which he understood only then.

## status
@version: 4.2
@spoiler: major
### LITE
Since Focalors's execution (4.2) the Oratrice no longer works. Neuvillette hears cases and passes verdicts himself.
### STANDARD +
The opera house no longer produces Indemnitium; Neuvillette's full command of Pneumousia replaces it.

## owners
### LARGE
Built and inhabited by Focalors. Furina publicly passed as its maker; asked to explain the Tartaglia verdict as its supposed creator, she could only bluff. It still stands in the Opera Epiclese.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay: a fixed object in the Opera Epiclese. In the Archon Quest courtroom sections its scales and lights track the arguments.
### LARGE +
A viewpoint named after it is in the Opera Epiclese. It appears in quest cutscenes; it is not an item the player can obtain.

## roleplay
### LARGE
Roleplay guidance (project recommendation): before 4.2, Fontainians revere the Oratrice as impartial justice and credit it to Furina; nobody knows Focalors is inside. After 4.2 it is silent and Neuvillette judges alone; do not have it pass new verdicts or produce Indemnitium. Its true purpose is known to Neuvillette, the Traveler and Paimon, and through them a few others; the public saw only the death sentence and the end of the prophecy.

## entry: Indemnitium
@keys: Indemnitium
@version: 4.0
### STANDARD
Indemnitium is the energy the Oratrice produced from trials, drawn from people's belief in justice. It powered lamps and transport in the Court of Fontaine and the island of Erinnyes. Large doses are harmful: getting close to the Oratrice's core contaminated Lyney with a great deal of it.
### LARGE +
Focalors stored almost all of it for 500 years to power the Oratrice's death sentence (4.2). Since the Oratrice stopped, none is produced; Neuvillette's command of Pneumousia replaces it.

## entry: The Oratrice's verdict on the Hydro Archon
@keys: death sentence, Sinner's Finale
@keys2: Oratrice, Focalors, Furina, Hydro Archon
@version: 4.2
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
At Furina's trial in Masquerade of the Guilty (4.2), the Oratrice found her guilty as Neuvillette had, then sentenced "the Hydro Archon" to death, a shock because the trial had just proved Furina was not an Archon. The All-Devouring Narwhal attacked the courtroom. Inside the Oratrice, Focalors explained her plan to Neuvillette and was executed by its blade, destroying the Hydro Archon's throne and returning its authority to the Hydro Dragon.
