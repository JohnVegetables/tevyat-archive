## summary
### LITE
Fontaine's Gnosis, the chess-piece-like divine focus of the Hydro Archon. Focalors, the real Archon, hid inside the Oratrice Mecanique d'Analyse Cardinale with it while Furina played the god in public for some 500 years. After Focalors's execution (4.2) it passed to Neuvillette, who gave it to the Harbinger Arlecchino as a "diplomatic gift".
### STANDARD +
He let it go because it had "lost all meaning" for Fontaine and because Skirk warned him it was the remains of the Third Descender, an object of "misfortune".

## appearance
### STANDARD
A gleaming chess-piece shape like the other Gnoses; its crown resembles an inverted fleur-de-lis.

## lore
### STANDARD
In 4.0 Lyney revealed that the Tsaritsa had let Arlecchino use its power first, to try to break the prophecy. In 4.1 Arlecchino recalled ambushing Furina in disguise, sensing no Gnosis on her and only real terror, and concluded the Archon was not what she seemed. In 4.2 Focalors told Neuvillette she had "become one" with the Oratrice, taking Fontaine's Gnosis with her.
### LARGE +
When the Oratrice's blade fell on Focalors, the Hydro Archon's divine throne was destroyed but the Gnosis survived, and as her divinity faded she handed it to Neuvillette. A throne and a Gnosis are therefore separate things. A Gnosis embodies the Heavenly Principles' control over Teyvat, links its Archon to Celestia and amplifies elemental power; Furina, the public "Hydro Archon", never held it.
### ULTRA +
In Finale (4.2) Lyney says the gift could be read as an apology for the trouble Childe had in Fontaine, thanks for his part in holding off the All-Devouring Narwhal, or thanks for Arlecchino's relief work at Poisson, but that Neuvillette's attitude to giving it away had clearly changed. Neuvillette gives two reasons. The Fatui's only remaining goal in Fontaine was the Gnosis, so handing it over avoided needless conflict. And Skirk, repeating her master, had sensed it on him and urged him to get rid of objects of "misfortune", so keeping it would put Fontaine at risk. 

## third_descender
@version: 7.1
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
What the Gnoses are made of was revealed in two steps. In Finale (4.2) Skirk called the Hydro Gnosis the remains of the Third Descender. In the same scene Neuvillette concluded that the Gnoses in general come from the Third Descender. In the 7.1 Archon Quest the Tsaritsa confirmed that they do not originate from Teyvat's own system, and Rhinedottir explained that the Third Descender consented to have his remains split into seven pieces to consolidate the Heavenly Principles' rule. (The wiki identifies him as Saarelainen; the quest dialogue checked here calls him only the Third Descender.) The Tsaritsa collects them to amplify her power against the Heavenly Principles and because only a Gnosis seems to respond to the seal he left beneath her palace.

## owners
### STANDARD
Focalors, then briefly Neuvillette (who says he does not need its power), then Arlecchino, then the Tsaritsa.
### LARGE +
Arlecchino received it in negotiations with Neuvillette after Furina left. At the docks she told the Traveler that taking it to Snezhnaya was her duty as a Harbinger.

## status
@version: 7.1
@spoiler: major
### LITE
As of 7.1 it is in Snezhnaya, one of the six Gnoses the Tsaritsa holds.
### STANDARD +
In 7.1 she used all six as nodes of a field that, amplifying her Cryo power, slowed time around Rhinedottir. The Third Descender's seal in Zapolyarny Palace is still unopened.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay: a story object only; it cannot be obtained or used.

## gameplay_event
@availability: limited
@version: 6.7
### ULTRA
During the limited web event "Snezhnaya Awaits" before Version 7.0, the Hydro Gnosis appeared on a recap card for the Fontaine Archon Quest (Masquerade of the Guilty), captioned with Neuvillette's line that it had lost all meaning for Fontaine.

## roleplay
### LARGE
Roleplay guidance (project recommendation): after 4.2 nobody in Fontaine has the Gnosis, and its absence does not weaken Neuvillette or Fontaine's power supply. Ordinary Fontainians do not know where it was hidden or that it was given away; the handover was a private negotiation. Never place it with Furina, and do not have Neuvillette or Arlecchino use it. Few people know of the Third Descender link: Neuvillette, the Traveler and Paimon (from Skirk in 4.2), and those present in Snezhnaya in 7.1.

## entry: Neuvillette's diplomatic gift
@keys: diplomatic gift
@keys2: Gnosis, Neuvillette, Arlecchino, Knave
@version: 4.2
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
After the prophecy was averted (Finale, 4.2), Neuvillette gave Fontaine's Gnosis to the Harbinger Arlecchino as a "diplomatic gift". Publicly it could pass as gratitude: the Fatui had helped at Poisson and Childe had helped hold back the All-Devouring Narwhal. Privately, Neuvillette judged that the Gnosis was meaningless to Fontaine now that the throne was gone, that the Fatui would otherwise keep pursuing it, and that it was dangerous to keep after Skirk called it the remains of the Third Descender.

## entry: Arlecchino's hunt for the Hydro Gnosis
@keys: tea party, near-assassination
@keys2: Furina, Arlecchino, Knave, Gnosis
@version: 4.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
In Calamitous Tread (4.1), Arlecchino pressed Furina at a tea party about the Oratrice and the Indemnitium it had stored. The quest also shows, in Arlecchino's recollection, how she had earlier approached Furina in disguise while Furina was chasing a stray cat, and struck at her to see whether she carried the Gnosis. She did not, and her terror was genuine, so Arlecchino left.
### LARGE +
Arlecchino sensed a curse on Furina and began to suspect Neuvillette might be the true Archon, in which case he probably held the Gnosis; otherwise it might be hidden somewhere ordinary people could not reach. She then summoned Lyney and her other children. Her informants reported that Furina told no one, cried alone in her quarters and could not sleep that night.
