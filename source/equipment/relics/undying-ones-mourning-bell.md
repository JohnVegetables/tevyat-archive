## summary
### LITE
The Undying One's Mourning Bell is a bronze bell carried by Sigurd, a Lightkeeper warrior who fought the Wild Hunt in Nod-Krai and, in the item's words, walked through the dark abyss. Its peal is said to be heard on pitch-black nights. The Traveler recovers it as a quest item and returns it to his grave, and it also exists as the Sands piece of the Long Night's Oath artifact set.
### STANDARD +
Beasts are said to flinch at the sound of Sigurd's warning bell. The Lightkeepers still try to carry on the work he left unfinished.

## appearance
### STANDARD
A bronze bell. The item text adds only that it is Sigurd's and that its peal can be heard on pitch-black nights; no further design is described.

## lore
### LITE
Sigurd was a formidable Lightkeeper who fought the Wild Hunt to the end. The bell is the sound of his stubborn watch: folk tales hold that whenever mournful bells toll, the one who swore to hunt the Wild Hunt is near.
### STANDARD +
The artifact text describes a terrible storm of black rain and a host of spirits crossing the night sky, which people of the borderlands call the Wild Hunt. A black-feathered flock of crows and an oath-bound legion pushed forward against it. Sigurd, the last one standing with a dark blade, reached the edge of the forest where the Hunt's source lurked, but his body had already been torn apart; the bell he carried kept ringing amid the blood and black muck, and the carrion feeders around him faltered. He was left to walk alone in the abyss to fulfil an oath not yet kept.
### LARGE +
Per the wiki's reading of Sigurd's story, he disappeared about a decade before the game's events in a great battle that also took the family and village of Illuga. The surviving Lightkeepers built an empty grave for him northeast of the Barrowmoss Barrens, and Nikita succeeded him as Starshyna. The Abyss's corrosion and his refusal to stop defending his homeland left him as a lingering, corrupted fighter.
### ULTRA +
Sigurd was close with Chicherin, and the two once saved a young Malusha, whom Chicherin took in. The Local Legend that bears his name, "The One Who Swore to Hunt the Hunt", reveals him as a corrupted Wilderness Ghoul and ends with the words that it is finally over. Flins and Illuga can also meet him through the exploration objective Secrets of the Tideseal Stones.

## quest_use
### STANDARD
In The Bell of Mourning Echoes (World Quest, Nod-Krai, Version 6.0), the Traveler and Paimon clear Wild Hunt corruption at Tideseal Stones with a strange, Lightkeeper-clad figure fighting beside them. Cleansing three stones yields this bell, A Horn Unwinded and a Dyed Tassel. Lushne and Sukhovykh in Nasha Town recognise them as Sigurd's.
### LARGE +
Sukhovykh asks the Traveler to take the relics to Sigurd's grave near the eastern battlefield ruins on Lempo Isle, hoping his spirit may rest. The grave is inscribed with the Latin motto "Pro aris et focis" ("for hearth and home"). When the Traveler returns the three relics, a voice asks whether they pursue the Wild Hunt or hunt with it, and the Sigurd Local Legend appears; the quest earns an achievement of the same motto name.

## owners
### STANDARD
Sigurd carried it in life. After the quest the Traveler returns it to his grave; in the artifact system it can be equipped by any character.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (labelled): as a quest item it has no use in play. As an artifact it is the Sands of Eon piece of Long Night's Oath, whose 2-piece bonus increases Plunging Attack DMG by 25%.
### LARGE +
The 4-piece bonus grants "Radiance Everlasting" stacks after Plunging Attacks, Charged Attacks or Elemental Skills hit, raising Plunging Attack DMG by 15% per stack for 6 seconds, up to 5 stacks. The set drops at the Derelict Masonry Dock.
