## summary
### LITE
The Envoy's Medal is a medal the Tsaritsa gave the Traveler at Zapolyarny Palace in Version 7.1. It is proof that the Traveler is her special envoy, officially titled "Consultant to the Tsaritsa", and it gives unrestricted access to Zapolyarny Palace.
### STANDARD +
The Traveler did not swear fealty to the court, so the Tsaritsa chose the looser title of Consultant rather than a formal court position. The medal is the token of that arrangement.

## lore
@version: 7.1
### STANDARD
The in-game description calls it a medal specially bestowed by the Tsaritsa, bearing the weight of her authority, proof of the holder's status as her envoy, with unrestricted access to Zapolyarny Palace.

It was awarded in the quest A Letter From Zapolyarny Palace. Vesna sent the Traveler and Paimon a cheerful letter promising a "prize" after some formalities. At the palace they found the Tsaritsa (Anastasya), Vesna, Danica and her secretary Chayka. Vesna proposed the Traveler as the Tsaritsa's new adviser: someone of great skill who belongs to no faction, to carry out decrees that ministers handle poorly and the military would overshadow. Danica doubted anyone who comes and goes freely would carry out orders without hesitation.
### LARGE +
The Tsaritsa explained that she wanted to understand her nation from a viewpoint outside military and political affairs. She said she cannot walk among her people as a god and ruler, and that her Harbingers, as court ministers, are shaped by their ties to her and by the interests at stake. The Traveler first asked what the decrees were for, then declined to become a servant of the court but offered to help as a friend. In return the Tsaritsa asked what the Traveler does while traveling other nations; the Traveler answered with stories of great events and also small amusing details. She accepted on the condition that the Traveler travel through Snezhnaya in the same way.

She then told Chayka to record the title "Consultant to the Tsaritsa" and to issue proof of the role, granting authority to carry out its duties. Chayka was assigned to receive the Traveler at audiences and arrange needed resources. Obtaining the Envoy's Medal is the result.
### ULTRA +
The quest is the first part of Secret Tsarist Assignment, a reputation system tied to the Tsaritsa at Zapolyarny Palace, with Chayka as the contact; the next quest in the chain is The Tsaritsa's New Portrait. The quest is a world quest and is permanent, not a limited event. Danica's reservation is the main in-story resistance: she accepts the Traveler's skill but not the idea of an unbound outsider handling the Tsaritsa's orders.

## owners
@version: 7.1
### LARGE
Held by the Traveler. Bestowed by the Tsaritsa; Vesna championed the Traveler for the role and Danica voiced reservations.

## gameplay
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
Gameplay note: a quest item received on completing A Letter From Zapolyarny Palace. It does not itself appear as a usable item in play, and the invitation letter that leads to it is read through quest navigation rather than the inventory.

## roleplay
### LARGE
Roleplay use (project guidance, not canon): the medal works as a pass in scenes at Zapolyarny Palace, and as a reminder that the Traveler holds a consultant's title on friendly terms rather than service. Avoid treating it as a rank within the Fatui or as a vow of loyalty; the Traveler explicitly refused to be a servant of the court.

## status
### LITE
The Traveler holds the medal as of Version 7.1.
### STANDARD +
Nothing in the canon read suggests it has been revoked.

## entry: Consultant to the Tsaritsa

@keys2: Tsaritsa, Traveler, Zapolyarny Palace
@version: 7.1

### STANDARD
The title the Tsaritsa gave the Traveler in A Letter From Zapolyarny Palace instead of a court post, because the Traveler would not swear fealty. It comes with the Envoy's Medal as proof, and the authority to carry out the position's duties. Chayka, the Tsaritsa's secretary, is the contact for questions and arrangements.
