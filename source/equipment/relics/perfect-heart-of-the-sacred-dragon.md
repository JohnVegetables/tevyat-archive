## summary
### LITE
The Perfect Heart of the Sacred Dragon is a flawless piece of jade, the heart of the Dragonlord Ix Uxul Tz'ib Bolon Ch'ule-L (nicknamed Ixlel), who ruled Chichen Uctokah, the original city of Ochkanatlan, before human civilisation was kindled. The Traveler receives it at the end of the Natlan World Quest "Open Your Heart to Me". Its energy is exhausted, so it can no longer command anything.
### STANDARD +
Its alternate name is Clustered Storage Array. The Traveler gathers the three Ancient Jadestone Fragments of Ixlel's broken heart, and she pours her last energy into them to restore it. Item text calls it a quest item and a memento.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Item text describes only "a flawless piece of jade." Canon gives no size, shape or glow.
### LARGE +
Ixlel's own statue is a huge dragon carving, and her human guise, remembered in a ruin note, had crystal skin and turquoise eyes. Do not assume the heart looks like either.

## lore
### LITE
The item text says the heart once let its owner command every device in Ochkanatlan, "the ineffable city". It is now spent. The text also remarks that, like the half-humans who chased it, the Dragonlord's quest for perfection ended in something as cold and lifeless as jade.
### STANDARD +
In "Open Your Heart to Me", Ixlel speaks through a great dragon statue in the Old Core of Chu'ulel, deep under Ochkanatlan. She says a traitor tore her heart apart, hid the pieces in devices and used her memories to hold her there, forcing her to "sing" for his empire because he did not know how to run the city. She would not die enslaved, so she asked the Traveler, the "Chosen of Dragons", to gather the fragments, let her restore the heart with her remaining energy, and then take it out.
### LARGE +
She calls the heart a Clustered Storage Array that holds all her memories, and says that for long-lived beings memories are curses and shackles. Her life was nearly spent and she expected her consciousness to run out within thirty winters. When the fragments go in, Ixlel cries out the name Och-Kan, and chains bind her consciousness. She asks the Traveler to cut them. Secret Source platforms and Secret Source Automatons attack as the chains fall, and Ixlel remarks that these "parasites" on her life have run dry. After the last chain is cut she thanks the Traveler and her will dissipates; only silence answers Paimon afterwards, and the Traveler takes the heart.
### ULTRA +
Wiki summaries of ruin notes elsewhere in Natlan give the wider story. Ixlel was the Ninth of the Thirteen Sovereign Lords and ruled Chichen Uctokah. Och-Kan, ruler of Ochkanatlan, first accepted her as an ally, but he grew alarmed by what she taught him of the old dragon empire and resolved to strip her of her authority. He sealed her in the Old Core of Chu'ulel and took her heart apart. He had modelled Ochkanatlan's power system on that of Chichen Uctokah, so only a dragon could run it, and for a time he drew on her energy until he built a new Core of Chu'ulel he could control. These notes are the wiki's account and are not given as speech in the quest.

## owners
### STANDARD
Original owner: Ixlel, the Dragonlord of Chichen Uctokah. It was taken from her and hidden in pieces by Och-Kan. Current holder as of 7.1: the Traveler, who keeps it as a memento after freeing her.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay: a quest item and memento from the 5.2 World Quest "Open Your Heart to Me".
### LARGE +
Completing the quest also grants the achievement "Devoted to Eternal Serenity", and the Ancient Flint Key can then be placed in the new chamber to create a spiritway out of the Old Core of Chu'ulel.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay interpretation (this project's recommendation, not canon text): treat the heart as a solemn keepsake of a proud ruler's chosen end, not a weapon or power source.
### LARGE +
Ordinary people of Natlan would not know Chichen Uctokah's history. Do not have the heart grant authority over Ochkanatlan's devices; the item text says it is exhausted. Do not give Ixlel a continued voice through it.

## entry: Ixlel
@keys: Ixlel, Ix Uxul Tz'ib Bolon, Chronicler of the End Times
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Ixlel, the Ninth of the Thirteen Sovereign Lords and ruler of Chichen Uctokah, was a proud dragon who insisted on her full name, and her nickname came from Paimon. Trapped for thousands of years in a statue, she asked the Traveler for a dignified release and died after the Traveler cut the chains that bound her consciousness. Her heart is the Perfect Heart of the Sacred Dragon.

## entry: Ancient Jadestone Fragment
@keys: Ancient Jadestone Fragment, Ancient Jadestone Fragments
### STANDARD
Three strange fragments scattered through the Old Core of Chu'ulel, which feel warm to the touch and are made of a material hard to identify. They are the pieces of Ixlel's torn heart. Once she restores them with her remaining life energy, they become the Perfect Heart of the Sacred Dragon.
