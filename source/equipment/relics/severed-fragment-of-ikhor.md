## summary
### LITE
The Severed Fragment of Ikhor is a quest item found in Snezhnaya (added in 7.0). Its description calls it a piece of Ikhor's power, split off by Aksinya when a "ritual" was interrupted, and notes it has taken the form of a glowing doll.
### STANDARD +
Two copies are earned at Fellfrost Peak, one in the House of Hesperides cellar and one in the garden's great tree, while the Traveler gathers fragments to restore the Eye of Graeae's power.

## appearance
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
A glowing doll. The item text suggests the doll form is not natural to the power: it speculates that Ikhor's wish to fulfil the wishes of others became the strings that once controlled him, leaving him a puppet carrying transcendent power.

## lore
@version: 7.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The description is the main lore. It credits Aksinya with splitting the fragment away when the ritual was cut short, and offers a sad reading of Ikhor as a being reduced to a puppet by a desire to grant others' wishes. It speaks of it as a reflection by the writer, not a statement from Ikhor himself.
### LARGE +
In the world quests, the Eye of Graeae senses the fragments and guides the Traveler and Paimon to them. In House of the Soulless the fragment is in a hidden cellar beneath a room in the House of Hesperides whose bed seems to be Ikhor's; a cat helps open the passages, and Prime Ice statues must be matched to shadows in paintings. In The Sun's Gilded Apple it lies in the great tree of the Garden Bathed in Radiant Dew, which the guard Ladon says has borne no fruit since the gardener Larisa died; Paimon keeps Ladon talking while the Traveler slips in alone.

Elsewhere in the same chapter, Pavlina is shown performing rituals that draw on Ikhor's power, and Aksinya, who is working to restore the Eye of Graeae, moves to stop her. These are related background, not an account of this item's creation.
### ULTRA +
Ikhor is a quest-only figure, shown with the appearance of the young Belyi Tsar in his human form, according to a line Aksinya speaks late in the story. Aksinya's role as the one who "split off" the fragment fits her wider story as the Tsar's adopted daughter and heir to his aims, but the item text itself says no more than that. Treat any detail about how and when the split happened as unknown.

## owners
@version: 7.0
### LARGE
No one keeps it as a possession in canon read for this entry. The Traveler receives it as a quest reward; Aksinya is named as the one who split it off.

## gameplay
### LARGE
Gameplay (game mechanics, not in-world fact). A quest item with no stats. Rewards: House of the Soulless gives one, with Adventure EXP, Primogems, Mora, Hero's Wit and Mystic Enhancement Ore; The Sun's Gilded Apple gives one with similar rewards. Both belong to the Procrustes' Iron Bed series in Hesperides of Love and Hate.

## entry: Severed Fragment of Ikhor
@keys: Severed Fragment of Ikhor, Fragment of Ikhor, Ikhor's fragment
@version: 7.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
A glowing doll-shaped fragment of Ikhor's power, said to have been split from him by Aksinya when a "ritual" was interrupted. Two are recovered at the House of Hesperides in Snezhnaya during the search for the Eye of Graeae's scattered power. Its text is melancholy, calling Ikhor a puppet held by a wish to fulfil others' wishes. Nothing beyond that is established.
