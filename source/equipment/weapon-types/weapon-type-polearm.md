## summary
### LITE
The Polearm is one of the five weapon types in Genshin Impact, and each playable character is bound to exactly one type. Polearms are long-reaching melee weapons with fast attacks and moderate damage per hit.
### STANDARD +
Polearm users in the archive include Xiangling, Zhongli, Xiao and the Raiden Shogun. Under Mondstadt's old nobility the polearm was looked down on as a commoner's weapon.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Polearms are spears, lances and similar long-handled weapons. The Royal Spear's item text says it was cherished by a member of the old nobility that governed Mondstadt long ago, has never seen the light of day, and is still incomparably sharp.

## lore
### STANDARD
Per the Royal Spear's text, as relayed by the wiki, nobles in Mondstadt's era of nobility regarded the polearm, like the bow, as a weapon of plebeian gladiators and commoners, and sword-wielding nobles disliked it as a possible threat.
### LARGE +
Legendary polearms include Primordial Jade Winged-Spear, which has its own entry. The first four polearm users released, Xiangling, Zhongli, Xiao and Hu Tao, were all from Liyue; Rosaria was the first non-Liyue playable character to use one.

## gameplay
### LITE
Gameplay (not lore): polearms have fast attacks, long forward reach and moderate damage per hit. A Normal Attack chain is typically 3 to 6 fast melee attacks.
### STANDARD +
A polearm Charged Attack is usually a forward lunge that hits enemies along the way and costs 25 stamina by default. Zhongli, Xiao, the Raiden Shogun, Arlecchino, Emilie, Escoffier, Ineffa and Flins have unique slashing Charged Attacks. Plunging attacks spin downward like a drill and count as blunt attacks.
### LARGE +
Zhongli kicks his polearm in his Normal Attack; medium-male-model characters such as Xiao and Cyno also kick it, but only in the menu screen. Rosaria's menu kick was removed in Version 2.1.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay interpretation (this project's recommendation, not canon text): polearm fighting is about distance, thrust and line control. A wielder with reach keeps opponents at the tip and lunges in straight lines, so show footwork and spacing.
### LARGE +
Do not claim all polearm users are Liyue natives: that was only the early roster. The old Mondstadt nobles' disdain for the weapon is a historical attitude, not a present one.
