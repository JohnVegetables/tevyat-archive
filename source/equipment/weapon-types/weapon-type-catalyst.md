## summary
### LITE
The Catalyst is one of the five weapon types in Genshin Impact, and each playable character is bound to exactly one type. Catalyst users cast magic from range through a focus such as a book or an orb. Both their Normal and Charged Attacks deal elemental damage, so their attacks carry the wielder's own element.
### STANDARD +
Catalyst users in the archive include Lisa, Klee, Mona, Ningguang, Nahida and Barbara. The Catalyst weapon type should not be confused with the Catalyze elemental reaction, which is a separate topic.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Catalysts vary far more in shape than other weapon types: spell books, grimoires, orbs and other charms. The Royal Grimoire's item text, for example, describes a book that once belonged to a royal mage of Mondstadt, holding faithful and comprehensive historical accounts as well as magic spells. A catalyst is a conduit for the wielder's own power, so no particular shape is required.

## lore
### STANDARD
The Royal Grimoire (Royal Series, Mondstadt) is described by its text as the book of a royal mage of the old nobility, mixing history with spells. Skyward Atlas, a legendary catalyst, has its own entry.

## gameplay
### LITE
Gameplay (not lore): all Normal and Charged Attacks of a Catalyst user deal elemental damage, and the element matches the wielder's own Vision or natural element. A Normal Attack chain is usually 3 to 4 ranged elemental attacks.
### STANDARD +
Normal Attacks come in three styles. Projectile attacks home in on the target and may still hit it out of targeting range, as with Ningguang and Kokomi. Non-projectile attacks hit near-instantly at range, as with Lisa and Sucrose. Fixed-hitbox attacks have a set area around the wielder and can be melee, as with Yae Miko, Nahida and Heizou. Charged Attacks typically cost 50 stamina and release a stronger area attack at the target's location.
### LARGE +
Many characters have unusual Charged Attacks: Ningguang fires one large homing projectile, Sucrose a cone of wind, Yae Miko zigzagging lightning, Heizou an upward kick for 25 stamina, Neuvillette moves while unleashing torrents with no stamina cost, Xianyun and Lan Yan project attacks that follow terrain height, Citlali aims a piercing shot while floating, and Varesa and Ifa have attacks of their own. Most catalyst plunging attacks involve the wielder turning into a glowing elemental ball, are ranged, and cannot damage Geo objects such as minerals; Varesa is the exception. Heizou was the first catalyst user to fight in melee with the hit-stop feel of sword, polearm and claymore users.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay interpretation (this project's recommendation, not canon text): show catalyst casting as concentration through a focus, such as pages turning or an orb glowing in the wielder's element, rather than a physical swing. Reach and style depend on the character, so avoid giving every catalyst user the same spell.
### LARGE +
Because the element comes from the wielder, not the book, do not imply that handing someone a catalyst grants them magic. Canon does not describe a catalyst working on its own.

## entry: Catalyst versus Catalyze
@keys: Catalyze
@keys2: catalyst, reaction
@version: 3.0
### LARGE
The word catalyst names the weapon type here. Catalyze is a separate elemental reaction involving Dendro and Electro. When the topic is a book or orb wielded by a mage, it is the weapon type; when it is Quicken and Aggravate, it is the reaction.
