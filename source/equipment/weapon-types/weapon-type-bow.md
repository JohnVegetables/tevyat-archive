## summary
### LITE
The Bow is one of the five weapon types in Genshin Impact, and each playable character is bound to exactly one type. Bow users fight at long range with quick arrow volleys and aimed shots. A fully charged aimed shot carries the wielder's element, so a bow wielder with a Vision fights as an elemental sniper.
### STANDARD +
Bow users in the archive include Amber, Venti, Fischl, Tighnari, Yelan and Tartaglia. Bows range from plain hunting recurves to heirlooms of the old Mondstadt nobility and the legendary Amos' Bow.

## appearance
### STANDARD
A bow is a drawn ranged weapon, carried slung or held ready, with arrows loosed from a standing or moving stance. Designs differ: the Royal Bow's item text calls it an old royal longbow of Mondstadt's erstwhile rulers whose string is still tight after countless generations. Do not assume every bow looks like a plain wooden hunting bow.

## lore
### STANDARD
Item text for the Royal Spear records that in the era of Mondstadt's nobility, bows and polearms were regarded as weapons of commoners and gladiators, and the polearm in particular was disliked by sword-carrying nobles because it could threaten them. A noble family's heirloom bow, such as the Royal Bow, is therefore an exception rather than the norm.
### LARGE +
The Royal Bow belongs to the Royal Series, a set of four-star weapons of one type each (sword, greatsword, spear, bow and grimoire) described as belongings of Mondstadt's old rulers. Legendary bows with their own entries include Amos' Bow and Skyward Harp.

## gameplay
### LITE
Gameplay (not lore): Bow users make long-range attacks. The Normal Attack chain is 3 to 6 quick shots whose paths are fixed once fired, so they can miss a moving target.
### STANDARD +
Holding the attack button enters Aiming Mode, where aimed shots fly farther and count as Charged Attacks. For most bow characters an aimed shot becomes fully charged after about 1.5 seconds, deals much more damage and is converted to elemental damage. A shot at an enemy's weak point, such as a Ruin Machine's exposed core or the head of most humanoid enemies, is a guaranteed critical hit with extra knockback and poise damage.
### LARGE +
Aimed shots keep full damage for the first 0.7 seconds of flight and then lose damage and element application the farther they travel. A charged arrow can still react with the environment: Anemo arrows can Swirl in rain, Pyro arrows can Vaporize in rain, and Hydro arrows can be frozen mid-air by Cryo. Bow plunging attacks, in which the user strikes down foot-first and releases a shower of arrows, count as non-blunt melee attacks. Bow shots are less effective than other weapons at breaking Geo constructs such as ore. Some bow characters have multiple charge levels.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay interpretation (this project's recommendation, not canon text): a bow wielder watches distance and wind, keeps a clear line of sight and prefers to open a fight before the enemy closes in. Keep arrows aimed rather than endless, and remember that moving targets can be missed.
### LARGE +
Describe a charged shot as a deliberate pause and a visible gathering of the wielder's element on the arrow, not an instant trick. Do not assume every bow character is a scout or hunter: Venti is a bard, Fischl an eccentric adventurer, Yelan an intelligence agent.

## entry: Aimed shots and weak points
@keys: aimed shot, aiming mode, weak point
@version: 1.0
### LARGE
Gameplay (not lore): aimed shots are the bow's signature attack. Holding the attack button lets the wielder aim freely. A fully charged shot is elemental, with about 1.5 seconds of charge typical, and a hit on a weak point is a guaranteed critical hit. Aimed shots weaken after 0.7 seconds of flight, so close and mid range suit heavy damage.

## entry: Royal Bow
@keys: Royal Bow
@version: 1.0
### ULTRA
The Royal Bow is a four-star bow of the Royal Series obtained from Paimon's Bargains. Its item text calls it an old royal longbow that belonged to the erstwhile rulers of Mondstadt, still strung tight after countless generations. In gameplay its Focus passive stacks critical rate on damage and resets on a critical hit.
