## summary
### LITE
The Claymore is one of the five weapon types in Genshin Impact, and each playable character is bound to exactly one type. Claymores are big two-handed weapons: slower than swords and polearms but heavier per hit, with strong interruption and the ability to break shields and Geo objects.
### STANDARD +
Claymore users in the archive include Diluc, Razor, Noelle, Beidou, Kinich and Mavuika. In the game's classification the claymore is any large two-handed weapon, not only the greatsword.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Claymores are oversized and heavy. Most are greatswords, but the category also includes clubs and two-handed axes. The Royal Greatsword's item text describes an old greatsword of the erstwhile rulers of Mondstadt, made from the finest materials and meant for royals only.

## lore
### STANDARD
The wiki notes that the word derives from Scottish Gaelic for great sword, and that only the Favonius Greatsword reasonably resembles a traditional claymore. Legendary claymores with their own files include Wolf's Gravestone.

## gameplay
### LITE
Gameplay (not lore): Claymores restrict the wielder to slower hits than other melee weapons but deal more damage per hit with higher interruption. A Normal Attack chain is typically 3 to 5 slow, wide, powerful attacks.
### STANDARD +
Claymores are blunt attacks, so they deal increased damage to Geo objects and certain shields and can trigger Shatter on frozen enemies. A Charged Attack has a short windup and then a rapid chain of strikes that drains stamina continuously, ending with a stronger slash when the player releases, runs out of stamina or reaches the maximum duration.
### LARGE +
Claymore Charged Attacks typically cost 40 stamina per second and last up to 5 seconds. Two forms exist: a spinning attack for short and medium-height characters and a continuous slash for tall ones, though Kaveh and Navia use the spinning form. Exceptions: Arataki Itto does a single slash with no Superlative Superstrength stacks and spends no stamina with stacks; Kinich throws and spins his claymore, hitting three times, for 50 stamina; Mavuika does a single slash for 50 stamina. Plunging attacks are downward slashes and also count as blunt.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay interpretation (this project's recommendation, not canon text): a claymore swing is deliberate and committing, so a wielder rarely fights delicately. Show the weight in how they carry it and recover from it.
### LARGE +
Heft does not define personality: Noelle is a diligent maid-knight, Diluc a reserved winery owner. Avoid making every claymore user a brute.
