## summary
### LITE
The Sword is one of the five weapon types in Genshin Impact, and each playable character is bound to exactly one type. Swords are the middle ground of melee weapons, with attack speed and damage comparable to polearms but a shorter reach.
### STANDARD +
Sword users in the archive include Jean, Kaeya, Keqing and Furina. The legendary swords include Skyward Blade, Mistsplitter Reforged and Aquila Favonia.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Swords range from straight longswords to unusual designs. The Royal Longsword's item text describes an old longsword of Mondstadt's erstwhile rulers whose carvings and embellishments testify to the stature of its owner.

## lore
### STANDARD
Per the Royal Spear's text, Mondstadt's sword-wielding nobles looked down on the polearm and bow as commoners' weapons. The text does not state that swords were the noble weapon, though this is a reasonable reading.
### LARGE +
Quest-exclusive swords form a separate list on the wiki, alongside the ordinary and legendary ones.

## gameplay
### LITE
Gameplay (not lore): a Sword Normal Attack chain is typically 3 to 5 fast melee attacks with relatively short reach.
### STANDARD +
A Sword Charged Attack has a small windup and then two fast, arcing attacks in front of the wielder, with high knockback and a wide area. It costs 20 stamina, except Keqing's at 25. It can only be performed after a Normal Attack and cannot be buffered after the final Normal Attack, apart from Furina and Skirk. Plunging attacks strike downward and count as blunt attacks.
### LARGE +
Exceptions to the usual Charged Attack: Jean's upward thrust, Ayaka's area scan, Ayato's forward lunge, Kirara's rapid claw strikes, Furina's attack that switches her Arkhe alignment, Clorinde's Pistolet Suppression and Xilonen's forward kick. Most sword users move forward during the attack; Keqing, Kaeya, Albedo, Chiori and Xilonen move backward, and Ayaka, Nilou and Furina barely move.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay interpretation (this project's recommendation, not canon text): sword fighting is quick and close, relying on footwork and timing. Because the reach is short, a sword user normally has to close the gap or fight alongside allies.
### LARGE +
Swords vary from knightly longswords to ornate or unusual weapons, so do not default every sword user to the same stance. Not every sword user is a knight or noble.
