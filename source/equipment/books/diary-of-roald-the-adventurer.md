## summary
### LITE
Diary of Roald the Adventurer is an 11-volume book collection of travel diaries by Roald, a seasoned adventurer. He walks through Liyue (Sal Terrae, Qingce Village, Jueyun Karst, Yaoguang Shoal, Guyun Stone Forest), climbs Dragonspine, waits out the Sakoku Decree in Ritou and ends on Tsurumi Island in Inazuma. Each entry pairs a travel log with local legends and a note to himself not to lose the diary again, which he keeps doing.
### STANDARD +
The diaries are a good in-world source for folk legends and how an outsider reads them. Roald is curious, a little accident-prone and wry, wanting above all to find an adeptus and to find out what ancient peoples left behind. The legends are what locals told him, not verified history.

## appearance
### STANDARD
A worn travelling notebook with a leather case, left behind at camps. The in-game descriptions mention small salt crystals (Vol. 1), the smell of Mt. Qingce herbs, a fox-paw watermark, sea breeze, moss, ice crystals, sakura and tobacco, and fog and fungus on the pages, matching each place he wrote in.

## lore
@spoiler: minor
### LITE
Roald records places and the legends told about them, from the God of Salt's fall at Sal Terrae (Vol. 1) and the sea monster Chi at Qingce to Dragonspine, Ritou and Tsurumi Island.
### LARGE +
- Recurring thread: Roald keeps misplacing diaries, tries safeguards such as a moss marker on the case, and in Vol. 9 swaps his soggy diary for a new one. In Vol. 3 he jokes about wasting paper and hoping the Dendro Archon will not mind.
- Roald's own inferences: Vol. 9 argues from matching architecture that a "mountain civilization" may lie beneath Dragonspine, though he found no solid inscriptions; Vol. 8 calls his theory about Lisha's ruins simple and still in need of evidence.
- Placement of his route: volume numbers follow the in-game collection, not strictly his chronology. Vol. 2 says he left Dragonspine, while Vol. 9 describes the climb and ends with the plan to explore Liyue next.
### ULTRA +
- Voice and tone for scenes: Roald writes in first person, practical and lightly self-mocking, noting aches, wet shoes and food. He is respectful of local legends but weighs them against what he sees, and flags when a story is only a tale.
- Roald crosses paths with the Traveler and Paimon only in Vol. 11; do not place him with them elsewhere unless a scene needs it.

## owners
### STANDARD
No individual owns the diaries; they are left at campsites and other spots in the world for the Traveler to find. Their author is Roald, whose fate is not stated in the text beyond Vol. 11.

### STANDARD
Roald is the adventurer who wrote the Diary of Roald the Adventurer. He is a seasoned traveller who tracks legends, searches for an adeptus and keeps losing his diaries. In Vol. 11 he thanks a blonde traveler and Paimon for helping him gather Fluorescent Fungus and bring back pictures of ancient murals on Tsurumi Island.

## entry: Salt God's Fall in Roald's Diary
@keys: God of Salt, Sal Terrae, Salt God
@keys2: Roald, diary, king
@version: 1.0
### STANDARD
In Roald's Vol. 1 account, the God of Salt sheltered civilians during the Archon War and built a settlement at Sal Terrae. Her city fell when its first and last king killed her, hoping to turn it into a military power. The king's fate is uncertain in the text. This is folklore Roald collected, and other sources should be used for the canonical history.

## entry: Chi and Qingce Village in Roald's Diary
@keys: Chi, Qingce
@keys2: Roald, diary, sea serpent
### STANDARD
Roald reports the elders' story that Morax slew the sea serpent Chi, whose body became Mt. Qingce and the terraced fields. He doubts it, since the rock looks impact-shattered and shows no sign of a Hydro monster. The diary also remarks that Chi was once pronounced Qingce.

## entry: Roald on Tsurumi Island
@keys: Roald Tsurumi, Fluorescent Fungus
@keys2: diary, Kama, child
### STANDARD
In Vol. 11 Roald reaches Tsurumi Island through the fog with the help of a youth named Kama, then meets a small child he suspects may be a hallucination. When he returns without telling Kama he cannot get through the fog; a blonde traveler and Paimon help him collect Fluorescent Fungus. He gives them credit for the discovery of the ancient murals.

## entry: Roald's Diary Volume Summaries
@keys: Roald diary volumes, Roald Vol
@keys2: diary, Roald, volume
### STANDARD
- Vol. 1, Sal Terrae: from the ruin's scale Roald suspects it is the refuge temple built by the God of Salt. Folklore calls her the gentlest of the gods. Her followers settled the area, and the city stood several centuries until the king, wanting military strength and thinking gentleness unfit for the times, killed her. Theories on the king's end differ: he ruled alone from the ruins until he turned to dust, or he took his own life from guilt. Survivors scattered across Liyue and, in Liyue Harbor under the Geo Archon, their stories survived. Rumour says her body, turned to salt, remains in the ruins in the pose of her death.
- Vol. 2, Qingce Village: elders say Morax defeated the sea serpent Chi; its body became stone, blood water, scales the terraced fields and its nest Mt. Qingce. Roald thinks the rocks look shattered by impacts and sees no sign of a Hydro monster, so wonders if it is just a tale. He also notes that young people moved to Liyue Harbor for work, which brings prosperity but will eventually empty the village.
- Vol. 3, Aocang Lake: he loses another diary and swims in the mountain lake. A farmer's tale says adepti can turn to mist and roam the sea of clouds. He meets Eduardo, a treasure-hunting Adventurers' Guild member who insists she has no tie to the Treasure Hoarders.
- Vol. 4, Luhua Pool: a herb gatherer says the pond was once a garden where two lovers met; the man went to fight for the Geo Archon and never returned, and the girl's tears gathered into the pond. Roald falls asleep bathing, and a baby fox steals a shoe and his food.
- Vol. 5, Yaoguang Shoal: fisherman's legend says Greenconch Lodge belonged to an adeptus who was the conch itself and sheltered the lost; older fishermen say it housed a family living in a giant shell. The lodge seems empty.
- Vol. 6, Guyun Stone Forest: legend says the Geo Archon carved a huge stone polearm and drove it through a sea monster; wind has worn it into the pillars. Roald dreams he is the impaled monster and decides not to camp there. He sees the Crux setting sail and wonders what Captain Beidou has been assigned.
- Vol. 7, Qingyun Peak: a cold, sleepless night above the clouds before his climb to the adeptus's dwelling.
- Vol. 8, Qingxu Pool: a Sumeru scholar named Soraya told him the Lisha ruins belong to an evil god whose name was erased and its people, flooded during the Archon War and exposed when the tide fell. He meets Eduardo again. Monsters now occupy the ruins since the Chasm mining stopped.
- Vol. 9, Dragonspine: he sets up camp on the southern slope, notes ruins whose style matches other ancient sites, finds old clothing and broken weapons from the aristocratic era that suggest a chase or murder, and discovers unfrozen water in a ruin. He nearly breaks a leg, gets frostbitten toes and decides the trip was a disaster. He hears a gentle yet disturbing song in a dream. Mondstadt tales call the mountain a place abandoned by the Wind of Time.
- Vol. 10, Ritou: delayed by the Kanjou Commission, he is helped by Mr. Kurisu, a reserved commerce chairman from Fontaine. He describes how the Sakoku Decree emptied the port, recalls that Hiroshi of the Hiiragi Clan founded it, notes rumours of a Shogunate attack on a Sangonomiya outpost, and hopes the Crux Fleet can smuggle him out.
- Vol. 11, Tsurumi Island: a youth named Kama helps him reach the fog-shrouded island. He glimpses ancient ruins and dream-like scenes and meets a small child he cannot explain. He is later guided out by a blonde traveler and Paimon, who help him gather Fluorescent Fungus, said to have formed from the memories of the island's dead. He finds it calming and plans to send some to a friend in Sumeru.
