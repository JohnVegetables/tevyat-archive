## summary
### LITE
The Little Witch and the Undying Fire is a seven-volume storybook written in turns by members of the Hexenzirkel, the circle of long-lived witches. It follows a young witch who draws the exam topic 'find the undying fire', travels through odd adventures, and finally finds it in an old, dying witch who is the last star still burning. It is a jokey fable that turns into an elegy.
### STANDARD +
The authorship is mixed on purpose: each volume has its own voice, and the authors' scribbled comments in the volume descriptions are part of the joke. In the Durin story quest (6.2) Alice says it began as a game at the witches' tea parties and that the final volume was left unfinished.

## appearance
### STANDARD
A beautifully bound set whose pages are all handwritten. The Vol. 1 and Vol. 7 descriptions note several different hands. Margin comments from the authors tease one another over padding, tangents and unresolved plot threads.

## lore
@spoiler: major
### LITE
Vol. 1: a young witch draws the exam topic 'find the undying fire'. Vol. 2: under a blood-green moon she meets a fortune teller and watches the witches' forbidden Night Carnival. Vols. 3 and 4: she hunts and captures demon kings. Vol. 5 (one line): her teacher says the trial is over. Vol. 6: Octavia says the cosmos is dying and the fire exists. Vol. 7: at the end of the universe the old witch is the last star burning, and the little witch carries her flame home.
### STANDARD +
Notable beats: the fortune teller's contract through the Night Carnival's embers and her gift of a crystal ball (Vol. 2); the mimic and chipmunk demon kings (Vol. 3); the Nephilim and the captured demon king's diplomacy (Vol. 4); Octavia's chair speech (Vol. 6).
### LARGE +
Vol. 1: in the far west of the 'magic continent', a young witch faces the traditional exam, whose task is chosen by lots. Others get tasks like making the Philosopher's Stone; she draws 'find the undying fire'. Her teacher says it does exist but cannot remember where she saw it. Vol. 2: under a blood-green moon she meets a fortune teller who explains astrology on other worlds, and they spy on the great witches' forbidden Night Carnival. Vol. 3: she joins demon-king hunters and records strange demon kings. Vol. 4: she captures a great demon king and brings him to a city, where the mayor turns the case into a diplomatic problem. Vol. 5 is one line: her teacher's face appears in the crystal ball to say the trial is over and she has not obtained her licence. Vol. 6: Octavia returns to tell her the cosmos is dying and that the undying fire exists. Vol. 7: at the end of the universe she finds the old witch, who is the Star of Scorpio, and carries her flame home.
### ULTRA +
- Vol. 2 explains that in Teyvat the stars are strings binding humans, while elsewhere astrology works from solar and lunar energies, a giant sky-shroud, celestial rulers called archons, hypothetical bodies, and a 'dark sun'. The narrator says the sky-shroud weakens such influences on Teyvat. The fortune teller wants to become a witch's familiar and proposes a contract: after the carnival, a mortal who takes the undying embers and offers them has signed with a witch, at the risk of becoming a monster. The little witch promises a quick end if that happens. The fortune teller gives her a crystal ball.
- Vol. 3 is a parody of naturalist field notes. A mimic demon king turns her companions into their ideal selves; a chipmunk demon king eats two of them. She keeps a Demon King-Slaying Chronicle for each hunt and learns that killing demon kings makes witches stronger.
- Vol. 4 notes the planet's dominant race, the Nephilim (children of gods and humans), and a rule that Nephilim who killed five humans or fewer cannot be tried by human law. The captured demon king has only stolen boars and chickens, so he accepts that only the little witch is the object of his anger.
- Vol. 6 gives the emotional centre. Octavia says many stars across the cosmos have gone out and that she came back to see her; the little witch insists everyone keeps their own chair, so that absent friends' memories are still seated.
- Vol. 1 jokes that the exam is random and that the organisation she belongs to is a direct successor of the old Western witches, who traded secret hexes for mutual benefit. Her teacher says she did not lie about the fire's existence.
- Vol. 7's closing speech: the little witch says the claim that long-lived witches lose the meaning of life, death, love and hate was only comfort given to those who left earlier, and that witches in truth treasure every small flame. The old witch's body is an empty husk; the little witch takes the fire and resolves to carry it across the universe to light every corner of time.
- The tone shifts: the first volumes are comic and digressive, the narrator interrupting itself, while Vols. 5 to 7 are quiet and sad.
- Authors, per the wiki citing Durin's character story: Alice (Vol. 1 and part of Vol. 7), Barbeloth (Vol. 2), Rhinedottir (Vol. 3), I. Ivanovna N. (Vol. 4), Nicole (Vol. 5), Octavia (Vol. 6), and Andersdotter (the first part of Vol. 7). Each volume's subject matches its author's field: Alice with out-of-Teyvat references, Barbeloth with astrology, Rhinedottir with alchemy and biology, I. Ivanovna with diplomacy (she was a human with no magic training).
- In the 6.2 quest Alice says she wrote the first volume and that the final volume was entrusted to 'M', who died before finishing; Alice then wrote an ending herself. The quest frames an unfinished story as a writer's greatest regret.
- Do not treat the fable as a record of the Hexenzirkel's actual history; its cosmology (a magic continent, Nephilim, a blood-green moon, a 'dark sun') is a story setting, and the authors are not shown to endorse it as fact.

## owners
### STANDARD
No individual owner. The volumes are collectible books found in the Theater Lobby, per the wiki. Alice is the member who speaks about the book in the Durin story quest.

## entry: The Undying Fire and the Star of Scorpio
@keys: undying fire, Star of Scorpio
@keys2: little witch, old witch
@version: 4.7
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
In the fable the 'undying fire' is first a mystery set as an exam topic and later the last star still alight at the end of the universe, called the Star of Scorpio and personified as an ancient witch whose flame has dwindled to an ember. The little witch takes the flame home rather than leaving it to die.

## entry: The Blood-Green Moon and the Night Carnival
@keys: Night Carnival, blood-green moon
@keys2: little witch, fortune teller
@version: 4.7
### STANDARD
In Vol. 2 the blood-green moon is explained as a symbol of great change whose result cannot be called good or ill, and as the colour of Nephilim blood. The same night is the witches' forbidden Night Carnival, a bonfire rite; the little witch and a fortune teller hide in the trees, and the embers left by the fire serve as the contract offering to a witch.

## entry: The Authors of the Fable
@keys: Little Witch authors, who wrote The Little Witch
@keys2: Alice, Hexenzirkel, Andersdotter
@version: 6.2
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Seven Hexenzirkel members took turns writing it, per the wiki's reading of Durin's character story. Alice says it began as a tea-party game. The final volume was meant for another member, 'M', who died; Alice wrote an ending on her behalf.
