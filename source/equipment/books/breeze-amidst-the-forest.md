## summary
### LITE
Breeze Amidst the Forest is a two-volume Mondstadt book. Vol. 1 is an anthology of bard ballads, including the tale of the young Wind Dragon who sought to be understood and became a protector of Mondstadt. Vol. 2 adds excerpts about the Four Winds and the battle between Dvalin and the corrupted dragon Durin. The compiler warns that bards embellish.
### STANDARD +
Read it as Mondstadt's folk memory of Dvalin, not as an official chronicle. It is also a main in-game text on the faded tradition of Four Winds worship.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Cover text reads Breeze in Woods in the Teyvat script. Vol. 1 is introduced as ballads recorded and arranged by scholars centuries ago, the companion to Breeze Upon the Lake; Vol. 2 as a supplement with excerpts from various sources.

## lore
@spoiler: minor
### LITE
In the ballad, a young dragon graced by Anemo is born in the high heavens, descends and tries to understand humans, but villagers throw stones and farmers curse him. He hears the Holy Lyre der Himmel, the Anemo Archon's lyre, and lands beside the bard. The people panic because dragons and archons never agreed, yet the dragon learns human speech and the bard's songs and stays. Later generations count him among the Four Winds.
### STANDARD +
- After the Eclipse Dynasty fell and disaster spread, the ballad says the alchemist Gold made an army of shadowy monsters. The black dragon Durin rose from the sea to cast a shadow over Mondstadt.
- At that moment the title of Lionfang Knight had no heir, so the Knights of Favonius could not fly their falcon flag. Prayers called on the Archon, and the Wind Dragon was summoned. He tore open Durin's throat but swallowed its poisonous blood, which was distorted gold, and then fell into a deep slumber.
- Vol. 2 lists the Four Winds: the Wolf (Knight of Boreas), the Lion (Lionfang or Dandelion Knight), the Falcon (Knights of Favonius) and the Wind Dragon Dvalin. After the Lionfang Knight liberated Mondstadt the Knights of Favonius were established and the Knight of Boreas joined them. The tradition of worship formed gradually, though Dvalin predates all of them.
### LARGE +
- The excerpt says Durin's remains still lie on the snowy peaks south of Mondstadt and that the story of how the battle ended is lost; it is said Dvalin ripped out Durin's throat and both fell.
- It adds that the belief in the Four Winds had eroded in peace and their temples lie deserted.
- The excerpt describes the era before the battle as one of darkness spreading, barbarians and foul creatures forcing people to live inside city walls, and Knights depleted by long wars.
- People used to believe Dvalin would awaken whenever Mondstadt faced a new threat.
- A closing passage of unknown origin says the Knights fought Stormterror many times before learning it was Dvalin of the Four Winds, and imagines Dvalin's sense of betrayal on waking after a long sleep to find the people he had protected had turned on him. It is a citation without a known source, so treat it as commentary.
### ULTRA +
- Footnote logic in the text: the ballad's own note says life arising from elements usually descends to become a slime or ascends to become a crystalfly, and only rarely produces dangerous monsters; elemental dragons are rare and match the old gods in power.
- Compare Tale of Durin and the Anemo story quests for the verified version of events. The ballad is older and lyrical, and some details (dates, Gold's motives) are marked above as altered or uncertain.

## entry: The Four Winds Tradition
@keys: Four Winds, Wolf of the North, Lion of the South, Falcon of the West
@keys2: Breeze Amidst the Forest, Mondstadt
### STANDARD
Breeze Amidst the Forest Vol. 2 explains the Four Winds as Wolf, Lion, Falcon and Wind Dragon, linked to the Knight of Boreas, the Lionfang Knight, the Knights of Favonius and Dvalin. Worship formed over time, the dragon predates them, and temples are now deserted. Who holds the Falcon title is disputed.

## entry: The Young Wind Dragon Ballad
@keys: Windborne Bard, young dragon ballad
@keys2: Dvalin, Breeze Amidst the Forest, Holy Lyre
### STANDARD
In the Breeze Amidst the Forest ballad, the young Wind Dragon learned human speech and the Windborne Bard's singing. He stayed with the bard hoping to be understood by all souls. This is a bardic, embellished account.

## entry: Dvalin and Durin in the Ballad
@keys: Dvalin versus Durin, Dvalin's slumber
@keys2: Breeze Amidst the Forest, Durin
### STANDARD
The ballad and its supplement say Durin, a corrupted black dragon, attacked Mondstadt and the summoned Wind Dragon killed it, at the cost of ingesting poisonous blood and then sleeping. Durin's remains are said to lie in the snowy peaks to the south.
