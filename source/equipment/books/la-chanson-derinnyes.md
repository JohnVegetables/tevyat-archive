## summary
### LITE
La Chanson d'Erinnyes is a three-volume epic of Fontaine in the style of a knightly song, about the Lochknight Erinnyes, called the Knight of the Lakelight. It shows her refusing King Remus's demand for tribute, the knights rallying behind her, and her farewell to the Mistress of Many Waters, after which she throws her sword into the lake and vanishes.
### STANDARD
The text is a fragment of a longer work: it quotes chapters 6 and 20 and refers to earlier episodes such as Otkerus of Rhenanie's battle against a venom-breathing dragon. Remus's emissary is Cassiodor, called Vivarium's heir. The book presents the Lochknights as loyal only to Egeria and the Capitoliani as liars, so it is a partisan, Fontainian legend.
### LARGE +
It is the knightly counterpart to Pulteney's rationalist history of Remuria and to the satirical Anecdota Septentrionalis; reading all three shows Remuria through its official historian, its own wits, and its enemies' epic.

## appearance
### STANDARD
A 4-star book collection of three volumes. Each volume begins with a chapter and part number and a one-line summary, then verse-like lines with a repeated formal style. Vol. 1 and Vol. 3 are found on the upper floor of Lumidouce Harbor, and Vol. 2 is sold by Hubel.

## lore
### LITE
Vol. 1: A musician sent by King Remus arrives at Aremori Castle during Erinnyes's victory feast and demands water and earth as tribute and submission to the Sumphonia Capitolii, the eternal harmony. Erinnyes forbids harm to him and answers that her people bend the knee only to the Mistress of Many Waters.
### STANDARD +
In Vol. 1 Erinnyes has just defeated the princes of Bellouacoi, Atrebatis and Uiromanduoi and accepted their allegiance, hence eight days of feasting. Sir Guillaume of Gellone draws his sword Narbonnais in anger, and Knights Blancandrin and Marsile too. The emissary answers with threats of war, hunger and plague and says Remus acts out of pity.

Vol. 2: Another knight, Drest, Prince of Lyonnais, and his companion Adsiltia, whose name means daughter of ice, call Vivarium's heir a liar and a traitor. Other knights, including Naimes and Tulpin, add their swords. Erinnyes thanks them and tells the emissary that her people are bound to no monarch, because the Mistress of Many Waters has given them all equal honour, and that she will lead the blessed army to destroy the white dragon and the grand city. She orders Sir Fierabras to prepare gifts for him and has him escorted back to Carubdis Castle.

### LARGE +
Vol. 3: Erinnyes bids farewell to the Mistress of Many Waters. She entrusts her surviving knights to Egeria's service under the noble musician, to pursue the lingering shadows and restore justice. She confesses her sins, asks for exile rather than forgiveness, and says that in Egeria's land only people can judge people. She then three times addresses her sword Hauteclaire, the Lakelight, and throws it into the lake. Twice it drifts back to her feet; the third time it sinks. She leaves with the spirit who had been by her side and is never seen again.
Details of Vol. 3: Hauteclaire is said to have been given to the chief of knights by Daeira, the maiden of the lake, and to have conquered Peiregord and defeated Aisias. Erinnyes says one nobler than herself, seven times so, will one day lift it again. The sword's golden hilt is inlaid with jewels. Her speech names the Pure Grail she desired and the restored justice her knights will pursue, a line the wiki ties to the Marechaussee Phantom.

The tribal names, such as Aremori, Bellouacoi and Lyonnais, are the book's own spelling and do not all correspond to other Fontaine sources.
### ULTRA +
Voice and form: the knights speak in formal oaths and self-introductions, with refrains repeated three times, in imitation of a medieval song. Erinnyes is described as the wise Knight Erinnyes, beloved leader of the knights, and with sea-colored eyes. Cassiodor is not named in the book, only as Vivarium's heir, musician and noble musician.

## owners
### STANDARD
No named owner. It is a legend preserved in books, with Vol. 2 sold by Hubel in the Court of Fontaine.

### STANDARD
Hauteclaire, Sword of Light, is the name Erinnyes gives her blade in Vol. 3. She throws it into the lake three times; twice it returns, and the third time it sinks. The book says Daeira, a maiden of the lake, gave it to the chief of knights and that it won battles at Peiregord and against Aisias.

### STANDARD
In the Chanson the emissary of King Remus is a lone musician with a golden-tipped fasces and a horse, who demands water and earth from Erinnyes and threatens war. The knights name him Vivarium's heir. Other sources identify the Harmost Cassiodor as this figure, but the book does not name him.

### STANDARD
Named knights in the epic include Sir Guillaume of Gellone, who wields the sacred blade Narbonnais, Knights Blancandrin and Marsile, Drest Prince of Lyonnais, the lady Adsiltia of the azure sail, Naimes, Tulpin, Fierabras, and Sir Agricane, who speaks before Vol. 2.
