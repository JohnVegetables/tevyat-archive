## summary
### LITE
String of Pearls is a five-act Liyue opera script by Yun Jin, split into five volumes. It follows Zixin, a young fisherwoman, and Fan Jie, a dock worker who returns her lost string of pearls. A misunderstanding, a false reward notice, a local bully named Wu Wang and a rescue in a dark stronghold bring them together.
### STANDARD +
The script is written as stage directions, role lists and sung lines in the "Dongtang" and "Ganging" styles. It uses stock roles of opera: a male lead, a female lead, a comic role and a villain. A comic character, Grandma Zhang, speaks to the audience and mentions Yun Jin's plays directly.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Printed script pages with an act title, a cast list and numbered scenes. Delivery notes in brackets (aside, aloud, and musical directions like lento or appassionato) mix with stage movements.

## lore
@spoiler: none
### LITE
Act I, Of Fish and Water: Zixin, sixteen by her own line, sells fish to support her ageing parents. She loses her string of pearls and Fan Jie, who found it, checks her wrist for wear marks. She takes this as an insult and scolds him before learning he meant well. He leaves before telling his full name, and she regrets it.
### LARGE +
- Humour and structure: the comic roles break the fourth wall, remark on Yun Jin's plays and theatre convention, and parody the reward trope. Wu Wang is a cartoonish bully who thinks nothing of unpaid meals and kidnapping.
- Themes are modest honesty and mistaken first impressions; the pearls link the pair in Acts I, III and V.
### ULTRA +
- Scene use: quote only a line or two. It suits an audience at a Liyue theatre, a bookhouse conversation or a scene where Yun Jin or an opera fan discusses the plot.

## entry: Zixin and Fan Jie
@keys: Zixin, Fan Jie
@keys2: String of Pearls, Yun Jin, opera
### STANDARD
Zixin and Fan Jie are the leads of the opera String of Pearls. Zixin is a fisherwoman who sells fish in Liyue Harbor. Fan Jie is a dock worker chosen by fellow workers as leader. He returns her pearls, and after misunderstandings and a rescue from Wu Wang they plan to marry. They are stage characters from Yun Jin's script.

## entry: String of Pearls Act Summaries
@keys: String of Pearls acts, Wu Wang, Grandma Zhang
@keys2: opera, Yun Jin, Zixin
### STANDARD
- Act II, The Search for the Gentleman: on Grandma Zhang's advice Zixin posts a Mora reward notice to find him. Three tricksters, Zhang San, Li Si and Wang Er'ma, try to claim it with false goods. Grandma Zhang drives them off with a trap, Fan Jie is brought in by mistake, and the two realise the misunderstanding and apologise to one another.
- Act III, The Pearls, Lost Again: the two meet daily. The local bully Wu Wang, with companions Wu Yi and Wu Er, seizes Zixin. Grandma Zhang gives Fan Jie the pearls, and he rides to rescue her.
- Act IV, Trial by Candlelight: Fan Jie scouts Wu Wang's stronghold. Zixin hits the drunk Wu Wang with a candlestick and flees in the dark without relighting it.
- Act V, Twin Pearls Returned: in the dark, Fan Jie and Zixin find each other, Wu Wang mistakes Fan Jie for someone else and is stabbed, and the couple leave together with a promise.
