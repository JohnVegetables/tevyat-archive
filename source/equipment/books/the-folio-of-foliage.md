## summary
### LITE
The Folio of Foliage is a three-volume devotional biography of the Dendro Archon during the Cataclysm, kept in the House of Daena in Sumeru. Its text and subject were rewritten after the Archon Quest Where the Boat of Consciousness Lies (3.2).
### STANDARD +
The narrator, who gives the name Sunitha Cosamvi, says they only wrote down what sages who followed the Archon told them. It reads as scripture, a follower's hymn, not an eyewitness record.

## lore
### LITE
She weeps as a black tide of decay swallows the world, walks through a ruined forest that blooms under her feet, and goes into the broken heart of the desert to heal it.
### STANDARD +
Vol. 1: after the moon's shadow shattered, beasts from the abyss devoured the living and the sages named the flood of decay the Black Tide. Her tears gave birth to blossoms full of sweet dew, and she vowed to save the creatures of the earth. Vol. 2: lotuses bloom in her wake, her singing quiets hurricanes, and she goes down alone into the hollowed heart of the land. Vol. 3: few ever heard or recognised her voice; the lord of all animals died protecting what she entrusted to him, and she left the dreamers a promise.
### LARGE +
Vol. 1 adds that springs once ringed with roses turned to filth and that the root of the blight lay beneath the earth. In Vol. 2 bells on her lapels ring as she crowns herself with flowers, makes an instrument of grass, plays a Karrenay (a metal trumpet, per the wiki) and turns thousands of demonic troops to dust, recalling her earlier gift of the wind of life over the sand sea. The land was still broken, its heart devoured by evil spirits and monsters. In the first edition she touches the heart's face and becomes the immortal Gaokerena and the earth itself; every thread of spiritual knowledge and every sweet-smelling plant is her enduring will. In Vol. 3 the labyrinth collapsed in flame, the princess of the kingdom of dreams broke the white branches at her guidance and rebuilt the hunting grounds from fallen leaves, and the children of the forest could sleep again. Her promise is that the hunter always finds the way home, and that sweet dreams are like pearls that wind and sand cannot change.
### ULTRA +
Place names tie the text to Sumeru's geography: the Ardravi River, the Girdle of the Sands, an eternal oasis (the Orchard of Pairidaeza), Harvisptokhm, the Vourukasha Oasis, the Khvarena birds and Irminsul's white branches; the lotuses are Nilotpala Lotuses. Some images belong to later canon (the Cataclysm and the Withering, Irminsul, the beast-lord Viaghara), but the books never explain them and name no other gods, so treat any such link as the reader's knowledge, not the narrator's.

## edited_version
@version: 3.2
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
After Where the Boat of Consciousness Lies (3.2), Nahida erased the Greater Lord Rukkhadevata from Irminsul, and books across Sumeru, this one included, changed to match. The edited collection names Lesser Lord Kusanali, the Dendro Archon people now remember. Vol. 1 has her set out on a radiant journey with her servants. In Vol. 2 she no longer walks into the dark cave and vanishes: she vows to cleanse the withered land, her goodness becomes the immortal Gaokerena and the earth, and she returns, and the volume ends with a four-line hymn praising wisdom's returning light. Vol. 3 says she still dwells in the Sanctuary of Surasthana watching over the dreams of everyone in the land, and turns the promise into an oath to the dreamers.
### ULTRA +
In the first edition she becomes the earth; in the edited edition she survives and keeps watch. This is how an Irminsul edit works: records change to fit the world's new memory while the events behind them stay. Natives do not notice such edits, so a character who read the books before 3.2 would remember only the rewritten text.

## owners
### LARGE
No owner is named. The books are found in the House of Daena, and the in-world description credits researchers of ancient Sumeru.

## entry: The Folio of Foliage
@keys: Folio of Foliage, Sunitha Cosamvi
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The Folio of Foliage is a three-volume devotional biography of the Dendro Archon during the Cataclysm, narrated by a follower who gives the name Sunitha Cosamvi. It describes her tears healing scorched earth, her flowering walk through a ruined forest, and her descent into the hollow heart of the desert to become the earth itself, in the first edition, or to cleanse it and return, after the Irminsul edit of 3.2. The books are found in the House of Daena.
### LARGE +
The rewritten collection names Lesser Lord Kusanali and ends with a hymn and a promise to the dreamers. It is an example of how Irminsul edits change books, not the events they describe.

## entry: Black Tide
@keys: Black Tide, moon's shadow, Gaokerena
@keys2: Folio, Sunitha Cosamvi, Dendro Archon
@spoiler: minor
### LARGE
In The Folio of Foliage the sages call the black tide of decay a dark flood that swallowed fields, villages and cities after the moon's shadow shattered and beasts rose from the abyss. The Archon's tears dispel foul flames, but the root of the blight lies beneath the earth. She finally goes into the empty heart of the land and becomes the immortal Gaokerena and the earth itself, so that flowers bloom again. The account is devotional and symbolic; for what is established about the Withering and the Cataclysm, see their own entries.
