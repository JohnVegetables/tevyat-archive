## summary
### LITE
The Byakuyakoku Collection is a five-volume set of old texts from Enkanomiya's Byakuyakoku era, obtained by completing the quest Collection of Dragons and Snakes. It holds a folk tale of the Profane Serpent, a creation chronicle, a hydrology study, vishap experiment records and a children's puzzle book.
### STANDARD +
Together the volumes show how the people of Enkanomiya understood their world before and after the arrival of Watatsumi Omikami.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Item art shows a slate-like tablet book. The volumes take different in-world forms: a novel, a restricted chronicle, a land survey, laboratory records and a riddle book.

## lore
### LITE
- Vol. 1, "The Serpent and Drakes of Tokoyokoku": a novel based on Watatsumi folktales. The sage Aberaku brings a sun to Enkanomiya and is imprisoned by the first Sunchild; years later a child finds a vast serpent who becomes the people's god.
- Vol. 2, "Before Sun and Moon": a restricted chronicle of the world's making and the people's fall into the dark.
- Vol. 3: a geography and hydrology of Byakuyakoku.
- Vol. 4: Bathysmal Vishap experiment records.
- Vol. 5: a children's riddle book.
### STANDARD +
Vol. 1 is a Watatsumi novel carried to Narukami Island by a merchant while its serpent god lived.
### LARGE +
Vol. 1 says the universe has no beginning or end. Aberaku was opened to wisdom by Tokoyo Ookami and brought light to Enkanomiya, but the jealous Sunchild imprisoned him for life. With the sun, the Dragonheirs, who shunned light, could no longer prey on humans. But the Sunchild became a brutal king. Then a child diving past the three corners for a Dragonbone Flower meets the Profane Serpent, who has fallen into this realm, and asks it to become their god.
- Vol. 2 recounts the world's making: the Primordial One fought the seven sovereigns, dragon-lords of the old world, and made four shining shades of itself. It lists the creation of land, animals and finally humans, a covenant, and a "Year of Jubilee". Then the second throne of the heavens arrives in the "Funerary Year", the heavens collapse and the ancestors fall into this place, starting the era of darkness.
- The Dragonheirs of the Depths hunt the survivors in the dark. The people know the Ruler of Time as Kairos, whose true name is written once in reverse ("Htoratsi", i.e. Istaroth), and the sage Abrax builds Helios, the chariot of the sun, bringing the Whitenight. The Primordial One's ban blocks the way home, and Abrax is imprisoned by the Sunchild. Parables follow: the sun and torch, the Lethied Lotus, and a gardener and tree spirit.
- Vol. 3 says Byakuyakoku has no mountains, and its wind ("Tokoyo Ookami", the thousand winds) and water (the vishaps) both persist. Three corners mark the border with the vishaps' realm and hold the Towers of the Three Realms, hidden by secret arts and summoned only by shrine maidens and Vassals of Watatsumi. It describes the Narrows, the Serpent's Heart (also "Delphi"; scaleless Ouroboros, coral-adorned Orobashi) and the Dainichi Mikoshi, whose earliest name is Helios, built by Aberaku.
### ULTRA +
- Vol. 4: after the founding of the Watatsumi calendar, Watatsumi Omikami directed all research on Dragonheirs (Bathysmal Vishaps). The researchers bred them under different temperatures, observing heritable traits (Cryo expression in cold-bred lines, no sign of Pyro in heat-bred ones), and tested their intelligence; fourth-generation vishaps approached a 12-year-old's language ability. They judged the experiments should stop, warned that the prophesied Dragon Sovereign of Water, born in human form, must not appear in Enkanomiya, and eventually grafted Omikami's blood into vishaps, which succeeded once they accounted for the vishaps being Light Realm beings and Omikami's coral vassals Human Realm. No vishap was killed in the experiments.
- Vol. 5 is a children's riddle book in a transitional style with Narukami-style names and Byakuyakoku subscripts. It comments that the Dragon of Water can no longer be born among the Dragonheirs, who evolved to wield other elements and lost their purity; that livestock died out after Byakuyakoku sank; that the mirages are Sunfire Phantasms and Ghostfire Phantasms; and that Watatsumi Omikami forbade a riddle about the primordial child Tokoyo Ookami, the "mother" of fourteen billion years.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay interpretation (this project's suggestion): treat each volume as a document with its own bias, written before the people returned to the surface. The creation chronicle is a Byakuyakoku account, and the book's riddles and parables are teaching tools, not proof. Do not use it to explain things the texts leave elided, and keep Enkanomiya revelations out of the mouths of characters who never read them.

## entry: Before Sun and Moon
@keys: Before Sun and Moon, Funerary Year, Parable of the Lethied Lotus
@version: 2.4
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
"Before Sun and Moon" is the Byakuyakoku chronicle in Vol. 2 of the Collection, recording the world from its making by the Primordial One to the fall of the ancestors into the dark and the building of Helios. It names the Ruler of Time "Kairos", whose secret name is Istaroth, written in reverse, and calls the sage Abrax the builder of the sun. Ordinary people were forbidden to read it. It ends mid-record with armour approaching.

## entry: Bathysmal Vishap Experimental Records
@keys: Bathysmal Vishap Experimental Records, Dragonheir experiments
@version: 2.4
### STANDARD
Vol. 4 holds laboratory records of experiments on the Dragonheirs of the Depths under Watatsumi Omikami's direction. The researchers study heritable traits and intelligence in captive vishaps, then graft Omikami's blood into them after earlier failures. Their asides note that none of them died, and that the prophesied Dragon of Water must not be born in Enkanomiya.
