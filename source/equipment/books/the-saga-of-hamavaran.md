## summary
### LITE
The Saga of Hamavaran is a three-volume light novel by the writer Pursina, sold at the Yae Publishing House in Inazuma City. It follows a Sumeru scholar named Hamavaran who travels to Inazuma.
### STANDARD +
Vol. 1 is a publisher's foreword, Vol. 2 opens the comic story of Hamavaran's voyage and Vol. 3 introduces his talkative companion, a shikigami who calls herself a "ghost-calling".

## appearance
### STANDARD
Three slim novel volumes, with the first collecting the prefaces of the new light novel into its own volume due to "a business model adjustment". The cover is written in the Inazuman script and reads LGDHMVR, which the wiki takes to stand for "Legend of Hamavaran".

## lore
### STANDARD
The foreword thanks writers drawn in by a recruitment drive and a contest called "This Novel is Amazing!", and thanks Kanjou Commissioner Hiiragi Shinsuke. It says that before the Sakoku Decree accomplished samurai from many lands lived in Inazuma, among them Hamavaran from the rainforest nation of Sumeru, whose tale was buried under the Shogun's eternity and is now retold by the budding author Pursina.
### LARGE +
Vol. 2: Hamavaran, an ordinary scholar given an unpopular specialisation, sails to Inazuma worried more about delaying his graduation than the storm. A voice outside his cabin bickers with him; on shore nobody is there and he reasons it away as a ley-line memory of a drowned person, which the voice rejects.

Vol. 3: the voice belongs to a talkative young girl who says she is a shikigami and eats glutinous-rice dango quickly. She calls herself a "ghost-calling", neither dead nor ever alive. Hamavaran concludes this is like Sumeru's spirit-warding, which also involves a pact sealed with a true name, and regrets choosing marine biology over mysticism.
### ULTRA +
The wiki notes the Japanese title may echo the light novel The Heroic Legend of Arslan, and that Hamavaran is a Persian-derived name connected to the Himyarite kingdom in the Shahnameh.

## owners
### LARGE
No owner is named. It is sold by Kuroda at the Yae Publishing House in Inazuma City.

### STANDARD
The Saga of Hamavaran is a light novel by Pursina sold at the Yae Publishing House. Its hero is Hamavaran, a Sumeru scholar of marine biology who sails to Inazuma, meets a shikigami who calls herself a "ghost-calling", and compares her to Sumeru's spirit-warding. The foreword claims Hamavaran was a foreign samurai active before the Sakoku Decree.
