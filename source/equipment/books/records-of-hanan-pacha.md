## summary
### LITE
Records of Hanan Pacha is a three-volume collection of Natlan folk tales about the Sage of the Stolen Flame, Waxaklahun Ubah Kan. Vol. 1 tells how he and a companion, Chaac, tricked a vain winged dragon and took the power of phlogiston. Vol. 2 tells how they raised the land of Hanan Pacha and taught people to use it. Vol. 3 tells how the people broke a taboo and were driven out. The game itself labels it a fable with no basis in historical fact.
### STANDARD +
The narrator is an unnamed storyteller who speaks to listeners and jokes with them about the plot. The books give Natlan's popular account of how humans first gained phlogiston and how the first permanent tribe came to be.

## appearance
### STANDARD
A readable book collection in Natlan, in three volumes. Vol. 1 and 2 are found at the Scions of the Canopy (on crates near the patrolling guard Yawar, and next to Amauta); vol. 3 is in front of Battuta at the Stadium of the Sacred Flame. No author or publisher is listed.

## lore
@version: 5.0
@spoiler: minor
### LITE
Told in the age of dragons, when the Lord of the Night ruled the night realm and no Pyro Archon yet existed. Humans had no Visions and were at the mercy of the elements until the Sage brought phlogiston from the "red and black land".
### STANDARD +
The sage tricks a vain dragon and takes its phlogiston, raises Hanan Pacha with Chaac to teach people, then expels them after the Lord of the Night gets them drunk. Chaac goes on to found settled tribes.
### LARGE +
- Vol. 1: A winged dragon, heir to the volcano lord Xiuhcoatl, claimed the sleeping lord's treasures, among them Pyrophosphorite, a rainbow crystal refined from the volcano's primal fire. Its glow made the dragon vain. The Sage blinded one eye with a bamboo blowdart, then he and Chaac posed as eye doctors and talked the dragon into letting them swap its eyes, fangs and claws for grainfruit. The dragon died, the Sage cracked open the crystal rather than give it to the Lord of the Night, and gained the dragons' power, called phlogiston.
- Vol. 2: Humans rejected phlogiston, since fire and fuel were already easy to find; the Sage argued that one day wood would run out but rock never would. Chaac's blunt idea was to raise a great stone above the mountains so curiosity would draw people. The two lifted a whole stretch of land, Hanan Pacha, above the clouds. Chaac guarded the lake and helped people ascend, and those who reached it learned phlogiston from the Sage and formed the first permanently settled tribe.
- Vol. 3: The people built a gem, feather and flower city and the Sage wished to raise the land higher, forbidding all food from the land beneath, allowing only water, since the Source of All Waters lies beyond the Lord of the Night's control. They fished and grew ball algae. The Lord of the Night, fearing humanity's departure, disguised as a tribesman and gave Chaac sweet water that was actually grain alcohol. Everyone was drunk when the Sage returned; he expelled them, they carried phlogiston across Natlan, and he set out "on the road home". Chaac then accepted the Lord of the Night's guidance and founded settled tribes on the land.
- Tone and framing: Chaac is called "the fool" and speaks casually, while the Sage is "the wisest in history". The narrator also pauses to correct rival tellings and answers a heckling listener, so scenes quoting the book should keep this storyteller's voice.
- The Sage's plan to climb ever higher is hinted rather than explained: only the phrases "Lunar continent" and "Newark Holy Stone" are given, and Chaac does not understand them. The wiki notes the second is a nod to a real-world hoax, which fits the collection's fictional-fable status.
- Related lore the book is cited for: The Upper Sanctum entry says that, according to legend, Hanan Pacha was created when the Sage and Chaac raised a tract of land into the sky and shared phlogiston there with those who passed their trials. Today it is a ring of smaller islands in the Toyac Springs area, reached through Spiritways.
- Chaac's own page cites the book for his tale: first human to receive phlogiston, founder of the first tribe, then leader of the scattered tribes after the exile.
### ULTRA +
- Names echo real-world mythology (Hanan Pacha, an Inca realm of the sky; Chaac, a Maya rain god); this is wiki etymology, not in-game explanation.

## entry: Sage of the Stolen Flame
@keys: Sage of the Stolen Flame, Sage of the Stolen Fire, Waxaklahun Ubah Kan
@version: 5.0
### STANDARD
In the Records of Hanan Pacha, the Sage is the cleverest figure in history, who took phlogiston from a dragon, taught it to humans and raised Hanan Pacha, then left Natlan after the people broke his taboo. The text says his homeland is not named and implies he will one day return. The book is a fable; do not use it as proof of what he actually did.

## entry: Hanan Pacha (legend)
@keys: Hanan Pacha, Upper Sanctum
@version: 5.0
### STANDARD
In the tale, Hanan Pacha was once a whole stretch of land floating above the clouds, visible from anywhere in Natlan. People came up through the power of phlogiston to learn from the Sage and settled there. Today the Upper Sanctum is a ring of islands, accessible only via Spiritways.
### LARGE +
The legend says the Sage wanted to raise it higher still and demanded that its people eat only water and food grown on the islands themselves.

## entry: The Winged Dragon and Pyrophosphorite
@keys: Pyrophosphorite, winged dragon
@version: 5.0
### STANDARD
The fable's dragon is a vain winged creature in the red and black lands that took the treasures of the sleeping volcano lord Xiuhcoatl. Its pride rested on Pyrophosphorite, a rainbow crystal of primal fire. The Sage and Chaac blinded and tricked it with disguises and grainfruit replacements, and the Sage broke the crystal to gain phlogiston.
