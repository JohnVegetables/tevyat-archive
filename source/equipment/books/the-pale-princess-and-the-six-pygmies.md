## summary
### LITE
A seven-volume fairy tale said to have been told across Teyvat for ages. Only Vol. 1 can be read in game. Lisa gives the Mondstadt library's copy to the Traveler as 'custodian' after retrieving it from the Abyss Order.
### STANDARD
Its title echoes the German fairy tale Snow White (wiki trivia, an out-of-world observation). The Abyss Order's interest in a 'secret' inside it is never explained.

## appearance
### STANDARD
A 4-star Book Collection of seven installments. Vol. 1 is received directly from a quest, unlike most books, which are found in the world or bought.

## lore
### LITE
Vol. 1: long ago the Night Mother ruled the Land of Night, where no light, trees or life but dark horrors existed. Only the Moonlight Forest escaped her rule, its people fair, light-haired and blue-eyed.
### STANDARD
The Night Mother is called the source of all sins, with neither heart nor mouth, always watching and punishing unpredictably. The one thing she could not bear was moonlight breaking through the clouds. The Moonlight Forest kingdom lived in that moonlight; the tale suggests their beauty came from it, in contrast to the abhorrent creatures at the forest edge.
### LARGE +
The other volumes are known only from descriptions: Vol. 2 the meeting of the Pale Princess and the Light Prince; Vol. 3 their decision to save the Moonlight Forest; Vol. 4 their meeting with the Six Pygmies; Vols. 5 and 6 the Pygmies' sinister scheme and how it was carried out; Vol. 7 the conclusion and the Night Mother's prophecy. Those volumes are unreadable ('currently unavailable') as of 7.1.
### ULTRA +
The wiki compares the situation with the book Before Sun and Moon, which Abyss Order member Enjou tried and failed to find in a later quest while Khaenri'ah's own attempt to steal it long ago was foiled; this is a wiki comparison, not something the tale's text says.

## owners
### STANDARD
Held by the Mondstadt library under Lisa as librarian. After Lost Book, the Traveler is registered as its custodian rather than borrower, a privilege Lisa says is reserved for trusted citizens, with no due date.
### LARGE +
Lisa says it is not from the restricted section, which is why she let a citizen borrow it. The borrower (Donna) lost it when it was stolen; Lisa had marked it with elemental traces, which let the Traveler track it.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Game mechanics: Vol. 1 is an automatic reward at the end of Lost Book (the second half of Troublesome Work).

## entry: Lost Book (stolen library copy)
@keys: Lost Book, stolen library book
@keys2: Lisa, Abyss Mage, Pale Princess
@version: 1.0
### STANDARD
In the Mondstadt story quest Lost Book (Tempus Fugit Chapter, Act I), Lisa asks the Traveler to help recover a borrowed library book whose copy of The Pale Princess and the Six Pygmies has been stolen. Following her elemental marks near Starfell Valley leads to a hidden Abyss Mage stronghold. Lisa defeats the mage, who says the book holds a secret, and the book is found in a chest below. Lisa and Paimon judge it an ordinary storybook.
### LARGE +
Lisa jokes about punishment had the book been damaged. She then makes the Traveler its custodian and, back at the library, hands over the Teyvat Travel Guide as reward.
