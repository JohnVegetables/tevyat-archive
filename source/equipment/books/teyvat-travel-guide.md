## summary
### LITE
The Teyvat Travel Guide is a three-volume magazine series from the Adventurers' Guild. Under the heading 'Teyvat Geographic Special Edition', each issue is a short travel diary by Alice, covering Mondstadt, Liyue and Inazuma. Alice is a cheerful experimenter and the diaries treat sightseeing as an excuse for explosives, potions and mischief.
### STANDARD +
The descriptions say each issue introduces great sceneries across Teyvat. Vol. 3 first became available in the limited-time Hues of the Violet Garden event (2.6), and Kuroda continues to sell it afterwards.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Glossy magazine issues whose cover reads 'Travel Guide' in the Teyvat script. Entries are headed by place names and written in the first person in a breezy, boastful, sometimes self-deprecating voice.

## lore
@spoiler: minor
### LITE
Vol. 1 (Mondstadt) visits Dadaupa Gorge, Starsnatch Cliff, Windrise, Falcon Coast, the Whispering Woods, Brightcrown Canyon and Stormterror's Lair. Vol. 2 (Liyue) visits Dihua Marsh and Wangshu Inn, Guili Plains, Jueyun Karst, Yaoguang Shoal and Guyun Stone Forest. Vol. 3 (Inazuma) visits Mt. Yougou and the Grand Narukami Shrine, Inazuma City, Tatarasuna and Yashiori Island.
### LARGE +
- Vol. 1: Alice proposes absurd energy schemes for the hilichurls of Dadaupa Gorge and for demolishing Starsnatch Cliff; Lisa answers with silence and Kaeya rejects her plan. She tries to launch hilichurls from the oak at Windrise (Vennessa is said to have ascended there), and Jean has an Outrider (Amber) assigned to watch her at Falcon Coast. She tries to control a Ruin Guard in Brightcrown Canyon with a hilichurl, with gruesome results, and comments on Decarabian's ring-shaped city.
- Vol. 2: at Wangshu Inn she notes a silent lodger on the top floor and enjoys the kitchen for alchemy practice. At the Guili Plains checkpoint she cured a guard's stutter with a potion whose side effect makes him imitate everyone. At Jueyun Karst she muses on adepti visions and mushrooms, her travel companion Zhongli laughs at her theory about the stone pillars, and at Yaoguang Shoal she loses shells and a prototype seashell-boat. At Guyun Stone Forest she wonders about the Geo Archon's victory over the sea monster and the unstable ley lines.
- Vol. 3: at the Grand Narukami Shrine she mentions Yae Miko, who out-drank her. In Inazuma City she meets Naganohara Ryuunosuke and Yoimiya and is sent off by the fire brigade; she mentions Klee's Golden Apple Archipelago, an accident in the Imperial Kitchen that hurt Kujou Sara, Tenshukaku's guards, and Kamisato Ayaka declining her idol group invitation. At Tatarasuna she meets Kagami Gozen and boasts of modifying the Mikage Furnace. At Yashiori Island she admires the Shogun's wards and mentions Washizu of Higi Village, Nazuchi Beach pirates and Asase Shrine.
### ULTRA +
- The diaries repeat Alice's habits across nations: building weapons from gadgets, testing potions on bystanders, and being watched by officials (the Knights of Favonius in Mondstadt and soldiers sent from Tenshukaku in Inazuma).
- Her remarks about Mondstadt's and Liyue's gods (the Anemo Archon is 'undisciplined', the Geo Archon's strike a possible joke) are her opinions, not confirmed lore.
- The Inazuma volume contains lines that can be read as speaking about the Raiden Shogun and Yae Miko with long acquaintance; note that they come from Alice's narration.
- The Vol. 3 remarks on Tatarasuna and Yashiori Island give an outsider's view of the Tatarigami curse and the miners' chronic illness despite the Shogun's wards.
- The diary mentions a possible coming war in Inazuma and astrologers' predictions; treat that as Alice's wry aside from the time of writing, not a date.
- Where Alice's diary and the places' own entries disagree, prefer the place entries.

## owners
### STANDARD
No individual owner. Alice is the diarist; Lisa gives Vol. 1, Chang the Ninth gives Vol. 2, and Kuroda (and, for a time, Murata) sells Vol. 3.

## gameplay
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
Game note: a three-volume collectible book collection. Vol. 1 comes from Lisa's quest Lost Book, Vol. 2 from Chang the Ninth's quest Justice Is Its Own Reward, and Vol. 3 from the limited-time Hues of the Violet Garden event (2.6) and Kuroda's shop afterwards. No stats.
