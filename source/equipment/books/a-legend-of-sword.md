## summary
### LITE
A Legend of Sword is a six-volume light novel sold in Inazuma through Yae Publishing House. It is a space-war epic with a swordsman strategist, and part of it flashes back to a ronin helping a village against bandits.
### STANDARD +
The wiki credits the pen names Zhenyu (author) and Calx (illustrator), which it identifies as Xingqiu and Albedo. It is a fictional story, not Inazuman history.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Each volume carries a short in-game blurb. The cover is written in the Inazuman script. The series is a collectible book collection, with five volumes sold by Kuroda at Yae Publishing House and one placed in the left-hand room of Yashiro Commission Headquarters.

## lore
### STANDARD
- Vol. 1: Secretary of Defense Faranges doubts Emperor Sher's long galactic war; his sister Princess Gopatha, scarred by the Intergalactic Anti-Guerrilla War, shuts the doubt down and trusts her brother's vision.
- Vol. 2: Admiral Geldafried trusts the Star Fortress Anusharwan, while the rebel pilot Bastinu slips through its vents to strike the core. Both hope to see Gopatha's face at that moment.
- Vol. 3: Ten years earlier, on a world under the Suruga Shogunate and its Tai-Shogun Imagawa Ujizane, the wandering swordsman Bitchuu Kyuubei, called Shinkurou, a former strategist who was framed, walks the land.
### LARGE +
- Vol. 4: the bandits Saitou Onisa (a former ninja) and Yonemata Tasuke (a farmer turned ringleader) wait for villagers to bring in the harvest. The text frames bandits and ninjas as products of war whose power fades in peace.
- Vol. 5: Shinkurou studies the crescent-shaped terrain of Asada Village and plans an ambush from the ridge, but the frightened villagers will not volunteer as bait or burn their own homes.
- Vol. 6: back in space, Shinkurou, now called Semimaru and a captain, recalls that the battle at Asada led to his blinding and imprisonment. After the Imagawa fell, a new Tai-Shogun freed him and sent him to the stars.
### ULTRA +
Details. Vol. 1 names the Intergalactic Anti-Guerrilla War as having cost Gopatha an eye and an arm. Vol. 2 mentions Yazata-class warships and the Lachsz Clipper, and says the fortress was designed by Gopatha and named by Sher. Vol. 3 says the Tai-Shogun's reign of terror began four years after he beheaded the Demon King Danjouchuu. Vol. 6 says the Imagawa clan was beaten by an alliance under the Oome daimyo clan five years before the space scenes, and that Shinkurou's clan and given names had been taken by the Imagawa. His aide Mahasti scolds him for sleeping in meetings, and Allied Fleet Command courts him.

## owners
### STANDARD
No in-game owner. Kuroda sells the volumes at Yae Publishing House. The wiki notes that Sangonomiya Kokomi can be seen reading one of the volumes in an idle animation.

## gameplay
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
Game note: Vol. 6 first became available through Kuroda and Murata during the Hues of the Violet Garden event, after the quest The Five Colors' True Form. The other volumes were part of the Inazuma book set from version 2.0.

### STANDARD
In A Legend of Sword, Shinkurou is the novel's protagonist, a swordsman strategist named Bitchuu Kyuubei. Once a trusted adviser to the Tai-Shogun, he was framed and fled. He wins a battle for Asada Village, is later jailed and blinded, and is eventually freed and given the name Semimaru to serve in a space fleet. He is fiction, not a figure of Inazuman history.
