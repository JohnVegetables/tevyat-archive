## summary
### LITE
A Thousand Nights is a six-volume collection of torn pages found in Sumeru's deserts. Its stories were gathered by a wandering researcher during the Cataclysm and only a fragment survives.
### STANDARD +
Each page is a separate tale: shadowless people and a dream-captor, a mage and a faithless scholar, a prince and a sumpter beast, a researcher cursed with fast thoughts, a dreamer in a palace of mirrors, and a man who chases talking birds.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Torn Pages, not whole books. The in-game description says a wandering researcher walked through rainforest, desert and city in a time of great catastrophe collecting these stories, and that the original held countless tales.

## lore
### STANDARD
- Vol. 1, The Tale of Those Without Shadows: a shadowless people learn from a lost adventurer that shadows hide the secrets of the soul; a young man seeks the dream-captor, learns the taboo of gazing into another's dream, and meets a woman whose shadow is mottled like gemmed drapery.
- Vol. 2, The Tale of the Dastur: a Vahumana scholar is saved in the desert by a mage with amber eyes who teaches him; as he rises to Herbad, sage and Grand Sage he breaks his promises to her, and she returns him to where he began.
- Vol. 3, The Tale of the Prince and the Sumpter Beast: a Port Ormos prince squanders his father's wealth, breaks a witch's rule not to feed or speak to her beast, frees it, and loses a palace that proves to be sand and insects.
### LARGE +
- Vol. 4, The Tale of the Researcher: a haughty, idle scholar asks an injured Jinni to make time just so he can write better theses; his thoughts race while his body crawls, and he can neither write nor speak coherently.
- Vol. 5, The Tale of the Mirror, the Palace, and the Dreamer: a woman dreams for years of a mirrored palace and a masked figure. She becomes a famed magician and finds the palace burned centuries ago; the masked woman in the last intact mirror stabs her and reveals her own face.
- Vol. 6, Tale of the Bird Chaser: a conscripted young man is told by a priestess that she will send talking birds; she dies, the birds bring her taught words to him, and he spends a lifetime chasing them in the forest to catch her voice.
### ULTRA +
Details by volume. Vol. 1: the dream-captor warns that nightmares will drag a repeat intruder into a lightless boundary where only the names of the dead can be heard, and that those names must never be spoken. Vol. 2: the mage's amber eyes see all the living and the dead have seen, including a bronze clock, whales that never left land, a city that exists by silver-mirror moonlight and a tower hung on seven strings; when the Grand Sage threatens her with a bronze jail she tells him to return to his own village, and he finds himself at Caravan Ribat. Vol. 3: the prince, once a Dey's heir, is told by a priest at a mariners' shrine to look for work at an outport market; the beast's palace lasts three years until a scarab servant confesses what the wealth was. Vol. 4: the researcher's only wish was for time to be just, and he ends silent in a moonlit courtyard. Vol. 5: the king in the dream spent two hundred years designing the palace. Vol. 6: the birds were taught countless words on the priestess's deathbed, enough to fill his every night since.

## tale_references
@spoiler: minor
### LARGE
The stories use places and institutions the player already knows: Vahumana, the Akademiya and its ranks, the Temple of Silence, Caravan Ribat, Port Ormos, Jinn, sumpter beasts and Dusk Birds. They echo ideas elsewhere in Sumeru, such as dreams, forbidden knowledge and bargains with spirits, but the pages never connect themselves to a named event and should be read as folk fiction.

## entry: The Tale of the Dastur
@keys: Tale of the Dastur, amber-eyed mage
@spoiler: none
### STANDARD
In A Thousand Nights Vol. 2, a lone Vahumana Dastur is lost in a sandstorm and rescued by a mage with amber eyes who takes him as her disciple in an underground hexagonal library. She asks only that he never deny her. As he becomes Herbad, sage and Grand Sage he refuses her pleas to admit her sister and then her displaced tribe, until she returns him to the desert at Caravan Ribat, where he is a Dastur once more.

## entry: The Tale of the Prince and the Sumpter Beast
@keys: Tale of the Prince and the Sumpter Beast
@spoiler: none
### STANDARD
In A Thousand Nights Vol. 3, a prince of Port Ormos, son of a Dey, spends his inheritance. A priest sends him to a woman who pays ten million Mora if he tends her emaciated sumpter beast for three months without filling it or speaking to it. He speaks, learns the beast was a god cheated by a witch, frees it, and is given a palace and wealth on condition he never asks their source. When he asks, the palace dissolves into sand and scarabs.

## entry: The Tale of the Bird Chaser
@keys: Tale of the Bird Chaser, bird chaser
@keys2: A Thousand Nights, Dusk Bird, talking birds
@spoiler: none
### STANDARD
In A Thousand Nights Vol. 6, a young man goes to war after a priestess says she will send him talking birds. She dies soon after, but the birds she taught keep delivering her words until the war ends. Learning the truth, he spends his life chasing the birds, hoping to catch one that still carries something of her.
