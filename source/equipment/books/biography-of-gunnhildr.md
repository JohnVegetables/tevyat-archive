## summary
### LITE
Biography of Gunnhildr is a Mondstadt history book written by the modern historian Eckhard Gunnhildr. It retells legends of the clan's apical ancestor, Gunnhildr: a chieftain's daughter whose prayer won a wind spirit's protection for her wandering people, and who later honoured Barbatos after the fall of Decarabian's tyranny. It ends by praising the clan's service to Mondstadt today.
### STANDARD +
The book is a quest item found inside Dawn Winery. Its author calls it a collection of fragmentary stories and legends from the aristocratic period, so it is a clan-friendly, partly legendary account rather than a neutral chronicle.

## appearance
### STANDARD
A history book in Mondstadt. Its cover shows the name Gunnhildr in the Teyvat script. The text opens with a blessing to Barbatos and a wish that the wind of freedom prevail in Mondstadt.

## lore
@spoiler: minor
### LITE
Three thousand years ago (the book's figure), Mondstadt was a frozen wasteland and Decarabian, the Lord of the Tower, fought the North Wind King Andrius. Gunnhildr's father, leader of a nomadic clan, had left Decarabian's service because of his despotism and led his people out into blizzards. Gunnhildr prayed, a wind spirit answered, and she gained the power to protect. She later became clan leader and first priestess, and after Barbatos freed the tyrant's subjects she placed laurels on him.
### STANDARD +
- The wind spirit gathered the faith formed by the young girl's sincere plea joined to her people's cry, drew its power from it, built a small shelter for the clan and gave her the power to protect.
- After her father's death she became leader and the clan's first priestess, and she led her people to make a stand during the war between Barbatos and the tyrant.
- Later, once Barbatos had departed, aristocrats with divine power rose across the land. The book says they would turn tyrannical a thousand years on, something no one, not even Barbatos, could foresee.
- Her descendants were a prominent aristocratic clan but, unlike the scheming Lawrence Clan, held to the motto "for Mondstadt, as always". In the later struggle against the Aristocracy they sided with the people, and so were spared the exile imposed on the rest of the aristocrats.
- The author closes by noting that the Gunnhildr Clan supplies many knights and clergy of the Knights of Favonius and expects them to keep serving the Anemo Archon.
### LARGE +
- The book frames the clan's origin as faith turned into power: a child's prayer, joined with a people's cry, became the faith the wind spirit drew on.
- The English text is muddled about who is "Anemo Archon" at the time of the battle (see uncertainties). Use the reading that Barbatos was crowned by Gunnhildr after Decarabian's subjects were freed, and avoid saying Decarabian was an Archon.
### ULTRA +
- Attribution matters in scenes: this is Eckhard's account, written by a Gunnhildr. It praises the clan against the Lawrences and says nothing of doubts or failures, so characters may cite it as the clan's own tradition rather than as proof.
