## summary
### LITE
The Fox in the Dandelion Sea is an 11-part Mondstadt fairy tale told by a hunter. He spares a fox trapped by a Mist Flower, is led to a boundless sea of dandelions, and teaches the fox's child to speak the human tongue in return for the promise of transformation magic. The promise ends with him turned into a dandelion and blown home, and he wakes at the same lake.
### STANDARD +
Fantasy and folklore, not history; the dandelions, wind wishes and the Anemo Archon fit Mondstadt's folk imagery.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Eleven slim volumes with the cover text Foxtale in the Teyvat script. The blurbs repeat the little fox's chant, "Dandelion, dandelion, ride the wind to a faraway land."

## lore
@spoiler: none
### LITE
A hunter near a village finds a white fox with its tail frozen in the ice by a Mist Flower and plans to turn it into stew, but its lake-like eyes make his arrow miss and the fox flees. That night the fox returns, becomes a tall black-haired woman and offers thanks. Days later she leads him through a storm of dandelion seeds to a sea of dandelions and asks him to teach her child human language in return for fox magic.
### STANDARD +
- The hunter learns that foxes who vanish during hunts hide in the dandelion sea. He teaches the little fox language and ordinary skills; the fox wants to become human to befriend a wolf-eyed boy in grey who once drove monsters off.
- The little fox makes a wish to the Anemo Archon by blowing a dandelion, to befriend his teacher. The mother notes the effort of teaching a fox.
- The hunter wants transformation to fly like a falcon, see far places and hunt with magic. As the tale goes on he begins to dread that he will be sent away once lessons end.
- The little fox leaves for the human world, blending into the dandelions. The mother then grows enormous and changes the hunter into a dandelion.
- She chants the same wish, blows the seeds, and he is carried away. A line asks the Anemo Archon to turn foxes into humans so they can be safe from hunters. The hunter wakes in the woods behind his village by the same lake, as if he had dozed off.
### LARGE +
- The recurring images are the lake with eyes like gem fragments, the dandelion seeds, and the line "be good and stay put" from the hunter's grandfather. The hunter also keeps confusing the fox with a girl he loved long ago.
- The ending loops back to the first scene, almost word for word, which makes the whole book read as a circular story.
### ULTRA +
- The text refers to Mondstadt landmarks and ideas: the stained glass of the cathedral, Windrise's plains, the Musk Reef and the Anemo Archon.

## entry: The Dandelion Wish Chant
@keys: Dandelion, dandelion ride the wind, dandelion wish
@keys2: Fox, Anemo Archon, dandelion seeds
### STANDARD
In The Fox in the Dandelion Sea, the little fox blows a dandelion and chants "Dandelion, dandelion, ride the wind to a faraway land," asking that wishes reach the Anemo Archon. It is a fairy-tale device, not a documented Mondstadt ritual.

## entry: The Hunter and the Fox Mother
@keys: Hunter and the Fox, fox mother
@keys2: Dandelion Sea, Mist Flower, fox magic
### STANDARD
The tale's hunter is nameless. The fox mother thanks him for freeing her from the Mist Flower ice, then asks him to teach her child human speech in return for transformation magic. In the end she turns him into a dandelion and sends him home on the wind.
