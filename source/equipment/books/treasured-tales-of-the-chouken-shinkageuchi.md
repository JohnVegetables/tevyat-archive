## summary
### LITE
Treasured Tales of the Chouken Shinkageuchi is a one-volume traditional Inazuman novel sold at the Yae Publishing House in Inazuma City. Also called "Shinkageuchi", it was once restricted. It tells a fantastical story of the first Shogun and her kagemusha, praising the first Shogun's wisdom and the kagemusha's valor.
### STANDARD +
It was published in a haphazard way because another book, informally called "Raiden Reincarnation", made readers hungry for this kind of story.

## lore
### LITE
The book opens with the Goshintou ceremony. When Narukami came to her people she brought the craft of swordmaking; after thousands of years mortal smiths could make swords that pleased her. The Grand Narukami Shrine and the Shogunate therefore gather the finest swords and offer them to the shrine, naming them "Goshintou".
### STANDARD +
Behind the ceremony, the book says, a master smith never forges just one blade. The finest work, the "Shinuchi", is given a lovely name and offered to the smith's lord or deity; it is not meant to take lives and stays pure. The others, "Kageuchi", go to retainers as weapons, are used in bloodshed and become stained.
### LARGE +
It then parallels the swords with the Shogun herself. From the day Narukami Gongen, the First Shogun, ruled Inazuma, she was accompanied by her younger twin sister, "Shin" and "Kage", light and dark. The younger was named Ei, a name that would be written plainly as "shadow", and she would become the successor. After the Archon War, from which only The Seven emerged, the Shadow Shogun, divinely skilled with the blade but doubting she understood mortal hearts, gave up her body so her sister could ascend to Celestia and take dominion over Inazuma. The True Shogun, Makoto, then established the Shogunate and, remembering their bond, restored Ei's divine will and reforged her body, so that her sister returned to her side as her kagemusha.
### ULTRA +
The book is an in-universe novel and idealizes. Per the wiki's note on Ei's voice-over, Ei calls it fairly accurate for the events up to the war against Orobashi and idealized for her motives and actions after that. Treat it as a flattering retelling by a writer, not an official history, and do not use it to settle what the Shogunate or the Narukami shrine later did.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay interpretation (this project's suggestion): use it as a popular, slightly sensational Inazuman novel, and as a source of flavour about Shinuchi and Kageuchi swords. Characters who know the real events (such as Ei) may remark that parts are idealized. Do not claim the novel states the full truth of Makoto and Ei.

## entry: Shinuchi and Kageuchi
@keys: Shinuchi, Kageuchi, Shinkageuchi
@version: 2.2
### STANDARD
In the novel's account of Inazuman swordsmithing, a master smith's finest blade is the Shinuchi, named and offered to a lord or deity and kept pure, while the other blades made at the same time are Kageuchi, given to retainers to use and stained by bloodshed. The novel links the pair of terms to the first Shogun and her shadow sister Ei, and ties it to the Goshintou ceremony.
