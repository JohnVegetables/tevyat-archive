## summary
### LITE
Perinheri is a two-volume Fontaine book, also called 'Hleobranto Innamorato', a legend said to come from Khaenri'ah. It follows the knight Perinheri, his friend and rival Hleobrant, and Angelica, a wandering woman who claims to be a princess.
### STANDARD
Sold by Hubel in the Court of Fontaine. Its author claims a Khaenri'ahn origin that nobody alive can verify, and it has grown over generations as an anthology with footnotes by later editors.

## appearance
### STANDARD
A 3-star Book Collection (2 volumes). Vol. 2 carries two scholar's notes and a list of thanks naming contributors across 15 editions, which gives it the feel of a long-edited text.

## lore
### LITE
In an age when a crimson moon shone over the underground kingdom, an orphanage raised foundlings to be knights. Perinheri, one of its best, and Hleobrant fall out over Angelica, a woman who will marry only the strongest knight; beyond the kingdom's borders Hleobrant is revealed cursed, and Angelica reveals herself as freedom.
### STANDARD
Vol. 1: things from outside the world leak into the Kingdom, and its weapons destroy calamities that slip in. A sage tells the ruler a tale of sea people who honor shipwrecked strangers as possible gods. The Kingdom then founds an orphanage in case a being who transcends the gods arrives; orphans and outside wanderers are accepted too. Perinheri's first memory is being sent through a pitch-dark soot-filled corridor and asked 'Are you dead?', then seeing the crimson moon turn into a horrified eye. The adults tell him he is reborn. The awaited being never came, but many orphans became great knights, and Perinheri led them unless he competed with his best friend Hleobrant, whose rivalry turned deadly.
Vol. 2: Angelica arrives, claiming her priestly line's nation fell to Deus Auri. She will wed only the mightiest knight. Hleobrant is smitten and picks duels; she lists Njord, Alf, Alberich and Perinheri as the strongest. Convinced that killing Angelica would cure Hleobrant's madness, Perinheri hunts her. Outside the borders Hleobrant becomes beast-like; Angelica says it is the curse of forebears who forsook their god, while Perinheri, who drifted in, is free of it. At sunrise she reveals herself as freedom and tells Perinheri it is his to take, and he faces empty land.
### LARGE +
The text says the ocean was used in the Kingdom as a metaphor for space projected by the stars. The editor's footnote on the black wolves of the first edition says archaeology indicates alchemically created warbeasts, mainstays of the Crimson Moon Dynasty that mechanical engineering overtook in the Eclipse Dynasty. The other footnote notes that Liyue's ancient people did not use a name like Angelica, and guesses 'Lady Miaoyin' or 'Tianwang Nu'.
### ULTRA +
Acknowledgements list Mr. Denon's archaeological studies (1st edition), Jean Figeac's refinements which gave the ending (2nd), corrections by two Figeac family members (3rd), a compilation of Karl Ingold's 'Investigations into an Abandoned City' records (13th), Karasawa's natural history help (14th) and Yun's notes on Liyue culture (15th). Wiki trivia suggests that Karasawa may be Eichi Karasawa, an Inazuman scholar turned romance novelist. It also notes the story resembles Orlando Innamorato and Orlando Furioso.

## entry: Hleobrant's curse
@keys: Hleobrant, Hleobranto Innamorato
@keys2: Perinheri, Angelica
@version: 4.5
### STANDARD
In the tale, Hleobrant is Perinheri's best friend and rival. Out of love for Angelica he grows reckless, and outside the Kingdom's borders his speech degrades into howls. Angelica attributes this to a curse on descendants of those who abandoned their god and came to the Kingdom, which she links to the pure-blooded aristocracy. The book does not say what became of him.
### LARGE +
Compare the real-world Orlando legends, where a knight's madness over Angelica is cured by another knight; the wiki treats this as the inspiration.
