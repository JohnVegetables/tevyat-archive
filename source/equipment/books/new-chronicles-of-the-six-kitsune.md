## summary
### LITE
New Chronicles of the Six Kitsune is a six-volume Inazuman novel sold at the Yae Publishing House in Inazuma City. It adapts "Urakusai's Chronicles of the Six Kitsune", a hit about 500 years ago, and follows the tales of Itaru, eldest of the six apprentices of the Great Kitsune Hakushin. Its last volume adds the author's commentary on the Kitsune Saiguu and why she left.
### STANDARD +
The author is unnamed: a writer who, in the Prologue, is talked back into writing for the publishing house by the chief editor (Yae Miko, as the linked text indicates) over drinks at the Uyuu Restaurant.

## appearance
### STANDARD
A set of novels with a cover lettered in the Inazuman script. The wiki reads it as an abbreviation of the Japanese title, which is its own reading and not stated in-game. The volumes are numbered one way in the Archive and another in shops and the overworld.

## lore
### LITE
The Prologue frames the book. An aging writer meets Yae Miko at the Uyuu Restaurant and is persuaded to write again; she notes she was not yet born when "she" (the Kitsune Saiguu) left, and that he is the only one still able to recite the stories she told.
### STANDARD +
Volume 2 explains the title: "new" implies an "old". Urakusai's Chronicles of the Six Kitsune was popular five hundred years ago. The story begins on Mt. Yougou, where the Great Kitsune Hakushin had six skilled apprentices.
### LARGE +
The apprentices were skilled in magic and shape-shifting and helped manage the shrine and defend the mountain. The eldest, Itaru the Black Fox, was broad as a bear and strong as a tiger, and wild. After she wrecked a shintai representing the Shogun while drunk, Hakushin banished her for self-reflection, and she went happily looking for trouble.
- Vol. 3 (the Twin Oni of Tokaku): on the road Itaru meets two armed women in lumberjack clothes, each carrying a nodachi, kodachi and wakizashi. They attack her as a youkai, and she beats them easily, recalling that she had already beaten them the year before for harassing villagers. She recruits them to help people in need. The volume was missing from the original text, was rediscovered later and is presented edited.
- Vol. 4: the trio, resting in Konda Village, meet a mother (surname Youzan) and daughter (given name Yui), musicians from Seirai Island travelling to a festival in Inazuma City. A Lavender Melon seller has tricked them into buying overpriced melons. The hot-tempered sisters want to cut him up; Itaru stops them and goes to talk to him. The wiki notes that in the original 500-year-old tale Youzan and Yui later got their own stories.
- Vol. 5: the seller Dozaemon, a former samurai who turned to extortion in peacetime, is outwitted by Itaru when she orders chopped melon, calls his scale crooked and knocks him down with a bag of Mora. A beating makes tanuki ears appear; he is a tanuki youkai in disguise. Itaru takes his stolen wakizashi, shares his wealth among the villagers and the musicians and spares his life. The text says Urakusai wrote this volume after returning from Liyue.
### ULTRA +
Vol. 6 is the author's commentary on "ancient matters", which the text invites readers to skip. Per the author, Yae told him fragments of history over drinks. The Kitsune Saiguu left Hakushin's lands to serve at the Grand Narukami Shrine before Yae was born, so Yae grew up on stories of her, loved and respected her, and later came to the Grand Narukami Shrine herself. Saiguu doted on Yae because they were close by blood, though Yae speaks little of it. After Urakusai was driven out, Saiguu stopped living at the shrine and settled at Tenshukaku, saying a disaster would engulf heaven and earth and she must be at the Shogun's side. When she left a second time, Yae was a young girl. The Cataclysm then swept the archipelago and the meaning of her words became clear, but too late; Saiguu left a third time, "forever". The author declines to say more about Yae's past, joking about the editor-in-chief's pen.

## owners
### STANDARD
Not a unique item. It is a published book, printed by the Yae Publishing House for general readers.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay (not lore): a collectible book collection. Vol. 1, 2, 3, 5 and 6 are sold by Kuroda at the Yae Publishing House; one volume is in the left-hand room of the Yashiro Commission Headquarters. Added in Version 2.0.

## roleplay
### STANDARD
Roleplay interpretation (this project's suggestion): an Inazuman may know the book as a popular novel with a fox heroine; only readers of the last volume or Yae's close circle know the commentary on the Saiguu. Do not present Itaru, Dozaemon or the Twin Oni as historical figures, and do not add detail to what the Saiguu or Urakusai did.

## entry: Itaru the Black Fox
@keys: Itaru, Black Fox Itaru, Twin Oni of Tokaku
@version: 2.0
### STANDARD
Itaru the Black Fox is the heroine of the novel: the eldest of Hakushin's six kitsune apprentices, huge, strong and unruly, banished from Mt. Yougou after destroying the Shogun's shintai while drunk. She travels as a self-described masterless youkai, takes the Twin Oni of Tokaku into her company and rights wrongs such as the Lavender Melon swindle in Konda Village. She is a character in an adaptation; the book does not say she was a real person.

## entry: Urakusai
@keys: Urakusai, Urakusai's Chronicles
@version: 2.0
### STANDARD
Urakusai is the kitsune writer of the original Chronicles of the Six Kitsune, popular about 500 years ago. Once prized by the Kitsune Saiguu for his writing and tea expertise, he made a grave mistake and was driven out; why is not recorded, though the new author says it may have been linked to the later incursion of the Abyss. Vol. 5 is said to have been written after Urakusai's return from Liyue.
