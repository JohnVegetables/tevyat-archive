## summary
### LITE
A woven scroll in Natlan about the hero Traore. Elder Traore traps the Volcano Lord's arm and pins it with four giant nails, creating the Shadow Pins of the Sulfurous Veins.
### STANDARD
The narrator tells the tale as a storyteller by a bonfire, skipping parts "less important to the listeners". The scroll is found close to Pacal in the Children of Echoes' territory. The wiki notes that the quest Shadows of the Mountains reveals what really happened.

## appearance
### STANDARD
A one-volume woven scroll, quality 4 per the wiki, found near Pacal in the Children of Echoes area.

## lore
### LITE
The Volcano Lord, a black-and-purple salamander-shaped giant, took over the Great Volcano of Tollan and tormented the tribes. Learning it meant to swallow the Children of Echoes' gems, Elder Traore baited it into a trap at the Sulfurous Veins and it lost an arm.
### STANDARD
The Lord fouled the springs of the People of the Springs with smog, sent sparks to the Scions of the Canopy, and swallowed the Collective of Plenty, who escaped only because Tatankasaurs and warriors breached its stomach while it slept. Wanting the gems of the Children of Echoes' valleys to make its dark belly shine, it was overheard by a flying squirrel; the news passed through an aphid, a rhino and a Tepetlisaurus to Elder Traore, whose Saurian companion that was. The Children's strongest warrior and chief Sundjatta had gone with the hero Tenoch to the island in the far west to stop a giant dark beast, so Traore and the guarding warriors asked their Wayob for aid. The Volcano Lord approached through smoke. At the Sulfurous Veins Traore met it, flattered it, and led it to a stone chamber with a human-sized gate and no room for it, saying it could reach in with its hand. To reassure it he entered first. As the Lord's fist closed around the gems and grew too large for the gate, Traore blew a golden whistle. The warriors in ambush called on the Wayob, which borrowed four sewing pins, three short and one long, and blew them at the Lord. The pins took on the tribe's blood and the Wayob's power and became four huge phlogiston nails. Three pinned the black arm to the Sulfurous Veins, and the Lord severed its own arm and fled before the longest could fall.
### LARGE +
The severed arm remained pinned like a shadow, hence the name Shadow Pins for the crystalline pillars. The longest pin never fell fully and hangs above the Sulfurous Veins, and the tale says that while it stays there the Volcano Lord will never dare approach the Children of Echoes again. The narrator notes that old tales confused salamanders with dragons, though everyone now knows a salamander is just that. Traore boasts that his tribe fears death and loves life almost as much as gems, which persuades the Lord that no trap could be intended. The tale does not explain how Traore escaped from the chamber blocked by the arm.
### ULTRA +
Per the wiki trivia, the quest Shadows of the Mountains presents a truer account: Traore escaped with help from the Khaenri'ahn warriors Zephaniah and Irizar, who gave their lives holding back the Abyss so he and his people could flee. In the legend neither is mentioned and the enemy is only the Volcano Lord, so the scroll and the quest differ in what they say happened. This entry has not checked the quest text itself.

### STANDARD
In the legend The Volcano Lord and the Shadow Pins, the Volcano Lord is a dark giant like a black-and-purple salamander that seized the Great Volcano of Tollan and harried Natlan's tribes. It was tricked by Elder Traore, lost an arm to the Wayob's phlogiston nails and fled. The wiki links this being with the Abyss, which the scroll itself does not say.

### STANDARD
In the legend, the Shadow Pins are the long crystalline pillars of the Sulfurous Veins, said to be the phlogiston nails that pinned the Volcano Lord's arm like a shadow. The longest is said to hang suspended, keeping the Lord away from the Children of Echoes. They are a real feature of the area, and the origin story is a legend.
