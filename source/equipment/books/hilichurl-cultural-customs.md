## summary
### LITE
Hilichurl Cultural Customs is a four-volume study by Jacob Musk, a Mondstadt ecologist whom the books call the 'Poet Laureate of Hilichurlian'. It records how hilichurl tribes organise themselves, what they revere and how three tribes of Dadaupa Gorge differ. It is a field scholar's account, often condescending and incomplete.
### STANDARD +
The cover and spine carry the title 'Hilifolk Study' in the Teyvat script. The books are useful for what hilichurls are seen to do, less for why, because Musk could not speak to them.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Four short volumes in an academic, observational tone, each headed by a topic. Vols. 1, 2 and 4 are kept in Mondstadt's library, and Vol. 3 in the Grand Master's office of the Knights of Favonius Headquarters.

## lore
@spoiler: minor
### LITE
Vol. 1 (social structure): hilichurl tribes are small and treated like a large family. The oldest member, the shaman, usually leads, wears a horned smiling mask and chants; but in some tribes a larger, stronger hilichurl with a horned mask takes over, which makes the tribe more aggressive. Resources seem to be shared according to need. Vol. 2 (spirituality): they worship elemental power itself rather than an Archon, and masks and body paint show a member's element. Vol. 3: the huge solitary Lawa are called 'king' but do not lead. Vol. 4: the Meaty, Sleeper and Eclipse tribes of Dadaupa Gorge show cultural diversity.
### LARGE +
- Vol. 1: all members are gatherers and soldiers; those who prize combat become the heavyweights. Musk puzzles over how hilichurls, even shamans, use the elements without Visions, and calls the question one for further analysis.
- Vol. 2: shamans dye their hair and skin with the colour of their element and lead hymns and dances. Leftover meat is left raw on the altar, even though the tribe collects Mora and valuables. Musk says hilichurls seem to have no sense of past or future, do not store food or honour the dead, and that their 'writing' is poor copying of ruin inscriptions. He also notes their odd attachment to ruins as camp sites.
- Vol. 3: Lawa have great strength and elemental control and use the elements to strengthen their bodies. Adventurers meet them rarely and prudently avoid the areas they roam.
- Vol. 4: the Meaty tribe keeps a boar pen with a swineherd, cooks a stew in a pot warmed by a Pyro Slime, and has an arena where challengers who lose are mocked. The Sleeper tribe lives in wooden huts with hide beds and is the cleverest and most cunning. The Eclipse tribe is the most occult, worships a crude eclipsed-sun symbol, has stronger shamans and a grand throne at the centre of its settlement.
### ULTRA +
- Musk is an observer, not a participant: he cites Knights of Favonius patrol reports for resource sharing, and says language barriers and hostility make precise research on distribution impossible.
- Take the claims on hilichurl intelligence and memory as Musk's opinion. The book is one scholar's early field view; the Traveler's voice-over comment on the Eclipse tribe and the Khaenri'ah curse are separate lore.
- Comparison: Vol. 2 says hilichurls revere elements as abstractions, not as the Seven; this matters when a hilichurl is shown near an Archon statue or Mondstadt's religion.
- Vol. 4 is the only volume to name specific tribes; the Eclipse tribe is the one the wiki connects, through a Traveler voice-over, with the Eclipse Dynasty. The book itself makes no such link.

## entry: Hilichurl Shamans and Chiefs
@keys: hilichurl shaman, hilichurl chief, Shaman's staff
@keys2: Hilichurl Cultural Customs, tribe
### STANDARD
Per Musk, the shaman is normally the oldest member of the tribe and the tribe's parent-figure, but a stronger, larger hilichurl can replace him, after which the tribe grows more aggressive. Shamans wear horned smiling masks, carry a staff, chant to themselves and dye themselves in the colour of their element.

## entry: The Lawa
@keys: Lawa, solitary hilichurl
@keys2: hilichurl, Jacob Musk
### STANDARD
Lawa are very large and strong hilichurls who use elemental power to defend and strike harder. The tribe addresses them with a respectful word translating to king or chief, yet they do not lead, preferring to avoid the reverent stares of smaller hilichurls.

## entry: The Dadaupa Gorge Tribes
@keys: Meaty tribe, Sleeper tribe, Eclipse tribe
@keys2: hilichurl, Dadaupa Gorge
### STANDARD
The Meaty tribe feasts, keeps boars and has a combat arena. The Sleeper tribe sleeps in hide-bedded huts and is the most cunning. The Eclipse tribe is the most occult, worshipping a crude eclipsed-sun symbol and enthroning its strongest shaman. Described in Vol. 4.
