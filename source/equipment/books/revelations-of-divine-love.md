## summary
### LITE
A book of poetry, a quest item, given by Hannelore, a Church of Favonius nun at Dornman Port. It is said to be by an ancient Church priest guided by the Anemo Archon.
### STANDARD
Its theme, per the in-game description, is the distinction between the love of the gods and human love. It concludes that compassion, friendship and romantic affection are all imbued with the sacred, since they arise from the same longing for freedom.

## appearance
### STANDARD
A quest item with an icon and description only. No text of the poems can be read in game.

## lore
### LITE
The description presents it as a work from the ancient Church of Favonius that ends by treating all human love as sacred, because it comes from a longing for freedom.
### STANDARD
The Anemo Archon is the god of freedom in Mondstadt, so the book's conclusion ties human warmth to the divine and to freedom alike. The authorship claim is hedged ("said to have been composed"), and no priest is named.
### LARGE +
**How it is given.** At Dornman Port, Hannelore listens to confessions and prays to Barbatos. If the Traveler has used Special Perfume: Wind's Breath and offers her a bottle, she says a sister of the Church cannot accept such gifts, because beautiful things belong to the wind and not to one person. She hands over this book as a small return gift and wishes the Traveler a road of virtue, freedom and compassion. In her ordinary dialogue she explains that Dornman Port's sanctuary was torn down by the Gunnhildr Clan after they threw a bishop sent from Mondstadt out of its tower, to break with the tyrannical Imunlaukr Clan and an aristocracy-bound Church, and that it was never rebuilt.
### ULTRA +
**Limits for portrayal.** Only the description's summary of the book's argument is canon; do not invent verses or a named author. Hannelore's refusal to keep the perfume and her gift of the book are consistent with her stated view that beautiful things belong to the wind. The English title matches a medieval real-world devotional work (wiki naming trivia), which carries no in-world meaning.

## owners
### STANDARD
Hannelore gives it to the Traveler. No other owner is established.
