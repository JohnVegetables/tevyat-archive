## summary
### LITE
The Witch's Revelation Case is a gadget the Traveler receives from the seer-witch Barbeloth in Fontaine. It is the key to the Witch's Revelation system: with it, the Traveler can view and accept revelation quests that unlock extra talent effects for certain characters.
### STANDARD +
Barbeloth carried it herself, and it is said to hold every oracle, whether revealed or not yet awake. It arrives at the end of the world quest Revelations by Chance.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Canon gives no physical description of the case beyond calling it a case that Barbeloth once carried. It is a five-star gadget kept in the inventory rather than placed in the world.

## lore
### LITE
Outside the city of Fontaine the Traveler and Paimon find a fortune-telling booth with a Dodoco in the chair. It is a stand-in through which Barbeloth, a witch of the Hexenzirkel, speaks, joined by her friend Alice. Barbeloth explains that as a Descender the Traveler's fate is not anchored in Teyvat, so most fortune tellers, herself included, cannot read it. She has glimpsed the faces of people tied to an omen, and she wants the Traveler's help gathering them.
### STANDARD +
Alice wants to throw a party for everyone involved and asks the Traveler to bring their friends to the stall. Barbeloth objects to sending invitations herself, since that would disturb the course of fate, but allows that the Traveler already shares ties with these people, so inviting them should have little effect on her divination. Barbeloth says she is still doing research and has only left a Dodoco here, with a reminder set to tell her when the right person arrives. The case comes to the Traveler as the quest reward.
### LARGE +
The item description gives Barbeloth's voice. She says the true colours of fate are not meant to be revealed lightly, and that a future with secrets yet to unfold is more delightful. This matches her manner in the quest, where she is amused and withholding rather than forthcoming. Barbeloth is a member of the Hexenzirkel and a teacher of Mona; her title in the wiki profile is Seer-Witch.
### ULTRA +
The wiki's system page records the promotional line that the witch began a new reading as the warp and weft of fate emerged through an ancient card spread, and ties each revelation to a Lunar Arcanum card (for example Wriothesley with The Emperor, Upright, and Beidou with Strength, Upright). The system was announced together with a new elemental reaction, Stellar-Conduct, in Version 6.7; this archive covers that reaction under its own entry.

## owners
### STANDARD
Barbeloth carried it before the Traveler. Alice helps her with the divination in the quest, but canon does not call the case hers.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Game mechanics (not in-world fact). The Traveler gets the case, along with Primogems, Hero's Wit and Mora, on completing Revelations by Chance; this opens the Witch's Revelation system. Each Witch's Revelation quest belongs to one character. The character must be owned and at level 70 or higher. Completing the quest unlocks an additional talent and enhances some of the character's other talents and constellations.
### LARGE +
Eligible characters on the wiki at the time of access are Wriothesley, Yae Miko, Cyno, Yumemizuki Mizuki, Qiqi, Diona and Beidou. Their passives let them enter a Radiance state under certain conditions.

## entry: Revelations by Chance
@keys: Revelations by Chance
@version: 6.7
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
A Fontaine world quest, starting outside the Court of Fontaine, that follows Song of the Dragon and Freedom. Barbeloth and Alice, speaking through a Dodoco at a fortune-telling booth, ask the Traveler to bring friends tied to Barbeloth's omen. It rewards the Witch's Revelation Case and unlocks the Witch's Revelation system.
