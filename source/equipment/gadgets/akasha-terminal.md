## summary
### LITE
The Akasha Terminal is a small leaf-shaped device that hovers over the user's left ear and links them to the Akasha, Sumeru's shared information network. Concentrate on a subject and the Akasha supplies what the user's clearance allows. It was a daily tool for most of Sumeru until the system was shut down after the events of Chapter III.
### STANDARD +
The Akasha began as Greater Lord Rukkhadevata's creation, built from her Gnosis, and the Sumeru Akademiya's sages expanded it after her death. Few users knew that the network ran partly on the dreams of the people connected to it.

## appearance
### STANDARD
A leaf-shaped earpiece that floats beside the user's left ear instead of being clipped on. Saying the activation phrase, "May the Mighty God bless us with their voice of wisdom", raises a green holographic visor over the eyes. The visor fades when the Akasha is idle and returns when the user calls on it. The visor gives some physical protection and its position can be adjusted, though only people who have read the system manual know how.
### LARGE +
The Neo Akasha Terminal, a modified version made by Lesser Lord Kusanali, looks different from the standard device. Terminals can also glow red when connected to Divine Knowledge Capsules.

## lore
### LITE
The network lets people look up information, send messages and share documents. A user whose clearance is too low simply gets silence.
### STANDARD +
Clearance depends on identity, age, demographic and other criteria canon does not list. The Akasha also carries public announcements, partly replacing bulletin boards, and its reach is strongest in large settlements such as Port Ormos and Sumeru City. Devantaka Mountain is a known dead zone. Sages watch private messages and transfers closely. With enough data the system can model a person's likely behaviour; the Akademiya used it to predict Cyno's investigation path until he learned of it and changed his habits.
### LARGE +
Most of the Akasha's content comes from Knowledge Capsules, made by scholars of Satyavada Life who gain knowledge by linking to Irminsul. New decrees, research and reports are entered in batches on Jnagarbha Day, a recurring deadline for the Akademiya, through the Grand Sage's console above the House of Daena. Heavy use dulls independent thought: knowledge from the Akasha asserts itself as true and is hard to correct. When Azar doubted that Kusanali had escaped, merely checking the Akasha's announcement of her absence made him fail at first to notice her.
### ULTRA +
Rukkhadevata made the first Akasha during the Cataclysm, when forbidden knowledge infected Irminsul. Her power alone could not fix it, so she used her Gnosis to pool the knowledge of all Sumeru's people. Originally only the Archon could operate it. After she died and Kusanali was confined in the Sanctuary of Surasthana, the sages gained permissions over it and so monopolised it, and they promoted the idea that dreams are irrational. People came to treat "outgrowing" dreams as a mark of maturity without knowing the Akasha was responsible. The system quietly harvests users' dreams, called Jnana Energy, as its computing power. Terminals were once limited to citizens and long-term residents; shortly before the Archon Quest the sages extended them to all visitors, which the Archon Quest implies served the Sabzeruz Festival samsara scheme to raise the Wanderer (then Scaramouche) to godhood. After Kusanali was freed she revoked the sages' permissions and halted the system, as it had served its purpose in restoring Irminsul, and she gave up her Gnosis to Dottore. People in Sumeru could dream again.

## owners
### STANDARD
Ordinary Sumeru citizens, residents and, later, visitors wore terminals. Alhaitham, as a Scribe, had read the system manual and knew how to alter the visor and the status signal. Lesser Lord Kusanali (Nahida) is described as the First Akasha Terminal: her consciousness has always been able to connect to the network, so she could watch the outside world through other people's terminals while confined. She can also move consciousnesses, her own and the Traveler's, through the Akasha in ways the sages cannot monitor.
### LARGE +
Her innate link let Dottore trap her consciousness in the Akasha and incapacitate her. To help fight Shouki no Kami, she made a weaponised upgrade, the Neo Akasha Terminal, and used dream simulations of the battle to send combat data to all of Sumeru through their terminals. The Eremites, commoners and some students avoided relying on the Akasha by using banned Knowledge Capsules.

## gameplay
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
Game note, not in-world lore: the terminal is a story device. Players do not equip it.

## entry: Knowledge Capsule
@keys: Knowledge Capsule, Canned Knowledge, Knowledge Capsules, Divine Knowledge Capsule
@version: 3.0
### STANDARD
A Knowledge Capsule holds a fixed amount of information and can be used by anyone with a terminal, bypassing clearance. It is linked to the user's own terminal. Capsules exist for loading data into the Akasha and were meant to be destroyed after Jnagarbha Day, but many leaked into the market over the last century. The Akademiya banned owning, selling or using them, citing harm to users and fear of losing its monopoly on information, and violators can lose their right to study there. A thriving black market exists anyway. Valuable capsules carry more Dendro energy and shine brighter under Elemental Sight, though this is unreliable.
### LARGE +
Divine Knowledge Capsules glow red and reportedly hold the wisdom of the gods, but are made from scholars driven mad by linking their minds to Irminsul, and their users go mad too, as Mizri did. The sages planned to infuse such capsules into the Wanderer (Scaramouche) but were stopped when the Archon was freed and the sages arrested. Kusanali also used capsules to restore the Traveler's and Paimon's dream-memories during the samsara, and later to hold Moseis's consciousness so it could return to his body.

## entry: Jnana Energy
@keys: Jnana Energy, Jnagarbha Day
@version: 3.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Jnana Energy is the name for the dreams the Akasha collects from its users, valued because brain activity is highest during dreams. Few people know about it. It is why Sumeru's people had a reputation for not dreaming. Jnagarbha Day is a separate occasion on which the Akademiya enters new information into the Akasha.
