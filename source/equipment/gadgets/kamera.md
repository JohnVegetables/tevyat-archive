## summary
### LITE
The Kamera is a photographic device that records a picture at the press of a button and prints it on paper. Canon calls it a special item from Fontaine. In Liyue the Feiyun Commerce Guild imports it as a novelty, and the Traveler receives one as thanks for a small errand.
### STANDARD +
Xu of the Feiyun Commerce Guild enjoyed it so much that the Guild offered free portraits as a trial, then took small fees for house calls. Several later quests and events use differently equipped Kameras.

## appearance
### STANDARD
In the inventory it is a four-star gadget. Its in-game description calls it a convenient tool that records and saves images, and says a Traveler roaming the world will have plenty of people and places worth remembering. Pictures come out printed on paper. The wiki's trivia compares its look to a twin-lens reflex camera, which is an out-of-universe observation.

## lore
### LITE
Xu, a member of the Feiyun Commerce Guild at Liyue Harbor, says the Guild recently imported a new product from Fontaine, reportedly very popular there. You press a button and an image appears on a piece of paper. He enjoys it a great deal, and the Guild's young master is convinced it will sell well.
### STANDARD +
Because this would be the first sale of the device in Liyue, the Guild ran a trial in which citizens could have a free photograph taken. It was well received, people offered to pay for the Kamera to be brought to them, and some wanted their pictures framed and delivered. Demand grew daily while the young master was away for several days.
### LARGE +
Overwhelmed, Xu asks the Traveler to deliver two finished pictures, one for Granny Shan and one for Tea Master Liu Su, but he forgot to write names on the backs. The Traveler works out who each is for from the pictures, which are clear enough to show where they were taken. Both customers are pleased, and both muse that they might buy a Kamera themselves. As thanks, Xu gives the Traveler a Kamera and says it is only a sample from partners in Fontaine, differing somewhat from the official model but with all basic functions; he hopes the Traveler will help advertise it.
### ULTRA +
Xu also muses on how people in Fontaine managed to build something so clever. Canon never names the inventor. Later content treats the Kamera as a settled part of Fontainian life: quests there give special models to the Traveler, for example a modified one from the photographer Bresson (4.0) and a Lepine-Pauline model for a Phantom Blubberbeast task (4.2). Use these as flavour for how common photography is in Fontaine, not as an origin story.

## owners
### STANDARD
The Traveler holds a Kamera from the Snapshots quest. Paimon also has her own, which appears in later events. Many variants are loaned or given to the Traveler for specific quests or events (see the variants entry).

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Game mechanics (not in-world fact). The Kamera is a reusable Quick Use gadget with no cooldown, obtained from the Liyue world quest Snapshots (Adventure Rank 25 required). Using it takes an instant picture of the current screen that can then be saved or discarded. It works in moments when Photo Mode is unavailable, including while Elemental Sight is active, during combat, gliding, falling and swimming, so it can catch actions such as combat talents. It lacks Photo Mode's camera, expression and pose adjustments and cannot be used in Domains.
### LARGE +
On PC, saved screenshots go into a ScreenShot folder in the game's install directory.

## entry: Snapshots
@keys: Snapshots quest, Feiyun Commerce Guild Kamera
@version: 1.2
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
A Liyue world quest, started by talking to Xu at Feiyun Slope near the northern waypoint of Liyue Harbor, for Adventure Rank 25 and above. The Traveler delivers two Kamera Pictures to Granny Shan and Tea Master Liu Su and reports back. The reward is 20,000 Mora, two Hero's Wit and a Kamera.

## entry: Kamera Gadget variants
@keys: Kamera variants, Kamera Gadgets, Special Kamera, Kurious Kamera, Bresson's Special Kamera, Image Sampling Kamera, Forensic Imaging Device, MAAN-Kamera
@version: 1.3
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
The wiki groups many reusable gadgets as Kamera Gadgets. Some are tied to the event that released them and expire with it. Examples from item text: the Kurious Kamera and Special Kamera (1.3, Fontainian models; the former, used for the Five Flushes of Fortune event, was imported by Ji Tong but does not seem to work as expected); the Exquisite Kamera (3.0), which Razi borrowed from Lord Sangemah Bay in the Graven Innocence event; the Windblume Kamera (3.5), borrowed from Fassben; Bresson's Special Kamera (4.0), a custom-built model made from the best materials; the Image Sampling Kamera (4.2), modified by Lepine-Pauline for a "Graph Adversarial Technology" task; Asagiri's Dedicated Fontaine Kamera (4.8), for gathering inspiration on a trip to Fontaine; the Graffiti Kamera (5.0); the Forensic Imaging Device (5.8), which compares past and present data to find clues; and the MAAN-Kamera (7.0), provided by Chichikova for the Mutual Aid Advancement Network.
### LARGE +
Further variants include the Shutterflash Kamera (fitted with Lepine-Pauline's lens for freezing fast motion), the Memento Kamera (an antique said to have belonged to a legendary Fontainian photographer), the Spotter Kamera (Paimon's own, used to record differences in the Homeward, He Who Caught the Wind event) and Paimon's Kamera from Wayfarer's Whispers (5.8). Scenes drawn from these were tied to events, so treat them as limited-time material.
