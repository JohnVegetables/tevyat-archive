## summary
### LITE
The Windsong Lyre is a plucked, lyre-style instrument from Mondstadt that the Traveler can play as a gadget. Its in-game text calls it a traditional Mondstadt instrument of fine workmanship, made for able hands. It first appeared in Version 1.4 during the Invitation of Windblume event.
### STANDARD +
Mondstadt is a nation of poetry and song, and the lyre stands for that tradition. Outside the story it is a performance tool: the player plays notes by key, click, tap or button.

## appearance
### STANDARD
A hand-held stringed lyre in Mondstadt style. Canon text gives no detailed description of its look beyond calling it a work of fine craftsmanship.

## lore
### LITE
The item text says Mondstadt has a long-standing tradition of poetry and song, and so a long history of building instruments for it. Bards are proud of their songs, and the text imagines the instruments being proud of their owners and that tradition.
### LARGE +
The lyre ties into the Windblume Festival, Mondstadt's festival of wind and freedom, because it was released with the Invitation of Windblume event. Its source there was a music challenge called Ballads of Breeze.

## owners
### STANDARD
No character is stated by canon to own this lyre. It is a player-facing gadget the Traveler can buy and play.

## gameplay
@availability: limited
@version: 1.4
### STANDARD
Game mechanics, not story. It is a reusable, Quick Use music gadget. Its range runs from C3 to B5 with no sharp or flat notes, and notes are labelled with solfège syllables (do, re, mi and so on). Several notes can sound together, but a note cannot be held, as with a plucked instrument. Controls are via keyboard rows, mouse or touch, or controller buttons. The game shows a notice asking players to perform original work or work they hold rights to.
### LARGE +
It was first obtained during the Invitation of Windblume event: the event shop sold it for 280 Festive Tour Tickets once all Ballads of Breeze challenges were completed in Normal Mode. Players who missed the event can buy it from Marjorie, who runs the With Wind Comes Glory souvenir shop in Mondstadt, for 150,000 Mora.
### ULTRA +
On PC keyboard the three note rows (treble, alto and bass) map to the Q-U, A-J and Z-M keys; with a PlayStation controller the middle row is shown by default and the L1 and R1 buttons switch to the lower or higher rows. The wiki lists the gadget's Chinese name as a phrase meaning a stringed instrument of wind scenery's poetry.
