## summary
### LITE
The Eye of Graeae is a palm-sized mechanism that often takes the form of a monocle. The Traveler finds it in an abandoned train in Snezhnaya, where it wakes, recognises them as a Descender, and agrees to serve them. In Snezhnaya it lets its user shape Prime Ice, protects against the killing cold by burning Kresnik Energy, calls a train, and speaks as a polite, forgetful voice that is trying to recover its lost power.
### STANDARD +
It carries a fragmentary mind that was made, by its own account, within the Hvergelmir project. Over the Snezhnaya story it moves from a helpful gadget to a character with its own mission, and the story ends with the device keeping its functions but losing that voice.

## appearance
### STANDARD
In the inventory it is a quick-use gadget, described as a strange mechanism from an abandoned station that "often appears in the form of a monocle" and seems to have many convenient functions. On the Traveler's train it also sits on a pedestal at the front of the carriage, where it is used to set a destination.
### LARGE +
Its readouts appear on the HUD as an orange Kresnik Energy bar above the active character's health bar. The gadget screen also shows the character's stats and current firearm loadout.

## lore
@version: 7.0
@spoiler: major
### LITE
The Eye was left in an abandoned train in Snezhnaya, untouched through the cataclysm. When it wakes, its memories are broken and its power is scattered across the tundra. It senses the pieces in three places and asks the Traveler for help, as the one it has "signed a contract to serve".
### STANDARD +
Its message log is also a window onto Snezhnaya. It explains the four cold zones (normal, low, frigid and subzero), which Kresnik's Torches created by warming parts of a land that was once only frigid and subzero. It tells how trains were first conceived for the needs of Hvergelmir's Shadow Realm exploration, and that the technology became public after the Tsaritsa took the throne. It explains that the Shadow Realm firearms were designed for fighting where living beings cannot use elemental power, and that Hvergelmir researchers modelled them on Hyperborean energy-storage devices.
### LARGE +
Its own account in the Pale Crown Palace quest is that Aksinya and Hvergelmir made it, and that it was divided into three segments before the cataclysm to create the first "prototype human", with the aim of bringing humanity to "Tomorrow". It realised that if the pieces fused and gained the authority of the Primordial Human, this would threaten humans and fae. It asked to be divided and persuaded Koshchei, Pavlina and Alvis to stop Aksinya finishing the experiment. They did, for their own reasons, and Aksinya was cast into the Shadow Realm.

The Traveler recovers the three fragments from the House of Hesperides, Flamefeather Valley and the Pale Crown Palace. Aksinya takes the restored Eye in the Court of Eternal Bliss, and the Primordial Human is awakened. At the end the Eye asks the Traveler what they would do if their sibling became a different person. The Traveler says they would learn to love who that person became. The Eye asks to be stopped along with Aksinya, and both fall into the Shadow Realm.
### ULTRA +
The Eye's messages add commentary on the people tied to its fragments: it says Pavlina was a major Hvergelmir researcher whose Prime Ice work built cold laboratories and preserved Hyperborean finds, and it doubts it can be certain whether Aksinya created it. About the Flamefeather Valley rite, it says it feels it has seen the relief before. Of Koshchei it says he turned his life into a near-eternal form because its fragment alone could not carry his full transcendence, and it sounds sorry for him. These are the Eye's own reconstructions, not neutral history.

## owners
### STANDARD
The Traveler carries the Eye as a standard gadget. Paimon is present for most of its story and helps them search for its power. Aksinya takes the restored Eye in the Court of Eternal Bliss.

## gameplay
### LARGE
Gameplay description, not story. The gadget works only in Snezhnaya.
- Fab-Vision harvests Prime Ice and builds or removes Prime Ice Constructs in the open world, usable while running, falling and in combat but not while gliding or swimming.
- It stores Kresnik Energy (up to 100%) and Prime Ice Resource (up to 400). Both are spent building constructs; Kresnik Energy is also drained to prevent cold damage, and drains quickly in frigid and subzero zones.
- It calls the Traveler's train near any railway track.
- It shows the character's stats and firearm loadout for the Shadow Realm third-person shooter mode.
- It keeps the message log and a Prime Ice Construct archive.

## entry: Eye of Graeae Messages

@version: 7.0
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
The message feature unlocks after the Archon Quest "Gunfire in the Silent Lands". New texts arrive as "a new message from the Eye of Graeae" and cover quest background and exploration tips. Examples are the cold zones, the train and its history, the Shadow Realm firearms, Snegovik and Aksinya. Most appear only after specific quests. The Eye says its information comes from a database fed by an uploader named Hvergelmir, which it suspects is not a real name.

## entry: Fab-Vision and Kresnik Energy
@keys: Fab-Vision, Kresnik Energy, Prime Ice Resource
@version: 7.0
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
Fab-Vision is the gadget's construction mode: tap to harvest Prime Ice or build and remove constructs from blueprints. Constructing needs Kresnik Energy and Prime Ice Resource. Kresnik Energy also guards against the cold and runs out fast in the harshest zones. The Eye once noted unusual energy fluctuations when constructs are condensed, and speculated, flagged as speculation, that condensing gives form to a possibility that also exists in many other worlds.
