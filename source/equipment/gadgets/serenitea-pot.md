## summary
### LITE
The Serenitea Pot is a small teapot the adeptus Madame Ping gives the Traveler in Liyue Harbor as thanks for helping save the city. Inside is a private pocket world made with the adeptal art of Sub-Space Creation, where the Traveler can build a house, garden and grounds and rest from the road. It is kept by a courteous finch-like teapot spirit named Tubby, who calls herself a butler.
### STANDARD +
The teapot is placed on the ground like a gadget and entered by interacting with it. The realm cannot become a full world: Tubby says it is only a moment of respite from the mortal realm, not a way of escaping it. Over time it becomes a home that the Traveler can fill with furniture, buildings and animals, share with visiting friends, and, later, with the characters they have met on their travels.
### LARGE +
Its Chinese name is read as "Ode-of-Dust Teapot"; the same words appear in Madame Ping's adeptus name in Chinese. English and several other translations play on "tea" and "serenity", which is where the English name comes from.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Seen from outside, it is a teapot carried in the inventory and set down on a surface. Its item text calls it a teapot from Madame Ping with "a whole realm within". Inside, the Traveler first finds a large house at the centre of otherwise empty floating ground, which the player then builds out.
### LARGE +
Tubby appears as a small floating blue finch with orange and white stripes around her beak, and her look changes at higher Trust Ranks (4, 7 and 10). Realm layouts include floating islands, a mountain peak, an island chain in water, and layouts themed on other nations. Tubby remarks that the realm has clear, sunny skies all year, and says it could reproduce the weather outside but that she simply likes dozing in the sun.

## lore
### LITE
Madame Ping used Sub-Space Creation, an adeptal ability she says was a blessing from Rex Lapis, to make a small autonomous world. The Traveler first walked through one such teapot in the Archon Quest, when she tested them while they collected the Cleansing Bell. After the Osial crisis she learned the Traveler camped outdoors and prepared a gift, with Yanfei running errands for the materials. Tubby, the teapot spirit, introduces the realm.
### STANDARD +
Ping says a teapot is a small trick compared with Rex Lapis, who moved mountains and seas; an adeptus of her sort must leave the real world to make a world of their own. Tubby explains that adepti create such realms but seldom have time to maintain them, so teapot spirits were created to guard realms and run their affairs. Anything built inside comes from blueprints and materials, shaped by thought, and the Traveler can refashion the realm as they please.

### LARGE +
Tubby also states the limits: Sub-Space Creation is a product of adeptal power and has bounds, and the realm is not a true world, so a whole city cannot be built there. A golden-eyed adeptus once taught her this. She cannot remember who he was and has not seen him since.

After the companion quest, Tubby explains that adepti usually seal their abodes away from the world and issue a Realm Dispatch to those they invite. A dispatch carries the signature of the abode's adeptal energy and its owner, and lets the holder come and go. Before that step the Traveler's abode was incomplete, and guests could only enter and leave with the Traveler.

Ping's part of the story: Smaragdus Jadeite, an adepti treasure that is harmful to humans after long contact, was needed to finish the teapot, along with Shimmersoil from Dihua Marsh. Both had become hard to find; The Chasm had been sealed off and Blackcliff Forge had stripped its mines. Yanfei, whom Ping says counts as an adeptus (her father is one), tracks down a Snezhnayan merchant, Krosl, whose jadeite hairpins were rented to a woman named Zhiqiao. The quest becomes a contract and affidavit problem solved with Yanfei's legal skill, and ends with the jadeite delivered to Ping.

Sub-Space Creation can give objects special properties: soil with unlimited nutrition, fish ponds that stay clear and warm, hot springs that keep their temperature. Even so, canon says it cannot restore plants that went extinct after the death of the Goddess of Flowers.
### ULTRA +
Teapot spirits are used by adepti to look after spaces made with Sub-Space Creation. Only two are named in the canon read here: Tubby (adeptus name "Rain Calmer") and Chubby ("Jade Seeker"). Tubby says her title comes from a time of unending battle, when great storms swept the land and neither sun nor moon shone; Ping and their old friends longed for sunshine and gave her the name. She says this is why she always takes the colour of a blue sky, whatever form she takes. Ganyu calls her Rain Calmer in some dialogue, which suggests they met earlier.

In Version 3.7's "A Guest From Liyue", Tubby shows she is not bound to the teapot: sent by Ping, she remodels a room in The Cat's Tail, Mondstadt, tied to the Genius Invokation TCG invitation board, so invited friends can appear without a long journey. She says Chubby, whom she calls "Jade Seeker", travels far and wide to gather rare treasures, whereas she rarely leaves the realm.

## owners
### LARGE
The Traveler owns the pot. Ping is its maker and giver, and Tubby runs it. The Traveler can invite the playable characters they have obtained to live there, and other players can visit as guests. Characters in the realm generate Companionship EXP over time; some favour particular furniture sets and give a one-time reward when invited into them. The Traveler cannot be invited as a companion.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay description, not story. The Serenitea Pot is the player-housing system.
- It unlocks at Adventure Rank 28 after "A New Star Approaches", through the world quest "A Teapot to Call Home" (Part I in Liyue Harbor, Part II inside the realm).
- The Traveler builds from furnishings made with blueprints and materials, limited by a Load meter.
### LARGE +
- Trust Rank 1 to 10 rises by crafting each new furnishing for the first time, and unlocks more areas, creation queue slots, Realm Currency and Companionship EXP storage, extra realm layouts, and the number of companions who can live there (up to 8).
- The Jar of Riches turns the realm's Adeptal Energy into Realm Currency, spent at the Realm Depot and on Chubby's goods.
- Chubby visits from Friday to Sunday; guests can buy from him on Saturday and Sunday.
- In Co-Op, friends can visit regardless of World Level, speed up crafting, and use most interactive furnishings, but cannot edit the realm.
Layouts listed by the wiki as of 7.1: Floating Abode, Emerald Peak and Cool Isle available early, then Silken Courtyard, Sublime Spicewood, Swirling Isles (4.3), Tletl Ilhuicatl (5.3) and Shadowflit Archipelago (Nod-Krai era). Later ones unlock through quests and the Realm Depot, and each comes with a themed mansion.

## entry: Tubby
@keys: Tubby, Teapot Spirit Tubby, Rain Calmer
@version: 1.5
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
Tubby is the Teapot Spirit who runs the Realm Within: a small floating blue finch with an orange and white beak, who speaks politely and calls herself a butler to the realm. She gives access to Trust Rank, furnishing creation, realm style and the Realm Depot. She mentions Tianheng Bohea, Yunlai white tea and Bishui green tea among her centuries-old stock, enjoys dozing in the sun, and says she has an adeptus name, "Rain Calmer", which she struggles to recall. She remembers a golden-eyed adeptus but not his name.
### LARGE +
Her talk changes with Trust Rank: at low ranks she speaks of settling in and of choosing auspicious days to build, at higher ranks she invites the Traveler to sit and share tea, and at Rank 10 she tells the story of her name.

## entry: Chubby
@keys: Chubby, Teapot Traveling Salesman, Jade Seeker
@version: 1.5
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
Chubby, the Teapot Traveling Salesman, is a second teapot spirit with the adeptus name "Jade Seeker". He appears in the realm from Friday to Sunday and sells furnishings (animals, Sub-Space Boulders and Radiant Spincrystals) for Realm Currency; how many goods depend on Trust Rank. Tubby says he travels widely for rare treasures and sometimes gives her rare flower teas.

## entry: Sub-Space Creation
@keys: Sub-Space Creation, Waijing
@version: 1.5
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
Sub-Space Creation is the adeptal ability to create a small autonomous pocket world through imagination. Ping says it was part of the illumination adepti received as a blessing from Rex Lapis. It can make teapot realms like this one and larger abodes. Its limit is that such a world is not true: it offers respite, not escape.

## entry: Realm Dispatch and companions
@keys: Realm Dispatch, Idle Teapot Talk, Realm Bounty
@version: 1.5
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
After "Idle Teapot Talk", Tubby gives the Traveler a Realm Dispatch, modified so that a light tap on a friend's palm invites them. Invited playable characters live in the realm and produce Companionship EXP (Realm Bounty). Up to 8 can live there at the highest Trust Rank.

### STANDARD
In the Archon Quest, Zhongli sends the Traveler to Madame Ping for the Cleansing Bell used in the Rite of Parting. Ping transports them into her magic teapot as a test, revealing herself as an adeptus. After Osial's defeat she prepares the Serenitea Pot as a gift.
