## summary
### LITE
The Wind Glider is a pair of wing-like frames that lets a person glide on the air. In Mondstadt, the city of wind, gliding is a beloved skill: the Knights of Favonius' Outriders use gliders constantly, and Amber gives the Traveler their first pair, the Wings of First Flight, when they arrive in the city.
### STANDARD +
The glider is not an ordinary machine. Lore says it works through the Anemo Archon's blessing rather than by physics alone. Mondstadt treats gliding as something to be taught, licensed and taken seriously, yet also as a romantic tradition.

## appearance
### STANDARD
A stylized set of wings worn on the back and opened mid-air. The Wings of First Flight is the default, described in-game as a stylized wind glider that carries the passionate longing of an Outrider. Many other designs exist, but they are cosmetic and each character can use whichever is chosen.

## lore
@version: 1.1
### LITE
Gliders come from Mondstadt. The Wings of First Flight were a pair Amber had long saved for a promising junior knight; she gave them to the Traveler, an outlander she chose to believe in.
### STANDARD +
Outriders use their gliders heavily, and the wings wear out. Amber's flying style is so hard on them that she went through more than ten pairs, but she never used the special pair she saved. Gliding in those days was especially dangerous because of the gale-swept weather around Mondstadt. Beginners are said to learn in Springvale, because its buildings have only two or three floors.
### LARGE +
Leonard, an adventurer who lived long ago, is credited with inventing the glider. His journal says his lifelong goal was to climb Pilos Peak, which later became Musk Reef; the wiki says he made the glider to help other explorers succeed where he had failed. The invention story comes through the Unreconciled Stars quest line.
### ULTRA +
The Wings of the Forest, a Sumeru reputation reward, tells a legend about a master Firnas, not the present Akademiya lecturer, who noticed that gliders break physical law and rely on the Anemo Archon's blessing. He tried to build one that flew by natural law alone. In a dream the Dendro Archon told him a story, and he afterwards accepted that the gods' blessings are part of the world's laws. The text presents this as a story told in Sumeru, not as verified history.

## owners
### STANDARD
Amber, an Outrider, owns and wears out many gliders. The Traveler receives the Wings of First Flight from her in the quest City of Freedom, and Paimon accompanies them while they learn.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Game mechanics, not story. Pressing jump in mid-air opens the glider; pressing it again, or a plunging attack, ends the glide. Gliding costs stamina and negates fall damage. Characters cannot use normal combat actions while gliding, with some exceptions. Anemo rings and updrafts boost a glider. Glider designs are only cosmetic.

## entry: Gliding License
@keys: gliding license, gliding licence, gliding exam, gliding manual
@version: 1.0
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
In the story quest Glide With the Wind, Amber tells the Traveler that gliding legally in Mondstadt needs a license from the Knights of Favonius, who set strict rules and an exam. She acts as examiner, since she gave the Traveler the glider, and says Acting Grand Master Jean would hold her responsible. The Traveler and Paimon had already been gliding all over without one. The manual states rules such as one person per glider, a weight limit and no take-off using an Anemo Slime. It is written as a gentle fable: birds without ways to fly ask the Anemo God for help and learn that courage, not stronger wind, is what they lacked. The quest continues with License to Glide.
### LARGE +
The license is a Mondstadt rule and a good example of Knights bureaucracy. It does not limit the player anywhere in the game.
