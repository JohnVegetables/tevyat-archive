## summary
### LITE
Moonfall Silver is a silver crystal as pure and pristine as moonlight, a local specialty of Nod-Krai. Canon says it has almost perfect elemental affinity, which is why Nod-Krai craftspeople treat it as an excellent elemental conductor. It is found on Hiisi Island.
### STANDARD +
Its name comes from Frostmoon Scions tradition, which held that the crystals were a gift from the moon. Older Snezhnayan chronicles tell a different, much older story about silver woven by the Frostmoon.
### LARGE +
It is also an object of local enthusiasm and folklore: a collector in Nasha Town runs a small appreciation event for it, and a Nod-Krai folk tale uses it as the material of an imaginary gown.

## appearance
### STANDARD
A silvery crystal with an iridescent white sheen. Dorota, a collector in Nasha Town, praises its flawless form, smooth rounded texture and unrivaled elemental affinity. Even good samples can hide tiny flaws that only a careful eye notices.

## lore
### STANDARD
The item text gives two traditions. In the legacy of the Frostmoon Scions, the crystals were born five hundred years ago alongside the Moon Maiden, and the Moonchanter of that time believed them a gift from the moon, hence the name. Yet older documents by Snezhnayan chroniclers say the descendants of the golden realm (Hyperborea) were already using secret silver woven by the Frostmoon thousands of years earlier. The text says plainly that how these two tales connect is unclear.
### LARGE +
Linnea, the Adventurers' Guild advisor, tells a folk tale in the Mineral Investigation Special Lecture. A fae governor of Nod-Krai who loved rare clothes hired a young tailor in black who claimed to spin moon-glow crystals into thread. The tailor took a large quantity of Moonfall Silver and said he had woven a gown too pure for a fool or unremarkable man to see. The governor pretended to see it and paraded in it until a child pointed out he wore nothing. By the tale, the tailor was Reed Miller in disguise, who sold the silver and gave the Mora to poor townsfolk. Linnea believes real garments of Moonfall Silver once existed and the weaving art is lost, and mentions a separate tale, "The Fisherman and the Beast", that also has a moonlight gown but that she holds back from the children present.
### ULTRA +
In the world quest Mineral Research: Moonfall Silver, Dorota hosts a Moonfall Silver Appreciation Event in Nasha Town and quizzes Linnea on which of three samples is flawless, leading to a Selected Moonfall Silver kept for exhibition. In the lecture, Maisie votes Moonfall Silver the most beautiful rock in Nod-Krai, while Mikko dismisses it as a stone that looks good but does little. Linnea replies that it has quite a few uses, though less common in daily life than Rainbowdrop Crystals.

## owners
### STANDARD
No notable owner. Collectors such as Dorota in Nasha Town keep prized samples, and Nod-Krai craftspeople use it as an elemental conductor.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Game mechanics, not lore: a Nod-Krai local specialty added in Version 6.0 (Luna I). It is gathered on Hiisi Island, especially between Frostmoon Enclave and the Light-Bathed Platform, and in Silvermoon Hall, and is used for crafting and character ascension.
