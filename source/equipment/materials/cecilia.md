## summary
### LITE
The Cecilia is a beautiful flower of Mondstadt that grows only where harsh winds blow. It is a Mondstadt local specialty, gathered on Starsnatch Cliff, and is the favourite flower of Venti the bard.
### STANDARD +
Its item text says the flower has a name that suits its appearance and is as intangible as the true heart of an unbound soul. In the language of flowers it stands for the true feelings of the prodigal son, according to a Paimon questions feature.
### LARGE +
Cecilias are a recurring emblem of Mondstadt's freedom theme. Venti wears one in his hat and decorates his lyre, Der Frühling, with it, and the Nameless Bard wore a three-petaled one on his vest. During the Invitation of Windblume event the Traveler could choose a Cecilia as their Windblume.

### ULTRA +
It has been in the game since Version 1.0. The name is rendered Cecilia in English and Cécilia in French.

## appearance
### STANDARD
A delicate wild flower that grows in clusters on windswept ground and appears on Venti's Azure Sky namecard. The Nameless Bard's version had three petals.

## lore
### STANDARD
Cecilias grow only in the high, windy places of Mondstadt, and canon ties them to freedom and wind. A vanished civilisation that lived at Cecilia Garden grew them in greenhouses.
### LARGE +
The ruins near Cecilia Garden show structure and ornament that suggest some link to Decarabian, though canon says no more. Whether the flower is named for a person is not stated.

## owners
### STANDARD
Venti is the character most associated with Cecilias; the Nameless Bard is shown wearing one.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay note: a local specialty found on Starsnatch Cliff, given by a few NPCs after dialogue, sold in shops, and grown via Luxuriant Glebe gardening.
