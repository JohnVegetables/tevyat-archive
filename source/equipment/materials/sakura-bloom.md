## summary
### LITE
Sakura Bloom is a petal that has fallen from the Sacred Sakura of the Grand Narukami Shrine, and a local specialty of Inazuma found on Narukami Island. Electro energy keeps the petals floating in mid-air.
### STANDARD +
Item text says the blossoms are filled with a fondness for Inazuma, and attaches to them local legends about the Thunder Sakura.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Clusters of pale petals drifting in the air rather than lying on the ground, found around Narukami Island and especially near Mt. Yougou and the Grand Narukami Shrine. They only come together into a collectable bloom when touched by Electro.

## lore
### STANDARD
The description calls them petals from the Sacred Sakura at the Grand Narukami Shrine, suspended by Electro. It adds that, according to legend, the roots of every Thunder Sakura join deep underground, and that following them always leads back to this sacred tree standing guard over Inazuma.
### LARGE +
A second legend in the same text holds that the Thunder Sakura are parts of the Almighty Narukami Ogosho, who divided her divine form so as to defend those under her rule more fully. The text presents this as one folk account among others, not as established fact.

## owners
### STANDARD
No notable owner. It belongs to the shrine's sacred tree and is gathered freely by travellers.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Game mechanics, not lore: an Inazuma local specialty added in Version 2.0, found on Narukami Island and used for cooking and character ascension. To harvest a floating cluster, a player must use an Electro ability, touch it while Electro-affected, or carry an Electrograna. In Japanese the name is written with an older character form for sakura, a detail noted by the wiki.
### LARGE +
Its Japanese name is Hiou-kyuu (緋櫻毬), roughly "scarlet cherry ball", written with an older form of the sakura character. The Chinese name 绯樱绣球 is read by the wiki as most likely meaning an embroidered ball too, though the same word can also mean hydrangea.
### ULTRA +
Many of the blooms lie around Mt. Yougou and the Grand Narukami Shrine, so the specialty is spatially bound to the shrine whose tree the text names as its source. Wiki farm guides count 55 blooms in total on the map.
