## summary
### LITE
Mora is the common currency of Teyvat, accepted in every nation. It takes its name from Morax, the Geo Archon, whom Liyue calls Rex Lapis. All of it is minted at the Golden House in Liyue. Loading-screen text calls it the one language everybody speaks.
### STANDARD +
Mora is also described as a kind of catalyst: a medium of physical transformation, which is why so many tasks, such as crafting and strengthening equipment, can only be done by spending it.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Mora comes as small coins stamped with a triquetra, a triangular symbol. Both faces are identical, so a coin cannot be flipped without marking one side. People in Teyvat still settle disputes by flipping a coin and still say "heads" and "tails", even though neither side has a head. Larger sums are carried in bags; a huge bag of gold coins appears as a quest item, and Childe's high-value coins could be redeemed for smaller change at the Northland Bank.

## lore
@version: 1.0
### STANDARD
In-game text calls Mora the currency accepted worldwide, whose value and standing nobody disputes. All of Teyvat's Mora is minted at the Golden House on Mt. Tianheng in Liyue, and the currency is named after the Geo Archon Morax. In Liyue he is called Rex Lapis, and Paimon notes it is considered impolite for outsiders to use the name Morax there.
### LARGE +
Zhongli said in the Archon Quest that making Mora requires the Geo Archon's power. Having chosen to walk the earth as a mortal, he lived by mortal rules and carried no Mora of his own, and he admitted he lacked the Mora for a pricey Liyue Harbor venue. After the Geo Archon handed his Gnosis to the Tsaritsa, Zhongli told the Traveler and Paimon that existing Mora would not vanish, but the Golden House would have to cease operations for a long time. He left the resulting financial problem to the Liyue Qixing.

Neuvillette, in the Fontaine quest A Gift From the Sea Spirits, recalled puzzling over why humans used the flesh and blood of a god as a medium of exchange, asking whether pebbles or seashells would not serve. Virda suggested stones and shells break easily; Neuvillette remarked that gods do not seem sturdier. This is his view as an outside observer of human customs, not an explanation of how Mora is made.
### ULTRA +
The Fond Farewell belongs to Archon Quest Chapter I Act III, A New Star Approaches. The wiki records the Golden House as no longer minting new Mora after that act. Nothing found through Version 7.1 reverses this, but canon never says how much Mora is in circulation or how long the shortage lasts.

## spread
@version: 1.0
### LARGE
Mora is used in every nation. In Liyue, the Feiyun Commerce Guild's heavy bag of it was enough to buy every Cor Lapis available in the region. The Northland Bank, a Snezhnayan institution with branches across Teyvat, is described as harnessing the flow of Mora; the Fatui use bank funds when operating abroad. Branches exist in Liyue and in Fontaine's Court of Fontaine.

## gameplay
### LARGE
Gameplay note, not in-world lore: Mora is the main currency for shops and is spent when levelling characters, ascending, enhancing weapons and artifacts, and crafting. Sources include quests, chests, domains, Blossoms of Wealth, enemies, events and expeditions. Wiki trivia ties this to the in-world idea that the weapons consume Mora as a catalyst.

## scene_use
### LARGE
Use Mora as the ordinary way anyone pays for anything, from a Liyue Harbor stall to a Fontaine shop. Liyue characters may mention that Rex Lapis, not Morax, is the polite name. Zhongli can be played as someone who knows exactly where Mora comes from but now lives without producing it.

## status
@version: 1.1
### STANDARD
As of 7.1 Mora remains the universal currency. The Geo Archon has given up his Gnosis, and the Golden House no longer mints new Mora, though the existing supply stays valid.
