## summary
### LITE
The Glaze Lily is a very old flower of Liyue, once common across the land and now rare in the wild. Its item text says it turns the land's memories into scent as it blooms. Legend says it opens to pure, beautiful music, and that someone once loved it above all other flowers.
### STANDARD +
It is a Liyue local specialty grown on the terraces of Qingce Village and in Liyue Harbor's Yujing Terrace, and blooms at night.

## appearance
### STANDARD
A lily whose blossoms open at night, between 18:00 and 06:00 in-game. Wild plants smell stronger than the planted ones, per Solitary Fragrance as cited by the wiki.

## lore
@version: 1.0
### STANDARD
The archive text calls it an extremely ancient flower, said to have been a common sight in Liyue, that converts the memories of the land into fragrance during florescence. A legend says it opens at the sound of pure, beautiful music, and that one person loved it more than any other; the wiki identifies that person as Guizhong, though the text does not name her. A full bloom in the wild is now rare.
### LARGE +
Madame Ping explained in Farewell, Archaic Lord that the plant listens to people singing. She also said the lilies in Liyue Harbor are planted ones; the flower once grew throughout Liyue, especially around Dihua Marsh, until overpicking left few in the wild. According to her, where wild lilies grew thick, shimmersoil could be found by digging beneath them and following the roots; this soil is used in building the Serenitea Pot.
### ULTRA +
Kaedehara Kazuha, during the quest Tuned to the World's Sounds, used lily juice on the body of the Ancient Zither to repair it. Besides Qingce Village and the Harbor, scattered lilies grow near the Chihu Rock waypoint, on Mt. Tianheng, in Yaodie Valley, and near Mt. Xuanlian, per the wiki.

## gameplay
### LARGE
Gameplay note, not in-world lore: a Liyue local specialty used in crafting and character ascension. Madame Ping gives five on a first dialogue branch. It can be grown in the Serenitea Pot.

## scene_use
### LARGE
Use it for quiet scenes at night near Liyue Harbor or Qingce Village: a bloom opening to someone humming, or a Liyue local mentioning how scarce wild ones have become. Do not assert Guizhong's feelings beyond the archive text.

## status
@version: 1.0
### STANDARD
As of 7.1 canon still describes the wild flower as rare, with planted lilies in the Harbor and Qingce Village.
