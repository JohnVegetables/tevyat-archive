## summary
### LITE
Lumenstone is a special stone mined from the depths of The Chasm in Liyue. Its item text says it has catalytic properties and nearly blazes with light in the gloomy mines, but loses all such effects once taken out of the Chasm. Entering the Underground Mines requires an adjuvant made from it.
### STANDARD +
In the quest Chasm Spelunkers it becomes the Lumenstone Adjuvant, a lantern-like gadget for the mines.

## appearance
### STANDARD
A stone that glows strongly in the dark of the mines. The Adjuvant is described as a crystal that can be a sun in your palm.

## lore
@version: 2.6
### STANDARD
In Surreptitious Seven-Star Seal Sundering, the adventurer Zhiqiong tells the Traveler that to enter The Chasm they must first find an adjuvant made from lumenstone, since only with it can anyone move in the dark below. The Traveler takes the stone from a table, after which Paimon wonders how the cage-shaped objects relate to it.
### LARGE +
The Adjuvant text says it resembles the Glazed Sand Crystal found only in The Chasm but stores greater power. Wiki notes say it illuminates the area, powers light-sensitive mechanisms, and refills from teleport waypoints, braziers and glowing ore, among other things; mining Noctilucous Jade in the mines also restores it.

## gameplay
### LARGE
Gameplay note, not in-world lore: the stone is a quest item; the Adjuvant is a reusable quick-use gadget with an energy gauge, usable only in the Underground Mines.

## scene_use
### LARGE
A lantern for any scene in the mines; outside the Chasm it is just an inert stone, so do not carry its glow into other places.

## chronology
@version: 2.6
### ULTRA
Introduced in Version 2.6 with The Chasm Delvers world quest series: the stone is obtained in the first required part, Surreptitious Seven-Star Seal Sundering, and turned into the Adjuvant in the following quest, Chasm Spelunkers.
