## summary
### LITE
Amethyst Lump is an uncut violet crystal that, by its in-game description, can only be found in Inazuma. It is mined from Violet Crystal ore deposits and treated as a raw forging ore.
### STANDARD +
The item text hints that some professional craftsmanship is needed to bring out its true value, which fits its role as a smithing material rather than a finished gem.

## appearance
### STANDARD
A rough, unpolished lump of violet crystal, harvested by breaking Violet Crystal nodes that stud the Inazuma landscape, including Tsurumi Island.

## lore
### STANDARD
The only in-world text is the item description: an uncut crystal found only in Inazuma, whose true value emerges through skilled craftsmanship. No story, owner or legend is attached to it.

## owners
### STANDARD
No notable owner. It is an ordinary resource gathered by travellers and used by smiths.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Game mechanics, not lore: a Forging Material added in Version 2.0. Each Violet Crystal node drops one to three lumps and, like other Geo objects, breaks faster under blunt attacks. It is used, like Crystal Chunks, to forge Mystic Enhancement Ore, and to forge weapons of the Amethyst Series.
### LARGE +
Further mechanics: Violet Crystal nodes respawn after 72 hours. In Co-Op Mode each nearby player gets separately randomised drops that others cannot see.
### ULTRA +
Unlike most materials, Amethyst Lumps cannot be moved by Anemo abilities. The wiki cites an event page (Graph Adversarial Technology Experiment Log, Day 5) for the fact that they come from breaking Violet Crystals.
