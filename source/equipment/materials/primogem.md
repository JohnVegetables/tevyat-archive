## summary
### LITE
Primogems are the premium currency of Genshin Impact. Their item text calls them a primordial crystalline gem beyond the mundane world, shining with the condensed hopes and dreams of universes that once were. In play they are spent mainly on wishes.
### STANDARD +
Primogems are different from Mora, the ordinary currency of Teyvat. Canon never shows characters trading in them.

## appearance
### STANDARD
A crystalline gem. The in-game description stresses that it is primordial and not of the ordinary world.

## lore
@version: 1.0
### STANDARD
The only in-world text is the item description: a primordial gem, beyond the mundane world, containing the condensed hopes and dreams of universes that once were. Its wording echoes Genesis Crystals, whose text describes an energy crystal formed from pure potential and hope, with enough energy to create a newborn star. Neither is explained by a quest.
### LARGE +
The related Intertwined Fate is described as a fateful stone whose glimmers can entwine fates and connect dreams. The wiki notes the Chinese name for Primogem means roughly "primordial stone", which resembles the term used for allogenes. This is a naming observation, not a canon link.

## gameplay
### LARGE
Gameplay note, not in-world lore: 160 Primogems buy one Intertwined or Acquaint Fate; they also replenish Original Resin and buy Battle Pass levels. Genesis Crystals convert one to one into Primogems, never the reverse.
### ULTRA +
Sources include Commissions, quests, chests, Statues of The Seven, Spiral Abyss, Imaginarium Theater, events, achievements and the Blessing of the Welkin Moon. Amounts are version-dependent.

## scene_use
### LARGE
Treat Primogems as a game-system item. In a story they work best as a mysterious gem tied to wishing, with no stated origin; do not invent a creator.
