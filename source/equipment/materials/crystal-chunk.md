## summary
### LITE
Crystal Chunk is a turquoise crystal, an uncut ore taken from Crystal Ore nodes in caves, mines and mountains across Teyvat. Its item text says only professional craftsmanship can bring out its true value. Smiths use it in forging, chiefly for Mystic Enhancement Ore.
### STANDARD +
It is found in the wild, including Stormterror's Lair, and can also come from shops and expeditions.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Raw, uncut turquoise crystals, grown in clusters inside breakable ore nodes. They are plainer than the blue Magical Crystal Chunk, whose crystals have been altered by the Ley Lines.

## lore
@version: 1.0
### STANDARD
The in-game description calls it an uncut crystal whose real value emerges only through professional craftsmanship. A longer form of the text names this clear rock a precious gift of the land, useful as a valuable weapon-making material. Nothing more is said about its origin.
### LARGE +
Item text contrasts it with the Magical Crystal Chunk, which Ley Line energy has eroded so that ordinary methods cannot process it. Crystal Chunks are the ordinary kind that a smith can work directly.

## gameplay
### LARGE
Gameplay note, not in-world lore: breaking a node gives 1 to 3 chunks, and nodes respawn after several days. Mystic Enhancement Ore, a weapon enhancement material, is mostly forged from them. The Chasm's underground mines also contain nodes.
### ULTRA +
Nodes can also spawn in places called Mining Outcrops, where a Magical Crystal Ore node appears alongside ordinary ones. Expeditions in Whispering Woods, Dadaupa Gorge and Yaoguang Shoal can reward chunks too.

## scene_use
### LARGE
A miner, smith or traveller can handle chunks as a common raw material. Nothing in canon ties it to a person or story, so avoid inventing a legend for it.
