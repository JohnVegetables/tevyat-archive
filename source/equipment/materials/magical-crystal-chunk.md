## summary
### LITE
Magical Crystal Chunk is a blue crystal from mines whose rock was eroded by the energy of the Ley Lines. Ordinary methods cannot process it. Only Original Resin can refine it, into Mystic Enhancement Ore for strengthening weapons.
### STANDARD +
Where the plain Crystal Chunk is ordinary ore, this is its Ley Line-altered counterpart.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Blue crystal chunks taken from Magical Crystal Ore nodes, which stand beside regular ore at Mining Outcrops.

## lore
@version: 1.1
### STANDARD
Item text says the crystal was eroded by Ley Line energy and so cannot be handled by ordinary means; Original Resin can, however, refine it into an invaluable material. The description does not say what Original Resin is in-world or how the process works.
### LARGE +
The shorter text on the item says the chunks can be refined into weapon EXP materials by Original Resin, and that crystals from mines eroded by Ley Line energy cannot be processed by ordinary means. The item text does not connect this to any specific event.

## gameplay
### LARGE
Gameplay note, not in-world lore: each node drops 1 to 3 chunks, nodes appear in Mining Outcrops after Adventure Rank 30, and they respawn daily in changing locations. They are currently used only to forge Mystic Enhancement Ore with Original Resin.
### ULTRA +
Outcrops also spawn ordinary Crystal Ore beside the Magical Crystal Ore, and the two respawn independently.

## scene_use
### LARGE
Use it as a curiosity for smiths and miners: a crystal that cannot be worked by normal craft. Do not invent how Original Resin works.
