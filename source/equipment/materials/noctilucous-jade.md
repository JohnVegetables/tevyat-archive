## summary
### LITE
Noctilucous Jade is a rare mineral from Liyue that glimmers in the dark. Its item text says it is a mutated gemstone condensed from the world's flourishing elements. It is found in caves and mines across Liyue, especially near Mingyun Village.
### STANDARD +
The Chinese name means Night-Staying Stone.

## appearance
### STANDARD
A gemstone that shines faintly in dark places, which is the sense of the word noctilucous.

## lore
@version: 1.0
### STANDARD
Beyond the item description, canon gives it no story. It is called a mutated gemstone, condensed from the flourishing elements of the world, and only said to be so. Chang the Ninth in Qingce Village gives four to the Traveler on a first dialogue branch.
### LARGE +
Mining it in The Chasm's Underground Mines restores Lumenstone Adjuvant energy, per wiki trivia.

## gameplay
### LARGE
Gameplay note, not in-world lore: Geo nodes at cliff bases, caves and mines drop one to three each; they are best broken with blunt attacks. It is used in crafting and ascension.

## scene_use
### LARGE
Use as a miner's or collector's find in a Liyue cave: a faint glow in the rock. Avoid claiming a source for its formation.

## chronology
@version: 1.0
### ULTRA
Present since Version 1.0 as a Liyue local specialty. Its use in the Chasm's Underground Mines, as an energy refill for the Lumenstone Adjuvant, dates from the mines' addition in Version 2.6, per the Lumenstone page.
