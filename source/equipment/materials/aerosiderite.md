## summary
### LITE
Aerosiderite is a rare black ore used to ascend weapons, sold in Liyue and gathered at the Hidden Palace of Lianshan Formula. Its item texts say it came from a black meteor that fell beyond Liyue's Sea of Clouds, dropped to vanquish a monster. The monster's tainted blood stained it, so it no longer holds the pure power of Rex Lapis.
### STANDARD +
It comes in four grades: Grain, Piece, Bit and Chunk. The name means meteoric iron, and the Chinese name translates the same way.

## appearance
### STANDARD
Dark, near-black metal, the colour its item text attributes to the monster's blood. The four grades are shaped like pieces of an interlocking burr puzzle, per wiki trivia.

## lore
@version: 1.0
### STANDARD
The Grain text says a black meteor once fell beyond the Sea of Clouds to vanquish a monster, but powerful tainted blood stained it. The Bit text frames the wider world: coastal nations call the region beyond The Seven's protection the Dark Sea, and defeated gods who fled the new order became evil gods, yet their power shared a source with Rex Lapis, separate from that devouring darkness.
### LARGE +
The Chunk text recalls the fall of Khaenri'ah, when a great sinner made endless monsters with alien blood, mutated by powers from beyond the world, who rampaged in the cataclysm. It names the black serpentine dragon Durin that attacked Mondstadt as one of them. Read together, the texts suggest the meteor's stain came from such a creature, though only the wiki draws that link.
### ULTRA +
The wiki notes that tainting by a monster's blood is something other ancient gods, whose power shares Morax's source, could not have done to their own materials; this is the wiki's reading of the Bit text, not a direct statement. A wiki summary also says Han Ce, son of Han Wu, forged a weapon from a fallen aerosiderite meteor and blackcliff ore; this is unverified here.

## gameplay
### LARGE
Gameplay note, not in-world lore: a weapon ascension material in four grades, obtained in the Hidden Palace of Lianshan Formula on Wednesday, Saturday and Sunday, bought at Liyue's Souvenir Shop, or crafted upward by alchemy (three of a grade plus Mora make one of the next).

## scene_use
### LARGE
A smith or collector in Liyue may prize it as black metal with a divine past, but it is not pure divine metal. Characters can say it was said to fall from the sky to slay a monster; nobody in canon is shown confirming the details.
