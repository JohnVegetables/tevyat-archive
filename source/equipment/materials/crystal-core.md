## summary
### LITE
Crystal Core is the core of a crystal butterfly, a crystalfly. Its item text says it absorbs elemental energy that has escaped into the atmosphere. It is obtained by catching a crystalfly, not by fighting it.
### STANDARD +
Moonglow Fireflies also yield it.

## appearance
### STANDARD
A small glowing core taken from a crystalfly, one of the elemental butterflies seen across Teyvat in several types, including Anemo, Geo, Electro, Dendro, Hydro, Pyro and Cryo.

## lore
@version: 1.0
### STANDARD
The item description presents it as the core of a crystal butterfly that takes in elemental energy that had escaped into the atmosphere. The wiki describes crystalflies as elemental life-forms. Their cores therefore stand for loose elemental energy gathered into a small body.
### LARGE +
The wiki records that Nahida remarks a Pyro crystalfly could easily be mistaken for a firefly because both glow. It also notes that crystalflies do not appear when it is raining, so they always appear in Dragonspine, where it does not rain.

## gameplay
### LARGE
Gameplay note, not in-world lore: killing a crystalfly makes it vanish with no drop; catching it gives one core. Without certain wildlife talents they flee on approach, and a caught one respawns after 24 hours.
### ULTRA +
Cores are spent in crafting and ascension. Crystalflies of every element share the same material.

## scene_use
### LARGE
A traveller may carefully catch a glowing butterfly for its core. Keep the explanation to the item text; canon gives no deeper mechanism.
