## summary
### LITE
Crimson Agate is a crystal found on Dragonspine, the snowbound mountain of Mondstadt. It forms when concentrated life force combines with an energy unique to Dragonspine, and it is ideal nourishment for the Frostbearing Tree.
### STANDARD +
The crystals are offered to the Frostbearing Tree, much as Oculi are offered to the Statues of The Seven, to earn rewards. They lie in the open, in chests marked by a dark red glow, and as rewards for Crimson Wishes.
### LARGE +
The archive description tells a short history. The nail that froze Dragonspine shattered the tree's trunk and canopy into frozen fragments. Long after, a black dragon fell into the valley and its blood seeped into the ley lines. Longer after that, someone broke the icy shackles and the ancient tree, having absorbed that crimson, sprouted anew.

### ULTRA +
It entered the game in Version 1.2 with Dragonspine. Its Chinese name is rendered by the wiki as Crimson Chalcedony, which is a translation note, not a separate item.

## appearance
### STANDARD
A red crystal. Overworld agates show a map icon and sound like Oculi do, and chests holding one are surrounded by a dark red aura.

## lore
### STANDARD
The item text frames the agates as the tree's nutrition after it was shattered and frozen by the Skyfrost Nail. The crimson is the blood of a black dragon that fell into Wyrmrest Valley, carried through the ley lines.
### LARGE +
The Lavawalker artifact text separately says the Lavawalker made a circlet from Crimson Agate to withstand heat in Mare Jivari, so the crystal appears outside Dragonspine in canon. The text gives no explanation.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Gameplay note: 80 agates can be found in the overworld and chests, enough to raise the Frostbearing Tree from Offering Level 1 to 9 before Crimson Wishes, which unlock at Level 8. Crimson Wishes keep giving agates until the tree reaches Level 12.

## entry: Crimson Agate and the Frostbearing Tree
@keys: Crimson Agate, Crimson Agates, Frostbearing Tree

### STANDARD
Crimson Agates are the offering that makes the Frostbearing Tree of Dragonspine grow. The tree was shattered and frozen when the Skyfrost Nail descended, and later absorbed the blood of a black dragon through the ley lines.
