## summary
### LITE
Starsilver is a rare, precious ore found only on Dragonspine, the snowbound mountain of Mondstadt. Its item text says it is formed by the mountain's unique geography and its ley lines. It is silvery, is worked in forges, and is also prized by artists as a pigment source.
### STANDARD +
Smiths use it to forge Fine Enhancement Ore and several weapons, notably the Dragonspine series. In the mountain's old lore a Starsilver greatsword was handed by the princess of Sal Vindagnyr to the outlander Imunlaukr.
### LARGE +
Despite the name, canon never says Starsilver fell from the sky or came from stars. It is a native ore of a mountain that was long a scene of ancient catastrophe, and it turns up in several of that mountain's stories.

## appearance
### STANDARD
A silver-coloured ore. Albedo notes that even impure Starsilver contains fine silver-coloured particles, but that lower grades lack luster, while good specimens have great luster and a lovely colour. Escoffier reports that it tastes like snow and ice mixed with limewash paint.

## lore
### STANDARD
Its item text calls it a rare and precious ore formed by Dragonspine's unique geographical conditions and ley lines. In the Snow-Tombed Starsilver text, the greatsword is forged from Starsilver and able to cleave ice and snow. The weapon lore has the princess of Sal Vindagnyr give it to Imunlaukr as he left to search for a way to end the frost; he later returned it, and it came to rest between the frescoes of the mural room.
### LARGE +
The Disaster and Remorse text, released in Version 6.6, has the same warrior hurl an astral-silver lance, described as made of Whitestone and Starsilver, in defence of a maiden. Those weapon entries tell the human stories; this entry only records that Starsilver is the metal in them. The wiki groups the forged Dragonspine weapons as the Dragonspine (or Starsilver) series, whose shared lore concerns what was entombed in the mountain's cold: a people, a tree and a dragon.
### ULTRA +
In the Version 2.3 event Shadows Amidst Snowstorms (limited-time, Act I: The Snowy Past), Albedo is found on Dragonspine gathering Starsilver for silver paint, having run out of his previous batch. The quest has the Traveler collect one Inferior Starsilver (dull and low in luster, unlikely to sell high) and one Quality Starsilver (great luster, lovely colour) near the cavern entrance. Earlier, in The First Experiment: Elements (Version 1.2), Albedo identifies a Starsilver Catalyst, ore that looks like ordinary Starsilver but that he can use as an alchemical catalyst.

## owners
### STANDARD
No notable owner. Albedo uses it for paint, smiths for forging, and in old Dragonspine legend its metal was carried as a greatsword by Imunlaukr and as a lance in the Disaster and Remorse tale.

## gameplay
### STANDARD
Game mechanics, not lore: a forging material added in Version 1.2, mined only on Dragonspine from destructible ore nodes that drop 1-3 each and respawn after 48 hours. Blunt attacks and Geo abilities break the nodes fastest, and Anemo cannot move the drops.
### LARGE +
Three Starsilver forge one Fine Enhancement Ore (weapon experience). The 4-star Dragonspine series, with the Frost Burial passive, requires 30 Northlander Billets and 30 Starsilver among its materials. Starsilver Catalyst, Inferior Starsilver and Quality Starsilver are quest items and cannot be used like the ore.

During the Shadows Amidst Snowstorms event, Albedo says Starsilver makes high-quality silver paint and that he has run out of his last batch.
