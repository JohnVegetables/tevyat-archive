## summary
### LITE
A three-quest Frost Moon world quest series from Version 6.7 (Luna VIII). In each, the Traveler boards a silver ship, the vessel of the Welkin Moon, which plays a memory of three sister moon goddesses, Aria, Canon and Sonnet, from the distant past.
### STANDARD +
The scenes are conversations aboard the ship: the sisters adopting human forms, Sonnet skipping duties to play on the surface, and a quarrel over whether to defy the ruler of the age as dragons and humans suffer. The Traveler only watches.
### LARGE +
The series gives a rare personal look at the Three Moons as characters: Canon is exacting and dislikes ambiguity, Aria is amused and teasing, and Sonnet is playful but angry at suffering. It also touches their view of the Heavenly Principles and of the Abyss.

## plot
### LITE
Quest (I) begins at the Bubble Pool, where a silver ship waits. Quest (II) begins at the Golden Hall and includes a fight. Quest (III) begins at Nuur Stone Circle. In each the Traveler boards the vessel and a memory plays.
### STANDARD +
(I) At the Bubble Pool, the Traveler hears voices calling a stainless child to ride the silver ship back to "Mother." Boarding the ship, with Floating Ooze and Moonglow Frostfin Whales as help, triggers a memory in the Moonheart Gap. Canon and Aria meet in human forms; Aria teases her sister for being sentimental, and Sonnet wants to sneak out and play with the people on the surface. Canon mentions that Istaroth wants to discuss the world's gravitational potential and time laws with her, and Aria remarks on a certain feeling about how Istaroth views the Descender.

(II) In the Golden Hall the Traveler may read the Chanson of the Boatman, a prayer by a man named Euthyphro of Arcadia to the goddess Prusmene, asking to be saved from his "dread master." After defeating Lusa, the Blighted Newborn, and touching the transit station, the Traveler boards. In the memory Canon scolds Sonnet for skipping work to play with humans; Sonnet points to the Moon Envoy who now shares her work; and Aria teases Canon for dancing at a human festival, then reminds her that the Envoy's name is Zibai.
### LARGE +
(III) The Traveler rides a lift at the Ancient Moon's Remnant in Nuur Stone Circle, then investigates a glowing spot where a boat arrives and boards it. This memory is darker. Sonnet is angry at the ruler's harsh "order" that has killed dragons and humans alike and asks why they stand by. Aria says their mission is only to preserve the world until their King returns, and that even a painful order is better than "pitch-black silence." Canon admits the cruelty, wants to save whoever can be saved, asks Sonnet not to defy the order openly while the world is fragile, and quotes Istaroth's word "hope."
### ULTRA +
Rewards: (I) unlocks a precious chest; (II) unlocks two exquisite and one luxurious chest and, through its hidden objective, the viewpoint The Boatman's Song-House. The wiki notes that the "silver shining ship" in (I) was previously mentioned in the Boatman's song in an earlier quest.

## revelations
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The three moon goddesses appear as sisters who chose human forms to respond to those who prayed to them. They serve a King who left and who told them that life across the sea of stars are allies against the darkness. Their present ruler is a "new master", which the dialogue links to the Heavenly Principles.
### LARGE +
Sonnet is the one most moved to resist the ruler's cruelty; Aria and Canon argue for patience. Canon has met Istaroth. A Moon Envoy named Zibai was sent to share Sonnet's work.
### ULTRA +
The memory also says the sisters were entrusted with a city of dragons and watched human civilisation on the surface, and that terrible losses to both occurred under the ruler. The Chanson of the Boatman, by a believer in Prusmene, concerns a Primordial One that wants to return all dreams to the earth; treat it as in-world scripture by one believer, not neutral narration.

## characters
### LITE
Traveler, Paimon and the three moon goddesses Aria, Canon and Sonnet.
### STANDARD +
Canon speaks formally and finds ambiguous human words hard (she cannot parse "endearing"). Aria is warm and teasing and wants Canon to remember people's names. Sonnet is energetic, impatient with duty, and fiercely protective of the living. Istaroth and Zibai are mentioned, not seen.

## consequences
### STANDARD
No lasting world change. The quests give chests and memory scenes. Their value is lore: they supply the moon goddesses' personalities and positions on the fate of the world.

## placement
### STANDARD
Released in Version 6.7 (Luna VIII) with the Frost Moon map as Lunar Highlands content. The memories themselves are set in the distant past. The wiki lists the three quests as concurrent and does not give a fixed play order.
