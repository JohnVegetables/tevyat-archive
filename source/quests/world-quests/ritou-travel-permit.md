## summary
### LITE
Ritou Travel Permit is a one-quest world series in Inazuma (2.6) whose only quest is Chisato's Letter. Under the Sakoku Decree the Traveler cannot leave Ritou without a permit, and the Kanjou Commissioner's daughter Hiiragi Chisato gives them a favour to earn it: carry a letter to her sweetheart Kujou Kamaji of the Tenryou Commission. Kamaji issues the Travel Permit and sends a reply with some treats. Afterwards the Traveler may come and go from Ritou freely.
### STANDARD
The quest follows Ritou Escape Plan, in which the Traveler secretly gets off Ritou with Chisato's help. The two belong to different institutions (the Hiiragi Clan leads the Kanjou Commission; Kamaji is a Kujou Clan member and a Tenryou Commission enforcer), and Kamaji worries that closeness between them could be exploited, so an outsider carries the letter.
### LARGE +
The series is listed in the journal as a chapter, but contains only one quest. The wiki notes this as an oddity of presentation.

## plot
@version: 2.6
### STANDARD
Chisato asks the Traveler to take her letter to Kamaji. The Traveler may first speak to Sanada, a Tenryou Commission subordinate who sends them to find Kamaji himself, walking near the bridge. Kamaji recognises the handwriting with relief and agrees to approve the permit as a personal favour to Chisato, but asks the Traveler to wait at Kiminami Restaurant while he prepares it.
### LARGE +
At the restaurant Kiminami Anna hands over treats Kamaji had ordered. Kamaji says to her that they are a courtesy gift in return for tea Hiiragi Shinsuke once sent his father, a public-relations gesture. He tells the Traveler privately that his father, Kujou Takayuki, would not approve of contact with Chisato, and that he understands why, since a closeness between a Kujou and a Hiiragi could be exploited by others. He then gives the Traveler the Travel Permit, his own carefully worded reply and the snack box, and goes back to official duties, wishing he could deliver the gift himself. The Traveler returns to Chisato, who tells them to thank Kamaji rather than herself.
### ULTRA +
Before the Traveler goes in search of Kamaji, Chisato says she is counting on them; Sanada, if asked, is wary and will only direct the Traveler on once shown that the letter really is from Chisato. The quest rewards are Adventure EXP, 30,000 Mora, Hero's Wit and the Travel Permit, plus 100 Inazuma reputation.

## revelations
@spoiler: none
### STANDARD
- The relationship between Chisato and Kamaji is a private romance between a Hiiragi and a Kujou, which Kamaji says could be taken advantage of by others given their positions.
- Kamaji's cover story for the gift (a return courtesy toward Shinsuke) is one he tells Anna; he is open about the real reason with the Traveler.

## characters
### STANDARD
Hiiragi Chisato (daughter of Hiiragi Shinsuke, head of the Hiiragi Clan), Kujou Kamaji, Kiminami Anna, Sanada (minor), Paimon and the Traveler. Takayuki and Shinsuke are only mentioned.

## consequences
### STANDARD
The Traveler receives a Travel Permit, which allows free entry to and exit from Ritou, plus Adventure EXP, Mora and Hero's Wit. Speaking to Chisato again afterwards, following a certain dialogue branch, gives a 4-star Storm Cage artifact once.

## placement
### STANDARD
Released in 2.6, as a follow-up to Ritou Escape Plan, among the Inazuma world quests that follow the Traveler's arrival in Ritou under the Sakoku Decree. It is unrelated to the main Archon Quest beyond its setting.

## entry: Travel Permit
@keys: Travel Permit, Ritou permit
@keys2: Kamaji, Chisato, Ritou
@version: 2.6
### STANDARD
The Travel Permit is the document Kujou Kamaji issues in Chisato's Letter. It lets the holder move freely into and out of Ritou, which the Sakoku Decree otherwise restricts. It is issued as a personal favour to Chisato and not as a general policy.
