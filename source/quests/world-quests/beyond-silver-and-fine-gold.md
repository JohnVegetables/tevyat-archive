## summary
### LITE
A Version 6.6 world quest series about Nicole. After the Traveler and Paimon unlock her Witch's Lodge in the Witch's Garden, Nicole turns its rooms into a treasure hunt: each of four rooms hides three golden feathers and a chest, and each room opens a week after the last. Between the puzzles she tells stories about her angelic past, the Hexenzirkel and her friends.
### STANDARD +
The four parts are The Joyous Floating Island, The Silent Theater, The Lost Palace and The Forgotten Lighthouse. They require the earlier Witch's Lodge quest and must be done in order; the next room unlocks at 04:00 on the Monday after the previous one is fully explored, so the series takes four weeks. The tone is light and teasing, but several rooms turn serious about angels, gods and betrayal.
### LARGE +
Structurally the series is a gift: Nicole, whose duty as an angel was to guide people to treasure, invents a way to do it for friends. The wiki's quest page itself notes that each room must be fully explored before the next opens.

## plot
### LITE
Nicole invites the Traveler and Paimon to her lodge. She has built a room full of tricks and hidden three feathers in it; finding them reveals a treasure chest. A new room opens each week: a tea party where everyone shrinks, a studio of magic paintings, a shrine of angel statues and a tall study full of books that make stairways when Nicole speaks.
### STANDARD +
The Joyous Floating Island: Nicole explains she wants a place to have fun and feel useful at once. A door with floating teacups sends the pair into the Great Mage's Tea Party, where they are shrunk to teacup scale. They heat a giant teapot with Pyro, sweeten a cup with three sugar cubes (Nicole mentions Alice takes three, Anya one, Barbeloth none), fend off a statue of a Mandragora Nicole animates, and open the chest.

The Silent Theater: a studio of empty frames. Three paintings pull the pair into scenes Nicole narrates: the day she agreed to join the Hexenzirkel, a love-and-horror tale of a Princess of the Morning Star, and her admiration for Anya and the stories of Simulanka. When each is "restored", the chest opens.

The Lost Palace: Nicole is busy elsewhere (she is selling sculptures at a stall in Fontaine), so she leaves scrolls with riddle hints; the pair arrange statues to follow them. Afterwards Nicole shows them the shrine where she lived as an angel.

The Forgotten Lighthouse: the final room. Nicole tries to explain why she is embarrassed, tells a fragment of the story of the first time the mages challenged the Anemo Archon, and hides the feathers at the top of a study. Words spoken aloud become golden platforms, and the Traveler and Paimon climb to the feathers on the stairs she speaks into existence.
### LARGE +
Details of the puzzles: in the tea room the teapot rises into the air to pour each cup and the feathers appear in sequence; in the theatre each emblem on a frame (a witch's emblem, a Domain emblem, the emblem of Simulanka) leads to a painting; in the palace the three scroll hints describe statue placements in plain riddles (a bird "walking the field", a creature in the valley, a choir that gazes toward the voice); in the lighthouse the platforms are words Nicole speaks aloud ("leave it to me", "let light shine the way ahead", "go up from here"). When Paimon complains that Nicole shouted, Nicole jokes about turning trinkets into turnips, then ends the quest with a teasing telling of a dark ending that Paimon objects to because "words are magic here."
### ULTRA +
Dialogue touches worth keeping: in the tea room the stone Mandragora statue begins to move stiffly, Nicole calls it a minor technical difficulty and turns it into a real Mandragora, then uses her burst when Paimon asks for help; in the theatre she says she did not attend her first Hexenzirkel tea in person and "concluded" Alice was punishing her for hesitating; in the palace she vows to chase a thief who stole her sculptures all the way to the Fortress of Meropide; and in the lighthouse she mutters that she hopes Alice never hears the story. The Silent Theater also mentions a painting of Simulanka with light novels from Yae Publishing House on the floor, and a cameo of Mr. Nine, her old Seelie friend, who is pushing her to read.

## revelations
@spoiler: major
@version: 6.6
### STANDARD
Nicole is an angel, a being created to love all living things and to guide and enlighten. She says the angels' emotions were crude and that a successful mission gave them a joyous relief that was their strongest feeling. In her own words, completing the mission she is "burdened with" is why she is happy to guide others to treasure.

Alice was the only person in the Hexenzirkel who knew Nicole's true identity when Nicole first attended a tea party, and Nicole decided to join them only after talking to Alice privately inside Alice's head.
### LARGE +
In the Silent Theater Nicole says the first angel's betrayal happened beneath an Irminsul bough. She tells, with the names withheld, that a star-wandering traveller visited the Princess of the Morning Star in a silver tree, taught her free will and love, and that the gods destroyed the lovers' home; the princess lost her power and wisdom and even her closest friends were cursed. She insists the story is true.

In the Lost Palace she says the shrine was her home when she was an angel and that she loved telling the other angels about life among humans, sending omens and guiding people to build civilisations. A statue in the lodge's studio, after the quest, has Nicole say she can reassure her "sisters" that humans now live comfortably.

In the Lighthouse she hints at a story about the first time the mages challenged the Anemo Archon, which she finds embarrassing; the Traveler hears only the fragment.

## characters
### LITE
Nicole is the host. The Traveler and Paimon are the guests and the only voices besides hers. Alice, Anya, Barbeloth, Rhinedottir and Ivanovna of the Hexenzirkel, and an old Seelie friend, are mentioned but do not appear.
### STANDARD +
Nicole is playful, verbose and slightly self-congratulating, and she talks to herself aloud when she thinks no one is listening. She calls herself an expert at hiding things, enjoys making the pair work for treasure, and is at her most sincere when she mentions the Hexenzirkel and the angels. Paimon plays straight woman, delighted by every chest and wary of Nicole's pranks.

## consequences
### STANDARD
Each part unlocks one more room of the Witch's Lodge and a chest; the Forgotten Lighthouse completes the series. Afterwards Nicole says there is nothing left to give for "a few hundred years". The lodge's rooms also gain small Nicole-only interactions (a Mandragora statue, a painting that burns away to show the witches around their table in Dodoco form, an angel statue, a bookshelf that leads to a Mora chest).
### LARGE +
The other Hexenzirkel members are not shown reacting. The series is also notable as the time Nicole's past as an angel is told directly to the player rather than hinted.
### ULTRA +
Wiki-listed mechanics, kept brief: the Pyro Traveler is a trial character in the tea room, theatre and lighthouse; Nicole is a trial character in the tea party's early steps. The final Mora chest from the lodge bookshelf (200,000 Mora) is a post-quest interaction, found by observing the study's bookshelf as Nicole.

## placement
### STANDARD
Released in Version 6.6 (Luna VII, 2026-05-20) alongside Nicole's debut as a playable character. It requires the Witch's Lodge world quest from Luna IV (6.3). It sits outside the Archon Quest chain and has no story effect on it.
