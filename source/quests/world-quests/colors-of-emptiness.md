## summary
### LITE
Colors of Emptiness is a short Nod-Krai World Quest series on Lempo Isle, added in Version 6.0. Two quests, Crimson Cleansing at the north end and Blues of the Old World at the south end of the Nothing Passage, can be done in either order. In each the Traveler and Paimon follow a glowing orb, red in one and blue in the other, that possesses objects and animals as it moves through a gorge once held by the Fatui. When both are done, Colors Out of Space begins: the orbs collide and the Traveler is flung through a strange ruined space. Nothing explains what the orbs are.
### STANDARD +
The series has no fixed antagonist and no named supporting cast. Its worldbuilding comes from leftover Fatui material in the passage and from the mystery of the orbs.

## plot
### STANDARD
Crimson Cleansing. At the north end of the Nothing Passage the Traveler finds a red orb that is chasing a blue kuuhenki. The orb enters an inactive Landcruiser, forcing a fight, then looks for blue stones in a container; the Traveler removes them, and the orb moves on through a mole den, a recon bot and a blue patch of mist.

Blues of the Old World. At the south end a blue orb escapes from a circle. It rides a mole, activates Landcruisers, enters a goat that heads for a blue crystal, and leads the Traveler to a red patch of mist. A red kuuhenki appears from the crystal and creates a Moonlane.

Colors Out of Space. With both quests done, the red and blue orbs collide in front of the Traveler and a vortex pulls the Traveler into a purple space full of floating ruins. The Traveler must wait for the flight to end. Paimon was blown away by the explosion and had trouble flying back; she doubts the Traveler's account and urges them to leave, since they found no treasure.
### LARGE +
If a player does only one first quest, its ending leads straight into the other; the wiki lists several Paimon variants depending on what has been started. Each orb leaves behind a permanent strong kuuvahki environment of its own colour as it travels.

Paimon's guess at the beginning is that the orbs are Seelie, who lead to treasure, so she is disappointed when they find none.
### ULTRA +
The kuuvahki barrier in the gorge was apparently a Fatui authorization-terminal system; notes found there record a dispute between two Fatui figures (see the Fatui notes entry). The quest rewards are typical for a world quest and the final quest also awards the achievement "Colors Out of Snezhnaya".

## revelations
### STANDARD
- The Nothing Passage had been designated a Military Administrative Area under an imperial edict issued in the Tsaritsa's name, with intruders to be treated as hostile agents.
- Fatui officers left notes and barriers, including a kuuvahki barrier and an authorization terminal.
- The Traveler glimpses a space of ruins that Paimon has never heard of in Teyvat.
### LARGE +
- Nothing in the series identifies the orbs or the space beyond them.

## characters
### STANDARD
Only the Traveler and Paimon appear. Fatui members are mentioned through notes: Anton Zarkovich and V. A. Preobrazhensky, who argue in a note about an authorization terminal and an ID card.

## consequences
### STANDARD
The series is self-contained and has no stated follow-up. It unlocks the Nothing Passage as open ground and grants the achievement "Colors Out of Snezhnaya".

## placement
### STANDARD
Released in Version 6.0 (Luna I, 2025-09-10) and set on Lempo Isle in Nod-Krai. It has no required story prerequisite on the wiki.

### STANDARD
A weathered notice at either end of the passage says the area is a Military Administrative Area by decree of Her Majesty the Tsaritsa of Snezhnaya, under the Imperial Edict on the Strengthening of Order, and that unauthorised entry will be treated as espionage. A messy note addressed to Anton Zarkovich warns him not to lock the writer's people out with an authorization terminal and threatens him with consequences; it is signed V. A. Preobrazhensky and accuses Zarkovich of obstructing work because his own northern experiments have failed.

## entry: Red and blue orbs
@keys: red orb, blue orb, strange light orb
@keys2: Nothing Passage, Lempo Isle
@spoiler: none
### STANDARD
Two unexplained glowing orbs that move through kuuvahki-affected ground and inhabit objects or animals. They are tied to the colours red and blue of the kuuhenki and mist patches around them. Paimon initially suspects they are Seelie; this is never confirmed.
