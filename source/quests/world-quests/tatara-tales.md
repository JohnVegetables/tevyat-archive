## summary
### LITE
A version 2.0 world quest series in Tatarasuna, Kannazuka. The Mikage Furnace, Inazuma's main Jade Steel smelter, runs on Tatarigami power that has spiralled out of control since the war between the Shogunate and the Watatsumi Army. The Traveler helps Xavier, a Fontaine engineer, bring it back under control.
### STANDARD +
The series runs across seven days of in-game daily resets, with eight quests. Short trips to the Furnace investigate its condition, gather data and repair paths, with small Fatui groups in the way. At the end the Traveler and Xavier enter the contaminated area to purify the Tatarigami core. Finishing the series removes the Balethunder from the Furnace.
### LARGE +
The series also shows how the war strained relations at home: Shogunate soldiers and shrine maidens disagreed over the Furnace. Its aftermath leads to Xavier's later film-making in Inazuma.

## plot
### LITE
The Tenryou Commission hired experts from Fontaine to expand Jade Steel production, powering the Furnace with Tatarigami. Because of the war the power began to spiral out of control, setting the soldier Toranosuke and the shrine maiden Miyuki at odds. The Traveler seeks out Xavier, a consultant who stayed behind, and discovers the Fatui are behind the trouble.
### STANDARD +
The quests are Tatara Tales, Priority Investigation, Purification Device, Data Collection, Process Is Everything, Functional Test, Final Preparations and The Last Act, each opening a day after the last. The Traveler uses three Kamuijima Cannons to break openings in the containment dome Xavier built, then works toward a purification device. In The Last Act the Traveler fetches three missing components, fights Fatui waves inside the Furnace, activates the Purification Device at the bottom, defends it from attackers, and reports to Toranosuke at the top.
### LARGE +
The wiki's Furnace page says that production had halted because high Tatarigami energy caused deactivation and the failure of even the auto-restart. Xavier put up the dome to contain the Balethunder. Before the war, Alice had visited and modified the Furnace, improving the dome and limiting production for better safety.

## revelations
@version: 2.0
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
The Fatui have taken an interest in the Tatarigami and have breached parts of the containment dome. Xavier's purification means they cannot take that energy without risking war with the Tenryou Commission.
### LARGE +
The wiki's Furnace page records that Tatarigami energy was sourced from Yashiori Island, and that the Shogunate and shrine maidens differed over the Furnace: the former worried about the loss of Jade Steel for the war, the latter about the Tatarigami's effects being felt as far as the Sacred Sakura at the Grand Narukami Shrine.

## characters
### LITE
The Traveler and Paimon; Xavier; Toranosuke; Miyuki; Fatui enemies.
### STANDARD +
Xavier is from Petrichor in Fontaine and stayed behind to film the Furnace in the hope of promoting Fontaine's technology; the wiki says he built the dome when it went critical. Toranosuke is a soldier of the Shogunate and Miyuki a shrine maiden. The Fatui include Skirmishers, a Mirror Maiden and Cicin Mages.

### ULTRA +
The wiki adds that Xavier also taught the people of Higi Village to make Imported Poultry, that his first film was titled Tatara Tales and re-enacted the Traveler's fight against the Fatui, and that he returned to Fontaine in the 4.3 Roses and Muskets event for a film festival. The Last Act's final fights include lightning bolts during the defence of the device.

## consequences
### STANDARD
The Tatarigami core is purified and the Balethunder around the Furnace dissipates. The NPC Hanayama Kaoru appears north of Tatarasuna and offers a flower-seed exchange and a small follow-up quest.
### LARGE +
After this Xavier turned to film-making and began the film The Seventh Samurai, which the Tenryou Commission accepted as good for the Shogunate's prestige; he moved to the Kujou Encampment after that quest. Completing the series gives an achievement of the same name.

## placement
### STANDARD
Released in Version 2.0 as one of the early Inazuma world quest series. Reaching the starting location requires Ritou Escape Plan. It is followed by The Seventh Samurai in the quest ordering.

## entry: Mikage Furnace
@keys: Mikage Furnace
@keys2: Tatarasuna, Jade Steel, Tatarigami
@version: 2.0
### STANDARD
The Mikage Furnace in Tatarasuna is the largest smelting facility in Inazuma, producing Jade Steel with Tatarigami power. War halted production, and the area was surrounded by a containment dome built by the Fontaine engineer Xavier and filled with Balethunder until the Tatara Tales series.
