## summary
### LITE
Blessings of the Frost Moon is a World Quest in the Frostmoon Enclave on Hiisi Island, Nod-Krai (Version 6.0). Lauma, the Frostmoon Scions' Moonchanter, who understands all animal speech, needs help to pull a buried statue called the Lunar Semblance out of a Moon Mirror, a task her animal friends cannot manage. The Traveler works paired mechanisms with her while she directs them through a watching ibis. The statue ends up in the Scions' library. A second part, unlocked later, shows the finished Grand Library and Lauma's reasons for opening it to animals as well as humans.
### STANDARD +
Tone is gentle and humorous: a weasel thief and an ibis help plan the "heist", and a puffin supplies fruit. The quest is the clearest early statement of Lauma's character: she cannot switch off her ability to understand animals, finds peace elusive as a result, and treats humans and animals as sharing one nature.

## plot
### STANDARD
Part 1. Lauma is arguing with a longplume ibis and a weasel thief about how to move the sealed Lunar Semblance. She explains to Paimon that her animal-speech is always on and cannot be controlled, so even in the forest she hears constant quarrelling, though she has grown used to it. The Traveler goes to the Moon Mirror, where the statue lies sealed by a previous Moonchanter. Two mechanisms in two places must be worked at the same time; Lauma takes one side, the ibis tells her where the Traveler is, and the Traveler takes the other. Once the path is cleared Lauma uses her power to pull the statue out. Animals then help carry it to the library. Lauma explains that the statue is crude and worth little as Mora, but is priceless to the Frostmoon Scions as proof that documents about it are reliable. She asks the Traveler to bring her any texts about the Frostmoon Scions to copy for the library.
### LARGE +
Lauma pays the weasel thief one Mora more each time it helps her, and thanks the Traveler for easy mastery of the mechanisms. The weasel thief says the Traveler is the best treasure-hunter it has seen and wants to join future hunts. A puffin brings extra fruit for the Traveler and Paimon, and the Midsommar Berries on a plate are quickly eaten.
Part 2 begins automatically when the Frostmoon Enclave Meeting Point reaches level 5. Lauma, in the finished Grand Library, says she must still find places for bird stands, cushions and deposit boxes, which she wants artisans to help build. She describes her duty as Moonchanter as listening to everyone's troubles, with animals visiting at set times of day (badgers at dawn, ibises after sunrise, finches in flocks in the afternoon, rimehorn deer at dusk, weasel thieves at night) and humans coming less often and telling only part of their story. She explains that she renovated the library as a space where humans and animals can mix, hoping the animals' lack of judgment will encourage people to speak openly.
### ULTRA +
Post-quest dialogues after Part 1 (only the wiki's listed sets are summarised): the weasel thief, now over-eager, digs up buried boxes, wish-boxes and fossils and drops them in the library, so Lauma hires a second, older weasel thief to return them to their places. She also gives the Traveler a coin from the first weasel thief as a "deposit" on a promised treasure-hunting partnership, with a suggestion to leave dried lakkaberries by the pillow. She reflects that "human nature" and "bestial nature" are both just nature, that she imagines the world might be better if humans and animals could talk, and that some people reject nature and hunt animals who can speak their language. A puffin, an ibis and a rimehorn deer appear in small recurring scenes.

## revelations
### STANDARD
- Lauma's understanding of animal speech is always on and beyond her control; she found it a joy at first and a burden later.
- A previous Moonchanter sealed the Lunar Semblance in a Moon Mirror.
- The Moon Mirror's seal can be undone by working paired mechanisms in two places at once.
- The Frostmoon Scions keep a library and Lauma wants it open to both humans and animals.
### LARGE +
- Lauma carries the role of confidante for both animals and Scions, and says people hide their true feelings in a way animals do not.
- Lauma thinks about writing: she says that books on human and "bestial" nature are written by humans who cannot hear the animals' side.

## characters
### STANDARD
Lauma, Paimon and the Traveler are the named participants. Animals shown include a longplume ibis, a weasel thief, a puffin, finches and a rimehorn deer. Lauma teases that the ibis learned its way of talking from a short girl with a metal arm and a hat, a reference to Jahoda.

## consequences
### STANDARD
Completing Part 1 unlocks the Frostmoon Enclave Meeting Point and sets up the library renovation. The Lunar Semblance is placed in the library and its documented existence confirms several earlier texts for Lauma. Part 2, a short conversation, follows later when the Meeting Point is at level 5.

## placement
### STANDARD
Released in Version 6.0 (Luna I, 2025-09-10) as part of the first Nod-Krai content. Part 2 is gated by Frostmoon Enclave Meeting Point level rather than story progress.

### STANDARD
A large, crudely carved stone statue sealed inside a Moon Mirror by an earlier Moonchanter. Lauma says it is worth little in Mora and carries no mystical power, but is a priceless historical relic of the Frostmoon Scions whose existence confirms several written accounts. Animals helped carry it to the Grand Library after Lauma pulled it from the mirror.

### STANDARD
Lauma renovated the library to be open to humans and animals alike, with furniture, bird stands, cushions and deposit boxes for animals.
