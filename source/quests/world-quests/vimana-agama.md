## summary
### LITE
A four-part Sumeru world quest series from Version 3.0, unlocked after the Aranara quest The World of Aranara. An Aranara named Ararycan has noticed that something is sickening the forest around Devantaka Mountain. The Traveler and Paimon team up with Jazari, an eager researcher from the Kshahrewar school, and Royinjan, an antique dealer in Port Ormos, to open a giant Khaenri'ahn Ruin Golem and reach the leaking Chaos Core beneath it. The pollution is traced to that core and to Abyss Order mages working in the cavern.
### STANDARD +
Tonally the series mixes comedy (Jazari's academic enthusiasm, Royinjan's haggling) with a quieter story about childhood friendship and forgotten memory. Humans lose the ability to see Aranara as they grow up, and the middle chapter shows what that costs. The series gives Ardravi Valley its Ruin Golem on Devantaka Mountain and introduces Dev's Cavern.
### LARGE +
The title combines Sanskrit terms: vimana (mythological flying palaces or chariots) and agama (a body of scriptural and tantric literature). The two sub-quests of the middle chapter, Linga and Yoni, are named for paired symbolic representations in Hindu tradition (wiki etymology notes). The series is also a chapter in the Aranyaka forest adventure journal item.

## plot
### LITE
Araja and Arama send the Traveler to Ararycan, who has seen a suspicious figure near Devantaka Mountain. The figure is Jazari, who has Akademiya permission to study the mountain's giant Ruin Golem. Ararycan explains that filth is making the forest sick. Jazari thinks the golem's removed Chaos Core, sealed in Dev's Cavern, is leaking energy. To enter the golem the party needs an Amiti Device, which leads them to Royinjan, then to his childhood hideout in Vimara Village, then through the golem to the cavern, where they defeat Abyss Mages and an Abyss Lector and switch off the extraction device.
### STANDARD +
The chain of errands: Ararycan's report leads to Jazari, whose research needs an Amiti Device. Royinjan haggles over a wrongly identified Chaos Core, then admits the real device was his grandfather Hasad's and guides the party to his childhood hideout in Vimara Village. Three fragments (one in the hideout, two in the Linga and Yoni caves) are repaired by Jazari. Inside the golem the Traveler restores power and fights a cursed knight; the golem's arm falls and opens Dev's Cavern, where the Traveler defeats Abyss Mages and an Abyss Lector and shuts off an extraction device. Ararycan then leaves for Festival Utsava.
### LARGE +
First Chapter: Ararycan calls the Withering "Marana" and says the forest is crying; the present pollution smells similar to it but not the same. Jazari cannot see or hear Ararycan, who explains that people lose that ability as they grow older and lose their innocence. Jazari reasons that the golem can be used to smash the blockage over the cavern, but its door needs an Amiti Device. In Port Ormos the Traveler haggles with Royinjan, who asks 500,000 Mora; the price paid depends on the haggling. What he sells turns out to be an ordinary-looking Chaos Core, which he insists is a unique treasure, not the device. He then realises the device belonged to his grandfather Hasad, a renowned adventurer called the Golden Adventurer, and agrees to take them to the secret base he and friends used as children.

Royinjan's Chapter: At Vimara Village the Vamadha vines blocking the cave only turn when sung to; Ararycan teaches the Traveler, since Aranara make Vamadha to keep humans out. Inside they find a large ruin machine part, one device fragment and an old childhood adventure handbook. Royinjan can no longer read its local cursive, so the Traveler consults Amadhiah, his old friend, who marks the other two fragment sites and gives them a dried flower. In the sub-quests, Linga (a flooded cave with Seelie, water puzzles and Dendro monuments) and Yoni (a cave with a Withering Zone), the Traveler recovers the other fragments. Royinjan accepts stories as payment. Shown the flower, he says it is not his and asks that it go back to its owner; the Traveler gives it to Ararycan, who was the forgotten third friend.

Jazari's Chapter: Inside the golem Jazari explains his ambition to follow the historical Kshahrewar scholar Pir Kavikavus, then stays on the lower level to study. The Traveler restores power through backup relays fed by energy blocks, fights a cursed knight, reaches the pilot cabin and starts the golem. Jazari rushes the controls; the machine lifts its arm and the arm drops onto the ground, breaking open the way to Dev's Cavern.

Dev Delver Chapter: With Ararycan, the Traveler descends to the cavern, drives off groups of Abyss Mages that are damaging the seal on the core, defeats an Abyss Lector and shuts down an extraction device draining energy from the core. Ararycan plans to attend Festival Utsava in Vanarana and then return to guard the forest.

The haggling: the Traveler's offer decides the outcome. A far too low offer is rejected as an insult, a moderate one is accepted reluctantly, and a high one is accepted as a friendly favour among dealers. If bargaining fails Royinjan holds to 500,000 Mora. Royinjan and Amadhiah are on strained terms: Royinjan thinks Amadhiah quit adventuring before he did, while Amadhiah says neither had the knack and Royinjan was simply stubborn. Royinjan tells his side with humour, claiming the pair could have been the greatest adventurers.

In the golem Jazari fears the damage will count against his report to the Akademiya. He describes the golem as an early model with a backup power system older than the first ruin machine, and says very few people in the Akademiya can read its blueprint. He offers afterward to help the Traveler apply for a research project on Chaos Cores, promising half the funding.
### ULTRA +
Smaller beats. Jazari occasionally seems to talk to himself in Ararycan's presence because he cannot perceive it. If the player has completed We Will Be Reunited, Paimon points out that Royinjan's claimed Stormterror's Lair core resembles the one Dain took, so it must be a fake. The Linga sub-quest reveals one of the Stone Pillar Seals used in Legends of the Stone Lock, and has an optional Withering Zone with a Ruins Torch puzzle. Ararycan insists it moved the ruin components into the cave itself, and does not recall playing with the children; an optional dialogue choice has the Traveler say the forest remembers everything. During the Dev Delver fight the Abyss Lector says its side seeks only what is "ours" and calls their blessing rejected; the Traveler and Paimon do not interpret this.

## revelations
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
The forest's illness around Devantaka Mountain is caused by a leaking Chaos Core in Dev's Cavern, tied to a Khaenri'ahn Ruin Golem, with the Abyss Order working in the cavern. Ararycan was once the friend of Royinjan and Amadhiah as children, but remembers nothing of it.
### LARGE +
The pilot-cabin knight is wiki-identified as Ynghildr, a Khaenri'ahn knight cursed to become an Abyssal creature. Jazari dismisses the Traveler's report because Khaenri'ahns could not have lived so long.

## characters
### LITE
The Traveler and Paimon, with Ararycan (an Aranara), Jazari (a Kshahrewar researcher), Royinjan (an antique dealer) and Amadhiah (a Vimara villager).
### STANDARD +
Ararycan is a gentle, timid Aranara who speaks of itself in the third person. Jazari is an enthusiastic scholar of the Kshahrewar school with a research permit from the Akademiya; he is short of funds and a little boastful but sincere. Royinjan, who calls himself the Chief Antique Appraiser, is a shrewd merchant in Port Ormos and the grandson of the adventurer Hasad. Amadhiah is an elderly Vimara villager and Royinjan's childhood friend. Arama and Araja, Aranara from the Aranyaka series, send the Traveler to Ararycan. Nahida's All Schemes to Know offers optional glimpses of Royinjan's and Jazari's thoughts.

## consequences
### STANDARD
The golem's arm is broken, the cavern is open, the Chaos Core is cut off from the extraction device and the forest's illness should slowly ease. Jazari remains in the golem to continue his research, and Ararycan will guard the forest after the festival. The final chapter gives a Sumeru reputation reward.

## placement
### STANDARD
Released in Version 3.0 with Sumeru; it requires the world quest The World of Aranara and so follows the opening of the Aranara story. Order of parts: First Chapter, Royinjan's Chapter (with sub-quests Linga and Yoni, which can be done in either order), Jazari's Chapter, Dev Delver Chapter.

## entry: Dev's Cavern and the Chaos Core
@keys: Dev's Cavern, Devantaka Mountain, Ruin Golem, Amiti Device
@keys2: Vimana Agama, Jazari, Ararycan
@version: 3.0
### STANDARD
Beneath Devantaka Mountain lies a giant Khaenri'ahn Ruin Golem whose power core was removed and sealed underground in Dev's Cavern. In the Vimana Agama series the sealed core leaks pollution into the forest while an extraction device and Abyss Order mages work in the cavern. The Amiti Device, assembled from three fragments, opens the golem. The Traveler breaks into the cavern using the golem's arm and shuts off the extraction device.

## entry: Royinjan and the Secret Base
@keys: Royinjan, Amadhiah, Hasad
@keys2: Vimana Agama, Vimara Village, Port Ormos
@version: 3.0
### STANDARD
Royinjan runs an antique shop in Port Ormos. As a boy he played in a secret base at Vimara Village with Amadhiah and a third friend he cannot remember, the Aranara Ararycan. Amadhiah drifted away from adventuring; Royinjan stayed on longer but later became an antique appraiser, partly inspired by the stories of his grandfather Hasad. They remain on tense but fond terms.
