## summary
### LITE
A seven-quest Sumeru world quest series from Version 3.6, set in the northern desert. The Traveler and Paimon meet Sorush, a Pari who believes she must sacrifice herself to remove the corrupting Sign of Apaosha, and help her gather five Great Songs of Khvarena. The Order of Skeptics' leader Nasejuna turns out to be using them, and the Pari's history from the cataclysm is revealed.
### STANDARD +
The series contrasts two ideas of duty: Sorush's wish to sacrifice herself and the Pari elder Zurvan's insistence that Pari fulfil their own duties. It also reveals that the Skeptics' own history was largely myth, that the Pari and Khaenri'ahn fighters including Dainsleif fought the Abyss together 500 years ago, and that Zurvan met Dainsleif.
### LARGE +
It requires only the Version 1.0 prologue quest The Outlander Who Caught the Wind, not any Sumeru Archon Quest.

## plot
### LITE
The Traveler finds a buried drum linked to an unfinished Adventurers' Guild commission about the Sign of Apaosha. Katheryne asks them to contact the isolated Order of Skeptics. They meet the Pari Sorush, who names the Traveler her Yasnapati. She must find two other Pari, Mihir and Rashnu, to earn the Twin-Horned Chaplet from the elder Zurvan, then gather five Great Songs for the Rite of Chinvat. Nasejuna betrays them at the last Song, and the group finally cleanses the tree Harvisptokhm.
### STANDARD +
The Splendorous Sky That Day: In Gavireh Lajavard the Traveler finds a drum and a note, and Katheryne explains the Akademiya wants the Sign of Apaosha, a strange phenomenon possibly tied to the cataclysm, investigated. After a fight with a Skeptic, Sorush appears and takes the party to Nasejuna, who says the Sign reflects the Abyss and corrupts the land, and that the Great Songs of Khvarena must be gathered for the Rite of Chinvat. Zurvan tells Sorush to prove herself by finding Rashnu and Mihir.

Asipattravana Itihasa: Mihir is found trapped by purple mist in Asipattravana Swamp, weakened by polluted energy. The group helps her complete a cleansing pilgrimage. Sorush and Mihir quarrel, but both agree Pari must do their duties themselves.

Awakening's Real Sound: In the Hills of Barsom Nasejuna tells the history of the Barsom and the Kory Drums, made in honour of Barsom's familiar, which has since stopped answering the Skeptics. The Traveler finds the carvings and drums, plays the ritual, and wakes Rashnu, who reveals she threw the drums away because they disturbed her sleep.

As the Khvarena's Light Shows: Zurvan refuses the chaplet because Sorush is not a Bloomguard, until Sorush prays to the Khvarena and grows one. The first Great Song is at Sunyata Lake; Nasejuna points out the rest. At the last Song, in a Khaenri'ahn factory he calls Dahri, Nasejuna reveals he has only pretended to worship Sorush and wants the Songs to release the Abyss's seal. The Traveler defeats him and an Abyss Herald, and he flees.

The Hymn of Tir Yazad: The group places the Songs at Tunigi Hollow and opens the path to the Harvisptokhm. Sorush sees a vision of the cataclysm, then in the Gaokerena collects Spenta Hearts and performs the Rite of Ab-Zohr, making a Flower of Sanctity bloom. The Sign of Apaosha vanishes and the tree regrows.
### LARGE +
The Order of Skeptics was once allied with the Pari; the Kory Drums sealed that bond, but the Skeptics lost their traditions. Nasejuna's friend Aryadeva, a Skeptics elder who knows the traditions best, has left for Sumeru City to pursue light novels and Genius Invokation TCG, and Nasejuna calls him a ridiculous old geezer while relying on him. The wiki says Nasejuna learned of the Songs from documents from a scholar, and later says the history of the Order of Skeptics was all myth, while the accounted history of the Khaenri'ahns was more factual.

Zurvan reveals the Abyss Herald they fought was once a Skeptic who joined the Abyss Order after entering Khaenri'ahn ruins, and that Abyss monsters attacking the Vourukasha Oasis led her to seal it and other ruins with the Great Songs. She then tells the Traveler about 500 years ago: she rescued a masked swordsman with a ring, and they fought the Abyss together. Scholars from the rainforest led by a one-armed sage arrived, found machines and documents in a ruin, and used them to enter the Harvisptokhm, purifying the land and creating more Pari from a sea of flowers. The masked swordsman left with a golden-haired person; the sage disappeared into the ruins; the other scholars became the ancestors of the Skeptics. Zurvan asks the Traveler to give Dainsleif her regards, sure their paths will cross soon.
### ULTRA +
Mechanical and incidental details from the quests. The Kory Drum ritual uses three actions (attack, plunge attack and standing still) matched to leaf symbols on the musical scores; the three drums lie in the swamp's eastern cave, near Vourukasha Oasis and by the Gate of Zulqarnain, each with a Soul Bell trial. Rifthounds, remnants of past wars, attack at the Gray Gate cave. Mihir accuses Sorush of being swayed by Nasuraja, and Sorush's pride is hurt by Mihir's achievements. After Nasejuna's betrayal Sorush is embarrassed at being tricked for so long, and the Traveler and Paimon reassure her that she is valuable.

## revelations
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Nasejuna feigned devotion to Sorush to take the Great Songs and release the Abyss's seal. The Skeptics' own histories are largely myth. The Sign of Apaosha is cleansed by the Rite of Ab-Zohr, not by Sorush's sacrifice.
### LARGE +
500 years ago Pari, Khaenri'ahn warriors including a masked swordsman (Dainsleif) and a one-armed sage fought the Abyss together, and the Harvisptokhm was purified then. Zurvan sealed the Vourukasha Oasis and other ruins with the Great Songs after Abyss attacks.

## characters
### LITE
The Traveler, Paimon, Sorush, Nasejuna, Zurvan, Mihir and Rashnu.
### STANDARD +
Sorush is a proud Pari who becomes the Traveler's partner and a Bloomguard. Nasejuna is the Skeptics' leader and the series' betrayer. Zurvan is the Pari elder who guards the Songs' secrets. Mihir and Rashnu are Pari: Mihir was stationed in Asipattravana Swamp to suppress corruption, Rashnu was asleep in the Hills of Barsom. Aryadeva, an absent Skeptic elder, is described but not met. Dainsleif and the one-armed sage appear only in vision and testimony.

## consequences
### STANDARD
The Sign of Apaosha is gone and the Harvisptokhm has regrown. Nasejuna escapes. Zurvan expects the Traveler to meet Dainsleif soon. Sorush becomes a Bloomguard and gains confidence.

## placement
### STANDARD
Released in Version 3.6 and set in the northern desert, in the Girdle of the Sands area. It is independent of the Archon Quest and requires only The Outlander Who Caught the Wind.

## entry: Nasejuna's Betrayal

### STANDARD
In Khvarena of Good and Evil, Nasejuna leads the Order of Skeptics and presents himself as a guide to Sorush. At the Khaenri'ahn factory where the last Great Song lies, he reveals he only wanted the Songs to release the Abyss seal. He fights the Traveler with an Abyss Herald and flees.
