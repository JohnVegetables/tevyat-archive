## summary
@spoiler: none
### LITE
Fontaine Research Institute Chronicles is a world quest series added in version 4.1. After the explosion that wrecked most of the Fontaine Research Institute, the surviving staff recruit outside adventurers. The Traveler and Paimon go through a slow qualification review, then escort a senior researcher named Nacker into the floating ruins of the Central Laboratory. A reporter from The Steambird, Chronie, follows them.
### STANDARD +
The series is a comedy of paperwork and institutional self-protection wrapped around a real danger: unstable Arkhium left over from the experiment of Edwin Eastinghouse. It consists of one main quest, "Fontaine Research Institute, Stagnating in the Rubble", and two child quests that retrieve the last two Survey Anchors. It requires The Outlander Who Caught the Wind (the Fontaine prologue) to be completed.
### LARGE +
The tone mixes bureaucratic satire with a serious thread: the Institute's public account of the disaster, its internal factions, and a journalist's attempt to get at the truth. The Traveler ends as an "Honorary Senior Researcher", but the quest leaves open who is lying about the missing core.

## plot
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
The Traveler is told by Katheryne at the Adventurers' Guild in Fontaine, or runs into a recruiter named Desnos at the Qualifications Review Representative Office, that the Fontaine Research Institute needs outside help recovering assets from its ruined Central Laboratory. Before anyone may start, the Traveler must pass a layered qualification review. After a fight with two Recon Log Meks in the office and stacks of forms, the Traveler is assigned to escort Nacker, a senior researcher who once worked under Edwin Eastinghouse, to the ruins. The political reporter Chronie of The Steambird attaches herself to the group.

The first test is retrieving a Survey Anchor, a device that monitors Arkhium energy flow. The Traveler clears an obstruction at the Materials Office and takes the Anchor without the protective instruments the Institute normally requires, which impresses the staff. At the new Institute the co-administrators Raimondo and Choiseul praise the feat, deflect Chronie's questions, and send the group to collect the last two Anchors from the dormitories and the Institute of Clockwork Applications. Having passed the review, the Traveler goes with Nacker to the floating ruins and helps start a plan to land them safely.

A second, unplanned explosion scatters components of the Arkhium inhibitor. Raimondo and the Traveler stabilise the large Arkhium and shut down the Experimental Field Generator. Nacker has vanished. Raimondo blames him for stealing a core and detonating the Arkhium as a diversion; Chronie suspects the Institute is hiding how dangerous the ruins still are. The Traveler may either keep to Raimondo's version or tell Chronie everything.
### LARGE
**Stagnating in the Rubble: the review.** At the Qualifications Review Representative Office Desnos, who manages recruitment, is being pestered by Recon Log Meks, and the Traveler defeats them. The forms are deliberately absurd. Raimondo later says the paperwork is mostly useful for weeding out "idiots" and is not what he really judges applicants by.

**Nacker and Chronie.** Nacker explains that the Institute was once the technological centre of the Court of Fontaine and that Edwin Eastinghouse, working on a gravity-lifting field generator powered by Arkhium, left roughly two-thirds of the Institute in ruins. He says Edwin would otherwise have been the next Director. Chronie, who has already tried to interview Choiseul, keeps cross-examining Nacker. She also raises that research materials are being sold off and asks who among the staff could do it. Nacker admits the Anchors are kept locked in a room because they were repeatedly stolen.

**The Survey Anchor.** The Anchors radiate Arkhium energy. Exposure makes Chronie violently ill, and the staff member Chalmet gives her medicine. Nacker has the Traveler operate an Energy Transfer Terminal and reach the Anchor through the radiation. Choiseul and Raimondo are astonished that it was done without instruments, since the Institute has only three complete sets.

**Dormitories and Clockwork Applications.** The child quests are short. In the dormitories Nacker explains that researchers think sleep is a waste of time and use the space to rest their brains by playing piano or reading. He also remarks that earlier applicants who tried the Anchor test used up a lot of antiemetics.

**To the ruins.** The group dives into allogravity-condensed water to reach the floating Central Laboratory ruins. Nacker takes the Traveler to the Experimental Field Generator that Edwin built. The thing that exploded earlier was not the Generator but the Arkhium ore that powers its Arkhium Kinetic Core. Nacker plans to use Raimondo's catalyst to turn inert Arkhium into stable Tri-State Arkhium and run the Generator at half speed so the ruins can land. He sends the Traveler to take photographs for his work record while Chronie waits elsewhere.

**The second explosion.** After the Traveler returns, another explosion occurs. Nacker has gone, and the Arkhium has overloaded while its cooling system is damaged. Raimondo and the Traveler fight a new wave of meka awakened by the energy, recover four condensing components and shut the Generator down. Raimondo explains that the Institute's Gravity Fields hold the Arkhium fragments together beneath the ruins and that this blast was a small rehearsal of a third great explosion.

**The two stories.** Raimondo claims Nacker stole the core and used the large Arkhium to draw attention, and that Nacker and other successors of Edwin sell information. In front of Chronie he calls the blast a "controllable hazard removal directional blast", but he also admits it was a small resonance of Arkhium fragments that someone must have piled up on purpose. Chronie rejects the Institute's account. The Traveler either sticks to Raimondo's request or tells Chronie the whole story; if told, she says she will report that the ruins can explode again and that the Institute tried to use the Traveler's credibility to blame Nacker. Either way, Choiseul gives the Traveler a certificate and trophy as Honorary Senior Researcher and poses for a group photo.
### ULTRA +
**Raimondo's account of Edwin (optional dialogue).** Raimondo calls Edwin a genius second only to the first Director, Alain Guillotin, but also an "inhuman monster" who cared about nothing but research. He says people under Edwin sold research assets, plagiarised results and used his name to scam others while Edwin ignored it as long as work advanced, and that Choiseul's reviews of Edwin's team were read as bureaucratic distrust. He says the Arkhium Kinetic Core was the fruit of his own joint research with Edwin, and that they split over a conservative plan versus a faster one after finding the large, very pure Arkhium chunk.

**Evidence about Nacker.** Raimondo says a blurry photo from Chronie shows Nacker working on the suppression device in a way not found in any procedure. This is the only concrete evidence given. Raimondo hopes Nacker and the core were destroyed so that the last trace of Edwin's influence is gone, then doubts it because most of the nearby fragments were spent ore waste that should not have exploded so violently.

**Gameplay and rewards (labelled as such).** The quest gives the achievements A Perfect Yesterday (reading a note in the Materials Office), Mired in Red Tape and This Ends Here, and the Honorary Senior Researcher Award. Afterwards the sky over the Institute region stays dark blue.

## revelations
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
- The Fontaine Research Institute of Kinetic Energy Engineering was once the technological centre of the Court of Fontaine. The explosion of Edwin Eastinghouse's Arkhium experiment destroyed most of it.
- The Central Laboratory ruins float, held up by a Gravity-Altering Field. The Institute plans to land them with an Experimental Field Generator running on a "Tri-State Arkhium".
- Survey Anchors measure Arkhium energy flow and are physically harmful to approach without instruments.
### LARGE +
- The Arkhium under the ruins is held together by Gravity Fields and can resonate again. Raimondo calls a further blast a "third great explosion".
- The Institute has been selling off or losing research assets for some time, and Raimondo and Nacker each blame the other's circle.
- Alain Guillotin was the first Director of the Institute.

## characters
### STANDARD
The Traveler and Paimon; Desnos, the recruiter; Nacker, a senior researcher who once worked under Edwin; Chalmet and Conjat, Institute staff; Raimondo and Choiseul, who run the new Institute; and Chronie, a political affairs journalist for The Steambird. Edwin Eastinghouse appears only through other people's accounts.
### LARGE +
Chronie is relentless and likes to interview with evidence in hand, and she does not back down when the Institute stonewalls her. Choiseul is evasive and mostly repeats praise for the Institute. Raimondo is smooth and controlled, courteous but openly cynical about forms. Nacker is polite, blunt about the Institute's flaws and the least forthcoming about himself.

## consequences
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
The Traveler becomes an Honorary Senior Researcher. The ruins are made safer for the moment but can explode again. Nacker and the Arkhium Kinetic Core are missing. Chronie goes away with a story critical of the Institute, or with the full truth if the Traveler tells it.
### LARGE +
Raimondo says Chronie will probably leave the Institute alone for a while once she has her story. The main quest has a follow-up, the world quest "An Expected Plan", which this entry does not cover.

## placement
### STANDARD
Requires The Outlander Who Caught the Wind, the Fontaine prologue. Released in version 4.1. The two Anchor-retrieval quests are children of the main quest and are completed in either order.

## entry: Edwin Eastinghouse's Arkhium experiment
@keys: Edwin Eastinghouse, Experimental Field Generator, Arkhium Kinetic Core, Central Laboratory
@keys2: Fontaine Research Institute, explosion, Arkhium
@version: 4.1
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
According to Nacker, Edwin Eastinghouse was developing a field generator powered by Arkhium that could lift an entire block of buildings. The Arkhium ore powering its Arkhium Kinetic Core exploded, leaving most of the Institute in ruins. The Generator itself survived. Raimondo says the core was the result of his own joint research with Edwin.
### LARGE +
Raimondo and Edwin had trouble keeping the core powered and tried a mix of Pneumousia and Arkhium energy and chains of ore before finding a huge, inert and unusually pure Arkhium chunk. They disagreed about how fast to treat it. Raimondo calls Edwin a genius and a monster who wanted only results.

## entry: Survey Anchors
@keys: Survey Anchor, Survey Anchors
@keys2: Fontaine Research Institute, Arkhium
@version: 4.1
@spoiler: none
### STANDARD
Survey Anchors are Institute devices used to monitor the strength of Arkhium energy flows. Proper retrieval needs special instruments, and exposure makes people ill. The Institute locks them up because they kept being stolen. Retrieving an Anchor serves as part of the qualification review for outside applicants, and Nacker says it consumed a lot of antiemetics.

## entry: Chronie of The Steambird
@keys: Chronie
@keys2: Steambird, Fontaine Research Institute
@version: 4.1
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Chronie is a journalist in the political affairs section of The Steambird. She follows Nacker and the Traveler, asks pointed questions about Arkhium safety and the sale of research assets, and refuses to be fobbed off. She gets sick from Anchor radiation but keeps going.

## entry: Raimondo and Choiseul
@keys: Raimondo, Choiseul
@keys2: Fontaine Research Institute, new Institute
@version: 4.1
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Raimondo and Choiseul are the co-administrators of the new Fontaine Research Institute. Raimondo, a Senior Technician, drives the Tri-State Arkhium plan and welcomes the Traveler as an Honorary Senior Researcher. Choiseul handles reviews and public statements and is evasive when Chronie presses him.
