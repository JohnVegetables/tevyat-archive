## summary
@version: 2.6
### LITE
A three-part Version 2.6 world quest series in The Chasm: Underground Mines. After The Chasm Delvers, the young cartographer Zhiqiong still works for the exploration team. The Traveler helps her place measuring beacons, rescues her when she is trapped in the dark mud, and finds the letter she leaves when she goes deeper alone.
### STANDARD +
It requires The Chasm Delvers and is released as three quests on consecutive days: Valor's Afterglow, Return by Sundown, and The Faint Light Remembered. Its theme is what drives ordinary mortals without Visions to risk their lives for the chance of being remembered.
### LARGE +
Zhiqiong's reasoning is voiced through her talk with the Traveler about Stanley's lost friend, through Jinwu's and Muning's worry, and through her long farewell letter. The series ends with her gone and the camp's members unsure whether they were right to hold her back.

## plot
@version: 2.6
### STANDARD
Part 1, Valor's Afterglow (Day 1). The Chasm is now safer, so Zhiqiong asks the Traveler to help place five measuring beacons built by Jinwu. The beacons track the dark mud and gather data for the scholar Khedive to analyse. The Traveler returns with her to the camp and is thanked. If the Traveler talks to her again, she explains why she is so stubborn and reckless: adventurers are drawn to danger, and she hopes to be remembered as one who faced The Chasm.

Part 2, Return by Sundown (Day 2). Jinwu is distraught: Zhiqiong went to retrieve the beacons and has been away five or six hours. The Traveler searches the beacon sites and finds her south of the Stony Halls, trapped in the dark mud and attacked by monsters. After clearing the monsters, the Traveler carries her to the camp. Khedive gives her medicine, and the Traveler brings her Jinwu's soup. Zhiqiong apologises and asks the Traveler to come back later.

Part 3, The Faint Light Remembered (Day 3). Zhiqiong has vanished from the camp, insisting on leaving. Jinwu and the Traveler go up to tell Muning, who learns she has gone and organises a search party. After the quest, the Traveler finds her abandoned camp in the southwest corner of The Glowing Narrows with her letter and a Frostglaze Crystal. The letter says she will follow a path deeper. Back at camp, Jinwu reads it and admits she cannot judge Zhiqiong.
### LARGE +
In Part 1 Zhiqiong asks whether Stanley's friend who did not return is truly dead, or "alive" because adventurers still remember him. She says she would rather leave an impression even in failure than be forgotten. She also says she does not adventure for anyone else, or even for herself. She describes mortals without Visions, in a world ordered by the gods, refusing to go quietly, and quotes a saying she heard from a senior from Sumeru who heard it from the Eremites: someone not afraid of dying so that they might truly live.

In Part 2 Zhiqiong, half-conscious, murmurs that Jinwu should not be disappointed, and speaks of a golden city, a black watchtower and envoys having left. Paimon dismisses it as nonsense. Jinwu reflects that she has watched many young people treat adventure as noble only to find it too late to regret, and wonders whether she even has the right to stop her.

In Part 3 Muning reveals that the Ministry of Civil Affairs had decided, because her maps were so useful to Huishan Hall, to invite Zhiqiong to become a formal consultant and guide. He argues that dreams should not come before life, and says he had hoped she had forgotten her unrealistic dreams of adventurers like Roald and Stanley. He admits he wondered whether asking the Traveler to come to The Chasm had reignited her drive.
### ULTRA +
Gameplay details per the wiki: Parts 1 and 2 each give Adventure EXP, 30 Primogems and 30,000 Mora (plus Hero's Wit), and Part 3 gives a smaller reward. The sites for the beacons are around the dark mud, and in Part 2 she is found at the beacon site south of the Stony Halls; the other sites need not be checked once she is found. Her letter includes a hand-drawn map.

## revelations
@version: 2.6
### STANDARD
Zhiqiong's motives are laid out: she wants to be remembered, to put her own mortal names on the unmapped places of The Chasm, and to show how far someone not chosen by the gods can go. Her letter also questions how the gods and the Traveler regard ordinary mortals and their labour, asked at a time when Rex Lapis had only just left Liyue.
### LARGE +
The letter describes what she saw: stone halls crawling into the dark, purple mud gushing like lava, translucent azure crystals floating in the air, damaged stone tablets carved with maps of ancient places. She left half-finished names on her map as a parting gift. It also gives each team member a personal farewell, thanks the Traveler, and says she is not blessed with a Vision and that Khedive may be right that the Chasm's secrets are beyond mere mortals.

## characters
@version: 2.6
### STANDARD
Zhiqiong: young, headstrong cartographer-adventurer who admires famed adventurers and keeps going despite injury. Jinwu: a veteran of the mines who builds the beacons and looks after the young adventurers, gruff and caring; she gave up on becoming great and feels she cannot judge. Khedive: a scholar of the team (Zhiqiong calls his terminology Sumeru jargon) who analyses the data and gives Zhiqiong medicine. Muning: a Ministry of Civil Affairs official at the surface who sees her as a friend and is angered by her risk-taking. Paimon and the Traveler: support her, rescue her, and disagree on whether to stop her.
### LARGE +
Her letter also names Clitopho, a former Treasure Hoarder, whom she apologises to for her earlier distrust; and wishes Leonard, Stanley and Roald as her adventuring models. Wiki trivia notes that Stanley's story, as revealed in the later quest Should You Be Trapped in a Windless Land, is not quite as Zhiqiong believed. The real Stanley died in the Mare Jivari, and his friend Hans Archibald took on his identity so he would not be forgotten. Zhiqiong's remarks in this series do not reflect that.

## consequences
@version: 2.6
### STANDARD
Zhiqiong's camp stays in The Glowing Narrows permanently. Collecting her letter gives the achievement "Valor's Afterglow" and unlocks a new viewpoint, The Court of Pillars. Her story is left open: the team does not know whether she will return.

## placement
@version: 2.6
### STANDARD
Released in Version 2.6 as a direct follow-up to The Chasm Delvers. Each part is unlocked after the daily reset following the previous one. Release and story order match. Her letter says Rex Lapis has only just left, which places the series shortly after his departure from Liyue.

## entry: Zhiqiong's Letter
@keys: Zhiqiong's Letter, Zhiqiong letter
@version: 2.6
### STANDARD
A quest item found in a camp in the southwest corner of The Glowing Narrows after The Faint Light Remembered. Zhiqiong addresses Jinwu, Khedive, Clitopho, Muning and the Traveler, asking questions about how the gods see mortals, and announces she will go deeper into The Chasm to mark it on her map with names that belong to mortals. The item text notes a slightly bitter scent of Qingxin.

## entry: Jinwu
@keys: Jinwu
@keys2: Zhiqiong, Chasm, exploration team
@version: 2.6
### STANDARD
Jinwu is a long-serving miner at the Chasm exploration team's camp. She builds the measuring beacons, cooks the soup for Zhiqiong, and watches over the young adventurers. She says she gave up on becoming great and has grown used to ordinary days, so she feels she cannot judge Zhiqiong's choice.

## entry: Muning
@keys: Muning
@keys2: Zhiqiong, Chasm, Ministry of Civil Affairs
@version: 2.6
### STANDARD
Muning is a Ministry of Civil Affairs official who helped Zhiqiong get her post in The Chasm. He is Zhiqiong's friend and, when she leaves, organises the Millelith search party. He tells the Traveler that the Ministry had decided to invite her as a formal consultant and guide.
