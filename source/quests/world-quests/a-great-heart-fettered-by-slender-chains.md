## summary
@spoiler: minor
### LITE
A Great Heart Fettered by Slender Chains is a Snezhnaya World Quest added in version 7.0. In a blizzard in the Everfrozen Earth, the Traveler and Paimon find a huge snow giant bound to the ground by chains of snow and ice. The chains can be broken only with help from a girl's shadow, who leads them through four small scenes from a house and its past. When all four are broken, the storm clears and the giant stands and walks away.
### STANDARD +
It starts when the player approaches the figure in the storm. It gives Adventure EXP, Primogems, Mora, Hero's Wit and Mystic Enhancement Ore as rewards (game-mechanics information), and the next quest in the chain is "Like a Labyrinth Imprisons Her Servants".
### LARGE +
The tone is quiet and almost wordless. Much of the story comes from short remembered voices and the setting of the objects, rather than from the Traveler's conversations. It is a good short scene source for Snezhnaya's mix of fae, human memory and Prime Ice constructs.

## plot
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
In the blizzard the Traveler and Paimon see a huge silhouette, a snow giant held down by chains of snow and ice. Paimon wonders how to help. Each of the four chains is guarded by a seal that only a ghost-like figure, the shadow of a girl, can break, and every time she reaches one the Traveler has to clear the way and then break the chain itself.

Chain 1. A man-made Chimeric Horned Bear is nearby. After it is defeated, the shadow comes out from behind some crates and breaks the seal on the nearest chain, then the Traveler destroys the device that controls the chains.

Chain 2. The shadow cannot cross a gap, so the Traveler places a Launching Platform. She then runs toward a Prime Ice window, which the Traveler breaks with the Eye of Graeae's Fab-Vision. In a corridor guarded by Snegurochka statues she kneels behind crates, and two voices are heard. A compassionate voice says Lady Pavlina has ordered that the human girl must not meet "him", and calls this too cruel. A cold voice, identified as Pavlina, replies that it could get Donskoy killed: his spirit was damaged during the war and he needs a long peaceful sleep. The Traveler destroys the statues, and the compassionate voice shows a secret passage by which she might glimpse Donskoy while he sleeps. More statues trap her and are destroyed, and she breaks the seal.

Chain 3. A buried wooden house plays faint music behind frozen doors. The Traveler goes underground, melts walls of ice, and meets the shadow in a room with a gentle male voice, who is Donskoy, asking for a phonograph to be placed beneath the picture frame, on the larger chair and beneath the cabinet, with lines about one last dance and being put "in my place" if he does not return from battle. After the phonographs are placed correctly she packs up the phonograph, the ice on the door breaks, and she goes out to break the seal.
### LARGE +
Chain 4. The Traveler builds a Snowball Launcher and sends a snowball through the Gate of the Sprouting Tsar's White Birch, then helps the shadow paint. An easel is empty until the Traveler places a Pale-Furred Wolf Construct and a Red-Bellied Plover Construct and sits on the chair: Paimon guesses she wants to paint a person surrounded by animals. A note found near the easel, "In Search of Inspiration", says animals that like warmth avoid Snegovik because of the cold, but ice sculptures and drawings of real animals might still fulfil his wish. The shadow paints, an ice wall breaks, and she goes to the last chain.

After all four are broken the snow giant wakes and slowly walks away. Paimon wonders where he went and how the girl is linked to him, and expects to meet him again.
### ULTRA +
Post-quest. At 18:00 in-game time the Eye of Graeae sends a message, "Regarding the giant figure by the sea...", saying people have reported a giant surrounded by ice and snow near the sea. The Eye compares data and thinks it may be the one chained in the canyon, and marks the area on the map as "Gigantic Figure by the Sea".

## revelations
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
- Pavlina has forbidden a human girl from meeting Donskoy, because his spirit was damaged in the war and he needs a long, undisturbed sleep.
- Donskoy's voice, in memory, speaks of a last dance and of leaving a phonograph "in my place" if he does not return from battle.
- Snegovik and Snegurochka statues help guard the places the shadow passes, and the shadow is afraid of them.
### LARGE +
- The painting scene suggests that Donskoy wished for a portrait of a person with animals, though the quest only hints at this through a note and Paimon's guess.

## characters
### STANDARD
- The Traveler and Paimon.
- The snow giant, who is probably Donskoy (inferred, not stated).
- The girl's shadow, who is unnamed.
- Pavlina, heard as the cold voice, and an unnamed compassionate voice who helps the girl.
- The Eye of Graeae, whose Fab-Vision breaks Prime Ice and who sends the later message.
### LARGE +
- Chimeric Horned Bears and Snegurochka statues appear as guardians; the bear is described as a man-made (aberrant chimeric) monster.

## consequences
### STANDARD
The giant is freed from his chains and leaves the canyon, and the blizzard ends. The Eye of Graeae's message sends the Traveler toward the coast and the next quest. No lasting change to named characters is shown within this quest.

## placement
### STANDARD
Story order: a standalone World Quest in the Everfrozen Earth, before "Like a Labyrinth Imprisons Her Servants". Release order: version 7.0. It shares Pavlina and Snegovik themes with Hesperides of Love and Hate.

## entry: Donskoy
@keys: Donskoy
@version: 7.0
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
In this quest Donskoy is a name spoken about and, in the page's attribution, a gentle male voice heard in a memory. The cold voice says his spirit was damaged in the war and that he needs long, peaceful sleep, so he must not be disturbed. The quest does not describe him directly, but the sleeping snow giant is very likely him.
