## summary
@spoiler: minor
### LITE
Canticles of Harmony is a five-part Fontaine World Quest chapter added in version 4.6, after Song of the Dragon and Freedom. The people of the town of Petrichor fall into a dreamlike state, and the Traveler and Paimon follow a strange talking cat, Osse, through the Faded Castle to the drowned Remurian capital to end the corrupted Symphony of the ancient empire of Remuria and send the stolen souls home.
### STANDARD +
The parts are Prelude "Petrichorror Dream" (The Shadow Over Petrichor), First Movement "In the Hall of the Sea King" (Underwater Nocturne), Second Movement "Prisoner in Shackles" (The Last Day of Remuria), Third Movement "Pharsalia's Rhapsody" (Gradus ad Capitolium, with two Golden Aqueduct Reconstruction sub-quests) and Finale "Requiem" (Fortune Plango Vulnera). It shows the story of Remuria and its ruin through the Traveler's dreams and the words of Osse, Scylla and Boethius.
### LARGE +
Titles and music. The wiki notes that the chapter takes its parts from musical terms and compositions (the second part echoes "In the Hall of the Mountain King"; the third alludes to the Latin poem Pharsalia; Fortune Plango Vulnera is a movement of Carmina Burana). The quest is mostly concerned with ending a corrupted Symphony and giving the Remurians' trapped souls rest.

## plot
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Prelude. In Petrichor the Traveler and Paimon meet a gang who say they are rescuing treasure from the "Dark Lord Remus" and are chased off. The town's people are dazed and speak as if in ancient times; a "Bellringer" falls unconscious. Este of the Daydream Club, who is unaffected, tells them of the legend of the Faded Castle. A glowing cat, Osse, calls himself Ouranides of Ouranopolis, a spirit-keeper of the ancient realm, and says the Symphony has taken the townsfolk's souls. He asks the Traveler to help him open a hidden path to the castle, and the Traveler collects harp strings, which Osse says are the Symphony's scores. The Ancient Score and an Ancient Autoharmonic Music Box are the quest rewards.

First Movement. In the Faded Castle Osse (called Monsieur Os) shows the Traveler a library and a stage, where they obtain a Blank Score and perform a play. The Traveler sees the memories of the God King Remus; Osse explains that Remurians were a stone-bodied race with Ichor for blood, that the Symphony was meant to weave fate, that Boethius was a noble Harmost who became a monster, and that Osse himself became a hunter of phantoms to oppose him.

Second Movement. Osse (identified as Cassiodor) and the Traveler enter the Sea of Bygone Eras. They free Scylla, a dragon prince who was sealed by Boethius at the Initium Iani, by breaking three seals. They meet a nameless golem who still guards a fortress and asks whether Remuria won, and lingering Remurian shadows and soldiers.

Third Movement. At the sunken capital the group reach the golden palace Domus Aurea. Scylla rebuilds the golden aqueducts with the Traveler, and they play the Symphony to open the gate. Boethius tells Cassiodor to join him and release the souls.

Finale. Cassiodor joins Boethius to trap Phobos in one body and urges the Traveler to destroy them both. Phobos reveals itself as the Symphony. The Traveler strikes; Cassiodor fades. The Traveler plays the Requiem, the souls return to the sea, and the Traveler comes back to Petrichor.
### LARGE +
Prelude details. A stone shard found in the bandits' crates replaces the "treasure"; they were taking statues from the ruins. The Traveler dreams as the "Imperator" and sees a speaker prepare to sound the Symphony Phobos to break the "shackles of Fortuna".

First Movement details. Osse explains the library as an archive where ancient plays and scores were gathered. He says that, in the past, "he and I and many companions" dwelt in the castle when the nation was new. Pipes and books respond to the music box, and the Traveler solves musical puzzles to get the Blank Score.

Second Movement details. Cassiodor says Scylla, once a friend of the Sebastos, led dragonborn to Machimos and was sealed when the Initium Iani was shut. Scylla replies that the attack was part of Remus's secret plan. The Traveler meets a Remurian golem who recognises Dominus Cassiodor and remembers a war that Remuria lost. They also see a replay of Legio IX troops carrying off the loot from tribal lands, which Este opposes.
### ULTRA +
Third Movement details. Boethius meets Cassiodor and the Traveler at Domus Aurea and says he serves no god or king but "mighty Remuria". He accuses Remus of colluding with dragons and of attempting to destroy the Grand Symphony, and says he spent thousands of years weaving the broken melodies together. He asks Cassiodor to join him. Scylla declines to enter the palace, saying he is merely occupying a body of Ichor.

Finale details. After Phobos is destroyed Praetorian Golems execute Boethius's wounded form. Cassiodor says he is certain the people of the present will live normal, happy lives, quotes lines about water and soil, and asks the Traveler to play the Requiem. Scylla says Cassiodor's soul must have returned to the High Sea and notes that a new true dragon, Neuvillette, has been born, and that he may go seek an audience.

## revelations
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
- The Remurians were a stone-bodied race with Ichor for blood, made by Remus so they could resist fate.
- The Symphony Phobos was built by Remus from the wisdom of Sybilla and his own power, and became a war-guiding will that consumed Boethius.
- Osse is Cassiodor, once a Harmost of Remuria, later one who hunts phantoms and is called the Golden Hunter.
- Scylla, once Remus's friend, was lured into a trap and turned to stone by Boethius.
- Phobos is destroyed in the Finale; Cassiodor does not survive it.
### LARGE +
- Cassiodor learns that Remus aimed to free everyone from the Symphony through his own sacrifice.
- The Traveler's dream shows Remus planning the ritual meant to defy the prophecy of ruin.
- Many Remurian souls remain bound beneath the sea; Cassiodor says they are held by their own obsession, and Boethius claims they are imprisoned.

## characters
### STANDARD
The Traveler, Paimon, Osse (Monsieur Os, Cassiodor), Boethius, Scylla, Remus (in memories), Phobos, Este (human of the Daydream Club, and a Remurian golem), the "Bellringer", and bandits in Petrichor.

## consequences
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Phobos and Boethius are gone and the souls are sent back to the High Sea; the Traveler ends the chapter in Petrichor. Cassiodor is no more and Scylla is free to roam the waters. The chapter's reward track is "Canticles of Harmony" in the Fontaine Reputation, and rewards include the Ancient Score and the Ancient Autoharmonic Music Box.

## placement
### STANDARD
Released in 4.6. Requires Song of the Dragon and Freedom. It is the Fontaine chapter that explains the origin of Remuria and the Faded Castle.

## entry: Osse and Monsieur Os
@keys: Osse, Monsieur Os, Ouranides of Ouranopolis
@keys2: Petrichor, Faded Castle, Remuria
@version: 4.6
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Osse is a white cat with a bronze collar and pendant who speaks in formal, archaic language, calls himself Ouranides of Ouranopolis and is later called Monsieur Os by Paimon. He turns out to be Cassiodor, a Remurian Harmost who stayed behind to keep the past from rising. He fades at the end of the chapter.

## entry: Phobos, the Symphony of Remuria
@keys: Phobos, Grand Symphony, Symphony of Remuria
@keys2: Boethius, Remus, Sybilla
@version: 4.6
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Phobos is a vast Symphony that Remus wrote so people could resist fate; in the Finale it says it was Sybilla's wisdom joined to Remus's power. It answered everyone's wishes, including hateful ones, and devoured Boethius. Cassiodor and the Traveler destroy it together.

## entry: Scylla in Canticles of Harmony
@keys: Scylla
@keys2: Initium Iani, Remus, Boethius
@version: 4.6
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Scylla is a Hydro vishap prince who was Remus's friend, was turned to stone at the Initium Iani and is freed in Second Movement. He speaks in an old-fashioned way, calls the Traveler and Cassiodor "little ones", helps rebuild the golden aqueducts, and sends the souls back. He says his attack was part of Remus's secret plan.

## entry: Este of the Daydream Club
@keys: Este
@keys2: Petrichor, Daydream Club, Remuria
@version: 4.6
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Este is a human member of Petrichor's Daydream Club who is unaffected by the Symphony and who tells the Traveler about the legend of the Faded Castle. Osse suggests he is a descendant of a Remurian who followed Cassiodor, and a golem named Este appears in Second Movement in a memory of Remuria.

## entry: Domus Aurea and the golden aqueducts
@keys: Domus Aurea, golden aqueducts, Initium Iani
@keys2: Scylla, Capitolium, Boethius
@version: 4.6
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Domus Aurea is the golden palace of Capitolium, built from a giant ship to broadcast the Symphony through pipes and golden waterways. To enter it, the Traveler and Scylla restore the aqueducts in the two Golden Aqueduct Reconstruction sub-quests and sound the symphony.
