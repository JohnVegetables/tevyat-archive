## summary
@spoiler: none
### LITE
Unfinished Comedy is a Fontaine World Quest series from version 4.1, set in the Fortress of Meropide. A prisoner called Caterpillar, who looks like a boy but says he is not a child, wants to get a young girl named Lanoire out of the fortress and back to her family. She was born inside and has no papers. The Traveler and Paimon join the plan, win a contest called Coupon Millionaire, and help the pair escape.
### STANDARD +
The series has three quests, played in order: Game of the Rich, An Eye for an Eye, and Hereafter... It requires The Outlander Who Caught the Wind to have been completed, and begins when the Traveler is caught by the fortress searchlights, enters through the dormitory pipes, or teleports in once the fortress is unlocked during To the Stars Shining in the Depths. The Fontaine Reputation menu lists it as "Caterpillar's Story: Prologue", and its name echoes a 1957 Chinese film, The Unfinished Comedy.
### LARGE +
The tone starts as a comic prison farce full of paperwork, Coupons and competing schemes, and darkens when Caterpillar changes shape and Noailles shows how far he will take the rules. Its centre is Caterpillar's wish to be more human through compassion, set against a fortress that treats people as case files.

## plot
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
**Game of the Rich.** The Traveler and Paimon are taken to reception in the Fortress of Meropide. The receptionist Monglane records them and sends them to the prisoner Darcy, who takes them to the guard Estienne. Estienne has invented a game called Coupon Millionaire: whoever earns the most Coupons wins a supervised job outside, under the sun. A small girl, Lanoire, says she will win because her friend Cater told her so, and a stricter new guard, Noailles, breaks up the crowd. The Traveler follows Lanoire around the fortress, meets Caterpillar and learns that she was born here, that her mother Agnes died of a long illness, and that she has family outside but no proof of identity. Caterpillar proposes using the game as an exit. The Traveler and Paimon join his team, the Kids Team, over several days of commissions.

**An Eye for an Eye.** The team wins and Estienne takes them outside at night, but Noailles intercepts them with Gardemeks. Caterpillar turns into an Anemo Hilichurl Rogue to fight. Noailles offers a deal, breaks it, and takes everyone back. Estienne and the guard Maupoil help them leave a second time, and the Traveler opens the Geode Mine Shaft's sealed passage. Noailles follows and is beaten, and Estienne takes him into custody. The group leaves.

**Hereafter...** Caterpillar leads Lanoire and the Traveler along a hidden path to a house with a garden of purple flowers at the foot of the mountains to the southeast. Earnshaw, her grandfather, takes her in. Caterpillar stays outside and tells Lanoire the first part of a story about a mage's apprentice and a little fish.
### LARGE +
**Game of the Rich in more detail.** The rival teams are Darcy's, the researchers Genevieve and Poiret, Rave's, and the Kids Team. Other prisoners such as Henaff, Delassaux and Nizami are seen around them. Monglane's records for Caterpillar are missing. When Noailles questions him, Caterpillar says he was sentenced for a task done under a master's orders, and gives an account that makes Noailles abandon the question and turn instead to a misused loudspeaker. Paimon senses something is wrong; Caterpillar admits he made Noailles "certain" of one plain fact, and says such tricks have limits. Rave later offers to team up and calls Caterpillar a monster in human skin; Noailles interrupts. Caterpillar says Rave once tried to have him taught a lesson. On the first two nights Caterpillar tells Lanoire a story about a girl in a giant's garden who is lured over the wall by a witch needing purple flowers and cannot return; Lanoire says she dislikes it. On the third night Lanoire tells her own version, in which the witch makes a deal and everyone is happy. At the final tally Genevieve's team has handed its Coupons to Rave, giving Rave 8,250. Caterpillar's team reaches 2,003,480, because he spent a long-saved hoard of Coupons to hire many workers, and he says the rules allow it. Estienne names Caterpillar, the Traveler and Paimon the winners.

**An Eye for an Eye in more detail.** Outside the walls Lanoire sees no walls, only sky, and takes the moon for something that would hurt her eyes if it were the sun. Noailles arrives with Gardemeks, calls them all criminals even if their records are missing, and Estienne is attacked too. After the Anemo transformation, Noailles accepts Caterpillar's offer to return to the fortress in exchange for Lanoire leaving, then says no one makes deals with monsters and takes all of them back. In the fortress Caterpillar answers the Traveler's questions: he came more than four hundred years ago, belonged to the Narzissenkreuz Ordo, and was a hilichurl to whom the master Narzissenkreuz gave speech and intelligence. He says he helps Lanoire out of compassion, which he practises by imitating how a human would act, and that he believes his own sins can never be absolved. Maupoil lets them out. The four cross the Production Zone by drawing guards away with a searchlight and the Rochelle Loudspeaker, then reach the Geode Mine Shaft, where a research terminal needs two people at once and a note warns that a Flying Energy Thief hunts anyone carrying an energy storage device. The Traveler and Paimon collect three energy devices while Caterpillar and Lanoire hold the terminals. Noailles catches up there without Gardemeks and is defeated. Estienne arrives, says Noailles used the Gardemeks without authorisation, and tells the group not to come back.

**Hereafter... in more detail.** Caterpillar tells Lanoire the goodbye is only temporary, and tells Paimon that children quickly forget, so she will forget him. Lanoire asks whether the characters at the end of a story ever meet again. Earnshaw learns what happened to Agnes from the Traveler and Lanoire, says Lanoire's home is now with him, and asks them to invite Caterpillar to visit. Caterpillar says they will meet again, then offers to tell the Traveler more about the Ordo.
### ULTRA +
**Ways in.** The Traveler can reach the fortress by being caught by the searchlights, by sneaking in through the broken piping, or by teleporting there. During Game of the Rich, at certain Coupon steps, the twelve small quests of Scenes from Life in Meropide unlock.

**Caterpillar and the Traveler.** If An Artist Adrift has been completed, Paimon links the name Caterpillar to the painter Julien in Sumeru. Caterpillar says Julien already had the skill and that he only gave him "certainty". This is the series' only link to that quest.

**The stories.** The mage's apprentice tale told in Hereafter... has a teacher who forbids the apprentice to go out and a fish separated from its family in the sea. The series does not say whether any of the stories are Caterpillar's own memories.

**The contest.** Genevieve criticises the vague rules and proposes restarting with clearer ones; when the others will not, she gives up her team's Coupons, and says she has been in the fortress before for striking someone in anger. Rave says it is a matter of honour. Estienne says the game would have been more exciting if everyone used the rules to the full.

## revelations
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
- Lanoire was born inside the Fortress of Meropide. She is the granddaughter of Earnshaw, and her mother Agnes died of a chronic illness in the fortress.
- Caterpillar is not a child. He has been in the fortress for over four hundred years.
- Caterpillar is a hilichurl who can change how he looks; his real form is an Anemo Hilichurl Rogue.
### LARGE +
- Caterpillar says he was taught speech, memory and intelligence by Narzissenkreuz and served in the Narzissenkreuz Ordo, whose plan, he says, failed. He says the ceremonial Holy Blade of Narzissenkreuz no longer exists.
- Caterpillar says he can make pure-hearted people "certain" of a statement, with limits and unpredictable results.
- Estienne could take Lanoire out himself, but chooses not to break the rules at that personal cost.
- The Geode Mine Shaft has an exit to the outside, sealed with a two-person locking mechanism. Arkhium mining there stopped after the Fontaine Research Institute accident.
### ULTRA +
- Earnshaw had not read Agnes' letters; he learns the truth only through the Traveler, and his idle lines afterwards show regret about not reading them sooner.

## characters
### STANDARD
Caterpillar ("Cater"), Lanoire ("Skylark" in the fortress), the guards Estienne, Noailles and Maupoil, the receptionist Monglane, Earnshaw, the Traveler and Paimon. Prisoners seen include Darcy, Genevieve, Poiret, Rave, Henaff, Delassaux, Nizami and Alvard. Clynes, Galvana and Galvaryet appear in support.
### LARGE +
Caterpillar speaks politely and calmly and is protective of Lanoire, who says he has never lied to her. Lanoire is trusting and curious, and calls the guards "evil sorcerers" or "good sorcerers". Estienne is easy-going, bends rules, and calls himself a small fry who will not take large risks. Noailles is rigid and punitive, and believes anyone can be a criminal regardless of age.

## consequences
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Lanoire lives with Earnshaw in the Court of Fontaine Region, near Mont Esus. Caterpillar leaves, and goes on to the 4.2 Narzissenkreuz Ordo quests, where he travels with the Traveler, Ann and Seymour. After the series the searchlights outside the fortress no longer react to the Traveler.
### LARGE +
The follow-up World Quest Happy Birthday (4.2) shows Caterpillar visiting Lanoire in his hilichurl form, telling her the end of the apprentice's story, and asking her never to approach anyone who looks like him.

## placement
### STANDARD
Released in version 4.1, with the Fortress of Meropide. In story order it sits just before the 4.2 In the Wake of Narcissus series, which Hereafter... leads into.

## entry: Caterpillar
@keys: Caterpillar, Cater, Chrysalis of the Phantasmic Moth
@keys2: Meropide, Lanoire, Narzissenkreuz, Unfinished Comedy
@version: 4.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Caterpillar, called Cater by Lanoire, is a prisoner in the Fortress of Meropide who appears as a white-haired boy. He says he is not a child. His real form is an Anemo Hilichurl Rogue; he says he is a hilichurl whom the mage Narzissenkreuz taught to speak and think. He says he has been imprisoned for more than four hundred years for his part in his master's plan. He helps Lanoire out of what he calls compassion, copying what a human would do.

## entry: Lanoire
@keys: Lanoire, Skylark, Little Skylark
@keys2: Meropide, Caterpillar, Earnshaw, Unfinished Comedy
@version: 4.1
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Lanoire is a young girl born inside the Fortress of Meropide, nicknamed Skylark by the prisoners. She has no official identity there. Her mother Agnes died, and Caterpillar and Estienne have looked after her. She loves stories, flowers pressed in books and the Tasses Ragout. After the series she lives with her grandfather Earnshaw and the three dogs Prince, Princess and Hero.

## entry: Estienne
@keys: Estienne
@keys2: Meropide, Coupon Millionaire, Noailles, Lanoire
@version: 4.1
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Estienne is an easy-going guard in the Fortress of Meropide who invents the Coupon Millionaire game to decide who is sent on outside jobs. He bends rules, dislikes Noailles' rigidity, and helps Lanoire and Caterpillar get out. He says he would not take Lanoire out himself, because he would pay too high a price.

## entry: Noailles
@keys: Noailles
@keys2: Meropide, Gardemek, Estienne, Caterpillar
@version: 4.1
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Noailles is a new, rule-bound guard in the Fortress of Meropide. Darcy says he dislikes Fonta and wants to close the Pankration Ring. He tries to recreate Caterpillar's lost records, calls the fugitives criminals whatever their documents say, uses Gardemeks without permission and breaks the deal he offered Caterpillar. He is beaten at the Geode Mine Shaft and taken into custody by Estienne.

## entry: Earnshaw
@keys: Earnshaw
@keys2: Lanoire, Agnes, Prince, Princess, Hero, Court of Fontaine
@version: 4.1
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Earnshaw is Lanoire's grandfather, a broad white-haired man who lives at the foothills of Mont Esus in the Court of Fontaine Region, in a house with a purple-flower garden. His daughter Agnes left after a quarrel and sent letters that he did not read. He cares for her three dogs, makes Tasses Ragout and takes Lanoire in at the end of the series.

## entry: Coupon Millionaire
@keys: Coupon Millionaire, Kids Team
@keys2: Meropide, Estienne, Coupons
@version: 4.1
@spoiler: none
### STANDARD
Coupon Millionaire is a game Estienne runs in the Fortress of Meropide. Teams earn Coupons however they like, and the winner gets a supervised job outside the walls. The contest has few rules, which Genevieve criticises. The Kids Team, made up of Caterpillar, Lanoire, the Traveler and Paimon, wins with 2,003,480 Coupons, thanks to a stash Caterpillar saved earlier and used to hire helpers.

## entry: Geode Mine Shaft Escape
@keys: Geode Mine Shaft, Flying Energy Thief
@keys2: Meropide, Caterpillar, Lanoire
@version: 4.1
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
The Geode Mine Shaft is the route the group uses out of the Fortress of Meropide. A locking mechanism there can only be opened by two people at once, and its terminal needs energy storage devices that attract a Flying Energy Thief, a trial device that disables whatever carries one. Noailles confronts the group here.
