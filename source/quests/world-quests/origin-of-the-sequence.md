## summary
### LITE
A Version 6.7 world quest in the Lunar Highlands of the Frost Moon. The Traveler and Paimon find a set of giant towers at the Nuur Stone Circle, repair a damaged ancient terminal and recalibrate a stone dragon idol. Finishing it unlocks the Ancient Moon's Remnant at the circle.
### STANDARD +
The terminal calls itself the Ia-Ilimmu No. 59 Storage Array and cannot recognise visitors because its central module is damaged or missing. It asks for Nin-Kiggia to be contacted for maintenance, and finally reports that its primary energy system is not connected. The quest ends with that unresolved error.
### LARGE +
The quest is a short environmental story: no named living character appears, and what is learned is through a machine that is still trying to serve people who may no longer exist. A later quest, Within the Sequence, continues from here.

## plot
### LITE
Paimon marvels at the giant towers and thinks they resemble the Frostmoon Scions' sanctum. The Traveler examines a terminal, follows its prompts to repair data nodes, and calibrates a Secret Source Dragon Idol that appears. Four glowing markers light up around the area.
### STANDARD +
On approach, the terminal checks for vital signs, tries an identification sequence and fails. A security check on the kernel also fails; it blames a damaged or missing central module and tells the visitor to contact Nin-Kiggia. It then switches to a "universal greeting", welcoming the visitor to the Ia-Ilimmu No. 59 Storage Array, which is used mostly to regulate the Engraving and store research resources. A critical component failure has left service dependencies broken; the terminal asks for a self-diagnostic command to restore critical data nodes. Paimon admits she did not follow most of this, and decides that a storehouse must hold treasure.

After the Traveler repairs the nodes, the diagnostic fails to run on schedule and manual path recalibration is needed. A Secret Source Dragon Idol appears. Paimon proposes climbing on its back and bopping it a couple of times to get it working, which the Traveler does. The terminal then reports repair complete and thanks them and lifts its lockdown. Four glowing markers appear nearby. A final error says the primary energy system is not connected and asks for Nin-Kiggia or an auxiliary power source.
### LARGE +
Steps: investigate the giant tower; examine the terminal; follow the prompts to repair the data nodes; investigate the Secret Source Dragon Idol; examine the terminal again. Completing the quest unlocks the Hidden Exploration Objective Fragments of Ia-Ilimmu and the Ancient Moon's Remnant at the Nuur Stone Circle. Rewards: Adventure EXP, 40 Primogems, 30,000 Mora, Hero's Wit and Mystic Enhancement Ore.
### ULTRA +
The pre-quest scene, triggered on first approach to the Ancient Moon's Remnant, has a conditional variant in the wiki transcript (Paimon's first line differs); the wiki does not say what condition selects it. In the follow-up quest Within the Sequence (opened when the Remnant reaches level 5), the repaired terminal reports that the lockdown is lifted and addresses some visitors by titles such as Xiuhcoatl and rulers of Natlantea; that quest is covered separately.

## revelations
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
The quest establishes that this ancient array is numbered No. 59, called Ia-Ilimmu, tied to something called the Engraving, and designed to be maintained by Nin-Kiggia. The terminal calls the repairs a self-diagnostic and does not explain what the towers do beyond that.
### LARGE +
Nin-Kiggia is the name of a data-based dragon-like lifeform created by the dragon researcher Lusalim, as the wiki describes her. The quest itself only says to contact her. For the larger story of the dragon researchers on the Frost Moon see Moon Gazing.

## characters
### LITE
The Traveler and Paimon, plus the Ancient Terminal. Nin-Kiggia is only named, and does not appear.
### STANDARD +
Paimon supplies curiosity and a treasure-hunting attitude: she assumes a "storage" array means a storehouse. The terminal is polite, formal and fussy about titles and procedure, and repeatedly apologises for its limits.

## consequences
### STANDARD
The Ancient Moon's Remnant at the Nuur Stone Circle becomes available, where Luna Sigils can be offered for rewards, and the Fragments of Ia-Ilimmu exploration objective is unlocked. The terminal remains partly broken because of its missing energy source.

## placement
### STANDARD
Released in Version 6.7 (Luna VIII) with the Frost Moon area. Within the Sequence follows it, and the Moon Gazing series covers the wider setting. The wiki lists no formal requirement beyond reaching the Nuur Stone Circle.

### STANDARD
A damaged ancient installation at the Nuur Stone Circle in the Lunar Highlands of the Frost Moon. Its terminal calls it the Ia-Ilimmu No. 59 Storage Array, used to regulate the operation of the Engraving and to store research resources. After the Traveler repairs it in Origin of the Sequence, it still reports that its primary energy system is not connected and that Nin-Kiggia should be contacted.
