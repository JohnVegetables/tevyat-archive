## summary
### LITE
A Frost Moon world quest from Version 6.7 (Luna VIII). A frightened kuuhenki and a crowd of moonglow jellyfish lead the Traveler and Paimon to a sealed, dragon-built facility beneath the Moontide Sea, the Curtain-Form Blackbody Containment Lab. Restoring its power opens the way to a hidden chamber with a great blue tree.
### STANDARD +
The quest is a short, mostly exploratory scene with almost no story revelation. Its practical effect is to unlock an Ancient Moon's Remnant (the Frost Moon's reputation-style monument) at the lab, and it ends with the Traveler loading the facility's map from a terminal.
### LARGE +
Its lore value is atmosphere: it shows that the dragons' ancient moon works still lie buried in the lunar sea and still respond to Archaeolune Energy, and it plants the name of the lab that later terminal records connect to the dragons' work on hiding the moon.

## plot
### LITE
Paimon spots a scared kuuhenki, and after the Traveler beats the monster harassing it, the creature guides them toward a cave. Jellyfish crowd into the cave too. Inside is a locked facility; the Traveler finds how to open it, gets the power back on and enters.
### STANDARD +
The kuuhenki leads the pair twice, each time after the Traveler defeats a hostile construct (a Precursor's Secret Source Matrix of Overseer Network). Paimon is uneasy that the place is hiding something, then reads the sign at the entrance, a Blackbody Containment "Generation Station", which she has never heard of. She decides a place like that must hold treasure.

Inside, a door to the Curtain-Form Blackbody Containment stays shut until an Ancient Moon Pearl is returned to its station. The Traveler frees trapped moonglow jellyfish, switches on a backup power generator and the Archaeolune-affinitive materials, and opens an energy barrier on the ceiling with Archaeolune Ripples. A lift lowers a storage chest but the pearl does not drop, so the Traveler must fire the pearl back to its station with a launcher to open the door.
### LARGE +
Beyond the door is a large chamber with a huge blue tree. Paimon is struck that something so beautiful could be hidden under the lunar sea and wonders what the dragons did there. The Traveler activates the facility terminal, which loads the area map, and the quest ends. Gameplay notes: the early cave stretch has two optional exquisite chests; the rewards are Adventure EXP, 30 Primogems, Mora, Hero's Wit and enhancement ore.
### ULTRA +
Step order for reference: help the kuuhenki (defeat one construct), follow it, help it again (defeat another), follow it into the cave; at the gate return the Ancient Moon Pearl; inside, free the jellyfish (two constructs), power the generator and Archaeolune-affinitive materials, open the ceiling barrier with Archaeolune Ripples, launch the pearl; talk to Paimon; activate the terminal. The facility lies in the Moontide Sea area of the Frost Moon.

## revelations
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
No question is answered in the quest itself. It establishes only that a dragon-era installation survives under the Moontide Sea, that its machinery still takes Archaeolune Energy, and that it is guarded by constructs. Paimon does not know what the name means.
### LARGE +
Terminal records at the lab, read outside the quest, describe a "Curtain-Form Blackbody Containment" that was tested during the early phase of Project Lunar Veil, an effort to hide the moon behind a veil. They tie the lab's work to the dragons' study of a black substance from the Stellar Blackbody, a creature from beyond the stars. These records are lore on the wiki's location page, not something the quest dialogue says. See quest.moon-gazing for the larger story.
### ULTRA +
One record describes the Curtain-Form Blackbody Containment as being tested while Ninnin, a young artificial lifeform raised by the researcher Lusalim, could barely speak; she let slip the words "Lunar Veil", which Lusalim took as the project's name. Another record says Blackbody Matter, the black filaments found on the hull of the spaceship Moongazer, reacts with Archaeolune Energy and can fuse with plants and animals, turning some blue creatures black. Neither record is cited by the quest, and neither explains why the lab was buried or sealed.

## characters
### LITE
The Traveler and Paimon. The only other presences are a frightened kuuhenki, moonglow jellyfish and the facility's guardian constructs.
### STANDARD +
Paimon supplies all the dialogue: curiosity, treasure-hunting optimism and a growing sense that the place is hiding something. The kuuhenki are small creatures of Archaeolune Energy native to the Frost Moon.

## consequences
### STANDARD
Completing the quest is required to unlock the Ancient Moon's Remnant at the Curtain-Form Blackbody Containment Lab, a Moontide Sea monument where Luna Sigils can be offered, and gives the map of the facility. No lasting change in the world or to any character follows.

## placement
### STANDARD
Released in Version 6.7 (Luna VIII, 2026-07-01) with the Frost Moon map. It is part of the Frost Moon exploration content that accompanies the Moon Gazing series, and the wiki marks no formal prerequisite or next quest. In story terms it takes place after the Traveler has reached the Frost Moon and before or alongside the Moontide Sea events of Moon Gazing's second and third acts.
