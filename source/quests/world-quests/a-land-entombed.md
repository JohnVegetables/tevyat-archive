## summary
### LITE
A Dragonspine world quest from Version 1.2. The Traveler finds an Ancient Investigation Journal and takes it to the researcher Stevens, who explains it belongs to an old expedition sent up the mountain by Mondstadt's aristocrats. The Traveler recovers the other two parts.
### STANDARD +
Stevens recalls an old annal, from before the Knights of Favonius existed, about a team sent by the aristocrats to investigate the mountain; the annals say almost nothing about what it found. Once all three journals are collected he concludes that very likely no member returned to Mondstadt, apart from possibly one man named Eberhart.
### LARGE +
The quest is a small piece of history-mystery and a quiet pointer toward the ruined kingdom under Dragonspine. It unlocks only an achievement and a reward.

## plot
### LITE
The Traveler and Paimon pick up a journal in the mountains and show it to Stevens at the camp. He asks them to find the two other parts, marks the sites, and after reading all three tells them an aristocrat-led expedition seems to have been lost, with Eberhart perhaps the sole possible survivor.
### STANDARD +
Stevens thinks the team investigated three sites. The Traveler finds the first journal at the secret chamber near the Entombed City's great door, the second in a cellar under the ice in the southwestern ruins (two Warming Seelie puzzles and a Ruin Grader guard it), and the third on a shelf in Starglow Cavern, behind a thin opening in the ice. Reading them, Stevens is taken aback and drifts into thought mid-sentence. He explains why the annals mention only the team's departure, and asks the Traveler to bring any other books they find in the mountains.
### LARGE +
Stevens first jokes that "treasure" means a treasure trove of knowledge. After reading the journals he says some minor details still need straightening out but the overall picture is clear, and the quest description says the journals seem to point to some sort of conspiracy. He ends by wondering aloud where Eberhart went, since there is no record of his return to Mondstadt. Stevens' conclusions are his own reading of the journals and the annals.
### ULTRA +
The journals are Part I (the secret chamber by the Entombed City's ancient palace door), Part II (the cellar in the southwestern ruins) and Part III (Starglow Cavern); the order of collection does not change the dialogue. The last passage can only be reached by short or medium-height characters. The first two sites are puzzle rooms with Warming Seelie, so the "treasure" is mostly access to history.

## revelations
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
The journals suggest that the aristocrat-sent investigation team did not come back as a group, which explains the gap in the old annals. One member, Eberhart, may have survived, but his fate is unknown.

## characters
### LITE
The Traveler, Paimon and Stevens.
### STANDARD +
Stevens is a history-minded researcher at the Dragonspine base camp who is enthusiastic about primary sources and promises compensation.

## consequences
### STANDARD
Stevens gains valuable historical sources and rewards the Traveler. The quest grants the achievement Snow-Stored History. The Ragged Records are related but optional.

## placement
### STANDARD
Released in Version 1.2 with the rest of Dragonspine's exploration content. It can be done in any order relative to In the Mountains.
