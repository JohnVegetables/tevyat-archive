## summary
### LITE
A Dragonspine world quest from Version 1.2. Iris, an adventurer at the Dragonspine Adventurer Camp, complains of strange unmeltable ice blocking expedition routes. The Traveler thaws glowing shards from that ice, follows them up the mountain and opens the way to the summit.
### STANDARD +
The quest requires The Outlander Who Caught the Wind and starts by talking to Iris at the camp. Completing it opens the Frostbearing Tree offering system, the Peak of Vindagnyr domain and the Cryo Hypostasis boss, as game-side unlocks. It is a gameplay-driven quest with little dialogue lore.
### LARGE +
Its story value is atmosphere and mystery: Dragonspine is shown as more hostile than anywhere else in Mondstadt, and the quest leaves its odd phenomena (a sealed windy cavern, ice that fire cannot melt, blue plants, a floating pillar) deliberately unexplained.

## plot
### LITE
Iris tells the Traveler about hard ice that will not melt in fire and a cavern near the summit that breathes a strange powerful wind. The Traveler thaws a shard in the ice, sees a blue orb fly toward the summit, and later thaws two more. Reaching the top, they find a floating pillar and a hollow beneath it, and report back to Iris.
### STANDARD +
Iris suggests looking at a large slab of ice on the mountain's outer edge. When it is thawed with Scarlet Quartz, a blue orb flies off toward the summit and a new Frostbearing Tree grows. Paimon and the Traveler try to climb the summit cavern but are driven back by the cold and the air current, so they return to the camp. Iris explains that earlier expedition teams were turned back by the ice and that blue plants cluster near it, pointing to the Entombed City outskirts and Starglow Cavern. After thawing the other two shards the Traveler follows the road past a Statue of The Seven to the summit and sees a giant pillar. The three shards have frozen again on it, so the Traveler thaws them a second time. The ice under the pillar then shatters, revealing a large space below. Iris thanks them and promises to send explorers.
### LARGE +
Details of the thawing: each shard needs a Scarlet Quartz effect, and the Warming Seelie lead the Traveler between the monuments and shards. The first two shards sit near the Entombed City outskirts and inside Starglow Cavern, where the wave fight is a timed challenge under falling icicles. The summit portion uses the Skyfrost Nail subarea. After the pillar is restored, Paimon can climb it and wonders whether it fell from the sky. Iris admits she saw something encased in the ice but could not melt it herself, and half-jokes that an adventure story would link the wind, ice and plants.
### ULTRA +
Game-side notes, kept separate from story: exactly enough Scarlet Quartz is provided at each stage, and any attack made with its effect melts a shard, not only Pyro. The second round of thawing needs only one hit per shard. A snowstorm and Ruin Guards or Ruin Graders guard parts of the Entombed City outskirts. Climbing the restored Skyfrost Nail gives a separate achievement, and Paimon's remark there is the only comment on the pillar's origin.

## revelations
@spoiler: none
### STANDARD
No question is answered. The quest only establishes that the ice shards belong to a floating pillar at the summit (later called the Skyfrost Nail) and that a chamber lies beneath it.

## characters
### LITE
The Traveler, Paimon and Iris.
### STANDARD +
Iris is an adventurer at the Dragonspine Adventurer Camp who organises expedition parties and warns the Traveler to prepare well before going up the mountain.

## consequences
### STANDARD
Iris can resume organising expeditions now that the ice can be melted. The summit becomes reachable, and the Peak of Vindagnyr domain and Cryo Hypostasis become accessible as game features. The achievement for restoring the Skyfrost Nail is granted.

## placement
### STANDARD
Released in Version 1.2 with the Dragonspine content. It follows The Outlander Who Caught the Wind in requirements and is a companion to the Dragonspine quest A Land Entombed, which uses the same mountain and ruins.
