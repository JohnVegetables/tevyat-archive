## summary
### LITE
A Version 6.7 world quest series that unlocks the Frost Moon map. The Traveler and Paimon follow what looks like Columbina to a buried station in Nod-Krai, reach the Frost Moon in an ancient dragon spaceship and travel the moon's dragon-built sites with a small black kuuhenki called Eggie, whose real name is Ninnin. Together they uncover Project Moongazer, an effort by dragon researchers to send a message into the stars.
### STANDARD +
Told in three acts: I, Pioneers; II, Visitors From the Stars; III, Project Moongazer (seven quests in all). Columbina appears as a companion partway through. The series is the first exploration world quest to use full voice-over and animated cutscenes, like an Archon Quest.
### LARGE +
Its emotional core is Eggie, a lifeform made from the data of dead dragon researchers who wants to finish their message. The series ends with Eggie sending it, shielded by Columbina, and with the Traveler hearing an old reply from their own sibling.

## plot
### LITE
At Misty Shoal the Traveler sees Columbina, follows her to a station and finds an underground machine. The "Columbina" turns out to be a black kuuhenki. Chasing it, the pair enter a spaceship, suffer an accident aboard and reach the Frost Moon in escape pods. They free Eggie from a stasis module, recover her name, repair the moon's Eye of Ibni-Belum, find the real Columbina, face a mad mega kuuhenki and finally enter a hidden base on the moon's far side.
### STANDARD +
Act I, Pioneers. At the Dunanna Forward Station the Traveler activates a ruined mechanism with Archaeolune Energy given by a Columbina who does not speak. It can only link to the Frost Moon. When the Traveler asks the figure to hold hands, as in Silvermoon Hall, it changes into a black kuuhenki. The Traveler chases it into a vast chamber with a spaceship and an alarm.

Act II, Visitors From the Stars. After a rough landing, Paimon and the Traveler find Eggie caught in a stasis module in a dragon energy station. She reads a terminal and remembers her name is Ninnin. They power three stations, activate the Eye of Ibni-Belum, and follow a lead to Ungien's Circle. There they reach the Space Lifeform Capture Station and find the real Columbina. A rampaging mega kuuhenki, Ungie, is calmed by removing Knotted Moonlight Vines. Columbina and Eggie learn what the dragons did there.

Act III, Project Moongazer. In a high-security base on the moon's far side, Eggie reads that the base was built for a "Lord of Darkness Who Returned From the Sea of Stars". Columbina vanishes. Records in Lusalim's laboratory show that Eggie carries the name of Ninnin, the artificial "daughter" of the researcher Lusalim, and was made to carry out the final step of Project Moongazer. The moon's core holds a recorded imprint of that original Nin-Kiggia.
### LARGE +
Pioneers (Part 1): the false Columbina does not answer greetings; she walks to the Dunanna Forward Station, ignores their questions and gives the Traveler Archaeolune Energy so a machine can be activated. The room's centre becomes a model of the Three Moons with a large blue sphere.

Pioneers (Part 2): the pair land in escape pods, find Eggie hanging in a stasis module, and bring power to three small energy stations that feed the Eye of Ibni-Belum. The third station is already active, which Paimon thinks means Columbina has been there. Eggie asks to be called just Ninnin, and as she reads more terminals she grows more confident and quicker with the data, to Paimon's irritation.

Visitors From the Stars (Part 1): at Ungien's Circle, a biosphere under a large tree with a tall antenna, and at the Space Lifeform Capture Station, Eggie finds blueprints and records, while the group dodges flywheels and Archaeolune storms. Visitors (Part 2): Columbina is treating Maagees (called Maggie in the cast list) and asks the Traveler to hold Ungie's attention; schools of kuuhenki mimic jellyfish and whales to help. Eggie learns the history of Ungien, who flew the spaceship Moongazer toward a second signal, fused with a creature from deep space, and was later turned by his wife Maagees into the kuuhenki Ungie. Maagees was converted the same way by her student Lusalim.

Project Moongazer (Part 1): After reading the Stellar Blackbody message, Eggie says she does not have the memories the message implies and cannot explain why. Columbina senses an odd familiarity in the base. Records state the base's lowest levels reach a fissure into the moon's core. Part 2: the Traveler and Columbina descend, beat the guardian Warden of the Flame, meet Nin-Kiggia's recorded imprint, and return to the Eye so Eggie can transmit.
### ULTRA +
Dialogue notes: in Visitors Part 2 Columbina says she will take back the hat she gave Eggie if Eggie hides anything, and Eggie promises not to. In Pioneers Part 2, after reading terminals, Eggie briefly claims the Traveler is more rational and reliable than Paimon, which annoys Paimon.

The moon's inhabitants had protective systems beyond the facilities: the false sky; a miniature Lunar Veil on Ungien's Circle; and a failed full-scale Veil whose generator was pierced by an entity from beyond the stars. Columbina suspects the Descender destroyed Lusalim's lab, because a veil that hides the moon resembles the false sky, but since the other facilities survived, she thinks Lusalim traded his research to the ruler of the heavens.

## revelations
@spoiler: major
@version: 6.7
### STANDARD
Eggie is a data-born lifeform, created to finish Project Moongazer from the merged information of the dragon researchers of the Frost Moon. The project's purpose is to send into space the message once carried by the Stellar Blackbody, a creature from beyond the stars: a message of hope that tells other civilisations they are not alone and may be in danger from a pitch-black annihilation spreading through the universe.
### LARGE +
The researchers form a lineage. Ibni-Belum, the first Moongazer, designed the Eye that bears his name; Ungien and Maagees were later Moongazers; Lusalim was their student and Nin-Kiggia's creator and "father". Eggie is the newest life form born from the merged data of the researchers, and her nickname Ninnin is inherited from Nin-Kiggia, the final inheritor of the project.

A sealed segment of the transmission reveals a reply from the Traveler's sibling, sent thousands of years ago, thanking the moon's inhabitants for their courage and promising to find them once their own troubles are over. The Traveler learns only now that a reply existed, and says they once received a message too garbled to read.
### ULTRA +
According to the wiki's act summary, the moon's core is the launch silo of a weapon once reforged by a Dragon Lord of Darkness to fire on Teyvat, defeated by the Heavenly Principles. Nin-Kiggia's imprint says the Traveler has already met earlier reincarnations of the researchers (Ibni-Belum, Ungien and Maagees). Eggie's final broadcast lists the names of every Moongazer who came before her, then her body dissolves, leaving only her hat.

## characters
### LITE
The Traveler, Paimon, Eggie (Ninnin), Columbina; recorded or remembered: Ibni-Belum, Ungien, Maagees (Maggie), Lusalim, Nin-Kiggia; the mega kuuhenki Ungie.
### STANDARD +
Eggie is quiet and mission-driven; Paimon fusses over her; Columbina is gentle, curious and protective, and she keeps her own sense of familiarity with the moon. The Frostmoon Scions are mentioned only in passing.

## consequences
### STANDARD
Completing the series opens the Frost Moon map and its Ancient Moon's Remnant exploration system. Eggie is gone after sending her message. Columbina and the Traveler agree to hold a "Home-Returning Night" party with friends when the Traveler's journey ends.
### LARGE +
Columbina's reading that the moon's veil resembles the false sky ties the story to the wider cosmology of the Heavenly Principles. Beneath the Lunar Sea, another Frost Moon world quest, explores one of the buried dragon facilities.

## placement
### STANDARD
Released in Version 6.7 (Luna VIII, 2026-07-01), in the same version as Song of the Welkin Moon: Scherzo, and before the Snezhnaya content of 7.0. It is a world quest with no listed prerequisite; the Frost Moon is reached through the series itself.
