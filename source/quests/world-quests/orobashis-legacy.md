## summary
@spoiler: minor
### LITE
Orobashi's Legacy is a six-part World Quest series on Yashiori Island, added in version 2.0. At Fort Fujitou the Traveler meets Kaji, who is studying why the island has endless rain and lightning. The cause is damaged ancient wards linked to the remains of Orobashi, a serpent god. The Traveler restores them across the island and the storm ends.
### STANDARD +
The parts are Prologue and Parts I to V. It requires the Archon Quest act A New Star Approaches, and the Ritou Escape Plan quest to reach its starting area. Finishing Part III opens the Formation Estate domain, and finishing Part V rewards the diagram for the Kitain Cross Spear.
### LARGE +
Besides the puzzle chain, the series is where Orobashi's role in Yashiori Island's past and the origin of the Tatarigami phenomenon are explained to players. Completing each part clears the Tatarigami from its own area, so progress is visible across the island.

## plot
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Kaji, met at Fort Fujitou, is investigating the island's unceasing storm. He reasons it comes from the failure of a series of ancient wards connected to the remains of Orobashi. Orobashi, the guardian deity of Watatsumi Island, sacrificed itself after learning a forbidden truth Celestia wished hidden, led an invasion of Yashiori Island, and was slain by the Raiden Shogun, Ei, in a battle that carved Musoujin Gorge and scattered the god's remains. Their lingering power is what causes the Tatarigami.

In Parts I to III the Traveler visits Musoujin Gorge, Serpent's Head and Jakotsu Mine, finds missing Rock Pillar Warding Stones and Rock Pillar Pearls, and returns them to broken wards by solving Sacred Stone Puzzles.
### LARGE
Each part clears the Tatarigami in its area. After the first three wards are repaired, Fort Mumei rises from the sea near Watatsumi Island. In Parts IV and V the Traveler and Kaji investigate it and find one last, larger ward guarded by a Maguu Kenki. The Traveler defeats the guardian and solves the final puzzle. The constant rain and storm over Yashiori Island stop. Kaji thanks the Traveler and goes on to the Kujou Encampment to continue his work.
### ULTRA +
This is condensed from the series overview; the individual part pages were not read in detail, and the page notes its own summary is incomplete.

## revelations
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
- The storm on Yashiori Island is a consequence of broken wards, not natural weather (Kaji's hypothesis, borne out by the quests).
- Orobashi's remains, scattered when the Raiden Shogun killed it, are the source of the Tatarigami.
### LARGE +
- Orobashi sacrificed itself after learning something Celestia wanted hidden, and attacked Yashiori Island.

## characters
### STANDARD
The Traveler, Paimon, Kaji (researcher), a Maguu Kenki as the final guardian. Orobashi and the Raiden Shogun appear in the history Kaji recounts.

## consequences
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
The storm and lightning over Yashiori Island cease and the Tatarigami are cleared from the repaired areas. Fort Mumei has surfaced near Watatsumi Island. Kaji leaves for the Kujou Encampment.

## placement
### STANDARD
Released in 2.0 with Inazuma. Requires A New Star Approaches; Ritou Escape Plan is needed to reach the starting location.
