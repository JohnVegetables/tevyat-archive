## summary
### LITE
Neko Is a Cat is a nine-quest Inazuma world series (2.1) that follows Seirai Stormchasers. Neko, a talking black cat who calls herself Provisional Head Priestess of the Asase Shrine, asks the Traveler to repair the ruined shrine on Seirai Island, feed the island's cats and commission a statue of herself, all in the hope that her old priestess Hibiki will come back. The quests unlock one per daily reset, and the whole series ends when a cloud takes the shape of a cat.
### STANDARD
Because Neko believes Hibiki, the former head priestess, and Hibiki's friend Domeki will return, she wants the shrine restored. The Traveler cleans the grounds, mends a wooden shelf and the prayer rack, finds the cats who have wandered off, helps arrange a meal like the one Hibiki cooked, and recruits Ooshima Junpei, a stonemason from Tatarasuna, to carve a statue. The adventurers Taisuke and Etsuko, already on the island from Seirai Stormchasers, help along the way. Completing it earns the Cat in the Clouds achievement, new commissions and a viewpoint at the shrine gate.
### LARGE +
The series is a gentle, humorous story with an undertone of sadness: Neko has waited a very long time and is not told that people have changed or gone. Her habit of describing Junpei as "the stone human" and not understanding salary or Mora keeps the tone light.
### ULTRA +
Follow-up content includes six daily commissions (A Fishy Flavor, Shadow of the Cat, Shrine Cleanup, The Cat's Trail, To Make Cat Ornaments and To Make a Cat Carving) and a world quest, The Narukami Trail, which needs four completions of certain commissions.

## plot
@version: 2.1
### STANDARD
Opening. After Seirai Stormchasers, the Traveler returns to Asase Shrine. Neko is pleased that Taisuke and Etsuko visited and thinks it is because the repaired offering box draws people to the shrine, so she wants to repair more. The first quest, Shrine Cleanup, requires clearing dirt around the shrine; Neko remembers Hibiki, who was head priestess, received guests, cared for the shrine and named Neko. Domeki would visit to play with Hibiki and help clean.

The next days. The Traveler repairs the Prayer Rack with Taisuke and Etsuko, and Etsuko grows suspicious of Neko's motives, since the island's people long ago moved away. Neko answers that she needs to look after the shrine, or Hibiki will be sad when she returns, not knowing what became of her. The following day Neko asks for help finding cats that have wandered off. After that Neko recalls the dish Hibiki used to make for the cats; Kiminami Anna devises the Invigorating Kitty Meal, which Neko says is different but wonderful.
### LARGE +
The statue. Neko remembers Hibiki once asking if she wanted a statue of herself and decides to have one built. Taisuke and Etsuko suggest looking for workers at Tatarasuna, where the Traveler meets Ooshima Junpei, who is baffled that a talking cat wants a statue of herself. He agrees but forgets to ask about pay while he is stunned by the speaking cat; over several days the Traveler fetches iron chunks and meat for him while Neko is puzzled by salary and Mora. During this time Neko asks the two adventurers about Hibiki and Domeki. They know nothing of Hibiki, and say that Domeki was a pirate with a bounty on his head; Neko, who remembers him playing with Hibiki, insists he must have been a good person.

Completion. In the last quest, Junpei leaves a little extra stone to chip off in Neko's presence, as statues of this sort deserve a formal finish. Neko says she only wants Hibiki, if she returns and loses her way, to see the statue and know where to go. At the final knock a cloud forms the shape of a cat. Neko believes it is Hibiki, and that the good thing Hibiki and Domeki promised would follow the statue has happened. She is certain that Hibiki, Domeki, Janome and the rest of Hibiki's friends will come back.
### ULTRA +
Neko lists what has been restored: the Offering Box, the Prayer Rack and now the statue. The final quest requires the player to leave Co-Op mode while it is active and rewards Adventure EXP, Primogems, Hero's Wit and Mora.

## revelations
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
- Neko named herself Provisional Head Priestess of the shrine while she waits; the real head priestess was Hibiki, who gave Neko her name.
- Neko still believes Hibiki and Domeki are alive and will return. The adventurers' remark that Domeki was a pirate is new to her.
### LARGE +
- The wiki profile places Hibiki and Domeki some five centuries earlier, in the era of the Cataclysm, and has the island kept in near-constant storms afterwards. That background comes from Seirai Stormchasers and is not told in this series itself.

## characters
### STANDARD
Neko (a talking black cat), the Traveler, Paimon, Taisuke and Etsuko (Adventurers' Guild members), Ooshima Junpei (stonemason), and Kiminami Anna (cook). Hibiki, Domeki and Janome are only remembered by Neko.

## consequences
### STANDARD
The shrine is tidier and has a statue, Neko is happier and more certain of Hibiki's return, and the player earns the Cat in the Clouds achievement, new commissions and a viewpoint beside the shrine gate near the Teleport Waypoint.

## placement
### STANDARD
Released in 2.1 and unlocked after Seirai Stormchasers (Part IV). Each following quest requires a daily reset before it can be accepted, so the series takes at least nine in-game days. It does not connect to the main Archon Quest.

## entry: Neko
@keys: Neko, Provisional Head Priestess
@keys2: Asase Shrine, Hibiki, Seirai Island
@version: 2.1
### STANDARD
Neko is a talking black cat who guards Asase Shrine on Seirai Island and calls herself its Provisional Head Priestess. She was named by Hibiki for her habit of sleeping. She does not understand human money, and is determined that the shrine should be cared for until Hibiki returns.
### LARGE +
Neko speaks of Hibiki with fondness and trusts that her promise of a good thing after the statue is finished has come true.

## entry: Invigorating Kitty Meal
@keys: Invigorating Kitty Meal
@version: 2.1
### STANDARD
A dish designed by Kiminami Anna after Neko describes the meal that Hibiki used to make for her and the other cats. Neko says it differs from Hibiki's but tastes wonderful, and asks the Traveler to feed the other cats with it.
