## summary
@spoiler: none
### LITE
Agnihotra Sutra is a Sumeru world quest series from version 3.0, following The World of Aranara. The Traveler and Paimon help the Aranara Aranakin, Aragaru and Aranaga collect three plants, the Zohrah Mushroom, the Barsam Flower and the Yajna Grass, to make the potion Arahaoma. It is offered to the gate-guardian Jamikayomars so they can enter Mawtiyima and clear the Marana, a corruption that is devouring her.
### STANDARD +
The series has three quests: Starry Night Chapter, Risen Moon Chapter and The Final Chapter. The first two can be done in either order; The Final Chapter follows both. Everything is told in the Aranara's halting, plant-centred way of speaking, with themes of memory, forgetting and nourishing life.
### LARGE +
In the Aranara's account Mawtiyima is something Nara Varuna left behind that protects the forest. The series uses the Kusava, a fruit the Aranara give the Traveler as a tool, for breaking seals, and the Aranara language and ritual to carry the lore.

## plot
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Aranakin asks for help getting the Zohrah Mushroom from a cave near Gandharva Ville, where a trio of Eremites led by Lunja are also prowling. Aragaru asks for help with the Barsam Flower in the Realm of Khab, a place the Aranara call a dream of the dead Nara. Both explain that the Marana is growing and eating the forests and that the Arahaoma ritual, led by Aranaga, needs three plants: Zohrah Mushroom, Barsam Flower and Yajna Grass.

With the plants and Aranakin's and Aragaru's help, the Traveler meets Aranaga in Mawtiyima Forest. Aranaga takes them to the Yajna Grass, which agrees to become Arahaoma. At night the three Aranara hold the ritual; the elixir is offered to Jamikayomars, who guards the gate to Mawtiyima, and the way opens. Inside, the Traveler and the Aranara clean away the Withering's pollution in three places, defeat three mutated fungi (Alap, Bet and Gamal) and destroy the last tumour. Mawtiyima is freed, and the Aranara stay behind to listen to her stories.
### LARGE
**Starry Night Chapter.** East of Gandharva Ville the Traveler finds three Eremites, Lunja, Minum and Ziri, who say they are of the Farrokhzadan and complain about the rainforest humidity. Lunja asks what the Traveler is doing there. The Traveler meets the Aranara Aranakin, who dislikes sand (his word for the Naravaluka, the desert folk) and who needs the Zohrah Mushroom for Arahaoma. They enter a cave, lower the water level, break the seals on the mushroom with the Kusava, and defeat assassins. Lunja then takes the mushroom. The Traveler can negotiate or fight to get it back. Aranakin gives the Traveler a songleaf with a song and heads to the Mawtiyima Forest.

**Risen Moon Chapter.** Near Chatrakam Cave Aragaru says the plants told him the Traveler would come. He explains that the Marana is growing and that Aranaga is preparing the ritual but needs a Barsam Flower, which lives in the Realm of Khab, once the home of the Nara. Aragaru gives the Traveler a Kusava, a bitter fruit that holds memories and dreams, and uses it to smash plant-eroded stones. Following Seelies, the Traveler breaks the seals on the Barsam Flower. When it wakes, the Realm of Khab (its dream) dissolves and floods the group with water. Aragaru heads to the Mawtiyima Forest.

**The Final Chapter.** Aranaga greets the Traveler in Mawtiyima Forest, which he calls his garden, and explains that Mawtiyima, what Nara Varuna left to gather Elements and nutrients and feed the forest, has been devoured by the Marana. In his account of the past, the Marana came out of the moons' shadows, followed by the walking Shroom-Kin. He leads the Traveler to the Yajna Grass, the last of many, which agrees to become Arahaoma and which he says means "the falling petals" in the old language. The three Aranara give the Traveler a flower each as a living "remembrance" before the ritual. At midnight they perform it, and Aranaga explains that the elixir will awaken Jamikayomars, who holds the seeds of memory and guards Mawtiyima's gate. Inside, the Traveler uses the Kusava to repair seals and clear three corrupted zones, fights Alap, Bet and Gamal, and destroys the last growth. The Aranara thank them but say Mawtiyima needs their company now, and they speak of new life being nourished by memory and of death as a loss of memory.

### ULTRA +
Aranaga says that stagnant wisdom is the Marana's lair, that living memory gives rise to living wisdom, which makes dead Marana dissipate. Aragaru describes the Kusava as the fruit of flowering plants and the seed of new life, and tells the Traveler not to eat it.

The Aranara speak of the Ad Oblivione, a "device that takes away dreams", which Aranaga says they dislike because it grinds living memory into juice for dominion. The text does not explain this device further.

## revelations
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
- Arahaoma is a ritual elixir the Aranara make from three plants (Zohrah Mushroom, Barsam Flower and Yajna Grass). It carries memory and awakens Jamikayomars.
- The Aranara believe that memory is alive, not dead, and must be nourished; they call the Marana a devourer of memory.
- Mawtiyima is something Nara Varuna left behind, according to the Aranara, and she was infiltrated by the Marana.
### LARGE +
- The Realm of Khab is described by Aragaru as the Barsam Flower's dream and the former home of the Nara.
- The Aranara refer to the Traveler as Golden Nara and to Paimon as White Floaty, and say the Traveler is like Nara Varuna and also golden. This echoes the Aranara's view of Nara Varuna but the text does not explain it.

## characters
### STANDARD
The Traveler, Paimon, the Aranara Aranakin, Aragaru and Aranaga, Jamikayomars (a guardian of Mawtiyima's gate), the Eremites Lunja, Minum and Ziri, Mawtiyima (spoken of), and Nara Varuna (spoken of). The fungi Alap, Bet and Gamal appear as fight targets.

## consequences
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Mawtiyima is freed from the Marana and the Aranara stay with her. The Traveler is left with remembrances of the three Aranara and has done their ritual. The Aranara hint that they may forget the Traveler.
### LARGE +
Aranaga, Aranakin and Aragaru each give the Traveler a flower as a living "remembrance", and Aranakin gives a songleaf. The Aranara say they will still be friends as long as they remember the Traveler.

## placement
### STANDARD
Requires The World of Aranara. Released in 3.0 with the rest of the Sumeru rainforest world quests. Starry Night Chapter and Risen Moon Chapter can be done in either order; The Final Chapter follows both.

## entry: Arahaoma
@keys: Arahaoma
@keys2: Aranara, Mawtiyima, Barsam Flower, Zohrah Mushroom, Yajna Grass
@version: 3.0
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Arahaoma is the elixir of memory that the Aranara make in a night ritual. It is brewed from the Barsam Flower, the Zohrah Mushroom and the Yajna Grass. Offered to Jamikayomars, it opens the way into Mawtiyima so the Marana can be driven out. Aranaga calls it the essence of memory and a messenger that drives away death.

## entry: Jamikayomars
@keys: Jamikayomars
@keys2: Mawtiyima, Aranaga, Arahaoma
@version: 3.0
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Jamikayomars guards the gate of Mawtiyima, according to Aranaga, and is said to hold the seeds of memory. Aranaga links the name to Nara Varuna's "memory cup", which he says grows there. Once Arahaoma is offered, he awakens and opens the way.

## entry: Marana
@keys: Marana
@keys2: Mawtiyima, Aranara, Shroom-Kin, Arahaoma
@version: 3.0
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
In the Aranara's telling, Marana is a devourer that came from the moons' shadows and eats Mawtiyima and the forests, making others forget. The quest steps describe the pollution cleansed in three zones and at a final tumour as caused by the Withering. The series does not state what Marana is.

## entry: Kusava
@keys: Kusava, Kusava fruit
@keys2: Aranara, Aragaru, Mawtiyima
@version: 3.0
@spoiler: none
### STANDARD
Kusava is a fruit that holds memories and dreams. The Aranara give one to the Traveler to wake mushrooms and vines and break plant-eroded seals. Aragaru calls it bitter and full of power, and says it is also the seed of new life. They warn not to eat it.

## entry: Lunja and the Farrokhzadan Eremites
@keys: Lunja, Minum, Ziri
@keys2: Eremite, Farrokhzadan, Gandharva Ville
@version: 3.0
@spoiler: none
### STANDARD
Lunja, an Eremite who calls herself a Farrokhdokht of the Farrokhzadan, leads two weary subordinates, Minum and Ziri, in the rainforest east of Gandharva Ville. She is chatty and mercenary, and ends up taking the Zohrah Mushroom from the Traveler, who may fight or bargain to get it back.
