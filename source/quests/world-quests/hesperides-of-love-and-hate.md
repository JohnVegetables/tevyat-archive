## summary
@spoiler: minor
### LITE
Hesperides of Love and Hate is a three-part Snezhnaya World Quest series added in version 7.0, set at the House of Hesperides on Fellfrost Peak. The Traveler and Paimon are invited to what is billed as a Snegovik wedding for Pavlina, the Snegurochka princess. It turns out to be a ritual meant to take a fragment of the Eye of Graeae's power from Ikhor, the "groom", and the house keeps looping each time a bell rings eight times.
### STANDARD +
The parts are "Through the Gates of Ivory", "Procrustes' Iron Bed" (with two sub-quests, "House of the Soulless" and "The Sun's Gilded Apple") and "Bell Ringing at Dusk". It runs concurrently with In the Dwelling of Life and On One Side a Palace, On the Other a Tomb, and one of its rewards is the Pebble of Freezing Heart, which unlocks An Ark That Used to Be a Soul.
### LARGE +
Tone: a dreamlike gothic mystery, with bells, looping time, dolls and statues, set among Snegovik who live "for love" yet are soulless. It frames Pavlina as a sympathetic antagonist and gives Aksinya's own aims.

## plot
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Through the Gates of Ivory. In a snowstorm the party climbs Fellfrost Peak and finds a lamp that eases the cold. A young man, Ikhor, tells them a woman he did not know told him to leave a house and he simply obeyed, because for him a strong wish must be granted. He lives at the House of Hesperides, home of Lady Pavlina, princess of the Snegovik and other fae, and says she is preparing a wedding, an important "ritual". The Eye of Graeae senses a piece of its body in him. He gives them a wedding invitation, since only guests may enter, and guides them through monsters to the door.

Inside, the host Aglaya welcomes them as guests, explains Larisa's earlier wedding and warns them away from forbidden rooms. After entertainments and a nap the party hears the bell strike eight and goes to the chapel with the Snegovik guests. Pavlina speaks of a people who were soulless and chased love because they longed for a soul, and calls Larisa's death the last page of a tragic chapter. She calls for Ikhor, who wears an icy collar, and he agrees to give "what we've been wishing for". As Pavlina raises her sword, Aksinya appears, objects, and turns Ikhor into a doll-like puppet. A portal to the Shadow Realm takes Aksinya and Pavlina away. The Traveler dreams of an endless wedding rehearsed again and again, wakes in a room, and learns from Paimon and the Eye that the ritual repeats because Pavlina can only draw the power bit by bit. Aksinya sends a message through the Eye saying she has trapped Pavlina for now, to take Ikhor's fragments away before the bell strikes eight, and that she will send a map.
### LARGE +
Procrustes' Iron Bed. Aglaya acts as if she has not met them, because the loop has reset. Bells ring one to eight as time runs out, and the party must recover two fragments ("Severed Fragment of Ikhor") through the sub-quests: in House of the Soulless, a secret room, a cat and Snegurochka statues puzzle lead to a cellar; in The Sun's Gilded Apple, Paimon keeps the guard Ladon talking while the Traveler sneaks through the garden, where Larisa once worked, to the tree that holds a fragment. With both found, they reach the chapel and remove Ikhor's collar. Ikhor says the ritual has been set in motion and can neither be halted nor reversed, and chooses to become one with it. Pavlina then arrives, pierces him with her sword, and absorbs him as golden energy. The Snegovik attack the party as she escapes.

Bell Ringing at Dusk. The party fights through Snegovik and rides ice lifts and Rumbletwig branches to the Cloudborne Altar, above the clouds, where Pavlina is trying to widen the ritual. Aksinya frees herself from the Shadow Realm and fights beside the Traveler. After Pavlina is beaten, she and Aksinya talk. Pavlina says she cannot accept Aksinya's "tomorrow" because the fae and loveless would not be part of it, and she wants a way for beings born of artifice to find meaning in their lives. Aksinya replies that the love of one person is too small, and that a love capable of saving more lives is greater. Pavlina disintegrates into icy mist, leaving the completed union ritual without its offering; Aksinya gives the Traveler a piece of the Eye (a Pebble of Freezing Heart), explains that the Eye's body was made so that "life may transcend life itself", and says she shares the goal of helping the will in the Eye find its body.
### ULTRA +
Details. Aglaya's welcome mentions the rule that the ritual starts "when the clock strikes eight times", that a human woman once tried to reach the groom and was caught, and that the vow was sworn on the sword Larisa once held. In the chapel the Snegovik are divided: some praise Larisa, and some think Pavlina was tainted by human ways at Hvergelmir. Pavlina says humans who share their souls with the Snegovik become terrible creditors. Aksinya notes that the Snegovik, created without flesh and blood, are incomplete and can only be whole through love, by taking another's soul, and that this is why so many Snegovik love stories end in tragedy.

## revelations
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
- The "Snegovik wedding" is a ritual to take the soul or power of the loved one; Snegovik, created without flesh and blood, have incomplete souls.
- Ikhor is a fragment of the Eye of Graeae's power in a Prime Ice body. He and the Eye each call the other "me".
- Pavlina is one of the people who betrayed Aksinya (the Eye says this if the Traveler has seen the Pale Crown Palace series).
- Pavlina's aim is to give her people souls; Aksinya opposes her because she wants the power for the Eye.
### LARGE +
- The House of Hesperides loops on a bell that strikes eight times; changes the Traveler makes in a cycle carry over.
- Aksinya says the Eye was built so that "life may transcend life itself", and that a number of fae were servants created by the Hyperboreans and never perfect.

## characters
### STANDARD
- Pavlina, the Snegurochka princess and host.
- Ikhor, the gentle, wish-granting "groom".
- Aglaya, the polite hostess who tells the story of Larisa.
- Aksinya, the Eye's creator, fighting Pavlina from the Shadow Realm.
- The Eye of Graeae and Paimon, who guide the Traveler.
### LARGE +
- Ladon, the garden guard who speaks of Larisa; Mesherskaya, who scouts guests in Svetloledovka and gives out the Invitation to the "Ritual"; the Snegovik attendants Saltykov and Safonov; Jack Frosts; and the dead Larisa, mentioned in speech but not seen.

## consequences
### STANDARD
Pavlina's ritual is stopped before it can offer up Ikhor's power, and the Traveler receives the Pebble of Freezing Heart. Ikhor is gone and Pavlina has vanished into mist. This is one of the three fragments needed in Her Palace Collapses Into the Blizzard. Aksinya, if the Traveler has not yet met her, introduces herself as the Eye's maker and says that she shares its goal.

## placement
### STANDARD
Story order: after the Snezhnaya Archon Quest chapter, one of three parallel series (with In the Dwelling of Life and On One Side a Palace, On the Other a Tomb) that all lead to Her Palace Collapses Into the Blizzard. Conditional dialogue changes depending on what the Traveler has already seen. Release order: version 7.0.

## entry: Pavlina's ritual
@keys: House of Hesperides, Pavlina's ritual, Snegovik wedding
@version: 7.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
At the House of Hesperides, Pavlina holds a "wedding" at which Ikhor is to offer his soul so that the Snegovik can gain souls. The bell strikes eight times, the house resets, and each cycle lets Pavlina take a little more of the power in the Eye of Graeae's fragments.

## entry: Ikhor
@keys: Ikhor
@version: 7.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Ikhor is a calm young man whose manner Paimon finds childlike, with a Prime Ice body who grants any wish he is asked for. He says he does not feel cold and calls the Eye of Graeae "me". Pavlina uses him as the sacrificial "groom" and, in Procrustes' Iron Bed, pierces him with her sword and absorbs him as golden energy.

## entry: Larisa
@keys: Larisa
@version: 7.0
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Larisa was a Snegurochka, described by Aglaya as the most beautiful of them. According to Aglaya her human husband betrayed her, she killed him, and the broken vow then took her own life. Her fury raised a blizzard that froze lightning in mid-fall. Pavlina called her a friend, and Ladon says plants thrived under her care in the garden.
