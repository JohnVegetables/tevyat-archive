## summary
@spoiler: minor
### LITE
On One Side a Palace, On the Other a Tomb is a Snezhnaya World Quest series added in version 7.0, set in the White Birch Snowgrave. In two quests the Traveler and Paimon explore the Pale Crown Palace, the hidden former home and tomb of the Belyi Tsar, and the frozen mausoleum around it, where they recover one of the three missing fragments of the Eye of Graeae.
### STANDARD +
The quests are "To Gaze Upon the Dreaming Pale Crown" and "The Stone Beasts, Like Ghouls Seated". The series runs concurrently with In the Dwelling of Life and Hesperides of Love and Hate, and all three feed into Her Palace Collapses Into the Blizzard.
### LARGE +
Most of the series is explored through flashbacks and environmental clues: it reveals what Hvergelmir was, why the Belyi Tsar's body is guarded, and how Zver came to be loyal to him. It introduces Aksinya's side of the Eye of Graeae story before the finale.

## plot
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
To Gaze Upon the Dreaming Pale Crown. The Eye of Graeae senses that one of the three pieces of its lost power lies inside the Pale Crown Palace. The palace was once the Belyi Tsar's private home and later his tomb, and the Eye also learns it was created there, in the hidden research facility Hvergelmir. The palace was a place where the Tsar welcomed humans alongside the fae, and where the flame of Kresnik was first kindled before it spread across what became Snezhnaya. After the Tsar died fighting the Abyss and waves of beasts around five hundred years ago, his remains returned to the palace and part of his body became the Tsar's White Birch.

The Stone Beasts, Like Ghouls Seated. With the Eye of Graeae, the Traveler and Paimon cross snow-covered ruins and royal halls, solving puzzles with statues and torches, and fight man-made monsters. A two-headed beast, "Zver", guards the Belyi Tsar's body and tells them to leave. Flashbacks show the Tsar, his servant Aksinya and the bio-alchemical work that produced Zver and other creatures. Zver was a failed prototype abandoned by its creator Koshchei; the Tsar saved it, gave it purpose and treated it as a loyal companion. Zver has guarded the corpse for centuries and hates those who wronged its master.
### LARGE +
To reach the palace the Traveler summons a hidden railway and rides an old prototype train run by the Eye. In transit the Traveler is separated from Paimon and the Eye and enters a vision of Hvergelmir's past, where two researchers, Izmaylov and Azazel, cannot see them. The two are monitoring the Proto-Man, a being held in an egg that stays stable despite the darkness of the Font of the Shadow Realm. Izmaylov fears the newly ascended Tsaritsa's rumoured ban on alchemy will end Hvergelmir's bio-alchemy work and the Tsar's last aspiration. Azazel questions whether to continue after a previous disastrous experiment, and they leave the choice to superiors.

The Traveler explores the facility. A message from Pavlina asks the researchers to look after her cat, Lara, with a joking threat to whoever lets the cat grow parts of another animal. The Traveler finds Lara starving but unable to be fed except from her own bowl, finds Prime Ice that contains something, and reads research notes about prisoners, the Snegovik and the concept of "love". One researcher wonders whether loveless fae could use the technology to gain love. Another claims a certain power should have been theirs rather than part of Aksinya's aberrant dream. Behind a picture frame is a hidden laboratory with an alchemical apparatus; a note says Kresnik Kristall may help change matter and that separated things must be brought back together. The Traveler realises this means combining materials, grinds yellow crystal and Kresnik Kristall ore into powder and bottles it, and a new substance begins to form.

After Zver is beaten, it reveals that the Eye of Graeae is also a prototype, made to hold a "Key", and that its memories have been erased. It warns the Traveler that the Eye may not be as trustworthy as it seems. More flashbacks show Zver's bond with the Tsar and its defiance of Koshchei's orders. The Eye begins to suspect its identity is tied to the Tsar's legacy, though it cannot remember details until its power is whole.

### ULTRA +
At the ruins of Hvergelmir the party finds another fragment of the Eye's body, with a note from Aksinya explaining how it was recovered and hinting at a plan to rebuild the "sacred form" and begin a new era. They learn that collecting fragments is not enough: all must be brought to the "Court of Bliss" to restore the Eye.

## revelations
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
- The Pale Crown Palace was the Belyi Tsar's private residence and later his tomb, and is where the Kresnik flame was first lit; the Tsar's White Birch grew from part of his body.
- Hvergelmir is where the Eye of Graeae was created; researchers there studied a Proto-Man held in an egg.
- Zver was a rejected prototype of Koshchei's, saved and trusted by the Tsar.
- The Eye of Graeae is a prototype made to house a "Key", and its memories were erased.
### LARGE +
- Hvergelmir's notes show experiments on prisoners and Snegovik and a research interest in whether loveless fae could gain love.
- Pavlina was linked to Hvergelmir: she is the author of the cat message, and the Hesperides series later shows her as one of the people who betrayed Aksinya.

## characters
### STANDARD
- The Traveler and Paimon.
- The Eye of Graeae ("Goggles"), whose past the series starts to open up.
- Zver, the two-headed guardian of the Tsar's body.
- The Belyi Tsar and Aksinya, shown in flashback; Aksinya also leaves the note at Hvergelmir.
- Researchers Izmaylov and Azazel, seen only in a vision of Hvergelmir's past.
### LARGE +
- Koshchei is the absent creator who abandoned Zver; the Tsaritsa is discussed by the researchers as newly ascended.

## consequences
### STANDARD
The Traveler recovers one fragment of the Eye of Graeae (a Pebble), and the series establishes that all fragments must be brought to the Court of Bliss. Zver is left defeated but not shown as killed in the available summary.
### LARGE +
The series is required, with Hesperides of Love and Hate and In the Dwelling of Life, to start An Ark That Used to Be a Soul in Her Palace Collapses Into the Blizzard.

## placement
### STANDARD
Story order: it is one of three parallel series after the Snezhnaya Archon Quest chapter that unlock Her Palace Collapses Into the Blizzard. Release order: version 7.0 with the other Snezhnaya World Quest series. Players who finish it first will see different conditional lines in Through the Gates of Ivory; for example, Aksinya's appearance at the false wedding and the Eye's comments about Pavlina's betrayal refer back to the Pale Crown Palace.

## entry: Zver
@keys: Zver
@version: 7.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Zver is a two-headed beast and man-made creature that guards the Belyi Tsar's body in the mausoleum below the Pale Crown Palace. Once an abandoned prototype of Koshchei, it was given purpose by the Tsar and has stayed loyal for centuries. It warns the Traveler away, is defeated in battle, and tells them the Eye of Graeae may not be trustworthy.

## entry: Hvergelmir (research facility)
@keys: Hvergelmir, Proto-Man
@version: 7.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Hvergelmir was the Belyi Tsar's bio-alchemy research organisation, hidden under the Pale Crown Palace. In the Traveler's vision, researchers Izmaylov and Azazel monitor a Proto-Man in an egg and fear that a ban on alchemy would end their work. The ruined site later holds a fragment of the Eye of Graeae and a note from Aksinya.
