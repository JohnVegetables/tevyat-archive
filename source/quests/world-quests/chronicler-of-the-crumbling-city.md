## summary
@spoiler: minor
### LITE
Chronicler of the Crumbling City is a Natlan world quest series in which the Traveler, Paimon and the Little One enter the Great Volcano and discover the buried dragon city of Tollan. A guiding voice speaking through old Secret Source machines helps them restore the city's power, cleanse Abyssal corruption, and obtain the permit needed to reach the Heart of the Sacred Mountain.
### STANDARD +
The series has three main quests: The Frozen, Rekindling Land, In Search of a Hidden Heart, and As the Burning Sun Sears Shadows. Each has sub-quests: two for the first, five for the second. Most of the story is told through machines that speak for the dead, and each time the voice uses a different borrowed name.
### LARGE +
The tone is elegiac. The city is dark, the dragons who built it are gone, and the voice describes it as barely alive. The player work is to relight, cleanse and unlock it, not to learn the whole history. The history that does surface comes in fragments: a war between the surviving dragonborn and humans, the death of the original master of flame, and a trial that ended in farce.

## plot
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
**The Frozen, Rekindling Land.** The party falls into the pitch-dark space below the volcano. A stone figure that looks like a rolled-up creature activates and speaks, naming itself Ehecatl, a name taken from a list of the fallen 150 years after a war. It explains that the master of this place, the original master of flame, died in the war between the remaining dragonborn and humans, and that the city has been dying since. It asks the Traveler to activate a mechanism so the voice can light the city, showing the first Sacred City, Tollan, in ruins. Its power fails and it goes quiet. The Traveler then restabilises the two other Astral Assemblages (The Forsaken Sea of Wisdom and Sealed Site of Sacrifice), and a Secret Source Servant now announces itself as Amaru, once the guardian of the sealed Celestial Axis. The Axis descends through the clouds to a region infested by the Abyss. Amaru warns that the city is infested and says a permit is needed to reach the Heart of the Sacred Mountain.

**In Search of a Hidden Heart.** A larger Secret Source Servant that has no name and no knowledge of the party says it was a prototype made to help Lord Xiuhcoatl with record keeping and has been alone since his fall. It marks sites of Abyssal corrosion where data can be recovered as Phlogiston Units. Three of them are needed to power the Secret Source Dragon Idol guarding the entrance. In the Chamber of Deliberation, the voice returns under the name Awanyu to help obtain the permit (Once, the Sacred Seat of Judgment). Three parallel sub-quests clear corroded areas (the Calculation Array, Force Inversion and the Armament Workshop). In The End of the Road the Traveler gives the Dragon Idol the permit and three Phlogiston Units, and it dives into the pit, opening the way down.

**As the Burning Sun Sears Shadows.** The Traveler enters the huge space beneath the volcano, the Heart of the Sacred Mountain, which is infested with the Abyss. A voice tells the "Chosen of Dragons" that the power of blazing fire now rests within them. The party clears the Abyss, releases the Astral Assemblages, and destroys the Oozing Cores that try to flee.
### LARGE +
Additional detail. The first guide says it can speak only as long as the city has enough phlogiston, and that it uses names from a list of the fallen. It tells the party that, because of an oath, only a "human" may activate the seals, and muses that history repeats itself in cycles of tyranny, revolt and revenge, comparing it to a staged play. Amaru tells them the fireballs (Astral Assemblages) cannot fight the Abyss directly: contact leads to mutual annihilation or corruption.

Records the Servant can read to the party once enough Phlogiston Units are recovered concern how Secret Source Servants were created, "humans and exceptional individuals thereof", and corrosion and physical conditions. The Servant also explains that Lord Xiuhcoatl excavated downward to make a stable experimental environment and there built the ultimate weapon against the Abyss, but the seal on it caused the deepest corruption.

In Once, the Sacred Seat of Judgment, a golden purification barrier rises in the Chamber. As it rises, the party sees shadows of dragon lords denouncing a prince in front of the Silver Prince, accusing him of delivering phlogiston to humans and of sheltering an escaped test subject (Och-Kan). Awanyu speaks scornfully of the assembly and says the denouncement was a power grab. The permit proves to be only a vessel that must be activated by an Iridescent Inscription at the top of a rising platform.
### ULTRA +
During the rise, Awanyu says that when the Abyss swallowed the Dark-Winged Lord and a Celestial Nail reshaped the world and its laws, the dragon race's old reach collapsed. He suggests the world might long ago have been cleansed by the flames of rebirth, asking the party whether they agree. He also says the Thirteen Lords and Three Princes withdrew their charges when the true king appeared, that the Three Princes later died fighting humanity, that ten lords fell when Tollan collapsed, that one remains in Chichen Uctokah and that one survives to the present day, in a form it would not have wished. After the permit is activated he vanishes again.

Gameplay notes (labelled as such, not lore): the Phlogiston meter does not decrease during parts of the third quest, and a Secret Source Beam Cannon with unlimited ammunition appears. Completing certain sub-quests awards achievements.

## revelations
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
- Tollan, the first Sacred City, lies inside the volcano and was once the seat of Lord Xiuhcoatl, the "original master of flame", who died in a war between surviving dragonborn and humans.
- The Heart of the Sacred Mountain (also named Huitzilopochtli by the Servant) was dug when Tollan was rebuilt; Xiuhcoatl built a weapon against the Abyss there, and the seal on it caused the worst corruption.
- The Abyss is invading Tollan, and ending it entirely might require bringing the mountain down.
### LARGE +
- The guiding voice has used the names Ehecatl, Amaru and Awanyu. Ehecatl's name was taken 150 years after the war from a list of the fallen.
- The Chamber of Deliberation preserved a trial of a prince by the Thirteen Lords and Three Princes, as replayed by shadows. The speaker says the charges were pretext for taking authority.

## characters
### STANDARD
The Traveler, Paimon and the Little One; the voice speaking as Ehecatl, Amaru and Awanyu; a nameless record-keeping Secret Source Servant; and shadows of dragon lords seen in the Chamber of Deliberation. Xiuhcoatl and Och-Kan are named but do not appear in person.

## consequences
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Tollan's Celestial Axis is working again, the Heart of the Sacred Mountain has been entered and cleansed, and a voice addressing the "Chosen of Dragons" says the power of blazing fire now rests within them (Paimon feels a warm strength). The party is now at the heart of the volcano with the Abyss pushed back, setting up the next quests (An Omen of Annihilation and the Final Entreaty is listed after the third quest on the wiki).

## placement
### STANDARD
Released in version 5.5. The series page lists Path to the Flaming Peaks before it and A Finale Emberforged after it. The first quest follows Nahuatzin's Leap according to its own infobox. It continues the Little One storyline that began in Lost Traveler in the Ashen Realm.

## entry: Ehecatl
@keys: Ehecatl
@keys2: Tollan, Amaru, Awanyu, Secret Source
@version: 5.5
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Ehecatl is the first name the guiding voice uses, speaking through a Secret Source mechanism that looks like a rolled-up statue. The voice says the name was assigned from a list of the fallen 150 years after the war, and that the real Ehecatl died long ago. It speaks in a formal, theatrical style, startles Paimon with a loud "Warggh", and says its long "exile" is running out. If Investigator of Ancient Ruins was done, Paimon realises this statue is the one Kimosi and Nzaya were looking for.

## entry: Amaru
@keys: Amaru
@keys2: Tollan, Celestial Axis, Ehecatl, Secret Source Servant
@version: 5.5
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Amaru is the second name used, speaking through the Secret Source Servant that once waited on a proud, arrogant master who held the threshold of the sealed Celestial Axis until his death. The voice reactivates the Axis after the Astral Assemblages are restored and warns about the Abyss before the machine shuts down.

## entry: Awanyu
@keys: Awanyu
@keys2: Chamber of Deliberation, Secret Source Servant, permit
@version: 5.5
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Awanyu is the third name the voice takes, from a scribe of the Chamber of Deliberation. As Awanyu it is bitterly scornful of the Chamber's judges and of the dragon lords' "games of authority and power", and it leads the Traveler through the permit sequence before vanishing again.

## entry: Secret Source Servant of Tollan
@keys: Secret Source Servant
@keys2: Tollan, Xiuhcoatl, Phlogiston Unit, record
@version: 5.5
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
A very large, unnamed Secret Source Servant is the only intelligent one without a master. It describes itself as a prototype built to help Lord Xiuhcoatl record information, has lost records to Abyssal invasion, and cannot recall its own name. It explains that phlogiston powers Source Mechanisms and that a Dragon Idol needs three Phlogiston Units. It says no human has come this far down in at least 500 years.

## entry: Heart of the Sacred Mountain
@keys: Heart of the Sacred Mountain
@keys2: Huitzilopochtli, Tollan, Secret Source Dragon Idol
@version: 5.5
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Guarded by a Secret Source Dragon Idol and requiring a permit, the Heart lies beneath fissures in the Sacred City, where corruption is deepest. The Servant says it was excavated when Tollan was rebuilt and also calls it Huitzilopochtli. The Traveler enters in the third quest and cleanses it.
