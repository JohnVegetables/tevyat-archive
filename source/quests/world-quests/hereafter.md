## summary
### LITE
A small Version 2.4 Liyue series. The Traveler buys grilled tiger fish to honour the Yaksha Pervases, meets Xiao and the reformed Wang Ping'an, and helps rebuild Pervases's neglected temple over three days.
### STANDARD +
It requires the quest Butterfly's Dream, and each of its three quests (The Trail of Pervases, All is Well, Return to the Mountains) becomes available only after the daily reset. The series is a gentle follow-up to earlier encounters with Wang Ping'an, once called "Starsnatcher".
### LARGE +
Thematically it is about remembrance of adepti who protected Liyue Harbor without seeking recognition, as Xiao says, and how ordinary people choose to honour them.

## plot
### LITE
The Traveler and Paimon buy fish at Wanmin Restaurant, intending a third portion as an offering to Pervases. Near the temple they meet Xiao and rescue Wang Ping'an from attackers. Wang Ping'an plans to restore the temple. Over later visits the temple is repaired, with Millelith guarding the site, and he stays to write a book about the Vigilant Yaksha.
### STANDARD +
Paimon wonders why nobody maintains the temple of Yakshas who defended Liyue Harbor; Xiao goes quiet and then says they did not do their duty for recognition. After helping Wang Ping'an, the Traveler is told to return once work has begun. On the second visit workers are building and the Ministry of Civil Affairs has posted Millelith. Wang Ping'an fears deliveries may be attacked, so the Traveler defeats nearby Treasure Hoarders, who say they did not know the temple belonged to an adeptus and promise to leave it alone. Wang Ping'an then asks for Sandbearer Wood. On the final visit the temple is complete and the Traveler lights incense at the censer.
### LARGE +
Paimon and the Traveler are unsure whether Wang Ping'an is sincere, which is why he asks them to come back and see the work for themselves. He says he will remain at the temple to compile his book about the Vigilant Yaksha, and welcomes the Traveler to offer incense any time.
### ULTRA +
Gameplay notes: the three quests are gated by the daily reset, so the restoration plays out over three days. The Treasure Hoarders' reaction (promising to leave an adeptus's temple alone for fear of the Adepti's judgment) is the series' only comment on how common Liyue folk regard the adepti.

## revelations
@spoiler: none
### STANDARD
No major revelation. The series shows Wang Ping'an's change of heart and gives a glimpse of Xiao's attitude to remembrance.

## characters
### LITE
The Traveler, Paimon, Xiao and Wang Ping'an, plus Millelith and Treasure Hoarders as background.
### STANDARD +
Xiao appears only at the start and is reserved: he says the Yakshas did not act for recognition. Wang Ping'an is a former thief shown rebuilding the shrine and writing about the Vigilant Yaksha.

## consequences
### STANDARD
Pervases's temple is repaired and guarded, and Wang Ping'an stays as its keeper. The Traveler can offer incense there whenever they want.

## placement
### STANDARD
Released in Version 2.4. It requires Butterfly's Dream and runs as a daily-gated trio of quests. The wiki notes a separate Fontaine quest called Hereafter..., unrelated to this one.
