## summary
@spoiler: none
### LITE
Varuna Gatha is a Sumeru world quest series set in Apam Woods, added in version 3.0 after The World of Aranara. The Traveler and Paimon meet the Aranara Arapandu, who is trying to repair the Varuna Contraption, an ancient machine that turns black rain into good rain. Spores, Fungi and strange rain have wrecked it, and Festival Utsava is near. With three of Arapandu's kin, the Traveler heals it.
### STANDARD +
The series has two parts. Varuna Gatha is the first quest and then three quests, Memory of Stone, Irate Iron Chunk and Slumbering Roots, can be done in any order, one per Aranara. The finale is A Prayer for Rain on the Fecund Land. Its reward is the ability to change the weather in Apam Woods using the Rhythm of the Source Water, and it unlocks the Sprouting Seedlings world quest.
### LARGE +
The tone is gentle and storybook-like. The Aranara speak in short, repetitive sentences that refer to people as "Nara" and to the Traveler as the Golden Nara, and the plot turns on their memory of an earlier Golden Nara, Nara Varuna, whom they compare to the Traveler.

## plot
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Varuna Gatha. Near the Yasna Monument, Paimon and the Traveler find an Aranara surrounded by Fungi and rescue it. It is Arapandu, who says he has an important task for Festival Utsava, for Apam Woods and for Nara Varuna. He tells how the sun and moon were once lost, black rain gnawed the trees, and a golden Nara called Nara Varuna came, and with the Aranara fixed the Varuna Contraption, which washed Vana's pain away. Now the machine's branches and leaves have weakened and the Varunastra, its great structure, flaps wildly and brings heavy rain; Fungi and spores are everywhere. Arapandu cannot clear the Fungi himself because his Ararakalari is weak. The Traveler defeats Fungi on two trees, they reach a "rest stop" and find the first stone "branch" and three stone "leaves", and the Traveler turns the leaves to face the branch. Arapandu heals them with a dance. Three more such sites remain and Arapandu is worried about the three other Aranara: Aranakula at the Yasna Monument, Arabalika at the Ruins of Dahri and Arakunti at the Grove of Dreams.

The three companions. Memory of Stone: the timid Aranakula hides from the Fungi and sobs but accepts the Traveler's help, and the two go together by a safe route to clean spores from the Memory of Stone, a monument the Aranara made for Nara Varuna, then fix the branch and leaves there. Irate Iron Chunk: the Traveler meets Arabalika, a master of Ararakalari who says little, defeats Ruin Guards beside him, and finds that the branch is deep underground like roots. Slumbering Roots: in the Grove of Dreams the Traveler plays Aranara music to bring out Arakunti, a dreamy Aranara, and enters a dream to repair the sites.

A Prayer for Rain on the Fecund Land. With all branches and leaves healed, the Aranara descend beneath the Varunastra to the roots of the Contraption, clear spores and defeat a Winged Dendroshroom, the "bad bug" damaging it. The rain clears and the air improves. Arapandu teaches the Traveler the music to control the Contraption, Rhythm of the Source Water, and says the Traveler is worthy of Aranara friendship like Nara Varuna.
### LARGE +
Details. At the start Arapandu calls the Traveler a "golden and brave Nara" and tells Paimon that the Varuna Contraption and Varunastra are neither weapons nor winged water-Aranara. He is startled when Paimon mentions picking firewood, and Paimon hastily clarifies she meant nothing by it. Aranara draw power for their Ararakalari from Vana's plants; a strong one can drive off Fungi and large iron "Nara" (Ruin Guards), which the Traveler can do without one. The Contraption's branches and leaves collect rain like streams feeding a lake; broken branches block the streams and the lake dries out, which is how Arapandu explains why it is failing. Arapandu stresses that nobody can be sure the repair will work "just like how the sun rose last time and the time before that". The Traveler must stay close to Aranakula's safe road and avoid alerting spore-carrying Fungi in Memory of Stone, and Arabalika deals environmental Dendro damage in his fights.

Arabalika's lines show a hard-edged sense of caution: he tests the Traveler's skill, scolds Paimon for being careless, and says Marana are still around after the repair, so there is more to do. Aranakula says he wants the Traveler to see the nursery flowers once they bloom again. Arakunti's lines, such as "Mushrooms yummy", are cheerful, sleepy, and say the humid dreams in the Grove are fine but the spores are bad.

After the repair Paimon asks about the odd events in Vimara Village, where children disappear for a time and always return safely. Arapandu says he does not know yet and will ask other Aranara in Vanarana.
### ULTRA +
Arapandu says the Contraption's branches and leaves were made by Aramuhukunda and the Lord of Dendro, are stone, and "channel Vanarana's power into the Varuna Contraption"; its roots are in the underground and supply it through the whole of Vana. The Aranara know the Contraption works with music: the melody the Traveler learns is called the Rhythm of the Source Water. Arakunti tells Paimon that the Contraption gets its power from all over Vana.

Gameplay notes, labelled as such: completing the series unlocks Sprouting Seedlings, and afterwards playing the Rhythm of the Source Water near the Varuna Contraption changes the weather in Apam Woods; rewards also include the item Stories of You and the Aranara.

## revelations
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
- The Varuna Contraption is an ancient machine, made by Aramuhukunda and the Lord of Dendro, that turns bad rain into good rain and heals the land of Vana.
- The Contraption has fallen into disrepair: the Aranara no longer fully understand it, Nara Varuna and Aramuhukunda are gone from Vanarana, and spores and Fungi grew thick around it.
- The Contraption can be controlled by Aranara music, and the Traveler learns it.
### LARGE +
- The Aranara keep a memorial to Nara Varuna at the Yasna Monument, the Memory of Stone.
- The Aranara believe the Traveler is a golden Nara like their earlier hero.
- A Winged Dendroshroom, a Fungus, was gnawing at the Contraption's roots.

## characters
### STANDARD
The Traveler and Paimon; four Aranara: Arapandu (leader of the effort), Aranakula (timid, loves soil and seeds), Arabalika (strong, terse, a master of Ararakalari) and Arakunti (dreamy, linked to the Grove of Dreams). Araja and Arama of Vanarana are mentioned as organisers of the festival preparations. Silapna is a feature of the Grove of Dreams before which the Traveler plays music.

## consequences
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
The Varuna Contraption works again, the rain in Apam Woods improves and the worst of the spores recedes, though Marana remain. The Traveler learns the Rhythm of the Source Water. Aranakula hopes to restore the nurseries, and the wider thread about Vimara Village is left to Arapandu to follow up.

## placement
### STANDARD
Released in 3.0 with the Sumeru launch. The wiki lists The World of Aranara as the requirement; the first quest begins southeast of the Yasna Monument. Memory of Stone, Irate Iron Chunk and Slumbering Roots start after Varuna Gatha and can be done in any order, and A Prayer for Rain on the Fecund Land follows them. The series leads to Sprouting Seedlings.

## entry: Varuna Contraption
@keys: Varuna Contraption, Varunastra
@keys2: Aranara, Apam Woods, Arapandu, Nara Varuna
@version: 3.0
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
The Varuna Contraption is a machine created by Aramuhukunda and the Lord of Dendro in Vana to purify rain; the Varunastra is its great visible structure in Apam Woods. When Arapandu meets the Traveler it is out of order: spore-laden rain falls and Fungi spread. Its "branches" and "leaves" are stone sites spread across the forest and its roots are underground. After the quests, playing the Rhythm of the Source Water near it changes the weather.

## entry: Nara Varuna
@keys: Nara Varuna
@keys2: Aranara, Arapandu, Varuna Contraption, Aramuhukunda
@version: 3.0
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Nara Varuna is an earlier golden Nara whom the Aranara remember for restoring the sun and moon and repairing the Varuna Contraption. The Aranara use the same words for the Traveler, and the Memory of Stone is a memorial for Nara Varuna. Canon in this series gives no further identity, and Nara Varuna is no longer in Vanarana.

## entry: Arapandu
@keys: Arapandu
@keys2: Aranara, Apam Woods, Varuna Contraption
@version: 3.0
@spoiler: none
### STANDARD
Arapandu is an Aranara who leads the effort to repair the Varuna Contraption. He is earnest and gentle, speaks in simple storybook terms, and has a weak Ararakalari. He trusts Paimon despite her teasing, and later promises to look into Vimara Village.

## entry: Aranakula
@keys: Aranakula
@keys2: Aranara, Yasna Monument, Memory of Stone
@version: 3.0
@spoiler: none
### STANDARD
Aranakula is a timid Aranara who is at ease with soil and seeds and who hides when Fungi are near. He keeps the Memory of Stone, dreads bad spores, and measures himself unfavourably against Arabalika. He hopes to revive the nursery flowers.

## entry: Arabalika

### STANDARD
Arabalika is a terse, strong Aranara with a powerful Ararakalari who defends the Ruins of Dahri against Ruin Guards. He tests the Traveler's strength, warns Paimon not to be careless, and reminds everyone that Marana remain.

## entry: Arakunti

### STANDARD
Arakunti is a dreamy Aranara who withdrew to the Grove of Dreams. He speaks in a drifting poetic way, enjoys music and mushrooms, and the Traveler must play Aranara music to draw him out.
