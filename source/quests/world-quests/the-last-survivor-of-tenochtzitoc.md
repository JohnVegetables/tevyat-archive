## summary
### LITE
The Last Survivor of Tenochtzitoc is a world quest at Wavey Bay in the Easybreeze Holiday Resort, Natlan, added in 5.8. A resort actor named Papar, who plays the hero Tenoch under the title "Tenoyollotzin", starts acting out a script and attacks the Traveler. Pucli, the resort's entertainment supervisor, recruits the Traveler and Paimon to help; the trail leads to the Masters of the Night-Wind shaman Leyla, the great shaman Citlali, invisible Asha spirits and finally Enjou of the Abyss Order, who had been hiding in the costume. The story ends in a hidden realm where the party fights a Bakunawa remnant.
### STANDARD
Behind the comedy are three threads: Enjou's search for the vanished Mare Jivari, Citlali's pursuit of him, and the legend of Tenoch, who let himself be eaten by Bakunawa to defeat it. The party learns that Papar was never the one speaking; Enjou, disguised in the costume, was reciting a script, then fled when Citlali arrived. Citlali and the Traveler follow Enjou's map to a cave and a woven scroll that opens a hall with memorial seats for the six heroes and Tenoch, and Enjou takes the chance to follow. Citlali binds a part of her spirit into Enjou in exchange for Papar's safety, and the Traveler fights Bakunawa with Enjou's body speaking for her. Enjou leaves them with a keepsake and a short, unreliable account of who he is.
### LARGE +
The quest is the first of the post-summer 5.8 content to link the Mare Jivari investigation to the Masters of the Night-Wind and to the Muratans, and it leaves Enjou's motives and loyalties open. It also gives the Traveler an item of Enjou's, Lavawalker's Resolution, and begins a series of investigative reports on Bakunawa that can be collected again after waiting 72 hours.
### ULTRA +
Gameplay and side details: the Local Legend "The Last Survivor of Tenochtzitoc" disappears from his usual location from step 3 and is replaced by a notice sign. The quest rewards 450 Adventure EXP, 50 Primogems, 50,000 Mora, 5 Hero's Wit, 4 Mystic Enhancement Ore, the three Investigative Reports, Someone's Tattered Notes (I) and Lavawalker's Resolution, plus an Iktomi Scroll: Map for those who skipped The Spirit of Ah K'ulbatil. It grants the achievement "Until the Day That the Fires Turn Me Into Ashes" and the avatar The Scholar Who Crossed the Mare Jivari.

## plot
@version: 5.8
### STANDARD
Tenoyollotzin. Near Wavey Bay, Paimon and the Traveler meet a Pipilpan-costumed man reciting a story about "Tenoyollotzin", Wanjiru and a snaggletoothed child. He treats Paimon as the insolent child and attacks. When his HP drops to 30 percent, Pucli stops the fight, and explains that the man is Papar, an on-site actor in the resort's Tenochtzitoc theme. Papar has been acting erratically and chanting a script. Pucli sedates him with a blowpipe dart, and with the party's help decides to look for a shaman while he looks into rumours of hidden spirits.

Leyla. The party finds Leyla, a young Masters of the Night-Wind shaman on holiday, and brings him to the spot where Papar first spoke. Using a ring of carrots as a divination medium, he sees an Asha on a swing, three Asha gathered around a woven scroll, then three fallen Asha in a burning wasteland approached by a man in a kimono. He reads this as Asha being connected to Papar's behaviour and gives the party his carrots, whose juice makes invisible Asha visible.

Asha and Citlali. Overnight Pucli reports that workers building the resort complained of missing materials and rippling water near the huts. The party finds and paints three hidden Asha, then meets Citlali, who has come for the man seen in Leyla's vision. She names him as Kokuchuu, who also used the alias Sanka, and Paimon identifies him as Enjou of the Abyss Order. Citlali has marked him with a spirit trace so she can sense him nearby. Back at the huts "Tenoyollotzin" suddenly calls Citlali an old lady. Citlali tears off the costume and finds nobody inside; Enjou had been in it. He watches from a cliff and complains that she is more of a nuisance than he is.

The cave. The party collects three investigative reports from Enjou's camp (on Bakunawa, the Mare Jivari and a secret cave). They follow Enjou's redrawn map to a cave behind a waterfall, where a woven scroll too old to work is revived by the Asha, who recognise Sanhaj's relic. It sends them to a hall unfamiliar to Citlali, where her connection to the Wayob is cut.
### LARGE +
The hall. Memorial seats bear inscriptions to Tupac, Menilek, Sundjatta, Tenoch (whose remains are buried here), Wanjiru, Sanhaj Kompore (also buried here) and Burkina. Sanhaj's seat is empty. When the party places his relic on it, the central seat glows and the Traveler and Paimon vanish. Citlali stays behind to avoid trapping everyone and is met by Enjou, who followed through the same scroll. They trade barbs. Enjou offers Papar's safe return for passage to the Mare Jivari, and Citlali sets conditions (no vandalism, no harm to plants, protect the Traveler and Paimon) and has Itzpapa pin him while she splits part of her spirit into him.

The realm. The Traveler and Paimon arrive in a Night Kingdom-like place where the Wayob link is missing but the Abyss is still present. After a short trek they reach what looks like the Mare Jivari and find Enjou speaking in Citlali's voice, with her spirit attached to him. She gives them Sanhaj's ritual sword, the Calamity of Eshu, which she borrowed from the chief before leaving. Enjou argues for killing the beast ahead, which he identifies as Bakunawa's "permeation", saying that it will otherwise eat them and then spread into Natlan. Using the Calamity of Eshu, the Traveler blocks claw swipes, severs the creature's arms from its head and interrupts a charged attack with Soulshard Rain until it falls.

Aftermath. Enjou tries to scavenge, and when Citlali's voice stops him he tells his tale of a divided kingdom and Tenoch's people, drives her spirit out, and says that without Bakunawa's power the Traveler cannot enter the true Mare Jivari. He gives the Traveler a flower that glows in flames and walks deeper in. Citlali's voice returns briefly to ask for his real name and he promises to give it one day. Back in the hall Citlali says she will report to Mavuika, and that tablets behind the hall name former Pyro Archons. Papar is safe, sleeping off Aphimead on Tete Isle after Enjou befriended him as a tourist called "Hayne".
### ULTRA +
Details. Citlali's pursuit differs by branch: if the player has not done The Spirit of Ah K'ulbatil, Citlali says she got Sanhaj's relic and a map from a previous encounter with Enjou and gives them to the Traveler; if they have, she asks the Traveler to produce the items they were left earlier. In both cases she tells them that Enjou once tried to use Leyla for information about the Mare Jivari until Ororon stepped in. A throwaway joke has Citlali define "Ororon" in the Night-Wind Dictionary as a cultisham, a shaman who cultivates vegetables.

## revelations
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
- Bakunawa appeared in western Natlan about five hundred years ago with other Abyssal monsters, devouring the tribespeople of Tenochtzitoc. Citlali says it could not be killed, as its parts regrow and seek each other out.
- Tenoch let Sanhaj engrave phlogiston on his body to release border spells, then allowed himself to be eaten. With his body as offering, Sanhaj summoned a miniature Night Kingdom in Bakunawa's belly. The resulting clash among the Abyss's power, the Lord of the Night and Tenoch's spirit shattered the monster; some believe it never truly died.
- The Mare Jivari is described by Pucli as a land west of Tenochtzitoc born from the Abyssal invasion, where warriors became dust and souls were bound to it, until it vanished about twenty years ago. Asha appeared around then and are said to be "ashes" blown from it as well as "benevolent souls".
### LARGE +
- The Investigative Report: Bakunawa describes it as an out-of-control creation of "Gold" whose immortality is not from the power of "Life" but a more transcendent Abyssal power. The Mare Jivari report says all records of its disappearance were erased and cites an ancient legend of a troupe wandering through "the blazing Mare Jivari" thousands of years ago, raising the possibility of a temporal disruption.
- Citlali says the hall was likely made by former Pyro Archons five hundred years ago to contain Bakunawa. Tablets mention a blocked Mare Jivari, holding back the Black Tide, an absent Kiongozi, and a Wind-Worn Booklet by Cochanina about sealing the borders across generations.
- Enjou says his remembered places (Vindagnyr, the Sunchildren, Gurabad, Moshiri Ceremonial Site, Remuria, the Vucub Caquix Tower and others) do not belong to him, and that the Abyss Order is not his home.
### ULTRA +
- Enjou claims in his story that Tenoch's people wielded Anemo, that their burial place was called the "Windless Land", and that kin who left 500 years earlier became the Muratans in Mondstadt. He frames the tale around a king (Xbalanque), his steadfast follower (Och-Kan), a civil war and two exile groups. These are presented as his account.
- A short note from Isaja reads "Task complete. Now I sing!", a more recent booklet in the hall.

## characters
### STANDARD
Enjou drives most of the plot, charming, evasive and flexible about his principles. Citlali is protective, sharp-tongued and comically hostile to him, calling herself Granny Itztli. Pucli is the worried resort supervisor and childhood friend of Papar. Papar is the actor, never really present. Leyla is the young shaman who treats Ororon as his master and Citlali as a great shaman, and calls the Traveler "Master Gramps/Granny". Paimon and the Traveler carry the investigation.
### LARGE +
Enjou masquerades as Papar's friend "Hayne" and as the costume's occupant; Citlali learns this only after the fight. Itzpapa and Citlalin, Citlali's spirit companions, assist her in the hall. The historical figures Tenoch, Sanhaj, Wanjiru, Menilek, Burkina, Sundjatta and Tupac appear in script, inscriptions and legend; Xbalanque and Och-Kan appear only in Enjou's tale.

## consequences
### STANDARD
Enjou disappears into the deeper realm and Citlali's spirit trace on him is consumed by a strange power. Citlali plans to consult Mavuika, asks the party to keep the hall secret for now, and warns them not to go deeper. The Traveler keeps the Calamity of Eshu only for the duration of the visit and gets Lavawalker's Resolution, a flower from Enjou. Papar is found safe. The Mare Jivari mystery is unresolved.
### LARGE +
After completion, Bakunawa can be fought again 72 hours later to yield Someone's Tattered Notes (II), and again 72 hours after that for Corroded Tattered Notes (III).

## placement
### STANDARD
Released in 5.8. It requires completing A Curtain Call for a Colorful Summer Night! and begins in Wavey Bay at the Easybreeze Holiday Resort. The Spirit of Ah K'ulbatil, the earlier Leyla quest, changes some dialogue but is not required. There is no previous or next quest listed in the infobox.

## entry: Papar

### STANDARD
Papar is a resort actor who plays the hero Tenoch under the honorific "Tenoyollotzin", the honored heart of Tenochtzitoc. Pucli says he and Pucli are close friends whose mothers are friends, and that Papar has recently recited a script and acted erratically. The behaviour was really Enjou in the costume. Papar himself was drugged with Aphimead and left sleeping on Tete Isle, unharmed.

## entry: Pucli
@keys: Pucli
@keys2: Easybreeze Holiday Resort, Papar, Tenoyollotzin
@version: 5.8
### STANDARD
Pucli is the entertainment supervisor at the Easybreeze Holiday Resort. He sedates the "Tenoyollotzin" actor with a blowpipe, apologises constantly to guests, and worries mostly about facing Papar's mother. He explains the Asha and the Mare Jivari to the party.

## entry: Asha
@keys: Asha
@keys2: Mare Jivari, Pucli, Leyla, Enjou
@version: 5.8
### STANDARD
In Tenochtzitoc culture, per Pucli, "Asha" means both "benevolent soul" and "ashes" blown from the Mare Jivari. People believe they are Monetoo with a soul and treat them as more approachable. They appeared roughly when the Mare Jivari vanished. They follow the party out of affection for Sanhaj's relic and unlock the old woven scroll.

## entry: Bakunawa
@keys: Bakunawa
@keys2: Tenoch, Mare Jivari, Enjou, Citlali
@version: 5.8
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Bakunawa is a mountain-sized Abyssal beast that devoured Tenochtzitoc's people about five hundred years ago. It regrows from its pieces and was shattered only after Tenoch's sacrifice and Sanhaj's border spell. In the quest the party fights what Enjou calls its "permeation", a possibly partial form that has an immortality unlike other creations of "Gold" and a power Enjou says can corrupt time.

## entry: Enjou's parting account
@keys: Enjou
@keys2: Muratans, Tenochtzitoc, Mare Jivari, Windless Land
@version: 5.8
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
After the fight Enjou claims to know the history of lost places and the people of Tenochtzitoc, says he serves the Abyss Order but that it is not his home, and asks where the Traveler's home is. His story of exiles and the Muratans is deliberately told to distract Citlali, and whether any of it concerns himself is left open.
