## summary
@version: 1.2
### LITE
Lost in the Snow is a Version 1.2 world quest on Dragonspine. A boy named Joel waits at the Dragonspine Adventurer Camp for his father, who went up the mountain and has not come back. The Traveler and Paimon follow the father's trail through three campsites by reading his notes, and learn that he was trapped in a cave by a snowstorm and a rockfall but planned a way out.
### STANDARD
Joel is told to wait at the camp by a Knight of Favonius. The quest is a short search that ends without a reunion; the notes show that the father intended to keep his promise to bring Joel to see the snow. It leads on to the quest Snowy Silhouette: Hope and, later, links to the Version 2.3 event Shadows Amidst Snowstorms, where the father is found.
### LARGE +
It requires finishing the Prologue's first act, The Outlander Who Caught the Wind, and starts at the Dragonspine Adventurer Camp. Completing it allows the hidden exploration objective "The Foxes' Affection".

## plot
@version: 1.2
### STANDARD
Joel, a child waiting at the camp, explains that his father promised to take him to see the snow. When the agreed time passed, Joel came to Dragonspine alone, only to be told by a Knight that his father had gone up toward the summit. The Knight would not let him follow. Joel asks the Traveler to look for him, and recalls that his father meant to follow the Snow-Covered Path first, then visit the outskirts of the Entombed City, then try for the summit.

The first note, at a campsite on the Snow-Covered Path, is cheerful. The father says the weather and supplies are good, and mentions finding a book from the series "The Boar Princess" that Joel must have slipped into his bag; he wonders if it is bringing him luck. The second note, found under snow that has to be melted with Pyro, is from a western-slope campsite, where he decides to try for the summit and says he will turn back if anything goes wrong. The third, in a cave near the summit, is scribbled: a snowstorm came suddenly, he lost half of his supplies, and rocks collapsed to seal the entrance. He plans to climb down a cliff and jump the rest into deep snow, adds that supplies will last only a few days, repeats his promise to Joel, and asks anyone who finds the note to put food in a bowl for the foxes that kept him company.

The group feed the foxes, which stay hidden, and report to Joel. Whatever the Traveler says, Joel refuses to believe the worst. He insists his father is a great adventurer who must have reached safety, is resting, or has made camp, and he decides to keep waiting at the camp.
### LARGE +
Details. The Traveler may tell Joel what was found or say nothing; either way he says he will wait. If told, he thinks his father may be resting or at a new camp at the summit. If told nothing, he thinks the Knight was mistaken. Either way he thanks the Traveler, gives a reward and ends with a worried line, wondering where his father is.
### ULTRA +
Gameplay detail: the second note requires melting snow with Pyro and the cave requires breaking a rock wall. Paimon notes that the third campsite has only the note and a feeding bowl. After completing the quest, the player can begin the hidden exploration objective "The Foxes' Affection".

## revelations
@version: 1.2
### STANDARD
The father's three notes are the quest's entire evidence. The cheerful tone of the first gives way to danger in the third, and nothing in them says whether his climb down succeeded.

## epilogue
@version: 2.3
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Later content (the Version 2.3 event Shadows Amidst Snowstorms) shows Joel's father, Joserf, found alive but with memory loss that leaves him recalling only Joel and Joel's mother. This quest itself never mentions that.

## characters
@version: 1.2
### STANDARD
Joel, a child at the Dragonspine Adventurer Camp, who trusts that his father is safe; the Traveler and Paimon; and an unnamed Knight of Favonius, who told Joel to stay. The father appears only through his notes.

## consequences
@version: 1.2
### STANDARD
Joel stays at the camp to wait. The Traveler receives rewards of 300 Adventure EXP, 40 Primogems, 2 Hero's Wit, 4 Mystic Enhancement Ore and 30,000 Mora, plus 100 reputation with Mondstadt (game rewards, not in-world).
### LARGE +
The next quest in the series is Snowy Silhouette: Hope. The father's fate is not settled until the later event.

## placement
@version: 1.2
### STANDARD
Added in Version 1.2, when Dragonspine became accessible. It is an optional Dragonspine world quest and not part of the Archon Quests. Later, the Version 2.3 event Shadows Amidst Snowstorms lists it as a prerequisite and resolves Joel's story.

### STANDARD
Joel's father left a trail of campsite notes on Dragonspine: cheerful ones at the Snow-Covered Path and the western slope, then a scribbled one in a cave near the summit, written after a snowstorm and a rockfall trapped him. He planned to climb down the cliff and jump into deep snow, and asked whoever read the note to feed the foxes that had kept him company.
