## summary
@spoiler: minor
### LITE
Lost Traveler in the Ashen Realm is a four-act Natlan world quest series added in version 5.2. The Traveler, Paimon and the Little One enter the forbidden ruined city of Ochkanatlan to find the "Jade of Return", a treasure sought by countless adventurers. They are joined by an adventurer named Bona and a small green guide, Cocouik, and are hunted by a violent dragon.
### STANDARD +
The route leads from the city streets through the Core of Chu'ulel and an Altar of Primal Flame up to the sky-ship called the Tonatiuh. The legend of the Jade of Return turns out to be bait, the dragon is a corrupted former ruler, and what the party recovers is a second Golden Entreaty, one of the tokens needed to lift the Little One's curse.
### LARGE +
The series is written as a story-within-a-story: the hero everyone is following (Bona) was herself following an earlier expedition, and the Traveler ends up completing an adventure that others began and failed.

## plot
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
**Act I, City Buried By Ash (Vaulting the Wall of Morning Mist).** Following a violent dragon, the party reaches Ochkanatlan, a ruin the Saurian Relics Association treats as forbidden. An explosion in the Vucub Caquix Tower drops them into the lower city. They find the notes of a nameless adventurer who came for the Jade of Return, meet a mysterious adventurer, and fight their way through Ashflow Street with the help of a small green guide whose rings of light can suppress Abyssal Blight. The dragon is shot down and driven off.

**Act II, A Dream of Gazing Upon the Distant Sky.** With Bona and Cocouik, the party looks for the Altar of Primal Flame so they can reach the Tonatiuh. Cocouik refuses to enter a ruin because of the Abyssal corruption inside. Below, the Traveler cleanses the Core of Chu'ulel and finds a second chunk of Pyrophosphorite wrapped in more of the nameless adventurer's notes. Offering both chunks on the Altar sends the group flying up through the clouds, and they land at the Tonatiuh.

**Act III, The Tonatiuh Quivers.** On the Tonatiuh the Traveler finds a stronger, flame-infused Pyrophosphorite that the Little One can handle, reunites with Bona and uses Firestones to break the rock holding the Jade of Return. The treasure is a Golden Entreaty. Bona then behaves oddly, the dragon snatches the Entreaty, and the group follows it. Cocouik helps the Traveler bring the dragon down, and a voice inside identifies itself as Och-Kan, former ruler of Ochkanatlan, asking for help against the corruption in his soul.

**Epilogue, The Chosen One's Promise.** The Traveler returns to the Tonatiuh and finds the Little One alive. Bona is nowhere to be seen. Paimon and the Traveler decide to keep the Golden Entreaty and carry it to the volcano.
### LARGE +
Details worth keeping in a scene: the nameless adventurer's notes say the Abyss is no longer as frightening as when the writer was born, mention an "Aunt Liriwu", and describe the Core of Chu'ulel as the old heart of the city, where the tribe says a Sacred Lord once turned himself into the heart of the dragon city. The guide's repeated, wordless chirps are interpreted by Bona (she admits she is guessing), and the party eventually learns that following strict logic does not work in this place. Before the flight to the Altar, Bona says her Flower-Feather Clan background gives her plenty of flying experience, and she races ahead of the others out of excitement.

On the Tonatiuh the dialogue differs by player progress: if Between Pledge and Forgettance is complete, Paimon and the Traveler recognise the Jade of Return as a Golden Entreaty identical to the one found in the Sage's ruins; if not, Paimon says it looks like a food platter.
### ULTRA +
In the Moment of Awakening, the Traveler enters Och-Kan's memories. Kukulkan appears to him there and presses him to abandon human-centred ideals, accusing him of betraying dragonkind and Xbalanque's oath, and proposing to wake Huitzilopochtli by combining the Golden Entreaties. Och-Kan refuses. The memories also show Taika, with Copan, Titu and Legba, climbing the Tonatiuh and falling to a trap, and years later Taika's daughter Bona doing the same with Cocouik, though she managed to dismantle the trap.

Gameplay note, labelled as such: Act I lets the player control Cocouik for a Super Awesomesauce Laser sequence; the Little One and Bona fight alongside the player as an ally. These do not change the lore.

## revelations
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
- The "evil dragon" that attacks the party in Ochkanatlan is Och-Kan, the former ruler of the city, whose soul was corrupted by poison and who was turned into a dragon.
- The Jade of Return is a Golden Entreaty. Och-Kan says it can cleanse Natlan's Abyssal filth but at a cost, and that he and the Little One both bear the Flamelord's Blessing. Two Golden Entreaties must be brought to the sacred mountain to remove that blessing.
- The legend of the Jade of Return was fabricated by Kukulkan, as bait for a trap.
### LARGE +
- Kukulkan wants to use the combined Golden Entreaties to activate Huitzilopochtli and bring about a new civilisation beyond both dragons and humans, even at great destruction. Och-Kan refuses and says he believes in humanity. Kukulkan warns he will return to claim Och-Kan's soul as part of an ongoing experiment.
- Och-Kan's memories are fragmentary, so he cannot fully explain the Entreaty's purpose.

## characters
### STANDARD
The Traveler, Paimon, the Little One (a young Tepetlisaurus companion), Bona (a self-described adventurer of the Flower-Feather Clan), Cocouik (a small green guide), Och-Kan (the corrupted dragon), and Kukulkan (in memory scenes). Taika, Copan, Titu and Legba appear only in Och-Kan's memories.

## consequences
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The party now holds a second Golden Entreaty and plans to take it to the volcano to help lift the Little One's curse. The unresolved question of Bona's nature remains, as does the matter of Kukulkan's plan.
### LARGE +
A pair of goggles left near the damaged mural on the Tonatiuh can be found after the quest and update the nameless adventurer's notes. The Cornerstone of Stars and Flames is received at the end.

## placement
### STANDARD
Released in version 5.2. The series page lists Between Pledge and Forgettance as the previous series and Path to the Flaming Peaks as the next. The dialogue changes depending on whether Between Pledge and Forgettance has been finished.

## entry: Bona
@keys: Bona
@keys2: Ochkanatlan, Cocouik, Tonatiuh, Jade of Return
@version: 5.2
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Bona describes herself as an adventurer of the Flower-Feather Clan who is chasing the Jade of Return. She is cheerful, impulsive and eager to prove herself, quick to claim she can read Cocouik's chirps and then admit she is guessing. According to Och-Kan's memories she is the daughter of Taika, who led an earlier expedition up the Tonatiuh. She disappears before the series ends and her nature is not explained.

## entry: Cocouik
@keys: Cocouik
@keys2: Ochkanatlan, Bona, Altar of Primal Flame
@version: 5.2
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Cocouik ("Coco" to Paimon) is a small green dragonspirit-like guide. Bona's notes say she woke it from the heart of the machine using Pyrophosphorite and named it after turquoise. It leads the party to the Altar of Primal Flame, and its rings of light can suppress Abyssal Blight. It is frightened of corrupted ruins. Och-Kan later retains memories of his time as Cocouik.

## entry: Och-Kan, the dragon of Ochkanatlan
@keys: Och-Kan
@keys2: Ochkanatlan, Dragon of the City of Flowing Ash, Golden Entreaty
@version: 5.2
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Och-Kan appears first as the "evil dragon", the Dragon of the City of Flowing Ash, then speaks as the former ruler of Ochkanatlan. He says his soul was poisoned and turned into a dragon. Tribal legend says he oppressed his people to build the Tonatiuh, which attracted the Abyss; Bona notes that storytellers tell different tales about him. He refuses Kukulkan's plan.

## entry: Jade of Return
@keys: Jade of Return
@keys2: Ochkanatlan, Tonatiuh, Golden Entreaty, Bona
@version: 5.2
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The "Jade of Return" is a legendary treasure that countless adventurers have sought in Ochkanatlan. In the series it is found sealed in a rock on the Tonatiuh and is a Golden Entreaty. Och-Kan's memories show the legend was invented by Kukulkan to lure people into a trap.

## entry: Altar of Primal Flame and the Tonatiuh route
@keys: Altar of Primal Flame
@keys2: Tonatiuh, Pyrophosphorite, Ochkanatlan
@version: 5.2
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
The Altar of Primal Flame is a mechanism that sends travelers up to the Tonatiuh when offered Pyrophosphorite; the party offers two chunks to be safe. The Tonatiuh, a sky-borne vessel, looks to Paimon like a ball of yarn or snakes. Firestones, a variant of Pyrophosphorite found on the Tonatiuh, activate its own altar.
