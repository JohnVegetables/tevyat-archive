## summary
### LITE
A short Version 2.6 series in the Chasm's Underground Mines. The Traveler meets Katarina Snezhevna, a Fatui mage cut off from her supply line, and looks for her missing teammate Nikolay over three days.
### STANDARD +
The series is started by defeating a Fatui Electro Cicin Mage near the Glowing Narrows waypoint. It has two quests, Lost in a Foreign Land (day one) and Lost in a Foreign Land: Seeking (days two and three), and later steps wait for the daily reset.
### LARGE +
It is a small portrait of Fatui loyalty and isolation: Katarina has been underground so long that she has never heard of the Traveler and does not know what has happened between the Fatui and Liyue.

## plot
### LITE
Katarina invites the Traveler to her hideout and offers Fire-Water. She says everything the Fatui does prepares for a final battle against a common enemy and an inescapable long night. She later asks the Traveler to find Nikolay; the Traveler finds his insignia and diary scraps. On the last day Katarina has vanished, leaving a letter next to a Shadowy Husk.
### STANDARD +
On day one Katarina gives only her name, fights Floating Fungi alongside the Traveler and says to visit her hideout. She is out of black tea, sugar and milk, so she serves Fire-Water. She has not heard of the Traveler because her information comes through the Fatui supply line, which Paimon notes must have been cut off long ago. Awkwardly, she tells them to leave, then asks them to return. On day two she asks them to find Nikolay, who lured enemies away from the team. The Traveler finds his insignia and diary fragments describing a secret chamber with two exits and a swordsman in black, and a tunnel sealed by fallen rocks. Katarina says Nikolay would not flee; she hopes he fought to the end. If he is alive she would punish him as a deserter, and if dead she will be proud of him, in the spirit of the Fatui saying "If not us, then who."
### LARGE +
She mentions that she was a good friend of Lyudochka, who looked after her while they grew up in the House of the Hearth, and guesses Lyudochka is doing well. On day three Katarina is gone from the hideout. The Traveler goes to the first diary spot and finds a letter from Nikolay that Katarina had carried because it smelled of Fire-Water, with a Shadowy Husk standing nearby.
### ULTRA +
Gameplay notes: the series gates on the daily reset (day one, then days two and three). Katarina's Fire-Water is offered twice, and the Fire-Water smell is why she kept Nikolay's letter, which the Traveler finds on the last day.

## revelations
@spoiler: none
### STANDARD
No world-shaking revelation. It shows that some Fatui members are cut off from news and loyal to ideals of sacrifice, and hints at the Fatui's wider goal of facing a common enemy.

## characters
### LITE
The Traveler, Paimon, Katarina Snezhevna, and by report Nikolay and Lyudochka.
### STANDARD +
Katarina is stern, proud and reticent about her mission. Nikolay is her missing teammate. Lyudochka, a friend from the House of the Hearth, appears only in her recollection.

## consequences
### STANDARD
No lasting change to the world. Katarina's hideout is left empty and her fate is implied but not shown.

## placement
### STANDARD
Released in Version 2.6 alongside the Chasm: Underground Mines content, in the same area as The Chasm Delvers. It is a small side story.
