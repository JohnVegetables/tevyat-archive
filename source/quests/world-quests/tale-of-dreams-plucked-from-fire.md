## summary
### LITE
Tale of Dreams Plucked From Fire is a two-quest world quest series in Toyac Springs, Natlan, made of Tracer No Tracing and Seeker No Finding. The Traveler, Paimon and Little One meet Chikya, a back-pained Masters of the Night-Wind scroll keeper who claims to be hunting an elixir that cures all injuries. Following her scrolls through ruins of the Sage of the Stolen Flame, they pass a phlogiston trial and reach the Upper Sanctum, where the "treasure" turns out to be a hot spring.
### STANDARD
Both quests are lighthearted and character-driven. Chikya, a self-described Phlogiston Usage Tutor, uses ancient woven scrolls to lead the party past Phlogiston Engravings and Spiritdoors, relying on the Traveler's ability to read phlogiston directly. The goal she names, a cure-all elixir, is not what she finds; the Sage's gift is a spring that grants a chosen hero the power to wield phlogiston. Chikya herself, who cannot use phlogiston because of an old Abyss-related injury, gets nothing from it except a little back relief.
### LARGE +
The series doubles as a short history lesson on how humans learned phlogiston, told from Chikya's partisan viewpoint, and as a small character study of someone who knows everything about a power she cannot use.

## plot
@version: 5.0
### STANDARD
Tracer No Tracing. The quest begins in the Underground Lava River beneath the Ameyalco Waters Statue of the Seven, either by talking to Chikya or by defeating a Secret Source Automaton: Hunter-Seeker at the end of a hallway. Chikya's back seizes up as she meets the party, and she introduces herself with a joke about giving a fake name. She says she hunts treasure: an "elixir that can cure all injuries" described in a secret scroll, which she offers to split. She explains Phlogiston Engraving, a language of patterns that imitates how dragons controlled phlogiston, and says analysing one door would take her a day. The Traveler reads it directly with phlogiston and learns that an Unveiling Script is needed; the party finds one (a Koholasaurus can retrieve it) and the door half-opens. Deeper in, past a Spiritdoor and Spiritway among lava, they find a clay ring with phlogiston patterning that opens the way to the Upper Sanctum. This door needs three Unveiling Scripts, one of which is already lit, and the party gathers the rest. Chikya waits because of her back, and a Fluid Avatar of Lava guards one of them. They ride the Spiritway to the surface.

Seeker No Finding. Chikya admits she left her tribe without telling friends or family. At the Upper Sanctum, floating islands held up by phlogiston, she asks the Traveler to activate mechanisms while she consults her memory of the scrolls. Touching Iridescent Inscriptions makes them vanish, each with a short message. After a Spiritway trial, a previously hidden island appears at the centre. At a large stone tablet only the Traveler and Little One notice faint words and a golden pattern. Reaching out to it triggers a fight with Avatars of Lava. Afterwards the water in the spring is full of phlogiston and warm.
### LARGE +
Chikya's scroll-reading gets partial credit. She correctly predicts the Spiritdoor and the existence of the trial, but doubts her memory when the mechanisms do not match her expectations, until the hidden central island shows she remembered correctly. In the first quest she is easily distracted by banter and quizzes the party, including on the name of the sage who stole the "flaming seed" of phlogiston. The ride back on the Spiritway is her first, and she suggests the Pyro Archon should install them everywhere.
### ULTRA +
Practical details. An optional note on a crate near the start records someone's failed attempts at the same cure: grinding Embercore Flower cores, applying powdered Iktomisaur claw-shell, bathing in the People of the Springs' hot springs ten times, and finally the trial of Xbalanque and the Sage of the Stolen Flame. It is not identified as Chikya's, though it suggests she is one of several seekers. The first quest awards the achievement "Like Floating Silk..." and the second gives "Hidden... Ahead" and "Not All Treasure Is Silver and Gold".

## revelations
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
- The Sage's gift at the Upper Sanctum is a hot spring that fills with phlogiston, which grants a chosen hero the ability to wield phlogiston; it is not a medical cure-all.
- When Paimon accuses her of knowing this, Chikya does not deny it: she says no single elixir could heal every injury and admits she tricked Paimon, framing it as a lesson.
- Chikya cannot use phlogiston at all, which is rare in Natlan. She blames a grave Abyss-related injury from early childhood.
### LARGE +
- Per Chikya, tribespeople who could not already use phlogiston only ever learned by passing the trial, and the spring did nothing for her.
- The Iridescent Inscriptions at the Upper Sanctum are both the key and the trial: touching them reveals the hidden central island.
- Chikya says the Phlogiston Chiselith found across Natlan were modelled on the mechanisms of the Upper Sanctum.

## characters
### STANDARD
Chikya is the main non-player character: a talkative woman of the Masters of the Night-Wind who looks after old woven scrolls and teaches children about phlogiston, with chronic back pain and a habit of awarding or deducting points for the Traveler's answers. Little One, the Tepetlisaurus companion, reacts and sometimes seems to sense where a Script is. Paimon asks the questions and gets teased. The Traveler is the one who reads and activates phlogiston.
### LARGE +
Chikya is jokingly proud of her expertise, wants friends, and feels regret over not being able to use phlogiston. She dislikes being called "Aunt" and prefers "Big Sis". She mentions a friend who drifted away after a joke about swimming in lava. Her age and full background are not given.

## consequences
### STANDARD
Chikya stays to rest in the spring and says that her back hurts less than after the People of the Springs' baths, though her real goal is still far off. The first quest rewards 250 Adventure EXP, 30 Primogems, 20,000 Mora, 2 Hero's Wit and 3 Mystic Enhancement Ore; the second gives 300 Adventure EXP, 40 Primogems, 30,000 Mora, 2 Hero's Wit, 3 Mystic Enhancement Ore and an Iridescent Inscription Fragment. Both grant 200 reputation with the People of the Springs.
### LARGE +
No later quest in the sources continues Chikya's story. She remains optimistic and plans to go back to poring over scrolls for a cure and to face a scolding for leaving home.

## placement
### STANDARD
Released in Version 5.0 as a Toyac Springs world quest series, starting at the Underground Lava River beneath Ameyalco Waters. Seeker No Finding follows Tracer No Tracing directly. No prerequisites are listed for either.

## entry: Chikya
@keys: Chikya
@keys2: Toyac Springs, Upper Sanctum, Little One
@version: 5.0
### STANDARD
Chikya is a Masters of the Night-Wind woman who tidies and studies ancient woven scrolls and calls herself a Phlogiston Usage Tutor. She cannot use phlogiston because of a severe childhood injury linked to the Abyss, which fuels both her scholarship and her regret. She is chatty, self-mocking and optimistic, wants to be called "Big Sis", and left her tribe without telling anyone to go on this hunt.

## entry: Phlogiston Engraving and Unveiling Scripts
@keys: Phlogiston Engraving, Unveiling Script, Spiritdoor
@version: 5.0
### STANDARD
In Chikya's account, Phlogiston Engraving is a language of patterns humans developed by imitating how dragons controlled phlogiston, after the Sage of the Stolen Flame stole its seed. Doors with engravings open when the required Unveiling Scripts, themselves engravings, are integrated. Scholars like Chikya can read them by comparing patterns over about a day; someone able to use phlogiston can read the meaning directly.

## entry: The Upper Sanctum trial
@keys: Upper Sanctum, Hanan Pacha, Sage's gift
@keys2: Chikya, Iridescent Inscription
@version: 5.0
### STANDARD
Called Hanan Pacha in the Natlan tongue, the Upper Sanctum is a group of floating islands held up by phlogiston and described by Chikya as a pure land of peace made by the first human to use phlogiston. The trial involves activating mechanisms and Iridescent Inscriptions with phlogiston, riding Spiritways and revealing a hidden central island, where a battle against Avatars of Lava closes the trial.
