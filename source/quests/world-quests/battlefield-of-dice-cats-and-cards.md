## summary
@version: 3.3
### LITE
Battlefield of Dice, Cats, and Cards is a Mondstadt world quest series, added in Version 3.3, that introduces the player to the card game Genius Invokation TCG. It has a single quest, Come Try Genius Invokation TCG!, set mainly at The Cat's Tail tavern. The Traveler, Paimon and the alchemist Sucrose learn the rules from Diona, win duels against three opponents and become officially recognised players.
### STANDARD
The quest begins in Mondstadt's alchemy shop, where Sucrose has received a package of strange cards from a Sumeru scholar. Timaeus explains that they belong to a popular game and sends the group to The Cat's Tail. There Margaret's staff, the cat Prince and his translator Shuyun, arrange a tutorial with Diona and a three-duel test. The tone is comic and light, and it works as an in-world welcome to the game.
### LARGE +
It requires Adventure Rank 30 and the completion of the Prologue's Act III, Song of the Dragon and Freedom. Its last scene leads straight into the next quest, A Guest From Liyue, which begins in the same place.

## plot
@version: 3.3
### STANDARD
Timaeus and Sucrose are discussing a package Sucrose received, probably a thank-you gift from a Sumeru scholar who had studied in Mondstadt. It contains only cards, among them one showing Sucrose herself. When the Traveler and Paimon join, Timaeus explains that these are cards for Genius Invokation TCG, a game said to have been invented by an Akademiya scholar and now popular everywhere. He admits he has been reading a light-novel series based on it, and he has promised himself not to touch the game until his research is further along, so he recommends The Cat's Tail, where players gather to play and trade.

At the tavern Margaret mentions that more TCG players now come in and that she has put Prince, her cat, and Shuyun in charge of looking after them. Shuyun claims to understand Prince's meows and passes on his remarks, including lofty refusals to play himself. Margaret hands the teaching to Diona, who grumbles that the game encourages drinking yet agrees once Shuyun argues that it might actually help her campaign against Mondstadt's wine trade. Sucrose, Paimon and the Traveler have no proper deck, so Diona lends them a pair of character cards a customer left behind. She walks them through a first game, in which the player controls Diluc, covering dice, skills, elemental reactions and winning by defeating all of the opponent's characters. Sucrose and the Traveler then play a practice duel against each other, and Sucrose loses and takes it a little hard.

Shuyun explains that becoming an official player recognised by the Genius Invokation TCG Society requires beating three opponents; the practice duel counts toward the tally. Everyone starting out gets a free Casket of Tomes, which Margaret hands over. The Casket can sense other nearby Caskets, which leads the Traveler to a Knight of Favonius named Swan on duty at the city entrance. Swan agrees to duel and loses. Prince, via Shuyun, chooses the final opponent: Fischl, who is also a beginner with two wins. The Traveler beats her. Fischl gives the Traveler her own Character Card as a prize, and Sucrose, who has won two games herself, joins Fischl for a game. Shuyun then explains what official players get: a TCG Player's Handbook, access to Genius Invokation merchandise at The Cat's Tail and the Invitation Board for challenging friends.
### LARGE +
Details. Timaeus summarises the light novel (published by Yae Publishing House in Inazuma) in which a young man in Sumeru finds a Casket of Tomes in his attic, releasing the soul of an ancient player called the Crocodile King, who once served King Deshret and was sealed away after losing to the Ibis King. Sucrose also remarks that Timaeus once asked for a deadline extension for "personal reasons". Margaret refers to Diona as "Little Diona". Sucrose mentions in passing that Margaret had earlier refused to sell her fur from The Cat's Tail staff for an experiment; the quest does not explain further. Shuyun says that all card cases in the game are called Caskets of Tomes, and Paimon's guess that each holds an ancient player's soul is a joke; the Caskets work through a mechanism invented by Sumeru scholars.
### ULTRA +
Other players in the tavern react to the Traveler before they are officially recognised: Gary, Belen, Makri, Nanaya Yuumi and Atra each say something about the game, for instance that Gary and Belen will duel the Traveler once the Traveler becomes an official player. Swan only duels because he calls himself a fellow TCG player, even though he is on guard duty. Fischl, speaking in her usual grand manner, addresses herself as Prinzessin and her bird Oz as her familiar, and Oz translates her bluster into plain meaning. After losing she blames the "cursed dragon" for hiding the threads of fate from her vision.

## characters
@version: 3.3
### STANDARD
The Traveler and Paimon; Sucrose, an alchemist who gets involved through the package; Timaeus, her colleague, who introduces the game; Margaret, owner of The Cat's Tail; Diona, a bartender at the tavern who teaches the tutorial; Prince and Shuyun, the tavern's cat and his translator who run the TCG corner; Swan, a Knight of Favonius and opponent; and Fischl with her companion Oz, the final opponent. Diluc appears only as a character card.
### LARGE +
Diona is the one who teaches the rules. She wants to "destroy Mondstadt's wine industry" because she dislikes drinking, which the quest plays for humour. Fischl is also learning the game, having won two duels before facing the Traveler.

## consequences
@version: 3.3
### STANDARD
The Traveler becomes an official TCG player: the TCG mode opens, and the Traveler receives the Casket of Tomes, a tutorial deck and Fischl's Character Card. Quest rewards are 60 Primogems, 2 Hero's Wit, 20,000 Mora, the Casket of Tomes and a Beginner Player Badge. Completing it also grants the achievement Visible, Yet Invisible.
### LARGE +
Rewards and achievements are gameplay content, not in-world fact.

## placement
@version: 3.3
### STANDARD
Released in Version 3.3 together with the TCG's introduction. It requires Adventure Rank 30 and the Prologue's Act III, Song of the Dragon and Freedom. It is a self-contained comic quest and not part of the Archon Quests; the next quest, A Guest From Liyue, starts immediately after.

## entry: Genius Invokation TCG tutorial at The Cat's Tail
@keys: Come Try Genius Invokation TCG, Casket of Tomes, TCG Society
@keys2: Cat's Tail, Prince, Shuyun, Diona, tutorial
@version: 3.3
### STANDARD
In Mondstadt, Genius Invokation TCG is taught and played at The Cat's Tail, where Margaret keeps a corner for players. To be recognised by the Genius Invokation TCG Society a newcomer must win duels against three opponents; each new player receives a Casket of Tomes, which senses other nearby Caskets and so helps players find each other. The tavern staff Prince the cat and Shuyun, his translator, oversee new players.
