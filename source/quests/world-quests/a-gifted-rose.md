## summary
@spoiler: none
### LITE
A Gifted Rose is a five-part world quest series in the Great Red Sand, Sumeru, added in version 3.1. On the edge of Aaru Village, a lonely old man named Affan is trying to grow three stone-like seeds left to him by a dead neighbour, an exile the villagers call the "old ghost". With the Amurta researcher Sabbah, the Traveler and Paimon get the seeds to sprout and bloom as Golden Roses.
### STANDARD +
The series is a quiet, small-scale story about exile, being forgotten, and keeping a dead person's wish alive. It requires The Exile (World Quest Series) to have been completed. It has five quests, plus one follow-up Hidden Exploration Objective, and it runs over several in-game days because the plants need real-time days to grow.
### LARGE +
The old man's garden belongs to Shafaqat, a former Akademiya researcher who was exiled to Aaru Village and died there. The story ties him to Sabbah, who is also an exile, so her arc from The Exile continues here.

## plot
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
After The Exile, the Traveler talks to Sabbah in Aaru Village and is pointed to Affan, an old man who lives at the edge of the village and is known for growing "rocks". He first assumes the visitors have come to mock him, then apologises and asks for help clearing a second nursery west of the village that has been swallowed by the Withering. The Traveler cleans it up and Affan shows three odd, stony seeds he plans to plant, which he says are a late friend's wish.

Over the next days the Traveler waters the seeds with Hydro, and Affan explains that he is growing Golden Roses from seeds and notes left by a man the village children call the "old ghost". The seeds do not sprout. Affan, discouraged, mentions the dead man's notes; the Traveler suggests asking Sabbah. Sabbah examines the seeds and concludes they are wrapped in a stone-like shell, possibly because the species strongly gathers Geo. Using a line from the village's rose folk song, "blooming from the stone", she has the Traveler break the shells with blunt hits and then apply Dendro. The seeds sprout, and in the last quest the Traveler uses Dendro again so the Golden Roses bloom. Sabbah decides to help Affan tend the roses from then on.
### LARGE
**Prickly as Thorns.** Sabbah has mentioned a flower grower in the village. Affan is brusque, believing children come to mock him, then apologises. He originally had two nurseries; the western one was ruined by black corruption, which the Traveler recognises as the Withering and destroys. Affan thanks them with food (shawarma wrap, tahchin and beans) rather than much Mora, which worries Paimon, who wonders whether he gave away his own rations. Affan will say only that he is planting "a friend's wish".

**Long Day Ahead.** The seeds have not changed. Paimon suggests the soil is too dry and poor in nutrients, so the Traveler waters them. Affan reveals he is growing Golden Roses, which he calls much rarer than the Sumeru Rose, from seeds and a house left behind by "the old ghost". He says village parents use that name to frighten naughty children. As a child he was chased off with a hoe from the old man's garden, and he now wants to recreate that garden from memory. He also tells the Traveler about his own life: he never left the village, once nearly joined the Eremites as a newlywed, and eventually moved to the village edge where he felt more at ease.

**Can Stones Bloom.** Still nothing grows, and Affan fears he has been planting stones. After another watering he apologises aloud to the dead man. Affan then gives the dead man's name: Shafaqat, "Uncle Shafaqat". He had left Sumeru City young, built the house and garden, and grew trees and flowers that children loved to visit, until he turned reclusive. The notes Affan has gathered describe Shafaqat's exile from the Akademiya, the loss of his labs and books, estrangement from his daughter, and his plan to scatter Golden Rose seeds across the desert before dying. The Traveler proposes asking Sabbah, whom Affan had once driven away.

**Ballad of Days Gone By.** Sabbah and Affan meet at the nursery. She finds that the seeds are petrified. She weighs two explanations, long weathering (unlikely) or a Geo-rich breed forming its own stone shell, and points to the folk song that Shafaqat composed: "Blooming from the stone, the Golden Rose sang to me." The Traveler breaks the casings with blunt attacks and awakens the seeds with Dendro. When shoots appear, Sabbah says that Shafaqat's wish can now be fulfilled. She and Affan speak about being exiles, and Affan tells her he does not want to see a second "old ghost".

**Some People Never Fade Away.** On the day of the bloom Sabbah has already been visiting and reading Shafaqat's notes. The Traveler awakens the flowers with Dendro. As the roses bloom, Affan recites the last stanza of the rose song and says he has fulfilled the dead man's wish. Sabbah says Shafaqat has surpassed time through the roses and his folk song, that seeing his traces makes her feel she has crossed time, and that she can carry his regrets and live on for him. Affan and Sabbah agree to plant more Golden Roses together.

### ULTRA +
A follow-up Hidden Exploration Objective (its name is unofficial on the wiki) lets the Traveler collect Golden Rose Seeds found elsewhere, plant them in the nursery and awaken them with Dendro over two days. Affan wonders where the seeds came from and concludes that the seeds Shafaqat once scattered over the desert somehow found their way back. This is a wiki-recorded optional objective.

Gameplay note, labelled as such: the quest asks the player to apply Hydro, then break stone casings with blunt attacks and apply Dendro. The text does not change the lore.

## revelations
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
- "The old ghost" of Aaru Village was an exiled researcher named Shafaqat (Affan's elders say this was his name). He grew Golden Roses and wrote the village's rose folk song.
- Affan has been caring for Shafaqat's house and nursery, and sorting the notes he left behind, in order to carry out his wish that Golden Roses grow widely in the desert.
- The Golden Rose seeds are wrapped in a stone-like casing (Sabbah's analysis) and must be cracked open before Dendro can wake them.
### LARGE +
- Shafaqat's notes say he once worked at the Akademiya, was exiled, and that his work was discredited and his name became a family taboo. He mentions a daughter he never saw again.
- Sabbah, herself exiled, sees his fate as what may await her. By the end of the series she decides to stay in Aaru and help Affan.

## characters
### STANDARD
The Traveler, Paimon, Affan, Sabbah (an exiled Amurta researcher), and Shafaqat, who appears only through Affan's memories and his notes. Nahida's All Schemes to Know can be used on Affan and Sabbah to read their thoughts.

## consequences
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Golden Roses bloom in Affan's nursery. Affan and Sabbah plan to grow more of them together. Affan has come to terms with the dead man's memory, and Sabbah, who had felt lonely in Aaru Village, says she feels less alone.
### LARGE +
The village rose song, composed by Shafaqat and handed down by the older generation, is revived through the quest. Affan notes that some in his generation no longer know it.

## placement
### STANDARD
Requires The Exile (World Quest Series), which comes just before it in the Great Red Sand. Released in 3.1. The five quests can only be done on successive days because the plants need time to grow.

## entry: Affan
@keys: Affan
@keys2: Aaru Village, Golden Rose, Shafaqat, Sabbah
@version: 3.1
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Affan is an open-world NPC, an old man who lives alone at the edge of Aaru Village in a house left to him by the "old ghost". He is gruff at first, then warm, and tends a nursery where he tries to grow Golden Roses from seeds in his dead friend's notes. He mentions a late wife, a youthful wish to travel that never happened, and that he feels he belongs neither to the village nor anywhere else. The wiki has no profile text for him beyond his quests and dialogue.

## entry: Shafaqat, the old ghost of Aaru Village
@keys: Shafaqat, Uncle Shafaqat, old ghost
@keys2: Aaru Village, Golden Rose, Affan
@version: 3.1
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Shafaqat is known to the village as "the old ghost" and, to those who remember, "Uncle Shafaqat". According to Affan's elders he left Sumeru City young, built his house in Aaru Village and rarely left it for decades. His notes say he was exiled from the Akademiya, that his work was discredited, that he missed his daughter, and that he wanted Golden Roses to grow across the desert. He is dead by the time of the quest.
### LARGE +
Children liked him when he was younger and asked for Ajilenakh Nuts and his stories, but by the time Affan met him he had become unfriendly toward children. His rose folk song says that the Golden Rose "sang to me" from the stone and that songs will surpass the singer and time.

## entry: Golden Rose
@keys: Golden Rose, Golden Roses
@keys2: Aaru Village, Affan, Sabbah, Shafaqat
@version: 3.1
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
In this series the Golden Rose is a rose breed that Shafaqat cultivated in Aaru Village. Its seeds are encased in a hard stone-like shell, which Sabbah thinks results from a strong affinity for Geo. Affan calls it more precious than the Sumeru Rose. The seeds can be kept dry for a very long time, according to the dead man's notes. Dendro awakens them after the shell is broken.

## entry: Sabbah in Aaru Village
@keys: Sabbah
@keys2: Aaru Village, Amurta, exile, Golden Rose
@version: 3.1
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Sabbah is an Amurta researcher living in exile in Aaru Village (first met in The Exile). In this series she examines Affan's seeds, finds the way to wake them, and says she identifies with Shafaqat because both were exiled. She chooses to stay and help Affan tend the roses and says she is less lonely as a result.
