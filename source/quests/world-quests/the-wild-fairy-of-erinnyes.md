## summary
@spoiler: none
### LITE
The Wild Fairy of Erinnyes is a world quest series in Erinnyes Forest, Fontaine, added in version 4.2. The Traveler and Paimon meet Pahsiv, a Melusine who speaks only a few words at a time and lives alone in the forest. She needs help to collect three Teardrop Keys and protect "the root" of Erinnyes from a "purple thing".
### STANDARD +
The series has three quests that can be done in any order, "Foggy Forest Path", "Wilting Weeping Willow" and "Furious Mouth of the Spring", each of which yields a Teardrop Key, and a finale, "Within the Depths of Erinnyes". The finale restores the dying great willow. Furious Mouth of the Spring requires The Outlander Who Caught the Wind.
### LARGE +
The tone is gentle and puzzling. Pahsiv's speech is built from a handful of words that Paimon slowly learns to read, and the mystery of who she is and who the "visitor" is never gets answered.

## plot
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Marcotte Station, the Weeping Willow of the Lake and a sleeping vishap by a spring each hide an altar. At each the Traveler finds a glittering drop of water, a Teardrop Key (the finale calls them Waterdrop Keys), and each time a small Melusine appears. Paimon names her Pahsiv after the one word she keeps repeating. Pahsiv says that Erinnyes is her home, that she wants to protect it, and that three keys must be taken to a square to the west. A "purple thing" is sneaking toward the root of Erinnyes, while a "blue thing", a vishap, was her ally.

With all three keys the Traveler opens a door under the fountain at Marcotte Station and follows a flooded passage to the Roots of Erinnyes, which are choked with filth. Pahsiv gives the Traveler a burst of strength, and the Traveler destroys the tumour-like growth feeding on the roots and fights off Rifthounds. A vishap joins the final wave. Pahsiv disappears and water floods the cave. On the surface the great willow has recovered, and Pahsiv reappears with a treasure chest.
### LARGE
**Foggy Forest Path.** Someone has scratched out most of the inscriptions on the stone tablets at Marcotte Station; one fragment survives about "clear dew" singing on the Foggy Forest Path. The Traveler follows the path, solves three Harmonious Reed Pipe puzzles and examines the altar. A drop of water appears on the altar.

**Wilting Weeping Willow.** At the Weeping Willow of the Lake, a huge tree that looks like a wilting waterfall, the Traveler uses local creatures to clear polluted mats and solves a Harmonious Reed Pipe puzzle to activate an altar whose inscription is mostly scratched out except for the word "foam". The altar yields a key. Paimon's guess that the drop of water might be a way to contain the Demon King is a joke, not lore.

**Furious Mouth of the Spring.** A vishap lies by a spring altar and wakes enraged. Pahsiv stops it by stroking its head, and it coughs up a key. Pahsiv calls the vishap the "blue thing" and says it was guarding the key and was knocked out when the "purple thing" stole it.

**Meeting Pahsiv.** The first conversation is Paimon decoding words: "Pahsiv" turns out to mean yes and "Melusine" no. Pahsiv likes the name Paimon gives her. If the Traveler asks about rewards, she does not grasp the word, and she does not seek payment. She says she is not from the Court of Fontaine or Merusea Village but from Erinnyes. Paimon asks who the visitor is and learns it is named "that visitor", who teaches Pahsiv language and wisdom and works to protect Fontaine.

**The finale.** Pahsiv tells Paimon that Erinnyes works like an information system: willows collect, fog handles, springs send, and the root is the centre of the water veins. The visitor senses evil through them. The purple thing is trying to destroy the root, and Pahsiv scratched the tablets to hide the way. It found another route. After the Traveler enters the passage Pahsiv says the blue thing fought the purple thing and was badly hurt.
### ULTRA +
**Pahsiv's help.** Pahsiv touches the Traveler's stomach with her palm and the Traveler feels warmth and enhancement. She is tired afterwards. When Rifthounds charge from a glowing formation she runs off; a vishap joins to help. After the water carries the Traveler out, Pahsiv returns, says she went for a box, and offers a chest. She learns "thank you". Paimon wants to return to teach her more words and notes that she acts more like a wild fairy.

**Aftermath in the world (labelled as gameplay).** The willow leaves in the area turn bright blue, young Primordial Bathysmal Vishap hatchlings appear along the river, and the local legend "Mageblade Corrouge" appears where the tumour was. The achievement "Semnai Sans Shadow" is for fully clearing the finale area.

## revelations
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
- A Melusine called Pahsiv lives in Erinnyes Forest and guards it. Her home is the forest, not a Melusine settlement.
- The altars at Marcotte Station, the Weeping Willow of the Lake and the spring each hold a Teardrop Key and are linked to a door under a fountain that leads to the Roots of Erinnyes.
- Erinnyes Forest is described by Pahsiv as an information system run through its water veins, which an unnamed "visitor" uses to sense danger.
### LARGE +
- A "purple thing" corrupted the roots; a vishap that tried to stop it was injured.
- Someone scratched the stone inscriptions at Marcotte Station. Pahsiv says she did it, to hide the way to the root from the purple thing.

## characters
### STANDARD
The Traveler and Paimon; Pahsiv, the Melusine who lives in Erinnyes; the vishap of the spring, which Pahsiv calls the "blue thing"; and two bystanders at Marcotte Station, Keratry and Remaudiere, who appear in the finale. Keratry sees the hole in the fountain and credits his brother's magic, and Remaudiere goes along with it. The "visitor" and the "purple thing" are mentioned, never shown.
### LARGE +
Pahsiv speaks in short fragments and uses her own name for yes. She never asks for reward but accepts a chest she finds. Paimon is delighted by her and promises to teach her more words.

## consequences
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
The root of Erinnyes is cleansed, the great willow recovers, and Pahsiv leaves. The Traveler and Paimon say they will visit and teach her more words.
### LARGE +
The threat behind the purple thing is not removed. These quests resolve only the damage to the root.

## placement
### STANDARD
Released in version 4.2. The three key quests may be done in any order; "Within the Depths of Erinnyes" unlocks after the Traveler holds all three keys. Furious Mouth of the Spring requires The Outlander Who Caught the Wind.

## entry: Pahsiv
@keys: Pahsiv
@keys2: Erinnyes, Melusine, Teardrop Key, Foggy Forest Path
@version: 4.2
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Pahsiv is the name Paimon gives to a Melusine met in Erinnyes Forest. She speaks in a few words at a time, does not understand rewards, and treats the forest as her home and duty. She calms a furious vishap with a touch, helps the Traveler fight the corruption at the roots and then slips away. Her origin and her relationship to the "visitor" are not explained.
### LARGE +
She uses "Pahsiv" for yes and "Melusine" for no, and calls the vishap the "blue thing" and the force behind the corruption the "purple thing". She did not know about the Court of Fontaine or Merusea Village as places where Melusine live.
