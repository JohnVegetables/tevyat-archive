## summary
### LITE
Film Notes is a two-quest Inazuma world series (2.8). The Fontainian filmmaker Xavier is making another film with help from the Yashiro Commission, but his supporting actors Sasano and the Kamisato servant Koharu quit over his confusing script, adapted from a novel by the unsuccessful writer Kayabuki Ikkei. After a story told by Okazaki Rikuto, Xavier rewrites it as "Mushounin", based on a legend of Seishimaru, and the film is shot with the Traveler in the lead.
### STANDARD
In "Outlander Brigade!" the Traveler finds Xavier midway through a troubled shoot at the Kamisato Estate's request, is offered the lead role, and plays two scenes of "When the Wind of Death Blew Towards the Blond Samurai" before the supporting actors walk out. Over a meal at Uyuu Restaurant, Rikuto's story of Seishimaru helps Xavier see what is wrong. In Mushounin, Koharu and Sasano are brought back and three new scenes are filmed at Komore Teahouse, Nazuchi Beach and a cliff on a small island east of Narukami Island.
### LARGE +
The series is a light comedy about creative frustration and adds no wider Inazuma lore. The Traveler's real combat is used for the action scenes, and a Film Kamera recording area limits where they can stand during the shoots.
### ULTRA +
The quests are spread over several in-game evenings, since Xavier plans each location for the light. Koharu's remark that Keisuke lets Seishimaru take revenge in order to cool his temper leads Xavier to admit the story needs another possibility, which he says he may adjust.

## plot
@version: 2.8
### STANDARD
Opening. Furuta, the Kamisato Estate's butler, tells the Traveler that Xavier, a young Fontainian, had received money from the Tenryou Commission for a film that the commission was not satisfied with, and then came to the Yashiro Commission, the body responsible for arts and the humanities, for help. Ayato agreed and sent Koharu, whom Furuta worries is not fond of Xavier.

The first shoot. Xavier reminds the Traveler of an earlier agreement that they would play the lead, and gives scripts for Act I, Scenes II and III. Sasano and Koharu are already weary after eleven takes. The script is Xavier's adaptation of a novel by Kayabuki Ikkei; Sasano recalls that Ikkei's manuscripts were always rejected by Yae Publishing House. In the story the two supporting characters sacrifice themselves so the lead can go on, which Sasano and Koharu find senseless. Xavier criticises stiff delivery and forgotten lines, and both actors quit.

Rikuto's advice. Xavier retreats to Uyuu Restaurant. Okazaki Rikuto, the owner, overhears the premise and remarks that a hero who charges into unwinnable fights with no hesitation seems hardly human. He tells a legend: Seishimaru's samurai parents were executed after an apparent error at work, and Seishimaru sought revenge on the Wakayama family who carried out the sentence, whose head was a childhood friend. Xavier decides to write a new script and asks to meet at Komore Teahouse the next day.
### LARGE +
Mushounin. Koharu, apologetic after being reprimanded by Ayato for walking out, asks the Traveler to speak for her, and Sasano returns too. The new script, based on the Seishimaru legend, adds an original character, Yano Machiko. The first scene is shot in a private room at Komore Teahouse that Thoma helped Xavier borrow. The Traveler plays Seishimaru against Sasano as Wakayama Keisuke, and Koharu plays Machiko. In the scene the Traveler must follow the marked lines; going off-script makes Xavier call another take.

The second scene, at Nazuchi Beach in the evening, uses a gang of toughs (Yamada Hifumi, Yamamoto and Tanaka) as extras after the Traveler persuades them by fighting; Xavier later says he gave their characters their real names with their permission. The final scene, at sunset on a cliff, has Sasano play Wakayama Keisuke's younger brother Kojuurou, who confronts Seishimaru. The Traveler is asked to glide down the cliff on a wind glider rather than jump.
### ULTRA +
In the film's third act, Machiko tells Seishimaru that Keisuke is gone and urges him to let it go. Kojuurou says that Keisuke worked all year to settle the clan's affairs and calls Seishimaru's revenge on the executioner alone a coward's choice. Sasano warns the Traveler to pull punches in the fight scene because he is not a fighter.

## revelations
@spoiler: none
### STANDARD
- Xavier's first script here is an adaptation of a novel by Kayabuki Ikkei, whom Sasano knows only as an author Yae Publishing House kept rejecting.
- Rikuto uses the Seishimaru legend to show a man whose revenge is aimed at someone who only followed orders, which pushes Xavier to rework the film.

## characters
### STANDARD
Xavier (a Fontainian aspiring director), Koharu (a Kamisato household servant), Sasano (supporting actor), Furuta (Kamisato butler), Okazaki Rikuto (Uyuu Restaurant), Yamada Hifumi, Yamamoto and Tanaka (toughs turned extras in Mushounin), Paimon and the Traveler. Kamisato Ayato, Thoma and Kayabuki Ikkei are only mentioned.

## consequences
### STANDARD
Both quests give Adventure EXP, Primogems, Mora and Hero's Wit; Mushounin also gives Mystic Enhancement Ore. The film is completed. Co-Op mode is locked during the shoots, and the Serenitea Pot is unavailable for part of the first quest.

## placement
### STANDARD
Released in 2.8. It requires Omnipresence Over Mortals (the Inazuma Archon Quest) and "The Seventh Samurai", the earlier world quest featuring Xavier. It has no bearing on later Archon Quests.

## entry: Seishimaru legend
@keys: Seishimaru, Kurotani Seishimaru, Wakayama Keisuke, Wakayama Kojuurou
@version: 2.8
### STANDARD
In-universe, Rikuto tells Xavier an Inazuman legend of Seishimaru, whose samurai parents were executed, and who sets out to take revenge on the Wakayama family that carried out the sentence. Xavier's "Mushounin" script retells it with film characters, including Wakayama Keisuke, who lets Seishimaru choose by a blind point between a sword and a temari ball whether they will fight or sever their bonds.
### LARGE +
The film's third act has Keisuke's younger brother Kojuurou accuse Seishimaru of cowardice for blaming only the executioner.

## entry: Xavier
@keys: Xavier
@keys2: film, Fontaine, Yashiro Commission, Seventh Samurai
@version: 2.8
### STANDARD
Xavier is a theatrical Fontainian filmmaker working in Inazuma. He calls the Traveler his dear friend and says they were the first friend he made in Inazuma. He loves art over commerce but his scripts confuse his actors. In this series he works with funding and staff granted by Kamisato Ayato.
