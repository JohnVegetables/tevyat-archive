## summary
### LITE
A Nod-Krai world quest series that begins in Version 6.0. Treasure hunters, many of them from the Snezhnayan city of Novokitezhgrad, are chasing the five Proofs of the Cognoscenti, legendary relics said to point to the Great Treasure of the thief Reed Miller. The Traveler and Paimon collect all five and find the treasure, learning in the process how the hunters' stories connect.
### STANDARD +
The series has four parts that can be done in any order: The Shoemaker's Children Go Barefoot, The Tale-Telling Heart, Whisper Beneath the Waves and Return to Sender. Once all four are complete, the final quest, The Raven's Legacy, starts automatically. Its title comes from a Norwegian fairy tale, "East of the Sun and West of the Moon" (wiki trivia).
### LARGE +
Each quest is a self-contained story in a different corner of Nod-Krai (the Kuuvahki Experimental Design Bureau on Paha Isle, Blue Amber Lake on Lempo Isle, the Eye of Kratti, and a bunker near Piramida), and each leaves the Traveler with one Proof. Several characters share a history in Novokitezhgrad that is revealed bit by bit. The final quest is the series' pay-off, revealing what the Great Treasure actually is.

## plot
### LITE
Four short adventures each yield a Proof of the Cognoscenti: a goblet won from a Fatui officer, a mask at a campfire by Blue Amber Lake, a feather from a ghost-haunted wreck, and a relic found in a doomsday bunker. Tolfin, a storyteller-merchant, leads the Traveler to his grandmother Hiljetta, who owns the fifth and tests the Traveler in a sparring match. The five Proofs open the Great Treasure site.
### STANDARD +
Each quest is a self-contained story. At the Fatui's Kuuvahki Experimental Design Bureau on Paha Isle, the Traveler and Paimon meet the thief-fan Netochka and beat the officer Raskolnikov for a Proof (Shoemaker's Children). At Blue Amber Lake, Tolfin sets a treasure hunt that exposes a family's guilty secret (Tale-Telling Heart). At the Eye of Kratti, a cave of shipwreck ghosts yields a feather (Whisper Beneath the Waves). In a doomsday bunker by Piramida the Traveler cleans up Abyssal contamination and defeats the Ultimate Calamity (Return to Sender). Finally Tolfin's grandmother Hiljetta spars with the Traveler, gives the last Proof, and the five Proofs open the Great Treasure site in Piramida, where a flashback shows the young Treasure Hoarders Relya and Silve (The Raven's Legacy).
### LARGE +
The Shoemaker's Children Go Barefoot (Luna I, 6.0). At the Fatui's Kuuvahki Experimental Design Bureau, the Traveler and Paimon sneak in and meet Netochka, a thief-fan of Reed Miller from Novokitezhgrad. She is the daughter of a police chief, and the Fatui officer Poruchik Raskolnikov, who was once her father's student, has collected a Proof. The three fight through the facility, defeat Raskolnikov, and he lets them take the Proof, saying he does not want it drawing thieves to the Bureau.

The Tale-Telling Heart (Luna I). At a campfire at Blue Amber Lake, the merchant Tolfin offers the Traveler a hunt for the Proof left by his late friend Papa Wrench, who built a set of mechanical toys for a sick child named Tinet. Two travellers, Danyusha and Zhorik Shesternin, are present; Danyusha is Papa Wrench's daughter and Zhorik is his heir (Tolfin calls them Mr. and Mrs. Shesternin, but the exact relationship between the two is not clear). They also want the Proof. The Traveler clears a trial, finds spare parts underground and wakes the giant machine Colonel Iron Whale. The Proof, a mask, goes to the Traveler.

Whisper Beneath the Waves (Luna I). At the Eye of Kratti, near the fisherman Ilmari, the Traveler enters a hidden cave beneath the sea and passes through memories of ghosts from old shipwrecks. Throwing Kuuvahki dewdrops into the tree roots makes the tree glow and brings up a chest holding a feather, the Inscripta-Cognoscenti's Feather.

Return to Sender (Luna IV, 6.3). A recording leads the Traveler into a bunker at the "end of the apocalypse" by Piramida. Trapped in Abyssal contamination, the Traveler restores the purification system, learns what happened to its staff, defeats the Ultimate Calamity in the supervisor's office, and leaves with the Devocina-Cognoscenti's Sorrow.

The Raven's Legacy (Luna IV). In Piramida, Tolfin takes the Traveler to his grandmother Hiljetta, the "Black-Barrel Hag", who spars with them and gives the last Proof, the Memorata-Cognoscenti's Jest. The Traveler offers all five Proofs to a huge Weasel Thief statue and descends into a puzzle site. Under a heap of iron coins lies an old ship; when Paimon urges the Traveler to take the helm, a flashback begins to two young Treasure Hoarders, Relya and Silve, who take a governor's treasure from Bounty Island, plan to share it with street children in Snezhnograd, and leave a raven insignia to show who took it. Back with Tolfin, the Traveler receives "The First Iron Coin" and Tolfin names the raven's owner as Avrelian, the one remembered as Reed Miller.
### ULTRA +
The Shoemaker's Children: the first of Netochka's fixations is a book, The Legends of Reed Miller, Thief Extraordinaire, and she renames the party with codenames (Goldfinch for the Traveler, Snowy Owl for Paimon). She admits she knew Raskolnikov all along and tells them a woman named Hidimbi in Nasha Town "chose" her and sold her a thief's kit, which Paimon suspects was a scam. The final fight uses the Bureau's kuuvahki hammers against Raskolnikov's machinery.

The Tale-Telling Heart: A weasel thief steals the lift lever, and Tolfin's trained weasel thief Huginn catches it and later digs up Papa Wrench's journal pages. Tolfin later privately explains that he sent a message telling the Shesternins where a Proof lay because they had fallen into debt, and wanted to see whether Zhorik had changed in ten years; he says Papa Wrench had hoped he would forgive. Zhorik takes a flashy goblet to be the Proof and tosses the mask aside; when the Traveler shows him a photo, he flees.

Whisper Beneath the Waves: the cave holds the spirits of passengers and crew from at least three ships (Atlas, Fosegrin, Fortunate One). The Traveler reads diaries about twin sisters who travelled without their father, and an investigator's notes on two murder cases that he solved, ending with a note that a Mr. Shesternin failed to meet Chief Stalanov on deck while Zhorik denied knowing where he was.

Return to Sender: recordings tell of Senior Researcher Grazina, a supervisor named Morozov whom a dying maintenance worker accuses of sending his staff to die, and Professor Rotwang's plan; Grazina records that a man she calls Brahe Reed Miller quietly led the surviving children out to people who would protect them. The Ultimate Calamity thinks the world outside the sky has ended.

The Raven's Legacy: puzzle steps use three Weasel Thief statues, notices with riddles, and a huge hoard of Strange Iron Coins.

The Shesternin storyline stays unresolved on screen: Zhorik flees into dangerous terrain and Tolfin hints that other friends of Papa Wrench may deal with him. Netochka is found at Speranza in Nasha Town after her quest and says she misses Novokitezhgrad but is staying a while. Tolfin says he will visit Colonel Iron Whale from time to time and remains in Piramida for a while.

A message signed by Reed Miller at the hoard greets whoever has gathered the Proofs, says the treasure is right in front of them, and advises the finder to take their share quietly, remove his note and pretend nothing was found, so that people keep chasing the Proofs. A second note by Brahe says he deliberately spread rumours of Proofs and invented stories of failed treasure hunters, and mentions a "Prototype Iron Coin". After the flashback the Traveler digs up an item at the flashback site, the First Iron Coin, and Tolfin links the raven insignia to the family crest of Avrelian's noble academy classmate.

## revelations
@spoiler: major
@version: 6.0
### STANDARD
The Great Treasure the Traveler finds is a vast pile of old iron coins above a buried ship, not a hoard of gold or modern Mora. Reed Miller founded the Treasure Hoarders and wrote that the hoard belongs to whoever gathers the five Proofs, but should be taken quietly.

Papa Wrench, the maker of Colonel Iron Whale, was Danyusha's father and was lost from the ship Fortunate One ten years earlier. Zhorik, his heir, is strongly implied to have pushed him overboard.
### LARGE +
Tolfin and Hiljetta are grandson and grandmother, and both are Treasure Hoarders. Hiljetta wants to restore the Treasure Hoarders to their old role defending Elysium and helping the poor, and asks the Traveler to be heir to Reed Miller's dream; Tolfin says that golden age is gone and stages a show so that the Traveler's choice is theirs.

Tolfin says the man legend calls Reed Miller was Avrelian, who in the flashback goes by Relya and fights alongside Silve; it is said that before the Treasure Hoarders existed he stole from the rich with his military-academy friends. In Return to Sender (6.3), recordings tell of a "Mr. Brahe Reed Miller" who helped hide surviving children from a research bunker and who knew an old hunter and the new Frostmoon Scion priestess.

## characters
### LITE
The Traveler and Paimon; Tolfin (merchant-storyteller and Treasure Hoarder) and his weasel thief Huginn; Hiljetta, the Black-Barrel Hag; Netochka and Poruchik Raskolnikov; Danyusha and Zhorik Shesternin; Ilmari the fisherman.
### STANDARD +
Netochka is idealistic and dramatic, the child of a police chief; Raskolnikov is a rule-bound Fatui officer who insists on following orders to the letter but yields the Proof because keeping it would only draw thieves to the Bureau. Tolfin tells stories as a form of manipulation and kindness. Hiljetta is a gruff old fighter who rejects Tolfin's lightness. In the bunker, Grazina, Morozov and Rotwang appear only in recordings; the Ultimate Calamity is a corrupted monster that still follows a dead master.

## consequences
### STANDARD
Each quest yields a Proof; the final quest ends the Great Treasure hunt. Raskolnikov stops searching for Proofs, Netochka stays in Nasha Town, and the Shesternin matter is left partly unresolved. Some quests unlock extra map content: the Viewpoint "Iron Whale's Dream" and Raskolnikov as a Local Legend.
### LARGE +
The series gives backstory for the Treasure Hoarders, who today are scattered across Teyvat, and shows the bunker's link to Professor Rotwang's plans. Nothing else in the world changes.

## placement
### STANDARD
Released as World Quests across 6.0 (Luna I, 2025-09-10) for the first three quests and 6.3 (Luna IV, 2026-01-14) for Return to Sender and The Raven's Legacy. They sit alongside the Song of the Welkin Moon Archon Quest chain but are not part of it; players typically complete them whenever they reach Nod-Krai.
