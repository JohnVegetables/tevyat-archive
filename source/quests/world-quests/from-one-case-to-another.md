## summary
@spoiler: none
### LITE
From One Case to Another is a four-part Natlan world quest series added in version 5.2. At Pakpaka Flying School in the Scorchwind Trial Grounds, a young student named Taruka suspects the cheerful instructor Lamizana of plotting something dangerous around an upcoming air race. The Traveler, Paimon and the Little One investigate and end up uncovering a scheme to steal and sell Saurians.
### STANDARD +
The tone is comic. Almost everyone misreads everyone else: Taruka's suspicions are half right, the real villain is a travelling merchant, and the Traveler is briefly accused of being an accomplice. The quest uses the Saurian Indwelling feature for some stealth and tracking.
### LARGE +
The story gives a view of daily life among the Flower-Feather Clan: flying lessons, training courses, races, and the closeness and friction between young riders and their Saurian partners.

## plot
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
**Incidents Are Ever Sudden.** Passing through a Recon Ring above the Scorchwind Trial Grounds brings the party to Pakpaka Flying School, where Temach introduces a training course with prizes. After the party passes it, the flashy instructor Lamizana gives out rewards, bickering with Taruka. Taruka asks the Traveler to investigate: he overheard Lamizana discussing something dangerous with an outsider, with phrases about being on time on race day and meeting at the north camp the next night.

**Beat the Clock: 24 Hours.** The party waits until the next evening, then uses an Iktomisaurus to sneak into the camp. They overhear two voices arranging a delivery of "goods" before the air race. Quilla, a travelling merchant, spots the Little One. A box of Saurus Crackers is knocked over elsewhere in the camp, drawing the Iktomisaurs, and the party slips away. Back at the school, Paran asks the Traveler to stand in for a contestant who has dropped out of the race against Taruka's sister Lliwa.

**An Air Race and an Alibi.** The Traveler races Lliwa. Lliwa's Saurian falters mid-race, and Taruka and his Qucusaurus Oberon go missing. Iktomisaurs attack Lamizana. He then confesses to a plan: he had arranged for young Saurians to stage a fake attack on Taruka at the finish line so that he could rescue the boy and impress Lliwa. Quilla suggests the real culprit is a Saurian trafficker named Hawka, mentions a blond youth with a white pet, and the group wrongly points at the Traveler and Paimon. The party escapes and goes back to the scene.

**Farewell, Final Saurus Cracker.** Following a scent, the party finds a letter from Hawka at Quilla's camp, revealing Quilla as the mastermind. They find Oberon in a cave; after he leaves, Quilla drops her "good merchant" act and sets her Saurian Furball on them. The Little One and Oberon win in a turn-based style fight. Taruka had already escaped by himself, Lamizana apologises, and the school offers its figurative open doors to the party.
### LARGE +
Hawka's letter, found at the camp, is addressed to an unnamed recipient whom Paimon takes to be Quilla. It says the last batch of goods was good quality, some clients wanted more types of command, feed will be delivered on time, and asks the recipient to tell him if she makes progress with Paran, "that useless nephew of mine" so he can prepare a welcome. It also asks about a replacement for Soncoyoc. The Traveler learns from it that Quilla works with Hawka.

In the fight, Oberon hides Saurus Crackers under his wings to lure Furball off task. Oberon's added weight lets him block Furball's aerial escape. Quilla herself is useless in a fight and flees with Furball.
### ULTRA +
Details that matter in a scene: Taruka says his sister Lliwa is extremely competitive; Paran, who does most of the school's organising with his companion Carrewoof, comes from a family exiled from the tribe because of relatives' misdeeds. Taruka says people use that as an excuse to make things hard for him. Quilla deflects blame by describing a blond youth with a white pet, a description that happens to fit both the Traveler with Paimon and Paran with Carrewoof; Paimon points the second match out herself.

Gameplay notes (labelled as such): the stealth section lets the player pull Saurus Crackers from smudges on the walls to distract Iktomisaurs; co-op is disabled during Beat the Clock; The Punchy Saurian! and Every Contact Leaves a Trace are achievements. The Flower-Feather Clan reputation reward is 400.

## revelations
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
- Quilla, the apparently honest travelling merchant, is behind the scheme, as Paimon concludes from Hawka's letter and Quilla admits when cornered. She works with Hawka.
- Hawka, a Saurian trafficker, is Paran's uncle, who was exiled from the tribe long ago.
- Lamizana's "dangerous plan" was an ill-advised staged rescue to impress Lliwa; Quilla's group turned it into a real kidnapping.
### LARGE +
- Hawka's letter treats trained Saurians, command sets and feed as goods supplied to clients.
- Taruka was never really in danger from the staged attack; he walked out of the camp himself once he learned the guards' patrol pattern.

## characters
### STANDARD
Taruka (a young student at Pakpaka Flying School) and his Qucusaurus Oberon, his older sister Lliwa, Temach and Cisik of the flying school, the flashy Lamizana, the organiser Paran and his companion Carrewoof, the merchant Quilla and her Saurian Furball. Hawka appears only in his letter. Also the Traveler, Paimon and the Little One.

## consequences
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Taruka and Oberon are safe, Lamizana apologises and offers compensation, and Quilla flees with Furball. Taruka asks the Traveler for the secret of getting along with young Saurians and says he wants to become the best adventurer of the Flower-Feather Clan and one day enter Ochkanatlan with Oberon.
### LARGE +
Hawka and the wider trafficking network are not dealt with, so the case is only closed locally. Lliwa has not forgiven Lamizana for involving her brother, though they remain on good terms.

## placement
### STANDARD
Released in version 5.2, with no required quest. The second part starts after waiting for the next evening; An Air Race and an Alibi begins when the player approaches the Tribal Totem Pole of the Flower-Feather Clan; the last quest starts automatically.

## entry: Taruka
@keys: Taruka
@keys2: Oberon, Pakpaka Flying School, Lamizana, Lliwa
@version: 5.2
@spoiler: none
### STANDARD
Taruka is a young student at Pakpaka Flying School who is protective of his sister Lliwa and doubtful of Lamizana. He is clever and articulate, tends to see conspiracies, and strictly manages the diet of his Qucusaurus Oberon. He asks the Traveler to investigate, escapes captivity on his own, and ends the series wanting to become the clan's best adventurer.

## entry: Lamizana
@keys: Lamizana
@keys2: Pakpaka Flying School, Taruka, Lliwa, Oberon
@version: 5.2
@spoiler: none
### STANDARD
Lamizana is a boastful, charming senior figure at Pakpaka Flying School, fond of giving advice and of feeding Oberon Saurus Crackers. His plan to look like a hero in front of Lliwa goes badly wrong, and he is quick to apologise at the end, offering compensation and accepting a threatened kick from the Little One.

## entry: Quilla
@keys: Quilla
@keys2: Furball, Hawka, Saurian, Pakpaka Flying School
@version: 5.2
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Quilla poses as a well-travelled merchant who trades in many kinds of goods (Paran says she deals in a wide variety). She is in fact a villain working with Hawka. She admits it only when cornered, and is better at talking than fighting.

## entry: Paran and Hawka
@keys: Paran
@keys2: Hawka, Carrewoof, exile, Pakpaka Flying School
@version: 5.2
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Paran runs much of the practical work at Pakpaka Flying School, with Carrewoof. His family was exiled from the tribe, which makes some people treat him badly. Hawka, his uncle, is a Saurian trafficker exiled long ago, who is trying to lure Paran into his scheme.
