## summary
@version: 6.5
### LITE
A Version 6.5 world quest set in Luyang Academy, the painting gallery inside the Temple of Space. The Traveler meets Zhangsun Jijing, a Guhua Clan swordsman, and passes three small agility trials he sets.
### STANDARD +
The Traveler restores a painting on the boat with Dex and then talks to the figure who leaps about inside it. He explains that no rain falls in this realm, so he cannot practise his master's Raincutter sword arts, and trains his lightfoot technique instead. He asks the Traveler to show their skill on the Plumwatcher Piles, then through roof-leaping, then in the air.
### LARGE +
The quest is a light, humorous interlude with no plot revelations of its own. It unlocks Zhangsun as an open-world NPC at Luyang Academy and grants an achievement on completion.

## plot
@version: 6.5
### STANDARD
At Luyang Academy the Traveler restores the painting on the boat with Dex, the Temple of Space guide, and sees an ink blot on a pillar. Touching it brings out Zhangsun Jijing, a Guhua Clan sword practitioner. He is polite and formal, and asks why he was called; Paimon says they were only curious about his jumping.

He says he was practising lightfoot techniques because rain never falls in the realm, so he cannot train the Rain Cutter arts his master taught him. He recalls the Great Master Guhua, who sharpened his agility on stone piles and reached the peak of his art, and asks the Traveler for a demonstration. Paimon, jokingly addressed as the master of the Paimon Clan, is amused.

There are three trials, each with a timed Geo-particle goal. First the Traveler steps over the Plumwatcher Piles, then performs the Leaping Techniques (roof-running and wall-scaling, which Zhangsun says the ancient Guhua masters used to scale city walls), and finally the Cloud-Striding Techniques, a legendary secret art said to have let Guhua tread the air and rise through purple haze. Paimon suggests the Wind Glider as the nearest equivalent.
### LARGE +
Each trial ends with Zhangsun praising the Traveler. When the Traveler jokes that the leaping skills sound like a burglar's, Zhangsun answers that if thieves may hop walls to escape justice while those upholding it cannot, the wicked would simply slip away. After the final trial he says gliding is not truly treading on air but that it inspired him anyway, and thanks the Traveler.

The wiki lists three chests as rewards for the trials (common, exquisite and precious) and quest rewards of Adventure EXP, 30 Primogems and 35,000 Mora. These are gameplay details.
### ULTRA +
The trials escalate: seven Geo particles in 90 seconds on the Plumwatcher Piles, 38 in 90 seconds across the roofs, and 55 in 90 seconds in the air. The quest's start step is to climb a pillar and interact with the ink blot after restoring the painting. The Plumwatcher Piles, Leaping Techniques and Cloud-Striding Techniques are the three Guhua skills Zhangsun asks to see.

## revelations
@version: 6.5
### STANDARD
Little is revealed. Zhangsun says he is a disciple of the Guhua Clan, that his master passed down the Rain Cutter arts, and that his sect's ancient masters mastered roof-running, wall-scaling and, by legend, walking on air. He is hampered by the absence of rain in the Temple of Space. The Plumwatcher Piles are an exact copy of the ones in front of Wangshan Hall on Mt. Lingmeng, the Guhua Clan's base (wiki trivia).
### LARGE +
The wiki profile for Zhangsun says he is one of the Uplifted, people preserved as living memories in the Temple of Space, and was known as the Rainbearer before. It also says he had been turned into an ink blot by Melanta before the Traveler freed him. These come from the profile page rather than the quest text read here.

## characters
@version: 6.5
### STANDARD
Zhangsun Jijing: earnest, courteous and formal, speaks in a stilted martial-arts register, and treats the Traveler as a fellow practitioner. The Traveler and Paimon supply the lighthearted tone. Dex's ability to restore paintings starts the quest. Guhua is mentioned only as the revered founder figure of the clan.
### LARGE +
Zhangsun's appearance per the wiki: a burly man with dark blue hair and gray eyes, a blue shirt under a striped gray vest, dark baggy trousers, brown shoes and a large straw hat on his back. His idle line complains that the clouds never release any rain.

## consequences
@version: 6.5
### STANDARD
Completing the quest unlocks the achievement "Where Now Lies the Raincutter's Hidden Light?" and lets players find Zhangsun at Luyang Academy afterward. There is no wider effect on the story.

## placement
@version: 6.5
### STANDARD
Released in Version 6.5 (Luna VI, 2026-04-08), set in the Temple of Space at Luyang Academy. The wiki states it requires completing Long Live Life, part of the A Long Day in the Mountains chapter, which opens the academy. The quest has no place in a main story sequence.

## entry: Zhangsun Jijing
@keys: Zhangsun Jijing, Rainbearer, Zhangsun
@version: 6.5
### STANDARD
Zhangsun Jijing is a Guhua Clan disciple and open-world NPC at Luyang Academy in the Temple of Space. Known as the Rainbearer, he trained in the Raincutter sword style and cannot practise it fully because it never rains there. He asks the Traveler to demonstrate lightfoot, leaping and gliding skills in Submerged Dragon, Soaring Phoenix. The wiki describes him as one of the Uplifted preserved as living memories in the Temple of Space.

## entry: Plumwatcher Piles
@keys: Plumwatcher Piles, Plumwatcher Pile
@keys2: Guhua, Zhangsun Jijing, Luyang Academy
@version: 6.5
### STANDARD
The Plumwatcher Piles are the stone pillars on which the Guhua Clan trains lightfoot techniques. The set at Luyang Academy copies exactly the ones in front of Wangshan Hall on Mt. Lingmeng. In the quest, Zhangsun calls them the pride of Guhua, and he says Great Master Guhua once honed his agility on them.
