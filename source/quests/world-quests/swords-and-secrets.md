## summary
### LITE
Swords and Secrets is a lighthearted 7.1 world quest starring Vesna, Commander of the Druzhna. After a rare Liyue "manual" teaches her to make sword spirits, they escape and start causing trouble around Snezhnaya. The Traveler and Paimon help her hunt them down over seven days. The manual is revealed at the end to be an unfinished work of fiction from Yae Publishing House. The reward is the Druzhna Special Dispatch Order.
### STANDARD +
It unlocks after White Night, Like a Dream Upon Waking, and takes seven real-time days because one part opens each day. Completing it gives Primogems, 30,000 Mora, Hero's Wit, Guide to Glory, the cosmetic Construct Appearance Flutterwing and the Druzhna Special Dispatch Order, which makes a sword spirit fly beside Vesna when she is in her Coursing state. The tone is comedic, and the cast of sword spirits are caricatures of cultivation tropes.

## plot
### LITE
Vesna's flying sword turns out to be one of several sword spirits she accidentally created while following a book bought from a rare-book dealer. Each has "mastered" a different cultivation art, and each is caught in turn. The last, a Greater Sword Spirit with two Volkodlak followers, makes a last stand on the Everfrozen Earth, and Vesna ends up sparing its group and giving them a second chance.
### STANDARD +
Day 1: A person named Blyushko shouts that a thief has struck at Sadov-Arbat; the Traveler chases a flying blue-green blur to Vesna, who admits it is one of her creations. She had bought an ancient manuscript from Liyue about people who cultivated mind and body to command flying swords, and tried the method; the sword spirits flew off, and one stole the second half of her copy of the "Ancient Sword Esoterica". She does not want to send the Druzhna because the unit is stretched and she hopes to clean it up quietly.

Days 2-5: Vesna makes a "Divine Sense Compass", which Paimon points out has nothing to do with divine sense because she uses her own elemental energy. Spirits found: the Master of Potions, who brewed an "immortal elixir" that turns out only to be sweet; the Master of Body, tricked into staying in a cauldron until it passes out; the Master of Sigils, who paints a portrait of the one person Vesna fears, which she defaces and glues in place; and the Master of Artifacts, a machine body based on a Landcruiser which the party defeats in battle.
### LARGE +
Day 6: The compass points to the Korolevskiy Theater, where Vodyanitsa and Odette have noticed an unseen watcher. During a rehearsal Vesna finds a shy Master of Sound hiding behind the curtains. A larger Greater Sword Spirit with two Volkodlak followers, "Second of the Pack" and "Third of the Pack", appears while the Master of Sound cowers behind it, and challenges Vesna to settle matters at a proper place, on the frozen river instead of the theater.

Day 7: The Traveler, Vesna, Odette and Vodyanitsa ambush the pair on the Everfrozen Earth. Danica, who followed them, appears and tells Vesna to end the farce. The Greater Sword Spirit sends its two followers away by spell and flees, but they return, and the Master of Sound, its "Disciple Four", gives it its power. Vesna acts the villain, retrieves the Master of Sound, revives it, and reveals her reason: unchecked wishes lead to disaster, and "guiding every fae" needs more than a gang of misfits. She sentences the group to study at the Druzhna garrison and to return the stolen half of the manual, and keeps the Master of Sound as a recruit.

Danica explains afterwards that Vila forge magical weapons by merging elemental energy with their own consciousness, so each sword spirit embodies a wish of Vesna's. She says the younger Vesna often declared she would guide lost fae onto the right path, and that she mellowed once she understood laws and discipline.

## revelations
### STANDARD
The "Ancient Sword Esoterica" is not a real cultivation manual. A cutaway shows Mr. Nine at Yae Publishing House, with a collaborator, discussing a draft work of fiction whose author and researcher disagree over whether to keep its adepti hierarchy consistent; the draft was only circulated within the publisher and shelved. Vesna had bought the books in a clearance lot of Yae Publishing House stock. Post-quest, the Greater Sword Spirit admits it could not read all of the Liyue script and took gaps as hidden mysteries.
### LARGE +
The sword spirits' personalities are Vesna's own repressed wishes (Danica's explanation). The two Volkodlak followers were roadside robbers for only a few days before the Greater Sword Spirit set them straight, so they have little record; Vesna lets them go. The "Divine Sense Compass" is ordinary elemental sensing; the incantation ("Spirit, roam the eight reaches!") was theatrical.

## characters
### LITE
Vesna (Commander of the Druzhna), Danica (Her Majesty's head housemaid), Odette and Vodyanitsa of the Korolevskiy Theater, Blyushko, the Greater Sword Spirit and its followers "Second of the Pack" and "Third of the Pack", the Masters of Potions, Body, Sigils, Artifacts and Sound, Mr. Nine, the Traveler (Cryo) and Paimon.
### STANDARD +
Vesna is playful, impulsive and quick to cut corners, yet carries real responsibility and a firm sense of justice. Danica is stern and dry; she watches over Vesna's affairs. The Greater Sword Spirit is dramatic and speaks in cultivation-novel verses, but is loyal to its followers.

## consequences
### STANDARD
Vesna's escaped sword spirits are all recaptured; the Master of Sound stays at her side, and the Greater Sword Spirit and its followers now help with Druzhna paperwork, in the Greater Sword Spirit's words, having laid down weapons and taken up the pen. Vesna learns the manual was fiction and the Druzhna avoid a larger scandal. The dispatch order gives her a sword spirit companion.
### ULTRA +
The Greater Sword Spirit says that guiding every fae onto the proper path is harder than it thought, which echoes Vesna's own history. Vesna and Danica's exchange also hints at how Vesna keeps much of her true self in check to set an example for the Druzhna.

## placement
### STANDARD
Released in 7.1 (the first patch version after Snezhnaya's launch in 7.0), unlocked by completing White Night, Like a Dream Upon Waking. One part is available per day for seven days. Some dialogue changes after A Rekviem for the Underworld. A trial Vesna and Traveler (Cryo) are used in some steps.

## entry: Vesna's sword spirits
@keys: sword spirit, sword spirits, Master of Potions, Master of Sigils, Master of Body, Master of Artifacts, Master of Sound
@keys2: Vesna, Druzhna
@version: 7.1
### STANDARD
Vesna's sword spirits are semi-sentient flying swords she created while experimenting with a manual; each is drawn to a different "art" such as potions, body tempering, sigils, artifacts or sound. They are comic, grandiose and easily thwarted, and each embodies a wish of Vesna's. All are recaptured by the end, and the Master of Sound stays with her.

## entry: Ancient Sword Esoterica
@keys: Ancient Sword Esoterica, secret manual, Sword Immortals
@keys2: Vesna, Yae Publishing House, Mr. Nine
@version: 7.1
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
A Liyue-style manuscript describing Sword Immortals who cultivate body and mind, command flying swords and nurture sword spirits. Vesna bought it cheaply from a rare-book dealer who had bought a clearance lot from Yae Publishing House; it is actually an unfinished novel by Mr. Nine, never printed.
