## summary
@spoiler: minor
### LITE
An Unwavering Culinary Dream is a Sumeru world quest series added in version 3.0 and unlocked after The World of Aranara. In Vanarana the Aranara Arapacati asks the Traveler to find her four "brothers", Arachatora, Arasaka, Arapas and Araphala, who left long ago for "cooking training" and do not know Festival Utsava is near. Each is cooking a Supreme Delicacy, and each needs a small favour to finish it. Arapacati finally asks the Traveler to cook human food at the festival.
### STANDARD
The series is five quests: the opener, Cooking, the Flavor of Nature (which finds Arachatora), then three that can be done in any order, Cooking, a Pleasant Memory (Arasaka), Cooking, the Aroma of Homecoming (Arapas) and Cooking, the Beauty of Sharing (Araphala), and finally A Delicacy for Nara. The tone is comic and tender: Aranara do not understand human tastes, so every Supreme Delicacy is a mild failure by human standards, but each one carries a memory of a golden Nara who once befriended them.
### LARGE +
Underneath the jokes the series explains why the Aranara cook at all. The Golden Nara they call Nara Varuna taught them how humans eat and what a "brother" or "sister" is, and the four cooks are practising so that Nara can again enjoy a meal in Vanarana. The series also gives the Aranara's view of time (moons, seedlings, hibernation), of The Withering ("Marana") and of salt.

## plot
@version: 3.0
### STANDARD
An Unwavering Culinary Dream. In Vanarana, Arapacati tells the Traveler her four Aranara "brothers" left long ago for cooking training after making Vanarana full of smoke, and have not come back for Festival Utsava. She says to follow the smoke pillars and splash any brother who acts strangely with Dendro.

Cooking, the Flavor of Nature. Arachatora, found near Vissudha Field, needs the Head Mushroom from a cave overtaken by a Withering zone. The Traveler purifies the zone and picks it; the dish is tough and tasteless, but he gives the Mushroom Hodgepodge recipe and points to his brothers.

Cooking, a Pleasant Memory. Timid Arasaka needs the "taste of happiness", which the merchant Iaanesh in Gandharva Ville identifies as sugar. Arasaka cooks a very sweet dish and teaches Selva Salad.

Cooking, the Aroma of Homecoming. Arapas wants only the best water for his soup. The Traveler fetches Living Spring water guarded by crocodile-like "Apshvaana". He refuses salt but promises to try a soup with taste, and teaches Masala Cheese Balls.

Cooking, the Beauty of Sharing. Araphala is asleep in a cave near Devantaka Mountain, hiding from a Marana-tainted monster. After the Traveler defeats the Ruin Drake, he shares fresh fruit as his Supreme Delicacy.

A Delicacy for Nara. Arapacati scolds the brothers, thanks the Traveler, explains why they call each other siblings (Nara Varuna spoke of family) and invites the Traveler to cook at Festival Utsava.
### LARGE
An Unwavering Culinary Dream. In Vanarana, Arapacati is nervous around the tall Golden Nara but trusts Arama, who vouches for the Traveler. She is upset: her big brothers have not come back, and the family cannot celebrate Festival Utsava without them. Paimon mistakes "missing" for a kind of seed, until Arapacati makes clear she knows where they are: they left for cooking training long ago. The four made so much smoke in Vanarana that she sent them away, scaring the other Aranara so much that some hibernated. She tells the Traveler to follow the rising pillars of smoke and, if a brother is acting strangely, to splash him with Dendro, since Aranara recover their senses from Dendro after touching strange mushrooms.

Cooking, the Flavor of Nature. Near Vissudha Field the Traveler heals a whimpering Arachatora with Dendro. He ate a strange mushroom because the Head Mushroom he meant to cook was in an area taken over by a Withering zone. He is certain mushroom dishes are the best food and wants to prove it to his brothers. The Traveler defeats two Ruin Sentinels, lowers the water in a cave, purifies the Withering zone and picks the Head Mushroom. Arachatora cooks it: the result is tasteless, tough and hard to chew, which baffles him because an earlier Nara visitor praised his cooking. He gives the Traveler the recipe for Mushroom Hodgepodge and says where the others are: near Gandharva Ville, Apam Woods and Devantaka Mountain.

Cooking, a Pleasant Memory. Arasaka is a timid cook who tends Zaytun Peaches near Avidya Forest and panics around tall Nara. His Supreme Delicacy needs the "taste of happiness", which he describes as white, crumbling like sand, and kept in a "hard bubble". He is too frightened of the crowds in Gandharva Ville to fetch it, and says a Nara with a bulge on his back had gone there. The Traveler finds the merchant Iaanesh, who laughs at the description and identifies it as sugar in a glass bottle. Arasaka tastes it and finds it is not as happy as the first time, then decides it must be shared with friends. He cooks peaches with sugar for the Traveler and Paimon, and the result is overwhelmingly sweet. He teaches them Selva Salad.

Cooking, the Aroma of Homecoming. Near Apam Woods the Traveler follows the smell of soup. Paimon drinks the unattended pot and tastes nothing; Arapas appears from underground, furious. He has been testing water from across Apam Woods and found a "Living Spring" that is the best, but the "Apshvaana" that live at its source bite. They turn out to be crocodiles. The Traveler fetches a new pot, and Arapas makes a plain, aromatic soup, rejects the idea of salt (land with salt is land without life, he says, and Aranara would shrink back into seeds) and repeats what a Nara visitor told him, that a soup needs only aroma. Persuaded that Araja and Arapacati approve of the Traveler, he promises to try a soup with taste and teaches the Masala Cheese Balls recipe.

Cooking, the Beauty of Sharing. At a camp near Devantaka Mountain the Traveler follows a trail of dropped Sunsettias to a cave, where Araphala is asleep. After Dendro wakes him, he panics (his sister warned that a bad Nara would make porridge of a sleeper). He explains Aranara hibernation: in grave danger they compress their memories and dreams into a seed that regrows when it is safe. He came to this place long ago with a golden Nara friend to gather the Unrivaled Fresh Fruits and has gathered fruit here ever since, hoping to eat it with them. A huge monster with Marana's aura, five Araphalas tall, appeared and he hid. The Traveler defeats the Ruin Drake. Araphala shares the fruit uncooked: for him finding better ingredients is the cooking, and travelling through the forest is the true training.

A Delicacy for Nara. Back in Vanarana, Arapacati scolds all four brothers: the forest is dangerous because of Marana, they quarrelled right after leaving, and each wandered somewhere risky. Arachatora may not pick mushrooms until Sunsettias have ripened ten times. Araphala and Arapas are told off for going far from Vanarana, and Arasaka for needing someone else's help to complete his training. Then she thanks the Traveler and explains why she and the others call each other siblings: Nara Varuna spoke about a sibling, said the Aranara should cherish family, and said sharing food is a happy thing. Arapacati asks the Traveler to join Festival Utsava and cook food only Nara can understand. The quest ends there.

Order. The first quest points to smoke pillars. Once Cooking, the Flavor of Nature is done the next three open together and can be completed in any order, and A Delicacy for Nara needs all three. Each cook's last line is some version of "now I only have to let them taste this". After the series each brother has a short line reflecting on his scolding: Arachatora dreads waiting for Sunsettias, Arapas wants to try cooking with Vanarana's water, Araphala hopes Arapacati calms down after some fruit, and Arasaka wonders how the taste of happiness could bring bitterness.
### ULTRA +
Dialogue details. Arachatora tells Paimon that when he ate bad mushrooms before, the Nilotpala Lotuses bloomed and closed twice before he recovered, and he does not know whether two days is long or short. He calls Arapacati his "younger sister" although they are not related. He says the Traveler can fight Marana because the Traveler could heal him with the power of Lesser Lord Kusanali. Araphala's leaf journal reads like a diary of seasons, while Arapas's leaf notes count frosts and record which water was "not acceptable". Iaanesh, a merchant whose family trades gems from Liyue for spices and travels between the Chasm and Sumeru, was left by his father in Gandharva Ville as a child and cannot now recall the friend he made there.

## revelations
@spoiler: minor
@version: 3.0
### STANDARD
- The four Aranara cooks are not related. Arapacati and the others adopted the words "brother" and "sister" after Nara Varuna spoke of a sibling with a mix of happiness and sadness.
- Aranara can fall into a stupor or fear after touching strange mushrooms, and recover through Dendro.
- Aranara hibernate when in grave danger, compressing memories and dreams into a seed that can regrow into an Aranara once it is safe.
- Nara Varuna taught the Aranara how humans eat, and sharing food is the reason the Aranara began cooking training.
### LARGE +
- The Aranara treat Marana's spread as the great danger of the forest, and Araja warned them especially about faraway places.
- Aranara fear fire, because forest fires can raze entire forests, but the cooks train themselves to control flames.
- The Aranara measure time by moons, trees and seedlings, not days.

## characters
@spoiler: none
### STANDARD
- **Arapacati**: the little sister of the group, fearful of tall Nara at first but in charge of the family. She sends the Traveler, gets angry when the brothers argue, and invites the Traveler to Festival Utsava.
- **Arama**: an Aranara of Vanarana who vouches for the Traveler and helps explain things to Arapacati.
- **Arachatora**: the mushroom cook. Gentle and formal, sure that the Head Mushroom is the best ingredient.
- **Arasaka**: the sugar cook. Anxious and prone to panic; believes in a "taste of happiness" tied to a friend.
- **Arapas**: the water and soup cook. Proud and short-tempered, with a firm view that soup needs only good water and aroma.
- **Araphala**: the fruit cook. Sleepy and easily frightened, but content that finding ingredients is cooking.
- **Iaanesh**: a merchant in Gandharva Ville who identifies sugar.
- **Paimon** and the Traveler, who taste each dish; Paimon says they are too plain or too sweet.
### LARGE +
Nara Varuna never appears. Several cooks mention a Nara visitor who praised their food (Arachatora's mushroom, Arapas's soup) or shared a fruit trip (Araphala), and Arapacati identifies the one who taught them about family as Nara Varuna, the same golden Nara the Aranara remember in Varuna Gatha.

## consequences
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
All four brothers return to Vanarana before Festival Utsava, are scolded, and promise to do better. The Traveler receives recipes (Mushroom Hodgepodge, Selva Salad and Masala Cheese Balls), and Arapacati asks them to cook at the festival. The Aranara's idea of cooking changes a little: Arapas says he will try adding taste, and Arasaka learns that happiness depends on who you eat with.
### LARGE +
Gameplay note, labelled as such: A Delicacy for Nara gives a Sumeru reputation reward of 20, and the series gives Primogems and other rewards.

## placement
@spoiler: none
### STANDARD
Released in version 3.0 with Sumeru. It requires The World of Aranara, which sets up the Aranara and Vanarana. Story-wise it occurs while the Aranara are preparing for Festival Utsava, in the period around Varuna Gatha, but the quests do not give an exact order.

## entry: Arapacati and her four brothers
@keys: Arapacati, Arachatora, Arasaka, Arapas, Araphala
@version: 3.0
### STANDARD
Arapacati is an Aranara of Vanarana who calls Arachatora, Arasaka, Arapas and Araphala her big brothers. They are not related by blood or age, and she explains that the family bond is a choice inspired by Nara Varuna. The brothers went off for "cooking training" so they could make a Supreme Delicacy for Nara at Festival Utsava, but they never knew when to come home. Arachatora cooks mushrooms and is gentle and formal. Arasaka cooks with sweetness and is timid. Arapas cooks soups and insists on good water. Araphala loves fresh fruit and sleeps a lot. See the quest An Unwavering Culinary Dream for what happened to each.

## entry: Head Mushroom
@keys: Head Mushroom, Mushroom Hodgepodge
@version: 3.0
### STANDARD
The Head Mushroom is the ingredient Arachatora has been watching grow in a cave near Vissudha Field. It is somewhat larger than a normal Shroom-Kin, and its habitat was taken over by a Withering zone. It must be freed from the zone before it can be picked. Cooked, it is tasteless and tough for the Traveler and Paimon, though very fragrant. Arachatora rewards the Traveler with the recipe for Mushroom Hodgepodge.

## entry: The taste of happiness
@keys: taste of happiness, Selva Salad, Iaanesh
@version: 3.0
### STANDARD
Arasaka's "taste of happiness" is sugar, which he first tried with a Nara friend near Gandharva Ville. He cannot name sugar and describes it as white, crumbling like sand, in a hard bubble. The merchant Iaanesh recognises it from this description and gives a bottle as a gift. Arasaka concludes that the taste is happier when shared with friends and teaches the Traveler Selva Salad.
### LARGE +
Iaanesh remembers being left in Gandharva Ville as a child and making a friend who exclaimed over sugar in much the same way, though he can no longer remember the friend. The series never states that this friend is Arasaka's friend; that is left to the player to infer.

## entry: Living Spring and the Apshvaana
@keys: Living Spring, Apshvaana, Masala Cheese Balls
@version: 3.0
### STANDARD
Arapas insists soup needs the best water and has tested water throughout Apam Woods. His favourite is the cold, clean Living Spring at a river's source, guarded by "Apshvaana", which turn out to be crocodiles. He believes a soup should only smell good: salt would kill land and force Aranara back into seeds. The Traveler's first plain soup is aromatic but tasteless. He agrees to try adding flavour and teaches the Masala Cheese Balls recipe.

## entry: Araphala and Aranara hibernation
@keys: Araphala, Unrivaled Fresh Fruit, Unrivaled Fresh Fruits, Aranara hibernation
@keys2: Aranara
@version: 3.0
### STANDARD
Araphala is found asleep in a cave near Devantaka Mountain. He explains that Aranara hibernate in grave danger by compressing memories and dreams into a seed that can regrow when it is safe. He was hiding from a huge Marana-tainted monster (a Ruin Drake that the Traveler defeats). He believes finding the best ingredients, such as Unrivaled Fresh Fruit from strong trees, is the real cooking training.

## entry: Nara Varuna in the cooking quests
@keys: Nara Varuna
@keys2: cooking, Arapacati, Aranara
@version: 3.0
### STANDARD
In this series the Golden Nara called Nara Varuna is the one who taught the Aranara how Nara eat and what siblings are. The Aranara note that Nara Varuna looked happy and sad when speaking of a sibling, and ask the Traveler (also a Golden Nara) to share food with them in turn. Reading Nara Varuna as the Traveler's lost sibling is supported by the wider Aranara storyline, not stated in these quests.
