## summary
### LITE
A Version 6.3 world quest series in Nod-Krai, in three acts. Illuga, the young captain of the Lightkeepers' Nightmare Orioles squad, is trying to stop a Wild Hunt outbreak spilling from Kipumaki Cliff, where an undying enemy called the "Professor" has been sealed for five hundred years. With the Traveler and Paimon he collects the sacred relics of the Torchforger, repairs the old seal and finally fires the Arrow of Terpikeraunas from Piramida. The enemy is destroyed, at a heavy cost.
### STANDARD +
The acts are Prelude to the Night Vigil (Wind-Stirred Ripples and Tower of Inversion), Darradarljod (When War Songs Rise, with three sub-quests called Focal Point of Ancient Array) and the final act A Star Out of Time (The Last Night, the First Light). The series is as much about the Lightkeepers and about Illuga's fear of failing those he leads as it is about the enemy.
### LARGE +
Alia, a human woman from the Torchforger's era who lingers outside normal time at the Pillar of Embla, supplies much of the history. The series explains the Lightkeepers' relics (Silver Lampwick, Spiralblade, the Arrow of Terpikeraunas), the three-year-old disaster at Kipumaki Cliff, and a prophecy that the undying serpent will rise twice and be struck down a third time.
### ULTRA +
Title note: the wiki's etymology note says 'Darradarljod' is a skaldic poem in Old Norse, found in chapter 157 of Njals saga. The final quest also rewards an Avatar called the Arrow of Terpikeraunas.

## plot
### LITE
At Piramida the Lightkeepers prepare an operation against the Wild Hunt at Kipumaki Cliff. The Traveler helps Illuga find the Silver Lampwick from Alia at the Pillar of Embla, joins his squad for the clifftop assault, and in the end helps load the Spiralblade into the great mead hall's giant arrow and fire it at the enemy's Cataclysm Egg. The Professor is killed with Alia's help, and the Lightkeepers celebrate and mourn.
### STANDARD +
Act by act: the Traveler meets Illuga and his squad and fetches the Silver Lampwick from Alia at the Pillar of Embla; the squad launches Operation Clifftop, loses Bjorn, repairs the old sealing array and recovers the Spiralblade; finally the Lightkeepers fire the Arrow of Terpikeraunas from Piramida, and Alia confronts the Professor at the tower's top.
### LARGE +
Chapter I: Prelude to the Night Vigil. In Wind-Stirred Ripples, near Piramida, the Traveler meets two Ratniki, Anleifr and Rollon, whose broken-down Moonwagon is interrupted when Rollon is suddenly overcome by a vision of a great purple eye and attacks. After he is brought around, Anleifr explains the Wild Hunt outbreak at Kipumaki Cliff, the pioneering-age ruins there and how the Wild Hunt can seize minds. In Piramida the Ratniki gather for an oath-taking led by Illuga, who recites the Lightkeepers' vow and announces the mission, to set foot on the cliff they last approached three years earlier. The old Ratnik Nikita asks the Traveler to find the Silver Lampwick, one of the Torchforger's three sacred relics (the Sword, the Lamp and the Mirror) that can raise a barrier against the Wild Hunt. Flins then speaks with Nikita, warning that this is no ordinary Wild Hunt and that he has met the ancient enemy before.

In Tower of Inversion, a voice at Wavechaser Plain calls on the "heroes of the sublunary sphere" to ascend a tower. At the Pillar of Embla the Traveler is nearly hit by Abyssal attacks and Illuga shields them with his lantern. He explains that the Lampwick is held by Alia, said to be a fae. His companion bird Aedon reveals hidden kuuvahki paths. When the second barrier core is recharged, time shifts and Alia appears. She is no fae, she says, but an ordinary human of five centuries ago who fought beside Solovei, the Torchforger, and who is now stuck in a time seam. She tells them of the prophecy and asks them to cleanse the corrupted tower. The three barrier cores are restored, Alia gives them the Lampwick and then holds the tower alone against the Professor. Illuga invites the Traveler to join his squad.

Chapter II: Darradarljod. In When War Songs Rise the squad gathers at Cliffwatch Camp. Illuga tells the story of the camp, three years earlier, when the Wild Hunt's mist set Lightkeepers against one another. The Traveler and Paimon meet Anleifr, Rollon and Bjorn, who also lost people. The next morning "Operation Clifftop" begins. A fog-wrapped voice, the Scourge of Endless Decay, taunts Illuga about running away. At the altar the barrier cannot be redeployed because a great Evil Eye destroys it, so Bjorn proposes using the four-point sealing array left by the Torchforger and the first Moonchanter. Illuga carries the Lampwick to three Verdant Crests with the Traveler. They destroy the Evil Eye, but Bjorn, who had been defending a position, was killed by its attack. Illuga blames himself. The Scourge torments him with illusions, but his team arrives. They return the Sealing Blades to their scabbards in the chasm of endless decay, destroy the Scourge and recover the Spiralblade. A scuffle then takes place off-screen (the wiki transcript implies that Anleifr struck Illuga); Illuga does not punish him, admits he lost his composure, and the team reconciles. The Scourge has spoken of Professor Rotwang as its master who is already on his way.

Final Chapter: A Star Out of Time. In The Last Night, the First Light, they return to Piramida. Nikita, whom Illuga calls his old pops, reveals the great mead hall is the Arrow of Terpikeraunas, a bow and arrow forged by the Torchforger and the first Moonchanter, and that the Spiralblade is its missing arrowhead. While the Lightkeepers hold back the Abyss in the plaza, the Traveler and Illuga load the Spiralblade, fight a Serpent of All Evils and an Ultimate Calamity, activate three amplifiers and fire the arrow at the Cataclysm Egg above the Pillar of Embla.

Atop the Pillar of Embla the Professor, trapped by the Sampo Mill, mistakes Alia for Relya. He offers her an "Elysium" for the shamed and forgotten and calls her his chosen one. Alia answers that he used Relya from the beginning, spinning promises so that Relya would seize the Sampo Mill, and that his fall into thievery was also a trap devised with Grand Duke Alvis. She says she carries Relya's role and legacy and will take the name Reed Miller, names the Professor as Rauthvangi, mad monk and undying serpent, and says his story ends here. Both fade away.

The aftermath is a feast in the mead hall, led by Nikita, in honour of the fallen and the Traveler's help, and a visit the next day to Bjorn's grave south of Cliffwatch Camp, where Illuga says Bjorn completed his mission and that the Lightkeepers will go on, like the Winter Icelea, enduring cold and storm.
### ULTRA +
Quest-by-quest notes. Wind-Stirred Ripples unlocks the achievement The Winds Awaken, the Leaves Whirl Round (the oath line). Tower of Inversion rewards the Silver Lampwick plus chests. When War Songs Rise gives Illuga as a trial character and includes two fights against the Scourge of Endless Decay (as a Nightmare's Avatar and later as Pathfinder to the Bedeviled Sky). The Last Night, the First Light is in a special story mode with Illuga on the team and rewards the Arrow of Terpikeraunas Avatar and the achievement Si le grain ne meurt. Rewards across the quests include Primogems, Mora, Hero's Wit and Mystic Enhancement Ore; individual amounts vary.

## revelations
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The Lightkeepers' relics, sealing array and Arrow of Terpikeraunas were all made five hundred years ago by the Torchforger Solovei and the first Moonchanter of the Frostmoon Scions. Alia is a human, not a fae. The Professor is an undying serpent who sought to ascend to an "Elysium" beyond the sky using the Sampo Mill, and who was already killed once by Alia.
### LARGE +
The Pillar of Embla is the tower the Professor built to reach his Elysium. By the prophecy's count the Professor has fallen three times: first at Alia's hands, then sealed at Kipumaki Cliff by Solovei and the Moonchanter, and finally by the Lightkeepers and the Traveler with the Arrow. The nightingale Aedon was once Solovei's bird, adopted by Alia when Solovei meant to cast aside the Nightingale family name, and was carried through the Sampo Mill across five hundred years.
### ULTRA +
Alia says the prophecy is less a prediction than an earnest plea, since its giver was, like her, a person from the past; the wiki dialogue has the Traveler recall a similar remark from Aila. The Scourge says the Professor's plan has been tried and failed ten years ago and three years ago. The series does not explain the Cataclysm Egg beyond its being a doom hatching above the Pillar.

## characters
### LITE
Illuga, young captain of the Nightmare Orioles; Alia; Flins; Nikita; the Ratniki Anleifr, Rollon, Bjorn, Egle, Vlaicu and Olav; Aedon the nightingale; the Professor and the Scourge of Endless Decay.
### STANDARD +
Illuga is dutiful and anxious about being responsible for others. Anleifr is brash, loyal and funny and a former treasure hunter who lost his wife; Rollon is calm and steady and the brother of Ivar; Bjorn has studied Frostmoon Scions texts he borrowed from the current Moonchanter and comes up with the plan for the sealing array. Nikita is a senior Ratnik, addressed as Starshyna, who directs the operation from Piramida and says his time is running out. Flins is theatrical but solemn about his friend Nikita. Alia is wry, kind and tired.
### LARGE +
The Scourge of Endless Decay mocks and isolates its victims with illusions of the dead. The Professor speaks in grand promises.

## consequences
### STANDARD
The Professor is destroyed and Bjorn's death is mourned. Illuga leans on his team more than before. The series ends with the Lightkeepers celebrating and with the loss that paid for it.

## placement
### STANDARD
Released in Version 6.3 (Luna IV) as one of Nod-Krai's world quest series. The transcripts include alternate lines depending on whether Wind-Stirred Ripples or Tower of Inversion was done first, and some lines for players who have done other quests. Lightkeepers' Oath (6.0) quotes the same oath.

## entry: Silver Lampwick
@keys: Silver Lampwick
@version: 6.3
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
One of three sacred relics (the Sword, the Lamp and the Mirror) the Torchforger used five hundred years ago against the fiend of Kipumaki Cliff. It deploys a barrier against the Wild Hunt. Solovei entrusted it to Alia at the Pillar of Embla, and the Traveler receives it after cleansing the tower for Illuga.

## entry: Spiralblade
@keys: Spiralblade, Sealing Blades
@version: 6.3
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
A spiral-shaped sacred blade the Torchforger used to seal demons. The Evil Eye seized it, it fell into the underground ruins, and Illuga and the Traveler recover it in When War Songs Rise. It is later loaded into the Arrow of Terpikeraunas as its arrowhead.

## entry: Arrow of Terpikeraunas
@keys: Arrow of Terpikeraunas, Terpikeraunas
@version: 6.3
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
A bow and arrow forged five hundred years ago by the Torchforger and the first Moonchanter, passed down by the Lightkeepers until the day it could be used. The great mead hall in Piramida is the arrow and the Spiralblade its missing arrowhead. It is fired from the lighthouse-cannon of Piramida at the Cataclysm Egg over the Pillar of Embla.

## entry: Alia of the Pillar of Embla
@keys: Alia
@keys2: Pillar of Embla, Silver Lampwick, Illuga, Professor
@version: 6.3
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
Called a fae in legend. She says she is an ordinary human of the Torchforger's age, an ally of Solovei, who killed the Professor the first time by casting herself into the Sampo Mill and who now lingers outside normal time. She guards the tower, hands over the Silver Lampwick, and holds the Professor back at the end, telling him she carries Relya's role and the name Reed Miller.

## entry: The Professor of Kipumaki Cliff
@keys: the Professor, Rauthvangi, undying serpent, Professor Rotwang
@keys2: Kipumaki Cliff, Lightkeepers, Alia, Pillar of Embla
@version: 6.3
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
An undying enemy sealed beneath Kipumaki Cliff five hundred years ago. He sought to use the Sampo Mill and a tower to reach an "Elysium" beyond the sky. Alia calls him Rauthvangi, a mad monk and the undying serpent; a prophecy says he will rise twice and be struck down a third time. His servant the Scourge of Endless Decay names him Professor Rotwang.

## entry: Operation Clifftop and the Kipumaki Cliff disaster
@keys: Operation Clifftop, Cliffwatch Camp
@keys2: Kipumaki Cliff, Illuga, Lightkeepers, Wild Hunt
@version: 6.3
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
Three years before the series, a Lightkeeper detachment camped on Kipumaki Cliff was drawn into a strange mist and turned on itself; Captain Olsson held on alone, died covering the retreat of Illuga and the badly injured Ivar, and the Wild Hunt spilled out and killed many civilians. The Lightkeepers rebuilt Cliffwatch Camp and held the line. In the series Illuga leads Operation Clifftop to seize the Clifftop Camp and begin the counterattack.

## entry: Bjorn
@keys: Bjorn
@keys2: Illuga, Lightkeepers, Anleifr, Rollon, Kipumaki Cliff
@version: 6.3
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
A Lightkeeper in Illuga's squad and a thoughtful tactician. He suggests using the Torchforger's four-point sealing array, having studied Frostmoon Scions texts lent by the present Moonchanter. He is killed by the Evil Eye while holding a position at Operation Clifftop, and Illuga blames himself. Anleifr and Rollon mourn him.

## entry: The Nightmare Orioles
@keys: Nightmare Orioles, Nightmare Oriole
@version: 6.3
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
Illuga's investigation squad of the Lightkeepers. Its members in the series include Anleifr, Rollon and Bjorn, with Egle and Vlaicu in supporting roles and the Traveler and Paimon as temporary recruits. Illuga formally invites the Traveler to join in Tower of Inversion.

## entry: Sampo Mill
@keys: Sampo Mill
@version: 6.3
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
A source of power the Professor tried to seize to fuel his ascent. Alia ignited it with her own flesh and blood to stop him, and it later pulled the bird Aedon through five hundred years. The Professor says in the final scene that it turns its face from him again. What the Mill is, beyond this, is not explained.
