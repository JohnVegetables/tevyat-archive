## summary
### LITE
A six-quest Great Red Sand world quest series from Version 3.4. Jeht, daughter of the Eremite Jebrael, invites the Traveler and Paimon to the Tanit Camps to bury her parents' remains at the Eternal Oasis. To enter it they need a jinni fragment, which brings in Liloupar and the Tanit's own factions.
### STANDARD +
The series follows Golden Slumber and finishes Jeht's personal arc. It adds the Tanit matriarch Babel, Liloupar's contract with the Traveler, betrayal by two Tanit members, and the discovery that the Eternal Oasis holds the long-dead Goddess of Flowers, to whom King Deshret lied about the jinn. Jeht chooses to stay with her tribe.
### LARGE +
The wiki trivia notes that Bilqis is the supposed name of the Queen of Sheba, and that the ruins known as the Throne of Bilqis lie in Yemen, where the real Hadramaut region is.

## plot
### LITE
Jeht asks the Traveler and Paimon to help her reach the Eternal Oasis. At Gurabad they free the jinni Liloupar, who contracts with the Traveler. Tanit members try to take her, and the group goes through King Deshret's old canal tunnels to Deshret's Glass Goblet, fights the guardian Ferigees, and reaches the Oasis, where Jeht lays down her father's axe and her mother's notes.
### STANDARD +
Wisdom Has Built Her House: Jeht's letter brings the Traveler to the Tanit Camps, where the matriarch Babel says she approved the invitation. After errands around the camps, Jeht says Babel has allowed her to bury her parents' remains at the Eternal Oasis, reached only through a Jinni Fragment found at Gurabad.

The Temple Where Sand Flows Like Tears: At Gurabad, Jeht's senior Azariq guides them to the fragment of the Mother of the Jinn. A new ruin appears and the group finds Liloupar, a jinni in a bottle who forms a contract of servitude with the Traveler. Eremites hired by Tanit member Aderfi ambush them to take her. Babel says Aderfi has fled and will be punished.

Dune-Entombed Fecundity, Parts I to III: With Azariq they use King Deshret's old canal tunnels to Mt. Damavand and Deshret's Glass Goblet, needing three entrances. Liloupar recovers fragments of herself. Azariq shows them a decommissioned Ruin Guard to cross quicksand; Liloupar grows suspicious of his excitement. In Part III Azariq demands Liloupar so the Tanit can restore the oases. Jeht, realising he learned the Ruin Guard technology from the Fatui and meant to kill the Traveler to end their contract, challenges and kills him. The group then reaches the center of Deshret's Glass Goblet, where Liloupar's sister Ferigees, in the form of an ancient contraption, blocks them and is defeated.

The Eternal Dream, Ever Lush: The Eternal Oasis is a paradise orchard where everything is frozen in time. Liloupar realises the Goddess of Flowers at its center has long been dead and that King Deshret lied to her and the other jinn, but bears him no hatred. Jeht lays her father's axe and her mother's notes before a tree, saying she feels at peace and will return when older. Back at the camps Babel tells Jeht she has always been a member of the tribe and must one day lead it. The Traveler may choose Mora, an Artifact or nothing as reward.
### LARGE +
Details. Babel warns the Fatui may be involved in Aderfi's betrayal and that Wenut activity has risen in the dunes. At the first night's camp Jeht asks why Liloupar dislikes Babel above all others. Liloupar tells of a jinni who loved a tyrant mortal king and then cursed their children for generations until their kingdom fell, and says Babel will use her people for her own ends without remorse. Jeht protests that Babel is different, but Liloupar urges her to judge for herself. Azariq had intended to replace Babel with Jeht as matriarch and marry her to become co-leader. Before entering the Oasis Jeht worries about her future among the Tanit. Babel says she had sent expeditions to the old camps long ago, but none returned alive, and she had faith that Jeht would be the one to reach the Oasis.
### ULTRA +
Optional and small points. The Traveler can play a round of Genius Invokation TCG with Azariq, though it is not required. Alternatively the quest can begin directly at the Tanit Camps, where scouts stop the party but let them in once they mention friendship with Jeht. Jeht apologises to Liloupar and the Traveler for her earlier attitude, and promises to catch Aderfi.

## revelations
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Azariq planned to replace Babel with Jeht and marry her, while Aderfi hired Eremites to take Liloupar. The Goddess of Flowers at the Eternal Oasis is long dead, and King Deshret lied to Liloupar and the other jinn.
### LARGE +
Liloupar does not hate Deshret for this. Jeht concludes Azariq learned the Ruin Guard technology from the Fatui.

## characters
### LITE
The Traveler, Paimon, Jeht, Babel, Liloupar, Azariq, Aderfi and Ferigees.
### STANDARD +
Jeht is the grieving daughter of Jebrael and her late mother Ufairah; Babel is the Tanit matriarch; Liloupar is a jinni who serves the Traveler under contract but distrusts Babel; Azariq is Jeht's senior and friend who turns on her; Aderfi is a Tanit schemer; Ferigees is Liloupar's sister, who blocks the way to the Oasis.

## consequences
### STANDARD
Jeht completes her parents' wish and decides to remain with the Tanit, with Babel naming her as a future leader. Azariq is dead, Aderfi has fled, and the Traveler keeps Liloupar's contract. The series leads onward to the Apocalypse Lost quest.

## placement
### STANDARD
Released in Version 3.4 and set in the Great Red Sand. It begins one Daily Reset after finishing Golden Slumber and is followed by Apocalypse Lost.

## entry: Eternal Oasis
@keys: Eternal Oasis, Dirge of Bilqis
@keys2: Jeht, Liloupar, Deshret's Glass Goblet
@version: 3.4
### STANDARD
The Eternal Oasis is a paradise orchard where everything is frozen in time, said to lie at the center of the sandstorm in the desert's heart. In The Dirge of Bilqis, Jeht, Liloupar and the Traveler reach it through the canal tunnels of Deshret's Glass Goblet, where Jeht buries her parents' remains. The Goddess of Flowers there has long been dead.
