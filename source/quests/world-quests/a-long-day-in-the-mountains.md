## summary
### LITE
A Version 6.5 world quest chapter set around Dornman Port and the Old Sanatorium Site on Windrest Peak, then inside the Temple of Space. A young woman named Zeitlind is searching for her older sister Hilda, who vanished from the sanatorium. A towering angelic Watcher, Nihilita, sends the Traveler to free the temple from his counterpart Melanta.
### STANDARD +
The chapter has four acts: Magic Mountain (the prologue), Paradise, Hell and Judgment. It is built around the Temple of Space, a place created by the God of Space, Asmoday, whose inhabitants have been terrorised since their Watcher Melanta took control. Zeitlind recovers her sister's belongings from the temple's districts. The trial ends with Melanta defeated and Zeitlind giving up her memories to open the way.
### LARGE +
The theme is memory, will to live and what freedom means to a created being. It deals gently with grief and an absent sibling, which parallels the Traveler's own search for a lost twin. Melanta, who wants to "correct" others, is shown as hurt and jealous rather than simply cruel.
### ULTRA +
The wiki notes the title echoes the novel The Magic Mountain; the chapter uses a sanatorium on a mountain and otherworldly figures in the same way, but that is an observation from outside the game.

## plot
### LITE
A man, Mann, warns people not to go up the mountain after seeing a ghost at the old sanatorium: a woman beside a grave with her own face. The Traveler meets Zeitlind, who is looking for her sister, and the red-sky Watcher Nihilita traps her and sends the Traveler to the Temple of Space. There the group helps the inhabitants, finds Hilda's belongings, and finally confronts Melanta.
### STANDARD +
**Magic Mountain.** In the north of Dornman Port, Mann and his dog Hauptmann tell of the encounter at the Old Sanatorium Site. At the graveyard Zeitlind says her older sister disappeared while staying there, and the grave turns out to be her sister's, though it looks like Zeitlind. The sky turns red, Nihilita appears and traps Zeitlind in cubes, and entrusts the Traveler with a juvenile Watcher, Dex. At the Temple of Space Zeitlind is freed. Istoria explains that after the God of Space vanished, Melanta took over and tormented the inhabitants. The group frees Josephus in the Memory Core, and Istoria reveals Hilda had been in the temple. A Domain Keeper steals Zeitlind's suitcase and scatters its contents across the three exhibition spaces.
**Paradise.** The Traveler and Zeitlind restore Luyang Academy, where people were turned into ink, Mahavaipulya Chamber, and the Desert Pavilion, where people were turned into sand. In each they recover an item of Hilda's: a photograph at Wangshu Inn, a stack of letters sent to Zeitlind from the sanatorium, and an amethyst brooch. Many inhabitants mistake Zeitlind for Hilda, and some knew her. Istoria says that to defeat Melanta they must construct the Path to the Heavens, which demands the sacrifice of memories.
**Hell.** Red cubes cast the group into the Apathic Interval, where those chosen for the temple must give up their desires. Dex and the Traveler break the Maranasati Caskets, rescue Istoria, Zeitlind and a man named Alara, and escape with three Deathmuse Shards without forgoing their desires. Paimon, curiously, was not drawn in.
**Judgment.** Istoria says Hilda once sacrificed her memories to make the Path and met Melanta herself; afterwards Melanta wrecked part of the Path. Istoria offers his own memories but is persuaded his knowledge is too valuable, and Zeitlind accepts the burden. The group crosses the Corridor of Nothingness, where the Traveler sees shared dreams of the sisters, and reaches the Atrium of Suchness. Melanta cannot account for the Traveler's sibling's fate and calls Nihilita a traitor. He loses the fight, accepts that all creations became free when Asmoday left, and admits his actions caused his own breakdown. Asked by Zeitlind why the sanatorium is cursed, he answers, then explains that he sought to correct others, admits envy of Nihilita's freedom and longing for his creator's approval, and self-destructs.
### LARGE +
Details worth keeping: Istoria once discussed philosophy with Hilda to strengthen her will to live; Zeitlind tells him Hilda was badly hurt when her fiancé abandoned her; Melanta exiled a figure called Maukie and illusions of it trouble the rescue at Luyang Academy. Istoria explains the names of the two Watchers. Zeitlind says her epitaph should read "To be continued" and loses her memories upon returning to the surface. Back at the sanatorium the Traveler and Paimon help her begin anew and remind her that her sister is still somewhere out in the world.

## revelations
@version: 6.5
@spoiler: major
### LITE
The Temple of Space is run by Watchers created by the God of Space, Asmoday, and one of them, Melanta, rules by fear.
### STANDARD +
Melanta says all creations were free the moment Asmoday left, and that Nihilita left because of that. Hilda, Zeitlind's sister, once went to the temple and sacrificed her memories to confront Melanta; her fate afterwards is unknown. Melanta also answers Zeitlind's question about why the Windrest Peak sanatorium is cursed.
### LARGE +
Melanta could not explain the Traveler's sibling's fate when asked, which keeps that mystery open in this chapter. The chapter never states how Hilda left the temple or where she went.

## characters
### LITE
The Traveler and Paimon; Zeitlind; Raumhilda (Hilda); Nihilita; Melanta; Dex; Istoria.
### STANDARD +
Mann and his dog Hauptmann; Josephus, Alara and Venipi among the inhabitants; Daozi, Bosch and Yoshitoshi at Luyang Academy; Calcerius, Okikuni and Yu Huangyi at Mahavaipulya Chamber; the Domain Keepers and the Punisher of Gomorrah as enemies.

## consequences
### STANDARD
The temple is freed and Melanta is gone. Zeitlind loses her memories and starts over on the surface. The chapter leaves Hilda's whereabouts open.
### LARGE +
The Temple of Space and the mystery of the Watchers are linked, through Melanta's words, to Asmoday's departure and the Traveler's sibling.

## placement
### STANDARD
Released in Version 6.5 (Luna VI, 2026-04-08) as a world quest chapter. It starts near Dornman Port in Mondstadt and then moves to the Temple of Space. The wiki page lists no quest requirement.
