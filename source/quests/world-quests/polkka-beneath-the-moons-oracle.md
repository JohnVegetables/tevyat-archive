## summary
### LITE
Polkka Beneath the Moon's Oracle is a Nod-Krai World Quest series on Hiisi Island, added in Version 6.0 (Luna I). Four quests can be done in any order: a flustered young Frostmoon priestess named Verna tries to calm unstable kuuvahki at Thunderclap Reef, an angler is puzzled by empty waters, a child hunts for pretty stones, and a self-styled master thief hunts a crab. Each leaves a stone fragment on a large rock that later flies to a pillar at the Sanctum of the Oathkeeper. The fifth quest opens a sealed sanctum and meets the recorded echo of Aila, the first Moonchanter.
### STANDARD +
The tone is comic on the surface (a priestess who "trusts science", a thief with borrowed bravado) with a quieter thread: a robed woman who speaks in riddles keeps appearing and, in the end, is explained by Aila's recorded blessing. The series is also the Traveler's first sustained contact with Frostmoon Scions customs outside the main Archon Quest.
### LARGE +
The English title plays on "polkka", the Finnish form of the polka dance, as the wiki notes. The stones are the series' running mechanism: kuuvahki-charged fragments that, thrown at or fixed to large rocks, draw those rocks toward the sealed Court of the First Edict area.

## plot
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
For a Green Island... In Thunderclap Reef the Traveler and Paimon meet Verna, a priestess in a kuuvahki-resistant suit who is on a secret mission to calm the reef's unstable kuuvahki. Her plan, which she calls science, is to throw charged stone fragments at two huge rotating boulders. Her Kraka robots fetch the fragments but get stuck, so the Traveler retrieves them; the first attempt makes things worse before a third fragment is found. A robed woman waits there, calls the Traveler the hero of "Elysium", hands over the last fragment in a Moon Mirror, and says she will wait at the Court of the First Edict. A Frostnight Scion wakes and must be defeated; afterwards the third fragment is freed and the boulders fly toward the Sanctum of the Oathkeeper.

Gift of the Mirage. The angler Pekkani, who is not a Frostmoon Scion and has no kuuvahki power, is catching no fish and asks the Traveler to look under the water west of the Frostmoon Enclave. Fragments placed on a stone there raise a platform and make a big rock fly off; the fish return.

The Tale of the Gate Stone. In the Frostmoon Enclave a child named Kasha is looking for pretty stones to give a friend named Mai, an outsider girl of about her age. The Traveler finds a kuuvahki-affected stone that Kasha senses wants to join a large stone; placing it makes the large stone rise and fly to the same pillar.

The Mirrors, the Maze, and the Tsar. In a pit west of the Enclave a thief named Maite Veliu, who claims to be the heir of the legendary thief Reed Miller, recruits the Traveler to take a treasure from the Crab Tsar. The Traveler clears blocked passages with the help of rock crabs, breaks a Moon Mirror at the advice of the same robed woman, and defeats the Crab Tsar, which has been carrying a large stone. The stone flies away and the crab, ashamed, hides.

Echoes of an Unfinished Past. See below (this quest is unlocked after the other four).
### LARGE +
Details of the Thunderclap Reef plan: after the first two fragments the kuuvahki turns to a violent "reversed chaotic" state with lightning, which Verna admits she mishandled. The robed woman greets the Traveler, who is addressed by her as the hero of "Elysium"; when Verna accuses her of being an impostor, the woman answers that masks only matter once someone sits in the seat. She says that "Via" (the wiki's tooltip reads Lovia; Verna takes it for her own name) has no place in a world where the "god" has been born, and invites the party to meet her at the Court of the First Edict, a place Verna says has been sealed for five hundred years.

Echoes of an Unfinished Past. Verna meets the Traveler again, unhelmeted, and has gathered more fragments from her Krakas. She plans to break the seal of the Court of the First Edict to trap the "impostor". At the Light-Bathed Platform a fragment sends the floating rock back to its pillar, which drops onto the lower one and draws the island's attention. A Moonlane leads into the Interstice of the Nearing Moon, where three pillars must be slid onto tracks. A mechanical voice runs an identity check, grants authority and asks for the seal to be lifted by hand; the final greeting depends on which Natlan quests the player has finished. Ehrnrooth, Krodo and Rahetar, other Frostmoon Scions, notice the intruders. Inside the Court of the First Edict, called the Sanctum of the Oathbearer in some lines, the party sees the Verdant Crest and the White Spring. A white kuuhenki leads to a recorded image of Aila, who speaks with an unnamed man later called Solovei. Aila shows awareness of the Traveler, explains she steered them there with the stones, and leaves a sword in the White Spring. Rahetar then leads Verna away to answer to the Lady Moonchanter for tampering with a sealed place.
### ULTRA +
Gift of the Mirage ends with Pekkani paying in assorted items he fished up and saying Mora is little use on Hiisi Island; he also grumbles about "that annoying bard" and a pet, a passing reference this series does not develop.

In The Mirrors, the Maze, and the Tsar, Maite says the Crab Tsar used to be gentle and could not pass through the mirrors until it picked up a stone about six months earlier, after which it grew cruel. She says she is a member of the Conch Gang gathering intelligence and rewards the Traveler with an IOU and her notes; she tells the Traveler to mention her name at the Speranza restaurant in Nasha Town, and that the Traveler must still settle bills with Katya. She describes the Conch Gang as a mutual aid group of like-minded young people and says her information on the Frostmoon Scions came from someone she calls Big Sis Hidimbi, who is still in Nasha Town. Maite also recalls being caught by a "naive little girl" among the Scions and released after telling stories; The Tale of the Gate Stone shows Kasha describing catching a girl called Mai, who tells her outside stories; the two accounts match closely but neither quest names the other's character.

## revelations
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
- The unstable kuuvahki at Thunderclap Reef worsened after two large stones fell from the sky; Verna says it has always been unstable since the first Moonchanter's time.
- The Court of the First Edict, a sacred site of the Frostmoon Scions, was sealed about five hundred years ago by Aila, the first Moonchanter.
- The robed woman is probably tied to Aila (see uncertainties). Aila's recording says its blessing only records awareness, and that she steered the Traveler with the fallen stones because the Traveler's thread would not catch in the loom of this world.
### LARGE +
- Aila speaks of sealing the evil and corruption left by Lovia so the children of the faith are not stained, and calls this part of the preparation for the birth of a god. She says Lovia trusted her because she obeyed, and that she severed Lovia's thread when guided by the Lady of the Underworld.
- Solovei, speaking with Aila, says he came to help, thanks her, and wants to uncover a "mad monk" using years of secret correspondence with Lovia as evidence.
- Aila gives the Traveler a sword forged from Hyperborean moonlight by an apostle of the golden city, left in the White Spring as an apology, and hopes the Traveler is the hero meant to fell an undying serpent.
- Verna says Aila's name was struck from the record for blasphemy and is known only to senior priestesses.
### ULTRA +
- Players who have completed the Natlan Archon Quest will hear the final seal greet the Traveler as the ruler named in that quest line; those who have not hear a more generic greeting. This is conditional dialogue, so do not treat it as true for every Traveler.

## characters
### STANDARD
Verna, a young priestess who misquotes scripture, trusts robots and blames "science" for her plans. Pekkani, an angler. Kasha, a child of the Frostmoon Enclave who tells stories borrowed from outside. Maite Veliu, a young thief and Conch Gang member. The Crab Tsar, a large crab. Aila, the first Moonchanter, seen as a recorded echo. Solovei, a voice speaking with Aila. Ehrnrooth, Krodo and Rahetar, Frostmoon Scions who pursue the intruders. Paimon and the Traveler throughout.
### LARGE +
Verna says she is a direct apprentice of "Papa Wrench" and the best mechanic on Hiisi Island; her faction views technology with suspicion. Her Kraka robots (III, V, VII) are her own successors to an earlier original, Kraka I, whose story she says is long.

## consequences
### STANDARD
The large stones are returned to the pillar at the Sanctum of the Oathkeeper, and the Court of the First Edict is opened for the first time in five hundred years. Verna is taken to answer to the Lady Moonchanter. The Traveler gains Maite's IOU and notes, and the final quest's reward list includes Moonweaver's Dawn, presumably the sword Aila left (the wiki does not state this outright).
### LARGE +
The quest links the Traveler to Aila's longer thread, which other Nod-Krai stories continue (see the Moonchanter and Frostmoon Scions entries). Lauma, the current Moonchanter, is named by Verna as someone she will need to apologise to.

## placement
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Released in Version 6.0 (Luna I, 2025-09-10) alongside the first Nod-Krai content. The four opening quests can be done in any order; Echoes of an Unfinished Past unlocks only after the other four. The wiki's chapter infobox uses the Song of the Welkin Moon chapter image.

## entry: Verna and the Kraka robots
@keys: Verna, Vernadska, Kraka III, Kraka V, Kraka VII
@keys2: Thunderclap Reef, Hiisi Island, Polkka
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Verna (full self-styled name Vernadska) is a young Frostmoon priestess in a kuuvahki-resistant suit and helmet who forgets scripture, trusts "science" and builds small Kraka robots. She introduces herself with a grand ritual line, takes the Traveler for a moon-blessed saviour, and hides that she is acting alone and against her faction's wishes because most Scions distrust technology. Her plans frequently go wrong and she admits she is panicking only after pretending not to.

## entry: Crab Tsar and Crab Tsar's Palace
@keys: Crab Tsar, Crab Tsar's Palace, Crabby Booty
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
The Crab Tsar is a large crab ruling a cave palace west of the Frostmoon Enclave. It was normally docile until it carried a fallen stone on its back; the stone made it aggressive toward smaller crabs and allowed it to pass through old Moon Mirrors. After the Traveler defeats it, the stone flies off and the crab hides in remorse. Maite calls the stone a "stone of desire", a name she says came from Hidimbi.

## entry: Court of the First Edict
@keys: Court of the First Edict, Sanctum of the Oathbearer, Verdant Crest, White Spring
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
A sacred site of the Frostmoon Scions sealed five hundred years ago by Aila. Inside are the Verdant Crest, a tree said to have witnessed the Moon Maiden's birth, and the White Spring, where Aila left a sword. It can be opened by sliding three pillars onto tracks in the Interstice of the Nearing Moon. The wiki gives the in-quest names inconsistently, using Oathkeeper for the sanctum with the pillar and Oathbearer for the inner site.

## entry: Aila's recorded blessing
@keys: Aila, first Moonchanter, Lady Aila
@keys2: Court of the First Edict, Polkka
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
In Echoes of an Unfinished Past the Traveler sees a recording of Aila made by a blessing that records awareness, so that a later "brave warrior" can overhear. Aila says past and future are one for her, that she grieves nothing about Lovia, and that she must seal Lovia's sins in the Court of the First Edict. She meets a man called Solovei, tells him he will leave soon, and declares him the hero meant to protect "Elysium" in a prophecy-like speech. The Traveler is separately invited to look for those of her bloodline.

## entry: Maite Veliu and the Conch Gang
@keys: Maite, Maite Veliu, Conch Gang
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Maite Veliu is a young self-styled master thief who claims descent from the legendary thief Reed Miller and is a member of the Conch Gang. She talks big and gets scared easily, wants the Crab Tsar's treasure, and later pays back the Traveler with an IOU and notes. She mentions Katya in Nasha Town as the person the Traveler must settle bills with, and warns that the Frostmoon Scions are dangerous. Kasha's story of catching a girl called Mai fits her own account of being caught, though no quest says so outright.
