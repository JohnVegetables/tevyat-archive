## summary
@spoiler: minor
### LITE
Ancient Colors is a three-act Fontaine World Quest series added in version 4.0, listed in the Fontaine Reputation menu as "Seymour's Story: Prologue". The Traveler meets Mamere, a Melusine who paints, and her talking machine friend Seymour, helps repair him, and then discovers that a man named Jakob is trying to revive the buried heart of Mamere's "Father" beneath Merusea Village.
### STANDARD +
The acts are "A Brush of Seafoam and Crimson" (The Illusion's Finishings), "A Gradient of Dreams and Ochre" (There Will Come Soft Rains) and "Blue Longing of Heart and Moon" (Limner, Dreamer, and Robotic Dog). It has no listed prerequisite, begins near the Statue of the Seven at Elynas, and leads on to In the Wake of Narcissus by way of Initial Facts.
### LARGE +
The tone stays gentle in the first two acts, with comic Eremites and village errands, and turns serious in the last quest, where the Traveler is trapped in Jakob's plan and then speaks to the dead being whose body Merusea Village sits inside. The series introduces the Narzissenkreuz Ordo plot from Seymour's side, as Ann of the Narzissenkreuz does from Ann's.

## plot
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Act I, The Illusion's Finishings. In the wilds near the Statue of the Seven at Elynas the Traveler and Paimon find a Melusine named Mamere being harassed by Eremites (Irus, Kaykubad and Kaykaus) who want her paintings for Mora. The Traveler drives them off. Mamere thanks them by offering to paint for them and takes them to Merusea Village, the underground home of the Melusines. At her house they meet Seymour, her talking machine friend. She also says a gentle collector, Mr. Jakob, takes an interest in her art. The Traveler and Paimon help her gather "paint" nearby and see her prepare to paint.

Act II, There Will Come Soft Rains. Seymour has been damaged and Mamere's house ransacked. In the village the Melusine Serene gives the Traveler spare parts, saying the incident must be reported to Monsieur Neuvillette, and suggests asking villagers for more. After collecting four Strange Parts, the Traveler returns and Seymour is repaired. The mercenaries return, saying Jakob, whom they have not been paid by, made monsters appear; Virda of the Maison Gardiennage takes them to the Fortress of Meropide. The ground shakes, and Mamere, in pain, says her "Father" is suffering and begs the Traveler to take her back to where the paint was gathered.

Act III, Limner, Dreamer, and Robotic Dog. At a giant red stone, Jakob uses Abyssal power to restart its "heart" in order to gather its blood. He says he serves the Narzissenkreuz Ordo, not the Abyss Order, and that blood from the heart is what Mamere's "paint" really is. Jakob traps the Traveler in a barrier and calls rifthounds. Seymour holds them off, Jakob taunts him about failing to protect "Mary-Ann", and a Breacher Primus attacks the barrier and frees the Traveler. Jakob escapes. Mamere paints the Traveler and Paimon, and the Traveler is drawn into a pink space where Elynas, the being whose body this is, speaks to them. After talking, she thanks them for stopping her heart and asks them to pass a message to Mamere. Back in the world the stone is stone again and the quakes stop, and the Traveler delivers the message to Mamere and receives her painting.
### LARGE +
Details. Mamere does not need Mora and would have given a collector a painting as a gift if he had not mentioned Mora. In Act II the Eremites admit that Jakob hired them to take the paintings and never paid a deposit. Seymour talks only to Mamere at first, then answers the Traveler. Mamere says she found him lying in a pile of junk. Jakob appears as a cliff-top onlooker commenting on the beauty of the place.

In Act II the Traveler can do small errands for villagers to earn parts: Cosanzeana wants a flower garden and says she is learning to be a florist. The earthquake and monsters came after Jakob activated arrays in nearby ruins that opened the way to a realm of beasts, in his own words.
### ULTRA +
Elynas's account in Act III. She says she was brought into being by her "Mother", floated lonely in cosmic darkness, was given siblings, then left a "world beneath the earth" with them. She found that the things she enjoyed hurt others, fell in this place and died. Later, a battle among many people, a Hydro spirit and metal animals took place within her body; an explosion wounded her and, from those wounds, small life (the Melusines) grew. She taught them to build homes. Jakob and another visitor came long ago to collect her blood; she says Jakob has something of the cold cosmos within him. Her message to Mamere: that her paintings are beautiful, that she is loved and that she should get along with her sisters.

## revelations
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
- Mamere's "paint" is the congealed blood of Elynas, whose body Merusea Village lies in, as Jakob and Elynas both say.
- Elynas's heart can only be restarted by Abyssal power; if revived, her body would crush and digest the Melusines in it.
- Jakob serves the Narzissenkreuz Ordo, wants "equality" and "liberation of the will", and mentions a "world-formula" and a coming moment.
- Seymour is linked to a person named Mary-Ann, whom Jakob says he failed to defend.
### LARGE +
- Elynas can link to the Traveler's mind through her blood and says only two people have not feared it: the Traveler and Jakob.
- Her cells act like an immune system and attacked the barrier trapping the Traveler.

## characters
### STANDARD
The Traveler, Paimon, Mamere, Seymour, Jakob, Elynas (as a ghostly Melusine in the pink space), the Eremites Irus, Kaykubad and Kaykaus, Serene, Cosanzeana, Virda, and other Merusea Village Melusines.

## consequences
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Jakob escapes with enough blood for his plans, and the Traveler is left with Mamere's painting as a reward. The series ends with Seymour's story still open. Initial Facts brings Ann's and Seymour's storylines together, and In the Wake of Narcissus resolves Seymour's link to Mary-Ann. Rewards include Mamere's Painting and completion of "Seymour's Story: Prologue" in the Fontaine Reputation.

## placement
### STANDARD
Released in 4.0 with Fontaine. The wiki infobox lists In the Wake of Narcissus as next. It runs in parallel with Ann of the Narzissenkreuz, and the two prologues feed into Initial Facts.

## entry: Mamere
@keys: Mamere
@keys2: Seymour, Merusea Village, Melusine
@version: 4.0
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Mamere is a Melusine painter who lives in Merusea Village, has a quiet house away from the other Melusines, and does not want Mora for her paintings. She calls Seymour family and her best friend, and refers to Elynas as her Father.

## entry: Seymour
@keys: Seymour
@keys2: Mamere, Merusea Village, Mary-Ann
@version: 4.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Seymour is a talking machine found by Mamere in a junk pile. At first he speaks only to her, uses clipped, logical language and calls the Traveler by "Mr./Ms." He is damaged in Act II and repaired with four Strange Parts. Jakob says he failed to defend someone named Mary-Ann, a name that surprises Seymour.

## entry: Elynas and the heart
@keys: Elynas, Breacher Primus, the giant heart
@keys2: Mamere, Jakob, Merusea Village
@version: 4.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Elynas is the dead being whose body holds Merusea Village. Jakob restarts her heart to take blood; the Traveler stops it, and Elynas thanks them because reviving would harm her children. In the pink space she shows herself as a ghostly Melusine and asks them to tell Mamere her work is lovely.

## entry: Jakob in Ancient Colors
@keys: Jakob
@keys2: Mamere, Eremites, Merusea Village
@version: 4.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Jakob first appears as a polite art collector; later he sends the Eremites for Mamere's paintings, restarts the heart with Abyssal power and traps the Traveler. He insists he is not of the Abyss Order, and says the Narzissenkreuz Ordo seeks equality and the liberation of will. He uses Abyssal power to reverse the crystallisation.
