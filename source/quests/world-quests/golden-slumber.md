## summary
### LITE
A four-quest Great Red Sand world quest series from Version 3.1. The Traveler and Paimon find a lost research party in the desert: the scholar Tirzad, the merchant's associate Nachtigal, and the Eremite mercenaries Jebrael and his daughter Jeht. Exploring King Deshret's ruins with the machine Benben, they are hunted by the Eremite leader Samail, and Jebrael gives himself to the Golden Slumber to stop him.
### STANDARD +
The series examines the gap between the Akademiya's picture of King Deshret as a tyrant and the faith his people had in him. It centres on Jebrael's secret past in the Eremite group Thutmose, the murder of Jeht's mother, and the Golden Slumber, a collective dreamland within the king's throne room. It leads straight into The Dirge of Bilqis, which continues Jeht's story.
### LARGE +
The quest requires only the Version 1.0 prologue quest The Outlander Who Caught the Wind, not any Sumeru Archon Quest. The series is the main window in the world quests onto the Eremites' pursuit of King Deshret's treasures and onto how those who worshipped him remember him.

## plot
### LITE
Looking for Tirzad's missing party, the Traveler finds the group in a sinkhole and works with them to escape through ruins. They find a strange machine, Benben, which opens the sealed doors of the Khemenu Temple and a mural-hidden passage to the Mausoleum of King Deshret. Fatui and Eremites under Samail capture Benben, but the group recovers it. Samail pursues the Scarlet Sand Slate and Benben to the throne at Khaj-Nisut, where Jebrael enters the Golden Slumber to free the others and close the door.
### STANDARD +
Bonifaz, a Mondstadt merchant at Aaru Village, rented two Sumpter Beasts to the scholar Tirzad, who studies desert ruins with Nachtigal and two hired mercenaries. Contact stopped two days earlier, so the Traveler searches and finds the party at the bottom of a sinkhole with Jebrael and Jeht. Together they escape through a ruin, where Jeht finds the machine Benben, which makes Tirzad's Scarlet Sand Slate light up. Benben opens the Khemenu Temple and a mural-hidden passage to the Mausoleum of King Deshret, where a trap separates the group. Fatui and Eremites led by Samail take Benben; the group recovers it and Samail flees. At Khaj-Nisut, Samail uses Jeht as a hostage to force Jebrael to speak the prayer that opens the Golden Slumber. Samail enters and gains control of the building, so Jebrael follows to free the others and fades into the Slumber as the door seals. Jeht says she will go back to her father's original tribe, the Tanit.
### LARGE +
Lost in the Sands: Contact by Dusk Bird stopped before the commission was posted. Jebrael is standoffish; Jeht welcomes help. Nachtigal has heatstroke from the fall; Tirzad is drunk after Jeht tells him Bonifaz and Nachtigal overcharged him for the Sumpter Beasts. In the ruin Tirzad says he owns the Scarlet Sand Slate because a relative abandoned her research to elope with a man she met in the desert. Jebrael criticises Tirzad's remark about the Akademiya's view of King Deshret so harshly that Tirzad faints in anger. Jeht names the machine Benben, and Tirzad entrusts the Slate to the Traveler.

An Introduction to Indoor Archaeology: At the oasis Jebrael tells the Traveler to keep the Slate safe and says he left the desert for a better path than seclusion. A mural of King Deshret leads Tirzad to call him a tyrant over insignificant subjects; Jebrael answers that Tirzad only repeats what the Akademiya says and knows nothing of the faith the people had in the king. Tirzad bumps the mural and a secret passage opens.

The Secret of Al-Ahmar: In the Mausoleum Jebrael says Ufairah would have loved to see it. The trapped door sends the Traveler to an unfamiliar ruin where Fatui and Eremites hold Benben. Samail knows Jebrael from Thutmose. Samail escapes the fight but leaves Benben, and Jeht and Tirzad demand the truth from Jebrael. The group then returns to the oasis.

Dreams Beneath the Searing Sand: The group rescues Nachtigal from Eremites and moves camp. At Khaj-Nisut the party passes three trials to reveal the whole city and reaches the throne room. Samail says Jebrael led them there only to enter the Golden Slumber; Jebrael warns it is a one-way trip. Inside, Samail says everyone joins a collective consciousness and loses individuality and urges Jebrael to join; Jeht's and the others' voices bring him back and he opens the door. He asks Jeht's forgiveness as he fades. Back at Aaru Village, Tirzad leaves to write his paper, and Bonifaz and Nachtigal stay. Jebrael had promised Tirzad to remain his bodyguard and help find King Deshret's secrets, something he and his wife had wanted to see together.
### ULTRA +
Small points. The commission can begin either by accepting Katheryne's Sumeru commission or by speaking to Bonifaz in Aaru Village. Tirzad's mercenaries are hired men separate from Jebrael and Jeht. Samail's leadership of the Eremites and his involvement with the Fatui is stated plainly in the Secret of Al-Ahmar quest. The series contrasts how the Akademiya teaches King Deshret with Jebrael's view of the faith the king's people had in him, and leaves the king's real nature unresolved.

## revelations
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Jebrael was once a member of the Eremite group Thutmose and used his knowledge for it. Jeht's mother Ufairah, a researcher, was valuable to Samail for her knowledge of the desert and King Deshret. After Jeht was born, Jebrael began to doubt the morality of Thutmose's missions and planned to leave with his family, but Samail found out and killed Ufairah to make him stay. Samail was after King Deshret's treasures using Benben and the Scarlet Sand Slate, and the Fatui were working with the Eremites.
### LARGE +
The Golden Slumber is described as a dreamland of King Deshret's knowledge, entered only through a prayer known to Jebrael, and entrants may never come back. The Akademiya's image of King Deshret as a tyrant is presented as secondhand opinion, countered by Jebrael's account of the faith his people had in him.

## characters
### LITE
The Traveler, Paimon, Jeht, Jebrael, Tirzad, Nachtigal, Bonifaz, Samail and Benben.
### STANDARD +
Jebrael is a taciturn former Eremite who works as a mercenary; Jeht is his friendly daughter who later returns to the Tanit; Tirzad is a scholar with Akademiya assumptions; Nachtigal is Bonifaz's associate; Bonifaz is a Mondstadt merchant in Aaru Village; Samail is the Eremite leader and villain; Benben is the machine that opens the ruins. Katheryne issues the commission.

## consequences
### STANDARD
Jebrael is lost to the Golden Slumber, and Samail is also taken into it. Jeht goes back to the Tanit and the story continues in The Dirge of Bilqis, where she buries her parents' remains in the Eternal Oasis. Tirzad writes his paper on King Deshret's ruins.

## placement
### STANDARD
Released in Version 3.1 and set in the Great Red Sand. Requires only The Outlander Who Caught the Wind. The Dirge of Bilqis (3.4) can begin a Daily Reset after finishing Golden Slumber; Old Notes and New Friends is the other listed follow-up.
