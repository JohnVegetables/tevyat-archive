## summary
@spoiler: minor
### LITE
In the Wake of Narcissus is a four-act Fontaine World Quest series added in version 4.2. It concludes the Narzissenkreuz storyline begun in Ann of the Narzissenkreuz and Ancient Colors. The Traveler, Paimon, Ann, Seymour and a hilichurl-bodied scholar called Caterpillar pursue the Narzissenkreuz Ordo, trace what happened to Mary-Ann, and reach the founder in the Primordial Sea.
### STANDARD +
The acts are Search in the Algae Sea, Savior's Wake, Waking from the Great Dream and Rowboat's Wake. The series is entered through the quest Initial Facts and its reward track is "In the Wake of Narcissus" in the Fontaine Reputation.
### LARGE +
It is the quiet end of a storyline that began as a fairy tale: the "Narzissenkreuz Adventure Team", the "Holy Blade" and the "Princess" are shown to come from an orphanage-turned-cult, and the series chooses between the story being a delusion and the people inside it being real.

## plot
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Act I, Search in the Algae Sea. The group deduce who Mary-Ann in Annapausis is, find the pieces of the Holy Blade in the Morte Region, and fight Jakob at the Tower of Gestalt, who dies when the Doomsday Clock strikes.

Act II, Savior's Wake. They descend and sink the Tower of Ipsissimus beneath the Ordo, leaving Caterpillar and Seymour behind.

Act III, Waking from the Great Dream. In the Primordial Sea the Traveler defeats Narzissenkreuz, and Ann forgives him.

Act IV, Rowboat's Wake. With Narzissenkreuz as guide they reach Mary-Ann in Annapausis, who chooses to live as herself; he fades, Seymour returns to Mamere and Caterpillar leaves.
### LARGE +
Act I, Search in the Algae Sea. At the Narzissenkreuz Ordo the group compare notes. They reason that the Mary-Ann in the Annapausis is the Oceanid Lyris, former director of the Narzissenkreuz Institute orphanage, and that Ann came from her dream. In Annapausis they fight Al and Jak, who are under Mary-Ann's control, but cannot open the door to the real Annapausis; Mary-Ann's thoughts are heard instead. Caterpillar explains Narzissenkreuz and the Ordo's goals and proposes to find the real Holy Blade. In the Morte Region they find that three seals were already broken and Jakob has the elements, so they get the fourth, the Persona. They are carried to the Tower of Gestalt, where Jakob demands the Persona so Narzissenkreuz can obtain the Holy Blade and "save the world". After a battle he gives up the missing elements; the Holy Blade is forged. Ann refuses to absorb his persona and memories. The Doomsday Clock strikes twelve and crushes Jakob, who says Narzissenkreuz is returning.

Act II, Savior's Wake. The group descends the Tower of Ipsissimus beneath the Ordo, reading notes, an investigation log and a storybook with margin comments. They find a key, deal with dormant Gardemek meka, and activate a mechanism that sinks the tower to the seabed. Caterpillar and Seymour are held back; the Traveler, Paimon and Ann go on.

Act III, Waking from the Great Dream. In the "Genesis of a Certain Micro-Universe" in the Primordial Sea, the Traveler faces Narzissenkreuz, who offers to save as many as he can and demands the Holy Blade. He is defeated; he says he sensed changes in the "world-formula", calls the Traveler the person "equal to a world", and says the Holy Blade cut through the will around him. Ann forgives him. He admits his plan failed and says he wishes to return to the time before the Narzissenkreuz heroes set off.

Act IV, Rowboat's Wake. They return to the Ordo, where Caterpillar meets him and addresses him as "Master" with little warmth. Narzissenkreuz offers to guide them to the Kingdom Through the Looking-Glass. In Annapausis, Ann reshapes the world; they dodge Mary-Ann's bubble defences and break the "Will to Resist" with the Holy Blade. Narzissenkreuz is addressed by Mary-Ann as Rene. After a conversation she chooses to live as herself, and the Holy Blade loses its meaning. Narzissenkreuz fades after a talk with Caterpillar and Ann. Seymour is freed from his old orders, and returns to Mamere with Mary-Ann's blessing. Caterpillar leaves on his own path.
Act I details. Seymour's files on Lyris show she was the director of the Narzissenkreuz Institute, which the Traveler links to Mary-Ann. The group discuss Annapausis as the work of an Oceanid. Jakob says the Ordo's nodes are a response to the apocalypse and that the Seal of Chymical Marriage was an ancient Fontainian ritual to seal the source of life, that the five nodes still working are only part of the plan, and that "Rene" will return.

Act II details. The tower's theme is bells and gears, according to Caterpillar. The investigation log describes archaeological research on temple ruins and mentions a copy kept at the Institute of Natural Philosophy and the deciphering of Remurian script. Seymour estimates that 60% of the dormant Gardemeks will activate. After they sink the tower, Seymour rates his own survival chance at 100%.

Act III details. Narzissenkreuz says Director Lyris holds on to Mary-Ann and hopes only to retain the past. He addresses Ann as "Oceanid Ann" and asks if she would inherit his work; she declines. If the Traveler has completed Masquerade of the Guilty, his lines say the world "has been saved" rather than "is still fated to be saved".
### ULTRA +
Act IV details. Caterpillar and Seymour wait at the Ordo while the Traveler, Paimon, Ann and Narzissenkreuz go to Annapausis. Narzissenkreuz tells Ann to imagine the place without obstacles; Ann drains the water. At the barrier Mary-Ann begs them to go away: she wants to decay in peace. After the Will to Resist breaks, Ann shows her a treasure image; Mary-Ann says she cannot forgive "Narzissenkreuz" but can forgive "Rene". Narzissenkreuz suggests that Ann could merge with her or the Holy Blade could separate Lyris and the young Mary-Ann, but she refuses, accepting her past. In the Primordial Sea, Narzissenkreuz says he spent ten years holding on to his will, then built a universe within himself, and that Mary-Ann was right that it is warm there. The Sword of Narzissenkreuz is obtained as a reward.

Seymour, whose designer was Alain Guillotin, tells Mary-Ann his components were damaged at Elynas and have not been maintained. She tells him to follow what he wants, not orders, and he decides to return to Mamere. Mary-Ann and Ann plan to travel the world together.

## revelations
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
- The Mary-Ann of Annapausis is the Oceanid Lyris merged with Mary-Ann Guillotin; the Narzissenkreuz Institute was an orphanage run by the Oceanid Lyris.
- Narzissenkreuz is Rene, one of the circle around the Institute (Jakob lists Alain, Rene, Director Lyris, Mary-Ann and himself), who became the Ordo's master and tried to save the world from the apocalypse foretold in the prophecy.
- Ann is a being born from Mary-Ann's dream (Caterpillar's and the group's deduction, confirmed by Mary-Ann and Narzissenkreuz), and the adventure-team members are Oceanid-born shapes of that dream.
- The Holy Blade of Narzissenkreuz can be any object of significance, and it can separate a will from water.
- Jakob dies when the Doomsday Clock strikes, after reversing the Seal of Chymical Marriage.
### LARGE +
- Caterpillar addresses Narzissenkreuz as "Master", resents how he was brought into being, and says he has long since seen the absurdities in the Master's theories.
- Jakob says the Ordo's aim is to unite all wills as one in a new world, to combat the apocalypse.
- The Persona is one of four elements required to forge the Holy Blade.

## characters
### STANDARD
The Traveler, Paimon, Ann, Seymour, Caterpillar, Jakob, Al, Jak, Narzissenkreuz (Rene), Mary-Ann, and Mamere. Lyris and Alain Guillotin are discussed. Petit Chou and Al are mentioned as friends Ann may never see again.

## consequences
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Jakob is dead, the Ordo's plan is broken and Narzissenkreuz fades in the Primordial Sea. Mary-Ann chooses to leave Annapausis and live as herself with Ann; Seymour returns to Mamere and Caterpillar goes off alone. The Traveler receives the Sword of Narzissenkreuz and the series' reward track.
### LARGE +
The ending leaves open what the Ordo's remaining nodes mean and what Narzissenkreuz meant by the world-formula; these are not answered in this series.

## placement
### STANDARD
Released in 4.2. Requires Initial Facts, which the wiki places after Mary-Ann's Story, Limner, Dreamer, and Robotic Dog and the Unfinished Comedy series. It is the last of the Ann/Seymour/Narzissenkreuz arcs.

### STANDARD

In Rowboat's Wake Mary-Ann calls him Rene. He spends the last of his power helping Ann and Mary-Ann and then asks to be left in the Genesis of a Certain Micro-Universe.

In Search in the Algae Sea the Traveler fights Jakob at the Tower of Gestalt. After the Holy Blade is forged he says he has reversed the Seal of Chymical Marriage and predicts Rene's return. The Doomsday Clock then strikes twelve and crushes him.

## entry: Caterpillar
@keys: Caterpillar
@keys2: Lanoire, Narzissenkreuz, Seymour
@version: 4.2
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Caterpillar is a hilichurl-bodied scholar who helps the Traveler and Ann with deductions, jokes dryly, and counts himself among the "things that used to be human". He calls Narzissenkreuz "Master" but disagrees with his theories. He says he owes Lanoire the rest of a story, and at the end sets off alone.

## entry: Mary-Ann (the Annapausis)
@keys: Mary-Ann Guillotin, Princess Lyris, Annapausis
@keys2: Ann, Seymour, Rene
@version: 4.2
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Mary-Ann in Annapausis is the fused form of the Oceanid Lyris and the human Mary-Ann Guillotin. She keeps the dream-world warm and tries to push visitors away, but accepts her past when Ann shows her a treasure image and when Rene appears. Afterwards she decides to live as herself and travel with Ann.

Seymour is Alain Guillotin's creation, first met in Ancient Colors. In Rowboat's Wake Mary-Ann tells him his old orders are void and asks what he wants to do. He decides to return to Mamere, who is waiting in Merusea Village and plans an art trip with him.
