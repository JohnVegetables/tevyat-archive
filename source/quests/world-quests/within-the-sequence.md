## summary
### LITE
A very short Version 6.7 world quest at Nuur Stone Circle on the Frost Moon. When the Ancient Moon's Remnant there reaches level 5, the Traveler examines an ancient terminal that greets them with the titles of old Natlan rulers.
### STANDARD +
It follows Origin of the Sequence, which repaired the terminal. It has one step, no combat and no named NPC. The wiki treats the name as unofficial.
### LARGE +
Its only point is atmosphere and a small mystery: why the machine addresses the visitor like Natlantean royalty, and how many cycles have passed.

## plot
### LITE
The Traveler raises the Ancient Moon's Remnant to level 5 and then examines the terminal. It runs a self-diagnostic, reports that lockdown is lifted and says an unreadable number of cycles has passed.
### STANDARD +
The terminal's greeting varies. Variants welcome the Master of Flame and Wisdom, Xiuhcoatl, and his companion, the Sovereign Ruler of Natlantea, either Ix Raq'lapuj Chue'q Buluk or Ix Uxul Tz'ib Bolon, or greet only one of them. It then gives a general welcome to "friends from afar" to its data array.
### LARGE +
In Origin of the Sequence the terminal could not identify visitors and asked that Nin-Kiggia be contacted for maintenance. Here it says the lockdown is lifted. The elapsed-time count displays as "ERROR".

## revelations
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
The quest answers nothing. It shows that this storage array, labelled No. 59 Ia-Ilimmu in the prelude, recognises Xiuhcoatl and Natlantean sovereigns by title, a link between the Frost Moon works and Natlantea that the quest does not explain.

## characters
### LITE
The Traveler only; the ancient terminal speaks. Xiuhcoatl and the Natlantean sovereigns are named but absent.

## consequences
### STANDARD
Completing the quest unlocks the achievement The Inner Light. No other change to the world is recorded.

## placement
### STANDARD
Released in Version 6.7 (Luna VIII) as Frost Moon content. It follows Origin of the Sequence and requires the Ancient Moon's Remnant at Nuur Stone Circle to be raised to level 5.
### ULTRA +
The quest infobox gives the start location as Nuur Stone Circle in the Lunar Highlands of the Frost Moon. The prelude quest rewards 350 Adventure EXP, 40 Primogems and 30,000 Mora and unlocks the hidden objective Fragments of Ia-Ilimmu; this quest itself lists no item rewards.
