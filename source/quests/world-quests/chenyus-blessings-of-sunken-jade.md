## summary
### LITE
A Version 4.4 world quest series set in Chenyu Vale, Liyue. The Traveler meets the adeptus Fujin, who says the vale's water and soil are declining, which endangers Qiaoying Village's tea. They gather her scattered power, learn that the beast Lingyuan has been disrupting the land, and see the Rainjade Rite completed.
### STANDARD +
The series has three quests: Qiaoying of the Sacred Mountain, Floating Jade, Treasure of Chenyu, and An Ancient Sacrifice of Sacred Brocade. It requires The Outlander Who Caught the Wind and entering Chenyu Vale. It ends with the village promised a fresh tea batch for the coming Lantern Rite.
### LARGE +
It doubles as local history: the mural, the boatman and Little Mao give differing tellings of how the Jademouth ring came to lie in the river, and the old friendship of Fujin and Lingyuan explains why the land is contested between a nurturing adeptus and a beast who remembers a wilder vale.

## plot
### LITE
Fujin pulls the Traveler into an adepti realm and says Chenyu Vale is declining. In Qiaoying Village tea quality has been dropping. The Traveler seeks a technician, follows a golden carp (Fujin), explores a shrine with Little Mao, gathers Fujin's power from carp across the vale, and faces Lingyuan, who admits she caused the disruption. The Rainjade Rite is then performed.
### STANDARD +
Grandpa Lu mistakes the Traveler for Feiyun Commerce Guild envoys and blames the soil and water for worse tea. Uncle Luo blames a tea machine and scolds a worker, Jin. A taste test of cakes from an old batch and a new batch shows the new one tastes off. Luo sends for a Fontainian technician at Yilong Wharf, Hautemontagne, whom the Traveler finds with Little Mao. Fujin, appearing as a golden carp, confirms Lu is right: if the decline continues the village could be abandoned and the vale return to a barren state. A beast watches the party unseen. Mao leads the Traveler to a mural of an old war, then to the Jademouth, and a boatman named Bosu gives his account of how it got there. In the shrine caverns, the Traveler meets a Solitary Suanni nicknamed Bluey, who says the new tea leaves were better before and then vanishes.
### LARGE +
Bluey turns out to be Lingyuan. Fujin needs her power back to perform the rite, and that power took the form of golden carp around the vale; the Traveler finds them all. Lingyuan interrupts, says she has the situation under control, warns Fujin away and leaves. Fujin is shocked that her former friend would unbalance the land: Lingyuan's disruption of the spirit veins let the miasma spread, and with full control of the mountain she could reshape the vale. After a fight, Lingyuan explains that as a beast she remembers everything while humans forget; the tea trees Fujin planted gave a taste she recalls and cannot find today. After Fujin and the white snake left, she wanted to return the vale to its original state, seeing it as subdued by humanity. Satisfied with the answers, she lets Fujin restore the vale. The effort sends Fujin back to her carp form to recover. Lingyuan wonders how the Traveler brought Fujin back, thanks them, and cannot yet say what she thinks of humans. Lu and Luo thank the Traveler and promise a fresh batch for the Lantern Rite.
### ULTRA +
Details of the middle quest: Little Mao contradicts Bosu's telling of the Jademouth. In Mao's version a bad god once tried to redirect the river to drown the Millelith, and a giant white snake and a carp disobeyed her orders, with the carp throwing jade into the river to pin it down and stop the flood. The mural shows a great war between two factions whose bloodshed turned the Bishui River red. Mao says the Jademouth was presumably thrown by an adeptus. The shrine is solved through puzzles after driving out Treasure Hoarders. The three tellings are not reconciled in the quest.

## revelations
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Lingyuan, not the tea machine, is behind the soil and water problems. Fujin and Lingyuan were once friends. A beast remembers what humans forget, and that memory motivated Lingyuan's wish to restore the vale's old state.

## characters
### LITE
The Traveler and Paimon; Fujin (adeptus, golden carp); Lingyuan (a beast called Bluey); villagers Grandpa Lu, Uncle Luo and worker Jin; Fontainian technician Hautemontagne; Little Mao; boatman Bosu.
### STANDARD +
Lu is the village elder who knows the land's history; Luo runs the tea workshop and is brusque with workers; Little Mao guides the Traveler through the Rainjade Rite and its sites.

## consequences
### STANDARD
The vale's balance is restored, Qiaoying Village's tea can recover, and the villagers say they will help the Traveler in future. Fujin is left to recover as a carp, and Lingyuan remains in the vale, unanswered on humanity.

## placement
### STANDARD
Released in Version 4.4 with Chenyu Vale. It requires The Outlander Who Caught the Wind. Its quests run in this order: Qiaoying of the Sacred Mountain, Floating Jade, Treasure of Chenyu, and An Ancient Sacrifice of Sacred Brocade.

## entry: Rainjade Rite
@keys: Rainjade Rite, Sunken Jade, Chenyu Vale rite
@version: 4.4
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
The Rainjade Rite is the ritual Fujin says is needed to drive the miasma out of Chenyu Vale. She does not explain how until the Traveler reaches the site. It requires her power, scattered as golden carp across the vale, and is performed after Lingyuan agrees not to stop it.

## entry: Lingyuan and Fujin
@keys: Bluey, Solitary Suanni, Lingyuan, Fujin
@keys2: Chenyu, Qiaoying, Rainjade
@version: 4.4
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
Fujin is the adeptus who made Chenyu Vale inhabitable and planted its tea trees. Lingyuan is a beast who disrupted the spirit veins because she remembered the vale's old taste and wanted to undo human influence. Their friendship and its break drive the series.
