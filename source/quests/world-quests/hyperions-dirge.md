## summary
### LITE
A version 2.4 world quest series in Enkanomiya. A proud shade who calls himself Aberaku, Helios-Forger, says he built the Dainichi Mikoshi, the solar chariot that lit the sunless land, yet was imprisoned and his soul scattered. The Traveler recovers three fragments of it so that he can rest.
### STANDARD +
The fragments lie at the Narrows, the Evernight Temple and the Serpent's Heart. Once Aberaku is whole he remembers everything and tells how the light of the Dainichi Mikoshi drove off the Dragonheirs of the Depths, how nobles then used it to seize power through puppet Sunchildren, and how he was jailed for opposing that. As thanks he gives the Traveler the Divine Bridle, a badge of the Sunchild's office.
### LARGE +
The series is a bitter portrait of a creator rewarded with prison. It is the main source for how the Sunchild system was kept in place by burning each Sunchild at a set age.

## plot
### LITE
Aberaku introduces himself theatrically. Paimon asks about the Helios, and he explains it is the Dainichi Mikoshi. He asks the Traveler to find three pieces of his shattered soul.
### STANDARD +
At the Narrows he speaks of how his creation brought joy and saved the nation from the Bathysmal Vishaps and wonders why he cannot rest; his bones are enshrined here and monsters hold the fragment. At the Evernight Temple he says he was jailed on a child's orders but bears the child no hate, since the Sunchildren were only puppets, and that he was held in a high, dark, ice-cold place; his body and clothes were enshrined in several places to honour his work, which split his soul. The Traveler recovers his sacred crown. At the Serpent's Heart he says he was betrayed and left to rot after the Helios was made, and the Traveler wins back his sacred robes. Back with Aberaku, now whole, he tells the full story.
### LARGE +
The series can be started with any one of its four quests. The introductory quest gives optional dialogue and is meant to be taken first, the three inquiries can run in any order and at once, and Hyperion's Dirge itself is finished last. In the Dainichi Mikoshi, Aberaku is first found in a hidden room under the central waypoint; afterwards he moves to the highest point of the structure.

## revelations
@version: 2.4
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Byakuyakoku was terrorised by the Dragonheirs of the Depths until the light of the Dainichi Mikoshi drove them off. When peace came, greedy nobles led the people to worship the Mikoshi and proclaimed the rule of the Sunchild over the land, using the child as a puppet. Aberaku opposed this, was accused of high treason by the Sunchildren and imprisoned.
### LARGE +
To stop the Sunchildren from realising they were manipulated, each was incinerated in a ceremony once they reached a certain age, as Aberaku confirms when Paimon wonders about this. The wiki profile, drawing on The Byakuyakoku Collection, names the Sunchild who imprisoned him as Rikoru.

## characters
### LITE
The Traveler and Paimon; Aberaku, a shade.
### STANDARD +
Aberaku's original name is Abrax; his title is Helios-Forger. The wiki profile also calls him a sage enlightened by the Tokoyo Ookami. The Sunchildren are mentioned but not met.

### ULTRA +
The wiki notes that Abrax likely derives from Abraxas, a figure in Gnostic tradition; this is an outside observation. The inquiries require unsealing the Key Sigil seals before Aberaku appears at each location.

## consequences
### STANDARD
Aberaku's soul is whole and he can rest, though his closing remark afterwards is that all his talent and ability came to nothing. The Traveler keeps the Divine Bridle.

## placement
### STANDARD
Released in Version 2.4 with Enkanomiya as a world quest series. Like Erebos' Secret, it requires From Dusk to Dawn in Byakuyakoku, so either can be done first.

## entry: Aberaku
@keys: Aberaku, Abrax, Helios-Forger
@version: 2.4
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Aberaku no Mikoto, born Abrax, is the creator of the Dainichi Mikoshi (the Helios) in Enkanomiya. He was imprisoned by the Sunchild and died; his memories remain as a shade. The wiki profile says he lived about 6,000 years ago, was a pupil of Istoria, and travelled to Enkanomiya (then Delphi Pytho) shortly before the war of funerary flame, becoming trapped there.
