## summary
### LITE
Mean Streets of the Despicable is a two-quest world series added in 7.0 in Everfrozen Earth, centred on Snezhnograd. The Traveler and Paimon side with the fae gang Luchkin against the larger Izmaylov gang, whose leader, the "Young Sire" Vasiliy, is collecting debts by force to fund a machine that would open a Shadow Realm. The pair steal a sample of his cargo from a train, rescue a captured Luchkin spy, and stop Vasiliy in the Train Boneyard, where he fails and the place begins to collapse.
### STANDARD +
Parts: Burn the Storehouses and the Stables, then Disputes Without Honor and Humanity, which starts immediately afterwards. The series is a crime story about snow-country gangs, not a fight against the Fatui. The Luchkin gang's reward includes a refined Kresnik Kristall "key" and a treasure map.

## plot
### LITE
The Traveler beats an Izmaylov debt collector in a duel to spare a girl named Marfa, and the Luchkin leader Zmei explains that the Izmaylovs are secretly moving materials for a Shadow Realm device. The Traveler follows a small Volkodlak named Lorie onto a freight train, takes a sample and escapes by sled. In the second quest, the Traveler rescues the Luchkin agent Gureva from an Izmaylov stronghold, then defeats Vasiliy in the Train Boneyard and flees the collapse.
### STANDARD +
In Burn the Storehouses and the Stables, Izmaylov collector Lindenovich presses Marfa to repay her father's gambling debt. Luchkin member Vseslav challenges him under the gangs' dueling rules; the Traveler fights as proxy and wins, buying Marfa two days. Sofya brings the Traveler to Zmei, who says the Izmaylovs are renting train cars to move materials that could build a Shadow Realm device. The Traveler follows a Volkodlak named Lorie to a freight car, takes a sample, fights him on the roof and escapes onto Sofya's sled.

In the second quest, the Luchkin learn the Young Sire is refining Kresnik Kristall as a "key" to the machine. The Izmaylov stronghold used to be a Fatui military base. Gureva, held for three days by the Young Sire's second-in-command, "the Butcher", has already used the stronghold to learn of the train shipments. Once freed, she insists on dealing with the Butcher herself: the Traveler hides outside while she overpowers him in his own room, then takes a box he kept and leads the party out by a passage behind his room.

The Luchkin trace the plan to Vasiliy, whom Zmei remembers as Vasya, a childhood friend and fellow adoptee of the Old Sire. After the Old Sire's death some months earlier, punishments among the gangs rose and the Izmaylovs spent heavily. Gureva says Vasiliy wanted to prove fae gangs could match the Energy Commission and win a seat at the Snezhnograd Veche. The Train Boneyard is a half-excavated tunnel, made a dumping ground for old trains after an earlier Shadow Realm accident collapsed it, and the Luchkin hire a train and enter with the password "Oedipus".
### LARGE +
Vasiliy answers the Luchkin with a bitter speech: fae gangs are used for jobs the Druzhna and Energy Commission will not do, the High Table is a pretence, and the Law is "a leash". He admits he killed the Old Sire after the old man refused to hand over the Shadow Realm work, saying "the mission is complete". He sends Izmaylovs against the party, then fights the Traveler himself in the unstable Shadow Realm. Defeated, he says it is dark and that he cannot see; he remembers a childhood game in which he hid so deep he could not find his way out, and Zmei recalls that he and Iryna found him. He asks to be taken away. The device shuts down and the tunnel begins to collapse, so the party escapes by mine cart. Zmei later says it could have toppled the High Table if Vasiliy had succeeded.

Afterwards the Luchkin leave the Izmaylov matter to the High Table. In Zolnyy, Zmei gives the Traveler the refined Kresnik Kristall block (the "key", now just a block of energy) and a treasure map from the Butcher's box that points to the "legendary thief's treasure" of an old rumour about gang-robbing thieves.

## revelations
### STANDARD
Vasiliy killed the Old Sire (his own account). The Izmaylov gang, the richest of the fae gangs, had set up a Shadow Realm machine and was refining Kresnik Kristall to power it, using Energy Commission leaks obtained through the Old Sire's contacts. Zmei himself entered a Shadow Realm as a child and does not like to talk about it.
### LARGE +
Vasiliy's speech also says the Old Sire had once been at Hvergelmir (as "someone's dog") and later led the Energy Commission and the High Table. These claims are Vasiliy's, not independently stated by the narration. Rival gangs in the series: Luchkin (Zmei, Iryna, Sofya, Gureva) is the fae gang that keeps the "rules"; Izmaylov (Vasiliy, Lindenovich, Lorie, the Butcher) is the biggest; Remusov, led by Lyubov (a childhood friend sent to Novokitezhgrad), is mentioned.

## characters
### LITE
Zmei Luchkin (Luchkin leader), Iryna, Sofya and Gureva of the Luchkin; Vasiliy, the Young Sire of the Izmaylovs; the Traveler and Paimon.
### STANDARD +
Others: Iryna Nikolayevna Voronina enforces the gang's rules; Sofya Pavlovna Zaitseva is a Volkodlak; Vseslav and Marfa (a girl in debt); the Izmaylov men Lindenovich, Lorie, Kirov, Meselson and Ishogu; and the Butcher. Zmei is calm, gentle and philosophical; Iryna is curt but warm; Sofya is impulsive, loyal and fond of her tail; Gureva is cheerful, witty and ruthless to her enemies. Vasiliy is shown as bitter and wounded by being overlooked, not as an ordinary thug.

## consequences
### STANDARD
The Shadow Realm gate fails and the machine is destroyed. The Izmaylov gang is left leaderless and the High Table decides its fate. The Traveler is given a refined Kresnik Kristall block and a treasure map.
### LARGE +
Zmei says the "key" had a use only while the machine existed. The Alyosha storyline (A Thread of Dawn-Light) is referenced optionally in dialogue, where Paimon thinks the block might be what Alyosha was looking for; this depends on the player's progress in that quest.

## placement
### STANDARD
Both quests released in 7.0 in Everfrozen Earth. Burn the Storehouses and the Stables starts near Snezhnograd's western waypoint, north of the Central Station; Disputes Without Honor and Humanity begins immediately after it. The dialogue refers back to the Traveler's earlier visit to a Shadow Realm with Alyosha.

## entry: Vasiliy, the Young Sire

@keys2: Izmaylov, Luchkin, Zmei, Snezhnograd
@version: 7.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Vasiliy Nikolayevich Izmaylov, called the Young Sire or Vasya, led the Izmaylov gang after the Old Sire's death. He grew up alongside Zmei, Iryna and Lyubov as adoptees of the Old Sire. He resented the fae gangs being treated as dogs by powerful people and tried to open the Shadow Realm to force them to give him a seat. In the Train Boneyard he is defeated.

## entry: Zmei and the Luchkin
@keys: Zmei, Luchkin, Iryna, Sofya, Gureva
@keys2: Snezhnograd, Izmaylov, High Table
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
Zmei Luchkin is the human leader of the Luchkin gang, a mostly fae gang that sees itself as a defender of the people and follows the Law's first line: do not act without mercy or justice. Its members include Iryna (enforcement), Sofya (impulsive Volkodlak), Gureva (infiltrator) and Vseslav. Zmei says justice is not worth Mora, which is why it stays clean.

## entry: Rules of the gangs
@keys: Old Sire, High Table rules, Fae Gang Federation, Law of the gangs
@keys2: Luchkin, Izmaylov, Remusov
@version: 7.0
### ULTRA
Zmei describes the Law as beginning with "do not commit acts devoid of mercy or justice"; Lindenovich and Vseslav invoke the rule that gang disputes may be settled by a sword duel using only the weapons in hand, which is why the Traveler may stand in as a proxy. The Old Sire taught his adopted children (Zmei, Iryna, Vasiliy, Lyubov) to rely on their own strength and gave each a gang and a surname. Zmei also mentions the Fae Gang Federation's High Table as the body that will decide the Izmaylovs' fate.

## entry: Train Boneyard
@keys: Train Boneyard, Oedipus
@keys2: Izmaylov, Shadow Realm, Snezhnograd
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
A half-excavated tunnel turned dumping ground for abandoned trains after an earlier Shadow Realm accident halted its construction. Zmei, Iryna and the Young Sire played hide-and-seek there as children. The Izmaylov gang used it for the Shadow Realm machine; the password was "Oedipus".
