## summary
### LITE
Between Pledge and Forgettance is a Natlan world quest series made of Lost in the Woods, Rite of the Bold and Revelations from the Past. The Traveler and Paimon rescue a young marked saurian (called Little One by default), learn that its Flamelord's Blessing is dangerous, win Chief Pacal's talisman, and then clear the ancient ruins of the Sage of the Stolen Flame to earn the first of two Golden Entreaties. It is the first half of the pilgrimage to cleanse the saurian at the Great Volcano of Tollan.
### STANDARD
Between Pledge and Forgettance (5.0) is a three-quest Natlan world series: Lost in the Woods, Rite of the Bold and Revelations from the Past. The Traveler and Paimon free a hurt young Tepetlisaurus from a barrier, travel with it, and learn from the researcher Manqu that the red mark on its horn is the Flamelord's Blessing, a power that often kills or maddens saurians as they grow. A volcano pilgrimage with two tokens is the only known remedy. Chief Pacal of the Children of Echoes grants the first token after a duel, and the ruins of the Sage of the Stolen Flame yield the first Golden Entreaty after trials in which human and saurian must act as a pair. The series is tied to a deliberate design by the Sage that only the later Natlan quests expose.
### LARGE +
The series opens the long Little One thread that runs through the Natlan world quests: Lost in the Woods is triggered by shining pillars after arrival in Natlan, Rite of the Bold is gated behind Shadows of the Mountains and Tale of Dreams Plucked From Fire, and Revelations from the Past follows automatically. Its murals are the first place the Traveler sees the Sage's pledge with Xbalanque and a vision of the fall of Xiuhcoatl.
### ULTRA +
The wiki's end-of-series recollection summarises it as the Traveler and Little One each enduring the test and obtaining one of the two Golden Entreaties. The second Entreaty comes from Lost Traveler in the Ashen Realm, and both are offered together in the finale series, A Finale Emberforged.

## plot
@version: 5.0
### STANDARD
Lost in the Woods. Shining pillars scattered around Natlan pull the Traveler and Paimon into a strange space full of Rifthounds. After the fight, a dying giant saurian addresses golden words to a "Chosen of Dragons" and, as the Traveler reaches out, the Traveler's consciousness enters the beast's body (a Saurian Indwelling). Using it, the Traveler breaks a rock that holds a trapped saurian whelp. Paimon bandages its tail with a scarf, and the whelp agrees to travel with the party. The player names it; the default name is Little One.

Rite of the Bold. Manqu, a researcher of the Saurian Relics Association, warns the party that the red mark on the whelp's horn is the Flamelord's Blessing, which saurians no longer survive well in the modern age, and that the Wayob and the Night Kingdom reject such saurians. He suggests handing it to the Children of Echoes, but when the Traveler refuses to abandon a companion, he reveals a legend: a warrior must bring the saurian to the volcano carrying a token that was split in two. Chief Pacal and Manqu then explain the pilgrimage custom. Pacal waives the old rituals of feather, pelts, fifty victories and ninety-six gems and instead tests the Traveler and Little One in a duel, with his own saurian Uchu fighting beside him. Defeated, he gives them the Talisman of Waxaklahun Ubah Kan, a name of the Sage of the Stolen Flame.

Revelations from the Past. At the ruins entrance, Abyss-tainted Rifthounds attack. Placing the talisman starts the Sage's trial. The Traveler and Little One are split between two viewpoints in the ruins. They solve puzzles using colour-coded crystals, cross a lava stretch, and enter Catastrophe's Edge, where they fight off enemies from the Abyss. At the end they receive the first Golden Entreaty.
### LARGE +
The ruins are guided by golden text addressed to the "Chosen of Dragons". In the Audience Hall a mural shows a dragon and a human each holding a golden ring. Manqu has already said the Sage believed humans could lead saurians to a better future than the dragons' age, perhaps from guilt at having stolen phlogiston secrets from dragonkind. The golden text says the pledge with Xbalanque was made at Catastrophe's Edge, and invites the Chosen to prove themselves there.
### ULTRA +
Rite of the Bold only starts after Manqu has been met and two in-game days have passed since Shadows of the Mountains and Tale of Dreams Plucked From Fire were finished. If the player has completed Lost Traveler in the Ashen Realm, a recollection plays during the later quests. In Revelations from the Past Paimon first wonders whether the golden text meant "chosen by the great Tepetlisaurus" and then whether the Sage himself is a dragon.

## revelations
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
- The Flamelord's Blessing is real and dangerous: in Manqu's account it is a power of the old dragons that modern saurians cannot withstand, and the pilgrimage is the only remedy.
- The Sage of the Stolen Flame and the first Pyro Archon each brought back one token from the volcano; whoever holds both can enter the cleansing fire. Pacal says the Sage seems to appear in tales from the tribal era to Ochkanatlan and beyond.
- The Sage's murals imply he was a dragon, and show the Sage and Xbalanque each holding a golden disc.
### LARGE +
A mural showing a giant black dragon, a tree and a figure gives the Traveler a vision of Nibelung sunk into the Abyss, of the Irminsul nearly withering, and of the fall of the Sacred City of Tollan, where a giant dragon with pierced pupils and a tiny human fall together and a man later rises from the dragon's heart wreathed in flame. A voice says the vision is not meant for the Traveler.
### ULTRA +
In the final room golden lines show a split seal: the dragon's eye cut in two by a human's sword, one disc given to the "silver dragon" and one to the "resurrected corpse", one buried beneath a great monument and one swallowed by the headstrong one. A further vision shows Och-Kan lifting a disc before the empty throne of Ochkanatlan, and the two discs merge into one golden seal. The quest speaks only in the golden text; the identification with Kukulkan, Xbalanque, Xiuhcoatl and Och-Kan comes from the later quests and the wiki's annotations.

## characters
### STANDARD
The Traveler and Paimon travel with Little One, a young Tepetlisaurus who speaks only in growls. Manqu of the Saurian Relics Association is the scholarly guide. Pacal, chief of the Children of Echoes, is jovial, strong and protective of guests. His saurian is Uchu. Titu is mentioned as someone who spoke highly of the Traveler. The Sage of the Stolen Flame (Waxaklahun Ubah Kan, later revealed as Kukulkan) appears only through golden text.
### LARGE +
Manqu is formal and blunt, apologising often for clumsy phrasing, and wants the pilgrimage to succeed partly to prove that old inscriptions remain true. Pacal dislikes pushing guests into a fiery pit to test a theory, but accepts the Traveler after seeing the party's resolve and Little One's reaction.

## consequences
### STANDARD
The Traveler holds the first Golden Entreaty and gains the avatar "Your Saurian companion!". Little One stays with the party. The second Entreaty comes from Lost Traveler in the Ashen Realm. Offering both is the next step towards the volcano (see Path to the Flaming Peaks and A Finale Emberforged). The series also opens the many Little One side quests in In the Footsteps of the Chosen of Dragons.
### LARGE +
Rite of the Bold awards the achievement The Legend of the Legendary Heroes. In the wiki's quest chain Revelations from the Past leads on to The Road Ahead.

## placement
### STANDARD
Released in 5.0 with Natlan. In story order Lost in the Woods usually comes first, Rite of the Bold after the party has spent time in Natlan, and Revelations from the Past last. The series connects directly to Lost Traveler in the Ashen Realm (the next chapter), Path to the Flaming Peaks, Chronicler of the Crumbling City and A Finale Emberforged, which resolve it.

## entry: Talisman of Waxaklahun Ubah Kan
@keys: Talisman of Waxaklahun Ubah Kan, Waxaklahun Ubah Kan, Sage of the Stolen Flame
@version: 5.0
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
The Talisman of Waxaklahun Ubah Kan is the proof Pacal gives the Traveler after the duel in Rite of the Bold. Waxaklahun Ubah Kan is a name of the Sage of the Stolen Flame, whom later quests reveal to be Kukulkan. Pacal admits he cannot remember the long name. The talisman is placed on a platform at the ruins entrance to open the Sage's trial.
### LARGE +
Pacal and Manqu recall that after Xiuhcoatl died and the first Pyro Archon was revived, the Sage and the Archon each brought back one volcanic token. Both are required to enter the cleansing fire.

## entry: Flamelord's Blessing
@keys: Flamelord's Blessing
@version: 5.0
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
The Flamelord's Blessing is the red mark on Little One's horn. In Manqu's explanation it grants strength, swimming speed or leaping, but is not the saurian's own power. It is the strength of the ancient dragons, which modern saurians cannot bear, so growth can kill them or drive them mad. The Wayob and the Night Kingdom refuse to accept saurians marked this way. The Flamelord is the Pyro Dragon Sovereign, Xiuhcoatl.
### LARGE +
Pacal calls it more curse than blessing. The later finale quest shows the Sage's own account is more complicated: he says the mark proves the dragon bloodline is unchanged, and that the promise of removing it was a lie of omission.

## entry: Volcanic pilgrimage
@keys: volcanic pilgrimage, Rite of the Bold
@version: 5.0
### STANDARD
The old pilgrimage required a warrior and saurian to be recognised by the saurian's tribal chief, who for Tepetlisauri is the chief of the Children of Echoes. The ritual tasks Manqu lists include a green quetzal feather, a stole of pelts, fifty victories for the tribe and ninety-six gems. Pacal drops them and fights the Traveler instead, with Uchu, in the duel grounds in the mountain behind the Stadium of the Sacred Flame. He warns that no one has returned from this road.
