## summary
@spoiler: major
### LITE
Her Foes Rage Like Great Waters... is a three-quest world quest series in the Sumeru desert from version 3.4, set among the Tanit tribe. The Traveler, Paimon and their friend Jeht become the target of the Tanit matriarch Babel, who wants control of the Eternal Oasis and of the Traveler's jinni. Babel sends assassins, tells each side the other has joined the Fatui, and is finally confronted and killed by Jeht, who leaves the tribe behind.
### STANDARD +
The series follows Apocalypse Lost and Rejoice With Me, for What Was Lost Is Now Found. Its titles come from biblical passages on the fall of Babylon (Babel in the Hebrew Bible), and Babel the matriarch is its antagonist. It is a story about a manipulative adoptive mother, a daughter who chooses a friend over her tribe, and what Jeht does with the name of her real mother.
### LARGE +
The series also records how Fatui dealt with the Tanit: the letters the Traveler finds show a Fatui desert expedition receiving a young woman from the tribe for experiments, after a pre-arranged ambush. The Fatui are a threat to Jeht in the middle part, then a plot device for Babel's lies.

## plot
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Yuften, a Tanit farmer, asks the Traveler to find a lost caravan carrying a supply called Anahitian Blessing. The trail of broken boxes leads to a hilichurl camp and an abandoned rug, which look staged, and Eremite assassins then ambush the Traveler and Paimon, claiming Jeht sent them to keep them in the desert. The Traveler reports to Babel, who promises protection and says Jeht is away on tribal duties.

The next day Babel says Jeht may have betrayed the tribe to the northerners (the Fatui) and asks the Traveler to find her and bring her back. At a Fatui outpost the Traveler finds documents and a frightened soldier, Orest, who says Jeht was captured, escaped her cage and killed a Fatui. Letters reveal a plan to move her to a canyon hideout. There the Traveler finds the wounded Jeht, who first attacks them, believing she has been betrayed. After she is calmed down, assassins from the Tanit arrive, sent by Babel. Jeht and the Traveler compare stories: Babel told Jeht the Traveler had joined the Fatui and told the Traveler the same about Jeht. They confront Babel at the Tanit Camps. Babel admits she wants the Eternal Oasis and the Traveler's jinni for the Tanit and to rebuild a desert nation. Jeht refuses to harm the Traveler and tells the tribe she is Ufairah Hindi's daughter. Babel sets the tribe on them, and Jeht defeats and kills Babel. Jeht, now homeless, leaves the camps with Benben.
### LARGE
**Behold, the Sign Comes Like A Thief...** Yuften, stuttering and anxious, says an outside party delivering Anahitian Blessing never arrived, and blames northerners who he says have been more active since Aderfi and Azariq left. The Traveler follows a trail of dropped goods: a cracked jar, an empty box with the tribe's symbol, a hilichurl camp that has no cart parts, and a rug with blotches on it. Paimon suspects the clues are laid out too neatly. Eremites, including one named Bouhanu, ambush them, saying they are friends of Jeht and have been commissioned to give the Traveler "hospitality". The Traveler decides to return to the tribe but not mention Jeht. Yuften tells them to report to Babel, who says the northerners have long tried to push into the tribe's lands, that traitors have been raiding for months, and that the Traveler will be safe among the Tanit while Jeht is brought to help. Babel thanks the Traveler for opening the Eternal Oasis and says it is central to the tribe's faith as the sovereignty of Nabu Malikata, the Queen of All Oases. She says the Oasis is guarded by Jeht, who she says guards her parents. When pressed, Babel insists the goddess "was" present even though the Traveler did not see her.

**Make Bright the Arrows, Gather the Shields...** The next day Babel says a captured northerner claims that Jeht revealed Tanit outposts and sent assassins after the Traveler. She asks the Traveler to bring Jeht back, whether she can prove her innocence or not. At the marked Fatui outpost, a soldier challenges the Traveler for a password; the passwords turn out to be mixed up. Documents say the Fatui's talks with the Tanit are over, that the tribe will hand over a young woman for experiments, and that the soldiers are being moved. The soldier Orest says Jeht was caged, tore the cage apart and killed a soldier, and was sedated with Electro bullets. Orest asks the Traveler to tell his superiors to give up, and goes. Letters at the next camp from a Fatui lieutenant, Ruslan Snezhevich, and captain, Zoya Snezhevna, show that a Tanit tribeswoman named Jeht was handed over, that the transfer code is "Red Ball Express", and that she is to be moved to a canyon.

**For Her Judgment Reaches to the Skies...** The Traveler follows the sound of fighting to a Fatui hideout in a cave, from which Fatui soldiers are fleeing in panic, and where Jeht is badly hurt. She attacks in a rage, believing the Traveler betrayed her, and they fight until she is down to a fraction of her strength. She says she did not betray the tribe, and the Traveler says the same. The assassins Nayram and Rezki, two Tanit Falcons who are like family to Jeht, then arrive to kill her; they are beaten. After they rest, Jeht and the Traveler find Babel at the entrance to a canyon. Babel says the Eternal Oasis is the tribe's hope, that outsiders must be kept away, that the Tanit will rebuild a nation of oases and challenge the god of Sumeru, and that she will be the Prophetess of Nabu Malikata. She asks Jeht to choose between the Tanit and the Traveler, offers her the succession, and Jeht refuses. Jeht lists the elders she says she removed on Babel's orders (Bleda, Gwaffah and Teyya), says her mother's name is Ufairah Hindi, and takes it as her own. Babel calls her the daughter of a traitor and of an outsider, names her father Jebrael, and sets the tribe on them. After the fight, Babel is wounded and dying. She says she has already sent word to the tribes that Jeht is a traitor and that she had planned to remove her. Jeht kills her. Outside the camp Jeht says she has nowhere to return to, then accepts the Traveler's suggestion that she can now call anywhere home. She says she has reclaimed her mother's name, parts from the Traveler with an embrace, and leaves with Benben.

### ULTRA +
After the series the Tanit Camps are empty. According to the wiki's gameplay notes a vault can be opened where Babel once stood, Jeht's tent carries a note saying she is out on business, and, after the Akasha Pulses, the Kalpa Flame Rises Archon Quest, an NPC named Tanjiyy comments on the aftermath. These are wiki-recorded notes.

The etymology notes record that each title is taken from the biblical chapters on the fall of Babylon, for example Jeremiah 51 and Revelation 16. The series also includes joke references, such as the passwords and the cry "shoot her", that are not plot-relevant.

## revelations
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
- Babel tells the Traveler and Jeht the other has joined the Fatui, in order to set them against one another. She wants to keep the Eternal Oasis and to be Prophetess of Nabu Malikata.
- Fatui documents say the Tanit agreed to hand a young woman over to the Fatui desert expedition for experiments, and to lure her into a pre-arranged ambush.
- Jeht says she has killed or removed several elders at Babel's command, and that her mother is Ufairah Hindi.
### LARGE +
- Babel hopes to unite the desert tribes around the Oasis under her authority and, with Nabu Malikata's power, to topple the god of Sumeru. She wants to control the Traveler's jinni by removing her master.
- Babel's last words are that she sent word to other tribes that Jeht is a traitor, so that no one will accept her again.

## characters
### STANDARD
The Traveler, Paimon, Jeht, Babel, Benben (a companion of Jeht who appears at the end), Yuften (a Tanit farmer), the Tanit Falcons Nayram and Rezki, Mendas (seen beside Babel), Eremite assassins including Bouhanu, and Fatui soldiers Orest, Ilyusha, Yevgraf, Lieutenant Ruslan Snezhevich and Captain Zoya Snezhevna. Mentioned: Professor Froderock, Lieutenant Muldoon, Ufairah Hindi, Jebrael, Aderfi, Azariq, Masseira.

## consequences
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Babel is dead, the Tanit Camps are left deserted, and Jeht, now outside the tribe and expected to be called a traitor along the trade routes, takes her mother's name and goes her own way with Benben.
### LARGE +
Jeht tells the Traveler she wants to share another adventure someday and hopes their paths will cross again. The Fatui expedition's attempt to take Jeht has failed, as the soldier Orest says in the second quest.

## placement
### STANDARD
Requires Apocalypse Lost and Rejoice With Me, for What Was Lost Is Now Found. The wiki lists Apocalypse Lost directly before this series. Released in 3.4. The three quests follow each other, the second starting automatically after the first, and the third after the second.

## entry: Babel, matriarch of the Tanit
@keys: Babel, Matriarch Babel
@keys2: Tanit, Jeht, Eternal Oasis, Her Foes Rage Like Great Waters
@version: 3.4
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Babel is the matriarch of the Tanit, a desert tribe, and the adoptive mother of Jeht. She is courteous and tender in public, calls the Traveler "esteemed Master of the Jinni", and tells each side that the other has betrayed them. Her aim is control of the Eternal Oasis and of the goddess Nabu Malikata's legacy, with Jeht as her heir. She dies at Jeht's hand.

## entry: Jeht and Ufairah Hindi
@keys: Jeht, Ufairah Hindi, Ufairah
@keys2: Tanit, Babel, Benben, Jebrael
@version: 3.4
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Jeht is a Tanit whom Babel raised as her own daughter. In this series she refuses to kill the Traveler, says she is the daughter of Ufairah Hindi and Jebrael (an outsider mother and a father Babel calls a traitor), and chooses her mother's name over the tribe's. She leaves with Benben, saying that she is now the head of her own house.

## entry: Eternal Oasis in this series
@keys: Eternal Oasis
@keys2: Babel, Tanit, Nabu Malikata, Queen of All Oases
@version: 3.4
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Babel treats the Eternal Oasis as the central pillar of Tanit faith: whoever holds it claims the sovereignty of Nabu Malikata, the Queen of All Oases. Jeht says the goddess is long gone and cannot be revived. Babel says that she is absent only in appearance and will return. The Tanit say they are its defenders.

## entry: Fatui desert expedition in Her Foes Rage Like Great Waters
@keys: Fatui desert expedition, Zoya Snezhevna, Ruslan Snezhevich, Red Ball Express
@keys2: Jeht, Tanit, Orest
@version: 3.4
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
A Fatui desert expedition led by Captain Zoya Snezhevna and Lieutenant Ruslan Snezhevich received a young Tanit woman, Jeht, from the tribe for experiments, after a plan to ambush her. The transfer was code-named Red Ball Express. She escaped her captors, and the expedition's mission failed. Professor Froderock Fronkonsteen is the scientist the documents address.
