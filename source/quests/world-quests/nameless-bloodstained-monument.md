## summary
### LITE
A Version 6.4 world quest on Ashveil Peak in Nod-Krai. With Varka as the active character, the Traveler touches a shadowy greatsword near the wolf-like enemy "The Homesick Lone Wolf" and is drawn into a sealed battlefield guarded by a shadow of Boreas. At its end Varka sees the memory of the last knights who followed the Bloodstained Knight Roland, and picks up their two greatswords.
### STANDARD +
The quest fills in what became of the knights of Favonius who left Mondstadt with Roland. They fought the cursed monsters of the north beside him until they died, and Roland used the power of Boreas to seal their battlefield so that their rest would not be disturbed. Varka, himself a knight of Mondstadt, treats the dead as comrades and carries their swords on.
### LARGE +
The quest ties together three things the player may already know: the legend of the Bloodstained Knight, the Wolf of the North as a power Varka can sense, and the Knights of Favonius' own gaps in its records. It is a quiet, elegiac story rather than a mystery.
### ULTRA +
Roland's farewell is the emotional core. He believes he must walk his blood-soaked path alone, the knights insist they chose it, and in the end he seals them away to spare them further injustice, a choice Varka challenges by calling them back to fight.

## plot
### LITE
The Traveler touches the shadowy greatsword, and Roland's voice tells them not to disturb the sleepers. Varka senses the Wolf of the North's power and also a hint of the Falcon of the West. Entering the domain, the party fights a shadow of Boreas, then sees the memory of Roland and his knights. Varka takes the swords of the fallen knights.
### STANDARD +
The quest begins when Varka is the active character and the player interacts with the greatsword behind the Homesick Lone Wolf. A voice, identified by the wiki as Roland's, whispers "do not disturb their peaceful slumber", and Varka comments on the power he feels there. He chooses to face the enemy. Inside the domain the area is sealed by a force that Varka suspects is a manifestation of Boreas, and he wonders who sealed it.

Mid-fight a distant voice recites lines about unyielding ice on an inscribed stele and a biting gale mourning the lost. The player can pick up flames of battle, which are the "unextinguished flame" of the knights, and the knights' heroic spirits indicate where to start. When Boreas's Shadow is weakened further, it is replaced by the Homesick Lone Wolf and its ancestral spirit while another voice urges, "continue your knightly journey".

After the fight Varka stands before two greatswords. He asks who left the wolf spirit there and what it guards, then holds out a hand and closes his eyes. Roland's spirit appears with several nameless knights and a pale-furred wolf. Roland says he has told them many times that this blood-soaked path is his alone and no one need follow. The knights answer that they simply happened to follow the same path "all the way from Mondstadt to Nod-Krai", revering a knight who saved them countless times, and that they would stand as witnesses even if only a few of them were left. The wolf howls. Roland says he has no right to deny them.
### LARGE +
A black-screen scene shows the aftermath. The knights ask whether the cursed monsters are dead and whether the nearby residents are safe, then admit they lack the strength to see him to the end and tell him he must walk the rest of the road alone. Roland turns to Varka, says his sword could not protect them all and that in the end only one remains on the path, "perhaps the way it was always meant to be". He takes off his mask (his face is hidden from the camera) and says that these swords and the wolf spirit will serve as guardians so that dark aberrations never disturb the knights' eternal rest.

Varka reflects that the Knights of Favonius' records say some knights followed the Bloodstained Knight when he left, and that they were never mentioned again. He is moved that they died fighting monsters, a worthy end for true knights of Favonius, and asks whether this was truly all they wished. Roland reappears and says he will use the power Boreas left there to seal the battlefield, and that the little wolf will stand vigil outside, so the knights can enjoy eternal peace and be spared from witnessing the injustice of the world. He says goodbye to his final comrades.
### ULTRA +
The knights refuse to be left sleeping. Their voices say "no" twice, then "let us fight, once more". Varka answers that surely their wishes were not meant to wither in such a place, asks them to join him, lifts both greatswords and shouts "To battle!" in unison with their voices. The player receives the weapon Unbreakable: Durandarte. Varka's last words are that Roland really was on his own once they were gone. If the player touches the shadowy greatsword again after the quest, Roland says nothing audible and Varka challenges the shadow to show what it has.

Gameplay notes: from the second step the player enters a domain and is limited to Varka. Picking up flames of battle grants a temporary Pyro infusion. Food effects cannot be consumed during the challenge, and the domain can be re-entered after the quest to fight Boreas's Shadow again. Rewards: 40 Primogems, Unbreakable: Durandarte, Hero's Wit, Mystic Enhancement Ore and 30,000 Mora.

## revelations
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The quest shows that some Knights of Favonius followed Roland out of Mondstadt and died fighting monsters in Nod-Krai, and that Roland used Boreas's power to seal their battlefield with a wolf spirit as guardian. It also answers Varka's question about the knights who vanish from the Order's records.
### LARGE +
It shows how Roland saw himself: someone who walked alone because he thought he could not protect others, even as the knights insisted they had chosen their path. It gives a human reason for the wolf spirit on Ashveil Peak.
### ULTRA +
It does not say when the battle took place, who the knights were, or what Roland's face looked like. The quest also does not say what the Falcon of the West is beyond Varka's remark that he felt a hint of it.

## characters
### LITE
Varka and Roland, the Bloodstained Knight. The nameless knights who followed him and a pale-furred wolf appear in the memory. The Traveler is present but the story belongs to Varka.
### STANDARD +
Varka is serious and respectful toward the dead; he speaks of the knights as "true Knights of Favonius" and refuses to let them rest if they still have something to fight for. Roland is weary and self-effacing: he says his sword could not protect everyone, and he wants his comrades at peace. The nameless knights are loyal and stubborn, and they joke about "fate".
### LARGE +
The knights never give names, ranks or ages.

## consequences
### STANDARD
Varka receives the two greatswords and the memory that goes with them, and the player gets the reward weapon. The domain stays available for repeat fights. No political or historical change follows.

## placement
### STANDARD
Introduced in Version 6.4 (Luna V). The wiki lists no prerequisite quest, only Varka as the active character at the starting greatsword on Ashveil Peak. The Bloodstained Knight's earlier legend comes from Mondstadt content, which this quest extends.
