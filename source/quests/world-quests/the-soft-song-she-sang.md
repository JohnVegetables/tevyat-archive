## summary
@spoiler: minor
### LITE
The Soft Song She Sang is a short Snezhnaya World Quest added in version 7.0, set on the Everfrozen Earth coast. It is the last part of the story of the giant snow figure first met in "A Great Heart Fettered by Slender Chains". The Traveler and Paimon thaw the ice around a lighthouse lamp, the giant lights it, and a ghostly young woman appears in its beam. The giant then walks out into the sea.
### STANDARD +
The quest is entered directly after "Like a Labyrinth Imprisons Her Servants", which ends with the giant kneeling before the lighthouse. It is quiet and nearly wordless: almost all of its meaning comes from a cutscene and from Paimon's reflections afterward. It rewards Adventure EXP, Primogems and Mora, a Luxurious Chest and an achievement (game-mechanics information).
### LARGE +
It is best treated as an epilogue to a love story that canon only hints at: a giant who wanted to return to a promised place and a young woman who may or may not have waited. The quest leaves her nature unexplained on purpose.

## plot
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Continuing from the previous quest, Paimon notices the giant is trying to light the lighthouse, but the whole building is covered in ice. The Traveler approaches the lighthouse, climbs to the top and uses a Basic Heat Source to melt the ice and snow around the lamp room.
### LARGE +
A cutscene follows. A glowing energy in the giant's chest forms an orb that travels to the lamp, and the lamp ignites, throwing a spotlight on the island. The ghost seen earlier appears inside the light and climbs the lighthouse. The giant stands and faces her. She clasps her hands in a prayer pose while he stares into space. Then he turns and walks away into the distant sea, the blizzard stills, and the ghost becomes an ice statue.

Afterward Paimon comments that the giant was chained up, but this lighthouse was where he always wanted to be. She notes that the young woman was only human and should not have lived so long, and asks what the shadow really was. The Traveler can suggest that the girl's own wish gave it form, or that it was made from the Snegovik's wishes. Paimon follows both thoughts: that the shadow may have lasted because the girl held on to her wish to see him, or that she never managed to wait for him and the shadow was a figment of his imagination, so that he gained his world at least there. Paimon watches him walk beneath the waves, says she has a feeling they will not see him again, and calls out goodbye, unsure if he can hear. The Traveler tells her he will.
### ULTRA +
Post-quest dialogue. A statue of a young woman in prayer stands where the ghost froze. Interacting with it gives only a short description: she may be praying that her beloved returns safely from the battlefield, or that they appear at the place they promised to meet. Completing this exchange unlocks a separate achievement (game-mechanics information).

## revelations
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
- The giant's long wait ends at the lighthouse. Lighting it is treated as the fulfilment of what he wanted.
- The ghostly woman is shown only as an apparition. Her nature is left for the player to interpret.
### LARGE +
- The statue text ties the ghost to a prayer about a loved one at a battlefield, matching the war background that the previous quest mentions for the giant.

## characters
### STANDARD
- The Traveler and Paimon.
- The snow giant, named Donskoy elsewhere in the quest chain, who does not speak in this quest.
- An unnamed ghostly young woman who becomes an ice statue.
### LARGE +
- Pavlina and the other voices of the previous quest do not appear here.

## consequences
### STANDARD
The giant leaves the land and wanders into the sea. The blizzard around the lighthouse stops. No effect on any named living character is shown.

## placement
### STANDARD
Story order: the third and last quest in the snow giant chain: "A Great Heart Fettered by Slender Chains", then "Like a Labyrinth Imprisons Her Servants", then this quest. Release order: version 7.0, all Everfrozen Earth World Quests. It does not depend on the main Archon Quest.

### STANDARD
