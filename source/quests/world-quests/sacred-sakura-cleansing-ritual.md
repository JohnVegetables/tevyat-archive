## summary
@spoiler: minor
### LITE
Sacred Sakura Cleansing Ritual is a World Quest series on Narukami Island, added in version 2.0. At Konda Village the Traveler meets a masked shrine maiden, Hanachirusato, who asks for help carrying out the ritual that purges filth from the roots of the Sacred Sakura and the Thunder Sakura. The Traveler lifts five barriers and cleanses the Depths of Mt. Yougou.
### STANDARD +
The series has four quests: A Strange Story in Konda, Sacrificial Offering, Cleansing Defilement (with the sub-quest Tanuki-Bayashi in the Forest) and Yougou Cleansing. The starting area requires the Ritou Escape Plan quest.
### LARGE +
It doubles as an in-world explanation of how the Sacred and Thunder Sakura cleanse Inazuma, and it ties the Grand Narukami Shrine's duties to the Electro Archon's watch over the land. Quest by quest: A Strange Story in Konda introduces Hanachirusato and the first barrier; Sacrificial Offering sends the Traveler to the shrines; Cleansing Defilement covers Araumi, the Kamisato Estate and Chinju Forest; Yougou Cleansing ends at the roots of the Sacred Sakura.

## plot
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Arriving at Konda Village, the Traveler and Paimon meet Hanachirusato, a masked shrine maiden who gives the Traveler a ward and asks them to lift a barrier and look into strange happenings. After questioning villagers, the Traveler finds the key to a locked well and a seal under the ground, lifts the barrier with the ward, and defeats an Ochimusha that appears. Hanachirusato then explains the Sacred Sakura Cleansing Ritual and asks for further help.

The Traveler collects the Sacred Sakura Cleansing Summary at an abandoned shrine near Mt. Yougou, but it is not enough, so they go to the Grand Narukami Shrine. There Inagi Hotomi explains more and hands over the Memento Lens. Back at the abandoned shrine the barrier is lifted and another Ochimusha is beaten. The Traveler then completes the rituals at Araumi, the Kamisato Estate and Chinju Forest. At Chinju Forest a bake-danuki sealed in stone, Ioroi, agrees to give up his ward after the Traveler plays with several tanuki.
### LARGE
With all five barriers broken and the filth in each driven out, Hanachirusato and the Traveler go to the roots of the Sacred Sakura. She dispels the barrier over the Depths of Mt. Yougou, which drains its Electro pool, and the Traveler goes down to defeat the Miasmic Tumor. Afterwards she reveals that she is filth condensed from the memory of a Kitsune Saiguu. For the ritual to finish, all the filth must be cleansed, herself included. The two say goodbye and Hanachirusato dissipates.
### ULTRA +
The ritual as explained in the series: the Sacred Sakura and Thunder Sakura symbolize the Electro Archon's eternal watch and absorb filth into their roots, which are all interconnected and cannot purify themselves. Minor cleansings are done often to check the roots and set barriers that protect the roots from malicious people such as the Fatui and protect people from leaking filth. A major cleansing is needed every 60 years: the barriers are broken with wards, the filth condenses into "defilement incarnate" (harmless forms such as tofu boys in peaceful times, dangerous ones in troubled times), first for the Thunder Sakura roots and then for the central Sacred Sakura roots, where it forms a tumor. According to Hanachirusato's records, the Thunder Sakura almost withered several hundred years ago, monsters raged and the seas turned black, until someone performed the ritual. The shrine had planned this ritual, but Yae Miko called it off and told Inagi Hotomi to give the Memento Lens to a blonde-haired outlander.

## revelations
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
- Hanachirusato is filth condensed from the memory of the Kitsune Saiguu and ends by being cleansed with the rest (her own statement).
- The Sacred Sakura Cleansing Ritual must be performed regularly or the Sakura trees wither, which would be catastrophic for Inazuma.
### LARGE +
- The major cleansing is due every 60 years and is split between the Thunder Sakura roots and the Sacred Sakura roots.
- Yae Miko arranged for the Traveler to perform it in place of the shrine.

## characters
### STANDARD
The Traveler, Paimon, Hanachirusato, Inagi Hotomi (shrine staff), Ioroi (a sealed bake-danuki), and villagers of Konda. Yae Miko and the Kitsune Saiguu are referred to rather than present.

## consequences
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The Sakura roots are purged of their accumulated filth for the time being. Hanachirusato dissipates at the end. The shrine's planned ritual is not needed.

## placement
### STANDARD
Released in 2.0 with Inazuma. The Ritou Escape Plan quest is needed to reach the starting location. The Kitsune Saiguu is a historical figure covered separately.
