## summary
### LITE
A light world quest at Dornman Port, Windrest Peak. The old lighthouse's alarm keeps flashing, the keeper Winslow is terrified of ghosts, and the tavern regular Odolf asks the Traveler to investigate the ruins under the tower. The cause is an Eye of the Storm drawn by a tampered mechanism.
### STANDARD +
It begins at the group of regulars by the Devil's Cut tavern and ends at the lighthouse base. The quest requires Song of the Dragon and Freedom and was released in Version 6.5. It mixes mild humour with a small moral about honesty.
### LARGE +
The quest also uncovers why the alarm doors misbehave: they are all linked to the alarm mechanism, so wind and flashing lights lock doors and open them. It unlocks a teleport waypoint in the Knights of Favonius Ruins and an achievement about someone else owing an apology.

## plot
### LITE
At the tavern the regulars say the lighthouse alarm keeps flickering and that locals whisper of the ghosts of old knights. Odolf, who lives nearby, asks the Traveler to help Winslow. They enter the ruins beneath the lighthouse, block drafts, solve the alarm doors, survive a cave-in, and defeat an Eye of the Storm. Odolf then tells Winslow the ghosts have calmed down.
### STANDARD +
Lennart and Matthew tell the story: Winslow first shouted that the Abyss had come, the Knights searched several times and found nothing, and the alarm still flickered. Odolf, back from a business trip in Snezhnaya, wants to check for himself. Winslow, panicking, has locked the main doors, so Odolf leads the Traveler through another entrance known only to locals. In the ruins the Traveler pushes crates and breaks a rock pile to block wind currents that trip the alarm, fights Hydro and Electro Slimes in a cemetery, and glides over a gate with Anemograna. A strong wind falls from above: an Eye of the Storm. After it is defeated the alarm stops. Odolf tells the shaken Winslow that the wind was the unhappy fallen knights, now soothed, and covers a loud cave-in with a story about "super spiritual arm wrestling". Winslow says he has been tending the graves, and Odolf encourages him to keep doing so.
### LARGE +
Earlier, Winslow bolts at the sight of Paimon, shouting about a white ghost, and later says the locals have always pushed him away. Odolf recalls that when Winslow wanted to move the "damp, horrible" things below out to the fields beyond the port, the locals tried to stop him, and he warned that there would be consequences. In the ruins the Traveler finds many old tombstones, eroded but recently tended. Odolf's reward is the preparation he mentioned; he says adventuring is not his strength and invites the Traveler back to the tavern.
### ULTRA +
Steps in the ruins: block the first wind current with a crate to open a door, close a main control switch that drops the floor into a cemetery, fight Hydro and Electro Slimes, then use Anemograna to pass a gate whose alarms flash faster. The ending dialogue gives the pun-heavy "ghost negotiation expert" title to the Traveler. Odolf reports he had been in Snezhnaya on business, a detail with no wider story use here.

## revelations
@spoiler: minor
### LITE
The "ghost" is Odolf's own doing.
### STANDARD +
In the optional post-quest talk, Odolf confesses he adjusted the alarm mechanism and added a lure for elemental creatures, meaning only to frighten Winslow a little so he would give up moving the old graves of knights who died fighting the Abyss. He thought a few Anemo Slimes would drift away, then left on business to Snezhnaya, and the lure instead drew an Eye of the Storm. He did not want Winslow to end up so frightened.
### LARGE +
Odolf says he thanked Barbatos when the Traveler arrived. Had they not, he would have tried to drive the creature away himself or told the Knights the truth. He fears Winslow would explode at the truth and might even smash the tombstones. He quotes his own line that an open heart and a clear conscience keep out all evils and admits he did not live up to it. The Traveler can raise the guess that the ghost was Odolf himself, and he does not deny it.

## characters
### LITE
The Traveler, Paimon, Odolf, Winslow, Lennart and Matthew.
### STANDARD +
Odolf is a local who talks in flowery proverbs and knows the lighthouse's secrets; Winslow lives at the old lighthouse and is anxious and prickly; Lennart and Matthew are tavern regulars, with Lennart staying out of it after too much drink. The Knights of Favonius appear only as having searched the ruins before.

## consequences
### STANDARD
The alarm stops, Winslow calms down and keeps cleaning the graves, and Odolf's secret stays between him and the Traveler. A teleport waypoint in the Knights of Favonius Ruins opens.

## placement
### STANDARD
Released in Version 6.5 (Luna VI, 2026-04-08), set in Windrest Peak and requiring Song of the Dragon and Freedom. It is a stand-alone local story with no bearing on the main plots.
