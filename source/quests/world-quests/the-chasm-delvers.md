## summary
### LITE
A Version 2.6 Liyue world quest series. The Traveler joins an Adventurers' Guild and Ministry of Civil Affairs backed exploration team to open the sealed Chasm, make a camp in the Underground Mines, and follow strange dark mud and Abyss activity to the deep ruins.
### STANDARD +
It requires A New Star Approaches. Six quests (one with two sub-parts) lead from breaking the Liyue Qixing's Seven-Star seal on the surface, through helping miners and Treasure Hoarders and lighting the dark with the Lumenstone Adjuvant, to a final descent where the source of the corruption is dealt with. Before Version 4.3, part of the series was needed to progress the Archon Quest In the Depths, an Unexpected Reunion; a waypoint now unlocks automatically instead.
### LARGE +
The series ties the Chasm's closure to events 500 years ago (the cataclysm) and to the Abyss: its depths hold an ancient ruin, a huge mechanism and Abyss mages and lectors, and the dark mud the team fights spreads Abyssal corruption.

## plot
### LITE
The Traveler takes a Guild commission and meets Muning of the Ministry of Civil Affairs, worried by odd events in the sealed Chasm. A team, including the engineer Zhiqiong, forms. Five Bedrock Keys are destroyed to open the mines, a camp is set up, and the team explores deeper until the cause of the dark mud and tremors is dealt with.
### STANDARD +
Quests in order: The Chasm Charters; Surreptitious Seven-Star Seal Sundering; Chasm Spelunkers (with First Miasmic Contact and Meeting New People... and Foiling Some Bandits); The Heavenly Stone's Debris; Perils in the Dark; and Wherefore Did the Spiritstone Descend? Inside, the Traveler finds missing miners such as Uncle He, deals with Treasure Hoarders, and uses the Lumenstone Adjuvant to clear Oozing Concretion that spreads Abyssal corruption. A rockfall blocks the way down, so the team uses a giant cannon the staff call the Mountainator (officially the Safe Blasting Mechanism 2156). It needs parts and special fuses, and Clitopho, a former fake-medicine seller turned Treasure Hoarder, joins the demolitions work.
### LARGE +
In Perils in the Dark, the Traveler reads logs left behind, rings two bells in the ruins, and defeats two Abyss Lectors (Egill and Agnarr) lurking in dark fog, picking up an object one leaves behind. In Wherefore Did the Spiritstone Descend?, Zhiqiong leads the Traveler to a new camp where Jinwu, Khedive and Clitopho already are. Khedive fears the place is caught between two great forces, one above and one below. Beyond a gate and a stone tablet, the Traveler purifies five crystals around a large crystal by staying near them with a charged adjuvant, fights Abyss mages, an Abyss Herald and rifthounds, and then fights the Ruin Serpent Haftvad. Afterward the main team secures the area and Muning makes the group part of Huishan Hall's operations. Zhiqiong, per the wiki, chooses to keep exploring the Abyss-touched area.
### ULTRA +
Camp details: Jinwu, an old miner, set up the camp, learned the trade from his father, and asks the Traveler to watch over Zhiqiong, who came from Mingyun Village after its ore dried up. Clitopho's introduction notes that a demolitions expert from Mondstadt was meant to come but was grounded after a restaurant stove was blown up. Gameplay: the fight with Haftvad ends near 25 percent health; map areas such as the Glowing Narrows and Nameless Ruins are revealed along the way. The Inverted City stays barred until Chapter II Act IV of the Archon Quest.

## revelations
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The Chasm's depths are tied to the cataclysm and to the Abyss, and the dark mud is a spreading corruption with a source far below. The wiki summary names a corrupted Skyfrost Nail from Celestia as that source, but the final quest's own steps stress crystals and Haftvad; see uncertainties.

## characters
### LITE
The Traveler and Paimon; Muning of the Ministry of Civil Affairs; engineer and adventurer Zhiqiong; Jinwu, Khedive and Clitopho at the camp; Uncle He, a missing miner.
### STANDARD +
Zhiqiong, from a mining village, is reckless and eager to explore. Jinwu looks after the camp's safety equipment. Khedive interprets the mine's strange readings. Clitopho is a reluctant Treasure Hoarder with a past as a seller of fake medicine.

## consequences
### STANDARD
The Chasm's Underground Mines are open, a camp stands as the team's base, and the exploration team becomes part of Huishan Hall's operations. Tremors and spreading dark mud are brought under control. The deepest area remains dangerous.

## placement
### STANDARD
Released in Version 2.6 with the Chasm: Underground Mines. It requires A New Star Approaches. The related series Lost in a Foreign Land takes place in the same mines.

## entry: Lumenstone Adjuvant
@keys: Lumenstone Adjuvant, Oozing Concretion
@version: 2.6
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
The Lumenstone Adjuvant is a gadget the Traveler uses in the Underground Mines to light dark areas, clear Oozing Concretion and recharge crystals. Oozing Concretion is a dark mud-like substance that spreads Abyssal corruption.

## entry: The Mountainator
@keys: Mountainator, Safe Blasting Mechanism 2156
@version: 2.6
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
A giant cannon used by the exploration team to blast open the rockfall in the mines. Engineers know it as the Safe Blasting Mechanism 2156. Using it requires finding parts, its manual and special essential oil fuses.
