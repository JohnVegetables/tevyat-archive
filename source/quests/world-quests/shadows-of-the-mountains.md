## summary
@spoiler: none
### LITE
Shadows of the Mountains is a two-quest Natlan world series added in version 5.0, set in Tequemecan Valley. A tribal storyteller named Titu is watching a giant stone pillar, the Greater Shadow Pin, and its three smaller Lesser Shadow Pins, which hold back the Abyss. The Traveler, Paimon and the Little One help restore them and then find out what is trapped beneath.
### STANDARD +
The series is told through a storyteller's lens. Titu keeps returning to how tales are shaped and warped, and the central reveal is that a villain from local legend was a hero. It ends with an act of mercy, not a victory.
### LARGE +
The two quests are Beneath the Crystal Rock and Peace to the Slumbering. The second starts automatically after the first. Both give Children of Echoes reputation.

## plot
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
**Beneath the Crystal Rock.** At a sentry post in Tequemecan Valley the sentry Yukom is waiting for Titu to relieve him. The Traveler meets Titu beside the giant Shadow Pin. Titu explains that the Wayob are said in stories to have hung the pins to pin the shadow of the Volcano Lord, but in truth what is sealed beneath is the Abyss. Two of the three Lesser Shadow Pins have floated upward and the Iridescent Inscription, the language the ancient dragons used to wield phlogiston, has been squeezed out of them. The Traveler touches the inscription, breaks crystals to gather its power and fights Abyss-aligned monsters to push each pin back. The second pin's inscription has also been scattered and must be found in other places, with the Little One's nose pointing the way. Afterward the party asks Titu to tell the real story of the pins.

When all three Lesser Pins are set, the ground trembles. Black, Nightsoul-warped warriors appear, the Greater Shadow Pin falls into a deep cavity beneath it, and a hoarse voice cries out.

**Peace to the Slumbering.** Titu and the party climb down the hole. A tablet from Elder Traore explains that two Khaenri'ahn soldiers, Zephaniah and Irizar, guided the black flood to the seal site and stayed in it, and that the tribe's warriors sacrificed themselves with them. The Iridescent Inscription at the bottom is shattered into fragments and the party breaks the stones containing them while fighting Rifthounds. Voices behind the inscription reveal that more than two souls are locked in endless struggle with the Abyss. The Traveler unseals the inscription and defeats the two warriors, who thank them with their last words. In the Night Kingdom the Traveler and Paimon meet a beast with night-coloured fur who says the Night has forgiven them. Titu, relieved, plans to retell the story with the two soldiers as heroes and returns to take over Yukom's shift.
### LARGE +
Titu's account of the old events, told in the first quest, runs thus. The mountains where the Children of Echoes live were once more complete and lovely. The Khaenri'ahn catastrophe damaged Natlan's delicate Ley Lines and the Night Kingdom, the totem poles dimmed and the Wayob could not hear the tribes. A dark tide of calamity reached the mountains, brought by two Khaenri'ahn soldiers. With the tribe's leader away at a lone island in the west resisting giant beasts, Elder Traore fought the Abyss. The warriors and Saurians fashioned giant stone nails out of solid phlogiston with the Iridescent Inscription, Traore leapt first into the dark pit, and the Abyss was sealed beneath the earth. Paimon notes this was about five centuries ago. Titu says even today the Children of Echoes, who mine solid phlogiston and gems, know to leave the phlogiston around the nails untouched.

Titu also explains the pun behind the name: a Shadow Pin is called that because it pins shadows.
### ULTRA +
Traore's tablet is a three-part blessing. He addresses it as elder, storyteller, and shield and spear of the tribe. The first blessing is for friends from afar, Zephaniah and Irizar, who he says would rather be called cowards or a thorn in the tribe's side than heroes and whom he nonetheless names heroes and brother-kin. The second is for the sacrificed warriors, illuminated by the Incandescent God and guided by Chief Sundjatta, and he prays to the spirit of Waxaklahun Ubah Kan, called a madman who stole fire from the throat of the great dragon, to block the Abyss's arcane magic with radiant glyphs. The third is for those who read it, reminding them that fortresses built from sacrifice cannot stand forever and that only an indomitable spirit is truly immortal.

Gameplay note, labelled as such: the challenges require indwelling a Tepetlisaurus or using a Children of Echoes character, and the Little One fights alongside the player in some steps. These do not change the lore.

## revelations
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
- The Shadow Pins are giant solid-phlogiston nails driven into the ground by the Children of Echoes ages ago, each carrying an Iridescent Inscription, to seal the Abyss. The "Volcano Lord's shadow" is a metaphor.
- The "evil Khaenri'ahn soldiers" of tribal legend, Zephaniah and Irizar, were sealed in with the Abyss and fought it for centuries; Traore's tablet calls them heroes.
### LARGE +
- Titu says most tales in Natlan are embellished warnings in which rhetoric is metaphor.
- More souls than the two soldiers are locked with the Abyss; the Traveler hears the sound of countless hands pushing outward.

## characters
### STANDARD
Titu (a Children of Echoes storyteller), Yukom (a sentry), the Traveler, Paimon and the Little One. Elder Traore, Zephaniah and Irizar, and the "Beast With Fur Like That of Night" appear through the inscription, boss fights and a vision. Sanhaj of the Masters of the Night-Wind is mentioned by Titu as a storyteller he reveres.

## consequences
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
The Greater Shadow Pin has fallen and the Abyss in the cavity is purified. The Iridescent Inscription is gone, the tormented voices fall silent, and Zephaniah and Irizar find rest. Titu intends to tell the tale of the golden-haired outlander, the flying white fairy and a small Tepetlisaurus, and to retell the soldiers as heroes.

## placement
### STANDARD
Released in version 5.0, in Tequemecan Valley. Beneath the Crystal Rock begins by talking to Titu or by finishing a Lesser Shadow Pin challenge; Peace to the Slumbering follows automatically.

## entry: Titu
@keys: Titu
@keys2: Tequemecan Valley, Shadow Pin, storyteller, Yukom
@version: 5.0
@spoiler: none
### STANDARD
Titu is a Children of Echoes storyteller who also looks after the stone pillars. He is verbose, self-deprecating and keeps thinking in plots, joking that he is not a fighter. He says he loves heroes' stories most and tells them so that they are remembered. The quest has him come down into the cavity himself, because witnessing things through is a storyteller's duty. He is bad at turning up for his sentry shift, which annoys Yukom. Note that Titu is also the name of a member of Taika's expedition seen in Lost Traveler in the Ashen Realm; the quests do not say they are the same person.

## entry: Shadow Pins
@keys: Shadow Pin, Shadow Pins, Lesser Shadow Pin, Greater Shadow Pin
@keys2: Tequemecan Valley, Abyss, Iridescent Inscription
@version: 5.0
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Giant solid-phlogiston pillars: one large and three small. They seal the Abyss and are driven into the earth by Iridescent Inscription. When the Abyss presses, the inscription is squeezed out and the pin floats upward. The Children of Echoes know not to disturb the phlogiston near them.

## entry: Zephaniah and Irizar
@keys: Zephaniah, Irizar
@keys2: Khaenri'ah, Traore, Shadow Pin, Black Serpent
@version: 5.0
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Two Khaenri'ahn soldiers whom Natlan folklore remembers as villains who brought the Abyss to the valley. Traore's tablet names them as friends who guided the dark flood to the place of the seal and stayed behind it as promised. They are fought as Black Serpent Knights with hoarse, pained voices; Zephaniah worries about leading the tribe away, and Irizar says he does not want a certain person to see him as he is.

## entry: Elder Traore
@keys: Traore
@keys2: Children of Echoes, Shadow Pin, tablet, Zephaniah
@version: 5.0
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Traore was the elder, storyteller and shield and spear of the Children of Echoes at the time of the sealing. According to Titu he was the first to leap into the pit. He left the Boundary Marker Inscription, a three-part blessing, and is remembered as a hero.

## entry: Yukom
@keys: Yukom
@keys2: Titu, Tequemecan Valley, sentry

@spoiler: none
### STANDARD
Yukom is a sentry in Tequemecan Valley who keeps waiting for Titu to relieve him from his post. He is tired and exasperated with Titu but worried when the earthquake strikes.
