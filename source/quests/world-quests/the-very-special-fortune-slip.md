## summary
### LITE
The Very Special Fortune Slip is a three-quest Inazuma world series (2.4) about a Fatui spy who replaced the shrine maiden Gendou Ringo at the Grand Narukami Shrine. The real Gendou Ringo, a Shuumatsuban ninja now using the codename Momoyo, asks the Traveler to find out what the Fatui are planning. They learn that the impostor, Lyudochka, has been ordered by her Teacher to poison Watatsumi Island. The Traveler helps stage her death, rescues Momoyo and persuades Lyudochka to stay and take up the name Gendou Ringo.
### STANDARD
The series is unlocked by a hidden exploration objective (Gendou Ringo's Strange Fortune Slips, named by fans) in which Ringo's odd fortune slips send the Traveler against Fatui agents. After Omnipresence Over Mortals, the three quests are Shuumatsuban Operations, The Replacement's Secret and Where the Dandelions Find Rest (the last needs Adventure Rank 40 for its quest domain). In order, the Traveler gathers intelligence from three disguised Fatui, tails the impostor with elemental powder to a meeting with the Teacher, takes her mission and finds her a hiding place, and finally frees Momoyo from the Fatui and talks Lyudochka out of leaving Inazuma.
### LARGE +
The quests combine spy comedy with an emotional core. Lyudochka is a House of the Hearth orphan who loves Inazuma and hates being a fraud, and who comes to admit what she actually wants. The Traveler acts alongside Paimon and the dry, impatient Momoyo, and the shrine's fortune slips are first a code and then a gift.
### ULTRA +
Momoyo's replacement means that for the whole series the shrine maiden the public sees is not the real Gendou Ringo. The Shuumatsuban's codes mention "Iron Rats" for themselves, "General Crabs" for the Kanjou Commission, and "Sun Crabs" for the International Trade Association, among other words, on the partly legible Fatui Code Sheet. Momoyo says Sayu helped copy the sheet, and jokes that the water stains are saliva.

## plot
@version: 2.4
### STANDARD
Prelude (hidden exploration objective). Over four real-world days, the Traveler draws a fortune slip, hands over the bamboo slip with the phrase "in the middle of a hazy night," and receives a "Strange Fortune Slip" assigning them a target. These are Fireblade Mourisu (a Fatui Pyro Agent), "Mousey Maki" (a Kairagi), Nobushi and shogunate samurai in a cave under Inazuma City, and a final location in a cave near Jakotsu Mine that leads to a safehouse. The wiki gives the objective's name as unofficial.

Shuumatsuban Operations. A passer-by slips a note with pictures of a shuriken and a metal gate into the Traveler's pocket outside the Adventurers' Guild. It leads to the safehouse, where Gendou Ringo, now using the codename Momoyo, gives them a mission. The Fatui have gone quiet, and the one at the shrine has not been in contact. Momoyo's team has found three undercover Fatui, and gives the Traveler a Fatui Code Sheet to pose as their comrade. They are Buzuleac, a Sumeru merchant at Ritou's south dock, Fujita, a merchant in Inazuma City, and Tae, a woman working at the hot springs. The Traveler has to pick the right responses to earn their trust. If they get it wrong the spy grows suspicious; if they reveal their allegiance they must fight. Together the three reveal that the replacement is named Lyudochka and that her superior, "the Teacher", will give her an assignment at Watatsumi Island that night. Momoyo hands over a bamboo slip dusted with elemental powder so she can be tailed with Elemental Sight.
### LARGE +
The Replacement's Secret. At the Grand Narukami Shrine the Traveler pretends to be an ordinary visitor and hands the dusted bamboo slip to the false Ringo. Her manner is nervous when the Traveler uses the old hazy-night phrase, and she answers that no such thing exists at the moment. She gives them a Modest Fortune (the Special Fortune Slip). When she leaves the shrine at night, the Traveler follows the powder trail to a meeting with the Teacher, Efim Snezhevich. He accuses Lyudochka of stalling and of not actually having the trust of the supposed Shuumatsuban members, and hands her written final instructions. Efim says that, with Signora dead in Inazuma, it is the right time to repay Arlecchino's kindness, and that chaos is the best soil for them.

Lyudochka, once alone, spots the Traveler and attacks, but Paimon says she did not seem to be fighting in earnest. She says she infiltrated the shrine only to reach the Shuumatsuban, learned that her supposed targets were the Traveler and Paimon, and kept stalling because she could not tell the Teacher the plan had failed, and because shrine life was like a dream. Her new order is "to poison": she and others must poison Watatsumi Island and expose themselves as a shrine maiden and as Tenryou Commission soldiers, to provoke conflict between the Shogunate and Watatsumi and give the Fatui an opening. She explains that "Snezhevich" and "Snezhevna" are surnames given to the orphans in Arlecchino's orphanage, that Efim is also a Snezhevich, and that his casual attitude toward more orphans being made disgusts her. The Traveler offers help, Lyudochka hides at Yae Publishing House's back room, where she once worked as a proofreader under a pseudonym, and gives them the written final instructions.

Back at the safehouse Momoyo recognises the importance of the instructions, but is first reluctant to save a Fatuus. She then asks for eight Raw Meat, four White Iron Chunks, three Sea Ganoderma and a set of clearly identifiable clothes to build a dummy corpse of Lyudochka, which takes a full day to make. Lyudochka's clothes are her Fatui uniform.

Where the Dandelions Find Rest. The safehouse has been raided by the Fatui, and Momoyo is gone, though she left a trail of elemental powder. In a cell beneath an abandoned house on Mt. Yougou the Traveler takes a key from Fatui junior leaders, who discuss that the Teacher was summoned back to Snezhnaya yet the Watatsumi operation continues, and that he had not reported it to Arlecchino. Momoyo is hurt: elemental powder got into one eye when the box was shot. She says the Teacher was fooled by the dummy and that Lyudochka will no longer need to live in fear, and the Shuumatsuban will deal with the poisoning plot.
### ULTRA +
Lyudochka has left Yae Publishing House when the Traveler returns, leaving a letter and a purse of Mora for them and Momoyo; she is leaving Inazuma, because she does not think she deserves to stay. Momoyo says that her eye was an occupational hazard, that Lyudochka can keep the name "Gendou Ringo" (which Momoyo gave herself on a whim) and that Yae Miko, the Guuji, had thought the impostor well suited to shrine work. At Ritou, the Traveler gives Lyudochka the Special Fortune Slip, whose words say to fly like seeds on the wind and one day become what you hope to be. She breaks down, and admits she always wanted to be a real shrine maiden who could bring people joy. She takes the name Gendou Ringo, and gives the Traveler a fortune slip of her own, "Lyudochka's Fortune Slip", as a blessing.

Momoyo also explains that Sayu is passing news to the Shuumatsuban, and says she was taken in as an orphan by Momoka, who let her join. After the series, Momoyo's dialogue (per the wiki) reveals Efim follows the directives of the previous Knave.

## revelations
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
- The shrine maiden of the Grand Narukami Shrine seen by the public since earlier events is a Fatui impostor named Lyudochka, not the real Gendou Ringo, who is a Shuumatsuban ninja now using the name Momoyo.
- The Fatui cell led by "the Teacher" planned to poison Watatsumi Island and have their agents exposed as shrine maiden and Tenryou Commission soldiers, to reignite conflict between the Shogunate and Watatsumi Island.
### LARGE +
- The Teacher is Efim Snezhevich, and the Snezhevich and Snezhevna names mark orphans raised in Arlecchino's House of the Hearth.
- Efim ordered the operation without reporting it to Arlecchino, according to his own subordinates.
### ULTRA +
- The Shuumatsuban faked Lyudochka's death with a prepared dummy to protect her from Efim.

## characters
### STANDARD
The Traveler and Paimon; Gendou Ringo (Momoyo); Lyudochka Snezhevna (the impostor); Efim Snezhevich (the Teacher); Buzuleac, Fujita and Tae (disguised Fatui); Aratani (Yae Publishing House); Pavel and Ilyin (Fatui junior leaders); Riku (who saw the impostor leave the shrine); Mikawa Akane, Yae Miko and Sayu in supporting roles. Arlecchino, Signora and Momoka are only mentioned.

## consequences
### STANDARD
The Watatsumi poison plot is exposed and the Shuumatsuban moves against it; Lyudochka stays in Inazuma and becomes the shrine's Gendou Ringo; Momoyo (the real Ringo) loses sight in one eye and stays in the shadows. The series gives Adventure EXP, Primogems, Hero's Wit, Mora, Mystic Enhancement Ore and quest items.
### LARGE +
The impostor stays at the shrine afterwards, under the name Gendou Ringo, and invites the Traveler to call her that. Efim appears to have left Inazuma, and Momoyo means to catch his remaining operatives.

## placement
### STANDARD
Released in 2.4. The Hidden Exploration Objective Gendou Ringo's Strange Fortune Slips and Omnipresence Over Mortals (the Inazuma Archon Quest) are required to start Shuumatsuban Operations. It follows the end of the Vision Hunt Decree and the Tenryou Commission's loss of cover for the Fatui, and is part of the Inazuma world quest storyline around the Shuumatsuban.

## entry: Lyudochka
@keys: Lyudochka
@keys2: Gendou Ringo, shrine maiden, Fatui
@version: 2.4
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Lyudochka Snezhevna is a House of the Hearth orphan who infiltrated the Grand Narukami Shrine in place of Gendou Ringo, hoping to reach the Shuumatsuban. She stalled her Teacher's orders rather than harm the Traveler, and in this series is persuaded to stay and become the real Gendou Ringo. See her own entry for her later life.

## entry: Momoyo
@keys: Momoyo
@keys2: Gendou Ringo, Shuumatsuban, safehouse
@version: 2.4
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Momoyo is the codename the Shuumatsuban ninja Gendou Ringo uses while the Fatui impostor holds her shrine post. She is gruff and sarcastic but helps the Traveler, builds the dummy corpse, loses use of one eye in the elemental powder accident and gives Lyudochka her blessing to use the name Gendou Ringo.

## entry: Fatui Code Sheet
@keys: Fatui Code Sheet, Shuumatsuban code
@version: 2.4
### STANDARD
A water-stained sheet given by Momoyo. It lists the Fatui's coded phrases, such as "Fellow countrymen who've forgotten their homeland" for self-identification, "hiking" for being on a mission and "sunbathing" for lying low, plus animal names for factions. The Traveler has to use it to pass as a Fatuus in conversation with the three spies.
