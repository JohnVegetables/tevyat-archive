## summary
### LITE
The Anomaly is a short world quest series added in Version 7.1 and unlocked by finishing the Chapter VII Act IV Archon Quest, A Rekviem for the Underworld. Celestial Nails (the "Nails of Retribution" cast down by the Heavenly Principles) around Teyvat have started to glow and make sounds, and the Traveler and Paimon visit six of them: in Snezhnaya, Mondstadt, Liyue, Inazuma, Sumeru and Natlan. Each stop is one conversation with a local figure who is watching the nail. Nothing is explained; the series records how the world is reacting.
### STANDARD +
Each quest is a "check out the situation" errand paying 20,000 Mora, with one conversation and an optional repeat line. The shared backdrop is that Ronova, the Ruler of Death, lost her authority and was defeated, after which the nails began to behave strangely; the Traveler's party suspects it is her revenge.

## plot
### STANDARD
All six quests share one briefing: something strange is happening with the Nail of Retribution across Teyvat. In each, the Traveler walks up to a named character already studying the nail.

Snezhnaya (east of Snezhnograd, White Birch Snowgrave). Alyosha, out hunting nearby, may have been among the first to see it. He told the local Druzhna so the Tsaritsa and the Fatui would know. Paimon asks whether this is Ronova's revenge; he says they must wait and see, notes that the nail only began acting oddly after Celestia's Ruler of Death was defeated, and hopes the military's best minds can work it out. His repeat line recalls that the Tsaritsa slew a monster near here recently.

Mondstadt (Dragonspine, Skyfrost Nail). Albedo says he has never witnessed such a thing in all his time on the mountain. He had assumed the nail's purpose ended once, after the Heavenly Principles' judgment, it destroyed the ancient civilisation of Sal Vindagnyr, and now sees it is not so simple. He stays to observe, especially the effect on the surrounding ecosystem.

Liyue (the Chasm, Cinnabar Cliff). Xiao says adepti and humans alike are concerned: more Millelith have been sent to the Chasm patrols and Cloud Retainer and other adepti are investigating. Rex Lapis (Zhongli) has told him to monitor the place so he can bring word swiftly. His repeat line is a brooding remark that if even Rex Lapis takes this so seriously, it is grave.

Inazuma (Tsurumi Island). Yae Miko, as Guuji, says someone reported unusual activity; she drew a fortune slip, got "Great Misfortune", and came to look. She teases Paimon about the form of address, then admits she cannot say what is happening but senses a faint power and plans to drag Kokomi there, who may know more.

Sumeru (Tanit Camps, Mt. Damavand). Candace and Dehya are already there. Dehya's mercenaries treat the pillar as a sightseeing spectacle; Dehya says Sumeru has suffered disaster after disaster and her crew camps nearby for a heads-up. Candace says the Desertfolk should not leave the Dendro Archon to bear the burden alone; both repeat lines say Sumeru is better prepared now because people stand together.

Natlan (Stadium of the Sacred Flame, Basin of Unnumbered Flames). Mavuika is lost in thought, staring at the pillar of light. She has heard the Traveler took down Ronova. Paimon says the party fears this is Ronova's revenge and feels no pride. Mavuika rejects the blame: she says Ronova and the Heavenly Principles always treated Natlan as a tool against the Abyss and the world's destruction, and that most of Natlan's past disasters came from them. She will tell local people to watch for danger.
### LARGE +
The quests are not chained: the wiki lists no previous or next quest for any of them, and each can be done on its own. In the Natlan scene Mavuika adds that, but for Capitano, it would have been her life Ronova took, and that she never liked Ronova either.

### ULTRA +
Quest steps as the wiki records them: each quest has a single objective to check out the situation, at the area east of Snezhnograd (Snezhnaya), Dragonspine, the Chasm, Tsurumi Island, Mt. Damavand (reached from the Tanit Camps) and the Stadium of the Sacred Flame. The wiki marks all six as voiced. Sumeru's nail sits in the Desert of Hadramaveth region, whose sandstorms the Celestial Nail page attributes to nails.

## revelations
### STANDARD
- Ronova fused the Eitr into a Celestial Nail at the end of A Rekviem for the Underworld, collapsing it; since then the other nails glow and give off a pillar of light that people everywhere have noticed.
- Albedo's assumption that Dragonspine's nail finished its work once it destroyed Sal Vindagnyr is now in doubt.
- Mavuika says outright that the Heavenly Principles used Natlan as a tool against the Abyss and the world's destruction.
### LARGE +
The wiki notes that Zhongli's and Mavuika's wariness suggests they know what such signs can mean, but neither says so in these quests. The glowing is tied to Ronova's fall only by the characters' suspicion, with no mechanism given. The wiki also notes the Snezhnaya spike is visible from Fontaine.

## characters
### STANDARD
The Traveler and Paimon appear in all six. Alyosha (Snezhnaya), Albedo (Mondstadt), Xiao (Liyue), Yae Miko (Inazuma), Candace and Dehya (Sumeru) and Mavuika (Natlan) each hold the position at their local nail. Mentioned but not present: the Tsaritsa, Zhongli (as Rex Lapis), Cloud Retainer, Kokomi, the Dendro Archon (Nahida), Capitano and Ronova.

## consequences
### STANDARD
No quest ends in a decision or a fight. The series leaves the world on watch: nails glowing worldwide, local powers observing, and the party aware that the Heavenly Principles may be reacting to Ronova's defeat. Nothing is resolved in 7.1.

## placement
### STANDARD
Available in 7.1 after A Rekviem for the Underworld (Chapter VII Act IV), and set right after that act. The six quests can be done in any order.
