## summary
### LITE
In the Footsteps of the Chosen of Dragons is the Natlan world quest series built around Little One, the young saurian that joins the Traveler and Paimon in Between Pledge and Forgettance. Fifteen small quests, available in any order, follow Little One's games, friends and gifts around Natlan; a sixteenth, Someday, We All Must Walk Alone, must be done last. The wiki summary is that the group hunts for a way to cleanse Little One's Flamelord's Blessing, which is finally done in the heart of the Great Volcano of Tollan, after which Little One leaves to travel alone.
### STANDARD
The series gathers the Little One side quests: A Saurian Lover's Ordinary Days, Feeling Like Fish Today!, Friends of Fire and Water (5.2), Gifts and Gifts in Return, Is "Intensity" Really the Key? (5.5), Lies and Promises, Lights, Kamera, Action!, Sauro-Vet's Dilemma (5.2), Special Friends, Stones, Coconuts, and Saurian Traffickers (5.2), Tepetlisaurus Hide-and-Seek, The Lone Isle Named Night (5.2), Treasures and Collectors, Waiting For Seeds to Sprout and Weighty Wings. They can be done in any order. Most are light errands that show Little One meeting other saurians and humans in Natlan. The final quest, Someday, We All Must Walk Alone (5.5), unlocks only after the main Little One quests are finished and a few hidden interactions around the map are completed. In it Little One takes a photograph with the Traveler and Paimon, then leaves on its own.
### LARGE +
Natlan is a nation where humans and saurians live side by side, so the series is a long, warm look at that relationship through one individual. The wiki frames the whole chapter as the search for a cure for the Flamelord's Blessing, a mark on Little One's horn that Manqu says kills or maddens saurians as they grow. The main journey for that is carried by Between Pledge and Forgettance, Path to the Flaming Peaks and A Finale Emberforged.
### ULTRA +
The wiki's summary also says that after the cure Little One gets to enjoy freedom and a connection to its dragon roots, and that the Traveler receives a smaller, permanent saurian pet for the Serenitea Pot. The pet detail is a gameplay note: two daily server resets after the last quest, a Little Tepetlisaurus appears at the Rest Area where Little One used to be, according to the quest page.

## plot
@version: 5.0
### STANDARD
The side quests share one setup. After Lost in the Woods, Little One travels with the Traveler and Paimon, and each quest starts from something Little One does or finds: catching fish, digging up a seed, meeting a group of alpacas, hiding among other Tepetlisauri, gifting its collected treasures, wanting to star in a film, or discovering a hidden chamber on the Isle of Night. Paimon narrates and mothers it; the Traveler handles the practical and puzzle work. Several quests take place in other tribes' territory, such as the Flower-Feather Clan range, and bring in local NPCs with their own saurian partners.

Someday, We All Must Walk Alone is the capstone. Once every Little One quest and a handful of unmarked puzzles are done, Little One leads the group back to the Rest Area where they first met, has found a Kamera, and sets up a group photograph. Afterwards it shows that it wants to go its own way. Paimon cries and makes the Traveler promise they will meet again; Little One tucks one photograph into its shell, nuzzles Paimon and the Traveler, and plods off.
### LARGE +
Quest-by-quest hooks from the wiki quest descriptions: A Saurian Lover's Ordinary Days starts when the Traveler meets someone surrounded by three saurians; Feeling Like Fish Today! has Paimon fishing for Little One; Friends of Fire and Water rescues a Blubberbeast that befriends Little One; Gifts and Gifts in Return has Little One giving the Traveler its collected treasures; Is "Intensity" Really the Key? asks whether intensity or something else will beat the cold; Lies and Promises centres on a Yumkasaurus blocking a treasure; Lights, Kamera, Action! is a film starring Little One; Sauro-Vet's Dilemma begins with a Qucusaurus apparently attacking a human; Special Friends is about Little One making new friends; Stones, Coconuts, and Saurian Traffickers helps a merchant and his saurian; Tepetlisaurus Hide-and-Seek starts when Little One does not come back from playing; The Lone Isle Named Night is a hidden chamber on the Isle of Tozoz; Treasures and Collectors is the Saurian Relics Excavation Site; Waiting For Seeds to Sprout follows a strange seed; Weighty Wings is an injured saurian in the wild.
### ULTRA +
The final quest's prerequisites include essentially all of Natlan's Little One content: the A Finale Emberforged, Between Pledge and Forgettance, Chronicler of the Crumbling City, From One Case to Another, Lost Traveler in the Ashen Realm, Path to the Flaming Peaks, Shadows of the Mountains and Tale of Dreams Plucked From Fire chains, several single quests (including The Way Into the Mountain, The Road Ahead and Open Your Heart to Me), and three hidden interactions: an S-rank in Urubamba's Warrior's Challenge, a Kamera puzzle on Quahuacan Cliff, and a Secret Source Dragon Idol in the Sea of Shifting Sentience. Paimon guesses Little One got the Kamera from Akatanga, a would-be director from the Children of Echoes whom she recalls using his Kamera the wrong way round; Akatanga is also listed in Lights, Kamera, Action!.

## revelations
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The series makes few story revelations of its own. It shows that Little One understands more than it can say and that it has quietly kept hold of keepsakes from the journey. In the closing quest Little One sets up the photograph and then departs; the narration says the Traveler recalls the mix of feelings in its eyes at the foot of the Sacred Mountain.
### LARGE +
Because the final quest is gated behind A Finale Emberforged, anyone reading it has already seen that the Flamelord's Blessing was cleansed in the volcano and that the Sage of the Stolen Flame (Kukulkan) had darker plans. The farewell is therefore a human-scale epilogue to the larger Natlan story.

## characters
### STANDARD
Little One is a young Tepetlisaurus with a red Flamelord's Blessing mark on its horn and a scarf from Paimon tied to its tail. It speaks only in growls and is treated as "he" by Paimon and the Traveler in several lines. It is energetic, curious, a hearty eater, and loyal. Paimon is protective and tearful at the parting; the Traveler is calmer but affected. Other quest NPCs include Icayo, Imix and Kulahaw, Linaha, Ma'chah and Yoleehe, Wayna, Mazkal, Qax, Quipia, and Akatanga, each tied to one quest.
### LARGE +
Little One is the only recurring character; the wiki character lists show the rest as one-off figures, such as the saurians "Jade", "Marigold" and "Ripple" in A Saurian Lover's Ordinary Days and the alpacas of Special Friends.

## consequences
### STANDARD
Completing Someday, We All Must Walk Alone ends Little One's time with the party. It gives the achievement Then You Will Be Free to..., the avatar "Till We Meet Again", 20 Primogems, a Photo With Travel Companions and Mora. After it the souvenirs in the Rest Area disappear, except for the Unhatched Saurian Egg.
### LARGE +
Per the series summary, the Traveler also receives a smaller, permanent saurian pet in the Serenitea Pot, a Little Tepetlisaurus.

## placement
### STANDARD
Most side quests released in 5.0 with Natlan; Friends of Fire and Water, Sauro-Vet's Dilemma, Stones, Coconuts, and Saurian Traffickers and The Lone Isle Named Night joined in 5.2; Is "Intensity" Really the Key? and Someday, We All Must Walk Alone arrived in 5.5. In story order the side quests can be done any time after Lost in the Woods, but the finale quest should be done after A Finale Emberforged and all other Little One content.
### LARGE +
Between Pledge and Forgettance, Path to the Flaming Peaks and A Finale Emberforged supply the main emotional arc; this series is the optional companion.

## entry: Someday, We All Must Walk Alone
@keys: Someday We All Must Walk Alone, Little One's farewell, Till We Meet Again
@version: 5.5
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Someday, We All Must Walk Alone is the final quest of the series. In the Rest Area where they first met, Little One shows the Traveler and Paimon a Kamera (a camera-like gadget), performs a move Paimon recognises as the Mighty Meaty Fireball, and takes a photograph with them. It then says goodbye. Paimon tearfully makes it promise to come find them if it needs help, and tells it to stay healthy and big. Little One nuzzles them and leaves.
### LARGE +
Quest text hints at the Sage's role too: Paimon complains that the Sage never showed his face, and the Traveler thinks back to the feelings in Little One's eyes at the foot of the Sacred Mountain.

## entry: Kamera and the Mighty Meaty Fireball
@keys: Mighty Meaty Fireball, Kamera
@keys2: Little One
@version: 5.0
### STANDARD
Lights, Kamera, Action! is the quest in which the Traveler shoots a film starring Little One, and Akatanga appears in it. In the farewell quest Paimon recalls Akatanga as a would-be director who used his Kamera backwards and guesses Little One got its Kamera from him. Paimon also recognises a pose of Little One's as the Mighty Meaty Fireball, and the three say the phrase for the photograph. It is a light, comic detail contrasted with the parting.
