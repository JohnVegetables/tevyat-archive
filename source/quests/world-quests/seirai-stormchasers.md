## summary
### LITE
A version 2.1 world quest series on Seirai Island, Inazuma. The Adventurers' Guild cannot explore the island because of constant storms. Katheryne sends the Traveler to help two adventurers, Etsuko and Taisuke, and the Traveler meets a talking cat who knows how to seal the Warding Stones that keep the storm at bay.
### STANDARD +
The series has four parts. The Traveler follows a cat's tracks to the Asase Shrine, repairs its Offering Box for the cat Neko, learns how to seal four Warding Stones, and seals them, two on the ground and two in the sky. A boss, the Thunder Manifestation, is fought on a platform of floating stones, and the Traveler finds a strange feather, the Peculiar Pinion.
### LARGE +
Completing the series removes the Balethunder from the island, unlocks the Thunder Manifestation as a boss and awards an achievement of the same name. It is also the first meeting with Neko, who returns in later Inazuma quests.

## plot
### LITE
Katheryne says the guild's Seirai exploration is blocked by wild storms and suggests the Traveler help. On the island Etsuko explains that going toward the centre is too dangerous because of Balethunder, and that they have found broken stone structures they think are called Warding Stones.
### STANDARD +
Etsuko and Taisuke focus on the stones while the Traveler and Paimon follow strange cat tracks to a shrine in better shape and fairer weather than the rest of the island. There the cat Neko, who calls herself the provisional head priestess of the Asase Shrine, is worried about the damaged Offering Box, since Hibiki would grieve to see the shrine in a sorry state. She agrees to teach the Traveler about the Warding Stones in exchange for repairs, and describes the bird of most prodigious size the stones were meant to keep sealed.
The Traveler tells the adventurers (Taisuke is thrilled by the talking cat) and the group seals the first two stones. The other two prove puzzling until the Traveler realises they are in the sky, and goes up through a Phase Gate. Sealing them forms a huge platform from the floating stones, where the Traveler defeats a Thunder Manifestation and then finds the Peculiar Pinion.
### LARGE +
Back on the ground the storm has passed. Taisuke cannot identify the Pinion and suggests posting a commission, and Etsuko plans to file the guild report, but Taisuke insists on visiting the Asase Shrine first. In Part IV the Traveler touches three paper-charm hangers and rotates the final Warding Stone; three chests that appeared on the floating rocks drop to sea level afterwards.

## revelations
@version: 2.1
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
The Warding Stones were made to seal a huge bird, and breaking them let the storm and the Thunder Manifestation loose. The wiki's profile of Neko, built on this and later material, says the wards were broken in the time of Asase Hibiki and Ako Domeki, when a Shogunate fleet attacked, after which Hibiki and Domeki vanished and the island stayed in storm for around 500 years. This series itself tells only part of that, through Neko's words.
### LARGE +
The Peculiar Pinion's origin is not explained in the series.

## characters
### LITE
The Traveler and Paimon; Katheryne; Etsuko and Taisuke of the Adventurers' Guild; the cat Neko.
### STANDARD +
Etsuko is a guild adventurer in the standard uniform and keeps Taisuke on topic. Neko is a large black cat with golden eyes who has lived for at least five centuries and styles herself provisional head priestess.

### ULTRA +
The wiki's Neko profile describes later events outside this series: Neko has a statue made by the artist Ooshima Junpei, takes a walk to find the island's village empty, and visits the Grand Narukami Shrine, where she admires its large offering box while insisting hers is better. These belong to Neko Is a Cat and are not part of the stormchasing.

## consequences
### STANDARD
The storm clears and Seirai Island is open to exploration. Neko stays at the Asase Shrine, and her story continues in the quest Neko Is a Cat.

## placement
### STANDARD
Released in Version 2.1. The wiki lists Ritou Escape Plan as its requirement. Part IV is followed by A Particularly Particular Author in the quest ordering.
