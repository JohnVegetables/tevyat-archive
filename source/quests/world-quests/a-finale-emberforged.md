## summary
### LITE
A Finale Emberforged is the last chapter of the Little One storyline in Natlan, consisting of a single quest, An Omen of Annihilation and the Final Entreaty. The Traveler and Little One offer both Golden Entreaties in the Heart of the Sacred Mountain and face the Sage of the Stolen Flame, who proves to be Kukulkan. He wants a dragon to activate the Huitzilopochtli array, which would turn Natlan into pure phlogiston. Little One refuses, breaks free, and with the Traveler defeats the Source Dragon Mech; its Flamelord's Blessing is cleansed.
### STANDARD
In An Omen of Annihilation and the Final Entreaty (5.5), the Traveler and Paimon bring Little One to the central device in the Heart of the Sacred Mountain and offer both Golden Entreaties. Inside the light they are ambushed by a Primeval Source Dragon Mech while a voice remarks that such stories never end without an evil dragon. The Traveler is locked in a Laser Jail and falls in battle. Little One, alone, uses its dragon blood, and a calm voice offers guidance. Little One is shown the truth in a vision and briefly gains a mature dragon's form and speech. The speaker is Kukulkan, whom humans called the Sage of the Stolen Flame. He explains that the cleansing was a lie of omission and that he wants the Natlan Phlogistonization Array, Huitzilopochtli, activated to wall off the Abyss. Little One cancels the activation, refuses to put the Traveler and Paimon at risk and fights its way out. Back at normal size it helps the Traveler break the party out and defeat the Mech again. The Flamelord's Blessing is lifted.
### LARGE +
Kukulkan's reasoning is that the Abyss will keep coming, that humans have not built a united defence in thousands of years, and that his faith in them has run out. His plan would convert Natlan to a land of phlogiston, with the old dragons ruling a "new world". He names the Chosen of Dragons as both the chosen dragon and the chosen human who brings it, saying the human's duty ends on arrival. The quest ends with Little One shrunk back down, the mark on its horn dimmed and cool, and the party leaving to celebrate with stew and cake.
### ULTRA +
The player controls an indwelled adult Little One for most of the middle of the quest and a trial Traveler in the two boss fights. The tutorial "Human and Saurian, as One!" appears when Little One joins the second fight, which introduces commanding Little One to break the Laser Jail and block the Mech's claw attacks. After the quest Titu has updated dialogue.

## plot
@version: 5.5
### STANDARD
Entry. At the central mechanism the Traveler hears golden text telling the Chosen of Dragons to offer the Golden Entreaties and ignite the Sacred Flame. The two Entreaties are shattered, and their markings spiral together into a single inscription that emits a pulse of golden light. Paimon recaps their journey, including the fight with Och-Kan (who turned out to be Coco), before the party steps into the light.

The trial. The space is empty. A voice says: "Learn the truth and make your choice." The Primeval Source Dragon Mech appears. About a minute into the fight the Traveler is trapped by its Laser Jail and falls. Paimon is also locked up.

Little One. From Little One's viewpoint, it tries to open the cage, uses the power in its blood despite the Traveler's warning, and begins to be overwhelmed. A voice calls it Chosen of Dragons and guides the power so that it supports rather than devours it. Little One is shown a white void and awakens as a mature dragon able to speak. The speaker is Kukulkan.
### LARGE +
Truth. Kukulkan shows Little One the Heart of the Sacred Mountain, where the first Pyro Sovereign shaped the Dragonborn, and tells his account: Xiuhcoatl was frozen in lava by the "reaver from beyond the skies", awoke when Nibelung descended, and was left lucid only in brief moments. Before his death he transformed the dragon city and created the Tzitzimimeh and Huitzilopochtli. The array would turn Natlan into pure phlogiston, so that the Abyss could never gain a foothold. Everyone in Natlan would die or give up their bodies. Kukulkan took Little One to the Tonatiuh and the Sacred City to show what had become of his people, and says he gave humanity extra time before losing faith.

Choice. Kukulkan asks Little One to confirm the activation three times. Little One remembers the Traveler and Paimon. If it confirms, the screen goes white; the narration describes a world where only "it" remains, pondering whether another choice existed. The route followed in the canon story is to cancel. Kukulkan tries to keep Little One with taunts (that the Traveler and Paimon do not care, that its power will vanish and its speech with it) but Little One says they matter to it and that if there is no path back to them, it will make one. It breaks through the golden obstacles to the cage and frees them.

The final fight. With the cage broken, Little One is again in adult form, helps the Traveler and Paimon, and the party defeats the Mech. Golden text says the Flamelord's Blessing has been dispelled, thanks the Chosen of Dragons for all they have done, and says it is hard to say, but "I love you". Little One returns to its normal size and faints; the volcano shakes. On the platform by Huitzilopochtli Kukulkan says he finally understands Xiuhcoatl's feelings and admits that if he truly wanted a dragon to activate the array, he should have raised it in the Sacred City to hate this world; he sent it to a human's side. On the surface the volcano only vents a little smoke and Little One wakes with the mark dim.
### ULTRA +
Details from the vision. Kukulkan changes into forms of Manqu and Bona to show off the transformation arts that phlogiston allows, and invites Little One to do the same. Little One declines. Kukulkan explains that Tonatiuh was once a cable to reach beyond the sky, later reconfigured, and that Och-Kan hid a Golden Entreaty there. The tower network that anchors Huitzilopochtli's coverage was mostly destroyed on Och-Kan's orders, but its fragments are enough. He says Xbalanque exploited Xiuhcoatl's one weakness, a modified pupil serving as Huitzilopochtli's control hub, and that both died; Xbalanque was reborn as the Pyro Archon.

## revelations
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
- The Sage of the Stolen Flame is Kukulkan, who also bears other names such as Waxaklahun Ubah Kan.
- The Flamelord's Blessing is the dragons' own bloodline mark on the horn. Kukulkan says it kills most unguided saurians, and that he chose a dragon and a human together to reach his goal, creating the legend on purpose; many humans have died for it.
- Huitzilopochtli is a Natlan-wide device built by Xiuhcoatl that would transform the land into phlogiston, leaving the survivors as phlogiston-based beings.
### LARGE +
- Phlogiston is described as an information-carrying substance in which Iridescent Inscriptions can imitate any form.
- The Golden Entreaties are Huitzilopochtli's control hub, split in two between Kukulkan and Xbalanque and hidden through Och-Kan and the Tonatiuh.
- Kukulkan hoped to learn from Och-Kan how the one seated upon heaven's throne turned the Ley Lines into a girdle defending the planet and to merge the human and dragon races, but says human souls vary too much and are too fragile.
### ULTRA +
- Xiuhcoatl was steeped in the Abyss, and his last lucid moments produced Huitzilopochtli. Kukulkan says the dragons who gave up their bodies and sleep in the sealed Sacred City consume vast resources.
- The wording "Chosen of Dragons" covers both the chosen dragon and the chosen human who brings it; the human's part ends on arrival.

## characters
### STANDARD
Little One (a whelp, briefly a speaking mature dragon), the Traveler, Paimon, and Kukulkan as the persuasive antagonist. The Primeval Source Dragon Mech is the boss.
### LARGE +
Kukulkan combines sorrow, contempt for humanity's stagnation and a clear affection for Little One. The quest also mentions Och-Kan, Xbalanque, Xiuhcoatl and Nibelung only in his account. Paimon appears in cutscenes and as a trapped companion.

## consequences
### STANDARD
Little One is no longer afflicted by the Flamelord's Blessing: the mark has cooled and dimmed. The party celebrates. The quest awards Adventure EXP, Primogems, Mora, Hero's Wit, Mystic Enhancement Ore and The Cornerstone of Stars and Flames. It also unlocks, with many other conditions, the farewell quest Someday, We All Must Walk Alone in In the Footsteps of the Chosen of Dragons.
### LARGE +
Titu has updated dialogue after the quest.

## placement
### STANDARD
Released in 5.5 as the finale of the Natlan world quest storyline for Little One. It follows Chronicler of the Crumbling City (whose last part is As the Burning Sun Sears Shadows), Path to the Flaming Peaks, Lost Traveler in the Ashen Realm and Between Pledge and Forgettance.

## entry: Huitzilopochtli
@keys: Huitzilopochtli, Natlan Phlogistonization Array, Sunquake, Sweeping Skyfire
@version: 5.5
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Huitzilopochtli is the Natlan Phlogistonization Array, built from Xiuhcoatl's design at the Heart of the Sacred Mountain. In Kukulkan's account it would burn everything in Natlan and log its information into phlogiston, turning the land into a realm of primal flame that annihilates the Abyss when it approaches. Life would either die or become phlogiston-based, and ley lines and bodies would be gone. Its activation requires both Golden Entreaties and three confirmations to start or cancel.
### LARGE +
Xiuhcoatl's control hub for it was one of his own pupils, which Kukulkan calls his only weakness. The sequence's name for the strike is Sweeping Skyfire.

## entry: Kukulkan's offer
@keys: Kukulkan, Sage of the Stolen Flame
@keys2: Little One, Golden Entreaty, Huitzilopochtli
@version: 5.5
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Kukulkan offers Little One wisdom, power and a place as shaper of a new world in exchange for activating the array. He is open that the legend of the cure was a deliberate fiction and that the human was only a means. He is moved when Little One refuses, saying that it made the same choice he and Och-Kan once made.

## entry: Primeval Source Dragon Mech
@keys: Primeval Source Dragon Mech, Source Dragon Mech
@version: 5.5
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
A guardian machine that fights the Traveler in the trial. It uses a Laser Jail to trap the active character, a charged beam and claw attacks. In the first fight Little One is an ally; in the second, Little One has grown and joins as an adult.
