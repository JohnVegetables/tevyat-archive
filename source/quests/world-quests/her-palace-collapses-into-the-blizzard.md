## summary
@spoiler: minor
### LITE
Her Palace Collapses Into the Blizzard is a World Quest series in Snezhnaya, added in version 7.0, that contains a single quest, An Ark That Used to Be a Soul. It ends the hunt for the three missing fragments of the Eye of Graeae, the talking device the Traveler and Paimon call "Goggles". Once all three are recovered, the Traveler opens the door to the Court of Eternal Bliss at the Ruined Hvergelmir and learns what Goggles was built for.
### STANDARD +
It begins at the Ruined Hvergelmir in the White Birch Snowgrave area (Pale Crown Palace subarea) and requires the Archon Quest chapter "Gunfire in the Silent Lands" plus the three sibling series: Hesperides of Love and Hate, In the Dwelling of Life and On One Side a Palace, On the Other a Tomb. Each of those yields one Pebble, which together are the three power fragments. The finale is a two-phase boss fight against Aksinya and then the Primordial Human.
### LARGE +
The series is the destination of a long mystery about who Goggles is. Its answer is deliberately bittersweet: the Eye's consciousness turns out to be the Key meant to complete the Belyi Tsar, and in the end the Traveler's party keeps only an empty, mechanical Eye of Graeae.

## plot
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Having collected the three fragments, the Traveler and Paimon go to the door of the Court of Eternal Bliss at the Ruined Hvergelmir. Goggles explains that his maker, Aksinya, once of Hvergelmir (the research body of the Belyi Tsar), built him to guide a great power toward a "Tomorrow": a world without suffering, envy, or the line between humans and gods. He fears what recovering his last power may do to his purpose. The Traveler and Paimon tell him he can choose once he remembers everything. They open the gate by feeding Kresnik Energy to the Tsar's White Birch.

Inside, Goggles vanishes and Aksinya appears. She says he has gone where he belongs. He was never meant to take back an old body: he is the "Proto-Man", the Key that completes the Belyi Tsar into the true Primordial Human. The vessel is the still-living husk of the long-dead Tsar, a body Hyperborea tried to forge from the flesh of the Cryo Dragon Sovereign Nibelung. That experiment once dragged Hyperborea into the Shadow Realm and froze Snezhnaya, but the Tsar later awoke and accepted the role of the failed project's successor.

Aksinya states her goal: to spread the Shadow Realm over the world so that love and joy never fade and every moment feels as fulfilling as the first, ending desire, envy and change. The Traveler observes this means a world without change, and she agrees, since in her view time wears love away. When the Traveler and Paimon reject it, she says there will still be a place for them, and teleports away into the Shadow Realm, so the Traveler chases her while her ritual proceeds.
### LARGE +
Beneath the White Birch they find a huge mechanical being. Aksinya gives them the now-empty Eye of Graeae as a "gift", saying Goggles' consciousness has become the core of the Velikan of Light, Svarod. The Primordial Human awakens and speaks as Goggles, with his memory complete. He says he deliberately split himself into fragments and encouraged Koshchei, Pavlina and Alvis to betray Aksinya, because he knew becoming the Primordial Human would set off an irreversible revolution of the world. He adds that Aksinya has left herself out of her own utopia, since someone who distrusts love cannot enter a realm of eternal love, and that she means to build it from her own corpse. Bound by duty and memory, he cannot simply refuse, so he asks the Traveler and Paimon to stop them both.

Before the fight he asks a question: if the Traveler's sibling became someone unrecognisable, what would the Traveler do? The Traveler answers that they would learn to love them again, however many times it took, and he calls this a wonderful answer. The fight has two phases, first Aksinya, then the Primordial Human, and the empty Eye of Graeae unexpectedly helps through a modified train and its "Final Protocol".
### ULTRA +
Defeated, the Primordial Human sheds the giant form and becomes a normal human body resembling the young Tsar. He and Aksinya fall together into the depths of the Shadow Realm. Aksinya admits defeat and reconsiders her philosophy: someone who truly believes in love should not fear losing it, since she could simply seek it again. He replies that loving the world begins with loving oneself, and that the best ending left to them may be the two of them, nameless, in a world "as small as a walnut shell".

Outside, the Traveler and Paimon slip away before anyone notices the Tsar's body is gone. Paimon repeatedly tries to "crack open" the Eye in case Goggles is hiding inside as in a story, but only a cold mechanical voice answers. Paimon reflects that he was brave to choose to stay with Aksinya, promises to stay loyal to the Traveler whatever happens, and hurries them out of the area.

## revelations
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
- Goggles (the Eye of Graeae's consciousness) is the "Proto-Man", a Key created by Aksinya at Hvergelmir to complete the Belyi Tsar into the Primordial Human.
- Hyperborea tried to forge the Tsar's body from the Cryo Dragon Sovereign Nibelung; the Tsar's husk was still living when the Traveler arrived.
- Aksinya intends to spread the Shadow Realm across the world as a changeless realm of lasting love.
- The Primordial Human says he engineered his own fragmentation and the betrayal of Aksinya by Koshchei, Pavlina and Alvis.
### LARGE +
- The Velikan of Light, Svarod, is the giant form the Primordial Human takes in the fight.
- Aksinya's answer after the defeat changes: she concludes that believing in love means not fearing its loss.

## characters
### STANDARD
- The Traveler and Paimon, who refuse a changeless world.
- The Eye of Graeae ("Goggles"), who becomes the Primordial Human and then leaves only a mechanical shell behind.
- Aksinya, Hvergelmir's former researcher and the Tsar's Court Jester, presented here as the series' antagonist, though sympathetic and ultimately self-doubting.
- The Primordial Human, the awakened Belyi Tsar's body with Goggles' mind in it.
### LARGE +
- Koshchei, Pavlina and Alvis are mentioned only as the three the Primordial Human says he provoked into betraying Aksinya. Pavlina is the antagonist of Hesperides of Love and Hate, and Koshchei is mentioned in the Pale Crown Palace flashbacks as Zver's creator.

## consequences
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The Traveler no longer has Goggles as a companion, only the empty Eye of Graeae. Aksinya's plan and the fight with the Primordial Human end there. After collecting all three fragments, Co-Op Mode (including the Serenitea Pot and Miliastra Wonderland access) is disabled until the quest is finished, as the wiki notes. Completing the quest awards the achievement "A Thousand Affections Caught in a Sigh" and the character avatar card of Aksinya.
### LARGE +
The quest reuses the Eye of Graeae's gadget heavily in its mechanics: during the chase in the Shadow Realm the gadget is temporarily removed and ammunition is set to zero, then both return afterwards (wiki gameplay notes, game-mechanic information rather than story).

## placement
### STANDARD
Story order: it is the capstone of the Snezhnaya Eye of Graeae arc and comes after the three sibling series, which can be done in any order. It also requires the Archon Quest "Gunfire in the Silent Lands". Release order: added in version 7.0 with the other Snezhnaya World Quest series.
### LARGE +
The related Pale Crown Palace quests, "To Gaze Upon the Dreaming Pale Crown" and "The Stone Beasts, Like Ghouls Seated", belong to On One Side a Palace, On the Other a Tomb and set up the Tsar, Zver and Hvergelmir background used here.

## entry: Proto-Man and the Primordial Human
@keys: Proto-Man, Primordial Human, Svarod, Velikan of Light
@version: 7.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
In Aksinya's account the "Proto-Man" is a Key, created at Hvergelmir to complete the Belyi Tsar into the Primordial Human. Goggles is revealed to be this Key. In the finale the Primordial Human appears as the giant mechanical Velikan of Light, Svarod, speaks with Goggles' restored memory, and asks the Traveler to stop both himself and Aksinya.
### LARGE +
After he is defeated the giant form falls away and he becomes a human body that resembles the young Tsar, which then sinks into the Shadow Realm with Aksinya.

## entry: Aksinya's Tomorrow
@keys: Aksinya's Tomorrow, Court of Eternal Bliss, world without change
@version: 7.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Aksinya's plan is to cover the world in the Shadow Realm so that love and joy never fade. In her view time erodes love, so this realm would hold every feeling at its first fullness, which also means ending desire, envy and change. The Traveler rejects this as a world without change. By the end she doubts her own reasoning.
