## summary
@spoiler: minor
### LITE
Through the Mists is a daily-gated World Quest series on Tsurumi Island, added in version 2.2. The author Sumida commissions the Traveler to find a Maushiro on the fog-shrouded island, where a child named Ruu and his people seem to live though their civilisation vanished long ago. The Traveler learns the island is locked in a curse from the Thunderbird Kapatcir and helps end it.
### STANDARD +
The series has four quests: A Particularly Particular Author, Octave of the Maushiro, The Sea of Fog and the Rite of the Trees and The Sun-Wheel and Mt. Kanna. Each needs the daily reset after the previous one. A follow-up quest, The Saga of Mr. Forgetful, and several hidden exploration objectives (such as Una's Longing and Rero's Joke) follow. It requires Seirai Stormchasers.
### LARGE +
It is the sequel in feeling to Seirai Island's story: it reuses the Peculiar Pinion found there and explains the thunderbird connection to the Raiden Shogun's earlier killing of Kapatcir. Finishing the series grants the achievement Thunder Is Forever and a viewpoint near Mt. Kanna.

## plot
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The Traveler takes Sumida's commission at the Inazuma Adventurers' Guild and has Kama ferry them to Tsurumi Island. The fog parts only after they go through a gate. They meet Ruu making a clumsy offering at a Perch, though Sumida says the island's civilisation ended thousands of years ago. Ruu says the island must hold a ceremony. After making offerings at three Perches the Traveler finds a Maushiro, but it vanishes before it is handed over, as it did for other adventurers.

In the second quest the adventurers compare notes. Ruu explains that the Thunderbird's feathers were the first Maushiro. The Traveler's Peculiar Pinion from Seirai Island lights up and shows an illusion of Kama burying something; digging there finds the Maushiro Sumida wants. The Traveler searches for Ruu, who is missing, then finds the locals gone again and delivers the Maushiro to Sumida.
### LARGE
Now asked to investigate further, the Traveler goes to Ritou and learns from Kama that his ancestors came from Tsurumi Island. The full story emerges: as conditions on the island worsened, Ruu offered himself as a sacrifice to Kapatcir, enraging the Thunderbird, who cursed the island and its people to re-live the ceremony and its destruction forever. Believing that the Perches are the key, the Traveler destroys them all, which brings a crimson lightning storm. Ruu, who thought the ceremony would please Kanna Kapatcir, is upset and runs off.

Returning with Kama, the Traveler uses the Peculiar Pinion to glimpse the Thunderbird's memories, and finds Ruu at the Autake Plains. Kama tells him his ancestors lived happily after leaving and only regretted not returning for him. The Traveler suggests the ceremony may not be what Kapatcir wanted and shows Ruu the memory of when he first sang for her. At Mt. Kanna they defeat a Thunder Manifestation, and learn the curse will lift only when Kapatcir hears Ruu's song again. Because the Raiden Shogun killed Kapatcir, the Traveler and Paimon say she moved to Seirai Island; the Pinion's power carries Ruu there and his song lifts the curse.
### ULTRA +
Back on Tsurumi the land is cleansed. Ruu thanks the Traveler, apologises for his mistake, and is keen to see the outside world later; for now he chooses to rest on the island, and with the promise kept and the loop ended the series implies he will soon pass on. The Traveler reports to Sumida. Gameplay notes: fog clears towards the end of each quest and returns the next day; during the last quest the fog and lightning turn red (cosmetic only).

## revelations
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
- Tsurumi Island's civilisation is gone; the people the Traveler meets are illusions of the past repeating a cursed ceremony.
- Kama's ancestors came from Tsurumi Island.
- The Thunderbird Kapatcir cursed the island after Ruu offered himself as a sacrifice.
### LARGE +
- The Thunderbird's feathers were the original Maushiro.
- The Raiden Shogun had killed Kapatcir; the Traveler and Paimon told Ruu she had moved to Seirai Island to spare him the truth.
- The curse could only end when Kapatcir heard Ruu's song again.

## characters
### STANDARD
The Traveler, Paimon, Sumida (author), Kama (boatman of Ritou), Ruu (child of Tsurumi Island), the tribal priest and other illusory islanders, and Kapatcir (Thunderbird, seen in memory).

## consequences
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The curse on Tsurumi Island is lifted, the fog returns daily as ordinary scenery, and new interactables open for exploration. Ruu is released from his loop. Sumida's commission ends.

## placement
### STANDARD
Released in 2.2. Requires Seirai Stormchasers (Seirai Island, 2.0) and reuses the Peculiar Pinion obtained there.
