## summary
### LITE
In the Dwelling of Life is a seven-part world quest series added in 7.0 in Flamefeather Valley, Snezhnaya. The Traveler and Paimon find a small girl named Nastya, who has branches growing from her head, asleep in a crate of loot, and escort her home to Okurov, a village sealed off from the outside world. There they learn that a "Doctor Moreau" has been leading the villagers into a ritual that turns people into a single eternal "Perfect Tree", and that the doctor is really the bio-alchemist Koshchei. Nastya chooses the ritual anyway, and the series ends with the Traveler recovering a fragment of the Eye of Graeae's power.
### STANDARD +
The series parts are: A Sprout Without a Gardener; Transplanted From "Yesterday" to "Tomorrow"; Down in the Dumps; Back on One's Feet; Forbidding Doors of Melancholy; To Rest in a Forgotten Field; and Chorus of Solace. Each title echoes a line from Nelly Sachs's poem "Chorus of the Consolers" (the final title echoes the poem's own title), according to the wiki's trivia; this is an observation of the title pattern, not an in-game statement.
### LARGE +
It is a small-cast, unsettling story about a sealed village, and it runs at the same time as the first Snezhnaya Archon Quest chapter. Inside Okurov the Traveler plays in a third-person shooter style, per the wiki's Okurov trivia.

## plot
### LITE
At the Morepesok train station, the Traveler and Paimon overhear Vinezka and her fiance Jary. Jary has been hired by criminals to blast open a tunnel to the abandoned town of Okurov, and Vinezka fears the local legend of a firebird that lures people into the valley and devours them. The Traveler takes the job. In the valley they defeat a Volkodlak gang led by "The Anvil" and find Nastya asleep in a crate; she lives in Okurov and speaks of a guardian called Zhara. She leads them through the tunnel toward her home. In the end Nastya enters the ritual, dissolves into glowing blue algae and joins the Perfect Tree.
### STANDARD +
In A Sprout Without a Gardener, The Anvil claims the "treasure" he stole from Okurov is inside the blocked tunnel and tries to enforce a hundred-times penalty on Jary. The "treasure" turns out to be Nastya, who fears she has been kidnapped by Volkodlak again, says she has slept in the dark for what felt like a short time, and clears the path with a magic hook. A newspaper in a wrecked carriage praises Okurov's warm weather, its exceptional doctor and its open-door policy; the air near the tunnel's end is unnaturally warm.

In the second quest, Nastya describes her town's belief that the assembly hall is the true home. She idolises "Doctor Moreau", whom she also calls "Doctor Birdie", who taught that becoming family means growing branches and leaves until everyone is the same. She calls outsiders "Badbugs", who in her telling are burned to feed the soil, and they play a game of "Catch the Bug" that she secretly uses to lead the party among hostile Leshy she regards as family. Then Zhara, a huge winged creature, attacks.
### LARGE +
In Down in the Dumps, the party wakes in a flooded sewer. The Eye of Graeae explains that Zhara tries to preserve the status quo and that the transformations are linked to Koshchei the Deathless, one of the creators of its body. The party solves gate and valve puzzles, collects Red, White and Blue keys and escapes by lift. In Back on One's Feet, the party reaches Nastya, who is blocked from the assembly hall by a hostile neighbour, the Leshy "No-Nose" Ove, who has hidden the elevator's control wheel and locks children in his cellar. They retrieve the wheel from his house.

In Forbidding Doors of Melancholy, Nastya's idol Furiosa, a woman who grew branches after an experiment by the Doctor, warns that no one should reach the hall and fights the Traveler. Inside the Hall of Prayers, Nastya recalls reciting prayers for Zhara about a "firebird" becoming a "silent tree". Past the curtain relief of Zhara, a dial door asks for a "salt" to replace the "body of the past"; Nastya offers a twig from her own head after explaining the three steps of casting off soul (sulfur), will (silver) and body. Notes found beyond reveal that the Doctor is Koshchei and describe the "final ceremony". To Rest in a Forgotten Field takes the party past Zhara again and through glowing blue algae to the great tree where Nastya's "home" is, with another fight against Zhara.

Chorus of Solace opens after Zhara falls. Nastya tells the Traveler that "Doctor Moreau" and her late mother have welcomed her into the Perfect Tree and that she wants to see her mother's eyes once more. Paimon begs her to leave and the Traveler reminds her of the life waiting outside, but Nastya says her wish to be with her mother outweighs playing with friends or eating. The two respect the choice. She says she will tell her mother about them in the "world beyond eternity" and then disintegrates into blue algae.

The Eye of Graeae recovers the ritual's power, which has gathered as a crystal fragment inside a giant egg. Aksinya appears outside the tree, says she has kept Zhara from reviving, and introduces herself as the "Royal Jester" and creator of the Eye of Graeae's vessel. She says Koshchei succeeded in creating a form of eternity but could not hold onto his personal love, and that her goal matches the Traveler's: helping the Eye of Graeae regain its power and find its body. She leaves cryptically, and the Eye recalls that it, Aksinya and Koshchei created it within Hvergelmir.

## revelations
### STANDARD
Okurov's "Doctor Moreau" is Koshchei the Deathless, a Snezhnayan bio-alchemist and former Hvergelmir member. His notes say the Perfect Tree grants great strength but regresses language, and that the "final ceremony" aims to turn the participants into embryos of a new, emotionless existence; pain, sorrow and individual love are to be cast off for eternal life, though the notes admit love might be the only thing that does not fade. The warm air, the blue algae and the Leshy are all products of this work. Zhara is Koshchei's guardian creature.
### LARGE +
The wiki's Koshchei page, which should be read as medium confidence, says he took his share of the Eye of Graeae's power to Flamefeather Valley after the Eye's betrayal of Aksinya, joined Okurov under the alias "Doctor Moreau", and was himself dissolved into blue algae in the experiment. Furiosa and Ove, who realised the truth, tried to keep villagers away from the hall. Wiki trivia notes the alias and setting resemble H. G. Wells's The Island of Doctor Moreau.

## characters
### LITE
Nastya, a child of Okurov who idolises "Doctor Birdie" and Furiosa; Koshchei (as Doctor Moreau); Zhara; the Leshy including Aunt LePuy and Ove; Furiosa; the Eye of Graeae; Aksinya; Vinezka and Jary; "The Anvil". The Traveler and Paimon escort Nastya throughout.
### STANDARD +
Nastya's mother, who has apparently already joined the tree, appears only through Nastya's words. Zhara can only be told apart from other constructs by its humanoid upper body and branch "mask" per the wiki. Nastya is a young child and should be portrayed as innocent and trusting, not as a villain.

## consequences
### STANDARD
Nastya is gone, and the ritual's power is recovered as a crystal fragment for the Eye of Graeae, which helps its quest to rebuild its body. Aksinya now knows the Traveler and shares the Eye's goal. Koshchei is dissolved, and the experiment has ended in Okurov. The wiki says that visiting Nastya's home afterwards shows a young Juvenile Tree Segment named Shar living there with blue algae by the bed.
### LARGE +
The series gives the Traveler their first long look at Hvergelmir's leftover work and at the relationship between Aksinya, Koshchei and the Eye of Graeae, which the Archon Quest develops later.
### ULTRA +
The Eye of Graeae, which already travels with the Traveler, is the main thread to keep: it wants its power and body back, and the crystal fragment from the Perfect Tree is a first step. Aksinya's statement that Koshchei failed to hold onto his personal love is the series' stated moral verdict on the experiment; Nastya's own choice is the human cost.

## placement
### STANDARD
Released 7.0 as part of the Snezhnaya launch. The wiki lists it as concurrent with Archon Quest Chapter VII Act I quests "On One Side a Palace, On the Other a Tomb" and "Hesperides of Love and Hate", with "Her Palace Collapses Into the Blizzard" next. No separate prerequisite quest is stated for the world quest series.
### ULTRA +
For roleplay, the Traveler and Paimon know everything in this series from 7.0 onward, but characters elsewhere (Fatui, Druzhna, other Archon Quest figures) generally do not, since Okurov was sealed and most of its people are gone.

## entry: Okurov

@version: 7.0

### STANDARD
Okurov is a hidden village in Flamefeather Valley, unlocked during the series. Its name references the 1908 novel The City of Okurov by Maxim Gorky, per the wiki. Its air is warm enough to help "pretty leaves" grow, its townsfolk see the assembly hall as home, and its Leshy residents include former villagers who did not complete the ritual. The way in is a collapsed rail tunnel leading from the Morepesok side, which Jary was hired to blast open.

## entry: Nastya

### STANDARD
Nastya is a young child from Okurov with branches growing from her head, found asleep in a crate. She is cheerful, proud of being "the smartest child", loyal to the doctor and to Furiosa, and eager to see the world outside the valley for the first time while longing for her mother. Her choice at the end is shown as hers; the quest treats it as tragic and does not call her foolish.

## entry: Koshchei and Doctor Moreau
@keys: Koshchei, Koshchei the Deathless, Doctor Moreau, Doctor Birdie, Perfect Tree
@version: 7.0
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
Koshchei is a Snezhnayan bio-alchemist and former Hvergelmir member who created the Aberrant Chimeric Monsters and later, as Doctor Moreau, led Okurov's villagers toward a state of eternal, unchanging life. The Eye of Graeae says it was created by him, Aksinya and a third party within Hvergelmir; by the series' end he has dissolved into the tree with the villagers. Aksinya says he achieved eternity but lost his personal love.

## entry: Zhara
@keys: Zhara, firebird of Okurov
@keys2: Okurov, Nastya, Flamefeather Valley
@version: 7.0
### STANDARD
Zhara is an Immortal Construct made by Koshchei, with a humanoid upper half, long blonde hair and branches masking her eyes. She guards Okurov from "Badbugs" (outsiders), attacks the Traveler twice and is subdued at the great tree. Aksinya stops her from reviving. The name echoes the Russian word for heat and the Firebird of folklore.
