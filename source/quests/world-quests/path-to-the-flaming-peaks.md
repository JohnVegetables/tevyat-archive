## summary
### LITE
Path to the Flaming Peaks is a very short Natlan world series containing one quest, Nahuatzin's Leap. The Traveler, Paimon and Little One reach the top of the Skyfire Circlet on the Great Volcano of Tollan and jump into the crater, which teleports them into the Ancient Sacred Mountain.
### STANDARD
Path to the Flaming Peaks (5.5) has a single quest, Nahuatzin's Leap. The party rides a Spiritway to the Skyfire Circlet, climb to the top and jump into the volcano. The golden text that has been guiding Little One grows clearer as they approach. Paimon is nervous about the height and the gaseous phlogiston, and notes that a Qucusaur grabs a statue and dives into the crater before them. The leap takes the party into the Ancient Sacred Mountain, the area explored in the following chapters.
### LARGE +
The series is a link between Lost Traveler in the Ashen Realm and Chronicler of the Crumbling City. Its wiki infobox places it after the former and before the latter. The quest can be triggered by reaching the Skyfire Circlet by any route; the intended route is a Spiritway from Tephric Ruins or Remnants of Tetenanco.
### ULTRA +
The name echoes Nanahuatzin, an Aztec god who sacrificed himself in fire; the wiki notes this as a real-world etymology, not an in-game statement. After the leap the following quest in the chain is The Frozen, Rekindling Land.

## plot
@version: 5.5
### STANDARD
Riding the Spiritway, Paimon wonders what is inside the volcano and worries it will be full of liquid phlogiston. On the Skyfire Circlet, Little One sees golden words telling it to climb to the highest pinnacle, hurl itself into the Sacred Mountain's flames, break the golden seal and reveal the radiance of remnant flames. They feel clearer to it than before. Paimon suspects whoever is speaking is inside the mountain.

At the summit, Paimon recalls being told there is a lot of gaseous phlogiston at the volcano's mouth; an attempt to jump without preparation sends them back to a waypoint. A strange statue near the rim is seized by a Qucusaur, which dives into the crater. The Traveler realises they can pass through the gas as the Qucusaur does. Paimon is frightened but agrees; Little One joins in, and the three leap together.
### LARGE +
If the Investigator of Ancient Ruins quest was done, Paimon links the statue to the one Kimosi said a Qucusaurus had stolen. The wiki notes that any character can complete the jump; the dialogue implies one has to negate the gas's HP loss, but the map access depends only on quest progress.

## revelations
### STANDARD
The only story point is the confirmation that the golden voice leads Little One towards the Sacred Mountain and that the mountain's interior is reached from the Skyfire Circlet.

## characters
### STANDARD
Little One, Paimon and the Traveler. Paimon provides the comic relief about weight and fear of heights; Little One responds with growls. Kimosi is mentioned in a conditional line. The golden-text speaker is unnamed here.

## consequences
### STANDARD
Rewards are Adventure EXP, Primogems, Mora, Hero's Wit and Mystic Enhancement Ore. The leap opens the Ancient Sacred Mountain map, which the player can enter only through this quest.

## placement
### STANDARD
Version 5.5. In story order it follows Lost Traveler in the Ashen Realm and The Way Into the Mountain (which can no longer be started once the leap is done) and precedes Chronicler of the Crumbling City and A Finale Emberforged.

## entry: Skyfire Circlet leap
@keys: Skyfire Circlet, Nahuatzin's Leap
@version: 5.5
### STANDARD
The Skyfire Circlet is the high rim around the volcano's crater. Paimon recalls being warned about heavy gaseous phlogiston at the crater mouth. A Qucusaur that steals a statue and dives in shows the way: the party must move through the gas the same way as the Qucusaur does. Golden text tells Little One to climb to the circlet's highest pinnacle and leap.
