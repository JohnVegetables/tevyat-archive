## summary
@spoiler: minor
### LITE
From Dusk to Dawn in Byakuyakoku is the World Quest series, added in version 2.4, that introduces Enkanomiya, a hidden realm beneath Inazuma whose old name is Byakuyakoku. The Traveler and Paimon come to recover the Bloodbranch Coral, pass through a gate with the Key of the Moon-Bathed Deep, and are drawn into a trial in the ruined kingdom. A helper named Enjou guides them, then proves to be an Abyss Order agent.
### STANDARD +
The series has two quests: The Entrance to Tokoyo and The Subterranean Trials of Drake and Serpent, the latter in three parts (The Trail of Drake and Serpent, The Heart of Ouroboros, Dreams in the Gaps). It is gated behind the earlier Enkanomiya quest The Still Water's Flow.
### LARGE +
It works as the introduction to Enkanomiya's geography and vocabulary: White-night and Ever-night, Sinshades, the national treasures and the central tower. It is also the first place the Abyss Order is shown operating in Inazuma's hidden realm.

## plot
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Sent to Enkanomiya to fetch the Bloodbranch Coral, the Traveler and Paimon find no sign of people but discover a great gate. A second door opens with the Key of the Moon-Bathed Deep. Inside a ruined library they meet Enjou, who calls himself clerical staff for a trial, says the realm's true name is Byakuyakoku, and says the next step is to enter the central tower, the Dainichi Mikoshi. A barrier protects it, and the kingdom's national treasure, the Golden Bridle, can break it. Enjou finds the Bridle at the back of the library.

At the tower Enjou asks to break the barrier himself. Inside, the central device is triggered and Ever-night falls. A Sinshade (a "Shade of Tokoyo", the remnant of a former inhabitant's thoughts) named Aru appears and explains the next trial: restore the Reins of Revival, a treasure able to make spirits of the Ever-night speak. The Reins are the Golden Bridle plus three fragments hidden around the tower, at The Narrows, The Serpent's Heart and the Evernight Temple. Enjou stays behind; the Traveler gathers the fragments.
### LARGE
Back at the tower the Traveler finds Enjou gone. Aru explains that the Bloodbranch Coral had been absorbed by special Bathysmal Vishaps beneath the tower and must be recovered by defeating them. Aru adds that Enjou left in displeasure and that he has a special art that could make a Sinshade vanish, so Aru doubts he comes from Watatsumi and warns the pair to be wary of him.

After defeating the Vishaps below the tower, the Traveler meets Enjou, who explains he was after historical records from before the tower was built and admits he belongs to the Abyss Order. He reveals himself as an Abyss Lector and fights the Traveler. Beaten, he leaves, with a parting remark that Paimon may one day betray them; Paimon replies that he is trying to drive a wedge between her and the Traveler. On the surface the pair give the Bloodbranch Coral to Tsuyuko and warn her that the Abyss Order has infiltrated Enkanomiya. She calls this grave and says Madam Kokomi will soon have to face it, in connection with the Watatsumi Goryou Matsuri.
### ULTRA +
Quest-by-quest: The Entrance to Tokoyo covers the gate and the key. The Trail of Drake and Serpent covers the library, Enjou and the Golden Bridle; The Heart of Ouroboros the tower, Ever-night and Aru; Dreams in the Gaps the fragments, the Vishaps and the confrontation. This is a summary of the series page; individual quest dialogue was not read.

## revelations
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
- Enkanomiya's real name is Byakuyakoku; the ruin first reached was its library (Enjou's account).
- The Dainichi Mikoshi provides the realm's imitation of sunlight, called White-night, which is separate from the Ever-night.
- Sinshades are remnants of the thoughts of Byakuyakoku's former people.
- Enjou is an Abyss Lector of the Abyss Order.
### LARGE +
- Bathysmal Vishaps beneath the tower had absorbed the Bloodbranch Coral.
- The Reins of Revival can make Ever-night spirits speak, and need the Golden Bridle and three fragments.

## characters
### STANDARD
The Traveler, Paimon, Enjou (Abyss Lector, Abyss Order), Aru (a Sinshade) and Tsuyuko, who receives the Bloodbranch Coral. Madam Kokomi is mentioned only.

## consequences
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Tsuyuko learns the Abyss Order has infiltrated Enkanomiya and expects Kokomi to have to deal with it. Enkanomiya's White-night and Ever-night cycle and its Sinshades are established for the later Enkanomiya quests.

## placement
### STANDARD
Released in 2.4. In-game it follows The Still Water's Flow, a prior Enkanomiya quest that is not written as a separate entry here.

## entry: Enjou
@keys: Enjou
@keys2: Byakuyakoku, Enkanomiya, Dainichi Mikoshi
@version: 2.4
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Enjou presents himself in Byakuyakoku as clerical staff assisting a trial, guides the Traveler to the Dainichi Mikoshi, then leaves. He is later revealed as an Abyss Lector of the Abyss Order who says he seeks records older than the tower. He is defeated in battle and departs, warning that Paimon might betray the Traveler.

## entry: Reins of Revival
@keys: Reins of Revival, Golden Bridle
@keys2: Byakuyakoku, Dainichi Mikoshi, Aru
@version: 2.4
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
A national treasure of Byakuyakoku made of the Golden Bridle and three fragments hidden at The Narrows, The Serpent's Heart and the Evernight Temple. Restored, it can make the spirits of the Ever-night speak. Aru, a Sinshade at the Dainichi Mikoshi, assigns the Traveler to reunite it.
