## summary
@spoiler: minor
### LITE
Upstream Variations is a World Quest series added in version 7.1. It is played over seven real-time days, one short scene per day, around Vodyanitsa, the principal soprano of the Korolevskiy Troupe. Over meals and visits she tells the Traveler and Paimon about a childhood friend who turned her tickets into social currency, and about the hollow feeling of singing to people who do not listen. The Traveler gives her a necklace-sized recording device, and she ends the week cheerful.
### STANDARD +
The quest is unlocked by completing "Wraith's Nocturne" and raising the Tidesong Cavern Meeting Point to Construction Level 2. The wiki notes that completing "White Night, Like a Dream Upon Waking" first gives the best experience. Completing it gives a special reward, the Happy-Quacky Phonograph Ducky, which gives Vodyanitsa a new decorative appearance (game-mechanics information). It also gives Primogems, Mora and talent materials.
### LARGE +
Its tone moves from teasing comedy to a quietly sad centre on Day 2 and Day 5, and back to comedy at the end. It shows a more fragile Vodyanitsa than the cheerful one met in earlier content, and gives Odette, Alyosha, Ineffa, Aino and Escoffier supporting roles.
### ULTRA +
On the wiki, the event menu was noted to play one of several short vocal sound effects. This is a minor user-interface detail.

## plot
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Day 1. At Podvorye Restaurant in Snezhnograd the Traveler and Paimon find Vodyanitsa at a table with only tea, sighing about a meeting with a "friend". She says food tastes like wax when she has to talk to that person, and meeting at a restaurant lets her pretend to eat instead. She orders a huge meal, then tells a story: a banquet in her honour, thrown by Lishnaya, head of a Snezhnayan guild, who praises the performance with exaggerated flattery. Vodyanitsa explains that Lishnaya was once a small girl who loved opera and worked part-time at the theater. Vodyanitsa gave her handwritten tickets, and the girl used to watch from behind the curtain. She stops the story there, gives the Traveler a blank notebook made at Maryyushka's Antykvariat with tickets inside, and says to bring it to the theater.

Day 2. The ticket is handwritten, so the theater policy sends the Traveler and Paimon backstage to watch from the wings. There Vodyanitsa continues. Lishnaya stopped using the backstage ticket, missed a performance with an excuse, and Vodyanitsa learned that a wealthy merchant sat in the seat she had saved. She says Lishnaya used the tickets to make powerful friends and is now part of the Zapolyarny elite. Vodyanitsa still thinks of her as a friend, because she does not like giving up on things that belong to her. She speaks of flower baskets from people who never come, and of the Water Imps' voices that she hears in the empty theater. Her tone turns bitter, then she suddenly asks if she scared them, claims a Water Imp named Xenia was speaking through her, and plays a prank. She offers a meal on her tab at Podvorye and says to meet at Sadov-Arbat. Alone, she repeats a lament as if answering voices, and names two Water Imps, Xenia and Paraskeva.
### LARGE +
Day 3. At Sadov-Arbat, Vodyanitsa has finished shopping. Two fans, Malinina and Rozhdestvensky, hover outside; she overhears them from a distance, invites them in and gives them signed tickets. She tells the Traveler she keeps handwritten tickets in her pocket and always gives them to people who like her singing but cannot get a seat. In another flashback, she tells Lishnaya the ticket will always be valid. She says others tried to bribe her, resold tickets on the black market, or used them to pretend to have connections. She asks if the Traveler will trade their ticket; the Traveler and Paimon say no, and propose a meal as thanks.

Day 4. At Podvorye the Traveler meets Escoffier and her sous-chef Savanna, who are asking the owner, Nefedov, to let them cook in return for taking ingredients. Escoffier says she guessed the Traveler's next stop would be Snezhnaya after reading about events in Nod-Krai in The Steambird. Vodyanitsa recalls tasting Hotel Debord's new cake on a visit to Fontaine and calls it dry and gritty, which upsets Savanna. Savanna explains that customers buy cakes to show off, not to eat them, and that the kitchen staff are losing heart. Vodyanitsa says it is like singing an aria to an audience that is not listening. Escoffier tells Savanna she must balance being a head chef with being a baker. Vodyanitsa suggests they bake a cake for Odette, the prima ballerina, and a montage shows Fontainian dishes made from Snezhnayan ingredients.

Day 5. The Traveler finds Vodyanitsa and Odette on the theater stage. Odette notes Vodyanitsa has been off for five days and asks if the voices are back; Vodyanitsa calls them not real. Vodyanitsa plans a special performance for those who hold handwritten tickets, and to declare that some tickets are invalid. While she is backstage, Odette tells the Traveler and Paimon why: Vodyanitsa knows many songs written by Water Imps and keeps the songs alive by performing them. When other singers were taught them, the shows sold poorly, so Vodyanitsa used her fame to bring people in. Odette adds that Vodyanitsa fears being forgotten. She then says she overheard it all while Vodyanitsa talked in her sleep.

Day 6. At Sadov-Arbat, Ineffa delivers the Happy-Quacky Phonograph Ducky, a small wearable phonograph that Aino built after the Traveler wrote to her. It has limited recording space and the memory must be replaced, which Aino wants paid in ice cream, which Ineffa rations. Ineffa has also come to look for Jahoda, and the exchange changes slightly depending on whether the Traveler has seen the Jahoda anecdote. An interlude follows from Vodyanitsa's point of view: she walks through Snezhnograd, hears fans' ambition and thinks of being forgotten, and later sits alone in her room. The Traveler and Paimon arrive at her window with food and the gift. The Traveler promises to listen and to remember any song, and mentions a director friend and actors who would give her songs attention. Vodyanitsa accepts, then asks for the Traveler's most sincere singing and drags everyone out for a Whopperflower hunt.
### ULTRA +
Day 7. The night's Whopperflower hunt ended with only a photograph of a Whopperflower, and Vodyanitsa gave the party medicine against colds. At the theater she then gives a performance for a small audience, mostly handwritten-ticket holders, with a few tickets sold to the public. Afterward she is cheerful, says the ducky is full of songs, including recordings of Paimon's and the Traveler's songs from the night before, and threatens to release them. She says that because the Traveler promised to listen, they will be a regular, and the handwritten ticket never expires. Characters in the audience react: Vesna and Danica discuss the fae, Odette says a Water Imp's song is the most sincere sound, Mitya and Valeriy comment, Alyosha with Tugarin listens, Escoffier and Savanna react, Aino and Ineffa discuss the rule against recording devices in the theater and Vodyanitsa's ticket for them, Arlecchino praises the performance and says Tartaglia missed it on another mission, and Sandrone, as Marionette, jokes that Columbina has heard the songs. Leaving the theater after talking to everyone ends the quest.

## revelations
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
- Vodyanitsa, a Water Imp, hears the voices of the dead Water Imps in the empty theater and keeps their songs alive through performance.
- She is hurt that many fans treat her handwritten tickets as status symbols, not invitations to listen.
- Her friendship with Lishnaya was real, and the break came when Lishnaya used a ticket for business.
- The Traveler promises to listen to and remember Vodyanitsa's songs, which ends the quest on a hopeful note.
### LARGE +
- Odette watches over Vodyanitsa; she says she learned the reason for Vodyanitsa's sadness only by overhearing her talk in her sleep.
- The Happy-Quacky Phonograph Ducky is named by Aino, who likes ducks, and looks like Vodyanitsa's own necklace.
- Arlecchino and Sandrone are present as audience members in the final scene and treat the performance respectfully.

## characters
### STANDARD
- Vodyanitsa, the central character, presented as sociable and teasing in public and lonely in private.
- The Traveler and Paimon.
- Odette, the prima ballerina, who explains Vodyanitsa's motives.
- Lishnaya, a guild head, in flashbacks.
### LARGE +
- Escoffier and Savanna, Fontaine chefs at Podvorye (Day 4).
- Ineffa and Aino, who deliver and designed the ducky (Day 6), with Jahoda mentioned.
- Staff and audience members, including Vzdykhalov, Buturlina, Nefedov, Yevlena, Malinina, Rozhdestvensky, Avreliya, Scheffield, Burnaya, Agarova, Arkady and Taisia, plus final-scene appearances by Vesna, Danica, Mitya, Valeriy, Alyosha with Tugarin, Arlecchino and Sandrone.

## consequences
### STANDARD
Vodyanitsa gains a recording device and a promise from the Traveler to hear her songs. She plans to keep performing the Water Imps' music. The Traveler's handwritten ticket remains valid and now gives access to regular shows. Ineffa and Aino also receive tickets.
### LARGE +
Vodyanitsa's decorative appearance changes when the ducky is used (game-mechanics information). The quest provides a notebook item, "Vodyanitsa's Special Notebook".

## placement
### STANDARD
Story order: after "Wraith's Nocturne" (a prerequisite). It is recommended after "White Night, Like a Dream Upon Waking". Release order: version 7.1. It shares the Korolevskiy Theater setting with "Variations on Belyi and Chernyi", where Odette plans a schedule; Vodyanitsa and Odette appear in both.

## entry: Handwritten tickets
@keys: handwritten ticket, handwritten tickets
@keys2: Vodyanitsa, Korolevskiy Theater
@version: 7.1
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Vodyanitsa gives out tickets she writes by hand to people who love her singing but cannot get a seat. They are valid for nearly any show: the holder sits in an empty seat if there is one, otherwise backstage. She meant them as invitations to listen, but some people tried to bribe her, resold them for large sums, or used them to pose as connected. She plans to declare some tickets invalid.

## entry: Water Imps' songs
@keys: Water Imp songs, Xenia, Paraskeva
@keys2: Vodyanitsa
@version: 7.1
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Vodyanitsa has memorized every song a Water Imp performed at the Korolevskiy Theater and sings them at special shows. She says she hears Xenia and Paraskeva, two departed Water Imps, in the empty hall. Odette says that to Vodyanitsa, those voices never really disappeared. Whether they are real is left open.

## entry: Lishnaya
@keys: Lishnaya
@keys2: Vodyanitsa, handwritten ticket
@version: 7.1
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Lishnaya is the head of a Snezhnayan guild and a member of the Zapolyarny elite. As a girl she loved opera, worked part-time at the theater and was Vodyanitsa's friend. Vodyanitsa says she later used her tickets for networking and to impress a wealthy merchant. Vodyanitsa has not given up on her.

## entry: Happy-Quacky Phonograph Ducky

A small wearable phonograph built by Aino at the Traveler's request, brought by Ineffa. It looks like Vodyanitsa's necklace and has limited recording space. Aino named it after her love of ducks and wanted ice cream for each replacement memory.
