## summary
### LITE
A reputation-style series of quests for Columbina, the Damselette, at Silvermoon Hall in Nod-Krai, added in Version 6.0 (Luna I). It is unlocked by the quest The Dark Side of Memory and is read through the Record-Keeping Kuuhenki. It consists of eighteen short quests in three parts, with small personal moments rather than world-changing plot.
### STANDARD +
Each completed quest gives the party the New Moon's Embrace effect for a time, and finishing groups of quests unlocks rewards. The series is a quiet companion to Columbina's role in the main Nod-Krai story: it shows her with her kuuhenki, a young painter and her memories of home.
### LARGE +
The wiki's in-game quest blurbs describe moments such as a young painter introducing its goddess, a mechanic repairing her instrument, a board game that mirrors the tides of history, and her bringing Luonnotar back into the world, so the series reads as a gentle character portrait rather than an adventure.

## plot
### LITE
The Traveler visits Silvermoon Hall now and then to meet Columbina. Each visit is a short quest; the next becomes available after seven daily resets.
### STANDARD +
Part I (six quests): The Dark Side of Memory, Her Past, The Artist By the Moon's Side (I), Moonlight Sonata, Homecoming's Faint Glow and May the Moonlight Connect Us. The official blurbs say she takes a beam of moonlight and gives the Traveler the "Moon Goddess's Favor"; that she claims to have "run away from home" yet seems to have fond memories of it; that a young painter tries to introduce its goddess; and that a genius mechanic repairs her broken instrument.
### LARGE +
Part II (six quests): The Artist By the Moon's Side (II) and (III), The Moon Adorning the Night, Shifting Moonlight, Moonlight Sonata: Lingering Resonance and A Toast Beneath the Moon. The blurbs show the painter seeing the Moon Goddess at her most joyful, a board that mirrors the tides of history, kuuhenki to play with, echoes of past journeys, doubt over whether an art exhibition on the moon will take place, and the puzzle of what drink "that isn't too heavy" she would want.

Part III (six quests): Come Play With the Moon, The Homebound Moon, The Artist By the Moon's Side (IV), The Moon Adorning the Night: The Three Moons, Where Does the Moon Hide? and A Gift From Her. The blurbs say she brings Luonnotar back into this world, that "I am home," that the painter's strokes bring out a happiness that feels unfamiliar to her, and that the board becomes an extension of the future itself.
### ULTRA +
This file records only those official blurbs, not the quests' full scripts. Ambient idle scenes in Silvermoon Hall, per the wiki, include Columbina telling the kuuhenki fables (a bird and a whale, a lake that reflects a false moon, a seesaw as a lesson in giving something back) and wondering what Teyvat would look like from the moon above.

## revelations
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The series is largely character material. Its blurbs imply that Columbina has a home she once left and now speaks of as home, and that Luonnotar can be brought back into the world by her, but the details belong to the Nod-Krai Archon Quest and are not reconstructed here.
### LARGE +
The blurbs use spoiler-heavy wording ("I am home", the Three Moons) tied to the Archon Quest, so they should not be given to a character who has not seen that story.

## characters
### LITE
Columbina, called the Damselette, her kuuhenki, the Traveler and Paimon. Part I also involves a young painter and a mechanic, and Part III involves Luonnotar, as named in the quest blurbs.
### STANDARD +
Idle dialogue shows Columbina as soft-spoken and unhurried, turning small observations into tales for the kuuhenki. She likes high places and sometimes appears behind the Traveler after the camera pans away. Her own entry carries her full portrayal.

## consequences
### STANDARD
Completing a quest gives the New Moon's Embrace effect until the seventh daily reset: while in Nod-Krai, the party's Moonsign Level is Ascendant Gleam, and it cannot be switched off. Completing groups of quests unlocks rewards (Part I: From Here I Gaze, Blueprint: Cloud-Wrapped Moon and Blueprint: Blanket of Stars; Part II: Nod-Krai: Moonlight; Part III: Wings of the Silent Moon). These are gameplay items; they do not change the story.

## placement
### STANDARD
Introduced in Version 6.0 (Luna I) with Nod-Krai. After each quest the player waits seven daily resets for the next. The Selenic Chronicles quests become unavailable when A Nocturne of the Far North starts (it begins automatically after Act IV, An Elegy for Faded Moonlight), and become available again after True Moon is completed.
