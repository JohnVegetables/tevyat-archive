## summary
@spoiler: minor
### LITE
Variations on Belyi and Chernyi is a Snezhnaya World Quest series added in version 7.0 and set at the Korolevskiy Theater. The Traveler and Paimon become Odette's temporary managers for two weeks. Odette is the troupe's prima ballerina, and she is about to premiere the ballet "Black Snow Swan" while also taking on a Fatui monster-clearing mission. The player fills in a schedule of hunts, rehearsals, fan meetings and rest, and the result decides one of five endings.
### STANDARD +
The quest is unlocked by raising the Korolevskiy Theater Meeting Point to Construction Level 2. It consists of a short setup quest and a longer "Schedule" quest. The planner affects Odette's inner sense of self ("Self-Identity"), how others see her ("External Recognition") and her energy (game-mechanics information); the wiki says that tasks matter more than points for the ending. The series grants Primogems, Mora and talent materials, and the Schedule quest grants a "Black Snow Swan Archive" item.
### LARGE +
Its real subject is Odette's divided life: a ballerina in the spotlight and a member of the Fatui who was recruited as a child. Her desire for both external praise and self-approval is the answer she reaches in the best ending.
### ULTRA +
In several scenes, the "Black Snow Swan" ballet mirrors Odette. It tells of a swan whose feathers are a different colour from the flock, who finally stops trying to fit in and flies higher than any white swan.

## plot
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Setup. At the theater the Traveler and Paimon see a poster for "Black Snow Swan" and overhear Vodyanitsa trying to talk Odette out of taking a Fatui monster-hunting mission only two weeks before the premiere. Odette insists she can do both, and has applied knowing the schedule. Vodyanitsa makes her agree to let someone else plan her time. Odette chooses the Traveler, citing trust built when they were trapped in the theater and familiarity with the Fatui, while Vodyanitsa objects that she has known Odette much longer and says she would schedule too much rest. The Traveler is given the planner. The premiere is in two weeks and the Fatui mission assessment is in one.

Schedule. Each day the player chooses a type of activity: monster hunt, dance rehearsal, fan meeting, rest or relaxation. Special scenes appear depending on the choice (see below). Week one ends with the Fatui mission examination and week two with the performance.
### LARGE +
Monster hunt. Odette is clearing monsters from an area she used to practise dancing in as a child. She says she loves dancing in nature and that she wanted the area to stay peaceful, but also that she hoped it would help her interpret the Black Snow Swan, a role she practised as a child and never performed. As a child it was her least favourite role, she says, because she did not want to be like the swan, yet her real life came to resemble it. She tells the story's ending: the swan stops trying to fit in, lives proudly alone and flies into the clouds. In another scene she says that she hated joining the Fatui as a child, because it took time from dance, and that her father's death and her mother's pain destroyed her family's happiness. She blamed Rosalyne, the Fatui and Project Stuzha, but now thinks what she hated was not being in control of her life. She also says her success as a dancer feels like a different kind of control, because the expectation of dazzling perfection does not feel like hers. She gives the Traveler a white-feather bookmark.

Dance rehearsal. Odette is professional and steady with other dancers. In one scene Vodyanitsa asks to learn pirouettes and finds them dizzying; Odette explains how to focus on a spot and says it will take about a month on land.

Fan meetings. A Theater Open Day is hosted by Shuvalov. Odette answers questions curtly: the ballet is difficult but worth it, it has special meaning for her, and she hopes the audience can find the strength to defy fate through art. She is asked about the most consecutive fouettes. She sometimes signs "I appreciate the support" repeatedly when tired. Mitya and Valeriy attend as fans. She says that she gets strength from outside validation but cannot rely on it, or she would be like a figure in a music box. She gives the Traveler a childhood photo, kept for herself, as an Autographed Black Snow Swan Poster item.

Rest. On a rest day the Traveler and Paimon find Odette buying mystery boxes of music boxes, hoping to get the secret edition. She explains it looks like her mother's antique music box, which broke when it fell as her mother helped her up after a dance practice fall. She keeps buying duplicates, and the Traveler offers to buy some too.

Examination. Sandrone, called Marionette, is the Harbinger assessing the mission. If Odette has done enough hunts, she passes at once, and Sandrone, who has seen the Black Snow Swan poster, tells her you cannot change who you are inside and to listen to her heart, and invites her to tea with Arlecchino. If not, she is mildly warned to think about her limits before accepting missions. Either way she says Arlecchino and she will attend the premiere.
### ULTRA +
Endings.
- A Solo Atop a Still Lake: Odette's Fatui work succeeded while rehearsal suffered. She decides to interpret the swan in her own way, whatever the audience's reaction. This ending is suggested by an imbalance in favour of Self-Identity.
- Lost Amidst the Applause: the performance is technically flawless, but Odette says she could not find herself in it. The hunt exam went poorly. This is suggested by an imbalance in favour of External Recognition.
- Still Dark the Snowy Night: Odette fails both the choreography and the missions and goes off alone. She says she would still try to do both and not give up either. She wishes to show a different black snow swan next time.
- The Wasting Daylight: little progress was made at all. Vodyanitsa scolds Odette; Odette is exhausted, admits she overestimated herself and withdraws.
- Dawn: both sides are balanced and the exam is passed. Odette concludes that the swan found her true self, and that she herself craves both external validation and self-approval. She recites the swan's final scene, and a verse about hope in the days of grief plays over a montage, saying a childhood dream has come true.

## revelations
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
- Odette joined the Fatui as a child, hated it at first, and now finds the work calming. Odette also refers to working with Vodyanitsa through the Rokot.
- The Black Snow Swan is closely tied to Odette's childhood and her sense of difference.
- Sandrone, as Marionette, shows an unexpectedly gentle side and offers Odette advice and tea.
### LARGE +
- Odette's father has died and her mother is described as in pain. Odette also says her mother used to tell her ballet is about both skill and feeling.
- Odette blames Rosalyne, the Fatui and Project Stuzha for her childhood but says what she resented was lack of control over her life.

## characters
### STANDARD
- Odette, the prima ballerina, central to every scene.
- The Traveler and Paimon, who are her managers.
- Vodyanitsa, who forces the arrangement and joins some scenes.
### LARGE +
- Sandrone, appearing as Marionette, for the exam; Arlecchino, mentioned and present at the premiere.
- Alyosha with Tugarin, Mitya and Valeriy, Vesna, Taisia, Shuvalov and Buturlina in smaller scenes.

## consequences
### STANDARD
Whichever ending occurs, Odette comes away with a clearer view of herself, though the way she gets there differs. The Traveler receives the Black Snow Swan Archive. The quest does not change Odette's standing in the Fatui or the troupe in any lasting way beyond its endings.

## placement
### STANDARD
Story order: after the time the Traveler was trapped in the theater, which Odette mentions as the reason she trusts the Traveler. Release order: version 7.0. "Upstream Variations" (7.1) returns to the same theater and cast.

## entry: Black Snow Swan
@keys: Black Snow Swan, black snow swan
@keys2: Odette, Korolevskiy Theater, ballet
@version: 7.0
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
A ballet adapted from a literary classic and staged by the Korolevskiy Troupe, with Odette in the lead. It explores the individual versus the collective and fate. The swan is born with feathers of a different colour, refuses to bow to fate, stops trying to fit in with the flock and flies alone into the clouds.
