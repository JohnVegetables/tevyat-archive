## summary
@spoiler: none
### LITE
Scenes from Life in Meropide is a series of twelve small world quests in the Fortress of Meropide, added in version 4.1. They are not chained together; each starts and ends on its own. The Traveler and Paimon do errands and favours for prisoners and guards, paid in Coupons, the fortress's currency, and glimpse the fortress's everyday routines, queues, jokes and regrets.
### STANDARD +
The quests unlock in batches. Seven become available during the quest "Game of the Rich", and access is also tied to finishing "Hereafter...". Three more open on the first daily reset after finishing "An Eye for an Eye" and the first seven, and the last two open on the next daily reset after those.
### LARGE +
Mora is useless in the fortress, so Paimon is always chasing Coupons for the Rag and Bone Shop and the Coupon Cafeteria. The series is mostly comic, but several quests touch on regret, loneliness and the Fontaine Research Institute.

## plot
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Each quest is a self-contained errand. In A Raw Deal the Traveler queues for the prisoner Decembre, who wants to post a birthday letter to his son, but he hands over the wrong letter. In Chit-Chat the Traveler negotiates for a study room on behalf of a scholar named Nakul. Dead End, Safe Operation, Treat the Symptoms and Unfinished Task are favours for Clynes, Caterpillar, the doctor Arderne and the photographer Nelly. Fists of Fury is a sparring bout, and Every Debt has a Creditor, Visible Hands and The Art of Negotiation involve guards and fortress bureaucracy. An Actor's Training and Memories close the series with a theatre rehearsal and a conversation with a former Institute researcher.
### LARGE +
**A Raw Deal.** Decembre asks the Traveler to stand in the mail room queue because he must rush to the production zone after a safety incident. The queue is the slowest in the fortress, because the clerk, Lutz, embellishes letters as he types them. Other prisoners tell how "Unlucky Decembre" has failed three times. The letter turns out to be the wrong one: a gushing note signed "White Lily" to Monsieur Neuvillette, begging to see him again at her next trial. Decembre says a sick young woman gave it to him and that he paid the Traveler from his own pocket; the Coupons are less than a meal's worth.

**Chit-Chat.** Nakul wants a place to study with shelves, a table and a board, and the room he would like is unavailable. A similar room is occupied by someone who never opens the door. Nakul's earlier bodyguard Nizami hammered on it and failed. The Traveler negotiates through the door, hearing talk of "darkness" and a rival bidder, Carranza. The occupant never opens the door; depending on the offers made, he either agrees to deal only with Nakul in person or refuses, and Nakul pays the Traveler regardless.

**Dead End.** Galvaryet sends the Traveler to help Clynes with a Research Terminal at the Rift of Erosion that needs two people at once. It seals a rift connecting to the outside, which previously needed guards. Clynes lets slip that an advanced version is deployed at the Geode Mine Shaft.

**Fists of Fury.** Nizami, bored and holding Coupons he won from Roussimoff, asks for a spar and takes the Traveler to the Pankration Ring.

**Safe Operation.** Caterpillar and the Traveler open a passage to Supply Warehouse 1 to free Mitul, who was trapped by his associate Koblenz tinkering with a drive valve. Mitul, who sells health drinks, complains about competition from Fonta, pays in Coupons and offers a drink the Traveler declines on Caterpillar's advice.

**Treat the Symptoms.** The former doctor Lechlade believes the "yellow-green stones" from the Institute's Central Laboratory cause his headaches. The certified doctor Arderne says he is not seriously ill, having misdiagnosed a patient on the outside and become paranoid. Arderne sends Pure Water as a "health potion". It works.

**Unfinished Task.** Nelly, an amateur journalist and novice angler with a Kamera, wants a photo of the guard Grainville at work. The Traveler turns off a loudspeaker and reports a fault so Grainville walks past her. She is delighted with the result.
### ULTRA +
**Every Debt has a Creditor.** Galvaryet and the guard Maupoil cannot scrub a gear-shaped mark from a wall. The Traveler finds it is a Cipher Letter, a technology developed by Mr. Butor, a benefactor of Clynes, that Clynes uses hoping an old acquaintance might decode it. Clynes admits he drew it. The Traveler can say so or defer to him as a technical expert.

**Visible Hands.** Decembre returns offering a hundred Coupons for jobs. Maupoil says he swindles newcomers into commissions for a fraction of the real fee, takes him to solitary and asks the Traveler to drive Fontemer Aberrants from the Geode Mine Shaft, arriving afterwards to find the work already done.

**The Art of Negotiation.** After buying a book of that name, a note in it directs the Traveler to take a satisfaction survey at the Coupon Cafeteria's front desk. Paimon follows the book's advice with the clerk Monglane, who rushes the survey and fills it in as "satisfied". Completing it earns the achievement "Non-Zero-Sum Game". The wiki notes the title references a real-world book.

**An Actor's Training.** The two-person Tri-Coupon Troupe, Meilhat and Fourmon, rehearse "The Countess of Monte-Mer", a revenge drama about Juliette. Fourmon says he was sentenced at the Opera Epiclese. They ask the Traveler which ending is better: Juliette giving up revenge, or completing it and finding emptiness.

**Memories.** A loudspeaker plays odd broadcasts that lead the Traveler to Clynes. He says the fortress is simpler than the outside, that nothing outside is worth worrying about, and that he is content, though his friends are gone. He names Butor, who died of illness, Teilhard, who died when the Central Laboratory exploded, and a fired colleague he will not discuss. Completing it earns "The Superfluous Man's Account".

## revelations
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
- The fortress runs on Coupons, not Mora. Prisoners queue for mail, which is read and checked, and take small jobs and wagers.
- Clynes is a former Fontaine Research Institute researcher, and the quests refer to the Institute's accidents and its trouble with funding.
- Clynes says he can leave whenever he wants and chooses to stay.
### LARGE +
- The Rift of Erosion once connected the fortress to the outside; a sealing device is used for it and a more advanced one at the Geode Mine Shaft.
- A Cipher Letter is a Butor-developed coding technique used at the Institute and known to few.
- Fonta and Pure Water compete as drinks inside the fortress.

## characters
### STANDARD
The Traveler and Paimon, with prisoners and guards including Decembre, Nakul, Nizami, Clynes, Caterpillar, Mitul, Koblenz, the doctors Arderne and Lechlade, the amateur journalist Nelly and the guard Grainville, the guards Galvaryet and Maupoil, the clerk Monglane, and the Tri-Coupon Troupe's Meilhat and Fourmon.
### LARGE +
Galvaryet is an official of the fortress who mentions her sister Galvana. Clynes is a technician and former Institute researcher who is deeply nostalgic. Decembre is a recurring swindler whose bad luck becomes a running joke.

## consequences
@spoiler: none
### STANDARD
The quests have only small local effects: a stuck mark removed, a rift sealed, a photograph taken. They add a record of life in the fortress rather than change the main story.

## placement
### STANDARD
Released in version 4.1. Availability comes in three batches tied to the archon quests "Game of the Rich", "Hereafter..." and "An Eye for an Eye", with the last quests opening after daily resets.

## entry: Coupons
@keys: Coupons, Coupon, Rag and Bone Shop
@keys2: Meropide, Fortress of Meropide
@version: 4.1
@spoiler: none
### STANDARD
Coupons are the currency used inside the Fortress of Meropide. Nakul says Mora is useless there. Prisoners buy food at the Coupon Cafeteria and goods at the Rag and Bone Shop with Coupons, and employ one another for errands.

## entry: Clynes
@keys: Clynes
@keys2: Meropide, Fontaine Research Institute, Butor, Teilhard
@version: 4.1
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Clynes is a technician in the Fortress of Meropide who worked at the Fontaine Research Institute. He repairs devices, is the author of the Cipher Letter mark on the wall, and says he stays by choice. He mourns Butor, who died of illness, and Teilhard, who died in the Central Laboratory explosion, and says a fourth colleague was fired.

## entry: Decembre
@keys: Decembre
@keys2: Meropide, mail room, Lutz, letter
@version: 4.1
@spoiler: none
### STANDARD
Decembre is a prisoner nicknamed "Unlucky Decembre" for repeated failures at the mail room queue. He hands the Traveler the wrong letter, and later offers jobs to newcomers at a fraction of the real pay, for which Maupoil takes him to solitary.

### STANDARD
The Tri-Coupon Troupe is a two-person theatre group of prisoners, Meilhat and Fourmon, rehearsing "The Countess of Monte-Mer" and promoting it by loudspeaker. Fourmon says he was sentenced at the Opera Epiclese.
