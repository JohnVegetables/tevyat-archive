## summary
### LITE
The Gourmet Supremos is a series of world quests and commissions that runs through Inazuma (2.0) and Sumeru (3.0, 3.1). It follows a mismatched party of food lovers: Xudong, a chef from Liyue, Parvaneh, a scholar of the Sumeru Akademiya, and Julie, an adventurer from Mondstadt, joined in Inazuma by the ex-samurai Kamei Munehisa. The Traveler helps them gather local ingredients, and in Sumeru helps them out-manoeuvre a pushy Fontainian merchant named Nète, who ends by sponsoring their journey to Fontaine.
### STANDARD
In Inazuma, the Supremos are stuck at a barrier around a grove of sakura, so the Traveler helps them reach the Sakura Blooms they want to cook with, and keeps helping them over the next quests (shrines and sakura, the seashore, deep divers, a road trip). They are rescued from Kairagi and Nobushi at one point, and finish with a feast that formally brings in Kamei Munehisa as bodyguard. Commissions then cover a cook-off between Xudong and Munehisa and a food quiz.

In Sumeru, Parvaneh brings a paper on an all-purpose "Omni-Spice" to the Akademiya. Her tutor Farzah and Nète, a merchant, clash over how to bring it to market. The Supremos give the spice away to undercut Nète. He concedes and offers to invest in them. A last quest, Cleanup, delivers a letter in which Xudong says they have accepted Nète's offer and left for Fontaine.
### LARGE +
Xudong, who runs a cooking rivalry with Xiangling's father Chef Mao and is searching for a "breakthrough", appears as the group's would-be leader. Parvaneh's ultimate seasoning, which she hopes will make any dish delicious, worries Julie and Xudong, who try to stop it from being used. Julie, a Mondstadt adventurer, will eat almost anything provided it tastes good; Munehisa, who says dishes should be of perfect quality, teaches Xudong small dishes he used to make with wine.
### ULTRA +
Later mentions are by event characters only: the Battle of Imagined Arrays co-op mini-game includes an encounter that alludes to the Supremos and Nète, and Nusta in the November 2025 event Dance Dance Dynamic Disco speaks of a four-person team of cooks travelling between Sumeru and Fontaine, though her description leaves out Nète.

## plot
@version: 2.0
### STANDARD
Inazuma (2.0). The series starts with Gourmet Supremos, Assemble!, after the Traveler finishes Ritou Escape Plan. At a sandbar between Ritou and Narukami Island they meet Xudong, Parvaneh and Julie, who are hungry and tired of grilled fish. Parvaneh has found a way through the barrier in an Akademiya travelogue (touch a Thunder Sakura Bough first), and they want Sakura Blooms for cooking. The Traveler helps and brings them a Special Sakura Bloom. The series then proceeds through four more world quests, with commissions interleaved (Breakthrough Thinking, then The Importance of Eating Well).

The finale in Inazuma. The Importance of Eating Well follows the commission in which Xudong asks Xiangling for advice. In it the Supremos are attacked by former comrades of Kamei Munehisa, who are trying to pull him back into their ranks. Munehisa is formally invited to join the Supremos as a bodyguard, and four commissions open. They cover a lost-ingredient search, a Foodie Quiz with Parvaneh, a cook-off between Xudong and Munehisa, and extreme cookery.
### LARGE +
Sumeru (3.0 and 3.1). The Supremos arrive at Port Ormos so Parvaneh can hand in her paper. At the Akademiya her tutor Farzah is arguing with Nète, who wants to help the research reach the market on his terms. Parvaneh declines, and Nète says they will find another way. The Supremos give Parvaneh's seasoning away free to undercut Nète, and the Traveler gathers ingredients such as fish at Vimara Village, potatoes at Port Ormos and Padisarah at Gilded Journey. Merchants in the market tell the party a Fontainian had offered them an expensive spice, which they cannot afford.

Nète then concedes. He says the free spice has captured the market and asks the Traveler to go easy on a poor businessman. He says Farzah, who refused to promote her own formula, was the wrong business partner, while the Traveler seems a better fit. He offers to invest in them and in exchange wants his "trademark" on the formula. The last quest, Cleanup, has Maryam give the Traveler Xudong's letter.
### ULTRA +
Xudong's letter says he and the others have decided on a new route. He had wanted to return to Liyue and then visit Mondstadt, where Julie has not been home for a long time and where he wants to try the wine. Nète first asked Xudong to advertise for him, then offered to sponsor the whole trip if they go to Fontaine next, with no interference in their business. The contract is thick, and Parvaneh found nothing wrong with it except the requirement for an interview.

## revelations
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
- The Supremos are three friends from three nations (Liyue, Sumeru, Mondstadt) plus an Inazuman, with a shared goal of improving their cooking. Xudong is probably the one who named the group.
- Nète's investment offer comes right after the party beats him in the market. Xudong's later letter says Parvaneh thought they should stay away from him, but that the contract she read through had nothing wrong in it apart from a required interview.
### LARGE +
- The Traveler has not met the Supremos again; their later route is known only through the letter and event mentions.

## characters
### STANDARD
Xudong, Parvaneh, Julie and Kamei Munehisa (the Supremos); Nète (the Fontainian merchant); Farzah (Parvaneh's tutor); Maryam (the Gilded Journey merchant who delivers the letter); Xiangling and Chef Mao as cooks consulted in the commissions; Paimon and the Traveler.

## consequences
### STANDARD
The Traveler gains cooking recipes, Mora and achievements, plus Sashimi Platter and Sakura Tempura recipes in Inazuma and Tandoori Roast Chicken, Potato Boat and Fish With Cream Sauce in Sumeru. According to Xudong's letter, the Supremos leave Sumeru for Fontaine under Nète's sponsorship.
### LARGE +
The series has no effect on any Archon Quest.

## placement
### STANDARD
Released in 2.0 with the Inazuma content, with the Sumeru section released in 3.0 (Within Our Duties, which requires The Morn a Thousand Roses Brings and achievements from the commissions) and 3.1 (Cleanup). Within Our Duties also requires finishing the commission Where'd the Ingredients Go? after Version 3.1 was released.

## entry: Gourmet Supremos
@keys: Gourmet Supremos, Gourmet Supremo
@keys2: Xudong, Parvaneh, Julie, Munehisa
@version: 2.0
### STANDARD
The Gourmet Supremos are a travelling party of food lovers: Xudong (Liyue chef and self-styled leader), Parvaneh (Sumeru scholar working on an "ultimate seasoning"), Julie (Mondstadt adventurer) and, after Inazuma, Kamei Munehisa (former Shogunate samurai, bodyguard). They travel to improve their cooking and use local specialties.

## entry: Nète
@keys: Nète, Nete
@keys2: Fontaine, Supremos, Parvaneh, spice
@version: 3.0
### STANDARD
Nète is a forceful Fontainian merchant who tries to monopolise Parvaneh's spice and then to sponsor the Supremos. Characters call him underhanded, but he concedes gracefully after losing in the market and says he is only a businessman.
