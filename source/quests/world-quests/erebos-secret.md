## summary
### LITE
A version 2.4 world quest series in Enkanomiya. After the Traveler returns the Bloodbranch Coral to Tsuyuko, a shade named Eboshi, an auxiliary of Sangonomiya and one of the shrine maidens of Enkanomiya, offers a set of trials that lead to more of the island's secrets.
### STANDARD +
The series is a single quest, The Three Great Martial Trials, with three sub-quests: Yachimatahiko's Trial, Yachimatahime's Trial and Kunado's Trial. Each is held at a hidden tower that rises out of the fog. Once all three are passed, Eboshi tells the Traveler about the Bathysmal Vishaps, the Watatsumi Omikami, the time before the sun and moon, and the Dainichi Mikoshi.
### LARGE +
In-universe, the trials tie into the Towers of the Three Realms, which the people of Byakuyakoku built to keep a border land stable. Eboshi is also called Erebos in Enkanomiya.

## plot
### LITE
Eboshi, a translucent shade, meets the Traveler near the Serpent's Heart and proposes a trial path. Agreeing makes three new areas surface from the fog. At each the Traveler is tested, then returns to Eboshi for her explanations.
### STANDARD +
The Traveler is told to use the Emanant Skylights to reach each trial area, where a Jibashiri waits. The Traveler is recognised by the Jibashiri at all three places, then returns to Eboshi, who confers knowledge about the vishaps, the Watatsumi Omikami, the age before the sun and moon, and the Dainichi Mikoshi.
### LARGE +
Starting the quest reveals three new subareas on the Enkanomiya map: Yachimatahiko's Locus, Yachimatahime's Locus and Kunado's Locus. Eboshi can be found east of the northwest-most waypoint of the Serpent's Heart and during Evernight.

## revelations
@version: 2.4
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
The Byakuyakoku Collection (as cited by the wiki) records that the people of Enkanomiya built the Towers of the Three Realms at the borders of Byakuyakoku, a land between the Human, Elemental (vishap) and Abyssal (Void) realms, to stabilise its tendencies and keep the realms in harmony. Because they are so important they are hidden with secret arts and can be summoned only by shrine maidens such as Eboshi and by the Vassals of Watatsumi.
### LARGE +
The towers' ancient names have been forgotten; the three Loci carry later names.

## characters
### LITE
The Traveler and Paimon; Eboshi.
### STANDARD +
Eboshi is a shade of Tokoyo, translucent like others of her kind, and the wiki lists Tsuyuko as her descendant. Three Jibashiri are met at the trials.

## consequences
### STANDARD
The three Loci become accessible and Eboshi stays in Enkanomiya. The Traveler gains background on the vishaps, the Watatsumi Omikami and the Dainichi Mikoshi that sets up more of the island's story, including Hyperion's Dirge.

## placement
### STANDARD
Released in Version 2.4 as an Enkanomiya world quest series. It requires From Dusk to Dawn in Byakuyakoku (handing the Bloodbranch Coral to Tsuyuko). Hyperion's Dirge has the same requirement, so the two can be done in either order.

### ULTRA +
The wiki notes her Enkanomiya name Erebos echoes the Greek Erebus, a personification of darkness, and her Narukami-style name Eboshi is the name of a traditional Japanese cap; these are etymological observations from the wiki, not in-game statements. Starting the series requires only the earlier Enkanomiya quest, and the rewards are of ordinary world-quest size.
