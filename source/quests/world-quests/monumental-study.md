## summary
@spoiler: minor
### LITE
Monumental Study is a Sumeru desert world quest, added in version 3.6, set in the Temir Mountains of Gavireh Lajavard. The Order of Skeptics scholar Sosi asks the Traveler, with the Pari Sorush, to collect four fragments of the Khvarena Monument and to chase off a Fatui camp. Its commander, Markozov, wants Sorush for her knowledge. When the fragments are restored, the inscription is a warning about past disasters, not the source of divine power Sosi hoped for.
### STANDARD
The series begins after the first step of As the Khvarena's Light Shows, either by talking to Sosi or by picking up a Khvarena Inscription Fragment. It has two sub-quests, Investigate the Fatui Camps Marked by Sosi and Search for the Lost Monument Fragments, which can be done in either order. The tone is light and satirical: Sosi's dignified scholarship is undercut by ambition, and Markozov's love of knowledge is menacing. The quest ties a small mystery about a lost monument to Sumeru's debates about knowledge, power and accountability.
### LARGE +
The quest is a short companion to the main Sumeru Archon quest and the Pari storyline. It treats the Order of Skeptics as an institution with ideals of scrutiny but also with human weaknesses, and treats the Fatui in Sumeru as people drawn by rumours of divine power.

## plot
@version: 3.6
### STANDARD
Setup. After the Traveler and Paimon first handle a Khvarena Inscription Fragment, Sorush says it is something for the Skeptics. Sosi greets them as if their timing was perfect. Depending on progress in the Archon quest, the Traveler may first confront her about Nasejuna's actions; she insists that one man's deeds do not represent the whole Order. She then leads them to the Khvarena Monument, which looks like one eroded slab because its fragments are lost. She says it carries the true names of every Pari and the first contract with the Skeptics, that the Pari's immortality is sustained by it, and that whoever restores it would obtain a power that rivals the gods. Sorush challenges her; Sosi insists she wants only the text, for research. She makes it a paid commission: find the four fragments and clear the Fatui camps nearby, whose commander she wants gone.

The Fatui. The Traveler and Paimon, with Sorush urging them on, defeat Fatui soldiers at several camps, read a soldier's journal that complains about the desert heat and about the commander's cheese-smelling breath, and a memo showing that reports go to the main camp at noon. At noon they find the camp empty and follow footprints through further fighters to Commander Markozov. He is delighted to meet a Pari, calls Paimon a pet, asks the Traveler to hand Sorush over and says he would use the Pari to its full potential. Sorush has the Traveler beat him.

The fragments. With Sorush's help, who can see what mortals cannot, the Traveler finds all four fragments, tracked by the quest item In Pursuit of the Lost Inscription. Sorush says her Yasnapati has already taught the Fatui a lesson, and Sosi hands the honour of restoring the monument to the Traveler.

The inscription. As soon as the fragments are placed, Markozov bursts in again, saying knowledge is the source of his power and he cannot let it be taken away; he wants the Pari because he can "smell" knowledge on her. The Traveler defeats him again and he is left muttering about not enough knowledge. The restored inscription gives records of past disasters and how the Skeptics' predecessors dealt with them. Sosi is disappointed, and says her predecessors' documents seem to conflict with what she had read and she must find out which is true. Sorush warns about falling for convincing knowledge, and Sosi answers that Skeptics believe true knowledge appears when it is scrutinised.
### LARGE +
Order. The commission can be taken from Sosi or started by finding a fragment, and the quest may complete automatically after the Fatui quest. If the Archon quest has been finished, the Traveler can also confront Sosi about Nasejuna before starting. Gameplay notes, labelled as such: the quest grants the achievement "This Mystery Is Solved!", and while Sosi stands at the monument in the Gate of Zulqarnain subarea, the Consecrated Red Vulture and its Exquisite Chest are removed until the quest ends.
### ULTRA +
Fine detail. The Fatui camps use Skirmishers (Anemoboxer, Geochanter, Pyroslinger, Cryogunner, Hydrogunner) and an Electro Cicin Mage, and Markozov is shown fighting as an Electrohammer Vanguard under the label "Leader of Those Pursuing Knowledge". Sorush calls the Traveler her "Yasnapati" and Paimon a "Pale Floater" throughout, bickering with her. When the Traveler cannot recall the Pari's gifts, Sorush reminds Markozov that she has given humans many "badges of honour". Sosi admits she is "envious of his strength" in an optional line.

## revelations
@spoiler: minor
@version: 3.6
### STANDARD
- The Khvarena Monument has an inscription that is a warning to later generations, mentioning Amrita and Khvarena, spirits of light, the Dahri, and the need to stay diligent and brave.
- The monument's inscription is not a contract with the Pari or a source of divine power.
- Sosi's own sources about the past are inconsistent with the inscription.
### LARGE +
- Markozov of the Fatui equates knowledge with power and honour and is willing to take a Pari for it. Sorush warns the Traveler about being consumed by it.

## characters
@spoiler: none
### STANDARD
- **Sosi**: a scholar of the Order of Skeptics. Formal and a little vain, with a taste for power she denies; she reads fast, but is humble enough to reconsider her premises.
- **Sorush**: a Pari who accompanies the Traveler, proud, sharp-tongued and protective of Pari secrets. She treats Paimon with teasing contempt.
- **Markozov**: a Fatui commander in the desert, strong and obsessed with knowledge, who speaks warmly but wants to claim Sorush.
- **Paimon** and the Traveler, who take the commission for a fair price and tease Sosi.
- **Nasejuna**: not present, but the Traveler can raise his actions with Sosi.
### LARGE +
Sosi's treatment of Nasejuna's misdeeds is a rare moment where the Skeptics are held to account by outsiders, and she defends the Order as a whole while admitting that his behaviour breaks its principles.

## consequences
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
The monument's inscription is revealed, Sosi continues her research, and Markozov is driven away. The Traveler is paid by the Order of Skeptics, with Primogems, Mora and other rewards, and unlocks an achievement.

## placement
@spoiler: none
### STANDARD
Released in version 3.6 in the Sumeru desert. It is tied to As the Khvarena's Light Shows, and the dialogue changes depending on whether the Traveler has already learned of Nasejuna's actions, so it is best placed after that Archon quest step.

## entry: Khvarena Monument
@keys: Khvarena Monument, Khvarena Inscription Fragment, Khvarena Inscription Fragments
@version: 3.6
### STANDARD
The Khvarena Monument stands in Gavireh Lajavard. It looks eroded and blank because four fragments are missing. Sosi claims the original text lists every Pari's true name and the first contract between the Pari and the Order of Skeptics, and that it sustains the Pari's immortality. Once the fragments are placed, the readable inscription turns out to be a warning from the past: with the power of Amrita and Khvarena the authors extinguished "phantoms mirroring the bedeviled sky", praised the spirits of light and the Dahri, and urged future readers to remain diligent and brave and not to fall into decadence and neglect.

## entry: Sosi of the Order of Skeptics
@keys: Sosi
@keys2: Skeptics, Khvarena Monument, monument, Sorush
@version: 3.6
### STANDARD
Sosi is a Skeptic scholar studying the Khvarena Monument in the Temir Mountains. She hides her interest in divine power behind talk of research and history, defends the Order against criticism of Nasejuna and sets the Traveler a paid commission. Her disappointment at the inscription is shown as a researcher's doubt about her own predecessors' records. She says the Akademiya and the Order parted over a clash of ideals but both prize history and heritage.

## entry: Markozov
@keys: Markozov
@version: 3.6
### STANDARD
Markozov is a Fatui commander who has set up camps near the Khvarena Monument. His soldiers' complaints show a punctual officer who inspects camps daily. He says knowledge is his only source of power and honour, calls Paimon a pet, covets the Pari Sorush and is beaten twice by the Traveler.
