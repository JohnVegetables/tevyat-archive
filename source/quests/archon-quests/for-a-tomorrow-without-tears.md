## summary
### LITE
For a Tomorrow Without Tears is Act II of the Mondstadt Prologue, the first Archon Quest chapter. After the Four Winds temples are cleared, the Traveler returns to Mondstadt, is made an Honorary Knight of Favonius and joins the bard Venti in a plan to cure the dragon Stormterror, who is really Dvalin, of the corruption the Abyss Order is exploiting. The plan needs the Holy Lyre der Himmel, which must be obtained, recovered from the Fatui and repaired with Dvalin's tears. At Starsnatch Cliff an Abyss Mage turns Dvalin against them and the lyre is broken.
### STANDARD +
The act is where Venti's identity as Barbatos, the Anemo Archon, comes into the open, where Jean and Diluc become active allies, and where the Fatui and Abyss Order stop being background threats. It requires Adventure Rank 10 and ends with the cliff confrontation that sets up Act III.

## plot
### LITE
Returning to report to Jean, the Traveler finds her outside the Goth Grand Hotel dismissing a masked Fatui diplomat who urges that the dragon simply be dealt with by the Fatui. The Traveler becomes an Honorary Knight. Paimon then tracks a boy in the streets who sings about Stormterror: Venti. He tells them to meet at the great tree in Windrise, explains that Dvalin is degrading and that the Abyss Order is exploiting it, and says the Holy Lyre der Himmel at the Favonius Cathedral can help. The attempt to borrow it fails, a Fatui mage takes it first, and Diluc and Jean help recover it. After restoring it with Dvalin's tears the party summons him at Starsnatch Cliff, where an Abyss Mage ruins everything.
### STANDARD
The act begins with Shadow Over Mondstadt. Near the Goth Grand Hotel the Traveler finds Jean refusing a masked Fatui diplomat, Anastasia, who offers to have the Fatui "put an end" to the dragon; Jean rebukes her for proposing to destroy one of the Four Winds of Mondstadt. Back at the Knights' headquarters, Lisa examines the crystal the Traveler picked up in the forest and finds that it gives off energy harmful to Vision users, so the Traveler pockets it again, keeping quiet about why. Jean names the Traveler an Honorary Knight.

Paimon recognises the presence that clung to the crystal in a boy running through the streets, and the party follows his footprints with Elemental Sight. The boy is Venti. He sings of Stormterror and the reason for the dragon's rampage, and recognises the Traveler. When the Traveler takes out the crystal it has been purified, so Venti realises the Traveler's power can save Dvalin. He sends them to the great tree at Windrise, where the Hero of Mondstadt is said to have ascended to Celestia. After the Traveler beats an Eye of the Storm, Venti explains that Dvalin is suffering from a degradation that the Abyss Order is exploiting. His own earlier attempt to cure the dragon had gone wrong when the Traveler's unexpected arrival left him poisoned, though he had since recovered.

Venti's remedy is the Holy Lyre der Himmel, kept at the Favonius Cathedral. He asks Sister Gotelinde for it, even telling her he is Barbatos, the Anemo Archon, and she takes him for a madman. The Traveler asks properly, but borrowing it needs papers signed by the Grand Master, the Seneschal and the community representative, since it is currently used only for Ludi Harpastum. The Traveler sneaks into the vault, but a Fatui Electro Cicin Mage teleports in and takes the lyre, and a guard spots the Traveler. Venti's wind carries the party to the Angel's Share, where Diluc, the owner, gives them a quiet table and misdirects the pursuing Knights.
### LARGE +
Diluc, who recognises the Traveler as the new Honorary Knight and calls it a shame that they joined the Knights, agrees to help after Venti performs the situation for him. He arranges a meeting for the next night. There the party finds that Diluc has also brought in Jean, who is present privately rather than as Acting Grand Master, because she has little room to act officially. She backs the plan because she knows Dvalin's past. Diluc has interrogated some Fatui and learned that the lyre is not at the Goth Grand Hotel but at a stronghold outside the city.

The party takes the lyre after fighting through its guards; a Fatui Agent warns that Signora will end them. The lyre is in poor condition, so they collect Teardrop Crystals, which are Dvalin's tears, and use them to restore it fully. They then go to Starsnatch Cliff to summon Dvalin. Venti begins to play and speaks to the dragon, but a Cryo Abyss Mage appears beside Dvalin, tells him that Venti abandoned him and that Mondstadt has turned its back on him, and says the bard is deceiving him again. Dvalin, calling Venti Barbatos, accuses him of planning to have him slain, and flies off in rage; the lyre is ruined beyond repair. Afterwards Jean begins to say "Barbat-" and corrects herself to Venti, who replies that she has known his identity for some time and thanks her for still using his name. Diluc commits to finding the Abyss Mage through his own contacts, noting he has already killed one in a temple.
### ULTRA +
The act is made up of fifteen quests in this order: Shadow Over Mondstadt, Unexpected Encounter, That Green Fellow, Let the Wind Lead, Venti's Plan, Venti's New Plan, Wild Escape, Behind the Scenes, Chasing Shadows, State of the Holy Lyre der Himmel, then three that can be done in any order (Fallen Tears at the Thousand Winds Temple, Hidden Tears at Eagle's Gate, Stolen Tears at the Eclipse Tribe), then Crystal Tears and Reunion With the Dragon. Completing the last quest grants the achievement named after the act, and the player receives a Damaged Holy Lyre der Himmel item as a reward.

The closing cutscene shows the Traveler's sibling overlooking Stormterror's Lair while two Abyss Mages bow to them. After Dvalin flies past, the Cryo Abyss Mage that tricked him reappears behind them and addresses the sibling as "Your Highness", saying that when their homeland returns to this world they will revel in its glory. Nothing in this act identifies the homeland.

## revelations
@version: 1.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
- Venti is Barbatos, the Anemo Archon. He tells Gotelinde so (she does not believe him), Dvalin addresses him by that name, and Jean shows she has known for some time and keeps calling him Venti.
- Stormterror is Dvalin, one of the Four Winds, and has been degraded by something the Abyss Order manipulates. Jean already knows of Dvalin's past; the wider public treats Stormterror as a vicious dragon.
- The Teardrop Crystals are Dvalin's tears, and the Traveler can purify them.
- The Fatui are involved and hostile to Mondstadt's approach: a diplomat urges killing the dragon, a Fatui mage steals the Holy Lyre, and an agent invokes Signora.
### LARGE +
- The Abyss Order is deliberately working on Dvalin, telling him that Venti abandoned him and that Mondstadt betrayed him. Venti's protest that it is not like that is cut off, so the act leaves open how Dvalin will take it.
- The Traveler's sibling is in Teyvat and is honoured as royalty by the Abyss Order's mages. The act shows this without explaining it.

## characters
### STANDARD
Traveler: Honorary Knight, bearer of the power that purifies the crystals. Paimon: recognises Venti by his presence. Venti: bard and Anemo Archon, trying to heal his dragon friend. Jean: Acting Grand Master, constrained by office, acting privately with Diluc. Diluc: owner of the Angel's Share, who supplies information and shelter. Lisa: studies the crystal. Dvalin: the afflicted dragon, whose tears are the key material. Gotelinde: the Cathedral sister who refuses the lyre. The Traveler's sibling appears only in the final cutscene.
### LARGE +
The Fatui are represented by the diplomat Anastasia, a Cicin Mage and an agent; Signora is named but absent. The Cryo Abyss Mage is the act's central antagonist and is dealt with in Act III.

## consequences
### STANDARD
The Holy Lyre is destroyed and Dvalin flees, now believing he was betrayed. Diluc takes on the job of tracking the Abyss Mage. These lead directly into Act III, where an Abyss Mage is killed at the winery, the Lair is entered and Dvalin is finally purified.
### LARGE +
Jean, Venti and Diluc become openly aligned, and the Fatui's interest in the lyre and in Venti sets up Signora's attack at the end of Act III.

## placement
### STANDARD
Prologue Act II, after The Outlander Who Caught the Wind (Act I) and before Song of the Dragon and Freedom (Act III). Released with version 1.0. Requires Adventure Rank 10.

## entry: Holy Lyre der Himmel Plot
@keys: Holy Lyre der Himmel, Teardrop Crystals, Dvalin's tears
@version: 1.0
### STANDARD
The Holy Lyre der Himmel is a sacred instrument kept at the Favonius Cathedral and in this period used only for Ludi Harpastum. Borrowing it formally needs signatures from three authorities. After a Fatui mage takes it, the party recovers it in poor condition and repairs it with Teardrop Crystals, which are Dvalin's tears, but the Abyss Order's interference at Starsnatch Cliff destroys it.

## entry: Starsnatch Cliff Confrontation
@keys: Starsnatch Cliff
@version: 1.0
### STANDARD
At Starsnatch Cliff, Venti plays the restored lyre to call Dvalin. Before the dragon can listen, a Cryo Abyss Mage turns him against Venti, calling the bard a deceiver. Dvalin flies off in a rage, the lyre is wrecked, and Diluc and Jean resolve to hunt down the mage.
