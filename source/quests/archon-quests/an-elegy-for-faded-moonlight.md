## summary
### LITE
Act IV of Song of the Welkin Moon. Dainsleif and Albedo, with Durin, join the Nod-Krai allies. In a ritual the group views Rerir's past as a Khaenri'ahn Sinner. Rerir turns on Nefer, and in the final fight Columbina, helped by an alchemical array and Dainsleif, throws him through a moon gate out of Teyvat.
### STANDARD +
The act has three quests: All Is Foretold, Dance of Death: Serpent and Scorpion and Echoes of Fate. It follows A Nation That Doesn't Exist and precedes A Nocturne of the Far North. It was released in version 6.1 (Luna II).
### LARGE +
The act is the resolution of the Rerir thread begun in Elegy of Dust and Lamplight. It also gives Nefer's backstory in a domain the group is pulled into and ends with a quiet conversation about the Lord-Artificer.

## plot
### LITE
At the allies' secret base the Traveler reunites with Flins, who met Albedo and a young man introduced as Mini Durin. Varka brings Dainsleif. The group enters Rerir's past at the Curatorium, then is pulled into a desert domain where Rerir attacks Nefer. Afterwards the group prepares and fights him, and Columbina flicks him into a moon gate held by Dainsleif.
### STANDARD +
The allies gather and Dainsleif joins them. A ritual at the Curatorium shows Rerir's past with King Irmin and Tholindis, then drags the group into a desert domain showing Nefer's past, where Rerir attacks her. In the final fight Columbina, with Albedo and Durin's array and Dainsleif's help, throws Rerir out of Teyvat.
### LARGE +
All Is Foretold: the Traveler arrives with Columbina and finds Flins in good spirits. Arlecchino appears only to accompany her friend and leaves to speak with Sandrone at the Kuuvahki Experimental Design Bureau. Varka introduces Dainsleif, whom Paimon describes as a Khaenri'ahn noble. Nefer, suspicious of a Khaenri'ahn who should not still be alive, joins them. Dainsleif asks for help investigating Rerir's past and explains how the Five Sinners came to be. The Traveler reports on Columbina and Arlecchino, and Columbina says that all three Moon Marrows in hand are still not enough to overpower Rerir. At the Curatorium Nefer says Dainsleif trusted the Traveler's group to keep the matter secret. With Jahoda and Lauma, they enter Rerir's past. Rerir carried out tasks for King Irmin until ordered to kill his own fiancee Tholindis. His next target, Frithr, was spared because Rerir had grown disillusioned. Dainsleif then asked him to help free his brother Vedrfolnir, imprisoned by Irmin for a bad prophecy, with Surtalogi, Rhinedottir and Hroptatyr. Vedrfolnir said he had not known the prophecy, though both had heard the same voice, and told Rerir to go to the Universitas Magistrorum, where Tholindis was in danger. He found her before a portal. She had learned the truth and fell in so he could live an ordinary life. Rerir, in despair, accepted the voice's offer of power in hope of reuniting with her.

Dance of Death: Serpent and Scorpion: the ritual seems to succeed, but Nefer finds the group in a desert. The domain shows her past: raised as an adopted child by Sinuhe, leader of the Shimti tribe, who unintentionally doomed it by taking water from a well of the Iab tribe. Later she went to Aaru Village, where Uncle Anpu arranged for the Sumeru Akademiya to invite her, but the Akademiya had not acknowledged her adoptive father's letter. She left for revenge on the tribes that killed her family. They reach a temple of Thoth, one of King Deshret's Seven Pillars. Ashru becomes an abyssal creature and grabs Nefer by the throat, and Rerir says she has seen too much. An attack from above interrupts him and the group is teleported out. Back in reality Nefer's chessbox shakes and she binds it with Dendro restraints.

Echoes of Fate: with Rerir restrained, the group prepares. When the seal breaks, Rerir expels Lauma, Aino and Flins and turns on Nefer. Albedo and Durin set up an alchemical array to siphon his power into Columbina, who overpowers him and opens a moon gate. Rerir believes she cannot hold both him and the gate, and she says someone else does. He sees Dainsleif keeping it open, and is flicked out of Teyvat. Nefer is temporarily blinded by the fight, and Lauma takes her for treatment. The following day the group celebrates at the Flagship. The Traveler then visits Columbina and Sandrone at the bureau, where a slightly peeved Sandrone gives them tea and a notebook to use as a coaster, then mentions the Lord-Artificer, Alain Guillotin, and asks the Traveler's opinion of his actions before saying they may do as they like and starting maintenance on Pulonia.

Rerir's tasks for Irmin are shown as a sequence ending with the order against Tholindis, after which he became disillusioned with Irmin's directives. The wiki summary says Nefer goaded Rerir into using his full power, which cost her sight for a time.

### ULTRA +
The act pairs two flashbacks, Rerir's and Nefer's, as parallel stories of loss and vengeance, and then closes with Sandrone raising the Lord-Artificer's past with the Traveler.

## revelations
### LITE
Rerir's tragedy: he lost his fiancee Tholindis and accepted power from a voice. Rerir himself is cast out of Teyvat.
### STANDARD +
Dainsleif explains how the Five Sinners came to be; the free-Vedrfolnir operation involved Dainsleif, Surtalogi, Rhinedottir and Hroptatyr alongside Rerir. Vedrfolnir and Rerir heard the same voice. Nefer's past as an Eremite is shown.
### LARGE +
Dainsleif is shown keeping the moon gate open and Nefer is suspicious of him, wondering how a Khaenri'ahn is still alive.

## characters
### LITE
The Traveler, Paimon, Columbina, Dainsleif, Nefer, Flins and Rerir are the main figures.
### STANDARD +
Allies: Albedo, Durin (introduced as Mini Durin from Simulanka), Varka, Lauma, Aino, Jahoda, Arlecchino and Sandrone. Characters seen in Rerir's past include Irmin, Tholindis, Frithr, Vedrfolnir, Surtalogi, Rhinedottir and Hroptatyr; in Nefer's domain, Sinuhe, Uncle Anpu, Thoth and Ashru.

## consequences
### STANDARD
Rerir is banished from Teyvat. Nefer is blinded for a time. The Traveler leaves the final conversation with Sandrone with the question of the Lord-Artificer's legacy unresolved, and the story continues in A Nocturne of the Far North.

## placement
### STANDARD
Song of the Welkin Moon Act IV, after A Nation That Doesn't Exist and before A Nocturne of the Far North. Released with Luna II (6.1, 2025-10-22).
