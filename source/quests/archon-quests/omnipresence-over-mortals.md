## summary
### LITE
Omnipresence Over Mortals is the third act of Chapter II of the Archon Quests, set in Inazuma and released in Version 2.1. The Traveler joins the Watatsumi Army's Resistance as captain of a special unit, discovers that the Fatui are the Resistance's secret sponsor and are supplying life-draining Delusions, and loses the soldier Teppei to them. With Yae Miko's help, the Traveler exposes Kujou Takayuki's betrayal, defeats Signora in a duel before the Shogun, and then fights the Raiden Shogun in the Plane of Euthymia, where the true Ei agrees to abolish the Vision Hunt Decree.
### STANDARD +
The act has nine quests, from Sword, Fish, Resistance to Wishes. It combines the war's resolution, the unmasking of the Shogun as a puppet made by Ei, the death of Signora, and the loss of the Electro Gnosis to the Fatui. It ends by pointing the Traveler to Sumeru.

## plot
### LITE
Kokomi makes the Traveler captain of Swordfish II, and the unit investigates missing supplies near Fort Mumei. Teppei is aged to death by a Fatui Delusion. The Traveler destroys the Delusion factory, but is beaten by the Balladeer and saved by Yae Miko. Miko trains the Traveler, helps win over Kujou Sara and has the Traveler face Signora and then the Shogun. After a duel in the Plane of Euthymia, Ei agrees to end the Decree.
### STANDARD
**Sword, Fish, Resistance.** Teppei takes the Traveler to Watatsumi Island to meet Sangonomiya Kokomi. She explains that she accepted an anonymous letter offering the Resistance supplies as long as it kept fighting the Shogunate, and that she doubted the sender's motives but had little choice because the island lacked resources. On the Vision Hunt Decree she says the attitude of the Tenryou Commission and of the Raiden Shogun does not add up. She makes the Traveler captain of a special operations unit, Swordfish II, and promises Teppei a combat role. After a first task the unit investigates vanishing supplies around Fort Mumei, staying at Bourou Village, where Teppei, now captain of a unit himself, praises the new sponsor's supplies.

**Those Who Yearn for the Gods' Gaze.** The Fort Mumei search finds nothing, but the area feels strange. Teppei arrives with greying hair and a cough, blaming a cold, and explains the Tatarigami, a phenomenon that appeared after the Raiden Shogun killed Orobashi, the god of Watatsumi Island, during the Archon War. He warns that it harms health. Back on Watatsumi Island the Traveler finds Kokomi and Gorou examining a secret weapon an officer handed in: a vitality-absorbing Delusion, which reveals the Fatui as the sponsor. Kokomi orders all users recalled. The Traveler realises Teppei's boosted strength came from a Delusion and rushes to him too late. Teppei, who now looks like an old man, dies in despair, and the Traveler swears revenge on the Fatui.
### LARGE +
**Delusion.** Kokomi suspects the Fatui had local support and reluctantly gives the Traveler the suspected factory's location. Inside, the Traveler learns that the Delusions are made with Crystal Marrow collected by the Kanjou Commission, and finds a notice that a Resistance member, Nathan, is a Fatui mole. The Traveler confronts the Balladeer, a Fatui Harbinger who mocks the Resistance and shows that Inazuma has fallen for the Fatui's schemes. Provoked, the Traveler succumbs to the Tatarigami, falls unconscious and is found by Yae Miko.

**The Servant's Path.** The Traveler wakes in the Grand Narukami Shrine. Miko says Kokomi has resolved the factory crisis, and explains that the Shogun who runs Inazuma is a puppet made in the likeness of Ei, who is the true Raiden Shogun and lives inside her inner world, the Plane of Euthymia, pursuing eternity. The Traveler is the first person Ei has met since entering the Plane, and Miko believes this is the key to saving both Inazuma and Ei from a disastrous future. She trains the Traveler hard to survive the Electro Archon's attacks and plans to turn Kujou Sara against the Tenryou Commission by proving that her adoptive father Kujou Takayuki has betrayed the Shogunate. She invites Sara to the shrine, promising evidence if Sara returns alone in three days.

**Proof of Guilt.** The Traveler goes back to Komore Teahouse to meet Kamisato Ayaka and Thoma. Yoimiya supplies a distraction so the Shuumatsuban ninja Sayu can slip into the Tenryou Commission, and they obtain Takayuki's falsified, over-optimistic report on the war and his correspondence with the Fatui.

**Blind Loyalty, Reckless Courage.** Sara reads the documents, is furious and storms off to confront her adoptive father. Miko tells the Traveler that to meet Ei they must recreate the way they first met her, and gives them an omamori as a parting gift with vague promises about its use. The Traveler and Paimon join Sara and fight through Tenryou guards to Takayuki, who confirms everything and admits his selfish reasons for accepting the Fatui's proposals. Learning that Signora is meeting the Shogun at Tenshukaku, Sara runs there, and the Traveler follows.

**Duel Before the Throne.** At Tenshukaku the Traveler finds Sara defeated by Signora. Signora says Sara's Inazuman heritage spares her, but the Traveler, as Inazuma's most wanted criminal, has no such protection. The Traveler remembers that the winner of a duel before the throne receives a second chance, and challenges Signora with the Shogun's approval. The Traveler wins, and the Shogun executes Signora with Musou no Hitotachi, her most powerful technique. Shaken, the Traveler and Paimon are allowed to leave Tenshukaku alive.

**The Omnipresent God.** Outside, the Traveler meets Gorou's Resistance forces. The Shogun cuts space open behind the Traveler to execute them, but Kaedehara Kazuha resonates with the Masterless Vision of his late friend and uses its Electro power with his own Anemo Vision to block the technique and knock her back. The Resistance rallies and charges, and to save them from being slaughtered the Traveler attacks and is pulled into the Plane of Euthymia.

Ei has known about the Fatui's involvement all along: she judged Visions, meaning the pursuit of aspirations, a greater threat to eternity than a temporary war, so her inaction amounted to tacit approval. Their clash becomes a duel. Midway, Miko uses the omamori to enter the Plane and reminds the Traveler that they fight for those who want the Decree abolished. The light of human ambition from the confiscated Visions outside lights up the dark Plane, and the Traveler defeats Ei.

Ei explains why she seeks eternity: she lost close companions and her sister Makoto, and she saw the Heavenly Principles destroy Khaenri'ah. Miko persuades her that this vision of eternity causes too much suffering and loneliness, even for herself, pointing out that the Plane's sky had brightened with a predawn glow when Ei lit up on meeting her friend after so long. Ei agrees to abolish the Vision Hunt Decree but says she needs time to reconsider how she rules Inazuma.

**Wishes.** After the dust settles, Miko reveals she saved the Traveler from the Balladeer by giving him the Electro Gnosis. Ei had entrusted it to her before entering the Plane, knowing Miko would sell it for something of equal value, since Ei no longer needed it. As thanks, Miko answers the Traveler's questions: the Balladeer was Ei's prototype puppet, recruited into the Fatui and strengthened until he became the Sixth Harbinger; and in Sumeru, the Traveler's next stop, knowledge is managed as a resource, though she does not know whether that was Lesser Lord Kusanali's choice or the Sages'. She also speculates that the Traveler has no Vision because they have not yet found an ambition strong enough to receive one.

## revelations
@version: 2.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The Shogun who governs Inazuma is a puppet; the true Ei is in the Plane of Euthymia. The Fatui sponsored the Resistance with Delusions that drain the lives of their users, using Crystal Marrow gathered by the Kanjou Commission, and Kujou Takayuki colluded with them. Signora is dead. The Balladeer is Ei's prototype puppet and the Sixth Harbinger. The Electro Gnosis passes from Ei through Miko to the Balladeer. Ei agrees to end the Vision Hunt Decree.
### LARGE +
Ei's inaction during the war was a deliberate stance: she saw ambition, the thing Visions represent, as more dangerous to eternity than the war. Her motives include the deaths of her companions and of her sister Makoto and the fall of Khaenri'ah, and the Tatarigami around Watatsumi dates from Orobashi's death in the Archon War.

## characters
### STANDARD
**Traveler and Paimon**: the Traveler becomes a Resistance captain, loses Teppei, breaks the Delusion factory, defeats Signora and Ei.
**Yae Miko**: the shrine's guide and Ei's old friend, who rescues, trains and pushes the plan, and in the end persuades Ei to relent.
**Sangonomiya Kokomi and Gorou**: lead the Resistance; Kokomi accepted the sponsor's supplies despite her doubts.
**Teppei**: grows in confidence with the Delusion's help and dies of its effects.
**Kujou Sara and Kujou Takayuki**: Sara is turned against her adoptive father when his betrayal is proved; he confesses selfish motives.
**Signora and the Balladeer**: Fatui Harbingers; she is executed, he is the Sixth Harbinger.
**Raiden Shogun and Ei**: the puppet Shogun and the true Electro Archon, who is defeated and persuaded.
### LARGE +
Ayaka, Thoma, Yoimiya and Sayu are the allies who help obtain Takayuki's documents, and Kaedehara Kazuha is the one who blocks the Shogun's strike outside Tenshukaku.

## consequences
### STANDARD
The Vision Hunt Decree is abolished, the Fatui's Delusion scheme collapses, and Signora is dead. The Traveler leaves with the Resistance's campaign effectively over; Ei says she must reconsider how she rules. The Electro Gnosis is in the Balladeer's hands. The Traveler is pointed toward Sumeru.
### LARGE +
The next Archon Quest acts continue the Inazuma story in Requiem of the Echoing Depths and The Crane Returns on the Wind.

## placement
### STANDARD
Released in Version 2.1 as the third act of Chapter II, after Stillness, the Sublimation of Shadow (2.0). Next in story order are Requiem of the Echoing Depths and The Crane Returns on the Wind. The act shares its name with the Chapter II title in some listings, so use "Act III" to be unambiguous. It requires Adventure Rank 30.

## trivia
### ULTRA
The act's Chinese title is Qian Shou Bai Yan, Tianxia Renjian and its Japanese title is Senju Hyakume no Ukiyo (a romanisation the wiki marks as assumed). The Chinese and Japanese titles refer to a thousand hands and a hundred eyes, which is not how the English title reads.
