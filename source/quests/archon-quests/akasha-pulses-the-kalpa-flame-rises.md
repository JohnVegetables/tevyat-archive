## summary
### LITE
Chapter III Act V of the Archon Quest. The Traveler, Paimon and a coalition of Sumeru's scholars, mercenaries and Eremites carry out a plan to free Nahida, then enter the Akasha with her to defeat Scaramouche, who is being made into a false god. Nahida cleanses Irminsul of the forbidden knowledge by erasing Rukkhadevata from it, and Dottore bargains with her for the Electro Gnosis.
### STANDARD +
The act has five quests: Through the Predawn Night, As by a God's Side, Jnagarbha Day, Where the Boat of Consciousness Lies and A Toast to Victory. It runs from the Eremites' staging at Caravan Ribat and Pardis Dhyai, through the Akademiya on Jnagarbha Day, to the Sanctuary of Surasthana and the inside of the Akasha, and ends with a celebratory feast in the Grand Bazaar. Released in version 3.2.
### LARGE +
It is the climax of Chapter III in Sumeru. It resolves the Akasha-driven control of the Akademiya, the false god plot and the forbidden-knowledge corruption of Irminsul, and is followed by the quests Caribert and Inversion of Genesis.

## plot
### LITE
Alhaitham, Cyno, Dehya and Rahman plan to rescue Nahida from the Akademiya's Akasha. After Cyno has Rahman's men arrested to get them into Sumeru City and the Traveler learns Dottore's movements, the plan is carried out on Jnagarbha Day. Alhaitham feigns insanity, the Traveler wakes Nahida inside the Akasha and Nahida takes the system back. Together she and the Traveler defeat Scaramouche in the Akasha, restore Irminsul by removing Rukkhadevata, and Nahida trades the Electro Gnosis to Dottore. A feast follows.
### STANDARD +
The group settles on acting on Jnagarbha Day, when the Akademiya is distracted. Cyno has Rahman's Eremites arrested so they can be brought into Sumeru City; Dottore draws the group to Port Ormos as a decoy while Scaramouche's manifestation warns them off at Pardis Dhyai. In the Akademiya Alhaitham stages a betrayal, the Traveler wakes Nahida inside the Akasha, and Nilou and the Eremites pull the guards away. Nahida and the Traveler then defeat Scaramouche, remove Rukkhadevata from Irminsul, and Nahida gives Dottore the Electro Gnosis on condition he destroys his other segments. The Traveler keeps Rukkhadevata's memory secret at the feast.
### LARGE +
Through the Predawn Night: the group meets at Aaru Village, where Candace stands guard, and settles on acting on the next Jnagarbha Day. Alhaitham briefs Rahman, prepares a "simulated rescue" with the Traveler using the headset and Knowledge Capsules, and argues that people have come to depend too much on the Akasha. Cyno objects to the plan's cost, since it needs Rahman's Eremites inside Sumeru City, but agrees: as a Matra he arranges with the Caravan Ribat guards, whose superior is Luxembarbo, to arrest Rahman's unregistered men. The arrest is staged two days later. Cyno then sends the Traveler to help Dehya win Tighnari's support.

As by a God's Side: at Pardis Dhyai Tighnari reports that Dottore has just visited to demand Haypasia and left, since the Tsaritsa has called him home. Dehya tracks the Fatui to Port Ormos, where Dottore waves at them and they realise they were misled; they hurry back and drive the Fatui off. Touching Haypasia calls up a manifestation of Scaramouche, who tells them they cannot beat him, says he was always meant to be a god, admits some affection for Haypasia as his first follower, and calls lightning down on the area. Tighnari is struck but will recover; Dehya takes Dottore's leaving as proof the plan is working.

Jnagarbha Day: Alhaitham leads the Traveler through the Akademiya's front door. Azar intercepts them in the House of Daena and has them detained; Alhaitham appears to betray the Traveler and then attacks Azar, throwing down the Divine Knowledge Capsule, and is exiled to Aaru Village, though his Akasha turns green again, showing it was a ruse. In their cell the Traveler and Paimon find a stable connection and reach Nahida's consciousness inside the Akasha. She doubts her ability as an Archon until an unknown voice encourages her, and the Traveler breaks the barrier around her and wakes her. Meanwhile Nilou performs at the Akademiya gate as a distraction; Azar's order to detain her is overridden by a false order that Kusanali has escaped. The guards leave, Dehya and the Eremites lure them to the Bazaar with Isak dressed as Kusanali, and Cyno confronts Azar at the Sanctuary of Surasthana. Dehya frees the Traveler, who meets Nahida in person. Nahida reverts the Akasha to her own use alone, revokes the sages' access, and says the false god must be stopped.

Where the Boat of Consciousness Lies: Nahida gives the Traveler an upgraded terminal she has just made and says she has found the false god's location. Inside a Fatui-built facility they find Scaramouche in a mechanical suit; the sages could not infuse the Divine Knowledge Capsules into him and he lacks a god's spiritual height. He insists on re-enacting a scene of the Archon War, reaches into Nahida's chest for her Gnosis, and Nahida reveals he has been caught in a repeating samsara. She passes combat knowledge to the Traveler and compiles Sumeru's collective knowledge into the terminal, which lets them defeat him. Nahida takes the Electro Gnosis from the suit and he falls out, begging her not to.

Using the two Gnoses, Nahida and the Traveler enter Rukkhadevata's final memory and find it contaminated. A boat takes them to Rukkhadevata's lucid consciousness, who explains that she made Nahida from the purest Irminsul branch as her incarnation for the next samsara, and that to save Irminsul Nahida must erase her from it, leaving no trace of her existence in Teyvat. Nahida complies, Irminsul is restored, the people of Sumeru can dream again and the forbidden knowledge is gone. Back in the real world Nahida and Paimon have forgotten Rukkhadevata, but the Traveler remembers. Dottore then walks in, having put the Traveler and Paimon to sleep with a sound wave. He reminds Nahida she cannot fight him, but she threatens to destroy the Electro Gnosis, which could awaken Celestia. She agrees to give it to him on condition that he destroys his other segments, and he does. He then proposes trading information about "the skies of Teyvat being fake" for her Dendro Gnosis. The Traveler wakes at Gandharva Ville.

A Toast to Victory: Nilou's letter invites the Traveler to a feast celebrating Cyno's return to the post of General Mahamatra. The Traveler learns that Azar and the other sages were sentenced by Nahida to spend the rest of their lives in Avidya Forest cultivating wisdom, that Alhaitham refused an offer to be Grand Sage, that Dehya intends to leave Dunyarzad's service now the Eleazar cases are cured, and that Setaria plans to leave the Akademiya and keep studying for her people. Everyone now believes Nahida was always the Dendro Archon, so the Traveler keeps Rukkhadevata's memory secret in honour of her last wish. Nahida thanks everyone through the Traveler and then arrives at the feast in person.

Alhaitham's plan rests on assumptions about the Akasha: it has formidable computation, but Alhaitham is confident it cannot yet uncover a plan they are all working on together; the guards at Caravan Ribat must not learn that Cyno has stepped down; and a false order about Kusanali's escape empties the Akademiya of guards. The Traveler's work inside the Akasha divides into Nahida's own state of mind (self-doubt, then resolve) and the fight against Scaramouche, where Nahida uses the samsara loop and Sumeru's pooled knowledge.

The next day the Traveler asks Nahida about Fontaine and its Archon Focalors. She tells them that Irminsul records their sibling, who is not from Teyvat, as appearing suddenly in Khaenri'ah and then travelling Teyvat after the cataclysm, but with blurred records around the climax of the journey, which suggests someone is withholding information. She adds that the Fatui classed them as Teyvat's fourth "Descender", a visitor from outside, suspects the first to be the Heavenly Principles, and does not know the other two. She promises to help and hints she may appear in their dreams.
### ULTRA +
The act is built as a heist told in parallel strands: a political infiltration through the Akademiya, an Eremite and mercenary diversion in the Bazaar, and a mental operation inside the Akasha, all keyed to Jnagarbha Day because the Akademiya would be preoccupied. Dottore figures three times: in Tighnari's report of his visit to demand Haypasia, as the decoy at Port Ormos that draws the group away while the Fatui go after Haypasia, and finally as Nahida's negotiating partner. Several threads depend on side content in play: the wiki notes that To the Wise, The Unanswerable Problems and Sands of Solitude must be finished or advanced if started, in order to complete the act.

## revelations
### LITE
Rukkhadevata made Nahida as her successor and is erased from Irminsul and from everyone's memory to cure the forbidden knowledge. Scaramouche was being turned into a false god. Dottore wants the Electro Gnosis and hints at the "false sky".
### STANDARD +
Rukkhadevata says Nahida was created from the purest Irminsul branch to be her incarnation in the next samsara, and that Irminsul can only be saved if she is erased from it, which removes every trace of her. Nahida connects the forbidden knowledge to King Deshret's time and to the cataclysm. Dottore offers information about "the skies of Teyvat being fake" and reasons that Nahida's Dendro Gnosis has no further use now that the Akasha is to be shut down.
### LARGE +
Nahida reveals the Fatui's classification of the Traveler's sibling as the fourth Descender, and that Irminsul records the sibling as arriving in Khaenri'ah and travelling after the cataclysm. Alhaitham says he had never known of forbidden knowledge while working at the Akademiya and suspects the scholars and mercenaries who used it fell to its corruption, and that the Akademiya treated it as something else.

## characters
### LITE
The Traveler, Paimon, Nahida, Alhaitham, Cyno, Dehya, Scaramouche and Dottore are the principals.
### STANDARD +
Supporting cast: Candace, Tighnari, Collei, Nilou, Kaveh, Dunyarzad, Azar and the other sages, Rahman, Setaria, Luxembarbo, Isak and Uday, Sheikh Zubayr, Haypasia, Viraf and Rukkhadevata's lucid consciousness.

## consequences
### STANDARD
The Akasha is back in Nahida's sole control and the sages' access is revoked. Azar and the other sages are sentenced to live in Avidya Forest. Irminsul is restored and the forbidden knowledge is gone, but everyone except the Traveler has forgotten Rukkhadevata. Dottore leaves with the Electro Gnosis and has destroyed his other segments. Cyno resumes the post of General Mahamatra, and Nahida is regarded as the Dendro Archon from the start. The Traveler learns more about their sibling's records in Irminsul and is pointed towards Fontaine.

## placement
### STANDARD
Chapter III Act V, following King Deshret and the Three Magi and preceding Caribert and Inversion of Genesis. Released in version 3.2. The wiki lists Adventure Rank 35 as the unlock requirement.
