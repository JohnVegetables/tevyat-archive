## summary
### LITE
Chapter III Act I of the Archon Quest, the opening of the Sumeru story. The Traveler and Paimon cross into Sumeru's rainforest looking for the Dendro Archon, experience a strange vision of a great white tree, and are taken in by the Forest Rangers Tighnari and Collei. Their search for a way to meet the Archon leads them to Sumeru City and then to Port Ormos, where they join the Akademiya scholar Alhaitham in tracking a stolen Akademiya item.
### STANDARD +
The act has seven quests: A Forest of Change; Further Observation; Chronic Illness; Silent Seeker of Knowledge; The Trail of the God of Wisdom; Lost in Prosperity; and Ever So Close. It introduces the Withering, the illness of Irminsul and the Dendro Archon's link to it, Collei's illness, Sumeru's two Dendro Archons, the Akasha System and the Akademiya, and it sets up the Sabzeruz Festival that the next act develops. Released in version 3.0.
### LARGE +
The act is a slow, clue-gathering opening rather than a crisis. Each stop gives the Traveler a piece of Sumeru: the rainforest and its rangers, the scholars' reliance on the Akasha, the neglected cult of Lesser Lord Kusanali, and the extremist Eremites of the desert who dispute the old order. Its closing moment (a captured man repeating the words from the vision) links the Akademiya's secret item to Irminsul.

## plot
### LITE
After leaving The Chasm, the Traveler and Paimon enter the Sumeru rainforest to ask the Dendro Archon about their sibling. A scholar's incense sends the Traveler into a vision of a glowing white tree; Tighnari and Collei take them to Gandharva Ville. The pair learn of the Withering and of two Dendro Archons, one dead, one living in Sumeru City who nobody can show them how to meet. They follow a theft from the Akademiya to Port Ormos and help Alhaitham recover a Divine Knowledge Capsule from the Eremite leader Mizri, who goes berserk.
### STANDARD +
In outline: (1) in the rainforest the Traveler is knocked into a vision of Irminsul and wakes in Gandharva Ville; (2) Collei, who knows Amber, explains Sumeru's two Dendro Archons; (3) Collei's illness and a Withering Zone show what Irminsul's disease does, and Tighnari admits the vision was real; (4) in Haypasia's cave an Aranara's dream shakes the Traveler, and the Akademiya tries to recruit Tighnari; (5) in Sumeru City nobody can say how to meet Kusanali, but Dunyarzad, Dehya and Nilou lead to the Sabzeruz Festival and to a robbery of an Akademiya convoy; (6) at Port Ormos the pair meet Alhaitham and win Dori's trust; (7) they stop Mizri, who is carried off repeating the words of the vision.
### LARGE +
A Forest of Change: The Traveler and Paimon emerge from the tunnel at Cinnabar Cliff into the rainforest. They try to ask a passing scholar, Haypasia, for directions and are ignored; they follow her, she lights incense in a cave, and the Traveler faints. In a vision they see a huge glowing white tree under a red sky and hear a woman's voice murmur "World... Forget me...". They wake in Gandharva Ville, where the Forest Rangers Tighnari and Collei brought them. Tighnari calls the vision a hallucination, and the Traveler suspects he is not being truthful.

Further Observation: Tighnari lets the pair walk Collei's patrol route while he and the rangers take a harder one. Collei explains that Sumeru has had two Dendro Archons: Greater Lord Rukkhadevata, who died in the cataclysm, and the current Archon, Lesser Lord Kusanali, who was found afterwards and now lives in the Sanctuary of Surasthana in Sumeru City. Collei knows little else because she spent much of her life away. She recognised the Traveler at once: she is a close friend of Amber and lived in Mondstadt for a time, and Amber's letters told her about the Stormterror affair. She defends Tighnari when Paimon criticises how he talks to her. When Paimon touches her in a joking moment, Collei panics, tries to play it down, then flees to her bed.

Chronic Illness: Next morning the pair find Collei bedridden. She was born with Eleazar, a disease endemic to Sumeru that brings a slow, painful death; the Fatui Harbinger Dottore once suppressed her symptoms, but the experiments left her traumatised and averse to touch. They offer to gather ingredients for her medicine with Tighnari, but detour when a Withering Zone forms on a ranger route; only elemental-ability users can resist one, so Tighnari and the Traveler handle it. Tighnari explains that the Withering comes from a disease in Irminsul, the great tree whose roots are the ley lines and which absorbs the memories the lines gather. The Dendro Archon is called the God of Wisdom because her consciousness is linked to it directly. The Aranara, Rukkhadevata's familiars, told Tighnari's ancestors this but cannot cure it. Back in the village, a Dusk Bird brings a plea from Haypasia for food and water. Tighnari now trusts the Traveler with Sumeru's fate and says the incense had connected them to Irminsul, so the vision was a memory stored there. He sends them with emergency rations to Haypasia, whose training involves reaching Irminsul.

Silent Seeker of Knowledge: In Haypasia's cave the Traveler stumbles on an Aranara, which casts a dream on them. The dream voices their doubts about their journey and their sibling through a conjured Abyss Herald they cannot defeat. After waking they find the Aranara had been looking after the unconscious Haypasia, and they revive her with fruit and then cook for the three of them. Haypasia explains that reaching Irminsul is very difficult and that many Akademiya researchers have lost their minds touching its divine knowledge. It took her three years, and the same day she first saw the Aranara. She notes a contradiction: Aranara control dreams, yet adult Sumeru residents cannot dream, which the Sages say is Rukkhadevata's way of shielding people from the disorder of dreams. Back in Gandharva Ville, Sage Khajeh and aides try to recruit Tighnari to a secret Akademiya project; even the mention of his teacher, Sage Naphis, does not move him. Tighnari then sends the Traveler to an acquaintance, Rohawi, in Sumeru City.

The Trail of the God of Wisdom: At Sumeru City's entrance the pair receive Akasha Terminals under a new policy, but neither the Akasha, Rohawi, Katheryne nor the Corps of Thirty advisor Asfand can say how to meet the Archon. The Akademiya values Kusanali less than Rukkhadevata, so nobody questions her absence. Dunyarzad, a follower of Kusanali, approaches them; her bodyguard Dehya arrives to take her home but relents on seeing her resolve. Dunyarzad wants to revive the Sabzeruz Festival, an obscure celebration of Kusanali's birthday, as thanks for the Archon's help during a childhood illness. They meet Nilou, who will perform the festival's Dance of Sabzeruz, once danced by the Goddess of Flowers. Nilou longs for real Padisarahs, a purple flower that went extinct when the Goddess died; Rukkhadevata made her own version in the goddess's memory but never matched the colour. Katheryne, on a break at the bazaar, chats informally with them. Dehya then brings a lead from Asfand: an Akademiya convoy at Aaru Village was robbed, Grand Sage Azar is alarmed, and the item (classified, but tied to "the Akasha, knowledge, and even the gods") is somewhere in Port Ormos.

Lost in Prosperity: At Port Ormos the pair learn that extremist Eremite groups such as Ayn Al-Ahmar are spreading word that King Deshret, whom they believe Rukkhadevata betrayed, will return. They witness a dispute between Ayn Al-Ahmar and an Akademiya man, Alhaitham, who agrees to fight them in a few days. The Traveler persuades him to cooperate. He names the missing item as a Knowledge Capsule, used to obtain knowledge beyond the Akasha's limits, but will tell them more only after they pass a test: becoming customers of the merchant Dori, who refuses to deal with anyone from the Akademiya. Given a large sum and told not to haggle, they earn her trust and return.

Ever So Close: Alhaitham and the Traveler explain their interests to each other. The item is a Divine Knowledge Capsule, which Alhaitham wants to study to learn what "divine knowledge" means. With Dori's help they learn that Ayn Al-Ahmar's leader, Mizri, holds it, and plan to use the scheduled fight to get access. Mizri goes berserk after using it and turns on his own people; Alhaitham ends the fight by cutting Mizri's link to his Akasha Terminal, and the Matra take Mizri away. As he is led off, Mizri mutters "...World ...Forget me...". Afterwards each hides something: Alhaitham that he took the Capsule from Mizri, the Traveler that they heard those words in the vision. Alhaitham compares Mizri's state to researchers driven mad by Irminsul and concludes the Capsule gives neither divine knowledge nor power. He knows nothing about Kusanali either, so the Traveler and Paimon return to Sumeru City for the Sabzeruz Festival, and Alhaitham is left debating whether to deal with the Capsule or file a report for the Sages.
### ULTRA +
Structure: the first three quests are set in the rainforest and ground the act in the Forest Rangers and the Withering; the next two bring the Traveler to the Akademiya's world and Kusanali's neglected following; the last two form a self-contained investigation in Port Ormos. The Traveler's failure to find any official route to the Archon is itself the act's argument: the city that prizes knowledge has no answer about its own god.

Tone: Collei's reaction to touch and the Traveler's dream-induced doubts about the sibling are the two personal threads, played quietly against the political ones (the Akademiya's recruiting, Eremite rumours).

## revelations
### LITE
Sumeru has had two Dendro Archons, Greater Lord Rukkhadevata (dead) and Lesser Lord Kusanali (alive, in Sumeru City). A disease in Irminsul called the Withering harms the rainforest and worsens Collei's illness.
### STANDARD +
Irminsul absorbs the memories gathered by the ley lines, and the Dendro Archon is called the God of Wisdom because her mind is directly tied to it. Burning the right incense can connect a mind to Irminsul, so the Traveler's vision was a stored memory. Many Akademiya researchers have gone insane touching Irminsul's divine knowledge. Sumeru adults cannot dream, which the Sages say is Rukkhadevata's protection; the Aranara, meanwhile, can induce dreams. Extremist Eremites expect King Deshret's return. The Akademiya's stolen Divine Knowledge Capsule drove Mizri berserk, and Mizri repeated the words heard in the vision.
### LARGE +
Collei and Amber write to each other, and Collei knows of the Traveler through Amber. Dottore earlier suppressed Collei's Eleazar symptoms through experiments. The Akademiya is running a secret project it wants Tighnari for. Alhaitham privately holds the Capsule and is undecided whether to report it.

## characters
### LITE
The Traveler, Paimon, Tighnari, Collei and Alhaitham.
### STANDARD +
Haypasia, Rohawi, Sage Khajeh (and his subordinates), Dunyarzad, Dehya, Nilou, Katheryne, Asfand of the Corps of Thirty, Dori, and Mizri of Ayn Al-Ahmar. Sage Naphis, Grand Sage Azar, Greater Lord Rukkhadevata, Lesser Lord Kusanali and Dottore are mentioned; Aranara, Dusk Birds and the Matra also appear.

## consequences
### STANDARD
The Traveler and Paimon are accepted by the Forest Rangers and have learned of Irminsul and the Withering, but have not yet met the Dendro Archon. Mizri is in the Matra's custody, Alhaitham keeps the Capsule, and the pair head back to Sumeru City for the Sabzeruz Festival, which opens the next act.

## placement
### STANDARD
Chapter III Act I, following Requiem of the Echoing Depths (Chapter II Act IV) and preceding The Morn a Thousand Roses Brings. Adventure Rank 35 per the wiki. Released in version 3.0 (2022-08-24). The opening of Sumeru's story. Irminsul and Dottore, both introduced here, return in much later content, notably Truth Amongst the Pages of Purana (Song of the Welkin Moon Act X, Version 6.6), in which the corrupted Irminsul is burned and Dottore dies.

## entry: The Withering and Eleazar
@keys: Withering Zone, Eleazar
@keys2: Sumeru, Tighnari, Collei, Irminsul
@version: 3.0
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
A Withering Zone is an area of decay that appears across Sumeru's rainforest; only people with elemental abilities can resist it. Tighnari traces the Withering to a disease in Irminsul and notes that Zones are appearing faster and also worsen Eleazar symptoms. Eleazar is a Sumeru-endemic illness that kills slowly and painfully; Collei was born with it. Not even the Aranara know a cure for Irminsul.

### STANDARD
Gandharva Ville is the rangers' village in the rainforest. Tighnari leads patrols and shields Collei from the harsher routes; Collei, who is touch-averse because of past experiments, still shows warmth and humour. Her friendship with Amber of the Knights of Favonius begins the act's link between Sumeru and Mondstadt.
