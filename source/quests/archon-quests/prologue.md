## summary
### LITE
The Prologue, "The Outlander Who Caught the Wind", is the first Archon Quest chapter and is set in Mondstadt. The Traveler and their twin are stopped on their way between worlds by an unknown god, who seals the sibling away and strips the Traveler of power. The Traveler later wakes, meets Paimon, and goes to Mondstadt to seek the Anemo Archon, only to find the city threatened by the dragon Stormterror. With the bard Venti, who is the Archon Barbatos, the Traveler frees the dragon Dvalin from the Abyss Order's corruption, and Venti loses his Gnosis to the Fatui Harbinger Signora. The chapter ends by sending the Traveler to Liyue.
### STANDARD +
The chapter has three acts: The Outlander Who Caught the Wind (Act I), For a Tomorrow Without Tears (Act II) and Song of the Dragon and Freedom (Act III), released in Version 1.0. It doubles as a tutorial for movement and combat and introduces the Knights of Favonius, the Fatui and the Abyss Order.

## plot
### LITE
After a short opening in which an unknown god seals the Traveler's sibling and the Traveler's power, the Traveler wakes on a Mondstadt shore, rescues Paimon from the water and sets out to find the Anemo Archon. They meet Amber and the Knights of Favonius, learn that the dragon Stormterror is attacking the region, and trace its power to the Four Winds' temples. A bard named Venti helps them seek a cure for the dragon. Dvalin is eventually purified, but Signora tears out Venti's Gnosis, and the Traveler is directed on to Liyue.
### STANDARD
Opening and Act I. In the introductory quest Wanderer's Trail the twins, travelling from world to world, are confronted by an unknown god who calls herself the sustainer of heavenly principles and declares that "the arrogation of mankind ends now". The sibling is cubed and taken, and the Traveler is also sealed and left powerless. The Traveler awakes after an unstated time, and about two months before the story begins they save Paimon from drowning near Cape Oath. Paimon becomes their guide and suggests meeting the Seven to find the sibling. At Starfell Lake the Traveler activates an Anemo Statue of the Seven and sees a green dragon fly toward a forest. They find the dragon and a stranger trying to calm it, but the Traveler's power draws the dragon's attention, and the stranger leaves behind a crimson teardrop crystal. Amber, the Outrider of the Knights, offers to escort them to Mondstadt and gives them a Wind Glider. Stormterror, as locals call the dragon, then attacks, and the Traveler, helped unseen by the stranger, drives it off. Kaeya sends them to the Acting Grand Master, Jean, who asks them to deal with the Ley Line disruptions at the Four Winds' temples on Lisa's advice.

Act II. Back in the city, the Traveler is named an Honorary Knight after Jean rejects a Fatui diplomat who wants the dragon killed. Venti, who is the stranger from the forest, reveals that the dragon is Dvalin, one of the Four Winds, who is suffering and being exploited by the Abyss Order. Their plan to cure him needs the Holy Lyre der Himmel from the Favonius Cathedral. The Fatui seize it first; with the help of Diluc and Jean the party recovers it, repairs it with Dvalin's tears and summons the dragon at Starsnatch Cliff, but an Abyss Mage persuades him that Venti betrayed him, and the lyre is destroyed.

Act III. Diluc reports that an Abyss Mage has been killed at the winery and that its death released energy that was cutting Venti off from Dvalin. Venti breaks the barrier over Dvalin's hideout, the old Mondstadt of the tyrant Decarabian, and the party fights through hilichurls to a light actuator that Venti charges with Anemo power. The Traveler purges the corruption from Dvalin. Venti explains that he refused to order Dvalin to be free, since a command could not give freedom, and Dvalin leaves. Jean corrects the people's belief that the dragon appeared from nowhere.
### LARGE +
At the same time the Knights beat back a coordinated hilichurl assault on the city. Kaeya, who stayed behind because he suspected the attack, intercepted Abyss Order infiltrators and learned from them that the Order is united under a single leader. Amber tells the Traveler that they will always be welcome in Mondstadt. At the Cathedral the Traveler gives Barbara the broken lyre; Venti makes it look repaired by magic and flees. Outside, a gust of cold freezes Venti and Paimon, Fatui Agents restrain the Traveler, and Signora, accompanied by two Cicin Mages, taunts Venti and rips his Gnosis from his chest. The Traveler is knocked out. Barbara finds them, and Venti leaves abruptly. Jean and the Traveler find him at the tree at Windrise, where he explains what a Gnosis is and who Signora is: the eighth of the Eleven Fatui Harbingers, who serve the Tsaritsa of Snezhnaya. Paimon realises the Tsaritsa is one of the Seven they are seeking. Venti says the Seven usually did not get along but respected each other, and that a certain catastrophe five hundred years ago severed relations completely. He sends the Traveler to Liyue, where the Geo Archon descends once a year for the Rite of Descension, and warns them to hurry.
### ULTRA +
Each act is a sequence of quests. Act I runs from Bird's Eye View through Unexpected Power, Forest Rendezvous, Wind-Riding Knight, Going Upon the Breeze, City of Freedom, Dragon Storm and Knights of Favonius, followed by optional Winds of the Past (Amber), Crash Course (Kaeya) and Sparks Amongst the Pages (Lisa). Act II lists fifteen quests, from Shadow Over Mondstadt to Reunion With the Dragon, including three tear quests (Fallen, Hidden, Stolen) done in any order. Act III runs Abyss Mage, Hurdle, Empty Abode, Light Guiding Ceremony, Calm Before the Storm, A Long Shot, When the Wind Dies Down and Ending Note. Act I requires completion of Wanderer's Trail, Act II requires Adventure Rank 10 and Act III requires Adventure Rank 18. Wanderer's Trail itself does not appear in the Archive's Travel Log.

The game's first closed beta differed in several ways, according to the wiki: the cataclysm was dated 100 years ago rather than 500, the Fatui hardly appeared, and Signora's ambush did not occur. These are development history, not canon.

## revelations
@version: 1.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
- The Traveler and their twin came from other worlds and were separated and sealed by an unnamed god; the Traveler has lost their power.
- Venti, the wandering bard, is Barbatos, the Anemo Archon.
- Stormterror is Dvalin, one of the Four Winds, who protected Mondstadt and was corrupted by the Abyss Order.
- The Fatui, acting through one of the Eleven Harbingers, will take a god's Gnosis by force. Signora removes Venti's.
- The Tsaritsa, the Cryo Archon, is one of the Seven and has broken from them since a catastrophe 500 years ago.
### LARGE +
- The Abyss Order has a single leader, and the Traveler's sibling is shown overlooking Stormterror's Lair as Abyss Mages bow to them and speak of a homeland returning to the world (Act II). The Prologue explains none of it.
- The Prologue ends with the Traveler's goal set: meet the Seven, starting with the Geo Archon in Liyue.

## characters
### STANDARD
Traveler and Paimon: protagonists. Venti: the Anemo Archon in disguise as a bard. Dvalin: the afflicted dragon of the Four Winds. Knights of Favonius: Jean (Acting Grand Master), Kaeya (Cavalry Captain), Amber (Outrider), Lisa (Librarian). Diluc: winery owner and informal ally. Barbara: Church of Favonius deaconess. Signora: Fatui Harbinger, the chapter's villain. The Cryo Abyss Mage and the Abyss Order are the dragon's corrupters. The Traveler's sibling and the unknown god appear in cutscenes.
### LARGE +
Gotelinde, a sister at the Cathedral, refuses the lyre in Act II. A Fatui diplomat appears in Act II, and Fatui agents and Cicin Mages carry out the thefts and the ambush.

## consequences
### STANDARD
Dvalin is freed and reconciled with Mondstadt; the people learn the truth of the dragon's attacks. The Traveler becomes an Honorary Knight. Venti loses his Gnosis, and the Fatui now hold it. The Traveler leaves for Liyue to seek the Geo Archon.
### LARGE +
The Prologue establishes the Traveler's search, the role of the Fatui and Abyss Order as recurring forces, and the Gnosis as a god's power item, themes that carry through the later chapters. Chapter I continues in Liyue with Of the Land Amidst Monoliths.

## placement
### STANDARD
The first Archon Quest chapter, released in Version 1.0 and in story order before Chapter I. Its acts must be played in sequence: Act I, Act II, Act III.

After the Dragon is calmed, Signora ambushes Venti outside the Favonius Cathedral and rips out his Gnosis, then flees before the Knights arrive. Venti tells the Traveler and Jean what a Gnosis is and who Signora is, and sends the Traveler to Liyue.
