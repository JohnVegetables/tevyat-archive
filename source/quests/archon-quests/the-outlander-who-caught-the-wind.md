## summary
@spoiler: minor
### LITE
Prologue Act I is where the Traveler's story in Teyvat starts in earnest. Wandering Mondstadt with Paimon, the Traveler watches a green dragon, called Stormterror by the locals, terrorise the region, meets Amber, Kaeya, Jean and Lisa of the Knights of Favonius, and catches sight of a mysterious stranger who seems to be trying to calm the dragon.
### STANDARD
This is the first act of the Prologue chapter of the Archon Quest. After an unknown entity seals the twins' powers and separates them, the Traveler wakes in Teyvat, rescues Paimon from the sea near Cape Oath and decides to seek the Seven in the hope that one of them can help find the lost sibling. In Mondstadt's wilds they touch an Anemo Statue of The Seven, see the dragon Stormterror, are escorted by the Outrider Amber, and are taken to the Knights of Favonius, who ask them to tackle the disrupted Ley Line energy around the Four Winds' temples. The act doubles as the game's tutorial for movement and combat.
### LARGE +
Narratively the act sets up three threads that the next two acts pay off: the stranger who keeps appearing near the dragon, the red teardrop crystal the Traveler is left with, and the Knights' handling of the crisis under Jean, who leads as Acting Grand Master because, as Kaeya explains, the Grand Master is away leading an expedition.

## plot
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
**Opening.** As the Traveler and their twin are about to leave a world, an unknown being confronts them. In the fight it seals the sibling, then the Traveler, and the Traveler's abilities are locked away. The Traveler eventually wakes in Teyvat after an undisclosed length of time.

**Meeting Paimon.** Near Cape Oath the Traveler fishes Paimon out of the water. She becomes their companion and guide and suggests contacting the Seven to find the missing sibling.

**The dragon and the stranger.** At Starfell Lake the Traveler touches the Anemo statue and both see a green dragon flying toward a forest. In the forest they find the dragon and a stranger trying to calm it. Because of a reaction triggered by the Traveler's new powers the dragon notices them; the stranger flees, leaving a crimson teardrop crystal that the Traveler takes.

**Amber and the glider.** Outside the forest the scout Amber, Outrider of the Knights of Favonius, offers to escort them to Mondstadt after clearing a nearby hilichurl camp, which the Traveler and Paimon help with. Impressed, she gives them a Wind Glider.

**Stormterror attacks.** After a test flight the dragon, which the locals call Stormterror, rampages and raises storms. The Traveler, helped without knowing it by the stranger, drives it off.

**The Knights.** Kaeya, Cavalry Captain, notices the fight and sends the newcomers to Acting Grand Master Jean at the Knights' headquarters. Jean asks them to handle Ley Line disruption at three of the Four Winds' temples, as advised by Lisa, the librarian. After the temples are dealt with Paimon proposes returning to headquarters.
### LARGE +
The wiki lists the act's quests as Bird's Eye View, Unexpected Power, Forest Rendezvous, Wind-Riding Knight, Going Upon the Breeze, City of Freedom, Dragon Storm and Knights of Favonius, followed by three optional character quests that may be done in any order: Winds of the Past (Amber), Crash Course (Kaeya) and Sparks Amongst the Pages (Lisa). Together with the preceding Wanderer's Trail it forms the opening tutorial.
### ULTRA +
Extra detail: Wanderer's Trail is the act's formal prerequisite. In the Knights of Favonius quest Lisa explains that the dragon whips up its storms by drawing power from the abandoned Four Winds' Temples, and Jean sets the objective of dealing with three of the four, after which three optional quests with Amber, Kaeya and Lisa can be done in any order. The stranger's identity and the dragon's name are held back until the next act. The Chinese title translates as "The Foreigner Who Catches the Wind".

## revelations
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
- The Traveler and their twin travel between worlds and were intercepted and sealed by an unnamed entity before arriving in Teyvat.
- The Traveler's powers are sealed; Paimon is introduced as the one who guides them.
- The dragon terrorising Mondstadt is known locally as Stormterror and its attacks are powered by disrupted Ley Line energy.
- A stranger (later revealed as Venti, in the next act) tries to calm the dragon; the red crystal he leaves matters later.
### LARGE +
In this act the Traveler does not yet know that the dragon is Dvalin, one of the Four Winds. That identification and the stranger's identity belong to the next act (see quest.for-a-tomorrow-without-tears).

## characters
### STANDARD
Traveler and Paimon (protagonists), Amber (Outrider, the first Knight they meet), Kaeya (Cavalry Captain who notes the fight), Jean (Acting Grand Master), Lisa (Librarian, advises on the temples), Venti (the unnamed stranger seen near the dragon) and the dragon Stormterror, Dvalin. The sibling and the sealing entity appear only in the opening.

## consequences
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
The Traveler is accepted by the Knights as an ally in the dragon crisis and has a Wind Glider for travel. The act hands over the crystal and the open questions of the stranger and dragon to Act II.

## placement
### STANDARD
First act of the Prologue (release 1.0), directly followed by For a Tomorrow Without Tears and Song of the Dragon and Freedom. It is entered after the Wanderer's Trail prelude.

## entry: Stormterror (Act I view)
@keys: Stormterror
@version: 1.0
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
In Prologue Act I the green dragon is known to Mondstadt's people only as Stormterror, a rampaging storm-bringer. The Knights do not yet connect it to the Four Winds. It attacks after the Traveler tests the glider, and is driven off with help from the unnoticed stranger. Its true identity as Dvalin is explained in the following acts.
