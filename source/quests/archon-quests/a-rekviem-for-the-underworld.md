## summary
@spoiler: major
### LITE
A Rekviem for the Underworld is Act IV of Chapter VII, released in Version 7.1 as the last Archon Quest content at the canon cutoff. The Fatui plan their showdown with Ronova, the Ruler of Death: Arlecchino helps test a new Delusion, the Harbingers vote to open a forbidden notebook left by the Sinner Hroptatyr, and the Traveler joins Pierro and Dainsleif in holding Ronova in place while the Tsaritsa outmaneuvers the Sinner Rhinedottir. Ronova loses her authority, then sabotages a Celestial Nail as she flees.
### STANDARD
The act has three quests: Delusion, Etched in Time and Black Feathers Scattered at Parting. It follows White Night, Like a Dream Upon Waking and completing it unlocks the Anomaly world quest series. It is the final Archon Quest act available at the 7.1 cutoff, and the Snezhnaya chapter continues beyond it.
### LARGE +
The act is largely preparation and payoff. Delusion sets the technical side (a stronger Delusion, with Arlecchino's Crimson Moon heritage as the key); Etched in Time deals with the cost of knowledge (Pierro's past and the notebook); and Black Feathers Scattered at Parting stages the trap, with the Traveler as bait and anchor. The act also moves several relationships: Arlecchino commits the House of the Hearth to leaving Fatui affairs, the Harbingers meet together with the Traveler, and Pierro and Dainsleif cooperate against a shared enemy.
### ULTRA +
Details such as Danica's conversation with the nervous maid Tropina, or Sandrone and Arlecchino's tea party at Podvorye Restaurant, give a human scale to the build-up. The act ends on an open threat: a Nail of Retribution damaged by Ronova, with Holy Soil potentially loose over Teyvat.

## plot
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
**Delusion.** After the Northland Bank incident, the Traveler and Paimon visit Pierro in Glupov, where he and Arlecchino are meeting. Pierro explains that a new Delusion is needed for the clash with Ronova. Before the Four Great Rulers, the authorities of Life, Death, Time and Space belonged to the Three Moons, and the Crimson Moon held death; when the moon goddesses fell, the broken Crimson Moon merged with the Abyss. Pierro began the project when he learned Arlecchino descends from the Crimson Moon but shelved it because she was not at full strength. Arlecchino agrees to cooperate, and in return the House of the Hearth will leave Fatui affairs and train no more child spies. In a test that simulates abyssal corrosion, the output goes off the scale and the Traveler pulls Arlecchino out; researchers say the new Delusions also drew on another authority under the Crimson Moon, adding an unstable variable.

**Etched in Time.** Looking through Pierro's memories, the Traveler finds him with Hroptatyr, who gave Pierro a notebook before leaving to merge with the Abyss. When Pierro read it, he found forbidden knowledge that would have driven him mad, and had the Tsaritsa seal it. Because the Traveler and Nahida removed forbidden knowledge from Irminsul in Sumeru, the notebook may now be safe, so Pierro convenes the active Harbingers in the Armory Palace; opening it needs a unanimous vote. Tartaglia, Pulcinella, Sandrone, Pantalone, Arlecchino and Pierro attend. Everyone else agrees at once; Sandrone hesitates, then votes yes after being told it holds the last of Hroptatyr's humanity. Most of the pages are empty; the rest are Hroptatyr's own thoughts, including that he copied the Curse of Immortality and regretted it. An object in the pages throws the group into a prism where each converses with him. Pierro reveals that when he summoned Paimon from Celestia, Hroptatyr summoned the Traveler's sibling at the same time.

**Black Feathers Scattered at Parting.** In preparation for Rhinedottir's visit, the Traveler hears the Tsaritsa's mind described as too deep to show any weakness. Danica prepares the palace and calms a frightened maid. The Traveler and Dainsleif, who came at Pierro's request, wait at Rankova Zorya. Ronova arrives earlier than expected; Rhinedottir arrives at the palace and meets the Tsaritsa, who asks about the Heavenly Principles and speaks of Asmoday. As Ronova casts the Curse, the Traveler escapes it and stalls her; Pierro and Dainsleif join in holding her. In the palace the Tsaritsa has used the six Gnoses to manipulate time and space around Rhinedottir, who misses the window to revive the victims. Celestia seizes Ronova's authority for abuse, and Rhinedottir must correct her mistake one last time. Ronova, no longer constrained, fights and is overpowered by Pierro's new Delusion. Project Mistilteinn is unrestricted. Ronova, mortally wounded, teleports to a Nail of Retribution above the Cryo Dragon's corpse and fuses Eitr with it, causing it to crumble and risking a release of Holy Soil.
### LARGE +
Delusion adds: Pierro says Delusions are given only strictly and only in emergencies, and that Ronova's clash qualifies. The Traveler asks whether he knows of the Tsaritsa's meeting with Rhinedottir; he does not, noting that even he is not privy to everything. Pulcinella remarks that the Rokot are mysterious and asks the Traveler to pass his regards to the Korolevskiy Troupe.

Etched in Time adds: Hroptatyr had given Pierro the notebook as he left to merge with the Abyss in search of infinite knowledge, together with the humanity he was abandoning. Inside the prism, everyone talks with Hroptatyr in some way and Pierro learns more about the Delusion work. The Traveler asks whether he saw their sibling; he did not.

Black Feathers adds: Tropina is afraid of Rhinedottir's reputation, and Danica tells her all guests deserve equal respect. While talking, the Tsaritsa steers the conversation to Rhinedottir's circumstances, which Naberius dislikes. After the battle, Naberius urges Rhinedottir to punish the Tsaritsa, but Rhinedottir instead pays her respect and leaves her unharmed. The Tsaritsa commends the participants. Dainsleif, uncomfortable, says he could not refuse the Mother of Khaenri'ah, met Hroptatyr's remnants and was disappointed, and calls the victory over the Ruler of Death the start of a new but turbulent era.

## revelations
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
- The authorities of Life, Death, Time and Space originally belonged to the Three Moons; the Crimson Moon held death and its broken remains merged with the Abyss.
- Arlecchino is a descendant of the Crimson Moon, which is why a new Delusion could be built around her.
- Hroptatyr's notebook had held forbidden knowledge; he also copied the Curse of Immortality.
- Hroptatyr summoned the Traveler's sibling at the same time as Pierro summoned Paimon.
- Celestia can revoke a Ruler's authority for abuse, and did so to Ronova.
### LARGE +
- The Tsaritsa can use all six Gnoses she holds to warp time and space around a target.
- Ronova, stripped of authority, may kill freely, but was overpowered by Pierro's new Delusion.

## consequences
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Project Mistilteinn is no longer restricted; Ronova loses her authority and is badly wounded; the Fatui have a new Delusion; the House of the Hearth separates from Fatui affairs; and a Nail of Retribution is damaged. The Anomaly world quest series opens.
### LARGE +
Rhinedottir leaves the Tsaritsa unharmed. Dainsleif prepares to leave after granting the Mother of Khaenri'ah an audience and meeting Hroptatyr's remnants.
