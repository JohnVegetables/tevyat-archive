## summary
### LITE
Chapter I of the Archon Quest, set in Liyue. The Traveler arrives for the Rite of Descension, finds Rex Lapis apparently assassinated, is hunted by the Millelith, and works with the adepti, the Qixing and a suspect ally in the Fatui Harbinger Childe. The Liyue acts end with the revelation that the Wangsheng Funeral Parlor consultant Zhongli is Rex Lapis himself.
### STANDARD +
The chapter has three Liyue acts: Of the Land Amidst Monoliths, Farewell, Archaic Lord and A New Star Approaches. A fourth act, We Will Be Reunited, is the "Traveler chapter" and moves the search for the Traveler's sibling forward with Dainsleif and the Abyss Order. Dainsleif's preview framed the chapter as the God of Contracts being slain before his people and then signing "the contract to end all contracts".
### LARGE +
The chapter is a political and religious mystery. Questions of who rules Liyue without its god, whether mortals or adepti should lead, and what a "contract" means run through each act. The Fatui are both antagonists and, as the finale shows, part of a deal.

## plot
### LITE
Act I: Rex Lapis falls dead at the Rite of Descension; the Traveler is blamed and seeks the three adepti. Act II: the Traveler and Zhongli gather items for a funeral rite while Childe hunts the Exuvia. Act III: Osial is released and defeated, and Zhongli reveals himself as the Geo Archon. Act IV: the Traveler meets their sibling again.
### STANDARD +
**Act I, Of the Land Amidst Monoliths.** At Liyue Harbor, Ningguang calls Rex Lapis, who appears dead before the crowd. The Millelith try to arrest suspects, including the Traveler. Childe rescues them and, at Northland Bank, tells them to win the adepti's word before the Millelith do and gives a Sigil of Permission for protection. At Jueyun Karst they meet Moon Carver, then find Xiao at Wangshu Inn (after settling a haunting), Mountain Shaper on Mt. Hulao and Cloud Retainer at Mt. Aocang, each learning of Rex Lapis' death. Childe says the Qixing hide the body and will help find a contact.

**Act II, Farewell, Archaic Lord.** Childe introduces Zhongli, a consultant of the Wangsheng Funeral Parlor, who plans a proper farewell. He and the Traveler buy Noctilucous Jade, make perfumes, fetch the Cleansing Bell from Madame Ping, collect kites from Granny Shan and seek Everlasting Incense at Bubu Pharmacy (meeting Qiqi and Baizhu); a Guizhong Ballista is repaired during a cocogoat hunt for Qiqi. Ganyu brings an invitation from Ningguang to the Jade Chamber.

**Act III, A New Star Approaches.** See the full entry. The Traveler visits the Jade Chamber, fights Childe at the Golden House, and then stands with the Qixing and the adepti against Osial; Zhongli finally admits he is Rex Lapis and passes his Gnosis to Signora. The Rite of Parting follows, and the Traveler is told that Inazuma is sealed.

**Act IV, We Will Be Reunited.** After meeting Dainsleif, the Traveler takes an Adventurers' Guild commission from Ganyu to investigate Abyss Order activity in ruins, finding a Defiled Statue of Barbatos and a dead thief. Dainsleif explains Khaenri'ah and the Field Tillers, and a talisman shows the Abyss Order's plan to combine Osial's limbs with the first Field Tiller's eye. Visits to Mondstadt's cathedral and Wolvendom lead to the Field Tiller in Stormterror's Lair, where Dainsleif extracts and keeps its eye. When the Abyss Herald is cornered, the Traveler's sibling appears, stops Dainsleif and leaves with the Herald.
### LARGE +
Act I details: Paimon's slip about visiting The Seven alerts Childe, and the Traveler's food offering is what calms Cloud Retainer. Act II details: Zhongli repeatedly has no Mora to pay, the Traveler pays out of Childe's funds, and Qiqi's confusion between a cocogoat and coconut milk drives the sub-plot. Act III details: Keqing is the one who explains the ballista's recent repair. In Act IV the sibling names Dainsleif as the Twilight Sword, a royal guard of Khaenri'ah cursed with immortality.
### ULTRA +
Reading order: Act IV lists Bough Keeper: Dainsleif and The Meaning of Lupical (Lupus Minor) among its requirements. Act III was released in Version 1.1, after Acts I and II, and Act IV in Version 1.4.

## revelations
@version: 1.1
@spoiler: major
### LITE
Zhongli is the Geo Archon, Rex Lapis (Morax), and his "death" was staged.
### STANDARD +
He gave his Gnosis to Signora under a deal with the Tsaritsa. The Traveler's sibling is allied with the Abyss Order, and Dainsleif is the Twilight Sword of Khaenri'ah.
### LARGE +
The Abyss Order plans a mechanized god built from Osial's limbs, the Defiled Statue and a Field Tiller's eye. The sibling says the Traveler will see the truth of the world on their travels.

## characters
### LITE
Traveler and Paimon; Zhongli; Ningguang; Childe; Signora; Xiao; Ganyu; Keqing; Dainsleif; the Traveler's sibling.
### STANDARD +
The adepti (Moon Carver, Mountain Shaper, Cloud Retainer, Madame Ping); Hu Tao; Qiqi and Baizhu; Verr Goldet; and Osial.

## consequences
### STANDARD
Liyue enters an era without its Archon, governed by the Qixing. Zhongli loses his Gnosis. The Fatui gain it, and the Traveler is directed to Inazuma, setting up Chapter II.
### LARGE +
The sibling has been seen working for the Abyss Order, and Dainsleif's past is partly revealed.

## placement
### STANDARD
Chapter I follows the Prologue (Song of the Dragon and Freedom) and precedes Chapter II. Acts I-III are the Liyue chapter; Act IV, We Will Be Reunited, is the Traveler chapter.

## entry: Zhongli the consultant
@keys: Zhongli, Wangsheng consultant

### STANDARD
A consultant of the Wangsheng Funeral Parlor who guides the Traveler through preparations for Rex Lapis' farewell rite. He often lacks Mora, knows quality jade and old traditions, and in Act III turns out to be Rex Lapis.
