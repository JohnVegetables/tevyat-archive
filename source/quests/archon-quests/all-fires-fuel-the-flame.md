## summary
@spoiler: major
### LITE
All Fires Fuel the Flame is the Interlude Act of the Natlan chapter, a quiet aftermath story set after the Abyssal invasion. The Traveler and Paimon help Iansan treat three Flower-Feather Clan warriors who were broken by the fighting, and Capitano appears with medicine and a few grim admissions about Ronova.
### STANDARD +
The act has a single quest, "For the Same Land". It sits between The Rainbow Destined to Burn and Incandescent Ode of Resurrection, requires Adventure Rank 40, and is about recovery rather than battle: the cures for Abyssal Corrosion Syndrome, the unlikely help of Capitano, and the tribes gathering to forge the Traveler's Ancient Name.
### LARGE +
The act also shows tribes cooperating beyond their rivalries (Iansan of the Collective of Plenty working in another tribe's territory) and explains where the Natlan cure for Abyssal sickness came from: a military doctor of Capitano's platoon.

## plot
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
After the invasion, Paimon suggests visiting the hard-hit Flower-Feather Clan. Iansan and Collective of Plenty volunteers are already there. Chief Mutota explains that warriors Atoco, Cochitta and Ocotlan were found in the wilderness traumatised after meeting Gosoythoth, the Abyssal rift called the Reaper of the Abyss. A treatment existed hundreds of years ago but its knowledge has been lost, and the Traveler's attempt to purify the corruption does nothing.

The three are treated one at a time. Atoco is brought round when the Traveler takes the form of his dead saurian companion Duarte and then jolts him back to his senses, after which Iansan tells him to accept the loss. Cochitta is persuaded to keep fighting after her young brother Cuallee turns up, and she kills a few stray riftwolves to regain her confidence. Ocotlan is the severe case: he sees the tribe as monsters, attacks his father Munay, and is only cured when Capitano arrives, names the condition as Abyssal Corrosion Syndrome and supplies the Draught of Lucidity. Afterwards the Traveler is summoned to the Stadium of Sacred Flame, where Xilonen, with the other tribes' champions, begins work on the Traveler's Ancient Name.
### LARGE +
Detail: the Traveler and Iansan first move the afflicted to calmer settings under watch. Around Atoco's bonfire they learn the name Duarte; Izcalli says Duarte died four years earlier in an Abyssal attack, so the Traveler takes the form of a Qucusaurus. The ruse works until Atoco grows suspicious and the Traveler jolts him back to himself.

Mutota tells the Traveler that Cochitta's parents died fighting the Abyss, that she became a warrior after shielding her younger brother from a monster, and that the tribe celebrated her prowess until the invasion. Cuallee arrives with a toy weapon asking where the monster is. Mutota says some riftwolves fled north; a pack of three is found and Cochitta kills them with help. Ohtli and Itotia are heard praising the Traveler afterwards.

For Ocotlan, Capitano explains that mild cases can be cured by words or contact but a deeply entrenched hallucination needs the Draught, which leaves the patient with lasting migraines. Munay consents. Ocotlan recovers and says he had a vision of the tribe overrun. Capitano leaves extra medicine for other warriors.
### ULTRA +
At the Stadium, Mualani, Kinich, Ajaw, Kachina, Citlali and Ororon wait (Ajaw is flung aside by Kinich), and Chasca arrives, having heard that the Traveler helped her tribe. Xilonen is pleased at the turnout. The Traveler's deeds are to be told to the Pilgrim's Chronicle while the Traveler waits elsewhere; she also calls in Paimon, who is counted as a companion though not from Natlan. Paimon proves to have much to share. Xilonen returns the chronicle and says she will need it to forge the Ancient Name, after which the war may be ended.

## revelations
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Capitano says he fled Khaenri'ah to Natlan with his platoon, saw the tribes fighting the Abyss, respected their resolve and sees a common enemy. The medicine was developed by Guthred, his second-in-command and a military doctor, who tested it on himself after several soldiers tried to kill each other and died soon after finishing it. Capitano says his goals do not match the Traveler's, that the souls of the land are not at peace, and that his work is not finished.
### LARGE +
The Traveler asks about Mavuika's use of Ronova's power, which would mean she is marked for death. Capitano says Ronova caused his and his people's suffering, removes his mask and shows his true face, which shocks Paimon and the Traveler. He says the Ruler of Death can interpret the form of death or grant immortality as she wishes, and that before the final moment comes they must fight harder than ever.

## characters
### STANDARD
Traveler and Paimon; Iansan (Collective of Plenty, the act's guide and caregiver); Mutota (chief of the Flower-Feather Clan); Capitano; Xilonen and the gathered Natlan champions. Minor named figures are the warriors Atoco, Cochitta and Ocotlan, Cochitta's brother Cuallee, Munay, Izcalli, Ohtli, Itotia, and the late saurian Duarte.

## consequences
### STANDARD
Three warriors are restored, the Draught of Lucidity is available again with stock left for other fighters, and the Ancient Name project moves forward. Capitano emerges as an ally of convenience, not a friend. The next act, Incandescent Ode of Resurrection, follows.

## placement
### STANDARD
Story order and release order match: The Rainbow Destined to Burn (Act IV), then this Interlude (5.2), then Incandescent Ode of Resurrection (Act V). It is a Chapter V quest set in Natlan.

## entry: Draught of Lucidity
@keys: Draught of Lucidity
@version: 5.2
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
A strong medicine for severe Abyssal Corrosion Syndrome, developed by Guthred, Capitano's second-in-command and military doctor. Capitano says it is only for the worst cases because the patient suffers chronic migraines afterwards. Ocotlan of the Flower-Feather Clan is given it in "All Fires Fuel the Flame".

## entry: Atoco, Cochitta and Ocotlan
@keys: Atoco, Cochitta, Ocotlan
@keys2: Flower-Feather Clan, Gosoythoth, Mutota
@version: 5.2
### STANDARD
Three Flower-Feather Clan warriors found traumatised after Gosoythoth's appearance in the Abyssal invasion. Atoco grieves the saurian Duarte; Cochitta, a renowned fighter whose confidence collapsed in the invasion, is rallied by her younger brother Cuallee; Ocotlan sees the tribe as monsters and is the severe case. All three recover during the act.
