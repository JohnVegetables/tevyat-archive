## summary
### LITE
Chapter II Act IV of the Archon Quest. The Traveler and Paimon investigate odd hilichurl activity in The Chasm, find the Inverted City, and meet Dainsleif again. Together they discover that hilichurls are gathering there to live out their last days, and stop the Abyss Order's attempt to amplify a pool that eases the Khaenri'ahn curse.
### STANDARD +
The act has four quests: In the Depths, an Unexpected Reunion; The Grave of the Guarded; Memories of Inteyvat; and The Black Serpent Knights' Glory. In it the Traveler asks Dainsleif directly about his background and about his past with their sibling, and it introduces Halfdan, one of his former subordinates. Released in version 2.6.
### LARGE +
Coming after the sibling's appearance in Chapter I Act IV, the act shows the curse's effect on ordinary hilichurls and on cursed Black Serpent Knights, and states the Traveler's own position on the sibling's plan.

## plot
### LITE
Yuehui, a miner, asks the Traveler to look into hilichurls in The Chasm. In the underground mines they find an Inverted City, and Dainsleif joins them. They learn that the hilichurls are gathering near a pool that weakens the curse, guarded by Black Serpent Knights. The Abyss Order tries to amplify the pool; Halfdan gives his life to delay it and the Traveler destroys the device.
### STANDARD +
In the Depths, an Unexpected Reunion: Paimon and the Traveler accept Yuehui's commission, suspect the Abyss Order, and go through the underground mines to an Inverted City that reminds them of the Defiled Statue. Dainsleif then steps out of a portal behind them, having pursued an Abyss Herald through it and lost it. The same thing happened when he last chased the Traveler's sibling into a portal. The Traveler asks about his background and about his relationship to the sibling: he says "Twilight Sword" was his title as captain of Khaenri'ah's Royal Guard five hundred years ago, and that the sibling was his travelling companion in a search for their fates, though they "did not make it to the journey's end together". He changes the subject to the Order's likely plans, and they continue inward.

The Grave of the Guarded: Dainsleif suspects the hilichurls have come to The Chasm to live out their last days, because the place seems to ease the curse of immortality laid on Khaenri'ahns in the cataclysm, though he does not know how. Two Black Serpent Knights, once his subordinates who lost their minds to the curse, attack; a third Knight only stares at Dainsleif and retreats. At the Inverted City he judges it older than Khaenri'ah. After the party defeats more Knights they find the Knights were protecting a group of dying hilichurls. The earlier Knight halts reinforcements with a silent order. Dainsleif believes it is Halfdan, a royal guard elite whom he ordered to tell all Black Serpent Knights to protect the people of Khaenri'ah at all costs; despite the curse eroding their minds, the Knights are still trying to carry out that order. The group climbs to the summit and finds a water pool, a relic of the ancient civilisation and the source of the area's curse-weakening effect, surrounded by strange devices. Halfdan arrives and urges them to follow.

Memories of Inteyvat: Halfdan leads them to a hilichurl camp where the Traveler finds an Inteyvat, Khaenri'ah's national flower. Touching it shows a vision of the sibling and an Abyss Herald discussing amplifying the pool's properties, lifting the curse from the gathered hilichurls and restoring "the homeland". Dainsleif calls this futile given the curse's reality-altering nature and expects only more suffering for the hilichurls. The Traveler chooses to help him stop it, holding that the sibling has no right to subject others to near-certain suffering for the slimmest chance of redemption. As they leave, the Order starts its plan, an Abyss Herald delays them, and after it falls the group rushes back to the device.

The Black Serpent Knights' Glory: they enter the chamber as an Abyss Lector activates the amplification device, which causes agonising pain to everyone cursed. Halfdan sacrifices himself to hold the device back temporarily, giving the Traveler time to defeat the Lector and destroy the mechanisms. Afterwards Halfdan's soul appears, speaks briefly with Dainsleif and passes on. Dainsleif leaves to recover and to investigate the Order's Loom of Fate project; the Traveler and Paimon report to Yuehui.
### LARGE +
The act's emotional spine is Dainsleif's relationship to his former subordinates: the Knights have lost their minds but still serve his last order, and he is only recognised by one of them. The vision of the Inteyvat also gives the first sight of the sibling's stated aim in this period, centred on restoring "the homeland" by lifting the curse from the hilichurls.
### ULTRA +
Dainsleif says that the Knights' recognition of him ought to be impossible without "it", an unnamed thing he does not explain here. He suspects the Order has designs on The Chasm before he sees the amplification device, and he does not claim to understand how the pool works.

## revelations
### LITE
Black Serpent Knights are former Khaenri'ahn soldiers who still guard their people; the Order wants to remove the curse from hilichurls by amplifying an ancient pool.
### STANDARD +
Dainsleif's title "Twilight Sword" is that of captain of Khaenri'ah's Royal Guard five hundred years ago, and the sibling was his travelling companion in a quest to find their fates. Hilichurls appear to gather at The Chasm to live out their last days because the place weakens the curse. The Inverted City is of an older civilisation than Khaenri'ah. The Abyss Order intends to remove the curse from the gathered hilichurls and "restore the homeland".
### LARGE +
The Black Serpent Knights' loyalty traces back to Dainsleif's order to Halfdan to tell all Black Serpent Knights to protect Khaenri'ah's people at all costs. The Order is also pursuing a project called the Loom of Fate.

## characters
### LITE
The Traveler, Paimon, Dainsleif and Halfdan.
### STANDARD +
Yuehui, a miner who commissions the investigation; the Traveler's sibling (seen in a vision); an Abyss Herald; an Abyss Lector; and other Black Serpent Knights. The hilichurls of The Chasm appear as people at the end of their lives.

## consequences
### STANDARD
The amplification device is destroyed and Halfdan dies and passes on. Dainsleif goes to recover and to look into the Loom of Fate. The Traveler has stated openly that they oppose their sibling's plan.

## placement
### STANDARD
Chapter II Act IV, following Omnipresence Over Mortals and The Crane Returns on the Wind and preceding Through Mists of Smoke and Forests Dark (Chapter III Act I, Sumeru). Adventure Rank 30 per the wiki. Released in version 2.6.

In the depths of The Chasm's underground mines lies an Inverted City, which Dainsleif judges older than Khaenri'ah.
