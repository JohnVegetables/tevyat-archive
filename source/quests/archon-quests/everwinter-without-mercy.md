## summary
### LITE
Chapter VII Act I of the Archon Quest and the opening of the Snezhnaya story. The Traveler and Paimon cross into the frozen north, the supposed last stop on the road set by the Traveler's sibling. Paimon falls ill; the pair find a town short of fuel because of Project Stuzha, help an honest hunter expose a hoarding scheme, meet Harbingers Sandrone and Tartaglia, and are recruited into the secret group Rokot.
### STANDARD +
The act has four quests: Into the Wind, Into the Frigid North; Gunfire in the Silent Lands; Great Deeds on the Tundra; and Silence Alone is Disfavored. It moves from the wilds near the border to Sretomorozsk, then to Snezhnograd, its Veche and the Korolevskiy Theater. It introduces the Eye of Graeae, the Shadow Realm and the Exaiphanes Blade, and sets the nation's central problem: a state-wide cut in energy use called Project Stuzha. Released in version 7.0.
### LARGE +
The act is a deliberate slow start. Rather than meeting the Tsaritsa, the Traveler learns the country from the bottom up: what the energy ration means for a town, how the Fatui and the Veche argue over it, and who works against both from the shadows. The Tsaritsa herself has gone east with her Druzhna to slay the monster behind the snowstorms, so while Paimon's illness keeps her resting, the Traveler sets out to understand the plan through the Harbingers, the Veche and the underground. The next act is Wraith's Nocturne.

## plot
### LITE
The Traveler and Paimon leave for Snezhnaya after a farewell party hosted by Columbina. A gust separates them; the Traveler meets the Eye of Graeae, an ancient device, and finds Paimon asleep in a cabin where a stranger with star-shaped pupils tells them to look after her. In the town of Sretomorozsk the hunter Alyosha explains Project Stuzha and helps unmask a Kresnik Kristall thief. In Snezhnograd the Traveler meets Sandrone, Tartaglia and Odette, then is invited into the Rokot while investigating the project.
### STANDARD +
Across four quests the Traveler is separated from Paimon, meets the Eye of Graeae, and reaches Sretomorozsk, where Alyosha explains the Kresnik Kristall shortage caused by Project Stuzha and helps expose the officials hoarding fuel. The train journey onward is pulled into the Shadow Realm; later, in the Svetloledovka mine, the Traveler forms the Exaiphanes Blade. In Snezhnograd the Traveler meets Sandrone, Tartaglia and Odette, sees the Veche vote to continue Stuzha with 61 percent, learns from Mitya that the project needs a Stellar Linchpin energy source, and joins Rokot.
### LARGE +
Into the Wind, Into the Frigid North: before leaving, the Traveler and Paimon are met by Columbina, who learned of their plans through Luonnotar and throws a farewell party on the Flagship; she also passes on a message from Sandrone, already in Snezhnograd, not to cause her trouble. Crossing into Snezhnaya, Paimon feels unwell but blames the cold. A powerful gust throws the Traveler off a bridge and separates them. In a wrecked train the Traveler meets the Eye of Graeae, which recognises them as a Descender and offers to find Paimon in return for a later favour. With its "Fab-Vision" they reach a cabin where Paimon sleeps beside a man the Traveler recognises as Khaenri'ahn by his star-shaped pupils; he gives no name, tells them to concentrate on Paimon's health and leaves. Paimon later says he was Pierro, Director of the Eleven Fatui Harbingers, and that he seemed to know her. Graeae suggests using the train; its knowledge of the current rails is out of date and it plunges off a cliff, but lands harmlessly within walking distance of a town.

Gunfire in the Silent Lands: in Sretomorozsk all businesses are shut and residents barely cope. Alyosha, a hunter, explains that the Tsaritsa's Project Stuzha reduces energy use so it can be redirected to crucial research, and Kresnik Kristall shortages leave many stoves cold. He takes them to a distribution where Shishkin calms an angry crowd. Alyosha lets Paimon rest in his home, and the Traveler offers the crashed but working train to reach a doctor in Snezhnograd. Suspecting Shishkin of skimming the town's Kristall, the Traveler, Alyosha and his dog Tugarin show that deliveries keep arriving carriages short of what the train carries, and find a hidden tunnel stocked with Kristall. Shishkin is exposed and, once more Kristall turns up in the mayor's house, the Fatui representative Polonsky has both arrested; he hints that Alyosha could suit Rokot, an organisation at odds with the Fatui. Alyosha delivers fuel to an elderly woman with dementia who, like him, has ties to the abandoned town of Alatyr, then works out who the Traveler is. He tells them the Tsaritsa and her Druzhna have gone east to kill the monster causing the snowstorms. As the train leaves, strange objects appear on it and everyone is pulled into the Shadow Realm, a place without wind, light or elemental energy. Graeae gives the Traveler a new outfit and weapons there, they escape just before the bubble sinks, and rest at Rankova Zorya while Graeae upgrades the train.

Great Deeds on the Tundra: in Snezhnograd Alyosha briefs the Traveler on local fae and sends them to Podvorye Restaurant, where Luneva examines Paimon and diagnoses a cold. Wanting to learn the Tsaritsa's plans without a direct approach to a Harbinger, the Traveler goes to Glupov to find Tartaglia but is stopped by Yuzhanin until Sandrone, interrupted at tea, takes over and arranges to meet at Torchlight Books. Zverev reports trouble at Svetloledovka; the Traveler accompanies Sandrone, rescues trapped miners and meets Odette, escapes a collapsing cavern, and draws on an unusual power to form the Exaiphanes Blade. Back in the city they meet Tartaglia, who is overworked by Veche duties, and discuss Project Stuzha's flaws; Odette is dismayed to learn the Traveler once duelled Signora and leaves. The Traveler uses the blade's Cryo power to empower the city's Statue of the Seven, then buys kristall in Zolnyy with Alyosha; Odette arrives and covers the costs through Fatui logistics. At the restaurant Sytin asks for identification, and the Traveler shows an Adventurers' Handbook. They ask Alyosha for Rokot leads and are told to meet him at the Korolevskiy Theater.

Silence Alone is Disfavored: at the theatre Kulish inspects them. A silent guard from the Armory Palace Special Assignments Battalion, Valeriy, stands nearby; Alyosha says he usually appears only when hunting a researcher named Mitya. They sit beside the irritable Chugunov and watch Vodyanitsa, the troupe's lead singer. Backstage she and Alyosha reveal themselves as Rokot members and say the Traveler has been approved by the president; members are anonymous, rarely meet and work as they see fit within the group's principles. The Traveler joins. Their target is Chugunov, boss of the Iskra Manufacturing Plant, suspected of illegal excess energy use. At Podvorye Restaurant Vodyanitsa questions Mitya, a respected energy expert, while the Traveler and Alyosha listen nearby. Mitya, whose scarf seems alive, admits he was distracted by Odette's dance, confirms the plant rumours, says Pulcinella now demands tracking of every Kristall batch, and reveals that the Armory Palace is seeking a new energy source drawn from Stellar Linchpins, burning vast amounts of Kristall; his scarf chokes him before he can say what it is for, and he leaves before Valeriy arrives. Paimon, still weak, feels a mysterious power seeming to come from the direction of the Armory Palace. At the Snezhnograd Veche, with Vodyanitsa and the reporter Mironova, Pulcinella presents energy figures and declares Project Stuzha will continue. Tartaglia argues that remote Fatui families receive fifteen percent less Kristall than Snezhnograd civilians, Pantalone replies that fairness is relative, and Sandrone steers the debate back to data. Stuzha passes with 61 percent, a narrower margin than before, and Chugunov surprisingly votes against. Vodyanitsa suspects coercion by "Lelek", the Rokot's most notorious member, who is obsessed with ending Stuzha and uses threats, coercion or bribery to change votes. Tartaglia and Sandrone share what the Fatui know (a highly skilled infiltrator who leaves threatening letters, with investigators split between human and fae explanations), and Sandrone floats the theory that Odette is Lelek, citing her father's death in the early stages of Project Stuzha and her mother's career ending with a broken leg. Tartaglia gives the Traveler his ticket to Odette's rehearsal of the ballet "Spirit Seeker" so they can talk to her while escorting his sister Tonia.
### ULTRA +
Sequence matters here. The party at the Flagship closes the Nod-Krai chapter of the Traveler's journey; Paimon's loss of her usual vitality opens the Snezhnaya chapter. The Kristall plot in Sretomorozsk is solved by evidence, not force, and ends with Polonsky showing awareness of Rokot. The Shadow Realm is the act's first combat-heavy set piece; the blade later used on the Statue of the Seven is formed separately, in the Svetloledovka mine. In Snezhnograd the story trades action for politics: Veche testimony, committee numbers, a reporter's slanted account, and the Fatui's honest uncertainty about their own intruder. Vodyanitsa explains that elites normally support Stuzha because ending it would make factories absorb the Tsaritsa's unchanged energy demand.

Names to keep straight: the town is Sretomorozsk, the mine town Svetloledovka, the fortress district Glupov, and the trading area Zolnyy. Paimon's diagnosis from Luneva is only a cold at this point; her later account of a mysterious power is the in-story hint that something else is wrong. The Traveler is repeatedly treated as an outsider until they show a Handbook or are vouched for by a Harbinger, which is how most access is gained. The Harbingers' attitudes to the Veche differ: Tartaglia, newly made a councillor through Capitano, is worn out by the work, while Sandrone dislikes its politics but takes part to speak for the subordinates under her command. Pulcinella chairs it.

## revelations
### LITE
Snezhnaya is rationing energy under Project Stuzha, ordered by the Tsaritsa to free power for research. Pierro is in Snezhnaya and acts as though he knows Paimon. A covert network, Rokot, gives the disaffected false identities and an outlet, and is at odds with the Fatui.
### STANDARD +
The Eye of Graeae treats the Traveler as a Descender and promises help in return for a favour. Project Stuzha's research needs a new energy source from Stellar Linchpins and consumes huge amounts of Kresnik Kristall with little progress. Mitya is hunted by the Armory Palace's Special Assignments Battalion and is silenced when he starts to explain the purpose. The Veche vote on continuing Stuzha is narrowing (61 percent).
### LARGE +
Rokot's rules: anonymous members, rare contact, freedom of method within shared principles, and an optional code of conduct. Alyosha and Vodyanitsa both belong, and Polonsky already suspects Alyosha. Fatui in remote areas receive less Kristall than Snezhnograd civilians, according to Tartaglia. Within this act, Sandrone's theory that Odette is "Lelek" is only speculation; later Chapter VII content confirms Odette operates in the Rokot as Lelek.

## characters
### LITE
The Traveler and Paimon; the hunter Alyosha and his dog Tugarin; the Eye of Graeae; and Snezhnograd's Harbingers and Fatui: Sandrone, Tartaglia, Odette, Pierro.
### STANDARD +
Others include Columbina (farewell), Shishkin and Polonsky (Sretomorozsk), Luneva (doctor), Yuzhanin and Zverev (Glupov), Sytin (restaurant), Kulish and Valeriy (theatre security), Vodyanitsa and Chugunov, Mitya, Pulcinella and Pantalone (Veche), the reporter Mironova, and Tonia.

## consequences
### LITE
The Traveler is a member of Rokot, has a working train and a Cryo blade, and plans to learn the real purpose of Project Stuzha from Odette. Paimon remains too weak to travel.
### STANDARD +
Shishkin and the mayor are sent to Snezhnograd for judgement. Chugunov's plant faces an investigation expected to take a week. The Traveler makes a first connection with Mitya through Vodyanitsa. The Statue of the Seven in Snezhnograd is awakened. The story continues in Wraith's Nocturne.

## placement
### LITE
First act of Chapter VII, after the Nod-Krai content and the Song of the Welkin Moon story. Released in version 7.0.
### STANDARD +
The Archon Quest requires Adventure Rank 18 and completion of Prologue Act III. The wiki recommends Truth Amongst the Pages of Purana and the event quest Sunny Summer Fontinalia first. The Snezhnaya chapter was originally designated Act VI in the Teyvat chapter storyline preview, and became numbered Chapter VII after the Song of the Welkin Moon was added. Acts I and II arrived together in 7.0, and Acts III and IV in 7.1.

## entry: Eye of Graeae
@keys: Eye of Graeae, Graeae
@version: 7.0
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
An ancient device the Traveler finds in a wrecked train near the Snezhnaya border. It recognises the Traveler as a Descender and agrees to help find Paimon in return for later help. It guides the Traveler with "Fab-Vision", gives them a new outfit and weapons in the Shadow Realm, and upgrades their train. Its picture of present-day Snezhnaya is out of date, which is why its first route ends off a cliff.

## entry: Project Stuzha in Sretomorozsk
@keys: Project Stuzha, Kresnik Kristall
@keys2: Sretomorozsk, Snezhnograd Veche, Alyosha
@version: 7.0
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
A policy of the Tsaritsa to lower energy use across Snezhnaya and redirect it to crucial research. In Act I it causes Kresnik Kristall shortages, closed shops and cold stoves in towns such as Sretomorozsk. The Veche votes on continuing it; at this point it passes with 61 percent. Elites normally back it because stopping would make factories absorb unchanged demand. The goal of the research is not stated.

## entry: Rokot recruitment
@keys: Rokot
@keys2: Alyosha, Vodyanitsa, Traveler
@version: 7.0
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
A covert, anonymous organisation at odds with the Fatui that gives the disaffected false identities and an outlet for their frustrations. Alyosha and Vodyanitsa belong; the Traveler is accepted after Alyosha writes to the president on their behalf. Members rarely meet and may act as they wish within its principles. The Fatui's Polonsky knows of Rokot and says Alyosha is suited to it.

Strange objects appear on the train leaving Sretomorozsk and its passengers are drawn into the Shadow Realm, a space without wind, light or elemental energy. They escape before the bubble sinks back. Later, in the Svetloledovka mine, the Traveler draws on an unusual power and forms the Exaiphanes Blade, which they use to empower the Statue of the Seven in Snezhnograd.
