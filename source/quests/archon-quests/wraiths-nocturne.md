## summary
### LITE
Wraith's Nocturne is Act II of Chapter VII, the Snezhnaya Archon Quest. At a rehearsal of the ballet "Spirit Seeker" in the Korolevskiy Theater, a blizzard traps the audience, among them Odette, who is secretly the saboteur "Lelek", the Fatui Alyosha and the singer Vodyanitsa. Each night two people die without a mark, and the Traveler relives each death in a dream of climbing a spiral staircase. The cause is the Curse of Death of Ronova, Ruler of Death. In the end Pierro explains why Paimon was taken to the Armory Palace.
### STANDARD +
The act follows Everwinter Without Mercy and precedes White Night, Like a Dream Upon Waking. It was released in version 7.0 and requires Adventure Rank 18. It has five quests: The White Curtain Falls, Lelek's Confession, Winter's Mysterious Silence, Wraith's Nocturne (the quest) and Mistilteinn.

## plot
### LITE
The Traveler attends the theater hoping to find Tonia, Tartaglia's sister, and to ask about Lelek. A blizzard seals everyone in. Two people die each night, the Traveler dreams of each death beforehand, and gradually the dead turn out to be ghosts or fellow victims of the Curse of Death. Ronova reveals herself, is driven off, and everyone wakes with no memory. The Traveler then rescues Paimon from the Armory Palace, where Pierro explains the curse and Project Mistilteinn.
### STANDARD +
Trapped by the blizzard, the group finds two dead on the first morning and Odette admits she is Lelek. Further deaths follow each night; the cast discover that Shram and the girl called Tonia (really Ptashka) are ghosts, that Mitya has died without knowing it, and that Alyosha is Ronova in disguise. Ronova kills Odette and Chugunov, is struck by the Traveler and Mitya, and vanishes, and everyone wakes with no memory. In the Armory Palace, Pierro shields Paimon from Ronova and explains the Curse of Death and Project Mistilteinn.
### LARGE +
The White Curtain Falls. The Traveler arrives at the Korolevskiy Theater, where the usher Kulish checks their ticket, and recognises faces from earlier in the chapter: the merchant Chugunov, Mitya and Valeriy, the Armory Palace officer escorting him. They watch a rehearsal of the classic ballet "Spirit Seeker", during which the lights flicker once. Afterwards a staff member, Dymov, calls everyone onstage for a group photo and the Traveler finds Tonia, but a snowstorm bars the doors. Dymov goes out for blankets and does not return, and Alyosha goes out alone and comes back warning that the storm is too severe. Vodyanitsa and Odette emerge from backstage and agree the group will shelter there. That night the Traveler is woken by Mitya on a spiral staircase while a black mist pursues them and two merchants, Soboleva and Torgov. The mist engulfs the two merchants and the Traveler faints at the top.

Lelek's Confession. The Traveler wakes to find Soboleva and Torgov dead without wounds. Chugunov insists Lelek did it to frighten him, since he voted against Project Stuzha. Vodyanitsa doubts it fits Lelek's methods. The Traveler, who suspects Odette is Lelek, shares this privately with Vodyanitsa and Alyosha, and Odette, defended by Vodyanitsa, admits it and then publicly confesses onstage. The group sets Lelek aside to find the real killer. On the second night the singer Vodyanitsa unconsciously sings a nervous melody that the mercenary Shram understands, and Valeriy organises night watches. Odette tells the Traveler how she trained under Signora and why she joined the Fatui. The Traveler falls asleep and dreams the staircase again with Kulish and Rampana, who are taken by the mist, and Odette tells them this is only the beginning.

Winter's Mysterious Silence. Kulish and Rampana are found dead, as the dream foretold. Chugunov blames Odette again, and Mitya suggests the cause may be supernatural. Searching, the group finds Vodyanitsa's diary, a logbook showing one extra person who never had a ticket, and a report that the theater is haunted, with a note reading "Death is near". By elimination the extra person is Shram. His talk of fae and human "factions" proves he is a ghost from over four hundred years ago, who died protecting his water imp girlfriend in the theater. Vodyanitsa, who was a child then, tells him what became of her. He apologises for the harm he has caused and walks into the storm. Odette tells the Traveler that she thought she saw Mitya dead in the Armory Palace a week earlier. The third staircase dream ends with Valeriy staying behind.

Wraith's Nocturne. Valeriy is found dead at his post. Mitya notes that only one person died this time and proposes that the nightly toll matches the number of dead in the building. Odette says Mitya is himself dead, which he cannot remember, and he leaves into the storm. Tonia and Vodyanitsa comfort the Traveler, who misses Paimon, and Vodyanitsa sings a water imp requiem. In the next dream she acts as the Traveler's "Guardian Angel" against a spirit that looks like Tonia, and then stays behind. She is dead in the morning. The Traveler asks Tonia about Teucer and her confusion confirms a suspicion; Chugunov admits that Tonia is a ghost of a girl who had Frostblight, and that he saw his own late daughter Iskra in her. Tonia gives her real name, Ptashka, and leaves. That night Mitya returns, having followed Vodyanitsa's requiem back, with a red eye carved into his palm. The Traveler recognises it as Ronova's mark and the group deduces that "Alyosha" is her. Ronova drops the disguise, kills Odette and Chugunov with a snap of her fingers, explains the Curse of Death, says that the ley lines took as many souls each night as there were lingering dead, and that the ancient angels' modifications to the theater's ley lines hindered them from accepting ordinary souls and capped how many they could receive each day; she also says Teyvat's ley lines would not accept a Descender's soul. The Traveler and Mitya strike her and she vanishes, leaving a sky of watching eyes before everyone wakes in their seats.

Mistilteinn. Everyone has forgotten the dream, and Mitya no longer recognises the Traveler until he sees the carved eye and recalls that he must return to the Armory Palace. Valeriy's questions let the group recover their memories. The Traveler and Odette go to Podvorye Restaurant to check on Paimon and learn from Tartaglia's guards that Pierro came himself and took her away. They hurry to the Armory Palace; on the way the curse triggers again and Odette uses her Cor Ignis to wake the Traveler faster. Inside the palace everyone is dead again. Ronova appears in her true form and tries to take Paimon, Pierro blocks her, and the Traveler's attack only scratches her. Paimon wakes, hardly any time having passed for her. Pierro explains the curse and Project Mistilteinn, and the act closes with the aftermath in Snezhnograd.

Pierro and Mitya explain that Project Mistilteinn is a weapon against Celestia and that its energy had been making Paimon ill, so Mitya cancelled it with another form of energy. Pierro adds that over five hundred years ago in Khaenri'ah he used a technology upon the heavens and Paimon descended from Celestia in response; he offered her to Irmin as a gift, who took no interest, and he now sees her as the only sane thing that survived that era of insanity, and so worthy of protection. Mitya explains that the Curse of Death blocks Snezhnaya's technological progress: when a moment of breakthrough occurs, all life nearby is taken, and the revived forget what happened while dead; the theater incident was triggered by Mitya thinking of a breakthrough for his experiment during the performance. Pierro says the Tsaritsa and the Fatui aim to challenge the Heavenly Principles. The Traveler chooses to side with them to keep Paimon safe from Ronova, and Pierro shows the two the secret weapon. Project Mistilteinn is paused until Ronova is eliminated.

In the aftermath Chugunov orders the Iskra Manufacturing Plant shut down permanently. Odette visits Signora's tomb in the Sanctuary of Grief, where a vision of Signora and a conversation about the Cor Ignis, Arlecchino, and memory and forgetting lead her to dance for Signora and to keep going as both ballerina and Fatuus. In Snezhnograd the Traveler and Paimon meet Tartaglia, who apologises for Pierro's misunderstanding and introduces his sister Tonia. A crowd then cheers the Tsaritsa's return from fighting a monster, with the Druzhna, its commander Vesna and the head housemaid Danica around her. She returns without a word to Zapolyarny Palace, where Pierro and the others bow to her.
### ULTRA +
Deaths by night as the wiki summarises them: Soboleva and Torgov on the first, Kulish and Rampana on the second, Valeriy alone on the third, and Vodyanitsa on the fourth, with Shram, Ptashka and Mitya each leaving of their own accord. Mitya reads the falling toll as matching the number of dead already in the theater. Patch notes show that 7.0 had a Mitya Elemental Type icon display error in a cutscene (it should show the Electro Stellar Linchpin), fixed in 7.1, along with collision fixes and Travel Log text corrections; none affect story content.

## revelations
@version: 7.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
- Odette is Lelek, and publicly admits it in the theater; she trained under Signora.
- Ronova, the Ruler of Death, has been manipulating the theater through a Curse of Death, and her symbol is a red eye.
- Some of the people in the theater were not alive: Shram, a water imp's boyfriend from over four hundred years ago, and "Tonia", whose real name is Ptashka, a girl who had Frostblight. Mitya had also died earlier at the Armory Palace without remembering it.
- The Curse of Death is triggered by moments of technological progress that could threaten the Heavenly Principles, killing everyone nearby; the revived forget what happened.
- A Descender's soul cannot be carried into the ley lines.
- The Fatui, with the Tsaritsa, intend to challenge the Heavenly Principles.
### LARGE +
- Project Mistilteinn is an anti-Celestia weapon whose energy sickened Paimon, a being of Celestia.
- Pierro claims he caused Paimon, a creation of Celestia, to descend to Khaenri'ah over five hundred years ago and presented her to Irmin, who was uninterested. Paimon lacks any Celestia power or authority, according to Ronova.
- The Traveler chooses to side with Pierro to protect Paimon.

## characters
### STANDARD
Traveler: dreams each death, wakes after dying, and joins the Fatui's side to protect Paimon. Paimon: absent in the theater, missed by the Traveler, then the object of Ronova's and Pierro's interest. Odette: a ballerina and secret Lelek, trained under Signora. Alyosha: a Fatui whose appearance Ronova wears. Vodyanitsa: the singer and the Traveler's "Guardian Angel". Mitya: a researcher tied to Project Mistilteinn, dead without realising, whose carved eye proves decisive. "Tonia" / Ptashka: a ghost girl who answered to the name Tonia, believing her own brother had chosen it as a code name; she is not Tartaglia's sister. Valeriy: an Armory Palace officer escorting Mitya, who organises watches. Chugunov: a merchant who knew Tonia was a ghost. Ronova: the Ruler of Death. Pierro: arrives at the end. The Tsaritsa, Vesna and Danica appear in the closing scene.
### LARGE +
Other theater staff and guests who die or vanish include Kulish, Rampana, Soboleva, Torgov, Dymov and Shram. Tartaglia appears only in the epilogue.

## consequences
### STANDARD
The Curse of Death is understood and the Traveler has now met Ronova in person. Project Mistilteinn is paused until Ronova can be dealt with. Odette and Chugunov survive; Chugunov shuts down the Iskra Manufacturing Plant. The Traveler allies with Pierro, and the Tsaritsa returns to Zapolyarny Palace.
### LARGE +
Ronova states she has no immediate interest in Descenders but warns the Traveler against meddling in Snezhnaya's affairs. These threads lead into White Night, Like a Dream Upon Waking.

## placement
### STANDARD
Chapter VII Act II, after Everwinter Without Mercy (Act I) and before White Night, Like a Dream Upon Waking. Released in version 7.0 with Act I. Requires Adventure Rank 18.

## entry: The Korolevskiy Theater Incident
@keys: Spirit Seeker, Korolevskiy Theater incident, Korolevskiy Theater

@version: 7.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
During a "Spirit Seeker" rehearsal a blizzard trapped the audience, and each night two people died with no wounds. Some of the people present were ghosts. The Traveler experienced the deaths as spiral-staircase dreams chased by a black mist. It ended when Ronova revealed herself, was struck by the Traveler and Mitya, and everyone woke in their seats with no memory.

Ronova says the ley lines took as many souls each night as there were lingering dead, and that the angels' old alterations to the theater's ley lines limited how many souls they could receive each day. Mitya's carved red eye was the clue that kept him from forgetting.

## entry: Ptashka (Tonia)
@keys: Ptashka, Tonia, Teucer
@keys2: Korolevskiy Theater, Wraith's Nocturne, Chugunov
@version: 7.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
In the theater a girl who answered to the name Tonia was actually a ghost: a child ill with Frostblight whose brother Razyk had snuck her out of hospital to see a show, where she fell asleep. When the Traveler said her brother had sent them and called her Tonia, she assumed her brother had chosen it as a code name. Chugunov knew and treated her as his late daughter Iskra. She tells the Traveler her real name is Ptashka and leaves. The real Tonia, Tartaglia's sister, is introduced in the epilogue.

## entry: Curse of Death
@keys: Curse of Death
@keys2: Ronova, Mitya, Project Mistilteinn, Celestia
@version: 7.0
@spoiler: major
### LARGE
The curse is triggered by moments of technological progress that could threaten the Heavenly Principles; it kills everyone nearby temporarily, and they forget what happened on revival. Armory Palace personnel had suffered unexplained temporary memory loss, which Mitya mentions as a hint. In the theater it was triggered by Mitya's thoughts during the ballet. Ronova, Ruler of Death among the Four Shades, says she wanted to watch a play about death and was hampered by the angels' changes to the theater's ley lines.
