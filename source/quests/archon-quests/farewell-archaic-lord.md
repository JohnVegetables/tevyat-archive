## summary
### LITE
Chapter I Act II of the Archon Quest, set in Liyue Harbor. With Rex Lapis dead and his body hidden by the Qixing, Childe introduces the Traveler and Paimon to Zhongli, a consultant at the Wangsheng Funeral Parlor, who offers to get them to the Geo Archon's vessel if they help him prepare the Rite of Parting.
### STANDARD +
The act has seven quests: An Organization Known as Wangsheng, Moonfinger, Three Poignant Perfumes, The Realm Within, Downtown, Guizhong and Zhongli's Treat. It is a long errand sequence through the harbor and surrounding countryside, and it ends when Ganyu delivers Ningguang's invitation to the Jade Chamber. Released in version 1.0.
### LARGE +
Tonally it is light, with errands, misunderstandings and Zhongli's habit of leaving the Traveler to cover costs, set against a background of a murdered god. The recurring theme is Zhongli's insistence that the old rites be observed properly, in contrast to Childe's undisclosed agenda.

## plot
### LITE
At Liuli Pavilion Childe introduces Zhongli, who says he can bring the Traveler to the Exuvia if they help with the Rite of Parting. They buy jade, make perfumes, borrow a bell from Madame Ping, order kites and workers, and get Everlasting Incense from Bubu Pharmacy. The act ends with Ningguang's invitation to the Jade Chamber.
### STANDARD +
**An Organization Known as Wangsheng.** At Northland Bank, Ekaterina tells the Traveler that Childe has found someone to break the stalemate and is waiting at Liuli Pavilion. Childe presents Zhongli as a consultant at the Wangsheng Funeral Parlor and a trusted contact of the Fatui. Zhongli explains that the parlor organises burials, that the age of the adepti is ending, and that the Qixing have broken tradition by not holding a memorial for a deceased adeptus. He asks the Traveler to help prepare the Geo Archon's last rites, the Rite of Parting, and the Traveler agrees; only by taking part can they again see the god's form. Zhongli says the Fatui are merely financial sponsors, that he does not know and does not wish to know what Childe gains from it, and that his own concern is that Liyue's traditions endure.
### LARGE +
**Moonfinger.** The first item on Zhongli's list is Noctilucous Jade, paid for with mora from Childe. At the Jade Mystery the three inspect Shitou's samples. Zhongli tries to buy all three, but Paimon stops him, and he explains how to judge the jade by its Pyro affinity. Shitou chips thin samples which Zhongli says could be reinforced with Hydro. The trio use the Meaty Tribe's cooking pot in Dadaupa Gorge to test them (angering the tribe). The third sample is the best. When Zhongli explains the purpose, Shitou, saddened, sells it at half price, which the Traveler pays.

**Three Poignant Perfumes.** Zhongli, who treats the deicide as secondary to giving an archaic lord a proper farewell, sends them for silk flowers. Bolai gives them the flowers free once he learns the reason. After asking women around the city, the Traveler makes perfumes with help from Ying'er, and the three perfumes are offered at the Statue of The Seven outside the harbor.

**The Realm Within.** Zhongli needs the Cleansing Bell from Madame Ping but declines to go himself, claiming other business. At Yujing Terrace the pair find that Madame Ping's teapot is itself a domain, and that she is an adeptus; they recover the bell.

**Downtown.** Zhongli collects the kites he had commissioned from Granny Shan, one of the few who care for Liyue's old customs. Childe supplies mora to hire workers, and the Traveler negotiates with Tic, Tac and Toe. Childe then offers to let them keep the leftover mora in exchange for information, with access to the bank's vaults in return, and the Traveler reflexively refuses, which upsets him. Zhongli walks in unaware and names the next item: Everlasting Incense from Bubu Pharmacy.

**Guizhong.** At the pharmacy Qiqi asks for a prescription, and then offers the incense in return for hunting a cocogoat with the Guizhong Ballista on Mt. Tianheng. The ballista has been sabotaged by Treasure Hoarders who looted the area; the group repair it with parts from a nearby post and fight off the bandits, then discover there is no cocogoat, because Qiqi had actually meant coconut milk. Baizhu appears, thanks them for looking after Qiqi, and offers the incense for three million mora. Childe, amused, pays for it and sets up coconut milk supply for the pharmacy. Out of earshot, Ekaterina reports overhearing that the Qixing have taken over the Golden House (Paimon had let it slip), and Childe concludes that is where the Exuvia is hidden.

**Zhongli's Treat.** Zhongli invites the Traveler to dinner at Third-Round Knockout, a small tavern, where Iron Tongue Tian tells a story about Ningguang's Jade Chamber. Ganyu arrives, introduces herself as the Qixing's secretary at Yuehai Pavilion and Ningguang's special emissary, and passes on a letter inviting the Traveler to the Jade Chamber to "snip every one of these entwining dark threads". Zhongli tells them to go, calling it a rare honor, and to meet him at Dihua Marsh afterwards.
### ULTRA +
For most of the act Zhongli carries no mora of his own and relies on others to pay, a running joke that Childe's funds and the Traveler's generosity cover (he does bring mora for his own treat at Third-Round Knockout). The specific items on his list (jade, perfumes, kites, workers, bell, incense) are what the Rite of Parting requires, and Shitou and Bolai lower or waive their price once they learn what the purchase is for.

## revelations
@version: 1.0
@spoiler: major
### LITE
The Qixing have hidden the Geo Archon's vessel, the Exuvia; Childe deduces that it is in the Golden House.
### STANDARD +
Zhongli is introduced as a funeral-parlor consultant who says the Geo Archon is also an adeptus; Madame Ping is an adeptus who keeps a domain inside her teapot; Ningguang, in her letter, speaks of cutting the "entwining dark threads" together with the Traveler.
### LARGE +
The act does not explain why Zhongli avoids going to Madame Ping himself, or why he knows the rites so well; it leaves both unexplained.
### ULTRA +
Madame Ping's teapot domain shows the Traveler another adeptus living quietly near the harbor, after those met in Jueyun Karst and at Wangshu Inn.

## characters
### LITE
Traveler and Paimon; Zhongli; Childe (Tartaglia); Ekaterina; Ganyu.
### STANDARD +
Also Hongru (Liuli Pavilion), Shitou, Bolai, Ying'er, Madame Ping, Granny Shan, Tic, Tac and Toe, Qiqi, Baizhu, Iron Tongue Tian, and Ningguang (appearing only through Ganyu's letter and the story).

## consequences
### STANDARD
Everything needed for the Rite of Parting is gathered except the final pieces. The Traveler now has an invitation to the Jade Chamber and a rendezvous with Zhongli at Dihua Marsh, leading into Act III, A New Star Approaches.
### LARGE +
Childe, from what Ekaterina overheard, believes the Exuvia is in the Golden House, and he has been put out by the Traveler's refusal to inform for him.
### ULTRA +
The Traveler's first impulse to refuse Childe's request is the act's one note of friction between them.

## placement
### STANDARD
Chapter I Act II, after Of the Land Amidst Monoliths and before Act III, A New Star Approaches. Released in version 1.0.

### STANDARD
Zhongli presents himself as a Wangsheng consultant, says the parlor arranges burials so the dead pass in peace, and takes on the Rite of Parting to give Rex Lapis a proper farewell. Childe introduces Zhongli as a trusted associate of the Fatui and jokes that the parlor does not dabble in killing, "well, ostensibly".

@keys: Exuvia

### STANDARD
The Geo Archon's vessel, which Zhongli says is traditionally called the Exuvia. Childe says the Tianquan Ningguang has forbidden anyone from accessing it; later, when Ekaterina reports overhearing that the Qixing have taken over the Golden House, Childe concludes the Exuvia is kept there.
