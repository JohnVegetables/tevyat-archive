## summary
@spoiler: major
### LITE
The Rainbow Destined to Burn is Act IV of the Natlan Archon Quest (Chapter V). Il Capitano's Fatui agree to fight the Abyss alongside Natlan, the Traveler helps shut down a dangerous device in a Fatui base, and when the Abyss launches an all-out invasion the sixth hero of Mavuika's plan is finally found. The Abyss is driven back into the Night Kingdom, but the victory costs lives and leaves Mavuika's own fate in doubt.
### STANDARD
The act has six quests: Beneath the Secret Source, As One We Watch the Setting Sun, The Surging Darkness, Despair Engulfs the Heavens, No One Fights Alone and A Fuel Named "Fate". It follows Act III, Beyond the Smoke and Mirrors, and precedes Act V, All Fires Fuel the Flame. It requires Adventure Rank 40. It is the point where Natlan's war with the Abyss becomes open, nation-wide fighting, and where the six Ancient Name bearers of Mavuika's plan are finally assembled.
### LARGE +
Thematically the act sets two answers to hopeless odds against each other: Capitano's insistence on whatever maximises survival, even at the price of using a Gnosis-like source of power, and Mavuika's refusal to give her people a victory that would make their lives meaningless. The act also explains Natlan's Ley Line problem, the nature of the Lord of the Night and Capitano's backstory in Natlan.

## plot
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Mavuika, Xilonen, Kinich and the Traveler raid a Fatui base and shut down the Source Mechanism that Capitano and Ororon activated; Ororon's Ancient Name appears and the Lord of the Night explains Natlan's Ley Lines. Mavuika refuses her offer to rebuild them at the cost of her life, and Capitano agrees to join the fight. The Fatui are welcomed into the alliance and the Lord of the Night makes the Pilgrim's Chronicle for the Traveler. The Abyss then launches a nation-wide invasion; Chuychu dies and Chasca's Ancient Name appears, completing the six heroes, and Mavuika leads the six in a ritual declaring a Night Warden War. The defenders then counter-attack, and Mavuika destroys the rift and drives the Abyss into the Night Kingdom, then plans to go there alone while Citlali warns that she is still fated to die.
### LARGE +
Quest by quest.

**Beneath the Secret Source.** In the Speaker's Chamber Mavuika reports that Capitano has been located. Citlali tells her what she learned from Ororon's memories: his plan, with Capitano, to activate a Source Mechanism is as dangerous as using a Gnosis, and must be stopped. Mavuika, Xilonen, Kinich with Ajaw, and the Traveler plan an attack: Kinich and Ajaw draw off the Fatui in a frontal assault while Xilonen leads the others through a tunnel to the device. Ororon starts the Source Mechanism and is suddenly overtaken by an unknown presence which Capitano subdues, while the Traveler turns the device off. Ororon, in his own memory space, resists a Khaenri'ahn soul named Guthred and his Ancient Name appears, revealing the hero Sanhaj as the fifth bearer. The Lord of the Night, Yohualtecuhtin, speaks from the cavern, explains the Source Mechanism and Natlan's Ley Lines, and offers to rebuild them at the cost of her existence. Mavuika refuses and Capitano reluctantly agrees to cooperate, lending his troops and aid.

**As One We Watch the Setting Sun.** Mavuika welcomes the Fatui into the alliance. Capitano explains how he found the Source Mechanism and why he knew of the Gnosis' power, and Mavuika says her opinion has not changed. Xilonen and Citlali visit the Lord of the Night about giving the Traveler an Ancient Name, which will cost the Name Engraver her life, and about Mavuika's foreseen death; the Lord creates the Pilgrim's Chronicle, a parchment recording the Traveler's deeds in Natlan. The Abyss attacks again.

**The Surging Darkness.** The Abyss, sensing the change in Ororon, intensifies attacks across Natlan. Mavuika sends the heroes to their tribes and the Traveler to handle matters nation-wide by hot air balloon. At a checkpoint near the stadium, a surge around the Sacred Flame drops pylons that release Abyssal hilichurls and riftwolves, and Mavuika briefly subdues the rift with the Gnosis.

**Despair Engulfs the Heavens.** The invasion begins to overwhelm Natlan. The Traveler sees the dead of both humans and Saurians, helps defend a camp in the Sulfurous Veins and investigates a tumour-like object that has fallen from the sky. Tumours fall across the nation, the evacuation of the Children of Echoes is helped, and the Traveler finds Chasca and Chuychu. Chuychu, contaminated by Abyssal energy, wakes after the Traveler purifies it but dies of her wounds after saying goodbye. In her grief Chasca's Ancient Name appears, bearer Menilek, making her the sixth hero. With all six heroes (Mualani, Kinich, Xilonen, Iansan, Ororon and Chasca) gathered at the heart of the stadium, Mavuika, invoking her name Haborym, declares a Night Warden War, extending the Ode of Resurrection to all Natlanese.

**No One Fights Alone.** The heroes destroy the tumours and defend the stadium from attempts to extinguish the Sacred Flame, and Mavuika destroys the rift. Her blast briefly exposes a "false sky", which no one pays attention to.

**A Fuel Named "Fate".** At a feast at the Weary Inn, Mavuika says the Abyss has been forced deep into the Night Kingdom and that she will go there alone to finish it. She tells the Traveler she regrets not saving Chuychu. Citlali warns the Traveler that using Ronova's power still means Mavuika is fated to die. Mavuika explains that the Traveler is the only known person resistant enough to the Abyss to accompany her, but only if their Ancient Name is complete; Paimon would have to stay behind. Elsewhere Capitano speaks to the Lord of the Night, aware that Mavuika's use of Ronova's power will cost her life, and says he has his own final foe to face.
Further details. In Beneath the Secret Source, Kinich and Xilonen advise against fighting Capitano again because Mavuika has lost the power to fuel the Sacred Flame; she is determined to destroy the Source Mechanism regardless. The entity inside Ororon addresses Capitano as "Commander" and is upset that he still cares about others even when it costs him. In his memories, Guthred insults Ororon and plans to take his body once he dies; Ororon, who feels he has never done anything meaningful, refuses, and his Ancient Name "Bidii" (devotion) appears. Mavuika commends him for enduring pain with a damaged soul.

The Lord of the Night says the Source Mechanism is the source of Natlan's old Ley Lines. When the Descender and the dragons fought, Natlan's Ley Lines were badly damaged; to stabilise them she rebuilt a similar network from the old fragments, the Night Kingdom. The dragons tried to strengthen the lines by pouring power into the device but could not navigate their intricate structures. She says that she was once worshipped as a "lord", that before her kind died out they were known as Angels, and that they are better known in their devolved form as Seelie. Capitano, who says he is a survivor of Khaenri'ah who personally witnessed the horrors of the Abyss, argues that false hope should be ignored; Mavuika argues that a future in which the Abyss returns is worse for later generations. Ororon, his life rekindled, says he wants a life with meaning, Xilonen agrees, and Capitano concedes, giving his subordinates and direct assistance.

In As One We Watch the Setting Sun, Capitano says that he tracked three scholars, Aberewaa, Bosomtwe and Cuxtal, and combined their research to find the Source Mechanism, because the Fatui were not getting the Gnosis. After Khaenri'ah became unsalvageable he fled with his platoon to Natlan, which was suffering a similar fate, and met the chief of the Masters of Night-Wind, Ayizu, who treated him with respect though many blamed Khaenri'ah. He fought beside Ayizu, who on his deathbed told him the secret of the Gnosis. Mavuika does not recognise him and assumes the mask is why; Capitano says his flesh still rotted despite his curse. He knows Dainsleif only by name and last saw him travelling with the Traveler's sibling, and so did not want to harm the Traveler. He admits he meant to use the Gnosis to save Natlan and then, if it survived, give it to the Tsaritsa. Mavuika explains that when all six heroes unite they will use a power Xbalanque gained from Ronova, a god older than the Archons with power over death. She expects the Abyss to attack harder.

In the Lord of the Night's exchange with Citlali, she distinguishes the fate of someone's death from death itself, and says the Traveler can only affect whether Mavuika dies soon or not. She adds that humanity's creator looked on humans favourably. Citlali says she hopes to find a way to keep the Lord of the Night alive after the war, which the Lord thanks her for but says cannot be done.

In Despair Engulfs the Heavens, Chimalli declares a Sulfurous Veins base lost and lets his soldiers retreat or support the Children of Echoes; they choose to fight. Maztla's group believes the tumours came from a "sky-demon" above, and Pacal orders a full evacuation of the Children of Echoes. Kinich reports that tumours have fallen across the nation and that the Flower-Feather Clan is hit hardest. The Scions of the Canopy are seen overrun during the Traveler's balloon journey.

### ULTRA +
Details of the end of the act. Mavuika promises Chuychu a worthy funeral, and the fallen are given a moment of silence at Xilonen's suggestion. Mavuika tells the Traveler she would go alone rather than risk the Traveler lacking the Ode of Resurrection, but would welcome a true Ancient Name bearer at her side. Capitano's last scene hints that the Ruler of Death also sent him on a long journey.

## revelations
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
- The Source Mechanism is the origin of Natlan's Ley Lines; the Night Kingdom was built by the Lord of the Night from fragments of the old network after the war between the Descender and the dragons.
- The Lord of the Night, Yohualtecuhtin, belongs to an extinct kind once called Angels; Seelie are their devolved descendants.
- Ororon's Ancient Name is Bidii ("devotion"), tied to the hero Sanhaj; Chasca's is tied to Menilek. Together they complete the six heroes of the plan.
- Capitano is a survivor of Khaenri'ah who fled to Natlan and fought alongside the Masters of Night-Wind chief Ayizu, who told him the secret of the Gnosis.
- Mavuika's plan relies on a power gained by Xbalanque from Ronova, a god with power over death, and Citlali believes this means Mavuika is still fated to die.
- The Name Engraver's life is consumed in forging an Ancient Name.
### LARGE +
- A "false sky" is briefly exposed when Mavuika destroys the rift, unremarked by the characters at the time.
- Another soul tried to use his damaged soul as refuge; the Lord of the Night does not know its origin.

## characters
### LARGE
Mavuika, Il Capitano, Ororon, Citlali, Xilonen, Kinich and Ajaw, Chasca and Chuychu, Kachina, Iansan, Pacal, the Lord of the Night, Paimon and the Traveler. Mualani is seen with Kachina tending to people after the alliance meeting, and both Mualani and Iansan take part in the six-hero ritual. Chimalli and Maztla appear as minor soldiers and villagers, and the figures Guthred, Sanhaj and Menilek appear through Ancient Name or memory sequences.

## consequences
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The Fatui and Natlan form a joint front against the Abyss, with Capitano lending troops. The invasion is broken, the Abyss is driven into the depths of the Night Kingdom and Chuychu is dead. Mavuika decides to enter the Night Kingdom alone, and the Traveler is asked to help her if their Ancient Name can be completed. The next act, All Fires Fuel the Flame, continues from here.
### LARGE +
The act's cost is borne by named people: the dead among the Saurian and human defenders, and Chuychu, whose death gives Chasca her Ancient Name. The Pilgrim's Chronicle keeps a record of the Traveler's deeds in Natlan.

## entry: Source Mechanism and the Night Kingdom
@keys: Source Mechanism
@keys2: Lord of the Night, Night Kingdom, Ley Lines
@version: 5.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The Source Mechanism is a device found in a Fatui-held cavern, the source of Natlan's original Ley Lines. Ororon activated it for Capitano's plan. The Lord of the Night, Yohualtecuhtin, says it could let her rebuild the Ley Lines at the price of her own existence, and Mavuika refuses to use it.

## entry: Chuychu's death
@keys: Chuychu
@keys2: Chasca, Abyss, tumour
@version: 5.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
In Despair Engulfs the Heavens Chuychu is found contaminated by Abyssal energy. The Traveler purifies it, but she dies of her wounds after saying goodbye to Chasca. Chasca's grief triggers her Ancient Name, making her the sixth hero.

## entry: Capitano's past in Natlan
@keys: Capitano, Il Capitano
@keys2: Ayizu, Khaenri'ah, Gnosis, Fatui
@version: 5.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Capitano says he is a survivor of Khaenri'ah who fled to Natlan with his platoon, was treated respectfully by Masters of Night-Wind chief Ayizu, and fought alongside him. Ayizu told him the secret of the Gnosis on his deathbed. Capitano wanted to use it to save Natlan and then give it to the Tsaritsa.
