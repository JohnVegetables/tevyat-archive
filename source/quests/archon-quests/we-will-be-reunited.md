## summary
### LITE
Chapter I Act IV of the Archon Quest. After first travelling with Dainsleif, the Traveler and Paimon follow the Abyss Order's plan to build a mechanical god from a Defiled Statue, hear the outline of Khaenri'ah's fall, and end the act facing their own sibling, who leaves with the Order.
### STANDARD +
The act has four quests: Involuntary Sacrifice, A Herald Without Adherents, Dishonorable Trial and A Soul Set Apart. It moves from Abyss Order ruins in Liyue and Order camps around Jueyun Karst to Mondstadt (the Favonius Cathedral and Wolvendom) and then Stormterror's Lair. It is where Dainsleif first speaks to the Traveler of Khaenri'ah and where the Traveler meets their sibling again. Released in version 1.4.
### LARGE +
The act closes Chapter I's Liyue-and-Mondstadt investigations: it ties the Abyss Order's activity in both nations to one scheme, gives Dainsleif a title and a past, and sets up the Traveler's decision to continue to Inazuma. Its next act is Autumn Winds, Scarlet Leaves.

## plot
### LITE
The Traveler and Paimon take an Adventurers' Guild commission about a Treasure Hoarder raid on Abyss Order ruins in Liyue. Inside they find a Defiled Statue of Barbatos hung upside down. With Dainsleif they work out that the Order wants to build a mechanised god and needs a Field Tiller's eye. They find the Field Tiller in Stormterror's Lair and Dainsleif takes its eye, but back at the ruins the Traveler's sibling intervenes and leaves with the Abyss Herald.
### STANDARD +
Involuntary Sacrifice: at the Liyue Adventurers' Guild, where they find Ganyu and Lan, Ganyu explains that Treasure Hoarders plan a heist on the Abyss Order and have posted a commission because the Qixing are too busy; the Traveler accepts. In the ruins they find a Defiled Statue of Barbatos hanging inverted, the dead Grand Thief of the Treasure Hoarders, and a tremor that sends them fleeing. An Abyss Herald stops them near the exit and, after a short fight, teleports away.

A Herald Without Adherents: outside, Dainsleif is surprised to see them. He has never heard of an inverted statue but, from the Traveler's description, ties it to the Abyss Order. Tracking Order groups around Jueyun Karst, he speaks of Khaenri'ah, which brings back part of the Traveler's memory of the fallen nation. A talisman he translates reveals the first phase of the Order's plan: attach the limbs of Osial, whom the Traveler fought alongside the Qixing and the adepti, to the Defiled Statue, and use the eye of the first Field Tiller in place of the orb a Statue of the Seven normally holds, to construct a mechanised god.

Dishonorable Trial: the Traveler goes to the Favonius Cathedral in Mondstadt while Dainsleif waits outside, having asked them not to mention the missing statue. Barbara confirms that one of the Cathedral's statues is missing and tells of the "Tyrant's Final Fury", missiles that struck people approaching Old Mondstadt for about a year before stopping. Rosaria reports that the Abyss Order is attacking Wolvendom. There the Traveler interrupts the Herald as it again subjects Boreas to a corrupting ritual, and the Herald flees. Boreas and Razor thank them, and Boreas recalls that a strange machine once entered his territory, which he fought and damaged before it fled.

A Soul Set Apart: combining Boreas' and Barbara's accounts, they conclude the Field Tiller is the machine behind the Tyrant's Final Fury, and Paimon realises it is the Weird Statue in Stormterror's Lair. Dainsleif extracts its eye and takes custody of it, and suggests destroying the Defiled Statue so the Order cannot restart the plan. Back at the ruins the Herald attacks with Abyss Mages; Dainsleif pins the Herald but is stopped by a sword shockwave. The interrupter is the Traveler's sibling.
### LARGE +
The sibling asks why the Traveler is with Dainsleif, and Dainsleif knows the sibling too. The sibling calls him the Twilight Sword, a royal guard of Khaenri'ah who failed to protect it and was cursed with immortality, forced to watch his people turn into monsters. The Traveler asks them to come home; the sibling refuses, saying they must fulfil the Order's goal or their war with destiny will not end, and that the Traveler will see the truth about the world on their own travels, as the sibling already has. The Herald opens a portal and the sibling leaves with it. Dainsleif dashes into the portal and the Traveler tries to follow, but it closes first. Paimon lifts the Traveler's spirits, points out what they have learned, tells them to remember Dainsleif, and urges them on towards Inazuma.
### ULTRA +
The act works as a chain of witness accounts: the Cathedral supplies the missing statue and the Tyrant's Final Fury, Wolvendom supplies Boreas' memory of the machine, and the Stormterror's Lair statue supplies the Field Tiller itself, before the return to the Liyue ruins and the confrontation. Dainsleif acts as guide who shares only part of what he knows: he cautions the Traveler to say nothing of the missing statue, and he names the Abyss Order's intent from the talisman rather than from first-hand knowledge of this scheme.

## revelations
### LITE
Khaenri'ah is named and its fall described; the Abyss Order is shown actively trying to make a mechanised god; the Traveler's sibling is alive and working with the Order.
### STANDARD +
Dainsleif's account of Khaenri'ah: a nation that never had a god, whose human civilisation flourished at a very advanced pace until it was destroyed by gods about five centuries ago. He says the Ruin Guards are a modern name for what were once called Field Tillers, powerful mortal weapons that wandered Teyvat after the destruction. The sibling identifies him as the Twilight Sword, a Khaenri'ahn royal guard under an immortality curse.
### LARGE +
The Order's plan, read from a talisman, is only its first phase and revolves around Osial and the first Field Tiller. Barbara's and Boreas' accounts identify the Tyrant's Final Fury at Old Mondstadt with the Field Tiller's encounter with Boreas.

## characters
### LITE
The Traveler, Paimon, Dainsleif, the Traveler's sibling and an Abyss Herald are the principals.
### STANDARD +
Supporting cast: Ganyu and Lan (Liyue Adventurers' Guild), Barbara and Rosaria (Mondstadt), Boreas and Razor (Wolvendom), the dead Grand Thief of the Treasure Hoarders, and Abyss Mages. Osial is not present but his limbs are the Order's target.

## consequences
### STANDARD
Dainsleif holds the Field Tiller's eye and the Order's attempt to build a mechanised god is halted for now. The Traveler's sibling has left again through a portal with the Herald, and Dainsleif followed them into it. The Traveler resolves to continue to Inazuma. The thread resumes in Requiem of the Echoing Depths, where Dainsleif recalls that the same thing happened when he chased the sibling into a portal.

## placement
### STANDARD
Chapter I Act IV, following A New Star Approaches and preceding Autumn Winds, Scarlet Leaves. Unlocked in play by Bough Keeper: Dainsleif and the Lupus Minor Chapter Act I (The Meaning of Lupical). Released in version 1.4.
