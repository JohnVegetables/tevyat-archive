## summary
### LITE
Act II of Song of the Welkin Moon, set in Nasha Town and around Nod-Krai. A phantom linked to the Wild Hunt is traced to Rerir, one of the Five Sinners of Khaenri'ah. He impersonates the Ratnik Sousi, who is found murdered with Flins blamed, and tries to use the Lightkeepers' kuuvahki cannon to regain strength. The Traveler, Flins and Ineffa blunt the shot, and Columbina pulls the group out of danger.
### STANDARD +
The act has five quests: Fulminations and Undercurrents, The Dark Side of Memory, One There Was, Who Hunted the Moon, The Ashen-White Order Burns and Rest Beyond Reach. It follows A Dance of Snowy Tides and Hoarfrost Groves and precedes A Nation That Doesn't Exist. It was released in version 6.0 (Luna I).
### LARGE +
The act turns the Wild Hunt from a local menace into a named enemy. It also forces Columbina into the open, since the Moon Marrow the Traveler carries makes them a target, and it sets up the alliance against Rerir that is assembled in later acts.

## plot
### LITE
The Traveler and Paimon help repair the cannon in Nasha Town and learn of a Wild Hunt phantom. Lauma and Columbina explain the Moon Marrow and identify Rerir. A false "Sousi" proves to be Rerir, the real Sousi is found dead and Flins is blamed. The group deduces that Rerir wants the cannon fired at himself, and it is fired at Starsand Shoal. Columbina teleports everyone out of danger. The act ends with Varka, who has been following Rerir, introducing himself to him.
### STANDARD +
The Traveler helps Aino's cannon repairs, meets Nefer, Lauma and Columbina, and learns the Wild Hunt's source is Rerir. Sousi is found murdered, a witness blames Flins, and the "Sousi" who helped with the repairs proves to have been Rerir; Rerir then manipulates the Lightkeepers' cannon at Starsand Shoal. Columbina rescues the group; the act ends with Varka, who has been trailing Rerir, introducing himself to him.
### LARGE +
Fulminations and Undercurrents: in Nasha Town residents speak of a recent Wild Hunt incursion. The Lightkeepers ask the inventor Aino to repair the Boom and Blast Kuuvahki Cannon, and she agrees as long as she gets her sweets. With Ineffa and a Ratnik called Sousi, the Traveler and Paimon gather what is needed to fix it. Back at the Flagship tavern a private Banquet is under way. Entry needs the proper "channel", so the Traveler goes to the Curatorium of Secrets, where Nefer agrees to arrange an invitation in exchange for carrying medicine to Lauma. Jahoda, who mistakes salt for the medicine, fetches the right package. At the Banquet Nefer presents the Traveler as a Mondstadt mixologist. The Traveler questions Dobrawa about her encounter with the Wild Hunt, and the informant Korhonen tells Nefer that spots had been cleared before the Lightkeepers arrived, but he has never heard of the phantom. Nefer and the Traveler conclude the phantom is a recent sighting and a mastermind may stand behind it.

The Dark Side of Memory: the Traveler takes medicine to Lauma on Hiisi Island. Lauma uses the Moon Marrow to draw out one of the Traveler's memories, which shows only their spaceship crashing into Nod-Krai. A Luonnotar signals that Columbina wishes to speak, so the Traveler goes to Silvermoon Hall, where Columbina looks weakened. She says the Moon Marrow is a piece of Aria's remains, that her own power comes from the Frost Moon and that she does not want the marrow, because two moons cannot rise together. She recalls a man obsessed with the moons known only by the title Rächer of Solnari, and Paimon and the Traveler recognise one of the Five Sinners of Khaenri'ah, which Paimon takes to be Rerir. Columbina says Rerir was said to have fallen into the moon's reflection, a point of no return. She also helps the Traveler look again at their crashed ship, where they see their sibling outside it, and teaches a way to hide the marrow's presence.

One There Was, Who Hunted the Moon: Lauma decides to keep the enemy's identity from her people to avoid panic. Aino prepares concentrated kuuvahki for the cannon and reports that Sousi has been found murdered. The Lightkeeper Sieciech explains that a witness saw Flins kill him. The Traveler finds Flins just in time to save him from Rerir. Flins explains where his lantern came from and why he kept the abyssal fragment. Back at the scene the group notices Sousi's mechanical arm flashing a maintenance error code. They realise the Sousi who had helped repair the cannon was Rerir. Hintsa, the town mechanic, confirms the real Sousi never turned up for the work, so Rerir seems able to copy only external appearance. That night Flins tells the Traveler privately that he is a fae who turned his heart toward humanity, suffered loss and wished to die until the Lightkeepers' fearless fighting drew him in, and asks that it stay secret.

The Ashen-White Order Burns: a mob demands Flins, and Nefer drives them off. Rumours that the Wild Hunt can wear faces make townspeople accuse each other. At the Curatorium the group splits to handle incursions. With Flins the Traveler searches the Ratniks' library and, helped by spirits, learns that kuuvahki and abyssal energy are opposites in which the winner absorbs the loser. They infer Rerir wants the cannon fired at him. A Lauma ibis warns that the cannon is operating on its own aimed at Starsand Shoal. At the shoal Rerir reveals that he is the origin of the Wild Hunt, that he has been gathering fragments of his former body and that his heart is in Flins' lantern. In town Lauma calms barricading citizens while Aino and Ineffa work to interfere with the barrel. The shot is partly deflected by Flins, the Traveler's Moon Marrow shield and Ineffa, though part of the beam is corrupted and the Kuuvahki Experimental Design Bureau is damaged. A mysterious dome shields Hiisi Island. Rerir restores a part of his body but is enraged to find it incomplete. Sandrone and her robot Pulonia arrive, Pulonia is overcome, and Columbina teleports the group to Silvermoon Hall. Sandrone leaves rather than fight her former colleague, and Columbina privately realises her strength is not returning.

Rest Beyond Reach: the group cannot name Rerir's motive but rules out revenge. The Traveler finds Columbina walking on the rooftops of Nasha Town. She says she wishes to return to the moon, where she would find answers and "home". Elsewhere Rerir notices he is being followed and Varka introduces himself.

Details worth keeping apart: the Traveler's Moon Marrow shield and Columbina's concealment technique are two separate uses of the marrow. Rerir's lines at Starsand Shoal include that humans did not understand the powers they wielded. Columbina says her recent calculations had been significantly off, and Sandrone reminds her of the Fatui warrant against her.

### ULTRA +
The act depends on rumour as much as combat: the false murder charge, the mob at the Flagship and the spreading fear that anyone may be a Wild Hunt mimic all weaken Nasha Town before the cannon is fired. Information is gathered through social channels (the Banquet, the informant Korhonen, the Ratniks' library spirits) rather than only by fighting.

## revelations
### LITE
The Wild Hunt's mastermind is Rerir, a Sinner of Khaenri'ah, and he is the origin of the Wild Hunt. The Moon Marrow is described by Columbina as a piece of Aria's remains.
### STANDARD +
Columbina says a moon marrow forms only when a moon is destroyed and its Moon Goddess dies, and that she has no marrow of her own. Rerir can copy only the outward appearance of people, and kuuvahki and abyssal energy are shown to be opposites. Flins is revealed to be a fae.
### LARGE +
Columbina says Rerir's present Wild Hunt is far deadlier than past versions and suspects something is holding him back. The Traveler's memory shows their sibling outside the crashed ship, which the Traveler believes should have been impossible because they recall the sibling never leaving it.

## characters
### LITE
The Traveler, Paimon, Lauma, Columbina, Flins, Nefer, Aino and Rerir are the main figures.
### STANDARD +
Others: Ineffa, Jahoda, Sandrone and Pulonia, Varka, the murdered Ratnik Sousi, and minor townspeople such as Valta, Dobrawa, Korhonen, Hintsa, Sieciech and Zvoni.

## consequences
### STANDARD
Rerir regains part of his body and remains at large. Flins is cleared in the Traveler's eyes, though the town was briefly gripped by suspicion. Columbina is exposed and weakened, and Varka approaches Rerir and introduces himself. The next acts gather allies against him.

## placement
### STANDARD
Song of the Welkin Moon Act II, after A Dance of Snowy Tides and Hoarfrost Groves and before A Nation That Doesn't Exist. Listed with Act I under version Luna I (6.0, released 2025-09-10).

## entry: Rerir impersonating Sousi
@keys: Rerir, Sousi
@keys2: Wild Hunt, Nasha Town
@version: 6.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The Sousi who helped the Traveler repair the cannon was Rerir. The real Sousi had been killed in his office, and his mechanical arm flashed a maintenance error code that exposed the lie. A witness had seen "Flins" commit the murder, and the Lightkeepers initially suspected him. The group concluded Rerir copies only appearance.

## entry: Starsand Shoal cannon firing
@keys: Boom and Blast Kuuvahki Cannon, Starsand Shoal, kuuvahki cannon
@version: 6.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Aino's cannon fires toward Starsand Shoal where Rerir stands, partly because he planned that kuuvahki would be converted by his abyssal energy. Flins, the Traveler's Moon Marrow shield and Ineffa limit the damage. Part of the beam is corrupted and Rerir restores some of his body.

## entry: Flins the fae
@keys: Flins' lantern, Flins
@keys2: Rerir, fae
@version: 6.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Flins tells the Traveler he is a fae who turned toward humanity, lost people and once wished to end his life, until the Lightkeepers' fearless fighting led him to join them. He kept an abyssal fragment in his lantern so others could not misuse it, and Rerir later says his heart is in it.
