## summary
### LITE
Act V of the Song of the Welkin Moon Archon Quest, set in Nod-Krai. As the festival of Moon-Prayer Night approaches, the Traveler and friends plan a farewell for Columbina, who intends to return to the moon, while phantoms of the past appear across the region. Then Columbina begins to vanish from the world, and the search for her true name begins.
### STANDARD +
The act has two quests, In the Shifting Light of the Moon and Ripples That Will Never Reach. It follows An Elegy for Faded Moonlight and leads into Melting Moonlight in the Morning Mist. It was released in version 6.2 (Luna III). About two months have passed since the Traveler and Paimon first arrived in Nod-Krai, and the Adventurers' Guild work is largely done.
### LARGE +
Tonally the act moves from a festival-preparation story with an uncanny backdrop (phantoms, mirages, fixed history) to a race against a quiet disappearance. Its two threads are the investigation of the phantoms, which gives Durin and Flins character moments, and the problem of Columbina, which Alice reframes as a question of identity and the world's own rejection of her.

## plot
### LITE
The Traveler and Paimon turn to Moon-Prayer Night after finishing their guild work. They learn Columbina plans to return to the moon at the festival, while mysterious phantoms appear across Nod-Krai. In the second quest, Columbina vanishes, Alice explains that she is fading out of existence, and the party seeks a way to rescue her.
### STANDARD +
**In the Shifting Light of the Moon.** Katheryne explains the festival's origins with the Frostmoon Scions and the Moon Goddess. The Traveler worries about Columbina (Kuutar), weakened after the fight with Rerir, and about Dottore's continued absence. At Silvermoon Hall they learn she plans to return to the moon on Moon-Prayer Night, when kuuvahki energy peaks; if she stays in Teyvat she will eventually cease to exist. The Traveler invites her to join the festival as a farewell and she reluctantly agrees, despite being wanted by the Fatui, asking them to tell Lauma that her answer to staying is "there is no need". Lauma and Varka decide to give her blessings and good memories rather than cling to her.

Meanwhile phantoms and mirages appear across Nod-Krai. Durin, Albedo, Flins, Nefer and Jahoda split the investigation: Durin's group studies Abyss-like illusions, while Nefer's Curatorium of Secrets pursues human phantoms who claim to come from ancient Hyperborea. Jahoda spots a phantom flying ship at Starsand Shoal matching the Traveler's own ship. Animals scouted by Lauma confirm it is untouchable, and phantoms around it replay fixed scenes of locals failing to open it; Varka compares this to a play whose actors can be spoken to but whose script cannot be changed.

**Ripples That Will Never Reach.** The Traveler goes to Silvermoon Hall to find out what gift Columbina might like, but she is absent, and the flowers in the hall are strangely pale. Lauma says Columbina should not even be able to walk in her weakened state. On the way back to Nasha Town a mysterious stall turns out to be run by Alice, who explains that the border of Teyvat is destabilising because the power of all three moons has gathered in Nod-Krai, and that Columbina has not left but is fading. Alice sets two tasks: find Columbina's true name, and find a way to send her to the real moon. She refers the party to the Hexenzirkel witch Nicole.
### LARGE +
Alice's explanation: the world is rejecting Columbina because she no longer has a foundation for her existence (no true identity, no accepted name, no divine function), and her Moon Marrow plan will no longer work. Columbina, now a fading consciousness without a physical form, wanders Nod-Krai unseen, watching ordinary life at the Frostmoon Enclave and Nasha Town and feeling bittersweet longing for freedoms she never allowed herself.

Nicole explains ancient Hyperborean attempts to reach the moons and a way to recreate such a gateway through phantom projection and kuuvahki resonance; she will look for a suitable phantom as a bridge. She says Columbina is fading because the moon goddesses were erased from the world's natural order and because Columbina has rejected every identity she was given, even "Columbina". Entering the Hyperborean ruins requires three compatible people to undergo three days of kuuvahki infusion, with fasting. After many candidates are tested, only the Traveler, Lauma and Arlecchino are fully compatible. Varka is not compatible enough despite trying; Jahoda has an allergic reaction; Ineffa and Flins are not human; and Nicole refuses Aino because she is a child.
### ULTRA +
Columbina follows the party and overhears that they are searching for her name and that people care about her, but she is too weak to pass the entrance of the ruins. Durin, Albedo and the Wanderer test whether a phantom's fate can be altered using Durin's Abyssal abilities; it partly works, and for the first time a phantom remembers them. Alice detects the disturbance and tells them to continue. At the end the ruins' gate opens, but Columbina realises she cannot follow, her consciousness nearly gone, and wonders whether she can survive until Moon-Prayer Night.

Durin's thread: his group sees visions of Nod-Krai during the Khaenri'ah Cataclysm, including deaths he is powerless to stop. Flins says he is some 600-700 years old and lived through those disasters, and that what they see is immutable history. Durin struggles with his Abyssal nature and whether he should exist, then concludes the darkness in him can also be a source of strength.

## revelations
@version: 6.2
@spoiler: major
### LITE
Columbina is fading out of existence because the world is rejecting her.
### STANDARD +
Alice states that the powers of all three moons have gathered in Nod-Krai, destabilising Teyvat's border, and that Columbina needs a true name and a route to the real moon. "Columbina" and "Kuutar" are not her real identity.
### LARGE +
Flins reveals he is roughly 600-700 years old and lived through the Cataclysm in Nod-Krai (the wiki summary also notes that Ineffa and Flins are non-human in Nicole's compatibility screening). Nefer's group meets phantoms claiming to be from Hyperborea, and Lauma and Nefer agree to share Hyperborean records.

## characters
### LITE
Traveler and Paimon; Columbina; Lauma; Varka; Alice; Nicole.
### STANDARD +
Also Katheryne, Durin, Albedo, Flins, Nefer, Jahoda, Aino, Ineffa, Arlecchino (as a compatible candidate), and the Wanderer. Dottore is discussed but absent.

## consequences
### STANDARD
The party commits to finding Columbina's true name and to opening a path to the moon, with Moon-Prayer Night as the deadline. The act ends with the gate to the Hyperborean ruins open and Columbina too weak to follow. The story continues in Melting Moonlight in the Morning Mist.
### LARGE +
Lauma and Nefer's agreement to share Hyperborean records and the partial success of altering a phantom's fate both remain available to later acts.

## placement
### STANDARD
Act V of Song of the Welkin Moon, after Act IV (An Elegy for Faded Moonlight) and before Act VI (Melting Moonlight in the Morning Mist). Released in version 6.2 (Luna III, 2025-12-03).

## entry: Phantoms of Moon-Prayer Night

@version: 6.2
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
As Moon-Prayer Night nears, phantoms and mirages appear across Nod-Krai. They replay fixed scenes of the past: a phantom flying ship at Starsand Shoal resembling the Traveler's own is untouchable, and phantoms around it re-enact locals failing to open it. The party concludes the phantoms are fragmented and harmless to the festival.
### LARGE +
Human phantoms claiming Hyperborean origin are pursued by Nefer's Curatorium of Secrets. Durin's group sees Cataclysm-era visions. A partial alteration of a phantom's fate is achieved later in the act.
