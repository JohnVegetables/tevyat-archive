## summary
@spoiler: major
### LITE
Song of the Welkin Moon is the Archon Quest chapter set in Nod-Krai, the moon-soaked autonomous borderland of Snezhnaya, and, in its last two acts, Sumeru. The Traveler and Paimon arrive with the automaton Ineffa, befriend the Moon Goddess Columbina and the Frostmoon Scions, and fight first the revived Khaenri'ahn Rerir, then the Fatui Harbinger Dottore, who builds a false moon. Dottore's experiment ends in Sumeru, where Irminsul is burned.
### STANDARD
The chapter runs from a prelude in Natlan (version 5.8) through ten acts (6.0-6.6). It follows the Traveler's search for clues about their spaceship while Nod-Krai is threatened by the Wild Hunt, by Rerir, and by Fatui who have moved in under Dottore and Sandrone. Columbina, the weakened Moon Goddess, is the emotional centre: friends restore her strength and her name, and she in turn helps them. Dottore then steals the power of the three moons and attacks Nod-Krai and, ultimately, Sumeru's Irminsul, with Sandrone's death and Dottore's end as the cost. It was never officially called Chapter VI.
### LARGE +
The chapter links the Traveler's own origin to Nod-Krai, since their spaceship crashed there, and ties Nod-Krai's moons, the Hyperborean past and the Khaenri'ahn Cataclysm together. Its closing acts shift to Sumeru and the fate of Irminsul.

## plot
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
**Prelude: The Journey Home (5.8).** In Natlan the Traveler and Paimon find Ineffa, an automaton looking for them, and learn of a message from Varka telling them to come to Nod-Krai.

**Acts I-II (6.0).** In Nasha Town they meet Flins, Lauma, Aino and Nefer. A Moon Marrow ends up carried by the Traveler, and a shape-shifting phantom linked to the Wild Hunt is traced to Rerir.

**Acts III-IV (6.1).** Rerir, one of the Five Sinners of Khaenri'ah, attacks Columbina. Allies recover the Iridescent Marrow with Arlecchino's help, delve into Rerir's past, and cast him out of Teyvat.

**Acts V-VI (6.2).** Columbina is fading; she is given a name, enjoys Moon-Prayer Night and is lost after Dottore's ambush (Act VI has its own entity).

**Acts VII-VIII (6.3).** Dottore captures the Traveler; Columbina recovers help in the moon's reflection and returns to defeat him; Sandrone dies.

**Acts IX-X (6.6).** In Sumeru, Nahida and her allies stop an Irminsul crisis traced to Dottore.
### LARGE +
Fuller account of each stage, condensed from the wiki summaries.

**Prelude.** Ineffa washes ashore in Natlan, carrying a cryptex for the Traveler. With Chasca, Ifa and Xilonen the Traveler learns her core comes from Natlan's Secret Source while the rest is not Natlan-made; memories of her past flicker, and an entity in her mind calls her a traitor for daring to seek a place to belong. In a Secret Source facility she learns she is the benevolent half split from the Eleventh Lord, one of the Thirteen Sovereign Lords; fighting the Lord's hostile self, she builds a new core and gains an Electro power that the Eleventh Lord recognises as a Moon Wheel. The cryptex holds Varka's letter asking the Traveler to meet him at a tavern called the Flagship.

**Act I.** Ineffa leaves Natlan with the Traveler and Paimon. In Nasha Town the Traveler feels kuuvahki, the moons' primordial power, and recognises it as like the power they felt on landing, suggesting their spaceship is nearby. They meet Aino, Flins, Lauma of the Frostmoon Scions, and Marionette, a Harbinger, who warns them off Fatui business. After a Wild Hunt attack, Lauma's Moon Marrow is stored in the Traveler's body; she tells the tribe the truth about their faith and some members leave.

**Act II.** With Nefer, Jahoda and Aino the Traveler pursues a phantom that can impersonate others. Columbina recalls a man titled the "Rächer of Solnari", from which the Traveler and Paimon identify the enemy as Rerir, one of the Five Sinners; Columbina had been told he fell into the moon's reflection. Flins is accused of killing the Ratnik Sousi, but the "Sousi" seen earlier had been Rerir in disguise.

**Act III.** Rerir attacks Columbina, who is hurt shielding Jahoda, and the Wild Hunt hunts them. Varka rescues the group and Nefer is hired. Columbina and the Traveler go to the Kuuvahki Experimental Design Bureau, where Rerir later disables Sandrone's automaton Pulonia. Arlecchino, present only in a personal capacity as Columbina's friend, opens a portal to a domain where they take the Iridescent Marrow; her hands are corrupted by the Abyss in forcing the way out.

**Act IV.** Albedo, a young Durin and Dainsleif join the group. Rerir's past emerges: a Khaenri'ahn under King Irmin who was engaged to Tholindis and was ordered to eliminate his own fiancee. In the battle that follows, Albedo and Durin siphon Rerir's power into Columbina, and Columbina flicks Rerir through a moon gate held open by Dainsleif, throwing him out of Teyvat. Sandrone mentions the Lord-Artificer, Alain Guillotin.

**Act V.** About two months after arrival, Moon-Prayer Night approaches. Columbina plans to return to the moon but is fading because the world rejects a being with no accepted name; Alice says her true name must be found, and Nicole explains the rejection and how to open the Hyperborean ruins. Phantoms from Hyperborea appear across Nod-Krai and the ruins' gate opens, but Columbina is too weak to follow.

**Act VI.** See quest.melting-moonlight-in-the-morning-mist.

**Act VII.** A few days after the ambush, a Fatuus passes on word that Arlecchino and Sandrone are going to Dottore's far-north facility. There Dottore, using an artificial Moon Marrow, overpowers the group, captures the Traveler and curses the others so that leaving Nod-Krai would trigger it. Dottore replaces the Frost Moon in the sky with an artificial one and Nod-Krai begins evacuating. Holding the Traveler, Dottore accidentally leaves them frozen for over a month and offers to restore their status as a Descender. In the moon's reflection, Columbina re-enters the past and creates Luonnotar to help.

**Act VIII.** Wanderer rescues the Traveler. Columbina meets the three Moon Sisters, who lend her their authorities and then face the end in their own time. Sandrone's world-formula, run partly through Wanderer's core, brings Columbina back. Dottore becomes the "Heretic of the False Moon". Sandrone dies saving the Traveler. Columbina arrives as the Trilune Goddess and, with the Traveler and friends, destroys his artificial Moon Marrow and Dottore. Afterwards a new League of Nod-Krai's factions is formed, and Columbina takes the Traveler to their spaceship on the Frost Moon and gives them a gift: the lifting of some of Teyvat's restrictions on how they use the elements.

**Act IX.** Collei's letter and Nicole's premonition warn Sumeru that identities will be switched. Irminsul stops recording and processing properly; Nahida takes over the load and collapses. Sumeru City is evacuated to Caravan Ribat and civilians later moved on to Aaru Village, while scholars search the Temple of Silence; the Seven Pillars of King Deshret agree to open Aaru.

**Act X.** Nahida plans to overload Irminsul with the Akasha System. She obtains Mavuika's Pyro Gnosis through the Traveler. Dottore, working inside a new Irminsul tree, is helped by Pantalone. Nahida uses the Gnosis to burn the corrupted tree; Dottore's remaining Segments drag him into the flames and he dies, naming Pantalone as Feofan. Aaru takes over Irminsul's recording role, the Pyro Gnosis disappears, and Collei has the god remains removed by Liloupar.
### ULTRA +
Details worth keeping separate: the two Moon Marrows Dottore stole from Columbina before building his artificial Moon Marrow; the Thirteen Sovereign Lords are artificial intelligences built from Secret Source technology, as Citlali says in the prelude; Flins reveals he is 600-700 years old and lived through the Cataclysm in Nod-Krai; Pulcinella's letter to Nahida apologises for Dottore's actions; and the wiki's Act X summary treats Dottore's death as final.

## revelations
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
- Nod-Krai is soaked in kuuvahki, the primordial power of the moons, and the Traveler's spaceship crashed there.
- The Moon Goddess is Columbina; Rerir is a Khaenri'ahn who served King Irmin and was sent out of Teyvat in Act IV.
- Dottore built an artificial Moon Marrow and a false moon and was destroyed in Act VIII; his larger experiment moved to Sumeru's Irminsul.
- The Heavenly Principles placed the Traveler's spaceship on the Frost Moon.
### LARGE +
The Moon Sisters (Aria, Canon and Sonnet) were imprisoned by Nibelung and lend their authorities to Columbina. Irminsul is destroyed by Nahida using the Pyro Gnosis in Act X, ending the corruption but leaving Aaru as the new recording system.
### ULTRA +
Details that matter for later portrayals: Dottore knew Wanderer's erased identity as Scaramouche and said so in Act X; Pulcinella's letter says Dottore killed Sandrone; Nicole is treated as an angel whose identity is kept quiet, and she reveals that the god remains in Collei come from Nabu Malikata.

## characters
### STANDARD
Traveler and Paimon; Columbina (the Moon Goddess); Lauma and the Frostmoon Scions; Ineffa and Aino; Flins; Nefer and Jahoda of the Curatorium; Varka (Mondstadt's Grand Master and Knight of Boreas); Sandrone and Arlecchino of the Fatui; Rerir (antagonist); Dottore (antagonist); Dainsleif, Albedo and Durin; Nicole and Alice of the Hexenzirkel; Wanderer, Nahida and the Sumeru allies in Acts IX-X.

## consequences
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The Frost Moon is back in Teyvat's sky, Columbina lives on Hiisi Island, Nod-Krai forms a joint League, and Dottore and Sandrone are dead. Sumeru keeps functioning under Aaru; Irminsul has been burned, and the Pyro Gnosis has gone.
### LARGE +
Nicole warns that the disaster has made Teyvat easier for the Abyss to detect. The expedition from Mondstadt starts returning home in waves, and Varka writes to Jean about the Fatui's disunity.

## placement
### STANDARD
Released as a prelude in 5.8 and Acts I-X across Luna I-VII (6.0-6.6), the chapter follows Chapter V (Natlan) and precedes Chapter VII (Snezhnaya), which opens in 7.0. Story order matches release order.
### LARGE +
The chapter's version labels are Luna I (6.0) for Acts I-II, Luna II (6.1) for III-IV, Luna III (6.2) for V-VI, Luna IV (6.3) for VII-VIII, and Luna VII (6.6) for IX-X.
