## summary
### LITE
Song of the Welkin Moon Act IX, set in Sumeru. A letter about Collei's warning dream sends the Traveler and Paimon from Nod-Krai back to Sumeru City, where Nahida has taken on Irminsul's overload and collapsed. With a barrier sealing Sumeru off, the Traveler helps scholars, Eremites and Nod-Krai visitors regroup, fend off Jinn and Fatui, find the sleeping Liloupar, and win the Seven Pillars' agreement to open Aaru.
### STANDARD +
The act has three quests: Where Flowers Awaken, A Divergence Leading to Oneself and Ancient Shadows Re-Emerge. It moves through Sumeru City, Caravan Ribat, the Desert of Hadramaveth, the Temple of Silence, Gurabad and the Mausoleum of King Deshret. Released in version 6.6 (Luna VII). It is the point at which the Nod-Krai-centred story returns to Sumeru.
### LARGE +
The act follows the Akasha System plan: to restart the system that once cleansed Irminsul of forbidden knowledge, the allies need a replacement for the Dendro Gnosis that originally powered it. This brings back several characters and places from Chapter III, and Nicole and Nefer arrive from Nod-Krai to help.

## plot
### LITE
Collei's letter and a dream warn the Traveler of trouble in Sumeru. Irminsul has stopped processing information, Nahida has taken over its work and collapses, and everyone is evacuated to Caravan Ribat. The allies plan to restart the Akasha System, the Traveler takes Nahida to Apep, and the Fatui and Jinn attack. After Liloupar is found and the Jinn pacified, the Seven Pillars agree to open Aaru.
### STANDARD +
Collei's letter and her warning dream bring the Traveler to Sumeru City, where Nahida has absorbed Irminsul's overload and collapsed and the population is moved to Caravan Ribat. The allies plan to restart the Akasha System and the Traveler takes Nahida to Apep. The Fatui and Jinn attack; Collei, guided by Nicole, calms the Jinn with a prayer of Nabu Malikata, and the Traveler finds Liloupar in Gurabad, who pacifies the Jinn. After Nefer's answer frees Thoth and his fellow Pillars and Cyno speaks for Hermanubis, the Seven Pillars agree to open Aaru.
### LARGE +
Where Flowers Awaken: at the Nod-Krai Adventurers' Guild, Katheryne gives the Traveler a letter from Collei, which begins a flashback of a dream she had in which everyone in Sumeru City had switched identities and Tighnari did not know her. A voice, later revealed as Nicole's, tells her it is a premonition and that she should tell Nahida and the Traveler. In Sumeru City the Traveler finds the city evacuating to Caravan Ribat on Nahida's and the new Sages' orders. In the Sanctuary of Surasthana, Nahida hovers inside a Dendro field: something has stopped Irminsul recording and processing information, creating a bottleneck that disrupts the ley lines, and she has taken over Irminsul's work herself despite the strain. Wanderer finds the cause is not an attack from outside. The field fails and Nahida collapses, so the group decides to move her to Caravan Ribat. Meanwhile in the Great Red Sand, a hooded Fatui leader says disrupting Sumeru City is a necessary step.

A Divergence Leading to Oneself: at Caravan Ribat the Sages are introduced and Rukh Shah reports that civilians returning near Yasna Monument went mad and killed one another. A Ley Line Blossom nearby is dissected and shows Irminsul can still be reached from outside, leading to a plan to restart the Akasha System, which needs a substitute for the Dendro Gnosis that once powered it. The scholars will search the Temple of Silence's records, while the Eremites move civilians to Aaru Village. The Traveler takes Nahida to Apep in the Desert of Hadramaveth, who agrees to share her Dendro energy, says the Heavenly Principles seized Irminsul from ancient dragonkind, and senses Jinn behind the sandstorms. Nahida wakes briefly and sends the Traveler a flower-shaped key containing a message. At the Temple of Silence the Traveler and Paimon are attacked by Jinn and the Fatui and trapped by the hooded man, while at Aaru Village Collei, with Nicole's guidance, uses a prayer of Nabu Malikata to make the Jinn submit. Jeht and Benben free the Traveler; with Jeht they open the way to Gurabad and find Liloupar, who explains she fought the Abyss there until Gosoythoth's defeat in Natlan let her sleep, and who then pacifies the Jinn and stops the sandstorms.

Ancient Shadows Re-Emerge: the group reunites at the Temple of Silence. A barrier now encloses Sumeru; Nicole explains it is one of the ley lines' defences to isolate Irminsul. She tells Collei that the god remains Dottore injected into Collei came from Nabu Malikata. At the Mausoleum of King Deshret, Nefer answers Thoth's question with "time", which frees Thoth, Heryshaf and Sobek and restores their true forms. Cyno, as Hermanubis' representative, with Apep already helping Nahida, secures the Seven Pillars' agreement to open Aaru. Jebrael's soul is among the first to emerge.
The sequence depends on layered plans: the Akasha System restart, the move to Aaru Village, and the Traveler's private message from Nahida. The Sages named in the act are Naphis and the five others (Baladhuri, Alkindi, Firnas, Shakir and Rushd) with their titles in parentheses (Vahumana, Spantamad, Kshahrewar, Rtawahist and Haravatat). Cyrus bemoans an "invisible hand" steering events. Dori and her Jinni partner Plumpy defend Aaru Village's north gate with Tighnari and Collei; Baladhuri and Firnas report the southern line has collapsed before Collei's prayer ends the attack. Nefer asks whether the scholars can modify Aaru, and Naphis says they are heading to the mausoleum.

Nicole and Nefer come from Nod-Krai and arrive before the barrier seals. Faruzan bonds with Nicole over a squirrel sculpture Nicole creates from nothing; the others agree not to tell Faruzan about Nicole's angel identity for the time being. Cyno tells Collei that however many fragments she carries she remains herself.
### ULTRA +
The act reuses Chapter III material: Apep's Heart of Oasis and the Fire Seed, the Mausoleum of King Deshret and its Pillars, and Gurabad. Liloupar's account links the Abyss's retreat in Gurabad to Gosoythoth's defeat in Natlan. Jeht reports that she settled her old grudge against the Tanit elder Masseira, and Alhaitham links the Jinn's sudden movement to Gurabad. The Fatui hooded man says his reason for following Dottore is "friendship" and a shared wish to write rules for the world.

## revelations
### LITE
Irminsul is blocked and overloaded, and Nahida is strained by it. The god remains inside Collei were Nabu Malikata's. The Heavenly Principles usurped Irminsul from ancient dragonkind. A barrier has sealed Sumeru.
### STANDARD +
Apep tells the Traveler that Irminsul was one of the things the Heavenly Principles took and repurposed from ancient dragonkind. Nicole reveals the god remains Dottore injected into Collei were from Nabu Malikata. Wanderer establishes the anomaly is not an external attack. Nahida's key says the statues of the gods hang inverted from the heavens, and that one alone is unique among them, with its location hidden.
### LARGE +
Nicole explains the barrier is a ley line defence to isolate Irminsul from the world. Liloupar says she fought Abyssal forces in Gurabad until Gosoythoth fell, after which they quieted.

## characters
### LITE
The Traveler, Paimon, Nahida, Cyno, Alhaitham, Collei, Tighnari, Liloupar, Jeht, Nicole and Nefer are the principals.
### STANDARD +
Supporting cast: Wanderer, Kaveh, Dehya, Sethos, Dori and Plumpy, Faruzan, Naphis and the Sages, Apep, Benben, Thoth, Heryshaf, Sobek, Shesepankh, Bennu, Jebrael, Cyrus, Rukh Shah, Mu'tasim, Katheryne, a hooded Fatui leader who calls himself Dottore's subordinate, and Dottore (referred to, not present).

## consequences
### STANDARD
Sumeru is sealed by a barrier, Nahida is in a coma under Apep's care, and the Seven Pillars have agreed to open Aaru. Liloupar is awake and has stopped the Jinn. The allies go on to prepare Aaru for modification.

## placement
### STANDARD
Song of the Welkin Moon Act IX, following True Moon and preceding Of Myriad Paths, Flux, and Dissolution. Released in version 6.6 (Luna VII, 2026-05-20). The wiki lists Adventure Rank 40 as the unlock requirement.
