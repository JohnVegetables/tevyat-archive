## summary
@spoiler: major
### LITE
Chapter VII is the Archon Quest chapter set in Snezhnaya, the Cryo nation of the Tsaritsa and the Fatui. It runs for four acts across Versions 7.0 and 7.1. The Traveler and Paimon cross into the frozen north, find the country strained by an energy-saving policy called Project Stuzha, get tangled in a haunting at the Korolevskiy Theater, and finally meet the Tsaritsa herself. By the end the Traveler has chosen to stand with the Fatui against Ronova, the Ruler of Death.
### STANDARD
The four acts are Everwinter Without Mercy (7.0), Wraith's Nocturne (7.0), White Night, Like a Dream Upon Waking (7.1) and A Rekviem for the Underworld (7.1). The chapter requires Adventure Rank 18 and the Prologue's Act III. Its teaser quotes Dainsleif calling the Tsaritsa a god with no love left for her people, and the chapter answers that line from several angles. Snezhnaya's Archon Quest was originally numbered Act VI in the Travail preview and became Chapter VII after Song of the Welkin Moon was added.
### LARGE +
The chapter has three threads that keep crossing. The first is the country: a winter that never lets up, Kresnik Kristall shortages, Project Stuzha, and ordinary people (a hunter, a ballerina, a singer) trying to get by. The second is the Fatui: the Harbingers Sandrone, Childe, Pantalone, Pulcinella and Arlecchino are shown as officials with duties and disagreements, and Director Pierro finally speaks with the Traveler. The third is the Curse of Death, which kills everyone in range whenever a technological breakthrough threatens Celestia, and which turns out to be the work of Ronova. Paimon's strange illness is the personal thread tying them together.
### ULTRA +
Structurally the chapter moves from outsider travelogue (Act I), through a closed-room mystery that is secretly a death-curse (Act II), to a court-and-politics act that reveals the Tsaritsa's reasoning (Act III), and ends with a war plan against a Shade (Act IV). Each act tightens the Traveler's alliance with the Fatui. As of the 7.1 cutoff the chapter is unfinished: no next chapter is named, and the Holy Soil threat that Act IV ends on is unresolved.

## plot
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
**Act I - Everwinter Without Mercy (7.0).** Columbina throws the Traveler and Paimon a farewell party on the Flagship before they head to Snezhnaya, where Paimon quickly falls ill. A gust of wind blows the Traveler off a bridge, separating them, and the Traveler meets the Eye of Graeae, an ancient device that recognises them as a Descender. Pierro, whom the Traveler does not recognise (Paimon later names him), watches over Paimon in a cabin and leaves. In the town of Sretomorozsk the hunter Alyosha explains Project Stuzha, and they expose a corrupt official hoarding Kresnik Kristall. In Snezhnograd the Traveler joins Sandrone at a troubled mine, rescues the trapped miners and creates the Exaiphanes Blade, meets the ballerina and Fatuus Odette (Signora's protégée, not a Harbinger), and is invited to join the Rokot.

**Act II - Wraith's Nocturne (7.0).** At the Korolevskiy Theater a rehearsal is followed by a snowstorm, and the people trapped inside begin dying, two a night. The group first suspects Odette, who admits she is Lelek, the thief who leaves threatening letters. The truth is that everyone is under the Curse of Death: some guests are revealed to be dead already, the "Alyosha" trapped with them turns out to be Ronova wearing his appearance, and the Traveler and Mitya strike her and escape. Everyone wakes with no memory of it until they gather and recall the shared "dream". Pierro has taken Paimon to the Armory Palace; there he explains Project Mistilteinn, the Curse, and the Fatui's goal of challenging the Heavenly Principles, and the Traveler sides with them to protect Paimon.

**Act III - White Night, Like a Dream Upon Waking (7.1).** The Tsaritsa returns from killing the storm-making monster. The Traveler is tested by Danica, attends court, and is told in private why the Tsaritsa collects the Gnoses and about the Third Descender's seal. Pierro unveils a memory-reading prism, and Sandrone and Pantalone propose using Vesna's small authority of Life as a counter to the Curse: Seals of Life, memory bubbles drawn from many people. A creature of the Ruler of Life steals seals from Northland Bank, and Rhinedottir appears and takes back the stolen power, only to be drawn into a meeting with the Tsaritsa.

**Act IV - A Rekviem for the Underworld (7.1).** See quest.a-rekviem-for-the-underworld: the Delusion experiment, Hroptatyr's notebook, and the battle that strips Ronova of her authority.
### LARGE +
Further Act I detail. Columbina also relays a message from Sandrone, who left earlier for Snezhnograd, telling them not to cause trouble for her. After a bridge fall and a wrecked train, the Traveler uses "Fab-Vision" with Graeae to climb out of a cavern. The corrupt official Shishkin and the mayor are arrested by the Fatui representative Polonsky, who hints that Alyosha has the makings of a Rokot member. The train is pulled into a Shadow Realm without wind, light or elemental energy, where Graeae gives the Traveler a new outfit and weapons. At the mine in Svetloledovka the Traveler draws on an unusual power to form the Exaiphanes Blade, and later uses it to empower the Cryo Statue of the Seven in Snezhnograd. Odette helps buy Kristall using Fatui logistics. At a Veche session Project Stuzha passes with 61 percent support, narrower than before, while the merchant Chugunov, against expectation, votes against it. Alyosha, who is secretly a Rokot member along with the singer Vodyanitsa, formally invites the Traveler to join; members stay anonymous and work freely.

Further Act II detail. The victims on the first night are the merchants Soboleva and Torgov, and on the second Kulish and Rampana. A man named Shram, who has no ticket, turns out to be a ghost from the era of human-fae hostility that ended over 400 years ago, who died in the theater protecting his girlfriend; he leaves peacefully. A girl called Tonia is revealed to be dead: she recalls being ill with Frostblight and falling asleep at a show, and Chugunov, who knew, had projected his own dead daughter Iskra onto her. She gives her real name as Ptashka and leaves. Mitya has been dead the whole time without remembering it, and carries a red eye mark on his palm that the Traveler identifies as Ronova's. Ronova explains soul-ferrying, says angels' renovation of the theater's Ley Lines hinders her, and says a Descender's soul cannot enter the Ley Lines. Afterwards Chugunov shuts down the Iskra Manufacturing Plant, and Odette visits Signora's tomb and decides to go on as both ballerina and Fatuus. Pierro tells the group he drew Paimon down from Celestia to Khaenri'ah and presented her to Irmin, who took no interest; this implies she is at least 500 years old, though she remembers none of it.

Further Act III detail. Pulcinella's report to the Tsaritsa mentions corruption cases; Mitya says no existing technology can deal with the Ruler of Death. The Tsaritsa tells the Traveler her authority is "love", which she refuses to use because the love the Heavenly Principles granted was false. Pierro explains she records each day in a transcription prism that Irminsul changes cannot alter. Pierro introduces the Mirror of Remembrance (the Svetovid Stone) and Vesna reveals she bears the mark of Life; reviewing memories through it shows that Ronova had been "killing" without permission by exploiting a gap in the Heavenly Principles' monitoring while Rhinedottir resurrected the dead before the Shades could be caught. The Seal of Life program is obligatory for officials and nobles, voluntary for the public through Northland Bank. The Tsaritsa arranged the collection partly to draw Rhinedottir out, then offers her information about Asmoday and a meeting in seven days.
### ULTRA +
Additional Act III detail. Danica says her family has served the Tsaritsa for three generations and that the Tsaritsa let her travel as a mercenary, which Danica regards as an act of love. The thief at Northland Bank is a "False Horned Steed" created by the Ruler of Life. Pantalone cooperates in the vault, explaining that the bank combines human and fae traditions, and later gives Vesna a small lantern holding her returned power.

Additional Act I detail. The Eye of Graeae helps the Traveler in exchange for a later favour; its knowledge of present-day Snezhnaya is out of date, which is why the train runs off a cliff on derelict tracks (it and its passengers come through unscathed). Odette reacts badly to learning that the Traveler once duelled Signora before the throne, though Childe says the two were not on good terms.

## revelations
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
- Snezhnaya's goal under the Tsaritsa is to build enough power to face the Heavenly Principles, which have kept the nation at risk of a Celestial Nail as it grows.
- The Tsaritsa's authority is love; she rejects using it because the love given by the Heavenly Principles was not real.
- Ronova, the Ruler of Death, is behind the Curse of Death, which strikes whenever a technological breakthrough threatens Celestia.
- Pierro drew Paimon down from Celestia; she is at least about 500 years old and was once presented to Irmin of Khaenri'ah.
- Project Mistilteinn is an anti-Celestia weapon; its energy was making Paimon, a being of Celestia, ill.
### LARGE +
- A crystalline seal in Zapolyarny Palace was left by the Third Descender before the Heavenly Principles killed them; it reacts only to the Gnoses and its contents are unknown.
- Vesna holds a small authority of Life, stolen by the Archangel in the Hyperborean era and kept by the first Vila.
- Ronova and Rhinedottir had been killing and reviving people within a loophole in the Heavenly Principles' monitoring cycles.
- The Pyro Gnosis burned with Irminsul in Sumeru (the Traveler's account); the Tsaritsa expects any remnant of a Descender to be ejected from the Ley Lines eventually.
- Act IV adds the Crimson Moon's connection to death and the Fatui's plan to create more Delusions.

## characters
### LARGE
The Traveler and Paimon; Columbina (farewell scene); the Harbingers Sandrone, Tartaglia (Childe), Pantalone, Pulcinella, Arlecchino and Director Pierro; the ballerina and Fatuus Odette (Lelek); the Rokot members Alyosha and Vodyanitsa; the researcher Mitya and guard Valeriy; Chugunov and the ghost girl Tonia (Ptashka); Danica and Vesna of the Tsaritsa's court; the Tsaritsa; Ronova, Rhinedottir and Naberius; Dainsleif (Act IV).

## consequences
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Project Stuzha is put on hold once the Tsaritsa returns, easing the energy shortage. Project Mistilteinn is paused at the end of Act II until Ronova can be dealt with (the Tsaritsa confirms this in Act III), then released when she is stripped of her authority in Act IV. The Traveler becomes a trusted ally of the Fatui and gains unrestricted access to Zapolyarny Palace. After 7.1 completion, the Anomaly world quest series opens.
### LARGE +
The Fatui plan to produce new Delusions with Arlecchino's help, and the House of the Hearth separates from Fatui affairs. Ronova's last act in Act IV threatens to unleash Holy Soil from a Nail of Retribution, a thread left open at the cutoff.

## placement
### STANDARD
Requires the Prologue's Act III (Song of the Welkin Moon is the preceding quest in the travel log but was never named Chapter VI). Acts I-II released in 7.0 (2026-08-12) and Acts III-IV in 7.1 (2026-09-23).

## entry: Project Stuzha
@keys: Project Stuzha, Stuzha, Kresnik Kristall
@version: 7.0
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Project Stuzha is the Tsaritsa's plan to lower energy use across Snezhnaya so that supply can go to crucial research. It caused a shortage of Kresnik Kristall, the fuel for stoves, and left towns like Sretomorozsk with closed businesses. A Veche vote kept it alive at 61 percent in Act I; it was put on hold when the Tsaritsa returned.

## entry: Curse of Death theater incident
@keys: Curse of Death, Korolevskiy Theater, Shram
@keys2: Ronova, Wraith's Nocturne
@version: 7.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
When Mitya conceives a technological breakthrough, the Curse of Death kills everyone nearby, who then wake without memory of it. At the Korolevskiy Theater the curse became a closed-circle mystery with deaths each night, the ghosts Shram and Ptashka among the guests, and Ronova disguised as Alyosha. Odette is revealed as Lelek in the process.

## entry: Seals of Life
@keys: Seals of Life, Seal of Life
@keys2: Vesna, Pantalone, Northland Bank
@version: 7.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
A Seal of Life is a bubble holding one meaningful memory from a person's life, made with Vesna's authority of Life. Enough of them could defy death. Officials and nobles were required to donate; the public could give voluntarily at Northland Bank. A creature of the Ruler of Life tried to steal them from the vault; the Tsaritsa froze it, and Rhinedottir let it escape, reclaimed the stolen Life power and restored the seals to their owners.

## entry: Project Mistilteinn
@keys: Project Mistilteinn, Mistilteinn
@version: 7.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Mitya's research programme, described as an anti-Celestia weapon. Its energy made Paimon ill, and every breakthrough risks the Curse of Death. Paused at the end of Act II (confirmed by the Tsaritsa in Act III) and no longer restricted after Act IV.
