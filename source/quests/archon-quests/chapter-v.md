## summary
@spoiler: major
### LITE
Chapter V is the Archon Quest chapter set in Natlan, the Pyro nation of war. The Traveler and Paimon arrive hoping to find their sibling and meet Mavuika, the human Pyro Archon, and six tribes who have fought the Abyss for centuries. Acts I to V and an Interlude follow the final push to end that war: six chosen heroes with Ancient Names, an invasion of the whole nation, and a last descent into the Night Kingdom. Act VI, "A Space and Time for You", is a separate Traveler chapter about the Abyss Order.
### STANDARD
Chapter V introduces Natlan: a land of Saurians and six tribes organised around the Stadium of the Sacred Flame, where the Pilgrimage of the Return of the Sacred Flame produces Contending Fire, the fuel that keeps the Abyss out. The chapter's title is the name of Act V, and the travel log shows Acts I to V plus the Interlude as one block and Act VI as a Traveler chapter. Every act requires Adventure Rank 40.

Its arc: Act I introduces the tribes and the Pilgrimage; Act II shows the Pilgrimage failing and the Fatui Harbinger Capitano attacking Mavuika; Acts III and IV bring Capitano into an uneasy alliance and the Abyss's nation-wide invasion; the Interlude treats traumatised survivors; Act V ends the war and Capitano's quest; Act VI is the Abyss Order's attack and the sibling's reappearance.
### LARGE +
The chapter is also the point at which the Traveler is given an Ancient Name, "Tumaini" (hope), forged by the Name Engraver Xilonen. It is the first such name given to an outlander. Thematically it keeps returning to sacrifice, memory and what a death means: the Ode of Resurrection lets bearers of Ancient Names be revived, so dying and being forgotten become the real stakes, and the Sacred Flame and Night Kingdom stand for the nation's shared memory and fuel. The Traveler's ability to purify Abyssal energy, first used on Dvalin in Mondstadt, is repeatedly decisive.
### ULTRA +
Release spread: Acts I and II in 5.0, III and IV in 5.1, the Interlude in 5.2, Act V in 5.3 and Act VI in 5.7. Threads that carry into later content: Capitano's Gnosis mission is cancelled, and the Doctor's research on Ancient Moon's Remnants in Nod-Krai is flagged.

## plot
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Act I (5.0): the tribes, the Pilgrimage and Kachina's win. Act II (5.0): Kachina is lost in the Night Kingdom, Capitano attacks Mavuika, and the six-heroes plan is revealed. Act III (5.1): an uneasy contact with Capitano and the Abyss's nation-wide attack. Act IV (5.1): Capitano joins the alliance and the invasion costs lives. Interlude (5.2): survivors suffer Abyssal Corrosion Syndrome. Act V (5.3): Gosoythoth is destroyed and Capitano takes Xbalanque's seat. Act VI (5.7): the Abyss Order's attack on the tribes.
### LARGE +
**Act I - Flowers Resplendent on the Sun-Scorched Sojourn (5.0).** See quest.flowers-resplendent-on-the-sun-scorched-sojourn. The Traveler befriends Kachina of the Children of Echoes and meets Mualani, Kinich and his companion K'uhul Ajaw. The Traveler cannot resonate with the Pyro Statue of The Seven. Kachina wins a place in the Night Warden War, and when the Abyss attacks the People of the Springs the Traveler purifies the fresh Abyssal wound of Atea, though her older contamination is too deep and she has only days left.

**Act II - Black Stone Under a White Stone (5.0).** Kachina falls in the Night Warden War but does not return when the Ode of Resurrection is sung. Mavuika suspends the Pilgrimage because Contending Fire is running out. Capitano, the First Fatui Harbinger, accuses Mavuika of failing an oath made 500 years ago and attacks her; she wounds him, but he escapes in a fog raised by a member of the Masters of the Night-Wind. A Spiritspeaker Stone ritual recovers Kachina's Ancient Name, and the Traveler, Mualani, Chasca and Iansan enter the Night Kingdom through a rift in old ruins and bring Kachina back. Mavuika burns the Pyro Archons' keepsakes to pull them out, and explains her plan: six heroes bearing Ancient Names are needed, four are known, and the remaining two must be found in less than a year. She asks Xilonen to forge a name for the Traveler.

**Act III - Beyond the Smoke and Mirrors (5.1).** Xilonen needs Citlali's help to approach the Wayob. Citlali agrees if they clear Ororon, who has been taken by the Fatui. In secret meetings Capitano tells the Traveler he wants them as an ally, doubts Mavuika's heroes will appear, and hints at a costly backup plan; Ororon's memories later show that Capitano means to rebuild Natlan's Ley Lines. The Abyss launches a nation-wide attack.

**Act IV - The Rainbow Destined to Burn (5.1).** Mavuika's group infiltrates Capitano's base and stops the Secret Source Mechanism. Ororon is attacked by the Khaenri'ahn soul Guthred but earns his Ancient Name, Bidii, as the fifth hero. The Lord of the Night, Yohualtecuhtin, speaks and explains the Night Kingdom's origin. Capitano reveals he is a Khaenri'ahn survivor and joins the alliance. The Abyss invades; the Scions of the Canopy fall, Chasca's adoptive younger sister Chuychu dies, and Chasca gains her name as the sixth hero. Mavuika declares a Night Warden War for everyone and destroys the rift.

**Interlude - All Fires Fuel the Flame (5.2).** The Traveler and Iansan help Flower-Feather Clan survivors who suffer Abyssal Corrosion Syndrome. Capitano supplies the Draught of Lucidity, unmasks his rotted face and names Ronova as the cause of his people's suffering. Xilonen begins the Traveler's name.

**Act V - Incandescent Ode of Resurrection (5.3).** The Traveler dreams of Capitano leading many souls. Xilonen finishes "Tumaini". Mavuika and the Traveler enter the Night Kingdom, are aided by fallen heroes, and destroy Gosoythoth, the Abyss's "Reaper". At the ruins of Ochkanatlan Ronova demands Mavuika's life; Capitano offers himself in her place, taking Xbalanque's seat and fusing with the Lord of the Night.

**Act VI - A Space and Time for You (5.7).** A Traveler chapter. The Abyss Order attacks every tribe; the Traveler meets Direidyth, who sees monsters invisible to others; Dainsleif and the Traveler break the Order's base at Ochkanatlan; the sibling reappears.
### ULTRA +
Further details by act. Act I: the Traveler and Paimon leave Fontaine from Romaritime Harbor with a photo taken by Charlotte, and farewells from Navia, Clorinde and Furina. They reach the Children of Echoes' canyon village and find Kachina abandoned by her would-be teammates before the Pilgrimage. Kinich says five people will earn the honour to fight in the war; Cival refuses to bend the rules for an outlander because Contending Fire is only produced by Natlanese fighters.

Act II: Mualani explains that in the Cataclysm the Abyss seemed to tailor its disasters to each tribe. Comonqui, an Abyss Lector in the Night Kingdom, taunts that the Pyro Archon hoards a primordial power. Tupac, a spirit, shows Mualani memories of the Cataclysm. In the aftermath Rezanov reports to Capitano that Mavuika has lost much of her power, and Capitano refuses to attack her while weakened.

Act III: Xilonen is startled to learn that the person she is working for is an outlander; the Night Kingdom records only the deeds of the Natlanese. Citlali's spell lets the Traveler overhear her thoughts, and she is embarrassed when she learns it. Ororon tells the Traveler he saved Capitano because otherwise the Fatui would have gone for Citlali. Ororon is, in Citlali's account, the tribe's only person with an incomplete soul, which his tribe regarded as a bad omen; an earlier ritual to use him as a vessel failed.

Act IV: the Lord of the Night says she is the Night Kingdom's builder from fragments of the old Ley Lines after the Descender's fight with the dragons, and that her kind, once called Angels, survives only in the form of Seelies. Capitano gives the history that he fled Khaenri'ah with his platoon to Natlan and learned about the Gnosis from Ayizu, chief of the Masters of the Night-Wind. Citlali confirms from the Lord of the Night that forging the Traveler's Ancient Name will cost Xilonen, the Name Engraver, her life; Citlali plans to use the Traveler's Descender status to delay Mavuika's death. The Lord of the Night creates the Pilgrim's Chronicle, which records the Traveler's deeds in Natlan.

Act V: at the party in the People of the Springs, Amina says Atea died after the attack. Ororon's ritual vision of Capitano names Guthred and the legendary hero Thrain. Tumaini's test in the Sulfurous Veins attracts too many souls and saves some, including Vichama, and a pair of Tepetlisaur souls who stay with Ayo. In the Night Kingdom Atea, Mallko, Vichama, Bona, Cocouik, Guthred and Chuychu help the Traveler. After Ronova's decision, Mavuika writes a letter for her sister Hine and burns it; the Traveler leaves the feathered crown they were given at a new monument to the fallen.

Act VI: Iansan's contest is cancelled when the Order attacks. The Traveler finds their sibling's Hibernation Chamber key, tells Mavuika that the sibling leads the Order, and fights the Order beside Citlali. Direidyth explains a sage told her of a doomsday only a Savior could stop. At the Ochkanatlan base the Iniquitous Baptist is Haden, Dainsleif's disgraced master, who claims the sibling re-wove Khaenri'ah's Ley Lines with the Loom of Fate. Dainsleif kills him repeatedly, and Haden admits the revival is unfinished and the attack was a ruse to harvest Dainsleif's memories. Direidyth and the Traveler realise they come from different points in time; as the Traveler decides what to do with the key, the sibling arrives, recognises Istaroth's hand in events, and says that Direidyth reaching them led them to abandon Khaenri'ah. The sibling explains the attack was a feint while Yohualtecuhtin let them weave the Atlas of the New World, holding New Khaenri'ah's Ley Lines.
Act IV and Act V rest on a detail of Ronova's curse: Capitano is immortal because of it, so his death forces a paradox; Ronova chooses to uphold the curse rather than break her rules, so Capitano may die and take the seat. Capitano's heart was altered to carry the souls of the Ley Lines.

## revelations
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
- Natlan's Pyro Archon, Mavuika, is a human who was already an experienced warrior before she ascended. She fights alongside the heroes and has spent much of her power to keep the Sacred Flame burning.
- The Night Kingdom is the nation's spirit realm built from fragments of the old Ley Lines by the Lord of the Night, Yohualtecuhtin, whose kind were once called Angels.
- The Fatui's Capitano is a Khaenri'ahn survivor of the Cataclysm who fought for Natlan 500 years ago. Ronova cursed him and his people with immortality in decay. He is the first Harbinger.
- Natlan's Ancient Name bearers are chosen by the Wayob; the six heroes are Kinich, Iansan, Xilonen, Mualani, Ororon and Chasca. The Traveler receives the outlander name Tumaini.
- The false sky is briefly exposed in Act IV.
### LARGE +
- Mavuika used a power from Ronova, the Ruler of Death, through Xbalanque; the price was her death, which Capitano spares.
- The sibling leads the Abyss Order, re-wove Khaenri'ah's Ley Lines with the Loom of Fate into the Atlas of the New World, and says it is needed to face the Heavenly Principles as equals. Direidyth, whom Dainsleif believes to be a pure-blooded Khaenri'ahn, comes from a different point in time, and the sibling says Istaroth played a part in their meeting.
- Mavuika learns Capitano had the Tsaritsa end his Gnosis mission, and that the Doctor's subordinates are studying Ancient Moon's Remnants in Nod-Krai.

## characters
### LARGE
The Traveler and Paimon; Mavuika; the six heroes Kinich (with K'uhul Ajaw), Mualani, Iansan, Xilonen, Ororon and Chasca; Kachina; Citlali; the Fatui Harbinger Capitano; Yohualtecuhtin the Lord of the Night; Ronova; chiefs such as Pacal and Amina; and Wayna, who supplies the Spiritspeaker Stone. In Act VI Direidyth, Dainsleif, Haden and the Traveler's sibling.

## consequences
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The war with the Abyss ends: The Natlanese honour the Traveler with a festival and a monument to the fallen. Capitano is gone, but Mavuika lives and the Lord of the Night is granted eternal life. Mavuika warns that Snezhnaya, with the Fatui, will be the next conflict. The Abyss Order's attack in Act VI leaves the sibling free with the Atlas of the New World.
### LARGE +
Song of the Welkin Moon, set in Nod-Krai, follows, and Chapter VII is set in Snezhnaya. Mavuika's warnings point the Traveler towards Snezhnaya and name Nod-Krai as the Doctor's base.

## placement
### STANDARD
Follows Chapter IV (Fontaine, with Act VI "Bedtime Story") and precedes the Nod-Krai content of 6.x and the Snezhnaya chapter (Chapter VII). Acts were released across 5.0 to 5.3, and the Traveler chapter, Act VI, arrived much later in 5.7.

### STANDARD
Mavuika's plan needs six modern heroes who inherit the Ancient Names of past warriors: Kinich (Malipo), Iansan (Uwezo), Xilonen (Baraka), Mualani (Umoja), Ororon (Bidii, previously borne by Sanhaj) and Chasca (Vuka, previously borne by Menilek). The Traveler is given the outlander name "Tumaini".

## entry: Capitano's role in Natlan
@keys: Capitano, Il Capitano, Captain
@keys2: Natlan, Mavuika, Source Mechanism
@version: 5.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Capitano is the first Fatui Harbinger and a Khaenri'ahn who fled to Natlan after the Cataclysm. He first attacks Mavuika for the Pyro Gnosis, then offers an alternative plan with the Source Mechanism, and finally joins the alliance. In Act V he sacrifices himself to Ronova's curse to spare Mavuika.
