## summary
@spoiler: minor
### LITE
Caribert is the sixth act of Chapter III, the Sumeru Archon Quest (version 3.5). Kaeya, unexpectedly in Sumeru, invites the Traveler and Paimon by letter to Djafar Tavern in Port Ormos, where Dainsleif tells him that his Alberich ancestors founded the Abyss Order. Dainsleif then leads them to a hut in Avidya Forest tied to the sibling's "Loom of Fate" operation, where a man calling himself Eide cares for a sick, masked hilichurl he names Caribert.
### STANDARD +
The act has four quests: Destined Encounter, Fortune-Mocking Pedigree, A Lamenter at Fate's End and Portended Fate. It requires Adventure Rank 35. It reveals the Abyss Order's founder, gives the first clear picture of how the Khaenri'ahns were cursed, and builds to a vision in which the Traveler sees themselves as their sibling.
### LARGE +
The act is a quiet, character-driven interval after the main Akasha and Irminsul plot. It carries the sibling thread forward by following where the sibling and Dainsleif once travelled, and introduces the Chlothar and Caribert family tragedy that Bedtime Story (4.7) later returns to.

## plot
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
In Port Ormos a messenger hands the Traveler a letter from Kaeya, who has come to Sumeru and asks to meet at Djafar Tavern. He claims to be scouting Sumeru's spice-based drinks. Pressed, he admits he once stowed away with Mondstadt merchants as a boy, hoping to learn about Khaenri'ah, and was dragged home. When he says his surname Alberich is the last link to Khaenri'ah, Dainsleif appears and states that the Alberichs founded the Abyss Order. Kaeya recognises him as a pure-blooded Khaenri'ahn and leaves.

Dainsleif then seeks the place in Avidya Forest where he once travelled with the sibling. He and the Traveler find an abandoned farm and hut, fight slimes and camp for the night; the Traveler dreams of the sibling. In the morning a gruff man scolds the Traveler for trespassing, introduces himself as Eide and shows a bandaged hilichurl he calls his son Caribert. Together they brew a remedy from Sumeru plants, which does not work at first. After an encounter at a hilichurl-worshipped sanctuary, where a voice calling itself a sinner speaks, a second dose wakes Caribert. Eide tells him to keep his mask on at all times.

When Caribert vanishes and the mirror is found broken, Eide and the Traveler search and find Caribert infused with Abyssal power. Eide reveals himself as Chlothar Alberich, addresses the Traveler as his "Prince" or "Princess" and declares he will use the Abyss to fight the gods. The Traveler sees themselves as the sibling in the mirror and collapses. Dainsleif and Paimon wake them, the remains of a man and a woman are dug up in the field, and Dainsleif leaves to confirm who the sinner is.
### LARGE
**Destined Encounter.** Masrur hands over Kaeya's letter, which was written so that delivery was guaranteed. At Djafar Tavern Kaeya has been drinking. Paimon expects him to be in Mondstadt, with Jean upset; he answers that he came to study Sumeru's spiced liquor. When the Traveler notes he is alone, he explains that as a boy he overheard Crepus discuss sending merchants to Sumeru, knew Khaenri'ah lay beneath it, and hid in the cargo before being caught. He says he knows little beyond books, that spending less time in Khaenri'ah left him caring less about it, and that he wonders whether his father wanted him to live happily in Mondstadt. After Dainsleif's announcement about the Alberichs, Kaeya says the conversation has become too serious and goes to meet a spice merchant. Dainsleif tells Paimon he does not know Kaeya well, and that while recovering he remembered the Loom of Fate operation the sibling mentioned when they travelled together in Sumeru. At the field in Avidya Forest he finds the surroundings familiar; after slimes appear he goes off alone to investigate the Ley Lines. Around the campfire the Traveler tells Paimon that they and the sibling used to look at the stars together and name worlds they wanted to visit, and that coming to Teyvat was the first time they had been separated.

**Fortune-Mocking Pedigree.** Eide fears the Traveler is from the Forest Rangers or the Akademiya. The Traveler recognises him as Khaenri'ahn from his features. He asks whether the Traveler believes in a god (the Traveler does not) and rants against the gods and their "gift" that ruined Khaenri'ah. Asked why some became hilichurls, he explains that one bloodline founded Khaenri'ah but all humans who rejected the gods were welcomed; the pure-blooded were cursed with immortality as the "greater sinners", the rest with the curse of the wilderness. He calls Caribert his illegitimate son with a woman from Mondstadt, born when he was dissatisfied with his life as a noble, and will not speak of the mother. He makes a medicine from Sumeru Rose and Kalpalata Lotus that needs the blessing of one of the Seven. It fails, so they prepare another batch, and fungi spawn while he makes fertilizer, which the Traveler repels.

**A Lamenter at Fate's End.** A hilichurl without the scarf that Eide gave Caribert leads them to a sanctuary where hilichurls pray. A being of the Abyss, not part of the Abyss Order, guards it and is defeated. A Defiled Statue like those met before hangs in the air; Eide bows and a voice calling itself a sinner speaks. Eide wakes in awe, sure that the voice saved his son once the second dose works. Caribert wakes horrified at what he has become, and Eide tells him it is a fairy tale and he must keep the mask on.

**Portended Fate.** Searching again, the sanctuary and statue are gone. At the house the mirror is shattered and Caribert gone. Forest Rangers point the way; Caribert, infused with Abyssal power, removes the mask and the Traveler is knocked out. Waking, the Traveler hears Eide call himself Chlothar Alberich and sees the sibling's reflection. Dainsleif says he repaired the Abyss's disturbance of the Ley Lines. Digging the field reveals two sets of remains. Dainsleif explains that the sinner may have seen the Traveler, and parts ways until he can confirm the sinner's identity. The Traveler embraces Paimon.

### ULTRA +
Further details. Eide asks the Traveler to keep the Forest Rangers from discovering the hut while he works. The Abyssal being at the sanctuary is explicitly not a member of the Abyss Order. Kaeya's boyhood stowaway attempt ended when he was caught and returned to Mondstadt by the ear.

## revelations
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
- The Alberich clan, Kaeya's ancestors, founded the Abyss Order, according to Dainsleif.
- Khaenri'ah was founded by a single bloodline and welcomed humans who followed no gods; the pure-blooded were cursed with immortality, the rest turned into hilichurls (Eide's account).
- The man calling himself Eide is Chlothar Alberich, who disappeared a century after founding the Order, and the masked hilichurl Caribert is his son by a Mondstadt woman, as Chlothar says.
- A "sinner" speaks through a Defiled Statue to hilichurls; Dainsleif says he suspects who it is.
### LARGE +
- The hut and the Ley Lines of Avidya Forest are connected to the sibling's Loom of Fate operation.
- The Traveler sees themselves as the sibling in the mirror, and Chlothar treats them as the prince or princess he serves: the events at the hut replay what the sibling once lived through with Chlothar after Khaenri'ah's fall, the Traveler having been drawn into that memory through the Abyss-disturbed Ley Lines (the wiki's reading of the act, consistent with Dainsleif's remarks).

## characters
### STANDARD
The Traveler, Paimon, Dainsleif, Kaeya, Chlothar (Eide), Caribert, Masrur, a sinner's voice (Vedrfolnir, named in 4.7), the sibling (in the dream and mirror), Forest Rangers, and mentions of Crepus and Jean.

## consequences
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The Traveler and Dainsleif learn who founded the Abyss Order and that Kaeya's family name leads back to it. Dainsleif leaves to confirm the sinner's identity. Kaeya's attitude toward his origins is left open.
### LARGE +
Bedtime Story (4.7) names the sinner as Vedrfolnir and returns to Caribert and the Loom of Fate.
