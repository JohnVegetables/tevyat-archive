## summary
### LITE
Stillness, the Sublimation of Shadow is the second act of Chapter II of the Archon Quests, set in Inazuma and released in Version 2.0. It has only two quests. In the first, the Traveler tries to save Thoma from becoming the hundredth person stripped of a Vision under the Vision Hunt Decree, and is crushed in a duel by the Raiden Shogun. In the second, the Traveler and Paimon, now wanted criminals, find the Watatsumi Army's Resistance, help defend it from the Shogun's Army under Kujou Sara, and are invited to Watatsumi Island by Sangonomiya Kokomi.
### STANDARD +
The act is where the Traveler becomes Inazuma's most wanted criminal and a participant in the war rather than a visitor. It requires two Story Quest openers, one from the Grus Nivis Chapter and one from the Carassius Auratus Chapter, and by number of quests it is the shortest Archon Quest act in the game.

## plot
### LITE
At Komore Teahouse the Traveler learns that the Tenryou Commission has seized Thoma and will use him as the hundredth Vision Hunt victim at a public ceremony the Raiden Shogun will attend. The Traveler rescues him, is overpowered by the Shogun, and escapes. They then seek out the Resistance, the Watatsumi Army, and join it.
### STANDARD
**Amidst Stormy Judgment.** The Traveler and Paimon return to Komore Teahouse to hear about their next operation from Kamisato Ayaka and Thoma, but Thoma is missing. Ayaka arrives with news that the Tenryou Commission has taken him to the Statue of the Omnipresent God as the hundredth victim of the Vision Hunt Decree, to be marked with a public ceremony attended by the Raiden Shogun. Ayaka is ready to storm in alone, but the Traveler and Paimon point out that any direct action by her would endanger the Yashiro Commission's neutrality, and persuade her to let them go instead.

At the ceremony the Shogun sees the Traveler use Electro power without a Vision. She calls the Traveler an exception and the enemy of eternity, then draws them into an alternate dimension and easily incapacitates them. Thoma distracts her at the critical moment and escapes with the Traveler and Paimon. The Shogun orders the Traveler's arrest and says she will strike twice next time.

**In the Name of the Resistance.** Thoma sends the pair west to the Watatsumi Army, which leads the resistance against the Decree, while he hides at Komore Teahouse inside Yashiro Commission territory. The Resistance's location is secret, so the Traveler has to find it. On the way through the war zone they save a lone soldier, Teppei, from Shogunate troops. He takes them to General Gorou, warning that the Resistance is at a disadvantage because the Shogun's Army has Kujou Sara's leadership. Gorou, impressed by what the Traveler did at the ceremony, welcomes them.
### LARGE +
Teppei shows the Traveler around the camp and the Traveler helps some soldiers. A sudden attack by the Shogun's Army makes Teppei suspect a traitor in the camp, but he is persuaded to use his logistics skill to reinforce the weak points instead, and he does so while complaining that he cannot contribute on the battlefield or make a name for himself.

When the Traveler returns to report, Gorou has gone to the front to meet the Shogun's Army, which Kujou Sara leads in person. She says she has come only for the Traveler, and Gorou refuses to hand them over. Sara proposes a fight of honor, a ceremonial contest in which each side's finest warrior represents it before battle. Gorou explains that a win can raise a weaker side's morale, that Sara's real motive is to capture the Traveler if they lose, and that they need not accept. The Traveler accepts and wins consecutively, which heartens the Resistance, and the two armies clash. As the Shogun's Army gains ground, Sangonomiya Kokomi, the Resistance's leader, arrives with surprise mercenary reinforcements that include Beidou and Kaedehara Kazuha. Sara orders a retreat, and Kokomi invites the Traveler to Watatsumi Island to plan the counterattack.

## revelations
@version: 2.0
### STANDARD
The Raiden Shogun treats the Traveler's Electro power without a Vision as a threat in itself, and shows that she can overpower the Traveler almost without effort. The Resistance is led by Kokomi, the Watatsumi Army's leader, and the Shogunate's forces are commanded in the field by Kujou Sara. Yashiro Commission neutrality limits what Ayaka can do openly.
### LARGE +
The Shogun's phrase "strike twice" sets up her next encounter with the Traveler in the following act, and the Traveler's survival here depends on Thoma's distraction, not on their own strength.

## characters
### STANDARD
**Traveler and Paimon**: now criminals in Inazuma, they move from the Kamisato side of events to the Resistance's side.
**Kamisato Ayaka and Thoma**: Ayaka is held back by her commission's neutrality; Thoma is the intended hundredth victim and is saved by the Traveler, then lies low at Komore Teahouse.
**Raiden Shogun**: appears in person at the ceremony, defeats the Traveler in an alternate space and orders their arrest.
**Teppei**: a Resistance soldier rescued by the Traveler, who guides them and wishes to prove himself.
**Gorou**: the Resistance's commanding general, who refuses to give up the Traveler.
**Kujou Sara**: leads the Shogun's Army against the Resistance and challenges the Traveler to a fight of honor.
**Sangonomiya Kokomi**: the Resistance's leader, who arrives with reinforcements and invites the Traveler to Watatsumi Island.
### LARGE +
Beidou and Kaedehara Kazuha appear among the mercenary reinforcements Kokomi brings, so they enter the story as fighters hired for the Resistance rather than as members of the Watatsumi Army.

## consequences
### STANDARD
The Traveler and Paimon are wanted by the Shogunate, so they join the Resistance. The Shogun's Army retreats from this clash. The way is open for Act III, in which the Traveler meets Kokomi on Watatsumi Island and becomes active in the Resistance's operations (see Omnipresence Over Mortals).

## placement
### STANDARD
Released in Version 2.0 as the second act of Chapter II, following The Immovable God and the Eternal Euthymia and followed by Omnipresence Over Mortals (Version 2.1). The act requires the player to have completed Act I of the Grus Nivis Chapter (The Whispers of the Crane and the White Rabbit) and Act I of the Carassius Auratus Chapter (Dreamlike Timelessness).

## entry: The Hundredth Vision Hunt Victim

@version: 2.0
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Thoma is seized by the Tenryou Commission to be the hundredth person whose Vision is confiscated under the Vision Hunt Decree, in a public ceremony at the Statue of the Omnipresent God where the Raiden Shogun appears personally. The Traveler and Paimon intervene because Ayaka cannot without compromising the Yashiro Commission's neutrality.
### LARGE +
The ceremony fails to go as planned: the Shogun's attention turns to the Traveler's Electro, Thoma helps the Traveler escape, and the Decree's centrepiece event ends with the Traveler becoming a fugitive.

## entry: Fight of Honor
@keys: fight of honor, honor duel, ceremonial contest
@keys2: Kujou Sara, Gorou
@version: 2.0
### STANDARD
A ceremonial contest before battle in which each side puts forward its best warrior. Gorou explains that a win can give a weaker side a boost in morale. Sara offers it intending to capture the Traveler if they lose. The Traveler's consecutive wins encourage the Resistance.

## entry: Teppei at the Resistance Camp
@keys: Teppei
@keys2: Resistance, Gorou
@version: 2.0
### STANDARD
Teppei is the Resistance soldier the Traveler rescues from Shogunate troops. He guides the pair, shows them the camp and reinforces its weak points when an attack makes him suspect a traitor. He is frustrated that his logistics work does not let him fight. His fate unfolds in the next act and is not part of this act.

## trivia
### ULTRA
By number of quests (two), this is the shortest Archon Quest act in the game. Its Chinese and Japanese titles both translate roughly as "Stillness & Nothingness, the Shattering of Mirage", which differs from the English wording.
