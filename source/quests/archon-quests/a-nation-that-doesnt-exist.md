## summary
### LITE
The third act of the Song of the Welkin Moon chapter. With Rerir's body restored, the master of the Wild Hunt drains kuuvahki from Nod-Krai and attacks Columbina, and the Traveler and their allies are forced to hide and prepare. Nefer's ceremony shows Rerir's life as a Khaenri'ahn, and Arlecchino, a fellow Fatui Harbinger, opens a path to the last Moon Marrow, the Iridescent one.
### STANDARD +
The act has three quests: Hidden in the Shadows, Special Operation, and Everlasting As the Moon. It follows Elegy of Dust and Lamplight and precedes An Elegy for Faded Moonlight.
### LARGE +
It shows Rerir, the Rächer of Solnari and one of the Five Sinners of Khaenri'ah, directly as an opponent too strong for the party, and it is the act in which the Fatui figures Sandrone and Arlecchino choose to aid the Traveler and Columbina.

## plot
### STANDARD
After the Wild Hunt strikes Nasha Town's quarry and leaves it drained of kuuvahki, Rerir attacks Columbina. The Traveler and Columbina escape and are sheltered by Varka, while Nefer is hired to help. Columbina proposes finding the Iridescent Marrow, the last of three, to restore her power; Sandrone lets them into the Bureau and the Wild Hunt attacks it. Nefer's ceremony shows Rerir as a Khaenri'ahn who had proposed to Tholindis. Arlecchino rescues the Traveler and Columbina at Thunderclap Reef, opens a portal to the Marrow's domain, and her hands are corrupted by forcing the exit open.
### LARGE +
After Rerir's physical form is restored, the Traveler and Paimon wonder what he wants. In Nasha Town the Ratnik Radimir says the Wild Hunt hit the quarry and Flins wants to see them. There Aino, Ineffa, Jahoda and Lauma note the area's kuuvahki is fully drained. Believing Rerir is hunting other kuuvahki-rich places, the group splits to check them, then regroups at Hiisi Island. Jahoda dares Rerir to appear, and the ground shakes; Lauma realises he is attacking Columbina. Lauma orders Jahoda to take Columbina away, but Rerir overpowers them and fires at Jahoda. Columbina shoves her aside and is grazed. She and the Traveler touch hands, and an explosion teleports them away. Rerir summons the Wild Hunt to find her.

Columbina tells the Traveler she cannot teleport them again. The Wild Hunt finds them; after they fight off ghouls, a Wolf's Gravestone slams down and Varka arrives, taking them to a secret base. He says he and his men were misled to Lake Amsvartnir, though he doubts Rerir could be in both places. Columbina proposes finding the last Moon Marrow, the Iridescent Marrow, so that with all three she can restore her power faster; she needs to visit the Kuuvahki Experimental Design Bureau and takes the Traveler. Varka introduces Nefer as the current owner of the Northern Intelligence Network. She refuses to work for free; Lauma offers payment and Jahoda asks her to take it from her own pay, and Nefer reluctantly accepts and asks Flins for the fragments of Rerir's heart. Aino and Ineffa will build decoys with Columbina's traces; Jahoda and Lauma go with Nefer.

At the bureau, Fevroniya and Ozerov threaten to call security until Sandrone lets them pass. Columbina asks for Arlecchino, whom Sandrone says has not been around since the facility was finished. Sandrone, annoyed by Columbina's past intervention but recalling her tea parties, agrees to help and has Pulonia ask Fevroniya to send a message to Arlecchino. The bureau shakes: the Wild Hunt attacks. Sandrone sends the Traveler and Columbina out by a secret passage while her landcruisers fight. Rerir arrives, no longer senses Columbina, and when Sandrone sets Pulonia on him he incapacitates it with a flick of the hands.

At the Curatorium of Secrets Nefer asks Jahoda to bring the "shady merchant" she met, who turns out to be Dori. Nefer calls in a favour for Dori's flower-shaped object, which Dori reluctantly lets her have for three days before she starts charging. Nefer then holds a ceremony using Rerir's heart fragments. Jahoda, Lauma and Nefer glimpse his past: a Khaenri'ahn under Irmin who had proposed to a woman named Tholindis, whose duty was to eliminate descendants of the Crimson Moon Dynasty and who did not tell her. They also see a glimpse of Tholindis' memory, and Nefer is puzzled how it came to be in Rerir's possession. The time runs out.

In the last quest the Traveler and Columbina are cornered by the Wild Hunt at Thunderclap Reef until the ghouls burn away: Arlecchino has come. At the bureau Sandrone repairs her landcruisers with Freminet's help while Lyney keeps Lynette away from the machines; one collapses when Lynette touches it. Arlecchino brings the pair to the secret base, where the others tense at who she is, and says she is present only on a personal level and can locate the Iridescent Marrow. On a peninsula between Silvermoon Hall and Hiisi Island she tells the Traveler she is a descendant of the Crimson Moon and learned of her past through Pierro. She agrees to Columbina's terms and opens a portal to the Marrow's domain, warning them to ignore the voices. Inside, the Traveler and Columbina retrieve it as riftwolves rush them and the exit seals; Arlecchino forces it open and her hands are corrupted by defying the Abyss. She declines the Traveler's offer to purify it, since that would destroy her hands. They share the power with Columbina and Arlecchino gives her the Marrow. Columbina hopes she will not be corrupted by the Abyss as Rerir was; Arlecchino says she intends to be, and suggests Columbina change her name once Rerir is dealt with.
Planning details. Nefer takes the job only under pressure from Lauma's payment and Jahoda's request that it come from her own pay. Aino and Ineffa's decoys are meant to draw the Wild Hunt away from Columbina. Columbina explains why they need Arlecchino's help, and Arlecchino agrees to the terms; the terms themselves are not spelled out in the summary.
### ULTRA +
The bureau's guards defer to Sandrone at once. Dori's flower-shaped object is lent to Nefer's ceremony in exchange for a favour. Lynette's contact with a landcruiser, and Lyney's attempts to keep her away from the machines, give the act a light moment between the fights.

## revelations
@version: 6.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Rerir was a Khaenri'ahn under Irmin who had proposed to Tholindis, and whose secret duty was to kill Crimson Moon descendants. Arlecchino is a descendant of the Crimson Moon Dynasty and learned of it through Pierro. The final Moon Marrow is the Iridescent Marrow, kept in an Abyss-guarded domain reachable through a portal.
### LARGE +
Columbina's strength is fading, and she believes holding all three Marrows will restore it much faster. Defying the Abyss corrupts Arlecchino's hands. Rerir has grown too powerful for the party and for Pulonia, and the quarry the Wild Hunt struck was left completely drained of kuuvahki.

## characters
### STANDARD
Traveler and Paimon with Lauma, Jahoda, Aino, Ineffa, Flins, Varka, Nefer, Columbina, Sandrone (aiding), Arlecchino (aiding, with Lyney, Lynette and Freminet at the bureau), Dori, Pulonia and Rerir (antagonist).

## consequences
### STANDARD
Columbina now holds the Iridescent Marrow, with its power shared to her, while Rerir remains at large. Arlecchino's hands are corrupted. The story continues in An Elegy for Faded Moonlight.
