## summary
### LITE
Act X of Song of the Welkin Moon, released in version 6.6 as the Sumeru climax of the Nod-Krai-and-Sumeru Archon chapter. Irminsul has been corrupted by Dottore's experiment, which now grows a vast tree over Sumeru City. Nahida, the Traveler, the Wanderer, the Seven Pillars and Sumeru's friends overload Irminsul through the Akasha System, then burn it down with the Pyro Gnosis, and Dottore dies with it.
### STANDARD +
The act has four quests: Darkness Looming; A Tower Unto Wisdom and Folly; Truth Amongst the Pages of Purana; and Your Past Is Like a Garden. It is the Sumeru plot's resolution, closing threads that began in Chapter III: Irminsul's disease, the Akasha System, King Deshret's legacy and Collei's god remains. The act's title is initially shown as Of Myriad Paths, Flux, and Dissolution and is replaced during Darkness Looming.
### LARGE +
Irminsul is destroyed in this act, not repaired: Aaru, the Deshret-era system re-opened in Act IX, takes over its work of collecting and storing information. The Traveler, being unaffected by Irminsul, walks out through the barrier around the sealed nation and back in with the Pyro Gnosis; because Irminsul cannot record them, Dottore cannot learn of the meeting through it (Mavuika, connected to the Night Kingdom, is likewise unrecorded while in Natlan). The act ends with a calm epilogue and a diplomatic letter from Snezhnaya.

## plot
### LITE
The Seven Pillars of Deshret and the scholars Kaveh and Alhaitham prepare to use Aaru, while Nahida learns that her attacker entered Irminsul through death and that Dottore's experiment has raised a huge Irminsul tree in Sumeru City. In a secret meeting at the Natlan border she asks Mavuika for the Pyro Gnosis, which the Traveler carries back. The next day they overload Irminsul through the Akasha, the Traveler and the Wanderer go inside to face Dottore, and Nahida burns the tree with the Gnosis. In an epilogue Collei, now a full ranger, has the god remains removed by the jinn Liloupar.
### STANDARD +
In outline: (1) Darkness Looming: the Pillars hand Aaru to the scholars, Nahida learns her attacker entered Irminsul through death, and a huge Irminsul tree rises in Sumeru City, tied to Dottore; (2) A Tower Unto Wisdom and Folly: a chess trial opens Aaru, and at the Natlan border the Traveler fetches the Pyro Gnosis from Mavuika; (3) Truth Amongst the Pages of Purana: Irminsul is overloaded through the Akasha, the Traveler and Wanderer face Dottore inside it, and Nahida burns it with the Gnosis while Dottore is dragged to death; (4) Your Past Is Like a Garden: Sumeru recovers, Snezhnaya sends a letter, the Gnosis has vanished, and Collei has her god remains removed.
### LARGE +
Darkness Looming: Kaveh gives the Seven Pillars a draft; Alhaitham's rudeness irritates Bennu, but Sobek and Thoth talk him down and Bennu hands over Aaru's mainframe with a warning. Thoth shows Kaveh and Alhaitham the system and asks them to close their eyes as he chants, letting them see more of its structure. Apep delivers Nahida to the mausoleum, having flung off the pests that tugged her tail; they are Aranara riding with the Pari Sorush, who then falls and is caught by the Traveler. The Aranara Arabalika reports seeing two suspicious Nara at Caravan Ribat. Nefer poses as a lost merchant to bait the spy, Qanafiya of the Corps of Thirty, and Cyno intercepts. Wanderer and Paimon scout the meeting place and Wanderer, finding no contact, senses a problem. Nahida reports that her attacker entered Irminsul through death, a danger she could not have blocked, and a huge Irminsul tree rises in Sumeru City. She links it to Dottore and sets out a new plan.

A Tower Unto Wisdom and Folly: Cyno, Sethos and Wanderer take apart Primal Constructs for parts. Kaveh and Alhaitham find a chessboard trial that will override Aaru and clear it. The Traveler goes to the sealed Natlan-Sumeru border and meets Mavuika, with Nahida speaking through a manifestation; Nahida explains that only the Traveler, who cannot be recorded, was sent so that Dottore, who has access to Irminsul's knowledge through the ley lines, would not learn the plan. Nahida asks to claim ownership of the Pyro Gnosis, not merely borrow it; Mavuika agrees. The Traveler walks through the barrier, absorbs the Gnosis through their command of Pyro, and walks back.

Truth Amongst the Pages of Purana: The Akasha System is used to overload Irminsul and stop the tree from growing. Dottore, inside Irminsul, is amused. In Sumeru City Pantalone, the hooded man seen since Gurabad, learns that his spy was caught; he and Dottore speak of Segments, debts and Northland Bank. Cyno, Dehya, Candace, Jeht, Rahman and Rana fight into the city as a distraction while Tighnari, Wanderer, Paimon and the Traveler climb the Divine Tree to the Sanctuary of Surasthana. Cyno exposes the Pantalone stalling them as an immaterial decoy. The Traveler and Wanderer enter Irminsul, guided by Nicole's voice, until Dottore appears in person and reveals he knows Wanderer's erased identity as Scaramouche. While the Wanderer distracts Dottore, the Traveler places Nahida's trinket in the ley lines and the world's information is redirected to Aaru. Nahida enters Irminsul and blocks Dottore's attack. Dottore drops the three into the depths, Nahida calls the Pyro Gnosis out of the Traveler, and in the presence of the Shades Ronova, Istaroth and Naberius she gains their permission to destroy the corrupted tree. The Gnosis burns Irminsul's roots; Dottore tries to drag the Traveler down but his own Segments drag him into the fire. Outside, he bids Pantalone farewell, calling him by his real name, Feofan, and Nahida says they will not meet again.

Your Past Is Like a Garden: Sumeru begins to rebuild. The Pillars Thoth and Heryshaf stay in the Temple of Silence. Collei is made a full Forest Ranger but still needs treatment. Nahida explains the Gnosis took seven days and nights to burn Irminsul, that Aaru now does Irminsul's job without lasting damage, and that Nicole's dependence has been transferred to it. Naphis brings a letter from Pulcinella apologising for Dottore, noting the Harbinger Sandrone's death, and promising relief supplies. In Natlan, Nahida tells Mavuika the Pyro Gnosis has vanished. In Mondstadt, at Windrise, Liloupar asks Collei whether she wishes to have the god remains removed; Collei agrees, Liloupar absorbs them and fades, and Collei says she has said goodbye to her old self.
### ULTRA +
Darkness Looming, details: Nahida thanks the Seven Pillars after learning the attacker entered through death; Heryshaf says the attacker feels familiar. The plan assigns roles: Kaveh and Alhaitham continue on Aaru, Cyno provides material support, Wanderer and Sethos deliver it, Dori, Faruzan, Collei and Nicole build an energy amplifier, and the Traveler goes to the border. Aranara tell Nahida their home stands firm and she tells them they must show themselves to humans, or call her name if frightened. Paimon admits to Wanderer that she feels useless; he says he knows the feeling and that there are two choices, staying or moving forward.

A Tower Unto Wisdom and Folly, details: Cyno, Sethos and Wanderer dismantle several Primal Constructs, including an Algorithm of Semi-Intransient Matrix of Overseer Network, and bring the parts to the scholars. After the trial, Alhaitham asks for a greater range and Thoth's side agrees. Before the operation everyone shares last thoughts with friends. Mavuika says she had wondered about handing the Gnosis to someone who needed it more; she asks for no more detail for tactical security.

Truth Amongst the Pages of Purana, details: Nahida asks the Pillars to bring her to the Eternal Oasis, and Thoth asks Nefer for the chessboard he gave her, revealing a new function. Pantalone informs Dottore that his last backup is in the ley lines and they are even for his fees; Dottore says Pantalone's life has been thanks to him. Pantalone mentions the crushing defeat of Dottore's other half in Nod-Krai; Dottore dismisses it. Dottore tells the Wanderer he has no intent to restore the erased information but that his regrets would never be absolved. Nicole's guidance is cut off before Dottore appears. Dottore says his supply of elixirs, which Pantalone depends on, will end with him.

Your Past Is Like a Garden, details: Sethos, Dehya and Candace part at Caravan Ribat. The Pillars decline to move their statues into the Temple of Silence out of respect for Hermanubis. Alhaitham and Kaveh return to repair the damage to their home. Dori complains of a loss to Nefer, who reunites with Katayoun. The Traveler and Paimon eat at Lambad's Tavern, then meet Nahida and Wanderer at the Sanctuary of Surasthana; Wanderer leaves to run errands. Nahida agrees to the Akademiya's feasts if there are no tomatoes. Nahida, the Traveler and Mavuika lie on grass atop a Natlan cliff. Two days later Collei, Tighnari and Cyno go to Mondstadt; Sucrose, Albedo, Amber and Nicole are there. Nicole is happy to grant wishes for her friends.
Gameplay note from the wiki: the quest is a Special Story Mode with trial parties (Cyno/Candace/Dehya in the city, Tighnari/Wanderer/Traveler (Pyro) at the Sanctuary, and Wanderer with the Traveler inside Irminsul). On the map the red mist of the corrupted ley lines over central Sumeru clears after the first step.

## revelations
### LITE
Dottore's experiment has made Irminsul a deliberate weapon; the corrupted tree is destroyed with the Pyro Gnosis, with the Shades' consent, and Aaru replaces it.
### STANDARD +
Nahida's attacker entered Irminsul through death, which cannot be guarded against except by blocking all souls' free access to the ley lines. The Irminsul tree that rose in Sumeru City is a new stage of Dottore's experiment. Dottore has direct access to Irminsul's knowledge through the ley lines, so the plan had to bypass recording. The Traveler is not recorded by Irminsul and can pass the barrier. Dottore has deduced that the Wanderer's erased past is that of Scaramouche. The three Shades Ronova, Istaroth and Naberius can grant the keeper permission to destroy Irminsul. Irminsul took seven days and nights to burn.
### LARGE +
Pantalone, the Ninth Harbinger, has been the hooded man seen since Act IX. In his farewell Dottore addresses Pantalone by his real name, Feofan ("Goodbye, Feofan"). Collei's god remains came from Nabu Malikata (Act IX). Pulcinella's letter says Dottore also killed the Harbinger Sandrone.
### ULTRA +
Nahida argues that Irminsul exists to protect humanity, so humanity's survival (and her own as a protector) outweighs the tree. Naberius agrees she has herself become a great tree.

## characters
### LITE
The Traveler, Paimon, Nahida, Wanderer, Dottore and Pantalone.
### STANDARD +
The Seven Pillars (Thoth, Heryshaf, Sobek, Bennu, Shesepankh, Apep and Hermanubis's representative Cyno), Kaveh, Alhaitham, Cyno, Nefer, Nicole, Tighnari, Collei, Dehya, Candace, Sethos, Jeht, Rahman, Rana, Dori, Faruzan, Mavuika, Sorush and the Aranara, Qanafiya of the Corps of Thirty, Ronova, Istaroth, Naberius, Liloupar, Naphis and, in the epilogue, Sucrose, Albedo and Amber.
### LARGE +
Layla, Katayoun and Nilou appear briefly; Pulcinella and Sandrone are named in the closing letter.

## consequences
### STANDARD
Irminsul is burned; the Akasha-linked system Aaru now collects and stores the world's information without lasting harm. Dottore is presented as dead. The Pyro Gnosis is gone. Collei no longer carries the god remains. Sumeru begins to rebuild and Snezhnaya sends a carefully worded apology and relief supplies, which Nahida and Naphis agree to watch.
### LARGE +
The act is followed in release order by Chapter VII, Everwinter Without Mercy (7.0), in Snezhnaya.

## placement
### STANDARD
Song of the Welkin Moon Act X, after As All Falls to Emptiness (Act IX) and before the Snezhnaya chapter. Released in 6.6 (Luna VII, 2026-05-20). Adventure Rank 40 per the wiki. The title also names Chapter III in a storyline preview, which is a different thing.
