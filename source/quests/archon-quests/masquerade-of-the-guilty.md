## summary
@spoiler: major
### LITE
Masquerade of the Guilty is Act V of Chapter IV, the climax of the Fontaine Archon Quest. The Primordial Sea erupts at Poisson, the Traveler and Neuvillette's allies trap and try Furina to force the truth about the prophecy, and the trial ends with the revelation that the real Hydro Archon, Focalors, hid in plain sight for five centuries. She sacrifices herself so that Neuvillette, the Hydro Dragon Sovereign, regains his authority and Fontaine's people survive the flood.
### STANDARD
Act V has six quests: Deluge of Wrathful Waters, Meeting Is Also Parting, Hunters, Prophets, Apocalypse, The Opera of Noirceur and Blancheur, and Finale. It requires Adventure Rank 40 and gives Fontaine reputation.

Water from the Primordial Sea bursts out at Poisson and dissolves residents, including Navia's companions Melus and Silver. Stone slates found in nearby ruins match the prophecy. Neuvillette concludes that only a trial can make Furina speak, and a staged trial in the Opera Epiclese proves her "a fraud". The Oratrice's verdict then condemns the Hydro Archon to death, an All-Devouring Narwhal surges from a rift, and Focalors executes herself, returning her authority to Neuvillette and absolving Fontaine's people so they are immune to the sea.
### LARGE +
The act resolves the prophecy plot that runs through the whole of Chapter IV. Its announcement text frames the answer to the Heavenly Principles' judgement as "a spin, a hop, and a skip" and a final bow by the sinner, rather than bowing one's head and waiting for death.

### ULTRA +
Reading notes. The act deliberately separates three trials: Navia's dream trial (a trap meant to keep her), the staged trial that proves Furina is not the Hydro Archon, and the Oratrice's verdict on "the Hydro Archon", which is the one Focalors uses. Neuvillette's reading of the slates corrects the Traveler's first hypothesis (that the water in the third slate was the Primordial Sea): it symbolises justice, and the people putting their Archon on trial is itself the prophecy's third image. He reads the fourth slate, the Archon alone weeping on her throne, as foretelling the narwhal.

## plot
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
**Deluge of Wrathful Waters.** The Traveler and Paimon wake in Meropide at the end of their 45-day sentence when a tremor shakes the prison. Neuvillette says the tremor came from Poisson. There they find Navia and the Spina di Rosula caring for survivors; water from the Primordial Sea dissolved people on the lower levels, and Melus and Silver, who went to rescue others, are among the dead. Arlecchino has lent Fatui help, and asks the Traveler to look at ruins near Poisson that a child found.

**Meeting Is Also Parting.** In the flooded ruins Navia falls into the seawater and dreams of an Opera Epiclese trial where Melus and Silver, as if waking, urge her not to admit guilt. Neuvillette rescues her; he believes two Oceanids shielded her. On the walls are stone slates echoing the prophecy, with the first missing. The Traveler and Neuvillette note that the second slate shows people kneeling before a sky island, with Egeria as the kneeling figure, and that the order of images is wrong.

**Hunters, Prophets.** At Navia's base, Mona (in Fontaine on business, and invited by The Steambird to a panel on the prophecy) and later Mage "N" help the Traveler. Furina refuses to share what she knows and orders Neuvillette never to mention the incident again; Neuvillette then plans a trial. After a riot at the opera over her inaction, Furina disappears.

**Apocalypse.** The Traveler finds Furina in Poisson, and she begins to confide, but the house collapses to reveal a stage trap prepared by Lyney, Navia, Neuvillette and others. The Traveler prosecutes Furina on a charge of fraud. A dilute sample of Primordial Seawater harms her like other Fontainians and the court deems her guilty, but the Oratrice sentences "the Hydro Archon" to death. Freminet then brings the missing first slate, from which Neuvillette explains the truth: Egeria turned her Oceanid followers into humans using the Primordial Sea, so Fontaine's people dissolve in it, and Celestia sentences the Archon for stealing that power. A giant narwhal emerges, Childe fights it in his Foul Legacy form, and the sentence begins to take effect as one of Furina's tears sends the Traveler and Neuvillette into separate realms.

**The Opera of Noirceur and Blancheur.** Focalors tells Neuvillette her plan and executes herself. Neuvillette absolves the people of their sin, and with the Traveler subdues the narwhal in the Primordial Sea. Skirk appears, seals the beast and throws it and Childe into a rift. The flood passes without harm to the people.

**Finale.** Fontaine rebuilds. Furina abdicates the Archon's duties.
### LARGE +
Details of Deluge and Meeting: Navia asks the Traveler to see her father's grave with her; Melus and Silver had asked to be buried beside Callas. Arlecchino has set the Fatui to relief work (supplies, water levels, headcounts). The Traveler and Navia cross a bridge that gives way; Navia falls. In her dream, a prosecutor named Joinville declares her guilty and the audience demands she "join" them; Melus and Silver defend her by accusing the crowd of jealousy that she can still live as herself. Neuvillette believes he saw two Oceanids protecting Navia, giving him the seconds he needed.

Details of Hunters, Prophets: Mona says her master is the astromancer Barbeloth Trismegistus and that she was born in Mondstadt; The Steambird invited her to a panel on the prophecy and she says the Hexenzirkel could decipher it. N's visit, as a tea set down by an unseen person, includes the line that even the gods' gaze has blind spots. Paimon asks Sedene and Charlotte what they would do if Fontaine flooded; both say they would carry on with their work. The Traveler says they would keep journeying. The Marechaussee Phantom deals with the riot at the opera and Isadora brings word that Furina has gone.

Details of Apocalypse: Navia's men lured Furina to the opera and Poisson; the Fatui built a "Giant Magic Box" and tunnelled to the opera house; Clorinde stood ready to duel if Furina refused. Furina avoids the duel by raising both hands, then agrees to the trial. Charlotte's interviews supply evidence, and Sigewinne confirms Furina shows the symptoms of other Fontainians exposed to impure Primordial Seawater. Neuvillette explains the third slate shows people in water that stands for judgement, not the Primordial Sea, so it is the people putting their Archon on trial; the fourth slate foretells the narwhal, which wandered the Primordial Sea, drove the rising levels, and would then hunt the people of Fontaine. Childe's efforts delay it but cannot stop it for good.

Details of The Opera of Noirceur and Blancheur: Focalors appears to Neuvillette like an older, wiser twin of Furina. She was an Oceanid made human who, on ascending, found she could save her people only by outwitting the Heavenly Principles. She split her divinity from her human body, which lived on autonomously as Furina, and cursed Furina so she could neither die while the divinity existed nor pursue happiness, forcing her to play the role in the opera for 500 years. The Traveler witnesses Furina's past 500 years while Focalors explains. Neuvillette's authority as the Hydro Dragon is fully restored, and the Oratrice stops working, so he asks the Traveler to accompany him as his executor. Skirk is impressed that they beat the narwhal without the power of the Abyss. She asks about her master, whom she calls Surtalogi, about Vedrfolnir and about Rhinedottir, whom Paimon recognises as Albedo's creator.

Details of Finale: The Steambird prints free copies to calm the masses. Charlotte documents Poisson's rebuilding with help from the Fatui and Clorinde, then interviews Jurieu and Lourvine about the flying ship before a meeting with Lyney, Lynette and Freminet at Romaritime Harbor. Lyney says "Father" was given the Hydro Gnosis, Childe returned to Snezhnaya to recover, and the Fatui and Fontaine have allied for the first time. Arlecchino says that as a Harbinger it is her duty to give the Gnosis to the Tsaritsa; the Traveler asks her to return Childe's Vision. Neuvillette tells the Traveler everything outside Meropide, including that he deemed Fontainians "true humans", that Furina left the opera house and the related affairs to him but kept the title, and that he promises to see to her needs.

## revelations
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
- The prophecy is a sentence from the Heavenly Principles: Egeria "sinned" by using the Primordial Sea to make Oceanids human, so her people dissolve and the Hydro Archon is condemned.
- Furina and Focalors are one being split in two: Furina is the human body and spirit; Focalors is the divinity.
- Focalors was also originally an Oceanid.
- Focalors' plan was to outwit the Heavenly Principles, which accused her people, by letting the Hydro Archon be judged and executed; her self-execution fulfils the sentence and returns the authority to Neuvillette, who absolves Fontaine's people.
- Neuvillette is the Hydro Dragon Sovereign, restored to full power; Focalors' plan also destroys the Hydro Archon's divine throne.
### LARGE +
- Skirk says the Hydro Gnosis is the remains of the third Descender and recommends removing such "items of misfortune"; she does not know who that Descender was.
- The Traveler tells Neuvillette they are the fourth Descender.
- Skirk lists Surtalogi and others as pursuing "some form of perfection".

## characters
### LARGE
Furina and Focalors, Neuvillette, Navia, Melus and Silver (who die at Poisson and appear in Navia's dream), Clorinde, Lyney, Lynette, Freminet, Sigewinne, Charlotte, Sedene, Isadora, Mona, Mage "N", Childe (Tartaglia), Skirk, Arlecchino, and Paimon. Joinville appears only as a prosecutor in Navia's dream.

## consequences
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The prophecy is averted: Fontaine floods, but its people do not dissolve. The Oratrice stops working. Furina abdicates the Archon's role, leaving the nation's affairs, though not the title, to Neuvillette. Childe, who had ended up in the Primordial Sea, is flung into a rift by Skirk and returns to Snezhnaya to recover. The Hydro Gnosis passes to the Fatui through Arlecchino. Neuvillette predicts the Traveler's next destination will be Natlan.
### LARGE +
Act VI, "Bedtime Story", closes Chapter IV, and Chapter V takes the Traveler to Natlan. The alliance between Fontaine's investigators and the Fatui is first of its kind according to Paimon.

## placement
### STANDARD
Fifth act of Chapter IV, after Cataclysm's Quickening (Act IV) and before Bedtime Story (Act VI). Released in Version 4.2; the same name is used for the Chapter IV block in the travel log.
