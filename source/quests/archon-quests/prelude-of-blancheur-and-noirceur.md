## summary
### LITE
Chapter IV Act I of the Archon Quest, set in Fontaine. After leaving Sumeru, the Traveler and Paimon arrive in the Hydro nation, meet the Archon Furina, and are drawn into a courtroom drama as the defence attorney for the magician Lyney, accused after a stage trick ends with a man dead and an audience member missing.
### STANDARD +
The act has three quests: Soloist's Prologue, The City of Rains and Remembrance, and Lies Cast Shadows Under Gathered Lights. It introduces Fontaine's trial-centred society, the water-level prophecy, the Oratrice Mecanique d'Analyse Cardinale, the Fatui connection of Lyney and Lynette, and the investigators of the Spina di Rosula. Released in version 4.0.
### LARGE +
Tonally it is a courtroom mystery. The Traveler's "duel" with the Archon is a legal contest, not a fight, and the verdict is swayed by audience opinion as much as by evidence. The act sets up threads that later acts follow: Childe's strange loss of control over his Hydro, the missing-women case, and the Fatui's interest in Fontaine's prophecy.

## plot
### LITE
At the Caravan Ribat the Traveler says goodbye to Dehya and heads for Fontaine. Furina has them charged with a minor law, but Lyney rescues them with a stage trick. Later, during Lyney's show at the Court of Fontaine, a man dies and a woman vanishes; the Traveler defends Lyney, and the trial ends with the real events exposed.
### STANDARD +
**Soloist's Prologue.** Before leaving Sumeru, the Traveler and Paimon meet Dehya, who offers a mercenary escort to the border and is turned down. At the Romaritime Harbor the locals Rena and Etienne explain that the Court of Fontaine works like an opera house, that Neuvillette and the Oratrice have ended false charges, and that Furina can only be seen by appointment. The Traveler meets Lynette, who says an area tied to a past memory is now underwater and that "they will be, too", and then her brother Lyney. Furina arrives with her entourage, welcomes the Traveler in her role as Archon, and declares a "duel" in court; she then points to a law against releasing a flying object on the first three days of a month and names Paimon as the offending object. Lyney snaps his fingers and a red cord of energy binds Paimon to the Traveler, so Paimon counts as a balloon that arrived attached, and Furina drops the matter, amused. Lyney explains the prophecy of rising water and gives the Traveler Magic Pockets to hand to residents near the waterline. The pair help him chase a pickpocket at the harbour, and Charlotte joins them on the aquabus to the court.

**The City of Rains and Remembrance.** At the twins' home they meet Freminet. Rain falls, which Lyney says usually happens when a trial is held. At the Beaumont Workshop, Truand of the Confrerie of Cabriere picks a fight, and Childe (Tartaglia), now in Fontaine, beats him; but Childe's Hydro weapons vanish mid-fight, and he says he has felt something stir in him. He recounts his time in the Abyss and his teacher Skirk, mentions he has been sparring with Fontaine's Champion Duelists, and entrusts the Traveler with his Vision before a duel against Clorinde. At the Fountain of Lucine a voice is heard, and Lynette offers a theory about tears and the fountain. In the opera house the Traveler sits next to Neuvillette, whom Lyney introduces. During the show, Lyney's box trick goes wrong: the tank falls and crushes the box, and Neuvillette locks down the house. Cowell is found dead in the box and the chosen audience member has gone missing, apparently the latest victim in the disappearances of young women. Furina names Lyney a suspect, turns the case into her second "duel", and invites the Traveler to act as defence.

**Lies Cast Shadows Under Gathered Lights.** The Traveler gathers evidence, meeting the sceptical Esmond and Navia of the Spina di Rosula, with her associates Silver and Melus. Lyney and Lynette demonstrate the box trick, and the group explores the tunnel under the stage. At trial, Furina exposes the twins as members of the Fatui's House of the Hearth, and Lyney confirms it. After a recess in which Lyney gives his account, the defence argues that Cowell used Primordial Sea water to change form. Esmond reports that Cowell's bag held labelled tubes of that water and that he belonged to a drug-selling organisation; Vaughn then produces the same water from Lyney's belongings. Navia arrives with the missing woman, who reappears from the magic box at Lyney's snap. She gives her name as Liliane (the harbour thief, not "Halsey") and confesses: terrified when the machine picked her and Lyney spoke of the Fortress of Meropide, she forced her way out of a soaked box, smashed a vase over Cowell's head in the confrontation, put him in the box, and hid in a costume chest. The Oratrice returns "Not Guilty" for the twins. Vaughn, exposed as Cowell's accomplice for presenting false evidence, begins to confess but dissolves into water before naming the "Boss". Afterwards Lyney explains the twins' past and apologises for the deception; Navia and her associates invite the Traveler to a farewell meal at Hotel Debord.
### LARGE +
Details worth keeping straight: the missing-persons case Charlotte mentions concerns young women vanishing over some twenty years; Navia found Liliane partly through the "extra macarons" Paimon swore she had not taken, which pointed to someone hungry and hiding in the opera house. Neuvillette also says the conduct of both Lyney and Liliane warrants further investigation, so the verdict is not a full clearing of everything. Lynette's cryptic remarks and the unexplained voice at the fountain are left unresolved in this act.
### ULTRA +
Structural note: each of the three quests ends on an escalation (the arrest threat, the crushed box, the exposed Fatui link), and each "duel" is rhetorical. The first is won for the Traveler by Lyney's technicality about whether Paimon counts as an object; in the second the Traveler's Primordial Sea hypothesis shifts the scales, but the case is only won when Navia produces Liliane. The audience's reaction is shown on a scale that visibly tilts during the trial, so persuasion of the crowd, not only logic, shapes how events are received in Fontaine's court.

## revelations
@version: 4.0
@spoiler: major
### LITE
Lyney and Lynette belong to the Fatui's House of the Hearth.
### STANDARD +
The twins are orphans who joined the House of the Hearth under the Knave, Arlecchino, who believes in the prophecy of Fontaine and intends to try to use the Hydro Gnosis to save Fontaine before taking it to Snezhnaya. A drug-selling organisation uses Primordial Sea water, and its member Vaughn dissolves into water on trying to name the "Boss". Childe is in Fontaine and his Hydro Vision is behaving oddly.
### LARGE +
Lyney's account of his history: after their parents died the twins lived on the streets, surviving on magic tricks Lyney learned by secretly watching an older street magician. An aristocrat took them in for Lyney's talent, then sent Lynette as a "gift" to an "eminent person" he met at a banquet. When Lyney broke into that person's mansion, the Knave had already killed him and rescued Lynette and several hidden orphan girls; she then offered Lyney a place in the House of the Hearth and also destroyed the noble who had first taken them in. Lyney says the Knave has the Tsaritsa's permission to use the Gnosis's power first, to find a way to break the prophecy. Childe also describes being taken as an apprentice by Skirk in the Abyss.

## characters
### LITE
Traveler and Paimon; Furina; Neuvillette; Lyney and Lynette; Navia.
### STANDARD +
Also Dehya (farewell), Freminet, Clorinde (the Champion Duelist), Charlotte (reporter), Childe, Esmond, Cowell, Vaughn, Liliane, Truand, Estelle, Rena and Etienne, and Navia's associates Silver and Melus.

## consequences
### STANDARD
Lyney and Lynette are acquitted but remain under suspicion for their Fatui ties. The Traveler has Childe's Vision in safekeeping. The act leads into Act II, As Light Rain Falls Without Reason, in which events around Childe unfold.
### LARGE +
The disappearances case is not closed: Liliane is a thief and not the abductor, and Vaughn's dissolution leaves the "Boss" unnamed.

## placement
### STANDARD
Chapter IV Act I, following Caribert (the end of Chapter III in Sumeru) and before Act II, As Light Rain Falls Without Reason. Released in version 4.0.
