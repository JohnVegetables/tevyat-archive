## summary
### LITE
Wanderer's Trail is the first quest of Genshin Impact's story. An opening cinematic shows the Traveler and their twin stopped by an unknown god who seals the Traveler and takes the sibling away. The Traveler then appears on a Mondstadt beach, tells Paimon that they intend to find out how long they were sealed, and follows Paimon toward a Statue of The Seven. It doubles as a movement tutorial.
### STANDARD +
It belongs to the Prologue and Act I, "The Outlander Who Caught the Wind", and was part of Version 1.0. The next quest is Bird's Eye View. The quest starts at Stormbearer Coast in Mondstadt and its tutorial teaches basic movement, climbing and stamina.

## plot
### LITE
The Traveler and their twin, who travel from world to world, are blocked by an unnamed god in white who says that humanity's arrogance ends now. The twins lose the fight, the sibling is turned into a cube and taken, and the Traveler is also sealed. On a beach, the Traveler tells Paimon they lost their power, were trapped in this world and awoke alone until meeting Paimon two months ago. They head for a Statue of The Seven.
### STANDARD
The opening cinematic is framed as the Traveler telling their story to Paimon: they fell into this world from another, and when they tried to move on, an unknown god barred the way. He names himself the sustainer of heavenly principles and says that the arrogation of mankind ends now. The twins fight him and are overpowered; the sibling is swallowed by a swarm of red and black cubes and condensed into one cube held in the god's hand. The Traveler tries to take their sibling back and is engulfed too, then the scene cuts to black.

On the beach, the Traveler explains that some seal robbed them of their power, so that they who once crossed world after world are now trapped. They do not know how many years have passed but resolve to find out. They woke alone, and only met Paimon two months earlier; Paimon says she would likely have drowned otherwise and promises to be a good guide.

The player then follows Paimon, defeats an optional Hydro Slime, unlocks a Teleport Waypoint, and comes to the foot of a cliff where there is no path. Paimon prompts the player to climb, notes that climbing uses stamina that needs time to recover, and says the planned route leads to a Statue of The Seven. She asks which of The Seven the Traveler is looking for, which leads into Act I.
### LARGE +
The quest's steps are: go to Paimon, follow her, unlock the Teleport Waypoint, go to the foot of the cliff, and climb the rock face. The Hydro Slime is optional and does not respawn if killed. The quest's in-game description says the god took the Traveler's only kin, and that after waking they wandered alone before meeting a strange companion named Paimon. Rewards listed by the wiki are Adventure EXP, Mora and Character EXP.
### ULTRA +
Details of the scene: the wiki transcript has the Traveler shout their sibling's name and demand them back before being engulfed, and the Traveler later says the seal took their power so that they can no longer travel between worlds. The player's choice of Aether or Lumine changes only the sibling's name and a few pronouns in the lines. Paimon's closing question about which of The Seven the Traveler seeks is left open here, so this quest has no resolution of its own.

## revelations
@version: 1.0
### LITE
The Traveler has a twin who was taken by a god, was themself sealed and lost their power, and is searching for their sibling. The two had travelled between worlds before being stopped.
### STANDARD +
Paimon has known the Traveler for two months, since they saved her from drowning. The god's title, sustainer of heavenly principles, is the only description of him given here; the Prologue and later Archon Quests develop what that means.

## characters
### LITE
The Traveler (Aether or Lumine according to the player's choice, named in the cinematic only through their sibling), Paimon, and the unnamed god who is later identified as Asmoday.
### STANDARD +
The twin is shown fighting beside the Traveler but is never heard speaking except through the Traveler's shout of their name. Paimon is the Traveler's companion and guide from here on.

## consequences
### STANDARD
The quest sets the Traveler's goals: finding the missing sibling, and learning what the seal and the god mean. It leads directly into Bird's Eye View and the rest of the Prologue, where the Traveler seeks The Seven in Mondstadt.

## placement
### STANDARD
Story order and release order agree: this is the first quest of the first act of the Prologue (Version 1.0) and precedes Bird's Eye View. Its cinematic event, the sealing, happens long before the present-day story; the beach scene is the story's present.
### LARGE +
The quest is not listed in the Archive's Travel Log, so it can be missed in chapter lists. In the full Prologue entry it is treated as the prelude to the rest of Act I.
