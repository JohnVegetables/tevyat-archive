## summary
### LITE
Chapter III of the Archon Quests takes the Traveler to Sumeru, the nation of wisdom ruled in name by the Dendro Archon. The Traveler finds that the Sumeru Akademiya's sages have locked the young Archon Nahida away, are harvesting people's dreams through the Akasha network, and are working with the Fatui to build an artificial god. With Nahida's guidance and the help of desert mercenaries and Akademiya dissidents, the Traveler frees her and defeats the false god, who turns out to be the Fatui Harbinger Scaramouche. A separate Act VI follows a memory of the Traveler's sibling.
### STANDARD +
Structure: Acts I and II (3.0), Acts III and IV (3.1), Act V (3.2) and the Traveler chapter Act VI, Caribert (3.5). The chapter's stated theme in its preview is wisdom as an enemy of itself: a city of scholars pushing folly while its god makes no argument against it.
### LARGE +
The act titles are: Through Mists of Smoke and Forests Dark; The Morn a Thousand Roses Brings; Dreams, Emptiness, Deception; King Deshret and the Three Magi; Akasha Pulses, the Kalpa Flame Rises; and Caribert. The announcement description for Acts I and II draws on quatrains of Omar Khayyam's Rubaiyat, and the Act V description praises the two Dendro Archons, Rukkhadevata and Kusanali.

## plot
### LITE
Arriving from the Chasm, the Traveler is sent by Tighnari and Collei toward Sumeru City to meet the Archon, and instead is pulled into a city-wide dream loop during the Sabzeruz Festival. The loop's secret leads to Nahida, who has been imprisoned for centuries, and then to the Akademiya's god-making project. Allied with Alhaitham, Cyno, Dehya, Nilou and desert Eremites, the Traveler gets Nahida out and, inside the Akasha, defeats the false god. Nahida purges the forbidden knowledge from Irminsul, and the sages fall.
### STANDARD
Act I: the Traveler is knocked out by incense in Avidya Forest, has a vision of Irminsul and a voice saying "World, forget me", and wakes in Gandharva Ville with Tighnari and Collei. They learn of the Withering, Eleazar and the two Dendro Archons, then follow a lead to Sumeru City where they meet Dehya, Nilou and the Akademiya scholar Alhaitham and trace a stolen Divine Knowledge Capsule to the Eremite Mizri. Act II: during the Sabzeruz Festival the day repeats; Nahida appears (and at the end speaks through Katheryne's body), and with the Traveler works out that the Akasha harvests dreams. Nilou's Dance of Sabzeruz breaks the loop. Act III: the Traveler and Nahida pressure the Grand Sage's secretary Setaria; the Fatui Harbinger Dottore ambushes them, traps Nahida's mind and sends the Traveler toward the desert. The Traveler meets Cyno, Candace and Dehya at Aaru Village. Act IV: the group tracks the missing Village Keepers into the desert, wins over the Eremite Rahman, and learns the sages are building a new god. Act V: the plan is carried out on Jnagarbha Day; Nahida is woken and freed, the false god is defeated inside the Akasha, and Irminsul is cleansed. Act VI: in Avidya Forest the Traveler dreams of the sibling's past and the origins of the Abyss Order.
### LARGE +
Act I, Through Mists of Smoke and Forests Dark (3.0): after leaving the Chasm via the tunnel at Cinnabar Cliff, the Traveler follows the scholar Haypasia, who lights incense in a cave; the Traveler faints and sees a white tree under a red sky. Tighnari and Collei, Forest Rangers in Gandharva Ville, treat them. Collei explains there have been two Dendro Archons: Greater Lord Rukkhadevata, who died in the cataclysm, and Lesser Lord Kusanali, found afterwards and living in Sumeru City. Collei was born with Eleazar, a Sumeru disease; Tighnari explains the Withering as an illness of Irminsul, the tree whose roots are the leylines, which stores the world's memories and to which the Dendro Archon is linked, making her the God of Wisdom. The Traveler's vision was a real memory in Irminsul. Haypasia's Aranara neighbour puts the Traveler through a dream and Haypasia explains that the Akademiya teaches that adults cannot dream because the old Archon protected them from it. Sage Khajeh tries to recruit Tighnari for a secret Akademiya project and fails. In Sumeru City, the Traveler and Paimon receive Akasha Terminals, learn that nobody can say how to meet the Archon, and meet Dunyarzad, who wants to revive the Sabzeruz Festival, and Nilou, who will dance in it. A convoy has been robbed at Aaru Village. In Port Ormos they meet Alhaitham, who is in dispute with the extremist Eremite group Ayn Al-Ahmar, spreading the rumour that King Deshret will revive. With the merchant Dori's help they find the missing Divine Knowledge Capsule is with the leader Mizri. Mizri uses it, goes berserk, and mutters the same line as the vision before he is taken away; Alhaitham quietly keeps the capsule.

Act II, The Morn a Thousand Roses Brings (3.0): at the Sabzeruz Festival, Dehya is hurt protecting Dunyarzad, who reveals she has terminal Eleazar. Grand Sage Azar and his secretary Setaria shut the festival down and ban public art performances. The day then repeats. The Traveler finally meets Nahida at Bimarstan, who restores their memory of the loops (a samsara), says the Dunyarzad in the hospital bed is the real one while the one at the festival is a copy, and that only those with Dendro power can perceive her. They work out that the Akasha harvests dreams, which the sages claimed were harmful, and that the loops put users into a shared dream of the festival. Nilou proves to be the dream's host; the Traveler makes her realise that she is dreaming, she dances, and the loop breaks. Nahida, speaking through Katheryne's body, reveals she has been confined in the Sanctuary of Surasthana for 500 years, explains that "World, forget me" came from Greater Lord Rukkhadevata, and says she needs rest.

Act III, Dreams, Emptiness, Deception (3.1): see the separate entry for the act. The Traveler and Nahida recruit Setaria as an inside source; the plan fails when Dottore traps Nahida's mind in the Akasha, the Traveler escapes Eremite assassins, and a final hint from Nahida points to the Great Red Sand. Alhaitham helps them shake off pursuers at Caravan Ribat, and at Aaru Village they meet Cyno, Dehya and Candace.

Act IV, King Deshret and the Three Magi (3.1): the group looks for the Village Keepers, exiled Akademiya scholars. At Dar al-Shifa, an abandoned Eleazar hospital, they find the scholar Razak. Alhaitham tells Cyno that he himself is the leak: the sages keep him under watch, and the Akasha can compute his likely movements, so the kidnappers went the opposite way, into the desert. Dehya offers her arm to the Eremite leader Rahman as a pledge for her friends' words; he relents. A Village Keeper's earthquake-shield carries them into ruins of King Deshret, where a priest's inscription tells how King Deshret introduced forbidden knowledge, Rukkhadevata saved the people but became small, and Deshret sacrificed himself. Rahman accepts that Rukkhadevata was his people's saviour. The kidnappers Morghi and Judar confirm the sages plan a new god, on Dottore's suggestion, after scientific progress stalled.

Act V, Akasha Pulses, the Kalpa Flame Rises (3.2): the plan is set. Cyno arranges the arrest of Rahman's Eremites at Caravan Ribat so they can be moved to Sumeru City; at Pardis Dhyai the group encounters a projection of Scaramouche, who states he was always meant to be a god. On Jnagarbha Day, Alhaitham and the Traveler enter the Akademiya openly; Alhaitham appears to betray the Traveler, is exiled, and the Traveler wakes Nahida inside the Akasha from a confinement room. Nilou's performance and a false report that Kusanali has escaped pull the guards out of the Akademiya. Azar goes to the Sanctuary and meets Cyno. Nahida reclaims the Akasha for herself alone. Inside the Fatui-built facility, Nahida and the Traveler face Scaramouche, who has been placed in a mechanical body as Shouki no Kami; Nahida takes the Electro Gnosis and with her own Gnosis enters Rukkhadevata's last memory, which is contaminated with forbidden knowledge. Rukkhadevata tells Nahida that Irminsul can be saved only by erasing her from it. Nahida complies; Irminsul recovers, the people can dream again, and everyone except the Traveler forgets Rukkhadevata. Dottore then negotiates with Nahida. Afterwards the sages are sentenced to spend their lives in Avidya Forest, Alhaitham declines the post of Grand Sage, and Nilou holds a feast. Nahida tells the Traveler about Fontaine and a few things about the sibling and the Descenders.

Act VI, Caribert (3.5): in Port Ormos, Kaeya meets the Traveler and Paimon and mentions his Alberich name; Dainsleif appears and says Kaeya's ancestors founded the Abyss Order. Dainsleif then leads the Traveler to a farmstead in Avidya Forest linked to the Loom of Fate project. The Traveler sleeps and has a sibling's memory of a Khaenri'ahn man calling himself Eide, who nurses his hilichurl son Caribert with medicine, and of a voice from the Abyss. In the memory the man is revealed as Chlothar Alberich and addresses the Traveler as his Prince or Princess, announcing he will use the Abyss against the gods. When the Traveler wakes, Dainsleif has fixed the leyline distortions and they find two sets of remains in the field. Dainsleif suspects the identity of the "sinner" and leaves.
### ULTRA +
Quest-level order. Act I: A Forest of Change, Further Observation, Chronic Illness, Silent Seeker of Knowledge, The Trail of the God of Wisdom, Lost in Prosperity, Ever So Close. Act II: The Coming, The Arrival, The Continuation, The Samsara, The Causality, The Empty Illusions, The End of the Sabzeruz Festival, and Dawn. Act III: Like a Triumphant Hero; The Gaze From a Certain God; The Four at Loggerheads. Act IV: The Missing Village Keepers; Cry from the Eleazar Hospital; Secret of the Scorching Desert. Act V: Through the Predawn Night; As by a God's Side; Jnagarbha Day; Where the Boat of Consciousness Lies; A Toast to Victory. Act VI: Destined Encounter; Fortune-Mocking Pedigree; A Lamenter at Fate's End; Portended Fate.

Act II's investigation details. The Traveler and Paimon removed their Akasha Terminals and found it made no difference. Dehya's training dummy showed that the loop cannot be rebuilt each time by memory edits, so the loop is not the real world. Attempts to leave the city were erased from the Traveler's memory; Nahida therefore changed the Terminal to record messages, which exposed the dream harvest. The first suspect for the dream's host was wrong; true purple Padisarah flowers in the dream confirmed Nilou as the host, and the tale of the First Sage helped her realise she was dreaming.

Act V's sub-events. Cyno insisted on minimal casualties and Alhaitham advised the plan; Alhaitham also prepared a simulated headset exercise so that the Traveler could rehearse conviction, because he suspected forbidden knowledge corrupts those who use it. At Pardis Dhyai Dottore had left after asking Tighnari to hand over Haypasia and saying he had to return when the Tsaritsa called; Dehya tracked him toward Port Ormos, only to learn it was a feint. Scaramouche is shown as capable and boastful, and he admits some regard for Haypasia as his first follower. Nahida takes the Electro Gnosis after revealing that Scaramouche had been caught in a repeating samsara, and she passes combat knowledge to the Traveler through the upgraded Terminal. In the Dottore scene Nahida threatens to destroy the Electro Gnosis, which might awaken the Heavenly Principles, and agrees to hand it over only if Dottore destroys his other segments; he does. He then asks about the Dendro Gnosis and offers the rumour that the skies of Teyvat are fake, and the scene cuts to black. In the closing quest the Traveler delivers Nilou's feast invitations: Collei and Tighnari (who report the sages' sentence) and Rahman and Setaria (who leaves the Akademiya to study for the desert's sake) are too busy to come, while Alhaitham (who turned down the post of Grand Sage), Dehya, Dunyarzad (relieved that the Eleazar cases have been dealt with) and Cyno attend, and Nahida arrives in person at the feast's climax. Sumeru's people remember Nahida as the Dendro Archon all along. The Traveler keeps the memory of Rukkhadevata secret, honouring her last wish.

Act VI's setup. Kaeya says he once stowed away with Mondstadt merchants bound for Sumeru as a child to learn about Khaenri'ah, but was returned; Kaeya recognises Dainsleif as a pure-blooded Khaenri'ahn by his eyes. The Loom of Fate is the project Dainsleif links to the sibling's time in Sumeru. In the vision the Traveler sees the sibling's own face in a mirror. The act ends with the Traveler hugging Paimon.

## revelations
@version: 3.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
- The Akademiya's sages confined the Archon Nahida in the Sanctuary of Surasthana for 500 years and ran the Akasha as a machine for harvesting dreams, lying that dreams were harmful.
- The Fatui Harbinger Dottore suggested a plan to build a new god; the vessel is Scaramouche, who holds the Electro Gnosis.
- Greater Lord Rukkhadevata was contaminated by forbidden knowledge, created Nahida to be her incarnation, and chose to be erased from Irminsul to heal it; only the Traveler remembers her.
- The Eremites' belief that Rukkhadevata betrayed King Deshret is contradicted by his last priest's inscription: Deshret's forbidden knowledge drove his people mad, Rukkhadevata saved them, and Deshret sacrificed himself. Rahman accepts this.
### LARGE +
- The Withering is a sickness of Irminsul and Eleazar is its human form; Irminsul's cleansing removes forbidden knowledge and lets people dream again.
- The Traveler learns from Nahida that the sibling was recorded in Irminsul as suddenly appearing in Khaenri'ah and later travelling Teyvat, but the records of the end of the journey are blurred, as if someone is hiding it. The Fatui count them as Teyvat's fourth Descender; Nahida suspects the Heavenly Principles to be the first.
- Act VI: the Abyss Order's founder was Chlothar Alberich, a Khaenri'ahn noble, according to the vision; Kaeya's surname, Alberich, is thus linked to the Order's founders by Dainsleif.

## consequences
### STANDARD
The sages are removed; Nahida rules openly; the Akasha is shut off; Irminsul is cleansed of forbidden knowledge; Rukkhadevata is forgotten. Scaramouche loses the Electro Gnosis and Dottore receives it. Cyno resumes his post. The Traveler turns toward Fontaine.
### LARGE +
Nahida devotes Sumeru's resources to the desert people. The Traveler's secret memory of Rukkhadevata remains a loose thread. Chapter IV, Masquerade of the Guilty, follows with Fontaine.

## placement
### STANDARD
Released 3.0 (Acts I and II), 3.1 (Acts III and IV), 3.2 (Act V) and 3.5 (Act VI, a Traveler chapter). It follows Chapter II's Act IV, Requiem of the Echoing Depths, and precedes Chapter IV (Fontaine). The interlude Inversion of Genesis comes before Act VI.
### LARGE +
The chapter's title was reused by Song of the Welkin Moon Act X, which returns to Sumeru; do not confuse that act with this chapter.

## entry: Sabzeruz Festival Samsara
@keys: Sabzeruz samsara, Sabzeruz Festival loop, dream harvest
@keys2: Nahida, Akasha
@version: 3.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
An Akademiya project in which Akasha users are put into a shared dream of the Sabzeruz Festival, repeating one day while their dreams are harvested. The real Dunyarzad's mind is being worn away; a puppet copy keeps the loop going. It breaks when Nilou, the dream's host, realises she is dreaming and dances.

## entry: The Akademiya's God-Making Project
@keys: Akademiya project, false god, Divine Knowledge Capsule, new god
@keys2: Dottore, Scaramouche
@version: 3.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The sages' secret plan to create a new Archon to replace the dead Greater Lord Rukkhadevata, using Divine Knowledge Capsules, dream harvest, Scaramouche and the Electro Gnosis. Morghi and Judar confirm that Dottore suggested it. Capsules do not give real divine knowledge; users go mad.

## entry: Rukkhadevata's Erasure
@keys: Rukkhadevata erased, final memory, forgotten Archon
@keys2: Nahida, Irminsul
@version: 3.2
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
In Irminsul, Rukkhadevata tells Nahida the only way to heal it is to remove her from it. Nahida agrees. Everyone except the Traveler forgets Rukkhadevata existed, and Sumeru comes to believe that Nahida was always the Archon.

## entry: Chlothar Alberich and the Origins of the Abyss Order
@keys: Chlothar Alberich, Eide, Caribert, Loom of Fate
@keys2: Dainsleif, Abyss Order
@version: 3.5
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
In the Act VI memory-vision, Eide, a Khaenri'ahn nobleman, cares for his hilichurl son Caribert in Avidya Forest. He is revealed as Chlothar Alberich, who addresses the sibling as Prince or Princess and says he will use the Abyss against the gods. Dainsleif says the Alberichs founded the Abyss Order.
