## summary
@spoiler: major
### LITE
"The Journey Home" is the prelude act of the Song of the Welkin Moon Archon Quest chapter. Before crossing the sea to Nod-Krai, the Traveler and Paimon meet Ineffa, an amnesiac automaton washed ashore in Natlan, and help her uncover who she is while the Fatui try to seize something hidden in her core.
### STANDARD
The act has three quests (Flamelets; Smoldering; Searing) and requires Adventure Rank 40. It follows "A Space and Time for You" and leads into "A Dance of Snowy Tides and Hoarfrost Groves". The Traveler is still struggling with what they have learned about their sibling. A sealed cryptex found beside Ineffa holds a message for the Traveler; her memories are blocked, and her core turns out to be linked to a Sovereign Lord of the ancient Secret Source. The act ends with Varka's letter pointing the Traveler to Nod-Krai and to a tavern called The Flagship.

## plot
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
**Flamelets.** At the Flower-Feather Clan, Chasca explains that Secret Source automatons have been attacking people nearby. The Traveler and Paimon help and find Ineffa on the shore, unconscious and surrounded by automatons; she is a machine, and she calls out for the Traveler and Paimon before waking. Beside her is a cryptex marked with a crude picture of the Traveler and Paimon. Xilonen, at the Children of Echoes workshop, finds the cryptex built from materials of many nations and cannot safely open it. Ineffa cannot recall her past, but cleans the workshop with a broom form of her arm, and malfunctions at an image of the first Baraka, firing Electro; Kinich arrives, and the caravan merchants who were looking for her explain that she is Ineffa, made by Aino, and that they come from Nod-Krai, where Fatui activity had stirred tension between religious groups.

**Smoldering.** The Traveler gives Ineffa a flower sent by the merchant's daughter Adieta, which triggers a memory of a woman who tells her to find a new place to belong across the sea. Ineffa asks Chasca what home means. Citlali explains the Thirteen Sovereign Lords, artificial minds made with Secret Source technology, and suspects Ineffa's core is the Eighth Lord, then the hostile Eleventh Lord. Ineffa disassembles attacking automatons and learns that the Fatui, led by a regiment connected to the Harbinger Sandrone ("Marionette"), forced them. The merchant Zhuoxin is revealed to be tied to the Fatui as an informant, and Ineffa is drawn into ruins that lead to the Lord's territory.

**Searing.** Beneath the ruins is the Temple of Cascading Colors. The Eighth Lord reveals that Ineffa is the benevolent half of a split Eleventh Lord; the malevolent half manipulated events to reclaim the core. Fatui under Velimiru chase them inside. Ineffa crushes the Eleventh Lord's core; when Velimiru revives her, Ineffa creates a new core, gains a Moon Wheel, and the group destroys her. The group escapes; the cryptex opens to a message from Varka.
### LARGE +
Opening details: Ifa has examined a qucusaur Chasca brought; Ifa warns against forcing the cryptex open in case it self-destructs. While the group heads to Xilonen, a mysterious man watches them from above. Chasca expected Xilonen to be napping, but Xilonen has just dealt with an automaton issue with Mavuika and assumes the group has come about the same thing. Ineffa answers that she is not from Fontaine or Mondstadt despite liking Fontaine pastries and resembling a maid; Xilonen finds clockwork knee joints and a core from the Secret Source, but other materials from beyond Natlan. Xilonen identifies the poster as the first Baraka, a machine-breaker who tried to help humanity, killed herself when Och-Kan went insane, and whose workshop Ixquieh burned so Och-Kan could not use it against humanity; Chasca adds that her friend Lianca saved some of her work. When Ineffa malfunctions, a voice in her mind calls her a traitor for seeking a place to belong. Kinich explains that the merchants hired him to find her and that not every job he takes involves hunting. The merchants named in the act include Zhuoxin, Asimu and Aamu, who left Nod-Krai when the Fatui's actions there, described as pulling the moon down, threatened the Frostmoon Scions; Ineffa's amnesia worsened as they neared Natlan. Ajaw acts unnaturally cowed by Ineffa.

Middle details: three tribal children, Yatlan, Capac and Kotimy, are playing with Ineffa's small robot when Adieta tells them her family is returning to Liyue, her father Zhuoxin's hometown. Ineffa thanks Adieta in person before the family leaves for Liyue Harbor. Citlali says Kinich put Ajaw in timeout for three days. The Eighth Lord was close to Lianca and ruled the territory of today's tribe, but was executed by the other lords for raising Lianca; a curse makes any pro-human goodwill cause her crippling pain. The group later learns Citlali and Xilonen privately believed Ineffa might be the hostile Eleventh Lord who went mad and vanished after Xiuhcoatl was defeated, and might have to be taken out; Chasca stops this, saying she trusts Ineffa. Secret Source Automatons attack Zhuoxin's family while Fatui led by Velimiru watch; a voice tells Velimiru he is playing with fire, and he orders a retreat. The automaton says the Fatui seized its privileges, mentions the Mirror of the Dawning Morn and asks Ineffa to return the Eighth Lord's memory; Zhuoxin says he heard in Nasha Town that the Fatui regiment is run by Sandrone. Ineffa's small robot reports that Zhuoxin's picture was found at a Fatui stronghold.

Closing details: the ruins hold the Temple of Cascading Colors, built by Administrator 1; a service bot calls Ineffa Administrator 3, and Administrator 2 later sealed the facility. Zhuoxin, who says he spied for the Fatui for over ten years, is promised the waiver of a debt to the Northland Bank in exchange; he refuses a final device built to control Secret Source mechanisms. The Eighth Lord, who has waited more than two thousand years for Ineffa's return, tells her that the temple is where she was reborn, that the curse was one Ineffa placed on herself, and that after the war, defeated and stranded, the Eleventh Lord split into one personality that sought revenge on humanity and one that sought a new home; the latter became Ineffa, and the former, she says, perished. That account proves incomplete: the malevolent self survived and reveals that it manipulated events from the start; Lianca had caught on and imprisoned it in the facility for trying to destroy the benevolent self. When the Eleventh Lord is destroyed, she recognises Ineffa's Moon Wheel as made by the usurper and the three gods who abandoned them. After the group escapes, Aamu slaps Zhuoxin, and Chasca offers the family shelter at her tribe. In Varka's message, he thanks the Traveler for protecting Mondstadt in his absence, warns that the Abyss Order and the Fatui are making trouble in Nod-Krai along with struggles among other factions, and asks them to meet at The Flagship. Ineffa explains that a Moon Wheel works like a Vision but is more a power source than a mere elemental power, and plans to stay in Natlan for now but offers to return to Nod-Krai with the Traveler later.

The service bot refers to a Secret Source Dragon Idol that carries the group down to the central zone. The Eleventh Lord is said to have lured Ineffa so she could reclaim the core, declaring that they will all have a new home, buried underground until the end of time. Velimiru, who turns out not to be human, briefly revives her with a replica core before she is finally destroyed. Ineffa's small robot takes part in the defence by raising a barrier on the Fatui.

## revelations
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
- Ineffa is an automaton whose core comes from the Secret Source's Sovereign Lords. According to the Eighth Lord, she is the benevolent half of the Eleventh Lord, split off after the ancient war; the hostile half survived separately and manipulated events to regain the core.
- The Thirteen Sovereign Lords were artificial minds created by the Pyro Sovereign with Secret Source technology, per Citlali (as relayed in the act summary).
- The Fatui regiment active in Natlan is said to be led by Sandrone ("Marionette") and to be interested in ancient history for "inference".
- According to the horrified Eleventh Lord, a Moon Wheel is something built by the usurper and the three gods who abandoned the Sovereign Lords; Ineffa gains one, appearing as an Electro Vision, when she creates a new core. Ineffa later describes it as working like a Vision but serving more as a power source.
### LARGE +
Citlali's guesses change within the act: first the Eighth Lord, then the Eleventh Lord. Each guess is a reasonable inference from the automatons' behaviour and is not confirmed until the Eighth Lord speaks.

## characters
### STANDARD
Ineffa is the central figure: an amnesiac maid-like automaton, made by Aino. Chasca of the Flower-Feather Clan, Xilonen, Citlali and Kinich help her; Mavuika is mentioned and appears at the end. Ajaw reacts oddly to her. Zhuoxin and his family are Nod-Krai merchants who guide the plot. The Fatui side is led by Velimiru, with Sandrone as the Harbinger behind the regiment. The Eighth Lord, who has waited for her and guides her through the temple, is an ally; the antagonist is the surviving malevolent half of the Eleventh Lord, Ineffa's own other self. Varka appears through his letter.

## consequences
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Ineffa is no longer in the dark about herself and now has an Electro Moon Wheel. She stays in Natlan for the near term but is willing to travel to Nod-Krai with the Traveler. Zhuoxin's family is given shelter in Chasca's tribe. The Traveler holds Varka's message asking for a meeting at The Flagship in Nod-Krai and has learned about the Fatui and Abyss Order trouble there, leading into "A Dance of Snowy Tides and Hoarfrost Groves".
