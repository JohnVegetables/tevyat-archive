## summary
### LITE
The fourth act of Fontaine's Archon Quest, set in the Fortress of Meropide where the Traveler and Paimon are serving a short sentence. While Lyney's family quietly searches for the missing Childe, the warden Wriothesley shows them a sluice gate that holds back the Primordial Sea. The gate fails and Neuvillette reseals it, Arlecchino confronts Furina about the Prophecy, and the Traveler realises that Neuvillette is the Hydro Sovereign.
### STANDARD +
The act has five quests: Into the Deepwater Murk, The Truth Shrouded in Shadow, Secret Keepers and Forbidden Zones, Calamitous Tread, and A Moment's Respite. Its title refers to the prophecy of Fontaine's end moving into its next stage.
### LARGE +
It combines prison-intrigue with the first physical sign that the Primordial Sea is a live threat to Fontaine, and it is also where the Traveler learns who Neuvillette is and why the Sovereigns' power now sits with the Archons.

## plot
### STANDARD
In the fortress, a card left in the Traveler's cell tells them to meet Lyney, who has been assigned by the Knave ("Father") to find Childe. Lyney's plan is for Freminet to dive the flooded tunnel while Lynette gathers information and Lyney helps from above. The Traveler feigns illness to look into the infirmary, run by Sigewinne and frequently visited by Jurieu and Lourvine, but learns nothing useful. On the ninth day Lynette has vanished from her infirmary bed, and Lyney confronts Wriothesley in anger. Wriothesley says he has known the siblings' purposes all along and had locked Freminet out only because Lyney would not have abandoned him. He quizzes the Traveler on the prison's hidden rules and then Sigewinne tranquillises Lyney. Clorinde brings in an unconscious Freminet, whom Sigewinne diagnoses with Primordial Seawater poisoning. He fell ill following Childe's route and is rescued in time.

Wriothesley admits he suspected Lyney wanted to see the "forbidden zone" and took the Traveler there. The central room holds a huge sluice gate with a gauge that has crept into the red over the past year. He concludes that the gate is holding back the Primordial Sea and will eventually fail. He explains that the fortress was first built when the former Hydro Archon Egeria let repentant exiles guard her secret beneath the waves. He is secretly building a great ship, inspired by Remurian legend, and has kept it from the prisoners. The Traveler and Paimon share this with the siblings, and the group stops treating each other as enemies.

That night alarms sound and the prisoners are evacuated upward. The gate fails; Wriothesley holds the water briefly with Cryo and freezes the gate, Clorinde drops the security shutters, and the Traveler is sent to fetch Neuvillette. Neuvillette is concerned about Furina's meeting with the Knave and sends the Traveler and Paimon to watch over her while he goes to the prison, where he pushes the Primordial Sea back and reseals the gate, remarking that the sentence was too severe for the people of Fontaine.
### LARGE +
At the tea party the Traveler and Paimon diplomatically defend Furina from Arlecchino's pressure, until shaking cups tell Arlecchino that the prophecy may have reached its next stage. Once the pleasantries end, Arlecchino accuses Furina of failing to protect her people, asks about her "primary and backup plans" (which Furina calls confidential) and asks what the Oratrice Mecanique d'Analyse Cardinale's large Indemnitium stores are for. She asks the Traveler to see her out.

Outside the opera house Arlecchino says that after Childe was detained she decided to meet the Archon. One night she found Furina playing with a cat and tried to take the Gnosis, but found none, and sensed a "curse" on her. Furina begged for mercy, so frightened that her subordinates later found she could not sleep or eat. She wonders whether Neuvillette might be the Archon but concludes, from her experience with the Tsaritsa, that he is not. Neuvillette arrives, Arlecchino leaves, offering to cooperate with the Traveler if needed, and he mourns the day's events, which makes it rain. From what Freminet had said about the Dragon of Water, the Traveler realises that Neuvillette is the Hydro Sovereign. He asks for secrecy.

A Moment's Respite closes the act: with the prison safe, Wriothesley lets the Traveler and Paimon leave whenever they wish, keeps their cell and free meals until the sentence ends, and asks them to return for paperwork after 45 days. In the final scene Childe wakes in the depths of the Primordial Sea beneath an enormous narwhal.
### ULTRA +
Prison details. The monthly "free day" gives prisoners the day off. Laverune and Cuistot, the "Bombshell Bros", pass on tips and rumours about prison life. Wriothesley tests the Traveler on two rules: the Production Zone's "strange meat" is actually Sigewinne's secret cooking, meant to restore the stamina of non-stop workers, which humans avoid because Melusines see it differently; and the red liquid is a new Fonta recipe being market-tested on prisoners, who learn to spend their Coupons wisely. He asks about the secret behind Sigewinne and the infirmary, which the Traveler cannot answer. Sigewinne also feeds the Traveler, Paimon and Clorinde before the alarms.

Neuvillette explains to the Traveler that when the First Usurper arrived on Teyvat, it took part of the Seven Sovereigns' power to create the Archons' Authorities. He says he can do little against the prophecy unless the Hydro Archon returns his Authority, and that other solutions must be sought.

## revelations
@version: 4.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Neuvillette is the Hydro Sovereign (Hydro Dragon) and tells the Traveler the Archons' Authorities came from the Sovereigns. Beneath the Fortress of Meropide, a sluice gate holds back the Primordial Sea; it fails and Neuvillette must reseal it. Primordial Seawater has grown stronger around the prison.
### LARGE +
Arlecchino attempted to take the Gnosis from Furina and found she did not hold one. The fortress was originally built for Egeria's repentant exiles. Wriothesley is building a ship in secret. Childe is alive in the Primordial Sea at the end of the act.

## characters
### STANDARD
The Traveler and Paimon investigate from within the prison. Lyney, Lynette and Freminet search for Childe, with Arlecchino in the background as their "Father". Wriothesley, the fortress administrator, keeps secrets of his own; Sigewinne tends the prisoners; Clorinde is hired for "side work". Neuvillette comes to save the prison. Furina is pressured at the tea party. Childe appears only in the final scene.

## consequences
### STANDARD
The siblings and the Traveler share their information and agree not to fight. Neuvillette confirms his identity to the Traveler, and the prison survives the gate failure. Arlecchino believes the prophecy may have reached its next stage, and she offers to cooperate with the Traveler if needed. Act V (Masquerade of the Guilty) continues the story.

## placement
### STANDARD
Chapter IV Act IV, added in version 4.1. It follows To the Stars Shining in the Depths (Act III) and precedes Masquerade of the Guilty. Story order equals release order.

## entry: Meropide sluice gate

### STANDARD
A giant gate in the forbidden zone of the Fortress of Meropide. Its gauge, which never moved when Wriothesley first saw it, had been rising into the red for a year. In Cataclysm's Quickening it fails, and Wriothesley and Clorinde hold the water long enough for Neuvillette to push it back and reseal it.

### LARGE

After Childe went missing from the fortress, she used the situation as diplomatic pressure to secure the meeting with Furina and Neuvillette.
