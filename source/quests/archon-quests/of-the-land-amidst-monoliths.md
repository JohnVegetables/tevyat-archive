## summary
### LITE
Chapter I Act I of the Archon Quest, set in Liyue. The Traveler and Paimon reach Liyue Harbor in search of the Geo Archon at the annual Rite of Descension, only to see Rex Lapis fall from the clouds apparently dead. Suspected by association, they are rescued by the Fatui Harbinger Childe and told to seek the adepti at Jueyun Karst.
### STANDARD +
The act has four stages: Rite of Descension, An Impromptu Change of Plan, three quests that can be done in any order (Wangshu, Moulder of Mountains, Custodian of Clouds), and Dust to Dust. It introduces Liyue's reverence for its god, the Liyue Qixing, the Millelith, the Fatui presence in the harbor, and the hidden adepti. Released in version 1.0.
### LARGE +
Tonally it moves from festival to panic to a tour of Liyue's mountains, with a recurring contrast between Liyue, whose god is present, and Mondstadt, whose god is not. The adepti scenes turn on contract-like bargains (a sigil of permission, food offerings) rather than combat.

## plot
### LITE
Venti has told the Traveler that Rex Lapis will appear in Liyue for the Rite of Descension. In Liyue Harbor the rite begins and Ningguang summons the god, but he drops lifeless onto the offering table and she orders the exits sealed. The Traveler is chased by the Millelith, rescued by Childe, and given a sigil to reach the adepti in Jueyun Karst.
### STANDARD +
**Rite of Descension.** Paimon explains that the Geo Archon is called Rex Lapis by Liyue's own people, since using the name Morax is considered impolite for outsiders. Locals such as Linlang, Guanhai and Bolai describe the yearly rite, in which the god gives guidance on Liyue's coming year, and send the pair to Yujing Terrace. They make wishes at the censers, then watch as Ningguang, who presides, calls the god down. As the clouds part, Rex Lapis's lifeless body crashes onto the offering table, and Ningguang declares that he has been killed and the exits must be sealed. The Millelith seize those who stood near the terrace. The Traveler tries to slip away but steps on a broken tile and is spotted. A Hydro user cuts down the guards and leads them off; he calls himself Childe, asks whether they met Signora in Mondstadt, and Paimon recognises him as a Fatui Harbinger. He says he had heard of their deeds in Mondstadt, kept his eye on them throughout the rite and so knows they did not do it, and sends them to the Northland Bank, where he gives them a Sigil of Permission and points them toward Jueyun Karst, the supposed home of the adepti.

**An Impromptu Change of Plan.** In Jueyun Karst the pair meet Moon Carver, who recognises the sigil. Millelith soldiers pursue them in, and Moon Carver tells the Traveler to see them gone; the Traveler fights them off. Told that Rex Lapis was killed at the rite and the Qixing are blaming onlookers, he is outraged, accepts that the accusation against the Traveler is unjust, and tells them to find Mountain Shaper and Cloud Retainer in the karst and the Conqueror of Demons, a guardian yaksha, at Wangshu Inn.
### LARGE +
**Wangshu.** At the inn Xiao, the Conqueror of Demons, is irritable and leaves. The innkeeper Verr Goldet says he is an adeptus who likes almond tofu. The inn's chef is too frightened to cook because a ghost haunts the inn; the pair follow the ghost, which possesses a Ruin Hunter, and defeat it, and she agrees to stop haunting the chef as long as the Traveler keeps playing with her. With almond tofu and a salad they catch up with Xiao, who is shocked to hear of the death and explains that the ghost is a lingering innocent spirit he had asked the inn to look after.

**Moulder of Mountains.** On Mt. Hulao a man begs the adepti to spare his brother, who has been encased in amber. The Traveler frees the brother, and Mountain Shaper appears, angry that a "thief" was freed. It explains that it plants karst flowers to protect the mountain's calm, treats trespassers as thieves, and encases those who step on the flowers; it then learns of Rex Lapis's death.

**Custodian of Clouds.** At Mt. Aocang the pair lay out dishes at a stone table as offerings. Cloud Retainer lets them into its domain on condition that they cross its puzzles. On hearing of the death it is furious and vows to destroy Liyue for the crime; the Traveler calms it by framing the offered food as a contract, which it reluctantly accepts.

**Dust to Dust.** Back at Liyue Harbor, Childe tells them the Qixing will not let anyone pay respects to the Exuvia, because the killer is still unfound. He explains that the Geo Archon is also an adeptus, Prime of the Adepti and the God of Contracts, and that the adepti signed contracts to protect Liyue and may act if the Qixing fail. Paimon lets slip that the Traveler is looking for the Seven; Childe promises to find someone who can break the stalemate and tells them to stay alive until they next meet.

### ULTRA +
The three adepti quests can be done in any order, and Childe's closing lines differ slightly if the Traveler has not met all of them. The three adepti respond to the news in different ways: Xiao with shock, Mountain Shaper with concern for its mountain, and Cloud Retainer with threats that are talked down.

## revelations
@version: 1.0
@spoiler: major
### LITE
Rex Lapis appears to have been assassinated at the Rite of Descension.
### STANDARD +
Childe is a Fatui Harbinger who had heard of the Traveler's deeds in Mondstadt and watched them throughout the rite. The adepti exist and are real. Rex Lapis is also an adeptus, the Prime of the Adepti, bound by contract with the other adepti to Liyue's protection. The Liyue Qixing are keeping the body out of sight.
### LARGE +
Childe says the Qixing have always been overly suspicious of the Fatui and that he is no fan of Signora; later he trades what he knows about the Exuvia for the Traveler's report on the adepti, calling it a returning gift. The act does not say who is responsible for the death.
### ULTRA +
The wish-making at the censers, the sigil and the later food offerings all show the act treating offerings and agreements as the working currency between mortals and adepti.

## characters
### LITE
Traveler and Paimon; Ningguang; Childe (Tartaglia); Moon Carver; Xiao.
### STANDARD +
Also Mountain Shaper, Cloud Retainer, Verr Goldet of Wangshu Inn, the inn's chef Smiley Yanxiao and its ghost, the brothers Li Ding and Li Dang on Mt. Hulao, and harbor locals including Linlang, Guanhai, Bolai, Ivanovich, Changshun, Gentry De'an, Fan Er'ye and Jifang. Venti is the one who sent the Traveler to Liyue.

## consequences
### STANDARD
The Traveler is wanted by the Millelith and has accepted Childe's help; Childe promises to find someone who can break the stalemate over the Exuvia. The next act, Farewell, Archaic Lord, begins with that help.
### LARGE +
The adepti now know of Rex Lapis's death and are angry, and the Traveler leaves with the sigil and the goodwill of the three they have met.
### ULTRA +
The act's last scene sets the Traveler's quest to find the Seven against the Exuvia's confinement: Paimon's slip means Childe now knows why they are in Liyue.

## placement
### STANDARD
Chapter I Act I, after Song of the Dragon and Freedom (the Mondstadt story) and before Act II, Farewell, Archaic Lord. Released in version 1.0. The quest requires completing the Mondstadt story and Adventure Rank 23.
