## summary
@spoiler: major
### LITE
Prologue Act III is the climax of the Mondstadt chapter. Venti, Jean, Diluc and the Traveler enter Stormterror's Lair, where the Traveler purges the Abyss Order's corruption from the dragon Dvalin and Venti and Dvalin part on better terms. Back in the city, the Fatui Harbinger Signora rips the Anemo Gnosis out of Venti, and Venti sends the Traveler toward Liyue.
### STANDARD
The act opens with the Abyss Order's mage dealt with at the winery, which frees Venti to dispel the barrier over Stormterror's Lair. Inside the lair, an old structure from Decarabian's Mondstadt, the group activates a light mechanism that Venti imbues with Anemo energy, and the Traveler purges Dvalin of the corrupting energy. Dvalin asks why Venti never asked for his protection, and flies away reassured. Jean explains the misunderstanding to the people.

Meanwhile hilichurls and Abyss infiltrators attack the city; Amber and the Knights hold, and Kaeya intercepts infiltrators. After Barbara receives the Holy Lyre der Himmel, a Fatui ambush freezes Venti and takes the Anemo Gnosis from him: Signora, the Eighth Harbinger, tears it out of his chest. Venti then explains the Gnosis, Signora's rank and the Tsaritsa, and advises the Traveler to meet the Geo Archon in Liyue at the Rite of Descension.
### LARGE +
Thematically the act is about freedom: Venti declined to command Dvalin because obedience given on an order would not be freedom. The cost to Venti is high, since the Fatui exploit the quiet moment after the victory to take the Gnosis.

## plot
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
**Preparation.** With the Abyss mage killed at the winery, Venti can dispel the barrier over Stormterror's Lair, and he and the Traveler go in to cleanse Dvalin.

**Dvalin purified.** The Traveler catches up with Dvalin and purges the corrupt energy. Dvalin protects the group and asks Venti why he did not ask for protection. Venti says he did not want him to obey the Abyss Order, nor to obey Venti either; an order to be free would not be true freedom. Dvalin leaves, assured that no one is after him. Jean tells the people that the dragon had not attacked for no reason.

**The city.** The Knights repel a hilichurl attack on Mondstadt while Kaeya intercepts Abyss Order infiltrators; Amber wishes the Traveler luck on their journey to meet the Seven.

**The Gnosis.** At the Cathedral Jean introduces the deaconess Barbara, who asks for the lyre. The Traveler hands over a damaged one and Barbara panics; Venti repairs it and Barbara leaves with it. Venti says he only used magic to make it appear fixed and runs off. Outside a cold gust freezes Venti and Paimon. Fatui Agents restrain the Traveler; Signora and two Fatui Cicin Mages arrive, and Signora, after trading insults with Venti, rips out his Gnosis and leaves before the Knights arrive. An agent strikes the Traveler unconscious.

**Aftermath.** Barbara finds them but her healing does not help Venti, who leaves. Jean guesses his purpose; the Traveler stays silent for her sake. They find Venti at the great tree in Windrise, where he explains the Gnosis, Signora as the eighth of the Eleven Fatui Harbingers, and the Tsaritsa, the Archon of Snezhnaya who is also one of the Seven. He says the Seven used to respect each other but a catastrophe five hundred years ago broke the relationship, and advises heading to Liyue to see the Geo Archon at the Rite of Descension.
### LARGE +
Fuller account of two stretches. **Preparation.** Diluc brings word of the Abyss Order, having killed one of its operatives before. Venti admits he is the weakest of the Seven because of long absences and so depends on Mondstadt's people. Diluc's men track the Abyss mage to the winery; it dies and dissipates the energy that had cut Venti off from Dvalin. Venti learns the nature of the barrier on the ruins and says he can dispel it. Jean thinks they may have to kill Dvalin; Venti insists the plan to cleanse him still works because the Traveler can purify the Teardrop Crystal. **The lair.** At Stormterror's Lair hilichurls, manipulated by the Abyss Order, attack and are repelled. Venti dispels the barrier. The ruins turn out to be part of old Mondstadt from the time of the tyrant Decarabian; Venti refuses to say more. The group activates a light actuator, and Venti infuses it with Anemo energy. **The city.** Amber reports that during the fight she and other Knights repelled a coordinated hilichurl attack. She is annoyed at Kaeya's absence, but at the Good Hunter tavern Kaeya says he suspected the attack and stayed to intercept Abyss Order infiltrators; he learned from them that the Order has a single leader. Amber asks the Traveler's plans; Paimon says they will travel Teyvat to meet the Seven, and Amber wishes them well. She reminds them Jean wants them at the Cathedral about the lyre.

The wiki lists the act's quests as Abyss Mage, Hurdle, Empty Abode, Light Guiding Ceremony, Calm Before the Storm, A Long Shot, When the Wind Dies Down and Ending Note. It requires Adventure Rank 18 and awards 200 Mondstadt reputation.
### ULTRA +
Small details: Jean and Venti note that hilichurls tend to avoid elemental areas, and Diluc says the Abyss Order is manipulating the hilichurls at the lair only to stall the party. Venti says outright that his power has waned from his absences. The Traveler is struck by an agent only after Signora orders her subordinates to flee before the Knights arrive. Venti later tells the Traveler he wants them to move quickly, since the Geo Archon descends only once a year.

## revelations
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
- The ruins that Dvalin took refuge in are part of old Mondstadt from Decarabian's time.
- Dvalin was cleansed of the Abyss Order's corruption and was not to blame for the attacks on the city. He wanted to know why Venti never asked him for help.
- The Anemo Gnosis was taken from Venti by Signora, the Eighth of the Eleven Fatui Harbingers.
- Venti reluctantly explains what the Gnosis is, and Paimon realises that the Tsaritsa of Snezhnaya, who gave the Harbingers their power, is one of the Seven.
- Kaeya learns that the Abyss Order has a single leader.
### LARGE +
The sibling's relationship to the Abyss Order is not explained here. The seeds of the Fatui-Seven conflict and the cataclysm five hundred years ago are only named, not explained, and are pursued in later chapters.

## characters
### STANDARD
Traveler and Paimon; Venti (the Anemo Archon, Barbatos, who has by now revealed his identity); Dvalin, cleansed of the corruption; Jean (Acting Grand Master); Diluc (brings word of the Abyss Order; his people track the Abyss mage); Amber and Kaeya (the defence of the city, the tavern conversation); Barbara (receives the lyre); Signora (antagonist) with two Fatui Cicin Mages and Fatui Agents.

## consequences
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Dvalin is free of the Abyss Order's influence and the dragon crisis ends. Venti loses the Anemo Gnosis to the Fatui, and the Traveler is set on the road to Liyue to meet the Geo Archon at the Rite of Descension, leading directly into Chapter I.
### LARGE +
Jean clears up the people's misunderstanding about Dvalin. When Venti walks away after the attack, Jean appears to understand his intentions, and the Traveler keeps quiet for her sake.

## placement
### STANDARD
Third and last act of the Prologue (release 1.0). It follows For a Tomorrow Without Tears and leads into Chapter I Act I, Of the Land Amidst Monoliths, set in Liyue.
