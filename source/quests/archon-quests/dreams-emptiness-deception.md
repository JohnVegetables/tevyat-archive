## summary
### LITE
Dreams, Emptiness, Deception is Act III of the Sumeru Archon Quests. After the Sabzeruz Festival samsara ends, Nahida, speaking through Katheryne, plans with the Traveler to learn what the Akademiya's sages intend. Their plan to turn Setaria, Grand Sage Azar's secretary, is ambushed by the Fatui Harbinger Dottore, who traps Nahida's mind in the Akasha. The Traveler flees toward the desert, where Nahida's last clue sends them to the people of the Great Red Sand and to a meeting at Aaru Village with Alhaitham, Cyno, Dehya and Candace.
### STANDARD +
It has three quests: Like a Triumphant Hero, The Gaze From a Certain God and The Four at Loggerheads. The announcement text calls it the end of the Sabzeruz samsara, the path to saving the god in the desert stars, and a boast from the lord of the scarlet soil.
### LARGE +
The act is where the Akademiya's collaboration with the Fatui first becomes certain, and where Nahida is lost to the Traveler until Act V. It is also where the desert-based allies of the later plan are brought together.

## plot
### LITE
Nahida, borrowing Katheryne, proposes recruiting Setaria. The Traveler possesses Setaria's acquaintances in turn to confront her about her guilt over the desert; she agrees to steal documents but is overheard, and Dottore stages a trap at the Akademiya gate. Nahida holds him off while the Traveler flees. In the forest, Eremite mercenaries attack, and Nahida's mind is caught while sharing the Traveler's body. The Traveler heads to the desert, finds Alhaitham, and at Aaru Village is drawn into a clash between Alhaitham and Cyno.
### STANDARD
Like a Triumphant Hero: some time after the samsara, Nahida takes over Katheryne at the Adventurers' Guild and says she wants to resolve the sages before turning to Irminsul and the Withering. The sages avoid wearing their Akasha Terminals, so she cannot read them. She chooses Setaria, a desert-born prodigy who is troubled by the sages' work and by being unable to help her people. Nahida learns the speech habits of Setaria's acquaintances and the Traveler, when Setaria goes shopping, speaks through their minds one at a time, pretending to be King Deshret's followers. Setaria cracks, agrees to steal confidential papers, and plans to hand them over at the Akademiya the next night. The place is full of cheering people: Dottore, who calls himself the Doctor, reveals that he overheard through a listening device, that Setaria is imprisoned, and that the crowd has been given the belief that the Traveler saved the world. Nahida steps in and tells the Traveler to run, revealing the scope of her abilities to Dottore. The Traveler and Paimon now know the Akademiya works with the Fatui and look for Tighnari.

The Gaze From a Certain God: paid Eremites ambush them near Gandharva Ville, sent by a client called the Outcast. Collei is there, unable to patrol because of Eleazar; Tighnari has gone to Pardis Dhyai. At Pardis Dhyai, "Katheryne" has escaped Dottore but expects him to find a way to trap her. Haypasia offers to show what she saw, but the Traveler sees Scaramouche's memories. In a fight "Katheryne" is speared; touching fingers with the Traveler, she swaps bodies with them, and the Traveler sees Nahida's true body in the Sanctuary of Surasthana and Scaramouche in the process of becoming a god. Nahida leaves a last clue before her mind is trapped. Tighnari rescues the Traveler and stays at Pardis Dhyai to watch Haypasia.

The Four at Loggerheads: Alhaitham helps them escape at Caravan Ribat and accompanies them to Aaru Village, where Cyno, the General Mahamatra, ambushes them. A sandstorm drives them into the village chief's house, where Candace presides. Cyno says he put himself in exile after noticing data missing from material Azar had submitted and being refused when he tried to investigate; Alhaitham admits he was ordered to track the Traveler but did not accept the job. Riftwolves attack; the Traveler, Dehya and Candace drive them off. The chief, Uncle Anpu, explains that the Village Keepers, exiled Akademiya scholars, have vanished. A boy, Isak, asks for help finding his grandfather.
### LARGE +
Like a Triumphant Hero in detail. Nahida has been posting commissions through Katheryne to watch the Traveler at work. She says she slept after the samsara and dreamed of a happy Sabzeruz Festival; Paimon remarks that this is how the festival should really be, and Nahida wonders whether the looks she gets are 'pity'. Dunyarzad is stable, but Eleazar can only be cured once Irminsul's Withering is dealt with. In planning, Nahida refuses to involve innocent students, reasoning that doing so would make her no better than the sages. Her description of Setaria uses a star-and-sun metaphor: Setaria was a bright star of the desert who was taken up by the Akademiya, saw the damage done, and covered her guilt. The Traveler, borrowing Nahida's power, possesses a series of people close to Setaria; one is a fortune-teller. Setaria's responses reveal that King Deshret's supposed resurrection is itself an Akademiya misinformation campaign. She calls Rima of the Corps of Thirty for help, and Rima too is taken over. Setaria finally agrees to work against the sages, though she says she knows little of the full project.

The Gaze From a Certain God in detail. The ambushers include a brigade leader who boasts of the highest commission rate in Sumeru and says their client is an outcast. In the fight at Pardis Dhyai, Katheryne's body is run through by a spear; Nahida indwells the Traveler, and the Traveler briefly occupies Nahida's real body and sees Scaramouche, who sees them in turn. Nahida's clue, "Even in a moonless night, a shower of starlight can still drown out illusions and lies", is read by the Traveler and Paimon as a pointer toward the Great Red Sand and its people.

The Four at Loggerheads in detail. Paimon recalls the Wall of Samiel as a protection against sandstorms. Alhaitham says he is still studying the capsule and suspects the Traveler of withholding something. At the village Cyno asks him to give up the Divine Knowledge Capsule; Alhaitham replies by questioning why Cyno is alone, since other matra do not know where he went. Dehya joins them and says she wanted to go home out of boredom. Candace asks them to explain, warns them not to fight in the village, and goes outside when growls are heard. Afterward Cyno and the Traveler meet privately: he explains that the Village Keepers were once shunned as useless until a scholar's glowing form shielded the whole village during an earthquake. The Traveler attributes this to the Dendro Archon, and Cyno is surprised to learn the Archon walks among people. Isak appears and Candace confirms his story.
### ULTRA +
Quest-level steps. Like a Triumphant Hero: talk to Katheryne; talk to Nahida elsewhere; gather information on Setaria's contacts; return to Nahida; wait until the next afternoon; carry out the plan in the market; follow Setaria; wait until the next night; meet Nahida; go to the Akademiya; and sort out the information. The Gaze From a Certain God: go to Gandharva Ville; defeat three waves of Eremites; find Tighnari; enter Pardis Dhyai. The Four at Loggerheads: go to Caravan Ribat; follow Alhaitham; go to Aaru Village; defeat two waves of rifthound whelps; exchange information with Cyno; talk to Candace; go to the chief's house.

Background details introduced. Setaria's genius earned her a place at the Akademiya, a rare chance for someone born in the desert; she takes a day off every ten days. Nahida's understanding is that the key members of the Akademiya, including Corps of Thirty guards, deliberately avoid wearing Terminals. Dottore's experiment tested what ideas can be put into the minds of Terminal users. The client who hired the Eremites is known only as the Outcast; Paimon wonders whether this means an outcast of the Akademiya, and the Traveler guesses it is the Doctor.

Name notes: the Wall of Samiel is the wall at Caravan Ribat that Paimon says blocks sandstorms; Aaru Village's chief is Uncle Anpu; Isak is the boy whose grandfather is a missing Village Keeper.

## revelations
@version: 3.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
- King Deshret's supposed resurrection is an Akademiya-run misinformation campaign, as Setaria reveals.
- The Akademiya is collaborating with the Fatui, and Dottore can use the Akasha to implant beliefs in a crowd.
- Haypasia's Paripurna Life vision shows Scaramouche's memories, not the Dendro Archon's.
- Alhaitham was assigned to track the Traveler; Cyno stepped down after discovering missing data and Azar refusing his request.
### LARGE +
- While swapped into Nahida's real body at the Sanctuary of Surasthana, the Traveler sees Scaramouche in the process of becoming a god, and he sees them.
- The Village Keepers are exiled scholars who were protected by a green light at an earthquake, which Cyno takes as the Dendro Archon's work.

## characters
### STANDARD
- **Traveler and Paimon**; **Nahida** (as Katheryne); **Setaria**; **Dottore**; **Tighnari and Collei**; **Alhaitham**; **Cyno**; **Dehya**; **Candace**; **Haypasia**, **Isak** and **Uncle Anpu**; **Scaramouche** (seen in vision).
### LARGE +
Roles:
- **Nahida**: leads the plan and loses her mind to the Akasha.
- **Setaria**: the target of the pressure plan; imprisoned after being overheard.
- **Dottore**: sets the trap and pays the Eremites.
- **Tighnari, Collei**: the Forest Rangers in Gandharva Ville and Pardis Dhyai.
- **Alhaitham, Cyno, Dehya, Candace**: the four at loggerheads, who meet at Aaru Village.
- **Rima**: a Corps of Thirty member briefly possessed by the Traveler.

## consequences
### STANDARD
Nahida is cut off from the Traveler and trapped; Setaria is imprisoned; the Traveler and Paimon are on the run from the Eremites and Fatui. They find allies in the desert and Isak's grandfather's disappearance becomes the next task in Act IV.
### LARGE +
Tighnari remains at Pardis Dhyai watching Haypasia. Alhaitham's divided loyalties and Cyno's exile become central to the Act IV and V plan.

## placement
### STANDARD
Released in Version 3.1. It follows Act II, The Morn a Thousand Roses Brings (3.0), and leads directly into Act IV, King Deshret and the Three Magi (3.1).
### LARGE +
Act III is the third of five Sumeru acts in Chapter III. The quest "Dawn" at the end of Act II leads into its first quest.
