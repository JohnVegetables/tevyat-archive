## summary
### LITE
The first act of the Song of the Welkin Moon chapter and the Traveler's arrival in Nod-Krai. After a send-off in Natlan, the Traveler, Paimon and Ineffa sail north, feel kuuvahki for the first time and look for clues to the Traveler's crashed spaceship. They meet the Moonchanter Lauma of the Frostmoon Scions, learn the Fatui are hunting a "relic", and meet Columbina, whom the Scions call their goddess Kuutar. The Traveler and their sibling break into the sanctum holding the Moon Marrow, the Wild Hunt attacks, and in the end the Traveler agrees to carry the Marrow in their own body.
### STANDARD +
The act has three quests: Where the Moon Rises, Reunion Beneath the Moonlight, and In the Shadows of Night. It requires the Interlude Chapter's last act, Paralogism, and is followed by Elegy of Dust and Lamplight.
### LARGE +
It introduces most of Nod-Krai's major local factions and people at once (the Ratnik Flins, the Frostmoon Scions, the Fatui under Marionette, Nefer's Curatorium of Secrets) and the region's central mystery: the three moons, the Moon Marrow, and the Traveler's memory of their crash.

## plot
### STANDARD
The Traveler, Paimon and Ineffa leave Natlan after a farewell party and sail to Nod-Krai, where the Traveler feels kuuvahki, the moon's power, and recalls the same feeling from leaving their spaceship. They learn Varka is away, and meet Flins, the Frostmoon Scions' Moonchanter Lauma, Marionette of the Fatui, and Aino, who suggests the Scions' ritual to see the true past. Lauma explains the Three Moons and the Moon Marrow relic that the Fatui demand; the Traveler tells her they are a Descender. They meet Columbina, who is called Kuutar by the Scions, and then the Traveler and their sibling break into the relic's sanctum. The Wild Hunt attacks, the relic goes missing, and with Nefer's Curatorium hiring Jahoda, Lauma and Jahoda help the Traveler track it to the Fatui and recover it from Marionette. A phantom impersonating the sibling is exposed, and the Traveler agrees to carry the Moon Marrow in their own body while Lauma tells the Scions that the sky is fake.
### LARGE +
In Natlan, Ineffa waits for the Traveler and Paimon outside the Stadium of the Sacred Flame, saying conditions for travel are good. Mualani, Kachina, Kinich and Ajaw arrive for a farewell party that Paimon had told them about, and Kachina invites Ineffa too. Unseen, someone watches and reports to Sandrone in Nod-Krai, who says it is about time she met the "variable" in person.

On the boat to Nod-Krai Ineffa says the moment she knew she had entered Natlan was when she could no longer sense the moon's power. At Nasha Town the Traveler feels a power in the land that matches how they felt when they left their ship after landing in Teyvat. Ineffa names it kuuvahki, the moon's power, a pure primordial elemental energy like Phlogiston or the Primordial Sea. The Traveler guesses it is the Light Realm power Yohualtecuhtin mentioned, and that their spaceship may be nearby, perhaps near a beach. Ineffa notes Nod-Krai is islands, so that fits many shores.

Following a kuuhenki (a local sprite and familiar of the Moon Goddess) around town, Ineffa explains Nod-Krai's past and present and Aino's "Boom and Blast Kuuvahki Cannon", used when the Wild Hunt grows too dangerous. At the Flagship the bartender Demyan says Varka has gone with his knights to Amsvartnir, in the north, where trouble had been brewing; he left lodging paid and a portion of Berries and Fried Meat for Ineffa to bring Aino. In the hallway Paimon bumps into Flins, a Ratnik who fends off the Wild Hunt at the northern graveyards and is in town to repair his lantern; the Traveler feels something is off about it. Lauma, a Moonchanter of the Frostmoon Scions, is handing out food; when the Fatui soldiers Oiva and Ognjen demand some, she still feeds them but warns that the Scions' kindness has limits. In the Adventurers' Guild branch the Traveler finds Marionette, the Seventh Harbinger, who gives a friendly warning not to interfere in Fatui matters; the Traveler realises the Fatui have been watching them.

Aino has never seen a spaceship and says she has taken apart most of Nod-Krai's machinery without finding anything alien. She suggests the Frostmoon Scions, who have a ritual to see the "true" past. On Hiisi Island Lauma speaks with the wildlife, explains the Scions' statues of their goddess Kuutar, and recognises the kuuhenki following the Traveler as a rare kind called a Luonnotar. In her library she tells of her people's origin in Hyperborea and the Three Moons: in the war with the Descenders only the Frost Moon stayed whole while the Eternal and Iridescent Moons shattered, their fragments falling on Nod-Krai, and the Fatui have pulled down parts of the Eternal Moon. A prophecy said the Moon Goddess would return. Oiva and Ognjen later give the Scions an ultimatum: surrender the relic and the location of their "Kuutar" in three days or the Fatui will cleanse the island. A scuffle drives them off. Lauma suspects the Fatui's "Kuutar" is the Harbinger Columbina. The Traveler admits they are the latest Descender; Lauma agrees to help, though the "Moon Marrow" can only be retrieved in a ritual when the moon is closest to Teyvat, about two months away.

The Traveler cannot sleep and walks out; following the Luonnotar's song they find a young woman in an underground flower cavern. She says she has been called Columbina and Kuutar but does not know her own name because she was born alone. The Traveler then meets their sibling outside; the sibling refuses to wait two months, uses Abyssal power to open a hidden staircase at the Kuutar statue, and the pair pass the sanctum's trials (a kuuvahki puzzle, weak monsters, moving statues) and its guardian. The sibling asks the Traveler to take the relic; afterwards the Traveler finds themselves outside, surrounded by the Wild Hunt, until Flins arrives and destroys them. The sibling has vanished.

The next day, overheard talk of the relic's theft leads them to Nefer's Curatorium of Secrets. The Traveler confesses to Lauma; she is not angry. Nefer notes that the Fatui have apparently called off their search. Through Lauma's manoeuvring, Jahoda is hired to find the relic. That night Lauma, her antlers grown, asks whether the sky is fake, which the Traveler confirms, and mixes her silver blood into medicine for Kapy's daughter Kaarina. The Traveler later brings Paimon and food to Columbina, who says the Frostmoon Scions once prayed to her for answers, that the Fatui also wanted only her power, and that she helped the Tsaritsa at the cost of much of her strength.

In the third quest, Lauma and Jahoda lead the Traveler to the Fatui site, using terminals and a ventilation shaft. Inside they find Vilemina, who left the Scions for the Fatui because she could no longer live a lie, and who took the Moon Marrow. Lauma admits she knew the truth about the sky. Vilemina cuts off her own antlers as the Scions' punishment for betrayal and says Marionette took the Marrow to Starsand Shoal. Marionette is confronted outside; her subordinates do not answer because the Wild Hunt is attacking. In the mist the Traveler hears the sibling's voice but realises it is a phantom, since they had been told the sibling found them still in their hypersleep pod, not that the two left the ship together as the voice claimed. Marionette extracts the Marrow from her robot Pulonia and tosses it to them. Flins arrives, learns the Wild Hunt has changed, and leaves to warn his superiors; Lauma later says animals fear him.
Back at the Curatorium, the Marrow can no longer be stored in the compromised chamber. Lauma offers to be its vessel, as a past Moonchanter had been for two days when the Scions' bloodline was strong, but she thinks she could manage five days at most. The Traveler convinces her to let them hold it, with Paimon arguing it is less dangerous than the Abyssal corruption they have already resisted. Nefer returns with medicine and learns of a phantom that can impersonate others. Lauma then tells the Scions the whole truth about the fake sky; some accept, some leave because losing faith felt like losing home, and some thank her for being closer to them. She asks the Traveler how she looks and says she feels she is "finally living on the bright side of the moon."
### ULTRA +
Smaller details. Dori runs a curios shop after the Fatui pulled down two Ancient Moon remnants; Katheryne is at the guild branch too. Ineffa stays with Aino, who had broken their agreement on snacks. Lauma tells Kapy not all Snezhnayans should be confused with the Fatui, while Kapy distrusts the Snezhnayan doctors. The Kuuvahki Experimental Design Bureau was built over one of the large moon remnants. Vilemina asks the Traveler to look after Lauma, and is later shown intending to spend her last night with the group, troubled that the other Scions refused to accept the truth. The Frostmoon statues were designed with Kuutar's likeness, and the Fatui have been defacing them except on Hiisi Island. Lauma says Kuutar left angry at the Scions' greed and has been reported on the island again.

## revelations
@version: 6.0
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Nod-Krai's land carries kuuvahki, the same kind of power the Traveler felt on leaving their ship; the Traveler concludes their ship is somewhere in the region. The Fatui's "Kuutar" is Columbina. The Three Moons (Frost, Eternal, Iridescent) are tied to a war with the Descenders. The Traveler tells Lauma they are a Descender.
### LARGE +
The sky above Nod-Krai is fake: Lauma has long felt the moon's power coming from a different direction than the moon she sees, and she tells the Frostmoon Scions at the end of the act. Phantoms of the Wild Hunt can impersonate people, including the Traveler's sibling, and the Wild Hunt has changed in nature. The Moon Marrow is now stored in the Traveler's body. Columbina says the Fatui, like the Scions, valued her only for her power.

## characters
### STANDARD
Traveler and Paimon, accompanied by Ineffa at first, with Aino, Varka (absent), Flins, Lauma (central ally), Columbina (Fatui Harbinger and the Scions' goddess), Sandrone (the Harbinger Marionette, first seen being told of the Traveler's departure, then confronting them in person), Nefer and Jahoda; Vilemina, Demyan, Katheryne and Dori in minor roles. The Traveler's sibling appears in the sanctum scene and a phantom later impersonates them.

## consequences
### STANDARD
The Traveler now carries the Moon Marrow and has Lauma, Nefer and Jahoda as allies, while the Fatui know them by sight. Lauma's revelation changes the Frostmoon Scions. The act sets up Elegy of Dust and Lamplight and the later acts that follow Flins and Columbina.

## placement
### STANDARD
Song of the Welkin Moon Act I, added in version 6.0 (Luna I, 2025-09-10). It requires Paralogism, the final act of the Interlude Chapter, and is followed by Elegy of Dust and Lamplight and then A Nation That Doesn't Exist. Story order equals release order.

### STANDARD
In A Dance of Snowy Tides and Hoarfrost Groves the Moon Marrow is the "relic" the Fatui and the Traveler's sibling are looking for.
