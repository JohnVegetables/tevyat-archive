## summary
### LITE
Act VIII of the Song of the Welkin Moon Archon Quest, released in Version 6.3. The Traveler and their allies rescue Columbina from the moon's reflection, fight Dottore, the self-styled "Heretic of the False Moon", and bring the Frost Moon down into Teyvat. Sandrone dies shielding the Traveler from him, and afterwards Columbina goes to live among the Frostmoon Scions of Nod-Krai.
### STANDARD +
The act has three quests: The First Sliver of Moonlight, Descending Moon and Welkin Moon's Homecoming. It resolves the Nod-Krai confrontation with Dottore, answers where Columbina has been, and closes with farewells, a new joint League and a meeting with Columbina outside the Silvermoon Hall, where she rarely met the Traveler before.
### LARGE +
It is the climax of the Nod-Krai chapter: the Moon Sisters' prison, Rerir's and Tholindis' sides of an old story, and Sandrone's long-planned world-formula all converge in one battle. Teyvat's sky now holds the real Frost Moon, where the Heavenly Principles had placed the Traveler's spaceship.

## plot
### LITE
Dottore pressures the Traveler to serve him; they refuse and are rescued by the Wanderer. Columbina, trapped in the moon's reflection, wins the help of the three Moon Sisters and sacrifices her body to return. In the final battle Sandrone dies, the Frost Moon is pulled into Teyvat, and Columbina and the Traveler defeat Dottore.
### STANDARD +
**The First Sliver of Moonlight.** In Dottore's realm the Traveler refuses his offer, even after he lowers his demands, because they will not kill innocents and become like Rerir. They try to strike first but Dottore survives and overpowers them. The Wanderer cracks the dome with an alchemical signal and rescues them, giving up a chance at revenge on Dottore. Back at the curatorium the Traveler shares what Dottore said. Nefer reveals that Columbina is still alive, trapped in a space that flows backwards, and Nicole recounts the origin of the Moon Sisters.

**Descending Moon.** Sandrone adds a channel to the Dodocommunication Devices so the group can follow her progress. Dottore's forces besiege Nasha Town and are repelled, then Dottore arrives in a new form and tears the false sky open, pulling everyone into a vast arena above the clouds. He separates Flins, Lauma and Nefer into pocket spaces and offers the Traveler a last chance to join him. Pulonia and Sandrone interrupt, but both are overpowered. Sandrone takes the blow meant for the Traveler and is killed after telling Dottore to go to hell.

With her formula seemingly frozen at 90 percent, Dottore gloats. Then the formula advances: the Frost Moon appears through the gash. Arlecchino, Varka and Nicole drag the moon into Teyvat, shattering the false sky. Columbina's body, which had been dispersed as moonlight over Nod-Krai, and her spirit, carried to Luonnotar, join the Trilunar Authority on the Frost Moon, and she descends with her eyes open for the first time. With the Traveler she retakes the Iridescent Marrow, frees her friends and destroys Dottore's artificial Moon Marrow. Afterwards she cradles Sandrone's body.

**Welkin Moon's Homecoming.** The Frost Moon stays above Teyvat, people return to Nod-Krai and a new League is formed by the region's factions. The Traveler says farewell to Arlecchino and the Wanderer at the port, meets Columbina on Hiisi Island, and visits their spaceship on the Frost Moon. Columbina gives the Traveler a Moon-Prayer Blossom and eases some of Teyvat's restrictions on them.
### LARGE +
Inside the reflection, Columbina meets Rerir, who now speaks calmly, has no wish to fight and admits her plan to siphon his power was ingenious. He says Dottore revived him, timing it to her weakness, and asks her to carry a message to Tholindis. Tholindis, once a moon marrow researcher, explains that she tried to enter the reflection to change her past mistakes and was torn apart, leaving only her soul. She wanted to prevent Irmin from meeting the Traveler's sibling. Columbina then meets Sonnet, the youngest of the Three Moons, and her elder sisters Canon and Aria, who are held in a prison made by Nibelung.

Outside, the Wanderer, Sandrone, Albedo and Durin prepare. The Wanderer offers his core so that Sandrone's equation can be computed directly in the ley lines, accepting that he might be reset or lost.

Details of the descent into the past: Columbina asks the sisters whether they ever escaped (they always returned to the prison), and demonstrates kuuvahki, which none of them know. They realise she wants to borrow their authorities against Dottore, which could tear her apart and still not let her leave alive. Her plan is to leave part of her soul in the future, "die" in the prison and scatter her body as moonlight across the Nod-Krai of the past, to be reassembled by a signal in the future. Nefer, using Luonnotar, watches this past at the risk of her sight, but Columbina bears that cost for her. Canon asks why Columbina keeps her eyes closed; she answers that she wanted to avoid the lies she saw everywhere, and Canon makes her promise to open them in the future. Transferring their powers to her makes the sisters mortal, and at their request she sends them back to face their end together in their own time.

In the arena, the plan Sandrone had laid out was that her formula would handle about 90 percent of the work (the link to Columbina) and the remaining 10 percent was bringing the moon down by any means. The Wanderer's Irminsul core, hidden inside Pulonia, finalised the formula. Arlecchino evacuated civilians beforehand, and Varka asked Nicole to extend her agreement to cover Nod-Krai in order to stall Dottore.

### ULTRA +
Welkin Moon's Homecoming in more detail: in Nasha Town, Jahoda greets the Traveler and Paimon, Aino and Ineffa help distribute food for the Frostmoon Scions, and Tarno addresses the crowd. At the port, Arlecchino and the Wanderer say Columbina mourned Sandrone alone for a long time, and Arlecchino credits the Traveler's friendship with Columbina's growth; Columbina comforted others even while grieving. On Hiisi Island, the older Frostmoon Scions are timid around Columbina while the children are curious. The Traveler returns the friends' greeting card she left behind when she fled into the reflection. On the ship, the flight logs show it passed Teyvat once long ago; the Traveler dons an outfit left in a cabinet, and says Teyvat is their home even if it differs from what they knew. Varka's letter to Jean notes a temporary alliance with a more benevolent Harbinger and his continued wariness of Rooster, Regrator and Jester.

## revelations
### STANDARD
- Columbina survived and was held in a backwards-flowing space; Rerir was revived by Dottore and has recovered his composure.
- The Three Moons (Canon, Aria, Sonnet) were imprisoned by Nibelung and were aware of what was coming.
- Sandrone deliberately placed the real formula inside Pulonia and a false one in herself, fooling Dottore.
- The Heavenly Principles placed the Traveler's ship on the Frost Moon, and the ship passed Teyvat once thousands of years before the crash-landing.
### LARGE +
- The Frost Moon is now permanently visible in Teyvat, and the disaster has made Teyvat easier for the Abyss to detect, per Nicole.
- Columbina shares impressions of Snezhnaya: major cities are built around enormous heat sources called Kresnik's Torch, and she mentions the term Mistilteinn in connection with Project Stuzha. She describes the Tsaritsa as clear yet unfathomable, like ice over a frozen lake, and warns that Jester, Regrator and Rooster are not united (Regrator and Rooster dislike each other in private).

## characters
### LARGE
Columbina is the central figure, supported by the Traveler and Paimon. Dottore is the antagonist; Sandrone is the pivotal sacrifice. The Wanderer, Albedo and Durin work beside Sandrone; Nefer, Flins, Lauma and Jahoda act from the curatorium and Nasha Town; Arlecchino, Varka and Nicole bring down the moon. Rerir, Tholindis, Sonnet, Canon and Aria appear inside the moon's reflection. Aino, Ineffa, Illuga and Tarno appear in the aftermath.
Jahoda takes over the curatorium at Nefer's request under an earlier agreement. Nicole and Alice offer help, and Nicole later reports that the border disturbance has been handled. Pulonia, Sandrone's construct, carries the Wanderer's Irminsul core.

## consequences
### STANDARD
Dottore is destroyed. Sandrone dies, and per her verbal will Arlecchino takes her core and Pulonia's to the Fontaine Research Institute while her notes go to the House of the Hearth, with one book delivered to the Traveler's room on the Flagship. Columbina is not to be told. The Tsaritsa revokes the Palestar Edict, Columbina's arrest warrant, and Columbina moves in with the Frostmoon Scions on Hiisi Island, hoping to attend the next Moon-Prayer Night openly. A new League of Nod-Krai factions is set up, with Flins urging Illuga to represent the Lightkeepers. The Wanderer returns to Sumeru to help Nahida with Dottore's fallout. Varka writes to Jean that the expedition will come home in waves.
### LARGE +
Columbina has given the Eternal Moon Marrow to Lauma and the Iridescent Moon Marrow to Arlecchino, and will return part of the Frost Moon to Nod-Krai as kuuvahki. She also lifts some of Teyvat's limits on the Traveler's use of the elements.

## placement
### STANDARD
Act VIII of Song of the Welkin Moon, following A Traveler on a Winter's Night and preceding As All Falls to Emptiness. Released in Version 6.3 (Luna IV), which also introduced Columbina.
### LARGE +
Story order and release order coincide for this act. The wiki lists an Adventure Rank requirement of 40.
