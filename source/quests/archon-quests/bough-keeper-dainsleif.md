## summary
### LITE
Bough Keeper: Dainsleif is a Mondstadt world quest from Version 1.3 that works as the prelude to Chapter I, Act IV of the Archon Quests. Katheryne tells the Traveler about a tall blond stranger with an eyepatch. The Traveler and Paimon find him at the Angel's Share, where he introduces himself as Dainsleif and agrees to be commissioned for 500 Mora and three questions. He takes only work against the Abyss Order, and together they search the Temple of the Falcon, Wolvendom and Stormterror's Lair for an Abyss Herald without finding one. The quest ends at a trampled dandelion that the Traveler senses a link to their sibling through.
### STANDARD +
The quest is a world quest by category but is voiced and structured like an Archon or Story Quest, and it carries a unique emblem combining the two siblings' constellations. Unlike the earlier Archon Quests, which centre on a nation and its Archon, it is built around the Traveler's own search for their sibling.

## plot
### LITE
Katheryne of the Adventurers' Guild says a tall, unusually dressed stranger with an eyepatch has arrived in Mondstadt and refused the Guild's invitation. Lawrence at the gate lost track of him, Wagner says he asked for odd forging materials, and Charles says he drinks at the Angel's Share every night. The Traveler waits on the tavern's upper floor, then meets him.
### STANDARD
The stranger first ignores the party, then relents when the Traveler says they are looking for a lost relative. He gives his name, Dainsleif, and agrees to join them in return for an advance of 500 Mora and three questions. The questions ask who was the key to ending the Stormterror crisis, who will defend Liyue Harbor after its god, and whether those with Visions or those without matter more to the gods. He says no answer is right or wrong and that he only wants to see the Traveler's attitude. He then states that he accepts only commissions concerning the Abyss Order, which he opposes, and that he is tracking an Abyss Herald to Mondstadt.

He leads the party to the abandoned Temple of the Falcon, where the Abyssal Traces domain is cleared of Abyss Mages and no Herald is found. Traces lead to Wolvendom, where Abyssal marks float over two bonfires, but Dainsleif judges them the work of mages without deeper Abyssal power. The last lead is Stormterror's Lair; the party fights more monsters near the Light Actuators and again finds no Herald.
### LARGE +
At the Lair the Traveler senses something familiar on a nearby cliff. Dainsleif and the party climb to the top and find nothing out of place except a Ruin Guard footprint and a trampled dandelion. When the Traveler touches the dandelion, they recall their encounter with the unknown god and feel their sibling's presence. Dainsleif suggests the flower may carry a "scent" only the Traveler can recognise, urges patience, and tells them that at least they now know the sibling is still in this world, and that the journey to find them is what matters most now. He then leaves, saying they will meet again, since he has taken the 500 Mora and the three answers.
### ULTRA +
Scene order: the quest begins from Katheryne in Mondstadt City; the three inquiries (Lawrence, Wagner, Charles) follow, each NPC having an optional repeat line. The wait on the tavern's second floor is a short skip-forward. If the player picks the third option of the questions at least twice, Dainsleif remarks that the Traveler really is similar to their sibling; otherwise he says they seem different from the sibling. He never explains the remark, and the Traveler's question about it goes unanswered. The 500 Mora is only a storytelling device and is never actually deducted.

## revelations
@version: 1.3
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
- Dainsleif already knows that the Anemo Archon is Venti, the course of the Stormterror crisis, and Rex Lapis's Contract to End All Contracts. He says he knows everything he should.
- The Abyss Order has stayed away from the Traveler since the Stormterror incident; Dainsleif says they may have been under orders to avoid the Traveler's path.
- Abyss Heralds exist: more twisted Abyssal beings that command Abyss Mages.
- He describes the Abyss as chaos and destruction eating at the world's foundations, strong enough that even the Dragon of the East, a servant of a deity, could not resist its corrosion; its creatures want to overthrow a world ruled by archons.
### LARGE +
- Dainsleif implies a long personal history with the Abyss ("some history") and with Old Mondstadt: he saw its ruins before the Dragon of the East fell or nested there, which puzzles Paimon, who thinks Dvalin's trouble began only hundreds of years ago. He tells her not to think too much about it.
- A former travelling companion, never named, once took him around Teyvat; that companion "no longer travels". He says he cannot remember his own favourite place because he merely followed this companion.
- The dandelion shows the Traveler that their sibling is still alive in the world.

## characters
### STANDARD
- **Dainsleif**: tall, blond, one eye covered by an eyepatch, calm, formal and cryptic; takes commissions only against the Abyss Order.
- **Paimon**: calls him "Dain", worries about the payment terms and asks about his past.
- **The Traveler**: answers his questions and senses the familiar presence at the cliff.
- **Katheryne, Lawrence, Wagner, Charles**: supply the trail. Lawrence is a gate guard who shadowed the stranger and lost him; Wagner is the blacksmith the stranger asked about unfamiliar materials; Charles is the Angel's Share bartender who calls him a connoisseur who mixes his own drinks.
### LARGE +
Katheryne's mention of the eyepatch prompts the Traveler to joke that the stranger is a distant relative of Kaeya; Paimon dismisses the idea. Lawrence prefers not to tell Jean, since she might overreact. Charles says the stranger ordered a bottle in advance, and Paimon assumes Diluc is watching him too.

## consequences
### STANDARD
Dainsleif now treats the Traveler as a client and says they will meet again, which leads into Act IV. The Traveler learns their sibling is still in the world and meets the Abyss Order as an ongoing threat. Rewards include 60 Primogems, 38,100 Mora, some materials and the achievement Gears of Destiny.

## placement
### STANDARD
Released in Version 1.3 as a world quest requiring A New Star Approaches. It is shown on the quest screen as Chapter I: Act IV - Prelude and leads into Archon Quest Chapter I, Act IV: We Will Be Reunited. It does not appear in the Archive's completed quest list, only in the quest overview Travel Log.
### LARGE +
The route passes places tied to each of the Four Winds: Mondstadt City (the Lion of the South), the Temple of the Falcon (Falcon of the West), Wolvendom (Wolf of the North) and Stormterror's Lair (Dragon of the East). This pairing is an observation about the locations, not something the characters say.

## entry: Dainsleif's Three Questions
@keys: Dainsleif's questions, three questions, 500 Mora
@keys2: Dainsleif
@version: 1.3
### STANDARD
At the Angel's Share, Dainsleif asks three questions as payment: who was the key to ending the Mondstadt crisis (Venti, the Traveler, or Mondstadt's united people); who will defend Liyue Harbor now that Rex Lapis has left (the adepti, the Liyue Qixing, or everyone in Liyue Harbor); and who matters more to the gods (those with Visions, those without, or perhaps neither). He treats the answers as showing attitude, not correctness.
### LARGE +
The answers change only a single line of dialogue near the end, in which he compares the Traveler to the sibling. The fee of 500 Mora is not actually taken.

## entry: Abyss Herald Search
@keys: Abyss Herald search, Abyssal Traces, Light Actuators
@keys2: Dainsleif
@version: 1.3
### STANDARD
The hunt visits the Temple of the Falcon (an Abyssal Traces quest domain with Abyss Mages), Wolvendom (Abyssal spell marks over bonfires, guarded by mages and hilichurls) and the Light Actuators of Stormterror's Lair (more monsters). No Abyss Herald appears. Dainsleif says the Herald may already have left and that the Traveler will meet one eventually.

## entry: The Trampled Dandelion
@keys: trampled dandelion, dandelion on the cliff
@keys2: Dainsleif, Traveler
@version: 1.3
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
On the cliff above Stormterror's Lair, Dainsleif notices a Ruin Guard footprint and stops by a crushed dandelion. The Traveler touches it, recalls the unknown god and feels their sibling. Dainsleif says the sibling is still in this world and tells them to keep the journey to find them in mind as the meaning behind their travels.
