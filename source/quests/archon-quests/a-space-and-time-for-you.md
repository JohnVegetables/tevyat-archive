## summary
@spoiler: major
### LITE
A Space and Time for You is Act VI of the Natlan chapter and a Traveler chapter, the Traveler and Paimon's personal storyline. In Natlan they find their lost sibling's spaceship key, meet a woman called Direidyth who seems to be looking for a "Savior", fight the Abyss Order with Dainsleif, and discover the sibling has used the Loom of Fate to weave new Khaenri'ah Ley Lines.
### STANDARD
Three quests make up the act: The Key That Unlocks Fate, "Savior", and A Space and Time Without You. It requires Adventure Rank 40 and follows the Traveler's pursuit of their sibling across the Abyss Order's coordinated attack on all Natlan tribes. The sibling does not fight here; the attack was a distraction.
### LARGE +
Themes are time and space, the Traveler and sibling as mirrored figures, and Dainsleif's and the Order's Khaenri'ahn pasts. It is also where the Traveler tells Mavuika, Iansan and Citlali that the Abyss Order's leader is their sibling.

## plot
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
**The Key That Unlocks Fate.** Iansan asks for help patrolling the Collective of Plenty's land before a bodybuilding contest. In a hidden spot the Traveler and Paimon find a sleeping hilichurl who runs off, leaving a club and the key to the Hibernation Chamber of the ship that brought the twins to Teyvat. The Traveler still has their own, so this is the sibling's. The Abyss Order then attacks every tribe at once. Mavuika lets the Traveler stay out, but the Traveler declines and reveals the sibling leads the Order. With Citlali and Iansan they destroy a stronghold.

**"Savior".** At the cave, a woman called Direidyth arrives, wounded, seeing monsters that the Traveler cannot unless they touch her hand. She was sent by her grandmother to find the Savior who would stop a doomsday prophecy; the Traveler matches all but the gender, so it is the sibling. She stays in the cave. Citlali senses a "noise" in Natlan's Ley Lines. The Traveler feeds Direidyth, they watch the sky together, and the Traveler dreams of the sibling speaking with Yohualtecuhtin in the Night Kingdom.

**A Space and Time Without You.** Dainsleif arrives, having crushed the Order's front line. He says "Savior" was a title for the sibling before the Cataclysm and guesses Direidyth is a pure-blooded Khaenri'ahn. The Scions of the Canopy find the Order's headquarters in Ochkanatlan and Dainsleif and the Traveler assault it. There an Iniquitous Baptist claims the Order's wish is fulfilled: the sibling has used the Loom of Fate to re-weave Khaenri'ah's Ley Lines. The Baptist is Haden, Dainsleif's former master. The sibling then appears.
### LARGE +
Details of "The Key That Unlocks Fate": Iansan says the Order had never attacked the tribes in a coordinated way before; Mavuika was about to send for the Traveler; the Traveler tells Citlali about the sibling and she is surprised. Iansan notices the Order did not seem to give their all.

"Savior": Direidyth is flustered by holding hands. Citlali is jealous and calls her bad news. Direidyth swings a club in fear of the monsters, wonders whether the starry sky is real (the Traveler thinks of the false sky), and holds the Traveler's hand while the Traveler kills Abyss Mages she cannot otherwise see. Paimon suggests she write to Mavuika, though she has no idea who that is.

Dainsleif says he will make the Order's campaign so painful it cannot be repeated. The tribes have waited for the Pyro Archon; he finds it wasteful and goes ahead. At the centre the Baptist asks if this is what Dainsleif wanted and says the Order's wishes were fulfilled; the Traveler pieces together what the Ley Lines imply, Dainsleif calls it a lie.
### ULTRA +
Dainsleif and Haden: Haden wounds Dainsleif, who kills him a hundred times (the curse of immortality) as punishment for joining the Order, and a second hundred after Haden admits that his claim of Khaenri'ah's revival was a bluff. To restore Khaenri'ah they needed its memories, fragmented since the Cataclysm; Haden sacrificed his army to obtain Dainsleif's. Dainsleif also reminds him of Surtalogi, who was punished for keeping the knightly code while Haden kept his prestige by suppressing newer knights.

The sibling: Direidyth and the Traveler conclude they are from different times. The sibling says Istaroth had a role, that Direidyth catching up to them made them abandon Khaenri'ah, and that the attack on Natlan was a feint so they could talk to Yohualtecuhtin, who gave them room to weave the new Ley Lines. The weaving caused a superimposition on Natlan, so Direidyth can see the Traveler, and produced the Atlas of the New World carrying New Khaenriah's Ley Lines. All that is needed is a place to lay them, in order to speak to the Heavenly Principles as equals. The sibling leaves hoping for a meeting in the new world.

Back with Citlali, who was surprised by Yohualtecuhtin's action but intrigued by preserving a nation as an "Atlas" and permitted it once told Natlan would be unharmed; he had another condition he did not share. Iansan offers a feast; the Traveler returns to the cave and sleeps with a soothing presence nearby.

## revelations
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The Abyss Order's attack on Natlan was a diversion. The sibling used the Loom of Fate to re-weave Khaenri'ah's Ley Lines, creating the Atlas of the New World. "Savior" was a title applied to the sibling before the Cataclysm. The Baptist leader is Haden, Dainsleif's old master. Direidyth and the Traveler are from different times, and Istaroth's hand is suspected.

## characters
### STANDARD
Traveler and Paimon; the sibling (seen in dreams and at the end); Direidyth, a wounded visitor who sees monsters others cannot; Iansan, Mavuika and Citlali of the Natlan side; Dainsleif; Haden; Yohualtecuhtin (in the Night Kingdom and by report); Istaroth (by implication).

## consequences
### STANDARD
Mavuika, Iansan and Citlali now know the Order is led by the Traveler's sibling. The Order's Natlan headquarters falls, and Dainsleif kills Haden two hundred times over (Haden bears the curse of immortality). The sibling has a new Ley Line set and is aiming to address the Heavenly Principles as equals. The next act is The Journey Home.

## placement
### STANDARD
This act follows Incandescent Ode of Resurrection and Paralogism in the Chapter V navigation and precedes The Journey Home. It is a Traveler chapter, released with Version 5.7.
