## summary
@spoiler: major
### LITE
Act VI is where Columbina, the fading Moon Goddess, finally gets a name that holds her in the world: with the help of the Traveler, Lauma, Arlecchino and Nicole she is called back and takes the name Columbina Hyposelenia. She spends a last festival, Moon-Prayer Night, among friends in Nasha Town. When she tries to return to the moon, Dottore ambushes the ritual with a false moon; Columbina gives up her homecoming, leaps into a moon gate and vanishes.
### STANDARD
The act begins with a search through Hyperborean ruins for Columbina's "true name", which Alice and Nicole believe is the only way to stop her fading from existence. Murals there tell how the three moons were created, turned into weapons and broken, and Nicole decides Columbina never had a true name at all: a name can be given in the present, and its emotional meaning gives it power. The Traveler's name for her, Columbina, is accepted, and she is restored at Silvermoon Hall as Columbina Hyposelenia.

Moon-Prayer Night then becomes her farewell. She is welcomed home by Nasha Town, plays and tastes and talks with friends and acquaintances, and receives a handmade card. At the ruins' lunar-communication platform, Dottore strikes: he has built a false moon, seizes the power of all three moons and freezes time. Columbina realises her presence lets him wield that power, escapes through a moon gate and is lost. The allies regroup, Arlecchino vows to act against Dottore as an individual, and Varka rallies factions across Teyvat. A short optional quest follows the Traveler to a Silvermoon Hall that is missing its goddess.
### LARGE +
Thematically the act is about belonging and naming. Columbina is shown as a friend among friends rather than a god or a Harbinger. It also sets up the cruelty that carries into Acts VII and VIII: the moment she gains a stable self is the moment Dottore is able to catch her, and her escape costs her the home she had just been promised.

## plot
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
**Where Does Her Name Rest?** Searching Hyperborean ruins for Columbina's true name, the Traveler, Paimon, Lauma, Arlecchino and Nicole read murals on how Nibelung made the three moons and how they were weaponised, shattered or cast away. Nicole concludes Columbina never had a true name; a name can be given in the present. The Traveler's name for her is accepted, and at Silvermoon Hall she is restored as Columbina Hyposelenia ("born beneath the moon").

**The Night the Moon Returns Home.** On Moon-Prayer Night Nasha Town welcomes her home. She plays games, tries sweets, and shares quiet and playful moments with the Traveler, Arlecchino, Nefer, Flins, Nicole, Sandrone, Alice and Barbeloth. Her friends give her a handmade card and the town sees her off.

**Return to the Moon.** At the Hyperborean platform Dottore ambushes the ritual with a false moon, seizes the power of all three moons and freezes time. Columbina gives up her homecoming, leaps into a moon gate and vanishes. Arlecchino vows to act against Dottore as Peruere if the Tsaritsa will not, and Varka rallies allies across Teyvat.

**The Empty Courtyard (optional).** At Silvermoon Hall the Traveler and Paimon meet Luonnotar and the kuuhenki, and later a lonely Sandrone.
### LARGE +
Fuller account. **Ruins.** Phantom Hyperboreans speak only of the old moon goddesses, since they lived long before Columbina. The group advances with kuuvahki-powered devices. The murals say the moons were used in Nibelung's war against the Heavenly Principles until their goddesses died; one moon survived, and that faint link is why Columbina could be born. They reach the lunar-communication platform where Columbina can be sent home on Moon-Prayer Night. Nicole says a name is powerful because of the feeling behind it. Lauma's antlers glow as the festive moon rises; the Traveler and friends call out her name across Nod-Krai. At Silvermoon Hall she explains that she followed them in spirit even when too weak to manifest. Nicole, appearing through a Dodoco projection, announces Moon-Prayer Night has begun, and the townspeople greet her with "Welcome home, Moon Goddess."

**The festival.** She tries candy flavours, plays with townsfolk and Fatui alike, and joins a balloon war with Arlecchino and Nefer. Flins names a free-spirited kuuhenki as her "spirit animal"; Nicole finally has someone test her game; Sandrone reluctantly shows concern in competitive banter; Alice gives her a whimsical Dodoco adventure; Barbeloth's divination touches her past loneliness, new bonds and a future that may be an ending or a beginning. She tells the Traveler she fears losing everything she has gained and is reassured she will not lose her friends even on the moon. After visiting Lauma she hangs a wish that all who live under the moon find belonging and that she may see her friends again. When the Moon-Prayer Blossoms bloom she receives the card and says she will write hidden messages on the moon in moonlight that only her friends can read.

**The ambush.** The Traveler urges her to return someday and she promises the moonlight will always connect them. Dottore, who has manipulated history through "variables" and built a false moon from Hyperborean knowledge, strikes as she enters the beam; only the Traveler can move and is quickly overwhelmed. Columbina realises her presence lets him wield the power and escapes into a moon gate to the unknown space where Rerir once fled. The broken ritual forces him to retreat and the Traveler falls into the sea. The group works out that Dottore distorted the Hyperborean phantoms' memories to extract forbidden lunar knowledge. Varka argues that if Dottore stacks variables, so can they; the Hexenzirkel, Curatorium, Lightkeepers, Frostmoon Scions and Mondstadt allies each take a role. The Traveler recalls her last words: her name, once a codename, became real because of her friends.

**The Empty Courtyard.** Luonnotar encourages the Traveler not to give up. Paimon suggests playing Columbina's song on the lyre, which lulls the kuuhenki to sleep. Outside, Sandrone, who expected the Moon Maiden not to be there, remarks that there is no such thing as a free miracle, declines their company and leaves.
### ULTRA +
Context from Act V: Alice told the party that the border of Teyvat is destabilising because the power of all three moons is gathered in Nod-Krai, and that Columbina is fading because the world rejects a being with no accepted name, identity or function. Only the Traveler, Lauma and Arlecchino proved compatible enough for the three-day kuuvahki infusion Nicole required to enter the ruins. What Dottore's false moon is and where Columbina went are explained only in later acts.

## revelations
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
- Murals in the Hyperborean ruins lay out the history of the three moons: created by Nibelung, weaponised in his war with the Heavenly Principles, shattered or cast away, with their goddesses dying. A surviving moon is the link that allowed Columbina to be born.
- Columbina never had an older true name. She takes the name Columbina Hyposelenia, "born beneath the moon", given meaning by her friends.
- Hyperborean phantoms are not simple recordings: Dottore secretly distorted their memories to extract forbidden lunar knowledge.
- Dottore has built a false moon and can command the power of the three moons and freeze time.
- Columbina creates a moon gate and disappears into the same unknown space Rerir entered.
### LARGE +
Arlecchino declares she will act as Peruere against Dottore, outside the Harbingers' political restrictions, if the Tsaritsa does not judge in time. The act leaves the nature of the false moon and the space Columbina entered to Acts VII and VIII.

## characters
### STANDARD
Traveler and Paimon; Columbina (the Moon Goddess, now named Columbina Hyposelenia); Lauma (the Frostmoon Scions' Moonchanter); Arlecchino (a Fatui Harbinger, present as Columbina's friend); Nicole (a Hexenzirkel witch, appearing partly through a Dodoco projection); Dottore (antagonist); Varka, Nefer, Flins and Sandrone; Alice, Barbeloth and Luonnotar in smaller roles; the people of Nasha Town.

## consequences
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Columbina is lost in an unknown space. The broken ritual forces Dottore to retreat, but he remains at large, and Arlecchino and Varka begin organising resistance. The Traveler and friends must find a way to rescue her; the story continues in A Traveler on a Winter's Night.
### LARGE +
From here Columbina is a named, stable person, which the later acts depend on.

## placement
### STANDARD
Act VI of Song of the Welkin Moon, released in version 6.2 (Luna III). It follows A Nocturne of the Far North (Act V, same version) and leads into A Traveler on a Winter's Night (Act VII). The chapter is not officially numbered Chapter VI.
