## summary
### LITE
Chapter II of the Archon Quests, titled Omnipresence Over Mortals, takes the Traveler to Inazuma, the closed island nation of the Electro Archon. The Raiden Shogun enforces the Vision Hunt Decree, seizing Visions to protect her ideal of eternity. The Traveler joins the resistance, uncovers Fatui meddling, defeats the Shogun's lieutenants and Signora, and finally reaches the true Archon, Ei. The chapter ends with an Act IV in Liyue's Chasm in which the Traveler and Dainsleif oppose the Abyss Order.
### STANDARD +
Structure: Prologue (1.6), Acts I and II (2.0), Act III (2.1) and the separate Traveler chapter Act IV (2.6). The theme is aspiration against permanence: Visions, tokens of mortals' ambition, are what the Shogun treats as a threat.
### LARGE +
The chapter's preview text frames it as asking what mortals see of the eternity their god pursues. By its end the Traveler has met the real Raiden Ei, learned that the Shogun who governs is a puppet, seen the Electro Gnosis pass to the Fatui, and received Yae Miko's guess that the Traveler lacks a Vision because they have not yet found an ambition strong enough.

## plot
### LITE
The Traveler, hoping to find the Electro Archon and learn about their sibling, wins passage to Inazuma in a tournament run by Beidou. In Ritou they meet Thoma and, through him, Kamisato Ayaka, who leads a quiet effort against the Vision Hunt Decree. After a failed attempt to save Thoma from a public ceremony, the Traveler joins the Watatsumi Army's resistance. Yae Miko later helps them expose a Fatui plot, reach the Shogun and defeat her in the Plane of Euthymia, and the Decree is abolished.
### STANDARD
Prologue (1.6): the Traveler wins Beidou's Crux Clash for passage on the Alcor and meets Kazuha. Act I (2.0): in Ritou, Thoma and Kamisato Ayaka draw the Traveler into the quiet campaign against the Vision Hunt Decree; they help three Visionless people and free the Vision-forger Masakatsu. Act II (2.0): the Traveler fails to rescue Thoma from the hundredth-Vision ceremony, is crushed by the Shogun, becomes a wanted criminal and joins Kokomi and Gorou's Watatsumi resistance. Act III (2.1): the Fatui's Delusions kill Teppei; Yae Miko rescues the Traveler from the Balladeer, trains them, and turns Kujou Sara against Kujou Takayuki. The Traveler beats Signora in a duel, then defeats Ei in the Plane of Euthymia, and the Decree is abolished. Act IV (2.6): in the Chasm in Liyue, the Traveler and Dainsleif stop an Abyss Order device meant to amplify a pool that eases the Khaenri'ahn curse; the Black Serpent Knight Halfdan dies stopping it.
### LARGE +
Detail by act. Prologue, Autumn Winds, Scarlet Leaves (1.6): the Traveler, advised by Atsuko, seeks Beidou's help. They win her Crux Clash tournament, and Kazuha's Masterless Vision is stolen and recovered. The prize is passage on the Alcor.

Act I, The Immovable God and the Eternal Euthymia (2.0): the Alcor reaches Ritou. Thoma, Beidou's business partner, helps them through the processing centre and introduces the International Trade Association, whose members are being bled by the Kanjou Commission's demand for Crystal Marrow. The Traveler exposes the corrupt officer Keijirou, and Thoma reveals he serves the Kamisato Clan and that Ayaka hopes the Traveler can help end the Vision Hunt Decree. To leave Ritou the Traveler runs an errand with Hiiragi Chisato, daughter of Commissioner Hiiragi Shinsuke. At the Statue of the Omnipresent God, the Traveler hears the aspirations of confiscated Visions. Ayaka sends them to help three people who lost their Visions: Tejima in Konda Village, the samurai Kurosawa in Inazuma City, and the swordmaster Domon, where Yae Miko examines the case. Convinced, the Traveler agrees to help the resistance and joins Yoimiya to free the Vision-forger Masakatsu from police custody, with Kujou Sara allowing them to leave.

Act II, Stillness, the Sublimation of Shadow (2.0): Thoma is to be the hundredth victim of the Vision Hunt Decree, in a ceremony the Shogun attends. The Traveler and Paimon rescue him. When the Traveler uses Electro power without a Vision, the Shogun calls them an exception and enemy of eternity, takes them into another space, easily overpowers them and promises to strike twice next time. Thoma helps them escape, and the Traveler becomes a wanted criminal. The Watatsumi Army's General Gorou accepts the Traveler after hearing of the ceremony. Sara arrives to capture them, offers a ceremonial duel of champions, and loses; Sangonomiya Kokomi appears with mercenaries including Beidou and Kazuha, and Sara retreats.

Act III, Omnipresence Over Mortals (2.1): Kokomi makes the Traveler captain of the special operations unit Swordfish II. Teppei's supplies come from an anonymous sponsor. He ages rapidly and dies after using a Delusion, a device that drains the user's life force; the sponsor is the Fatui. The Traveler finds the factory, which uses Crystal Marrow supplied via the Kanjou Commission, and a Fatui mole in the resistance, then meets the Balladeer, a Harbinger, and collapses under the Tatarigami. Yae Miko rescues them, explains that the true Raiden Shogun, Ei, lives in the Plane of Euthymia and that the ruler of Inazuma is a puppet, and trains the Traveler. She turns Kujou Sara against her adoptive father Kujou Takayuki using evidence of his collusion with the Fatui, which Sayu and Yoimiya help obtain. Signora, who beat Sara at Tenshukaku, is defeated by the Traveler in a duel before the throne and then executed by the Shogun. The resistance arrives; Kazuha awakens his friend's Vision to block the Shogun's strike. The Traveler enters the Plane of Euthymia, defeats Ei with Miko's help, and Ei abolishes the Vision Hunt Decree. Afterward, Miko says she gave the Electro Gnosis to the Balladeer in exchange for the Traveler's rescue, answers questions about the Balladeer's origin as Ei's prototype puppet, and points the Traveler toward Sumeru.

Act IV, Requiem of the Echoing Depths (2.6): in Liyue's Chasm, on a miner's commission, the Traveler meets Dainsleif, who tells of his title Twilight Sword and of the sibling as his former travelling companion. They find Black Serpent Knights guarding dying hilichurls in the Inverted City, Halfdan, a Knight who seems to recognise Dainsleif, and a plan by the sibling and the Abyss Order to amplify a pool that eases the curse. Halfdan sacrifices himself to stop the device, and the Traveler destroys it.

Further points in Act I: the Kanjou Commission's taxes switched from Mora to Crystal Marrow, driving up its price and ruining outlander merchants; Katsuie, a passing official, drops the matter when Thoma shows a Yashiro Commission card. Chisato tells the Traveler that a woman had conspired with her father to keep the Traveler in Ritou. The Traveler's three Vision cases are all about lost aspiration: Tejima had forgotten why he waited at Konda; Kurosawa had been judged to be slacking and lost his Vision, forgetting that he had been quietly going into debt to buy food for the people; Domon's Vision had given him ambitions whose loss made him violent, and Anzai, the eldest disciple of their old school, tells him he had already passed his own aspirations to Domon and urges Domon to pass his on to his own disciples.

Further points in Act III: Kokomi accepted the sponsor's offer reluctantly because Watatsumi lacked resources. Teppei explains that the Tatarigami arose after the Watatsumi god Orobashi was slain by Ei in the Archon War, which is why Narukami and Watatsumi have poor relations. Ei knew about the Fatui's involvement all along and considered the pursuit of aspiration a greater threat to eternity than the war, her inaction amounting to tacit approval. Ei describes the deaths of companions and her sister Makoto, and the destruction of Khaenri'ah by the Heavenly Principles, as the root of her pursuit of eternity. The sky of the Plane brightens as the confiscated Visions' light enters it, and Miko persuades Ei that her ideal is causing loneliness even to her. Ei agrees to abolish the Decree but asks for time to reconsider her rule.

Act IV adds: Dainsleif suspects the hilichurls come to the Chasm to endure the end of their lives because the area dampens the immortality curse placed on Khaenri'ahns. The Inverted City is judged older than Khaenri'ah. Halfdan was a Royal Guard elite told to protect Khaenri'ah's people at all costs, an order the Black Serpent Knights have followed ever since despite losing their minds to the curse. Touching an Inteyvat flower, Khaenri'ah's national flower, shows the Traveler their sibling planning with an Abyss Herald to free the hilichurls from the curse and 'restore the homeland'.
### ULTRA +
Quest-level order inside the chapter. Prologue: A Path Through the Storm, The Crux Clash, Follow the Wind. Act I: Setting Sail; Confessions of an Outlander; Ritou Escape Plan; Three Wishes; The Meaning of Meaningless Waiting; To Treat the Well-Meaning Well; A Swordmaster's Path Is Paved With Broken Blades; A Flower Blooms in a Prison. Act II: Amidst Stormy Judgment; In the Name of the Resistance. Act III: Sword, Fish, Resistance; Those Who Yearn for the Gods' Gaze; Delusion; The Servant's Path; Proof of Guilt; Blind Loyalty, Reckless Courage; Duel Before the Throne; The Omnipresent God; Wishes. Act IV: In the Depths, an Unexpected Reunion; The Grave of the Guarded; Memories of Inteyvat; The Black Serpent Knights' Glory.

Act II contains only two quests. Act II's Teppei, a logistics soldier who wants to make a name for himself, builds into Act III's tragedy.

## revelations
@version: 2.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
- The Raiden Shogun who rules Inazuma is a puppet made in the likeness of the true Archon, Ei, who dwells in the Plane of Euthymia; the Traveler is the first person to meet Ei since she withdrew there.
- The Fatui, using Crystal Marrow from the Kanjou Commission, were supplying the resistance with Delusions, devices that drain life force. The Tenryou Commission's head, Kujou Takayuki, had colluded with them. The Balladeer is revealed as an augmented prototype puppet of Ei who became the Sixth Harbinger.
- Ei allowed the war to continue because she saw Visions and aspirations as a threat to eternity, and she ends the Vision Hunt Decree after losing to the Traveler.
- The Electro Gnosis passes to the Balladeer in exchange for the Traveler's rescue; Ei had entrusted it to Miko knowing it would be traded for something of equal worth.
- Yae Miko speculates that the Traveler has no Vision because they have not yet found an ambition strong enough.
### LARGE +
- Ei traces her pursuit of eternity to the deaths of her close companions and her sister Makoto and to the Heavenly Principles' destruction of Khaenri'ah.
- Act IV: Dainsleif was captain of Khaenri'ah's Royal Guard with the title Twilight Sword; the sibling was his travelling companion in a search for their fates, but they 'did not make it to the journey's end together'. The Black Serpent Knights are his former subordinates driven mad by the curse, and a plan exists to amplify a pool in the Chasm to lift the curse from hilichurls. The Abyss Order also has a project called the Loom of Fate, which Dainsleif goes to investigate.
- Miko adds that knowledge in Sumeru is treated as a managed resource, though she does not know whether that was the Archon's or the Sages' decision.

## characters
### STANDARD
- **Traveler and Paimon**; **Ei** (the puppet Shogun and the true Archon); **Yae Miko** (guide and trainer); **Kamisato Ayaka and Thoma** (the quiet resistance); **Kokomi and Gorou** (the Watatsumi Army); **Kujou Sara and Takayuki**; **Kazuha and Beidou**; **Signora and the Balladeer** (Fatui); **Dainsleif and Halfdan** (Act IV).
### LARGE +
Roles:
- **The Traveler and Paimon**: the leads; the Traveler is refused a Vision, called an 'exception' by the Shogun and eventually wins.
- **Raiden Shogun / Ei**: the puppet who enforces the Decree; the true Ei appears in the Plane of Euthymia.
- **Yae Miko**: the Guuji of the Grand Narukami Shrine, who rescues, trains and guides the Traveler.
- **Kamisato Ayaka and Thoma**: lead the quiet campaign against the Decree from the Yashiro Commission's neutral ground.
- **Kujou Sara and Kujou Takayuki**: the Tenryou Commission's general and head; Sara turns on her adoptive father.
- **Sangonomiya Kokomi and Gorou**: the resistance's leader and general at Watatsumi.
- **Kaedehara Kazuha and Beidou**: allies from the Prologue who later fight for the resistance; Kazuha's friend's Vision awakens in his hands.
- **Yoimiya, Sayu, Teppei, Hiiragi Chisato, Hiiragi Shinsuke**: supporting figures.
- **Signora and the Balladeer**: the Fatui Harbingers behind the Delusion scheme.
- **Dainsleif, Halfdan, the sibling**: Act IV.
Antagonists on the Inazuman side include the corrupt Kanjou officer Keijirou, who is exposed in Act I, and Nathan, a Fatui mole inside the resistance.

## consequences
### STANDARD
The Vision Hunt Decree is abolished, Signora is dead, the Electro Gnosis is in the Balladeer's hands, and the Traveler leaves Inazuma for Sumeru with news that knowledge is a managed resource there. Teppei is dead, and the Kujou Clan's head is exposed. The Traveler learns more about Dainsleif and the sibling's plans in Act IV, and the Chapter III story begins in Sumeru.
### LARGE +
The sakoku policy is repealed in later accounts, but this chapter's own text ends with Ei needing time to reconsider her rule; the repeal belongs to the Inazuma entries. The Traveler's lack of a Vision remains open.

## placement
### STANDARD
Released across Versions 1.6 (Prologue), 2.0 (Acts I and II), 2.1 (Act III) and 2.6 (Act IV). It follows Chapter I (Liyue) and precedes Chapter III (Sumeru). Act IV is listed under Chapter II but was a later addition, and the interlude The Crane Returns on the Wind sits before it on the wiki's navigation.
### LARGE +
The chapter's name, Omnipresence Over Mortals, is shared by Act III. Do not confuse the chapter entry with the Act III quest of that name.

## entry: Vision Hunt Decree (Chapter II)
@keys: Vision Hunt Decree, Vision Hunt Ceremony, hundredth Vision
@keys2: Inazuma, Raiden Shogun
@version: 2.0
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
The Raiden Shogun's decree to confiscate Visions from Inazuma's people. The chapter shows its human cost in the Visionless samurai Kurosawa, Tejima and Domon, and its public face at the Statue of the Omnipresent God, where seized Visions are set. Thoma is slated as the hundredth victim in Act II. Ei abolishes it after the duel in Act III.

## entry: The Kamisato Resistance Network
@keys: Ayaka's resistance, Yashiro resistance, fake Visions
@keys2: Ayaka, Thoma
@version: 2.0
### STANDARD
Ayaka and Thoma resist covertly, constrained by the Yashiro Commission's neutrality. One operation made counterfeit Visions to hand to Tenryou Commission seizures; the forger Masakatsu was jailed and then rescued by the Traveler and Yoimiya. They cannot join the Watatsumi Army openly.

## entry: Swordfish II and the Resistance Sponsor
@keys: Swordfish II, anonymous sponsor, Resistance supplies
@keys2: Kokomi, Teppei
@version: 2.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Kokomi accepted an anonymous letter's offer of supplies for the Watatsumi Army. The Traveler becomes captain of the special operations unit Swordfish II. The supplies include Delusions; Teppei's rapid ageing and death reveal the sponsor as the Fatui. Kokomi orders the Resistance to stop using Delusions and has their users recalled for treatment.

## entry: Duel Before the Throne
@keys: Duel Before the Throne, Signora's duel, Musou no Hitotachi
@keys2: Signora, Raiden Shogun
@version: 2.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
At Tenshukaku, Signora has already beaten Kujou Sara. The Traveler invokes the duel before the throne, with the Shogun's approval. After the Traveler wins, the Shogun executes Signora with the Musou no Hitotachi, then lets the Traveler leave.

## entry: The Omnipresent God Confrontation
@keys: Plane of Euthymia battle, Ei confrontation, Ei's eternity
@keys2: Ei, Traveler, Yae Miko
@version: 2.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
In the Plane of Euthymia the Traveler fights Ei while Miko enters with an omamori. Ei explains her pursuit of eternity as a response to loss, and Miko argues that it brings loneliness. The Traveler wins; Ei abolishes the Vision Hunt Decree but wants time to reconsider.
### LARGE +
Kazuha's use of his friend's Vision to block the Shogun's strike outside Tenshukaku gives the resistance the courage to charge her, which prompts the Traveler to attack the Shogun and be drawn into the Plane.

## entry: Act IV - The Chasm and the Black Serpent Knights
@keys: Requiem of the Echoing Depths, Inverted City, Black Serpent Knights, Halfdan
@keys2: Dainsleif, Chasm
@version: 2.6
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
In the Chasm the Traveler and Dainsleif find Black Serpent Knights guarding dying hilichurls in the Inverted City, a city thought older than Khaenri'ah. Halfdan, who has held to his last order to protect the people of Khaenri'ah, sacrifices himself against an amplification device built by the Abyss Order. Dainsleif then leaves to investigate the Loom of Fate.
