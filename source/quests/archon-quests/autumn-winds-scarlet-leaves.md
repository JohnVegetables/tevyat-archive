## summary
### LITE
Autumn Winds, Scarlet Leaves is the prologue act of Archon Quest Chapter II, released in Version 1.6, and it is set in Liyue, at Liyue Harbor and Guyun Stone Forest. The Traveler wants to reach Inazuma, which is sealed off by storms and a strict isolation policy, and is sent to the Crux Fleet captain Beidou, who runs a tournament for fighters without Visions. The Traveler wins it, and in the process meets Kazuha, a wandering crew member who carries the Vision of a dead friend.
### STANDARD +
The act is a three-quest lead-in: A Path Through the Storm, The Crux Clash and Follow the Wind. It contains no Inazuman locations and no Electro Archon; its purpose is to set up the voyage (the prize is a berth on the Alcor) and to introduce Kazuha, who becomes a major figure in the Inazuma story.
### LARGE +
Thematically it opens Chapter II's central question, what a Vision means and who it belongs to, through Kazuha's Masterless Vision, before the Vision Hunt Decree is shown directly in the next act.

## plot
### LITE
Looking over Liyue Harbor after the events of We Will Be Reunited, the Traveler decides to keep going toward the Unknown God. Atsuko, the one Inazuman they know, warns that the storm around Inazuma and the nation's isolation make the trip dangerous and points them to Beidou. Beidou offers passage on the Alcor if the Traveler wins her tournament, the Crux Clash. The Traveler wins after Kazuha retrieves a stolen Vision.
### STANDARD
Atsuko explains that besides the storm, outsiders face the Sakoku Decree and the authorities who would hunt them if found, but she cannot dissuade the Traveler and recommends Beidou. At the Alcor, anchored at Guyun Stone Forest, Beidou introduces a temporary crew member, Kazuha, and mistakes the Traveler for a tournament entrant. The Crux Clash is a martial contest for fighters without Visions. Its prize is a Masterless Vision supplied by Kazuha, who hopes the winner will reawaken it. The Traveler is not interested in the Vision, so Beidou adds a second option: the winner may choose the Vision or a ride to Inazuma on the Alcor.

At the arena, Zhuhan of the crew handles registration and is startled to see the Traveler who helped Liyue against an ancient god; the Traveler must prove they have no Vision. The first opponent, Jinyou, is easily beaten without elemental powers, and Beidou sends the Traveler straight to the semi-finals because so many new challengers now want to fight them. The semi-final opponent, Rongshi, is harder; he is not after the prize, only a worthy fight, and he accepts defeat humbly and asks for a rematch someday.

Nobody can say much about the finalist except that they are fast. Kazuha takes the Traveler somewhere quiet, reveals he already knows the Traveler can use elemental power without a Vision and, when the Traveler cannot explain how, tests them against slimes, and gives advice on the finalist's style and a little background on Inazuma and its Electro Archon.
### LARGE +
On the day of the final the challenger does not appear, which annoys Beidou. Kazuha senses something is wrong, and the display box holding the Vision is empty. He offers to find it since it was his responsibility and goes with the Traveler to a Treasure Hoarder named Fei the Flyer, whom he had already identified. Fei says he had planned to win fairly but lost hope after seeing the Traveler beat Rongshi. He fights and loses. Kazuha threatens him with harsh punishment (breaking his arms under the law of the sea, branding him a thief), but when the cornered Fei still does not awaken the Vision, Kazuha lets him go and takes the Vision back.

Kazuha admits he had noticed the theft all along and had hoped Fei might resonate with the Vision, since gods usually grant Visions to those who show strength and resilience. He asks the Traveler to try, and the Traveler cannot either. Paimon asks why it matters so much to him. Kazuha explains that after the Raiden Shogun issued the Vision Hunt Decree, his friend, another Vision bearer who longed to face the Musou no Hitotachi, challenged the Vision Hunters to a duel before the throne and lost. Kazuha reached Tenshukaku as the duel ended and divine punishment was pronounced, snatched the dying Vision and fled, and has since kept it in the hope that it will awaken for someone, having heard rumours of Masterless Visions doing so.

Back at the arena, Zhuhan tells them that Ningguang has called Beidou back to Liyue Harbor because the tournament got out of hand; Kazuha explains that the thief was the Traveler's final opponent. Under the rules Fei is disqualified and the Traveler is declared champion. Kazuha warns that Inazuma will be harsher once they land, says the Alcor must resupply and will send word when it is ready, and goes off to travel Liyue in search of someone who can reawaken the Vision.
### ULTRA +
Details of the structure: the act has exactly three quests (A Path Through the Storm, The Crux Clash, Follow the Wind), and the first sets the tournament up while the second and third are the matches and the theft. The Traveler beats the first opponent without using any elemental power. Beidou, who has heard of the Traveler from Ningguang, is also the person who chooses to let the winner pick a voyage instead of the Vision.

## revelations
@version: 1.6
### STANDARD
- The Vision Hunt Decree exists: the Raiden Shogun is confiscating Visions, and Kazuha's friend lost a duel before the throne opposing it and was struck down by divine punishment.
- Kazuha is a temporary Alcor crew member; the next act shows he is still a wanted fugitive, which keeps Beidou from lingering in port with him aboard.
- Masterless Visions, Visions whose bearers have died, are said to have reawakened for other people.
- Inazuma is hard to reach: a permanent storm, the Sakoku Decree, and pursuit by authorities.
### LARGE +
- Kazuha already knows the Traveler can use elemental powers without a Vision; the Traveler cannot explain how.
- Beidou has been briefed on the Traveler by Ningguang, so the Qixing are aware of the Traveler's deeds.
- The tournament and its Vision prize are Kazuha's attempt to find a new bearer for his friend's Vision.

## characters
### STANDARD
- **The Traveler and Paimon**: decide to go to Inazuma; the Traveler wins the tournament without a Vision.
- **Beidou**: captain of the Crux Fleet's flagship Alcor, who runs the Crux Clash.
- **Kazuha**: a temporary Alcor crew member who carries a dead friend's Vision.
- **Atsuko**: an Inazuman in Liyue Harbor, the only Inazuman the Traveler has met, who warns them about the trip.
- **Zhuhan**: crew member who registers tournament entrants.
- **Jinyou, Rongshi**: the Traveler's tournament opponents; Rongshi is a fighter who wanted only a challenge.
- **Fei the Flyer**: a Treasure Hoarder who steals the Vision.
### LARGE +
Ningguang appears only by reference: her summons pulls Beidou from the arena at the end. The finalist the Traveler would have faced turns out to be Fei the Flyer, who never shows up for the match.

## consequences
### STANDARD
The Traveler is champion and has a berth on the Alcor, which sails after resupplying. Kazuha keeps the Masterless Vision and sets out through Liyue to find someone who can reawaken it. The next act, The Immovable God and the Eternal Euthymia, begins with the sailing.
### LARGE +
Kazuha does not travel with the Traveler at this point; he rejoins the Alcor and is aboard on arrival in Inazuma, where he is a wanted fugitive.

## placement
### STANDARD
Released in Version 1.6 as the Prologue of Chapter II, before Inazuma opened in Version 2.0. It follows Chapter I Act IV, We Will Be Reunited, and leads into Chapter II Act I, The Immovable God and the Eternal Euthymia (Version 2.0). Story order and release order match.
### LARGE +
Because the prologue came out in 1.6 and the rest of the chapter in 2.0, Kazuha and the Masterless Vision were introduced before Inazuma itself was playable.

## entry: The Crux Clash
@keys: Crux Clash, Beidou's tournament
@keys2: Beidou, Kazuha, Traveler
@version: 1.6
### STANDARD
A tournament held by Beidou at an arena in Guyun Stone Forest, where the Alcor is anchored, for renowned fighters without Visions. The prize is a Masterless Vision supplied by Kazuha; for the Traveler, Beidou adds a ride to Inazuma as an alternative. Entrants are registered by Zhuhan. The Traveler beats Jinyou and Rongshi, and wins the title when the finalist, Fei the Flyer, is disqualified for stealing the prize.
### LARGE +
Beidou skips the Traveler past the first rounds because other fighters now want to face them. Rongshi enters only for the fight, not the prize.

## entry: Kazuha's Masterless Vision
@keys: Masterless Vision, Kazuha's friend's Vision
@keys2: Kazuha, Beidou
@version: 1.6
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Kazuha carries the Vision of a friend who opposed the Vision Hunt Decree, lost a duel before the throne and suffered the Raiden Shogun's divine punishment. He offers it as the tournament prize in the hope that a new bearer will reawaken it. The Traveler and Fei both fail to resonate with it, so Kazuha keeps it. Later events in Inazuma decide its fate; this act does not.

## entry: Fei the Flyer
@keys: Fei the Flyer
@keys2: Kazuha, Crux Clash

### STANDARD
A Treasure Hoarder who entered the Crux Clash intending to win, then stole the Vision after watching the Traveler fight. Kazuha traced him and the Traveler beat him. Kazuha threatens him with harsh punishment, partly to test whether pressure would awaken the Vision, then lets him go and takes the Vision back.

## entry: Atsuko's Warning

### STANDARD
Atsuko, the Inazuman the Traveler already knows in Liyue Harbor, tells them the storm is only part of the danger. Inazuma's isolation policy and the authorities who would pursue illegal entrants are the rest. She yields when the Traveler is determined and sends them to Beidou, whose seafaring experience may get a ship through.
