## summary
### LITE
Chapter VII Act III of the Archon Quest. The Tsaritsa Anastasya, back in Zapolyarny Palace after killing the monster behind the snowstorms, suspends Project Stuzha because of a curse of death. The Traveler finally meets her, learns why she wants the Gnoses, and helps a Seal of Life plan that draws out Rhinedottir.
### STANDARD +
The act has three quests: Light and Shadow, Intertwined; Sparks Converging Upon the Tundra; and One Move Left to Make. It places the Traveler in the Tsaritsa's court for the first time, shows her daily memory-recording habit and the sealed relic left by the Third Descender, introduces Vesna's authority over life, and ends with a theft in the Northland Bank vault. Released in version 7.1.
### LARGE +
The act answers why the energy rationing paused but not why the Tsaritsa hoards Gnoses: she wants enough power to face the Heavenly Principles, which could destroy Snezhnaya with a Celestial Nail. Against the curse of death she sets a plan to harvest memory-bubbles called Seals of Life, a plan that is also bait. The next act is A Rekviem for the Underworld.

## plot
### LITE
Tartaglia tells the Traveler and Paimon that suspending Project Stuzha has lightened the Veche, and arranges an audience. Danica, the Tsaritsa's head maid and personal attendant, tests the Traveler over a game of Mora. At court Anastasya cancels all investigation of the Traveler and speaks to them privately. Pierro and Mitya show a plan to counter the curse using Vesna's power over life. When Seals of Life go missing from Northland Bank, the Traveler and Vesna find a creature stealing them, and Rhinedottir arrives, as the Tsaritsa had intended.
### STANDARD +
Across three quests the Traveler is tested by Danica, attends the Tsaritsa's court, hears why she seeks the Gnoses and sees the seal left by the Third Descender. Pierro's Mirror of Remembrance reveals Ronova's collaboration with Rhinedottir, and Vesna's power of life inspires the Seal of Life collection. When seals are stolen from Northland Bank, the Traveler and Vesna find a creature of the Ruler of Life, and Rhinedottir arrives, as the Tsaritsa had planned.
### LARGE +
Light and Shadow, Intertwined: at Glupov Tartaglia explains the mood after Stuzha's suspension and agrees to help file for an audience. At Podvorye Restaurant the Traveler is approached by Danica, who has read many reports on them and wants to see what sort of person they are. She names Capitano, who fought alongside the Traveler in Natlan, as her mentor and calls his passing an enormous loss for Snezhnaya, then proposes a Mora game he taught her, in which she tries to land a coin on the table and the Traveler must stop it. Vesna ends the game, saying it troubles guests, and pays for the meal.

The next day, at Zapolyarny Palace, the Traveler joins their first court session. The Tsaritsa wants only good manners. She hears from Pulcinella that civilian disruption was low, with a few corruption cases to review, and has the House of the Hearth's extra entries checked with the 3rd Druzhna Company. She tells Mitya and Pierro that she accepts their report: work on Project Mistilteinn stays halted until Ronova is dealt with. Mitya says none of their weapons can seriously harm the Ruler of Death, and Pantalone notes that any sign of progress triggers a mass death; Sandrone suggests moving the research materials to the Kuuvahki Experimental Design Bureau in Nod-Krai, but Mitya says the curse afflicts them directly and will follow them anywhere. Anastasya assures them the problem will be overcome and leaves it to Pierro. She tells the Traveler they are a formidable adversary she respects, and orders all investigations of them to stop and grants unrestricted access to the palace.

In her office, Anastasya asks about the Pyro Gnosis. The Traveler says it burned with Irminsul; she answers that, as part of the Third Descender's remains, it cannot stay among the Ley Lines long and will be ejected. Why does she want the Gnoses? To gain the power to face the Heavenly Principles. Snezhnaya has always been hostile land, forcing its people to grow quickly in military and economic strength, and so they have pushed to the edge of what the Principles allow and have long been at risk of a Celestial Nail. In a hidden basement she shows a large crystalline structure, a seal the Third Descender left, watched over by her predecessor the Belyi Tsar and undetected by the Principles. It reacts only to Gnoses, so she thinks all seven must be gathered to open it, though she does not know what it holds. Asked about her own authority, she says it is "love", but she will not use or identify with it because the love granted by the Heavenly Principles was false. The Traveler recalls Venti's similar words about freedom. Danica then reports that Pierro wishes to see her.

Pierro says their experiment is ready. Before leaving, Anastasya finishes the day's memory recording with a transcription prism; he explains that she believes memory cannot be completely trusted, and that a transcription prism, like a Stellar Linchpin, contains the Third Descender's power and so is free from certain laws of Teyvat. At the Armory Palace Mitya and Vesna wait. Pierro presents a large prism, the Mirror of Remembrance, or Svetovid Stone, a recent Armory Palace innovation. Mitya's soul now bears the mark of the Ruler of Death, and Anastasya reveals Vesna's secret: during the Hyperborean era the former Archangel stole a small portion of the Ruler of Life's power, which after changing hands was entrusted to the Vila, and Vesna inherited it with the blood of the first Vila. Memory fragments extracted with the prism show that Ronova had worked with Rhinedottir, "killing" without permission by exploiting a gap in the Heavenly Principles' monitoring cycles while Rhinedottir revived them before the Shades could catch it. Sandrone and Pantalone suggest using Vesna's small authority of life against death. Vesna extracts a "Seal of Life" from herself, a bubble holding an impactful memory that could defy death if enough are gathered. Everyone agrees; Vesna takes bubbles from all of them and notes the Tsaritsa has no happy memories. Mitya is told to assist Sandrone in researching how life force can be used to fight the Ruler of Death. Pantalone then voices concerns to Anastasya, and she agrees to listen.

Sparks Converging Upon the Tundra: Danica, given a few days off, apologises to the Traveler for her test, tidies their room and makes hot milk. She says she has worked as a maid for about three years and two months, though the Postelnitskaya family has served the Tsaritsa for three generations; her father was a travelling mercenary, the Tsaritsa persuaded her mother and grandmother to let her seek adventure abroad, and she returned when her homeland needed her. She hints that this was an act of love on the Tsaritsa's part. Two days later news that Seal of Life collection is under way has leaked, and many people are eager to help. Councilor Belozersky stumbles through questions. Pantalone explains that donation was obligatory for government officials and nobles, hence the Druzhna, while the public may donate at Northland Bank for compensation. Alyosha, Odette and Vodyanitsa volunteer. Vesna is overworked; Pevtsova, whom the Traveler had saved from monsters earlier, says the people of Svetloledovka will help. After harvesting seals there the party returns to Snezhnograd by train, during which the Traveler senses a presence that vanishes.

One Move Left to Make: Odette's subordinate reports that Seals of Life may be stolen, with the suspect targeting the frail and elderly. Alyosha is made up as an old man and, at Central Station, approaches Avaz, who questions him; if Alyosha convinces him, Avaz names a price in Mora and a meeting in two days before Alyosha seizes him, while failure leaves Avaz fleeing and caught by the Traveler. Back at the theatre Avaz threatens to report them to the Veche but, with Alyosha's rifle aimed, admits that as a Northland Bank employee he noticed seals missing from the vault and hints that a Harbinger, Pantalone, is responsible. The Traveler tells Anastasya, who sends Avaz to Fatui Internal Affairs and rules out Pantalone's direct role. With Vesna the Traveler goes to Northland Bank on the pretext of a withdrawal. Deep vaults require a train; Vesna disables an employee and takes his radio and keycard, and the pair infiltrate, take clearance cards, trigger an alarm and defeat guards before reaching Pantalone's office, where he expected them. He defends himself: before he was a Harbinger there were no state-owned banks in Snezhnaya, humans and fae kept separate rules and customs, and the Fatui unified them and founded the Northland Bank. Another alarm leads them to the vault where a creature of the Ruler of Life, the False Horned Steed, is draining the seals through portals. Pantalone activates turrets, Vesna revitalises the seals, and the Traveler subdues the creature. It rises again and charges Vesna, but Anastasya freezes it. Rhinedottir arrives, stops her, lets the beast (her own failed experiment) flee, and reclaims from Vesna the stolen "morsel" of power. At a snap of her fingers all seals return to their owners. It emerges that the collection was meant to force Rhinedottir to appear: Pantalone's concerns had been that mass collection would alert the Ruler of Life, and he had the Northland Bank spread the news so she could not ignore it. Rhinedottir says she had foreseen that Anastasya would try to win her over as her conflict with Ronova escalates, and refuses to help fight Ronova. Anastasya says she has information on Asmoday and offers to meet in seven days; Naberius judges this as placing the ball on their side, and Rhinedottir agrees. At Podvorye Restaurant Vesna says she is content to lose the power, which belonged to the Ruler of Life. Pantalone returns a beacon crafted from the sample of that power she had lent the bank, saying the Ruler of Life is uninterested in reclaiming every last morsel so long as no further large-scale collection is attempted, and that this was Her Majesty's design; he refuses to lend Vesna a train car, citing her insufficient credit. Lyney, Lynette and Freminet arrive but the Traveler lets them get on with their work.
### ULTRA +
The structure mirrors Act I's political tour in a more exclusive setting. The first quest is almost all conversation and ritual: court etiquette, Danica's test, the office, the sealed relic, the memory recording, the Mirror of Remembrance. The second shifts to community response: how Snezhnaya reacts when asked to give a memory for the Tsaritsa. The third is an investigation that ends in a staged confrontation, with the Tsaritsa already ahead of everyone. Details worth keeping straight: the Tsaritsa's name in court is Anastasya; the Mirror of Remembrance and the transcription prism are different objects; the Seal of Life is a bubble holding one strong memory, extracted from a person by Vesna.

The act is careful about who makes each claim. The history of Vesna's power (stolen by the former Archangel, passed down the Vila line) is Anastasya's explanation in the Armory Palace. The account of Ronova and Rhinedottir comes from fragments played back through the Mirror of Remembrance. Anastasya's reason for gathering Gnoses is her own, given privately to the Traveler. Pantalone's account of Northland Bank is his own, and Avaz's accusation is later shown to miss the real thief. The bait plan is revealed only after the fact: Pantalone raised the risk of alerting the Ruler of Life and proposed the leak, and he says the returned beacon was part of Her Majesty's design.

## revelations
### LITE
Project Stuzha is on hold because of the curse of death. The Tsaritsa wants all seven Gnoses to unlock a seal left by the Third Descender and to gain strength against the Heavenly Principles. Her authority is love, which she refuses to use.
### STANDARD +
The Tsaritsa records every day with a transcription prism because she does not trust memory; like a Stellar Linchpin, the prism holds the Third Descender's power and is free from certain laws of Teyvat. Vesna carries the Ruler of Life's authority, via the first Vila. Ronova used a loophole in the Heavenly Principles' monitoring cycles with Rhinedottir's help to kill and revive without being caught. Seals of Life are memory bubbles that could resist death.
### LARGE +
Snezhnaya's growth has brought it to the limit the Heavenly Principles allow, so a Celestial Nail is a standing threat. The sealed crystal in the basement was watched by the Belyi Tsar and reacts only to Gnoses. Collecting Seals of Life was bait to force Rhinedottir to appear; Anastasya offered her information on Asmoday and a meeting in seven days. The Tsaritsa has no happy memories.
### ULTRA +
Danica's family, the Postelnitskayas, has served the Tsaritsa for three generations. Capitano was Danica's mentor (Vesna also studied under him). Pantalone says that despite endless accusations against him, the Tsaritsa still trusted the Northland Bank to keep the Seals of Life. Pyro Gnosis, according to Anastasya, will be ejected from the Ley Lines because it is part of the Third Descender's remains.

## characters
### LITE
The Traveler, Paimon, Tartaglia, Danica, Vesna, the Tsaritsa Anastasya, Pierro, Mitya, Pantalone, Sandrone, Rhinedottir and Naberius.
### STANDARD +
Others: Pulcinella (court report), Alyosha, Odette and Vodyanitsa (volunteers), Councilor Belozersky, Pevtsova of Svetloledovka, the suspect Avaz, and Lyney, Lynette and Freminet (briefly, at the end). Capitano is mentioned. Ronova and Asmoday are discussed but absent.

## consequences
### LITE
The Traveler gains unrestricted access to Zapolyarny Palace. Project Mistilteinn waits on a way to counter the curse of death. Rhinedottir has agreed to meet the Tsaritsa again in seven days.
### STANDARD +
All seals go back to their owners; Pantalone returns to Vesna a beacon made from a sample of her power. Avaz is handed to Fatui Internal Affairs. The act leads directly into A Rekviem for the Underworld.

## placement
### LITE
Third act of Chapter VII, between Wraith's Nocturne and A Rekviem for the Underworld. Released in version 7.1.
### STANDARD +
The act requires Adventure Rank 18; its place in the chain is Act II, then Act III, then Act IV. Acts I and II are 7.0 content and Acts III and IV are 7.1 content.
