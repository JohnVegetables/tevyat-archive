## summary
@spoiler: minor
### LITE
Black Stone Under a White Stone is the second act of Chapter V, the Natlan Archon Quest (version 5.0). When Kachina fails to return from the Pilgrimage of the Sacred Flame and Mavuika cannot find her or her Ancient Name, the Traveler, Mualani and Chasca (later joined by Iansan) go into the Abyss-corrupted Night Kingdom to bring her back, while the Fatui Harbinger Capitano publicly challenges the Pyro Archon.
### STANDARD +
The act has five quests: A Decision, To Reclaim an Ancient Name, Echoes of Life, Into Eternal Night and Past and Future. It requires Adventure Rank 40. It deepens the picture of the Abyss's attack on Natlan, shows how an Ancient Name is tied to the Night Kingdom, and closes with Mavuika asking the Traveler to help save Natlan, followed by a short epilogue with Capitano.
### LARGE +
The act is both a rescue story and a statement of Natlan's situation: the pilgrimage is in danger, the Sacred Flame is strained, and the Pyro Archon has spent part of her power. Mavuika's request to an outsider sets up the Ancient Name thread continued in the next act.

## plot
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Kachina fails to return from the Night Warden Wars, Mavuika cannot find her Ancient Name, and the pilgrimage is suspended. Capitano and the Fatui challenge Mavuika at the Stadium and he is wounded. The Traveler, Mualani, Chasca and Iansan obtain Kachina's name through Vichama and a Spiritspeaker Stone (which Chasca destroys), enter the Abyss-corrupted Night Kingdom, meet Mallko's spirit, rescue Kachina and purify her Wayob's totem against the Abyss Lector Comonqui, and escape when Mavuika breaks the Night Realm's ceiling. Mavuika then explains the tribes' recovery plan and asks the Traveler to help save Natlan.
### LARGE
**A Decision.** After a short stay with the People of the Springs, Mualani and Tonina bring news that the group from the Night Warden Wars has returned. Kachina, the inexperienced member, fell in battle, and the Ode of Resurrection should bring her back. At the Stadium of the Sacred Flame, Mavuika enters the Sacred Flame to fetch her but emerges alone: neither Kachina nor her Ancient Name can be found. Tepexpan and Tamuin heckle Kachina's failure; Chasca and Mualani defend her, and Mavuika shuts the heckling down and suspends the pilgrimage until further notice. In the Speaker's Chamber she suggests the Abyss may have found a way to undermine the pilgrimage's rules. With the help of the Masters of the Night-Wind she can locate Kachina's Ancient Name, but a party must physically enter the Night Kingdom. Mualani, Chasca and the Traveler volunteer. The Fatui then arrive, led by Capitano, the first Harbinger, who accuses Mavuika of failing the oath made 500 years ago. They clash; Mavuika wounds him, and someone from the Masters of the Night-Wind produces a fog that lets him escape.

**To Reclaim an Ancient Name.** Entering the Night Kingdom physically needs a Spiritspeaker Stone. Chasca asks Wayna of the Huitztlan, who refers her to Vichama, experienced at navigating the Night Kingdom. He agrees if they afterwards help him find his friend Mallko. The party fetches rope and obsidian and learns why Vichama is downcast: injured on the eve of a pilgrimage battle, he got an exemption from the Pyro Archon, and his group was then overwhelmed in his absence. During the ritual he finds Kachina's name while the Traveler siphons and purifies Abyssal energy from him, but he cannot find Mallko's. The stone cannot be disconnected, so Chasca shatters it with her bow, and the Traveler manages to find Mallko's final memory, which Vichama is glad yet grieved to hear. Chasca's younger sister Chuychu, whose family adopted Chasca, quarrels with her over the risk but backs her. Wayna promises to explain the broken stone to Citlali.

**Echoes of Life.** At the Stadium the party learns Mavuika has clashed with Capitano and sacrificed some of her power. She shows them a chamber of keepsakes kept by Pyro Archons over the years and explains that after Tenoch united the tribes and saved Natlan, the first Pyro Archon laid down the rules still followed today. The ritual with Kachina's name begins and Kachina appears. She says an Abyssal entity attacked her, the Wayob helped her flee and hide, and she fears the Abyss is affecting the Wayob and blocking her return. Mualani promises to find her. Mavuika directs the party to an entrance to the Night Kingdom, and Iansan joins them. The ruins on the way belong to the civilisation of the elemental sovereigns, and the Abyss's presence has reactivated devices there.

**Into Eternal Night.** In the corrupted Night Kingdom the party meets the spirit of Mallko, who helps them while he still can. They find Kachina fighting riftwolves and escort her to her Wayob's totem. As they purify it, the Abyss Lector Comonqui arrives, having destroyed Mallko's spirit, and claims the Pyro Archon refuses to share "primordial" power. Mualani rallies Kachina and they drive it off. A spectral figure, Tupac, appears and recognises Mualani as embodying her tribe's Wayob; on touching him she is flooded with memories showing that the plan to save Natlan began during the cataclysm. As an earthquake closes the rift, Mavuika appears as a projection of her consciousness and breaks the Night Realm's ceiling, letting everyone out.

**Past and Future.** In the Speaker's Chamber Chuychu treats the party for Abyssal contamination and notes the Traveler seems immune; Paimon is also unaffected. Mavuika explains that after the cataclysm the tribes and Wayob were weakened, and a Wayob's choice of bearer shows its tribe is strong again. Three tribes had heroes (Kinich, Iansan, Xilonen); Mualani makes a fourth; two remain, and less than a year is left. The keepsakes carried traces of Contending Flame, which she burned for the emergency boost. At the Weary Inn the group celebrates Kachina's return, and Mavuika asks the Traveler to help save Natlan, promising to have Xilonen forge them an Ancient Name. Elsewhere Rezanov asks Capitano whether to take the Gnosis; Capitano refuses, honouring her win. An unnamed ally tells him Mavuika has not dispelled the fog.
### ULTRA +
Further details. The pilgrimage was suspended partly because production of Contending Fire is far from sufficient and the tribes have reached a breaking point; the pause also keeps each tribe strong against the Abyss. Kinich favours finding the remaining bearers; Mavuika answers that time should pass, because the Wayob chose them and it is for them to decide how the story ends. She tells Tepexpan and Tamuin that doubt should motivate a search for truth, not serve as a weapon, and they back down when invited to ask her directly. Chasca blames herself for not protecting Kachina; Wayna fears Citlali's anger over the broken stone; Iansan is quietly upset at how Mavuika managed her rescue.

## revelations
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
- During the cataclysm the Abyss crafted a specific disaster for each tribe; Natlan repelled it under the then-Pyro Archon but at heavy cost, and recovery has been slow.
- The Abyss has reached into the Night Kingdom and the Wayob, threatening the Ode of Resurrection and the pilgrimage's rules.
- After the cataclysm the tribes and their Wayob were weakened; a Wayob's choice of an Ancient Name bearer signals its tribe is ready, and the plan to save Natlan began during the cataclysm.
- Mavuika has burned the Archons' keepsake collection and spent part of her power; Contending Fire reserves are low.
- Capitano, the first Harbinger, has come to end the old order and, for now, will not take the Gnosis from a weakened Archon.
### LARGE +
- Mavuika asks the Traveler for help and promises an Ancient Name; Act III shows that forging one for a non-Natlanese is considered impossible.

## characters
### STANDARD
The Traveler and Paimon, Mavuika, Kachina, Mualani, Chasca, Iansan, Kinich, Chuychu, Wayna, Vichama, the spirit of Mallko, Comonqui, Tupac, Capitano, Rezanov, Tepexpan, Tamuin and Tonina.

## consequences
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Kachina is rescued, the pilgrimage is suspended, and Mavuika is weakened. The Traveler agrees to help save Natlan and Xilonen is to forge them an Ancient Name. Capitano withdraws into Natlan's wilds.
### LARGE +
The unnamed Masters of the Night-Wind member who covered Capitano's escape is identified as Ororon in Act III.

## placement
### STANDARD
Chapter V, Act II. It follows Act I, Flowers Resplendent on the Sun-Scorched Sojourn, and precedes Act III, Beyond the Smoke and Mirrors. Released in 5.0.
