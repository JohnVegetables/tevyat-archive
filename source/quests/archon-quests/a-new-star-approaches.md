## summary
### LITE
Chapter I Act III of the Archon Quest, set in Liyue. The Traveler is invited to the Jade Chamber, helps the adepti and the Qixing stop the sealed god Osial, and discovers that Zhongli is the Geo Archon, Morax, who staged his own "death" and hands his Gnosis to the Fatui.
### STANDARD +
The act has six quests: The Floating Palace, Equilibrium, Solitary Fragrance, Heart of Glaze, Turning Point and The Fond Farewell. It finishes the preparations for the Rite of Parting, brings the Qixing and the adepti into open conflict and then into cooperation, and ends with Zhongli's reveal and a hand-off to the Inazuma storyline.
### LARGE +
The act is the payoff of the Liyue chapter. Rex Lapis' "assassination" in the Rite of Descension (Act I) turns out to have been Zhongli's own design, a test of whether Liyue can stand without a god. He reveals that the whole series of events, beginning with that supposed assassination, was his doing.

## plot
### LITE
The Traveler and Paimon reach the Jade Chamber, join the Qixing and adepti against the ancient god Osial, and then find Zhongli at Northland Bank, where he reveals he is Rex Lapis and gives his Gnosis to Signora.
### STANDARD +
**The Floating Palace.** Ganyu has delivered Ningguang's invitation. The Traveler and Paimon look for a way up to the Jade Chamber at Mt. Tianheng; a mechanism there does not take them far enough. The Traveler recalls the Guizhong Ballista that Zhongli repaired during the cocogoat hunt in the previous act. Millelith guarding it clash with them because Paimon assumes a trap; Keqing arrives, learns they are Ningguang's guests, and explains how to reach the Chamber. She adds that the Millelith are there because someone repaired the long-derelict ballista in a single day.

**Equilibrium.** Paimon insists tradition demands a gift for Ningguang, so the pair gather ingredients for a tasty slime snack, rescuing a man, Meng Dan, from Treasure Hoarders along the way. After visiting Mingxing Jewelry and finding a guide, they ascend to the Jade Chamber. Ningguang accepts the gift, offers one in return and talks at length about gods, Archons and the Fatui. The Traveler takes a piece of "paper snow" from the Chamber's intel wall.

**Solitary Fragrance.** The paper snow leads them to ruins where the Fatui have been researching the Sigils of Permission. They then meet Zhongli at Dihua Marsh, where he asks for wild Glaze Lilies for the Rite of Parting. While looking for them the Traveler meets Ganyu again and learns she is one of the adepti, who has watched over Liyue Harbor since its founding 3,700 years ago; she shares her thoughts on Rex Lapis' passing. On return the harbor is in turmoil: the adepti and the Qixing are in open confrontation, and Ningguang has started cracking down on the Fatui. Felix tells Zhongli the Millelith are questioning the Wangsheng Funeral Parlor over its Fatui ties; Zhongli leaves to help Hu Tao and tells the Traveler to find Childe.

**Heart of Glaze.** Believing Childe has gone to the Golden House after the Exuvia and the Gnosis, the Traveler duels him there. Childe reaches into the Exuvia and finds nothing, then wrongly assumes the Traveler has the Gnosis. In the fight he draws on his Delusion and a monstrous transformation, and is beaten. He admits Signora's report on the Traveler was wrong. His fallback is to use the Fatui-copied Sigils of Permission to release Osial, an ancient god Rex Lapis sealed under the Guyun Stone Forest, although he dislikes involving bystanders.

**Turning Point.** The adepti and the Qixing join forces. Ningguang's replica Guizhong Ballistas are manned by Cloud Retainer, Mountain Shaper and Moon Carver, while Fatui reinforcements arrive through portals; Keqing leads the Millelith and the Traveler is blessed by Xiao, Ganyu and Madame Ping. The ballista volley fails to hurt Osial, who destroys the platform, and Xiao pulls the Traveler clear. Ningguang then sacrifices the Jade Chamber, dropping it on Osial to press him into the seabed.

**The Fond Farewell.** The Traveler finds Zhongli at Northland Bank with Signora and Childe. He is Rex Lapis, the Geo Archon, and he arranged his own apparent assassination as a test of whether Liyue could do without him. In return for an undisclosed goal he struck a "contract to end all contracts" with the Tsaritsa. Satisfied, he gives his Gnosis to Signora, and the two Harbingers leave.
### LARGE +
After the battle the adepti and Qixing settle their differences and the Qixing hold the Rite of Parting, where the Traveler overhears rumours about the battle and the Fatui's involvement and hears the official story the Qixing have put out to explain Morax's demise. Keqing offers the Traveler a reward for stopping Osial; the Traveler only asks for help posting notices about their missing sibling. After talking with townspeople, the Traveler speaks to Zhongli about Liyue's future. Zhongli, aware of the Traveler's aim, says Inazuma is sealed and the Archon Baal has issued the Vision Hunt Decree, and he expects his own "death" to push her plans further. He tells the Traveler not to give up on finding a way in.
### ULTRA +
Structurally the act mirrors Act II: the first half is errands and courtesy (a gift for Ningguang, flowers for the rite), the second half escalates sharply. Childe's mistaken belief that the Traveler holds the Gnosis is the hinge, because it pushes him to the Osial plan. Zhongli's reveal recasts everything since the Rite of Descension as part of his test.

## revelations
@version: 1.1
@spoiler: major
### LITE
Zhongli is the Geo Archon, Rex Lapis (Morax), and staged his own death.
### STANDARD +
He did so as a test of Liyue's ability to survive without a god, working through a deal with the Tsaritsa, and handed his Gnosis to Signora. The Fatui's Sigil of Permission copies were used to free Osial.
### LARGE +
The Exuvia held no Gnosis, so Childe's plan and Signora's report on the Traveler were both based on wrong assumptions. Zhongli's ultimate goal is not stated in this act. Osial is stopped by the Qixing and adepti working together, not by the Traveler alone.

## characters
### LITE
Traveler and Paimon; Zhongli; Ningguang; Ganyu; Keqing; Childe (Tartaglia); Signora.
### STANDARD +
Also Xiao, Hu Tao, Madame Ping, Cloud Retainer, Mountain Shaper, Moon Carver, Osial, Felix, Meng Dan and the Millelith.
### LARGE +
The Tsaritsa is referred to in Zhongli's deal but does not appear.

## consequences
### STANDARD
Liyue continues without a god, the Qixing and adepti repair their relationship, and Zhongli no longer holds his Gnosis. Signora carries it to the Fatui. The Traveler is pointed toward Inazuma.
### LARGE +
Act IV, We Will Be Reunited, picks up the Traveler's search for their sibling with Dainsleif, and the Inazuma storyline follows in Chapter II.

## placement
### STANDARD
Chapter I Act III, following Farewell, Archaic Lord and before We Will Be Reunited. The wiki lists Adventure Rank 28 as the requirement for this act. Released in Version 1.1.

## entry: Osial
@keys: Osial, Overlord of the Vortex
@version: 1.1
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Osial is an ancient god whom Rex Lapis defeated in the Archon War and sealed beneath the Guyun Stone Forest. The Fatui release him with copied Sigils of Permission; he withstands the adepti's ballista fire and wrecks the platform, and is finally pinned under the Jade Chamber.
### LARGE +
The Abyss Order later plans to use Osial's limbs in a mechanized god (We Will Be Reunited).

## entry: Sacrifice of the Jade Chamber
@keys: Jade Chamber falls, Floating Palace, Ningguang's palace
@version: 1.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Ningguang's floating palace, the Jade Chamber, is dropped on Osial to crush him into the seabed. It is a large personal loss and a statement that the Qixing will spend what they must to protect Liyue.

## entry: Zhongli's reveal at Northland Bank
@keys: Zhongli reveal, contract to end all contracts
@version: 1.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
In a private meeting with Signora and Childe at Northland Bank, Zhongli admits he is the Geo Archon, says he arranged his own "assassination" as a test and hands over the Gnosis in exchange for an unnamed goal agreed with the Tsaritsa. He calls it a "contract to end all contracts".
