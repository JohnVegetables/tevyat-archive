## summary
### LITE
Act VII of the Song of the Welkin Moon Archon Quest, set in Nod-Krai. Dottore, armed with an artificial Moon Marrow, defeats the party's attempt to stop him and captures the Traveler. While the region is evacuated and strange energy fields appear, Columbina, trapped in the moon's reflection, travels back through memories to leave messages and aid for her friends.
### STANDARD +
The act has three quests: Moonless Night, Where Our Space and Time Intersect, and Tracing the Footsteps of the Past. It follows Melting Moonlight in the Morning Mist and precedes True Moon. It was released in version 6.3 (Luna IV). Its three strands are the confrontation with Dottore and the Traveler's captivity, the group's response (evacuation, research, alliances with the Fatui), and Columbina's attempts to change the past.
### LARGE +
The act is structured around who can act where: the Traveler is isolated with Dottore, Columbina is outside time in the moon's reflection, and the others work on logistics and research. Several of Columbina's interventions are shown to be the hidden cause of events the party earlier found puzzling, so earlier acts are recast in light of her later actions.

## plot
### LITE
After the Dottore incident, the Fatui send word that Arlecchino and Sandrone are heading for Dottore's lab. The party follows, fights Dottore, and the Traveler is captured. The rest of the group organises an evacuation and tries to find the Traveler, while Columbina uses the moon's reflection to influence the past.
### STANDARD +
**Moonless Night.** A few days after the Dottore incident, the group at the Curatorium of Secrets senses a Fatuus, Gediminas, who delivers a message: Arlecchino and Sandrone are heading to Dottore's lab in the far north, and the party should meet them there. He gives them a map and says the Fatui will be allies if they choose to rely on them. Aino, Ineffa, Varka and Jahoda stay to guard Nasha Town. The others reach the facility, finding wrecked Landcruisers scorched by Arlecchino's flames and slain Fatui soldiers. A Fatui grunt, Jotautas, reluctantly lets them pass after being persuaded that Dottore could endanger all of Snezhnaya. He explains Dottore had been building something ambitious and returned with two gemstones that Lauma recognises as the Moon Marrows stolen from Columbina. Jotautas served in Columbina's former unit, which came under Dottore's command after she left the Fatui, and learned Dottore was trying to make an artificial Moon Marrow. He declines to serve Lauma, says his loyalty is to the Tsaritsa, and is directed to the Design Bureau.

At the facility's heart Dottore, who expected them, says Arlecchino "disappeared" on entering. He recounts Sandrone's confrontation with him: she said his ascension was going too far, he replied it was fact, and she left to check on her unit after he said he was sending his people to her. The group fights Dottore. Arlecchino joins mid-fight, but with the artificial Moon Marrow he simply restores his injuries. Arlecchino orders them to leave; Dottore captures the Traveler and teleports the others away.

**Where Our Space and Time Intersect.** The Traveler finds Nasha Town's residents behaving strangely, and Dottore changes the venue with a gesture. Elsewhere Columbina wakes in the moon's reflection, surprised to be alive since Rerir and his fiancee Tholindis were presumed killed on entering it. She explores, finds memories (including one of Moon-Prayer Night and her time with Barbeloth) and discovers a kuuvahki pulse that can alter past events: she makes a tarot reading draw the "Death" card. An unknown force pulls her back.

### LARGE +
At the Curatorium, Paimon is horrified at the Traveler's capture. Arlecchino says Dottore has used his artificial moon to replace the Frost Moon in the sky and, not being rejected by Teyvat, could gain its primordial power if he controls all three moons. Nefer reports strange spaces emerging in Nod-Krai. Sandrone arrives with her research: the Design Bureau was swallowed by an energy field. Pulonia scans everyone who entered the facility and finds Dottore cursed them, with a mocking message encoded in the curse; Sandrone believes it activates only if they leave Nod-Krai. As a Descender, the Traveler is presumed not to be killed yet. More energy fields appear, so the group decides to evacuate the islands. Arlecchino suggests Snezhnaya as temporary refuge. Dottore shows the Traveler a Trilune holding the Moon Marrows and offers to work together and end their journey instantly; the Traveler plays along to learn his motives, noting that his words mix truth with manipulation, including a pull toward seeing their sibling again.

The evacuation plan: Lauma handles the Southern Isles and Hiisi Island, with refugees heading toward Natlan; Arlecchino and the Fatui handle everything north of the Design Bureau, sending people to Snezhnaya; Aino and Ineffa help; Nefer asks Jahoda to assist Lauma while she handles intelligence; Dori returns to Sumeru to tell Nahida; Durin flies to Mondstadt to tell Venti; Albedo stays to work with Sandrone. Nicole arrives, says Alice has recruited every Hexenzirkel member except her to the border, and gives each person a Dodocommunication Device. Flins tells Illuga he has been cursed and cannot leave, and Illuga tells him he will ask again whether Flins is human once this is over. Columbina meanwhile makes a third attempt in the past, arriving at the Flagship (and breaking a vase), and leaves a message in kuuvahki on a wall; Nefer and Flins find a vase knocked over, then throw a cup at a grimy window so moonlight reveals the message. They conclude she is still alive.

**Tracing the Footsteps of the Past.** Flins and Nefer report to Albedo and Sandrone. Paimon and Varka have left for Natlan. Sandrone studies the world-formula using Rene and Alain's research. Arlecchino takes Sandrone's subordinates into her own unit and meets Childe, who says the Tsaritsa has Fatui Internal Affairs investigating Dottore and has given Arlecchino and Sandrone clearance to kill him. A Fatuus turns into a monster, which Arlecchino calls one of Dottore's experiments. Childe wants to rescue the Traveler but accepts Arlecchino's instruction to complete his refugee escort to Snezhnaya, and she asks him to have Pulcinella warn the other nations. Albedo and the Wanderer, with Durin's Pyro and Abyssal power, crack an energy field that Albedo judges to be Light Realm energy, and inside they find human bodies and animals: Dottore has swapped human minds into animals.
### ULTRA +
Further details. Nefer tries to reach the Traveler's mind through their Adventurer's Handbook; Dottore notices, tells the Traveler he will freeze them briefly, and Nicole cuts off Nefer's connection. When the Traveler wakes, Dottore apologises for having knocked them out for over a month rather than a few moments, admitting he cannot yet control time precisely. He argues that the Traveler does not understand humans, that their long life and alien status mean Teyvat will never fully accept them, that they have been treated unfairly, and offers to restore them to their rightful status as a Descender.

Columbina's later trips: she arrives at Starsand Shoal before Rerir regained a physical form. As the cannon prepares to fire and the Traveler summons the kuuvahki shield she taught them, she realises lethal kuuvahki would strike the Frostmoon Enclave, and that the unexplained shield that protected Hiisi Island (which Flins had asked her about) was her own work. She spends most of her power creating it, collapses, and dreams of a talk with Sandrone in Snezhnaya. Next she reaches the Final Night Cemetery, moves books and records the Lightkeepers' library needed, and is seen by Luonnotar. She realises the familiar presence she felt near the Traveler on each trip was Luonnotar. In the final portal, set when the Traveler and Paimon head for Hiisi Island after reuniting Ineffa and Aino, she creates Luonnotar from a part of her soul, tells her to trail the Traveler, and instructs her to lead them to Silvermoon Hall to meet Columbina's past self, "a little lost dove".

On the earlier trips Columbina taps the Traveler's shoulder in the days before Moon-Prayer Night; the Traveler responds without sensing her. She realises this is why the Traveler seemed to notice her when she first disappeared, which had encouraged her past self to follow them and learn of their plan to bring her back. She learns she is travelling backwards in time on the Flagship trip. Her use of power risks throwing her out of time, so she tries to leave minimal, subtle clues.

The Landcruisers, Jotautas's account and Sandrone's scanning give a Fatui-side view of Dottore's isolation: Fatui who were once Columbina's subordinates now serve under him, and the Tsaritsa has set Internal Affairs on him. Arlecchino calls his ambitions treason of the highest order against the Tsaritsa.

## revelations
@version: 6.3
@spoiler: major
### LITE
Dottore has built an artificial Moon Marrow, carries a Trilune holding the Moon Marrows, has replaced the Frost Moon with an artificial moon, and captured the Traveler.
### STANDARD +
Dottore stole Columbina's Moon Marrows, and with the artificial one can heal himself. Arlecchino infers he intends to hold all three moons for Teyvat's primordial power. He cursed everyone who entered his facility, offers the Traveler a partnership, and has swapped human minds into animals in an experiment. Columbina is alive in the moon's reflection and can alter the past via kuuvahki pulses.
### LARGE +
Columbina is behind earlier unexplained events: the kuuvahki shield over Hiisi Island, the shift in the Traveler's perception of her, and the creation of Luonnotar from part of her soul. The Tsaritsa has authorised Arlecchino and Sandrone to kill Dottore if he is a threat. Albedo identifies the energy fields as pure Light Realm energy. Dottore says the Traveler's alien status means they will never be fully accepted by Teyvat.

## characters
### LITE
Traveler and Paimon; Dottore; Columbina; Arlecchino; Sandrone; the Curatorium group.
### STANDARD +
Also Gediminas, Jotautas, Pulonia, Lauma, Varka, Durin, Albedo, the Wanderer, Flins, Nefer, Jahoda, Aino, Ineffa, Dori, Illuga, Nicole, Childe, and Luonnotar.

## consequences
### STANDARD
Dottore holds the Traveler in an unknown space, Nod-Krai has energy fields spreading and a curse restricting those who entered the facility, and the region is being evacuated, with refugees sent to Snezhnaya and toward Natlan. Dori goes to tell Nahida in Sumeru and Durin flies to tell Venti in Mondstadt, and Arlecchino asks Childe to have Pulcinella warn the other nations. The story continues in True Moon.
### LARGE +
Columbina's interventions leave the party knowing she is alive and a guide, Luonnotar, trailing the Traveler. Fatui Internal Affairs and the Tsaritsa's clearance to kill Dottore set up the Fatui moving against him.
