## summary
### LITE
Chapter IV Act III of the Archon Quest, in Fontaine. With Childe jailed and then vanished, Neuvillette secretly sends the Traveler and Paimon into the Fortress of Meropide on false charges to find out what happened to him.
### STANDARD +
The act has four quests: A Tea Party Most Thorny, Fortress of Meropide, The Proscribed, Hidden in Plain Sight, and Lost in Deep Seas. It follows Act II (As Light Rain Falls Without Reason), in which Childe was apprehended, and introduces the prison's economy of Credit Coupons, its "hidden rules", the Duke Wriothesley and the head nurse Sigewinne. Released in version 4.1.
### LARGE +
The tone shifts from courtroom drama to prison investigation. The act advances the search for Childe, but the Traveler only reaches the flooded pipe exit by the end; what lies beyond it is left for the next act.

## plot
### LITE
Neuvillette arranges for the Traveler and Paimon to be imprisoned in the Fortress of Meropide so they can quietly look into Childe's disappearance. Inside, they meet Wriothesley, Sigewinne and Lyney's group, learn of the prison's hidden rules, and discover that Childe escaped through the pipes.
### STANDARD +
**A Tea Party Most Thorny.** Days after settling in with the Spina di Rosula, the Traveler is told by Isadora that Neuvillette wants to meet at Palais Mermonia. A flashback shows the Knave asking Furina for a meeting; Neuvillette advises refusing because Childe has been detained in the Fortress of Meropide, but Furina agrees, trusting his help. The Knave thanks them for the House of the Hearth's children being better off and asks for Childe's custody, which Neuvillette refuses because the trial has ended. He also says the prison lies outside the court's jurisdiction, and proposes that someone else investigate on her behalf, which she reluctantly accepts. Back in the present, Neuvillette reveals that Childe has since disappeared from the prison and was not seen outside. He asks the Traveler to investigate from inside, and will arrange false charges to get them in. He gives them the Fruity Trio cake. Paimon eats it and orders heavily at Cafe Lutece; Charlotte, whom they meet there, asks them to gather information on Wriothesley, titled "Duke", for her stories, and pays for the meal. At the prison gate the charges are read out: eating cake meant for Furina, with a sentence of 45 days.

**Fortress of Meropide.** Deakin guides them and explains Credit Coupons, the prison's currency. At the entrance they see many Gardemeks and Wriothesley, who takes over the tour. They glimpse Lyney, who pretends not to know them, and finish at the infirmary with head nurse Sigewinne. Later, Lyney and Lynette (who got in with a staged "theft", alongside Freminet) say the prison hides a secret and that the Knave confirmed Furina does not hold the Hydro Gnosis, though they do not know how. They should focus on the "forbidden zone". Vidoc warns the Traveler about "hidden rules". That night the Traveler dreams of Childe's time in the prison.

**The Proscribed, Hidden in Plain Sight.** The next day they explore the facilities and learn two rules: three straight days of work leads to mystery meat, and betting on both fighters in a match is forbidden. They conclude that these rules are not directly related to Childe and rest.

**Lost in Deep Seas.** Vidoc warns them to avoid the pipes late at night because of rumours of cannibals who turn victims into other "forms", then goes missing, and others nearby refuse to talk. Fielding confirms strange sounds on monthly pipe-cleaning days. A second dream shows the Traveler that Childe used the pipes. They find the three men from earlier, who admit the rules were a pretence to protect Childe: they first despised him, then followed him after he proved his strength. He left after hearing "that voice" again, and the entrance he used is flooded. The Traveler has Lyney ask Freminet to help with the dive.
### LARGE +
The prison arc quietly cross-references earlier acts: Lyney explicitly avoids hiding secrets again after the opera-house trial; Neuvillette cannot use the court to investigate because the fortress sits outside the court's jurisdiction, though he knows the administrator personally. The charges and the 45-day sentence are a cover, and Charlotte is told only a minor offence. Fielding's information comes from colleagues, and the pipe sounds are tied to scheduled cleaning days.
### ULTRA +
Quest-by-quest function: the first sets the political stakes and cover story; the second maps people and rules; the third gathers rule trivia without progress on Childe; the fourth finds the escape route. The act ends before the Traveler learns where Childe went.

## revelations
@version: 4.1
@spoiler: major
### LITE
Childe has escaped the prison via the pipes after hearing a voice.
### STANDARD +
The Fortress of Meropide hides a "forbidden zone" and operates outside the court's jurisdiction. The Knave has confirmed Furina does not have the Hydro Gnosis. The prison's "hidden rules" were partly invented to protect Childe.
### LARGE +
The Knave sought custody of Childe and was refused because the trial had concluded; since the prison is outside the court's jurisdiction, she reluctantly accepted Neuvillette's proposal of a third-party investigation.

## characters
### LITE
Traveler and Paimon; Neuvillette; Wriothesley; Sigewinne; Lyney, Lynette and Freminet.
### STANDARD +
Also Furina and the Knave (Arlecchino) in flashback, Isadora, Charlotte, Childe (in dreams and by reference), Deakin, Vidoc, Fielding, and the three prisoners who helped Childe.

## consequences
### STANDARD
The Traveler finds Childe's escape route, which leads to a flooded passage. The investigation continues in the next act, Cataclysm's Quickening.
### LARGE +
Freminet is recruited to help dive the flooded pipe.
