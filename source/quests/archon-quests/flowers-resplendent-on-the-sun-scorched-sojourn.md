## summary
@spoiler: minor
### LITE
Act I of Chapter V is the Traveler's arrival in Natlan. The Traveler and Paimon meet Kachina of the Children of Echoes, follow her to the Stadium of the Sacred Flame, and meet Mualani and Kinich. They watch the Pilgrimage of the Return of the Sacred Flame, a tournament that decides who fights the Abyss, and then visit Mualani's village, where the Traveler purges fresh Abyssal corruption from Mualani's gravely wounded friend Atea but cannot undo the old damage; she has only days to live.
### STANDARD +
Act I has three quests: "Natlan! A New Adventure", "Pilgrimage of the Return of the Sacred Flame" and "Home of the Hot Springs". It requires Adventure Rank 40, and a quick start was offered from 5.0 until 6.0 to players who had completed "A New Star Approaches" (Adventure Rank 28). The Traveler cannot gain Pyro from the local Statue of The Seven, learns that Natlan's Pyro Archon is a human, and learns the basics of the tribes, Saurian companions, Ancient Names and the Ode of Resurrection.
### LARGE +
The act is a tour of the nation's culture before the war story begins. It stages three tribes in turn (the Children of Echoes, the Scions of the Canopy and the People of the Springs), sets up the Pilgrimage's stakes and ends on Atea's approaching death; her talisman reaches Mavuika in Act II, and her passing is mourned in Act V.
### ULTRA +
A separate Version 5.0 event with the same name ("Flowers Resplendent on the Sun-Scorched Sojourn (Event)") is not the same as this Archon Quest act.

## plot
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
**Natlan! A New Adventure.** After a farewell from Charlotte, Navia, Clorinde and Furina at Romaritime Harbor, the Traveler and Paimon sail to Natlan and find a village in a canyon. Kachina is told by her teammates that they are leaving her for the Pilgrimage. The Traveler offers to help and she guides them around the village, to the black stone that represents the tribe's Wayob and to the chief, Pacal. At the stadium city they meet Mualani, Kinich and his companion K'uhul Ajaw. The Pilgrimage is open only to Natlanese, so the Traveler cannot join. The group has dinner at the Weary Inn.

**Pilgrimage of the Return of the Sacred Flame.** Mavuika opens the tournament with an address to the contestants. The Traveler and Paimon watch from the stands as Kachina and Mualani clear the team stage. Two disqualified contestants, Tepexpan and Tamuin, threaten Kachina and are stopped. Kachina wins her one-on-one matches, including a duel with her friend Mualani, and earns a place in the Night Warden Wars with Chasca, Iansan, Kaiulani and Kibangu.

**Home of the Hot Springs.** Mualani takes the Traveler to her village, the People of the Springs. Atea explains that outsiders must acclimate in the artificial springs first. During the night the Abyss attacks. Atea has destroyed an Abyssal pylon but is gravely wounded. The Traveler purifies the Abyssal energy from her wounds, as they once did for Dvalin, but the damage is too deep to heal. At a party the next day Atea tells Mualani and the Traveler she has only a few days to live and gives the Traveler her talisman to hand to the Pyro Archon.
### LARGE +
Detail from the first quest: at Natlan the Traveler follows human signs to the Children of Echoes' canyon, where Kachina's team leaves her. Kachina explains that her people can align themselves to a rhythm and that the Tepetlisaurs, such as her companion Ayo, are their Saurians. Pacal at first doubts the Traveler and Paimon and asks for proof of identity; he reads their Adventurers' Guild handbook and apologises, having heard of them. Kachina is unsure why the Wayob chose her for an Ancient Name. Kachina shows them the Statue of The Seven, learns the Traveler can use many elements, and explains that the Pyro Archon is human. The Saurian Toto offers a ride to the stadium city. Kinich explains the Pilgrimage's purpose: a test of who may fight the Abyss, with five people to earn the honour that round. Cival refuses to let the Traveler register, since Contending Fire, the fuel of the Sacred Flame, is made only in fights between Natlanese. He adds that the Ode of Resurrection works only on Ancient Name bearers and that those who fail against the Abyss die and have their Ancient Name forsaken and forgotten.

Detail from the second quest: Kachina defeats Nkunga and the more experienced Liliuo. Before the final, the ranking leaves Kachina and Mualani as each other's last opponent, and Mualani accepts either result; Kachina wins with a surprise attack.

Detail from the third quest: Mualani's village is a hot-spring resort; Atea is a good friend of Mualani and could no longer enjoy the springs after being impaled by an Abyssal monster. Chief Amina and her guards plan a party for Atea. After the attack Mualani and the Traveler destroy one pylon and follow the monsters to an abandoned spring, where Atea has already destroyed the second at the cost of severe injuries; she had been going there because she knew her end was near.
### ULTRA +
Fine detail: Paimon worries on the voyage that Natlan may have no human population because no Natlanese have been seen in other nations; the act shows the tribes living there. Kinich remarks that Kachina was ditched by teammates again, and says Mualani is a three-time veteran of the Night Warden Wars who has never needed to be resurrected, and that he has himself fallen several times and been brought to the Wayob's realm, the Night Kingdom. Mualani first asks whether the Traveler could join under special circumstances, expecting a refusal; if refused, she will pair with Kachina and Kinich will lead the team Mualani had gathered.

## revelations
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
- Natlan's Pyro Archon, Mavuika, is a human, and opens the Pilgrimage in person.
- The Pilgrimage produces Contending Fire, which fuels the Sacred Flame that keeps the Abyss from invading; the tournament also picks who fights in the Night Warden Wars.
- Ancient Names mark those chosen by the Wayob, and the Ode of Resurrection can revive only those who hold them; fallen bearers go to the Night Kingdom.
- Natlan has six tribes: the Children of Echoes, Scions of the Canopy, People of the Springs, Masters of the Night-Wind, Flower-Feather Clan and Collective of Plenty.
### LARGE +
- The Traveler can purify Abyssal corruption, but cannot save deep injuries.

## characters
### LARGE
Kachina, Mualani, Kinich with K'uhul Ajaw, Pacal, Cival, Atea, Amina, Chasca and Iansan appear. Charlotte, Navia, Clorinde and Furina farewell the Traveler in Fontaine. Mavuika appears in person to deliver the Pilgrimage's opening address.

## consequences
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Kachina becomes a Night Warden War fighter, Mualani and Kinich become the Traveler's guides, and Atea's talisman is left with the Traveler. Act II follows: the Night Warden Wars are won, but Kachina falls in battle and Mavuika cannot find her in the Sacred Flame.

## placement
### STANDARD
Follows Chapter IV Act VI "Bedtime Story" and is the first act of Chapter V. It was released in 5.0 alongside Act II, with Acts III and IV in 5.1.

## entry: Atea's talisman
@keys: Atea, Atea's Talisman
@version: 5.0
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Atea is a respected member of the People of the Springs and a friend of Mualani; long unable to fight or enjoy the springs since being impaled by an Abyssal monster, she volunteers for night patrol and is gravely wounded destroying an Abyssal pylon. The Traveler cannot heal the old Abyssal contamination. Atea hands her talisman to the Traveler to give to the Pyro Archon.

## entry: Pilgrimage of the Return of the Sacred Flame
@keys: Pilgrimage, Night Warden Wars, Contending Fire
@version: 5.0
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
The Pilgrimage is a tournament held in the Stadium of the Sacred Flame. Teams collect Contending Sparks before one-on-one duels. It produces Contending Fire, which keeps the Sacred Flame burning, and chooses the Natlanese who fight in the Night Warden Wars.
