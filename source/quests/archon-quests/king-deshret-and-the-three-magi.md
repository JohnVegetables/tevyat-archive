## summary
### LITE
The fourth act of Sumeru's Archon Quest. Village Keepers, scholars of the desert who tend its villages, are going missing, and rumours say King Deshret has returned. The Traveler, Paimon, Cyno, Candace, Dehya and Alhaitham of the Akademiya trace the kidnappings to an Akademiya-backed plot, find the buried ruins of King Deshret's civilisation, and learn that the sages mean to create a new god.
### STANDARD +
The act has three quests: The Missing Village Keepers, Cry From the Eleazar Hospital and Secret of the Scorching Desert. It pairs the Traveler with Cyno and Alhaitham in the desert, shows Dehya's standing among the Eremites, and uncovers the Akademiya's own plan for a god to replace Kusanali.
### LARGE +
The act is the point where the sages' project stops being rumour: two of the people responsible are caught and confirm the plan to create a new god. It is also where the story discards the rumour that King Deshret is returning and replaces it with the story of what he did.

## plot
### STANDARD
The Traveler, Cyno, Candace, Dehya and Alhaitham investigate the disappearance of Village Keepers around Aaru Village, where rumours say King Deshret is returning. Radical Eremites confess that a masked "envoy" gave them incense to lure the Keepers out, and Alhaitham, who stayed behind, leads the group to an abandoned Eleazar hospital where one Keeper is found. He reveals that the Akademiya's misinformation drives radicals to act and that it monitors Cyno through the Akasha, so the culprits have gone the opposite way, into the desert.

There Dehya bargains with the Eremite leader Rahman, offering her own arm as a pledge for her friends' honesty. After an earthquake the group falls into buried ruins of King Deshret, where Alhaitham reads the last priest Kasala's runes about Deshret's forbidden knowledge, Rukkhadevata's rescue of his people and Deshret's sacrifice. Rahman accepts the truth and returns the Keepers. The sages' agents Morghi and Judar then confirm that the Akademiya, prompted by Il Dottore, plans to create a new god, and the group resolves to oppose the sages.
### LARGE +
In detail.

In Aaru Village the Traveler and Cyno question locals about the missing Village Keepers. Cyno overhears two men, Sharkan and Makan, speaking of King Deshret's revival, and frightens out of them that a radical group is behind the disappearances. At the house of a missing Keeper, the Traveler is nearly knocked out by incense; Cyno works out that someone lured Isak's grandfather away with it. Dehya takes the Traveler to Caravan Ribat to find her drinking friends among the Eremites. She tells the Traveler how she hates the Wall of Samiel, which the sages use to keep the desert people out of Sumeru. She has Zaki tell Delavar she has a spice deal for him; the meeting turns out to be an ambush by Delavar and Engur, who underestimate her and are easily defeated by her and the Traveler, then taken to the village. Candace questions them: a masked man claiming to be King Deshret's envoy gave them incense, and they were to meet him at a junction. Uncle Anpu suggests the man is linked to the Akademiya, which had asked for the Keepers back before.

Alhaitham, who stayed in the village, points out that the villager Shani lied. She heard screaming at night near an abandoned Eleazar hospital, Dar al-Shifa. There the group finds Razak, a Keeper, but evidence of a hasty withdrawal. Alhaitham explains the Akademiya's misinformation campaign spurs radicals to act unwittingly, so no blame reaches the sages, as at Port Ormos. When Cyno wonders how the Akademiya learned their plans, Alhaitham tells him Cyno himself is the leak, much to his anger: the sages knew his convictions and used the Akasha to predict his movements, so the culprits head where he would not go, the desert.

In the desert they find buried headsets, then Dehya arguing with the Eremite leader Rahman. Alhaitham offers himself as a hostage and Dehya offers her right arm to prove her friends' honesty; Rahman orders it cut off, then relents, seeing it as cutting his own fingers, and sets a fight for the next day. The next day Rahman has brought only one Keeper, and Dehya beats him quickly. An earthquake swallows them in sand, but Isak's grandfather shields them and they wake in ruins belonging to King Deshret, infused with the Dendro Archon's power. Alhaitham reads runes by the last priest Kasala. They state that Deshret's forbidden knowledge drove his people mad, Rukkhadevata gave them life, Deshret sacrificed himself so the knowledge would not return, and Rukkhadevata became a small child. Rahman accepts that his people have made an enemy of their savior, apologises, and returns the Keepers.

At Rahman's stronghold the culprits, Morghi and Judar, panic at the sight of Alhaitham and Cyno. They confirm that the sages plan to create a new god because Akademiya breakthroughs have dwindled since Rukkhadevata's death. Il Dottore proposed the plan; the dream samsara and the harvest of Divine Canned Knowledge from the mad scholars are part of it. Everyone agrees this is unacceptable and plans to crush the sages and rescue their true god.
### ULTRA +
Before leaving Aaru, Candace stays behind to handle the radicals while Cyno knocks them out so they cannot learn more; Dehya will not let Cyno join the visit to Caravan Ribat because he might frighten her friends. The headsets in the sand are recent. Alhaitham stakes himself as hostage and Dehya checks whether he will keep his promise and whether Rahman trusts her, which both do, before offering her arm.

## revelations
### STANDARD
- The rumour that King Deshret is returning is a misinformation campaign aided by the Akademiya, which nudges radicals into acting while hiding the sages' involvement.
- The sages are working to create a new god after the decline in the Akademiya's breakthroughs; the plot was proposed by Il Dottore.
- The Akasha can predict a user's activity for the day, which let the sages monitor Cyno.
- King Deshret's ruins contain a record, by his last priest Kasala, of the forbidden knowledge that drove his people mad, of Rukkhadevata saving them, and of Deshret's self-sacrifice.
- The dream samsara and "Divine Canned Knowledge" harvested from mad scholars are parts of the sages' project.
### LARGE +
The desert people who hate the Akademiya and the Dendro Archon learn that the god they regarded as an enemy had saved their ancestors.

## characters
### STANDARD
Traveler and Paimon with Cyno, Candace, Dehya and Alhaitham. Rahman, leader of an Eremite band and Dehya's friend, is the main antagonist and then ally. Isak and his grandfather, a Village Keeper, Shani, Razak, Uncle Anpu, Zaki, Delavar, Engur, Sharkan and Makan appear. Morghi and Judar are the sages' agents. Tighnari is mentioned as Cyno's friend, and Dottore is named but absent.
### LARGE +
Locals are visibly afraid of Cyno, which is why Dehya keeps him away from her friends; Alhaitham is the one who can read Deshret's runes.

## consequences
### STANDARD
The group returns to the village with the Keepers and the captured culprits and begins planning against the sages. The story proceeds to Act V.
### LARGE +
Labelled as later developments: in Act V Nahida and the Traveler use Setaria to learn more, and Dottore, named here only in passing, appears in person; the sages' plan to ascend Scaramouche (the Wanderer) as a new god is stopped, and Kusanali is freed.

## placement
### STANDARD
Chapter III, Act IV. Released in Version 3.1. The previous act is Dreams, Emptiness, Deception; the next is Akasha Pulses, the Kalpa Flame Rises. Story order and release order match.

### STANDARD
Kasala is the last priest of King Deshret, whose runes in the buried ruins tell how Deshret's forbidden knowledge drove his people mad, how Greater Lord Rukkhadevata used her power to give them life, and how Deshret sacrificed himself while Rukkhadevata reverted to a small child. Alhaitham deciphers the text.

## entry: Rahman and Dehya
@keys: Rahman
@keys2: Dehya, Eremites
### STANDARD
Rahman leads an Eremite band and is Dehya's friend. He refuses to trust the Traveler's group and has Village Keepers hostage until Dehya offers her arm. He stops at the last second, saying that harming her would be like cutting off his own fingers. After the ruins, he apologises and promises to return the Keepers and share supplies.
