## summary
@spoiler: major
### LITE
"Incandescent Ode of Resurrection" is Act V, the last act of Chapter V, the Natlan Archon Quest. The Traveler and Paimon prepare for the final strike against the Abyss: they receive an Ancient Name forged by Xilonen, go with the Pyro Archon Mavuika into the Night Kingdom, and help her destroy the enemy's core. Afterwards Natlan celebrates the Traveler, and the cost of Mavuika's plan is settled when Capitano takes her place at the Lord of the Night's side.
### STANDARD
The act has five quests (Subterranean Ruins; As the Sun Rises and Sets; Journey of Stars and Flames; Where All Hopes Lie; When All Becomes a Monument) and requires Adventure Rank 40. It follows "All Fires Fuel the Flame"; the act's navigation lists "A Space and Time for You" and "Paralogism" as what comes next. The travel log recap frames it as the end of Natlan's millennia-long war against the Abyss, with the nation facing a hopeful future that its sacrifices have paid for. Within the act: Capitano appears in a dream, the Traveler is given the Ancient Name "Tumaini" (hope), the final battle with Gosoythoth is won, and the aftermath settles Mavuika's fate and points the Traveler toward Snezhnaya and Nod-Krai.

## plot
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
**Subterranean Ruins.** The Traveler dreams of the Night Kingdom and sees Capitano and Guthred leading "soldiers" toward a destined place; a rift shows on Capitano's chest.

**As the Sun Rises and Sets.** Mavuika, Xilonen and Citlali plan the descent, and the tribes hold a send-off feast. Ororon's ritual on the Traveler's dream identifies Guthred, and Mavuika confirms that Capitano is Thrain, a legendary hero of Natlan from five hundred years ago. Xilonen delivers the Ancient Name "Tumaini" and tests it at the Sulfurous Veins, where it draws in unusual numbers of souls that the group helps to the Night Kingdom.

**Journey of Stars and Flames.** The Traveler reveals the Ancient Name at the Stadium of the Sacred Flame, and Mavuika takes them into the Night Kingdom. They split: Mavuika seeks the enemy's core, the Traveler its lair, aided by departed allies and Guthred.

**Where All Hopes Lie.** Mavuika and the Traveler, empowered with Pyro by the Lord of the Night, destroy the dragon-formed Gosoythoth.

**When All Becomes a Monument.** After a ceremony honouring the Traveler, Ronova demands Mavuika's death at Ochkanatlan, and Capitano offers himself instead. See revelations and consequences.
### LARGE +
Subterranean Ruins: Paimon suggests resting at the Weary Inn because the Ancient Name should soon be ready; the Traveler passes through a wall of light behind which souls are tormented.

As the Sun Rises and Sets: Mavuika will go to the deepest parts in three days, and if the Ancient Name is not ready she must go alone. The Traveler invites Mavuika to the feast at the People of the Springs. Kachina takes the Traveler on a walk that ends with helping the despondent saurian Ayo. At the Sulfurous Veins the group helps souls including Vichama, and Ayo is reunited with the spirits of his grandparents, who choose to stay with him.

Journey of Stars and Flames: departed allies include Atea, Mallko, Vichama, Bona, Cocouik and Chuychu. The heroes of 500 years ago bless the Traveler and the Lord of the Night sends seelies to guide them to Mavuika.

Where All Hopes Lie: Gosoythoth takes control of the Traveler while Mavuika is cast into a void that tries to sow doubt in her; she refuses it. The Sacred Flame goes out in Natlan, but Kachina steadies the crowd, and with their strengthened resolve the two win.

When All Becomes a Monument: Chasca presents a feathered crown on Natlan's behalf. Citlali tells the Traveler that Mavuika is still due to die as the price of restoring the Night Kingdom; at Ochkanatlan a rift of watching eyes appears and Ronova, the Ruler of Death, demands her death as payment.

Details of the feast and the dreams: Amina is overwhelmed by the quantity of supplies the tribes send, because the party grew into a collective send-off; she mentions that Atea, who would normally have handled logistics, died shortly after the attack on the tribe. Iansan recalls a dream of her saurian companion who died in the war. Ororon calls the Traveler's dream an outlier compared to those granted from the divine throne, and learns from the vision that Guthred's soul has persisted for hundreds of years and that Capitano's body holds an amalgamation of souls. Mavuika suggests Ororon ask Capitano directly. Mavuika also tells the Traveler she paints as a hobby, though duty rarely leaves her time.

Details of the Night Kingdom: at a pit stop the chiefs and friends wish the pair luck; Mavuika explains the enemy has a core that behaves like the tumors seen during the invasion. She meets a Cryo-aligned Abyss herald and then her sister Hine, who promises support. The Traveler is met by Atea and a group of Natlanese who stay behind to keep the Abyssal corrosion from the square behind them, because if it spread the Abyss could surge back into Natlan. Mallko and Vichama maintain a protective barrier against the fog. Bona realises Guthred is not from Natlan; Guthred says he is a story created by the Masters of the Night-Wind and so can manifest. The spirits can go no further because of the Ley Lines, and Chuychu, who also visited Chasca in dreams, provides an iktomisaurus and a qucusaur for the last stretch.

The act's ending: Ronova says she has no interest in Teyvat and names Mavuika's death as the price of using her power. Mavuika argues the Lord of the Night is still weak and the Abyss may return, so she means to put her life into the Sacred Flame again. Ronova accepts Mavuika's sacrifice as the death owed, but the Lord of the Night pauses, saying another wishes to take part in the ceremony and she must wait for him.
### ULTRA +
After the ceremony the Traveler leaves the feathered crown at a new monument for the fallen of the final battle. Back in the Speaker's Chamber, Mavuika writes a letter to her sister Hine and burns it so that it may be "sent" to her, telling her that Natlan is at peace. At the stadium, Sadigua tells children a story that includes the Traveler's deeds, and the Traveler and Paimon leave without interrupting. When they ask about their next journey, Mavuika reports that Capitano had asked the Tsaritsa to abolish his mission to take the Pyro Gnosis, and the request was granted; she still fears another Harbinger may try, and warns that entering Snezhnaya will place the Traveler in direct conflict with the Fatui. She adds that the Doctor's subordinates have been studying fragments in the sky, said to be Secret Source technology relics called Ancient Moon's Remnants, and that his stronghold lies in Nod-Krai, a land with a power said to predate the elements.

## revelations
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
- The Traveler's Ancient Name is "Tumaini" (hope); this is the first time in Natlan's history that an outsider has received one. Its power is so great that it attracts souls from beyond Natlan.
- Capitano is Thrain, the "Sentinel Knight" of Khaenri'ah and one of Natlan's legendary heroes of five hundred years ago.
- With Khaenri'ahn technology, Capitano's heart was modified to convert memories and souls from the Ley Lines into knowledge; after the catastrophe five hundred years ago he gave up that capacity and used his heart as a vessel for the souls of his comrades and many Natlanese. By merging his existence with the Lord of the Night he means to become master of the Ley Lines and change the rules so they can go home.
- Capitano, like many Khaenri'ahns, regards himself as innocent of what the Five Sinners of Khaenri'ah did, and wants revenge on Ronova for the curse of immortality by turning it into a paradox: if he gives his life for the Lord of the Night she receives the death she demands, yet his immortality negates that death.
- Guthred says he is a story created by the Masters of the Night-Wind.
- Ronova is called the Ruler of Death in the act's dialogue, and the watching rift above Ochkanatlan is attributed by Citlali to her.
- Capitano had asked the Tsaritsa to cancel his mission to take the Pyro Gnosis, and she agreed.
### LARGE +
Mavuika's plan always involved her own death by the Sacred Flame; Citlali says she remains slated to die even after the war is won. The act shows her leaving the question of her own wishes unanswered when the Traveler asks. Her refusal of the void's doubts in the Gosoythoth fight is framed as her conviction that her fate is to end the war.

## characters
### STANDARD
Mavuika, the Pyro Archon, leads the plan. Xilonen forges the Ancient Name and Citlali interprets the souls it draws. Kachina, Mualani, Kinich and Chasca represent the tribes, and Ororon's ritual reveals Guthred. Capitano, a Fatui Harbinger and Khaenri'ahn, appears in a dream and then at Ochkanatlan. The dead helpers include Atea, Vichama, Mallko, Bona, Cocouik and Chuychu. Gosoythoth is the final enemy; Ronova and Yohualtecuhtin, the Lord of the Night, appear at the end.

## consequences
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Faced with the paradox, Ronova keeps the curse of immortality and relinquishes her demand for a death, telling Capitano to use it to fulfil his final wish. He sits on the Throne of the Primal Fire with the souls he carried, guiding them back to the Night Kingdom and, as Citlali puts it, granting the Lord of the Night eternal life by fusing his existence with hers. The Lord of the Night, grateful for the chance to live forever, apologises for having once thought so little of how long a life lasts, and returns to the Night Kingdom. Mavuika accepts that Natlan still needs a leader and lives; the five present pay respects to Capitano. Natlan is at peace, the Traveler is a celebrated hero, and Mavuika's briefing sends the Traveler toward Snezhnaya and Nod-Krai (the Song of the Welkin Moon chapter, beginning with the prelude "The Journey Home").
### LARGE +
Ronova had already agreed to accept Mavuika's sacrifice for the Lord of the Night as her death, concluding her deal with Xbalanque from several thousand years ago. Capitano's challenge was to forsake one of her rules or let him trigger a paradox that could uproot the natural order; the cutscene narration says she relinquished the price of death to sustain the curse. The Lord of the Night explains that Natlan's Ley Lines could not connect to other nations, which is why Capitano's earlier attempts elsewhere failed and why he came to Natlan.
