## summary
@spoiler: minor
### LITE
Beyond the Smoke and Mirrors is the third act of Chapter V, the Natlan Archon Quest (version 5.1). Xilonen is asked to forge an Ancient Name for the Traveler, an outsider, and the search for help leads to the shaman Citlali and her missing tribesman Ororon, who turns out to be working with the Fatui Harbinger Capitano. The act ends as the Abyss attacks the whole nation and the Traveler learns of a dangerous backup plan.
### STANDARD +
The act has three quests: All Names are Noble, To Whence the Smoke Drifts and The Flickering Light Splits in Twain. It requires Adventure Rank 40. It follows Black Stone Under a White Stone and adds the Masters of the Night-Wind to the story's central cast.
### LARGE +
Structurally the act pairs a gentle, comic search for an Ancient Name with a secret meeting in which the Traveler is offered an alliance by an opponent, then turns to a nationwide attack and a flashback through Ororon's memory that explains what the Fatui are doing in Natlan.

## plot
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Xilonen is asked to forge an Ancient Name for the Traveler, which should be impossible for an outlander, so they seek Citlali and help clear her tribesman Ororon, who has been in Fatui hands. Capitano secretly meets the Traveler, argues that Mavuika's plan may fail and that a costlier plan exists, and the Traveler later learns from Mavuika that the backup plan would erase the Night Kingdom's contents. When the Abyss attacks all of Natlan, Ororon's memories show Capitano wants to rebuild Natlan's ley lines.
### LARGE
**All Names are Noble.** Two days after the previous act, the Adventurers' Guild and the Scions of the Canopy have been ordered by the Pyro Archon to share information and tackle Abyssal threats together, and Kachina has volunteered as a stationed warrior. She takes the Traveler and Paimon to Xilonen's workshop in the Children of Echoes. Xilonen had been told only that the Ancient Name was for a traveler and would be "quite difficult"; she is surprised to learn it is for an outlander and explains it should be impossible, since the Night Kingdom records only the deeds of Natlanese in Natlan. She chooses to treat it as a test of her skill. The first step is to be acknowledged by the Wayob, so they decide to consult Citlali, an expert on the Night Kingdom, though the Spiritspeaker Stone was broken in the rescue. Kinich agrees to deliver Xilonen's letter and tells them the person who rescued Capitano was Ororon of the Masters of the Night-Wind. After a wait, Citlali arrives and agrees to help if they help prove Ororon's innocence. She examines the Traveler's past, dispels residue of souls clinging to them from the Night Kingdom, and explains that the decision ultimately lies with the Lord of the Night, a Night Kingdom entity that uses the Sacred Flame's power against the Abyss. It is asleep, so they must enter its dream by trance.

**To Whence the Smoke Drifts.** Following Ororon's phlogiston trail from his last known location, Citlali tells them he is an orphan raised by the tribe. Her spell lets the Traveler and Paimon hear her private thoughts. When the party splits, the Traveler and Paimon are pulled into the Night Kingdom, where Ororon appears and Capitano speaks with the Traveler, explaining his goals and mistrust of Mavuika. He asks to meet again east of the Stadium and asks them to tell no one. Citlali finds and frees Ororon from a Fatui camp, though he is made to labour there. She asks why he saved Capitano; he says that otherwise they would have gone for her. The Traveler later tells Paimon, but not Citlali, about the meeting and meets Capitano again with Ororon. Capitano argues that Mavuika's plan may fail and that there is another plan that would cost dearly; he vanishes mid-sentence as an angry Citlali, suspicious since they were separated, arrives and discovers the meeting. At the Weary Inn Citlali becomes drunk, learns the Traveler heard her thoughts, and in private reveals her insecurity about being called old and that Ororon has an incomplete soul, which his tribe treated as a bad omen and tried to use as a vessel for Night Kingdom souls. The Traveler decides to ask Mavuika about the backup plan.

**The Flickering Light Splits in Twain.** The Abyss launches a nation-wide attack on the Masters of the Night-Wind, the Children of Echoes, the Collective of Plenty and many outposts. The Traveler goes to Mavuika, who confirms that the backup plan exists but would erase everything from the Night Kingdom, including history and culture, and might still fail without ley line repairs. She asks them to keep it secret and says she will join the defence. The Traveler helps rescue a caravan, while Chasca and Chuychu infiltrate a Fatui camp and learn the Fatui seek the "Source Mechanism". Citlali reports that Ororon has returned to defend the tribe; the replacement soul-stabilising gem she had Ifa deliver to him carried a spell that copied his memories into the old gem. His memories show Capitano's visit to Ororon, Tarko's suspicion, and Ororon's rescue of the wounded Capitano, and Capitano states that the only way to save Natlan is to reconstruct its ley lines. The group decides to inform Mavuika at once.
### ULTRA +
Further details. In All Names are Noble, The Traveler wonders whether all six Wayob would have to acknowledge them. Citlali reveals she arrived late because she had examined Ororon's home, where his Phlogiston Aphids were off and there were marks from Snezhnayan weapons. She is unsure why the Fatui would take interest in a young man in his twenties. Citlali was also unsure how to approach the Traveler without awkwardness; Xilonen apologises for not warning them that Citlali looks much younger than her title suggests.

In To Whence the Smoke Drifts, Capitano says he heard about the Traveler from Childe (Tartaglia) and wants them as an ally rather than an enemy. Paimon suspects he wants the Gnosis for the Tsaritsa, and the Traveler realises the backup plan involves the Gnosis; before Capitano can say more he disappears. Ororon later tricks the group with a powder that causes an illusion and says he acted in Natlan's best interests. A Fatuus named Kosterlitzky orders Ororon back to work in the camp. Citlali and the Traveler report to Coyopa, who tells Ororon to return once the Archon decides.

In the memory sequence, a local tribesman first warns Ororon about the Fatui, and Capitano later visits and asks why he lives alone and whether he knows Citlali. Ororon follows the Fatui to a camp, where Tarko spots him; Tarko becomes dizzy as souls from the Night Kingdom escape, and Capitano dispels them. Days later, when Mavuika wounds Capitano at the Stadium, Ororon senses a familiar soul trying to escape, realises the lead would be lost if Capitano died, and intervenes. Capitano admits he was looking into the Night Kingdom. In a second camp, Capitano says that rather than a net to stop the damage, the nation's ley lines must be rebuilt. Citlali is angry that Ororon cannot let go of the past, while the group notes that rebuilding like the Loom of Fate would affect everyone's memories.

## revelations
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
- An Ancient Name cannot normally be forged for an outlander, because the Night Kingdom records only the deeds of Natlanese done in Natlan.
- The Lord of the Night is an entity in the Night Kingdom that uses the Sacred Flame's power to repel the Abyss; it is asleep and can be reached only through its dream.
- Ororon's memories show Capitano intends to rebuild Natlan's ley lines, an action that would alter everyone's memories.
- Mavuika has a backup plan that would erase the Night Kingdom's contents, including history and culture; she asks the Traveler to keep it secret.
- Ororon has an incomplete soul and relies on a gem to stabilise it; his tribe once tried to use him as a vessel for Night Kingdom souls.
### LARGE +
- Chasca and Chuychu's reconnaissance finds the Fatui defending civilians during the nationwide attack and searching for the "Source Mechanism"; their purpose is unclear.
- The Abyss attack hit the Masters of the Night-Wind, the Children of Echoes and the Collective of Plenty at once.

## characters
### STANDARD
The Traveler and Paimon, Katheryne, Kachina, Xilonen, Kinich, Citlali, Ororon, Capitano, Mavuika, Mualani, Chasca, Chuychu, Iansan and Ifa; also Piltzin, Chanca, Coyopa, Tarko and Kosterlitzky.

## consequences
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The Traveler now knows of both Capitano's proposal and Mavuika's backup plan. Ororon is brought home, though his standing with the tribe is unresolved. The group is left with the task of telling Mavuika what Capitano intends for the ley lines.
### LARGE +
Xilonen's Ancient Name for the Traveler remains unfinished: the Traveler must be acknowledged by the Wayob, and Citlali says the decision lies with the Lord of the Night. The Traveler kept the Capitano meetings from Citlali, who discovered the second one by following them. The story continues in Act IV, The Rainbow Destined to Burn.

## placement
### STANDARD
Chapter V, Act III. It follows Act II, Black Stone Under a White Stone, and precedes Act IV, The Rainbow Destined to Burn. Released in 5.1.
