## summary
@spoiler: none
### LITE
Hangout Event: Shikanoin Heizou is a branching, player-choice side story added in version 2.8 (Act I, "Trap 'Em by Storm"). Ryuuji of the Bantan Sango Detective Agency commissions the Traveler to find the missing Tenryou Commission detective Shikanoin Heizou, who is actually investigating a feeling that something is missing on Watatsumi Island. Depending on choices, the Traveler follows him through a trade-smuggling case, a staged anti-fraud play with Arataki Itto, or the old "Ryuuji Case" murder. Five endings are possible.
### STANDARD +
The event needs two Story Keys and first requires the Archon Quest "Omnipresence Over Mortals", the story quest "Transient Dreams" and the story quest "Rise Up, Golden Soul". The branches are alternate paths, not one timeline. Heizou is shown as talkative and theatrical in public and more thoughtful and uncertain in private, with an intuition he trusts more than evidence.
### LARGE +
The recurring themes are intuition against evidence, the limits of the law, and whether protecting someone from a painful truth is a kindness. Heizou treats the Traveler as a partner and, in the last branch, as the person he trusts to judge him.

## plot
### LITE
At the detective agency, Ryuuji and the cynical Sango explain that Heizou has gone missing. The Traveler finds him on Watatsumi Island pretending to be captured by Nobushi. He says he took the mission because a recurring dream told him "something vital is missing on this island". Whether he returns with the Traveler to the Police Station or stays to investigate depends on the Traveler's choices.
### STANDARD
If the Traveler agrees to investigate, the trade-smuggling case ("The Missing Thing") ends in "Memories Do Not Evaporate". Otherwise, Heizou goes back to the Police Station ("Report back"), where Kujou Sara makes the Traveler his temporary supervisor. There the Traveler picks a case file: the anti-fraud play "Heizou Talks Fraud" (endings "Free Onikabuto Lunch" and "The Three Undeceivables"), or one of the other two files, which sends the story to Sango's complaint letter and the "Long-Sealed Mystery" about the Ryuuji Case (endings "There's One Secret" and "The Famous Detective's Principles").
### LARGE +
Branch "The Missing Thing" - Heizou and the Traveler find Sango Pearls and Crystal Marrow in the Nobushi camp. They ask Shibata at Sangonomiya Shrine, then Gorou, who admits the caravans were robbed and asks them to investigate the Ritou merchant Godot, because Watatsumi cannot easily investigate on Ritou. Heizou privately suspects Gorou of lying and follows the caravan leader Todoroki to Bourou Village, where wounded veterans (Tokuda and Masashi, who knew the Traveler as "Captain of Swordfish II") report seizing goods from bandits. Crates of medicinal herbs without tax clearance, with a torn bill naming a "Foreign Trade Association" Heizou has never heard of at Ritou, suggest smuggling. Captured thieves say pirates led by a woman with an eye patch took an earlier cargo, and Heizou concludes Todoroki is making a secret deal with pirates, with Gorou covering for him. The next morning at Sangonomiya Shrine, Heizou confronts Todoroki, who orders the veterans to arrest them until Gorou intervenes and admits misleading them. Todoroki explains the island cannot grow food or herbs, that buying from Ritou is heavily taxed, and that both his sons died, one of them for lack of medicine. Gorou says a new commodity tax bill would solve it. Heizou offers to stay on Watatsumi Island, as a reassurance, until the bill passes, and has Gorou promise not to lie to him or the Traveler again. His private conclusion is that what the island "lacked" was only apparent: he sensed the islanders' vitality. Ending: "Memories Do Not Evaporate".

Branch "Report back" - At the Police Station, Uesugi and Yoriki Owada greet Heizou; Sara scolds him for missing work and assigns the Traveler as his supervisor for the day. Heizou finds three case files: an approval for his anti-fraud event, "The Case of the Missing Eastern Express" (a lost dog) and "The Prince of Mischief in Hanamizaka". He is also served a complaint letter from Sango. Choosing the anti-fraud file starts "Heizou Talks Fraud"; choosing either of the others sends Heizou and the Traveler to Sango's detective agency about her complaint.

Branch "Heizou Talks Fraud" - Heizou needs two "scam artists" and one "mark" for a short play. He recruits Etsu (a former fraud convict doing community service at the Grand Narukami Shrine) and the elderly Mr. Iba (also caught in a fraud case; he agrees when Heizou mentions his granddaughter would attend) and, on Paimon's suggestion, Itto as the mark, bribing him with a photographed Emperor Onikabuto. In the play Itto plays "Doushin Arataki", who helps Etsu after her grandfather (Iba) loses money to notes posing as the Raiden Shogun, Ms. Hina and Itto himself. Etsu then begs for 500,000 Mora, offering a share of 10,000 Onikabuto; a prop cheque appears. Itto is tempted but asks whether a Doushin really earns so much, and the Traveler is asked to help him. If the Traveler helps, Itto spots that Etsu and Iba are the scammers ("Free Onikabuto Lunch"); otherwise he follows his heart and decides the cheque-carrying Doushin must be the criminal and refuses to hand it over ("The Three Undeceivables"). Heizou closes by explaining that scams cast wide nets. His promised Emperor Onikabuto prize for Itto turns out to be a non-moving figurine: in the Undeceivables branch he admits he was fooled by false advertising and Itto passes the figurine to the Traveler as a reminder.

Branch "Long-Sealed Mystery" - Sango demands that the Ryuuji Case be reopened. Years earlier, the head of the Police Station, Takatsukasa Isamu, died of poison. Ryuuji, his assistant, was arrested, and Sango, hired by the Takatsukasa Clan, identified the forensic doctor Shiroyama as the real killer. Shiroyama died by suicide before he could testify. Both convictions were signed by Kujou Sara. The Traveler reviews the case file with Heizou, identifies the motive (Isamu blackmailed Shiroyama with his pregnant wife) and the means (a toxin dissolved in the cold medicine rather than the fish-liver paste, as the autopsy suggests). Heizou then shows a torn research-log page: the poison works only when a particular cold medicine and the expensive fish-liver paste are combined, both harmless alone. Heizou says he found it in Kujou Takayuki's warehouse, that Shiroyama had worked for the Kujou Clan, and that Ryuuji only delivered the paste. He hid the truth to protect Ryuuji and asks the Traveler to decide whether to reveal it. Telling Sango and Ryuuji only that Shiroyama was guilty leads to "There's One Secret"; telling everything leads to "The Famous Detective's Principles", where Ryuuji is shaken but recovers the next day and Sango admits she now understands why Heizou left.
### ULTRA +
Further detail:

- Opening: Ryuuji, Sango's assistant, describes Heizou as lively and accurate at spotting bad actors, almost as if relying on a sixth sense. Sango, who dislikes Heizou, calls him an obstacle in her path. Ryuuji later says Heizou was once a detective at the agency before leaving. Ryuuji says Sango forbids him from acting friendly with Heizou in public.
- On Watatsumi Island Heizou explains that during the war Kujou Takayuki, then head of the Tenryou Commission, secretly sent him to investigate Watatsumi Island, and that he treated the trip as a paid vacation; he says Sangonomiya's soldiers have been watching him since. He teases Paimon about being a "Voice", meaning an external voice for the Traveler.
- "Complaint Letter Branch": Sango's complaint accuses Heizou of abusing his power to conceal the truth of the Ryuuji Case. Heizou says the case is solved and he has disclosed what he could.
- In "Heizou Talks Fraud", Heizou's cousin Kano Nana calls him a "brat" and tells the Traveler to discipline him; Etsu says the community-service deal he arranged was not the reduction she hoped for. Iba says Heizou knows how to get information from elders by being polite. Iba says criminals have a nickname for Heizou that makes him sound like a force of nature.
- In the Long-Sealed Mystery review, the clues include the autopsy report (Isamu was 41), Ryuuji's testimony, Shiroyama's testament, Isamu's threatening letter, the two convictions, the toxicology report (the toxin dissolves only in water and was not on any guild ledgers) and a blank page of the research log. The Traveler can raise doubts on points such as the International Trade Association's records. Heizou says Sara changed Ryuuji's sentence before signing it, from death to temporary incarceration pending trial, because the case had no confession or complete evidence.
- Ryuuji tells Chika, Isamu's young daughter, he is her "brother" because Isamu treated him like a son. The ending quote, "Some things will always be more important than the truth", is Heizou's principle.
- Rewards: Adventure EXP plus Primogems or enhancement materials depending on the ending; a furnishing, the Fabled Emperor Onikabuto, is awarded in the anti-fraud branch.

## revelations
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
- Heizou is a special detective of the Tenryou Commission (rank: Doushin) and cousin of the shrine maiden Kano Nana. Sango dislikes him; Ryuuji admires him.
- Watatsumi Island runs caravans to bring in food and medicinal herbs that its poor soil cannot grow; some of the goods are untaxed, so Gorou hid the truth from Heizou until the Traveler's presence made him reconsider. The Kanjou Commission is drafting a commodity tax bill that exempts needed goods.
- In the Ryuuji Case, Takatsukasa Isamu used Shiroyama's family as leverage; Shiroyama killed Isamu with a two-part poison that Ryuuji unwittingly helped deliver. Heizou's private conclusion is that Kujou Takayuki ordered it and planned to blame Ryuuji.
### LARGE +
- Sara changed Ryuuji's conviction from death to temporary incarceration. Rumours blame her or the Kujou Clan; Heizou argues she acted from principle.
- Heizou has never heard of a "Foreign Trade Association" at Ritou and suspects smuggling.

## characters
### STANDARD
Heizou is the lead; the Traveler and Paimon are his partner and supervisor. Ryuuji and Sango (Bantan Sango Detective Agency) open and close the main branches. Gorou, Todoroki, Tokuda, Masashi and Shibata appear on Watatsumi Island. Kujou Sara, Uesugi and Owada appear at the Police Station. Kano Nana, Etsu, Iba and Arataki Itto appear in the anti-fraud branch; Takatsukasa Chika, Isamu's daughter, in the Long-Sealed Mystery ending.

## consequences
### STANDARD
The event does not change the main story. It shows Heizou's dual nature and adds background to the Kujou Clan, the Police Station and Watatsumi Island's trade difficulties. Whether the Ryuuji Case truth is revealed is a branch choice.

## placement
### STANDARD
Released in version 2.8. It is set after the Watatsumi war and "Omnipresence Over Mortals", with the Sakoku Decree already ended. No content from 6.x to 7.1 was found that revises the event.

## entry: Ryuuji Case
@keys: Ryuuji Case, Takatsukasa Isamu, Shiroyama poisoning
@keys2: Heizou, Sango, Ryuuji
@version: 2.8
@availability: permanent
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Years before the story, the head of the Police Station, Takatsukasa Isamu, died of poison. Ryuuji, his assistant, who regarded him as a foster father, was convicted within a week on a conviction signed by Kujou Sara and jailed. Sango's investigation then identified the forensic doctor Shiroyama, who died by suicide before he could be tried. Heizou's private reading: a harmless-looking cold medicine and an expensive fish-liver paste combined into a lethal poison; Shiroyama had once worked for the Kujou Clan, and Kujou Takayuki directed it. Ryuuji, who delivered the paste, does not know this. Sango believes Heizou is hiding something. Whether the truth is revealed depends on the player's choice.

## entry: Watatsumi Island Trade Case
@keys: Todoroki caravan, Watatsumi caravans, commodity tax bill
@keys2: Heizou, Gorou
@version: 2.8
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
Watatsumi Island cannot grow enough food or medicinal herbs. To ease this Kokomi set up caravans under the farmer Todoroki, but Ritou's taxes are heavy. Nobushi and Treasure Hoarders prey on the caravans, and Todoroki also appears to buy scarce goods, including medicinal herbs, outside legal channels; a torn bill bears the name Foreign Trade Association, which Heizou does not recognise. Gorou concealed the arrangement from Heizou until the Traveler's presence and Heizou's offer to stay as a safeguard. A new commodity tax bill from the Kanjou Commission would exempt needed goods.

## entry: Heizou Talks Fraud
@keys: Heizou Talks Fraud, anti-fraud play
@keys2: Heizou
@version: 2.8
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
Heizou's public-awareness play about scams. Etsu and Iba, both former fraud convicts, play the scammers; Arataki Itto plays a Doushin who must resist offers that are too good to be true. In the end Heizou's lesson is "Don't get greedy and you won't be needy; stay alert and you won't get hurt." The Tenryou Commission approved the project.
