## summary
### LITE
Hangout Event: Diona is a replayable, choice-driven story for Diona, the bartender of The Cat's Tail, released in Version 1.5, with one act, The Cat and the Cocktail. The Traveler visits The Cat's Tail, where Diona is out of ingredients and exasperated with her drunken customers, and joins her either in finding the bar's missing cats or in hunting for ingredients for a new special cocktail. It is a comedy with no effect on the main story.
### STANDARD +
It requires 2 Story Keys, Adventure Rank 26 and the Archon Quest Song of the Dragon and Freedom. There are five endings. Permanent, repeatable content.
### LARGE +
The act shows two sides of Diona: her dislike of drunkenness and of cats (she insists she does not understand why people like them), and the pride she takes as a bartender. It also gives a glimpse of her relationship with her father, Draff.
### ULTRA +
Its quest list is The Cat's Tail's Bartender, To Catch a Kitten, A Special Base Drink, The Shadow Over Dadaupa, Samachurl Herbal Soup, Special Cocktailing Condiments and Exchanging Pointers.

## plot
### LITE
At The Cat's Tail, Nimrod praises the bar's cocktails and its cats. Diona complains to Margaret that she is out of ingredients again and does not understand why adults drink so much. The Traveler chooses to come for the cats, or to help with her new cocktail.
### STANDARD
Cats branch: the Traveler helps Diona find three cats (Roger, Nelson and Paisley) around Mondstadt, using a cat toy for one and cat food for another. Depending on later choices the story ends either with the cats behaving unpredictably (The Unfathomable Felines) or with the cats coming home and The Cat's Tail starting a cat party (Cat Party).
Cocktail branch: Diona decides to use the herbal soup that samachurls make in Dadaupa Gorge as a base for a new cocktail. A hunter told her the soup smells overwhelming. She and the Traveler go to the gorge and meet Allan, a hunter who works with her father.
### LARGE +
In Dadaupa Gorge, Diona's father Draff has wandered off drunk, believing he is hunting hilihounds and hilihogs. Which ending follows depends on how the Traveler handles it:
- Diona brews a concoction from the soup to sober Draff up, keeps the rest, and the day ends with her special cocktail back at The Cat's Tail (Diona's Special).
- The Traveler instead mixes the potent soup under Diona's guidance, and a whiff of it makes Draff retch. Diona remarks that it is the best ingredient for the worst possible drink and doubts it can be used in a cocktail ("I... Only Had... a Little...", whose tagline is that wine is fine in moderation).
Another route, Special Cocktailing Condiments, has Diona collecting fresh fish in Springvale and slime condensate near Dawn Winery for her cocktail, where she meets Connor and Elzer. In Exchanging Pointers she and Connor trade mixing tips and each speeds up their mixing in turn. The ending line has Diona telling Connor and Elzer to stop joking around (The Ultimate Special).
### ULTRA +
Draff, once sober, forgets what he did and promises to drink less. Diona says she has seen worse and lists earlier episodes: chasing dogs and calling them hilihounds, filling bottles from the town's spring water and drinking from them, and asking Myweiss to write a song about a hunter and his loyal hilihound. When Draff is sobered up she mutters a quiet thanks, then tells him not to drink so much. Allan was about to report to the Knights of Favonius if they had taken longer.

## revelations
### STANDARD
Little lore is revealed. The act confirms that Diona thinks of herself as a professional bartender and is annoyed by adults who cannot control their drinking, that her father Draff drinks heavily and takes part in hunts with Allan, and that The Cat's Tail is a popular bar with resident cats.
### LARGE +
Diona refers to the Kätzlein bloodline when a customer calls her a kitty, and her affection for her father shows through her irritation. The hangout does not explain why she dislikes cats.

## characters
### LITE
Diona and the Traveler are the main characters.
### STANDARD +
Supporting characters include Margaret and Nimrod at The Cat's Tail, Draff and Allan in Dadaupa Gorge, the Dawn Winery's Connor and Elzer, and the cats Roger, Nelson and Paisley.

## consequences
### STANDARD
No lasting effect on the main story. Rewards are given per ending (Adventure EXP, Primogems, Hero's Wit, talent or ascension materials, Diona's special dish), and endings can be shown as Lingering Moments in the Serenitea Pot.
### LARGE +
The wiki notes that Jackie Lastra temporarily voiced Diona in the Hangout Event until Version 2.4, when Dina Sherman returned to the role.

## placement
### STANDARD
Released in Version 1.5. It unlocks after Song of the Dragon and Freedom but sits outside the main timeline, with no dated events.
### LARGE +
Like other Hangout Events, it is a standalone day that does not alter the world.

### STANDARD
In Diona's Hangout Event she works the bar at The Cat's Tail, which is known for specialty drinks and its cats. She refuses to compromise on her recipes: any drink made by her hands, she says, can only be a good one. Customers love the cocktails, which is why she is often out of ingredients.

### STANDARD
A strong-smelling soup made by samachurls in Dadaupa Gorge. Diona hears of it from an adventurer and wants it as a base for a cocktail. In the hangout it proves powerful enough to sober up Draff immediately, but Diona decides it is probably unusable in a drink.
