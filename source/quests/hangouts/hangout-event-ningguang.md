## summary
@spoiler: minor
### LITE
Ningguang's Hangout Event, a replayable, choice-driven side story set after the Liyue Harbor crisis. Short of staff because her three secretaries are running an auction, Ningguang asks the Traveler to cover as her assistant for a day. The Traveler either schedules a serious working day, or a day off, and the event ends in one of five outcomes: an auction that reclaims her lost treasures, a lunch-and-informants day, a chess-piece commission, a fishing-and-treasure outing, or a trip to Northland Bank.
### STANDARD +
It was added in Version 2.4 and requires two Story Keys, Adventure Rank 28 and completion of The Crane Returns on the Wind (Liyue's Archon Quest conclusion). It holds eight quests: The Jade Chamber Once More (hub), Time Off!, Endless Smoke, Starting From Zero, Liyue Millennial, Business as Usual, Auction Games and Exchanging Intelligence. It shows the private person behind her polished public image: her humble origins, her grief for the original Jade Chamber, and the care she takes of the children she uses as informants.
### LARGE +
Hangout Events are replayable permanent content. Each ending is a Memory that can be shown in the Serenitea Pot. The tone leans on dry wit: Ningguang treats every outing as a business case, yet the closing beats of each route reveal her loneliness, her fear of being seen only as a profit-seeker and her wish to leave a legacy.

## plot
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
In The Jade Chamber Once More the Traveler arrives at the Jade Chamber to find Ningguang and her secretaries Baishi, Baiwen and Baixiao in need of a stand-in. The secretaries must run an important auction the next day, leaving Ningguang's day-to-day work without cover; she wants someone trusted who has no conflict of interest. Baishi briefs the Traveler: 133 unscheduled items, 17 of them high priority, including meetings with the Eight Trades, tax revisions, Keqing on land-conveyance deals and the guilds. The Traveler then drafts a schedule.

**Rigorous schedule.** Choosing a serious, well-ordered day leads to Business as Usual. The next morning the Traveler wakes Ningguang, has breakfast with her, collects her tailored clothes from Lanyang, and attends a lunch banquet at Liuli Pavilion with a businesswoman, Luo Qiao, who asks for advice on getting into the Luminescent Spine trade. Ningguang advises against it, saying the price far exceeds likely profit and the hype may be engineered. After the banquet the Traveler chooses between telling Ningguang her secretaries will be fine (Exchanging Intelligence) or going to check on them (Auction Games).

**Simple schedule.** Choosing a single day off sends the Traveler to Time Off! Baishi worries but agrees, noting Ningguang rarely rests. Next morning Ningguang can spend the day on something she never has time for (Liyue Millennial) or something she never has the chance to do (Starting From Zero or Endless Smoke).
### LARGE +
**Liyue Millennial (ending: Unchanging Will).** Ningguang shows off her Liyue Millennial, a chess game of her own design with over thirty editions, and plans a new set based on the battle of Liyue Harbor against Osial and the Fatui. She wants a figurine of the Traveler, sold only to those who pre-order. The pair visit Ying'er at Scent of Spring for measurements, then Xingxi at Mingxing Jewelry to commission the piece. Ningguang says the set is meant to commemorate rather than profit. Back at the Jade Chamber she confesses she lied to Xingxi: sacrificing the Jade Chamber was not a calm calculation, and she grieved it like losing a lifelong companion. She says she is not always as strong as people think and would make the same choice again.

**Starting From Zero (ending: Fortunes Shared).** Ningguang wants to re-live her life before the Qixing. The first attempt, trying to sell unclaimed goods, fails because everyone defers to her rank, so they hunt treasure chests with the Traveler (Ningguang joins the party as a trial character), chase a Weasel Thief for Mora, and finally catch fish by hand and grill them on the shore. She talks about her mother, who disliked the idea of her starting a business, and her affection for fish. Ending: Fortunes Shared.

**Endless Smoke (ending: Feint and Riposte).** The pair visit Northland Bank, where Ekaterina is hostile and refers to the huge reparations Ningguang imposed after the Fatui's actions. Leaving, they sense someone watching, quietly study the crowd, and identify the follower (a blond man in Snezhnayan dress). Returning, they overhear Northland staff arranging a surveillance operation. Ningguang notes their reaction shows a guilty conscience, and vows to investigate by the book.

**Business as Usual to Auction Games (ending: The Weight of a Collection).** Ningguang hosts the auction herself and sells everyday objects from her own collection at huge prices to hand-picked bidders. She reveals that after the Jade Chamber fell many of her belongings were salvaged and passed through many hands; each bidder is in possession of one. She offers refunds in exchange for returns, since proving ownership through courts would take too long and she would not pay twice for her own property.

**Business as Usual to Exchanging Intelligence (ending: While the View Remains Good).** Ningguang collects a repaired comb from Mingxing Jewelry for one of her child informants. The informants (Zhizhi, Little Long and Nannan) report on boats in the harbour; Nannan falsely repeats a story about a Fontaine merchant buying Luminescent Spines because two men gave her a replacement comb. The men, Lao Ke and Big Xu, turn out to have gone into debt buying Luminescent Spines. Ningguang scolds them for using a child, but offers to buy their stock under a contract that recoups any premium. Nannan chooses for them to play hide-and-seek with the children. Ningguang then explains that she has taught the children observation and critical thinking, never business, and hopes her name will outlive her as a symbol rather than a person.

### ULTRA +
**Quest tree (for reference).** The Jade Chamber Once More forks on the schedule: the rigorous schedule leads to Business as Usual, then either Exchanging Intelligence or Auction Games; the one-day-off schedule leads to Time Off!, then Liyue Millennial, or a choice of Starting From Zero or Endless Smoke. Endless Smoke can be failed outright: talking to bystanders or naming the wrong follower costs hearts, and running out ends the event as the quarry flees.

**Rigorous schedule contents.** The Traveler's serious day plan is: get ready, lunch with Luo Qiao at Liuli Pavilion, meet three informants, a conference at Yuehai Pavilion in the evening, a light meal and any follow-up negotiation, and a return to the Jade Chamber. Baishi praises it as rigorous and well-structured.

**Banquet gossip.** At Liuli Pavilion Luo Qiao (textile businesswoman) repeats rumours that the Traveler is part of Ningguang's inner circle alongside the Crux's captain, which Ningguang waves off while praising the Traveler as a hero of Liyue.

## revelations
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Ningguang's Jade Chamber sacrifice hurt her badly. She grew up in a humble family, worked odd jobs, and associates fish with childhood. She distrusts the Fatui-linked Northland Bank and knows her reparations demand still sours relations. She regards Beidou as a business partner who supplies rare treasures but also costs her investment opportunities.
### LARGE +
Ningguang confirms she is willing to be seen as tight-fisted for strategy, that she values trust over profit in dealing with informants, and that her goal for the children is to leave them able to judge for themselves.

## characters
@spoiler: none
### STANDARD
Ningguang and the Traveler lead. Her secretaries Baishi (daily schedule and image), Baiwen and Baixiao (experienced tasks) appear in the hub. Others by route: Gou San'er and Bolai (Starting From Zero), Ekaterina and Andrei of Northland Bank (Endless Smoke), Hongru and Luo Qiao (banquet), Lanyang (tailored clothes), Ying'er of Scent of Spring and Xingxi of Mingxing Jewelry (chess figurine), Xiaofei, Yingfeng and Little Bin (auction bidders), the children Zhizhi, Little Long and Nannan, and the merchants Lao Ke and Big Xu.
### LARGE +
Zhizhi, Little Long and Nannan are young children who act as Ningguang's informants. The event stresses that she treats them protectively and that she is angry when others exploit them.

## consequences
@spoiler: none
### STANDARD
Each ending is a Memory with no effect on later quests. Rewards scale with the number of endings completed (Primogems, Adventure EXP, Hero's Wit, Guide to Prosperity, Prithiva Topaz Fragments and Qiankun Mora Meat).

## placement
@spoiler: none
### STANDARD
Release order: Version 2.4. Story order: after The Crane Returns on the Wind, the finale of the Liyue Archon Quest, since Ningguang refers openly to the Jade Chamber's sacrifice and the battle against Osial, and to Fatui reparations. It precedes any later Liyue material.

## entry: Liyue Millennial

### STANDARD
A strategy board game of Ningguang's own design, based on classical chess with different rules, pieces and boards. She has made over thirty editions, wants it to carry Liyue's culture, and plans a commemorative set of the battle for Liyue Harbor with limited-edition figurines.

## entry: The Jade Chamber Auction
@keys: Jade Chamber auction, Ningguang auction
@keys2: Ningguang, Jade Chamber
@version: 2.4
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
In Auction Games Ningguang sells ordinary items from her private collection to hand-picked bidders at inflated prices. She explains that she was not trying to profit: after the Jade Chamber fell, many of her belongings were salvaged and resold, and every bidder holds one. She offers to refund them in exchange for the return of her property.

## entry: Luminescent Spine Speculation
@keys: Luminescent Spine, Luminescent Spines
@keys2: Ningguang, Luo Qiao, Lao Ke
@version: 2.4
@spoiler: none
### STANDARD
A rumour that a Fontaine merchant will pay a premium for Luminescent Spines inflated their price. Ningguang warns Luo Qiao off, saying the current price is far above what the end product can earn, and later finds Lao Ke and Big Xu ruined by it.

## entry: Ningguang's Informants
@keys: Zhizhi, Little Long, Nannan
@keys2: Ningguang
@version: 2.4
@spoiler: none
### STANDARD
Three children whom Ningguang treats as helpers and friends. She teaches them to observe, analyse and think critically but never business. When two merchants coach Nannan to lie, Ningguang is angry, gives Nannan the repaired comb (a gift from her mother, who has left Liyue Harbor), and lets Nannan decide the punishment.

## entry: Northland Bank
@keys: Northland Bank
@keys2: Ningguang, Ekaterina
@version: 2.4
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
In Endless Smoke Ekaterina greets Ningguang coldly, citing the reparations Ningguang imposed on the Fatui after the Liyue Harbor battle, which Northland Bank paid. After a brief visit the staff order surveillance of Ningguang, which she reads as a sign of guilt. She warns the Traveler that the bank is more than a commercial organisation.
