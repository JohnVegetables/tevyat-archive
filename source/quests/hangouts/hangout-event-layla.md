## summary
### LITE
Layla's Hangout Event, a single-act, branching side story added in Version 3.6. The Traveler meets Layla, a worn-out Rtawahist student at the Akademiya who cannot finish her thesis and is secretly being helped by an unknown "blessing from the stars". Depending on the Traveler's choices they trace a legendary "Wisdom Seelie" through the House of Daena and Layla's old failures, help her prepare for her thesis defence, or take her out for a break. Some routes reveal that the helper is a second self who emerges when Layla faints from exhaustion. There are six endings.
### STANDARD +
The act is Ever Silent Stars. It requires Adventure Rank 40, two Story Keys and the Archon Quest Akasha Pulses, the Kalpa Flame Rises. It opens with Drowsy Evening Star and then branches into the Wisdom Seelie investigation (The Wisdom Seelie and its follow-ups) or a "change of pace" line (A Change of Pace, with The Helper and Fresh Air).
### LARGE +
The event is the only extended look at Layla's life as a student: her fear that she does not belong at the Akademiya, her lack of sleep, and her kindness. It is permanent content, not a limited-time event.

## plot
### STANDARD
At the Akademiya the Traveler meets Layla, who is rushing out of the House of Daena. She is behind on her thesis and has had little sleep for days. She explains that her homework and papers keep getting finished before their deadlines without her remembering, and she has been calling this a "blessing from the stars". She does not want to rely on it, but time is short and she hopes a rumoured Wisdom Seelie, who answers questions left in two astrology books, will help with the thesis. The Traveler can go with her to investigate the Seelie or suggest she rest.

In the Seelie investigation, the Seelie refuses to answer because it says she meant to copy its answer, and leaves a chain of riddles instead. The riddles lead to places from Layla's past failures. The Traveler can follow them to the end, which gives The Price to Pay and Its Returns, take a break to fight monsters, which gives Once Bitten, Twice Shy, or gather information from students, which leads to a night watch and That Which Remains Unspoken. In the "change of pace" line, the Traveler helps Layla borrow equipment, rehearse her thesis defence, or relax outdoors; these routes lead to A Matter of Course, The Stars' Expectations and A Moment of Respite.
### LARGE +
Opening: Layla is still shaking from days without sleep. She calls herself a Rtawahist student "for now", fears the thesis will fail, and mentions the Seelie. She also remarks that she has not seen Twirly-Whirly for a long time; the event does not explain what that is. The Traveler can say that her effort matters as much as her results, or that she should rest.

The Wisdom Seelie: Rabah, a reader at the House of Daena, explains how to leave a letter in one of two astrology books and wait for a red stamp to appear as a reply. Layla leaves her question and receives a reply that refuses to answer for her and leaves hints "on the road of wisdom" instead. In Taking the Opposite Route the pair question the librarian Katayoun, a student Hunayn who saw a black shadow fall into the closed library one night, and a reader named Latifah who describes the Seelie as very strong, metal-bodied, able to freeze water and call meteors. In Compiling the Clues Layla reasons that the Seelie is a Rtawahist researcher studying the same subject, and the pair wait through the night at an observation site. In the end, as Layla's stress peaks she faints, and a calmer second Layla wakes. She is willing to answer questions: she entered the House of Daena by accident one night when a book had been left inside, put a student to sleep, and later answered questions to help other students; the legends have grown from that. She says she and Layla are "two sides of the same coin", that Layla's emotions are unstable, and that Layla already has the key data for her thesis, which she will organise for her. She asks the Traveler not to tell Layla. When Layla wakes she suspects she sleepwalked; whether the Traveler keeps the promise or not, the ending is That Which Remains Unspoken.

Further Investigation and One Final Step: the next hint points to Layla's arrival road, where she accidentally stepped on an Avidya Leopard's tail and lost the talisman her mother made. The second site is where she once ruined borrowed equipment during an observation attempt, and the third is a crossroads where she saw Treasure Hoarders robbing a family and ran for help rather than intervene. The final stop is Vimara Village, where Mundhir, the man she once helped, returns the lost package with a letter from the Wisdom Seelie encouraging her and giving her the missing summary for her thesis. Layla suspects she might be the Seelie but is mostly grateful. The ending is The Price to Pay and Its Returns.

An Exasperating Prank: if the Traveler chooses to rest, Layla insists on helping when monsters attack and joins as a trial character; afterwards she is out of breath but says her will is strong and resolves not to repeat her regrets. The ending is Once Bitten, Twice Shy.

A Change of Pace: at Puspa Cafe, Layla's classmate Munirah complains that Layla always seems on the verge of a breakdown even though she never misses deadlines. Layla says she cannot tell her classmates that something else helps her. If the Traveler offers to face the problem head-on, The Helper follows: Katheryne introduces the astrology collector Azzam, who will lend his equipment if Layla explains her research on the triple-orbit shift. In Thesis Defense Practice Layla practises the defence with the Traveler. If she keeps her composure she answers every question and decides she may no longer need the "blessing", which ends in A Matter of Course. If her hidden stress reaches its limit in the middle of an answer, the second Layla steps in and the line ends in The Stars' Expectations. In The Remaining Problem the second Layla appears while talking to Azzam, writes notes for the real Layla, explains that she must keep her existence secret, and says Layla's condition may worsen if she learns of her, which also leads to The Stars' Expectations.

Fresh Air and One Hearty Meal Later: instead, the Traveler takes Layla outside, where she cooks fruit and fish over a fire as she did as a child, remembers the home she left when she passed the Akademiya's admissions exam, swims a race at a lake and shouts from a mountain top. She says she studies astrology because the stars are beautiful, and this is why she calls the helper the "blessing from the stars". This ends in A Moment of Respite.
### ULTRA +
Smaller details. The books used for Seelie letters are Theoretical Astrology and A Quick Reference Guide to Astrology Cases. Layla says the Seelie is said to carry dozens of books and several pieces of astrology equipment. A Matter of Course rewards a keepsake called The Arrayed Stars Mirrored. Layla's thesis topic is the triple-orbit shift phenomenon and her adviser has put related methods into practice at Rtawahist. In her first thesis defence she froze, and Layla's practice questions include a trap about a "Great Machine" she has not prepared for. In Drowsy Evening Star a stress value tracked by the game changes how Layla responds in later scenes, which is why the same choice can lead to different endings.

## revelations
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Layla's "blessing from the stars" is a second personality that takes over when she loses consciousness from anxiety and exhaustion. The Wisdom Seelie legend is a rumour that grew from its activity.
### LARGE +
The second Layla describes herself as the shape Layla would take if rested and healthy, relies on Layla's own knowledge, and fears she would cease to exist if Layla's condition worsened. She never signs her notes. She believes in Layla and wants her to finish the thesis herself.

## characters
### STANDARD
Layla is the lead, shy, apologetic and exhausted but determined. Her second self is poised and practical. Other characters include Rabah, Katayoun, Hunayn, Latifah, Mundhir, Munirah, Azzam and Katheryne; each appears in one or two branches only.

## consequences
### STANDARD
No branch changes Teyvat's main story. The endings settle whether Layla learns of her other self, whether she gains confidence or keeps leaning on it, and whether she rests.

## placement
### STANDARD
Released in Version 3.6, between Faruzan's and Kaveh's Hangout Events. It requires the Sumeru Archon Quest Akasha Pulses, the Kalpa Flame Rises and is set after Sumeru's main story. Describing Layla generally should not assume any single ending.

## entry: The Wisdom Seelie
@keys: Wisdom Seelie, blessing from the stars
@keys2: Layla, Rtawahist, House of Daena
@version: 3.6
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
A rumour in the House of Daena of a strong, metal-bodied Seelie that answers questions placed in astrology books. In Layla's Hangout Event the "Seelie" is revealed to be Layla's second persona. Students attribute abilities to it, such as freezing water and calling meteors, that are exaggerated tales of her night-time activity.

@keys: Azzam

### STANDARD
An astrology enthusiast and equipment collector whom Katheryne introduces. He lends his celestial equipment to Layla if she can explain her research to him.
