## summary
@spoiler: none
### LITE
Hangout Event: Thoma is the branching Hangout Event for Thoma, the Kamisato Clan's housekeeper, added in version 2.2 and unlocked after Omnipresence Over Mortals. Thoma wants to thank the Traveler for helping during the Vision Hunt Decree by spending a day off with them. Depending on the Traveler's choices, the day ends in one of six endings: a housekeeping class, a knitting lesson, a vigil with a grieving ninja dog, sweaters for stray animals, a trip to Ritou, or a quiet scheme to rescue the Yashiro Commission from a price-gouging merchant.
### STANDARD +
It is a single act, "A Housekeeper's Daily Chores", made of ten quests in a decision tree. Requirements are Adventure Rank 30, two Story Keys and completion of Omnipresence Over Mortals. The event shows Thoma at ease: a cook, seamstress and animal lover, but also a capable fixer who settles the Yashiro Commission's problems through contacts and does not care much about reputation. As a Hangout Event it is optional, and only one route is played per run.
### LARGE +
Two themes recur on every route. One is Thoma's calm attitude to the disdain of some Yashiro samurai: he calls it ordinary gossip, says he cares about the wellbeing of the Kamisato Clan, and says it is enough that those close to him know the real him. The other is his preference for sincere, simple bonds over transactional ones, which the stray animals, the ninken Inu Shoushou and the Traveler's gift of a Windwheel Aster each illustrate.

## plot
@spoiler: minor
### LITE
Thoma invites the Traveler to the Kamisato Estate for a day off. After overhearing two samurai mock him, the Traveler chooses either to let it pass or to look for a way to win him respect. Thoma then offers a hospitable day: tea at Komore Teahouse, the stray animals in Hanamizaka, or a stroll in Ritou. Each path ends differently.
### STANDARD
After overhearing the samurai Kazumasa and Furusawa grumble about him, Thoma shrugs it off. The Traveler's choices then pick one of six routes. Over tea at Komore Teahouse the Traveler suggests a housekeeping class (a full class, or a quiet knitting lesson). Among the strays in Hanamizaka, Thoma and the Traveler either keep a grieving Shuumatsuban ninken company or knit sweaters for the animals. In Ritou the two tour the town and swap gifts, Thoma giving a Lucky Omamori. If the Traveler objects to the gossip, Thoma instead quietly brings a price-gouging merchant to heel for the Yashiro Commission.
### LARGE
**Behind His Back.** At the Kamisato Estate, the Traveler overhears the samurai Kazumasa and Furusawa grumbling that men like Thoma are why the samurai spirit is declining, adding that he is from Mondstadt and wondering what Miss Kamisato sees in him. Thoma arrives yawning (the rain has kept him awake), realises what was said and brushes it off as the usual treatment of people who do not fit in. Agreeing with him leads to Host's Invitation; disagreeing sends the Traveler off to ask around the Yashiro Commission and leads to Unforeseen Circumstances.

**Host's Invitation.** Thoma, as host, asks whether the Traveler prefers staying in or going out. Staying in means Komore Teahouse. Going out offers the stray cats and dogs by a sakura tree in Hanamizaka, or a day in Ritou, where the two first met.

**Thanks for Your Hospitality!** At Komore Teahouse Thoma serves a home-cooked meal, insists his cooking is ordinary despite the odd hot pot game, and offers to mend loose threads on the Traveler's cape. The Traveler suggests a housekeeping class for the Yashiro Commission to win him respect. Thoma doubts it would change opinions of him but likes the idea of sharing life-maintenance skills. The Traveler then chooses to advertise widely or keep it inside the Commission.

**Start Spreadin' the News (Popular Class).** The Traveler gathers Lavender Melon, Fowl and Flour, and helps Thoma recruit through the shopkeeper Ogura Mio, the housewives Yuuko and Yuuri, and the elderly Nagano. Thoma presents it as open to all genders and ages with free trial lessons. On the day the room is full, including Kazumasa and Furusawa, brought by Yuuko. Thoma starts teaching and then notices the Traveler dozing.

**Small-Scale Changes (Alone Time).** Keeping it within the Commission, the Traveler asks Hirano and Furuta, the Kamisato Clan's butler. Furuta suggests a spring-clean of the Yashiro Commission as a demonstration, and Thoma, lent as a trial character, weeds and waters the grounds with the Traveler. Few people come to the class. Thoma is unbothered and teaches the Traveler to knit.

**Under the Same Shade.** Thoma takes the Traveler to strays he feeds whenever he visits Komore Teahouse. He says he has trained them not to be aggressive. He then mentions a Shuumatsuban ninja dog he has seen alone on the beach north of Konda Village, which bit him when he tried to pet it. The Traveler chooses to visit it or to give it time.

**Ninken by the Shore (The Sea Breeze Carries Memories).** Thoma and the Traveler find the dog, clear monsters nearby, and still cannot coax it to eat or leave. Kazuaki of the Shuumatsuban identifies it as Inu Shoushou and says its owner went out to sea on a dangerous mission and will not return. He hands over the owner's worn training kunai. With it, the dog is more affectionate, and Thoma and the Traveler resolve to remember the owner alongside it.

**Bits and Bobs for Cats and Dogs (New Clothes!).** Instead, the Traveler and Thoma photograph the strays so Thoma can estimate their sizes. He tells how he first came to feed them, knits sweaters overnight and dresses the animals with the Traveler. One sweater does not quite fit.

**A Trip for Two in Ritou (A Lingering Fragrance).** In Ritou Thoma shows the Traveler a Chuyu Trading Company branch, Sailing Breeze (a Mondstadt wine shop), the International Trade Association and a quiet lookout. He tells of his arrival from Mondstadt. After a merchant gives him sweets as repayment for a favour, the Traveler gives him a Windwheel Aster, which moves him far more. He gives the Traveler a Lucky Omamori in return.

**Unforeseen Circumstances (The Housekeeper's Other Side).** Asking staff about Thoma, the Traveler learns from Kiyoe that ceremonial implements for next month's festival sank in a storm and that the merchant Yuusaku is demanding extortionate prices. Yuusaku also holds a grudge against Thoma. Thoma meets him, fails to move him, and then quietly squeezes Yuusaku's trade and loan terms through his own contacts. A panicked Yuusaku arrives at the Commission offering to sell at half price. Thoma slips away to sweep the corridors.
**The Behind His Back exchange.** Thoma tells the Traveler he does not mind the gossip because it does no harm to the Kamisato Clan. If the Traveler objects, he says that as a housekeeper it is not his place to throw his weight around with other Yashiro staff, but invites serious suggestions.

**Cooking and housekeeping.** When the Traveler jokes about the hot pot game, Thoma says he added odd ingredients only for the game and cooks ordinary dishes normally. He names cleaning, cooking and mending as his strengths and argues that looking after yourself is needed to do your best at work. In class he argues housekeeping matters most in marriage, because otherwise one partner shoulders the other's burden.

**The Unforeseen Circumstances route.** Thoma takes the Traveler to Yasutake, the quartermaster of the Tenryou Commission, who is choosing Iron Ore suppliers. Thoma offers an assessment of the bidders (two veterans whose prices could be talked down to around 80 percent, a high-volume newcomer needing inspection, and a loan-funded latecomer he advises against). In return Yasutake asks for help with Iron Ore deliveries being stolen between Konda Village and Inazuma City, and the Traveler and Thoma escort a cargo balloon through three waves of monsters. Yasutake also mentions that Sakai had lent Yuusaku money. Thoma then secures some Noctilucous Jade for Sakai in exchange for Sakai pressing Yuusaku about his debt. Back at the Commission, Kazumasa and Furusawa, who were reluctantly preparing to beg, see Yuusaku apologise to Thoma.

**Ritou details.** At the Chuyu Trading Company, Qiuyue sells a Noctilucous Jade necklace and a Cor Lapis bracelet, and Thoma buys both after asking for a fair price for his friend from Liyue. At Sailing Breeze he says he never got to share a drink with his father at the Angel's Share and would love to meet its owner. The Ritou dialogue changes depending on whether the Traveler has completed Transient Dreams, the Raiden Shogun's story quest: before it, Ritou is still under the Sakoku Decree; afterwards the decree has been repealed and trade is recovering. Outside the International Trade Association, a merchant named Keiichi thanks Thoma with sweets from Yumeno Tennenan for a favour done through Kurisu.

**The animal story.** Thoma recalls first feeding the stray dog on a rainy day, after an obstinate Kamisato Clan elder refused to see him during a visit about the clan's new policies. He sat under a tree with the dog and shared a cold onigiri. He says powerful people expect him to read what they want, but with animals one onigiri makes friends for life.
### ULTRA +
**Requirements and rewards.** The event requires Adventure Rank 30, two Story Keys and completion of Omnipresence Over Mortals. Each ending gives Adventure EXP and a mix of Hero's Wit, Primogems, Warmth, Guide to Transience or Agnidus Agate Fragments, according to the wiki. A Trip for Two in Ritou also grants a Lucky Omamori. This is gameplay information, not lore.

**Gameplay notes.** Co-op is disabled during several quests. In Small-Scale Changes Thoma is lent as a trial character. Attacking the strays in Under the Same Shade ends the event. A wiki trivia note observes that the housekeeping classes appear to be followed up in a later Thoma story quest, Thoma: Training Exam.

**Branch map.** The choices produce six endings: Popular Class, Alone Time, The Sea Breeze Carries Memories, New Clothes!, A Lingering Fragrance and The Housekeeper's Other Side. The first two follow Thanks for Your Hospitality!, the next two follow Under the Same Shade, the fifth follows A Trip for Two in Ritou and the last follows disagreeing in Behind His Back.

## revelations
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
- Thoma grew up in Mondstadt. His father returned to Inazuma and stopped writing, so Thoma sailed to find him with Dandelion Wine as a gift. A storm sank the boat and he drifted to Inazuma on wreckage (Ritou route).
- Thoma's mother gave him the omamori his father had given her, just before he left Mondstadt (Ritou route).
- He feeds strays because simple bonds are what he values most. He describes himself to powerful people as an expendable servant boy who must always consider what they want.
### LARGE +
- Thoma quietly handles disputes involving merchants for the Yashiro Commission using personal contacts rather than authority, and is trusted by figures such as Yasutake and Sakai.
- The Yashiro Commissioner, Kamisato Ayato, has allowed Thoma to inquire into Shuumatsuban matters in exceptional cases, though not to command them.
- The Shuumatsuban never discloses the identities of ninja who die on missions and does not commemorate them publicly.
- When he first arrived in Inazuma Thoma would stand on the beach looking out at the horizon.

## characters
### STANDARD
Thoma (host) and the Traveler (guest). Also: the Yashiro Commission samurai Kazumasa and Furusawa, the shopkeeper Ogura Mio, the housewives Yuuko and Yuuri, the elder Nagano, the Kamisato servants Hirano, Furuta and Koharu, Kiyoe, Yuusaku, Yasutake, Sakai, Kazuaki of the Shuumatsuban and the ninken Inu Shoushou, Qiuyue, Kaede and Keiichi in Ritou, and Kurisu, mentioned in passing. Kamisato Ayato and Kamisato Ayaka are referred to but do not appear.

## consequences
@spoiler: none
### STANDARD
No lasting change to the main story. Depending on the route, Thoma holds a first housekeeping class, adds the Traveler to those who remember an unnamed Shuumatsuban ninja, clothes strays for winter, gives the Traveler a Lucky Omamori, or settles a festival supply dispute at Yuusaku's expense.

## placement
@spoiler: none
### STANDARD
Released in version 2.2 with the Hangout Event feature. It takes place after Omnipresence Over Mortals and refers back to the Vision Hunt Decree. Some Ritou dialogue differs if the Traveler has completed Transient Dreams. Nothing after version 7.1 is used.

## entry: Thoma's housekeeping class
@keys: housekeeping class, Housekeeping Course
@keys2: Thoma, Yashiro Commission
@version: 2.2
@spoiler: none
### STANDARD
In two routes of Thoma's Hangout Event, he and the Traveler organise a housekeeping class at the Yashiro Commission headquarters, open to any gender or age. In the widely advertised route (Popular Class) a full room attends, including Kazumasa and Furusawa, brought by Yuuko. In the quiet route (Alone Time) few attend and Thoma teaches the Traveler to knit instead. These are route-specific outcomes.

## entry: Inu Shoushou
@keys: Inu Shoushou
@keys2: ninken, Shuumatsuban, Thoma
@version: 2.2
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Inu Shoushou is a red Shiba ninken with a scar by one eye, in black stealth gear, who belonged to a Shuumatsuban ninja who left on a dangerous sea mission and did not return. It waits on a beach north of Konda Village and refuses food. Kazuaki gives Thoma the owner's blunt training kunai, and the dog becomes more affectionate when Thoma and the Traveler keep it company.
### LARGE +
The Shuumatsuban pays for the dog's upkeep. Thoma talks of building a kennel and making winter clothes for it. The owner is never named.

## entry: Thoma and the stray animals
@keys: stray cats and dogs, Hanamizaka strays
@keys2: Thoma, sweater
@version: 2.2
@spoiler: none
### STANDARD
Thoma feeds stray cats and dogs under a sakura tree in Hanamizaka whenever he visits Komore Teahouse. He says it began when a stray shared shelter with him on a rainy day after an elder turned him away. In the New Clothes! route he knits sweaters for them, naming Petal, Darko and Goldilocks.

## entry: Thoma's trip to Ritou
@keys: Thoma Ritou trip, Sailing Breeze
@keys2: Thoma, Ritou
@version: 2.2
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
On the Ritou route Thoma tours the town with the Traveler. He tells how he arrived from Mondstadt looking for his father, recalls the town's freer days, and praises the quiet lookout he visits every time. He buys a necklace and bracelet at the Chuyu Trading Company, accepts sweets from a grateful merchant, and is deeply touched by a Windwheel Aster from the Traveler, giving them a Lucky Omamori in return.

## entry: Yuusaku and the ceremonial implements
@keys: Yuusaku, ceremonial implements
@keys2: Thoma, Yashiro Commission
@version: 2.2
@spoiler: none
### STANDARD
Yuusaku is a merchant on Narukami Island who once sold the Yashiro Commission defective ceremonial goods, was exposed by Thoma, and was held by the Tenryou Commission. He bears a grudge and raised his prices when the Commission's festival shipment sank. In the Unforeseen Circumstances route Thoma privately cuts off Yuusaku's trade and loan terms until he apologises and offers to sell at half price.
