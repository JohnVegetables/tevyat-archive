## summary
### LITE
Kaveh's Hangout Event, a single-act, branching side story added in Version 3.7. The Traveler finds the architect Kaveh depressed in a tavern after the sixth rejected design for a vague, stingy client, and follows him through one of several routes: a children's library in the desert village of Aaru, a hunt for sponsors with the merchant Dori, a locked box of his mother's mementos, or a scam that exploits his former classmate's architecture training centre. There are five endings.
### STANDARD +
The act is The Pendulum of Weal and Woe. It requires Adventure Rank 40, two Story Keys, and completion of the Archon Quest Akasha Pulses, the Kalpa Flame Rises and the story quest The Illusions of the Mob. The first quest, The Sixth Proposal, leads to three branch quests: A Refuge in the Sands, Past Mementos and Treasure and True Interest.
### LARGE +
It is the main place where the Traveler sees Kaveh's private doubts about whether his art and his income can coexist, his past with his single mother, and how his friends, especially Alhaitham, Tighnari and Cyno, treat him. It is permanent content, not a limited-time event.

## plot
### STANDARD
In The Sixth Proposal Kaveh argues with a client, Badawi, who keeps asking for changes to a design in the desert. The Traveler meets him afterwards in a tavern, where Kaveh asks whether art is a divine gift or the product of one's own struggle and asks the Traveler to keep another secret. The Traveler then chooses what to do. Helping Kaveh convince the client leads to A Refuge in the Sands. Doing something else to clear his mind leads to Past Mementos. Elsewhere, when the talk turns to his money troubles, Kaveh remembers a former classmate who offers him a lucrative teaching job, which leads to Treasure and True Interest.

In A Refuge in the Sands the pair follow Badawi and learn that he is a former Eremite mercenary who has been disguising himself as a merchant because he feared being cheated; the building is a library for children in the desert, to be built at Aaru Village. The Traveler can propose a slightly different style, which sends the group to Aaru and ends in Blueprint for the Future, or a search for sponsors, which goes through Dori and ends in A Done Deal. In Past Mementos Kaveh digs out a box of his mother's old things and a locked notebook; the Traveler can look for her old acquaintances, ending in Leisurely Gathering after a dinner at Pardis Dhyai, or make a trip to places Kaveh loves, ending in Beneath the Stars. Treasure and True Interest ends in First Class.
### LARGE +
In the tavern Kaveh admits that none of his projects since graduation has passed on the first review, and that his client has asked for changes that would destroy the design, such as turning the building into a single tall trunk. He says arguing with clients is bad for his reputation and asks the Traveler to add it to the secrets already being kept for him. Later he says that Alhaitham, whom he has argued with for years over his ideals, remains unconvinced.

Blueprint for the Future: Badawi and Kaveh clear monsters on the road to Aaru Village, with Kaveh as a trial character. Badawi talks about the short, hard lives of mercenaries and his wish to build the library before he dies, so that desert children can learn their letters. Kaveh says some people call him an idealist and that he wants everyone to lead a happy life. At the village, with Mehrak's help and answers from a villager, Setaria, he surveys erosion and wind, and the cost-heavy parts of the plan become insulation and ventilation, so he agrees to a plainer style to save money while compensating with layout, lines and colour. The ending text stresses that present suffering is nothing next to giving hope to future children.

A Done Deal: Dori introduces three merchants, Tippu, Rubab and Qasimi, who refuse to fund the project once they hear that most land around Aaru Village has already been bought up. Dori then reveals that she herself will pay, since she already owns the land, and Kaveh signs a contract, realising that she was the buyer all along. In the closing scene he insists on ordering the best food and drink to recoup his commission fees.

Past Mementos: The box holds a drawing Kaveh made at Port Ormos before he was five, a copy of his mother's blueprint with the door taller than the roof, and the locked notebook. His mother has since moved to Fontaine; his father died when he was young and she raised him alone. Wondrous Times: Kaveh and the Traveler find Professor Zaha Hadi, who remembers Kaveh's mother Faranak as a quiet, gifted student with many admirers who argued with clients before she met his father. Zaha Hadi's account leads to the password, and Kaveh reads that his parents chose his name together, and sees a photograph of a gathering that includes people he knows. The notebook ends with a message from his mother about forming ties outside one's work. Tighnari's dinner the next evening gathers Alhaitham and Cyno to discuss Collei's studies, and Kaveh drinks heavily while complaining about clients. The First Ideal: Kaveh brings the Traveler to places he loves, including the House of Daena, where Alhaitham appears and teases him about his habit of scribbling annotations in library books, and to the Port Ormos expansion he designed early in his career. The two end at a favourite desert site, where Kaveh says he wants to build things that move others and that he will try to define art for himself.

Treasure and True Interest: Alkami offers Kaveh work as a lecturer at his architecture training centre at three hundred thousand Mora per lecture. Kaveh discovers that Alkami's students are being led into debt on false promises of wealth, confronts him, resigns, and with the Traveler and Mehrak catches the moneylender Fakhr. Cyno takes them to the matra, who close the centre and investigate. In First Class Kaveh offers to keep teaching the remaining students real design and takes them out to look at buildings and draw their own ideas.
### ULTRA +
Smaller details. In A Refuge in the Sands, Kaveh recounts being cheated himself by a man he tried to help in the desert, who borrowed Mora repeatedly and then hired mercenaries to ambush him. The merchants Dori introduces are all friends or clients of hers. Kaveh refers to himself as the most famous architect in Sumeru and is called the Light of Kshahrewar by Alkami. Alhaitham appears in The First Ideal reading Kaveh's marginal notes. Faranak's notebook mentions a man with long ears among the friends of her husband.

## revelations
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Kaveh is the son of a gifted architect, Faranak, who now lives in Fontaine; she left the locked notebook and her old things to him, and his father died when Kaveh was a child. Badawi, who commissions the library, used to be an Eremite mercenary. Dori already owned most of Aaru Village.
### LARGE +
Kaveh says he knows how miserable living in debt is. He values his profession above comfort and accepts that this makes him stubborn with clients.

## characters
### STANDARD
Kaveh is the lead; the Traveler and Mehrak accompany him. Badawi is the client and former mercenary. Dori is the merchant and creditor. Alkami is the exploitative former classmate. Zaha Hadi is a retired professor who taught his mother. Alhaitham, Tighnari and Cyno appear in the friendship-oriented routes, and Cyno in the training-centre case.

## consequences
### STANDARD
No branch changes Teyvat's main story. Each ending settles one thread for the player: a library design for Aaru Village, funding through Dori, a closer link between Kaveh and his friends, a reflection on his art, or students freed from a debt scam.

## placement
### STANDARD
Released in Version 3.7. It requires the Sumeru Archon Quest Akasha Pulses, the Kalpa Flame Rises and is set after Sumeru's main story. Describing Kaveh generally should not assume any single ending.

## entry: Badawi
@keys: Badawi
@version: 3.7
### STANDARD
Badawi is the client of Kaveh's sixth proposal. He is an older former Eremite mercenary who hid his identity from Kaveh because he feared swindlers. He wants an affordable library for the children of the desert, placed at Aaru Village. He is stingy because he is saving the Mora himself.

## entry: Alkami
@keys: Alkami
@version: 3.7
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
A Kshahrewar graduate and former senior classmate of Kaveh who runs an architecture training centre. He pushes students into loans with a lender named Fakhr and promises a wealth that architecture rarely gives. After Kaveh and the Traveler expose him, the matra close the centre and investigate.

## entry: Faranak's notebook
@keys: Faranak's notebook, Kaveh's mother's notebook
@version: 3.7
### STANDARD
A locked notebook that Faranak left behind when she moved to Fontaine. Kaveh could not open it as a child. In the Wondrous Times branch he reads her diary and a message addressed to him about forming connections beyond one's art.
