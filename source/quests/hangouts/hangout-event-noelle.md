## summary
### LITE
Noelle's Hangout Event is a two-act branching side story in which the Traveler spends time with Noelle, the Knights of Favonius' maid who dreams of becoming a knight. Act I (Chivalric Training) follows her small efforts to improve; Act II (Knightly Exam Prep) is about her anxiety over the Knights' selection exam. The Traveler's choices decide which of six endings each act reaches.
### STANDARD +
It is a Hangout Event, played with Story Keys. Each act is a tree of short quests: the Traveler's dialogue choices change what Noelle does, and some paths end in a failed hangout instead of an ending. The tone is warm and comic, with Noelle's earnestness, over-preparation and difficulty saying no driving most of the jokes. Nothing in it alters the main story.
### LARGE +
Across both acts the theme is the same: Noelle measures herself against more accomplished Knights (Jean, Kaeya, Amber) and worries she is not ready, and the Traveler helps her see that the small, caring work she already does is part of the kind of knight she wants to be.

## plot
### LITE
Act I: Noelle asks the Traveler for help with her "chivalric training". Act II: with the exam looming, she studies herself ragged until the Traveler steers her toward a trip to Liyue, a mock adventurer's exam, or practising knightly roles.
### STANDARD +
**Act I - Chivalric Training (summary-level).** Noelle asks the Traveler for guidance in the Knights' library. Paths lead to errands around Mondstadt and the merchant Alois, strength training, or a rest at Good Hunter and Beatrice's date problem, ending in a paper rose or a study of love books.

**Act II - Knightly Exam Prep.** After the Traveler returns from Liyue, Noelle is found cooped up in the library. She works for the Knights as a maid but must still pass the selection exam to become a knight, has failed it several times, and says it is about half a year away. She plans to sleep two hours less a day to study and nearly faints. The Traveler offers three routes: a change of scenery (Journey to Liyue), facing the stress by taking another exam (Adventurer's Entrance Exam), or imagining herself passing (Noelle Rides Out).


### LARGE +
**Act I - Chivalric Training (summary-level).** Noelle meets the Traveler in the library of the Knights of Favonius Headquarters and asks for guidance. The Traveler can say it is fine not to be a knight, or ask where she wants to start. Telling her it is fine leads to small tasks around Mondstadt and to Alois, a travelling merchant who wants Mondstadt wine for Liyue; Noelle's heavy hospitality unsettles him and, depending on the Traveler's replies, he either confesses a scheme to copy Dandelion Wine and leaves, or leaves grateful without confessing. Asking about the training leads Noelle to say something feels lacking. If it is strength, they visit the training grounds, where she smashes every dummy but laments always losing to other Knights; the Traveler can blame inexperience (fights outside the city, then a rescue of Henning from a hilichurl camp) or insist on strength (a mining site where monsters and a rockfall appear and she clears them). If it is rest, they go to Good Hunter, where Beatrice asks for help getting Quinn's attention. A gift of Mondstadt Grilled Fish fails and Noelle goes back to the library to study books about love; a gift from With Wind Comes Glory leads to planning a date, which Beatrice settles at Cape Oath, and Noelle gives the Traveler a paper rose.

**Journey to Liyue.** Noelle, shy and self-conscious in Liyue, is repeatedly mistaken for a Knight of Favonius. At Wanmin Restaurant she insists on the local Black-Back Perch Stew, finds it extremely spicy and then learns the broth was not meant to be drunk. At Hanfeng's Ironmongers a Liyue boy, Dugu Shuo, suspects her of loitering, asks to see her greatsword and Geo Vision, and leaves declaring they will meet again. The Traveler then chooses the harbour or Third-Round Knockout.

**Adventurer's Entrance Exam.** Noelle asks Katheryne about a Guild ranking exam, but only members may take them. Katheryne sends her to Cyrus, the Mondstadt branch master, who is drafting an entrance exam and wants a trial run at top difficulty. It has three parts and passing two is enough: three knowledge questions, a survival dish (steak is praised; Mondstadt Grilled Fish is accepted less warmly), and defeating a Ruin Guard at Brightcrown Canyon within a time limit. A perfect score ends with Cyrus trying to recruit her; missing one part ends with Noelle returning to the library to study even more. Failing two parts is a failed hangout.

**Noelle Rides Out.** The Traveler asks what kind of knight she might become. Starting with an Outrider like Amber, she gathers fresh Valberries and Pyro Slime Condensate in the Stormbearer Mountains and makes a Baron Bunny, but it neither draws the hilichurls nor explodes, and she has to fight them herself. She concludes she is not suited to be an Outrider, and the Traveler offers two other models: Jean or Kaeya.

**While the Millelith Stands Guard.** At Liyue Harbor the Millelith soldier Sha'yin, who heard a Knight of Favonius was in Liyue, asks for help arresting Treasure Hoarders from Mondstadt near Stone Gate; he needs a Knight as a witness because the Millelith could not pursue them across the border. The Traveler, as an Honorary Knight, can qualify. Noelle asks to guard the escape route alone and succeeds, then asks the Traveler to stay a little longer and shares tea at a stall, saying she is less afraid of the exam. The ending is "Rest Amidst the Rocks".

**A Knight's Journey Through Liyue.** At Third-Round Knockout the storyteller Iron Tongue Tian wants Noelle as source material for a story called "A Knight's Journey Through Liyue". She agrees reluctantly because she is not an official Knight. She cleans out a hilichurl camp and fights the returning hilichurls while he takes notes, then helps him get a Chaos Device from a Ruin Guard as a "memento". His retelling is wildly exaggerated; the audience, including Qian and Guanhai, doubts it until he shows the part, and they then recognise Noelle. She flees. The ending is "The Maid-Knight's Tale".

**Substitute Acting Grand Master Noelle.** Modelling herself on Jean, Noelle helps Marjorie find a monocle imported from Sumeru, asking Mondstadt locals and following a flapping sound to a finch nest on a rooftop. Marjorie jokes that Noelle is like a second Jean; Noelle says she helps because she wants to, not because she is a maid, and that she may simply keep doing small things when she is a knight. The ending is "The One and Only Noelle".

**Noelle the Intelligence Operative.** Modelling herself on Kaeya, Noelle goes to the Angel's Share to gather intelligence, but Charles gives her a waitress shift. She serves a drunk regular, Payne, tries to sober him with dried fruit, clears the upper floor and works until late, forgetting the intelligence goal. Charles says her service made the night better and may hire waiting staff. The ending is "Work Made for a Maid".
### ULTRA +
Every Act II branch follows the same shape: Noelle starts with a plan to fix her nerves, the plan partly fails, and what helps is what she already does. The Traveler's choices mostly change how she answers; the Act II endings are "Rest Amidst the Rocks", "The Maid-Knight's Tale", "Perfect Score", "Points Deducted", "The One and Only Noelle" and "Work Made for a Maid". The six Act I endings are "A Cold Reception", "Goodbye, Miss Maid!", "A Defender's Will Is Their Strength", "A Maid Above Ground", "A Conundrum Called Love" and "Whisper of the Paper Rose". Each act's endings give Adventure EXP plus a mix of Hero's Wit, Primogems and materials.

## revelations
@version: 1.5
### LITE
No main-story revelations. The event shows how Noelle sees herself and her exam.
### STANDARD +
Noelle has failed the Knights' selection exam several times and becomes anxious and blank when something unexpected happens in an exam. She works for the Knights as a maid and still needs to pass it to be an official Knight. She once got Amber to teach her to make Baron Bunnies, and finds she is not suited to being an Outrider.
### LARGE +
She says she never dared imagine what kind of knight she would be, fearing disappointment, and by the end of Substitute Acting Grand Master Noelle decides she may simply keep doing what she does now. She says the Traveler's presence calms her during exams.

The Traveler and Noelle.

Liyue: Chef Mao, Dugu Shuo, Sha'yin, Iron Tongue Tian, Qian and Guanhai. Act I: Alois, Henning and Quinn. Jean, Kaeya and Amber are discussed as models but do not appear in the Act II quests read.

## consequences
### STANDARD
No lasting canon change; the event is a self-contained character study. The real exam is never taken.

## placement
### STANDARD
Act I was released in Version 1.4 and Act II in Version 1.5. Act I requires the Mondstadt Archon Quest (Song of the Dragon and Freedom) and Act II follows the Liyue Archon Quest A New Star Approaches, since Noelle has just welcomed the Traveler back from Liyue. It is a side story with no fixed place in the in-world timeline.

## entry: Knights of Favonius selection exam
@keys: selection exam, knights selection exam
@keys2: Noelle
@version: 1.5
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
In this event Noelle says she has failed the Knights of Favonius selection exam several times. It assesses knowledge, including a thesis on chivalry and Favonius bladework, and combat. She has already worked for the Knights as a maid for some time but must pass it to be an official Knight. The exam is described as about half a year off at the start of Act II and is never actually held.

### STANDARD
Cyrus, the Mondstadt Adventurers' Guild branch master, was drafting a new entrance exam when Noelle asked for a trial. It has knowledge, survival-cooking and Ruin Guard combat parts; passing two of three succeeds. Noelle never intended to join the Guild, and Cyrus tries to recruit her anyway.
