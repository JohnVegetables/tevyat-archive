## summary
### LITE
Lynette's Hangout Event (Version 4.5, one act called "Checks & Cats") is a branching day spent with Lynette in Fontaine. She has slipped out of a drinks reception for her acting debut and meets a lost cat she names Bonnie. The player's choices lead to one of five endings: a debut, a quiet seat in an audience, a detective case, a day of doing nothing, or a hidden-conspiracy case that ends in an arrest.

### STANDARD
Lynette's Hangout Event is a one-act, voiced, branching story in Fontaine released in Version 4.5. Its act, "Checks & Cats", is built from five quests (A Decision, Mask, Break, Detectives, Closure) and ends in one of five endings. The Traveler meets Lynette outside Hotel Debord just after the opening of her first-ever acting role, hiding from the reception with a stray cat she has named Bonnie. Whether she goes back to the party, helps the cat, or does neither decides the whole day. Throughout, the event leans on her established traits: she conserves social energy by switching between named "modes", she reads people through tells, and she is quietly devoted to her twin Lyney.

### LARGE +
The act mixes character comedy with two full mysteries. The theatre branch is a small drama about a director, a sidelined understudy and an investor-driven publicity machine, resolved by Lynette and Lyney through a staged protest. The detective branch involves an animal-smuggling ring and, in its deepest path, brings back the Lefevere family, the aristocratic house that once abducted Lynette as a child. Which sub-quests the player sees depends on three fork points: the first choice at the hotel, the result of a card draw in the Detectives quest, and a late choice in Mask.

### ULTRA +
Mechanically, the event uses a Hangout Event structure (Story Keys, "hearts" for Lynette's energy, and skill-check style dice draws against a numbered deck), but this entry treats those only as the way branches are selected. The wiki act page records the unlock requirements as Adventure Rank 40, two Story Keys, and completion of "Masquerade of the Guilty" and "The Little Oceanid". The wiki treats this as the most recent Hangout Event as of Version 7.1, and lists only Act I.

## plot
### STANDARD
**A Decision.** Outside Hotel Debord, Lynette tells the Traveler she has just accepted a leading role in a masked mime show, The Lost Puppet, directed by Merieux. She is playing a puppet, so no lines and no facial expressions are needed. The show opened well, but the reception afterwards drained her, so she left to enter "standby mode". She met a collared, hungry cat she named Bonnie, and cannot decide between returning to the party and finding Bonnie's owner. Lyney had Freminet build her a card-box device, the Fateometer, for such moments. Her first draw is botched when the box jams and the cards fall, so she hands the decision to the player. The Traveler can send her back to the reception (Mask), help Bonnie (Detectives), let the Fateometer decide, or suggest doing neither, which can end the event as "Standby Mode".

**Mask.** At the reception, reporters and critics pour praise on The Lost Puppet while Lynette answers in minimal stock phrases. She has a name for her power-saving conversation style, "tea party mode", but the interview burns far more energy than a normal tea party. Furina, also a guest, gives a more measured opinion and mentions that an earlier draft of the script was more complex. Back with her envelope containing the group photo, Lynette finds an anonymous letter telling her that she is a heartless puppet who has ruined the work and must give up the lead. Merieux blames a rival, but the investor Maloney lets slip that the role was first meant for the actress Gillian. Gillian, living in the Fleuve Cendre, confirms it. With the troupe's actor Jaiques, the group reads the original script and the official script side by side and finds that the original ending, a lone dance among ruins for dead companions, is far more demanding and moving than the simplified stage version. Lynette proposes that Gillian take the lead for the second half of the show at the Opera Epiclese.

### LARGE +
**Mask, continued.** Two days later, Lynette and Gillian swap costumes and masks for the interval, so the critics file reviews after the first half praising Lynette's work, then see a different actress take the second half. When Merieux confronts them, Lynette announces she is quitting the lead role and Lyney arrives with a draft lawsuit, a contract and reimbursement money, an offer meant to force a choice. Lyney also reveals that other famous actresses had turned Merieux down before Lynette said yes, and that the troupe had chipped in for the money. Cyril, Jaiques and Gillian say plainly that Merieux had slid into the publicity-and-backroom-deal methods he used to despise. Merieux gives in, the second half runs with the original ending, and when Gillian removes her mask at the curtain call the audience, led from the balcony by Furina, applauds. Faced with angry critics, Lynette claims the actor swap was deliberate performance art, using the language of the show to explain the confusion, and secures a promise that Furina will write a review focused on Gillian.

**The letter.** After the show, Ginna, Gillian's younger sister, admits to having written the letter. In the "wait and see" path, Lynette suggests letting it go as a misunderstanding and gives Ginna a backstage job, which leads to the quest Break. In the alternative, Lynette claims not to remember anything, and the act ends with a new group photograph.

**Break.** A few days later the Traveler visits the opera house at Lynette's invitation. She is dozing in the audience, glad she no longer needs her energy-hungry modes, and says Merieux thinks Ginna is a promising actress with natural chemistry with her sister. Lynette says she would rather cheer on others from a quiet seat than seek fame herself.

### ULTRA +
**Detectives.** If Bonnie is helped, the pair post a notice at The Steambird, which the Traveler and Lynette are invited to follow up with tea the next day at Lynette's home. Later, a woman and the Humane Society director Bernard both turn up claiming the cat. The woman is the Gardes officer Elodie, who says Bonnie belongs to a suspect, the researcher Pierre Lefevere, in an investigation into a new drug called Imitation Sinthe. Lynette, who notices that Elodie's breathing stays calm when challenged, hears the family name with visible dread and tells the Traveler she was once kidnapped by the Lefevere head and rescued by Arlecchino. The twins then joined the House of the Hearth. Lyney had been avoiding her, and she realises he and Chevreuse, captain of the Special Security and Surveillance Patrol, were already working the case, and that he arranged the acting job to keep her busy. The group is merged into one team. A pendant bearing a Lefevere insignia for an illegitimate child, the Humane Society director Bernard's attempt to take the cat, and Lynette's reading of Bonnie's body language all point toward a secret base north of the Court of Fontaine. There they find equipment for a drug-making operation, special waterproof bags and barrels, and evidence of weighted floating barrels used for dead drops, from which the group concludes that animals were being used as carriers. At Lumidouce Harbor, travel logs show the Humane Society's repeated "overseas adoption" departures. The group confronts Bernard, who confesses to transporting animals for a partner he has never met. That partner, he says, is Pierre, but they only ever wrote letters and he has never met him.

**Fork.** In Falling Into Layers of Fog, Lynette enters "deduction mode" with the Traveler. If the Traveler fails the final check, they let the Gardes take Bernard away and go to rescue animals at the Humane Society (ending "Save the Kitty!"). If it succeeds, the Traveler stops Elodie from leaving, and Closure follows.

**Closure.** The Traveler and Lynette reason that a third person controlled both men: they were kept apart on purpose, Pierre was told to leave Bonnie and the pendant where the Phantom would find them, and Pierre was lured to his base and made to disappear. The third person is Elodie, who is revealed to be a Lefevere. Chevreuse and Lyney arrive with evidence that Elodie often swapped her port shifts to match smuggling activity. Elodie threatens to blow up the Humane Society with a crude device, but Lynette and the group subdue her, Bernard is knocked out, and the device turns out to be a toy. Over tea with Bonnie afterwards, Lynette explains that the cat's reluctance to approach Elodie raised her first strong suspicion (ending "Adventurers, Investigators, and Cats").

## revelations
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
- The anonymous letter in the Mask branch is the work of Ginna, Gillian's younger sister, not a professional intimidator or a rival troupe; Lynette judges her feeling to be indignant but not malicious.
- The detective branch discloses that Lynette was abducted by the head of the Lefevere family at a dinner party and rescued by Arlecchino, and that this is how the twins joined the House of the Hearth.
- The same branch shows Lyney working with Chevreuse as a consultant on a smuggling case without telling Lynette, and using the acting job to keep her away from a case that touches the Lefevere name.
- The Closure path names the mastermind of the smuggling ring as Elodie, a Gardes officer who is an illegitimate Lefevere descendant, planted in the Gardes before the family's fall and kept there afterwards.

### LARGE +
- Merieux changed the script after investor pressure and chose Lynette because of her name; the original lead was Gillian. He is shown as sincere about his art but compromised by the publicity routine.
- Lynette explains that the Fateometer deck has twenty cards and that she sets the cutoff as she likes. She says she agreed to do the show only if she drew a 1, and later suspects that this draw was part of Lyney's plan to keep her busy (her own suspicion, not confirmed).
- Lynette says she has younger siblings and that Lyney has always been overprotective.

## characters
### STANDARD
Lynette is the focus: her modes (standby, event, tea party, search, deduction, serious), her reluctance about fame, her talent for reading tells, and her protectiveness toward the weak. The Traveler is her main companion and the player's choices drive the story. Bonnie, a cat, appears throughout the story and is the thread connecting the branches.

### LARGE +
- **Lyney:** Lynette's twin, who arrives in the theatre climax with legal paperwork and in the detective storyline as Chevreuse's consultant.
- **Chevreuse:** Captain of the Special Security and Surveillance Patrol, working with Lyney, who welcomes Lynette and the Traveler to the team.
- **Furina:** A guest at the Hotel Debord reception, attending as a theatre expert, who later applauds the audience reaction and agrees to review the show.
- **Mask troupe:** Director Merieux, actress Gillian and her younger sister Ginna, actors Jaiques and Cyril, investor Maloney, and the critics Corentin and Amaury. Argalia, Leone, and two reporters have small roles.
- **Detective cast:** Euphrasie (The Steambird), Gardes officer Elodie, port guard Chourei and the dog Girard, Humane Society director Bernard, and the researcher Pierre Lefevere, who is never seen.
- **Freminet:** Mentioned as the builder of the Fateometer.

## consequences
### STANDARD
Each ending ends the day but not the characters' ongoing lives. Nothing in the event changes the main storylines of Fontaine. Depending on the branch, it leaves the Traveler with a closer bond to Lynette, a restored troupe (Gillian in the lead role), or a closed smuggling case.

### LARGE +
Per the wiki's ending reward table, each ending gives Adventure EXP plus either Primogems and a book or food material, or Hero's Wit and Vayuda Turquoise Fragments. The Fateometer is listed as the reward of A Decision.

## placement
### STANDARD
Released in Version 4.5, after the Fontaine Archon Quest chapters. It requires completion of 'Masquerade of the Guilty' and the story quest 'The Little Oceanid'. It is a side story and does not advance the main plot.

### LARGE +
Because the event has five endings, there is no single canonical timeline. The recommended roleplay interpretation is that the Traveler experiences one of the branches and that Lynette remembers only the branch actually played.

## entry: Endings of Hangout Event Lynette
@keys: Checks & Cats, Lynette hangout ending, Lynette Hangout Event
@version: 4.5
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
The act has five endings. "A Roaring Success!" (Mask, with the late choice): a new group photo after the Gillian-led show. "A Seat in the Audience" (Break): Lynette relaxes in the audience. "Adventurers, Investigators, and Cats" (Closure): tea with Bonnie after the arrest. "Save the Kitty!" (Detectives): the Traveler and Lynette leave the case to the Gardes and go to rescue animals. "Standby Mode" (A Decision): the Traveler suggests doing neither task and the day is spent doing nothing.

## entry: Fateometer
@keys: Fateometer
@version: 4.5
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
A box of twenty numbered cards that Lyney had Freminet make for Lynette, so she can let a random draw decide things. The cutoff number is whatever she decides at the time. In the event she uses it for the question of whether to return to the reception or help Bonnie, but the box jams and the cards fall. The player receives it as a quest reward.

## entry: The Lost Puppet
@keys: The Lost Puppet, masked mime show
@keys2: Lynette, Merieux, Gillian
@version: 4.5
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
A masked mime show with no spoken lines and no facial expression, written and directed by Merieux and staged at the Opera Epiclese. Its protagonist is a dancing puppet who realises that her failing legs are the aging of her components. In the original script, she ends by dancing alone among ruins for her dead companions until her parts fail. In the commercial version, she is welcomed into a lavish stage and left uncertain about whether anyone will appreciate her. Lynette plays the lead in the first half of the opening run; Gillian takes it for the director's cut.

## entry: Imitation Sinthe smuggling case
@keys: Imitation Sinthe
@keys2: Lynette, Elodie, Bernard
@version: 4.5
@availability: permanent
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
In the detective branch, a new drug that imitates Sinthe is made in a hidden base, carried in thin sealed bags swallowed by animals, floated in weighted barrels as dead drops and shipped abroad as "overseas adoptions" by the Humane Society. The researcher Pierre Lefevere and the director Bernard are made to appear as the culprits. In the Closure path, the Gardes officer Elodie is revealed as the coordinator who kept them apart and meant to sacrifice both.

## entry: Elodie
@keys: Elodie
@keys2: Lynette, Humane Society, Imitation Sinthe
@version: 4.5
@availability: permanent
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
A Gardes officer who introduces herself to Lynette and the Traveler as an investigator of the case. In the Closure path, she claims to be the last of the Lefevere family (Chevreuse names her a Lefevere), who hid her origin and used her post to organise the smuggling ring. She resents the family that made her an infiltrator, and her plan relies on blaming Pierre and Bernard.
