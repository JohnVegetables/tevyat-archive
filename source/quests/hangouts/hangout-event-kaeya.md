## summary
### LITE
Hangout Event: Kaeya is a replayable, choice-driven story for Kaeya, the Knights of Favonius Cavalry Captain, released in Version 3.8, with one act, Shenanigans and Sweet Wine. The Traveler meets Kaeya far from home in Port Ormos in Sumeru, where he is on a Knights business trip, and spends the following days with him: watching and joining a theatre troupe, returning to Mondstadt, or going on to Liyue. It is a light, witty story with no effect on the main story.
### STANDARD +
It requires 2 Story Keys, Adventure Rank 35, and the Archon Quest Caribert plus the Story Quest Secret Pirate Treasure. There are five endings. Permanent, repeatable content.
### LARGE +
This Hangout Event moves across three nations: Sumeru, Mondstadt and Liyue. The wiki notes that its Heartbeat Value stays at full and does not drop with any of the Traveler's choices. Its tone is playful, with glimpses of the people who care for Kaeya.
### ULTRA +
The act's quests are The Birdwatcher, All the World's a Stage, A Familiar Face in a Faraway Place, Reunion, Poems Dedicated to the Wind, An Ordinary Day for the Knights of Favonius, Fragrance of Wine, Taste of Home and Feathers Adrift in a Spring Breeze.

## plot
### LITE
In Port Ormos, Kaeya is serenading birds and is pleased to see the Traveler. Near a theatre troupe's stage he is drawn into the play, then the two continue together. Kaeya suggests turning his business trip into a shared holiday.
### STANDARD
Opening: in Port Ormos Kaeya, travelling alone and bored, jokes about luring a Dusk Bird onto his shoulder, and greets the Traveler warmly. He proposes making his business trip a joint trip and, since the Knights count all expenses on a trip as travel expenses, treating it as a chance to live a little.
Stage branch (Questions): at Port Ormos a troupe is performing a drama about Prince Qubad, who rejects his father's war. When the actor playing Qubad is missing, Kaeya fills in, wearing Sailwind Shadow. He improvises, asks the audience if they believe in fate and throws the prop intaglio to the Traveler. The Traveler's answer shapes the scene, and Kaeya ends it by having the prince defy fate and leave. The ending is named Questions.
Mondstadt branches: if the Traveler declines to spend public money, Kaeya packs and they return to Mondstadt; he gives the Traveler a lamp as a souvenir. From there:
- A Boat Untethered: Kaeya, wanting to relax, takes the Traveler to the Cathedral plaza. Sister Grace has a problem, which the Traveler explains to Venti at the Angel's Share. A group including Barbara and Diona later gathers in the plaza to prepare a new song, and Kaeya hesitates to join; Venti gives him a poem and invites him to sing with everyone.
- The Grapes of Warmth: they visit Dawn Winery, where Kaeya teases Diluc and picks grapes with Tunner, who asks Kaeya and the Traveler to read a letter from his son, Guy.
- Our Sun: back at the winery, Kaeya and Adelinde, with the Traveler, share a home-cooked meal; Klee turns up to rescue Kaeya from a grounding.
### LARGE +
Liyue branch (An Informant, or a Friend?): if the Traveler goes along with Kaeya's holiday trip, they go to Liyue Harbor. They meet Captain Wu, to whom Kaeya once gave unexpected help in Mondstadt (a Treasure Hoarder attack near Dawn Winery during a family trip). Captain Wu has been swindled by a con man who invoked a connection to Dr. Baizhu's master. Kaeya suggests reporting to the Ministry of Civil Affairs, but the Traveler wants to help. Kaeya devises a sting in which he poses as Albert Rich, a wealthy young lord from Mondstadt, with the Traveler as a disciple of the Guhua Clan, and the con man is exposed and taken away by the Millelith. Afterwards Kaeya asks Captain Wu whether he wants to be Kaeya's informant or his friend and tosses a coin, then declares him a friend.
### ULTRA +
In An Ordinary Day for the Knights of Favonius, Jean thanks him for his report and says a letter from the wine merchants' association in Sumeru credited his on-the-spot decisions with avoiding further losses from delays; Kaeya returns the leftover travel funds while the Traveler suspects he never meant to spend them. In Fragrance of Wine, he teases Diluc over a rumour about the winery's purchase of limited Northland Valberry Liquor. In Taste of Home, Adelinde tells him he has always been part of the Dawn Winery family. Klee, thinking Kaeya is in trouble with Jean, apologises for him; Kaeya says he has people to care for him now.
After the Port Ormos play, Kaeya says he might act full time if he ever left the Knights, and tells the Traveler that everyone is both actor and audience: if one dislikes the script, one can walk off the stage and join the audience.

## revelations
### STANDARD
Little new lore is revealed. It adds personal detail: Kaeya's work for the Knights extends to travel, the Dawn Winery's staff think of him as family, and he says that people who owe him favours are either his friends or his informants.
### LARGE +
Tunner remarks that something deep-rooted seems to bother Kaeya and that he guards his secrets closely, and asks the Traveler to look after him. Kaeya's remarks on the stage about an inept god who sets fathers against sons are framed as a figure of speech; he tells the Traveler not to take them literally.
### ULTRA +
The wiki notes that Kaeya's alias Albert Rich plays on his family name Alberich.

## characters
### LITE
Kaeya and the Traveler are the main characters.
### STANDARD +
Mondstadt: Venti, Barbara, Diona, Sister Grace and the bards (Poems Dedicated to the Wind); Jean, Lisa, Albedo and Klee; Diluc, Elzer, Tunner, Adelinde and Connor at Dawn Winery. Sumeru: the theatre troupe including Darbil, Zabi and Tus. Liyue: Captain Wu, Guangzhi, Aunt Zhang and Mr. Bai.

## consequences
### STANDARD
No lasting effect on the main story. Rewards are given per ending (Adventure EXP, Primogems, Hero's Wit, talent or ascension materials, Kaeya's special dish), and endings can be shown as Lingering Moments in the Serenitea Pot.
### LARGE +
It does not add or resolve any plot threads about Kaeya's past.

## placement
### STANDARD
Released in Version 3.8. It unlocks after the Archon Quest Caribert and the Story Quest Secret Pirate Treasure. The act sits outside the main timeline.
