## summary
### LITE
Hangout Event: Bennett is a replayable, choice-driven story for the Adventurers' Guild member Bennett, released in Version 1.4, with one act, Fantastic Voyage. Bennett asks to adventure with the Traveler, and whichever outing they pick, his famously bad luck shapes the day. It is a comedy with no effect on the main story.
### STANDARD +
It requires 2 Story Keys, Adventure Rank 26 and the Archon Quest Song of the Dragon and Freedom. There are six endings. Permanent, repeatable content.
### LARGE +
The three outings (around town, in the wilds, in ruins) each show a different side of his luck. The ruins routes also bring out how he copes with it: he leaves decisions to the Traveler, apologises for trouble he did not choose, and is delighted when something simply works.
### ULTRA +
The act is built from the quests Adventurous Acquaintance, Something... Other Than Adventure?, What Is This, a Day Trip?, Treacherous Thermadon and Expansive Eya.

## plot
### LITE
The Traveler meets Bennett, who is envious of the Traveler's achievements and asks to tag along. They choose to hang out in town, walk in the wilds, or explore newly discovered ruins.
### STANDARD
Around town, Bennett needs materials. With Wind Comes Glory is out of stock, the Good Hunter turns them away because the cook, Sara, is busy with a meal order for a delegation from Snezhnaya, and Mondstadt General Goods seems to be offering 50% off everything. When Blanche finally appears she says the shop is closed and the promotion was for the day before (ending: Fruitless Anticipation).
In the wilds, Bennett suggests a stroll in a dandelion meadow. The dandelions have been stripped of seeds, a monster camp is empty and its chests hold only vegetables and a little Mora, and the weather turns into a thunderstorm. Jack arrives to take Bennett home because his dads have been asking for him, and the weather clears the moment he goes (Hasty Farewell).
### LARGE +
In the ruins, the route depends on whether the two prepare food first.
- Food prepared: they make an Ominous Dish and go to the Deserted Ruins of Thermadon. A mechanism closes the door behind them, then jams. They meet Royce, another adventurer stuck there, who blames Bennett and then asks for food. If the Traveler lets Bennett share, Royce collapses from food poisoning (Taste Tester Royce). If not, Bennett eats the dish himself, hoping it will knock him out and let his bad luck lapse for a while (Ready to Make Sacrifices).
- No food: they go to the Deserted Ruins of Eya and find mechanisms. If Bennett operates one alone, they are trapped (No Wonder It's a Top-Class Commission). If the Traveler acts with him, they solve a series of puzzles and reach a Luxurious Chest, and Bennett is thrilled (Fortunate Outcome).
### ULTRA +
In Eya, Bennett is explicit that he is rarely lucky and defers the choice of mechanism to the Traveler. In a later room he says four people would be needed for the mechanisms, and explains that Benny's Adventure Team now exists only in name. Katheryne has not deregistered it out of sympathy, and Royce, Heckler and Jack were all former members who left because of his bad luck. In Thermadon, Royce tells the Traveler why he left: years of accidents such as ruins collapsing, lightning and a sudden explosion. Bennett apologises for the trouble he caused, and the Traveler can encourage him to look after himself instead of putting others first.

## revelations
### STANDARD
The act confirms that Bennett's bad luck is a long-running, widely known fact, that Benny's Adventure Team has dwindled to Bennett alone, and that his former teammates left because of the danger, without bitterness.
### LARGE +
Jack says Bennett's dads have all been asking for him, and Bennett says he wants Benny's Adventure Team's name known across Teyvat.

## characters
### LITE
Bennett and the Traveler are the main characters.
### STANDARD +
Depending on the route: Sara, Blanche and the shops of Mondstadt (town); Jack (wilds); Royce (Thermadon ruins); Katheryne is mentioned in the Eya ruins.

## consequences
### STANDARD
No lasting effect on the main story. Rewards are given per ending (Adventure EXP, Primogems, Hero's Wit, talent or ascension materials, Bennett's special dish), and endings can be shown as Lingering Moments in the Serenitea Pot.
### LARGE +
Endings are independent of one another; none leads on to the next.

## placement
### STANDARD
Released in Version 1.4. It unlocks after Song of the Dragon and Freedom but sits outside the main timeline, with no dated events.
### LARGE +
Like other Hangout Events, it is a standalone day that does not alter the world.

## entry: Benny's Adventure Team
@keys: Benny's Adventure Team
@version: 1.4
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
In Bennett's Hangout Event, Bennett says Benny's Adventure Team is now a team only in name, because he is the only member left. Royce, Heckler and Jack were once part of it; they left because of the accidents that follow his bad luck. Katheryne has kept the team registered out of pity. Bennett hopes its name will one day be known all over Teyvat.

## entry: Royce
@keys: Royce
@keys2: Bennett
@version: 1.4
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
Royce is a former member of Benny's Adventure Team who left after repeated accidents. In the Thermadon ruins route he is trapped alongside Bennett and the Traveler, blames Bennett, then asks for food. If he eats the Ominous Dish he falls ill; if refused, he parts ways with Bennett, wishing him well.

## entry: Bennett Hangout Endings

### LARGE
The six endings are: Fruitless Anticipation (the shop discount was yesterday's), Hasty Farewell (Jack takes Bennett home and the weather clears), Taste Tester Royce (Royce eats the Ominous Dish first), Ready to Make Sacrifices (Bennett eats it himself), No Wonder It's a Top-Class Commission (trapped in the Eya ruins) and Fortunate Outcome (the Eya puzzles are solved and a Luxurious Chest is found).
