## summary
@spoiler: none
### LITE
Hangout Event: Kuki Shinobu is a branching, player-choice side story added in version 2.7 (Act I, "The Gang's Daily Deeds"). After three Arataki Gang members are arrested for illegally posting flyers, deputy Kuki Shinobu bails them out and spends the day repairing the damage, with the Traveler as her improvised "Chief Advisor". Depending on choices, the day leads to a forced shift at the Grand Narukami Shrine, a reunion with her sister, a manhunt for runaway gang members, or a misunderstood job interview. It is a comic story about Shinobu's patience and her real attachment to the gang. Five endings are possible.
### STANDARD +
The event needs two Story Keys and first requires the Interlude "Perilous Trail" and the story quest "Rise Up, Golden Soul". The branches are alternatives of one day, not a continuous chronology. The running idea is that Shinobu fixes the gang's mess with law, bookkeeping and discipline, and that the "Gang Bylaws" she invents are how she hopes to make them behave.
### LARGE +
Shinobu is never relaxed in this event: the shrine, her sister, the Tenryou Commission and even her own gang each press on a different part of her past or reputation. The gang, for its part, shows how much it relies on her and, in the last branch, how badly it fears losing her.

## plot
### LITE
At the Police Station, Shinobu talks Doushin Uesugi into releasing the three young gang members (Mamoru, Akira and Genta), claiming the "Arataki Support Group" helps people with mental instabilities. Outside, she lectures them, and the Traveler chooses whether to defend them, to propose written rules, or to stay out of it.
### STANDARD
Shinobu's first plan is to scold the three and make them apologise. The Traveler's choices lead along five routes. Defending them sends everyone to the Grand Narukami Shrine, where the gang had posted flyers and met Yae Miko (ending "Unexpected Wages" or "Time For Some Secret Dealings..."). Proposing written rules sends Shinobu after the boys scattering through town (endings "Official Business!" or "Doom Arrives Just the Same"). Standing back lets her discipline them herself; the three then follow her, with the Traveler, to Uyuu Restaurant (ending "The Arataki Gang Community Service Chronicle").
### LARGE +
Branch "To the Shrine" - Yae Miko, the shrine's Guuji, says the flyers insult the Shogun herself, and demands Shinobu serve as a shrine maiden for a day to make amends. If the Traveler urges Shinobu to play along, she agrees on conditions (one day only, and no shrine maiden outfit) and Yae Miko extends the offer to the Traveler. At the shrine Shinobu advises a visitor to avoid an investment scam, tells an editor blocked on ideas to travel, and makes omamori for an old woman whose granddaughter is leaving. The woman recognises her voice as that of a former shrine maiden. Shinobu admits she trained here briefly at her family's insistence, hated it, and wears her mask to avoid prying. Yae Miko pays them wages; the Traveler's envelope contains only a thin sheet, ending "Unexpected Wages".

If the Traveler instead refuses the shrine maiden role, Yae Miko tells Shinobu to remove the flyers from the mountain path with the shrine maiden already assigned: Shinobu's sister Miyuki. In "In the Aftermath" Shinobu tells Miyuki the Arataki Gang is a legitimate international organisation and the Traveler its Chief Advisor, until the gang members arrive and expose the truth. Shinobu admits she joined freely and is proud of the gang. Miyuki promises not to tell the family and asks Shinobu to carry out the dreams she once described. Ending: "Time For Some Secret Dealings...".

Branch "Gang Bylaws" - Mamoru, Akira and Genta scatter and Shinobu tracks them down: Mamoru on a bathhouse roof, Genta under a bridge where he took the spot used by local children, and Akira far outside the city. She recites Article 1 (do not damage property), Article 2 (do not bully small children) and, later, Article 3 (insubordination is punished by reciting all bylaws). Akira has fled far to the north and tries to hide in a crate. If the Traveler coaxes him out and takes him back, Shinobu runs "That'll Learn Them!", a classroom quiz where the trio must say which bylaws a hypothetical "John Doe" violated, and punishes wrong answers by making them copy out the rules three times. Ending: "Official Business!". If the Traveler instead helps him hide, Akira ends up caught between Kairagi smugglers and Fatui skirmishers; in "Temporary Escape" Shinobu and the Traveler defeat them and learn the Kairagi were fronting for the Fatui. Shinobu plans to pass that to the Tenryou Commission to offset the gang's past offences, but still says Akira needs hands-on training. Ending: "Doom Arrives Just the Same".

Branch "Key Decision" - Mamoru, Akira and Genta overhear Shinobu at Uyuu Restaurant with Kujou Sara and a woman named Norika, apparently interviewing for a Tenryou Commission job. They report to Itto, who panics, orders the flyers taken down and apologies made, and tells the Traveler to ask Shinobu for an interview. Shinobu gives the Traveler an "internship" apologising to shopkeepers, who report Itto has already apologised. Doushin Amano nearly arrests the gang again until Norika vouches for Shinobu. The gang gives Shinobu a farewell gift and confess hidden misdeeds, only for Norika to explain she is a Liyue-Inazuma merchant who hired Shinobu as a legal consultant for a first-aid supply deal. Shinobu then asks about their confessions and chases them. Ending: "The Arataki Gang Community Service Chronicle".
### ULTRA +
Further details:

- "Ways and Means": Shinobu tells the Doushin that Mamoru, Akira and Genta were posting flyers to reach "more people in need", and that she and the other gang members will remove them. The three had hung flyers on the Grand Narukami Shrine's mountain path and been caught by a "terrifying fox lady" they will not name. She lists past incidents: nearly summoning the fire brigade while roasting Lavender Melons, and a neighbourhood-angering "Great Singalong".
- If the Traveler backs away at the start, Shinobu disciplines the trio herself and the story continues at "Back to Business".
- The shrine maiden shift: visitors include Takuma, who is considering a 40 percent overseas-shipping scheme that Shinobu identifies as a Ponzi-style scam, and Hayato, a writer who asks the shrine for inspiration and is told to travel. The omamori scene involves Asami, a nearly blind old woman who refuses help up the mountain, and Inagi Hotomi and Maki of the shrine.
- Asked why she did not stay on, Shinobu says simply that she does not enjoy the work. She says separately that relying on gods for everything frustrates her, but that prayer sometimes is the only thing people can do.
- "Key Decision": Mamoru notes that a day's Shogunate pay could buy the gang a week of snacks; Itto replies that the gang's relaxed working environment compensates. His backup plan is a farewell gift of a Naku Weed bracelet bought at Tsukumomono Groceries. Doushin Amano recognises Itto from a flyer drawing.
- Rewards: Adventure EXP plus Primogems or enhancement materials per ending.

## revelations
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
- Shinobu, once a trainee at the Grand Narukami Shrine at her family's arrangement, quit because of the strict etiquette and constant monitoring at home. She later studied bookkeeping, worked as an editor and, by her own account, studied law in Liyue. She wears a mask so people do not pry.
- Shinobu has a sister, Miyuki, who works as a shrine maiden of the Grand Narukami Shrine (she mentions dealing with matters in Tatarasuna earlier), and who agrees to keep Shinobu's gang membership secret from their family.
- The Arataki Gang has no formal rules until Shinobu writes the Gang Bylaws in this event.
- The Kairagi group at the beach was handing supplies to Fatui skirmishers.
### LARGE +
- Kujou Sara recommended Shinobu to Norika as a legal consultant, and a Police Station doushin notes that the gang's deputy has studied abroad and knows the law.
- Shinobu admits she dislikes the shrine, her family's expectations and Yae Miko's teasing, but is moved that the gang came to support her despite fear.

## characters
### STANDARD
Shinobu is the lead; the Traveler is her "Chief Advisor". Arataki Itto and gang members Mamoru, Akira and Genta recur. Yae Miko and shrine staff (Inagi Hotomi, Maki) appear in the shrine branches, plus Shinobu's sister Miyuki. Tenryou Commission figures include Kujou Sara, Doushin Uesugi and Doushin Amano; Norika is a merchant. Shopkeepers Shimura Kanbei and Aoi, and a boy, Shouta, are apologised to.

## consequences
### STANDARD
The event does not change the main story. It adds the Gang Bylaws as a running joke and shows Shinobu choosing the gang over her family's expectations. In the final branch it also shows her doing legal consulting work introduced by Kujou Sara.

## placement
### STANDARD
Released in version 2.7. It is set after the Interlude "Perilous Trail". No content from 6.x to 7.1 was found that revises the event.

## entry: Gang Bylaws
@keys: Gang Bylaws, Arataki Gang Handbook, Arataki Gang bylaws
@keys2: Shinobu
@version: 2.7
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
Shinobu's improvised rulebook for the Arataki Gang: Article 1 - no damage to others' property; offenders repay the full loss through a gang loan, with their gang assets such as Onikabuto and Genius Invokation cards frozen until it is paid. Article 2 - no bullying small children; offenders apologise sincerely and are banned from gang activities such as Lavender Melon roasting until the victim forgives. Article 3 - when one gang member is in trouble, all come to help (in a later scene, insubordination is punished by reciting the bylaws from memory). Additional notes, such as Clause 36 of Article 2, are quoted in class. Shinobu runs a quiz as a deterrent.

## entry: Arataki Gang Flyer Incident
@keys: Arataki Gang flyers, flyer campaign, Stick It to the Man flyer
@keys2: Arataki Gang
@version: 2.7
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
Itto designed flyers himself (his gang members call them avant-garde), including slogans like "Stick It to the Man, Join the Arataki Gang!" and "Arataki Numero Uno Itto: Ousting His Foes Like a Sumo Supremo!". Mamoru, Genta and Akira posted them around Inazuma City, the Grand Narukami Shrine path, Shimura's and Tsukumomono Groceries, and were arrested. The flyers are the cause of every branch of the event.

## entry: Shinobu and the Grand Narukami Shrine
@keys: Shinobu's trainee shrine maiden past, shrine maiden for a day
@keys2: Kuki Shinobu
@version: 2.7
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
Shinobu was once a trainee at the Grand Narukami Shrine; the placement was arranged by her family. She remembers nagging about etiquette and interrogations after each shift, and left to become anything but a shrine maiden. She finds it hard to refuse Yae Miko, who knows the history, and wears her mask partly to avoid being recognised there.
