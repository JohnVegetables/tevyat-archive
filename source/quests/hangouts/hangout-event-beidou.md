## summary
@spoiler: minor
### LITE
Beidou's Hangout Event, a replayable, choice-driven side story in which the captain of the Crux fleet invites the Traveler aboard the Alcor to celebrate the fleet's return from Inazuma. It has one act, When the Crux Shines Bright, and five endings, depending on which of three routes you take: sailor training, a quiet treasure hunt, or a day of business in Liyue Harbor. It unlocks after the Inazuma Archon Quest Omnipresence Over Mortals.
### STANDARD +
Unlocking needs two Story Keys, Adventure Rank 30 and the Archon Quest Omnipresence Over Mortals; it arrived in Version 2.3. The act holds five quests: A Special Guest (the hub), Life on the High Seas, Beneath the Surface, Guili Plains Treasure Hunt and Qingce Village Treasure Hunt. Hangout Events as a whole are replayable permanent content, so none of this is limited to a time window.
### LARGE +
The tone is warm and crew-centred: Beidou reveals how she leads (trading favours, trusting specialists and keeping her crew's welfare in view) and, on one route, the harsh story of her childhood. Each ending is a "Memory" that can be displayed in the Serenitea Pot.

## plot
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
In A Special Guest the Traveler returns to the Alcor and is greeted by cheerful crew (Sea Drake, Furong, Huixing) who want to recruit them after hearing of the end of the Vision Hunt Decree. Beidou declines to let the crew press the Traveler, then offers a choice. Interested in sailor life, the Traveler goes to training; otherwise Beidou takes them aside to talk about a map she found.

**Sailor training.** Beidou explains that new recruits are split into the Shield of the Crux (survival, navigation, entertainment) and the Spear of the Crux (naval fighting and cargo deals). Choosing the Shield leads to Life on the High Seas; choosing the Spear leads to Beneath the Surface.

**Treasure hunt.** Beidou found a battered treasure map in an Inazuman pirates' wreck. She asks the Traveler to read it. Naming Guili Plains starts the Guili Plains Treasure Hunt; naming Qingce Village starts the Qingce Village Treasure Hunt.
### LARGE +
**Life on the High Seas.** The Traveler learns from three crew: Yinxing (the ship's surgeon, who warns that the biggest danger to newcomers is underestimating the sea), Huixing (navigator, chart reading and the coloured distance lines on the supplementary chart) and Xu Liushi (lookout, who explains the warning signs of a waterspout). Beidou then quizzes the Traveler with three questions. Scoring zero or one sends them to Beneath the Surface instead; two or three correct answers pass them to the recreation programme. Because the regular photography teacher is ill, the Traveler becomes the teacher for the crew's photography class, and Beidou is cajoled into being the model. Choosing Yaoguang Shoal or the summit of Mt. Tianheng as a photo location ends the event without a Memory (Beidou prefers fun with her crew to a lifeless Kamera). Naming the abandoned fishing village near Wangshu Inn leads to the best-known story of the event (see the childhood entry). Depending on the pose chosen the event ends with Photography Class for Two or with the comedic Flirtatious ending.

**Beneath the Surface.** On old Skirmish Boats from the Alcor, Beidou sets a timed boat-handling course in the Sea of Clouds, then takes the Traveler to Liyue Harbor to show "the less official side" of the fleet's trade. Following coded phrases, the pair deal with two fishmongers and a warehouse owner, Mr. Iron Abacus. That night the Millelith arrive on a tip and search the goods, finding only potatoes, flour and correct paperwork. Beidou explains that she and the Traveler are both on the Ministry of Civil Affairs' watch list, so their visible presence acted as cover while her accountant Mora-Grubber ran the real, quiet transaction. A firework over the Domain of Guyun signals success. Beidou notes Ningguang is, in a sense, her business partner (Beidou fetches rare treasures Ningguang wants) and says she will say more if the Traveler ever finishes their journey. Ending: Once Everything Is Over.

**Guili Plains Treasure Hunt.** Treasure Hoarders led by Golden Alligator are already digging; they surrender at Beidou's name. Beidou honours first-come, first-served, offers to pay, and they hand it over as an apology, receiving an offer of honest port work in return. They later report back with a poem clue to a second cache, and the Traveler solves it by combining the ruins, the red-leafed tree and the time of day. The hoard is a Northland Bank cheque and a note left by an Inazuma-bound traveller for his elderly mother, who lives in a wooden house by the sea. Beidou has the former hoarders deliver the cheque as a government welfare payment, and the Traveler brings a Sakura Bloom as a keepsake from her son. Ending: Sakura Blooms Upon Guili Fell.

**Qingce Village Treasure Hunt.** In Qingce Village, Beidou is asked to settle three small disputes (a farmer and a neighbour over finches, a sunlit window and a tree planted by a dead father, and a farmer and a helper over a chest) before she can claim the chest. She resolves them with a mix of fair judgment, an offer to buy the finches, and promised payments from the Crux's village relief funds, depending on the Traveler's advice. At a village banquet she tells the Traveler why she trades in favours; they open the chest and find only rotten cabbage. Ending: Between Us.

### ULTRA +
**Qingce disputes in detail.** Ji is accused by Wenjing of letting a flock of wild finches destroy half her crops; the Traveler can hold him responsible (Beidou buys the finches and makes him compensate Wenjing) or call him innocent (Beidou pays from the Crux's relief fund, which Granny Ruoxin says is never used up). Pops Xing complains that a tree planted by Ren's late father blocks his sunlight; Ren says the tree was meant to suppress bad family luck and honours his father's dying wish, though he does not share the superstition himself. Beidou either has the tree moved with crew help and a gardener, or lets it stand with compensation for Pops Xing. Defu and Changping quarrel over a chest turned up while ploughing Defu's land; Beidou offers a legal expert from Liyue Harbor at her own cost, and when neither wants that, asks them to hand her the chest, promising the Crux's support in return.

**Guili poem.** The clue is a short verse about gazing at forsaken ruins while lying beneath a red-leafed tree whose crooked shadow marks the spot as dusk falls; the Traveler works out the vantage point and the shadow's direction rather than Golden Alligator, who admits he is not clever enough.

## revelations
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Beidou, in her own words, was homeless and about five or six years old when she reached a fishing village near Wangshu Inn and was taken in by the village chief. After the chief fell ill and died the following winter and the catch shrank, the villagers blamed her and drove her out, citing her name: Beidou is the Northern Dipper constellation, whose Alcor star is read as an omen of death, and some villagers shouted that the Northern Dipper controls death. She tells the Traveler this at the empty village (known to its former residents as "Downriver"). The Traveler can answer that they too might be called cursed, and Beidou accepts the company: she will share the blame, as captain, for anyone who tries to scapegoat the Traveler.
### LARGE +
Other established details: Beidou describes the Crux as an armed carrier that also trades, with some of that trade not entirely above-board; she says almost every confrontation in the fleet's history was settled by negotiation, that skirmish craft and armed crew are enough for most situations, and that the Alcor itself would open fire only in a fight to the death. She also mentions that Kazuha has led poetry, music and communal wind-listening sessions for the crew. Her philosophy of favours (an unseen net of mutual obligation that keeps a person from falling alone) is stated in the Qingce route.

## characters
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Beidou (captain of the Crux; confident, generous, and fond of drinking) carries the event. The Traveler is the player-chosen guest, and many dialogue options colour the mood. Notable recurring Crux crew: Sea Drake, Furong, Huixing (navigator), Yinxing (surgeon), Xu Liushi (lookout), Juza (chief mate), Mora-Grubber (accountant). One-route characters include Golden Alligator and his Treasure Hoarders (Guili), the villagers of Qingce (Granny Ruoxin, Ji, Wenjing, Pops Xing, Ren, Changping, Defu), Mr. Iron Abacus, Nei and Wai, and the Millelith squad.
### LARGE +
The wiki's trivia notes that Minamoto Kotomi stands, non-interactive, outside the wooden house during the delivery step; the dialogue itself speaks only of an old lady living there.

## consequences
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Each ending is a self-contained "Memory"; no ending changes the open-world story or any other quest. Rewards scale with how many endings the player has unlocked (Primogems, Adventure EXP, Hero's Wit and other items). The Guili route establishes that the Crux will give former Treasure Hoarders a path to honest work, and the Qingce route that villagers treat the fleet as an ally whose members come from their villages.

## placement
### STANDARD
Release order: 2.3 (hangout system introduced earlier in 1.4). Story order: set after the Traveler returns from Inazuma, so after the Archon Quest Omnipresence Over Mortals and after the Vision Hunt Decree has been abolished. Characters refer to the Traveler as the person who ended it and to the Liyue Harbor event as past.

## entry: Beidou's Childhood and the Name Beidou
@keys: Beidou's childhood, Downriver, Upriver
@keys2: Beidou, Crux, Alcor
@version: 2.3
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
In the Life on the High Seas photography route the Traveler brings Beidou to the empty fishing village near Wangshu Inn. She says it was once called Downriver because the villagers came from a place called Upriver, who taught her fishing. At five or six she chased a stray dog that had snatched half a rice bun; the dog collapsed and died, and fishermen fed her, and the village chief took her in. A year or two later the chief died, the catch fell, and the villagers called her a bringer of bad luck and drove her out, shouting about the Northern Dipper and death. She learned her name was a constellation, with the Alcor as an omen of death, only then. She recounts it without self-pity.

## entry: Golden Alligator
@keys: Golden Alligator
@version: 2.3
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
A Treasure Hoarder leader who is digging for treasure on Guili Plains when Beidou arrives. He panics at her name, begs mercy for his men, and is spared. He and his crew choose to leave banditry for port work offered by the Crux, and later share a poem clue to a second treasure. He wants to join the Crux someday; Beidou says nothing is impossible if they earn her crew's respect.

## entry: Mr. Iron Abacus and the Guyun Deal
@keys: Mr. Iron Abacus, Mora-Grubber
@version: 2.3
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Mr. Iron Abacus is the warehouse owner in Beidou's covert deal in Liyue Harbor. When the Millelith raid the meeting, they find only ordinary sacks, so Beidou's presence works as a decoy while her accountant Mora-Grubber runs the actual transaction. The event portrays the Crux as an armed carrier that also trades goods outside strict official channels.

## entry: Beidou's Philosophy of Favours
@keys: Beidou favours, trading favours
@keys2: Beidou, Crux
@version: 2.3
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
In Qingce Village, Beidou says she is not an arbiter like Ningguang or Yanfei; her strength is trading favours. She argues that favours keep people connected and form an invisible net that catches anyone who falls, and that these bonds, not law, are what the Crux has survived on. She admits some of her solutions are "stupid" but says the point is that nobody leaves feeling cheated.

## entry: Crux Training Staff
@keys: Yinxing, Huixing, Xu Liushi, Juza
@keys2: Crux, Beidou, Alcor
@version: 2.3
@spoiler: none
### STANDARD
Yinxing is the Crux's surgeon and has Beidou's full authority to keep unfit recruits ashore. Huixing is the fleet's navigator, deciphering old charts with books Beidou obtains. Xu Liushi is the lookout, once a laughingstock for a drunken fall into the sea, and credits Beidou's judgment for his place. Juza is the chief mate who schedules the training. Together they show that Beidou lets each person master a role so the fleet does not rest on her alone.
