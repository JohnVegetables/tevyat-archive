## summary
@spoiler: none
### LITE
Hangout Event: Gorou is a branching, player-choice side story added in version 2.3 (Act I, "The Canine General's Special Operations"). Gorou, general of the Watatsumi Island resistance, spends a day on Narukami Island with the Traveler. He is rattled by Yae Miko's teasing, secretly answers readers' letters for a magazine under the pen name "Ms. Hina", and wants to recover a buried box of fallen soldiers' belongings. The tone is light and warm with one quietly sad thread. Five endings are possible.
### STANDARD +
The event needs two Story Keys and first requires the Archon Quest "Omnipresence Over Mortals" and the story quest "Warriors' Dreams Like Spring Grass Renewing". Everything starts from one scene near Yae Publishing House and fans out into five branches, each closing on a one-line ending card. The branches are alternate versions of the same day, not a continuous chronology.
### LARGE +
Across branches the recurring beats are Gorou's discomfort with people who read him easily, his trust in the Traveler, his private worry about being a good leader, and post-war life on Watatsumi Island (soldiers moving on, the army slimmed down, closer contact with the Shogunate since the peace treaty).

## plot
### LITE
Near Yae Publishing House, Yae Miko teases Gorou about his tail until the Traveler steps in. Gorou admits he is out of his depth with her. He is in Inazuma to buy books for Kokomi, to see Shogunate-held lands, and to hand in his answers to the magazine "That's Life". From there the Traveler can help him train against Yae Miko, keep him company, or help find a buried box.
### STANDARD
The first choice splits the day. Telling Gorou to ignore Yae Miko leads either to the readers' letters (endings "The Smiling Send-Off" or "That's, Uh...") or to the box left on Nazuchi Beach (ending "Final Remembrance"). Offering to help him overcome his weakness leads to seeking advice or role-play training (endings "Tactical Retreat" and "Desserts Are the Best!").
### LARGE +
Branch "What Shall We Do?" - Gorou goes to Watatsumi Island and asks soldiers for advice "for a friend". Shuuhei advises going through the motions and not hanging on a talker's every word; Miwa advises hiding what he really thinks. A Yae Publishing House clerk says Yae Miko personally picks the novels and rescued the magazine "That's Life" with a marketing plan. At the Grand Narukami Shrine they draw fortune slips (results randomised per player) and confront Yae Miko. Gorou's last tactic is to claim she is just like him and secretly wants her tail groomed; she answers that she has no tail. Ending: "Tactical Retreat".

Branch "Special Training" - they role-play difficult people. A merchant in Ritou fails because Gorou knows nothing of prices (Kokomi has always handled procurement); Fatui impressions are cut short by a real Fatui attack. The Traveler imitates Yae Miko in Inazuma City, but what unsettles Gorou is her knowing smile and sense that she sees through him, which the trusted Traveler lacks. The training fails; they buy cake instead and Gorou happily shares it. Ending: "Desserts Are the Best!".

Branch "Secret Identity" - Gorou asks the Traveler to scout Yae Publishing House, where the Ms. Hina Fan Club is waiting for the magazine to unveil Ms. Hina's "official likeness". The Traveler realises Gorou is Ms. Hina and decides not to tell him. The fans say Gorou strongly resembles Ms. Hina and take photos with him; the Traveler stalls him with an excuse about Yae Miko returning. Ending: "That's, Uh...", with Gorou sensing the Traveler is hiding something.

Branch "A Friend's Path" - after answering the letters together (one is from a resistance soldier torn between staying on Watatsumi Island and visiting his Narukami Island home), Gorou worries about losing touch with comrades. Soldiers tell him they rarely meet but would return at once if the island were in danger. A soldier, Hiroaki, asks to leave the resistance, crediting "Ms. Hina's" encouragement, and Gorou approves. Ending: "The Smiling Send-Off".

Branch "Ordinary Memorial" - Katheryne explains that the box Gorou buried at Nazuchi Beach is held because the Adventurers' Guild recovered it while investigating Treasure Hoarders who stole from Komore Teahouse. Guided by Taroumaru, whom Gorou can understand, they find the thieves hidden beneath Tenshukaku and defeat them. The box is returned, holding keepsakes of soldiers who died in the battle. Ending: "Final Remembrance".
### ULTRA +
Further detail by branch:

- Opening: Aratani of Yae Publishing House tells Yae Miko that a skilled artist has been engaged for work she ordered; she says she will check it and display it in front of the store. She wishes Gorou and the Traveler a good time on Narukami Island, remarking that much has changed. Gorou explains his errand: buying books for Kokomi and seeing a Shogunate-ruled area now that the two sides are in closer contact.
- "Special Training": Gorou wishes he were taller and bulkier so soldiers could follow him more easily on the field. He says cake is a luxury in the resistance, where special meals mean meat and wine, not dessert. A 100,000-Mora slice briefly tempts him until the Traveler admits no such cake exists.
- "A Friend's Path": Gorou recalls Kazuha telling him that bonds between people whose paths crossed never break. He fears distance will grow after parting; the soldiers' answer settles it. He does not want Hiroaki to leave without saying goodbye.
- "Ordinary Memorial": Taroumaru tells Gorou the gang has five members, the leader is Kousuke and his second is Yasuhisa. Kousuke offers half of everything they have saved as a bribe; Gorou refuses, then notes this is not a military camp so he need not be so heavy-handed.
- "What Shall We Do?": the fortune slips produce four different reactions (Great Fortune, Good Fortune, Misfortune, Great Misfortune); on Great Misfortune Gorou briefly suspects Yae Miko rigged the draw.
- The event pays Adventure EXP plus Primogems or enhancement materials depending on the ending.

## revelations
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
- Gorou writes the advice column of Yae Publishing House's magazine "That's Life" as "Ms. Hina". Readers see a gentle older-sister figure and write to him as a friend. He enjoys it: people in the army are reserved with a general, but letters remove that distance.
- Yae Miko oversees marketing for the magazine and rescued it from cancellation; she remains the final approver for the shop's books.
- Gorou does not realise the fan club's Ms. Hina is himself; he is never told in this event.
- The box Gorou buried held soldiers' everyday keepsakes, left for their families because weapons and armour were broken or hard to carry.
### LARGE +
- Gorou can understand Taroumaru's barking, describing it as like a regional dialect: he catches the gist but not everything.
- Gorou finds Yae Miko hard to deal with because she towers over him, speaks cryptically and seems to see through him.

## characters
### STANDARD
Gorou is the lead; the Traveler is his confidant. Yae Miko (Guuji of the Grand Narukami Shrine and chief editor of Yae Publishing House) teases him in the opening scene. Also present, depending on branch: Aratani and Kuroda of Yae Publishing House; Shuuhei, Miwa and other soldiers on Watatsumi Island; the soldier Hiroaki; the Ms. Hina fan club (Nobuhiro, Hiroshi, Yuka); Katheryne; Kozue of Komore Teahouse; the dog Taroumaru; and the Treasure Hoarders Kousuke and Yasuhisa.

## consequences
### STANDARD
The event does not change the main story. Its value is characterisation: Gorou off the battlefield, shy, kind and a little overwhelmed by Inazuma's clever people.
### LARGE +
The "Smiling Send-Off" and "Final Remembrance" branches deepen his sense of responsibility for the soldiers who followed him; the "Tactical Retreat" and "Desserts" branches keep him comic and warm.

## placement
### STANDARD
Released in version 2.3. It is set after the Watatsumi Island war and the Archon Quest "Omnipresence Over Mortals", with Sangonomiya Kokomi governing and Gorou as her general. Watatsumi Island is at peace with the Shogunate, so Gorou can travel Narukami Island. No later-version content (6.x to 7.1) was found that revises the event.

## entry: Ms. Hina Advice Column
@keys: Ms. Hina, Miss Hina, That's Life magazine, Ms. Hina Fan Club
@keys2: Gorou
@version: 2.3
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
In Gorou's hangout, "That's Life" is a monthly Yae Publishing House magazine whose advice column is signed "Ms. Hina". Gorou writes it. The editors have staff copy each letter by hand and strike out names. Fans think of Ms. Hina as a gentle, understanding older sister who is weaker on romance questions, and a fan club formed. As recited by Nobuhiro, its rules are: do not pry into Ms. Hina's identity, make no controversial statements as club members, do not force the message on the uninterested, and keep improving oneself. Gorou has no idea the fans mean him.
### LARGE +
Sample replies show his style: urging a lovestruck reader to seize the moment as on a battlefield, advising a cold-struck reader on rest and warmth, telling a neighbour-debt writer to demand repayment (or, in the softer option, to borrow a little in return). To the resistance soldier torn about home he writes "Narukami Island has changed. You should go back and visit."

## entry: Nazuchi Beach Memorial Box
@keys: Gorou's box, Nazuchi Beach keepsakes, Taroumaru's Reserve Fund
@keys2: Gorou
@version: 2.3
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
Gorou's buried box held water bottles, towels, knee guards and similar ordinary items, each marked with its owner's name, left by soldiers who expected to die in the Nazuchi Beach battle. He buried it hastily during the retreat. The Adventurers' Guild dug it up while investigating the theft of Taroumaru's reserve fund from Komore Teahouse by a five-person Treasure Hoarder gang led by Kousuke, hiding below Tenshukaku. After Gorou and the Traveler catch them, Katheryne returns the box and Gorou takes it to Watatsumi Island for the families. He tells himself that next time he must be stronger.

## entry: Gorou and Yae Miko
@keys: Gorou's weakness, tail teasing, Yae Miko and Gorou
@keys2: Gorou, Yae Miko
@version: 2.3
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
Throughout the event Yae Miko teases Gorou about his tail and ears, clearly enjoying herself. Gorou's side is not fear of her power but discomfort at being read: her smile and unhurried gaze make him feel he has nowhere to hide. He says he can handle her in battle but not in everyday conversation. The Traveler's impersonation of her fails because he trusts the Traveler.
