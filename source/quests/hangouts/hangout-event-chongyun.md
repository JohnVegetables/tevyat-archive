## summary
@spoiler: minor
### LITE
Chongyun's Hangout Event, a replayable, choice-driven side story. The earnest young exorcist approaches the Traveler on Liyue Harbor's wharf asking whether they have seen any evil spirits. The one-act event, Signs of Evil, branches on whether the Traveler tells him his leads are far-fetched (popsicles, children and the Wanwen Bookhouse) or humours him (the Test of Courage at Wuwang Hill), and ends in one of six outcomes, none of which changes the wider story.
### STANDARD +
It was added in Version 1.4 with the Hangout Event feature and requires two Story Keys, Adventure Rank 26 and completion of the Archon Quest Song of the Dragon and Freedom. Its quests are Hard to Track, Distant Gaze, The Exorcist's Path, Popsicles and Their Curious Uses, For A Child's Smile, Test of Courage, The Bottom of Things, Sudden Discomfort, Ever Deeper, Killjoy and Independent Actions. The tone is gentle and comic: Chongyun takes everything seriously, and the humour comes from the gap between his earnestness and what the Traveler finds.
### LARGE +
Because Chongyun's pure-yang constitution (managed with cold popsicles) shapes the event, many beats revolve around keeping his yang energy in check. All endings are Memories that can be shown in the Serenitea Pot.

## plot
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
In Hard to Track Chongyun explains that he is an exorcist, that all demons and monsters are "evil spirits" to those who practise exorcism, and that he protects Liyue's people from them. He praises the yakshas and says exorcists and yakshas have long guarded Liyue together, one openly and one in secret. He then lists several clues he has gathered, including a flyer for the Test of Courage at Wuwang Hill. The Traveler's choice here forks the event.

**Branch one: A Curious Gaze.** If the Traveler says the clues sound far-fetched, Chongyun grows dejected, his pure-yang energy heats him, and he eats a popsicle to cool down. A boy, Dalong, watches them. The boy admires the popsicle, saying he and his sister are poor and rarely eat snacks. Giving the boy one popsicle sends him off happy and the pair visit Wanwen Bookhouse to check Chongyun's clues (given by his friend Xingqiu), find them unreliable, and then follow a last snippet to Wuwang Hill. There they find strange smoke Chongyun says an evil spirit left, fight the hilichurls around it and cannot catch the spirit, but he counts the trip a success. Ending: Blessing in Disguise. Giving the boy both popsicles instead leaves Chongyun short; the Traveler helps him make more at Wanmin Restaurant. A cooling mix or sweet mix works. A "curious" mix with spicy ingredients sends Chongyun haywire and ends the event: Can't Beat This Heat. With good popsicles, they take some to Dalong and his sister Little Le, play hide-and-seek and share cold noodles. Ending: Idle Amusements.

**Branch two: To Take a Test of Courage.** If the Traveler humours him, they join the Test of Courage at Wuwang Hill, where couples are being frightened by staged scares. The staff (run by Zhouliang, with Fu San'er) are annoyed by Chongyun's questions. Drinking a potion can leave Chongyun suffering from unstable yang energy, requiring cooling recipes (Cryo Slime condensate or Cryo Whopperflower nectar) in Sudden Discomfort. In The Bottom of Things or Ever Deeper the Traveler learns the "ghost" is a staff member in costume, and in Killjoy must decide how to tell Chongyun. Telling him ends the route at Nothing Gained or Small Regrets, depending on the path.
### LARGE +
**Independent Actions.** If, after being told it was all staged, the Traveler asks to investigate the strange mist found earlier, they split up. A girl ghost named Qianqian asks the Traveler to lead Chongyun away because his yang energy frightens her; she offers to scare off the troublemakers if he lets her go. While Chongyun argues with the staff about whether they are using ghosts for profit or running a scam, panic breaks out and the staff leave. Chongyun learns there is no evil spirit but a wandering ghost who scared the crowd to save him, and decides, if she harms no one, to pretend he never knew about her, stating that he will purge her if she ever does. Ending: Goodbyes.

### ULTRA +
**Quest tree (for reference).** Hard to Track forks into Distant Gaze (the far-fetched-clues branch) or Test of Courage. Distant Gaze leads to The Exorcist's Path (Wanwen Bookhouse, then Wuwang Hill, ending Blessing in Disguise) or to Popsicles and Their Curious Uses (mix choice; the curious mix ends at Can't Beat This Heat, the others go to For A Child's Smile and Idle Amusements). Test of Courage leads to The Bottom of Things (ending Nothing Gained), Sudden Discomfort (the Cryo recipes, which then continue to Killjoy) or Ever Deeper, which also goes to Killjoy. Killjoy ends at Small Regrets if the Traveler suggests heading back, or continues to Independent Actions (ending Goodbyes) if they suggest investigating the mist.

**Rewards.** Each ending gives Adventure EXP plus a rotating prize: Primogems, Hero's Wit, Guide to Diligence, Shivada Jade Fragments or Cold Noodles with Mountain Delicacies (Chongyun's special dish).

## revelations
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
The event establishes that Chongyun regards exorcist work as an ordinary job that simply must be done, that he respects the yakshas, and that in the Qianqian ending he weighs good and evil in each spirit rather than treating every ghost as an enemy. It also reaffirms that his pure-yang energy needs regular cooling to stay under control.
### LARGE +
Qianqian is the ghost of a young child and is not shown to be malevolent; Chongyun's decision to spare her on his own responsibility is the closest the event comes to a character statement. The "Blessing in Disguise" ending carries his line that the sights and encounters along the way are as much a reward as catching a spirit.

## characters
@spoiler: none
### STANDARD
Chongyun leads; the Traveler is the player guide. Dalong and Little Le are siblings, young children who say they are poor; Dalong hurries off to Chihu Rock with his popsicle. Zhouliang and Fu San'er run the Test of Courage at Wuwang Hill, with other staff and visitors (Uncle Jia, Lu Zheng, Dandan, Junjun, Chan'er) appearing in the scares. Xingqiu is mentioned as the friend who passed Chongyun the rumours.

## consequences
@spoiler: none
### STANDARD
No ending changes any other quest or the wider story. Rewards scale with the number of endings unlocked; the six endings are Blessing in Disguise, Idle Amusements, Can't Beat This Heat, Nothing Gained, Goodbyes and Small Regrets.

## placement
@spoiler: none
### STANDARD
Release order: Version 1.4, alongside the hangout system itself. Story order: it requires the Liyue Archon Quest Song of the Dragon and Freedom, so it takes place after Rex Lapis' apparent death and the Traveler's Liyue adventure. Nothing in the event depends on later story.

## entry: Test of Courage at Wuwang Hill
@keys: Test of Courage, Wuwang Hill
@keys2: Chongyun, exorcist, ghost
@version: 1.4
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
A staged "haunted" attraction at Wuwang Hill where visitors, often couples, are frightened by staff in costume. Chongyun hopes it hides a real evil spirit. The staff, led by Zhouliang, treat it as entertainment and resent being questioned; a real ghost, Qianqian, is present in one branch.

## entry: Qianqian
@keys: Qianqian
@keys2: Chongyun, Test of Courage
@version: 1.4
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
The ghost of a young child at Wuwang Hill. She fears Chongyun's pure-yang energy, which she believes could make her disappear, and she asks the Traveler to lead him away. After scaring off the staff, she is spared by Chongyun on the condition that she never harms anyone.

## entry: Dalong and Little Le
@keys: Dalong, Little Le

### STANDARD
Dalong is a boy who approaches Chongyun and the Traveler hoping for a popsicle for himself and his sister Little Le; the siblings say they are poor and seldom get snacks. In the best path Chongyun plays hide-and-seek with them and serves cold noodles, and the children invite him to play more.

## entry: Chongyun's Popsicles
@keys: Chongyun popsicle, pure-yang spirit
@keys2: Chongyun
@version: 1.4
@spoiler: none
### STANDARD
Chongyun keeps popsicles on hand to suppress his pure-yang constitution, which makes him run hot and can destabilise when he is stressed. The event lets the Traveler make cooling or sweet versions at Wanmin Restaurant from Mist Flowers and Qingxin, or Valberries and Sunsettias, while a spicy "curious" version backfires.
