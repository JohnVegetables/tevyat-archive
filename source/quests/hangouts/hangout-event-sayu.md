## summary
@spoiler: none
### LITE
Hangout Event: Sayu is a branching, player-choice side story added in version 2.2 (Act I, "Yoohoo Art: Seichou no Jutsu"). Sayu, a young ninja of the Shuumatsuban who would rather nap than work, tells the Traveler she has been given a mission to ambush them, though she has no intention of doing it. Together they dodge her shrine-maiden supervisor Kano Nana, try to help Sayu grow taller, and uncover who forged the order. The tone is gentle and funny, with a tender thread about Sayu's absent sensei and her fear of disappointing people. Six endings are possible.
### STANDARD +
The event needs two Story Keys and first requires the Archon Quest "Omnipresence Over Mortals". The branches are alternate versions of the same few days, not a single timeline. Throughout, Sayu is portrayed factually as a child-like, sleepy, earnest ninja: she is anxious about her height, hides in disguises, and wants to be useful to someone she trusts.
### LARGE +
The main emotional line is that Sayu feels everyone has hopes for her (Kano Nana, Kamisato Ayaka, her sensei) and fears she cannot meet them, so she retreats into sleep. The Traveler is allowed to treat her as a friend and a kind of stand-in for her sensei.

## plot
### LITE
The Traveler sees Sayu disguised as a barrel in Inazuma City. She admits that she was ordered to ambush the Traveler and asks to be taken to the shrine maiden Kano Nana, who supervises her, so she can ask about the order. Kano Nana says Sayu is too young for such tasks and takes her back to Chinju Forest for training. Sayu secretly asks the Traveler to come along and help her avoid work.
### STANDARD
At Chinju Forest Sayu explains her grievance: she sleeps constantly to grow taller, yet has not grown, and the shrine maiden keeps assigning chores. The Traveler can tell her sleep alone will not help, advise on quality of sleep, or urge her not to neglect her work. The first two lead to hiding and eating schemes; the third leads to a day of ninjutsu training. In parallel the forged-order mystery resolves in one of two ways, depending on whether the Traveler thanks Kano Nana and drops the matter or keeps investigating.
### LARGE +
Branch "Bottleneck Breaking" - Sayu tries to grow taller by eating. At Kiminami Restaurant she orders a large meal and struggles to finish it. Pushing on ("Hard Work Pays Off") leaves her ill after eating at Shimura's, until Kano Nana's voice makes her bolt and she falls; ending "Everything in Moderation". Instead, the Traveler suggests exercise after eating ("Exercise Follows Eating"); they chase Treasure Hoarders for Kiminami Anna, but a tall hoarder's hand lands on Sayu's head, which she considers disastrous for her growth. The Traveler takes her to a high place to comfort her, where she imagines being tall enough to pick fruit and swim in deep water. Ending: "Like Soaring Through the Skies".

Branch "Sleep Alone Can't Wait" - Sayu looks for better napping places. Her usual spot is overrun with Pyro Slimes; her backup spot has caltrop-like training devices set by the Shuumatsuban, which she and the Traveler clear away so kitsune are not hurt. She decides the safest place is the sakura tree at the Grand Narukami Shrine and sneaks in with the Traveler despite the Yashiro Commission staff there. Kano Nana nonetheless tracks her there, and Sayu dashes off. Ending: "Definitely No Work Today!".

Branch "Operation Substitution" - Kano Nana gives agility and live-combat ninjutsu training; Sayu demonstrates, then offers to teach the Traveler her "Twinjutsu". On a break by a tree with a rope marking her height from when her sensei left, Sayu reflects on how a tree grows, blooms and gives shade, and wonders whether she can fulfil everyone's hopes if she never grows tall. She brings the Traveler fruit and says they remind her of her sensei. Ending: "With the Trees".

Branch "Shuumatsuban Suspicions" - If the Traveler instead continues investigating the order, Sayu arranges a meeting with the Yashiro Commission's order courier, Ogawa. Ogawa believes Sayu completed the ambush; then the Traveler appears and he admits forging the order because the Kairagi were holding his daughter Kotomi hostage. The Traveler can have Sayu hold him and bring him to Kamisato Ayaka, who opens an investigation (ending "Little Sayu's Troubles"), or help him: they decoy the Kairagi, Sayu swaps places with Kotomi, the Tenryou Commission arrests Inou, and Kotomi calls Sayu "Ms. Ninja" (ending "The Best Rest").
### ULTRA +
Further detail:

- Opening: Sayu says she is a member of the Shuumatsuban, whose job includes handling matters that are "inconvenient" to superiors, "which sometimes includes ambushing people", and that she has been assigned such a task even though she is never given them. She blames the order for disrupting her sleep.
- Kano Nana tells the Traveler she has heard of them from Kamisato Ayaka. She says Sayu is "extremely loyal" and neglects duties but would never act presumptuously; "given her age", serious tasks are not assigned to her. She is exasperated by Sayu's habit of copying other people's phrasing.
- The Traveler can find the forged order's origin quickly by pretending the ambush succeeded: Sayu bags "evidence", sends it through the reporting crate and receives a new meeting place from Ogawa.
- In "Operation Substitution" the agility course gives rating lines based on how quickly the Traveler finishes (a fast mujina, an ordinary kitsune, or a slow turtle), recalling her sensei's scoring.
- Sayu says her sensei measured her height on a tree and tied a rope there; she feels the rope keeps rising with the tree. She did not ask her sensei's name, and cannot say how tall they were beyond being taller than her.
- "Shuumatsuban Suspicions" (Face-Off): Ayaka tells the Traveler that Sayu's sensei asked her to watch over Sayu, which is why she assigned Kano Nana. She says Sayu has been lost since the sensei's sudden departure, and that she wants to help but suspects Sayu keeps her at a distance. Sayu tells the Traveler she dislikes visiting Ayaka because the expectations press harder there.
- Rewards: Adventure EXP plus Primogems or enhancement materials depending on the ending.

## revelations
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
- Sayu is a member of the Shuumatsuban, the ninja corps that handles tasks for the Yashiro Commission, usually assigned errands such as carrying documents and organising tools rather than missions.
- Kano Nana is the Grand Narukami Shrine's shrine maiden and acts as Sayu's supervisor; Ayaka says Sayu's sensei asked her to watch over Sayu's progress, which is partly why she assigned Kano Nana.
- Sayu's sensei taught her ninjutsu and left suddenly; she never learned their name and is unsure what they were.
- The ambush order was forged by Ogawa, a Yashiro Commission courier, under coercion from the Kairagi who held his daughter.
### LARGE +
- Sayu can transform into a ball and prefers to avoid fights, but is shown to be skilled in combat and stealth, and clever enough to stage a swap with the hostage.
- The Shuumatsuban's training devices are not marked for outsiders and endanger forest wildlife until Sayu and the Traveler clear them.

## characters
### STANDARD
Sayu is the lead; the Traveler is her helper and eventual confidant. Kano Nana, the shrine maiden of the Grand Narukami Shrine, is her supervisor. Kamisato Ayaka appears in the "Shuumatsuban Suspicions" Face-Off scene. Ogawa (Yashiro Commission courier), his daughter Kotomi, the Kairagi leader Inou, Tenryou Commission member Aoki, and Kiminami Anna of Kiminami Restaurant also appear.

## consequences
### STANDARD
The event does not change the main story. It deepens Sayu's characterisation: she is sleepy and avoidant but also caring, and moved by a child's thanks.
### LARGE +
The rescue branches end with Sayu willing to help the Traveler with future work and with her wanting to be reachable; the troubled branches end with her struggling to cope with the weight of expectation.

## placement
### STANDARD
Released in version 2.2. It is set after the Archon Quest "Omnipresence Over Mortals" and the Watatsumi war, with the Yashiro Commission and Shuumatsuban in normal operation. No content from 6.x to 7.1 was found that revises the event.

## entry: Sayu's Sensei
@keys: Sayu's sensei, Sayu's teacher, height rope tree
@keys2: Sayu
@version: 2.2
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
Sayu's sensei taught her ninjutsu, was patient and strong, and never pushed her to do more work. They measured her height on a tree with a rope marker and told her nothing is more satisfying than growing. They left suddenly for "important matters" and asked Ayaka to watch over Sayu. Sayu never asked their name and only half-remembers whether they described themselves as a ninja. She says the Traveler reminds her of them.

## entry: The Forged Ambush Order
@keys: forged ambush order, ambush the Traveler order, Ogawa Kotomi Inou
@keys2: Sayu, Shuumatsuban
@version: 2.2
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
Ogawa, a Yashiro Commission official responsible for delivering orders to the Shuumatsuban, was forced by the Kairagi Inou to forge a Commission order for an ambush of the Traveler; his daughter Kotomi was taken. He assigned it to Sayu because she never completes confrontational jobs and Kano Nana oversees her. When the Traveler learns of the order, Kano Nana checks with the Yashiro Commission and confirms it did not come from them. In the helpful branch the Traveler and Sayu stage a fake ambush report, Sayu swaps places with the hostage, and the Tenryou Commission arrests Inou.

## entry: Kano Nana and Sayu
@keys: Kano Nana
@keys2: Sayu
@version: 2.2
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
Kano Nana is the shrine maiden of the Grand Narukami Shrine who also takes work from the Shuumatsuban. Sayu regards her as a "bane" because she always finds Sayu's napping places and turns them into training grounds. Kano Nana is stern but dutiful, protective of the Traveler's safety and cautiously proud of Sayu's real skills.
