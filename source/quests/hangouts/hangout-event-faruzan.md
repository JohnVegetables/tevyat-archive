## summary
### LITE
Faruzan's Hangout Event, a single-act, branching side story added in Version 3.5. Tighnari asks the Traveler to help bring the veteran Haravatat researcher Faruzan to Pardis Dhyai for help with banned research, and the Traveler finds her cornered by Akademiya administrators over her lack of teaching or collaboration. Depending on the Traveler's choices the story becomes a reading course for Collei, a children's-toy collaboration with Kshahrewar and Acara Crafts, or a trip into the desert about a machine called the Ruin Wanderer, where Faruzan is reunited with an old companion. There are five endings.
### STANDARD +
The act is A Confounding Conundrum. It needs Adventure Rank 40, two Story Keys, and completion of the Archon Quest Akasha Pulses, the Kalpa Flame Rises and the story quest The Unanswerable Problems. It opens with A Mysterious Senior...? and then splits into three lines: Essential Classic, Early Learning Mechanism and Wanderings of a Lonesome Shadow.
### LARGE +
It is where the player sees Faruzan's everyday friction with the present-day Akademiya, her relationship with Collei, and the fate of the machine she left behind a century earlier. As a Hangout Event it is permanent content, not a limited-time event.

## plot
### STANDARD
Tighnari is waiting at Pardis Dhyai for Faruzan and sends Collei, who is nervous around her, with the Traveler to Sumeru City to find out why she is late. They find Faruzan being pressed by an Akademiya official, Nadwah, to either teach a course or join a collaborative project, because Haravatat students and other advisers have complained that she does neither. She puts the matter off, and the Traveler has a private word with Collei.

The player then chooses how to help. Either they help settle her Akademiya problem first, choosing between a Haravatat course and a Kshahrewar collaboration, or they find a pretext to take her to Pardis Dhyai. Helping with the course leads to Essential Classic, in which Faruzan founds a reading course for Collei as a public pilot, hunts for teaching material and learns how much taste in books has changed. Helping with the collaboration leads to Early Learning Mechanism, in which she designs a puzzle toy and a reference book for Acara Crafts. Taking the pretext route leads to Wanderings of a Lonesome Shadow, in which she repairs Collei's companion Karkata and is drawn into a desert mystery. Every route ends with a short ending scene; the five endings are Stories of the Past, Knowledge is Wealth!, The Gardener and the Nursery, Start Over, and "Welcome Back, Faruzan."
### LARGE +
Nadwah explains the situation: Kshahrewar and Acara Crafts want Faruzan on a collaboration, and the other option is to teach a course; if she does neither the complaints may reach the sages. Faruzan suspects the complaints come from Haravatat colleagues and calls the metric unfair. She insists on being addressed as "Madam" and dislikes being called young, and Tighnari warns the Traveler of this beforehand. Collei explains that Faruzan's body stopped ageing while she was gone, so she looks as she did a hundred years ago.

If the Traveler asks about the Haravatat course, Faruzan describes her field as reading the carvings and documents of ruins to interpret their mechanisms; Nadwah says few students follow it and unexplored ruins are running out. The Traveler suggests teaching Collei to read, and that the course be public rather than held only for Akademiya students. Faruzan agrees, because the old Akademiya ignored the public, and goes with Nadwah to file the paperwork. In Essential Classic the group fills in the application with her, then searches Treasures Street for a text. Faruzan dislikes a modern light novel, tries to find a century-old series called Tales of Shahryar, finds a single stray volume in an antique shop, and learns from the storyteller Maddah that the series faded after its author kept extending it. Collei's liking for modern books leads Faruzan to choose a book Collei enjoys as the teaching material. This line ends with Stories of the Past.

If the Traveler asks about the Kshahrewar collaboration, Nadwah explains that the request comes from Acara Crafts and that a Kshahrewar representative, Anis, recommended Faruzan out of respect. In Early Learning Mechanism the group meets Anis and the shop owner Mamdouh at Port Ormos. Faruzan makes a paper prototype of a stacking puzzle called Pagoda Stack, and volunteer children play it; the minimum number of moves for seven layers is 127. At a launch conference she presents a reference book. If the Traveler urges a detailed, accurate book, the child audience is bored, but Kamal, Branch Master of Sumeru's Adventurers' Guild, offers an order of models and booklets for adventurers who cannot read, and the line ends in Knowledge is Wealth! If the Traveler urges a simple, playful book, the children are delighted and one says they want to build mechanisms; Faruzan realises with some dismay that this may not lead them to Haravatat, and the line ends in The Gardener and the Nursery.

If the Traveler chooses the pretext route, Faruzan claims that Tighnari wants her help with a fully automated irrigation system, which Nadwah accepts for the moment, and the group goes to Pardis Dhyai. There Tighnari shows a malfunctioning Karkata, a mechanical life form that Collei is fond of. Faruzan diagnoses a damaged Restitution Module and needs replacement parts, which sends her and the Traveler to the desert, where villagers speak of a "Ruin Wanderer" and an archaeologist named Raed keeps people away from his dig. Faruzan suspects a machine wreckage in the camp is the Wanderer and that Raed has swapped wreckage to deceive the Akademiya. At night the Wanderer attacks the camp; Faruzan recognises it as Tamimi, a machine from her own past, remodelled by her teacher and others so that it could search for her. It carries their last letters to her. Raed is exposed by his own logbook. Back at Pardis Dhyai the Traveler decides how much to tell Tighnari and Collei, which leads to either Start Over (the whole truth is shared) or "Welcome Back, Faruzan." (the Traveler keeps Tamimi a secret and Faruzan thanks them for it).
### ULTRA +
Smaller details. In Essential Classic Faruzan reads a modern light novel on sale at Treasures Street and finds it all fights and simple sentences; the bookseller is Farruq and the antique seller who has the single Tales of Shahryar volume is Derafsh. In Early Learning Mechanism the volunteer children include Qasit, Ruhi and Alia, and Mamdouh rewards them with Genius Invokation TCG cards. In Wanderings of a Lonesome Shadow Faruzan and the Traveler question villagers evacuated from Aaru Village, guarded by Raed's man Bakr, and learn that the Ruin Wanderer has been rumoured to be centuries old, which she rejects because she heard nothing of it a century ago. Ending rewards, in the wiki's order, vary: Stories of the Past and "Welcome Back, Faruzan." give Primogems and Guide to Admonition; Knowledge is Wealth! and Start Over give Hero's Wit and Vayuda Turquoise Fragments; The Gardener and the Nursery gives Primogems and Traditionally-Made Charcoal-Baked Ajilenakh Cake.

## revelations
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Faruzan's old machine Tamimi spent roughly a hundred years searching for her until its Restitution Module wore out, carrying letters from her teacher and family. Raed, who leads the desert expedition, passed off other wreckage as the "Ruin Wanderer" in an attempt to mislead the Akademiya.
### LARGE +
The letters let Faruzan remember her family and teacher clearly again and learn that they never gave up on her. She says she cannot repair Tamimi with her present abilities and that the replacement parts are better used to extend Karkata's life; she intends to revive Tamimi once she can reproduce the ancient creations.

## characters
### STANDARD
Faruzan is proud, insists on "Madam", is affectionate toward Collei, scornful of the present Akademiya's priorities but respectful of Kshahrewar's methods. Collei is anxious around her but trusts the Traveler. Tighnari is the quiet organiser of the real task. Nadwah, Anis, Mamdouh, Maddah, Kamal and Raed are minor figures of the individual branches. Karkata and Tamimi are the machines.

## consequences
### STANDARD
No branch changes Teyvat's main story. The event settles Faruzan's standing in the Akademiya for the player (course, collaboration or neither), shows her bond with the Traveler, and leaves Karkata repaired with parts from the desert. Whether Tighnari and Collei learn of Tamimi depends on the ending.

## placement
### STANDARD
Released in Version 3.5. It requires the Sumeru Archon Quest Akasha Pulses, the Kalpa Flame Rises and is therefore set after Sumeru's main story. Describing Faruzan generally should not assume any single ending.

## entry: Tamimi
@keys: Tamimi, Ruin Wanderer
@version: 3.5
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Tamimi is a machine from Faruzan's past. Her teacher and friends remodelled its Self-Repair function into Self-Learning so it could search for her and deliver letters. In the desert it was known as the Ruin Wanderer. After roughly a hundred years its Restitution Module was failing. Faruzan cannot yet repair it and uses the replacement parts for Karkata instead, promising to revive it later.

## entry: Pagoda Stack
@keys: Pagoda Stack
@version: 3.5
### STANDARD
A puzzle toy devised by Faruzan for Acara Crafts: rings of different sizes are moved one at a time between poles and a larger ring may not sit on a smaller one. It follows the Tower of Hanoi puzzle. Seven layers need 127 moves.

## entry: Tales of Shahryar
@keys: Tales of Shahryar
@version: 3.5
### STANDARD
A popular series from a hundred years ago, written by a Vahumana researcher who studied folk legends. Faruzan remembers it as a classic and cannot find it now; the storyteller Maddah says its author kept extending it instead of writing an ending and abandoned it. The more lasting Legend of Antara was the same author's original draft.
