## summary
### LITE
Hangout Event: Barbara is a replayable, choice-driven story for Barbara, the Favonius Cathedral deaconess, added in Version 1.4. It has one act, Wellspring of Healing. The Traveler is asked to find Barbara, helps her avoid an overeager fan, tidies the Cathedral and shares a drink with her. It is lighthearted, low-stakes and has no effect on the main story.
### STANDARD +
Players need 2 Story Keys, Adventure Rank 26 and the Archon Quest Song of the Dragon and Freedom to start it. Different choices lead to five named endings. It is permanent, repeatable content, not a limited-time event.
### LARGE +
The act is built around Barbara's popularity as a public figure in Mondstadt. Sister Victoria's concern at the start frames the day as the Traveler looking out for Barbara.
### ULTRA +
Some Hangout Events use a Heartbeat Value that can end the quest if it empties. In this act the notable hidden scoring is in the conversation with Albert (the quest Sudden Shouting), where small answers decide which of two branches follows.

## plot
### LITE
Sister Victoria is worried about Barbara's whereabouts and asks the Traveler to find her. Barbara is picking red Wolfhooks in Wolvendom for Tunner when Albert, the leader of the Barbara Fan Club, comes looking for her. The Traveler either drives him off or leads Barbara away. Later Barbara asks what the Traveler wants to drink: her home-made Chilibrew or the new drink at The Cat's Tail.
### STANDARD
After Victoria's request, the Traveler finds Barbara in Wolvendom. Hearing Albert approach, Barbara does not want to deal with him yet. The Traveler can chase Albert away, which is decided by a hidden score from the conversation. If it goes badly, Albert keeps searching and the day ends on an anticlimax (Untimely Enthusiasm). Otherwise Barbara goes to check on Tunner while the Traveler returns to the Cathedral, where Victoria asks them to clean it for Barbara.
Barbara then returns and offers the choice of drink. If the Traveler picks the new drink at The Cat's Tail, Barbara goes ahead and is swamped by a group of fans. Persuading each fan to leave her alone, or failing to, still ends with the two relaxing over drinks (A Holiday in Mondstadt).
### LARGE +
If the Traveler picks the home-made Chilibrew instead, they must gather ingredients: Jueyun Chili and Sweet Flowers. The two can split up or stay together.
- Split up: the Traveler picks Jueyun Chili near Stone Gate while Barbara gathers Sweet Flowers in Whispering Woods. They meet in the Woods, where Barbara says she has been trailing Treasure Hoarders who were harassing a merchant. After they rescue Caishan, the merchant, he offers a Liyue Chilibrew or Sweet Flowers in exchange for Barbara's own recipe; the Liyue Chilibrew route leads to A Fiery Flavor From Liyue.
- Stay together: in Whispering Woods they meet a wounded man named Uwe. If the Traveler helps Barbara heal him on the spot, he is grateful, and Barbara can make her home-made Chilibrew (The Taste of Hard Work). If they instead take him to the Knights of Favonius, he tries to escape because he is a Treasure Hoarder; the Traveler defeats him and the day ends on Incompletely Cured.
### ULTRA +
The wiki's quest list for the act names: Sister Victoria's Vexation, The Church's Affairs, The First Chilibrew Ingredient, Knight and Deaconess, Ready for Battle!, The Second Chilibrew Ingredient, Healing Is My Job, Ulterior Motive...?, A Very Special Beverage and Sudden Shouting. A side branch, Temporary Relief From the Fanfare, covers the choice to wait out Albert deeper in the forest, where Barbara's reasons for joining the Church or picking herbs alone can come up in conversation (Of Wolfhooks Red). A separate answer there ends the event early.

## revelations
### STANDARD
The act reveals very little lore. It is character material: Barbara is well known and sought out by admirers, and the Church sisters worry when she is away for long. It also shows that Wolfhooks from Wolvendom, Sweet Flowers from Whispering Woods and Jueyun Chili from Liyue are what go into her Chilibrew.
### LARGE +
It also shows Treasure Hoarders operating in the Whispering Woods, near Mondstadt, and a Hoarder pretending to be an injured traveler.

## characters
### LITE
Barbara, the Traveler and Paimon are the main characters. Sister Victoria, Albert (leader of the Barbara Fan Club) and Tunner appear early on.
### STANDARD +
Branch characters include the merchant Caishan, the wounded Uwe (a Treasure Hoarder), and fans at The Cat's Tail named Herman, Schulz and Susie.

## consequences
### STANDARD
The Hangout Event has no lasting effect on the main story. Completing endings grants rewards (Adventure EXP, Primogems, Hero's Wit, talent or ascension materials and Barbara's special dish), and endings can be displayed as Lingering Moments in the Serenitea Pot mansion.
### LARGE +
Hangout Events also award Memories of the Heart achievements, generally one for unlocking all endings. Each ending gives a different reward set, so replaying is expected.

## placement
### STANDARD
Released in Version 1.4. It unlocks after Song of the Dragon and Freedom but sits outside the main-story timeline: a day in Mondstadt with no dated events.
### LARGE +
Its structure (named endings, branching choices, rewards by number of endings reached) is shared with every other Hangout Event.

## entry: Barbara Fan Club
@keys: Barbara Fan Club, Barbara-sama
@version: 1.4
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
In Barbara's Hangout Event, Albert leads the Barbara Fan Club, a group of enthusiastic admirers who look for Barbara around Mondstadt. Barbara does not want to meet Albert in Wolvendom, so the Traveler either sends him away or leads her off. Later, fans at The Cat's Tail crowd Barbara until the Traveler persuades them to leave her in peace. They are portrayed as harmless and over-eager.
### LARGE +
Albert's outcome depends on a hidden score from the Traveler's answers: a score of zero or higher leaves him still searching (Untimely Enthusiasm), while a negative one makes him more willing to be reasoned with.

## entry: Barbara's Chilibrew
@keys: Chilibrew, Liyue Chilibrew, Home-Made Chilibrew
@keys2: Barbara
@version: 1.4
@availability: permanent
### STANDARD
Chilibrew is the drink at the center of Barbara's Hangout Event. Her home-made version needs Jueyun Chili and Sweet Flowers, which the Traveler and Barbara collect around Stone Gate and Whispering Woods. On another route the merchant Caishan gives them a Liyue Chilibrew in exchange. The ending for the home-made brew is The Taste of Hard Work, and the one for the Liyue version is A Fiery Flavor From Liyue.

## entry: Barbara Hangout Endings
@keys: Untimely Enthusiasm, Incompletely Cured, A Holiday in Mondstadt, A Fiery Flavor From Liyue, The Taste of Hard Work
@version: 1.4
@availability: permanent
### LARGE
The five endings are: Untimely Enthusiasm (Albert keeps searching for Barbara after a failed conversation), A Fiery Flavor From Liyue (Caishan's Liyue Chilibrew), The Taste of Hard Work (home-made Chilibrew), Incompletely Cured (Uwe is revealed to be a Treasure Hoarder after being brought to the Knights) and A Holiday in Mondstadt (drinks at The Cat's Tail after dealing with the fans).
