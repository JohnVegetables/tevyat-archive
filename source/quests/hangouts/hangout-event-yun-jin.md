## summary
@spoiler: minor
### LITE
Yun Jin's Hangout Event, a replayable, choice-driven side story. Days before a major performance, the star of the Yun-Han Opera Troupe vanishes to a dangerous spot in search of inspiration, and the Traveler brings her back. The one-act event, A Song That Knows Grace, then branches: a thank-you night that ends in a tangle with fake reporters or in a relaxed meal and a photograph, or a crash course in acting from Yun Jin herself. There are five endings.
### STANDARD +
It was added in Version 2.4 and requires two Story Keys, Adventure Rank 28 and completion of The Crane Returns on the Wind. Its quests are Countryside Wandering (hub), After the Curtain Falls, Backstage Suspicions, Half a Day's Leisure and First Glimpse of Meaning. The title is a partial quotation of a Chinese saying about hearing a stringed song and understanding the deeper meaning, associated with the friendship of Bo Ya and Zhong Ziqi, according to the wiki's trivia.
### LARGE +
Hangout Events are permanent, replayable content, and each ending is a Memory that can be displayed in the Serenitea Pot. The event gives a close look at Yun Jin's craft, her anxiety about her public image, and her habit of using theatrical skill to solve real problems.

## plot
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
In Countryside Wandering the Adventurers' Guild's Katheryne introduces Yunning, manager of the Yun-Han Opera Troupe, who is frantic: Yun Jin has left a note saying she has gone to Lingju Pass to find inspiration for the "Divine Maiden" play. The Traveler finds her there, defeats a Ruin Hunter threatening her, and learns why she went. Yun Jin was inspired by Shenhe's remarks about the divine damsel, whose real behaviour was less heroic than the opera shows, and wanted to feel genuine danger to deepen her portrayal. She admits she was close to a breakthrough and does not promise to stop, but returns. At the Guild, Yunning begs the Traveler to talk her out of dangerous practice. The Traveler's answer forks the event.

**Agree with Yunning (safety first).** Yun Jin promises to rehearse in safety and invites the Traveler to meet at the Heyu Tea House after her show. This leads to After the Curtain Falls.

**Side with Yun Jin.** She is delighted. Yunning, panicking, asks the Traveler to keep her busy until the next show, and when the Traveler is cast as a would-be student, Yun Jin agrees to give informal lessons, meeting at the bridge at Feiyun Slope. This leads to First Glimpse of Meaning.
### LARGE +
**After the Curtain Falls.** After her performance, Yun Jin meets the Traveler. Two admirers, Maksim and Gentry Maocai, invite her to dinner; she declines, citing her plans with the Traveler. At the tea house two people who say they are from The Steambird (the editor Boyi and journalist Shiyun) corner her with questions that drift from theatre to her private life, to Snezhnaya and the Fatui. The Traveler either urges her to take the interview (Backstage Suspicions) or advises her to book it through her manager (Half a Day's Leisure).

**Backstage Suspicions.** Yun Jin senses something wrong and asks the Traveler to follow the pair. Shiyun and Boyi are quarrelling: Boyi is a fake editor planning a sensational piece, and Shiyun, bound by a harsh contract with ruinous penalty clauses, is reluctant. Failing the stealth section ends the event at A Shame That the Road Is Rocky. Succeeding, the Traveler delivers a letter and returns with Shiyun to the tea house, where Yun Jin plays along with the interview, then bluffs about the Millelith and legal scrutiny so that Boyi flees back to his headquarters, leading Yunning and the Millelith to him. Yun Jin invites Shiyun to submit an article to the real Steambird. Ending: And Few Friends You Shall Find.

**Half a Day's Leisure.** Declining the interview frees the evening. At Chihu Rock Yun Jin takes the Traveler to Chen the Sharp's barbecue stall for Grilled Tiger Fish and plays a "hot pot" guessing game with mixed leftovers of experimental drinks. She tells the Traveler she is not the same offstage as on, and the Traveler can say her offstage self is the cuter one (Commemorative Photo at Chen the Sharp's stall, then a photo at Feiyun Slope; ending Brought Together by Common Interests) or prefer the stage persona (she hands over a signed publicity picture; ending A Souvenir Without Sentiment Is of Little Worth).

**First Glimpse of Meaning.** Yun Jin teaches three acting methods, each tested with a scene from the Traveler's fight at the Jade Chamber: Immersive Performance (re-live the emotion; the Traveler fails to convey it), Well-Honed Performance (use familiar experience in place of unfamiliar events) and Meticulous Performance (choreograph the scene and rehearse it). The last is a timed exercise at a stage she builds to the north of Liyue Harbor, breaking red scarecrows while avoiding blue ones. She admits she saw through Yunning's scheme to keep her busy, hopes to put part of the Traveler into her portrayal of the divine damsel, and suggests the title "Mentor". Ending: The Cast Contemplates the Play's Meaning.

### ULTRA +
**Endings (for reference).** A Shame That the Road Is Rocky (stealth failed; the troupe must be more cautious), And Few Friends You Shall Find (the fake reporters are exposed), Brought Together by Common Interests (the shared photographs), A Souvenir Without Sentiment Is of Little Worth (the autographed picture) and The Cast Contemplates the Play's Meaning (the acting lessons).

**Small details.** At the tea house after her show, several regulars remark on the performance, including the silk dance she added to the divine damsel's entrance, which the false journalist Shiyun praises as crane-like. In the barbecue scene Yun Jin says she avoids Wanmin Restaurant's spicy stew because it is hard on her throat and prefers open-air dining. When asked to improve the divine-damsel portrayal she says the operatic tradition keeps the voices and faces of bygone heroes alive, and hopes the Traveler's will one day appear on a Liyue stage too.

## revelations
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Yun Jin pursues realism: she joins in dangerous experience to understand a character, writes to performers from many nations, and calls acting her bread and butter. She cherishes the audience members who truly appreciate nuance. The tabloids once ran a story casting her as a diva after she left an interview, so Yunning now handles all interview requests. The troupe's rules forbid taking private gifts from patrons.
### LARGE +
Yun Jin says she hopes to one day write an opera about the Traveler and that she can use their courage as a reference for the divine damsel. She also states she is not an expert in law, and her legal-sounding bluff in Backstage Suspicions is an act meant to lure Boyi into revealing himself.

## characters
@spoiler: none
### STANDARD
Yun Jin and the Traveler lead. Yunning is the troupe's manager and a worrier. Katheryne appears at the Guild. Others by route: Maksim and Gentry Maocai (admirers), Boyi and Shiyun (self-declared Steambird staff), Chen the Sharp and Dongsheng (food stalls) and Shenhe (mentioned only).

## consequences
@spoiler: none
### STANDARD
No ending alters the main story. Rewards scale with the number of endings unlocked (Primogems, Adventure EXP, Hero's Wit, Guide to Diligence, Prithiva Topaz Fragments and Cloud-Shrouded Jade). Within the event, Boyi's business is set to be investigated by the Millelith, and Shiyun is offered a chance to write for the real Steambird.

## placement
@spoiler: none
### STANDARD
Release order: Version 2.4. Story order: after The Crane Returns on the Wind, since Yun Jin and others refer to the Traveler having defeated an ancient god and the Fatui at Liyue Harbor, and to the Jade Chamber battle.

## entry: Yun Jin's Acting Methods
@keys: Yun Jin acting, Immersive Performance, Meticulous Performance
@keys2: Yun Jin
@version: 2.4
@spoiler: none
### STANDARD
In First Glimpse of Meaning Yun Jin teaches three approaches: relive the real emotion, substitute familiar experiences for unfamiliar ones, or choreograph and rehearse every detail. She says all three are valuable and that she will use any method that improves her performance.
