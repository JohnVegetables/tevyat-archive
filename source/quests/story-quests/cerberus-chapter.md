## summary
### LITE
Wriothesley's Story Quest, set in the Fortress of Meropide. A contraband box and a missing hat lead the Traveler, Paimon and the Duke of Meropide to the Beret Society, a supposedly model prisoners' club. Its leader Dougier is secretly controlling members through fear and preparing to overthrow Wriothesley. Wriothesley breaks the Society and later tells the Traveler why he reacted so strongly.
### STANDARD +
The chapter has one act, Reborn in the Land of Grievances, made of three quests: An Opportunity for Rebirth, The Proud "Beret Society" and It's What He Deserves. It was released in version 4.1 and is unlocked after Cataclysm's Quickening. It shows Wriothesley as an administrator who treats the prison as a place of rebirth and who is capable of overwhelming force.
### LARGE +
The story is about authority, trust and punishment: Dougier uses fear and surveillance to rule a community of prisoners, while Wriothesley argues the Fortress is held together by something freely shared. The act ends gently, with two former members choosing to stay in Meropide after their sentences and asking to marry there.

## plot
### LITE
The Traveler and Paimon return to Meropide and help a Beret Society member find his hat. Wriothesley shows them a contraband box containing a gem that floods holders with bad memories, then takes them to meet Beret Society leader Dougier. Wriothesley secretly investigates, finds that the Society's members are being punished with thorns and a fear solution, raids Dougier's hidden base, and subdues him. Afterwards he explains his past and the Society is dissolved.
### STANDARD +
An Opportunity for Rebirth: a man called Faissolle has lost his beret, and the Traveler and Paimon buy one at the Rag and Bone Shop run in the owner's absence by Chamboduc. It is the wrong hat. Wriothesley appears with the right one, tells Faissolle to take better care as a Beret Society member, and takes the pair to the Coupon Cafeteria, where he tells them how he came to administer the prison. The guard Guelet reports contraband in the latest shipment: a box of items. Paimon picks up a gem, is flooded with bad memories and puts it back. Wriothesley and the Traveler feel it too, and the Traveler recalls the Abyss and their sibling. He offers to take them to the Society.

The Proud "Beret Society": Dougier greets them and claims the Society was founded by his efforts alone. Members wear berets as a symbol of reform. Wriothesley sees faint signs of dissonance, and when the Traveler revisits the members they seem cold and cagey. Wriothesley confirms the show and says guards still need to investigate. Back at his office Wriothesley explains that he feigned ignorance, dropping the gem to make Dougier think the inquiry was too early. Avice, Faissolle's lover, begs for help, saying he will be "censured". Dougier arrives and Wriothesley orders her to show her head. The Traveler works out that her hair clip, which she removed secretly, holds the proof: a thorn-like pin that, when poked into the gem, releases a dark liquid. Wriothesley guesses it is a concentrated solution of "fear".

It's What He Deserves: guards led by Ielmini have stopped members trying to leave. Wriothesley notes their thorn wounds under the berets. They find a surveillance port Dougier used to watch members, an empty fake headquarters, and signs that an overseer was bribed. In the production zone they find an unconscious guard and members who admit Dougier ordered them to kill any guard who appeared. They infiltrate Dougier's real base, find his Gardemeks, missing Clockwork Meka and the Society's real rules, free imprisoned members, and reach a central chamber where Dougier is about to punish Faissolle and Avice with an injection of "Aqua Doloris". Dougier demands a share of the fortress and sets Gardemeks on them. Wriothesley destroys them, dodges Dougier's gunfire, disarms him and takes him down.
### LARGE +
An Opportunity for Rebirth: Chamboduc is awed to meet the Traveler and Paimon, whom the prison counts among Wriothesley's friends. He waives the cost of the hat because the owner Alvard lets him take items rather than being paid in Mora or Credit Coupons. Wolsey at the Coupon Cafeteria serves drinks in place of the Super Deluxe meal because the visit was unplanned. Wriothesley states the Society's members are model prisoners and offers to introduce them.

The Proud "Beret Society": Dougier takes Wriothesley's praise as flattery, as members simply do what he himself would do outside; the Society's foundation, he says, was due to him alone. The Traveler learns from members about the good they do. When Faissolle sees Paimon he flees. After Wriothesley drops the gem the mood in the room changes, but the visit ends on good terms. Wriothesley explains the Society reduced his workload, which is why he wanted to meet them, and says confrontation is inevitable. In his office he says too many were beginning to think him incapable, so he began investigating in secret, and the plan was nearly ruined when Paimon called out to Faissolle; he plays music while waiting for a fish to bite. Dougier says her mind has been deteriorating and advises Wriothesley to ignore her rambling, but Wriothesley chooses to listen. When he leaves, Wriothesley tinkers with the clip and the Traveler realises the gem is a container.

It's What He Deserves: Wriothesley deduces the bribery because his overseer had not warned him. The base also confirms his suspicion about odd decommission requests and missing Clockwork Meka. Wyler is promised leniency for pointing to the hidden base. In the cell area some members are in a troubled state of mind; the main door is opened to let guards in. When Dougier says Wriothesley "shares" while Dougier "loots", Wriothesley answers that his methods differ. Dougier says everyone in the fortress has been shunned by society and that Wriothesley uses them; Wriothesley refutes this and says the prison is a place of rebirth. When Dougier aims at Paimon, Wriothesley disarms him, throws him down, and says the rule against unauthorised punishment does not apply to him now; as the Duke he does not need justification to kill. He then comforts each member in person and dissolves the Society.
### ULTRA +
Aftermath: Wriothesley tells the Traveler and Paimon in his office that he feels helpless and answerable for what happened on his watch. He intends to do nothing to Dougier for the time being, so that Dougier's dread builds, and to draw on former members' accounts to devise a suitable punishment. He thanks the pair and offers a meal. Two days later, at the cafeteria, doctors say the thorns can be removed and members will recover in a few days.

Backstory: asked about his flashback and anger, Wriothesley says he was an orphan adopted by two parents who had many children and passed them on to influential families when they came of age. He later learned his adoptive parents were traffickers who sold those children, and that those who could not be sold, or who knew the truth, were disposed of. He had thought himself fortunate because of the bonds with his "siblings". He killed the adoptive parents, freed the other children and was sent to Meropide to serve his sentence. He knew it was an extreme act but did not know what else to do, and says he does not want his new home to resemble that house.

Epilogue: Avice and Faissolle thank them and announce that they will marry; they met in the Society, saw Dougier trying to divide them and trusted each other. They want a ceremony in the prison and plan to remain after their sentences end, trusting Wriothesley to run it. He agrees to bring anything needed from the surface and tells a surprised Paimon that he would take a genuine expression of faith over an obligation to obey.

## revelations
### LITE
Dougier controlled Beret Society members with thorn-pin "censure", a fear solution and surveillance, and meant to overthrow Wriothesley.
### STANDARD +
The "Aqua Doloris" fear solution is a concentrated dose of fear, which Wriothesley relates to how Fontainians perceive water. Wriothesley also explains his own past: an orphan adopted by traffickers.
### LARGE +
Dougier bribed an overseer, hid an armed base and trained Gardemeks, and prepared for open war. The Traveler's sense of the Abyss and their sibling on touching the gem is noted but not explained.
### ULTRA +
Wriothesley's account of killing his adoptive parents to free their children is the quest's key personal revelation and explains his strict, protective view of the prison. It is taken from the wiki summary and is not corroborated elsewhere in this entry.

## characters
### LITE
Wriothesley, the Traveler and Paimon.
### STANDARD +
Beret Society figures: the leader Dougier, Faissolle and Avice, and members such as Wyler. Prison staff: guards Guelet and Ielmini, shopkeepers Chamboduc and Alvard, and cafeteria cook Wolsey.
### LARGE +
Maupoil is referenced as an NPC who may be tied up by a related side quest.

## consequences
### STANDARD
The Beret Society is dissolved and Dougier is held. Members receive medical care and thorn removal. Faissolle and Avice plan to marry in Meropide and remain after their sentences.
### LARGE +
Wriothesley's relationship with the Traveler is deepened by trust: he lets them see his past and treats them to a meal. Dougier's punishment is deliberately left for later.

## placement
### STANDARD
Unlocked at Adventure Rank 40 after Cataclysm's Quickening (Chapter IV Act IV). Released in 4.1 on 2023-09-27, so it sits between that Archon Quest act and the Chapter IV finale in release order.

## entry: Beret Society
@keys: Beret Society, Dougier
@keys2: Wriothesley, Meropide
@version: 4.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
A prisoners' club in the Fortress of Meropide, with berets as its sign of reform, presented as a model society. In truth its leader Dougier ruled it by "censure", thorn pins hidden under the berets, surveillance ports and a fear solution called Aqua Doloris, and planned to overthrow Wriothesley. The Society was broken up by the Duke in the Cerberus Chapter and its members taken for care.

## entry: Aqua Doloris and the thorn gem
@keys: Aqua Doloris, Fear solution, Thorn gem
@keys2: Wriothesley, Beret Society
@version: 4.1
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
A gem-shaped container found in a contraband shipment that floods the holder with bad memories. Pierced with a thorn-like pin from a member's hair clip, it leaks a dark liquid that Wriothesley believes is a highly concentrated solution of fear. Dougier injected it into members as punishment.
