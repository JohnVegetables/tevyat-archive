## summary
### LITE
The Imperatrix Umbrosa Chapter is the Raiden Shogun's Story Quest. In Act I, Reflections of Mortality (Version 2.1), the Traveler brings Ei, the Electro Archon, out of her Plane of Euthymia to walk through Inazuma City. In Act II, Transient Dreams (Version 2.5), Ei confronts the memories of the catastrophe five centuries ago and her sister Makoto, defeats her own puppet in a duel and abolishes the Sakoku Decree.
### STANDARD +
Both acts take place after the Archon Quest Omnipresence Over Mortals. Act I has four quests: The Lonesome Euthymia, To Traverse the Mortal Plane, The Fabric of Constancy and To Hear Mortal Hearts. Act II has three: Cleansing Light, Farewell to the Past and Radiant Sakura. The chapter shows Ei beginning to take a living part in rule, and settles her relationship with the Shogun puppet.
### LARGE +
The chapter is the personal sequel to the Archon Quest. The first act is about Ei learning that the present is not a threat to eternity; the second is about why she feared change and how Makoto's worldview survived in her. Its events also open Yae Miko's, Kazuha's and Ayato's Story Quests.

## plot
### LITE
At the Statue of the Omnipresent God the Traveler learns that the Shogun is in a strange state and that Inazuma is troubled. Yae Miko lends the Traveler a pass, and Ei brings them into her plane, then takes the Shogun's body for a walk through Inazuma City. Later, rifthounds attack the Sacred Sakura; Ei fights them with the Traveler, relives old memories, and resolves her past with Makoto and the puppet.
### STANDARD
Act I, Reflections of Mortality. Thoma, Kamaji and Ippei are worried because the Shogun wants private time, the storms around Inazuma are advancing, and the Kujou Clan has lost control over its vassals after Kujou Takayuki's dealings with the Fatui and his poor health. Kamaji goes to see Takatsukasa Susumu. At the Grand Narukami Shrine, Yae Miko gives the Traveler a pass to see the Shogun, who at Tenshukaku does not recognise them and behaves strangely. Ei speaks through the puppet and takes them to her plane, saying she has disabled most of its functions because it clings to the old protocols she wants to change, and the puppet resists change so only Miko may see her. She says a small change to the Shogun could stop the storm. The Traveler asks about her view of Eternity, and Paimon suggests she take a walk.

Outside, Ei notes that the market has changed. She has no Mora because as an Archon she never shopped. At Tomoki's stall she tries Dango Milk, likes it, and her praise keeps him from dropping the product. At the Yae Publishing House she browses novels but cannot grasp fiction. She decides she should not hamper human lives for her pursuit of eternity. The Traveler takes a photo, and Ei is surprised that it shows only one person; they remind her she can exist in more than one form, like the puppet. She says Inazuma has changed a great deal and perhaps she held on too tightly.
### LARGE +
Ippei reports that Kamaji has not returned from negotiations with the Takatsukasa Clan, who met at one of their own bases and not at the Commission. At the hidden base Ei and the Traveler find ronin guarding Susumu and his men and Kamaji. Susumu says it was for security; Kamaji says Susumu was pressing him to confess the Kujou Clan's crimes and claim the Takatsukasa had stood against injustice. Ei reminds them she chose the first Tenryou Commissioner by duel and that any replacement must beat her. Susumu and his retainers refuse out of fear, which Ei calls hypocrisy given Susumu's talk of giving his life. Kamaji challenges her, knowing he is outmatched, to atone for his father; he does not yield and the duel ends only when he collapses. She orders Susumu's party to look after him. Afterward she says the Shogun would have killed him and that she saw in his eyes he accepted that, and that Kamaji keeps the Kujou trait of honour. She will still punish the clan but gives them more time. Back at the shrine Miko says the Traveler may borrow the pass to visit Ei again.

Act II, Transient Dreams. At the Inazuma City Adventurers' Guild Katheryne passes on a Yashiro Commission request: rifthounds are attacking the Sacred Sakura. In the field Ei joins the Traveler. She says the rifthounds recall the cataclysm 500 years ago, which the Traveler recognises as Khaenri'ah's fall. The sakura's sap, which purifies filth in the Ley Lines, produces memories; tainted filth evokes aggressive ones. From the roots appear echoes: Kitamura, a samurai who warns that monsters roam and that both the Shogun and Kitsune Saiguu are gone, and Furuyama, a tea specialist from her entourage, who accepts the end of things as the Shogun taught. Ei says Saiguu led Inazuma's defence and died, and that she herself was too fixated on the past. Furuyama's attitude, she says, came from her sister Makoto.

At the Kamisato Estate they fetch a tea set, but by the time they return Furuyama has faded. Ei makes tea and tells how Makoto went to Khaenri'ah without telling her and was dying when Ei found her. Ei entered her consciousness to say goodbye, resolved on eternity, preserved the realm of Makoto's mind and took it home. Back in Inazuma she found the Sacred Sakura on Mt. Yougou, which she had never seen, though everyone else said it had always stood there. She believes Makoto's remnants made it and used its power against the monsters. Asked about the Traveler's sibling, she says she arrived too late to know.
### ULTRA +
Tracking the residue to a cave, Ei freezes while fighting. The roots call up three yoriki, Fujiki, Fukami and one unnamed, who fight the rifthounds and then say the Shogun gave them hope. Ei says she neglected her duties as an Archon and promises to be worthy. Under the Grand Narukami Shrine she opens a torii gate into Makoto's consciousness, where the puppet greets her. The puppet is rejecting her commands, believing her new views a sign of erosion. They duel, the puppet with Engulfing Lightning and Ei with Musou Isshin, and Ei expels the Traveler, telling them to ask Miko to govern in her stead.

Miko reopens the torii and sends the Traveler to the plane, where centuries have passed. The Shogun accepts a final duel. Ei wins, the Shogun finds she is not eroded, and Ei offers to make it her kagemusha, which it accepts, impressed and looking forward to a rematch. The Musou Isshin then releases a fragment of Makoto's consciousness. Makoto apologises for dumping rulership on Ei and not persuading her slowly, says she sensed what was coming in Khaenri'ah and had no time to wait, and left a fragment in the sword to wait for Ei to understand her view. She gives Ei a seed, which Ei plants in the past and becomes the Sacred Sakura, then bids her farewell. Back in the present, Ei looks at Inazuma, abolishes the Sakoku Decree and thanks everyone.

## revelations
### STANDARD
- Ei has disabled most of the puppet Shogun's functions while she tries to change its old protocols.
- Ei can occupy the Shogun's body in the real world, and sees herself as one being with several forms.
- Ei has never dealt with Mora or shops, and enjoys Dango Milk.
- The Takatsukasa Clan tried to take the Tenryou Commission from the Kujou by pressuring Kamaji.
### LARGE +
- Ei appointed the first Tenryou Commissioner by a duel, which the Kujou founder won.
- Ei says the Shogun puppet would have killed Kamaji in the duel; she holds back and he survives.
- Act II: Sacred Sakura sap purifies Ley Line filth and returns memories of the past.
- Act II: Makoto went to Khaenri'ah without telling Ei and died there; Ei resolved on eternity afterwards.
- Act II: A fragment of Makoto lived on in the Musou Isshin sword.
- Act II: Ei ends the Sakoku Decree.

## characters
### STANDARD
- **Raiden Shogun**: the Electro Archon Ei and her puppet, the chapter's protagonist.
- **The Traveler and Paimon**: bring Ei into the city and then fight beside her.
- **Yae Miko**: Guuji who lends the pass, and governs for Ei when she retreats.
- **Thoma, Kujou Kamaji, Ippei**: Inazuman retainers and officials concerned for the Shogun.
- **Tomoki, Kuroda**: a stall owner and a publishing house editor.
- **Takatsukasa Susumu**: leader of the Takatsukasa plot.
- **Kitamura, Furuyama, Fujiki, Fukami**: memory echoes from the Sacred Sakura.
- **Makoto**: Ei's late sister, first Electro Archon.
### LARGE +
Katheryne, Hirano and Takeshi have minor roles, and Kitsune Saiguu is present only through memory.

## consequences
### STANDARD
Ei takes an active role in governing Inazuma. The Shogun puppet becomes her kagemusha. The Sakoku Decree is abolished. The Kujou Clan gets more time. Susumu and the Takatsukasa are checked.

## placement
### STANDARD
Act I is Version 2.1; Act II, Transient Dreams, is Version 2.5. Both require Omnipresence Over Mortals and Adventure Rank 40; Act I is a prerequisite for the Kaedehara, Kamisato Ayato and Yae Miko chapters, and Act II is also required for Yae Miko's. Story order and release order match.
### LARGE +
The puppet's later rematch is the subject of End of the Oneiric Euthymia, a separate quest.

### STANDARD
The Shogun that Ei made to rule in her place. In Transient Dreams it turns against Ei, believing she is suffering erosion; after a duel in Makoto's consciousness it accepts that she is not, and becomes her kagemusha.

## entry: Furuyama and the memory echoes
@keys: Furuyama, Kitamura, Fujiki, Fukami
@keys2: Ei, Sacred Sakura
@version: 2.5
### STANDARD
Memories of the catastrophe 500 years ago that appear from the Sacred Sakura's roots. Furuyama, a tea specialist in Ei's entourage, accepts life's end as Makoto's teaching. Kitamura, Fujiki and Fukami are samurai who feared for Inazuma and take heart when Ei returns. They fade once the Ley Lines can no longer sustain them.

## entry: Takatsukasa plot
@keys: Takatsukasa Susumu, Takatsukasa Clan
@keys2: Kamaji, Ei, Tenryou
@version: 2.1
### STANDARD
Takatsukasa Susumu tries to replace the Kujou Clan as Tenryou Commissioner by pressuring Kamaji at a hidden base guarded by ronin. Ei interrupts, says only someone who defeats her can hold the office, and Susumu backs down. Kamaji duels her instead.
