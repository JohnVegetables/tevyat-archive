## summary
### LITE
The Lotos Somno Chapter is Nilou's Story Quest, released in Version 3.1, and as of 7.1 it has one act, To the Wise. After the Sabzeruz Festival, the Sumeru Akademiya orders the Zubayr Theater shut down and its stage demolished within 30 days. Nilou, backed by the troupe and the Traveler, stands up in a formal debate against the Akademiya's representative Sharif, and the confrontation turns into a quiet first step toward reconciliation between Sharif and his estranged daughter Inayah.
### STANDARD +
The act has three quests: The Warm Rhythm of the Streets, Show Canceled and The Reason We Are Gathered Here. It is a character piece about belonging and about how Sumeru's scholarly institutions treat art, not an adventure with combat set-pieces; the only fight is a brief rescue of Inayah from tigers outside the city. Nilou is shown nervous, kind and stubborn, and she is the one who has to carry the argument.
### LARGE +
The quest follows the Archon Quest chapter in which the Sabzeruz Festival went ahead despite Akademiya interference, and it builds on that festival's success. Its central question is not only whether the theater broke the rules but what a place of belonging is worth against an institution that values intellectual output.

## plot
### LITE
At the Grand Bazaar, Nilou is overloaded with free goods from shopkeepers for the theater's celebration party. The party is interrupted by Sharif, an Akademiya official, who announces that the theater has broken Akademiya rules and will be demolished within 30 days. Nilou agrees to meet him in a debate. After a tense contest that has no clear verdict, Sharif walks away, later suspends himself, and gives his daughter Inayah a gift.
### STANDARD
The Traveler and Paimon find Nilou in the Grand Bazaar with more donated goods than she can carry. They help her haul them to the Zubayr Theater, with help from the porter Farhad, and the troupe starts a small party celebrating the festival's success. Sharif interrupts and demands to speak to the manager, Sheikh Zubayr, saying the Akademiya has decided to shut the theater down and demolish its stage in 30 days because of repeated rule violations. Zubayr objects that nobody communicated with him properly. Nilou explains that the Akademiya judges the theater's shows not intellectual enough. Inayah, a troupe member who ran away from home, argues with Sharif and addresses him as father. The Traveler suggests settling the matter by debate, a normal way of resolving disputes in the Akademiya, and persuades a reluctant Nilou to represent the theater.

The troupe and locals talk about what the theater means to them. Inayah slips away, and Nilou guesses she blames herself for bringing Sharif to the theater. They search near an old camp outside the city and rescue Inayah from a pack of Rishboland tigers. Nilou convinces her the fault is not hers, and Inayah agrees to help at the debate. The Traveler points out what Nilou's case is missing: the theater is not only a stage but a place of belonging for the people there now and in future.

Two days later the debate is held, with Safwan of the Akademiya as adjudicator. The first topic, whether the theater is useful, ends inconclusive. On the second, Nilou argues that Zubayr met all legal and documentary requirements for running a theater, and Safwan rules that threatening demolition before the Akademiya's discussions was premature. Sharif then notes that the Akademiya could instead revoke the troupe's performance license, which catches Nilou off guard, and Safwan agrees. Nilou argues that the theater is a place of belonging, but Sharif replies that such bonds are formed through work, much as scholars share knowledge and then move on to other projects. Inayah steps onto the stage and tells her father he is too wrapped up in his own world to understand relationships. The audience sides with her, and Sharif says, almost to himself, that it is a relief, and leaves, with Safwan following. The debate ends without a clear verdict.
### LARGE +
Afterwards Nilou thanks the Traveler for keeping her from losing her temper and tells them how she became a dancer: through hard work rather than natural talent, after being captivated as a child by a troupe's dancer who later became her teacher. She wonders what Sharif wanted to say but could not.

Two days after the debate, Nilou introduces Inayah as the troupe's newest member before a performance the Traveler and Paimon attend. Sharif tells them he is currently suspended at his own suggestion, because losing the debate hurt the Akademiya's image. He still does not understand the theater's purpose, but his curiosity is piqued. Nilou brings Inayah to him and the pair share an awkward moment; Sharif gives her a framed piece of amateur embroidery he made himself, showing he had not forgotten her gift. Nilou says reconciliation will take a while and that going slowly is a step in the right direction. She gives the Traveler a miniature replica of the theater made by the troupe, so they can keep the memory while travelling Teyvat, and says they are welcome back at any time.
### ULTRA +
Details of the debate worth keeping straight: Safwan swears an oath of impartiality before it starts, warns both sides to restrain their emotions after the first topic grows heated, and finds Sharif's licence argument well supported even while faulting the early demolition threat. The Traveler tells Nilou to calm down when she nearly lashes out, and afterwards observes that Sharif need not have agreed to a debate at all and could have used other measures against the theater.

## revelations
@version: 3.1
### STANDARD
- The Akademiya's objection is that the theater's shows are not intellectual enough, and its tools are rule compliance and licensing.
- Inayah is Sharif's daughter; she did not get along with her parents and has lived at the theater.
### LARGE +
- Nilou's skill as a dancer came from effort, not innate talent.
- Sharif gives Inayah a handmade embroidery as a sign he remembers her gift, though he struggles to say what he feels.

## characters
### STANDARD
- **Nilou**: dancer of the Zubayr Theater, the act's protagonist, who must speak for the troupe.
- **The Traveler and Paimon**: advisers who propose the debate.
- **Sharif**: Akademiya official who issues the demolition order and argues the Akademiya's side. He is Inayah's father.
- **Inayah**: young troupe member, Sharif's daughter, estranged from her parents.
- **Sheikh Zubayr**: theater manager.
- **Safwan**: Akademiya scholar who adjudicates the debate.
- **Farhad**: a porter who helps carry the goods.
### LARGE +
Other troupe members and local shopkeepers appear as supporters and give their views on what the theater means to them.

## consequences
### STANDARD
The demolition is not carried out, Sharif suspends himself, and Inayah joins the troupe formally. The theater keeps its stage; the personal rift between Sharif and Inayah has only begun to heal.

## placement
### STANDARD
Released in Version 3.1 and unlocked after Chapter III Act II of the Archon Quest and Adventure Rank 40. Story order and release order match. Only Act I exists as of 7.1.
