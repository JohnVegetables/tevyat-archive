## summary
### LITE
Historia Antiqua Chapter is Zhongli's Story Quest, in two acts. In Sal Flore he and the Traveler accompany two self-described archaeologists to the ruins of the god Havria and learn why the God of Salt really fell. In No Mere Stone, a missing-miners commission leads them to the sealed Azhdaha and his benevolent consciousness.
### STANDARD +
Both acts treat Zhongli as the former Rex Lapis living as a consultant. The chapter is about memory: how history is written, who preserves it, and what Morax feels when old friends and old gods resurface.

## plot
@spoiler: major
### LITE
Act I: The Traveler meets Zhongli at the Pearl Galley with a Fatui archaeologist, Kliment, and a second, Wanyan. They search Guyun Stone Forest and Sal Terrae, open a sealed ruin, and Zhongli exposes both as treasure seekers. Wanyan is a follower of Havria who wanted proof that Morax killed her. Zhongli shows that Havria's own followers betrayed and killed her, then asks the Traveler to be a record of history. Act II: Zhongli helps investigate missing miners at The Chasm, finding that a small girl lured them for a corrupted Azhdaha, and that the expert Kun Jun is Azhdaha's benevolent consciousness.
### STANDARD +
Act I ends with Zhongli deciding to keep the truth in the Traveler, who has seen many worlds. He throws Havria's relics into the sea. Act II ends with Azhdaha re-sealed, the miners saved, and the benevolent half learning that Morax has stepped down as the Geo Archon. Zhongli also refuses to say what he knows of Khaenri'ah's fall.
### LARGE +
**Act I: Sal Flore.**
*Shadow of Yore.* At the Pearl Galley the Traveler and Paimon find Zhongli and Liyue archaeologists debating the origin of the first Mora. A Fatuus, Kliment, who claims to be registered with the Ministry of Civil Affairs, says he hired Zhongli as a consultant; Wanyan joins. Zhongli asks that the Traveler come along, and Kliment gives in when told treasure is easy to find.

*Vortex's Wake.* In Guyun Stone Forest Zhongli says the place suits studying ancient gods because he sealed them there as Rex Lapis. Hydro Mimics guard a tablet with traces of divine power. The two archaeologists argue over each other's inexperience; a teacup is identified as Ningguang's and Kliment is warned not to sell it. Kliment heads for Sal Terrae.

*A Ruin Under Lock and Key.* Treasure Hoarders have already pilfered Sal Terrae. Wanyan knows of a sealed ruin; Zhongli finds a second mechanism with Electro monuments and, reciting a puzzle, lets the Traveler activate them in order to open it. Tired of their quarrels, Zhongli makes them sign a contract: they claim items alternately, and he will leave if it is broken. Wanyan adds a clause that they must judge the God of Salt's fate honestly, even if it harms Morax's image.

*Farewell, Sweet Salt.* Kliment takes a chalice that never runs out of salt; Wanyan's item is a ruler that, by its depth, produces endless salt. Kliment breaks the contract; Zhongli defeats him, confiscates the chalice and warns him off. Wanyan, who cannot manage archaeology basics yet knows Havria in detail, admits she is a follower who wanted proof that Morax killed the God of Salt. A broken sword is found; Zhongli says it was the weapon used to kill Havria. In an open chamber salinised statues include one in the pose of her killer. Zhongli explains that when her followers betrayed Havria, her power turned many of them to salt; the survivors fled to Liyue and Morax's care, then returned to break the sword in hopes of forgiveness, which her benevolence could not have required. Wanyan refuses to accept this and leaves; Zhongli confiscates the salt ruler.

*A Record of All Things.* At the top of Guyun Stone Forest Zhongli says that as Rex Lapis he wrote history with his own hands, but the incident showed him he needed another way. He tells the Traveler they were chosen because they have travelled many worlds and can retain Teyvat's history if the world is destroyed. He casts Havria's relics into the sea and offers to bring the Traveler along when he preserves Liyue's past.

**Act II: No Mere Stone.**
*Strange Sights, Stolen Souls.* At the Adventurers' Guild Katheryne passes on a commission from Uncle Dai, who needs workers and has an expert arriving. Paimon brings Zhongli from the Third-Round Knockout; Dai's expert, Kun Jun, shows a rare rock with water inside. Dai says he found Kun Jun by the roadside, and the group sets out for The Chasm.

*Truth in the Stones.* At the mine Dai explains that four miners (Long, Bold Cen, Brave Cen and Mao) vanished after a shift change. They took only pickaxes and baskets. Bravo had slept through it. Kun Jun, beside a Cor Lapis deposit, says he can see the memories of stones and reports that a young girl led the miners away. Zhongli discourages pursuit since six days have passed; Kun Jun says he seeks Dragonfall, which might restore his hazy memory.

*Shifting Earth and Lost Threads Come to Light.* At Lingju Pass Ling saw lifeless miners following a small blue-haired girl. After rescuing Wanyu from Pyro Slimes and Geovishap Hatchlings, they confirm the route toward Nantianmen, where a collapsed miner is found. Kun Jun gives the Traveler and Zhongli special stones, which Zhongli accepts hesitantly. Zhongli explains Dragonfall is forged by elemental reactions rather than heat and was mined out centuries ago, making Kun Jun's search strange. The Traveler and Paimon worry, but Zhongli advises watching.

*Amidst Chaos, the Rock Is Unmoved.* Kun Jun explains that stones keep only the most prominent memories. A tunnel under a huge tree holds the rest of the miners under a malevolent aura. The aura slips beneath Zhongli's stone barrier and becomes the girl; an attack redirected into the seal pulls the party into Azhdaha's domain. Azhdaha recognises Zhongli as Morax's vessel and calls him a betrayer. The pair subdue and re-seal him. Kun Jun then reveals himself as Azhdaha's benevolent consciousness. He tells how Azhdaha, a friend of humanity and the Geo Archon, suffered erosion and lost Morax's face; humans disturbing the Chasm's ley lines split him into a savage body and a kind consciousness. Morax failed to stop the erosion and, with the benevolent half, Mountain Shaper, Moon Carver and a third adeptus, sealed his friend. Afterwards Dai is found, Kun Jun asks to be taken to the harbor, and the benevolent half learns Zhongli has stepped down as the Geo Archon. He leaves Kun Jun's body. Zhongli recalls Azhdaha once wishing to see the surface and being given a form and sight by Morax, with a warning. The Traveler asks about the fall of Khaenri'ah; Zhongli declines, bound by a contract older than the Traveler's arrival.
### ULTRA +
The chapter's title in Latin means Ancient History; Zhongli's story chapter and Neuvillette's are, per the wiki, the only ones whose names do not match the character's constellation (Zhongli's is Lapis Dei). Per the wiki, Zhongli was the first character to receive a second act. The Act II wiki summary spells one quest title two ways, so minor naming details are unconfirmed.

## revelations
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
- Havria, the God of Salt, was betrayed and killed by her own followers, not by Morax; the survivors fled to Liyue and Morax protected them.
- Zhongli is the former Rex Lapis, and treats the Traveler as a keeper of memory.
- The little girl and Kun Jun are two halves of Azhdaha: a corrupted body and a benevolent consciousness.
### LARGE +
- Azhdaha's erosion was worsened when humans disturbed the Chasm's ley lines.
- Zhongli knows of the fall of Khaenri'ah but is bound by a contract not to say.

## characters
### LITE
Zhongli, the Traveler and Paimon; Act I adds Kliment and Wanyan (Havria appears only in the story told); Act II adds Uncle Dai, Kun Jun, Katheryne and Azhdaha.
### STANDARD +
Act II also includes Wanyu, Ling and the miners. Zhongli is shown as courteous and dry, a former archon who prefers contracts and records to force.

## consequences
### STANDARD
Havria's relics are cast into the sea; Wanyan loses her proof and Kliment his chalice. Azhdaha is re-sealed with the miners saved, and the benevolent consciousness departs. The Traveler is treated by Zhongli as an ally for preserving Liyue's history.

## placement
### STANDARD
Act I (Sal Flore) was released in Version 1.1, requires A New Star Approaches and Adventure Rank 40. Act II (No Mere Stone) requires Sal Flore and We Will Be Reunited, per the wiki. Zhongli is the former Geo Archon, so the Archon Quest's revelation precedes these acts.
