## summary
### LITE
Sigewinne's Story Quest. On a holiday in the Court of Fontaine at the Duke's insistence, the head nurse of the Fortress of Meropide is drawn into a case: prisoners who seem to swap identities, and a new beauty lotion that is not what it seems. The Traveler helps her trace the potion behind it, and learns the story of why she kept a human face.
### STANDARD +
The chapter has one act, The Warmth of Lies, of four quests: Beauty Expert, Switcheroo, Underground Crime and The Truth of What Was. It was released in version 4.7 and is unlocked at Adventure Rank 40 after Masquerade of the Guilty and the Diluvies Chapter. It shows the medical, practical and sometimes scheming side of a character who is gentle but firm.
### LARGE +
The theme is care and self-sacrifice. Sigewinne treats a thief and his dying mother with the same kindness, and the chapter ties the mystery to her own past choice to trade what her friend loved for a way to heal her.

## plot
### LITE
Sigewinne tests a beauty lotion made by Rawat, and the Traveler sees an inmate's face melt away at the fortress infirmary. Serious offenders swap places with prisoners about to be released using a face-changing potion called Butterfly Dew. The Traveler, Sigewinne and the Marechaussee Phantom track the maker to a hidden plant and to his mother's house. The mastermind is revealed, and Sigewinne tells her own story at her teacher's grave.
### STANDARD +
Beauty Expert: the Traveler and Paimon find a beauty promotion in the Court of Fontaine and meet Sigewinne, who writes for a magazine run by a beauty association. Paimon asks if she is a half-Melusine; Sigewinne is not offended and says she chose long ago to keep a human form. She tells a story of a Melusine who befriended a human girl, was refused help by the girl's parents when she fell ill, went to a 'witch' for a potion that gave her a human form and lost what the girl loved about her, and then cured her. Sigewinne tests Rawat's lotion, then returns to Meropide to treat inmates. There a prisoner, Erick, claims to have seen his double; an inmate, Potton, has his face melt away. Sigewinne uses the Traveler to scare a child patient into taking his medicine, then prepares the inmates' meals.

Switcheroo: suspects arrested the day before now claim mistaken identity, and some prisoners due for release were swapped for serious offenders. Wriothesley orders a hold on releases, but some have already left. Sigewinne suspects a potion that even Melusines cannot detect. The party visits the Research Institute to ask after Rawat, then his mother's house, where Sigewinne treats a sick old lady and the Traveler finds a note pointing to a hidden laboratory.

Underground Crime: in the plant they find the escaped criminals, who argue with Potton about the potion. Potton traps the party and leaks gas; Sigewinne stalls him by saying she can make a better potion without Primordial Water and escapes. Back at Rawat's house, Potton claims to be Rawat, saying he used a potion to change his appearance because he was reviled as he was. Sigewinne lets him see his mother, who recognises only Sigewinne; he is taken away.

The Truth of What Was: the Butterfly Dew is taken off the market. Potton confesses to Morgane that he worked with a man called Vacher, and that after the flood cut the supply of Primordial Seawater the gang had turned to attacking people. Sigewinne takes the Traveler and Paimon to the grave of her teacher in the Morte Region and tells them about her past.
### LARGE +
Beauty Expert: Sigewinne tells the Traveler that Melusines judge people by their cuteness. The Duke sends Erick to the ward for observation and Sigewinne handles the case while the Marechaussee Phantom deal with another matter. Her teacher, she says, was a human travelling doctor who frightened children into taking medicine and died long ago. She cooks inmates' meals from Slime Condensate, Xenochromatic Crystals and frog meat, finished by the Coupon Cafeteria, because inmates would refuse to eat food made in front of them.

Switcheroo: Erick fails the test Sigewinne set him earlier, which supports the swapping theory. Sigewinne goes with the Marechaussee Phantom, with Morgane's concern and Wriothesley's approval. Rawat's colleagues at the Institute show disdain for him, and Bognor says Rawat never got deep into conversations. The legal address Rawat registered his brand to was his mother's house.

Underground Crime: Sigewinne works out that the lotion contains remains of Fontainians dissolved before Neuvillette removed their sins, and that this is why the potion needed Primordial Water. Potton calls her out for using a similar potion herself and says they are the same; she argues otherwise.
### ULTRA +
The Truth of What Was: Neuvillette tells the Traveler, as relayed in the wiki summary, that Sigewinne was once an inmate at the fortress because she broke an ancient Fontainian law against forcibly changing a species into a human, a law he believes existed because Egeria wanted to keep the true origins of Fontainians a secret. Her teacher had discovered the truth too and left her a potion she could use. The Traveler and Paimon realise that her teacher was the 'witch' of the story. After her sentence Sigewinne chose to stay and became beloved there. She later received an invitation and reunited with her old friend, who recognised her in spite of her form. She had wanted to tell Potton the same but felt the warmth of his hand fade. She shows the Traveler an extra potion she made; she declines to use it herself and it is destroyed.

## revelations
### LITE
Butterfly Dew, a potion that changes a person's face, was being sold by a gang as a lotion. Sigewinne's human appearance came from a similar potion she took to heal a friend.
### STANDARD +
Sigewinne's teacher was the 'witch' who gave her the potion. The lotion's key ingredient was Primordial Seawater, and the gang had hidden a production plant under a remote site.
### LARGE +
The lotion originally depended on remains of Fontainians dissolved before Neuvillette removed their sins. The Marechaussee Phantom Melusines cannot detect the potion's effect.
### ULTRA +
As reported in the wiki summary, Sigewinne once served a sentence in the fortress for breaking a law about forced species change, and Neuvillette suspects Egeria's secret.

## characters
### LITE
Sigewinne, the Traveler, Paimon.
### STANDARD +
Wriothesley and Neuvillette appear. Morgane, Ceasth and Odilon work for the Marechaussee Phantom. The inmates Erick and Potton, and the researcher Rawat, appear.
### LARGE +
Luc, a child patient. Bognor and Rawat's colleagues at the Research Institute. The guards Lambersart, Courbevoie, Dupont and Wolsey. The seller Calcagni.

## consequences
### STANDARD
Potton and the gang are arrested and the remaining Butterfly Dew is withdrawn from sale. Sigewinne keeps the secret of her own potion, and the extra potion she made is destroyed.
### LARGE +
The Traveler learns the truth of Sigewinne's past and her friendship with a human girl that began it.

## placement
### STANDARD
Unlocked at Adventure Rank 40 after Chapter IV Act V, Masquerade of the Guilty, and Diluvies Chapter Act I, The Remains of the Past Day. Released in 4.7. Wriothesley's earlier story in the fortress is the Cerberus Chapter.

## entry: Butterfly Dew
@keys: Butterfly Dew, Rawat's lotion
@keys2: Sigewinne, Potton
@version: 4.7
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
A lotion sold in the Court of Fontaine under the name of Mr. Rawat of the Fontaine Research Institute. In truth it contains a potion that changes the user's face, allowing serious offenders to swap with prisoners about to be released. Its ingredient Primordial Water had become scarce after the flood. Potton, who used it to hide his looks, was part of the gang and worked with a man called Vacher.

## entry: Sigewinne's teacher
@keys: Sigewinne's teacher
@keys2: Sigewinne, Nereides
@version: 4.7
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
A human travelling doctor who taught Sigewinne her skills and died long ago. She is buried in the Morte Region, where Sigewinne takes the Traveler. The story Sigewinne tells in the first quest casts her as the 'witch' who gave a potion to a Melusine, and, having discovered the truth about the law, left her a potion she could use.
