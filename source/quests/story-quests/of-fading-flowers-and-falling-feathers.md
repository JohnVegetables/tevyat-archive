## summary
@spoiler: major
### LITE
The Flower-Feather Clan's Tribal Chronicles chapter and Chasca's story. The tribe's Qucusaur Rider trials have left many applicants "Wingless", and a merchant caravan arrives with Phlogiston Wings, jetpacks powered by phlogiston. When a wild qucusaur, Chasca's old "sister" Coya, starts attacking merchants, Chasca and the Traveler discover that a plot to harvest phlogiston from captive qucusaurs is being run by Allpa, a trusted member of the tribe.
### STANDARD +
The chapter has three acts, all released in Version 5.2: The Wingless One of Tlalocan, The Night Before the Trial and Guns and Wings, the last needing the Archon Quest act The Rainbow Destined to Burn. Guns and Wings has three quests: Tracks Through the Wilderness, Mortal Pursuit and Time for the Showdown. The chapter shows Chasca's mixed identity as a human raised by qucusaurs and her ties to her adoptive father Cusco and her qucusaur mother and sister.
### LARGE +
The chapter weighs technology that replaces a bond with the qucusaurs against the bond itself: the Wings lower the bar for flight but are made from qucusaur phlogiston, and the people most hurt by the tribe's losses in the Abyss war are the ones most tempted to replace the riders' old ways.

## plot
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
In Act I Chasca keeps watch over Coyllur, a Qucusaur Rider applicant who failed her trial and is at risk of becoming one of the Wingless. The Traveler cheers her up by indwelling a qucusaur, and Chasca's adoptive father Cusco asks the Traveler to persuade Chasca to take care of herself. In Act II, before the retried trial, merchants led by Isidore arrive after being attacked by a qucusaur and show the Phlogiston Wings they carry. Chasca finds a bloodstained feather she recognises. In Act III the applicants try the Wings, a qucusaur attacks the merchants again, and Chasca recognises it as her "sister" Coya. They trace the wings to a hidden airborne workshop attributed to a man named Tepal and find that Allpa, who helps run the trials, is the mastermind. The workshop is destroyed and Coya, with whom Chasca had fallen out long ago, has forgiven her.
### LARGE +
**The Wingless One of Tlalocan (5.2).** The Traveler and Paimon hear whistling in the Flower-Feather village and find Coyllur, who has just failed the Qucusaur Rider tests. Chasca explains that applicants must be skilled in combat and win a qucusaur's approval, and that those who fail too often give up and are called the "Wingless". The Traveler indwells a qucusaur to lift Coyllur's mood. Allpa announces the tests will be redone because the arena was not properly inspected. Chasca says she could tell the qucusaur was the Traveler because, being raised by qucusaurs, she knows they would not be so flustered. Cusco arrives; he says Chasca skips her checkups and has been different since Chuychu's death, and that he does not want to lose another daughter.

**The Night Before the Trial (5.2).** Chasca helps staff prepare the trial arena. A merchant, Isidore, begs for help after his caravan is attacked by a qucusaur. When they arrive Allpa is already there and says the qucusaur fled; the goods, the Phlogiston Wings, are undamaged. Chasca picks up a bloodstained feather that she appears to recognise.

**Tracks Through the Wilderness (5.2).** The applicants are now using the Wings, handheld-trackball jetpacks; Coyllur and Zhunan like them while Mukki feels they are cheating. Chasca wants to change the standards for Wing users, and Allpa says that would take too long after the tribe's losses in the invasion. The qucusaur returns and attacks the merchants; Chasca realises it is her sister Coya. Chasca's qucusaur "mom", Chimpu, tells them Coya had not visited in a while and brings them to Coya's nest on the edge of Ochkanatlan. Coya says humans have taken her child. Isidore's merchants ensnare Coya and Chimpu; during the fight Chasca throws his malfunctioning trackball into the sea, where it explodes. Isidore says a man named Tepal hired him and that the trackballs run on phlogiston drawn from qucusaurs. Allpa says Tepal was a Children of Echoes member who failed to become a rider.

**Mortal Pursuit (5.2).** Xilonen helps trace Tepal; his relative Puma says she had disowned him and hands over his tattered notes. They suggest his workshop is powered by a relic from Ochkanatlan, the balloon seen near the trial site. Mutota is exasperated that Och-Kan keeps sowing discord. The party seals all vents but the top one in the trial area so pressurised phlogiston catapults them into the workshop, with Coya following.

**Time for the Showdown (5.2).** In the workshop they free captive qucusaurs and learn from Puma's note that Tepal died long ago and someone used his name. The mastermind is Allpa, who, the wiki says, turned against qucusaurs on learning of her son's death and believed only humans should shape Natlan's future. She threatens a qucusaur chick, Chime, to make them surrender; Coya rams her, the relic becomes unstable and Chasca throws it away. It explodes and Coya shields Chasca, then collapses. The workshop is dismantled, part of it becomes a jail for Allpa and her lackeys, and Cusco tells Chasca to speak with Coya, who has forgiven her.

### ULTRA +
Finer points. The Wings were being tested as a new entry method, and Allpa told Chasca that changing the standards would take too long to put into practice. Chasca asked the party to hide when they reached Coya's nest, believing an unannounced human presence would make matters worse. Isidore believed Tepal wanted qucusaurs alive. After the explosion at the end Cusco examines Coya and says her old injuries, plus an outburst of energy, made her collapse; he adds that his wife had written from the Masters of the Night-Wind scolding him for his inaction. Mutota says he is upset the plot was not caught sooner, because many qucusaurs were hurt. Chasca's plan at the end tells Coya to act as in old times.

## revelations
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The Phlogiston Wings are made with phlogiston drawn from captured qucusaurs. "Tepal" was a front; the real mastermind is Allpa, who helps run the trials; Tepal himself had died long before. Coya's attacks were defence of her stolen chick.
### LARGE +
The wiki summary says Allpa had aimed to use unruly qucusaurs for production and to overthrow the chief, Mutota, with her lackeys. Puma's note and Tepal's disorganised journal are what exposed the deception.

## characters
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
Chasca is the lead and the chapter's emotional centre. Cusco is her adoptive father; Chimpu and Coya are the qucusaurs who raised her and her sister. Allpa is trusted organiser and villain, Mutota the tribe's chief, Coyllur, Zhunan and Mukki the applicants, Isidore the merchant, Puma Tepal's relative. Xilonen helps trace Tepal.
### LARGE +
Chasca's behaviour mirrors the qucusaurs, which is how she notices the Traveler's indwelling; she tends to hide her own hurt behind duty. The chapter also puts weight on the death of Chuychu and its effect on Chasca and her tribe.

## consequences
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Allpa and her followers are jailed in a part of the dismantled workshop. Captive qucusaurs are freed, though some drained of phlogiston still need rescue. Coya is injured saving Chasca but recovers, and the two reconcile. The tribe still has to decide how the Wings fit into the riders' future.

## placement
### STANDARD
Released entirely in Version 5.2. Acts I and II need only the Pilgrimage of the Return of the Sacred Flame quest, but Act III requires The Rainbow Destined to Burn, so it takes place after the Abyss invasion and Chuychu's death.

## entry: Phlogiston Wings
@keys: Phlogiston Wings, Phlogiston Wing, Wingless
@version: 5.2
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
A phlogiston-powered jetpack operated by a handheld trackball, brought to the Flower-Feather Clan by merchants. Using them, "Wingless" applicants can fly without a qucusaur. They are made with phlogiston drawn from captive qucusaurs.

## entry: Qucusaur Rider Trials
@keys: Qucusaur Rider, Qucusaur Riders, flying trial
@version: 5.2
@spoiler: minor
### STANDARD
The Flower-Feather Clan's entry test. Applicants need combat skill and the approval of a qucusaur; those who repeatedly fail may give up and are known as the Wingless.

## entry: Allpa
@keys: Allpa
@version: 5.2
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
A Flower-Feather Clan member who helps run the Qucusaur Rider trials and, after learning of her son's death (per the wiki summary), is revealed as the mastermind behind capturing qucusaurs for phlogiston. She is arrested after the workshop is destroyed.

## entry: Coya and Chimpu
@keys: Coya, Chimpu
@version: 5.2
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
Chimpu is the qucusaur who raised Chasca; Coya is her qucusaur daughter, whom Chasca calls her sister. After Chasca was returned to human society they drifted apart; Coya attacked the merchants because humans took her chick, Chime. She forgives Chasca.
