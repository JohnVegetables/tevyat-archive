## summary
### LITE
Grus Serena Chapter is Xianyun's Story Quest. In its one act, A Thousand Moonlit Miles, the adeptus formerly known as Cloud Retainer visits her disciples in human form, takes the name Xianyun, and helps the Traveler restore the memories of an elderly woman, Yuandai, who turns out to be a former crane in her care. It ends with Xianyun moving to Liyue Harbor.
### STANDARD +
It unlocks after The Crane Returns on the Wind at Adventure Rank 40. The tone is gentle and family-centred: a story of disciples, caring from a distance, and how adepti handle the passing of time among mortals.

## plot
@spoiler: major
### LITE
Paimon visits Mt. Aocang and meets Cloud Retainer in human form. She goes to see Ganyu and Shenhe, takes the name Xianyun, and at Wanmin Restaurant meets Shuyu and her grandmother Yuandai, who has dementia. The group retraces Yuandai's past, then uses Xianyun's Suspensus Somnium Mechanism to restore her memory. Yuandai was a crane, Xianyun's follower, and reverts to her crane form. Shuyu becomes Xianyun's first disciple in the human world.
### STANDARD +
Xingqiu recalls a nameless heroine of chivalric novels who cleared spirits and bandits during a drought around fifty years ago and met an exorcist from Mt. Tianheng. The places tied to her are Wangshu Inn, Qingyun Peak and an area north of Jueyun Karst. At each, Yuandai recalls parts of her life, including the pills she took, and the group returns to Mt. Aocang for the device that helps her remember. Xianyun then moves to Liyue Harbor and sells some belongings, with Zhongli authenticating them.
### LARGE +
**Leisurely Chatter.** Paimon decides to visit Cloud Retainer at Mt. Aocang and finds a woman at the table whom they realise is Cloud Retainer herself, in human form so that she can check on her disciples Ganyu and Shenhe without fuss. At Yuehai Pavilion Ganyu, speaking with Huixin, recognises her at once; Ganyu suggests she take a human name and she chooses Xianyun, with Ganyu explaining its meaning. Learning that Shenhe works at Wanmin Restaurant, they go there; Shenhe recognises her master immediately, which startles Xianyun, who had never shown her that form. During a break behind the restaurant Shenhe tells a story of her master losing a contest of flying machines to Moon Carver, and asks if Xianyun has ever felt lonely, which she declines to admit. At lunch, a girl called Shuyu shares their table with her grandmother Yuandai, who looks younger than expected and seems familiar to Xianyun. Shuyu explains that Yuandai's dementia has worsened and that her parents died young. Xianyun suggests retracing places from Yuandai's youth, and Shuyu says her father told her Yuandai was a martial-arts heroine.

**A Search for Secrets.** Xingqiu at Feiyun Commerce Guild recalls the nameless heroine and marks three sites. Shuyu wonders whether her late grandfather was the nameless exorcist, having once found a box of sigils, and privately tells the Traveler and Xianyun that a white crane once flew her to an adeptus's home and gave her a potion that cured a fever. Xianyun does not confirm that it was her. At Wangshu Inn Xiao teleports in, sensing a non-human presence, and is glad to find no trouble; he recalls fighting alongside her during the war and describes her as solemn and quiet then. Yuandai remembers having stayed at the inn to recover from an illness and being given Soul-Revitalizing Teapills by a merchant. North of Jueyun Karst, after Treasure Hoarders are driven off, she recalls staying in a house and then another, where Shuyu's father was born after she and her husband were chased by an unknown being in fog. At Qingyun Peak she points toward Mt. Aocang in fear.

**Lovely Dreams.** At Mt. Aocang Xianyun reveals the Suspensus Somnium Mechanism, which releases a gentle breeze and an adeptal tune that relaxes listeners. Under its influence Yuandai calls her Cloud Retainer and tells her story: once a long-lived wild crane who gained sentience and was taken in by Cloud Retainer, she admired the heroine tales and was given a potion of human mimicry lasting ten years. She fell in love with an exorcist from Mt. Tianheng and stayed with him past the potion's term, which led to her dementia. Ashamed, she left a letter and lived in seclusion. She now realises the merchant at the inn was Cloud Retainer in disguise. Xianyun confirms she was angry at the letter but, seeing his devotion, accepted the choice, and made the pills, and also healed Shuyu. Yuandai asks why she was not forced back; Xianyun says everyone must leave one day, but that she is welcome to return. As the medicine fades, Yuandai reverts to a crane without sentience and flies away. Shuyu asks to become Xianyun's disciple; Xianyun says she will move to Liyue Harbor, making Shuyu her first disciple in the human world.

Two days later at Chihu Rock, Xianyun argues with Shaozu over selling her items. His hired expert is Zhongli, who verifies the goods; Shaozu pays a deposit from Northland Bank, as the total is more than he can pay at once. Zhongli and Xianyun talk briefly about past and future before planning a dinner to introduce Shuyu to Ganyu and Shenhe.
### ULTRA +
While the Suspensus Somnium Mechanism runs, the Traveler dreams of their sibling and of travelling Teyvat together, and Xianyun notes they were not ready to wake. This is the quest's one touch on the Traveler's own search and is presented as a dream, not a revelation.

## revelations
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
- Yuandai was originally a long-lived wild crane, taken in by Cloud Retainer, not a human heroine.
- The merchant who gave Yuandai medicine at Wangshu Inn was Xianyun in disguise.
- Xianyun was also the white crane who helped the sick Shuyu.
### LARGE +
- Xianyun says she had no need of human form after the war while living in the wilds.
- Yuandai's husband was an exorcist from Mt. Tianheng.

## characters
### LITE
Xianyun (Cloud Retainer), Paimon and the Traveler; Ganyu, Shenhe, Xiao, Xingqiu, Zhongli; new characters Yuandai and Shuyu.
### STANDARD +
Supporting cast includes Huixin, Chef Mao, Madame Ping, Shaozu, Wenliu and Qingji. Xianyun is shown as warm, proud and bluntly practical, quietly caring for those she has taken in.

## consequences
### STANDARD
Yuandai returns to her crane form and flies off, losing her sentience. Xianyun moves to Liyue Harbor and takes Shuyu as her first disciple in the human world, and adopts the name Xianyun in place of the title Cloud Retainer.

## placement
### STANDARD
Released in Version 4.4 as Xianyun's Story Quest; requires The Crane Returns on the Wind (Interlude Chapter, Act I) and Adventure Rank 40. Xianyun's move to Liyue Harbor happens in this act.

### STANDARD
A device kept at Mt. Aocang that releases a soft breeze and an adeptal tune. It puts listeners in a relaxed, semi-hypnotic state that helps lost memories surface. Xianyun uses it on Yuandai and the Traveler.
