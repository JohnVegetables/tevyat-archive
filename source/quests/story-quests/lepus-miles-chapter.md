## summary
### LITE
The Lepus Miles Chapter is Lohen's Story Quest, released in Version 6.6. It has one act, A Knight's Legacy, in three quests, set in Mondstadt. Lohen is due to receive the title Benevolent Knight from the ailing Adorno and command the 5th Company, but he is secretly helping Theodore use Ursa the Drake's flesh to prolong Adorno's life and to chase power himself. Adorno dies, and Lohen is punished.
### STANDARD +
The three quests are A Knight's Merit, A Knight's Pursuit and A Knight's Resolve. It follows the Archon Quest An Elegy for Faded Moonlight (Song of the Welkin Moon, Act IV) and requires Adventure Rank 40. Its tone mixes dark comedy (Lohen's "friendship fee" and his grisly "patrol methods") with a tragedy about obsession and mortality.
### LARGE +
Its themes are what makes a knight (merit, conviction, or raw power), the lure of "extraordinary" power versus ordinary mortal life, and the shadow of Eroch's old betrayal over a new generation. Lohen's current struggle was sharpened by his defeat at Rerir's hands during the Nod-Krai expedition.
### ULTRA +
The chapter links the Nod-Krai arc to Mondstadt: Lohen's obsession is rooted in a childhood rescue from Eroch and Dottore's experiments, sharpened by being beaten by Rerir, and finally refused when Ursa's voice offers him the same kind of power. The wiki event description frames the question as whether a mortal who glimpses fate-rewriting power should submit or defy.

## plot
### LITE
At an award ceremony for returning expedition knights, Lohen avoids his title and drags the Traveler into a pretence of friendship to face Adorno. After a memory-simulation test goes wrong, the Traveler learns Lohen and Theodore were once test subjects rescued by Adorno. Strange Ursa-tainted monsters appear, Adorno is hurt protecting a child, Lohen and Theodore vanish with him to a hidden lab, and Lohen holds off the escaped monsters while Varka, Diluc and the Traveler destroy Ursa's remains. Adorno dies; Lohen is confined and denied the title.
### STANDARD
Kaeya tells the Traveler and Paimon that returning expedition knights are to be honoured, and that Lohen is expected to be named the Benevolent Knight and given command of the 5th Company. Lohen, who does not want either, pays them a "friendship fee" to pose as his friends while he visits Adorno at Dornman Port, and tells Adorno carefully disguised, grisly tales of his "training" and "patrols". Adorno takes him on a day of small kindnesses and a monster patrol, where Diluc says someone has cleaned up the monsters before them. In a test of the Simulation Training Bubble, Type 02, Lohen fights Adorno's memory of Rerir, loses control and is hurt; Adorno reveals that Lohen was a child saved from Eroch's experiments, and Sucrose notes unfamiliar power in him. Lohen and Theodore then pursue Ursa-tainted monsters, Adorno is badly hurt shielding a child, and Lohen swears to confess the next day. Instead Lohen, Theodore and Adorno vanish to a hidden lab, where Theodore has been using Ursa's flesh to make a serum to prolong Adorno's life and Lohen has been helping for his own pursuit of power. Adorno comforts both. Lohen draws the escaped monsters while Varka, Diluc and the Traveler destroy Ursa's remains, and he rejects Ursa's offer of power. Adorno dies; Jean confines Lohen, rescinds his commendation and buries the Benevolent Knight title.
### LARGE +
**A Knight's Merit.** Kaeya tells the Traveler and Paimon that returning expedition knights, including Winfried, Leifhelm and Lohen, will get titles; Lohen is expected to be called the Benevolent Knight and to take command of the 5th Company, which would break Diluc's record as youngest Captain. Using Varka's name, Lohen summons them to the headquarters and pays a "friendship fee" for them to act as his friends when he visits Adorno, the retiring Captain, at Dornman Port. He tells Adorno gruesome, lightly disguised stories of his "group training" and "patrols" (drugged wine, traps, night raids and coercing captives), then admits that Rerir beat him in Nod-Krai. Adorno takes him through an ordinary day of candy for children, dogs and a lost cat, then a monster patrol. Diluc says someone cleaned up the monsters first. Adorno explains Ursa, Eroch and the Doctor's old plot. At the 10th Company's Simulation Training Bubble, Type 02, built from Alice's magic, Lohen asks to face the Rächer of Solnari from Adorno's memory. The test spirals, Adorno is strained, and Lohen is hurt. Adorno reveals that Lohen was one of the children saved from Eroch's experiments. Sucrose finds unfamiliar power in Lohen. Theodore, treating him, warns that they could be exposed.

**A Knight's Pursuit.** Lohen has left a note for Adorno promising to wipe out monsters. Diluc says the monsters fight each other and match no natural pattern; Varka explains Ursa's flesh and Eroch's old serum, and the Traveler tells them about the power sensed in Lohen. Lohen pursues a Geovishap Hatchling, then presses Theodore to use him as a test subject; it fails. A hatchling attacks Adorno while he is saving a child's cat, and he is badly hurt. Lohen swears on his honour to confess the next day. Theodore, at Adorno's bedside, hints at what he is about to do. Soon Adorno, Lohen and Theodore are gone.

**A Knight's Resolve.** Following Lohen's Cryo trail, Varka, Diluc, the Traveler and Paimon enter a hidden lab that looks like the work of one of Dottore's Segments, and a memory projection shows a captive being carried through it in a box. Lohen finds Theodore tending Adorno; flashbacks show how Lohen had offered himself as a subject and kept the Knights from noticing. Theodore completes a refined, high-concentration serum, which Lohen says would kill Adorno in his present state; Theodore ends up too weak to stand after using the highest concentration. Adorno, who has overheard everything, comforts both as he did when he first rescued them. Lohen takes the serum, crushes a vial to draw the restless monsters, and holds them while Varka, Diluc and the Traveler defeat Ursa. A voice tempts him with power; he stabs his own hand and rejects it. Adorno is later buried in Mondstadt. Lohen is confined; Jean rescinds his commendation, denies him promotion and buries the Benevolent Knight title with Adorno.

Further detail: at the ceremony Kaeya says Lohen's title was delayed by "personal reasons". Varka says Adorno's final wish is to see Lohen inherit the title, and the two had a pact: Adorno recommended Lohen for the expedition, and Lohen would reflect on what a virtuous knight is. and Lohen hurts himself trying to hold back his final blow. Sucrose says the bubble's parameters were normal but the energy released was neither elemental nor "Void Realm energy".

In the second quest Varka tells Diluc that he, Crepus and Eroch were once three young men who imagined their futures together: Crepus the rich young master who inherited the winery, Varka the rule-breaker who became Grand Master, and Eroch the one who got neither wealth nor power. In the third quest the three documents (notes, origins, scribbled lines) hint at an organisation's earlier plan to make Ursa's flesh a controllable asset.

In the lab, Theodore first restarted Eroch's experiments from hazy childhood memory, and Lohen discovered him later and joined in, wanting power. The serum helped Adorno get out of bed, but Lohen said it would kill Adorno in his present state. Theodore asks Lohen to destroy Ursa's flesh before he collapses. Lohen asks Varka for formal permission to attack and Varka orders him not to die.

At the grave Lohen chews bubblegum he made during confinement; he says Adorno liked giving sweets to children. His penalties were solitary confinement, loss of the commendation and no promotion; the 5th Company captaincy stays vacant for now. He says he will keep winning and still pursues power, and ends by asking the others not to grow old before he can shoulder everything alone.
### ULTRA +
Theodore, like Lohen, was a child rescued by Adorno; he has long wanted the Benevolent Knight title, which Adorno had meant to pass to Lohen. Lohen refuses Ursa's offer because he wants to remain master of what he wields, and states that forgetting why he pursues power would be true defeat. The epitaph on Adorno's grave pairs a couplet about not weeping at the grave with the line "Here rests the Benevolent Knight".

## revelations
@version: 6.6
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
- Lohen and Theodore were both children rescued by Adorno from Eroch and the Doctor's experiments.
- Ursa the Drake's flesh remained in a hidden lab, and Theodore restarted its serum experiments to prolong Adorno's life; Lohen joined for his own pursuit of power.
- Monsters carrying Ursa's mark appear near Dornman Port; they attack each other.
- Adorno, already ailing, dies after the lab episode and is buried with the Benevolent Knight title.
- Varka reports that Ursa was once an angel (hearsay), sealed by the first Grand Master Vennessa.
### LARGE +
- The Simulation Training Bubble, Type 02, recreates memories as training; Sucrose and the 10th Company are testing it.
- The experiment site was concealed so well that the Knights never found it.
- Lohen was beaten by Rerir during the expedition and is haunted by the word "mortal".
### ULTRA +
- Lab documents suggest a hidden plan to seed fear in Mondstadt; authorship is unstated.

## characters
### STANDARD
- **Lohen**: Vice Captain of the 5th Company, titled "Sharpest Arrow", the chapter's protagonist.
- **Adorno**: retiring Captain of the 5th Company, the Benevolent Knight.
- **Theodore**: trainee medic of the 8th Company, treating Adorno.
- **The Traveler and Paimon**: pretend friends, witnesses and fighters.
- **Varka, Diluc, Kaeya, Sucrose, Jean**: the Grand Master, a former Cavalry Captain, the current Cavalry Captain, a researcher working on the bubble, and the Acting Grand Master who sets the punishments.
### LARGE +
Ursa the Drake, Eroch, Rerir and Dottore appear mostly as memories or reports. Winfried, Leifhelm, Ekbert, Lietzberg, Wernher and Porthos are minor knights; Max, Moricztill and Hannelore are Mondstadt locals.

## consequences
### STANDARD
Ursa's flesh at the site is destroyed. Adorno has died by the closing scene. Lohen is placed in solitary confinement, his commendation is rescinded, he is denied promotion, and the Benevolent Knight title is buried with Adorno.
### LARGE +
The 5th Company captaincy remains vacant for now. Theodore's penalty is left for Jean and the others.

## placement
@availability: limited
### STANDARD
Released in Version 6.6 ("Luna VII"). It requires An Elegy for Faded Moonlight, so it happens after the Nod-Krai expedition returns to Mondstadt. Story order and release order match. The chapter has no further acts as of 7.1.
### LARGE +
A same-named event ran from 2026-06-09 to 2026-06-30, giving Primogems and materials for finishing the quest and offering a Quick Start to players at Adventure Rank 28 who had completed Chapter I, Act III (A New Star Approaches). Only these rewards were limited; the quest remains playable.

## entry: Benevolent Knight
@keys: Benevolent Knight, Benevolent title
@keys2: Lohen, Adorno, Theodore
@version: 6.6
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
The honorary title held by Adorno, earned when he rescued kidnapped children from Eroch's experiments. He wanted Lohen to inherit it with the 5th Company captaincy, and had once planned to leave the title to Theodore. After Adorno's death, Jean has the title buried with him.
### LARGE +
Adorno himself says he never deserved it, as he failed to find Eroch's site or ease Lohen's trauma.

## entry: Ursa's Flesh and Serum
@keys: Ursa's Flesh, Ursa serum, Ursa the Drake serum
@keys2: Lohen, Theodore, Eroch
@version: 6.6
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
A serum drawn from the preserved flesh of Ursa the Drake. Eroch and a Doctor Segment first made it, Eroch's followers injected it and went mad, and Theodore later restarted production to prolong Adorno's life. Monsters carrying it fight one another and are far stronger than normal. Lohen takes a vial into the final fight as bait.
### LARGE +
The flesh needs repeated stimulation to give high-concentration serum; overflow of its power changes nearby plants and monsters.

## entry: Simulation Training Bubble: Type 02
@keys: Simulation Training Bubble, Type 02
@keys2: Sucrose, Alice, Adorno
@version: 6.6
### STANDARD
A memory-recreating magic bubble based on Alice's work and used to welcome the expeditionary force, now being tested by the 10th Company as a safe combat training aid. It recreates enemies from knights' memories.
### LARGE +
In the quest it misbehaves when Lohen fights Adorno's recollection of Rerir, though Sucrose finds the bubble's parameters normal.
