## summary
### LITE
Draco Rubedo Chapter is Durin's Story Quest (Version 6.2, "Luna III"), one act titled "The Magic Known as Storytelling". Alice brings the Traveler and Paimon a book that the Hexenzirkel witches wrote together, which Durin's mother Andersdotter died before finishing. Together they enter the book repeatedly over several days to complete Durin's story and, with Albedo and the Wanderer's help, solve the problem that Durin is mistaken for the evil historical Durin.
### STANDARD
The act has nine parts: five main quests and four "A Moment's Rest" interludes. It combines Mondstadt scenes (the library, Good Hunter, Dragonspine) with trips inside the book, where Durin's life is retold as a story. The central question is what happens to a character after "happily ever after", and the answer is that Durin must write the ending himself. It ends with his new outfit, a cure for the symptoms of disconnection, and a flight with Dvalin.
### LARGE +
It is also the quest where the Hexenzirkel's quiet, long-running plan is shown: Barbeloth's astrology, Rhinedottir's alchemy and Alice's magic combined to give Andersdotter's son a future, with the Traveler cast as the "reader" and Durin as the "writer".

## plot
### STANDARD
Alice sends the Traveler and Paimon to Durin at Starsand Shoal and gives them the unfinished Hexenzirkel book. Over several days they follow its clues through Mondstadt (the library, Venti, Good Hunter, Stormterror's Lair) and into the story world, where Durin's life appears as a tale. Durin collapses when people who do not know him take him for the evil historical Durin; the Wanderer, in Sumeru, explains that the world refuses to see him as a separate being. Albedo and the Hexenzirkel's plan lets him break out of the story by writing his own ending, with his mother's pen.
### LARGE +
**A Mother's Last Wish.** Alice tells the Traveler and Paimon telepathically to meet Durin at Starsand Shoal, where he stands beside four slain Radiant Beasts. He briefly appears in his Denial of Darkness form, recognises them and returns to his Confirmation of Purity form. Durin says his power has been harder to control lately. Alice opens a book into a special space and explains it is the joint work of the Hexenzirkel witches; Andersdotter died before finishing her part and asked that Durin receive it, since only he could resolve it. Durin accepts the task and Alice gives them a magic ink bottle of Andersdotter's. The first clue sends them to the Mondstadt library, where Lisa (who once volunteered to teach Durin to read) helps them find books missing from the catalogue; the book then pulls them in. Inside, they find a Dodoco narrator and a dragon egg that hatches into a baby Durin.

**A Prophecy From the Past.** Next day, with Anemo energy pointing to Venti, Venti tells them to go to where the mages met and play a melody. Durin asks him why an author would leave a story unfinished; Venti says there are many reasons and that, knowing Durin's mother, it is not his place to pry. Back in the story, four witches in Dodoco form welcome the infant with gifts (a book, a slingshot and a sword), and Durin chooses one.

**Fate's Accursed Wiles.** At Good Hunter Durin is playing Genius Invokation TCG against Kaeya while Fischl and Klee watch. Durin then takes the Traveler to Stormterror's Lair and into the story, where the grown Mini Durin is shunned; they persuade him to open his heart. They think they are finished and go to Good Hunter, but Marjorie and Shuyun are suspicious of Durin, believing he is the Durin they remember from history, and the rest are confused why nobody knew him. Durin collapses from a burst of abyssal energy, which the Traveler purifies. Jean says Barbara found no issue and takes custody of the book, deciding to tell Albedo. Venti and Dvalin watch from afar, Venti saying the story is not over.

**Revelation Beneath the Stars.** Durin explains that when people forgot him he felt disconnected from his body and only reconnected when friends spoke his name. New clues lead to Sumeru City, where the Wanderer is returning a modified book to Katayoun. The Wanderer says that whenever he tried to write about Durin the text changed to the Durin of history, and, as a Descender unaffected by Irminsul's changes, he concludes the world refuses to accept this Durin as a separate being. He joins them in a simulated Sumeru in which he is a cat and Durin follows the path he took after Simulanka, and concludes the Hexenzirkel built a way for Durin to exist separately.

**The Solution of Fate.** Albedo summons them to Dragonspine, examines Durin and explains the witches' plan: it needs Barbeloth's astrology, Rhinedottir's alchemy and Alice's magic. In the book Albedo becomes a Simulanka resident and the Wanderer a cat. Destroying a heart fails with a bad ending; Alice's magic resets it. Durin decides to break out of the story. A flashback shows Andersdotter wanting her son to live happily in Teyvat; she knew the story had to end and chose that Durin write his own. A manifestation of his mother gives him her pen and he defies the original's fate in a climactic battle. Back in Mondstadt, he wears a freer outfit and Albedo notes his symptoms are gone.

Afterwards Alice tells the Traveler she chose them as reader so her magic could work. Barbeloth looks at the stars and no longer finds Durin's fate there, calls him a lucky child and then stops responding. Venti whispers that Durin may fulfil a final regret, and Durin flies with Dvalin and befriends him. A fragment of Andersdotter's consciousness says goodbye to Alice, pleased with how her child has grown, and Alice gives the Traveler a copy of the finished book.
### ULTRA +
Between main quests four "Interlude: A Moment's Rest" quests mark the passage of days; the wiki notes that the Story Quest cannot be completed in less than five days of real time, unlike earlier Story Quests. Each visit to the book covers a later stage of Durin's life, ending with his mother's pen.

## revelations
@version: 6.2
@spoiler: major
### STANDARD
- Andersdotter wrote a story to bring her son's character into Teyvat, but died before finishing it; her last wish was that Durin write the ending.
- Durin was forgotten or mistaken for the evil historical Durin by people who did not know him; he felt disconnected until friends spoke his name.
- The Hexenzirkel prepared a solution (astrology, alchemy and magic) that lets Durin exist separately, and it works.
### LARGE +
Barbeloth no longer sees Durin's fate among the stars after the story is completed; she calls him a lucky child.

## characters
### STANDARD
Durin (lead), the Traveler and Paimon, Alice (guide and maker of the reader/writer arrangement), Andersdotter (remembered), Lisa, Venti, Kaeya, Fischl, Klee, Diona (mentioned), Marjorie and Shuyun, Jean, Barbara (examines him, offstage), Albedo, the Wanderer, Katayoun, Barbeloth, Rhinedottir (referenced), Dvalin.
### LARGE +
Albedo is Durin's brother in this quest's wording; the Wanderer is the only character who explains why the world misreads Durin.

## consequences
### STANDARD
Durin's disconnection symptoms are gone and he gets the outfit "Toward the Distant Horizon" as the chapter reward. He becomes friends with Dvalin. The Traveler receives a copy of the newly made book.

## placement
### STANDARD
Released in Version 6.2 (Luna III) with an event period starting 2025-12-03; the chapter stays available. It requires Adventure Rank 40 and Act VI of Song of the Welkin Moon, "Melting Moonlight in the Morning Mist". During the event, Quick Start allowed access at Adventure Rank 28 after Chapter I Act III of the Archon Quest.

## entry: Andersdotter's Book

@keys2: Durin, Alice, Hexenzirkel

### STANDARD
The book is the joint work of the Hexenzirkel witches. Andersdotter left her part unfinished and asked Alice to give the work to Durin. Reading it moves the Traveler, Paimon and Durin into a story world where he is a Dodoco-narrated baby, a shunned adult, and later a character in a replica of Sumeru.
### LARGE +
The Traveler plays the "reader", Durin the "writer". Alice's magic only works if both roles are filled.
