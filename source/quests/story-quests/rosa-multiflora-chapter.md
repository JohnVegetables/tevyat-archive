## summary
### LITE
The Rosa Multiflora Chapter is Navia's Story Quest (Version 4.3), set in Fontaine after Masquerade of the Guilty. In its one act, Braving the Tides Together, she oversees the rebuilding of Poisson, faces former Spina di Rosula members who resent her father, and decides how to honour her parents' legacy.
### STANDARD +
The act has three quests: Shimmering Memories, Lost and Inherited and Yesterday's Tears, Tomorrow's Light. It requires Adventure Rank 40 and completion of Masquerade of the Guilty. The chapter's flower name is a wild rose, echoing Spina di Rosula, the organisation her parents led.
### LARGE +
The chapter shows Navia as a leader who has lost her parents and her trusted attendants and still chooses support over grievance. Themes are inheritance, the cost of mistakes, and the difference between nostalgia for glory days and loyalty to people.
### ULTRA +
It links Navia's backstory to the old aquabus funding dispute that split the organisation, to the aftermath of the Confrerie of Cabriere and its Sinthe production being exposed, and to Poisson's recovery from the Primordial Sea flood.

## plot
### LITE
Navia takes the Traveler to Poisson. Colter, an old member just released from the Fortress of Meropide, joins them, but former members led by Romeuf plot to bomb the Clementine Line and bury Poisson. Navia and the Traveler stop it, and the town reveals a statue design of Navia with her parents.
### STANDARD
**Shimmering Memories.** At the Court of Fontaine the Traveler and Paimon find Navia overworked, supervising Poisson's reconstruction without her attendants Melus and Silver. There is no aquabus line to Poisson, so they go by boat. There Florent, who has taken over some of Melus's duties, mentions a planned statue honouring Navia and her parents. Colter, having served his sentence in Meropide, arrives unaware that Melus and Silver have died or of Marcel's plot involving the Primordial Water. He asks about Romeuf and recalls the old quarrel: Romeuf wanted funds spent on Poisson, Callas backed the aquabus, and Romeuf left with his men on bad terms. Navia takes Colter to fetch a new uniform and shares macarons on an old road; something stirs in a bush behind them.

**Lost and Inherited.** In the town Navia notices they are followed, chases three men and defeats them. Their leader Martinat confirms they are Romeuf's men who were angered at seeing her drinking. She lets them go with a warning but asks Florent to increase security. The group stays at Fleuve Cendre and goes fishing; Navia speaks of her childhood immaturity, how Melus was hurt because of a mistake of hers, and how her mother's care inspires her own kindness. Meanwhile Martinat tells Colter the plan begins the next day.
### LARGE +
**Yesterday's Tears, Tomorrow's Light.** Back in Poisson, Navia meets Adele and Coralie, and promises Adele a place in Spina di Rosula when she is old enough. Florent reports that Romeuf has brought explosives to bomb the Clementine Line so that the debris would fall on the town. During the evacuation Colter tries to attack Navia but Florent stops him. Colter says he blames her for ruining what the organisation meant, and that he had fallen out with Melus before learning he had died. Navia chooses to show trust and subdues him. The Traveler disables one set of explosives; another goes off, but Navia stops the falling debris. The Maison Gardiennage arrives and arrests Romeuf's followers, and Navia refuses to handle them privately as former members. She sympathises with Colter, dismisses him from the organisation but says he may return after his release, and says she will try to bring an aquabus to Poisson. After resting she asks the Traveler whether they see her as a partner; they confirm. Florent then shows her the statue design, an adult Navia standing with her parents.
### ULTRA +
Romeuf's faction held that Clementine, while pregnant with Navia, could no longer act as an impartial mediator, and so rejected the aquabus decision that Callas carried out after her death. Navia's remark that she did not want to lose another link to her parents explains her protectiveness of the Clementine Line.

## revelations
### STANDARD
No world-level revelations. It deepens Navia's backstory, explains why Spina di Rosula split, and shows the state of Poisson after the flood.

## characters
### LITE
Navia, the Traveler and Paimon. Florent, Colter, Romeuf and his men, Adele and Coralie appear.
### STANDARD +
Navia is portrayed as dutiful, generous and tired, willing to forgive but firm when people are endangered. Florent acts as her steady deputy; Colter is the sympathetic, embittered veteran; Romeuf is the rebel who left the organisation.

## consequences
### STANDARD
Romeuf and his followers are arrested; Colter is dismissed but welcome to return; Navia plans an aquabus link for Poisson; and the town prepares a family statue.

## placement
### STANDARD
Release order: 4.3. Story order: after Chapter IV Act V, Masquerade of the Guilty, in the aftermath of Fontaine's flood. This is not an Archon Quest.
